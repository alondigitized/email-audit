# Tick tock! Black Friday isn't over yet — and we think you'll LOVE these deals
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-28**
## Executive summary

- This is the same DICK'S Black Friday template we've seen in the 11/26 and 11/27 sends, including "Biggest Sale of the Year? Unlocked." and "Big savings alert!". The dark green "BIGGEST SALE OF THE YEAR" hero leads into "Black Friday: Up to 50% Off" with a "Limited Time" tag and a red Shop Now button. It is competent, but it is the same email again. Only the subject changed.
- The new content is a red "EXTRA 25% OFF" clearance block and a ScoreCard+ "$20 Bonus Reward" module. The offer stack is now crowded. There are four or five competing deals (50% off, 30% off Nike/Jordan, 60% off bats, 50% off tables, extra 25% off) plus the loyalty bonus. The scroll is very long and the eye has no single place to land after the hero.
- Verdict: solid execution and a credible deadline (the exclusions note runs through 11/29), but this is fatigue territory. Across about a dozen near-identical sends in four days, the "Tick tock" urgency is the only thing that feels fresh. I don't see render bugs in the image.

## What's working

- The hero and a single headline offer ("Up to 50% Off") sit at the top with a visible red Shop Now button.
- Category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Golf, Fan Shop) give easy entry points.
- The red "EXTRA 25% OFF" block is the strongest visual break in the scroll and stands out clearly.
- The ScoreCard+ module offers a concrete member benefit ($20 bonus reward on a $150+ purchase).
- Product cards carry specific prices ($49.98 VRST fleece, $49.98 CALIA scuba shorts) and a "Gift picks" grid with named items (Stanley, Callaway, UGG, HOKA).

## What's weak

- It is the same template as the last 11/26 and 11/27 sends, so it adds no new reason to open or click.
- The subject says "Black Friday isn't over yet," but the sale is not clearly framed as ending. The email never shows a real countdown.
- Too many competing offers and about eight CTAs make the hierarchy flat after the hero.
- The fine print is dense. The exclusions are tiny and sit directly under the hero, and the "Extra 25%" block has its own separate conditions.
- Several modules (the tennis table, baseball bats, fan shop) feel unrelated to each other. Nothing in the email says it was chosen for the recipient, despite the subject's "we think you'll LOVE."

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the one real deadline: a countdown or "Ends tonight 11:59 PT" banner above the hero. The "Tick tock" promise isn't visible in the render.
- 2. Cut the body to three modules: the hero offer, the extra 25% clearance, and the ScoreCard+ bonus. Move the rest to category pills.
- 3. Merge the ScoreCard+ bonus into the top offer so members see it without scrolling.
- 4. Segment by category (shoes, team sports, fitness) so "you'll love" is actually true.
- 5. Vary the creative on sends two or more per day. Don't reuse the same hero.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: up to 50% off + extra 25% clearance`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: Black Friday deals end 11:59 PM PT`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus earn a $20 ScoreCard+ bonus reward on $150+`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Nike, Jordan, bats and gear: shop before the deals are gone`
