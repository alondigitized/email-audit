# Gone in a FLASH: Up to 50% off ends today ⚡⏰
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-01**
## Executive summary

- This is the third outing for the green locker-room "FLASH SALE" hero — it debuted 2026-07-30 as "You've got 2 days! Score up to 50% off Flash Sale deals NOW" (8.00/10) and now returns with a harder-urgency subject ("Gone in a FLASH... ends today ⚡⏰") for what should be the actual final day. The creative itself is still strong — bold typography, clear green "Ends Today" tag, unambiguous "Shop Now" CTA — but it's immediately undercut by the red "UP TO 70% OFF Summer Clearance Event" block stacked right beneath it, the same clearance module DICK'S has run since 7/29. Two competing sales in the first two scrolls dilutes which offer is actually urgent.
- Below that it's the familiar kitchen-sink cascade: Stanley tumblers, cleats, golf electronics, life vests, MLB gear, ScoreCard signup, then a "must-have gear" grid. Nothing here is broken, but nothing here is new either — this is a reused hero wrapped in a slightly punchier subject line.
- Verdict: solid render, real urgency copy, but it's a rerun of assets subscribers have already seen twice this week, and the two-offer stacking at the top blunts what should be a clean "last chance" moment.

## What's working

- Bold, high-contrast "FLASH SALE" hero renders cleanly with no overlap or broken assets.
- Green "Ends Today" badge plus explicit "Flash Sale: Up to 50% Off" subhead gives immediate offer clarity.
- Primary CTA ("Shop Now") is a clear green button directly under the offer, no scrolling needed.
- Category quick-links (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Golf, Back-to-School) give a fast secondary path for shoppers not sold on the flash sale itself.
- ScoreCard loyalty block is visible and explains the earn rate concretely (1 point per $1, 300 = $10).

## What's weak

- The red "UP TO 70% OFF Summer Clearance Event" block sits immediately below the Flash Sale hero, competing for the same "last day" urgency and confusing which deal is actually ending.
- This is the same hero creative as the 7/31 send two days ago — visually identical locker/typography treatment, just a new subject line.
- After two big sale blocks, the email reverts to the same generic category cascade (Stanley, cleats, golf electronics, life vests, MLB) seen in prior sends — no fresh merchandising.
- "Back-to-School Shop" pill sits oddly among Flash Sale category links, an off-season/off-message mix for a "flash sale ends today" email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the "Up to 70% Off Summer Clearance" module entirely from this send, or move it well below the fold — a true flash-sale-ends-today email should have one hero offer, not two competing "last day" claims.
- 2. Swap in a new hero image/graphic — this is the third appearance of the locker-room creative in a week; visual fatigue is real even if the subject rotates.
- 3. Lead the category cascade with the highest-margin or highest-interest item (cleats/back-to-school) instead of Stanley tumblers, since school shopping is the more timely seasonal driver right now.
- 4. Add a countdown timer or explicit end time ("Ends tonight at 11:59pm ET") in the hero copy itself, not just in fine print, to reinforce the subject line's urgency.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cleats, golf gear, and MLB fan gear — all included before it's gone.`

# Technical Audit — DICK'S "Flash Sale" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders via a standard Epsilon/Harmony-hosted template with MSO conditional comments and responsive breakpoints, but ships with a broken unsubscribe link, no captured unsubscribe/authentication headers, and an invalid (comment-containing) JSON-LD block.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken unsubscribe redirect (FAIL):** The "Unsubscribe" link (`https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/U/v610000019fbccc7fdbbeeba8369fea47bc/0c551a78c53740d30000021ef3a0bcff`) resolves to an error page: `/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription`. This is a functional dead link, not a tracking artifact.
- All sampled content links route through the same click-tracking redirector pattern (`app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/{campaignId}/{linkId}/{contentId}?__dU__=...`), consistent with Epsilon Harmony ESP link wrapping. No malformed tracking URLs were found among the probed links.
- QA probed 12 of 62 material links (footer/social/utility links deprioritized by design); the unsubscribe link is always included in probing and is the one that failed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` text on 10 images**, including primary content images (`cleats.jpg`, `golfelectronics.jpg`, `vests.jpg`, `mlb.jpg`, `banner.jpg`) and the SSL/open-tracking pixel. Screen readers and image-blocked clients (common default in Outlook) will render no fallback text for these.
- `html lang="en"` is correctly declared.
- Responsive breakpoints for 480px are defined via `@media screen` classes (`.mobile-off`, `.header-xl`, etc.), and MSO conditional comments (`<!--[if (gte mso 9)|(IE)]-->`) are present for Outlook fallback — no structural rendering issues found in the provided source.
- Web font (`Archivo`) is loaded via `@font-face` with `font-display:swap` inside a `@media screen` guard, correctly excluded from Outlook/MSO rendering.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) are visible in the truncated source.
- The `application/ld+json` schema block contains **unresolved empty template fields**: `"description": ""`, `"discountCode": ""`, `"availabilityEnds": ""`, and `"image": ""` under the `PromotionCard`/`DiscountOffer` types — these appear to be unfilled build-time placeholders left in production output.
- **Invalid JSON:** the same `<script type="application/ld+json">` block contains JavaScript-style `//` comments (e.g. `// GIF & WEBP images are not supported...`, `// DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!`). JSON does not support comments — this will cause `JSON.parse` failures for any consumer parsing this structured data (e.g. Gmail annotations, schema.org crawlers), effectively invalidating the promotional markup for that image block.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe link is non-functional** (see §2) — a direct CAN-SPAM risk, as the mechanism must actually work, not merely be present.
- `List-Unsubscribe` header: not found. May be a relay-capture limitation (AgentMail) rather than confirmed absence, but as observed it means one-click unsubscribe support cannot be verified.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058): not found — one-click unsubscribe cannot be confirmed as supported.
- `Authentication-Results` header: not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown from this capture; flagged as unknown, not a confirmed authentication failure.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Links use ESP redirector URLs (`app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...?__dU__=<opaque token>`) rather than direct landing-page URLs with visible UTM parameters. The `__dU__` token is an opaque tracking parameter, not a standard `utm_*` param.
- Because final destination URLs are only reachable after redirect resolution, UTM/landing-page alignment cannot be verified from the HTML source alone — this would require following the redirect chain, which was not part of the provided data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the unsubscribe link target — `/pc/subscription` currently 404s via `aspxerrorpath`; this is a CAN-SPAM compliance blocker, not cosmetic.
2. Remove the `//` comments from the `application/ld+json` block or move them outside the script tag — as written the block is invalid JSON.
3. Populate or remove the empty `description`, `discountCode`, `availabilityEnds`, and `image` fields in the JSON-LD schema before send.
4. Add `alt` attributes to all 10 flagged images, particularly the four content banners, so the offer is legible with images blocked.
5. Confirm whether `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually sent by the origin MTA (vs. dropped by the AgentMail relay capture) — if genuinely absent, add them for RFC 8058 one-click compliance and Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
6. Verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status directly at the sending MTA, since `Authentication-Results` was not captured here.
