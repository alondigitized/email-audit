# 👀 Up to 80% OFF? You read that right! Score *unreal* savings on select styles now
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- The hero is unmistakable. A huge gold "80% OFF" on red, a "Limited Time" tag and one green "Shop Now" button tell you the offer in about a second. The 80% claim beats every number in DICK'S recent run of 40%, 50% and 70% sends. It's the strongest hook they've had this week.
- The email then buries its own hero. Directly under it are an "Early Black Friday Sale" block (up to 50%), a "40% off top brands" block, and about six more category blocks. It's the same shape as the 11/20 "Starting NOW" send, which ran a 50% hero and then stacked more offers. This is the fourth or fifth discount-led DICK'S email in 48 hours, following the 70% clearance sends on 11/16, 11/17, 18 and 19. The "You read that right!" subject tries to treat 80% as news, but a subscriber who has seen "up to 70%" three times will read this as the same clearance with a bigger ceiling.
- Verdict: a good hero and a strong subject, weighed down by a body that argues against its own headline. It should score a little above the usual 7, but the cluttered scroll and the repeated cadence cap it.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 80% OFF" hero is huge, high-contrast and readable at phone size.
- There is one clear green "Shop Now" button directly under the hero, with a "Limited Time" tag and an end date (11/22/26 at 11:59PM ET).
- The subject's 80% claim is backed by the same number in the hero, so the promise matches what the reader sees.
- Pill buttons for Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 & Under, Fan Shop and All Sale let shoppers go straight to their category.
- The category blocks (golf, drinkware, fitness, team sports) are cleanly laid out, and the photos are real and render without broken images.

## What's weak

- The top-line number, 80%, appears only in the hero, while the blocks beneath it say 50%, 40% and 50%. The rest of the email makes the 80% look like an outlier.
- The "Early Black Friday Sale" block repeats the 11/20 "Starting NOW" creative almost exactly, which adds to the fatigue.
- Around eight stacked offer blocks plus a gift-picks grid make for a long scroll with no single priority after the hero.
- The legal text is small, and "select styles" and "while quantities last" make the 80% feel like a bait-and-switch risk. Nothing shows what actually costs 80% less.
- The header has a utility strip (ScoreCard, order today, store pickup) that adds noise above the offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body to the hero plus two or three modules, or lead with the 80% and keep every other block secondary. One email should carry one story.
- 2. Show 3 to 4 real products with their marked-down prices directly under the hero. That makes the 80% credible and gives a click target other than "Shop Now".
- 3. Drop the repeated "Early Black Friday Sale" block, or fold it into one line.
- 4. Change the CTA to something like "Shop 80% Off Clearance", so the button says what you'll find.
- 5. Space out the clearance sends. The 70% emails ran on three days in a row, which is what makes the 80% feel less special.

- **Subject Alt B:** `80% off? Yes. Shoes, jackets & gear, 3 days only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Clearance prices on shoes, apparel and gear, through 11/22 at 11:59PM ET.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our deepest markdowns of the season, while sizes last.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is structurally sound (`lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` tables, MSO conditionals, responsive media queries). Confirmed defects are missing alt text on 10 visible images, a malformed JSON-LD block that is still template boilerplate, and a `_dev` asset path in production footer images. The source was truncated, and the header checks come from the relay, so several categories can't be fully verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage:** 12 of 67 links were probed and 55 skipped (footer, utility and social links were deprioritized). QA reported no failed probes, so no broken links are confirmed. The skipped links are unverified.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/v61000001a1f9c132afa278f2f4bbcf6cc0/43fa1e557608470800004c5a42963aa1` is a hidden `<img>` with `display:none; max-height:0; mso-hide:all`. It has no `alt`, `width` or `height`. Hiding it is intentional, so the alt warning on it is a false positive. Some clients that ignore inline `display:none` could render it as a stray broken image.
- **Dev asset path:** the footer icons load from `https://dsg.images.harmony.epsilon.com/_phoenix/_dev/AEM/img/footers/...` (`icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png`, `icon-location-2025.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`). The `_dev` segment suggests a non-production asset path. If that path is retired, the icons will break.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on content images (confirmed by QA):**
  - `fanshop-2026-1120-swd-11am`
  - `golf-2026-1120-swd-11am`
  - `team-2026-1120-swd-11am`
  - `fitness-2026-1120-swd-11am`
  - `50-off-banner-dks2026-1115-11`

  These look like the main content blocks, with the offer banner among them. With images blocked or a screen reader in use, the "Up to 80% OFF" offer and the category labels would be lost.
- **Missing alt on footer icons:** `icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png` and `icon-location-2025.png`. If they are decorative they should carry `alt=""`. If they sit beside link text, the missing alt matters less.
- **Malformed JSON-LD:** the `<script type="application/ld+json">` block contains `//` comments, which are invalid JSON, so parsers will reject the whole block. It also still holds unfilled template values:
  - `"image": ""`
  - `"discountCode": ""`
  - `"availabilityEnds": ""`
  - `"availabilityStarts": "2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"` (stale)
  - the placeholder comment `DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!`

  The result is that no Gmail promotion annotation will render.
- **Malformed CSS (minor):** the MSO conditional style ends with `padding:0;"}`, and the stray `"` makes the rule invalid. It affects Outlook only.
- **Image dimensions:** the Scene7 URLs request `wid=1200&hei=1600`, which may be heavy for mobile. I can't confirm the delivered file size from the source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible source. The remainder of the HTML was truncated, so this is not exhaustive.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No confirmed defects.** The three WARNs (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` not found) are flagged by QA as probable AgentMail relay capture limits. They are not evidence that the sender omitted the headers, so I'm not reporting them as failures.
- **Not verifiable:** the body unsubscribe link and the physical address fall in the truncated section. QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed and reported no failure.
- **Sender:** `DSG@e.dcsg.com` is a subdomain of the brand's `dcsg.com` domain. SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment can't be assessed without raw headers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The truncated source exposes no CTA hrefs or UTM parameters, and QA gave no landing-page results. The only observed items are the five `2026-1120` image names, which match today's date. I can't confirm that they map to the right landing pages.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the five content images, especially the 50%-off banner. Use `alt=""` on decorative footer icons.
2. Fix or remove the JSON-LD block. Delete the `//` comments, and either populate it (`image`, `discountCode`, current `availabilityStarts`/`availabilityEnds`) or drop the unused `DiscountOffer` and `PromotionCard` entries.
3. Move the footer icons off the `_phoenix/_dev/` path to a production-stable path.
4. Add `width="1" height="1" alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
5. Correct the stray `"` in the MSO `div` style rule.
6. For future audits, capture the raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) and the full untruncated HTML, then re-run the compliance, link and UTM checks.
