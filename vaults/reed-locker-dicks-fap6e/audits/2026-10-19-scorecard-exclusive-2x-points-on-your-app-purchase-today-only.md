# ScoreCard exclusive! 2X Points on your app purchase TODAY ONLY 📲
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand Mobile Monday send with one clear hero: a phone showing the DICK'S app and a big "2X Points" graphic. It's one of the few DICK'S sends this week to lead with loyalty instead of a percent-off clearance banner. After the 10/16 to 10/18 run of "Extra 25% off", "Up to 40% off shoes" and "Up to 70% off" emails, a ScoreCard angle is a welcome change of beat.
- The hero works, but everything below it dilutes it. Below the 2X block there are about ten stacked modules: 40% off shoes, $50 & Under clothing, NFL, FootJoy golf, outdoor, cardio, "This Week's Deals" at 50% off, and a credit card pitch. The "40% Off Shoes for the Family" module is the same creative as the 10/17 and 10/18 sends. That turns a focused loyalty promo into a generic weekly circular. Dense fine print closes the email, and the 2X offer has caveats (in-app only, member-only, an 11:59 PM cutoff).
- My judgement: the strongest idea this week, buried under a catalog. A good send, not a great one.

## What's working

- The hero is a phone mockup with a large "2X POINTS" graphic. The eye lands on the offer first.
- The sender name "DICK'S | Mobile Monday" tells you the cadence and context.
- "Today Only" sits above the headline, with a visible deadline in the body. The urgency is credible.
- The offer is concrete: 2X points on an app purchase.
- Category chips (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Team Sports, Halloween Shop) give quick navigation.
- The layout has no visible render bugs. Images load and spacing is consistent.

## What's weak

- The "Get Offer" button is vague. It doesn't say "Shop in the app" or "Earn 2X points", so it doesn't tell you what happens next.
- The 40% off shoes module repeats the 10/17 and 10/18 creative, so it reads like a rerun.
- There are too many competing offers: 40%, $50 & Under, 50%, 70%, $30 off golf, and the credit card $30 bonus. None of them supports the 2X message.
- The fine print is dense and long. It undercuts the "honest offer" feel, and the app-only requirement isn't clear in the hero.
- The subject runs about 64 characters, so it will truncate on mobile. "TODAY ONLY" in caps plus an emoji pushes it toward spam-adjacent.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body to the 2X offer plus 2 or 3 modules, such as top app-exclusive picks. Make every module serve earning points, not another discount.
- 2. Make the offer terms visible in the hero: "In the app only. Today until 11:59 PM." Say it plainly instead of leaving it for the fine print.
- 3. Change the CTA from "Get Offer" to "Shop in the App" or "Earn 2X Points", and link it straight to the app store or a deep link.
- 4. Drop or rotate the repeated 40% shoes module. Don't resend creative that went out 10/17 and 10/18.
- 5. Shorten the subject to under 50 characters and drop the caps.
- **Subject Alt A:** `ScoreCard: 2X points in the app today 📲`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Today only: double points on app orders`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Open the DICK'S app, check out before 11:59 PM, and your points double.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `ScoreCard members earn 2X on every app order through tonight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally sound (role="presentation" tables, a `lang="en"` attribute, a viewport meta tag, and MSO fallbacks). However, it has one hard QA failure (a 9-character plain-text part) and widespread missing `alt` attributes. The header and authentication findings could not be verified because of the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Only 12 of 70 links were probed, and none were reported broken. The other 58 were skipped (footer, utility, and social links), so their status is unverified.
- The open pixel is `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1.…&pid=…&tuid=…&configId=…`. It is `width="1" height="1"` with no `alt` and no `display:none` or `style`. It sits as a bare `<img>` directly after `<body>`, outside the wrapper table. The tracking host is a subdomain of the sender domain (`em.dcsg.com`), which is good for alignment.
- Links carry `data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"` and `class="arc-link"`. The truncated source doesn't show the `href` targets, so I can't confirm that the app deep links have web fallbacks for users without the app installed. That matters for an app-purchase offer.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA, 11 images):**
  - The open pixel and the hero (`2026-0817-hero-dks`).
  - Four content images: `clothing-2026-1019-swd-6am`, `nfl-…`, `outdoor-…`, `cardio-…`.
  - Five icons: `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`.
  - With images blocked or a screen reader, any content in these images is unavailable. The decorative ones (the pixel and likely the icons) should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Empty preheader:** `<td id="ssl">` contains only the template comment `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->`. The following `#pht-spacer` div holds only `&zwnj;&nbsp;` padding, with no preheader text. Inbox clients will therefore pull preview text from body content. The leftover placeholder comment suggests the template slot wasn't filled.
- **Plain-text fallback (QA FAIL):** the text part is 9 characters. For a multipart message that means effectively no text alternative.
- **Outlook styles to test:** the MSO-only style block sets `div {font-size:0; line-height:100%}` for all divs. If any content sits in plain `<div>`s rather than table cells, it could collapse in Word-engine Outlook. I haven't confirmed that any does, so this needs an Outlook render check.
- `<link rel="icon">` in the `<head>` has no effect in email clients and is unnecessary.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The source is truncated, so the rest is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. The QA notes say the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a verified defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both, so check the raw message headers from a direct-delivery test.
- `Authentication-Results` was also missing, so SPF, DKIM, and DMARC are unknown. Verify with a seed test that bypasses the relay.
- The QA tool always probes an unsubscribe link, but the output doesn't show the result or the footer. I can't confirm either the in-body unsubscribe link or the physical postal address from this data.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The truncated source doesn't show the `href` values, so I can't check UTM parameters or landing-page alignment. The only visible destination markers are `data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"` on the CTA anchors. The subject's "TODAY ONLY" offer ties to an app purchase, so the deep link and its fallback should be tested.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Replace the 9-character plain-text part with a real text alternative that includes the offer, links, and unsubscribe.
2. Add `alt` to all 11 flagged images. Use `alt=""` for the pixel and decorative icons, and descriptive text for the hero and category tiles.
3. Fill the preheader slot, or remove the `Replace/Paste SSL Here` placeholder comment.
4. Check the raw headers from a non-relay send for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment before treating these as defects.
5. Re-run the audit on the full HTML source so UTM parameters, the postal address, and the unsubscribe link can be checked.
6. Test the app deep links with and without the app installed, and run an Outlook (Word engine) render test for the `div` rule.
