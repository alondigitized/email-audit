# EXTRA 25% off select clearance? You got it!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-07**
## Executive summary

- This is a DICK'S clearance send that opens strong and then turns into a catalog. The hero is a red diagonal-striped panel with a giant gold "EXTRA 25% OFF". The offer lands at once, and the "Shop Now" button sits right under it. The "Limited Time" kicker and the pill nav (Men's, Women's, Kids', Fan Shop, All Sale) make the clearance angle clear.
- After the hero, the email loses focus. A second red "UP TO 50% OFF" banner for the Holiday Deal Dash appears almost straight away, so two competing offers sit in the first two screens. Below that come Stanley, fan gear, outdoor, team sports, golf, fitness, a third green "UP TO 50% OFF" banner, and a gift grid. That is about eight modules and three headline discounts. A shopper who came for clearance gets pulled into everything else.
- The subject promised "EXTRA 25% off select clearance," and the hero delivers it. But this is the same 50%-off and clearance rotation I saw on 11/6 ("🎁 EXTRA 25% off select clearance styles 🎁") and again across 11/5–11/7. That earlier clearance send used almost the same hero. Subscribers see this as the same email again. Judgement: a solid hero, a bloated body, and a cadence problem.

## What's working

- The giant gold "EXTRA 25% OFF" on red is readable at a glance, even in a thumbnail.
- A "Limited Time" kicker, a headline and a single red "Shop Now" button sit directly under the hero.
- The category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', Fan Shop, All Sale) let a shopper jump to their department.
- Most modules have short category buttons, such as Bike Deals and Cleat Deals, which are specific and clickable.
- The Stanley 1913 module and the gift picks add some seasonal relevance for holiday shoppers.

## What's weak

- Three large discount banners (25% extra, up to 50%, up to 50% again) compete, so the clearance message gets diluted.
- The body runs about eight modules deep, with each category repeating "Up to 50% off." It reads as a catalog, not a campaign.
- The "Holiday Deal Dash" banner sits right under the clearance hero. It repeats the 11/7 send and cannibalizes this one.
- The fine print is dense and small, and the "select styles" and "exclusions apply" wording is easy to miss. The clearance offer reads as vague about what is actually included.
- There is no hero product and no price. Clearance shoppers want to see actual items and deals.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the clearance story. Keep the hero, then show 6–8 real clearance products with was and now prices. Drop the Deal Dash banner and most category modules.
- 2. Move the 50%-off and category content into its own send, so the 25% clearance offer has a clear purpose.
- 3. Put the fine-print exclusions right under the hero in plain language, such as "Extra 25% off already-reduced styles."
- 4. Add a deadline that fits the offer, such as "Ends Sunday 11:59 PM PT," next to the CTA. Make the button more specific, such as "Shop clearance."
- 5. Space out the cadence. After the 11/6 clearance send, this one needs a new angle, such as a size-run or category focus.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 25% off clearance ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Clearance just got cheaper: extra 25% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shoes, apparel and gear already marked down, now 25% lower. Ends 11:59 PM PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sizes go fast. See what's left in men's, women's and kids' clearance.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally sound (`lang="en"`, `<title>`, `role="presentation"` layout tables, responsive media queries). The problems are missing alt text on 12 images, a malformed JSON-LD block with leftover template placeholders, and unverified compliance and authentication headers. The source was truncated, so the footer, links, and UTM parameters could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage is partial.** 12 material links were probed and 59 were skipped. The QA data reports no failures among the probed links, but the other 59 are unverified.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/v61000001a1b6d7855d9bcd736e96c660f0/56896cf7ce284c8900004c5a42963aa1` has no `alt` attribute and no `width`/`height`. It relies on `display:none; max-height:0` for hiding. Pixel hosts of this kind are often blocked by image-proxying and privacy clients, so open tracking will under-count.
- **Template placeholder left in:** `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` is still present after the pixel.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 12 images:**
  - **Content images (6):** `holiday-2026-1027-d-1`, `fan-shop-2026-1107-d`, `outdoor-2026-1107-d`, `sports-2026-1107-d`, `golf-2026-1107-d`, `fitness-2026-1107-d`. With images blocked, these render as blanks, and screen readers get nothing or the filename.
  - **Footer icons (5):** `icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png`, `icon-location-2025.png`. These likely need `alt=""` if decorative, or descriptive alt if not.
  - **Tracking pixel (1):** the pixel above should have `alt=""`.
- **Invalid JSON-LD:** the `<script type="application/ld+json">` block contains JavaScript `//` comments (`// GIF & WEBP images are not supported…`, `// DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!`). Comments are not valid JSON, so Gmail will reject the whole block and the promo annotation will not render.
- **Empty and stale schema fields:** `"description": ""`, `"discountCode": ""`, `"availabilityEnds": ""`, `"image": ""`. `availabilityStarts` is `2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00`, which is a stale template date.
- **Unbalanced comment:** `<!--/GMAIL PREVIEW CODE-->` closes a marker that has no visible opener.
- **Malformed Outlook CSS:** in the `mso 9` conditional block, `div {…margin:0; padding:0;"}` has a stray `"` before the closing brace. It may invalidate that rule in Outlook.
- **Small mobile text** at `max-width:480px`: `.legal` is 8px, `.body-sm` and `.link-sm` are 9px, and `.body-m` is 11px. These are below common legibility minimums.
- **Image weight:** the category images request `wid=1200&hei=1600` with `fmt=png-alpha` (for example `fan-shop-2026-1107-d`) inside a 600px container. Six large PNGs risk slow loads and Gmail clipping.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)** were not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both for senders at this volume, so it should be verified against the raw message at the origin.
- **Authentication-Results** was not found, so SPF, DKIM, and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `e.dcsg.com`, and I cannot confirm alignment from the data provided.
- **Footer content:** the physical address and unsubscribe link are in the truncated portion. QA says an unsubscribe link was included in the probe set, but its result is not reported.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. No campaign link hrefs or UTM parameters are visible in the truncated source or QA output. The `<meta name="content-page-ref">` value is opaque. The subject promises "EXTRA 25% off select clearance", and I could not verify that any probed destination matches it.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` text to the six content images. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixel and the decorative footer icons.
2. Strip the `//` comments from the JSON-LD and populate or remove the empty fields, or drop the `PromotionCard` and `DiscountOffer` objects. Update `availabilityStarts`.
3. Remove the leftover template comments (`Replace/Paste SSL Here`, `DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL`).
4. Fix the stray `"` in the MSO conditional CSS.
5. Raise the mobile minimums for `.legal`, `.body-sm`, and `.link-sm` to about 11–12px.
6. Check raw headers at the origin for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment, since the relay may be hiding them.
7. Re-run link probing with the full link set, and capture the CTA hrefs and UTM parameters to confirm the offer carries through to the landing page.
