# Just for TODAY ⚡️ Up to 50% off Flash Sale deals
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-15**
## Executive summary

- This is a one-day flash sale email with an identity problem. The hero is a bold orange-and-black "FLASH" graphic with a green "Shop Now" button. It's loud and on-brand, but the hero image never says "50% off". You only get the offer from the small "Flash Sale: Up to 50% Off" headline underneath. The hero builds urgency but carries no value.
- The email then undercuts its own premise. The second block is the same "UP TO 70% OFF" clearance creative DICK'S ran on 10/14 ("Wow! You'll want to see up to 70% OFF select clearance"). A bigger discount sits right under a "just for today" 50% offer, so the flash sale looks weaker than the evergreen clearance. After that come shoes, a hoodie, Owala bottles, cleats, golf and outdoor gear. That's six or more offers in one email, so the flash theme is lost.
- Overall it's a competent but cluttered catch-all, and it's the fourth near-identical DICK'S promo this week. It's fine for a sports shopper but not sharp enough to feel like a flash event.

## What's working

- The "FLASH" hero is high-contrast, fills the first screen, and signals urgency right away.
- Specific time-bounding sits under the hero: "One Day Only" and "valid online only through 10/15/26 11:59PM PT".
- The green "Shop Now" buttons are consistent and easy to find.
- The category pill row (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Team Sports) gives fast navigation.
- The "Check out these picks" product grid shows real items (adidas Samba, Nike socks) and ends the email with a concrete product-level path.

## What's weak

- The hero image has no discount figure. "Up to 50% Off" only appears in small text below it.
- The 70% clearance block outshouts the 50% flash offer and repeats the 10/14 creative, which confuses which deal is the real one.
- There are too many competing offers: $100 & under shoes, $14.98 hoodie, Owala, 40% cleats, $350 golf, 50% outdoor. No single hero product is linked.
- The product picks have no prices, ratings or reviews, so there's little social proof or product detail to click on.
- The CTAs are generic ("Shop Now" on every block), and the legal text is small and dense.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in the hero. Make the image "FLASH: UP TO 50% OFF · TODAY ONLY" and add a visible countdown, like the "Final Hours" timer DICK'S used in its 10/12 send.
- 2. Remove the 70% clearance block from a flash send, or move it to the bottom. Don't let a bigger standing offer outshout the time-limited one.
- 3. Cut the body to three or four tiles that are actually in the flash sale, and make sure each one is.
- 4. Use specific CTAs such as "Shop Flash Deals", "Shop Cleats" and "Shop Golf Clubs".
- 5. Add prices and star ratings to the product picks, and show the sale price next to the original.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Flash sale ends tonight: up to 50% off ⚡`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Today only: 50% off gear, shoes & more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ends 11:59PM PT. Cleats, golf clubs, hoodies and more.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `One day, hundreds of deals. Shop before they sell out.`

- Links carry `class="arc-link"` and `data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"` (e.g. `brand-header`). That looks like platform link-wrapping metadata. I can't tell from the truncated source whether the `href`s resolve to app deep links or to web fallbacks.
