# Dig into amazing Black Friday savings! 👏 100s of deals on 1000s of styles
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S eleventh or so Black Friday send in five days. It follows 11/26's "Happy Thanksgiving! Biggest Sale of the Year is served" and "Black Friday has arrived!" The hero is the same "BIGGEST SALE OF THE YEAR" banner with a holiday-ball and football theme, and the headline offer is again "Up to 50% Off." A subscriber has seen this creative repeatedly, so the novelty is gone.
- The body is better organized than the hero suggests. There is a category pill row (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Golf, Fan Shop). Below it is a bold "EXTRA 25% OFF" clearance block, and then a run of single-product modules: Nike fleece, YETI cooler, golf layers, cardio equipment, basketball hoops and fan gear. Each module has its own button, and the offers are specific. The gift-picks grid at the bottom adds visible products with prices omitted.
- Verdict: competent and on-season, but it is a long, repetitive, scroll-heavy send. The seven-plus stacked offers dilute the main message. It is fine for a sports shopper, and it will not stand out from the earlier sends in this run.

## What's working

- The hero "BIGGEST SALE OF THE YEAR" with "Up to 50% Off" is bold and sets the headline offer immediately.
- The category pill row gives a quick way into Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Golf or Fan Shop.
- The "EXTRA 25% OFF" red banner is the strongest visual break, and it creates a second, stackable offer.
- The product modules carry concrete numbers: "Up to 25% Off Nike Fleece," "25% Off YETI Tundra 35 Cooler," "Up to $1100 Off Select Cardio."
- The "Top Gifts" labels and the store hours module tie the send to the holiday.

## What's weak

- The hero image is nearly identical to the 11/24 to 11/26 sends, so it reads as a repeat.
- Two stacked offers, "Up to 50% Off" and "Extra 25% Off," compete for attention, and the fine print under each blocks a clear read on how they combine.
- The email is very long, with about ten modules. The eye has no single path, and the lower modules (basketball hoops, cardio, fan gear) are far from the top.
- The fine-print blocks under the offers are dense and small, which hurts the sense that the offer is honest.
- The gift grid at the bottom shows products without prices or deal callouts, so it adds little urgency.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero, one clearance offer and about four product modules. The current length buries the offer and invites skimming.
- 2. Refresh the hero for Black Friday day itself, for example with a countdown or "Ends tonight" framing, so it differs from the earlier sends.
- 3. Explain the discount stack in one clear line, such as "Up to 50% off, plus extra 25% off clearance," and move the legal text lower.
- 4. Add prices or "was/now" callouts to the gift grid.
- 5. Use a more specific CTA than "Shop Now," such as "Shop Nike Fleece."
