# Ends TOMORROW ⏳ Up to 50% off deals from brands you love
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- This is the same "Fall Deal Drop" creative DICK'S has run since 10/5, and the only new thing is the subject line's "Ends TOMORROW ⏳" urgency. The hero is a big "UP TO 50% OFF / FALL DEAL DROP" over a stormy sky. Below it sit "3 Days of Savings," a Shop Now button and a credible 10/7 deadline. It's a clear, honest, well-organised promo. At this size, though, the image is hard to read closely. I could only read the body copy and fine print at a coarse level.
- The body wanders after the hero. Category pills, a Scorecard $10/$20 bonus-reward block, shoes and cleats, Under Armour, Maxfli balls, pickleball, a treadmill, a second "UP TO 70% OFF" clearance banner and a product grid all compete. The 70% banner contradicts the "50% off" headline and dilutes the one-offer focus that earlier sends held. Because the 10/5 and 10/6 emails look nearly identical, a subscriber would see this as a deadline nudge on the same campaign, not a fresh send.
- Verdict: a solid reminder send with a real deadline, held back by a long body with several competing offers and repeated creative.

## What's working

- The hero is a bold "UP TO 50% OFF" headline, and the eye lands on it first.
- The "Ends TOMORROW" subject lines up with the 10/7 deadline stated in the body, so the urgency is credible.
- The green Shop Now button directly under the hero is clear and easy to find.
- The Scorecard $10/$20 bonus-reward block gives members a concrete perk.
- The category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Team Sports, Fan Shop) make it easy to jump to a category.

## What's weak

- A second banner promises "UP TO 70% OFF" markdowns, which muddies the single 50% message.
- The body has about eight competing modules (rewards, shoes, Under Armour, Maxfli, pickleball, treadmill, clearance, picks), so there is no single next step beyond the hero.
- The hero creative is unchanged from 10/5, so the only fresh element is the subject line.
- Many "Shop Now" buttons give generic CTA copy with no product-specific verbs.
- The footer is full of dense fine print, and the "valid online only, select styles" caveats weaken the sense of an honest offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rebuild the hero for the last-day moment. Add a visible countdown or "Ends 10/7" inside the hero image itself, so the urgency isn't only in the subject line.
- 2. Cut the body to three or four modules and drop the 70% clearance block, or move it to its own send.
- 3. Put a real hero product or category next to the offer, for example "Up to 50% off sneakers."
- 4. Make the CTA specific, like "Shop the last-day deals" in place of a generic "Shop Now."
- 5. Vary the creative between reminder sends so the third email in 48 hours doesn't look identical.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Score big on brands you love before the 10/7 deadline.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: DICK'S Sporting Goods, "Ends TOMORROW ⏳ Up to 50% off deals from brands you love"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML structure is sound: `lang="en"`, a `<title>`, `role="presentation"` layout tables, an MSO fallback and a hidden preheader. The confirmed problems are a missing plain-text part, 10 content images without alt text, and a tracking pixel without alt. The header-based compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because of the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Coverage gap:** only 12 of 66 links were probed and 54 were skipped, so link health is only partly verified. Footer and social links were deprioritized, and the unsubscribe link was always included.
- **Opaque click tracking:** links go through `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/mclick?params=b.v1...&pid=v1...`. The destination can't be read from the HTML, so I can't confirm where they land without following the redirects. The QA data shows no probe failures.
- **Tracking domain:** it is on the sender's own subdomain (`em.dcsg.com`), which is good for alignment. No issue found there.
- No broken links are confirmed by the QA data.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, 10 content images** (all on `s7d6.scene7.com/is/image/DSGAEMSites/`):
  - `shoes-2026-1006-d`, `ua-2026-1006-d`, `golf-2026-1006-d`, `pickleball-2026-1006-d`, `cardio-2026-1006-d`
  - `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`
  - The QA check can't tell a missing `alt` from an empty `alt=""`. If the icons are decorative, `alt=""` is correct. The 1200×1600 hero images (`wid=1200&hei=1600`) are content, and with images blocked or read by a screen reader they carry no text.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=...` has no alt text. This is low severity. Add `alt=""`.
- **Leftover template comment:** `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` is still in the production send inside `td#ssl`. It is harmless but is an authoring-template artifact.
- **Positives seen in the visible source:**
  - Outlook `OfficeDocumentSettings` and conditional table wrappers
  - `mso-hide: all` on the preheader spacer
  - Font stack falls back to `Arial,sans-serif`
  - Mobile rules under `@media screen and (max-width:480px)`
  - `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` reset
- The source was truncated, so I can't audit the rest of the body.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the supplied source. The truncated portion is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] Plain-text fallback missing or too short:** the text part is 9 characters. This is the one confirmed deliverability defect. HTML-only or near-empty text parts raise spam-filter scoring and leave text-only clients with nothing. It is worth confirming whether the relay stripped the text part, since AgentMail may only preserve part of it.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found:** the QA note says AgentMail may not capture these headers. Treat them as unverified, not as confirmed missing. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so check the raw message from the sender-side path.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status can't be assessed through the relay. It is not a confirmed defect. The `From` domain `em.dcsg.com` matches the tracking domain `data.em.dcsg.com`, which suggests alignment but doesn't prove it.
- **Footer checks:** the physical postal address and the visible unsubscribe link are in the truncated portion. The QA tool says it probed an unsubscribe link, but the result isn't shown, so I can't confirm either.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All destinations sit behind `data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/mclick` redirects, and the supplied HTML and QA data don't show the resolved URLs. The final `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` values, and whether the landing pages match the "up to 50% off" offer, are unconfirmed. I'm not flagging a problem here, because there is no evidence either way.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a real plain-text multipart alternative.** The current 9-character version is the only hard fail.
2. **Add `alt` text to the 5 hero images** (shoes, UA, golf, pickleball, cardio). Use `alt=""` on the 5 decorative icons and on the `/ee/v1/open` pixel.
3. **Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` in the raw headers.** Do this with a direct-to-mailbox seed test, not through the relay, since the relay may be dropping them.
4. **Check SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment** the same way, with a seed test or the `Authentication-Results` header at a real mailbox.
5. **Resolve the `mclick` redirects** for the primary CTAs. Confirm the final URLs return 200 and carry the expected UTM parameters.
6. **Remove the `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` comment** from the production template.
7. **Widen link probing beyond 12 of 66 links** on future runs, or at least probe the full set of hero and CTA links.
