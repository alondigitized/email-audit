#  Go, go, go! An EXTRA 25% off select clearance starts now. 🏃‍➡️ ➡️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-07**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S doing a big weekly-digest send rather than a single-focus promo — "EXTRA 25% off select clearance" up top, then seven more distinct offers stacked below it (Epic Shoe Sale, adidas apparel, drinkware, Walter Hagen golf, Quest kayaks, a "picks" grid). Compared to the tightly-focused sends in the brand history (the "$25 & UNDER" 8/5 send, or the repeated blue-pool 50%-off creative), this one trades punch for breadth. The hero itself — a red "EXTRA 25% OFF" card over a lake-dock photo — is clean and legible, and the ScoreCard points bar at the very top is a nice loyalty touch that most of the recent sends skip.
- The tradeoff is real: by offer #4 (drinkware) the email has stopped being "an extra 25% off clearance" email and become a general catalog. That dilutes the one thing the subject line promised. It's competently built and bug-free, but it's the kind of email a fatigued subscriber skims past rather than acts on — especially given how often DICK'S has hit this inbox in the past week with near-identical "up to X% off" framing.
- Judgment: solid execution, mediocre focus. Fine as a mid-week catalog blast, not a standout.

## What's working

- The hero is unambiguous — big red "EXTRA 25% OFF" block, "Starts Now" tag, and a single green "SHOP NOW" button with no competing CTA in the fold.
- ScoreCard loyalty balance ("300 Points") is surfaced right at the top, which none of the recent brand-history sends called out.
- Clean render throughout — no broken images, no overlapping text, no lorem-ipsum, consistent green CTA styling across every section.
- Good category navigation bar (MEN'S / WOMEN'S / KIDS' / $25 AND UNDER / FAN SHOP / ALL SALE) gives an escape hatch if the hero offer isn't relevant.
- Fine print for the hero offer is visible and legible right under the headline, not hidden in 6pt footer text.

## What's weak

- Eight distinct offers in one email (clearance, shoes, adidas apparel x2, drinkware, golf, kayaks, plus a 6-item product grid) buries the subject line's single promise under a pile of unrelated deals.
- The hero links to "select clearance" broadly, not a specific product or category — nothing to click toward beyond a generic landing page.
- No social proof or reviews anywhere in the email despite multiple product callouts (shoes, apparel, drinkware, golf gear).
- Per the brand history, this is yet another "up to X% off" framing from DICK'S in the same week — cadence fatigue is a real risk here.
- "SHOP NOW" is repeated verbatim on every single section (7+ times) — no differentiated CTA copy to match each offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the top 2-3 offers (clearance + shoe sale + one apparel deal) and move drinkware/golf/kayaks to a follow-up send — focus beats volume, and it'll match the higher-scoring single-offer sends in this brand's own history.
- 2. Link the hero "SHOP NOW" to the clearance category page directly, not a generic landing spot, and differentiate at least the top 2 CTAs (e.g., "Shop Clearance" vs. "Shop Shoes") instead of repeating "SHOP NOW" seven times.
- 3. Add a one-line social proof/rating snippet near the top offer (e.g., star rating on a featured clearance item) — nothing in this render backs up the offer with trust signals.
- 4. Tighten the subject line — it's long and "Go, go, go!" reads as filler ahead of the actual offer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `EXTRA 25% off clearance — starts now ⏱️`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your clearance cart just got 25% cheaper`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus up to 30% off shoes and cleats from Nike, adidas, HOKA`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Extra 25% off ends 8/8 — shop clearance before it's gone`

# Technical Email Audit — DICK'S Sporting Goods "Extra 25% Off Clearance"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard Epsilon/Harmony-hosted DSG template with functional mobile-responsive CSS, but ships with an invalid/incomplete JSON-LD block and a broken unsubscribe redirect that fails CAN-SPAM's functional-mechanism requirement.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All primary links route through the Epsilon click-tracking domain `app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/v610000019fdbc04fa68a57a3f4bbcfb648/...?__dU__=...` (e.g., header logo link). This is standard ESP redirect tracking and resolves normally.
- **Broken redirect**: The unsubscribe tracking link (`https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/U/v610000019fdbc04fa68a57a3f4bbcfb648/67c057104a6e48f90000021ef3a0bcfe`) resolves to an error page: `unknown url type: '/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription'`. The link text is "Unsubscribe," meaning the redirect target (`/pc/subscription`) is misconfigured or deprecated on the destination site.
- QA probing covered 12 of 61 material links (49 skipped per click-likelihood ranking); no other broken links were reported among probed links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 10 images are missing `alt` attributes, including customer-facing content images (not just tracking pixels): `tertiary-base-01.jpg`, `tertiary-base-02.jpg`, `banner4.gif`, `two-images-1.png`, `secondary-base-03.jpg`, plus four footer icons (`icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png`, `icon-location-2025.png`). This degrades screen-reader accessibility and image-blocked rendering (Outlook/older clients default to blocking remote images).
- One flagged image is the open-tracking pixel (`67c057104a6e48f900004c5a42963aa1`), which is set `display:none` — missing alt is not a practical accessibility issue here, but is still worth excluding from future flags since it's non-content.
- Responsive CSS (`@media screen and (max-width:480px)`) is present and scoped correctly with mobile-specific classes (`.mobile-off`, `.header-xl`, etc.), plus MSO/Outlook conditional comments for table rendering — no structural rendering issues found in the provided source.
- The template's `<script type="application/ld+json">` block contains JavaScript-style `//` comments (e.g., `// GIF & WEBP images are not supported...`, `// DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!`). This is **invalid JSON** — comments are not permitted in JSON-LD, so any crawler or client attempting to parse this structured data block will fail silently or throw a parse error.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The JSON-LD `DiscountOffer` object has empty `"description"` and `"discountCode"` fields, and `"availabilityEnds": ""` — these read as unfilled template placeholders never populated by the sender's merge process.
- `"availabilityStarts": "2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"` is a stale leftover date (six years prior to this send), indicating this JSON-LD block is boilerplate that was not updated for the current campaign.
- `"PromotionCard"` object has `"image": ""`, consistent with an unfilled designer placeholder (comment directly above it reads "DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!").
- No visible `{{merge_tag}}`-style broken tokens found in the body HTML provided.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe mechanism is broken**: the Unsubscribe link redirects to an error page (`aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription`) rather than a functioning opt-out flow — this is a CAN-SPAM compliance risk since the required functional return-address/unsubscribe mechanism is not operative.
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not present in the captured headers (may be a relay-capture limitation rather than sender omission — noted as WARN, not confirmed absent at origin).
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058, one-click unsubscribe) not present — same caveat as above.
- `Authentication-Results` header not present, so SPF/DKIM pass/fail status cannot be confirmed from this capture.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Outbound links use Epsilon redirect URLs (`app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...?__dU__=...`) rather than direct UTM-tagged destination URLs, so UTM parameter presence/alignment cannot be verified from the truncated source provided.
- Cannot confirm landing-page alignment (e.g., whether the offer link lands on a matching clearance/25%-off collection page) without the resolved destination URL or the untruncated HTML — flagging as unverified rather than a confirmed issue.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the unsubscribe redirect target so `/pc/subscription` (or its replacement path) resolves correctly — this is the highest-priority item given CAN-SPAM implications.
2. Remove `//` comments from the `application/ld+json` block or populate it via proper JSON serialization — the current markup is invalid JSON and will fail structured-data parsing.
3. Populate or remove the empty `DiscountOffer`/`PromotionCard` JSON-LD fields (`description`, `discountCode`, `availabilityStarts`, `availabilityEnds`, `image`) so stale 2020 boilerplate isn't shipped in production sends.
4. Add descriptive `alt` text to the five content images and four footer icons listed in Section 3.
5. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually sent at origin (vs. lost in relay capture) and confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status via a direct header capture.
