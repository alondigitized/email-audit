# << Get clearance deals under $25 >>
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-03**
## Executive summary

- DICK'S opens strong and immediately breaks its own promise. The hero is a genuinely bold, single-focus "$25 AND UNDER" graphic that matches the subject line word-for-word — a real improvement over the mismatched sends in this brand's recent history. But that's where the "under $25" story ends: everything below the first "SHOP NOW" button — Epic Shoe Sale (up to 30% off), Nike underwear, drinkware (up to 40% off), pro baseball gloves (up to 45% off), Top Flite golf sets (up to $100 off), Peloton shoes (20% off) — is unrelated full-category clearance with no price ceiling in sight. This is the same kitchen-sink cascade DICK'S has run all week, just with a sharper hero.
- The subject promises a narrow, high-intent deal (cheap stuff, under $25); the body delivers the brand's standard everything-must-go inventory dump. A subscriber who opens for bargain-bin items scrolls past six unrelated categories before hitting a "must-have gear" grid that includes a $130 Hydro Flask tumbler — nowhere near $25.
- Verdict: good hero craft, dishonest follow-through. This is a top-of-funnel bait email dressed as a targeted deal alert.

## What's working

- The hero graphic and headline execute the subject line's promise almost perfectly — "$25 AND UNDER" in giant white type over a bold red background is unmissable and un-ambiguous.
- Clean render throughout — no overlapping text, no broken images, no lorem ipsum across a very long (6700px) scroll.
- ScoreCard loyalty bar ("300 Points = $10 Reward," "Shop to Earn Points") is placed prominently right under the logo, which rewards subscribed members.
- Clear green "SHOP NOW" CTAs repeat consistently after every section, so the reader is never lost on how to act.
- Category nav bar (Men's / Women's / Kid's / Shoes / All Clearance / All Sale) gives an escape hatch for browsers who don't want to scroll the whole cascade.

## What's weak

- Classic bait-and-switch: the entire value proposition of the subject and hero ("under $25") disappears after one CTA — every subsequent section (shoes up to 30% off, drinkware up to 40% off, gloves up to 45% off, $100 off golf sets) has no connection to a $25 price ceiling.
- No urgency or deadline attached to the headline offer itself — "Valid through 8/10 @ 11:59PM ET" only shows up buried in the Epic Shoe Sale block, not on the $25-and-under hero that the whole email is supposedly about.
- Same repetitive-cadence problem flagged across the last 5 days of brand history (7/30–8/2) — this is at minimum the 8th clearance/sale send in a week, and the format (hero + endless category cascade) is unchanged.
- The "Must-have gear for an epic summer!" product grid near the footer includes a Hydrojug tumbler and Jordan cleats that read nowhere near "$25 and under," undermining the hero's specific promise even further.
- Primary CTA copy ("SHOP NOW") is generic and repeated verbatim after every section — no section-specific CTA copy to differentiate what's actually being clicked into.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Make the body match the hero.** If the send is "clearance under $25," show only sub-$25 SKUs (with visible prices) below the hero — drop the shoe/golf/Peloton/drinkware blocks into a separate send. This is the single highest-leverage fix; right now the email is one honest section and six bait sections.
- 2. **Add a real deadline to the hero itself** — a countdown or "Ends Sunday" stamp on the $25-and-under banner would convert the browsing behavior this subject is designed to trigger.
- 3. **Show 3-4 actual under-$25 products with prices directly beneath the hero** instead of a single generic "Shop Now" — proof of the promise drives the click.
- 4. **Vary CTA copy per section** ("Shop Shoe Deals," "Shop Drinkware") instead of repeating "SHOP NOW" nine times.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Under $25: clearance finds worth grabbing today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Everything in this email is $25 or less`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shoes, drinkware, and gear — all under $25, while it lasts.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New under-$25 clearance just dropped. Shop before it's gone.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: DICK'S Sporting Goods — "Get clearance deals under $25"

## 1. Technical Summary
Email uses an Epsilon Harmony ESP template with redirect-wrapped tracking links; automated QA found a broken unsubscribe redirect, missing unsubscribe headers, and 11 images without alt text, yielding a 43% pass rate.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken unsubscribe link (FAIL):** The "Unsubscribe" link (`https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/U/v610000019fc672840690c8e2969125ccc4/d67c8c8a2bb943160000021ef3a0bd02`) resolves to an error page — `unknown url type: '/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription'`. This is a functional failure, not a tracking-only issue; see Compliance below.
- All observed links (logo, header CTA, SSL/preheader beacon) route through Epsilon Harmony's redirect wrapper (`app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...` and `/O/...` tracking-pixel paths) with an opaque `__dU__` token param rather than plain destination URLs. This obscures final destinations and any UTM parameters from static source inspection.
- QA probed 12 of 68 total links (material/high-click-likelihood links); 56 footer/utility/social links were deprioritized and not verified individually — no per-link errors reported for the probed set besides unsubscribe.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **11 images missing `alt` text**, including content-bearing product images (`undies.jpg`, `tempo.jpg`, `gloves.jpg`, `golf.jpg`, `peloton.jpg`, `banner4.gif`), a tracking pixel (`d67c8c8a...`), and footer icons (SMS, BOPIS, favorite-store, location). Screen reader users get no fallback text for any of these.
- Custom webfont (`Archivo`, via Google Fonts CDN `fonts.gstatic.com`) is loaded only inside a `@media screen` block with `font-display:swap` and falls back to `Arial, sans-serif` — acceptable fallback behavior, but the font request depends on an external Google Fonts host being reachable inside the mail client, which not all clients honor (Outlook/MSO will use the fallback stack per the conditional-comment `mso` styles).
- Hidden preheader text ("Get $20 off $100+ when you sign up for Text Alerts**") is implemented via a `display:none; mso-hide:all` cell containing a 0-height tracking image — standard preheader pattern, renders correctly across major clients.
- `<script type="application/ld+json">` block contains JavaScript-style `//` comments inside the JSON array (e.g., `// GIF & WEBP images are not supported...`), which makes the block **invalid JSON**. Mail clients ignore this script tag entirely, but if this markup is ever reused on a web landing page or parsed by a schema validator, it will fail to parse.
- Structured data (`DiscountOffer`, `PromotionCard`) ships with empty required fields (`discountCode: ""`, `availabilityEnds: ""`, `image: ""`), indicating template placeholders were not filled in before send — no functional rendering impact in-client, but represents dead/unused markup.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, AMPscript, Liquid, or Handlebars-style placeholders) are present in the provided HTML source. No issues found in the visible markup, though the source is truncated and later sections (not included) could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe mechanism is broken** — the unsubscribe link resolves to an error page (`/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription`) instead of a subscription-management page. This is a CAN-SPAM concern: the visible unsubscribe link does not deliver on its function.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** — either omitted from the source message or stripped before reaching the AgentMail relay; cannot confirm compliance with bulk-sender one-click requirements.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found** — RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe (required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender rules) cannot be confirmed as supported.
- **`Authentication-Results` header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown from this capture; cannot confirm domain alignment for `e.dcsg.com`.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Cannot verify UTM parameter alignment or landing-page continuity: all material links are wrapped in Epsilon Harmony redirect URLs (`app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/.../O/...`) with an opaque `__dU__` token in place of plain query-string UTMs. Actual destination URLs and UTM values are only resolvable by following the redirect, which is outside the scope of the static HTML/QA data provided.
- The one destination that *is* directly observable — the unsubscribe redirect — fails to land on a working page (see Section 2/5), confirming at least one broken email-to-site link in this send.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the unsubscribe redirect immediately** — `/pc/subscription` target is erroring; this is the highest-priority item (functional + compliance risk).
2. Add `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers to the send (or confirm the ESP is generating them and investigate why AgentMail isn't capturing them) to meet Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender requirements.
3. Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC are passing for `e.dcsg.com`/`dcsg.com` — surface `Authentication-Results` in the relayed headers so this can be verified per-send.
4. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 11 flagged images, particularly the product shots (`undies.jpg`, `tempo.jpg`, `gloves.jpg`, `golf.jpg`, `peloton.jpg`) which currently convey offer content with no text fallback.
5. Remove the invalid `//` comments from the `application/ld+json` block (or delete the block if unused) and either populate or strip the empty `DiscountOffer`/`PromotionCard` placeholder fields.
6. If UTM/landing-page auditing is required going forward, resolve the Epsilon Harmony redirect chain (`/T/`, `/O/`, `/U/` paths) to capture final destination URLs rather than auditing the wrapper links alone.
