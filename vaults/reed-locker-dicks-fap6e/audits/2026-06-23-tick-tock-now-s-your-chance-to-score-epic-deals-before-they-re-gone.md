# Tick, tock! ⏰ Now's your chance to score EPIC deals before they're gone. ➡️
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-23**
## Executive summary

- This is a second DICK'S email on June 23, functioning as the urgency-reminder counterpart to the "Biggest Sale of Summer is ON!" launch send from earlier that morning (audited at 6/10). The Deal Days visual system — green branding, "TODAY ONLY!" product tiles, category nav — carries over cleanly and keeps the email from feeling random. The structure is ambitious: hero, three "1-Day Deal" product drops, a Nike/DSG savings section, a BOGO socks offer, and a full Summer Shop module. That's five distinct content blocks in one scroll.
- The problem is the subject line completely fails to represent any of that specificity. "Tick, tock! ⏰ Now's your chance to score EPIC deals before they're gone. ➡️" is 75+ characters, uses all-caps "EPIC," and never mentions a single offer or category. Subscribers who've received six DICK'S emails since June 21 have already been told deals are live — this subject reads like a generic nudge, not a reason to open today.
- Inside the email, the BOGO 50% OFF DSG Socks is the most concrete offer and the one most likely to drive a tap. But it's buried past the mid-point. The cadence is becoming a liability: this is the seventh DICK'S email in three days, and the sense of urgency is starting to erode.

## What's working

- "TODAY ONLY!" labels on individual product tiles create in-body urgency that the subject line fails to deliver.
- The BOGO 50% OFF DSG Socks offer is specific, honest, and immediately scannable — the best single offer in the email.
- Green Deal Days branding stays consistent with the June 21–23 campaign, giving this wave of sends a unified visual identity rather than looking like another one-off clearance blast.
- Category nav (YOUTH, RUNNING, GOLF, SHOES) lets subscribers self-select into their focus area quickly without hunting.
- The Summer Shop module at the bottom adds genuine seasonal breadth beyond pure sale language.

## What's weak

- Subject line is ~75 chars — well over the mobile-friendly ceiling — and "EPIC deals" is among the most overused phrases in retail email.
- No ScoreCard / loyalty mention visible anywhere, despite the June 21 ScoreCard email landing at 8/10 and clearly resonating with members.
- The email is extremely long with five distinct content blocks; no single hero offer "wins," so eye-tracking diffuses across everything.
- Cadence fatigue: seven DICK'S emails in three days means this send is fighting subscriber exhaustion before the subject line even renders.
- The hero CTA is a plain "SHOP NOW" — no category or offer specificity to pull the click.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Rewrite the subject to name the best offer.** BOGO 50% on socks or a specific "THREE deals, TODAY ONLY" hook would immediately outperform "EPIC deals."
- 2. **Consolidate to 2 content blocks maximum.** A daily deals email should feel like a flash — hero deal + one supporting offer, not a department store scroll.
- 3. **Surface ScoreCard perks here.** The June 21 "last chance" email proved that member-specific framing drives measurable engagement; skipping it in this send is a missed lever.
- 4. **Make the primary CTA specific.** "Shop TODAY's Deals" or "Grab BOGO Socks" outperforms the generic "SHOP NOW" even within the same button design.
- 5. **Pull back cadence or batch.** Merging this send with the June 23 morning launch email (as a single, richer send) would have preserved urgency without the fatigue penalty.
- **Subject Alt A:** `TODAY ONLY: BOGO 50% off DSG Socks + 3 big Deal Days drops`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Deal Days drop: 3 deals expire tonight ⏰ Don't miss BOGO Socks`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Three 1-Day Deals end at midnight — grab BOGO Socks before they sell out.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Today's best: BOGO 50% off socks, Nike Club Fleece, and summer gear.`

### 5. Compliance

**Invalid JSON in `<script type="application/ld+json">`** — The block contains JavaScript-style single-line comments (`//`) which are **not valid JSON**. JSON parsers (including Gmail's schema processor) will reject the entire block, silently dropping all promotional annotations.

```js
// GIF & WEBP images are not supported...
// DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!
```

These must be removed. JSON has no comment syntax.

**CAN-SPAM / unsubscribe / postal address** — Cannot confirm from truncated source. These elements must be present in the footer:
- One-click unsubscribe link
- Physical mailing address of sender
- Clear "from" identity (present: `DICK'S Deal Days / DSG@e.dcsg.com`)

**Authentication headers** — Not derivable from HTML source. Sending domain is `e.dcsg.com` (Epsilon subdomain). SPF, DKIM, and DMARC alignment for this subdomain should be verified in actual message headers.
