# Want up to 50% off? Explore this week's deals 💰
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-10**
## Executive summary

- This is the same pampas-grass, red-frame "UP TO 50% OFF" hero DICK'S already ran on 9/8 ("📫 Delivered: Up to 50% off this week's DEALS"), just reshuffled behind a new subject line. Below the fold it's the standard DICK'S kitchen-sink template — men's/women's/kids' category tiles, drinkware, team sports gear, a second "UP TO 60% OFF SELECT CLEARANCE" red banner, and a "check out these picks" product grid — all of which have appeared near-verbatim across the last week and a half of sends.
- The render itself is clean: no broken images, no overlapping text, good category organization. But there's nothing in this email that differentiates it from Tuesday's, Monday's, or last Wednesday's — same hero art, same offer tier, same layout skeleton. A subscriber who's opened even two of the last five DICK'S emails has already seen this exact page.
- Judgement: technically solid, creatively exhausted. This is a cadence problem more than an execution problem — the email works, but it's the same email again.

## What's working

- Clean, bug-free render — no layout breaks, no image failures, no lorem-ipsum.
- Clear top-of-email quick nav (Men's/Women's/Kids'/Shoes/Fan Shop/Team Sports) gives fast wayfinding.
- Category sections each pair a lifestyle shot with a specific discount line and a "Shop Now" CTA — easy to scan.
- Second clearance banner ("UP TO 60% OFF") gives a distinct offer tier beyond the top hero, adding some depth.
- "Order Today, Spend It Tomorrow" same-day pickup line at the very top is a genuinely differentiated, concrete value prop.

## What's weak

- Hero creative (pampas grass + red frame, "UP TO 50% OFF") is a repeat of the 9/8 send — zero visual novelty for anyone who opened that email.
- Subject promises "this week's deals" but the offer (up to 50% off) and hero art are identical to two days ago — nothing signals a fresh week.
- Extremely long, undifferentiated scroll — six+ full sections before reaching "check out these picks," with no single clear hero moment beyond the top banner.
- Generic top-of-funnel CTAs ("Shop Now" repeated 6+ times) with no specific product or price called out until deep in the email.
- No loyalty/ScoreCard member-specific pricing or benefit shown despite ScoreCard branding in the header.

## Recommendations

- 1. Retire the pampas-grass hero — it's now appeared in two sends within 48 hours (9/8 and 9/10); swap in unused creative or a genuinely new product hero to avoid subscriber fatigue.
- 2. Cut the email length by half — consolidate the men's/women's/kids' blocks into one grid module instead of three stacked full-width sections.
- 3. Lead with the 60%-off clearance banner instead of burying it two-thirds down — it's the stronger offer and gets diluted by list position.
- 4. Add one loyalty-specific line (e.g., "ScoreCard members get an extra 10%") to justify the ScoreCard logo real estate at the top.
- 5. Vary CTA copy — "Shop Now" six times in one email reads as filler; try "Shop Men's," "Shop Kids'," etc.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Marketing email from DICK'S Sporting Goods with functional Salesforce/Epsilon Harmony redirect tracking, but missing plain-text alternative and unsubscribe-related headers were not captured by the audit tooling.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All CTAs route through `data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/mclick` and `/ee/v1/click` redirect tracking (Epsilon Harmony ESP), with `params`, `pid`, `tuid`, and `configId` tokens present and populated — no empty/placeholder tokens observed.
- Open tracking pixel present: `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=...` (renders as an `<img>`, flagged by QA as "Image missing alt text" — expected/benign for a tracking pixel, not a real accessibility issue).
- QA probe coverage: 12 of 68 total links were probed; 56 deprioritized (footer/utility/social). No broken or malformed redirect URLs found among probed links.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 10 content/creative images missing `alt` text: `mens-2026-0910-d`, `womens-2026-0910-d`, `kids-2026-0910-d`, `sports-2026-0910-d`, `banner4.gif`, `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`. These are informational/CTA-adjacent images (category tiles, value-prop icons) and should carry descriptive `alt` attributes for screen readers and image-blocked clients.
- MSO/Outlook conditional comments and `@font-face` (Archivo, web-safe fallback to Arial) are correctly implemented with `mso-*` fallback table wrapper.
- Responsive `@media screen and (max-width:480px)` block present with mobile-specific classes (`.mobile-off`, `.mobile-w100`, `.mobile-cta`, etc.) — standard responsive email pattern, no issues found.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge/personalization tokens (e.g., `%%FIRSTNAME%%`, AMPscript `%%=...=%%`) present in the visible/truncated source. No issues found.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header: not found by QA — may be a relay-capture limitation (AgentMail) rather than a true absence; cannot confirm from HTML source alone since unsubscribe headers are SMTP-level, not HTML.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058, one-click unsubscribe): not found — same caveat.
- `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC): not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status unknown from this data.
- Footer unsubscribe link: QA confirms an unsubscribe link is always included in probe scope, but it was not among the 12 probed links shown, so its destination URL was not evidenced in the provided data — cannot verify functionality directly.
**Flag:** These are gaps in what could be *captured/verified*, not confirmed compliance violations — recommend re-checking against raw SMTP headers outside the relay.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Links use Epsilon's proprietary `mclick`/`click` redirect+token scheme (`params`, `pid`, `tuid`, `configId`) rather than standard UTM query parameters — final destination URLs and any UTM tagging occur after redirect resolution, which is not visible in the truncated source.
- Cannot confirm landing-page alignment (destination URL, query params) since redirect targets are opaque tokens; no destination URLs were provided in the truncated HTML/QA data.

7. Recommendations
- Add descriptive `alt` text to the 10 flagged content/icon images (category tiles: "Men's deals", "Women's deals", "Kids' deals", "Sports deals"; icons: SMS signup, ScoreCard/loyalty, "Buy Now Pay Later", favorites, store locator).
- Provide a proper plain-text MIME part — current plain-text fallback is 9 characters, effectively empty, which hurts deliverability/spam scoring and violates CAN-SPAM's implicit requirement for a readable non-HTML version.
- Verify `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) directly against raw SMTP headers (bypassing the AgentMail relay) to rule out a capture artifact vs. an actual compliance/deliverability gap.
- Resolve and spot-check a sample of the `mclick`/`click` redirect targets to confirm they land on live, matching product/category pages (cannot be verified from the data provided here).
