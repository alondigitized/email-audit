# 🎁 EXTRA 25% off select clearance styles 🎁
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- This is a DICK'S clearance send with a big hero and a long, scattered body. A red argyle panel with a gold "EXTRA 25% OFF" fills the top of the email, and the eye lands on it first. A "Limited Time" kicker, a headline and a single green Shop Now button sit right under it. The offer is easy to read.
- The send is also stacked on top of the 11/3 to 11/5 run of "up to 70% off select clearance" and "up to 50% off" emails. "Extra 25%" reads as a sweetener on top of those, which is a mildly new angle. Below the hero, though, the email turns into a catalog: running shoes, fleece, NCAA, golf balls, bikes, basketball hoops and a gift grid. Most of it has nothing to do with clearance. The hero promises clearance, and the body mostly sells full-price and gifting categories.
- Verdict: the hero is strong and the "extra" framing is the best hook of the week. The rest dilutes it, and the sixth near-identical promo in four days is working against it.

## What's working

- The hero is huge and high-contrast, so "EXTRA 25% OFF" is unmissable.
- A "Limited Time" kicker and a Shop Now button sit right under the hero. The fine print gives a deadline of 11/9 at 11:59 PM PT.
- The category pill row (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 & Under, Fan Shop, All Sale) gives a quick way into the sale.
- The Brooks Adrenaline GTS 22 tile shows a concrete price ($124.98), and the Bridgestone tile has a specific 2-for-$94.99 deal.
- The green and holiday styling matches the season.

## What's weak

- The "Extra 25%" offer is not tied to any product in the body. None of the tiles say which items are clearance, so the hero's promise is never delivered.
- The email is very long, with 8 or more modules, and the offers compete with each other: $50 and under fleece, 40% off NCAA, 50% off bikes and hoops.
- Every module carries the same small green Shop Now button, so no single CTA stands out after the hero.
- The fine print under the hero is dense and exclusion-heavy ("Exclusions apply", "Interim markdowns may have been taken"). That weakens how honest the offer feels.
- It is another promo in the same week as the 11/4 and 11/5 clearance sends. The cadence looks repetitive.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fill the body with clearance items that actually carry the extra 25%. Show was/now pricing on 3 to 4 products, and cut the bikes, hoops and gift grid from this send.
- 2. Add a "price after extra 25%" callout on the product tiles, so the offer reads as concrete savings.
- 3. Shorten the fine print and move the exclusions behind a "Details" link. State the deadline in plain text in the hero, for example "Ends Sunday."
- 4. Differentiate the CTAs: use "Shop Clearance" in the hero, and category-specific verbs such as "Shop Running Shoes" below.
- 5. Space the sends out, or make each one distinct, so the offer escalates instead of repeating.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (12 images).** The affected images are:
  - Seven promo images: `1106-stw-6am-brooks-running-shoes`, `-50-nike-fleece`, `-40-ncaa`, `-e6-golf`, `-nishiki`, `-50-hoops`.

  QA reports these as missing `alt`. If these tiles carry the offer text only as image content, the offers are invisible with images blocked or in a screen reader. Decorative icons should use `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Image weight.** Promo images are requested as `fmt=jpeg&qlt=100&wid=1200&hei=1600`. That is quality 100 at 1200x1600 for a 600px container, which is oversized for mobile loads.
- **Preheader is empty.** `<div id="pht-spacer">` contains only `‌&nbsp;` filler. The `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` comment is unreplaced, so inboxes will pull preview text from body content instead.

- **Web font.** Archivo is loaded from fonts.gstatic.com inside `@media screen`, with an `Arial, sans-serif` fallback. This is handled correctly.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The per-recipient `tuid` in the open pixel is populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is 9 characters.** There is effectively no text/plain alternative. This hurts deliverability, since multipart-with-empty-text is a spam signal, and it breaks accessibility for text-only clients.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip these, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers for senders over 5,000 messages a day, so verify on the raw original.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown because of the relay. The sender is `info@em.dcsg.com`, so check alignment of the From domain against the DKIM `d=` and Return-Path domains.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and physical address are in the truncated footer, so they could not be verified. QA says an unsubscribe link was always included in probing, but it reported no result for it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable from the data provided. The truncated source and the QA output don't show UTM parameters or the landing-page results for the 12 probed links. I'm not flagging a problem, but continuity is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a real text/plain part with offers, key links, and the unsubscribe URL. This fixes the one QA failure.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the six promo tiles, including the offer text (e.g. "Extra 25% off Brooks running shoes"). Use `alt=""` on the decorative icons and the tracking pixel.
