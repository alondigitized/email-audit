# Homepage snapshot · www.dickssportinggoods.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- I pulled up Dick's on my phone and got a wall. No homepage, no hero, no deals — just a generic error page with a logo and "Oops, Something Went Wrong." That's it. The whole screen is a white error card floating on a gray void, with a raw error token and an IP address printed at the bottom like I'm a developer reading logs. This isn't a brand experience — it's a technical failure with a mask on.

## What's working

- The Dick's Sporting Goods logo renders cleanly and is recognizable — at least I know I'm in the right place.
- The error message is readable, with decent font sizing and decent contrast.

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage:** zero content, zero navigation, zero offers. A dad on the sideline with 10 minutes to grab cleat replacements just hit a dead end.
- **"Please try accessing the site again after 12 hours"** — 12 hours? That's a Thursday evening turned into a Friday morning. No workaround link, no store locator fallback, nothing.
- **Raw error token exposed** (`Error: 0.23a7cb17.1778095222.45a46420 IP: 47.41.15.19`) — that's debug output dumped straight onto a customer-facing screen. Feels unfinished and unpolished, not a brand I trust with my credit card.
- **No fallback content** — no static "browse our app" redirect, no link to store finder, no cached navigation. Total content blackout.

## Recommendations

- **Add a meaningful fallback page** — a static HTML page with top categories, store-finder link, and ScoreCard login that serves even when the main stack is down.
- **Replace the 12-hour message with a real-time status link** — something like "Check dickssportinggoods.com/status for updates" is far better than asking me to wait half a day.
- **Strip the raw error token and IP address from customer-facing output** — log it server-side, don't print it on my phone screen.
- **Surface the app download CTA on the error page** — if the web is down, send me to the app where I can still browse and buy.
