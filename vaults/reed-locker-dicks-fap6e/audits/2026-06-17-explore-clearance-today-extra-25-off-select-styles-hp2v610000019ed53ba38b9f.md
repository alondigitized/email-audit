# Explore clearance today! Extra 25% off select styles 👏
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-17**
## Executive summary

- This is the second clearance email from the DICK'S `Clearance` sub-account in roughly 24 hours — June 16 sent the same dark-red "EXTRA 25% OFF" hero, and this June 17 send is a near-carbon copy of it. The hero is bold and communicates value instantly: big white type on a red field with a green CTA lands immediately. But the creative fatigue is real. Anyone who opened yesterday's clearance send is looking at an almost identical email, and the Clearance sub-account has now appeared at least twice in the same inbox week that already includes Flash Sales, a ScoreCard exclusive, and a championship reactive send.
- The module structure is competent — Running Shoes, Men's Tops, Women's, Golf, and a hard-priced Franklin badminton set break up the % grids with a specific dollar anchor. The Father's Day urgency hook is timely (four days out), and the "Get It by Father's Day!" module pushing in-store pickup is smart logistics thinking. Nothing is broken, nothing is confusing.
- The judgment: functional, honest, and well-executed in isolation — but its biggest liability is the one you cannot see inside the email itself. It's the wrong email to send twice.

## What's working

- **Hero immediacy.** "EXTRA 25% OFF" on a saturated red field is the single clearest discount signal in DICK'S recent history — the eye lands there in under a second.
- **Discount laddering.** 25% off clearance at the hero, 40% off running shoes, 70% off men's apparel, and a $29.99 hard price on Franklin paddles gives the reader a reason to scroll.
- **Father's Day logistics module.** The "Get It by Father's Day!" BOPIS push with a store-front image is the right in-store play four days out — addresses the "too late to ship?" objection without saying it out loud.
- **Category nav tiles.** The Men's / Women's / Kids / Ball Sports / Golf shortcut row is visible and scannable — it lets someone self-sort without reading the whole email.

## What's weak

- **Near-duplicate creative from 24 hours ago.** The hero, color palette, and "Extra 25% Off Select Clearance" framing are essentially identical to the June 16 clearance send (8/10). A second hit this fast trains subscribers to skim or batch-delete.
- **"Explore" is the wrong anchor verb.** "Explore clearance today!" signals browsing, not buying. The discount is real; the verb should be too — "Score," "Save," "Grab."
- **"SHOP NOW" CTAs are generic.** Every module uses the same button copy. "Shop Running Shoes" or "Shop Men's Clearance" would sharpen the click intent and reduce the feeling that all roads lead to a homepage.
- **Subject line is too long.** 55 characters including the emoji exceeds the ~50-char mobile cutoff — the "👏" likely clips or disappears on many Android clients.
- **"Select" double-qualifier dilutes the offer.** "Extra 25% off **select** styles" on top of an already-discounted clearance rack is two layers of qualification before the product. The headline works harder than the fine print deserves.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Differentiate from June 16.** Swap the hero copy from a repeat "25% Off" umbrella to a single category story — "70% Off Men's. Today Only." — so subscribers get a new reason to open vs. a refreshed version of yesterday's email.
- 2. **Change "Explore" to an action verb.** "Score Extra 25% Off Clearance Today" in the subject is one word shorter and signals intent.
- 3. **Module-specific CTA copy.** Replace all "SHOP NOW" buttons with category labels: "Shop Running Shoes," "Shop Men's Apparel," "Shop Golf Shoes." Tiny lift to implement, measurable lift in CTR.
- 4. **Add a time deadline to the clearance hero.** "Ends Sunday" or a short countdown banner would give the 25% off real urgency instead of borrowing it from the Father's Day footer.
- 5. **Hard-price more items.** The $29.99 Franklin paddles feel trustworthy because they're concrete. One more dollar-off or dollar-price callout per module (e.g., "Men's tops from $12") would accelerate clicks.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Running shoes, men's tops, golf shoes — all on sale now`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Father's Day is Sunday. Clearance ships fast.`
