# 📢 Game on, Black Friday... score up to 50% OFF early & SAVE BIG! 💰 🙌
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the third "Black Friday has started" push in three days from DICK'S. The "BLACK FRIDAY STARTS NOW" hero and the red "Up to 50% Off" block are loud and clear. A second giant "UP TO 80% OFF" clearance banner sits right under it, so the email has two competing headline offers. That is the same 80% creative from the 11/20, 11/21 and 11/22 sends. A subscriber who has seen "Unreal savings" and "WOW! 🚨" in the last 48 hours will read this as more of the same.
- Below the heroes the email runs long, about eight modules. They cover sneakers, clothing, golf balls, fan gear, basketballs, fire pits, socks, store hours and gift picks. Each has its own "Shop Now" button. It looks like a clean, well-built retail layout, but nothing in it says why this send differs from yesterday's. The subject adds to the problem. It is long and emoji-heavy, and it says "early" in a way that undercuts the "starts now" hero.
- Judgement: competent and on-season, but a repeat. Execution is fine and differentiation is weak, so expect average engagement and a tired audience.

## What's working

- The "BLACK FRIDAY STARTS NOW" hero is the first thing the eye lands on. It matches the subject's Black Friday promise.
- "Up to 50% Off" with a dark red "Shop Now" button sits directly under the hero, so there is one clear primary action.
- The category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Golf, Fan Shop) give a fast way to self-select.
- The product modules have concrete prices and offers, like the $49.99 fan shop fleece, $89.99 Wilson basketballs and 25% off golf balls. These are more persuasive than generic percentage banners.
- The render is clean. I see no broken images, no overlapping text and a consistent green and red brand look.

## What's weak

- The 80% clearance banner is the same creative as the last three sends. It also competes with the 50% hero for the headline.
- The email is very long, with about eight or nine modules, each with its own button. The offers are scattered (50%, 80%, 25%, 15%, "BOGO Free") and there is no single story.
- There is no visible deadline or countdown. "Limited Time" is a label with no date, and the real dates sit in small fine print. The 11/21 "final hours" send had a live countdown. This one has less urgency than that.
- The store-hours and "Learn More" modules sit mid-email as a second hero. That pulls attention from the offers.
- The footer is dense with fine print and exclusions.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story per send and drop the repeated 80% block. Lead with Black Friday 50% and move clearance to one small tile lower down.
- 2. Cut the body to about five modules. Keep the best-priced products and drop store hours and the secondary promos.
- 3. Add a real deadline and a countdown to the hero, such as "Ends 12/5 at 11:59 PM PT".
- 4. Make the CTA copy specific, for example "Shop Black Friday Deals" instead of "Shop Now".
- 5. Shorten the subject to under 50 characters and drop the stacked emojis and the all-caps "SAVE BIG".

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Black Friday deals start now. Ends 12/5`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sneakers, golf and fan gear from $49.99. Free shipping on BOGO socks and blankets.`

The truncated source and the AgentMail relay limit what can be confirmed about headers and links.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links are reported. The QA probe covered only 12 of 67 links (55 skipped as footer, utility or social), so coverage is partial. The unsubscribe link was included in the probe by design.
- The open pixel is `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1...&tuid=6b00a79ff5a17e3d26c3720c&configId=512ffb51-...`. It is a 1x1 `<img>` with no `alt` and no `display:none` or inline style. It sits directly after `<body>`, before the layout table.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] Plain-text fallback is 9 characters.** This is a missing multipart/alternative text part, or the relay stripped it. A near-empty text part hurts spam scoring and text-only clients.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the relay may not capture these headers, so this is unconfirmed rather than a verified sender defect.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture. The From domain is `em.dcsg.com`.
- The physical postal address and body unsubscribe link are not verifiable because the footer is truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. The destination hrefs, and any UTM parameters, are not visible in the truncated source or in the QA output. No landing-page probe results were provided for the 12 probed links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add meaningful `alt` text to all 14 flagged images.** Use descriptive text for the banners, short labels for the icons, and `alt=""` for the open pixel.
2. **Ship a real plain-text part.** The current 9 chars is the only hard QA failure. If it is a relay artifact, confirm the part exists in the raw MIME from the sender.
3. **Verify headers on the original message, not the relay copy.** Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `em.dcsg.com`.
4. **Populate the preheader** in `#pht-spacer`, and remove the `Replace/Paste SSL Here` comment and the `is-html-mode` / `data-contenteditable` attributes.
5. **Hide the tracking pixel explicitly**, for example with `style="display:block;border:0"` plus `alt=""`.
6. **Re-run with the full HTML and a wider link probe** to confirm UTM tagging, redirect chains and landing-page continuity.
