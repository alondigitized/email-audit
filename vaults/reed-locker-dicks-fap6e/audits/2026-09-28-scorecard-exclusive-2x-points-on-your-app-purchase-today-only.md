# ScoreCard exclusive! 2X Points on your app purchase TODAY ONLY 📲
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S finally trying something different — a Mobile Monday app-exclusive push instead of the umpteenth "EXTRA 25% off clearance" red hero. The 2X ScoreCard Points hook is a real point of differentiation and the phone-mockup hero is clean and on-brand green. But the offer itself is soft: "2X Points" has no dollar value attached for a casual shopper to anchor on, and it's buried under a dagger footnote before you even hit the CTA.
- After the hero it's the standard DICK'S kitchen-sink scroll — six or seven unrelated category blocks (Nike, Fan Shop, golf, drinkware, weights, pickleball, Halloween) with zero connective tissue to the "Mobile Monday" theme. This is a reasonably strong open (novel subject, clear sender) but a mediocre click experience once you're past the fold.

## What's working

- Hero is a single, legible offer: big green background, phone mockup showing "2X POINTS" clearly, no clutter.
- "Today Only" + "Mobile Monday! Earn 2X Points on Your App Purchase" headline is a genuine change of pace from the repetitive clearance-hero cadence seen 9/25–9/27.
- Clear category nav bar (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Team Sports, Halloween Shop) right under the CTA gives an easy secondary path.
- Product grid at the bottom ("Check out these picks") adds concrete, shoppable items with visible pricing/imagery.

## What's weak

- No dollar or point value is quantified anywhere in the hero — "2X Points" means little without a baseline (points = what value?).
- Fine print ("Must sign in to redeem offer... Not a ScoreCard member? Join today") sits directly under the CTA button, adding friction and exclusion for non-members right where the eye lands next.
- The email pivots immediately from an app/loyalty offer into six unrelated generic promos (Nike, Fan Shop, golf, drinkware, weights, pickleball, Halloween) — this is the same kitchen-sink pattern flagged in prior DICK'S audits, just with a different hero bolted on.
- No countdown or explicit expiration time for "Today Only" — urgency is stated but not made visually credible (no clock, no "ends 11:59pm ET" near the CTA itself, only in tiny fine print).

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a concrete value next to "2X Points" — e.g., "2X Points = up to $20 back" or reference typical redemption value — so non-power-users understand the offer instantly.
- 2. Move or shrink the ScoreCard membership fine print so it doesn't compete visually with the primary CTA; keep the button as the clear next step.
- 3. Cut the post-hero scroll from seven unrelated category blocks to two or three, or reframe them as "Stack these with your 2X Points today" to tie back to the headline instead of feeling like a separate email pasted on.
- 4. Add a visible countdown or explicit end-time treatment near the CTA to make "Today Only" feel real, not just declarative.
- **Subject Alt A:** `App-only today: Double your ScoreCard Points 📲`
- **Subject Alt B:** `2X Points, app only — ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sign in to the DICK'S app before midnight to double up.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `ScoreCard members: your points just doubled — app purchases only.`
