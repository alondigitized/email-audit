# Enjoy up to 50% off select styles with our Epic End-of-Summer Sale 🌅
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-31**
## Executive summary

- This is the same red/maroon stadium-seating "UP TO 50% OFF" hero DICK'S ran on 8/30 for the "Send off summer" send, now relabeled "Epic End-of-Summer Sale" — the fourth-plus near-identical creative rotation from this brand in two weeks. The brand strip (Under Armour, Nike, HOKA, adidas, TaylorMade, DSG) is a nice touch that signals breadth without a wall of logos, and the category-nav pills right below the hero are genuinely useful. But past the fold this collapses into the same bloat pattern as every recent DICK'S send: running shoes, "Only at DICK'S" apparel, golf/outdoor/team sports, hydration/fan shop/fitness, then six individual product picks — eight distinct modules with no single dominant CTA.
- The offer itself is fuzzy. "Up to 50% off select styles" in the subject, "up to 50% off select Nike, adidas and more" under the hero, then "up to 40% off running shoes" and "up to 70% off select VRST/CALIA/DSG" two modules down — three different percentages competing for the same eyeball before you've scrolled past the second screen. That's not a hero offer, that's a sale-wide catalog dump with a number slapped on top.
- Judgement: functional, on-brand, zero render bugs, but it's cadence fatigue at this point — same hero shape, same sprawling structure, a new subject line wrapped around it. A subscriber who opened the 8/30 email will recognize this instantly and may not bother scrolling twice.

## What's working

- Zero render bugs — clean layout, all images load, no overlapping text or broken assets.
- Brand strip under the hero (Under Armour/Nike/HOKA/adidas/TaylorMade/DSG) efficiently signals "this applies across brands" without cluttering the headline.
- Category pill nav (Men's/Women's/Kids'/Shoes/Golf/Outdoor) gives quick-scanners a real shortcut past the scroll.
- "Only at DICK'S" module (VRST, CALIA, DSG, Alpine Design) is a legitimate differentiator vs. competitors who can't sell those private brands.
- ScoreCard rewards line up top (300 points + $10 reward) is a real loyalty hook, not filler.

## What's weak

- Third consecutive near-duplicate hero treatment (red stadium seats + bold white "UP TO 50% OFF") since 8/29/8/30 — visual fatigue is real at this cadence.
- Three different percentage claims (50%, 40%, 70%) stacked in the first two screens dilutes the "hero offer" — nothing tells the eye which number is the real deal.
- Eight separate merchandising modules below the hero is the same overstuffed structure flagged in the 8/29 and 8/28 audits — no editing discipline.
- "Shop Now" is reused as the CTA copy on nearly every module (hero, running shoes, VRST/CALIA) — no differentiation in what each button actually does.
- No urgency mechanism visible (no countdown, no explicit end date) despite "Limited Time" label — weaker than the 8/29 send that had a live timer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body to 3 modules max: hero + one "shop the sale" grid + one loyalty/app footer block. The six-item "Check out these picks" and the three secondary category rows (Golf/Outdoor/Team, Hydration/Fan/Fitness) are catalog filler that a mobile reader will never reach.
- 2. Pick one offer number and lead with it everywhere. If 70% off VRST/CALIA is the best deal in the email, that's the hero — not buried as module three under a generic "50%" headline.
- 3. Vary the hero creative. This is the third red-stadium "UP TO 50% OFF" build since 8/29 — even a palette swap (the 8/30 maroon-clearance send proved a fresh hero exists in the asset library) would break the pattern-recognition fatigue.
- 4. Add a real deadline. "Valid online only for a limited time" is vague filler; either state a date/time or drop the urgency framing entirely — the 8/29 countdown timer performed the "hurry" job better.
- 5. Differentiate CTA copy per module ("Shop Shoes," "Shop VRST," "Shop Golf") instead of repeating "Shop Now" four times.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Nike, adidas, HOKA and more — plus private-brand deals you won't find elsewhere.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Running shoes, golf gear, fan shop — all on sale through this weekend.`
