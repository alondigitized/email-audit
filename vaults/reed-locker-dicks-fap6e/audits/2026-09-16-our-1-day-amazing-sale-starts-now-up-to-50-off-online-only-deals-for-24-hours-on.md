# Our 1-day AMAZING sale starts now! Up to 50% off online-only DEALS for 24 hours only ➡️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-16**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S at its most legible: a black-and-white "ONE DAY" hero with a spotlight effect, followed by an unambiguous "Up to 50% Off / 24 Hours of Online-Only Deals!" subhead and a clean green "Shop Now" button. It's a genuine departure from the red-gradient hero this brand has leaned on for the last five sends (9/12–9/15) — different color story, different urgency mechanic (a true 24-hour countdown vs. vague "this week's deals"). The category rail (Men's/Women's/Kids'/Shoes/Team Sports/Fan Shop) right under the fold is a nice touch for fast self-segmentation.
- Where it falls apart is length and repetition within the same email: after the strong hero, it sprawls into eight-plus stacked product blocks (Nike shoes, adidas clothing, Nike fleece, drinkware, gloves, griddle, golf bags) before hitting a *second* "UP TO 50% OFF This Week's Deals" banner — the exact red-gradient hero from the prior sends — buried mid-scroll, undermining the "1-day, 24-hour" urgency of the actual headline offer. Then an app-download pitch and a "check out these picks" grid pile on after that.
- Net: strong hero, diluted message. This would score well on open (genuinely different, urgent subject) but the body doesn't sustain the urgency it opens with.

## What's working

- Hero is high-contrast, legible, on-brand, and visually distinct from the last five DICK'S sends — a real break from repetition.
- "24 Hours of Online-Only Deals!" gives credible, specific time-bound urgency, reinforced by the ONE DAY graphic.
- Primary CTA ("Shop Now") is clear, above the fold, in a color that pops against the black hero.
- Category nav bar (Men's/Women's/Kids'/Shoes/Team Sports/Fan Shop) offers immediate self-segmentation without scrolling far.
- No render bugs — images, buttons, and type all appear clean and intact.

## What's weak

- A second "UP TO 50% OFF This Week's Deals" red-gradient hero appears mid-email — the exact recycled creative from 9/12–9/15 — which contradicts and dilutes the "1-day only" framing at the top.
- The email is extremely long, stacking 8+ individual product promos (shoes, apparel, drinkware, gloves, griddle, golf bags) before even reaching the second sale banner — most recipients won't scroll that far.
- No clear countdown timer or explicit end-time reinforcement beyond the initial subhead, despite "24 hours only" being the entire premise.
- The app-download pitch and "check out these picks" grid at the bottom feel like unrelated bolt-ons that further bury the one-day sale message.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email roughly in half — keep the ONE DAY hero, the category nav, and 3-4 top product blocks max; move the rest to a "see more deals" link. Length is actively working against the urgency.
- 2. Remove or replace the mid-email "UP TO 50% OFF This Week's Deals" red banner — it's the same asset used in five straight prior sends and it contradicts "one day only."
- 3. Add a visible countdown or explicit end-time ("Ends tonight at 11:59PM ET") near the top hero, not just buried in fine print at the very top.
- 4. Move the category nav bar higher or keep it sticky, since it's the fastest path to relevant product for a self-segmenting shopper.
