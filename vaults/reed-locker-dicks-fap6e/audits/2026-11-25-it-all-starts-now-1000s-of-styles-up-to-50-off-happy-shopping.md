# It all starts NOW. ➡️ 1000s of styles 🤝 up to 50% off. Happy shopping!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S eighth or so Black Friday send in three days, and it reuses the same template as the 11/25 "Beat the rush!" email and the 11/24 "Save now, thank us later" email. The hero is a big "BLACK FRIDAY STARTS NOW" banner with a "Biggest Sale of the Year / Up to 50% off 1000s of styles" block and a single red "Shop Now" button. The offer is clear and the layout is clean. The "starts NOW" story, though, is the same one the brand has told for three days.
- Below the hero, the email stacks a lot of separate modules. These include an "Extra 25% off" clearance banner, shoes, Carhartt, Nike shorts, golf balls, a camp chair, a treadmill, store hours and a gift-picks grid. Each module has its own button and its own fine print. The result is a long scroll with no single path to follow. Fine print about clearance exclusions sits under both main offers, which weakens the "honest offer" read.
- Verdict: a competent, well-built send that adds nothing new to the run. It will probably be opened out of habit and clicked by deal hunters, but a subscriber seeing the eighth near-identical promo this week will skim it.

## What's working

- The hero is bold, on-brand and unmistakable: "BLACK FRIDAY STARTS NOW" with "Up to 50% off 1000s of styles" directly below.
- The red "Extra 25% Off" banner is a strong visual break and a second concrete offer.
- Category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Golf, Fan Shop) let a shopper jump straight to their own category.
- The product tiles are clean and alternate left and right, and each has its own CTA.
- The gift-picks grid at the bottom and the store-hours block add practical value for late shoppers.

## What's weak

- Cadence: this is yet another "starts now" send after the 11/23, 11/24 and 11/25 emails, so there is no new news.
- The email is very long, with about ten modules, and the eye has no clear second step after the hero.
- The primary "Shop Now" button is small relative to the giant hero image, and it sits below the fold.
- The fine print under both main offers ("Exclusions apply... not valid in conjunction with other sales") adds friction.
- The sender name "DICK'S | Black Friday" is fine, but the subject has emoji and "Happy shopping!" filler that dilutes the offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to 4 to 5 modules and make the hero itself clickable. Move the "Shop Now" button above the fold.
- 2. Vary the creative day to day. Lead with one hero product or doorbuster instead of repeating the same banner.
- 3. Move the exclusions into a single consolidated footer disclaimer.
- 4. Add reviews or ratings to the product tiles, and show "was/now" pricing on more of them.
- 5. Put real urgency in the subject, such as a deadline or a one-hour pickup, rather than a generic "starts now".

- **Subject Alt B:** `Extra 25% off clearance + BOGO Nike, ends tonight`

- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stacking savings on shoes, Carhartt, Nike and golf. Ends 11/26 at 11:59PM PT.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is structurally sound: it has `lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` layout tables, a viewport meta tag and MSO conditionals. Confirmed defects are limited to missing image alt text, malformed JSON-LD and CSS, and very small mobile type. The compliance and authentication warnings come from relay capture limits, not from anything in the HTML.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage is partial.** QA probed 12 of 65 links and skipped 53. The probed links showed no failures, and the unsubscribe link was included in the probe set. Skipped footer and social links are unverified.
- **The open-tracking pixel is hidden correctly.** `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/v61000001a213c66efc9afe80f4bbe5c7c0/7456f3e72d9f436500004c5a42963aa1` uses `display:none; max-height:0; mso-hide:all`. QA flags its missing alt text, which is harmless because it is not rendered.
- The HTML is truncated, so I couldn't check redirect chains or click-tracking domains for the body CTAs.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text is missing on 12 visible images.** Seven are Scene7 content images: `selectwomenson1122footaccess`, `carhartt-3`, `tempo`, `balls-2026-1122-dks-swd-golf-cyber-8-bids-banner`, `kijaro`, `2026-1122-dks-stwd-fitness-cyber-8-bid-1` and `1101storefront`. Five are footer icons on `dsg.images.harmony.epsilon.com/.../footers/`: `icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png` and `icon-location-2025.png`.
  - **Images-off clients:** Any content image that carries offer or product copy will be blank for screen readers and in images-off clients.
- **The JSON-LD block is invalid.** `<script type="application/ld+json">` contains JS-style `//` comments, which are not valid JSON, so parsers will reject the whole block. It also has an empty `"image": ""`, an empty `"discountCode": ""` and `"availabilityEnds": ""`. `"availabilityStarts"` is a stale `2020-03-06` date, and the designer placeholder text was never replaced.
- **The MSO stylesheet has a syntax error.** `div {background-color:inherit; font-size:0; line-height:100%; margin:0; padding:0;"}` has a stray `"` before the closing brace. Outlook/IE may drop that rule.
- **Mobile font sizes are very small.** In the `@media (max-width:480px)` block, `.body-sm` and `.link-sm` are 9px, `.legal` is 8px and `.body-m` is 11px. These sizes are likely to fail readability and accessibility guidelines.
- **The page title is generic.** `<title>DICK'S Sporting Goods</title>` doesn't describe the campaign. This is minor.
- **Gmail promo markup is orphaned.** A closing `<!--/GMAIL PREVIEW CODE-->` comment appears alongside the unfilled `PromotionCard` placeholder.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible source. The truncated HTML limits this check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) are not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed, not a verified violation**. Check the raw message from the sender side. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules expect one-click unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results is absent.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `DSG@e.dcsg.com` can't be assessed from this capture.
- QA shows the unsubscribe link was probed, but its result isn't listed. The footer physical address wasn't visible in the truncated source, so it is unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Unverifiable. The truncated HTML and the 12 probed links don't expose their query strings. No UTM parameters, landing page destinations or promo-code carry-through could be assessed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 7 Scene7 content images, using the offer or product text each one shows. Use `alt=""` on the 5 footer icons if their labels are adjacent text, and on the tracking pixel.
2. Fix or remove the JSON-LD block. Delete the `//` comments, populate the `PromotionCard` image and offer fields, or drop the unused `DiscountOffer` and `PromotionCard` entries.
3. Remove the stray `"` in the MSO `div{...}` rule.
4. Raise the mobile minimums to about 12–14px for `.body-sm`, `.link-sm` and `.legal`.
5. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` in the raw headers, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC from a direct seed inbox rather than through the relay.
6. Re-run QA on the full HTML with link probing extended to the skipped links and UTM extraction enabled.
