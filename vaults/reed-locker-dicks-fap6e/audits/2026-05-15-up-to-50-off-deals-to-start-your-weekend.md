# Up to 50% off DEALS to start your weekend ➡️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth DICK'S deals email in two days — a structurally identical sibling to the May 14 "Best DEALS of the week = up to 50% OFF" send and the May 13 "Open for up to 50% off" send, all scoring 7/10. The bones are fine: a clean hero, bold "UP TO 50% OFF" leading the eye, green SHOP NOW button, and six tidy category sections below. Execution is competent, templated, and by now deeply familiar.
- The only differentiation in this send is the "start your weekend" framing in the subject — a soft urgency hook that doesn't show up anywhere in the email body. The creative below the fold is the same scatter-shot department-store dump: Men, Women, Kids, Golf, Team Sports, then a clearance rail, then a product grid. No new angle, no fresh hook, no member pricing, no single hero product to anchor intent.
- At this cadence, the real problem isn't the email — it's the inbox. A subscriber who opened Monday, Tuesday, and both Wednesday sends is now facing a fifth near-identical promo in 72 hours. That's a fast track to fatigue or a filter rule.

## What's working

- **Hero is immediately dominant.** "UP TO 50% OFF" is large, bold, and the first thing the eye hits — no ambiguity about the offer.
- **Green SHOP NOW button** has good contrast and is placed directly below the hero with no interstitial clutter.
- **Category architecture is clean.** Men / Women / Kids / Golf / Team Sports / Clearance lets a subscriber scan and self-select in seconds.
- **Clearance rail adds a second hook.** "HUNDREDS of NEW CLEARANCE STYLES" lower in the email gives price-sensitive shoppers a second reason to engage if the first section didn't grab them.
- **No render bugs visible.** Images load, text doesn't overlap, layout is intact throughout.

## What's weak

- **"Start your weekend" urgency is a subject-only promise.** The body has no deadline, no end-date, no "Ends Sunday" badge — the hook dissolves the moment you open.
- **"SHOP NOW" is a wasted CTA slot.** Six sections, six identical "SHOP NOW" buttons — none of them say what you're shopping for or why now.
- **Cadence is at breaking point.** This is the fourth deals email from DICK'S in two days. A real subscriber is now in "unsubscribe" territory, not "excited to open" territory.
- **No price anchors on product cards.** The lower product grid shows items but no prices, no "was / now" strikethrough — the deal proof requires a click rather than scanning.
- **Loyalty/member pricing absent.** No ScoreCard mention, no member-exclusive framing — a missed lever given how many sends this week have already diluted "50% OFF."

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a deadline to the body.** If the subject says "start your weekend," the hero should say "Ends Sunday 11:59pm." One badge, no redesign needed.
- 2. **Rotate the CTA copy by section.** "SHOP MEN'S SHOES," "SHOP WOMEN'S DEALS" — costs nothing to produce, communicates relevance instantly.
- 3. **Surface a single hero product.** Pick one viral or bestselling SKU, show a "was $120 / now $59" price pair in the hero. Every successful DICK'S send from this audit history that hit 8/10 had a concrete anchor (the May 13 extra-25%-off had a single red badge; the May 12 digest had one clear price-threshold block).
- 4. **Gate one day's send to ScoreCard members only.** Friday sends could show member-exclusive pricing or early access — it reframes the cadence as a perk rather than broadcast noise.
- 5. **Suppress after 3 opens in 72 hours.** Automation, not creative — the best fix for fatigue isn't a better subject line.

### 5. Compliance

**Unresolved preheader placeholder — high risk:**
```html
<td id="ssl" …>
  <img src='…tracking_pixel…' style="display:none;…"/>
  <!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->
  On now: Up to 40% off select clothing & shoes for the fam

```
The comment `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` indicates the physical/legal mailing address or state-specific disclosure was never substituted. **CAN-SPAM §7704(a)(5) requires a valid postal address in every commercial email.** If the "SSL" token is the address block and was omitted, this email is non-compliant.

- **Unsubscribe link**: Not visible in the truncated source — cannot confirm presence. Must appear per CAN-SPAM and CASL.
- **Authentication headers** (SPF/DKIM/DMARC): Not assessable from HTML source alone; must be verified via raw message headers. Sending domain is `e.dcsg.com` (subdomain of `dcsg.com`) — confirm DKIM signing is scoped to this subdomain.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**Subject/preheader discount discrepancy:**
- Subject line: *"Up to 50% off DEALS to start your weekend"*
- Preheader (rendered plain-text): *"On now: Up to 40% off select clothing & shoes for the fam"*

These are different discount claims. If both are intentional (different product sets), this is a content issue outside this audit's scope. If one is a stale copy-paste, the wrong figure is live in inbox previews.

**JSON-LD structured data — malformed and unpopulated:**
```json
{ // GIF & WEBP images are not supported…
  "@type": "PromotionCard",
  // DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!
  "image": ""
}
```
- JavaScript-style `//` comments are **invalid JSON** — the entire `<script type="application/ld+json">` block will fail to parse in Gmail's structured data engine and any JSON parser. Gmail uses this block to render promotional annotations (deal badges, promo cards).
- `"availabilityStarts": "2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"` — hardcoded 2020 date, six years stale.
- `"discountCode": ""`, `"availabilityEnds": ""`, `"image": ""` — all unpopulated. The PromotionCard will not render even if JSON is fixed.
