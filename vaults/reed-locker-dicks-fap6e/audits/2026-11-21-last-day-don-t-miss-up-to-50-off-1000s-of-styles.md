# Last day! 🚨 Don't miss up to 50% OFF 1000s of styles...
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a "last day" send whose hero doesn't say what ends today. The red "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY SALE" banner is bold, and "Last Day / Up to 50% Off" sits right under it. The urgency only registers if you read the small red tag. Below that, a second full-width block shouts "UP TO 80% OFF" for clearance. The email now has two competing headline numbers.
- The body is a long scroll of eight or more modules: shoes, Under Armour, Walter Hagen golf layers, Nishiki bikes, Chirp, hoops, an app promo and a gift-picks grid. Each has its own "Shop Now" button. A recipient who opened for "50% off" gets a catalog, not a deadline. This is the fifth or sixth DICK'S "up to X%" send in about three days (11/19 "up to 40%", 11/19 "up to 70%", 11/20 "up to 80%", 11/20 "up to 50% expire tomorrow"). The brand is training people to wait for the next one.
- Verdict: competent template, low urgency, high fatigue. The deadline is clear in the fine print ("11:59PM PT") but weak where it matters.

## What's working

- The "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY SALE" hero is unmistakable and fits the season.
- The "Last Day" tag and "Order Today. Spot It Tomorrow." banner at the top support the subject.
- The category pill row (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Holiday Shop) lets people self-select.
- The green "Shop Now" buttons are consistent and visible.
- The ScoreCard points bar at the top adds a loyalty reminder.

## What's weak

- Two competing hero offers (50% and 80%) split attention. The 80% block is clearance-only, and the "Select Clearance Styles" qualifier is small.
- The "Last Day" tag is a tiny red label, not part of the hero. The urgency in the subject isn't carried into the creative.
- Product modules each carry a different discount: 25% off shoes, 25% off Under Armour, $59.98 golf layers, 50% off bikes, 25% off Chirp, 50% off hoops. The "up to 50%" promise is diluted by many 25% offers.
- The email is very long, and the app promo and gift grid sit well below the fold.
- The fine print under each hero ("Valid online only... Exclusions apply") reads as restrictive and undercuts the offer's honesty.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a countdown or "Ends tonight 11:59 PM PT" in the hero itself, and drop the second 80% hero block. One offer, one deadline.
- 2. Cut the body to three or four modules, ordered by strongest discount (50% bikes and hoops first, not 25% items).
- 3. Make the CTA specific, such as "Shop 50% Off Deals" instead of repeated "Shop Now".
- 4. Move the app promo and gift grid out of a "last day" send.
- 5. Throttle cadence. Skip a send or consolidate into one daily email.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: up to 50% off 1000s of styles`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: Early Black Friday ends at 11:59 PM PT`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Bikes, hoops, shoes and more. Prices go back up at midnight PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your final shot at Early Black Friday pricing, plus free in-store pickup.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally sound (`lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` layout tables, MSO conditionals, viewport meta). However, the plain-text part is effectively empty (9 chars), the preheader slot is unfilled, and the offer-carrying images have no alt text. The headers and link tracking can't be fully verified from the captured data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- QA probed only 12 of 65 links and skipped 53, so link health is mostly unverified. The report gives no per-link status codes, so I can't confirm any broken links.
- Links carry `class="arc-link"` and `data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"`, which suggests app deep-link or universal-link handling. The wrapped click-tracking domain is not visible in the truncated source, so I can't check the redirect chain or fallback behavior.
- The open-tracking pixel (`https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=…`) is 1×1 and sits directly after `<body>`, before the wrapper table. It has no `alt` (flagged by QA) and no `style="display:block"` or `border="0"` in the visible markup.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 12 images (QA WARN).** Ten are content images and two are tracking or icon images. The content images are the large Scene7 assets served at `wid=1200&hei=1600`, for example `2026-1121-dks-storewide-6am-1`, `…-6am-new`, `…-6am-3`, `…-6am-4`, `2026-1112-dks-storewide-4pm-3-bikes` and `…-4pm-4`. Others are `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1` and `icon-location-2025-1`. With images blocked, or for screen-reader users, these show nothing meaningful.
- **Heavy image requests.** `fmt=jpeg&qlt=100&wid=1200&hei=1600` requests 1200×1600 JPEGs at quality 100 for a 584–600px container. This inflates load weight, especially on mobile. Actual file sizes were not measured.
- **Unfilled preheader slot.** `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` is still in `td#ssl`, and `#pht-spacer` contains only `&zwnj;&nbsp;` padding. The inbox preview text will fall back to the first body text or alt text, and with no alt text on the hero images that fallback is poor.
- **Plain-text fallback fails (QA FAIL).** The text part is 9 characters, so there is no usable text alternative for text-only clients or for spam filters that weigh multipart parity.
- No issues found in the viewport meta, `<title>`, or the `@font-face` fallback stack (`Archivo, Arial, sans-serif`).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source or in the subject. The truncated source can't rule out tokens further down.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) are not found (QA WARN).** QA notes they may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so verify against the raw original message.
- **Authentication-Results is absent (QA WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sending domain is `em.dcsg.com` and the visible From address is `info@em.dcsg.com`; alignment can't be verified without the raw headers.
- The footer is outside the truncated source, so the visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not verifiable. The truncated HTML doesn't show destination URLs or UTM parameters. The probed links' redirect targets and landing-page status were not included in the QA output. The asset name `2026-1112-dks-storewide-4pm-3-bikes` shows a Nov 12 image in a Nov 21 send. Whether its destination is still valid couldn't be checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the plain-text part.** Generate a real multipart/alternative text version (currently 9 chars) with the offer copy, key links and the unsubscribe URL.
2. **Add `alt` text to all 10 content and icon images.** Put the offer text in the alt of the hero images, and use `alt=""` on decorative icons and the 1×1 open pixel.
3. **Populate the preheader.** Replace the `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` placeholder with actual text before the `#pht-spacer` padding.
4. **Verify headers on the raw message.** Check that `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment are present. If they are, the QA warnings are relay artifacts. If not, fix them at the ESP.
5. **Reduce image weight.** Lower the Scene7 `qlt` (about 75–85) and size `wid` to roughly 2× the display width (about 1200 or less, with proportional `hei`).
6. **Widen link probing.** Probe all 65 links, or at least all CTA and deep links, and log the final landing URLs and UTM parameters to settle section 6.
