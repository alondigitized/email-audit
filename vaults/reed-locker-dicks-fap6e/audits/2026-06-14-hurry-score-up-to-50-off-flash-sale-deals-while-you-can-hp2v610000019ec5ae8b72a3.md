# 💸 Hurry & score up to 50% off Flash Sale deals while you can!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-14**
## Executive summary

- This is the DICK'S Flash Sale sub-account's second send in 48 hours — running the identical multi-module compilation format it ran on June 13 (scored 6/10 in this inbox). Arriving one day after the genuinely well-executed Knicks championship email (8/10), the contrast is jarring: that send had a single news hook and a single purpose. This one has five competing offer modules, no specific deadline, and no creative reason to exist beyond filling a send slot. By June 14 this inbox has received seven distinct DICK'S emails in four days across three sub-senders — no click intent survives that frequency.
- The email does carry real deals: 50% off Flash Sale, a 30% off module, Extra 30% Clearance, and a $10 bonus. But stacking all of them without hierarchy means the subscriber has to do cognitive work to find the thing that applies to them. That is the opposite of a Flash Sale, which should mean one urgent action.
- The cadence problem is now the story. DICK'S is burning list equity at a rate that no individual offer quality can compensate for. This send reads like a holdover scheduled without checking what already went out this week.

## What's working

- **Multiple real, visible discounts** — 50%, 30%, and $10 off are all surfaced in the body; no bait-and-switch.
- **Category navigation tiles** — Men's / Women's / Golf / Outdoor modules let a subscriber self-select without hunting.
- **Father's Day peg is well-timed** — arriving June 14, one week before Father's Day (June 21), the gifting content lands at the right moment.
- **Flash Sale brand cues** — the hero communicates limited-time without needing a timer.

## What's weak

- **Creative is a copy-paste of June 13** — same dense multi-module layout, same 50% ceiling, nothing escalated or differentiated from the email that ran 24 hours prior.
- **Clearance module undercuts the Flash Sale** — running "Extra 30% Off Clearance" inside a Flash Sale email signals that DICK'S perpetually has everything on sale simultaneously; neither feels urgent.
- **Subject is padded past mobile length** — "Hurry & score... while you can" adds 15 characters of urgency filler with no delivery; 62 total characters truncates on most phones.
- **No end date anywhere in the visible body** — "Flash Sale" implies time-sensitivity; the email never backs it up.
- **Frequency fatigue is the real ceiling** — this is the 7th DICK'S email in four days; open rate math is ugly before a subscriber even reads the subject.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Suppress to anyone who opened or clicked the June 13 Flash Sale send** — resending an identical format 24 hours later to the same audience is pure list burn, full stop.
- 2. **Pick one offer and commit to it** — drop the Clearance and $10 modules from Flash Sale sends. "50% off, ends Sunday" is a complete email; five competing deals is a catalogue.
- 3. **Add a hard deadline** — "Ends Sunday 11:59 PM" is worth 10× "while you can."
- 4. **Trim the subject to under 50 characters and remove the filler urgency.**
- **Subject Alt A:** `Flash Sale ends Sunday — up to 50% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Score 50% off before it's gone this weekend`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Men's, Women's, Golf — shop before the Flash Sale closes`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Grab the $10 bonus deal before Sunday night`
- 5. **Give the Father's Day module a specific destination** — "Shop Dad's Day Gifts" landing on a curated gift guide, not a category root.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)

**Footer** — source is truncated before the footer; cannot confirm presence of:
- Physical mailing address (CAN-SPAM §7(d))
- One-click unsubscribe mechanism
- Company identification

These **must be verified** on the full source. If missing, this is a CAN-SPAM violation.

**Sender domain** — `DSG@e.dcsg.com`. The `e.dcsg.com` subdomain is the SFMC sending domain. SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment cannot be confirmed from HTML alone; should be verified via email headers post-send.

**JSON-LD structured data block — CRITICAL:**

The `<script type="application/ld+json">` block contains **JavaScript-style single-line comments**, which are illegal in JSON:

```json
{ // GIF & WEBP images are not supported and will be filtered out.
  // Sample image is 538x138, 3.9 aspect ratio
  "@context": "http://schema.org/",
  "@type": "PromotionCard",
  // DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!
  "image": ""
}
```

`JSON.parse()` will throw on `//` comments. Gmail's structured data processor will silently discard the entire block. Additionally:
- `DiscountOffer.availabilityStarts` is hardcoded to `"2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"` — 6 years stale
- `DiscountOffer.availabilityEnds` is `""` — empty string is not a valid ISO 8601 datetime
- `DiscountOffer.discountCode` is `""` — empty, providing no value to schema processors
- `PromotionCard.image` is `""` — designer placeholder was never filled in
