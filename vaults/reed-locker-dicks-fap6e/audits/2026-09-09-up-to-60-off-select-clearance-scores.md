# 🚨 Up to 60% OFF select clearance scores! 🚨
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a new hero for the cycle — a black stadium-seating shot with "UP TO 60% OFF SELECT CLEARANCE SCORES" in a white card overlay, distinct from the maroon stadium seats and pampas-grass hero DICK'S has cycled through since 8/30. The 60% figure is also a genuine escalation from the "up to 50%" ceiling this brand has run all week, so there's a real reason to open beyond habit. But once past the hero, this is the same sprawling everything-store scroll DICK'S always sends — running shoes, YETI drop, cleats, golf trade-in, camping tent, a second "up to 50% off" banner that undercuts the subject's 60% claim, then a grid of six unrelated SKUs.
- The clearance framing in the subject doesn't survive contact with the body: the hero CTA goes to a generic "Shop Now," and the very next banner ("This Week's Deals") reverts to 50% off, diluting the headline claim. For a persona shopping clearance specifically, there's no clearance-only section, no clearance badge on any product tile, and no price/percentage shown on a single actual SKU. It reads as bait: strong subject, unfocused body.

## What's working

- Genuinely new hero creative (black stadium seats, "UP TO 60% OFF") — a real departure from the maroon loop that's been repeating since 8/30.
- 60% is a real number escalation versus this week's steady 50% ceiling, which stands out in the brand history.
- Clear green "Shop Now" CTA button directly under the hero with good contrast.
- Category chip row (Men's / Women's / Kids' / $25 and Under / Fan Shop / All Sale) gives quick wayfinding right after the offer.
- No render bugs — clean images, no overlapping text, no broken tiles.

## What's weak

- The "This Week's Deals" banner further down reverts to "UP TO 50% OFF," directly undercutting the 60% headline claim and reading as inconsistent.
- Nothing in the body is labeled "clearance" — the subject promises clearance scores but the hero button just says "Shop Now" with no clearance-specific landing signal.
- No product-level pricing or percentage-off shown anywhere — not on the shoe tiles, cleats, camping gear, or the final SKU grid — so the headline discount is never grounded in an actual price.
- The email sprawls across seven unrelated categories (running shoes, apparel, YETI, cleats, golf trade-in, camping, deals grid) before any clearance-specific content appears, which buries the stated offer.
- Same everything-store structure and generic "Shop Now" buttons repeated throughout, indistinguishable from the 8/30–9/8 sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an actual clearance product grid directly under the hero (with visible original vs. sale prices) instead of jumping to a generic Men's/Women's/Kids' chip row — right now the 60% claim is never substantiated with a real SKU.
- 2. Fix the internal contradiction — the "This Week's Deals" banner says 50% off two screens after the hero says 60% off; align these or move the higher number's supporting products up top.
- 3. Change the hero CTA from "Shop Now" to something clearance-specific like "Shop Clearance" so the click destination matches the subject's promise.
- 4. Trim the body — six unrelated categories before reaching the deals grid is fatigue-inducing for a brand already sending near-daily; cut to 3-4 sections max.

- **Subject Alt B:** `60% off clearance: shoes, apparel, gear`

# Technical Audit — DICK'S "Up to 60% OFF" Clearance Email

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard DSG/Salesforce Marketing Cloud (em.dcsg.com) template using `mclick`/`click` redirect tracking; email fails deliverability/compliance checks due to a missing plain-text fallback and undetectable unsubscribe/authentication headers, plus widespread missing alt text on content images.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All links routed through `data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/mclick` and `/ee/v1/click` with opaque `params=b.v1...` tokens, `pid`, `tuid`, and `configId` — standard SFMC click-tracking wrapper, functioning as expected.
- Open-tracking pixel present: `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1.T8Tx0Y...` — flagged by QA as an "image missing alt text," which is a false positive for a tracking pixel (1x1 pixels don't need alt text); no actual issue.
- QA probed 12 of 64 total links (52 skipped per click-likelihood ranking) — no broken/dead links identified among probed set.
- No issues found with link destination consistency (all resolve through the same mclick domain/configId).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **9 image `alt` attributes missing**, confirmed by QA: `2026-0909-dks-storewide-6am-1`, `-2`, `-3`, `-5`, `whitehead-2026-0901-d`, `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`. These are content/promo images and icons, not decorative pixels — screen readers will announce nothing or fall back to filenames.
- MSO/Outlook conditional comments (`<!--[if (gte mso 9)|(IE)]-->`) present for font and table fallback handling — correctly implemented.
- Responsive/mobile breakpoint CSS (`@media screen and (max-width:480px)`) defined with `.mobile-off`, `.mobile-on`, `.mobile-w100`, etc. — standard responsive pattern, no issues found.
- Web font (`Archivo`) loaded via `@font-face` inside `@media screen` block (correctly scoped to avoid Outlook desktop rendering it) with local-font fallback — no issues found.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g., `%%FirstName%%`, `{{...}}`) visible in the truncated source.
- `tuid` (transactional/subscriber ID) present in tracking URLs, confirming per-recipient personalization is applied at the link level.
- No issues found in visible source; full-document scan would be needed to rule out broken tokens further down.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL/WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header not found** — either not sent or not captured by the AgentMail relay. If genuinely absent, this is a CAN-SPAM/Gmail-Yahoo bulk sender requirement gap.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found (RFC 8058)** — one-click unsubscribe via header not confirmed; in-body unsubscribe link is reportedly present (QA notes "an unsubscribe link is always included") but was not among the probed set shown.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` header not found** — SPF/DKIM pass/fail status cannot be verified from this relay; cannot confirm authentication compliance either way.
- Note: these three warnings may be artifacts of the AgentMail relay not surfacing headers rather than the sender actually omitting them — recommend checking raw headers at the source MTA before treating as confirmed failures.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Links use SFMC `mclick`/`click` redirect params (`params`, `pid`, `tuid`, `configId`) rather than standard UTM query parameters — final landing page UTM tagging cannot be verified without following redirects.
- No issues found in visible source; would need to resolve redirect chains to confirm landing-page alignment and UTM presence.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 9 flagged images (promo banners should describe the offer; icon images like `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1` should describe their function, e.g., "Sign up for SMS alerts").
2. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers exist at the originating MTA — required for Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender compliance (>5,000 msgs/day); if truly missing, add both headers.
3. Provide a substantive plain-text MIME alternative — current fallback is 9 characters, which will hurt deliverability/spam scoring and fails accessibility for text-only clients.
4. Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment via raw headers at the sending MTA, independent of the AgentMail relay, since `Authentication-Results` could not be verified here.
5. Follow redirect chains on the 12 probed links to confirm landing pages carry consistent UTM/campaign parameters for attribution.
