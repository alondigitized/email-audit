# You don't want to miss an extra 25% OFF select clearance... ⏳ 👉
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the same "EXTRA 25% OFF" red hero DICK'S has now run at least three times in three days (9/25 "Limited time!", 9/25 "starts now!", 9/26 "ends tonight" at 6.00/10 each) — this one just swaps the subject to "You don't want to miss..." with the hourglass/pointing-hand emoji combo, but the creative itself is byte-for-byte the same block: bold white "EXTRA 25% OFF" on deep red, followed by "Final Hours" and a green "Shop Now" button. It's legible and the offer is unambiguous, which keeps it from scoring low, but subscribers who opened yesterday's near-identical send have zero reason to open this one.
- Below the hero it's the usual DICK'S kitchen sink: category chips, Men's/Women's/Kids' up-to-50% carousels, drinkware, team sports, a Halloween shop, and a "Check out these picks" product grid. Nothing broken, nothing exciting — just volume.
- Judgement: functional, on-brand, zero-defect execution of a promo that's gone stale from repetition. The hero mechanics work; the fatigue is the real problem.

## What's working

- Hero is instantly legible — big white "EXTRA 25% OFF" on red, readable at a glance.
- Single unambiguous CTA button ("Shop Now") directly under the offer, no scroll needed.
- "Final Hours" adds real urgency directly above the headline.
- Clear post-hero navigation via category chips (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 and Under, Fan Shop, All Sale).
- No render bugs — all product images loaded, text is clean, layout holds together end to end.

## What's weak

- Third near-identical "EXTRA 25% OFF" red-hero send in as-many days — creative fatigue is real for anyone who's opened the last two.
- Subject uses a vague "you don't want to miss" hook instead of leading with the concrete offer or a hard deadline.
- No visible expiration date/time on the hero itself (just "Final Hours" — no clock).
- Long generic catalog scroll (drinkware, team sports, Halloween shop) dilutes the single-offer focus the hero sets up.
- Halloween shop section is out of season/off-message wedged into a clearance-deadline email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop reusing the identical red hero — swap layout/copy each send so subscribers who opened Wednesday's or Thursday's version don't instantly disengage from a fourth repeat.
- 2. Put a real countdown or explicit deadline ("Ends 9/26, 11:59PM PT") directly in the hero, not just "Final Hours."
- 3. Cut the subject line's vague teaser for something concrete and shorter.
- 4. Trim the catalog scroll — move Halloween/drinkware/team sports to a separate send so this one stays a focused single-offer email.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Final hours: Extra 25% off clearance ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call — Extra 25% off select clearance`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ends tonight at 11:59PM PT. Exclusions apply.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stock's moving fast — grab your size before it's gone.`
