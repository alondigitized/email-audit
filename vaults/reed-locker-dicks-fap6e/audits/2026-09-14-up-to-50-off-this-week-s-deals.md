# 📥 Up to 50% off this week's DEALS
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-14**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S running the same "UP TO 50% OFF" red-gradient hero one more time, but the email doesn't stop there — it's one of the longest, most category-sprawling sends in this recent stretch, stacking running shoes, DSG clothing, Owala bottles, team sports, fan shop, outdoor gear, and a second "$25 AND UNDER" clearance hero all in one scroll. The top hero is clean and legible (bold white "UP TO 50% OFF" on red, clear "Shop Now" button), consistent with the 9/13 and 9/12 sends' best creative choices, but by the time a reader scrolls through eight-plus distinct offer blocks, the "best deals of the week" framing from the top has lost all meaning — everything is a deal, so nothing is the deal.
- The subject line itself is weak: "📥 Up to 50% off this week's DEALS" is a near-verbatim repeat of the 9/13 "This week's deals coming your way (and they're up to 50% OFF)" send, and the inbox emoji (📥) reads as an odd, unexplained visual tic rather than a hook.
- Given the brand history — this is the third "up to 50%" framed send in three days with no new urgency mechanic (no countdown, no hard deadline) — this lands as pure cadence fatigue. Judgement: functional but forgettable, a rerun dressed as a new email.

## What's working

- Top hero is high-contrast and instantly legible: bold white "UP TO 50% OFF" on a saturated red gradient.
- Clear primary CTA button ("Shop Now") directly under the hero headline.
- Category chip row (Men's/Women's/Kid's/Shoes/Fan Shop/Team Sports) gives fast wayfinding for a persona shopping specific categories.
- "$25 AND UNDER" mid-email module is a strong secondary hero — same legible red/white treatment that's worked well in prior sends (referencing 9/13's "$25 & under" send).
- Loyalty-relevant modules are present near the bottom (ScoreCard order/pickup banner at top, "Sign Up for Texts," "Best Price Guarantee," "Create a Favorites List").

## What's weak

- No visible countdown or firm deadline anywhere — "up to 50% off" with zero time pressure, a step backward from the 9/11 "Ends Tomorrow" and 9/12 countdown-timer sends.
- Extreme length and category sprawl (8+ separate offer modules) dilutes the "Best Deals of the Week" hero promise — nothing reads as curated.
- Subject line and hero copy are near-identical to yesterday's (9/13) send, reinforcing cadence fatigue rather than differentiating this email.
- The 📥 inbox emoji in the subject is unexplained and doesn't map to any visible content — reads as noise, not a hook.
- Models/demographic mix is broad and generic (family, team sports, outdoor) rather than signaling anything to a specific shopper segment.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to 3-4 modules max, anchored by the strongest single offer, and move the rest to a "shop more deals" link — length is the single biggest drag here.
- 2. Add a real deadline or countdown to the top hero — the brand has already proven (9/11, 9/12) it can execute urgency; this send abandons it.
- 3. Differentiate the subject from yesterday's near-identical line and drop the unexplained emoji.
- 4. Lead with the "$25 AND UNDER" module instead of burying it two-thirds down — it's historically the brand's clearest-performing creative.
