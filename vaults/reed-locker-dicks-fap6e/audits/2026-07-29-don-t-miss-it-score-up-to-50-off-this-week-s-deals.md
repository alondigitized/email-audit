# Don't miss it! Score up to 50% OFF this week's DEALS. 💰
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-29**
## Executive summary

- This is the third time this exact paddleboard-at-sunset "UP TO 50% OFF" hero has run this week — it first appeared 2026-07-24, was reused 2026-07-26 ("Up to 50% off is available in this week's deals," 8.00/10), and here it is again on 2026-07-29 with only the subject line reshuffled to "Don't miss it!" urgency framing. The creative itself is still solid: clean hierarchy, a legible offer, and a real CTA above the fold. But the fatigue is now undeniable — a subscriber who's been paying attention has seen this beach scene three times in five days, and no amount of subject-line urgency fixes a stale hero image.
- Below the fold it's the same kitchen-sink pattern DICK'S has leaned on all week: the Nike Deal Days block (also a rerun, previously the standalone hero on 7/26), then category tiles, then a "must-have gear for summer" product grid. Nothing here is broken or poorly executed — it's a competent, on-brand promotional email. The problem is entirely cadence: this is a repeat of a repeat, dressed up with a more urgent subject line to disguise it.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 50% OFF" hero type treatment is bold, legible, and impossible to miss at a glance.
- Clear single primary CTA ("SHOP NOW") directly under the hero, no scrolling needed on mobile.
- Category navigation pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Golf, Summer Shop) give an easy secondary path for browsers who don't want the blanket 50% deal.
- Nike Deal Days section clearly states the deadline (valid through 8/15/26) and includes a legible secondary CTA.
- No render bugs — images, buttons, and text all display cleanly.

## What's weak

- This is the same sunset paddleboard hero image used on 2026-07-24 and 2026-07-26 — zero visual novelty for anyone who's opened DICK'S emails this week.
- The Nike Deal Days block is also a straight reuse from the 7/26 send, compounding the repetition within a single email.
- "This Week's Deals" copy under the hero is generic filler that doesn't name a single product or category.
- Subject line urgency ("Don't miss it!") doesn't match the content — nothing in the email actually conveys a countdown or expiring offer, it's the same open-ended "while quantities last" language as before.
- Email is very long (dozens of product tiles) with no indication of what's actually new versus recycled from prior sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. Retire the paddleboard hero for at least two weeks — reusing it a third time in five days is the single biggest drag on this campaign; swap in a genuinely different lifestyle shot or a product-forward hero tied to back-to-school (mentioned in the Nike block copy but not reflected visually up top).
- 2. Make the subject's urgency real — add a specific end date or countdown ("Ends Sunday") instead of "Don't miss it!" with no matching deadline anywhere in the body.
- 3. Replace "This Week's Deals" placeholder copy with a specific category or dollar anchor (e.g., "Up to 50% off shoes, apparel & gear").
- 4. Trim the email — six-plus product grids after two already-large promo blocks is bloat; cut to the top 2 categories to reduce scroll fatigue.
- 5. Vary the Nike Deal Days creative from the 7/26 version so repeat openers see something new even within a recurring campaign.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends Sunday: up to 50% off + Nike Deal Days`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 50% off pick is still here (for now)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus up to 40% off Nike, back-to-school picks, and more.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New gear drops before the deal disappears.`

## Technical Audit: DICK'S Sporting Goods — "Score up to 50% OFF" (DSG@e.dcsg.com)

**1. Technical Summary**
Email renders via a standard DKS/Cheetah Digital template with MSO conditional fallbacks and a mobile media-query layer; automated QA passed 43% of checks, surfacing one broken link (unsubscribe), missing email-auth/list-unsubscribe headers, and widespread missing `alt` attributes.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- **Broken unsubscribe link (FAIL):** The unsubscribe CTA (`https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/U/v610000019faf87db86bf549f6e966a3578/98f747ae7f7248be0000021ef3a0bcff`) redirects to an application error page — `unknown url type: '/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription'`. This is a functional break in a legally required link, not a tracking artifact.
- All other tracked links route through the Cheetah Digital/Experian tracking domain `app.dickssportinggoods.com` using `/T/` (click-track redirect), `/O/` (open pixel), and `/U/` (unsubscribe) path conventions — consistent with the ESP's standard redirect scheme.
- 12 of 62 total links were probed (ranked by click likelihood); 50 footer/utility/social links were deprioritized and not verified — those are unconfirmed, not necessarily broken.
- The tracking/open pixel `<img>` at the top of the document (`id="ssl"`) is a 0×0 tracking beacon — expected behavior, not a defect.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **12 images missing `alt` text**, including all five content banner images (`golf.jpg`, `outdoor.jpg`, `team-sports.jpg`, `drinkware.jpg`, `fan-shop.jpg`) served from a single sprite/creative-module source (`2026-0705-dks-stw-condensed-creative-module-banner`), plus the logo/open-pixel and five footer trust/social icons (`icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png`, `icon-location-2025.png`). This affects screen-reader users and clients that block images by default (no fallback text shown).
- Template includes proper MSO conditional comments (`<!--[if (gte mso 9)|(IE)]-->`) and a mobile breakpoint at 480px with `!important`-scoped classes — standard responsive/Outlook handling, no issues found.
- `@font-face` (Archivo) is wrapped in `@media screen` with Arial/sans-serif fallback in the base `font-family` stack — correct progressive enhancement, no issues found.
- Malformed inline CSS in the MSO conditional style block: `div{...padding:0;"}` — a stray trailing `"` is embedded in the `<style>` text for `IE`/Outlook rendering. This is invalid CSS and will be dropped/ignored by Outlook's Word rendering engine, though impact is likely limited to the `div` background/font-size reset rule.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No merge tokens (e.g., first-name, loyalty-tier fields) are present in the visible/truncated markup. The embedded JSON-LD `DiscountOffer` schema object has empty `"description"` and `"discountCode"` fields, and the `PromotionCard` schema has an empty `"image"` field — these appear to be unpopulated template placeholders shipped to production rather than intentional blanks (see comment `"DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!"` left in source).

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **Unsubscribe mechanism is broken** — see Link & Tracking Issues above. This is a CAN-SPAM risk since the one-click path a recipient would use fails with an application error.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** — one-click unsubscribe support via mail client UI cannot be confirmed (may be a capture gap in the AgentMail relay rather than absence at origin).
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found (RFC 8058)** — same caveat; without it, Gmail/Yahoo one-click unsubscribe cannot be confirmed as supported.
- **`Authentication-Results` header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from this capture.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
No UTM query parameters are visible on any link in the captured source — all outbound links use opaque Cheetah Digital redirect IDs (`/T/<campaign-id>/<recipient-hash>/...?__dU__=...`) rather than direct `?utm_source=...` tagging. This is typical for ESP-brokered redirects (UTM values are often injected server-side after the redirect resolves), but it means source/medium/campaign attribution cannot be confirmed from the HTML alone — would need to trace a resolved redirect to the landing page to verify UTM presence and destination-page alignment.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Fix the unsubscribe redirect — `/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription` indicates a misconfigured or dead route on the `/pc/subscription` preference-center path; this is the highest-priority fix given CAN-SPAM exposure.
2. Add `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers (or confirm with the sending platform that they're set at origin and simply not captured by the relay).
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to the five creative-module banner images and footer trust/social icons.
4. Remove or populate the empty `DiscountOffer`/`PromotionCard` JSON-LD fields and strip the leftover designer placeholder comment before send.
5. Fix the malformed trailing `"` in the MSO conditional `<style>` block's `div` rule.
6. Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment directly against the sending ESP's logs, since `Authentication-Results` wasn't observable via this relay.
