# *** Get CLEARANCE DEALS during the Summer Clearance Event ***
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-30**
## Executive summary

- This is the same red "UP TO 70% OFF" hero and stacked-category-block structure DICK'S sent earlier the same day as "< CLEARANCE: up to 70% OFF select DEALS >" (2026-07-30, 6.00/10) — same kitchen-sink layout: Nike Deal Days, team sports, drinkware, outdoor, golf, fan shop, fitness, then a six-item product grid. Sending what appears to be a near-duplicate creative twice in one day with only the subject line changed reads as a deliverability/list-fatigue risk more than a merchandising strategy.
- The top hero is bold and legible — big red block, huge "70%" numeral, clear "SHOP NOW" button — but everything past it is undifferentiated grey-and-white blocks that blur together on scroll. There's no reason to keep scrolling past block three unless you specifically want Nike shoes, drinkware, or golf gear, and even then the offers are generic "up to X% off select" language repeated eight times.
- Net: a competently produced but lazy send, recycling the same day's creative under a new subject. Middle-of-the-pack score, same as its sibling from earlier today.

## What's working

- The hero is unambiguous: giant "70%" numeral, red background, single "SHOP NOW" CTA — no confusion about the top offer.
- Category nav bar (Men's / Women's / Kids' / $25 and Under / Fan Shop / All Sale) gives an immediate self-select path for shoppers who don't want to scroll.
- Each product block has its own clear CTA button(s), so no dead-end sections.
- Clean, consistent DICK'S green/black/white brand system throughout — no render bugs, broken images, or overlapping text.
- App download and social footer are present and complete, no broken links visible.

## What's weak

- Nine-plus near-identical grey category blocks after the hero create serious scroll fatigue — no visual hierarchy differentiates the "best" deal from the rest.
- Every offer is vague ("up to 40% off select," "up to 50% off select") with no specific dollar amount or flagship product called out.
- This is functionally the same creative structure as the 6.00/10 "< CLEARANCE >" send from earlier the same day — the brand is re-running its own inventory within hours.
- Subject line wraps the whole thing in asterisks ("*** ... ***"), a mild spam-flag pattern that gains nothing over plain text.
- No urgency signal beyond a generic "Limited Time" tag — no countdown, no date, nothing that forces action now versus next week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email from 9 category blocks to 3-4 max, and use visual hierarchy (size, color) to signal the actual best deal instead of treating everything as equal.
- 2. Stop re-sending the same-day hero under a new subject line — pick one creative per day and vary the campaign, not the wrapper.
- 3. Drop the asterisk-bracketed subject formatting; it doesn't help scannability and edges toward spam pattern-matching.
- 4. Add a real deadline (specific date/time) to the hero to justify "Limited Time."
- 5. Lead with the single strongest offer (likely Nike Deal Days at 40% off, since that's the freshest sub-campaign) rather than burying it as block two.

# Technical Audit: DICK'S "Summer Clearance Event" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
Email is built on Salesforce Marketing Cloud (ExactTarget) tracking infrastructure with Epsilon Harmony/Scene7-hosted assets; it has one confirmed broken link (unsubscribe), a malformed JSON-LD block, and widespread missing alt text.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken unsubscribe link (FAIL):** `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/U/v610000019fb4b8c880a36d8bf4bbe5be68/0ace015b7fea4a170000021ef3a0bd07` resolves to an error page — `unknown url type: '/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription'`. This is the link-text "Unsubscribe."
- All CTAs route through SFMC click-tracking redirects (`/T/`, `/U/`, `/O/` path prefixes with hex tokens), consistent with standard ESP click-tracking — not itself an issue, but it means true destination URLs cannot be verified from source alone (see §6).
- QA probed 12 of 71 total links (material/high-click-likelihood ones); footer/social links were deprioritized by design, so absence of findings there is not confirmation of correctness.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **11 images missing `alt` text**, confirmed via QA: the SSL/tracking pixel (`0ace015b...`), `sports.jpg`, `banner4.gif`, `outdoor.jpg`, `golf.jpg`, `fanshop.jpg`, `fitness.jpg`, and four footer icons (`icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png`, `icon-location-2025.png`). This breaks screen-reader accessibility and image-blocked fallback rendering (common in Outlook desktop).
- MSO conditional comments (`<!--[if (gte mso 9)|(IE)]-->`) and mobile media queries are present and structured correctly — no issues found in the responsive/Outlook scaffolding itself.
- Web font (`Archivo`) is loaded via `@media screen{@font-face...}` with a system-font fallback in the base `body` rule — correct pattern, no issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The `application/ld+json` schema.org block contains **unresolved template placeholders and invalid syntax**:
  - `"discountCode": ""`, `"image": ""` (PromotionCard), `"availabilityEnds": ""` (DiscountOffer) — all blank.
  - Literal unfilled dev instruction left in production markup: `// DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!`
  - JS-style `//` line comments (`// GIF & WEBP images are not supported...`, `// Sample image is 538x138...`) are embedded inside the JSON-LD payload. **This is invalid JSON** — any strict JSON-LD parser (including Google's structured-data tooling and Gmail's promo-card annotation reader) will fail to parse this block, so the intended promo card will not render.
- No unresolved `{{merge}}`-style personalization tokens observed in the visible/truncated HTML.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **Unsubscribe mechanism is non-functional** per §2 — direct CAN-SPAM risk, since the visible link does not lead to a working opt-out flow.
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found (WARN):** may not be captured by the AgentMail relay rather than genuinely absent — cannot confirm compliance either way from this data.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found (WARN):** if genuinely absent, this means no RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe, which Gmail/Yahoo require for bulk senders as of their 2024 sender guidelines.
- **Authentication-Results header not found (WARN):** SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown from this relay; cannot confirm authentication compliance from available data.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- All CTA links are wrapped in SFMC redirect URLs rather than direct `dickssportinggoods.com` links, so **UTM parameters and final landing-page destinations cannot be verified from the HTML source** — they only become visible after redirect resolution, which was not performed here.
- Image assets are split across two CDNs (`dks.scene7.com` for logo/brand assets, `dsg.images.harmony.epsilon.com` for campaign creative/icons) — consistent multi-vendor hosting, no issue found.
- No issues found regarding domain consistency (`DSG@e.dcsg.com` sending domain vs. `dickssportinggoods.com` brand domain) within the data available.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the unsubscribe link immediately** — it currently 404s to `/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription` instead of the subscription-center page. This is the highest-priority item (compliance-blocking).
2. Remove the invalid `//` comments from the JSON-LD block and either populate or delete the empty `discountCode`/`image`/`availabilityEnds` fields so structured data parses correctly.
3. Add descriptive `alt` attributes to the 11 flagged images (empty `alt=""` is acceptable only for the tracking pixel).
4. Confirm at the MTA/relay level (outside AgentMail) whether `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and SPF/DKIM/DMARC authentication are actually present — current warnings are inconclusive, not confirmed failures.
5. Resolve one sample of each CTA's tracking redirect to verify final landing-page URLs and UTM parameter alignment with the campaign.
