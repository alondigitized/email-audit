# It's Flash Sale time! Score up to 50% off TODAY ONLY
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-12**
## Executive summary

- DICK'S opens strong this time — "FLASH" in a bold diagonal orange/black hero is the clearest, most legible hero graphic in the recent brand history, and "Flash Sale: Up to 50% Off" is unambiguous. But the email immediately undercuts itself by stacking a second, near-identical orange "EXTRA 25% OFF" clearance block right below the fold, which is the same "Last Day / Extra 25% Off Select Clearance" beat from yesterday's 9/11 send ("Ends Tomorrow... extra 25% off select clearance"). Two competing hero offers in the first two screens muddies what the actual CTA should be.
- After that it's the standard DICK'S kitchen-sink scroll: $100-and-under shoes, CALIA/VRST/Alpine private labels, HydroJug, FootJoy golf, football gear, camping gear, then a picks carousel. Functionally solid, visually clean, no render bugs — but it's the same long-scroll structure DICK'S runs daily. Judgment: a genuinely better subject/hero than the week's cadence, dragged down by redundant "Extra 25%" clearance messaging duplicated from yesterday, and it's yet another all-categories mega-scroll rather than a focused flash-sale email.

## What's working

- Hero "FLASH" graphic with strong orange/black diagonal contrast is the most attention-grabbing creative in this brand's recent run.
- "Flash Sale: Up to 50% Off" headline + green "Shop Now" button is a clear, unambiguous primary CTA.
- Category quick-links (Men's/Women's/Kids'/Shoes/Fan Shop/Team Sports) give an easy secondary path right under the hero.
- No visual bugs — images load, text is legible, buttons render correctly throughout.
- Clear expiration date/time stated ("Valid online through 9/12/26 11:59PM PT") reinforcing urgency.

## What's weak

- The "EXTRA 25% OFF" clearance block competes directly with the Flash Sale hero for primary-offer attention — two "the deal" moments back to back.
- That 25%-off clearance block is a near-repeat of yesterday's (9/11) "Ends Tomorrow... Extra 25% Off Select Clearance" send — same offer, same framing, one day later.
- Below the two hero offers, it reverts to the same all-category, everything-under-the-sun scroll (shoes, apparel, golf, football, camping) DICK'S runs in nearly every send, diluting the "flash sale" framing.
- "Flash Sale" implies short/limited-time excitement, but the sale window (through 9/12 11:59PM) is a full day — same durability as regular promos, not truly flash.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the "Extra 25% Off Select Clearance" block entirely from this send (or move it below all flash-sale-eligible categories) — it directly competes with and dilutes the flash-sale hero, and it's a repeat of yesterday's lead offer.
- 2. Trim the mega-scroll: a true flash sale should feature only the categories on flash-sale pricing, not golf/camping/football side offers with different terms — that turns "flash sale" into "everything sale."
- 3. Add a countdown or explicit hours-remaining callout near the hero to make "flash" feel real given the sale actually runs through end of day tomorrow-ish.
- 4. Move category quick-links below a single unified offer block instead of sandwiching a second full offer between hero and categories.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Flash Sale: 50% off ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `⚡ Today only — up to 50% off everything`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop before midnight — new markdowns just dropped`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Gear, shoes, and apparel — flash prices end at 11:59PM PT`
