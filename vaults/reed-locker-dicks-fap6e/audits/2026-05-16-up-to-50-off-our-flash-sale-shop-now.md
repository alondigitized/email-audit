# ⏳ Up to 50% off our Flash Sale — shop now! ⏳
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- This is the second Flash Sale email from DICK'S today — a classic "Final Hours" urgency push following the launch send that scored 8/10 this morning. The countdown timer is the meaningful creative upgrade: "FINAL HOURS 07:59:48" is immediately dominant above the hero and it's the single biggest differentiator over every other DICK'S send this week. The double-decker offer (up to 50% off Flash Sale + Extra 25% Off Select Clearance) gives deal-hunters two reasons to scroll, and the "Ends Tonight" badge appears on both offers so the deadline lands twice without feeling repetitive.
- The real liability is cadence. This is DICK'S email number eight-plus in four days. Even well-executed sends erode at this frequency — subscribers have been trained by the May 13–15 "50% OFF" blast cycle to treat DICK'S emails as ambient noise. The countdown timer punches through that noise, but only barely. Without it, this email is indistinguishable from the morning Flash Sale send.
- On net: the best DICK'S execution of the week. The countdown is credible, the hierarchy is clean, the product picks (HOKA Clifton, adidas Samba) are desirable. But it's operating inside a trust deficit of DICK'S own making from the prior six days.

## What's working

- **Countdown timer ("FINAL HOURS 07:59:48")** is immediately above the hero — the single most effective addition over any prior send in this cycle. Real deadline, real urgency.
- **"Ends Tonight" badge** appears on both the Flash Sale and Extra 25% Off Clearance blocks — reinforces the timeline without relying on the countdown alone.
- **Double-decker offer** (50% off Flash Sale + Extra 25% off Clearance) gives two distinct landing destinations; a deal-hunter exits with two reasons to click.
- **Product picks section** grounds the abstract "50% off" in recognizable SKUs — HOKA Clifton 10, adidas Samba OG, VRST pants are brands that carry click credibility on their own.
- **Clean render throughout** — no broken images, no layout shifts, countdown and hero load correctly, clear visual flow from timer → FLASH SALE hero → SHOP NOW.

## What's weak

- **Cadence fatigue is real** — the "Final Hours" story only lands if it actually feels final; after eight emails in four days, this reads as the latest in a series, not a genuine closing window.
- **Preheader is wasted** — the email appears to leak utility bar copy ("Order Today, Sport it Tomorrow") as its preheader rather than extending the Flash Sale urgency into the inbox preview.
- **"SHOP NOW" is generic** — the CTA button copy does nothing to tell you where you're landing; "Shop Flash Sale" or "Claim 50% Off" is a trivial change with a real specificity lift.
- **No social proof on product picks** — HOKA and adidas Samba are doing all the trust work by brand name alone; one "Bestseller" or review-count callout would meaningfully reduce click hesitation.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the preheader immediately** — "Ends at midnight · HOKA, Nike, adidas all in the sale" extends the subject rather than wasting the slot on a brand tagline. This is the single change that lifts inbox performance most.
- 2. **Sharpen CTA copy** — swap "SHOP NOW" for "Shop Flash Sale" on the hero button; the Extra 25% Off section CTA could be "Shop Clearance" to separate the two offer destinations and reduce decision paralysis.
- 3. **Add one social proof callout** on the product picks grid — "#1 Seller" on the HOKA Clifton or a star rating on the adidas Samba is enough to move a hesitant opener to a click.
- 4. **Cap the Flash Sale cycle at two sends** — this launch + tonight's Final Hours push is the correct two-send structure. A third send tonight or a replay tomorrow breaks the credibility of every countdown that follows.
- **Subject Alt A:** `⏳ Final hours: Flash Sale ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `HOKA, Nike, adidas — up to 50% off, ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ends at midnight — your Flash Sale picks are still here`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Extra 25% off clearance + Flash Sale ends tonight`

### 7. Recommendations

**P0 — Fix the JSON-LD block (will silently break Gmail structured data):**

The `<script type="application/ld+json">` block contains three critical problems:

1. **JavaScript-style comments inside JSON** — JSON does not support `//` comments. The entire block will fail to parse:
   ```json
   // GIF & WEBP images are not supported and will be filtered out.
   // Sample image is 538x138, 3.9 aspect ratio
   // DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!
   ```

2. **Unfilled template placeholders** — these fields are empty strings and should be populated or the object omitted:
   - `"description": ""` (DiscountOffer)
   - `"discountCode": ""` (DiscountOffer)
   - `"availabilityEnds": ""` (DiscountOffer)
   - `"image": ""` (PromotionCard)

3. **Stale `availabilityStarts` date** — hardcoded to `2020-03-06`, six years ago:
   ```json
   "availabilityStarts": "2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"
   ```
   This is clearly a default never updated at send time.

4. **Non-standard Schema.org types** — `DiscountOffer` and `PromotionCard` are not recognized Schema.org types. Gmail's structured data only renders recognized types (e.g., `SaleEvent`, `SpecialAnnouncement`). These objects provide no benefit in their current form.

**P1 — Verify UTM pass-through** by following at least one CTA redirect to its final URL and confirming `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` are appended.

**P1 — Confirm footer compliance** in full HTML: physical address and unsubscribe link must be present and the unsubscribe URL must be live.

**P2 — Add `alt=""` to tracking pixel** and confirm the logo `<img>` carries a descriptive `alt` attribute.
