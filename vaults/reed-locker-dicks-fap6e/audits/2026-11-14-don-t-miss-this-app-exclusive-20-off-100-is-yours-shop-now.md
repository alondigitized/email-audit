# 📢 Don't miss this app exclusive — $20 off $100+ is yours, shop now!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- This is a near-repeat of the earlier 2026-11-14 send, "App exclusive starts now! 🙌 Get $20 off $100+ for 2 days ONLY." The offer is the same: $20 off $100+, or $30 off with ScoreCard+. The subject is just reworded. A subscriber would see this as the same email twice in one day.
- The hero is a dark phone mockup that says "APP EXCLUSIVE" twice and never states the offer. The dollar amount first appears in the headline below it. A red "Get Offer" button and a "Join ScoreCard+" button follow, with a dense disclaimer under them.
- Below that, the email turns into a long catalog. There are five "Up to 40–50% off" category tiles, a giant "UP TO 50% OFF" panel, and four gift picks. Those compete with the app offer, so the email never settles on one message.
- Verdict: the offer is clear and the member tie-in is good. The send feels redundant and diluted, and the hero wastes the best real estate on a phone image.

## What's working

- The headline states the offer plainly: "$20 Off $100 or More | ScoreCard+ $30 Off $100 or More."
- The "Two Days Only" tag adds credible urgency.
- The red "Get Offer" button stands out against the white and green layout.
- The ScoreCard+ upsell gives loyalty members a clear reason to act.
- The holiday imagery and category buttons (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes) fit the season. The render has no broken images.

## What's weak

- The hero never shows the offer, only a phone mockup with "APP EXCLUSIVE."
- The second send in one day with the same $20-off-$100 offer reads as cadence fatigue, on top of the 11/13 Deal Dash and clearance sends.
- The disclaimer under the CTA is long and small. "Must shop in app" and "no code needed" make the offer feel restrictive.
- The body drifts into five category tiles and a "50% OFF" block. The "up to 40–50% off" claims compete with the $20 app offer.
- The footer carries several paragraphs of fine print, and the gift picks have no CTA or price.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put "$20 OFF $100+" in the hero, large, on top of the phone image. Add an "Open in App" button above the fold.
- 2. Cut the body to the app offer plus one or two hero products. Drop the five category tiles and the "50% OFF" block.
- 3. Don't send the same offer twice in a day. If you must, make the second a "last chance" reminder with a real end time.
- 4. Shorten the disclaimer and move the exclusions behind a "Details" link.
- 5. Show prices on the gift picks and link each one to its product page.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: $20 off $100+ in the app`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Ends tomorrow: $30 off $100+ with ScoreCard+`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `App-only, no code needed. ScoreCard+ members save $30 on holiday gifts.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Open the app, shop $100+, and the discount applies at checkout.`

# Technical Audit: DICK'S Sporting Goods, "$20 off $100+" app-exclusive email

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is structurally sound. It is a table-based layout with `role="presentation"`, `lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag and a mobile media-query block. It carries leftover template scaffolding (invalid JSON-LD, a malformed MSO style rule), 11 images without alt text, and unverifiable compliance headers. The source was truncated, so footer and link findings are limited to the QA data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links. 12 material links were probed with no failures reported. 55 were skipped (footer, utility and social links are deprioritized), so those are unverified.
- The open-tracking pixel is `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/v61000001a1dae153a1b4527e6e96c660c0/38527f245b63401600004c5a42963aa1`. It is hidden (`display:none; max-height:0; mso-hide:all`) and has no `alt`, `width` or `height`. This is functional but flagged by QA. Adding `alt=""` is the correct fix for a decorative or tracking image.
- No other issues found.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content images (5):** `mens-2026-1114-d`, `womens-2026-1114-d`, `kids-2026-1114-d`, `fan-shop-2026-1114-d` and `fitness-2026-1114-d` (`s7d6.scene7.com/is/image/DSGAEMSites/...?fmt=png-alpha&wid=1200&hei=1600`). With images blocked, or for screen readers, these category tiles have no accessible name.
- **Missing alt text, footer icons (5):** `icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png` and `icon-location-2025.png`. Each needs either meaningful alt text or `alt=""` if decorative.
- **Dev asset path in production:** the footer icons load from `dsg.images.harmony.epsilon.com/_phoenix/_dev/AEM/img/footers/`. A `_dev` path suggests a non-production asset location, which is a stability and caching risk.
- **Malformed MSO CSS:** in the `<!--[if (gte mso 9)|(IE)]>` block, the rule `div {...; padding:0;"}` contains a stray `"`. Outlook may drop or misparse that declaration.
- **Invalid JSON-LD:** the `<script type="application/ld+json">` block contains JS-style `//` comments (`// GIF & WEBP images are not supported...`, `// DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!`). JSON does not allow comments, so parsers such as Gmail's will reject the whole block.
- **Empty or stale schema fields:** `"description": ""`, `"discountCode": ""`, `"availabilityEnds": ""`, `"image": ""`, and `"availabilityStarts": "2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"`, which is six years stale. The promo annotation is effectively non-functional.
- **Orphaned comment:** `<!--/GMAIL PREVIEW CODE-->` closes a section that has no matching opening comment.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens such as `{{...}}`, `%%...%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear in the visible source. The truncated portion is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe not found (WARN):** the QA tool notes this may be a capture artifact of the AgentMail relay. It cannot be confirmed as absent.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post not found (WARN):** the same caveat applies. RFC 8058 one-click support is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **Authentication-Results not found (WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `e.dcsg.com`, while the tracking pixel and web assets use `dickssportinggoods.com` and `epsilon.com`. Alignment can't be assessed without headers.
- The QA run did confirm an unsubscribe link was included in its probe set. The physical address and unsubscribe text are outside the truncated source, so they are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues found. The data provided includes no CTA hrefs, UTM parameters or landing page results. The 12 probed links reported no failures, but the landing page content was not evaluated. Whether the "app exclusive" offer lands on an app-install or deep-link path cannot be verified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 5 category images. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel and decorative footer icons.
2. Remove the `//` comments from the JSON-LD and populate `description`, `discountCode`, `availabilityStarts/Ends` and `image`. Otherwise remove the block, so the template placeholder text isn't shipped.
3. Fix the stray `"` in the MSO `div {...}` style rule.
4. Move the footer icons off the `_phoenix/_dev/` path to a production asset path.
5. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` on the raw message at the origin, not via the relay. Also confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `e.dcsg.com`.
6. Re-run the audit with the full HTML so the footer, the CTA hrefs and UTM parameters, and the remaining 55 links can be checked.
