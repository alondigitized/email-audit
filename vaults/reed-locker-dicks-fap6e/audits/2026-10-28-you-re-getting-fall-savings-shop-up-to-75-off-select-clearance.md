# You're getting fall savings 🍁💰 Shop up to 75% OFF select clearance!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-28**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S clearance template for the fourth time in four days. A huge white "UP TO 75% OFF" sits over autumn leaves, with "Limited Time" and "Up to 75% Off Select Clearance Styles" and a red Shop Now button below. The eye lands on the number first, and the hero is clear. The fall-savings subject matches the leaf imagery, so the send feels seasonal.
- Below the hero the email turns into a catch-all roundup. It has a category chip row, shoes, CALIA/VRST apparel, baseball bats, fan gear, golf bags, outdoor, a second "UP TO 50% OFF This Week's Deals" hero, a $20-off-$100 text-alert promo, and product picks. Several offers compete with the clearance promise. The 50% block repeats the 10/25 and 10/26 weekly-deals hero almost exactly. The 40%, 70%, 50% and $220-off tiers dilute the 75% message.
- The send is competent but repetitive. A subscriber who got "Wow! 🚨 Don't miss up to 75% OFF" on 10/27 and "Stop scrolling! Up to 75% OFF" on 10/25 will see the same creative again. Only the subject wording has changed.

## What's working

- The giant "UP TO 75% OFF" hero reads instantly, and the red Shop Now button is unambiguous.
- The autumn-leaf imagery fits the "fall savings" subject and the season.
- The category chip row (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 & Under, Fan Shop, All Sale) lets shoppers jump to their own category.
- The product picks at the bottom (adidas Samba, Nike Club fleece) are concrete and give shoppers a specific item to click.
- The sender label "DICK'S | Clearance" signals what the email is about before it's opened.

## What's weak

- The "Up to 50% Off This Week's Deals" block repeats the weekly-deals hero from the 10/25 and 10/26 sends and competes with the clearance message.
- The roundup runs long, with about eight sections, each carrying a different percentage. No single offer stands out below the hero.
- The $20-off-$100+ text-alert block interrupts the shopping flow with a signup ask.
- The fine print under the hero and throughout the email is dense and tiny, which makes the offer feel less transparent.
- Cadence is heavy. This is the fourth near-identical "up to 75%" clearance send in four days, and the template is the same as the 10/27 and 10/25 versions.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the lower half down to a single 75% clearance story. Drop the second "This Week's Deals" hero and merge the categories into one clearance grid with real prices, such as "was $X, now $Y".
- 2. Give each tile one specific product and a deal price instead of a generic "Up to X% off" category block.
- 3. Space the clearance sends out. Alternate this with a send that has a different hook, such as a new arrival or a member offer, so the 75% message stays credible.
- 4. Move the text-alert signup to the footer, or make it a small banner, so it doesn't break up the product flow.
- 5. Add a deadline, such as "ends Sunday", to make the urgency credible.
