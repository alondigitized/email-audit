# 🚨 Biggest SALE of the Year: Up to 50% OFF starts now. 🚨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- This is the seventh or so Black Friday send from DICK'S in three days, and it's the same competent template as the 11/24 "Waiting for Black Friday? Don't. 👀 💰" send and the 11/23 "Black Friday starting now 🤝" send. The green-and-gold "UP TO 50% OFF" hero is loud. A single red "Shop Now" button sits under "Black Friday Starts Now," and the eye lands on the offer first. Below it is a long stack of offer modules: Extra 25% off clearance, Crocs and HEYDUDE, clothing, BOGO socks, drinkware, NCAA, Blackstone, then store hours.
- The problem is cadence and sprawl. A subscriber who has already seen "up to 50% off" in this inbox all week has no new reason to open this one. Inside, there are eight or nine competing offers, each with its own "Shop Now." The hero's "UP TO" figure is also weakened by dense fine print ("Exclusions apply… Offer may not be valid in conjunction with other sales"). It's well made, but it's wallpaper by now.
- Verdict: solid execution, low marginal value. Send fewer of these, and give each one a distinct angle.

## What's working

- The hero is a big "UP TO 50% OFF" block that reads at a glance, with a red "Limited Time" tag and a clear "Shop Now" button right beneath it.
- The "Extra 25% Off Select Clearance Styles" red-and-gold banner is a second strong, specific, price-reducing offer.
- The BOGO free "Cozy Socks, Blankets & Accessories" module has a concrete deal and a visible regular-price range.
- The category chips (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Golf, Fan Shop) give shoppers a fast route to their own category.
- The "View Our Holiday Store Hours" block plus the "Gift picks" product grid add genuine seasonal utility.
- There are no visible render bugs: no broken images, no overlap, and the layout is consistent.

## What's weak

- It's the near-identical sibling of the 11/23 and 11/24 sends, so there's nothing new to reward an open.
- There are too many modules and too many generic "Shop Now" buttons. The email has no single hero product and no priority among its offers.
- The legal text under the hero and the clearance banner is dense and small, so "up to 50%" feels hedged.
- The ScoreCard member benefit is a tiny strip at the top, and no member-exclusive price is shown anywhere.
- There are no reviews or social proof, and most tiles carry no price.
- The gift-picks grid at the bottom has product names but no prices or offers. Those are the most specific items in the email, and they sit buried in the last screen.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Differentiate the send.** Pick one new angle per email, such as doorbusters, a 24-hour price drop, or a category-specific deal. Don't re-send "up to 50% off" daily. This is the biggest lift.
- 2. **Cut the module stack to 3–4 offers** and put one featured product with a price near the top.
- 3. **Make the CTAs specific.** Use "Shop Crocs deals" or "Shop 50% off clothing" in place of repeated "Shop Now."
- 4. **Surface ScoreCard member pricing** in the hero area, not as a fine-print strip.
- 5. **Add prices to the gift-picks grid** (e.g. "Stanley 40 oz. $35") and move it higher.
- 6. **Shorten the subject** to under 50 characters and drop the double siren emoji.

The email is structurally sound (`lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` layout table, viewport meta, MSO fallbacks). Its main confirmed defects are missing `alt` text on 13 images and a malformed, template-placeholder JSON-LD block. Header-level compliance and authentication checks are unverifiable here because the relay stripped the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Only 12 of 64 links were probed, and the QA output gives no failures for them. The other 52 are unverified.
- The open-tracking pixel is `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/v61000001a20e9b0d9abbe054f4bbe5bf30/ff41cc8fefc6442e00004c5a42963aa1`. It has `display:none; max-height:0; mso-hide:all`, so it is hidden correctly. It has no `alt`, `width` or `height`, which is harmless for a hidden pixel.
- The truncated HTML doesn't show the CTA hrefs, so I can't evaluate redirect chains or tracking-domain alignment.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed, 12 content/footer images):**
  - Hero and banner images on `s7d6.scene7.com/is/image/DSGAEMSites/`: `2heydude`, `clothing-1`, `1030-dks-accessories-cozy-cabin-banner`, `50-off-banner-dks2026-1115-11`, `2026-1122-dks-stw-fanshop-cyber8-banner-ncaa`, `blackstone-2`, `1101storefront`.

  - The 50%-off sale banner is likely image-only, so with images blocked the key offer won't show.
- **Mobile font sizes below a readable minimum:**
  - `.body-sm` and `.link-sm` are `font-size:9px`, and `.legal` is `font-size:8px`.
  - `.body-m` is `11px`.
- **Malformed CSS in the MSO conditional:** `div {...; padding:0;"}` has a stray `"` before the closing brace. Outlook may drop that declaration.
- **Image payload:** the banner URLs request `wid=1200&hei=1600&qlt=100`, which is a large JPEG at maximum quality for a 600px layout.
- **Web font source:** the Archivo `@font-face` URLs point to `fonts.gstatic.com/s/archivo/v7/...`. The stack falls back to `Arial,sans-serif`, so this is low risk.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible HTML shows no unresolved tokens such as `{{...}}`, `%%...%%` or `[FIRSTNAME]`. The source is truncated, so the rest of the body is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance
- The QA flags **List-Unsubscribe** and **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)** as not found, and **Authentication-Results** as not found. The tool itself says these may be relay artifacts, so none of the three is a confirmed defect.
- Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe headers. Verify them on the raw message at the origin, not through AgentMail.
- The footer unsubscribe link and physical address aren't visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm CAN-SPAM body requirements. The QA says an unsubscribe link was probed but reports no failure.
- The sender `DSG@e.dcsg.com` is a dedicated subdomain. There is no evidence of misalignment.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be assessed. The CTA hrefs aren't in the truncated source, and 52 links were skipped.
- Within the visible source, the "Biggest Sale of the Year / up to 50% off" subject maps to the `50-off-banner-dks2026-1115-11` asset. That filename suggests a Nov 15 creative.
- The hero banners have no alt text, so landing-page and offer messaging can't be verified from text.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 7 banner images. In particular, put the offer text ("Up to 50% off") in the alt of `50-off-banner-dks2026-1115-11`. Use `alt=""` for the 5 decorative footer icons.
2. Fix the JSON-LD block, which has several problems:
   - It contains `//` comments, which are invalid JSON, so Gmail will likely ignore the whole block.
   - It still has the template placeholder "DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!" with `"image": ""`.
   - `discountCode` and `availabilityEnds` are empty.
   - `availabilityStarts` is the stale `2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00`.
   - Populate it correctly or remove it.
3. Remove the leftover `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` placeholder and the orphan `<!--/GMAIL PREVIEW CODE-->` closing comment. Fix the stray `"` in the MSO `div{}` rule.
4. Raise the mobile minimums for `.legal` (8px), `.body-sm` and `.link-sm` (9px) to about 11–12px or more.
5. Request the raw `.eml` from the sender-side seed or ESP. Use it to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
6. Re-run link probing on the full CTA set, including the unsubscribe link, and capture the `utm_*` parameters and final landing URLs.
7. Consider lowering the banner JPEG request from `qlt=100` and `1200×1600` to a size closer to the rendered dimensions.
