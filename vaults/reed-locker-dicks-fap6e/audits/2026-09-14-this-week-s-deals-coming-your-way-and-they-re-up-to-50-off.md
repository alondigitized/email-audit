# This week's deals coming your way (and they're up to 50% OFF)
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-14**
## Executive summary

- DICK'S runs its go-to "UP TO 50% OFF" red-gradient hero again — a near-identical creative to the 9/12 and 9/13 sends, just retitled "This Week's Deals." The hero itself is legible (bold white type, high contrast red block) and the layout below is a clean, well-organized catalog scroll: category pills, then a long stack of individual deal modules (shoes, fleece, soccer gear, YETI, NFL gear, golf balls, bikes) each with its own "Shop Now" button. It's competent execution, but there's no single headline offer, no urgency, and no differentiation from the last several sends in this brand's daily clearance/deals cadence.
- The subject line — "This week's deals coming your way (and they're up to 50% off)" — is generic and long, reading more like filler copy than a real hook, and it duplicates the "up to 50% off" claim the brand has used verbatim across at least four recent sends. This is a maintenance email, not a driver of incremental opens.
- Verdict: solid production quality, zero freshness. This is the brand coasting on a template rather than making a case for why today's email matters.

## What's working

- Hero is high-contrast and instantly readable — "UP TO 50%" in large white type on red/photo background.
- Clear green "Shop Now" CTA repeated consistently across every module, so scanning is easy.
- Category navigation pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Team Sports) up top help readers self-route.
- Good product variety and modular structure (YETI new-launch callout, golf BOGO, bike sale, curated picks) gives multiple entry points for different shoppers.
- Order-by-tomorrow shipping banner at the top adds a small credible utility signal (same-day pickup/delivery).

## What's weak

- No single, prioritized offer — the hero says "50% off" but the specific deals below (soccer gear 40%, golf BOGO, bikes 30%) all compete for attention, diluting the headline claim.
- Subject line is vague and long ("This week's deals coming your way") — it's the fourth+ consecutive send anchored to "up to 50% off" language with no new hook.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere in the visible copy — no countdown, no "ends tonight," nothing time-bound like recent sends have used.
- Preview text isn't distinguishable from boilerplate in this render — nothing complementing the subject is visible.
- This is a near-clone of the 9/12–9/13 red hero treatment; a subscriber who's opened those recently will recognize this as the same email reshuffled.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero offer and commit to it — either lead with "Up to 50% Off" alone with a hard deadline, or lead with a single flagship deal (e.g., the golf BOGO or bike sale) instead of stacking every category into one generic "This Week's Deals" umbrella.
- 2. Add real urgency copy under the hero — even a simple "Ends Sunday" beats no deadline at all, since every deal here reads evergreen.
- 3. Differentiate the subject line from the brand's own recent history — stop reusing "up to 50% off" verbatim; call out a specific category or the YETI drop instead.
- 4. Tighten the preview text to name a concrete product or deadline rather than repeating the subject's vague framing.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New YETI Riverhead drop + up to 50% off this week`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Buy 3, Get 1 Free Maxfli balls — plus 50% off ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Soccer gear, bikes, and the new YETI collection — all discounted this week.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free-shipping threshold applies. Deals end Sunday at midnight.`
- 5. Move the golf BOGO or YETI "Just Launched" module higher — those are the only genuinely fresh hooks in the email and they're buried mid-scroll.
