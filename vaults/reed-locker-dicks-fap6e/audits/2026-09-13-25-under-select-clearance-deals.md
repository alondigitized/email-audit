# $25 & under 🤝 select clearance DEALS
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-13**
## Executive summary

- This is the cleanest hero DICK'S has run in this recent stretch, and it's referenced again in the brand-history log for exactly that reason: "$25 AND UNDER" in big white type on a dark red gradient photo background is instant and legible, no cropping issues. But the email buries its own good idea almost immediately — right below the hero it downshifts into the generic "Clearance Deals... Score low prices on select styles" module, then spends the next six screens on category carousels (Men's/Women's/Kids' at "up to 50% off," drinkware, team sports, fan shop) that have nothing to do with the "$25 and under" promise in the subject and hero.
- The structural problem is the same one flagged in the 9/10 and 9/8 audits: this is a template reshuffle, not a new campaign. The "UP TO 50% OFF" pampas-grass red-frame banner reappears again mid-email, meaning the send actually contains two competing headline offers ($25-and-under vs. 50%-off) with no reconciliation — a reader who opens for the $25 promise never actually gets a dedicated $25 product rail.
- Net: strong subject/hero hook, weak follow-through. This scores a touch above the recent baseline on hero clarity but loses ground on offer consistency and freshness of the body content.

## What's working

- The hero — "$25 AND UNDER" in huge bold white sans-serif on a dark red/maroon textured background — is the most legible, scroll-stopping graphic DICK'S has sent recently.
- Clean top utility bar (SCORECARD rewards line, "Order Today, Sport It Tomorrow" same-day delivery note) gives a concrete, time-relevant reason to act beyond the discount.
- Category quick-links ("Men's / Women's / Kids' / Shoes / All Clearance / All Sale") right under the hero give fast wayfinding for a shopper who isn't there for $25 items specifically.
- No render bugs — all product photography (apparel, sneakers, drinkware, jerseys) is intact and properly cropped.

## What's weak

- The subject and hero promise "$25 and under," but there is no dedicated "$25 and under" product grid anywhere in the body — the shopper is redirected into generic 50%-off category carousels instead.
- A second, visually competing hero ("UP TO 50% OFF" over pampas grass) appears mid-email — the same graphic already used in the 9/8 and 9/10 sends — creating two unreconciled headline offers in one email.
- The email is extremely long (7+ full-width modules: apparel x3, drinkware, team sports, fan shop, "this week's deals," picks grid) before reaching footer utilities, which buries the actual hook.
- "Score low prices on select styles before they disappear for good!" is vague filler copy sitting directly under the specific, punchy hero — a tonal drop-off.

## Recommendations

- 1. Build an actual "$25 and under" product rail immediately below the hero (6-8 SKUs with visible prices) — right now the hero writes a check the body doesn't cash, which is the single biggest gap.
- 2. Cut the email roughly in half — collapse the Men's/Women's/Kids' carousels into one combined "Shop Clearance by Category" module with the three thumbnails side by side instead of three full-width repeats.
- 3. Remove or relocate the "UP TO 50% OFF" pampas-grass banner — it's a repeat asset from 9/8 and 9/10 and directly competes with the $25 hero for attention.
- 4. Replace the generic "Score low prices on select styles" subhead with something concrete, e.g. "Tees, socks, accessories & more — all $25 or less."
