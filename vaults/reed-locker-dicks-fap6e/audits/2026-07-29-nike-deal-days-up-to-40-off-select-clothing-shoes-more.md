# Nike Deal Days 🤝 Up to 40% OFF (select clothing, shoes & MORE!)
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-29**
## Executive summary

- DICK'S runs the "Nike Deal Days" campaign back a third time in four days — the same "Nike Deal Days" subject line already ran on 2026-07-26 (scored 9.00/10 there for being the first genuinely different creative in the stretch), and now it's back with an emoji swapped in and a percentage bumped to 40%. The hero itself is still strong: locker-room backdrop, laid-out Nike apparel, a clean red "DEAL DAYS" badge, and a single unambiguous green SHOP NOW button — that part hasn't gotten worse.
- Where this send undercuts itself is what happens after the fold. Instead of staying disciplined on the Nike story, it cascades into eight-plus unrelated modules — Stanley tumblers, cleats, golf electronics, camp chairs, dumbbells, a "Sports Mom" lifestyle block — and then drops a competing **70% Off Summer Clearance** banner two-thirds of the way down, which is a bigger number than the hero's 40% and directly steals attention from the thing the subject line promised. That's the same kitchen-sink instinct that worked on 7/28 (9.00/10, no single hero to dilute), but here it actively competes with a strong hero instead of standing alone.
- Net: well-executed top third, self-sabotaging body. A subscriber who already saw "Nike Deal Days" on Sunday has limited reason to open this one, and if they do, the 70% banner buried mid-scroll will pull clicks away from the Nike products the subject actually sold.

## What's working

- The hero is clean and product-forward — real apparel laid out on a locker shelf, not stock lifestyle photography, with a legible "DEAL DAYS" badge.
- Single, unambiguous green "SHOP NOW" CTA directly under the offer headline, with fine print (dates, exclusions) visible rather than hidden.
- ScoreCard loyalty banner and "See How to Earn Points" sit right at the top, reinforcing membership value before any product content.
- Quick category nav (Men's/Women's/Kids'/Shoes/Golf/Back-to-School) gives an easy secondary path for shoppers not sold on Nike specifically.
- No render bugs — every image loads cleanly, no overlapping text or broken layout anywhere in the scroll.

## What's weak

- This is the third "Nike Deal Days" send in four days (2026-07-26 ran the identical subject); a subscriber who opened that one has no new reason to open this.
- The 70% Off Summer Clearance banner mid-email is a bigger, bolder offer than the 40% off Nike hero — it competes with and undercuts the subject line's promise instead of supporting it.
- Every CTA in the email, from cleats to golf electronics to clearance, says the same generic "SHOP NOW" — nothing tells the eye which button leads where without reading the header above it.
- The email sprawls through eight-plus unrelated category blocks (tumblers, chairs, dumbbells, family lifestyle shot) before the footer, diluting the Nike focus the subject line set up.
- No product-level detail (sizing, fit, stock) anywhere near the Nike hero — just "Shop Now" with no specifics on what's actually included.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the 70% Off Clearance block out of this specific send entirely, or move it to its own email — right now it directly competes with and outguns the Nike hero it's supposed to support.
- 2. Vary the creative from the 7/26 "Nike Deal Days" send — new hero photography, different product mix, or a different lead category — so this doesn't read as a straight rerun to anyone who opened three days ago.
- 3. Swap generic "SHOP NOW" for category-specific CTA copy ("Shop Cleats," "Shop Hoodies") so each module's button is scannable on its own.
- 4. Trim the body to 3-4 modules max, all Nike/back-to-school adjacent, instead of eight-plus unrelated categories — let the kitchen-sink approach be its own email like 7/28's was, not bolted onto a focused hero.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New Nike Deal Days styles: up to 40% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Nike hoodies, shorts & cleats — up to 40% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Back-to-school Nike essentials, while sizes last`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Score Nike apparel and shoes before deals end 8/16`

# Technical Audit: DSG "Nike Deal Days" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a standard Epsilon Harmony-templated DSG marketing email with click-tracking redirects on all links. It ships with a broken unsubscribe redirect, invalid/incomplete JSON-LD markup, a CSS syntax error in the Outlook conditional block, and widespread missing alt text.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken unsubscribe link (confirmed by QA):** `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/U/v610000019fad746e3b92428f6e96c66058/000be4b2847043ff0000021ef3a0bcff` resolves to `/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription` — a 404/error page rather than the subscription-management page. This is a functional failure, not just a UX nit.
- All tracked links route through Epsilon Harmony redirect infrastructure (`app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...` and `/O/...`) with an encrypted `__dU__` deep-link token, e.g.: `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/v610000019fad746e3b92428f6e96c66058/000be4b2847043ff0000021ef3a0bcc6/000be4b2-8470-43ff-b6e2-dadab79b6a5b?__dU__=v0G4RBKTXg2Gs8NEX0-RpwlL2_Q96sIof5wvgkGa1VsPIAdb-6PwUuXUbOES4DYwVKlEBH4wAjEqvhvBlpUfxLCgwho1v8uViS`. Destination is opaque prior to redirect resolution — can't verify final landing target from source alone.
- Open tracking pixel present at `<td id="ssl">`: `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/v610000019fad746e3b92428f6e96c66058/000be4b2847043ff00004c5a42963aa1` (correctly hidden via `display:none; max-height:0px`, standard practice).
- QA notes 12 of 62 links were probed (click-likelihood ranked); the other 50 (footer/social/utility) were deprioritized, so their target integrity is unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **CSS syntax error in Outlook/mso conditional block:** the embedded `<style>` inside the `<!--[if (gte mso 9)|(IE)]>` comment contains a stray quote: `div {background-color:inherit; font-size:0; line-height:100%; margin:0; padding:0;"}`. The trailing `;"}` is malformed — the stray `"` before `}` will either be ignored or break subsequent rule parsing in Outlook's Word rendering engine.
- **10 images missing `alt` text** (confirmed by QA), including the SSL/open-tracking pixel and all four hero/product images (`banner.jpg`, `cleats.jpg`, `electro.jpg`, `chair.jpg`) plus footer icons (`icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png`, `icon-location-2025.png`). Screen readers relying on image content (e.g., promo banner text baked into `banner.jpg`) will announce nothing.
- Responsive/mobile CSS (`@media screen and (max-width:480px)`) is well-formed and uses standard `!important` overrides for Gmail/Apple Mail mobile clients; no issues found there.
- Web font (`Archivo`) is loaded via `@font-face` scoped inside `@media screen` (correctly excluded from Outlook/mso, which doesn't support `@font-face` reliably) with system fallback (`Arial,sans-serif`) — no issues found.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) are present in the visible/truncated source. No issues found in the visible portion — note that content past the truncation point was not reviewed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe mechanism is broken** — see §2. This is a CAN-SPAM concern: the unsubscribe link must lead to a working opt-out mechanism, and this one 404s.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** (QA WARN) — may be a capture limitation of the AgentMail relay rather than confirmation of absence; cannot be verified as a true sender-side omission from this data alone.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found** (QA WARN) — same caveat; if genuinely absent, RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is not supported, which major mailbox providers (Gmail/Yahoo) increasingly require for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` header not found** (QA WARN) — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be confirmed from available data.
- **Invalid JSON-LD structured data:** the `application/ld+json` block contains JavaScript-style `//` comments (e.g., `// GIF & WEBP images are not supported and will be filtered out.`), which is not valid JSON. Any consumer (Gmail Promotions markup, schema.org parsers) attempting `JSON.parse` on this block will fail, and the entire structured-data payload — including the `DiscountOffer` type used for promotional rich display — will be dropped.
- **Unfilled template placeholders shipped to production:** the same JSON-LD block has empty `description`, `discountCode`, `availabilityEnds`, and `image` fields, plus a literal internal dev instruction left in the payload: `"DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!"`. This is template scaffolding that was never populated before send.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
All outbound CTA/product links are wrapped in Epsilon Harmony redirect tokens (`/T/.../__dU__=...`) rather than exposing direct UTM-tagged destination URLs in the source. This means:
- UTM parameters (if any) are encoded inside the opaque `__dU__` token and not independently verifiable from HTML source.
- Landing-page alignment cannot be confirmed without following redirects — out of scope for static HTML/QA-log review.
- One data point is available and it's negative: the unsubscribe link's *own* redirect resolves to a broken error path (`/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription`), indicating at least one redirect mapping in this send is misconfigured. This raises the question of whether other redirect mappings in the same batch share the underlying misconfiguration — worth spot-checking the probed CTA links, not just unsubscribe.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the unsubscribe redirect mapping** for token `000be4b2847043ff0000021ef3a0bcff` so it resolves to the subscription-preferences page instead of `/pc/Error/Error`. This is the highest-priority item — it's a compliance-facing broken link.
2. **Remove the `//` comments from the `application/ld+json` block** (or convert to valid JSON without comments) so structured data actually parses; populate the empty `description`, `discountCode`, `availabilityEnds`, and `image` fields, and strip the leftover `"DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!"` instruction text.
3. **Fix the malformed Outlook conditional CSS** — remove the stray `"` in `padding:0;"}` inside the mso `<style>` block.
4. **Add `alt` attributes** to all content images (banner, product shots, footer icons); at minimum use descriptive text for product/promo images and empty `alt=""` for purely decorative footer icons.
5. **Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent** at the MTA level (independent of the AgentMail relay capture limitation) to support one-click unsubscribe per RFC 8058.
6. **Spot-check redirect mappings on other probed CTA links**, since the unsubscribe link's broken mapping suggests a possible batch-level tracking-token misconfiguration in this send.
