# 💸 Up to 40% off select Nike, Jordan, adidas & more is a click away...
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, well-organized DICK'S promo, and the hero is the strongest part. A big gold "UP TO 40% OFF" on deep green is impossible to miss, and the single "Shop Now" button below it is clear. The copy ("Up to 40% Off Top Brands... Nike, Jordan, adidas and Under Armour") matches the subject.
- The email then turns into a long catalog scroll. It has seven or more modules (socks, NCAA, golf, drinkware, racquet, saunas, Holiday Shop) and a product grid. Each module has its own "Shop Now" button and its own offer ("BOGO Free", "Up to 50% Off", "$69.98", "Up to $500 Off"). The hero promises 40% on Nike/Jordan/adidas, but the body mostly sells socks, drinkware and saunas. A sneaker buyer gets almost no footwear to click on.
- It also lands in an odd place in the cadence. In the 24 hours before it, DICK'S sent the 11/16 "One Day. One Amazing SALE!" email, its "Final Hours" follow-up, and two "Up to 70% OFF select clearance" emails. That makes "up to 40%" a weaker offer than the "up to 70%" and "up to 50%" ones already in the inbox. Judgment: a solid, polished send with a strong hero, but it is diluted by its own length and a headline offer that looks smaller than recent ones.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 40% OFF" hero is high-contrast and reads at a glance.
- The headline names the brands (Nike, Jordan, adidas, Under Armour), which is concrete.
- Category pill buttons (Men's, Women's, Kids', Clearance, Fan Shop, All Sale) give quick, low-friction routes.
- The holiday gifting modules and the gift-picks grid are timely for mid-November.
- The footer has useful trust items: Best Price Guarantee, ScoreCard, and Sign Up for Texts.

## What's weak

- The email is very long, with about eight offer modules and eight or more "Shop Now" buttons, so there is no single focus after the hero.
- The Nike/Jordan/adidas promise isn't followed by any shoes or apparel from those brands. The hero image and body don't back up the subject line.
- Offers are inconsistent and compete with each other: 40%, BOGO, 50%, $69.98 and $500 off.
- The CTA copy is generic: every button says "Shop Now" with no noun.
- The fine print under the BOGO module and in the footer is small and dense. The 40% terms aren't visible near the hero.
- The 40% offer is weaker than the 70% clearance and 50% sale sends from the previous 24 hours, which weakens the urgency.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero plus three or four modules. Make the top two modules Nike/Jordan and adidas product with real shoes.
- 2. Make the hero image show the brands. Put a Jordan or Nike sneaker beside the 40% so the image matches the subject.
- 3. Give each module a specific CTA, such as "Shop Nike Deals", "Shop Jordan" or "Shop adidas".
- 4. Add a deadline, for example "Ends Sunday", to give the offer credible urgency.
- 5. Move the fine print for the 40% offer directly under the hero.
- 6. Space out the sends. Don't follow a 70% clearance email with a 40% brand email the same day.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Nike, Jordan & adidas: up to 40% off ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `💸 Up to 40% off sneakers & gear from top brands`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Same-day pickup on Nike, Jordan, adidas & Under Armour. Plus gift ideas for everyone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop shoes, apparel and holiday gifts before the deals change.`

Several compliance and authentication checks could not be verified because of the relay and the truncated source.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- Only 12 of 64 links were probed and 52 were skipped. The QA data shows no probe failures, but the skipped footer, utility and social links are unverified.
- The HTML is truncated, so I can't confirm the tracked link URLs or their redirect behavior. Anchors carry `class="arc-link"` and `data-nl-type`, which points to ESP click-wrapping, but no hrefs are visible.
- The open-tracking pixel is `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1...&tuid=6afb6852f5a17e3d26bd7a90&configId=512ffb51-...`. It is a 1×1 `<img>` placed directly under `<body>`, before the wrapper table. It has no `alt` and no inline `display:block;border:0`, so some clients may render a stray image or gap.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **Plain-text fallback [FAIL]:** the text part is 9 characters. This is a deliverability and accessibility problem, since text-only clients and screen-reader or Apple Watch previews get nothing. It is confirmed by the QA check, though a relay could have stripped the part.
- **Missing alt text on 13 images.** These are the 7 Scene7 banners (`1030-dks-accessories-cozy-cabin-banner`, `2026-1117-dks-stwd-4pm-catchall-ncaa`, `…-catchall-1-new`, `…-catchall-2`, `…-catchall-3`, `50-off-banner-dks2026-1115-11`, `2026-1012-dks-swd-gift-guide-banner`), the 5 icons (`icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`) and the tracking pixel.
  - For the banners, an alt-less image that carries the offer leaves a blank in image-blocked clients and gives screen readers only the filename.
  - The icons presumably sit next to text labels. If so, they should have `alt=""` rather than no attribute.
  - The pixel should also have `alt=""`.
- **Unfilled preheader.** `<td id="ssl">` contains `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` and nothing else. `#pht-spacer` holds only `&zwnj;&nbsp;` padding. No preheader text is supplied, so inbox previews will fall back to the first body text.
- **Authoring-tool leftovers.** `<body class="is-html-mode" data-contenteditable="true">` is editor residue that ships to subscribers. A `<link rel="icon">` in `<head>` has no effect in email.
- Fonts have an Arial fallback and `font-display:swap`, which is fine. Dark-mode meta (`color-scheme`) is not present in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found [WARN].** The QA tool flags that the AgentMail relay may have dropped these headers, so this is **unconfirmed**. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so verify against the raw message at the source.
