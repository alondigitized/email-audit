# These deals are EPIC! Score up to 50% off for a limited time 😎
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-06**
## Executive summary

- This is the same red/maroon stadium-seating "UP TO 50% OFF" hero DICK'S has run every day since 8/30 — it's now shown up under at least six different subject lines ("EPIC," "Score up to 50%," "New deals added," "Send off summer," etc.), and today's is a near carbon copy of 9/4's and 9/5's versions right down to the stacked EXTRA 25% OFF block underneath. Where this send does differentiate itself is body depth: after the two generic percentage blocks, it fans out into a genuinely long, varied product ladder — running shoes, swim, golf electronics, a Stanley 1913 x Caitlin Clark collab, soccer gear, a $10 Scorecard bonus reward, and a "check out these picks" strip with real product thumbnails and prices-adjacent copy.
- The problem is sequencing: the two most generic, least-targeted blocks (the wall-to-wall 50% stadium hero and the 25% clearance block) eat the entire above-the-fold and first scroll, while the actual interesting stuff — the Stanley collab, the loyalty bonus, the personalized picks grid — is buried five to seven screens down. A subscriber skimming on mobile never gets there.
- Judgement: functional but fatigued creative. The subject line escalates urgency ("EPIC," "limited time," 😎) that the brand has now used identically for a week-plus, so it reads as noise rather than signal. Score accordingly — mid-pack, dragged down by repetition, not by execution quality.

## What's working

- The product ladder deep in the email is genuinely diverse (running, swim, golf, drinkware collab, soccer, loyalty reward) with no obvious render bugs anywhere in the scroll.
- The Stanley 1913 x Caitlin Clark block is a real point of novelty this brand hasn't run in prior audited sends — worth pulling higher.
- The Member Exclusive $10 Bonus Reward block is clean, has a clear "Sign In" / "Get Coupon" CTA pair, and correctly gates on loyalty status.
- "Check out these picks" grid shows actual product thumbnails (shoes, backpack, socks, Pokémon TCG tie-in, HOKA shoes) rather than a generic banner.
- Every CTA button ("Shop Now") is visually consistent and easy to spot against white space.

## What's weak

- Hero and second block are the identical red stadium-seat "UP TO XX% OFF" creative flagged repeatedly on 9/1–9/5 — zero visual differentiation from yesterday's send.
- Vague fine print ("Select styles apply," "Exclusions apply," "may not be valid...") stacked under both top blocks with no link to specifics — the classic buried-condition pattern the rubric penalizes.
- The single most novel, on-brand asset (Stanley x Caitlin Clark) is buried roughly two-thirds down the email instead of leading.
- No hero product image until the swim/running blocks — the first full screen is pure text-on-color-block, which reads as low-effort compared to the photography-driven blocks below.
- Subject line urgency language ("EPIC," "limited time") is now stale after a week of nearly identical claims.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap the lead block: put the Stanley 1913 x Caitlin Clark collab or the $10 Scorecard Bonus Reward above the fold instead of the fourth stadium-seating 50%-off hero this week — it's the one thing that's actually new.
- 2. Cut the redundant 50%-off + 25%-off double-stack at the top to one block; use the reclaimed space to surface the "Check out these picks" personalized grid earlier.
- 3. Replace vague fine print with one concrete exclusion line (e.g., "excludes Nike, Jordan, and clearance golf") so the offer reads as honest rather than hedged.
- 4. Rotate hero creative off the red stadium-seat background — it's been the visual identity of every send since 8/30 and is now background noise to a daily opener.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `New drinkware collab, golf tech deals, and a loyalty reward inside`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary

- All links route through Sailthru/ExactTarget-style tracking (`data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/click` and `/mclick`), consistent per-link `tuid` values — standard ESP redirect pattern, no issue with the mechanism itself.
- QA probed 12 of 68 total material links; 56 were deprioritized (footer/utility/social) per click-likelihood ranking — coverage caveat, not a defect.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 9 images confirmed missing `alt` attributes, including 4 content images (`2026-0906-dks-swd-6am-shoes`, `-golf`, `-drink`, `-football`) and 5 icons (sms, sportsmatter, bpg, favorite, location). Screen reader users get no fallback text for these.
- The open-tracking pixel (`.../ee/v1/open?params=...`) also lacks alt text, though this is standard/inconsequential for a 1x1 tracking pixel.
- MSO conditional comments and `@media screen` font-face fallbacks are correctly structured for Outlook/webmail compatibility — no issues found there.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found — no unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{firstName}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) present in the visible HTML source.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — either absent or not surfaced by the AgentMail relay; cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe compliance from headers alone.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058) not found — one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed.
- `Authentication-Results` header not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from this capture.
- Note: QA tooling states an unsubscribe link is always included in the probed footer set, but the specific unsubscribe URL was not surfaced in the truncated source for direct verification.
- These are flagged as WARN (relay-visibility gaps), not confirmed CAN-SPAM violations — recommend verifying at the raw MIME/header level outside the relay.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Links use proprietary ExactTarget/Sailthru-style `params`/`pid`/`tuid`/`configId` tracking rather than standard UTM query parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, etc.) — this is expected for this ESP's redirect architecture, not a defect, but means standard UTM-based attribution tooling won't read these directly without server-side resolution.
- Cannot confirm final landing-page alignment (post-redirect destination) since redirect targets resolve server-side and aren't visible in the truncated static source.

7. Recommendations
- Fix or remove the "Get Coupon" CTA link returning HTTP 403 — currently a dead conversion path.
- Add descriptive `alt` text to the 4 content images and 5 icon images flagged above for accessibility/screen-reader compliance.
- Add a proper multipart plain-text MIME alternative (current fallback is 9 characters — effectively empty), which also hurts spam-filter scoring.
- Verify `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers exist at the raw MIME level (outside the relay) to confirm RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe compliance.
- Verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment via raw `Authentication-Results` header directly from the sending MTA, since the relay isn't surfacing it.
