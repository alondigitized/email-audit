# 📣 Last call, ScoreCard members! ⏳ Earn 2X Points only in the app now...
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the same "Mobile Monday 2X Points" beat DICK'S already ran earlier the same day (2026-07-27, "ScoreCard exclusive! 2X Points on your app purchase TODAY ONLY," 9.00/10) — except this time the subject leads with "Last call" urgency for that offer, and the email itself buries it. The actual hero at the top of the render is "Nike Deal Days: Up to 40% Off," a completely different campaign; the 2X Points/Mobile Monday block the subject promises doesn't show up until the second content section, well below the fold.
- That's a real mismatch: a reader who opens because of "Last call... Earn 2X Points" and lands on a Nike apparel hero will likely bounce before scrolling to find the thing they clicked for. Below the 2X Points block, the email sprawls into six more unrelated promos (drinkware, cleats, golf electronics, a patio chair, 70% off clearance, 50% off deals) — this reads less like a targeted "last call" and more like the full weekly catalog with an urgent subject line bolted on.
- Judgment: solid production values and a real loyalty offer buried under subject/body mismatch and end-of-week promo fatigue. Call it a 6 — competent execution, poor prioritization.

## What's working

- The Nike Deal Days hero (models in the pink shorts/grey hoodie/navy jacket set against the Nike swoosh) is clean, on-brand, and legible with a clear "SHOP NOW" button.
- The 2X Points block uses a real visual metaphor (phone screen showing "2X POINTS" mid-scan) rather than a generic banner, and it's tagged "Today Only" with a hard deadline (7/27/26 11:59PM PT).
- The category nav grid (MEN'S / WOMEN'S / KID'S / SHOES / GOLF / BACK-TO-SCHOOL) right under the hero gives restless scrollers an escape hatch without hunting.
- No render bugs — every image loads, text doesn't overlap, and the footer app-download/social-icon block is intact.

## What's weak

- Subject promises "Last call... 2X Points... in the app now," but the email opens with an unrelated Nike apparel hero — the promised offer is buried in section two.
- This is effectively a same-day rerun of the earlier "ScoreCard exclusive! 2X Points" send from 2026-07-27 — two emails chasing the same offer within hours reads as inbox spam to a subscriber, not urgency.
- The email is extremely long (10+ distinct offers: Nike, drinkware, cleats, golf electronics, a patio chair, 70% clearance, 50% weekly deals, plus a product grid) — there's no single clear priority after the fold, undermining the "last call" urgency framing entirely.
- Subject line is ~71 characters with two emojis and a trailing ellipsis — it'll truncate on most mobile inboxes before the actual offer detail lands.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Make the subject match the hero.** If "Last call, 2X Points" is the message, put the phone/2X-Points creative as the top hero, not Nike Deal Days. Right now the send over-promises and under-delivers on open.
- 2. **Don't re-send the same offer same-day.** If Mobile Monday 2X Points already went out earlier as its own send, this "last call" version should be a short, single-offer reminder — not a full 10-block catalog dump.
- 3. **Cut the email in half.** Everything past "70% Off Summer Clearance" (drinkware, cleats, golf electronics, patio chair) dilutes urgency. Move it to a separate weekly-deals send.
- 4. **Trim the subject.** Drop one emoji and the trailing ellipsis to get under 50 characters so it doesn't truncate on mobile.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 2X ScoreCard Points, app only ⏳`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Few hours left — 2X Points on your next app buy`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Redeem in the DICK'S app before 11:59PM PT tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `ScoreCard members: your 2X Points window closes tonight.`

# Technical Audit — DICK'S "Mobile Monday" Email (2026-07-27)

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard Epsilon Harmony/ESP template with Scene7/Harmony-hosted images and tokenized redirect tracking, but ships with a broken unsubscribe link, malformed JSON-LD, and no alt text on any image.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken unsubscribe redirect**: `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/U/v610000019fa532036c8f7704f4bbe5be50/c8c6e06b4e7f40a50000021ef3a0bd01` resolves to `/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription` — an unhandled server-side error page, not the preference/unsubscribe form (QA: `[FAIL] Link error: unknown url type`).
- All other probed links use the same ESP tracking wrapper pattern (`app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...` with an encrypted `__dU__` payload) — consistent with standard redirect tracking; 12 of 66 links were probed by click-likelihood ranking (QA info note), so long-tail footer/social links weren't individually verified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed IE/MSO conditional CSS**: the `<!--[if (gte mso 9)|(IE)]>` block's inline `<style>` contains a stray character: `div {background-color:inherit; ... padding:0;"}` — the trailing `;"}` includes an unmatched quote, corrupting the rule for Outlook/IE rendering.
- **JSON-LD contains inline `//` comments** (`// GIF & WEBP images are not supported...`, `// Sample image is 538x138...`, `// DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!`) — this is not valid JSON; any strict JSON-LD/schema.org parser (including Google's structured data parser) will fail to parse the `<script type="application/ld+json">` block entirely.
- **All 10 flagged images are missing `alt` text**, including the SSL/open-tracking pixel, hero banner (`dks-banner.jpg`), promo banners, and footer value-prop icons (`icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png`, `icon-location-2025.png`) — screen readers will announce nothing for these, and images blocked by default in most mail clients render as blank space.
- One image (`banner4.gif`) is a `.gif` — the email's own JSON-LD comment explicitly notes "GIF & WEBP images are not supported and will be filtered out," meaning this asset is expected to be stripped by the ESP's own image pipeline.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tags (e.g. `%%FIRSTNAME%%`, `{{...}}`) are present in the visible source. However, the JSON-LD `DiscountOffer` (`"description": ""`, `"discountCode": ""`, `"availabilityEnds": ""`) and `PromotionCard` (`"image": ""`) objects are unfilled template placeholders — these are shipped empty rather than removed or populated, indicating a template field was left blank at send time.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe link is functionally broken** (see §2) — this is a CAN-SPAM risk since the required unsubscribe mechanism does not resolve.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** (QA WARN) — may be a relay/capture artifact of AgentMail rather than confirmed absence, but as observed, one-click unsubscribe support cannot be verified.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found** (QA WARN) — RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe cannot be confirmed as supported.
- **`Authentication-Results` header not found** (QA WARN) — SPF/DKIM pass/fail status is unknown from this capture; cannot confirm authentication compliance.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be fully assessed — the HTML source is truncated before revealing CTA/offer destination URLs (only the header logo link and SSL tracking pixel are visible in the provided excerpt). The visible logo link routes through the same `app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...` tracking redirect used elsewhere; no UTM parameters are visible on the destination side of any redirect in the truncated excerpt, so UTM presence/landing-page alignment cannot be confirmed or denied with current evidence.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the unsubscribe link target so it resolves to the subscription preference page instead of `/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription`.
2. Strip the `//` comments from the `application/ld+json` block or move them outside the script tag — the block is currently invalid JSON.
3. Remove the stray `"` in the MSO conditional `<style>` block (`padding:0;"}` → `padding:0;}`).
4. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, and empty `alt=""` (not omitted) to the tracking pixel and purely decorative icons.
5. Replace or remove the `.gif` asset (`banner4.gif`), since the template's own comment confirms it will be filtered by the image pipeline.
6. Populate or remove the empty `DiscountOffer`/`PromotionCard` JSON-LD placeholder fields before send.
7. Confirm SPF/DKIM/`Authentication-Results` and `List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually present at the SMTP layer (independent of the AgentMail capture) to verify CAN-SPAM/RFC 8058 compliance.
