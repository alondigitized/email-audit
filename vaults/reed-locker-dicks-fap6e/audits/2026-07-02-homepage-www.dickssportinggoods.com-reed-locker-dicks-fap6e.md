# Homepage snapshot · www.dickssportinggoods.com · 2026-07-02
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-02**
## Executive summary

- I pulled up Dick's on my phone and got a giant error page — "Oops, Something Went Wrong." No hero, no products, no navigation, nothing. The site is completely down from where I'm sitting. That's the whole story here: Dick's just lost me at the door before I even got inside.

## What's working

- The Dick's logo renders cleanly — I at least know I'm on the right site before the gut-punch error.

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is a dead end.** "Oops, Something Went Wrong. We are working on the problem. Please try accessing the site again after 12 hours." Twelve hours? That's not a maintenance window, that's a lost Saturday of shopping.
- **Exposed internal error token.** The page literally prints `Error: 0.2ca7cb17.1782987831.33198b86 IP: 75.140.9.0` in bold. That's sloppy and it erodes trust instantly — I'm not a dev, and even I know you're not supposed to show that to customers.
- **No fallback CTA.** Not a "find a store near you," not a "download the app," not a "call us." Just a gray void below the error card.
- **No ETA credibility.** "12 hours" is vague and feels like a canned message. If the site's actually in maintenance, give me a real timestamp.

## Recommendations

- **Add a failover static page** with a store-locator link and app download CTA — if the main site blows up, at least route me somewhere useful instead of a dead gray screen.
- **Strip the raw error token from user-facing copy** — `0.2ca7cb17...` means nothing to me and looks like a security leak.
- **Replace "try again in 12 hours" with a specific timestamp** ("Back online by 8 PM ET") — that's the difference between me bookmarking and me going to Academy Sports instead.
- **Add a loyalty/email capture fallback** — "Site's down, but drop your email and we'll send you a heads up plus 10% off when we're back" turns a dead end into a list-builder.
