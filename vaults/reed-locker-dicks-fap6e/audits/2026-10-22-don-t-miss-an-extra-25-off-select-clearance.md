# 🚨 Don't miss an extra 25% off select clearance! 🚨
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, loud clearance email with an unmistakable hero. A red block reads "EXTRA 25% OFF" at the top, with "Extra 25% Off Select Clearance Styles" and a green Shop Now button directly under it. The eye lands on the number first. It's the same offer and nearly the same creative as this morning's "Ready for an EXTRA 25% off select clearance? 💰 Shop now!"
- Below the hero the email turns into a long stack of category blocks: clothing and shoes, drinkware, NFL/NCAA gear, golf, outdoor, fitness, and a "This Week's Deals" 50% off banner. Each block has its own "Up to 40%" or "50%" headline. The extra 25% off clearance is the only offer that matters, and it gets buried under six competing discounts. The email also doesn't say which clearance items qualify.
- Judgement: competent and on-brand, but it's the second send of the day for the same 25% clearance promo. The 🚨 emoji subject is louder and more spam-flavored than the earlier send. The email carries a real offer and a clear hero, but it isn't a fresh reason to open.

## What's working

- The hero is huge and legible: "EXTRA 25% OFF" in white on red, with the number dominating.
- The green Shop Now button right under the hero headline is clear and easy to find.
- The category chips (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 & Under, Fan Shop, All Sale) give quick routes in.
- Product photography is clean and varied, with no broken images.
- The "One Day Only" tag on the NFL/NCAA block adds a real urgency beat.
- The footer carries useful trust elements: ScoreCard, price guarantee, and app download.

## What's weak

- This is a repeat. It's the second extra-25%-clearance send today, after "Ready for an EXTRA 25% off select clearance?", and it follows three weekly-deals sends in the last two days.
- The email is far too long. Seven or eight stacked offers dilute the one headline offer.
- The hero's "Limited Time" tag has no deadline visible near the button. The dates are only in tiny fine print below.
- The fine print is dense, and the ScoreCard member benefit is barely visible.
- The "This Week's Deals 50% off" banner near the bottom competes with, and partly contradicts, the clearance message.
- The "Check out these picks" row is generic. The products are unpriced and have no clear link to the clearance offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero plus two or three clearance-specific modules. Show actual clearance products with the sale price and "extra 25% applied" pricing. Move the generic category stack to a different send.
- 2. Put a deadline in the hero, such as "Ends Sunday 11:59 PM PT", and make the CTA specific: "Shop clearance now".
- 3. Suppress or consolidate this send against the morning one. Two identical offers in one day trains people to ignore the next one.
- 4. Add a ScoreCard line near the hero if members get anything extra.
- 5. Drop the 🚨 emojis. Lead with the concrete offer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 25% off clearance ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your extra 25% off clearance is still on`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sneakers, apparel and gear marked down again. Extra 25% comes off at checkout.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Order today, pick up in store tomorrow. Sizes are going fast.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally sound (`lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` layout tables, MSO conditionals, mobile media queries). The email fails the plain-text fallback check, and most content images lack alt text. Header-based compliance and authentication checks can't be confirmed from this capture.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- The QA probe covered 12 material links and skipped 60. It reported no broken or redirecting links, but 60 links, mostly footer, utility and social, were not verified.
- The open-tracking pixel is `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1...` (`width="1" height="1"`). It has no `alt` and no inline `style` or `border="0"`. It's the first element in `<body>`, outside any table, so some clients may render a stray blank line. This is minor.
- Links use `class="arc-link"` and `data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"`. Because the HTML is truncated, I can't confirm whether the hrefs are wrapped tracking URLs.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text part (QA FAIL):** the text version is 9 characters. Clients that prefer text/plain, and spam filters that compare the two parts, will see an effectively empty message.
- **Missing alt text (QA WARN, 11 content images plus the pixel):**
  - Hero and category images: `clothing-2026-1022-swd-4pm`, `banner4-2026-1022-swd-4pm`, `nfl-2026-1022-swd-4pm`, `golf-2026-1022-swd-4pm`, `outdoor-2026-1022-swd-4pm`, `fitness-2026-1022-swd-4pm`.
  - Icons: `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`.
  - If these images are blocked or read by a screen reader, the promotional content (the "extra 25% off" offer) isn't conveyed.
  - The six category and banner images appear to carry the offer, so they matter most.
- **Preheader:** `#pht-spacer` contains only `&zwnj;&nbsp;` filler and no visible preheader text. Inbox preview text will fall back to body copy, such as the header or nav text.

- **Title:** `<title>DICK'S Sporting Goods</title>` is generic and not specific to this campaign. This is low impact.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible HTML. The source is truncated, so the rest is unverified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe (WARN) and List-Unsubscribe-Post / RFC 8058 (WARN):** neither header was found. QA notes that the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a proven defect. Gmail and Yahoo require both for bulk senders (5,000+ messages a day).
- **Authentication-Results (WARN):** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain is `em.dcsg.com`. QA attributes the missing header to the relay.
- **Footer unsubscribe and postal address:** the QA probe always includes an unsubscribe link and flagged nothing. The footer is outside the truncated HTML, so I can't verify the physical address.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The truncated HTML shows no destination URLs, and the QA data doesn't report UTM parameters or landing-page status for the 12 probed links. I'm not flagging a problem, only noting the gap.

2. **Add descriptive `alt` text** to the six category and banner images (for example "Extra 25% off select clearance – NFL"). Use `alt=""` for the decorative icons and the tracking pixel, and add `style="display:block;border:0"` to the pixel.
