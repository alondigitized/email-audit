# Up to 40% off select clothing & shoes (Nike, CALIA & more!)
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S catch-all category roundup. It starts with "Up to 40% off select clothing & shoes" and then runs seven more sections: drinkware, fan gear, team sports, golf, outdoor, fitness and a 75% clearance block. The subject names Nike and CALIA, which makes it feel more specific than the last few sends. But the body spreads across so many offers that the eye has nowhere to settle.
- The hero is a clean three-image collage with a red Shop Now button and a row of category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Team Sports, Fan Shop). It's well built, but the fashion-and-apparel promise is diluted below the fold. Drinkware leads, then fan gear and golf, and each section carries a different percentage (40%, 50%, 75%). That looks more like a catalog than a campaign.
- Compared with the Oct 25 "Stop scrolling! Up to 75% OFF" send and the Oct 24 "Final. Hours." clearance email, this one has no countdown and no single bold number. It's useful for browsing but weak on urgency. Verdict: solid, forgettable, a 6.

## What's working

- The subject names Nike and CALIA, so the apparel and shoe offer is concrete.
- The hero collage shows apparel and a sneaker, and a clear red Shop Now button sits right below the headline.
- The category pills let a shopper jump straight to Men's, Women's, Kids' or Shoes.
- The top strip says "Order Today. Sport & Tomorrow" and promises same-day pickup, which is a real convenience benefit.
- The 75% clearance block with the big red leaf graphic gives the page one strong visual anchor near the bottom.

## What's weak

- The email has eight offer blocks with different percentages, so no single hero offer dominates.
- The strong "UP TO 75% OFF" block sits near the bottom, below a lot of scrolling, and it competes with the 40% headline.
- Nothing is time-bounded in the render. There is no countdown or end date, so there's no reason to act now.
- Some sections carry two or three small buttons (Club Deals, Clothing Deals, Shoe Deals), which splits clicks.
- The product picks near the end show no prices or ratings.
- The footer is dense, and the fine print is tiny.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a single message. Keep "Up to 40% off clothing & shoes" as the hero and cut the lower sections to two or three. Move the 75% clearance block up or drop it.
- 2. Add a deadline, such as "Ends Sunday" or a countdown bar like the Oct 24 send. Without it there's no urgency.
- 3. Show prices or "from $" on the product picks, along with ratings, so they sell instead of just displaying.
- 4. Use one button per section, and keep the pills as the only secondary navigation.
- 5. Make the Nike and CALIA logos or products visible in the hero, since the subject promises them.

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The visible markup is structurally sound: it has `lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` layout tables, MSO conditionals and a responsive media query. The one confirmed failure is a missing plain-text part (9 characters), and 13 content images have no alt text. The HTML was truncated, so anything below the header (hrefs, footer) could not be checked.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- No broken links were reported. 12 links were probed and 60 skipped, so about 83% of links are unverified.
- The open-tracking pixel (`https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=...`) is a 1x1 `<img>` with no `alt`. It has no inline `style`, and its `border`/`display:block` attributes are unset. It sits directly in `<body>`, outside the wrapper table.
- Some asset filenames look like they belong to other campaigns: `banner4.gif` (`2026-0623-dks-outdoor-40-off-drinkware`), `fanshop-2026-0929-dks-swd-6am`, `outdoor-2026-0914-dks-swd-4pm` and `2026-1002-dks-storewide-4pm-2-new`. They may be hidden dynamic-content variants. The source shown can't confirm either way, so this needs a manual check.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Alt text:** 13 content images are missing alt text. This includes the main hero images (`2026-1026-dks-storewide-4pm-hero`, `2026-1026-dks-storewide-4pm-1`, `2026-1026-dks-storewide-4pm-2-new`) and the footer icons (`icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`). If images are blocked, the offer content disappears. Screen readers will read the filenames or skip the images.
- **Plain text:** the text version is 9 characters ([FAIL]). Text-only clients and some spam filters get no content.
- **Preheader:** the `#ssl` cell holds the leftover `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` comment followed directly by the `#pht-spacer` div, which contains only `&zwnj;&nbsp;` padding. No preheader text is visible in the source shown, so clients will likely use the first visible body text as the inbox preview.
- **Editor leftovers:** `class="is-html-mode"` and `data-contenteditable="true"` are still on `<body>`. They are harmless but show the HTML came straight from the editor.
- **Hero image size:** hero image URLs request `wid=1200&hei=1600`, which is heavy for a 584px container. Check that the rendered width is constrained.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **List-Unsubscribe** and **List-Unsubscribe-Post** (RFC 8058) were not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Verify against the raw message at the origin, because Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results** is absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `em.dcsg.com` could not be assessed. This is also likely a relay artifact.
- The footer, physical address and unsubscribe link are in the truncated part of the source and could not be confirmed. The probe was set to always include an unsubscribe link, and no failure was reported.

No issues confirmed. The CTA hrefs are truncated (`<a ... class="arc-link" data-nl-type="…`). I couldn't check UTM parameters or whether the landing pages match the "up to 40% off clothing & shoes (Nike, CALIA)" subject. No probed link was reported as redirecting incorrectly or failing.

1. Add a real plain-text part. It should include the offer, key CTAs and the unsubscribe link.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 13 images, starting with the hero images. Use `alt=""` for decorative images, and add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
