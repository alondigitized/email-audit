# 💲 Clearance savings you'll want to see 💲 EXTRA 25% off select styles
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, loud clearance send, and the hero lands at once. A red diagonal-striped panel with giant gold "EXTRA 25% OFF" fills the first screen. "Limited Time," the headline "Extra 25% Off Select Clearance Styles" and a maroon Shop Now button sit right under it. The offer reads in about a second.
- The body is the problem. It stacks a second red hero ("Up to 50% Off," "Ends Tonight"), then a "Up to 40% off clothing & shoes" block, then two category grids and a service footer. That is four competing offers in one email. The "Explore more deals" pills and the 50% block pull attention away from the clearance message the subject promises.
- This is the same recipe DICK'S sent on 11/11 ("Want an EXTRA 25% off? Shop select clearance & SAVE!") and the same Holiday Deal Dash 50% block from this morning's 11/13 send. A subscriber who got both today sees a recycled middle half. The hero is strong, but the email is a long catalog with no single product to click.

## What's working

- The giant gold "EXTRA 25% OFF" on red is legible at thumbnail size and has no render bugs.
- "Limited Time" in red, plus fine print giving the 11/13/26 11:59PM ET deadline, makes the urgency credible.
- The Shop Now button is a high-contrast maroon, easy to spot, and appears right under the hero.
- The "Explore more deals" pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 & Under, Fan Shop, All Sale) let shoppers jump to their own category.
- The footer carries useful trust items: ScoreCard points at the top, a Best Price Guarantee, and app download badges.

## What's weak

- The hero never says what is in the clearance. There are no products, brands or categories, so "select styles" is vague.
- The "Up to 50% Off Holiday Deal Dash" block repeats this morning's send and undercuts the 25% hero, because 50% beats 25% in the reader's eye.
- The "Up to 40% off clothing & shoes" block adds a third percentage. Three numbers (25%, 50%, 40%) in one email make the offer feel muddled.
- The Drinkware, Accessories, Golf, Fan Shop, Team Sports and Fitness grids carry no price, offer or CTA, so they are decoration.
- The fine print ("select clearance only," "exclusions apply," "interim markdowns may have been taken") sits in small grey text under the button, which weakens the "honest offer" feel.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the clearance story. Drop the 50% and 40% blocks, which duplicate other sends, and put 3-4 real clearance products below the hero with was/now prices. This one change would lift clicks the most.
- 2. Name the category in the hero ("Extra 25% off clearance shoes & apparel") so the "select styles" vagueness goes away.
- 3. Move the fine print closer to the offer, or add a one-line "what's excluded" summary, so the deal feels honest.
- 4. If the Deal Dash block stays, make it a small text link so it doesn't compete with the hero.
- 5. Don't send a near-identical clearance email two days after the 11/11 one. Give each send a distinct angle.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 25% off clearance ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Clearance shoes & gear: extra 25% off, today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ends 11:59PM ET. Nike, Under Armour & more, marked down again.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Extra 25% comes off already-low clearance prices. Shop before it's gone.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is structurally sound (`lang`, `<title>`, `role="presentation"` tables, viewport meta). However, 10 of the 12 probed tracked links failed (9 read timeouts and one 403), so destinations could not be verified. There are also widespread missing image alt attributes and invalid or leftover template code in the head.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **9 of 12 probed links timed out.** The links are `app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/v61000001a1d5b6d986a207666e96c660f0/.../70dac142-d439-4d02-8bba-5fcbbf6d1ff6?__dU__=...`.
  - Affected link text: "Shop Now" (×3), "Fan Shop", "Download on the App Store.", "Get it on Google Play.", "Scorecard", "Extra 25% off.", "Men's".
  - The primary promo CTA ("Extra 25% off.") and both app-store links are among them.
- **1 link returned 403.** The header logo link, "Dick's Sporting Goods", `.../70dac142d4394d020000021ef3a0bcc6/...`, returned 403.
- **Cause not confirmed.** Timeouts and 403s on ESP click-tracker redirects are consistent with bot or rate protection against the QA prober. They are also consistent with real tracker latency. This QA run cannot tell which, so these are unverified rather than confirmed broken.
- **Coverage is limited.** Only 12 links were probed and 40 were skipped.
- **Per-recipient identifier in every URL.** The `__dU__=` token is long and unique to the recipient. It is normal for this kind of tracker, but it means the links are not shareable or cacheable, and they can expose recipient identity if forwarded.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 13 images:**
  - Tracking pixel `.../O/v61000001a1d5b6d986a207666e96c660f0/70dac142d4394d0200004c5a42963aa1` (hidden via `display:none`, so low impact).
  - Content images on `s7d6.scene7.com`: `1113-stw-11am-clothing-shoes`, `drinkware1101stwdcondensed`, `accessories1101stwdcondensed`, `golf1101stwdcondensed`, `fanshop1101stwdcondensed`, `teamsports1101stwdcondensed`, `fitness1101stwdcondensed`.
  - Footer icons on `dsg.images.harmony.epsilon.com`: `icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png`, `icon-location-2025.png`.
  - If the content images carry the offers, the offer text is lost when images are blocked. Decorative icons should use `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Sub-legible mobile font sizes** in the `@media screen and (max-width:480px)` block:
  - `.body-sm` and `.link-sm` are 9px.
  - `.legal` is 8px.
  - `.body-m` is 11px.
- **Invalid CSS in the MSO conditional block.** The rule `div {...padding:0;"}` contains a stray `"`. It may invalidate the declaration in Outlook.
- **Invalid JSON-LD.** The `<script type="application/ld+json">` block contains `//` comments, which are not valid JSON, so Gmail will likely ignore the whole block.
- **Gmail promo annotation left unfilled.** The `DiscountOffer` has `"discountCode": ""` and `"availabilityEnds": ""`. `availabilityStarts` is `2020-03-06`, which is stale. The `PromotionCard` has `"image": ""` under the template comment "DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!".

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) were visible in the truncated source. The footer and lower body were not available for inspection.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No confirmed violations.** All three header findings are artifacts of how the QA tool received the message:
  - `List-Unsubscribe` was not found.
  - `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was not found.
  - `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown.
  - The tool notes AgentMail may strip these headers.
- **What would need checking.** The raw message headers should be inspected from an inbox that preserves them, such as Gmail "Show original".
- **Footer content not reviewed.** The physical postal address and the visible unsubscribe link were beyond the truncated HTML, so they could not be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters cannot be verified.** Every link is wrapped in the `app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...` redirector, so UTM and campaign parameters sit behind the redirect.
- **Landing pages not resolved.** With 10 of 12 probes failing, no landing page was confirmed for any promo CTA. Alignment between "EXTRA 25% off select styles" and the destination is untested.
- **Overall pass rate was 19%** (10 issues, 3 warnings), so continuity should be treated as unverified rather than good.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-probe the 10 failed tracked links.** Use a longer timeout, a browser-like User-Agent and retries. Check the "Extra 25% off." and app-store links first. If they still fail, it is a tracker or CDN problem.
2. **Follow the redirect chain** for the primary CTAs to the final URL and confirm UTM parameters and the landing page match the clearance offer.
3. **Add alt text** to all 7 content images, with the offer text for the promo ones. Set `alt=""` on the tracking pixel and footer icons.
4. **Fix the head code:**
   - Remove the `//` comments from the JSON-LD, or drop the block.
   - Populate or remove the empty `discountCode`, `availabilityEnds` and `image` fields.
   - Update the 2020 `availabilityStarts`.
   - Remove the stray `"` in the MSO `div` rule.
5. **Raise the mobile minimums** for `.legal`, `.body-sm` and `.link-sm` (8–9px) to roughly 11–12px.
6. **Verify the headers outside AgentMail.** Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `e.dcsg.com` on the raw message.
