# ScoreCard exclusive 🙌 2X Points on your app order TODAY ONLY
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a strong opening wrapped in a catalog. The hero is a phone showing the ScoreCard logo with a giant "2X POINTS" over a green holiday backdrop. Below it sits "Mobile Monday! Earn 2X Points* on Your App Purchase" and a maroon "Get Offer" button. The loyalty hook is clear and different from the "Holiday Deal Dash" sends this week (11/7 and 11/8), which were all red-striped "UP TO 50% OFF" panels.
- The email then loses focus. Right under the hero is a row of category pills, followed by a second red "UP TO 70% OFF" clearance hero, then six more product modules, a gift-picks grid and a footer. Two competing hero offers, 2X points and 70% off, split attention. The 2X points offer is also gated to the app and has fine print about ScoreCard membership and a deadline of 11/9 at 11:59PM PT. A non-member sees a "Get Offer" button with little explanation of what it does.
- Judgement: a good segmented loyalty send for app-using members, diluted by a long, unfocused body. It's better than the repetitive deal-dash sends, but it doesn't commit to one idea.

## What's working

- The "2X POINTS" hero is large and readable, and it ties to ScoreCard membership.
- "Today Only" plus "Mobile Monday" gives credible same-day urgency.
- It breaks the week's pattern of near-identical red-striped "Up to 50% off" panels.
- Each module has a single clear "Shop Now" button and a tidy layout.
- App download badges near the bottom support the app-order premise.

## What's weak

- The second hero ("UP TO 70% OFF" on clearance) competes with the 2X points offer and shifts the email back to the generic deal-dash feel.
- The "Get Offer" CTA is vague. It doesn't say whether it opens the app, applies the offer or goes to a product page.
- The hero offer's fine print sits right below the button in small text: members only, app only, and the exclusions are buried further down.
- The body runs about 8 modules plus a gift grid, so the loyalty message is lost by the second scroll.
- The 1913 Stanley, NCAA, table tennis and Top Flite modules have no tie to points or the app, so they read as filler.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to a single idea. Keep the 2X points hero and two or three modules that explain how to earn, such as "Open the app → shop → points post in 48 hrs". Drop the 70% clearance hero or move it to its own send.
- 2. Rewrite the CTA to something specific: "Shop in the App" or "Open the App & Earn 2X". Add a second link for non-members: "Join ScoreCard free".
- 3. Show a concrete example of the benefit, such as "$100 order = 200 points" with a dollar reward equivalent.
- 4. Move the key exclusions into one visible line near the button.
- 5. Add an app-only product row, for example top sellers, to reinforce why the app is the place to buy today.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Open the DICK'S app and earn 2X on everything you buy before midnight PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Members earn double today. Not a member? Join free and start earning.`
