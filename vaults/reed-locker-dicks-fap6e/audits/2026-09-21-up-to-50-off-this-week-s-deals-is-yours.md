# 📢 Up to 50% off this week's DEALS is yours!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-21**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S running the exact "twilight-field UP TO 50% OFF" hero it used on the 9/20 "Great news!" send (6.00/10) — same photo, same box, same headline, just a new subject line stapled on. The kitchen-sink body underneath is nearly identical in structure to the 9/20 and 9/19 catalog sends: category pills, then a long vertical stack of Clothing, Team Sports, Drinkware, ScoreCard/3X Points, Fan Shop, Fitness, and a "Check out these picks" product rail — no fewer than 8 distinct offer blocks before the footer.
- The render itself is clean — no broken images, no overlapping text — but there's zero differentiation from what this subscriber has already seen twice this week. The "This Week's Deals" hero doesn't say what's actually on sale (which category, which brands), so the click decision gets pushed all the way down to whichever category tile happens to match the reader's interest.
- Judgement: a competently produced but completely undifferentiated repeat of a creative this brand has now run three times in a week. Cadence fatigue is the dominant story here, not execution quality.

## What's working

- Hero renders cleanly with legible "UP TO 50% OFF" text over the field photo — no crop bugs like the 9/19 "FLAS" incident.
- Category pill row (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Team Sports) gives a fast secondary navigation path if the hero doesn't land.
- ScoreCard 3X Points block is a genuine loyalty-member benefit, visually distinct with its own golf-themed image.
- Footer has clear app download, social, and ScoreCard sign-up CTAs — good account-value reinforcement.

## What's weak

- The hero offer is generic — "This Week's Deals" with no category, brand, or product hint, so there's no reason to click over just browsing the site directly.
- This is the same twilight-field/50%-off hero as the 9/20 "Great news!" email — zero creative rotation in under a week.
- The body is a long undifferentiated scroll of 8+ offer blocks (Clothing, Team Sports, Drinkware, 3X Points, Fan Shop, Fitness, product picks) with no personalization signal for which one matters to this recipient.
- "Order Today, Sport it Tomorrow" same-day delivery banner competes with the hero for top-of-email attention and adds clutter before the reader even sees the offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the generic "This Week's Deals" hero copy with the single strongest concrete offer (e.g., lead with "Up to 40% Off Nike, adidas & Under Armour" or whichever category is actually driving margin) — right now the reader has to scroll to find out what's on sale.
- 2. Rotate the hero creative — three near-identical twilight/50%-off sends in a week trains subscribers to skip without opening.
- 3. Cut the body from 8 stacked category blocks to 3-4, and use send-time personalization to surface the category the recipient has previously browsed/purchased.
- 4. Move the 3X Points ScoreCard block higher — it's the one genuinely differentiated, loyalty-specific offer in the email and it's buried two-thirds down.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your ScoreCard picks: up to 40% off Nike & adidas`
- **Subject Alt B:** `3X Points on TaylorMade — plus up to 50% off gear`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 3X ScoreCard points on TaylorMade, ends soon`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping on team sports gear this week only`
