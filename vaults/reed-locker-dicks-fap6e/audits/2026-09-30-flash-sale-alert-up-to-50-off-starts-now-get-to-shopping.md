# Flash Sale alert 🚨 Up to 50% OFF starts now... get to shopping!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S "Flash Sale" on a Wednesday, and the hero undercuts it. The orange lightning-bolt banner shows "FLAS" cut off at the right edge, so the headline word is visibly truncated. The real offer, "Flash Sale: Up to 50% Off," only appears in small text below the image. The email tries to be urgent ("One Day Only," "valid through 9/30/26 11:59PM PT"), but the creative doesn't deliver it.
- Below the hero it becomes a long stack of unrelated promos: $100 and under shoes, a $19.99 fleece hoodie, 25% off Peloton shoes, NCAA tees, golf clubs, fishing gear, and a $25 and under clearance block. This is the third "up to 50%" or "$25 and under" send in four days, following the 9/27 Deal Drop, the 9/29 "INSIDE: Up to 50% off" send, and the 9/29 "$25 & UNDER" send. The bottom $25 block is the same creative as yesterday's, so a subscriber will read it as recycled. There is no single hero product and no clear hierarchy after the top banner.
- Verdict: a well-organized module stack, but the broken hero, the diluted offer and the fatigue from repeated promos hold it to a middling score.

## What's working

- The "One Day Only" tag and the explicit 9/30 11:59 PM PT deadline give the urgency a credible endpoint.
- Every module has a clear grey "Shop Now" button, so the actions are easy to find.
- The category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Halloween Shop) add quick navigation and a seasonal touch.
- The $19.99 fleece hoodie is a concrete price point, and the "$100 and under" shoe module has real brands (ASICS, New Balance, Nike, adidas).
- The "Check out these picks" product grid adds specific items for browsers.

## What's weak

- The hero headline is cropped to "FLAS", which looks like a render bug and leaves the campaign name unreadable.
- The offer is buried. "Up to 50% Off" is small text under the image, and the hero has no number in it.
- The email has about ten competing offers (50%, $100 and under, $19.99, 25% off, up to $350 off, $25 and under) with no single lead.
- The bottom "$25 AND UNDER" block repeats yesterday's send, which makes the "flash" feel stale.
- The Shop Now buttons are grey and low-contrast, and the primary CTA doesn't stand out from the secondary ones.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the hero so the full "FLASH SALE" and "UP TO 50% OFF" sit inside the visible frame, with a bold, high-contrast button below.
- 2. Cut the module count to 3-4 and lead with one hero product that shows the deal price.
- 3. Drop the recycled "$25 and under" block, or vary the creative so it isn't a repeat of yesterday.
- 4. Put a countdown or "ends tonight at 11:59 PM PT" line above the fold.
- 5. Use a stronger CTA color and specific copy, such as "Shop Flash Deals."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: up to 50% off gear & apparel`
- **Subject Alt B:** `24 hours only — up to 50% off, plus $19.99 fleece`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fleece hoodies $19.99, shoes from $100 and under. Sale ends 11:59 PM PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `One day, select clothing, shoes & gear. Shop before it's gone.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally sound: `lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` tables, a hidden preheader, and MSO conditionals. It has one confirmed failure, a plain-text part of only 9 characters. It also has widespread missing `alt` attributes. The header-based warnings can't be confirmed from this capture.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Wrapped links:** All observed links are ESP click-tracking redirects, e.g. `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/mclick?params=b.v1.…`. The destination URLs are encoded in `params`, so they can't be checked from the source.
- **Coverage:** QA probed 12 material links and skipped 51. It reported no probe failures, but the skipped links, mostly footer, utility and social, were not verified.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=…` has no `alt`, which is minor. It is served from the same first-party tracking domain as the clicks.
- No broken or mismatched links are confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on 6 content images.** All are Scene7 images with filename-style names:
  - `shoes09306am`
  - `lightweightfleece09306am`
  - `peletonshoes9306am%202`
  - `nca09306ama`
  - `golfclubs09306am`
  - `fishing09306am`

  Since the images are used as tiles, likely linked, a screen reader or image-blocked client will get no label or a bare URL for those links.
- **Missing `alt` on 5 icon images:**
  - `icon-vc-sms-1`
  - `icon-vc-sportsmatter`
  - `icon-vc-bpg-1`
  - `icon-vc-favorite-1`
  - `icon-location-2025-1`

  Use `alt=""` if they are decorative and adjacent text carries the meaning. Otherwise give them descriptive `alt` text.
- **Image weight:** Content images request `wid=1200&hei=1600` for a 600px container. This is likely oversized, but file size wasn't measured, so it's a possible payload concern rather than a confirmed defect.
- **Head:** `<link rel="icon">` in `<head>` is ignored by email clients. It's harmless but unnecessary.
- Otherwise no issues found. Viewport meta, `@media (max-width:480px)` rules, the Outlook `<xml>` block and the `mso-hide` preheader are all present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or subject. No personalization is evident. The source is truncated, so the footer wasn't inspected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is 9 characters.** This is effectively a missing `text/plain` alternative. It hurts deliverability scoring and leaves text-only clients and assistive tools with nothing useful.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Check the raw message at the sender. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC can't be assessed here. This is a capture limitation, not evidence of failure.
- **Physical address and in-body unsubscribe link:** Not visible in the truncated HTML, so they can't be confirmed. QA says an unsubscribe link is always included in the link probe.
- The From domain is `em.dcsg.com`, a dedicated sending subdomain. That is consistent with aligned authentication, though not verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Can't be assessed. UTM and campaign parameters sit inside the encoded `params=` payload of the redirect links. No landing-page resolution results were provided, and the subject ("up to 50% OFF") can't be matched to a destination from the source. No issues are confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a real `text/plain` part** (a full plain-text rendering with links and the unsubscribe URL). This is the only confirmed failure.
2. **Add `alt` text** to the 6 product tiles, for example a category name plus offer. Use `alt=""` on the 5 decorative icons, and on the open pixel.
3. **Verify the raw headers at the sender**, not through the relay: `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `em.dcsg.com`.
4. **Improve the audit capture:** feed the full HTML, not the truncated version. Follow redirects for the 51 skipped links, or at least sample footer links. Record the final landing URLs and their UTM parameters.
5. **Optional:** serve images at 2× the display width (about 1200px wide is fine, but drop the 1600px height cap if the layout doesn't need it). Remove the unused `<link rel="icon">`.
