# Up to 50% off our best DEALS of the week 👉
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S running its "Best Deals of the Week" template for the second consecutive day — a near-identical creative to the May 18 send that scored 8/10 in this audit series. The hero is competent: a bold "Up to 50% OFF" badge sits in the right visual position, the SHOP NOW CTA is unambiguous, and the multi-category grid renders cleanly. But that's exactly what yesterday's email looked like too.
- The bigger problem is a body/subject disconnect: the subject promises 50% off, yet most of the individual category sections inside the email show "Up to 40% OFF" — clothing, shoes, golf, fan shop, drinkware, team sports, fitness equipment. Only Outdoor Essentials reaches the 50% ceiling. A subscriber who actually opens and scrolls will notice the gap.
- With Memorial Day days away and the brand already sitting on a strong "Memorial Day Event" email sent yesterday, this send had an obvious seasonal anchor to grab — and didn't. Instead it fires the same evergreen "Deal Check of the Week" creative, competing against itself in the inbox (alongside the same-day clearance email). Competent execution; diminishing-returns strategy.

## What's working

- **50% OFF badge lands in the hero** — visible above the fold on desktop, no scrolling needed to see the value prop.
- **SHOP NOW button is clear** — high contrast, unambiguous placement directly under the hero offer.
- **Multi-category grid gives broad SKU exposure** — golf, outdoor, fitness, team sports, drinkware all get a module, covering different subscriber interests.
- **Clean render** — no broken images, no overlapping text, consistent layout throughout.
- **Brand identity is instant** — DICK'S logo + color palette require zero cognitive effort to recognize.

## What's weak

- **Near-identical to yesterday's send** — same template, same "Deal Check of the Week" framing, same "Up to 50% OFF" hook. A subscriber who opened May 18's version opens a copy.
- **Subject/body discount mismatch** — subject says 50%, but five of seven category modules show 40% OFF. The math doesn't hold up past the scroll.
- **Zero Memorial Day tie-in** — the holiday is 4 days out; the brand sent a successful "Memorial Day Event" email yesterday and completely abandons that anchor here.
- **No personalization or category targeting** — every subscriber gets the same seven categories regardless of whether they golf, run, or play team sports.
- **Same-day inbox collision** — DICK'S already sent a clearance email today (scored 6/10); two sends in one day with similar discount language cannibalizes both.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Anchor to Memorial Day** — this send exists the week of Memorial Day; that's the entire reason to exist. Even one line — "Last chance before the holiday weekend" — differentiates it from yesterday and gives subscribers a reason to act today specifically.
- 2. **Reconcile the 40%/50% split** — either lead the body with the strongest 50% offer (Outdoor Essentials) at the top, or drop the subject to "Up to 40%" to match what most subscribers will actually find. The current disconnect erodes trust.
- 3. **Add a single featured product** — one hero SKU with a specific price (not a category page) dramatically lifts click likelihood over a generic "SHOP NOW" to the department.
- 4. **Suppress yesterday's openers** — subscribers who clicked May 18's version don't need the same creative 24 hours later; resend to non-openers only.
- 5. **Surface ScoreCard pricing** — a member-exclusive price callout on one module would reward loyalty subscribers and create differentiated urgency.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Golf, outdoor gear, fitness equipment and more — shop before the holiday`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your weekly deals roundup: top categories, up to 50% off select styles`

## Technical Audit — DICK'S Sporting Goods "Up to 50% off" Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Standard Epsilon/Harmony table-based build. The most significant technical failure is a broken `<script type="application/ld+json">` block containing JavaScript-style comments, which invalidates all three Schema.org/Gmail annotation objects and disables promotional tab enrichment entirely.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Open-tracking pixel**
`https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/v610000019e41e367cf9a7b786e96c569c8/...`
Pixel is correctly styled `display:none; max-height:0px` — no rendering bleed risk.

**Click-tracking redirects**
All links route through `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...` (Epsilon Harmony redirect). Format is consistent; no bare untracked `href`s visible in the provided source.

**Logo image src truncated**
`https://dsg.images.harmony.epsilon…` — the src attribute is cut off in the supplied source. Cannot confirm the resolved URL or whether the image returns a 200. Needs verification in the live send.

**No UTM parameters visible in click URLs**
Epsilon's redirect layer may inject UTMs server-side; cannot confirm from HTML alone (see §6).

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`border-collapse:separate` on all layout tables**
Unusual choice — the global reset for Outlook conditional styles correctly sets `border-collapse:collapse`, but the outer wrapper and all inner tables override to `separate`. This can produce 1–2 px gap artifacts between cells in Outlook 2016/2019 when backgrounds differ across adjacent TDs.

**Web font loading**
Archivo loaded from `fonts.gstatic.com` via `@font-face` inside `@media screen` — correct pattern for email. Fallback chain `Archivo, Arial, sans-serif` is appropriate. Will silently fall back in Outlook; no action needed.

**`role="presentation"` on layout tables** — present and correct throughout.

**`lang="en"` on `<html>`** — present. ✓

**`alt` text on logo image** — cannot verify; img src is truncated in the provided source.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

`id="header-non-sc-member"` indicates conditional block logic keyed to loyalty membership state. No unresolved merge tokens (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%VARIABLE%%`) are visible in the supplied portion. Conditional variant rendering (`sc-member` vs. `non-sc-member`) cannot be confirmed complete without the full source.

---

### 5. Compliance

**CAN-SPAM / unsubscribe** — footer not present in the truncated source. Cannot confirm unsubscribe link, physical mailing address, or sender identification. These must be verified in the full HTML before send.

**Authentication headers** — not derivable from HTML source. DKIM/SPF/DMARC alignment for `DSG@e.dcsg.com` (Epsilon sending domain `e.dcsg.com`) must be confirmed via headers on a test send.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM / Landing Page)

**UTM parameters absent from HTML-visible URLs**
No `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, or `utm_content` parameters appear in any `href` in the provided source. If Epsilon's redirect layer does not append UTMs server-side, click attribution in GA4/Adobe will be lost and sessions will appear as direct traffic. Confirm with a decoded redirect test before deployment.

**Schema.org `availabilityStarts` is stale**
```json
"availabilityStarts": "2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"
```
Hardcoded to 2020-03-06 — six years old. This is a copy-paste template failure. Any client or feed consuming this date will surface incorrect offer timing.
