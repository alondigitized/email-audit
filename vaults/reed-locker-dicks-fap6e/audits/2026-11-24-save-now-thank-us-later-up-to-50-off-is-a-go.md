# Save now, thank us later: Up to 50% OFF is a GO! 🟢 💸
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- This is the seventh or so DICK'S Black Friday send in three days. It repeats the template from the 11/24 "Biggest SALE of the Year" and "Waiting for Black Friday? Don't." sends. The green-and-gold "UP TO 50% OFF" hero is loud and the red "Shop Now" button is easy to find. Below it, a tidy stack of category tiles (running shoes, fleece, bikes, ping-pong tables, golf sets, sleep pants) gives plenty to scan.
- The email is well built, but it is a template swap rather than a new idea. The same "Black Friday Starts Now / Biggest Sale of the Year" hero has shown up all week. The only fresh element is the red "Extra 25% off clearance" block, which comes late in the scroll. A subscriber who has seen six near-identical sends has little reason to open this one.
- Verdict: competent and on-season, but fatigued. It is a solid 7 on execution and a 5 on open appeal because of cadence and a subject line that adds nothing new.

## What's working

- The hero is unmistakable. "UP TO 50% OFF" fills the top screen, with "Black Friday Starts Now" and one red "Shop Now" button right under it.
- The pill navigation (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Golf, Fan Shop) lets a reader jump to their category quickly.
- Each tile has one headline, one line of copy and one green "Shop Now" button, so the hierarchy is easy to follow.
- The red "Extra 25% Off" block breaks up the green and adds a second, distinct price lever.
- The "Order Today, Sport it Tomorrow" strip at the top sells same-day delivery, which fits the holiday timing.

## What's weak

- The creative is nearly identical to the last several sends, so the email adds no new reason to engage.
- Every CTA is "Shop Now" or "Learn More". None names a product or benefit.
- Exclusion and fine-print blocks sit directly under the hero and the clearance block. They make the "up to 50%" claim feel conditional.
- The email is very long, about seven screens, with a lot of tiles. The mix of 40%, 50%, $30, $100 and 25% offers dilutes the single message.
- Most tiles are "Up to X% off" with no price, rating or review count, so there is little product-level proof.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with something new for this send, such as a doorbuster, a specific hero product or a countdown, instead of recycling the same "Starts Now" hero. Rotate the hero by category.
- 2. Make the CTAs specific: "Shop Hoka Deals," "Shop Bikes Up to 50% Off," "Get Fleece for $30."
- 3. Cut the email to the top 4–5 tiles and move the clearance offer higher so the second price message is seen earlier.
- 4. Show real prices or "was/now" on product tiles, and add star ratings to the gift picks.
- 5. Shorten the subject to under 50 characters and drop at least one emoji. Use a preheader that adds information, such as the delivery cutoff.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Order by 8 PM for same-day pickup. Extra 25% off clearance ends 11/28.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Bikes, golf sets and fleece for the whole family, from $30.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source has sound structure: `lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag, `role="presentation"` layout tables, MSO conditionals and a preheader spacer. The QA run confirms two defects: the plain-text part is effectively empty, and many content images have no alt text. The header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified because of the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link coverage is incomplete.** QA probed 12 material links and skipped 53. The data shows no broken links among the probed set, but the other 53 are unverified. The HTML is truncated, so I can't confirm per-link tracking parameters or redirect behavior.
- **The open-tracking pixel is present and has no alt attribute.** The pixel is `<img src="https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1...&configId=512ffb51-..." width="1" height="1">`, placed directly after `<body>`. It is a standard 1x1 pixel with no inline `style`, so it falls back to default image rendering. This is low severity.
- **A template placeholder comment is still in the source.** `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` sits in `td#ssl`. It is harmless but shows the template wasn't cleaned before send.
- **Image URLs use a cache-buster.** Examples are `...&cb=1794946604619` and `...&cb=179483...`. Each send serves unique image URLs, which can reduce CDN cache efficiency. This is informational, not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text is missing on 13 images (QA WARN).** Eight are content/banner images:

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are not found.** The QA tool notes that the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. This is a WARN, not a confirmed sender defect. Verify against the raw message at the sending ESP.
- **`Authentication-Results` is not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture. From `info@em.dcsg.com`, the `em.dcsg.com` subdomain should be checked directly.
- **The footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address can't be confirmed.** The HTML is truncated before the footer. QA states an unsubscribe link was always included in the probe set, but it didn't report the result.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable from the provided data. The visible source shows only anchor attributes (`class="arc-link"`, `data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"`), which suggests deep-linking via a link-wrapping layer. The truncated source doesn't expose `href` values, UTM parameters or landing-page results. No QA data covers landing-page alignment.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a real plain-text part.** The current 9 characters should be replaced with a full text alternative that includes the offer, key links and the unsubscribe URL.
2. **Add descriptive alt text to the 7 content/banner images.** Use `alt=""` on decorative footer icons, or meaningful alt text if the icons are links. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
3. **Verify headers at the source.** Check the raw message at the ESP (not the AgentMail relay) for:
   - `List-Unsubscribe`
   - `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`
   - SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `em.dcsg.com`
4. **Re-run link QA with higher probe coverage.** At least verify all CTA-class anchors and the footer links.
5. **Remove template placeholder comments** such as `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` from the production build.
6. **Capture the full HTML** so UTM parameters, the unsubscribe link and the footer address can be audited.
