# ☀️ Send off summer with up to 50% off EPIC deals ☀️
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-30**
## Executive summary

- At least this one has a fresh hero — a red-tinted stadium-seating shot with "UP TO 50% OFF" in bold white/red type, breaking from the green-locker creative DICK'S has run since 8/16. But the subject and structure are the same summer-close-out beat, and the body itself is a wall of nine near-identical "Shop Now" blocks (sneakers, DSG family clothing, golf shoes, racquet gear, NFL gear, camping gear, clearance, plus a picks grid) with no single hero offer to anchor the eye.
- The "Epic End-of-Summer Sale" framing is at least seasonally honest — it's genuinely late August — but "up to 50%" is the exact same ceiling DICK'S has quoted in five of the last six sends, and "Score up to 50% off Nike, adidas and more!" tells you nothing about what's actually discounted. This is a scroll-forever catalog dump, not a targeted send.
- Judgement: mid-pack — visually cleaner top section than recent sends, but it collapses into the same undifferentiated multi-offer sprawl once you scroll, and the headline claim is stale.

## What's working

- The hero swaps DICK'S recycled green-locker backdrop for a red stadium-seat image — a real visual reset after weeks of repetition.
- "Epic End-of-Summer Sale" and "Starts Now" give a clean, legible entry point with one clear "Shop Now" CTA right under the hero.
- Category pill nav (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Golf, Outdoor) gives fast wayfinding for scanners who don't want to scroll.
- Product photography throughout (models, shoes, tennis players) is clean and on-brand, no visible render bugs or broken images.
- "Hundreds of New Clearance Styles" section has a distinct red block treatment that breaks the monotony of the repeating white sections.

## What's weak

- Nine separate offer sections stacked back to back with no visual hierarchy differentiation — by the fourth "Shop Now" block the eye has nothing new to latch onto.
- No specific dollar or percentage anchor beyond the repeated "up to 50%/60%" ceiling that's shown up in nearly every send since 8/16.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere in this email, unlike the last four sends which all leaned on countdown timers or "final day" framing — inconsistent cadence signal.
- The "Explore more deals" pill nav sits oddly close to the hero and duplicates the category info found later at the footer's shop-by-sport bar.
- ScoreCard Rewards points banner at the very top is cluttered small text competing with the "Order Today, Spot It Tomorrow" line right above the hero.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body from nine offer blocks to three or four — pick the strongest categories (based on send data) and drop the rest, or move them to a "Shop More" link instead of a full image+CTA block each.
- 2. Put a concrete number in the subject/hero instead of "up to 50%" for the sixth time running — e.g. lead with the DSG family clothing deal ("Up to 60% Off") since that's the highest number in the email.
- 3. Add urgency back in — this is the first recent send without a countdown or "ends tonight/tomorrow" framing; that's a real inconsistency versus the last four sends and likely hurts click-through.
- 4. Simplify the top: merge the ScoreCard banner and "Order Today, Spot It Tomorrow" line into one row so the hero isn't crowded before the fold.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Summer's ending — grab your gear before it's gone`
