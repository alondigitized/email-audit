# Beat the rush! Score up to 50% off HUNDREDS of Black Friday deals
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- This is another competent DICK'S Black Friday send, and it's the same template as the 11/24 "Save now, thank us later," "Biggest SALE of the Year," and "Waiting for Black Friday? Don't." emails. The hero is a big green-and-gold "BLACK FRIDAY STARTS NOW" block. Under it, "Biggest Sale of the Year / Up to 50% Off 1000s of Styles" has one red "Shop Now" button. The module stack below is clean and easy to scan.
- The email tries to do too much. After the hero there are category pills, a Squishies advent-calendar sweepstakes, four product or category tiles (adidas Handballs, Brooks, Nike Fleece, Wilson), an NBA tile, a red "EXTRA 25% OFF" banner, a store-hours block, a gift-picks grid and a footer app promo. That makes at least three competing offers: 50% off, up to 25% off Nike and NBA, and an extra 25% off clearance. The "Beat the rush" subject promises urgency that the body doesn't deliver, because the email has no countdown and no clear deadline. By day three of this run, I'd see it as one more near-identical send in a pile.
- Verdict: well built but undifferentiated. It's a solid 6-7 that does nothing new for a subscriber who has already seen six of these.

## What's working

- The hero is loud and seasonal. "BLACK FRIDAY STARTS NOW" in green and gold lands first.
- There is one clear red "Shop Now" button under the "Up to 50% Off" headline.
- Category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Golf, Fan Shop) give an easy way into a category.
- The product tiles alternate image sides, with a specific price or percentage on each ($59.99 adidas, $109.98 Brooks, 25% off NBA).
- The red "EXTRA 25% OFF" banner breaks up the page and is a strong visual offer.
- ScoreCard points and the order-by-2PM delivery note at the top reinforce loyalty and fulfillment.

## What's weak

- The "Beat the rush" urgency is vague. The only deadline is fine print ("1/25/26 11:59PM PT") that is hard to read.
- Three overlapping offers (up to 50% off, extra 25% off, up to 25% off Nike) dilute the main message.
- The Squishies advent calendar takes the second-biggest block for a sweepstakes-style entry, not a deal.
- The email is very long, and the store-hours block and gift picks sit far below the first screen.
- The gift-picks grid has product names but no prices or CTAs that I can see.
- The creative is the same as the recent sends, so it will feel stale.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one offer and one deadline. Lead with "Up to 50% off, ends tonight" and put a visible countdown or time in the hero.
- 2. Move the red "EXTRA 25% OFF" banner up directly under the hero, or merge it into the hero, since it is the strongest visual.
- 3. Drop the Squishies block or move it below the product tiles. It's a different job (an entry, not a purchase).
- 4. Add prices and ratings to the gift-picks grid, and move the store-hours block lower.
- 5. Make the subject specific and change the creative or hook enough to break the repetition across the 11/23-11/24 sends.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: up to 50% off + extra 25% off clearance`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Black Friday head start: 50% off before the crowds`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Adidas, Nike, Brooks and Wilson deals, plus delivery by Thanksgiving if you order by 2PM.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stack an extra 25% off clearance. Free ScoreCard points on every order.`
