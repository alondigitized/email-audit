# 📥 INSIDE: $25 & under select clearance deals is a click away...
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a legible, high-contrast "$25 AND UNDER" red-net hero, and it's the cleanest version of this beat DICK'S has run yet — right up there with the 9/15 "$25 and under clearance deals" send it's an almost identical creative reuse of. That's the problem: this is the *same* $25-and-under hero, same subhead, same green "Shop Now" button, sent again two days later under a retitled subject ("INSIDE:... is a click away"). The wrapper copy has changed, the creative hasn't.
- Past the hero, the email sprawls hard — HOKA, sweatshirts, tennis/racquet gear, NFL gear, a TaylorMade wedge trade-in, bikes, a "Sports Mom Shop" module, then a six-item product grid. None of that is "$25 and under" — it's a full-catalog dump loosely hung off a clearance headline, which undercuts the hero's promise the moment you scroll.
- Judgement: solid top-of-email execution dragged down by repeat creative and a mismatched, overlong body that doesn't deliver on its own subject line.

## What's working

- The hero is unambiguous: big white "$25 AND UNDER" on a dark red gradient with a net texture, plus a clear "Clearance Deals" subhead and green "Shop Now" CTA.
- Fine print sits directly under the hero ("Valid online... Exclusions apply...") rather than buried at the footer, which reads as more honest.
- Category chips (Men's / Women's / Kids' / Shoes / All Clearance / All Sale) right below the hero give a fast way to narrow in if the offer lands.
- No render bugs — images, buttons, and text all display cleanly with consistent spacing.

## What's weak

- Below the hero, nothing is actually $25-and-under: HOKA shoes, sweatshirts, racquet gear, NFL gear, a golf wedge trade-in, and bikes are all full-price/percent-off modules unrelated to the clearance premise.
- The email is extremely long (11+ distinct modules before the footer picks grid), which buries the one offer the subject promised.
- Creative reuse: this is essentially the same $25-and-under hero as the 9/15 send two days ago — a subscriber who opened that one gets deja vu.
- The subject's "INSIDE:" framing over-promises curiosity/urgency the body doesn't deliver — it reads like a teaser for a single deal, not a 15-module catalog blast.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the actual $25-and-under promise: keep the hero + chips, then follow with an actual $25-and-under product grid, not unrelated full-price categories. This is the single biggest lift — right now the body contradicts the subject.
- 2. Shorten dramatically — drop or move NFL/wedge trade-in/bikes/Sports Mom Shop to a separate send; a focused 4-6 module email converts better than a 15-module scroll.
- 3. Retire the $25-and-under red-net hero for at least one cycle — it's now run at least twice in three days; swap in a new visual treatment even if the offer strategy stays.
- 4. Tighten the subject to match body reality, or restructure the body to match the subject's implied specificity.
- **Subject Alt A:** `$25 and under: new clearance markdowns just added`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Shop $25 and under before sizes sell out`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New clearance styles just dropped below $25 — shop before they're gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sneakers, gear and more, all $25 and under.`
