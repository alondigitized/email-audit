# HURRY 🏃‍♀️ DICK'S Deal Days end TODAY! Score epic savings while you can...
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the campaign-close installment of DICK'S Deal Days — by brand history count, roughly the eleventh email in three days. The creative logic is sound: "end TODAY" is the one authentic urgency beat left, and the email delivers a clean product grid with genuine percentage-off figures, a prominent Extra 30% Off clearance kicker, and no render bugs. If a subscriber actually opens it, there's real shopping to do.
- The problem is everything upstream of the open. This send arrives hours after the June 25 "Time's running out" email (scored 9/10 in this audit), which already played the campaign-close card. The cadence has been indefensible — three days, ten-plus sends, and this one leads the subject with "HURRY" in all caps. That word, after this many sends, doesn't read as urgency; it reads as desperation. The subject also runs roughly 71 characters, which means most mobile inboxes will truncate it before "Score epic savings while you can..." ever shows.
- The body is a solid executional template. The campaign fatigue sits entirely in the inbox layer, not in the render.

## What's working

- Extra 30% Off clearance banner is visually distinct and prominent — the highest-value, most scannable deal in the email.
- Specific percentage figures are attached to every product block (50% off basketball shoes, 40% off women's Nike fleece, up to $200 off Blackstone griddles, up to 50% off camping equipment) — real reasons to click, not vague "savings."
- Product category range covers multiple summer purchase intents: footwear, athletic apparel, outdoor cooking, camping.
- Visual hierarchy holds: green Deal Days hero at top, descending product offer cards with Shop Now CTAs, clearance kicker before the footer.
- Email renders cleanly — no broken images, no layout breaks, no overlapping text.

## What's weak

- Subject line runs approximately 71 characters — well over the mobile-friendly 50-char threshold — and will truncate before the back half of the message reaches most inboxes.
- "HURRY" in all caps leading the subject, after ten prior sends, reads as sender panic rather than subscriber urgency.
- Cadence is the dominant drag: this is approximately the eleventh Deal Days email in three days; the June 25 "Time's running out" send already deployed the final-countdown frame.
- No ScoreCard or loyalty mention visible — a missed chance to activate member identity on the campaign's last eligible day.
- Six product offer blocks is a lot for a send that should be driving one decision, not six.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead the subject with the concrete deadline and kill "HURRY."** A specific time cutoff ("ends midnight") beats an all-caps panic word — and fits in 50 characters, which "HURRY" plus everything that follows clearly doesn't.
- 2. **Collapse the product grid to one hero offer.** A subscriber who ignored ten prior sends needs a single easy decision. Feature the Extra 30% Off clearance as the sole CTA above the fold; let the rest be secondary links.
- 3. **Add a ScoreCard line in the hero copy.** "Earn your final Deal Days ScoreCard points" activates member identity as a second purchase driver, independent of the discount.
- 4. **Replace "Score epic savings while you can..." in the subject with the offer itself.** The subject already has a deadline; the trailing clause should name the deal, not restate the vibe.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Deal Days end tonight — Extra 30% off clearance`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 50% off shoes + 30% off clearance`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Jordan, Nike, Blackstone, camping — final hours on every offer.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Earn your last Deal Days ScoreCard points. Shop before midnight.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Possible dev/staging asset path in production logo URL:**
```
https://dsg.images.harmony.epsilon.com/_phoenix/_dev/AEM/img/headers/brand-logo--dk…
```
The `/_dev/` path segment is concerning for a live send. If this is a dev-environment asset origin, it may return 404 or be CDN-restricted in production. Verify the asset resolves publicly and is served from the production origin path.

**`<link rel="icon">` inside `<head>`:**
```html
<link rel="icon" type="image/x-icon" href="https://www.dickssportinggoods.com/home/assets/icons/dsg/favicon.ico"/>
```
Ignored by all email clients. Harmless but dead markup — strip it.

**`alt` attributes** — the logo `<img id="logo">` src is truncated in the provided source; could not confirm `alt` is present. The hidden tracking pixel has no `alt`, which is correct for a 0×0 tracker. Flag logo alt for verification.

**`role="presentation"` on all layout tables** — correctly applied throughout. No issues.

**`border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0`** on outer wrapper table — valid technique for border-radius support in Outlook; no rendering issue.

**Archivo font loaded via Google Fonts CDN** (`fonts.gstatic.com/s/archivo/v7/...`) — `v7` is an older version slug. Not broken, but the current path is higher-versioned. No functional impact since fallback stack (`Arial, sans-serif`) is present.

### 5. Compliance

**JSON-LD structured data block contains invalid JSON — comments are not legal JSON syntax:**
```json
{ // GIF & WEBP images are not supported and will be filtered out.
  // Sample image is 538x138, 3.9 aspect ratio
  "@context": "http://schema.org/",
  "@type": "PromotionCard",
  // DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!
  "image": ""
}
```
JavaScript-style `//` comments will cause the entire `<script type="application/ld+json">` block to fail JSON parsing. Gmail will silently discard all three structured data objects — the Organization card, DiscountOffer, and PromotionCard will not render in the Promotions tab.

**`http://schema.org/` — should be `https://`:**
All three `@context` values use the non-secure schema.org URL. Google's structured data guidelines require `https://schema.org/`. Minor but technically incorrect.

**Stale hardcoded date in DiscountOffer:**
```json
"availabilityStarts": "2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"
```
This is a 2020 template date, not the current campaign date. `availabilityEnds`, `description`, and `discountCode` are all empty strings. Even if the JSON were valid, this structured data would be rejected or render incorrectly.

**`PromotionCard.image` is an empty string** — required field left unfilled.

**CAN-SPAM footer** — not visible in the truncated source provided; audit cannot confirm physical address and unsubscribe link are present. Verify in full HTML.

**Authentication headers (SPF/DKIM/DMARC)** — not derivable from HTML source. Sending domain is `e.dcsg.com` (ESP subdomain); authentication must be verified at the MTA/header level from a delivered message.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All links are Epsilon redirect-wrapped. Cannot confirm presence of UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content`) on the destination URLs without resolving the redirect chain.

**Action required:** resolve one ESP redirect URL and confirm the landing page receives consistent UTM tags. If UTM params are appended by the ESP at redirect-resolution time, verify they are present in the Epsilon send configuration — omitting them would cause all traffic to land as direct/unattributed in GA4.
