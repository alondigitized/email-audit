# Select clothing & shoes = Up to 40% OFF
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-18**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S sprawling weekly-digest format again — same shape as 8/16's "Up to 50% off awaits" send, just with clothing/footwear as the lead category instead of a generic wordmark. The hero is clean: a woman in magenta activewear and a man in a linen shirt, both mid-30s/athletic-casual, over "Up to 40% Off Select Clothing and Shoes, Save on Nike, CALIA, VRST and more." That's a legible, on-brand open.
- Past the hero it's the now-familiar kitchen-sink scroll: ScoreCard+ $20 bonus reward, YETI cooler launch, golf electronics, baseball gloves, kayaks, "Up to 50% Off This Week's Deals," "Up to 60% Off Clearance," then a six-item product grid. Nine distinct offers/categories in one email is a lot of competing CTAs, and this brand has now run six-plus near-identical "up to X% off" sends in the last eight days (8/12 through 8/18) — the fatigue is real and this one doesn't do anything to break the pattern.
- Solid execution, tired format. The hero alone would score well; burying it under eight more offers dilutes it.

## What's working

- Hero models and copy are directly on-category for the subject line — no bait-and-switch between subject and hero.
- "Up to 40% Off" with named brands (Nike, CALIA, VRST) is a concrete, specific offer.
- "Shop Now" button is high-contrast dark green, clearly clickable, sits right under the offer.
- Category nav pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Team Sports, Back-to-School) let a scanner self-route fast.
- No render bugs — every image loads, text is crisp, no overlap.

## What's weak

- Nine separate offers stacked in one email (clothing, ScoreCard+, YETI, golf, gloves, kayaks, weekly deals, clearance, product grid) — no single dominant CTA once you scroll past the hero.
- This is the sixth-plus "up to X%" discount send in eight days (8/12 Flash Sale through 8/18); a subscriber who's been getting these has full offer fatigue by now.
- The clothing/shoes offer that's the actual subject line gets one screen, then is instantly buried under seven unrelated categories — it doesn't get to be the star of its own email.
- "Explore more deals and essentials" plus the pill nav immediately undercuts the specificity of the subject line by pivoting to "everything."

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to 2-3 blocks max (hero clothing offer, ScoreCard+ bonus, one more) and move golf/baseball/kayaks/YETI into a separate targeted send — this single change would do the most to make the subject-to-content promise land.
- 2. Move ScoreCard+ $20 Bonus Reward higher or make it its own send — it's a stronger, time-bound offer ("2 Days Only") than a generic clearance stack and is getting lost mid-scroll.
- 3. Drop or consolidate the "Up to 50%," "Up to 60% Clearance," and "Up to 40%" percentage messaging in one email — three different competing discount tiers confuses which one applies to what.
- 4. Add a size/fit or availability cue near the hero product callouts (Nike, CALIA, VRST) since apparel/footwear clicks live or die on fit confidence, and none is visible here.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus: ScoreCard+ members get a $20 bonus reward through 8/20`

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

2. Add `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers at the sending MTA if not already present (verify directly with the ESP/Epsilon, since header capture here is relay-dependent).
