# 📥 Get to shopping! EXTRA 25% off select clearance styles is in! 📥
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a loud, clean DICK'S clearance send with a clear lead offer. The hero is a huge red-and-black "EXTRA 25% OFF" graphic. A "Two Days Only" tag, the headline "Extra 25% Off Select Clearance Styles" and a green Shop Now button sit right under it. The offer registers in about a second, and it is the only hero in this week's sends where the image states the discount plainly.
- The body then dilutes the message. Below the hero it becomes a long catalog: running shoes, clothing, soccer, FootJoy golf, a Hydro Flask drop, Sports Mom Shop, a second "Up to 50% Off" banner, a text-alert promo and a product grid. That is about ten modules. Several of them are not clearance, so the "extra 25% off" promise gets buried. The send also repeats the "Up to 50% off" banner from the 10/13 "This week's DEALS" email. It is competent but crowded, and it is a near-repeat of the earlier clearance sends.

## What's working

- The hero states "EXTRA 25% OFF" in giant type, so the offer is clear without reading anything else.
- "Two Days Only" gives a believable deadline, and the fine print confirms it with an end date (10/17/26).
- The headline repeats the offer and sits directly above a green Shop Now button, which is the clearest CTA in the email.
- The category chips (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 and Under, Fan Shop, All Sale) give fast routes to the shopper's own category.
- The product grid at the bottom has real items with names, and ScoreCard loyalty is visible in the header.

## What's weak

- The "Up to 50% Off" and "Up to 40% Off" modules compete with the "extra 25%" headline. Shoppers can't tell which discount applies to what.
- The email has about ten modules. Hydro Flask, Sports Mom Shop and FootJoy are only loosely tied to clearance.
- The second "Up to 50% OFF This Week's Deals" banner repeats the earlier 10/13 creative and adds nothing new.
- The legal and fine print under the hero is dense and small, with exclusions and "interim markdowns may have been taken" language. It weakens how honest the offer feels.
- The Text COACH "$20 off $100+ instantly" block is a second, unrelated offer that pulls attention from the clearance CTA.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero, the category chips and one product row of true clearance items. Drop Hydro Flask, Sports Mom and the second 50% banner. Every module should serve "extra 25% off clearance."
- 2. Replace the lifestyle modules with real clearance products that show a was/now price and the stacked 25% savings.
- 3. Add a promo code or an "applied at checkout" line, plus a countdown or "ends 10/17" next to the hero button.
- 4. Move the Text Alerts promo and the app promo to the footer, so the email has one primary ask.
- 5. Shorten the legal copy or collapse it behind a Details link.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 25% off clearance ends tomorrow`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Clearance just got cheaper: extra 25% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Nike, adidas, Under Armour and more. Ends 10/17, online only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stack it on markdowns: running, golf, soccer and apparel.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally sound: it has `lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` layout tables, an MSO conditional block, a hidden preheader spacer, and a mobile media query. The QA run found one hard failure, a plain-text part of only 9 characters, plus widespread missing `alt` attributes. The HTML provided is truncated, so findings about footer content and link parameters are limited to what the QA data shows.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 12 material links were probed and 56 were skipped. The QA data reports no broken or redirecting probed links, so nothing is confirmed broken. The 56 skipped links, mostly footer, utility and social, are unverified.
- The open-tracking pixel is `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1...&configId=512ffb51-...`. It is a 1x1 `<img>` with no `alt`. This is a minor accessibility flag, because screen readers may announce the URL.
- The hero and secondary images (`runshoes1016stwd6am`, `2selectclothign1016stwd6am`, and others) use Scene7 URLs with `fmt=jpeg&qlt=100&wid=1200&hei=1600`. At `qlt=100` and 1200px wide, these are likely heavy for a 600px layout. File size was not measured, so this is a performance risk, not a confirmed defect.

- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found.** The QA tool says this may be an AgentMail relay artifact, so it is unconfirmed.

- The QA data says an unsubscribe link was always included in the link probing, but it reports no result for it. The footer, including the physical address, falls outside the truncated source, so it could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a real plain-text part** (the only QA FAIL). It should include the offer, key links and the unsubscribe URL.
2. **Add `alt` text to all flagged images.** Use descriptive text for the hero and offer images and `alt=""` for spacers and decorative icons. Add `alt=""` to the open pixel too.
3. **Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers** on the raw message at the origin, outside the relay. Add them if they are missing.
