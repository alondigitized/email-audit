# Ready for an EXTRA 25% off select clearance? 💰 Shop now!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a loud, clean clearance email with a clear hero. A red "EXTRA 25% OFF" block fills the top of the screen, with "Extra 25% Off Select Clearance Styles" and a green Shop Now button right under it. The eye lands on the number first, which is correct. The brand is recognizable, the offer is concrete, and the deadline is stated in the body.
- Below the hero it stops being a clearance email. It turns into a general DICK'S catalog. You get 40% off family shoes, adidas training shorts, NFL and NCAA gear, a HydroJug collab, L.A.B. Golf putters, team sports, cardio equipment, an app-download banner and a product grid. The clearance offer gets one block. The rest is competing promos.
- This continues the pattern in the brand history. On 10/20 "New markdowns added 🤝 Up to 70% OFF select clearance" used a similar hero. 10/21 had two near-identical weekly-deals sends. This send comes in the same cadence and reuses the same hero-plus-stack structure. The "extra 25%" angle is a sensible way to refresh the clearance message. A subscriber who got the 70%-off clearance email two days ago will still read this as the same offer. The email is competent but not special. Judgement: solid and repetitive, held back by a diluted body and a generic CTA.
- *Note: the screenshot is heavily downscaled, so small fine print and the inbox preview are not fully legible. I read the preview from the top banner.*

## What's working

- The "EXTRA 25% OFF" hero is huge and high-contrast, and it reads in under a second.
- The green Shop Now button sits directly under the headline, so the primary action is obvious.
- A "Limited Time" tag and fine print give a deadline (valid through 10/24, 11:59 PM PT) without cluttering the hero.
- The "Explore more deals" row of category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', K35 & Under, Fan Shop, All Sale) gives a quick way to browse.
- The content is seasonal. NFL and NCAA gear is promoted as "One Day Only", which fits fall.
- The layout renders cleanly, with no visibly broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The "extra 25%" clearance offer sits at the top, then at least eight other promos pile up beneath it and dilute the message.
- Every CTA says "Shop Now". None names a product or category, so none is specific.
- The percent-off messages conflict: Extra 25%, up to 40%, up to 50% and $100 off. A reader can't tell which one matters.
- The layout is a very long scroll. The "ALL IN THE APP" banner and the product grid sit far below the hero, which is a lot of length for one clearance message.
- Loyalty is almost absent. A ScoreCard logo sits in the header, but no member pricing or points are visible, even though the 10/19 sends led with ScoreCard.
- The footer is heavy with fine print, exclusions and legal text. "Select" clearance and "select" styles keep the real scope vague.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body to the clearance story. Show 3 to 4 actual clearance products with the was/now price and the extra 25% applied. The hero promises a price, but the email never shows one.
- 2. Replace "Shop Now" with specific CTAs, such as "Shop clearance shoes", "Shop clearance apparel" and "Shop clearance gear". Link each to its own landing page.
- 3. Make the deadline prominent in the hero, for example "Ends Friday 11:59 PM PT", and show a code or "no code needed" line next to it.
- 4. Move the app-download banner and the product grid out of this send, or shrink them. This email's job is clearance.
- 5. Space it away from the 10/20 clearance and weekly-deals sends. Alternate the creative so the offer stops looking like a repeat.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 25% off clearance ends Friday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Clearance just got cheaper: extra 25% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shoes, apparel and gear marked down again. No code needed, ends 10/24 at 11:59 PM PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stack it on top of existing markdowns. Nike, adidas and more, while sizes last.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source looks sound: `lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` layout tables, MSO conditional comments, and a font fallback stack. The one QA FAIL is the near-empty plain-text part (9 chars), and many images are missing alt text. The source was truncated, so footer and CTA link markup could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage was partial.** QA probed 12 material links and skipped 56. The report lists no broken or redirecting links, so none are flagged. The skipped links, including footer and social links, are unverified.
- **The open pixel is ESP-wrapped and has no alt text.** The URL is `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1...&tuid=6ad913ad3e5e18754a7fbae0&configId=512ffb51-...`. It is `width="1" height="1"` with no `alt` and no inline `display:none` or `border:0`. Some clients may render a stray border or alt placeholder.
- **Click-tracking URLs are not visible in the truncated source.** I can't confirm how links are wrapped.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images.** The Scene7 hero and category images flagged by QA are:
  - `2026-1022-dks-swd-6am-clothing`
  - `2026-1022-dks-swd-6am-fanshop`
  - `hydrojug-easton2026-1022-d`
  - `2026-1022-dks-swd-6am-sports`
  - `2026-1022-dks-swd-6am-cardio`

  If these carry the offer or category labels, image-blocking clients and screen readers get no content. The offer then depends on the image loading.
- **Missing alt text on icons.** `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1` and `icon-location-2025-1` have no alt. If they are decorative, they need an explicit `alt=""`. A missing attribute is different from an empty one: screen readers may read out the filename.
- **Plain-text part is effectively empty.** QA reports a 9-character text version. The multipart/alternative text part is unusable for text-only clients and can add a small spam-filter penalty.
- **Editor artifacts remain in the body tag.** `<body ... class="is-html-mode" data-contenteditable="true">` is harmless but is left over from the authoring tool.
- **No issues found with:** viewport meta, the MSO fixes, the `@font-face` fallback to `Archivo, Arial, sans-serif`, or the hidden preheader (`display:none; max-height:0; mso-hide:all`).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered tokens such as `%%...%%`, `{{...}}` or `[FirstName]`.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them. This is unconfirmed, not a verified sender defect. The raw `.eml` from the original delivery would settle it, and it matters for Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **Authentication-Results was not captured.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. The From domain `em.dcsg.com` was not checked against the DKIM `d=` domain, so alignment is unverified.
- **The footer could not be checked.** The postal address and in-body unsubscribe link are past the truncation point. QA did probe an unsubscribe link, since it is always included, but the report shows no result for it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The truncated source and QA output show no destination URLs, UTM parameters or landing-page probe results. I'm not making any claim here.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a real plain-text part with the offer, key category links and the unsubscribe URL, replacing the 9-character one.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the five Scene7 content images. Use `alt=""` on the five decorative icons.
3. Add `alt=""` and `style="display:block;border:0"` to the open-tracking pixel.
4. Re-run QA against the raw `.eml` to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. If they are present, close the Compliance warnings.
5. Re-run QA on the full HTML without truncation, and probe the skipped links, to cover UTMs, the footer address and unsubscribe markup, and landing-page continuity.
6. Remove `is-html-mode` and `data-contenteditable` from the production template.
