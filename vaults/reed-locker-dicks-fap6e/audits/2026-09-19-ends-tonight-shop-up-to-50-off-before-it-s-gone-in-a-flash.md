# Ends. Tonight! ⏰ Shop up to 50% off before it's gone in a flash. ⚡
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-19**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S back on the "FLASH" orange-and-black hero, and this time the wordmark actually renders in full — no repeat of the 9/19 "FLAS" crop bug flagged in brand history. The countdown timer ("Final Hours 07:59:48") paired with "Ends. Tonight!" in the subject is a legitimate urgency stack, and the offer structure (up to 50% off site-wide, stacked with an extra 25% off clearance) gives two distinct reasons to click.
- The render isn't clean, though: there's a broken/placeholder image tile sitting between the adidas Samba and New Balance 530 product shots in the "Check out these picks" grid — a visible defect a subscriber would notice immediately. And this is the same flash-sale format DICK'S has run repeatedly this month (9/19, 9/17, 9/16), so the creative fatigue flagged in prior audits continues.
- Net: solid urgency mechanics and a fixed hero defect, dragged down by a new broken-image bug and the ongoing sameness of the flash-sale template.

## What's working

- "FLASH" wordmark renders fully and legibly in the hero — the 9/19 cropping defect is not present here.
- Live countdown clock ("Final Hours 07:59:48") reinforces the "Ends Tonight" urgency from the subject line.
- Two distinct offers are stacked clearly: 50% off flash sale, then extra 25% off clearance further down — each with its own CTA button.
- Category pill navigation (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Team Sports) gives an easy secondary path if the hero offer doesn't land.
- ScoreCard points balance ("300 Points + $10 Reward") is shown up top for loyalty personalization.

## What's weak

- Broken image: the middle product tile in "Check out these picks" shows a blank box with a small broken-image icon instead of a product photo.
- This is the same orange flash-sale hero format run on 9/19, 9/17, and 9/16 — no creative variation, just a new countdown number.
- "Save on select clothing, shoes and more!" is vague — no hero product or specific category is called out despite "up to 50% off" being the headline number.
- Fine print under both offers ("Exclusions apply," "Offer may not be valid in conjunction with other sales") is easy to miss and slightly undercuts the "up to 50%" headline.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the broken product image before send — a blank/broken tile in the product grid undermines trust in a sale email and is the single most damaging visible defect here.
- 2. Vary the hero creative — after four "FLASH"/countdown-style sends this month, swap in a lifestyle photo or hero product shot to break the pattern fatigue.
- 3. Name one specific hero product or category in the subhead instead of "clothing, shoes and more" — e.g., "Up to 50% off sneakers, apparel & gear."
- 4. Move the countdown timer's actual deadline (11:59PM PT 9/19) into the preview text so the urgency is confirmed before the open, not just implied by "Tonight."

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Email uses standard ESP tracking-redirect architecture (data.em.dcsg.com) with click and open tracking present, but fails baseline deliverability/compliance checks and has multiple accessibility gaps.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- CTA link uses `data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/mclick?params=...&pid=...&tuid=6a82f9b315ef4803387fe9cc&confi…` — standard click-tracking redirect, correctly wrapped.
- Open-tracking pixel present at `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1.VX7qKfhfaiLrThoTwGJr6JEFddqsIJh0umxTE-bqHGPm1JHqpitiQEdRR7Myd_AzyjwOHMLC` but flagged for missing `alt` text (see Rendering section — pixel should have `alt=""` regardless).
- QA note: 12 of 52 total links were probed (deprioritized footer/utility/social links skipped by design) — no broken-link data available for the unprobed set from this pass.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 6 images missing `alt` text: `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`, and the tracking pixel (`open`). Non-decorative icons need descriptive `alt`; the tracking pixel should get `alt=""` to avoid screen readers announcing a raw URL.
- MSO/Outlook conditional comments and `@font-face` (Archivo, gstatic-hosted) are correctly scoped inside `@media screen` to avoid Outlook rendering fallback issues.
- Hidden preheader spacer (`#pht-spacer`) uses zero-width space/nbsp padding — implemented correctly with `mso-hide:all` and `display:none` for Outlook/other clients.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No merge tags or personalization tokens (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`) visible in the truncated source. No issues found in the visible markup.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- [FAIL] `List-Unsubscribe` header not found — may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not capturing it, but this is a CAN-SPAM/RFC 2369 risk if genuinely absent at send time.
- [FAIL] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found — one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) cannot be confirmed as supported.
- [WARN] `Authentication-Results` header not found — SPF/DKIM pass/fail status cannot be verified via this relay.
- QA confirms an unsubscribe link is present in-body (always included per probing methodology), but header-level unsubscribe support is unconfirmed.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- CTA destination is obscured behind the `mclick` redirect (`params=b.v1.AN2Ekphvnp...`); actual landing URL and UTM parameters are not visible in the encoded payload, so UTM/landing-page alignment cannot be verified from source alone — would require following the redirect.

7. Recommendations
- Add descriptive `alt` attributes to the 6 flagged images; set `alt=""` on the open-tracking pixel.
- Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually sent (not just missed by the relay) — verify via raw headers at the sending MTA, not just the AgentMail capture.
- Provide a plain-text MIME part with real content — current fallback is 9 characters, which will hurt deliverability/spam scoring and violates CAN-SPAM's practical readability expectation.
- Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `em.dcsg.com` directly at the MTA, since `Authentication-Results` wasn't observed here.
- Resolve the `mclick` redirect to verify final landing URL UTM parameters match the landing page's expected campaign tracking.
