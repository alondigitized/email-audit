# Holiday Deal Dash = Final hours! ⏳ Don't miss up to 50% off...
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth Holiday Deal Dash send in about four days, and the only one with a live countdown. A green "Final Hours 07:59:48" bar sits at the top, and it is the best thing in the email. It makes the 11/8 11:59PM PT deadline feel real instead of decorative. The giant gold "UP TO 50% OFF" on a red striped hero lands the offer at once, with a single maroon Shop Now button right under it.
- Then it sprawls. A second full-size hero for "EXTRA 25% OFF" clearance competes with the first one. After that come a gift-guide section and six unpriced product tiles. The offer is clear, but the email asks the reader to pick between two big offers and then wade through more. On the 11/8 "Last day" and clearance sends, the same pattern appeared as two separate emails. Here they are merged, which is more efficient but diluted.
- Judgement: a solid, urgent send with a clear hero. It is held back by a second hero, a pile of category pills, and product tiles with no prices or reviews. About a 6.

## What's working

- The countdown timer bar is a credible urgency device and sits above the hero.
- The gold "UP TO 50% OFF" on red is readable at a glance and the visual hierarchy is clear.
- Both offers have one maroon Shop Now button each, so the CTA is unambiguous.
- The ScoreCard strip shows "300 Points = $10 Reward", a visible loyalty benefit.
- The deadline is stated in plain text under each CTA (11/8/26 11:59PM PT).

## What's weak

- The two stacked heroes, 50% off and EXTRA 25% off, split attention and weaken both.
- The category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Holiday Shop) and the gift-budget pills turn the email into a menu.
- The six product tiles carry no prices, no discount badges and no ratings, so nothing ties them to the sale.
- "Save on brands you love" is generic. Naming brands such as Nike or CALIA would be more concrete.
- The footer is crowded with app badges, utility blocks, a nav bar and social icons, all after the point where most people stop reading.

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop the second hero, or move the clearance offer into a one-line text link. One offer plus one countdown is the stronger email.
- 2. Replace the generic product grid with sale-priced items showing "was/now" prices and star ratings. Pull in brand names to back up the "brands you love" line.
- 3. Cut the pills to one row of four categories at most, and remove the gift-budget block from a final-hours send.
- 4. Put the countdown timer or the deadline in the subject line and preheader, since that is the strongest hook.
- 5. Shorten the subject so it is not cut off on mobile.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: up to 50% off, 8 hours left ⏳`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: Holiday Deal Dash ends at 11:59PM PT`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus an extra 25% off select clearance. Pickup in an hour.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Nike, CALIA & more, marked down until midnight PT.`
