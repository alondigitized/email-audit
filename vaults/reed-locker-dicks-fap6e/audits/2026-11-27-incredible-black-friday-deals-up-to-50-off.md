# Incredible Black Friday DEALS = Up to 50% OFF! 💰
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S Black Friday template again, and it's solid. The "BIGGEST SALE OF THE YEAR" hero sits above a clear "Up to 50% Off" block with a Shop Now button. Below that are category pills, then a long run of product tiles for shoes, North Face, golf, basketballs, a treadmill and bikes. It ends with a clearance banner and a ScoreCard+ $20 bonus reward.
- The problem is the send itself. By my count it's the 12th or so Black Friday email in five days, after this morning's "Biggest Sale of the YEAR" and yesterday's "Black Friday has arrived!" and "Happy Thanksgiving!" sends. The creative is nearly identical each time. The subject is also the weakest part: ALL-CAPS "DEALS" and "OFF", an "=" sign, and an emoji read as spam-adjacent noise.
- Judgement: a competent email, but at this cadence it's a fatigue risk. One more near-duplicate adds little.

## What's working

- The hero and the "Black Friday: Up to 50% Off" block are the first things the eye lands on, with one clear Shop Now button.
- The category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Golf, Fan Shop) let a reader jump straight to what they want.
- Specific price points make the offers feel real: $699.95 SOLE F63 treadmill, $89.99 Wilson basketballs, up to $200 off golf clubs.
- The ScoreCard+ $20 bonus reward module gives loyalty members a reason to act.
- The "Gift picks" product grid works well for last-minute shoppers.

## What's weak

- The cadence is the biggest miss: the same hero and "up to 50%" message every day for five days.
- Several tiles say only "Up to 30% on shoes" or "Up to 50% off bikes" with a generic Shop Now, so no CTA names a specific product.
- The email is very long. Many separate offers (shoes, apparel, golf, basketballs, treadmill, bikes, clearance, ScoreCard+) dilute the single message.
- Most of the deadline and exclusion information is buried in small fine print. The only urgency above the fold is the "Limited Time" label.
- The long block of legal copy at the bottom makes the "up to 50%" claim feel conditional.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the send frequency and make each email different. Day-of-week creative like "Last 24 hours" or "Doorbusters end at midnight" beats repeating the same hero.
- 2. Shorten the body to about 4 modules: hero, top 3 deals, loyalty, gift picks. Move the rest behind the category pills.
- 3. Put the deadline in the hero as visible text, such as "Ends Sunday 11:59 PM PT", instead of burying it.
- 4. Make the CTAs specific: "Shop Golf Clubs" and "Shop Treadmills" instead of repeated "Shop Now".
- 5. Drop the "=", the all-caps words and the emoji from the subject line.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Black Friday ends Sunday: up to 50% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Top deals on shoes, golf & gear: 3 days left`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Treadmills from $699.95, clubs up to $200 off, plus $20 ScoreCard+ bonus.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Doorbusters end Sunday at 11:59 PM PT. Shop before they're gone.`
