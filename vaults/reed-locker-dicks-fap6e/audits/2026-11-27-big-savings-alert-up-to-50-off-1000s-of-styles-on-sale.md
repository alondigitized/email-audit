# Big savings alert! 👏 🔥 Up to 50% off 1000s of styles on sale.
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S Black Friday template again, and it's a competent but exhausting one. The dark green "BIGGEST SALE OF THE YEAR" hero leads straight into one clear offer, "Black Friday: Up to 50% Off", with a red Shop Now button. Category pills for Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Golf and Fan Shop follow.
- It then keeps going: an "EXTRA 25% OFF" clearance banner, adidas and Brooks shoes, Nike fleece, baseball apparel, drinkware, TaylorMade golf balls, a ScoreCard+ $20 bonus reward, and a gift-picks grid. That's about eight offers competing for one click. The hero is strong and the product tiles are well built. The email gives no reason to act now rather than on the next send.
- The subject, "Big savings alert! 👏 🔥 Up to 50% off 1000s of styles on sale.", is nearly identical to the 11/26 "Black Friday has arrived! 🙌 1000s of styles on SALE" and the 11/27 "Incredible Black Friday DEALS = Up to 50% OFF!". A subscriber who got those has seen this one already. It's a solid send that's lost to cadence fatigue.

## What's working

- The hero is bold and on-brand, and the 50% off offer sits directly beneath it.
- The red Shop Now buttons are consistent and unambiguous.
- The category pills let a shopper jump straight to Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Golf or Fan Shop.
- The ScoreCard+ block gives members a concrete $20 bonus reward.
- Each product tile has a specific name and price cue, such as "Sale $59.99 Women's adidas Originals Handball Spezial Shoes".
- No render bugs are visible. Images load and the layout holds.

## What's weak

- Roughly eight offers stack in one scroll: 50% off, extra 25% off clearance, adidas, Brooks, Nike, baseball, drinkware, TaylorMade, ScoreCard+ and the gift picks. The eye has no single destination.
- The email is very long, about 6,800px, and the fine print at the bottom is dense.
- The "Limited Time" labels give no visible deadline in the headline copy. The dates are buried in the small print.
- It repeats the template and offer from the last several Black Friday sends, with no new hook for today.
- Each tile's copy is generic ("Select styles"), and the visible reviews or social proof are missing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut it to one hero, one category row and three products. Move ScoreCard+ and the gift picks to a short footer strip.
- 2. Put the deadline in the hero copy, for example "Ends 11/29 11:59 PM PT", instead of leaving it only in the fine print.
- 3. Vary the creative against the prior sends. Show a different hero or a daily doorbuster so the email isn't a repeat of the last one.
- 4. Add ratings or "Top rated" badges to the product tiles.
- 5. Turn "Select styles" into specifics such as "Save $40 on running shoes".
