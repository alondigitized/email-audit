# 🙌 Best DEALS of the week = up to 50% OFF! 🙌
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent, brand-consistent mass blast from DICK'S. The hero executes well — the 50% OFF is immediately dominant, and the price-threshold framing ("$120 and Under Shoes," "$60 and Under Clothing") gives the email a useful editorial shape rather than a raw SKU dump. Those are real creative choices that earn attention past the hero.
- The problem is everything after that. Four separate modules, a product grid, a curated picks row, and an app download section compete for the same scroll real estate. Every CTA says "SHOP NOW" regardless of context. Nothing is personalized, there is no deadline, and no social proof appears anywhere. This is a solid infrastructure email — it will move volume on a Tuesday — but it leaves real conversion on the table.
- Clear verdict: functional but forgettable. Competent marketers shipped this; elite ones would have tightened it to two modules and six words of deadline copy.

## What's working

- **Hero is instant**: The red 50% OFF callout is the first thing the eye lands on — no hunting required.
- **Price-threshold editorial**: "$120 and Under Shoes" / "$60 and Under Clothing" is a smarter hook than a percentage alone; it answers "what can I actually afford?"
- **No render bugs**: Layout is clean, images load, text is legible throughout, no overlapping elements.
- **Category nav strip**: MEN'S | WOMEN'S | GOLF | TEAM SPORTS | CLEARANCE gives fast segmentation above the main modules.
- **Brand consistency**: Red/white/black palette is on-brand and tight from hero to footer.

## What's weak

- **Every CTA says "SHOP NOW"**: Five sections, five identical buttons — no pull, no specificity, no reason to choose one over another.
- **Subject line spam signals**: 🙌 bookends + "DEALS" in ALL CAPS + trailing exclamation mark read as low-quality blast to both filters and humans.
- **Zero personalization**: No name, no preferred sport/category, no returning-customer signal anywhere.
- **No urgency**: "Best deals of the week" is soft — no end date, no countdown, no "ends Sunday."
- **No social proof**: Not a single star rating or review on any featured product.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill the emoji bookends and ALL CAPS "DEALS" in subject** — the 🙌🙌 pattern is the single biggest drag on open rate; replace with a specific product or dollar threshold.
- 2. **Make each CTA match its section headline** — "Shop Shoes Under $120" and "Shop Clothing Under $60" outperform "SHOP NOW" in every test; this is free lift.
- 3. **Add a deadline** — even "Ends Sunday" adds credibility and urgency without feeling manipulative.
- 4. **Surface ScoreCard/member pricing if recipient is a member** — an entirely missed loyalty hook that would separate this send from a competitor.
- 5. **Cut to two modules** — the curated picks grid at the bottom dilutes the hero offer; two focused modules with specific CTAs will outperform five shallow ones.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 50% off HOKA, Adidas + more — this week only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Shoes under $120. Clothing under $60. Ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Running, hiking, golf — your category is on sale. Don't miss the reset.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Top brands, real prices. Stock moves fast on these.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — DICK'S Sporting Goods Promotional Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is structurally sound for an Epsilon/Harmony-built campaign, but three confirmed defects in the JSON-LD block will silently kill Gmail Promotions Tab rendering, and a placeholder comment signals a missing suppression-list link slot.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Click-tracking redirect domain:** All CTAs route through `app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...` with long opaque tokens. No UTM parameters are visible pre-redirect; final destination UTMs cannot be verified from the source alone — see §6.

**Open-tracking pixel:** Present at `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/v610000019e282b6ba6901143f4bbc786c8/...` via a `display:none` `<img>`. Standard and functional.

**Logo href (header):** Routes through the same click-tracking redirect — confirmed functional pattern.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**JSON-LD contains illegal JavaScript-style comments** — this is the most impactful rendering defect:

```json
{ // GIF & WEBP images are not supported and will be filtered out.
  // Sample image is 538x138, 3.9 aspect ratio
  "@type": "PromotionCard",
  // DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!
  "image": ""
}
```

`//` comments are not valid JSON. Any JSON parser (Gmail, validators) will reject the entire `<script type="application/ld+json">` block, meaning **all three schema objects fail silently** — no Promotions Tab card, no discount badge, no offer annotation.

**Logo `<img>` alt text:** The source is truncated (`src="https://dsg.images.harmony.epsilon.com/_ph…`), so alt text cannot be confirmed. Flag for QA verification against the full source.

**Preheader text implementation:** `font-size:0; line-height:0; height:0` on the containing `<td>` is a standard suppress-from-body technique. No issue.

**MSO/Outlook conditional CSS:** Present and structured correctly (`<!--[if (gte mso 9)|(IE)]>`). `border-collapse:collapse` and `a:link` overrides are in place.

**Web font loading:** Archivo loaded via `fonts.gstatic.com` (v7) inside `@media screen` only — Outlook correctly falls back to Arial. No issue, though `v7` is a pinned version; if Google deprecates that path the fallback activates silently.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**`header-non-sc-member` table ID** implies a ScoreCard-member variant should exist in the template logic. Cannot confirm from this source alone whether the conditional branch is wired correctly — verify in the ESP template.

**`DiscountOffer` schema fields are empty stubs:**

```json
"description": "",
"discountCode": "",
"availabilityEnds": ""
```

If these were meant to carry merge tokens (e.g., `%%PROMO_CODE%%`), they were never populated. `discountCode` as an empty string will cause Gmail to suppress the discount badge even if the schema were otherwise valid.

**`availabilityStarts` is hardcoded to `"2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"`** — a six-year-old stale placeholder. This field should either reference today's send date or be omitted.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` comment is present in the live source:**

```html
<td id="ssl" ... style="font-size:0; height:0;">
  <img ... style="display:none; ..."/>
  <!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->
  $100 & under select shoes is this way ➡️
</td>
```

The placeholder comment was not replaced with a SafeUnsubscribe or suppression-list tracking pixel link. If the ESP uses this slot for list-level unsubscribe tokens, its absence could affect suppression accuracy. Verify with the Epsilon production template.

**Unsubscribe mechanism:** HTML is truncated before the footer — cannot confirm a CAN-SPAM-compliant unsubscribe link and physical address are present. This must be verified against the full source before send.

**Sender authentication:** Cannot be assessed from HTML alone. DKIM, SPF, and DMARC must be verified against the `DSG@e.dcsg.com` sending domain's DNS records and the raw message headers post-send.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All CTAs go through `app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...` redirect URLs. The final destination URLs and their UTM parameters are not inspectable from the HTML source. Required verification:

- Click a sample CTA through the redirect and confirm the landing URL carries `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, and `utm_campaign` values consistent with the campaign
- Confirm the "Best DEALS of the week / up to 50% OFF" landing page reflects current inventory and the stated discount range (≤50%)

---
