# Up to 50% off expires TONIGHT ⏰ Shop The Fall Deal Drop & save BIG on top brands
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-07**
## Executive summary

- This is the Fall Deal Drop creative from 10/5 and 10/6 with a "Final Hours" countdown banner added. A red 07:59:48 timer sits on top, then a big "UP TO 50% OFF / FALL DEAL DROP" hero, "3 Days of Savings," and a Shop Now button. The deadline is real (10/7, 11:59 PM PT), and the timer is the most credible urgency device DICK'S has used this week.
- The body is crowded. The 70%-off clearance block, which ran as its own email on 10/6 and 10/7, is stacked underneath, followed by the ScoreCard $10/$20 bonus reward block. Three offers compete in one scroll, and every CTA is the same generic "Shop Now". The hero's "3 Days of Savings" and "Last Chance" labels also contradict each other on the final day.
- Judgement: a solid urgency send held back by repetition and by too many offers. The product picks at the bottom are the weakest section. It is a decent last-day reminder, but nothing in it is new.

## What's working

- The "Final Hours 07:59:48" countdown bar is the first thing you see and makes the deadline feel real.
- The hero is bold and unmistakable, with "UP TO 50% OFF" in high-contrast green, white and red over a dark sky.
- The green Shop Now button sits directly under the offer copy with a clear valid-through date.
- The ScoreCard $10 / ScoreCard+ $20 bonus block gives members a concrete reason to buy.
- Category pill links (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Team Sports, Fan Shop) make it easy to jump to a category.

## What's weak

- The "3 Days of Savings" headline is stale next to a "Final Hours" timer and the "Last Chance" eyebrow.
- The 70%-off clearance block repeats the email from 10/6 and 10/7, which dilutes the 50% hero and makes the send feel like a recycled mashup.
- Every button reads "Shop Now," "Get Coupon," or "Join Now", with no verb-plus-noun copy or product link.
- The product picks (adidas Samba, Nike joggers, New Balance 530) have no prices, no savings and no "% off" tags, so they don't connect to the "up to 50% off" promise.
- The fine print ("Select styles. While quantities last") sits right under the hero in small gray text, and "up to" does a lot of work.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one offer. Lead with the countdown and the 50% hero, and drop the 70% clearance block or move it to a footer link.
- 2. Rewrite the hero sub-headline to match the deadline, for example "Ends tonight at 11:59 PM PT," and replace "3 Days of Savings."
- 3. Add price and savings badges to the product picks, such as "Now $59.99 (was $90)," and link each to its product page.
- 4. Change CTA copy to something specific, such as "Shop deals before midnight."
- 5. Move the ScoreCard block above the product grid, so members see the extra reward before the picks.
- 6. Shorten the subject to under 50 characters.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: up to 50% off Fall Deal Drop ⏰`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 50% off adidas, Nike & more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus earn a $10–$20 ScoreCard bonus reward on $150+. Deals end at 11:59 PM PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Samba OGs, Nike fleece and more, marked down until midnight PT.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, `role="presentation"` layout with a `lang` attribute, a viewport meta tag, MSO conditionals and a hidden preheader. The automated QA gives a 50% pass rate, with one hard fail (the plain-text part is effectively empty) and several unverifiable header checks. The HTML we received is truncated, so findings about the footer, unsubscribe link and UTMs are limited to what QA reported.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All inspected links are redirect-wrapped.** The header link is `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/mclick?params=b.v1.…&pid=v1.…`, with `class="arc-link"` and `data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"`. The destination URL is hidden inside the encoded `params` blob, so the final landing URL can't be verified from source.
- **Link probing was partial.** QA probed 12 material links and skipped 44. It reported no 4xx or 5xx on the probed set, but the 44 skipped links (footer, utility, social) are unverified.
- **The open-tracking pixel is `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=…`.** It has no `alt` attribute (flagged by QA). It is functional, but it should have `alt=""` and explicit `width="1" height="1"`. I can't confirm those from the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Five images have no `alt` attribute (QA WARN).** All are Scene7 icons:
  - `icon-vc-sms-1`
  - `icon-vc-sportsmatter`
  - `icon-vc-bpg-1`
  - `icon-vc-favorite-1`
  - `icon-location-2025-1`

  All use `https://s7d6.scene7.com/is/image/DSGAEMSites/<name>?fmt=png-alpha`. If they are decorative, they need `alt=""`. If they carry meaning, they need descriptive alt text. A missing attribute can make screen readers announce the filename or URL.
- **Plain-text part is effectively missing.** QA reports a FAIL with a text version of 9 characters. This is a multipart/alternative defect that hurts accessibility and spam scoring.
- **Web fonts have a fallback.** `Archivo` is loaded from `fonts.gstatic.com` inside `@media screen` with an `Arial, sans-serif` fallback. No issue.
- **Structure is sound.** `<html lang="en">`, `<title>`, the viewport meta tag, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, `max-width:600px` wrappers, MSO ghost tables and a hidden preheader (`#pht-spacer`, `mso-hide:all`) are all present. No issues found.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`) are visible in the available source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are not found (QA WARN).** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed as a sender defect.
- **`Authentication-Results` is not found (WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status can't be assessed. The From domain is `em.dcsg.com`.
- **Unsubscribe link and physical address can't be confirmed.** QA says an unsubscribe link is always included in the probe set. The footer is outside the truncated source, so the unsubscribe link and the postal address can't be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
UTM parameters and landing URLs can't be audited. Every link is wrapped in the `mclick` redirect, and the destination is encoded in `params`. The probed links produced no reported errors. Whether the CTAs land on the "Fall Deal Drop" and "up to 50% off" content can't be verified from this data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the plain-text part.** Generate a full text alternative; it is currently 9 characters.
2. **Add `alt` to the five Scene7 icons and the open pixel.** Use `alt=""` for decorative images and descriptive text for meaningful ones.
3. **Confirm the headers on the raw message.** Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `em.dcsg.com`. Treat these as unverified, not failed, until the raw MIME is checked.
4. **Resolve the redirect chain in QA.** Follow `mclick` links to the final URL to validate UTMs and landing-page status, and extend probing beyond the top 12.
5. **Capture the full HTML.** Re-run the audit on untruncated source to check the footer, address and unsubscribe markup.
