# Save on style for the whole fam! 🙌 Shop up to 40% off select clothing & shoes
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-20**
## Executive summary

- This is the sprawling weekly-digest format DICK'S has run all month, this time led by "clothing & shoes up to 40% off" instead of the wordmark or clearance angle — most similar structurally to 8/18's "Select clothing & shoes = Up to 40% OFF" (7.00/10) but with a busier, more crowded assembly. The hero is clean and on-brand: a "UP TO 40% OFF" price-tag graphic next to a young woman in a magenta skirt and a Nike sneaker, with a men's shirt tucked in below — legible, no render bugs, and it matches the subject line's "whole fam" framing reasonably well since it shows both a men's and women's look.
- Where it falls apart is everything after the hero. The email crams in a ScoreCard+ 3X Points pitch, football gear, golf balls, MLB gear, cardio equipment, and a second "up to 60% off clearance" mega-banner before finally getting to a six-item product grid — this is not a "shop clothing for the family" email, it's the same everything-store digest DICK'S sends six times a week with a different top banner. A recipient scanning for family apparel deals has to scroll past unrelated categories (golf balls, treadmills, MLB jerseys) to find anything on-theme.
- Judgement: functional, on-brand, zero visual bugs, but this is cadence fatigue on display — the subject over-promises a focused family-clothing sale and the body delivers the same undifferentiated everything-digest DICK'S has been running all month.

## What's working

- Hero banner is clean and legible: bold "UP TO 40% OFF" tag graphic paired with real apparel/footwear imagery, no overlapping text or broken assets.
- Subject's "whole fam" promise is at least partially backed by the hero showing both a women's and men's look.
- Clear, consistent green "Shop Now" CTA buttons throughout — unambiguous and repeated at every section.
- ScoreCard+ 3X Points module is visually distinct (green/black) and calls out a real, time-bound member benefit ("Today Only," valid through 8/20 11:59PM PT).
- Bottom product grid gives concrete, specific SKUs (Nike backpack, New Balance 530, HOKA Clifton 10) rather than vague category links.

## What's weak

- Subject promises "clothing & shoes" but the body is a full-store digest — golf balls, football gear, MLB gear, and cardio equipment dilute the family-apparel theme entirely.
- A second unrelated mega-banner ("UP TO 60% OFF SELECT CLEARANCE") competes with the hero for attention and repeats a creative beat from 8/19's clearance send almost verbatim.
- No pricing specificity anywhere except the two banners and the $39.98 golf balls — most modules just say "up to X% off" with no anchor price.
- This is essentially the same shape as three-plus prior sends this month (8/16, 8/18, 8/19) — a subscriber who's opened those will recognize the format immediately and may not scroll.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email down to an actual family-clothing/footwear send — drop football gear, golf balls, MLB, and cardio equipment entirely, or move them to a separate digest. A themed subject deserves a themed body.
- 2. Move the product grid (Nike backpack, socks, sneakers, HOKA) up near the hero — it's the most concrete, clickable content in the email and it's currently buried near the bottom.
- 3. Kill the redundant second "up to 60% off clearance" banner in this send — it's a near-repeat of 8/19's clearance creative and just adds scroll fatigue without adding a distinct offer.
- 4. Add at least one concrete price point in the hero copy (e.g., "kids' tees from $X") to back up the "40% off" headline with something scannable.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Kids' & family styles: up to 40% off Nike, CALIA & more`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New markdowns for the whole family — up to 40% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fresh fall looks for everyone, from $X`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Nike, CALIA, VRST and more — while sizes last`
