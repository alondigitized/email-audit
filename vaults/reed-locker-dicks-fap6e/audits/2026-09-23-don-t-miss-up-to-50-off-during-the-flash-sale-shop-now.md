# Don't miss up to 50% OFF during the Flash Sale! Shop now. ⚡ ➡️
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the same twilight-into-orange "FLASH" hero DICK'S teased in the 9/23 "Up to 50% off starts now!" send (5.00/10) — but this version has a real production defect: the hero text reads "FPO Urgency" directly above the headline, which is placeholder/internal label copy that clearly wasn't stripped before send. On top of that, the giant "FLASH" wordmark is cropped at the right edge so it reads "FLAS" — a legibility bug sitting in the single most important pixel real estate in the email.
- Below the broken hero, it's the now-familiar DICK'S kitchen-sink scroll: category nav pills, $100-and-under Nike, fan shop gear, a ScoreCard 3X points panel, bikes, bats, trending picks, and shoe/apparel tiles — all competent, on-brand, nothing new relative to the five prior sends in this history.
- This lands low. The offer and layout are fine by DICK'S usual standard, but a visible "FPO" placeholder string in the hero is the kind of QA miss that actively erodes trust, and it's compounded by the cropped headline. Fix the hero before this goes out again.

## What's working

- Clear, singular offer stated plainly: "Flash Sale: Up to 50% Off" with a green "Shop Now" CTA directly beneath it.
- Fine print (dates, exclusions) is present but small and out of the way, not blocking the offer.
- Category navigation pills (Men's/Women's/Kids'/Shoes/Fan Shop/Halloween Shop) give quick wayfinding right under the hero.
- ScoreCard 3X Points module adds a loyalty-specific incentive beyond the flash sale.
- Consistent DICK'S green/black brand system throughout, with real product photography (bikes, bats, shoes) rather than stock filler.

## What's weak

- "FPO Urgency" placeholder text is visible directly above the hero headline — a clear pre-send QA failure that leaks internal labeling to subscribers.
- The "FLASH" wordmark is cropped at the right edge, rendering as "FLAS" — a broken hero is the worst place for a broken hero.
- This is the same orange lightning-bolt "FLASH" concept and structure as the 9/23 send five days prior, with an obvious pixel-level bug this time — cadence and creative fatigue plus regression.
- Kitchen-sink body scroll (Nike, fan shop, TaylorMade, bikes, bats, trending) buries the actual flash-sale offer after one screen — no returning to reinforce the 50% off hook lower down.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the hero render immediately** — resolve the "FPO Urgency" placeholder leak and the cropped "FLASH" wordmark before any further sends; this is a blocking QA issue, not a style note.
- 2. Re-anchor the flash sale offer lower in the email (e.g., a repeat banner after the fan-shop section) so it isn't abandoned after the first scroll.
- 3. Differentiate the hero treatment from the 9/23 "FLASH" send — new color/crop/photo — to break the visible creative-repeat pattern in this brand's history.
- 4. Move the ScoreCard 3X points module higher, since loyalty members are a warmer audience than the generic scroll suggests.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Flash Sale: up to 50% off, ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Flash Sale picks are up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shoes, gear, and bikes — all on sale through 11:59PM PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Score 3X points on TaylorMade while the Flash Sale runs.`
