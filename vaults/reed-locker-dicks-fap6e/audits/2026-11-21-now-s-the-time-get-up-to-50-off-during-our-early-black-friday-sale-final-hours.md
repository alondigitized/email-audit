# 🗣️ Now's the time! ⏰ Get up to 50% off during our Early Black Friday Sale (final hours!)
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is the "final hours" send of a three-day Early Black Friday run. The live 07:59:29 countdown bar is the strongest element, and it delivers the urgency the subject promises. The red "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY SALE" hero with "Up to 50% Off" and one dark red "Shop Now" button states the offer in about a second.
- The body then undercuts the urgency. Below the hero there are three more offers: 40% off top brands, a drinkware tile, and 80% off clearance. There is also a six-item gift grid and a long footer. Only the 50% hero is tied to the countdown. The 80% clearance tile says it runs through 11/22, so it outlasts the "final hours" claim. A reader can't tell what actually expires tonight. This is the same pile-on pattern as the 11/20 "expire tomorrow" send and the 11/21 "Last day!" send earlier today.
- Judgement: a solid, readable promo with real urgency in the top third. It is the third near-identical DICK'S send in about 24 hours, and the lower half works against its own deadline.

## What's working

- The countdown timer bar (07:59:29) is a credible, visible deadline right under the header.
- The "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY SALE" hero and "Up to 50% Off" are unmissable, with a clear "Final Hours" tag in red.
- The quick-nav pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Holiday Shop) let shoppers go straight to their category.
- The ScoreCard bar and "Order Today. Sport it Tomorrow" shipping line show loyalty and fulfilment benefits up top.
- The product grid (Stanley, Callaway, Nike Kids') names real products, which gives the gifting angle some substance.

## What's weak

- Four stacked offers (50%, 40%, drinkware, 80%) dilute the "final hours" message. The 80% tile runs through 11/22, so it contradicts the urgency.
- The fine print under the hero is small and dense. "Select styles," "while quantities last" and "not in conjunction with other sales" make the 50% claim feel hedged.
- The gift grid shows no prices or discounts, so there is nothing to pull a click.
- The email is very long, with a heavy footer. The hero deadline is the only urgent thing, and the rest reads as a generic catalog.
- This is the third send in a row with an "up to X% off" hero (see "Last day! 🚨 Don't miss up to 50% OFF…" earlier today), so the creative feels repetitive.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the countdown, the 50% hero and one secondary block. Drop or move the 80% clearance tile, since it conflicts with the "final hours" claim.
- 2. Add price or percentage callouts to the gift grid (for example "Stanley Quencher, now $X") so the products carry the offer.
- 3. Name what ends tonight in the hero copy: "Ends 11:59 PM PT tonight," not just "Final Hours."
- 4. Shorten the legal copy under the hero and move the exclusions to a single footer line.
- 5. Pick one hero product with a real price instead of a generic sale banner, to break the pattern of identical sends.

- **Subject Alt B:** `8 hours left on Early Black Friday deals ⏰`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sneakers, gear and gifts, all marked down until 11:59 PM PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Last call: free same-day pickup on thousands of styles.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally sound: it has a doctype, `lang="en"`, a viewport meta, `role="presentation"` layout tables and Outlook MSO conditionals. The one confirmed failure is a 9-character plain-text part. The remaining compliance and authentication warnings are most likely artifacts of the AgentMail relay rather than sender defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links reported.** The QA probe covered 12 material links and 43 were skipped, so only about 22% of links were verified. Footer, utility and social links were deprioritized and are unverified.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1...` is placed as the first child of `<body>`. It is 1x1, has no `alt`, and has no `style="display:block"` or `border:0`. It sits outside any table, so some clients may render a stray gap or inline-image artifact.
- **Tracking domain:** `data.em.dcsg.com` shares the parent domain of the From address (`em.dcsg.com`), which is good for alignment.
- **Truncated source:** the HTML was cut off after the header, so I can't evaluate the CTA hrefs or the unsubscribe URL.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` attributes (confirmed by QA, 7 images):**
  - `1115-allbrandsNEW2` (`s7d6.scene7.com/is/image/DSGAEMSites/1115-allbrandsNEW2?fmt=jpeg&qlt=100&wid=1200&hei=1600...`) is a 1200×1600 image that appears to carry content. Without `alt`, a screen reader or an image-blocked client conveys nothing.
  - `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1` and `icon-location-2025-1` are icons. If they are decorative, they should have an explicit `alt=""`. If they sit beside text labels, `alt=""` is correct. If they are standalone, they need descriptive `alt` text.
  - The open pixel should also carry `alt=""`.
- **Preheader:** `#pht-spacer` contains only `&nbsp;`/`&zwnj;` padding. The `#ssl` cell still holds the template comment `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->`, so no secondary preheader text was inserted. Inbox preview will fall back to body text.
- **Stray `<link rel="icon">`** in `<head>` has no effect in email clients and is stripped by most of them.
- **Dimension attributes:** the wrapper uses `width="600"` with `width:100%; max-width:600px`. This is fine, but `max-width` is ignored in some Outlook versions. The MSO ghost table is 584px, so this is likely handled.
- **Web fonts:** Archivo is loaded via `@font-face` inside `@media screen`, with an `Arial, sans-serif` fallback. This is acceptable.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the available source. This check is limited to the truncated portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool itself attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly not capturing the headers. I can't confirm a sender-side defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe headers, so verify against the raw message at the origin.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` absent.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture. This is a relay limitation, not evidence of failure.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** not visible in the truncated source, so unverified. The QA tool reports it always probes an unsubscribe link, but it lists no result for it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No UTM parameters or landing-page URLs are visible in the truncated source, so I can't assess them.
- The `class="arc-link" data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"` attributes on the header link suggest links are routed through a tracking or deep-link redirector. The final destination and parameter retention were not verified.
- The 43 skipped links are unaudited for landing-page and UTM alignment.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a real plain-text part.** This is the only hard FAIL. At 9 characters it is effectively empty, and a multipart/alternative with a text body helps spam scoring.
2. **Add `alt` text.** Use a descriptive `alt` for `1115-allbrandsNEW2` and `alt=""` for the decorative icons and the tracking pixel.
3. **Fill the preheader.** Replace the `Replace/Paste SSL Here` placeholder with actual preview text, or remove the dead comment.
4. **Harden the pixel.** Add `alt=""`, `style="display:block;border:0"`, and ideally move it inside a table cell.
5. **Verify the headers at the origin.** Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results in the raw original message, not the relay copy.
6. **Re-run the audit on the full HTML.** That would cover the footer unsubscribe and address, CTA hrefs and UTM retention, which the truncated source and link sampling couldn't.
