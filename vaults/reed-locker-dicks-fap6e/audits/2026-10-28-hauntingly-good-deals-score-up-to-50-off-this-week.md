# 👻 Hauntingly good DEALS! Score up to 50% off this week
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-28**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S weekly-deals roundup in a new layout. The autumn-leaf hero is gone. In its place is a collage of a YETI tumbler, pickleball paddles, a mountain biker and a treadmill, with a red "UP TO 50% OFF" tile. The offer is easy to see, but the collage tells you nothing about what the 50% applies to. "Best Deals of the Week" and a green Shop Now button follow.
- The rest is a long, unfocused catalog. It has a pill nav, a 40% clothing and shoes block, six category thumbnails and six product picks with no prices. Nothing is tied to a persona or a specific offer.
- The subject promises "hauntingly good DEALS" for Halloween, but the body has no Halloween content. This is the fifth "up to 50%" or "up to 75%" send in about four days, after 10/25 ("We've conjured up some DEALS!"), 10/26 and 10/27 ("Fresh deals for fall?"). Cadence fatigue is the main issue. It's a clean, competent send that a subscriber would skim and forget.

## What's working

- The red "UP TO 50% OFF" tile is the strongest element and reads instantly.
- The hero collage is bright and sporty, and it shows several categories at a glance.
- The category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Team Sports) give quick routing.
- The Scorecard points bar and the "Order Today. Sport It Tomorrow." delivery line are useful member and convenience cues.
- The layout renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The Halloween subject has no payoff in the body. There are no spooky visuals or themed products.
- Four different "deal" headlines (50% off, 40% off, "Best Deals of the Week", "top deals") compete, and none says what is actually discounted.
- The product picks have no prices, no sale badges and no percent-off markers, so the discount is never shown on a product.
- Both Shop Now buttons are generic and go to category-level destinations.
- The email is very long, with six category tiles and six products. That dilutes the hero offer.
- There is no deadline. "This week" is soft urgency.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the Halloween hook real, or drop it. Either add a themed hero and a "Halloween weekend" window, or use a plain, honest subject.
- 2. Put prices on the product picks (e.g. "$89.99, was $120") and add a "Save 25%" badge so the 50% claim shows up on real items.
- 3. Replace "Shop Now" with specific CTAs such as "Shop Nike & adidas deals" and "Shop pickleball paddles".
- 4. Cut the category grids to one row and keep one hero plus one secondary offer.
- 5. Give the offer an end date ("Ends Sunday").
- 6. Space out these promo sends, or vary the creative so they don't read as the same email.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 50% off Nike, adidas & more — ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `👻 Not scary: up to 50% off top brands this week`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus up to 40% off clothing & shoes. Same-day pickup available.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Paddles, tumblers, treadmills and more. Prices drop through Sunday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is structurally sound (`lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` layout tables, MSO conditionals, hidden preheader block). The automated QA found one hard failure: the plain-text part is effectively empty (9 chars). It also flagged 12 images without alt text and could not verify auth or unsubscribe headers. The source was truncated, so link, UTM and footer checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Coverage gap:** QA probed 12 of 57 links and skipped 45 (footer, utility and social). No broken-link results were reported, so there is no evidence of dead links. The skipped links are unverified, not confirmed good.
- **Open pixel:** `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1...&configId=512ffb51-...` is a 1x1 `<img>` with `width="1" height="1"` and no `alt` or `style`. It works, but it has no `display:block` or `border:0`, and it sits directly after `<body>`. This is low severity.
- **Click-tracking and UTM:** these can't be assessed because the source is truncated before the link markup (`<a target="_blank" class="arc…`).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text:** QA flagged 12 of the images it listed as missing alt text (the report shows 13 rows, counting the open pixel).
  - **Likely informational images:** seven category banners on `s7d6.scene7.com/is/image/DSGAEMSites/`: `clothing-2026-1028-swd-4pm`, `golf-…`, `outdoor-…`, `team-sports-…`, `drinkware-…`, `lic-…` and `fitness-…`. If these are the click targets, the links have no accessible name and the content disappears when images are blocked.
  - **Icons:** `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1` and `icon-location-2025-1`. These should carry `alt=""` if they are decorative.
  - **Unclear which case applies:** QA doesn't say whether `alt` is absent or empty. Absent is a defect, while `alt=""` is valid for decorative images.
- **Preheader:** `<td id="ssl">` still contains the template comment `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->`. The adjacent `#pht-spacer` holds only `&zwnj;&nbsp;` padding and no preheader text. Inboxes will therefore pull the first body text as the preview instead of the intended secondary subject line.
- **Editor artifacts in production:** `<body class="is-html-mode" data-contenteditable="true">`. This is harmless to rendering but is leftover authoring-tool markup.
- **Fonts and `<head>`:** Archivo is loaded from fonts.gstatic.com inside `@media screen` with an Arial fallback, so the fallback is fine. The `<link rel="icon">` in the `<head>` has no effect in email clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The remainder is truncated, so this isn't a full clearance.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is 9 characters.** There is effectively no `text/plain` alternative. This hurts deliverability scoring and accessibility.
- **[WARN] No `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip these. Treat it as unverified until the raw message headers are checked. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **[WARN] No `Authentication-Results` header.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `em.dcsg.com` can't be confirmed from this capture. This is the same relay limitation.
- **Footer:** the visible in-body unsubscribe link and postal address are cut off by truncation. QA says an unsubscribe link was always included in probing, but it reported no result for it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. No hrefs, UTM parameters or destination URLs are visible in the truncated source or the QA output. The banner filenames (`*-2026-1028-swd-4pm`) suggest a dated "swd" campaign, but that isn't evidence of tagging.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a real `text/plain` part.** It should have a meaningful body, the unsubscribe URL and the postal address. This fixes the only hard failure.
2. **Add `alt` text to the seven category banners.** Use the category name plus the offer, and use `alt=""` on the five decorative icons. Add `alt=""` to the open pixel and `style="display:block;border:0"`.
3. **Fill or remove the SSL/preheader slot.** Put the intended preheader text in `#ssl` and delete the `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` comment.
4. **Re-run header checks against the raw message,** not the relay copy. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass.
5. **Re-audit with the full HTML and a wider link probe.** Extend probing beyond 12 links to cover the footer and unsubscribe, and verify UTM consistency across CTAs.
6. **Strip editor attributes** (`is-html-mode`, `data-contenteditable`) in the export step.
