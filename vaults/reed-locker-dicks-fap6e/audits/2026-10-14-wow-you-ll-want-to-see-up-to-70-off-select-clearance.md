# Wow! You'll want to see up to 70% OFF select clearance. 👀
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-14**
## Executive summary

- This is the same clearance send DICK'S ran on 10/11 and earlier today, with a new subject line. The hero is a giant white "UP TO 70% OFF" over autumn leaves, followed by "New Markdowns Added: Up to 70% Off Select Clearance" and a green Shop Now button. The offer registers in about a second. As a clearance email it works.
- Below the hero it becomes a long category catalog. There are eight or more stacked modules: clothing and shoes, Owala, team sports, fan shop, golf, outdoor, fitness, and a product grid. Each has its own discount tier (40%, 50%) and its own pill buttons. The 70% hero draws you in, and then the email offers a dozen small choices with no single product to click.
- My judgment is that it's competent but repetitive. It would be a 7 on its own. It is also the third near-identical "new markdowns / 70% off" send in four days, and the subject is the only thing that changed.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 70% OFF" hero is huge and high-contrast, and the price message lands immediately.
- The green Shop Now button sits right under the headline and is easy to find.
- The autumn-leaf backdrop fits the October timing.
- The "Explore more deals" pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 and Under, Fan Shop) let a shopper jump to a category quickly.
- The footer is clean, and the header strip with the "Order Today. Sport It Tomorrow." fulfillment line adds a useful reassurance.

## What's weak

- There is no deadline or credible urgency. "Limited Time" is the only cue, and there is no end date.
- The body has no clear hero product. Eight or more category blocks, each with its own buttons, dilute the click.
- The offer tiers are inconsistent: 70% in the hero, then 40% and 50% in the modules. The 70% headline feels like a bait-and-switch by the time you scroll.
- Most CTAs are generic ("Shop Now", "Club Deals", "Cleat Deals") with no specific product or price.
- The email is very long, and the fine print is small and grey. Both the "Valid online only" line under the hero and the footer legal block are hard to read.
- It repeats the 10/11 "Did somebody say NEW markdowns?!" and 10/14 morning "New markdowns added!" creative almost exactly. That creates fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero a specific product. For example, show a $59.99 Nike or adidas shoe, marked down from $110, with a "Shop Nike Clearance" button. A real price outperforms a percentage range.
- 2. Add a real deadline, such as "Ends Sunday" or a countdown. The 10/12 "Final Hours" countdown send used urgency well, and this one has none.
- 3. Vary the creative. If clearance is the topic every day, rotate the hero category (shoes, outerwear, team sports) so the sends don't look identical.
- 4. Cut the body from about 8 modules to 3 or 4, and give each one a priced product.
- 5. Make the CTA copy specific: "Shop Clearance Shoes" and "Shop Clearance Fan Gear".
- **Subject Alt A:** `Clearance just dropped: up to 70% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Shoes, fan gear & more, up to 70% off 👀`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns on Nike, adidas & DSG, plus free store pickup today.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Clearance prices from $9.99. Sizes go fast, so shop before the weekend.`
