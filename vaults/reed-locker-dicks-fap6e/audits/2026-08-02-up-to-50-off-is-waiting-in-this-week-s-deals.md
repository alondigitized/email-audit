# Up to 50% off is waiting in this week's deals
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-02**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S pivoting away from the red "CLEARANCE" / green "FLASH SALE" template it's been running non-stop since 7/30 into a softer, beach/pool-themed "weekly roundup" — a blue hero with beach balls and "UP TO 50% OFF" replaces the urgency-driven kitchen sink. That's a welcome break in creative, but the body underneath is the exact same long kitchen-sink cascade (shoes, adidas, golf, life vests, pickleball, fan shop, summer gear grid) this brand has sent at least six times in the past five days.
- The hero number itself is a soft bait: "up to 50% off" up top, but the actual category blocks below mostly deliver 25–40% off (30% shoes, 40% adidas clothing, 25% pickleball). Only the Fan Shop block hits 50%, and it's buried six sections down. Subscriber trust erodes when the headline number isn't the number you actually see repeated.
- Net: a competent, clean-rendering send with real loyalty-program visibility (Scorecard bar) and genuinely seasonal styling, but it's the Nth near-identical promo this week with a subject line too generic to stand out in an inbox already full of DICK'S mail. Mid-pack — a 6-7, not a standout.

## What's working

- Render is completely clean — no overlapping text, no broken images, no lorem-ipsum, every product photo loads.
- Scorecard loyalty callout sits right at the top ("300 Points = $10 Reward" style bar), which is real value for existing members.
- Seasonal creative shift is genuine — pool/beach hero and life-vest/pickleball blocks fit early-August, and the nav row includes a Back-to-School Shop tab that's timely.
- Several sub-offers carry real specificity and dates (e.g., "Valid through 8/15/26," "One Day Only... Valid through 8/20"), which reads more credible than a blanket "ends today."
- Clear single green "SHOP NOW" CTA style is consistent and easy to spot throughout.

## What's weak

- Hero "UP TO 50% OFF" oversells relative to the actual category discounts shown underneath (30%, 40%, 25%) — classic anchor-then-downshift pattern.
- Subject line is completely generic ("this week's deals") with zero category hook or deadline, blending into every other DICK'S send this week.
- The email is enormous — a dozen-plus stacked category blocks (shoes, adidas x2, golf, life vests, pickleball, fan shop, summer gear grid) with no way to skim quickly on mobile.
- Every CTA button says the identical "SHOP NOW" with no variation, so nothing signals which block is the actual best deal.
- Per the brand history, this is at least the sixth DICK'S promo since 7/30 — cadence fatigue is real and this send does nothing to acknowledge it (no "new this week" framing).

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick the single strongest, most specific offer (e.g., "$100 Off Odyssey Putters" or the Fan Shop 50% off) and put it in the subject/hero instead of a generic "up to 50%" — specificity beats a vague top-line number that the body doesn't consistently deliver.
- 2. Add a credible deadline to the subject to differentiate from the plain "this week" framing and match the urgency that's actually printed in the fine print of individual blocks.
- 3. Cut the email length — collapse the 12+ category blocks into a curated top-5, with a "See all this week's deals" link for the rest, to fight the scroll fatigue this brand's cadence is already creating.
- 4. Vary CTA copy per block ("Shop Shoes," "Shop Putters," "Shop Pickleball") instead of repeating "SHOP NOW" everywhere.
- **Subject Alt A:** `$100 off Odyssey putters + up to 50% off Fan Shop gear`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New this week: up to 50% off Fan Shop, ends Thursday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 30% off shoes, 40% off adidas, and Scorecard points on every order.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Golf, pickleball, fan gear, and back-to-school picks — all in one place.`

# Technical Audit — DICK'S Sporting Goods "Up to 50% off" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard Epsilon Harmony/DCSG-platform HTML email with MSO conditionals and mobile breakpoints; automated QA measured a 43% pass rate, flagging one broken link (unsubscribe), missing alt text on 10 images, and gaps in unsubscribe/authentication headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken unsubscribe redirect (FAIL):** `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/U/v610000019fc1f66d75b2ee50f4bbcf76f8/7e5d69971135457c0000021ef3a0bcff` resolves to an error page — QA reports "unknown url type: '/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription'" on the link labeled "Unsubscribe."
- All probed links route through the Responsys/Epsilon click-tracking redirector (`app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...`), e.g. the header logo link: `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/v610000019fc1f66d75b2ee50f4bbcf76f8/7e5d69971135457c0000021ef3a0bcc6/7e5d6997-1135-457c-80f8-5a62cf5d74fa?__dU__=...`. This is expected redirector behavior, not itself a defect, but it means final destination URLs can't be verified from source alone.
- QA probed 12 of 63 material links (51 skipped per the tool's click-likelihood ranking); footer/utility/social links were deprioritized. This limits confidence that all CTAs were exercised — only the unsubscribe failure surfaced.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` text on 10 images** (QA WARN), including the open/tracking pixel and multiple content images:
  - `7e5d69971135457c00004c5a42963aa1` (open-tracking pixel, `id="ssl"`)
  - `mens-adidas.jpg`, `womens-adidas.jpg`, `life-vests.jpg`, `pickleball.jpg`, `fan.png`
  - Footer icons: `icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png`, `icon-location-2025.png`
  
  The pixel and decorative footer icons should use `alt=""`; the product images (`mens-adidas.jpg`, etc.) need descriptive `alt` text for screen-reader users.
- MSO conditional blocks and mobile `@media (max-width:480px)` rules are present and structured correctly (`.mobile-off`, `.header-*`, `.p-*` utility classes), no malformed conditional comments detected.
- The inline `<style>` MSO block has a stray unclosed quote: `div {background-color:inherit; ...padding:0;"}` — the trailing `"` before `}` is a leftover artifact and technically invalid CSS syntax inside the MSO conditional, though Outlook typically tolerates it.
- Schema.org JSON-LD comment notes "GIF & WEBP images are not supported and will be filtered out" — no GIF/WEBP `src` attributes appear in the visible source, so no violation found in the truncated markup.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The `PromotionCard` and `DiscountOffer` JSON-LD blocks contain **unresolved empty template fields**: `"description": ""`, `"discountCode": ""`, `"availabilityEnds": ""`, and `"image": ""` (with a literal placeholder comment `"DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!"` left in the shipped source). These are Gmail annotation/schema markup fields, not customer-facing merge tags, but sending them empty means Gmail promo-card rendering (discount badge, expiration) will not populate.
- `availabilityStarts` is hardcoded to `"2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"` — a stale date unrelated to the current campaign, suggesting this JSON-LD block was copy-pasted from a template and never updated.
- No customer-facing merge tokens (e.g., `{{FirstName}}`) are visible in the truncated body; nothing to flag there.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **Unsubscribe link is broken** — see §2. This is a functional CAN-SPAM risk: the mechanism must work, and QA confirms it routes to an error page (`/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription`).
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** (QA WARN) — may be a relay-capture limitation (AgentMail) rather than a sender-side omission; cannot confirm absence at the source.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found** (QA WARN) — RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe support cannot be confirmed.
- **Authentication-Results header not found** (QA WARN) — SPF/DKIM pass/fail status is unknown from this capture; cannot confirm authentication compliance either way.
- No physical mailing address was visible in the truncated HTML provided — cannot confirm CAN-SPAM address requirement from the given source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- All sampled links point to the `app.dickssportinggoods.com` Responsys click-tracker rather than directly to `dickssportinggoods.com`, so UTM parameters and final landing-page URLs are not observable in this source — cannot verify landing-page alignment without following redirects.
- No `utm_*` query parameters appear on any link in the visible/truncated markup.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the unsubscribe redirect target (`/U/...ff`) — it currently 404s to `/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription`; this is the highest-priority fix given compliance exposure.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to `mens-adidas.jpg`, `womens-adidas.jpg`, `life-vests.jpg`, `pickleball.jpg`, `fan.png`; set `alt=""` on the tracking pixel and the four footer icon images.
3. Populate or remove the empty JSON-LD `PromotionCard`/`DiscountOffer` fields (`description`, `discountCode`, `image`, `availabilityEnds`) and correct the stale `availabilityStarts` date so Gmail promo annotations render correctly, or strip the block if unused.
4. Remove the stray trailing `"` in the MSO `<style>` block's `div` rule.
5. Confirm with deliverability tooling (outside this HTML capture) that `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` headers are actually being sent — this audit could not confirm their presence or absence due to relay capture limitations.
