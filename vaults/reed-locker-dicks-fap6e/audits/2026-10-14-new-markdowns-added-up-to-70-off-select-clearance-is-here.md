# New markdowns added! Up to 70% off select clearance is here. 📍
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-14**
## Executive summary

- This is a loud, clean clearance send. The hero is a giant "UP TO 70% OFF" over autumn leaves, followed by "New Markdowns Added: Up to 70% Off Select Clearance" and a green Shop Now button. The offer lands in about a second. It is also the third near-identical clearance beat in four days, after the Oct 11 sends ("Did somebody say NEW markdowns?!" and "Up to 70% OFF select clearance + NEW markdowns just dropped") and the Oct 13 "Up to 50% off" sends. "New markdowns added" is hard to believe a third time.
- The body is a long, unfocused scroll. It covers HOKA running shoes, DSG, Stanley x Devin Booker, Walter Hagen BOGO, NHL, cardio equipment, a second "50% OFF" block, a credit card promo and a product grid. Only the hero and the first few tiles are really about clearance. The clearance message gets diluted into a general catalog. The credit card block and the second giant "50% OFF" also compete with the hero. A subscriber sees two big percentages with different numbers.
- Judgement: competent and on-brand, but repetitive and over-stuffed. It will perform about as well as the prior sends and add no new reason to click.

## What's working

- The hero is unmissable: the oversized "70% OFF" over fall foliage reads instantly and fits the season.
- The Shop Now button sits directly under the headline, so the path from offer to action is short.
- The "Explore more deals" pill row (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 and Under, Fan Shop, All Sale) gives quick category entry points.
- The two-up product tiles with specific offers (up to 40% off HOKA, up to 50% off DSG, BOGO 50% off Walter Hagen) give people concrete things to click.
- No visible render bugs. Images load and spacing is consistent.

## What's weak

- The email is very long, with about ten modules, and the clearance story is buried under unrelated brand and category promos.
- The second "50% OFF" block ("This Week's Deals") undercuts the 70% hero and reads like a leftover from the Oct 13 send.
- The credit card block ($30 bonus reward) interrupts the shopping flow mid-email.
- Almost every CTA is a generic "Shop Now". The "Limited Time" label has no date, so there is no real deadline.
- The fine print is dense and dominates the footer. The hero's "while supplies last / markdowns may have been taken" language softens the offer's honesty.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero, the pill row and 3-4 clearance-only product tiles. Drop the second 50% block and move the credit card to a separate send.
- 2. Differentiate from the last three clearance sends. Show specific marked-down items with before and after prices instead of repeating "new markdowns".
- 3. Add a real deadline ("Ends Sunday") instead of "Limited Time".
- 4. Use category-specific button copy such as "Shop running shoes" or "Shop clearance jackets".
- 5. Shorten the subject to under 50 characters and make it concrete.
- **Subject Alt A:** `70% off clearance: HOKA, DSG & more ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Fall gear marked down up to 70% 🍂`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `HOKA runners from $X, DSG hoodies and Walter Hagen BOGO. See what just dropped.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sizes go fast on markdowns. Order today, pick up in an hour.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally sound (`lang="en"`, viewport meta, `role="presentation"` tables, MSO conditionals, hidden preheader). The one hard failure is the missing plain-text part (9 chars). Alt text is missing on 12 content images plus the tracking pixel. The source was truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link, and most hrefs could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links were reported. The probe covered 12 of 67 links, ranked by click likelihood, and the 55 skipped are mostly footer, utility, and social links.
- The open pixel `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1...&configId=512ffb51-...` is a 1×1 `<img>` placed directly after `<body>`. It has no `alt`, which is also the first QA alt-text warning. This is the ESP's standard tracking endpoint, so there's no defect beyond the missing attribute.
- Click-tracking wrapper URLs and UTM parameters are not visible in the truncated source, so I can't assess them (see section 6).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA, confirmed).** These 12 images have no `alt` attribute:
  - `2026-1014-dks-storewide-6am-1` through `-5`
  - `devin-booker-2026-0915-d`
  - `icon-vc-sms-1`
  - `icon-vc-sportsmatter`
  - `icon-vc-bpg-1`
  - `icon-vc-favorite-1`
  - `icon-location-2025-1`

  The scene7 hero and promo images are likely to carry the offer content. With images blocked, the email will render blank or show filenames. Screen readers will announce the filenames or URLs.
- **Editor artifacts in production markup.** `<body ... class="is-html-mode" data-contenteditable="true">` is left over from the authoring tool. It's harmless to rendering but shouldn't ship.
- **Plain-text part missing** (see section 5). It also affects accessibility and clients that fall back to text.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source or the subject. The truncated portion wasn't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] Plain-text fallback is 9 chars.** The multipart/alternative text part is effectively empty. This hurts spam scoring and text-only clients. It is a real finding, since it comes from the MIME body rather than the relay's headers.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA notes say the AgentMail relay may strip these. Treat them as unverified, not confirmed absent. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check them on the raw message at the origin.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status can't be assessed here. The From domain is `em.dcsg.com`, and alignment can't be verified without the headers.
- **Footer not inspected.** The unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated section, so CAN-SPAM footer compliance is unverified. The QA notes say an unsubscribe link is always probed, and no failure was reported.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The visible source contains no campaign hrefs, and the probe reported no redirect or landing-page failures for the 12 links it checked. I'm not flagging anything, because I'd be guessing.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a real plain-text alternative with the offer headline, key links, and unsubscribe URL.
2. Add `alt` to all 12 images. Use descriptive text for the hero and promo images and `alt=""` for decorative icons. Add `alt=""` to the 1×1 open pixel.
3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` on the raw message at the origin, not through the relay.
4. Check SPF, DKIM, and DMARC alignment for `em.dcsg.com` from the original headers.
5. Strip `class="is-html-mode"` and `data-contenteditable` from the production template.
6. Re-run the audit with the full, untruncated HTML to verify the footer, UTM parameters, and the 55 unprobed links.
