# The 11.11 Sale is HERE! 🗓️ Up to 70% off your faves
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a 1-day "11.11" sale, and the hero doesn't sell it. A dark clock-style graphic shows glowing green letters "H E", presumably part of an animated "HERE" that is frozen on its first frame. Under it, "Up to 70% Off Your Favorite Brands" and a red Shop Now button carry the offer. The offer is strong, but the biggest visual is an ambiguous image with no price or deadline on it.
- The email then stacks eight more modules. They cover running shoes up to 40% off, CALIA scuba pants, DSG fleece, Maxfli and Top Flite golf balls, baseball gloves, table tennis tables, an Extra 25% Off clearance banner and a six-item gift grid. It reads as a catalog, not a one-day event. The 11/11 11:59PM PT deadline sits in small grey fine print, so there is no countdown and no urgency in the render.
- Compare it with the 11/10 "Holiday Deal Dash" sends, whose "Final Hours" countdown and giant gold "UP TO 50% OFF" made the offer land at once. This one has the deeper discount (70% against 50%) but is less focused. A relevant subscriber would still open it, but the email works against its own offer.

## What's working

- "Up to 70% Off Your Favorite Brands" is a clear headline, and the red Shop Now button stands out against the white background.
- Brand chips (CALIA, VRST, DSG, Maxfli, All Sale, All Clearance) let shoppers jump to a brand.
- The "Extra 25% Off Select Clearance Styles" banner is a bold red and gold block that is easy to read.
- Each module has a single CTA button, and the modules with prices ("Starting at $24.98", "Sale $19.98") show concrete numbers.
- The footer is useful: ScoreCard sign-in, Best Price Guarantee, app download badges and store info.

## What's weak

- The hero image is just "H E" on a clock with brand logos. It doesn't show a product, the 70% figure or the date.
- The deadline (11/11 11:59PM PT) is in small grey text under the chips. There is no countdown timer, even though the previous week's sends used one.
- There are too many modules. Running shoes, gloves, ping-pong tables, golf balls and fleece don't add up to one story, and each module pulls attention away from the 70% offer.
- The "Gifts Only at DICK'S" kickers and holiday backdrops are inconsistent. Gift framing and a 70%-off sale compete for the same space.
- The "up to 70%" claim is exclusion-heavy. Each module has its own offer, and the fine print is dense (exclusions, "other offers available in-store"). That weakens how honest the offer feels.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the hero with a static, readable frame: "11.11 SALE: UP TO 70% OFF" plus "Today only, ends 11:59PM PT". Add a live countdown bar like the 11/10 "Final Hours" strip. This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Cut the body to three or four modules. Keep running shoes, the DSG fleece, the 25% clearance banner and one gift module, and drop the rest.
- 3. Move the deadline out of the fine print and into the button area, for example "Shop now – ends tonight".
- 4. Show real discounted products in the hero or directly under it, with the 70% brands (CALIA, VRST) labeled.
- 5. Put the ScoreCard member benefit near the top. It is only in the footer now.
- **Subject Alt A:** `11.11 Sale: up to 70% off, today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Today only: 70% off CALIA, VRST, DSG + more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `One day, 11:59PM PT. Plus extra 25% off clearance and $24.98 fleece.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Running shoes up to 40% off, golf, gloves and gifts. Ends tonight.`
