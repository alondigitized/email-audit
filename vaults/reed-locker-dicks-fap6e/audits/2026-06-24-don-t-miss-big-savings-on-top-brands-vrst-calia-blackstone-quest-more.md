# Don't miss BIG savings on top brands: VRST, CALIA, Blackstone, Quest & more!
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-24**
## Executive summary

- This is at minimum the second DICK'S email on June 24 — following the "NEW DEALS ADDED🚨" send audited at 8/10 — and the creative team has made the smartest subject-line decision of the entire Deal Days run: naming specific brands (VRST, CALIA, Blackstone, Quest) instead of another generic "biggest sale of summer" umbrella. For a subscriber who has VRST or Blackstone on their radar, this is the first email in the series that gives a content-specific reason to re-open rather than a cadence-specific reason to ignore.
- The email body executes the Deal Days playbook competently: green/neon hero banner, tabbed category navigation (Running, Golf, Bags, Summer), "TODAY ONLY" urgency badges on featured modules, and specific-price callouts — VRST at up to 50% off, Quest kayak at $179.99. These are real deals with real numbers, and the layout keeps them visible high in the scroll. No render bugs, no broken images, and the visual hierarchy is clean.
- The problem is arithmetic. This is approximately email eight from DICK'S in 72 hours, and the brand-callout approach — which would be a sharp tool on day one — arrives late and truncated. At 77 characters, the subject gets cut on most phones right around "Blackstone," eliminating half the specificity the strategy depends on.

## What's working

- Brand-name callout in the subject (VRST, CALIA, Blackstone, Quest) is the first concrete content-filter in this entire series — subscribers who care about those brands have a reason to open that wasn't available in any prior send.
- "TODAY ONLY" badges are applied selectively rather than slapped on every module, which preserves credibility.
- VRST "Up to 50% Off Select Active Clothing" is a clear, honest discount statement — no fine print visible, no vague "up to" umbrella hiding a 10%-off floor.
- Quest kayak at $179.99 is a specific price-point deal — the kind of concrete number that triggers real purchase intent, not just browsing.
- Deal Days visual identity (green/yellow hero, tabbed navigation) is coherent and immediately scannable for subscribers who've trained on this series.

## What's weak

- Subject line at ~77 characters is well over the 50-char mobile threshold — "Blackstone, Quest & more!" gets cut on most phones, which destroys the brand-specificity that's the only real hook.
- "SHOP NOW" button copy is identical across every module — nothing signals that one click lands on VRST activewear vs. golf bags vs. kayaks.
- "& more!" at the end of the subject is a hedge that undercuts the precision of naming four brands — commit to the list or make it a round number (five brands, not four + vague tail).
- CALIA and Blackstone — two of the four named brands — are not clearly visible in the upper half of the email render, meaning the subject over-promises on what's immediately accessible.
- No personalization signal anywhere: no name, no "based on your shopping," no member tier — reads like a broadcast to the full list.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the subject to under 50 chars and front-load the best deal.** "VRST 50% off + Quest, Blackstone — today only" (46 chars) surfaces the strongest deal first and survives mobile truncation. Every word before the brand names in the current subject is wasted space.
- 2. **Move CALIA and/or Blackstone above the fold.** If you're naming them in the subject, they need to be visible on open — currently the email either buries them or they're below the visible render area.
- 3. **Differentiate CTA copy per module.** "Shop VRST Deals" / "Shop Kayaks" / "Shop Blackstone" — five seconds of work per module, but it signals intent and improves click quality.
- 4. **Write a preheader that names a price or product, not the shipping threshold.** "Free 2-Day Shipping on $49+" as the inbox preview squanders the brand-callout setup in the subject.
- **Subject Alt A:** `VRST 50% off · Quest, Blackstone on sale — today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 50% off VRST, CALIA + Blackstone ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Outdoor griddles, women's activewear, kayaks — all Deal Days pricing, today only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `$179 kayaks, 50% off VRST, Blackstone on sale — pick yours before tonight.`

## Technical Audit — DICK'S Sporting Goods "Deal Days" Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email uses Epsilon/Harmony for ESP routing and click-tracking. Three confirmed code-level defects exist: invalid JSON-LD syntax that breaks Gmail Promotions Tab annotations, a stale 2020 date in structured data, and a CSS syntax error in the Outlook conditional block.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Open pixel** — `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/v610000019efa4722c0c82667f4bbe5cfc0/...` rendered via a hidden `<img>` in `#ssl`. Standard Epsilon open-tracking; no defect, but the pixel `<img>` is missing an `alt=""` attribute, which can trigger accessibility linter warnings.

**Click-tracking** — All links route through `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/v610000019efa4722c0c82667f4bbe5cfc0/...` (Epsilon redirect). Cannot confirm downstream UTM parameters without following the redirect chain (see §6).

**Logo `src` truncated in source** — `https://dsg.images.harmony.epsilon.com/_phoenix/_dev/AEM/img/headers/brand-logo--dk…` is cut off. The `_dev` path segment in a production send is a risk indicator — confirm the full URL resolves to a production asset, not a dev/staging image host.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Outlook conditional CSS syntax error** — The `<![if (gte mso 9)|(IE)]>` style block contains a malformed `div` rule:

```css
div {background-color:inherit; font-size:0; line-height:100%; margin:0; padding:0;"}
```

The stray `"` before `}` is invalid CSS. Outlook's CSS parser will reject this rule, potentially collapsing `div` spacing in Outlook 2016/2019/365.

**Web font via Google Fonts CDN** — `fonts.gstatic.com` references version `v7` of Archivo (both 400 and 700 weights). This version string is from ~2021; Google periodically purges old version paths. Confirm the URLs still resolve (both woff2 files). Correctly scoped inside `@media screen` to avoid non-rendering clients fetching them.

**`<link rel="icon">` in `<head>`** — Ignored by all major email clients; harmless but adds unnecessary bytes.

**`role="presentation"` on layout tables** — Correctly applied throughout. `lang="en"` on `<html>` is present.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unfired `%%token%%`, `{{token}}`, or `*|token|*` merge syntax visible in the rendered portion.

**JSON-LD placeholder fields not populated** (see §5 for syntax issue):
- `"discountCode": ""` — empty string
- `"description": ""` — empty string
- `"availabilityEnds": ""` — empty string
- `"image": ""` in `PromotionCard` block — empty string (designer note embedded in source: `// DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!` — unfulfilled at send time)

---

### 5. Compliance

**CAN-SPAM / unsubscribe** — HTML is truncated; footer not visible in provided source. Cannot confirm physical mailing address or one-click unsubscribe. Flag for manual footer verification.

**JSON-LD structured data — two confirmed defects:**

1. **JavaScript comments inside `<script type="application/ld+json">`** — JSON does not support `//` comments. The block contains three such comments:
   ```json
   // GIF & WEBP images are not supported...
   // Sample image is 538x138, 3.9 aspect ratio
   // DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!
   ```
   These will cause JSON parse failures in Gmail's Promotions Tab annotation parser, disabling the promotional card and discount badge features entirely.

2. **Stale `availabilityStarts` date** — `"availabilityStarts": "2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"` is a template default from March 2020, six years stale. Gmail's parser may suppress the annotation as expired.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All click links use Epsilon's redirect wrapper. Without resolving the redirect chain, UTM parameter passthrough cannot be confirmed from source alone. The tracking token structure (`v610000019efa4722c0c82667f4bbe5cfc0`) is consistent across open pixel and click URLs, indicating correct cohort binding.

The `_dev` segment in the image CDN hostname (`harmony.epsilon.com/_phoenix/_dev/...`) should be verified — if this resolves to a development/staging image server, images may return 404 or wrong assets in production inboxes.
