# Black Friday is ON! 👏 Check off that holiday list with 100s of DEALS. ✅ 🎁
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth Black Friday or clearance push from DICK'S in three days. The hero is strong: a festive green-and-gold "UP TO 50% OFF" block, then "Black Friday Starts Now" with one red "Shop Now" button. The email also tells you it's the real thing with "Order Today. Sport It Tomorrow" and a store-pickup and delivery banner. The seasonal framing and top-of-email logistics are good.
- The body gives that up. After the hero, it stacks about ten modules, each with its own discount (40%, 30%, 50%, $1600 off, $49.98, $48.98, then another giant "UP TO 70% OFF" banner). The 70% clearance banner competes with the 50% hero, and it repeats the 80% clearance hero from the 11/21 and 11/22 sends with a smaller number. A subscriber who has seen this exact rhythm for three days has little reason to feel urgency.
- Verdict: a competent, seasonal, well-built catalog email. It's too long and too busy to read as a single offer, and the repeated cadence hurts it more than the layout does.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 50% OFF" hero fills the first screen and is festive and on-season.
- The top bar ("Order Today. Sport It Tomorrow") gives a concrete fulfillment promise and adds urgency.
- The hero has one clear red "Shop Now" button under "Black Friday Starts Now."
- The category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Golf, Fan Shop) let people jump to their own interest.
- The holiday store hours module and the "Gift picks" grid are practical for shoppers in the days before Thanksgiving.

## What's weak

- The second giant "UP TO 70% OFF" banner mid-email competes with the 50% hero and muddies the headline offer.
- The email runs very long, with about ten modules and mixed discount framings (%, $ off, fixed prices). The scan path is tiring.
- Every module repeats a small dark "Shop Now" button, so no single CTA stands out.
- The 80%-to-70% clearance creative and the "Black Friday starts now" copy repeat sends from 11/21–11/23.
- Fine print under the hero and the clearance block is tiny and dense, with exclusions and date limits.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the 50% hero plus three or four modules, and drop the second big clearance banner or move it to a separate send. One offer per email.
- 2. Add a real deadline in the hero (for example "Ends Monday 11:59 PM PT") or a countdown, as the 11/21 send had. Right now "Starts Now" has no end date.
- 3. Vary the creative day to day. Don't send the same 70-80% clearance hero a fourth time.
- 4. Make the hero CTA more specific ("Shop Black Friday Deals") and give it more weight than the module buttons.
- 5. Move the fine print out of the hero's line of sight, or collapse it into a single linked line.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Black Friday is live: up to 50% off, ends Monday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Gifts, shoes & gear: up to 50% off today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Order today, pick up tomorrow. Up to 70% off clearance too.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stanley, HOKA, Nike & more. Free same-day pickup on select orders.`

# Technical Audit: DICK'S Sporting Goods, "Black Friday is ON!"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally sound. It has `lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag, `role="presentation"` layout tables, MSO conditional blocks and a hidden preheader. The confirmed defects are an effectively empty plain-text part (9 chars) and widespread missing `alt` attributes. The header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be evaluated through the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Coverage gap:** 12 material links were probed and 55 were skipped (ranked by click likelihood). No broken or redirecting links were reported among the 12 probed, so the other 55 are unverified.
- **Tracking:** The open pixel is `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1...&pid=...&tuid=...&configId=512ffb51-...`, a 1×1 `<img>` placed before the main table. It is first-party and on the sending domain, so there's no tracking-domain mismatch.
- **Link markup:** The header link is `<a class="arc-link" data-nl-type="DEEPLINK" target="_blank">`. The truncated source doesn't show its `href`, so I can't validate its redirect or UTM handling.
- **Unsubscribe link:** The QA tool says it always probes one, but no result for it was reported.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed by QA, 14 images):**
  - Tracking pixel: `data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open`.
  - Content banners (Scene7), including `selectrunningshoes1122footaccess`, `top-gifts`, `50-off-banner-dks2026-1115-11`, `2026-1122-dks-stwd-fitness-cyber-8-bid-1`, `calia-vrst-2026-1122-dks-swd-golf-cyber-8-bids-ban...`, `2026-1122-dks-stw-fanshop-cyber8-banner-fleece` and `1101storefront`.
  - Icons: `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1` and `icon-location-2025-1`.
- **Impact on the banners:** If the `alt` attribute is absent, screen readers may read out the filename. With images blocked, the offer content disappears. The report doesn't say whether these are absent or `alt=""`.
- **Decorative images:** The pixel and icons should carry an explicit `alt=""`.
- **Image weight:** The Scene7 URLs request `wid=1200&hei=1600&qlt=100` with a `cb=` cache-buster. That's a large payload for a 600px layout, though the file sizes weren't measured.
- **Head element:** `<link rel="icon">` in `<head>` has no effect in email and is typically stripped.
- **Other markup:** The `lang`, viewport, MSO conditionals, hidden preheader (`mso-hide:all`) and web-font fallback to Arial raise no issues.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source contains no unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`). The footer was truncated and not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] Plain-text fallback is 9 chars.** This is a confirmed deliverability and accessibility defect. A multipart/alternative with a near-empty text part is a spam-filter signal and gives text-only clients nothing.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. It matters because Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `info@em.dcsg.com` can't be assessed from this capture. The relay is the likely cause.
- **Footer:** The physical address and unsubscribe text weren't visible in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The truncated HTML shows no landing URLs or UTM parameters, and the QA data has no landing-page results. The `data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"` attribute suggests app deep-link handling, so click-through behavior should be checked on mobile.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Plain-text part:** Generate a full text alternative with offer copy and URLs. This is the only confirmed FAIL.
2. **Alt text:** Add descriptive `alt` to the banners that carry offers, for example "Black Friday: 50% off". Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixel and the `icon-vc-*` / `icon-location-*` icons.
3. **List-Unsubscribe headers:** Check the raw message headers at the source, bypassing the relay, for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`.
4. **Authentication:** Inspect the original headers for `Authentication-Results`, and verify SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment on `em.dcsg.com`.
5. **Link probing:** Re-run with a higher probe limit, or sample the 55 skipped links, to confirm none are broken. Capture the footer and the unsubscribe URL.
6. **Image sizes:** Request images at 2× the rendered width (about 1200px) with lower quality (for example `qlt=80`), and measure payload size.
7. **Head cleanup:** Remove the unused `<link rel="icon">`.
