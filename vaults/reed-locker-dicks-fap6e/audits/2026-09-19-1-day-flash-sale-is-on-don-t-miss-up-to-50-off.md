# 1-day Flash Sale is ON! 💰 ⚡ Don't miss up to 50% OFF...
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-19**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S running its go-to "orange lightning-bolt FLASH SALE" hero, and the render has a real defect: the big "FLASH" wordmark is cropped to "FLAS" at the edge of the hero banner, cutting off the sale's own headline art. Below that, the email actually does a lot of things well — clear "Up to 50% Off" subhead, a ScoreCard/loyalty banner up top, a tidy category nav row, and a long, well-organized cascade of secondary offers (extra 25% off clearance, $100-and-under shoes, DSG family clothing, Stanley Halloween drop, football gear, Top Flite golf sets) each with its own "Shop Now."
- The problem is less this specific email and more the pattern: per brand history, this is at least the sixth "Up to 40-60% off" flash/clearance send DICK'S has fired in the past ten days (9/16, 9/17, two on 9/18, now 9/19). The creative is legible and on-brand, but a subscriber who's opened three of the last five nearly-identical sends is going to start tuning this out regardless of execution quality.
- Judgment: solid execution undercut by a visible cropping bug and heavy promo fatigue — a 6-7/10 send, not the 8+ it could be with a fresh hero and a subject that doesn't blur into the rest of the week.

## What's working

- The hero clearly states "Flash Sale: Up to 50% Off" with a supporting green "Shop Now" CTA immediately below the (broken) banner art.
- The quick-nav pill row (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Team Sports) gives an easy alternate path in if the hero offer doesn't land.
- Deal cascade is logically organized with distinct, scannable offers (25% off clearance, $100-and-under shoes, DSG family apparel, Stanley Halloween cup, football gear, Top Flite golf) each paired with its own product photo and button.
- ScoreCard loyalty banner sits at the very top, giving members a reason to feel this is "their" email.
- Fine print (valid dates, exclusions) is present under each offer rather than buried at the very bottom only.

## What's weak

- The hero banner's "FLASH" wordmark is visibly cropped to "FLAS" — the sale's own headline art is cut off in the render.
- This is essentially the same red/orange "UP TO X% OFF" flash-sale template DICK'S has run repeatedly this month (9/16, 9/17, 9/18 x2), just recolored orange — no creative refresh.
- "Shop Now" is repeated on nearly every single block (8+ times) with no variation, so nothing differentiates which offer is most urgent or best.
- The hero CTA links to a generic "Flash Sale" collection rather than a specific hero product, so there's no singular thing to click toward.
- No reviews, ratings, or social proof anywhere in a very long scroll of products.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the cropped "FLASH" hero art immediately — a headline sale email with its own headline word cut off undermines the whole send.
- 2. Break the "Shop Now" monotony — vary CTA copy per section ("Shop Shoes," "Shop Clearance," "Get the Tumbler") so each block reads as a distinct action, not a copy-pasted button.
- 3. Retire or meaningfully re-skin the orange lightning-bolt flash-sale template before the next send — five+ uses of this exact beat in ten days is measurable fatigue risk per the brand history.
- 4. Lead the subject with the single sharpest number instead of a vague "up to 50%" that's now identical to prior sends — pick whichever offer (25% off clearance, $100-and-under shoes) is actually the freshest and name it.
- 5. Move a specific hero product (not just the collection link) into the hero CTA to give the hero board a concrete destination.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Today only: extra 25% off clearance, on top of sale prices`
- **Subject Alt B:** `1-Day Flash Sale: $100 & under shoes from adidas, New Balance + more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack an extra 25% off clearance — ends tonight at 11:59PM PT`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New Stanley Halloween cup just dropped, plus up to 50% off sitewide`

## Full review
## 1. Overview

This is DICK'S running its go-to "orange lightning-bolt FLASH SALE" hero, and the render has a real defect: the big "FLASH" wordmark is cropped to "FLAS" at the edge of the hero banner, cutting off the sale's own headline art.
