# Black Friday has arrived! 🙌 1000s of styles on SALE
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S ninth or so Black Friday send in four days, and it's the same template as the 11/25 "It all starts NOW" and "Beat the rush!" emails. The hero is a big green-and-gold "BLACK FRIDAY STARTS NOW" banner with "Biggest Sale of the Year, up to 50% off 1000s of styles" and a single red Shop Now button. Below it sits a long stack of category tiles. The offer is clear and the hierarchy holds up.
- The subject says "Black Friday has arrived!", but the hero says "starts now" and the prior sends said the same. The 11/26 date is the actual day, so the urgency is real this time. The email still gives me nothing new beyond "it's here." An extra 25% off clearance block and a store-hours module add some variety but also add length.
- It's a competent, well-built send that suffers from sameness and length. A subscriber who has seen eight near-identical emails will skim this one.

## What's working

- The hero is bold, on-season and instantly readable, with "up to 50% off" and one red CTA.
- The "Explore more holiday deals" pill row (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Golf, Fan Shop) lets a reader jump straight to their category.
- Product tiles name concrete deals: up to 30% off Nike & Jordan, 25% off YETI, up to 60% off basketball hoops, up to $120 off golf electronics.
- The red "EXTRA 25% OFF" clearance block gives a strong visual break and a second offer.
- The store-hours module is useful on Black Friday and Thanksgiving, and it helps drive foot traffic.

## What's weak

- The email is very long, with at least ten modules, and the best offers sit below the first screen.
- Cadence fatigue: it repeats the template from the 11/25 and 11/24 sends with almost no creative change.
- Some fine print is small and dense, including the top "One-Hour Pickup" strip and the exclusions text under the hero.
- The subject says "has arrived" while the hero says "starts now," which is an inconsistent message.
- The "up to" percentages are vague, and the hero names no specific product.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one concrete doorbuster (a named product at a named price) instead of the generic "up to 50% off", and give it a visible countdown to the deadline.
- 2. Cut the module count roughly in half. Keep the hero, the pill row, the top three product tiles and the clearance block, and drop the rest.
- 3. Make the subject and hero say the same thing.
- 4. Vary the creative day to day, or suppress this send for people who clicked yesterday.
- 5. Make the exclusions and fine print more legible.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Today only: Nike, YETI & Under Armour up to 30% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Extra 25% off clearance + 1000s of Black Friday deals`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Doorbusters in-store and online, plus free same-day pickup before 8PM.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stores open 6AM. Shop Nike, golf and gifts before they sell out.`
