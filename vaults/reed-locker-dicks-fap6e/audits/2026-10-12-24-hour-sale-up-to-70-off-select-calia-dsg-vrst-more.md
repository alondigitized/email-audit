# 24-hour SALE: Up to 70% OFF select CALIA, DSG, VRST & more! 👉
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean "24-hour sale" send with a clear message. The hero is a big "24-HOUR SALE" banner with the brand logos (DSG, CALIA, VRST, Maxfli, Walter Hagen, Quest). Below it is "Today Only: Up to 70% Off Our Exclusive Brands" and a green Shop Now button. The deadline is stated (10/12 at 11:59 PM ET), and the eye lands on the offer first.
- The weakness is what follows. After the hero, the email becomes a long grid of single-category tiles: HOKA, VRST, CALIA, canopies, dumbbells, irons and bikes. A second "70% OFF" clearance banner sits near the bottom, so the email has two competing 70% headlines. For a subscriber who has had clearance emails from this brand almost daily, such as the 10/11 "Did somebody say NEW markdowns?!" and the 10/10 "EXTRA 25% off... ends tonight" sends, "24-hour" urgency is hard to believe. It reads as the next beat in the same promo, not a new event.
- Judgement: well executed and easy to scan, but not distinctive. It's about a 7 and fits the week's pattern.

## What's working

- The "24-HOUR SALE" hero is large and legible, and "Today Only" with a dated cutoff gives the urgency a real deadline.
- "Up to 70% Off Our Exclusive Brands" with a green Shop Now button directly beneath is an unambiguous offer and CTA.
- The brand logo row (DSG, CALIA, VRST, etc.) tells you which private labels are on sale.
- The category pills (CALIA, VRST, DSG, Alpine Design, All Sale, All Clearance) offer quick routes.
- The product tiles carry concrete numbers, such as "Sale $14.99 CALIA Tees & Tanks", "$40 Off ETHOS Dumbbells" and "Up to $500 Off Maxfli XC Irons". The ScoreCard header is visible. I don't see broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The email is very long and has about eight single-product tiles. Most of them (canopies, dumbbells, irons, bikes) have nothing to do with the apparel brands in the hero.
- Every tile uses the same generic grey "Shop Now" button. None says what it leads to.
- The second banner, "UP TO 70% OFF New Markdowns Added", repeats the hero offer. It is a different sale with a similar number, which muddies the "24-hour" message.
- The hero fine print is small ("Select deals valid online only 10/12/26 11:59PM ET. Other offers available in-store"). The tiles also use "Up to" and "Select" language, so the real depth of the discount is unclear.
- The subject has two shouted words ("SALE", "OFF") plus an emoji, and the "Order Today, Sport It Tomorrow" line at the top competes with the hero for attention. The product picks at the bottom (adidas Samba, Nike hoodie) have no prices, ratings or reviews.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero plus three or four tiles that match the private-label theme (VRST, CALIA, DSG). Drop the golf, bike and canopy tiles into an "Explore more" link row. A tighter email makes the 24-hour framing more credible.
- 2. Remove the second "70% OFF" banner, or move it to its own send. The 24-hour sale should be the only headline.
- 3. Give the CTAs specific copy, such as "Shop VRST Hoodies" and "Shop CALIA Tees $14.99". Make the hero CTA "Shop the 24-Hour Sale" instead of "Shop Now".
- 4. Add a live countdown or "Ends 11:59 PM ET" above the hero, as the 10/10 "Final Hours" email did. Put the deadline in larger type than the fine print.
- 5. Show prices or "was/now" values on the pick-for-you products, and add star ratings.
- 6. Trim the subject to under 50 characters and drop one of the caps words.

- **Subject Alt B:** `24 hours. $14.99 CALIA tees, 50% off VRST 👀`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sale ends 11:59 PM ET. Hoodies, tees, dumbbells and more, marked down for one day.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally sound: it uses presentation tables, `lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag, MSO conditionals and a hidden preheader. The automated QA run has one hard failure (the plain-text part is effectively empty) and 13 images with no alt attribute. The compliance header warnings could not be verified, because the relay may have stripped those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Every link goes through a click-tracking redirect.** The logo link is `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/mclick?params=b.v1.…`, and the open pixel is `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=…`. The wrapped links are normal ESP behavior.
- **The final destinations and UTM parameters are not visible.** The redirect params are opaque and the source is truncated.
- **Probe coverage was partial.** QA probed 12 of 65 links and skipped 53 (footer, utility and social links deprioritized). No probe failures were reported, but there is no pass/fail detail for those 12 either. Footer links are unverified.
- No broken links are confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **[Issue] 13 images have no `alt` attribute:**
  - Seven product and promo images: `hokarun1012stwd6`, `2vrst1012stwd6`, `2caliateetank1012stw`, `canopies1012stwd6`, `ethos21012stw`, `2golfset10126am` and `nikishi1012stwd6`.
  - Five icons: `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1` and `icon-location-2025-1`.
  - The tracking pixel `open` (`…/ee/v1/open?params=…`).
  - With images blocked or read by a screen reader, the product tiles may have no text equivalent, and some of that content may be image-only. Decorative icons and the pixel should carry `alt=""`. The product images need descriptive alt text, or live text beside them.
- **Image weight.** The product images are requested as `fmt=jpeg&qlt=100&wid=1200&hei=1600` on Scene7. That is quality 100 at 2× a 600px container, so each file is likely large. `qlt=75–85` would cut the payload with little visible loss.
- **Template residue.** The source contains `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->`, `class="is-html-mode"` and `data-contenteditable="true"` on `<body>`. A `<link rel="icon">` in the head has no effect in email clients. None of these break rendering, but they show unscrubbed editor output.
- **Fonts.** Archivo loads from `fonts.gstatic.com` with an `Arial, sans-serif` fallback, which is handled correctly.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source shows no unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`). Content past the truncation point was not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] The plain-text fallback is 9 characters.** There is effectively no text/plain alternative. It hurts deliverability and accessibility, and spam filters weigh it. If the relay is only extracting the part, confirm against the raw MIME.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA notes the relay may strip these headers. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so verify on the original message.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` is missing,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment cannot be confirmed. The sender domain is `em.dcsg.com` with From `info@em.dcsg.com`, which looks aligned, but this is unverified.
- **The footer unsubscribe link and postal address are not confirmed.** They fall beyond the truncated source. QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result was reported.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. UTM parameters and final landing URLs are hidden behind the `mclick` redirects. The probe results did not include destination URLs or landing page content, so there is no evidence of a mismatch.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Ship a proper multipart/alternative text/plain body with the offer, key links and the unsubscribe link. This is the only confirmed hard failure.
2. Add `alt=""` to the icons and the open pixel, and descriptive alt text to the seven product images.
3. Check the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`. Check SPF, DKIM and DMARC in the original headers rather than through the relay.
4. Lower Scene7 `qlt` from 100 to about 80.
5. Strip the template residue: the SSL placeholder comment, the `is-html-mode` and `data-contenteditable` attributes, and the `<link rel="icon">`.
6. Improve the audit pipeline by capturing the redirect destinations for the probed links, so UTM and landing-page continuity can be checked.
