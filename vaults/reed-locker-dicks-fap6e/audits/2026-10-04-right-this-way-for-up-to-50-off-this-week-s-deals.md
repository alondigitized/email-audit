# Right this way for up to 50% OFF this week's deals! 👉
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-04**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, loud, single-offer hero. A giant white "UP TO 50% OFF" sits over a moody prairie photo, with "This Week's Deals" and a green Shop Now button right below it. The eye lands on the offer first, and I see no broken images or overlapping text. Below it, the email turns into a long catalog: category pills, clothing and shoes, an NFL fan shop block, a TaylorMade wedge trade-in, team sports, outdoor, fitness, a $25-and-under clearance banner, and a six-product grid.
- The problem is cadence and focus. This is DICK'S fifth "up to 50% off this week's deals" send since 9/29. The most recent were "🙌 Best DEALS of the week = up to 50% OFF! 🙌" on 10/1 and "Weekend plans: Shop up to 50% OFF deals ✅" on 10/3. The $25-and-under clearance banner near the bottom also repeats yesterday's and today's earlier "$25 & under" hero. A subscriber would feel the sameness. The body also dilutes the message with seven or more competing offers, each with a different percentage (40%, 45%, 50%, 2X trade-in).
- My judgment: competent execution, tired idea. It will get opened out of habit and clicked mostly by people already shopping, not because the subject gave them a reason.

## What's working

- The hero offer is huge, high-contrast and readable at a glance. There's one green Shop Now button directly under it.
- The category pill row (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Team Sports) gives a quick route into the shopper's own category.
- The modular layout is clean, with consistent image-plus-headline-plus-button blocks that are easy to scan.
- The closing product grid (adidas Samba OG, Birkenstock Arizona, New Balance 530, Nike hoodie) gives concrete items to click.
- The ScoreCard / Order Today header strip and the app-download and favorites footer blocks reinforce loyalty and the brand's services.

## What's weak

- The hero says "up to 50% off" but doesn't name a product or category. It also gives no real deadline, only "limited time" in small gray text.
- The body is a catalog of competing offers (40%, 45%, 50%, 2X trade-in, $25 and under) with a button on each, so no single message wins after the hero.
- The CTAs are generic ("Shop Now", "Clearance Deals", "Find a Store"). The product grid shows no prices or sale markers, so the deal isn't visible where the products are.
- The disclaimer block at the bottom is long and dense. "Select" and "up to" qualifiers throughout make the 50% claim feel soft.
- The $25-and-under clearance banner repeats the hero from today's earlier send, so the message feels recycled.

## Recommendations

- 1. Break the repetition. Rotate the hero by category or day ("Today: up to 50% off shoes") instead of the same generic weekly-deals banner. This would lift the email most.
- 2. Add a real deadline to the hero, such as "Ends Sunday 11:59 PM PT", in large type near the button.
- 3. Cut the body to three or four blocks. Drop the wedge trade-in and the NFL block unless the send is segmented to those shoppers.
- 4. Put sale prices and "was/now" markers on the product grid. Replace "Shop Now" with verb-plus-noun copy like "Shop shoes up to 50% off".
- 5. Shorten the legal block, or move it behind a "Details" link.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends Sunday: up to 50% off shoes, fleece & gear`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your weekend deals: up to 50% off Nike, adidas & more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Samba OGs, Nike hoodies and Birkenstocks, all marked down through Sunday night.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus extra clearance picks at $25 and under. Pickup in one hour.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally sound (`lang="en"`, `<title>`, viewport meta, `role="presentation"` layout tables, MSO conditionals). The QA run found one hard failure: the plain-text part is only 9 characters. It also found 11 images with missing `alt`, and the compliance and authentication headers could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque tracked links:** CTAs go through `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/mclick?params=b.v1...&pid=v1...`, for example the header link with `class="arc-link" data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"`. The destination can't be read from the source, so landing URLs and UTM parameters can't be verified statically.
- **Low probe coverage:** only 12 links were probed and 59 were skipped, so link health beyond the top-ranked CTAs is unconfirmed. QA reported no broken links among the 12 probed, but it also listed no probe results.
- **Open pixel:** `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=...` is present and tracked. It has no `alt`, which is covered in section 3.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Hero and category images missing `alt` (5):** QA flagged these on `scene7.com`, each requested at 1200×1600:
  - `clothing-2026-1004-dks-swd-4pm`
  - `fanshop-2026-1004-dks-swd-4pm`
  - `team-2026-1004-dks-swd-4pm`
  - `outdoor-2026-1004-dks-swd-4pm`
  - `fitness-2026-1004-dks-swd-4pm`

  If these carry the offer content, images-off clients and screen readers get nothing.
- **Icons missing `alt` (5):** `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1` and `icon-location-2025-1`. If they are decorative, they should have `alt=""` explicitly. If they sit next to link text, they need a label.
- **Tracking pixel:** the `open` image has no `alt`. It should be `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Plain-text fallback fails:** the text version is 9 characters. Clients and filters that use the text part get effectively empty content.
- **Preheader spacer:** `#pht-spacer` uses `display:none; max-height:0; mso-hide:all`, which is a standard pattern.
- **Leftover template comment:** `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` is still in the `td#ssl` cell. This is harmless but shows an unfilled slot in the template. Check whether a preheader/SSL string was meant to go there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible HTML. The source is truncated, so tokens further down can't be ruled out.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found:** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so verify against the raw message at the sending ESP.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture. The sending domain is `em.dcsg.com`.
- **Footer not verified:** the truncated source doesn't show the footer, so the physical postal address and the body unsubscribe link can't be confirmed. QA says an unsubscribe link was included in the probe set, but gives no result.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable from the source. All links are wrapped in `data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/mclick` redirects, and no landing URLs or UTM parameters are exposed. Resolving the redirect chain for the top CTAs would be needed to check that the destinations match the "up to 50% OFF this week's deals" offer.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a real plain-text part:** a 9-character text part fails QA (priority: high).
2. **Add `alt` text to the 5 hero and category images:** the alt text should name the category and offer.
3. **Set `alt=""` on the 5 decorative icons and the open pixel:** do this explicitly.
4. **Verify the raw headers at the ESP:** check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `em.dcsg.com`. Don't treat the QA warnings as confirmed failures until you have.
5. **Remove or fill the leftover `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` slot.**
6. **Improve the audit tooling:** follow the `mclick` redirects for the top CTAs, and capture full headers, to close the gaps in sections 2, 5 and 6.
