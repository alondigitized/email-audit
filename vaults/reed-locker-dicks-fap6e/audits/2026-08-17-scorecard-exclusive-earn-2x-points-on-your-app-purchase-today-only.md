# ScoreCard exclusive 📲 Earn 2X Points on your app purchase TODAY ONLY
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-17**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S "Mobile Monday" app-exclusive send, a genre they haven't run in this stretch — a real departure from the six-day "up to 50%" wordmark grind. The hero is clean and legible: a hand holding a phone showing a "2X POINTS" app screen against a green gradient, with a clear headline "Mobile Monday! Earn 2X Points on Your App Purchase" and a single "Get Offer" CTA. It's honest about mechanics too — "Must sign in to redeem offer," "Valid through 8/17," "Not a ScoreCard member? Join today" — no bait-and-switch.
- The problem is the offer itself is thin and narrow: it's a points multiplier, not a discount, and it only matters to people who (a) are ScoreCard members and (b) will buy via the app today. That's a small slice of the list. Below the hero it reverts immediately to the same product-tile grid this brand runs every day (Nike shoes, DSG clothing, HydroJug, TaylorMade balls, life vests, Peloton shoes) — so after one novel screen it's business as usual.
- Judgement: a decent one-off change of pace creatively, but the core offer is low-value for most recipients and the email doesn't sustain differentiation past the hero.

## What's working

- Hero image is a genuinely different format from the last six sends — a phone-in-hand photo instead of a repeated wordmark placard.
- Single, unambiguous primary CTA ("Get Offer") sits directly under a clear headline with no scrolling needed on mobile.
- Fine print is upfront and specific (expiration time, member requirement, sign-in requirement) rather than buried.
- Category pill row (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Team Sports, Back-to-School Shop) gives a fast secondary path for browsers.

## What's weak

- The offer is a points multiplier restricted to app purchases only — no dollar/percent savings, so it's low-relevance for anyone not already an engaged ScoreCard app shopper.
- After the hero, the email is indistinguishable from any other DICK'S digest — eight more unrelated deal blocks (Nike, DSG, HydroJug, TaylorMade, life vests, Peloton) dilute the "today only, app-only" urgency.
- "Mobile Monday" as a subhead is confusing on a day like Sunday/whatever this actually sent — no visible tie to the calendar day beyond the label itself.
- The "‡" and asterisk footnote markers on the hero and top banner aren't explained near the hero, only in dense legal copy at the very bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the product grid down significantly or gate it behind a "while you wait, shop these" framing — right now it undercuts the "one narrow app-only offer today" urgency by burying it in an unrelated digest.
- 2. Add a visible ScoreCard points value anchor near the hero (e.g., "that's up to $X back") — most shoppers don't intuitively value "2X points," so translate it to real money.
- 3. Move the sign-in/member disclaimer into a short one-line caption directly under "Get Offer," not just in small gray text — it's currently the make-or-break condition for the whole offer and reads like fine print.
- 4. Test sending this without the full deals digest at all — a tight, single-offer "Mobile Monday" email would better differentiate this from the daily 50%-off sends and could lift open rates on the next Mobile Monday send.

- **Subject Alt B:** `📲 Your app purchase is worth double points today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sign in, shop the app, earn 2X — offer ends 11:59PM ET`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `ScoreCard members: double points on any app order today`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Email renders via a standard Epsilon/Harmony ESP template with MSO/Outlook conditionals and responsive breakpoints, but ships a broken unsubscribe link and is missing all inspected compliance/auth headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken unsubscribe link (FAIL):** The "Unsubscribe" link (`https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/U/v61000001a00f4263d9ab61536e96c65848/aa5377e952894bad0000021ef3a0bcfe`) resolves to `/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription` — an application error page, not a functioning preference/unsubscribe form.
- All tracked links route through Epsilon Harmony redirect infrastructure (`app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/...` for the open pixel, `/U/...` for click tracking) rather than direct-to-site URLs, consistent with standard ESP click tracking.
- QA probed 12 material links (CTA/button-styled, high click-likelihood) out of 63 total; 51 footer/utility/social links were deprioritized and not verified — those remain unconfirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 12 images missing `alt` text, including the SSL/open-tracking pixel and all hero/product creative:
  - `aa5377e952894bad00004c5a42963aa1` (open pixel)
  - `2026-0817-hero-dks`, `shoes-2`, `clothing`, `hydro`, `golf`, `vests`, `shoes-1-1` (Scene7-hosted product imagery)
  - `icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-location-2025.png` (footer icons)
- MSO conditional comments and `@media screen` font-face/breakpoint blocks are present and structurally correct for Outlook/mobile-client fallback rendering.
- `<script type="application/ld+json">` block contains an inline `//` comment (`// GIF & WEBP images are not supported...`), which is invalid JSON — if this block is parsed by any client/schema consumer rather than ignored, it will fail to parse.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge/personalization tokens (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) are present in the visible HTML source. No issues found — but note the source was truncated, so tokens later in the document were not reviewed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe mechanism is functionally broken** (see §2) — the unsubscribe link errors out instead of processing the opt-out, a CAN-SPAM functional-compliance issue independent of the header checks below.
- `List-Unsubscribe` header: not found — one-click unsubscribe via mail client UI is unavailable (or not captured by the relay).
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058): not found — even if `List-Unsubscribe` is added, one-click POST unsubscribe won't function without this.
- `Authentication-Results` header: not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status could not be verified via the AgentMail relay.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
All observed CTA/click links are wrapped in Epsilon Harmony redirects (`app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/` and `/U/` paths) rather than exposing direct destination URLs with UTM parameters in the source HTML. Because these are opaque tracking redirects, UTM parameterization and final landing-page alignment cannot be confirmed from source inspection alone — this would require following redirects live, which was outside the scope of the automated QA pass.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the unsubscribe destination — `/pc/subscription` is erroring; repoint the redirect target so it lands on a working preference-center page.
2. Add `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers to enable RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe.
3. Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC are passing and that `Authentication-Results` is present at the sending MTA (may be a relay-visibility gap rather than a true absence — verify at the source).
4. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 7 product/hero images (leave the tracking pixel and pure decorative footer icons with `alt=""` if intentionally decorative, rather than omitted).
5. Remove the inline `//` comment from the `application/ld+json` block to keep it valid, parseable JSON.
6. Spot-check the remaining 51 unprobed links (footer/legal/social) for validity, since QA coverage was intentionally limited to high-click-likelihood links.
