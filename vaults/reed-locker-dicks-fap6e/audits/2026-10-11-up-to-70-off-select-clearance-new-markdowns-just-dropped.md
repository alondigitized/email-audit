# 💰 Up to 70% OFF select clearance + NEW markdowns just dropped
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-11**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S loudest clearance send of the week. A giant "70% OFF" sits over autumn leaves, and a green Shop Now button follows right under it. The eye lands on the offer first. It also escalates the clearance story: on 10/9 and 10/10 the clearance hook was "EXTRA 25% off" (the 10/10 send was the "Tick, tock!" closer). Today it jumps to "up to 70%" with "NEW markdowns just dropped."
- The body weakens the hero. After the 70% banner it stacks 40%, 40%, "$50 and under" and "$100 and under" tiles, then a second giant "50% OFF" banner for This Week's Deals. That is five different offers in one scroll. A reader who just saw 70% sees 50% and 40% below it and starts to wonder what the real deal is. The "Limited Time" label has no end date, and "up to" does most of the work in the headline.
- Overall it's a solid, clean promo that sits in the same crowded cadence as the last six sends. It's strong in the hero but loses focus below it, and the subject line is the weakest part.

## What's working

- The 70% hero is huge, high-contrast and readable in about a second.
- The headline "New Markdowns Added: Up to 70% Off Select Clearance" repeats the subject, so the open-to-click story holds together.
- The green Shop Now button is clearly visible directly under the hero.
- Quick-filter pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 and Under, Fan Shop, All Sale) let a reader jump straight to their category.
- The fall leaf imagery fits the season, and the "One Day Only" fan shop tile has a real date.

## What's weak

- The stacked offers (70%, 40%, 40%, $50 and under, $100 and under, a Peloton cycling shoe sale, then a second 50% banner) dilute the clearance message.
- "Limited Time" has no deadline, so the urgency isn't credible. The only dated deadline is on the fan shop tile.
- Almost every CTA says "Shop Now." No button names a product or category.
- The "Check out these picks" product grid is just six items with names and no prices, ratings or sale tags. It's a missed chance to show real clearance value.
- The email is long, and the footer is dense with legal text, asterisks and app badges. The 50% banner near the bottom repeats last week's message.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the middle section to one clearance story. Keep the 70% hero and show 3–4 actual markdown products with was/now prices, then drop the second 50% banner and the redundant tiles.
- 2. Put a real deadline in the hero, such as "Ends Sunday 11:59 PM PT," and make the CTA specific: "Shop Clearance Shoes" or "Shop 70% Off Styles."
- 3. Shorten the subject so the offer isn't clipped on mobile, and drop "NEW markdowns" for something with a fresh hook.
- 4. Use the "One Day Only" fan shop tile as a real urgency anchor, higher up the email.
- 5. Add price, review stars or a size/availability cue to the picks grid.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fresh markdowns on Nike, adidas & more. Fan Shop deals end tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sizes go fast on clearance. Shop 70% off before it's gone.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally sound (table layout, `role="presentation"`, `lang="en"`, viewport meta, MSO conditionals). The real defects are a near-empty plain-text part and 13 images without alt attributes. The compliance and authentication warnings are most likely capture limits of the AgentMail relay, not confirmed sender faults.

- **Probe coverage:** QA probed 12 of 65 links and skipped 53. It reported no broken links among the probed ones, so I found no confirmed link failures. Coverage of the remaining 53 is unknown.

The path is dated 2026-06-23 and says "40-off-drinkware", but this is an October clearance send. It may be a reused module, and the truncated source doesn't show what it links to.
- **Template leftovers:** The source still has `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->`, `class="is-html-mode"` and `data-contenteditable="true"` on `<body>`. They're harmless but show authoring-tool residue.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, 13 images (QA WARN):**
  - Hero and content images: `2026-1011-dks-stw-6am-kicks2`, `-threads`, `-fan`, `-cleats`, `-peloton`, and `banner4.gif`. These are content images served at `wid=1200&hei=1600`, so a screen reader or an images-off client gets no information from them.
  - Footer icons: `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`. If they sit next to text labels, `alt=""` is acceptable, but the attribute is missing entirely.

- **Plain-text part (QA FAIL):** The text version is 9 characters. This is a deliverability and accessibility defect if it matches the raw message. The relay may have truncated it, so check the raw MIME.
- **Preheader:** The pre-header text sits inside `<td id="ssl" ... height="0" style="font-size:0; line-height:0">`, followed by a hidden `#pht-spacer` div (`display:none; mso-hide:all`). The hiding pattern is standard and I found no rendering issue with it.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** QA says an unsubscribe link is always included in probing, but the truncated source doesn't show the footer, so I can't verify the link or the physical address.

Not assessable. UTM parameters and landing URLs are hidden behind the `mclick` redirect. The probe results don't include final destinations, and the visible source doesn't show the click-through targets. The one concrete risk is the `2026-0623` drinkware banner (see section 2).
