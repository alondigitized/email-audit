# EXTRA 25% off select clearance is happening now!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-06**
## Executive summary

- This is the cleanest DICK'S send I've reviewed this cycle — the hero actually matches the subject line for once. "EXTRA 25% OFF" in white-on-red stadium seating leads, followed by a clear "Extra 25% Off Select Clearance" subhead and a single green "Shop Now" button above the fold. No bait-and-switch where the subject promises one thing and the hero shows another, which has been the recurring problem in this brand's last several sends.
- That said, it's still the same DICK'S kitchen-sink format underneath: category nav, then the now-infamous "UP TO 50% OFF" stadium-seat banner reappears just two screens down (the exact creative flagged repeatedly since 8/30), followed by seven more product modules (clothing, drinkware, golf, football gear, outdoor chairs, ScoreCard bonus, "check out these picks"). The email works hard to be everything to everyone, which dilutes the one clean offer it opened with.
- Net: a stronger open-to-hero match than recent sends, but the body reverts to sprawl and reuses creative I've now seen five-plus times this week.

## What's working

- Hero offer ("EXTRA 25% OFF") matches the subject line exactly — no mismatch between promise and payoff, unlike several recent sends.
- Clear visual hierarchy at the top: red hero → "Extra 25% Off Select Clearance" subhead → single green "Shop Now" CTA, all visible without much scrolling.
- "Limited Time" label adds credible urgency directly under the hero.
- ScoreCard $10 Bonus Reward module is a genuine loyalty perk, clearly gated to members with Sign In/Get Coupon CTAs.
- No visible render bugs — images load, text doesn't overlap, layout is intact top to bottom.

## What's weak

- The "UP TO 50% OFF" stadium-seating banner (same asset flagged on 8/30, 9/1, 9/2, 9/3, 9/4, 9/5) reappears again just below the clearance hero, muddying which offer is actually the headline.
- Body sprawls into eight-plus unrelated modules (golf, football, drinkware, outdoor chairs, shoes) that bury the "select clearance" offer the subject promised.
- "Select clearance" and "exclusions apply" fine print sits directly under the CTA — the offer isn't fully honest about scope at a glance.
- Cadence fatigue: this is at least the seventh near-identical DICK'S promotional email this week with overlapping creative.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the "UP TO 50% OFF" banner from this send entirely — it directly competes with and undercuts the "EXTRA 25%" hero the subject sold, and it's the sixth+ repeat of that exact asset this week.
- 2. Trim the body to 3-4 modules max (clearance, one cross-sell, ScoreCard bonus, picks) instead of nine — the current length dilutes a genuinely clear opening offer.
- 3. Move a specific clearance category or product image right under the hero (e.g., "clearance shoes from $19") to make "select clearance" concrete instead of abstract.
- 4. Vary the creative asset pool — reusing the same stadium-seating banner across a week of sends is measurably training subscribers to skim past it.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your clearance picks, now an extra 25% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack savings on already-marked-down shoes, gear, and apparel.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Extra 25% off ends 9/28 at 11:59PM ET — shop before it's gone.`
