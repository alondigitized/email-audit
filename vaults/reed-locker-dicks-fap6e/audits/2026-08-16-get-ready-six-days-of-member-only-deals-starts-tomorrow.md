# Get ready 👀 Six days of member-only DEALS starts TOMORROW!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuinely different animal from the last six "up to 50% off" wordmark blasts — it's a paid membership pitch, not a discount blast, and DICK'S clearly saved a real hero for it. "ScoreCard+ Member Week" with a date-stamped "August 17-22" callout and a specific "$350 in benefits" claim gives this send a concrete, well-produced hero that stands out from the repetitive clearance/flash-sale creative seen all week.
- The problem is what happens after the hero: the CTA is "Join for $99/Year," which is a hard paywall ask buried in a promotional inbox, not a deal. For a subscriber conditioned by five straight discount emails, asking for $99 upfront is a big pivot with no immediate discount payoff shown. The product grid below is generic ("Check out these picks") with no pricing, no member-price comparison, and one visibly broken image tile — undercutting the polish of the hero.
- Solid concept, weak execution on the ask and on proving the $99 is worth it. This lands as a mid-tier email: good subject/hero hook, but the moment it asks for money the value story disappears.

## What's working

- The hero has genuine production value — collage background, angled badge card, bold "MEMBER WEEK" wordmark, and a specific date range (August 17-22).
- "First-Ever ScoreCard+ Member Week!" plus "$350 in benefits" gives a concrete, quantified value prop instead of vague "deals."
- Subject line urgency ("starts TOMORROW") is echoed and reinforced by the date-bound hero, so subject and body are consistent.
- Clear single primary CTA ("Join for $99/Year") sits directly under the value copy with strong color contrast (white button on black).
- Utility footer (order-today, App Store/Google Play, best price guarantee, favorites list) is tidy and scannable.

## What's weak

- No visible discount, dollar amount, or example of what a member actually saves this week — "$350 in benefits" is a big unverified number with zero supporting detail in the render.
- The product grid ("Check out these picks") has a visibly broken image — a small blue box with a question mark sits where a sixth product thumbnail should be.
- Product tiles show no price, no member-vs-nonmember price comparison, which is the single most persuasive lever for a paid-membership pitch and it's completely absent.
- $99/year is a real financial ask dropped into a promo inbox with no risk-reducer (no "cancel anytime," no trial, no money-back framing) visible anywhere in the render.
- The transition from "Member Week" hero straight into an unrelated generic product grid feels disjointed — there's no bridge explaining these are member-exclusive prices or deals tied to the week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a number on the shelf: show 2-3 concrete Member Week deals ("Member price $89.99 vs. $119.99") directly under the hero so the $99 ask has visible payback before the CTA.
- 2. Fix the broken product image in the grid — a question-mark placeholder next to real product photography kills trust on a page that's otherwise trying to look premium.
- 3. Add a low-risk framing near the "Join for $99/Year" button (e.g., "Cancel anytime" or "First deal covers the fee") — asking for money cold, with no risk reducer, is the biggest drop-off point in this email.
- 4. Label the product grid as member-exclusive pricing if it is one — right now "Check out these picks" reads like a generic merch module unrelated to the membership pitch above it.
- 5. Consider a secondary, lower-commitment CTA ("See this week's deals") for browsers not ready to pay $99 up front, so the email isn't single-path membership-or-nothing.
- **Subject Alt A:** `ScoreCard+ Member Week starts tomorrow — $350 in benefits inside`
- **Subject Alt B:** `6 days, member-only deals: your first look before it starts`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Join now and unlock August 17-22 deals plus $350 in year-round perks`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `See what's in this week's member-exclusive drops before doors open`
