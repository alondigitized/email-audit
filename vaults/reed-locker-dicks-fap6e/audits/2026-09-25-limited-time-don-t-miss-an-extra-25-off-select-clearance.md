## What's weak

- The hero repeats a shape DICK'S has now run repeatedly this week (orange FLASH bolts 4x, red clearance block twice) — fatigue risk for a daily subscriber.
- After the hero, the email reverts to five near-identical "Up to X% Off Select [category]" blocks with generic stock photography — no connection back to the 25%-off clearance hook.

- Subject's "Limited time" and preview's "Don't miss" are both generic urgency phrases repeated in the body's "Limited Time" label — three touches of the same weak word choice instead of a hard countdown number.
