# Homepage snapshot · www.dickssportinggoods.com · 2026-05-07
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- I pulled up dicks.com to price out cleats for my youngest's travel soccer season and got a full-on error wall — logo, a generic "Oops" message, and an error string with my IP address printed on the screen. No hero, no nav, no deals, nothing. This isn't a brand experience, it's a dead end.

## What's working

- **Logo is present and legible** — the Dick's Sporting Goods green-and-white logo loads cleanly, so I at least know I'm in the right place before the wheels fall off.

## What's weak

- **Full site outage** — zero content loads below the error card. No nav, no hero, no products, nothing.
- **Raw error code exposed to end users** — printing `Error: 0.23a7cb17.1778181549.50eaae5f IP: 47.41.15.19` on a customer-facing page is technically sloppy and mildly creepy (my IP is right there).
- **No recovery path** — no "go back," no search bar, no link to the app, no customer service number. I'm just stuck.
- **"Try again after 12 hours"** — that's the entire CTA. Twelve hours. I'm shopping for cleats tonight before practice tomorrow.
- **Infinite gray void below** — the page is essentially infinitely tall empty gray space. It looks broken on mobile.

## Recommendations

- **Ship a real maintenance page** — swap this generic error card for a branded hold page with the store locator, app download link, and a customer service number. I can still buy cleats in-store if you give me the address.
- **Strip the raw error code and IP address** — that output belongs in server logs, not in front of a 44-year-old dad trying to buy Adidas Predators on his phone.
- **Add a retry CTA** — a simple "Tap to refresh" button would at least give me something to do besides stare at a gray screen.
- **Surface the app link** — if the web is down, push me to the iOS app. A banner that says "Download the Dick's app to keep shopping" keeps the conversion alive.
