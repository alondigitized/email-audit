# Get ready for this week's DEALS! (They're up to 50% off, too)
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S weekly "deals" roundup. It's clean and well organized, but it's the same "up to 50% off" message the brand has sent since the 10/5 Fall Deal Drop. The hero is a big "UP TO 50% OFF" over a football field, with a "This Week's Deals" headline and a Shop Now button. The eye lands on the offer first, and the category chips make it easy to navigate.
- The body is a tidy stack of category modules: men's, women's, kids', drinkware (up to 40%), and team sports (up to 45%). Each has a two-product image pair and a grey Shop Now button. A second hero follows near the bottom: "EXTRA 25% OFF clearance styles." That is the same clearance creative DICK'S sent this morning, so it works as a recycled module. The email is long, with two competing big-number heroes, and the grey buttons are weak against the loud hero.
- Judgment: a competent, predictable send. It's well built, but it's the fourth or fifth near-identical "up to 50%" email this week, so it blends into the pile.

## What's working

- The hero offer is huge and unambiguous: "UP TO 50% OFF" over a sports-field image.
- The category chips (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Team Sports) give quick routing.
- Each category module pairs real product imagery with a specific discount, such as "Up to 50% Off Select Men's Clothing and Shoes."
- The ScoreCard loyalty strip and the "Order Today. Sport It Tomorrow." delivery line add reassurance.
- A "Check out these picks" row of named products (adidas Samba OG, New Balance 530) gives specific click targets.

## What's weak

- The "up to 50% off" message repeats the 10/8 "This week's deals = up to 50% off!" send almost verbatim.
- The "EXTRA 25% OFF" clearance block is the same creative as this morning's send. It creates a second competing hero and dilutes the primary message.
- Only the first Shop Now button is green. The module buttons are pale grey and easy to miss.
- The email runs very long, with the key offer repeated across six or seven modules.
- Time-bounded urgency is weak. "Valid online only for a limited time" is vague, and the real deadline is buried in fine print further down.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story per send. Drop the recycled 25%-off clearance block, or lead with it and cut the rest, so the email doesn't compete with itself.
- 2. Give the hero a real deadline, for example "Ends Sunday," and put it in the headline instead of the fine print.
- 3. Make the module CTAs the same green as the hero and use specific copy, like "Shop Men's Deals" instead of "Shop Now."
- 4. Lead with a concrete hero product or brand (Nike, adidas), as the 10/8 send did, instead of a generic "This Week's Deals."
- 5. Vary the weekly creative. The deals roundup needs a new angle each time to stop it blurring with the clearance sends.

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email is well-formed HTML (table layout, `role="presentation"`, `lang="en"`, viewport meta, MSO conditionals) and uses ESP-wrapped click tracking. The confirmed defects are a missing plain-text part, missing alt text on 11 images, and one tracking pixel with no alt. The QA run's 50% pass rate mostly reflects relay-capture gaps, not confirmed sender defects.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- All inspected links go through the ESP redirector, e.g. `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/mclick?params=b.v1...&pid=v1...`. The header logo link carries `data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"`. Destinations are hidden behind opaque params, so landing URLs and UTM parameters can't be verified from the source.
- The QA probe covered only 12 of 67 links (55 skipped, deprioritized footer, social and utility links). Link health for the other 55 is unverified. The unsubscribe link was always probed, but the data shows no failure for it.
- The open pixel `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=...` is present, and the QA report flags it as an image missing alt text. This is a minor accessibility warning, and `alt=""` would be correct for a tracking pixel.
