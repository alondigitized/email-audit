# Today only ⚡ Up to 50% off Flash Sale DEALS!!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-23**
## Executive summary

- This is a DICK'S flash sale that gets lost in its own catalog. The "Up to 50% off" headline is clear and the green/red Shop Now buttons are easy to find. But the hero is a giant "FLASH" wordmark that is cropped to "FLAS" at the right edge. The first thing a subscriber sees is a clipped graphic, and the offer sits in small text below it.
- The body then becomes a long vertical stack of unrelated deals: HOKA up to 40%, DSG crewneck $34.98, NFL up to 25%, TaylorMade $200 off, cardio up to 50%, fishing up to 50%, then another "EXTRA 25% OFF" clearance banner. A "flash sale" should have one hero deal, and this has about seven. The clearance banner repeats the 10/22 sends ("Don't miss an extra 25% off select clearance!"), so it feels recycled.
- Judgement: a competent retail template that is diluted. It's a 6, not better, because of the cropped hero, the six competing offers and the heavy cadence. DICK'S has sent 25% clearance and 50% weekly-deals emails almost daily since 10/20.

## What's working

- "Today only ⚡" in the subject, with a "One Day Only" tag and a stated 10/23 11:59PM PT deadline in the body, gives credible urgency.
- The "Flash Sale: Up to 50% Off" headline with a dark red Shop Now button sits in a clean white block below the hero.
- Specific price points ($34.98 DSG crewneck, $200 off TaylorMade P-Series irons) give the generic percentages some concrete value.
- The ScoreCard rewards strip and the "Order Today. Sport It Tomorrow." pickup and delivery line at the top add member and convenience value.
- Alternating image-left and image-right modules with consistent green buttons keep the scroll rhythm easy to follow.

## What's weak

- The hero wordmark is cut off at the right edge ("FLAS"), so the key image looks broken.
- Six faded gray category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Team Sports) look disabled and are nearly invisible.
- There are too many unrelated offers, with a different discount depth in each module (25%, 40%, 50%, $200 off, $34.98). No single hero deal stands out.
- Every button says "Shop Now", so the CTA copy gives no hint where it leads.
- The second "EXTRA 25% OFF" clearance banner re-runs the 10/22 creative and competes with the flash-sale message.
- The footer is dense with fine print, and the pre-footer "Check out these picks" grid has no prices.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the hero so the whole "FLASH SALE" lockup and "UP TO 50% OFF" are visible and readable, and put the offer inside the hero image.
- 2. Cut the body to one hero deal plus three or four category tiles. Drop the clearance banner so a flash-sale email isn't also a clearance email.
- 3. Make the category pills real buttons with solid fill and contrast, or remove them.
- 4. Replace the "Shop Now" buttons with specific copy ("Shop HOKA Running", "Shop Golf Iron Deals") and show prices on the product grid.
- 5. Add a countdown or an explicit "Ends 11:59 PM PT" line near the top, and rest the audience for a day or two after the 10/20 to 10/22 volume.

- **Subject Alt B:** `⚡ 24-hour sale: HOKA, NFL & golf deals`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `HOKA up to 40% off, $200 off TaylorMade irons, plus free store pickup in 1 hour.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sale ends at 11:59 PM PT. Order today, pick up in store within the hour.`
