# 100s of NEW clearance styles you don't want to miss! 💸
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-02**
## Executive summary

- DICK'S is recycling the dark red "HUNDREDS OF NEW CLEARANCE STYLES" hero from the 8/30 send ("HUNDREDS of new clearance styles just added! Take a look 👉") almost verbatim — same bold red/black stadium-seat creative, same headline treatment, now with a slightly punchier subject and a 💸 emoji bolted on. It's the least-repeated creative in this brand's recent rotation (only its second outing vs. the green-locker hero's fourth-plus), so it still reads fresher than most of what's landed this month, but it's no longer "the first genuinely different email" — it's a repeat.
- The body is a long, well-organized scroll: category chips, a $100-and-under shoes module, an exclusive-brands apparel block, a Stanley x Caitlin Clark launch, cleats, Maxfli golf balls, and then — buried near the bottom — the actual "Epic End-of-Summer Sale, up to 50% off" banner that's been the hero of at least five other sends this month. That banner should either be the lead offer or should be cut; sandwiched here it just confuses which promo this email is actually about.
- Overall this is a middling, safe send — decent variety, no render bugs, but no single unmistakable offer and a hero creative that's already been used once this week.

## What's working

- Clean, bug-free render — no overlapping text, broken images, or placeholder content anywhere in the scroll.
- Hero headline ("HUNDREDS OF NEW CLEARANCE STYLES") is legible and high-contrast against the dark red backdrop.
- Category chip row (Men's / Women's / Kids's / $25 and Under / Fan Shop / All Sale) gives an easy secondary path to browse.
- Genuine product variety — golf, cleats, apparel, backpacks, socks — gives multiple entry points for different shoppers.
- Stanley x Caitlin Clark "Just Launched" module adds a timely, non-clearance hook that breaks up the promo monotony.

## What's weak

- The hero creative is a near-repeat of the 8/30 "HUNDREDS of new clearance styles" send — same red stadium backdrop, same bold red-on-black type treatment.
- No concrete number, percentage, or dollar figure appears anywhere near the hero — "Hundreds of new clearance styles" and "Fresh Deals You Don't Want to Miss" are both vague.
- The "Up to 50% Off / Epic End-of-Summer Sale" banner — this brand's actual highest-value offer, run as the hero in five prior sends this month — is buried two-thirds of the way down, competing with golf balls and cleats for attention.
- Email is very long (11+ modules) with no anchor navigation, so most recipients will bail before reaching the 50%-off banner or the "Check out these picks" grid.
- "Explore more deals" chip row and the "Shop by Sport / Footwear / Clothing..." footer links are redundant with each other and add scroll without adding decision-making value.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real number in the hero. "Hundreds of new styles" with no percentage reads weaker than the 50%-off banner buried below it — either lead with "Up to 50% Off Clearance" as the hero framing, or add a supporting line under the headline with the actual discount range.
- 2. Move the "Epic End-of-Summer Sale — Up to 50% Off" banner to directly beneath the hero (or merge it into the hero itself) — it's the strongest offer in the email and currently sits after seven other modules.
- 3. Cut the email by a third — the $100-and-under shoes, cleats, and Maxfli golf modules can consolidate into a single "Shop the Sale" carousel rather than three full-width sections.
- 4. Vary the hero art directly, not just the subject line — this same red stadium-seat creative ran 8/30; swap the background photo or color treatment so repeat subscribers don't get instant creative fatigue.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 50% off + hundreds of new clearance styles`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New clearance just dropped — shop before it's gone`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Score up to 50% off Nike, adidas, New Balance and more`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Same-day delivery, free pickup, and hundreds of new markdowns`
