# ScoreCard exclusive! 2X Points on your app purchase TODAY ONLY 📲
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-27**
## Executive summary

- The hero is the strongest thing DICK'S has run in this stretch: a hand holding a phone showing a "SCORECARD 2X POINTS" push notification is a genuinely different visual than the recycled paddleboard/beach-lifestyle heroes from the 2026-07-24 through 2026-07-26 sends, and it ties directly to a specific, time-boxed mechanic (2X points, app-only, today only). It's the first email in this string that isn't just another "Up to X% Off" clearance rerun with a new headline pasted on.
- The problem is everything below the fold. Scroll past the ScoreCard hero and it's the exact same Nike Deal Days block, Stanley 1913 tumblers, Rawlings glove promo, and DSG outdoor-gear grid that ran in the 2026-07-26 "Nike Deal Days are live now" send. This isn't a new campaign — it's last week's body copy with a fresh ScoreCard banner bolted on top, which matters a lot given this is roughly the fourteenth promotional blast from this sender in under two weeks.
- Net: a well-executed, well-targeted hero wrapped around a tired, over-reused body. Worth opening for the loyalty hook; not worth clicking past the first fold for anyone who's already seen the last three sends.

## What's working

- The hero mockup (phone + push notification + big "2X POINTS" callout) is instantly legible and visually distinct from the last five sends in this cadence.
- The offer is genuinely specific and time-boxed: "Mobile Monday," "Today Only," 2X points on an app purchase, with an exact expiration ("Valid through 7/27/26 12:59PM PT") rather than vague "limited time" language.
- ScoreCard membership is foregrounded as the mechanism (exclusive framing, "Not a ScoreCard member? Join today"), which is a real loyalty hook rather than a generic discount.
- The GET OFFER button sits immediately below the hero with no scrolling required, and the render itself is clean — no broken images, overlapping text, or layout breaks.

## What's weak

- The entire body below the hero (Nike Deal Days, Stanley 1913, Rawlings, DSG outdoor gear, Maxfli) is a direct repeat of the 2026-07-26 send — no visual or copy differentiation for a subscriber who opened that email three days ago.
- The offer is app-only and sign-in-gated, which is a meaningful restriction not surfaced until the small fine print below the CTA — most readers scanning the hero will assume it applies in-app or in-store universally.
- "GET OFFER" is a generic CTA — it doesn't tell the reader what they're getting (points on what? any purchase, any amount?) the way a more specific button would.
- At roughly the fourteenth send in two weeks, cadence fatigue is a real risk — this is now background noise territory for anyone triaging an inbox.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the recycled Nike Deal Days / Stanley / Rawlings / DSG stack or swap it for content that's actually new — right now the ScoreCard hero is doing all the work and everything after it reads like a rerun.
- 2. Make the CTA specific to the mechanic: "Shop & Earn 2X" or "Activate 2X Points" instead of the generic "GET OFFER" — ties the button directly to the hero promise.
- 3. Surface the app-only / sign-in restriction above the fold, not in fine print — a line like "In the DICK'S app only — today until 12:59PM PT" under the hero avoids a bait-and-switch feel for anyone who clicks expecting it to apply anywhere.
- 4. Space this send out from the last ScoreCard/Mobile Monday-style promo — back-to-back near-identical bodies erode open rates faster than any one email can make up for.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Mobile Monday: 2X ScoreCard Points, app only, today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your ScoreCard doubles today — app purchases only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sign in to the app before 12:59PM PT to activate.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Not a member yet? Join ScoreCard and earn 2X today.`
