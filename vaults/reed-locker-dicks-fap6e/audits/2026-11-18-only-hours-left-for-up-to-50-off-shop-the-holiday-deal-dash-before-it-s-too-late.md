# Only hours left for up to 50% OFF ⏳ Shop the Holiday DEAL Dash before it's too late...
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent but redundant last-call send. The red-and-gold "UP TO 50% OFF" hero, the "Final Hours" tag and the single "Shop Now" button make the offer easy to understand. The deadline is also stated plainly in the fine print: "through 11/18/26 11:59PM PT." The subject's "only hours left" is backed by the body, which is the main reason it works.
- It also repeats the same offer as the 11/18 "Hurry, the Holiday Deal Dash ends today!" send earlier the same day. Below the 50% block sits a second hero, "UP TO 70% OFF" clearance, which is the clearance campaign from the 11/16, 11/17 and 11/18 "Up to 70% OFF select clearance" subjects. Two equally loud red heroes compete, so the urgency of the first one dilutes. A subscriber who has already seen this deal today has no new reason to open or click.
- Judgment: solid execution and credible urgency, but weak on freshness and a little too busy. Expect average engagement and some fatigue.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 50% OFF" hero is huge and high-contrast, so the offer is clear at a glance.
- "Final Hours" above the headline, plus the explicit 11:59PM PT deadline, gives the urgency credibility.
- Each block has one clear green or red "Shop Now" button.
- The ScoreCard line ("300 Points = $10 Reward") puts loyalty at the top without clutter.
- The category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Holiday Shop) give an easy way to browse by interest.

## What's weak

- The second 70% off clearance hero competes with the 50% offer, so there are two headline numbers and no single priority.
- Both buttons say a generic "Shop Now" with no category or product, so the copy is not specific.
- The gift grid of six products (tumbler, advent calendar, hoodie and others) is unrelated to the Deal Dash, and none of the items show a price or discount.
- The subject is 86 characters, so it will truncate on mobile. The all-caps "OFF" and "DEAL", the emoji and the trailing "..." read as spammy.
- Nothing is new compared with the earlier Deal Dash email and the recent clearance sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. Send one offer per email. On the last day, lead with the 50% Deal Dash and move clearance to its own send or drop it.
- 2. Replace the generic gift picks with products that are actually discounted in the Deal Dash, with the sale price and "% off" on each.
- 3. Make the button copy specific, for example "Shop Deal Dash Brands" or "Shop 50% Off Shoes".
- 4. Cut the subject to under 50 characters and stop reusing the "ends today" framing across sends. Suppress this send for anyone who opened or clicked the earlier Deal Dash email.
- 5. Add a live countdown timer, as in the 11/16 "Final Hours" email, to make the deadline feel real.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Deal Dash ends tonight: up to 50% off ⏳`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 50% off ends at 11:59PM PT`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Last hours to save on brands you love, plus free ScoreCard rewards.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shoes, gear and team wear, all marked down until midnight PT.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Well-structured, table-based markup with a hidden preheader, `lang="en"` and `role="presentation"` layout tables. The one confirmed failure is a near-empty plain-text part (9 chars), and the tracking pixel and five icons have no `alt` attribute. The HTML was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe, link `href`s and UTM parameters could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage is partial.** 12 of 51 links were probed and 39 were skipped as footer, utility or social. No probe failures are reported, but nothing is confirmed for the skipped 39.
- **Open pixel.** `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1.…` is a 1x1 `<img>` with `width="1" height="1"`. It has no `alt` and no inline `display:none`/`border:0`. Clients that render it may show a broken-image box or add a stray gap.
- **Link markup.** Anchors carry `class="arc-link" data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"`. These are click-tracking wrappers, and their `href` values were cut off, so I can't check redirect behavior.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text** on 6 images (QA-confirmed):
  - the open pixel
  - `icon-vc-sms-1`
  - `icon-vc-sportsmatter`
  - `icon-vc-bpg-1`
  - `icon-vc-favorite-1`
  - `icon-location-2025-1`
- The icons are `s7d6.scene7.com/…?fmt=png-alpha` assets, probably footer or value-prop icons. Decorative ones should have `alt=""`. Informative ones need real text. A missing attribute makes screen readers announce the filename or URL.
- **Plain-text part:** QA reports a FAIL at 9 chars. The multipart/alternative text body is effectively empty, which hurts accessibility, text-only clients and spam scoring.
- **Dead tag:** `<link rel="icon" href="…favicon.ico">` in `<head>` is ignored or stripped by email clients.
- **Fonts:** Archivo is loaded from `fonts.gstatic.com` with an `Arial, sans-serif` fallback, which is handled correctly. Outlook MSO conditionals are present.
- No other rendering issues were found in the visible source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible HTML or the subject.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** QA reports both as not found. The QA note attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly stripping them, so this is **unconfirmed**. Verify against the raw message from the origin ESP. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, expected via the relay. SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. The From domain `em.dcsg.com` aligns with the tracking host `data.em.dcsg.com`.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address:** not visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm CAN-SPAM body requirements.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable from the provided data. UTM parameters and destination `href`s were not in the truncated HTML, and QA didn't report landing-page or redirect results. No issues are confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the plain-text part (FAIL).** Generate a full text alternative with CTAs, URLs and the unsubscribe link, not a 9-char stub.
2. **Add `alt` to all 6 flagged images.** Use `alt=""` for the pixel and any decorative icons, and descriptive text for informative ones. Add `border="0"` and `style="display:block"` or `display:none` to the pixel.
3. **Check the raw headers at the source** (not the relay) for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, plus SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass. If the relay strips them, make the audit pipeline capture the original headers so these warnings stop being ambiguous.
4. **Re-run on the full, untruncated HTML** to verify the footer unsubscribe link, postal address, UTM consistency and all 51 `href`s. Consider probing the skipped footer links, at least the unsubscribe and preference-center links.
5. **Remove `<link rel="icon">`** from the template. It has no effect in email.
