# Act fast! 100s of new clearance styles won't be here for long... 💸
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-09**
## Executive summary

- This is the same DICK'S weekly-digest format the brand has run repeatedly — "Hundreds of New Clearance Styles" hero over a red track/stadium background, then a long stack of category offers (shoes, clothing, gloves, putters, drinkware, fan gear) cascading down the page. It's essentially the structural twin of the 8/7 "Go, go, go!" send that scored 7.00: one hero banner, no single dominant offer, then eight-plus distinct promos competing for attention below the fold.
- The hero itself is vague by design — "hundreds of new styles" with no percentage or dollar figure attached, which is a step down from the "Up to 50% off" and "EXTRA 25% off" heroes that have anchored the brand's higher-scoring sends this week. The strongest concrete number on the page (Up to 50% Off Select Clothing, $100 Off Odyssey Putters) is buried three and four scrolls down. This reads as a mid-tier browse-the-catalog digest, not a targeted push — solid execution, unremarkable offer.

## What's working

- Clean, on-brand red/white DICK'S styling with no render bugs — no overlapping text, broken images, or placeholder content.
- Clear category nav bar ("Men's / Women's / Kids' / $25 and Under / Fan Shop / All Sale") right under the hero for fast wayfinding.
- Concrete percentage offers are visible once you scroll (Up to 30% Off Shoes, Up to 50% Off Clothing, Up to 45% Off Gloves, $100 Off Putters).
- Consistent green "SHOP NOW" CTA button repeated after every section — no ambiguity about the click target.
- "This Week's Deals" mid-page banner and the "$20 off $100+ instantly" SMS opt-in add secondary conversion paths.

## What's weak

- Hero headline has zero offer specificity — "Hundreds of New Clearance Styles" gives no percentage, dollar amount, or urgency beyond "won't last long."
- No single hero product or price anchors the top of the email; visual hierarchy leads to a vague claim rather than the day's best deal.
- This is at least the fourth near-identical stacked-digest send this week (8/6, 8/7 x2), and the format is starting to blur together for a repeat subscriber.
- The email is very long — nine-plus distinct offer blocks before reaching "This Week's Deals," which dilutes urgency rather than building it.
- No countdown timer or hard deadline like the 8/8 "07:59:48" send that scored 8.00 — the "won't be here for long" line in the subject isn't backed by any visible expiration in the body.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the hero with the strongest concrete number instead of "hundreds of styles" — pull "Up to 50% Off Select Clothing" or the $100 putter offer into the hero banner itself, matching the higher-performing 8/6 and 8/8 sends that scored 8-9.
- 2. Add a visible countdown or explicit end-date/time to the hero, since the subject promises urgency ("won't be here for long") that the body doesn't deliver.
- 3. Trim the offer stack — six to seven distinct category blocks is a lot; consider splitting into two sends or moving lower-priority categories (fan gear, drinkware) into a "more deals" footer link.
- 4. Move the SMS opt-in ($20 off $100+) higher, near the hero, since it's a stronger, more concrete offer than the vague clearance headline.

- **Subject Alt B:** `New clearance drop: up to 50% off shoes, gear & more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fresh markdowns on shoes, apparel and gear — sizes are going fast.`
