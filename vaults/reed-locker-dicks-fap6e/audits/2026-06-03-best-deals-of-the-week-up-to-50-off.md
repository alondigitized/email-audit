# Best DEALS of the week = Up to 50% OFF 🙌
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-03**
## Executive summary

- DICK'S swaps the clearance-blitz creative for a "Best Deals of the Week" compilation, and that pivot alone makes this the most structurally sound send since the May 30 launch of the extra-25%-off-clearance series. The hero is clean — a lifestyle shot with a definitive 50% OFF badge, above-the-fold "SHOP NOW" button, and a category nav row that lets subscribers self-select immediately. The Daly Life partnership callout adds editorial texture that none of the recent flash-sale or clearance sends had.
- The problem isn't the email; it's the inbox. Yesterday (June 2) alone delivered two clearance variants — "Happening NOW!" and "Worth a look!" — and this arrives the morning after. That's the tenth-plus DICK'S email in roughly five days. Even a well-built send loses opens at that cadence. Golf subscribers are the clearest exception: two dedicated golf modules (Titleist outdoor stands, select clubs) plus the Daly Life launch give a high-AOV shopper genuine reasons to scroll.
- Well-executed compilation that would score higher if it weren't competing against nine prior DICK'S emails in the same inbox. It's worth opening; it's hard to feel urgency about opening.

## What's working

- **Hero hierarchy resolves instantly**: The 50% OFF badge over the cyclists/swimwear lifestyle image is the strongest above-the-fold execution in this week's DICK'S cadence.
- **Golf gets proportionate depth**: Two product modules plus a partnership launch is the right weight for a sport with high average order values.
- **Specific brand and product names appear in section headers**: "20% Off Men's Carhartt KZ1 Tees" and "BONUS 50% OFF Titleist Outdoor Stands" are purchase-intent copy, not category filler.
- **Summer timing is spot-on**: "Must-have gear for an epic summer!" lands cleanly on June 3.
- **No render issues**: Layout is clean across all sections, no broken images or overlapping text visible.

## What's weak

- **Cadence fatigue is the lead problem**: Arriving as email number ten-plus in five days (the June 2 double-send was already a stretch), this competes against subscriber exhaustion more than inbox competition.
- **Eight sections collapse into visual sameness below the hero**: Every module has equal weight — same image size, same button, same "SHOP NOW" copy — with no scannable tiebreaker for a skimming reader.
- **"SHOP NOW" on every CTA discards purchase intent**: A golf shopper who scrolled to the clubs section wants "Shop Golf Clubs," not a blank redirect verb.
- **No ScoreCard integration**: After the June 1 2X Points push, cutting all loyalty hooks from the very next batch wastes the program momentum that send built.
- **Daly Life callout requires prior knowledge**: "Welcome to the Team, Daly Life!" has no descriptor, no logo, and no product pull — uninitiated subscribers will read it as filler and scroll past.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Reduce to three sections.** Based on click history, golf + shoes + one apparel module would outperform eight equal-weighted modules. Every additional section past five halves the average read depth on mobile.
- 2. **Make every CTA category-specific.** "Shop Golf Deals," "Shop Clearance Shoes," "Shop Basketball" each carry purchase intent. "SHOP NOW" carries none.
- 3. **Add a concrete expiration.** "Deals available through Sunday" turns "of the week" from vague to bounded — no hard oversell required.
- 4. **Give Daly Life one sentence.** "Golf icon John Daly's signature gear is officially here." Removes the knowledge-gap friction for the 80% of the list unfamiliar with the collab.
- 5. **Reintroduce ScoreCard.** A single line — "Earning ScoreCard points on all of these" — bridges back to the June 1 loyalty push and rewards the subscriber for staying engaged through a heavy-send week.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Best of the week: up to 50% off golf, shoes & more`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your weekly picks — half off select golf, hoops & kicks`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Golf clubs, Carhartt tees, basketball gear — curated, discounted, ending Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New Daly Life collab just dropped + deals across 6 categories`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — DICK'S Sporting Goods: "Best DEALS of the week = Up to 50% OFF"

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Email is built on Epsilon/Harmony ESP infrastructure with standard MSO-conditional table layout and Epsilon click-tracking redirects. The most significant technical issues are invalid JSON-LD (JavaScript comments in JSON, stale hardcoded dates, and unpopulated merge fields) and no visible UTM parameters on click-through URLs in the truncated source.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Epsilon click-tracking confirmed** — all links route through `app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/v610000019e8d203ac6cae008f4bbc780a0/…` with a `__dU__` encoded subscriber token. This is expected for the ESP.

**UTM parameters — not confirmed present.** The click URL visible in the logo anchor (`…2a641f94-fa25-4004-8f51-fae8ed2d69cf?__dU__=…`) contains only the Epsilon tracking token. If UTMs are appended by the redirect service at delivery time, this is fine — but if they are absent, organic/email attribution will be lost in GA4/site analytics.

**Open pixel** at `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/v610000019e8d203ac6cae008f4bbc780a0/…` is present and correctly hidden via `display:none; max-height:0px`. No issues.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`overflow:hidden` on a `<table>` element** (`header-non-sc-member` table, `border-radius:8px 8px 0px 0px; overflow:hidden`) — `overflow:hidden` has inconsistent support on table elements across email clients; Outlook will ignore it entirely, and some Android clients may clip content unexpectedly. The border-radius is decorative and safe to keep; the `overflow:hidden` on the table is the risk.

**Web font loading** — Archivo loaded via `https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/archivo/v7/…` scoped inside `@media screen`. Arial fallback is declared. No issues; pattern is correct.

**MSO conditional wrapping** — `<!--[if (gte mso)|(IE)]>` table wrapper is present for Outlook; structure looks correct in the visible fragment.

**`role="presentation"` on layout tables** — applied correctly throughout visible source.

**`lang="en"` on `<html>`** — present. Good.

**Logo `alt` text** — truncated before the `alt` attribute is visible. Cannot confirm — should be verified in full source to ensure it's non-empty.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Segment flag present but unflagged merge fields in JSON-LD:**

```json
"description": "",
"discountCode": "",
"availabilityEnds": ""
```

All three fields in the `DiscountOffer` block and the `image` field in `PromotionCard` are empty strings. These appear to be unpopulated merge tokens. If the Gmail Promotions tab annotation is a goal, these must be filled or removed.

**`id="header-non-sc-member"`** — indicates this is the non-ScoreCard-member variant. Confirm the member variant (`header-sc-member`) is suppressed correctly for this segment.

No unresolved `{{token}}` or `[MERGE_TAG]` syntax visible in the HTML fragment.

---

### 5. Compliance

**Sending domain:** `DSG@e.dcsg.com` — `e.dcsg.com` is Epsilon's authenticated subdomain for DSG. SPF/DKIM alignment against this subdomain should be confirmed via header inspection (not available in HTML source alone).

**Physical mailing address and unsubscribe link** — not visible in the truncated source. Both are required under CAN-SPAM. Their presence in the email footer should be verified against the full HTML. This is a critical gap to close if they are missing.

**Unsubscribe mechanism** — cannot confirm from truncated source. Must be present and functional (one-click or ≤10-day manual process per CAN-SPAM).

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**UTM attribution gap (see §2)** — if Epsilon's redirect service does not append UTMs, clicks will land on `dickssportinggoods.com` without campaign tagging. Standard expected parameters: `utm_source=email`, `utm_medium=email`, `utm_campaign=<campaign_id>`.

**JSON-LD `availabilityStarts` is hardcoded to `2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00`** — this is a six-year-old stale date, almost certainly a template placeholder that was never updated. Gmail Promotions tab renders this as the deal start date; a 2020 date will either be ignored or show incorrect information.

**`DiscountOffer` and `PromotionCard` schema types** — `DiscountOffer` is not a canonical `schema.org` type (correct type is `Offer`); `PromotionCard` is also non-standard. Google may silently ignore unrecognized types.

**`http://schema.org/`** — the `@context` uses `http://` not `https://`. Should be `https://schema.org/` per current Google guidance.

---

### 7. Recommendations

| Priority | Issue | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | JSON-LD contains JavaScript-style `//` comments — **invalid JSON**, will throw parse errors in all consumers | Strip all `//` comments from the `<script type="application/ld+json">` block |
| Critical | `availabilityStarts` hardcoded to `2020-03-06` | Populate dynamically per send date or remove if unused |
| High | `description`, `discountCode`, `availabilityEnds`, `image` are empty strings in JSON-LD | Populate via merge tags or remove the block if Gmail Promotions tab annotation is not in use |
| High | CAN-SPAM footer (physical address + unsubscribe) — not confirmed in truncated source | Verify presence in full HTML; this is a legal requirement |
| Medium | UTM parameters not visible on click-through URLs | Confirm Epsilon appends UTMs at delivery; if not, add `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` to destination URLs |
| Medium | `overflow:hidden` on `<table>` element | Move decorative border-radius to a wrapping `<td>` or use an outer container `<div>` where overflow is safe |
| Low | `http://schema.org/` → `https://schema.org/` | Update `@context` values in JSON-LD |
