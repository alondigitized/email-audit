# 📥 INSIDE: Up to 50% off our best deals of the week ➡️
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-03**
## Executive summary

- This is the same DICK'S playbook again — grid of scattered products, a red "UP TO 50% OFF" stamp, and a subject line ("INSIDE: Up to 50% off our best deals of the week") that adds zero new information over the 8/30-9/2 sends already logged. The 📥 and ➡️ emojis are a new subject wrapper on old creative — the hero polo/shorts/pickleball-paddle collage and red 50% block have run under at least five different subject lines this week alone.
- The render itself is clean — no broken images, no overlapping text — and the ScoreCard/rewards bar at top is a nice loyalty touch. But there's no single hero product or specific offer to click toward; it's "Shop Now" into an undifferentiated deals page, then six more sections of unrelated categories (golf, fan shop, drinkware, outdoor, fitness) before you even get to named products like adidas Samba or HOKA Clifton. This is a browse email dressed up as a "best deals" email, and the cadence fatigue is now the dominant signal — it reads like an inbox photocopy of Monday's send.

## What's working

- ScoreCard points balance (300 Points + $10 Reward) is a real loyalty personalization touch, visible above the fold.
- Clean render — no layout bugs, broken images, or lorem ipsum.
- Named specific SKUs lower in the email (adidas Samba, Nike Varsity Elite Backpack, HOKA Clifton 10) give something concrete to click.
- Category pill navigation (Men's/Women's/Kids'/Clearance/Team Sports/Back-to-School) offers a fast path past the generic hero.

## What's weak

- Hero has no single offer or product focus — polo, shorts, and pickleball paddles are crammed next to a generic "50% OFF" block with no anchor item.
- This is the same red-stamp "UP TO 50% OFF" creative logged repeatedly since 8/30 under five-plus different subject wrappers — zero creative refresh.
- "Best Deals of the Week" + "Shop Now" is a vague CTA into an undifferentiated deals page, not a specific product or category.
- Email requires scrolling through ~7 sections (golf, fan shop, drinkware, outdoor, fitness, "check out these picks") before reaching any named product.
- Subject line emojis (📥 ➡️) read as filler, not information — doesn't disclose an actual dollar/percent figure beyond the now-generic "up to 50%."

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the collage hero with one specific hero product + one specific price cut (e.g., "adidas Samba $54.99, was $85") instead of the generic 50%-off stamp that's been recycled all week.
- 2. Move a named-SKU section (adidas Samba, HOKA Clifton, Nike backpack) up near the top — those are the only genuinely clickable items in this email and they're buried near the bottom.
- 3. Retire the red stadium-seat "UP TO 50% OFF" die-cut entirely for at least one send cycle — the brand history shows it's now shown up 6+ times in two weeks.
- 4. Tighten the CTA copy from generic "Shop Now" to something specific like "Shop Samba Deals" tied to the actual hero product.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your ScoreCard points + up to 50% off, inside`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus: HOKA, Nike, and Back-to-School picks under $30`
