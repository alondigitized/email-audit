# Start TOMORROW! 🎉 Get ready for our biggest sale of summer 👉
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-21**
## Executive summary

- "DICK'S Deal Days" is a legitimate creative refresh — a named summer sale event with its own visual identity (green athletic hero, event branding) breaking cleanly from the six-plus consecutive dark-red clearance blasts. For the first time in this stretch of brand history, Dick's is building anticipation rather than just repeating a discount. That's the right instinct.
- The problem is that this is a pure teaser email: the sale starts June 22 (tomorrow), so the primary hook leads nowhere actionable today. Worse, they filled the gap with the identical EXTRA 25% OFF clearance banner that has run since June 16 — the same creative subscribers have seen at minimum five times this week. The contrast between "biggest sale of summer" in the hero and "here's the same clearance offer you've ignored all week" in the body is jarring and undercuts the excitement they're trying to build.
- The app push is intelligently placed — if Deal Days has app-exclusive access or early-unlock features, surfacing that here makes sense. But without spelling out a concrete app benefit tied to tomorrow's launch (a specific drop time, a members-only preview), it reads as a generic download plea.

## What's working

- **New brand identity**: The green "DEAL DAYS" hero is a clean visual break from the red clearance drumbeat; first time this week the email actually looks different on open.
- **Preview text adds context**: "kicks off on 6/22/26. You won't want to miss daily deals, exclusive offers" provides the who/when the subject teases.
- **Product grid is functional**: Six specific SKUs with names and model details (Stanley 40 oz., HOKA Bondi 8, etc.) give the eye somewhere to land.
- **App section is integrated, not bolted on**: Framing Deal Days access as an in-app feature is a plausible reason to download — stronger than a naked "get the app" CTA.

## What's weak

- **The hero promise has no payoff today**: "Start TOMORROW!" in the subject then nothing to shop in the Deal Days section is a friction wall — subscribers click, land, and find a locked sale.
- **Recycled clearance creative kills the build-up**: Inserting the identical EXTRA 25% OFF red banner underneath the new branding signals either poor coordination or a "might as well" decision that dilutes the event launch.
- **No Deal Days specifics**: "Daily deals, exclusive offers and so much more" is marketing filler — no category teaser, no door-buster, no hint of how deep the deals go.
- **Subject line is over 50 chars and emoji-heavy**: At ~60 characters with two emoji, it truncates on most mobile inboxes; "Get ready for our biggest sale of summer" is the clause that gets cut.
- **No ScoreCard/loyalty mention**: The June 20 and 21 loyalty emails built goodwill; this event launch abandons that thread entirely.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Give the teaser a concrete hook**: Even one "door-buster" hint ("Up to 40% off Nike. Starts 6/22.") turns this from vapor into anticipation. Add a countdown — hours until launch, not just "tomorrow."
- 2. **Kill the clearance banner or reframe it**: Either remove the recycled EXTRA 25% OFF creative (it collides with "biggest sale ever" messaging) or rename it "Start shopping early while you wait" with a fresh treatment.
- 3. **Tie the app CTA to Deal Days access**: "Get early access to Day 1 deals — App only, 6AM" is a reason to download. "Easier Shopping Is a Tap Away" is not.
- 4. **Shorten the subject and earn the urgency**: Drop emojis, lead with the sale name.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Deal Days starts tomorrow — here's what's coming`
- **Subject Alt B:** `DICK'S Deal Days: biggest summer sale kicks off 6/22`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Daily drops, up to 40% off Nike, Under Armour & more — unlock early access in the app`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `One day away: daily deals, exclusive drops, and early app access starting June 22`
- 5. **Tease the category lineup**: Show the categories going on sale (footwear, outdoor, fitness) so subscribers know whether to set a reminder. Right now the product grid is random rather than curated to the event.

## Full review
## 1. Overview

"DICK'S Deal Days" is a legitimate creative refresh — a named summer sale event with its own visual identity (green athletic hero, event branding) breaking cleanly from the six-plus consecutive dark-red clearance blasts. ## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — DICK'S Deal Days Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email uses Epsilon/Harmony as the ESP with standard click-tracking redirects and an open-pixel tracker. Two confirmed bugs will silently break Gmail Promotions tab rendering: invalid JSON-LD (comments in JSON) and a stale 2020 date in the structured data block.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Open pixel** — `<img src='https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/v610000019eebe5571fc847e66e96c569c8/...'>`  
Standard Epsilon open tracker. No issue, but it has no `alt=""` attribute, which some spam filters flag.

**Click tracking** — All links wrap through `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/v610...`. Pattern is consistent with Epsilon click-tracking. No bare/untracked links visible in the truncated source.

**UTM parameters** — Cannot confirm from the truncated source. UTM values are typically baked into the encoded destination URL inside the `T/` redirect. Requires full link decode to verify (see §6).

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**CSS syntax error in Outlook conditional block** — Critical for Outlook rendering:
```css
<!--[if (gte mso 9)|(IE)]><style>
  div { ... padding:0;"} ← trailing " before } is invalid CSS
```
The spurious `"` before `}` in the `div` rule will cause Outlook's MSO renderer to drop the rule or corrupt subsequent declarations.

**Google Fonts version pinned to `v7`** — Font URLs reference `archivo/v7/...`:
```
https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/archivo/v7/k3kQo8UDI-1M0wlSfdnoLmvDIaI.woff2
```
v7 is several major versions behind. Google's CDN keeps old versions live, but this is a latent breakage risk if Google ever deprecates the path. Not urgent.

**Tracking pixel missing `alt` attribute** — The `display:none` SSL pixel at `id="ssl"` has no `alt=""`, which can trigger spam heuristics in some filters.

**Logo `<img>` tag truncated in source** — Cannot audit `alt` text on the main logo. Needs full source to confirm.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens visible in the truncated source (no `{{firstName}}`, `%%FIRST_NAME%%`, or similar). The `id="header-non-sc-member"` suggests a loyalty-status conditional block exists — cannot confirm the corresponding `sc-member` variant is present without the full source.

---

### 5. Compliance

**JSON-LD block contains JavaScript comments — invalid JSON**

The `<script type="application/ld+json">` block uses `//` comments, which are not valid JSON syntax:
```json
{ // GIF & WEBP images are not supported...
  "@type": "PromotionCard",
  // DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!
  "image": ""
}
```
This is a **dev/designer comment left in production**. Any JSON parser (Gmail, validators) will reject the entire structured data block, disabling Gmail Promotions tab annotations.

**Stale `availabilityStarts` date in DiscountOffer schema:**
```json
"availabilityStarts": "2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"
```
This is a 6-year-old placeholder. Gmail may suppress the promotion card if the availability window is not current.

**Empty required DiscountOffer fields:**
```json
"description": "",
"discountCode": "",
"availabilityEnds": ""
```
Empty `availabilityEnds` means the offer has no expiry in structured data — inconsistent with a time-limited sale.

**Unsubscribe block** — Not visible in the truncated source. Cannot confirm CAN-SPAM footer compliance (physical address, unsubscribe link, opt-out mechanism). Requires full HTML.

**Authentication headers** — Not visible from HTML source alone; would require email header inspection (DKIM, SPF, DMARC). Not auditable from the HTML excerpt.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

Cannot fully audit — all destination URLs are encoded inside Epsilon `T/` redirect tokens and the source is truncated. To verify:
1. Decode each `T/` redirect to extract the final URL
2. Confirm UTM params are present: `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content` at minimum
3. Confirm landing pages are live and match the "Deal Days" sale context

**PromotionCard `image` field is empty** — `"image": ""` in the JSON-LD means the Gmail Promotions tab will show no hero image even if the JSON-LD were otherwise valid.
