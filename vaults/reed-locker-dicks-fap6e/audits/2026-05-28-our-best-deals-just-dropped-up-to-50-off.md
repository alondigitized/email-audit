# Our best deals just dropped! (Up to 50% off!)
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-28**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S third consecutive send with an "Up to 50% off" hero — following yesterday's "Up to 50% off! Don't miss these deals...👀" (6/10) and the day-before's "Best deals of the week are HERE! 📍 Up to 50% OFF" (6/10). The structure is identical: lifestyle hero with the 50% badge, multi-category tab nav (Men's/Women's/Kids/Golf/Outdoor/Clearance), price-point product sections, and a "Check out these picks" grid. Nothing has materially changed except the subject line phrasing.
- The strongest execution here is the product-level "Check out these picks" section — HOKA, Nike, New Balance, DSG SKUs with actual names and prices. That's concrete and useful. The $100-and-under shoes and $50-and-under clothing sections also give the subscriber a clear filter.
- But this email is doing real damage to the brand's subscriber relationship. Sending three nearly identical "50% off" emails in two days trains subscribers to tune out, and there's no credible urgency mechanism — no "ends tonight," no countdown, no threshold. It feels like a permanent sale, not a drop.

## What's working

- **Price-point filter sections** ("$100 and Under Shoes," "$50 and Under Clothing") are scannable and reduce decision friction.
- **Named SKUs in "Check out these picks"** — HOKA Clifton, Nike Kali, New Balance 530, DSG shorts — give the subscriber something real to click to.
- **Clean render** — no broken images, no overlapping text, good visual hierarchy from hero badge down through product grid.
- **45-character subject** stays mobile-friendly and communicates the offer without truncation.

## What's weak

- **Third near-identical email in two days** — same hero badge, same tab nav, same "SHOP NOW" CTA — this is cadence self-destruction.
- **Zero urgency mechanism** — "best deals just dropped" implies freshness but there's no deadline, no "ends tomorrow," nothing to act on now vs. later.
- **Generic CTA copy throughout** — every button is "SHOP NOW" regardless of category or product; no contextual pull.
- **No ScoreCard or loyalty angle** — the May 25 app-exclusive $20-off email (8/10) showed what targeted loyalty looks like; this email ignores it entirely.
- **Parenthetical subject structure** — "Our best deals just dropped! (Up to 50% off!)" buries the discount in a parenthetical rather than leading with it.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill the cadence** — don't send a third "50% off" email until either the offer changes or creative is completely refreshed. Deal fatigue is real and measurable in unsubscribe spikes.
- 2. **Add a hard deadline** — "Ends Sunday at midnight" or "2 days left" converts a browse into a session.
- 3. **Lead with one hero product** — pick the HOKA Clifton or a single breakout SKU with a specific discount, not a blanket "up to 50%."
- 4. **Rewrite CTA copy** — "Shop HOKA Deals," "Find Your Size," or "Shop $50 Picks" instead of repetitive SHOP NOW.
- 5. **Surface the ScoreCard** — even a one-line member callout ("ScoreCard members, stack your points") would differentiate this from the last two sends.
- **Subject Alt A:** `HOKA, Nike & more — now under $100`
- **Subject Alt B:** `50% off ends Sunday — HOKA, New Balance, Nike`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `HOKA Clifton, New Balance 530, DSG shorts — all under $100 while stock lasts.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your price-point picks: shoes under $100, clothing under $50 — sale ends Sunday.`
