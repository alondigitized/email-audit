# This is your LAST CHANCE! Shop EPIC savings before DICK'S Deal Days disappear
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-26**
## Executive summary

- This is a campaign-close send from DICK'S on June 26 — but the brand history already logged a separate June 26 "HURRY 🏃‍♀️ DICK'S Deal Days end TODAY!" that scored 9/10. Sending two "last chance" emails on the same calendar day is the single biggest liability here: any subscriber who opened the first one knows the urgency is manufactured, and anyone who didn't open it won't be more convinced by a second try. The subject doubles down on caps-lock urgency ("LAST CHANCE," "EPIC") that borders on spam-folder cadence.
- That said, the email body is the strongest creative execution in this campaign. The live countdown timer ("Final Hours: 07 | 59 | 48") is the most credible urgency signal DICK'S has used across the entire Deal Days run. The five named product deals (Nike Club Fleece, basketball shoes, drinkware, footballs, Stagg bags) are specific and scannable, and the Extra 30% Off Clearance banner at the bottom is a genuine new hook — the one piece of information that wasn't in the prior campaign-close send.
- The email deserves more credit than the subject line earns it. The body is well-executed; the inbox presentation is fatigued.

## What's working

- **Live countdown timer** ("Final Hours: 07 | 59 | 48") is the single most credible urgency signal in the entire Deal Days campaign — more persuasive than any subject line word.
- **Five distinct named deals** (Nike Club Fleece, basketball shoes, drinkware, footballs, Stagg bags) with specific percentages give subscribers something to scan for before clicking.
- **Extra 30% Off Select Clearance** is a new, differentiated offer not prominently in the prior campaign-close send — it gives a reason to act even for subscribers who ignored the earlier "TODAY" send.
- **Category navigation bar** (Men's / Women's / Kids / Shoes / The Running Shop) lets different audience segments self-direct immediately without hunting.

## What's weak

- **Two campaign-close emails on the same day** (this one and the earlier "HURRY...end TODAY!" 9/10 send) kills urgency credibility — "LAST CHANCE" means nothing if it's the second last chance of the afternoon.
- **Subject is long and all-caps-heavy** ("LAST CHANCE!" + "EPIC" + all-caps brand name) — 76 characters, won't render fully on mobile, and reads like spam-folder bait.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA copy is generic across every product block** — no block-level specificity ("Shop Nike Fleece," "Shop Clearance") to match the specific deals.
- **No social proof or reviews** visible anywhere in the render — for a clearance push specifically, star ratings or "X sold" signals would help justify action.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Collapse to one campaign-close send per day, not two** — or if you must send two, make this the only June 26 send and retire the "end TODAY" email. Duplicating urgency on the same day is a credibility burn.
- 2. **Lead the subject with the specific new hook** — the Extra 30% Off Clearance is genuinely new information; bury it in the body and it's invisible in the inbox.
- 3. **Make CTA copy block-specific** — "Shop Nike Fleece," "Shop Clearance," etc. instead of five identical "SHOP NOW" buttons.
- 4. **Trim and de-caps the subject** — "Final hours: extra 30% off clearance + Deal Days end today" is shorter, spam-cleaner, and surfaces the new offer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Final hours: extra 30% off clearance + Deal Days ends today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Deal Days close tonight — plus 30% off clearance just added`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Countdown timer is live. Nike, shoes, drinkware — all on sale until midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New clearance drop: 30% off + Deal Days finale savings across Nike, YETI & more.`

## Technical Audit — DICK'S Deal Days "Last Chance" Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is built on Epsilon Harmony's ESP infrastructure and follows standard email-safe table layout. Three confirmed code-level defects exist: invalid JSON in the structured data block, stale placeholder values from a 2020-era template, and an unfilled designer instruction left in production output.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Open-tracking pixel — `id="ssl"` naming mismatch**
The tracking beacon `td` is labeled `id="ssl"` with the comment `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->`, suggesting the Send-time Loyalty (Epsilon "SSL") pixel was never injected into this slot. The only image present is the Epsilon open-pixel at `app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/v6100000…`, which is a different signal than SSL. If loyalty send-time logic is required, this slot is empty.

```html
<td id="ssl" ...>
  <img src='https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/v610000019f05b35486c2323d369ff1d668/...'/>
  <!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->
```

**CTA redirect chain** — All links route through `app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/…?__dU__=<encoded>`. The destination URLs are not visible in the truncated source; UTM passthrough cannot be confirmed (see §6).

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**No confirmed issues** from the visible source:
- `<html lang="en">` present.
- All tables carry `role="presentation"`.
- Google Fonts scoped inside `@media screen` with Arial fallback — correct Outlook pattern.
- Mobile breakpoint at `max-width:480px` with `!important` overrides — standard for hybrid rendering.
- Logo `img` alt text is truncated in the provided source; cannot confirm.

**Font version staleness (low severity):** Both `@font-face` blocks reference `archivo/v7` from `fonts.gstatic.com`. The current Archivo family is at a later version; while functionally harmless (Google Fonts serves the file regardless), it indicates these URLs have not been refreshed since at least 2021.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Stale template date in JSON-LD**
`availabilityStarts` is hardcoded to `2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00` — a 6-year-old placeholder that was never replaced with the actual campaign start date.

```json
"availabilityStarts": "2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00",
"availabilityEnds": ""
```

**Unfilled `DiscountOffer` fields**
`description`, `discountCode`, and `availabilityEnds` are all empty strings. These are schema.org fields Google uses for Gmail Promotions Tab annotations. Empty values mean no promo card surfaces.

**Unfilled `PromotionCard` image**
The `image` field is blank, and a designer-facing instruction was not removed before send:
```json
// DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!
"image": ""
```
This comment is also syntactically illegal in JSON (see §5).

---

### 5. Compliance

**Invalid JSON-LD — JavaScript comments in a `<script type="application/ld+json">` block**
JSON does not allow `//` comments. Three such comments are present in the structured data block:
```
// GIF & WEBP images are not supported and will be filtered out.
// Sample image is 538x138, 3.9 aspect ratio
// DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!
```
Most parsers (including Gmail) silently discard the block on parse failure, meaning all three schema.org annotations are likely non-functional. This is the root cause of the empty promo card.

**CAN-SPAM / unsubscribe** — Not verifiable from the truncated source. Physical mailing address and one-click unsubscribe link must be present in the footer; the visible HTML ends before that section.

**Sending domain** — From header uses `DSG@e.dcsg.com`. SPF/DKIM/DMARC authentication on `e.dcsg.com` (the Epsilon sending subdomain) cannot be confirmed from HTML alone and requires header inspection of a delivered copy.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All clickable links use Epsilon's redirect wrapper (`/T/v6100000…?__dU__=<base64>`). The decoded destination and any embedded UTM parameters are not visible in the provided source. Specifically:

- Cannot confirm `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` are present on landing page URLs.
- Cannot confirm the landing page state (Deal Days sale page) matches the "last chance" urgency claim — requires live URL decode and destination check.

**Action required:** Decode at least one `__dU__` value and verify UTM parameters reach `dickssportinggoods.com`.
