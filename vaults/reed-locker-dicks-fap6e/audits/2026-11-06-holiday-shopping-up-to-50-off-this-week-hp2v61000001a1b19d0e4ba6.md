# Holiday shopping 🤝 up to 50% off this week ➡️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- This is another DICK'S "up to 50% off" catch-all, and it is the weakest of the week. The hero is clean: a green argyle panel with a giant gold "UP TO 50% OFF" and a single red Shop Now button. It lands the offer instantly. Below it the email turns into a long scroll of category tiles, each at "up to 40%", with nothing that makes you choose one over another.
- The bigger problem is cadence and sameness. The subject "Holiday shopping 🤝 up to 50% off this week ➡️" is the fifth near-identical up-to-50% send since 11/3. It follows "Great deal: Up to 50% off select clothing & shoes 📥" and "Are you ready for up to 50% off HOT holiday deals?" on 11/5, and "Check off their wish list with up to 50% off…" on 11/4. Earlier today's "EXTRA 25% off select clearance" at least had a different hook. A subscriber has seen this hero three times this week, so it reads as wallpaper.
- Judgement: competent execution, little reason to act now. The "this week" framing is soft, and the deadline in the body is "valid for a limited time". Several categories get equal weight and no product is the hero. Fine as a reminder send, but it adds to fatigue.

## What's working

- The hero is unmissable: huge gold "UP TO 50% OFF" on deep green, with the Shop Now button right beneath it.
- Pill links under "Explore more deals & essentials" (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Holiday Shop) give fast navigation.
- The holiday styling on the tiles (garland, ornaments, red and green) fits the season.
- The "On Their List. In Your Budget." gift section has price-tier pills (Gifts Under $25, $50, $100) that are useful for a shopper.
- Order Today / Sport It Tomorrow with one-hour store pickup and same-day delivery is a real benefit, and the ScoreCard points bar at the top shows loyalty status.

## What's weak

- The category tiles are generic and repetitive. Men's, Women's and Kids' are all "Up to 40% Off Select Clothing & Shoes" with identical layout and a button that just says "Shop Now".
- Every CTA is "Shop Now", so none of them says what you're shopping for.
- The urgency is vague. "Valid online only for a limited time" is the only deadline, and nothing names an end date.
- The email is very long, at roughly six screens of tiles before the gift picks. The gift-pick products (Stanley, Callaway advent calendar, Bucketstreet hoodie, squishmallow) have no prices or deal tags, so they are no help to someone comparing.
- The headline offer is "up to 50%", but the tiles show "up to 40%", and only Fitness and Fan Shop reach 50%. That mismatch feels like a bait-and-switch.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with one hero product or category instead of a blanket discount.** The 50% claim only applies to a few categories, so name them in the hero.
- 2. **Add a real deadline.** Replace "this week" and "limited time" with "Ends Sunday 11/9".
- 3. **Make the CTAs specific.** Use "Shop Men's Deals", "Shop Fan Gear" and "Shop Fitness Gear" instead of repeated "Shop Now".
- 4. **Put prices on the gift picks.** Show sale price and original price, or "Under $25".
- 5. **Cut the send frequency, or cut the body.** After five similar sends in four days, trim to three tiles.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Fan gear & fitness: up to 50% off through Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Gifts under $25 + up to 50% off, ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus free same-day pickup on gifts for everyone on your list.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fan shop, fitness and kids' picks, all marked down this week.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is structurally sound (`lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` layout tables, mobile media queries). Its main defects are an invalid JSON-LD block that still holds template placeholders, missing `alt` attributes on 13 images, and a malformed MSO conditional style rule. Compliance and authentication headers could not be verified from the captured data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed link defects.** 12 material links were probed, and the QA run reports 0 issues. 55 lower-priority links (footer, utility, social) were skipped, so they are unverified.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/v61000001a1b19d0e4ba6ac19369ff04b84/c8560354adcb494800004c5a42963aa1` is hidden with `display:none; max-height:0; mso-hide:all`. That's the right pattern, but the `<img>` has no `alt`. Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Leftover template comment:** `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` remains in production HTML.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on 13 images.**
  - Six Scene7 category/gift-guide images: `mens-2026-1106-swd-11am`, `womens-…`, `kids-…`, `fanshop-…`, `fitness-…` and `gift-guide-2026-1101-d`. If these are wrapped in `<a>`, the links have no accessible name. With images blocked, the primary navigation is blank.
  - Five footer icons on `dsg.images.harmony.epsilon.com`: `icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png` and `icon-location-2025.png`.
  - The tracking pixel (above). This accounts for the 57% pass rate.
- **Malformed MSO CSS.** The Outlook conditional block ends `div {…; padding:0;"}`. The stray `"` is a syntax error, so Outlook may drop or misparse the rule. The same rule sets `font-size:0` on every `div`, which can collapse div text in Outlook.
- **Tiny mobile type.** At ≤480px, `.body-sm` and `.link-sm` are 9px, `.legal` is 8px and `.body-m` is 11px. These are below common legibility minimums, and iOS may auto-enlarge them unpredictably.
- **Dev-path assets.** Footer icons load from `/_phoenix/_dev/AEM/img/footers/`. A `_dev` path on a third-party host is fragile.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved merge tokens appear, but the HTML is truncated, so the body and footer are unchecked.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool itself says these headers may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. Treat this as **unverified, not confirmed missing**. Check the raw message headers. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, and the cause is again probably the relay. From address `DSG@e.dcsg.com` is a subdomain of `dcsg.com`, so alignment should be confirmed.
- The unsubscribe link and physical address are not visible in the truncated source. The QA run reports no failure on the probed unsubscribe link.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **No confirmed issues.** CTA hrefs and UTM parameters are not in the truncated source, so UTM consistency and landing-page alignment can't be assessed.
- **Stale JSON-LD:** `availabilityStarts` is `2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00`, over six years old.
- The Scene7 image URLs end in an empty `cb=` parameter in the QA output, which may just be truncation. Confirm it's populated.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the JSON-LD block.** It contains `//` comments, which make it invalid JSON, so Gmail will ignore it. It also contains empty `description`, `discountCode`, `availabilityEnds` and `image` values, plus the placeholder text "DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!". Either populate it with valid data (the `PromotionCard` image must not be GIF or WebP) or remove it.
2. **Add descriptive `alt` text** to the six category images, since they likely act as links. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixel and decorative footer icons.
3. **Fix the MSO style rule:** remove the stray `"` and drop `font-size:0` from the global `div` selector.
4. **Raise mobile minimums** to about 11–12px for `.legal`, `.body-sm` and `.link-sm`.
5. **Verify the headers** (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC) against the raw message at the receiving mailbox, not through the relay.
6. **Clean up template residue:** the `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` and `<!--/GMAIL PREVIEW CODE-->` comments.
7. **Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML** to cover UTMs, merge tokens, the footer address and the skipped links.
