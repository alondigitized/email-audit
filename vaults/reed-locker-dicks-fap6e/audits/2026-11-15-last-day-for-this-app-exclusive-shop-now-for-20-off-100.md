# LAST DAY for this app exclusive! Shop now for $20 off $100+
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- This is the third or fourth "$20 off $100+ in the app" send from DICK'S in about 36 hours. It follows 11/14's "App exclusive starts now!", "📢 Don't miss this app exclusive" and "Lucky you! 💰 ... ends tomorrow". The "LAST DAY" framing is credible, since the fine print says the offer runs through 11/15 at 11:59 PM PT. But a subscriber who has seen the same offer three times already will read it as noise.
- The render has a weak hero. The first screen is two dark phone mockups with "APP EXCLUSIVE" and a ScoreCard+ logo, and the dollar offer isn't in the image. The "$20 Off $100 or More | ScoreCard+ $30 Off $100 or More***" headline only appears below it as small body text. A red "Get Offer" button follows, with a secondary "Join ScoreCard+" button.
- Below that, the email runs into a long catalog: shoes, Nike hoodies, drinkware, golf, ping-pong tables, racquet gear, clearance and a gift grid. It dilutes the one-day app message. The email is clean and has no visible render bugs. As a "last chance" nudge it is competent but bloated, and the creative is stale.

## What's working

- The subject's "LAST DAY" matches the 11/15 11:59 PM PT deadline in the fine print, so the urgency is real.
- The offer is concrete: $20 off $100+, or $30 off with ScoreCard+. That gives a loyalty-tier upsell.
- The red "Get Offer" button stands out against the white background and is clearly the primary action.
- The "Shop Now" buttons are consistent, and the modules are seasonal (holiday backdrops, gifting).
- The email renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The hero is a phone mockup with no dollar amount. The offer sits in small text under it, so the eye lands on decoration first.
- The email repeats the same $20-off-$100 app offer as three sends in the previous 36 hours, with no new creative or escalation.
- The catalog below the hero (shoes, hoodies, drinkware, golf, tables, racquets, 70% clearance) competes with the single-day app offer.
- The offer is buried in fine print. It needs the app, has no code, has exclusions, and carries "***" footnotes. That undercuts how honest it feels.
- The subject is about 59 characters, so it will likely truncate on mobile. "LAST DAY" in caps plus "!" is mildly shouty.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rebuild the hero around the number. Use a giant "$20 OFF $100+" with a "Ends tonight 11:59 PM PT" line and an "Open the App" button. Drop the phone mockup or shrink it.
- 2. Cut the body to one or two modules, or none, for a final-day reminder. The full catalog belongs in the first send of the sequence, not the fourth.
- 3. Suppress or consolidate the sequence. Send a "last day" email only to people who haven't opened or redeemed.
- 4. Move the key terms ("app only, no code needed, through 11:59 PM PT") up next to the headline, and leave the legal detail in the footer.
- 5. Add a deep link or app-store badge near the top, since the whole offer depends on an app action.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: $20 off $100+ in the app`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call, $20 off $100+ (app only)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Tap to activate in My Account. $30 off with ScoreCard+. Gone at 11:59 PM PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `No code needed. Open the app and it applies at checkout.`
