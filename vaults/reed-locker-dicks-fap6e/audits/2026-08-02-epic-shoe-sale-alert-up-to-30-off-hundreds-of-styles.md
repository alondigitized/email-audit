# Epic Shoe Sale Alert! 🚨 Up to 30% off hundreds of styles
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-02**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S finally breaking format after a week of interchangeable red-and-green "CLEARANCE" / "FLASH SALE" blasts (7/30–8/2). "Epic Shoe Sale" swaps the kitchen-sink category cascade for a single-category hero — Nike, adidas, and HOKA logos stacked over a pile of sneakers in the same red locker-room set piece DICK'S has been reusing, but this time the copy and product grid actually stay on-topic (shoes, cleats, kids' Air Force 1s, Jordan cleats, Crocs) instead of sprawling into golf clubs and kayaks halfway through.
- The offer itself is soft — "up to 30% off," "select styles," no specific brand/style called out — and it's the same vague qualifier language DICK'S leans on constantly. But the execution is clean: no render bugs, a legible CTA, ScoreCard points visible up top, and a "Must-have gear for summer" module that's genuinely coherent with back-to-school timing.
- Net: a better-built send than the last five DICK'S clearance emails, but the subject line's siren-emoji urgency isn't backed by any real deadline in the body (sale runs two more weeks), which is the classic move that erodes trust over repeated opens.

## What's working

- Single-category focus (shoes/cleats) is a real break from the sprawling "everything's on sale" template DICK'S has run all week.
- Recognizable footwear brands (Nike, adidas, HOKA) are called out by name and logo in the hero, which builds credibility faster than a generic sale banner.
- ScoreCard loyalty points ("300 Points & $10 Reward") are visible immediately below the logo — good use of prime real estate for members.
- Clean render throughout — no overlapping text, no broken images, consistent DICK'S green/white brand system.
- Fine print (valid through 8/15/26, select styles) is present and legible rather than buried in tiny gray text.

## What's weak

- Subject's siren emoji + "Alert!" implies urgency the body doesn't deliver — the sale runs another two weeks, so the framing oversells the clock.
- Primary CTA is a generic "SHOP NOW" repeated twice — neither button says "Shop Shoes" or names a brand, wasting the category focus the hero just established.
- Hero links to a category, not a hero product — no specific shoe, price, or size info anywhere in the email despite this being a shoe-specific send.
- No reviews, ratings, or social proof anywhere in the body.
- After the shoe hero, the email still drifts into golf, kayaks, drinkware, and fan shop — undercutting the "epic shoe sale" premise it opened with.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the CTA category-specific: "Shop Shoes" or "Shop the Sale" instead of generic "SHOP NOW" — ties directly to the hero promise.
- 2. Cut or shorten the urgency framing in the subject to match the real deadline, or add a countdown ("2 weeks left") if urgency is the hook.
- 3. Feature one hero shoe (price, brand, maybe a review star rating) directly under the "Up to 30% Off" headline instead of jumping straight to nav buttons.
- 4. Trim the back-half category sprawl (golf, kayak, drinkware) or move it to a clearly separate "more deals" section further down — protect the shoe focus longer.
- 5. Tighten the subject under 50 characters for mobile truncation.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your sneaker upgrade is up to 30% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Hundreds of styles, one sale — ends 8/15.`

## Technical Audit: DICK'S Sporting Goods — "Epic Shoe Sale Alert!"

### 1. Technical Summary
The email renders via a standard Epsilon/Harmony ESP template with responsive mobile CSS, but ships a broken unsubscribe link, invalid JSON-LD markup, and 12 images with no alt text; email-authentication headers could not be verified via the current relay.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken unsubscribe link (FAIL):** The "Unsubscribe" CTA (`https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/U/v610000019fc4190fab8e5c13969125ccc4/9502cf2642aa49b80000021ef3a0bcff`) resolves to `/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription` — an ASP.NET error page instead of the preference/unsubscribe center. This is a hard compliance-adjacent bug (see §5).
- All probed CTAs (header logo, body links) route through the Epsilon Harmony click-tracking redirector `app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...` with opaque session tokens (`v610000019fc4190fab8e5c13969125ccc4/...?__dU__=...`) rather than direct merchant URLs — expected for this ESP, not a defect.
- Open-tracking pixel present and correctly hidden (`display:none; height:0`) at `app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/...`.
- QA probed 12 of 62 total links (material/high-click-likelihood links); 50 footer/utility/social links were deprioritized and not individually verified — link-health coverage is partial.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **12 images missing `alt` text**, including the SSL/open-pixel image and 11 content images (`clothes.jpg`, `golf.jpg`, `outdoor.jpg`, `team-sports.jpg`, `drinkware.jpg`, `lic.jpg`, `fitness.jpg`, and 4 footer trust/social icons). This breaks screen-reader accessibility and degrades rendering in image-blocked clients (Outlook default).
- **Malformed CSS in the MSO/IE conditional block:** `div {background-color:inherit; font-size:0; line-height:100%; margin:0; padding:0;"}</style>` contains a stray `"` before the closing `}`, which will break parsing of this rule in Outlook (mso 9+/IE) rendering.
- **Invalid JSON-LD (`application/ld+json`):** The structured-data block contains inline `//` JavaScript-style comments (e.g., `// GIF & WEBP images are not supported...`, `// DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!`), which are not valid JSON syntax. Any strict JSON parser (including Gmail's promotional-markup ingestion, which this block is formatted for) will fail to parse it. Additionally, the `PromotionCard.image`, `DiscountOffer.description`, and `DiscountOffer.discountCode` fields are empty strings — template placeholders that were not filled in before send.
- Mobile breakpoint CSS (`@media screen and (max-width:480px)`) is present and well-formed, with appropriate `.mobile-off`/`.mobile-w100` fallback classes.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tokens, personalization variables, or dynamic-content placeholders (e.g., `{{firstName}}`, AMPscript, Handlebars) were observed in the available HTML. Cannot confirm behavior of any personalization logic beyond the visible/truncated portion of the source.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe link is broken** (see §2) — lands on an error page rather than confirming opt-out. This is a CAN-SPAM risk if the underlying subscription-management endpoint is actually down, not just an artifact of the tracking-domain redirect.
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found (WARN).** Note: this may be a relay artifact — the QA tooling flags that AgentMail may not surface this header even if DSG's ESP sends it — so this should be verified against the raw SMTP headers before treating as a confirmed gap.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found (WARN)** — if genuinely absent, RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is not supported, which is increasingly required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender rules.
- **Authentication-Results header not found (WARN)** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be confirmed from this capture; same AgentMail-relay caveat applies.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Cannot be assessed from the available data: all CTA links resolve to the Epsilon Harmony click-tracking redirector (`app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...`) rather than direct dickssportinggoods.com URLs, and the truncated HTML does not expose final landing-page destinations or UTM parameters. No landing-page URL or UTM string was present anywhere in the visible source to audit for alignment.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the unsubscribe endpoint — `/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription` indicates the subscription-center route is broken server-side; this should be treated as high priority given compliance exposure.
2. Remove `//` comments from the `application/ld+json` block and populate the empty `image`, `description`, and `discountCode` fields (or remove the incomplete `PromotionCard` object) so Gmail's structured-data parser doesn't silently fail.
3. Add `alt` text to all 11 content/footer images for accessibility and image-blocked rendering.
4. Fix the stray `"` in the MSO conditional-comment CSS block (`padding:0;"}`).
5. Verify List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post / Authentication-Results directly against raw SMTP headers (outside the AgentMail relay) to confirm whether these are real gaps or relay-capture artifacts.
6. Re-run link QA against the 50 deprioritized/skipped links to confirm the unsubscribe failure isn't isolated (e.g., check other `/pc/` subscription-center routes).
