# 📧 INSIDE: Up to 50% off this week's deals
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- This is the "Deal Drop" from 9/27 again with a different subject line. The hero is the same golden-hour prairie photo with a huge white "50% OFF" and a lone "Shop Now" button. It is clean and easy to read, and there are no render bugs. But it's the third or fourth pass at "up to 50% off / select clearance" in four days, right after the 9/26 "EXTRA 25% off" red hero and the 9/29 "$25 & under" send. Nothing here is new.
- The body is a long, uniform stack of category tiles, each labelled "Up to 50% Off Select…" with a grey "Shop Now" button. That covers everyone and excites no one. The only distinct moment is the Stanley Día de Muertos tumbler, which is the one real hook in the email and sits mid-scroll. The ScoreCard banner at the top is tiny, and the "Order Today, Sport It Tomorrow" line is easy to miss.
- Verdict: competent and on-brand, but it's inbox wallpaper. It will get opens on brand recognition alone and only shallow clicks.

## What's working

- The hero is instantly legible: a huge "50% OFF" on a rich photo, with the eye landing on the offer first.
- The category pill row (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Team Sports) gives fast self-selection.
- Content is seasonal: NFL, NHL and NBA gear, fall golf, and the Día de Muertos Stanley tumbler.
- The layout is clean and consistent, with no broken images or overlapping text.
- The "Check out these picks" product row (adidas Samba, Hoka-style runners, Nike joggers) gives some product-level specificity near the end.

## What's weak

- The offer is a vague "up to 50% off select." It doesn't say which items or what the typical discount is.
- Every tile uses the same copy and the same grey "Shop Now" button, so nothing feels prioritised.
- It is a repeat of the 9/27 "Deal drop! See what's up to 50% off this week" creative, and the cadence is heavy.
- The ScoreCard/points banner is tiny and gets no member-specific value, even though the 9/28 sends leaned on 2X points.
- The urgency is weak: "Valid online only for a limited time" is buried below the hero and has no date.
- The email is very long, with seven category blocks before any specific products appear.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one real hero product or price point instead of another "up to" umbrella. Something like "Hoka Clifton from $89" in the hero gives the email a reason to exist next to the 9/27 send.
- 2. Add a real deadline in the hero, such as "Ends Sunday," and surface it above the fold.
- 3. Vary the CTAs with verb-plus-noun copy: "Shop men's fleece," "Shop Hoka running shoes."
- 4. Cut the body from seven category tiles to three or four, and move the Stanley drop and the product picks up.
- 5. Give ScoreCard members a visible perk, like "Members: +2X points on deals," instead of the tiny header line.
- 6. Vary the creative week to week so the 50% hero doesn't read as a repeat.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 50% off Hoka, Nike & adidas — through Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `This week only: 50% off fleece, shoes & fan gear`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus a new Stanley drop and ScoreCard members earn 2X points.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Running shoes, fleece and fan gear, marked down through Sunday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a well-structured, table-based template with `lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` layout tables, a viewport meta tag, MSO conditional comments and a hidden preheader. The automated QA flags a near-empty plain-text part (9 chars), 13 images without `alt` attributes, and unverifiable unsubscribe and authentication headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links reported.** QA probed 12 material links and skipped 53 (footer, social and utility links were deprioritized). The 53 skipped links are unvalidated, so coverage is partial.
- **Links are wrapped in an opaque click tracker.** For example, the header link goes to `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/mclick?params=b.v1...&pid=v1...&tuid=...`. The final destinations can't be read from the source.
- **The open pixel is `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1...`.** It has no `alt` attribute (see section 3).
- **Template placeholder left in production.** `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` sits in `td#ssl`. It is harmless to rendering but shows an unfinished template step.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **13 images are missing `alt` attributes** (QA WARN). The QA data doesn't say whether the attribute is absent or empty, so I'm treating it as absent:
  - Content and promo images:
    - `2026-0929-dks-swd-4pm-mens`
    - `-womens`
    - `-kids-new`
    - `-jersey`
    - `-fitness-new`
    - `-golf-new`
    - `muertos-2026-0928-d`
  - Value-prop icons on `s7d6.scene7.com/is/image/DSGAEMSites/`:
    - `icon-vc-sms-1`
    - `icon-vc-sportsmatter`
    - `icon-vc-bpg-1`
    - `icon-vc-favorite-1`
    - `icon-location-2025-1`
  - The tracking pixel `open`.
- **Effect of the missing alt text on the promo images.** With images blocked, they render as blank space or as the filename. If these are the CTA tiles, the links become unlabeled for screen readers, which may announce the raw Scene7 URL.
- **Decorative images and the pixel need `alt=""`.** The icons and `/ee/v1/open` should carry an explicit empty `alt`.
- **Plain-text part is effectively absent.** QA FAIL: the text version is 9 chars. That leaves no fallback for text-only clients and hurts accessibility. Spam filters also weigh a missing or empty text/plain part negatively.
- **Large image request.** `muertos-2026-0928-d?fmt=jpeg&qlt=100&wid=1200&hei=1600` requests a 1200×1600 image at quality 100 inside a 584px container. This is likely heavier than needed.
- **Fonts and Outlook.** The Archivo `@font-face` is inside `@media screen`, with an Arial fallback. The Outlook `mso` conditionals and the 584px ghost table look correct. No issues found.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found (WARN).**
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found (WARN).** The check says one-click unsubscribe may not be supported.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found (WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- **These header findings are unconfirmed.** QA itself attributes them to the AgentMail relay stripping headers. Don't treat them as sender defects without checking the raw message. The From domain `em.dcsg.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is consistent with normal authenticated ESP setups.
- **Footer unsubscribe and physical address.** QA states an unsubscribe link is always probed, and it reported no failure. The footer is past the truncation point, so I can't confirm the postal address or the link's destination from the source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues confirmed. Every link is behind the `data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/mclick` redirect, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be verified from the HTML. The QA data reports no redirect failures among the 12 probed links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a real plain-text alternative.** The current 9-char part is the only QA FAIL. Generate a multipart/alternative text/plain version with the offers and links.
2. **Add `alt` text to the six promo images.** The `swd-4pm-*` tiles and `muertos-2026-0928-d` should carry alt text that reflects their link purpose. Set `alt=""` on the five `icon-*` images and the `/ee/v1/open` pixel.
3. **Check the raw message headers.** Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass on the original send, bypassing the relay. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
4. **Resize the `muertos-2026-0928-d` image.** Use roughly 2× the rendered width (about 1168px) and a lower `qlt` value.
5. **Remove the `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` comment** from the production template.
6. **Widen link-probe coverage.** Include the 53 skipped links, or at least the footer unsubscribe and preference-center targets, in a periodic check.
