# Milwaukee fans! 📣 Rep the WIN 🎉⚾
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-29**
## Executive summary

- This is a well-timed, well-built fan-merch send. The "REP THE WIN" hero shows a World Series Champions tee, "Congratulations, Milwaukee!" sits right under it, and a green Shop Now button follows. Brewers branding repeats in every module, so the geo-targeting is obvious. The subject's "Milwaukee fans!" is the segmentation signal that sets this apart from DICK'S usual discount blasts.
- The email has no price, percentage or promo code anywhere I can see. After four days of "up to 75% off" (10/24–10/28), this is a pure event-driven send. That suits the moment, but it gives a price-driven reader no reason to act now. The six module blocks below the hero (hats, outerwear, clothing, essentials) are all the same layout with a grey Shop Now button. That is repetitive, and the grey buttons look disabled next to the green hero CTA.
- The late "Limited-Edition 44 Pro 12" Jacob Misiorowski glove" draw, closing 10/28 at 3PM ET, is the only real urgency. It's buried near the bottom of a very long scroll. Overall this is a strong, relevant send that undersells its best hook.

## What's working

- The hero is clear: a champions tee, "Congratulations, Milwaukee!", and a green Shop Now button.
- "Milwaukee fans!" in the subject and the Brewers imagery throughout show real local segmentation.
- The timing is credible, right after the World Series win and with the parade in view ("Gear Up Like the Pros for the Parade").
- The product modules are clean and easy to scan: clothing, outerwear, hats and essentials each get their own tile.
- The "ORDER TODAY. SPORT IT TOMORROW." banner with one-hour pickup and same-day delivery gives a concrete fulfillment benefit. It is also the only conversion-friendly hook in the email.

## What's weak

- No price, discount or member offer appears anywhere. Nothing reduces cost, and there is no visible ScoreCard bonus beyond the small header strip.
- Five modules use identical layouts and low-contrast grey Shop Now buttons, so the middle of the email blurs together.
- The glove draw, which is the only scarcity hook, sits about 70% of the way down, and its deadline (today, 3PM ET) comes too late to matter for most readers.
- The CTA copy is generic ("Shop Now" everywhere), with no product-specific verb and noun.
- The email is very long, and the legal and footer text is dense.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the glove draw, or a "Draw closes today 3PM ET" banner, up next to the hero. It is the only urgency and scarcity element, and it is currently buried.
- 2. Add one concrete incentive to the hero, such as ScoreCard bonus points or free same-day pickup on champion gear, so the message is more than a congratulation.
- 3. Give the module CTAs real copy: "Shop Brewers Hats", "Shop Parade Outerwear". Make the buttons green to match the hero.
- 4. Cut the six modules to three or four, and lead with the champions tee and the hat, which are the most emotional, highest-intent items.
- 5. Add visible social proof or stock signals, such as "Selling fast" or "Most popular".

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Coverage gap:** 12 of 50 links were probed and 38 were skipped. The QA data gives no status codes, so I can't confirm or rule out broken links.
- **Hidden tracking pixel:** `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/v61000001a185d1fa1ebf492f6e96c65848/2491e460a76b4e3800004c5a42963aa1` uses `display:none; max-height:0; mso-hide:all`. Some clients and privacy proxies don't load `display:none` images, so open tracking may undercount. The pixel also has no `alt` attribute.
- **Dev path in production:** Footer icons load from `dsg.images.harmony.epsilon.com/_phoenix/_dev/AEM/img/footers/…`. A `_dev` path suggests a non-production asset path, which is a risk if that path is later cleaned up.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (12 images):**
  - 6 content images on `s7d6.scene7.com`: `2026-1027-lic-mlb-ws-if-win-brewers-1` through `-5-new`, plus `2026-1020-dks-baseball-misiorowski-44pro-bb-glove-res-banner`.
  - 5 footer icons: `icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png`, `icon-location-2025.png`.
  - The tracking pixel.
  - If the hero images carry the offer text and are linked, images-off clients and screen readers get no content or link name. The footer icons and pixel should at least have `alt=""`.
- **Invalid JSON-LD:** The `<script type="application/ld+json">` block contains `//` comments, which are not valid JSON, so Gmail will ignore the whole block. The template placeholder text is still in it:
  - `// DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!` (the PromotionCard `"image": ""` is empty).
  - `DiscountOffer` has `"description": ""`, `"discountCode": ""` and `"availabilityEnds": ""`.
  - `availabilityStarts` is `2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00`, a stale date.
- **Malformed MSO CSS:** `div {background-color:inherit; font-size:0; line-height:100%; margin:0; padding:0;"}` has a stray `"` before `}`. Outlook may drop that rule or the one after it. The same rule sets `font-size:0` on every `div`, which can hide div text in Outlook.
- **Preheader structure:** The visible preheader text "+ Score official World Series™ championship gear" sits directly in `td#ssl`. It is hidden only by that cell's `font-size:0`, not by its own hidden wrapper. Clients that ignore `font-size:0` could show it. The `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` template comment is also still in the source.
- **Image weight:** Hero images request `fmt=jpeg&qlt=100&wid=1200&hei=1600` for a 600px container. Quality 100 JPEGs at that size are heavy for mobile loads.
