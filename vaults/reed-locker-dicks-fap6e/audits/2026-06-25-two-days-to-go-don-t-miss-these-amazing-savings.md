# Two days to go! ✌️ Don't miss these amazing SAVINGS
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the second DICK'S email on June 25 — arriving the same day as the "Time's running out" urgency-close send that scored 9/10. The creative team has doubled down on countdown language ("Two days to go!") with the same Deal Days visual system — green-dominant, badge-heavy, multi-product grid — and the result is diminishing returns. The email is technically clean and the offer stack is real, but the subject line retreats from the strongest card played yesterday ("Don't miss BIG savings on top brands: VRST, CALIA, Blackstone, Quest & more!" at 9/10), which named specific brands and products in the subject line itself. "Amazing SAVINGS" is the kind of generic filler that erases differentiation mid-campaign.
- The product mix — football cleats, golf cleats, grills, drinkware, running shoes — is genuinely broad, and none of the tiles is given enough visual weight to feel like a hero moment. The running shoe (adidas Adizero EVO SL) is the one product a Walker-persona subscriber would click on, but it's buried fifth of six tiles with the same "TODAY ONLY!" treatment as Blackstone grills.
- At send number ten-plus across three days, the campaign needed to either name specific products in the subject line again or find a new angle. Instead it recycled the countdown formula with less-specific copy. The email will convert loyalists who haven't bought yet, but it's burning through list goodwill fast.

## What's working

- **Deal Days visual identity is tight and consistent** — green branding, bold badge treatment, and clear "SHOP NOW" buttons throughout require no learning curve from subscribers who've seen previous sends.
- **"TODAY ONLY!" badges create real urgency** — applied to multiple product tiles with visible specific products, not just a generic banner.
- **"Up to $80 Off" on CALIA is a concrete anchor** — dollar-amount framing is more compelling than percentage-off for apparel.
- **SHOP NOW buttons are consistently placed and high-contrast** — no ambiguity about the click target on any tile.
- **Countdown framing ("Two days to go!") is accurate and credible** — aligns with the campaign timeline and doesn't feel manufactured.

## What's weak

- **"Amazing SAVINGS" in the subject is a step backward** — the June 24 brand-naming send proved that naming VRST, CALIA, Blackstone, Quest in the subject outperforms generic urgency copy; this email abandons that learning.
- **Product grid is six-wide with no visual hierarchy** — football cleats, golf cleats, grills, drinkware, running shoes, and women's dress all receive identical badge-and-button treatment; nothing reads as the hero product.
- **Second email in one day, same format** — following the "Time's running out" send from the same date at 9/10, this one arrives with no new creative angle and near-identical countdown copy; subscriber fatigue risk is high.
- **No loyalty or member pricing signal** — ten emails in and members have received no differentiated benefit; the entire campaign has been one-size-fits-all.
- **Running shoe is buried fifth of six tiles** — the most Walker-relevant product gets the least prominent position.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put the running shoe or footwear deal in the subject line** — the June 24 brand-naming email proved this approach lifts performance; "Two days to go on adidas Running + 50+ more deals" names a product and a scale simultaneously.
- 2. **Create a hero tile, not six equal tiles** — pick one product category as the lead, give it full-width or 2x visual weight, then let the supporting tiles breathe beneath it; this is how you manufacture urgency within the email body.
- 3. **Add a member-exclusive signal** — even a one-line banner ("Members: extra 10% on Deal Days picks, login to see") gives loyal subscribers a reason this email is different from the public sale.
- 4. **Drop to one email per day for the final 48 hours** — two sends in one day on June 25 with nearly identical countdown copy compresses list tolerance right before the close; a single, well-crafted send would outperform two diluted ones.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last 2 days: adidas, CALIA & Blackstone deals end Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Deal Days closing: $80 off, shoes, grills, cleats — 48 hrs left`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop the biggest summer sale before it's gone — plus an extra member perk inside.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Running shoes, grills, apparel — all on sale. Two days only.`

## Technical Audit — DICK'S Deal Days Email

### 1. Technical Summary

Email is built on the Epsilon/Harmony ESP platform with standard MSO conditional comment scaffolding. Several structural defects exist in the JSON-LD block (invalid syntax, stale dates, unfilled placeholders) and a possible staging-path image URL is present in the truncated source.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **Tracking redirect domain**: All links route through `app.dickssportinggoods.com` (Epsilon redirect layer). Standard practice, but UTM passthrough cannot be confirmed from the truncated source — see §6.
- **Tracking pixel**: `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/v610000019eff670df5c1e478f4bbc786c8/…` is hidden with `display:none; max-height:0px` — correct implementation.
- **Logo link**: Routes through the same Epsilon redirect (`/T/v610000019eff670df5c1e478f4bbc786c8/…`). Confirm destination resolves to the correct homepage, not a campaign-specific landing page.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **Duplicate `font-family` declaration**: `body{font-family:Archivo,Arial,sans-serif; font-family:Archivo,Arial,sans-serif!important}` — redundant but harmless; clean up to reduce CSS noise.
- **Google Fonts CDN load**: Archivo v7 loaded from `fonts.gstatic.com`. Font version is pinned to v7; current release is higher. Functionally fine but will eventually drift from upstream metrics.
- **Image URL contains `_dev` path segment**: Logo src (truncated) reads `https://dsg.images.harmony.epsilon.com/_phoenix/_dev/AEM/img/headers/brand-logo--dk…`. The `/_dev/` path segment suggests a staging/development asset URL. Confirm the final send resolves to a production CDN path, not a dev environment that may be rate-limited or unavailable externally.
- **`role="presentation"`** on layout tables: present and correct throughout visible markup.
- **MSO conditional comments**: Properly structured for Outlook 2007–2019 compatibility.
- **`alt` text on logo**: Not visible in truncated source — must be confirmed present and non-empty (`alt="DICK'S Sporting Goods"` or equivalent). Missing alt on the primary logo is an accessibility defect.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**JSON-LD `<script type="application/ld+json">` block contains multiple unfilled placeholders and one syntax error:**

| Field | Value | Issue |
|---|---|---|
| `DiscountOffer.description` | `""` | Empty — not populated |
| `DiscountOffer.discountCode` | `""` | Empty — not populated |
| `DiscountOffer.availabilityStarts` | `"2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"` | **Stale hardcoded date from 2020** |
| `DiscountOffer.availabilityEnds` | `""` | Empty — not populated |
| `PromotionCard.image` | `""` | Empty — designer note still present as a code comment |

**Critical syntax defect — JavaScript comments inside JSON-LD:**

```json
{ // GIF & WEBP images are not supported and will be filtered out.
  // Sample image is 538x138, 3.9 aspect ratio
  "@context": "http://schema.org/",
  "@type": "PromotionCard",
  // DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!
  "image": ""
}
```

`//` comments are **invalid JSON**. Any structured-data parser (Gmail, Google Search) will reject the entire JSON-LD block. The designer instructions were left in the source and never removed. This kills Gmail's Gmail Promotions tab annotation and any Google rich-result eligibility.

---

### 5. Compliance

- **Physical address / CAN-SPAM footer**: Not visible in truncated source — must be confirmed present in the email footer. Required by CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5).
- **Unsubscribe mechanism**: Not visible in truncated source — must be confirmed present and functional. Required by CAN-SPAM §5(a)(3).
- **Sending domain authentication**: FROM header shows `DSG@e.dcsg.com`. `e.dcsg.com` is a subdomain used for ESP delivery. SPF, DKIM, and DMARC alignment on this subdomain should be verified — misalignment is the most common cause of inbox placement failures for subdomain-based senders.
- **`lang="en"`** on `<html>`: Present — correct.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

- All visible links pass through the Epsilon redirect layer. Whether UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content`) are appended after redirect cannot be determined from the truncated source.
- Confirm the Epsilon redirect is configured to append or preserve UTMs before the final destination URL, otherwise campaign attribution in GA4/Adobe will be lost and sessions will land as `(direct)`.
- The `availabilityStarts: 2020` date in JSON-LD (see §4) means any Gmail Promotions tab display of offer timing will show incorrect dates.
