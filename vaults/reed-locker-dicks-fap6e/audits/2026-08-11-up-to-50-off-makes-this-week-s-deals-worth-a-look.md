# *** Up to 50% off makes this week's deals worth a look *** 👀
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-11**
## Executive summary

- This is the DICK'S weekly-digest format again — beach ball pool imagery, "UP TO 50% OFF" hero, then a long stack of category modules (Clothing, Team Sports, Drinkware, Golf, Outdoor, Fan Shop, Fitness, and finally a shoppable picks grid). It's essentially the same shape as the 2026-08-10 "Shop up to 50% off today" send, right down to the pool-ball hero motif — this is the second time this week that exact creative has run, which is a real cadence problem for anyone who opened both.
- The hero itself is clean and above-the-fold on mobile (percentage callout, clear "SHOP NOW" button, category chip nav right underneath), so a first-time viewer gets a good first impression. But past that hero, this is undifferentiated sprawl: eight nearly identical "Up to X% Off Select ___" modules with generic lifestyle photography and stacked CTA buttons, no single hero product, no urgency, no loyalty messaging. It reads as a catalog dump rather than a curated pitch.
- Judgement: functional but forgettable, and repetitive against the brand's own recent history. Fine as a background/browse email, not a strong driver of incremental clicks for someone who's already seen the pool-ball 50%-off hero once this week.

## What's working

- Hero section renders cleanly with no layout bugs — bold "UP TO 50% OFF" badge is legible and lands the eye immediately.
- Category chip navigation (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Team Sports, Back-to-School) right under the hero gives quick wayfinding for multiple shopper intents.
- Consistent visual system throughout (photography style, green CTA buttons, section headers) keeps the long scroll coherent.
- Closing "picks" grid (Nike backpack, adidas Sambas, HOKA Clifton, etc.) gives concrete, specific products with visible imagery — the strongest click bait in the email.

## What's weak

- Same pool-ball "UP TO 50% OFF" hero as the 2026-08-10 "Shop up to 50% off today" send — near-duplicate creative twice in two days.
- No fine print or deadline shown anywhere — "Valid online only for a limited time" under "This Week's Deals" is vague and not time-bound, so there's no real urgency.
- Eight back-to-back "Up to X% Off Select ___" modules with near-identical layout create scroll fatigue; nothing signals which category is the actual priority.
- No loyalty/member pricing or points messaging visible despite ScoreCard branding in the header ("300 Points if Reward... Shop to Earn Points").
- Product imagery in most modules is generic lifestyle shots, not the actual on-sale products — only the final picks grid shows real, specific merchandise.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop reusing the same pool-ball 50%-off hero within a 24-48 hour window — swap creative or at least the background art so back-to-back opens don't feel like a repeat.
- 2. Move the closing "picks" grid (Sambas, HOKA, Nike backpack) up near the top — it's the most concrete, clickable content in the email and it's buried at the bottom.
- 3. Add a real deadline ("Ends Sunday 11:59pm ET") to replace the vague "valid for a limited time" line — gives Open/Click a credible urgency signal.
- 4. Trim the module count — 8 near-identical "Up to X% Off Select ___" sections is too many; pick 3-4 and let the rest live on a landing page.
- 5. Surface ScoreCard/loyalty points value in-line with at least one CTA (e.g., "Shop Now · Earn 2x Points") since the header teases rewards but never follows through.
