# 💸 Biggest Sale of the YEAR! 💸 Hundreds of deals are a click away​
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S Black Friday email again, now with a long scroll of category tiles. The hero is a dark, festive "BIGGEST SALE OF THE YEAR" graphic. Directly under it is one clear offer: "Black Friday: Up to 50% Off," with a "Shop Now" button. It is competent and on-brand, and the offer and CTA sit near the top.
- Below the hero, the email stops being one message and becomes a catalog. There are ten or more modules: HOKA, Nike fleece, Bombas slippers, trading cards, Callaway golf balls, bats, ping-pong tables, "Extra 25% off" clearance, store hours, and a gift-picks grid. Each has its own percentage and its own button. Nothing leads, and the "Extra 25% OFF" banner in red competes with the hero. It works as a utility email for someone already planning to shop. It is weak as a persuasive one.
- Against the recent history, this is the same template for roughly the twelfth time in a week. The earlier sends were "Dig into amazing Black Friday savings!" and "Happy Thanksgiving! Biggest Sale of the Year is served." The "Biggest Sale of the Year" language is a repeat of the 11/26 Thanksgiving subject. A subscriber has no reason to treat this one as new.

## What's working

- The hero is bold and seasonal, and "Up to 50% Off" with a "Shop Now" button sits directly below it.
- Category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Golf, Fan Shop) let the reader jump to their interest.
- Product modules carry concrete numbers, such as "20% Off HOKA Bondi 9," "2 for $100 Callaway," and "50% Off Monarch Navigator Table Tennis Tables."
- Badges like "Limited Time," "Top Gifts," and "Online only" add some urgency and clarity.
- The footer is complete, with store hours, app download, ScoreCard and price-match info.

## What's weak

- Too many offers: about ten modules, each with a different discount, so no single message lands.
- The "Extra 25% OFF" red banner pulls attention away from the "Up to 50% Off" hero.
- The reader has to scroll a long way to reach the gift-picks grid, which is the most shoppable part.
- Several modules show no price or brand, and some are niche (trading cards, ping-pong tables). The email doesn't speak to a single persona.
- The fine print is dense. There are exclusions at the top and a long block at the bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to a hero plus 3–4 modules, led by one offer. Move the gift-picks grid up, since it is the most scannable part.
- 2. Differentiate it from the last week of sends. Either name what's new today (final hours, doorbusters) or drop the "Biggest Sale of the Year" language that already ran on 11/26.
- 3. Make the "Extra 25% off" clearance a secondary strip rather than a full-bleed red banner.
- 4. Add prices and star ratings to the gift picks so they carry social proof.
- 5. Put the Black Friday delivery cutoff and the store hours in the header or the hero.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Black Friday ends tonight: up to 50% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `HOKA, Nike & Callaway deals: last call`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Extra 25% off clearance, plus gift picks under $50`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Open 6 AM at most stores. Free ship-to-store on every order`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a well-formed, table-based email with `lang`, `<title>`, `role="presentation"` tables and an Outlook MSO fallback. It fails on deliverability hygiene: the plain-text part is effectively empty, and 14 images are missing alt text. The truncated source and relay-limited headers leave unsubscribe headers, SPF/DKIM and UTM parameters unverified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracking pixel:** `<img src="https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1.…&pid=…&tuid=…&configId=…" width="1" height="1">`. It has no `alt`, which is a QA warning, and no `style` hiding it. It sits as a direct child of `<body>`, before the wrapper table. The URL is very long, at roughly 600+ characters.
- **Link wrapping:** Links use `class="arc-link" data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"`, which indicates ESP-side click wrapping. The destination URLs are cut off in the supplied source, so they can't be audited.
- **Probing coverage:** The QA probe covered 12 of 68 links (56 skipped). No broken-link results were reported for the 12 probed, but that is not full coverage.
- No other link issues are confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text fallback fails (QA `[FAIL]`):** The text part is 9 characters. Text-only clients and some spam filters see essentially no content.
- **Missing alt text (14 images):** All hosted on `s7d6.scene7.com/is/image/DSGAEMSites/`.
  - Content banners: `hokabondi91122footaccess`, `nike-6`, `2bombas`, `2026-1122-dks-stw-fanshop-cyber8-banner-tcgs`, `chrome-balls-2026-1122-dks-swd-golf-cyber-8-bids-banner`, `1122-stw-team-sports-cyber8-bbsb`, `table-tennis-1`, `1101storefront`.
  - Icons: `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`.
  - The pixel `open` image also lacks alt.
  - With images blocked, or for screen readers, the banner content is lost. For the icons, `alt=""` would be correct if they are decorative.
  - The QA can't distinguish a missing attribute from `alt=""`, so confirm in the full source.
- **Preheader appears empty:** `<td id="ssl">` contains the template comment `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->`, followed by `#pht-spacer`, which holds only `&zwnj;&nbsp;` filler. No preheader text precedes it in the visible source. Clients will fall back to the first body text.
- **Leftover editor artifacts:** `<body class="is-html-mode" data-contenteditable="true">` and the unreplaced `Replace/Paste SSL Here` comment. These are harmless to rendering but indicate an unclean export.
- **Image URLs:** Scene7 images request `wid=1200&hei=1600&fit=constrain` with `cb=` cache-busters. They are likely oversized for a 584px layout, which is a payload concern, but the actual file sizes are unverified.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The subject also contains no tokens.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unverified, likely relay artifacts:** The QA flagged missing `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058), and missing `Authentication-Results`. The QA notes these may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so I'm not treating them as confirmed defects. Verify on the raw message at the sending ESP. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a real plain-text part:** It should mirror the content, including links and the unsubscribe URL. This is the only hard QA failure.
2. **Add descriptive `alt` to the 8 banner images:** Use `alt=""` plus `role="presentation"` on the 5 decorative icons. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
3. **Fill the preheader:** Replace the `Replace/Paste SSL Here` placeholder with real text, keeping the `&zwnj;&nbsp;` filler after it.
4. **Confirm headers at the ESP:** Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment on `em.dcsg.com` using the original message rather than the relay copy.
5. **Remove the trailing U+200B from the subject.**
6. **Strip editor artifacts:** `is-html-mode`, `data-contenteditable`.
7. **Re-run the audit on the full HTML:** Include all 68 links so UTM consistency, the unsubscribe/address footer, and link health can be checked.
