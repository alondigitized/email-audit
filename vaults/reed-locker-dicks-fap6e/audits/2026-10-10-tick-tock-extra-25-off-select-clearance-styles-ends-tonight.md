# Tick, tock! ⏰ EXTRA 25% off select clearance styles ends tonight...
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-10**
## Executive summary

- This is the closing-night version of the clearance push. It opens with a red "Final Hours 07:59:55" countdown, then a giant "EXTRA 25% OFF" hero with "Ends Tonight" and a Shop Now button. The offer and the deadline are clear in the first screen. That's the right order for a last-day send.
- Below the hero, the email turns into the usual DICK'S catalog: "Up to 50% off" clothing and shoes, 40% off drinkware, golf, NFL, outdoor, and dumbbells. A second "UP TO 50% OFF" hero and a product grid close it out. The email is clean and well built, but it dilutes its own urgency. A single-deadline message turns into a full weekly circular, with five or six other "up to" offers competing with the 25% headline.
- Anyone subscribed since 10/5 has seen this red-hero, "up to 50%" pattern almost daily. It matches the 10/9 "Reduced prices? Yes, please! EXTRA 25% off select clearance" send, and the "up to 50% off" sends of 10/8 and 10/10. The deadline is the only new information. The email is competent and loud, but it's not fresh.

## What's working

- The "Final Hours" countdown at the top gives the deadline a visual anchor, and "Ends Tonight" repeats it above the headline.
- The hero is large and legible, and "EXTRA 25% OFF" is the first thing the eye lands on.
- A single green Shop Now button sits directly under the hero. It's unambiguous and easy to tap.
- The category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', 425 and Under, Fan Shop, All Sale) let shoppers go straight to their segment.
- Nothing is visibly broken: images load, layout is consistent, and there's no overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The "ends tonight" urgency is buried under about eight more modules, most of which are evergreen "up to 50%" offers with no deadline.
- The email shows two competing hero offers, "EXTRA 25% OFF" and "UP TO 50% OFF". It never says which discount applies to which products.
- The CTAs read "Shop Now" in every module. None names a category or product.
- The product grid at the bottom is generic: Samba, Handball Spezial, fleece. It has no prices, no clearance markdowns, and no tie to the clearance offer.
- The fine print under the hero ("Excludes... select clearance styles only... quantities last") weakens the headline. The word "select" appears three times, and nothing shows what is actually included.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the clearance story only. Keep the hero, add 4–6 real clearance products with was/now prices, and drop the "up to 50%" modules that the 10/8–10/10 sends already covered.
- 2. Make the countdown timer larger, or put it inside the hero, so the deadline and the 25% share one visual block.
- 3. Replace "Shop Now" with category-specific CTAs such as "Shop clearance shoes" or "Shop clearance jackets".
- 4. Show the offer's coverage with one line, for example "25% off already-reduced shoes, apparel & gear," instead of "select" alone.
- 5. Tighten the subject line to under 50 characters and drop the trailing ellipsis.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: extra 25% off clearance ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `⏰ Tonight only: extra 25% off clearance`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shoes, jackets & gear from Nike, adidas & more. Code-free, ends at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stack 25% off on top of markdowns. Online and in store until 11:59 PM.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is well-formed, with a doctype, `lang="en"`, a `<title>`, `role="presentation"` layout tables and Outlook conditional blocks. Its main confirmed defects are a missing plain-text part (9 chars) and 11 images with no `alt` attribute. The auth and unsubscribe-header warnings are probably artifacts of the AgentMail relay, so they are unconfirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Links use an opaque ESP redirect, for example `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/mclick?params=b.v1.…&pid=v1.…`. The final destination can't be read from source, so the HTML can't confirm that the link target matches the CTA.
- The open-tracking pixel `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=…` has no `alt`. It's flagged by QA but is harmless if it's 1x1 with `alt=""`.
- QA probed only 12 of 68 links (56 skipped as footer, utility or social). Coverage of the remaining links is unverified. No broken links were reported among those probed.
- All images carry a `cb=1791170387880` cache-buster, for example `banner4?cb=1791170387880`. This forces a fresh fetch from Scene7 on every open and can slow image loading.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt (QA-confirmed):** 11 images, including:
  - `banner4`
  - `2golf1010stwd4pm`
  - `jersey1010stwd4pm`
  - `2bikepaddlecamp1010stwd4pm`
  - `2weighttraining1010stwd4pm`
  - the icons `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1` and `icon-location-2025-1`
  - the `open` pixel
- The hero and category images look like the main content carriers. With images blocked or on a screen reader, there is no text equivalent.
- Leftover editor artifacts remain on `<body>`: `class="is-html-mode" data-contenteditable="true"`. These are harmless but unnecessary.
- The outer `table.wrapper` has `width="600"` plus inline `width:100%; max-width:600px`. This is fine, but Outlook relies on the `width="600"` attribute plus the MSO ghost tables, which are present.
- The web font Archivo is loaded via `@font-face` from `fonts.gstatic.com` inside `@media screen`, with an `Arial, sans-serif` fallback. No issue.
- The preheader (`#ssl` / `#pht-spacer`) is correctly hidden with `display:none; max-height:0; mso-hide:all`.
- The `#ssl` cell contains visible text, "Get $20 off $100+ when you sign up for Text Alerts**". It sits in a cell with `font-size:0; line-height:0; height:0`. This is a standard preheader technique and renders as intended.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The source is truncated, so the footer wasn't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] Plain-text fallback is 9 chars.** The email is effectively HTML-only. This raises spam-filter scoring risk and hurts text-only clients and accessibility tools. It could also be an extraction artifact, but as captured it's a defect.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them. Treat as unconfirmed. If they're really absent, this fails Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender one-click requirements.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status can't be assessed from this capture. The sending domain `em.dcsg.com` and the From domain are aligned, which is a good sign but not proof.
- The visible-footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address weren't visible in the truncated source and couldn't be verified. QA says an unsubscribe link is always included in probing, but no result was reported for it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters can't be evaluated. Links are wrapped in the `mclick` redirect, and the destination URLs are encoded in the opaque `params=` blob. Attribution is likely handled server-side by the ESP.
- Landing page alignment with the "EXTRA 25% off select clearance" offer can't be confirmed from source. The 12 probed links produced no reported failures.
- The preheader promotes a "$20 off $100+ for Text Alerts**" offer. The asterisk disclaimer was not visible in the truncated source.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a real plain-text part** (multipart/alternative) with the offer, key links and the unsubscribe URL. This is the only confirmed FAIL.
2. **Add descriptive `alt` text to the 5 content images.** Add `alt=""` to the 5 decorative icons and the tracking pixel.
3. **Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` in the raw message at the origin**, not the AgentMail relay copy. Likewise confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass in the original headers.
4. **Review the cache-buster `cb=` parameter on Scene7 images.** It helps when assets change but costs caching.
5. **Remove editor leftovers** (`is-html-mode`, `data-contenteditable`) from the production template.
6. **Extend link probing beyond 12 links** so the full set, including the footer unsubscribe link, is checked.
