# Ends. TONIGHT! ⏳ ScoreCard members Earn 2X Points only in the app...
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-17**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S closing out the Mobile Monday app-exclusive push from earlier today (8/17, "ScoreCard exclusive 📲 Earn 2X Points... TODAY ONLY," 5.00/10) with a last-call urgency variant. The hero is essentially the same creative — green "Final Hours 07:59:48" countdown bar, the phone-in-hand "2X POINTS" screenshot, black "Get Offer" button — just reframed as "Ends. TONIGHT!" in the subject. Good discipline reusing a distinct hero instead of another wordmark blast, and the live countdown timer is a legitimate urgency signal that's rare in this brand's cadence.
- Where it falls apart is the same problem as the morning send: the entire body below the fold has nothing to do with the app offer. It's the standard wall of unrelated category tiles — adidas shoes, CALIA/VRST apparel, Top Flite golf, cleats, fishing gear, fan shop gear, a 60% clearance banner, then a six-item product grid. A subscriber who opens for "earn 2X points before it ends tonight" gets one clean CTA and then eight more competing offers, which dilutes the single action the email exists to drive.
- Two sends of the same creative concept in one day is a real cadence flag. If this is a genuine reminder to non-openers of the morning email, fine — but nothing in the subject or preview signals "second reminder," so a subscriber who saw both will read this as repetitive rather than urgent.

## What's working

- Live countdown timer ("Final Hours 07:59:48") is a rare, credible urgency device this brand doesn't often use.
- Hero image (phone showing "2X Points" screen) is on-brand, legible, and ties directly to the app-only mechanic.
- Primary CTA "Get Offer" is a real button, high-contrast, above the fold.
- Fine print ("Must sign in to redeem offer. Valid through 8/17/26...") is present and not buried in a footer wall.

## What's weak

- Body is 80%+ unrelated category promos (golf, fishing, fan shop, clearance) that have nothing to do with the app-exclusive offer or urgency framing.
- This is the second "2X Points app exclusive" send today — no differentiation in the copy to signal it's a reminder, which reads as redundant rather than urgent.
- "Get Offer" is vague — doesn't say what the click does (open the app? redeem in-app?) for a mechanic that already requires extra steps (sign in, app-only).
- Subject leads with "Ends. TONIGHT!" but the countdown shows ~8 hours remaining — mismatched urgency framing if send time is late afternoon/evening.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the app offer only, or clearly section it off — move all unrelated category tiles to a secondary "while you're here" block below a hard visual break, not an unbroken scroll.
- 2. Make the CTA specific: "Open App & Shop" or "Redeem in App" instead of generic "Get Offer," since the mechanic requires the app.
- 3. If this is a follow-up to this morning's send, say so explicitly ("Last call" / "Final reminder") so it doesn't read as duplicate spam.
- 4. Verify subject urgency matches actual countdown value at send time — "Ends Tonight" next to an 8-hour clock undercuts credibility.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Final hours: 2X Points in the app ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `⏳ Last call — 2X ScoreCard Points, app only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sign in to the app before midnight to earn double points.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your countdown: under 8 hours to redeem.`
