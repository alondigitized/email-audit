# Starting NOW: Up to 50% off deals during our Early Black Friday Sale
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a well-built but overloaded Black Friday kickoff. The red "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY SALE" hero, "Up to 50% Off" and a single green "Shop Now" button make the headline offer clear in about a second. The ScoreCard bar and the "Order Today. Sport It Tomorrow." line at the top add useful loyalty and fulfillment context.
- Below the fold the email loses discipline. It stacks three competing percentages (50%, 80%, 40%), then a BOGO sock offer, a dumpling mystery item, a golf ball deal, table tennis, racquet gear, a six-item gift grid, an app promo and dense legal copy. The hero says 50%, but the next block shouts "UP TO 80% OFF". The reader can't tell which deal is the real one.
- It also lands in a crowded week. On 11/19 DICK'S sent "Up to 40% OFF top brands" and "up to 70% off clearance", and on 11/18 two more "up to 50%" and "up to 70%" sends. The 40% block here is nearly the same creative as the 11/19 and 11/17 emails. My judgement is a solid 7: the hero works, but the length and offer sprawl dilute it.

## What's working

- The hero is huge and unmistakable, and "Up to 50% Off" with one green "Shop Now" button sits directly under it.
- The "Two Days Only" tag and the dated fine print ("through 11/22/26 11:59PM PT") give the deadline credibility.
- The ScoreCard bar and "Order Today. Sport It Tomorrow." line surface loyalty and fast fulfillment up top.
- The category pill row (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Holiday Shop) gives a fast route for shoppers who don't want the hero offer.
- Each module has a single clear button, and the render shows no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The 50%, 80% and 40% headline offers compete, and the 80% block contradicts the hero's 50% claim at a glance.
- The email is far too long at about 6,500px, with eight or more modules, so most mobile readers will stop well before the gift grid.
- Every button says "Shop Now" (or close to it), so none tells the reader what they're getting.
- The 40% top-brands block repeats creative from the 11/17 and 11/19 sends, and the cadence is the fourth near-identical percent-off promo in four days.
- The legal and exclusions copy is tiny and stacked under nearly every module, which weakens how honest the offer feels.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body to the hero plus two or three modules, and drop the 80% clearance block. If clearance stays, make the hero "Up to 50%" the only headline number and label the other blocks as categories, not competing percentages.
- 2. Replace the generic "Shop Now" buttons with specific ones such as "Shop Nike & adidas Deals" or "Shop Golf Balls 2 for $100".
- 3. Move the countdown or "ends 11/22" into the hero and the subject line, since the deadline is currently buried in fine print.
- 4. Add a product-led module with prices (the gift grid has no prices) and, ideally, ratings.
- 5. Space out the percent-off sends this week so Black Friday itself has more impact.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Early Black Friday starts now: up to 50% off, ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Nike, Jordan & adidas up to 40% off. Early Black Friday is on`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Order today, pick up tomorrow. Plus BOGO free socks and 2-for-$100 golf balls.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Two days only: doorbusters on shoes, gear and gifts. Free store pickup.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML structure is sound (`lang`, presentation-role tables, MSO conditionals, hidden preheader), but the automated QA gives a 50% pass rate. The one hard failure is a plain-text part of only 9 characters. Eleven images also lack `alt` text.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Only 12 of 63 links were probed. The other 51 were skipped, so the QA data doesn't show whether most links resolve. No per-link results (status codes, redirect chains) were supplied, so no broken links are confirmed.
- The open-tracking pixel (`https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1...`) is a 1x1 `<img>` with no `alt`. It sits directly after `<body>`, which is normal placement. It is a functional tracker, but the empty `alt` is flagged by QA.
- The `<a class="arc-link" data-nl-type="DEEPLINK">` markup suggests links are wrapped for tracking or app deep-linking. The `href` values are cut off in the truncated source, so I can't verify them.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on 11 images (QA WARN).**
  - Content banners: `1030-dks-accessories-cozy-cabin-banner`, `2026-1120-dks-swd-6am-squishy`, `-golf-new`, `-tt`, `-gear`. These are `s7d6.scene7.com` JPEGs at `wid=1200&hei=1600`. If any carry offer text or CTAs, that content is invisible with images blocked or in a screen reader.
  - Icons: `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`. These are probably paired with text labels, but they still need `alt=""` or a descriptive `alt`.
  - Tracking pixel: the `open` image, as noted above.
- **Image weight:** The banner URLs request `fmt=jpeg&qlt=100` at 1200px wide. That is unusually high quality for a 600px layout. File sizes weren't measured, so this is a possible load-time concern, not a confirmed defect.
- **Plain-text fallback fails (QA FAIL).** The text part is 9 characters. Clients and filters that use the text part get effectively no content, and a near-empty text part is a minor spam-filter negative.
- **No issues found** in the visible markup otherwise. `lang="en"`, `<title>`, viewport meta, `role="presentation"` tables, and the `mso-hide` preheader are all present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible source. The footer is truncated, so I can't check the rest of the message.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** QA reports both as not found, with the note that the AgentMail relay may strip them. This is unconfirmed. Verify against raw headers from a direct delivery. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results:** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown. The header is absent from the relay copy, so this is not a confirmed failure.
- **Unsubscribe link and physical address in the footer:** The footer is cut off, so I couldn't check them. QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result was reported.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Can't assess. The truncated source exposes no CTA `href`s, so UTM parameters, landing-page alignment, and redirect behavior are unverified. The only visible domains are `em.dcsg.com` (sending and tracking) and `scene7.com` (images).

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a real plain-text part.** Include the offer headline, key links, and the unsubscribe URL, in place of the current 9-character stub.
2. **Add `alt` text to all 11 flagged images.** Use descriptive text for the five banners, `alt=""` for decorative icons, and `alt=""` for the pixel.
3. **Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers** on a direct-to-inbox test. Check `Authentication-Results` there too.
4. **Re-run link probing with a higher cap,** or at least include the primary CTAs, and capture the full HTML. That would verify the 51 skipped links, the UTM tagging, and the footer compliance elements.
