# $25 & UNDER select clearance? You'll want to see this!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- DICK'S opens with the same bold, single-focus creative that's worked before — "$25 AND UNDER" in oversized white type on a saturated maroon background, no clutter, no competing offer. It's a strong, scroll-stopping hero, and the green "SHOP NOW" button sits right where the eye lands next. But the promise dies almost immediately: one section down, the email pivots to "Epic Shoe Sale" (30% off), then "Nike Deal Days" (40% off), then cleats, golf apparel, life vests, and an app-download banner — none of it $25-and-under, none of it tied to the subject line at all.
- This is the same "bait-and-switch clearance" pattern flagged in the 2026-08-03 "$25 and under" send (8/10) — a genuinely great hero followed by a kitchen-sink catalog dump. It's also recycling the "Epic Shoe Sale" red-locker creative that's now run at least three times in the past week (8/2, 8/3, 8/4) with no visual variation. The render itself is clean — no broken images, no overlapping text — so the execution is fine; the problem is content discipline and cadence fatigue.
- Judgement: a well-designed hero wasted on an email that doesn't deliver what it opens with, sent by a brand whose subscribers have now seen this exact structure and this exact shoe-locker image multiple times in a week.

## What's working

- The hero is a single, bold, unambiguous offer — "$25 AND UNDER" reads instantly, even at a glance.
- The green "SHOP NOW" CTA has strong contrast against the dark hero and is impossible to miss.
- The category pill row (MEN'S / WOMEN'S / KID'S / SHOES / ALL CLEARANCE / ALL SALE) gives immediate self-selection right under the hero.
- Scorecard rewards status ("500 Points • $10 Reward Bucks") is visible at the very top — a real personalization touch for logged-in members.
- No visual/render defects — images load, text doesn't overlap, layout is clean top to bottom.

## What's weak

- The subject and hero promise "$25 and under," but every subsequent section (30% off shoes, 40% off Nike, 25% off golf, 40% off life vests) breaks that promise with unrelated, higher-price offers — this is the exact bait-and-switch pattern already called out in the 8/3 "$25 and under" send.
- The "Epic Shoe Sale" red-locker/stacked-shoe module is a verbatim repeat of creative already sent 8/2, 8/3, and 8/4 — zero visual refresh in a week.
- The email crams seven unrelated offers (clearance, shoe sale, Nike Deal Days, water bottles, cleats, golf, life vests) into one send with no organizing theme beyond "stuff DICK'S sells" — it reads like a catalog dump, not a curated pick.
- The disclaimer/fine print under the hero ("Valid online and in select stores for a limited time. Exclusions apply...") is small and easy to skim past relative to the bold offer above it.
- "Limited Time" on Nike Deal Days has no actual date or countdown, so the urgency is unearned.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the body match the hero: if the subject and top module say "$25 and under," the next 2-3 modules should surface actual $25-and-under products, not a jump to 30-40% off different categories. This is the single biggest lift available — it's the difference between a promise kept and a promise broken, and subscribers who click through expecting $25 items and land on a 30%-off shoe sale will bounce.
- 2. Retire the red-locker "Epic Shoe Sale" creative for at least one send — it's now visually identical across three consecutive emails. Even a palette swap or different shoe lineup would break the fatigue.
- 3. Cut the email down to 3-4 modules max. Golf clothing, life vests, and water bottles feel bolted on; move them to a separate lifestyle/seasonal send rather than tacking them onto a clearance email.
- 4. Give "Nike Deal Days" a real deadline (a date or countdown) instead of a generic "Limited Time" badge — credible urgency converts better than vague urgency.
- 5. Move the fine print closer to the hero offer or make it slightly larger — right now it reads as an afterthought under a very bold claim.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Everything's $25 and under right now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Clearance just dropped: shoes, gear, apparel under $25`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Score low prices before these styles are gone for good.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New markdowns just added — shop $25-and-under styles now.`

# Technical Audit — DICK'S Clearance Email ($25 & UNDER)

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard Epsilon/Harmony-built HTML template with MSO/Outlook conditionals and mobile media queries; automated QA shows a 43% pass rate driven by a broken unsubscribe redirect, missing email authentication/compliance headers, and widespread missing alt text.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken unsubscribe link (FAIL):** The "Unsubscribe" link (`https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/U/v610000019fd167c901aeb42df4bbcf76f8/74c3fc660ee649c70000021ef3a0bcff`) redirects to an error page: `unknown url type: '/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription'`. The preference-center target is misconfigured or deregistered.
- All outbound links use Epsilon Harmony click-tracking redirects (`app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...`), including the header logo link — consistent with standard ESP redirect wrapping, no issue there.
- Open-tracking pixel present and correctly hidden: `<img src='https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/.../74c3fc660ee649c700004c5a42963aa1' style="display:none; ...">`.
- QA notes 12 of 62 total links were probed (click-likelihood ranked); 50 were skipped as footer/utility/social. Only the unsubscribe link failed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **10 images missing `alt` text**, including the SSL open pixel, `owala.png`, `selectcleats.jpg`, `mapbreak.gif`, `lifevests.jpg`, and 5 footer icons (`icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png`, `icon-location-2025.png`). This affects screen-reader users and image-blocked email clients.
- **Malformed CSS in MSO conditional block:** `div {background-color:inherit; font-size:0; line-height:100%; margin:0; padding:0;"}` — a stray `"` before the closing brace is invalid CSS syntax inside the `<!--[if (gte mso 9)|(IE)]>` style block, which can cause Outlook to drop or misparse the rule.
- Mobile responsive classes (`.mobile-off`, `.header-xl`, `.body-m`, etc.) are defined via a standard `@media screen and (max-width:480px)` block — no structural issues found in the provided markup.
- Web font (`Archivo`) is loaded via `@font-face` inside `@media screen` (correctly scoped to avoid Outlook/desktop breakage), with a `font-family: Archivo, Arial, sans-serif` fallback — no issue found.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tags, personalization fields, or dynamic tokens (e.g., first name, loyalty status) are present in the provided source. No issues found in the visible markup, though the source is truncated and body content beyond the header was not available for review.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe link is broken** (see §2) — this is a CAN-SPAM risk since the required functioning opt-out mechanism is not working.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found (WARN):** May be a capture limitation of the AgentMail relay rather than absence in the original send — cannot be confirmed either way from this data.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found (WARN):** RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe support cannot be confirmed.
- **`Authentication-Results` header not found (WARN):** SPF/DKIM pass/fail status is unknown from the available data.
- The `application/ld+json` structured data block contains **JavaScript-style `//` comments** (e.g., `// GIF & WEBP images are not supported...`, `// DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!`), which are **invalid JSON syntax**. Any strict JSON-LD parser (schema.org validators, Gmail promo-card renderer) will fail to parse this block. Additionally, `"discountCode"`, `"availabilityEnds"`, and `"image"` are empty strings — template placeholders left unfilled in production markup.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
All links route through Epsilon Harmony click-tracking redirects (`app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...`) with opaque `__dU__` tokens rather than direct URLs, so destination UTM parameters and landing-page alignment cannot be verified from the truncated source provided. The confirmed exception is the unsubscribe link, which resolves to a preference-center error page rather than a valid unsubscribe/subscription-management page (see §2/§5).

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the unsubscribe redirect target (`/pc/subscription` errors on `/pc/Error/Error`) — this is a compliance-blocking bug, highest priority.
2. Remove the two `//` comment lines from the `application/ld+json` block, or delete the block if the promo-card/discount fields aren't being populated — invalid JSON in structured data can break parsers silently.
3. Add `alt` text to the 10 flagged images (empty `alt=""` is acceptable for the tracking pixel and purely decorative icons, but content images like `owala.png`, `selectcleats.jpg`, `lifevests.jpg` should have descriptive text).
4. Remove the stray `"` in the MSO conditional CSS (`div {...padding:0;"}`) to prevent Outlook style-parsing issues.
5. Confirm with the sending platform/AgentMail relay whether `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` headers are actually being sent but dropped in transit, or genuinely absent — verify via raw SMTP headers rather than relay-captured headers.
