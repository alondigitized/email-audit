# The EPIC Shoe Sale continues! Up to 30% off select shoes & cleats from top brands
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- This is the "Epic Shoe Sale" hero again — same red locker background, same shoe stack (Nike/adidas/HOKA), just re-cut with an "Up to 30% off" headline instead of yesterday's version. Where the 8/9 send stopped at a clean, single-focus shoe hero, this one bolts the entire weekly-digest sprawl back onto it: men's/women's/kids' clothing, team sports, golf, and then the same beach-ball "Up to 50% off This Week's Deals" banner and product-pick grid that anchored the 8/10 digest send. It's a Frankenstein of the two formats DICK'S has been alternating between.
- The shoe hero itself is still strong — clear headline, visible "Save on hundreds of styles from Nike, adidas, HOKA and more," green SHOP NOW button, no render bugs. But by burying it under seven more offers before the reader hits "This Week's Deals," the email dilutes the one thing that made yesterday's version work. Subscribers who opened yesterday's "Epic Shoe Sale" email will recognize this as the same creative continued into another sprawling digest, which reads as filler rather than a fresh sale.
- Judgment: functional but redundant — this brand has now sent shoe-focused and digest-focused versions of nearly the same assets on back-to-back days, and merging them here undercuts both.

## What's working

- Hero shoe stack (Nike/adidas/HOKA on red locker background) is crisp, well-composed, and legible with no render bugs.
- "Up to 30% Off Select Shoes and Cleats" headline is specific and immediately visible above the fold.
- Green SHOP NOW button under the hero is high-contrast and unambiguous.
- Category nav pills (MEN'S / WOMEN'S / KIDS' / SHOES / TEAM SPORTS / BACK-TO-SCHOOL) give quick wayfinding for browsers who aren't there for shoes specifically.
- Consistent DICK'S green/black brand system throughout — footer, buttons, and app-download block all on-brand.

## What's weak

- The email doesn't stay focused — after the shoe hero it pivots into the exact same men's/women's/kids'/team sports/golf sprawl seen in the 8/10 digest send, diluting the "Epic Shoe Sale" premise.
- "This Week's Deals" beach-ball banner (identical creative to the 6.00/10 8/10 send) resets the visual hierarchy halfway through, competing with the shoe hero for "the" offer.
- No urgency or countdown here, unlike the 8/8 sends that used a literal countdown clock — this one just says "Valid through 8/26/28 11:59PM PT."
- Product picks at the bottom (backpack, socks, HydroJug) are generic back-to-school filler, not shoes, further blurring the subject line's promise.
- Subject says "shoes & cleats" but over half the visible email is clothing, golf, and general deals — a subject/body mismatch a click-through reader will notice fast.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email back to the shoe/cleat focus promised in the subject — drop the clothing, kids', golf, and "This Week's Deals" blocks entirely, or move them to a separate weekly-digest send (which DICK'S is already running). This is the single biggest lift: it removes the redundancy with the 8/10 digest and lets the strong hero actually be the star.
- 2. If more content is needed, follow it with shoe-adjacent cross-sells only (cleats, team sports footwear, shoe care) rather than golf and backpacks.
- 3. Add a real deadline/countdown like the 8/8 sends used — "Valid through 8/26" buried in body copy has none of the urgency that scored well two days ago.
- 4. Move the product-pick grid to feature shoes/cleats, not a backpack and socks, to reinforce the subject line's promise all the way to the bottom.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Shoes & cleats up to 30% off — Nike, adidas, HOKA`
- **Subject Alt B:** `30% off cleats + shoes: your back-to-school lineup`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns just added — shop before your size sells out`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Nike, adidas, HOKA and more — up to 30% off today only`
