# Say hello to our best DEALS of the week! (They're up to 50% off)
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-29**
## Executive summary

- This is at minimum the eighth DICK'S email to land since June 26 — and the most telling thing about it is the subject line pivot: the 4th of July banner has been quietly dropped in favor of "Best Deals of the Week," signaling the brand is refreshing the frame while largely recycling the same underlying offer. The 50% OFF hero badge is prominent and the $25 Under section (last seen on June 28, scored 8/10 that day) returns as a secondary anchor. The execution is clean by DICK'S standards.
- The problem is the category soup. Five separate department blocks — Men's, Women's, Kids', Golf, Team Sports — each with their own "Up to 40% Off" or "50% Off" header and SHOP NOW button, scatter attention without giving the eye a single reason to click. The email reads like a sitemap, not a campaign. There's no hero product, no editorial voice, no reason this specific deal cohort was assembled.
- The cadence is the elephant in the room. Eight emails in four days is inbox abuse for anyone who hasn't explicitly opted into daily sends. The subject's "Say hello to our best DEALS" playfulness lands as hollow when you've already heard this exact pitch six times. A subscriber is already fatigued before the open.

## What's working

- **50% OFF badge dominates the hero** — visible at a glance, no ambiguity about the value proposition.
- **Category tabs below the hero** (Golf, Women's, Men's, etc.) give power-shoppers a fast jump to their department without scrolling.
- **$25 Under section** is a clean, concrete price anchor — the red badge stands out and the products shown are specific and visually distinct.
- **No render bugs** — the email is visually intact, no broken images or layout breaks visible.
- **"Best Deals of the Week" framing** is a defensible creative reset away from the now-stale 4th of July banner.

## What's weak

- **Five simultaneous department pitches kill the click** — Men's/Women's/Kids'/Golf/Team Sports all competing equally dilutes the focal point.
- **"SHOP NOW" repeated five times** with no product-specific copy is the weakest possible CTA; it tells the user nothing about what they'd find.
- **No hero product or editorial pick** — there's no "this week's standout item," just a grid of category tiles.
- **Cadence fatigue is now the biggest conversion threat** — this is the 8th send in 4 days; "Say hello to our best DEALS" reads as brand tone-deafness at this volume.
- **Subject line is 65 characters and leads with filler** — "Say hello to" burns 9 characters before any information is conveyed; on most mobile clients the "(They're up to 50% off)" parenthetical clips entirely.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill four of the five department blocks; feature one.** Pick the department with the deepest discount (Golf at 50% off is the only one that differs from the 40% baseline) and make that the hero with a real product image. Let the category tabs carry the rest.
- 2. **Rewrite the subject line to lead with the offer, not the greeting.** The current structure wastes the first 9 characters.
- **Subject Alt A:** `50% off this week — Golf, shoes, apparel & more`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Best deals of the week: up to 50% off select gear`
- 3. **Write a real preheader** that extends the hero rather than repeating the subject.
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop men's, women's, kids' and golf — clearance finds from $25`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New markdowns just dropped — plus $25-and-under clearance`
- 4. **Throttle the send cadence or segment the list.** Eight emails in four days pushes unsubscribe risk higher than any creative fix can offset. Even splitting into "deal seekers" vs. "product browsers" and targeting accordingly would recover open rates.
- 5. **Give the $25 Under section a named product anchor** — a single hero shoe or water bottle with the price shown is far more compelling than a generic clearance badge.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — DICK'S Sporting Goods Promotional Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is deployed through Epsilon/Harmony ESP with standard click and open tracking. The primary technical issues are a broken JSON-LD structured data block (invalid syntax + stale data) and a suspected dev-environment asset URL in the image CDN path.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Open pixel** — standard Epsilon pattern, present and hidden correctly:
```
https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/v610000019f1509f8acc51f9d369ff0aab4/ae69b972...
```

**Click tracking** — all links route through `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...` redirect wrapper. UTM parameter passthrough **cannot be confirmed** from truncated source; destination URLs are encoded inside the wrapper and not visible.

**Suspect image CDN path** — logo src contains `/_dev/` path segment:
```
https://dsg.images.harmony.epsilon.com/_phoenix/_dev/AEM/img/headers/brand-logo--dk…
```
This pattern commonly indicates a staging/dev asset path. If it resolves to a dev origin in production, the asset may be unavailable or served from an unintended environment.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Duplicate `font-family` declaration** in `<body>` inline style — harmless but sloppy:
```css
font-family:Archivo,Arial,sans-serif; font-family:Archivo,Arial,sans-serif!important
```

**No dark mode handling** — no `prefers-color-scheme` media query and no `<meta name="color-scheme">` tag. On Apple Mail and Gmail iOS in dark mode, `background:#F5F5F5` and `color:#141414` may invert to near-unreadable values.

**Logo `alt` attribute** — the `<img id="logo">` tag is truncated at the end of the provided source. Cannot confirm presence of `alt` text. If absent, image-blocking clients and screen readers receive no fallback.

**Residual template comment left in production output:**
```html
<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->
```
This appears immediately after the open-tracking pixel. It is a developer instruction that was not removed before send.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens visible (`{{first_name}}`, `*|FNAME|*`, etc.) — good.

The header table carries `id="header-non-sc-member"`, indicating conditional content segmentation (ScoreCard vs. non-member). The ScoreCard member variant is not present in the provided source, so rendering correctness of that branch cannot be verified here.

---

### 5. Compliance

**JSON-LD block is syntactically invalid** — the `<script type="application/ld+json">` block contains JavaScript-style `//` comments, which are **not valid JSON**. Gmail's structured data parser will reject the entire block and suppress email annotations:
```json
{ // GIF & WEBP images are not supported...
  "@type": "PromotionCard",
  // DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!
  "image": ""
}
```

**Stale `availabilityStarts` date** — hardcoded to `2020-03-06`, six years out of date:
```json
"availabilityStarts": "2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"
```

**Empty required fields sent to production:**
```json
"description": "",
"discountCode": "",
"availabilityEnds": "",
"image": ""
```

**Invalid Schema.org `@type`** — `"DiscountOffer"` is not a recognized Schema.org type. The correct type for a discount offer is `"Offer"`. This compounds the JSON parse failure.

**CAN-SPAM physical address and unsubscribe link** — not visible in the truncated source. Cannot confirm or deny presence. These are required fields and must appear in the full render.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All click-through URLs are Epsilon-encoded redirects. No bare `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, or `utm_campaign` parameters are visible in the provided HTML. UTM continuity depends entirely on what Epsilon appends when resolving the redirect — this requires a live link check against the decoded destination URLs to confirm.

The `/_dev/` segment in the image CDN path (noted above) should be verified against the production asset path to ensure no broken images in delivered mail.

---

| Priority | Issue | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **High** | JSON-LD invalid syntax | Strip all `//` comments from the structured data block |
| **High** | JSON-LD stale/empty fields | Populate `availabilityStarts`/`Ends`, `description`, `discountCode`, `image` dynamically at send time |
| **High** | `@type: "DiscountOffer"` invalid | Change to `@type: "Offer"` per Schema.org spec |
| **High** | `/_dev/` in image CDN URL | Confirm this resolves to a production asset; replace with production path if not |
| **Medium** | No dark mode CSS | Add `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` overrides for background and text colors |
| **Medium** | UTM passthrough unverified | Decode one Epsilon redirect URL and confirm `utm_source=email&utm_medium=...` is present on the destination |
