# NEW DEALS ADDED🚨 Don't miss our biggest sale of summer...
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-24**
## Executive summary

- This is the seventh Deal Days email from DICK'S in three days — and the creative team appears to know it. "NEW DEALS ADDED🚨" in the subject is a genuine attempt to give fatigued subscribers a reason to re-engage, but the email body undermines that promise immediately: the hero still says "Our Biggest Sale of Summer" in the same green-on-dark template used in every prior send. The content inside has rotated (basketball shorts, New Balance, Nike Metcon, Calia section, 30% off clearance block), but the frame is identical.
- Within the Deal Days system, this email is competent. Multiple real offers are legible — a clearance markdown at 30% off, specific product pricing in the grid, the 10% Rewards Back section — and the layout is clean with no render issues. The visual hierarchy works: hero lands first, product rows flow naturally, the dark clearance section creates a deliberate break.
- The problem is cadence. Six earlier sends in 72 hours means the "NEW DEALS ADDED" hook has to do a lot of heavy lifting against a subscriber who has already seen this exact green banner repeatedly. As a standalone email, it earns a solid mid-range score. As the seventh touch in three days, it is friction.

## What's working

- The 30% off clearance block stands out visually — the dark background creates a real contrast break in a long scroll.
- "NEW DEALS ADDED" in the subject is meaningfully different from prior sends; it signals rotation rather than repetition.
- Specific brand callouts (New Balance, Nike Metcon) are visible in the product rows, giving shoppers something to anchor on.
- The loyalty "Earn 10% Back in Rewards" section is properly surfaced mid-email, not buried in the footer.
- No render bugs, broken images, or layout issues — the email displays cleanly.

## What's weak

- The hero copy is word-for-word identical to June 23's launch email ("Our Biggest Sale of Summer") — the subject promises new content that the body fails to deliver.
- "Shop Now" is the CTA on every product row — generic, with no verb+noun specificity to distinguish basketball shorts from water bottles.
- No visible deadline for Deal Days itself — "don't miss" urgency without a specific end date or countdown is hollow by the seventh send.
- The email is long and product-dense; without a single hero product, the eye scatters across competing rows.
- No social proof anywhere — no review counts, star ratings, or "best seller" badges despite clear room in the product tiles.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Retire "Our Biggest Sale of Summer" from the hero** — it has appeared in every Deal Days email. Swap in a rotating category headline tied to today's actual deals (e.g., "Today: Footwear Markdowns — New Styles Just Dropped").
- 2. **Add a specific Deal Days end date to the hero** — "Ends Sunday" or "3 days left" converts the 🚨 emoji into a real urgency signal.
- 3. **Give the clearance block its own CTA verb** — "Shop Clearance" beats "Shop Now" because it completes the promise of 30% off.
- 4. **Pick one hero product and link directly** — the product grid works as a secondary section, but a single featured product (e.g., the New Balance shoe with a specific price) would anchor click intent.
- 5. **Surface a review count or "fan favorite" badge** on at least the top-selling product tile to add social proof without layout changes.
- **Subject Alt A:** `NEW: 30% off clearance + today's best deals — DICK'S Deal Days`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Today only: these deals just dropped (Deal Days ends Sunday)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Basketball shorts, Nike, New Balance + extra 30% off select clearance — new today.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fresh drops in footwear, apparel + clearance. Deal Days ends in 3 days.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Email Audit — DICK'S Deal Days (2026-06-26)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email's structural HTML and ESP tracking appear standard, but the JSON-LD structured data block contains invalid JSON syntax and multiple unfilled template placeholders that will cause Gmail's annotation parser to silently reject the entire schema payload. No rendering-breaking issues detected in the visible markup.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**No blocking issues.** All href values route through Epsilon's redirect domain (`app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...`), which is the expected pattern for Salesforce Marketing Cloud click tracking.

One observation: the encoded `__dU__` query parameter embedded in click URLs (e.g., the logo href) contains what appears to be a per-recipient encrypted identifier. This is standard Epsilon behavior, but confirm it resolves correctly for anonymous/non-identified recipients if this segment includes prospecting.

Open pixel located at `app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/...` with correct `display:none; max-height:0px; mso-hide:all` suppression — no issues.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Minor issues:**

- **Tracking pixel attribute quoting inconsistency**: The open-pixel `<img>` uses single quotes (`src='...'`) while all other attributes use double quotes. Valid per spec but worth normalizing for template consistency and linter hygiene.
- **Logo `alt` attribute**: The `<img id="logo">` tag is truncated in the provided source — verify `alt="DICK'S Sporting Goods"` or equivalent is present. Missing alt on the header logo fails WCAG 1.1.1 and degrades image-blocked rendering.
- **`bgcolor` on `<body>`**: `bgcolor="#F5F5F5"` is deprecated HTML but intentional for Outlook fallback — acceptable practice in email.
- **Web font version**: `Archivo v7` loaded from `fonts.gstatic.com` — check whether a newer version has been published if tracking fallback metrics.

Conditional MSO comments are present and correctly structured for Outlook. `role="presentation"` applied to layout tables. `lang="en"` on `<html>`. No issues there.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**One confirmed issue:**

The outer table carries `id="header-non-sc-member"`, indicating this is a conditional block variant for non-ScoreCard members. The full template likely uses dynamic content rules or A/B blocks. Confirm that:
- The ScoreCard-member variant (`header-sc-member`) is actually suppressed for this segment, not accidentally rendered alongside.
- No unreplaced `%%merge_field%%` or `{{token}}` syntax appears downstream in the body (not visible in the truncated source).

In the JSON-LD block (see §5 below), `discountCode` and `description` are empty strings — these look like unfilled merge tokens rather than intentionally blank fields.

---

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)

**Unsubscribe and physical address** are not visible in the truncated source — assumed to be in the footer. Flag for spot-check: CAN-SPAM requires a functioning unsubscribe mechanism and a valid physical postal address in every commercial email.

**Authentication headers** (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) cannot be verified from HTML source alone — these live in the message envelope. DSG/Epsilon's `e.dcsg.com` sending domain should have DKIM signatures present; confirm via a raw message header dump in any inbox tester.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM Params, Landing Page Alignment)

UTM attribution cannot be confirmed directly from this source because click URLs pass through the Epsilon redirect (`/T/...`) before resolving. The redirect should be appending UTM parameters server-side. **Verify** by following at least one product CTA link in a test send and inspecting the final destination URL for `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, and `utm_content` parameters. If these are absent on the landing pages, attribution in GA4/Adobe Analytics will be broken for this send.

---

### 7. Recommendations

**P0 — Fix before next send:**

1. **Remove JavaScript comments from JSON-LD.** The `<script type="application/ld+json">` block contains `//`-style comments, which are illegal in JSON. This causes the entire structured data payload to fail parsing in Gmail (and any other JSON-LD consumer), silently dropping any Gmail Promotions tab annotation or offer card.

   Offending lines:
   ```
   // GIF & WEBP images are not supported and will be filtered out.
   // Sample image is 538x138, 3.9 aspect ratio
   // DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!
   ```

2. **Fill or remove empty JSON-LD fields.** The `PromotionCard` block has `"image": ""` with an explicit designer TODO comment left in production code — the promo image was never inserted. The `DiscountOffer` block has `"discountCode": ""`, `"description": ""`, and `"availabilityEnds": ""`. Either populate these dynamically or remove the unfilled objects entirely.

3. **Update the stale `availabilityStarts` date.** `DiscountOffer.availabilityStarts` is hardcoded to `"2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"` — a 6-year-old placeholder. If this field is used, it must reflect the actual campaign window.

4. **Remove or replace the non-standard `PromotionCard` schema type.** `PromotionCard` is not a recognized schema.org type. The correct type for Gmail Promotions annotation is `DiscountOffer` or `SiteLinksSearchBox`; for offer cards use the [Gmail Promotions schema](https://developers.google.com/gmail/promotab/schema).

**P1 — Verify before next send:**

5. **Confirm UTM parameters resolve correctly** on all destination URLs via a live test send and link inspection.
6. **Verify logo `alt` text** is present in the untruncated source.
7. **Spot-check footer** for CAN-SPAM physical address and functional unsubscribe link.
