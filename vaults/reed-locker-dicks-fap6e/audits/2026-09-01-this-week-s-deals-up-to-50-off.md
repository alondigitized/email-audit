# This week's deals = up to 50% OFF!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-01**
## Executive summary

- This is the green-locker "UP TO 50% OFF" hero again — the exact same creative DICK'S has been running since 8/16, appearing here for at least the fourth time this month with only the subject line swapped ("This week's deals = up to 50% OFF!"). At this point the hero is pure wallpaper; a subscriber who's opened even two of these prior sends has already seen this exact image.
- Past that fatigue problem, the body itself is one of the more comprehensive DICK'S has sent — six category modules (Men's, Women's, Kids', Team Sports, Golf) plus a "Hundreds of New Clearance Styles" banner and a "Check out these picks" product grid. It's thorough but bloated: there's no single clear CTA, just a wall of "Shop Now" buttons competing for attention, and the offer language is inconsistent (50% here, 40% there) without ever landing on one concrete headline deal.
- Judgment: functional but tired. The creative needs to be retired, and the body needs to be cut roughly in half to have any real chance at conversion.

## What's working

- Category chip navigation (Men's/Women's/Kids'/Shoes/Team Sports) right under the hero gives quick wayfinding for someone who knows what they want.
- Product photography is clean and demographically varied — men's, women's, kids', team sports, and golf are all represented.
- "Free ship to store now available" and "Best Price Guarantee" trust signals are present near the footer.
- No render bugs — images load, text doesn't overlap, buttons are legible.

## What's weak

- The hero is a repeat of the same green-locker "UP TO 50% OFF" image run on 8/16, 8/29, and multiple times since — zero creative refresh.
- Six-plus stacked category modules plus a clearance banner plus a product grid make this email exhausting to scroll; there's no hierarchy telling the eye where to land first.
- The subject line ("This week's deals = up to 50% OFF!") is generic and nearly interchangeable with 8/29's and 8/31's subjects — no differentiation in the inbox.
- No preview/preheader text worth noting appears distinct from boilerplate — nothing complements the subject.
- The one genuinely different asset ("Hundreds of New Clearance Styles" banner) is buried two-thirds down the email instead of leading.

## Recommendations

- 1. Retire the green-locker hero entirely — it's been run at least 4 times in three weeks. Lead with the "Hundreds of New Clearance Styles" red banner instead since it's the only fresh creative in the send.
- 2. Cut the body from six category modules to two or three — pick the highest-margin categories (e.g., Back-to-School + Team Sports) and drop Golf/Kids to a single combined module.
- 3. Pick one number and commit — either lead with "up to 50%" or "up to 40%," not both scattered across modules; inconsistent percentages undercut urgency.
- 4. Add real preview text that names a specific category or deadline instead of leaving it blank/boilerplate.
- 5. Swap the subject for something concrete instead of the fourth "up to 50% off" variant this month.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Back-to-School gear: up to 50% off ends soon`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Hundreds of new clearance styles, up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns across shoes, gear, and team sports`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fresh clearance styles just dropped — shop before they're gone`
