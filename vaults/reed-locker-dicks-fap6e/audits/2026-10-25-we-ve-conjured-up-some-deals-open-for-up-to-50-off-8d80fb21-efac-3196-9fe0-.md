## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally sound: `lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` layout tables, viewport meta, Outlook conditionals and a hidden preheader. The confirmed defects are a missing plain-text part (9 chars) and absent `alt` attributes on hero and icon images. Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified from this capture.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links reported. 12 material links were probed and 58 were skipped (footer, utility and social links are deprioritized). The unsubscribe link is always probed, but the QA output gives no per-link result.
- The open-tracking pixel is `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1...&configId=512ffb51-...`, at `width="1" height="1"`. It has no `alt`, no `style="display:block"` and no `border="0"`. The QA flags its missing `alt` ("open"). This is minor, but the pixel sits directly in `<body>` before the wrapper table, which can cause a stray line or gap in some clients.
- Link wrappers carry `data-nl-type` and `class="arc-link"` attributes, which indicates ESP-side link wrapping. Destination URLs are cut off in the truncated source, so I can't confirm redirect behavior.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **[FAIL] Plain-text fallback is 9 characters.** The multipart/alternative `text/plain` part is effectively empty. This hurts deliverability scoring and leaves text-only clients and screen-reader or accessibility pipelines with nothing.
- **Missing `alt` text on 12 images**, from the QA data:
  - Hero and content images: `2clothing1025`, `teamsports1025stwd4`, `2golf1025`, `jersey10254pm`, `banner10254pm`, `cardiofitness1025stwd4`. These are Scene7 URLs requested at `wid=1200&hei=1600`. With images blocked, the offers in them are invisible.
  - Icons: `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`.
  - The tracking pixel (`open`).
  - Decorative images should use `alt=""` explicitly. A missing attribute is read differently, since screen readers may announce the filename.
- The hero images are requested at `1200x1600` with `fmt=jpeg&qlt=100`. That is heavy for a 584px container, and `qlt=100` inflates file size unnecessarily. This is an inference from the URLs, not a confirmed load-time measurement.
- Positives: web font `@font-face` is under `@media screen` with an `Arial, sans-serif` fallback, and `x-apple-data-detectors` is handled.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source and subject line contain no unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`). The remainder of the source is truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` not found** and **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. This is unconfirmed, not a verified sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers, so check the raw message from a direct-delivery inbox.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture. The From domain is `em.dcsg.com`, and the tracking domain `data.em.dcsg.com` is on the same organizational domain, which is good for alignment. I can't verify alignment itself.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The truncated source exposes no destination URLs, so UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) and landing-page alignment can't be assessed. The QA found no link failures among the 12 probed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a real `text/plain` part** (offer copy, key CTAs with URLs, unsubscribe link). This is the only hard QA failure.
2. **Add `alt` to all 12 flagged images.** Use descriptive text for the six content images, matching the offer text baked into the creative. Use `alt=""` for the decorative icons and the pixel. Add `style="display:block;border:0"` to the pixel.
3. **Reduce hero image weight.** Request widths of roughly 2x the 584px display width (about 1168px) and lower `qlt` to about 80.
4. **Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`** (one-click, RFC 8058) plus SPF, DKIM and DMARC on a raw copy delivered outside the relay. Treat this as unconfirmed until then.
