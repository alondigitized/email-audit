# Stop scrolling! Up to 75% OFF select clearance is happening now
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-25**
## Executive summary

- This is a loud, clear clearance send. A huge white "UP TO 75% OFF" sits on an autumn-leaf hero, and the eye lands on the number first. A "Limited Time" label, the headline "Up to 75% Off Select Clearance Styles" and a red Shop Now button sit right under it. The offer is bigger than anything in the last few days. On 10/24 and 10/22 the brand pushed "Extra 25% off," and "up to 75%" is a real escalation.
- The body is where it loses focus. Below the hero there are about seven more modules: Nike $100 & Under, fan shop at 40% off, Maxfli golf balls, pickleball at 50% off, a HydroJug launch, ski and snowboard at 35% off, and a credit card promo. The clearance message gets buried. The "Clearance" sender label also promises a focused clearance send, but most modules aren't clearance. The hero and first CTA are strong. The rest reads like the weekly-deals template.
- The subject tries too hard. "Stop scrolling!" is generic, and this is the fourth or fifth clearance-themed send in about four days. Fatigue is the main risk here, not creative quality.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 75% OFF" hero is huge, high contrast and readable at a glance.
- The "Limited Time" label, headline and red Shop Now button sit directly under the hero in a tight stack.
- The category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 & Under, Fan Shop, All Sale) give quick routes into the sale.
- The Fan Shop module shows "One Day Only" and "No code needed", and the module title says up to 40% off.
- The offer escalates from the "Extra 25%" sends of 10/22–10/24, which gives it a fresh angle.

## What's weak

- Most of the modules below the hero aren't clearance. Golf balls, the HydroJug launch, ski gear and the credit card all dilute the "75% off clearance" promise.
- "Up to 75% off select" has no product shown in the hero, so there's no proof of what actually gets that discount.
- The fine print is long and dense. The terms say "select," and the discount is not tied to a deadline or to named categories.
- The offer stack is incoherent: 75%, 40%, 50%, 35%, BOGO and a $30 credit card bonus. None of them gets a clear lead.
- There's no deadline on the main offer. "Limited Time" is vague, while the 10/24 send had a real countdown.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the template down to a true clearance email. Keep the hero, add a 4–6 product grid with real "was/now" prices and discounts, and drop the golf, HydroJug, ski and credit card modules.
- 2. Put a real deadline on the hero, such as "Ends Sunday 11:59 PM PT", ideally with a countdown like the 10/24 send.
- 3. Change the CTA to "Shop Clearance Deals" and add category links directly under the hero.
- 4. Give clearance a break for a day. This is the 5th clearance-themed send in 4 days, so space them out or vary the hook.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 75% off clearance ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Clearance markdowns hit 75% off: shoes, apparel, gear`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Nike, adidas and more marked down. Sizes go fast, so shop before midnight PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Deepest clearance cuts of the season on shoes, apparel and outdoor gear.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML is structurally sound, with role="presentation" tables, a `lang` attribute, a viewport meta tag and MSO conditionals. It has one hard QA failure: the plain-text part is only 9 characters. It also has widespread missing `alt` attributes on content images. Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be confirmed from the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- The HTML source is truncated and the QA output reports no per-link results (status codes, redirects). I can't confirm any link outcome. 12 links were probed and 53 skipped, so most of the link surface is unverified.
- The open-tracking pixel (`https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=...`) is a 1x1 `<img>` placed directly under `<body>`, before the wrapper table. It has no `alt` and no inline `display:none` or `style`. Some clients may render it as a stray inline image, and screen readers may announce it. No other tracking issue is evident.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed, 12 images flagged).** The affected images are the 6 scene7 hero/category images, the 5 icon images, and the open pixel.
  - Hero images: `2026-1025-dks-swd-6am-clothing`, `...-jersey`, `...-golf`, `...-6ampickleball`, `hydrojug-easton2026-1022-d`, `...-snowgear`.
  - Icons: `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`.
  - With images blocked or read by a screen reader, the hero blocks have no text equivalent. Decorative icons should carry `alt=""` explicitly rather than omit the attribute.
- **Image weight.** Hero images are requested as `fmt=jpeg&qlt=100&wid=1200&hei=1600`. Quality 100 at 1200x1600 produces large files for a 600px-wide layout and can slow load on mobile.
- **Generic `<title>`.** `<title>DICK'S Sporting Goods</title>` doesn't match the subject line. This is low impact.
- No issues found with the viewport meta tag, responsive media queries (`max-width:480px`), the Outlook `OfficeDocumentSettings` block or the `x-apple-data-detectors` override.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible HTML contains no unresolved tokens such as `{{...}}`, `%%...%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The truncated portion could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text fallback missing (FAIL).** The text part is 9 characters. This hurts deliverability and accessibility for text-only clients, and spam filters can penalize a near-empty text part.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found (WARN).** The QA notes the relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. If they are absent at the sender, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements for one-click unsubscribe aren't met.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found (WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sending domain is `em.dcsg.com`, and alignment should be verified from the original message headers.
- **Unsubscribe link and physical address.** The QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result is shown. The footer is outside the truncated HTML, so I can't confirm the link or postal address.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The visible HTML has no `href` values, and the QA gives no UTM or landing-page data. The 53 skipped links include any that might show tracking parameters.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a real plain-text part.** Make it a proper multipart/alternative with offer copy and key links. This is the only FAIL.
2. **Add `alt` text to every content image.**
   - Hero blocks need descriptive alt text, for example "Clearance clothing – up to 75% off".
   - Decorative icons need `alt=""`.
   - The open pixel needs `alt=""` plus `style="display:block;width:1px;height:1px"`, or it should move inside the wrapper table.
3. **Verify headers at the source, not through the relay.** Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment on the raw message.
4. **Reduce hero image weight.** Use `qlt=80–85` and a width near 1200 for retina only if needed.
5. **Re-run QA with the full HTML and a wider probe set.** This would let UTM consistency, the unsubscribe link and the footer address be checked. Prioritize the 53 skipped links.
