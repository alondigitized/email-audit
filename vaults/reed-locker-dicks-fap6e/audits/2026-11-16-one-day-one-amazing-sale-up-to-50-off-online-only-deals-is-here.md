# One Day. One Amazing SALE! 💸 Up to 50% OFF online-only deals is HERE. 📍
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, well-built one-day sale email, and it's a welcome change from the app-exclusive loop DICK'S has been running since 11/14. The "ONE DAY" hero is bold and the headline says exactly what you get: "24 Hours of Online-Only Deals: Up to 50% Off." There is one red CTA, "Shop Now," right under the hero.
- The body gets crowded fast. The email has a 70% clearance banner, $100-and-under shoes, $34.98 fleece, 50% off NCAA, $100 off golf bags, 50% off fitness gear and a four-product gift grid. The "up to 50%" hero and the "up to 70%" clearance banner compete, so the eye has no single winner. The jump from "50%" to "70%" also reads as two offers.
- Overall it's a competent, seasonal, promo-heavy send, better than the repetitive app pushes but still a catalog. Good for a broad audience and weak on persona targeting.

## What's working

- The "ONE DAY" hero is high-contrast and clear, and it carries the urgency in the creative itself.
- The headline states the offer and the deadline plainly, and the red "Shop Now" button is visible right away.
- The category pill row (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Holiday Shop) gives quick navigation.
- Holiday styling and a gift-picks grid fit the mid-November season.
- It breaks the 11/14–11/15 "$20 off $100+ in the app" pattern with a fresh offer.

## What's weak

- "Up to 50%" in the hero and "Up to 70%" in the clearance block undercut each other and blur the offer.
- Every module has its own "Shop Now" button, so none of them stands out.
- The "Up to" framing and the fine print under the hero and the clearance block ("while quantities last," "may not be valid in conjunction…") weaken the sense of a clear deal.
- The footer is long, with a lot of legal and secondary content, and the app download block is buried at the bottom.
- The product grid shows no prices or ratings, so there's no social proof and no concrete price anchor beyond the $34.98 fleece.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one headline number and make every module support it. If 70% is clearance, label it separately, or cut it from a "50% off" email.
- 2. Cut the module count roughly in half and lead with the two or three best categories.
- 3. Use specific CTA copy such as "Shop 50% off NCAA" or "Shop $34.98 fleece" instead of repeated "Shop Now."
- 4. Add prices and star ratings to the gift grid.
- 5. Move the fine print below the CTAs and add a countdown timer for credible urgency.

- **Subject Alt B:** `24 hours. Up to 50% off. Ends tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fleece $34.98, NCAA 50% off, shoes under $100. Online only, ends 11:59 PM PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `One-day online deals on shoes, fleece and fan gear. Free store pickup.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is structurally sound (`lang="en"`, `<title>`, `role="presentation"` layout tables, an MSO fallback block, and a hidden preheader). The confirmed problems are a near-empty plain-text part (9 chars) and missing alt attributes on 11 images. The header-based compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the relay may not have captured those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage is partial.** 12 material links were probed and 49 were skipped. The QA data reports no broken or redirecting links among the probed set. The skipped footer, utility, and social links are unverified.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1.…&configId=512ffb51-…` is a 1×1 `<img>` with no `alt`. The URL is very long (roughly 700+ characters) and carries a per-recipient `tuid`. This is normal for the ESP, but the pixel is flagged by the alt check.
- **Link markup:** the visible anchors use `class="arc-link"` and `data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"`. The source was truncated, so I can't confirm the final `href` values or any redirect wrapper.
- **Template residue:** the production HTML still contains the authoring comment `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` and `<!--DKS Email Marketing 2026-->`. This is harmless but unminified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (11 images, from QA):**
  - Tracking pixel: `data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open`.
  - Content images: `3shoes1116`, `2gifts116stwd`, `ncaa1116stwd6am`, `golf1116stwd6am`, `ethos1116stwd6am`.
  - Icons: `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`.
- **Impact:** the five content images are the main body of the email. With images blocked or read by a screen reader, those sections are blank or announced only by filename. For the tracking pixel and decorative icons, `alt=""` would be the correct fix.
- **Image weight:** the Scene7 URLs request `wid=1200&hei=1600&fmt=jpeg&qlt=100` for a 584–600px container. That is about 2× oversampled at maximum JPEG quality, which adds payload on mobile. This is inferred from the URL parameters, not from measured file sizes.
- **Good practice observed:** the `role="presentation"` tables, the Outlook (`mso`) conditional blocks, a web-font stack with an `Arial` fallback, and `font-display:swap`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The only per-recipient value seen is the `tuid` in the pixel URL. Source after the truncation point was not reviewed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] Plain-text fallback is 9 characters.** The email is effectively HTML-only. This hurts deliverability scoring and accessibility for text-only clients and some assistive tools.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool notes that the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. It matters for Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements, and the raw headers should be checked at the receiving mailbox.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status are unknown. The sender is `info@em.dcsg.com`, and the tracking domain `data.em.dcsg.com` is on the same subdomain, which is consistent with alignment, but that is unverified.
- **Unsubscribe link:** the QA tool states one is always included in the probe set. No failure was reported for it, and the in-body footer was not visible in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable from the data provided. The truncated HTML shows no `utm_*` parameters, and the QA output has no landing-page comparison. No issue is flagged.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a real plain-text part** that mirrors the offer copy and key links, replacing the 9-character version. This is the only confirmed FAIL.
2. **Add `alt` attributes** to all 11 flagged images:
   - Use descriptive alt text on the five content images (`3shoes1116`, `2gifts116stwd`, `ncaa1116stwd6am`, `golf1116stwd6am`, `ethos1116stwd6am`).
   - Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixel and the five decorative icons.
3. **Verify headers on a direct-to-inbox test.** Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass and align, because the relay could be hiding them.
4. **Resize the images.** Request Scene7 renditions at about 1200px wide at most, with `qlt` of about 80 to 85 instead of 100.
5. **Extend link probing** to the 49 skipped links, at least the footer unsubscribe and preference-center links, and check `utm_*` parameters on the final landing URLs.
6. **Strip the leftover template comments** (`Replace/Paste SSL Here`) from production sends.
