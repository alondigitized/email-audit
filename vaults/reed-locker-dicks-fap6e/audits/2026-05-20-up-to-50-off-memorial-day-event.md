# Up to 50% OFF 🤝 Memorial Day Event
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- This is at minimum DICK'S fifth Memorial Day-themed send in three days, and the subject line nearly mirrors their May 18 anchor send ("Memorial Day Event = Up to 50% OFF 🙌") that legitimized the campaign. The creative is competent — dominant hero, category grid, a smart National Runners Month section that brings a sliver of fresh air — but the template is showing its seams. Most tellingly: the CTA buttons read "SUMMER DEALS" and "SUMMER SHOP" inside a "Memorial Day Event" email. That copy collision signals a rushed swap, not deliberate campaign thinking.
- The bones are fine. Hero delivers the offer above the fold, the category grid short-circuits navigation, and the product picks section at the bottom serves browsers who are already converting. But a subscriber who opened the May 18 anchor and the May 20 morning send ("Up to 50% off Memorial Day deals") will process this in half a second and archive it without clicking.
- Bottom line: competent execution of an exhausted creative. The National Runners Month section is the only element that earns a second look and hints at what thoughtful segmentation could do here.

## What's working

- **Hero hierarchy is immediate** — "Memorial Day Event" is the visual anchor and "Up to 50% Off Deals!" lands directly below; the offer is readable without a single scroll on mobile.
- **National Runners Month section** — timely tie-in (May is National Running Month) that genuinely differentiates this send from the prior clearance templates.
- **Multi-category grid** — Men's, Women's, Golf, Outdoor, Shoes lets different sub-audiences self-route without hunting; reduces friction for the category browser.
- **Product picks at the bottom** — specific items with images do real work for buyers in purchase mode.
- **Clean render** — no broken images, no overlapping text, no lorem ipsum visible anywhere.

## What's weak

- **CTA copy says "Summer," email says "Memorial Day"** — "SUMMER DEALS" / "SUMMER SHOP" buttons clash directly with the "Memorial Day Event" hero. Looks like a seasonal template that got a hero swap but not a CTA update.
- **No explicit deadline** — "Memorial Day Event" is implied urgency, but "Ends Monday" or "Last 48 hours" is nowhere visible; the offer reads evergreen despite the campaign hook.
- **Fifth-plus near-identical send in ~3 days** — subscribers who engaged with the May 18 anchor have already made their decision or tuned out; incremental sends at this cadence cannibalize prior open rates.
- **ScoreCard/loyalty entirely absent from the body** — the one differentiator that could separate this from a generic clearance send is buried in the footer.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add "Ends Monday" in the hero subhead** — one line converts implied urgency to real urgency; biggest single lift available with no design change required.
- 2. **Fix the CTA copy** — "SHOP MEMORIAL DAY DEALS" inside a Memorial Day email beats "SUMMER DEALS." The mismatch creates a half-second of hesitation in a scanning inbox.
- 3. **Surface the ScoreCard hook** — "ScoreCard members: earn bonus points through Monday" differentiates this send from the clearance template and rewards loyalty.
- 4. **Cut cadence or rotate creative harder** — this email is functionally identical to the May 18 anchor. The sixth send in this window will underperform the fifth.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last chance: 50% off before Memorial Day`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop men's, women's, golf + outdoor — all marked down now`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `ScoreCard members: stack your savings before it's over`

## Technical Audit — DICK'S Sporting Goods "Memorial Day Event"

### 1. Technical Summary

The email uses Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) infrastructure with standard table-based layout and MSO/IE conditional comments. Two high-severity issues are present: invalid JSON-LD that will silently break Gmail Promotions tab annotations, and a preheader copied from a prior campaign that directly contradicts the subject line.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**No broken link mechanics detected**, but context is limited by HTML truncation.

- Open pixel (`/O/v610000019e465f00b58ef810f4bbc786c8/...`) is correctly hidden via `display:none; max-height:0px; overflow:hidden; mso-hide:all`.
- CTA links use SFMC redirect format (`/T/v610000019e465f...?__dU__=...`). Final destination URLs cannot be verified from the truncated source — confirm UTM params are appended on the resolved URLs.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**MSO conditional CSS syntax error** — stray double-quote before closing brace in the IE/Outlook inline stylesheet:

```html
<!--[if (gte mso 9)|(IE)]><style type="text/css">
  ...
  div {background-color:inherit; font-size:0; line-height:100%; margin:0; padding:0;"}
</style><![endif]-->
```

The `"` immediately before `}` produces invalid CSS. In Outlook 2013–2019 (MSO rendering engine), this may cause the entire `<style>` block to be discarded, breaking `div` background inheritance and font resets.

- `<html lang="en">` present — correct.
- `role="presentation"` on layout tables — correct.
- Image alt text cannot be confirmed from the truncated source; the logo `<img id="logo">` is cut off before the `alt` attribute.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Three issues in the `<script type="application/ld+json">` block:**

**a) JavaScript-style comments are invalid JSON** — the block uses `//` comments, which are not valid JSON syntax:

```json
{ // GIF & WEBP images are not supported and will be filtered out.
  // Sample image is 538x138, 3.9 aspect ratio
  "@type": "PromotionCard",
  // DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!
  "image": ""
}
```

All major JSON parsers (including the Gmail Promotions tab annotation parser) will reject the entire `<script type="application/ld+json">` payload on a syntax error. This disables Gmail deal/promotion card rendering entirely.

**b) Stale `availabilityStarts` date** — hardcoded to `2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00`. This is 6 years old and was never updated:

```json
"availabilityStarts": "2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00",
"availabilityEnds": ""
```

**c) Empty required fields** — `description`, `discountCode`, `availabilityEnds`, and `PromotionCard.image` are all empty strings `""`. These unfilled template placeholders confirm the structured data block was never production-ready.

- `id="header-non-sc-member"` on the header table signals a loyalty-member/non-member segmentation fork. Confirm the SC-member variant block exists in the full template and that the SFMC audience split is wired correctly.

---

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)

Sending domain: `e.dcsg.com` (SFMC subdomain).

- Physical address and unsubscribe mechanism are not visible in the truncated source — **must be present in the footer**. Cannot confirm or deny from the provided HTML.
- `From` display name is `"DICK'S | Memorial Day Event"` (campaign name embedded in From header). This is legal but non-standard; some spam filters penalize From-name volatility across campaigns. Low risk, worth noting.
- Authentication (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) cannot be assessed from HTML source alone — requires header inspection of a delivered message.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**Critical preheader/campaign mismatch.** The preheader text in `id="ssl"` reads:

```
Celebrate National Runners Month with the best brands in running 🏃
```

The subject line and sender name both reference **Memorial Day Event**. This is copy-pasted from a prior campaign (likely the National Runners Month send) and was not updated. In inbox preview, subscribers see subject + preheader together — this combination signals a broken or irrelevant email and will suppress open rates.

- The `DiscountOffer` schema `availabilityStarts` date (`2020-03-06`) does not align with the current promotional period — Google's parser may deprioritize or reject the annotation.
