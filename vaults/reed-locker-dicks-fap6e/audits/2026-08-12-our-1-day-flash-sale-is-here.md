# Our 1-day Flash Sale is here ⚡
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-12**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S dressing up the same weekly-digest sprawl in "Flash Sale" clothing. The hero says "Starts Now / Flash Sale: Up to 50% Off" over a green locker-room backdrop, but the "FLASH SALE" wordmark is cropped off the right edge of the image — a real render problem on a hero that's supposed to create urgency. Below the fold it's the exact same module stack seen in the 8/9–8/11 sends: Epic Shoe Sale, clothing, soccer balls, golf balls, kayaks, weighted vests, then the recycled "UP TO 50% OFF" beach-ball banner from yesterday's send, then the app pitch.
- The "1-day" urgency promised in the subject line isn't reinforced anywhere in the body — no countdown, no end time, no "today only" banner near the hero. By the time a reader scrolls past six unrelated category modules, any flash-sale urgency has evaporated into the same everyday-digest experience.
- Judgement: a decent hero concept (locker room + "flash sale") undercut by a cropped headline graphic and a body that doesn't behave like a flash sale at all — it's the standard digest with new hero art bolted on.

## What's working

- Clear "Starts Now" flash-sale badge and bold "Up to 50% Off" subhead sit right under the hero.
- Category chip nav (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Team Sports, Back-to-School) gives quick wayfinding for a scanning reader.
- Each module has its own visible "SHOP NOW" button, so no ambiguity about where to click within a section.
- Product photography (shoe stack, golf balls, kayak) is clean and well-lit, consistent with prior sends' quality bar.

## What's weak

- The "FLASH SALE" hero wordmark is cut off at the right edge — the word literally reads "FLAS / SAL" in the render, a visible layout bug on the most important asset in the email.
- No actual "1-day" or countdown urgency device anywhere in the body despite the subject promising a one-day flash sale.
- This is the fourth-plus consecutive send reusing the "up to 50%" beach-ball banner and the Epic Shoe Sale hero art verbatim from 8/9–8/11 — creative fatigue is now obvious even to a casual subscriber.
- The email sprawls into six unrelated category modules (soccer balls, golf balls, kayaks, vests) that dilute the flash-sale framing into another everyday digest.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the cropped hero graphic first — "FLASH SALE" needs to render fully within the image bounds; this is the single biggest visual credibility hit in the email.
- 2. Add a real urgency element near the hero — an end time or countdown ("Ends tonight at 11:59PM ET") since the subject promises "1-day."
- 3. Cut the module count for a true flash-sale send — lead with 2-3 tightly flash-sale-tagged categories instead of the full six-module digest; save kayaks/vests/soccer balls for the regular weekly digest format.
- 4. Retire the beach-ball "UP TO 50% OFF" banner and Epic Shoe Sale hero art — both have now appeared in back-to-back sends this week and read as stale to a daily-cadence subscriber.

- **Subject Alt B:** `⚡ 24 hours only — up to 50% off sitewide`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Locker room prices, gone by midnight — shop now.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shoes, gear, and clothing all marked down today only.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard DKS/Epsilon Harmony-hosted responsive template with MSO conditionals and inline/embedded CSS; automated QA measured a 43% pass rate driven by one broken link, missing unsubscribe headers, and widespread missing `alt` text.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken unsubscribe link (FAIL):** The "Unsubscribe" link (`https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/U/v610000019ff593583bc78752369fb45280/058da42b12d649360000021ef3a0bd01`) resolves to `/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription` — an application error page, not the subscription-management page. This is a functional defect, not just a compliance risk.
- All tracked links use the Epsilon/Harmony redirect domain `app.dickssportinggoods.com` with `/T/`, `/O/`, `/U/` click/open/unsub tracking tokens — consistent, expected pattern for this ESP.
- 12 of 64 links were probed (click-likelihood ranked); footer/utility/social links deprioritized per QA scope — no broken CTA links reported among probed set.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **11 images missing `alt` text**, including all four promotional lifestyle images (`clothing.jpg`, `soccerball.jpg`, `golf.jpg`, `kyak.jpg`), the `ethos.jpg` banner, the SSL open-tracking pixel, and 5 footer icons (SMS, SportsMatter, BPG, favorite-store, location). Screen readers will announce these as unlabeled images or skip them entirely.
- MSO/Outlook conditionals (`<!--[if (gte mso 9)|(IE)]-->`) and a `.wrapper` fallback table are present, indicating standard Outlook rendering support.
- `@font-face` for Archivo is scoped to `@media screen` (correctly excluded from Outlook/DPI-scaling clients) with system fallback `Arial, sans-serif` declared inline on `<body>`.
- Mobile breakpoint at 480px present via `@media screen and (max-width:480px)` with a full utility-class set (`.mobile-off`, `.header-*`, `.p-*`, etc.) — no rendering issues surfaced by QA.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{`, `%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) present in the visible HTML/text sampled.
- The embedded JSON-LD `PromotionCard`/`DiscountOffer` schema block has empty `"discountCode"`, `"description"`, and `"image"` fields, and `"availabilityStarts": "2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"` — a stale placeholder date from what appears to be an unedited template default. Also note the schema block contains `//` comments, which are invalid inside `application/ld+json` and will cause structured-data parsers to fail silently on this block.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header missing (WARN):** No `List-Unsubscribe` header was captured, so mailbox providers may not surface a native one-click unsubscribe UI (relay-capture caveat noted, but should be verified at source).
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post missing (WARN):** RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe (`List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`) not present — combined with the broken unsubscribe landing page (§2), this is a compliance-relevant defect: CAN-SPAM requires a working, functional opt-out mechanism, and the in-body link currently errors out.
- **Authentication-Results header missing (WARN):** SPF/DKIM verification status could not be confirmed via the relay; sending domain is `e.dcsg.com` (`From: DSG@e.dcsg.com`) — recommend verifying SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment at the source MTA rather than relying on relay capture.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Not directly assessable from the truncated source — outbound links route through Epsilon redirect tracking (`/T/...`) rather than exposing final destination URLs with UTM parameters directly in the HTML. Recommend resolving one or two probed CTA redirects to confirm they land on `dickssportinggoods.com` pages with expected UTM tagging (source/medium/campaign) and that the destination reflects the "1-Day Flash Sale" offer.
- The one confirmed resolvable destination — the unsubscribe flow — lands on an **error page** (`/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription`), indicating a broken route from email to site for at least that one path.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the unsubscribe redirect target so `/pc/subscription` resolves correctly instead of throwing `/pc/Error/Error` — highest priority, both functional and compliance-relevant.
2. Add `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` headers at the sending MTA to support RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe.
3. Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment and `Authentication-Results` presence for `e.dcsg.com` at the source (outside relay capture).
4. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four hero/lifestyle images and `alt=""` (decorative, empty) to the tracking pixel and footer icon images.
5. Remove the invalid `//` comments from the `application/ld+json` block (or delete the unused empty schema fields) so structured data parses correctly.
6. Spot-check resolved CTA destination URLs to confirm UTM parameters and correct landing-page alignment with the flash-sale offer.
