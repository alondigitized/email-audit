# The EPIC Shoe Sale is here — up to 30% off select styles
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-04**
## Executive summary

- This is the third "Epic Shoe Sale" DICK'S has run in three days, and the creative barely changes: the same red-locker backdrop, the same Nike/adidas/HOKA logo strip, the same stacked-shoe hero shot that scored 9/10 on 2026-08-03 as "Epic Shoe Sale 👏 Deals on 100s of styles won't last forever." A subscriber who opened Monday's and Tuesday's sends will recognize this instantly, and not in a good way — this is the exact repetition problem the rubric flags as a cadence killer.
- The hero itself is still strong in isolation: bold single-category focus, recognizable brand logos, clear "Up to 30% Off Select Shoes and Cleats" subhead, unambiguous green CTA. But just like the 8/3 send, DICK'S immediately abandons the single-offer promise and tacks on five more unrelated category promos (clothing, golf, team sports) plus the recycled blue "UP TO 50% OFF" beach-ball banner that already ran on 8/2 and again on 8/4 earlier today. That banner reappearing in the same email, on the same day it was already flagged in a separate send, is a real production/scheduling smell.
- Net: a well-executed hero buried in a kitchen-sink email that's now visibly reusing both its headline creative and a secondary promo banner within the same 24-48 hour window. Good asset, poor deployment discipline.

## What's working

- Hero is single-focus and legible: red locker backdrop, stacked sneakers, Nike/adidas/HOKA logos all read clearly at a glance.
- Subhead and CTA are unambiguous — "Up to 30% Off Select Shoes and Cleats" directly matches the subject line, with a clean green "SHOP NOW" button.
- Valid-through date ("8/15/26") gives the offer a credible, checkable deadline.
- ScoreCard rewards banner at the very top ("690 Points") is a nice loyalty touch for members.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, no overlapping text or broken layout anywhere in the scroll.

## What's weak

- The exact same red-locker/stacked-shoe hero creative ran 2026-08-03 under "Epic Shoe Sale 👏 Deals on 100s of styles won't last forever" — this is a near copy-paste, not a fresh send.
- The blue "UP TO 50% OFF" beach-ball banner further down is the identical asset flagged twice already (2026-08-02 hero, and again in today's earlier 8/4 send) — reusing it a third time inside the same week undercuts the "This Week's Deals" framing.
- After the shoe hero, the email cascades into six unrelated category blocks (clothing, golf, team sports) before circling back to another "up to 50%" claim — the single clean offer promised by the subject gets diluted fast.
- No product-level detail (sizes, specific shoe models, price points) anywhere near the hero — "select styles" stays vague through the whole shoe section.
- Subject line offers no urgency hook ("is here" is soft), despite the body carrying a real deadline (8/15) that could have been surfaced.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop reusing this exact hero creative — three near-identical "Epic Shoe Sale" sends in three days trains subscribers to skip the next one. Rotate the shoe styling (different angle, different color story) even if the offer stays the same.
- 2. Pull the blue beach-ball "50% off" banner from rotation entirely until enough time passes — it's now appeared in at least three separate sends across two days.
- 3. Cut the email after the shoe hero + one adjacent cross-sell block; the five-category cascade dilutes the "30% off shoes" promise the subject made.
- 4. Surface the 8/15 deadline in the subject line itself to add real urgency instead of implying it with "is here."
- 5. Add one specific shoe model or price callout near the hero (e.g., "Nike Pegasus now $89") to give the click something concrete to land on.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 30% off Nike, adidas & HOKA — ends 8/15`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your shoe upgrade is 30% off right now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Hundreds of styles, one big sale — shop before 8/15.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New markdowns on Nike, adidas and HOKA just dropped.`

# Technical Audit: DICK'S Sporting Goods — "EPIC Shoe Sale" (2026-08-04)

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard Epsilon Harmony-templated commerce email with MSO conditional markup and redirect-tracked links; one confirmed functional defect (broken unsubscribe destination) and malformed embedded JSON-LD, plus widespread missing alt text.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All links route through Epsilon Harmony's click-tracking domain (`app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...`, `/O/...`, `/U/...`) with an opaque `__dU__` payload token — actual destination URLs cannot be verified from the HTML source alone.
- **Broken unsubscribe link (confirmed by QA):** the "Unsubscribe" link (`https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/U/v610000019fce6ca408b2fc30f4bbcf76f8/6493074536064d090000021ef3a0bd05`) resolves to `/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription` — an application error page, not the preference/unsubscribe center.
- 12 of 69 links were probed by QA (57 footer/utility/social links deprioritized per probing methodology); no other broken links were reported among the probed set.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 11 images missing `alt` text (QA-confirmed): tracking pixel `6493074536064d0900004c5a42963aa1`, `mens.jpg`, `womens.jpg`, `kids.jpg`, `golf.jpg`, `team.jpg`, and 5 footer icons (`icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png`, `icon-location-2025.png`). Category shop-by-department images and footer value-prop icons will be inaccessible to screen readers and invisible if images are blocked.
- MSO/Outlook conditional comments (`<!--[if (gte mso 9)|(IE)]-->`) and a mobile media-query block (`max-width:480px`) are present and correctly structured.
- Custom web font (Archivo) is wrapped in `@media screen{@font-face{...}}` with `local()` fallbacks and an Arial/sans-serif stack — standard safe pattern for email client font support.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tokens (e.g. `%%first_name%%`, `{{...}}`) are visible in the provided HTML — content appears static/non-personalized. Cannot confirm behavior of any tokens outside the truncated portion of the source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe mechanism is broken** (see §2) — the visible unsubscribe link does not resolve to a working preference-center page, which is a functional CAN-SPAM risk regardless of header-level support.
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — QA notes this may be a relay artifact (AgentMail may not capture/forward the header) rather than confirmed absence at origin.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058, one-click unsubscribe) not detected — same relay caveat applies.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not detected — SPF/DKIM pass/fail status is unknown via this relay; cannot confirm authentication posture from available data.
- Physical mailer address / footer compliance block not visible in the truncated HTML provided — not assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed: all outbound links are wrapped in Epsilon Harmony redirect tracking (`/T/{listId}/{msgId}?__dU__=...`) rather than exposing direct landing-page URLs with UTM parameters. Destination and UTM alignment would require following the redirects, which is outside the provided HTML/QA data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the unsubscribe link target — it currently 404s to an internal ASPX error page (`/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription`) instead of the subscription preference center. This is the highest-priority fix given CAN-SPAM exposure.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 5 category images (`mens.jpg`, `womens.jpg`, `kids.jpg`, `golf.jpg`, `team.jpg`) and empty `alt=""` to the decorative tracking pixel and footer icons.
3. Fix the malformed `application/ld+json` block: it contains `//` comments (invalid JSON) and empty-string placeholder fields (`description`, `discountCode`, `image`, `availabilityEnds`) that were left unfilled from the template — this will fail JSON parsing by any consumer reading structured data (e.g., Gmail promotional annotations).
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` are actually present at the raw SMTP/header level (independent of the AgentMail relay) to rule out a genuine deliverability/compliance gap vs. a relay-capture limitation.
