# 🏃 Run, don't walk! EXTRA 25% off select clearance is happening now
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-21**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S back on the clearance angle, and it's a cleaner send than most of what's shown up in this run — a single, legible dock-photo hero with a big red "EXTRA 25% OFF" price tag, a clear "Shop Now" button right below it, and only two ScoreCard+ member modules before it drops into the usual product-grid sprawl. Compared to 8/21's earlier "Shop now for an EXTRA 25% off select clearance!!" (5.00/10) and the string of "up to 50%/60%" digest sends since 8/16, this is the same offer restated with a more urgent subject line and a tighter top-of-email — but it's still the same clearance percentage DICK'S already sent today, just repackaged with "Run, don't walk!"
- The hero-to-CTA path is the strongest part: photo, price tag, headline, button, all stacked without noise. Where it falls apart is past the fold — two nearly identical black ScoreCard+ modules (20% off / 50 Bonus Points) that aren't clearance-relevant, then six unrelated category tiles, then a "Check out these picks" grid of full-price sneakers and apparel that undercuts the clearance framing entirely.
- Judgement: a competent single-offer hero wrapped in the same over-stuffed digest DICK'S runs every time, and same-day repeat of a clearance percentage is a real fatigue risk.

## What's working

- Hero is a single unambiguous offer — a red "EXTRA 25% OFF" tag on a lake-dock photo, easy to read at a glance.
- Primary CTA "Shop Now" is a clear green button placed directly under the offer restatement.
- "Limited Time" and the exclusions line under the button ("Valid online only through 8/22/26 11:59PM PT") give the urgency a specific, credible deadline.
- No render bugs — images load, text doesn't overlap, layout is clean top to bottom.

## What's weak

- Same-day repeat: this brand already sent an "EXTRA 25% off select clearance" email earlier on 2026-08-21 — this is a near-duplicate offer, just with a punchier subject line.
- The two ScoreCard+ modules (member week 20% off, 50 Bonus Points) are visually loud (black background, giant type) but off-topic for a clearance-titled email, diluting the hero's focus.
- The bottom "Check out these picks" grid shows Nike/adidas product at what reads as full price with no clearance tagging, contradicting the "clearance" premise of the whole send.
- No visible product photography of actual clearance items — the hero is a lifestyle photo, not merchandise, so there's nothing concrete to click toward.

## Recommendations

- 1. Don't send two "EXTRA 25% off clearance" emails in one day — if this is a resend for non-openers, suppress it from users who already opened the earlier one, or swap the offer entirely.
- 2. Cut one of the two ScoreCard+ modules — 20% off and 50 Bonus Points are redundant asks in the same send; pick one.
- 3. Replace the bottom "Check out these picks" grid with actual clearance SKUs, or drop it — full-price product directly under a clearance hero undercuts the email's premise.
- 4. Add 2-3 clearance product thumbnails with strikethrough pricing near the hero so there's something concrete to click besides "Shop Now."

- **Subject Alt B:** `25% off clearance, no ScoreCard required`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shoes, apparel, and gear — up to 25% off marked clearance prices.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Ends 8/22 at 11:59PM PT — stock is limited.`
