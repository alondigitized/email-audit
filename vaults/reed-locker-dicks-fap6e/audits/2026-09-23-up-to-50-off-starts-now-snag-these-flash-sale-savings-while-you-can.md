# Up to 50% off starts now! 🙌 ⚡ Snag these Flash Sale savings while you can...
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-23**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S "FLASH SALE" hero (orange, black lightning bolts, "Up to 50% Off") stacked on top of the same kitchen-sink catalog scroll — clearance shoes, DSG family apparel, gloves, fan shop, fishing gear, then a second "HUNDREDS OF NEW CLEARANCE STYLES" red banner. It's two promo emails glued into one send: a flash sale up top and a full clearance catalog crammed underneath, with a text-club upsell wedged in between for good measure.
- The subject/hero combo is at least concrete about the flash sale, which is a step up from the vaguer "great news!" sends this week, but the body immediately abandons that focus and reverts to the same undifferentiated category rail this brand has run five times in the last week. There's no urgency on when the flash sale actually ends beyond tiny fine print, and the single email tries to be a flash sale, a clearance blowout, and an SMS signup pitch simultaneously.
- Judgement: functional, on-brand, no render bugs, but it's the same creative bones as the last five sends with a new headline swapped in. Fatigue is now the dominant signal in this brand's cadence.

## What's working

- Hero is bold and immediately legible — orange/black "FLASH SALE: Up to 50% Off" with a clear green "Shop Now" CTA above the fold.
- Category quick-links (Men's/Women's/Kids'/Shoes/Fan Shop/Halloween Shop) give a fast secondary navigation path right under the hero.
- Product photography is clean throughout — clearance shoe, DSG apparel models, gloves, fan shop grid all render without breakage.
- Footer has real utility (Find a Store, Best Price Guarantee, Create a Favorites List, app download links).

## What's weak

- The email tries to do three things at once: flash sale, clearance catalog, and SMS opt-in — none gets the spotlight it needs.
- No visible countdown or specific end date/time for the flash sale in the main visual — urgency is only in unreadable fine print under the hero.
- This is the fifth near-identical "up to 50% off" catalog-scroll structure this week (9/20, 9/20, 9/21, 9/21, 9/22) — same red/orange hero + category rail formula, just re-skinned headlines.
- Second clearance banner ("HUNDREDS OF NEW CLEARANCE STYLES") mid-email dilutes the flash sale framing the subject line promised.
- No loyalty/ScoreCard member pricing or personalization visible anywhere despite this being a loyalty-heavy retailer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one offer. If it's a flash sale, end the email after the flash sale hero + 2-3 relevant product rails — drop the second clearance banner and let clearance own its own send.
- 2. Put a real countdown/deadline in the hero itself (e.g., "Ends tonight at 11:59pm ET") instead of burying it in fine print — this is the single biggest lever since "flash sale" implies urgency that isn't visually reinforced.
- 3. Break the cadence — five near-identical hero/rail structures in a week trains subscribers to skim past this format. Vary layout, not just headline color.
- 4. Add loyalty pricing or a ScoreCard-specific callout since this brand has a loyalty program and none of the last several audited sends have used it.
- 5. Move the SMS opt-in block to the footer — it currently interrupts the shopping flow between two clearance sections.

- **Subject Alt B:** `⚡ 50% off flash sale — few hours left`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ends at 11:59pm ET — shoes, gear, and fan finds included`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Score 50% off before the clock runs out tonight`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Deploys cleanly via Salesforce Marketing Cloud (`em.dcsg.com`) with standard Scene7 image hosting, but fails multiple deliverability/compliance checks and ships with no meaningful plain-text fallback or image alt text.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Primary CTA link uses SFMC `mclick` redirect (`https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/mclick?params=...`) — standard tokenized tracking wrapper, resolves server-side.
- Open-tracking pixel present: `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=...` (flagged by QA as an "image missing alt text," but this is a 1x1 tracking pixel, not a content image — not a real accessibility issue).
- QA probed 12 of 63 total links (material/high-click-likelihood); 51 skipped (footer/utility/social, deprioritized). No broken links confirmed among probed set from source provided.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 10 content images missing `alt` attributes: `shoes-2026-0923-d`, `dsg-2026-0923-d`, `bbsb-2026-0923-d`, `fan-shop-2026-0923-d`, `fishing-2026-0923-d`, plus icons `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`. Screen readers will announce filenames or skip these entirely.
- Mobile responsive CSS present (`@media screen and (max-width:480px)` block with `.mobile-off`, `.mobile-on`, `.mobile-cta`, etc.) — standard MSO/Outlook conditional comments included for VML/font fallback.
- Preheader (`#ssl` block) correctly hidden with `display:none; mso-hide:all; font-size:0` pattern and padded with zero-width space entities for preview-text control.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `%%FirstName%%`, `{{...}}`) visible in the provided source — No issues found.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN]** `List-Unsubscribe` header not found — either absent or stripped before reaching the audit relay; cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe support in-header.
- **[WARN]** `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found — one-click unsubscribe compliance unconfirmed.
- **[WARN]** `Authentication-Results` header not found — SPF/DKIM pass/fail status unknown for this message as received.
- Footer unsubscribe link was not in the truncated source but QA notes "an unsubscribe link is always included" in its probe set — consistent with CAN-SPAM body-link requirement, though this doesn't substitute for the missing List-Unsubscribe header check above.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Links use SFMC's proprietary `mclick` param-encoding rather than plain UTM query strings, so UTM alignment can't be verified from source alone — would require decoding the `params=` token or checking final landing URL after redirect.
- No issues found regarding domain consistency (tracking domain `em.dcsg.com` and destination brand `dickssportinggoods.com` are consistent, expected SFMC subdomain pattern).

7. Recommendations
- Add descriptive `alt` text to the 5 content images (shoe/product category tiles) at minimum, per WCAG/ADA email accessibility norms; decorative icons can use `alt=""`.
- Investigate plain-text MIME part — 9-character fallback is effectively broken for text-only clients and spam-filter scoring; regenerate a full text version from the HTML.
- Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent by the ESP and not just dropped by the relay/audit tooling before treating this as compliant.
- Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status directly from raw headers at a receiving mailbox, since `Authentication-Results` wasn't captured here.
- Decode a sample `mclick` params token (or crawl the resolved landing URL) to confirm UTM parameters match analytics expectations on the receiving landing page.
