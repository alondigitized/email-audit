# 🚨 Score an EXTRA 25% off select clearance styles before it's too late!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- This is the clearance version of the "last day" email. It has a big red striped hero with a gold "EXTRA 25% OFF", a "Last Day" kicker, a single maroon Shop Now button and a deadline of 11/8 at 11:59PM PT. You get the offer in about a second, and the urgency is credible. It follows the Nov 7 "EXTRA 25% off select clearance? You got it!" send almost exactly, and it comes the same day as the "Last day! Shop the Holiday Deal Dash" send.
- The problem is that the email never shows a single clearance product. The hero is a typographic panel. Below it, a second type-only "UP TO 50% OFF" hero repeats the Deal Dash creative from four sends this week. Then come a 40%-off clothing block and a six-tile "holiday deals" grid. A reader who clicks for clearance gets no sense of what is on clearance, and the three different percentages dilute the "extra 25%" pitch.
- Judgement: a solid 6, a good above-the-fold hero that falls into catalog sprawl by the second scroll. It is also a fifth near-identical promo this week, so fatigue is a real cost.

## What's working

- The hero is huge and legible. "EXTRA 25% OFF" in gold on red is the first thing the eye lands on.
- The "Last Day" kicker, together with the explicit "11/8/26 11:59PM PT" deadline in the fine print, makes the urgency believable.
- The single maroon Shop Now button sits directly under the headline, with a clear "Extra 25% Off Select Clearance Styles" line above it.
- The category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 & Under, Fan Shop, All Sale) give a quick way to narrow down without scrolling.
- The render is clean. I can see no broken images, overlaps or layout bugs.

## What's weak

- No clearance product appears anywhere. The clearance hero is pure type, so there is nothing concrete to want.
- The second block repeats the "UP TO 50% OFF / Ends Tonight" Deal Dash hero, which has dominated this week's sends. It competes with the primary offer.
- Three percentages (extra 25%, up to 50%, up to 40%) in one email blur the message and make the "extra" deal feel smaller.
- Both CTAs say "Shop Now", which names neither a verb-plus-noun action nor a category.
- Below the fold, the six-tile grid (drinkware, socks, golf, NFL, team sports, fitness) has no prices or offers. The bottom half reads as filler, and the fine print ("exclusions apply," "interim markdowns may have been taken") is dense and easy to miss.
- The ScoreCard strip is tiny and low-contrast, so member value is nearly invisible.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put 3–4 real clearance products with prices (was/now, after the extra 25%) directly under the hero button. This is the single change that would lift the email the most.
- 2. Cut the Deal Dash block, or fold it into one line. One offer per send: if clearance is the story, end the email at the product grid.
- 3. Change the CTA to something specific, like "Shop Clearance Shoes" or "Take an Extra 25% Off".
- 4. Move the ScoreCard / member benefit up and make it readable, or drop it.
- 5. Trim the bottom promo grid to one row, or tie each tile to the clearance offer.
- 6. Drop the emoji and mixed caps in the subject, and lead with the deadline.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 25% off clearance ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day: clearance gets an extra 25% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shoes, apparel and gear marked down again. Ends 11:59PM PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Prices shown are already marked down. Take 25% off more before midnight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is mostly sound (`lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` layout table, viewport meta, responsive media queries). Confirmed defects are missing `alt` text on 13 images, a malformed JSON-LD block, and sub-10px mobile font sizes. The header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be evaluated because of the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- QA probed 12 material links and reported no broken or redirect-failure results. 40 links were skipped (footer, utility and social links are deprioritized), so those are unverified.
- The open-tracking pixel `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/v61000001a1bc08ddc1ad8ecef4bbe5cfc0/49c69a4056cd41e700004c5a42963aa1` has no `alt` (flagged by QA). It is hidden (`display:none; max-height:0; mso-hide:all`), so the impact is low. Adding `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` would satisfy the checker.
- No other link or tracking issues are visible in the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on 12 visible images**, per QA:
  - Hero: `2026-1108-dks-storewide-11am-1-new` (s7d6.scene7.com).
  - Category tiles: `drinkware1101stwdcondensed`, `accessories1101stwdcondensed`, `golf1101stwdcondensed`, `fanshop1101stwdcondensed`, `teamsports1101stwdcondensed`, `fitness1101stwdcondensed`.
  - Footer icons: `icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png`, `icon-location-2025.png` (dsg.images.harmony.epsilon.com).
  - If the hero and tiles are the only carriers of the offer ("EXTRA 25% off") and the category links, image-blocking clients and screen readers get no content or link purpose. Footer icons that sit beside text labels should use `alt=""`.
- **Very small mobile type** in the `@media screen and (max-width:480px)` block: `.body-sm` and `.link-sm` are 9px, `.legal` is 8px, and `.body-m` is 11px. These fall below common legibility guidance of about 14px for body text and 10–11px for legal text.
- **Stray quote in the MSO conditional CSS**: `div {background-color:inherit; font-size:0; line-height:100%; margin:0; padding:0;"}`. The trailing `"` is a syntax error. It is limited to the `gte mso 9 | IE` block, so Outlook is the client most likely to mis-parse that rule.
- **Image sizing**: Scene7 URLs request `wid=1200&hei=1600` for the hero and tiles. The truncated source doesn't show the rendered widths, so payload size can't be confirmed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The HTML is truncated, so the lower body wasn't inspected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post and Authentication-Results**: all three were "not found" in QA. QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay not capturing headers, so these are unverified rather than confirmed missing. Check the raw `.eml` or the sender's DNS for SPF, DKIM, DMARC and RFC 8058 support.
- QA states an unsubscribe link was included in the probe set and raised no failure. The footer's physical address wasn't visible in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters and landing-page content can't be assessed: the truncated source doesn't show the CTA `href`s, and QA reports no per-link UTM or redirect data. No issues are confirmed.
- **Invalid JSON-LD block** (`<script type="application/ld+json">`):
  - It contains JavaScript-style comments (`// GIF & WEBP images are not supported…`, `// DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!`). Comments are illegal in JSON, so Gmail will fail to parse the whole block and the promo-annotation feature won't work.
  - Template placeholders were left in: `"description": ""`, `"discountCode": ""`, `"availabilityEnds": ""`, `"image": ""`.
  - `"availabilityStarts": "2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"` is a stale date from 2020.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the hero and the six category tiles. These are the clickable content. Use `alt=""` on the footer icons and the tracking pixel.
2. Fix the JSON-LD: remove the `//` comments, then either populate the `DiscountOffer` and `PromotionCard` fields with current values or delete the unused objects.
3. Remove the stray `"` in the MSO conditional `div` rule.
4. Raise the mobile minimums for `.body-sm`, `.link-sm` and `.legal` (9px/9px/8px). Aim for at least 11–12px.
5. Verify the unsubscribe and authentication headers (List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, SPF, DKIM, DMARC) from the original message rather than the relay copy.
6. Re-run the audit on the untruncated HTML to check CTA `href`s for UTM tagging and the footer for the postal address.
