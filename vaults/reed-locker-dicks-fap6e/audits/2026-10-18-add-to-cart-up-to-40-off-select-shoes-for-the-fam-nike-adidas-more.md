# 🛒 Add to cart: up to 40% OFF select shoes for the fam (Nike, adidas & more!)
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-18**
## Executive summary

- This is a rerun. On 10/17 DICK'S sent "Up to 40% OFF select shoes 🙌 Save BIG on Nike, adidas & more top brands" with the same hero and the same headline, "Up to 40% Off Shoes for the Family". Only the subject line changed. A subscriber who got the first one has no reason to open this one. After the three "Extra 25% off clearance" sends on 10/16 and 10/17 and the 70% markdown email earlier today, the cadence is wearing thin.
- The email itself is competent. The hero is a big "40% OFF" over a trio of sneakers, with a clear headline and a green Shop Now button right under it. The module stack below is clean. It also has the first credible deadline of the week, in the Fan Shop block: "Final Hours," valid through 10/18 at 11:59 PM PT. The problem is length. It runs seven or eight modules and about 6,300px, and nearly every one is a variation on "up to 40–50% off." The one real deadline is buried in the middle instead of leading the email.
- Verdict: a solid, well-built send that is a repeat and is diluted by sprawl. It's a 7 on execution, but it's a low-urgency, low-novelty send that adds to inbox fatigue.

## What's working

- The hero reads in one glance: oversized "40% OFF" over three sneakers, with the headline and green Shop Now button directly below it.
- The "Final Hours" label and the explicit date and time (10/18, 11:59 PM PT) on the Fan Shop module is the only believable deadline in the email.
- The category pill row (Men's, Women's, Kids', Team Sports, Fan Shop, Halloween Shop) lets people jump straight to their segment.
- The "Check out these picks" product grid at the bottom has real named items (adidas Samba OG, Nike Club fleece, Under Armour hoodie). That's a better click target than category buttons.
- The seasonal Halloween Shop module and the ScoreCard and pickup strip at the top keep it timely and tied to the loyalty program.

## What's weak

- The shoe offer is identical to the 10/17 send, in the same hero and the same headline, so it feels recycled.
- Every module is "up to 40%" or "up to 50%" off, so no single offer stands out. A viewer has to read each one to find where the best deal is.
- The hero's "40% OFF" is stacked vertically and partly cropped by the sneaker photo, which makes it harder to read at a glance.
- The email is very long, with seven or eight stacked modules. Most readers will drop off before reaching the product picks.
- The only deadline sits mid-email on one module, and the hero says only "Limited Time." The fine print uses asterisks (the "***" on Fan Shop gear), and exclusions are tucked into small text.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the deadline. If the shoe offer ends tonight, say so in the hero ("Ends tonight") and in the subject. If it doesn't, don't run the same offer twice in two days.
- 2. Cut the body to three or four modules: shoes, one apparel module, one team/fan module, and the picks grid. Move the product picks up, since they are the strongest click targets.
- 3. Rework the hero so "40% OFF" is horizontal and fully visible, and make the CTA specific ("Shop Nike & adidas shoes" instead of "Shop Now").
- 4. Space out similar offers. Don't resend the same offer within 24 hours.
- 5. Shorten the subject so the offer and the deadline show before truncation.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: up to 40% off shoes for the family`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Nike & adidas up to 40% off, last day 👟`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus up to 50% off fitness and team gear. Sale ends 11:59 PM PT tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Kids' sneakers, running shoes and Samba OGs, all in one cart.`
