# Lucky you! Up to 50% off our best DEALS of the week is here. 📍
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-17**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S running the exact same "UP TO 50% OFF" red-gradient hero it's used repeatedly this stretch (9/12, 9/13, 9/14, 9/15), now with a green-clothing/sneaker photo swap up top and a fourth subject-line rewrite. The email itself is long and category-sprawling — Men's/Women's/Kids', Team Sports, Fan Shop, Stanley 1913 Halloween, $25 & Under clearance, then a picks carousel — which gives it broad reach but zero focus, and nothing about the layout signals urgency or specificity beyond the generic "up to 50%" banner.
- The execution is clean — no render bugs, good contrast, clear green CTA buttons throughout — but this is pure volume-over-precision marketing: one email trying to be every category at once, with a subject line ("Lucky you!") that reads like filler copy layered onto a creative DICK'S has already run into the ground this week.
- Judgment: competent production, worn-out creative, generic subject. This is a mid-pack send that will underperform its own recent higher scorers (9/14, 9/17 clearance) because the subject fails to hook and the hero offers nothing new.

## What's working

- Hero image swap (green tumblers/apparel/treadmill) gives the top of email a fresher look than the plain black-and-white "ONE DAY" spotlight reused earlier this week.
- Clear visual hierarchy: red "UP TO 50% OFF" block leads straight into a green "Shop Now" CTA.
- Category navigation pills (Men's/Women's/Kids'/Shoes/Fan Shop/Team Sports) give scanners a fast way to jump to their interest.
- $25 & Under clearance module reuses the brand's cleanest, highest-performing red hero treatment from 9/15–9/17.
- No layout breakage, broken images, or overlapping text anywhere in the render.

## What's weak

- Subject line "Lucky you!" is vague, generic filler — it doesn't reference a category, deadline, or specific product, unlike the brand's better clearance sends.
- This is essentially the same "Up to 50% Off" offer DICK'S has sent daily for a week (9/12–9/17) with no new discount tier or escalation.
- The email is extremely long and unfocused — seven+ distinct offer modules (general sale, Stanley Halloween, team sports, fan shop, clearance, picks) dilute the single hero message.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere in the visible copy — "Best Deals of the Week" implies a window but never states when it ends.
- Demographic mix in hero (couple) vs. Men's/Women's/Kids' modules below is generic stock-photo styling with no distinct personality per section.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill or heavily rewrite the subject** — "Lucky you!" adds nothing; lead with the strongest, most specific offer (clearance $25 & under, or a named category) to break from four straight days of "up to 50%" fatigue.
- 2. **Cut the email in half** — move Team Sports, Fan Shop, and the picks carousel to a follow-up send; a shorter, single-offer email will convert better than this sprawl.
- 3. **Add a real deadline** — "Best Deals of the Week" needs an actual end date/time stamp to create urgency; right now it reads open-ended.
- 4. **Rotate the hero creative concept, not just the photo** — swapping stock images while keeping the identical red "UP TO 50% OFF" block for a fifth straight day trains subscribers to skip it.
- 5. **Surface the $25 & Under clearance earlier** — it's the strongest creative in the email (per prior sends) but buried near the bottom; move it up.
- **Subject Alt A:** `$25 & under is back — plus up to 50% off everything else`
- **Subject Alt B:** `This week only: up to 50% off + new $25 clearance drops`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns on shoes, apparel, and team gear — ends soon.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Score $25-and-under clearance before it's gone.`
