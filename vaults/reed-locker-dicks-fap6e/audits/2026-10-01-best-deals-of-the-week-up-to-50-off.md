# 🙌 Best DEALS of the week = up to 50% OFF! 🙌
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-01**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S fourth "up to 50% off" send in about four days. It follows the 9/29 "INSIDE: Up to 50% off this week's deals", the two 9/30 Flash Sale emails, and the 10/1 "📢 Up to 50% OFF this week's deals is calling your name!" Only the subject changed. A subscriber would see the same offer again with an emoji swapped in.
- The render itself is clean. The hero has a soccer ball, a bottle, a model, and a bold red "UP TO 50% OFF" block, and it leads straight into "Best Deals of the Week" with one green "Shop Now" button. I don't see the clipped-headline bug from the 9/30 sends. The email then runs a long, busy stack of modules: clothing, NFL, softball bats, a golf wedge trade-in, cardio, and Halloween. Each module has its own offer and CTA, so the hero's "50% off" gets diluted. The "up to 50%" headline also isn't supported by what follows: clothing is "up to 40%", NFL gear "up to 25%", and only bats get "up to 50%".
- Judgement: well built and honest-looking, but repetitive and unfocused. It's a competent filler send, not a standout.

## What's working

- The hero is bold. A big white "50% OFF" on red is readable at a glance, with one clear "Shop Now" button right under it.
- The category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Team Sports) give fast self-segmentation.
- The NFL module has a "One Day Only" label and a specific deadline of 10/1 at 11:59 PM PT, so the urgency feels credible.
- The "Check out these picks" product grid (adidas Samba, Nike Fleece, New Balance 530) shows named products with real imagery.
- The ScoreCard bar at the top and the app-download badges near the bottom carry the loyalty message.

## What's weak

- The hero promises up to 50% off, but the modules below say up to 40% and up to 25%. That weakens the headline and the trust in it.
- The email has too many competing modules and CTAs. The hero offer has to fight the golf wedge trade-in and cardio equipment.
- The gray "Shop Now" buttons in the lower modules look low-contrast and secondary. They contrast poorly with the green hero button.
- The footer has a lot of dense fine print, and the NFL module carries an asterisk-heavy disclaimer. That cuts against the "honest offer" feel.
- Some modules are off-persona or odd for the week: softball bats, golf wedges, a Halloween shop.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop re-sending the same "up to 50%" email under new subjects. Rotate the hero offer, or skip a day. This would help most with fatigue.
- 2. Make the headline match the modules. If clothing is 40% off, say so in the hero, or put the real 50% items up front.
- 3. Cut the email to three or four modules and let the hero offer lead.
- 4. Make every module CTA the same strong color, with specific copy such as "Shop NFL gear" instead of a generic "Shop Now".
- 5. Put the "One Day Only" NFL deadline into the subject or preheader as the urgency hook.
- **Subject Alt A:** `NFL gear up to 25% off, today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 50% off bats, 40% off apparel`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ends 11:59 PM PT: NFL fan gear, plus Nike and adidas deals.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Samba, Nike fleece, and New Balance picks are marked down.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible source is well-structured: it uses `role="presentation"` tables, Outlook conditional comments, `lang="en"`, and a hidden preheader spacer. QA flagged one hard failure (a 9-character plain-text part) and about 11 images with no alt text. The header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified because the AgentMail relay doesn't capture those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque click-wrapping:** CTAs are wrapped as `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/mclick?params=b.v1.…`, with `data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"` and `class="arc-link"`. Final destinations can't be read from the source.
- **Partial link probing:** 12 material links were probed and 57 were skipped as footer, utility or social links. No broken-link or redirect failures were reported for the probed set. The skipped 57 are unverified.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=…` is present and has no alt text (see section 3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text fallback fails:** the QA check reports "Text version is 9 chars". The relay may have stripped it, but as received there is effectively no text alternative for text-only clients and spam filters.
- **Missing alt text, 11 images:**
  - **Hero and promo images:** `2026-1001-dks-stw-4pm-drip`, `-nfl`, `-bbsb`, `-fit`, and `halloween-banner-2026-0911-d`. If these carry the offers, images-off clients lose the message.
  - **Icons:** `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1` and `icon-location-2025-1`. These are likely paired with text, so `alt=""` would be acceptable if they are decorative. They should still be explicit.
  - **Tracking pixel:** `open`. This should be `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Oversized image requests:** the Scene7 URLs use `wid=1200&hei=1600&fmt=jpeg&qlt=100&fit=constrain` inside a 600px (584px inner) container. That is about 2x for retina, but `qlt=100` JPEG at 1200×1600 is a heavy payload. Lower quality (80–85) would cut weight with little visible loss.
- **Preheader slot is a leftover template placeholder:** the `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` comment sits directly above `#pht-spacer`, which contains only `&zwnj;&nbsp;` filler. The comment looks like an unfilled secondary-subject-line slot, so inboxes will show body text or alt text as the preview.
- **Minor:** `<link rel="icon">` in the `<head>` has no effect in email clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The source is truncated, so the rest is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both are not found. QA notes this may be a relay capture limitation, so it is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect. For a sender at this volume (`info@em.dcsg.com`), one-click unsubscribe is expected for Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender compliance.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment is unknown. The sending domain `em.dcsg.com` and the tracking host `data.em.dcsg.com` share the `dcsg.com` parent domain.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** these fall in the truncated portion of the source. QA says an unsubscribe link was included in probing, but no result was shown, so neither is confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable from the data provided. UTM parameters are hidden inside the encoded `mclick?params=` payloads, and no resolved landing URLs were included in the QA output. The subject line promises "up to 50% OFF" deals. Whether the landing pages match could not be checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a real plain-text part.** Make it a proper `multipart/alternative` with the offer copy and links. This is the only hard FAIL.
2. **Add alt text to all 11 images.** Use descriptive alts for the four promo tiles and the banner, `alt=""` for decorative icons, and `alt="" width="1" height="1"` for the open pixel.
3. **Fill the preheader slot** in place of `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->`, or remove the placeholder.
4. **Confirm headers at the source.** Check the raw message at the ESP or a seed inbox for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. Don't treat the relay-based WARNs as confirmed failures.
5. **Reduce image weight.** Drop `qlt=100` to about 80–85 on the Scene7 URLs.
6. **Resolve the click-tracking redirects** for the 12 probed links, and ideally the 57 skipped, to verify landing pages and UTM parameters.
