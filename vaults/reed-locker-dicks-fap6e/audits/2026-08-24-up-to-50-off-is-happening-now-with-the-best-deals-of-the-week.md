# Up to 50% OFF is happening NOW with the BEST DEALS of the week
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-24**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuinely different creative from the padlock/locker sends this brand has been running all week — a lifestyle collage of polo shirts and shorts up top, a red "UP TO 50% OFF" price tag, and a clean "Best Deals of the Week" headline with a single green "Shop Now" button. It's the strongest hero DICK'S has shown in this run so far: no lock/vault gimmick, real product photography, legible copy.
- But the strength stops at the hero. Below the fold this becomes an exhausting nine-section scroll — clothing, drinkware, team sports, golf, fan shop, outdoor, fitness, then a "Check out these picks" product grid — each with its own "Up to X% Off" and its own Shop Now button. There's no single offer to remember, no urgency, and the subject line ("BEST DEALS of the week") is the same generic superlative-stacking DICK'S has used repeatedly (8/23's "this week's DEALS," 8/20's near-identical phrasing). It reads as a catalog dump wearing one good hero image, not a targeted send.
- Judgement: a solid top, a bloated middle, and a subject that blends into the rest of this week's inbox noise. Score reflects the hero quality but is capped hard by cadence fatigue and lack of focus.

## What's working

- The hero image is a real departure from the repeated green-locker/padlock creative used on 8/16, 8/20, 8/22, and 8/23 — lifestyle shorts/polo photography reads fresher.
- "UP TO 50% OFF" red tag is bold, high-contrast, and immediately visible without scrolling.
- Primary CTA ("Shop Now") is clear, on-brand green, and sits directly under the headline.
- Category pill navigation (Men's, Women's, Kids', Clearance, Team Sports, Back-to-School) gives quick wayfinding for a scanner.
- No render bugs — images, buttons, and text all display cleanly.

## What's weak

- Nine near-duplicate "Up to X% Off [category]" sections in a row is fatiguing; there's no hierarchy telling the reader which deal matters most.
- Subject line is another generic superlative stack ("BEST DEALS of the week") — barely distinguishable from 8/23's "this week's DEALS" or 8/20's near-identical phrasing.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere in the hero or body — nothing time-bounds this offer, unlike the flash-sale sends earlier in the week.
- No loyalty/member pricing surfaced despite DICK'S ScoreCard rewards being referenced in the header ("300 Points Left") — a missed personalization opportunity.
- "Check out these picks" product grid at the bottom has no visible prices, undercutting the whole email's price-forward premise.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body from nine repetitive category sections to three max — lead with the categories most likely to convert (Clothing/Shoes, Back-to-School, Clearance) and drop drinkware/fan shop/fitness into a single combined "more deals" strip.
- 2. Add a real deadline to the hero (e.g., "Ends Sunday") — this brand's flash-sale sends already show urgency drives their better opens; this one has none.
- 3. Surface ScoreCard member pricing or bonus points on this specific sale — the header teases 300 points but the body never ties it to the offer.
- 4. Add prices to the "Check out these picks" grid — an unpriced product grid at the bottom of a percent-off email is a wasted click opportunity.
- 5. Vary the subject line pattern — stop reusing "up to 50% off is happening now" phrasing sent 8/20, 8/22, 8/23.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Back-to-school deals: up to 50% off, ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your ScoreCard points + up to 50% off, all in one place`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop shorts, kicks, and gear before the deals reset this week.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `300 points away from a reward — plus deals across every category.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Email uses a heavily-templated Epsilon/Harmony ESP build with a broken unsubscribe redirect, un-populated JSON-LD promo fields, and no verifiable authentication or list-unsubscribe headers at the relay layer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken unsubscribe redirect (confirmed FAIL):** `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/U/v61000001a03567c0df9fc402369fe94bd8/239678380ef1491c0000021ef3a0bd06` resolves to an error page — `unknown url type: '/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription'`. The preference-center redirect target is misconfigured or the subscription-management path has moved.
- All material links route through the ESP's proprietary click-tracking wrapper (`app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/v61000001.../__dU__=...`) rather than exposing destination URLs directly in markup — this is expected ESP behavior but means true destination/UTM alignment can't be confirmed from source alone (see §6).
- Tracking pixel present and correctly hidden: `<img src="https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/..." style="display:none; ...">` (open-tracking beacon, functioning as designed).
- 58 of 70 links were deprioritized/skipped by the link prober (footer/utility/social); only 12 material CTAs were probed. No broken CTA links reported among those probed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **13 images missing `alt` text**, including the SSL/open-tracking pixel, hero/category banners (clothing, golf, fanshop, outdoor, fitness), two legacy `.gif`/`.jpg` banners served from `dsg.images.harmony.epsilon.com`, and 5 footer trust/social icons. Screen readers will announce these as unlabeled or skip them entirely, and if images are blocked (common in Outlook/older clients) recipients see no fallback text.
- MSO/Outlook conditional comments present (`<!--[if (gte mso 9)|(IE)]-->`) with a **malformed inline style**: `div{...padding:0;"}` — a stray trailing `"}` inside the Outlook `<style>` block, which is invalid CSS and may cause the Outlook-specific rule block to fail parsing in some renderers.
- Responsive rules under `@media screen and (max-width:480px)` look complete for mobile typography/spacing classes (`.header-xl`, `.body-sm`, `.p-*` utilities) — no issues found there.
- Web font uses `@font-face` inside `@media screen` with `local()`/`format('woff2')` fallbacks and `font-display:swap` — standard, correctly guarded from Outlook (won't attempt to load in MSO).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The `schema.org` `DiscountOffer` and `PromotionCard` JSON-LD blocks contain **empty/unfilled template fields**: `"description": ""`, `"discountCode": ""`, `"availabilityEnds": ""`, `"image": ""`. These are Gmail Promotions-tab annotation fields left blank — likely a template default that wasn't populated by the send system, so Gmail can't render the promo card/annotation for this send.
- `"availabilityStarts": "2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"` is a **stale hardcoded date** (nearly 6 years old relative to send date) left over from the template boilerplate — not updated per campaign.
- No visible customer-facing merge tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}`) in the truncated source to evaluate for fallback behavior; nothing else to flag here.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- Unsubscribe link is present in the footer (satisfies CAN-SPAM's requirement for a visible opt-out mechanism) but **is functionally broken** (see §2) — this is a compliance risk since the mechanism must actually work, not just be present.
- `List-Unsubscribe` header **not detected** and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click) **not detected** — flagged as WARN because these may simply not have survived the AgentMail relay rather than being absent at origin; cannot confirm true header state from this data alone.
- `Authentication-Results` header **not detected** via the relay — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown from available data; recommend checking headers at the originating MTA rather than post-relay.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All destination URLs are obscured behind the Epsilon/Harmony redirect wrapper (`/T/{listId}/{linkId}/...?__dU__=...`) in the truncated source provided — no raw destination URLs or UTM query strings are visible to audit directly.
- Cannot confirm UTM parameter presence/consistency or landing-page alignment with the available (truncated) HTML; would need either the un-truncated source or resolved redirect targets to complete this section.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the unsubscribe redirect** — `/U/...` currently 404s to `/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription`; this is a compliance-critical bug and should be prioritized over any other finding here.
2. Populate or strip the empty `DiscountOffer`/`PromotionCard` JSON-LD fields (`description`, `discountCode`, `availabilityEnds`, `image`) and update the stale `availabilityStarts` date — currently non-functional boilerplate.
3. Add `alt` attributes to all 13 flagged images, particularly the category banners and footer trust icons, for accessibility and blocked-image fallback.
4. Remove the stray `"}` inside the Outlook conditional `<style>` block (`div{...padding:0;"}`) to avoid malformed CSS in MSO rendering.
5. Confirm at the originating MTA (not just post-relay) that `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/`Authentication-Results` headers are actually being set — current absence may be a relay artifact, but should be verified given finding #1.
6. Request an untruncated HTML source or resolved redirect map to complete the UTM/landing-page continuity check (§6), which couldn't be evaluated from the data provided.
