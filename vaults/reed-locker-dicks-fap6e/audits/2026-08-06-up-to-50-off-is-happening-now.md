# Up to 50% off is happening now 🙌
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- This is the same blue pool/beach-ball "50% off" creative DICK'S has now run three times in five days — it debuted 8/2 as "Up to 50% off is waiting," came back 8/4 as "Check out this week's DEALS 💰 Up to 50% off is happening now!," and reappears today with an almost identical subject line. The execution itself is clean: high-contrast hero, tidy sectioned layout, a real deadline buried further down in the Epic Shoe Sale block, and a loyalty nod up top.
- The problem is stacking, not craft. Nine separate offer blocks (shoes, underwear, shorts, water bottles, gloves, putters, back-to-school, picks) sit under a headline that promises "50% off," but most of what follows is 25-45% off — the hero overpromises relative to the body. And a subscriber who opened Monday's or Tuesday's nearly-identical email has already seen this exact visual and near-exact subject twice this week.
- Judgment: well-built individual send, but it's creative reuse dressed as a fresh promo — a subscriber who's been getting DICK'S daily will register this as the third "50% off" email of the week, not a new deal.

## What's working

- Bold, high-contrast "UP TO 50% OFF" hero box pops against the blue pool backdrop — the eye lands there first with no competition.
- Clean, consistently formatted product blocks (image, headline, one-line detail, "SHOP NOW" button) repeated for every category.
- A real, specific deadline is visible ("Valid through 8/12/26 11:59PM PT" under Epic Shoe Sale) — not vague "limited time" language.
- Loyalty touchpoints appear twice: ScoreCard callout at the very top and a "Sign Up for Perks" module near the footer.
- No render bugs — no broken images, overlapping text, or placeholder copy anywhere in the send.

## What's weak

- The hero creative is a near-exact repeat of the 8/2 and 8/4 sends — same pool/beach-ball art, same blue overlay box, third time in five days.
- The subject line duplicates 8/4's phrasing almost word for word ("...Up to 50% off is happening now!" → "Up to 50% off is happening now 🙌").
- The "This Week's Deals" CTA directly under the hero has no product or category attached — it's a generic click into an undefined 50%-off umbrella.
- Nine stacked offer blocks dilute the headline promise — most deals shown are 25-45% off, not the 50% advertised up top.

## Recommendations

- 1. Retire the blue beach-ball hero — it's the third outing in five days and both the visual and the subject are now recognizable as a repeat, which trains subscribers to skip.
- 2. Tie the "50% off" claim in the hero to the specific category it actually applies to, so the headline number matches what's below it instead of reading as bait for a broader sale.
- 3. Move the deadline copy that's already written for Epic Shoe Sale ("Valid through 8/12/26 11:59PM PT") up into the hero itself — it's the one piece of real urgency in the email and it's buried mid-scroll.
- 4. Cut the email from nine offer blocks to three or four, with a "See all deals" link for the rest — the current length buries the headline offer under low-priority items like the Owala bottle launch.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit — DICK'S Sporting Goods "Up to 50% off" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
Email is built on Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) tracking infrastructure with Epsilon Harmony-hosted images; automated QA shows a 43% pass rate driven by a broken unsubscribe link, missing email authentication headers, and 10 images lacking alt text.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All probed links route through SFMC click-tracking redirects (`https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/v610000019fd69ffff48aa69e6e96c660c0/...`), consistent with standard SFMC `%%[ ]%%` personalization/tracking wrapping.
- **Broken unsubscribe redirect (FAIL):** the unsubscribe link (`https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/U/v610000019fd69ffff48aa69e6e96c660c0/337fe157e3ab4f9c0000021ef3a0bcff`) resolves to an SFMC error page: `unknown url type: '/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription'`. The preference-center URL mapping behind this tracking alias appears misconfigured.
- QA probed 12 of 62 material links (50 skipped per click-likelihood ranking); no other link errors reported among probed links.
- Invalid JSON in the `application/ld+json` block: it contains `//` line comments (e.g., `// GIF & WEBP images are not supported...`, `// DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!`), which is not valid JSON and will cause structured-data parsers (and any strict `JSON.parse` consumer, e.g. Gmail annotations) to fail silently or reject the block.
- The `DiscountOffer` and `PromotionCard` JSON-LD objects have empty required-looking fields (`"description":""`, `"discountCode":""`, `"image":""`), indicating template placeholders were not populated before send.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **10 images missing `alt` text** (QA WARN), affecting screen readers and image-blocked clients: SSL/open-tracking pixel, `underwear.jpg`, `shorts.jpg`, `owala.png` banner, `gloves.jpg`, `callaway.jpg`, and 4 footer icons (`icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png`, `icon-location-2025.png`).
- Malformed inline CSS in the Outlook/MSO conditional block: `div {background-color:inherit; font-size:0; line-height:100%; margin:0; padding:0;"}` — a stray `"` before the closing `}` is invalid CSS syntax and may cause MSO/Word rendering engines to drop or misparse the rule.
- Mobile responsive rules are gated behind `@media screen and (max-width:480px)`, standard for Outlook.com/mobile webmail; no structural issues found in the truncated markup.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No merge/personalization tokens (e.g., AMPscript `%%...%%`, Handlebars `{{ }}`) are present in the visible HTML — links use static SFMC tracking IDs rather than visible dynamic tokens, so token-resolution failures can't be confirmed from source alone.
- No issues found in the visible source, but note the source is truncated — token usage in the omitted body content (offer blocks, product grids) could not be audited.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe link is broken** (see §2) — this is a functional CAN-SPAM risk since the required unsubscribe mechanism does not resolve.
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** (QA WARN) — may be a relay capture gap (AgentMail) rather than absence at origin; cannot confirm from HTML alone.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found** (QA WARN) — one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) cannot be confirmed as supported.
- **Authentication-Results header not found** (QA WARN) — SPF/DKIM pass/fail status is unknown via the AgentMail relay; cannot verify authentication compliance from available data.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- All observed CTA/logo links terminate in SFMC redirect URLs (`app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...`), which forward to final landing pages not visible in the truncated source — final destination domain, path, and any UTM parameters could not be verified.
- No UTM parameters are visible on the redirect URLs themselves (SFMC typically appends them post-redirect); landing-page alignment cannot be confirmed with the data provided.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the SFMC unsubscribe alias/preference-center mapping so `/U/...` no longer resolves to `/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription` — this is the single confirmed FAIL and a compliance risk.
2. Remove `//` comments from the `application/ld+json` block or move them outside the script tag; populate the empty `discountCode`, `description`, and `image` fields or remove the incomplete `DiscountOffer`/`PromotionCard` objects before send.
3. Add descriptive (or empty `alt=""` for purely decorative) attributes to the 10 flagged images, prioritizing the product images (`underwear.jpg`, `shorts.jpg`, `owala.png`, `gloves.jpg`, `callaway.jpg`) for accessibility.
4. Fix the stray `"` in the MSO conditional `div` CSS rule.
5. Confirm with the sending platform/ESP whether `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` headers are present at origin — if the AgentMail relay is stripping them, that's a relay capture issue rather than a sender defect and should be validated against raw SMTP headers.
