# Stop scrolling! Up to 60% off select clearance is here 👉
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S ninth-ish send in roughly four days, and it shows. The creative has pivoted back to evergreen clearance framing — nearly identical to yesterday's "Up to 60% off select clearance" send that scored 8/10 — with a different subject but the same skeleton. The hero wordplay "CLEARANCE SCORES!" is genuinely clever for a sporting goods brand, and the sectioned gender/category layout is clean and scannable. What undermines it: the subject leads with "60% off" but the majority of the email body shows 40% off (the 60% is confined to Outdoor Essentials, well below the fold). That's a bait-and-switch feeling that erodes trust, even if technically accurate. The email also stuffs a Memorial Day Event banner mid-scroll that reads like a second email was stapled to the bottom — it breaks flow and creates cognitive load. A "Submit Email" CTA inside an email already received by a subscriber is baffling.
- The bones of this template work. The execution is fatigued — too many sends, too little differentiation, and an offer mismatch between subject and body.

## What's working

- **"CLEARANCE SCORES!" hero** — clean sports pun, reads instantly, dominant visual.
- **Gender/category split** — Men's, Women's, Kids', Golf, Outdoor Essentials are logically sequenced and give the reader a fast path to their lane.
- **Hero CTA placement** — SHOP NOW is visible above the fold without scrolling.
- **Memorial Day seasonal hook** — anchoring to a real shopping moment (Memorial Day) gives the email a credible time peg that pure clearance lacks.

## What's weak

- **Subject-to-body offer mismatch** — "60% off" is the lead claim but 40% off dominates four of five category sections; the honest headline would be "Up to 40% off."
- **"Stop scrolling!" opener is spent** — borrowed from influencer-speak, wastes 16 characters, and signals desperation from a brand that doesn't need it.
- **All CTAs are "SHOP NOW"** — no specificity, no verb-noun pairing that matches the section ("Shop Clearance Shoes" or "Gear Up for Memorial Day" would outperform).
- **"Submit Email" section for an existing subscriber** — asking a recipient who is already on your list to "submit" their email is a broken experience.
- **Memorial Day block feels grafted on** — the visual language (red/blue/stars) is jarring after a green-toned clearance creative; it reads as two separate emails glued together.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Align the subject to the body's actual lead offer.** If 40% off is what most sections deliver, lead with that — or move the 60% Outdoor Essentials block above the fold and make it the hero. The mismatch is the single biggest trust problem.
- 2. **Kill "Stop scrolling!"** — open with the offer. Every character before "Up to" is noise.
- 3. **Give each SHOP NOW a category noun** — "Shop Men's Clearance," "Shop Outdoor Deals" — takes one word, doubles click intent signal.
- 4. **Treat Memorial Day as a separate send or give it its own campaign lane.** Don't bury it below five category blocks.
- 5. **Remove the Submit Email prompt** — redirect that module to a loyalty/ScoreCard sign-in upsell or a product spotlight instead.
- 6. **Pull back send cadence.** Nine emails in four days burns the list. Consider a 48-hour gap before the next blast.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Memorial Day clearance: 40–60% off starts now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Tents, shoes, clothing — score deals before they're gone`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `60% off camping, 40% off kicks — clearing it all for the holiday`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — DICK'S Sporting Goods Clearance Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email uses a standard MSO-conditional table layout with correct `role="presentation"` usage and mobile media queries. Two confirmed structural defects exist: malformed JSON-LD structured data (invalid syntax + stale/empty template fields) and a discount-percentage mismatch between the subject line and preheader.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Open-tracking pixel** — present and correctly suppressed via `display:none; max-height:0px; overflow:hidden; mso-hide:all`. No `alt=""` attribute, which is a minor omission but harmless for a hidden element.

**ESP redirect wrapping** — all CTA links route through `app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/v610000019e379291f0a7645c369fe94bd8/...`. Cannot confirm downstream UTM parameters without resolving the redirect chain (see §6).

**Logo `src` is truncated in the provided source** (`https://dsg…`). If this reflects the actual send, the logo will be broken. Verify against the live send source — likely an artifact of source truncation provided for this audit.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

No critical rendering failures found. Positives:

- `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` override is present — prevents iOS from auto-linking phone numbers/addresses.
- MSO conditional XML + table-collapse overrides are correct.
- `border-collapse:separate` on tables is intentional for `border-radius` support.
- Google Fonts loaded via `@font-face` with `font-display:swap` inside `@media screen` — correctly scoped so Outlook/MSO never attempts the external font fetch.

Minor issue: the `<img>` tracking pixel at `#ssl` has no `alt=""` attribute. Should be `alt=""` for strict accessibility conformance, even though the element is visually hidden.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

The table ID `header-non-sc-member` confirms variant-based personalization is in use (ScoreCard member vs. non-member), which is expected.

**Unfilled JSON-LD template fields** — the `DiscountOffer` and `PromotionCard` schema objects contain empty placeholder values that were never populated:

```json
"description": "",
"discountCode": "",
"availabilityEnds": "",
```

```json
"image": ""   // PromotionCard
```

**Stale hardcoded date** — `"availabilityStarts": "2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"` is six years in the past. This field was never updated from a template base.

No dynamic merge tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `%%firstname%%`) are visible in the preheader or body — acceptable for a broadcast clearance send, but the empty JSON-LD fields indicate the template automation did not complete.

---

### 5. Compliance

**CAN-SPAM / unsubscribe** — the footer is not present in the truncated source. Cannot confirm unsubscribe link or physical mailing address inclusion. These are required; verify in the full send.

**Sending domain** — `e.dcsg.com` (subdomain of dcsg.com). SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment cannot be verified from HTML alone; authentication headers must be confirmed via received message headers (`Authentication-Results` field).

**Stale comment** — `<!--DKS Email Marketing 2025-->` in a May 2026 send. Not a compliance issue but indicates the template header was not updated this year.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**Subject/preheader discount discrepancy (confirmed):**

- Subject line: *"Up to 60% off select clearance"*
- Preheader (`#ssl` cell): *"Up to 40% off select clothing & shoes"*

These are different claims for the same send. This is the most operationally significant technical finding — it creates message inconsistency and may trigger spam-classifier flags for misleading subject lines.

**UTM parameters** — all CTA links are ESP-wrapped (`app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...`). Whether `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` are appended post-redirect cannot be confirmed from the HTML source alone. The redirect chain must be resolved to verify landing page attribution is intact.
