# Have you seen this? Extra 25% OFF select clearance styles is yours! 💰 ➡️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-16**
## Executive summary

- This is the same "Extra 25% off select clearance" send DICK'S ran earlier today ("📥 Get to shopping! EXTRA 25% off select clearance styles is in! 📥"), with a new subject line. The hero is a huge white "EXTRA 25% OFF" on a red leaf background. The offer reads in under a second, and the "Limited Time" tag, the headline and the green Shop Now button sit right below it. The top of the email is strong.
- Below that, the email turns into a catch-all. It has Nike shoes at up to 40% off, $50 & under clothing, a Minecraft collection, NFL fan gear, fitness equipment and the Halloween shop. That is six or seven competing messages, and most of them are not the clearance offer from the subject. The "Valid online only through 10/17/26" line is small grey text. The only real urgency is that two-day window, and it is easy to miss.
- My judgement is a solid, loud hero on a repeat creative. The subject is the weakest part, and the cadence is the real problem. This is the second near-identical clearance send in one day, after a week of clearance and flash-sale variants on 10/13, 10/14 and 10/15.

## What's working

- The "EXTRA 25% OFF" hero is huge, high-contrast and readable at a glance.
- The "Limited Time" tag, headline and green Shop Now button form a clear hero-to-CTA path.
- Category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 & Under, Fan Shop, All Sale) let shoppers jump straight to their area.
- Product tiles with specific offers, such as "Up to 40% Off Select Nike Shoes", give concrete secondary hooks.
- The layout renders cleanly with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The hero's "Valid online only through 10/17/26" deadline sits in small grey text, so the urgency is buried.
- The body drifts into Minecraft, NFL, fitness and Halloween blocks that have nothing to do with extra clearance savings. The 25% offer is diluted.
- This is the second near-identical send today, so the creative and offer are stale.
- The "Check out these picks" product grid shows no prices or sale badges, so it doesn't reinforce the discount.
- The fine print is dense: "select styles only", exclusions, and a restriction on combining with other sales.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one offer. Keep the hero, then show 4–6 clearance products with strike-through and sale prices. Drop the Minecraft, NFL and fitness blocks from a clearance send.
- 2. Put the deadline in the hero: "Ends 10/17" on the graphic or next to the button, in a bold, large font.
- 3. Stop sending the same creative twice in one day. Suppress anyone who got this morning's version, or swap in a different hero.
- 4. Change the button copy from "Shop Now" to "Shop Clearance".
- 5. Fix the subject line. "Have you seen this?" is filler, and the 💰 ➡️ emoji pair adds nothing.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 25% off clearance ends tomorrow`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Clearance just got cheaper: extra 25% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Nike, adidas & more. Online only through 10/17.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shoes, apparel and gear. Extra 25% off select styles, two days only.`

# Technical Audit: DICK'S | Clearance (2026-10-16)

## 1. Technical Summary
The markup is structurally sound: `lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` tables, a viewport meta tag, MSO fallbacks and a hidden preheader. Its main confirmed defect is a near-empty plain-text part (9 chars), along with widespread missing alt text on content images. The HTML supplied is truncated, so the footer and most links could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links were reported. 12 material links were probed and 57 were skipped as lower priority (footer, utility, social), so most of the link set is unverified.
- The open-tracking pixel (`https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=…`, 1×1) is first in `<body>`, outside the layout table. It has no `alt`, which is why QA flags it. This is harmless for rendering, but it shows up in the alt-text warnings as noise.
- I could not verify click-tracking wrappers or the unsubscribe URL, because they fall in the truncated or skipped portion.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images (confirmed by QA).** These are the Scene7 images, which look like primary content blocks:
  - `2026-1016-dks-swd-4pm-footwear`
  - `2026-1016-dks-swd-4pm-shorts`
  - `minecraft-2026-1016-d`
  - `2026-1016-dks-swd-4pmjersey`
  - `2026-1016-dks-swd-4pm-fitness-new`
  - `halloween-banner-2026-0911-d`
  
  If these images are blocked, the offer content is lost. Screen readers will announce filenames or nothing.
- **Missing alt text on icons.** `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1` and `icon-location-2025-1` have none. Use `alt=""` if they are decorative, or real text if they carry meaning.
- **Image weight.** Scene7 URLs request `wid=1200&hei=1600&qlt=100&fmt=jpeg` for a container capped at `max-width:584px/600px`. That is about 2× over-sized at maximum JPEG quality, which adds download weight on mobile.
- **Editor artifacts left in production markup.**
  - `<body class="is-html-mode" data-contenteditable="true">` is a leftover from the authoring tool.
  - A `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` template comment sits inside `td#ssl`.
  
  Neither breaks rendering.
- **What passes.** `<meta name="viewport">`, `lang="en"`, presentation roles, Outlook `mso` conditionals, a web-font fallback stack (`Archivo, Arial, sans-serif`) and `font-display:swap` are all present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or headers. The truncated body is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text part (FAIL).** The text version is 9 chars, effectively empty. A multipart/alternative with no real text part raises spam-filter risk and gives text-only clients and assistive tools nothing to read.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058)** were not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**, not a proven sender defect. Check the raw message headers at the source.
- **`Authentication-Results`** is absent for the same relay reason, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sending domain is `em.dcsg.com`. The visible From address (`info@em.dcsg.com`) and the tracking host (`data.em.dcsg.com`) are on the same domain.
- A body unsubscribe link and postal address could not be confirmed in the truncated source. QA says an unsubscribe link was included in probing, but no result was reported.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters could not be evaluated. The visible portion has no campaign links, and the probe results don't list final URLs.
- **Stale asset reference (possible).** `halloween-banner-2026-0911-d` has a 09-11 date stamp, while the other creatives are 10-16. The asset may be reused or outdated. Confirm it is intended.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a real multipart plain-text alternative that includes the offer, key links and the unsubscribe link (currently 9 chars).
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the five Scene7 content images and the stale Halloween banner. Add `alt=""` to the decorative icons and the 1×1 open pixel.
3. Inspect raw headers outside the relay to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment.
4. Re-run the QA with the full HTML and a wider link probe to cover UTMs, click-tracking and footer compliance.
5. Request Scene7 images at about 2× the displayed width (for example `wid=1168`, `qlt=80`) to cut payload.
6. Strip the editor-only attributes (`class="is-html-mode"`, `data-contenteditable`) and the leftover SSL template comment from the production template.
