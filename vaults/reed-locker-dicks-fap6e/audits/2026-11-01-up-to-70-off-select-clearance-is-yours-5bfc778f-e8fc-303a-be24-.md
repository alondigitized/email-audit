# Up to 70% off select clearance is YOURS
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean clearance send with one loud idea. A red hero with a giant gold "UP TO 70% OFF" sits above a red "Shop Now" button, so the eye lands on the offer immediately. It's the fourth clearance-flavored DICK'S email in about two days, and the 10/31 "Ends. Tonight!" and "Extra 25% off" sends already hit the same theme. The 70% headline is a bigger number than the earlier 25% extra, but there is no deadline, only "Limited Time" in small red text.
- Below the hero, the email drifts into a generic catalog: running shoes, Nike fleece, a Stanley collection, Callaway golf balls, BOGO socks, NCAA gear, a text-alerts banner and a row of picks. The Stanley, golf and socks blocks have nothing to do with "clearance", so the 70% promise gets diluted. The mix of "Up to 40% off", "Sale $29.98" and BOGO also muddies what the headline offer applies to.
- Overall it's solid but ordinary. The hero works, and everything after it is a long scroll with too many equal-weight CTAs.

## What's working

- The hero is huge and high-contrast, with "UP TO 70% OFF" in gold on red and a "Shop Now" button directly beneath it.
- The category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', Fan Shop, All Sale) give quick navigation into clearance.
- The specific price callouts ("Sale $29.98 Men's Under Armour Waffle Hoodie") make the deal concrete.
- The "Order Today. Sport It Tomorrow." banner with same-day delivery adds a useful reason to buy now.
- The product imagery is crisp and the layout renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlaps.

## What's weak

- The urgency is weak. "Limited Time" has no date, which is notably softer than the "Ends. Tonight!" send the day before.
- The email is very long, with about 10 CTAs, so the clearance message gets buried.
- The Stanley, Callaway and BOGO blocks are full-price or off-theme and dilute the clearance focus.
- "Up to 70%" is a ceiling, and the lower blocks show only "up to 40% off". The hero overpromises relative to the products shown.
- The Text Alerts "$20 off $100+" banner competes with the main offer and pulls attention away at the bottom.
- The fine print is dense and pushed to the bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero plus 3 or 4 clearance-only product blocks, with real sale prices on every item. Drop the Stanley, golf and text-alert blocks from this send.
- 2. Add a real deadline to the hero, such as "Ends Sunday", with a countdown or date to replace "Limited Time".
- 3. Show the actual 70%-off item in the hero or right below it, so the headline is proven by a product.
- 4. Move the text-alerts promo to a footer strip or a separate send.
- 5. Put the key terms (exclusions, "select styles") next to the button instead of at the bottom.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Clearance up to 70% off — ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Nike, Under Armour & more: up to 70% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Hoodies from $29.98, running shoes up to 40% off. Same-day pickup available.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Select clearance ends Sunday. Shop top brands before sizes sell out.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally sound (`lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` tables, MSO conditionals, a hidden preheader spacer). The one confirmed failure is a near-empty plain-text part (9 chars), and 12 images have no `alt` attribute. The authentication and unsubscribe header results are inconclusive because of the capture method.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage is partial.** 12 links were probed and 56 skipped, and no failures were reported for the probed set. The skipped links (footer, utility, social) are unverified, so I can't confirm or rule out broken links there.
- **The open pixel has no `alt`.** It is `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1.…&configId=512ffb51-…`, at 1×1 and placed as the first child of `<body>`. The tracking host (`data.em.dcsg.com`) shares the sender's organizational domain (`em.dcsg.com`), which is good for alignment.
- **Editor artifacts leaked into production.** `<body class="is-html-mode" data-contenteditable="true">` is harmless to rendering but shows the template editor's state was not stripped.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on 12 images (QA WARN).** These are:
  - the open pixel;
  - hero/product images: `2026-1101-dks-swd-6am-shoes`, `…-6amhoodie`, `…-6am-fleece`, `…-6am-new-golf`, `…-6am-ncaa-new`, `holiday-2026-1027-d-1` and `1030-dks-accessories-cozy-cabin-banner`;
  - the footer icons `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1` and `icon-location-2025-1`.
  
  With images blocked or read by a screen reader, the promotional images convey nothing. A missing `alt` also lets some clients show the filename or src. The pixel and any purely decorative icons should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Image weight.** Scene7 URLs request `wid=1200&hei=1600&fit=constrain&qlt=100`. The images render in a ~584px container, and maximum JPEG quality at 2× width or more adds payload, which matters for load time and Gmail's clipping threshold on image-heavy sends.
- **Preheader container.** `<td id="ssl" … height="0" style="… font-size:0; line-height:0; height:0;">` holds the visible-text preheader "Get $20 off $100+ when you sign up for Text Alerts**". The text itself is not wrapped in a hidden element (`display:none` / `mso-hide:all` is applied only to the sibling `#pht-spacer`), so clients that ignore `font-size:0` on a `td` could show it. I can't confirm this without a render test.
- **Non-blocking items.** The Archivo `@font-face` has a fallback stack (`Archivo,Arial,sans-serif`). The `<title>` is generic ("DICK'S Sporting Goods").

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible HTML contains no unresolved merge tokens (e.g. `{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`). The HTML is truncated, so the footer and lower modules were not inspected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text part (QA FAIL).** The text version is 9 chars. For a multipart email this is effectively no usable alternative. It hurts spam scoring and text-only or accessibility clients, and any required unsubscribe link or postal address is absent from that part.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found (WARN).** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these, so this is **inconclusive** and not evidence of a sender error. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require RFC 8058 one-click for large senders, so it should be verified against raw headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found (WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, again attributed to the relay. This can't be confirmed from the data given.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address.** An unsubscribe link is always included in link probing, but its result isn't in the data provided, and the postal address is in the truncated portion. Neither can be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues found, but this can't be fully assessed. The visible HTML is truncated before the primary CTA hrefs, and no probed-link URLs or redirect chains were provided, so UTM parameters, landing page matching and the "Text Alerts" offer destination are unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a real plain-text alternative** that mirrors the offer, key links, unsubscribe URL and postal address. This is the only hard QA failure.
2. **Add `alt` text to all 7 promotional images**, such as "Up to 70% off clearance shoes". Set `alt=""` on the tracking pixel and decorative icons, and use descriptive `alt` text for any icon that is the only label on a link.
3. **Check raw headers** (not the relay copy) for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass for `em.dcsg.com`.
4. **Wrap the preheader text** in a hidden element (`display:none; mso-hide:all; max-height:0; overflow:hidden`) rather than relying on `font-size:0` on the `td`.
5. **Reduce image weight.** Use `qlt` of about 75–85 and `wid=1200` only where a 2× asset is needed.
6. **Strip editor artifacts** (`class="is-html-mode"`, `data-contenteditable="true"`) from the production template.
7. **Re-run link probing with the full HTML** and the footer links to confirm UTM consistency and the unsubscribe endpoint.
