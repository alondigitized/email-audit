# 📢 Last day! Shop the Holiday Deal Dash (up to 50% OFF) 💸
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth "Holiday Deal Dash" send in the past few days. The hero is the same red striped panel with a giant gold "UP TO 50% OFF", and a "Last Day" kicker sits above it. The offer lands instantly, and a single red Shop Now button sits right under the headline. The urgency is the best thing here, since "Last Day" is a credible reason to act.
- The body is the usual DICK'S catalog. After the hero come a second "EXTRA 25% OFF" clearance panel, Brooks running shoes, Nike fleece, drinkware, Callaway golf balls and basketball hoops. Then there is a six-item picks grid and a long footer. The second panel competes with the hero. It is the same red and gold treatment with a different offer, so the "last day" message gets diluted. Nothing here is aimed at a specific shopper, and the picks grid is generic.
- Judgement: a competent, urgent top and a sprawling bottom, in line with the other sends this week (6/10 on 11/7). The "Last Day" framing helps it a little, but this is still a catch-all.

## What's working

- The giant gold "UP TO 50% OFF" on red is unmissable and reads at once.
- "Last Day" in red above "Holiday Deal Dash: Up to 50% Off" adds real urgency.
- One red Shop Now button sits directly under the hero headline, so the primary CTA is clear.
- The category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Holiday Shop) give quick navigation.
- The ScoreCard bar at the top surfaces the loyalty points and rewards.

## What's weak

- The "EXTRA 25% OFF" clearance panel repeats the hero's look and splits attention between two big offers.
- The hero says "Up to 50% off" for "brands you love", but the subhead names no brands or products, so it stays vague.
- Fine print is stacked under each hero ("Valid online only through 11/8/26 11:59PM PT… Offer may not be valid in conjunction…"). It is dense and a little exclusionary.
- The body runs about 5,600px with seven or more modules and a picks grid. The Brooks, Nike, drinkware, golf and hoops sections are unrelated, so no one product is the hero.
- The CTAs are all a generic "Shop Now", and the picks grid has no prices or discounts.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero plus one secondary module. Drop the second red panel or fold the clearance offer into a thin banner. Doing this would lift the email more than anything else.
- 2. Make the deadline specific, for example "Ends tonight 11:59 PM PT", and put it in the hero itself. Right now it sits in the fine print.
- 3. Name two or three hero products with prices, such as the $124.98 Brooks, so the 50% claim feels real.
- 4. Make the CTAs specific, such as "Shop deals ending tonight" or "Shop running shoes".
- 5. Add sale prices to the picks grid, and personalize it by category if possible.

- **Missing alt text (QA WARN, 11 images):**
  - Hero and content images: `2026-1108-dks-swd-6am-shoes`, `1106-stw-6am-50-nike-fleece`, `1106fruitpunchsecondary-1`, `2026-1108-dks-swd-6am-balls`, `2026-1108-dks-swd-6am-hoops`.
