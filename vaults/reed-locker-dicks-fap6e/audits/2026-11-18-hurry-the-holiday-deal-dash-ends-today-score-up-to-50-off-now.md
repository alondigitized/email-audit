# Hurry, the Holiday Deal Dash ends today! 🏃‍♀️💰 Score up to 50% off NOW
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent, well-built DICK'S flash-sale email. The red-and-gold "UP TO 50% OFF" hero, the "One Day Only" tag, and a single "Shop Now" button make the offer unmissable. The subject's "ends today" is backed by the fine print ("Valid online only through 11/18/26 11:59PM PT"), so the urgency is credible.
- The weakness is the length and the repetition. The email stacks nine or more modules after the hero: shoes, a fleece, a henley, baseball bats, golf balls, table tennis tables, a second "UP TO 70% OFF" hero, and a gift grid. The 70% clearance block is the same creative DICK'S ran on 11/16 ("Limited time! ⏰ Up to 70% OFF select clearance styles") and 11/17 ("Up to 70% OFF select clearance? Yes, please!"). A subscriber who has seen four or five sale sends in three days will see the same offers again and skim past. Two competing "UP TO" heroes also split attention, and the 70% block undercuts the "50% off" the subject promises.
- Overall it is a solid 6-7 send that is held back by cadence and sprawl, not by execution.

## What's working

- The hero is big and high-contrast, with a clear "Up to 50% Off" headline and one red "Shop Now" button.
- "One Day Only" matches the subject's "ends today", and the deadline is stated in the fine print.
- The category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Holiday Shop) give a quick way to navigate.
- The shoes module ("New Markdowns on Shoes, up to 30% off Nike, HOKA, adidas") is specific and brand-named.
- Modules alternate image left and right, with a seasonal holiday-themed feel and actual sale prices ($59.98 henley, $269.98 table).

## What's weak

- The email is very long, with about nine modules after the hero, so there is no clear second action.
- The second "UP TO 70% OFF" hero repeats the 11/16 and 11/17 clearance creative and competes with the 50% headline.
- Every CTA says "Shop Now", which is generic, and the hero button doesn't name a category.
- The shoes module sits below the first fold, even though footwear is the strongest, most specific offer.
- The subject is long, has two emojis, and ends in "NOW", which reads as pushy. The preheader is not visible in the render, so I can't confirm it adds anything.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body to the hero plus 3-4 modules, and lead with shoes as the first module under the hero.
- 2. Drop the second 70% hero, or merge it into one line ("Plus up to 70% off clearance") so there is a single offer story.
- 3. Give the CTAs specific copy, such as "Shop Shoes Up to 30% Off" and "Shop the Deal Dash".
- 4. Add a visible countdown timer, like the "Final Hours" bar on 11/16, to make the deadline more concrete.
- 5. Cap the subject near 50 characters with one emoji, and make sure the preheader adds new information.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Deal Dash ends tonight: up to 50% off + shoes from 30% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `⏰ Last call: 50% off Holiday Deal Dash, midnight PT`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns on Nike, HOKA & adidas, plus up to 70% off clearance. Online only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Ends 11:59 PM PT. Free same-day pickup on thousands of styles.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's visible HTML (truncated) is structurally sound: it has `lang="en"`, a `<title>`, a viewport meta tag, MSO conditional comments, `role="presentation"` layout tables and a hidden preheader spacer. Two automated QA findings are real, though: a plain-text part of only 9 characters (FAIL) and 11 content or icon images flagged for missing alt text. Header-level checks (auth, List-Unsubscribe) could not be assessed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links were reported. 12 material links were probed and 51 were skipped by the click-likelihood ranking, so about 80% of links are unverified.
- The open-tracking pixel is `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1.…&configId=512ffb51-…`. It has `width="1" height="1"` but no `alt`, no `style="display:block"` and no `border="0"`. It sits directly after `<body>`, before the layout table.
- Links carry `class="arc-link"` and `data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"`, which suggests tracked or redirected links. The truncated source doesn't show the final hrefs, so I can't confirm their destinations.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** 12 `<img>` elements were flagged by QA.
  - Hero/content images (all `s7d6.scene7.com/is/image/DSGAEMSites/…`): `2026-1118-dks-swd-6am-shoes-new`, `-men`, `-clothing`, `-baseball`, `-balls`, `-tt`.
  - Icons: `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`.
  - The tracking pixel is also flagged. For it, `alt=""` is the correct fix, not descriptive text.
- **Image-only offer content:** The `wid=1200&hei=1600` hero images are probably the sale content. If so, with no alt text a screen reader or image-blocked client gets none of the offer. I can't confirm this from the truncated source.
- **Web font:** Archivo is loaded from `fonts.gstatic.com` with an `Arial, sans-serif` fallback. This is acceptable.
- **`<link rel="icon">`** in `<head>` has no effect in email clients. It is harmless but unnecessary.
- **Dark-mode support:** The visible source has no `color-scheme` meta tag and no `prefers-color-scheme` media query. This is a gap, but I can't rule out later sections of the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRSTNAME]` are visible in the truncated source or reported by QA.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text part (FAIL):** The text version is 9 characters, effectively empty. Multipart/alternative with a real text body is expected for deliverability and accessibility.
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (WARN):** Neither header was found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them. Treat this as unverified, not a confirmed sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo require RFC 8058 one-click for bulk senders, so check the raw message at the source.
- **Authentication-Results (WARN):** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. It is expected to be absent through the relay. The From domain `em.dcsg.com` matches the tracking domain `data.em.dcsg.com`, which is consistent with aligned sending, but this is not proof.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** The footer is beyond the truncated source, so I couldn't verify either. QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result for it appears in the findings.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No UTM parameters are visible in the truncated source, and the QA output includes no landing-page probe results. I can't assess UTM consistency or landing-page alignment for the offer ("up to 50% off", "ends today").
- The deep-link classes (`arc-link`, `DEEPLINK`) suggest the app/web routing is handled by the redirect service. Whether the redirect resolves correctly on web is untested.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a real text/plain part that mirrors the offer and key links. This is the only confirmed FAIL.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the six content images, especially if they carry the offer copy. Use `alt=""` on the decorative icons and the tracking pixel, and add `style="display:block"` to the pixel.
3. Check the raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and for SPF, DKIM and DMARC results. The relay may be hiding them.
4. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and a wider probe budget (the 51 skipped links) to confirm UTMs, redirect resolution, the unsubscribe link and the physical address.
5. Optionally add `color-scheme` and `supported-color-schemes` meta tags and a dark-mode media query if they aren't already in the untruncated source.
