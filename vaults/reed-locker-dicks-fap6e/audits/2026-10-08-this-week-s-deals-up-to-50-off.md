# This week's deals = up to 50% off! 💰
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, well-built weekly roundup. Its biggest weakness is that it repeats the "up to 50% off" message DICK'S has sent since the 10/5 Fall Deal Drop. The hero is a giant "UP TO 50% OFF" over a football field with a green "This Week's Deals" headline and a Shop Now button. The eye lands on the offer first.
- Below the hero, the email becomes a general mix: Nike under $100, fan gear up to 40% off, golf clubs up to $350 off, a Horizon treadmill at $300 off, YETI at 20% off, a Halloween shop and a text-alerts promo. Each block has its own offer and button, so nothing is wrong, but nothing is a clear second hero either. It reads as a catalog, not a story.
- My judgement is that it's competent and on-brand but forgettable. It follows the 10/7 "expires TONIGHT" send and the 10/6 and 10/7 clearance blasts. After days of "up to 50%" and "up to 70%," a subscriber has little reason to see this as new. "This week's deals" also carries no deadline, so there's no reason to click today.

## What's working

- The hero is huge and legible. "UP TO 50% OFF" can't be missed, and the green Shop Now button sits right under it.
- Each module has its own specific offer: $100 and under Nike, up to 40% off fan gear, up to $350 off golf, 20% off YETI.
- The fan gear block has a credible deadline ("Valid through 10/8/26 11:59PM PT"), which makes it feel real.
- The seasonal Halloween Shop module fits the date, and its sub-links (Costume Boo-Tique, Trick-or-Treat Tailgate) are specific.
- The layout renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlap. The category pill row (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Team Sports) helps with navigation.

## What's weak

- The top offer is "up to 50% off" with no brand, product or category named. It's the same claim as the last four sends.
- The hero deadline is vague ("Valid online only for a limited time. While quantities last."). It doesn't match the specific dates further down.
- The email is long, with eight or more modules and generic "Shop Now" buttons on almost all of them. The text-alerts "Want $20 off $100+ instantly?" banner competes with the product offers.
- The fine print is dense and asterisk-heavy ("***", "††"). It weakens how honest the offer feels, and "No code needed" appears only in the fan gear block.
- The product grid at the bottom (adidas Samba, Nike Club Fleece, New Balance 530) shows names but no prices or ratings, so there's no social proof or price anchor.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero with a real deadline. Replace the generic "This Week's Deals" with a dated hook like "Ends Sunday: up to 50% off," or lead with the single strongest category.
- 2. Cut the body to three or four modules and move the text-alerts banner below the product content.
- 3. Make the CTAs specific: "Shop Nike under $100," "Shop golf clubs," "Shop fan gear," not repeated "Shop Now."
- 4. Add prices or "from $X" and star ratings to the product grid.
- 5. Differentiate from the 10/5-10/7 sends with a new angle, such as personalised categories or a new-arrivals hook.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based with `role="presentation"`, `lang="en"`, an MSO conditional block, and tracked links on a first-party domain. Its main defects are a near-empty plain-text part (9 chars, the one automated FAIL), 12 images with no alt text, and the unverified status of the unsubscribe and authentication headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque redirect tracking.** Links use `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/mclick?params=b.v1...`, with the destination encoded in `params`. The truncated source and QA data don't expose the final URLs or any UTM parameters, so destination correctness can't be confirmed from this data.
- **Coverage gap.** QA probed 12 of 73 links (61 skipped, ranked by click likelihood). No probe failures were reported, so the other 61 are untested.
- **Open pixel.** `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=...` is present, with no `alt` (see section 3). It's first-party and on the same domain as the click tracking.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 12 images.** Hero and product images:
  - `nike1008stwd6am`
  - `jersey10021002`
  - `golf1008stwd6am`
  - `treadmill1008stwd6am`
  - `yetitroppink10081008stwd6am`
  - `halloween-banner-2026-0911-d`

  Icons:
  - `icon-vc-sms-1`
  - `icon-vc-sportsmatter`
  - `icon-vc-bpg-1`
  - `icon-vc-favorite-1`
  - `icon-location-2025-1`

  The open pixel (`open`) is the 12th. The six content images are probably the primary content, and the five icons probably sit beside text labels. With images blocked or read by a screen reader, the deal content is lost. Decorative images and the tracking pixel should use `alt=""`, and informational images need descriptive alt text.
- **Editor artifacts in production markup.** `<body class="is-html-mode" data-contenteditable="true">` is left over from the authoring tool. It's harmless but should be stripped.
- **Image weight.** Scene7 URLs request `fmt=jpeg&qlt=100&wid=1200&hei=1600`. Quality 100 at 1200×1600 per hero image is much heavier than needed for a 600px container, even for 2x retina. This is an inference from the URL parameters, since actual byte sizes weren't measured.
- **Wrapper width.** The wrapper `<table width="600" ... style="width:100%; max-width:600px">` is fine in modern clients. Outlook relies on the `width="600"` attribute plus the MSO ghost table, which is present.
- **Preheader.** The `#ssl` cell holds "Get $20 off $100+ when you sign up for Text Alerts**" followed by the `pht-spacer` filler. The cell is `font-size:0; line-height:0; height:0`, so that text may render invisibly in some clients while still being used as preview text. The trailing `**` footnote marker is also pulled into the preview.
- **Dark mode.** No `color-scheme` meta or `prefers-color-scheme` rules appear in the visible source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens such as `{{...}}`, `%%...%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear in the visible source or QA output. The source is truncated, so tokens in the footer or lower sections are unverified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] Plain-text fallback is 9 chars.** The multipart/alternative text part is effectively empty. This hurts deliverability scoring and leaves text-only and accessibility clients with nothing.
- **[WARN] List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them. Treat this as unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect, and re-check against the raw message. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe for senders at this volume.
- **[WARN] Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because the relay strips or doesn't capture it. The sender domain `em.dcsg.com` aligns with the tracking host `data.em.dcsg.com`, which is a good sign, but nothing here proves it.
- **Unsubscribe link and physical address.** QA says an unsubscribe link was always included in the probe set, with no failure reported. The footer address wasn't visible in the truncated source, so it's unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The mclick wrapper hides the destination URLs and any UTM parameters, and the 12 probed links were not reported as broken. The only direct site reference in the source is the favicon `https://www.dickssportinggoods.com/home/assets/icons/dsg/favicon.ico`. To check landing-page and UTM alignment, follow the redirects and record the final URLs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a real plain-text part. It's the only automated FAIL, and the current 9-char version is effectively empty.
2. Add descriptive `alt` to the six content images. Use `alt=""` for the five icons that sit beside text and for the open pixel.
3. Re-test headers against the raw message, bypassing the relay. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` plus `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `em.dcsg.com`.
4. Resolve the mclick redirects for the main CTAs and confirm the final URLs, UTMs and landing pages. Probe more than 12 of the 73 links if possible.
5. Reduce Scene7 image weight, for example `qlt=80–85` and a width matched to the container (about 1200px for 2x retina).
6. Remove the authoring-tool attributes (`is-html-mode`, `data-contenteditable`) from the production template.
7. Move the "Text Alerts**" preheader out of the zero-height cell, or write a deliberate preheader without the `**` marker.
