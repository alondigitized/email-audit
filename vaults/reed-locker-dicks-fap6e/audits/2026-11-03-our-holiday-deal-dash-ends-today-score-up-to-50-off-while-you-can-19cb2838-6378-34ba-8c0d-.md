# Our Holiday DEAL Dash ends today! Score up to 50% off while you can
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-03**
## Executive summary

- This is a loud, product-heavy DICK'S send. A red argyle hero with a giant gold "UP TO 50% OFF" sits above "Holiday Deal Dash: Up to 50% Off". A "Last Day" kicker and a red Shop Now button give it a credible deadline (valid through 11/3/26 11:59PM PT). The eye lands on the offer first, and the subject's "ends today" matches the hero.
- The email then buries its own lead. A second full-size hero for "UP TO 70%" clearance follows within one scroll, and below it come Brooks, Nike fleece, YETI, fan gear, bikes and bats, plus a product grid. That is nine or more competing offers. A recipient who has already seen three Deal Dash sends in 48 hours (11/2 "ends tomorrow", "2. Days. Only!") gets no new reason to act, only a re-skinned urgency line.
- Overall it's a competent, conventional retail blast. The deadline is real, but the email is fatigued and over-stuffed.

## What's working

- The giant gold "UP TO 50% OFF" hero is unmistakable and matches the subject's promise.
- The "Last Day" kicker, with a stated expiry time in the fine print, makes the urgency credible.
- Red Shop Now buttons are consistent and easy to spot against the white background.
- The category pill row (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Holiday Shop) gives quick navigation.
- The top bar shows ScoreCard points and the "Order Today. Sport It Tomorrow" same-day delivery promise, which adds a useful benefit.

## What's weak

- The second hero, "UP TO 70% Off Select Clearance Styles", competes directly with the 50% hero and splits attention.
- Every module ends in an identical "Shop Now" button, so none of the CTAs says what the click leads to.
- The long scroll of unrelated modules (bikes, bats, YETI, fan gear) dilutes the "Deal Dash" message.
- There is no social proof or reviews, and the fine print under each hero is small and dense.
- The email repeats the Deal Dash theme from 11/2 with only the deadline changed, so it looks like a fourth near-identical promo this week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the 70% clearance hero and push it to its own send, or demote it to a text line. One offer should own the top of the email.
- 2. Replace the generic "Shop Now" buttons with specific CTAs such as "Shop Deal Dash Shoes" or "Get 50% Off Fleece".
- 3. Trim the body to three or four modules, and put a countdown timer or "Ends 11:59 PM PT" in the hero itself.
- 4. Add star ratings to the product grid and show the real sale price on each tile.
- 5. Suppress or soften the send for people who already clicked in the last 48 hours.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Final hours: up to 50% off Nike, adidas & Brooks`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Same-day pickup on thousands of deals. Ends 11:59 PM PT tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fleece, running shoes and fan gear: last chance to save before it's over.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has a missing plain-text alternative (9 chars, the only QA FAIL) and 12 images with no `alt` attribute. The HTML is truncated, so the unsubscribe link, footer address and link hrefs could not be checked, and the missing auth and `List-Unsubscribe` headers are probably relay artifacts.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links were reported. 12 material links were probed and 52 were skipped by ranking (footer, utility and social links are deprioritized), so most of the link inventory is unverified.
- The open-tracking pixel `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=...` is `width="1" height="1"` with no `alt` and no hiding style. Some clients show a broken-image placeholder or the raw URL when images are blocked. Add `alt=""` and `style="display:block;border:0"`.
- Links carry `class="arc-link" data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"` (Salesforce-style deep links). Their hrefs are cut off in the truncated source, so tracking redirects and destinations could not be checked.

- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is 9 chars.** The email is effectively HTML-only. Accessibility tools, text-only clients and some spam filters rely on this part, so it should mirror the content.
- **12 images are missing `alt`**:

2. Add `alt` text to the six content images. Add `alt=""` to the decorative icons and the tracking pixel.
3. Fill the preheader slot, which still holds the template placeholder.
