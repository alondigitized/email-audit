# 📢 Extra 25% OFF select clearance is calling your name!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuinely different lead offer than the "up to 50/60% off" clearance and deals loop DICK'S has run daily since 8/30 — "Extra 25% Off Select Clearance" with a red-toned hero is a distinct hook, not a reshuffled repeat. But structurally it's the same DICK'S template: one clear offer up top, then a long undifferentiated scroll of category blocks (drinkware, golf BOGO, cleats, NFL gear, dumbbells) before circling back to the pampas-grass "UP TO 50% OFF" banner that's shown up in nearly every prior send.
- The hero is clean and legible — big red block, bold "EXTRA 25% OFF," a single "Shop Now" CTA right below it, no scroll needed on mobile. That's the strongest part of the email. Everything after the first fold, though, is generic assortment-of-the-week filler that dilutes the clearance framing the subject promised.
- Verdict: solid subject/hero pairing that would earn an open and a first click, but the body doesn't stay on-message — it becomes a catalog blast, and the recycled 50%-off banner near the bottom undercuts the "extra 25%" urgency established at the top.

## What's working

- Hero delivers on the subject immediately — "EXTRA 25% OFF" is legible, high-contrast, and above the fold with a direct "Shop Now" button.
- Clear fine-print/terms line under the offer ("Valid online through 9/13/26...") gives the offer credibility without burying it.
- Category quick-links (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Team Sports) right after the hero help users self-navigate fast.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text doesn't overlap, layout holds together end to end.

## What's weak

- The clearance offer gets one shot at the top, then the email pivots into six unrelated category promos before circling back to a second "UP TO 50% OFF" banner — this dilutes the "clearance" narrative the subject set up.
- That closing 50%-off pampas-grass banner is the same creative flagged in the 9/10 and 9/8 sends — visible creative fatigue for a regular subscriber.
- No product-level clearance items are shown (sizes, prices, specific SKUs) — it's all category-level "Shop Now" buttons, so there's nothing concrete to click toward.
- The app-download banner and "Check out these picks" product grid push the actual offer even further from view, adding length without reinforcing the 25%-off hook.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body content by half — keep the clearance hero, one supporting clearance category grid (shoes/apparel actually on clearance), and drop the golf/drinkware/NFL/dumbbell blocks entirely; they don't belong under a clearance subject line.
- 2. Replace the closing "UP TO 50% OFF" banner with a second clearance CTA (e.g., "Shop All Clearance") so the email reinforces one offer instead of introducing a competing, already-overused one.
- 3. Show 3-4 actual clearance products with strikethrough pricing under the hero — concrete price cuts convert better than category buttons alone.
- 4. Move the app-download and "Check out these picks" sections to the very bottom, after any clearance-specific content, so they don't compete with the core offer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 25% off clearance — final markdowns`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Clearance just got 25% cheaper`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack an extra 25% off already-reduced shoes, apparel and gear.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Ends 9/13 — extra savings on clearance styles while they last.`

# Technical Audit — DICK'S Clearance Email

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC/ExactTarget `em.dcsg.com`) email using MSO/Outlook conditional comments and standard responsive table markup. The template is structurally sound (proper DOCTYPE, viewport meta, MSO fallbacks) but fails on plain-text fallback, unsubscribe header compliance, and image alt text.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All sampled CTA/header links route through SFMC tracking redirectors (`https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/mclick?params=...` and `/ee/v1/click?params=...`), each carrying opaque `params`, `pid`, `tuid`, and `configId` tokens — standard for this ESP, not a defect.
- Open tracking pixel present: `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=...` (0-height/width, correctly hidden from layout).
- QA reports 12 of 66 total links were probed for validity (54 skipped by design — footer/social/utility deprioritization); no broken/dead links flagged among the probed set.
- No issues found with link structure itself (targets, `target="_blank"` usage consistent).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **11 images missing `alt` text**, confirmed by QA: `2026-0911-dks-stw-6am-thread`, `2026-0623-dks-outdoor-40-off-drinkware-banner`, `2026-0911-dks-stw-6am-golf`, `2026-0911-dks-stw-6am-kicks`, `2026-0911-dks-stw-6am-fan`, `2026-0911-dks-stw-6am-weight`, `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`, plus the open-tracking pixel `open`. This fails WCAG 1.1.1 and degrades screen-reader/images-off rendering, since these appear to be content-bearing product/banner images, not purely decorative.
- Logo image (`id="logo"`) does have `alt="Dick's Sporting Goods"` — correctly implemented, contrast with the above.
- MSO conditional comments (`<!--[if (gte mso 9)|(IE)]-->`) and `mobile-off`/`mobile-on` responsive classes are present and correctly scoped to `max-width:480px`, indicating standard Outlook/mobile-client handling — no issues found here.
- Web font (`Archivo`) is loaded via `@font-face` inside `@media screen` only, with `Arial,sans-serif` fallback in `body` — correct progressive-enhancement pattern for email clients that strip `<style>`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No merge/personalization tokens (e.g. `%%FirstName%%`, AMPscript `%%=...=%%`) are visible in the truncated source provided.
- No issues found in the visible portion; recommend re-checking the untruncated body for AMPscript blocks lower in the template, since SFMC templates of this type commonly personalize the ScoreCard/loyalty-points module that appears to be present (`<!--ScoreCard Header--> <!-- No Points/Only Points Available -->`) but was not rendered with resolved values in this excerpt.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL]** No `List-Unsubscribe` header detected — QA notes this may be an AgentMail relay capture gap rather than a sender-side omission; still, a footer unsubscribe link is confirmed present in-body (per QA note "an unsubscribe link is always included" among probed links), so in-body CAN-SPAM opt-out is likely satisfied even if the header is missing.
- **[FAIL]** No `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe) detected — if confirmed absent at the true SMTP layer (not just relay-capture loss), this is a Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirement gap as of their 2024 sender guidelines.
- **[WARN]** `Authentication-Results` header not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from available data due to the AgentMail relay; cannot confirm compliance either way.
- Physical mailer address / footer compliance text not visible in the truncated source — cannot confirm CAN-SPAM footer requirements from available data.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Landing destinations are obscured behind SFMC tracking redirectors (`ee/v1/mclick`/`ee/v1/click`), so UTM parameters and final landing URLs cannot be directly inspected from the HTML source alone.
- No issues found that can be confirmed from available data; recommend resolving one sampled redirect (e.g. the header logo link) to verify it lands on `dickssportinggoods.com` with expected `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` values and that the destination matches the "Extra 25% OFF clearance" offer in-subject.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 10 content images flagged by QA (product/banner images); confirm the open-tracking pixel and any purely decorative icons use `alt=""` rather than a missing attribute.

4. Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `em.dcsg.com` via a direct header capture (bypassing the relay) since `Authentication-Results` is unavailable here.
5. Spot-check one or two `ee/v1/mclick` redirect targets to confirm UTM parameters and landing-page/offer alignment.
