# 💸 Today only: up to 50% off
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-15**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S again — third "Flash Sale" wordmark send in four days, and it's a near-exact replay of 8/12's "Final Hours" execution: bold black-and-white "FLASH SALE" diagonal wordmark over the same locker-room backdrop, "Up to 50% Off," same men's/women's/kids' pill-nav row underneath. The one thing missing this time versus the 8/12 send is the countdown timer — "Today only" appears only in the subject line, not reinforced visually anywhere in the hero, so the urgency that made 8/12's version work is gone from the render itself.
- Below the fold it's the usual weekly-digest sprawl: an orange Extra 25% Off clearance block (the same offer that's been running since 8/13-8/14), Epic Shoe Sale, Alpine Design clothing, fan shop gear, golf balls, racquet gear, then a six-item "Check out these picks" product grid. Nothing is broken, images render cleanly, and the hero offer is legible at a glance — but there's no single hero product, no price-off anchor, and a subscriber who's opened three of these emails this week has seen every one of these modules before.
- Verdict: clean execution, stale strategy. This is a 5 — competent production undercut by repetition fatigue and a subject-line urgency claim ("Today only") that the body doesn't back up.

## What's working

- Hero is legible and high-contrast — bold "FLASH SALE" wordmark reads instantly even at a glance.
- "Up to 50% Off" headline matches the subject line exactly, so open-to-hero continuity is solid.
- Clear category pill-nav (Men's/Women's/Kids'/Shoes/Team Sports) gives immediate self-segmentation right under the hero.
- Fine print with a real end date/time ("Valid online through 8/15/26 11:59PM PT") is present and not buried.
- No render bugs — every module, thumbnail, and CTA button displays correctly.

## What's weak

- Subject promises "Today only" urgency but the hero has no countdown timer or "ends tonight" callout like the 8/12 send did — the strongest urgency lever from a prior winning send was dropped.
- This is the third "FLASH SALE" hero and the second appearance of the "Extra 25% Off" clearance block within four days — a subscriber will recognize the reused creative.
- No single hero product or price point anchors the top of the email — it's a generic "%off select" banner, not a specific deal to click toward.
- Extremely long single-column digest (8+ offer blocks plus a 6-item product grid) with no clear "best deal here" hierarchy — everything is weighted equally.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a visible countdown timer in the hero (as done successfully on 8/12) to back up the "Today only" subject claim — that's the single biggest lever, since the send currently promises urgency the render doesn't deliver.
- 2. Lead with one specific hero product/price (e.g., a callout shoe at a named price) instead of a generic "up to 50%" banner — gives the eye and the first click somewhere concrete to land.
- 3. Retire or visually differentiate the "Extra 25% Off Clearance" block — it's now run unchanged for three straight sends and is starting to read as wallpaper rather than a deal.
- 4. Trim the digest — six-plus offer modules plus a product grid is a lot for one email; consider splitting golf/racquet/fan-shop into a separate send to sharpen focus.
