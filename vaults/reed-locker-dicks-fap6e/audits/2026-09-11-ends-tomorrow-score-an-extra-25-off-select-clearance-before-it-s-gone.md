# Ends. Tomorrow! 🚨 Score an extra 25% off select clearance before it's gone...
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuine urgency escalation, not another reshuffle — "Ends Tomorrow" with a hard deadline (9/12/26 11:59PM PT) is a legitimate shift from the daily "up to 50/60% off" clearance loop DICK'S has run since 8/30, most recently the 9/11 "Extra 25% Off Select Clearance" send earlier today. The all-red "EXTRA 25% OFF" hero is bold and unmissable, and the deadline gives a real reason to act now instead of tomorrow's identical email.
- The problem is this is functionally the second email today with the same offer and nearly the same hero treatment — just with urgency copy bolted on. A subscriber who already saw "Extra 25% Off Select Clearance" this morning will recognize the repeat instantly, and the fatigue risk is real given the cadence history. Below the fold it's the standard DICK'S kitchen-sink layout: HOKA/clothing blocks, category tiles, six-pack of picks — competent but generic.
- Judgment: solid urgency mechanic undercut by same-day repetition of an offer already sent. The deadline framing alone should lift open rate over a generic "deals" subject, but click-through will suffer from novelty fatigue.

## What's working

- "Ends Tomorrow" badge + explicit expiration date/time in body copy creates credible, verifiable urgency.
- Hero is unambiguous — huge "EXTRA 25% OFF" in white-on-red, impossible to miss even skimming.
- Primary CTA ("Shop Now") appears immediately under the offer with no scroll needed.
- Category chips (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 and Under, Fan Shop, All Sale) give a fast path to narrow the sale without hunting.
- Secondary offers (HOKA 20% off, $50-and-under clothing) are visually distinct and add real incremental value beyond the clearance line.

## What's weak

- Same offer, same day: this repeats "Extra 25% Off Select Clearance" sent earlier on 9/11, with a near-identical red hero — a subscriber will feel the repetition immediately.
- Fine print undercuts the "25% off" headline: "Interim markdowns may have been taken... Prices as marked" is buried gray text that reads as hedging on the discount's real value.
- No product imagery in the hero itself — it's pure typography on a red background, so there's nothing concrete (a shoe, an apparel shot) to anchor the offer to a category before scrolling.
- Bottom two-thirds of the email (Team Sports/Golf/Fan Shop, Drinkware/Outdoor/Fitness, six product picks) is boilerplate carried over from prior sends, not tailored to the "ending tomorrow" urgency.

## Recommendations

- 1. Don't re-send the same 25%-off clearance offer same-day — if urgency is genuine, this should replace the morning send in the queue, not stack on top of it. Sending both trains subscribers to ignore the second one.
- 2. Add a product shot into or beside the hero (a shoe, apparel silhouette) so the offer has a visual anchor beyond typography — pure text-on-red is the weakest hero in this brand's recent rotation.
- 3. Move the countdown/deadline higher and make it visually louder (e.g., a small clock icon or bolded time) — right now "Ends Tomorrow" is a small green label easy to skim past above the big font block.
- 4. Tighten the fine print or move exclusions to a footnote link instead of a full paragraph directly under the CTA — it currently dilutes the urgency message.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your favorite styles, marked down twice — gone after tomorrow.`
