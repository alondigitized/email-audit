# Happy Thanksgiving! Biggest Sale of the Year is served: Up to 50% OFF! 🍴💰
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S tenth or so Black Friday send in five days. It reuses the template from the 11/26 "Black Friday has arrived!" email and the 11/25 "It all starts NOW" and "Beat the rush!" sends. The Thanksgiving tie-in is thin: a fork-and-knife emoji in the subject and a "Stores closed on Thanksgiving" banner at the top. The hero is the same dark green-and-gold "BIGGEST SALE OF THE YEAR" image, with a "Black Friday: Up to 50% Off" headline and one red "Shop Now" button.
- The offer is clear and the hierarchy works. After the hero come category pills, then a long stack of product modules. The stack covers UGG, North Face, BOGO socks, Fan Shop pajama pants, Top Flite golf sets, Wilson basketballs and a red "EXTRA 25% OFF" clearance banner. Each module has its own button.
- The email is competent but exhausting. At this cadence a subscriber has seen this exact layout nine times, and the length buries the best offer. The "Extra 25% Off" clearance banner is the loudest creative in the email, and it sits mid-scroll.

## What's working

- The hero is bold and unmistakable, with "Up to 50% Off" right under it and a single red "Shop Now" button.
- Category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Golf, Fan Shop) let people jump straight to their area.
- The BOGO socks and "Extra 25% Off" clearance modules each carry a concrete, specific offer.
- The Thanksgiving store-closure banner and the holiday store hours module give useful, timely information.
- The gift picks row near the bottom and the app download block give extra paths to a click.

## What's weak

- The Thanksgiving theme is only a subject emoji and a banner. The hero is the same Black Friday creative as the last several sends, so "Happy Thanksgiving" doesn't show up where it counts.
- The email is far too long, with about eight offer modules plus a gift row and a store hours block. That dilutes the single hero message.
- The offers are inconsistent: up to 50%, 30% off UGG, up to 30% off The North Face, BOGO, 25% off pants, up to $100 off, and an extra 25% off. The reader can't tell what the real deal is.
- The "Biggest Sale of the Year" claim has been used in prior sends, such as the 11/24 "Biggest SALE of the Year" email, so it reads as stale.
- The fine print is dense and the red alert banner at the top is small and easy to miss.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero plus three modules (UGG, clearance, and one gifting row), and let the extra 25% clearance offer lead. That raises both clarity and click rate.
- 2. Make the hero Thanksgiving-specific: a "Stores closed today, shop online" message with a single deal.
- 3. Use one consistent offer anchor ("Up to 50% off") and put category-specific discounts in smaller type.
- 4. Reduce cadence. Skip a send when nothing new is on offer.
- 5. Make the CTAs specific, such as "Shop UGG deals" instead of repeated "Shop Now".

- **Subject Alt B:** `Extra 25% off clearance, today only online`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `UGG, North Face, golf sets and more, all online while you're full of turkey.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our stores are closed, but the sale is open. Doors open 6 AM Friday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's visible markup has no broken tokens, but 13 images lack `alt` attributes, there is no usable plain-text part (9 chars), and the template leaves a placeholder comment where the preheader should be. The HTML was truncated and the relay stripped some headers, so the compliance, link and UTM checks are only partly verifiable.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Coverage gap:** QA probed 12 of 66 links (54 skipped), and no per-link status codes were provided. I can't confirm that any link resolves or lands correctly.
- **Wrapped links:** The visible anchors carry `class="arc-link" data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"`, so they are ESP-wrapped. Their final destinations aren't visible in the truncated source.
- **Open pixel:** `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=…` is a 1×1 `<img>` with no `alt` and no `style="display:none"`. It sits directly under `<body>`, so it is unlikely to cause problems.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on 13 images.** QA flagged these 13:
  - the open pixel;
  - 7 content banners: `uggboots1122footaccess`, `top-gifts`, `1030-dks-accessories-cozy-cabin-banner`, `2026-1122-dks-stw-fanshop-cyber8-banner-pants3`, `sets-2026-1122-dks-swd-golf-cyber-8-bids-banner`, `1122-stw-team-sports-cyber8-basketball`, `1101storefront`;
  - 5 footer icons: `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`.

  Screen readers will announce filenames or URLs. With images blocked, any banner that carries its own offer text renders as empty space.
- **Heavy image requests:** Scene7 URLs use `fmt=jpeg&qlt=100&wid=1200&hei=1600` for a `max-width:600px` layout. Quality 100 at 2× width inflates payload. A lower quality, such as 80–85, would be nearly indistinguishable.
- **Dead tag:** `<link rel="icon" …favicon.ico>` in `<head>` is ignored or stripped by email clients.
- **Fonts:** The Archivo `@font-face` rules are in `@media screen` with an `Arial, sans-serif` fallback, so degradation is handled.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible HTML. I saw no unresolved `{{ }}`, `%%`, or `[[ ]]` tokens. The rest of the body was truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **QA findings:**
  - `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.
  - `Authentication-Results` was not found.
  - QA attributes all three to the AgentMail relay, so they are unverified rather than confirmed defects. They matter for Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **Plain-text part (the one FAIL):** The text part is 9 characters. This is the only hard failure, and it is a deliverability and accessibility risk. It could also be a relay artifact, so check the raw message.
- **Footer:** The in-body unsubscribe link and postal address were not in the truncated source, so I can't confirm them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The tracked hrefs are truncated and QA returned no resolved final URLs or UTM data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Plain text:** Ship a real `text/plain` alternative, with offers and links, instead of the 9-character stub. First confirm whether the stub is a relay artifact.
2. **Alt text:** Add `alt` to all 13 images. Use meaningful text for the 7 banners, `alt=""` for decorative icons, and `alt=""` on the 1×1 open pixel.
3. **Preheader:** `td#ssl` holds only the `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` comment and the `&nbsp;` filler in `#pht-spacer`. Put real preheader text before the filler, or the inbox preview will show whatever body text comes first.
4. **Headers:** Check the original `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `em.dcsg.com`. Capture headers upstream of the relay if possible.
5. **Images:** Reduce `qlt` to 80–85 and size `wid` to roughly 2× the rendered width, and remove the unused `<link rel="icon">`.
6. **QA coverage:** Raise the probe limit, or log the 54 skipped links, and record redirect-chain destinations and UTM parameters to make section 6 verifiable.
