# Up to 50% off! Don't miss these deals...👀
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-28**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S delivering a nearly identical email to the May 27 "Best deals of the week are HERE! 📍 Up to 50% OFF" send that scored 6/10 — same five-lane multi-category structure, same "UP TO 50% OFF" hero, same flotilla of SHOP NOW buttons. The subject just inverts the formula: where May 27 opened with the brand boast and closed with the offer, this one opens with the offer and closes with the FOMO hook. Structurally it's the same email.
- The mid-body intrusion of "HUNDREDS OF NEW CLEARANCE STYLES" is jarring. That block was the centrepiece of yesterday's dedicated clearance send (May 27, 7/10); wedging it into a "This Week's Deals" email means DICK'S is now running two creative strategies inside a single send. Neither wins. The weekly-deals narrative loses momentum when the clearance section cuts in, and the clearance section gets none of the spotlight it earned yesterday.
- After a strong May 26 Nike-specific email (8/10) and the May 26 reset email (9/10), this send is a regression — too many swim lanes, too little targeting, a subject line that could have been drafted six times this month.

## What's working

- The hero "UP TO 50% OFF" block is visually dominant and immediately readable at mobile size — the eye lands there first.
- The BOGO 50% OFF lane is a cleaner, more compelling offer structure than the soft "up to X%" language elsewhere; it stands out.
- Brand-consistent execution: clean renders, no visible broken images or layout breaks throughout.
- The "All in the App" section with the mobile screenshot gives app adoption a dedicated visual moment rather than just a text link.

## What's weak

- Subject and creative are effectively recycled from the May 27 send — a subscriber who opened both this week will feel nothing new here.
- "HUNDREDS OF NEW CLEARANCE STYLES" dropped mid-email clashes with the "This Week's Deals" narrative and feels like a content block pasted from yesterday's clearance send.
- Six-plus "SHOP NOW" CTAs diffuse intent — when everything is equal priority, nothing is.
- The app promo section ("Summer Shopping Is a Tap Away") lands late in a long scroll, competing with product picks and a busy footer; the placement suggests it was bolted on rather than integrated.
- The "$100 and Under" category lanes (Nike, Soccer) are useful price anchors but the renders are small enough at email width that product detail is essentially invisible.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pick one hero offer and commit to it.** The BOGO 50% off is the most compelling mechanic in this email — lead with that in the hero and subject instead of the generic "up to 50%." Everything else becomes supporting content.
- 2. **Kill the clearance mid-section.** Yesterday's dedicated clearance send already seeded that message. Including it here cannibalizes both sends without adding subscriber value.
- 3. **Retire "SHOP NOW" across the board.** Replace with offer-specific copy: "Shop the BOGO," "See Nike Picks," "Browse Fan Shop." One verb, one noun, per section.
- 4. **Subject: anchor to the strongest specific offer.** The BOGO is it.
- **Subject Alt A:** `BOGO 50% off ends soon — shop before it's gone`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Buy one, get one 50% off: Dick's best deal this week`
- 5. **Preheader: complement, don't echo.**
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus $100-and-under Nike finds and new clearance styles just dropped`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Nike, Fan Shop, racquet gear — and our biggest BOGO of the week`
- 6. **Trim or consolidate swim lanes.** Four focused sections perform better than eight thin ones. Keep the highest-margin / most persona-relevant categories and cut the rest.
