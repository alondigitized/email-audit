# 100s of styles = up to 30% off! Don't miss our Epic Shoe Sale
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth "Epic Shoe Sale" send from DICK'S in four days, and the creative is essentially unchanged from 8/3 and 8/4: same red-locker backdrop, same Nike/adidas/HOKA logo strip, same stacked-shoe hero shot, same "EPIC SHOE SALE" wordmark. Execution-wise it's still clean — a bold hero, a clear percentage-off subhead, and a well-placed green "SHOP NOW" button with no visible render bugs. But by day four of the identical visual, this reads less like a campaign and more like autopilot; a subscriber who opened Sunday's, Monday's, and yesterday's near-twin emails has already seen this exact locker scene.
- The body does a lot of things well individually — ScoreCard loyalty points up top, a clean single-offer hero, a second "Up to 40% Off Select Clothing" module, then category tiles and product picks. But it's the same modular template DICK'S runs every day, just with the shoe module swapped to the top. Nothing here signals "new drop" or escalates the previous offers — it's flat "up to 30%" again.
- Judgement: solid craft, zero novelty. This email would perform fine on execution alone, but the four-day repetition of the same hero art is the kind of fatigue that erodes open rates faster than any copy tweak can fix.

## What's working

- The hero is legible and on-brand: "EPIC SHOE SALE" in bold white type over a saturated red locker-room scene, with a clean stacked-shoe product shot and recognizable Nike/adidas/HOKA logos.
- The subhead directly restates the offer ("Up to 30% Off Select Shoes and Cleats") and names the brands on sale, so there's no ambiguity about what's discounted.
- ScoreCard loyalty status ("300 Points • $10 Reward") is surfaced at the very top, giving members a reason to feel this email is personalized.
- The offer terms are visible and unburied — "Valid through 8/15/26 11:59PM PT. Select styles. Prices as marked." sits right under the CTA, not hidden in a footer.
- The green "SHOP NOW" button has strong contrast and sits directly beneath the offer copy — no scroll needed to find the CTA.

## What's weak

- This is the same hero image DICK'S has now run three days running (8/2, 8/3, 8/4) with only the subject line reworded — zero new visual investment for a shopper checking their inbox daily.
- The subject line front-loads a vague framing ("100s of styles = up to 30% off!") before naming the actual sale, when the hero itself leads with "EPIC SHOE SALE" — subject and hero don't match on first read.
- The single hero offer gets diluted almost immediately by a second full offer module ("Up to 40% Off Select Clothing"), then six more category grids below the fold — the "one clean offer" strength that scored well on 8/3 gets buried under kitchen-sink merchandising further down.
- Nothing distinguishes this "Epic Shoe Sale" from the prior three — no escalated discount, no new category, no countdown urgency beyond a flat expiration date over a week out.

## Recommendations

- 1. Retire the locker-room hero for at least a week — reusing identical creative four sends running trains subscribers to skim past it. Swap in a fresh angle (e.g., a specific franchise like HOKA Clifton 10, which is already in the product picks below) as the hero instead of recycling the same collage.
- 2. Tighten the subject to lead with the sale name, not a generic "100s of styles" opener, so subject and hero reinforce each other on open.
- 3. Cut the email to one offer above the fold — either shoes or clothing, not both — and push the second offer module lower, closer to the category grid.
- 4. Add a real urgency hook (e.g., "3 days left" ticking down toward 8/15) instead of a flat date, since a week-plus runway reads as low urgency.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Epic Shoe Sale: up to 30% off Nike, adidas, HOKA`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your ScoreCard points + up to 30% off shoes today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Nike, adidas, HOKA and more — select styles, ends 8/15`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Score deals on cleats and running shoes before back-to-school`

## Technical Audit — DICK'S Sporting Goods "Epic Shoe Sale" Email

### 1. Technical Summary
Email renders via Epsilon Harmony ESP infrastructure with standard MSO/Outlook conditional markup; QA shows a 43% pass rate driven by one broken redirect (unsubscribe), missing deliverability/compliance headers, and widespread missing `alt` attributes.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Broken unsubscribe redirect**: `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/U/v610000019fd3937f45927badf4bbcfd118/5257f24087b449fa0000021ef3a0bcff` resolves to `unknown url type: '/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription'` — the tracking redirect is pointing at a broken/dead preference-center path instead of the subscription page.
- All probed links route through the Epsilon Harmony click-tracking domain (`app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...`) with an opaque `__dU__` encrypted parameter (e.g. the header logo link) — final destination cannot be verified from source without following the redirect.
- 12 of 62 total links were probed (QA prioritizes by click likelihood); 50 footer/social/utility links were deprioritized, which is expected behavior, not a defect.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed inline CSS in MSO conditional block**: `<!--[if (gte mso 9)|(IE)]><style>...div {background-color:inherit; font-size:0; line-height:100%; margin:0; padding:0;"}</style>` — stray `"` character before the closing brace on the `padding:0;` declaration. This is invalid CSS and may cause Outlook/Word rendering engines to drop or mis-parse the rule.
- **Invalid JSON-LD**: the `application/ld+json` script block contains `//` line comments (e.g. `// GIF & WEBP images are not supported...`, `// Sample image is 538x138...`, `// DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!`). JSON does not support comments — this will fail JSON parsing, so the structured data (Organization/DiscountOffer/PromotionCard) will not be read by any consumer. Additionally `description`, `discountCode`, and the `PromotionCard.image` fields are empty strings, and this appears to be unedited template boilerplate left in production output.
- **10 images missing `alt` text** (QA-confirmed): SSL/tracking pixel (`5257f24...`), `clothing.png`, `golf.jpg`, `outdoor.jpg`, `team-sports.jpg`, `drinkware.jpg`, `lic.jpg`, `fitness.jpg`, `icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png`, `icon-location-2025.png`. This affects screen-reader accessibility and image-blocked email client fallback text.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tokens (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, AMPscript, Handlebars) are present in the visible/truncated HTML source. No issues found in the reviewed portion.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe link is functionally broken** (see §2) — the link exists (satisfying visible CAN-SPAM presence requirements) but errors out for the user, which undermines the requirement that opt-out mechanisms remain functional.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header not found** — not captured via the AgentMail relay; cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe list support at the header level.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found (RFC 8058)** — one-click unsubscribe (Gmail/Yahoo requirement for bulk senders) may not be implemented.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be confirmed via the relay; this is a visibility gap in this audit, not confirmed proof of an auth failure.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be evaluated from available evidence — all material links resolve through Epsilon Harmony tracking redirects (`app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...`) rather than exposing direct destination URLs with UTM parameters in the truncated source, and none of the QA link probes returned a final landing-page URL other than the unsubscribe failure noted above. No landing-page alignment issue can be confirmed or ruled out with current data.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the unsubscribe redirect mapping so `/U/.../5257f24087b449fa0000021ef3a0bcff` resolves to the subscription/preference center instead of `/pc/Error/Error`. This is the highest-priority item — it's both a broken-link bug and a compliance risk.
2. Remove `//` comment lines from the `application/ld+json` block (or move them outside the script tag) so structured data is valid, parseable JSON; populate or remove the empty `description`/`discountCode`/`image` fields.
3. Remove the stray `"` in the MSO conditional `<style>` block's `padding:0;` rule.
4. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 10 flagged images (empty `alt=""` is acceptable only for the purely decorative tracking pixel).
5. Confirm with the ESP/relay whether `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent — if AgentMail is simply not capturing them, verify via raw header capture at another relay point before treating as a true gap.
