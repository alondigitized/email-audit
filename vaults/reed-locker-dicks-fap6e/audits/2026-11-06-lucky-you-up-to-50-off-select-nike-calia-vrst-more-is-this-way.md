# Lucky you! Up to 50% off select Nike, CALIA, VRST & more is this way 👉
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- This is a DICK'S catch-all that works more like a category menu than a campaign. The hero is a model in blue leggings and a Nike sneaker, with "Up to 50% Off Select Clothing & Shoes" in plain black text and a red Shop Now button. It gets the offer across, but it is much flatter than the green-argyle "UP TO 50% OFF" heroes from 11/4 and 11/6. Below it sits a long stack of mini-promos: cleats, golf electronics, drinkware, NHL, outdoor, saunas, an Extra 25% clearance banner, and gift picks.
- The subject says "Lucky you!", but the body never delivers a "lucky" moment. Nothing is a surprise or a one-off, and it is the same up-to-50% pitch as the earlier sends this week (11/5 "Great deal: Up to 50% off select clothing & shoes", 11/6 "Holiday shopping 🤝 up to 50% off this week"). A subscriber who got those would likely skim this one. The brand names in the subject (Nike, CALIA, VRST) are the most useful signal here.
- Judgement: competent but forgettable, and it adds to the week's fatigue. The body is too long and the hero is too quiet for the weight of the offer.

## What's working

- The subject names specific brands (Nike, CALIA, VRST), which beats a generic "up to 50% off".
- The hero offer is clear: bold black headline, a "Save on Nike, CALIA, VRST & more" subline, and a red Shop Now button.
- The green pill row (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Holiday Shop) gives quick category entry.
- Each module has its own button and a specific discount, such as "Up to 40% Off Select Cleats" or "Up to $100 Off Golf Electronics".
- The red "EXTRA 25% OFF" banner, with a "Limited Time" kicker and an 11/8 deadline, is the strongest visual in the email and adds some real urgency.
- The gift picks grid and the Price Guarantee, Gift Registry and app download blocks add useful footer content.

## What's weak

- The hero is visually weak. The offer is small black text on white, and the photo carries more weight than the discount.
- The email is very long, with about eight modules, and the main offer gets buried. The eye is pulled to the bigger red 25% banner far down the page.
- Two offers compete: "up to 50% off" at the top and "Extra 25% off clearance" near the bottom.
- "Lucky you!" and "this way 👉" are gimmicky and don't match anything in the body.
- The "Order Today, Sport It Tomorrow" strip at the top is tiny and easy to miss.
- It repeats the week's catch-all creative, so cadence fatigue is real.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rebuild the hero as a bold offer panel with a large "UP TO 50% OFF" lockup, as the 11/4 and 11/6 emails did. Put the brand names under it.
- 2. Cut the body to three or four modules. Keep the 25% clearance banner as the second beat and move it higher.
- 3. Make the "Order Today, Sport It Tomorrow" shipping promise a visible banner.
- 4. Give the subject a reason to be "lucky", such as a named deadline or a specific deal.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 50% off Nike, CALIA & VRST ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Nike, CALIA, VRST: up to 50% off, plus extra 25% clearance`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Order today, sport it tomorrow. Plus extra 25% off clearance through 11/8.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The markup is mostly structurally sound: `role="presentation"` layout tables, MSO conditionals, and a `lang="en"` attribute. The one confirmed failure is the missing plain-text part (9 chars), and 13 images have no `alt` attribute. The HTML was truncated and the relay dropped the headers, so the header-based and link-level checks below are unverified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links found.** 12 material links were probed, and none were flagged. 55 were skipped (footer, social, utility), so their status is unknown.
- **Open pixel is unlabeled.** The `<img src="https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1...">` pixel (`width="1" height="1"`) has no `alt` and no `style="display:none"`. It sits as the first child of `<body>`, before the layout table. That is harmless for tracking, but it is flagged by the alt check.
- **The tracking pixel URL is very long.** The `params=` payload is roughly 700 characters. It is fine functionally, but it adds to HTML weight and could be clipped if the message approaches Gmail's ~102KB clipping limit. The full size isn't visible in the truncated source.
- **Link wrapper markers.** Links use `class="arc-link" data-nl-type="DEEPLI…"`, which suggests deep-link wrappers. The truncation prevents verifying the redirect chains.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` attributes (13 images).** This is a confirmed accessibility defect. Affected images:
  - Hero and category images: `1106-stw-4pm-hero`, `-cleats`, `-100-golf-electronic`, `1106-dks-stw-4pm-40-drinkware`, `-nhl`, `-50-off-outdoor`, `-500-saunabox`
  - Icons: `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`
  - The tracking pixel

  With images blocked, the offer content is invisible and screen readers may announce filenames. Several of these are likely promotional images that carry the offer text.
- **Heavy image requests.** Scene7 URLs request `fmt=jpeg&qlt=100&wid=1200&hei=1600`, which is maximum quality at 1200px wide for a 600px container. That is an unnecessarily large payload, and the `hei=1600` parameter is suspicious. The `1106-dks-stw-4pm-40-drinkware` URL has no sizing parameters at all (`/is/content/...?cb=...`), so it serves the original asset.
- **Editor artifacts left in production markup.** `<body class="is-html-mode" data-contenteditable="true">` is a leftover from the authoring tool.
- **Generic `<title>`.** `<title>DICK'S Sporting Goods</title>` doesn't reflect the campaign. This is minor.
- **Web font is progressive enhancement only.** Archivo falls back to Arial, so there is no rendering risk.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible source or the QA data. The truncated remainder is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text fallback missing (FAIL).** The text part is 9 characters. This is confirmed and is the only hard failure. Multipart messages with an empty text part hurt deliverability and break text-only clients.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found (WARN).** The QA note says the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. It is unverified, not confirmed absent.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found (WARN).** Same caveat. One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders, so check the raw message.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found (WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because of the relay. It can't be audited from this data.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address.** The QA report says an unsubscribe link was probed, but the footer is outside the truncated HTML. I can't confirm the postal address.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The visible source doesn't include the destination hrefs (the truncation cuts off before the first link), and the QA data reports no UTM or landing-page results. No issues can be confirmed or denied.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a real plain-text part.** It should mirror the offer and CTAs, with plain URLs and the unsubscribe link.
2. **Add `alt` text to all 13 images.**
   - Use descriptive text for the promotional images, including the offer text.
   - Use `alt=""` for decorative icons and the pixel.
3. **Fill the preheader.** `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` is an unfilled placeholder, and the `pht-spacer` div holds only `&nbsp;` and ZWNJ padding. The inbox preview will fall through to body text.
4. **Verify the raw message headers.** Pull the original `.eml` and confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `em.dcsg.com`.
5. **Right-size Scene7 images.**
   - Use `wid=1200` only for 2x retina, with `qlt` around 80 to 85.
   - Drop `hei=1600`.
   - Add sizing parameters to the drinkware URL.
6. **Strip authoring leftovers.** Remove `is-html-mode` and `data-contenteditable`.
7. **Re-run the audit on the full HTML.** UTM params, merge tokens, the footer address, the unsubscribe URL, and message size need the untruncated source.
