# 💲 Shop now — today only for up to 50% off deals
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-08**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S running its now-familiar "flash sale hero up top, eight more offers stacked below" digest format — same shape as the 8/7 "Go, go, go!" send that scored 7.00, just with a green-locker "FLASH SALE" tear-effect hero instead of the red one. The top of the email does its job: bold ripped-paper "FLASH SALE" graphic, "Up to 50% Off," green SHOP NOW button, all visible fast. But by the fourth screen you're through Epic Shoe Sale, $50-and-under clothing, $100 off Odyssey putters, drinkware, life vests, and a JOOLA pickleball set — nine distinct offers competing for one click.
- The subject line is honest and concrete ("today only," "up to 50% off") without slipping into spam-trigger territory, which is a step up from some of DICK'S emoji-stacked subjects in the brand history. The real problem isn't this specific send — it's that a subscriber getting this many near-identical "up to X% off, shop now" emails per week trains themselves to skim past the subject entirely.
- Net: solid execution, weak differentiation. This is another competent-but-forgettable entry in a cadence that's now sending effectively the same email daily.

## What's working

- The hero delivers a clear, single message (Flash Sale, 50% off, Starts Now) with unmistakable visual hierarchy before anything else competes for attention.
- ScoreCard loyalty messaging ("Earn One Point for Every $1 Spent... $10 Reward") is included and gives existing members a reason to engage beyond the discount.
- Category nav bar (Men's/Women's/Kids'/Shoes/Team Sports/Back-to-School) right after the hero lets a shopper self-select instead of scrolling the whole digest.
- Seasonally coherent — back-to-school nav item plus paddleboard/life-vest/pickleball imagery matches an early-August shopping mood.
- No render bugs: images load, text doesn't overlap, buttons are legible.

## What's weak

- Nine stacked offers (Flash Sale, Extra 25%, Epic Shoe Sale, $50-and-under, Odyssey putters, drinkware, life vests, JOOLA pickleball, plus the product grid) dilute the "flash sale" urgency the subject promised.
- Every section uses the identical "SHOP NOW" CTA — there's no differentiation telling the eye which offer is actually best for them.
- Two conflicting urgency windows in the first two screens ("Starts Now" on the flash sale, "Ends Tonight" on the Extra 25% clearance) muddy which deadline actually matters.
- The hero links to "hundreds of deals" broadly rather than one specific hero product — nothing to click through to except a general sale page.
- This is functionally the same weekly-digest structure as the 8/7 "Go, go, go!" send — a subscriber who opened that one gets deja vu here.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the digest to 2-3 offers max and let the flash sale own the entire top of the email — the current structure buries good individual creative (the putter and pickleball sections are genuinely differentiated but arrive too far down to matter).
- 2. Vary CTA copy per section ("Shop Shoes," "Shop Drinkware," "Shop Putters") instead of repeating "SHOP NOW" nine times — it currently reads as one button copy-pasted.
- 3. Pick one urgency deadline and repeat it consistently; don't run "Starts Now" and "Ends Tonight" in the same email.
- 4. Link the hero to a specific bestseller or category page instead of a generic sale landing page, so the "50% off" promise resolves into an actual product fast.
- 5. Since this cadence pattern is already flagged across five prior sends this week, rotate creative themes daily (e.g., footwear-only Monday, apparel-only Tuesday) rather than re-running the "hero + long stack" template with a different color banner.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Today only: up to 50% off flash sale`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your flash sale is live — 50% off ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shoes, apparel, drinkware, and gear — all up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: earn ScoreCard points on everything you shop today`

# Technical Email Audit — DICK'S Sporting Goods "Flash Sale"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders via a standard MSO/Outlook-conditional table layout with Epsilon Harmony-hosted assets and tracking redirects, but ships with a broken unsubscribe link, invalid JSON-LD markup, and no captured authentication/unsubscribe headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken unsubscribe redirect (confirmed failure):** The "Unsubscribe" link (`https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/U/v610000019fe03755e7a873bf369ff0b0c8/3a24f81d932148590000021ef3a0bd02`) resolves to `/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription` — an application error page, not the preference/unsubscribe center. QA flagged this as `[FAIL] Link error: unknown url type`.
- All probed links route through the Epsilon/Ensighten tracking domain `app.dickssportinggoods.com` (`/T/...`, `/O/...`, `/U/...` redirect prefixes) rather than direct destination URLs, consistent with standard ESP click-tracking wrapping. No malformed tracking tokens observed in the visible source beyond the unsubscribe case above.
- 12 of 65 total links were probed (material links, ranked by click likelihood); 53 footer/utility/social links were deprioritized per standard probing methodology, not flagged as broken.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **10 images missing `alt` text**, confirmed by QA:
  - `3a24f81d9321485900004c5a42963aa1` (SSL/open-tracking pixel — expected, but should still carry `alt=""`)
  - `threads.jpg`, `banner4.gif`, `vest.jpg`, `joola.jpg` (product/promo imagery — content-bearing, need descriptive alt text)
  - `icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png`, `icon-location-2025.png` (footer icons — need alt text or explicit `alt=""` if decorative)
- Mobile responsiveness uses standard `@media screen and (max-width:480px)` class overrides (`.mobile-off`, `.header-xl`, `.p-b-24`, etc.) — structurally sound.
- Outlook/MSO handled via conditional comments (`<!--[if (gte mso 9)|(IE)]-->`) with `mso-hide:all` on the tracking pixel and `<!--[if (gte mso)|(IE)]-->` ghost tables for fixed-width fallback — standard practice, no issues found.
- Web font (`Archivo`) is loaded via `@font-face` inside a `@media screen` block with `font-display:swap` and correctly falls back to `Arial, sans-serif` in the base `body` rule for clients that block remote fonts.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No merge-tag syntax (e.g. `%%`, `{{ }}`, `[[ ]]`) present in the visible source — no unresolved personalization tokens found.
- **Invalid JSON-LD payload:** the `<script type="application/ld+json">` block contains `//` line comments (e.g. `// GIF & WEBP images are not supported...`, `// Sample image is 538x138...`, `// DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!`). This is not valid JSON — a strict JSON-LD parser (Gmail annotations, schema validators) will fail to parse this block, meaning the `DiscountOffer` and `PromotionCard` structured data will likely be silently dropped.
- Within that same block, `discountCode`, `availabilityEnds`, and `PromotionCard.image` are empty strings (`""`) — these read as unfilled template placeholders left in production output.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe mechanism is broken** (see §2) — the unsubscribe link terminates in an application error rather than confirming removal. This is a functional compliance failure, not just a UX issue, since CAN-SPAM requires a working opt-out mechanism.
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not found — one-click unsubscribe via mail client UI is unavailable (or not captured by the AgentMail relay; header presence at the true SMTP layer is unconfirmed).
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058) not found — even if `List-Unsubscribe` exists, one-click POST-based unsubscribe cannot be confirmed as supported.
- Both header findings are caveated by QA as possibly a relay-capture limitation rather than confirmed sender omission — recommend verifying against raw SMTP headers directly from the sending MTA rather than the relay copy.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, etc.) are visible on any link in the truncated source — all outbound links use opaque Epsilon Harmony tracking-redirect paths (`/T/...`, `/O/...`) rather than direct destination URLs with query params, so UTM presence/alignment on the final landing page can't be confirmed from this source. Recommend auditing the resolved (post-redirect) destination URLs directly to verify UTM tagging and landing-page match.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the unsubscribe link routing — `/U/...` currently resolves to `/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription` instead of the preference center; treat as urgent given CAN-SPAM implications.
2. Remove the `//` comments from the JSON-LD `<script>` block or convert them to valid JSON (drop them entirely) so structured data actually parses; populate or remove the empty `discountCode`, `availabilityEnds`, and `PromotionCard.image` fields.
3. Add `alt` attributes to all 10 flagged images — descriptive text for product/promo images (`threads.jpg`, `banner4.gif`, `vest.jpg`, `joola.jpg`), and `alt=""` for the decorative tracking pixel and footer icons.
4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) headers directly against raw sent-mail headers, since the current relay may not be capturing them even if present at origin.
5. Re-run link resolution on the full (untruncated) HTML to confirm UTM parameter presence and landing-page alignment on the resolved destination URLs.
