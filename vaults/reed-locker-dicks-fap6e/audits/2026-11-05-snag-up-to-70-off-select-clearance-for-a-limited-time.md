# Snag up to 70% OFF select clearance for a limited time!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent DICK'S clearance send, and it is almost a copy of the 11/3 "We think you'll like this...Up to 70% off select clearance" email and the 11/4 "Up to 70% OFF select clearance styles 🚨" email. The hero is a red argyle panel with a giant white and gold "UP TO 70% OFF", so the offer lands first. A "Limited Time" kicker and one red Shop Now button sit right under it. The offer is easy to read.
- Below the hero the email turns into a long product catalog. It has adidas Special Shoes, Brooks running shoes, CALIA/VRST/DSG apparel, fan gear, Callaway golf balls, Nishiki bikes and baseball bats. Each block has its own Shop Now button and a different offer: 70%, 40%, "2 for $100" and 50%. The "Explore more deals" pill row is a useful shortcut. The app-download banner and the gift-picks grid add more weight to an already long scroll. It is a third near-identical 70% clearance send in three days.
- Verdict: solid and readable, but repetitive and diffuse. The first screen works and the rest dilutes it.

## What's working

- The giant "UP TO 70% OFF" hero is the first thing the eye lands on, and the offer is unambiguous.
- A "Limited Time" kicker and a single red Shop Now button sit directly under the hero.
- The category pill row (Men's, Women's, Kids', Fan Shop, All Sale) lets a reader jump to their own category.
- The modules carry specific offers, such as "2 for $100 Callaway Chrome Series Golf Balls" and "Up to 40% Off Select Fan Shop Gear".
- The brand look is consistent: the DICK'S logo is clear, the layout is clean and the seasonal holiday imagery is on theme.

## What's weak

- The email is very long, with 8+ product modules, an app banner and a gift grid. It has no single focus after the hero.
- The offers compete: 70%, 40%, 50% and "2 for $100" all appear at once, so the hero's 70% reads as a headline over smaller discounts.
- The clearance framing is weakened by modules that are not clearance, such as the bikes, the golf balls and the fan gear with its "One Day Only" tag.
- Generic "Shop Now" buttons repeat in every block, and none of them names a category.
- The app-download banner and the gift-picks grid at the bottom feel tacked on, and the gift picks show no prices or discounts.
- The 70% clearance hero is identical to the 11/3 and 11/4 sends, so it signals fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero plus three or four modules, and drop the app banner and the gift grid. A tighter email would lift clicks the most.
- 2. Show a hero product or a price-drop example, such as "adidas Special Shoes $79.98", so the offer feels real rather than a bare percentage.
- 3. Rotate the creative and the deadline between clearance sends, for example by naming an end date instead of "Limited Time".
- 4. Make the button copy category-specific, such as "Shop Clearance Shoes" or "Shop Fan Gear".
- 5. Add star ratings or "Best Seller" tags to the product modules.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Clearance up to 70% off, ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `adidas & Brooks shoes from $79.98 🔥`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Order today, Sport It Tomorrow: same-day delivery on clearance shoes, apparel & gear`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shoes, golf, fan gear & more. Prices drop through 11/8, while sizes last.`

## Technical Audit: DICK'S | Clearance, "Snag up to 70% OFF select clearance for a limited time!"

### 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML is table-based, has a `lang` attribute and a viewport meta tag, and uses `role="presentation"` on layout tables. Three problems are confirmed: the plain-text part is effectively empty (9 chars, QA FAIL), 13 images lack `alt` text, and the preheader slot is an unfilled template placeholder. The subscription headers and SPF/DKIM could not be verified through the relay.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- The QA probe covered 12 material links and 54 were skipped by design. It reported no broken or failing links among those probed, so there are no confirmed link defects.
- The open-tracking pixel is `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1...&configId=512ffb51-...` at `width="1" height="1"`. It has no `alt` attribute (flagged by QA) and no `style="display:none"`. This is low severity, but an empty `alt=""` is the correct form for a tracking pixel.
- Links carry `class="arc-link"` and `data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"`. The rest of the source is truncated, so I can't confirm the final href format or whether any links bypass tracking.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images (QA WARN, 12 images besides the pixel).**
  - Seven are large promo images on Scene7: `womensshoes1105stwd6am`, `menrun1105stwd6am`, `2caliavrstdsg`, `jersey1105stwd6am`, `2golf1105stwd6am`, `bikes1105stwd6am` and `bats1105stwd6am`.
  - Five are icons: `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1` and `icon-location-2025-1`.
  - With images blocked, or with a screen reader, the category content is lost unless the surrounding links carry text or `aria-label`. That isn't visible in the truncated source.
- **Preheader placeholder not filled.**
  - `<td id="ssl">` contains only the comment `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` plus the `#pht-spacer` div, which holds only `&zwnj;&nbsp;` padding.

- **Wrapper table.** It has `width="600"` and also `width:100%; max-width:600px`. The Outlook `mso` conditional uses `width="584"`. This is consistent and no defect is evident.
- **Web font.** Archivo is loaded via `@font-face` inside `@media screen`, with an `Arial, sans-serif` fallback. This is acceptable.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (such as `{{...}}`, `%%...%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or QA data. The rest of the source is truncated, so I can't rule out tokens later in the file.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text fallback missing (QA FAIL).** The text part is 9 characters. This is a confirmed deliverability and accessibility issue, since multipart/alternative text should mirror the content and include unsubscribe and address information.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found (QA WARN).** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unverified, not confirmed**. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so verify against the raw original headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found (QA WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown through the relay. The sender is `info@em.dcsg.com`, so check alignment of the From domain with the DKIM `d=` domain on the raw message.
- **Footer unsubscribe and postal address.** An unsubscribe link was reportedly included in the probe. The footer is truncated, so I can't confirm the physical address or link text.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be confirmed from the data provided. The truncated source shows no hrefs, and the QA report lists no UTM or landing-page checks. The click-tracking wrapper (`arc-link`, `DEEPLINK`) probably redirects, so UTM parameters would only be visible on the final landing URLs.

### 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a real plain-text part** that mirrors the offer, category links and unsubscribe link. This is the only QA FAIL.
2. **Add descriptive `alt` text** to the seven promo images (for example "Women's shoes, up to 70% off"). Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixel and decorative icons, or give the icons real labels.
3. **Fill the `#ssl` slot** with preheader text, or remove the placeholder comment.
4. **Remove `data-contenteditable` and `class="is-html-mode"`** from `<body>`.
5. **Check the raw message headers** for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC). Treat the QA warnings as inconclusive until then.
6. **Re-run the audit on the full HTML** so UTM parameters, the footer address and any later merge tokens can be assessed.
