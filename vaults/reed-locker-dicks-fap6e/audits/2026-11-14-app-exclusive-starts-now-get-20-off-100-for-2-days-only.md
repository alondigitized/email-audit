# App exclusive starts now! 🙌 Get $20 off $100+ for 2 days ONLY. ➡️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- This is a clear app-download push wrapped in a very long catalog email. The subject promises "$20 off $100+ for 2 days ONLY," and the headline repeats it, with a ScoreCard+ upgrade to $30 off. The hero is a dark phone mockup with "APP EXCLUSIVE" and a green "$". It sells the app more than the offer, because the dollar amount only appears in the text below the image.
- Below the hero, the email turns into a generic holiday catalog. It has category pills, running shoes, fleece, tumblers, golf shoes, racquet gear, a ping-pong table, cardio gear and a gift grid. None of these modules ties back to the app-only $20 coupon, so the 2-day urgency is lost within one scroll. The fine print under the CTA says the offer is claimed in the app via a coupon in My Account, and that it is not stackable. That friction undercuts a "starts now" send.
- Judgement: a solid, recognizable promo with a credible deadline, but diluted by the catalog body and a hero that doesn't show the number. This is DICK'S fourth or fifth sale-driven send in two days. In the 11/13 sends ("Holiday DEAL Dash," "EXTRA 25% off clearance"), the hero stated the offer in giant gold type. This one is a step down in visual punch.

## What's working

- The headline states the offer plainly: "$20 Off $100 or More | ScoreCard+ $30 Off $100 or More."
- The member tier gives loyalty customers a clear upsell, with a dedicated "Join ScoreCard+" button next to "Get Offer."
- The two-button stack is visible near the top of the email.
- The category pills (All Sale, Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop) give quick navigation.
- The product modules are consistent and scannable. Each has a label, a price or deal line, and a "Shop Now" button, and they match the holiday season.

## What's weak

- The hero image has no number in it. The "$" and "APP EXCLUSIVE" don't say $20 or $100, so the offer is only readable once you hit the small headline.
- The fine print (no code needed, must claim in-app, My Account coupon, exclusions) is dense and sits right under the CTAs. It reads as a hurdle for an offer that is supposed to be instant.
- The body is about eight product modules plus a gift grid, none of which reference the 2-day app deal, so urgency evaporates after the hero.
- The two top CTAs have similar visual weight (red "Get Offer," green "Join ScoreCard+"), which splits attention.
- The footer is long, with legal text and an app-download strip placed after all the product content. The app badges should be near the top, since the whole premise is the app.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the number in the hero: "$20 OFF $100+" in giant type with "App only · 2 days" and a countdown. This matches the loud hero style DICK'S used on 11/13.
- 2. Add App Store and Google Play badges directly under the hero CTA, so that people who don't have the app aren't left stranded.
- 3. Cut the body to three or four modules, and make each one say "use your $20 off here."
- 4. Move the "Join ScoreCard+" CTA to a secondary text link, and keep one primary button.
- 5. Shorten the fine print to one line with a "Details" link.
- 6. Add a "Ends Sunday 11:59 PM PT" timer line to keep urgency visible through the scroll.
- **Subject Alt A:** `$20 off $100 — app only, ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your app-only $20 off is live (2 days)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Open the app, tap Get Offer, and the coupon is in My Account. ScoreCard+ gets $30.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `No code needed. Shop $100+ in the app through Sunday night.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally sound (`lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` layout tables, hidden preheader, Outlook conditional CSS). The automated checks found one hard failure, a near-empty plain-text part, plus widespread missing image alt text. Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified from this capture.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links were reported. 12 material links were probed and 53 were skipped under the click-likelihood ranking, so the footer, utility and social links are largely unverified.
- The open-tracking pixel is `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=…` (`width="1" height="1"`). It has no `alt` attribute and no inline `display:none`. That is low impact, but `alt=""` would be cleaner.
- CTA anchors use `class="arc-link" data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"`. The source is truncated, so I can't confirm how these resolve for users without the app installed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **[FAIL] Plain-text fallback is 9 characters.** The email is effectively HTML-only. That hurts the spam score and text-only clients, and it gives screen readers and clients that prefer the text part nothing to read.
- **12 images are flagged as missing alt text.** Content images:
  - `secondaryFull-base-01114stwd6am`
  - `sweatshirts114stwd6am`
  - `1110-hydrojug-cocoa-croc-launch-banner`
  - `2piclkeball`
  - `pingpong`
  - `cardio114stwd6am`
  
  Icons: `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1` and `icon-location-2025-1`.
  - The content images are likely the primary tap targets. With images blocked or read by a screen reader, the offer and destination are lost.
  - Decorative images should carry an explicit `alt=""` rather than omit the attribute.
- Scene7 image URLs request `wid=1200&hei=1600&fmt=jpeg&qlt=100`. That is a large source size at maximum JPEG quality for a 584–600px layout, so it is a likely payload and load-time concern. I inferred this from the URLs, not from measured byte sizes.
- `<link rel="icon">` in the email `<head>` has no effect in mail clients and can be removed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear in the visible source, and the QA checks flagged none.

## 5. Compliance
- **[WARN] No `List-Unsubscribe` header and no `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. If the headers are absent in production, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements for one-click unsubscribe would not be met.
- **[WARN] No `Authentication-Results` header.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `em.dcsg.com` can't be verified from this capture.
- A body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are not visible in the truncated source. The QA notes say an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result is reported, so I can't confirm either.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not verifiable from the data provided. UTM parameters and landing-page destinations are not visible in the truncated HTML, and the probe results contain no redirect-chain or landing-page data. No mismatch is confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Generate a real multipart/alternative text part.** The 9-character text version is the only hard failure.
2. **Add descriptive `alt` text to all six content images.** Use `alt=""` on the icons and the tracking pixel. For example, the banner `1110-hydrojug-cocoa-croc-launch-banner` should name the product and offer.
3. **Verify the headers at the source, not through the relay.** Check the raw message in a seed inbox for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass. If the relay is stripping them, fix the capture so these WARNs stop being ambiguous.
4. **Reduce Scene7 request size** to roughly 2x the rendered width (about 1200px) with `qlt` around 80, and make sure the height parameter isn't forcing oversized crops.
5. **Probe the unsubscribe and footer links explicitly**, and log UTM parameters and final landing URLs for the CTAs, so section 6 can be assessed.
