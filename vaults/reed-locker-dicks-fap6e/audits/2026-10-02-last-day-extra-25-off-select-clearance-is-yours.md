# 📢 Last day! 🚨 EXTRA 25% off select clearance is yours.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, loud, single-message hero. A giant white "EXTRA 25% OFF" sits on deep red with a green "Shop Now" button right under it. The eye lands on the offer immediately, and nothing is broken or truncated. That is a step up from the cropped "FLAS" banner in the 9/30 Flash Sale sends. "Last Day" is real, and the fine print confirms it: valid through 10/2 at 11:59 PM PT.
- The urgency is underplayed, though. "Last Day" appears only as a small green kicker above the headline, not in the hero art. After that the email fragments into six or seven competing offers: up to 40% off running shoes, $50-and-under clothing, up to 30% off fan gear, $30 off FootJoy golf shoes, 50% off Lotto pickleball paddles, and the ScoreCard promo. A subscriber who got "up to 50% off" four times in the past four days (9/29, both 9/30 Flash Sale sends, and 10/1's "Best DEALS of the week") will read this as more of the same promo cadence. The "EXTRA 25%" framing is a genuinely different mechanic from the 50% run, and that helps a little.
- Verdict: a well-executed hero on a fatigued cadence. It is good enough to get a click from people already inclined to shop, but it does little to cut through the noise.

## What's working

- The "EXTRA 25% OFF" hero is huge and legible, with strong red/white contrast. There is no ambiguity about the offer.
- The green "Shop Now" button sits directly under the headline, so the primary CTA is obvious.
- "Last Day" is paired with a concrete deadline (10/2, 11:59 PM PT) in the fine print, so the urgency is credible.
- The "Explore more deals" pill nav (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 and Under, Fan Shop, All Sale) lets people self-select by category.
- The hero is not cropped and no images are broken, which is cleaner than the 9/30 Flash Sale heroes.

## What's weak

- "Last Day" is a tiny kicker. The urgency should be in the hero itself, not in a small green label below the fold of the image.
- Too many competing offers in the body (40%, $50, 30%, $30 off, 50%, ScoreCard) dilute the one message the subject sells.
- Every CTA below the hero is a generic "Shop Now", with no verb plus category, such as "Shop running shoes".
- The "select clearance" language and the exclusions in small grey fine print make the offer feel less clean than the hero implies.
- The email is very long for a "last day" push. The footer carries texts, Best Price Guarantee, app download and legal blocks that add friction.
- The cadence is fatigued. This is the fifth or more sale email in about four days, following 10/1's "Best DEALS of the week = up to 50% OFF!" and 9/29's "$25 & UNDER" clearance send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero art itself, for example "LAST DAY · ENDS TONIGHT 11:59 PM PT" above or below "EXTRA 25% OFF". This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Cut the body to the hero plus two or three modules that all support clearance. Drop the golf, pickleball and fan-gear tiles on a last-day send.
- 3. Replace the generic "Shop Now" buttons with specific CTAs such as "Shop clearance shoes" and "Shop clearance clothing".
- 4. Add a code or "applied at checkout" line so the "extra" mechanic is clear, and surface the exclusions in one short line near the CTA.
- 5. Cap the weekly sale cadence, or vary the offer type. Four near-identical "up to 50%" sends in a row train people to ignore the next one.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: extra 25% off clearance`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: extra 25% off shoes & apparel`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ends 11:59 PM PT. Running shoes, apparel & more, marked down again.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `One more day to stack 25% off on top of clearance prices.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard table-based layout with tracked redirect links and a tracking pixel. The confirmed issues are a near-empty plain-text part (9 chars), 9 content images with no `alt` attribute, and an apparently unfilled preheader slot. The header-related warnings come from the AgentMail relay, so they are unverified rather than confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All observed links are wrapped in ESP redirects (`https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/mclick?params=b.v1...&pid=...&tuid=...`). The destination URLs are opaque in the source, so they can't be checked from the HTML.
- 12 links were probed and 50 skipped (ranked by click likelihood). No probe failures were reported. The 50 skipped links are unverified.
- The open pixel is `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=...` and has no `alt`. This is minor because it is a tracking image.
- No broken or mismatched links were confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (WARN, confirmed by QA):** 9 content and icon images have no `alt` attribute:
  - Content images: `shoes1002`, `clothhing1002`, `jersey10021002`, `paddle` (all `s7d6.scene7.com/is/image/DSGAEMSites/...`)
  - Icons: `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`
  - Screen readers may announce the file names. If the icons are decorative, they need `alt=""`. If the content images carry the offers, they need descriptive alt text.
  - If the product tiles are the only carrier of the offer, they are inaccessible with images off.
- **Plain-text part (FAIL):** the text version is 9 chars. This is a deliverability and accessibility issue unless the relay stripped the text part, which can't be confirmed from the data given.
- **Oversized images:** the content images request `wid=1200&hei=1600&qlt=100` from Scene7. That is likely 2x or more the display size at a 600px container, and `qlt=100` adds payload weight.
- **Template leftovers:** `<link rel="icon" ...>` in `<head>` is ignored by email clients and is dead markup.
- **Positives (not issues):** `lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, `mso-hide` on the preheader spacer, MSO conditional comments, and a mobile `@media` block are all present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unresolved tokens such as `{{ }}`, `%%`, or `[FIRSTNAME]`. The `tuid=` parameter in the redirect URLs is a tracking identifier, not visible content. Content past the truncation point is unchecked.

- **Footer content:** an unsubscribe link was reportedly always included in probing, but no result is shown. The physical address and unsubscribe text are not in the truncated source, so they can't be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

- No landing-page mismatch was confirmed. The probe results were not provided in detail beyond "no failures".

1. Add `alt` to all 9 flagged images. Use `alt=""` for the `icon-vc-*` and `icon-location-2025-1` icons if they are decorative. Use descriptive alt text for `shoes1002`, `clothhing1002`, `jersey10021002` and `paddle`.
