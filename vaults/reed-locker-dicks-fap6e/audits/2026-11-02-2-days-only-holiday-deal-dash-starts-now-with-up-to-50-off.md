# 2. Days. Only! Holiday Deal Dash starts now with up to 50% OFF 🙌
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-02**
## Executive summary

- This is a loud, product-heavy DICK'S promo. A red argyle hero with a giant gold "UP TO 50% OFF" sits above "Holiday Deal Dash: Up to 50% Off" and a red Shop Now button, so the eye lands on the offer first. The "2. Days. Only!" subject is a real hook, and the body backs it up: a "Two Days Only" kicker and a note that valid online-only dates run through 11/3. That matches the 11/2 send date.
- The email then undercuts its own offer. A second, equally loud "UP TO 70% OFF" clearance hero comes right after the first, and the body has about eight more modules (women's adidas, Brooks, CALIA/VRST apparel, Owala, gloves, table tennis, a picks grid). Two competing headline percentages dilute the "Deal Dash" message. The "Two Days Only" urgency also sits only in a small kicker, not in the hero itself. This brand has sent several near-identical 50% and 70% emails in the last few days. The 11/1 "Holiday deals are here! (and they're up to 50% OFF)" send used the same up-to-50% offer.
- Judgement: solid, clear, conventional. It's well built, but repetitive and cluttered below the fold.

## What's working

- The hero has one dominant idea: a giant gold "UP TO 50% OFF" on red with a red Shop Now button directly under it.
- The "Two Days Only" kicker and the stated valid-through date make the deadline credible.
- The category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Holiday Shop) give quick navigation.
- Specific product modules show prices, such as "Sale $79.98 Select Women's adidas Spezial Shoes" and "Sale $124.98 Men's Brooks Adrenaline GTS 25 Running Shoes", so the deals feel concrete.
- Each module has its own Shop Now button, and the product photography is consistent and on-season.

## What's weak

- Two huge percentages ("UP TO 50%" and "UP TO 70%") compete in the first two screens, so the "Deal Dash" has no single hero.
- The "Two Days Only" urgency is a tiny red kicker. It isn't in the hero or the CTA.
- The email is very long. Modules for Owala ("Coming Soon"), baseball gloves, table tennis and a picks grid scatter attention and dilute the sale.
- The fine print under the heroes is small and dense, which weakens the sense that the offer is honest.
- Nearly every module uses the same "Shop Now" button copy, so no CTA is specific.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer. Cut the 70% clearance module, or move it far below the fold, so the 50% "Deal Dash" is the only headline.
- 2. Put the countdown in the hero. Add a line like "Ends Tuesday 11:59 PM PT" next to the button, not just in the kicker.
- 3. Trim the body to 3-4 modules. Cut Owala, table tennis and the picks grid, or fold them into the category pills.
- 4. Make CTAs specific: "Shop Deals Under $100" or "Shop adidas Deals" instead of repeated "Shop Now".
- 5. Move the fine print to a short, readable line under the hero, with the exclusions stated plainly.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Deal Dash ends Tuesday: up to 50% off brands you love`
- **Subject Alt B:** `48 hours only: adidas, Brooks & more up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Online only through 11/3. Sale $79.98 adidas, $124.98 Brooks and more.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Two days, one big markdown. Shop shoes, apparel and gear before it's gone.`

## Technical Audit

The one confirmed failure is the effectively empty plain-text part (9 chars). All 11 content and icon images also lack alt text. Most compliance and authentication checks could not be verified from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Coverage gap:** 12 of 63 links were probed and 51 were skipped. The QA output reports no probe failures. The `href` values are truncated in the source (`href="htt…`), so I can't confirm redirect or tracking behavior for individual links.
- **Link markup:** CTAs use `class="arc-link" data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"`. Nothing in the visible source shows a defect.
- **Open pixel:** `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=…&tuid=…&configId=…` is 1x1, placed as the first child of `<body>`, and shares the `em.dcsg.com` sending domain. It has no `alt` and no inline `display`/`border` styles. This is low severity, and the QA tool flags it only as missing alt.
- No broken or malformed links are confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **[FAIL] Plain-text fallback:** the text part is 9 characters. That is effectively no multipart/alternative content. Text-only clients, some spam filters and screen-reader-oriented clients get nothing usable.
- **Missing alt on content images (11):** all are served from `s7d6.scene7.com/is/image/DSGAEMSites/`.
  - Banners and promo images: `womensshoes`, `shoes1102stwd6am`, `2caliavrst1103`, `1101-owala-winter-meadow-plaid-banner`, `2softball1102stwd6am2`, `2paddletable1102stwd6am2`.
  - Icons: `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`.
  - Impact: with images blocked, or in a screen reader, the promo content is invisible. A missing `alt` also lets some clients announce the filename or URL.
- **Open pixel missing alt:** this is the 12th flagged image. It is cosmetic and low priority.
- **Preheader:** the `#pht-spacer` div holds only `&zwnj;&nbsp;` filler. The visible source has no preheader text before it, and the `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` template placeholder comment is still in the `#ssl` cell. Clients will probably pull preview text from the body, so this should be checked in a client preview.
- **Minor:** `<link rel="icon">` in `<head>` is ignored by email clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unresolved tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The tracking `params`/`pid`/`tuid` values are resolved.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

That means this is unconfirmed rather than a verified sender defect. If the headers are really absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this data. Raw headers from the original delivery are needed.
- The unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are in the footer, which is cut off in the truncated source. I can't confirm either. The QA tool says an unsubscribe link was included in link probing.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Can't assess. The `href` values are truncated, and there is no visible evidence of UTM parameters, landing page URLs or probe results. Nothing is flagged.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **High:** Generate a real multipart plain-text part, with the offer, key links and unsubscribe URL. A 9-char text part is a deliverability and accessibility defect.
2. **High:** Add descriptive `alt` text to all 11 content and icon images. Use `alt=""` for decorative ones. Put the offer text in the alt for the promo banners.
3. **Medium:** Check the raw original message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. This would settle whether the compliance warnings are relay artifacts or real gaps.
4. **Medium:** Confirm the preheader renders in Gmail and Apple Mail. Remove the leftover `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` placeholder, or populate it.
5. **Low:** Add `alt=""` and `style="display:block;border:0"` to the open-tracking pixel.
