# Up to 50% off is available in this week's deals
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the same paddleboard-at-sunset hero and the same "UP TO 50%" block treatment DICK'S already ran on 2026-07-24 in "Up to 50% OFF is here — check this week's DEALS we selected for you," just with the copy sanded down further into pure filler ("Up to 50% off is available in this week's deals"). Worse, it lands the same day as the 07-26 "Nike Deal Days are live now" send and reproduces that exact Nike Deal Days block wholesale as a mid-scroll module — this subscriber would be looking at the third variation of the same two offers (50% off hero + Nike Deal Days) inside about 48 hours.
- Mechanically the email is clean: no render bugs, a legible hierarchy, a real ScoreCard rewards nudge up top, and a well-organized shop-more grid at the bottom. But there's zero new creative idea here — it's a recombination of assets from two higher-scoring recent sends, with a subject line so generic it could be attached to almost any DICK'S promo from the last month.
- Judgement: technically fine, strategically stale. This is a rerun wearing a flatter subject line, sent into a cadence that's already fatigued.

## What's working

- Clean render — no overlapping text, no broken images, all product tiles load correctly.
- ScoreCard loyalty points (300 Points + $10 Reward) surfaced immediately at the top, which is a genuine member-relevant hook.
- The "Order Today. Sport It Tomorrow." fulfillment line (pickup/delivery/shipping) adds real utility info most competitors skip.
- Bottom-of-email structure (category tiles, then curated "epic summer" product grid) gives browsers something concrete to click even if the hero doesn't land.
- Nike Deal Days sub-block includes a hard expiration date (valid through 8/15/26), which is more credible than the vague "this week" framing up top.

## What's weak

- The hero image and "UP TO 50% OFF" treatment is a near-repeat of the 2026-07-24 send — same beach/water lifestyle photography, same oversized percentage callout.
- This send duplicates the entire Nike Deal Days module from the dedicated 07-26 "Nike Deal Days are live now" email sent the same day — the two emails are cannibalizing each other's content.
- Subject line has no product, category, or urgency anchor — "available in this week's deals" is the least specific phrasing DICK'S has used in this run.
- Hero offer ("Up to 50%") never resolves to which categories qualify until you scroll past two more sections — no product or department is named in the hero itself.
- "SHOP NOW" is reused as the CTA copy three separate times in this single email (main hero, Nike block, implicitly on category pills) with zero variation.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill the duplicate Nike Deal Days block.** If Nike Deal Days already went out as its own campaign today, this send should either lead with something distinct (golf, outdoor, back-to-school) or fold into a single consolidated email — sending both same-day trains subscribers to skip one.
- 2. **Replace the hero image.** Retire the paddleboard/sunset photo until it's had real time off; it's the second appearance in under a week and is what's making this read as a rerun.
- 3. **Name a category or product in the subject and hero**, not just a percentage — "Up to 50% Off Golf, Outdoor & Fan Gear" gives the eye somewhere to go and differentiates from every other "Up to X% Off" send in this stretch.
- 4. **Vary CTA copy per module** ("Shop Nike Deals," "Shop Summer Gear") instead of three identical "SHOP NOW" buttons.
- 5. **Add a real deadline to the top offer**, mirroring the credible 8/15/26 date already used for Nike Deal Days.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Golf, outdoor & fan gear — up to 50% off this week`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your ScoreCard deals: up to 50% off select gear`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus Nike Deal Days: up to 40% off select styles`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Same-day pickup available — shop before deals end Sunday`
