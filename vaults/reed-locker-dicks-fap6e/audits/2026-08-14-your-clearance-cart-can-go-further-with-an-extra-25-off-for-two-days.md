# Your clearance cart can go further with an extra 25% off for two days
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-14**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S again, and the hero is actually clean this time — a red "EXTRA 25% OFF" placard over a plain lake/dock photo, with a legible headline ("Extra 25% Off Select Clearance") and a specific end date ("Valid online thru 8/16/26 11:59PM PT"). That's a real, time-bounded offer stated plainly, which puts it a notch above the vaguer "up to 50% off" digest sends this brand keeps running (2026-08-11, 2026-08-13).
- The problem is everything below the fold: it immediately reverts to the same weekly-digest sprawl DICK'S has sent five times this run — Epic Shoe Sale, clothing, soccer balls, golf, fan shop, kayaks, then yet another "Up to 50% Off" beach-ball module duplicating the hero's own offer type. The clearance angle promised in the subject line gets buried after one screen; a subscriber who opened for "extra 25% off my clearance cart" scrolls into an unrelated full-price catalog dump.
- Net: strong top, same tired bottom. This reads as a 6 — better hero discipline than most recent sends, but no improvement to the underlying sprawl problem.

## What's working

- Hero has one unambiguous offer (Extra 25% Off) with a real exclusion/expiration line instead of buried fine print.
- "Explore more deals" category chips (Men's/Women's/Kids's/$25 & Under) give quick self-segmentation right under the CTA.
- No render bugs — images load, text is legible, buttons are consistently styled black "Shop Now" pills.
- Credit card module ("Earn 10% Back") is visually distinct and doesn't compete with the clearance CTA above it.

## What's weak

- Subject promises a "clearance cart" callback (implying cart/browse personalization) but the hero and every module below is generic category merchandising — no visible cart contents or personalization.
- The email reverts to the same 8-module weekly digest structure (shoes, clothing, sports, fan shop, kayaks, "This Week's Deals") seen in the 8/11 and 8/13 sends — clearance gets one screen, then six unrelated full-price modules.
- The "Up to 50% Off" beach-ball module two-thirds down duplicates the hero's discount framing with a different (non-clearance) offer, diluting the single clear CTA the top established.
- "Clearance" as a stated theme disappears entirely after the hero — no clearance-specific product grid, just generic category shopping.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the digest — this is a clearance-specific send in the subject line, so the body should stay clearance-only: swap the shoe/clothing/soccer/golf/fan-shop/kayak modules for an actual grid of clearance product tiles (with strikethrough pricing) so the "cart can go further" promise pays off visually.
- 2. If personalization data exists (browsing/cart), surface it — even one row of "items in your cart" would justify the subject's specific framing; otherwise, soften the subject so it doesn't over-promise.
- 3. Drop the duplicate "Up to 50% Off" beach-ball module — it competes with the hero's 25% off math and isn't clearance-related.
- 4. Move the credit-card 10% Back module up near the hero since it stacks with the clearance discount — that combo is a stronger click driver than another unrelated category block.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 25% off clearance — 2 days only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your clearance picks just got 25% cheaper`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack savings on already-marked-down styles, ends 8/16.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Final markdowns, plus an extra 25% off — through Sunday.`
