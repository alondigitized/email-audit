# ⏳ Our Amazing Sale is over TONIGHT — shop up to 50% off online now! ⏳
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a reminder for this morning's "One Day. One Amazing SALE!" send, and it does the job. A red "Final Hours 07:59:48" countdown bar sits above a big "ONE DAY" hero. The headline, "24 Hours of Online-Only Deals: Up to 50% Off", states the offer plainly, and a single "Shop Now" button sits right under it. The urgency is credible because the countdown matches the 11:59PM PT deadline in the fine print.
- The email then undercuts itself. Below the hero there is a second, louder "UP TO 70% OFF" clearance block with its own button. That's the same offer as the "Limited time!" clearance email sent earlier today. So a skimmer sees 50% and 70% in one email and has to guess which one the countdown applies to. The gift-picks grid is generic: a tumbler, golf balls, a kids' hoodie and a squishy toy. It has no prices and no reviews, so it weakens the focus.
- It's a solid reminder with a clear hero, held back by a second competing offer and product tiles that don't sell. I'd call it a 6.

## What's working

- The "Final Hours 07:59:48" countdown bar is the first thing you see, and it matches the stated 11:59PM PT deadline.
- The "ONE DAY" hero is bold and high contrast, and the headline right under it gives the exact offer.
- The single "Shop Now" button under the hero is clear, and the green/red palette makes the buttons stand out.
- The category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Holiday Shop) let a shopper reach their own category quickly.
- The ScoreCard strip and the footer extras (app download, price match, texts) add useful perks without cluttering the top.

## What's weak

- The "UP TO 70% OFF" clearance block is visually bigger than the 50% hero. It competes with the one-day offer and repeats this morning's clearance email.
- The gift picks have no prices, discounts, ratings or reviews, so there is no reason to click a specific product. The mix (golf balls, a squishy toy, a tumbler) doesn't feel curated.
- Both hero buttons say "Shop Now", which is vague, and neither is tied to a specific category.
- The fine print under each offer is dense, and "select styles" and "may not be valid in conjunction with other sales" tone down the 50% claim.
- The ONE DAY hero image (a football among holiday ornaments) shows no product or deal.

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop the 70% clearance block, or move it below the product grid. The countdown email should carry one offer only.
- 2. Replace the generic gift picks with 3-6 of today's actual doorbusters, showing sale prices and "was" prices. This also lifts the click signals.
- 3. Make the CTA specific, for example "Shop 50% Off Deals" or "Shop One-Day Deals".
- 4. Put the product grid before the category pills, so the pills aren't pushing the offers further down.
- 5. Put "Ends tonight 11:59PM PT" in the subject and preheader, and drop the hourglass emojis on both sides of the subject. They read like clutter and look like the earlier emails.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sale ends at 11:59PM PT. Hoodies, shoes, fan gear and more, all marked down.`
