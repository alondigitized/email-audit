# 👋 Your app-exclusive expires soon...Score $20 off $100+ while it lasts!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth or fifth send of the same "$20 off $100+ in the app" offer in about 36 hours. It follows 11/14's "App exclusive starts now!", "📢 Don't miss this app exclusive" and "Lucky you!", plus this morning's "LAST DAY" send. The render itself is clean and the offer is clear. The problem is cadence. A subscriber has now seen the same dark phone mockup and the same headline several times.
- The hero is strong on structure. A "Last Day" red kicker sits over a bold headline, then a red "Get Offer" button, with a green "Join ScoreCard+" button below it. The ScoreCard+ upsell to $30 off is a good value ladder. The headline wraps awkwardly, though. "Exclusive: $20 Off $100 or More | ScoreCard+ $30 Off $100 or More***" reads like two offers jammed together, and the triple asterisk undercuts the "no code needed" promise.
- Below the hero, the email drifts into a generic holiday catalog. It has category pills, an "Up to 40% off" banner, a six-tile category grid and a long footer. Those blocks dilute the app push, and they carry no product, price or review. The email lands as a repeat with a thin new hook: "expires soon" has now been said three times.

## What's working

- The hero offer is unmistakable: "$20 Off $100 or More" in large bold type, with a red "Get Offer" button.
- The ScoreCard+ tier ($30 off) gives members a visible reason to act, and a second button to join.
- The "Last Day" kicker plus "Valid through 11/15/26 11:59PM PT" gives credible, time-bound urgency.
- "No code needed" and "Sale items included!" remove friction and objections.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text. The category pills and tile grid are easy to scan.

## What's weak

- Cadence fatigue. The 11/14 sends ("App exclusive starts now!", "Lucky you!") and today's "LAST DAY" send carry the same offer and the same phone mockup.
- The hero image is two blank phone screens with "APP EXCLUSIVE" and ScoreCard+ logos. It shows no product and no app UI, so it conveys little.
- The headline is cluttered. Two offers are joined by a pipe and trailed by `***`, which blurs the single clear message.
- Everything below the hero competes with it: the "Up to 40% off" block, category pills, six category tiles and a footer. No product, price or review gives a reason to click.
- The subject line is long, about 75 characters. The emoji plus the ellipsis ("soon...Score") reads as spammy, and it will truncate on mobile.
- The "Get Offer" button and the "Join ScoreCard+" button are stacked and both prominent, which splits the primary action.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Suppress or consolidate repeat sends.** Cap the same offer at one reminder per day, and send the last-chance email only to people who haven't opened or redeemed.
- 2. Show real app UI in the hero, such as the offer card on a phone with a "Tap to activate" state, instead of blank screens.
- 3. Split the headline into a main line, "$20 off $100+ in the app", and a subline, "ScoreCard+ members: $30 off". Move the asterisks into one fine-print line.
- 4. Cut the lower catalog to one or two blocks, or swap the category grid for three actual products with prices.
- 5. Make "Get Offer" the only prominent button, and turn "Join ScoreCard+" into a text link.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: $20 off $100+ in the app`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call, $20 off $100 (ScoreCard+: $30)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Open the app and tap "Get Offer." No code needed, and sale items are included.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `ScoreCard+ members get $30 off $100+. Valid until 11:59 PM PT tonight.`

## Technical Audit

Its QA warnings come from missing alt text on 12 images, and its header checks came back empty. The HTML was truncated, so the footer, links and CTAs could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links were reported. 12 material links were probed and 40 were skipped, and the QA output gives no per-link status. I can't confirm the skipped links.
- The tracking pixel is `<img src='https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/v61000001a1dfff68ee99e504369fe94b70/833ece…'>`, hidden with `display:none; max-height:0; mso-hide:all`. This is a standard open pixel. QA flags it for missing alt text, which is harmless because the image is hidden.
- The pixel's `src` uses single-quoted attributes, which is valid HTML.
- The unsubscribe link wasn't visible in the truncated source, so I can't verify its target.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 12 images.** `<img>` tags with no `alt` attribute are announced by screen readers by filename, and they show nothing when images are blocked.
  - Six category tiles: `drinkware1101stwdcondensed`, `accessories1101stwdcondensed`, `golf1101stwdcondensed`, `fanshop1101stwdcondensed`, `teamsports1101stwdcondensed`, `fitness1101stwdcondensed` (all `s7d6.scene7.com/...?fmt=jpeg&qlt=100&wid=1200&hei=1600`). If these are wrapped in `<a>`, those links have no accessible name.
  - Five footer icons on `dsg.images.harmony.epsilon.com/_phoenix/_dev/AEM/img/footers/`: `icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png`, `icon-location-2025.png`. Use `alt=""` if they are decorative.
  - The hidden tracking pixel, noted above.
- **Malformed CSS in the MSO conditional style.** The rule `div {background-color:inherit; font-size:0; line-height:100%; margin:0; padding:0;"}` contains a stray `"` before `}`. Outlook may drop the declaration that follows it. It sits inside `<!--[if (gte mso 9)|(IE)]>`, so only Outlook is affected.
- **Invalid JSON-LD block.** The `<script type="application/ld+json">` contains `//` comments, which are not valid JSON, so parsers will reject it. It also has empty values: `"description": ""`, `"discountCode": ""`, `"availabilityEnds": ""`, and `"image": ""`. `availabilityStarts` is a stale `2020-03-06`. Gmail's Promotions annotation will not render, and the leftover template instruction "DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!" is still in the source.
- **Leftover template comment.** `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` remains in the source.
- **Image size.** The tile images request `wid=1200&hei=1600` at `qlt=100`, which is heavy for a 600px container. I can't confirm the final rendered size or file weight from the truncated source.
- Positive findings: `<html lang="en">`, `<title>`, `role="presentation"` and web-font fallbacks (`Archivo, Arial, sans-serif`) are all present.
