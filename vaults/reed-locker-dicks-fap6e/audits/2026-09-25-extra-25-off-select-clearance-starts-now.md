# Extra 25% off select clearance starts now! 🚨
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-25**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-offer hero followed by DICK'S usual kitchen-sink catalog scroll — but at least the hero itself is legible and the offer is dead simple: "Extra 25% Off Select Clearance," starting now, deadline 9/26 11:59PM PT. Compared to the brand's last five sends (all "FLASH SALE" orange lightning-bolt reruns scoring 4-5/10), this is a different creative — dark red brick texture, bold white "25% OFF" — so at least the creative well isn't completely dry here.
- The problem is everything after the fold. Once you scroll past the hero and the six-pill category nav, it's eight more unrelated deals stacked with no hierarchy: running shoes, CALIA/VRST apparel, MLB gear, weighted vests, golf bags, then a "check out these picks" product grid. None of it reinforces the clearance hook — it dilutes it. A clearance shopper opens this for the 25% off, scrolls three screens, and lands on golf bags.
- Verdict: solid hero, weak follow-through — a middling clearance email that would benefit from either trimming the scroll or actually curating it around clearance categories.

## What's working

- Hero is bold, high-contrast, and instantly readable — "EXTRA 25% OFF" is the first and only thing your eye catches.
- Offer specificity is good: "Extra 25% Off Select Clearance," with a clear deadline (9/26 11:59PM PT) stated right under the CTA.
- Primary CTA ("Shop Now") is green, high-contrast, and impossible to miss directly below the headline.
- Category pill nav (Men's/Women's/Kids'/$25 and Under/Fan Shop/All Sale) gives an immediate self-segmentation option.
- No visible render bugs — images, buttons, and text all render cleanly.

## What's weak

- The email sprawls into seven unrelated deal blocks (shoes, CALIA apparel, MLB, weighted vests, golf bags, product grid) that have nothing to do with the clearance hook in the subject/hero.
- No loyalty/ScoreCard member pricing or points callout tied to this specific offer, despite ScoreCard being called out at the very top.
- "Select styles" / "Exclusions apply" appears immediately under the offer — expected, but there's zero indication anywhere of what's actually included in the 25% off.
- Visual hierarchy collapses after the hero — every subsequent block uses the same card/button pattern, so nothing beyond the top signals importance.
- Repeats the brand's pattern of burying a strong hero under an endless generic scroll (same structural complaint as the 9/22 "Slow your scroll" send, 4.00/10).

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the catalog scroll by half — keep clearance-adjacent blocks (Men's/Women's/Kids' clearance, $25 and under) and drop or move MLB/golf bags/weighted vests to a separate send; a clearance-titled email diluted by golf bags reads unfocused.
- 2. Add a second clearance CTA a few screens down (e.g., "Still shopping? Back to Clearance") since the golden hero CTA is only visible above the fold.
- 3. Name at least one category or brand included in the 25% off directly in the hero subtext, not just "select clearance styles" — specificity drives clicks.
- 4. Surface ScoreCard points multiplier or member-exclusive pricing tied to this sale if one exists — you already reference ScoreCard at the top, so connect it to the offer.
