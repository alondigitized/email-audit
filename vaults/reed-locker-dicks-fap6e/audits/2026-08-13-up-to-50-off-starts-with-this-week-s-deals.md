# Up to 50% off starts with this week's deals
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S back on the "up to 50% off" beach-ball pool hero, structurally identical to the 8/10 send ("Shop up to 50% off today 🙌") — same imagery, same weekly-digest sprawl underneath. The hero at least is clean this time: no cropped wordmark, centered "UP TO 50% OFF" tile with clear contrast against the pool scene, and a single "This Week's Deals" / Shop Now CTA right below it before the digest kicks in.
- But past the hero it's the same nine-module wall this brand keeps sending — shoes, clothing, drinkware, golf, gloves, life vests, back-to-school, then a six-item product grid. Nothing here signals "this week" specifically; it's the evergreen category rotation with a generic top-line offer that doesn't map to any one product. Subject and preview are also functionally identical to two prior sends this week, which is the real problem — a subscriber who opened Sunday's "up to 50% off" email has no reason to open this one.
- Judgement: solid execution of a stale format. This is cadence fatigue more than a creative failure.

## What's working

- Hero renders cleanly — "UP TO 50% OFF" is legible, well-contrasted, and not cropped like the 8/9 EPIC Shoe Sale send.
- "This Week's Deals" heading + single green Shop Now CTA gives a clear first action above the digest sprawl.
- Category chip nav (Men's/Women's/Kids'/Shoes/Team Sports/Back-to-School) gives scanners a fast way to skip to their category.
- No render bugs — images load, no overlapping text, no broken layout.
- Order/pickup utility bar and rewards line at top are functional and on-brand.

## What's weak

- Third near-duplicate "up to 50% off" hero/subject in the last four days — no differentiation from 8/10's send.
- The 50% off headline offer is never tied to a specific product below the fold — every module underneath has its own different discount (30%, 15%, $100 off, 45%, $40 off), so the hero number is essentially decorative.
- Nine stacked category modules before reaching a real "shop your category" moment — high scroll cost, no single hero product to click.
- Subject line is generic and repeats week-over-week language ("this week's deals") with no urgency or specificity.
- Preview text is boilerplate order/pickup copy, not a hook — wasted real estate.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero offer and make the whole top-of-email support it — if 50% off is the headline, show the actual category/products that hit 50% off directly under the hero, not a generic "This Week's Deals" button that dumps into everything.
- 2. Cut the digest to 3-4 modules max, or reorder so the best offer ($100 off Odyssey Putters, 45% off gloves) leads — currently the deepest discounts are buried mid-scroll.
- 3. Break the "up to 50% off" subject pattern for the next send — rotate to a category-led or urgency-led subject to reset open behavior after three near-identical sends.
- 4. Add a real time-bound deadline to the hero (dates are only in fine print at "Up to 30% Off" and "Up to 50% Off Clothing" modules) — surface it at the top.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New drops in your size: 45% off gloves, $100 off putters`
- **Subject Alt B:** `3 days left: up to 50% off select clothing`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus $100 off Odyssey putters and up to 40% off Stanley`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Gear up for fall — shoes, gloves, and BTS picks inside`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
Email uses Epsilon Harmony/DSG tracked links and images; core rendering markup is standard, but the automated QA run surfaced a broken unsubscribe link, missing email authentication headers, and 10 images lacking alt text.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- All probed links route through the Epsilon-hosted redirect domain `app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...` and `/O/.../` for pixel tracking — consistent with expected ESP tracking wrapper pattern.
- QA flagged a **broken redirect on the Unsubscribe link**: `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/U/v610000019ffaa0184bbc0c096e966a3578/c8d3dae7ef75425f0000021ef3a0bcff` resolves to `unknown url type: '/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription'`. This is a functional defect, not just compliance — see Section 5.
- 12 of 62 total links were probed (click-likelihood-ranked); 50 (mostly footer/utility/social) were skipped by design, so absence of findings there is not full coverage.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- 10 images missing `alt` text: the SSL/tracking pixel (`c8d3dae7ef75425f00004c5a42963aa1`), 4 product images (`clothes.png`, `hydrojug.png`, `gloves.jpg`, `life-vests.png`), and 5 footer icons (`icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png`, `icon-location-2025.png`). Screen readers will announce these as unlabeled/skip content.
- MSO/Outlook conditional comments and `@media screen and (max-width:480px)` mobile overrides are present and structurally intact (`.mobile-off`, `.header-xl`, etc.).
- The `<script type="application/ld+json">` block in `<head>` contains inline `//` comments (e.g., `// GIF & WEBP images are not supported...`), which is invalid JSON — if any client or scraper attempts to parse this as structured data it will fail. Also contains unfilled placeholder fields (`"discountCode": ""`, `"image": ""`, `"availabilityEnds": ""`), and a stale hardcoded `"availabilityStarts": "2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"`.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- No visible unresolved merge tags (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) in the truncated source.
- No issues found beyond the JSON-LD placeholders noted in Section 3.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **Unsubscribe link broken**: confirmed by QA — the tracked unsubscribe URL redirects to an internal error page (`/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription`). This is a CAN-SPAM functional risk (unsubscribe mechanism must work), not just a UX bug.
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found**: no `List-Unsubscribe` header captured, so mailbox-level one-click unsubscribe (Gmail/Yahoo "Unsubscribe" button) is likely unavailable. Given Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements, this is a deliverability risk for a sender at this volume.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found**: RFC 8058 one-click POST unsubscribe unsupported, compounding the above.
- **Authentication-Results header not found**: SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status could not be verified via the relay; cannot confirm authentication compliance from available data. (Caveat: this may be a limitation of the AgentMail relay capturing headers rather than the sender's own configuration — flagged as unknown, not confirmed-failing.)

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Tracked links use the Epsilon Harmony redirect domain (`app.dickssportinggoods.com`) rather than exposing UTM parameters directly in the visible href — UTM/campaign parameters are presumably appended server-side after redirect resolution and were not visible in the truncated source.
- Cannot confirm final landing page alignment (post-redirect destination, UTM tagging) from the truncated HTML/QA data provided — insufficient evidence to assess this category further.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Fix the unsubscribe redirect immediately — it currently 404s/errors to `/pc/Error/Error`, a legal-compliance-relevant functional break, not cosmetic.
2. Add `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers to enable one-click unsubscribe and improve inbox-provider deliverability standing.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 4 product images (`clothes.png`, `hydrojug.png`, `gloves.jpg`, `life-vests.png`) at minimum; decorative footer icons and the tracking pixel can use `alt=""` to be explicitly marked decorative rather than omitted.
4. Remove the invalid `//` comments from the `application/ld+json` block (or strip the block if unused) and either populate or remove the empty `discountCode`/`image`/`availabilityEnds` fields and the stale 2020 `availabilityStarts` date.
5. Verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC authentication results directly at the sending MTA (independent of the AgentMail relay) since it could not be confirmed here.
