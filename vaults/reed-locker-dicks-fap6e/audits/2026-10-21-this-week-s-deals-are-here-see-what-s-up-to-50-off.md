# This week's deals are here! 👏 See what's up to 50% off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent weekly-deals email with a loud hero. A big "UP TO 50% OFF" sits over fall leaves, with "This Week's Deals" and a green Shop Now button right under it. The eye lands on the number first. Below that, category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Team Sports) and a long stack of modules cover cleats, DSG clothing, a Stanley mug, fan gear, FootJoy golf shoes, fishing and a Sports Mom Shop.
- The problem is cadence and sameness. Yesterday (10/20) DICK'S sent "Ready... set... shop up to 50% off this week's DEALS!" with the same hero and the same weekly-deals structure. Today's send is the same email with a new subject. A subscriber who saw both will see a repeat. The "Up to 40% / 50% off" module headlines also blur together, so nothing in the body feels like a reason to click today rather than yesterday.
- Overall it is clean and on-brand but undifferentiated. It's a 6-7 send that works if you haven't seen the last one, and it mostly trains people to ignore the weekly deals email if you have.

## What's working

- The "Up to 50% OFF" hero is huge and high-contrast, and it reads in a second.
- The green Shop Now button sits directly under the hero headline, so the first action is obvious.
- The category pills let the reader jump straight to their own lane.
- A few modules break the "up to X%" pattern with something concrete: "$30 Off Select FootJoy Shoes" and the Stanley 1913 District Pulse Mug.
- The fall-leaf imagery fits the season, and the "Order Today. Sport It Tomorrow." banner at the top adds a real convenience benefit.

## What's weak

- It repeats yesterday's "this week's deals" send almost exactly, with the same hero and the same structure.
- Most modules say "Up to 40% / 50% Off Select…" with no product, price or reason to prefer one over another.
- The email is very long, with about eight stacked modules plus a pick grid and a text-alert banner, so the main offer gets diluted as you scroll.
- The CTAs are all a generic "Shop Now", and the urgency line, "Valid online only for a limited time", is vague with no deadline.
- ScoreCard is only a small link in the header. The loyalty angle that carried the 10/19 sends is absent.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending a near-identical weekly-deals email on back-to-back days. Either make today's version actually different (a named hero deal, a real end date) or hold it.
- 2. Replace the generic hero with one specific, dated hook, such as "Deals end Sunday" or "Today only: extra $X off a named product". Then cut the module stack to three or four.
- 3. Put real products and prices in the modules. For example, "Under Armour cleats from $XX" works better than "Up to 40% off select cleats".
- 4. Make the CTAs specific ("Shop Cleats", "Shop Fan Gear") and add a ScoreCard line, such as member points or pricing, near the top.
- 5. Cut the subject to under 50 characters, and make the preheader add information instead of reusing the standard pickup and shipping banner.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your weekly deals: $30 off FootJoy, 50% off DSG 👏`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus up to 40% off Under Armour cleats. Ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New this week: Stanley mugs, fan gear and fishing deals.`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email has a missing plain-text part (9 chars), four tracked "Shop Now" links that timed out during probing, and 11 images without alt text. Several compliance and authentication checks couldn't be confirmed because the AgentMail relay doesn't capture those headers.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- All four failed probes hit the same click-tracking redirector: `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/mclick?params=b.v1.wV8m2DSo...` with link text "Shop Now". The error was "The read operation timed out", and the four URLs differ only in `tuid` (`...ab21`, `...ab2b`, `...ab2d`, `...ab2f`).
- The failure is a read timeout, not a 4xx/5xx. This could be a slow redirector, or the probe could be throttled or blocked by the ESP's bot filtering. The QA data can't tell these apart, so I can't confirm a broken link for real recipients.
- Coverage is partial: 12 links were probed and 56 skipped (footer, social and utility links were deprioritized). The unsubscribe link was always included, and no failure is reported for it.
- The open-tracking pixel `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=...` is a 1x1 `<img>` with no `alt`. It is the first element in `<body>`, ahead of the table. This is common practice, but screen readers may announce it.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- 11 images are flagged as missing alt text:
  - Hero and promo banners: `1021-stw-40-off-ua-cleats`, `1021-stw-50-off-dsg-clothing`, `1020stanleydistrictpulsebanner`, `1021-stw-50-off-fan`, `1021-stw-50-off-fishing`.
  - Icons: `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`.
  - The open pixel (listed above).
- If the banners carry the offer text (for example "50% off"), image-blocking clients show nothing. The deal copy is not reliably available as live text in the source I was given. The HTML was truncated, so I can't verify that.
- The `<html lang="en">`, viewport meta, `role="presentation"` on layout tables and MSO conditional comments are all present. No issues there.
- The scene7 URLs request `wid=1200&hei=1600&fit=constrain` with `qlt=100`. That is large for a 600px email. This is a payload concern, not a break.
- The preheader (`#ssl`) contains "Get $20 off $100+ when you sign up for Text Alerts**" instead of content that previews the subject line. The trailing `**` suggests a disclaimer is expected somewhere. The truncated HTML doesn't let me confirm it.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. The visible source contains no unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`). The source is truncated, so this covers only the portion shown.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may strip these, so this is unverified, not confirmed missing. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so the raw message headers need checking.
- `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `em.dcsg.com` matches the tracking domain `data.em.dcsg.com`, which is a good sign but not proof of authentication.
- The plain-text part is 9 characters, which is effectively empty. This is a deliverability and accessibility defect. It affects spam scoring and text-only clients.
- The physical postal address and unsubscribe link are in the footer, which is cut off in the truncated source. They can't be verified from the HTML provided. The QA probe did include an unsubscribe link and reported no failure for it.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- All CTA URLs are wrapped in the ESP redirector (`/ee/v1/mclick?params=b.v1...`). Any UTM parameters are encoded inside the opaque `params` blob, so I can't audit them.
- I can't confirm landing-page alignment because the four "Shop Now" probes timed out before resolving a destination.

**7. Recommendations**
1. **Plain text:** generate a real multipart/alternative text part with offers, links and the unsubscribe URL. This is the only confirmed FAIL on the deliverability side.
2. **Link probes:** re-run the four "Shop Now" probes with a longer read timeout and a realistic User-Agent, or time `data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/mclick` directly. Only escalate if it is still slow or failing.
3. **Alt text:** add descriptive `alt` text to the five banners (include the offer, such as "Up to 50% off clothing"). Use `alt=""` on the decorative icons and the 1x1 pixel. Add real labels to the icons if they sit next to text that doesn't already describe them.
4. **Headers:** inspect the raw message in a direct-delivery inbox for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Don't treat the relay warnings as sender defects until you've done that.
5. **Image weight:** request the scene7 images at about 1200px wide (2x for a 600px layout) with `qlt` of 80–85, and drop `hei=1600`.
