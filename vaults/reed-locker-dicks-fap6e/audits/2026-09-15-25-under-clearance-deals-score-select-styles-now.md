# $25 & under clearance deals! 💸 Score select styles now...
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- This is the cleanest "$25 AND UNDER" hero DICK'S has sent yet — big white type on a dark red gradient, legible and unambiguous, echoing the same clean-hero approach the brand used on 9/13. But it's a repeat beat: the 9/13 send already ran "$25 & under" twice in one day, so this is now the third time in three days on the exact same hook, and cadence fatigue is real.
- The bigger problem is what happens after the hero. The email sprawls into eight-plus unrelated categories — clothing, drinkware, cleats, golf apparel, camping gear, weights, a second "UP TO 50% OFF" banner, ScoreCard signup, and a product grid — none of which is "$25 and under." The subject promises one thing; the body delivers an entire storewide circular. That mismatch, repeated across nearly every DICK'S send in this history, is the brand's core structural issue, not a one-off mistake.
- Judgement: legible hero, honest subject-to-top-of-email match, but zero category focus and a third consecutive day of the same "$25 and under" framing. Solid open bait, weak coherence once opened.

- Hero art is the strongest and most legible of the recent stretch — bold white "$25 AND UNDER" on high-contrast red, readable instantly even scaled down.
- Category pill nav (Men's/Women's/Kids'/Shoes/All Clearance/All Sale) right under the hero gives a fast way to route without scrolling.
- ScoreCard loyalty block is present and clearly explains the earn rate (1 point per $1, 300 = $10), which is a real, honest member benefit.
- No render bugs — images load, text doesn't overlap, layout holds together end to end.

## What's weak

- The email is a full storewide circular (clothing, drinkware, cleats, golf, camping, weights, apparel picks) stapled onto a "$25 and under" subject and hero — most of it isn't $25 and under at all.
- A second, redundant "UP TO 50% OFF" red banner appears mid-email, diluting the hero's own offer and repeating creative DICK'S has run daily this week.
- This is the third "$25 & under" framed send in three days (9/13 sent it twice already), which will read as stale/repetitive to anyone opening daily.
- BOGO golf clothing block uses a fine-print dagger symbol (†††) next to the offer, which is the kind of buried-condition signal that erodes trust on a "score low prices" email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email down to actually-$25-and-under merchandise, or rename the send if it's going to be a general circular — right now the promise breaks the moment you scroll past the hero.
- 2. Drop the mid-email "UP TO 50% OFF" banner — it's a different, competing offer that undercuts the hero's own clarity and has appeared in nearly every recent send.
- 3. Space out the "$25 & under" hook — it's run three times in three days; rotate to a different category or price-point hook to avoid fatigue.
