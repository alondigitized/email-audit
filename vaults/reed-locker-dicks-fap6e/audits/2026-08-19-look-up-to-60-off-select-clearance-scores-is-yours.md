# Look! Up to 60% OFF select CLEARANCE scores is yours! 👀
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-19**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S clearance edition of the same weekly-digest machine they've been running all month — the "UP TO 60% OFF SELECT CLEARANCE" hero is a moody dusk-lake photo with a big red/white price-tag graphic, then it immediately sprawls into ScoreCard+ bonus reward, Nike favorites, category tiles, and a six-item product grid. It's clean and legible, but it's the seventh-plus near-identical structural clone of 8/16's "Up to 50% off awaits" send — same layout skeleton, same category-tile row, same "explore more deals" pattern, just swapped art and a bumped discount ceiling (50%→60%).
- The hero itself is actually a step up creatively from the flat green locker-room background DICK'S has leaned on all week — the dusk-lake image gives it a bit more atmosphere. But "up to 60% off" with no named category and "select clearance styles" fine print underneath is the same vague-clearance bait this brand keeps running, and clearance emails historically underperform their "up to 50% off" wordmark siblings on clarity since there's no anchor item.
- Verdict: solid execution, stale format. The creative refresh (new hero photo) isn't enough to offset six-plus days of structurally identical sends — subscriber fatigue risk is real here.

## What's working

- Hero is unambiguous: "UP TO 60% OFF SELECT CLEARANCE SCORES" in large red/white type reads instantly, even at a glance.
- Single clear green "Shop Now" button sits directly under the hero with no scrolling needed.
- ScoreCard+ $20 Bonus Reward section has strong visual contrast (black background, neon green accents) and a credible deadline ("Valid through 8/19/26 11:59PM PT").
- Product grid at the bottom (Nike backpack, socks, Samba shoes, New Balance 530, etc.) gives concrete, clickable items rather than just category tiles.
- No render bugs — images, buttons, and text all display cleanly with no overlap or broken assets.

## What's weak

- No specific category or product is named in the hero — "select clearance scores" tells the reader nothing about what's actually discounted.
- This is a near-identical structural rerun of 8/16, 8/17, and 8/18 sends — six-plus days of the same digest shape is fatigue-inducing for anyone opening daily.
- Fine print undercuts the offer immediately ("Exclusions apply... Savings off original prices. Interim markdowns may have been taken") — buries how real the 60% actually is.
- The email is extremely long (10+ sections: hero, ScoreCard+, Nike, golf/outdoor/team sports, drinkware/fan shop/fitness, six-item picks) — no one is scrolling through all of this on a commute.

## Recommendations

- 1. Name the actual discounted category or a hero product in the subject and hero copy (e.g., "Up to 60% off clearance footwear") instead of the generic "select clearance scores" — specificity drives both open and click.
- 2. Cut the email length by half — pick the top 2 sections (hero + one merchandising block) and drop the rest into a "shop more" footer link instead of full tile rows.
- 3. Vary the hero treatment more aggressively from the last six sends — the dusk-lake photo is a good start, but the layout order (hero → ScoreCard+ → Nike → categories → picks) is now on repeat; shuffle the sequence or lead with the product grid instead.
- 4. Move the exclusions/fine-print language further down or shrink it — right now it sits directly under the CTA and dampens the offer's credibility.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Clearance shoes: up to 60% off, ends soon`
- **Subject Alt B:** `60% off clearance is here — shop before it's gone`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sneakers, apparel, and gear — marked down while supplies last.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New clearance drops daily. Grab yours before sizes sell out.`
