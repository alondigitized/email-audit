# Up to 40% OFF select shoes 🙌 Save BIG on Nike, adidas & more top brands
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, well-structured DICK'S promo with a clear lead offer. The hero is an oversized "40% OFF" over a trio of sneakers, and the headline "Up to 40% Off Shoes for the Family" sits right under it with a green Shop Now button. The eye lands on the offer first. Compared with the clearance blasts this week ("Act fast! Extra 25% off select clearance ENDS TODAY," 10/17), it is calmer and more category-led.
- The body is a long stack of near-identical modules: clothing, drinkware, fan gear, bats and outdoor, each with a grey "Shop Now" button. Only shoes get the green button and the hero, which is correct. But the offers dilute quickly: 40%, 40%, 50%, 40%, 50%. The "Limited Time" tag has no date, so the email has no real deadline. That's a step back from the "ENDS TODAY" send earlier today.
- Verdict: solid mid-funnel promo. It's strong on layout and brand trust, and weak on urgency and product-level specificity. The six-pack of sneaker and apparel picks at the bottom is where the actual product is, and it's buried below the fold.

## What's working

- The hero ("40% OFF" behind real Nike/adidas-style sneakers) is legible and ties directly to the subject line.
- The green Shop Now button is visible and unambiguous. It is the only high-contrast CTA on the page.
- The brand names (Nike, adidas, Under Armour, YETI, Owala, Stanley) are repeated in the sub-copy, which builds trust.
- The top strip with ScoreCard points and the "Order Today. Sport It Tomorrow." fulfilment line gives useful value-adds without cluttering the hero.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- "Limited Time" has no end date, so it carries no urgency. This follows an "ENDS TODAY" send, which makes it feel even softer.
- Every module below the hero uses the same grey "Shop Now" button. They all look identical, so there is no signal about which to click.
- The "Check out these picks" product grid has no prices or sale tags. Shoppers can't tell if the 40% applies to those items.
- The shoe hero is shown with no people, and the Walker persona sees no fit, size or style cue.
- The fine print under the hero ("Select styles. While quantities last. Offer may not be valid in conjunction with other sales or products in this email") undercuts the headline. "Up to 40%" with exclusions reads as a ceiling, not a deal.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a hard deadline in the hero and the tag, for example "Ends Sunday" in place of "Limited Time." This single change would lift both open and click rates.
- 2. Move the product grid ("Check out these picks") up under the hero, and add sale prices and "% off" badges to each item.
- 3. Cut the five secondary categories to two or three, and make the buttons category-specific ("Shop Hoodies," "Shop Bats") instead of repeating "Shop Now."
- 4. Shorten the subject to under 50 characters and drop the all-caps "BIG."
- 5. Use the preheader for the offer or deadline, and move the generic fulfilment copy into the header strip.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Shoes for the whole family: up to 40% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Select styles from Nike, adidas & more. Ends Sunday.`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email's markup looks sound (`lang="en"`, viewport meta, `role="presentation"` layout tables, MSO conditionals). However, the QA data shows a near-empty plain-text part (9 chars) and many images with no alt text. Header-based checks (List-Unsubscribe, SPF/DKIM) could not be confirmed because the AgentMail relay didn't expose them.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- QA probed 12 of 64 links and skipped 52. The source was truncated, so I can't confirm the status of the other 52, including footer links.
- The open-tracking pixel is `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1.…&pid=…&tuid=…&configId=…`. It is 1x1 and has no `alt`, which is expected for a pixel but is flagged by QA.
- The pixel sits directly after `<body>`. If images are blocked, opens go untracked, which is normal.
- No broken links were reported in the probed set.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Missing alt text (confirmed by QA, 11 images).** Affected `src` values:
  - `…/DSGAEMSites/2selectclothign1016stwd6am`
  - `…/email-marketing/dks/outdoor/2026/_banners/2026-0623-dks-outdoor-40-off-drinkware-…` (banner4.gif)
  - `…/DSGAEMSites/fanshop-2026-0929-dks-swd-6am`
  - `…/DSGAEMSites/2026-1017-dks-storewide-4pm-3`
  - `…/DSGAEMSites/outdoor-2026-1015-dks-swd-6am`
  - `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`
  - Most of these appear to be content or banner images. With images blocked, or in a screen reader, the offer messaging would be lost. The `icon-vc-*` images may be decorative, but they should carry `alt=""` explicitly rather than omit the attribute.
- **Stale banner asset.** The banner filename `2026-0623-dks-outdoor-40-off-drinkware-` is dated June 23, but this email went out October 17. The filename alone doesn't prove the content is stale, but it should be checked.
- **Images use `wid=1200&hei=1600&fit=constrain`.** Scene7 serves 1200px-wide assets into a 600px container. That's fine for retina, but the payload is heavier than necessary. I can't verify the file sizes.
- **Web font.** `fonts.gstatic.com` Archivo is referenced inside `@media screen` with an `Arial, sans-serif` fallback. This is handled correctly.
- **Hidden preheader spacer.** `#pht-spacer` uses `display:none; max-height:0; mso-hide:all`, which is the standard pattern.
- **`<table class="wrapper" width="600" … width:100%; max-width:600px>`.** `max-width` isn't supported in some Outlook versions, but the `width="600"` attribute and MSO ghost tables cover that.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. The visible source contains no unresolved merge tokens such as `{{`, `%%`, or `[FIRSTNAME]`. The tracking pixel carries a `tuid` parameter, which is rendered. The source was truncated, so I can't check the body or footer.

**5. Compliance**
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058): not found.** QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay stripping headers. This is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers, so verify them against the raw message at the origin.
- **Authentication-Results: not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `em.dcsg.com` are unverified. Check the raw headers.
- **Plain-text part: FAIL.** The text version is 9 characters, which is effectively empty. That is a deliverability risk, since spam filters penalize HTML-only and near-empty text parts. It also hurts users on text-only clients. It could be a relay artifact, but QA flags it as a failure.
- **Physical address and unsubscribe link in the body.** The truncated source doesn't show the footer, so I can't confirm either. QA says an unsubscribe link was included in the probe set, but it didn't report the result.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity**
- I can't evaluate UTM parameters or destination alignment. The truncated HTML has no visible `href` values, and QA reports no per-link results beyond the 12 probed.
- The subject promises "Up to 40% OFF select shoes … Nike, adidas & more". Whether the CTA destinations match that offer can't be confirmed from the data.
- The `class="arc-link"` anchors suggest redirect-based click tracking. Check that the redirect chain resolves to dickssportinggoods.com and keeps campaign parameters.

**7. Recommendations**
1. **Fix the plain-text part (priority).** Generate a full multipart/alternative text version with the offer, key links, the unsubscribe link and the address. Deliverability is the only QA failure.
2. **Verify headers on the raw message.** Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass for `em.dcsg.com`. Don't rely on the relay's copy.
3. **Add alt text to the content images.** Use descriptive text for the promo banners (for example "Up to 40% off select shoes"). Use `alt=""` for the decorative `icon-vc-*` and `icon-location-2025-1` images.
4. **Review the `2026-0623` drinkware banner.** Confirm it is intentional and not a leftover from a June template.
5. **Re-run the audit on the untruncated HTML.** That would cover the 52 skipped links, the footer compliance elements, UTM presence and merge tokens.
