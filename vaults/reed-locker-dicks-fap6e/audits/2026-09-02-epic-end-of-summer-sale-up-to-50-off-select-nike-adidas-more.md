# Epic End-of-Summer Sale: Up to 50% off select Nike, adidas & more!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-02**
## Executive summary

- This is the same red/maroon stadium-seating "UP TO 50% OFF" hero DICK'S has now run repeatedly since 8/30 — first as "Send off summer," then "Epic End-of-Summer Sale" on 8/31, again on 9/1, and here it is a third or fourth outing of that exact creative under yet another subject line variant. The brand logos strip (Nike, HOKA, adidas, TaylorMade, DSG) up top is a nice touch for scanability, but the email itself is a wall of near-identical modules — clothing, team sports, golf, drinkware, fan shop, outdoor, fitness — each with the same "Up to X% Off" header and gray pill buttons, with zero differentiation in urgency or visual pacing.
- The render is clean (no broken images, no overlapping text), and the hero does clear the "visible without scrolling" bar for a desktop capture, but there's no real news here. Anyone who's opened DICK'S mail this week has seen this exact stadium hero already, and the deep, seven-module scroll is fatigue-inducing rather than persuasive. Judgement: technically solid, creatively stale — this is cadence-fill, not a send designed to convert.

## What's working

- Brand-name draw upfront: Nike, HOKA, TaylorMade, adidas, DSG logos give instant category recognition before any scrolling.
- Hero offer ("UP TO 50% OFF") is bold, high-contrast, and unmissable at the top of the email.
- Category chip row (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Golf, Outdoor) gives quick self-select navigation right under the hero.
- Clean render — no layout breakage, no lorem ipsum, no broken product images across any of the seven modules.
- "Free ship to store" and curbside pickup call-outs are specific, useful details buried mid-email rather than generic filler.

## What's weak

- This is the same maroon stadium-seat "UP TO 50% OFF" creative from 8/30–9/1 with only the subject swapped again — a subscriber has now seen this exact hero at least three times in four days.
- Seven back-to-back category modules (clothing, team sports, golf, drinkware, fan shop, outdoor, fitness) with no visual hierarchy differentiation — everything reads as equally (un)urgent.
- No real deadline — "Limited Time" appears once under the hero with no date/time, so the urgency is generic and easy to ignore.
- No loyalty/ScoreCard member pricing or points call-out despite ScoreCard being in the top bar — a missed personalization opportunity.
- CTA copy is repetitive and generic across modules ("Shop Now" x6+), with no distinct action language per category.

## Recommendations

- 1. Retire the maroon stadium hero for at least one cycle — reusing the identical creative for a 3rd–4th time in under a week is actively training subscribers to skim past it. Swap in a fresh background or product-forward hero shot.
- 2. Cut the email from seven modules to three or four — lead with the top two performing categories (footwear, fall team sports) and link the rest to a "Shop All Deals" landing page instead of stacking every department.
- 3. Add a real countdown or explicit end date/time to the "Limited Time" line — even "Ends Sunday 11:59pm ET" would materially lift urgency over the current vague label.
- 4. Surface ScoreCard member perks (bonus points, early access) somewhere in the top third — the logo's already in the header bar, so there's an easy personalization lever going unused.
