# Up to 40% OFF top brands is something you'll want to see 👉
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, scannable DICK'S promo with a huge gold "UP TO 40% OFF" hero on deep green and one red "Shop Now" button. A reader knows the offer within a second. The hero and the copy under it ("Up to 40% Off Top Brands", naming Nike, Jordan, adidas, HOKA and Under Armour) match the subject line, so the email delivers what the subject promised.
- The body is the weak part. After the hero come about seven more category blocks, each with its own "Shop Now" button and its own discount: up to 30% off boots, up to 50% off drinkware, a TaylorMade golf ball deal, up to 50% off bikes, bats and fan gear, and cardio equipment. A product grid and a long footer follow. The 40% headline gets buried, and for the 11/17 "up to 40% off Nike, Jordan, adidas" send, the same hero already existed.
- Verdict: a solid, competent send that is a repeat of the 11/17 creative. For a subscriber, the brand is on its fifth or sixth discount email in four days, so this one is easy to skip.

## What's working

- The gold-on-green "UP TO 40% OFF" hero is impossible to miss and sits at the top of the email.
- The brand names under the hero (Nike, Jordan, adidas, HOKA, Under Armour) make the offer concrete.
- The top strip ("Order Today. Sport It Tomorrow.", same-day delivery) and the ScoreCard Points banner add a useful, credible incentive above the hero.
- The "Explore more deals" pill navigation (Men's, Women's, Kids', Clearance, Fan Shop, All Sale) lets people jump to their category.
- The visuals are consistent and seasonal, with holiday styling in the boots, drinkware and bike shots.
- No broken images or layout bugs are visible.

## What's weak

- The email has too many competing offers: 30%, 40%, 50% and a "2 for $100" golf ball deal, each with its own CTA. This dilutes the headline promise.
- Every CTA is the generic "Shop Now". None names a product or category.
- The hero is not tied to a specific product. Only the brand list says what is discounted.
- The email is very long, about 6,100px, with a gift picks grid and heavy footer legalese. Most of that sits far below where a mobile reader stops scrolling.
- It follows several near-identical "up to X% off" sends (11/17 and 11/18), so there is no sign of a fresh angle.
- The fine print on the golf deal ("Excludes NFL SpeedSoft golf balls") is small and tucked below the block.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body to the hero plus two or three modules that support the 40% brand story, for example a footwear block and an apparel block. Drop the bikes, bats and cardio blocks, or move them into a separate send.
- 2. Make the CTAs specific: "Shop Nike & Jordan", "Shop Women's adidas", "Shop Kids' Deals".
- 3. Put brand logos or a product shot in the hero, so a shopper sees what is on sale rather than only a number.
- 4. Differentiate from the 11/17 send. Add a real deadline ("Ends Sunday") or a ScoreCard bonus angle.
- 5. Move the gift picks grid above the fold or drop it, and shorten the footer.
