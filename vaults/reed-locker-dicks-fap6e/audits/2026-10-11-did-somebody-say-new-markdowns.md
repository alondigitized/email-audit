# Did somebody say NEW markdowns?! 🙋‍♀️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, loud clearance send. The hero is a giant white "UP TO 70% OFF" over autumn leaves, with "New Markdowns Added: Up to 70% Off Select Clearance" and a Shop Now button directly underneath. The eye lands on the offer first. The subject's "NEW markdowns" promise is paid off in the first headline, which is a good match.
- It's also the 4th clearance push in 3 days. On 10/11 there was "Up to 70% OFF select clearance + NEW markdowns just dropped", on 10/10 "Tick, tock! EXTRA 25% off... ends tonight", and on 10/9 "Reduced prices? Yes, please! Score an EXTRA 25% off". The 70% number and the "new markdowns" angle repeat the earlier send from the same day almost exactly. A subscriber would see this as more of the same, and the "new" claim is the weakest part. Below the hero it turns into a long, generic stack of "Up to 40–50% off" category tiles, so the clearance focus fades fast.
- Verdict: it executes well but adds nothing new. It works as a reminder and not as a reason to open.

## What's working

- The "70% OFF" hero is huge, high contrast and seasonal, so the offer is unmistakable.
- The Shop Now button sits right under the hero headline, with no friction between offer and action.
- The row of category chips (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 and Under, Fan Shop, All Sale) gives quick routes into the sale.
- A "Final Hours" Fan Shop block with a visible 10/12 11:59PM PT date adds credible urgency.
- The layout renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlapping text, and the product picks near the bottom (Samba, Nike Club Fleece) add concrete products.

## What's weak

- It's the 4th near-identical clearance send in 3 days, and the 70% hero is a repeat of the earlier 10/11 email.
- "Up to 70%" is vague, and the hero names no product. The fine print says it's "select" and "while quantities last", and the discount is a ceiling nobody can verify.
- Below the hero, six near-identical "Up to 40–50% off" tiles dilute the message with grey, low-contrast buttons. The "This Week's Deals" 50% block repeats the offer a second time.
- Every CTA says "Shop Now" or similar, with no verb-plus-noun specificity.
- The footer fine print is dense, with several asterisks and exclusions, and the "Limited Time" hero has no explicit end date.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make "new" mean something. Lead with 3 or 4 actual newly marked-down products with was/now prices instead of a generic 70% banner. That's the single biggest lift.
- 2. Cut the page by half. Drop the generic category tiles and the repeated 50% block, and keep clearance, one fan-shop callout and the product picks.
- 3. Put an end date in the hero ("Ends Sunday"), not just in the Fan Shop block.
- 4. Make the CTA specific, such as "Shop clearance up to 70% off".
- 5. Space out the clearance sends or rotate the angle (a category, a brand, a price point) so the cadence doesn't feel like one email resent.
