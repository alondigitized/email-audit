# Up to 50% off awaits in this week's deals 💸
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-16**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S sixth "up to 50% off" wordmark send in a week, and it's the most sprawling weekly-digest version yet — a green locker-room "UP TO 50% OFF" hero gives way to a long scroll of ten-plus category tiles (shoes, DSG clothing, baseball/softball gloves, Odyssey putters, Quest kayaks, MLB gear, back-to-school, credit card, and a "check out these picks" product grid). Nothing is broken, but nothing is prioritized either — nine different offers compete for the same visual weight, so there's no single thing to click.
- Compared to 8/13's near-identical "Up to 50% off starts with this week's deals" (6.00/10), this is the same beach-ball-pool-hero format swapped for a locker hero, same weekly-digest sprawl underneath. The brand is clearly recycling the "weekly deals roundup" template on a near-weekly cadence, and it reads as filler between the sharper single-offer Flash Sale sends (8/15's countdown-bar execution scored highest of the run at 7.00).
- Judgment: a competent, on-brand digest email that will get opened on subject-line recognition alone but underperforms on click because it never picks a hero product or offer to lead with.

## What's working

- Hero is clean and legible — bold "UP TO 50% OFF" on a green chalkboard-style locker background, no render bugs.
- Category nav bar (Men's/Women's/Kids'/Shoes/Team Sports/Back-to-School) gives quick wayfinding right under the hero.
- Individual tile offers are specific and varied (30% off shoes, 60% off DSG family clothing, 45% off gloves, $100 off Odyssey putters, $170 off Quest kayaks, 50% off MLB gear) rather than one vague blanket claim.
- Credit card module clearly states the loyalty hook: "Earn 10% Back in Rewards... new cardholders earn a $30 Bonus Reward."
- Bottom "Check out these picks" product grid with real product shots and names (Nike Varsity Elite Backpack, adidas Samba OG Shoes, etc.) adds concrete, shoppable specificity.

## What's weak

- No single hero product or CTA — nine near-equal-weight offer tiles create decision paralysis, unlike 8/15's countdown-bar send which had one clear focal point.
- Subject/hero promise "up to 50% off" but the actual best-match tile buried mid-scroll is only 30-60% off across totally different categories — the headline number isn't tied to any one visible product.
- This is the third "weekly deals" digest format in six days (8/13, and now 8/16) with the same sprawling tile structure, just a different hero color — cadence fatigue is real for a daily subscriber.
- "Shop Now" is repeated 8+ times with zero differentiation — no CTA copy tells you what's actually in each tile beyond the tile headline itself.
- Fine print immediately under the hero ("Valid online only for a limited time. While quantities last. Other offers may be available. Details") undercuts urgency before the reader even scrolls.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero offer (the $170 kayak deal or the 60% DSG family clothing deal are the most differentiated/high-ticket) and lead with it above the fold instead of a generic "up to 50%" wordmark — that's the single change that would most lift click-through, since right now the hero promise doesn't map to any one product.
- 2. Cut the tile count roughly in half — nine competing offers is digest overload; consolidate similar categories (gloves + kayaks + putters could be one "Sports & Outdoor" module) to reduce scroll fatigue.
- 3. Vary CTA copy per tile ("Shop Kayaks," "Shop DSG Clothing") instead of repeating generic "Shop Now" eight times.
- 4. Move the credit card 10% back / $30 bonus module higher — it's a stronger, more personal hook than most of the mid-scroll category tiles and is currently buried two-thirds down.
- 5. Since this brand has sent five near-identical "up to 50%"-themed emails in six days, rotate in a genuinely different angle (single-category spotlight, or a "trending now" curated list) to break pattern fatigue for repeat openers.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your Quest kayak just dropped $170`
- **Subject Alt B:** `This week: 60% off DSG, 50% off MLB gear, more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Kayaks, cleats, clubs, and back-to-school — this week's best drops`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New markdowns on shoes, gloves, and family gear inside`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Marketing-cloud template (Iterable-hosted) with a broken unsubscribe link and no verifiable email authentication/compliance headers from the relay.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All CTAs and the header logo route through Iterable click-tracking redirects (`https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/v61000001a009652a85b64c066e96c65848/...?__dU__=...`) — standard redirect wrapping, no issue with the pattern itself.
- 12 of 67 links were probed by automated QA (by click-likelihood ranking); 55 skipped as low-priority footer/social/utility links — not itself a defect, but means footer/legal links are unverified.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 10 images missing `alt` text, including the invisible open-tracking pixel (`.../O/v61000001a009652a85b64c066e96c65848/94211e94830d455c00004c5a42963aa1`) and content images: `2026-0816-dks-stw-6am-kicks`, `2026-0814-dks-stw-4pm-threads2`, `2026-0816-dks-stw-6am-gloves`, `2026-0816-dks-stw-6am-kayaks`, `2026-0816-dks-stw-6am-mlb`, plus four footer icons (`icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png`, `icon-location-2025.png`). Screen reader users get no description of promo content.
- MSO/Outlook conditional block has a stray unescaped quote: `div {background-color:inherit; font-size:0; line-height:100%; margin:0; padding:0;"}` — the trailing `"` inside the CSS rule is malformed and could break the Outlook `<style>` block.
- JSON-LD `<script type="application/ld+json">` block contains inline `//` comments (`// GIF & WEBP images are not supported...`, `// Sample image is 538x138...`, `// DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!`) — JSON does not support comments, so this schema.org markup is invalid JSON and will fail to parse if consumed by any structured-data reader (e.g., Gmail annotations).
- Several `DiscountOffer`/`PromotionCard` JSON-LD fields are empty strings (`"description": ""`, `"discountCode": ""`, `"availabilityEnds": ""`, `"image": ""`) — appears to be an un-filled template left in production.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No merge-tag/personalization placeholders (e.g., `{{firstName}}`) visible in the truncated source; no unresolved tokens observed in the provided HTML.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Broken unsubscribe link (critical):** QA flagged the Unsubscribe link resolving to an error page — `unknown url type: '/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription'` from `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/U/v61000001a009652a85b64c066e96c65848/94211e94830d455c0000021ef3a0bd03`. This is a CAN-SPAM violation risk if recipients cannot successfully opt out.
- **List-Unsubscribe header missing:** No `List-Unsubscribe` header captured — may be a relay/capture limitation (AgentMail) rather than the sender's fault, but cannot be confirmed as present.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header missing:** No RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe support confirmed, which Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements (effective 2024) mandate for high-volume senders.
- **Authentication-Results header missing:** SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified via the relay.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Tracking links use Iterable's `/T/` redirect + `__dU__` token pattern rather than direct UTM-tagged destination URLs — final landing URLs and UTM parameters aren't visible in the truncated source, so continuity to the site cannot be verified from what's provided.

7. Recommendations
- Fix the unsubscribe endpoint immediately — `/pc/subscription` currently 404s/errors instead of processing opt-outs; this is the highest-priority issue.
- Add descriptive `alt` text to all 10 flagged images (empty `alt=""` is acceptable for the tracking pixel and purely decorative footer icons, but the 5 product/promo images need real descriptions).
- Fix the malformed CSS in the Outlook conditional comment (stray `"` after `padding:0;`).
- Remove `//` comments from the JSON-LD block or convert to valid JSON — as written it will fail JSON parsing.
- Populate or remove the empty `DiscountOffer`/`PromotionCard` JSON-LD fields rather than shipping placeholders.
- Confirm with the sending platform (Iterable) that `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and SPF/DKIM/DMARC are actually set at send time, since relay capture may just be masking them.
