# It's the LAST DAY for up to 50% off Flash Sale deals!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-24**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth "FLASH" hero DICK'S has run this week wearing the exact same orange lightning-bolt lockup — but the copy finally earns real urgency, since the deadline lands on send day (9/24, 11:59PM PT) and the subject correctly leads with "LAST DAY." The problem is the hero image itself is broken: the word "FLASH" is clipped at the right edge, rendering as "FLAS" with the last letter and any accompanying graphic cut off the canvas. That's a production bug sitting in the first thing a recipient sees.
- Below the fold it's the same kitchen-sink scroll DICK'S has repeated all week — fan shop, $100-and-under shoes, fleece, a Frost Buddy tumbler, cleats, then a six-item "Check out these picks" grid — none of which is personalized or reordered to reflect the actual urgency of the headline. If this is genuinely the last day of a sale, the whole email should be built around that single deadline, not buried it under seven more offers.
- Given the repeated creative fatigue documented in the brand history (this is the same hero as 9/23's "Up to 50% off starts now!" at 5.00/10) plus a visible clipping defect, this lands below that baseline.

## What's working

- "Ends Tonight" tag and "Valid... through 9/24/26 11:59PM PT" give the urgency real teeth — unlike prior sends this week where "last day" language wasn't backed by an actual same-day deadline.
- Clear primary CTA ("Shop Now") directly under the offer with good contrast (green button, white text).
- Loyalty context is visible up top — ScoreCard points balance and "Ship To Earn Points" — relevant to a member.
- Category quick-links (Men's/Women's/Kids'/Shoes/Fan Shop/Halloween Shop) give an easy secondary path if the hero doesn't convert.

## What's weak

- The hero image is visibly clipped — "FLASH" renders as "FLAS" with the final character cut off the canvas edge, a clear production defect.
- Identical orange lightning-bolt hero to the 9/23 "Up to 50% off starts now!" send — zero creative differentiation despite this supposedly being the campaign's final, most urgent day.
- The single most time-sensitive message DICK'S has sent this week gets diluted by eight unrelated offer blocks (fan gear, shoes, fleece, a drinkware launch, cleats) before you even reach the product grid.
- No countdown timer or visual urgency device (clock, banner) reinforcing "last day" beyond one small green tag.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the clipped hero image first — re-export or re-crop "FLASH" so the full word is visible; this is a visible bug undermining the one send this week that finally has real urgency behind it.
- 2. Cut the body to the flash sale only, or at minimum move it above all secondary categories — a true last-day deadline should dominate the email, not share equal billing with cleats and a tumbler drop.
- 3. Add a visual countdown or repeated "Ends tonight at 11:59PM" callout further down the email, since urgency fades fast once a reader scrolls past the hero.
- 4. Vary the hero creative from the 9/23 send — same photo/box/copy three times in a week reads as automation, not a real event.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last chance: 50% off ends at midnight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Flash Sale ends tonight — up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ends 11:59PM PT — shoes, fleece, fan gear and more`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Final hours: your ScoreCard points still apply`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
The email uses standard ExactTarget/SFMC tracked links and responsive CSS, but fails automated QA on plain-text fallback, missing List-Unsubscribe headers, and widespread missing alt text.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- CTA and content links route through `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/mclick?params=...&pid=...`, consistent with Salesforce Marketing Cloud click-tracking redirects — no malformed URLs observed in the sampled markup.
- Open-tracking pixel present: `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=...` (flagged only because it lacks `alt=""`, not because of the tracking itself).
- QA probed 12 of 60 total links (48 skipped by design per the tool's click-likelihood ranking); no broken/redirect-loop links were reported among the probed set.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 11 images missing `alt` text, including product imagery (`2026-0924-dks-swd-6am-jersey`, `-shoes`, `-clothing`, `-cleats`), a lifestyle image (`frost-buddy2-2026-0922-d`), several utility/social icons (`icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`), and the tracking pixel itself. Screen-reader users get no context for product content; decorative icons should get `alt=""` rather than omitting the attribute.
- MSO conditional comments and `@media screen` font-face/mobile blocks are present and correctly scoped; no structural rendering defects found in the truncated source.
- Hidden preheader spacer (`id="pht-spacer"`) uses zero-width joiners/nbsp padding — functions as intended for inbox preview control, not a defect.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No merge tokens (e.g., `%%FirstName%%`, AMPscript) visible in the truncated source. No issues found.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — QA notes this may be a relay artifact rather than a true absence; needs raw-header confirmation rather than treating as a confirmed CAN-SPAM violation.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe) not detected — if genuinely absent, this fails Gmail/Yahoo's 2024+ bulk-sender requirements.
- `Authentication-Results` header not found via the AgentMail relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unconfirmed — evidence gap, not a confirmed failure.
- An in-body unsubscribe link is confirmed present per QA notes ("an unsubscribe link is always included" in the probe set).

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Tracked links use SFMC's opaque `params=b.v1...` token format rather than plain UTM query parameters — campaign attribution is handled server-side by the mclick redirect rather than visible UTM tags in the HTML. No UTM parameters were observed to audit for consistency; this is a tooling/observability gap rather than a confirmed defect, since the destination URL after redirect resolution is not shown in the truncated source.
- Cannot confirm landing-page alignment without following the `mclick` redirect chain to its resolved destination.

7. Recommendations
- Add descriptive `alt` text to all 5 product/lifestyle images; add `alt=""` to the 5 decorative utility icons and the tracking pixel.
- Fix plain-text MIME part — currently 9 characters, effectively empty, which hurts spam scoring and violates CAN-SPAM's requirement for a functional text alternative.
- Confirm via raw SMTP/MIME headers (not just the AgentMail-relayed view) whether `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` are actually present; if absent, add both per RFC 8058 to stay compliant with Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender rules.
- Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment directly against `em.dcsg.com`'s sending infrastructure, since the relay's missing `Authentication-Results` header leaves this unverified.
- Resolve one `mclick` redirect to verify final landing-page URL and query-param structure before drawing conclusions about UTM/landing-page continuity.
