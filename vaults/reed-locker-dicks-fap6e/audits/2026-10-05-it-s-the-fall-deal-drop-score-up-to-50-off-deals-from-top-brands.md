# 🍂 It's The Fall Deal Drop 🍂 Score up to 50% off deals from top brands
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent, loud promo that undercuts itself. The hero is a big "FALL DEAL DROP / UP TO 50% OFF" over a stormy sky. Directly under it sits "3 Days of Savings," a Shop Now button, and a credible deadline of 10/7. A second banner then shouts "UP TO 70%" on a leaf-covered red background. A reader gets two headline numbers and has to work out that 70% applies only to clearance markdowns.
- This is also the second near-identical send of the day. This morning's "Starts NOW! Up to 50% off DEALS from brands you love 🙌 The Fall Deal Drop is here!" carried the same creative and the same three-day window. DICK'S has also run 50% and $25-and-under banners on 10/3 and 10/4. Cadence is heavy, so a subscriber will read this as a re-send and delete it.
- Below the hero, the module stack is orderly: running shoes, fleece, fan gear, golf, and a ScoreCard bonus block. Nothing is broken. Each tile has its own button, which is clean but dilutes the click. Judgement: solid execution on a tired, repetitive campaign.

## What's working

- The hero names the event and the discount in one glance. "UP TO 50% OFF" reads even at thumbnail size.
- The "Three Days Only" label and the 10/7/26 11:59PM PT deadline are specific and believable.
- The category chips (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Team Sports, Fan Shop) let people jump straight to their own aisle.
- The ScoreCard $10 / $20 Bonus Reward block gives members a concrete reason to click, with Get Coupon and Join Now buttons.
- The autumn palette (leaves, orange and green) fits the season, and the render has no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- Two competing headline numbers, 50% in the hero and 70% in the clearance banner, split attention and make the offer feel slippery.
- Every button says "Shop Now." None names a product or category, so the CTAs are generic.
- The discounts are mostly "up to" or "select." The 40%, 50%, 30% and 75% tiers apply only to select items, and the fine print under each module is dense.
- Nothing links to a single hero product. The product row near the bottom, "Check out these picks," is small and sits below a long scroll.
- The top-of-email message about same-day delivery has nothing to do with the Deal Drop. It uses prime space and adds noise.
- There is no review or social proof, and nothing signals why these deals differ from last week's.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one headline number. Make 50% the single hero and demote the 70% clearance block to a secondary strip, or lead with 70% if it's the real draw.
- 2. Make the CTAs specific: "Shop Running Shoes," "Shop Fleece," "Shop Fan Gear."
- 3. Move the "Check out these picks" product row, with prices, up to just below the hero.
- 4. Add one concrete example in the hero, such as "Brooks Ghost from $X," to prove the discount.
- 5. Space this send out from the morning send, or use the second send for a different angle, such as a last-chance reminder.
- 6. Replace the delivery line in the top bar with deadline copy.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Fall Deal Drop: up to 50% off, ends Wednesday 🍂`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Running shoes & fleece up to 50% off — 3 days only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Nike, Brooks, HOKA & more. Deals end 10/7 at 11:59 PM PT, plus up to 70% off clearance.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `ScoreCard members: grab a $10 or $20 bonus reward on top of the savings.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based, `role="presentation"` layout with `lang="en"`, an MSO conditional block and a viewport meta tag. The main defects are a missing plain-text part (QA FAIL, 9 chars) and 10 content or icon images with no `alt` attribute. The header-level checks (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) could not be verified because the relay didn't capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Every link seen in the truncated source goes through the ESP click-tracker, e.g. `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/mclick?params=b.v1...&pid=...&tuid=...`. The tracking domain `data.em.dcsg.com` shares the brand's `em.dcsg.com` sending subdomain, which is good for reputation alignment.
- QA probed 12 of 66 links and skipped 54 (footer, utility and social), and reported no broken links among the probed set. The skipped links were not validated.
- The open-pixel `data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=...` is flagged for missing alt text (see section 3).
- One image URL carries a hard-coded cache-buster: `.../is/content/DSGAEMSites/2026-1005-dks-storewide-4pm-3?cb=1790871110291`. It is also the only image served from `/is/content/` rather than `/is/image/`, so it has no `fmt`, `wid` or `hei` transform parameters.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** 11 `<img>` elements have no `alt`:
  - Content images: `2026-1005-dks-storewide-4pm-1`, `-5-new`, `-2-new`, `-3`, and `fanshop-2026-0929-dks-swd-6am`.
  - Icons: `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`.
  - Tracking pixel: `open`.
- **Image-only risk:** if the content images are the primary carriers of the offer and CTAs, a client with images blocked, or a screen reader, gets no usable content. The HTML shown is too truncated to confirm how much of the offer text is live text.
- **Image payload:** the scene7 sources request `wid=1200&hei=1600` (and `fit=constrain`), which is much larger than the 584px layout column. This is a likely weight and bandwidth issue, but actual rendered dimensions weren't in the excerpt.
- **Preheader:** the `#pht-spacer` div contains only `&zwnj;&nbsp;` filler and no preview text. That is consistent with the unfilled placeholder in section 4.
- **Positives:** `lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, a font stack with an `Arial, sans-serif` fallback, and `@media screen` guarding the `@font-face`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the truncated source.
- **Template placeholder left in production:** the comment `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` sits inside `td#ssl`, directly above the empty `#pht-spacer`. The template's secondary subject line / preheader slot appears not to have been filled in.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text fallback [FAIL]:** the text part is 9 characters, so effectively no multipart/alternative text. This hurts deliverability and accessibility, and is a spam-filter signal.
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) [WARN]:** neither header was found. QA notes that the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a verified sender defect. It matters in practice because Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results [WARN]:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because the relay doesn't expose the header. This should be checked at the source (raw message headers or a seed-list test) before treating it as a defect.
- **Footer:** the visible unsubscribe link and physical address are in the truncated portion, so they can't be confirmed from this data. QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result was reported for it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Destination URLs and UTM parameters can't be verified. Every href is an opaque `mclick?params=b.v1...` token, and the redirect targets weren't exposed in the HTML or the QA output.
- Landing-page alignment couldn't be assessed, and the probe summary reports no redirect-chain or landing-page mismatch data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a real plain-text part.** Generate it from the HTML, including offer copy, CTA URLs and the unsubscribe link, rather than the current 9 characters.
2. **Add `alt` to all 11 images.**
   - Content images: descriptive alt that carries the offer text.
   - Decorative icons: `alt=""`.
   - Tracking pixel: `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"`.
3. **Fill the SSL/preheader slot** in `td#ssl` and remove the `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` comment.
4. **Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`** on the original send (raw headers or a seed inbox), and likewise SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `em.dcsg.com`. Treat both as unconfirmed until checked outside the relay.
5. **Right-size scene7 requests** (e.g. `wid=1168` for 2x of 584px) and normalize the one `/is/content/` URL with `?cb=` to the `/is/image/` pattern the others use.
6. **Resolve a sample of mclick redirects** in QA to capture final URLs and verify UTMs. Widen the probe beyond 12 of 66 links so the 54 skipped links, including the footer and unsubscribe, get at least a status check.
