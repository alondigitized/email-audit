# We think you'll like this...Up to 70% off select clearance
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-03**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean DICK'S clearance send, and the hero does its job. A red argyle panel with a giant gold "UP TO 70% OFF" lands first. A "Limited Time" kicker and one red Shop Now button sit right under it. The offer is easy to read. The problem is that it doesn't commit to a message.
- After the hero, the email turns into a deals buffet. It adds a second "UP TO 50% OFF" hero for Holiday Deal Dash, then a third "Up to 40% Off Select Clothing & Shoes" block, then a six-tile category grid. That is three different discounts in one send. The Deal Dash block also says "Ends Tonight," which undercuts the hero's vague "Limited Time." Anyone who got the Nov 3 "Our Holiday DEAL Dash ends today!" email will see the same creative again.
- Judgement: a competent 6. The hero is strong, but the stacked offers dilute it. The deadline is also inconsistent. The real deadline (11/3/26 11:59PM PT) appears only on the secondary block.

## What's working

- The giant gold "UP TO 70% OFF" on red is the first thing the eye lands on.
- One red Shop Now button sits directly under the hero headline.
- The pill row (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 & Under, Fan Shop, All Sale) lets a shopper jump to a category.
- The SCORECARD bar with "300 Points = $10 Reward" shows a member benefit at the top.
- The "Order Today. Sport It Tomorrow." line with one-hour pickup and same-day delivery is a good value line.

## What's weak

- Three different discount tiers (70%, 50%, 40%) compete in one email, so there is no single message.
- The hero says only "Limited Time," while the 50% block says "Ends Tonight." The hero's own deadline is hidden in the small print.
- The "Ends Tonight" block has the same creative as the previous days' Deal Dash sends.
- The hero offers no product, only a percentage. "Select clearance styles" gives me nothing to picture.
- Fine print sits under every block. It says "interim markdowns may have been taken" and "prices as marked," which feels a bit hedgy.
- The bottom six-tile grid has category labels only, with no prices or offers.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the send down to one offer. Make clearance the hero and drop or demote the stale Deal Dash and 40% blocks. Or, if the Deal Dash is the real deadline story, lead with it.
- 2. Put the actual deadline in the hero kicker, for example "Ends tonight 11:59PM PT," in place of "Limited Time."
- 3. Add two or three real clearance products with sale prices under the hero. This shows what 70% off looks like.
- 4. Make the CTA specific: "Shop Clearance" instead of "Shop Now."
- 5. Add visible proof such as ratings or "Nike, adidas, Under Armour" brand names near the hero.
- 6. Give the grid tiles price hooks, for example "Drinkware from $X."

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Select styles from top brands, online only through 11:59PM PT.`

## Technical Audit: "We think you'll like this...Up to 70% off select clearance"

**1. Technical Summary**
The email is a table-based, `role="presentation"` layout with a viewport meta tag, MSO conditionals and a hidden preheader spacer. The confirmed defects are missing `alt` text on 13 images and malformed or unfinished code in the head (JSON-LD and the MSO style block). Compliance and authentication could not be verified from the captured data, and the supplied HTML is truncated.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- No broken links were reported. QA probed 12 material links and skipped 40, including footer, social and utility links, so link coverage is partial.
- The open-tracking pixel is `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/v61000001a1a2332e51b1fb28f4bbcfd118/4d83418c498044a000004c5a42963aa1`. It is HTTPS and hidden (`display:none; max-height:0; mso-hide:all`), which is expected. It has no `alt`, `width` or `height` attributes.
- Click-tracking wrapper URLs aren't visible in the truncated HTML, so redirect behavior can't be assessed.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Missing `alt` on 13 images (QA-confirmed):**
  - 7 content tiles on `s7d6.scene7.com/is/image/DSGAEMSites/`: `2clothing1103`, `drinkware1101stwdcondensed`, `accessories1101stwdcondensed`, `golf1101stwdcondensed`, `fanshop1101stwdcondensed`, `teamsports1101stwdcondensed` and `fitness1101stwdcondensed`.
  - 5 footer icons on `dsg.images.harmony.epsilon.com/_phoenix/_dev/AEM/img/footers/`: `icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png` and `icon-location-2025.png`.
  - The tracking pixel is the 13th. It is decorative, so the omission is low impact there.
  - If the content tiles are linked images, the links have no accessible name when images are blocked or read by a screen reader. The footer icons need `alt=""` if decorative, or descriptive text if not.
  - The footer image path contains `/_dev/`, which suggests assets are served from a dev path.
- **Heavy images:** The category tiles request `wid=1200&hei=1600&qlt=100` JPEGs. At `qlt=100` these are large relative to a 600px layout, which hurts load time on mobile.
- **Invalid JSON-LD:** The `<script type="application/ld+json">` block contains `//` comments, which are not valid JSON, so Gmail annotation parsing will fail. The template placeholders were also never filled in:
  - `"discountCode": ""`
  - `"availabilityEnds": ""`
  - `"image": ""`, with the leftover comment "DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!"
  - `availabilityStarts` is `2020-03-06`
- **MSO style block:** `div {background-color:inherit; font-size:0; line-height:100%; margin:0; padding:0;"}` has a stray `"` before the closing brace. It sets `font-size:0` on all `div`s for Outlook/IE, so any div text without an inline font-size could collapse in Outlook.
- **Outlook width:** `max-width:600px` on the wrapper is ignored by Outlook desktop. The `width="600"` attribute covers it, so this is minor.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or QA data. The truncated HTML limits this check.

**5. Compliance**
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unverified, not confirmed missing**. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both, so check the raw message headers at the source.
- `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `DSG@e.dcsg.com`. This also stems from the relay and needs verification on the raw message.
- The unsubscribe link and physical address are not visible in the truncated source. QA says an unsubscribe link was included in probing, but its result isn't shown.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity**
Not assessable. The truncated HTML shows no destination hrefs or UTM parameters. The only visible reference is the favicon at `www.dickssportinggoods.com/home/assets/icons/dsg/favicon.ico`. The `cb=1793305` cache-buster on one image URL is unrelated to campaign tracking.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 7 category tiles. Use `alt=""` on the footer icons if decorative, and add `alt=""` plus explicit `width` and `height` to the tracking pixel.
2. Check the raw message source for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. Don't treat the relay-driven warnings as confirmed failures.
3. Fix or remove the JSON-LD block: strip the `//` comments and either populate the `PromotionCard` and `DiscountOffer` fields or delete them.
4. Remove the stray `"` in the MSO `div` rule. Review `font-size:0` on `div`s for Outlook.
5. Lower the Scene7 tile size and quality (e.g. `wid=1200&qlt=80`, or `wid=600` with 2x only where needed). Move footer icons off the `/_dev/` asset path.
6. Re-run the audit on the full HTML to verify UTM parameters, unsubscribe and address presence, and click-tracking behavior across all 52 links.
