# 2 days ONLY! Score up to 50% off FLASH SALE deals
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-22**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S seventh-or-eighth promotional blast in roughly four days, and rebadging the offer as a "Flash Sale" does not make it feel like one. By July 22, any subscriber paying attention has seen "up to 50% off" in their inbox on July 18, 19, 19, 20, 20, 21, and 21 — so the 2-day urgency premise is already burned. The email's one genuinely new signal is the stacked "Extra 30% Off Select Clearance" offer: that's additive on top of existing markdowns, which is actually useful information. Everything else, including a recycled Nike Deal Days section lifted wholesale from the July 19 and July 20 sends, reads as familiar wallpaper.
- Structurally the email is well-assembled. The hero is immediately readable, the product tiers are organized, and the "MAKE YOUR $100+ INSTANTLY!" section buried mid-scroll is the most distinctive element in the whole send — and it's illegible at scroll speed. That's the tell: DICK'S has a potentially interesting hook and no idea where to put it.
- Verdict: a competent blast suffering from cadence exhaustion. The stacked clearance discount and flash-sale framing are worth keeping; the recycled Nike creative and the generic "SHOP NOW" CTA carpet make it forgettable.

## What's working

- **Hero reads in under a second.** "FLASH SALE / Up to 50% Off" on a locker-room background is visually distinct from the week's clearance-event treatment.
- **Stacked clearance offer is genuinely new.** "Extra 30% Off Select Clearance" on top of already-reduced items is the first incremental deal framing in this send cycle.
- **"2 days ONLY!" is the week's most explicit deadline.** Prior sends used softer urgency; this one commits.
- **Product category breadth is clear.** Shoes, clothing, and outdoor are all visible in a single scroll without hunting.
- **Clean render.** No broken images, no layout collapse, no overlapping copy visible in the screenshot.

## What's weak

- **Nike Deal Days section is recycled from July 19 and July 20.** Any subscriber who opened those emails will recognize the creative on contact — kills the flash-sale novelty.
- **"SHOP NOW" is the only CTA copy across every section.** No section earns a specific verb; nothing tells you *what* to shop.
- **"MAKE YOUR $100+ INSTANTLY!" is buried and too small to read.** If this is a referral or loyalty trigger, it belongs at the top, not at scroll depth where it disappears.
- **"Up to 50% off" headline is week-worn.** The Flash Sale label is new; the underlying claim is not, and subscribers know it.
- **No personalization visible anywhere.** A week of identical broad blasts with no name, category, or tier differentiation compounds fatigue fast.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Make the stacked clearance the hero.** "Extra 30% Off Clearance Already Up to 70% Off" is genuinely newsworthy and changes the math for subscribers who checked prices earlier this week. The Flash Sale label is just a label; that stacking is a real reason to come back.
- 2. **Cut or refresh the Nike Deal Days section.** It has appeared in two prior sends unchanged. Either update the image and price points or replace it with something that hasn't been in their inbox already.
- 3. **Move "MAKE YOUR $100+ INSTANTLY!" above the fold.** If this is a referral bonus or loyalty earn, it is the most differentiated hook in the email. Lead with it in the hero or at minimum put it as the first section after the hero.
- 4. **Pick one hero product with a specific price.** A single shoe or jacket at "was $120, now $59" does more click-pull than six sections of "up to X% off a category."
- 5. **Swap generic CTAs for category-specific ones.** "Shop Men's Shoes," "Shop Clearance," "Claim Your Bonus" — anything that signals what happens next.
- **Subject Alt A:** `48 hrs: extra 30% off clearance already up to 70% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Flash Sale stacks on clearance — two days, ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack an extra 30% off markdowns that are already 70% off — while Flash Sale pricing lasts`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Clearance. Flash Sale. Both — but only through Sunday`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Issues found:**

- **Preheader slot not finalized.** The `<td id="ssl">` contains the comment `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` followed by the live preheader text. This comment is a workflow artifact indicating a manual paste step — if the template is re-used, this slot may be left blank or double-populated.

  ```html
  <td id="ssl" ... style="padding:0; font-size:0; line-height:0; height:0;">
    <!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->
    Get $20 off $100+ when you sign up for Text Alerts**
  </td>
  ```

  The `font-size:0` on the `<td>` suppresses rendering in most clients, but Yahoo Mail and some older Android clients may ignore it and surface the raw text.

- **Archivo font loaded from `fonts.gstatic.com` via `@media screen`** — correct pattern; non-supporting clients fall back to Arial. No issue.

- **`role="presentation"` on all layout tables** — correct.

- **`lang="en"` on `<html>`** — present and correct.

- **No `alt` text visible** on the logo `<img>` in the truncated source — cannot confirm; should be `alt="Dick's Sporting Goods"`.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No broken or unfilled merge tokens are visible in the provided source. The `__dU__` tracking token is populated. The preheader text is hardcoded (not merged from a profile field), which is typical for broadcast flash-sale sends.

---

### 5. Compliance

**Critical issue in structured data block — invalid JSON-LD:**

The `<script type="application/ld+json">` block contains JavaScript-style `//` line comments, which are **not valid JSON**. This will throw a parse error in any strict JSON parser, including Google's Gmail Promotions tab renderer, silently dropping all structured data:

```js
{ // GIF & WEBP images are not supported and will be filtered out.
  // Sample image is 538x138, 3.9 aspect ratio
  "@context": "http://schema.org/",
  "@type": "PromotionCard",
  // DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!
  "image": ""
}
```

**Secondary issues in the same block:**

| Field | Value | Problem |
|---|---|---|
| `DiscountOffer.availabilityStarts` | `"2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"` | Stale — 6 years ago; should reflect actual campaign window |
| `DiscountOffer.availabilityEnds` | `""` | Empty — Gmail may refuse to render the Promotions annotation |
| `DiscountOffer.discountCode` | `""` | Empty — acceptable for no-code promotions but flagged |
| `PromotionCard.image` | `""` | Empty — no hero image in Gmail Promotions tab |

**CAN-SPAM / Unsubscribe:** Not visible in the truncated source. Cannot confirm physical mailing address or unsubscribe link presence — these must appear in the footer and are standard for DSG. Verify footer block is not accidentally clipped.

**Sending domain:** `e.dcsg.com` is the ESP subdomain. Verify SPF, DKIM (selector `s1` or equivalent), and DMARC alignment for `dcsg.com` are active — this is infrastructure-level, not visible in HTML.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

UTM parameters are not visible in the raw redirect URLs (e.g., `app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/v610…`). Responsys appends UTMs server-side at redirect time — **verify the redirect destination includes at minimum** `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, and `utm_campaign`. The campaign date in the subject ("2 days ONLY") should align with landing page sale end-date logic to avoid post-expiry traffic landing on ended-sale pages.
