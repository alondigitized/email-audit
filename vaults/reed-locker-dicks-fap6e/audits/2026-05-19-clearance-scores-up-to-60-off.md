# Clearance scores = Up to 60% OFF
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S Sporting Goods' clearance email — their third clearance-framed send in three days, following near-identical "Up to 60% off select clearance" emails on May 17 and May 18. The hero is competent: a bold "60% OFF" on a dark background reads cleanly, and the running shoes section buried below it has a genuinely strong offer (up to 75% off HOKA and ON). But the email is structurally bloated — seven or eight distinct sections stacked vertically, each with its own CTA, diluting any single narrative.
- The single most damaging issue: an "Easter Shopping in the Store" section is visibly present mid-email. Easter 2026 was in April. This isn't a minor styling bug — it's stale promotional content in a live May 19 send, and any subscriber who gets that far will feel this email was auto-populated without review.
- Judgment: this is a salvageable creative buried under cadence fatigue and a live production embarrassment. The email shouldn't have shipped in this state.

## What's working

- **Hero is unambiguous.** "UP TO 60% OFF SELECT CLEARANCE SCORES" in large, high-contrast type is the strongest signal in the email — reads at a glance.
- **Running shoes section has the best offer.** "Up to 75% Off Select DICK'S, HOKA, ON and more" with specific brand callouts is concrete and relevant.
- **Subject line is mobile-friendly.** At ~32 characters, it clears the truncation threshold on every client; the "scores" sports pun is brand-consistent.
- **Memorial Day Event section is present.** Anchors the email to a real calendar moment, which the May 18 send showed works.
- **Section headers are scannable.** Even as a long scroll, the section breaks with discount percentages are easy to skim.

## What's weak

- **Easter section is live.** A visibly stale "Easter Shopping in the Store" block is present mid-scroll. Easter was six weeks ago. This is a content QA miss that undermines the brand.
- **Cadence is broken.** This is the third clearance email in three days — all variations of the same 60% off framing. A real subscriber has already seen this twice and is likely ignoring it.
- **No hard deadline.** "While They Last" is the only urgency signal, and it's soft. Every prior send in the brand history has shown that countdown timers (like May 16's Flash Sale) materially improve the send.
- **Too many competing CTAs.** Running shoes, fan shop, complete sets, footwear, app install, Memorial Day — the email has no single conversion story; it's a catalog dump.
- **The "scores" wordplay is weak.** It reads fine but also reads like the subject was workshopped to seem clever. "Clearance scores" doesn't add urgency or specificity.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pull the Easter section immediately.** This is the most urgent fix — an expired promo is active in a live email. Audit the template for other evergreen placeholder blocks that may have slipped through.
- 2. **Add a hard deadline to the subject and hero.** The May 16 Flash Sale emails demonstrated this works. Even "thru Sunday" or "ends midnight" would lift the open rate on this send.
- 3. **Lead with the strongest offer, not the category name.** "Up to 75% off HOKA and ON" is a stronger hero than "clearance" — consider leading with the brand callout.
- 4. **Collapse to three sections max.** Running shoes, one apparel block, and the Memorial Day anchor. Everything else should be cut or moved to a browse page.
- 5. **Break the cadence.** The brand is burning its list. If a send is the fourth variation of the same offer this week, delay it or merge it into the next real campaign anchor.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Clearance week: 60% off athletic gear, 75% off running`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Running shoes, fan shop, and footwear — all on clearance through the weekend.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `HOKA, ON, DICK'S brand — up to 75% off while sizes last.`

### 1. Technical Summary

The email uses a standard MSO-safe table layout with reasonable mobile breakpoints and proper `role="presentation"` hygiene. However, the JSON-LD structured data block contains **invalid JSON** (JavaScript-style `//` comments) and multiple unfilled template fields, both of which will silently fail schema parsing in Gmail and other clients.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Tracking redirect domain:** All CTAs route through `app.dickssportinggoods.com` (e.g., `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/v6100000...`). The redirect wrapper is consistent, but final destination URLs are opaque from the source alone — UTM carrythrough cannot be confirmed without following redirects (see §6).

**Open-tracking pixel** at `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/v6100000...` is hidden via `display:none; max-height:0px; overflow:hidden; mso-hide:all`. Implementation is standard; no issue.

**Logo link** (`id="logo"`) is truncated in the source provided — cannot confirm the `href` resolves correctly or carries tracking params.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- `<html lang="en">` present. `charset="UTF-8"` and `viewport` meta both present. MSO conditional comments are present and well-formed. No issues here.
- `role="presentation"` applied to all layout tables — correct.
- `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` override CSS is present — suppresses unwanted iOS link detection.
- **Preheader text is co-located with the tracking pixel** inside `id="ssl"`. The cell contains both `<img ... style="display:none">` and raw text ("Download the app 📲..."). This is functional but fragile — some clients may expose the hidden image's surrounding whitespace or collapse the preheader unpredictably. Recommend a dedicated `<td>` for preheader text, hidden via `max-height:0; overflow:hidden`.
- **Logo `<img>` alt text**: the source is truncated before the `alt` attribute is visible. Cannot confirm alt text is set — flag for manual verification.
- Emoji (`📲`) in preheader will render as a glyph box in older Outlook clients.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No visible unfilled `%%TOKEN%%` or `{{handlebars}}` merge fields in the rendered content.

**However**, the JSON-LD block has three unfilled template placeholders:

```json
"discountCode": "",          // blank — should be populated or omitted
"availabilityEnds": "",       // blank — should be a valid ISO 8601 timestamp
"image": ""                   // PromotionCard image URL never filled
```

Additionally, `"availabilityStarts": "2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"` is a **2020 date** — almost certainly stale boilerplate carried forward from an old template. This is the live value that schema parsers will read.

**Invalid JSON-LD (critical):** The `<script type="application/ld+json">` block contains JavaScript-style single-line comments (`//`), which are **not valid JSON**:

```js
// GIF & WEBP images are not supported...
// Sample image is 538x138...
// DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!
```

JSON does not support `//` comments. Any client or parser attempting to extract Gmail Promotions Tab annotations will throw a parse error and silently discard the entire schema block. The PromotionCard and DiscountOffer annotations will not render.
