# We can't let you miss an EXTRA 25% off select clearance!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-06**
## Executive summary

- This is the eighth execution of DICK'S "Extra 25% off select clearance" creative since May 30 — the brand history makes that unmistakable. The email is technically competent: the red hero banner is dominant, "EXTRA 25% OFF" lands immediately, and the multi-category grid gives a subscriber several paths to click. But at this send frequency, none of that matters. A real subscriber has seen this subject line, this hero, and these product blocks so many times that the email registers as noise before the inbox preview even finishes loading.
- The subject line's framing — "We can't let you miss" — tries to inject urgency into an offer that has been open for a week straight. That gambit only works once. After seven sends, it reads as the brand's anxiety, not a subscriber's opportunity. There's no deadline, no new angle, and no escalation from the June 5 send that earned a 7/10 with identical creative.
- The strongest case for this email is the multi-category layout — golf bags, bikes, apparel, outdoor gear — which does give different subscriber types a reason to click. The weakest case is everything about timing and cadence. Ship the same clearance push nine days in a row and even a loyal DICK'S customer learns to tune it out.

## What's working

- **Hero is instantly legible.** "EXTRA 25% OFF" in white on the red banner requires zero decoding — offer clarity is there from the first pixel.
- **Multi-category grid earns its space.** Golf bags, bikes, YETI coolers, and apparel give four distinct subscriber types a relevant entry point.
- **"Must-have gear for an epic summer!" product tile row** is seasonal and functional — moves the email beyond a generic clearance dump.
- **SHOP NOW CTA repetition** means a buyer doesn't have to scroll back to act.

## What's weak

- **Cadence fatigue is the dominant problem.** This is send #8 of the same clearance creative in eight days. Open rates on this email are almost certainly in freefall by now.
- **No deadline anywhere visible.** "Can't let you miss" implies scarcity but provides no end date — subscribers learn quickly that the offer is perpetual.
- **"Select clearance" is doing a lot of vague lifting.** The June 4 "Best Deals of the Week" sends (9/10 each) succeeded precisely because they named specific categories with specific discounts; this hero just says "select."
- **Subject line is over 50 chars and leans on emotional manipulation.** "We can't let you miss" is a weak urgency lever at this point in the cadence — it should have been retired after send #2.
- **No loyalty or member-price callout visible,** which DICK'S ScoreCard program could use to differentiate this from a generic clearance blast.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill this creative and pivot.** The June 4 and June 5 "Best Deals of the Week" compilations both scored 9/10 — return to that format. Name a category hero: "Week's Best: Golf Bags Under $230" or "Summer Clearance: Bikes, Coolers, Shoes." Specificity is the fix.
- 2. **Add a hard expiry.** Even "Ends Sunday" would convert "can't let you miss" from an empty phrase into a credible call to action.
- 3. **Surface ScoreCard / member pricing** in the hero band — e.g., "ScoreCard Members: EXTRA 25% off clearance." Differentiates the send for loyalty subscribers.
- 4. **Collapse the subject.** Under 50 chars, verb-first, with a specific category.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 25% off Nike & adidas clearance — ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Golf bags under $230 + extra 25% off clearance`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Bikes, coolers, shoes — clearance prices drop further today`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `ScoreCard members: stack your points + extra 25% off now`
