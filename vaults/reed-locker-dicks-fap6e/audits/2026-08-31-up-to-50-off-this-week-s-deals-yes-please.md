# Up to 50% OFF this week's deals? Yes, please! 🙌
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-31**
## Executive summary

- This is the green-locker "UP TO 50% OFF" hero again — the same backdrop DICK'S has run on repeat since 8/16, now paired with a genuinely bloated body: seven separate category modules (clothing/shoes, soccer gear, TaylorMade golf balls, drinkware, bikes, weighted vests) stacked before a second promo (the maroon "HUNDREDS OF NEW CLEARANCE STYLES" block) closes it out. It's less an email than a scrolling catalog with no clear hierarchy — nothing tells the reader which of these eight offers is the one worth clicking.
- The subject line ("Up to 50% OFF this week's deals? Yes, please! 🙌") is trying for a conversational hook but it's vague — "this week's deals" could mean anything, and the brand has used near-identical "up to 50% off" framing in at least four of the last six sends. Compared to 8/30's clearance email or 8/29's countdown-timer flash sale, this one has no urgency mechanic at all — no deadline, no timer, nothing time-bound — which is a step backward from the cadence DICK'S has otherwise leaned on.
- Judgment: functional but forgettable — a grab-bag of merchandising modules wearing a recycled hero, with a subject line too generic to stand out in an inbox that's already seen five DICK'S emails in two weeks.

## What's working

- Clean render — no overlapping text, broken images, or layout breakage anywhere in the scroll.
- The hero is legible and on-brand: bold white "UP TO 50% OFF" on textured green locker/turf background.
- Category quick-links (Men's/Women's/Kid's/Shoes/Fan Shop/Team Sports) right under the hero give multiple fast paths in without excessive scrolling.
- Product photography is varied and season-appropriate (soccer, bikes, drinkware, golf) rather than generic stock.
- ScoreCard loyalty banner and free shipping/pickup line are visible right at the top for members.

## What's weak

- No single hero offer — eight nearly-equal-weight modules compete for attention with zero visual hierarchy pointing to a "best" deal.
- Zero urgency: no countdown, no "ends tonight," no date — a regression from the countdown-timer version DICK'S ran on 8/29.
- Subject line is vague ("this week's deals") and recycles the "up to 50% off" framing used repeatedly this month.
- Preview text is generic filler ("Order Today. Sport It Tomorrow...") that doesn't tease any specific product or deal.
- Two competing promo blocks (green 50% OFF hero at top, maroon CLEARANCE block near bottom) dilute focus rather than reinforcing one message.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the module count from eight to three or four and pick one hero product/category to lead with — right now nothing signals "click here first."
- 2. Add a real deadline back in — a countdown or "ends Sunday" — this send has none, unlike the 8/29 flash sale.
- 3. Tighten the subject to name an actual category or product instead of generic "this week's deals."
- 4. Rewrite the preheader to tease a specific offer instead of the store-pickup boilerplate currently leaking through.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Soccer, bikes, drinkware: up to 50% off this week`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your fall gear list, up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns on Nike, YETI, TaylorMade and more — shop before sizes sell out`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Soccer cleats to golf balls: this week's best deals inside`
- 5. Drop or consolidate the second promo block (clearance) — one clear CTA per email performs better than two competing offers.
