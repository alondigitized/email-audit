# EPIC 4th of July SALE: Shop deals today!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-04**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S fourteenth-plus promotional send since June 26 — roughly two emails per day — and July 4th is the exact moment it should land hardest. The hero delivers: a bold, patriotic red banner with "UP TO 50% OFF" is clean, legible, and seasonally on-point. But the email immediately squanders that momentum by cascading into six or more competing offer blocks: fishing clothing, clearance apparel, golf balls, app promotion, and — oddly — a "Book Clearance" section. The message frags into a catalog instead of a campaign.
- The sender name makes this worse. "DICK'S | Weekend Savings Event" is factually wrong on July 4th — it's not a weekend savings event. A subscriber who sees that name in their inbox before opening will feel a small but real credibility wobble. And "EPIC 4th of July SALE: Shop deals today!" is the third near-identical "EPIC" subject DICK'S has fired this week, following July 2 ("EPIC 4th of July Flash Sale") and July 3 ("EPIC 4th of July deals are one click away"). The word has lost all weight.
- The fundamentals are in place — offer is real, brand is known, timing is defensible — but execution is autopilot creative at peak send frequency. A subscriber who has survived thirteen previous emails is more likely to swipe-delete than open one more "EPIC" send.

## What's working

- **Hero is strong**: the red-white-blue "UP TO 50% OFF" block is visually dominant, immediately legible, and seasonally correct for July 4th.
- **Category nav is present**: MEN / WOMEN / KIDS / FOOTWEAR / FITNESS tabs give a quick shortcut for subscribers who know what they want.
- **Multiple price points are visible**: 50% off, 30% off clearance, and 40% at checkout — there's genuine discount depth, not just a teaser.
- **App CTA section**: the "ALL IN THE APP" block is a legitimate secondary conversion point that past sends have omitted.

## What's weak

- **Sender name is wrong for the occasion**: "DICK'S | Weekend Savings Event" on July 4th reads as a template error and erodes trust before the email is even opened.
- **"EPIC" is exhausted**: this is the third consecutive subject line to use that word in four days; it no longer signals anything.
- **Offer scatter kills focus**: fishing clothing, golf balls, book clearance, and app promo compete with each other — there is no single hero CTA the eye can follow.
- **"Book Clearance" is off-brand**: even if DICK'S carries sports books, a clearance book section in a 4th of July email reads as inventory scraping, not a curated offer.
- **Cadence fatigue is real**: fourteen-plus sends in eight days is unambiguously over the threshold where subscribers disengage or unsubscribe, and this send does nothing to earn its place in the inbox.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the sender name**: change "DICK'S | Weekend Savings Event" to "DICK'S | 4th of July Sale" — this one fix costs nothing and removes a credibility drag before the email is opened.
- 2. **Drop to a single hero offer**: kill the fishing clothing, book clearance, and app sub-sections and lead the entire body with the single most-compelling 4th of July deal (e.g., the 50% off footwear or the golf balls Buy More Save More offer). One offer, one CTA, higher click rate.
- 3. **Retire "EPIC"**: it has appeared in at least three subjects this week. Any alternative is better at this point.
- 4. **Anchor a deadline**: "Shop deals today!" is vague. "Sale ends tonight at midnight" creates the same urgency with credibility. The 4th of July is a hard deadline — use it explicitly.
- 5. **Remove or demote Book Clearance**: it reads as filler. Replace with a 4th of July-specific category that fits the brand (grilling gear, coolers, outdoor furniture).
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 50% off — 4th of July Sale ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day: 4th of July deals up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop footwear, outdoor gear, and apparel before midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Golf balls, clearance kicks, and summer gear — all on sale today only.`
