# This week's DEALS 👉 Up to 50% off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a loud, on-brand DICK'S roundup. A giant "UP TO 50% OFF" over a football field fills the top of the email, followed by a green "Shop Now" button. The offer registers in about a second. Below it, the email turns into a long catalog of tiles: Under Armour cleats, Nike fleece, NFL gear, golf bags, Peloton shoes, bats, Halloween and an app-download block.
- It's a clean send but not a sharp one. It's the third "up to 50% off" hero in four days, after the 2026-10-10 "INSIDE: Up to 50% off select clothing & shoes" send. The 70% clearance and 24-hour sale sends from 10-11 and 10-12 also used the same loud-number approach. "This Week's Deals" is a generic frame with no deadline. The fine print says "Valid online only for a limited time," so nothing creates a reason to act today.
- Verdict: competent and easy to read, but it's a repeat of the week's pattern. The hero lands, and the body is a scroll-heavy grab bag with no clear lead product.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 50% OFF" hero is huge and immediately readable. The green Shop Now button sits directly under it.
- The sport-category chips (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Team Sports) give quick routes into the catalog.
- Specific price points in the tiles ("$50 & Under Select Nike Fleece," "$220 & Under Select Golf Bags," "Sale $90 Peloton Original Cycling Shoes") are concrete and credible.
- The Halloween Shop block keeps the email seasonal, and the "All In The App" block adds a second action.
- I can't see any broken images or overlapping text in the render.

## What's weak

- There's no real urgency. "This week" and "limited time" are vague, with no end date or countdown, unlike the 10-12 "Final Hours" send.
- The email repeats the week's "up to X% off" pattern. It reads as the same message as 10-10 and 10-11.
- The body has about eight sections with near-identical gray "Shop Now" buttons. No single product leads, so the eye has nowhere to land after the hero.
- "Up to 50% off" is vague, because the tiles show different discounts (40%, 50%, flat $ price points). The hero number overstates most of the content.
- The "Shop Now" CTA is generic, and there are no reviews or social proof.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real deadline to the hero, such as "Ends Sunday" or a countdown. This is the single change that would lift clicks most, because the offer itself isn't new this week.
- 2. Make the hero number honest and specific. Say "Up to 50% off NFL gear + select Nike" instead of a bare 50%, so the click is tied to a product.
- 3. Cut the body to 3–4 modules and lead with the strongest one. The current eight dilute the hero.
- 4. Change the button copy to a verb plus noun, such as "Shop Nike fleece" or "Shop the deals."
- 5. Space this send away from the other 50% and 70% sends, or give it a distinct angle.

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email is a table-based, `role="presentation"` layout with a viewport meta tag, MSO conditionals and a hidden preheader spacer. Its main technical gaps are a missing plain-text part, missing alt text on 13 images including the tracking pixel, and no confirmed unsubscribe or authentication headers. Header-related findings may be artifacts of the AgentMail relay.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- Links are wrapped in the ESP click tracker `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/mclick?params=b.v1...&pid=v1...`. The first one is the header `<a class="arc-link" data-nl-type="DEEPLINK">`. The destination can't be verified from the source, because the HTML is truncated and the destination is encoded in `params`.
- QA probed 12 material links and skipped 59 (footer, utility and social links). Those 59 are unverified. The unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result for it appears in the findings.
- The open-tracking pixel `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=...` is flagged for missing alt text. That is low severity, but an empty `alt=""` is the conventional value for a pixel.
- No broken or redirect-looping links are reported.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Missing alt text on 13 images (all in the QA data).**
  - Seven are content or banner images on Scene7: `cleats-2026-1013-d`, `fleece-2026-1013-d`, `nfl-2026-1013-d`, `golf-2026-1013-d`, `peloton-2026-1013-d`, `bbsb-2026-1013-d` and `halloween-banner-2026-0911-d`.
  - Five are icons: `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1` and `icon-location-2025-1`.
  - The last is the open pixel.
  - With images blocked or read by a screen reader, the content images convey nothing. If they carry the offer or category text, that content is lost.
- **Plain-text part missing (QA FAIL).** The text version is 9 chars. This is the only hard failure. It hurts accessibility, spam scoring and text-only clients.
- **Stale asset.** `halloween-banner-2026-0911-d` is dated 2026-09-11, a month before this 2026-10-13 send. This may be an intentional seasonal reuse, but it is a template carry-over worth checking.
- **Request size.** Scene7 URLs request `wid=1200&hei=1600` for a 600px layout. That is acceptable for retina, but the payload is heavy.
- **Positives.** The email has `lang="en"`, `<title>`, a viewport meta tag, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, a mobile media query at 480px, and a hidden preheader with `mso-hide:all`.
- **Minor.** The outer `table.wrapper` has `width="600"` plus `width:100%; max-width:600px`, and `data-contenteditable` and `class="is-html-mode"` remain on `<body>` from the authoring tool. They are harmless.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. No unresolved tokens such as `{{...}}`, `%%...%%` or `[FirstName]` are visible in the provided source or QA output. The source is truncated, so this covers only the visible portion.

**5. Compliance**
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found (WARN).** The QA note says it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. It is unconfirmed rather than proven missing.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found (WARN).** The same caveat applies. If the header is truly absent, it is non-compliant with the Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements for one-click unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results not found (WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment can't be assessed. The From domain is `em.dcsg.com`, and the tracking domain `data.em.dcsg.com` shares that organizational domain, which supports alignment.
- **Footer.** The footer (physical address, unsubscribe link text) isn't in the truncated source, so CAN-SPAM footer elements couldn't be confirmed.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity**
- UTM parameters can't be evaluated. Destinations are hidden behind encoded `mclick?params=` redirects, and the truncated source shows no visible UTM query strings.
- The click-tracker wrapping means attribution is handled server-side by the ESP, which is a normal pattern.
- The subject ("Up to 50% off") and the landing pages can't be compared, because the redirect targets weren't resolved in the data provided.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add a real multipart plain-text part. This fixes the only FAIL.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 7 content images. Use `alt=""` for the 5 decorative icons and the open pixel.
3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` on the original send. Verify against the raw message at the mailbox rather than the relay copy.
4. Check SPF, DKIM and DMARC from the original headers, not the relayed ones.
5. Resolve the `mclick` redirect chains for the primary CTAs and check final URLs for UTM parameters and 200 status. Include the unsubscribe link.
6. Confirm the Halloween banner reuse is intentional.
