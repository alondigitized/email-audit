# New markdowns added 🤝 Up to 70% OFF select clearance
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, loud clearance email. A giant "Up to 70% OFF" sits over fall leaves, with "New Markdowns Added: Up to 70% Off Select Clearance" and a green Shop Now button right below. The eye lands on the number first. It matches Sunday's 10/18 "New markdowns alert! 🚨 Up to 70% off" send almost exactly.
- Cadence is the problem. This is the fourth clearance push in five days (10/16 and 10/17 "Extra 25%", 10/18 "Up to 70%", now 10/20), and the 10/18 70% creative is nearly identical. The only thing "new" is the word "added." Below the hero, the email becomes a long, generic grid of eight or so category blocks. Nothing in them tells you which markdown is actually new.
- Verdict: a competent, on-brand send that loses points on freshness and focus. The top fold works. Everything after it is a catalog dump with no deadline and no hero product.

## What's working

- The oversized "Up to 70% OFF" hero is instantly readable and dominates the first screen.
- The autumn leaf backdrop fits the season and gives the email some texture.
- One primary CTA (green Shop Now) sits directly under the hero headline.
- The "Explore more deals" pill row (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 and Under, Fan Shop, All Sale) lets shoppers jump to their own category.
- The render is clean, with no broken images, overlaps or empty blocks.

## What's weak

- The headline repeats the subject word for word, so the hero adds nothing new.
- "Limited Time" is the only urgency, with no date or deadline. Last Friday's "ENDS TODAY" was the only credible deadline this week.
- Every product block is a different category (adidas cleats, Nike fleece, fan gear, drinkware, JOOLA pickleball, cardio) with a gray "Shop Now" button. Nothing is a clear hero, and the repeated "Shop Now" copy is generic.
- The mid-email ScoreCard+ upsell ("Get more every season… Sign Up") breaks the clearance flow. A second "Up to 50% OFF" banner repeats the same message.
- The fine print is heavy: exclusion language under the hero and a dense legal block at the bottom.
- No prices, ratings or reviews are visible. Product tiles show names only, with no "was/now" pricing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero prove "new." Show three or four actual newly marked-down products with a was/now price, instead of leaving the 70% hero as an abstract number. That is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Cut the body to 3 or 4 blocks and drop the duplicate 50% banner and the mid-email membership upsell. Move the upsell to the footer or to a separate send.
- 3. Add a real deadline, such as "Ends Sunday" or a countdown, to match the one send this week that had a credible deadline.
- 4. Replace the generic "Shop Now" buttons with category verbs, such as "Shop adidas cleats" or "Shop Nike fleece."
- 5. Space out clearance sends and rotate the offer or angle. Vary the creative so the 70% hero isn't repeated every other day.

- **Subject Alt B:** `New clearance: Nike & adidas up to 70% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fresh markdowns on shoes, fleece and fan gear. Prices end Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cleats from $20 off, fleece under $60 and more, while sizes last.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is structurally sound: it has `lang`, a `<title>`, a viewport meta tag, `role="presentation"` layout tables, MSO conditionals and a hidden preheader. The one confirmed failure is a missing plain-text alternative. The other findings are missing alt text on content images and header-capture gaps that are probably artifacts of the AgentMail relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- QA probed 12 of 69 links, ranked by click likelihood, and reported no broken links. The other 57 links (footer and social) were skipped, so their status is unverified.
- The open-tracking pixel sits at the top of `<body>`: `<img src="https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1...&tuid=6ad680ec364aac4e3827667d&configId=..." width="1" height="1">`. It has no `alt` and no `style="display:block"` or `border="0"`. Some clients may render it as a visible 1px gap or a broken-image icon above the content.
- The pixel URL carries a per-recipient `tuid`. This is normal for ESP tracking and not an issue on its own.
- The HTML source was truncated, so individual link `href` values could not be inspected.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text fallback [FAIL]:** the text version is 9 characters. This is effectively a missing `text/plain` part. It hurts text-only clients and screen-reader or accessibility workflows, and spam filters score HTML-only mail worse. If the relay stripped the part, this is a capture artifact. The result is the same if the sender really ships a stub.
- **Missing alt text, 12 images:**
  - Content images: `adidascleat1020stwd`, `nikefleece1020stwd`, `jersey21020stwd`, `1020drinkbanner`, `joola1020stwd`, `selectcardio1020stwd` (all `s7d6.scene7.com/is/image/DSGAEMSites/...`).
  - Footer icons: `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`.
  - Tracking pixel: `data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open`.
  - With images blocked, the six content images are blank. If they are linked, those links have no accessible name. The decorative icons and the pixel should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Editor artifacts:** `<body class="is-html-mode" data-contenteditable="true">` is leftover from the authoring tool. It is harmless but unnecessary.
- **Preheader:** the `#ssl` cell is set to `font-size:0; line-height:0; height:0` and contains visible text ("Get $20 off $100+ when you sign up for Text Alerts**"). It is followed by a `#pht-spacer` div that is correctly hidden (`display:none; max-height:0; mso-hide:all`). Some clients may still show the preheader text, so check its rendering.
- **Fonts:** Archivo is loaded from `fonts.gstatic.com` with `font-display:swap` and an `Arial,sans-serif` fallback. This is fine.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered tokens such as `{{`, `%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The truncated portion could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe [WARN] and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) [WARN]:** both are not found. QA notes the relay may not capture them. This cannot be confirmed from the data. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so verify these headers on the original message.
- **Authentication-Results [WARN]:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is an expected relay limitation, not a confirmed defect. The From domain `em.dcsg.com` is a branded sending subdomain.
- A footer unsubscribe link and physical address are not visible in the truncated source. QA says an unsubscribe link is always included in probing and reports no failure. Not independently verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Could not be assessed. The truncated source exposes no `href` values or UTM parameters, and QA reported no landing-page failures for the 12 probed links.

1. Add a real `text/plain` part with the offer, key links and unsubscribe URL. This is the only confirmed FAIL.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the six scene7 content images, and `alt=""` to the five footer icons and the tracking pixel. The pixel should also get `style="display:block;border:0"`.
3. Check the original message headers for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and for SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment on `em.dcsg.com`. The relay probably dropped them from the capture.
