# Epic Shoe Sale 👏 Deals on 100s of styles won't last forever
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-03**
## Executive summary

- DICK'S nails the hero and then undoes it. "EPIC SHOE SALE" is a genuinely strong open — red locker backdrop, Nike/adidas/HOKA logos, a stacked-shoe hero shot, and a clean "Up to 30% Off Select Shoes and Cleats" subhead with a real deadline (8/15/26) sitting right above a single green "SHOP NOW" button. That's the best kind of single-focus hero this brand has run.
- Then the email completely abandons the shoe premise. Scroll past the fold and it's the same kitchen-sink cascade DICK'S has been running non-stop since 7/30 — golf, outdoor essentials, team sports, drinkware, fan shop, fitness equipment, a six-item gift grid. None of that is shoes. This is the exact pattern the 8/2 "Epic Shoe Sale Alert" send broke away from (and scored 8.00/10 for doing so) — this send takes the win of that hero and then reverts the body to the sprawling format that's been dragging scores down all week.
- Net: strong top-of-funnel, false-advertised body. A recipient who opens for shoes and scrolls past the fold gets golf shorts and a Peloton bike instead.

## What's working

- Hero image is bold and legible — single message ("EPIC SHOE SALE"), brand logos visible, product clearly shoes/cleats.
- Concrete offer stated twice: "Up to 30% Off" plus a real expiration ("Valid through 8/15/26 11:59PM PT").
- Loyalty program is visible up top — ScoreCard banner ("300 Points = $10 Reward, Shop to Earn Points") is a nice touch for members.
- Clean category nav bar (Men's/Women's/Kids'/Shoes/Team Sports/Back-to-School) gives quick wayfinding.
- No render bugs — every image loads, no overlapping text, no broken layout.

## What's weak

- Subject and hero promise a shoe sale; roughly 80% of the email body is golf, outdoor gear, drinkware, fan shop, and fitness equipment — a bait-and-switch against the subject line.
- Subject line runs ~59 characters, past the ~50-char mobile-safe threshold, risking truncation of "won't last forever."
- Cadence problem: this is functionally a re-run of 8/2's "Epic Shoe Sale Alert" (same 30% figure, same category), one day later, undermining "won't last forever" urgency when the same deal existed yesterday.
- "Won't last forever" oversells urgency the fine print doesn't back up — the actual window is 12 days out (through 8/15), not an imminent cutoff.
- Primary CTA copy is generic ("SHOP NOW") rather than category-specific ("Shop the Shoe Sale"), a missed chance to reinforce the hero's promise.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the cascade or gate it clearly.** If cross-sell blocks stay, put a "You might also like" divider after the shoe section so the email keeps its promise before branching out — this is the single highest-leverage fix, since it's what turned the well-executed 8/2 send into this one.
- 2. Tighten the subject under 50 characters so it doesn't truncate on mobile.
- 3. Swap "SHOP NOW" for "Shop the Shoe Sale" to keep the CTA copy tied to the hero offer.
- 4. Align urgency language with the actual deadline — either shorten the real window or drop "won't last forever" for something the 12-day date supports.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off shoes & cleats — Nike, adidas, HOKA`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your shoe upgrade: up to 30% off, ends 8/15`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Nike, adidas, HOKA and more — before the back-to-school rush.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `300 ScoreCard points on every pair — shop the sale now.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Email Audit: DICK'S Sporting Goods — "Epic Shoe Sale"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on Epsilon Harmony infrastructure with a branded click-tracking domain, but ships with a broken unsubscribe link, missing email authentication/list-unsubscribe headers, invalid JSON-LD markup, and no alt text on any content image.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All links route through a branded redirector (`app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...`, `/O/...`, `/U/...`) with Epsilon Harmony-style tokens (e.g. `__dU__=v0G4RBKTXg2Gs8NEX0-RpwlL2_Q96sIof5wvgkGa1VsPIAdb-6PwUuXUbOES4DYwVKlEBH4wAjEqvhvBlpUfxLCgwho1v8uViS`) — consistent, trackable click paths.
- **Broken link:** the Unsubscribe link (`https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/U/v610000019fc93a67c2bcea366e9666b528/1172503d889441210000021ef3a0bd07`) resolves to an application error page: `/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription`. This is a functional failure, not a false positive — see Compliance below.
- Tracking pixel present in the hidden `#ssl` cell (`.../O/.../1172503d8894412100004c5a42963aa1`, `display:none; height:0`) — standard open-tracking beacon, no issue.
- QA probed 12 of 71 total links (material/high-click-likelihood links only); footer, social, and utility links were deprioritized. This is expected sampling behavior, not a coverage gap.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed Outlook conditional CSS:** the `<!--[if (gte mso 9)|(IE)]>` block contains a stray `"}` fragment — `div {background-color:inherit; ... padding:0;"}` — an unclosed/misplaced quote-brace sequence that will break parsing of that rule in Outlook/Word rendering engines.
- **Invalid JSON-LD:** the `<script type="application/ld+json">` block contains JavaScript-style `//` comments (e.g. `// GIF & WEBP images are not supported and will be filtered out`, `// Sample image is 538x138...`). JSON does not support comments — this makes the structured data block invalid JSON, meaning Gmail/other clients cannot parse it for rich preview rendering (annotated content will silently fail).
- **Comment contradicts implementation:** the JSON-LD comment states "GIF & WEBP images are not supported and will be filtered out," yet the HTML references `banner4.gif` and `fit.gif` as content images — a production/template inconsistency worth flagging to the sending team.
- **12 images missing `alt` text** (confirmed via QA): `1172503d...` (tracking pixel — low impact), `two-images-1.png`, `two-images-2.png`, `three-images-2.png`, `team.png`, `banner4.gif`, `fan.png`, `fit.gif`, `icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png`, `icon-location-2025.png`. All primary content and footer trust/value-prop icons are unlabeled, which will render as blank space with images-off and provide no context for screen readers.
- Mobile responsive rules (`@media screen and (max-width:480px)`) and Archivo web-font `@font-face` declarations are well-formed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No visible merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`) in the truncated source.
- The `DiscountOffer` JSON-LD object has empty `"description"` and `"discountCode"` fields, and the `PromotionCard` object has an empty `"image"` field. These read as unfilled template placeholders (comment `"DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!"` confirms this) left in the production send rather than personalization tokens — combined with the invalid JSON noted above, this structured data block is non-functional as shipped.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] Unsubscribe link is broken**, resolving to an error page instead of the subscription-management flow. This is a CAN-SPAM risk: the law requires a functioning opt-out mechanism.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header not found** — may be a relay/capture artifact (AgentMail) rather than absence at the source, but could not be confirmed present.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found** — one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) cannot be confirmed as supported, which affects Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status could not be verified through the relay.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All outbound links are wrapped in the Epsilon Harmony click-tracking redirector rather than exposing direct destination URLs with UTM parameters, so UTM/landing-page alignment cannot be verified from the HTML source alone — actual destination and query params are only resolvable after redirect resolution.
- The one fully-visible non-tracking URL, the favicon reference (`https://www.dickssportinggoods.com/home/assets/icons/dsg/favicon.ico`), is unrelated to campaign continuity.
- No issues found in what's inspectable; continuity cannot be fully assessed without resolving the tracked redirect targets.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the Unsubscribe link — it currently 404s to `/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription` instead of the subscription center. This is the highest-priority fix (compliance risk).
2. Remove the `//` comments from the `application/ld+json` block so it parses as valid JSON, or strip the block if the `DiscountOffer`/`PromotionCard` fields aren't going to be populated.
3. Populate the empty `discountCode`, `description`, and `image` fields in the structured data, or remove the unused schema objects before send.
4. Add `alt` text to all 12 flagged images, particularly the footer value-prop icons and promotional banners.
5. Fix the stray `"}` syntax error in the MSO conditional `<style>` block.
6. Confirm with the sending platform/relay whether `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` are present at the true SMTP layer — these may be relay-capture gaps rather than true absences, but should be verified directly against raw headers at the MTA.
7. Resolve one round of click-tracking redirects on the top CTAs to confirm UTM parameters and landing-page alignment, since this couldn't be verified from the wrapped links alone.
