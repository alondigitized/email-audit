# Wow! 🚨 Don't miss up to 75% OFF select clearance styles. 🚨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-27**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S clearance template again, and it's the third "up to 75%" clearance send in three days. It follows the 10/25 "Stop scrolling! Up to 75% OFF select clearance" and the 10/24 "Final. Hours." extra-25% send. The giant white "UP TO 75% OFF" on the autumn-leaf hero is unmissable, and the "Limited Time" label, headline and red Shop Now button sit right under it. The top of the email works.
- Below the hero it turns into a catalog. Men's, Women's and Kids' blocks each say "Up to 40% off," and the headline is 75%. Then come Stanley, team sports, a second "UP TO 50% OFF" hero, and a product grid. A reader who came for 75% off meets three different numbers and no clear next step. The deadline is vague ("limited time"), where the 10/24 send had a real countdown.
- Verdict: a competent, loud hero on a repetitive, diluted send. It will get opens from DICK'S loyalists, but it won't create urgency this week, because the subscriber has seen this creative already.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 75% OFF" hero is huge and high-contrast. The eye lands on the offer first.
- The "Limited Time" label, headline and single red Shop Now button give the hero a clear CTA.
- The "Explore more deals" pill row (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 & Under, Fan Shop, All Sale) gives fast category navigation.
- The Men's, Women's and Kids' blocks use clean, consistent photography with matching green CTAs.
- The ScoreCard points bar and the "Order Today, Sport It Tomorrow" pickup/delivery line at the top are useful member and fulfilment hooks.

## What's weak

- The offers conflict: 75% in the hero, 40% in the three category blocks, and 50% in the "Best Deals of the Week" block.
- The email is very long, with at least eight distinct sections, and the 75% clearance story gets buried.
- "Limited Time" has no date or countdown, so the urgency isn't credible. The 10/24 "Final Hours" countdown set a higher bar.
- Stanley "Gifts that outlast the holidays" and the team sports block pull attention away from clearance.
- The "Check out these picks" product grid has no prices or discount tags, so a clearance shopper can't see the savings.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the email about clearance only. Cut the Stanley, team sports and "Best Deals of the Week" sections. Replace them with a grid of real clearance products showing was/now prices and "% off" badges.
- 2. Add a real deadline, such as "Ends Sunday 11:59 PM" or a countdown, to replace "Limited Time".
- 3. Make the category blocks say "Up to 75% off" where it's true, or say "Clearance from $X". Don't drop to 40% right after a 75% hero.
- 4. Vary the creative from the last two clearance sends. Show a specific product with a price, not another leaf hero.
- 5. Put the Shop Now button inside the hero image area so the first screen carries both offer and action.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Clearance Nike & adidas from $19.99`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Extra-deep markdowns on shoes, apparel and gear. Sizes are going fast.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `ScoreCard members: free store pickup on clearance, ready in an hour.`

# Technical Audit: DICK'S | Clearance, "Up to 75% OFF select clearance styles"

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible markup is structurally sound (`lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` tables, viewport meta, Outlook conditionals). It has one QA failure, a 9-character plain-text part. It also has widespread missing `alt` attributes and some leftover template and editor artifacts. The HTML is truncated, so link, footer and header checks are partly unverifiable.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Open pixel:** `<img src="https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=…" width="1" height="1">` is on a first-party tracking domain. It has no `alt` and no `style="display:block"` or `border:0`. Clients that add image borders or spacing may render it visibly.
- **Link probing:** 12 material links were probed and 51 skipped. The QA output reports no broken or redirecting links among the probed set, so no link failures are confirmed.
- **Cache-buster:** `holiday-2026-1027-d-1?cb=1792445069329` carries a timestamp parameter. This can defeat CDN and client caching for that asset.
- **Hrefs:** the truncated source only shows `class="arc-link" data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"` on anchors. Redirect and tracking-wrapper behavior can't be assessed from the evidence.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` (QA WARN, 11 images):**
  - Four large content images, which are likely the primary clickable tiles: `2026-1027-dks-stw-4pm-mens`, `-womens`, `-kids`, `-team`.
  - The banner `holiday-2026-1027-d-1`.
  - Five icons: `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`.
  - The open pixel.
  - The QA check can't tell an absent `alt` from `alt=""`. Decorative icons and the pixel should have `alt=""`. The content tiles need descriptive text, because with images blocked they may be blank links.
- **Image weight:** the tile images are requested as `fmt=jpeg&qlt=100&wid=1200&hei=1600`. That is quality 100 at 1200×1600 for a 600px-wide layout, so payloads are likely much heavier than needed on mobile.
- **Editor artifacts in production:** `<body … class="is-html-mode" data-contenteditable="true">` is left over from the authoring tool.
- **Template leftover:** the comment `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` was not removed.
- **Empty preheader:** `#pht-spacer` contains only `&zwnj;&nbsp;` filler and no preheader text. Clients will pull preview text from the first body content instead.
- **Plain-text part:** the plain-text part is 9 characters (QA FAIL). Effectively no text alternative exists, which hurts accessibility and some spam filters.
- **Minor:** `<link rel="icon">` in the head has no effect in email clients. The Archivo web font is declared only under `@media screen`, with an Arial fallback, so fallback rendering is acceptable.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) are visible in the available source. The email doesn't appear to be personalized. The truncated portion couldn't be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** neither header was found (QA WARN). QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. If they are really absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met for one-click unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `info@em.dcsg.com`, a subdomain used for sending. Alignment can't be verified here.
- **Body unsubscribe and postal address:** the footer is truncated, so neither could be checked. QA states an unsubscribe link is always included in probing, but no result is reported.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues confirmed. The anchor hrefs are not visible in the truncated source, and QA reports no landing-page or UTM mismatches. UTM presence and landing alignment remain unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a real plain-text part (the 9-character version is a QA FAIL), with the key offers and links plus the unsubscribe URL.
2. Add `alt` text to the four tile images and the banner. Set `alt=""` on the five icons and the open pixel. Add `style="display:block;border:0"` to the pixel.
