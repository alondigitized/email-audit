# 📥 INSIDE: Up to 50% off select clothing & shoes — shop now! 📥
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-10**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S "up to 50% off" send again. The hero is a huge "UP TO 50% OFF" in white on red, with Nike, adidas and Under Armour logos above it and a Shop Now button below. The offer lands instantly. But it's the same message DICK'S has sent since the 10/5 Fall Deal Drop, including 10/8's "Up to 50% off select clothing & shoes (Nike, adidas & more!)" and 10/9's "Get ready for this week's DEALS!". Nothing here feels new.
- The body is a long scroll of seven or more modules. These are NFL, baseball bats, Walter Hagen golf, Owala tumblers, cardio equipment, an "Extra 25% off clearance" banner and a credit card promo. Each module has a grey Shop Now button that looks timid next to the loud hero. The 25% clearance banner is a separate hero fighting the first. The credit card block and product grid add more clutter.
- Verdict: a competent, clean render with no visible bugs, but it's a tired offer in a crowded module stack. The subject is also longer and louder than it needs to be.

## What's working

- The hero is unmissable: giant "UP TO 50% OFF" with recognizable brand logos, and a clear Shop Now button right below it.
- The "Order Today, Sport It Tomorrow." banner adds a real service benefit (same-day delivery) at the top.
- The category pill row (Men's, Women's, Kids', Clearance, Fan Shop, All Sale) gives fast navigation.
- The "Last Day / Extra 25% Off Select Clearance Styles" banner has credible urgency and a distinct visual treatment.
- The ScoreCard rewards and credit card sections show loyalty value, and the render is clean with no broken images.

## What's weak

- The "up to 50% off" message has now run for six days in a row (see 10/5 through 10/9), so fatigue is a real risk.
- The subject is 61 characters with two emoji bookends, "INSIDE:" and an exclamation mark. "INSIDE" adds nothing and the line truncates on mobile.
- Two competing offers (50% off and Extra 25% off clearance) dilute the hierarchy.
- Every module CTA is a same-weight grey "Shop Now" button, so nothing signals which one matters most.
- The "up to" language, the BOGO 50% asterisks and dense fine print make the offer feel less transparent. The email is very long, and there's no product or review content until the "Check out these picks" grid near the bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero and cut the module count to about four. Move the "Last Day" 25% clearance banner up as the single urgency driver, or drop it.
- 2. Make the CTAs specific and visually distinct: "Shop NFL Gear 50% Off" in the brand green, not generic grey "Shop Now".
- 3. Put the real product grid (Samba OG, Nike Club Fleece) higher, with prices, so there's a concrete reason to click.
- 4. Vary the creative day to day. A rotating hero (fan gear, footwear, golf) would break the repetition.
- 5. Drop "INSIDE:" and the emoji bookends, and lead with the deadline or the category.

- **Subject Alt B:** `NFL gear, golf & shoes up to 50% off today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus extra 25% off clearance ends tonight. Order today, sport it tomorrow.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Samba OGs, Nike Club Fleece and NFL gear, all marked down. Free same-day pickup.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML structure is sound: it has a `lang` attribute, `role="presentation"` layout tables, an MSO conditional block and a preheader. The one confirmed failure is a near-empty plain-text part (9 chars). Alt text is missing on 11 images, and several compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified from the captured data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque redirect wrappers:** Links go through `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/mclick?params=b.v1...&pid=...&tuid=...`. The visible destination and any UTM parameters can't be read from the source, which is truncated.
- **Probe coverage gap:** 12 of 65 links were probed and 53 were skipped (the QA tool deprioritizes footer and social links). No broken links were reported among the 12 probed.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=...` has no `alt` attribute (flagged by QA). Use `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Leftover template placeholder:** `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` is still in production HTML, inside `td#ssl`. It does no harm, but the authoring step was not cleaned up.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (11 images, QA WARN):**
  - Content images: `2026-1010-dks-stw-6am-nfl`, `-bbsb`, `-golf2`, `-fit`, and `owala-2026-0905-d`.
  - Icons: `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`.
  - All are served from `s7d6.scene7.com/is/image/DSGAEMSites/...`.
  - With images blocked or in a screen reader, the content images carry no readable information. Purely decorative ones need an explicit `alt=""`.
- **Plain-text part (QA FAIL):** The text version is 9 chars, so the email is effectively HTML-only. Clients that prefer text, and spam filters, get an empty alternative.
- **Wrapper width mismatch (minor):** `table.wrapper` has `width="600"` and inline `width:100%; max-width:600px`. Outlook desktop ignores `max-width`, so it renders at the 600 attribute. The MSO ghost table handles the inner 584px.
- **Meaningless head tag:** `<link rel="icon" ...favicon.ico>` in `<head>` has no effect in email clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens such as `{{...}}`, `%%...%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]` are visible in the captured source.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (QA WARN):** Neither header was found, so RFC 8058 one-click support is unconfirmed. QA notes AgentMail may not capture these headers, so this may be a relay artifact.
- **Authentication-Results (QA WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown for the same reason. The From domain is `em.dcsg.com`, and alignment can't be confirmed from this data.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** Neither appears in the truncated source, so I can't confirm CAN-SPAM body requirements. QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no probe result was reported for it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Can't be assessed. The click-tracking redirects hide the final URLs and any `utm_*` parameters. The 12 probed links returned no reported errors, but their landing-page content wasn't compared to the email's "Up to 50% off select clothing & shoes" claim.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Plain text:** Generate a full text/plain part, with links and the unsubscribe URL. This is the only confirmed FAIL.
2. **Alt text:** Add descriptive `alt` to the five content images. Use `alt=""` on the five icons if their adjacent text duplicates them, and on the open pixel.
3. **Headers:** Check the raw headers at the MTA or ESP, not the AgentMail copy. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `em.dcsg.com`.
4. **Template hygiene:** Remove the `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` comment and the `<link rel="icon">` tag.
5. **Audit coverage:** Re-run with the full (untruncated) HTML and resolve the tracking redirects. That would let the footer, unsubscribe link, UTM parameters and landing-page match be checked.
