# Blink and it's gone: Enjoy up to 50% off the Flash Sale before it ends tonight!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-15**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S fourth "Flash Sale" wordmark send in five days, and it's the strongest execution of the run — a live "Final Hours 07:59:48" countdown bar sits above the fold, the green locker-room "FLASH" hero is clean with no render bugs, and unlike 8/12's send it doesn't stop at one deal: it stacks Flash Sale, Epic Shoe Sale, DSG family clothing, Extra 25% Off clearance, and Fan Shop gear into one long digest.
- The problem is exactly what the brand history flags — this is the same green-locker "FLASH SALE" diagonal wordmark from 8/12 and 8/15's earlier send, over the same backdrop, with the same countdown mechanic. A subscriber who opened either of those recognizes this instantly as a repeat, and the "before it ends tonight" urgency in the subject is undercut by the fact that this exact urgency claim has now shown up three times this week.
- Verdict: well-built individual email, badly managed as a sequence — the creative is getting reused faster than the offer is actually changing, and that's a deliverability/fatigue risk more than a design one.

## What's working

- Live countdown timer ("Final Hours 07:59:48") is the strongest urgency signal used all week — more credible than a static "ends tonight" claim.
- Hero offer ("Flash Sale: Up to 50% Off") is visible with a single clear black CTA button, no scrolling needed.
- Deal stack below the hero is well-segmented — Epic Shoe Sale, DSG clothing, Extra 25% clearance, Fan Shop gear — each with its own CTA and valid-through date.
- Product grid at the bottom ("Nike Varsity Elite Backpack," "HOKA Women's Clifton 10") gives concrete, clickable inventory instead of a vague homepage link.
- No layout breakage, overlapping text, or broken images anywhere in the render.

## What's weak

- Identical green-locker "FLASH" wordmark hero to the 8/12 send — same backdrop, same diagonal type treatment, third time this creative has run.
- "Up to 50% Off" is a wide, vague range with no anchor product or starting price shown in the hero itself.
- Extremely long single-email digest (6+ separate offers) dilutes the flash-sale urgency the subject promises — it reads as a weekly circular, not a ticking-clock event.
- Demographic mix in the DSG clothing section (three models, one hoodie/one tee/one crewneck) is generic and doesn't signal who this specific deal is for.
- Fourth near-identical "Flash Sale" framing in five days risks the same fatigue flagged in the 8/12 audit.

## Recommendations

- 1. Retire the green-locker wordmark creative for at least two weeks — it's now been used 3x and any subscriber who's opened more than one of these sees the repeat immediately.
- 2. Trim the email to 2-3 offer blocks max and move the rest to a "shop more deals" link — six stacked sales flattens the flash-sale urgency into a generic sale digest.
- 3. Put a real number in the hero (e.g., "Shoes from $29.99") instead of "up to 50% off," which is now a stale phrase repeated across four of the last five sends.
- 4. Keep the countdown timer — it's the best urgency element in the whole brand history — but make it visually match the hero art direction instead of sitting in a separate black bar above it.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: Flash Sale drops shoes from $29.99`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus an extra 25% off clearance — both end at 11:59PM PT`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Epic Shoe Sale, DSG clothing, and Fan Shop gear also on sale tonight`

# Technical Audit — DICK'S Sporting Goods Flash Sale Email

## 1. Technical Summary
Email is built on the Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) tracking platform (`app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/`, `/O/`, `/U/` link patterns); core rendering/personalization infrastructure looks standard, but the unsubscribe flow is broken and required compliance headers are absent.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken unsubscribe link (FAIL):** The "Unsubscribe" CTA (`https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/U/v61000001a006a60ad4948806369ff0b384/d5e738b6b6f14c030000021ef3a0bcfa`) redirects to `/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription` — an unhandled URL type/error page rather than a preference center.
- All observed CTA/header links use SFMC redirect wrappers (`/T/...?__dU__=...`) — standard click-tracking format, resolving correctly for the header logo and points-balance links sampled.
- QA probed 12 of 58 material links (footer/utility/social links deprioritized); no broken links reported among the probed set other than unsubscribe.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **7 images missing `alt` text**, including the invisible open-tracking pixel (`d5e738b6...`, expected/benign) and content-bearing images: `0815-4pm-60-off-dsg`, `0815-4pm-fan-shop`, plus four footer icons (`icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-location-2025.png`). The two promotional banner images are the significant ones — screen readers will announce nothing for the flash-sale creative.
- MSO/Outlook conditional comments and `@media` mobile rules are present and structured correctly (`.mobile-off`, `.mobile-w100`, etc.).
- Web font (`Archivo`) is loaded via `@media screen { @font-face ... }` wrapped in a screen-only query, which is a correct pattern to keep Outlook desktop from attempting external font loads.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g., `%%FirstName%%`, `{{token}}`) visible in the truncated source — links use opaque SFMC subscriber tokens (`__dU__=...`) rather than exposed PII.
- `content-page-ref` meta tag present with an opaque token, consistent with SFMC/Content Builder publish metadata — not a functional issue.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe link is functionally broken** (see §2) — this is a CAN-SPAM violation risk since the mechanism must work, not just be present.
- **List-Unsubscribe header missing (WARN):** may be a relay-capture limitation (AgentMail) rather than the sender's fault — cannot confirm the header was never sent.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header missing (WARN):** no evidence of RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe support.
- **Authentication-Results header missing (WARN):** SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from available data.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No UTM query parameters are visible on any link in the provided source — all tracked links route through SFMC redirect endpoints (`/T/`, `/O/`) rather than direct `dickssportinggoods.com` URLs with UTM tags. Downstream landing-page UTM attribution cannot be verified from this data; would need to follow redirects to confirm final destination and query string.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the unsubscribe redirect — `/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription` indicates a broken or misconfigured preference-center route; this is the highest-priority item (compliance + deliverability risk).
2. Add `alt` text to the two promotional banner images (`0815-4pm-60-off-dsg`, `0815-4pm-fan-shop`) at minimum; footer icons are lower priority but still should carry descriptive or empty (`alt=""`) attributes if decorative.
3. Confirm with the sending platform whether `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent — if AgentMail relay is stripping them, that's a monitoring gap, not necessarily a sender defect; verify against raw SMTP headers if possible.
4. Verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC status directly against the sending domain (`e.dcsg.com`) since `Authentication-Results` wasn't captured here.
