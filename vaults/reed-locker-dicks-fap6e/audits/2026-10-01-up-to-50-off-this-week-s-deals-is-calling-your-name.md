# 📢 Up to 50% OFF this week’s deals is calling your name!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-01**
## Executive summary

- This is the third "up to 50% off" send from DICK'S in about 72 hours, after the 9/29 "INSIDE: Up to 50% off this week's deals" and the two 9/30 Flash Sale emails. The hero is the same clean prairie photo with a huge white "50% OFF" that scored 7/10 on 9/29. It works. The offer is the first thing you see, and the "This Week's Deals" headline and green "Shop Now" button sit right under it.
- Below the hero, the email turns into a dense catalog: clearance shoes (up to 80% off), Nike fleece, NFL fan gear, team sports (up to 40% off), Maxfli golf (up to 30% off), cardio equipment (up to 50% off), "$25 and under," a credit-card promo, and a product grid. That is eight or nine competing offers, each with its own percentage. The "50% off" headline is also an "up to" claim, and the real discounts underneath range from 30% to 80%. The result is a catalog that tells a shopper to browse, not to act.
- For a subscriber seeing the same markdown story three times this week, the email gives no reason to open it now, and the only new thing is the subject-line wording. Verdict: well built and legible, but repetitive and over-stuffed.

## What's working

- The hero is clean. A large "50% OFF" on a photo has strong contrast, and the eye lands on the offer first.
- The green "Shop Now" button is clear and sits directly under the headline.
- The "Explore more deals and essentials" pill row (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Team Sports) gives quick category entry points.
- The modules have clear structure and consistent alignment, with no broken images or overlapping text.
- The ScoreCard header strip and the "Order Today, Sport It Tomorrow" fulfillment banner at the top add brand trust and convenience.

## What's weak

- The send repeats the 9/29 "Up to 50% off this week's deals" email almost exactly, and it follows two Flash Sale sends on 9/30. Cadence fatigue is real.
- The "up to" framing is stacked on every module (80%, 50%, 40%, 30%, 25%). No single deal stands out.
- The credit-card module ("$30 bonus reward") interrupts the shopping flow with a financing pitch.
- Several modules have no clear product to click. The NFL, team-sports, golf and cardio tiles lead to categories, not hero items.
- The footer fine print is a very long block of legal text. It reads as buried conditions on an "up to" offer.
- The "This Week's Deals" subtext says "While quantities last. Offer differs available in-store" and gives no specific deadline.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the email to one hero offer plus 3 to 4 modules.** Drop the credit-card module from the main flow and move it to a footer slot. A tighter email would lift clicks more than any copy change.
- 2. **Give each module one concrete product and price**, such as a named Nike shoe at a specific sale price, instead of "up to X%" category tiles.
- 3. **Add a real deadline** to the hero, such as "Ends Sunday 11:59 PM PT" with a visible countdown or date. The 9/30 flash-sale framing already claimed urgency, so this send needs a different reason to act.
- 4. **Differentiate from the prior sends.** Lead with a different category (for example fall footwear) or a ScoreCard-member angle, so the week doesn't read as one long sale.
- 5. **Shorten the subject line** to under 50 characters and drop the 📢 emoji.
- **Subject Alt A:** `50% off this week: shoes, fleece & more`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Fall gear deals end Sunday, up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Nike, adidas, and New Balance picks, plus $25 & under clearance.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `ScoreCard members earn points on every deal. Ends Sunday.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: DICK'S Sporting Goods ("Up to 50% OFF this week's deals")

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has a well-formed responsive template with `lang="en"`, a `<title>`, a viewport meta, MSO fallbacks, and `role="presentation"` layout tables. Its main problems are a 9-character plain-text part (QA FAIL), 12 images with no alt text, and an empty preheader. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the headers weren't captured. The HTML source was truncated, so footer content could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Links are wrapped in ESP click-tracking redirects. The header link is `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/mclick?params=b.v1.…`, and the open pixel is `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=…`. The tracking domain `data.em.dcsg.com` is on the same organizational domain as the sender (`em.dcsg.com`), which is good for alignment.
- QA probed 12 of 70 links (58 skipped, deprioritized as footer or social). No probe failures were reported, so no broken links are confirmed. Coverage is partial.
- The redirect URLs hide the destination, so the final landing URLs could not be checked from the source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA WARN), 12 images.**
  - Six content images on `s7d6.scene7.com/is/image/DSGAEMSites/`: `shoes-2026-1001-d`, `fleece-2026-1001-d`, `nfl-2026-1001-d`, `sports-2026-1001-d`, `golf-2026-1001-d`, `cardio-2026-1001-d`. If any carries the offer or category text, that content is lost with images off or in a screen reader.
  - Five icons: `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`. These need meaningful alt text if they stand alone, or `alt=""` if decorative.
  - The open-tracking pixel (`/ee/v1/open`) also lacks `alt`. Use `alt=""`.
- **Empty preheader.** The source has `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` followed only by `#pht-spacer`, which holds `&nbsp;`/ZWNJ padding and no text. The template placeholder was never filled, so clients will show body or fallback text as the preview.
- **Editor artifacts in production markup.** `<body class="is-html-mode" data-contenteditable="true">` is a leftover from the authoring tool. It is harmless but unclean.
- **Web font.** The Archivo `@font-face` loads from `fonts.gstatic.com` inside `@media screen`. It falls back to `Arial,sans-serif`, so there is no rendering risk.
- **Tables.** The wrapper table has `width="600"` plus `width:100%; max-width:600px`. This works because it has mobile overrides at `max-width:480px`, but Outlook may ignore `max-width`. The `(gte mso)` ghost-table wrapper (584px) only partly covers this.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible, truncated source contains no unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`). The remainder of the HTML was not available to check.

## 5. Compliance
- **[FAIL] Plain-text fallback is 9 characters.** There is effectively no text alternative. This hurts deliverability, since spam filters penalize HTML-only or near-empty text parts. It also hurts accessibility for text-only clients.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe for senders above 5,000 messages a day. Verify against the raw message at the origin.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown because of the relay. The From domain `em.dcsg.com` and the click-tracking domain are aligned, but authentication is not verified.
- The physical address and the in-body unsubscribe link are in the truncated footer, so they could not be checked. QA reports that an unsubscribe link was included in the probed set but gives no result.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Cannot be confirmed. UTM and campaign parameters are encoded in the opaque `params=b.v1.…` payloads, and the truncated source doesn't show the destination URLs. No mismatches are evidenced.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a real plain-text alternative** (multipart/alternative) with the offer copy and key links. This is the only hard QA failure.
2. **Add alt text** to the six content images (category and offer text) and the five icons. Use `alt=""` on decorative icons and on the open pixel.
3. **Fill the SSL/preheader placeholder** (`<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->`) with preview text. Keep the `#pht-spacer` padding after it.
4. **Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`** on the raw origin message, since the relay may be stripping them. Also confirm SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass for `em.dcsg.com`.
5. **Strip the editor attributes** (`class="is-html-mode"`, `data-contenteditable="true"`) from the production template.
6. **Probe the remaining links** and capture the final landing URLs and UTM parameters to complete the continuity check.
