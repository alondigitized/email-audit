# 🔥 Holiday deals are here! (and they're up to 50% OFF)
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, conventional DICK'S holiday-deals send. The green argyle hero with a gold "UP TO 50% OFF" is the one thing the eye lands on, and a red "Shop Now" button sits directly under "This Week's Deals." The scorecard strip and the "Order Today. Spot It Tomorrow." shipping banner at the top also help.
- The body breaks into seven or eight category blocks: shoes, performance clothing, cleats, golf electronics, outdoor, hoops, and weights. Each has its own green "Shop Now." That's a lot of choice, and it reads as a catalog, not a holiday story. The subject says "Holiday deals," but only the small "Holiday Shop" pill and a few ornaments in the photos carry that theme. The hero is purely a percent-off graphic with no product, and the "50%" is a ceiling, not a promise.
- It's well built and easy to scan, but generic. It's the same "up to 50%" idea as the 11/1 "YES!! Up to 50% off select clothing & shoes" send and the 10/30 "Savings to fall for" send, so a subscriber would see it as a repeat. It's a solid 6-7.

## What's working

- The hero offer is huge and high-contrast. "UP TO 50% OFF" is readable at a glance.
- The red "Shop Now" directly under the hero is unambiguous.
- Category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Holiday Shop) let people jump straight to their segment.
- Each tile pairs a lifestyle photo with a specific deal, such as "$100 & Under Select Shoes" and "Up to $120 Off Select Golf Electronics."
- The top strip shows the ScoreCard rewards status and the same-day pickup promise, which adds member value.
- The product grid near the bottom (Samba OG, Fleece Hoodie, Handball Spezial) gives concrete products with names.

## What's weak

- The hero has no product or holiday imagery. "Holiday deals" in the subject isn't backed by the creative, and the hero is just a number on a pattern.
- Too many CTAs compete: about eight "Shop Now" buttons with identical copy, so nothing is prioritized.
- The "up to" discounts vary (40%, 50%, $120 off, $100 and under), so there is no single clear offer.
- The product picks at the bottom show no prices or sale markers, so the grid doesn't convey a deal.
- The email is very long, and the fine print about the offer is small and light ("Valid online only for a limited time"). The "limited time" has no actual date.
- Buttons switch between red and green without any clear logic.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real holiday hero in front: a gift-ready product or family scene with the 50% offer, so the creative matches the subject's promise.
- 2. Cut the body to three or four blocks, and let the top-performing categories lead.
- 3. Name an end date ("Ends Sunday") to make the urgency credible.
- 4. Add sale prices and "was/now" to the product grid.
- 5. Make the CTAs specific: "Shop Cleats," "Shop Weights," "Shop Hoops," instead of repeating "Shop Now."
- 6. Use one button color.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Gift-ready deals: up to 50% off, ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Holiday gifts for every athlete, up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shoes, gear and fitness picks, plus same-day pickup on most orders.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Nike, adidas, New Balance and more. Prices drop through Sunday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is structurally sound (lang attribute, viewport meta, `role="presentation"` layout tables, MSO conditionals). It has one hard QA failure, a missing plain-text part, plus a gap in image alt text and an empty preheader. The HTML is truncated and header capture is limited by the relay, so the compliance, link and UTM checks are only partly verifiable.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Of 63 links, 12 were probed and 51 skipped by design (footer, utility and social links are deprioritized). The QA output lists no probe failures, so no broken links are confirmed.
- The open pixel is `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=…&configId=512ffb51-…`, a 1x1 image on a subdomain of the sender's own domain (`em.dcsg.com`). This is an expected configuration with no issue.
- The 51 skipped links, including the unsubscribe path, were not individually verified here, apart from the always-probed unsubscribe link, whose result isn't shown.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **13 images have no alt text** (QA WARN):
  - Eight promo images from `s7d6.scene7.com/is/image/DSGAEMSites/`: `1101-stw-4pm-100-under-shoes`, `-50-calia`, `-40-off-cleats`, `-100-golf`, `-50-off-outdoor`, `-50-hoops`, `-50-weights`.
  - Five icons: `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`.
  - The offers in the promo images are invisible with images blocked and to screen readers. The 1x1 open pixel is also flagged, which is harmless.
- **Preheader is empty.** The element `<div id="pht-spacer" …>` contains only `&zwnj;&nbsp;` padding. The template placeholder `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` was left unfilled, so inbox clients will pull preview text from body content instead.
- **Heavy hero images.** The Scene7 URLs request `wid=1200&hei=1600&qlt=100` JPEGs, which means large payloads for a 600px container.
- **Editor artifacts in `<body>`.** `class="is-html-mode" data-contenteditable="true"` is leftover from the authoring tool. It is functionally harmless.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible source. The open-pixel `tuid` parameter shows per-recipient tracking is populated.

## 5. Compliance
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is 9 characters.** There is effectively no text/plain alternative. This hurts deliverability and accessibility, though the relay may have truncated it.
- **[WARN] No `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header found.** The QA note says the relay may not capture these headers, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so verify against the raw message.
- **[WARN] No `Authentication-Results` header.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `em.dcsg.com` matches the tracking domain, which suggests alignment, but this isn't confirmed.
- The visible HTML doesn't show the footer, so the physical address and unsubscribe link couldn't be checked for CAN-SPAM. The QA output doesn't flag them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. UTM parameters and destination URLs are cut off in the truncated source, and the QA output has no landing-page alignment data. It doesn't show any failures either.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a real text/plain part** with the offers and links (the one hard failure).
2. **Check the raw message** for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and for SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment on `em.dcsg.com`. Confirm whether the relay is dropping the headers.
3. **Add descriptive alt text** to the eight promo images (for example "Up to 50% off Calia") and the five icons. Use `alt=""` on the decorative icons and the open pixel.
4. **Fill the preheader** (`#pht-spacer`) with real preview text and keep the padding after it.
5. **Reduce hero image weight** by lowering `qlt` to about 80 and requesting 2x of the rendered width instead of 1200x1600.
6. **Re-run the audit on the untruncated HTML** to verify UTM consistency and the footer compliance elements.
