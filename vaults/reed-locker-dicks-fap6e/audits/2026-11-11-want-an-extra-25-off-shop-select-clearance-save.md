# Want an EXTRA 25% off? Shop select clearance & SAVE!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a clear, loud clearance send. The red hero shows a giant "EXTRA 25% OFF" with a single Shop Now button, and the offer registers in about a second. That is the strongest part of the email. DICK'S has kept the same 11/11 sale chassis for days. This is the third or fourth variant of that sale, after the 11/11 "The 11.11 Sale is HERE!" send and the 11/10 Holiday Deal Dash sends.
- The email loses focus below the hero. A second offer ("Up to 70% off your favorite brands") sits under a clock graphic stuck on a frozen "H E" frame. That is the same broken animation as in this morning's 11.11 email. After that come about eight modules, each with its own "up to X%" claim, and the "extra 25%" message gets lost. "Select clearance" and the small-print exclusions also make the headline promise feel narrower than it looks.
- Verdict: a competent, high-volume promo with a strong hero and a cluttered body. It would get a solid click from a deal-seeker, but it adds little to a subscriber's inbox beyond what the earlier sends already said.

## What's working

- The hero is unmistakable: a huge "EXTRA 25% OFF" on solid red, with a "Limited Time" label and one Shop Now button.
- The category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 & Under, Fan Shop, All Sale) give quick paths to the right section without scrolling.
- ScoreCard loyalty branding and a pickup/delivery line sit at the top, so the member and fulfillment benefits are visible straight away.
- The holiday gift-picks grid at the bottom (Stanley, UGG, Under Armour) fits the season and shows named products with real prices context.
- The sender name "DICK'S | Clearance" tells you what the email is before you open it.

## What's weak

- The "H E" clock graphic in the second module looks frozen mid-animation. It is a black box with green letters that doesn't communicate "70% off." It also repeats the same bug as the earlier 11.11 send.
- The offers compete with each other: extra 25%, up to 70%, up to 50% (three times) and up to 40%. A reader can't tell which deal matters most.
- The key conditions are in very small fine print: "select" styles only, exclusions apply, and the deadline is hard to read. The 25% promise feels qualified.
- Almost every CTA is a generic "Shop Now," and the hero links to a category rather than a specific product or a clear "see clearance" destination.
- The email is very long, at roughly 6,400px. The body repeats the same module pattern, so the hero's message fades by the middle.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body to the hero plus two or three modules that support the extra 25%, such as clearance by category and a few clearance products with was/now prices. Move the "Up to 70% off" and fan-shop blocks to separate sends.
- 2. Fix or replace the frozen "H E" animation with a static fallback image that carries the offer. Check that animated blocks render a useful first frame in clients that don't animate.
- 3. Put the deadline and the key exclusions in plain type under the hero button, for example "Ends 11/13 at 11:59 PM ET. Select styles."
- 4. Replace "Shop Now" with specific copy such as "Shop clearance deals" or "Shop 25% off shoes."
- 5. Show 4-6 actual clearance products with prices and star ratings above the category tiles.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 25% off clearance, ends Friday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Clearance just got cheaper: extra 25% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shoes, fan gear and more, plus free store pickup on select orders.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Select clearance styles only. Ends 11/13 at 11:59 PM ET.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML is mostly sound (`lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` layout tables, a viewport meta tag, MSO conditionals, a preheader spacer). It has two real defects: 12 `<img>` tags with no alt text, and an invalid JSON-LD block carrying template leftovers. The header-related compliance warnings can't be confirmed as defects from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links were reported. QA probed 12 material links and skipped 55, so most of the link inventory was not verified, including footer links.
- The open-tracking pixel is `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/v61000001a1cb5ce3eda3bc7a6e966a3578/dc7bbecafa2549b200004c5a42963aa1`. It is hidden with `display:none; max-height:0; mso-hide:all`, which is standard practice. It has no `alt`, `width` or `height`.
- The CTA hrefs and UTM parameters are not visible in the truncated source, so tracking-parameter hygiene can't be assessed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **12 images are missing alt text.**
  - Six are content images: `1111-stw-11-100-shoes`, `2026-1110-dks-swd-4pm-jersey`, `1111-stw-11-outdoor`, `1111-stw-11-40-sports`, `1111-stw-11-50-golf` and `1111-stw-11-50-fitness`. They are served at `wid=1200&hei=1600` from `s7d6.scene7.com`. If the offer copy is baked into the images, which is likely but not confirmed from the truncated source, images-off clients and screen readers get no offer information.
  - Five are footer icons on `dsg.images.harmony.epsilon.com/.../footers/`: `icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png` and `icon-location-2025.png`. Use `alt=""` if they are decorative. Use descriptive alt text if they sit next to a link with no text label.
  - The tracking pixel is the twelfth. It should carry `alt=""`.
- **The Outlook conditional CSS is malformed.** In `<!--[if (gte mso 9)|(IE)]><style>…`, the `div{...padding:0;"}` rule has a stray `"` before the closing brace. Outlook may drop the declaration or the whole rule.
- **The wrapper table has a conflicting width.** It uses `width="600"` as an attribute and `width:100%; max-width:600px` inline. Outlook desktop ignores `max-width`, so it will take the attribute and render the table at 600px. That is likely acceptable.
- **Web fonts are served from `fonts.gstatic.com`, version v7 of Archivo.** The `@font-face` rules are wrapped in `@media screen`, and the stack falls back to Arial. No issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source. The truncated portion wasn't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post and Authentication-Results are not found.** QA itself attributes all three to the AgentMail relay stripping or not capturing the headers. From this data, the absence is unconfirmed, not a verified defect. Check the raw `.eml` from the original delivery to Gmail or another mailbox before acting on it.
- The sender is `DSG@e.dcsg.com`, a subdomain of a different registrable domain from `dickssportinggoods.com`. This is normal for ESP sending. SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment can't be checked here.
- The unsubscribe link and physical address are in the footer. QA says an unsubscribe link was included in probing, but no failure was reported and the footer is outside the visible truncated source, so it wasn't verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Can't be assessed. The truncated source exposes no CTA hrefs, UTM parameters or landing page data, and QA reports no redirect or landing mismatches among the 12 probed links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add alt text.**
   - Give the six content images alt text that states the offer, for example "Extra 25% off select clearance shoes".
   - Set `alt=""` on the footer icons and the tracking pixel.
2. **Fix the Gmail promotion annotation (JSON-LD).**
   - The block contains `//` comments, which are invalid JSON, so the whole script will fail to parse and Gmail will ignore it. Remove the comments.
   - Remove the template leftovers: the "DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!" note, the empty `"image": ""`, `"discountCode": ""`, `"description": ""` and `"availabilityEnds": ""`, and the stale `availabilityStarts` of `2020-03-06`.
   - Either populate the `DiscountOffer` and `PromotionCard` entries properly or drop them. Also drop the comment `<!--/GMAIL PREVIEW CODE-->`, which has no matching opener.
3. **Fix the Outlook conditional style block.** Remove the stray `"` in `padding:0;"}`.
4. **Verify the headers on the raw message.** Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC `Authentication-Results` outside the relay. This matters for Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
5. **Expand link probing.** Cover the 55 skipped links, at least the unsubscribe, preference-center and primary CTA destinations, and check UTM consistency on them.
