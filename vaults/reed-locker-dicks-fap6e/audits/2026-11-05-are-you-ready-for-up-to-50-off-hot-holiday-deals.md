# Are you ready for up to 50% off HOT holiday deals?
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth "up to 50% off" DICK'S send in three days. It follows 11/3 "FINAL HOURS for up to 50% off", 11/4 "Unwrap early savings: up to 50% off this week's deals" and 11/5 "Great deal: Up to 50% off select clothing & shoes". The hero is the same green argyle panel with giant gold "UP TO 50% OFF" and one red Shop Now button. The eye lands on the offer immediately, and the execution is clean.
- Below the hero it becomes a long, multi-category scroll. It runs from running shoes and fleece to fan gear, Callaway clubs, a YETI bottle, a ping-pong table, dumbbells and a gift grid. Each block has its own offer and button, so the hero's single message gets diluted. The "Final Hours" kicker on the fan gear block reads as urgency, but its deadline is 11/6, so it is mild at best.
- Judgement: a competent, on-brand template send with weak differentiation from the last three days. The subject's "HOT holiday deals" gives no new reason to open. It lands around 6 for a general sports shopper.

## What's working

- The hero's gold "UP TO 50% OFF" on deep green is large and unmissable, with a red Shop Now button right beneath it.
- Category pill buttons (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Holiday Shop) give fast navigation.
- The gift picks grid (Stanley, Callaway balls, UGG slippers) fits the holiday season.
- Each block has a clear specific offer, such as "Up to $200 Off Select Callaway Quantum Woods & Irons" and "Sale $269.98 Monarch Navigator Table Tennis Tables".
- The footer has useful utility: ScoreCard Rewards, a price guarantee, app download links and a store finder.

## What's weak

- Mid-email blocks fight each other. Nothing says which of the 8 or so offers matters most.
- The fine print on offers is dense and tiny, and the footer carries several asterisked disclaimers.
- There is no member pricing or personalized hook above the fold, only a small "ScoreCard" link in the header.
- The "Final Hours" deadline on fan gear conflicts with the broader "limited time" hero copy.
- At about 5,900px tall, the email is a very long scroll for a generic 50% promotion.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero product category, or add a real deadline and countdown, to separate this send from the previous three days.
- 2. Cut the body to 3 or 4 blocks. Keep the highest-margin or seasonal items (gifts, fan gear) and drop the niche ones (ping-pong table, YETI).
- 3. Add a ScoreCard member line near the hero, such as "Members get extra points on gifts".
- 4. Make the hero CTA specific: "Shop holiday deals" instead of "Shop Now".
- 5. Move the gift grid higher, since it is the most seasonal and persona-relevant section.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fan gear, golf and fitness picks, plus same-day pickup.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "Are you ready for up to 50% off HOT holiday deals?"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML structure is sound: `lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` layout tables, a viewport meta tag and a 600px max-width. The weak points are accessibility (13 of the images QA checked have no alt text), a near-empty plain-text part (9 chars), an unreplaced preheader placeholder, and header checks that could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Open pixel:** `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1.…&pid=…&tuid=6aeb8fb6aa64c1741b297bc9&configId=512ffb51-…` is a 1×1 `<img>` with `width="1" height="1"`. It has no `alt` and no `style="display:block;border:0"`. It also sits directly under `<body>` ahead of the layout table, which is acceptable.
- **Probe coverage:** QA probed only 12 material links and skipped 52 of the roughly 64 total (about 19% coverage). The QA output has no HTTP status results, so I can't confirm any broken links.
- **Link markup:** Anchors use `class="arc-link" data-nl-type="DEEPLI…"`, but the source is truncated, so I can't check the href format or redirect chain.
- **Hero images:** The Scene7 images request `qlt=100&wid=1200&hei=1600` (for example `2026-1105-dks-storewide-4pm-1?fmt=jpeg&qlt=100`). JPEG quality 100 at 1200px is likely heavy for a 584px content column. Actual file sizes are not in the QA data.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (13 images):**
  - `open` (tracking pixel)
  - `2026-1105-dks-storewide-4pm-1`, `-2`, `-3-new`, `-4-new` and `-5`
  - `fanshop-2026-0929-dks-swd-6am`
  - `2026-1103-dks-outdoor-pl-yeti-wildrose-banner-1`
  - `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1` and `icon-vc-favorite-1`
  - `icon-location-2025-1`
- **Impact of the missing alt text:** The file names suggest the promotional content is image-based. With images blocked, or with a screen reader, that content would be missing or read out as file names. Mail clients may fall back to the URL or file name as the label.
- **Preheader placeholder:** `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` is still in the source. The `#pht-spacer` div holds only `&zwnj;&nbsp;` padding and no preview text. Inbox preview text will therefore fall back to the first body text, or to nothing.
- **Plain-text part:** QA reports the text version is 9 characters. This can be a relay capture artifact, but if it is accurate the email has no usable text alternative.
- **Unneeded head element:** `<link rel="icon" …favicon.ico>` has no function in email and is stripped by most clients.
- **No issues found with:**
  - Outlook handling: the MSO conditional blocks (`OfficeDocumentSettings`, `border-collapse`) are present.
  - Web-font fallback: `Archivo, Arial, sans-serif`.
  - Dark-mode and responsive hooks: the `@media screen and (max-width:480px)` classes are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered `{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]` or `${}` tokens. The source is truncated, so I can only vouch for the part shown.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** QA did not find either header. QA itself notes that the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. If they are really absent, there is no RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe, which Gmail and Yahoo require of bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `info@em.dcsg.com` is unknown. This can't be assessed from this capture.
- **Footer:** The footer is outside the truncated source. I can't confirm the physical address or the in-body unsubscribe link, though QA states an unsubscribe link was included in the probe set.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The truncated source and QA data show no hrefs, UTM parameters or probe destinations. Tracking appears to be handled through the `data.em.dcsg.com` redirect and `data-nl-type` attributes, not visible UTMs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 12 content and icon images. Use `alt=""` plus `style="display:block;border:0"` on the open pixel and the decorative icons. Put the offer text, "up to 50% off" and the CTA, in alt or live text.
2. Replace the `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` placeholder with real preheader text. Keep the `&zwnj;&nbsp;` padding after it.
3. Add a real multipart `text/plain` alternative with the offer and links. The current 9-character part fails the QA check.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` in the raw headers at the sending ESP, not through the relay. Also confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
5. Lower the Scene7 `qlt=100` to about 80 and check image weights to reduce load time and the risk of Gmail clipping.
6. Re-run link probing with a larger budget, since 52 links were skipped. Also capture the full HTML so the footer, address and UTM parameters can be audited.
7. Remove the `<link rel="icon">` from the head.
