# Ends Tonight 📲 2X Points in the app! Don't let this ScoreCard exclusive get away...
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-09**
## Executive summary

- This is the "last call" version of this morning's ScoreCard 2X Points send (11/9: "ScoreCard exclusive 🙌 2X Points on your app order TODAY ONLY"). It's the right follow-up. The green "Final Hours 07:59:48" countdown bar sits at the top, and a phone hero with a giant "2X POINTS" sits under it. A single "Get Offer" button sits right beneath the headline "Mobile Monday! Earn 2X Points on Your App Purchase." The deadline, 11/9 at 11:59PM PT, is visible in the fine print.
- The email then loses focus. Below the hero are a row of category pills, a second full-width hero ("UP TO 70% OFF"), four product grids, a gift-picks block and a long legal footer. The 70% clearance banner is the same size as the points hero and competes with it. A shopper who opened for the points offer is shown a bigger-looking dollars-off number and a pile of "Shop Now" buttons.
- Judgement: good urgency, and the countdown and "Ends Tonight" kicker are credible. But this is a catalog wrapped around a one-line promo. The points offer is also an app-only perk, and the app download block is buried near the bottom. It's a solid reminder that dilutes its own message.

## What's working

- The "Final Hours 07:59:48" countdown bar at the top makes the deadline feel real.
- The phone mockup with a giant "2X POINTS" lands the offer in about a second.
- The "Ends Tonight" kicker and the single "Get Offer" button under the headline make the primary action clear.
- The sender and branding are consistent, with the DICK'S logo up top and the ScoreCard logo in the header.
- The holiday backdrop, with green and gold, fits the season.

## What's weak

- The "UP TO 70% OFF" hero is almost as large as the points hero. It steals attention from the one offer the subject promised.
- Four product grids, category pills and a gift-picks block turn a single-offer reminder into a catalog.
- The "Get Offer" button goes to an unclear destination. It doesn't say whether it opens the app or a page, and the copy has no noun.
- The "Download The App" block with the App Store and Google Play badges sits near the bottom. The 2X offer needs the app, so this should be near the top.
- The offer terms are tiny. The "±" and "Must sign in to redeem" notes are easy to miss, and the legal footer is very long.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body down to the points offer. Keep the hero, the headline, one app CTA and the app badges directly under it. Move the 70% clearance block and the product grids out of this send, or push them far below.
- 2. Change the CTA from "Get Offer" to "Open the App & Earn 2X". Add the App Store and Google Play badges right beneath it.
- 3. Move the redemption terms ("must sign in," "not a ScoreCard member? Join today") into a visible line under the button. Keep the full legal text in the footer.
- 4. Put a time on the deadline in the preview, such as "Ends 11:59PM PT," so the urgency is concrete.

## 1. Overview
