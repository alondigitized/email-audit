# Up to 70% OFF select clearance? Right this way >>>
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- This is the third "up to 70% off clearance" subject DICK'S has sent in about 48 hours, after 11/16's "Limited time! ⏰ Up to 70% OFF select clearance styles is all yours..." and 11/17's "Up to 70% OFF select clearance? Yes, please! 🙌 ➡️". Only the arrow changed, from 🙌 ➡️ to ">>>". The body is a well-built, but long, modular promo. A big red-and-gold "UP TO 70% OFF" hero sits above a single "Shop Now" button. Below it come five or six category blocks (40% off top brands, fan shop, golf, team sports, drinkware, fitness) and a holiday wish list.
- The hero does its job. The weakness is everything after it. The email has no deadline, only a vague "Limited Time" tag. The clearance promise in the subject turns into a general sale catalog, and the 70% hero is followed by five other offers at 40-50%. The long scroll dilutes the one message the subject sold.
- Verdict: competent execution and a clear offer, but the cadence is stale and the urgency is missing. Subscribers who got the 11/16 and 11/17 sends have seen this hero before.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 70% OFF" hero is huge, high-contrast and instantly readable.
- A single green "Shop Now" button sits directly under the hero headline, so the primary CTA is unambiguous.
- Category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 & Under, Fan Shop, All Sales) give a quick way to browse by interest.
- Holiday imagery (ornaments and trees on the golf, sports and fitness blocks) and the gift picks fit the season.
- The consistent green, red and gold palette makes each block's discount easy to scan.

## What's weak

- There is no hard deadline. "Limited Time" is the only urgency, and it's weaker than the 11/18 "Holiday Deal Dash ends today" send.
- The email repeats the clearance beat from 11/16 and 11/17, so it reads as near-duplicate creative.
- Below the hero, five more blocks at 40-50% off compete with the 70% headline and split attention.
- The fine print under the hero is small and dense, covering exclusions and "interim markdowns may have been taken". It undercuts the headline claim.
- The email is very long, about 6,500px, and the buried CTAs mostly say "Shop Now" or "Deals", so they don't tell the reader what's behind the click.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real deadline and put it in the hero, for example "Ends Sunday" or a countdown bar like the one used on 11/16. This is the biggest single lift.
- 2. Cut the body to the hero plus two or three blocks. Keep the clearance message instead of repeating generic category promos.
- 3. Show two or three specific clearance products with a was/now price under the hero, so the 70% claim has proof.
- 4. Make the CTA specific: "Shop Clearance Up to 70% Off" instead of "Shop Now".
- 5. Space out the clearance sends. Give this beat a new angle (a size run, a category, a last-chance tier) instead of repeating the same hero.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Clearance up to 70% off: ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 70% off clearance cleats, hoodies & more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Extra markdowns on shoes, apparel & gear. Ends Sunday at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sizes are going fast. Shop clearance before it's gone.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is structurally sound, using a `role="presentation"` table layout, a 600px max-width wrapper, and `@media` mobile classes. It has three real defects: 11 images with no `alt`, a JSON-LD block that isn't valid JSON, and leftover template placeholders. The header-based compliance checks are inconclusive, not failed. The HTML source was truncated, so the link and footer findings below are limited to what the QA data shows.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links were reported. QA probed 12 material links and skipped 55 (footer, utility and social links are deprioritized). The 55 skipped links, including social links, are unverified.
- The truncated source exposes no CTA hrefs, so I can't check redirect chains or tracking-wrapper behavior.
- The open-tracking pixel is `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/v61000001a1ef76b1c78dff9f369fb45280/abf3ee231ef3428000004c5a42963aa1`. It is hidden with `display:none; max-height:0; mso-hide:all`, which is correct. It has no `alt`, `width` or `height`, which is harmless for a hidden pixel but is counted in the alt-text warnings.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on 11 images (QA-confirmed):**
  - The hidden tracking pixel.
  - Five content images from `s7d6.scene7.com/is/image/DSGAEMSites/`: `1118-stw-11am-fan`, `-golf`, `-sports`, `-fitness` and `50-off-banner-dks2026-1115-11`.
  - Five footer icons from `dsg.images.harmony.epsilon.com/_phoenix/_dev/AEM/img/footers/`: `icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png` and `icon-location-2025.png`.
  - Because the content images are the promo banners, an image-blocked client likely shows no readable offer. Screen readers will announce the filenames or skip the images.
  - Decorative images should carry `alt=""`. Informational images need descriptive alt text.
- **Invalid JSON-LD:**
  - The `<script type="application/ld+json">` block contains `//` comments (`// GIF & WEBP images are not supported…`, `// DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!`), which are not valid JSON. Parsers such as Gmail's will likely reject the whole block, so the promo annotation won't render.
  - The block also has `"image": ""`, `"discountCode": ""`, `"description": ""` and `"availabilityEnds": ""`.
  - `"availabilityStarts"` is `2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00`, a stale date from a template.
- **Leftover template artifacts:**
  - `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->`.
  - A closing `<!--/GMAIL PREVIEW CODE-->` with no matching opening comment in the visible source.
- **Malformed MSO CSS:** the conditional-comment style `div {…margin:0; padding:0;"}` has a stray `"` before `}`. Outlook may drop that rule.
- **Fonts:** `Archivo` is loaded from `fonts.gstatic.com` inside `@media screen`, with an `Arial, sans-serif` fallback. This is fine.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source contains no unrendered merge tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The truncated remainder was not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA flags that the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is not confirmed as a sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo require both headers for bulk senders, so check them on the raw message at the sending ESP.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, and the relay is the stated likely cause. The From domain `e.dcsg.com` should be checked against the original message.
- The footer, physical address and unsubscribe link were not visible in the truncated source. QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but it reports no probe failure.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
This can't be assessed. No CTA hrefs or UTM parameters are visible, and QA reports no probe failures. The hero asset filename `50-off-banner-dks2026-1115-11` suggests a reused 50%-off banner in an email whose subject says "Up to 70% OFF". This is an inference from the filename only, so check the landing page offer against the creative.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` text to the 5 content and 5 footer images, using `alt=""` for decorative ones. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
2. Fix the JSON-LD: remove the `//` comments, fill or remove the empty fields, and update or drop `availabilityStarts`. Validate it with Google's email markup tester.
3. Remove the template remnants (`Replace/Paste SSL Here`, the orphan `/GMAIL PREVIEW CODE` comment) and fix the stray `"` in the MSO `div` rule.
4. Check the raw message at the sending ESP for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and passing SPF/DKIM/DMARC, then update the QA tooling to account for relay header stripping.
5. Extend link probing to the 55 skipped links, or at least confirm the UTM parameters and landing-page offer on the CTAs.
