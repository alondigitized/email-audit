# Don't miss it! Extra 30%% off clearance finds you'll love👀
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-02**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S eleventh-plus promotional send since June 26, and it's a clearance segmentation play that trips over its own layout. The hero is "Extra 30% Off Select Clearance," clean and readable — but scroll half an inch and you hit a competing "50% Off Epic 4th of July Sale" banner. Two heroes, two discount depths, two vibes. The email can't commit to what it is, and a subscriber who's already skim-reading after a week of daily blasts won't stick around to sort it out.
- There's also a quiet QA miss baked into the subject: "Extra 30%% off" — the double percent sign is a plaintext escape artifact. It doesn't break the email but it's the kind of thing a trained eye spots immediately, and at this send volume there's no excuse for it slipping through.
- The clearance-sender segmentation ("DICK'S | Clearance") is the single smartest move in this send and arguably the only thing that gives it a distinct inbox identity. Everything else — the generic "SHOP NOW" CTA, the vague urgency, the split creative — works against it.

## What's working

- **Clearance sender segmentation** — "DICK'S | Clearance" correctly signals this is a self-contained clearance send, not just another generic blast.
- **Hero badge is immediately readable** — "EXTRA 30% OFF" in oversized white type dominates the render and communicates the offer in under a second.
- **Product-level specificity below the fold** — New Balance 990 shoes, DSG Agility Tees, YETI gear, Columbia — actual SKU callouts give subscribers a concrete reason to click rather than dumping them on the homepage.
- **Timely calendar alignment** — dropping on July 2 with 4th of July creative is on the right day.

## What's weak

- **Dual-hero splits the offer** — a 30% clearance hero and a separate 50% 4th of July Sale banner compete directly and dilute both.
- **%% bug in the subject line** — visible escape artifact; minor but signals a copy QA miss on a high-volume list.
- **No credible deadline** — "Don't miss it!" with no end date is hollow at this cadence; subscribers know there'll be another email tomorrow.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA is maximum-generic** — at send eleven of the week, "Shop Clearance Shoes" or "Shop Men's Sale" would move the needle more than a homepage redirect.
- **Cadence is destroying open-rate equity** — the July 2 "USA wins!" send earned its inbox slot with a genuine news hook (scored 9/10); this one doesn't. Running this close to a reactive win dilutes the memory.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Commit to one offer per send** — either clearance or holiday sale, not both. The 30% clearance frame is the stronger clearance angle; the 50% 4th of July sale belongs in its own email.
- 2. **Fix the %% bug before the next send** — it's a mail merge escape issue and should be caught in template QA.
- 3. **Replace "SHOP NOW" with category-specific CTAs** — "Shop Running Clearance," "Shop Men's Tees Under $20" — specificity converts better on clearance sends where the subscriber is browsing on price.
- 4. **Add a concrete deadline** — "Ends July 4 at midnight" is all you need to make the urgency play credible.
- 5. **Pull back the cadence** — a 2–3 day cooling period after a standout send like "USA wins!" would make this clearance email land harder.

No standard `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, or `utm_content` parameters are visible in the decoded redirect chain from the truncated source. ### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Subject line double-percent escape unresolved**
```
Subject: Don't miss it! Extra 30%% off clearance finds you'll love👀
```
`%%` is the literal escape sequence for `%` in ExactTarget/SFMC AMPscript. If this is the raw captured subject before send-time rendering, it will resolve correctly to `30% off`. If subscribers received `30%%`, this is a rendering failure. **Verify against a seed inbox send.**

**Empty JSON-LD DiscountOffer fields**
```json
"description": "",
"discountCode": "",
"availabilityEnds": ""
```
Empty required fields in the PromotionCard and DiscountOffer blocks mean Gmail will not surface a promotional annotation card. These should either be populated or the schema block omitted.

**Stale `availabilityStarts` date**
```json
"availabilityStarts": "2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"
```
This is a copy-paste artifact from a template dated March 2020 — over 6 years stale. Gmail may suppress the annotation entirely on date-range validation failure.
