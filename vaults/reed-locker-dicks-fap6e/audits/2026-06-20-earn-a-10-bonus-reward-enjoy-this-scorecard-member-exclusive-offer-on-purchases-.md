# Earn a $10 Bonus Reward!🎉 Enjoy this ScoreCard member-exclusive offer on purchases of $150+!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a ScoreCard loyalty email from the main DICK'S Sporting Goods account — a meaningful break from the seven consecutive clearance blasts that have dominated this brand's recent cadence. The $10 Bonus Reward hook is the right lead: it's a real, member-exclusive offer with a clear trigger threshold ($150+), and the green-and-white hero treatment stands apart from the dark-red clearance wallpaper that's been running since June 16.
- The loyalty framing earns its placement. But below the hero the email reverts to kitchen-sink mode: apparel deals, kayaks, golf balls, YETI products, a summer shop block, and — inevitably — the Extra 25% Off Clearance module makes yet another appearance. That module has now run in essentially every DICK'S email this past week, and at this point it reads as filler, not urgency. It actively undercuts the ScoreCard exclusivity message that opened strong.
- Verdict: the best email this brand has sent in the past week. The loyalty hero is well-executed, the summer angle is seasonally coherent, and the ScoreCard framing implies real list segmentation. The recycled clearance cameo is lazy, the subject line is 40 characters too long, and the $10 bonus has no visible deadline — which is the one thing that would have made this genuinely excellent.

## What's working

- **$10 Bonus Reward hero is clear and specific** — dollar amount, trigger threshold, and ScoreCard exclusivity all visible above the fold without scrolling.
- **Green-and-white visual treatment differentiates** from the brand's recent parade of dark-red clearance banners — the eye immediately registers "this one is different."
- **Summer-relevant category compilation** (kayaks, YETI, summer shop) holds together thematically rather than feeling arbitrary.
- **Golf ball 2-for-$24 module is unusually specific** — a concrete unit price for a consumable is exactly the kind of detail that triggers category-aware clicks.
- **ScoreCard member-exclusive framing** implies real segmentation, which raises perceived relevance for loyalty members.

## What's weak

- **Extra 25% Off Clearance module appears again** — this is at minimum the eighth time this module has run in roughly five days. It signals creative exhaustion, not urgency, and dilutes the exclusivity of the loyalty hero above it.
- **No visible deadline on the $10 Bonus offer** — the hero has no "Ends Sunday" or expiry callout, removing the one element that would create urgency for an otherwise compelling offer.
- **Kitchen-sink layout below the fold** — six competing offer blocks with no clear hierarchy after the hero means the eye has nowhere to land and the loyalty story dissolves.
- **Generic "SHOP NOW" CTAs throughout** — every module, regardless of category, gets the same button copy. No specificity, no pull.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add an expiry date to the $10 Bonus hero** — "Offer ends Sunday, June 22" or "Through 6/22 only" adds urgency without dishonesty. Single highest-lift change in this email.
- 2. **Cut or bury the Extra 25% Off Clearance module** — running it for the eighth consecutive send is now actively hurting perceived send value. If clearance must appear, move it below the fold and strip the giant banner treatment.
- 3. **Trim the subject line by 40+ characters** — it's 93 chars and truncates well before "$150+" lands on mobile. The exclusivity and reward hook are enough.
- 4. **Give each CTA specific copy** — "Shop Kayaks," "Shop Golf Balls," "Explore YETI" instead of five identical "SHOP NOW" buttons. Specificity lifts CTR on individual modules.
- 5. **Use preheader to carry the urgency** — the subject can hold the reward hook; preheader should hold the deadline and a supporting detail.
- **Subject Alt A:** `ScoreCard Members: Earn a $10 Bonus on $150+`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your $10 Bonus Reward is waiting — members only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Offer ends Sunday — plus up to 50% off summer gear`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop $150+ by Sunday, earn a $10 ScoreCard bonus`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — DICK'S Sporting Goods ScoreCard Bonus Reward Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Email is delivered via Epsilon Harmony (`e.dcsg.com` / `app.dickssportinggoods.com`), using standard table-based layout with MSO conditional comments. Two issues require immediate fix before deployment: invalid JSON-LD that will fail schema parsing, and a CSS syntax error in the Outlook conditional block.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**`__dU__` subscriber token exposed in click URLs**
The click-tracking redirect at `app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...` appends a `__dU__` query parameter containing a base64-encoded subscriber identifier:
```
?__dU__=v0G4RBKTXg2Gs8NEX0-RpwlL2_Q96sIof5wvgkGa1VsPIAdb-6PwUuXUbOES4DYwVKlEBH4wAjEqvhvBlpUfxLCgwho1v8uViS
```
If a recipient forwards or shares a tracked link, the subscriber token is exposed to third parties. Standard practice; flag to legal/privacy if PII is encoded.

**No UTM parameters visible in click URLs**
UTM parameters are absent from the tracked URLs in the visible source. If the ESP does not append them post-redirect, attribution to this campaign will be missing from site analytics. Verify Epsilon redirect config appends `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` before the send.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Malformed CSS in MSO conditional block**
The `<!--[if (gte mso 9)|(IE)]>` style block contains an invalid `div` rule:
```css
div {background-color:inherit; font-size:0; line-height:100%; margin:0; padding:0;"}
```
A stray `"` appears before the closing `}`. This is invalid CSS; Outlook's parser may drop this rule and any rules that follow it in the block, causing layout breakdown in Outlook 2007–2019.

**`<link rel="icon">` in `<head>`**
```html
<link rel="icon" type="image/x-icon" href="https://www.dickssportinggoods.com/home/assets/icons/dsg/favicon.ico"/>
```
Email clients do not render favicons. This generates an unnecessary external HTTP request that may be blocked by security-conscious clients and adds noise to the web server logs. Remove it.

**Image `alt` text**
The logo `<img id="logo">` tag is truncated in the provided source before the `alt` attribute appears. Confirm `alt="DICK'S Sporting Goods"` (or equivalent) is present — required for image-blocked clients.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Segment ID contradicts email targeting**
The primary header table carries `id="header-non-sc-member"` in an email explicitly targeted to ScoreCard members. This is either a stale ID from a template fork or indicates the wrong content block was rendered. Verify suppression/segmentation logic selects the correct variant.

**Unfilled merge tokens in JSON-LD**
Three fields in the `DiscountOffer` schema are empty strings — unfilled at send time:
```json
"description": "",
"discountCode": "",
"availabilityEnds": ""
```
And in `PromotionCard`:
```json
"image": ""
```
Empty required fields will cause Gmail's promotional annotations to silently fail (no deal chip, no image in the Promotions tab).

**Stale `availabilityStarts` date**
```json
"availabilityStarts": "2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"
```
This is a 2020 timestamp in a 2026 deployment. This is placeholder data left in the template. Gmail may ignore the schema block entirely when dates are incoherent.

---

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM / Unsubscribe / Authentication)

**Cannot confirm from truncated source:** Physical mailing address and unsubscribe link presence require the full HTML. The sending domain `e.dcsg.com` is a subdomain used for ESP relay — verify SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass for this domain (separate from `dickssportinggoods.com`).

**Subject line length**
`Earn a $10 Bonus Reward!🎉 Enjoy this ScoreCard member-exclusive offer on purchases of $150+!` is 93 characters. Many clients truncate previews at 60–80 characters. Not a compliance issue; flagged for awareness.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**UTM gaps (see §2)** — without confirmed UTM parameters on click redirects, GA4/Adobe Analytics will classify ScoreCard member click traffic as direct or unattributed.

**JSON-LD schema dates (see §4)** — the incoherent 2020 `availabilityStarts` will prevent Gmail Promotions tab deal surfacing, breaking the intended email→deal discovery flow.
