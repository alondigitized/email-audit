# Shop now for an EXTRA 25% off select clearance!!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-21**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S at its most sprawling: a lake-photo hero for "EXTRA 25% OFF Select Clearance" up top, then a full digest scroll — ScoreCard member week (20% off), 50 Bonus Points, fan shop gear, Topps trading cards, clothing/shoes, Odyssey putters, cleats, and a second "UP TO 50% OFF" green banner near the bottom before the product grid. The hero offer is at least specific and honest-sounding ("Extra 25% Off Select Clearance," limited time), which is a step up from the vaguer "up to X%" wordmark sends this brand has been running daily. But burying a second, bigger "UP TO 50% OFF" banner two-thirds down the same email undercuts the 25% hero entirely — why would a shopper click the smaller number when a bigger one is one thumb-swipe away?
- This is the same weekly-digest machine seen in 8/20's "up to 50% off" and 8/19's "clearance" send, just with a new lead number and lake-photo hero swapped in. Subscriber fatigue is real here — this is roughly the sixth near-identical structural send in six days.
- Judgment: a middling execution of a format that's overstayed its welcome. The hero itself is clean and legible, but the internal offer-hierarchy conflict and relentless cadence keep this in the "flag scroll past" bucket rather than "meaningful engagement."

## What's working

- Hero is clean and legible: bold "EXTRA 25% OFF" over a lake photo, high contrast, no render bugs.
- Specific, singular clearance offer up top (not another vague "up to X%" wordmark), with a clear "Shop Now" CTA button.
- Category quick-links (Men's/Women's/Kid's/$25 and Under/Fan Shop/All Gear) right below the hero give multiple easy entry points.
- No overlapping text, broken images, or lorem-ipsum anywhere in the render.

## What's weak

- A second "UP TO 50% OFF" banner appears about two-thirds down, directly competing with and outshining the 25% hero — confusing offer hierarchy.
- Sprawling digest again: clearance, ScoreCard member week, bonus points, fan shop, trading cards, clothing, putters, cleats, and a product grid all in one send — no focus.
- This is essentially the same structural send as 8/19's clearance email and 8/20's "up to 50%" email, just re-skinned — cadence fatigue is real.
- The lake-photo hero has nothing to do with "clearance" or golf/football gear below it — generic stock imagery, no product tie-in.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the second "UP TO 50% OFF" banner or move the 25%-off clearance hero to be the single loudest number in the email — right now they cannibalize each other.
- 2. Trim the digest to 3-4 sections max; split fan shop/Topps/putters/cleats into separate sends rather than one mega-scroll.
- 3. Replace the generic lake stock photo with an actual clearance product shot (shoes, apparel) so the hero visually matches the offer.
- 4. Slow the cadence — six near-identical structural sends in six days trains subscribers to skim past, not click.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 25% off clearance — today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your clearance picks just dropped an extra 25%`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Styles are going fast — shop before they're gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Extra savings on already-reduced gear, while it lasts.`
