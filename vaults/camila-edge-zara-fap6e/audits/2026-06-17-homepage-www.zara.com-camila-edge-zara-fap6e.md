# Homepage snapshot · www.zara.com · 2026-06-17
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-17**
## Executive summary

- I opened Zara on my phone and got a full-screen gate asking me to pick my location and language before I can see anything. This isn't a homepage — it's a wall. There's zero editorial content, no campaign, no product, nothing that tells me what Zara is doing right now. For a brand that drops editorial-quality content every week, this landing experience is a dead end.

## What's working

- **Clean render:** The gate itself loads fast and looks sharp — no broken elements, no layout shift. The ZARA logotype is bold and the hierarchy (logo → fields → CONTINUE) is instantly readable on mobile.
- **"Remember selection" checkbox:** Small detail, but it signals the site is at least trying not to annoy repeat visitors with this same gate next time. I'd check it.

## What's weak

- **The gate is the entire experience:** I can't see a single product, campaign image, seasonal story, or navigation link. For a drops-watcher, this is the worst possible first screen — I've learned nothing about what dropped this week.
- **No bypass or "continue as guest" skip:** If I've been to Zara before, I already know I'm in the US. Making me click through every time (if the cookie expired) is pure friction.
- **Zero brand voice:** The only copy is `SELECT YOUR LOCATION`, `LOCATION`, `LANGUAGE`, `CONTINUE`, and `DO NOT SELL OR SHARE MY PERSONAL INFORMATION`. That last line at the bottom is the most text-heavy thing on the page, which is a weird energy.
- **No shortcut to SRPLS or Studio:** A returning shopper like me would want to go directly to a collection. There's no nav, no quick-link, nothing.

## Recommendations

- **Detect location automatically and skip the gate for returning users** — use the stored cookie + IP fallback. Only show this screen when detection fails.
- **If the gate must exist, background it with the current campaign visual** — at minimum drop the current hero image behind the modal so I know what's waiting for me. Zara's editorials are beautiful; use them.
- **Add a ghost "skip / continue to US site" link** under the CONTINUE button so power users can bypass in one tap without engaging the dropdowns.
- **Surface the SRPLS / Studio collection name somewhere on this screen** — even a single line of editorial copy ("SRPLS 26 is here") would make me more eager to hit CONTINUE.
