The headline "A touch of fur. Not new but still cool!" and the subline "A curated second-hand selection" tell you the premise quickly. ## What's working

- The "ZARA PRE-OWNED" wordmark and the fur hero make the topic obvious at first glance.
- The headline "Not new but still cool!" gives the second-hand idea a fresh, non-apologetic tone.
- The "Nights in Red" module with the red sequin skirt is a strong focal point and a seasonal party cue.
- The module structure is easy to scan: grid, feature image, grid, feature image.
- The Repair and Donation links at the bottom support the sustainability story.

## What's weak

- There is no price, discount or example saving, so the main reason to buy second-hand (value) is missing.
- There is no visible button or explicit CTA. Section labels like "The Gift Edit" are the only affordances.
- The product cutouts are small and unlabelled, with no condition, size or availability. Second-hand buying depends on that detail.
- There is no urgency or deadline, and no explanation of how Pre-Owned works.
- The footer is cluttered, with tiny legal text and a "Download our app" block.

- 2. Label the grid products with price and condition ("Like new · size M · €45") so the cutouts read as shoppable.
- 3. Add a short three-step explainer (sell, buy, repair) to remove the first-time hurdle.
- 4. Add a time hook, such as "Gift edit: order by 15 Dec for delivery".
- 5. Replace the generic section labels with verb-led CTAs, such as "Shop the coats".

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has real accessibility and markup defects (19 images without alt text, a zoom-blocking viewport tag, invalid `<div>` placement inside a `<table>`) and a 0-char plain-text part. The compliance and authentication warnings may be relay capture artifacts rather than sender faults, because the HTML is truncated and AgentMail may strip headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Incomplete link probing:** 12 material links were probed and 6 were skipped. The QA data shows no link failures in the probed set, so this is a coverage gap rather than a defect. The 6 unprobed links are unverified.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://news.zara.com/pub/as?_ri_=…` is a Responsys open-tracking image (`/pub/as`). It was flagged for missing alt text but is a 1x1 pixel, so the flag is benign.
- **Link destinations:** The truncated source does not show the CTA hrefs, so redirect chains and UTM presence can't be confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, 19 images:**
  - Campaign images: `img_01_en.jpg`, `img_02_en.jpg`, `img_03.jpg`, `img_04_en_US.jpg` through `img_08_en_US.jpg`, `img_09.jpg`.
  - Footer images: `latredes142.jpg` (twice), `download_en_b.jpg`, `rrss1b.jpg` through `rrss6b.jpg`.
  - Images-off clients get blank space, and screen readers get nothing.
  - The `rrss*` social icons are presumably wrapped in links. If so, those links have no accessible name.
  - The campaign images appear to carry the content, so the email is effectively unreadable with images blocked.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width; initial-scale=1.0; maximum-scale=1.0; user-scalable=no;">`
  - `user-scalable=no` and `maximum-scale=1.0` block pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
  - Semicolon separators are non-standard, since the spec uses commas.
- **Invalid markup:** `<div style="mso-hide:all;">` sits directly inside `<table width="640">` and wraps `<tr>` elements. A `<div>` is not a valid child of `<table>`, and clients may foster-parent it out of the table.
- **Outlook:** The `<!--[if !mso 9]><!-->` wrapper's closing `<!--<![endif]-->` isn't visible in the truncated source, so balance can't be verified.
- **Web font:** `fonts.googleapis.com` Roboto Condensed loads via `<link>`. Gmail and Outlook ignore it, and no fallback font stack is visible in the truncated source.
- **Link colour:** The global `a { color: #999898 }` is about 2.8:1 against `#ffffff`, below the 4.5:1 WCAG AA minimum. This applies to any link without an inline colour override.
- **Preheader:** The preheader is present ("New second-hand arrivals" plus `&#8199;&#847;` padding), so no issue there.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is 0 chars.** There is no `text/plain` alternative. That is a deliverability and accessibility risk, and some spam filters penalise HTML-only mail.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so this is unconfirmed.
- **[WARN] No physical address detected.** The footer appears to be a dynamic image block (`footers_dinamicos/…`), and the HTML is truncated. If the address is rendered inside an image, it is not machine-readable text and has no alt text. Unconfirmed.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- The sending domain `news.zara.com` is a subdomain of the brand, which is a good sign, but alignment can't be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The truncated source exposes no destination hrefs, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be assessed.
- The visible tracking mechanism is Responsys-style (`_ri_=` on the `/pub/as` pixel). This is likely redirect-based click tracking rather than UTM tagging.
- The campaign code `…preowned_producto_G4_17_11` is not confirmed to be passed to the site.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 19 images. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixel and decorative images, and use "Instagram", "Facebook" and so on for the `rrss*` icons.
2. Change the viewport tag to `width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0` and remove `maximum-scale` and `user-scalable=no`.
3. Add a `text/plain` alternative that includes the unsubscribe URL.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` on the raw sender-side message, since the relay may be dropping them. Also confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment from the raw headers.
5. Make sure the physical address and unsubscribe link exist as real text in the footer, not only inside `latredes142.jpg`.
6. Move the `<div style="mso-hide:all;">` inside a `<td>`, or replace it with `<tr style="mso-hide:all">`.
7. Strip internal comments (`PAISES_SKIP`, `PAISES_ENVIO`, `Copiar aquí`) in the build step.
8. Darken the default link colour from `#999898` to at least `#767676`.
9. Re-run link probing with the 6 skipped links included, and re-run QA against the full, untruncated HTML to settle the unconfirmed items in sections 5 and 6.
