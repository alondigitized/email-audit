# ZARA ORIGINS
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-10**
## Executive summary

- "ZARA ORIGINS" is a good menswear lookbook that doesn't try to sell anything. The black-and-white hero of five men gathered around coffee cups sets a mood. Below it come warm, earthy portraits: a long-haired model in an olive trench holding a mug, and an older man in a leather armchair. Then a 3×3 grid of full-length looks in browns, tans and charcoal. It reads as autumn tailoring for grown men, and it's a coherent campaign.
- As a commercial email it's thin. There's no offer, no price, and no hero product. The only real CTA is a small outlined "VIEW COLLECTION" button after about 3,500px of scroll. The category labels (SUITS, OUTERWEAR, TROUSERS, SHIRTS) are tiny underlined text under the images, so they're easy to miss. The subject gives no reason to open beyond the brand name.
- It's a notch better than the 10/9 "In Between Seasons" send because it's a single focused menswear story. It still follows the same Zara template: moody imagery, minimal copy, a buried CTA. Judgement: nice brand content, weak conversion mechanics.

## What's working

- The hero is striking. The grayscale group portrait with the "ZARA ORIGINS" wordmark is clear and cinematic.
- The palette is consistent: browns, olives, and creams look seasonally right for October.
- The campaign has a theme. The coffee-cup and armchair setups tie together as storytelling rather than a catalog dump.
- The 3×3 full-length grid shows real outfits, so you can judge fit and styling.
- Nothing is visibly broken. The images load cleanly and the layout is tidy.

## What's weak

- There's no offer, price, or urgency. Nothing tells you to act today.
- The category links (SUITS, OUTERWEAR, TROUSERS, SHIRTS) are tiny underlined labels that are easy to miss.
- The single CTA is a small, low-contrast outlined button at the very bottom. "VIEW COLLECTION" is generic and goes to a whole collection, not a product.
- The email is very long. The 3×3 grid has no captions, names, or links I can see, so each look is a dead end.
- It's a near-daily cadence after the 10/9 "Woman | In Between Seasons" send. A subscriber sees two lookbooks in two days.

## Recommendations

- 1. Link each look in the 3×3 grid to its product page, with a small name and price caption. This is the biggest lift, since the grid is the most shoppable part.
- 2. Make the CTA a filled, high-contrast button and move a copy of it right under the hero. Use specific copy like "Shop the Suits" or "Shop Outerwear".
- 3. Make the category labels real, larger buttons rather than 10px underlined text.
- 4. Lead the subject with the product and season, such as the tailoring and coats on show, instead of only the campaign name.
- 5. Add one concrete hook, such as "new in" or a hero coat, so the send has a reason to exist beyond mood.
- 6. Space out lookbook sends so they aren't back to back.
