# Home | The Retro Selection
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-11**
## Executive summary

- This is Zara Home's "The Retro Selection" — a pure lifestyle lookbook for mid-century furniture and decor (vases, chairs, mirrors, desk lamps, side tables) shot in warm wood-and-green tones. It's the Zara Home counterpart to the fashion verticals I've been seeing daily: zero commerce copy, no prices, no product names, just a long vertical scroll of styled still-life photography with a single "THE NEW" button buried two-thirds down and a "DISCOVER" button for Home Fragrances near the bottom.
- The photography is genuinely well-composed — moody, consistent, on-trend for the retro/mid-century revival — but it does almost nothing to convert. There's no way to know what any of these items are, what they cost, or how to buy the specific piece you like; you'd have to click through blind and hunt. It's a mood board wearing a marketing email's clothes.
- Judgement: pretty, on-brand, and completely commerce-agnostic — same structural complaint as every other Zara vertical audited this week, just with furniture instead of clothes.

## What's working

- Cohesive, high-production styling — warm wood tones, green tile accents, brass/chrome hardware repeat as a visual thread across all ~10 images.
- Clear category framing up top ("The Retro Selection ... Trends") tells the reader what aesthetic they're browsing.
- No render bugs — every image loads cleanly, text is crisp, no overlap or broken assets.
- Logical secondary offer at the bottom (Home Fragrances) gives a second entry point beyond furniture.

## What's weak

- Zero pricing or product names anywhere in the render — every item is anonymous.
- Only two CTA buttons ("THE NEW" and "DISCOVER") in a ~2000px-tall scroll — most images aren't clickable to anything specific.
- No hero offer, discount, or urgency signal at all — this reads as inspiration, not a sales email.
- Subject "Home | The Retro Selection" gives no concrete reason to open over the identical-pattern Kids/Men/Woman sends already seen this week (2026-08-09, 4.00/10; 2026-08-08, 4.00/10).
- Preview text is invisible/generic based on the render — no complementary hook to the subject.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add visible pricing and product names as overlays or captions under each image — right now nothing is shoppable without guessing.
- 2. Turn every image block into a real CTA, not just two buttons for the whole page — "Shop the Desk Lamp," "Shop the Console Table."
- 3. Front-load one hero product with an actual offer or "new arrival" framing above the fold so there's a reason to click before scrolling 2000px.
- 4. Shorten the scroll — 10+ full-bleed images with no copy is a lot of dead weight; cut to 4-5 curated hero shots.
