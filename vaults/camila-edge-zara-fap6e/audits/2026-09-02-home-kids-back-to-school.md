# Home Kids | Back To School
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-02**
## Executive summary

- This is Zara Home's Kids line running the identical playbook I've now flagged on 2026-08-26 ("Home Bedroom Collection"), 2026-08-23, and 2026-08-30's Kids send — hero shot, then an unbroken scroll of desk lamps, throw pillows, plaid rugs, a stuffed dinosaur, and lifestyle shots of kids in pajamas, with a single "VIEW" button at the very bottom. There isn't one price, one product name, or one piece of copy beyond "Home Kids / Back To School Collection" anywhere in this render. It's a mood board, not a marketing email.
- "Back to School" is a real, timely hook — a parent opening this in late August/September is primed to buy desk lamps, bedding, and study-corner furniture — but the email does nothing to capitalize on it. No "shop the study corner," no price anchor, no urgency. It's the same aesthetic-only Zara skeleton, just reskinned for kids' rooms this time. Judgement: another low-effort scroll from a brand that clearly has the assets and season-relevance to do much better.

## What's working

- The "Back To School" theme is genuinely seasonally relevant and well-matched to a Home Kids send in early September.
- Photography is cohesive and on-brand — warm, lived-in kid's-room styling that's consistent with prior Zara Home sends.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, layout holds together end to end, no overlapping text or broken assets.
- The dinosaur plush and clock product shots are genuinely cute standalone hero images that could anchor a stronger layout.

## What's weak

- Zero prices, zero product names, zero copy beyond the two-line header — pure lookbook, no reason to click any specific thing.
- Single CTA ("VIEW") sits after 15+ images of scroll, with no shoppable entry points along the way.
- No back-to-school specific offer (no bundle, no % off, no "get the desk sorted" framing) despite the subject promising exactly that angle.
- This is the fourth near-identical unbroken-scroll lookbook I've logged from Zara Home/Zara in the last two weeks — cadence fatigue is real.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add 3-5 shoppable price tags directly under key hero images (the desk lamp, the bed frame, the dinosaur plush) — this is the single highest-leverage fix since right now there's nothing to click toward.
- 2. Break the lookbook into 2-3 named sections ("Study Corner," "Bedtime," "Playroom") each with its own CTA button, instead of one 15-image scroll ending in a single generic "VIEW."
- 3. Lean into the back-to-school urgency explicitly — "Get the desk set before term starts" or a specific date-bound framing — since the subject line promises this angle but the body never delivers it.
- 4. Move the CTA up — repeat "Shop Home Kids" after the first 4-5 images, not just at the bottom.

- **Subject Alt B:** `The Study Corner Starts Here`
