The warm candlelit photography is cohesive: a tree by the fireplace, wreaths, a set dining table, a bedroom, a kitchen. ## What's working

- The photography is cohesive. The amber, candlelit palette reads as holiday without needing any copy.
- The hero is strong. Tree, fireplace and the oversized ZARA wordmark make the brand obvious at a glance.
- The layout varies well, alternating two-up portrait pairs with full-width landscape frames. That keeps the long scroll from feeling monotonous.
- The timing is right. A home holiday collection on 11-09 fits the gifting and decorating season.
- The ending is clean: a small CTA block, a candle closing image, and social and app links.

## What's weak

- There is no offer, price, product name or shipping or return info anywhere in the render.
- The CTA is a single thin outlined "VIEW" button, small and low contrast, at the very bottom of the scroll.
- None of the images shows a product label or caption, so a reader can't tell what is purchasable or where to click.
- The dark, similar-toned images blur together on a long scroll, with no focal point after the hero.
- "ZARA HOME  HOLIDAY COLLECTION" is small light text and a weak headline under such a large hero.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a headline with a concrete hook near the top, such as "Tableware from $__" or "Gift-ready candles", with a visible button.
- 2. Caption 3–4 of the images with product name and price, and link each to its product page.
- 3. Move the CTA up to right after the hero, and make it filled and higher contrast. Use specific copy like "Shop holiday tableware" instead of "View."
- 4. Add one practical element: a shipping-by-Christmas cutoff date or a gifting guide.
- 5. Break up the dark run of images with one lighter or text-led block.
