# Homepage snapshot · www.zara.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- I opened Zara and immediately hit a location gate — white screen, giant logo, two dropdowns, one `CONTINUE` button. That's it. No imagery, no campaign, no product, no reason to feel anything about this brand in this moment. Zara reads as cold, minimal, and weirdly bureaucratic right here, which is not the editorial drop energy I came for.

## What's working

- **The `CONTINUE` button** is the only actionable element and it's unambiguous — I know exactly what to tap to get past this screen.
- **"Remember selection" checkbox** is a small but real convenience signal — if I check it, I won't see this gate again.
- **Location already pre-filled to "United States"** — I didn't have to hunt for my country in a 200-option list, which is a low bar but appreciated.

## What's weak

- **The entire screen is a friction gate** — there is no homepage here, just an interstitial. For a returning shopper on mobile, this is a wall, not a welcome.
- **No editorial imagery whatsoever** — Zara's whole identity is moody campaign photography and I'm staring at a white form.
- **No bypass for logged-in users** — if I already have an account and location saved, I should never see this. The fact that I do means cookie/session state isn't being leveraged.
- **"DO NOT SELL OR SHARE MY PERSONAL INFORMATION" footer** is literally the only other text on the page — a legal notice is doing the emotional work of a homepage.
- **Zero campaign signal** — I have no idea what Zara is pushing right now. No SRPLS tease, no Studio drop, nothing.

## Recommendations

- **Auto-redirect returning users** past the location gate using stored cookies or geolocation — I should never see this if I've been here before.
- **Add a campaign image behind or above the gate form** — even a blurred editorial background would keep the brand alive while I click through.
- **Surface a one-line editorial teaser** ("New SRPLS. Limited.") so the gate doubles as anticipation-building, not just admin.
- **Use geolocation to pre-confirm and collapse the gate** — show "Shopping from United States? `Continue →`" in a single dismissible banner rather than a full-screen form.
