## What's weak

- **No hook, no concept** — compare this to the June 11 "THE FOLD-ER" (four named women, a point of view) or the Pukas surf collab. This email has no editorial throughline — it's a product dump, not a story.

- **No offer or incentive whatsoever** — pure browse email in an era where inboxes compete for attention. There's nothing to act on now vs. tomorrow.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[HIGH] Viewport meta uses semicolons as delimiters — syntax error:**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width; initial-scale=1.0; maximum-scale=1.0; user-scalable=no;" />
```
The `content` attribute requires comma-separated tokens, not semicolons. iOS Safari and some Android browsers may silently ignore the entire directive, falling back to desktop rendering on mobile.

**[HIGH] Zoom disabled — WCAG 2.1 SC 1.4.4 violation:**
`maximum-scale=1.0; user-scalable=no` both present. Combined, they prevent pinch-to-zoom entirely. This fails WCAG 2.1 Success Criterion 1.4.4 (Resize Text) and is flagged by accessibility auditors on iOS 10+.

**[MEDIUM] External Google Fonts `<link>` will be stripped or ignored by major clients:**

Gmail strips `<link>` tags entirely. Outlook (all versions) does not support web fonts. The declared `cyrillic,cyrillic-ext` subset is irrelevant for a `US_en` send. Verify that system-font fallbacks are visually acceptable as the actual rendered state for the majority of recipients.
