# Homepage snapshot · www.zara.com · 2026-07-01
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-01**
## Executive summary

- This isn't the homepage — it's a location/language gate, and it's the entire viewport. I didn't land on any product content at all; I'm stuck on a country selector before Zara will even let me in. The brand is presenting itself as a locked door before anything else, which is a rough first impression no matter how clean the typography looks.

## What's working

- **Location pre-selected to "United States"** — at least I don't have to hunt through a dropdown to find my country. Saves one tap.
- **"Remember selection" checkbox** — the intent is good; if it worked cleanly it would mean I never see this gate again.
- **Minimal layout** — the page is uncluttered and won't cause confusion about what to do next. Press `CONTINUE` and move on.

## What's weak

- **The gate itself** — I'm a returning Zara shopper. I've been to this site before. Why am I being asked to select my location again? This is friction before any value has been delivered.
- **Zero product content above the fold** — no campaign, no imagery, no season. I have no idea if there's a summer sale, new SRPLS drop, or anything worth staying for.
- **"Remember selection" checkbox renders with a ■ icon** — looks like a broken or unstyled checkbox on mobile. Small but janky on a brand that leans hard into precision aesthetics.
- **"DO NOT SELL OR SHARE MY PERSONAL INFORMATION" footer** — the only secondary copy on the entire page is a legal opt-out link. It's the last thing my eye catches and it's a mood killer.
- **No skip or "continue as guest" shortcut** — the only path forward requires interacting with the gate.

## Recommendations

- **Detect location via IP and skip the gate entirely** for returning visitors — drop them directly onto the homepage. Reserve the selector for edge cases (VPN, first visit, explicit region change).
- **Replace the ■ checkbox with a proper mobile-rendered checkbox** — the current one looks like a broken render artifact and undermines Zara's premium feel.
- **If the gate is non-negotiable, add a teaser image or headline below** — even a single campaign image peeking above the fold would give me a reason to push through.
- **Surface a seasonal hook in the gate itself** — one line like "New Summer Studio Collection — Select your region to shop" costs nothing and creates pull instead of bureaucracy.
