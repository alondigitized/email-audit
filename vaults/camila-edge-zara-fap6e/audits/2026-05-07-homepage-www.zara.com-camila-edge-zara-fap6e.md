# Homepage snapshot · www.zara.com · 2026-05-07
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- What I got was not a homepage — it's a country/language selector gate before I can even see Zara's actual site. The entire above-the-fold is the ZARA logo, two dropdown fields, a "CONTINUE" button, and a privacy footer link. There is zero product, zero editorial, zero campaign — just a speed bump between me and whatever collection I came to browse.

## What's working

- **Clean render, no visual bugs** — the form itself is crisp; dropdowns are legible, button has clear contrast, no layout breakage on mobile viewport.
- **"Remember selection" checkbox** — at least the intent is there to reduce repeat friction on future visits, even if it doesn't help me right now.
- **"CONTINUE" button is unambiguous** — I know exactly what it does, even if I resent having to tap it.

## What's weak

- **The entire first screen is a gate, not a homepage** — I came to browse drops and I hit a form. That friction costs them me immediately when I'm in a casual scroll mindset.
- **No personalization memory surfacing** — I've almost certainly visited Zara.com before. My region should be auto-detected or cached. Showing me the selector every time is a known failure.
- **Zero campaign signal** — not a single image, copy line, or product hint. Zara could have loaded a hero behind a lightweight modal overlay; they chose a blank white page instead.
- **Privacy footer as the only secondary content** — "DO NOT SELL OR SHARE MY PERSONAL INFORMATION" is the only other text on screen. Cold open for a fashion brand.
- **No loyalty / member recognition** — if I'm signed in or have cookies, none of that is leveraged to skip or pre-fill this step.

## Recommendations

- **Auto-detect and skip the gate for repeat visitors** — use geolocation + a cookie so returning users land directly on the localized homepage. One tap saved = real retention uplift.
- **Overlay the selector on top of the actual homepage** — load the editorial hero and campaign imagery behind a modal; let me see what I'm walking into before the gate closes on me.
- **Surface the current season/collection in the background** — even a blurred Studio or SRPLS hero would telegraph brand energy and keep me warm while I confirm my location.
- **Add a "Stay on US / English" quick-confirm** for users whose browser locale already matches — skip the full form entirely and show a single dismiss banner instead.
