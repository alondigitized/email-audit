## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **[FAIL] Plain-text fallback is 0 chars.** The email has no usable `text/plain` part. This hurts deliverability scoring and affects text-only clients and some assistive setups.
- **Missing alt text on 10 content images:** `img_01.jpg`, `img_02.jpg`, `img_03A.jpg`, `img_03B.jpg`, `img_04.jpg`, `img_05A.jpg`, `img_05B.jpg`, `img_06A.jpg`, `img_06B.jpg` and `img_07.jpg`. All are under `https://news.zara.com/assets/responsysimages/industria007/contentlibrary/campaigns/2026/10.octubre/2026_10_18_norte_kids…`. If these images carry the product and CTA content, then with images blocked or in a screen reader the email has no readable content. The `.imagemobile` class styles alt text (`font-size:16px; text-align:center`), which suggests alt text was intended but is not populated.
- **Viewport meta:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width; initial-scale=1.0; maximum-scale=1.0; user-scalable=no;">`.
  - `user-scalable=no` and `maximum-scale=1.0` block pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
  - The semicolon separators are non-standard. Comma separators are the spec.
- **Web font:** The Google Fonts `<link>` for Roboto Condensed is ignored by Gmail and most clients. The `.font*` classes should fall back to Helvetica/Arial.
