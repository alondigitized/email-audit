# Kids | Once upon a party
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- This is Zara Kids' party-season lookbook. It is a very good-looking mood piece and a weak sales email. A black canvas carries a confetti-covered kid in a sequin dress, a "Once upon a party" serif wordmark and an Autumn/Winter 2026 tag. Below that is a run of black-and-white and warm-toned editorial shots: tuxedo-style tailoring, bow ties, a tiara, velvet blazers, a baby in a mini suit.
- The photography is cohesive and the party theme fits mid-November. But there is no price, no offer, no product name and no button. The only text-based action is a small "Download our app" line at the bottom. A parent who loves the look has no obvious next step.
- Like the last six Zara sends (11-15 "This week's arrivals", 11-14 "Trending now: Party", 11-08 "The Holiday Fun Capsule"), it reads as a catalog scroll rather than a campaign. It is a 5/10, pretty but not built to convert.

## What's working

- The confetti hero against black is the strongest frame. It is festive and instantly says "party".
- Age labels under each image ("GIRL / 6–14 YEARS", "BOY / 1½–6 YEARS", "BABY / 0–18 MONTHS") quietly handle sizing and segmentation.
- The black layout is clean and consistent, with no broken images or overlapping text.
- Mixing monochrome portraits with the warm library scene gives the scroll rhythm and range across ages.
- The subject and the creative agree. "Once upon a party" matches the wordmark and the formalwear styling.

## What's weak

- There is no offer, price, product name or CTA button anywhere. Every image is a dead end.
- The only visible action is "Download our app" at the bottom, which has nothing to do with the party looks.
- The top bar says "NEWSLETTER. ONLINE VERSION", which is filler, not preview copy.
- Image-only content with no readable product detail makes it hard to tell what to buy. That is a problem for any inbox that blocks images.
- The send repeats the party angle from the 11-14 "Trending now: Party" email, so the cadence feels repetitive.

- 1. Add a clear CTA with a verb and a noun, such as "Shop kids' party looks". Put it under the hero and again at the bottom.
- 2. Name two or three hero pieces, for example the sequin dress and the velvet blazer, with prices or "from $X".
- 3. Link each tile to its product or category page, and label the tiles with the item name as well as the age range.
- 4. Replace the bottom "Download our app" block with a party-edit CTA, and keep the app promo as a smaller secondary link.
- 5. Tie the send to a real moment ("Holiday party dressing, ships before the 25th") for credible urgency.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has a 38% QA pass rate. The one hard failure is the missing plain-text part (0 chars). Other findings are missing alt text on every image (16 flagged), an invalid, zoom-blocking viewport meta tag, and compliance items that can't be confirmed because headers and the footer weren't captured. ## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 12 material links were probed and 4 were skipped (deprioritized as footer, utility or social). No broken or redirecting links were reported, so the 4 skipped links are unverified.
- The open-tracking pixel is served first-party from `https://news.zara.com/pub/as?_ri_=…` (Responsys). It has no `alt` attribute and was flagged as "as". It should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`.
- The footer image `latredes142_black.jpg` appears twice (two QA warnings), so it is duplicated in the footer.
- Per-link tracking parameters couldn't be assessed, because the `href` values fall in the truncated portion of the source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 16 images:**
  - Hero images `img_01.jpg` and `img_02_en.jpg` through `img_06_en.jpg` (all under `/2026_11_16_Norte_ki…`).
  - Footer images `latredes142_black.jpg` (×2), `rrss1b_black.jpg` through `rrss6b_black.jpg`, and `download_b_en.jpg`.
  - With images off or in a screen reader, the body and the social and app-download links have no accessible text.
- **Invalid viewport meta:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width; initial-scale=1.0; maximum-scale=1.0; user-scalable=no;">`.
  - Semicolons are used as separators where the spec calls for commas.
  - `user-scalable=no` and `maximum-scale=1.0` block pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Invalid markup:** `<div style="mso-hide:all;">` is a direct child of `<table>`, wrapping `<tr>`. A `div` is not allowed there, and parsers may hoist it out of the table.
- **Link contrast:** the global rule `a { color: #999898; }` gives about 2.8:1 contrast on the `#ffffff` background, below the WCAG AA 4.5:1 minimum. This applies unless inline styles override it, which I couldn't see.
- **Other:**
  - The Google Fonts `<link>` in `<head>` is unsupported in many clients and falls back silently.
  - `<title>` is the generic "Zara".

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens were visible in the captured HTML. The source is truncated, so tokens further down can't be ruled out.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] Plain-text fallback:** the text version is 0 chars. This hurts deliverability and accessibility, and the email has no plain-text alternative.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found:** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. If the headers are really absent, one-click unsubscribe is unsupported, and Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **[WARN] No physical address detected:** the footer is built from dynamic image blocks (`footers_dinamicos/…`). The address may be inside an image, which isn't machine-readable, or in the truncated source. It needs manual verification.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because of the relay. The From domain `news.zara.com` is aligned with the asset and tracking host.
- An unsubscribe link was reported as always probed, and no failure was reported for it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. UTM parameters and destination URLs aren't in the visible source. The only campaign identifiers are HTML comments, `CAMPAIGN_CODE:zara;2026_11_16_Norte_kids_party_16_11;US_en` and `…;en`. These two comments carry different locale values.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a real `text/plain` part (currently 0 chars). This is the only hard failure.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 6 body images and the footer images, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
3. Fix the viewport meta to `width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0`, and remove `user-scalable=no` and `maximum-scale=1.0`.
4. Fix the invalid `<div style="mso-hide:all;">` inside the `<table>`. Use `<tr style="mso-hide:all;">` or conditional comments.
5. Darken the `#999898` link color to at least 4.5:1 contrast, for example `#6b6b6b`.
6. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` on the original send, not through the relay. Confirm that the physical address is present as text in the footer.
7. Remove template leftovers from the production HTML: the Spanish dev comments ("Copiar aquí", "Recogemos los países…") and the duplicate, conflicting `CAMPAIGN_CODE` comments.
8. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check UTM parameters, merge tokens and the 4 unprobed links.
