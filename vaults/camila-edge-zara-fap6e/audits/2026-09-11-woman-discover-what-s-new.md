# Woman | Discover what’s new

## Executive summary

- This is the exact same skeleton I flagged on 2026-09-04 for this identical subject line — hero editorial shot, then an unbroken vertical scroll through ZW Collection → New Arrivals → TRF Collection → T-Shirts Edit → Selected Pieces → gift card promo, all with zero prose connecting any of it. It's the fourth near-identical "Woman | Discover what's new" style send this week counting Kids, Man, and the co-branded collaborations, and there is nothing in this one that differentiates it from the prior lookbook grinds.
- There's no discount, no price, no urgency, no personalization — just eight category tiles and a gift-card teaser at the bottom. It functions as a lookbook, not a marketing email, and it reads that way: pretty, static, and impossible to act on without already knowing what you want.
- Judgement: this is a low-effort repeat of a template Zara has now run at least five times this month with only the category labels swapped. Skip.

## What's working

- Clean, consistent editorial photography with no render bugs, broken images, or overlapping text.
- Category labels (KNITWEAR, TRENCH COATS, SKIRTS, CO-ORD SETS, etc.) are clear and legible under each tile.
- Two-column grid layout is tidy and scans well on mobile.
- The "A SPECIAL GIFT" gift-card block at the bottom adds a small point of novelty versus a pure lookbook.

## What's weak

- Zero offer, price, or discount anywhere in the email — nothing to click toward beyond "browse a category."
- No hero copy or subject differentiation from the 2026-09-04 send with the identical subject line "Woman | Discover what's new."
- No CTA button at all — just underlined text links under each image, all visually identical in weight.
- No social proof, sizing/fit info, or urgency anywhere in the body.
- The gift card block is confusing on first glance — it's not clear if it's a purchasable product or a promotional freebie.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the flat category dump with one hero offer above the fold — a specific new-in item, a bestseller, or even a "shop the edit" curated capsule — so there's a reason to open beyond "see everything we sell."
- 2. Add a single clear CTA button (not just underlined text) tied to the top hero image so there's an obvious first action.
- 3. Vary subject/creative from the 2026-09-04 send — this is a repeat, and subscribers who got both will notice.
- 4. Introduce any timeliness signal — new season framing, a limited restock note, anything beyond "new arrivals."
