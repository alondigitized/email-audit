## What's working

- The cream and earth-tone palette is cohesive, and the studio grid of tall full-length shots makes the outfits easy to compare.
- The oversized cropped ZARA wordmark hero is on-brand and instantly recognizable.
- The bold black sans headlines ("Zara Origins," "The Party Edit," "Zara Athleticz Speed Run") give clear section breaks without clutter.
- The menswear focus is consistent, so the email fits a male subscriber.
- The footer is clean, with the app download prompt and social links.

## What's weak

- No offer, price, or product name anywhere, so there is nothing concrete to act on.
- The CTAs are tiny "VIEW" text links and a small "Discover the collection" label floating in the gap between the images. Neither reads as a button.
- "This week's arrivals" isn't reflected in the body. Nothing is marked new, and the sections read as campaign names ("Origins," "Athleticz").
- The Party Edit is a minor module squeezed between outerwear and running gear. That repeats the unfocused structure from the 11-14 "Trending now: Party" send.
- The white gap in the second studio row and the large empty space beside the "Zara Origins" image make the layout feel unfinished.

- 2. Cut the module count from about six to three, and label what is actually new.
- 3. Replace the "VIEW" links with full-width button CTAs, and link each studio shot to its product page.
- 4. Fix the white gaps in the grid by filling them with a product or a caption.
- 5. Add one reason to act now, such as limited stock or a size-run warning, since there is no sale.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email passes only 38% of automated QA. Its main defects are a missing plain-text part (0 chars), 18 images without `alt`, and a viewport meta that disables zoom. The unsubscribe and authentication warnings may be relay capture gaps, so they need verification against the raw message.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken or redirecting links were reported among the 12 probed material links.
- 19 of 31 links were skipped (footer, utility and social links). Their health is unverified.
- Links and the open pixel go through Responsys redirects, e.g. `https://news.zara.com/pub/as?_ri_=...`. This is expected for the ESP. Final destinations and UTM parameters can't be checked from the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on 18 images.** `img_01.jpg` through `img_12.jpg`, `img_13A.jpg`, `img_13B.jpg`, `img_14.jpg`, `img_15.png` and `img_16.jpg` all lack `alt`. They are served from `https://news.zara.com/assets/responsysimages/industria007/contentlibrary/campaigns/2026/11.noviembre/2026_11_15_Norte_ma…`. With images blocked, screen readers and image-off clients get no content. If these are the linked product tiles, the links will also have no accessible name.
- **The tracking pixel** (`/pub/as`) has no `alt`. It should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Zoom is disabled and the viewport meta is malformed.** The tag is `content="width=device-width; initial-scale=1.0; maximum-scale=1.0; user-scalable=no;"`. `user-scalable=no` and `maximum-scale=1.0` block pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4. Semicolons are also non-standard separators, since the spec uses commas.
- **Invalid CSS.** `.heightmobile { line-height: auto!important; }` is not a valid value, so the declaration is dropped.
- **External web font.** `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Roboto+Condensed...">` is stripped by Gmail and Outlook. Rendering there relies on the fallback stack, and the only fallback visible in the truncated source is the Outlook-only `HelveticaNeue, Helvetica, Arial` rule.
- **Internal comments left in the production HTML.** Examples are `CAMPAIGN_CODE:zara;2026_11_15_Norte_man_weekly_15_11;US_en` and Spanish notes about `PAISES_SKIP` / `PAISES_ENVIO` Data Source logic. This is a minor information leak and adds bytes.
- The HTML is truncated, so findings beyond the `<head>` and opening `<body>` are limited to the QA output.
