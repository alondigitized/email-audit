# Homepage snapshot · www.zara.com · 2026-06-10
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-10**
## Executive summary

- What I'm looking at isn't a homepage — it's a location gate. Zara caught me at the door and asked me to confirm I'm in the United States before I can see a single product, campaign, or editorial image. For a brand that lives and dies on editorial cool-factor and drops culture, this is a jarring cold open. It tells me nothing about what's new this season and gives me zero reason to be excited.

## What's working

- **Clean render:** The gate itself loads without jank — logo is crisp, dropdowns are legible, CONTINUE button has clear contrast. No broken assets.
- **"Remember selection" checkbox:** Low-friction UX detail. One tap and I won't see this again. That's the one small win here.
- **Logo presence:** The Zara wordmark is instantly recognizable and confident — no confusion about what brand I'm on.

## What's weak

- **Zero editorial content above the gate:** I came here to see what dropped. Instead I get a form. For a brand that does SRPLS and Studio collections, this is a missed moment to show me *why* I should bother clicking through.
- **No campaign signal whatsoever:** Is it a summer drop? A Studio collection launch? A sale? I have no idea. This screen tells me nothing about the current moment.
- **No offer or hook to incentivize continuing:** If I'm a fence-sitter, there's nothing here pulling me through the gate — no "new arrivals just landed" teaser, no promo strip.
- **Forced interstitial on mobile feels especially punishing:** I'm on my phone, probably on a lunch break. This extra tap before I see anything is friction I didn't ask for, and it signals the site doesn't remember me.
- **"DO NOT SELL OR SHARE MY PERSONAL INFORMATION" at the bottom:** The only other copy on the screen is a legal footer. That's the vibe we're setting? Cold and compliance-forward is not a great entrance.

## Recommendations

- **Bleed editorial imagery behind the gate:** Show a blurred or partially revealed hero image of the current campaign behind the selector overlay. Give me a taste of what's waiting. Makes the CONTINUE tap feel rewarding, not obligatory.
- **Add a one-line teaser strip above or below the form:** Something like "NEW IN: SRPLS SS26 — just landed" costs nothing and immediately signals that there's a reason to proceed.
- **Store my location from my first visit and skip this gate entirely on return:** I'm high-tech-comfort and I've been here before. Recognizing returning visitors and bypassing the gate would be a simple win that respects my time.
- **Swap the bottom footer copy for a trust/returns line:** "Free returns in store" or "New drops every week" reframes the mood from legal-defensive to brand-forward.
