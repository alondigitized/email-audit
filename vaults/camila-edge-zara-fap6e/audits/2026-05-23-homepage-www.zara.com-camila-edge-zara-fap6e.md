# Homepage snapshot · www.zara.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- I didn't get a homepage — I got a gate. Zara hit me with a country/language selector before showing me a single product, campaign, or collection. For a brand I already know and shop, this is friction before the first impression. It signals they either don't remember me or actively don't care to.

## What's working

- **The logo** is bold, high-contrast, and unmistakably Zara — at least I know where I am.
- **"Remember selection" checkbox** is a small thoughtful touch — if I check it, I won't see this screen again. That's the one concession to repeat visitors.
- **"United States / English" pre-filled** — so the browser geolocation worked, which means I don't have to hunt for my country in a dropdown. Marginally reduces friction.

## What's weak

- **Zero brand content above the fold** — no image, no campaign, no collection, no product. I opened Zara and got a form.
- **"CONTINUE" is the primary CTA** — it's not shopping, it's tollbooth. The copy does nothing to build excitement or pull me in.
- **No personalization signal** — I've bought from Zara before. There's no cookie-based recognition, no "welcome back," nothing that treats me as a known customer.
- **Half the screen is empty white space** below the form — looks broken or unfinished on mobile.
- **"DO NOT SELL OR SHARE MY PERSONAL INFORMATION"** crammed at the very bottom in tiny caps — the only legal/trust copy on the entire screen, and it's placed like an afterthought.
- **No seasonal or campaign context** — I don't know if there's a new drop, a Memorial Day offer, or anything worth clicking through for.

## Recommendations

- **Auto-detect and skip the gate for returning visitors** — if my browser has a prior session or geolocation resolves cleanly to the US, drop me straight onto the homepage. The friction of this screen is entirely avoidable for repeat shoppers.
- **Inject a campaign teaser behind the form** — even a blurred or dimmed hero image in the background would communicate the current drop and give me a reason to hit "CONTINUE" faster.
- **Rename the CTA** — "CONTINUE" is utilitarian. "SHOP ZARA US" or even "ENTER" carries more energy and tells me what I'm walking into.
- **Add a small new-arrival or seasonal callout** below the form — one line like "New Studio collection now live" costs nothing and gives me context while I wait.
