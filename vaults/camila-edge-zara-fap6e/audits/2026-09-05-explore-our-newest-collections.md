# Explore our newest collections
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- This is Zara Man running the exact same lookbook skeleton I've now flagged across Woman, Kids, and Home sends throughout August — hero editorial shot, then an unbroken vertical scroll of category blocks (Puffer Jacket → Street Fits → Fragrance → Selected Pieces) with zero connective copy, no pricing, and no single clear CTA. The puffer jacket hero is genuinely striking — moody, glossy, well-lit — but it's the only thing in the entire email doing any persuasive work.
- At least this is a fresh angle for the men's category rather than another repeat of the same Woman/Kids grind, and the fragrance cross-sell block is a mildly interesting addition I haven't seen in Zara's other recent sends. But it's still a scroll of five unlinked lookbooks stitched into one email with a subject line ("Explore our newest collections") so generic it could be reused indefinitely — which, per the brand history, it apparently is close to being (echoes the 2026-08-21 "Explore our newest collections" and 2026-08-28 "This week's selection" sends). Judgment: a nicely shot, poorly structured catalog dump — not an email built to convert on this send, just brand-awareness filler.

## What's working

- Hero puffer jacket shot is high-production, moody, and on-trend — best single visual asset in this email.
- Clear section labels (THE GLOSSY PUFFER JACKET, STREET FITS, FRAGRANCE COLLECTION, SELECTED PIECES) make scanning easy even without copy.
- Fragrance block is a genuine cross-sell beat not present in Zara's recent Woman/Kids/Home sends.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, layout is consistent, text is legible throughout.

## What's weak

- No offer of any kind — no price, no %-off, no free shipping, nothing to click for beyond "browse the category."
- Subject "Explore our newest collections" is nearly identical to prior sends and carries zero urgency or specificity.
- Six straight category blocks with only one-word links (JACKETS, SHIRTS, TROUSERS, PERFUMES, KNITWEAR, BLAZERS, JEANS) — no product names, no prices, no reason to click one over another.
- No preview/preheader text visible — inbox will likely show boilerplate or nothing, wasting free real estate next to the subject.
- No urgency, no personalization, no loyalty tie-in anywhere in the email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the block count from six down to two or three and add one line of copy per block naming the actual product and price — right now nothing in the body differentiates from browsing the site's category nav.
- 2. Add a real preheader that teases the puffer jacket specifically, since it's the strongest visual asset and currently gets no supporting copy at all.
- 3. Introduce a concrete hook — new-drop framing, a bundle idea (jacket + trouser fit), or at minimum "new in" badges — anything beyond a flat lookbook grid.
- 4. Vary the subject line away from the "Explore our newest collections" template that's already been used; name the hero product instead.
- **Subject Alt A:** `The glossy puffer just dropped`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New in: puffers, knits & fragrance`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shine-finish outerwear leads this week's Man drop.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Street fits, new scents, and the puffer everyone's asking about.`
- 5. Move fragrance and "Selected Pieces" further down or into a separate send — they dilute the puffer jacket's spotlight by competing for attention in the same undifferentiated format.
