# Kids | Explore our newest collections
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the Kids-line version of Zara's now-familiar editorial skeleton — hero lifestyle shot, then a stack of lookbook blocks (Athleisure, Julius and the Country House, Daddy & Oli, Mickey Collection), zero prices, zero CTAs, and a footer of app/social links. It's the same "This is the same X skeleton" pattern flagged in every Zara vertical this month (Woman 08-21, Man 08-22, Kids 08-16), just re-skinned with children and a Mickey Mouse tie-in. There's no offer, no button, and no reason to click beyond "browse a lookbook."
- The Mickey Collection block is the only differentiator versus prior Kids sends, but it's buried at the very bottom after four other segments, so most scrollers never reach it. Subject "Kids | Explore our newest collections" is generic and matches the brand's repeated low-effort naming pattern (same phrasing as the 08-15 "Kids | The latest arrivals" send). Judgment: another pure-browse newsletter with no commercial urgency — fine as brand upkeep, weak as a performance email.

## What's working

- Hero image is clean, well-lit, on-brand editorial photography with clear age-segment labeling (Girl 6-14, Boy 6-14, etc.) under each block.
- No visible render bugs — images load, text doesn't overlap, layout is consistent top to bottom.
- Age/size segmentation (Baby 0-18 months, Girl 1½-6 years, etc.) is a genuinely useful signal for a parent shopper.
- Mickey Collection tie-in adds a recognizable, seasonal-feeling hook not present in prior Kids sends.

## What's weak

- No price, discount, or offer anywhere in the email — nothing time-bound or actionable.
- No CTA buttons at all; the entire email relies on clicking product images, which is low-intent friction.
- Subject line is near-identical boilerplate to the brand's own 08-15 Kids send ("The latest arrivals" vs. "newest collections") — pure cadence fatigue.
- Five separate lookbook sections with no hierarchy — nothing tells the eye where to look first or what's actually new/on-sale.
- Preview text is not visible/distinct from the "Newsletter, online version" header — no real preheader copy doing work.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add at least one concrete offer or "shop now" CTA button under each section — right now this is a lookbook, not a marketing email with a job to do.
- 2. Move Mickey Collection (the one novel, licensed-IP hook) to the top of the email instead of the bottom — it's the most clickable asset here and it's buried.
- 3. Differentiate the subject line from prior Kids sends instead of reusing "Kids | [adjective] collections" — call out the Mickey tie-in by name.
- 4. Add real preheader copy that references a specific product or the Mickey collection rather than leaving it blank/generic.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Kids | Mickey Mouse has landed`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New school-year looks for kids, 0-14`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Disney's Mickey, back-to-school basics, and family matching sets`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `From baby rompers to teen athleisure — shop the new kids' drop`
