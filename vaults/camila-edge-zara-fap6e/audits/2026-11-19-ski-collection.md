# Ski Collection
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- This is Zara's Ski Collection lookbook, and it's the most seasonally on-point send in the recent run. The hero is a model in an olive floral-print ski jacket against snowy peaks, with a huge "ZARA SKI COLLECTION" wordmark overlaid. Below it come Woman, Man, Kids and Baby/Girl/Boy modules. The photography is crisp and the alpine setting is cohesive. The red kids' deckchair shot is the best frame in the scroll.
- As a sales email it does almost nothing. There is no price, no offer, no product name, no member benefit and no visible button. The only text labels are small category captions like "WOMAN COLLECTION" and "MAN COLLECTION". The email is also very long, about 4,500px of nearly all image. Like the 11-17 "ZARA PRE-OWNED" and 11-16 "Kids | Once upon a party" sends, it's a mood piece that relies on the reader to find the way in.
- Judgement: well made and timely, but a catalog with no commercial hook. It earns a middling score, the same band as the rest of Zara's recent sends.

## What's working

- The hero is strong. The wordmark overlay sits on the model and mountains and reads clearly as "Ski Collection".
- The send fits the season. Ski gear lands in mid-November, unlike a stale promo.
- The segmented layout (Woman, Man, Kids, Girl/Boy/Baby) lets a reader find their own category quickly.
- The photography is consistent. The same alpine light and colour palette run through every module.
- The footer is tidy, with app download and social links.

## What's weak

- No offer, price or urgency anywhere. Nothing tells the reader why to act now.
- No visible CTA buttons. The captions like "WOMAN COLLECTION" are tiny and look like labels, not tappable links.
- No product detail. There are no names, prices, sizes or availability, so the reader can't tell which garment they're seeing.
- It's very long and image-heavy. If images are blocked, the email is blank. Several modules repeat the same lifestyle shot with no hierarchy.
- It's the sixth near-identical lookbook-style send in about ten days (SRPLS, Party, arrivals, Kids, Pre-Owned). The cadence feels samey.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a clear CTA under each module, with button copy like "Shop women's ski" and "Shop kids' snow". Make them real buttons, not tiny captions.
- 2. Put prices or "from $X" and 2-3 named hero products in the hero module, so the reader can see what's being sold.
- 3. Cut the length. Keep the hero plus 3 modules (Woman, Man, Kids) and drop the repeated Girl/Boy/Baby strip, or move it behind a link.
- 4. Add a reason to act now, such as "Ready to ship before your trip" or a size-availability note.
- 5. Break up the Zara lookbook cadence with at least one send a week that has a product or price anchor.

- **Missing `alt` on 12 content images:** `img_01b.jpg`, `img_02Ac.jpg`, `img_02Bc.jpg`, `img_03.jpg`, `img_04A.jpg`, `img_04B.jpg`, `img_05.jpg` through `img_10.jpg`. If these are the primary content, the email is unusable without images.
- **Tracking pixel `as` has no `alt`:** `https://news.zara.com/pub/as?_ri_=...`. This is lower severity, but it should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width; initial-scale=1.0; maximum-scale=1.0; user-scalable=no;">` fails WCAG 1.4.4 (resize text). It also uses semicolons instead of commas, which is non-standard.
