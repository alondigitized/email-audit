# Trending now: Party
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- This is another Zara image-first lookbook, and the subject "Trending now: Party" promises more focus than the body delivers. Party is only one module of about six. The scroll opens on "The Projects AW26" and "Villa Borsani". "Party Story" doesn't appear until about a third of the way down. After that the email moves to leather accessories, "The Item" satin pyjama trousers, and a Zara Athleticz "Speed Run" block. It reads as a weekly catalog with a party label on it.
- The photography is strong and the typography is consistent. The oversized stacked headlines (THE PRO JECTS, VILLA BORSANI, PARTY STORY, THE LEA THER EDIT) give the layout rhythm. The black jumpsuit, halter trouser and green dress row under "Party Story" is the clearest commercial moment. But there is no price, no offer, no deadline and no button anywhere. Every CTA is a tiny "VIEW" or a category label. Ten days before Black Friday, the email asks the reader to do all the work.
- The verdict is on brand and well shot, but low on conversion. It is the same format as the 11-06 "Woman | Discover what's new" send, which also centered on a party edit. That repetition makes it feel redundant.

## What's working

- The photography is cohesive and editorial, with a black, cream and emerald palette that fits the party season.
- The oversized stacked type gives each module a clear entry point and a distinct identity.
- The Party Story trio of jumpsuit, trousers and dress has clear sub-labels, so the reader can shop by garment type.
- "The Item | The Satin Pyjama Trousers" spotlights one hero product with a lifestyle shot and a flat product shot side by side.
- The page is easy to scan. Alternating left and right image layouts keep it from feeling monotonous.

## What's weak

- Party is only one section of the email, so the subject over-promises.
- The "VIEW" links are tiny, and the labels under the images (TOPS, PARTY, SHOES, BAGS) are barely legible at inbox scale. There is no real button.
- There is no price, offer, member benefit or urgency, even though Black Friday is close.
- "THE ITEM" appears twice in the banner, and the second copy is cropped and fades out at the right edge. It looks like a layout glitch.
- The mix of AW26 projects, Villa Borsani, leather, athleticz and Speed Run dilutes the message and dilutes the party theme.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the email match its subject. Lead with the Party Story, show 6–8 products, and drop the Athleticz and Projects blocks.
- 2. Add a real button, such as "Shop party dresses", and put it in the first screen.
- 3. Add something concrete: a starting price, a "new in" tag, or a holiday delivery cutoff.
- 4. Fix the cropped "THE ITEM" banner so it shows once and cleanly.
- 5. Enlarge the category labels so they work as tap targets on mobile.

# Technical Audit: Zara "Trending now: Party" (2026-11-14, campaign `zara;2026_11_14_Norte_woman_weekly_14_11;US_en`)

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Responsys-sent XHTML table layout with a 38% QA pass rate. The one hard failure is a missing plain-text part (0 chars). Alt text is missing on 16 content images and the tracking pixel. The compliance and authentication checks could not be verified from the captured data. The HTML source was truncated, so footer and link-level findings are limited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- QA probed 12 of 43 links and skipped 31 as footer, utility or social links. No broken-link failures were reported for the 12 probed, but the other 31 are unverified.
- Click tracking uses Responsys redirects (`https://news.zara.com/pub/as?_ri_=...`). This is expected for the platform, and the redirect chain and final destinations were not captured.
- No other issues found.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (WARN):** 16 images lack `alt`: `img_01.jpg` through `img_13.jpg`, `img_14A.jpg`, `img_14B.jpg` and `img_15.jpg`. All are under `news.zara.com/assets/responsysimages/industria007/contentlibrary/campaigns/2026/11.noviembre/...`. With images blocked, the email renders as blank cells. The `.imagemobile` class styles alt text for mobile, but it has nothing to display.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://news.zara.com/pub/as?_ri_=...` also lacks `alt`. For a 1x1 pixel, `alt=""` is the correct fix.
- **Viewport meta is malformed:** `content="width=device-width; initial-scale=1.0; maximum-scale=1.0; user-scalable=no;"` uses semicolons, where the spec calls for commas. It also sets `user-scalable=no` and `maximum-scale=1.0`, which block pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Web font in `<head>`:** `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Roboto+Condensed:400,700&display=swap&subset=cyrillic,cyrillic-ext">` is stripped by Gmail and several other clients. The `subset=cyrillic` parameter is unneeded for a `US_en` send. The MSO fallback stack (`HelveticaNeue, Helvetica, Arial`) covers Outlook.
- **Default link color:** `a { color: #999898 }` is about 2.8:1 on white, below WCAG AA's 4.5:1. This only applies if the links render on a light background, which the truncated source doesn't confirm.
- **Plain-text fallback (FAIL):** The text version is 0 chars, so there is no `text/plain` part for text-only clients or accessibility tooling. Spam filters also penalize HTML-only mail.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `${ }`, `%%`, `$firstName`) appear in the visible source.
- Responsys macro comments leaked into the delivered HTML: `<!-- Macros para el contenido -->`, `<!-- Recogemos los países del PAISES_SKIP... -->` and `<!-- CAMPAIGN_CODE:zara;2026_11_14_Norte_woman_weekly_14_11;US_en -->`. They are harmless to rendering but expose internal segmentation and campaign naming, and they add bytes. Stripping them in the send pipeline is cleaner.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` missing (WARN)** and **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) missing (WARN):** QA notes the headers may simply not be preserved by the AgentMail relay. This is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so verify against the raw message.
- **No physical address detected (WARN):** CAN-SPAM requires one. The source is truncated, so the footer wasn't inspected. Treat this as unconfirmed until the full HTML or the raw message is checked.
- **`Authentication-Results` missing (WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, because the relay doesn't expose it. Check the original message headers.
- QA states an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result for it was reported.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No UTM parameters are visible in the truncated source. Responsys `_ri_` tokens carry the attribution server-side, so the final landing URLs and any UTM append can't be confirmed.
- Landing page alignment can't be assessed, because no post-redirect URLs were captured.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a plain-text part.** This is the only hard FAIL.
2. **Add `alt` text to all 16 content images**, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel at `/pub/as`.
3. **Fix the viewport meta.** Use `width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0` and drop `user-scalable=no` and `maximum-scale=1.0`.
4. **Verify compliance on the raw `.eml`.** Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results, and the footer postal address, since the relay may have stripped the headers.
5. **Re-run link probing on the 31 skipped links**, at least the unsubscribe and preference-center links, and record the final redirect URLs and any UTMs.
6. **Minor hygiene:** strip Responsys macro comments, drop `subset=cyrillic` from the font URL, and check the `#999898` link contrast on its actual background.
