# Woman | The New
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-18**
## Executive summary

- This is the same lookbook skeleton Zara has run for Woman since August, and it's a near-clone of the 2026-09-11 "Woman | Discover what's new" send I flagged just a week ago: hero editorial image, then an unbroken vertical scroll through category blocks (Western Codes → Accessories/Co-ord Sets/Knitwear → Clean Lines → Jeans/T-Shirts → On Trend → Wardrobe Faux Leather) with zero copy explaining what's actually new or why. There is no price, no discount, no offer of any kind anywhere in this render — it's pure lookbook, functioning more like a mood board than a commercial email.
- The execution is clean and on-brand — moody black-and-white editorial photography, bold condensed type for section headers, consistent grid — but it's creatively identical to the last several sends with the category names swapped (Western Codes/Clean Lines/On Trend this time vs. ZW Collection/TRF Collection/Selected last time). A subscriber who's opened the last three Woman emails will not be able to tell this one apart from memory. Judgement: competent production, zero incremental reason to open or click, and the brand is visibly running out of variation on a template it's over-leaned on since early August.

## What's working

- Hero editorial image and bold ZARA wordmark establish brand identity instantly.
- Clear section labels (WESTERN CODES, CLEAN LINES, ON TREND) give the scroll some visual rhythm and act as de facto category navigation.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text is legible, layout is consistent top to bottom.
- "VIEW ALL" and app download CTA are visible near the footer for further engagement.

## What's weak

- No offer, price, or discount anywhere — nothing gives a reason to act now versus browsing the site whenever.
- Single vague CTA ("VIEW ALL") buried near the bottom after a very long scroll — no per-section "Shop Now" buttons.
- Subject "Woman | The New" is nearly interchangeable with 2026-09-11's "Woman | Discover what's new" — same brand, same vagueness, same lack of specificity.
- No social proof, sizing/fit info, or any product detail beyond a category label — pure imagery.
- This is at least the fourth near-identical Woman/lookbook-skeleton send in six weeks with no evolution in format.

## Recommendations

- 1. Break the monotonous skeleton — add at least one hero product callout with a price or "New Arrivals from $X" to give the email commercial teeth; this is the single biggest lever, since right now there's no reason to click over just browsing the app later.
- 2. Add a real primary CTA per section (e.g., "Shop Trench Coats") instead of one "VIEW ALL" at the very bottom.
- 3. Differentiate the subject line from the last few Woman sends so repeat subscribers don't autopilot past it.
- 4. Trim the scroll — six-plus category blocks is a lot of vertical real estate for a mobile reader with no incentive to keep scrolling.
