# Homepage snapshot · www.zara.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- This isn't even a homepage — it's a geolocation gate. I opened zara.com and landed on a location/language selector screen. No product, no hero, no campaign, no energy. The entire viewport is a form asking me to confirm I'm in the United States before I can see anything. That's a hard stop between me and whatever collection they're pushing right now.

## What's working

- **"Remember selection" checkbox** — small but useful. At least they're offering to not ask me this again next visit, which is the right move for a returning shopper like me.
- **Clean render** — no broken images, no layout shift, no overlapping elements. The page does what it does without breaking.
- **Location pre-filled to United States** — they correctly guessed my region, so I'm not hunting through a 200-country dropdown from scratch.

## What's weak

- **Zero product exposure** — I came to browse drops and I'm staring at a form. Every second I spend on this screen is a second I'm not seeing a coat I want to buy.
- **No hero, no campaign** — Zara is one of the most visually driven brands in the world and the first thing I see is a white page with two dropdown menus. That's a complete waste of brand equity.
- **No returning-user recognition** — I've shopped Zara before. There's no cookie recognition, no "welcome back," no "pick up where you left off." The gate treats me like a stranger every time.
- **`CONTINUE` CTA is a gate, not an invitation** — the only button on screen is asking me to jump a hurdle, not enticing me to explore. This is friction disguised as UX.
- **"DO NOT SELL OR SHARE MY PERSONAL INFORMATION" footer** — the only other text on the screen is a legal notice. That's a grim first impression for a fashion brand.

## Recommendations

- **Remember my location and skip the gate for return visitors** — if I've been to the site before, drop me directly into the US homepage. The "Remember selection" checkbox exists but apparently isn't doing the job.
- **Show a teaser hero behind the gate** — blur or dim a campaign image behind the location selector so I can see product before I commit to clicking through. It builds anticipation instead of friction.
- **Swap the gate copy from "SELECT YOUR LOCATION" to something with brand voice** — even "Where are you shopping from?" feels more Zara than this sterile all-caps bureaucratic header.
- **Add a skip/dismiss path** — if my browser locale already signals US/English, auto-select and let me tap one button to confirm, or just redirect me automatically.
