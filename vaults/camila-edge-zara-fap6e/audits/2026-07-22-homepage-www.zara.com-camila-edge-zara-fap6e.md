# Homepage snapshot · www.zara.com · 2026-07-22
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-22**
## Executive summary

- I opened Zara.com and I didn't get a homepage — I got a gate. The entire above-the-fold experience is a location/language selector with a "CONTINUE" button. No hero, no products, no campaign imagery, nothing. It reads like an airport kiosk, not a fashion brand that drops new collections I care about.

## What's working

- **Clean, unambiguous gate mechanic** — Location pre-selected as "United States" and Language as "English" means I probably only need one tap on CONTINUE to get through. That's the one thing working here.
- **"Remember selection" checkbox** — Small but thoughtful. If I check it, I don't see this again. That's a returning-visitor nicety, even if it's buried at the bottom of a friction wall.

## What's weak

- **Zero fashion content above the fold** — I came here to check if SRPLS or Studio dropped anything new and I'm looking at a dropdown form. There's no reason to think this is a brand I care about.
- **No brand voice** — The copy is pure utility ("SELECT YOUR LOCATION," "LANGUAGE," "CONTINUE"). Zara's editorial aesthetic is completely absent. This could be a visa application portal.
- **Massive dead white space** — Below the Continue button there's a huge blank area before the privacy footer. On mobile, this reads as a broken or unloaded page. I'd assume something failed to render.
- **"DO NOT SELL OR SHARE MY PERSONAL INFORMATION" footer** — That's the only other text on the screen. The first thing I read after the form is a privacy opt-out. Not a vibe.
- **No skip option** — If I've been here before, there's no "already set, take me in" shortcut unless I previously checked "Remember selection." First-time mobile visitors are stuck in the gate.

## Recommendations

- **Load the actual homepage behind the gate** — Render the hero content in the background so it's visible as soon as CONTINUE is tapped, or better, lazy-detect my locale from IP and skip the gate entirely for obvious markets like US/English. Every other Zara competitor does this.
- **Inject one campaign image into the gate itself** — Even a cropped hero behind a translucent overlay would keep the brand alive while I fill out the form. Make me feel like I'm entering a store, not a spreadsheet.
- **Move "Remember selection" to be opt-out pre-checked** — Right now it's an unchecked checkbox most people will miss. Flip the default so returning visitors are automatically remembered.
- **Kill the dead white space** — If something was supposed to load below the Continue button and didn't, fix the render. If it's intentional, remove it — that blank expanse reads as a broken page on mobile.
