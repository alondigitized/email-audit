# Homepage snapshot · www.zara.com · 2026-05-04
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-04**
## Executive summary

- This isn't the homepage — it's a geo-gate. The entire first screen is swallowed by a location/language selector before I've seen a single product, campaign image, or reason to care. Zara is a brand I actually check regularly, and my first impression on mobile is a blank white form. That's a rough start.
- **2/10**
- − Hero copy or imagery explicitly speaks to my persona: no hero whatsoever, just a form
- − Returning-shopper hook visible: nothing — no "welcome back," no recently-viewed, no loyalty
- − Concrete offer visible above the fold: zero
- − Unmistakable primary CTA above the fold: `CONTINUE` exists but it's a gate, not a shopping CTA
- + Visual hierarchy is technically clear — only one action available, so the eye lands on CONTINUE by default
- + No render bugs — the form renders cleanly, dropdowns and checkbox are intact
- − Demographic signals match my persona: no imagery, no signals at all
- − Current campaign / season reflected: not visible
- − Loyalty / membership benefits visible: absent
- − Offer feels honest: N/A — no offer to evaluate

## What's working

- **The form itself is clean.** Location pre-filled as "United States," language as "English" — I don't have to hunt for my region. One tap on CONTINUE and I'm through.
- **"Remember selection" checkbox** is a small but thoughtful UX detail. At least I won't see this again next visit if I check it.
- **No visual clutter.** The white space is minimal and the Zara logotype is crisp and recognizable — brand mark lands instantly.

## What's weak

- **Zero product content above the fold.** I opened Zara.com to browse. Instead I'm staring at a form. Every second I spend on this screen is friction that could kill my session.
- **No campaign image or editorial hook.** A brand as visual as Zara is showing me a blank white page with a dropdown. That's a failure of art direction on mobile.
- **`CONTINUE` reads like a wall, not an invitation.** Copy doesn't say "Shop Zara," "Enter," or anything that feels like the start of a browsing experience.
- **No loyalty or account recognition.** If I've been here before, there's no "Welcome back, Camila" or "Sign in to see your wishlist." Repeat visitors get the same cold gate as first-timers.
- **"DO NOT SELL OR SHARE MY PERSONAL INFORMATION" footer text** — this is the only secondary message on the screen, and it's a legal/privacy disclaimer. On a fashion homepage, that's a deflating final note.

## Recommendations

- **Background the geo-gate behind a campaign image.** Show the current editorial campaign full-bleed; float the location selector as a small modal or bottom sheet overlay. I get context and tone before I'm gated.
- **Pre-resolve location via IP and skip the gate for repeat visitors.** If my cookie says I've selected US/English before, just land me on the homepage. The "Remember selection" checkbox exists — actually use it aggressively.
- **Swap `CONTINUE` for something with editorial weight** — even `SHOP ZARA` or `ENTER` with the campaign name would align with the brand's editorial voice.
- **Surface a returning-user hook on this screen.** Even a small "Sign in" link next to CONTINUE would let me bypass the gate directly into my account, which is how I actually shop.
