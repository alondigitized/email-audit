# Homepage snapshot · www.zara.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- I opened Zara and got a geo-gate. Not a homepage — a location selector screen with the logo, two dropdowns, and a "CONTINUE" button. Zero merch, zero campaign, zero vibe. If I wasn't already loyal to Zara I'd close this tab before I even got to the actual site.

## What's working

- **The ZARA wordmark** — large, sharp, immediately recognizable. I know exactly where I am.
- **Clean render** — no clutter, no layout chaos. The form is legible on mobile.
- **"Remember selection" checkbox** — at least they're trying to reduce repeat friction for return visits. That's a small but appreciated signal for a high-frequency shopper like me.

## What's weak

- **The gate itself** — a full-screen location interstitial is the single biggest bounce risk on mobile. I'm already on zara.com; my phone's locale is set to US English. Zara knows this.
- **No product, no campaign, no reason to care** — the screen gives me zero emotional or commercial reason to push through. Cold open into a form is brutal.
- **"DO NOT SELL OR SHARE MY PERSONAL INFORMATION" at the footer** — legally required but visually jarring as the only secondary text on screen. It's not a trust signal, it's a legal disclaimer in an otherwise empty page.
- **No account shortcut** — a returning user like me can't log in or skip the gate. There's no "Already have an account? Sign in" path that could auto-detect my location and bypass this.

## Recommendations

- **Auto-detect locale and skip the gate for returning visitors** — if I've been to Zara before, read my cookie and take me straight to the US homepage. This interstitial should only fire on a genuine ambiguous locale.
- **If the gate must stay, put a campaign image behind it** — a full-bleed Studio or SRPLS editorial behind a frosted overlay gives me something to look at and a reason to hit CONTINUE faster.
- **Add a "Sign in to skip" shortcut** — let returning account holders bypass the selector entirely. Rewards loyalty and reduces friction for my exact use case.
- **Move the privacy footer link to a subtle icon** — "DO NOT SELL OR SHARE MY PERSONAL INFORMATION" as the only visible secondary text makes the screen feel like a legal document, not a fashion site.
