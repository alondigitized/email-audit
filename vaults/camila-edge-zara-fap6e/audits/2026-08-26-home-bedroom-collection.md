# Home Bedroom Collection
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-26**
## Executive summary

- This is Zara Home running its usual lookbook skeleton — hero shot, then an endless, unbroken scroll of moody bedroom lifestyle photography (linens, throws, a lamp, a bench, folded blankets) with no product names, no prices, and no distinguishing offer of any kind. It's the same pattern flagged in the 2026-08-18 "Home Office Collection" send: gorgeous, editorial, and functionally inert as a commerce email.
- There's not a single CTA button until "DISCOVER MORE HOME COLLECTIONS" appears roughly two-thirds down, and even that leads to a vague "THE NEW" link rather than anything bedroom-specific. A second "HOME FRAGRANCES / CAFETAL COLLECTION" teaser is bolted onto the bottom, diluting the subject line's promise entirely. This email photographs beautifully and converts by accident, if at all — it's a catalog page pretending to be a marketing email.

## What's working

- Consistent, high-quality neutral-toned photography that reinforces Zara Home's aesthetic positioning.
- The "THE BEDROOM COLLECTION" section header clearly orients the reader early on.
- Grid pacing (2-up image blocks) is visually clean with no rendering bugs, broken images, or overlapping text.
- Hero image and wordmark at the top are crisp and load correctly.

## What's weak

- Zero prices, product names, or fabric/fit details anywhere in the entire email.
- No CTA button until deep in the scroll, and even that one ("THE NEW") doesn't map to "Bedroom."
- Subject promises "Home Bedroom Collection" but the email tacks on an unrelated "Home Fragrances" section at the end, undercutting focus.
- No urgency, no offer, no loyalty messaging — nothing that would compel action versus just looking.
- Excessively long scroll (I count 20+ images) with no hierarchy telling the eye where to stop and click.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a single, prominent CTA button directly under the hero image ("Shop the Bedroom Edit") instead of burying the only link two-thirds down.
- 2. Cut the image count by half — pick 6-8 hero products, name them, and link each directly to its product page.
- 3. Drop at least one concrete price or "from $X" anchor to give the email commercial teeth.
- 4. Remove the Home Fragrances section or split it into its own send — it dilutes the bedroom focus promised in the subject.
- 5. Move "DISCOVER MORE HOME COLLECTIONS" up near the top as a secondary nav, not the only CTA in the whole email.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New in: Bedroom linens & throws`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The Bedroom Edit is here`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Layered linens, wool throws, and warm wood — shop the edit`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cozy up your bedroom with our latest arrivals`
