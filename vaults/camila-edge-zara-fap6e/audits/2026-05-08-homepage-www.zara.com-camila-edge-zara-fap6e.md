# Homepage snapshot · www.zara.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay so instead of a homepage I got a location/language gate — the full-screen "SELECT YOUR LOCATION" interstitial. No imagery, no campaign, no product. This is the brand's way of saying "hold on, prove you belong here before we let you in," and as a returning Zara shopper who is obviously in the US and obviously speaks English, it's an immediate friction tax. The only thing I can tell about the brand right now is the logo — which does look sharp.

## What's working

- **The CONTINUE button** is clearly labeled and tappable — unambiguous affordance, no guessing.
- **Location pre-selected to United States / English** — at least I don't have to hunt through a dropdown; defaults are right for me.
- **Zara wordmark** is clean and on-brand; the logo itself communicates the brand's editorial aesthetic instantly.
- **"Remember selection" checkbox** is a small but real UX courtesy — if it actually works, I only see this once.

## What's weak

- **The entire above-the-fold experience is a dead wall.** There's no image, no campaign teaser, no product — just a bureaucratic form sitting in the middle of a white page.
- **Huge blank space below the form** looks like a render failure or broken lazy-load; it reads as broken on mobile.
- **No campaign preview behind or around the gate** — competitors often let the homepage bleed through a dimmed overlay so you're already curious before you click through. Zara shows nothing.
- **"DO NOT SELL OR SHARE MY PERSONAL INFORMATION" at the very bottom** is visually jarring as the only other text on screen — it's a legal link but it reads as a warning label, not a trust signal.
- **Zero personalization signal** — I've presumably been to Zara before; there's no cookie-based skip or recognition that I exist as a returning shopper.

## Recommendations

- **Skip the gate for return visitors** — use the existing cookie/geo signal to drop returning US/English users straight onto the homepage. "Remember selection" exists but clearly isn't firing reliably if I'm seeing this again.
- **Layer the gate over the actual homepage** as a translucent modal so I can see the new collection bleeding through behind it — I'm already teased, I'm more motivated to click CONTINUE.
- **Fill the dead white space** below the form with something: a single editorial image from the current drop, a seasonal campaign line. Even a subtle texture would feel intentional.
- **Move the CCPA link to the footer of the gate**, not the footer of the page — right now it reads like the punchline of the entire screen.
