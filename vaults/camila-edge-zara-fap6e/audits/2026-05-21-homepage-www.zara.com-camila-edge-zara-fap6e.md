# Homepage snapshot · www.zara.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Okay so I didn't even get to the homepage — what loaded is the location/language selection gate. Giant logo, two dropdowns, a "CONTINUE" button, and nothing else. This is the first thing I see on my phone and it's already making me do work before showing me a single piece of clothing. Zara is a brand I genuinely love, but this entrance is cold and transactional.

## What's working

- **The "CONTINUE" button** is clearly labeled and easy to tap on mobile — no ambiguity about what to do next
- **"Remember selection" checkbox** is a small but thoughtful UX detail that saves friction on future visits
- **No render bugs** — the gate itself is clean, nothing broken or overlapping
- **The ZARA wordmark** is bold and recognizable; brand ID is instant

## What's weak

- **The gate itself is the problem** — forcing a location/language selector before showing any content is pure friction; I already know I want Zara and I'm on a US device
- **Zero merchandise** — not a single product image, no seasonal context, no editorial hook to build anticipation while I complete the selector
- **"DO NOT SELL OR SHARE MY PERSONAL INFORMATION"** as the only footer text is a cold legal note that does nothing for brand warmth
- **No personalization signal** — a returning shopper like me gets exactly the same cold gate as someone who's never heard of Zara; there's no "welcome back" or geo-detect shortcut
- **The lower half of the screen is dead white space** — the form sits in the top third and the rest is empty; on mobile this feels unfinished

## Recommendations

- **Auto-detect location from IP/device locale and skip the gate** — or at minimum pre-fill the dropdowns and reduce this to a single confirm tap so it disappears in under a second
- **Add a background editorial image** — even a blurred runway or SRPLS campaign shot behind the form would remind me why I came here and build momentum toward the actual homepage
- **Surface one teaser line** beneath the logo — something like "New Collection In" or "SRPLS Drop: Now Live" so the gate doubles as a hook while I confirm my region
- **Move "Remember selection" to pre-checked** — most mobile users are on their own phone; default should save friction, not require an extra tap to opt in
