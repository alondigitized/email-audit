# Homepage snapshot · www.zara.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- This isn't a homepage — it's a location gate, and it's the first thing I hit on my phone. The full ZARA wordmark is gorgeous and unmistakably the brand, but beyond that I'm looking at a blank white form with zero product energy. As a weekly browser who knows exactly where I'm going, this friction point before I can even see what's dropping is the kind of thing that makes me question why I didn't just open the app.

## What's working

- **The ZARA logo treatment** — that massive, editorial serif wordmark takes up a third of the screen and immediately signals "you're in the right place." The brand equity does the heavy lifting.
- **`CONTINUE` button legibility** — clear border, centered label, obvious tap target on mobile. No guessing.
- **"Remember selection" checkbox** — small but thoughtful. Tells me that if I check this I won't see this screen again. That's the right instinct even if it's buried below the CTA.
- **Clean render** — zero layout breakage, no overlapping text, the form fields are evenly spaced. At least it's not broken.

## What's weak

- **It's a gate, full stop** — I didn't come to select a country, I came to see if there's a SRPLS drop or a Studio coat I need. Every second I spend on this screen is friction before intention.
- **No recognition of a returning visitor** — I'm a weekly browser. There's no "hey, you're back" signal, no cookie-based skip, no account shortcut in the corner. The "Remember selection" checkbox is buried *below* the CONTINUE button — by the time I notice it I've already tapped through.
- **Dead white void below the fold** — the bottom half of the viewport is pure white except for "DO NOT SELL OR SHARE MY PERSONAL INFORMATION" in tiny gray type. That legal footer at the bottom of a near-empty screen is almost comedically bleak.
- **Zero campaign presence** — no teaser, no "new collection now live" strip, not even a muted background image. Competitors gate-page with a hero bleed behind the form. Zara chose nothing.
- **All-caps location header feels bureaucratic** — "SELECT YOUR LOCATION" reads like a government portal, not a brand I pay full price to shop.

## Recommendations

- **Auto-detect and skip the gate for repeat visitors** — if my browser has a cookie or geolocation, pre-fill the country and surface a "You're set to United States / English — continue" one-tap confirmation instead of a blank form every session.
- **Bleed a campaign image behind the form** — even a full-bleed, desaturated editorial shot from the current collection as the background would turn this dead screen into a brand moment. The gate becomes a teaser.
- **Promote "Remember selection" above the CTA, not below it** — move the checkbox to a line directly above the CONTINUE button with slightly larger type. Right now it's functionally invisible.
- **Add a soft account shortcut in the top-right corner** — a small person icon that lets logged-in users skip the gate entirely and land directly on their personalized feed. High-frequency shoppers like me would use it every time.
