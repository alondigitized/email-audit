# Discover the new collection
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- This is Zara's menswear lookbook scroll, and it's the same "A Winter Edit" creative from the 2026-10-30 "Woman | This week at Zara" send, re-cut for men. The black-and-white hero with the oversized ZARA wordmark is striking. After that it's a long grid of model shots with no price, no offer and no product names. The whole email asks the reader to browse, and nothing gives a reason to buy today.
- The category blocks do give some structure: "Leather Pieces", "Urban Fits", "The Olfactive Atlas" (perfumes) and "Selected Pieces" (jackets). Each is a large black headline beside an image. The only labels are tiny "VIEW" links and one small "Discover the Collection" in a white gap in the middle of the grid. A man who wants a coat has to guess which tile leads to one.
- It's good brand content and weak commerce. This is the sixth lookbook-style Zara send in two weeks with no offer, so it reads as routine. I'd rate it a 4 or 5.

## What's working

- The monochrome hero with the oversized ZARA wordmark is confident and immediately recognizable.
- The stacked headline blocks (Leather Pieces, Urban Fits) give the scroll a clear rhythm and signal different categories.
- The model casting and styling fit a menswear shopper in autumn/winter, with coats, knitwear, turtlenecks and leather.
- The perfume block with the bottle on stones adds a different product category and a gifting angle.
- The layout renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- There is no offer, price, or member benefit anywhere in the email.
- "Discover the Collection" is a tiny black text label floating in white space. It isn't a button, and it's buried in the middle of the grid.
- The "VIEW" links are small and easy to miss, and nothing says which product each one leads to.
- The long grid of nine near-identical model shots has no product names or prices, so a reader can't tell what is being sold.
- The footer has a large "Download our app" heading but no actual store badges or button.
- It repeats the "Winter Edit" concept from yesterday, and Zara has sent four lookbooks in the past two weeks with the same structure.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add one concrete commerce hook near the top, such as a price-anchored hero ("Wool coats from $129"), and link the hero to a specific category page.
- 2. Replace the tiny "VIEW" and "Discover the Collection" labels with real buttons that say what they lead to, such as "Shop leather jackets" and "Shop coats".
- 3. Add product names and prices under the nine grid shots, or reduce the grid to the three best sellers.
- 4. Replace the "Download our app" text with App Store and Google Play badges and one line about what the app offers.
- 5. Vary the creative between sends. Back-to-back lookbooks train subscribers to skim past them.

# Technical Audit: Zara, "Discover the new collection"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Responsys-built XHTML table layout (campaign `zara;2026_10_31_Norte_man_weekly_31_10;US_en`). It has no plain-text part and no alt text on any of 19 images, and the QA run scored a 38% pass rate. Several compliance findings can't be confirmed because the HTML was truncated and the relay may have stripped headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links were reported. 12 material links were probed and 17 were skipped, so about 59% of links are unverified. The QA output lists no probe failures.
- The open-tracking pixel `https://news.zara.com/pub/as?_ri_=...` has no `alt`. It is flagged along with the content images, but it is a 1x1 beacon, so this is low severity.
- Production HTML still contains internal comments: `<!-- CAMPAIGN_CODE:zara;2026_10_31_Norte_man_weekly_31_10;US_en-->`, Spanish-language macro notes (`Macros para el contenido`, `PAISES_SKIP`, `PAISES_ENVIO`) and `RPL` references. These leak internal campaign and segmentation naming. They are harmless to rendering but add bytes.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** 19 images have no `alt`. These are `img_01.jpg`, `img_02.jpg`, `img_03A/B.jpg`, `img_04A–H.jpg`, `img_05.jpg`, `img_06A/B.jpg`, `img_07.jpg`, `img_08A/B.jpg`, plus the tracking pixel. With images blocked, the email renders blank. There is also no useful text for screen readers. The `.imagemobile` class styles fallback text (`font-size:16px`, `text-align:center`), but that only works if `alt` is populated.
- **Viewport meta:** `content="width=device-width; initial-scale=1.0; maximum-scale=1.0; user-scalable=no;"` uses semicolons instead of commas. It also disables pinch-zoom (`user-scalable=no`, `maximum-scale=1.0`), which is an accessibility problem.
- **Web font:** `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Roboto+Condensed...&subset=cyrillic,cyrillic-ext">` is stripped by Gmail and Outlook, so the font falls back. The Cyrillic subset is unnecessary for a `US_en` send.
- **Markup:** the `<link>` tag is not self-closed in an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. This is minor.
- The Outlook handling (`mso-table-lspace`, `o:PixelsPerInch`, MSO fallback font stack) is present and correct.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `$!{}`) appear in the visible source. The remaining body is truncated, so tokens further down can't be ruled out.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is 0 chars.** The email has no `text/plain` alternative, which hurts deliverability and accessibility. This is confirmed.
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both are not found. The QA note says AgentMail may not capture these headers, so this is **unconfirmed**. Verify against the raw message. If they are truly absent, this violates Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **No physical address detected:** the footer is past the truncation point, so this can't be confirmed from the data. Check the footer. Zara's US sends typically carry one.
- **Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM/DMARC):** unknown, because the relay doesn't expose it. This is a limitation of the tooling, not a finding about the email.
- The sending domain `news.zara.com` is a subdomain, which is good practice.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Can't assess. The visible HTML contains no hrefs, and the QA data doesn't list destination URLs or UTM parameters. Links most likely route through Responsys click-tracking redirects, but that isn't confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a `text/plain` part. This is the only confirmed hard failure.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images. Use `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` on the tracking pixel.
3. Fix the viewport meta: use `width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0` and drop `user-scalable=no` and `maximum-scale`.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` in the raw headers, and the physical address in the footer. Re-run the audit with untruncated HTML.
5. Strip internal comments (`CAMPAIGN_CODE`, macro notes) from the production render.
