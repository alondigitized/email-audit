# Woman | The New
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-14**
## Executive summary

- This is the exact same "Woman | The New" send I audited on 2026-08-07 (4.00/10) and 2026-07-30 — same category-block skeleton (ZW Collection → Floral Prints editorial → TRF Collection → The Item → Selected Pieces), same subject line verbatim, same lack of any offer, price, or CTA button. Zara is running this template on what looks like a weekly or biweekly cadence with only the imagery swapped out, and the floral/desert-boho styling here just recycles the mood from the "Floral Prints and Delicate Details" block already seen in past sends.
- The one new wrinkle is a render bug: "THE ITEM" section header has a second, larger "THE ITEM" watermark-style text overlaid directly on top of it in white, creating an illegible double-exposure of text in the hero image for that block. That's a genuine defect, not just a design choice repeated from prior sends.
- This is pure lookbook merchandising with zero commercial urgency — no discount, no CTA, no personalization — and it's now the fourth near-identical structural clone I've seen from this account. Judgement: skip.

## What's working

- Clean, consistent editorial photography with a cohesive warm/earth-tone palette across all six category blocks.
- Section headers (ZW COLLECTION, TRF COLLECTION, THE ITEM, SELECTED PIECES) give clear scannable structure for a long-scroll layout.
- Underlined text links (KNITWEAR, DRESSES, BLAZERS, JACKETS, JEANS, etc.) under each image make individual product categories clickable, not just a single blob link.
- No broken images or missing assets elsewhere in the render — photography loads cleanly.

## What's weak

- The "THE ITEM I THE BUSTIER TOP" block has overlapping white text ("THE ITEM" duplicated at two sizes) rendered on top of itself — an actual visible render bug, illegible at both sizes.
- Zero offer of any kind — no %/$ off, no free shipping, nothing to click toward beyond "browse a category."
- No single hero CTA button anywhere in the email — only small underlined text links buried under each image.
- Structurally identical to the 2026-08-07 and 2026-07-30 "Woman | The New" sends — same six-block skeleton, same subject line, just re-shot photography.
- Requires a very long scroll (this render is ~3700px) before reaching any social/app footer — no way to jump to a specific section.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the "THE ITEM" overlapping text bug immediately — it's the most visible defect in the email and makes that block's headline unreadable.
- 2. Add one clear primary CTA button ("Shop New Arrivals") near the top instead of relying on small underlined category links scattered throughout.
- 3. Break the template pattern — three sends in a row with this exact structure trains subscribers to stop opening. Introduce a new layout or lead with a specific product story instead of the generic collection recap.
- 4. Surface something concrete — new-in count, restock notice, or limited pieces — to give a reason to click today versus browsing later.
