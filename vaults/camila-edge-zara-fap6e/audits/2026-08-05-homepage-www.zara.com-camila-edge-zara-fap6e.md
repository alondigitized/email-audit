# Homepage snapshot · www.zara.com · 2026-08-05
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- I tapped into zara.com on my phone hoping to scope the new drops and got... a location/language picker. No hero, no product, no vibe — just the wordmark and a "SELECT YOUR LOCATION" form. This isn't a homepage, it's a gate in front of one, and for a brand I check weekly for Studio pieces, that's a dead-on-arrival first impression.

## What's working

- **The wordmark itself** — big, confident ZARA logo up top, so at least brand recognition is instant.
- **The form is dead simple** — two dropdowns (Location: United States, Language: English) and one button. No clutter, no fifteen fields.
- **"Remember selection" checkbox** — small thing, but it tells me I won't have to do this every single visit, which matters since I'm a weekly browser.

## What's weak

- **There's no homepage here** — I opened the site to see product, not to configure region settings. Zero merchandising, zero imagery, zero indication of what's dropping this week.
- **No visual identity beyond the logo** — everything else is plain black-on-white form UI, could be any e-commerce site's settings page.
- **Defaults aren't pre-confirmed** — it already knows I'm probably in the US on English, so why not skip straight to the homepage with a tiny "not your region?" link instead of a full-screen blocker?
- **Footer link is oddly prominent for what it is** — "DO NOT SELL OR SHARE MY PERSONAL INFORMATION" sitting alone at the bottom of an otherwise empty screen just reads as legal clutter with nothing else to balance it.

## Recommendations

- Auto-detect region/language from device settings and skip the gate entirely for US/English users — save it for an edge-case toggle in the menu.
- If a gate is unavoidable, put a small teaser image or new-arrivals thumbnail behind/above the form so it doesn't feel like a totally blank wall.
- Make "Remember selection" checked by default — I already have to trust the browser locale is right, don't make me opt in to not seeing this again.
- Once past the gate, land me on something that reflects right now — a Studio or SRPLS callout, not a generic grid.
