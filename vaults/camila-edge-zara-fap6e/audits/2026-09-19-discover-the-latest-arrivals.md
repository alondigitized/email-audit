- Consistent black-and-white editorial photography reinforces a cohesive brand look.
- Clear category labels (Waistcoats, Jeans, Jackets, Bags, Blazers, Trousers, T-Shirts) make the grid scannable if someone does scroll.
- No visible render bugs — images load cleanly, text is legible, no overlap.
- "90's Archive" and "Street Fits" give a loose narrative thread rather than just a flat product dump.

## What's weak

- No visible price, discount, or any concrete offer anywhere in the email.
- Only one real CTA link ("VIEW COLLECTION") on the entire send — everything else is a static image with a category label, not an actionable button.
- Subject is generic and near-identical to prior sends ("Discover the new collection," 09-12; "Woman | Discover what's new," 09-11/09-04) — zero differentiation in the inbox.
- The Tobacco Collection perfume block appears abruptly at the very end with no header transition, breaking the "wardrobe" narrative established above it.
- Extremely long unbroken scroll (14+ image blocks) with no anchor navigation or "shop by category" jump links, so anything below the first three panels is unlikely to be seen on mobile.

- 2. Convert at least the top 3 category blocks into real buttons ("Shop Jackets," "Shop Waistcoats") instead of relying on the whole image being clickable with only a caption.
- 3. Cut the email length by half — 14 sequential image blocks is fatigue-inducing; pick the 5-6 strongest categories and drop the rest into a "See more" link.
- 4. Give the perfume block its own send or at minimum a section break/header so it doesn't read as a mis-tagged afterthought.
