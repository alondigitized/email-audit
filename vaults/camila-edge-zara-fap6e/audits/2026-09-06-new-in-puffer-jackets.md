# New In: Puffer Jackets
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-06**
## Executive summary

- This is Zara Kids, not the usual "Woman | This week's arrivals" grind — narrow product focus (puffer jackets) segmented by age band (Girl 6-14, Boy 6-14, Girl 1½-6, Boy 1½-6, Baby 0-18mo), which is at least a sharper premise than the endless lookbook scrolls I've flagged from this brand all August. But execution undercuts the concept: the hero's "ZARA" wordmark is cropped off the right edge of the image, there isn't a single word of body copy, price, or CTA button anywhere in the entire render, and every section link is just tiny underlined text ("GIRL / 6-14 YEARS") rather than an actual button.
- Compared to the run of near-identical Woman/Kids/Home sends I've been logging since late August (2026-08-23 through 2026-09-04, all landing ~4/10), this one is thematically distinct enough to not feel like the sixth rerun of the same email — but it repeats the brand's core structural sin: hero image, then an unbroken vertical scroll of near-identical outdoor editorial shots with no offer, no price, no urgency, nothing that gives a reader a reason to click today versus next week.
- Judgement: a slightly-better-targeted version of the same underperforming template. Without a price, a promo, or a real CTA, this reads as a lookbook, not a marketing email.

## What's working

- Clear age-band segmentation (Girl/Boy, two age tiers, plus Baby) makes it easy to jump straight to the relevant kid's size without scrolling through irrelevant categories.
- Consistent, moody outdoor-editorial photography across all age groups gives the send a cohesive visual identity.
- Simple, uncluttered layout with generous whitespace between sections avoids visual clutter despite being a long scroll.
- Clean footer with functional-looking unsubscribe and social links.

## What's weak

- The "ZARA" wordmark in the hero is cropped off the right edge of the image — a visible render/crop bug on the single most prominent asset in the email.
- Zero price, discount, or offer anywhere in the email — this is pure lookbook, no reason to buy now.
- No CTA buttons at all — the only "links" are small underlined age-range labels, easy to miss and weak to click.
- No product detail (sizing chart hint, materials, price point) despite this being a parent-facing kids' apparel email where sizing/fit is the #1 concern.
- Six nearly identical outdoor mountain/rain photoshoot images in a row with no copy breaking up the monotony — same structural complaint as every other Zara send logged this month.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a visible price or offer to the hero band immediately below "NEW IN: PUFFER JACKETS" — even "New arrivals, from $49.90" gives the reader a reason to scroll rather than bounce.
- 2. Fix the cropped "ZARA" logo in the hero image — it's the brand's own wordmark getting cut off, which undermines the polish this brand is known for.
- 3. Replace the underlined text links under each photo with real button CTAs ("Shop Girl 6-14" / "Shop Baby") — buttons convert better than inline text links, especially on mobile.
- 4. Cut the six-image gallery to 3-4 age bands or add short copy dividers between sections to break the monotony a subscriber has now seen five times this month.
- 5. Surface a sizing/fit callout for parents (e.g., "True to size — size up for layering") since fit uncertainty is the top purchase blocker for kids' outerwear bought online.
