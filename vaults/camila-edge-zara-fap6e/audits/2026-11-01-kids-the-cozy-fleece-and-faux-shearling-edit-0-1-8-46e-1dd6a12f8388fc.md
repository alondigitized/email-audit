# Kids | The Cozy Fleece and Faux Shearling Edit
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- This is Zara Kids' standard lookbook scroll, this time on fleece and faux shearling. The photography is the strongest part: kids in bright, textured jackets on a blue sky and dune-grass hero, then a clean 3x3 grid on saturated backdrops. The seasonal timing is right for early November. The oversized ZARA wordmark and the stacked black headline make the hierarchy easy to read.
- As a commerce email it does almost nothing. There is no price, no offer and no named product. The calls to action are a tiny "VIEW" and a text-only "DISCOVER THE COLLECTION" floating in white space. A parent has to already want fleece to tap anything. Like the 2026-10-18 "Kids | Discover what's new" send, it works as brand content and not as a conversion tool.
- Judgement: a good-looking, low-effort send that earns a mid score. The creative is a 7, the selling is a 3.

## What's working

- The hero is arresting. Three kids in bold outerwear against a blue sky, with the oversized ZARA wordmark cropped on the right.
- The headline is on-topic and seasonal. "The Cozy Fleece and Faux Shearling Edit" tells you the category immediately.
- The 3x3 grid shows variety: Snoopy, Hello Kitty, hearts, colorblock and plain sherpa. That helps a parent picture their own child in one of them.
- The home-kids strip at the bottom (bedding, plates, mugs) is a cheap cross-sell into another category.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- There is no offer, price or urgency anywhere in the email.
- The CTAs are tiny. "VIEW" beside the headline and "DISCOVER THE COLLECTION" in the grid centre look like captions, not buttons.
- The grid images give no product names, prices or sizes, so nothing tells a parent what to tap.
- The preview text is the generic "NEWSLETTER. ONLINE VERSION" line, which wastes the second line of the inbox row.
- The footer has "DOWNLOAD OUR APP" as plain text with no badge or incentive.
- Another Zara lookbook with no hook, after the 10/30 and 10/31 sends and the 10/18 kids send, adds to the fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Turn "VIEW" and "DISCOVER THE COLLECTION" into real buttons with specific copy, such as "Shop kids' fleece". Next to that, add a price-from line ("Fleece jackets from $X") so there is a reason to click.
- 2. Put names and prices under the grid images, or at least link each tile to its product.
- 3. Write a real preheader that adds information beyond the subject.
- 4. Add one reason to act now, such as a restock, a sizes-selling-fast note or a seasonal deadline.
- 5. Give the app a download badge and one benefit.
