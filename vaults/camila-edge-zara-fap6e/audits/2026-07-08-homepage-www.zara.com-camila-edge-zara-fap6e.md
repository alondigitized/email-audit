# Homepage snapshot · www.zara.com · 2026-07-08
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-08**
## Executive summary

- This isn't the homepage — it's a location gate. What I'm looking at is a full-screen "SELECT YOUR LOCATION" interstitial with two dropdowns (Location: United States, Language: English), a "CONTINUE" button, a "Remember selection" checkbox, and a privacy disclaimer at the bottom. There's no campaign, no product imagery, no editorial direction whatsoever. As a mobile-first shopper who browses Zara regularly, hitting this wall before seeing anything is a friction point, full stop.

## What's working

- **"Remember selection" checkbox** — small but smart. Means next visit I skip this gate. That's the right move.
- **Clean minimal layout** — the Zara wordmark is massive and confident, which does land some brand equity even on a gate screen. It doesn't feel cheap.
- **"DO NOT SELL OR SHARE MY PERSONAL INFORMATION" footer link** — privacy-forward and visible without burying it. I appreciate that it's not hidden.

## What's weak

- **The gate itself is the problem** — I'm on mobile, I'm in the US, and this is a known locale from any prior cookie or device signal. There's no reason I should be hitting this unless it's a cold first visit, and even then it should be a slim banner, not a full-screen takeover.
- **Zero brand communication above the fold** — no product, no campaign, no mood. The entire screen is administrative. I lose all browsing energy immediately.
- **"United States" is already pre-filled** — so why am I being asked to confirm it? The UX work is already done; just let me in.
- **No shortcut to skip or browse anyway** — there's no "continue without selecting" or an X to dismiss. It's a hard gate.

## Recommendations

- **Reduce this to a slim bottom sheet or banner**, not a full-screen overlay — pre-fill my locale and let me tap one "confirm" without blocking the actual content behind it.
- **Load a hero or editorial image behind the gate** so I can see what season/campaign is live while the selector resolves — converts dead time into appetite.
- **Add a "Remember this device" toggle that's on by default** — not unchecked. Most shoppers won't think to check it; flip the default.
- **On repeat visits (any cookie/session signal), skip the gate entirely** — if my location is already known, go straight to the homepage. This is the highest-ROI fix.
