# Homepage snapshot · www.zara.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- This isn't a homepage — it's a location gate, and I'm stuck at the door. The entire viewport is just a "SELECT YOUR LOCATION" interstitial with a dropdown pre-filled to United States and a CONTINUE button. There's nothing to browse, no product, no editorial, no drop. As someone who opens Zara on my phone weekly, having to confirm my location every single time is friction I did not ask for.
- **1/10**

## What's working

- **"Remember selection" checkbox** — at least it's there. If I tick it and it actually works, I won't see this screen again. That's the one thing saving this from a zero.
- **Logo presentation** — the oversized Zara wordmark is gorgeous, high-contrast, unmistakably on-brand. Even on a gate page, the identity is clean.

## What's weak

- **Zero product above the fold** — I came to browse and I'm looking at a form. There's no hero, no editorial image, no drop teaser, nothing. The bottom two-thirds of the screen is just white space.
- **"DO NOT SELL OR SHARE MY PERSONAL INFORMATION" footer link** — this is the only other text on the page besides the location gate. Cold, legal, and the worst possible final impression on a first visit.
- **No auto-detection** — my browser and phone both know I'm in the US. Pre-filling the dropdown is a half-measure; just skip the gate entirely for returning users or use geolocation to confirm silently.
- **No skip or dismiss option** — there's no X, no "stay on global," no escape route. It's a hard wall.

## Recommendations

- **Auto-advance past the gate for returning US users** — if "Remember selection" was checked last session, skip directly to the homepage. Don't make me confirm again.
- **Use geolocation to pre-confirm silently** — detect US IP/locale, skip the modal entirely, drop me straight into the editorial homepage.
- **If the gate must exist, add a background image** — put the current season hero behind the form so I'm already seeing product while I tap CONTINUE. One visual hook is all it takes.
- **Move the privacy footer link out of this screen** — burying "DO NOT SELL MY DATA" as the only secondary text on a gate page reads as paranoid. Put it in the actual footer where it belongs.
