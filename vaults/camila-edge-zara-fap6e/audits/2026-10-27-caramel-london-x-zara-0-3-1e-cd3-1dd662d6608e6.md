# CARAMEL LONDON x ZARA
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a kids' collaboration lookbook: Zara x Caramel London, a British children's label. The art direction is the strongest part. Children are shot in muted English-country interiors and woods, and the cream background, script-italic copper type and autumn palette (corduroy, mustard florals, aubergine, chocolate knit) make it feel editorial and cohesive. It is seasonally on point for late October.
- It is a poor commercial email, though. There is no price, no offer and no product names. There is one tiny, low-contrast "View Collection" button, placed after five large images. The 4x3 product grid at the bottom has no labels, prices or individual links I can see. A parent has to scroll about 4,500px to find any product detail. As in the 10/18 "Kids | Discover what's new" send, the brand relies on mood alone.
- Verdict: a beautiful brand piece that does little to drive clicks. It suits a shopper already in Zara Kids, but it gives no reason to act today.

## What's working

- The hero co-brand lockup (Caramel London x Zara) over a staircase shot with two children is immediate and clear.
- The cohesive autumn palette and the warm cream and copper script give it a distinct, premium identity.
- The copy is short and evocative ("layering throughout, clever pieces designed to be worn together and mixed freely"), and it hints at how to wear the pieces.
- The 12-look grid at the bottom shows range across ages, from toddler to older child.
- The layout renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlap.

## What's weak

- No offer, price or urgency anywhere in the email.
- The single "View Collection" CTA is small, pale and thin-outlined, and it sits below roughly 60% of the scroll.
- The product grid has no names, prices or sizes, and nothing marks which items are clickable.
- The footer is cluttered with social links and "DOWNLOAD OUR APP", which pull attention away from the collection.
- The email is image-heavy, and the two body copy blocks are small italic script that is hard to read on mobile.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move a strong, high-contrast CTA ("Shop the Caramel edit") directly under the hero, and repeat it after the grid.
- 2. Add product names and prices under the grid tiles, and link each tile to its product page.
- 3. Add a concrete hook, such as "New in", "Limited edition" or "Sizes 3 months–14 years", to create urgency around a collab that may sell out.
- 4. Replace the script body copy with a legible sans-serif for the key lines.
- 5. Trim the footer so the app and social links no longer compete with the collection.

# Technical Audit: "CARAMEL LONDON x ZARA"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with Responsys-hosted images and tracking. It has one hard QA failure (an empty plain-text part) and no `alt` attributes on any of the roughly 27 flagged images. The HTML source was truncated, so link, address and footer checks are limited to what the QA data shows.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- QA probed 12 material links and skipped 3. It reported no broken or redirecting links, so link health is unverified rather than clean for the skipped 3.
- Links and the open pixel use Responsys redirects (e.g. `https://news.zara.com/pub/as?_ri_=X0Gzc2X%3D…`). The final destinations can't be read from the source, which limits the continuity checks in section 6.
- The tracking image `…/pub/as?_ri_=…` is flagged for missing alt. A 1×1 pixel should carry `alt=""`.
- Internal build comments are left in the delivered HTML:
  - `<!-- CAMPAIGN_CODE:zara;2026_10_27_Norte_all_caramel_27_10;US_en-->` and a second `…;en-->` tag. Two different locale codes are present in one send.
  - Spanish authoring notes: `<!-- Recogemos los países del PAISES_SKIP… -->` and `<!-- Copiar aquí -->`.
  - An unreplaced `<!--PREHEADER-->` placeholder.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `img_01.jpg` through `img_17.jpg` (including `img_02_en.jpg` and `img_04_en.jpg`) have no alt. So do the footer assets `latredes142.jpg` (twice), `rrss1b.jpg` through `rrss6b.jpg` (social icons), and `download_en_b.jpg` (app download). With images blocked, the content images, social links and app-download links have no text equivalent.
- **Invalid markup:** `<div style="mso-hide:all;">` wraps `<tr>` elements directly inside `<table width="640">`. A `div` is not a valid child of `table`, and browsers or clients may hoist it out of the table (foster-parenting). That can break the preheader row in some clients.
- **Viewport meta:** `maximum-scale=1.0; user-scalable=no` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4. The semicolon-separated syntax is also non-standard (commas are expected).
- **Mobile font classes:** `.font5`, `.font7` and `.font8` set 5px, 7px and 8px text on mobile. I can't tell from the truncated source whether they are applied to readable copy.
- **Fonts:** the Roboto Condensed Google Fonts `<link>` is loaded, but web fonts are unsupported in several major clients. No fallback stack was visible in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `${…}`) appear in the visible source, apart from the `<!--PREHEADER-->` placeholder noted above. The truncated body is not fully verified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is 0 chars.** There is no `text/plain` alternative, which hurts deliverability and accessibility.
- **[WARN] No physical address detected.** CAN-SPAM requires one. The footer appears to be assembled from dynamic image assets (`footers_dinamicos/images/…`), so the address may be rendered as an image or sit past the truncation point. It is unconfirmed either way. If it is image-only, it is not machine-readable and is invisible with images blocked.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes this may be an AgentMail relay artifact, so it is unconfirmed. If the headers are truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not captured.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The relay is the likely cause, not the sender.
- The QA run did not report whether an in-body unsubscribe link exists. It says one is always included in probing, but I can't confirm that from the data.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The destination URLs are truncated or hidden behind Responsys redirects, and QA reported no UTM or landing-page data. The only campaign identifier in the source is the HTML comment `2026_10_27_Norte_all_caramel_27_10`, which does not reach the site. No issues are confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a `text/plain` alternative (currently 0 chars) to the multipart message.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, social icons and the app-download badge. Set `alt=""` on the `/pub/as` tracking pixel.
3. Confirm a text-readable physical address in the footer.
4. Check the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers against the raw message from the sender, bypassing the relay. Likewise check SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
5. Fix the `<div style="mso-hide:all;">` wrapping `<tr>`. Use `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` conditional comments or move the `mso-hide` style onto the `<td>`.
6. Remove `user-scalable=no` and `maximum-scale=1.0` from the viewport meta.
7. Strip the build comments from production output: the two `CAMPAIGN_CODE` tags, the Spanish authoring notes and the `<!--PREHEADER-->` placeholder.
8. Re-run QA on the full, untruncated HTML. That would cover the 3 skipped links, UTM parameters on destination URLs, and footer and address verification.
