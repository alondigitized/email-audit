# Woman | This week’s selection
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-28**
## Executive summary

- This is the same skeleton I flagged in the 2026-08-21 "Woman | Explore our newest collections" send, and it's arguably even less differentiated this time — hero editorial shot, then eight straight category blocks (Knitwear, ZW Collection, Transitional Pieces, TRF Collection, Selected Pieces, The Item) with zero prices, zero % off, and category-tile links instead of product links. The one genuinely new element — a flagship store opening in Gangnam, Seoul — is buried at the very bottom below six unrelated product blocks, which is backwards: that's the only piece of copy in this email that isn't a repeat of last week.
- There's no offer, no urgency, no reason to click today versus next week. It reads as a lookbook, not a marketing email, and it's the fourth near-identical "Woman" send I've seen from this brand in the past month.
- Judgement: this is a low-effort repeat of a template that hasn't moved the needle in prior audits — score accordingly.

## What's working

- Hero image and wordmark render cleanly with no layout bugs.
- Section labels (KNITWEAR, ZW COLLECTION, TRF COLLECTION, etc.) give some scannable structure to an otherwise undifferentiated scroll.
- All product/category thumbnails load correctly — no broken images or overlapping text anywhere in the render.
- "THE ITEM" block has a distinct visual treatment (oversized wordmark overlay) that breaks the monotony slightly.

## What's weak

- No price, discount, or offer anywhere in the email — pure lookbook with no commercial hook.
- Eight category blocks with no clear single CTA — the eye has nowhere obvious to land first.
- The one actually newsworthy item (Gangnam flagship opening) is the last block before the footer, essentially unseen by anyone who doesn't scroll the full length.
- Subject "Woman | This week's selection" is generic boilerplate, identical in structure to five prior sends this month, with no indication of what's actually new.
- All links route to category/collection landing pages, not specific products — highest-friction path to conversion.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the Gangnam flagship opening as its own hero moment (or at minimum a top-of-fold callout) — it's the only genuinely novel content and it's currently wasted at the bottom.
- 2. Cut the eight-block category wall to 2-3 curated picks with visible pricing or a stated offer; a scroll this long with nothing to click toward guarantees drop-off.
- 3. Replace the generic subject with something that names the actual new thing (the store opening, or a specific piece from "The Item").
- 4. Add real preview text — right now the preview is functionally invisible/generic and wastes the highest-leverage real estate for a persona-specific hook.

- **Subject Alt B:** `New in Gangnam, plus this week's key pieces`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `A first look inside our newest flagship, and the pieces to shop now`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Blazers, transitional dresses, and knitwear — plus a new store opening`
