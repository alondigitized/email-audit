# Homepage snapshot · www.zara.com · 2026-09-30
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- I opened Zara and got a "SELECT YOUR LOCATION" gate instead of a homepage, so I haven't seen a single product, drop, or coat yet. It looks like a high-fashion, editorial brand with a moody, gallery-style campaign photo. Right now it's pushing only "pick your country and continue."

## What's working

- **The ZARA wordmark:** it's big, sharp and instantly recognizable, so I know I'm in the right place.
- **The location form:** it's short. "United States" and "English" are already filled in, so it's one tap on "CONTINUE."
- **The campaign photo:** a model in a headband and a soft striped, floral-print set, sitting in a vintage floral chair in front of faded wallpaper. It's moody and expensive-looking and feels like a drop editorial, which fits how I shop.
- **The "Remember selection" checkbox:** it's already ticked, so I won't see this gate again on my next visit.

## What's weak

- **This is a gate, not a homepage.** There's no nav, no categories, no "New In", no coats and no SRPLS/Studio, so nothing here tells me what's new.
- **The layout is unbalanced.** The logo floats top-left of center, the top right is empty, and the form is tucked into a narrow left column. On my phone I'd expect a tighter, stacked layout.
- **The photo is cropped at the bottom.** It's a big image with no product name, price or link, so it's pretty but I can't act on it.
- **The "CONTINUE" button is small and low-contrast.** It's a thin outline that's easy to miss next to the huge image.

## Recommendations

- Default the location from IP and show a small dismissible banner over the real homepage. Then the drop and new-arrivals rail are visible in the first screen.
- Put a headline and a button on the campaign photo, like "New Season Outerwear — Shop Coats." Right now the biggest visual on the page leads nowhere.
- If the gate has to stay, make "CONTINUE" a filled black button with white text, and center the form under the logo on mobile.
- Add one line under the form, like "Free returns" or "Join for early access to drops," so the gate does some work for me.
