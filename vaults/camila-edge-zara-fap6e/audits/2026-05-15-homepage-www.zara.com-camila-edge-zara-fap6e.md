# Homepage snapshot · www.zara.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- I opened Zara and got a location gate — that's the whole first screen. No product, no campaign, no editorial, nothing that tells me what Zara is pushing right now. The brand looks clean and editorial by instinct (that logo is *chef's kiss*), but I have zero idea what's happening this season because I'm staring at two dropdowns and a "CONTINUE" button before I've seen a single piece of clothing.

## What's working

- **The ZARA wordmark:** massive, confident, full-bleed — brand recognition is instant and I trust it
- **"Remember selection" checkbox:** small but smart; if I check it, I skip this wall next time
- **Location pre-populated as United States:** at least it guessed right so I don't have to hunt through a dropdown

## What's weak

- **The entire above-the-fold experience is a geo-gate:** I came to browse, not fill out a form — this is the first impression and it's dead air
- **Zero product or campaign visibility:** I don't know if SRPLS dropped something new, if there's a Studio collab, nothing
- **No loyalty or sign-in hook:** if I'm a returning customer, there's no shortcut — I'm treated the same as a bot hitting the site for the first time
- **"DO NOT SELL OR SHARE MY PERSONAL INFORMATION" at the very bottom:** feels like a legal footer dumped on a modal — weird placement for what should be a clean entry moment
- **The CONTINUE button is undersized:** on mobile, that button width is barely half the screen — my thumb has to aim

## Recommendations

- **Detect my location via IP and skip the gate entirely** for returning visitors — drop me straight into the US English storefront; only show this modal if detection fails or I'm explicitly switching regions
- **If the gate is unavoidable, layer in campaign imagery behind it** — blur or dim a seasonal editorial shot as the background so I get visual context while I tap CONTINUE
- **Add a "Sign in" link on this screen** so returning customers can authenticate and land on their personalized feed, bypassing the generic geo-flow
- **Resize the CONTINUE button to full-width** — this is a mobile-first brand, the primary action should be thumb-friendly edge-to-edge
