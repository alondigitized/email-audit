# Homepage snapshot · www.zara.com · 2026-05-27
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-27**
## Executive summary

- What I'm looking at isn't even the homepage — it's a location gate. Zara is making me confirm I'm in the United States and want English before I can see a single product. As someone who opens the app weekly and knows exactly what I'm looking for, this interstitial is pure dead air. Zero editorial. Zero drops. Zero reasons to stay.

## What's working

- **The "CONTINUE" CTA** is clearly labeled and centered — no ambiguity about how to get past this screen.
- **"Remember selection" checkbox** is present, which means repeat visitors can skip this gate next time — smart, but I had to notice it myself.
- **Page renders fast and clean** — no jank, no broken images, no layout chaos. Minimal by design.

## What's weak

- **The gate exists at all.** I've been to Zara.com before. I'm on a US IP. Forcing a location confirmation is friction Zara hasn't earned the right to put in front of me.
- **Complete absence of editorial content.** No campaign image, no drop teaser, no seasonal hook. SRPLS season is exactly when I'd be checking in — this is wasted real estate.
- **No personalization or recognition signal.** Returning user, no cookie pickup, no "Welcome back" — I'm treated like a first-time visitor from nowhere.
- **"DO NOT SELL OR SHARE MY PERSONAL INFORMATION"** is the only secondary copy on the page. That's a cold, bureaucratic note to land on before I've even seen a product.

## Recommendations

- **Skip the gate for returning visitors** — use geo-IP + stored cookie preference to route me directly to the US storefront. I should never see this screen after my first visit.
- **If the gate must exist, put something behind it.** Drop a campaign image or a single teaser headline ("New SRPLS arrivals" or the season name) so the brand voice lands even before I click Continue.
- **Surface a drop or editorial tease** where the blank white space currently is. That lower third is completely dead — it could be a cropped hero image or even just a typographic campaign headline.
- **Pre-check "Remember selection"** by default — the vast majority of US visitors will never need to change this. Opt-out, not opt-in.
