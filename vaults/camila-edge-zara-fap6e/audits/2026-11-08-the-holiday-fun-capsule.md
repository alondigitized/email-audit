# The Holiday Fun Capsule
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- This is Zara Kids' holiday lookbook, and it's the best-photographed Zara send in recent weeks. The cream background, red serif "the holiday fun capsule" wordmark and rounded red-outlined frames are cohesive. Gingerbread sweaters, an armchair shot with a candy cane, and a family on a garlanded staircase give it real seasonal warmth. The oversized "ho ho ho" in the middle is a nice editorial touch.
- It has no commerce at all. There's no price, no offer, no product name and no button. The only call to action is a small "discover the collection" line floating mid-scroll, and I can't tell what it links to. It's the same image-first formula as the 2026-11-01 "Cozy Fleece and Faux Shearling Edit" and the 2026-10-27 Caramel London send, so a regular subscriber gets the same experience with a different theme. For a parent shopping for the holidays, it's lovely but not actionable.
- Verdict: strong brand and mood, weak conversion. It's a 5 or 6 email that could be a 7 with a single clear CTA and one product anchor.

## What's working

- The hero typography is distinctive: red serif wordmark over a staircase family portrait, on-brand and instantly seasonal.
- The photography is warm and relatable. The kids' expressions, gingerbread knits and plaid pajamas read as real holiday wear.
- The layout has good rhythm. It alternates two-up, three-up and full-width frames, and "ho ho ho" gives a playful pause.
- The red-and-cream palette is consistent and holds together across the whole scroll.
- The closing product strip (home and kids collection) hints at gifting beyond clothes.

## What's weak

- There is no visible offer, price or product detail, so nothing gives a reason to click now.
- The "discover the collection" CTA is small red text, not a button. It sits in the middle, far from the hero.
- There's no urgency, no delivery cutoff and no gift-guide framing, even though it's a holiday send.
- The bottom strip has tiny tiles with a barely legible "discover home kids collection" box. The social links and "Download our app" block are generic filler.
- The images carry no product labels, so a parent can't tell which sweater is which.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real button under the hero, such as "Shop kids' holiday knits". Repeat it at the end of the scroll.
- 2. Label two or three hero products with name and price (the gingerbread sweater, the plaid pajamas), linked directly to their product pages.
- 3. Add a gifting or delivery-cutoff line so the season creates urgency.
- 4. Make the bottom strip larger and give it a clear label, so the home and kids tiles are worth tapping.
- 5. Cut the repetitive social and app block, or push it below the footer copy.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email passes few of the automated checks (38% pass rate, 1 FAIL). The main problems are a missing plain-text part, missing alt text on all 16 flagged images, and malformed table markup. The HTML provided is truncated, so the footer and all `href` values were not visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 12 material links were probed and 1 was skipped. The QA output does not say which link was skipped or whether any returned an error, so no broken links are confirmed.
- The `<img>` at `https://news.zara.com/pub/as?_ri_=…` is the Responsys open-tracking pixel. It has no `alt` and no visible size attributes. It should be `alt="" width="1" height="1"`.
- Link hrefs were not in the truncated source, so click-tracking wrapping and UTM presence could not be checked.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (16 images):**
  - `img_01.jpg` to `img_06.jpg` (the campaign content images under `/contentlibrary/campaigns/2026/11.noviembre/2026_11_08_Norte_se…`).
  - Footer images `latredes142.jpg` (twice), `rrss1b.jpg` to `rrss6b.jpg` (social icons), and `download_en_b.jpg` (app badge).
  - The tracking pixel `as`.
  - If these images are wrapped in links, the links have no accessible name. With images off, the content is blank.
- **Invalid table structure:** `<div style="mso-hide:all;">` sits directly inside `<table width="640">`, wrapping `<tr>`. A `div` is not a valid child of `table`. Clients parse this differently, and the `mso-hide` on the div may not apply to the row in Outlook.
- **Viewport meta is malformed and blocks zoom:** `content="width=device-width; initial-scale=1.0; maximum-scale=1.0; user-scalable=no;"` uses semicolons instead of commas. `user-scalable=no` and `maximum-scale=1.0` disable pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Link contrast:** the global rule `a { color: #999898 }` is about 2.8:1 on the `#ffffff` background, below the WCAG AA 4.5:1 minimum for text. This only matters where links have no inline color override.
- **Invalid CSS:** `.heightmobile { line-height: auto!important }` is not a valid value, so it is ignored.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns=…>` has no `lang`.
- **Leftover template markers:** `<!--PREHEADER-->` is an unreplaced placeholder. Internal Spanish comments are also in the shipped source ("Copiar aquí", "Recogemos los países del PAISES_SKIP…").
- The web font is loaded with an external `<link>` to fonts.googleapis.com. Gmail and Outlook strip this, so those clients fall back to system fonts. This is a minor issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `$(…)`) appear in the visible source. The only placeholder is `<!--PREHEADER-->`, noted above. The footer was not visible.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is 0 chars.** There is no `text/plain` alternative. This hurts deliverability scoring and accessibility.
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. This cannot be confirmed from the capture. If they are absent at send time, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules (one-click unsubscribe) are not met.
- **Physical address not detected.** The source is truncated before the footer. The footer comes from a dynamic block (`footers_dynamicos`), so this may be a false positive. Verify against the full HTML.
- **Authentication-Results missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, and QA attributes this to the relay. The From domain `news.zara.com` matches the asset host, which is consistent with a properly configured sending domain, but this is not verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- UTM parameters and landing-page alignment could not be assessed. Hrefs are not in the truncated source, and QA reported no landing-page results.
- The source has two different campaign codes: `2026_11_08_Norte_kids_holidayfun_08_11;US_en` and `2026_11_08_Norte_sec_weekly_08_11;en`. This looks like a template reused from another campaign (kids holiday fun, US_en, versus a weekly send, en). It may indicate mismatched campaign attribution in analytics.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a multipart `text/plain` alternative (currently 0 chars).
2. Add `alt` text to `img_01` to `img_06` and the app badge. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixel. Give the social icons descriptive alt, such as "Instagram".
3. Fix the table structure. Remove the `div` wrapper, or move `mso-hide:all` onto the `<tr>` or `<td>`.
4. Change the viewport meta to `width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0`, and drop `user-scalable=no` and `maximum-scale`.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` are present in the raw message, and that SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass. Capture the headers outside the relay.
6. Remove the duplicate `CAMPAIGN_CODE` comment, the unreplaced `<!--PREHEADER-->`, and the internal comments.
7. Raise the default link color above 4.5:1 contrast, and add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
8. Re-run the audit with untruncated HTML to check the footer address, the hrefs and their UTM parameters, and the identity of the skipped link.
