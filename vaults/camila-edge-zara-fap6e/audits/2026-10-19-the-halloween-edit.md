- Woman, Man and Kids are covered in one send, so it fits mixed households better than the single-category sends of 10/16–10/18.

## What's weak

- No offer, price or visible product CTA appears anywhere in the body.
- The only navigation is three tiny "Collection" labels at the bottom, after roughly 6,700px of scrolling.

- It is the fifth consecutive Zara lookbook with the same no-offer, no-button structure, so it will blur into the others.
