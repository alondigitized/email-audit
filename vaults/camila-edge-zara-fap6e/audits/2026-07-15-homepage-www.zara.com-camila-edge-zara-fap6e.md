# Homepage snapshot · www.zara.com · 2026-07-15
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-15**
## Executive summary

- I open Zara.com and get a bureaucratic location gate — a blank white page with two dropdowns and a "CONTINUE" button standing between me and the actual site. As someone who checks Zara on my phone every single week, I should not be seeing this. It reads less like a fashion brand and more like a visa application form. There is zero merchandise, zero energy, zero reason to feel excited about what's on the other side.

## What's working

- **"Remember selection" checkbox** — at least it's there. If it actually works, I won't see this gate next visit. One small mercy.
- **Clean render** — the page loads fast, no jank, no broken assets. It's doing the bare minimum, but it's doing it correctly.
- **"CONTINUE" button** — outlined, clear label, easy tap target on mobile. I know exactly what to press to escape this.

## What's weak

- **The gate itself** — a weekly returning shopper on mobile should never land here. My location is not a mystery. This is pure friction that kills any opening-second excitement I'd have tapping into the app.
- **Zero brand expression** — the logo is the only thing that tells me this is a fashion brand. The rest is a utility form. No color, no campaign image, no model, no collection tease. Nothing.
- **"DO NOT SELL OR SHARE MY PERSONAL INFORMATION"** footer — this is the only secondary text visible, and it reads like a compliance warning, not a brand. It's the last impression before I tap through and it's a weird, slightly cold note.
- **No reassurance about what's waiting** — if I'm going to sit through a gate, at least hint that there's a new drop or a sale on the other side. Right now, the gate promises nothing.
- **"Remember selection" checkbox appears partially-filled/ambiguous** — the checkbox icon looks like a small filled square, not a clear checked/unchecked state. On mobile this is confusing — did I opt in or not?

## Recommendations

- **Skip the gate for returning mobile visitors** — use geolocation or stored cookies to pre-select United States/English and forward me straight to the homepage. Zara should know where I am by now.
- **If the gate must exist, inject a campaign teaser behind it** — a full-bleed editorial image of the current collection, with the form overlaid as a bottom sheet. At least I'd see what I'm clicking toward.
- **Fix the checkbox state** — replace the ambiguous filled-square icon with a standard visible checkbox so I know with certainty whether "Remember selection" is active.
- **Add one line of copy** — something like "NEW IN: Summer Studio Drop" above the Continue button. Costs nothing, makes the gate feel intentional rather than accidental.
