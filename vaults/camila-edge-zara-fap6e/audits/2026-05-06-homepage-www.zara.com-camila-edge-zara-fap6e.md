# Homepage snapshot · www.zara.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- I opened Zara's URL and hit a location gate — this isn't the homepage, it's a country/language selector with nothing behind it yet. No hero, no campaign imagery, no product, no editorial. On mobile, this is a cold stop before I've seen a single thing the brand wants to sell me. Zara positions itself as a high-fashion fast-mover and this first screen communicates none of that.

## What's working

- **Location pre-fill** — "United States" and "English" are already selected, so if I'm a return visitor the form is at least not blank.
- **Clean typography** — the Zara wordmark is large, confident, and renders crisply at this viewport.
- **"Remember selection" checkbox** — small friction-reducer for future visits, even if it looks broken visually.

## What's weak

- **Zero fashion content above the fold** — I came for new drops and I got a dropdown menu. There's no reason this gate can't sit behind a full editorial hero.
- **Broken checkbox icon** — the "Remember selection" checkbox renders as a filled black square rather than a real checkbox. On mobile that reads as a bullet, not a control.
- **Half the screen is white void** — below the "CONTINUE" button there's a huge empty white block before the CCPA footer link. Wasted real estate even on a gate screen.
- **"DO NOT SELL OR SHARE MY PERSONAL INFORMATION"** is the last thing I read — the legal footer as the page's closing statement is a weirdly cold ending.
- **No skip or "I'm returning" path** — no shortcut for people who have been here before. I have to click through every time if I don't have the cookie.

## Recommendations

- **Overlay the gate on a full-bleed editorial image** — put the location form as a modal over whatever the current campaign hero is, so I get visual context even before confirming my region.
- **Make the "Remember selection" checkbox a real visible checkbox** — fix the glyph rendering (the filled square looks like a broken asset on mobile).
- **Trim the dead space** — the white gap between the button and the CCPA link is dead real estate; either collapse it or surface a thin brand tagline.
- **Auto-detect and pre-confirm for return visitors** — if I've been here before, skip me past the gate entirely and only show this on locale mismatch.
