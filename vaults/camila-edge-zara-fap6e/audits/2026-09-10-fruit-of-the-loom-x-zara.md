# FRUIT OF THE LOOM™ x ZARA
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-10**
## Executive summary

- This is genuinely a different creature from the last six Zara sends I've flagged — a real co-branded collaboration (Fruit of the Loom™ x Zara) with a distinct editorial voice, a defined "'90s preppy" narrative, and mixed-generation casting (women, men, kids, baby) instead of the usual single-category lookbook scroll. The color-blocked orange hero, the fruit motif worked into the wordmark, and the actual prose paragraphs explaining the collaboration's inspiration set it apart from the copy-free product dumps Zara Woman/Man/Kids have been running all August.
- That said, it still ends in the exact same failure mode: after the editorial section, it collapses into an undifferentiated grid of 14 product shots with zero labels beyond "WOMAN," "MAN," "KIDS," "BABY" and a single "VIEW" button sitting alone above the entire grid — not tied to any specific product. There's no price, no discount, no "shop the collection" per section, and the CTA-to-product ratio is one button for fourteen images.
- Compared to 2026-09-03's "0FR. PARIS and ZARA" collab (5/10) — which had similar strong concept execution — this one leans harder into the '90s Americana story but repeats the same structural flaw: gorgeous concept, weak conversion mechanics. Call it a 5/10 — best creative concept in weeks, but still functionally a lookbook, not a shoppable email.

## What's working

- Real narrative copy ("This collection draws inspiration from the heritage, authenticity, and cultural recognition...") — a rare departure from Zara's usual caption-free scroll.
- Distinct visual identity: orange color-blocking, fruit-themed wordmark treatment, varsity/collegiate styling cues that feel on-brand for a Fruit of the Loom tie-in.
- Multi-generational casting (women, men, kids, baby) signals this is a true family collection, not just another Woman drop.
- Clean image quality throughout — no broken images, no overlapping text, no render bugs.

## What's weak

- Single "VIEW" button for the entire email, floating above 14 product images with no indication of what it links to.
- Zero price or offer information anywhere — this reads as a lookbook, not a marketing email with a conversion goal.
- Product grid has no per-item CTAs or labels beyond broad category tags (WOMAN/MAN/KIDS/BABY) — no way to jump to a specific piece.
- Very long single-column scroll (14+ full-width images) with no visual break or "shop now" reinforcement partway through.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a CTA under every 2-3 products (or at minimum per section: Women/Men/Kids/Baby), not one lonely "VIEW" button floating above the whole grid — that's the single biggest lift available here.
- 2. Surface a price point or "new arrivals from $X" line — pure lookbook copy with zero pricing signal kills urgency and click intent.
- 3. Break the wall of 14 images into labeled subsections with mini-headers ("WOMEN'S COLLECTION," "FOR THE FAMILY") so scanners get a wayfinding cue instead of an undifferentiated scroll.
- 4. Move a stronger CTA higher, right after the hero paragraph, so mobile users who don't scroll the full length still get a clear path to shop.
