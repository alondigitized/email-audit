# Homepage snapshot · www.expedia.com · 2026-05-31
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-31**
## Executive summary

- Expedia is presenting me with a clean, functional search-first homepage that's clearly in World Cup mode right now — the "Your summer of soccer" promo is the seasonal play above the fold. What immediately catches my eye is the "Bundle & Save" option baked right into the search form, which honestly feels made for me. It's a utility-forward brand doing the right thing structurally, but it's leaving loyalty benefits and concrete dollar savings completely off the table in this viewport.

## What's working

- **"Bundle & Save" option in the search form** — this is exactly why I'm on Expedia and not Google Flights. Seeing it baked into the default search experience before I even type a destination is the right move.
- **Pre-populated dates (Jun 1 – Wed, Jun 3)** — I like that the form is already thinking about the near term. Reduces friction when I'm in Sunday-morning research mode.
- **"Your summer of soccer" promo with "See all deals"** — the timing is smart. FIFA World Cup is weeks away and flight/hotel bundles for game-week travel are exactly the kind of deal I scan for.
- **Blue "Search" button** — high contrast, full-width on mobile, impossible to miss. Good.
- **Flights / Cars / Pack[ages] icon tabs** — the category row with illustrated icons is quick to parse. I can see "Packages" is there (even if clipped) and I'd tap it first.

## What's weak

- **No One Key Cash / loyalty balance visible** — I have rewards sitting in my account and I want to see that number the moment I land. It would immediately anchor me. It's nowhere in this viewport.
- **"Bundle & Save*" asterisk** — that asterisk without a visible explanation feels like a little trap. I notice these things.
- **Left-side text clipping** ("to?", "lers, 1 room") — the layout is getting cut off on the left edge. On mobile that's a render bug that would make me wonder if the site loaded right.
- **"Your summer of soccer" copy** — I appreciate the seasonal push but I'm planning a long weekend with two kids under 10, not a soccer trip. There's zero family-travel framing anywhere above the fold.
- **No refundable/flexible booking callout** — I never book non-refundable. If I don't see "Free cancellation" or "Book now, pay later" in the first screen, I'm already anxious.
- **No specific offer value** — "See all deals" without a number (20% off, $200 back, whatever) is a soft CTA that I'll mentally deprioritize.

## Recommendations

- **Surface my One Key Cash balance in the header** — even a logged-out prompt like "Sign in to see your One Key Cash" would remind me why I picked Expedia over Booking. I need that nudge.
- **Add "Free cancellation available" or a refund-policy badge near the Search button** — one line, no design cost, and it removes the biggest anxiety point for someone like me who always books refundable.
- **Swap or supplement the soccer promo with a family bundle tile** — "Pack the whole family" with a specific dollar offer (e.g., "Save $150 on flight + hotel + car bundles") would speak directly to the biggest Expedia bundle use case.
- **Fix the left-side clipping on mobile** — the destination and travelers fields are getting cropped. It looks broken and erodes confidence before I've even started searching.
