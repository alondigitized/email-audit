# Homepage snapshot · www.expedia.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Okay, first impression: Expedia goes straight to work — it opens on the search widget, not a mood board. As someone who opens the app every Sunday morning to obsess over trip logistics, I appreciate the no-nonsense approach. Right now it's pushing Memorial Day getaways and I can see the "Bundle & Save" option in the search form, which is literally my love language. This looks like a functional planner's tool, not a dreamy travel magazine.

## What's working

- **"Bundle & Save" checkbox in the search form** — the moment I see this, I know Expedia speaks my dialect. Bundling is the whole reason I'm here, not Hotels.com or Booking.
- **Memorial Day promo banner** — "up to 20% off" on a holiday weekend I'm actively planning for lands perfectly. The dates pre-filled as "May 22 – Mon, May 25" tell me Expedia knows the weekend is right now.
- **Big blue Search button** — no ambiguity, zero friction to start a search. It's the whole point of the visit.
- **Tabs for Flights, Cars, Packages** — all three of my booking categories are one tap away right at the top.

## What's weak

- **Left-side text clipping** — "Where to?", "Bundle & Save", and the Memorial Day headline are all cut off at the left edge. If a real user sees this, they may not even notice the bundle option exists. Critical UX failure.
- **Zero loyalty surface area** — I have One Key Cash sitting in my account and the page doesn't acknowledge me at all. A returning member gets the same cold search widget as a first-time visitor.
- **No refundability signal** — I never book non-refundable. There's no mention of "free cancellation" or "refundable rates" anywhere above the fold. That friction is a real conversion blocker for me.
- **No people/lifestyle imagery** — the only visuals are flat illustrated icons (airplane, car, suitcase). Nothing that makes me *feel* the vacation I'm planning for my kids.

## Recommendations

- **Fix the left-viewport clip immediately** — the "Bundle & Save" option and the Memorial Day headline are both cut off. This is the highest-priority visual bug; ship a fix this week.
- **Swap flat icons for a Memorial Day family travel lifestyle photo** — a hero image of a family loading a car or arriving at a hotel would connect instantly with me and stop the scroll.
- **Surface One Key Cash balance in the search header** — even a small "You have $X One Key Cash" chip near the search widget would make me feel seen and push me toward booking rather than comparison-shopping on Hotels.com.
- **Add "Includes free cancellation" as a sub-label under the Search button** — one line of copy removes my biggest hesitation without requiring any product change.
