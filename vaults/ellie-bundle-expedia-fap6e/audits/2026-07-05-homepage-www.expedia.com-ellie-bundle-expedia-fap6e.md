# Homepage snapshot · www.expedia.com · 2026-07-05
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-05**
## Executive summary

- I opened Expedia on a Sunday morning with my coffee and got hit with a bot wall before I ever saw a single flight deal. Instead of the homepage, I'm staring at a CAPTCHA slider asking me to "show us your human side" — which, honestly, is a bit rich when all I wanted to do was bundle a flight and hotel for our beach trip. This isn't a homepage review. It's a friction audit, and Expedia failed at step zero.

## What's working

- The Expedia logo and wordmark at the top are clean and recognizable — at least I know I'm in the right place.
- The "Submit feedback" link is a small saving grace; at least there's an escape hatch if the CAPTCHA fails.
- The IP address disclosure (`75.140.9.0`) is oddly transparent — I appreciate knowing why I'm stuck, even if I don't want to be stuck here.

## What's weak

- The entire homepage is blocked — I see zero travel content, zero deals, zero search UI. As a high-intent Sunday-morning trip researcher, I bounced mentally the second this loaded.
- "Show us your human side" is a tone-deaf headline for a frustrated mom trying to plan a family vacation. The copy tries to be cute and misses badly.
- The CAPTCHA UI looks like it's from 2015 — a slider widget with no visual polish, totally mismatched from Expedia's usual app-quality design.
- The error rationale ("browsing faster than expected") is accusatory toward a real human user. I'm on a phone — I tapped once. That's it.
- No fallback path offered (app deep-link, alternative login, customer service number). I'm just stuck.
- The session/device ID shown at the bottom (`3bc551a3-2813-4d23-1439-ed7605aef07f`) is meaningless to me as a consumer and adds visual noise.

## Recommendations

- **Add an app-store redirect banner** — if the mobile browser session gets flagged, immediately offer "Open in the Expedia app" as an escape; bundle-builders live in the app anyway.
- **Soften the CAPTCHA copy** — replace "We can't tell if you're a human or a bot" with something like "One quick tap and you're in" to reduce the accusatory tone.
- **Surface a support number or chat link prominently** — "Having problems? Chat with us" above the fold instead of burying "Submit feedback" in small text at the bottom.
- **Pre-authenticate returning One Key members via cookie/token** so loyalty users never see this wall — if I'm logged in on my phone, this shouldn't happen at all.
