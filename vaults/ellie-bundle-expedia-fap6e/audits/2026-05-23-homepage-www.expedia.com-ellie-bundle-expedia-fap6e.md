# Homepage snapshot · www.expedia.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is actually more functional than I expected for a Sunday-morning mobile open — the search widget is front and center, "Bundle & Save" is literally the first hook below the form fields, and the Memorial Day promo is timely. But it reads like a utility dashboard, not like a brand that wants me to dream about a beach vacation with my kids. It's pushing Memorial Day weekend deals right now, which is exactly on-calendar, but there's zero warmth to it.

## What's working

- **"Bundle & Save*" checkbox/link in the search form** — this is literally why I'm here. Seeing it inline with the hotel search means I don't have to hunt for the Packages tab.
- **"Packages" tab in the top nav** — it's the third icon (truncated as "Pack" in the viewport), right next to Flights and Cars. One tap to get to my workflow.
- **Memorial Day promo banner** — "up to 20%" with "deals on stays and more" is timely and I would click "Book now" to at least see what's in scope.
- **Pre-populated dates** — the date field already shows May 24–May 26, which matches the long weekend I'd actually be researching. That's a smart or lucky default that reduces friction immediately.
- **Blue "Search" button** — large, high-contrast, unmistakable. No confusion about what to tap.

## What's weak

- **Left-edge layout overflow** — the text on every line is cut off on the left ("to?", "ay 24 - Tue, May 26", "lers, 1 room"). This is a visible render/overflow bug on mobile and it makes me mildly nervous about whether my search inputs are being captured correctly.
- **Zero lifestyle imagery** — no beach, no family at a pool, no hotel lobby. Just icons and a form. It doesn't make me *want* to go anywhere.
- **No One Key Cash hook** — I have accumulated One Key Cash on this account and there's nothing up here reminding me of my balance or telling me how much I'd earn on a bundle. That's free motivation they're leaving on the table.
- **No refund policy mention** — I don't book non-refundable. There's nothing here about free cancellation, flexible rates, or refundable options. That's a blocker for me before I even start a search.
- **"Book now" is a text link, not a button** — the Memorial Day banner CTA is an underlined link, not a button. On a phone, that's a smaller tap target and it signals lower priority than the blue Search button.

## Recommendations

- **Fix the mobile overflow bug immediately** — text clipping on the left edge of every search field looks broken. On a flagship homepage this erodes trust before I've typed a single character.
- **Add a One Key Cash balance chip above the search form** — even just "You have $47 One Key Cash" in a small yellow badge would make me feel seen and keep me in the app instead of opening Booking.com in another tab.
- **Inject a "Free cancellation available" badge or filter** into the search widget or directly below it — one line, right there. I need to know refundable rates exist before I bother searching.
- **Swap the flat icon nav for a "Bundles" hero moment** — show me a family loading a rental car outside a hotel, with copy like "Flight + hotel + car: your Memorial Day sorted." That image does more work than three cartoon icons.
