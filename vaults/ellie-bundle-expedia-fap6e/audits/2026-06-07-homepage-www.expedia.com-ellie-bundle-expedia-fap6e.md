# Homepage snapshot · www.expedia.com · 2026-06-07
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-07**
## Executive summary

- I literally cannot get to the Expedia homepage — I hit a bot wall instead. The entire viewport is a CAPTCHA slider ("Show us your human side...") with a cold, accusatory message telling me my browser "caught their attention." As a real Sunday-morning mom on her phone just trying to plan a summer trip, this is a brutal first impression. Expedia is running hard enough bot-blocking that it's catching legit customers.

## What's working

- The Expedia wordmark and logo are clearly rendered in the top-left — at least brand recognition lands in the header
- The "Submit feedback" link at the bottom gives me somewhere to go if I'm frustrated — small mercy

## What's weak

- **The bot wall itself** — I'm a real person on my phone on a Sunday morning and I'm being told my behavior "caught their attention." That's accusatory copy directed at their own customers.
- **No fallback content** — there's zero homepage content behind the CAPTCHA; the whole screen is locked
- **The IP blame line** — "There is a robot on the same network (IP 75.140.9.0) as you" is technical jargon that means nothing to me and just makes me feel vaguely guilty for sharing WiFi
- **Tone is terrible** — "We can't tell if you're a human or a bot" is one of the most off-putting things a travel brand can say to me when I'm just trying to book a family vacation
- **No estimated resolution** — no "refresh and try again," no alternative path, no customer service number

## Recommendations

- **Rewrite the verification copy** — swap "We can't tell if you're a human or a bot" for something warmer: "Just a quick check before we get you to the deals." Same function, 10x less alienating.
- **Surface a low-friction escape hatch** — a visible "Try again" button or a link to call/chat support so customers don't just bounce to Booking.com
- **Tune the bot-detection thresholds for mobile Safari** — if a mobile browser on a residential IP is tripping this on a Sunday morning, the sensitivity is miscalibrated and they're burning real conversion
- **Keep the logo and a hero tagline in the background** — even locked behind a CAPTCHA, showing "Bundles from $X" or the One Key Cash badge would maintain brand context and remind me *why* I'm putting up with this friction
