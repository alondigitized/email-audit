# Homepage snapshot · www.warbyparker.com · 2026-08-08
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-08**
## Executive summary

- This reads like a design-forward eyewear brand leaning hard into a "Fall 26" seasonal drop, with a collegiate, slightly younger energy than I'd expect for someone my age. It's pushing the new fall collection and a first-order contacts discount, with the $95 starting price doing exactly what it should — grabbing my eye before I even scroll.

## What's working

- **"Premium eyewear, starting at $95" top banner** — this is the exact number I care about. It's the first thing I read and it confirms the floor I trust.
- **The trust strip under the hero** (Free shipping, Free 30-day returns, Vision benefits accepted) — these are the practical details that make me comfortable ordering frames sight-unseen.
- **"Start with a quiz" CTA** — as a repeat customer who cycles through home try-on every year, a quiz shortcut to relevant frames is a low-friction way back in.
- **Clean, uncluttered layout** — nothing overlapping, nothing broken, easy to scan on my phone.

## What's weak

- **No mention of home try-on anywhere above the fold** — that's literally my whole ritual with this brand and it's invisible on the homepage.
- **The models read as college-age** ("See fall semester through fresh eyes" copy, backpack/campus vibe) — I'm 30, not a freshman, and nothing here signals "for guys like you."
- **No sign that I'm a returning/loyal customer** — no "welcome back," no recently-viewed frames, no rewards balance. I've bought here before and the site doesn't seem to know it.
- **No blue-light coating callout** — that's an add-on I always tack on, and it's nowhere in this above-the-fold real estate.

## Recommendations

- Add a "Try 5 frames free at home" banner or button near the hero — it's Warby's signature hook and it's currently buried.
- Swap one of the two lifestyle panels ("fresh semester") for a slightly older, more established-looking model to widen the age range being represented.
- Surface a "Welcome back, reorder your favorites" or recently-viewed-frames module for logged-in repeat shoppers like me.
- Call out blue-light coating as an add-on chip somewhere near the eyeglasses CTA — it's a common upsell you're leaving on the table.
