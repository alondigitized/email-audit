# Homepage snapshot · www.warbyparker.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- First thing I see when I land on warbyparker.com? A cookie consent modal eating the entire screen. There's a gorgeous shot of two tortoise frames peeking out behind it — dark navy acetate on top, warm reddish-brown underneath — which is literally my aesthetic, but I can't engage with any of it until I deal with the legal wall. The nav is clean and the brand marks are right, but the homepage is functionally inaccessible at first load.

## What's working

- **Tortoise frame imagery**: The two frames peeking above the modal — one dark navy, one warm burgundy — are exactly the aesthetic I'd put in a home try-on box. If I can get past the modal, I'll want to look at those.
- **Top nav is tight**: "Eyeglasses / Sunglasses / Contacts / Style quiz" — four tabs, no bloat, I can get to frames in one tap the second the modal is gone.
- **"Style quiz" tab**: That's smart placement for someone like me who cycles try-ons yearly and sometimes needs a nudge toward a new shape.

## What's weak

- **The cookie modal blocks everything**: I mean *everything* — the hero headline, any CTA, any offer, any product rail. This is the entire above-the-fold experience and it's a legal disclaimer.
- **No returning-customer signal**: I've done home try-on multiple times. There's zero acknowledgment — no "resume your try-on," no loyalty balance, no "hey, your prescription might need updating."
- **No pricing signal whatsoever**: The $95 starting price is Warby's biggest differentiator and I can't see it anywhere. Even a "frames starting at $95" strip above the nav would do it.
- **No urgency or reason to act now**: No limited collection callout, no seasonal push, nothing that says "this week specifically."

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the cookie modal by default on return visits** (or at minimum shrink it to a bottom bar) — a returning customer should land on the hero, not a privacy disclaimer.
- **Add a persistent "Frames from $95" strip above the fold** — that price point is the whole value story; surface it immediately before the hero even loads.
- **Wire a "Your try-on history" or "Shop your saved frames" CTA** for logged-in returning users — I'm back here annually and I want to feel recognized, not like a first-time visitor.
- **Swap one of the static frame shots for a lifestyle frame** — a person actually wearing the tortoise rounds in a coffee shop or at a desk would land harder than product-on-surface for someone in my cohort.
