# Homepage snapshot · www.warbyparker.com · 2026-05-30
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-30**
## Executive summary

- Warby Parker is doing its clean, confident-brand thing — navy header, tight nav, lifestyle group shot of young people rocking shades outdoors. Very on-vibe for me aesthetically. But the first real interaction I get is a cookie consent modal that eats half the screen, which means I literally cannot see whatever hero copy or CTA they spent money on. Not the welcome-back energy I was hoping for as someone who does this home try-on cycle every single year.

## What's working

- **Clean nav bar** — "Eyeglasses / Sunglasses / Contacts / Style quiz" is exactly what I need; I can tap to my category in one shot
- **Lifestyle group hero** — fun, diverse, outdoorsy crew wearing shades; it hits the right tone for who Warby Parker is targeting and I feel represented
- **Wordmark and brand restraint** — the navy/white color palette reads premium-but-accessible, which is exactly the $95 magic I believe in
- **"Style quiz" tab** — surfacing that in the top nav is smart; it's their best acquisition tool and I've used it before

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal kills everything** — it's a wall. Half the viewport is a consent dialog, blocking the hero CTA and any promotional messaging. For a returning user like me, this is an immediate friction spike.
- **Zero Home Try-On signal above the fold** — this is Warby's single biggest differentiator. It should be in the hero or a sticky banner. I see nothing about it.
- **No price anchoring** — I care deeply that the $95 tier exists. Not a hint of it visible. If I were a first-timer I'd have no idea what I'm walking into.
- **No blue-light or lens upgrade hook** — that's literally how they make money on me every cycle. It's invisible on this screen.
- **No returning-customer recognition** — no "your try-on history," no rewards nudge, nothing that says they know I've been here before

## Recommendations

- **Auto-dismiss or replace the cookie modal with a slim banner** — a full-screen consent wall on mobile is a bounce trigger; a one-line footer bar or a small badge keeps compliance without eating the hero
- **Put "Home Try-On" in the primary nav or hero CTA** — it's the reason people like me choose Warby over a random DTC brand; it should be impossible to miss in the first 3 seconds
- **Add a sticky promo strip above the nav** — something like "5 frames. 5 days. Free Home Try-On." anchors the value instantly without scrolling
- **Surface a "Welcome back" or "Your saved frames" module** — I have an account, I've done this before; a personalized re-entry hook (even a simple "Pick up where you left off") would dramatically increase my engagement speed
