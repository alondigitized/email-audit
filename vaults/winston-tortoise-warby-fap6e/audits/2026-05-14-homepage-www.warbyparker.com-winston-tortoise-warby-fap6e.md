# Homepage snapshot · www.warbyparker.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- First thing I see is a cookie banner eating the bottom half of the screen — which, fine, legally necessary, but it's a rough first impression when I'm trying to remember why I love these frames. Underneath it, the hero is two stacked round frames, one tortoise, one dark navy, no copy, no price, no CTA. Warby still reads like the brand I trust and come back to every year, but the homepage isn't doing me any favors right now.

## What's working

- **The nav strip** — "Eyeglasses / Sunglasses / Contacts / Style quiz" is clean, one tap to where I want to go, no guessing
- **The hero product shot** — two round frames stacked, one obviously tortoiseshell, one navy; as a returning buyer I immediately recognize the aesthetic and feel like I'm in the right place
- **Logo placement and brand color** — the deep navy nav bar is unmistakably Warby, zero brand confusion

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal as first impression** — blocks the lower two-thirds of the screen; any CTA, price point, or home try-on hook is invisible until I dismiss it
- **Zero copy above the fold** — I have no idea what Warby is pushing right now; no headline, no seasonal angle, no hook
- **No home try-on mention** — this is literally my annual ritual with this brand and there's zero prompt for it
- **No price anchoring** — the $95 starting price is the whole reason I keep coming back; it's not here
- **No returning-customer signal** — I've done home try-on multiple times and there's nothing that acknowledges I exist

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the cookie modal by default on return visits** — or at minimum shrink it to a slim bottom bar so the hero copy and CTA are still readable; losing the entire value prop to a consent overlay is a conversion killer
- **Add a headline to the hero** — even something like "Frames from $95. Try five at home." tells me everything I need in one line
- **Surface the home try-on CTA above the fold** — this is Warby's single biggest differentiator; it should be a visible button, not buried after I scroll past the modal
- **Add a returning-shopper hook in the nav or hero** — "Continue your try-on" or a visible account-state indicator for logged-in users would make me feel like the brand knows me
