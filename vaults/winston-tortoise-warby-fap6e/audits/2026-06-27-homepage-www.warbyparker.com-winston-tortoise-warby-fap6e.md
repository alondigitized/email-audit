# Homepage snapshot · www.warbyparker.com · 2026-06-27
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-27**
## Executive summary

- I open Warby Parker and before I can see literally anything, I'm hit with a full-screen cookie consent wall. The brand is trying to come across as clean and considered — I can see the classic navy navigation bar, the familiar "Eyeglasses / Sunglasses / Contacts / Style quiz" strip — but I genuinely cannot read the hero headline, the CTA, or any promotional copy because a "Website Cookies" modal is sitting over all of it. What I can make out in the background is a pair of dark green frames against a white surface, which honestly looks nice, but I'm working around a legal wall to even notice it.

## What's working

- **Navigation strip** — "Eyeglasses / Sunglasses / Contacts / Style quiz" is exactly how I think about Warby Parker. One tap to what I need; no friction there.
- **Dark green frames in the hero** — What little I can see of the product shot looks genuinely nice. Dark, sculptural frames on a clean white surface. That's my wheelhouse as a four-frames guy who gravitates toward the tortoise-adjacent palette.
- **Nav bar clarity** — Hamburger, logo, account, cart. Mobile-native layout that doesn't waste space. Account icon matters to me because I have order history here.

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal eating the entire viewport** — This is the whole ballgame. I cannot see the hero headline, the hero CTA, any active promotion, pricing, or campaign context. As a returning customer who knows this brand cold, I'm annoyed. As a new visitor, I'd bounce.
- **No visible price signal** — Warby's $95 entry point is their biggest weapon against every other eyewear brand. It's not visible anywhere. That floor price is why I'm here and they're not leading with it.
- **"Manage Preferences" is the only CTA on screen** — Instead of "Shop Glasses" or "Start Home Try-On," the one button I can tap is a cookie settings dialog. That's a terrible first impression.
- **No home try-on hook above the fold** — HTF is their signature product differentiator and the thing that's brought me back every cycle. I see nothing about it.
- **No loyalty or account recognition** — I'm a repeat buyer. Nothing says "welcome back" or surfaces my account.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss or shrink the cookie modal** — At minimum, make it a bottom banner, not a full-modal overlay. Let the homepage breathe. Most browsers send consent signals already; the hard block is aggressive for a low-stakes eyewear brand.
- **Pin the $95 price point in the nav strip or a thin promo bar** — "Frames starting at $95, including prescription" above the nav category row would remind every visitor why Warby exists. Mobile real estate is tight but this line earns its pixel.
- **Add a "Start Home Try-On" CTA to the top nav** — If "Style quiz" gets a slot, Home Try-On deserves one. It's the conversion engine for returning shoppers like me and it's entirely absent above the fold.
- **Surface a returning-customer signal** — Even a subtle "Welcome back — your last try-on was X" or a rewards balance indicator under the account icon would make me feel like this site knows me.
