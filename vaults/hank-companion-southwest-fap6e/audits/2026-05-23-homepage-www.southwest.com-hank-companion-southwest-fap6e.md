# Homepage snapshot · www.southwest.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Southwest comes in hot with a $69 fare hero and that "Wanna land a low fare?" headline that might as well have my name on it. This is a budget airline leaning into exactly what it does best — cheap fares, zero pretense, get-to-the-search-form energy. What it's pushing right now is flight acquisition, specifically off a Burbank origination, which tells me there's some fare sale happening for summer 2026.

## What's working

- **"Wanna land a low fare?" headline** — Southwest's conversational brand voice is exactly the right register for me. Not corporate. Not "Discover Possibilities." Just straight talk.
- **$69 price point as the visual anchor** — That number is enormous on mobile. I see it before I read anything. That's good information architecture.
- **"Book now" button in signature Southwest yellow** — No ambiguity. One tap to get moving.
- **Rapid Rewards® in the nav** — I know it's there when I need it. Doesn't clutter the hero.
- **Tab bar (Flights / Vacations / Hotels / Cars / Cruises)** — Surface-level wayfinding is solid. I know exactly where I am and what I can do from this screen.

## What's weak

- **No "Bags Fly Free" hero mention** — That is Southwest's single biggest differentiator over United, Delta, and American. It should be *on this page* as its own callout, not buried as a credit card acquisition perk in the top banner. Hank's love language is two free bags and Southwest is hiding it.
- **Companion Pass zero acknowledgment** — I have a Companion Pass. This homepage treats me like a stranger. No logged-in prompt, no "your Companion Pass is active" badge, nothing. Delta would at least say "welcome back, Medallion member."
- **Banner is credit card acquisition, not loyalty reward** — "Earn 50,000 points + first checked bag is free" is for someone who doesn't have the card yet. I do. This banner is noise for me.
- **47 travel days with restrictions** — The $69 fare has substantial asterisk copy visible right there in the hero. "Seats/days/mkts lmtd; restr./excl. apply" with only 47 days of availability makes this feel like a teaser rate, not a genuine fare. It may be true, but it erodes trust.
- **No no-change-fee callout** — Southwest's no-fee change/cancel policy is a massive conversion driver for families booking uncertain summer travel. It's nowhere on this screen.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Bags Fly Free — Always." strip** directly below the hero fare, separate from any credit card offer. That's the conversion line Southwest keeps burying.
- **Surface Companion Pass / Rapid Rewards balance for logged-in users** — Swap the "Log in" button to show "Hi, Hank — 47,000 pts" for anyone with an active session. Huge retention signal, zero implementation complexity.
- **Replace credit card acquisition banner with a fare deals rail** — Show me 3–4 routes and prices from my home airport instead of pitching me a card I already have.
- **Add a "No change fees, ever" trust line** near the "Book now" button — one short line, massive conversion impact for family bookers who aren't 100% sure of their travel dates.
