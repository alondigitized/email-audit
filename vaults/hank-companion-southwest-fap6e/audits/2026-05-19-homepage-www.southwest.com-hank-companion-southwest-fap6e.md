# Homepage snapshot · www.southwest.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Southwest is doing what Southwest does — loud sale headline, a fare number front and center, and zero pretense. The "Tide's out, summer sale's in" hook with a $69 one-way is exactly the kind of subject-line energy I scan for in my inbox, and it landed fine on the homepage too. This feels like a brand that knows its audience: price hunters who already have the app and the Rapid Rewards card in their wallet.

## What's working

- **$69 fare callout** — big, bold, black numerals. I read the price before anything else, which is exactly how I scan a deal. This works.
- **"Tide's out, summer sale's in" headline** — punchy, seasonal, reads like a tweet not a press release. My generation appreciates this.
- **Yellow "Book now" button** — Southwest's brand color doing real work. Unmistakable CTA, thumb-sized, centered.
- **Sale date window visible** — "Book 5/19–5/21" means I know the clock is ticking without a gimmicky countdown. Respects my intelligence.
- **Top nav strip** — FLIGHT | HOTEL | CAR | VACATIONS | CRUISES plus SPECIAL OFFERS and RAPID REWARDS® are all one tap away. No hamburger menu hiding the goods.
- **"NEW!" badge on Flights tab** — something changed; I'll at least look.

## What's weak

- **No Companion Pass mention anywhere** — this is Southwest's most powerful loyalty hook and their most loyal customers' favorite flex. For a homepage, especially during a sale window, zero Companion Pass visibility is a miss. I'm a Companion Pass holder and the homepage doesn't care.
- **"Earn 50,000 points + first checked bag is free" banner** — this is a credit card pitch, not a member benefit. It's aimed at converting someone new, not rewarding someone like me who already has both.
- **"From Burbank, CA"** — hardcoded departure city feels weird on a national site. This might be geo-targeted and right for me, but if it's not my market it reads like an afterthought.
- **No family/group angle** — I'm booking for a family. Nothing hints at companion fares, lap-infant policy, or group booking. The page is optimized for the solo points hacker, not the dad buying four seats.
- **Fine print density** — four asterisk conditions crammed under the price. I can parse it, but it cheapens the deal energy right before the CTA.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Companion Pass module above the fold** — even a one-liner like "Flying with someone? Your Companion flies free." with a link to the companion booking flow. This is Southwest's clearest differentiator and it's invisible here.
- **Swap the credit-card banner for a logged-in member balance strip** — if I'm logged in, show me my points balance and how close I am to status. That's a retention hook, not an acquisition pitch.
- **Make the departure city dynamic and labeled** — "One-way as low as $69 from Burbank, CA" should either be geo-personalized with a "Not you? Change origin" link, or show the cheapest fare nationally and let me pick my city. Right now it just sits there unexplained.
- **Add a "Book for the whole family" secondary CTA** — a text link or secondary button below "Book now" that routes to multi-passenger search. Low dev lift, big relevance gain for family flyers.
