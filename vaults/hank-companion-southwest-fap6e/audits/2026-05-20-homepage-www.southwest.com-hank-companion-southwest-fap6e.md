# Homepage snapshot · www.southwest.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Southwest came in hot with the "sale" headline — that word in the first sentence is basically catnip for me. This is their standard summer fare push, leaning on a $69 lead price and that warm, palm-frond aesthetic. The bones are solid for a loyal Rapid Rewards member, but I'm immediately annoyed that my Companion Pass isn't acknowledged anywhere — not even a quiet "bring someone free" whisper.

## What's working

- **"Tide's out, summer sale's in."** — That headline earns its keep. Punchy, seasonal, and that "sale" word is the hook I need to stop scrolling.
- **$69 fare price, large and centered** — No burying the lead. I see the number, I know what I'm dealing with.
- **"Book now" button** — Yellow, high contrast, above the fold. Zero ambiguity about what Southwest wants me to do.
- **Rapid Rewards® in the nav + points banner** — Two loyalty touchpoints before I even engage with the hero. That's the right priority order for a repeat customer like me.
- **"NEW!" badge on Vacations tab** — Small but effective. Makes me curious enough to tap even if I came here for flights.

## What's weak

- **Zero Companion Pass mention** — This is Southwest's single most powerful loyalty hook and it's nowhere. I have the Pass. Remind me to bring my wife for free. That's the whole pitch.
- **"From Burbank, CA"** — Geo-targeting a fare from Burbank on my phone in a city that may not be Burbank is lazy. Either show my nearest airport or keep it generic.
- **"Earn 50,000 points + first checked bag is free"** — This reads like a credit card acquisition banner, not a returning-member perk. I already know all Southwest bags fly free. This banner is confusing the benefit with a card upsell.
- **2-day booking window buried in fine print** — The $69 offer expires 5/21 (tomorrow), but that urgency is hidden in grey 9pt fine print. If this is expiring tomorrow, that's a conversion lever — use it.
- **No people in the imagery** — A decorative palm frond is fine aesthetically, but there's nobody here I can identify with. Family on the jetway, couple at the gate — anything human.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Companion Pass strip** — A single banner above or below the hero: "Flying with your Companion Pass? Book one, she flies free." One sentence. Costs nothing. Converts Pass holders who came here to use the benefit.
- **Surface the booking deadline as urgency copy**, not fine print — Move "Sale ends tomorrow, May 21" into the hero subhead. That 2-day window is a real closer if you show it.
- **Personalize the departure city or go generic** — Either pull my location for a real localized fare or drop "From Burbank, CA" and write "One-way fares from $69" with a city selector in the CTA flow.
- **Swap the palm-frond graphic for a lifestyle shot with a family at a gate** — A real family scene reinforces Southwest's family-friendly DNA and gives me someone to see myself in.
