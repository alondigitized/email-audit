# Homepage snapshot · www.southwest.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Southwest is doing exactly what Southwest does — leading with a low fare number and zero pretense. The homepage is clean, fast, and intentional: they want me in the booking flow before I even think about it. No lifestyle photography, no aspirational nonsense — just "Wanna land a low fare?" and a $69 price point that makes me lean in. Very on-brand, very functional, maybe a little too generic once I remember I've got a Companion Pass they haven't acknowledged yet.

## What's working

- **"Wanna land a low fare?" headline** — that casual, conversational register is exactly how Southwest talks to me and I respond to it every single time
- **$69 price point with origin city** — showing the actual number from an actual city is more honest than "fares from low prices" vagueness
- **"Book now" CTA** — yellow on blue, you can't miss it; no ambiguity, no "Discover," no "Explore" — just book
- **Rapid Rewards in top nav** — I'm clocking it immediately; the program exists and is one tap away
- **Tab navigation (Flights / Vacations / Hotels / Cars / Cruises)** — exactly the categories I use, clean hierarchy, Flights is already active

## What's weak

- **50,000-points banner** — I already have the Southwest card and the Companion Pass; this credit card acquisition pitch is dead air for me and takes up premium real estate
- **No Companion Pass mention anywhere** — this is Southwest's most aspirational loyalty product and they're not using it to make retention plays on the homepage for people who have it
- **Zero lifestyle imagery** — no travelers, no families, no airports — the page is 100% functional UI. I get it, but there's nothing emotionally sticky that makes me feel like flying sounds fun right now
- **Fine print is genuinely tiny** — "Seats/days/mkts lmtd; restr./excl. apply. First travel date available 6/9/2026" — the restrictions are there but you need good eyes
- **"Recent searches" is barely visible** — the personalization hook is right there at the bottom of the fold and I can barely see it; if it surfaces my last search it's incredibly useful but I almost miss it

## Recommendations

- **Swap the credit card acquisition banner for a Companion Pass status nudge** — if you detect I'm logged in with a Companion Pass, show "Your Companion flies free. Book a trip for two." That's the retention play that prints money.
- **Add one destination/deal tile below the search form** — even a single "Flights from $69 to Las Vegas this weekend" card turns the booking form from functional to compelling.
- **Surface my points balance in the header next to Log In** — "12,450 pts" next to the login button is the cheapest personalization win on the page and makes me feel seen as a member before I even tap anything.
- **Make the fine print one-liner slightly larger** — or surface the key restriction (origin city + date window) in the headline itself: "One-way from Burbank as low as $69." Saves the trust hit when people click through and realize it's not for them.
