# Homepage snapshot · www.southwest.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- Southwest opens exactly how I expect it to — a big, punchy low-fare headline, a specific dollar number, and a yellow "Book now" button that a toddler could find. This brand knows its lane: no-frills, cheap fares, loyalty math. Right now it's pushing a $69 one-way off Burbank, which tells me they're running a sale-fare campaign and not wasting the above-the-fold real estate on fluff.

## What's working

- **"Wanna land a low fare?" headline** — casual, punchy, and exactly the register I use when I'm telling my wife I found a deal. This is my language.
- **$69 one-way displayed in giant type** — Southwest knows I'm scrolling fast and opens with the number before anything else. Respect.
- **"Book now" in Southwest yellow** — impossible to miss, clear action, no second-guessing what it does.
- **Rapid Rewards in the nav** — I know that's my loyalty home base and it's one tap away; doesn't make me hunt for it.
- **"Earn 50,000 points + first checked bag is free" banner** — grabs my attention because I'm always counting toward my next Companion Pass threshold.
- **Flights tab is already selected by default** — I'm here to book a flight, and the booking module is pre-set. Zero friction.
- **"Recent searches" visible at the bottom** — returning user hook, I'd immediately see my last Burbank → Denver search.

## What's weak

- **Zero Companion Pass callout** — this is the #1 reason Millennial families are loyal to Southwest, and it's nowhere above the fold. Huge miss.
- **No family or couples imagery** — the hero is just a light blue gradient with text and a price. Sterile. I don't see myself in this page.
- **"Earn 50,000 points" banner feels like a credit card ad** — it probably is. If I already have the card, that banner is noise, not a loyalty hook.
- **"As low as*" with heavy fine print** — 61 travel days across what will be roughly 8 months of schedule is restrictive. The asterisk footnote is small and easy to miss until you're annoyed.
- **No Wanna Get Away Plus callout** — that fare class is the one budget-savvy flyers actively look for (same-day standby perk, transferable credits). Not visible.
- **No urgency signal** — no countdown, no "sale ends Sunday," nothing. I'm a "reads every email for 'sale'" guy; tell me when this expires.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Companion Pass progress teaser to the logged-in state banner** — "You're 12,400 points from your next Companion Pass" above the fold would make me book a flight I don't need. Ship it.
- **Swap the blank blue gradient hero for a lifestyle shot of a couple or young family at the gate** — Southwest's "two bags fly free" positioning works best when I can see someone who looks like me actually using it.
- **Add a sale expiration line** — even "Fares available through Sunday" or "Limited seats — grab yours" turns a passive browser into an active booker.
- **Surface "Wanna Get Away Plus" as a fare filter badge** — one small label on the price display telling me which fare class this is saves me a click and builds trust.
