# Homepage snapshot · www.southwest.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- Southwest is coming in with its whole thing on full display — fare-first, no-frills, direct. The hero is basically a search widget with a $69 teaser fare baked in, and the Rapid Rewards signup banner is right there at the top. This is exactly the airline that knows its people: price shoppers who want the number fast, not a mood board.

## What's working

- **$69 fare callout** — Leading with a specific dollar amount from a real departure city (Burbank, CA) is exactly how you hook a points-and-cash optimizer. I'm already doing the mental math.
- **"Wanna land a low fare?"** — That casual, slightly cheeky register is on-brand Southwest and it lands for a millennial who's allergic to corporate airline speak.
- **"Book now" button** — Yellow, full-width, impossible to miss. No "Explore possibilities" nonsense. Just book.
- **Top nav with FLIGHT | HOTEL | CAR | VACATIONS | CRUISES** — I can get to my thing in one tap, no buried menus.
- **50,000 points + first checked bag is free** banner — That free bag hook is my love language. Instant credibility.
- **Flights / Vacations / Hotels / Cars / Cruises** tab strip below the hero — Clean category switcher without needing to dig through nav.

## What's weak

- **No Companion Pass mention anywhere** — I have a Companion Pass. That is my superpower with Southwest and this page doesn't acknowledge it exists. A returning member login could surface "Your Companion Pass is active" or at least "Companion Pass holders: book smart."
- **Credit card promo banner feels like a bait-and-switch** — "Earn 50,000 points + first checked bag is free" looks like a Southwest perk until you read it — it's a card offer, not a base benefit. Feels a little sneaky at first glance.
- **No family or multi-passenger framing** — I'm booking for 3-4 people. The $69 is one-way for one person. Nothing tells me what this looks like for a family of four.
- **Fare disclaimer is tiny** — "*Seats/days/mkts lmtd; restr./excl. apply. First travel date available 6/16/2026 with 51 travel days in schedule." — all crammed into one small-font line. I know to read it, but my partner won't.
- **No urgency signal** — No "Offer ends Sunday" or "Only 12 seats left at this price." The $69 teaser just floats there with no deadline pressure.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Companion Pass surface for logged-in members** — Even a small badge under the logged-in state: "Companion Pass active — your travel companion flies free on this booking." That's the single biggest loyalty hook Southwest has and it's invisible here.
- **Swap the credit-card-disguised banner for a genuine Rapid Rewards benefit callout** — "Two free bags on every flight, no catch" or "Points never expire" — something that rewards existing members, not just new cardholders.
- **Add a family-fare estimator hook** — A line like "Traveling with family? See fares for 2, 3, or 4 passengers" below the solo fare teaser would convert family bookers without cluttering the primary flow.
- **Add a deadline or seat-scarcity signal to the $69 fare** — "51 travel days available starting 6/16" is actually useful info but it reads like fine print. Flip it: "51 travel days to choose from — book before they fill up."
