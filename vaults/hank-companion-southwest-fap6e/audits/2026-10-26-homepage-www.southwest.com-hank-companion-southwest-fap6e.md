# Homepage snapshot · www.southwest.com · 2026-10-26
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- Southwest still looks like the no-nonsense low-fare airline I like. The search form is on the left, and a $58 one-way fare from Burbank sits on the right, with Christmas ornaments filling the background. It's pushing "Book your winter travel now" plus a 50,000-point credit card offer in the blue strip. I don't see anything about Companion Pass or bags, though, and "Updated checked baggage policies" in the fine print under the search button makes me nervous.

## What's working

- **Search form as the hero.** Flights is the default tab, and the form is pre-filled with BUR as the departure airport and dates. That saves me taps.
- **"Show fares in: $ / Points / $ + Points" radio buttons.** Points-and-cash is how I book, and it's one tap away on the homepage.
- **"Wanna land a low fare?" with a big "$58."** It's the right register, and the price is the largest thing on the right half.
- **"Recent searches" dropdown.** It's a returning-flyer convenience that fits how I book repeat routes.
- **Yellow CTAs.** Both buttons pop against the white and blue.

## What's weak

- **No Companion Pass and no bag messaging.** Those are the reasons I fly Southwest, and nothing above the fold mentions them. The only bag text is a vague "Updated checked baggage policies" link, which reads like bad news.
- **Fine print under the $58 fare.** The first available travel date is 11/16/2026 and it says "restr./excl. apply." I can't tell where the fare goes, because there's no destination.
- **"Earn 50,000 points + first checked bag is free."** This is a credit card ad in the banner, and the "first checked bag is free" line cuts against my two-free-bags expectations.
- **Holiday decor.** The ornaments and candy canes are pretty but say nothing about flights or destinations.
- **"Log in to view points balance."** That's a weak prompt. I'd rather see my balance and my Companion Pass status.

## Recommendations

- Add a slim Companion Pass strip under the nav, something like "Fly 2 for the price of 1, all year." Put "Bags fly..." or the current baggage rule in plain language next to the search button instead of the run-together link.
- Show a destination with the $58 fare ("Burbank → Las Vegas, $58 one-way, travel by [date]"). Add a sale-end date so the offer has a deadline.
- Swap the ornament background for a lifestyle shot of a family at a gate or on a plane, or a destination photo. Keep the holiday tone but make it relevant to flying.
- Fix the footer text so the baggage and optional-fee links are separated. Move the fare restrictions out of tiny grey text and into a "Fare details" link.
