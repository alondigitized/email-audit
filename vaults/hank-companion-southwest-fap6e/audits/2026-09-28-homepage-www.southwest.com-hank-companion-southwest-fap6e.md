# Homepage snapshot · www.southwest.com · 2026-09-28
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- This is Southwest doing exactly what Southwest does best — no seat map flex, no first-class glamour shots, just a big yellow "Book now" button and a $69 fare slapped front and center. It reads like an airline that knows its audience is checking fares on a lunch break, not dreaming about a business-class lie-flat. Right now it's pushing a $69 BUR one-way fare and a 50,000-points-plus-free-bag card offer.

## What's working

- **The booking widget is dropped right on the homepage, no detour.** Trip type, my home airport pre-filled kind of thing (BUR shown), dates already defaulted to next week — that's how you get me to not bounce.
- **"$69 one-way" in big blue numbers.** That's the headline I actually read. No fluff copy around it, just the number.
- **"Earn 50,000 points + first checked bag is free."** That's Companion Pass bait and free-bag bait in one line — literally my two favorite words on this airline.
- **Points toggle in the search widget ("Show fares in $ / Points / $+Points").** That's built for someone who already knows their points balance matters, i.e. me.

## What's weak

- **No people anywhere on this page.** Just a plane on a runway. Fine, but zero "family of four with two free checked bags" energy — feels a little cold for a brand that sells itself on being the friendly one.
- **The fine print under the $69 fare is doing a lot of quiet work.** "*Seats/days/mkts lmtd; restr./excl. apply. First travel date available 10/20/2026 with 57 travel days in schedule." That's the kind of small text that makes we-know-you-know-this-loophole flyers like me squint before trusting the number.
- **No deadline on the points/bag offer.** "Earn 50,000 points" — by when? If there's no urgency, I've got zero reason to act today instead of next week.
- **"NEW!" badge is on Cruises, not Flights.** I don't care about cruises. Wasted badge real estate on my screen.

## Recommendations

- Add an expiration date or countdown to the "50,000 points + free bag" banner — even "Offer ends 10/5" would get me moving today instead of bookmarking it.
- Put the fine print on the $69 fare in a tappable "see restrictions" link instead of a paragraph of gray 8pt text under the price.
- Swap the empty tarmac plane shot for something with a family or a couple boarding with their two free bags visible — reinforce the actual value prop visually, not just in text.
- Surface my Rapid Rewards points balance right in that top banner if I'm logged in, instead of a generic "Log in to view points balance" nudge.
