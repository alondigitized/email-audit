# Homepage snapshot · www.southwest.com · 2026-07-20
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-20**
## Executive summary

- Look, Southwest opens with their bread and butter — a big fat "$69" fare dangled right in my face with a "Wanna land a low fare?" headline that's practically written for me. The mobile layout is clean, the yellow "Book now" button is impossible to miss, and RAPID REWARDS is front and center. This is a no-frills, get-you-into-the-funnel homepage — exactly Southwest's style, and honestly, it works for me.

## What's working

- **"Wanna land a low fare?" headline:** Casual, direct, millennial-coded. Southwest talking to me like a person, not a frequent flyer number. I respect it.
- **$69 price in huge type:** I came here to find a deal. The deal is the first thing I see. Zero friction getting to the point.
- **RAPID REWARDS® in the nav + 50K points banner:** Two loyalty touchpoints without scrolling. I know exactly that my points are here and accessible.
- **Yellow "Book now" button:** There is one job on this page and it's that button. Full-width, high-contrast, unmissable.
- **Flights / Vacations / Hotels / Cars / Cruises tab row:** One tap to switch contexts. Clean wayfinding.
- **"Now accepting reservations through April 05, 2027":** Sets expectations on booking horizon — useful for a trip planner like me who's always looking three months out.

## What's weak

- **Zero Companion Pass mention:** This is Southwest's single most powerful loyalty hook for frequent flyers. I have the Pass, I'm the target customer, and it's nowhere on the page. A "Traveling with your Companion?" nudge or even a small badge would hit different.
- **"First checked bag is free" in the promo banner:** Southwest's actual policy is TWO free bags for everyone, always. This phrasing makes it sound like a consolation prize and reads like a credit card acquisition play. As someone who knows the product, it's confusing and slightly disappointing.
- **"From Burbank, CA" geo-lock on the $69 fare:** The deal is hyper-local. If I'm not near Burbank, this fare is window dressing. No way to quickly swap origin without going deeper.
- **Green "NEW!" badge is orphaned:** It sits below the "Book now" button with no visible label explaining what's new. Unclear if it refers to a route, a feature, or the fare itself. Visual clutter without payoff.
- **No urgency signal on the fare:** I don't know if this $69 deal ends tonight or next month. "Seats/days/mkts lmtd" is boilerplate — a "Sale ends Sunday" or even a seat counter would actually move me faster.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Companion Pass surface above the fold** — even a subtle "Companion Pass holder? Tap to log in and apply your pass" link next to the Log in button converts me instantly.
- **Fix the free-bag copy** — change "first checked bag is free" to "2 bags fly free®" (Southwest's actual branded line) or make it explicit this is a card offer with a "with Rapid Rewards® card" qualifier.
- **Make the $69 origin searchable inline** — a small "Change origin" tap target under "From Burbank, CA" lets me swap cities without leaving the hero; right now I have to scroll to find the full search form.
- **Give the "NEW!" badge a label** — "New low fares added" or "New fall routes" turns noise into a reason to tap.
