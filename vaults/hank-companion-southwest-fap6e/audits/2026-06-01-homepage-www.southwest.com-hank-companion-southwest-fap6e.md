# Homepage snapshot · www.southwest.com · 2026-06-01
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-01**
## Executive summary

- Opened Southwest.com and got hit with a credit card acquisition pitch in full-screen yellow. I know the brand, I love the brand, but I came to find a flight deal — not apply for another Visa. The free-bag callout is the one line that speaks my language; everything else above the fold is a retention-marketing detour that assumes I don't already have the card.

## What's working

- **"Plus, first checked bag is free."** — That single line does more for me than the entire points headline. Two bags free is why I fly Southwest with my family. Seeing it here, even in a card context, confirms the brand knows its value prop.
- **Strikethrough "50K → 80,000 points"** — Classic anchoring and it works. My brain immediately reads the delta as a deal, even if I know the tricks.
- **"LIMITED-TIME OFFER" + "Offer ends 7/1/26."** — Hard deadline creates real urgency. I actually check the date on offers like this.
- **Rapid Rewards® in primary nav** — One tap to my loyalty account. No digging.
- **Log in button top-right** — Instantly visible, doesn't require a modal or menu excavation.

## What's weak

- **No flight search above the fold** — I opened the Southwest app to find a cheap seat to Denver for the 4th of July weekend. There is zero booking interface visible. The flight tabs are there at the very bottom of the screenshot but they're not actually actionable — I'd need to scroll or tap before I can even start a search.
- **"Learn more" CTA** — Learn more about WHAT? This is the only call-to-action and it's deliberately vague. Southwest knows I know what a credit card is. Say "Apply now" or even "See the offer." Don't make me guess what I'm clicking into.
- **Credit card as the hero** — I almost certainly already have this card (hello, Companion Pass). Showing me a card acquisition offer as the entire above-fold experience is tone-deaf for returning Rapid Rewards members who land here directly.
- **No sale fares or flight deals visible** — Southwest's "Sale" link in the nav is what I actually open emails for. There's nothing here about low fares or Wanna Get Away deals. Zero. That's a miss for a budget-focused flyer.
- **No family or lifestyle imagery** — A floating credit card on a flat yellow background feels like a bank's landing page, not a company that runs "Bags Fly Free" TV spots.

## Recommendations

- **Add a flight search widget above the fold**, even a compact one below the card banner. The "Flights" tab at the bottom of the screen is good but inactive — give me an origin/destination field I can tap immediately.
- **Swap "Learn more" for "Apply now – 80K points"** — give the CTA the specificity of the headline. The points number is the sell; the button should say so.
- **Add a sale fares strip** — even a one-liner like "Wanna Get Away fares from $49 — ends Sunday" between the card banner and the booking tabs would keep budget-flyers from bouncing to Google Flights.
- **Detect logged-in returning members and suppress the card offer** — if I'm already a cardholder, replace this with my Rapid Rewards balance or an upgrade prompt. That data exists. Use it.
