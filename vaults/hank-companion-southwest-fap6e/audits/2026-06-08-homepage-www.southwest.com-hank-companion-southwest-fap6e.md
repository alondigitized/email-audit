# Homepage snapshot · www.southwest.com · 2026-06-08
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-08**
## Executive summary

- Southwest is going full sale mode right now — big blue hero, bold yellow "Book now" button, and a promo code screaming "FLYWOW." This looks like a brand that knows its people want deals, not mood boards. As a Rapid Rewards guy who watches every points promotion, this page caught my eye in the first two seconds, which is more than I can say for most airline homepages.

## What's working

- **"BIGGEST SALES of the year" hero copy** — I don't need a lifestyle photo. Tell me there's a sale and tell me the number. 40% is a number. That works.
- **Promo code FLYWOW with a hard deadline (6/11/26)** — the specific date creates real urgency without feeling manufactured. I'm checking my calendar right now.
- **80,000-point offer banner at the very top** — as a Companion Pass holder watching my points balance, this caught me before I even hit the hero. "Offer ends 7/1/26" gives me a runway without feeling panic-y.
- **Log in button visible in the nav** — quick path to see my account balance and apply member pricing. One tap.
- **Rapid Rewards® tab in nav** — I don't have to go hunting to find the program I actually care about.

## What's weak

- **"Off base fares*" asterisk** — that asterisk is doing heavy lifting. As someone who has booked Southwest for years, I know base fares are Wanna Get Away, which are already cheap. Forty percent off those isn't 40% off a real fare. Feels like a headline-number discount that softens in the fine print.
- **No bag-fee callout** — two free bags is literally Southwest's biggest differentiator vs. United and Delta. It's not on this page. That's a miss for any family flyer comparison-shopping right now.
- **No Companion Pass hook** — I have a Companion Pass and the homepage has no idea. Nothing about bringing someone free, nothing about the Companion Pass benefit. That's leaving the most loyal cohort completely cold.
- **Zero imagery or lifestyle context** — I don't need a family on a beach, but there's not a single visual signal that this is about family travel, summer trips, or anything beyond a transaction. It's a coupon, not an experience.
- **Fine print is dense** — "Seats/days/mkts ltmd. Disc. applied pre-govt. taxes/fees. Add'l fees may apply." Below a sale headline is a lot of caveats on a small phone screen.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "2 Free Bags. Always." badge somewhere above the fold** — even a small line under the CTA. It's the fastest way to differentiate from Spirit/Frontier and close a comparison shopper.
- **Create a Companion Pass-aware hero variant** — if I'm logged in and I have a Companion Pass, show me something like "Bring your travel buddy free — sale fares start here." That surfaces a benefit I already paid for.
- **Reword the headline to "40% off sale fares" and drop the asterisk from the hero** — move the fine print where it belongs (below the CTA), and be straight about "sale fares" rather than "base fares" so the discount framing holds up on closer read.
- **Swap dense fine-print block for a single tap-to-expand "See full terms"** — the paragraph of restrictions makes the deal look worse than it is on a five-inch screen.
