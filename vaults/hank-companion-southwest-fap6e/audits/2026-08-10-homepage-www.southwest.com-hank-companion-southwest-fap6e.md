# Homepage snapshot · www.southwest.com · 2026-08-10
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- This is exactly the no-nonsense booking-tool-first homepage I expect from Southwest — no lifestyle fluff, just a flight search widget parked front and center with a "40% off base fares" banner behind it. Right now they're pushing a fall/winter sale window (8/31-3/3) and a Rapid Rewards Visa card pitch. It reads like a brand that knows its regulars just want to search flights fast, not get sold a vacation fantasy.

## What's working

- **The flight search widget is already pre-filled** with BUR as my depart airport and dates auto-populated — that's the kind of frictionless return-visitor behavior that gets me to actually hit search instead of bouncing.
- **"Earn 50,000 points + first checked bag is free"** banner right under the nav — free bags is basically my love language, so this catches my eye immediately.
- **The 40% off base fares callout** is bold, high-contrast yellow-on-navy, easy to read at a glance while scrolling past on my phone.
- **Show fares in Points / $+Points toggle** is sitting right in the search widget — huge for someone who books in a points-and-cash mix like I do.

## What's weak

- **No mention of Companion Pass anywhere above the fold.** I've got one, it's my whole reason for loyalty to this airline, and there's zero acknowledgment of it on the homepage — not even a "manage your Companion Pass" link near Rapid Rewards.
- **The hero image is just an abstract blue swirl texture** — no people, no destination shot, nothing that sells the "escape plan" emotionally. Feels like a placeholder more than a campaign hero.
- **The 40% off fine print is dense** ("Restr., excl., & blkouts apply... Disc. applied pre-govt. taxes/fees. Add'l fees may apply") — not dishonest, but it's the kind of copy that makes me want to double check the fare before trusting the headline number.
- **Points balance isn't visible** — it just says "Log in to view points balance," which is a missed chance to hook returning members with an actual number.

## Recommendations

- Swap the abstract blue-swirl hero background for an actual destination or travel lifestyle photo tied to the sale markets.
- Add a small "Manage Companion Pass" or Companion Pass badge near the Rapid Rewards nav item — it's a differentiator Southwest owns and this page ignores it.
- Surface an actual points balance teaser (even a generic "You could have X points" placeholder) instead of just a login prompt.
- Tighten the fine print under the 40% offer to one line with a "see details" expandable instead of a full paragraph block.
