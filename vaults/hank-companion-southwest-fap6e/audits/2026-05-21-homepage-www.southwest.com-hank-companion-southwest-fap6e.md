# Homepage snapshot · www.southwest.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Classic Southwest — no-frills, points-forward, sale-first. The homepage opens with a summer sale fare drop and a credit card bonus point offer, which is exactly how they hook people like me. It's leaning hard into affordability, which is on-brand, but as a Companion Pass holder the real value prop for me is hidden behind a "Learn more" link that I have to hunt for.

## What's working

- **$69 fare + "Book now" CTA** — I scan subject lines for "sale" before I open emails, and this homepage IS the subject line. Price is front and center, no burying it.
- **"Tide's out, summer sale's in" headline** — Punchy. I get it instantly. Southwest keeps it casual and I respect that.
- **50,000 points + first checked bag is free banner** — Two free bags is literally my love language. Seeing bag language above the fold earns a nod.
- **Flights / Vacations / Hotels / Cars / Cruises tab bar** — One tap to switch context. Exactly how I actually shop — I'll check flight, then bundle into Vacations if the math works.
- **Sale booking window pinned** — "Book 5/19–5/21" is right there in the fine print. I notice that. It tells me I need to move today.

## What's weak

- **No Companion Pass mention anywhere** — I earned this thing. It should be greeting me. "Bring a companion free all year" is Southwest's biggest loyalty differentiator and it's invisible above the fold.
- **"Log In" is in the corner but there's no personalization payoff shown** — If logging in doesn't surface my points balance or saved travelers, why is the prompt there? It's a dangling hook.
- **Fine print volume is aggressive** — "Restrictions, exclusions, and blackout dates apply. 21-day advance purchase required. Seats/days/mkts lmtd." all stacked under a headline price feels like small-print whiplash. I know the game, but my spouse does not.
- **"From Burbank, CA" is hyper-specific** — Maybe I'm not in SoCal. There's no "change origin" affordance visible. If I'm in Chicago this $69 means nothing to me and I bounce.
- **"Learn more" next to the points banner** — Two-word link on a white strip for a 50,000-point offer? That deserves its own CTA treatment, not a gray afterthought.

## Recommendations

- **Surface Companion Pass for logged-in users** — If I'm authenticated, replace the card-acquisition banner with "Your companion flies free — add them now" and a direct deep-link to the companion booking flow.
- **Add origin-city picker to the hero** — Let me swap "From Burbank, CA" to my home airport inline so the $69 price is actually relevant to me; a single dropdown or geolocation auto-fill would do it.
- **Upgrade the points banner to a real module** — 50,000 points is a meaningful offer; give it a yellow CTA button ("Earn 50K points") instead of a "Learn more" text link buried in a thin strip.
- **Add a family/travel-stack callout** — A one-liner like "2 free bags per person, every flight" in the hero subtext would confirm the Southwest value prop without requiring a scroll or a click.
