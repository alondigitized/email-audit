# Homepage snapshot · www.southwest.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- Southwest knows exactly who they're talking to and wasted zero pixels on the intro — first thing I see is "$69 one-way from Burbank" and a yellow "Book now" button. This is the airline that built its whole identity around the budget traveler, and the homepage delivers that in about two seconds. Right now they're pushing low fares with points as the sweetener.

## What's working

- **The $69 fare with a real origin city.** "From Burbank, CA" makes it feel real and searchable, not bait-and-switch. I'm already mentally checking if Burbank works for my next trip.
- **The Rapid Rewards + free bag banner.** "Earn 50,000 points + first checked bag is free" hits two of my personal loyalty obsessions in one line — points accrual *and* the no-bag-fee brand promise.
- **The booking tabs (Flights, Vacations, Hotels, Cars, Cruises).** Everything I might bundle is one tap away, and Flights is already selected — no hunting.
- **"Log in" button above the fold.** I can sign in immediately and see my Rapid Rewards balance before I book. That's respecting my workflow.
- **"NEW!" badge on the booking widget.** Gets my attention — I want to know what changed.

## What's weak

- **No Companion Pass callout anywhere.** This is Southwest's most powerful loyalty product and the single reason I'm more loyal to them than any other carrier. The homepage treats me like a first-time visitor.
- **Zero family or lifestyle imagery.** The hero is pure text on a blue gradient. A dad with two kids breezing through security — bags fly free — would've landed for me instantly. Right now it feels like a spreadsheet, not a brand.
- **Fine print is functionally unreadable.** The asterisk restriction text is maybe 8px. "43 travel days in schedule" is a real limitation that affects decision-making — putting it in readable type would actually build trust, not kill the conversion.
- **The "Learn more" link on the points banner goes... somewhere?** No indication of what I'm learning more about. Feels lazy for a loyalty program CTA.
- **No points-balance hook for logged-out users.** Even a "Sign in to see your Rapid Rewards balance" nudge would speak directly to me. Instead, the login button is generic.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Companion Pass strip or badge near the fare offer.** Even "Book your companion for taxes only" in one line of yellow text would transform this for loyal customers — ship it above the "Book now" button.
- **Replace the blue gradient hero with a lifestyle shot.** A family at the gate, bags in hand, zero baggage-fee anxiety on their faces — that sells the Southwest difference better than a fare number alone.
- **Make the fine print scannable.** Pull "43 travel days" and "first travel date 6/16/2026" out of the asterisk blob and put them in a readable badge next to the price. Honesty at legible size builds loyalty.
- **Turn the loyalty banner CTA into something specific.** Change "Learn more" to "See card offer" or "Earn with Chase" — give me a destination so I know what I'm tapping into.
