# Your tool collection could use a +2 👌
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- The subject sells a tool deal, and the body delivers it: "Get up to 2 FREE select power tools when you buy a select battery kit." That hero is the strongest part. It has a big green "2 FREE" block, DEWALT and Kobalt tools, and a clear "Shop now" button. The problem is that it isn't the first thing you see. A generic "Black Friday: BIG deals made an early entry" banner sits above it and pushes the tool offer down the page.
- After the hero, the email loses focus. It runs through outdoor power equipment, tool storage, a rake lifestyle image, Traeger, HomeCare+, a credit card, a product grid and Deals of the Day. That is about ten blocks, each with its own CTA. Compared with the 11/4 appliance send, where the offer got buried, the offer here is clearer. The Black Friday banner and the long tail still dilute it.
- Judgement: a solid tool-category send with one good offer. It is weakened by a generic banner above the hero and a body that keeps adding unrelated offers.

## What's working

- The "2 FREE" hero is a concrete, visual offer with real product photos and a clear "Shop now" button.
- The subject's tool angle matches the hero, so the open and the click tell one story.
- The outdoor and storage tiles ("Save up to $300", "25% off") give specific numbers with matching buttons.
- The "FREE same-day delivery" banner and the Rewards logo sit in the header, so the member benefit is visible straight away.
- The brand look is consistent, with strong blue and red blocks and a clear section rhythm.

## What's weak

- The generic "Black Friday: BIG deals" banner comes before the tool hero and has no specific offer.
- The email is too long. The Traeger, HomeCare+, credit card and Deals of the Day blocks pull attention away from tools.
- The "Curated with you in mind" product tiles have truncated, awkward names ("Commander Large 27.0 -Gallons ( 108.0 -", "DEWALT 20 -Volt 2 - Pack Battery/charger ( 5 Ah 5 Ah ) - DCB205-"). Two of the three also show no price, and one of those is a paper yard bag, which doesn't fit a tool email.
- The "2 FREE" offer has no visible terms or deadline in the hero, so it reads as vague. The "select power tools" and "select battery kit" wording is unclear.
- The credit card and HomeCare+ blocks carry heavy fine print and exclusions.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the "2 FREE" tool hero to the top and drop the generic Black Friday banner, or shrink it to a thin strip. The tool offer should be the first thing the eye lands on.
- 2. Cut the email to the hero, the outdoor and storage tiles, and one product row. Move Traeger, HomeCare+ and the credit card to a separate send.
- 3. Name the offer. Say which battery kits qualify, for example "Buy a DEWALT 20V 5Ah kit, get 2 tools free", and add an end date.
- 4. Fix the product grid. Use clean product titles, show prices, and pick tools instead of paper bags.
- 5. Make the CTA more specific, such as "Shop free-tool deals" instead of "Shop now".

- **Subject Alt B:** `2 FREE tools with select battery kits`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus up to $300 off outdoor power equipment. Ends 11/8.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `DEWALT, Kobalt and more. Pick your kit and your free tools.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has no confirmed blocking defects. The confirmed issues are missing `alt` attributes on 19 images and the recipient's email address sent in plaintext in 17 `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixel URLs. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the headers weren't captured. The HTML source is truncated, so findings about the body, footer and link targets are limited to what the QA data shows.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link coverage:** 42 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, so no destinations, redirect chains or 404s were verified.
- **Impression pixels:** 17 `https://sli.lowes.com/imp?...&stpe=pixel|static|default` beacons are in the body. Examples are `s=127768300` to `127768304` with `p=529544`, and `s=2019290`, `2019286`, `2019287`, `2019289`, `1090838`, `1090834`, `1090835` and `1090837` with `p=1162026`.
  - `s=2019286` and `s=1090834` each appear twice, so they may double-count impressions.
  - `li_coord=` is sent with an empty value on both `2019286` and both `1090834` pixels, which suggests an unpopulated parameter.
- **Third-party dependency:** Each pixel is a separate request to `sli.lowes.com`. Many are blocked by Apple Mail Privacy Protection and by image-blocking clients, and opens counted this way are unreliable.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` text on content images** (confirmed by QA):
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg`
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png`
  - With images off or a screen reader, these render as nothing, or announce a filename.
- **Missing `alt` on tracking pixels:** The 17 `sli.lowes.com/imp` images should carry `alt=""`, ideally with `width="1" height="1"` and `style="display:none"`. Without that, screen readers may announce the URL.
- **DOCTYPE:** The source reads `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" ...>`. There is no space after `PUBLIC`, so it is malformed. It also uses an `https://` DTD URL and `xmlns="https://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"`. The correct namespace is `http://`. Most clients tolerate this, but strict parsers may drop into quirks mode.
- **Web fonts:** Fellix and DIN Next load from `lowescdn.com` as `.woff` files and are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. Outlook falls back to system fonts, so a font-family fallback stack should be confirmed in the inline styles (not visible in the truncated source).
- **Responsive CSS:** A `@media screen and (min-width:375px) and (max-width:600px) and (-webkit-min-device-pixel-ratio:0)` block and a `max-width:480px` block are present. The first targets WebKit only, so Gmail and other non-WebKit clients may not receive those rules. This is unverified without a render test.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `[FirstName]`) appear in the visible source. The truncated body can't be fully checked.
- **PII in URLs:** The recipient's email address is embedded as `e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app` in all 17 `sli.lowes.com/imp` URLs. It is sent unhashed, in the query string, to a third-party ad-server domain. This will appear in server logs and in any proxy or image-cache logs. A hashed or opaque ID would avoid it.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Neither header was found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip or not capture them. Because of that, this is **unconfirmed, not a confirmed defect**. Check the raw `.eml` before acting on it. Gmail and Yahoo require both headers for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `e.lowes.com`. Verify against the original message headers.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** Both are outside the truncated source and were not verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Cannot evaluate.** All 42 click links go through redirect domains and were not probed. The destination URLs, UTM parameters and landing page match are not visible.
- Check that the redirects resolve to `lowes.com` pages that carry consistent `cm_mmc` or UTM parameters.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the two content images (`c301e24e-…jpg`, `ad569dc5-…png`).
2. Add `alt=""` and hide all 17 `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixels.
3. Stop passing the plaintext recipient email in the `e=` pixel parameter. Use a hashed or opaque subscriber ID.
4. Check why `li_coord=` is empty on the `s=2019286` and `s=1090834` pixels. Deduplicate the repeated `s=` IDs if they are not intentional.
5. Correct the DOCTYPE to `PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" "http://www.w3.org/TR/xhtml1/DTD/xhtml1-transitional.dtd"`, and use `http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml` for the namespace.
6. Capture the full raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from the original message, not the relay copy, to settle the compliance and deliverability warnings.
7. Resolve a sample of the 42 tracking links with a redirect-following probe to check destinations and UTM consistency.
