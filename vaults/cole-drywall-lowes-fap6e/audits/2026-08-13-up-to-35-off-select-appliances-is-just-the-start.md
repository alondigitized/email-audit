# Up to 35% OFF select appliances is just the start 🤩
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- This is Lowe's stacking two campaigns into one send: the appliance discount hero it's run all week, followed by a sprawling "back-to-campus" module for dorm/apartment move-in. The appliance hero is clean and on-brand — big "35% off," a tidy product lineup, tiered "Buy more, save more" table — but it's identical creative to the recent appliance-closeout cycle (8/4, 8/5), just with different numbers. The back-to-campus section is the more interesting beat here, a fresh seasonal pivot that Lowe's hasn't run before in this history, but it's buried five scrolls deep behind decor lighting, vanities, and a doors/windows install pitch.
- The email is trying to be six emails at once — appliances, lighting, vanities, home services, back-to-campus, Deals of the Day signup — and none of them get a strong enough hook to be "the reason" to click. Subject promises appliances "is just the start," which is honest framing for the kitchen-sink structure, but it also signals to a scanning inbox reader that the real value is scattered and vague.
- Solid execution on the top module, weak overall focus. **6/10 territory** — competent, on-brand, no bugs, but overstuffed and not targeted at any one shopper.

## What's working

- The 35%-off appliance hero has real visual hierarchy: big number, product photos (Frigidaire fridge/washer/dryer/range/dishwasher), tiered discount table, all above the fold.
- No render bugs — clean layout, no overlapping text, no broken images across the entire long scroll.
- "Buy more, save more" tiered table is a clear, honest, easy-to-parse mechanic (Buy 2/Save $100, Buy 3/Save $200, Buy 4+/Save $300).
- Back-to-campus section is a genuinely fresh seasonal angle not seen in the last five audited sends — good cadence variety.
- Member-only "Trending move-in essential" tote bag with a dated validity window (8/13–8/15) adds real urgency and loyalty-tier exclusivity.

## What's weak

- The email tries to cover appliances, decor lighting, ceiling fans, vanities, doors/windows install, back-to-campus, and a Deals-of-the-Day signup — no single focal offer survives the scroll.
- Back-to-campus, arguably the most novel and timely content this week, doesn't appear until roughly two-thirds down the email.
- Repeated blue-gradient-hero-plus-white-CTA-button pattern across nearly every module makes the whole email visually monotonous by the third or fourth section.
- The appliance hero itself is a near-repeat of 8/4 and 8/5's Closeout Event visual template, just with new percentages — subscribers who opened those will find this familiar to the point of fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this into two sends: one appliance-focused (leading with 35% off + the bundle stack), one back-to-campus-focused (leading with the rolling tote + dorm essentials). Combining them dilutes both.
- 2. If keeping one email, move back-to-campus up to be the second module, right after the appliance hero — it's the freshest hook and shouldn't be buried under lighting/vanities filler.
- 3. Cut or consolidate the middle modules (decor lighting, ceiling fans, vanities, doors/windows) into a single "more ways to save" tile grid instead of four separate full-width sections — tightens the scroll significantly.
- 4. Vary the visual template for at least one module (e.g., a lifestyle photo-forward layout for back-to-campus) so it doesn't blur into the same blue-gradient rhythm as the rest.
