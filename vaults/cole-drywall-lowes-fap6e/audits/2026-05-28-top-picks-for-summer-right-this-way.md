# Top picks for summer? Right this way👉
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-28**
## Executive summary

- This is Lowe's post-Memorial Day reset email — a summer "best of" digest that arrives after nine straight days of countdown timers, LAST CHANCE banners, and MrBeast urgency. The creative correctly shifts tone: no emergency language, just a clean pivot to summer content. That's the right call. The problem is the email tries to carry five distinct messages at once (tools deal, grill discount, loyalty points, Father's Day gift cards, American-made outdoor furniture), and none of them get enough real estate to become the hero.
- The DeWalt/EGO "FREE item with $299 purchase" is the sharpest offer in the email — specific brand, specific price threshold, specific reward — but it's buried in a stack of comparably sized modules rather than anchored as the lead. The Father's Day module is smart forward-planning (three weeks out is the right window), but it's positioned late in the scroll.
- Net verdict: a well-intentioned digest that does a lot of things adequately and nothing brilliantly. It'll drive some clicks on loyalty and tools, but won't win inbox on subject alone.

## What's working

- **DeWalt/EGO deal is concrete.** Free product with $299 spend — specific brand names, specific dollar threshold, visible product images. This is the most actionable offer in the email.
- **Loyalty 3× points module.** Surfacing a points multiplier right after a big sale week is smart retention play; it gives members a reason to come back now that the holiday discount is gone.
- **Father's Day timing.** Three weeks to June 15 is the right window to plant the gift-card seed. The module is clean and the "it's what he wants" framing is appropriately self-aware.
- **Clean render.** No broken images, no overlapping text, layout holds across all visible sections.
- **Tone reset.** After nine Memorial Day emails, dropping the countdown timers and scarcity language is a genuine relief. The "here comes the sun" hero copy breathes.

## What's weak

- **Subject is a dead end.** "Top picks for summer? Right this way" tells me nothing about price, category, or brand. After the specificity of "Save up to $1,700" or "FREE [product] for members," this reads like a placeholder.
- **No visual hierarchy.** Five distinct content modules at equal weight means the eye bounces without landing. There's no single offer the brand is willing to bet the send on.
- **Preheader appears to be navigation junk.** The very top of the render shows what looks like "Shop now: Appliances | Grills..." nav links — if that's bleeding into the inbox preview, the subject loses its best complement.
- **CTAs are all generic.** "Buy now" and "Shop now" appear across multiple modules with no specificity — "Get the free DeWalt" or "Activate 3× points" would do real work.
- **"Made here at home" module feels misplaced.** Adirondack chairs and the American flag are a fine product story, but it sits disconnected from everything else in the email and has no offer attached.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the DeWalt/EGO offer as the full-width hero.** It's the most concrete thing in the email. Put it above the fold with a specific CTA: "Claim your free DeWalt." Move every other module below it.
- 2. **Rewrite CTAs to match the module.** "Activate 3× points" on the loyalty row, "Shop Father's Day gifts" on that module, "Get 20% off grills" on the grill row. Generic "Shop now" is invisible.
- 3. **Cut or relocate the "Made here at home" module.** It has no offer and breaks momentum mid-scroll. Either attach a specific deal (20% off patio furniture) or save it for a dedicated send.
- 4. **Preheader should echo the sharpest offer.** If the DeWalt deal is the lead, the preheader should read: "Free DeWalt tool with your $299 EGO purchase — this week only."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free DeWalt when you spend $299 on EGO — plus summer deals`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your summer checklist: tools, grills, 3× points`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free DeWalt tool with $299 EGO purchase + 20% off select grills`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `3× points this week, Father's Day gifts sorted, and your summer gear — all inside`
