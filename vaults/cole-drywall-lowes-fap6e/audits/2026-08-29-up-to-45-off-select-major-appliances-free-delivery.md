# Up to 45% OFF select major appliances + FREE delivery
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-29**
## Executive summary

- This is Lowe's leading with the clearest single offer they've run all month — "Get up to 45% off select appliances" with "FREE Delivery" stacked right underneath, a full stainless kitchen suite (fridge, range, microwave, dishwasher) as the hero image, and a plus-sign connector visually tying the two offers together. It's a tighter, more legible opening than 8/27's nine-module Labor Day cascade, though it's really the same appliance beat as 8/26 and 8/21's "Get up to 35% OFF select appliances" sends — just bumped to 45% and now bundled with free delivery instead of standalone.
- The problem is what comes after the hero: vanities, bathroom products, blinds/shades, three unrelated "curated" SKUs (a storage tote, cleaning powder, blinds), and a Samsung sponsored placement — the exact module-sprawl pattern that's dragged every recent Lowe's send down to a 6. The hero itself is strong; the brand just can't resist tacking on five more categories after it.
- Verdict: best-executed appliance hero from this brand recently, but it repeats a beat subscribers have already seen twice this month and dilutes it with the usual cascade.

## What's working

- Single, high-contrast hero number ("45% off") paired with a real product shot of the actual appliance suite, not a lifestyle scene.
- "FREE Delivery" gets its own green banner directly under the percentage, and spend threshold ($999+) is disclosed inline rather than buried in fine print only.
- Perk icons (free basic install, free haul-away, 2-year protection plan) are compact and scannable without needing a click.
- Clear, unambiguous "Shop now" CTA button directly below the offer stack, no scrolling needed to find it.
- No render bugs — clean layout, no overlapping text or broken images.

## What's weak

- This is the third appliance-percentage send in about a week (8/21 "35% off", 8/26 "35% off", now 8/29 "45% off") — cadence fatigue is real for anyone who opened the last two.
- After the appliance hero, the email sprawls into five more categories (vanities, bathroom, blinds, curated picks, Samsung ad) — the same dilution pattern flagged in the 8/27 and 8/28 reviews.
- The Samsung "Bespoke AI refrigerator" module is sponsored content sitting inside a Labor Day savings email — it reads as an ad break, not a curated pick, and could undercut trust in the rest of the send.
- Preview text ("Shop these Labor Day deals on appliances, bathroom essentials and more.") just previews the module list rather than adding anything the subject didn't already say.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the email after the appliance hero + one adjacent module (vanities or bathroom).** The blinds/shades, curated three-pack, and Samsung ad module should be a separate send — this single email could convert better standalone, matching the discipline of the 8/19 "Patio clearance ends tomorrow" send this brand history calls out as a high point.
- 2. **Drop or clearly disclose the Samsung placement earlier** — right now it's visually indistinguishable from Lowe's own merchandising until you spot "Sponsored" in small type at the bottom right.
- 3. **Vary the appliance beat's framing** — three sends this month have opened with "up to X% off appliances." Consider rotating in a specific product callout (e.g., "Samsung 4-door French door, was $2,999, now...") to break the repetition.
- 4. **Add urgency to the subject** since the offer has a real end date visible in the footer (8/26–9/16) — right now the subject doesn't signal a deadline at all.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus free install, haul-away & a 2-year protection plan on orders $999+.`
