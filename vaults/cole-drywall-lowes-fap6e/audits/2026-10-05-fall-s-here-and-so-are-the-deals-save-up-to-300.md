# Fall's here and so are the deals. Save up to $300
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a well-built, seasonally timed Lowe's send. The "Save up to $300" subject matches the hero, which is an EGO mower with a big "Save up to $300" badge. That fixes the mismatch from 9/30, when the subject said 65% and the hero showed 50% patio. The offer is real and the CTA is clear.
- The body is too crowded for a subject that promises one thing. It carries about seven modules: patio, power tools ($90), grills (30%), lawn and garden (25%), Kobalt, MyLowe's Money Days ($10/$20), and a batteries row. Each module has its own number and its own "Shop now." The $300 hero is also a narrow, high-end tool offer ("select outdoor power equipment"), so it isn't broadly useful. MyLowe's Money Days is the best member perk in the email, but it sits below the fold in the middle of the scroll.
- Overall, it's a solid, professional promo that does the job but doesn't stand out. It lands in the usual 6-7 range for this brand. Compared with the 10/3 "FREE Trick-or-Treat tote! Today only," it has no urgency in the hero. The only date, "October 6 – 14," is in small text inside the Money Days module.

## What's working

- The subject's "$300" matches the hero badge, so the open-to-hero handoff is clean.
- The hero is high contrast: a bold lime "Save up to $300" block with a white "Shop now" button on a real lifestyle photo.
- The "fall cleanup" and "Fall's here" framing fits the season. The leaf-blower and mower imagery is on theme.
- The MyLowe's Money Days module is clear, with tiered $10 and $20 rewards, a $200 minimum, and a visible date range, "October 6 – 14."
- Each module has its own CTA, and the Deals of the Day signup near the bottom is a useful retention hook.

## What's weak

- The email has too many competing offers: $300, $90, 30%, 25%, $10, $20. Nothing gets to be the single memorable number after the hero.
- The $300 savings is qualified only as "select outdoor power equipment and accessories." The hero doesn't say which products qualify or give a price point, which weakens it.
- The member offer and its deadline are buried below the fold, and the date range is in tiny text.
- The "Curated with you in mind" product row looks weak. It shows a storage tote and two Energizer battery packs, with truncated, awkward product names ("Project Source Commander Large 27.0 -Gallons ( 108.0 -"). That isn't personalization and it looks unpolished.
- The "Explore NEW patio arrivals, plus savings on power tools, grills and more" preheader is a laundry list. It doesn't add anything specific beyond the subject.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body to three modules: the $300 hero, one category grid (power tools plus grills), and MyLowe's Money Days. Drop the Kobalt and patio blocks into a "More new arrivals" link, or send them separately.
- 2. Move MyLowe's Money Days up to second position and add "Oct 6–14" to its headline, for example "Ends Oct 14." That gives the email the urgency it lacks.
- 3. Name the hero products or a price. For example, "EGO 56V mower kits, save up to $300," so the click has a destination.
- 4. Fix the product row. Use clean short names, drop the generic tote, and show real prices or ratings. Otherwise remove the row.
- 5. Make the preheader complement the subject instead of listing categories.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Save up to $300 on EGO mowers & more`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Fall cleanup deals: up to $300 off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus $10–$20 MyLowe's Money, Oct 6–14`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Mowers, blowers, tools & grills, all on sale`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a table-based XHTML template with web-font fallbacks and responsive media queries. The confirmed issues are a malformed DOCTYPE, a missing `lang` attribute, missing `alt` on 7 images, and the recipient's email address in plaintext in tracking-pixel URLs. The unsubscribe and authentication headers could not be verified from the data provided. The HTML source was truncated, so the body links and footer were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **34 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and link health are unverified.
- **Duplicate impression pixel.** `https://sli.lowes.com/imp?s=1090834&…&stpe=default&li_coord=` appears twice with identical parameters. This can double-count impressions.
- **Empty `li_coord=` parameter.** It is blank on both `s=1090834` pixels, which suggests a dynamic value that did not populate. It may be intentional if the value is set client-side.
- **Recipient email in the pixel URLs.** Five `sli.lowes.com/imp` URLs carry `e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app` in the query string. This exposes PII to proxies, logs and image caches.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed DOCTYPE.** The source has `PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN"` with no space after `PUBLIC`. Strict parsers and some clients may fall back to quirks mode. The `xmlns="https://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` namespace also uses `https`, where the standard value is `http`.
- **Missing `lang` attribute.** `<html xmlns=…>` has no `lang`, which screen readers use to pick pronunciation.
- **Missing `alt` on 7 images (QA-confirmed):**
  - `…/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg` and `…/m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png` are content images, so they need descriptive alt text. Without it, image-blocking clients show a blank space.
  - The 5 `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixels should carry `alt=""` and ideally `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Web fonts.** `@font-face` for Fellix and DIN Next is wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, so Outlook skips it. This is correct as long as the font stacks have web-safe fallbacks. The stacks were not visible before truncation, so this is unverified.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source. The recipient email is correctly merged into the pixel URLs, but see the PII concern in section 2. The visible portion has no greeting or name token.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found.** QA flags this, noting the AgentMail relay may strip it. It is unverified rather than confirmed missing.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found (RFC 8058).** One-click unsubscribe is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `e.lowes.com`, so alignment could be checked from a raw message or DNS.
- **Footer not inspected.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address were cut off by truncation and could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues found that can be confirmed. All 34 click links were skipped by the QA probe, and none of the visible source shows UTM parameters or landing URLs. UTM coverage and landing-page alignment cannot be assessed from this data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the DOCTYPE to `PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN"` and use `http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml` for the namespace.
2. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to `c301e24e-…jpg` and `ad569dc5-…png`, and `alt=""` plus 1x1 dimensions to the `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixels.
4. Deduplicate the `s=1090834` pixel and check why `li_coord=` is empty.
5. Replace the plaintext `e=<email>` in pixel URLs with a hashed or opaque recipient ID.
6. Re-run QA against the raw `.eml` to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC). Resolve the tracking redirects with a following probe to check landing pages and UTMs.
