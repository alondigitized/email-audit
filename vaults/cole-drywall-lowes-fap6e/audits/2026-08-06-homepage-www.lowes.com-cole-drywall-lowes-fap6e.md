# Homepage snapshot · www.lowes.com · 2026-08-06
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- First thing that hits me is a "Sign in or create an account" popup slapped right over the homepage before I've even seen what Lowe's wants to show me. Behind it I can make out a garage shot with a Craftsman tool bag and some cordless drivers, plus a "FREE Same-day Delivery" banner — that part actually looks like it's talking to a guy mid-project. But right now this reads less like "welcome back" and more like a toll booth.

## What's working

- The "Recommended" pills — Electric Range, Washing Machine, Bathroom Vanity — actually line up with what I'd be shopping for on a remodel. Feels like the site remembers me.
- The hero image behind the popup: a garage/workshop with a Craftsman tool bag and impact drivers front and center. That's my aisle.
- "FREE Same-day Delivery" banner is the kind of thing that matters when I've got drywall going up this weekend and forgot a box of screws.
- Loyalty perks spelled out plainly — points, $5 off, Silver Key free shipping — no digging required.

## What's weak

- The sign-in modal eats two-thirds of the screen the second the page loads. I can't even read the search bar behind it.
- That nav bar and "Find a..." link are half-covered — I can't tell what I'm actually navigating into.
- No deadline or urgency on any of this. "$5 off" for how long? Doesn't say.
- Nothing tells me if this Craftsman bundle in the hero is actually a discount or just a stock photo — no price, no "shop now" tied to the tools themselves.

## Recommendations

- Let me see the hero and nav before throwing the sign-in wall at me — make it a dismissible banner instead of a near-full-screen takeover.
- Put a price or "Shop the Craftsman Bag Set" CTA directly under that garage hero image, not buried behind a login prompt.
- Give the $5-off offer a deadline ("through Sunday") so it feels like something to act on now, not whenever.
- Tighten the modal placement so it doesn't clip the search bar and nav labels — looks unfinished on a phone screen.
