# BIG Black Friday savings start EARLY
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a kitchen-sink Lowe's send under a "Black Friday" banner, and it still has no single offer to hold onto. The hero says "Holiday deals are here early" with a big red block and a Shop now button, but it gives no number and no product. The first real offer is "Get up to 40% off select fire pits and outdoor heaters," and it sits below the fold in a half-width tile.
- After that the email scatters. Traeger grills, pre-lit Christmas trees from $59.98, a $500k home sweepstakes, three random "Curated with you in mind" products and a Deals of the Day sign-up all compete for attention. The "FREE same-day delivery*" banner and the myLowe's Rewards tie-in are the strongest hooks, but they are squeezed into a thin strip above the hero. It follows the pattern of the 11-12 teaser, "Members, something BIG is coming," and the 11-10 send, "Why wait?" Both used Black Friday framing without real specifics. Verdict: a tidy, well-built template with weak focus, a mid-pack send.

## What's working

- The "FREE same-day delivery*" banner with myLowe's Rewards branding is a clear, member-tied benefit.
- The "Get up to 40% off" fire pit tile has a big numeral, a strong photo and a clear button.
- The pre-lit Christmas trees tile shows a concrete "Starting at $59.98" price in a bright lime badge.
- Button treatment is consistent: navy "Shop now" and "Buy now" buttons are easy to find.
- Lowe's branding and the nav (Black Friday, Tools, Daily Deals, Appliances) are clean and trustworthy.

## What's weak

- The hero "Holiday deals are here early" has no discount, product or deadline, so the top of the email is empty.
- Too many competing offers: fire pits, Traeger, trees, sweepstakes, three random products and a Deals of the Day sign-up.
- The "Curated with you in mind" row looks arbitrary: a DEWALT battery, a toilet and a storage tote. The product names are truncated ("108.0 -") and show no price or reason to buy.
- The $500k home sweepstakes takes a big block, and its fine print is tiny and hard to read.
- The delivery claim carries an asterisk, and the conditions ($25 minimum, order by 2pm, weight limits) only show in the footer.
- The tools and appliances angle that the brand pushed on 11-06 and 11-09 is missing, so the email feels disconnected from recent sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero the offer. Replace "Holiday deals are here early" with the strongest number, such as "Up to 40% off fire pits + free same-day delivery," and put the date window next to it.
- 2. Cut the sweepstakes and Deals of the Day sign-up blocks to a single line each, or move them to a separate send.
- 3. Give the product row a theme (for example, "Top Black Friday picks") and add sale prices and full names.
- 4. Put the delivery conditions directly under the banner ("Orders $25+ before 2pm") instead of only in the footer.
- 5. Don't repeat "early" in the subject, hero and body. Pick one concrete deal and say when it ends.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML source is structurally sound for a legacy ESP template, with responsive media queries, Outlook and Hotmail fixes, and web-font fallbacks. The confirmed defects are a malformed DOCTYPE, a missing `lang` attribute, and two images without alt text. The compliance and authentication findings come from the AgentMail relay, so they are unverified. The HTML was truncated and the 30 tracking links were not probed, so the body markup, link targets and footer could not be reviewed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Not verified:** 30 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so redirect chains and destinations are untested.
- **Not verifiable:** the truncated source shows no `href` values, so I can't comment on UTM or tracking parameters.
- No broken links were confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed DOCTYPE (confirmed):** `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" ...>` has no space between `PUBLIC` and the quoted public identifier. Most clients fall back to quirks or lenient parsing, but it is invalid and can change box-model behavior in stricter renderers.
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):**
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg`
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png`

  Screen readers will announce these by filename, and nothing displays when images are blocked. Alt text may be `alt=""` if the images are decorative, but the QA check flags it as absent.
- **No `lang` attribute:** the root element is `<html xmlns="https://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">`, so screen readers can't pick a pronunciation language.
- **Namespace and DTD over `https`:** `xmlns` and the DTD URL use `https://www.w3.org/...`. The canonical XHTML namespace is `http://`, so this is a nonstandard string. It is harmless in practice, since email clients don't validate it, but it is non-conformant.
- **Web fonts:** the Fellix and DIN Next `@font-face` rules are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, so Outlook is excluded as intended. Only a few clients (Apple Mail, iOS) will load the `.woff` files, and the rest fall back. I couldn't confirm in the truncated source that every `font-family` stack ends in a web-safe fallback.
- **Legacy CSS:** `.ReadMsgBody` and `.ExternalClass` are dead weight for modern clients but harmless.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or the subject line. The truncated body couldn't be scanned.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. The QA tool says they may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. This is **unconfirmed**: the headers could have been stripped in transit, so it can't be called a sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check the raw message headers before drawing a conclusion.
- `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment status is unknown. The From domain `e.lowes.com` is a sending subdomain, but alignment can't be verified from this data.
- The physical address and visible unsubscribe link are in the truncated portion and were not checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The landing URLs were skipped (30 tracking links) and truncated out of the source, so UTM presence and landing-page alignment are unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the DOCTYPE to `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" "http://www.w3.org/TR/xhtml1/DTD/xhtml1-transitional.dtd">` and use the `http://` namespace.
2. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to `c301e24e-...jpg` and `ad569dc5-...png`, or `alt=""` if they are decorative.
4. Check the raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` before treating the compliance warnings as real.
5. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and resolve the tracking redirects. That is needed to check the link targets, UTMs, footer address and unsubscribe link, and to scan for merge-token leaks.
6. For the tool itself, treat relay-related header warnings as "unverified" rather than failures. They are likely what drags the pass rate to 57% with 0 actual issues.
