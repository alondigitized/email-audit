# Up to 65% OFF select smart home picks. Ends today
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-07**
## Executive summary

- The subject and the creative don't match. The subject says "Up to 65% OFF select smart home picks. Ends today," but the first thing a reader sees is a large blue "It's MyLowe's MONEY DAYS!" hero with $10 and $20 MyLowe's Money. The 65% smart home block comes second, and its "Ends Today" tag sits in the middle of the email. A reader who opens for the smart home deal has to scroll past a different promotion to find it.
- The email is clean and well built. There are no render bugs, and the sections are clearly separated: Money Days, smart home, 3x points, appliances, vanities, a 0% APR credit deal and a Deals of the Day sign-up. The cost is focus. It has about eight offers and almost all the CTAs read "Shop now." This is a catch-all loyalty roundup with a smart home subject line on top.
- Cadence also hurts. Up to 65% off Ring/Blink smart home gear has led sends on 9/24, 9/27 and 10/1, and it's back again today, one day after the 10/6 "MyLowe's Money Days are HEEEREEEE" send. The Money Days hero here largely repeats that email, so a subscriber sees the same lead two days running.

## What's working

- The "Ends Today" tag and the 65% number are bold and clear. The Ring doorbell and camera images make the smart home block easy to read.
- The Money Days window (Oct 6–14) and the $10 / $20 tiers are clear. The Rewards Credit Card upsell is a good loyalty hook.
- The 3x points banner with an "Activate now" button gives members a second reason to act on the smart home deal.
- The email renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlapping text. The blue header and the section dividers keep it easy to scan.
- The "Curated with you in mind" product row (storage tote, Energizer batteries, garden soil) with "Buy now" buttons offers low-friction impulse buys.

## What's weak

- The hero is Money Days, not the subject's offer. The 65% block starts roughly a fifth of the way down, so the subject's promise isn't the first thing the reader sees.
- Nearly every CTA is a generic "Shop now." None names a product or category, and the smart home button doesn't say what it opens.
- There are too many competing offers: Money Days, 65% off, 3x points, 35% off appliances, 50% off vanities, 40% off bath, blinds installation, 0% APR, and Deals of the Day. The reader's eye has nowhere to settle.
- The smart home fine print is dense, and the exclusions text at the bottom is a wall of copy. The offer claims "up to 65%," but the email shows no specific product and price.
- Smart home has been the lead in four sends in two weeks. The repeat is visible, and it risks training subscribers to ignore the "up to 65%" line.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the subject's offer first. Make the smart home 65% block the hero and move Money Days into a slim banner or the second block. If Money Days is the real priority, change the subject to match.
- 2. Replace "Shop now" on the smart home block with a specific CTA such as "Shop Ring & Blink deals," and show one product with a price (for example the doorbell at $X, was $Y).
- 3. Cut the secondary offers to three or four. Appliances, vanities and the credit deal can wait for a later send.
- 4. Rest the "ends today" urgency on one thing only. Today it competes with the Oct 6–14 Money Days window.
- 5. Rotate the hero category. After four smart home leads in two weeks, test fall projects or tools.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day: smart home deals + 3x points`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ring, Blink and Alexa deals end tonight. Members earn 3x points.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus $10 MyLowe's Money on $200+. Ends at midnight.`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The source is a table-based XHTML Transitional email with a mobile-responsive media-query set. The QA run reported 0 hard failures and 3 header warnings, which are probably relay artifacts. Confirmed issues are limited to missing `alt` attributes, a malformed DOCTYPE, and the recipient's email address appearing in plain text in tracking-pixel URLs. The HTML was truncated, so the footer, CTA hrefs and UTM parameters could not be inspected.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 36 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Their destinations and status are unverified.
- Five `sli.lowes.com/imp` impression pixels carry the recipient address in clear text in the query string, for example `https://sli.lowes.com/imp?s=1090838&e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app&p=1072026&lctg=410146703&stpe=static`.
  - This is an unhashed email address in a GET URL. It can end up in proxy, CDN and referrer logs.
  - Two pixels (`s=1090834`) are identical and fire twice, and have an empty `li_coord=` parameter.
  - The empty parameter looks like an unresolved token or missing value.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- Missing `alt` text on two content images:
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg`
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png`
  - If they carry content, they are inaccessible with images blocked. The first filename is a UUID with no descriptive fallback.
- Missing `alt` on the five `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixels. These are decorative, so they should have `alt=""` (and ideally `width="1" height="1"`). A missing attribute can make screen readers announce the URL.
- The DOCTYPE is malformed: `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN"` has no space after `PUBLIC`. Most clients tolerate it, but it is invalid.
- Custom fonts (`Fellix`, `DIN Next`) load via `@font-face` from `lowescdn.com` inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` guards. This is correctly scoped, and Outlook falls back to system fonts.
- Not assessable: dark-mode handling, and the remainder of the CSS and body, because of truncation.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found in the visible source. The recipient address is resolved in the pixel URLs, so there are no leaked `%%token%%` or `{{ }}` strings. The empty `li_coord=` value noted above may be an unpopulated dynamic field. It is not clearly a visible merge failure.

**5. Compliance**
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found, and `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The QA tool itself says these headers may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. This is unconfirmed, so it is not a verified sender defect. Verify against raw headers from the original delivery.
- The footer (unsubscribe link, physical address) is outside the truncated source, so CAN-SPAM body requirements could not be checked.
- The From domain `e.lowes.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain, consistent with aligned sending. This is not verifiable without the headers.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity**
Not assessable. The 36 tracked links were not resolved, and no UTM parameters or landing URLs appear in the truncated HTML. The subject line says "Up to 65% OFF select smart home picks. Ends today". Landing-page alignment to that offer is unverified.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the two content images, and `alt=""` plus 1×1 dimensions to the five `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixels.
2. Stop putting the raw recipient email in impression-pixel query strings. Use an opaque or hashed subscriber ID. De-duplicate the repeated `s=1090834` pixel and populate or remove the empty `li_coord=`.
3. Fix the DOCTYPE spacing: `PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD…`.
4. Capture full raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) outside the relay before judging compliance.
5. Raise the HTML truncation limit, and resolve the tracking redirects with a follow-redirects probe. That would cover the footer, UTM and landing-page checks that could not be run here.
