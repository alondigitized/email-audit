# Enjoy up to 50% OFF! Sunday Deals are here 😍
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a "weekly reset" grid email — six category tiles (appliances, patio, power tools, décor lighting, grills, outdoor power equipment), each with a distinct discount, stacked on top of each other. It's clean and well-produced, but the subject's "up to 50% OFF" claim maps to only one of six tiles (patio furniture) — the same disconnect flagged in the 08-16 "50% OFF to make the most of summer at home" send, where the hero didn't match the headline discount either. This is now a repeated pattern worth fixing at the template level.
- Where this send actually improves on recent history: the hero itself is honest about being a category rollup ("Late summer meets weekly savings") rather than over-promising a single deal, and the grid below is well-organized with a clear price-then-CTA structure per tile. But at six stacked modules plus a rewards banner, a cross-sell strip, a credit-card pitch, and a Deals-of-the-Day footer, this is a long, undifferentiated scroll with no single moment that says "this is the one to click."
- Net: solid production, zero render bugs, but this reads as inventory-clearing rather than a curated offer — and it's Lowe's sixth distinct beat in eleven days, which is starting to blur together for a subscriber checking dates against the brand history.

## What's working

- Clean, consistent tile layout — each of the six category modules has product photo, bold discount callout, one-line description, and a single "Shop now" CTA in the same visual position every time.
- No render bugs — all images load, text doesn't overlap, buttons are legible and properly aligned throughout.
- The hero headline avoids overpromising a specific number, unlike the 08-16 "50% OFF" send where the subject/hero pairing was misleading.
- Nav bar at top (Appliances, Daily Deals, Patio, Outdoor Tools) gives a quick scan path for someone who already knows what they want.
- "FREE Same-day delivery" callout on the cooling module is a genuinely differentiated, seasonally relevant hook (mid-August heat).
- Subject says "up to 50% OFF" but that number only applies to one tile (patio furniture/accessories) out of six — appliances are 35%, power tools $100, décor 20%, grills 20%, outdoor power $200. A reader clicking in expecting a blanket 50% off will be disappointed on 5/6 categories.
- Six near-identical tiles in a row with no visual hierarchy differentiation means the eye has nowhere obvious to land — nothing is "the" hero, everything is equally weighted.
- This is the sixth distinct campaign beat in eleven days (workshop, sweepstakes, patio 50%, tote, appliance/back-to-campus, now this) — cadence risk for open fatigue is real at this frequency.
- Fine print at the bottom is dense and generic across all six offers — no way to tell at a glance which restrictions apply to which tile without deep reading.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the subject/hero mismatch first — either lead with "Up to 50% off patio + more weekly deals" so the 50% claim is scoped correctly, or restructure the hero to feature patio furniture as the visual lead since that's the actual 50% item.
- 2. Cut six tiles to three — pick the top 3 offers by margin/inventory need and move the rest to a "See all weekly deals" link; six flat modules dilutes each one.
- 3. Move the "FREE Same-day delivery" cooling banner higher — it's the most differentiated, timely hook (mid-August heat) and it's currently buried two-thirds down the email.
- 4. Add a deadline to the hero — "Sunday Deals" with no explicit end-date/time reads as evergreen, not urgent; if it truly ends Sunday, say so in hero copy and subject.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 50% off patio — plus appliances, tools & more`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Sunday only: your weekly reset starts here`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `6 categories on sale through Sunday — patio, appliances, tools, décor`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free same-day delivery on cooling, plus up to 50% off patio`
