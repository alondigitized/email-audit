# Sunday Deals + Memorial Day deals = 🎉💃
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-24**
## Executive summary

- Day eight of Lowe's Memorial Day push and the brand is now stacking holidays on top of holidays — "Sunday Deals + Memorial Day deals" reads like a marketer running out of new angles two days before the actual holiday. The hero is a clean blue "Sunday Deals — Hot savings to kickstart your week" panel that lands fine visually, but the subject's emoji-math gimmick (🎉💃) is the weakest opener of the week.
- The body itself is workmanlike: a featured patio set at $298, a free LG fridge promo (eye-catching), an Extra Big Deals strip with a $1,199 LG fridge, a "summer refresh" pair (backyard / patio finds), an Affirm financing block, and Deal of the Day at the bottom. That is six distinct modules in one email, which is the same problem as the May 21 "Extra Big Deals" send — too many things competing for the same scroll.
- Net: this is filler between the May 23 member-grill email and whatever the actual Memorial Day Monday send will be. It will perform like an average Lowe's promo — fine, forgettable.

## What's working

- Hero "Sunday Deals" panel reads cleanly with strong blue/red brand color and a hot-pink accent.
- The $298 patio set price is bold, specific, and anchored next to a "FREE" fridge tile that earns attention.
- "Hurry! After tomorrow, it's gone" on the Extra Big Deals strip is the email's most credible urgency line.
- Affirm "upgrade your space starting at 0% APR" block addresses real big-ticket purchase friction.
- Layout is tidy — no broken images, no overlapping text, modules are visually separated.

## What's weak

- Stacking "Sunday Deals + Memorial Day deals" in the subject reads as cadence-fatigue, not a real campaign.
- Six modules in one email dilutes the hero — Sunday Deals, free fridge, Extra Big Deals, summer refresh, Affirm, Deal of the Day all compete.
- "Deals we thought you'd like" subheader is generic — no signal that this is actually personalized.
- Backyard / patio "Shop now / Shop now" twin CTAs are visually identical and lazy.
- No mention of Memorial Day in the visible hero despite being in the subject — the email under-delivers on its own promise.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pick one hero.** Either lead with the free LG fridge (genuine attention-grabber) or the $298 patio set — not a generic "Sunday Deals" panel that says nothing.
- 2. **Drop the emoji math in the subject.** "Sunday Deals + Memorial Day deals = 🎉💃" is the laziest construction of the week.
- 3. **Cut the modules in half.** Kill the Affirm block and the generic "summer refresh" pair; keep hero + Extra Big Deals + Deal of the Day.
- 4. **Differentiate the two CTAs** under summer refresh — "Shop patio" and "Shop outdoor décor" instead of two "Shop now" buttons.
- 5. **Reference the holiday explicitly in the hero**, not just the subject line — readers who opened expecting Memorial Day deals see "Sunday Deals" first.

# Technical Audit — Lowe's "Sunday Deals + Memorial Day deals"

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard table-based marketing email with the usual heavy use of tracking pixels and CDN-hosted webfonts. Primary technical issues are missing alt text on multiple images (including a hero JPG and a PNG), unverifiable List-Unsubscribe/auth headers via the relay, and an outdated XHTML 1.0 Transitional DOCTYPE using `https://` schema URLs.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 20 tracking/click-redirect links skipped by automated probe — link reachability could not be verified end-to-end.
- Multiple open/impression pixels firing to `sli.lowes.com/imp` and `mi.lowes.com/p/cp/…/o.gif` with the recipient address (`cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app`) exposed in plaintext query strings (`e=…`). Common for ESP relay tracking but means the email address is leaked to anyone with access to the rendered HTML or referer logs.
- `lctg=410146703` and `mi_u=410146703` recipient/list-target IDs are also exposed in the clear.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt attributes** on at least 9 images per QA findings, including:
  - Hero/content image: `image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg`
  - Content image: `image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png`
  - Tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `imp`) — these should have `alt=""` (empty) explicitly to be marked decorative.
- DOCTYPE uses `https://www.w3.org/TR/xhtml1/DTD/xhtml1-transitional.dtd` — the W3C DTD URL is canonically `http://`. Won't affect rendering in mail clients but is technically malformed.
- `xmlns="https://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` — same issue; the XHTML namespace is `http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml`.
- Four separate `@font-face` blocks loading `.woff` from `lowescdn.com` — webfonts are stripped by most clients (Gmail, Outlook desktop); fine as progressive enhancement but adds weight to `<head>`.
- Media query at `min-width: 375px and max-width: 600px` with `-webkit-min-device-pixel-ratio:0` is a WebKit-only hack and won't fire on Firefox/Outlook mobile preview — duplicate `max-width: 480px` block below covers it.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FNAME]`) detected in the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** in captured headers — may be a relay capture limitation, but cannot confirm RFC 2369 compliance.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found** — one-click unsubscribe per RFC 8058 cannot be confirmed; required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender requirements (Feb 2024+).
- **Authentication-Results header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC status cannot be verified from the relay capture. From domain `e.lowes.com` is a standard ESP subdomain that typically passes, but unverifiable here.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Cannot verify directly — all 20 outbound links route through tracking redirectors (`sli.lowes.com` / equivalent) and were skipped by HTTP probe. UTM parameter presence on final destinations is unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the two content images (`c301e24e-…jpg`, `ad569dc5-…png`); set `alt=""` and `role="presentation"` on tracking pixel `<img>` tags.
2. Verify List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post headers are emitted at the sender (likely present but stripped by the AgentMail relay capture) — required for Gmail/Yahoo bulk compliance.
3. Confirm `Authentication-Results` propagation through the relay so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status can be audited in the pipeline.
4. Fix DOCTYPE and XHTML namespace to use canonical `http://` URLs (`http://www.w3.org/TR/xhtml1/DTD/xhtml1-transitional.dtd` and `http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml`).
5. Consider re-running the link probe with an allowlist for `sli.lowes.com`/`click.e.lowes.com` redirectors to validate final destination 200s and UTM coverage.
6. Recipient email is exposed in plaintext in tracking pixel URLs — evaluate whether hashed/opaque tokens would be acceptable given list-leakage risk in forwarded emails.
