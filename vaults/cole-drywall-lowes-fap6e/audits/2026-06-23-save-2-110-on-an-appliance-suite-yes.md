# Save $2,110 on an appliance suite? YES😍
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-23**
## Executive summary

- This is a classic high-ticket appliance suite email — and the $2,110 savings figure does real work. Lowe's put the number in the subject, mirrored it immediately in the hero badge, and backed it with four specific product tiles and real sticker prices. The logic is tight: see the headline claim, scroll for the proof, and hit "Buy Now" on whatever piece you need. That's the email working as designed.
- Where it gets complicated is the cadence. This lands June 23 as the third distinct campaign direction in two days — HomeCare+ subscription (June 22), a Halloween members-teaser (also June 22), and now a full appliance push. A subscriber who opened all three in 48 hours is already trained to skip. The email itself is strong; the context it's landing in is noisy.
- Mid-email, the "Build it, spin it!" sweepstakes promo is an energy thief. A reader in purchase mode on a $1,799 fridge does not want a gamification detour with a $14 prize. It fractures the focus right before "Member Only Deals," which is actually the stronger loyalty hook.
- Verdict: technically well-executed, contextually cluttered. Best of the recent run for appliance clarity; penalized by being send #3 in 48 hours.

## What's working

- The $2,110 savings claim appears in the subject and immediately in the hero badge — zero ambiguity, subject-to-body payoff is clean.
- Product tiles with specific prices ($1,799, $1,998, $729, $499) and "Buy Now" CTAs deliver the proof the hero promises.
- The "Member Only Deals" section is well-placed mid-email as a loyalty layer for MyLowe's members.
- Navigation tabs (REFRIGERATORS, WASHERS, TOOLS, FREE PICKUP) at the top let browsers self-select without hunting — smart for an appliance email with multiple categories.
- No render bugs visible: images loaded, layout is intact, no overlapping elements.

## What's weak

- The "Build it, spin it!" sweepstakes block with a ~$14 prize interrupts what was otherwise a clean appliance-to-member-deals flow.
- The LG Craft Ice section at the bottom feels like a second email stitched on — different brand voice, different visual treatment.
- No visible urgency mechanism (no end date, no "while supplies last" callout in the hero), which lets a reader defer the $1,799 refrigerator indefinitely.
- Arriving as the third Lowe's email in roughly 36 hours (after HomeCare+ and the Halloween teaser) means most engaged subscribers have already hit their tolerance ceiling.
- "Shop now" on the hero CTA is generic; an appliance email this specific deserves something like "See all suite savings" or "Shop appliances."

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a deadline to the hero.** "Save $2,110 — this week only" or "Ends Sunday" would turn a browsing email into an action email. The number is compelling; the urgency is absent.
- 2. **Cut or relocate "Build it, spin it!"** The $14 sweepstakes tangent sits between two strong commercial sections and undermines purchase intent. Move to footer or a dedicated retention email.
- 3. **Make the hero CTA specific.** Swap "Shop now" for "Shop the suite" or "See all appliance deals" to match the subject's specificity.
- 4. **Merge or defer the LG Craft Ice section.** It's a product-spotlight email competing with a savings-suite email in the same scroll. One of them should go.
- 5. **Rest the list.** After HomeCare+ and Halloween in the same day, this send needed a 24-hour gap minimum.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Missing alt text — 2 content images** (high priority):
- `c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg` (`image.e.lowes.com`) — likely a hero/banner image; no `alt` attribute
- `ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png` (`image.e.lowes.com`) — likely a product or promo image; no `alt` attribute

Both will render as broken image icons with no fallback text in image-blocking clients (Outlook default, corporate firewalls).

**Missing alt text — tracking pixels** (low priority): 5 `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixels and the `o.gif` pixel lack `alt=""`. These should carry an empty alt, not be omitted. Minor WCAG 1.1.1 violation but functionally negligible for tracking pixels.

**Overlapping media query breakpoints** — the CSS defines two conflicting responsive ranges:
- `@media screen and (min-width: 375px) and (max-width: 600px)` (Fellix/custom fonts block)
- `@media only screen and (max-width: 480px)` (layout block)

The 375–480px range triggers both simultaneously. In practice this likely causes no visual bug (the second ruleset wins via cascade specificity), but it creates unpredictable rendering on mid-range viewport widths.

**Font loading** — `woff` format only (no `woff2`). Not a rendering defect in email clients, but `woff2` would improve load time on supporting clients (Apple Mail, some mobile). Custom fonts will gracefully fall back to system fonts in Outlook and Gmail.

### 5. Compliance

| Header | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `List-Unsubscribe` | **NOT FOUND** | AgentMail relay may not capture; unconfirmed |
| `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) | **NOT FOUND** | One-click unsubscribe cannot be verified |

**List-Unsubscribe gap is the only actionable compliance risk.** Gmail and Yahoo now enforce one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058 `List-Unsubscribe-Post`) for bulk senders. If Lowe's SFMC deployment does not set this header, bulk sending reputation can be degraded. This is a **relay capture gap** — the header may exist on the wire but not be surfaced through AgentMail. Verify by pulling raw headers directly.

CAN-SPAM: sender domain `e.lowes.com` is a recognized Lowe's ESP subdomain. Physical address and unsubscribe mechanism are expected in the body (truncated source does not show footer, but Lowe's transactional emails consistently include these).

Cannot confirm or deny UTM presence without redirect resolution. Recommend adding a link-resolution step to the QA pipeline.

### 7. Recommendations

**P1 — Add alt text to content images.** `c301e24e...jpg` and `ad569dc5...png` need descriptive `alt` attributes. Lowe's image-blocking rate in enterprise Outlook clients is significant; these images carry the offer value (appliance suite, pricing) and are invisible without alt text.

**P1 (pipeline) — Resolve tracking redirects in QA.** 21 links are unverified. Add a redirect-following HTTP probe to the QA pipeline to confirm UTM parameters and landing page reachability. Without this, broken links post-deploy go undetected.
