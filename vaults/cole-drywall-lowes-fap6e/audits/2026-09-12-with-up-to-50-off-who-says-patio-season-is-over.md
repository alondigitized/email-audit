# With up to 50% OFF, who says patio season is over?
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-12**
## Executive summary

- This is Lowe's leading with a clean, well-hierarchied patio hero — "Up to 50% off select patio furniture and accessories" is the first thing you see, with a "Lowe's Deals" red flag badge and a legible wicker-furniture photo, no scroll needed. It's a stronger open than the recycled "$800" leaf banners that ran three times this week (8/31, 9/2, 9/7) — this is a genuine new creative direction into patio/grill/game-day territory rather than another Labor Day recolor.
- Where it falls apart is depth: after the hero, this becomes an unfocused kitchen-sink scroll — grills, outdoor lighting, 3x points activation, a $99 fire pit, NFL buckets, Kobalt toolboxes, a free ice mold "Sponsored by LG," curated picks, and EGO mowers, none of which reinforces the patio-furniture subject line. The subject promises patio season; by the third screen you're being sold Buffalo Bills buckets and a Craftsman hedge trimmer.
- It's a solidly built hero with a genuinely honest, specific offer, undercut by a body that sprawls into unrelated categories and buries the thing that got you to open.

## What's working

- Hero delivers exactly what the subject promises — "50% off" patio furniture, big legible numeral, badge, visible CTA, all above the fold.
- Clear price-point product cards ($99 fire pit, $8.98 buckets) give a concrete reason to click beyond vague percentage-off copy.
- No render bugs — images load, text is legible, buttons are consistent navy pills throughout.
- Loyalty tie-in ("Activate and earn more," MyLowe's Rewards 3x points) is visible for members without cluttering the hero.

## What's weak

- Body sprawls far outside "patio season" — NFL merch, Kobalt toolboxes, an LG-sponsored ice mold, and EGO mowers dilute the subject's promise.
- Two sponsored/third-party product placements (LG ice mold, EGO mower "Sponsored by") sit inside a promotional email without visual separation, muddying trust in what's an actual Lowe's deal vs. an ad.
- Grills and outdoor lighting sections repeat the "Get up to X% off" pattern three times in a row with no variation, creating scroll fatigue.
- "Curated with you in mind" section (storage bin, drill battery, hedge trimmer) reads generic and unrelated to patio, weakening the personalization claim.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email down to patio/outdoor living only — furniture, grills, lighting, fire pit — and move NFL/Kobalt/EGO/ice-mold content to a separate send; this single change would sharpen relevance the most.
- 2. Move the 3x points/Activate module higher, right under the hero, since it's the most differentiated content (per 9/9's send) and currently gets buried under three flat percent-off blocks.
- 3. Visually flag sponsored placements (LG mold, EGO mower) with a distinct background or "Ad" label so they don't read as native Lowe's offers.
- 4. Add a deadline/timer to the patio hero — "up to 50% off" with no end date reads less urgent than the 9/8 "ENDS TOMORROW" ticker send.
