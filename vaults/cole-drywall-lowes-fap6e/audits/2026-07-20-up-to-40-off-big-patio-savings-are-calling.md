# Up to 40% OFF. Big patio savings are calling📞
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-20**
## Executive summary

- This is Lowe's playing a straight summer-patio game — "up to 40% off select patio furniture and accessories" as the hero, flanked by 25% off garden plants and outdoor lighting, with a free same-day delivery sweetener. The creative is clean and seasonally on-point. The 40% anchor in the subject carries the email.
- The strategic issue is a familiar Lowe's problem: too many competing asks in one scroll. The "MyLowe's Money Days returning soon!" banner at the very top — before even the hero — pulls the eye off the main offer and teases a future event that isn't actionable today. It's a classic case of Lowe's trying to do five things in one email (patio savings, plants, lighting, cooling gear, 12-month financing) and none of them getting the full attention they deserve. The Closeout Event from July 17–18 showed Lowe's can do focused, category-tight sends; this one doesn't hold that discipline.
- Net judgment: a useful, honest patio email that will convert casual browsers but misses the ceiling because of split focus and no deadline pressure on the main offer.

## What's working

- **Hero is immediately readable** — "Up to 40% OFF" is large, high-contrast, and confirmed by the sofa lifestyle image. No ambiguity about the category.
- **Loyalty value prop is explicit** — the 3x points member benefit is called out as a separate module, not buried in fine print.
- **FREE same-day delivery** is a strong low-friction signal in the cooling section — removes a logistical objection.
- **Subject does its job** — the 40% and "patio" make it category-clear before you open.
- **The 25% tiles are clean** — garden plants and outdoor lighting in side-by-side blocks is a digestible upsell without overshadowing the hero.

## What's weak

- **"Money Days returning soon" banner hijacks the entry point** — the first thing the eye hits is not the patio offer; it's a teaser for a future event. That's a diversion, not a warm-up.
- **No deadline on the main 40% offer** — summer clearance pricing without an expiration date removes urgency entirely. Compare to July 17–18 Closeout sends which ran "Ends tomorrow" and earned their click.
- **"Shop Now" CTAs are generic across every module** — five modules, five "Shop Now" buttons. None differentiate the offer type.
- **Financing module at the bottom feels misplaced** — the 12-month credit deal is a different purchase mindset than "I want a new patio chair." It interrupts the flow.
- **The hero headline "Make more of your summer rolling"** is soft and filler — doesn't reinforce the discount or create urgency.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the "Money Days returning soon" banner or move it to the footer.** It leaks attention from the hero. If you must tease it, use it as the preheader, not the opening visual.
- 2. **Add a deadline to the 40% offer.** Even "through Sunday" or "this weekend only" would materially lift urgency. The July 18 send proved Lowe's subscribers respond to closing windows.
- 3. **Replace generic "Shop Now" buttons with offer-specific copy** — "Shop Patio Furniture," "Shop Plants," "Shop Lighting" — each CTA should name the category.
- 4. **Rewrite the hero headline** to reinforce the savings: "Your patio for 40% less. All season." is sharper than "Make more of your summer rolling."
- 5. **Move the financing module out of the main email** — include it as a footer note or reserve it for a dedicated credit-promo send.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Patio furniture up to 40% off — this weekend`
- **Subject Alt B:** `40% off patio. Plants. Lighting. All summer.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Outdoor furniture, garden, lighting — all on sale now through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop the full patio line-up before the deals end.`
