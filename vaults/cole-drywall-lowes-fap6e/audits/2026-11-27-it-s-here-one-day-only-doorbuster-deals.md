# It's HERE‼️ One-day-only Doorbuster Deals
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Lowe's Black Friday send in 48 hours, and the weakest of the run. The hero promises "One-day-only Doorbuster Deals" and shows a DeWalt miter saw. There is no price, no percentage and no product name anywhere in it. The subject and hero rely on urgency and a vague "get 'em before they're gone." Earlier today's "Black Friday Doorbuster Deals are LIVE" at least showed three priced products, and this email doesn't.
- The only concrete offers are low in the layout. They are "up to 50% off select major appliances" in the Whirlpool block and "FREE same-day delivery*" in the top banner, which carries an asterisk and a $25 minimum plus loyalty membership. Every other module says "Shop now" with no offer attached. The "12 Days of Deals / Ending tonight" block on holiday décor competes with the doorbuster message, and "Deals of the Day" at the bottom adds a fourth.
- The render itself is clean, with no broken images. For a persona focused on drywall and home projects, the miter saw is a mild fit. Overall it reads as a template stack, not a targeted send.

## What's working

- The hero is bold and easy to read, with a black field, red accent and a large product shot that pulls the eye first.
- The Whirlpool block has the only real offer ("up to 50% off select major appliances") with free delivery, install and haul-away, plus the fine print visible right below it.
- The nav bar (Black Friday, Tools, Daily Deals, Appliances) and the "FREE same-day delivery" banner give quick orientation.
- "Ending tonight" on the 12 Days of Deals block is credible, time-bound urgency.
- The layout renders cleanly with no overlap or broken images.

## What's weak

- The hero has no price, discount or product name, and the miter saw isn't tied to any deal.
- There are five "Shop now" or similar buttons with identical copy, and none says what you'd get.
- "One-day-only," "Ending tonight," "Offer ends 12/2/26" and "Deals of the Day" send mixed deadlines in one email.
- The free-delivery banner is asterisked and exclusionary ($25 minimum, loyalty membership, 2pm order cutoff, weight and size limits).
- It's the third near-identical Black Friday send in two days, after "Black Friday Doorbuster Deals are LIVE" and "Thanksgiving Day is serving up to 50% OFF," so it adds little new.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a specific priced deal in the hero, for example "DeWalt 12-in miter saw, $X, today only," and link the button to that product.
- 2. Collapse the stacked modules into one deadline and one story. Drop "Deals of the Day" and the sign-up row, which is odd to ask of someone already subscribed.
- 3. Replace the repeated "Shop now" copy with specific labels like "Shop tool doorbusters," "Shop appliance deals" and "Shop holiday décor."
- 4. Move the delivery terms out of the top banner or state them plainly ("Free same-day delivery on $25+").
- 5. Fix the cadence by suppressing or merging sends on a day that already had a Black Friday email.

# Technical Audit: Lowe's "It's HERE‼️ One-day-only Doorbuster Deals"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a conventional table-based ESP template with working viewport and media-query handling. Confirmed problems are a malformed DOCTYPE, missing alt attributes on 19 images, and the recipient's email address in plaintext in tracking-pixel URLs. The three compliance and header warnings are most likely artifacts of the AgentMail relay, not defects in the email.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 29 tracking or click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destination status and redirect chains are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Duplicate impression pixels:** several `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixels appear twice with identical parameters. Duplicates can double-count impressions.
  - `s=127768300` through `s=127768304`, all with `p=532009&stpe=pixel`
  - `s=2019286` and `s=1090834`, both with `stpe=default&li_coord…`
- **PII in tracking URLs:** every `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixel carries `e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app`, the recipient's address in plaintext. This exposes the address to the ad-server host and to any logs or proxies that fetch the image.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed DOCTYPE:** the source has `PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN"` with no space between `PUBLIC` and the opening quote. Clients typically tolerate it, but it can trigger quirks mode in strict parsers.
- **Wrong XHTML namespace:** `xmlns="https://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` uses `https`. The canonical namespace is `http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml`. This is harmless in practice but technically incorrect.
- **No `lang` attribute:** the `<html>` tag has none (`<html xmlns=…>`). Screen readers cannot pick the right pronunciation without it.
- **Missing alt on content images:**
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg`
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png`

  If these are meaningful, they are invisible with images blocked or in a screen reader.
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels:** 17 `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixel `<img>` tags have no `alt`. These are decorative, so they need `alt=""`, not descriptive text. Without it, some screen readers read out the filename or URL.
- **External web fonts:** Fellix and DIN Next load from `lowescdn.com` inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` blocks. Fallbacks are not visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm the font stack degrades cleanly.
- **Not assessable:** the HTML was truncated, so I can't check `<title>`, the full font stacks, or the body markup.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[[…]]` appear in the visible source or the QA data. The only personalization is the recipient address in the pixel URLs, covered above.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** and **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found:** the QA check itself says these may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. Treat both as unconfirmed.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same reason. This is not evidence of a failure.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** these were not checked because the source is truncated.
- **Sender identity:** `From: "Lowe's Home Improvement" <lowes@e.lowes.com>` is a consistent branded subdomain, with no issue in the evidence.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The 29 tracking links were not resolved, and no UTM parameters or final landing URLs appear in the supplied data. I can't confirm campaign parameters, deal-page alignment, or "one-day-only" landing-page consistency.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the DOCTYPE spacing and the XHTML namespace, and add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the two `image.e.lowes.com` images. Add `alt=""` to all `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixels.
3. Remove the plaintext recipient email (`e=`) from impression-pixel URLs, using an opaque or hashed subscriber ID instead.
4. De-duplicate the repeated pixels (`s=127768300` to `304`, `2019286`, `1090834`) to avoid inflated impression counts.
5. For QA coverage, capture the raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from the original message source, not the relay copy, before treating the compliance warnings as defects.
6. Resolve the 29 skipped tracking links in a follow-up pass to verify redirect chains, UTM parameters and landing pages.
