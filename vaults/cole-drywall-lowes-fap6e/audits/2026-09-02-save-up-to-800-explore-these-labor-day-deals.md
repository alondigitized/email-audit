# Save up to $800! Explore these Labor Day deals
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-02**
## Executive summary

- This is nearly the same hero Lowe's ran on 8/31 ("Save up to $800! Labor Day lawn deals are here") — same blue leaf-scattered background, same "$800" headline, same "Shop now" button placement — just retitled as a generic Labor Day event opener rather than a lawn-specific push. It reads as a template reuse rather than a fresh creative, and a subscriber who opened yesterday's email will recognize it instantly.
- Below the hero it sprawls the way most recent Lowe's sends do: a same-day delivery banner, a "Curated with you in mind" grid of three completely unrelated products (a storage tote, an all-purpose cleaner, and mini blinds), then an Affirm financing ad, then a "Deals of the Day" newsletter signup block. None of these modules connect to the $800 offer or to each other — there's no product actually shown that ties to the headline claim.
- The hero alone is clean and legible, so it'll likely perform fine on the open, but the body does nothing to earn the click past the first screen. Net: a repeat asset with a diluted, disconnected body — a step down from 8/31's stronger, more cohesive send.

## What's working

- The hero headline "Get ready for fall with savings up to $800" is bold, high-contrast, and readable at a glance.
- "Shop now" CTA button is clearly visible directly below the offer with no scrolling needed.
- The free same-day delivery banner for members is a distinct, concrete loyalty benefit with its own CTA.
- No render bugs — all images, buttons, and text render cleanly with proper alignment.

## What's weak

- The "Curated with you in mind" product grid (storage tote, cleaner, mini blinds) has zero thematic connection to Labor Day or to each other — it reads as random inventory filler.
- No specific product or category is shown to back up the "$800" claim — the hero is a number with no proof point.
- This is a visual repeat of the 8/31 "$800" hero — same background art, same layout, same button style, just re-labeled — which will read as stale to anyone who opened both.
- The email cascades into an Affirm ad and a separate newsletter signup CTA, which compete with and dilute the primary Labor Day offer instead of reinforcing it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the repeated 8/31 hero art with a new Labor Day visual, and show at least one actual $800-eligible product (appliance, grill, patio set) directly under the claim so the number has a receipt.
- 2. Cut or re-theme the "Curated with you in mind" grid — a tote, a cleaner, and blinds undercut the Labor Day narrative; swap in seasonal/fall items or drop the module.
- 3. Move the free same-day delivery banner higher or merge it into the hero as a supporting line, since it's a strong differentiator that's currently getting lost mid-scroll.
- 4. Trim the Affirm and newsletter-signup blocks to the footer — they're distractions competing with the one CTA that matters.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Labor Day: fall savings up to $800`
- **Subject Alt B:** `$800 off fall projects — Labor Day starts now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus, free same-day delivery for members.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop appliances, patio, and more before it's gone.`
