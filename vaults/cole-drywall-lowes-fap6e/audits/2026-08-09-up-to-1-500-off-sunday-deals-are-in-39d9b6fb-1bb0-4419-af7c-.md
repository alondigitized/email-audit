# Up to $1,500 OFF! Sunday Deals are in 👉
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-09**
## Executive summary

- This is Lowe's back on its safe, generic "Sunday Deals" beat — a blue gradient hero, "Shop now" buttons, a two-tile appliance/patio grid, then a cascade of unrelated modules (Essentials sub-brand, moving-supplies cross-sell, financing offer, Deals-of-the-Day signup). It's competent and on-brand but diffuse: the subject promises "up to $1,500 off," yet the hero itself doesn't restate that number, and by the time the eye reaches the $1,500 LG appliance tile it's competed against six other CTAs.
- Compared to 8/4's "Closeout Event ends tomorrow" and 8/6's Storage Event sends, this one reads as filler — a Sunday catch-all with no urgency mechanic (no expiration date on the headline offers) and a title ("Sunday Deals") that's now stale by the time most subscribers open weekday mail. The two named offers (LG appliances, patio furniture) are real and specific, which keeps this from being a total miss, but the email tries to do five things (appliances, patio, essentials, moving supplies, financing) instead of leading hard with one.
- Net: a middle-of-the-road send. Solid production values, no render bugs, honest specific offers — but unfocused messaging and no urgency keep it out of the brand's top tier.

## What's working

- Hero headline "Sunday Deals" plus two clean product photos (fridge, patio set) with quantified offers directly beneath them — $1,500 off LG appliances, up to 40% off patio furniture.
- "Clearance" badge on the patio photo adds a credible urgency cue that the generic hero lacks.
- Consistent navy/blue Lowe's branding and a repeated, unambiguous "Shop now" button pattern throughout.
- No render bugs — images, buttons, and text all display cleanly with no overlap or broken assets.
- Financing offer (12 months special financing on $299+) is concrete and clearly laid out with dates (7/9/26–8/19/26).

## What's weak

- Subject promises "Up to $1,500 OFF" but the hero banner doesn't repeat that number — the eye has to scroll past a generic tagline before finding the actual dollar offer.
- No expiration/urgency date on the two headline deals (LG appliances, patio) — only the buried financing offer has a date range.
- Message sprawl: appliances, patio, "Essentials under $10," moving supplies, financing, and Deals-of-the-Day signup all compete in one email with no clear hierarchy after the top fold.
- "Sunday Deals" as a title feels stale/cadence-fatigued — this brand has sent five distinctly-themed promos in the last two weeks (Closeout, Storage, MrBeast, campus points), and this one reads as the least differentiated.
- Moving-supplies section ("Curated with you in mind") is a jarring non-sequitur next to appliances/patio — no clear rationale for why it's here.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the actual number in the hero: replace "Sunday Deals / Just what your home needs at prices you'll love" with "Save up to $1,500 on Appliances" so subject and hero match and the offer is visible without hunting.
- 2. Add a real deadline under the hero CTA (e.g., "Ends Sunday at midnight") — right now nothing in the top two-thirds of the email is time-bound, which kills urgency Open/Click signals.
- 3. Cut the module count — drop or move the moving-supplies cross-sell out of this send; it dilutes an already appliance/patio-focused email.
- 4. Reorder so financing (12 months, 0% interest) sits right under the appliance tile it applies to, not four screens later — that's a strong purchase-decision lever being wasted.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 40% off patio furniture and 12-month financing on $299+`
