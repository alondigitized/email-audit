# Get up to 35% off select LG appliances.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-21**
## Executive summary

- This is Lowe's finally pairing a real category with a real, specific offer — "Get up to 35% off select LG appliances" over a clean hero of an NFL-jerseyed spokesperson leaning on an LG fridge, with a legible "35% off + up to $600 bundle bonus" stack and an explicit 10/7/26 deadline. Compared to 9/16's appliance send (also 45% off, also LG-adjacent), this one is more organized: it segments straight into four sub-category tiles (refrigerators 40%, laundry 30%, ranges 35%, dishwashers 35%) each with its own "Shop now" button, so there's no ambiguity about where to click for your specific need.
- The NFL/LG crossover branding is a little busy for an appliance email — the jersey and NFL shield logo compete with the actual appliance and percentage callout for eye space — but it doesn't break the read. MyLowe's Rewards perks (free delivery/haul-away, free install, 2-year protection plan) are called out cleanly in a distinct black band, which is a good touch for loyalty members.
- Verdict: solid, on-brand appliance push with a real number and real urgency — a clear step up from the vague "Sunday Deals" send, and more focused than the free-gift/concert-ticket detours of the past week.

## What's working

- Concrete, large "35% off" hero number paired with a hard deadline ("Offer ends 10/7/26") — no vague savings language.
- Four-tile category grid (refrigerators, laundry, ranges, dishwashers) each with its own %, product photo, and "Shop now" CTA — easy self-navigation.
- MyLowe's Rewards perk block (free delivery/haul-away, free install, 2-year protection) is visually distinct and gives loyalty members a clear extra reason to click.
- LG SIGNATURE section adds an aspirational/premium beat without derailing the primary savings message.
- Clean rendering throughout — no overlapping text, no broken images, no lorem-ipsum.

## What's weak

- The NFL jersey/shield branding crowds the hero and doesn't obviously connect to appliance shopping — it dilutes rather than reinforces the offer.
- "Up to" is used on every single tile (35%, 40%, 30%, 35%, 35%) with no anchor product or price shown anywhere — a shopper can't tell what they'd actually pay until they click.
- The "$600 bundle bonus" fine print ("when you bundle select LG and LG SIGNATURE appliances") is a real restriction buried in small text under the hero — borderline bait-and-switch for anyone not buying two appliances.
- Preview text ("Your dream appliance upgrade just got a lot more innovative") is vague filler that doesn't mention LG, 35%, or the deadline — a missed chance to reinforce the subject.

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop or shrink the NFL crossover branding in the hero and let the appliance + percentage own the space — it's competing with the offer for attention.
- 2. Add one anchor price example (e.g., "LG French Door Fridge, was $2,599, now $1,689") somewhere in the hero so "up to 35%" feels concrete rather than a ceiling nobody hits.
- 3. Move the bundle bonus restriction ("must bundle 2+ LG/LG SIGNATURE appliances") into slightly larger text near the $600 callout, not just a fine-print line — protects trust and reduces post-click frustration.
- 4. Rewrite the preheader to reference the actual offer instead of generic "innovative" copy.
- **Subject Alt A:** `LG appliances: up to 35% off, ends 10/7`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Save up to $600 more on LG appliance bundles`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus up to $600 off when you bundle LG + LG SIGNATURE.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Refrigerators up to 40% off — deal ends 10/7.`
