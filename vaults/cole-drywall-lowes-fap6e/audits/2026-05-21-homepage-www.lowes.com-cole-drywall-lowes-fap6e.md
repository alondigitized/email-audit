# Homepage snapshot · www.lowes.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- First thing I see when I open Lowe's is a sign-in modal blocking half the page — classic. But I'll give them this: they're pushing Memorial Day hard, which is exactly when I buy appliances, so the timing is right. Feels like a store that knows what weekend this is, but is making me do a little work before I can get to the good stuff.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day timing is dead-on.** I specifically come to Lowe's during Memorial Day for appliances. The banner and hero are both locked in on that message.
- **Appliance category chips** (Electric Range, Washing Machine) below the modal are one-tap shortcuts to exactly what I'd be shopping. That's smart placement even if they're half-covered.
- **Sign-in modal benefits are specific.** Silver Key free shipping and points earning are things I actually care about as a MyLowe's Rewards + Advantage card holder. It's not just "join our newsletter."
- **Free same-day delivery callout.** For a weekend project, being able to order and get it same-day matters. That line earns attention.
- **Memorial Day tribute banner** (the flag + "We remember loved ones...") is a classy touch that fits the weekend without being heavy-handed.

## What's weak

- **The forced sign-in modal is a wall.** I came to see Memorial Day deals, not to create an account. Blocking the page on load on mobile is annoying — I know where the sign-in button is.
- **No tools, no Kobalt, no DIY imagery anywhere.** Nothing in this viewport signals "hey, guys who are knee-deep in a basement project, this is for you." The hero is a generic navy graphic.
- **"Shop All" is a weak CTA.** Both the hero CTA and the link peeking through the modal just say "Shop All." Where am I shopping? Give me "Shop Memorial Day Deals" or "Shop Appliances" — something with direction.
- **No urgency deadline visible.** The Memorial Day deals are live but I can't see any "ends Sunday" or countdown. I know Memorial Day ends Monday, but nothing on screen is pushing me to act now.
- **Asterisks on everything.** $5 off* — asterisk. FREE shipping with Silver Key status* — asterisk. Free same-day delivery on select items.* — asterisk. Three fine-print flags in one modal is a trust drag.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the sign-in modal or convert it to a persistent bottom sheet.** Don't block the homepage on load — surface it as a rewards reminder at the bottom of the screen after a scroll, not a gate.
- **Swap the generic navy hero graphic for a lifestyle shot of a guy mid-project** — someone hauling a new washer into the laundry room, or running Kobalt tools in a basement. That's the Cole scene.
- **Replace "Shop All" with a category-specific CTA**: "Shop Appliances" or "Memorial Day Tool Deals." Give me somewhere to land, not a department store entrance.
- **Add an explicit deadline** — "Memorial Day deals end May 26" would take five words and actually push me to buy before I close the tab.
