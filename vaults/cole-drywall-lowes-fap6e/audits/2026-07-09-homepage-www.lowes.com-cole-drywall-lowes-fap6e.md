# Homepage snapshot · www.lowes.com · 2026-07-09
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-09**
## Executive summary

- First thing that hits me when I open the app is a sign-in modal blocking the entire page — I'm already a MyLowe's Rewards member, already have the Advantage card, and Lowe's decides the first thing I need to see is a pitch to create an account. That said, once I can see past it, the MyLowe's Money Days section is exactly the kind of deal I watch for, and the $30 extra on a $200 purchase is the type of offer I'll actually use when I'm running out mid-project for drywall compound and screws. Right now the page feels like it's more interested in signing me up than helping me shop.

## What's working

- **MyLowe's Money Days promo** — "Members earn up to $30 extra in MyLowe's Money on an eligible purchase of $200 or more" with a hard deadline of 7/12/26 is exactly the kind of stacking deal I watch for when I've got a big material run coming. That offer would make me consolidate a shopping trip.
- **Dual Rewards branding** — showing both MyLowe's Rewards and MyLowe's PRO Rewards in the same section signals they know their audience skews toward serious project guys, not just casual browse-and-go shoppers.
- **Deadline specificity** — "Offer ends 7/12/26" is credible and time-bound. I know this isn't a permanent markdown that's been sitting there for months.
- **Top banner** — "Shop this week's top deals now. >" is small but it tells me there's a deals page with fresh inventory. That's worth a tap once I close the modal.

## What's weak

- **Sign-in modal as the first-screen experience** — I'm a returning member with the Advantage card. Hitting me with "create an account" before I can even see what's on the homepage is exactly why I sometimes just go to the HD app instead. This is anti-shopper.
- **Category pills are appliance-heavy** — "Electric Range," "Washing Machine," "Bathroom Vanity" — none of those are what I came here for mid-basement-project. I want drywall, fasteners, Kobalt tools, maybe lighting. The recommendations feel AI-generic, not pulled from my actual purchase history.
- **No visible navigation to building materials or tools** — the hamburger menu is there but nothing shortcut-wise to my actual shopping zone is visible below the modal.
- **"$5 off when you join"** — this is a new-member pitch buried in a modal aimed at returning customers. Feels like a misfire for anyone with an existing account.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the interstitial sign-in modal for logged-in members** — if the app knows my account, drop the modal entirely. Show me my rewards balance and personalized deals instead. That swap would eliminate the biggest friction point on the page.
- **Swap the recommended category pills to reflect my actual purchase history** — I've bought drywall compound, Kobalt tools, and subfloor screws. Surface those, not Electric Range, based on what I actually buy.
- **Lead with a "MyLowe's Money Days — Shop Now" button** in the promo section instead of just "Learn More." The offer is solid; the CTA is weak. Make it a direct path to qualifying products.
- **Add a building materials or tools shortcut chip** to the horizontal category row so a basement-project guy can get to lumber, drywall, or fasteners in one tap without hunting the hamburger nav.
