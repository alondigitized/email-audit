# Save up to $800! Labor Day lawn deals are here
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-31**
## Executive summary

- This is the strongest hero Lowe's has run in this brand history — "Save up to $800" against a genuinely styled porch scene with mums, pumpkins, and lanterns, immediately followed by a specific, real offer ("Save up to $300 — Select outdoor power equipment") instead of the vague "Sunday Deals" filler seen on 8/30 and 8/23. It also actually follows through on the subject line's lawn focus: pressure washers, riding mowers, mulch, grass seed, garden soil — every module below the fold is on-category, which is a first for this brand's recent cadence.
- Where it falls down is the same place every recent Lowe's send falls down: after the strong opening two modules, it sprawls into a "Curated with you in mind" grid of unrelated items (storage bin, all-purpose cleaner, faux-wood blinds) that has nothing to do with Labor Day or lawn care, diluting the focus the top of the email earned.
- Net: this is a top-tier execution for Lowe's relative to its last six sends — clear hero, matching subcopy, on-category cascade — dragged down only by the late unrelated cross-sell block. Call it a clear open-and-click winner within this brand's own history.

## What's working

- Hero delivers exactly what the subject promises — "$800" in the subject matches "$800" in the hero art, first time in this run that's happened cleanly.
- Seasonal styling is genuinely on-theme: mums, pumpkins, lanterns on a porch — feels like Labor Day, not stock inventory.
- Every module through "FREE same-day delivery" stays on the lawn/outdoor category promised by the subject line (mowers, seed, mulch, soil, pressure washers).
- Specific numeric offers stack cleanly (up to $300, up to $100, up to $200, 4-for-$10, 50% off, 3-for-$10) — no vague "big savings" language like the 8/30 send.
- Clean single-CTA-per-module layout with no overlapping text or broken images.

## What's weak

- "Curated with you in mind" block (storage bin, cleaner, faux-wood blinds) breaks category focus right after a well-targeted lawn cascade — undercuts the specificity that made the top of the email work.
- Nine-plus modules is still a long scroll, continuing the sprawl pattern flagged on 8/27 and 8/23, even though the content itself is better targeted this time.
- "Deals of the Day" sign-up CTA near the footer competes with the primary Shop Now CTAs for attention this late in the email.
- No urgency/deadline copy anywhere (no "ends Monday" or similar) despite Labor Day being an inherently time-boxed event.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the "Curated with you in mind" module entirely for this send — it's the single biggest focus-breaker in an otherwise on-category email; if cross-sell is required, swap in lawn/outdoor adjacent items instead.
- 2. Add an explicit deadline ("Ends Labor Day, Mon 9/7") near the hero CTA to convert the implied urgency of "Labor Day" into a credible countdown.
- 3. Trim the module count from nine to five or six — keep hero, power equipment, mowers, mulch/seed/soil — and drop the redundant "Deals of the Day" sign-up block, which has no place this deep in a promo email.
- 4. Move "FREE same-day delivery" copy up near the hero since it's a strong differentiator currently buried mid-scroll.
