# Homepage snapshot · www.lowes.com · 2026-06-25
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-25**
## Executive summary

- Lowe's is pushing a July 4th sale hard and I can see why — that's when guys like me pull the trigger on appliances and big-ticket tool buys. But the first thing I see when I open the app is a sign-in modal blocking half the page, which is annoying before I've even looked at a single product. Brand feels familiar and solid, but right now it's in "give me your email" mode before it gives me anything useful.

## What's working

- **July 4th banner at the very top** — I know this sale is happening and that's exactly when I'm planning to pull the trigger on a new range for the basement wet bar area. Timing is right.
- **Sign-in modal loyalty pitch** — "Earn points on eligible purchases" and "FREE shipping with Silver Key status" are real reasons to log in. I'm already a MyLowe's member, so that resonates.
- **Category chips below the modal** — "Bathroom Vanity" chip is right there. That's actually useful for where I am in the project.
- **Same-day delivery callout** — "get free same-day delivery on select items" plus the $25 threshold is practical info I care about.

## What's weak

- **Modal blocking the hero on open** — I came here to browse, not to get a sign-in popup before I've seen one product. That's the kind of move that makes me want to close the app.
- **Hero text is clipped** — "mmer's hottest deals are he" is literally cut off mid-word because the modal is covering it. That looks broken even if it isn't technically a bug.
- **"Shop Now" CTA is completely generic** — July 4th, shop what? Tools? Appliances? Lumber? I need a category signal to know if this sale is for me.
- **Category chips don't match a finisher's priorities** — "Electric Range" and "Washing Machine" are appliance plays. Where's "Drywall," "Paint," "Flooring," "Lighting"? The chips feel randomly ordered.
- **No Kobalt or Allen + Roth signal anywhere** — These are my brands. If you know I'm a Lowe's loyalist, lead with them.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the sign-in modal on open, or make it a slide-up strip** — put the loyalty pitch in a less aggressive location so I see the actual deals first.
- **Make the "Shop Now" hero CTA category-specific** — "Shop Power Tools" or "Shop July 4th Appliance Deals" is far more actionable than a generic "Shop Now."
- **Swap the category chips to reflect project-stage categories** — "Drywall & Insulation," "Flooring," "Lighting," "Paint" would land better for someone mid-project than "Electric Range."
- **Surface a "Kobalt Deals" or "Allen + Roth" featured block somewhere in the above-fold content** — it would feel like Lowe's knows who I am and why I'm here.
