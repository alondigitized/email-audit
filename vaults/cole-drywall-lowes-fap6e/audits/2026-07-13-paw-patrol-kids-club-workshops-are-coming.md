# PAW Patrol & Kids Club Workshops are coming🐾
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-13**
## Executive summary

- This is Lowe's Kids Club collab with the PAW Patrol Dino Movie — a free in-store workshop on building a Dino Island Volcano, wrapped around an IP tie-in and a member sweepstakes for movie tickets. The timing is defensible: Money Days ended yesterday (July 12), so this isn't competing with a live promo the way the "Tiny pallet coasters" email (July 10, 6/10) awkwardly stepped on Day 3 of that event. Clean break is fine.
- The execution is adequate but the targeting question dominates everything else. Lowe's has enough purchase and household data to know which MyLowe's Rewards members have children. If this was sent to the full list rather than the parent segment, most recipients will read "PAW Patrol & Kids Club" and immediately tune out. The email itself is clean — no bugs, logical flow, one strong free-offer hook — but it's wasted on an unsegmented send.
- The three-section structure (movie announcement → workshop registration → sweepstakes) is logical but dilutes the primary CTA. "Register now" is where the email wants you to go, but it competes with "Learn more" above and "Enter today" below. Pick a lane.

## What's working

- **FREE is prominent.** The "FREE Kids Club Workshop!" callout is styled to stand out — anyone who registers a child will clock it immediately.
- **Preheader adds value.** "A dino-adventure awaits! Grab a FREE kit inspired by PAW Patrol: The Dino Movie." introduces the free-kit hook that the subject never mentions — a rare case where the preheader does supplementary work.
- **Visual brand is coherent.** PAW Patrol characters, the volcano craft, and the FundamentZ sweepstake image all feel on-brief for the IP collab. No obvious render issues.
- **Member sweepstakes is a smart loyalty add.** Tying the prize (movie tickets + FundamentZ pack) to the MyLowe's Rewards membership gives members a reason to care beyond the workshop.

## What's weak

- **No list segmentation signal visible.** Nothing in the email indicates it was sent to a parent or family segment — this reads like a full-list blast, which means the majority of recipients see PAW Patrol and stop reading.
- **"Are coming" is stale on arrival.** Registration opened July 11 (per the small-print in the email). The subject says "coming" but it's already here. Feels like the send was delayed.
- **Three CTAs, no clear winner.** "Learn more," "Register now," and "Enter today" each ask for something different. The hierarchy should funnel toward registration and treat the sweepstakes as a footer bonus.
- **No workshop date in the subject or above the fold.** The specific date (August 11 or similar) is visible only in the workshop section body. A date in the subject would create the urgency this email completely lacks.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Segment the list before the next Kids Club send.** If Lowe's can flag households with children under 12 based on purchase history (kids' workshop attendance, toy/craft purchases, family-adjacent categories), this email should only go to that cohort. Sending it to home improvement DIYers who don't have kids is a deliverability tax and trains the wrong subscribers to ignore the sender.
- 2. **Lead the subject with FREE and the date.** "Are coming" wastes the urgency budget. Put the date and the offer in the subject where they drive opens.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free PAW Patrol Dino workshop at Lowe's — Aug 11🐾`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Build a volcano FREE at Lowe's with PAW Patrol Aug 11`
- 3. **Rewrite the preheader to add the sweepstakes hook.** The current preheader focuses on the free kit — good, but the movie-ticket sweepstakes is a stronger lure for the parent segment that would open this.
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free workshop Aug 11 + enter to win PAW Patrol movie tickets.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Register for a free in-store Dino Island Volcano build — plus win movie passes.`
- 4. **Collapse to one CTA above the fold.** Kill "Learn more" from the hero — the workshop registration IS the product. Merge the movie announcement and workshop block into one section with a single "Register now" button. The sweepstakes can stay as a loyalty footer.
- 5. **Surface the August date in the hero.** "August 11" should appear in large type in the workshop section, not buried in fine print. Specificity is what converts lookers into registrants.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Confirmed missing alt text:**
```
src: https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/1/a22207c1-63c7-47c0-9e00-61227828e1fe.jpg
```
This image has no `alt` attribute. Screen readers will skip it silently; image-blocked clients (Outlook default, corporate proxies) will render a broken image placeholder with no fallback text.

**WOFF-only font declarations — no WOFF2:**
```css
url('https://www.lowescdn.com/sffonts/Fellix-Regular.woff') format('woff')
```
All six `@font-face` blocks specify only `.woff`. WOFF2 is ~30% smaller and is the browser default since 2016. Missing WOFF2 increases font load time on all modern clients.

**CDN version inconsistency in DIN Next fonts:**
- `DIN Next` (regular): `scaffold/1.4.4/fonts/…`
- `DIN Next Bold` / `DIN Next Black`: `scaffold/1.2.0/fonts/…`

This is a potential drift issue if `1.2.0` assets are ever removed from CDN.

**Dead legacy hacks (low severity):**
`.ReadMsgBody` and `.ExternalClass` selectors target Hotmail (discontinued ~2013). These are harmless dead code but inflate stylesheet size.

**Non-standard media query condition:**
```css
@media screen and (min-width: 375px) and (max-width: 600px) and (-webkit-min-device-pixel-ratio:0)
```
`-webkit-min-device-pixel-ratio` is non-standard and effectively limits this breakpoint to WebKit/Blink engines only. Firefox and older email clients will not apply these styles.

**Hiding via color (fragile Gmail hack):**
```css
a.mobileshow, .mobilehide { display: none; color: #fff; }
```
The `color: #fff` is a Gmail-specific workaround for Gmail stripping `display:none`. If background color changes, this element becomes visible.

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** (QA confirmed).
Required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk sender policies (enforced since February 2024 for senders >5K/day). Its absence risks inbox placement degradation or deferrals on both platforms.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found** (RFC 8058).
One-click machine unsubscribe (required alongside `List-Unsubscribe` by Google/Yahoo policy) is not present or not captured by the AgentMail relay. If the header exists at the MTA layer and is being dropped by the relay, that needs to be confirmed with the ESP.

**`Authentication-Results` header not captured.**
SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status is unknown. Given the sending domain is `e.lowes.com` (a dedicated ESP subdomain), DKIM should be configured — but this cannot be confirmed from the relay-captured headers. A direct SMTP-layer capture or ESP dashboard check is needed.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

Cannot confirm UTM parameter presence on destination URLs because all 30 CTAs route through redirect/tracking wrappers that were not probed. Risk: if UTM params are applied at the redirect layer (common) rather than in the HTML, any redirect misconfiguration silently drops attribution.

**Recommended spot-check:** Manually click one CTA in a test send and inspect the final landing URL for `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, and `utm_content`.
