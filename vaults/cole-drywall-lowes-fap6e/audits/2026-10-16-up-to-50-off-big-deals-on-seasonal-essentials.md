# Up to 50% OFF! Big deals on seasonal essentials🍂
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent but scattered Lowe's seasonal roundup. It promises "Up to 50% OFF" in the subject, but the hero is a 25%-off backyard BBQ under the headline "Is it even fall without a backyard BBQ?" The 50% Halloween clearance doesn't appear until the fourth module, so the best offer is buried. The same mismatch hit the 10/7 smart-home send, where the subject and hero didn't line up.
- The body is eight or so stacked modules: grills, lawn care, power tools, snow blower, Halloween, lighting, ceiling fans and a holiday tree. Each has its own "Shop now" button. That makes it easy to scan but gives the eye nowhere to land. For a subscriber who got Money Days sends on 10/11 and 10/14 and the 50% Halloween send on 10/9, this reads as another general blast. It doesn't read as something chosen for them.
- My judgment is a middling send, solid in execution and weak in focus. It would work better led by the 50% Halloween offer the subject promises, or with a subject that matches the grill hero.

## What's working

- The grill hero is bold. The oversized "25% off" on a blue field next to a real grill photo reads in a second.
- Lowe's branding is consistent: the navy header and nav, blue module headers and the red "Deals of the Day" bar.
- The Halloween scarecrow module with "Get up to 50% off" is the strongest visual in the email.
- The seasonal framing (grills, Halloween, cozy evenings, holiday tree) fits mid-October.
- The "Deals of the Day" signup bar near the bottom gives a clear retention path.

## What's weak

- The subject leads with "Up to 50%", but the hero offer is 25%. The 50% sits roughly a third of the way down.
- There are too many offers (25%, $95, $200, 50%, 25%, 25%) and ten or more equal-weight "Shop now" buttons, so there's no single hero.
- The "Curated with you in mind" row isn't personalized in any way I can see. The items are a storage tote, lawn bags and garden soil.
- The product names in that row are truncated, e.g. "Commander Large 27.0 -Gallons ( 108.0 -". The paper lawn bag image is also a plain brown placeholder-looking bag.
- Nothing mentions MyLowe's Rewards or member pricing, even though the last several sends leaned on Money Days.
- Every button says "Shop now", so none of the CTAs tells you what's behind the click.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the 50% Halloween clearance as the hero, or change the subject to match the grill hero. The subject and first screen need to say the same thing.
- 2. Cut to three or four modules, with one primary CTA and the rest secondary.
- 3. Fix the "Curated" row: use full product names, real product imagery and prices, and make it actually reflect the recipient's browsing (the recipient looks like a drywall or contractor profile, so tools and supplies would fit).
- 4. Make CTA copy specific, e.g. "Shop grills", "Shop Halloween clearance", "Shop power tools".
- 5. Add a time-bounded line such as "Halloween clearance ends 10/31", and a member-benefit line to bring back the rewards angle.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is mostly standard ESP-generated table HTML, but the QA data shows missing alt text on 7 images and the recipient's address exposed in tracking-pixel URLs. The List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post and Authentication-Results headers could not be verified. The HTML was truncated, so the footer, body links and UTM parameters were not available to inspect.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 36 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe (QA: "36 tracking link(s) skipped"). Their destinations and status codes are unverified, not confirmed broken.
- **Address in impression pixels:** `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixels carry the recipient address in clear text. Example: `https://sli.lowes.com/imp?s=1090838&e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app&p=10162026&lctg=410146703&stpe=static`. The same pattern appears on 5 pixels (`s=1090834` twice, `1090835`, `1090837`, `1090838`). This leaks PII into image URLs, which end up in proxy logs, image-proxy caches and third-party logs. The address is also not URL-encoded (`@` is raw).
- **Pixel count:** Five separate impression pixels (`stpe=static` and `stpe=default`) fire per open. This adds to load and tracking surface, and the pixels are only worth keeping if each one is used.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** 7 images have no alt attribute (QA flagged).
  - Content images:
    - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg`
    - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png`
    These are not tracking pixels and need meaningful alt text. With images blocked, they render as nothing, or screen readers announce the filename.
  - Tracking pixels (`sli.lowes.com/imp`) should carry `alt=""` (plus `width="1" height="1"`) so screen readers skip them.
- **Malformed DOCTYPE:** `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" ...>` has no space after `PUBLIC`. The DTD URL and `xmlns` also use `https://`, which doesn't match the canonical identifiers. Most clients tolerate this, but it is invalid and can trigger quirks mode in some renderers.
- **Responsive CSS:** No issues found beyond the above. The `@media` rules and `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` font wrappers are well-formed in the visible portion.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) were found in the visible HTML. The only personalization is the recipient address in the pixel URLs (see section 2), and the truncation limits what can be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both are reported absent. QA notes the headers "may not be captured by AgentMail relay", so this is unconfirmed, not a confirmed sender defect. Check the raw `.eml` or the original delivery. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not present, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The relay is the likely cause. The `From` domain `e.lowes.com` is a subdomain, so alignment should be checked on the raw message.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** The truncated HTML does not show them, so they cannot be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The 36 tracking links were not probed, the HTML was truncated before the body links, and no final landing URLs or UTM parameters are visible. There is no evidence of a defect, and none can be ruled out.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the data leak:** Remove the plaintext `e=<email>` parameter from the `sli.lowes.com/imp` URLs, or replace it with an opaque hashed or ID-based token.
2. **Fix alt text:** Add descriptive alt text to the two content images (`c301e24e-…jpg`, `ad569dc5-…png`). Add `alt=""` and explicit 1×1 dimensions to the `imp` pixels.
3. **Fix the DOCTYPE:** Change it to `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" "http://www.w3.org/TR/xhtml1/DTD/xhtml1-transitional.dtd">` and use `http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml` for `xmlns`.
4. **Close the QA gaps:** Capture raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`, DKIM-Signature) outside the relay. Resolve a sample of the 36 tracking redirects to check final URLs, status codes and UTM parameters. Stop truncating the HTML before the footer, so unsubscribe and address compliance can be checked.
5. **Review the QA scoring:** The report shows a 57% pass rate with 0 issues and 3 warnings, which doesn't reconcile with the 10 warnings listed. Worth checking the scoring logic.
