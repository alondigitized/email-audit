# MrBeast Workshops are almost out 😳
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-27**
## Executive summary

- This is Lowe's third dedicated MrBeast Kids Club email in nine days (May 18, May 21, now May 27), and the scarcity angle is the right call for a Saturday workshop that's three days out. The "LAST CHANCE" ticker at the top plus the "Going FAAAAST" hero headline makes the urgency unmistakable — this is a close send, not an awareness send, and it behaves like one. The $14.78 price anchor is visible without scrolling, and the layout is clean.
- The weakness is cadence fatigue. Anyone who received the May 21 "Comin' in HOT 🔥 MrBeast Workshop's here 🎉" email — which led with essentially the same MrBeast creative — will feel déjà vu before they read the urgency copy. The secondary block ("Plan ahead: There's more MrBeast lined up!") undercuts the LAST CHANCE frame; if there's more coming, why is this urgent?
- Clear judgement: a solid functional close email that will convert the already-interested, but won't pull in anyone who passed on the last two sends.

## What's working

- **"LAST CHANCE" ticker** is immediate and visually dominant — zero ambiguity about the email's purpose.
- **Price ($14.78) and date (Saturday, May 30) are both visible in the hero** — the two facts needed to decide are right there.
- **Three workshops shown in one scroll** — smart to surface the upcoming Swarm Spinner and Swarm Jet events for parents who missed the first workshop but want the next one.
- **Digital badges section** — the gamification mechanic ("collect exclusive MrBeast digital badges") is a real differentiator and gives kids a reason to drag their parent back.
- **No render bugs** — layout is clean, images load, buttons are intact.

## What's weak

- **Secondary block contradicts the urgency frame** — "Plan ahead: There's more MrBeast lined up!" signals abundance, not scarcity; a last-chance email should not have a "coming up next" section.
- **"Register now" is generic across all three CTAs** — no differentiation between the imminent Swarm Launcher (Saturday) and the future workshops; the urgency gradient is lost.
- **MrBeast art is recycled from the May 21 send** — same crossed-arms pose, same blue background; returning subscribers see an email that feels like a resend.
- **No social proof** — workshops this popular to warrant "LAST CHANCE" language, but zero reviews, attendance numbers, or waitlist signals to validate the scarcity claim.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Remove or relocate the "Plan ahead" secondary block.** If it must stay, move it below the fold or to a follow-up email after the Swarm Launcher event closes. The close email should have one job.
- 2. **Differentiate CTA copy by urgency tier** — "Reserve my spot (3 left!)" or "Grab the last seats — May 30" for the hero vs. "Save my date" for future workshops.
- 3. **Add a seat count or attendance signal** — even "Only a few spots remaining" directly under the CTA would validate the LAST CHANCE claim without speculation.
- 4. **Swap MrBeast art** — use a different photo or action pose to avoid the "resend" feeling for anyone who got the May 21 email.
