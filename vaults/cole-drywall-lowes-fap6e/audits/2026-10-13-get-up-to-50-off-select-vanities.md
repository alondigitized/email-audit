# Get up to 50% OFF select vanities👏
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a clear, well-built category send. The hero says "Get up to 50% off select vanities" with a big "50%" and a white vanity. A "Save up to an additional $300" tier table sits under it (spend $500–1,499 save $50, $1,500–2,499 save $175, $2,499+ save $300), followed by a members-only "FREE next-day delivery" strip. The subject matches the hero, and the "Ends Tomorrow" tag adds credible urgency. This fixes the subject/creative mismatch from 10/7 ("Up to 65% OFF select smart home picks" over a Money Days hero).
- The weakness is the rest of the email, which is a long stack of unrelated modules. Bathroom is covered well for two modules, then paint, gifts, Money Days, a random product row and an NFL/PROSharp ad follow. The tail is generic. The "Curated with you in mind" row shows a storage tote, a paper lawn bag and garden soil, which have nothing to do with vanities. Money Days appears for the fourth time in eight days (10/6, 10/7, 10/9, 10/11). It is the same $10/$20 offer, so the fatigue is real.
- Verdict: strong top third, weak bottom two-thirds. It would sit at the top of Lowe's recent sends if the lower modules were cut.

## What's working

- The hero is unmistakable. The oversized "50% off" and the vanity photo put the eye on the offer first.
- The spend-tier table (save $50, $175, $300) is concrete and rewards a bigger basket.
- "Ends Tomorrow" is credible and matches the Money Days end date of Oct 14.
- The members "FREE next-day delivery" strip adds a real loyalty perk on a bulky item.
- The bath modules ("main character era", up to 40% off bathroom products) deepen the category with a lifestyle image and a product image.

## What's weak

- The "Curated with you in mind" row is off-topic and weak. The tote, paper lawn bag and garden soil have truncated, awkward product names ("27.0 -Gallons ( 108.0 -") and no prices.
- Paint, gifts, Money Days and the PROSharp ad dilute a focused vanity send. The email scrolls roughly 4,000px on the original.
- Money Days is repeated again with the same $10/$20 offer. It is the fourth send in eight days and adds nothing new.
- Fine print is dense, and the vanity hero offers no product names, prices or sizes. The hero has a single "Shop now" for all vanities.
- The "Great gifts" module is vague and has no offer. The Deals of the Day banner and signup at the bottom are generic.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut everything below the bath modules except one Money Days strip. Keep the email on vanities.
- 2. Replace the "Curated" row with three real vanities showing size, price and a "was/now" line. Add star ratings.
- 3. Add a second hero CTA that names the action, such as "Shop vanities" or "Shop 50% off vanities", in place of the generic "Shop now".
- 4. Fix the truncated product titles and add prices wherever products appear.
- 5. Fold the Money Days $10/$20 into one line, since subscribers have seen it all week.

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email's markup is mostly standard (XHTML 1.0 Transitional, MSO conditionals, responsive media queries), but the QA run found missing alt text on 7 images and could not confirm unsubscribe headers or authentication. The automated pass rate was 57% with 0 hard failures and 3 warnings.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 34 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they use click-redirect domains. Their destinations are unverified, so I can't confirm they resolve or land on the right page.
- Five `https://sli.lowes.com/imp?...` impression pixels carry the recipient address in plaintext in the query string: `e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app`. The pixels are `s=1090838`, `1090834` (twice), `1090835` and `1090837`. Any proxy, cache or log along the image path can read the address. That is a PII-in-URL concern.
- The `1090834` pixel appears twice with `li_coord` parameters, so it may fire twice per open. The truncated source doesn't show whether that is intentional.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- Seven images are flagged for missing alt text:
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg`
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png`
  - The five `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixels.
- The two `image.e.lowes.com` files are content images (`.jpg` and `.png`). Without alt text, clients that block images show nothing, and screen readers announce the filename or nothing.
- The tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` and ideally `width="1" height="1"`. I can't confirm those attributes from the truncated source.
- The DOCTYPE is malformed: `PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD...` has no space after `PUBLIC`. Most clients tolerate it, but it is invalid and may trigger quirks mode in strict parsers.
- The XHTML namespace and DTD URLs use `https://` rather than the canonical `http://`. This is harmless in practice but non-standard.
- Web fonts (`Fellix`, `DIN Next`) are loaded via `@font-face` inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` blocks. Gmail and Outlook ignore these and fall back, so I'd check that the font stacks have web-safe fallbacks. The truncated source doesn't show them.
- Responsive rules are gated by `(min-width:375px) and (max-width:600px) and (-webkit-min-device-pixel-ratio:0)`. That targets WebKit only, so non-WebKit clients between 375 and 600px rely on the separate `max-width:480px` query.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) appear in the visible source or QA output. The truncated HTML limits this check.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` was not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed sender defect.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was not found, so one-click unsubscribe support is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a relay limitation, not a defect in the email.
- The physical address and visible unsubscribe link are not in the truncated source. They need checking against the full HTML.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Landing pages could not be verified. All 34 tracking links were skipped, so UTM parameters and destination alignment with the "up to 50% OFF select vanities" offer are unconfirmed.
- The `p=10132026` parameter on the impression pixels suggests a send date of 10/13/2026. That is consistent with today's date, but it isn't a UTM parameter.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the two content images. Add `alt=""` to the five `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixels.
2. Replace the plaintext email address in the impression-pixel `e=` parameter with a hashed or opaque subscriber ID.
3. Fix the DOCTYPE spacing (`PUBLIC "-//W3C//...`) and use `http://` namespace and DTD URLs.
4. Capture raw headers directly, not through the relay. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC alignment).
5. Resolve the redirect chains for the 34 skipped links, using a redirect-following probe or the sender's click-tracking API. Then verify UTM parameters and that the vanity-sale landing page matches the offer.
6. Check the full HTML for the footer unsubscribe link, the physical postal address, and font fallbacks.
