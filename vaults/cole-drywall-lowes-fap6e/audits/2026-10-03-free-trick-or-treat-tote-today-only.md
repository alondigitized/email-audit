# FREE Trick-or-Treat tote! Today only 🎃
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-03**
## Executive summary

- Lowe's leads with a Halloween giveaway: a big green "FREE" over a glowing ghost tote, with "In-store Only" and "Today Only" badges. The hero is loud and clear, and the 10 a.m. start time gives it real urgency. It's also a kid's trick-or-treat bag, available only in a store, only today, only while supplies last. For a drywall contractor shopping Lowe's for work, that's a novelty, not a reason to get in the truck.
- The email has five modules for five different audiences: a tote, a first-responder upgrade, dryer-vent cleaning, a $10/$20 MyLowe's Money spend-and-get, and $9.98 builder kits. The most useful offer for this persona is the Oct 6–14 "$10 MyLowe's Money on $200+" (or $20 with the credit card). It sits fourth, under a pile of other offers. After last week's near-daily run of Ring/Blink and patio discounts (10/1, 9/27, 9/24), a rewards-program send is a welcome change of pace, but it's a scattered one.
- Verdict: a competent, well-rendered loyalty email that is a better fit for families than for a contractor. It would score higher if the $200 spend-and-get led the email.

## What's working

- The hero is unmistakable. The big "FREE" block, ghost tote and "Today Only" badge make the eye land on the offer first.
- The urgency is credible: "Starting at 10 a.m." and "Limited quantities. While supplies last" explain why it's today only.
- The seasonal fit is strong. The Halloween tote, October fire-safety month and the Oct 6–14 date window all match the calendar.
- The $10/$20 MyLowe's Money module is clear. It has a real threshold ($200), a real window (Oct 6–14) and a credit-card upsell.
- The email renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlapping text, and the dark-navy "We Help. You Save." footer gives it a clear close.

## What's weak

- The hero offer is irrelevant to this persona. A trick-or-treat tote has nothing to do with drywall or contractor work, and "In-store Only" adds friction.
- The CTAs are weak. "Learn more" on the hero, and "Verify now", "Subscribe" and "Shop now" below it, don't tell the reader what they'll get.
- The module order is off. The best member offer ($10–$20 back) is fourth, behind a first-responder module that only a small group can use.
- The preheader promises "earn 3x points". I don't see that offer anywhere in the rendered email.
- The footer fine print runs to six-plus dense paragraphs of legal text. The MyLowe's Money terms (no cash back, 30-day expiry, exclusions) cut against the hero's friendly tone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the money. For members who buy at volume, make "$10 back on $200+, $20 with the Rewards card, Oct 6–14" the hero and move the tote to a secondary banner. Better still, segment: send tote-first to household and family members, and spend-and-get-first to pro and trade buyers.
- 2. Rewrite the hero CTA. "Learn more" should be "Claim my free tote" or "Find my store".
- 3. Make the preheader match the body. Either show the 3x points offer in the email or stop mentioning it.
- 4. Cut or conditionally show the first-responder module for people who aren't verified first responders. That makes the email shorter and sharper.
- 5. Add a "Pro" or "Supplies" row (drywall, joint compound, tools) to the MyLowe's Money module so it reads as relevant to contractors.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free Lowe's tote at 10 a.m. + $10 back on $200`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Members: free tote today, $10 back Oct 6–14`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `In-store only, while supplies last. Plus $10 MyLowe's Money on $200+ starting Oct 6.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Pick yours up at 10 a.m. — then stack $20 MyLowe's Money with your Rewards card.`

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **15 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destinations and redirect chains are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a defect.
- No broken links were confirmed. Asset hosts seen in the visible source (`www.lowescdn.com` fonts, `image.e.lowes.com` images) share the `lowes.com` parent domain with the sender.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed by QA):** `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/1/a22207c1-63c7-47c0-9e00-61227828e1fe.jpg`. Screen readers and image-blocked clients will get no fallback for this image. If it carries the tote offer, that content is lost for those users.
- **Malformed DOCTYPE (confirmed in source):** `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" ...>` has no space between `PUBLIC` and the quoted identifier. Most clients tolerate it, but strict parsers may drop to quirks mode.
- **Non-standard namespace and DTD scheme:** `xmlns="https://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` and the DTD URL use `https://`. The canonical namespace is `http://`. This is harmless in practice, but it deviates from the spec.
- **Present and correct in the visible source:**
  - `viewport` meta

  - `charset=UTF-8`
  - the `[if !mso]` guard around the web-font `@font-face` blocks

- The `lang` attribute and role="presentation" on layout tables can't be assessed from the truncated source.

No issues found. The truncated source shows no unresolved merge tokens such as `%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, or `[FIRSTNAME]`. The body wasn't visible, so this is not a full clearance.
