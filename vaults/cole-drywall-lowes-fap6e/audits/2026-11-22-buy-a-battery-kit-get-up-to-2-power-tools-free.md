# Buy a battery kit, get up to 2 power tools FREE
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises one thing: buy a battery kit, get up to 2 power tools free. The body opens with something else. The first hero is a generic red "Black Friday" banner with a "Shop now" button and no offer. The actual "2 FREE select power tools" block sits second, under a black "Keep the savings going as you power up" header. The subject is the strongest asset, and the top of the email wastes the attention it earns.
- After the power-tool block the email turns into the usual Lowe's kitchen-sink template. It stacks $135 off, 25% off tool storage, 50% off ladders, a $500k home sweepstakes, a credit card promo, a three-product row with a reindeer animatronic, and Deals of the Day. Too many offers compete for attention. This is close to the 2026-11-19 "Up to 2 FREE power tools? Yes, please" send, which scored 8 because the free-tools hero led. This one repeats the offer but buries it behind a content-free banner.
- Judgement: a good subject let down by a weak hero order and a cluttered body. It's a mid-pack Lowe's send, about a 6.

## What's working

- The subject names a concrete, unusual offer (up to 2 free power tools with a battery kit), and the body delivers it.
- The "2 FREE" green highlight block is the strongest visual in the email. It has a clear hierarchy and shows real DEWALT and Kobalt products.
- The brand names (DEWALT, CRAFTSMAN, Kobalt) make the offer credible and specific.
- The black mylowe's Rewards "FREE same-day delivery" bar at the top shows a member benefit right away.
- The layout renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlapping text, and every block has a visible button.

## What's weak

- The first hero is a bare "Black Friday" banner with a "Shop now" button and no offer. It pushes the subject's promise below the first screen.
- The email has about nine offer blocks. The power-tool promise gets diluted by $135 off, 25% off, 50% off ladders, the sweepstakes, 5% off with the credit card, and Deals of the Day.
- Nearly every button says "Shop now," which tells the reader nothing about where it leads. "Enter only in the Lowe's app" and "Learn more" are the only variations.
- The preheader leads with ladders and Black Friday deals, not the headline power-tool offer.
- The fine print is dense. The "select power tools" qualifier, the battery kit requirement and the sweepstakes legal text are all small, which weakens how honest the offer feels.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap the order so the "2 FREE select power tools" block is the first hero. Remove the empty Black Friday banner or merge it into the offer.
- 2. Cut the body to three blocks at most: the free-tools hero, one tool-storage or savings block, and one loyalty or credit block. Move the sweepstakes and the curated row to a different send.
- 3. Replace the generic "Shop now" buttons with specific copy such as "Get 2 free tools" and "Shop battery kits."
- 4. Put the real qualifier ("with select DEWALT, CRAFTSMAN or Kobalt battery kit") in readable type directly under the headline.
- 5. Add a deadline. Nothing here says when the deal ends.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your DEWALT kit just got 2 free tools 🛠`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Pick a DEWALT, CRAFTSMAN or Kobalt battery kit. We'll add up to 2 select tools free.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus $135 off select power tools and 25% off tool storage, this week only.`

- **Recipient email in pixel URLs:** All 16 `sli.lowes.com/imp` impression pixels carry the recipient address in plaintext. Examples:
