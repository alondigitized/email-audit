# Get our NEW summer candle before everyone else☀️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-07**
## Executive summary

- This is Lowe's pulling a hard creative pivot after nine consecutive July 4th deal emails. Where every prior send in this run was percentage-off, deadline-driven, SKU-heavy — this one is soft: a new candle, "It's sunshine in a jar," member-only early access, fragrance notes, warm yellow aesthetic. That pivot is smart from a fatigue standpoint (the July 4th cadence had to end somewhere), but the mismatch between "MyLowe's Rewards" and "summer candle lifestyle email" is real. This feels like it wandered in from a Bath & Body Works playbook.
- The "Member Only Access" badge is the email's best asset — it makes opening feel like a privilege, not a promotion. But the execution squanders that by showing no price, no discount, and no deadline. "Before everyone else" is urgency by implication only. There's nothing to act on except a "Shop now" button to somewhere unspecified.
- The single-product focus is clean and the visual hierarchy is clear. This is a well-rendered, non-cluttered email — a genuine upgrade over the six-offer chaos of the July 1 Countdown Deals send. But it's selling a scented candle at a hardware store, and the value proposition is entirely invisible until you click.

## What's working

- **"Member Only Access" badge** is prominent and positioned correctly — it signals privilege before the offer, which is exactly right for early access
- **Single-product focus** is a sharp contrast to the cluttered multi-SKU July 4th emails; eye goes directly to the candle
- **Fragrance copy** ("hints of soft florals, neroli, bright citrus and vanilla") does actual product work — that's more specific than most candle launches get
- **Visual tone** — warm yellow, citrus imagery, summer palette — is cohesive and seasonally on-point
- **Clean render** — no broken images, no layout bugs, no overlap; structurally sound

## What's weak

- **No price anywhere** — member early access with no price is a tease, not an offer; there's nothing to evaluate
- **"Shop now" CTA is maximally generic** — for a single hero product, "Shop the Candle" or "Get Early Access" would close the loop the headline opened
- **"Before everyone else" urgency has no teeth** — no date, no countdown, no quantity limit makes this feel aspirational rather than actionable
- **Product-brand fit gap** — Lowe's sending a scented candle email creates real cognitive dissonance; the subscriber base is skewed toward home improvement deals, not fragrance launches
- **Top banner text is illegible** at render size — whatever early-access copy is there is functionally invisible

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a price or a concrete member benefit in the hero** — even "Members get it first at $X" or "Exclusive member pricing" closes the value loop without a click
- 2. **Rewrite the CTA** — "Get Early Access" or "Shop the Candle" beats "Shop now" for a single-product early-access email
- 3. **Add a hard early-access window** — "Member access ends [date]" or "Public launch [date]" turns implied urgency into real urgency
- 4. **Make the top banner legible** — whatever that copy is, bump the font size; it's currently invisible on mobile-scale renders
- **Subject Alt A:** `Members only: our new summer candle is here first`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New drop: sunshine in a jar — members get early access`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Bright citrus, soft florals, vanilla — shop before it goes public`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Early access ends soon. See the new summer scent.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Image missing alt text (confirmed by QA):**
```
src: https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/1/
     a22207c1-63c7-47c0-9e00-61227828e1fe.jpg
```
Empty or absent `alt=""` on a content image degrades screen readers and image-blocked clients (Outlook default, many corporate environments).

**Duplicate `local()` font hints** in every `@font-face` declaration:
```css
src: local('Fellix Regular'), local('Fellix Regular'), url(...)
```
The duplicate `local()` hint is harmless but indicates a template copy-paste error.

**Inconsistent CDN versions** for DIN Next font family:
- `din-next.woff` → `scaffold/1.4.4/fonts/`
- `din-next-bold.woff` → `scaffold/1.2.0/fonts/`
- `din-next-black.woff` → `scaffold/1.2.0/fonts/`

DIN Next Regular is on a newer scaffold version than Bold/Black. If `1.2.0` paths are ever deprecated, Bold and Black weights will fall back to system fonts silently.

**WOFF only, no WOFF2.** Not a blocker — email client support for WOFF2 is minimal — but the CDN could serve WOFF2 with a `format('woff2')` hint for clients that support it (Apple Mail, newer Outlook).

**Overlapping media queries** with different breakpoints (`max-width: 600px` + `-webkit-min-device-pixel-ratio:0` targeting iOS Safari, and a separate `max-width: 480px` block) define some of the same class names (`.mobile-hidden`, `.drop`, `.container`) at different widths. If a 500px viewport hits the 600px query but not the 480px query, class behavior diverges. Low risk on current device landscape but worth unifying.

### 5. Compliance

| Check | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `List-Unsubscribe` header | **MISSING** | Not captured by AgentMail relay |
| `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click) | **MISSING** | Not captured by AgentMail relay |
| Physical mailing address | **NOT DETECTED** | QA plain-text scan found no address block |
| SPF/DKIM/DMARC | **UNKNOWN** | `Authentication-Results` header absent from relay capture |

**CAN-SPAM risk:** A physical mailing address is legally required. It may exist in the HTML body below the truncation point, but the QA plain-text scan did not detect it — this warrants manual verification against the full render.

**`List-Unsubscribe` / RFC 8058:** Gmail and Apple Mail use these headers to surface the native unsubscribe button. Absence degrades deliverability reputation and increases "mark as spam" rates. These are set at the ESP (ExactTarget/Salesforce Marketing Cloud, based on `e.lowes.com`) send configuration, not in the HTML — the fix is on the ESP side.

**Authentication gap:** SPF/DKIM status is unverifiable because `Authentication-Results` was not relayed. This is a monitoring gap, not necessarily a sending gap — Lowe's ESP almost certainly signs outbound mail. Confirm via a direct inbox header inspection (`Show original` in Gmail).
