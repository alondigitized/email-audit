# Homepage snapshot · www.lowes.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- First thing I see when I hit Lowe's is a sign-in modal blocking half the page — not exactly rolling out the welcome mat for a guy who already has the Lowe's Advantage card. Once I push past that, the homepage is clearly running Memorial Day hard, and the hero is pushing high-end kitchen appliances. That's actually right in my wheelhouse since I've been eyeing a new range anyway, but the "luxury kitchen" framing feels a notch above where I shop.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day savings banner** — Dead on. This is literally the sale I wait for to pull the trigger on big appliances. Seeing it the second I land? Good.
- **Sign-in modal with loyalty benefits** — I already have the MyLowe's Rewards card and the Advantage card, but the modal lays out Silver Key free shipping and points earning in plain language. Useful reminder even for existing members.
- **Category recommendation chips** — "Electric Range" and "Washing Machine" sitting right there below the modal. That's exactly the shortlist I'd have in my head walking into the store.
- **Hero subtext: "premium installation available"** — I'd consider paying for installation on a range, so surfacing this early is a plus.

## What's weak

- **Sign-in modal as the first interaction** — I'm a returning customer with a card. Blocking the homepage with a modal to tell me about a $5 new-member offer is friction before I've seen a single product. This is annoying.
- **"Luxury kitchen" hero positioning** — Feels like it's talking to someone building a Houzz kitchen, not a guy finishing a basement and replacing a 15-year-old Whirlpool. The tone is off.
- **No Kobalt or tool section visible** — Tools are half my Lowe's spend. Nothing above the fold speaks to that.
- **Fine print stacking** — Every offer in both the modal and the hero has an asterisk. "Valid on select models only. Terms and conditions apply. *Available in select markets only." That's a lot of hedging before I've even scrolled.
- **No visible price anchor** — "Memorial Day savings" is a promise, but I can't see a single dollar amount or percentage off before the fold. What are we actually talking here — 10%? 40%?

## Recommendations

- **Swap the modal trigger for a persistent loyalty badge** — Instead of blocking the homepage for new-member $5 off, surface a "You have [X] points — see your Memorial Day member deals" banner for signed-in users, and an inline "Join & save" chip for new visitors. Don't wall off the page.
- **Add a specific savings callout to the hero** — Replace "for less" with "Up to 40% off during Memorial Day" (or whatever the actual number is). I'm comparison shopping HD right now; a concrete number makes me stop scrolling.
- **Surface a tool / Kobalt module above the fold or in the recommendation chips** — Swap "Bathroom Vanity" for "Power Tools" or "Kobalt" in those chips. A basement finisher has that tab open before appliances.
- **Tone down the "luxury" language in the hero** — "Get the kitchen upgrade you've been putting off — for less" would hit me a lot harder than "luxury kitchen you want." I'm practical, not aspirational.
