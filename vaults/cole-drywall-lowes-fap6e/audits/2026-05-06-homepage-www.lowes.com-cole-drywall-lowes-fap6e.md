# Homepage snapshot · www.lowes.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- First thing I see when I open this on my phone is a sign-in pop-up blocking my whole screen — that's already annoying. When I look past it, the hero is pushing Halloween decorations. It's May 6th. I'm in the middle of drywalling my basement and I came here to look at joint compound and maybe check out a new Kobalt drill, not spiders and haunted stuff. The top banner says "fresh spring savings" but the hero is screaming October — somebody forgot to swap the campaign.

## What's working

- **Sign-in modal perks list** — showing "Earn points on eligible purchases" and "FREE shipping with Silver Key status" right at the top is actually smart. I'm a MyLowe's cardholder so this is talking to me, even if the timing is intrusive.
- **Top promo banner** — "Take a look at these fresh spring savings. Shop Now >" is at least seasonally on-point for a May project run.
- **"Tool Box" recommendation chip** — barely visible below the modal, but it's the one thing that says "hey, we know you're not just here for throw pillows."
- **Sign In button** — clear, high-contrast blue, no ambiguity about what it does.

## What's weak

- **Halloween hero in May** — "It's halfway to Halloween. Hurry — these haunted favorites won't last long." This is either a stale campaign or a very aggressive advance push. Either way it's a miss when I'm in project mode in spring.
- **Modal blocking the homepage** — I opened the app to browse, not to be immediately walled off by an account prompt. If I'm already a MyLowe's cardholder, this feels like I'm being sold something I already bought.
- **Spring savings vs. Halloween disconnect** — two simultaneous messages pulling in opposite directions kills confidence in the brand's editorial control.
- **No project/DIY imagery above fold** — nothing says "basement, drywall, tools, weekend warrior" anywhere I can see.
- **"Shop All" CTA is weak** — tied to the Halloween hero, which I don't care about, so even the button is misdirected for me.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the stale Halloween hero** — swap it for a "Spring Project Kickoff" lifestyle shot: a guy in his 40s in a half-finished basement holding a Kobalt drill. That's me. That's the sell.
- **Push the MyLowe's Advantage card offer into the banner**, not as a modal interrupt — a sticky top bar with "Cardholder? Get 5% off today" keeps the loyalty hook without blocking navigation.
- **Surface a "Projects" or "What are you building?" category shortcut** — a chip row for Drywall, Flooring, Electrical, Plumbing would let me skip the spray-painted spiders and get to the actual aisle.
- **Sync the campaign** — one season, one message. Right now spring savings and Halloween are fighting each other on the same screen.
