# It’s Labor Day, get savings up to $800 😎
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-07**
## Executive summary

- This is the same "Save up to $800" leaf-scattered blue hero Lowe's has now run three times this campaign week — 8/31 ("strongest hero"), 9/2 ("nearly the same hero"), and now 9/7. The creative fatigue flagged on 9/2 and 9/3 hasn't been addressed; it's the identical asset again, just with "HUGE savings end today" urgency copy bolted onto the header this time. At least the urgency framing is new and the subject-to-hero match is honest — "up to $800" appears in both subject and hero with no bait-and-switch gap.
- Below the fold this send earns its keep more than most: MyLowe's Rewards members get a real tailgating points multiplier, free same-day delivery is a concrete and seasonally relevant perk, and the financing offer has actual dated terms (8/27–9/9). But the "$800" headline is still an umbrella claim with no visible product tied to it, and this is the third near-identical hero this week — a subscriber who saw 8/31 and 9/2 has now seen this exact banner three times in eight days.
- Verdict: solid mid-tier send, dragged down by asset reuse rather than execution. The member/loyalty layer is genuinely good; the hero needs to stop being recycled.

## What's working

- Subject's "$800" figure is immediately backed up by the hero banner — no gap between promise and payoff.
- "Members are in for MORE" section delivers three concrete, differentiated perks: 3x tailgating points, free same-day delivery, and 12/18-month financing with explicit dates.
- "Curated with you in mind" shows real, specific products (storage tote, cleaner, garden soil) with individual Buy now buttons rather than a generic category link.
- Clean render — no overlapping text, broken images, or layout breaks anywhere in the email.
- "Deals of the Day" signup CTA is a low-friction secondary conversion path.

## What's weak

- The hero banner is the same blue/leaf "$800" asset used on 8/31 and 9/2 — third appearance in a week, and it was already called out as stale.
- "Save up to $800" is never tied to an actual product or category in the hero — you have to scroll well past it to find anything concrete to buy.
- "HUGE savings end today" creates same-day urgency, but nothing in the hero states what specifically ends today or gives a time.
- Visual hierarchy is crowded up top — three navigation bars, a headline block, and the hero all stack before any single clear CTA lands.

## Recommendations

- 1. Retire the "$800" leaf hero and replace it with a new seasonal asset — this is its third outing in eight days and it's the single biggest drag on the send.
- 2. Put a real product or category grid directly under the hero so "$800" has something concrete backing it, instead of forcing scroll to the curated section.
- 3. If "ends today" is genuine, add a visible countdown or explicit end time in the hero — right now it's an unsupported urgency claim.
- 4. Promote the MyLowe's tailgating points offer higher — it's the most differentiated, timely piece of content in this email and it's buried below a generic hero.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Members: 3x tailgating points + up to $800 off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus free same-day delivery on tailgate essentials`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `12–18 months special financing ends 9/9`
