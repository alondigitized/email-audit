# Win a home for the holidays? Yes, really. 🏠
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- This is a sweepstakes send with a strong hero. "Dream big. Win big. Like, new-home big." sits over a snowy house that fades into a blueprint, and the "Enter to win" button is right there. The $500,000 prize is the first thing you see. It's the most distinctive Lowe's send in the recent run. Compare the Oct 23 "FREE power tool" email and the Oct 27 "fall to-do list" email, which were both product-and-price promos.
- It does nothing for a drywall contractor like this persona. There's no tool, material or job-site angle, and no price break. A $500K new-build credit is aspirational, not something this persona acts on this week. The key facts (ends 12/15, app exclusive) are shrunk into a phone mockup, and the eligibility terms are tiny white type on blue.
- Verdict: a well-built brand and loyalty play that will probably get opens on novelty. It's low on relevance and urgency for a trade-focused subscriber.

## What's working

- The hero headline is huge and clear, with a single visible "Enter to win" button.
- The photo-to-blueprint house is on theme for a build-your-dream-home prize and stands out from the usual product grids.
- The "Grand Prize" block has three short bullets: the $500,000 credit, a matched preferred builder, and a location of your choice.
- The MyLowe's Rewards header makes the loyalty tie-in obvious.
- The holiday snow scene fits the season.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- Nothing here speaks to a trade or DIY buyer. There's no product, no savings, and no reason a contractor would care.
- The deadline (ends 12/15) and "app exclusive" appear only in tiny text inside the phone mockup. The email has no visible urgency.
- The legal copy at the bottom of the blue panel is very small and low-contrast. Eligibility and "no purchase necessary" are hard to read.
- The preheader says "Enter in the Lowe's app," but the button's destination is unclear. Is it the app or the web?
- The email has a single CTA and nothing else to click, such as a secondary offer or a category link.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero, for example "Enter by Dec 15", and make it a real line of body copy.
- 2. Add a second row of everyday offers below the Grand Prize block, such as a tool or building-materials deal. Sweepstakes alone give a trade buyer nothing to click.
- 3. Say "Enter in the app" on the button itself, or add an app-store badge, so the preheader and CTA agree.
- 4. Make the legal text larger and higher-contrast, or link clearly to the Official Rules.
- 5. Segment this send. Send trade and pro personas a variant with a "Pro rewards" angle or pro-priced items.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Win a $500K credit to build your dream home 🏠`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Enter by 12/15: $500K toward a new home`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Rewards members only. Enter in the Lowe's app before Dec 15 and earn bonus entries.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `One winner, one builder, one dream home. Tap to enter.`
