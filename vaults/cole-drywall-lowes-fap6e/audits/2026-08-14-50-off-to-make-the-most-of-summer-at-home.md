# 50% OFF to make the most of summer at home 😍
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-14**
## Executive summary

- This is Lowe's fourth "outdoor/patio" beat in ten days, and the subject line's "50% OFF" doesn't even map to the hero — the pizza-oven/movie-night hero is uncoupled from the actual 50% off patio furniture deal buried in the second module. That's a real disconnect: the thing that got you to open isn't the thing you see first. Once you scroll past the hero, the email settles into familiar, competent Lowe's territory — a clean two-tile clearance grid, then a sweepstakes module, then an inspiration banner, then a Deals of the Day cascade.
- Compared to 8/13's tote-focused single-product send (6.00/10) and 8/10's backyard-reset email (7.00/10), this is closer to the latter in structure but muddier in message — it's stacking three campaigns (movie night, patio clearance, concert sweepstakes) under one subject that only accurately describes the middle one. The "Bring the big screen home" hero is genuinely well-composed and seasonally on-point (pizza oven + movie night = good creative), but it's the wrong lead for this subject.

## What's working

- Hero image is well-composed and on-theme for August — pizza oven, backyard movie night, warm lifestyle shot.
- The 50%/45% off patio furniture and lighting tiles are clean, with a legible offer, clear "Clearance" flag, and unambiguous "Shop now" CTAs.
- Nav bar (Appliances, Daily Deals, Patio, Outdoor Tools) matches the email's outdoor/summer theme.
- No render bugs — images load, text is legible, buttons are intact.

## What's weak

- Subject promises "50% OFF" but the hero — the first thing you see — doesn't show any offer or percentage at all, just "Shop now."
- Three unrelated campaigns crammed into one send: movie-night hero, patio/lighting clearance, then a $3,000 concert-gift-card sweepstakes that has nothing to do with summer-at-home savings.
- The sweepstakes module (blue, dense with fine print) is a jarring tonal break after two clean product modules — it reads like a bolted-on insert.
- "Your next summer idea starts here" banner is a third distinct CTA ("Get inspired") with no offer, adding scroll fatigue after two module cascades already asked for attention.
- Preview text ("Save on patio, lighting and more for outdoor movie nights...") mentions patio/lighting but never mentions 50% off, so the numeric hook in the subject is unsupported anywhere above the fold.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rebuild the hero to lead with the 50% off patio furniture offer directly — move the clearance tiles' content up, or overlay "Up to 50% off" on the hero image itself, so subject and first-glance match.
- 2. Cut the sweepstakes module or move it to its own send — it doesn't share a message with "summer at home" savings and dilutes the CTA hierarchy.
- 3. Drop the "Get inspired" banner — a third CTA type in one email flattens the visual hierarchy the reader already had to parse twice.
- 4. Tighten the preview text to reference the actual number in the subject.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 50% off patio furniture — outdoor season starts now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `50% off patio + backyard movie night essentials`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Up to 50% off select patio furniture, plus 45% off outdoor lighting.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your patio clearance event is live — save up to 50% before it's gone.`
