# Set up your space, your way 👉
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-17**
## Executive summary

- This is Lowe's dorm/apartment "set up your space" send — a sprawling nine-module cascade covering campus living, tailgate/grilling, Kobalt toolboxes, moving boxes, wall decor, a free member-gift cooler, and a closing "Curated with you in mind" product triad. It's the same kitchen-sink assembly Lowe's ran on 2026-08-13 with the back-to-campus module stacked onto the appliance hero — except here campus living IS the hero, and it's diluted further by six unrelated sub-campaigns crammed into one scroll.
- The individual pieces are fine — clean product photography, a genuinely nice "FREE Member gift alert" cooler offer with a calendar CTA, and the Kobalt NFL toolbox is a fun college dorm/tailgate tie-in. But there's no single offer a subscriber walks away remembering. Grills, toolboxes, moving boxes, wall decor, and a free cooler all compete for the same eyeball real estate with zero prioritization, and the top hero ("These items are a total game changer") doesn't name a single product or price.
- Judgement: a well-produced but unfocused send — more catalog than campaign. The free cooler gift and 10% off moving boxes are the only two offers with real teeth, and both are buried past the fold.

## What's working

- The "FREE Member gift alert" cooler module has a specific, dated mechanic (Saturday, August 22, 10 a.m.) with an "Add to calendar" CTA — a genuinely differentiated beat versus the discount-grid emails this brand usually sends.
- "10% off moving boxes when you spend $30+" is a concrete, well-scoped offer tied to a clear campus-move-in visual.
- Product photography throughout (grill/tailgate scene, Kobalt toolboxes, plant/decor shots) is crisp with no rendering bugs — no broken images, no overlapping text.
- Section dividers ("Fan-approved and in demand," "Curated with you in mind") give the long scroll some visual breathing room and signal a new theme is starting.

## What's weak

- The hero has no offer, no price, no product name — just "Make the most of your space. These items are a total game changer," which tells the reader nothing about what's actually for sale.
- Nine distinct sub-campaigns (campus decor, tailgate, Kobalt toolboxes ×2, moving boxes, wall decor, rental upgrades, free cooler, deals-of-the-day products) in one email — there's no dominant CTA, so the eye has nowhere obvious to land.
- Subject line "Set up your space, your way 👉" promises personalization/curation but the body is a generic grid — no segmentation signal anywhere in the render.
- The closing product triad (storage bin, cardboard box, microwave) feels like a random leftover-inventory dump with no unifying theme after the free-gift module already closed the email's emotional arc.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to one hero offer and move everything else to a "shop more" strip. The free cooler gift-with-purchase is the most novel, dated, and clickable thing in the email — lead with it, not bury it two-thirds down.
- 2. Give the top hero a real product and price instead of vague "game changer" copy — pick the single best campus item (e.g., a specific storage/decor bundle) and put a dollar figure on it.
- 3. Consolidate the two Kobalt modules (NFL toolboxes + tool cabinets) into one "Kobalt" section instead of two side-by-side near-duplicate CTAs.
- 4. Drop or relocate the closing three-product grid — it reads as filler after the free-gift high point and dilutes the send's close.

- **Subject Alt B:** `10% off moving boxes + a free gift this week`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Grab a free mini cooler with My Lowe's Rewards — plus dorm and campus deals`
