# Your kitchen's craving a refresh 👀
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-07**
## Executive summary

- Lowe's pivots cleanly off four days of Father's Day content into a kitchen/appliance category reset. The subject ("Your kitchen's craving a refresh") is a smart gear-shift and the hero delivers immediately on that promise — "Appliance deals for the heart of your home" over a full kitchen image is probably the most focused Lowe's hero in this recent run. The email earns its open.
- The problem is what happens after the hero. This is a six-section marathon — appliances, laundry, kitchen design consultation, 0% financing, GOAT clearance, new-homeowner pitch — and none of those sections are bad individually, but stacked together they dilute each other. By the time you hit the "Limited Time Credit Deal" module the reader has already been asked to shop four different categories. The subject sells kitchen; the body sells everything.
- The 0% APR / 36-month financing module is the single most powerful offer in this email and it's buried in the middle. That should be section two, not section four.

## What's working

- Hero headline "Appliance deals for the heart of your home" lands hard — concrete category, emotional hook, clean visual.
- Yellow SAVE badges on the product cards create fast price-reduction proof without requiring the reader to do math.
- "Kitchen design? We got you." consultation module is a differentiated offer (service, not just product) that competitors can't easily replicate.
- "The G.O.A.T. is almost g.o.a.t." is genuinely clever wordplay — the only module in this email that earns a second look.
- Subject line at ~36 characters is well inside mobile-safe range with only one emoji, no spam signals.

## What's weak

- Six distinct modules with six "Shop now" CTAs creates decision paralysis — no single conversion priority is established.
- 0% / 36-month financing is buried in position four; that offer could move a refrigerator purchase and it's getting skipped.
- "Shop now" appears on nearly every CTA button — not one button tells you what you're shopping for.
- No member pricing or MyLowe's loyalty signal anywhere visible — a miss given that the June 1 member-only send was one of their strongest recent emails.
- The new-homeowner "Now home? Make it yours." module feels out of place in a kitchen refresh email; it's a separate audience targeting a separate intent.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Move the 0% APR / 36-month financing block to position two**, directly under the hero. A $1,200 refrigerator at 0% for three years is the reason to buy today, not "someday."
- 2. **Replace every "Shop now" CTA with category-specific copy**: "Shop Ranges," "Shop Dishwashers," "Explore Kitchen Design." One verb + one noun, every button.
- 3. **Cut the new-homeowner module entirely** — it serves a different audience intent and pads the scroll length past where most readers disengage.
- 4. **Surface a MyLowe's exclusive angle** in the hero or financing block; the June 1 member email proved that positioning moves the needle.
- 5. **Tighten the subject to name an anchor offer**, not just a mood.
- **Subject Alt A:** `0% for 36 months on kitchen appliances`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Appliance deals for the heart of your home`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ranges, fridges, dishwashers — major savings, no interest for 3 years.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free kitchen design consult + deals on top appliance brands.`

## 1. Overview

Lowe's pivots cleanly off four days of Father's Day content into a kitchen/appliance category reset.
