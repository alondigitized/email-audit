# MyLowe’s Money Days are so OOONNN 🤩
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-11**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Money Days send in six days, after 10/6 ("Money Days are HEEEREEEE 🎉") and 10/7, and it carries the same $10/$20 offer. The new angle is an in-store giveaway for the first 150 members, 10 a.m. to 1 p.m. today. That is a decent hook, but the render buries it in a small module above the real hero. Below that sits a long run of unrelated modules: 3x points, MrBeast Day, MrBeast builder kits and Dan + Shay concert tickets.
- The Money Days module itself is clear. It shows $10 on $200, $20 with the credit card and an Oct 6–14 window, over a blue field with confetti. The rest of the email is cluttered. There are five or six competing CTAs and four distinct offers, and lawn concert tickets and kids' builder kits sit alongside a home-improvement loyalty offer. There are no visible render bugs, but nothing leads. The "Your Sunday just got more rewarding" banner opens with a vague headline, and the number only appears one block later.
- Verdict: a competent loyalty send that tries to do too much. It scores about the same as the 10/4 Sunday roundup, which also offered a lot but led with nothing.

## What's working

- The Money Days block states the offer plainly: $10 on $200+, $20 for credit card holders, and a visible Oct 6–14 window.
- The "FREE MyLowe's Money" giveaway badge with its 10 a.m.–1 p.m. window adds a real time-bounded reason to act today.
- The blue-and-white brand look and the MyLowe's Rewards header are consistent and recognizable.
- The MrBeast kit module shows a concrete price, $9.98 with "Save $5" and the "Was" price, which makes it the one clearly priced product in the email.
- The footer fine print is present and legible, and the offer terms are clearly marked.

## What's weak

- The top banner's headline, "Your Sunday just got more rewarding," has no number in it, and the giveaway is limited to in-store members during a short window.
- It repeats the $10/$20 Money Days offer already sent on 10/6 and 10/7, so the cadence feels stale.
- There are too many unrelated modules: 3x points, MrBeast Day, builder kits and concert tickets. The eye has no single place to land.
- The Dan + Shay concert section takes up a large amount of space, and its offer applies to only a few cities and venues.
- The giveaway says "first 150 members," which is exclusionary. Most readers cannot get to a store in time, and the "Learn more" button gives no clear next step.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one hero. Either put the $10/$20 Money Days offer first, or make the giveaway the hero with a clear store-locator CTA. Do not stack both at the top.
- 2. Cut the module count to three or four. Drop the concert block, or send it as its own email to a segment.
- 3. Change the CTA copy. "Learn more" appears four times. Use specific verbs, such as "Find my store" or "Shop Money Days."
- 4. Make the headline carry the offer, such as "$10–$20 MyLowe's Money back, ends 10/14."
- 5. Vary the creative from the 10/6 and 10/7 sends, or add a countdown to show the deadline is closing.
- **Subject Alt A:** `$20 MyLowe's Money back. Ends 10/14`
- **Subject Alt B:** `First 150 members get FREE MyLowe's Money today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stop by a store 10–1 today, then earn $10 on $200+.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Card holders get $20 back. 3x points on fall favorites, too.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a table-based XHTML email with mobile media queries and MSO-guarded web fonts. Confirmed defects are a malformed DOCTYPE, a missing `lang` attribute, and one image without alt text. The header warnings are probably relay artifacts and aren't confirmed defects. The source is truncated, so the body and footer were not auditable.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No confirmed issues.
- 16 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe because they are click-redirect domains. Their destinations and redirect health are **unverified**, not failing.
- The truncated source doesn't show the individual `href` values, so I can't comment on link structure.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed DOCTYPE:** `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" ...>` has no space between `PUBLIC` and the quoted public identifier. Some parsers may drop out of the intended doctype mode. The DTD URL and `xmlns` also use `https://www.w3.org/...`, but the XHTML namespace is defined as `http://`. This is low risk in email clients.
- **Missing `lang`:** `<html xmlns="https://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` attribute, so screen readers can't pick the correct language profile.
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):** `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/1/a22207c1-63c7-47c0-9e00-61227828e1fe.jpg`. If this image carries content or is linked, it is announced as a filename or nothing at all. If it is decorative, it needs `alt=""`.
- **Good practice present:** `viewport` meta, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` reset, and `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` guards around `@font-face`.
- **Font fallbacks:** the font stacks used in the body couldn't be seen. Fellix and DIN Next load from `lowescdn.com` and fall back to system fonts in clients that strip `@font-face`. Confirm each inline `font-family` has a web-safe fallback.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens such as `%%...%%`, `{{...}}` or `[FirstName]` appear in the truncated portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found [WARN]:** the QA note says the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. This is inconclusive, not a confirmed violation.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found [WARN]:** same caveat. One-click support can't be confirmed from this capture.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found [WARN]:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown here. The sender is `lowes@e.lowes.com`, a subdomain, so alignment can't be assessed without the raw headers.
- **Not auditable:** the footer unsubscribe link and physical address fall after the truncation point.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The 16 tracking links were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing pages can't be checked. No issues are asserted.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` text to `a22207c1-63c7-47c0-9e00-61227828e1fe.jpg`, or `alt=""` if it is decorative.
2. Fix the DOCTYPE to `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" "http://www.w3.org/TR/xhtml1/DTD/xhtml1-transitional.dtd">`, and use `http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml` for `xmlns`.
3. Add `lang="en"` to the `<html>` element.
4. To close the compliance gaps, re-run the check against the raw message with full headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`, `DKIM-Signature`) instead of the relay copy.
5. Resolve the 16 redirect links with a follow-redirect probe (HEAD or GET) so that final URLs, status codes and UTM parameters can be checked.
6. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to cover the footer unsubscribe link, the physical address, and any merge tokens in the body.
