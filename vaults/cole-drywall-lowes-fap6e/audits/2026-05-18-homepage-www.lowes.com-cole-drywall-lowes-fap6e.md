# Homepage snapshot · www.lowes.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- First thing I see is a sign-in popup blocking the whole screen — I've been shopping Lowe's for years and they're making me log in before I can even browse. The Memorial Day savings banner up top is exactly what I came for, and the kitchen appliance hero underneath actually makes sense for me since I default to Lowe's on the big appliance buys. But that modal is straight-up in the way.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day savings banner** — I came to the site today specifically because I know Memorial Day is appliance-buying season at Lowe's. Seeing that banner immediately confirms I'm in the right place.
- **Silver Key free shipping call-out** — that reward tier mention in the modal reminds me why I have the Lowe's Advantage card; it's a real benefit I actually use.
- **"Tool Box" category chip** — partially visible in the recommended section below the modal, that's exactly the kind of shortcut I'd use mid-project to jump straight to tools.
- **Luxury kitchen hero imagery** — dark appliances, stone counters; Lowe's is telling me they've got the goods to compete with the kitchen showrooms, and that message works on me.

## What's weak

- **Forced sign-in modal** — I'm Cole Drywall, I've got a Lowe's Advantage card with a balance on it, and before I can see a single product you're asking me to log in. That's friction I don't need on my lunch break.
- **"Memorial Day savings" with no actual number** — the banner draws me in and then delivers nothing. No "up to 40% off," no dollar threshold. I can't act on that.
- **Hero is luxury kitchen, I'm finishing a basement** — appliances are in my wheelhouse, but the Subzero-adjacent vibe of this kitchen isn't exactly the Kobalt-and-Allen-Roth world I live in. It's slightly high-cotton for my project.
- **Zero trust signals in view** — no review counts on featured items, no "free returns" badge, no mention of the Lowe's Advantage card benefits I'd actually care about. All of that is buried under the modal or below the fold.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the mandatory sign-in modal** — or at least make it dismissible with one tap and persistent-cookie it so I don't see it every visit. I'm an existing rewards member; treat me like one.
- **Put a specific number in the Memorial Day banner** — "Up to 40% off appliances — Memorial Day savings" would stop my thumb immediately versus the current vague "Shop Now."
- **Swap or supplement the luxury kitchen hero with a project-based image** — a basement laundry room setup or a garage workshop with Kobalt storage would speak directly to where I am in my project right now.
- **Surface my MyLowe's Rewards balance in the header** — even a "Hi, Cole — you have 420 points" chip next to the account icon would make this feel like a personalized experience instead of a cold storefront.
