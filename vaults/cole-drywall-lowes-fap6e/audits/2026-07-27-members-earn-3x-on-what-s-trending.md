# ⭐ Members earn 3x on what’s trending
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuine departure from the Money Days drumbeat that's dominated Lowe's recent sends (per brand history: five-plus MyLowe's Money Days emails between 7/23–7/26) — instead of another loyalty-event push, it's a "trending/viral products" 3x-points campaign. The hero product mix is the strange part: a MrBeast Lab kids' building kit and a football. For a home-improvement retailer's loyalty program, that's a hard swerve away from category, and it reads more like a novelty cross-promo than something core to why a Lowe's member opens this brand's email.
- The mechanics are clean — bold "Trending now" hero, high-contrast 3x Points badge, a clear "Activate now" CTA, and a hard deadline (8/5). The "Next drop loading..." teaser for a mystery mini bucket collectible (July 31, "Members shop first") is a nice secondary hook that plays into the bucket-collectible program without crowding the primary offer.
- Net: well-executed creative, poorly-targeted category. If this persona is a homeowner shopping Lowe's for tools/appliances/home projects, a toy kit and a football won't pull a click — this feels built for a different (younger/gift-buying) segment than Lowe's typical email base.

## What's working

- Bold, high-contrast "Trending now" hero with a clearly readable 3x Points badge — impossible to miss at a glance.
- Single, unambiguous CTA ("Activate now") with strong button contrast against the blue background.
- Concrete offer deadline ("Valid through 8/5") removes guesswork.
- Secondary teaser block (mystery bucket drop, July 31, "Members shop first") adds an engagement hook without competing with the primary offer.
- Clean render throughout — no overlapping text, broken images, or layout breaks.

## What's weak

- The featured products (MrBeast Lab toy kit, football) are off-category for a home-improvement retailer and don't match a typical Lowe's shopper's reason for opening this email.
- Neither the subject nor the hero specifies which "trending" products are included — a reader has to open and scroll to learn it's toys/sporting goods, which risks a bounce for anyone expecting tools or seasonal home items.
- Terms/exclusions are pushed to dense small print at the very bottom, requiring a trip to Lowes.com/Terms to understand what's actually excluded.
- Arrives in the middle of an already heavy July send cadence (per brand history), competing for attention with the Money Days and Closeout Event threads.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap the hero products for something in Lowe's actual wheelhouse (grills, patio, tools) if this program is meant to serve the whole loyalty base, or explicitly re-segment this send to members who've shown interest in kids/outdoor gear — right now it reads as sent to everyone.
- 2. Front-load the category in the subject/hero so people self-select before opening — "trending" alone is too vague to signal relevance.
- 3. Surface the key exclusion terms (e.g., "excludes X") in slightly larger type near the CTA instead of only in the bottom legal block.
- 4. Give the CTA button product-specific copy instead of a generic "Activate now" — e.g., "Activate 3x Points."

- **Preheader Alt A:** `New trending picks just added — activate 3x points now.`
