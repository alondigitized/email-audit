# Dad's Day is just two days away 😳
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-19**
## Executive summary

- This is Lowe's final Father's Day push, landing two days before the holiday — and the concept is right. The email splits its job cleanly: a hero that sells urgency ("It's Dad's weekend!"), a mid-section product anchor at $649, and a smart gift-card bailout lane for late deciders. That structure is better than the June 16 "What do you get the dad who has everything" send, which had no clear throughline.
- The big visible problem is the countdown timer: it's rendering at 00:00:00 across all three digits. Whether that's a post-expiry capture artifact or a render failure at open time, what subscribers see is a clock that says "too late" right below a subject line that says "two days away." That contradiction bleeds urgency from the one moment in this campaign where urgency is genuinely earned.
- The gift card section is the smartest idea in the email. After a 9-day campaign of product pushes, the "Still deciding? Start here" lane correctly acknowledges that last-minute shoppers exist and Lowe's has a frictionless answer for them. Overall: a solid-but-not-great finale that needed its countdown to actually work.

## What's working

- **"It's Dad's weekend!" framing** lands better than the question-mark hedging of the June 16 send — it's a statement that creates FOMO, not a shrug.
- **Gift card middle section** is smart last-minute positioning: "Still deciding? Start here" is the only Lowe's Father's Day email this run that directly addresses the procrastinator.
- **"The G.O.A.T. is almost g.o.n.e." wordplay** is a light, fun closer that earns a second look — the best piece of copy in the email.
- **Visual hierarchy** is clean: hero → featured product → gift card fallback → GOAT closer flows logically without visual clutter.

## What's weak

- **Countdown timer at 00:00:00**: the single biggest visible bug — it directly undercuts the "two days away" urgency in the subject and the banner copy above it.
- **No concrete discount**: after nine days of specific percentage-off offers, this finale shows only a $649 price tag and gift card denominations — there's no headline deal to anchor the send.
- **"Shop now" / "Buy now" CTAs are generic**: every button says the same thing and none names what you're buying; the GOAT closer especially deserves a "Shop the G.O.A.T." button.
- **No loyalty or Pro rewards callout**: the campaign's Day 6 Brunson send hit 11x points to great effect; the finale doesn't remind members what they're earning on a Father's Day purchase.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the countdown timer** — even a static "Order by Saturday" deadline text would be preferable to three visible zeros. A broken urgency mechanism is worse than no urgency mechanism.
- 2. **Add a headline offer** — even "Free shipping on orders $45+" or "Up to 30% off tools" in the hero would anchor the send. The previous nine days conditioned the subscriber to expect a number; there isn't one here.
- 3. **Rename the CTAs** — swap "Shop now" for "Find Dad's gift" on the hero and "Shop gift cards" on the gift card section to reduce generic blur.
- 4. **Surface loyalty benefits** — one sentence: "Members earn points on every gift" under the hero CTA would recapture the engagement momentum from the Brunson 11x points email.
- **Subject Alt A:** `2 days left to find Dad's gift — shop now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Father's Day in 48 hours — free shipping if you hurry`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Gift cards, grills, and the G.O.A.T. — something for every dad.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Order by Saturday. Free shipping on $45+.`

## Technical Audit — Lowe's "Dad's Day is just two days away" (cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Standard retail ESP send via `e.lowes.com` (Salesforce Marketing Cloud). The email is structurally sound but has accessibility gaps in tracking pixel alt attributes and authentication header visibility is blocked by the AgentMail relay layer.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**20 tracking links skipped** — all click-through URLs route through a redirect domain (likely `click.e.lowes.com` or similar). HTTP probing was skipped, so broken destination URLs cannot be confirmed or ruled out. This is expected for ESP-wrapped links but means dead-link detection requires manual spot-check or ESP reporting.

No issues found with link structure in the HTML itself.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**9 images missing `alt` text** (confirmed by QA scan):

| Image | URL |
|---|---|
| Hero image | `https://image.e.lowes.com/…/c301e24e-….jpg` |
| Open-pixel (×2) | `https://mi.lowes.com/p/cp/…/o.gif` |
| Impression pixel (×5) | `https://sli.lowes.com/imp?s=109083{4,5,7,8}…` |
| Content image | `https://image.e.lowes.com/…/ad569dc5-….png` |

The `o.gif` and `imp` pixels are tracking beacons and conventionally carry `alt=""` (empty string), which is technically correct for decorative/invisible elements — but they are currently missing the attribute entirely, which causes screen readers to announce the filename or URL. The hero and content images are a harder accessibility failure.

**Font loading:** `@font-face` blocks load `.woff` only — no `.woff2` fallback. Not a breakage, but `.woff2` is universally supported in modern email clients that honor web fonts (Apple Mail, iOS Mail) and would reduce payload by ~30%.

**Duplicate `@font-face` blocks:** Two separate `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` blocks each declare the full Fellix family. Redundant declarations; no functional impact but adds unnecessary parse weight.

**Legacy Hotmail hacks present** (`.ReadMsgBody`, `.ExternalClass`): These target Hotmail/Windows Live Mail, which is EOL. Dead code, but harmless.

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header not detected** — QA warns this may be a relay-layer visibility issue (AgentMail not forwarding the header), not necessarily an absence in the original send. Lowe's is a large sender and almost certainly includes this header; the gap is in the audit pipeline's header capture, not the email itself. Verify by checking raw headers in the AgentMail message store directly.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not detected** — Same caveat as above. Gmail and Apple Mail use this for one-click unsubscribe. If genuinely absent in the original send, Lowe's would be out of compliance with Google/Yahoo bulk sender requirements (effective Feb 2024). Low probability for a sender of this size; more likely a relay capture issue.

**Action:** Fix the AgentMail relay to forward all received headers before treating these as sender-side compliance failures.
