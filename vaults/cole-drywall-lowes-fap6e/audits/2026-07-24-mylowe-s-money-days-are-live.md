# MyLowe's Money Days are LIVE🔥
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-24**
## Executive summary

- This is Lowe's formal "Money Days is LIVE" launch email — but the event was already name-dropped in at least two July 23 sends (the sweeping summer deals umbrella and the credit card pitch). Subscribers who opened those already heard the name. "LIVE🔥" in the subject line feels like catching up to your own hype rather than starting a moment.
- The strongest single block in the email is the All-In Kitchen Appliance 35%-off section with a Members-get-FREE badge — that is a specific, differentiated, loyalty-forward offer and it lands clearly. Everything after it loses discipline. The email staggers through a 50%-off pod, a 40%-off pod, a "we'll send deals to your space" interlude, a car-key duplication service pitch, and a Deals of the Day section. That's five distinct messages in one scroll, which is structurally the same mistake as the July 23 "Get summer deals up to 50% OFF" send.
- Judgment: a competent 7. The appliance offer alone would be a focused 8. The kitchen-sink execution drags it back down.

## What's working

- All-In Kitchen Appliance 35%-off block is specific, clearly framed, and well-placed above the fold
- "Members get FREE [item]" loyalty callout is visually prominent and genuinely differentiating
- Money Days branding coheres with the July 23 build-up sends — the event reads like a real campaign moment, not a random promo
- Blue/teal event palette signals "loyalty/event email" in the inbox — brand recall is strong
- No render bugs — clean layout, no broken images, no overlapping elements

## What's weak

- "LIVE" urgency is spent — the event launched in yesterday's sends; "LIVE" today is just confirmation, not an opening beat
- Car-key duplication block is a jarring service pitch mid-promotional-email — it signals list-padding and breaks offer momentum dead
- 50% and 40% pods are vague — no visible category label or product shot that tells you what the deal is at a glance
- No expiration date anywhere in the email — "LIVE" with no end date creates zero clock pressure
- The "we'll send deals to your space" interlude adds another CTA without adding an offer, further fragmenting attention

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a hard end date to the hero** — "Money Days: July 24–27 only" printed in the hero section converts vague LIVE urgency into a real countdown.
- 2. **Cut the car-key block entirely** — it belongs in a targeted service email, not sandwiched between appliance and patio deals.
- 3. **Make the free member gift explicit in text** — show what the item is in a headline ("Members get a free [item name] with qualifying purchase") so it reads in screen readers and carries into the preview.
- 4. **Collapse or rename the 50%/40% pods** — label the category clearly ("50% off patio seating") or cut to one that supports the appliance story.
- 5. **Rewrite the preview text** — if it's leaking "view in browser" junk, replace it with the free-gift callout.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Money Days: 35% off appliances + a free member gift`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Money Days is on — 35% off kitchens, 50% off patio`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Members get a free gift with qualifying appliance purchase. Ends July 27.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `35% off All-In Kitchen bundles, 50% off patio, 40% off [category] — through Sunday.`

## Technical Audit — Lowe's "MyLowe's Money Days" Email

**1. Technical Summary**

Email is structurally sound with standard responsive layout patterns, but the AgentMail relay is not capturing compliance headers (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results), and 8 images lack alt text including tracking pixels that should be empty-string attributed.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**

- **20 tracking links skipped** by the HTTP probe — all routed through click-redirect domains (likely `click.e.lowes.com` or similar). Cannot confirm destination URL integrity or UTM parameter presence from QA data alone.
- No broken link errors reported, so redirect infrastructure appears live.

---

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**

- **8 images missing `alt` text**, confirmed by QA:
  - `c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg` — content image, should have descriptive alt
  - `ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png` — content image, should have descriptive alt
  - `o.gif` (`mi.lowes.com`) — tracking pixel; should have `alt=""`
  - Five `imp` pixels (`sli.lowes.com`) — tracking pixels; should have `alt=""`
- Custom web fonts (`Fellix`, `DIN Next`) loaded via `@font-face` from `lowescdn.com` — correct fallback stack not visible in truncated source; unconfirmed but typical risk in Outlook/Gmail where web fonts are stripped.
- Duplicate `<!--[if !mso]><!--> ... <!--<![endif]-->` conditional comment blocks for font loading — second block is redundant but harmless.
- Media queries use both `max-width: 480px` and a range query `(min-width: 375px) and (max-width: 600px)` — overlapping breakpoints could produce conflicting styles on 375–480px devices.
