# These brands are only at Lowe’s🌟
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-14**
## Executive summary

- This is a brand-awareness play — Lowe's leaning on private-label exclusivity (Kobalt, allen+roth) to give subscribers a reason to care beyond the Money Days blitz that just ended July 12. The strategic pivot makes sense on paper: you can't run urgency-close emails every day, and exclusive brand franchises are a real differentiator. But the execution buries the lead under five competing modules (brand picks → top deals → member deals → 12-month financing → July 19 Maxx Event → Deals of the Day signup), none of which get enough room to close. This is a kitchen-sink email masquerading as a brand story.
- The hero is actually decent — clean layout, the Kobalt and allen+roth logos are prominent, and the lifestyle shot communicates "premium outdoor living." The problem is everything below the hero fights for the same attention. By the time you reach the July 19 Maxx Home Event promo, you've already been asked to click "Shop now" twice and "Learn more" once. The signal-to-noise ratio collapses.
- Compared to the Money Days run (July 10–12), which scored 8/10 each because they had one clear offer and one CTA, this send is a regression. It looks like an internal template that got populated with every active campaign rather than an email that was designed around a single subscriber outcome.

## What's working

- **Hero brand placement is clear.** Kobalt and allen+roth logos are visually prominent; the "only at Lowe's" exclusivity hook is stated cleanly above the fold.
- **Member Only Deals module is visible.** The loyalty section is present and will resonate with MyLowe's Rewards members coming off Money Days.
- **July 19 Maxx Home Event gives the email a forward hook.** A near-term, named event with a specific date is a credible urgency signal.
- **No render bugs.** Clean layout throughout — no broken images, overlapping copy, or obvious layout failures.
- **Subject line length is mobile-safe.** At ~32 characters it clears the mobile truncation threshold easily.

## What's weak

- **Five separate CTAs, five separate storylines.** Brand picks, top deals, member deals, credit financing, and an event — nothing is allowed to close. The email reads like a sitemap, not a pitch.
- **No concrete offer in the hero.** "Brands you'll only find at Lowe's" is a brand claim, not an offer. The Money Days emails showed that Lowe's subscribers respond to specific dollar/percent signals — this hero has none.
- **Preview text repeats the subject.** "Get picks from brands you'll only find at Lowe's" is a near-verbatim restatement of "These brands are only at Lowe's." Both subject and preheader slots are spent saying the same thing.
- **$1,798 product shown without a deal.** Showing a price this high without a discount framing (was/now, member price, % off) makes it feel like a catalog listing, not a deal email.
- **Deals of the Day signup module is wrong email.** Asking engaged subscribers to sign up for another email list at the bottom of a promotional send adds friction and splits the conversion goal.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Name the brands in the subject line.** The brands ARE the offer here — putting them in the subject (see alts below) turns a vague "only at Lowe's" into a recognizable product hook for anyone who uses Kobalt tools or allen+roth lighting.
- 2. **Give the hero a member price anchor.** Pick the best-performing allen+roth or Kobalt SKU, show regular price vs. member price side by side ("$179 / Member: $139"), and make that the single CTA. That's an email.
- 3. **Kill three modules.** Drop the Deals of the Day signup and the 12-month financing teaser — those are separate campaigns. Optionally fold the Maxx Home Event into a one-line footer banner: "Tune in July 19 →". Maximum two CTAs for an awareness send.
- 4. **Rewrite the preheader to complement, not echo, the subject.** The subject says "only at Lowe's" — the preheader should name what you get by clicking, not restate the claim.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Kobalt. Allen + roth. Only at Lowe's 🌟`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your exclusive brands are waiting — member prices inside`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Tools, fans, and furniture you won't find at Home Depot. Member prices on the best.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Kobalt power tools. Allen + roth lighting. Shop with your MyLowe's price.`

## Technical Audit — Lowe's "These brands are only at Lowe's" Email

### 1. Technical Summary

XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with standard email-client compatibility shims. Three compliance-level header gaps and ten images missing alt attributes are the primary actionable findings; 21 click-tracking links could not be probed for destination integrity.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**21 click-redirect links skipped** — all routed through Lowe's ESP click-tracker; destination URLs and UTM parameters cannot be verified from the relay capture.

**Persona email exposed in tracking pixel query strings** — five separate impression pixels on `sli.lowes.com` and one on `mi.lowes.com` embed the recipient address as a plain-text URL parameter:
```
https://sli.lowes.com/imp?s=1090838&e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app&p=7142026...
https://mi.lowes.com/p/cp/36db229c2a5e16e1/o.gif?mi_u=410146703
```
The `e=` parameter exposes the etell persona address in server logs, referrer headers, and any proxy that handles the image request. This is expected behavior for ESP impression tracking but confirms the persona address is live and linked to segment ID `lctg=410146703`.

**CDN font version inconsistency** — DIN Next Regular loads from scaffold `1.4.4`; DIN Next Bold and Black load from `1.2.0`. Cross-version mixing is not a link error but creates a latent breakage risk if either version is deprecated.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**10 images missing `alt` attributes** (confirmed by QA):

| Image | Type | Missing alt? |
|---|---|---|
| `c301e24e-...jpg` | Content image | Yes — should describe image |
| `f6065b83-...jpeg` | Content image | Yes — should describe image |
| `ad569dc5-...png` | Content image | Yes — should describe image |
| `o.gif` | Open-pixel (mi.lowes.com) | Yes — should be `alt=""` |
| 6× `imp` pixels (sli.lowes.com) | Impression pixels | Yes — should be `alt=""` |

Content images failing without alt text render as broken-image icons in text-only/screen-reader environments. Tracking pixels should use `alt=""` (empty string) rather than omitting the attribute entirely — omission triggers accessibility checker failures in some clients.

**WOFF-only web fonts** — all six `@font-face` declarations load `.woff` only; no `.woff2` source is provided. WOFF2 delivers ~30% smaller payloads. No fallback stack is defined in the `@font-face` blocks themselves (fallback handling appears to rely on inline `font-family` stacks elsewhere in the template, not verified in the truncated source).

**Duplicate `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` blocks** — Fellix and DIN Next families are each wrapped in separate conditional comment guards. Functionally equivalent, but redundant; consolidating reduces parse surface.

No unrendered merge tokens (`{{`, `%%`, `[%`, etc.) are visible in the truncated HTML — token substitution appears complete. The tracking pixel `lctg=410146703` and `mi_u=410146703` values indicate subscriber-level ID was injected correctly at send time.

### 5. Compliance

| Check | Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `List-Unsubscribe` header | **WARN** — not captured | AgentMail relay may strip; cannot confirm presence |
| `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) | **WARN** — not captured | One-click unsubscribe status unknown |

The missing `List-Unsubscribe-Post` is the most consequential gap: Gmail and Apple Mail suppress the one-click unsubscribe UI when this header is absent, which can elevate spam complaint rates. Whether the header exists on the actual SMTP envelope or is simply not forwarded by the AgentMail relay is not determinable from this capture alone.

Sending domain `e.lowes.com` is a subdomain of `lowes.com` — consistent with standard ESP envelope configuration. No CAN-SPAM physical address block is visible in the truncated source; presence in the full footer is assumed but not confirmed.
