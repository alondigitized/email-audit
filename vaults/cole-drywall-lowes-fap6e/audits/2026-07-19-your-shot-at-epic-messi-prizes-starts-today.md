# Your shot at EPIC Messi prizes starts today
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-19**
## Executive summary

- This is Lowe's activating a Lionel Messi sponsorship around a live TikTok Halftime Event — a sweepstakes for Messi memorabilia, a 10-ft Messi outdoor inflatable product, and a "Today Only" 10x points loyalty offer cleverly pegged to Messi's #10 jersey number. The creative is polished, the hero image is genuinely striking, and the loyalty hook is the smartest thing in the email: #10 jersey → 10x points is elegant. But for the core Lowe's subscriber who was clicking through Closeout Event appliance deals yesterday, this is a hard left turn.
- What saves it from mediocrity is the Today Only loyalty offer — concrete, time-bounded, and anchored to a creative brand moment. The sweepstakes with "no purchase necessary" lowers the barrier. The 10-ft outdoor inflatable also fits Lowe's product catalog (seasonal outdoor décor), so there's a thin home-improvement thread. But three of the four body sections are pure Messi content, and a four-CTA lineup (Enter now / Buy now / Learn more / Activate now) scatters attention.
- This is a competent celebrity partnership activation. It is not a well-targeted email for the home improvement shopper who defines Lowe's subscriber base — and the abrupt category pivot from yesterday's "Ends tomorrow: appliance deals" makes the whiplash worse.

## What's working

- **Messi hero is premium retail creative** — full-width, Lowe's-branded jersey, high production value; immediately communicates something different is happening.
- **#10 jersey → 10x points tie-in** — the strongest creative idea in the email; specific, loyalty-relevant, and earns the "Today Only" urgency badge.
- **"No luck required" contrast copy** — smart pivot line for subscribers who don't want to enter a sweepstakes but do want to buy the inflatable.
- **Clean render throughout** — all four CTAs render fully, no broken images, no layout breakage, no overlapping text.
- **Sweepstakes entry barrier is low** — "no purchase necessary" is visible in the legal block; accessible to any subscriber.

## What's weak

- **Category whiplash from yesterday's email** — July 18 closed a Closeout Event on appliances; July 19 opens a Messi sweepstakes. Subscribers expecting deal follow-up get celebrity soccer content instead.
- **Four competing CTAs** — Enter now / Buy now / Learn more / Activate now is decision paralysis; no single action dominates the scroll.
- **TikTok halftime mechanic is buried** — "Tune in during halftime on Lowe's TikTok" is small copy below the hero. The entry mechanic (you have to watch a livestream) is the key friction point and it's not surfaced.
- **"Learn more" for Messi exclusives** — vague and unearned; a Lowe's subscriber has no prior expectation for "Messi exclusives and content" and no reason to click blind.
- **Home improvement is absent for three of four sections** — a subscriber who doesn't follow soccer has nothing actionable until the 10x points section at the bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Surface the TikTok watch mechanic in the hero** — "Watch halftime on TikTok to enter" must be the hero subheadline, not buried in small type below the CTA. The mechanic is the story.
- 2. **Promote 10x points above the Messi inflatable** — it's universally relevant to any loyalty member; the inflatable is niche. Swap sections 2 and 4.
- 3. **Collapse to two CTAs** — Enter the sweepstakes OR activate 10x points. Drop "Learn more" for Messi content entirely; fold the inflatable into one of the two primary flows.
- 4. **Add one home improvement anchor** — a single deal banner below the fold ("While you're here: this week's outdoor sale") keeps non-Messi-fans in the email.
- 5. **Rewrite subject to surface the mechanic or the loyalty hook:**
- **Subject Alt A:** `Tune in at halftime: win Messi prizes today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `10x points today + your shot at Messi memorabilia`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Watch Lowe's TikTok halftime — prizes worn, touched, or signed by Messi. No purchase needed.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Activate 10x points today, plus enter to win Messi collectibles — no purchase required.`
