# 💰TODAY & TOMORROW: Win MyLowe's Money Days prizes💰
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-11**
## Executive summary

- This is the Money Days urgency-close email — the in-store event play on Days 3 and 4 (July 11–12) of a four-day window. The strategy is sound: pull the most engaged loyalty subscribers off the couch and into a store during a narrow time slot with a scarcity mechanic (first 150 members/day). The blue hero is clean, the dates and hours are front-and-center, and "FREE MyLowe's Money!" is a genuinely appealing anchor offer.
- The execution problem is the CTA. Driving someone to a physical store with a "Learn more" button is a misfire — the one action this email needs (find a nearby store, check hours, add to calendar) is buried in the footer or absent entirely. You've built urgency in the subject, created scarcity in the body, then offered the blandest possible next step.
- The broader pattern: this is at least the fifth Money Days touch since July 9. Compared to yesterday's mystery savings angle and the credit card push, this one is the most legitimate and event-specific — but subscribers who've already tuned out the series won't rediscover the energy from "Learn more."

## What's working

- **Date and time specificity is excellent** — "7/11 and 7/12 · 10 a.m. – 1 p.m." leaves zero ambiguity; the window creates real urgency.
- **Scarcity mechanic is disclosed upfront** — "first 150 members per day" is a credible, visible limit that rewards subscribers who act early.
- **"FREE MyLowe's Money!" is the strongest value anchor in recent sends** — concrete, loyalty-relevant, and more compelling than the mystery savings framing from July 10.
- **Visual hierarchy is clean** — blue hero, event title, date/time, offer, single CTA — the eye flows without friction.
- **Consistent Money Days branding** — the creative matches the July 9 launch email, so the event feels cohesive rather than ad hoc.

## What's weak

- **"Learn more" is the wrong CTA for a physical store event** — the logical next action is "Find my store" or "Get directions," not a generic discovery prompt.
- **Subject says "Win prizes," body says "giveaways"** — minor copy mismatch that reads slightly inconsistent.
- **No giveaway specifics beyond FREE MyLowe's Money** — "one of many giveaways" is vague; naming one or two more items would sharpen desire.
- **ALL CAPS + flanking dual emoji in subject reads promotional** — the urgency is real, but the formatting signals spam before the content can prove otherwise.
- **In-store-only with a narrow 3-hour window is a high bar** — no acknowledgment that online Money Days deals still run separately, leaving digital-first subscribers with nothing to do if they can't make it in person.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Replace "Learn more" with "Find my nearest Lowe's"** — this is the single highest-leverage fix. The in-store CTA needs to point to a store locator, not a generic landing page.
- 2. **Name at least one additional giveaway** — even "tools, gift cards, and more" beats the current vague plural.
- 3. **Add a one-liner for online subscribers** — "Can't make it in? Money Days deals still live online through 7/12." This retains relevance for the majority who won't attend.
- 4. **Fix the subject caps and emoji stacking** — one emoji, sentence-case urgency, same message.
- 5. **Add a store-locator link in the hero area**, not just the footer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `💰 Last 2 days: Claim your MyLowe's Money Days prize`
- **Subject Alt B:** `In-store only: Free MyLowe's Money giveaway — today & tomorrow`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `First 150 members each day win. Doors open 10 a.m. – 1 p.m.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Pick up FREE MyLowe's Money + more — 7/11 and 7/12 only.`

The email is structurally sound with standard table-based layout and responsive CSS, but the AgentMail relay is suppressing critical deliverability and compliance headers, and one promotional image is missing alt text. Authentication posture cannot be verified due to missing relay passthrough.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **29 tracking links skipped** by HTTP probe — all routed through `e.lowes.com` click-redirect domain. Normal for ESP-tracked sends, but means destination URL health (404s, redirect chains) is unverified for this capture.
- **Plain-text version is 70% URLs** (3,039 / 3,977 chars): the plain-text alternate is likely auto-generated from the HTML with no human-readable prose. Spam filters score high URL density in plain-text negatively. Lowe's volume likely offsets this via domain reputation, but it's a flag.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **Missing alt text** on `a22207c1-63c7-47c0-9e00-61227828e1fe.jpg` (`https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/1/a22207c1-63c7-47c0-9e00-61227828e1fe.jpg`). If images are blocked (default in Outlook, corporate environments), this image renders as a blank box with no context.
- **Dual `@font-face` block duplication**: both Fellix and DIN Next font stacks are loaded inside separate `<!--[if !mso]><!--> ... <![endif]-->` conditional blocks. This is correct for MSO exclusion, but DIN Next references two different CDN versions (`1.4.4` for regular, `1.2.0` for bold/black) — a version skew that could produce inconsistent weight rendering if the CDN paths diverge.
- **`max-width: 480px` on `body`** in the mobile media query (`@media only screen and (max-width: 480px)`) is a known pattern but can clip content on some Android clients where viewport != body width.
- No issues with XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype — appropriate for broad email client compatibility.

### 7. Recommendations

**High priority:**
