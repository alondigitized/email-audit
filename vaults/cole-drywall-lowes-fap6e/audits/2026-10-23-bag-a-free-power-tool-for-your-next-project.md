# Bag a FREE power tool for your next project🎁
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-23**
## Executive summary

- The hero is the strongest part. A big green "3 FREE" block sits beside a DEWALT jigsaw and battery kit, so the eye lands on the offer first. The subject's "FREE power tool" promise carries through into the render. But the hero is hedged: "Get up to 3 FREE select tools when you buy a select battery kit." That means a purchase, a specific kit, and a "select" list. The subject says "Bag a FREE power tool," which promises more than the body delivers.
- Below the hero, the email loses focus. It goes into DEWALT "Save up to $200", EGO "Save up to $60", a fall seasonal block, and a "Curated with you in mind" row. That row has three odd, off-theme products: a storage tote, a paper lawn bag and a cleanser. Then comes a Deals of the Day banner. Every module has its own "Shop now" or "Buy now" button, so no single action stands out.
- Judgement: a good hero on a clear campaign, diluted by a scattered body and a weak product row. It's decent, not great. It's a Lowe's send that would suit a DIY or tool buyer but gives a casual subscriber little reason to act now.

## What's working

- The "3 FREE" hero is bold and high contrast, and the DEWALT jigsaw and batteries make the product obvious.
- The subject and hero match on the free power tool theme, and the gift emoji adds a little seasonal feel.
- The top nav (Appliances, Daily Deals, Bathroom, Outdoor Tools) and the clean blue-and-white modules keep the email scannable.
- The DEWALT "Save up to $200" and EGO "Save up to $60" tiles give concrete dollar-off figures.
- Lowe's branding is consistent, and the Deals of the Day banner in red is easy to see.

## What's weak

- The "FREE" claim is hedged by "up to," "select tools" and "buy a select battery kit." There is no kit price, no end date and no tool list visible.
- There are too many equal-weight CTAs (about 10 "Shop now" or "Buy now" buttons), so no one path stands out.
- The "Curated with you in mind" row is irrelevant to the tool theme. A black tote, a paper lawn bag and a cleanser look random. The tote's product title is also truncated ("27.0 -Gallons ( 108.0 -"), and none of the three has a price.
- There is no urgency or deadline anywhere, and no loyalty or MyLowe's benefit is visible.
- The CTA copy is generic: "Shop now" and "Buy now" with no product or offer attached.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the free-tool offer concrete and honest. Name the kit price and the free tools, and add an end date, e.g. "Buy a DEWALT 20V battery kit, get 3 tools free. Ends Sunday."
- 2. Cut the body to the hero plus two supporting tiles. Drop the seasonal block and the random product row.
- 3. If a product row stays, fill it with tools or kits that tie to the offer, with prices and clean titles.
- 4. Use one primary CTA with specific copy, such as "Get 3 free tools." Make the other buttons secondary or text links.
- 5. Add a deadline line near the hero and a MyLowe's tie-in.

- **Subject Alt B:** `3 free tools with select battery kits 🔧`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus save up to $200 on DEWALT. Ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Pick your kit, then choose your free tools. Kobalt, DEWALT and CRAFTSMAN.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is mostly standard table-based email markup. The automated QA run found no hard failures, and a 57% pass rate with 3 warnings. The main verifiable issues are 17 images with no alt text, including 15 retail-media impression pixels, and the recipient's email address in plaintext in those pixel URLs. The compliance and authentication items are unverified, not confirmed defects. The source was truncated and 36 links were not probed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified destinations:** 36 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so I can't confirm their status codes, redirect chains or final landing URLs.
- **Impression pixels:** 15 `https://sli.lowes.com/imp?...` pixels are embedded (`s=127768300`–`304`, `2019286`–`2019290`, `1090834`–`1090838`). They carry `stpe=pixel`, `static` or `default` and `li_coord` parameters, so they look like a retail-media ad-serving integration. Each is an extra third-party request at open time. Some appear to repeat the same `s` (`2019286`, `1090834` each appear twice), which may double-count impressions.
- **No other link defects:** I found nothing else in the visible source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (17 images):**
  - Content images: `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg` and `.../m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png`. If these carry content, screen readers and image-blocked clients get nothing.
