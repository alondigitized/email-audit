# Up to 40% OFF! More reasons to reset your backyard
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- This is Lowe's back in patio/outdoor mode, and it's a clean, coherent execution of a beat we haven't seen since the early-August cycle — a real shift from the appliance-closeout and storage promos that dominated late July/early August. The hero is unambiguous: "Get up to 40% off select patio furniture and accessories" over a styled outdoor lounge photo with a single "Shop now" button, and the layout stays disciplined below it — tools/fencing, outdoor power equipment ($200 off), grills (20% off), then a Rewards 2x points module and a soft "Find fresh ideas" CTA before the Deals of the Day footer.
- Compared to 8/9's "Sunday Deals" grab-bag (6/10, unrelated cross-sell cascade) and 8/6's "Summer Storage Event" pivot, this send is more visually focused — everything on the page is genuinely outdoor/patio-related, which is a meaningful improvement in coherence even if none of it is personalized.
- It's a solid, on-brand seasonal send with no render bugs and a real offer up top, but it's still a generic blast — no name, no loyalty-tier callout until the 2x points module, and a subject line that's functionally identical to a dozen past Lowe's patio pushes.

## What's working

- Hero image and headline are on-brand and immediately legible: "40% off" is the largest element on the page, eye lands there first.
- Single clear primary CTA ("Shop now") on the hero, and every subsequent module also carries its own "Shop now" button — no dead-end tiles.
- Every module ties back to the backyard/outdoor theme (tools, fencing, power equipment, grills) — no unrelated cross-sell noise like the 8/9 send had.
- Concrete secondary offers are visible and quantified: "$200 off" outdoor power equipment, "20% off" grills.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text doesn't overlap, buttons are all styled consistently.

## What's weak

- No personalization anywhere in the render — no first name, no "based on your recent purchase," nothing that signals this is targeted vs. a mass blast.
- The 2x Points Rewards module ("Activate now") is buried mid-email after four other CTAs — for a loyalty member this could realistically be higher-value than the patio furniture, but it's visually subordinate.
- "Find fresh ideas for every corner of your home" is a vague, low-intent CTA that breaks the reset-your-backyard focus right before the footer.
- Fine print at the very bottom ("One time offer only. Valid 8/6/26-9/30/26... MyLowe's Rewards Members must log into their loyalty account and 'activate'") reveals the 2x points offer has real friction (manual activation, exclusions) not hinted at in the module itself.
- Subject line's "more reasons to reset your backyard" is soft/generic — doesn't specify what's new versus prior patio sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a segmented subject/preheader for loyalty members that name-checks the 2x points offer, since that's the highest-margin ask in the email but currently reads as an afterthought — pull it up near the hero or fold it into the subject for members.
- 2. Cut or merge the "Find fresh ideas" module — it dilutes the backyard-reset narrative right before the close and doesn't add a new offer.
- 3. Add a countdown/date directly on the hero or 2x points module (the "8/6/26-9/30/26" window in the fine print is a real, usable urgency hook that's currently hidden).
- 4. Swap the generic "Shop now" copy on at least the hero for something offer-specific like "Shop 40% off patio" to reinforce the discount at the click point.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Patio sets up to 40% off — plus $200 off outdoor power tools`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your backyard, 40% off: patio, grills, tools`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Save on patio furniture, grills, and outdoor power equipment — plus earn 2x points on select brands.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `40% off patio sets, $200 off outdoor tools, 20% off grills. Limited time.`
