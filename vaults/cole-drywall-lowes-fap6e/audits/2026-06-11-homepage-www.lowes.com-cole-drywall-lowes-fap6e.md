# Homepage snapshot · www.lowes.com · 2026-06-11
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-11**
## Executive summary

- Lowe's is playing the Father's Day angle hard, which — yeah, I get it, it's ten days out — but I land on their site to pick up drywall mud and joint tape, and instead I get a sign-in pop-up blocking half the page before I even see what's on sale. The store I know and trust is under there somewhere, but the door's getting slammed in my face before I can look around. It's a loyalty-first play that feels more like a toll booth.

## What's working

- **Father's Day urgency banner at the very top** — "Father's Day is 6/21/26" in the top strip and repeated in the hero. As a dad who buys himself tools around this holiday, that date lands.
- **Silver Key + points pitch in the modal** — I actually have a MyLowe's card, so spelling out Silver Key free shipping and points earning in the sign-in prompt is the right bait. Makes me think about logging in.
- **"Fast and free delivery" in the hero** — short, direct, no jargon. I've had stuff show up fast from Lowe's before and that reinforces it.
- **Clean mobile layout** — header icons are crisp, the logo is prominent, the font stack renders sharp. No janky overflow.

## What's weak

- **Sign-in modal fires immediately** — I haven't even scanned the page and I've got a pop-up eating 60% of the screen. This is the number-one reason I bounce. I already have an account; this feels punishing.
- **No tools, no building materials, no project-mode content above fold** — the entire visible hero is a family grilling outside. I'm here to finish a basement, not shop for a Father's Day BBQ set.
- **"Deals" badge is cut off at the right edge** — I can see a sliver of a red badge at the top-right of the hero but can't read it. That's a missed click.
- **$5 off and Silver Key both carry asterisks** — the fine print isn't shown, which makes me skeptical. Is the $5 off on a $50 minimum? Does Silver Key require a credit card? I don't know.
- **No search bar accessible** — the modal covers where the search bar would be. My first instinct on any home improvement site is to type what I need. That's blocked.

## Recommendations

- **Make the sign-in modal dismissible with one tap and load it later** — fire it after a scroll event or on the second page view. Right now it's the first thing I see and it creates friction before I've seen a single product.
- **Swap the outdoor grill hero for a Father's Day tools angle** — a workbench, a Kobalt tool chest, or a guy power-washing a deck would hit closer to home for Lowe's core customer. Keep the date urgency, just show the right product world.
- **Surface a MyLowe's balance or recent-order module** if I'm logged in — "Your last order: joint compound — reorder?" would make me feel like Lowe's knows me.
- **Show the free-shipping threshold in plain text** — "FREE shipping on orders $45+" is more useful than "FREE shipping with Silver Key status*" because it doesn't require me to already be in the program to act on it.
