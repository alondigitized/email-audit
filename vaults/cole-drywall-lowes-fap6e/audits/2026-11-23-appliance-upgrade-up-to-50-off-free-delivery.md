# Appliance upgrade? Up to 50% OFF + FREE delivery
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the best-aligned Lowe's send in the recent run. The subject promises "Up to 50% OFF + FREE delivery," and the second hero delivers it: a big red "ALL-IN Appliance Offer" with a giant "50% off," a green "FREE Delivery" block, free installation and haul-away, and a 2-year protection plan. Subject and body match, which wasn't true of the Nov 22 "battery kit" send, where a generic Black Friday banner led.
- The hierarchy is still weak. Before the appliance offer you get a black "FREE same-day delivery" Rewards strip and a red "Black Friday: Don't wait. Start checking things off your list" banner with no offer at all. The appliance hero sits a full scroll down, and the email then runs through smart home, vanities, trees and "curated" picks. It's the same kitchen-sink template as the Nov 17 appliance send.
- Verdict: a strong core offer in a bloated container. It's a solid send, and it would be a very good one if the appliance hero were the first block.

## What's working

- The subject's "50% OFF + FREE delivery" is repeated in the hero with a huge "50% off" and a green "FREE Delivery" block.
- The hero shows real appliances (washer, fridge, dishwasher, microwave), so it's clearly in-category.
- The bundle is concrete: free delivery, free installation, free haul-away, a 2-year protection plan, and up to $250 off laundry.
- The Lowe's branding, nav (Black Friday, Tools, Daily Deals, Appliances) and red/blue palette are consistent and trusted.
- The rewards strip and "free for members" notes show loyalty benefits.

## What's weak

- The "Black Friday: Start checking things off your list" banner sits above the real offer. It has no discount and a generic "Shop now."
- The appliance hero starts about 280px down in the rendered view, so it likely falls below the mobile fold.
- Eight or more competing blocks dilute the focus: 70% smart home, 50/40/30% vanities, bathroom items and fans, a holiday tree, 12 Days of Deals at $59, and three product tiles.
- The $896 spend threshold and the "loyalty membership required" conditions are small print at the bottom of the hero. The FREE claims carry an asterisk, and the legal block is huge.
- "FREE same-day delivery*" in the top strip competes with, and slightly muddies, the "FREE Delivery" in the hero.
- The three "Curated" tiles (griddle tool set, animatronic reindeer, impact socket set) are random and have truncated titles.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the appliance hero to the top, directly under the nav. Cut the generic Black Friday banner, or fold it into the hero.
- 2. Surface the $896 threshold in the hero headline area, e.g. "Spend $896+, get free delivery, install and haul-away."
- 3. Cut the secondary blocks to two or three: appliances, smart home, and one holiday block. Drop the vanity and fan trio and the random product tiles.
- 4. Use a specific CTA like "Shop appliance deals" instead of "Shop now" on every block.
- 5. Show actual appliance prices or a hero SKU to give the click a concrete target.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 50% off appliances + free install`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Spend $896, get free delivery & haul-away`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus free installation, haul-away and a 2-year protection plan on select major appliances.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Save an extra $250 on laundry. Deals end 12/2, order by 2pm for same-day delivery.`

## Technical Audit: "Appliance upgrade? Up to 50% OFF + FREE delivery"

### 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is mostly standard ESP-generated XHTML with responsive CSS. The confirmed problems are the recipient's email address in plaintext in 16 ad-pixel URLs, missing alt text on 2 content images, and an `<html>` tag with no `lang`. The source was truncated and the relay captured no headers, so compliance, authentication, and most link and UTM checks can't be verified.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **41 tracking links were not probed.** The QA check skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains, and link health are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Ad impression pixels:** 16 `https://sli.lowes.com/imp?s=…&e=…&p=…&lctg=410146703&stpe=pixel|static|default` beacons are embedded. Examples are `s=127768300` through `127768304` with `p=531434`, and `s=2019286`, `s=1090834` and others with `p=11232026`.
  - Several URLs include `li_coord`, and the QA output cuts them off there.
  - These are third-party-style retail-media impression calls on a separate subdomain from the click-tracking domain. They add many extra image requests.
  - Each request exposes the recipient address (see section 4).

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images:**
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg`
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png`

  With images blocked or a screen reader, these are silent or announced by filename. Add descriptive alt text, or `alt=""` if they are purely decorative.
- **Tracking pixels without alt:** The 16 `sli.lowes.com/imp` images were flagged too. These should carry `alt=""` and explicit `width="1" height="1"` so they are ignored by assistive tech and don't affect layout.
- **No `lang` attribute:** The root tag is `<html xmlns="https://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">`. Without `lang`, screen readers may use the wrong pronunciation rules.
- **Malformed DOCTYPE:** The source reads `PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN"` with no space after `PUBLIC`. Most clients tolerate this, but it is invalid.
- **Namespace and DTD URLs:** Both use `https://www.w3.org/...`. The canonical XHTML namespace is `http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml`. This is harmless in email clients but non-standard.
- **No other rendering issues found in the visible portion.** It includes the viewport meta, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, a `!mso` guard around the web fonts, `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` resets, and responsive queries at 480px and 375–600px. I can't judge the rest because the source is truncated.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Recipient email address exposed in URLs (confirmed):** Every `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixel carries `e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app` as a plaintext query parameter, for example `…/imp?s=127768300&e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app&p=531434&…`.
  - This is PII in a URL. It will show up in server logs, proxy logs, and referrer or CDN logs, and it can reveal the address if the message is forwarded or its images are inspected.
  - Best practice is a hashed or opaque subscriber ID.
- **No unresolved merge tokens** (such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, or `[FIRST_NAME]`) were seen in the subject or the visible source.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**. It is a gap in the evidence, not a confirmed violation. Large senders need both headers to meet Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found,** so SPF, DKIM, and DMARC alignment for `e.lowes.com` is unknown. This is also likely a relay artifact.
- **Visible footer elements can't be assessed.** The unsubscribe link, physical postal address, and sender identification fall in the truncated portion. The From address `lowes@e.lowes.com` is a clearly identifiable sender on a subdomain.
- **No confirmed compliance defects.**

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- Click URLs went unprobed, so UTM parameters, redirect targets, and landing-page alignment with the "Up to 50% OFF + FREE delivery" offer are **unverifiable** from this data.
- The only parameters visible in the source are on the impression pixels (`lctg=410146703`, `p=…`, `s=…`), which are ad-attribution IDs rather than campaign UTMs.

### 7. Recommendations
1. **Remove the plaintext email from the `sli.lowes.com/imp` URLs.** Use a hashed or opaque subscriber key.
2. **Add alt text** to `c301e24e-…jpg` and `ad569dc5-…png`, and use `alt=""` plus 1×1 dimensions on all impression pixels.
3. **Add `lang="en"`** to `<html>` and fix the DOCTYPE spacing (`PUBLIC "-//W3C…`).
4. **Re-run the audit against raw `.eml` source** to verify the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and the `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) line.
5. **Resolve the 41 skipped tracking links** by following redirects, then check final destinations and UTM or campaign parameters.
6. **Review the full, untruncated HTML** for the footer (unsubscribe link, postal address) and any remaining tokens or `<title>` tag.
