# Great deals on picks from your go-to brand 🤩
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-15**
## Executive summary

- This is Lowe's returning to a straightforward multi-category deals email after a week of event-driven sends (Money Days → PAW Patrol → brand-awareness). The creative is clean — the patio hero sets a seasonal mood, and the offer blocks below deliver real discounts (40% off patio furniture, 20% off grills, $399 pressure washer). Nothing is broken.
- The central problem is the subject line. "Great deals on picks from your go-to brand 🤩" promises something personalized — a brand I care about, curated for me — and the email delivers… five unrelated categories across a standard promotional blast. That's a trust tax. When a subject implies behavioral targeting and the body doesn't follow through, skeptical subscribers start wondering if every "personalized" send is just marketing copy.
- The heavier structural issue is cadence. This is the seventh Lowe's email in seven days. The Money Days burn was worth it; this send doesn't have the urgency to justify it.

## What's working

- **Summer relevance is on-point.** Patio hero on July 15 is exactly right — mid-summer, peak outdoor season, no argument.
- **Concrete discounts are present.** "Save up to 40%" on patio furniture and "20%" on grills are specific enough to motivate a scan.
- **The $399 Greenworks pressure washer is the standout unit.** A named product, a specific price, and a visible "Buy now" CTA — this is the closest thing to a real-deal anchor.
- **"Limited Time" badge on power tools** adds mild urgency without being obnoxious.
- **No render bugs.** Layout holds, images load, buttons are visible.

## What's weak

- **Subject-body mismatch is the biggest miss.** "Your go-to brand" implies I'm getting brand-specific personalization (like an allen+roth drop or a Kobalt restock — as teased in the July 14 send). The email delivers five unrelated categories. That's a broken promise.
- **Too many competing CTAs.** At minimum 6 distinct calls-to-action (Shop now × 3, Learn more, Buy now, Get started, Explore now). No primary action wins; attention fragments.
- **"We'll send deals you'll love / Learn more" section breaks the shopping flow.** Embedding a preferences-center pitch mid-scroll is a dead stop. It belongs in a dedicated preference-management email, not buried between grills and pressure washers.
- **No MyLowe's loyalty layer.** After seven days of Money Days loyalty messaging, this email has zero member-exclusive framing. That's a missed retention signal right when subscribers are most activated.
- **Patio hero doesn't show a discount.** The biggest visual real estate (the hero image) has "Shop now" but no offer — the deals are one scroll down.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fulfill the subject's personalization promise or rewrite it.** If behavioral targeting is actually running, name the brand or category ("Your Dewalt picks, now 20% off"). If not, drop the pretense entirely and lead with the actual best offer.
- 2. **Kill the "We'll send deals you'll love" preference block.** It kills momentum mid-email. Move it to a post-purchase or re-engagement flow.
- 3. **Add the offer to the hero.** Overlay "Up to 40% off outdoor furniture" directly on the patio hero image. Don't make subscribers scroll to find the discount.
- 4. **Pick one hero CTA.** Either the pressure washer (specific, priced) or the 40% patio sale (broad). Remove the competing sections or push them below a clear fold.
- 5. **Re-inject a MyLowe's loyalty signal.** Even "MyLowe's members get early access" adds a tier that converts fence-sitters — especially after seven days of loyalty conditioning.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 40% off patio furniture — shop the season`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Dewalt picks: save up to $100 this week`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 20% off grills and a $399 Greenworks pressure washer.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Patio furniture, power tools, grills — real discounts, no code needed.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**8 images missing `alt` text (QA-confirmed)**

| Image | URL fragment | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| Hero/content | `c301e24e-...jpg` | High — content image needs descriptive alt |
| Content | `ad569dc5-...png` | High — content image needs descriptive alt |
| Open pixel | `o.gif` | Low — should be `alt=""` (decorative) |
| 5× impression pixels | `sli.lowes.com/imp?...` | Low — should be `alt=""` (decorative) |

Tracking pixels require `alt=""` (empty, not absent) to be valid per WCAG 1.1.1. Content images need meaningful alt text for screen readers and image-blocked rendering.

**Overlapping media query breakpoints**
Two `@media` blocks cover overlapping ranges:
- `@media screen and (min-width: 375px) and (max-width: 600px)` 
- `@media only screen and (max-width: 480px)`

Both fire on 375–480px viewports (most iPhones). Rule conflicts in this range (e.g., `.drop`, `.container`, `.no-padding`) will resolve by source order, which may not match intent.

**`woff2` absent from all `@font-face` declarations**
Fellix and DIN Next font stacks only declare `woff`. All modern clients support `woff2` (30% smaller). Not a breakage, but a performance gap.

**CDN version mismatch in DIN Next**
```
DIN Next Regular: /scaffold/1.4.4/fonts/din/din-next.woff
DIN Next Bold:    /scaffold/1.2.0/fonts/din/din-next-bold.woff
DIN Next Black:   /scaffold/1.2.0/fonts/din/din-next-black.woff
```
Regular references a newer scaffold version than Bold/Black. If `1.2.0` is deprecated, Bold and Black could 404 on CDN cache eviction.

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header missing — HIGH**
QA warning: `List-Unsubscribe header not found`. The AgentMail relay is not capturing this header. Gmail has required `List-Unsubscribe` for bulk senders (>5k/day) since February 2024; without it, Gmail may display a less prominent unsubscribe path or classify the message as promotional in ways that hurt placement.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) missing — HIGH**
QA warning: `List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found`. This header enables one-click machine-readable unsubscribe. Also a Gmail bulk sender requirement since February 2024. Absence does not mean Lowe's isn't sending it — the relay isn't forwarding it.

**Authentication-Results not captured**
QA warning: `Authentication-Results header not found`. SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail for `e.lowes.com` cannot be confirmed from the captured message. This is a relay pipeline gap, not confirmed as a sending failure. The sender domain (`e.lowes.com`) is a standard ESP subdomain that should have its own DKIM and SPF records.
