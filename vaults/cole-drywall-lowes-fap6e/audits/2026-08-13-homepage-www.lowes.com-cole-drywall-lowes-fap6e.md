# Homepage snapshot · www.lowes.com · 2026-08-13
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- Pulled it up on my phone during a coffee break from the basement drywall. First thing I see is a sign-in popup shoved in my face, and behind it a Halloween skeleton hero — in mid-August, before I've even finished mudding this ceiling. This reads like a big-box that's still finding its footing between "help me finish my project" and "buy a plastic skeleton." Nothing here is speaking to the guy elbow-deep in a basement remodel right now.

## What's working

- The **sign-in popup's benefit list** ($5 off, earn points, free shipping with Silver Key) is a real reason for a card-carrying MyLowe's guy like me to log in before I shop.
- **"Stay organized with storage solutions"** tile with the garage cabinets — that's exactly the kind of module I stop and look at mid-basement-project.
- **"Ceiling Fans Under $150"** — specific price point, relevant if I'm finishing out a room.
- The **MyLowe's Rewards / MyLowe's Pro Rewards banner** at the bottom ("Same-day delivery. For FREE.") is right there without scrolling, which I appreciate since I don't dig for loyalty stuff.
- **Recommended Searches** row (Electric Range, Washing Machine, Bathroom Vanity, Air Compressor) is actually reading my mind a little — appliance and tool territory.

## What's weak

- The **Halloween hero banner** is the first thing I see and it's got nothing to do with a basement build. Feels early for late-summer too — I'm not thinking pumpkins, I'm thinking drywall mud and a compressor.
- The **sign-in popup auto-launched over the page**, blocking half the top nav (I can't even read "Bathroom" — it's cut off to "Bathroo").
- Every good offer has an **asterisk hanging off it** — $5 off*, FREE shipping*, Same-day delivery for FREE* — that's the fine-print stuff that makes me want to just close the tab and go to the store.
- **No trust signals** anywhere on this screen — no review counts, no "Pro" badge callouts beyond the nav link, nothing reinforcing I'm making a smart buy.
- Zero **appliance or tool-specific hero content** even though that's clearly what Lowe's knows I search for (see the recommended searches row right there).

## Recommendations

- Swap the Halloween skeleton hero for a **rotating hero tied to what I'm actually searching** — Air Compressor, Bathroom Vanity, storage — since you already have that data one row down.
- Kill the auto-popping sign-in modal on first load, or at least don't let it clip the nav bar — make it a dismissible corner nudge instead.
- Put a **real number** on the shipping/delivery offer instead of an asterisk — "Free shipping on $45+" beats "FREE shipping with Silver Key status*" for someone deciding whether to keep scrolling.
- Add a **Pro/Rewards points balance teaser** for logged-in-adjacent users — something like "You could be earning 5% back today" right next to that Deals banner.
