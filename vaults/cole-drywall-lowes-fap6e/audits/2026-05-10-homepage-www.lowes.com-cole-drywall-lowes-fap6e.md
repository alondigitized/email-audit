# Homepage snapshot · www.lowes.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- First thing I get is a login modal blocking almost everything — sign in or create an account, get $5 off. I'm mid-project on my basement, I opened the app to find drywall compound or maybe check a price on a vanity, and the first thing Lowe's does is gate me behind an account push. Below that I can see a Flash Sale on appliances ending 5/10 and a Mother's Day gift push. Feels like a general retail homepage, not a home improvement hub.

## What's working

- **Flash Sale urgency** — "Offers end 5/10/26" on hot appliance deals is specific and time-stamped; if I were in the market for a washer this would grab me fast
- **Sign-in perks callout** — listing the three benefits (earn points, free shipping with Silver Key, $5 off) is smart positioning even if the modal timing is wrong
- **"Recommended" category chips** — I can see "Tool Box" in that pill row, which is at least in my neighborhood as a DIYer
- **Clean mobile layout** — nav bar, search bar, and content stack without any breakage

## What's weak

- **The modal fires immediately** — I haven't seen anything yet and I'm already being asked to sign in; for a logged-in MyLowe's member this is a fail, and for a new visitor it's an instant annoyance
- **Mother's Day hero is completely off-target** — I'm 47, mid-basement-finish, I came here for drywall screws or a shop vac, not gift ideas for mom
- **No rewards balance or account welcome** — I carry the Lowe's Advantage card and have a MyLowe's account; the page treats me like a stranger
- **Appliance deal hero is below the modal** — the most relevant promo for a project shopper is physically blocked by the sign-in overlay
- **Zero drywall, flooring, framing, or finishing product category** visible above the fold — my actual shopping mission is invisible

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the immediate modal for returning visitors** — if I've got a MyLowe's cookie, skip the join popup and surface my rewards balance + recent projects instead
- **Swap "Find the perfect gift for Mom" for a project-category hero** — basement finishing, flooring, drywall, paint — that's who is actually buying at Lowe's on a Saturday
- **Surface MyLowe's points balance and Advantage card savings in the top nav** — even a small chip showing "1,240 pts" would make me feel seen and keep me in the app
- **Promote the Flash Sale deadline with a countdown clock** — "Ends today at midnight" is more urgent than a static date stamp
