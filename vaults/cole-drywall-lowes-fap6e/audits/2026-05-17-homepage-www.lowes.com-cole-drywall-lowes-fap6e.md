# Homepage snapshot · www.lowes.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Alright, so I opened Lowe's and the first thing I get is a sign-in modal shoved in my face before I can see anything. I can make out there's a Memorial Day banner underneath, which is exactly the sale I'd be shopping — I time my big appliance and supply hauls around these holiday weekends. But this popup is killing the moment before it even starts.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day banner:** That navy/red "Memorial Day — Summer's hottest deals start now" strip is dead-on for when I actually pull the trigger on big purchases. This is exactly why I'm here.
- **Sign-in perk list:** The modal clearly explains what I get — $5 off, points on purchases, free shipping with Silver Key. That's a real pitch for my MyLowe's Rewards card, laid out plainly.
- **"Shop All" CTA on the Memorial Day strip:** Simple, one tap, gets me to the sale. That's how it should work.
- **Recommended chips ("Tool Box", "Bathroom Vanity"):** Some of those quick-tap chips are relevant to project work — Tool Box lands close to home for me.
- **Top banner:** "Get your home summer-ready with Memorial Day savings. Shop Now >" — small but clear, consistent message, right timing.

## What's weak

- **The sign-in modal hijacks the entire viewport:** I haven't even seen the homepage yet and I'm being asked to log in. I know my rewards login — I'll do it eventually — but don't make it the first thing I fight through on mobile.
- **"Grow a beautiful garden with organic…" hero peeking at the bottom:** That's not Cole Drywall's homepage. I'm finishing a basement, not planting tomatoes. That slide is already fighting for my attention and losing.
- **No Kobalt, no power tools, no building materials above the fold:** My category is completely invisible. Not one mention of lumber, drywall, fasteners, or electrical in the first screen.
- **Fine print asterisks on every offer:** "select items†", "Silver Key status*" — already making me suspicious about what actually qualifies.
- **"Recommended" section is cut off:** I can see "Bathroom Vanity", "Tool Box", "Bathroom Vanity With S…" — the horizontal chip rail is truncating. On mobile this feels sloppy.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the auto-pop sign-in modal on return visits** — if I've been to Lowe's before, surface the loyalty bar inline instead of blocking the whole page. First-time visitors only.
- **Lead the Memorial Day hero with a project-specific angle** — swap "Summer's hottest deals" for something like "Finish the project this weekend — up to 30% off tools, lumber & appliances" with a Kobalt tool or drywall visual.
- **Surface a "Building Materials" or "Tools" category chip prominently** — ahead of "Bathroom Vanity" in the recommended strip, since tools/hardware is core traffic for guys like me.
- **Show a concrete dollar threshold for free shipping** — "Free shipping on orders $45+" lands harder than "FREE shipping with Silver Key status*" which sounds conditional and complicated.
