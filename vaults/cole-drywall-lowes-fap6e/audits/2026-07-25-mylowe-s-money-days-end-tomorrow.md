# MyLowe’s Money Days end tomorrow‼️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-25**
## Executive summary

- This is Lowe's closing the loop on MyLowe's Money Days with a same-day-before-deadline push — and the "Money Days are BACK!" headline is a little jarring given the brand history shows this event has been running continuously since it "went LIVE" on July 24 and was previewed as early as July 23. Calling it "back" the day before it ends reads like a templating leftover rather than intentional copy.
- The offer itself is clear and personalized: $10 extra MyLowe's Money on a $200+ purchase, explicitly flagged for Bronze members. That tiering is a real signal of segmentation, which is more than most of this week's Lowe's sends have shown. But there's a visible render bug right at the top of the hero — the "ENDS TOMORROW" ribbon has a ghosted, overlapping duplicate of itself, which looks broken on first glance and undercuts the urgency it's trying to create.
- Net: solid single-offer clarity and real personalization, dragged down by a rendering glitch and a fifth-plus touch this week on the same underlying event (per July 21–25 history). Middle-of-pack send, saved by the member-tier specificity.

## What's working

- Single, unambiguous offer: $10 extra MyLowe's Money on $200+, no competing pods or clutter.
- Explicit membership tier callout ("Bronze members!") — real personalization most of this week's Lowe's emails lacked.
- "Shop now" CTA button is high-contrast and impossible to miss.
- Deadline urgency ("ENDS TOMORROW") is placed at the very top, above the fold.
- Clean footer/legal block with no missing images or broken layout below the hero.

## What's weak

- The "ENDS TOMORROW" banner has an overlapping/ghosted text render bug — it looks visually broken, not urgent.
- "MyLowe's Money Days are BACK!" is confusing copy for an event that's been continuously live since at least July 24 and already had its "LIVE" launch email.
- This is at least the sixth Lowe's touch on Money Days/related offers in five days — fatigue risk for anyone who opened the July 23–24 sends.
- The reward mechanic (MyLowe's Money, not a straight discount) requires a beat of explanation the subject/hero doesn't give newcomers.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the "ENDS TOMORROW" banner render — the overlapping ghost text is the single biggest drag on this email; a clean, single instance of that ribbon would restore the urgency it's meant to convey.
- 2. Drop "are BACK!" — it contradicts the event's own launch narrative from July 24. Replace with a close framing that matches the lifecycle stage.
- 3. Add a real countdown or specific end time/date instead of the generic "ends tomorrow" — Lowe's has used this exact phrase before (July 24 smart-home close), so specificity would help this one stand out.
- 4. Since this is a late-cycle reminder, consider trimming send frequency for members who already opened the July 23/24 Money Days emails — reserve this last touch for non-openers.
