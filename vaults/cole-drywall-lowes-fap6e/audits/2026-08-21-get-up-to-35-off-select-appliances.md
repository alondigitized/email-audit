# Get up to 35% OFF select appliances🥳
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-21**
## Executive summary

- This is Lowe's back on the appliance beat that opened the month, and it's the most coupon-dense send I've seen from them yet: 35% off appliances, member perks (free delivery/install/haul-away plus a 2-year protection plan), $300/$100 bundle savings, 25% off smart home, 35% off home accents, 40% off bed/bath, plus a cooling/dehumidifier push at the bottom. The hero module itself is genuinely strong — clean product lineup (washer, fridge, microwave, range, dishwasher), one clear "Shop now" CTA, and the member-benefit block reads honestly (delivery/install/haul-away, not vague "extras").
- The problem is everything after the fold. Once you scroll past the hero, this becomes a nine-module cascade (curated picks → smart security → home accents/bed-bath → kitchen/bath → cooling/rewards → Deals of the Day → newsletter signup) with a different discount on nearly every screen. It's the same sprawl pattern as the 8/17 "Set up your space, your way" send, just wearing an appliance hat up top. If you only care about appliances, you get what you came for in module one; everything else is noise diluting a focused subject line.

## What's working

- Hero delivers exactly what the subject promises: "Get up to 35% off select appliances" with a matching product lineup directly beneath it.
- Member benefits are itemized concretely (free delivery, basic install, haul-away, basic parts, 2-year protection plan) rather than a vague "member perks" tease.
- Bundle savings are quantified in dollars, not just percent ($300 off Frigidaire bundles, $100 off Electrolux laundry pairs), which reads more tangible than another %-off badge.
- No render bugs — images, buttons, and text all display cleanly with clear "Buy now"/"Shop now" CTAs throughout.

## What's weak

- Nine distinct offers stacked in one email (appliances, storage/moving picks, smart home, home accents, bed/bath, kitchen/bath, cooling) — no way to tell what this email is actually "about" past the first screen.
- Discount percentage changes almost every module (35%, 25%, 35%, 40%) with no unifying sale name, which reads as scattergun rather than a curated event.
- The "Curated with you in mind" module (storage bin, moving box, microwave) feels like generic browse-abandonment filler bolted onto an appliance-themed hero, not a coherent follow-through.
- Cooling/dehumidifier block near the bottom competes with the appliance hero for attention and undercuts the subject line's focus.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to 3-4 modules max: hero appliance offer, member benefits, one adjacent category (e.g., smart home OR cooling, not both), and Deals of the Day. Everything else should be its own send.
- 2. Give the whole email one sale name/theme instead of four different %-off numbers competing for attention — even something as simple as "Appliance Event" banner reused in each module header.
- 3. Move the "Curated with you in mind" module to a separate browse-abandonment email — it doesn't reinforce the appliance narrative and reads as filler.
- 4. Drop the cooling/dehumidifier block from this send entirely; it's a seasonal push that deserves its own subject line, not a footnote after bed/bath.
