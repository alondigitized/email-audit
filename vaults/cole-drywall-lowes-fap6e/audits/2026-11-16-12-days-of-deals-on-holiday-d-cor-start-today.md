# 12 Days of Deals on holiday décor start today
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- This is another Lowe's kitchen-sink send, and the subject and the body don't match. The subject leads with "12 Days of Deals on holiday décor start today." The first hero is a generic "Black Friday: Your shopping list loves an early start on savings" block. The actual 12 Days promo is the second block, and the email never says what Day 1's deal is. Compare 2026-11-15, "Why wait for Black Friday? Up to 50% OFF appliances," where the subject had a number and the hero didn't. Here the subject has a campaign name and no number.
- The email is built for a gift and décor shopper. The recipient is a drywall contractor, and it gives them almost nothing: one DeWalt battery kit in the "Curated with you in mind" row. The most useful thing for them is "FREE same-day delivery" in the rewards banner. It's buried in a thin strip with an asterisk, and the footnote adds a $25 minimum and a membership requirement.
- Judgement: a competent, on-brand seasonal blast that stacks five or six competing ideas (Black Friday, 12 Days, trees, gift guides, free gifts, Deals of the Day). It will get opens on brand recognition. Clicks will be low because no single offer owns the page.

## What's working

- The subject has a real hook: "12 Days of Deals… start today" is a named campaign with a credible daily cadence.
- The preheader adds new information (pre-lit trees from $59.98) instead of repeating the subject.
- The "Starting at $59.98" pre-lit tree block is the one concrete price in the email, and the tree photo is a strong image.
- The "mylowe's Rewards: FREE same-day delivery" banner puts a member benefit right under the nav.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images, and the red and blue blocks give a clear scroll rhythm.

## What's weak

- Two hero blocks compete. The "Black Friday / early start on savings" red block comes first and has no offer, so the 12 Days campaign is pushed below it.
- "12 Days of Deals / New deals on holiday décor drop every day" never says what today's deal is. It promises daily deals but shows none.
- The CTAs are generic. Almost every button says "Shop now," and the product row says "Buy now."
- The copy is irrelevant for a drywall pro. Gift cards, Christmas trees and a Stanley tumbler have nothing to do with the persona. Only the DeWalt kit, a toilet and a storage tote make it into the personalized row.
- The product titles are truncated and messy: "DEWALT 12 -Volt 2 - Pack Battery/charger ( 3 Ah 5 Ah )" and "27.0 -Gallons ( 108.0 -". The product row also shows no prices.
- Free delivery sits behind an asterisk, and the footnote adds a $25 minimum and loyalty membership. Because it appears in the banner with no qualifier, it reads as slightly less honest.

## Recommendations

- 1. Remove the generic "Black Friday" block and make "12 Days of Deals" the hero. Show Day 1's actual deal with a price and a one-day-only deadline.
- 2. Lead the "Curated with you in mind" row with trade-relevant products at real prices (DeWalt, drywall supplies, fasteners) and move it above the gift guides for pro segments.
- 3. Replace "Shop now" and "Buy now" with specific CTAs such as "Get today's deal," "Shop pre-lit trees" and "Shop DeWalt kits."
- 4. Put the free-delivery qualifier ($25 minimum) next to the banner copy instead of only in the footnote.
- 5. Clean up the product titles and add prices to each card.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Day 1 of 12: pre-lit trees from $59.98`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Today's 12 Days deal + free same-day delivery`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `A new holiday deal drops every day until Black Friday. See today's.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free same-day delivery for My Lowe's Rewards members on $25+.`
