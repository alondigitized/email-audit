# Backyard Deals Are Going Fast — Save Up to 25%
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-01**
## Executive summary

- This is Lowe's first genuinely clean summer email after eight days of Memorial Day anchoring. The June 1 backyard/outdoor framing is exactly right for the calendar — unlike the May 31 send that was still leading with "Memorial Day yard deals" six days after the holiday. The hero sets the tone well: "Make the backyard your favorite place" is aspirational and seasonally correct.
- The structural problem is dilution. Five distinct offer pods (patio furniture, grills, Harbor Breeze outdoor lights, pool care, plus a Deals of the Day signup module) make this feel like a browse digest, not a conversion email. Every CTA says "Shop now." There is no single hero product, no countdown timer, and no hard deadline — so the urgency promised in the subject ("Going Fast") evaporates the moment you open it. The "Ending soon — Don't miss $10 appliance deals" crosssell banner is a Memorial Day holdover that clashes with the summer-outdoor narrative.
- Bottom line: seasonally smart, structurally scattered. The brand correctly turned the page but didn't commit to a single message.

## What's working

- Summer pivot is well-timed and the hero copy earns it — a clean break from nine consecutive Memorial Day emails.
- Concrete discounts (25% patio furniture, 20% grills) appear above the fold after one scroll.
- "Set the vibe" / Harbor Breeze solar lights section introduces a "New" badge and a fresh angle that stands apart from the discount pods.
- Pool care module expands outdoor seasonal breadth without feeling redundant.
- Clean render — no broken images, no overlapping text, no visible layout bugs.

## What's weak

- All five CTAs say "Shop now" with zero differentiation — the email is impossible to navigate by intent.
- The "Ending soon — $10 appliance deals" crosssell banner is a Memorial Day holdover planted inside a summer backyard email; it undercuts the narrative.
- "Going Fast" urgency in the subject line has no inside-the-email support — no countdown, no low-stock flag, nothing.
- Hero image is a lifestyle family shot, not a specific product — misses the chance to anchor the 25%-off patio offer on something you'd actually click to buy.
- Deals of the Day signup module is mid-email, interrupting the conversion flow.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Collapse to one hero offer.** Pick the 25%-off patio furniture pod, feature a specific product image with a price, and put a 48-hour countdown timer under it. The May 25 email proved timers convert.
- 2. **Differentiate the CTAs.** "Shop Patio Furniture," "Shop Grills," "Shop Outdoor Lights" — each one tells the reader exactly where they land. Generic "Shop now" costs clicks.
- 3. **Kill the appliance crosssell banner.** It's a Memorial Day remnant and breaks the summer-backyard story. Move appliance promotions to a dedicated send.
- 4. **Move Deals of the Day to the footer.** It's a list-growth module, not a conversion unit — it should never sit in the middle of an offer email.
- 5. **Add a specific deadline.** "Ends June 4" is more compelling than "Going Fast" with nothing to back it up.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 25% off patio & grills — ends Thursday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your backyard, on sale: up to 25% off this week`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Patio furniture, grills, and solar lights — all discounted through June 4`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `25% off patio sets + 20% off grills — shop the summer sale now`

### 1. Technical Summary

Standard XHTML 1.0 Transitional email delivered via Lowe's ESP infrastructure (`e.lowes.com` / `sli.lowes.com`). QA flags three compliance header absences (likely AgentMail relay artifacts) and 7 images without alt text; two source-level defects — a malformed DOCTYPE and a CDN version split on DIN Next fonts — are confirmed in the HTML itself.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**20 click-tracking links not probed** — all route through an ESP redirect domain. Destination URLs and UTM parameter integrity are unverifiable from this source. Spot-check required (see §7).

**5 impression pixels embed recipient email in plain-text query parameter:**
```
https://sli.lowes.com/imp?s=1090838&e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app&p=612026...
```
Standard ESP behavior; confirms open/impression tracking is persona-email-aware and correctly resolved.

**Duplicate send ID `s=1090834`** appears in two pixels with differing `stpe` values (`default`) and `li_coord` variants (`d`, `m`). This is consistent with desktop/mobile split impression tracking — expected, but confirm deduplication logic is intentional and not a copy-paste error in the send template.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**DOCTYPE malformed — missing space (source defect):**
```html
<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN"
```
Should be `PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD`. Most parsers tolerate this, but it is a source-level defect that could trigger quirks mode in stricter renderers.

**DIN Next fonts load from two different CDN scaffold versions:**
- `DIN Next` → `scaffold/1.4.4/fonts/din/din-next.woff`
- `DIN Next Bold` / `DIN Next Black` → `scaffold/1.2.0/fonts/din/din-next-bold.woff`, `din-next-black.woff`

Version mismatch risks inconsistent glyph metrics if the font binaries differ between versions. Should be normalized to a single scaffold version.

**Duplicate `local()` hints in all Fellix @font-face declarations:**
```css
src: local('Fellix Regular'), local('Fellix Regular'), url(...)
```
Redundant but harmless. Minor source hygiene issue.

**Overlapping media queries — 375–480px range hits both breakpoints:**
- Query 1: `(min-width: 375px) and (max-width: 600px) and (-webkit-min-device-pixel-ratio:0)`
- Query 2: `only screen and (max-width: 480px)`

On a 430px WebKit device (iPhone 14/15 default viewport), both fire. Rule ordering determines the winner. Verify no layout conflicts at current iPhone viewport widths.

**7 images missing alt text (QA confirmed):**

| Image | Type | Issue |
|---|---|---|
| `c301e24e-...jpg` | Product/hero image | Needs descriptive alt text |
| `ad569dc5-...png` | Decorative or badge | Needs `alt=""` if purely decorative |
| 5× `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixels | Tracking pixels | Must carry `alt=""` (empty string) — missing `alt` causes screen readers to announce the `src` URL |

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header absent** (QA WARN): RFC 2369 compliance unverifiable at this layer. QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay not forwarding the header — confirm by inspecting raw MTA-level headers before treating as a sender-side gap.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent** (QA WARN): RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe not confirmed. Gmail and Apple Mail require this for bulk senders exceeding 5,000 messages/day. If the header is genuinely absent at the sender (not a relay artifact), this is an active deliverability risk for a sender at Lowe's volume.

**`Authentication-Results` header absent** (QA WARN): SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status not captured by relay. Sending subdomain is `e.lowes.com` — verify SPF `include:` coverage and DKIM selector alignment with the `lowes.com` DMARC policy.

**CAN-SPAM footer** not verifiable from truncated source. Lowe's is a known compliant sender; confirm physical address and unsubscribe mechanism are present in the full HTML.

All 20 click links route through an ESP redirect — no raw destination URLs are exposed in the source. Cannot confirm `utm_campaign`, `utm_medium`, or `utm_source` alignment with the "Backyard Deals" campaign from this source alone. UTM audit requires decoding redirect targets.

### 7. Recommendations

**P1 — Compliance risk:**
Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers at the MTA level. If absent at the sender (not a relay artifact), this is a bulk-sender compliance gap with Gmail and Apple Mail.

**P1 — Accessibility:**
Add descriptive `alt` text to `c301e24e-...jpg` (hero/product image) and `ad569dc5-...png`. Set `alt=""` (empty string, not omitted) on all 5 `sli.lowes.com/imp` impression pixels.

**P2 — Source defect:**
Fix DOCTYPE: `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD` (add missing space after `PUBLIC`).

**P2 — Font consistency:**
Normalize all DIN Next `@font-face` declarations to a single scaffold CDN version (either `1.4.4` or `1.2.0`).

**P3 — UTM verification:**
Decode and spot-check 2–3 click-redirect URLs to confirm UTM tagging matches the "Backyard Deals" campaign taxonomy.

**P3 — Media query audit:**
