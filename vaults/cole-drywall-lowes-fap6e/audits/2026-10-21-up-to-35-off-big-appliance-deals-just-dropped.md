# Up to 35% OFF! Big appliance deals just dropped 🚨
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a dense Lowe's appliance send that reruns the Oct 12 "ALL-IN Appliance Offer" almost as is. The hero is strong: a big "Get 35% off select major appliances" over a blue field with a stainless suite. Members get free delivery, free install and free haul-away on $2,500 or more. The eye lands on the offer first, and the "Shop now" button is clear.
- The email then turns into a catalog. Below the hero come GE bundles, a 30% interior-home tier table, fire safety, HomeCare+, a "Curated with you" row (a skeleton, a Halloween bucket, a utility knife), a sponsored doors ad and a Deals of the Day banner. For a drywall-and-home-reno persona, the appliance hero is only a partial fit. The 30% "select interior home essentials" block is a better fit but sits mid-email. The subject says 35% and "just dropped", but the offer is a week-old repeat, so the urgency is partly manufactured. Overall it is competent but cluttered.

## What's working

- The hero states one clear offer, "35% off select major appliances", with a visible "Shop now" button.
- The member stack (free delivery, free install, free haul-away, 2-year protection plan) gives concrete loyalty value.
- The "Save up to $300" and "$100" tiles show real dollar amounts for kitchen bundles and laundry pairs.
- The "ENDS TODAY" banner on the 30% interior block adds credible urgency.
- The spend-more-save-more table (10% / 20% / 30%) is easy to scan.

## What's weak

- Too many offers compete: 35%, 30%, 30% fire safety, $99 HomeCare+, 15% doors, plus the Deals of the Day banner. Nothing is the single focus after the hero.
- The "Curated with you" row looks random. A Halloween skeleton, a bucket and a utility knife have no price or offer on them.
- The sponsored doors ad (15% off, ends 10/28) breaks the brand flow and takes space from Lowe's own offers.
- The legal footer is a huge wall of fine print. The $2,500 threshold and "select GE" limits in the hero are only partly visible.
- It repeats the Oct 12 appliance creative nearly unchanged, and the subject's "just dropped" overstates how new it is. This is the second appliance send in ten days, after "Deals incoming: Up to 35% OFF select appliances" on Oct 12.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body to the hero plus one secondary offer. Move the "ENDS TODAY" 30% interior block directly under the hero, since it carries the real deadline.
- 2. Drop the "Curated with you" row or add prices and a reason to buy. Remove the sponsored doors ad from promo sends.
- 3. State the qualifying spend ("$2,500+") in the hero headline area, not only below it. Link the member perks to the specific products.
- 4. Refresh the creative so it doesn't read as a rerun of the Oct 12 send.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends today: 35% off GE appliances + free delivery`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 30% off home essentials, 35% off appliances`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Members get free delivery, install and haul-away on $2,500+ GE appliance orders.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Spend $7,500 and save 30% on select home essentials. Today only.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is structurally sound for a marketing email. The confirmed issues are 18 images with no `alt` attribute and the recipient's email address sitting in plain text in 16 third-party pixel URLs. The compliance and authentication warnings can't be confirmed: the QA harness reads from the AgentMail relay, which may strip those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **38 links were not probed.** The QA run skipped them as tracking/click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and final landing URLs are unverified.
- **Pixel URLs expose the recipient address.** 16 impression pixels on `sli.lowes.com/imp` carry the address in the query string, for example `https://sli.lowes.com/imp?s=127768300&e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app&p=527566&lctg=410146703&stpe=pixel`.
  - The address is not hashed, so it can end up in proxy, CDN and image-cache logs.
  - Image-proxying clients such as Gmail rewrite these URLs, so the address is visible to the proxy.
- **Duplicate impressions.** The `s=2019286` and `s=1090834` pixels each appear twice (`stpe=default&li_coord…`). If both fire, impressions are double-counted. The truncated output doesn't show whether their `li_coord` values differ.
- **Truncated source.** The HTML was cut off, so I can't audit the body links, button markup or footer links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images (2).** These can't be confirmed as decorative, so they likely need descriptive alt text. If they carry offer text, that text is invisible when images are blocked.
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg`
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png`
- **Missing alt text on tracking pixels (16).** The `sli.lowes.com/imp` images should carry `alt=""`. Without it, some screen readers announce the file name or URL. Confirm they also have `width="1" height="1"` and are hidden from layout.
- **Malformed DOCTYPE.** It reads `PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN"`, with no space after `PUBLIC`. Most clients tolerate this, but strict parsers may not.
- **Conditional comments and fonts.** The `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` wrapping for `@font-face` is correct, and the web fonts have fallbacks via `local()`. I can't check the font-family fallback stacks in the truncated body.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source.
- The recipient address is resolved correctly in every pixel URL (`e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app`). The privacy concern is covered in section 2.
- Body personalization can't be assessed because the source is truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found [WARN].** This is unconfirmed. The QA note says the relay may not capture it. Lowe's is a major sender, so the header is probably present at origin. Verify against the raw message.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found [WARN].** Same caveat. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe, so verify this on the raw message too.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found [WARN].** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `e.lowes.com` are unknown. This is a relay limitation, not evidence of failure.
- **In-body unsubscribe and postal address.** Not visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The 38 click-tracked links were skipped, so the final URLs, UTM parameters and landing-page status are unknown. The sender uses redirect-based tracking, so UTMs are probably applied after the redirect. This needs a redirect-follow check.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run the QA against the raw RFC 822 message (not the relay-parsed copy) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Until then, treat the 57% pass rate as understated.
