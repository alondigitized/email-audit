# 🚨Ends TOMORROW: Extra Big Deals
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-14**
## Executive summary

- This is the finale of Lowe's declared "10 Days of Deals" Father's Day campaign — Day 9 or 10 depending on when you count the June 11 soft launch. The urgency is legitimate and the calendar widget showing June 14 earns it. What the email loses relative to the June 13 member-spatula send is focus: that email had one clean hook (free gift, members only). This one throws a free battery, a $1,149 fridge, three grid items, a "G.O.A.T." countdown, and a Deals of the Day signup pitch at you in a single scroll. A lot of product for a "last chance" send.
- The countdown timer rendering as "00 00 00" in the "10 Days of Dad Deals" section is the single biggest liability. Whether it's a static placeholder or an actually-expired live timer, it reads as broken — and a dead clock in a urgency-first email is an own goal.
- The hero holds up well — clean calendar date, "end tomorrow" copy, visible "Shop now" CTA — but the subject line ("Extra Big Deals") wastes the finale moment. You're on Day 9 or 10 of a campaign you've trained subscribers to track. Lead with the product, not the adjective.

## What's working

- Calendar widget anchoring the June 14 date makes "ends tomorrow" feel factual, not manufactured.
- Three concrete price points in the secondary grid ($678, FREE, $478) give subscribers something to scan quickly.
- The "Deals of the Day" subscription pitch at the footer is smart list-building — right moment, right audience (engaged campaign readers).
- Father's Day campaign thread is maintained cleanly from the June 12 send through here; subscriber knows what story they're inside.

## What's weak

- Countdown timer shows "00 00 00" — reads as expired or broken, which undercuts the urgency the entire email is built on.
- "The G.O.A.T. is almost g.o.n.e." section is the vaguest block in the email: a person, some copy, a Buy now button, no named product. If this is a hero SKU, earn the space.
- No MyLowe's Rewards hook — the June 13 send got a 9.00 partly because it made membership feel like an exclusive. Nothing here.
- Free battery is conditional on purchasing a charger kit; the qualifier is visible but small. Easy for a skim-reader to miss and feel misled.
- Subject drops the category and the "10 Days" frame entirely — a long-time subscriber doesn't know if this is Day 1 or the finale.

## Recommendations

- **1. Name a product (or the campaign day) in the subject.** "Extra Big Deals" is the weakest possible headline for a campaign closer. "Day 10: Fridge, free battery, and it's over" is better than anything with "Extra Big" in it.
- **2. Fix or cut the "00 00 00" countdown.** If the timer can't render live in static email, replace it with a static deadline callout ("Ends midnight June 14").
- **3. Give the G.O.A.T. section a product name.** If you're building suspense around a specific SKU, name it and price it — don't make readers click to find out what they're buying.
- **4. Re-surface the member benefit.** The June 13 email used the free spatula as a member hook. This finale is the last chance to convert non-members before Father's Day passes. A single line — "MyLowe's members: check your exclusive finale offer" — does the job.
- **5. Drop the 🚨 emoji or swap it.** Alarm siren reads as spam-adjacent; 🛠️ or 🎁 carries the same urgency with less risk.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Day 10 of 10: Fridge deal + free Craftsman battery`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day — $1,149 fridge, free battery, and more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free 9-Ah battery with any V20 charger. Ends tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `10 Days of Deals closes at midnight — grab the finale.`

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is structurally standard table-based HTML with no critical rendering blockers, but it has meaningful accessibility gaps (missing alt text on 8 images, including tracking pixels) and unresolved authentication/compliance header visibility through the AgentMail relay.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **21 tracking links skipped** — All click-redirect links route through Lowe's ESP redirect domain; HTTP probing was skipped, so broken-link status is unverifiable from this audit. No dead links confirmed, but no live-link confirmation either.
- **Impression pixels exposed** — 5 `sli.lowes.com/imp?` tracking beacons embed the recipient address in plaintext in the URL query string (`e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app`). This is standard ESP practice but means the email address is transmitted in GET request logs on any intermediate proxy.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Missing alt text — 8 images:**

| Image | src (truncated) |
|---|---|
| Hero JPG | `image.e.lowes.com/…/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg` |
| Content PNG | `image.e.lowes.com/…/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png` |
| Open-pixel GIF | `mi.lowes.com/p/cp/1161d2b90543045e/o.gif` |
| 5× impression pixels | `sli.lowes.com/imp?s=109083{4,4,5,7,8}&…` |

The tracking pixel `alt=""` omissions are cosmetically harmless but the hero and content images without alt text fail WCAG 2.1 SC 1.1.1 and will render blank in image-blocked clients (common in corporate Outlook).

**Responsive CSS:**
- Two overlapping media queries: `@media screen and (min-width: 375px) and (max-width: 600px)` and `@media only screen and (max-width: 480px)`. The 375–480px band is covered by both, with potential rule conflicts on `.container`, `.drop`, and `.photo` selectors. No confirmed rendering break, but the overlap is fragile.
- `.mobilehide { display: none; color: #fff; }` — the color rule on a hidden element is dead CSS; harmless but indicates copy-paste template debt.

**Font loading:**
- Fellix and DIN Next families are loaded from `lowescdn.com` via `@font-face`. Both `@font-face` blocks are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!--> … <!--<![endif]-->` which correctly suppresses them in Outlook (MSO renders fallback fonts). No issue.

### 7. Recommendations

**High priority:**
1. **Fix pipeline header capture** — Configure AgentMail relay to preserve `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` headers. Current 50% pass rate is artificially deflated by this instrumentation gap.
2. **Add alt text to hero and content images** — `c301e24e…jpg` and `ad569dc5…png` need descriptive alt attributes for accessibility compliance and image-blocked rendering.

**Medium priority:**
3. **Resolve overlapping media queries** — Consolidate the 375–600px and 0–480px breakpoints into a single consistent ruleset to eliminate the 375–480px conflict zone.

**Low priority:**
5. **Suppress tracking pixel alt warnings** — Add `alt=""` and `role="presentation"` explicitly to all `sli.lowes.com/imp` and `mi.lowes.com` pixel images to silence accessibility scanners cleanly.
6. **Clean dead CSS** — Remove `color: #fff` from `.mobilehide` and audit for other unreachable rules.
