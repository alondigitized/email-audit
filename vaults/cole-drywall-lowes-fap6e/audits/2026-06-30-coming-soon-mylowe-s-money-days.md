# 💰COMING SOON: MyLowe's Money Days!💰
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-30**
## Executive summary

- This is a loyalty-event teaser from MyLowe's Rewards announcing "MyLowe's Money Days" running July 9–12, positioned as a first-ever member perk: up to $30 in extra MyLowe's Money on a $200+ purchase. The visual execution is clean and on-brand — bold blue background, scattered card graphics, clear date stamp, and a legible offer line. It reads as a program launch, not a product promo, which differentiates it from the wave of July 4th deal emails Lowe's sent this past week.
- The central tension is the "coming soon" framing paired with a "Shop now" CTA. You can't actually earn the bonus for nine more days, so "Shop now" misleads slightly — it pushes people toward the store before the window is open. That's a friction point that will cost clicks.
- Overall this is a well-produced loyalty announcement that earns its send. It's better than the June 28 mini-bucket collectible email (4/10) and closer to the June 29 free-beach-bucket send (9/10) in terms of clarity and member relevance, though this one's a teaser rather than an immediately actionable offer.

## What's working

- **"For the first time ever"** is a strong novelty hook — it signals something new and exclusive, not just another deal.
- **Dates are front and center** — "July 9–12" is in the hero, above the fold, so the time frame is immediately clear.
- **Offer is specific and quantified** — "$30 in extra MyLowe's Money with a $200+ purchase" is a concrete value prop, not vague savings language.
- **Visual hierarchy is clean** — the blue hero card-stack graphic, date, and offer copy all land in the right order; nothing competes with the main message.
- **Preheader ("Like MyLowe's Rewards? We're giving you MORE!")** avoids repeating the subject line and extends the excitement angle.

## What's weak

- **"Shop now" CTA is wrong for a coming-soon email** — the offer isn't live for nine days; clicking "Shop now" is a dead-end or at best a preview page, neither of which earns the click.
- **Subject line uses ALL CAPS ("COMING SOON")** — raises mild spam-filter and subscriber-fatigue flags; the double 💰 emoji already signals excitement without the shout.
- **No indication of what to shop or plan for** — "Start planning your cart!" section has no featured products or categories, making the planning hook hollow.
- **No urgency lever in the body copy** — the July 9–12 window should have a countdown or a "mark your calendar" CTA rather than a generic shop prompt.
- **Seventh Lowe's email in roughly five days** — cadence fatigue is real; even a differentiated send risks getting skipped by members who are numbed by the recent holiday-sale flood.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Replace "Shop now" with a date-appropriate CTA** — "Save the date" or "Add to calendar" or "See what's on sale" makes the teaser honest and sets expectations correctly. This single change removes the biggest friction in the email.
- 2. **Move specific dates into the subject line** — "💰 MyLowe's Money Days: July 9–12" is under 50 chars, eliminates ALL CAPS, and gives the subscriber a concrete peg to remember.
- 3. **Add at least one featured category or deal preview** — a row of 3 product tiles (appliances, tools, outdoor) with teaser pricing would turn the "plan your cart" copy into something actionable.
- 4. **Reduce subject emoji to one** — double 💰 reads as promotional noise; a single one signals excitement without stacking.
- **Subject Alt A:** `💰 MyLowe's Money Days: July 9–12`
- **Subject Alt B:** `First-ever: Earn $30 in MyLowe's Money this weekend`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Members earn up to $30 extra on a $200 purchase — mark your calendar`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New loyalty event: for the first time ever, your rewards multiply`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Missing alt text (confirmed):**
- `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/1/a22207c1-63c7-47c0-9e00-61227828e1fe.jpg` — no `alt` attribute present. For a promotional email where this image likely carries offer copy ("Money Days"), a blank alt means screen reader users and image-blocked clients (Outlook default, corporate proxies) lose that content entirely.

**Font loading:** Four `@font-face` declarations (Fellix family + DIN Next) load `.woff` only — no `.woff2` fallback. `.woff2` is now universally supported and significantly smaller. Not a breaking issue but increases load time in webmail clients that render fonts.

**Duplicate `@font-face` blocks:** Fellix fonts are declared inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`; DIN Next is declared in a *separate* identical conditional block immediately after. This is redundant — both blocks use the same MSO conditional and could be merged. Minor, but adds parse overhead.

**Media query overlap:** Two breakpoint blocks target different width ranges (`375px–600px` and `max-width: 480px`) with overlapping `.drop`, `.container`, and `.no-padding` rules. On devices 375–480px wide, both blocks fire and the second overrides the first. Likely intentional but worth confirming no rule conflicts exist in the full source.

**Hotmail/ExternalClass hacks:** `line-height: 100%` applied to `.ExternalClass *` — this is a legacy Hotmail fix that predates Outlook.com's modern renderer. It's harmless but unnecessary for current Outlook.com.

### 5. Compliance

**CAN-SPAM — physical address: WARN**
No physical mailing address detected in the QA scan. CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid physical postal address in every commercial email. If the address is in the footer (outside the truncated HTML provided), this warning is a false positive — but it should be confirmed against the full rendered template.

**List-Unsubscribe: WARN**
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not found
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found (RFC 8058 one-click)

These are likely present on the actual ESP-sent message but were not captured through the AgentMail relay. Gmail and Yahoo Mail (as of Feb 2024 bulk sender requirements) require `List-Unsubscribe-Post` for one-click unsubscribe. Lowe's volume almost certainly qualifies as a bulk sender — confirm with ESP that both headers are injected at send time, not just an unsubscribe link in body copy.

**Authentication: WARN**
`Authentication-Results` header not present in relayed copy — SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status unknown. For a domain sending at Lowe's volume, `lowes@e.lowes.com` should show DKIM pass on the `e.lowes.com` subdomain aligned to DMARC policy on `lowes.com`. Must be verified against a live received header, not this relay capture.
