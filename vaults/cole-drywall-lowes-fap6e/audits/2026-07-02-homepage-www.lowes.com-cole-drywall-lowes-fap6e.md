# Homepage snapshot · www.lowes.com · 2026-07-02
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-02**
## Executive summary

- First thing I see when I land on Lowe's is a sign-in pop-up blocking the entire page — and I already have an Advantage card. Feels like getting carded at a store you've shopped at for ten years. Past the modal, they're running a July 4th deals push with same-day delivery on orders over $25, which is actually useful when you're mid-basement and realize you need more compound at 7pm.

## What's working

- **July 4th same-day delivery hook:** "Plus, get free same-day delivery on select items" on orders $25+ is legitimately useful when I'm mid-project and need something tonight — that's the one thing that would stop me from going to Home Depot instead
- **Silver Key free shipping reminder in the modal:** Annoying placement, but I genuinely forget I have that status — the reminder has real dollar value for me
- **Clean "Shop Now" CTA on the hero:** No ambiguity, button is findable, color contrast is good on the dark navy background
- **Search bar stays accessible:** Even with the modal up, the search bar is visible above it — I'd close the pop-up and type "drywall screws" and be on my way

## What's weak

- **Sign-in modal blocking the entire homepage on arrival:** I'm a card holder with a MyLowe's account — this is not a welcome, it's an obstacle. Should know I've been here before from a cookie
- **Recommended chips: "Electric Range, Washing Machine, Bathroom Vanity":** Nothing tools-adjacent, nothing building-materials-adjacent. If I'm mid-project, these are three categories I'm not shopping today
- **Hero text overflow on mobile:** "mmer's hottest deals are h..." — "Summer's hottest deals are here" is clipping on both sides. For a national retailer this is sloppy and breaks trust
- **Zero prices above the fold:** July 4th deals are teased but there's not a single dollar figure or percent-off anywhere I can see — I don't know if this sale is worth my time
- **"Extra Big Deals" heading at bottom:** Visible but completely empty — a label with no product, no price, no image. Decorative at best, lazy at worst

## Recommendations

- **Kill the blocking sign-in modal for cookied/repeat visitors** — replace with a non-blocking top banner ("Hey Cole, your Advantage Card is ready") that doesn't eat the homepage
- **Swap the Recommended chips to project-mode categories:** "Drywall & Sheetrock," "Kobalt Tools," "Fasteners & Hardware" would actually mirror why someone like me shows up at Lowe's in the first place
- **Fix the hero text overflow on mobile:** "Summer's hottest deals are here" should never clip — basic responsive CSS test that should catch this before it ships
- **Put one specific deal number on the July 4th hero:** Even "Up to 30% off power tools" gives me a reason to tap Shop Now instead of going straight to search
