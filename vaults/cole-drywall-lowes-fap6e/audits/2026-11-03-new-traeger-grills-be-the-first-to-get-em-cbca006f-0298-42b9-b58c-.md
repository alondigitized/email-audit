# NEW Traeger grills? Be the first to get 'em 🔥
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-03**
## Executive summary

- This is a launch email for Traeger grills, now sold at Lowe's, sent as an early Black Friday teaser. The hero is strong: a big Traeger smoker photo, "Member Only Access," a "New!" badge and a deadline of 11/4. The 3x points band and the Black Friday banner stack well under it. The hierarchy is clean and nothing is broken.
- The problem is the audience. This inbox belongs to a drywall contractor, and a pellet grill is a lifestyle purchase with no link to the job. There is also no dollar or percent offer. The only incentive is 3x points on an eligible Traeger purchase, with exclusions in small print. The send also tries to be a product launch, a points promo and a Black Friday teaser at once.
- Judgement: a polished send that hits the right members, but it's wrong for this persona and has a thin offer. It's a solid brand-level email and a weak targeted one.

## What's working

- The hero photo and the "Member Only Access" lock badge make the exclusivity clear.
- "Member access ends on 11/4" gives a credible, near-term deadline. The preheader reinforces it with "ends tomorrow."
- The 3x Points hexagon is easy to read, and "Activate now" tells the member what to do.
- The Black Friday banner is separated from the Traeger content, so it works as a second hook.
- The layout is clean and renders without broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- Grills are off-category for a drywall contractor. Nothing here speaks to tools, materials or job-site needs.
- There is no price, percent off or dollar figure anywhere. "3x points" is abstract, and the "subject to exclusions" fine print undercuts it.
- Three CTAs ("Shop now," "Activate now," "Shop now") compete, and two of them say the same generic thing.
- The Black Friday banner has no offer. It is the third teaser-without-a-number in a row, after the 10/29 "BIG savings" send.
- The cadence is heavy. This is another near-daily send after the 11/2 Christmas trees and 11/1 sweepstakes emails.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a concrete hook, such as a starting price on a hero Traeger model or a points-to-dollars value ("3x points = ~$X back on a $999 grill"). That would do the most to lift clicks.
- 2. Segment the send. Pro and contractor members should get Pro-relevant Black Friday deals like power tools and building materials, not grills.
- 3. Cut to one primary CTA and make it specific, for example "Shop Traeger grills" or "Get 3x points."
- 4. Move the 11/4 deadline into the hero headline, not a small line under the copy.
- 5. Give the Black Friday banner a real teaser, such as "Up to $X off tools," or drop it.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Members-only: Traeger grills, 3x points 🔥`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your early access ends 11/4. Plus, Black Friday savings start now.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Earn 3x points on eligible Traeger grills before access closes tomorrow.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML has a malformed DOCTYPE and one image with no alt text. The compliance and authentication warnings come from a relay that may not capture headers, so they are unconfirmed. The source was truncated, so footer, link and UTM checks are limited to what the QA data reports.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 14 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Their destinations and status codes are unverified, so this isn't a confirmed defect.
- The plain-text part is 3604/4677 characters URLs (about 77%, over the 70% threshold). This points to long tracked URLs in the text alternative. It is a mild spam-filter and readability risk.
- No broken links were confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/1/a22207c1-63c7-47c0-9e00-61227828e1fe.jpg` has no `alt` attribute. Screen readers will announce the filename, and image-blocking clients will show nothing.
- **Malformed DOCTYPE:** the source has `PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN"` with no space after `PUBLIC`. The DTD URL and the `xmlns` namespace also use `https://www.w3.org/...`. The XHTML namespace identifier is defined as `http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml`. Most clients tolerate this, but a strict parser might not, so it is worth fixing.
- Custom web fonts (`Fellix`, `DIN Next`) load from `www.lowescdn.com` inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` wrappers. This is handled correctly, so no issue.
- The mobile media queries are present: `max-width: 480px`, and `min-width: 375px` and `max-width: 600px`. No issue found.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source contains no unresolved merge tokens such as `%%...%%` or `{{...}}`. Because the HTML was truncated, I could not check the full body.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both are reported as not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check the raw headers at the origin before treating this as a defect.
- **Physical address:** the check did not detect one. CAN-SPAM requires it. The source was truncated and the footer may not have been scanned, so this is unconfirmed.
- **Authentication-Results:** the header is absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `lowes@e.lowes.com`. The relay is the expected cause, not a sender fault.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The 14 tracking links were not followed, and the truncated source shows no destination URLs or UTM parameters. No issues are confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a meaningful `alt` attribute to `a22207c1-63c7-47c0-9e00-61227828e1fe.jpg`. Use `alt=""` if the image is decorative.
2. Correct the DOCTYPE to `PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN"` and use `http://` for the `xmlns` namespace.
3. Shorten the tracked URLs in the plain-text part, or reduce the number of links, to lower the URL ratio below 70%.
4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` in the raw headers captured before the relay. Also confirm a physical address in the full footer. If either is missing, fix it.
5. Capture the full HTML and resolve the tracking redirects in a follow-up run, so the final URLs and UTM parameters can be checked.
