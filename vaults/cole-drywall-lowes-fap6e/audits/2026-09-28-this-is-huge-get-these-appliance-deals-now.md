# This is HUGE⚡️Get these appliance deals now 😎
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- This is Lowe's strongest appliance-specific send in the recent run — the "All-In Appliance Offer" hero delivers a real, hard-numbered 35% off with a clean product-family shot (fridge, range, stackable washer/dryer, microwave) and then stacks it with a genuine wall of member perks: free delivery, free install, free haul-away, and a 2-year protection plan. That's more concrete value stacked in one hero than 9/23's "FREE tool" send or 9/21's LG appliance send. The three-product carousel below (microwave, dishwasher, stacked laundry) with individual "Buy now" buttons is a smart touch — it gives browsers a direct path to a specific SKU instead of dumping everyone on a category page.
- Where it falls apart is the subject line and the mid-email derailment. "This is HUGE⚡️Get these appliance deals now 😎" is generic hype-copy with two emoji that don't match Lowe's plainer recent subjects (35% off LG, 65% off Ring/Blink) — it undersells a genuinely well-stacked offer. And directly under the appliance carousel, the email pivots hard into a holiday/Rudolph collection banner, which is a jarring category and seasonal whiplash right when the reader's attention is on appliances.
- Net: strong hero, real offer depth, decent product-level CTAs — dragged down by weak subject packaging and a hard left turn into holiday decor mid-scroll.

## What's working

- Hero offer is unambiguous: "Get up to 35% off select major appliances" in huge type, unmissable above the fold.
- Real member-benefit stacking is visible and legible: Free Delivery, Free Install, Free Haul-away, 2-Year Protection Plan, plus $600/$100 additional bundle savings — more depth than most recent Lowe's sends.
- Three specific products with individual images, names, and "Buy now" buttons give a direct path past the generic "Shop now" hero CTA.
- Clean nav bar and consistent brand blue/red Lowe's styling throughout — no render bugs.

## What's weak

- Subject line ("This is HUGE⚡️...😎") is vague hype-speak that doesn't name the 35% number or "appliances" clearly, undermining a genuinely strong offer.
- The holiday/Rudolph collection banner right after the appliance section is a jarring category and tone break — no transition, breaks the "appliance shopper" flow.
- Preview text ("You get up to 35% OFF and more savings. Plus, something for the holidays.") buries the holiday teaser in a way that makes the subject/preview combo feel scattered rather than focused.
- The three featured products have truncated names cut off mid-word ("Whirlpool 1.7-cu ft Over-the-Range Microwave (", "Bosch 300 Series 24-in Front Control Built-in Dishwasher ( Stainless") — visibly broken copy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the truncated product names first — "Microwave (" and "Dishwasher ( Stainless" cut off mid-parenthesis look broken and erode trust in a $2,500+ purchase decision.
- 2. Rewrite the subject to lead with the number, matching the brand's better recent sends (9/21's LG 35% email, 9/23's FREE tool email).
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 35% off major appliances + free delivery & install`
- **Subject Alt B:** `35% off appliances. Plus free delivery, install & haul-away.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `LG bundles save up to $600 more — before 10/7.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free delivery, install & haul-away on qualifying orders.`
- 3. Either cut the holiday/Rudolph banner from this send entirely or move it to the very bottom below Deals of the Day — an appliance shopper mid-consideration doesn't need a decor detour breaking momentum toward checkout.
- 4. Add a visible deadline in the hero itself (footer fine print says "Valid 9/17/26 – 10/7/26" but nothing above the fold signals urgency) — a small "Ends 10/7" badge near the 35% callout would lift urgency without new copy work.
