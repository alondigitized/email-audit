# Thanksgiving Day is serving up to 50% OFF online🔥
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is a Black Friday kitchen-sink send that happens to land on Thanksgiving. The hero is the strongest part. A big white "Get up to 50% OFF" on black, with a "Thanksgiving Day deals" tagline, matches the subject. It's also the first time in the recent run that the subject and the first screen agree. The Nov 25 ladder send and the Nov 22 battery-kit send both opened with a generic banner and made the reader dig for the promised offer.
- After the hero, the email sprawls. It stacks a members' early-access block, an in-store prize event, power tools, smart home, appliances, an advent calendar, a Forno range and a Deals of the Day banner. Eight or nine CTAs compete with each other. The "up to 50% OFF" in the hero never says what it applies to. The only 50% anchor is appliances, which sits about two-thirds of the way down. "Online only" and "Today only" are credible urgency, but the Nov 21 "150 members win BIG" send already ran the in-store prize idea. Repeating it here is a rerun, and it competes with the online-offer theme.
- Judgement: a well-timed, clear-headed hero on top of an overstuffed body. It's good for a holiday blast and below the Nov 23 appliance send, where the subject and a single hero lined up.

## What's working

- The hero delivers the subject. "Get up to 50% OFF" with "Thanksgiving Day deals" is large and high-contrast, and the eye lands on it first.
- The "ONLINE ONLY / TODAY ONLY" ribbon and the "Doors open at 6 a.m. on Black Friday" line give credible, date-specific urgency.
- The members' early-access block ("Members get online-only early access to Doorbuster Deals") surfaces loyalty value above the fold.
- The product cards use concrete numbers: "Save up to $150" on power tools, "up to 55% off" smart home, "$19.99, save $29" on the MrBeast Advent Calendar.
- The black, red and white palette is consistent with Lowe's Black Friday voice, and the layout renders cleanly with no broken images or overlaps.

## What's weak

- The hero's 50% has no product category. "Up to 50% off" what? The reader can't tell until the appliance block far below.
- Too many CTAs and themes. Early access, an in-store event, tools, smart home, appliances, an advent calendar, a range and Deals of the Day all fight for attention, and the single clearest offer gets diluted.
- The in-store "Win BIG on Black Friday" block repeats the Nov 21 send, and it contradicts the "online only" hero.
- Every button says "Shop now," except two ("See what's up for grabs" and "Explore more"), so the copy gives no signal about where each one goes.
- The Forno range ad is tagged "Sponsored" and sits in the middle of a promo about Thanksgiving savings, which feels off-theme. The footer fine print is a dense wall of exclusions, which undercuts the "honest offer" feel.

## Recommendations

- 1. Name the category in the hero: "Up to 50% OFF major appliances, today only." Better still, make the appliance block the hero. It already carries 50% off, 25% off bundles and free delivery, which was the strongest offer in the Nov 23 send.
- 2. Cut the body to three blocks: the hero, the doorbuster picks and one secondary offer. Move the in-store prize event to a separate send or a footer strip, and drop the Forno ad.
- 3. Write CTA copy with a verb and a noun: "Shop appliance deals," "Shop power tool deals" and "Shop smart home deals."
- 4. Pair the 6 a.m. Black Friday line with a live countdown or end-time so the urgency is specific.
- 5. Add review stars or "top rated" tags on the product cards for social proof.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Today only: up to 50% OFF appliances + free delivery`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Thanksgiving doorbusters: tools from $150 off 🦃`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Online only through midnight. Members get first pick of Doorbuster Deals.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Appliances, power tools and smart home, all marked down. Doors open 6 a.m. Friday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout. Automated QA reported 0 hard failures and 3 warnings (57% pass rate). The HTML source was truncated, so findings are limited to the visible head and the QA data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **32 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped the HTTP probe for them because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Duplicate impression pixels.** `https://sli.lowes.com/imp?s=127768300` through `s=127768304` (`p=531907`, `stpe=pixel`) each appear twice, 10 requests in total. Duplicate `s=1090834` and `s=2019286` pixels (`stpe=default&li_coord…`) also appear. This could double-count impressions. It may be a deliberate mobile/desktop split, which the truncated source can't confirm.
- **Several pixel URLs are cut off** at `&li_coord` in the QA output, so their full parameters can't be assessed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt attributes on content images:**
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg`
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png`

  If these carry content, screen readers and image-blocked clients get nothing.
- **Tracking pixels without `alt`.** About 17 `sli.lowes.com/imp` images have no `alt` attribute. They are non-visual, so they should carry `alt=""` and ideally `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Malformed DOCTYPE.** The source reads `PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN"`, with no whitespace after `PUBLIC`. Most clients tolerate it, but it is invalid.
- **`@font-face` webfonts** (Fellix, DIN Next, loaded from `lowescdn.com`) are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, which is correct. Fallback font stacks couldn't be checked in the truncated source.
- **Conflicting media queries.** `@media screen and (min-width:375px) and (max-width:600px)` and `@media only screen and (max-width:480px)` both define `.container`, `.photo` and `.drop`. The overlap is a cascade risk but not a confirmed defect.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Recipient email address exposed in plaintext URLs.** Every `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixel carries `e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app` as an unhashed query parameter. This is PII in image request URLs. It will appear in proxy and CDN logs and leak through any Referer-style logging.
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) were visible in the retrieved source or QA data.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** neither header was found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them. Treat this as unverified rather than confirmed missing. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both for senders at Lowe's volume.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found. SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `e.lowes.com` is unknown, again because of the relay.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address:** not visible in the truncated source, so they can't be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues found in the data provided. The source was truncated before the link bodies, and the 32 tracking links were not resolved. UTM parameters and landing page alignment can't be assessed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run the QA against the raw `.eml`, not the relay copy, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC).
2. Resolve the 32 skipped tracking links with a redirect-following probe, and record the final URLs and UTMs.
3. Add `alt` text to `c301e24e-…jpg` and `ad569dc5-…png`, and `alt=""` to all `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixels.
