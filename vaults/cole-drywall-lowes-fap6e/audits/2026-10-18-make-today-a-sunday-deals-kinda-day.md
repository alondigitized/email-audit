# Make today a Sunday Deals kinda day 🎁
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-18**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, scannable Lowe's category roundup. The "Sunday Deals" hero is a big blue block with a single "Shop now" button. Below it is a tidy 2x2 grid of offers: 35% off appliances with next-day delivery, 40% off bathroom, 30% off tool storage, and $100 off power tools. The offers are real and the hierarchy is easy to follow.
- It also reads like a template, not a campaign. The hero says nothing specific ("Grab 'em while you can."), and the best number in the email sits below the fold. There are seven near-identical "Shop now" buttons. The "Curated with you in mind" product row has visibly truncated titles ("Project Source Commercial Large 27.0 -Gallons ( 108.0 -"). Its picks, a tote, a paper lawn bag and garden soil, are low-value and don't fit someone shopping for tools or drywall.
- For a subscriber, this is the latest in a near-daily run. Lowe's sent "Up to 50% OFF!" on 10/16, Money Days last-call on 10/14, vanities on 10/13, and appliances on 10/12. This one recycles the appliance and vanity offers in a weaker wrapper. Judgement: competent and low-risk, but a filler send, and the "Sunday Deals" brand beat is carrying too much.

## What's working

- The 2x2 offer grid pairs big "35% / 40% / 30% off" numerals with a clear product image. The eye lands on the numbers first.
- The "Next-day delivery" badge on appliances adds a concrete benefit beyond the discount.
- The tool storage and power tool tiles are the strongest fit for a trades or DIY recipient, and the "Save up to $100" callout is prominent.
- The top nav (Appliances, Daily Deals, Patio, Outdoor Tools) is clean, and the Lowe's branding and blue/red palette are consistent.
- The Deals of the Day module and "Sign up" CTA give a clear next step.

## What's weak

- The hero offers no discount, product or deadline, only "Sunday Deals" and "Grab 'em while you can." A big blue block is spent on a brand label.
- Every button says "Shop now" or "Buy now." Seven identical CTAs give the reader no reason to choose one.
- The "Curated with you in mind" product titles are cut off mid-phrase. The picks (a storage tote, paper lawn bags, garden soil) have no price, rating or deal attached.
- The urgency is vague. The real expiry ("Valid through 10/21") and the delivery cutoff ("Order by 4pm") are buried in the footer fine print.
- Offers are repeated from earlier this week (appliances on 10/12, bathroom/vanities on 10/13), so cadence fatigue is likely.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the hero with the single strongest offer, for example "Up to 40% off bathroom + 35% off appliances. Ends Tuesday." Put the real deadline in the hero, not the fine print.
- 2. Give each tile a distinct, specific CTA: "Shop tool storage," "Shop power tool deals," "Shop appliances."
- 3. Fix or remove the "Curated" row. Show products with sale price, a rating, and complete (not truncated) titles. Pick them by what the subscriber browses, such as tools, not paper lawn bags.
- 4. Cut the repeated mid-page "Fall favorites" banner and the Deals of the Day sign-up. Fewer buttons would help the main ones.
- 5. Vary the Sunday Deals concept week to week (a rotating "hero deal") so it doesn't feel like a repeat of the 10/12 and 10/13 sends.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 40% off bathroom + 35% off appliances`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Sunday only: $100 off power tools, 30% off storage`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Deals on appliances, tool storage and bath refreshes. Ends Tuesday 10/21.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Next-day delivery on select appliances, plus $100 off power tools.`
