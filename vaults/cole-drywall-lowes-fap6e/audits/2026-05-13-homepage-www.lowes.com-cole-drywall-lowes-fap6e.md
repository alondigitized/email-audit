# Homepage snapshot · www.lowes.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- First thing I see when I land on Lowe's is a sign-in pop-up eating half my screen — before I can even clock what's on sale. Once I squint past it, there's a Samsung Bespoke appliances pitch taking up the hero and a "Recommended" row with chips for bathroom vanity and tool box. It's reading like a store that wants my login more than it wants to show me deals, which is annoying when I've got a punch list and I'm just trying to find finishing supplies or check if my Kobalt stuff is on promo.

## What's working

- **Sign-in modal loyalty pitch** — stacking "$5 off + earn points + Silver Key free shipping" in one card is efficient; I already carry the Advantage card, so the points reminder lands
- **Recommended chips row** — "Tool Box" and "Bathroom Vanity" show up as chips, which tells me the algo has my purchase history and is trying to steer me right
- **Top banner deal teaser** — "Discover this week's top deals" is low-friction and I'd probably tap it to see if anything hits
- **Samsung appliance hero** — I actually do buy appliances at Lowe's over Home Depot; a new Samsung promo is at least in the right neighborhood even if the "Bespoke" angle feels bougie

## What's weak

- **Forced sign-in modal as the first interaction** — I came to browse deals, not log in; blocking my view before showing me a single product is a fast way to lose me
- **"Bespoke" hero language** — "Pro Style Design ranges" and "Zero Clearance Fit" sounds like a Williams-Sonoma pitch, not a Lowe's contractor special; doesn't match how I shop
- **No above-the-fold category nav** — the modal covers whatever nav is underneath; I can't see Lumber, Drywall, Flooring, or any of the aisles I actually need
- **No visible pricing or project-oriented content** — no "Memorial Day deals on tools," no "finish your basement" editorial, just a modal and a Samsung ad
- **Asterisk stacking on the offers** — $5 off has one asterisk, Silver Key shipping has another; feels like bait-and-switch until I read the fine print

## Recommendations

- **Kill the full-screen sign-in interstitial on first load** — or at minimum make it a bottom sheet that doesn't obscure the hero; let me see the deals first, then prompt me to sign in to unlock them
- **Swap the Bespoke hero for a Memorial Day project deals banner** — it's mid-May; lead with "Up to 40% off tools, lumber & flooring for Memorial Day weekend" — that's what I came for
- **Pin a category strip below the search bar that survives the modal** — Lumber, Drywall, Paint, Flooring, Tools — six tappable tiles I can hit before the page fully loads
- **Add a MyLowe's balance or "X points from your next reward" line for signed-in returning shoppers** — give me a reason to care about being logged in beyond $5 one-time
