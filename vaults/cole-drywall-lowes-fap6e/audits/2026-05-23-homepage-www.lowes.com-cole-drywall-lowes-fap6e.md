# Homepage snapshot · www.lowes.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- I opened Lowe's and immediately got a sign-in pop-up shoved in my face before I could see a single power tool or piece of lumber. The brand reads like it's running a real Memorial Day push — summer deals, same-day delivery — which is good timing for me since I'm mid-project. But leading with a sign-in gate instead of deals is like a contractor blocking your driveway before saying hello.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day banner and "Summer's hottest deals" headline** — the timing is dead-on. This is when I buy big-ticket stuff. The "Shop All" CTA below it is clear enough.
- **Sign-in modal loyalty pitch** — "Earn points on eligible purchases" and "FREE shipping with Silver Key status" are genuinely compelling for a MyLowe's Rewards card holder like me. I'd probably sign in.
- **Top-of-page banner copy** — "Get your home summer-ready with Memorial Day savings. Shop Now" is direct and readable. No fluff.
- **"We remember loved ones who served and sacrificed"** — I respect the Memorial Day acknowledgment. Lowe's knows its customer base. This lands.

## What's weak

- **Forced sign-in modal blocks everything** — I can't see products, categories, deals, or navigation without dismissing it first. That's friction before I've even committed to being here.
- **Recommended categories are appliance-heavy** — Electric Range, Washing Machine, partially-visible "Bathr..." — none of those are why I came to Lowe's today. No tools, no lumber, no drywall. Makes me wonder if this is personalized at all.
- **"Shop All" is the only conversion CTA I can reach** — it's too broad. I want to tap directly into building materials or tools, not scroll through the whole catalog.
- **No product imagery visible** — the entire above-the-fold experience is a modal and a text banner. Nothing to look at, nothing to make me want to click.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the forced sign-in modal on landing** — move the Silver Key pitch to a persistent sticky bar or let me see deals first, ask me to sign in second. I'm a paying customer, not a lead to capture.
- **Personalize the recommended chip row** — if I'm a logged-in MyLowe's member (or even just returning via cookie), surface Tools, Drywall, Lumber, or Power Tools in that chip rail instead of appliances.
- **Swap "Shop All" for Memorial Day-specific category CTAs** — "Shop Tools," "Shop Building Materials," "Shop Appliances" as parallel buttons so I can self-sort in one tap.
- **Put one lifestyle image of a project in progress behind the hero text** — a basement or garage shot beats the current blank navy rectangle every time for someone like me.
