# The wait ends. Black Friday deals showed up early🤩
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-29**
## Executive summary

- This is a Black Friday teaser from Lowe's with three stacked offers. The red hero says "Get these BIG savings ahead of the big day" and has no number in it. The first real offer is "Save up to $120" on power tools, followed by the Samsung "ALL-IN Appliance Offer" at up to 35% off. The module is tidy and on brand, but it is the same Samsung creative I saw on Oct 12, Oct 21 and Oct 26.
- The hero promises "Black Friday deals" and then shows no deal. Dated 10/22–11/3, the fine print confirms the appliance offer is a rerun and not a new Black Friday event. A subscriber who has seen the 35% off Samsung block three times this month will read this as recycled. The subject's "showed up early" claim is therefore not backed by anything new.
- My judgement: clean render, clear buttons, but undifferentiated. The hero is a missed chance, and the cadence is stale.

## What's working

- The layout is clean, with strong red and blue blocks and no visible render bugs or broken images.
- The nav bar (Appliances, Daily Deals, Bathroom, Outdoor Tools) gives quick category access.
- The power tools block has a concrete "$120" offer and a clear "Shop now" button.
- The Samsung block stacks member perks: free delivery, free installation, free haul away and a protection plan.
- The product row ("Curated with you in mind") has three specific products, each with its own "Buy now" button.

## What's weak

- The hero has no offer. "BIG savings" is vague, and the Black Friday headline sits over empty copy.
- The Samsung 35% off block is a repeat of the Oct 12, Oct 21 and Oct 26 sends.
- There are too many CTAs: seven "Shop now" or "Buy now" buttons plus "Explore now" and "Sign up". Nothing is the clear primary.
- The product row looks random: a battery pack, a router and a squishy dumpling toy, with no prices shown. The toy has nothing to do with the rest of the email.
- The Samsung fine print is long and dense, and the real terms (a $1,999 spend threshold, MyLowe's membership) are buried. The "FREE" perks are headline-sized but conditional.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the best real number in the hero, for example "Save up to $120 on power tools" or "Up to 35% off appliances," so the Black Friday promise has a number behind it.
- 2. Pick one lead category and cut the rest. Two or three modules would do more than five.
- 3. Show prices or savings under each product in the product row. Drop the toy, and cut the multiple "Buy now" buttons in favor of one per row.
- 4. Surface the $1,999 threshold in the Samsung block near the "FREE" perks, so the terms are honest.
- 5. Drop the Deals of the Day block or move it to the footer area.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Lowe's Black Friday email, sent from `lowes@e.lowes.com`. The visible source has a malformed DOCTYPE, two content images with no alt text, and tracking pixels that put the recipient's email address in plaintext in the URL. The compliance and authentication warnings come from AgentMail relay capture limits, so they aren't confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **PII in pixel URLs (confirmed):** every `sli.lowes.com/imp` impression pixel carries the recipient address in the query string. Example: `https://sli.lowes.com/imp?s=127768300&e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app&p=528589&lctg=410146703&stpe=pixel`. This appears in at least 16 pixels. The address is exposed to proxies, logs and referrer-style leakage. It is also unhashed, which is a privacy weakness.
- **Pixel volume:** 16 separate `imp` calls to a third-party ad-server domain (`sli.lowes.com`). That's a lot of request overhead, and any slow response delays image loading in clients that fetch them.
- **Unverified links:** 35 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so their destinations and redirect health are untested. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- No broken links were detected (0 issues).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed DOCTYPE (confirmed):** `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" ...>` has no space after `PUBLIC`. Strict parsers may fall into quirks mode. The DTD URL and `xmlns` also use `https://`, whereas the canonical namespace is `http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml`.
- **Missing alt text on content images (confirmed):**
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg`
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png`

  If these are informational, they're invisible to screen readers and blank when images are blocked.
- **Missing alt on `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixels:** these are tracking beacons, so the risk is low. They should still carry `alt=""` to avoid being announced. I can't confirm their dimensions because the source is truncated.
- **Positive:** the viewport meta, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` reset, and `@media (max-width:480px)` rules are present. Web fonts are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` conditionals.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source. The recipient address resolved correctly in all pixel URLs. The source is truncated, so the body copy can't be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both are flagged as not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture them, so I can't confirm they're absent. Verify against raw headers from a direct-to-mailbox send. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. This is a relay limitation, not evidence of failure.
- **Footer (physical address and visible unsubscribe link):** not checkable because the HTML is truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No confirmed issues. The 35 tracking links were not probed, so UTM presence and landing-page alignment are unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Hash or tokenize the recipient identifier in `sli.lowes.com/imp` URLs instead of passing the plaintext email.
2. Add descriptive alt text to `c301e24e-...jpg` and `ad569dc5-...png`, and `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
3. Fix the DOCTYPE to `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" "http://www.w3.org/TR/xhtml1/DTD/xhtml1-transitional.dtd">`, with `http://` in the `xmlns`.
4. Consolidate the 16 impression pixels where possible.
5. Re-test headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) with a direct-delivery seed to separate relay artifacts from real gaps.
6. Resolve the tracking-redirect chains in a follow-up run to check destinations and UTM carry-through.
