# Homepage snapshot · www.lowes.com · 2026-07-16
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-16**
## Executive summary

- Look, I've got a Lowe's Advantage card in my wallet and I come here probably twice a week — I don't need to be sold on signing up. The first thing I see when I open the app is a modal blocking the whole page asking me to create an account. Push that out of the way and the hero underneath is a GE Appliances summer event with 35% off fridges. I'm not looking for a refrigerator right now, I'm trying to finish my basement. Lowe's is pitching me the wrong aisle.

## What's working

- **Silver Key perk callout in the modal** — "FREE shipping with Silver Key status" is exactly the kind of thing I care about. I've got the Advantage card, so knowing that perk exists keeps me on the app.
- **"Up to 35% off Select GE Appliances"** — even though I'm not buying a fridge today, appliances are in my wheelhouse and I file this away for Memorial Day shopping.
- **"Closeout Event" banner at the very top** — clearance deals are my jam. Short copy, direct link. That's how you do it.
- **Category pills below the modal ("Electric Range", "Washing Machine", "Bathroom Vanity")** — tells me the recommendation engine at least knows I shop home improvement, not lawn and garden.

## What's weak

- **Sign-in modal fires immediately and blocks everything** — I'm a MyLowe's member. If I'm already logged in on this device, why is this the first thing I see? Feels like it doesn't recognize me, which is annoying.
- **GE Appliances hero is brand-specific in the wrong way** — "Summer Savings Event" for GE feels like a paid placement, not a Lowe's-curated deal. I trust Lowe's to show me what's on sale; I don't need to feel like I'm on the GE microsite.
- **No DIY/building materials signal above the fold** — there's zero hook for the guy who came here for drywall compound, wood screws, or Kobalt tools. The homepage completely ignores me.
- **"Shop All" is a dead-end CTA** — two "Shop All" buttons visible and neither tells me what I'm shopping for or where I'll land. That's lazy copy.

## Recommendations

- **Kill or delay the sign-in modal for recognized devices** — if I've logged in before, show me my rewards balance and recently viewed items instead. Treat me like a returning customer.
- **Swap the GE Appliances hero for a summer project-season creative** — "Finish It Before Fall" with a basement or deck image would land harder for a guy like me in July.
- **Add a "Your Projects" or "Buy It Again" rail above the fold** — I buy the same box of 1-5/8" drywall screws every two weeks. Surface that.
- **Tighten the "Shop All" CTA copy** — make it "Shop GE Appliances" at minimum, or better, point me to a specific product like the French door fridge in the hero shot.
