# Pre-lit Christmas trees, starting at $59.98🎄
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-02**
## Executive summary

- This is a Black Friday head-start email that tries to do too much. The subject promises "Pre-lit Christmas trees, starting at $59.98." The tree hero delivers that: a green "Starting at $59.98" tag over a row of lit trees, with a "Shop now" button. It's the strongest block in the email. The persona is a drywall contractor, though, and nothing here is about drywall or jobsite work.
- Above the tree hero, a red "Black Friday / The deals have dropped EARLY" banner leads the email with no number in it. Below the trees the email keeps going: string lights at $3.98, inflatables at $34.98, free gifts, fireplaces and fire pits at up to 40% off, and a DeWALT router and charger. The eye bounces between a dozen blocks and about ten near-identical navy buttons. It reads as a seasonal catalog, not one clear offer.
- The verdict is a competent seasonal promo that is mismatched to this persona. The subject is concrete, but the body spreads attention thin. It's better than the Oct 29 "Black Friday deals showed up early" send because the subject names a real product and price. It's the same Black Friday theme again, though, and the brand has now sent several promos in a week.

## What's working

- The "Starting at $59.98" price tag on the tree hero is large and easy to read. It matches the subject line.
- The "Your choice" pricing tiles ($3.98 lights, $34.98 inflatables) put a real price in front of each product.
- The "40% off" fireplace and fire pit tiles use big numerals and are easy to scan.
- "FREE same-day delivery" sits in the my Lowe's Rewards bar at the top, which gives members a visible benefit.
- The "Curated with you in mind" row at the bottom shows DEWALT items, the one place the email nods to a tools buyer.

## What's weak

- The first thing you see is the red "The deals have dropped EARLY" banner. It has no number, so the best offer is pushed below it.
- The email has about ten "Shop now" and "Buy now" buttons with identical styling. Nothing signals which one matters.
- The persona is a drywall contractor, but almost all of the content is Christmas decor, inflatables and fireplaces. The three DEWALT/toy products at the bottom are the only relevant ones, and they sit near the end.
- The "Curated with you in mind" row mixes a Squishy Dumpling sensory toy in with the DEWALT tools. That undercuts the claim of personalization.
- The free-delivery line carries an asterisk, and its terms ("$25 min & loyalty membership required") are only in the footer in small type. The "FREE gifts with select purchases" block also has no visible terms.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one hero: the tree price. Move the generic "Black Friday" banner below it or fold it into the hero. The $59.98 should be the first thing you see.
- 2. Cut the body to three or four blocks. Drop the inflatables and the "FREE gifts" block, or merge them into one holiday tile.
- 3. Make the "Curated" row actually segment-aware. For pro or tool buyers, put DEWALT and other tools near the top and drop the toy.
- 4. Use one primary button style for the hero and a quieter text-link style for secondary tiles.
- 5. Put the free-delivery terms next to the claim, not only in the footer.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Trees from $59.98 + 40% off fire pits`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus $3.98 light strands and free same-day delivery for Rewards members.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Early Black Friday pricing on trees, lights and heaters, while stock lasts.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with responsive media queries and mostly working tracking. The confirmed issues are a malformed DOCTYPE, a missing `lang` attribute, missing alt text on two content images, and the recipient's address in plaintext in third-party pixel URLs. The header-level compliance warnings can't be confirmed from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 40 tracking links were skipped as click-redirect domains, so none of their destinations are verified.
- **Recipient email in pixel URLs:** the `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixels carry the address in cleartext, e.g. `https://sli.lowes.com/imp?s=127768300&e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app&p=528931&lctg=410146703&stpe=pixel`. It appears in all 16 pixel URLs, so it will land in third-party logs, proxies and image-cache logs.
- **Empty parameter:** `li_coord=` is blank in the `stpe=default` pixels (`s=2019286`, `s=1090834`). A value that failed to populate may degrade that ad-impression tracking.
- **Duplicate pixels:** `s=2019286` and `s=1090834` each appear twice with identical URLs, so impressions may be double-counted.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed DOCTYPE:** `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" ...>` has no space after `PUBLIC`. Some parsers may fall back to quirks mode.
- **No `lang` attribute:** the root tag is `<html xmlns="https://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">`. Screen readers can't pick the language from it.
- **Missing alt on content images:** `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg` and `.../m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png`. Both look like visible content, so they need descriptive alt text. Image-blocking clients will show nothing for them.
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels:** the 16 `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixels should carry `alt=""` (and ideally `width="1" height="1"`). This is low severity.
- **Web fonts:** Fellix and DIN Next are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, so Outlook skips them as intended. I can't see the fallback font stacks in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` are visible in the available source. The recipient address is resolved in the pixel URLs.
- The blank `li_coord=` is the only possible gap (see section 2).
- The HTML is truncated, so the rest of the body is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** the QA check didn't find either header, but its own note says the AgentMail relay may strip them. This is unconfirmed. Check the raw message headers before treating it as a sender defect.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, with SPF/DKIM/DMARC status unknown. This is also an expected relay artifact, so I can't confirm a failure.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** not visible in the truncated source, so I can't assess them.
- The From domain `e.lowes.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain. That's consistent with proper alignment, but alignment can't be verified without the headers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters:** none can be evaluated, because all 40 links are click-tracking redirects that weren't resolved.
- **Landing page:** no destination was checked against the subject's "Pre-lit Christmas trees, starting at $59.98" claim, and the price on the landing page is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the DOCTYPE spacing (`PUBLIC "-//W3C…`) and add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
2. Add descriptive alt text to `c301e24e-…jpg` and `ad569dc5-…png`, and `alt=""` plus explicit 1×1 dimensions to the `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixels.
3. Stop putting the raw recipient email in third-party pixel query strings. Use an opaque or hashed ID instead.
4. Check why `li_coord=` is empty, and remove the duplicate pixels (`s=2019286`, `s=1090834`) if they aren't intentional.
5. Re-run the compliance checks against raw SMTP headers, either fetched directly or captured upstream of the AgentMail relay. Look for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and `Authentication-Results`.
