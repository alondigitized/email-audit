# LAST DAY of MyLowe’s Money Days 🚨
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-14**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand last-call send. The offer is clear: $10 MyLowe's Money on $200 or more, or $20 with the Rewards Credit Card. The blue field, confetti and card art make it easy to read in seconds. As a loyalty reminder it does its job.
- It is also the fourth Money Days send since 10/6, after "Money Days are HEEEREEEE" (10/6), the 10/7 smart-home send and "So OOONNN" (10/11). Nothing is new here except the deadline. The subject says "LAST DAY," but the body says "end soon" and shows "October 6 – 14" without ever saying "today." For a drywall pro on a jobsite, a generic $200 threshold with no drywall, tools or bulk-material hook gives little reason to act now.
- Judgement: competent urgency reminder, but generic and repetitive. It will convert existing intent and won't create any.

## What's working

- The "$10" is oversized and the offer is readable at a glance.
- The credit card upsell ("$20 … Even better!") adds a second tier without cluttering the hero.
- The "October 6 – 14" window with the calendar icon states the deadline plainly.
- The white "Shop now" button contrasts strongly with the blue field.
- The preheader reads like real copy ("Members, you've still got time to earn more") and nods to loyalty status.

## What's weak

- The creative says "end soon" while the subject says "LAST DAY," and the date range never says "today." The urgency is softer than the subject promises.
- Nothing speaks to a drywall or contractor shopper. There is no product, category or pro angle.
- "Shop now" is generic and leads to the Money Days landing page, not a specific category or product.
- The $200 threshold and "eligible purchase" are qualified by a tiny asterisk. The fine print is hard to read, with exclusions, 30-day expiry and credit approval buried.
- This is the fourth near-identical Money Days message in nine days, so fatigue is likely.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real deadline in the hero: "Ends TONIGHT at 11:59 PM" in place of or next to "October 6 – 14." That matches the subject and gives the urgency teeth.
- 2. Add a category strip below the hero for pros (drywall, joint compound, fasteners, tools) with "Spend $200, get $10 back" examples. That gives the offer a reason to apply to this shopper.
- 3. Make the CTA specific, e.g. "Earn my $10 today," and have it link to a qualifying-products page.
- 4. Make the fine print legible and put the expiry ("expires 30 days after issuance") closer to the offer.
- 5. Space out the Money Days sends and vary the angle, since three earlier sends used the same hero.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: $10 MyLowe's Money on $200+`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: earn $10 back before midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `$20 back with your Rewards Card. Offer ends at 11:59 PM tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stock up on job supplies and earn MyLowe's Money before it's gone.`
