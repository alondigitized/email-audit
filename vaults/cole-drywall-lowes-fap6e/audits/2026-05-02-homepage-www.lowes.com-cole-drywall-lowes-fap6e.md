# Homepage snapshot · www.lowes.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- First thing I see when I land is a sign-in modal blocking everything — which, okay, I get it, but I'm already a MyLowe's card holder, so hitting me with a "get $5 off when you join" pitch feels like the site doesn't know me at all. Once I get past that, the page is pushing a 40% off lighting and ceiling fans sale, which — honestly — isn't terrible timing since I've got a basement ceiling to deal with. But the overall vibe is "spring décor sale" more than "get your project done."
- **5/10**

## What's working

- **40% off lighting and ceiling fans promo** — I actually need to pick ceiling fixtures for the basement, so this hits at the right moment
- **"Up to 40% off" framing** — big number, easy to read on mobile, and "Select Décor Lighting and Ceiling Fans" is specific enough that I know exactly what's on sale
- **Recommended chips row** — "Electric Range," "Washing Machine," "Bathroom Vanity" — appliances are in my wheelhouse; fast-scrolling to categories I shop is useful
- **Sign-in modal perks** — FREE shipping with Silver Key is a real hook; I'd want to know if I qualify
- **Top banner spring savings** — small but persistent, keeps the deal context alive even with the modal up

## What's weak

- **Sign-in modal fires immediately** — I'm not creating a new account, I already have one, and being greeted with "get $5 off when you join" makes me feel like a stranger at my own store
- **Hero is décor lighting, not projects** — the lifestyle photo underneath the 40% off looks like a decorative pendant, not a shop-light or utility fan I'd actually buy for a basement build
- **No project or contractor context above the fold** — nothing says "finishing a basement?" or "working on drywall?" — it's generic spring home décor
- **Recommended chips are appliances, not hard goods** — Electric Range and Washing Machine are things I buy on Memorial Day weekend, not on a random Friday when I'm mid-project
- **Modal blocks the nav** — I can't see what's under "Shop All" or browse categories until I deal with the popup

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the sign-in modal for returning visitors** — or at minimum detect if I have a MyLowe's cookie and greet me by name instead of pitching new-account benefits I already have
- **Swap the hero to a project-focused lifestyle shot** — a guy wiring recessed lights in a basement drop ceiling would land harder than a decorative pendant lamp in a staged dining room
- **Add a "Build & Remodel" or "Current Project" category chip** to the recommended scroll row alongside the appliances, so mid-project customers have a one-tap shortcut
- **Surface MyLowe's Rewards balance or loyalty status** in the header once I'm recognized — something like "You have 450 points" would immediately make me feel like the site knows who I am
