# Up to 50% OFF! Sunday Deals are back
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the third "Sunday Deals" beat from Lowe's this month, and it's essentially a rerun of the 8/17 "Enjoy up to 50% OFF! Sunday Deals are here" send with the subject softened from "here" to "back" — same grid format, same category sprawl, same blue hero. The hero is clean (blue gradient, "Sunday Deals," clear "Shop now"), but it doesn't lead with a single offer — it's a mood-setter, and the real numbers (50% off patio, 20% off grills, save up to $200 on riders) only show up after a scroll.
- The email then cascades through nine-plus modules: patio, grills, backyard lighting, lawn equipment, mowers, mulch/soil, storage bins, moving boxes, cleaning supplies, and a Deals-of-the-Day signup — which is the same sprawling, unfocused structure that dragged the 8/17 email down to a 5.00/10 and the appliance send on 8/21 to a 6.00. Nothing here is broken, nothing looks stale for late August (lawn care, patio, backyard), but there's no reason for a subscriber to feel this is urgent or personalized — it reads as a generic weekly circular.
- Verdict: competently executed, zero render bugs, but repetitive both internally (too many categories, no hierarchy) and across the cadence (third near-identical patio/lawn/grid send in a week). Lands mid-pack.

## What's working

- Hero is visually clean — bold "Sunday Deals" headline, clear blue gradient, single "Shop now" CTA with no clutter.
- Discount badges (50% off patio, 20% off grills, save up to $200 on riders) are legible and placed directly under each product photo.
- "Under $500" and "Late-summer lawn care essentials" module headers add some visual break points between the otherwise uniform product grid.
- No overlapping text, broken images, or layout glitches anywhere in the render.
- Sale unit pricing on soil/potting mix (3 for $10, 2 for $15) is concrete and easy to scan.

## What's weak

- The hero doesn't carry a single headline offer — you have to scroll past it to learn what "Sunday Deals" actually means in dollars.
- Nine+ distinct product modules (patio, grills, lighting, power tools, riders, lawn care, soil, storage bins, boxes, cleaner) with no clear priority — everything is weighted equally, so nothing stands out as the offer.
- Nearly identical structure/subject to the 8/17 "Sunday Deals are here" send just six days ago — same grid cascade, same categories, minimal creative refresh.
- Late modules (storage bins, moving boxes, all-purpose cleaner) have no discount shown at all, diluting the "deals" premise of the whole email.
- No urgency element anywhere — no "ends tonight," no countdown, unlike the 8/19 patio clearance send that had a bold "ENDS TOMORROW" ribbon.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one number in the hero. "Sunday Deals" is a mood, not an offer — lead with "Up to 50% Off Patio" as the hero headline instead of burying it in module two. This is the single highest-leverage fix.
- 2. Cut the module count. Nine-plus categories in one email is the same mistake as 8/17. Pick 3-4 (patio, grills, lawn care, one wildcard) and drop storage bins/boxes/cleaner into a separate send.
- 3. Add a deadline. Every module with a "Save up to $X" should pair with a credible end date — this brand has shown it can do urgency well (8/19's "ENDS TOMORROW" ribbon hit 6.00 despite a repetitive week).
- 4. Differentiate from the 8/17 send. If this is genuinely a new week's deals, say so explicitly ("New week, new deals") rather than reusing near-identical grid structure and subject language.
- 5. Show a discount on every tile, or drop the tile — the undiscounted storage/moving/cleaning items at the bottom read as filler next to bold percentage-off tiles above them.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Sunday Deals: 50% off patio, ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your yard, up to 50% off this week`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus save up to $200 on riding mowers and $100 on power tools.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New patio, grill, and lawn care markdowns — shop before they're gone.`
