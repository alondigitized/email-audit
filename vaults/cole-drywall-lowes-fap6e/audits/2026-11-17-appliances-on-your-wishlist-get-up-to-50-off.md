# Appliances on your wishlist? Get up to 50% OFF🙌
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- This is Lowe's kitchen-sink Black Friday template again, but the subject, hero and body line up better than in recent sends. "Up to 50% OFF" in the subject lands on a red "ALL-IN Appliance Offer" hero with a giant "50% off" and a clear Shop now button. Below it sit concrete price points ($24.99 Crock-Pot, $59.99 Keurig or Gourmia) and a 40% off vacuum block. For an appliance shopper this is a solid, readable send.
- Two things hold it back. "Appliances on your wishlist?" implies personalization that the body doesn't deliver. Nothing here is tied to a wishlist, and the bottom half drifts into flooring, DeWalt batteries, toilets and storage totes. The recipient address (`cole-drywall-lowes`) points to a trade or DIY persona, and the only on-persona item is a DeWalt battery pack with a mangled title. This is also roughly the seventh near-identical Black Friday promo in nine days. The 11/9 "Up to 50% OFF! Black Friday deals on appliances" send already used this same hero and free-delivery framing.
- Overall it's competent but a mismatch for this persona, and a fatigue risk for the cadence. It's a good appliance email sent to the wrong lens.

## What's working

- The subject's "up to 50% OFF" is carried straight into the hero, so the promise is kept.
- The red hero has strong hierarchy. The huge "50% off" leads, and the white Shop now button is easy to spot.
- The FREE Delivery, Basic installation and Haul away badges stack the value clearly. The "Save up to $250" line adds a second hook.
- The "Your choice" Crock-Pot and Keurig/Gourmia modules show real prices ($24.99, $59.99), so the gift angle feels concrete.
- The mylowe's Rewards "FREE same-day delivery" banner sits at the top and gives members a visible benefit.

## What's weak

- "On your wishlist?" is fake personalization. Nothing in the body reflects a wishlist or the recipient's category.
- The fine print is buried. The hero's "FREE delivery" and "$250" savings depend on a $896 minimum spend and select models. Only the tiny footer spells out the terms, including the 11/4–12/2 window.
- Product titles in "Curated with you in mind" look truncated or unedited: "DEWALT 12 -Volt 2 -Pack Battery/charger ( 3 Ah 5 Ah ) - DCB135C" and "Commander Large 27.0 -Gallons ( 108.0 -". They read as feed errors and weaken trust.
- The email stacks too many unrelated modules: appliances, small appliances, vacuums, flooring, tools, a toilet, totes, Deals of the Day. The eye has nowhere to settle after the hero.
- The deadline is not visible. "Black Friday" urgency has no stated end date outside the footer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by persona. Send this appliance-led version only to people with appliance or home-goods behavior. For the trade/DIY persona, lead with a tool or contractor-supply offer, such as DeWalt or Pro pricing, instead of the appliance hero.
- 2. Match the subject to the hero, or cut the "wishlist" line. If you can't back it with real wishlist or browse data, say what the email actually offers: a number, a deadline and free delivery.
- 3. Move the delivery condition and the end date into the hero. "FREE delivery on $896+" and "Ends 12/2" beside the 50% would feel more honest and add urgency.
- 4. Clean up the product feed. Fix the titles and trim them to a readable "DEWALT 20V Battery + Charger" style.
- 5. Cut the bottom half to one or two themed modules instead of five. That removes the clutter and the repeated-send feel.
- 6. Change the button copy. "Shop appliances" and "Shop vacuums" beat a generic "Shop now" or "Buy now".

- **Recipient address in URLs:** the `sli.lowes.com/imp?...&e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app&...` pixels carry the unhashed address in the query string. These URLs show up in proxy, CDN and referrer logs. Confirmed on all 15 impression pixels (`s=127768300`–`127768304`, `2019286`–`2019290`, `1090834`–`1090838`).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content images:**
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg`
