# Homepage snapshot · www.lowes.com · 2026-05-04
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-04**
## Executive summary

- First thing I see when I open Lowe's is a sign-in popup blocking half the page — not exactly a warm welcome when I'm trying to price out drywall compound on a Saturday morning. Behind the modal there's a woman weed-eating a lawn, which is fine, but that's not why I'm here. The banner says "fresh spring savings," so at least it's current, but right now this homepage is working harder to get my login than to sell me anything.
- **6/10**
- − Hero copy/imagery doesn't speak to my persona: woman doing lawn care, not a 47-year-old guy finishing a basement
- + Returning-shopper loyalty hook visible: modal explicitly calls out Silver Key status, points, $5 off
- + Concrete offer above the fold: "$5 off* when you join"
- + Unmistakable primary CTA above the fold: blue "Sign In" button, hard to miss
- − Visual hierarchy: modal blows it up — eye doesn't know whether to dismiss, sign in, or find the thing it came for
- + No render bugs: image renders clean, no broken assets
- − Demographic signal mismatch: female lawn-care model, not a male DIYer mid-project
- + Current/seasonal campaign: "fresh spring savings" is appropriate for early May
- + Loyalty/membership benefits visible without scrolling: Silver Key, points earn, free shipping all in the modal
- − Offer feels honest: asterisks on both "$5 off*" and "FREE shipping with Silver Key status*" — buried conditions on both

## What's working

- **Loyalty modal benefit copy** — "Earn points on eligible purchases" and "FREE shipping with Silver Key status*" are exactly the kind of hook that keeps me on Lowe's instead of driving to Home Depot. As a MyLowe's Rewards + Advantage card holder, that lands.
- **Quick-category chips** — "Electric Range," "Washing Machine," "Bathroom Vanity" are showing up behind the modal. Appliances are absolutely in my wheelhouse (Lowe's is my default for those), so at least the category suggestions are directionally right for me.
- **Seasonal banner** — "Take a look at these fresh spring savings" is timely. Spring is when I'm most active on projects, so this doesn't feel stale.
- **"Lowe's Deals" badge on the hero image** — that red badge signals there's actual pricing action, not just lifestyle fluff.

## What's weak

- **Sign-in modal as the first experience** — I didn't ask to sign in. I'm here to browse. This is friction before I've seen a single product. On mobile this is especially annoying because the modal fills the screen.
- **Hero image is off-target for me** — woman with a string trimmer is lawn and garden, not building materials or basement work. Nothing about drywall, lumber, or even power tools aimed at a finishing project.
- **No drywall, framing, or building materials category visible** — the chips I can see are all appliances. I'd have to dismiss this modal and dig into nav to find what I actually came for.
- **Double asterisks kill confidence** — "$5 off*" and "FREE shipping with Silver Key status*" both carry asterisks with no immediate explanation. That makes me wonder what I'm missing before I even get started.
- **Visual hierarchy is broken** — the modal covers the hero and recommended section, so I can't tell what the main value prop of the page is. The eye has nowhere to go except "close this popup."

## Recommendations

- **Kill the auto-launch sign-in modal on mobile** — or at minimum delay it to after two page interactions. Let me see the homepage first. The loyalty pitch lands better when I'm already engaged, not as a gate.
- **Swap the hero to a building/finishing lifestyle shot** — a guy mudding drywall or hanging cabinet doors in a basement would speak directly to my Saturday. Outdoor power equipment can be a secondary carousel slide.
- **Surface a building materials or tools category chip** — "Drywall & Plaster," "Power Tools," or "Lumber & Composites" would make me feel like this page is built for someone like me, not just appliance shoppers.
- **Resolve the asterisk ambiguity inline** — put the Silver Key tier requirement right in the modal copy: "Silver Key members (Silver tier+)" so I know where I stand without hunting for fine print.
