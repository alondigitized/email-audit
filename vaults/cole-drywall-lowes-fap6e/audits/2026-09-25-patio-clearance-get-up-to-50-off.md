# Patio clearance! Get up to 50% OFF😍
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-25**
## Executive summary

- This is Lowe's clean, well-executed seasonal patio play, and it's a stronger structural send than most of the recent run. The hero is unambiguous — "Get up to 50% off select patio furniture and accessories" over a real lifestyle shot of a patio umbrella and seating set, with a "Clearance" badge doing double duty as urgency and honesty (clearance implies real markdowns, not manufactured discounts). Below the fold it sprawls into fire pits, fall décor, Halloween inflatables, and a community donation module — that's a lot of ground for one email, but each section is visually distinct and doesn't fight the hero for attention.
- Compared to 9/24's Ring/Blink send (6/10) or 9/21's LG appliance send (7/10), this one nails the single biggest thing those got half-right: a specific percentage tied to a specific, clearly-photographed category, sitting alone at the top with nothing competing for the eye. It's not going to blow away 9/23's BOGO tool/battery send (8/10), but it's a solid, honest, on-season execution.

## What's working

- Bold "50% off" number is the largest visual element on the page — impossible to miss on open.
- "Clearance" badge sets honest expectations (deep-discount language matches deep-discount visual).
- Hero product photography (umbrella + patio set) is high-quality and matches the copy exactly — no stock-photo mismatch.
- Clear content hierarchy: patio hero → fire pits → fall décor/Halloween → community give-back → deals-of-the-day signup, each in its own visually separated block.
- Single, unambiguous "Shop now" CTA button directly under the offer, high contrast (navy on white) against the blue gradient background.

## What's weak

- The email keeps going long after the offer — fire pits, fall décor, Halloween inflatables, a charity donation ask, and a newsletter signup all stack below, diluting focus from the patio clearance the subject promised.
- "Halloween inflatables starting at $34.98" sits awkwardly close to the patio/fall content with no visual or thematic transition — feels bolted on.
- No urgency element (no "ends Sunday," no countdown) on a clearance offer that would benefit from a deadline to drive faster action.
- The donation module ("Join us in giving the gift of a good night's sleep") is a nice brand-goodwill play but is visually heavier (full-width photo) than some of the actual shopping CTAs above it, which is a hierarchy misstep.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the fire pits section (or move Halloween/décor/donation to a separate send) — a tighter, patio-and-fall-focused email would keep the promised offer as the clear star instead of getting buried under four other pitches.
- 2. Add a real deadline under the "50% off" headline (e.g., "Ends Sunday") — clearance + urgency is a proven combo this brand hasn't been using.
- 3. Move the Halloween inflatables block to its own dedicated send — it's a big thematic left turn mid-email and dilutes the patio story.
- 4. Shrink the donation module's visual weight (smaller photo, less prominence) so it doesn't visually outcompete the "Shop now" CTAs above it.
