# Homepage snapshot · www.lowes.com · 2026-05-03
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-03**
## Executive summary

- Lowe's right now is pushing spring outdoor living hard — patio furniture is the whole hero — but the first thing I actually see is a "Sign In" modal blocking the page. Feels like a chain home-improvement store that knows I have a rewards account and wants me to log in before I can even look around. Not wrong, just a little pushy for a guy who came here to price out drywall and maybe check appliance deals.
- **7/10**
- + Returning-shopper hook visible — sign-in modal surfaces MyLowe's rewards ($5 off, points, Silver Key free shipping) without me hunting for it
- + Concrete offer above fold — "Up to 25% off Select Patio Furniture and Accessories" is specific
- + Primary CTA visible — blue "Sign In" button and "Shop Now" both present
- + No render bugs — clean render, no broken layout I can see
- + Current season — spring savings banner + patio furniture is seasonally right for May
- + Loyalty benefits visible — Silver Key status, earn points all in the modal before scroll
- + Offer feels honest — "up to 25% off Select" is qualified, not a bait-and-switch headline
- − Hero copy doesn't speak to me — patio furniture is not basement finishing
- − Visual hierarchy disrupted — the modal blocks the page; I'm landing on a forced gate, not the homepage
- − Demographic signals off — outdoor lifestyle imagery vs. mid-project basement guy

## What's working

- **Sign-in modal with loyalty perks** — I have a MyLowe's account and an Advantage card, so seeing "Earn points on eligible purchases" and "FREE shipping with Silver Key status" right up front actually does something for me. That's the hook that keeps me in the ecosystem.
- **Seasonal coherence** — "Fresh spring savings" banner up top and the patio furniture hero match the calendar. It doesn't feel like they forgot to swap out the Christmas decorations.
- **"Lowe's Deals" badge** — that red badge on the hero tells me this isn't a generic promo; it's tagged to their deals program.
- **Category pills below the fold** — "Electric Range," "Washing Machine," "Bathroom Vanity" are visible. Those are exactly the kind of appliance categories I default to Lowe's for. One tap and I'm where I need to be.

## What's weak

- **Modal blocking the page on load** — I came here to browse, not get immediately prompted to log in. I know Lowe's wants my account, but this is aggressive. A guy in the middle of a basement project doesn't have patience for a gate.
- **Patio furniture hero is a miss for me** — I'm not pricing out deck furniture. The whole hero real estate is spent on something I'll scroll past.
- **No project or building-materials signal anywhere above the fold** — zero indication that Lowe's has drywall, framing lumber, or anything I'd actually buy this week. I have to already know they carry it.
- **"Burb..." text visible but cut off** — whatever's in that navigation strip is clipped. That's sloppy on mobile.
- **No visible price point on a specific product** — "up to 25% off" is fine but I can't see what anything actually costs. No anchor price to compare to Home Depot in my head.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the hard-modal on load; swap for a persistent bottom-sheet loyalty strip.** Let me see the homepage first, then surface the "sign in to earn points" nudge as a sticky banner. I'd tap it after I see something I want.
- **Rotate the hero to a split seasonal — patio + project.** Show one patio furniture panel and one building/flooring/paint panel with a "Continue Your Project" hook. Lowe's knows from my purchase history that I'm mid-project; use it.
- **Add a "My Project" or "Recently Viewed" rail above the fold for logged-in users.** If I'm already signed in, don't give me the generic homepage — show me what I was looking at last time.
- **Surface a category shortcut for Drywall / Lumber / Building Supplies** in the pill row alongside the appliances. Right now I see Washing Machine and Electric Range — those are good — but a "Building Materials" pill would tell me I'm in the right store in two seconds.
