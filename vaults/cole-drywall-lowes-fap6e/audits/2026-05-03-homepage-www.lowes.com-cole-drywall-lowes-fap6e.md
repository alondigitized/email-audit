# Homepage snapshot · www.lowes.com · 2026-05-03
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-03**
## Executive summary

- First thing I see when I open the app is a sign-in modal blocking everything — that's already annoying. Once I look past it, Lowe's is pushing a spring décor lighting and ceiling fans sale, which is mildly useful since I've got a basement to light, but this feels more like it's aimed at someone decorating a living room than a guy mid-framing a basement. The spring savings banner up top is fine, but the homepage isn't working very hard for someone like me.
- **7/10**
- + returning-shopper hook visible: sign-in modal explicitly lists "Earn points on eligible purchases" and "FREE shipping with Silver Key status" — that's my MyLowe's Rewards program front and center
- + concrete offer above fold: "Up to 40% off Select Décor Lighting and Ceiling Fans" — specific discount percentage, not vague
- + primary CTA visible: "Sign In" button (blue, prominent) and "Shop Now" link both above the fold
- + no render bugs: page looks clean, no broken images, no placeholder text
- + current season/campaign: "fresh spring savings" banner aligns with May timing
- + loyalty benefits visible without scrolling: the modal literally sells me on Silver Key and points
- − hero explicitly targets my persona: décor pendant lanterns aren't basement-finishing materials — they're for someone redoing a dining room
- − visual hierarchy is confused: the sign-in modal hijacks the hero, so there's no clean eye path
- − demographic signals don't match: no power tools, no building materials, no Kobalt branding — nothing that screams 47-year-old DIY guy with drywall dust on his jeans
- − offer honesty: both Silver Key shipping and the $5 join bonus carry asterisks — fine print vibes

## What's working

- **Sign-in modal loyalty pitch** — "Earn points on eligible purchases" and Silver Key free shipping is legit useful info. As a MyLowe's Rewards card guy, that's actually a decent reminder to stay logged in.
- **"Up to 40% off" hero offer** — The percentage is right there in massive type. I don't have to hunt for what the deal is.
- **Recommended category chips** — "Electric Range," "Washing Machine," "Bathroom Vanity" — I do buy appliances here, especially around Memorial Day. Having those chips one tap away is handy.
- **Top announcement bar** — "Take a look at these fresh spring savings. Shop Now" is low-friction, doesn't scream at me, gives me an out if I don't want to deal with the modal.
- **Lowe's Deals badge on hero** — That red badge is clear shorthand that this is a sale, not just editorial content.

## What's weak

- **The forced sign-in modal** — I came here to find drywall compound or price-check a shop vac, not wade through a membership pitch. This is the first thing I see. Kills momentum.
- **Décor lighting hero for a project shopper** — That lantern pendant is pretty but it's not talking to me. Someone finishing a basement wants recessed lighting, rough-in supplies, or at minimum something that looks utilitarian — not moody lifestyle photography.
- **No tools, no building materials anywhere visible** — Not a single piece of content above the fold (or just below it) speaks to lumber, drywall, concrete, Kobalt, or anything in the pro/DIY build category.
- **Silver Key shipping asterisk** — I can see the asterisk but not the terms. "FREE shipping with Silver Key status*" — what status level? That uncertainty makes me trust it less.
- **Carousel with no visible context** — There are five carousel dots at the bottom, but I can only see the first slide. No indication what else is in the rotation. I might never swipe.

## Recommendations

- **Lead with a project-focused hero** — Swap the pendant lantern for a basement renovation lifestyle shot (guy taping drywall, new drop ceiling going in, workshop lighting install) paired with "Finish the Project" messaging. Even spring savings can be framed around home projects, not décor.
- **Kill the blocking sign-in modal on first visit** — Replace it with a persistent but dismissable loyalty bar at the top. If I'm already a MyLowe's member, reward me; don't gate me.
- **Surface a tools or building materials chip** — Add "Drywall & Panels," "Power Tools," or "Flooring" to the recommended category strip. Right now it skews appliance, which misses the project shopper.
- **Show me the free-shipping threshold plainly** — "Free shipping on orders $45+" (or whatever it is) is more compelling than "Silver Key status*." Make the number visible, remove the asterisk mystery.
