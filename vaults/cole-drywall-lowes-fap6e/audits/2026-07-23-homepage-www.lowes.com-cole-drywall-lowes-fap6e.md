# Homepage snapshot · www.lowes.com · 2026-07-23
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-23**
## Executive summary

- First thing I see when I open the app is a sign-in pop-up shoved in my face before I can look at anything — that's not a great welcome mat. Behind it I can make out bathroom products on sale and some appliance quick-links, which at least tells me Lowe's knows who shops here. But right now it feels less like a store and more like a tollbooth.

## What's working

- **"Closeout Event" top banner** — simple, direct, and "before they're gone" is the right kind of urgency for a Gen X guy who knows good deals disappear fast
- **Appliance category chips** (Electric Range, Washing Machine) — I default to Lowe's for appliances, so seeing those immediately confirms I'm in the right place
- **40% off Bathroom Products** — mid-basement-finish, I'm probably looking at a bathroom rough-in anyway; this is actually useful timing
- **"This Week's Top Deals"** section — no-nonsense label, I know exactly what I'm clicking into
- **Silver Key / earn points callout** — I have the MyLowe's Rewards card, so any reminder that I'm earning something keeps me engaged

## What's weak

- **Forced sign-in modal before I can see the page** — I just opened the app; I haven't even looked around yet. Dark pattern, pure and simple. On mobile this is especially aggravating
- **Hero is completely invisible** — whatever Lowe's actually wants to push is buried under the modal; I have no idea what the "real" message of this page is
- **No tool or building material category visible** — I'm mid-basement-finish; I need drywall screws, compound, maybe some subfloor. The chips show Range and Washing Machine and Bathroom Vanity — nothing for the project guy
- **No free-shipping threshold clearly stated** — "FREE shipping with Silver Key status*" has an asterisk and requires me to already be a Silver Key member; that's not a clean promise
- **No trust signals below the fold line** — no review counts, no return policy mention, nothing to build confidence before I tap

## Recommendations

- **Kill the forced sign-in modal on app open** — move it to a soft banner or delay it until after I've added something to cart; the interruption before I even see the store is the single biggest conversion killer here
- **Swap bathroom vanity hero for a building-materials lifestyle shot** — a guy running a jigsaw in a half-framed basement would speak directly to the summer project crowd; don't waste the hero on a category I might buy from anyway
- **Add a "Project Builder" or "Tools & Hardware" quick-chip** alongside Range and Washing Machine so project shoppers can get to their aisle in one tap
- **Show a concrete free-shipping threshold** ("Free shipping on orders $45+") instead of tying it to tier status — that alone would make the offer feel more accessible to someone browsing before logging in
