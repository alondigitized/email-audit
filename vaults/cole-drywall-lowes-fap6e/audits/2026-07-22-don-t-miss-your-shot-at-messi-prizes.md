# Don't miss your shot at Messi prizes
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-22**
## Executive summary

- This is an urgency-close email for the Messi Halftime Event campaign that launched July 19 ("Your shot at EPIC Messi prizes starts today," 9/10). Lowe's is doing the right structural thing — countdown timer, compressed copy, single primary action — but the execution is thinner than the launch. Three days on, the creative is nearly identical to the opener, which means subscribers who didn't act then are unlikely to convert on déjà vu alone.
- The countdown timer is the strongest element here: 12:29:18 shown as a live three-column block is a concrete, credible deadline that does more urgency work than any subject-line trick. The secondary product push (10-ft Giant Messi Inflatable) is smart — it gives non-sweepstakes shoppers something to actually buy. But a third section peddling vague "Messi exclusives and content" with a "Learn more" button dilutes the close.
- Verdict: competent urgency follow-up that doesn't add enough new creative or incentive to reactivate subscribers who passed on the launch send. A time-gated offer (bonus entries, loyalty points, inflatable discount) would have pushed this into meaningful lift territory.

## What's working

- **Countdown timer is precise and visible above the fold** — 12:29:18 in a three-column display is hard to ignore and the most action-driving element in the email.
- **"No luck required" inflatable section is a smart pivot** — converts sweepstakes-skeptics into buyers with a real product CTA.
- **Visual hierarchy is clean** — Messi hero, then sweepstakes CTA, then product, then content; the eye travels logically.
- **No render bugs** — layout is crisp, no broken images, no overlapping text, fine print visibly present.

## What's weak

- **Creative is nearly identical to July 19** — no new hook, no escalated offer, no fresh angle to justify the re-send three days later.
- **"Learn more" is a dead CTA** — the third section ("Even more Messi? Absolutely.") is vague filler with the weakest possible button copy.
- **No price incentive anywhere** — sweepstakes + inflatable at full price means no savings signal for the deal-motivated subscriber.
- **The third section undercuts the urgency close** — stacking a soft "Learn more" after two hard CTAs breaks the momentum of a ticking clock.
- **Preheader repeats the body** — "Your chance to win Messi memorabilia, signed by Lionel Messi" is the same beat as the countdown copy inside; a missed opportunity to add new information.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a loyalty sweetener to the urgency close** — the July 19 launch offered 10x points "Today Only" which scored 9/10. This email has no equivalent. Add "Earn 5x MyLowe's points on the inflatable — today only" to convert fence-sitters with a transactional reward.
- 2. **Cut the third "Learn more" section entirely** — it adds scroll depth with no conversion value. Redirect that space to a stronger inflatable CTA or a product image with a visible price.
- 3. **Rewrite the CTA progression** — "Enter now" → "Buy now" is good; the third slot should be a benefit-forward variant ("Shop all Messi gear") not a content tease.
- 4. **Give the preheader a new angle** — it's currently a repeat of the body. Use it to surface the deadline or the inflatable.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Clock's ticking — last chance for Messi prizes`
- **Subject Alt B:** `12 hours left: win Messi memorabilia`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sweepstakes closes tonight — plus the 10-ft inflatable is still in stock`

**Media query overlap:** Two breakpoint blocks exist — one targeting `min-width: 375px` and `max-width: 600px`, and a second targeting `max-width: 480px`. The 375–480px range is governed by both blocks simultaneously. Specificity conflicts are possible if the same class appears in both with different values (e.g., `.mobile-only`).
