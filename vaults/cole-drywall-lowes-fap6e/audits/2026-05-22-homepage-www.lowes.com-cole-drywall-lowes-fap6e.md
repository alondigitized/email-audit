# Homepage snapshot · www.lowes.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- I land on Lowe's and immediately get a sign-in popup shoved in my face before I can even see what's on sale — not great when I already have a MyLowe's Rewards account and I'm trying to get to Memorial Day appliance deals. Behind the popup I can see the page is doing the right thing for the holiday weekend, but the modal blocking the whole hero is the kind of thing that'd make me hit the back button on a busier day. Timing is good; execution has some friction.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day timing is spot-on.** I specifically hold appliance buys for this weekend — seeing "Memorial Day" in the hero immediately tells me I came at the right time.
- **Recommended category chips** — Electric Range, Washing Machine — are exactly the categories I'd be shopping. That's not an accident, and I appreciate it.
- **Silver Key callout in the modal** actually means something to me. Free shipping with status is a real perk and I didn't have to dig for it.
- **Top banner "Shop Now" link** above the Lowe's logo gives me an immediate escape if I dismiss the modal — good fail-safe.

## What's weak

- **The sign-in modal blocking the entire page** is the first thing I see. I'm a returning customer — the page should know that or at least let me browse before demanding credentials.
- **No visible urgency or deadline.** Memorial Day is a limited window. There's no "sale ends Sunday," no countdown — nothing that makes me move faster.
- **"Get $5 off when you join"** is a new-member offer. As a MyLowe's Rewards + Advantage card holder I'm already signed up — this reads like I don't exist yet.
- **No product imagery visible whatsoever.** The Memorial Day hero is all text on a dark blue background. I can't see a washer, a drill, a range — nothing to pull me in.
- **American flag memorial section** between the modal and the sale hero breaks the commerce flow. Patriotic sentiment is fine but wedging it between a loyalty prompt and a sale banner feels clunky.

## Recommendations

- **Auto-recognize returning visitors and suppress the sign-in modal** — or at minimum, show a lighter "Welcome back, Cole" banner instead of a full-page takeover.
- **Add a countdown timer to the Memorial Day hero.** "Sale ends Monday at midnight" would get me moving in under 5 seconds.
- **Swap the text-only Memorial Day hero for a product lifestyle shot** — a washer/dryer set in a clean laundry room, or a tool kit in a half-finished basement — something that makes me picture my own project.
- **Surface my Advantage card balance or MyLowe's points** for signed-in users instead of the new-member $5 off pitch; I want to know what I've accrued before I shop.
