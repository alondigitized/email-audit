# These concert perks? Goated 🪩
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-19**
## Executive summary

- This is Lowe's chasing a second lifestyle beat off the 9/17 "Your FREE gift is coming" send — same Dan+Shay/Live Nation partnership, same non-appliance angle, but this time it's a sweepstakes-first layout with zero home-improvement product anywhere in the email. The hero leads with "Enter our concert sweepstakes" and a $100,000 prize claim, followed by a full concert-date grid across eight cities, before finally landing on a vague "Member perks, lined up" CTA. For a hardware/home-improvement retailer, this is about as far from category-relevant as an email gets — there's no product, no % off, no home angle at all, just a co-branded ticket promo.
- The render itself is clean — no broken images, no overlapping text — and the visual hierarchy is genuinely strong (bold dollar figure, clear CTA buttons, readable date grid). But this is pure novelty/engagement content, not a sales-driving send, and the brand history shows Lowe's alternates between this kind of stunt and hard appliance/patio discounts. Judgement: well-executed as a standalone piece of creative, but it's the second concert-themed send in ~10 days and has nothing to do with why a Lowe's subscriber opened the account in the first place.

## What's working

- The $100,000 prize headline is bold, legible, and impossible to miss above the fold.
- "Enter to win" and "Learn more" CTAs are both high-contrast white buttons on dark backgrounds — unambiguous.
- The concert date/city grid is clean and well-organized (8 cities, clear dates, venue names) with no layout breakage.
- Consistent Lowe's/MyLowe's Rewards branding at header and footer keeps it recognizable despite the odd content.
- No render bugs — every image, band photo, and text block displays correctly.

## What's weak

- Zero product or home-improvement content — nothing to click if you're a Lowe's shopper looking for tools, appliances, or patio gear.
- No discernible discount or dollar-off offer for the recipient — the "offer" is 2-for-1 concert tickets, gated by a code and "select shows," not a Lowe's purchase incentive.
- "Member perks, lined up" CTA is vague — doesn't say what the perk actually is.
- This is the second Dan+Shay/concert-themed send in about a week (9/17's free-gift email), risking fatigue on a gimmick rather than core inventory.
- Fine print at the bottom (50-state NO PURCH NEC., ticket restrictions, code requirements) undercuts the "$100,000 in prizes" headline with real friction.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pair this with an actual category tile (patio, grills, outdoor speakers) so there's something to buy, not just something to enter — sweepstakes-only emails don't move revenue.
- 2. Cut "Member perks, lined up" for a concrete CTA like "See your 2-for-1 code" — tell the reader what they get before asking them to click.
- 3. Move the entry deadline (10/2/26) up into the hero — right now urgency is buried in the fine print at the very bottom.
- 4. Space this out further from the 9/17 free-gift send — back-to-back non-product emails read as filler, not value.
- 5. If this is meant to build loyalty engagement rather than drive sales, label it that way internally and don't count it against product-conversion cadence.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your shot at $100,000 in Ticket Cash starts now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Enter by 10/2 for a chance at concert tickets for a year.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `2-for-1 lawn tickets to Dan + Shay, plus $100K in prizes up for grabs.`
