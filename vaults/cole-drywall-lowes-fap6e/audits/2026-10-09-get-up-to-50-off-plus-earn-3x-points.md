# Get up to 50% OFF! Plus, earn 3x points✨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a Halloween clearance send with Money Days stacked underneath. The top two modules are strong. The hero has "Get up to 50% off select Halloween finds" over a lit-up scarecrow, and the 3x Points banner follows right after it. A reader sees a big number, a brand they know and a "Shop now" button in the first scroll. The subject matches that top half, which fixes the mismatch from the 10/7 send ("Up to 65% OFF smart home," with a Money Days hero).
- The rest of the email loses focus. It moves from Halloween to Christmas trees at $248, then to the Money Days block. After that it adds a "Curated with you in mind" product row and a Deals of the Day banner. That is five or six offers with no priority. The strongest loyalty content is the $10 and $20 MyLowe's Money block (Oct 6–14). It sits fourth, and the subject never mentions it. The 10/6 send led with that offer and was cleaner.
- Judgement: a competent, seasonal, loyalty-flavored send that tries to serve everyone and serves a specific persona poorly. For a drywall-focused recipient like cole-drywall, Halloween décor and artificial Christmas trees are noise.

## What's working

- The hero puts "50% off" in huge type next to a clear "Shop now" button, so the offer reads in about a second.
- The subject (50% off, 3x points) matches the first two modules, unlike the 10/7 send.
- The 3x Points module has its own "Activate now" action, which gives members a reason to engage.
- The Money Days block gives concrete dollar figures ($10 on $200, $20 with the credit card) and a visible Oct 6–14 window.
- The header nav (Appliances, Daily Deals, Bathroom, Outdoor Tools) and the Lowe's branding feel familiar and trustworthy.

## What's weak

- Product names in the "Curated with you in mind" row are cut off mid-phrase ("27.0 -Gallons ( 108.0 -", "Use 0.75 Cubic feet Garden soil -"). The middle product is a generic paper lawn bag, and none of the three has a price.
- The Halloween and Christmas modules have no connection to a drywall or contractor persona, and nothing in the email reflects that focus.
- Almost every CTA says "Shop now" or "Buy now". Six buttons with the same label give the reader no reason to pick one.
- The 3x points offer is only for eligible Halloween décor and party supplies, and the exclusions are in tiny footnote text. The subject's "earn 3x points" reads broader than it is.
- The Money Days block, the strongest member benefit, sits fourth. It is also repeated from the 10/6 and 10/7 sends, so this is at least the third look at it this week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story per send. Either lead with Halloween 50% off and cut the Christmas and "curated" modules, or lead with Money Days and make Halloween the secondary offer. Don't ship both at full weight.
- 2. Fix the product row. Show full, clean product names and prices, and use real product imagery instead of the paper-bag placeholder. Better still, pull items from the recipient's category (drywall, tools) instead of generic garden soil.
- 3. Move the dated Money Days deadline (Oct 6–14) into the subject or the first module. It is the only credible urgency in the email.
- 4. Make the CTAs specific: "Shop Halloween clearance," "Activate 3x points," "Shop Christmas trees from $248."
- 5. Segment. Send contractor and pro-category recipients a tools or building-materials version in place of seasonal décor.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Halloween clearance up to 50% off + 3x points`
- **Subject Alt B:** `$10 MyLowe's Money + 50% off Halloween, thru 10/14`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Activate 3x points on Halloween décor. Money Days ends Oct 14.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Spend $200, get $10 back in MyLowe's Money. Clearance while it lasts.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is mostly standard table-based XHTML with MSO and Apple Mail handling. The confirmed problems are missing `alt` attributes on 7 images, the recipient address exposed in `sli.lowes.com` pixel URLs, and a malformed DOCTYPE. The HTML was truncated and header and link probes were skipped, so compliance, authentication and UTM checks are unverified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 33 tracking links were skipped as click-redirect domains, so none were checked for destination, status or redirect chain.
- **Duplicate impression pixel:** `https://sli.lowes.com/imp?s=1090834&...&stpe=default&li_coord=` appears twice. This may double-count impressions.
- **Empty parameter:** `li_coord=` has no value in both `s=1090834` pixels. It is probably an unpopulated dynamic variable.
- **Recipient address in URLs:** every `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixel carries `e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app`. The plaintext address in image URLs can leak through proxy logs, CDN logs and forwarded copies. Hashing it would avoid that.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on content images (confirmed):**
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg`
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png`

  If either is linked, a screen reader or an image-blocked client has no text for it.
- **Missing `alt` on pixels (confirmed):** five `sli.lowes.com/imp` tracking pixels (`s=1090834` ×2, `1090835`, `1090837`, `1090838`) have no `alt`. These should carry `alt=""` so assistive tech skips them.
- **Malformed DOCTYPE:** `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" ...>` has no space after `PUBLIC`. Most clients tolerate this, but it is invalid.
- **Namespace and DTD over `https`:** `xmlns="https://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` and the DTD URL use `https`. The canonical XHTML namespace is `http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml`. This is harmless in email clients but non-standard.
- **Web fonts:** Fellix and DIN Next `@font-face` rules are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. They fall back to system fonts in most clients, which is expected. I didn't see the fallback `font-family` stacks in the truncated source.
- **Mobile CSS:** I didn't see mobile CSS problems in the visible portion. The `@media (max-width: 480px)` rules and `.mobile-hidden` handling look consistent.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- **Possible empty variable:** `li_coord=` (see Section 2).
- **Not verifiable:** the visible body, greeting and footer are cut off, so I can't confirm token handling there.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found [WARN]:** the QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip it. I can't confirm the sender omitted it.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found [WARN]:** same caveat. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so this should be checked in the raw message at the source.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found [WARN]:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. `From` is `lowes@e.lowes.com`, and the relay likely removed the header.
- **Body checks not possible:** the visible HTML doesn't include the footer, so I can't confirm the unsubscribe link or the physical postal address.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not verifiable:** all 33 click-tracking links were skipped. I have no landing-page URLs, UTM parameters or destination status codes to compare against the "up to 50% off / 3x points" offer.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt=""` to the five `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixels. Add descriptive `alt` text to `c301e24e-...jpg` and `ad569dc5-...png`.
2. Replace the plaintext address in `e=` with a hashed or opaque recipient ID.
3. Check the `li_coord=` population logic and remove the duplicate `s=1090834` pixel if it isn't intentional.
4. Fix the DOCTYPE spacing (`PUBLIC "-//W3C//..."`) and use the canonical `http://` XHTML namespace.
5. Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` on the original message, not the relayed copy, before treating these as real deficiencies.
