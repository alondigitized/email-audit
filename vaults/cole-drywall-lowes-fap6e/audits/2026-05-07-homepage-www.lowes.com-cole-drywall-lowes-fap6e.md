# Homepage snapshot · www.lowes.com · 2026-05-07
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- First thing I see when I hit lowes.com is a sign-in pop-up blocking half the page — not exactly rolling out the red carpet for a guy who's been swiping his Lowe's Advantage card for years. Once I peek past it, there's a Samsung Bespoke appliance ad taking up the hero, which... okay, Memorial Day's coming and I do buy appliances here, but "Bespoke" with "Pro Style Design" reads like a kitchen showroom pitch, not a lumber-and-drywall homepage. The Mother's Day gift banner at the top tells me everything about who Lowe's thinks is browsing right now — and it ain't me.

## What's working

- **Loyalty modal content** — "Earn points on eligible purchases" and "FREE shipping with Silver Key status" are exactly the benefits that make me reach for the Advantage card. That copy is dead-on for a MyLowe's member.
- **"Tool Box" category pill** — visible below the modal and it's one tap to what I actually came for. That's the right category showing up.
- **Samsung appliances placement** — I do default to Lowe's for appliances, and Memorial Day is right around the corner. Timing of an appliance push isn't wrong for me even if the copy overshoots.
- **Clean render** — no broken tiles, no misaligned text. Page loads and displays without obvious tech issues.

## What's weak

- **Forced sign-in modal** — I opened the app to look at drywall compound or check an Allen + Roth vanity, not to be immediately confronted with an account prompt. This is the first thing I see. It's a dark pattern and it's annoying.
- **Mother's Day top banner** — "Find gifts for the greatest mom" is not speaking to me at all. That banner slot is prime real estate wasted on a demographic that isn't me.
- **"Bespoke appliances" hero copy** — "Bespoke" and "Pro Style Design ranges" reads fancy. I'm not shopping a showroom; I'm finishing a basement. The language is off-register for how I actually shop.
- **No project-focused or DIY hook visible** — a guy mid-project with a cart full of Kobalt tools wants to see something like "Your project, your timeline — shop building materials" or a project-specific entry point. None of that is above the fold.
- **Truncated navigation** — the "Bathroom Vanity With S…" category pill is cut off. On a phone screen that's sloppy UX.

## Recommendations

- **Kill or delay the sign-in modal for return visitors** — if I'm already cookied from a prior session or I have the Lowe's app, don't block my first view with a join prompt. Show me my recent browsing instead.
- **Swap the top banner from Mother's Day to a Memorial Day project/appliance tease** — Memorial Day is the biggest appliance sale of the year; that's two weeks out and I am literally in the market. "Memorial Day Deals Start Now — Appliances, Tools, and More" converts for me.
- **Add a "Continue Your Project" or "Recently Viewed" module above the fold** — returning MyLowe's members should see personalized continuation, not a generic brand ad.
- **Fix the truncated category pills** — either reduce font size or cap the pill count so text isn't cut off mid-word on mobile.
