# Make it merry with Black Friday savings🎄
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a holiday-décor send with a Black Friday banner on top. The subject says "Black Friday savings," but the hero offer is "Starting at $49.98" on outdoor holiday décor. The red "Black Friday" header over a lit-up yard scene is clear and seasonal. The email then loses focus. After the hero it moves to a category grid, ladders, an ornament and snow globe promo, gift cards, three unrelated products, Deals of the Day and a sign-up block.
- This is close to the 2026-11-02 pre-lit tree send ("starting at $59.98"). The offer is nearly the same and the creative is similar, so a subscriber would see it as another décor push. The "FREE same-day delivery" rewards strip is the best member-value hook, but it sits in the header and gets overlooked. The row of DEWALT battery, toilet and storage tote under "Curated with you in mind" has no connection to Christmas and reads as filler. Overall it's a competent but generic send, and it falls short of the earlier tool and appliance emails.

## What's working

- The hero is clear. A red banner, "Bring a little merry to the outdoors," a festive yard photo and a green "Starting at $49.98" tag with a "Shop now" button put the offer in the first scroll.
- The four-tile category grid (trees, lights, ornaments, tree skirts) is easy to scan and links to specific shopping paths.
- The "FREE same-day delivery" banner with My Lowe's Rewards is a concrete loyalty benefit.
- The "Up to 50% off select ladders" block has a large, readable number and a visible button.
- The seasonal imagery and red/blue palette are consistent with the brand.

## What's weak

- The "Black Friday savings" in the subject is not backed by a Black Friday discount. The only price shown is $49.98 on décor.
- The email has too many competing offers: décor, ladders, ornament/snow globe, gift cards, Deals of the Day. Nothing is priority-ranked after the hero.
- The "Curated with you in mind" products (DEWALT charger, toilet, storage tote) don't fit the holiday theme. The product titles are truncated ("( 3 Ah 5 Ah ) - DCB135C", "27.0 -Gallons ( 108.0 -"), and there are no prices or reviews.
- The ornament/snow globe block has no price, and "before they're gone" is vague urgency. The "Limited Time Deals" tag has no deadline.
- The ladder promo has nothing to do with the holiday theme.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one story. Keep the hero and category grid, then add 3 to 4 priced holiday products with a stated deadline. Remove the ladders, gift cards and unrelated products.
- 2. Make the subject match the offer. Name the real discount or price point, and drop "Black Friday" unless a Black Friday price is shown.
- 3. Move the "FREE same-day delivery" benefit closer to the hero and add the cutoff time.
- 4. Fix the product tiles: clean up the truncated titles, add prices and star ratings.
- 5. Give the ornament/snow globe promo a price or a ship-by date.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Trees, lights and inflatables, plus free same-day delivery for Rewards members.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The source shows a table-based XHTML email with MSO conditionals and mobile media queries. Its main confirmed defects are a malformed DOCTYPE, missing `alt` attributes on 19 images, and the recipient's email address embedded in plaintext in the `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixel URLs. The QA warnings about headers and tracking links could not be verified from the data available.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified destinations:** 41 tracking links were skipped, so none of their redirect chains, final landing pages or HTTP status codes were checked.
- **Duplicate impression pixels:** `s=2019286` and `s=1090834` each fire twice (`stpe=default&li_coord=`). That will double-count impressions in the ad-server logs.
- **Pixel volume:** There are 17 `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixels (`p=529686` ×5, `p=1182026` ×12). That adds many third-party requests on open and delays the load of any client that waits on them.
- **PII in URLs:** Every pixel carries `e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app` as a plaintext query parameter. The address will appear in server, proxy and CDN logs and in any link-scanner logs.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed DOCTYPE:** `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" …>` has no whitespace after `PUBLIC`. Strict parsers may reject it or fall back to quirks mode, though most mail clients tolerate it.
- **Wrong XHTML namespace:** `xmlns="https://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` uses https, but the namespace URI is `http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml`. It is harmless in practice.
- **Missing alt text on content images (2):**
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg`
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png`

  If these carry offer content, screen readers and image-blocked clients get nothing.
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels (17):** These are decorative and should carry `alt=""`, plus explicit `width="1" height="1"` and `style="display:none"` where appropriate. Without them, clients with images blocked may show broken-image icons or alt-less placeholders.
- **Truncated source:** The HTML is cut off inside the CSS, so I could not check the body structure, contrast, `lang` attribute or preheader.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[%…%]`) appear in the visible source. The recipient address resolves correctly in the pixel URLs. The truncated source leaves the body unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

If the headers are really absent, the email would fall short of Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements for one-click unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results:** It was not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a relay limitation, not evidence of failure. The From domain `e.lowes.com` is consistent with the sending subdomain.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and physical address:** The truncated source does not show them, so they could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
UTM parameters and landing pages could not be assessed. The 41 links were skipped as click-redirect domains, and the visible source does not expose their destinations. No continuity issues are confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Move recipient identifiers out of the pixel URLs.** Replace `e=<email>` with an opaque subscriber ID or hash, so the address does not appear in query strings.
2. **Add `alt=""` to all 17 `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixels,** and give the two content images (`c301e24e…jpg`, `ad569dc5…png`) meaningful alt text.
3. **Fix the DOCTYPE** to `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" "http://www.w3.org/TR/xhtml1/DTD/xhtml1-transitional.dtd">`, and use the `http://` namespace in `xmlns`.
4. **Deduplicate the repeated impression pixels** (`s=2019286`, `s=1090834`) to avoid double-counted impressions.
5. **Fix the QA tool so it can confirm compliance.** Capture raw headers before the relay, or read them from the original message, so that List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post and Authentication-Results can be verified. Then follow the 41 redirect links to check UTMs and landing pages.
