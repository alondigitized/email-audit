# Last chance to enter the sweepstakes❗
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-14**
## Executive summary

- This is Lowe's fifth distinct beat in ten days, and it's the least "Lowe's-y" of the bunch — a soccer sweepstakes tie-in with Leagues Cup 2026, fronted by a lineup of pro players in club jerseys, not a single power tool or patio set in sight. It's a clean, confident execution: one hero, one CTA, no cross-sell cascade dragging the eye down. But the "50/50" split of persona relevance is real — this only lands if you actually follow soccer, and there's zero signal in the copy about who's playing or why a home-improvement retailer is running this.
- The "Final Call!" marquee banner is a strong urgency device and the header hierarchy (headline → CTA → prize breakdown) reads clearly top to bottom. The bonus-entry "Shop now" module is smart — it's the one place this actually ties back to Lowe's core business, nudging a soccer sweepstakes into an actual purchase incentive.
- Compared to the run of appliance/patio/back-to-campus sends this brand has been cycling through, this is a nice change of pace for engagement variety, but as a home-improvement subscriber, I have no personal stake in the prize (trip + jersey + game code), and the email never explains the Lowe's connection. Judgement: well-built creative, weak category fit — a 5/10 for anyone not already a soccer fan.

## What's working

- The "FINAL CALL!" marquee ticker immediately signals urgency and scarcity without shouting in all-caps body copy.
- Single, unambiguous primary CTA ("Enter now") sits directly under the headline — no scrolling needed to find it.
- The prize breakdown module (tickets, jersey, game code, gift card) is specific and itemized, not vague "amazing prizes."
- The bonus-entry "Shop now" tile smartly links the sweepstakes back to an actual Lowe's purchase incentive.
- No render bugs — clean gradient background, sharp player photos, legible white-on-blue text throughout.

## What's weak

- Zero connection is drawn between Lowe's (a home-improvement retailer) and a soccer tournament — the "why" is never explained, which will confuse or alienate non-soccer subscribers.
- The hero photo is unlabeled — none of the players are named, so even soccer fans get no specific draw ("see Messi" would land harder than an anonymous jersey lineup).
- Subject line's urgency ("Last chance") isn't visually reinforced with a specific deadline/date anywhere in the body — "Time's running out" is vague.
- This is a fifth different campaign theme in ten days (patio, totes, appliances, backyard, now soccer) — cadence is scattershot with no throughline for a subscriber trying to track what Lowe's actually sells.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add one line connecting Lowe's to the sweepstakes (e.g., "Lowe's is bringing you closer to the pitch this season") — right now the brand tie-in is entirely absent, which is the single biggest trust/relevance gap.
- 2. Call out at least one recognizable name (Messi's jersey is literally a prize — say so in the hero, not buried in the prize grid) to convert casual scrollers into openers.
- 3. Put a hard date/time on the deadline near the headline, not just "Time's running out."
- 4. Reorder prize tiles so the Messi jersey and Leagues Cup Final tickets — the most emotionally resonant prizes — lead the grid instead of tickets/jersey/game/gift-card in undifferentiated order.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Final hours: win 4 tickets to the Leagues Cup Final`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call — enter to win Messi's signed jersey`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus a $500 Lowe's gift card and EA Sports FC 27 codes — ends tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sweepstakes closes 8/15 — no purchase necessary to enter.`
