# This Black Friday morning, 150 members win BIG 💥
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is Lowe's best-built Black Friday send of the run. It has one clear idea: an in-store prize event on Friday, Nov 27, with the first 150 members at every store winning something. The "FREE" green highlight blocks and the bucket imagery carry the story well, and the gold, silver, red and green prize tiers make a good scavenger-hunt visual. After the kitchen-sink sends of Nov 13–20, this feels focused.
- The weakness is that it's a sweepstakes-style, in-store-only, 6 a.m. door-buster. Most recipients won't be in the first 50 or 150 at a store, and the hero never says what the "instant prize" is. The $15,000,000 figure is big but abstract. The email also never tells you to do anything online. Both CTAs are "Learn more", and nothing says whether you need to be a member to win or how to sign up. The subject says "This Black Friday morning, 150 members win BIG," but Black Friday is Nov 27, six days away. Calling it "this" morning overstates the urgency.
- My judgement: strong creative and a real hook, but it's a low-probability prize rather than a price offer, and the CTAs are weak. It will get opens from curiosity and loyalty members, and fewer clicks.

## What's working

- The sender "MyLowe's Rewards" signals loyalty content, and the first-150-members framing is exclusive and member-specific.
- The visual hierarchy is clear: red header, dark hero, then stacked blocks that each carry one prize.
- The green "FREE" highlights draw the eye to the value in every block.
- The gold, silver, red and green tiers turn the bucket into a game ("your bucket could hold a winning ticket").
- Date and time are visible in the hero: Friday, Nov 27, doors open at 6 a.m., In-store only.
- The sections are well paced, and the "There's more to go around" block for the next 100 members reduces the "I'll never win" feeling.
- The Dream Kitchen Sweepstakes (entries open Thanksgiving Day) is a good second reason to come back.

## What's weak

- Both main CTAs say "Learn more", which is generic and doesn't say what happens next.
- The hero says members get an "instant prize" but doesn't name it until the next block, so the offer is buried one scroll down.
- The offer is a lottery, not savings. There's no price discount or member pricing, so it appeals little to a shopper who just wants a deal.
- The legal copy is dense and repeated, with "No purchase necessary", "Void where prohibited", and 18+ and enrollment rules in tiny type. It undercuts the "honest offer" feel.
- It's a long email. The LG hot chocolate and Bosch holiday music blocks feel like filler.
- Nothing is shoppable: no product, category or online link, so the click has nowhere useful to go for non-store shoppers.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace "Learn more" with an action that matches the offer, such as "Find your store" or "Get my bucket plan". Put the store locator in the hero and tell people to be in line by 5:30 a.m.
- 2. Name the prize in the hero. For example: "First 150 members at every store win a FREE bucket of surprises, including an appliance worth up to $2,000."
- 3. Say how to qualify: add a clear "Not a member? Join free" line and button above the fold.
- 4. Cut the LG and Bosch blocks, or fold them into one line, to shorten the email.
- 5. Add a secondary shoppable row (for example, Black Friday doorbusters) so non-winners have somewhere to click.
- 6. Move the key terms (18+, member, in-store) into one short line near the hero and keep the full legal text in the footer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Nov 27, 6 a.m.: win a free appliance at Lowe's 🎁`
- **Subject Alt B:** `150 members per store win free prizes Friday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Be in line at 6 a.m. on Nov 27. Gold bucket = a free appliance under $2,000.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your bucket could hold a free Blackstone griddle, CRAFTSMAN tools or an EGO blower.`
