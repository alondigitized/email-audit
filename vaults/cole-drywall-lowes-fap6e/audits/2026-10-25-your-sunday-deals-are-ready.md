# Your Sunday Deals are ready 🛒
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-25**
## Executive summary

- This is a thin Sunday Deals send. The hero is a big blue "Sunday Deals / Let the savings roll." block with one clear "Shop now" button. It looks sharp, but it has no offer in it. There's no percentage, no dollar amount and no deadline. A subscriber has to scroll past a gifts banner to reach the only product content.
- The product row is the only part that speaks to a tools buyer. It shows a DEWALT 20V charger and battery kit, a DEWALT XR router and a Klein FLICKBLADE knife. The "Buy One Get 3" and "Free With Purchase" ribbons are tiny, and the product names are cut off ("Lithium-ion ) -"). The best promotional hooks are barely readable.
- The Oct 18 send used the same "Sunday Deals" framing but came with a clear 2x2 grid of offers like 35% off appliances. This one is a weaker version of that. It's clean and on-brand, but it's a brand-awareness template rather than a deal email.

## What's working

- The hero is bold and instantly legible, with one dark "Shop now" button against the blue field.
- The top nav (Appliances, Daily Deals, Bathroom, Outdoor Tools) gives a quick way to jump to a category.
- The tool-focused product trio (DEWALT, Klein) fits a contractor or DIY reader far better than the gifts banner does.
- The layout is clean, with consistent buttons and no broken images or overlapping text.
- The "Deals of the Day" sign-up block is a sensible retention play for a daily-deals brand.

## What's weak

- No concrete offer sits above the fold, so "Let the savings roll" promises savings without naming any.
- The "Buy One Get 3" and "Free With Purchase" ribbons are too small to read at a glance, which buries the real offer.
- Every product button says a generic "Buy now" or "Shop now", with no price, discount or product name in the button.
- The product titles are truncated ("DEWALT 20 -Volt 2 - Pack 8 Ah 8 Ah Lithium-ion ) -"), which looks sloppy.
- The holiday gifts banner competes with the Sunday Deals hero, so the email has two themes and neither is specific.
- "Deals of the Day" appears twice, as a red banner and as a sign-up block, which is redundant.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real offer in the hero, such as "Buy 1 DEWALT battery kit, get up to 3 free", with the deadline "Today only". The ribbon text is the actual hook, so make it the headline.
- 2. Show a price or savings badge on each product tile and make it large. Put the discount in the button copy, for example "Get the router free".
- 3. Drop the gifts banner or move it below the product row. Two heroes dilute the Sunday Deals message.
- 4. Fix the product title truncation, and cut the duplicate "Deals of the Day" blocks to one.
- 5. Vary the Sunday template so it isn't a repeat of Oct 18. Rotate in a different headline offer each week.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Today's Sunday Deals: free router + tool savings`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free DEWALT router with purchase, plus Klein tools and early holiday gift picks.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Ends tonight: battery bundles, free-with-purchase tools and more.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML has a malformed DOCTYPE and about 18 images with no `alt` attribute, most of them ad-impression pixels. Those pixels also put the recipient's email address in the URL in plaintext. Unsubscribe and authentication headers could not be verified from the data provided. The HTML source was truncated, so only the head and the QA findings were reviewed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 34 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so their redirect targets and status codes are unverified.
- **Ad-server pixels:** 16 `https://sli.lowes.com/imp?s=…&p=527979|10252026&lctg=410146703&stpe=pixel|static|default` calls fire on open. Some URLs carry an extra `li_coord…` parameter. They don't count against the QA pass rate, but they add third-party requests and fail if images are blocked.
- **Plaintext email address in the URL:** every `sli.lowes.com/imp` URL includes `e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app`. The recipient address is exposed in query strings, which end up in proxy and server logs and in any forwarded copy of the email.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on content images:**
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg`
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png`

  If these are meaningful images, they need descriptive alt text.
- **Missing alt on pixels:** the `sli.lowes.com/imp` tracking pixels have no `alt`. They should have `alt=""` so screen readers skip them.
- **Malformed DOCTYPE:** `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" "https://www.w3.org/TR/xhtml1/DTD/xhtml1-transitional.dtd">` has no space after `PUBLIC`. The `xmlns` is also set to `https://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml`, but the canonical namespace URI is `http://`. Most clients tolerate both, but the DOCTYPE is invalid.
- **Overlapping media queries:** two `@media` blocks, `(min-width:375px) and (max-width:600px)` and `(max-width:480px)`, both target `.container`, `.drop` and `.photo`. They overlap at 375–480px, and the `!important` rules make the outcome depend on source order.
- **Web fonts:** the `@font-face` rules for Fellix and DIN Next load from `lowescdn.com` and are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, so Outlook is excluded. I couldn't check the fallback font stacks because the source was truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unrendered merge tokens (such as `%%…%%` or `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source or QA data. The only personalization is the recipient address in the impression-pixel URLs (see section 2).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** the QA check did not find the header (WARN).
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found (WARN), so one-click unsubscribe isn't confirmed.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found (WARN), so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- **Caveat:** the QA output says all three may be stripped by the AgentMail relay. Treat these as unverified rather than confirmed failures. The body unsubscribe link and the physical address are likely in the truncated footer and couldn't be checked.
- **Sender domain:** the From address is `lowes@e.lowes.com`, a dedicated subdomain.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
UTM parameters and landing-page URLs could not be evaluated. The 34 links were skipped by the probe and the source is truncated. No findings.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the plaintext recipient email from `sli.lowes.com/imp` URLs, or replace it with an opaque ID or hash.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to `c301e24e-….jpg` and `ad569dc5-….png`, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
3. Fix the DOCTYPE spacing (`PUBLIC "-//W3C…`) and use `http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml` for `xmlns`.
4. Consolidate the overlapping 375–600px and 480px media queries so the cascade is predictable.
5. Re-run the QA against the raw `.eml` with full headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
6. Resolve the 34 skipped tracking links with a redirect-follow check to verify the final landing URLs and UTM parameters.
