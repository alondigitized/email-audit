# Kickstart summer with more points ☀️
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- This is a loyalty-activation email from MyLowe's Rewards — a deliberate pivot from the two discount-led Memorial Day sends in the past 24 hours. Rather than another "45% OFF" headline, it asks loyalty members to activate a 3x Points bonus on outdoor picks. That's a smart lane change: it targets a different motivation (earning, not saving) and theoretically a different segment. The execution is clean — the silver status banner at the top is genuinely personalized, the hero image reads fast, and the "Activate now" CTA is unambiguous.
- The problem is cadence. This is the second Lowe's email landing on May 15 — the earlier send this morning was headlined "Up to 45% OFF." A subscriber who saw both in sequence will likely treat this as noise. The subject ("Kickstart summer with more points ☀️") is also the weakest of the three Memorial Day subjects: it gives you the reward type but not the magnitude or deadline, which kills urgency before the open even happens.
- Verdict: a competent loyalty send with real personalization signals buried under fatigue-inducing frequency.

## What's working

- **Silver status progress banner** at the top is the only genuinely personalized signal in the email — it makes the subscriber feel seen, not batch-blasted.
- **3x Points badge** on the hero is visually distinct and clearly communicates the multiplier at a glance.
- **Free same-day delivery** module is a clean, confident secondary hook — "Yep. Like today today." is the best copy in the email.
- **"Activate now" CTA** is direct and creates a micro-commitment loop; prompting activation is smarter than a generic "Shop now."
- Render is clean — no broken images, overlapping text, or layout gaps.

## What's weak

- **Third Lowe's email in under 36 hours** — cadence fatigue is real and this one arrives second on the same calendar day as the 45% OFF send.
- **No explicit deadline** — "Memorial Day" is implied urgency but the email doesn't say "through May 26" or "ends Monday," which deflates the activation prompt.
- **Hero image is generic** — a grill/patio lifestyle shot identical in feel to the earlier sends; no visual differentiation signals "this is the loyalty email."
- **"More points" in the subject is vague** — a subscriber doesn't know if that's 2x, 3x, or 10x before opening; the multiplier is the hook and it's hidden.
- **Preview text appears truncated or broken** — the render shows what looks like partial copy at the top ("See which on are. Earn on an eligible purchase.") that doesn't complete a coherent sentence.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put the multiplier in the subject.** "3x" is the compelling number — it belongs before the open, not after. The subject as written buries the lead.
- 2. **Add a deadline.** "Activate by Monday" or "Ends Memorial Day" turns a soft loyalty nudge into an actual conversion driver.
- 3. **Differentiate the hero visually from the deals email.** If two emails land on the same day, a subscriber needs an instant visual cue that this is the rewards email. A graphic treatment (e.g., the MyLowe's Rewards badge overlaid prominently on the hero) rather than a lifestyle photo alone would do it.
- 4. **Fix the preview text.** Whatever is leaking there needs to be replaced with a sharp single sentence that complements the subject.
- 5. **Consider suppressing the deals segment from this send** (or vice versa) — two emails on the same day from the same brand is the single largest open/click risk here.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Earn 3x points on outdoor picks this weekend`
- **Subject Alt B:** `3x Points, activated. Your Memorial Day bonus is live`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Activate before Memorial Day ends — no minimum spend required.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stack your summer rewards: 3x points on grills, patio, and more through Monday.`

### 5. Compliance

**[HIGH] No physical mailing address detected.** CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid physical postal address in the email body. Not found in the truncated source or QA scan. This is a statutory requirement, not a best practice.

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header absent from relay capture.** QA notes this may be a relay artifact — AgentMail may not forward the header. Confirm the raw envelope at the MTA. If genuinely absent, major inbox providers (Gmail, Yahoo) will suppress the one-click unsubscribe UI and may reclassify bulk mail.

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent (RFC 8058).** Without this, Gmail's and Yahoo's one-click unsubscribe buttons (required for bulk senders ≥5k/day since February 2024) are not activated. Given Lowe's send volume, this is almost certainly a relay capture gap rather than a real omission — but must be verified against the raw SMTP headers.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All 25 outbound links route through `click.e.lowes.com` — UTM parameters, if any, would be appended at redirect resolution and are not directly inspectable here. Recommend spot-checking two or three resolved destination URLs to confirm `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, and `utm_campaign` are present and consistent with the sending campaign.

The pixel domain `mi.lowes.com` and click domain `click.e.lowes.com` are separate from the asset CDN (`image.e.lowes.com`, `lowescdn.com`) — three distinct hostnames, each a potential single point of failure for tracking fidelity.
