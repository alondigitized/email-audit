# Homepage snapshot · www.lowes.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- This looks like Lowe's leaning hard into Memorial Day weekend, which — good timing, because that's literally when I plan to pull the trigger on the washer we've been putting off. But the first thing I hit is a sign-in popup blocking the whole screen, and that's enough to make me want to close the tab before I've even seen a single deal. The brand still reads solid and familiar, but the execution out of the gate is annoying.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day savings banner at the very top** — "Get your home summer-ready with Memorial Day savings. Shop Now >" lands exactly right. That's why I'm here.
- **Recommended category chips** — "Electric Range" and "Washing Machine" are right there under the modal. That's the shortlist on my project board.
- **Sign-in modal loyalty pitch** — Mentioning Silver Key status and points earning in the same breath as the $5 offer is smart. I already have a MyLowe's account, so seeing the incentive reminds me why I use it.
- **Memorial Day hero below modal** — "Summer's hottest deals start now" with stars and the promise of free same-day delivery on select items is solid once you get past the popup.
- **"Shop Now >" top banner CTA** — clean, low-commitment, easy to tap.

## What's weak

- **The sign-in modal blocking the entire homepage** — I'm trying to shop, not create an account. Hitting this wall right out of the gate is friction I didn't ask for. I've got a MyLowe's card in my wallet and a cart in my head, and you're making me dismiss a popup first.
- **"Grow a beautiful garden with organic"** — that's the first lifestyle section I can see below the hero. I don't grow tomatoes, I hang drywall. That content is dead air to me.
- **No Kobalt or Allen + Roth visibility** — Memorial Day is huge for tools and appliances in my world. I see appliance category chips but zero brand callouts for the lines I'm loyal to.
- **Free same-day delivery fine print (†)** — same thing as the asterisks: the offer is there but the qualifier undercuts the confidence.
- **"Sign In" as the primary CTA** — it's not a shopping action. It's a barrier. The real CTA (shop Memorial Day deals) is buried below it.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the blocking modal with a persistent banner or slide-in** — put the loyalty pitch in the top utility bar or a non-intrusive strip, not a full overlay that hijacks the session.
- **Swap the gardening lifestyle section** for a tools/appliances Memorial Day callout featuring Kobalt or a big-ticket appliance with a price badge — that's the trade that matters for my demo this weekend.
- **Surface a concrete dollar-off or percent-off on appliances above the fold** — "Up to 40% off appliances — Memorial Day only" with a real deadline beats the generic "deals start now."
- **Add a MyLowe's Rewards balance or "your points" nudge for signed-in users** — if I'm already in the account, show me what I've earned and how close I am to a reward instead of asking me to sign in again.
