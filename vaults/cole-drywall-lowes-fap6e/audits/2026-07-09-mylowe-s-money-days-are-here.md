# 💰MyLowe's Money Days are HERE💰
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-09**
## Executive summary

- This is the launch-day execution email for MyLowe's Money Days (July 9–12) — and it earns that occasion. The blue hero lands with authority, the dates are prominent, and the loyalty-rewards mechanic is front-and-center rather than buried in the footer. After a solid teaser on June 30 (9/10) and a capable reminder on July 6 (8/10), this third email in the sequence does what launch-day emails are supposed to do: it signals "it's time, go shop."
- The weakness is the rewards mechanic itself — the points structure (some kind of multiplier on $45+ purchases) requires reading fine print to decode. A subscriber who missed the prior two sends lands here with no quick answer to "what do I actually get?" The "$399" mower and outdoor product deals in the lower body are doing more persuasive work than the hero, which ironically undersells itself.
- Overall: a competent launch-day send that converts subscribers who were already primed by the sequence. Cold openers or lapsed members will bounce before they understand the offer.

## What's working

- **Event dates (July 9–12) are in the hero** — no ambiguity about timing, zero scroll required.
- **"MyLowe's Money Days" brand consistency** — three sends with the same name builds recognition; this one benefits from that priming.
- **Product deals section ("Shop deals. Get Money.")** delivers specific price points ($399 mower, $100 savings) that the hero alone doesn't.
- **In-store event callout** is a differentiator — omnichannel signals add credibility over a pure e-commerce email.
- **Visual hierarchy is clean** — blue hero → white body → deal cards → footer, no layout chaos.

## What's weak

- **The points multiplier mechanic is opaque** — "EXTRA" points on $45+ is only legible if you already know how MyLowe's points work; the reward value isn't visible at a glance.
- **"Shop now" CTAs are fully generic** — three identical "Shop now" buttons with no distinction by category or offer type.
- **The in-store event block eats screen real estate** with partner logos that are too small to read; it adds clutter without adding decision-making context.
- **No urgency ratchet** — "July 9–12" is right, but no "Ends Saturday" or countdown language to push action today vs. tomorrow.
- **Hero copy doesn't surface a dollar figure** — the July 3rd "50% OFF!" send (scored 8/10) led with a hard number; this one doesn't, making the hero feel like a brand announcement rather than an offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead the hero with a dollar or percent hook.** "Earn 5× Points This Weekend" or "Up to $200 Back in Rewards" is scannable in 1 second. Right now the hero says an event is happening, not why I should care.
- 2. **Rewrite "Shop now" into offer-specific CTAs.** "Earn Points on Outdoor" / "Shop Deals, Get Money" / "See All Money Days Picks" — each button should describe its destination.
- 3. **Kill or compress the in-store block.** The partner logos are unreadable; reduce it to one line ("In-store events this weekend — find your store →") and reclaim the space for a third deal card.
- 4. **Add a deadline anchor to the first "Shop now" CTA.** "Shop now — ends Sat." is four words but converts skeptics.
- 5. **Preheader should carry the reward value, not repeat the subject.** If the subject is "Money Days are HERE," the preheader should say exactly what members earn.
- **Subject Alt A:** `💰 Earn 5× Points This Weekend Only · MyLowe's Money Days`
- **Subject Alt B:** `It starts today: MyLowe's Money Days (Jul 9–12)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Members earn extra points on every $45+ purchase — shop deals, get money back.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Big savings + bonus points on outdoor, appliances, and more. Ends Saturday.`
