# Summer Storage Event starts NOW🤩
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-29**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuine change of subject for Lowe's — after a week-plus of MyLowe's Money Days and Closeout Event repeats (per the 7/24–7/27 brand history), "Summer Storage Event" gives subscribers a new reason to open rather than the fourth variation of the same loyalty push. The hero does its job: a clean shed product photo, a bold "$1,000" number, and a single white CTA button land the eye exactly where it should on open.
- The problem is everything after the hero. By the third screen you've hit garage organization (30% off), tool storage (30% off), a Closeout Event banner, a 0% APR mower financing pitch, and a Deals of the Day signup — five distinct asks stacked with five distinct "Shop now"/"Buy now" buttons. None of them are wrong individually, but together they turn a focused storage-event email into a general circular, and the generic button copy never differentiates what you're actually clicking into.
- Net: strong top-of-funnel hook, weak follow-through. It'll open fine off a fresh subject line, but click intent gets diffused by mid-scroll.

## What's working

- Hero banner is unmissable: green "$1,000" badge, clear product photo of the actual shed, single CTA — no competing elements.
- Visual hierarchy is textbook: header → hero offer → deal grid → sub-categories → financing → footer, no crowding at the top.
- Product deal cards (DEWALT, Craftsman, Greenworks) show price, strikethrough/was-price, and dollar savings together — the clearest pricing info in the whole email.
- No render bugs — every image loads, no overlapping text, no lorem ipsum.
- Genuine topic departure from the recent Money Days/Closeout drumbeat, which should help this stand out in-inbox against recent Lowe's sends.

## What's weak

- Every CTA button says "Shop now" or "Buy now" with zero differentiation — six identical-looking buttons across the email give no signal about what's behind each one.
- The headline "$1,000" offer never states which product it applies to or what "select storage solutions" actually means — it reads as a ceiling number designed to look bigger than the real average discount.
- No deadline anywhere for the storage event itself — "starts NOW" implies urgency but never says when it ends, so there's no reason to act today vs. next week.
- The email sprawls into four unrelated offers (garage/tool storage, Closeout Event, credit financing, Deals of the Day signup) that dilute the storage-event focus established in the hero.
- "View email as a webpage" sits above the fold before any brand content — minor friction on open.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real deadline on the storage event ("Ends Sunday," "3 days only") next to the $1,000 badge — right now there's launch energy but no reason to click before browsing later.
- 2. Replace generic "Shop now"/"Buy now" with specific CTA copy per section ("Shop sheds," "Shop tool chests," "See mower financing") so each button earns its own click intent.
- 3. Cut the email after the storage/garage/tool-storage block and closeout banner; spin the 0% APR mower financing and Deals of the Day signup into separate sends — this email is trying to be three campaigns at once.
- 4. Anchor the "$1,000" claim to a real reference point ("on our largest storage shed, reg. $2,499") so the offer doesn't read as a bait ceiling.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Storage sheds up to $1,000 off — starts today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New: Summer Storage Event is live`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sheds, garage systems, and tool storage — all on sale this week.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your outdoor storage upgrade just got $1,000 cheaper.`
