# Up to 50% OFF! Black Friday deals on appliances
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a near-repeat of the 2026-11-04 send, "Up to 50% OFF select appliances + FREE delivery 🥳." It has the same ALL-IN Appliance Offer block, the same 50% off hero and the same free delivery. The subject is clear and the hero delivers on it. The big red "Get up to 50% off select major appliances" block is the first thing the eye lands on.
- The problem is everything underneath. A vacuum offer at 40% off, a KitchenAid 0% APR credit deal, a second Black Friday banner, a product row, a Samsung ad and a Deals of the Day footer all compete with the hero. The offer is also conditional: the "$896 or more" spend threshold is in the hero, but the main exclusions sit in the long fine print. That makes the 50% feel less honest than the headline does.
- Overall it's a solid appliance email. It is also the fourth Black Friday-flavored Lowe's send in under a week, and it adds little that is new for a subscriber who saw the 11/4 version.

## What's working

- The subject and hero match. "Up to 50% OFF" is the first thing in both, in huge white type on red.
- Free delivery, installation and haul-away are stacked as green chips under the hero. They make the offer feel concrete.
- The "Shop now" button sits right next to the appliance photo and is clearly visible.
- The My Lowe's Rewards banner ("FREE same-day delivery") and the "Save up to an additional $250" laundry line give members a reason to act.
- The nav bar (Appliances, Daily Deals, Bathroom, Outdoor Tools) is clean and on-brand.

## What's weak

- The spend threshold is tucked into small text under the chips: "when you spend $896 or more." The "50% off" can't be read as a flat discount, so it feels like bait.
- Too many competing blocks. The vacuum 40% off, the credit deal, the second red Black Friday banner and the Samsung ad dilute the single appliance message.
- The "Curated with you in mind" product row is generic. It shows a DEWALT battery, a toilet and a storage tote, none of which relate to appliances.
- The footer fine print is a very long block of legal text. The Frigidaire and Samsung ad blocks are marked "Sponsored" and break up the page.
- This is near-duplicate creative from 11/4, so the send feels stale.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email down to the appliance offer and a Rewards line. Move vacuums, credit and tools to a separate send.
- 2. Put the spend threshold in the hero next to "50% off," for example "50% off when you spend $896+." That builds trust.
- 3. Replace the generic product row with 3 specific appliances showing the sale price and a "Save $X" line.
- 4. Add a real deadline in the hero. The fine print says 12/2/26, but the visible urgency is "Limited Time" and nothing more.
- 5. Change the creative so it differs from the 11/4 send. For example, lead with a specific hero product like the Samsung fridge at $1,799.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Fridges, washers, ranges: 50% off ends 12/2`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $896+ and get free delivery, install and haul-away.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus up to $250 more off select laundry for Rewards members.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML has a malformed DOCTYPE and 17 images with no alt text, 15 of them third-party ad-impression pixels that carry the recipient's email address in the URL. The header-based compliance checks (List-Unsubscribe, SPF/DKIM) could not be verified, because the AgentMail relay does not expose those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Recipient PII in pixel URLs:** All 15 `https://sli.lowes.com/imp?...` pixels include `e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app` in plaintext. For example: `imp?s=127768300&e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app&p=529577&lctg=410146703&stpe=pixel`. The address is exposed in image-request logs and to any proxy or caching layer.
- **Empty parameter:** `li_coord=` has no value in `s=2019286` and `s=1090834`. Each of these appears twice, with `stpe=default`. This looks like an unresolved merge or coordinate value.
- **Duplicate impressions:** `s=2019286` and `s=1090834` each appear twice, which could double-count impressions.
- **Unverified links:** 38 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe as click-redirect domains. Their destinations, status codes and redirect chains were not checked, so I can't confirm or rule out broken links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed DOCTYPE:** `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" ...>` has no space after `PUBLIC`. Strict parsers may reject it, and clients that don't will probably fall back to quirks mode.
- **XHTML namespace:** `xmlns="https://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` uses `https`. The canonical namespace is `http://`. This is nonstandard but usually harmless.
- **Missing alt on content images (2):**
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg`
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png`

  With images blocked or a screen reader, these are silent or announced by filename. I can't tell from the truncated source whether they are decorative.
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels (15):** The `sli.lowes.com/imp` images should carry `alt=""` and `role="presentation"` or `width="1" height="1"`. This is a minor issue.
- **Web fonts:** The `@font-face` fonts (Fellix, DIN Next) are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, so Outlook is excluded. The source is truncated, so I can't confirm the fallback font stacks.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved tokens visible** in the available source, apart from the empty `li_coord=` above, which I can't confirm is a merge token.
- The recipient address is correctly populated in `e=`, but see section 2 for the exposure.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Not found. The QA check notes the relay may strip them, so this is **unverified**, not a confirmed violation. Check the raw message at the source or with a seed inbox.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found, with the same relay caveat. SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `lowes@e.lowes.com` is unknown.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** Not visible in the truncated HTML, so I can't assess them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Can't be assessed. All 38 click links are redirect-wrapped and were skipped, so the final destination URLs and UTM parameters are unknown. The truncated source shows no direct `lowes.com` hrefs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the DOCTYPE to `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" "http://www.w3.org/TR/xhtml1/DTD/xhtml1-transitional.dtd">`, and use `http://` in the `xmlns` value.
2. Add `alt` text to the two content images (`alt=""` if decorative). Add `alt=""` to the 15 `imp` pixels.
3. Stop putting the raw email address in the `e=` parameter of the pixel URLs. Use a hashed or opaque ID instead.
4. Check the `li_coord=` empty value and the duplicate pixel IDs (`s=2019286`, `s=1090834`) in the ad-server template.
5. Capture the raw message headers to verify List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post and Authentication-Results.
6. Resolve a sample of the 38 redirect links with a redirect-following probe to confirm destinations and UTM carry-through.
