# Up to 45% OFF and FREE delivery on appliances 😍
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- This one delivers what the subject promises — "ALL-IN Appliance Offer, Up to 45% off select major appliances" is the hero, front and center, immediately below the Labor Day banner. No bait-and-switch gap between subject and hero like the 8/30 send had. It stacks the offer well: 45% off, FREE Delivery, FREE Installation/Haul-Away, a 2-Year Protection Plan, all in one tight blue block with a clean "Shop now" CTA.
- Where it falls apart is everything after the fold. A second full appliance sale (bath/kitchen "flash sale," up to 40%+30%), then an LG kitchen suite deal, a ceiling fan deal, flooring services, a credit card pitch, "Extra Big Deals," a curated grab-bag (a storage tote, cleaner, closet rod), a Frigidaire pizza-oven callout, and a Deals-of-the-Day signup — nine distinct offers in one email. It's the Labor Day dump-everything-in approach, and it dilutes an otherwise strong lead.
- Compared to the 8/29 send (7/10, cleanest single-offer appliance push) and 8/30 (6/10, subject/hero mismatch), this lands in between: the hero finally matches the subject line again, but the sprawl below it drags it down. Solid open, mediocre click-through past the first CTA.

## What's working

- Hero offer matches subject exactly — 45% off appliances + free delivery, no bait-and-switch.
- Clear visual hierarchy at the top: bold "45% off" numeral, blue background, easy-to-spot "Shop now" button.
- Concrete tiered savings table for the flash sale ($999–$2,499 = 10% off, up to $5,000+ = 30% off) — specific and legible.
- Product-level proof point: "$4,516 LG kitchen suite, Save $2,660" gives a real anchor price, not just a vague percentage.
- No render bugs — images, buttons, and text blocks all display cleanly.

## What's weak

- Nine separate offers crammed into one email (appliances, bath/kitchen, LG suite, ceiling fans, flooring, credit card, "extra big deals," curated grab-bag, Frigidaire ovens) — no single clear next action after the hero.
- The "Curated with you in mind" section (storage tote, cleaner, closet rod) reads generic and unrelated to the appliance offer that got the open.
- Repeated "Shop now" CTA text across at least five different blocks — no differentiation on what you're actually shopping for.
- This is now three appliance-themed sends since 8/29 (8/29, 8/30, today) — cadence fatigue risk for anyone who already opened one of the earlier two.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email in half — lead with the appliance hero and one supporting deal (LG suite makes sense as it reinforces the appliance category), and move flooring, credit card, curated grab-bag, and Deals-of-the-Day into a separate send. One offer category per email converts better than nine competing for attention.
- 2. Vary CTA copy per block ("Shop appliances," "See flash sale," "Compare fans") instead of five identical "Shop now" buttons — helps eye-tracking and click intent.
- 3. Drop or relocate the unrelated "Curated with you in mind" grab-bag (tote/cleaner/closet rod) — it has nothing to do with the appliance/Labor Day theme and weakens brand coherence.
- 4. If flash-sale and appliance offers must coexist, pick one as primary and demote the other to a smaller module — right now they're visually equal-weight, which splits attention right after the open.
