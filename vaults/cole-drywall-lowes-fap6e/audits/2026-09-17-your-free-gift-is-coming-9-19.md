The hero is clean and unblurred — FREE in a bright lime block, a real product photo, a clear "Learn more" button, and a credible date/time ("Starting 10 a.m. on September 19") — genuinely well-executed on craft.

- Hero image is sharp, unblurred, and the FREE badge + product shot land immediately without scrolling.

- Three distinct member perks are cleanly separated with their own headline, image, and CTA (ice mold, candle, concert tickets) — no visual clutter or overlapping elements.

## What's weak

- No home-improvement offer anywhere in the email — a stark departure from every recent send (appliances, patio, power equipment) with no discount, no product category a typical Lowe's shopper came for.
- The candle and concert-ticket blocks use generic stock photography (candle on a table, tour poster) that could belong to any brand — nothing ties them visually to Lowe's beyond the navy color blocking.
