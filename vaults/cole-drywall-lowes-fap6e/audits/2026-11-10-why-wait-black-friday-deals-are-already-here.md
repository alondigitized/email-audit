# Why wait? Black Friday deals are already here🙌
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a tool-heavy Black Friday send with a good middle and a weak top. The subject promises "Black Friday deals already here." The hero says "Say hello to the season of BIG savings" and gives no number, no product and no deadline. The concrete offers sit below it: DEWALT 20V MAX at $99, CRAFTSMAN V20 at $59, Kobalt 24V at $49, and "Save up to $90" on select power tools. Those price tiles are strong, but the reader has to scroll past a vague red hero and a sponsored LG ad to reach them.
- The email is also cluttered. It has a free same-day delivery banner, a Black Friday hero, a sponsored LG ad, a "Limited Time Deals" strip, tool tiles, a gift section, a credit card block, a product row, Deals of the Day and a newsletter sign-up. That is roughly ten blocks and ten competing CTAs. Compared with the 11/06 tool send ("Your tool collection could use a +2 👌"), which had one clear "2 FREE" hero, this one is less focused. It is also the fifth Black Friday-tagged send in about a week. The brand is trusted and the tool pricing is real, but the email does not choose a lead.
- Judgement: a decent, deal-rich send with a vague hero and too many blocks. Lead with the $49 / $59 / $99 tool prices and cut the rest.

## What's working

- The price tiles are concrete: $99 DEWALT, $59 CRAFTSMAN and $49 Kobalt, each with a large numeral and a product photo.
- "Gift-worthy tools, priced under $100" is a clear banner with a real price ceiling.
- The "FREE same-day delivery" strip at the top is a credible perk, and the My Lowe's Rewards badge ties it to membership.
- The tool photography is clean, and the blue and red blocks give the layout a steady rhythm.
- The footer has a sensible fine-print line on the $25 minimum and the loyalty requirement.

## What's weak

- The hero copy, "Say hello to the season of BIG savings," has no offer, no product and no date. It spends the prime screen space on branding.
- The sponsored LG ad sits between the hero and the real deals. It interrupts the flow and adds an unrelated appliance message.
- There are about ten CTAs. Many are generic "Shop now" buttons repeated on every tile.
- The credit cardmember block shows "Offer valid 1/29/26 – 1/29/27" and tiny disclaimer text, which looks stale and is hard to read. The date range is also odd for a November send.
- The product row at the bottom has awkward truncated names ("DEWALT 12 -Volt 2 -Pack Battery/charger ( 3 Ah 5 Ah ) - DCB135C", "( 108.0 -"). It also has no prices, so "Buy now" gives the shopper nothing to go on.
- The "Save up to $90" tile does not say which tools or what the price is.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the hero with the best tool offer: "DEWALT 20V MAX kit, $99. Black Friday starts now." Show the product and put a deadline on it.
- 2. Move the LG sponsored ad below the tool tiles, or drop it from this send.
- 3. Cut the secondary blocks to the tool tiles plus one gift or credit block. Drop the Deals of the Day banner or merge it into the footer.
- 4. Give each CTA a specific label, such as "Shop DEWALT $99" or "Shop Kobalt $49".
- 5. Add prices, star ratings and clean product names to the bottom product row.
- 6. Check the credit offer's dates and either update them or pull the block.
- **Subject Alt A:** `DEWALT kits $99, Kobalt $49: early Black Friday tools`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Power tools under $100 start today 🔧`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `DEWALT $99, CRAFTSMAN $59, Kobalt $49. Plus free same-day delivery.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Early Black Friday prices on tools, ready to ship today.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit: Lowe's "Why wait? Black Friday deals are already here🙌"

### 1. Technical Summary
The markup is a conventional table-based XHTML Transitional template with MSO conditionals and responsive media queries. The confirmed problems are missing `alt` attributes on 17 images and the recipient's email address sent in plain text in tracking-pixel URLs. The header and authentication checks couldn't be verified from the data supplied.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 41 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so redirect health and destination are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **PII in pixel URLs:** All 15 `https://sli.lowes.com/imp?s=…` impression pixels carry the recipient address in the query string, for example `e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app`. The address is unhashed and unencoded. It can leak through proxy and CDN logs, and through a Referer header if the pixel redirects.
- **Repeated pixel IDs:** `s=2019286` and `s=1090834` each appear twice, with the `li_coord` variants. If both fire on load, impressions could be double-counted. This is a possible issue only, because the source is truncated.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Content images without alt text (2):**
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg`
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png`
  
  With images blocked or a screen reader, these are unannounced or read as filenames.
- **Tracking pixels without alt text (15):** The `sli.lowes.com/imp` images have no `alt`. They should carry `alt=""` and explicit 1×1 dimensions so assistive tech skips them.
- **Malformed DOCTYPE:** `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN"` has no space after `PUBLIC`. Most clients tolerate this, but it's invalid.
- **Web fonts:** Fellix and DIN Next are loaded from `lowescdn.com` inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` blocks, so Outlook is excluded as intended. Nothing further is flagged.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source. The recipient address is resolved in the pixel URLs.

### 5. Compliance
- **Missing headers:** The QA run did not find `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058). The QA tool itself notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so absence is not confirmed on the sender side. For a bulk sender, check the raw message at the origin. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **SPF/DKIM/DMARC:** Not assessable, since `Authentication-Results` wasn't captured.
- **Footer:** The in-body unsubscribe link and postal address are not visible in the truncated source, so CAN-SPAM footer elements are unverified.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. All 41 links are tracking redirects that were not resolved, and the truncated HTML doesn't show final landing URLs or UTM parameters. No mismatch is confirmed.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the two `image.e.lowes.com` content images.
2. Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` to all `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixels.
3. Stop putting the raw email address in the `e=` parameter. Use a hashed or opaque subscriber ID.
4. Fix the DOCTYPE spacing: `PUBLIC "-//W3C//…`.
5. Re-run QA against the raw MIME, not the relay copy, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
6. Resolve the skipped tracking links with a redirect follower to check final URLs and UTM parameters.
7. Review the full HTML, not the truncated copy, for the footer unsubscribe link and physical address.
