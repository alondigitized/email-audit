# Up to 50% OFF! Sunday Deals are on 👉
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a busy, kitchen-sink Lowe's send — a generic "Sunday Deals" hero followed by a four-tile grid (free power tool items, 50% off patio, 45% off appliances, 45% off vanities), then a Halloween teaser, a charity donation ask, a "curated for you" product strip, and a Deals-of-the-Day banner. It's more organized than the leaf-scattered blue heroes from the 8/31–9/7 Labor Day run, and the grid layout is legible with clear discount callouts, but the hero itself is content-free — "More savings for your home, yard and beyond" tells you nothing a subscriber can act on immediately.
- The real offers only appear once you scroll past the hero, which undercuts the "50% off" promise in the subject line — a mobile reader sees a blue gradient box and a "Shop now" button with no percentage or product in view. Compare this to the 9/10 appliance send, which led with the actual $1,500 appliance deal above the fold; this one buries its best specifics one screen down.
- Net: functional, cleanly hierarchied grid, but a weak hero and a scattered, unfocused body (four categories + Halloween + charity + more products) dilute any single reason to click. It lands mid-pack, consistent with this brand's typical promo cadence.

## What's working

- The four-tile grid clearly states discrete offers ("Get up to 50% off," "45% off," "FREE items") with bold black percentage numbers that are easy to scan.
- "FREE Delivery" and "For members" badges add concrete value/loyalty signals under two of the tiles.
- No rendering bugs — images, buttons, and text all display cleanly with no overlap or broken assets.
- The nav bar (Labor Day, Appliances, Daily Deals, Outdoor Tools) gives quick categorical wayfinding for scanners.

## What's weak

- The hero has no discount, product, or urgency — just "Sunday Deals" and vague copy, wasting the most valuable visual real estate in the email.
- Subject promises "Up to 50% OFF" but that number is invisible until the second screen — a mobile-first reader has to scroll past the hero to find it.
- The email tries to do too much: appliances, patio, vanities, power tools, Halloween decor, a charity donation ask, and more curated products all in one send — no single dominant CTA.
- The Halloween banner and charity/donation block feel like unrelated inserts breaking the "Sunday Deals" promotional flow.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the actual "Up to 50% off patio furniture" (or whatever the strongest deal is) directly in the hero with the percentage visible, matching what the subject promises — don't make readers scroll to find it.
- 2. Cut the email down to 2 offers max in the primary fold; move Halloween, charity, and "curated for you" content to a secondary section or a separate send.
- 3. Make the CTA copy specific per tile (e.g., "Shop patio," "Shop appliances") instead of repeating generic "Shop now" four times.
- 4. Tighten the subject to reference a concrete category so open rate benefits from specificity, not just a vague "Sunday Deals" framing.
- **Subject Alt A:** `50% off patio furniture — Sunday only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Sunday Deals: up to 45% off appliances + more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus free delivery on major appliances over $999`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Patio, appliances, vanities — all on sale today`
