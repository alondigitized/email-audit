# Sunday Deals 🎁 Up to 65% OFF smart home products
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-27**
## Executive summary

- This is Lowe's best-structured send of the run by a clean margin, and it beats 9/23's "FREE tool" email on stacking depth. The hero delivers three real, hard-numbered offers in immediate succession — 65% off Ring/Blink, 50% off patio, and $120 off power tools — with a real Ring doorbell camera set and a real DeWalt tool shown, not stock lifestyle filler. The "Curated with you in mind" personalized product row (Dremel battery, Mapesil grout, Kobalt tool chest) is a genuinely differentiated touch none of the last five sends had.
- The subject line undersells it though — "Sunday Deals 🎁 Up to 65% OFF smart home products" leads with the weakest of the three offers structurally (the patio and tool offers are equally prominent in the render) and the preview text just repeats more offers rather than adding anything new. Compare to 9/23's tight, single-offer BOGO subject — this one is trying to summarize a busy email in one line and doesn't quite land the single sharpest hook.
- Overall this is a strong, well-organized deals email let down by an unfocused subject/preview pairing. Judgment: 7/10.

## What's working

- Three distinct hard-numbered offers (65% off, 50% off, up to $120) each paired with a real product photo and its own "Shop now" CTA.
- "Curated with you in mind" section adds personalized product recommendations with individual "Buy now" buttons — a rare touch of segmentation in this brand's recent history.
- Clean nav bar and category breadcrumbs (Appliances, Daily Deals, Patio, Outdoor Tools) orient the reader immediately.
- No render bugs — all images load, text is legible, hierarchy flows top to bottom without overlap.

## What's weak

- Subject picks only the smart-home stat and ignores the equally strong patio and $120 tool offers — undersells the email's actual best asset (having three offers).
- The "That's all... Also get up to 50% off patio furniture, power tools and more" line sits oddly above the logo, reading like leaked preheader text rather than intentional copy.
- Hero banner ("Sunday Deals — BIG savings for a great start to the week") is generic gradient-blue filler with zero number attached, echoing 9/20's laziest send before the real offers even start.
- "Deals of the Day" and email-signup blocks stacked back-to-back near the bottom feels like padding after the strong upper section.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rewrite the subject to lead with the strongest combined hook rather than picking one category — e.g., mention the multi-category stack directly, since that's the actual differentiator over past sends.
- 2. Cut or relocate the "That's not all..." line — it's currently misplaced above the header and reads as a bug, not a feature.
- 3. Replace the generic "BIG savings for a great start to the week" hero copy with a number — even just restating "Up to 65% off" in the hero would remove a dead beat before the reader reaches the real content.
- 4. Trim the bottom stack (Deals of the Day banner + email signup) to one block — two calls to subscribe to more deals back to back dilutes the send.
