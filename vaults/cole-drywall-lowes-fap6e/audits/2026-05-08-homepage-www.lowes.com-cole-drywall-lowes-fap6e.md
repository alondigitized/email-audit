# Homepage snapshot · www.lowes.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- First thing I see when I open the app is a sign-in modal blocking half the screen — not ideal when I just want to check if my drywall mud is on sale. Once I mentally push past that, Lowe's is running a Flash Sale on appliances and a Mother's Day gift push. Neither one is what I came for, but at least the appliance deal has a hard deadline on it, and the Flash Sale banner is hard to miss.

## What's working

- **Flash Sale banner with hard deadline ("Offers end 5/10/26")** — I actually respond to time-bounded deals. Even though it's appliances, the urgency format is exactly how I shop Black Friday. Credible date, not vague "while supplies last."
- **Sign-in modal perks list** — "$5 off when you join," earn points, free Silver Key shipping. Three distinct hooks stacked cleanly. If I weren't already a MyLowe's member, this would move me.
- **Blue "Sign In" CTA button** — high contrast, full-width, impossible to miss. Good mobile execution.
- **"Recommended for You" pill bar** — Bathroom Vanity, Tool Box, Bathroom Vanity with S[omething] suggests the system is trying to personalize. Tool Box is in my orbit.

## What's weak

- **Sign-in modal blocks the homepage on load** — I'm already a MyLowe's Rewards + Lowe's Advantage card holder. This modal treating me like a new visitor is frustrating. I want to get in, not re-pitch me.
- **Appliance Flash Sale hero is off-target** — I'm mid-basement-finish. I need drywall compound, joint tape, 1/2" screws. A washing machine deal doesn't move me right now.
- **Mother's Day gift module** — "Find the perfect gift for Mom" with a little girl in a dress. Zero relevance to what I'm doing this weekend. It's filler for my use case.
- **No above-the-fold navigation to my categories** — I can see "Shop All" and "Burba[nks?]" text partially visible but no quick tap to Building Materials, Drywall, or Tools without dismissing the modal first.
- **No loyalty balance or project status** — If I'm logged in, show me my points. If I'm not, fine — but don't block the whole screen for it.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the sign-in modal for returning app sessions** — if I have the Lowe's app and I've logged in before, skip the modal. Straight to the homepage. Existing customers with Advantage cards shouldn't get the new-user pitch every visit.
- **Swap "Appliance Flash Sale" hero for a rotating builder/DIY offer** — run an A/B where users with past drywall/lumber/tools purchase history see a "Weekend Project Deals" hero instead. The deadline urgency works; just point it at the right category.
- **Surface a "My Project Materials" or "Buy Again" rail above the fold** — even a simple "You've bought these before — on sale now" strip would hook mid-project shoppers like me immediately.
- **Add a "Building Materials" or "Drywall + Flooring" quick-shop chip** to the pill bar visible without scrolling — right now it shows Bathroom Vanity twice and a Tool Box. Swap one vanity for a Lumber/Drywall chip.
