# Homepage snapshot · www.lowes.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- First thing I get is a sign-in pop-up blocking half the page — that's my store and I already have the Advantage card, so starting with a modal nag is annoying. Once I look past it, Lowe's is clearly running a Memorial Day push, which is exactly when I'm looking to grab an appliance or two for the basement build-out. Brand feels familiar and seasonal, but the pop-up is doing its best to make me bounce before I even see what's on sale.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day banner timing** — "Summer's hottest deals start now" lands right when I'm actually shopping for appliances. The star-flanked "Memorial Day" treatment is hard to miss.
- **Appliance category pills** — "Electric Range" and "Washing Machine" are visible in the Recommended row. Those two categories are exactly why I default to Lowe's over Home Depot during holiday weekends.
- **Free same-day delivery callout** — Mid-project, I need stuff fast. That line hits even if the fine print limits it.
- **Sign-in modal loyalty list** — Silver Key free shipping and points earning are things I care about. At least they're spelled out clearly.

## What's weak

- **Forced sign-in modal on arrival** — I didn't ask for this. I want to see the deals, not a recruitment pitch. As a card-carrying member I shouldn't be seeing a "create an account" modal.
- **"Grow a beautiful garden with organic"** — The first section I see below the hero is garden/organic content. I'm in a basement hanging drywall. This is noise.
- **"Shop All" CTA is too broad** — Neither CTA on the hero goes anywhere specific. One tap should take me to appliances or the Memorial Day deal page, not a catch-all.
- **No rewards balance or personalized hook** — I have a MyLowe's account with history. Nothing on this page acknowledges that. No "Welcome back, Cole" or points balance, just a cold modal.
- **Asterisk overload** — $5 off*, Silver Key status*, same-day delivery† — every single offer has a qualifier. Starts to feel like bait.

## Recommendations

- **Detect logged-in users and kill the sign-in modal** — If I'm already signed in (or cookie-recognized), show me my points balance and a personalized "Back to your project?" hook instead of a recruitment overlay.
- **Swap the garden hero for an appliances/building-materials Memorial Day module** — Lowe's knows Memorial Day is appliance season; lead with that. Show a washer-dryer stack or a tool combo deal, not raised beds.
- **Make the "Shop All" CTA specific for the holiday** — "Shop Memorial Day Appliance Deals" is one tap; "Shop All" is a shrug. That one copy change alone could lift taps.
- **Add a timer or "ends Sunday" badge to the Memorial Day banner** — No deadline = no urgency. A simple "Ends May 26" label would push me to act today instead of coming back tomorrow and forgetting.
