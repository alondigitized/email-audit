# Thanksgiving greetings from our CEO, Marvin Ellison
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- This is a pure goodwill send, and a good one for what it is. It's a plain CEO letter with no offer, no product and no hero image. It lands on Nov 25, the day before Thanksgiving, after a week of Lowe's Black Friday promotions. After the Nov 19–23 run of "Up to 2 FREE power tools," "25% OFF pre-lit trees" and "Up to 50% OFF + FREE delivery," a quiet, human note stands out.
- For a DIY-minded subscriber like Cole, there's nothing to act on. The one useful fact is that stores close Thanksgiving Day and reopen Friday, and it's buried in the third paragraph. The nav bar (Black Friday, Tools, Daily Deals, Appliances) is the only commercial hook, and it sits above a wall of text.
- Judgement: well executed as brand-building, and weak as a revenue driver. It's fine as a one-off. A subscriber who got six deal emails in six days will probably read it, not click, and move on.

## What's working

- The tone is warm and sincere, and the signed letter with the CEO's signature feels personal.
- The sender is trusted and the subject is honest about what the email is, so there's no bait-and-switch.
- It's clean and fast to read, with no render bugs and good contrast in navy on white.
- The timing is good: the day before Thanksgiving, with store-closure information that is actually useful.
- It gives subscribers a break from the promo cadence, which helps brand trust.
- The nav bar keeps the Black Friday, Tools and Appliances paths one tap away.

## What's weak

- There is no offer and no CTA button. The body links to nothing beyond the nav bar.
- The store-closure and reopening detail is buried mid-letter instead of being called out.
- The text is long for mobile and runs about 200 words in a single column.
- The decorative ornaments and gift boxes are generic and add little.
- The "We Help. You Save." footer banner is the only brand-value line, and it appears after the letter instead of leading into a next step.
- Nothing is tailored to a drywall or DIY persona.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add one soft CTA below the signature, such as a "Shop Black Friday deals starting Friday" button. This is the single biggest lift, since right now the email has no conversion path.
- 2. Pull the key fact into a callout at the top: "Stores closed Thursday, open Friday at [time]." This makes it useful, not just warm.
- 3. Trim the letter by about a third so it reads in one mobile screen.
- 4. Add a small row of Friday doorbusters, or a "preview Black Friday" link, under the sign-off.
- 5. Replace the generic illustrations with a Lowe's associate or store photo for more authenticity.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Lowe's stores closed Thursday, open Friday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `A thank-you from Lowe's (and Friday's hours)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stores reopen Friday. Lowes.com is open all day, every day.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Thank you for trusting us with your projects this year.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML is mostly standard ESP output (Lowe's via `e.lowes.com`, ExactTarget-style `image.e.lowes.com/lib/...` assets). Confirmed issues are two images with no alt text and a malformed DOCTYPE. The header-level compliance and authentication warnings come from the capture pipeline, so they can't be confirmed as defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** The QA run skipped the HTTP probe on all 20 tracking links because they are click-redirect domains, so none were checked. It reports 0 failures, but that only means they weren't tested.
- The HTML was truncated before the body, so I can't verify the final landing URLs or the unsubscribe and preference links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed, 2 images):**
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg`
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png`
  - Either image may be decorative, but a CEO-greeting email likely has a portrait or banner that needs alt text. With images blocked, the content is lost, and screen readers will read out the filename.
- **Malformed DOCTYPE (source-confirmed):** `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" ...>` has no space between `PUBLIC` and the quoted FPI.
  - The DTD URL and `xmlns` use `https://www.w3.org/...`. The canonical identifiers use `http://`.
  - Most clients parse this leniently, so the impact is low. Strict parsers may fall back to quirks mode.
- **Positives:** viewport meta, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, and `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` resets are present. Mobile breakpoints are at 480px and 375–600px. Web fonts are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, so Outlook won't load them.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered tokens such as `%%...%%`, `{{...}}` or `[FirstName]`. The source is truncated, so the body wasn't fully checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. That makes this **unverified, not a confirmed defect**. Check the raw message headers from the original delivery path.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** The QA note says this is expected through the relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `e.lowes.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, but alignment can't be assessed from this data.
- The footer's physical address and in-body unsubscribe link couldn't be checked because the source is truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 20 links go through tracking redirects that weren't resolved, and no UTM parameters or final destinations appear in the captured source. This is a data gap, not a finding.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the two images listed above, or `alt=""` if they are purely decorative.
2. Fix the DOCTYPE to `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" "http://www.w3.org/TR/xhtml1/DTD/xhtml1-transitional.dtd">` and use the `http://` `xmlns`.
3. Inspect the original message headers, not the relayed copy, for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Until then, treat them as unknown rather than failing.
4. Re-run the audit with untruncated HTML. Also resolve a sample of the tracking redirects, using a follow-redirect request that stops short of any unsubscribe link, to check final URLs and UTMs.
5. Pipeline note: if the relay strips headers, capture them from the raw MIME before relaying. This stops the same three warnings appearing on every audit.
