# It’s LIVE 🎉 Memorial Day deals are ready for you
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- This is a well-executed seasonal promotional send from Lowe's, anchored cleanly around Memorial Day. The hero does its job: large "Memorial Day" headline, lifestyle home photography with a flag, and a clear entry offer (30% off select outdoor furniture) visible without scrolling. The email is dense but organized — it stacks multiple category promotions (grills, outdoor power equipment, garden hoses, patio furniture) before transitioning to a loyalty nudge (3x points) and a "Deals of the Day" email signup block.
- The core weakness is targeting. Lowe's is treating this as a batch-and-blast seasonal promo, not a segment-specific send. The persona here is a general adult homeowner, not anyone with a specific category affinity — no signal of purchase history, browsing behavior, or regional differentiation. The offers are real and the layout is clean, but nothing is personalized enough to feel like it was sent *to me* rather than to 30 million addresses.
- Honest verdict: competent, conversion-oriented, no major creative ambition. It will do its job at volume.

## What's working

- **Hero is immediately legible** — "Memorial Day" in large type over a home exterior with an American flag makes the campaign context instant.
- **Offer specificity is strong** — 30% off outdoor furniture, 25% off grills, 30% off outdoor power equipment are all visible with specific discount percentages, not vague "save big" language.
- **Visual category bucketing** — each product section has a clear heading, product image, and "Shop now" CTA, making it scannable.
- **Loyalty upsell is well-placed** — the 3x points section (MyLowe's Rewards) sits mid-email and adds a reason to buy beyond the discount, which is smart for member retention.
- **No render bugs** — layout is clean, images loaded, no overlapping text or broken elements visible.

## What's weak

- **No personalization anywhere visible** — no name, no "based on your recent purchases," no regional cue. Pure broadcast.
- **CTA copy is generic across the board** — every section says "Shop now." No verb+noun specificity (e.g., "Shop Grills," "Shop Patio Sets").
- **The "5 for $10" garden hose section is visually weak** — the product image is small and the offer reads confusingly at a glance (is it 5 hoses? 5 items?).
- **Deals of the Day signup feels tacked on** — the email-within-an-email pitch at the bottom is a secondary conversion goal that dilutes the Memorial Day purchase intent built up above it.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Segment the send by past purchase category** — A buyer who bought a grill last year should get grill accessories + a 25%-off headline up top, not outdoor furniture. Category personalization is the single highest-lift change available here.
- 2. **Rewrite CTAs to be product-specific** — Replace every "Shop now" with "Shop Grills," "Shop Patio Furniture," "Shop Outdoor Tools." Costs nothing, makes click tracking meaningful, and raises click intent.
- 3. **Subject line: drop the emoji, add the offer** — The current subject buries the value. Lead with a number.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Memorial Day sale: 30% off outdoor furniture`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 30% off grills, furniture & more — live now`
- 4. **Fix the preheader** — The current preheader appears to repeat "Memorial Day" messaging. Use it to extend with a second category or urgency signal.
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Grills, patio sets, power tools — all discounted through Monday`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Deals on 100s of items, plus 3x points for members`
- 5. **Clarify the "5 for $10" garden hose section** — Label the product category explicitly ("Hose Fittings & Connectors") and show 2-3 product thumbnails so the offer reads clearly at a glance.

### 5. Compliance

**[HIGH] `List-Unsubscribe` header absent.** Gmail and Apple Mail use this header to surface one-click unsubscribe UI. Its absence means:
- Gmail may suppress the native unsubscribe button, increasing spam complaints
- Google's February 2024 bulk sender requirements mandate `List-Unsubscribe` for senders exceeding 5,000 messages/day

**[HIGH] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) absent.** Gmail requires this alongside `List-Unsubscribe` for one-click compliance. Without it, unsubscribe is degraded to a GET-based flow, which Gmail may not honor for the one-click action.

Both headers are likely present in the original ESP-sent message and are being stripped or not captured by the AgentMail relay. This needs to be verified at the raw message level before flagging Lowe's ESP as non-compliant.

**[WARN] `Authentication-Results` header not captured.** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status cannot be confirmed from the relayed copy. The sending domain `e.lowes.com` is a dedicated ESP subdomain — alignment should be verified via a direct inbox delivery.

**CAN-SPAM physical address and unsubscribe link** — presence cannot be confirmed from the truncated HTML, but standard for Lowe's sends. Full source review needed to confirm.
