# 50% OFF select patio furniture and accessories 😍
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-28**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, focused Labor Day patio hero that finally does what the 8/19 "Patio clearance ends tomorrow" send did well — one offer, one number, one product shot — before sprawling into the module cascade Lowe's has leaned on all month (grills, ceiling fans, Mylow AI assistant, credit card pitch, deals-of-the-day signup). The 50% off patio hero is legible and on-brand, but this is the fourth-plus patio/outdoor beat this month and the subject line doesn't differentiate it from 8/19's near-identical patio clearance email.
- The "Curated with you in mind" module is the oddest miss — a storage tote, an all-purpose cleaner, and mini blinds have nothing to do with patio, Labor Day, or outdoor living, and they dilute an otherwise coherent hero. The credit financing and Mylow AI blocks by this point in the funnel read as filler rather than persuasion.
- Net: solid top-of-email execution dragged down by an unfocused back half and a subject that's a rerun of ground already covered this month. 6/10 territory, same as most of this month's Lowe's sends.

## What's working

- Hero is unambiguous: yellow "Clearance" badge, bold "50% off," clear "Select patio furniture and accessories" subhead, one white "Shop now" button, sitting right below the fold.
- Lifestyle photo (patio table, umbrella, chairs) directly supports the offer — no generic stock imagery.
- Secondary modules (grills, ceiling fans) stay in the outdoor/Labor Day lane and each carries a specific number ($80 off, 25% off).
- No render bugs — text is crisp, buttons are all visible, no broken images or overlapping elements.

## What's weak

- "Curated with you in mind" module (storage tote, cleaner, blinds) is thematically disconnected from patio/outdoor and drags the email off-message.
- Subject and hero are a near-repeat of the 8/19 "Patio clearance ends tomorrow" send — same category, same discount range, no new urgency hook.
- No deadline anywhere in the visible email — "Labor Day" implies a date range but nothing says when this 50% off actually ends.
- Email runs long with low-value filler (Mylow AI assistant pitch, credit card financing, Deals of the Day signup) between the offer and the footer, pushing scroll depth without adding conversion value.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a visible deadline to the hero (e.g., "Ends Labor Day, 9/7") — urgency is the one rubric signal completely absent, and it's cheap to add.
- 2. Cut or re-theme the "Curated with you in mind" module to outdoor-adjacent SKUs (patio cushions, outdoor cleaner, outdoor lighting) instead of an unrelated tote/cleaner/blinds grab-bag.
- 3. Differentiate this from the 8/19 patio email — if it's the same 50% off band, say so explicitly ("Still 50% off" / "Last call") rather than repeating the same framing as if it's new.
- 4. Move Mylow AI and the credit card pitch below Deals of the Day, or cut one entirely — two non-offer distractions back-to-back weakens the send's focus.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus up to $80 off grills and 25% off ceiling fans.`
