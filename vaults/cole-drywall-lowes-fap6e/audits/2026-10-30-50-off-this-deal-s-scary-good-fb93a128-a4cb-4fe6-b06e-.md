# 50% OFF? This deal's scary good 👻
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-30**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, loud Halloween clearance send with a clear hero. "Get 50% off select Halloween items" sits in huge white type on blue, next to a skeleton on a porch. Your eye lands on the offer first, and "Shop now" is obvious. The subject's "scary good" pun carries into the creative.
- For this recipient, a drywall contractor, it's the wrong send. Halloween décor and inflatables have nothing to do with the job. The only relevant content is the lower "Curated with you in mind" row: a DEWALT battery pack and a DEWALT router. That row is buried below the fold, and the third tile is a squishy toy. The "curated" claim doesn't hold up.
- It also lands one day before Halloween, and it follows the Oct 29 "Black Friday deals showed up early" and Oct 27 power-tool sends. That's a sixth Lowe's email in six days, with a theme change each time. I'd call it a well-built send that targets the wrong person at the wrong moment.

## What's working

- The hero offer is unmissable. "50% off" is the biggest element, with a "Clearance" tag for credibility.
- The skeleton photo is on-theme, striking, and looks good at inbox size.
- The three circular category links (Outdoor décor, Inflatables, Lights) are easy to scan and clearly tappable.
- The subject and the render agree. The "scary good" pun matches the Halloween creative, so there's no bait-and-switch.
- The layout is tidy, with no broken images or overlapping text, and the navy CTA buttons are consistent.

## What's weak

- Nothing in the email speaks to a pro or contractor. The first screen is all consumer holiday décor.
- "Curated with you in mind" is contradicted by the products shown. A sensory toy sits next to the DEWALT items, and the DEWALT tiles have no price, deal, or sale detail.
- The first DEWALT tile's name ends in a dangling "Lithium-ion ) -", which looks sloppy. The "Buy One Get 3" and "Free With Purchase" badges are too small to read.
- "Buy now" is repeated three times and "Shop now" once, with no product-specific copy.
- Nothing says the sale ends soon, even though Halloween is tomorrow. "Select items" is also vague, and the email has no end date.
- Cadence is heavy. The Oct 29 and Oct 27 sends already used the pre-holiday and Black Friday framing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment on category. Send contractor and drywall subscribers a pro-tools or Black Friday email instead of Halloween décor. If the Halloween send goes out anyway, lead the product row with the DEWALT deals and put real prices on them.
- 2. Add an end date to the hero, such as "Ends Halloween night". The holiday is the credible deadline, and the email currently ignores it.
- 3. Replace the generic buttons with specific ones: "Shop Halloween clearance", "Get the router deal", "Shop the 8Ah batteries".
- 4. Fix the product names and badge legibility, and show a price or savings on each tile.
- 5. Cap Lowe's sends at one thematic push per two or three days.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Halloween clearance: 50% off ends tonight 🎃`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: half off Halloween décor + DEWALT deals`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Skeletons, inflatables and lights, 50% off select items through Halloween night.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus free-with-purchase DEWALT deals in the Deals of the Day.`
