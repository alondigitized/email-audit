# Homepage snapshot · www.lowes.com · 2026-05-28
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-28**
## Executive summary

- Right away I'm hit with a Memorial Day deals banner at the top and then a sign-in popup blocking half the page — classic Lowe's, asking me to log in before I can even look around. Behind it I can see Craftsman Days is the main push right now, and the recommended categories down below are showing Electric Range and Washing Machine, which is exactly why I'm here this weekend. This looks like a home-improvement store that knows what time of year it is.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day banner ("Don't miss out on the final days of Memorial Day deals. Shop Now")** — hits me at the exact moment I'd be shopping for appliances. Timing is perfect.
- **Craftsman Days hero** — "Build on. Our best deals of the season are here." is straightforward guy talk. No fluff, no lifestyle nonsense. I respect that.
- **Recommended category pills (Electric Range, Washing Machine, Bathroom Vanity)** — these are visible right behind the modal and they are precisely the categories I'd browse on a Memorial Day weekend at Lowe's.
- **Silver Key free shipping callout** — good to see that benefit surfaced immediately in the modal. Relevant to my Advantage card status.
- **Sign-in modal with loyalty benefit summary** — compact and efficient. Points, Silver Key, and the sign-in button all in one screen.

## What's weak

- **Forced sign-in modal blocking the page** — I came here to shop, not to get an account pitch. The modal covers the page before I've seen a single product. That's a friction wall, not a welcome mat.
- **$5 off is a new-member offer, not a loyalty reward** — I already have a MyLowe's account and an Advantage card. Showing me a new-member signup perk as the top loyalty benefit feels tone-deaf to returning shoppers.
- **"Shop All" CTA in the Craftsman hero is too generic** — if you're running Craftsman Days, give me "Shop Craftsman Deals" or "Shop Power Tools." "Shop All" sends me to the whole catalog from a sale-specific hero. Wasted urgency.
- **No Kobalt anywhere visible** — Kobalt is Lowe's own brand and my go-to. On a tool-focused day like Craftsman Days, not seeing any Kobalt callout above the fold is a missed shot at brand loyalty.
- **Asterisks on both featured offers** — $5 off\* and Silver Key\* signal conditions I have to hunt for. That slows my confidence.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the sign-in modal by default for returning users** — if I've been to the site before, skip the popup and let me browse. Keep a persistent sign-in prompt in the header where it belongs.
- **Swap the $5 off new-member pitch for a returning-member reward** — show me my current points balance or a "members save extra X% this Memorial Day" message instead of a new-user acquisition pitch.
- **Change the Craftsman hero CTA from "Shop All" to "Shop Craftsman Deals"** — one word swap, sharper intent, higher click-through from anyone here for the sale.
- **Add a Kobalt feature strip below the hero** — even a single-row "Kobalt Memorial Day Picks" rail right below Craftsman Days would keep me scrolling instead of heading to the Home Depot app to compare.
