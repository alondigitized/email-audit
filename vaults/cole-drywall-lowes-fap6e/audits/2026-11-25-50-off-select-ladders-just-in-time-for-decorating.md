# 50% OFF select ladders just in time for decorating
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- The subject sells ladders for decorating, but the email opens with a generic "Black Friday / The good stuff got here early" banner and a "Shop now" button that has no offer attached. The 50% ladder hero doesn't show up until the second block. It is strong when you reach it: a huge "50% off" and two clearly rendered ladders. Because the first screen is promo wallpaper, the subject's promise is delayed by one full block.
- The email is also a kitchen-sink Black Friday send. Below the ladders sit a "2 FREE" power-tool offer, a Deals of the Day strip, and three more categories (outdoor kitchens, countertops, handheld tools). This repeats the Nov 22 "Buy a battery kit, get up to 2 power tools FREE" send almost exactly. The recipient is a drywall persona, so ladders and handheld tools fit, and the outdoor kitchen and countertop tiles don't.
- Judgement: a competent, on-brand Lowe's promo with a clear 50% offer. It loses points for a diluted hierarchy, a repeated creative beat, and a weak "decorating" tie-in that the body never shows.

## What's working

- The "50% off select ladders" block is the clearest element: oversized numeral, two product shots, one white button.
- The black "FREE same-day delivery" Rewards bar gives a second concrete benefit, and it sits near the top.
- The "2 FREE" green highlight on the power-tool block is high contrast and easy to scan.
- The "1 Day | Online Only" pill and "Hurry - these deals end soon" add urgency in the Deals of the Day strip.
- The layout renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The first hero is a no-offer "Shop now" banner, so the subject's ladder offer is pushed below it.
- Nothing in the body mentions decorating, holiday lights or hanging ornaments. The subject's hook has no visual support beyond the ornament graphics in the banner.
- The "2 FREE" tool block repeats the Nov 22 send and competes with the ladders for the main click.
- The email has eight or more "Shop now" CTAs and several unrelated categories (outdoor kitchens, countertops), which splits attention.
- The "Plus, 2 FREE tools…" preheader text is tiny gray header copy. It also restates an offer that appears lower in the body.
- The deadline is vague. "Ends soon" and "While supplies last" give no date, which is weak for the Wednesday before Thanksgiving.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the 50% ladders hero first, replacing the generic Black Friday banner, so the subject and the first screen match.
- 2. Tie it to decorating with a headline such as "Hang the lights safely" and a ladder-plus-lights image.
- 3. Cut the send to one offer, ladders, plus one secondary tool block. Drop the outdoor kitchen and countertop tiles for this persona.
- 4. Add a real deadline, such as "Ends Sunday" or "Ends Cyber Monday".
- 5. Name the product in the CTA, for example "Shop ladders".
- **Subject Alt A:** `50% off ladders: hang the lights safely`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Ladders up to 50% off, plus 2 free tools`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Step ladders and extension ladders on sale through Sunday. Free same-day delivery for Rewards members.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Buy a battery kit, get up to 2 power tools free. Black Friday pricing is live now.`
