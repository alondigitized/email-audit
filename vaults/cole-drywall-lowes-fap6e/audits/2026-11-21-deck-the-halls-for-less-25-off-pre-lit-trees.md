# Deck the halls for less🎄25% OFF pre-lit trees
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a focused subject on top of a kitchen-sink body. "25% OFF pre-lit trees" is a concrete, seasonal offer, and the second block delivers it: a big red "25% off select pre-lit artificial Christmas trees" hero over a strong photo of lit trees. A recipient who opens for the trees gets what they came for.
- The email then dilutes itself. The first block is a generic "Black Friday: See what early deals are waiting for you" banner, which pushes the tree hero down the page. Below it come a sponsored Affirm ad, a FREE gifts block, a 2 for $4 poinsettia, $10 MyLowe's Money, gift guides, three product tiles and Deals of the Day. That is eight or more competing messages. The preheader sells yet another offer, the $10 MyLowe's Money, instead of reinforcing the trees.
- Against the recent run, this is better aligned than the Nov 16 "12 Days of Deals" send, where subject and hero disagreed. It is still the same template. Seven Black Friday-flavored sends in a week (Nov 15 to 21) means recipients are being trained to skim. Verdict: a solid subject and one good hero, buried in clutter and a crowded cadence.

## What's working

- The subject's offer (25% off pre-lit trees) is matched by a large hero with the same "25% off" number in oversized type.
- The tree photography is aspirational and seasonal, and it makes the product obvious at a glance.
- The red and white "Shop now" buttons are visible and consistent through the email.
- Loyalty hooks are present: the MyLowe's Rewards "FREE same-day delivery" bar and the $10 MyLowe's Money block.
- The product tiles (Blackstone griddle tools, Rudolph reindeer, Klein socket set) give "Buy now" a specific target, and the gift-guide row ("For the home chef", "For the gadget guru") adds some personalization.

## What's weak

- The generic "Black Friday: See what early deals are waiting for you" banner sits above the hero and delays the promised offer.
- Too many offers compete (trees, FREE gifts, poinsettia, MyLowe's Money, gift cards, Deals of the Day), so the 25% message loses its focus.
- The MyLowe's Money banner is cropped at the right edge ("mo..."), and the sponsored Affirm/Zeta ad breaks the brand flow mid-email.
- CTAs are generic ("Shop now", "Buy now", "Explore now"). The tree hero links to a category with no deadline or "ends" date.
- The fine print at the bottom is long and dense, and the "select" trees qualifier is vague, so the offer feels less clean.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the tree hero the first block under the header and drop the generic Black Friday banner. This is the single biggest lift: the subject's promise would be visible without scrolling on mobile.
- 2. Cut the body to three blocks: trees, one gift or loyalty offer, and Deals of the Day. Move the poinsettia, FREE gifts and Affirm blocks to other sends.
- 3. Add a real deadline ("Ends Sunday") and make the CTA specific, for example "Shop pre-lit trees". Say which sizes or price points are included instead of just "select".
- 4. Put two or three actual tree products in the hero area with prices and star ratings, to add the social proof that is missing now.
- 5. Space out sends. Rotate themes (trees, then tools, then appliances) instead of re-sending the same template with a different headline.
- **Subject Alt A:** `25% off pre-lit trees, ends Sunday 🎄`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your tree, already lit: 25% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Select 7.5-ft and 9-ft pre-lit artificial trees. Plus free same-day delivery for members.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Skip the string lights. Prices drop on pre-lit trees through Sunday.`

## Technical Audit: "Deck the halls for less🎄25% OFF pre-lit trees" (lowes@e.lowes.com)

### 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with media queries and web-font fallbacks. The HTML is truncated, so the checks below cover only the visible head, the QA output and the image list. Confirmed issues: a malformed DOCTYPE, two content images without alt text, and the recipient address exposed in plaintext in 16 tracking-pixel URLs. The header warnings come from the AgentMail relay and are not confirmed defects.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 38 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the QA probe, so none of the 38 were checked for redirect chains, dead links or final destinations.
- **Recipient email in pixel URLs:** the recipient address appears unencoded in the query string of every `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixel, for example `https://sli.lowes.com/imp?s=127768300&e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app&p=531383&lctg=410146703&stpe=pixel`.
  - A plaintext email in a GET parameter ends up in proxy, CDN and server logs, and in referrer data where applicable.
  - The raw `@` is also not percent-encoded.
- **Pixel volume:** 16 separate `sli.lowes.com/imp` impression pixels were flagged (`s=127768300`–`127768304`, `2019286`–`2019290`, `1090834`–`1090838`). Some `li_coord` URLs appear cut off in the QA output. That may be an artifact of truncation, so it is not flagged as a defect.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed DOCTYPE:** the source reads `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" ...>`, with no space between `PUBLIC` and the quoted identifier. This is invalid and can push some clients into quirks mode.
- **Missing alt on content images:**
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg`
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png`
  - If these carry content, screen readers and image-blocked clients get nothing.
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels:** the 16 `imp` pixels have no `alt`. This has little user impact, but `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` would suppress the warnings and keep them out of the accessibility tree.
- **Web fonts:** the `@font-face` blocks for Fellix and DIN Next are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, so Outlook is excluded as intended. No rendering defects are confirmed in the visible source.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The recipient email is resolved in the pixel URLs, and no unresolved merge tokens such as `%%`, `{{` or `[[` appear in the visible source. The truncated body could not be checked.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both were not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The cause is the same relay limitation.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** not verifiable because the HTML is truncated before the footer.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The 38 click-tracking links were not resolved, and the truncated source shows no destination URLs, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the "25% OFF pre-lit trees" offer are unconfirmed.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the DOCTYPE to `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" ...>`.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to `c301e24e-…jpg` and `ad569dc5-…png`. Add `alt=""` to the `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixels.
3. Stop putting the raw email address in `sli.lowes.com/imp?e=` URLs. Use a hashed or opaque subscriber ID instead.
4. Capture the raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`, `DKIM-Signature`) from the original delivery path rather than the relay, to confirm RFC 8058 support and authentication.
5. Resolve the 38 skipped tracking links with a redirect-following probe, then check the final URLs for UTM parameters and the landing-page offer.
6. Re-run the audit on the full HTML, since the footer, unsubscribe link and CTA hrefs were cut off.
