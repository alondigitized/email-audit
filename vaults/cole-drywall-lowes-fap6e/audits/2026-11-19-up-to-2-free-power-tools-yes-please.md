# Up to 2 FREE power tools? Yes, please 🛠
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the best-focused Lowe's send in the recent run, and the subject finally matches the first big hero. "Up to 2 FREE power tools" is a concrete, unusual offer. It lands on a red hero with a "2 FREE" green highlight and DEWALT, CRAFTSMAN and Kobalt tools. A tool-oriented persona has a clear reason to open and click.
- The email is still a kitchen-sink Black Friday template. Above the free-tools hero sits a generic "Black Friday: The good stuff got here early" banner with its own Shop now button. Below it come Pella windows, Kobalt colors, a tool chest, ladders, Christmas trees, a "Something BIG" teaser and a product row. The 11/17 appliances send worked because the subject and hero lined up. This one gets that alignment but still dilutes it.
- Judgement: a good subject-to-hero match and a strong hero, held back by clutter, a vague banner above the fold and a fine-print-heavy offer. It's the best of the recent run, around a 7.

## What's working

- The subject's "2 FREE power tools" is echoed by the hero's big "2 FREE" green block, so the promise is delivered on open.
- The hero shows real products from recognizable brands (DEWALT, CRAFTSMAN, Kobalt) on a bold red background, and the eye goes straight to the offer.
- The tools theme repeats below the hero: Kobalt member access, a $260 CRAFTSMAN cabinet and 50% off ladders. That makes the tool category worth scrolling for.
- The "Member Only Access ends November 20" block adds loyalty pricing and a credible deadline.
- The "FREE same-day delivery" banner and the clean "Shop now" and "Buy now" buttons keep the next step obvious.

## What's weak

- The "Black Friday: The good stuff got here early" banner sits above the free-tools hero. It has no offer and pushes the real hero down.
- The "Get up to 2 FREE... when you purchase a select... battery kit" wording is vague. It doesn't say which kits, what price or what the catch is, which weakens trust in the offer.
- Too many unrelated modules follow: Pella financing, Christmas trees and a "Something BIG" teaser with nothing to see. They dilute the tool message.
- The Pella sponsored block is tiny and unreadable, and the footer's fine print is also tiny.
- The "Curated with you in mind" product row has truncated, clunky product names such as "DEWALT 12 -Volt 2 - Pack Battery/charger (3 Ah 5 Ah ) - DCB135C". The row also has no prices or reviews.

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop the generic "Black Friday" banner, or fold it into the header, so the "2 FREE" hero sits first.
- 2. Name the qualifying kit and price in the hero, for example "Buy a DEWALT 20V battery kit, get 2 tools free." Link each tool to a specific product page rather than a generic Shop now.
- 3. Trim the middle to the tool theme (Kobalt, the CRAFTSMAN cabinet, ladders) and cut the Christmas trees and the "Something BIG" teaser.
- 4. Clean up product names and add prices, ratings and savings to the product row.
- 5. Add a clear end date to the free-tool offer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Buy 1 DEWALT kit, get 2 tools free`
- **Subject Alt B:** `2 free power tools with select battery kits 🛠`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `DEWALT, CRAFTSMAN and Kobalt kits qualify. Member Access on Kobalt ends 11/20.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Pick a battery kit, get up to 2 tools free. Plus 50% off ladders.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Lowe's email has no confirmed hard failures. The confirmed findings are a malformed DOCTYPE, two content images without alt text, and the recipient's email address placed in plaintext in ad-impression pixel URLs. Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified because the AgentMail relay doesn't capture those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **40 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains and UTM presence are unverified.
- **16 `sli.lowes.com/imp` impression pixels** carry parameters such as `s=127768300..304`, `s=2019286` and `p=11192026`. These look like retail-media ad impressions. They are third-party-style beacons, so they add load and are blocked by Apple Mail Privacy Protection and by image-blocking clients. Impressions will be undercounted in those clients.
- The HTML is truncated, so the full link inventory is unconfirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed DOCTYPE:** `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" ...>` has no space after `PUBLIC`. Most clients tolerate it, but strict parsers may drop to quirks mode.
- **XHTML namespace and DTD use `https://`:** `xmlns="https://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` and the DTD URL should be `http://`. This is non-standard but harmless in practice.
- **Content images missing alt text (real a11y issue):**
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg`
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png`

  With images off, or in a screen reader, these may carry the offer content.
- **Tracking pixels flagged for missing alt:** all 16 `sli.lowes.com/imp` images. These are decorative beacons, so `alt=""` is the correct fix. It is low severity, but the attribute is absent.
- **Web fonts:** Fellix and DIN Next are `.woff` only and loaded inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` blocks. Fallback stacks aren't visible in the truncated source, so they are unverified.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) are visible in the available source.
- **PII in URLs:** every impression pixel contains `e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app`, the recipient's address in plaintext (not hashed) in a query string. The address can end up in third-party logs, proxy caches and referrer data.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **List-Unsubscribe not found (WARN) and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found (WARN).** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **not confirmed** as a sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders. Verify against raw headers from a direct inbox.
- **Authentication-Results not found (WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `lowes@e.lowes.com`, so alignment can't be assessed from this data.
- The footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address aren't visible in the truncated HTML, so they can't be confirmed or denied.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. The 40 click-tracked links were skipped, no landing-page URLs or UTM parameters appear in the truncated source, and the offer's destination isn't verifiable. The impression pixels reference `lctg=410146703`, which may be a campaign ID, but no continuity inference can be drawn from it.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to `c301e24e-…jpg` and `ad569dc5-…png`. Set `alt=""` on the `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixels.
2. Hash or tokenize the recipient email in the `e=` parameter of the impression pixels instead of passing it in plaintext.
3. Fix the DOCTYPE spacing (`PUBLIC "-//W3C//…`) and use `http://` namespace and DTD URLs.
4. Re-test headers (List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, Authentication-Results) from a non-relay inbox before treating the compliance warnings as real.
5. Resolve the redirect chains on the 40 skipped tracking links with a header-only follow, and check UTM and landing-page alignment.
6. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer unsubscribe link, postal address and link inventory.
