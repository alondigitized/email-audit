# Grab these Closeout Event deals FAST ⚡️
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-25**
## Executive summary

- This is Lowe's fifth-plus touch this week on the same rotating cast of offers — patio 40% off, smart home 50% off, and MyLowe's Money Days have all appeared across the July 21–24 sends per the brand history, and this email just restaples them under a new "Closeout Event" wrapper. The execution itself is clean: strong hero banner, two well-organized product tiles with clear percentage-off callouts, and a Money Days loyalty block that's visually distinct. But the "Surprise! It's Christmas in July" section right after is a jarring non-sequitur that undercuts the urgency the rest of the email is trying to build.
- The render is bug-free and the visual hierarchy works — eye goes hero banner → two offer tiles → Money Days → grab-bag of secondary CTAs. The problem is content strategy, not execution: this reads like a template stuffed with every active promo rather than a single coherent "Closeout Event" story, and a subscriber who opened any of the July 21–24 emails has already seen the 40%/50%/Money Days beats at least twice.
- Verdict: well-built, poorly differentiated. Ship the layout, cut the redundant modules.

## What's working

- Hero banner ("Closeout Event / Hurry! These deals are too good to wait on.") is bold, legible, and impossible to miss on open.
- Two-tile offer layout (40% off patio, 50% off smart home) leads with big, scannable percentages — the strongest visual element in the email.
- MyLowe's Money Days block is visually differentiated with its own blue gradient and date range (July 23–26), giving it standalone weight without competing with the hero.
- Clean render throughout — no overlapping text, broken images, or placeholder content.

## What's weak

- "Surprise! It's Christmas in July" with an elf and reindeer feels tonally disconnected from the urgency-driven "Closeout Event" framing right above it — it reads like a leftover module, not a deliberate beat.
- This is the same 40%-patio / 50%-smart-home / Money Days combination the brand has already run July 21–24 (per history) — nothing here signals it's a new offer versus a recap.
- Subject line ("Grab these Closeout Event deals FAST") names no specific percentage or product, so the strongest number (50% off) is buried in the preview text and the body, not the subject.
- Generic "Shop now" CTA repeated five times with no differentiation by product/category.
- "View email as a webpage" sits as the very first visible line above the logo — technically minor, but it's the first thing the eye hits before any offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the strongest number in the subject line instead of leaving it generic — this single change would do the most to lift open rate, since the 50% figure currently only appears in the preview and body.
- 2. Cut or relocate the "Christmas in July" module — it dilutes the closeout urgency and reads as filler; if it must run, give it its own dedicated send instead of appending it to a deals-fast email.
- 3. Differentiate the CTA copy per tile ("Shop Patio" / "Shop Smart Home") instead of five identical "Shop now" buttons — improves scannability and click intent signal.
- 4. Acknowledge the repeat exposure directly — since this is at least the third time Money Days has appeared this week, consider swapping it for a countdown ("Money Days ends in 24 hours") rather than a flat re-announcement.
