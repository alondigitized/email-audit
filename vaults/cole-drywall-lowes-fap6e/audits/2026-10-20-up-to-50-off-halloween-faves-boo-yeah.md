# Up to 50% OFF Halloween faves? Boo-yeah👻
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**
## Executive summary

- The hero is strong and seasonal. A skeleton rocker in a red cape with a keytar sits next to a huge "50% off Halloween products" and a "Clearance" tag. It matches the subject, and the eye lands on the offer first. Then the email loses focus. Right under the hero it announces "And just like that… it's holiday time," and from there it is a pile of unrelated modules: 3 free power tools with a battery kit, Christmas trees from $59.98, inflatables from $34.98, a Member Only teaser, and a "Curated" row.
- This is Lowe's usual kitchen-sink roundup, a step down from the focused sends of 10/12–10/14. The 10/16 "Up to 50% OFF! Big deals on seasonal essentials" had the same problem: the subject promised 50% and the body scattered. The Halloween clearance is a real, time-sensitive offer, but the email undercuts it by pivoting to Christmas in the second fold. The member teaser is also dim. "Two merry surprises" sits on a dark blue image and says nothing concrete until 10/22. Verdict: a good hero in a mediocre email, around a 6.

## What's working

- The "50% off Halloween products" hero is large, high-contrast and tied to the subject line.
- The skeleton keytar image fits the season and has some personality.
- Starting prices ($59.98, $34.98) are clear and sit in green tags that stand out.
- "3 FREE" on power tools is a punchy hook with a clear product visual.
- Buttons are consistent, and the Deals of the Day banner in red gives a strong footer call to action.

## What's weak

- The "it's holiday time" pivot dilutes the Halloween clearance and leaves the 50% offer with only one block.
- The "Two merry surprises" Member Only block is low-contrast, with faint ghosted product shapes and no concrete benefit until 10/22.
- There are about eight CTAs, nearly all "Shop now" or "Buy now," so nothing is specific.
- The "Curated with you in mind" row mixes a skeleton, a pail and a utility knife. It doesn't read as curated, and none of the products show prices or ratings.
- The preheader at the top ("The treats keep coming with deals on trees, décor, FREE items and more") is generic and lists items without a hook.

## Recommendations

- 1. Let Halloween own the send. Fill the second and third blocks with Halloween clearance products at their sale prices, and move Christmas to a separate send after November 1. This is the biggest lift.
- 2. Make the member teaser concrete, for example "Members: $10 off $50 starts 10/22," with a readable background.
- 3. Replace the "Shop now" labels with specific ones such as "Shop Halloween clearance," "Get 3 free tools," and "See tree deals."
- 4. Add prices and sale badges to the Curated row, or drop it.
- 5. Add an urgency line to the hero, such as "While supplies last" or an end date, so the clearance feels time-bound.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The sampled HTML is a table-based XHTML email with MSO conditionals and `@media` queries. The confirmed defects are a malformed DOCTYPE/namespace, two content images with no alt text, and the recipient's email address in plaintext in impression-pixel URLs. Compliance headers and click-tracking destinations could not be verified from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **32 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and link health are unverified.
- **Recipient email in plaintext in pixel URLs.** Five `https://sli.lowes.com/imp?...` requests carry `e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app`. Examples:
  - `imp?s=1090838&e=...&p=10202026&lctg=410146703&stpe=static`
  - `imp?s=1090834&e=...&stpe=default&li_coord...`, which appears twice
  - `imp?s=1090835&e=...&stpe=static`
  - `imp?s=1090837&e=...&stpe=static`

  The address is unhashed and goes to a third-party impression endpoint. It can end up in server and proxy logs and in referrer data.
- **Duplicate pixel.** `s=1090834` is requested twice. This may inflate impression counts.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed DOCTYPE.** `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" "https://www.w3.org/TR/xhtml1/DTD/xhtml1-transitional.dtd">` has no whitespace after `PUBLIC`. The system identifier also uses `https://`. Clients generally tolerate this, but it is invalid.
- **Non-standard namespace.** `<html xmlns="https://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` uses `https`. The canonical value is `http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml`.
- **Content images missing alt (QA-confirmed):**
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg`
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png`

  Screen readers will announce the filename or skip the image, and nothing displays when images are blocked.
- **Tracking pixels missing alt.** The five `sli.lowes.com/imp` images have no `alt`. They should have `alt=""` and explicit zero dimensions so they are ignored by assistive tech.
- **Not assessable.** The HTML was truncated, so I can't confirm `lang`, `<title>`, role attributes or the preheader.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the supplied source or QA data. The only recipient-specific value is the email in the pixel URLs (see section 2).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe not found (WARN).** QA notes the AgentMail relay may not capture the header, so this is unconfirmed rather than a proven defect.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post not found (WARN).** Same caveat. One-click unsubscribe under RFC 8058 can't be confirmed.
- **Authentication-Results not found (WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain is `e.lowes.com`.
- **Body footer not evaluated.** The truncated source doesn't show an unsubscribe link or a physical postal address, so neither can be confirmed or ruled out.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues can be confirmed. Click URLs are tracking redirects that QA did not resolve, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the "Up to 50% OFF Halloween" offer are unverified. The `p=10202026` parameter on the pixels looks like a send-date key (2026-10-20), but that is an inference.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to `c301e24e-...jpg` and `ad569dc5-...png`. Use `alt=""` on the impression pixels.
2. Hash or remove the plaintext `e=` email parameter from the `sli.lowes.com/imp` URLs. Check whether the duplicate `s=1090834` pixel is intentional.
3. Fix the DOCTYPE to `PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN"` with the `http://` DTD URL, and set `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"`.
4. Capture the raw headers by testing outside the relay or using a direct-delivery seed. That would verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
5. Resolve the 32 skipped tracking links with a redirect-following probe. This would validate the final destinations and UTM parameters.
6. Re-run QA on the full, untruncated HTML to check `lang`, `<title>`, the footer unsubscribe link and the postal address.
