# Don't miss this Closeout Event 👉
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-17**
## Executive summary

- This is Lowe's pivoting from the post-Money Days scatter (brand awareness July 14, generic deals July 15) into a proper clearance event. The creative is well-organized: a clean "Closeout Event / before the deals disappear" hero, then GE Appliances at up to 35% off, then outdoor at up to 40%, then two product-level deals at $100 savings each. Three distinct discount hooks in one scroll is aggressive but defensible for a clearance send.
- The structural miss is urgency: "before the deals disappear" is the only deadline signal, and it's vague. No end date, no countdown, no "ends Sunday" — which undercuts the entire "don't miss" positioning in the subject. If this is a genuine closeout, give it a date. And the bottom section ("We'll send deals you'll love") promoting Deals of the Day sign-up inside an email to existing subscribers is wasted real estate that would've been better spent on a loyalty tie-in.
- Overall, this is a competent mid-tier clearance email that does the basics right but doesn't push hard enough on the time-bound urgency that "Closeout Event" implies.

## What's working

- Three distinct discount layers (35% appliances, 40% outdoor, $100 product-level) give different shopper intents something to grab.
- GE Appliances co-branding adds manufacturer credibility to the appliance section — feels less like a Lowe's house mark-down.
- Visual hierarchy is clean: hero → appliances → outdoor → specific deals flows naturally.
- "Shop now" CTAs appear at every section — no friction in finding a click path.
- July clearance timing is on-brand and seasonally intuitive.

## What's weak

- "Before the deals disappear" is phantom urgency — no date, no hours-remaining, nothing credible to act on.
- Two identical "$100 savings" product tiles with nearly the same visual weight compete with each other instead of hero-ing one.
- The "We'll send deals you'll love / Deals of the Day" sign-up block at the bottom is misplaced — this is an existing subscriber, not a prospect.
- No loyalty signal anywhere — no MyLowe's badge, no member-exclusive pricing, which is a notable step back from the Money Days emails that leaned heavily on loyalty.
- Subject "Closeout Event" with a pointing emoji is generic — it names the mechanic but not the category or the saving.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a hard end date to the hero** — change "before the deals disappear" to "ends Sunday" or "3 days only." Closeout events live and die on perceived scarcity; vague copy kills the urgency the subject line sets up.
- 2. **Surface a MyLowe's member benefit** — even a "Members save an extra 5%" banner would re-engage the loyalty angle Lowe's leaned on all of Money Days week.
- 3. **Pick a hero product, not a category** — instead of "Refrigerators >" lead with one GE model at a specific price. One killer deal outconverts two generic category links.
- 4. **Replace the Deals of the Day sign-up block** with a countdown or a "today's top closeout" module — subscribers already opted in; use that slot to close the sale.
- 5. **Make the $100 savings tiles distinct** — different visual treatments (one tool, one outdoor) so the eye doesn't flatten both into the same block.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Closeout starts now: up to 40% off — ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `GE appliances, outdoor, tools — all on closeout`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Up to 35% off GE appliances + 40% off outdoor. 3 days only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Refrigerators, patio furniture, power tools — closeout pricing inside.`

## Technical Audit — Lowe's "Closeout Event" Email

### 1. Technical Summary

Standard retail HTML email using table-based layout with custom web fonts and multi-breakpoint media queries. No critical rendering failures detected, but several accessibility gaps and relay-layer compliance blind spots require attention.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**21 tracking links skipped by QA probe** — all click-redirect URLs route through `lowes.com` ESP infrastructure (likely `click.e.lowes.com` or similar). This is expected behavior for an ESP-managed send, but it means no dead-link validation was performed on destination URLs. If any redirects point to expired sale pages post-send, there is no automated catch.

No issues with the base link structure otherwise.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Missing alt text — 7 images flagged:**

| Image | Type | Issue |
|---|---|---|
| `c301e24e-...jpg` | Content image | No `alt` attribute |
| `ad569dc5-...png` | Content image | No `alt` attribute |
| 5× `sli.lowes.com/imp?...` | Pixel trackers | No `alt=""` (empty string) |

The content images (`image.e.lowes.com`) require descriptive alt text for screen reader users and for plain-text/images-blocked rendering environments. The tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` (explicitly empty) rather than being omitted entirely — absence vs. empty string behaves differently across assistive technology.

**Font loading:** Seven `@font-face` declarations load `.woff` only — no `.woff2` fallback. WOFF2 offers ~30% smaller payloads; supported by all modern email clients that support web fonts at all. Not blocking, but wasteful on mobile.

**MSO conditional fencing:** Custom fonts and some styles are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!--> ... <!--<![endif]-->` — correct. Outlook will fall back to system fonts as intended.

**Media queries:** Two breakpoints defined (`min-width: 375px and max-width: 600px` and `max-width: 480px`). The 375–600px range uses `-webkit-min-device-pixel-ratio:0` to limit to WebKit — this is an intentional pattern but excludes Android non-WebKit clients in that range. Acceptable for Lowe's likely audience, but worth noting.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

```
https://sli.lowes.com/imp?s=1090838&e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app&p=7172026&lctg=410146703
```

The `e=` parameter encodes the recipient email address (`cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app`) in plaintext in the pixel URL. This is a Lowe's ESP behavior, not a merge token failure — the token resolved correctly. Flagging for awareness: recipient PII is embedded in image request URLs logged by `sli.lowes.com`.

No unresolved `{{merge_tag}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]`-style placeholders detected in the truncated source.

### 5. Compliance

**List-Unsubscribe header: NOT CONFIRMED**
- QA relay did not capture a `List-Unsubscribe` header.
- This is likely a relay capture gap (AgentMail not forwarding all headers), but cannot be ruled out as absent. CAN-SPAM requires a functional unsubscribe mechanism; RFC 2369 `List-Unsubscribe` and RFC 8058 `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (one-click) are additionally required by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders as of 2024.
- **Action required:** Verify headers are present on raw SMTP delivery, not just the relayed copy.

**Authentication-Results: NOT CONFIRMED**
- SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status unknown — relay did not surface `Authentication-Results`. Sending domain `e.lowes.com` is an ESP subdomain and should have SPF/DKIM aligned. Cannot confirm without raw headers.
- **Action required:** Pull raw headers from a direct inbox delivery to confirm DKIM alignment on `e.lowes.com`.
