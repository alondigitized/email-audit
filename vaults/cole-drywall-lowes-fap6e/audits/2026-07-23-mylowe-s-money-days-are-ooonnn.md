# MyLowe's Money Days are OOONNN 🤩
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-23**
## Executive summary

- This is Lowe's fourth MyLowe's-branded communication on July 23 — and it's the standalone push for the "Money Days" loyalty event that's been name-dropped in at least two earlier sends today (the credit card pitch and the summer deals umbrella). The core mechanic is clearly executed: "Earn $10 EXTRA on $200+" is explicit, Bronze-member segmentation is real, and "2 Days Only" provides credible urgency. This is structurally competent loyalty marketing.
- The problems are above the fold. The subject line buries the reward behind event-name theater — "OOONNN" signals excitement without earning it, and a Lowe's shopper scanning an inbox already hit by four sends today gets zero concrete reason to open. The email also asks the creative to do double duty: Money Days cashback event AND Christmas in July decor. Neither gets the space it deserves.
- Compared to the July 21 smart home send (8/10) and the Messi urgency close (8/10), this one plays weaker. Those emails had a clear product category and a sharp hook. This one leads with a program name most members don't have a strong emotional relationship with.

## What's working

- Bronze member tier callout is visible above the fold — genuine segmentation signal, not just "valued customer"
- "$10 EXTRA" and "$200 or more" are both explicit — the math is readable at a glance
- "Member Exclusive / 2 Days Only" badge adds urgency without feeling manipulative
- Christmas in July module gives a product landing point (trees up to 30% off) beyond the abstract cashback event
- Render is clean — no broken images, overlapping text, or layout issues visible

## What's weak

- Subject hides the actual dollar value — "OOONNN" is noise where "$10 EXTRA" belongs
- Two creative themes (loyalty cashback event + holiday decor) split the email's focus and neither lands a decisive punch
- "Shop now" is a generic CTA that doesn't reinforce the earn mechanic — "Start earning" or "Get my $10" would convert harder
- This is the fourth Lowe's send of the day; cadence fatigue is a real open-rate drag regardless of execution quality
- The $200 threshold for $10 back (5%) feels modest when Bronze members are already earning baseline points — the email doesn't contextualize why this is incremental value

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the dollar amount in the subject.** Put "$10 EXTRA" where "OOONNN" lives — the event name means nothing to a member scanning their inbox.
- 2. **Split Christmas in July into its own send.** Run it tomorrow or Friday; letting it compete with the Money Days message weakens both modules.
- 3. **Rewrite the primary CTA** from "Shop now" to something reward-forward: "Activate your $10" or "Start earning now."
- 4. **Add a member-tier progress hook** below the hero — even a one-liner like "You're X points from Silver" would deepen the loyalty narrative and justify a second click.
- 5. **Contextualize the bonus** — "$10 on top of your usual 1% Bronze earn rate" makes the incremental value tangible.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Earn $10 EXTRA in MyLowe's Money — 2 days only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Bronze member: your $10 bonus is live right now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $200, pocket $10 in MyLowe's Money — member exclusive, ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your Bronze bonus is live: $10 extra on any $200+ purchase this weekend only`

## Technical Audit — MyLowe's Money Days (2026-07-23)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Well-structured XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with standard responsive patterns and multi-client compatibility CSS. Primary issues are header capture gaps in the AgentMail relay and two images missing alt text.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**27 tracking links skipped by QA probe** — all click-redirect domains (likely `e.lowes.com` or a Salesforce Marketing Cloud redirect domain). This is expected behavior for tracked emails, not a defect. No broken destination URLs were flagged.

No issues found with link structure in the HTML source itself.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Two images missing `alt` text:**

| Image | src |
|---|---|
| Hero/content image | `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/1/a22207c1-63c7-47c0-9e00-61227828e1fe.jpg` |
| Tracking pixel | `https://mi.lowes.com/p/cp/8d3e558453f86f40/o.gif?mi_u=410146703` |

The tracking pixel missing `alt` is a minor cosmetic issue (screen readers will announce the src filename). The content image missing `alt` is a real accessibility gap — subscribers using screen readers or image-blocking clients get no context.

**CSS client targeting:** `ReadMsgBody`/`ExternalClass` hacks for legacy Hotmail are present but harmless. The two media query blocks (`min-width: 375px` and `max-width: 480px`) overlap at 375–480px, which could produce conflicting rule application on some devices — worth verifying in a test client matrix.

**Custom fonts** (`Fellix`, `DIN Next`) loaded via `.woff` only. No `.woff2` fallback. Most modern clients support woff2 and it's ~30% smaller; this isn't a rendering blocker but is an unnecessary bandwidth cost.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

HTML source truncated — unable to fully audit merge token syntax. No unresolved tokens (e.g., `%%FIRST_NAME%%`, `{{first_name}}`) visible in the provided excerpt. No issues confirmed.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header not detected** — flagged as a relay capture gap, not necessarily absent from the original SMTP envelope. However, if missing from delivered mail:
- Gmail and Apple Mail suppress the native unsubscribe UI that appears in the header chrome
- Per Google/Yahoo 2024 bulk sender requirements, `List-Unsubscribe` is mandatory for senders at Lowe's volume

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not detected** — one-click unsubscribe (POST method) is also a Google/Yahoo bulk sender requirement. If this header is absent from the delivered message, Lowe's is non-compliant with current bulk sender rules.

**`Authentication-Results` not captured** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status unknown via relay. The sending domain `e.lowes.com` is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud subdomain and almost certainly has DKIM configured, but this cannot be confirmed from the current capture.

**Action required:** Verify these three headers are present in the raw SMTP envelope, not just missing from relay capture.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

27 tracked links were skipped so destination URLs and UTM parameters could not be verified. No UTM parameter issues confirmed or denied. A manual spot-check of 2–3 CTA links through the click-redirect chain is recommended to confirm `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` parameters survive the redirect.
