# Homepage snapshot · www.lowes.com · 2026-08-20
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-20**
## Executive summary

- This looks like Lowe's doing what Lowe's does best — pushing appliances hard with a real dollar-off deal. It's a clean, no-nonsense page, but right now it's 100% appliance sale (Electrolux/Frigidaire), and there's nothing here for a guy elbow-deep in a basement drywall job. If I wasn't already mid-fridge-replacement, I'd have no reason to stick around.

## What's working

- **The Frigidaire bundle table** ("Save $100/$200/$300 depending how many you buy") — that's the kind of straight math I can do in my head, no gimmicks.
- **"Members Get More" strip** — free delivery, free basic install, 2-year protection plan tied to MyLowe's Rewards. I've got the card, so this actually moves me.
- **The sign-in flyout** showing "$5 off when you join" and "FREE shipping with Silver Key status" — good reminder of what my account's worth, even if I didn't ask for it right then.

## What's weak

- **Whole hero is appliances** — I'm mid basement build, I need drywall, Kobalt tools, maybe Allen + Roth trim. Nothing above the fold speaks to that.
- **Nav row gets cut off mid-word** ("Bathroo...") because the sign-in dropdown is covering half the screen — makes the page feel cluttered on a phone screen.
- **No people in the kitchen shot** — just a staged stainless kitchen, nobody using the stuff. Doesn't hurt much, but doesn't help either.
- **Exclusion fine print** (MA, MD, WI, NJ, PA, Pro Rewards members) sitting right under the "Members Get More" pitch — feels like it's walking back the offer before I even click.

## Recommendations

- Swap in a second hero rotation slide (or split hero) for building materials/tools — something with drywall sheets, a Kobalt drill, basement remodel framing — so it's not 100% appliances.
- Close that sign-in flyout by default or move it so it doesn't truncate the category nav.
- Put an actual countdown or "Sale ends [date]" on the Electrolux banner — "End-of-Summer Sale" with no end date doesn't push me to act today.
- Add a "Shop by Project" or "Basement Finishing" tile near the top for guys like me who aren't there for a fridge.
