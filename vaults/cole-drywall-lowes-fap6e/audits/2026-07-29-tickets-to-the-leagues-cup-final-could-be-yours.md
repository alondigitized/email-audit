# Tickets to the Leagues Cup Final could be yours ⚽️
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-29**
## Executive summary

- This is Lowe's stepping outside its usual promo cadence — instead of another Money Days or % off push, it's a branded sweepstakes tie-in with Leagues Cup 2026 soccer, dangling four Final tickets, a Messi-signed Inter Miami jersey, an EA FC27 code, and a $500 gift card. Compared to the brand history (Smart Home 50% off, Closeout Event, Summer Storage Event), this is a genuine novelty in subject matter, which should help it cut through inbox fatigue even if it has nothing to do with home improvement.
- The execution is clean: strong hero visual with a lineup of pro players, a clear "Enter now" CTA, and a smart secondary hook ("bonus entry with any qualifying purchase") that ties the giveaway back to actual Lowe's transactions. The prize breakdown is well organized and legible.
- The weak spot is relevance — a soccer sweepstakes is a fun brand moment but doesn't serve the primary purchase-intent job Lowe's emails usually do, and there's no discount or purchase incentive beyond "buy something, maybe get a bonus entry." It's a good engagement/list-health play, not a revenue email.

## What's working

- Hero banner is high-impact: bold typography, recognizable soccer stars, and a single unmistakable CTA ("Enter now") with no competing clutter.
- Prize hierarchy is scannable — grand prize items are grouped with icons, runner-up prizes are visually subordinate but still visible.
- The bonus-entry-with-purchase panel smartly reconnects a non-commerce sweepstakes back to Lowe's core shopping behavior.
- Legal/fine print (No Purchase Necessary, official rules link, sponsor disclosure) is present and transparent rather than buried in a way that feels deceptive.
- Clean rendering throughout — no overlapping text, broken images, or placeholder content.

## What's weak

- Zero connection to home improvement — no product tie-in beyond "shop to earn a bonus entry," so a shopper opening for deals gets nothing to buy.
- No dollar/percent offer anywhere in the email — this fails the "concrete offer" test that most of Lowe's other sends pass easily.
- Both CTAs ("Enter now," "Shop now") are generic verb-only labels rather than specific verb+noun copy.
- The entry deadline (August 15) only appears in body copy — it's absent from the subject and preview, so it can't drive open-time urgency.
- "View email as a webpage" sits above the fold before any real content, adding a small speed bump before the hero even loads visually.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pull the August 15 deadline into the subject/preview to convert this into a time-bounded push rather than an open-ended contest — deadlines drive opens more than prize lists do.
- 2. Make CTA copy specific: "Enter to win" or "Claim your entry" instead of the generic "Enter now."
- 3. Consider pairing the sweepstakes with an actual limited-time discount in the same send (e.g., "Get $X off + a bonus sweepstakes entry") so the email does double duty for shoppers who don't care about soccer.
- 4. Move "View email as a webpage" below the logo/nav bar so the hero is the very first thing rendered.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus $500 in Lowe's gift cards and 50 runner-up prizes. Enter free.`
