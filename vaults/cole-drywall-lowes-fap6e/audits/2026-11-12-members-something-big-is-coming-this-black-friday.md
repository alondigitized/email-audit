# Members, something BIG is coming this Black Friday
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a teaser with nothing to tease. The subject promises "something BIG," and the body delivers a "Black Friday" banner, a blurry red image and a red block saying "Grab Black Friday savings ahead of schedule." There is no percentage, product, price or date in the body. The only specifics are in the preheader: "More details on 11/19." That is the one real piece of information, and it's hidden in preview text.
- The hero is the weakest part. A dark, out-of-focus red image with a barely readable bucket silhouette fills about 40% of the screen. It has two ornaments and two gift boxes pinned to the corners. It looks like a background waiting for a product that never got placed. For a drywall-focused recipient, nothing here signals joint compound, sheetrock, tools or any trade category.
- This send is the lightest in a heavy cadence. It follows 11/10 ("Why wait? Black Friday deals are already here"), 11/09 (appliances), 11/08 (holiday décor), 11/06 (tool +2 FREE) and 11/05 (gifts under $25/$50/$100). The brand voice is consistent, and the member framing is the one thing that makes it feel like a rewards send. Verdict: a thin placeholder that costs a send slot and gives the reader no reason to click.

## What's working

- The "MyLowe's Rewards" logo and the "Members," subject opener make it feel like a loyalty send.
- The "Black Friday" wordmark in white and red is bold and instantly readable, so the theme lands in about a second.
- A single white "Shop now" button on a red block is easy to see and easy to tap.
- The layout is clean, with no overlapping text or broken images, and the "We Help. You Save." sign-off keeps the brand voice intact.

## What's weak

- The body has no offer, no number, no product and no date, so "something BIG" is never paid off.
- The hero image is a large dark, blurred red field with a faint bucket shape. It looks like an unfinished or unrendered asset and wastes the prime screen space.
- "Shop now" points to nothing specific. "Savings ahead of schedule" is vague, and the real information ("More details on 11/19") appears only in the preheader.
- Nothing speaks to a drywall or pro-contractor audience. There is no category, no tool and no bulk or pro pricing.
- This is the 6th Black Friday send in about a week. The "ahead of schedule" framing repeats the 11/10 "Why wait?" idea.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put the 11/19 date and a hard number in the hero.** For example, "Black Friday starts 11/19. Members get early access to up to 40% off." Replace the blurry image with a real product or a clear date lockup.
- 2. **Give members a concrete early-access perk,** such as double rewards points or a deal that is members-only before launch. "Members" in the subject is wasted without one.
- 3. **Add a category row for the audience.** Tiles for drywall and joint compound, power tools and hand tools, each linking to its own page, would give a trade buyer something to click.
- 4. **Make the CTA specific.** "Shop early Black Friday deals" or "Browse tools & building supplies" beats "Shop now."
- 5. **Pull a send like this, or merge it** into the next real offer send, so the cadence isn't padded with near-empty emails.
