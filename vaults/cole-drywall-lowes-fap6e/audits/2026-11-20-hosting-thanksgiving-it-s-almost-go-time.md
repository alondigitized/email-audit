# Hosting Thanksgiving? It’s almost go time🌟
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- Lowe's sent another kitchen-sink Black Friday email, and the subject sets up a different story from the body. "Hosting Thanksgiving? It's almost go time" is a good, seasonal hook, and the turkey-fryer block ("The big feast is almost here," $129) delivers on it. But the first screen is a generic red "Black Friday: The deals came early" hero. A subscriber who opened for Thanksgiving hosting has to scroll past the member banner and that hero to find the fryer.
- After the fryer, the email turns into a catalog: a GE sponsored ad, a 50% off appliance block, a cutting board, GE lighting at 20% off, a $500k home sweepstakes, three random products, and a Deals of the Day signup. That is about nine competing CTAs, none of them clearly the lead. It looks like the same template as the 11/17 appliances send and the 11/16 "12 Days of Deals" send, with the fryer block swapped in.
- Judgement: a strong subject over a diluted body. The email would work better if the fryer led it.

## What's working

- The subject targets a real, time-sensitive need (Thanksgiving hosting) rather than a generic "Black Friday" message.
- The turkey fryer block is the best part. It has a single concrete price ("Your choice $129"), a strong lifestyle photo and a clear "Shop now" button.
- The appliance block puts "up to 50% off" in large type, with free delivery and a $250 laundry add-on stacked underneath.
- The myLowe's Rewards "FREE same-day delivery" banner gives members a visible benefit.
- The red and navy brand look is consistent and easy to recognise.

## What's weak

- The thing the subject promised sits third from the top. The first hero is the generic "Black Friday: The deals came early" banner.
- There are too many competing blocks and CTAs: fryer, GE ad, appliances, cutting board, lighting, sweepstakes, product row and Deals of the Day. No single action stands out.
- The cutting board tile ("For the home chef who makes every meal special") has no price or offer, so there is no reason to click.
- The "Curated with you in mind" product row (DEWALT battery, toilet, storage tote) has nothing to do with Thanksgiving. The product text is also cramped and truncated ("108.0 -").
- The legal fine print is long and dense. The appliance offer carries a lot of conditions, which weakens how honest it feels.
- The GE sponsored ad in the middle cuts the flow between the fryer and the appliance blocks.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the Thanksgiving fryer the hero and move it above the generic Black Friday banner. Follow it with a short "Thanksgiving host" strip: roasting pans, ovens, extra fridge space and folding tables.
- 2. Cut the body to three or four blocks: fryer, appliances at 50% off, one rotating "last-minute hosting" row, and the sweepstakes at the very end.
- 3. Remove or replace the random product row with items that fit the theme. Add a price and a "Pick up today" badge to each.
- 4. Give the cutting board tile a price or offer, or drop it.
- 5. Add Thanksgiving urgency ("Order by Tuesday for pickup before the holiday"), which would be more credible than the generic deal language.
- 6. Move the GE ad to the bottom, or remove it.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fryers from $129, plus up to 50% off appliances and free delivery.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Order by Tuesday: same-day delivery for members.`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The visible markup is mostly standard, but there are a few confirmed issues. The `<!DOCTYPE>` is malformed, `<html>` has no `lang`, and two content images lack alt text. The recipient's address also appears in plaintext in the ad-impression pixel URLs. Compliance, authentication and link-destination checks could not be confirmed from the data provided.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- **Recipient email in plaintext query strings.** All 17 `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixels carry `e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app`, for example `https://sli.lowes.com/imp?s=127768300&e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app&p=531283&lctg=410146703&stpe=pixel`. An unhashed address in a URL ends up in server and proxy logs and in any image-proxy cache.
- **39 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and HTTP status are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- No broken links were detected, but the QA data doesn't cover them.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Malformed DOCTYPE.** The source has `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" ...>`. There is no space after `PUBLIC`. Clients are usually tolerant of this, but it is invalid and can trigger quirks mode in some parsers.
- **No `lang` attribute.** The tag is `<html xmlns="https://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">`. Screen readers can't pick the language.
- **Content images missing alt text.**
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg`
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png`

  If either carries content or a CTA, it is invisible with images off.
- **Tracking pixels without `alt`.** The 17 `sli.lowes.com/imp` images have no alt. These are likely 1×1 pixels, so the fix is `alt=""` (plus `width="1" height="1"`). That is low severity.
- A viewport meta tag and mobile media queries (`max-width: 480px`) are present. The Outlook `<!--[if !mso]>` font wrappers are used correctly.
- The HTML was truncated, so I can't evaluate `<title>`, table roles or the body.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source. The only personalization is the address embedded in the pixel URLs (see section 2).

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed**, not a verified violation.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, for the same relay reason. The From domain `e.lowes.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, but alignment couldn't be checked.
- The body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated source, so they could not be checked.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity**
No issues found, but little could be verified. Because the 39 click-tracking links were not resolved, UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be assessed. No UTMs are visible in the supplied source.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Stop putting the raw recipient address in pixel URLs. Use an opaque or hashed subscriber ID in `sli.lowes.com/imp`. This is the highest-priority item.
2. Fix the DOCTYPE by adding the missing space: `PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD…"`.
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
4. Add descriptive `alt` to `c301e24e-….jpg` and `ad569dc5-….png`, and `alt=""` to the `imp` pixels.
5. To close the QA gaps, re-run with raw `.eml` headers (List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, Authentication-Results) and the full untruncated HTML. Also resolve the 39 tracking redirects to check destinations and UTMs.
