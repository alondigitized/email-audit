# Homepage snapshot · www.lowes.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- First thing that hits me is a login popup before I can even look at what's on sale — feels like the door greeter stopping me before I can grab a cart. Lowe's is clearly pushing Memorial Day hard, which is exactly the right call for me since I'm always shopping appliance deals this weekend. But the modal blocking everything is annoying when I just want to see what's marked down on tools and appliances.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day banner up top** — "Get your home summer-ready with Memorial Day savings" lands exactly right since this is my appliance-buying window every year. Good timing.
- **Sign In modal with Silver Key callout** — I've been building toward Silver Key status; seeing that benefit listed reminds me there's a reason to be loyal here.
- **"Tool Box" recommendation chip** — even buried under the modal with bathroom vanity options, that pill shows the site has some sense of what I buy.
- **"Free same-day delivery on select items"** — that's genuinely useful when I'm mid-project and run out of compound or screws.

## What's weak

- **The forced Sign In modal** — I came here to shop Memorial Day deals, not to authenticate before I can even see what's on sale. I already have a MyLowe's Rewards account and a Lowe's Advantage card. This feels like Lowe's asking me for ID before letting me walk in.
- **Garden section visible below** — "Grow a beautiful garden with organic…" is the first branded hero I can see under the modal. I'm finishing a basement. That's a miss.
- **"Bathroom Vanity" chips dominating recommendations** — two of the three visible chips are bathroom vanity. Not why I'm here.
- **Asterisks without visible fine print** — both the $5 off and the Silver Key free shipping have asterisks but no explanation visible. That's the kind of thing that makes me skeptical.
- **No Kobalt, no appliance, no tools imagery** — nothing above the fold speaks to what I actually come to Lowe's to buy.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the modal for recognized returning users** — if my MyLowe's cookie is active, just greet me by name in the nav and skip the modal entirely. Show me my rewards balance instead.
- **Swap the garden hero for a tools/appliance Memorial Day hero** — show a job site refrigerator, a Kobalt tool set, or a washer-dryer stack with a big red "Memorial Day Price" tag. That's what I'm here for.
- **Surface a concrete appliance deal above the fold** — "Whirlpool washer — $150 off through May 26" beats "Summer's hottest deals start now" for guys like me who show up specifically for appliance markdown weekends.
- **Replace two of the three recommendation chips** with category-relevant ones — if the site knows I buy tools, show "Power Tools", "Drywall", or "Appliances" before "Bathroom Vanity."
