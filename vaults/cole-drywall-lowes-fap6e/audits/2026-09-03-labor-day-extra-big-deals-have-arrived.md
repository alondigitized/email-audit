# Labor Day Extra Big Deals have arrived👉
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-03**
## Executive summary

- This is Lowe's leading with the same "Save up to $800" blue hero it's run twice already this week (8/31, 9/2) — third time in four days for near-identical creative, and the fatigue is starting to show. The layout below the hero is a genuine sprawl: appliances, power tools, then a jarring skeleton Halloween decoration next to a wheelbarrow lawn-care shot, a credit financing block, three unrelated "curated" items, and a Deals of the Day banner — nine-plus modules with no single clear next action.
- The one standout, and the one real problem: a glowing-eyed Halloween skeleton prop ("Haunted Living 5-ft Ground Breaker Reaper") is sitting in a Labor Day email in early September. It's visually well-shot but completely out of season and off-message, and next to a lawn-mower photo it reads like two random modules got shuffled together rather than curated.
- Net: this is a lower-tier send from Lowe's — recycled hero, sprawling body, and one genuinely confusing seasonal mismatch that undercuts the "curated with you in mind" line further down the email.

## What's working

- The hero is clean and legible: "Extra Big Deals," "5 Days Only," "Save up to $800" in a clear vertical stack with one white CTA button.
- The two-up "Limited Time Deals" module (appliances $800 off + free delivery, power equipment $130 off) has real, specific numbers and its own CTAs.
- The $199/Save $130 haunted decoration card has genuine price-and-discount specificity, which is more than most modules below the fold offer.
- Footer credit-offer disclosure and unsubscribe/account-management links are present and legible.

## What's weak

- Recycled hero: same "Save up to $800" blue leaf-scatter treatment as 9/2 and 8/31 — third run of this exact creative pattern in a week.
- The Halloween skeleton prop next to a lawn/wheelbarrow photo is a jarring, out-of-season, out-of-place pairing for a Labor Day email — no visual or thematic connection to the deals around it.
- Nine-plus stacked modules (appliances, tools, Halloween decor, lawn care, credit financing, three random "curated" products, Deals of the Day) with no hierarchy — the eye has nowhere obvious to land after the hero.
- The "Curated with you in mind" module (storage tote, cleaner, faux-wood blinds) reads as generic cross-sell, not personalization — undercuts its own headline.
- "More savings? Just a click away" banner is a filler CTA with no offer attached to it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Retire the "Save up to $800" blue hero — it's run three times in a week and is losing all differentiation. Rotate to a fresh visual (the 8/31 porch-scene version tested better at 8.0/10; go back to lifestyle imagery, not flat color fields).
- 2. Cut the Halloween decoration module entirely from a Labor Day send, or move it to its own seasonal email — it actively confuses the message right now.
- 3. Trim the module count to 3-4 max: hero, one appliance/tool deal, one lawn/patio deal, one CTA to browse more. Cut the credit financing block and "curated" product trio, or push them below a clear fold marker.
- 4. Give the "More savings" banner an actual offer or number instead of vague filler copy.
- **Subject Alt A:** `5 days left: up to $800 off appliances`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Labor Day tools & lawn deals — up to $800 off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus free delivery on major appliances over $999`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Power tools, lawn gear, and appliance deals — 5 days only`
