- This is a tidy, seasonal Lowe's send with a clear hero: "Save up to $150" on select power tools, with a DeWALT kit pictured and a visible "Shop now" button. The "DIYing this fall? The subject's "fall to-do list" is picked up again in the mid-email banner, "Let's knock that fall to-do list out."
- The rest of the email splits its attention. After the hero come leaf blowers, pressure washers and mowers, then tool storage and ladders, then gutter-protection services, then a "Curated with you in mind" product row, then a Deals of the Day banner. It follows the Oct 23 "FREE power tool" email, though, so the power-tool angle is a near repeat. I'd call it a solid 6: concrete offer, clear hero, too many competing asks.

## What's working

- The hero offer is concrete: a green "Save up to $150" tag beside a DeWALT tool cluster.
- The "Let's knock that fall to-do list out" banner follows through on the subject's theme with a lifestyle image of leaf blowing.
- Each secondary block has its own number: "up to 30% off" tool storage and "Save up to $40" ladders.
- The category icon row (leaf blowers, pressure washers, mowers) is easy to scan and fits the season.
- The nav bar and the Lowe's branding are clean, and I see no broken images or layout bugs.

## What's weak

- Too many blocks and too many identical blue buttons dilute the main offer.
- The "Curated with you in mind" row shows no prices or savings, and the product names look truncated ("DEWALT 20 -Volt 2 - Pack 8 Ah 8 Ah Lithium-ion ) -").
- The "Buy now" buttons on those products give no reason to buy now.
- The gutter-protection banner has an asterisk and no price. It is a services upsell that doesn't fit a power-tool and cleanup email.
- The Deals of the Day banner and signup block close the email with a generic ask.
- The "Save up to $150" claim has no deadline or end date in the render.
- Nothing visible tells me the email is personalized to me, and the audience isn't clear.

- 2. Put a deadline in the hero, for example "Ends Sunday."
- 3. Add prices or "was/now" savings to the product row, and fix the truncated product names.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's visible markup is a table-based XHTML Transitional template with MSO/Outlook handling. Findings are a malformed DOCTYPE, two content images without alt text, and recipient email addresses embedded in ad-impression pixel URLs. The source was truncated and header checks were inconclusive, so compliance and UTM checks could not be completed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **40 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains and landing status are unverified.
- **Recipient email in pixel URLs.** Every `sli.lowes.com/imp?...` request carries `e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app`. For example: `https://sli.lowes.com/imp?s=127768300&e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app&p=528290&lctg=410146703&stpe=pixel`. This puts a plaintext identifier in URLs that proxies, CDNs and ad-server logs will record. It was seen on at least 17 impression pixels, spanning `p=528290` and `p=10272026`.
- **Many third-party impression pixels.** The ad-server (`sli.lowes.com`) calls include `stpe=pixel`, `static` and `default` variants, plus a `li_coord` parameter. Some of these are truncated in the QA output, so their full form couldn't be checked.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed DOCTYPE.** The source has `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" ...>`, with no space between `PUBLIC` and the quoted identifier. Some parsers may drop to quirks mode, though most clients ignore it.
- **Missing alt on content images.**
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg`
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png`

  If these carry content or are linked, screen readers and images-off clients will get nothing or a filename.
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels.** The 17 `sli.lowes.com/imp` images lack `alt`. If they are 1x1 pixels, they should use `alt=""` explicitly, so this is low severity.
- **Namespace and web fonts.** `xmlns` and the DTD URL use `https://www.w3.org/...`. This is harmless. Web fonts (Fellix, DIN Next) are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, so Outlook gets fallbacks. The font stacks aren't visible in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The recipient address is substituted correctly in the pixel URLs, and no unresolved tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) are visible in the truncated source. The rest of the body wasn't available to check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed sender defect.
- **`Authentication-Results` is missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown from this data. The sender is `lowes@e.lowes.com`.
- **Footer not verified.** The visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address are outside the truncated source, so they could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Unable to assess. Click URLs are wrapped in redirect domains that were skipped, and no destination or UTM parameters appear in the visible source. No issues are confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Stop putting the raw email address in ad-pixel query strings. Use a hashed or opaque subscriber ID in `e=`.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to `c301e24e-...jpg` and `ad569dc5-...png`. Use `alt=""` on the `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixels.
3. Fix the DOCTYPE to `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" ...>`.
4. Re-test with raw headers. Check for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment on `e.lowes.com`. The relay strips them, so the QA harness can't verify these.
5. Follow the 40 redirect links to confirm final destinations and UTM carry-through.
6. Run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer unsubscribe link and postal address.
