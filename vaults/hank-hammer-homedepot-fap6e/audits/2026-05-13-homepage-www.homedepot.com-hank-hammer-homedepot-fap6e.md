# Homepage snapshot · www.homedepot.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Good — I can see the page clearly. Writing the review now.
- This is my store, no question about it — The Home Depot mobile homepage is recognizable the second it loads, and it's pushing garden soil, power tools, and appliance deals all at once. The orange is loud, the layout is clean, and within about two seconds I can see something relevant to me. Feels like a late-spring push: get the yard going, grab a drill while it's on sale.

## What's working

- **"Create Thriving Gardens" hero** — Mid-May timing is perfect. I've got raised beds to fill and I'm already thinking topsoil. Vigoro bag is right there on screen. That's a real product I buy.
- **"Up to $120 Off Select Tools" banner** — DeWalt drill in the image. That's my brand neighborhood. Price-off on tools gets my attention every single time.
- **Location + hours at top** — "Burbank 10PM" tells me instantly which store I'm tied to and that it's open right now. Huge for in-store trip planning.
- **Search bar front and center** — I use this thing constantly for inventory check before I drive over. It's right where I expect it.
- **Bottom nav is clean** — Shop All, Services, DIY, Log In — simple enough that I don't have to hunt.

## What's weak

- **No Pro Xtra / rewards hook above the fold** — I've been a Pro Xtra member for years. Not a single mention of my points balance or member pricing. That's a missed open for a returning customer.
- **The Maytag banner is thin and easy to ignore** — "Up to $400 off Laundry Pairs" is buried in a navy strip. For a $400 deal that's surprisingly easy to scroll past.
- **Garden hero has no product specificity** — "Must-Haves for Outdoor Projects" is vague. Show me mulch, topsoil, raised-bed kits — something I can tap directly.
- **Tool deal doesn't say which tools** — "Select Tools" is doing a lot of work. I want to know if it's DeWalt, Milwaukee, Husky — before I bother tapping.
- **No urgency on any offer** — no "ends Sunday," no countdown. I can come back any time, so there's no pull to act now.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Pro Xtra balance chip in the header** — even a simple "You have 1,240 points" next to the cart icon would remind me why I'm loyal here and pull me deeper.
- **Give the tool deal a brand callout** — swap "Select Tools" for "DeWalt, Milwaukee & Husky — Up to $120 Off" so I know in one glance whether it's worth tapping.
- **Put a deadline on one of the offers** — add "Ends Sunday 5/18" to the tool banner. Costs nothing and creates a reason to act today instead of next weekend.
- **Make the garden hero shoppable by category** — swap the single "Shop Now" for two quick-tap tiles: "Soil & Mulch" and "Raised Beds." One extra tap is one extra reason to leave.
