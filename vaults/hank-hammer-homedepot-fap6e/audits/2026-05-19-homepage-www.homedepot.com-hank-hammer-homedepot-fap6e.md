# Homepage snapshot · www.homedepot.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Home Depot comes out swinging for Memorial Day — this is my weekend and they know it. Big orange hero, Milwaukee tools front and center, specific price drops on the stuff I actually buy. Feels like a store circular that loaded right on my phone, which is exactly what I want before I head over to Alhambra.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day hero with real prices**: Showing $379 was $419 on a trimmer and $389.99 was $549.99 on a grill — I don't need to hunt for the deal, it's right there. That's how you talk to me.
- **"Up to $175 Off Select Tools & Outdoor Power" banner**: Milwaukee lawn mower and angle grinder with an orange "Shop Now" — this is the section I came for. One tap away.
- **"Fast Free Delivery" copy in the hero**: I already know the store but for the heavy stuff, free delivery matters. Good to see it.
- **Store locator with hours ("Alhambra 6AM")**: Already knows my store, already shows me it's open. I'm not calling ahead.
- **Bottom nav with "DIY" tab**: Quick path to project guides if I need to check something while I'm in the aisle.
- **Memorial Day secondary strip ("Get It Today / Do More Summer Hosting")**: Reminds me I've got that patio project sitting on my list.

## What's weak

- **No Pro Xtra hook anywhere visible**: I've got the card, I've earned the points. Showing me my balance or a member-price badge would've had me tapping immediately instead of just browsing.
- **"While Supplies Last" is weak urgency**: No countdown, no "ends Monday" — if Memorial Day deals run out Monday at midnight, tell me. I'll drive over Sunday instead of waiting.
- **No product ratings or reviews**: The grill at $389.99 — is it any good? Even one star rating would help. I don't buy tools blind.
- **The 25% card at the bottom is half cut off**: I can see "UP 25%" and greenery but have no idea what the offer is. That's a missed impression.
- **Live Chat and Feedback tabs floating on the right edge**: They're cropping into the content area on mobile. Clutters the look, and I'm not going to use Live Chat anyway — I'll just ask someone in the orange apron.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Pro Xtra banner strip just below the hero** — "Welcome back, Hank. Your balance: X points." Takes one line and it would stop me from bouncing to check the app separately.
- **Put a hard deadline on the Memorial Day deals** — "Ends Monday, May 26 at midnight" in the hero subhead. "While Supplies Last" doesn't make me move, a date does.
- **Add star ratings to the featured products** — The grill and the refrigerator are big-ticket. Show even a condensed "4.5 ★ (2,341 reviews)" under the price. Takes no extra space in the card layout.
- **Scroll-reveal or expand the cut-off 25% card** — Either make it full height or turn it into a tappable strip so I know what the deal is. Right now it's just dead real estate.
