# Homepage snapshot · www.homedepot.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- This is my store, and it knows it. First thing I see is spring yard stuff — mower on sale, patio set marked down, a grill at $499 — that's exactly the kind of stuff I'm hauling out to the driveway to size up right now. Home Depot's pushing "Spring Backyard Days" hard, which makes sense for May, but there's a sign-in pop-up sitting right on top of the deals and it's the first thing eating my screen.

## What's working

- **"Spring Backyard Days / Wake Up Your Yard" hero banner** — that copy lands perfectly for me. I've got a list of yard projects and this banner is basically reading my mind.
- **Deals grid with was/now pricing** — the $449 mower (was $479), $448 patio set (was $648), and the 5 for $10 garden supply tile all show real numbers. I can do the math in my head and decide whether it's worth a run to Burbank.
- **"FAST FREE DELIVERY*"** — I'd prefer to pick up in-store, but knowing delivery is free on big items like that patio set takes the pressure off hauling it myself.
- **Burbank / 91502 store locator at top** — my local store hours right there (10PM). That's practical. I check inventory before I drive, and this tells me the store's still open.
- **"#1 Home Improvement Retailer" banner** — sure, I already know that, but it's a clean trust signal for anyone second-guessing the site.
- **Grill tile at $499** — BBQ season is here. That price point is in my range and one tap might show me something worth adding to the run.

## What's weak

- **Sign-in modal blocking the page** — that "Sign in for the best experience" pop-up is covering the bottom third of my screen. It doesn't mention Pro Xtra rewards or anything that tells me *why* I'd bother. I'm going to tap the X immediately.
- **No Pro Xtra / rewards hook** — I'm a Pro Xtra member and I earn on every purchase. This homepage doesn't acknowledge that at all. Wayfair and Lowe's both surface your points balance on load. That's leaving money on the table.
- **Category nav missing** — I don't see any quick-tap category row (Tools, Lumber, Electrical, Plumbing). If I want to check lumber prices, I'm going straight to the search bar, which I would've done anyway, but a browse option helps me discover things I didn't know I needed.
- **No free-shipping threshold shown** — "Fast Free Delivery*" is nice, but what's the minimum? I have to dig for that. If it's $45 or $50, I probably hit it. Just tell me.
- **No ratings on the product tiles** — every tile shows a price but zero stars. I'm not going to buy a lawn mower without checking reviews first, and I'd like to see whether what's featured is actually well-rated before I tap.

## Recommendations

- **Surface my Pro Xtra balance in the header** — even just a small "Hi Hank — 4,200 points" next to the cart icon would stop me from tapping the X on that sign-in modal and actually make me want to log in.
- **Replace the generic sign-in modal with a Pro Xtra-specific hook** — "Sign in to see member pricing and your reward balance" is a reason. "Best experience" is not.
- **Add star ratings (even just aggregate) to every product tile** — the deals grid would convert harder if I could see "4.5★ (1,247)" on that mower tile without having to tap in.
- **Stamp the free-shipping threshold on the delivery callout** — "Fast Free Delivery on orders over $45" fits in the same space and answers the question before I ask it.
