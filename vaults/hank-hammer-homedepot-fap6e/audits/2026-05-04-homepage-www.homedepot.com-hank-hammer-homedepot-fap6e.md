## What's working

- **Store location pinned at the top (Burbank, 10PM, 91502):** That's exactly what I need when I'm doing an inventory check before a store run. Saves me a phone call.
- **"#1 Home Improvement Retailer" badge in the orange header:** Not fancy, but it's the kind of credibility signal I notice and appreciate. Straightforward.
- **Concrete "Was / Now" pricing in the product grid:** $199 was $249, $398 was $548 — I know exactly what the deal is. No hunting for the real price. That's how you talk to me.
- **"Fast Free Delivery" callout:** That's useful for big-ticket items I don't want to haul in the truck. Good to see it front and center even with the asterisk.
- **Spring Backyard Days is seasonally accurate:** It's May, backyard season is on. They're not running a Christmas banner in spring. That's table stakes but at least they cleared it.

## What's weak

- **Forced sign-in modal blocking content:** A "Sign in for the best experience" sheet covering the bottom half of the screen is the kind of thing that makes a 58-year-old tap the X and go back to Google. I haven't even seen the page yet — why are you asking me to log in?
- **Zero tools visibility above the fold:** I buy tools every weekend. Dewalt, Milwaukee, Husky — none of it is on screen. This might as well be a patio furniture site right now.
- **Pro Xtra rewards nowhere to be found:** I have a Pro Xtra account. Show me my rewards balance, my tier, something. That's the hook that makes me buy here instead of Ace.
- **No lumber, no hardware, no project materials:** This is a homeowner homepage for a homeowner who shops by category, and the categories I live in (lumber, electrical, plumbing, power tools) are completely absent above the fold.
- **Asterisk on "Fast Free Delivery\*":** That little star is going to mean "lower 48, some exclusions, ground shipping only" — I know the game. Not dishonest, just mildly annoying.

## Recommendations

- **Surface a "Tools & Hardware" quick-access tile above the fold** alongside the patio deals — one row of category tiles (Tools, Lumber & Building Materials, Electrical, Plumbing) would let guys like me get to what they need in one tap without fighting through seasonal promo content.
- **Replace or relocate the forced sign-in modal with an inline Pro Xtra rewards badge** — show my points balance and tier right in the header once I'm in the app. That's a returning-shopper hook that earns a tap, not a speed bump that earns an X.
- **Add a "Check Store Inventory" shortcut near the location line** — since Burbank and 91502 are already pinned, a one-tap "What's in stock near me?" CTA right there would be the single most useful thing for a twice-a-weekend store runner like me.
- **Swap the generic "Shop Now" CTA copy on the hero for something project-specific** — "Build Your Backyard" or "Outfit the Deck" gives me something to mentally engage with instead of the most generic three words in retail.
