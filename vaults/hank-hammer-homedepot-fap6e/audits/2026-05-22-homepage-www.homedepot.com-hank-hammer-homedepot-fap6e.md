# Homepage snapshot · www.homedepot.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- This is Home Depot doing exactly what Home Depot does best around a holiday weekend — hammering you with deals on tools, outdoor power, and grills before Memorial Day closes out. The orange brand is everywhere, the pricing is upfront, and the message is simple: final days, shop now. For a guy like me with a project list and Pro Xtra card, this feels like my store talking directly to me.

## What's working

- **"FINAL DAYS Memorial Day Deals End 5/27"** top banner — I see that and I know I've got five days. That's exactly the kind of nudge that gets me off the couch and into the app.
- **$175 off Tools & Outdoor Power section** — the string trimmer and chainsaw imagery is on point. I'm always in that aisle. This is the deal I came for.
- **Product tile grid with "Was" prices** — $299 grill, $449 mower, $398 patio set with original pricing visible. I can do that math fast and know if it's a real deal.
- **Store location pinned at top (Alhambra, 6AM)** — I check inventory on the app and then drive in. Seeing my store already dialed in feels right.
- **"FAST FREE DELIVERY*"** — good to know even if I usually grab it myself.

## What's weak

- **Zero Pro Xtra callout** — I've got a rewards card and I'm earning points on every run. Not a single mention of Pro Xtra, member pricing, or my balance. That's a missed swing at a loyal customer.
- **The $729 appliance tile** — looks like a washer. I'm here for tools and lumber, not laundry. Wastes a prime slot in the product grid for my use case.
- **No product ratings on the tiles** — I see prices but no star counts. For a grill at $299 or a mower at $449, I want to know what other guys think before I drive in.
- **Hero is graphic-only, no real product or person** — Memorial Day text on a flat color background. A lifestyle shot of a guy actually running one of those mowers would pull me in harder.
- **Asterisk on "FAST FREE DELIVERY*"** — fine print on a free offer always makes me suspicious. What's the catch?

## Recommendations

- **Inject a Pro Xtra member banner above the fold** — even a one-line "Pro Xtra members earn 2X on Memorial Day deals" would make me feel seen and tap faster.
- **Swap the $729 appliance tile for a tool or lumber product** — miter saw, impact driver, pressure-treated 2x4 bundle pricing. That's what I came here for.
- **Add star ratings to the product tiles** — just the average score and review count. Four stars with 2,800 reviews tells me more than the "Was" price.
- **Run the asterisk out** — either make free delivery actually free with no fine print, or state the threshold right in the banner. "Free delivery on orders $45+" is honest and still compelling.
