# Homepage snapshot · www.homedepot.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- Home Depot's coming at me hard with Memorial Day deals right out of the gate — big orange banner across the top, splash hero with product pricing, the whole nine yards. This looks like a brand that knows their customer is going to be making runs this weekend to kick off outdoor projects. They're pushing tools, outdoor power equipment, and patio/grill stuff, which is squarely in my wheelhouse. My one gripe right off the bat: there's a "Sign in for the best experience" modal sitting right on top of the page I'm trying to read.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day hero banner** — hits exactly at the right moment. I'm already mentally planning this weekend's store run, and this confirms they've got deals I need to look at.
- **Specific price callouts** ($5.97 on what looks like a spray product, $379 string trimmer, $389.99 grill, $1,599 refrigerator) — I appreciate seeing real numbers, not just "great deals." I can immediately compare to what I've seen in-aisle.
- **"Up to $175 Off Select Tools & Outdoor Power" module** — that Milwaukee drill and Ryobi circular saw image? That's my language. Those brands, that gear, those savings — I'm reading it.
- **Local store context** — "Alhambra 10PM" and zip "91803" tells me they know where I am and what's open. I check inventory before I drive; this builds confidence.
- **"Get it Today / Do More Summer Hosting" strip** — I don't do delivery if I can grab it in-store. That "Get it Today" message is exactly what keeps me off Amazon.
- **Top banner urgency** — "MEMORIAL DAY DEALS While Supplies Last. Shop Now" is direct, no fluff.

## What's weak

- **Sign-in modal blocks content** — A "Sign in for the best experience" overlay covers the lower portion of the page. I don't want to be nagged to log in before I've even seen what's on sale. I'll dismiss it, but it's friction I don't need.
- **No Pro Xtra / rewards hook above the fold** — I've got a Pro Xtra account and I spend real money here. Not a single badge, balance, or member-pricing callout visible. That's a miss when I'm the guy who'd buy the $1,599 fridge if I knew I was racking up points.
- **Small fine print under hero** — "Configured U.S. Only. *Subject to availability" sits under the main hero. Not a dealbreaker, but it introduces a hesitation tick right when you want me confident.
- **Refrigerator feels out of place** — The $1,599 refrigerator price callout is sitting next to a string trimmer and a grill. The appliance category break feels random, not curated for a weekend project mindset.
- **No category navigation visible** — I don't see a browsable department list. If I want lumber or electrical, I'm going straight to search rather than browsing — that's a missed upsell opportunity for someone like me who impulse-picks up related items.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the sign-in modal by default or delay it** — show it after I've been on the page 15 seconds or when I try to add to cart. Don't gate the homepage with it.
- **Add a Pro Xtra badge or rewards balance callout** in the header next to the cart icon — even just "Pro Xtra Member | X points" would make me feel seen and incentivize a bigger basket.
- **Swap the refrigerator price callout for a lumber/building materials deal** — Memorial Day is when I'm planning deck projects, not appliance shopping. Feature a deck board sale or fastener bundle instead.
- **Add a "Available in Alhambra Store" chip to the featured deals** — a small "In Stock at Your Store" badge on the Milwaukee and Ryobi products would push me straight to the store this afternoon.
