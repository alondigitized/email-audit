# Homepage snapshot · www.homedepot.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Right off the bat, Home Depot's talking my language — Memorial Day sale, grills, lawn equipment, and tools discounted up to $175. This is a working-man's weekend store, and the homepage knows it. They're pushing hard on the holiday deal with a clear end date, which I appreciate more than some vague "limited time" line.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day hero with real prices:** $299 for a grill and $449 for a mower in the tile grid — I don't have to guess, I can see the deal immediately
- **"Ends 5/27" urgency:** Four days out is a credible deadline. I'll stop by before Tuesday.
- **"UP TO $175 OFF Select Tools & Outdoor Power" banner:** That string trimmer and Ryobi saw image gets my attention fast — that's the stuff sitting on my garage shelf or on my list
- **"Get It Today" summer hosting section:** Patio furniture and outdoor entertaining — that's May in a nutshell
- **"Fast Free Delivery" copy in the hero:** I might be a store guy, but knowing it's an option means I can buy the big stuff without hauling it in the truck
- **My local store and hours right up top:** "Alhambra 6AM" — I know when I can get there tomorrow morning

## What's weak

- **No Pro Xtra or rewards hook:** I've got points sitting in that account and this page doesn't acknowledge me as a member at all. Just a generic "Log In" in the footer nav. That's a miss.
- **No people in the imagery:** Every shot is a product on a white or orange background. Would be nice to see a guy like me actually using one of those tools.
- **The $1,599 refrigerator tile feels out of place:** Four product tiles in the hero grid — grill, mower, patio chairs, and then a full-size fridge. The fridge breaks the outdoor/summer pattern and dilutes the focus.
- **No review counts on any of the featured products:** Prices are there, but zero stars or ratings visible. I want to know if that $299 grill is a crowd-pleaser or a return magnet.
- **"Subject to availability" asterisk on fast delivery:** Fine print right in the hero. I notice those.

## Recommendations

- **Surface my Pro Xtra balance in the header when I'm logged in:** Even just "Hi Hank — 3,200 points" builds loyalty and gives me a reason to stay in the app instead of just walking the aisles.
- **Replace the refrigerator tile with a decking, mulch, or concrete product:** Keep the hero grid seasonal and outdoor-focused — that's what Memorial Day means for a homeowner with a weekend project.
- **Add a "Top-Rated" badge or star count to the price tiles:** Even a "4.6★ (2,841)" under the grill price would close me faster. I've been burned by cheap grills before.
- **Swap the all-product-shot banner for one lifestyle photo** — a guy on a riding mower or running a circular saw in a backyard. Give me something to see myself in.
