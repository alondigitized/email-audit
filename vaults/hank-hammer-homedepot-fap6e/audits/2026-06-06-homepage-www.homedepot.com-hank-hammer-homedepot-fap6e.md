# Homepage snapshot · www.homedepot.com · 2026-06-06
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-06**
## Executive summary

- Right out of the gate, this is my store — orange everywhere, Father's Day front and center, and a DeWalt drill staring me in the face. Home Depot is clearly running a full Father's Day push with real price tags showing what things "were" versus what I'd pay today. Nothing fancy, no lifestyle fluff — just products, deals, and a deadline.

## What's working

- **Father's Day banner at the very top**: "FATHER'S DAY IS JUNE 21st. Save on Gifts For Dad. Shop Now." — that's the first thing I see. Hard to miss, tells me exactly what's on sale and why.
- **"TOP 100+ GIFTS FOR DAD / FIND THE RIGHT PICK"**: The hero copy is direct and aimed squarely at me, or honestly at my wife shopping *for* me. Either way, I'm interested.
- **Product grid with Was/Now pricing**: $129 was $149 on a DeWalt drill, $229 was $279 on the trimmer, $299 was $399 on what looks like a grill setup, $24.97 on string lights — those are real numbers I can evaluate without digging. This is how I shop: I know what things cost, and I can tell a real deal from a fake one.
- **"FAST FREE DELIVERY"**: Visible right on the promo block. That removes the mental math of "do I hit the threshold?"
- **Grill section at bottom**: "Up to 25% off Select Grills, Outdoor Lighting & More" with a pellet-style grill photo — Father's Day and summer grilling overlap perfectly for me.
- **Location pinned to "Alhambra 6AM"**: Tells me the store's open early. I do my hardware runs before traffic. That time stamp is actually useful.

## What's weak

- **No Pro Xtra rewards callout**: I'm a Pro Xtra member. I get exclusive pricing. Not a word about it anywhere on screen. That's a missed hook — they've got my data, they know I've spent thousands, and the homepage treats me like a stranger.
- **No star ratings on the product tiles**: I see the DeWalt drill at $129 but no rating below it. I know my DeWalt but a newer guy doesn't. One line of stars would close that doubt.
- **"Live Chat" and "Feedback" tabs floating on the right**: These partially overlap the product grid content. Functional but sloppy — they cover part of the layout on a phone screen.
- **Gift card banner feels like filler**: The "Give Him the Gift of Choice" gift card strip between the deal grid and the grill section — if I wanted a gift card I'd already know where to find it. Takes up real estate that could show me another deal in power tools or lumber.
- **No category shortcut for "Power Tools" or "Lumber"**: My two reasons for being in this app. The bottom nav has "Shop All," "Services," "DIY," "Log In" — nothing that gets me directly to my aisles in one tap.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Pro Xtra loyalty bar under the header**: One line — "Pro Xtra Member: You have X points · Member pricing active." I should know my balance before I even think about browsing. It's why I signed up.
- **Put star ratings on the deal tiles**: The product grid shows price cuts — add a rating below each tile. Two seconds of extra trust that would seal the tap for me or a gift-buyer on the fence.
- **Swap the gift card banner for a "Top Power Tool Deals" row**: Gift cards are fine, but a guy who runs here twice a weekend wants to see drills, batteries, and circular saws, not placeholder card art. Replace that strip with a horizontal scroll of tool deals.
- **Pin a "My Store: Alhambra" quick-link to power tools availability**: If the app already knows my store is Alhambra with a 6AM open, one tap to "Check In-Store Availability" would be the most practical feature on the page.
