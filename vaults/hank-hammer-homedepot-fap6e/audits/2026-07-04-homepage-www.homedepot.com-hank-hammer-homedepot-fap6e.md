# Homepage snapshot · www.homedepot.com · 2026-07-04
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-04**
## Executive summary

- Alright, got a good look at it. Here's my take.
- Home Depot knows it's the Fourth of July and they're not wasting anybody's time — the whole page is running hot with sale pricing on tools, outdoor power, and A/Cs. It feels like walking into the store and having a floor guy wave you straight to the clearance aisle before you even set your coffee down. Right now this site is pushing 4th of July deals hard, and for someone like me who's got a project list and a patio to finish before the cookout starts, that's exactly the right message on the right day.

## What's working

- **The 4th of July hero** hits at exactly the right moment — red/white/blue, clear sale framing, no ambiguity about what's happening
- **"In Store & Online + Fast Free Delivery"** — that combo matters to me. I want to know I can check stock and grab it today if I need to
- **The "Up to $165 OFF Select Tools & Outdoor Power" banner** with the DeWalt bags and the green mower is a direct shot at my Saturday morning shopping list
- **"While Supplies Last" + specific original prices** — gives me urgency without feeling like a scam. I can see the real number was $849 and now it's $589
- **My store location ("Alhambra") already set** — the app remembered where I shop, which tells me this isn't my first rodeo with their app

## What's weak

- **No Pro Xtra hook whatsoever** — I've got that card, I use it every visit, and there's nothing up top telling me my points or whether this sale stacks with member pricing. That's a miss.
- **No product names, just prices** — I can see "$589 Was $849" but what IS that item? Looks like maybe a pressure washer but I can't tell from the thumbnail. Make me work to find out what I'm shopping.
- **No trust signals** — no review counts, no "4.7 stars on 2,400 reviews" on any featured item. I don't buy tools blind.
- **"While Supplies Last" without a specific deadline** — if it ends tonight at midnight, tell me. Don't string me along.
- **Bottom nav is just four items** — Shop All, Services, DIY, Log In — and none of them say "Tools" or "Outdoor Power" by name. One extra tap I shouldn't need.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Pro Xtra welcome strip above the hero** — "Welcome back, Hank. 1,240 points. Sale prices stack with your member discount." That's what I came to see and it would take me two seconds to decide to shop.
- **Put the product name on each deal tile** — even six words like "Ryobi 2300-PSI Pressure Washer" next to "$589 Was $849" so I don't have to tap through to find out if it's worth my time.
- **Add a countdown or specific end date** — "Sale ends July 6 at midnight" does more work than "While Supplies Last." I'll come back tomorrow if I know I have time; I'll skip it if I don't.
- **Swap the bottom nav "Shop All" for "Tools" and "Outdoor"** — two taps becomes one for the customer who is already holding a shopping list.
