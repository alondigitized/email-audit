# Homepage snapshot · www.homedepot.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Right out of the gate this looks like my store — Memorial Day deals plastered everywhere, tools and outdoor power up front, real prices with the "was" crossed out. This is Home Depot doing what Home Depot does: give the weekend warrior a reason to buy something he was going to buy anyway, just a little faster. Heavy push on outdoor power and summer entertaining right now, which makes sense given the season.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day banner with real markdown prices** — $389.99 was $549.99 on the grill, $379 was $419 on that string trimmer. That's the kind of specific number I can hold in my head when I walk the aisle.
- **"Select Tools & Outdoor Power — Up to $175 Off"** banner — that lawn mower and angle grinder image is exactly what I've got on my list right now. It stops the scroll.
- **Store context at the top** — shows me "Burbank 10PM" and zip 91502. I know my store is open, I know my location is set. That's useful before I even start tapping.
- **"#1 Home Improvement Retailer" credibility bar** — low-key but it's there, reinforces I'm in the right place.
- **Bottom nav is clean** — Shop All, Services, DIY, Log In. Four taps, no clutter. Easy to get where I'm going.

## What's weak

- **No Pro Xtra anywhere above the fold** — I've got a rewards account. I spend real money here. Not seeing my balance or a "member pricing" badge feels like a missed opportunity.
- **"Get It Today — Do More Summer Hosting" banner** — patio furniture and outdoor entertaining? That's more my wife's department. It breaks the tool-and-project momentum the page had going.
- **No one like me in the imagery** — the mower and tools are just products on a white background. A guy in a work shirt using that Milwaukee setup would actually land harder.
- **"Up 20%"** — bottom of the screen is cut off and I can't tell what that refers to. Feels like a tease with no payoff.
- **No free-shipping threshold or delivery callout beyond the asterisk** — "FAST FREE DELIVERY*" has a configure asterisk right below it. That asterisk is tiny. I want to know the number before I shop.

## Recommendations

- **Surface my Pro Xtra balance or a "Members Save Extra" badge** in the header or hero strip — I'm logged in at some level given it shows my location, make me feel like a regular.
- **Swap the "Summer Hosting" banner for an outdoor power or lumber/materials promo** — keep the momentum on my project lane; patio sets belong on a separate page.
- **Add a specific free-shipping number** — replace the asterisk disclaimer with "Free delivery on orders $45+" in the hero banner itself so I'm not hunting for it.
- **Drop a "What's in Your Store" or "Low-Stock Near You" callout** on the tools rail — inventory check is literally why I open the app first; surfacing local stock would close the loop and push me to convert same-day.
