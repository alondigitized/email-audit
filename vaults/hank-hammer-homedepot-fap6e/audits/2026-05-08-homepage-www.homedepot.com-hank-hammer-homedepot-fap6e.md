# Homepage snapshot · www.homedepot.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- Got it. Here's my full review as Hank Hammer.
- Home Depot knows their audience — this homepage is all business, no fluff. They're pushing Spring Deals hard, Final Days urgency, and tools + outdoor power right up front. For a guy like me who's got a deck project queued up and a lawn mower that's on its last legs, this hits the right notes. It's not pretty, but it's practical.

## What's working

- **"FINAL DAYS. Spring Deals Ends 5/10. While Supplies Last. Shop Now"** — orange urgency banner at the very top. That's exactly the kick I need to not put off the lawn mower decision another week.
- **Lawn & Garden hero banner** — hydrangeas and the "Dig Into a Beautiful Outdoors" copy land right. My wife's been after me to redo the front beds.
- **Spring Deals price grid** — showing was/now pricing ($448 was $649 on patio furniture, $499 was $648 on the grill) gives me instant comparison without hunting around. That's the kind of deal I'd clip out of a Sunday circular.
- **"Up to $200 Off Select Tools & Outdoor Power"** — drill and circular saw visible. Right in my wheelhouse. This is exactly why I came to the site.
- **Store location + hours (Burbank, 10PM)** — I use the app to check if something's in stock before driving over. Seeing my store already set is a good sign.
- **Fast Free Delivery** callout — I'll take it, especially on heavy items like the mower.

## What's weak

- **No Pro Xtra callout above the fold** — I have a Pro Xtra account and I earn points on every big purchase. No mention of rewards balance, member pricing, or bonus events anywhere I can see. That's a missed hook for guys like me who buy tools in volume.
- **No product names on the deal tiles** — I see "$29.97 was $48.24" on what looks like flowers, but I have to squint to figure out what's being sold. Label the products plainly.
- **Zero people in any imagery** — Not asking for a model shoot, but even a guy in a work shirt using the drill would make this feel less like a warehouse flyer.
- **"Get It Today — Bold Blooms & More" strip** is visually muddy — dark flowers on dark background, text gets lost, I almost skimmed past it.
- **Bottom nav is generic** — "Shop All / Services / DIY / Log In" — I'd rather see "Lumber", "Tools", or "Garden" there for faster one-tap access to my actual categories.

## Recommendations

- **Surface Pro Xtra balance or member-exclusive pricing** in the header or right below the urgency banner — a line like "Pro Xtra members save an extra 5%" would stop guys like me cold.
- **Add product names to the deal tiles** — even just "Ryobi 40V Mower" or "4-Piece Patio Set" under the price. I'm not tapping blind on a $449 item.
- **Swap the bottom nav icons for category shortcuts** relevant to current season — "Mowers," "Mulch & Soil," "Power Tools" during Spring Deals would cut my path to purchase by two taps.
- **Brighten the "Get It Today" banner** — use the same orange/white treatment as the top strip so it doesn't disappear into the scroll.
