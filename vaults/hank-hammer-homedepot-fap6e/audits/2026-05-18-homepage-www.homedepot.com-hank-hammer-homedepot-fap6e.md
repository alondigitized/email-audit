# Homepage snapshot · www.homedepot.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- Right away this feels like my kind of store — Memorial Day deals front and center, tools and outdoor power visible before I even scroll, and it knows I'm in Alhambra and opens at 6AM. This is a working-man's holiday weekend sale page, not some lifestyle magazine. Home Depot is pushing the Memorial Day event hard, and from where I sit with a lawn to mow and a deck to finish, that's exactly the right call.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day hero banner with real prices** — "$1599 was $3099" on what looks like a refrigerator, "$389.99 was $549.99" on a grill. I can do math in my head and see the value immediately. No percentage games.
- **My store, my time** — "Alhambra 6AM" and zip 91803 at the top. I know exactly when I can roll in this morning. That's genuinely useful.
- **"Up to $175 OFF Select Tools & Outdoor Power" strip** — with a lawn mower and Milwaukee tools in the image. That's Saturday-morning fuel for me.
- **"Get It Today / Do More Summer Hosting" banner** — the BOPIS angle (get it today) matters to me because I don't wait for shipping when I've got a project running.
- **Orange "Shop Now" CTA** — unmistakable, high contrast against the white/orange background. Doesn't make me hunt for the button.
- **Top banner "While Supplies Last"** — honest scarcity, not a fake countdown. That's the kind of straightforward language I trust.

## What's weak

- **Pro Xtra rewards nowhere above the fold** — I've got points sitting there and I can't see my balance or any member-pricing badge. That's a miss for a loyalty customer like me.
- **The $5.97 product is too small to identify** — I can see the price but the image is thumbnail-sized. I have no idea if that's something I need.
- **"Up to 20% off" at the very bottom** — getting cut off, can't see what category that's for. Tease without payoff is annoying.
- **No category navigation visible** — the bottom nav has "Shop All / Services / DIY / Log In" but nothing drill-down like Lumber, Electrical, or Plumbing. I have to tap "Shop All" which is an extra step.
- **"Feedback" tab floating on the right edge** — visually clutters the right side, looks like a UI artifact rather than a feature I'd trust.

## Recommendations

- **Surface Pro Xtra balance in the store/zip row** — right next to "Alhambra 6AM" put "You have 1,240 points." One line, huge loyalty payoff, zero extra screen real estate needed.
- **Make the $5.97 deal product identifiable** — enlarge the image or add a two-word product label. Right now it's a price floating next to a mystery white canister.
- **Add one category shortcut strip below the hero** — four icons: Lumber, Electrical, Tools, Outdoor. Saves me the "Shop All" tap and gets me to aisle faster.
- **Swap the floating "Feedback" tab for an inline support chip** — or remove it from the viewport entirely on mobile. It reads as a broken UI element, not a feature.
