# Homepage snapshot · www.homedepot.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- Home Depot knows exactly who walks through that door — someone like me with a project list and a Saturday morning planned. The orange banner up top is screaming "Spring Deals Final Days," there are lawn equipment prices right up front, and they're showing tools and outdoor power at a discount. This is a home improvement retailer doing what it does best: product-first, price-forward, no fuss.

## What's working

- **Spring Deals Final Days urgency banner (orange strip, top of screen)** — "Ends 5/10. While Supplies Last." with a deadline that's today. That gets my attention immediately; I know I need to check inventory before I drive over.
- **Lawn & Garden hero with flower imagery** — I've got a backyard project this weekend, so "Dig Into a Beautiful Outdoors" speaks directly to the season and to me.
- **Price grid with strikethrough was-pricing** — The $29.97 (was $46.74), $449 (was $479), $448 (was $649), $499 (was $648) format is exactly how I evaluate a deal. No fluff, just the markdown.
- **"Fast Free Delivery"** — called out prominently in the Spring Deals module. I don't always want to haul a riding mower home myself.
- **Up to $200 Off Tools & Outdoor Power** — That circular saw and drill combo in the green module is right in my wheelhouse. Ryobi or similar yellow/green tools, price reduction clearly stated.
- **Burbank 8PM / 91502** — My store, my hours. That local context is immediately useful.

## What's weak

- **No Pro Xtra hook visible** — I'm a Pro Xtra member and my rewards balance or member-exclusive pricing should be front and center. Nothing here acknowledges I'm a returning, loyal customer.
- **Lawn & Garden hero is flowers/hydrangeas, not projects** — I'm buying mulch, edging, and maybe a pressure washer attachment, not a flower arrangement. The hydrangea imagery skews toward a different shopper.
- **No category quick-links visible above the fold** — For a store with 30+ departments, I can't see quick taps to Lumber, Tools, Plumbing, or Electrical. I'd have to use the search bar or scroll past all this to get to what I need.
- **The "Get It Today, Bold Blooms & More" banner is tiny and hard to read** — That strip between the price grid and the tools module is visually weak; the text is small and the contrast is low.
- **No inventory/store check CTA tied to the deals** — The app's best feature for me is knowing if an item is in stock at Burbank before I drive. Nothing here nudges me toward that functionality.

## Recommendations

- **Surface a Pro Xtra rewards tile above the fold** — Even a small banner showing "Your rewards: $X available" or "Pro Xtra members save an extra 5%" would make me feel recognized and more likely to click through.
- **Swap the hydrangea hero for a project/tool lifestyle shot** — A guy my age on a deck project, or someone edging a lawn with the product on sale, converts better than pretty flowers for my demographic.
- **Add a horizontal category row below the search bar** — Six to eight icon-and-label taps (Lumber, Tools, Electrical, Plumbing, Outdoor Power, Paint, Garden, Rentals) would let me get to my aisle in one tap instead of hunting.
- **Make the "Get It Today" strip readable** — Increase text size and contrast, or replace it with a check-inventory CTA tied to my local store.
