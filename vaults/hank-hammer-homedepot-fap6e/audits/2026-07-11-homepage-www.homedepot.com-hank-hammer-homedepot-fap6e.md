# Homepage snapshot · www.homedepot.com · 2026-07-11
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-11**
## Executive summary

- Home Depot's app home is clean and orange as always, but what it's pushing right now is appliances and furniture — not exactly what I fire up my phone to check. As a brand it looks exactly like Home Depot: big savings numbers, no nonsense layout. But today it's talking to the guy replacing his fridge, not the guy with a deck project and a cart full of 2x6s waiting on a Saturday morning.

## What's working

- **The $1000 off appliances hero** — big number, big image of a French door fridge and a washer/dryer pair. I can tell immediately what I'm getting and what I'm saving. That's how you talk to me.
- **Orange "Shop Now" button** — can't miss it, above the fold, unambiguous. Good.
- **Store location and hours** — "Alhambra 6AM" right under the search bar. That's actually useful to me. I know my store opens at 6, I can plan my Saturday run.
- **"Fast Free Delivery" strip** — I'm not always buying online but knowing it's there for a big appliance purchase matters.
- **Clean layout, no pop-ups, no forced modals** — I opened the app and it just showed me the homepage. No "sign up for texts!" ambush. Appreciated.

## What's weak

- **Nothing for a project guy above the fold** — Tools, lumber, electrical, plumbing — zero. This is a tool-and-materials store and there's not a single drill bit or 4x4 post in sight on the first screen.
- **No Pro Xtra hook** — I'm a rewards member. There's no balance shown, no "member deals," no reminder I'm earning points. I have to remember that myself.
- **Second promo is online-only furniture** — "Online Only, Up to 35% off Select Furniture, Rugs & Home Decor" — that's less than zero relevance to me. I don't come to HD for a beige accent chair.
- **No search pre-population or suggestion** — The search bar is blank. For a returning customer who checks inventory before driving over, a "shop your recent searches" or top-category shortcut would save me time.
- **No price/availability context** — I use the app to check if the Husky 3/8" impact driver is in stock at my store before I leave the house. None of that is surfaced anywhere.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Tools & Hardware" shortcut tile above the promo section** — put a quick-link row under the search bar for the top 4-5 departments: Tools, Lumber & Composites, Electrical, Plumbing. One tap to the aisle I need.
- **Surface the Pro Xtra balance for logged-in members** — swap a small strip under the location line for "Your Pro Xtra: 2,400 pts | Member deals this week →". I'd tap that every visit.
- **Swap the furniture banner for a seasonal project promo** — it's July, people are building decks, fencing yards, painting exteriors. "Up to 30% off Pressure-Treated Lumber" or "Deck-building sale" would pull me deeper into the app instead of making me scroll past.
- **Put an in-store inventory signal on the hero** — even just a small badge: "Available at Alhambra." If I know the fridge is in stock at my store I'm more likely to act.
