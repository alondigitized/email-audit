# Homepage snapshot · www.homedepot.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- This is Home Depot doing what Home Depot does — spring season is here and they're pushing outdoor living hard, with patio furniture and grills front and center. The orange is loud, the deals are visible, and it's got my store's hours and zip right at the top, which I appreciate. Nothing about this page says "weekend warrior with a bathroom tile job" but I know exactly what I'm looking at.
- **6/10**

## What's working

- **Store locator strip (Burbank / 10PM / 91502)** — First thing I notice after the logo. I know my store is open late and they know where I am. That's useful before I even scroll.
- **"Spring Deals" tile with Fast Free Delivery** — The orange badge grabs attention immediately, and "fast free delivery" matters when I'm hauling a grill or patio set.
- **Actual price tags on the product tiles** — $398, $199, $448, $499, each with a struck-through "Was" price. That's how I shop. I can compare before I'm even in the store.
- **"Shop Now" CTA on Spring Backyard Days** — Clear, orange, hard to miss. No mystery about what to do next.
- **Bottom nav: Shop All / Services / DIY / Log In** — Clean and functional. "DIY" is a direct line to my brain.

## What's weak

- **Zero tool or lumber presence above the fold** — I'm not here for patio cushions. My weekend project is a deck subframe and there's not a single fastener, power tool, or dimensional lumber callout anywhere.
- **"Spring Backyard Days / Wake Up Your Yard"** — That headline is written for someone who wants to entertain. I want to build. Different buyer.
- **No Pro Xtra or rewards hook visible** — I've got Pro Xtra rewards and not one badge or CTA for it. That's a miss on someone who shops twice a weekend.
- **"Get It Today — Bold Blooms & More" banner** — Garden flowers? I'm sure someone wants this. Not me.
- **"Up to 25% Off Select Online Patio Furniture"** — The asterisk-style "Select Online" qualifier is buried in small print at the bottom. Fine print fatigue.

## Recommendations

- **Swap at least one product tile for a tool or building-materials deal** — Slide a Milwaukee or DeWalt deal or a treated lumber special into the Spring Deals grid. I'm looking for a reason to stay, and right now I'm not finding one.
- **Surface a Pro Xtra loyalty badge or member-pricing ribbon** — Even one line — "Pro Xtra members save extra on Spring Deals" — keeps me engaged and reminds me my card is worth using.
- **Add a "This Weekend's Projects" or "Build It" editorial strip** — A deck, a fence, a pergola. One lifestyle shot of a real project in progress would keep a builder like me scrolling.
- **Move "Up to 25% Off" language above the lifestyle photo** — Right now the discount is below the fold on the patio module. Headline the deal, then show the furniture.
