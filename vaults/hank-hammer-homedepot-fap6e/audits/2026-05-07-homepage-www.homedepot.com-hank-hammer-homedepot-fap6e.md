# Homepage snapshot · www.homedepot.com · 2026-05-07
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- Home Depot's leading with Spring Deals and it lands — lawn equipment, a grill, patio furniture, flowers. That's my weekend project list right there. Page feels like they know their customer: homeowner, May, outdoor season is cranking up. Only thing pulling me up short is a forced sign-in popup covering the bottom half of the screen before I can even see what's below the fold.

## What's working

- **"LAWN & GARDEN — Dig Into a Beautiful Outdoors" hero:** That's the first thing I see and it's exactly what I've got going this weekend. Right category, right season.
- **Spring Deals product grid with was/now pricing:** Four products with clear markdowns. The Ryobi mower at $449 (was $879) and the grill at $499 (was $649) are the kind of numbers that make me stop scrolling.
- **"FAST FREE DELIVERY*" + Shop Now button:** Delivery option surfaced right next to the deals. For a big item like a mower, that matters — I don't want to wrestle it into the truck.
- **"FINAL DAYS" urgency badge:** That green badge gives me a reason to act now rather than put it off until next weekend.
- **Store location + hours visible (Burbank, 10PM):** I check inventory before I drive over. Seeing my store right at the top tells me this is tuned to my location.

## What's weak

- **Forced sign-in modal:** Before I've even looked at a product, there's a popup asking me to log in. I know my Pro Xtra login but this thing pops up uninvited and covers the page. That's irritating, not helpful.
- **No Pro Xtra callout:** I've got a rewards account. There's no "Pro Xtra member? Your deals are better" nudge anywhere above the fold. Just a generic "Sign in for the best experience."
- **Fine print on free delivery is easy to miss:** The asterisk and "Contiguous U.S. Only. *Subject to availability" are tiny. I've been burned by delivery exclusions before.
- **No product names on the deal tiles:** I can see prices and photos but the mower and grill don't have brand or model names visible. I want to know if it's Ryobi, Milwaukee, or something I've never heard of before I tap.

## Recommendations

- **Replace generic sign-in modal with a Pro Xtra banner strip:** Something like "Sign in to see your member price on Spring Deals" placed above the product grid — same goal, zero friction, zero blocked content.
- **Add model names to the deal tiles:** Even just "Ryobi 40V Mower" under the photo. Homeowners shop by brand, and I'm not tapping blind.
- **Make the free delivery threshold explicit:** Change "FAST FREE DELIVERY*" to "Free Delivery on Orders $45+" so I know exactly what the deal is without hunting for fine print.
- **Swap the hero for a lifestyle shot with a real project:** A guy redoing a back patio or mowing a lawn beats hydrangeas on a white background for someone like me. Show me the job, not the garden center.
