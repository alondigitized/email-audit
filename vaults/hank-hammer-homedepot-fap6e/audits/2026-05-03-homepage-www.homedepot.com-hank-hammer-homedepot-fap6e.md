## What's working

- **Was/Now pricing in the deals grid** — specific numbers ($398 was $549, savings of $151) tell me exactly what the deal is. I respect that, no games.
- **"Spring Backyard Days" hero** — timely. It's May, I do have yard work queued up. Seasonal timing is right.
- **Burbank / 10PM store info** — the app knows my local store and closing time. That's actually useful when I'm on the couch at 9:45 wondering if I can make a run.
- **Orange "Shop Now" CTAs** — consistent, easy to spot, no mystery about where to tap.
- **Gas grill in the deals grid ($499 was $649)** — that one caught my eye. That's a legitimate purchase for a homeowner thinking about summer.

## What's weak

- **No Pro Xtra anywhere** — I'm a Pro Xtra member and I spend real money here every weekend. There's zero acknowledgment of that above the fold. No points balance, no member pricing badge, nothing.
- **Zero tools, lumber, or hardware visible** — the entire homepage is patio furniture and flowers. I've got a framing project on deck and this page offers me no on-ramp into the aisles I actually shop.
- **Four competing sections, no clear winner** — hero, deals grid, strip banner, and lifestyle module all stack without breathing room. It reads more like an ad insert than a homepage.
- **"Fast Free Delivery\*" asterisk** — fine print says "Contiguous U.S. Only \*Subject to Availability." That asterisk does real work; the headline promise is softer than it looks.
- **Bottom nav is generic** — Shop All, Services, DIY, Log In. "DIY" is close but vague. A guy who comes in for a specific Husky tool or a sheet of plywood wants a direct path, not a browse menu.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Pro Xtra strip above the hero** — one row with my points balance and a "Based on your recent purchases" prompt. I'm a loyal customer; show me you know that.
- **Mix one tools or building materials tile into the deals grid** — even a single Husky or Milwaukee deal next to the patio furniture tells me this app covers the whole store.
- **Swap "DIY" in the bottom nav for "Tools & Hardware"** — that's where I navigate first on nearly every session. One tap to the tool aisle is the actual reason I keep this app installed.
- **Add a deadline to the Spring Deals** — "Ends Sunday" or "This Weekend Only" would make me act instead of figuring I can browse next Saturday.
