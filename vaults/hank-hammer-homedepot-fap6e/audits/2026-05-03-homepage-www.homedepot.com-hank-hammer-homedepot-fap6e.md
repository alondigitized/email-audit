## What's working

- **"Spring Backyard Days / Wake Up Your Yard" hero banner** — lands immediately. I've got a patio project going right now, so this speaks to me.
- **Specific was/is pricing** — $398 (was $549) tells me exactly what I'm saving. I know lumber prices by heart; I respond to numbers, not percentages.
- **"Fast Free Delivery" call-out** — as someone who usually buys in-store, the reminder that delivery is free on these big items makes me think twice about lugging a grill home in the truck.
- **Store location personalized to Burbank** — it knows my store and shows it has hours left today (8PM). That "Get It Today" mentality is exactly how I shop.
- **"#1 Home Improvement Retailer" top banner** — I already know this, but it doesn't hurt to remind me I'm in the right place.
- **Orange "Shop Now" CTA** — that orange button is as Home Depot as it gets. I know what to tap.

## What's weak

- **Forced sign-in modal blocking the lower page** — the first thing that interrupts me is a wall asking me to log in. I'm here to look at deals, not to sign in before I've seen anything worth signing in for. That's backwards.
- **No Pro Xtra hook** — I'm a Pro Xtra member. I should see my points balance or member pricing the moment I open this thing. Instead I get a generic sign-in prompt that doesn't mention any reward.
- **Outdoor furniture and grills only — no tools, no hardware** — I get that it's spring deals, but I'm also Husky-loyal and I check tool deals every time I open the app. Not a single wrench, drill, or power tool in sight above the fold.
- **"Get It Today: Bold Blooms & More" banner** — flowers and garden color are fine, but it feels like it's targeting my wife's shopping list more than mine. Not where I'm hunting.
- **Product tiles have no review counts** — I can see the prices but there are no star ratings on those grill or furniture tiles. I'd want to know what other guys think before I drop $499 on a grill.

## Recommendations

- **Drop the forced sign-in modal — replace with a Pro Xtra welcome strip.** Show my points balance and a "member pricing" badge on featured products. I'll sign in when I have a reason to.
- **Add a "Tools & Hardware Deals" secondary row** below the outdoor living grid — even two or three Husky or Milwaukee tool tiles with discounted pricing would pull me deeper into the page.
- **Put star ratings and review counts on the deal tiles** — I'm deciding whether to spend $448 on a patio set. Show me the 4.6 stars from 1,200 reviews so I don't have to go looking for them.
- **Add a deadline or "while supplies last" line to the Spring Deals section** — something like "Offers valid through May 11" gives me a reason to tap now rather than wait until my next store run.
