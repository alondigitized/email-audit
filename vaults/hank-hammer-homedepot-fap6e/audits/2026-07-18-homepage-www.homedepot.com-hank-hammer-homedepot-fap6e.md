# Homepage snapshot · www.homedepot.com · 2026-07-18
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-18**
## Executive summary

- First thing I see when I open the app is jack-o-lanterns and a skeleton — I came here to check if Alhambra has that Husky 1/2-inch impact driver in stock, not to buy a fog machine. The appliance deal below it is the real meat, and that catches my attention, but Home Depot's leading with Halloween decor in the number-one slot feels like they aimed at someone else. The bones of the page are solid — my store, my hours, a big orange button — but the editorial choices don't match why guys like me open this app.

## What's working

- **"UP TO $1,000 OFF Select Appliances" module** — big, credible number, clear product imagery (washer/dryer stack and a French door fridge), and a visible "Shop Now" button. If my refrigerator was on its last legs, I'd tap that right now.
- **Store-aware header** — "Alhambra 6AM" and zip 91803 tells me the app knows exactly where I'm going and when it opens. That's genuinely useful at 5:45 in the morning.
- **"FAST FREE DELIVERY" strip** — I always check delivery before I haul something myself. Nice to see it called out.
- **Bottom nav bar** — Shop, Services, DIY, Log In. Clean and simple. I can find what I need in one tap without hunting.
- **No popups or forced modals** — page just loads and lets me look around. That alone puts it ahead of half the retail apps I've used.

## What's weak

- **Halloween hero as the lead** — "Fright & Delight / Creepy Creations Are Here" is the biggest thing on the page. I don't buy Halloween stuff at Home Depot. This slot should be project-season content or a tool deal, not a seasonal decor push targeting a completely different customer.
- **Zero Pro Xtra presence** — I've been a rewards member for years and the homepage doesn't acknowledge me at all. No points balance, no member-exclusive pricing, no "welcome back." That feels like a miss.
- **"Online Only" caveat on the 30% off furniture/rugs** — I'm a store shopper. The second I see "Online Only" I tune out. That label undercuts the offer for someone like me.
- **No tool or building-materials touch point** — Not a single SKU, category link, or promo aimed at tools, lumber, or hardware anywhere I can see. That's my entire reason for opening this app.
- **Fast Free Delivery threshold not stated** — The strip says "FAST FREE DELIVERY" but I can't see the minimum order amount. I want a number, not a tease.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the Halloween hero for a rotating project-season tile** — Lead with "Get Your Projects Done Before Winter" with a power tool or lumber visual. Seasonal decorators are a fraction of the traffic; contractors and weekend DIYers are the core. Run the Halloween push as a secondary banner or a second-swipe carousel.
- **Surface Pro Xtra rewards balance front and center** — Put my points total and any bonus offer right below the header for logged-in users. One line: "You have 4,200 Pro Xtra points — redeem today." That single change would make this feel like it knows who I am.
- **Add a "Tools & Hardware" quick-tap row above the fold** — Four category chips (Power Tools, Hand Tools, Fasteners, Lumber) that get me one tap into my actual aisles. Right now I have to go through the generic "Shop" nav to get there.
- **Put the free-delivery threshold on the strip** — Change "FAST FREE DELIVERY" to "FAST FREE DELIVERY on orders $45+" so I know exactly what I'm working with before I add to cart.
