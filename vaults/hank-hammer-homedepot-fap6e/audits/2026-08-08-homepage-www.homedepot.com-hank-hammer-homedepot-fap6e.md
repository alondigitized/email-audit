# Homepage snapshot · www.homedepot.com · 2026-08-08
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-08**
## Executive summary

- This looks like my Home Depot, plain and simple — tools, appliances, grills, bath fixtures, right up front. No lifestyle fluff, no models in yoga pants, just the goods and the discount. Right now they're pushing tool deals, appliance markdowns, and a "Fire Up the Grill" push, which tells me it's still summer inventory clearing.

## What's working

- **"UP TO $120 OFF Select Tools"** banner with the DeWalt impact driver front and center — that's exactly the kind of deal I check the app for before a store run.
- **"Tool Innovation" banner** with the gloved hand on the circular saw — feels authentic, like real jobsite use, not a staged photo.
- **"UP TO $1000 OFF Select Appliances"** — big number, easy to read at a glance, no squinting needed.
- **Daily Deals module** at the bottom with the countdown ("Ends in 20 hrs 49 mins") — that's a real reason to act today instead of putting it off.
- Store info right at the top (Alhambra, 6AM, zip 91803) — I like knowing my store hours before I even search.

## What's weak

- There's a big awkward gap of blank white space between the top banner row and the "Fire Up the Grill" row — looks like something didn't load right.
- No Pro Xtra rewards badge or balance shown anywhere I can see — I'm a member, I'd like to see my status or points without digging.
- "Log In" is just a generic icon with text, no personalized greeting ("Welcome back, Hank") to make it feel like they know me.
- Small orange smudge under the first tool banner near the "Shop Now" button — looks like a stray graphic glitch.

## Recommendations

- Close up that blank gap between the promo banners and the grill/bath row — tighten the layout so it scrolls clean.
- Put a Pro Xtra rewards strip right under the search bar — show points balance or member pricing so loyalty guys like me see value immediately.
- Add "Welcome back, Hank" or recently-viewed items near the top since I'm logged in on the app most of the time.
- Swap the generic "Shop Now" copy on at least one banner for something specific like "Shop DeWalt Deals" — tells me exactly what I'm getting before I tap.
