# Homepage snapshot · www.homedepot.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- Right off the bat, Home Depot knows exactly who's coming to this page the week before Memorial Day — guys like me who've been eyeing that lawn mower since April and finally have a three-day weekend to justify the purchase. The whole page is dripping orange, stacked with concrete deal prices, and it's pushing tools and outdoor power hard. No fashion. No lifestyle fluff. Just merchandise with numbers on it.

## What's working

- **The deal grid with was/now prices** — $379 was $419 on that green outdoor tool, $1,599 on the fridge, specific dollar savings. I don't have to do math, I can just confirm my gut.
- **"Up to $175 OFF Select Tools & Outdoor Power"** with Milwaukee tools and a red lawn mower front and center — that's exactly the section I showed up for.
- **Location awareness** — it already knows I'm in Alhambra, shows "6AM" store hours, and the zip 91803. That means inventory I'm looking at is probably actually there.
- **"FAST FREE DELIVERY*"** in the hero — I hate going back for a second trip. Knowing delivery is free takes some pressure off the decision.
- **Memorial Day urgency banner** at the very top: "While Supplies Last. Shop Now." That's honest urgency. Not a fake countdown — just truth.

## What's weak

- **No Pro Xtra callout anywhere** — I'm a rewards member. I've earned points. Show me my balance or member pricing. Costco does this. Sam's Club does this. Home Depot hides it.
- **The "GET IT TODAY — DO MORE SUMMER HOSTING" banner** in the middle is vague and soft — "summer hosting" sounds like a party-planning app, not a hardware store. That copy doesn't land for me.
- **No star ratings on any featured product** — the grill is $389.99 but I can't see even a single review count. I want to know if 4,000 people bought it or 12.
- **Bottom nav is bare-bones** — "Shop All, Services, DIY, Log In." There's no quick-tap to "Tools," "Lumber," or "Outdoor Power" without going through "Shop All." One extra tap every time adds up.
- **"Contiguous U.S. Ship. Subject to availability*"** fine print under Free Delivery is there but small — I'd want that upfront, not asterisked out.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Pro Xtra strip** just below the header: "Welcome back, Hank — you have 2,340 points. Member deals unlocked." That converts loyalty members immediately without them hunting for it.
- **Put review counts under the deal grid items** — even just "★★★★☆ (3,218)" next to the grill convinces fence-sitters like me. Takes one line of UI.
- **Swap "DO MORE SUMMER HOSTING" copy** with something more concrete: "Patio Set-Up Weekend — Get It Today" with a tap to outdoor furniture/grills. Give me a destination.
- **Add a one-tap toolbar link to "Tools & Hardware"** in the bottom nav alongside "Shop All." Two taps versus one makes a real difference for guys checking inventory in the parking lot.
