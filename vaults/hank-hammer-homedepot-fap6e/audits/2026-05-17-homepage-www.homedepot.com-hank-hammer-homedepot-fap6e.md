# Homepage snapshot · www.homedepot.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Alright, this is Home Depot doing what Home Depot does — hitting you over the head with Memorial Day the second you open the app. Tools, outdoor power, a grill thrown in for good measure. For a guy like me who's already got a lawn mower to replace and a deck project waiting, this front page is almost reading my mind. Brand looks confident, seasonal, and priced to move.

## What's working

- **"Up to $175 Off Select Tools & Outdoor Power" banner** — that's the section that makes me stop scrolling. Lawn mower and power tools right in the imagery. Speaks directly to what I'm shopping this weekend.
- **Memorial Day hero with "Was/Now" pricing** — I can see the $389.99 grill was $449.99 and the fridge was $3,099 now $1,599. I know what a deal looks like; this passes the sniff test.
- **Store location pinned at top** — "Alhambra 9PM / 91803" tells me my store is still open tonight. That's exactly how I use this app: check stock, head to the store. Having it right there without digging saves me a step.
- **"Fast Free Delivery" called out in the hero** — good signal even with the asterisk. I don't always need delivery, but knowing it's available for the big stuff matters.
- **Orange "Shop Now" buttons** — hard to miss, placed right under the relevant offers. No confusion about what to tap.

## What's weak

- **Pro Xtra rewards are completely invisible** — I've got points sitting there and this page doesn't acknowledge me as a member at all. That's a missed shot; loyalty pricing and my balance should be front and center for a returning customer.
- **"While Supplies Last" is soft urgency** — I know that phrase means nothing specific. Give me an end date. "Ends Monday May 26" would actually get me off the couch today.
- **No free-shipping threshold spelled out** — it says "Fast Free Delivery*" but the asterisk conditions are tiny print at the bottom of the hero. I want to know if my $389 grill ships free before I go digging.
- **Bottom nav is generic** — "Shop All / Services / DIY / Log In" doesn't help me find lumber, plumbing, or electrical fast. A 58-year-old who's been in the store a hundred times wants category shortcuts, not a four-item menu.
- **No product reviews or ratings visible** — I can see sale prices but not a single star rating anywhere on this screen. For a $1,599 refrigerator, I want to know it's not a dud before I tap.

## Recommendations

- **Surface Pro Xtra balance and any member-exclusive price in the hero** — even a small "Pro Xtra Member Price" badge on one of the products would make a returning customer feel recognized and push toward conversion faster.
- **Replace "While Supplies Last" with a hard date** — "Memorial Day Sale ends May 26" with a simple countdown badge does more work than vague scarcity language.
- **Add a quick-category strip below the hero** — Lumber · Plumbing · Electrical · Outdoor Power as tappable chips. That's one row, ships in a week, and cuts navigation time for the guy who knows exactly what aisle he needs.
- **Pin free-shipping threshold plainly** — "Free delivery on orders $45+" (or whatever the actual number is) in the banner itself, not buried in an asterisk footnote.
