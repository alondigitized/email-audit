# Homepage snapshot · www.homedepot.com · 2026-05-30
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-30**
## Executive summary

- Home Depot on Memorial Day weekend is exactly where I want to be — they're running a full appliance push as the hero, but they haven't forgotten the tool guys either. That top banner screaming "$120 off select tools" and a dedicated Ryobi Days block further down tells me this place knows its audience. Heavy on deals, light on the loyalty stuff I actually care about as a Pro Xtra member.

## What's working

- **Top banner "UP TO $120 OFF Select Tools"** — first thing I clock before anything else, and it's exactly the hook that makes me stay
- **Ryobi Days "FREE TOOL With Select Ryobi Power Tool Battery Purchase"** — Ryobi is my cordless platform and a free tool with a battery buy is the kind of deal I'd drive to the store for right now
- **Memorial Day deal specificity** — "$1000 off," "$396+ free delivery," "Ends 6/3" — real numbers, real deadline, I know exactly where I stand
- **Location and store hours ("Alhambra 6AM")** — it knows I'm already thinking about a morning run; showing the store opens at 6AM is exactly the info a twice-a-weekend guy needs
- **"Shop Now" CTAs** — orange, high contrast, no ambiguity about what to tap

## What's weak

- **No Pro Xtra hook anywhere on screen** — I'm a rewards member and this page treats me like a stranger; my points balance, my tier, my member pricing — invisible
- **Appliance hero doesn't speak to me** — a stainless French door fridge is a nice upgrade but I'm here for tools and building materials, not kitchen appliances
- **"No Membership Required" copy** — fine for a first-time shopper but it actually reminds me that my loyalty isn't being rewarded here
- **No path to lumber/hardware from this screen** — the bottom nav has "Shop All" but nothing that lands me in building materials or tools in one tap
- **Ryobi Days is below the fold** — the section most relevant to me is the second scroll, buried under appliances

## Recommendations

- **Surface a Pro Xtra member greeting** — put my points balance or "Member Exclusive Pricing" badge in the header or just below the search bar; takes one line of personalization and makes me feel like a regular, not a walk-in
- **Swap the hero for a dual-panel layout on mobile** — left panel: appliance deal for the appliance shoppers; right panel: tool/Ryobi deal for the project crowd; split the screen and stop making me scroll past a refrigerator to find my section
- **Add a "My Store: Alhambra" quick-link strip** — tap to check in-stock availability on the Ryobi deal without opening a new search; I already use the app for inventory checks, make it one tap
- **Move Ryobi Days above the fold** — or at minimum, tease it in the hero strip so I know it's waiting for me before I bounce
