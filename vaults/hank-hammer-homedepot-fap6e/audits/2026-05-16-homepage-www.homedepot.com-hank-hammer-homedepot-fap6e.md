# Homepage snapshot · www.homedepot.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Home Depot's going all-in on Memorial Day — orange everywhere, deal grid front and center, and they're hitting me with grills, a string trimmer, and Milwaukee power tools right on the first screen. This reads like a company that knows its customer: a guy with a project list and a long weekend to execute on it. That said, a sign-in modal blocking the page is the first thing I have to deal with, and that starts things off on the wrong foot.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day deal grid with specific prices**: Four products, actual before/after numbers. $379 was $419 on the trimmer — I can do that math in my head and know if it's worth a trip. That's how I shop.
- **"UP TO $175 OFF Select Tools & Outdoor Power" module**: I can see Milwaukee tools — drill, circular saw, jigsaw, angle grinder. Milwaukee is a brand I trust. That gets my attention immediately.
- **Campaign timing**: Memorial Day weekend is when I plan the big stuff. This is exactly the right moment to hit me with tool deals.
- **Store hours in header**: "Alhambra 10PM" tells me the store's still open tonight without me hunting for it. Useful.
- **Search bar is prominent**: Large, easy to tap. I'm not digging through three menu levels to find what I need.

## What's weak

- **Sign-in modal blocking content**: First thing on the page is an overlay I have to dismiss. "Sign in for the best experience" is the most generic ask in retail. Give me a reason — tell me I've got Pro Xtra points waiting.
- **No Pro Xtra mention anywhere**: I'm a Pro Xtra member. If you want my login, show me my rewards balance or member pricing. A generic sign-in prompt wastes that leverage completely.
- **Free delivery asterisk with no threshold**: "FAST FREE DELIVERY*" with "Subject to availability" fine print tells me nothing. Is that free on a $45 order or a $500 order? For a lawn mower, that's a real question.
- **Refrigerator in the deal grid**: The $1,599 stainless appliance slot — that's not Memorial Day tools territory. It dilutes the homeowner/outdoor narrative.
- **No star ratings on featured products**: I can see the price on that $379 trimmer but not whether anyone who bought it is happy with it. Reviews are the last check before I add to cart.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the generic sign-in modal with a Pro Xtra banner**: "You've got 840 Pro Xtra points — sign in to apply them" is a hook. "Sign in for the best experience" is dismissable noise in under a second.
- **Disclose the free delivery threshold in the hero**: Drop "Free delivery on orders $45+" right next to the Shop Now button. The asterisk is killing the credibility of the offer.
- **Swap the refrigerator slot for a power tool or building material**: A cordless drill set or a pressure washer fits the Memorial Day homeowner narrative. An appliance is a different purchase entirely.
- **Add review counts to the deal grid product tiles**: One line — "4.6 ★ (1,204 reviews)" — beneath the price. That's the difference between browsing and buying.
