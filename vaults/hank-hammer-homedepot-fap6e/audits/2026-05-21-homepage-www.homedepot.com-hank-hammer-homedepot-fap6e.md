# Homepage snapshot · www.homedepot.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- This is exactly what I expect from Home Depot — no-nonsense, deal-forward, built for guys like me who know what they came for and want the price front and center. Memorial Day is the whole story right now: grills, mowers, patio sets, power tools, and they're not hiding the numbers. Feels like walking into the store during a sale event — busy, but purposeful.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day hero with real prices on product tiles** — "$299 Was $519" on what looks like a grill, "$449 Was $619" on a mower. I know lumber and tool pricing cold; showing me the was/now immediately tells me if this is a real deal or fluff. These feel real.
- **Top urgency banner** — "FINAL DAYS Memorial Day Deals End 5/27 – Shop Now" in orange across the top. That's a deadline I can act on this weekend.
- **"Get It Today — Do More Summer Hosting"** strip with Memorial Day branding — speaks directly to the weekend project mindset. I've got a deck gathering Saturday.
- **"Up to $175 Off Select Tools & Outdoor Power"** with a string trimmer and a Milwaukee circular saw in the image — that's my aisle. Seeing brand-name tools (looks like Greenworks/EGO and Milwaukee) anchors trust.
- **Local store context** — "Alhambra 6AM" and zip code 91803 up top. I always check if the item's in stock before I drive over. Starting with my store is right.
- **Bottom nav "DIY" tab** — one tap to my world without digging through menus.

## What's weak

- **No Pro Xtra loyalty hook visible** — I'm a Pro Xtra member. My rewards balance, my member pricing, nothing. That's a miss for a returning customer who checks in twice a weekend.
- **Product tile images are small and hard to read on mobile** — four tiles crammed into the right column of the hero. I had to squint to identify that one's a mower and one might be patio chairs. At 58, my eyes don't love 100×100 thumbnails.
- **No pricing visible on the Tools & Outdoor Power banner** — it says "Up to $175 Off" but doesn't show a single price. After the hero tiles train me to expect was/now numbers, this feels like a step backward.
- **"Up to 25%..." — cut off at the bottom** — I can see it starts but can't read what category or what the deal is. Unfinished information annoys me more than no information.
- **No search suggestions or recent searches visible** — I usually check inventory on the app before I go. A "recently searched" shortcut would save me 30 seconds.

## Recommendations

- **Surface Pro Xtra rewards balance in the header** — even a badge showing "You have 1,240 points" next to the cart icon would hook me immediately as a returning member. Home Depot knows who I am when I'm logged in; use it.
- **Enlarge the product tiles or use a horizontal scroll rail** — instead of four tiny stacked tiles, give me a swipeable row with bigger images and legible prices. My thumbs can handle a swipe; my eyes can't handle a postage stamp.
- **Complete the "Up to 25%" banner** — either move it fully above the fold or don't show a cropped headline. Half a deal is worse than no deal.
- **Add one in-store availability signal near the hero CTA** — something like "In stock at Alhambra" next to "Shop Now" would bridge the app-to-store gap that's core to how I actually shop.
