# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- This is a focused Mother's Day campaign launch — seasonal florals, warm lifestyle shot, double-stacked promo banners. The brand knows what holiday it is. What I'm looking at is a clean gift-shop entry point aimed squarely at women in my age bracket, and that part works. What it is not is an easy door into a systematic category walk, which I would need the moment the hero stops interesting me.

## What's working

- **BOGO banner placement**: Blue banner is the very first element — impossible to scroll past. Specific, scannable, actionable.
- **25% OFF Apparel secondary strip**: Layering two distinct offers signals a broad sale event, not a narrow promo. I'd note both.
- **Hero casting**: The model is clearly not a 22-year-old influencer. She looks 38–44, seated with a child. For a Mother's Day campaign targeting women who are actually mothers, this is correct casting.
- **Mother's Day timing**: May 2026 is exactly right. No disconnect between the campaign and the calendar.
- **"Recently Viewed" stub**: That label at the bottom edge tells me the site tracks my history. Returning auditor / returning shopper signal is there.

## What's weak

- **No category navigation above fold**: The only nav entry point is a hamburger MENU. If I want Women's Athletic or Slip-ins, I have to open a drawer. That is one extra tap that costs conversion.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible**: Common hygiene for footwear — nowhere on this screen.
- **No trust signals**: No review counts, no "4.7 stars across 200K reviews," no free-returns badge. The page looks like an ad, not a store.
- **BOGO mechanics unclear in banner**: "Applied at Cart" could mean automatic or it could mean "go find out." The "details" link buries the clarification.
- **No loyalty program hook**: No visible rewards points balance, no "Skechers Elite members earn double" — nothing that rewards return visits.

## Recommendations

- **Drop a category shortcut strip below the hero**: Four tappable tiles — Women's Shoes, Men's Shoes, Slip-ins, Apparel — before the "SHOP NOW" gets the auditor and the casual browser to their category in one tap instead of two.
- **Add a free-shipping threshold strip**: Between the hero and the CTA, one line: "Free shipping on orders $50+." It costs one line of screen space and removes a conversion objection before it forms.
- **Append a trust signal to the BOGO banner**: "Free returns on all orders" or "4.8★ rated by 1M+ customers" in the same blue strip. The offer is good; it just needs credibility next to it.
- **Clarify the BOGO mechanics inline**: Change banner copy to "BOGO 50% OFF — auto-applied at cart" so the shopper doesn't need to click "details" to know whether the discount just works.
