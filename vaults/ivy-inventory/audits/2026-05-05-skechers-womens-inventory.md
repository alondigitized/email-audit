## Inventory summary

**163 styles · 586 (color, width) variants · 68.9% avg size coverage**

The Skechers women's shoes inventory shows an overall average size coverage of 68.9%, with no failed categories but several areas needing improvement, particularly in the Slippers and Boots categories. The most significant gaps are in sizes 10.5, 12.0, and 13.0 across various categories such as Wide Fit and Casual Sneakers. Additionally, the Slippers category has notably low coverage at 52%, with sizes 11.0, 10.0, and 8.5 missing in most variants. The Boots category also struggles, with only 59% average size coverage and frequent gaps in sizes 5.0, 11.0, and 12.0.

Width-specific issues are evident across multiple categories, especially in the Wide Fit section where common sizes like 10.5 and 13.0 are frequently missing. The Lace Up category also shows a thin spread for wide widths, with similar size gaps observed. These patterns suggest that while there is generally good coverage, specific sizes and width options could be more consistently stocked to meet customer demand.

The Waterproof category stands out positively with an average of 82% size coverage, indicating strong inventory management in this area. However, the Hands Free Slip-ins and Canvas Shoes categories also show room for improvement, particularly regarding sizes 10.5 and 13.0 across both medium and wide widths.

In summary, while Skechers women's shoes have a decent overall size coverage, addressing gaps in Slippers and Boots could significantly enhance customer satisfaction. A focus on consistent stocking of key sizes like 10.5, 12.0, and 13.0 would improve inventory performance further. **Bottom line: 7/10**
