# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-07
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- This is a seasonal gift-push homepage — Skechers is running hard at Mother's Day with a clean lifestyle hero and two stacked discount banners. As a catalog auditor, I note the brand is squarely in promotional mode: BOGO plus a category sale running simultaneously. Inventory audit access is one tap behind a hamburger menu, so I'm not getting to category pages fast, but the page itself is competent.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% banner at the very top** — highest-visibility real estate, auto-applied at cart removes friction, no code to copy
- **25% OFF Apparel strip** — a second, distinct offer stacked immediately below; two discounts in the first 80px builds value perception fast
- **Hero lifestyle shot** — the mom-and-daughter image is warm and on-brand; both subjects are wearing pink Skechers, so product is actually visible in the hero
- **"SHOP NOW" button** — full-width, high-contrast, unambiguous; no guessing what happens when you tap it
- **Seasonal timing** — "The Mother's Day Shop" on May 7 is exactly right; this is not a recycled banner

## What's weak

- **Hamburger-only navigation** — no exposed category tabs means I need an extra tap to reach Women's Shoes; for a catalog walk, that's dead friction
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — I can see two discount offers but nothing about shipping; that's a standard close-driver missing above the fold
- **No trust signals** — no review counts, no return-policy callout, no "free exchanges" — nothing that would nudge a hesitant first-timer toward committing
- **Lifestyle-only hero** — the shoes are visible on their feet but there's no product name, no SKU callout, no "See the styles" rail yet in view; I'm being sold a mood, not inventory
- **"Recently Viewed" label is cut off** — personalization feature is there but barely renders; for a returning shopper, this is a missed re-engagement moment

## Recommendations

- **Expose two or three category pills below the logo** (Women's, Men's, Kids') — one tap to a category page instead of two; this directly benefits both browsers and auditors who want to navigate fast
- **Add a free-shipping threshold line to one of the two promo strips** — swap "Shop | details" on the apparel strip for "Free shipping over $XX — Shop the sale"; free shipping is a top conversion lever and there's room in that strip
- **Float a trust signal near the CTA button** — a single line under "SHOP NOW" like "Free returns · 4.6★ from 200K reviews" costs nothing and reduces bounce from first-time visitors
- **Name the shoe in the hero** — add a micro-label or overlay calling out the shoe style worn by the mother; that single addition turns a lifestyle image into a shoppable one without redesigning anything
