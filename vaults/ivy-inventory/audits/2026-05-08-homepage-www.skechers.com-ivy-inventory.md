# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- Mother's Day seasonal push, two stacked promos above the fold, lifestyle hero aimed squarely at the gifting occasion. Skechers is running a clean mobile layout with a clear offer hierarchy. The homepage tells me what's on sale and why I'm here; it does not help me navigate the full catalog in any hurry.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF strip at the top**: Hard to miss. "Applied at Cart" removes code-entry friction — that's a good UX call.
- **Dual-offer stacking**: Two separate promos (BOGO on footwear, 25% on apparel) address different cart intents without fighting for the same eyeballs.
- **Age-appropriate hero model**: The woman in the shot reads mid-30s to mid-40s. That's my demographic. This is not a 22-year-old athlete in a spotlight.
- **"SHOP NOW" button**: Full-width, black, high contrast on white. Thumb-sized tap target on mobile. No ambiguity about what it does.
- **Campaign timing**: May, Mother's Day, florals, pink palette — none of this is stale. It reads as current.

## What's weak

- **"SHOP NOW" is a dead end**: It routes to the Mother's Day collection, not the catalog. For anyone who already knows what they want, that's a wrong turn they have to back out of.
- **No free-shipping threshold anywhere above fold**: This is a standard conversion closer for footwear; it's absent.
- **Script font on the hero headline**: "The Mother's Day Shop" is in a flowing pink cursive that takes a beat to parse on a small screen. Decorative, not efficient.
- **No returns or sizing reassurance**: Gift purchases especially need an easy-returns mention. Nothing.
- **Loyalty program invisible**: No Skechers Elite callout, no member pricing, no join prompt. If the rewards program exists, it's not earning its above-fold real estate.
- **Category access requires an extra tap**: No horizontal category rail visible. Everything funnels through the MENU drawer, which isn't open in this view.

## Recommendations

- **Rewrite the primary CTA**: Change "SHOP NOW" to "Shop Women's Gifts" or "Shop All Women's" — keeps the gift frame but opens catalog depth without an extra step.
- **Add free-shipping threshold to one of the promo strips**: Something like "Free shipping on $50+ · BOGO 50% OFF" collapses two barriers into a single line.
- **Surface a loyalty hook adjacent to the BOGO banner**: One line — "Skechers Elite members save even more — Sign in" — captures returning customers without a separate section.
- **Replace the script headline with set type**: A clean italic slab or bold sans would register faster at thumb-scroll speed and still feel seasonal.
