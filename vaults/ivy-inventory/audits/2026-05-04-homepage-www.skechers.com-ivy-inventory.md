## What's working

- **BOGO 50% top banner** — high-contrast blue strip, hard to miss, concrete dollar math, no ambiguity about what the offer is.
- **Stacked promo architecture** — two offers in the first 80 pixels before the hero even loads. Whoever set up that strip order knows what they're doing.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA** — full-width button, black on white, straightforward copy. No "Explore the Journey" copy here.
- **Hero model age** — the woman in the image reads mid-30s to mid-40s. That's closer to my demographic than the 24-year-old athlete most athletic brands default to.
- **Seasonal accuracy** — Mother's Day, May, pink flowers. Nothing is stale or left over from a prior campaign.

## What's weak

- **No category navigation above the fold** — everything except the hero destination is behind a hamburger MENU. Women's, Men's, Kids', Slip-ins — none are one tap away. That's a miss.
- **Hero routes to a curated subset, not the catalog** — "The Mother's Day Shop" is a filtered collection. If I need to walk the full line, I'm starting behind.
- **No free-shipping callout** — standard conversion lever on footwear sites, nowhere on this viewport.
- **No loyalty/rewards prompt** — a 42-year-old repeat customer is likely enrolled; there's no "sign in to see your rewards" hook visible.
- **Script font on mobile** — "The Mother's Day Shop" in the pink cursive is decorative overhead that costs legibility on a small screen.

## Recommendations

- **Add a four-tile category strip below the hero** — Women's / Men's / Kids' / Slip-ins as tappable tiles, so I don't have to crack the hamburger to navigate the actual catalog.
- **Drop the free-shipping threshold into the promo strip** — one line, "Free shipping over $X," next to the apparel sale. It fits, and it removes a checkout exit.
- **Replace the script headline with a clean sans** — keep the florals and the model; lose the cursive. Same emotional register, better mobile legibility.
- **Add a member sign-in prompt near the CTA** — "Rewards member? Sign in to apply your points" costs nothing to place and captures existing loyalty customers before they bounce.
