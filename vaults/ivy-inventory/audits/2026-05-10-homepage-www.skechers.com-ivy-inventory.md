# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a solid seasonal execution — Skechers is running hard on Mother's Day with a dual-promo push and a lifestyle hero that's clearly aimed at women my age. As a brand, it reads mid-tier accessible athletic with a soft-goods expansion play (the apparel sale is front and center). What it doesn't do is help me, as a catalog auditor, find my way into a category quickly — the whole above-the-fold real estate is thematic, not structural.

## What's working

- **Dual-promo banner stack**: BOGO 50% on top (blue bar, high contrast), apparel 25% off in the secondary strip — two different value propositions stacked cleanly without competing with each other
- **Mother's Day hero timing**: May 10 is Mother's Day. Running this today is exactly right. No stale "Spring Sale" residue
- **"SHOP NOW" button**: full-width, high contrast black-on-white, no ambiguity about what to do next
- **Lifestyle photography**: the mother-daughter pairing wearing matching pink Skechers is warm without being saccharine — works for someone my age who's seen every retail cliché in the book
- **"Recently Viewed" tab**: partially visible below the fold signals personalization, which is a useful feature for a return visitor

## What's weak

- **"SHOP NOW" is generic**: it routes into a thematic Mother's Day collection, not into a navigable product category. For a catalog auditor — or any structured shopper — this is a dead end until I scroll again
- **No loyalty program visible**: Skechers has a rewards program (Elite program) but there's zero mention of it above the fold. That's a missed retention hook for a returning shopper
- **No free-shipping threshold**: for a footwear brand where most items price between $50–$120, the absence of a visible shipping threshold is a conversion gap
- **No urgency copy**: Mother's Day is today. A "last day" or "order by X for delivery" message is conspicuously absent — that's leaving conversion on the table
- **Apparel sale feels like an odd lead-alongside**: Skechers is a footwear brand. Foregrounding a 25% apparel discount in the secondary banner above a footwear hero sends a slightly mixed message about what this brand is

## Recommendations

- **Add "Order by [time] for Mother's Day delivery" or "Last day — BOGO ends tonight" copy** to the secondary banner or below the CTA — urgency is free conversion and Mother's Day is a hard deadline
- **Swap the generic "SHOP NOW" button for category-specific copy**: "Shop Women's Styles" or "Shop Slip-ins for Mom" — same button slot, meaningfully higher click intent
- **Inject a one-line shipping threshold**: "Free shipping over $75" or whatever the actual threshold is — put it in the secondary strip next to the apparel callout
- **Add a loyalty hook to the secondary banner**: "Skechers Elite members earn double points today" takes up one line and gives returning shoppers a reason to be logged in
