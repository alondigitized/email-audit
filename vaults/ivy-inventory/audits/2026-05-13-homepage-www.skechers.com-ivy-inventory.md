# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- This looks like a mid-market athletic/comfort footwear brand running a two-pronged promo push — BOGO on core product and 25% off a seasonal apparel extension. The hero is exclusively Slip-ins, which tells me that's their volume driver right now. Spring positioning is coherent; the execution is competent but demographically narrow.

## What's working

- **Double-promo banner stack** — BOGO 50% in the blue top bar AND the spring apparel strip below it means I can't miss that there's an active deal; no hunting required
- **"Recently Viewed" tab** — that's a useful shortcut for a returning visitor doing a category walk; saves me retracing steps
- **"SHOP NOW" button** — large, high-contrast, centered; the only interactive element competing for my eye below the hero
- **Category tab strip** — Women / Men / Kids' / Recently Viewed in one horizontal row is efficient navigation for someone who needs to move between departments quickly
- **Spring Apparel Sale timing** — May is correct; the campaign isn't stale

## What's weak

- **Model age gap** — both hero models are clearly young (early-to-mid 20s at most); a 42-year-old auditor walking the catalog has zero self-identification here
- **No loyalty/rewards callout above fold** — if Skechers has a points program, it's invisible at load; that's a missed hook for a returning visitor
- **"SHOP NOW" is generic** — sends me somewhere, but where exactly? Women's Slip-ins? All Slip-ins? Ambiguous destination reduces click confidence
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — if it exists, it should be in the promo strip; it's not
- **No review counts or ratings** — the hero product gets no social proof signal in the viewport; I'm looking at marketing copy with nothing to validate it

## Recommendations

- **Add age range to hero imagery** — shoot or slot a lifestyle image with a 40+ woman in the Slip-ins Glide-Step; doesn't need its own hero, even swapping the left model would broaden the read
- **Retarget the CTA copy** — change "SHOP NOW" to "Shop Women's Slip-ins" or "Shop Glide-Step" so the destination is explicit; reduce bounce from misaligned expectation
- **Surface the shipping threshold in the promo strip** — "Free shipping on orders $75+" next to the 25% OFF line costs nothing and improves conversion math for the visitor
- **Pin a loyalty one-liner below the hero CTA** — a single line like "Skechers Elite members earn double points today" keeps the rewards hook above the fold
