# Homepage snapshot · www.gnc.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- GNC is leading hard with a "Get Ready for Summer SALE" and a BOGO 50% off deal — which honestly is exactly the kind of thing that gets my attention since I auto-deliver protein and stack promos whenever I can. The homepage is supplement-forward and sale-heavy, which fits my world, but the massive cookie consent modal blocking half the hero on load is immediately annoying. Feels like a solid sale event but the presentation is sloppy.

## What's working

- **"Buy 1, Get 1 50% Off!" sticky promo bar** — I see this the instant the page loads and it's immediately relevant. I buy protein in bulk, so BOGO is the exact promo that makes me click.
- **CREATINE category pill** — That's a deliberate placement right under the carousel. One tap and I'm in my aisle. Smart.
- **"GET READY FOR SUMMER SALE" branding** — Seasonally timely, ties a reason to buy into the campaign. I get it: pre-summer stack-up window.
- **Search bar prominent above the fold** — High visibility, easy to hunt specific SKUs when I know what I want.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal** — It fires immediately and covers the entire lower half of the hero. On mobile this kills the first impression. I can't see the "SHOP NOW" CTA or whatever lifestyle imagery is behind it.
- **No gym/training imagery visible** — The hero is a graphic-design sale card (red background, big text). There's no model lifting, no gym scene, nothing that makes me feel like this brand knows my world.
- **No loyalty or rewards hook** — I know GNC has a Pro Access membership. Where is it? No badge, no "member pricing," no rewards balance. I'm probably a repeat customer and they're treating me like a stranger.
- **MULTIVITAMINS and CREATINE are the only two quick-nav pills** — Protein powder — GNC's core SKU for guys like me — isn't one of them. That's a miss.
- **Carousel navigation arrows on mobile** — Left/right arrows on a touch-scroll carousel feel clunky on a phone screen. Wastes the pill real estate below.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the graphic-design hero for a training lifestyle shot** — A guy mid-lift with copy like "Stack Your Summer. BOGO 50% Off Protein & Creatine" speaks directly to me instead of feeling like a generic retail sale.
- **Add "PROTEIN" as a third quick-nav pill** — It's GNC's most purchased supplement category. It should be the first pill, not missing entirely.
- **Surface the Pro Access / rewards hook in the sticky bar** — "Members get double points this week" next to the BOGO banner would immediately activate repeat buyers and convert fence-sitters.
- **Lazy-load or minimize the cookie banner on return visits** — Fire it on first visit only. On mobile it's eating half the hero and blocking the primary CTA on a page meant to convert.
