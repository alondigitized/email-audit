# Homepage snapshot · www.gnc.com · 2026-07-06
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-06**
## Executive summary

- Okay, GNC is my go-to for protein and stack deals, so I came in expecting to see something that immediately validates my visit — but the first thing I'm hit with is a giant cookie consent modal eating the entire above-the-fold experience. I can tell there's a "Buy 1, Get 1" promo lurking behind it and a PROTEIN category quicklink that's right on target for me, but this modal wall is genuinely killing the vibe before I even get to the goods.

## What's working

- **"Buy 1, Get 1 50% Off!" top banner** — this hits immediately. B1G1 is the exact deal format that gets me to call my gym buddy and say "yo, stock up this week." The placement in the very first sticky bar is smart.
- **PROTEIN and MULTIVITAMINS quicklink pills** — two taps from the nav and I'm in my lane. These category pills are the right shortcut for a returning customer who knows what he wants.
- **GNC PRO teaser** — I can barely see "GNC PRO" peeking behind the modal, and that brand line signals to me this isn't beginner-supplements territory. That's the right signal for me.
- **Lean, clean nav bar** — hamburger, search, cart. No clutter. I can get around fast once the modal clears.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal blocks the entire hero** — this is the biggest problem on the page. I came to GNC for a deal, and instead I'm reading about analytics partners. The whole above-the-fold experience is dead until I dismiss it.
- **Zero loyalty/rewards hook visible** — I'm a returning customer with a Pro Access sub and an auto-deliver order. Show me my points balance or a "member price" badge before I even think about converting. Nothing here.
- **No urgency on the B1G1 promo** — the banner says the deal exists but gives me no reason to act today vs. next week. No "ends Sunday" or countdown anywhere visible.
- **Search bar takes up significant real estate** but the quicklinks below it are cut off — I can see PROTEIN and MULTIVITAMINS but there are clearly more categories that are off-screen without any visual cue that I can scroll right.

## Recommendations

- **Auto-dismiss or delay the cookie modal by 3–5 seconds** — let me see the homepage for a beat before blocking it. At minimum, shrink it to a bottom banner instead of a full-screen takeover.
- **Add a deadline or countdown to the B1G1 banner** — "Buy 1, Get 1 50% Off — Ends Sunday" would turn a passive awareness promo into an actual conversion trigger for me and my crew.
- **Surface a loyalty/rewards widget in the sticky nav** — a "You have 420 points" chip or a PRO member badge next to the account icon would immediately validate my relationship with the brand before I even scroll.
- **Make the horizontal category quicklinks scrollable with a visible overflow indicator** — a subtle gradient fade on the right edge signals "there's more" so I actually swipe through PRE-WORKOUT and BCAA instead of stopping at Multivitamins.
