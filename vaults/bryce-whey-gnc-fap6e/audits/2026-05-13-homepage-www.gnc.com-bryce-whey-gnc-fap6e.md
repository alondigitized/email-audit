# Homepage snapshot · www.gnc.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- GNC is coming in loud with a BOGO 50% off summer sale — that's the whole vibe above the fold, and honestly, as someone who auto-delivers protein every month and stacks promos whenever I can, the headline offer lands. But the cookie consent modal is eating a big chunk of the hero, and there's zero loyalty program visibility despite the fact that I've been a GNC Pro Access member for two years. Feels like a sale event page, not a homecoming.

## What's working

- **"Buy 1, Get 1 50% Off!" top banner** — repeated twice (sticky top strip + carousel) so it registers immediately. This is the exact kind of stackable promo I'm there for.
- **Quick-filter pills ("Multivitamins", "Creatine", "Protein")** — one tap to my category, no hunting. Perfect for mobile. Creatine and Protein are literally my two core line items.
- **"GET READY FOR SUMMER SALE" hero** — the seasonal hook is on-point for May. I'm thinking about cutting cycles and summer stacks right now.
- **Search bar prominent above the fold** — "What can we help you find today?" makes sense for a catalog this big. I use search when I'm looking for a specific formula.
- **In-store expert chat widget** — as someone who brings friends in for sale events, the "GNC Expert in Arcadia" touchpoint is actually useful.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal covering the hero** — the giant consent overlay is sitting right on top of the sale image. Before I can even engage with the page I'm clicking "OK." Kills momentum.
- **Zero loyalty/rewards visibility** — I'm a Pro Access member and there's no "Welcome back, your rewards balance is X" or member pricing badge anywhere. GNC Pro Access is a real differentiator; not showing it is leaving money on the table.
- **No product imagery** — everything above the fold is text and a sale graphic. I can't see a tub of protein, a pre-workout pouch, nothing. For a supplement brand, visual product merchandising should be front and center.
- **BOGO copy cuts off** — the hero "BUY 1, GET 1" text is truncated; I can't see the full offer in the main panel.
- **No urgency signal** — "Buy 1 Get 1 50% Off!" but no "Ends Sunday" or countdown. Feels like a standing promo, not a real event.

## Recommendations

- **Add a loyalty balance chip in the nav bar** — right next to the cart icon, show "⭐ 840 pts" so returning Pro Access members feel recognized the second they land.
- **Add a deadline to the BOGO banner** — "Buy 1, Get 1 50% Off — Ends Sunday 5/18" makes the promo feel like a real event and drives urgency without being fake about it.
- **Swap the text-only sale graphic for a product lifestyle shot** — show a gym bag, a shaker, a tub of whey in the hero. This is GNC — give me something to want.
- **Dismiss the cookie modal with a less intrusive banner** — drop it to a slim bottom strip instead of a full-bleed overlay that buries the hero on load.
