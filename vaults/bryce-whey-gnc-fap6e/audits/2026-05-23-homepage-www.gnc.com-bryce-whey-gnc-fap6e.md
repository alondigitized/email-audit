# Homepage snapshot · www.gnc.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- GNC's homepage is throwing a solid promo at me the second I land — Barebells at 25% off for $1.94 is the kind of thing that actually catches my eye. But a cookie consent banner is blocking the bottom third of the screen, so I can't even see the full hero or CTA without tapping through it first. This feels like a supplement destination that knows its core shopper, but the execution on mobile is getting in its own way.

## What's working

- **Barebells hero with $1.94 price point** — I know this brand, it's legit, and calling out a specific price rather than just "% off" is actually compelling. That's the kind of micro-signal that makes me stop scrolling.
- **"PROTEIN" and "MULTIVITAMINS" quick-nav pills** — exactly my categories, right at the top. One tap to get where I'm going. That's smart mobile UX.
- **BOGO 1 Get 1 50% Off banner** — stacks with my already-planned Auto-Deliver mentality. A deal on top of a deal is how I shop.
- **Search bar front and center** — I know what I want (whey isolate, specific brand), so the ability to search immediately without hunting through nav is big.
- **GNC branding is clean** — "Live Well" is a bit soft for my vibe but the red logo is familiar and trust-building.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal blocking the hero** — the overlay covers the CTA button (I can see "SHOP NOW" is partially visible but blocked). That's a direct conversion killer on mobile.
- **BOGO banner is vague** — "Buy 1, Get 1 50% Off!" with no product category, no brand, no minimum spend. I'm not clicking something I can't pre-qualify.
- **No gym-culture visual language** — no athlete, no workout imagery, no social proof from anyone who looks like me. Just a floating protein bar.
- **No loyalty/rewards callout** — I'm a GNC repeat buyer and I have no idea if I have points, a member price, or a tier benefit waiting for me. That info should be surfaced.
- **The hero portion behind the cookie banner** — there's clearly more copy and a CTA below the Barebells 25% off graphic, but it's completely hidden by the consent overlay. I'm essentially forced to dismiss a legal banner before I can even see the product.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the cookie banner before rendering the hero** — or at minimum render it as a slim bottom bar, not a full-screen overlay. You're blocking your own conversion above the fold.
- **Qualify the BOGO banner** — change "Buy 1, Get 1 50% Off!" to "Buy 1, Get 1 50% Off — Protein Powders & Bars" so I know immediately whether it applies to my cart.
- **Add a loyalty/member price badge to the Barebells hero** — something like "PRO Access: $1.65" next to the $1.94 would immediately make me think about logging in.
- **Swap the generic hero background for a gym-lifestyle image** — a training scene or an athlete holding the bar would resonate way harder than a floating product shot on blue.
