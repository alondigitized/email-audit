# Homepage snapshot · www.gnc.com · 2026-05-07
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- GNC is hitting me with a BOGO 50% off deal the second I land — that's the right call for a supplement stacker like me. The site reads like a promo-first, product-second experience right now, which is fine when the deal is actually solid. But a massive cookie consent modal is eating the entire lower half of my screen, and I can't see any actual products, stack recommendations, or lifestyle content — just a wall of legal text and an OK button blocking what should be my entry point.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% off triple placement** — sticky top bar, carousel, and dark hero block means the deal lands before I even consciously read it. That's smart repetition for someone like me who's scanning for a reason to buy.
- **GNC PRO ACCESS call-out** — "MEMBER EXCLUSIVE!" badge in the hero tells me my membership is worth something right now. That's a retention hook done right.
- **Creatine and Protein quick-select pills** — these are literally two of the four things in my monthly stack. One tap and I'm in the right category without digging through nav.
- **Search bar prominent** — "What can we help you find today?" is front and center. I know exactly what I want to search when I land, so this works for me.

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal covers 40%+ of the viewport** — this is the biggest friction point. I can't see the hero image, any product CTAs, or what's below the PRO ACCESS block. I'm forced to dismiss it before I can actually shop.
- **No actual product imagery visible** — I'm a label reader, but I still want to see what's featured. Zero product shots visible above the modal.
- **"ALMOST EVERYTHING" qualifier on the BOGO** — gym guys talk to each other. If the exclusions are too gnarly, word gets out and the deal feels bait-and-switch.
- **No stack or bundle recommendations** — GNC knows I buy multiple things. No "complete your stack" or "pairs well with" module visible at all.
- **Chat widget + cookie modal simultaneously** — two overlays fighting for my tap at the exact moment I'm trying to orient myself on the page.

## Recommendations

- **Auto-dismiss the cookie modal after 3 seconds or shrink it to a bottom bar** — the current full-block approach is costing them above-the-fold real estate and first impressions. A slim "We use cookies — OK" strip at the bottom would clear the deck.
- **Add a "Build Your Stack" CTA to the PRO ACCESS hero block** — right now it says the deal exists but doesn't give me a path. A single "Shop Member Deals" button would convert the awareness into a click.
- **Surface the BOGO exclusions clearly** — one line like "excludes whey isolates over $60" builds trust instead of eroding it. Gym community is vocal about bait-and-switch.
- **Add a "Trending in Protein" or "This Week's Top Stack" horizontal scroll rail** — I want to know what's moving. Best-sellers with star ratings would get me scrolling immediately.
