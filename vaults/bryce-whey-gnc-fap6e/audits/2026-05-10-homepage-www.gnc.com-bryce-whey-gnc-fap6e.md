# Homepage snapshot · www.gnc.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- GNC's homepage is doing exactly what GNC always does — leading hard with a BOGO sale and plastering "GET READY FOR SUMMER SALE" across the hero. It's a supplement store I know well, and that BOGO 50% deal is the kind of thing that actually gets me to open the app. The cookie consent wall swallowing the bottom third of my screen on first load is genuinely annoying though — classic GNC, never quite nailing the mobile experience.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% banner** — it's the first thing I read, shown twice (top persistent bar + hero carousel), and it's the exact promo that makes me stock up on protein
- **Category pill strip** — "Multivitamins," "Creatine," "Protein" are right there; one tap and I'm browsing my category without touching the hamburger menu
- **"Get Ready for Summer Sale" seasonal hook** — May is prime shred season; this messaging lands for where I'm at in my training cycle
- **Search bar front and center** — I know what I'm looking for; being able to search immediately vs. digging through nav is appreciated

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal blocking the hero** — first thing I see on load is a legal wall asking me to OK tracking. It covers the CTA and breaks immersion immediately
- **No loyalty/rewards surface** — I have a GNC account and presumably points; nothing tells me my balance or nudges me to stack rewards on this BOGO. Lost opportunity
- **Hero is a graphic, not a lifestyle shot** — big red "SALE" text with no gym imagery whatsoever. No athlete, no lifting scene, nothing that says "this brand is for people like you"
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA context is unclear** — the only visible button appears to be associated with a local store chat widget ("GNC Expert in Arcadia"), not the main sale. Confusing
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — I need to know if I'm hitting minimum spend before I start adding to cart

## Recommendations

- **Swap the all-type sale graphic for a lifestyle hero** — a guy in the gym mid-lift with the BOGO overlay reads way better for my demo than red block letters; keep the promo, ditch the clipart energy
- **Surface my rewards balance in the nav bar** — if I'm logged in, show "You have 420 points" next to the account icon; that alone pulls me toward checkout to redeem
- **Pin a clear hero CTA to the sale** — a "Shop the BOGO" button in the center of the hero that isn't blocked by cookie overlays or confused with the local store widget
- **Add free-shipping threshold to the persistent top bar** — swap one of the two BOGO callouts for "Free shipping over $49" so I know the deal before I start browsing
