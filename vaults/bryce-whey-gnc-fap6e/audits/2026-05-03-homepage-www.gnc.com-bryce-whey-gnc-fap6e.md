## What's working

- **BOGO 50% banner (top strip + hero block):** Doubled down — appears in the blue header strip AND in the dark hero section. I clock the deal in under two seconds. As someone who auto-ships protein anyway, a BOGO on my next creatine order is genuinely tempting.
- **GNC PRO ACCESS "MEMBER EXCLUSIVE!" callout:** This makes me feel like logging into my account actually means something. Loyalty-gating the best deal is smart — it trains me to stay signed in.
- **Creatine + Protein quick-link chips:** These are right below the search bar. One tap and I'm in my category. Zero digging through a mega-menu.
- **Search bar placement:** Prominent, above the fold, with friendly placeholder copy. High tech comfort = I'd use this immediately.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent overlay:** The modal is covering the bottom third of the visible screen — whatever was below the PRO ACCESS block is hidden. First impression of the page is partially obstructed.
- **Zero product imagery:** Everything visible is text and logos. There's no protein tub, no shaker bottle, no athlete — nothing to spark "I need that" for a visual browser.
- **No CTA button above the fold:** The deal is loud but there's no actionable button ("Shop the BOGO" / "See All Deals") that I can tap without scrolling or guessing.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible:** I always calculate shipping into my stack cost. If it's buried below the fold I'll open a competitor tab before I even scroll.
- **"BIG SAVINGS, ONLY FOR PROS" copy is cut off:** The section heading is partially visible but truncated. It teases something I can't act on without dismissing the cookie overlay first.

## Recommendations

- **Add a tappable "Shop the BOGO" button** directly on the dark hero block — same section as the "50% OFF" copy. Right now the deal is announced but not actionable without hunting for it.
- **Swap the blank dark hero background for a lifestyle shot:** A guy in a gym with a protein tub or mid-lift would signal "this is your store" to me in 0.5 seconds. The deal copy can still overlay the image.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold in the top banner** alongside the BOGO — even "Free shipping on orders over $49" in small text converts supplement buyers because we're always buying in bulk.
- **Auto-dismiss or reduce the cookie overlay footprint:** At minimum, shrink it to a thin bottom bar so it doesn't eat 30% of the viewport on first load.
