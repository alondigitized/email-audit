# Homepage snapshot · www.gnc.com · 2026-06-08
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay, GNC is leading with Ghost and a BOGO 50% banner — that combo lands immediately for me. This looks like a supplement-first destination that knows its core audience, but a full-screen cookie consent modal is physically blocking the hero before I can even see what's behind it. Strong intent, rough first impression.

## What's working

- **Ghost BOGO strip** — Ghost is legitimately one of the hottest supplement brands in my demo right now; leading with a BOGO 50% on it is the right call and I immediately stop scrolling
- **Top banner redundancy** — the "Buy 1, Get 1 50% Off!" appears in the top ticker AND the Ghost section so it registers even if I skim fast
- **"PROTEIN" as first category pill** — I don't have to hunt; my primary category is the literal first tap available
- **Search bar placement** — prominent and first interactive element below the nav; if I want to look up a specific stack I'm two taps in

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal blocking the hero** — I can see maybe 20% of what looks like a lifestyle shot and partial copy ("FEEL YOUR BEST. PERFORM STRONGER"). That modal fires before I've decided I care, which is immediately annoying
- **No loyalty hook above fold** — I have a GNC rewards account. I want to see my points balance or a "Members get X% more" nudge to remind me why I'm here vs. Amazon
- **No free-shipping callout** — for a supplement haul I'm usually spending $60–$100; that threshold should be surfaced so I know I'm already close
- **No trust signals** — no review counts, no "4.8 stars from 12K reviews" badge; just price and brand name

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the modal or replace with a bottom banner** — cookie consent should be a slim bar at the bottom, not a full overlay blocking the hero on first load; you lose me in the first two seconds
- **Add a sticky loyalty strip** — "You have 340 GNC points — $X to your next reward" with my account state visible would immediately differentiate this from Amazon
- **Surface free-shipping threshold** — add "Free shipping on orders $49+" to the top ticker alongside the BOGO offer; I do the math and add to cart faster
- **Swap the Ghost logo treatment for a lifestyle shot** — the Ghost ghost-face logo on a cyan background is brand-correct but cold; a quick gym action shot with the Ghost product would connect harder and preview the hero content the modal is hiding
