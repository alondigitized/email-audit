# Homepage snapshot · www.gnc.com · 2026-06-01
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-01**
## Executive summary

- Alright, first tap and GNC is already in my lane — PROTEIN button front and center, Ghost collab with a fat BOGO deal, no fluff. This looks like a supplements-first brand in full promo mode right now. The cookie consent modal stomping over the bottom third of the screen is annoying, but honestly the bones here are solid for someone like me.

## What's working

- **"PROTEIN" and "MULTIVITAMINS" quick-nav pills** — I can tap into my category in one shot without digging through a hamburger menu. That's exactly how I shop on mobile.
- **Ghost BOGO 50% off module** — Ghost is legitimately the brand I'd bring a buddy to the store for. A BOGO on Ghost pre-workout or protein drops is a real buy-two-get-deal, not some 10%-off-joke.
- **Top promo banner ("Buy 1, Get 1 50% Off!")** — persistent, above everything, impossible to miss. Sets expectations immediately.
- **Search bar placement** — right under the nav, prominent. As someone who comes in looking for a specific SKU (like Optimum Nutrition Gold Standard 5lb), I can skip straight to it.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal blocks ~40% of the viewport** — I landed, saw the Ghost deal start to load underneath, and then got a full-width cookie wall with an "OK" button I have to dismiss before I can see anything else. That's friction at the worst possible moment.
- **No GNC Pro Access / loyalty info legible above the fold** — I can see the "GNC PRO ACCESS" module starting to appear, but it's cut off. If there's member pricing on the BOGO, I have no idea. That's a miss for a returning subscriber.
- **No lifestyle/gym imagery** — it's all typography and brand logos. Nothing showing someone like me in a gym or post-workout. It's functional but not inspiring.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — I'm about to spend $80+ on a Ghost haul; if there's free shipping I'm buying more to hit it. That number should be pinned somewhere obvious.
- **No urgency signal on the Ghost promo** — "ends when?" If this is a limited collab deal, put a countdown or an end date on it. I'll procrastinate and forget.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the cookie banner by default or shrink it to a slim footer strip** — the full-page-bottom overlay is killing the first impression. A thin "we use cookies / OK" bar loses zero compliance and stops blocking the GNC Pro deal module.
- **Pin a free-shipping threshold to the promo bar** next to the BOGO copy: "Buy 1, Get 1 50% Off + Free Shipping Over $49" — one bar, two motivators.
- **Add a countdown or "through June X" badge to the Ghost promo module** — limited-collab urgency converts browsers like me who would otherwise close the tab and come back never.
- **Expose GNC Pro Access member pricing above the fold** — even a "Members save extra on BOGO" badge on the Ghost module would make me log in immediately instead of buying as a guest.
