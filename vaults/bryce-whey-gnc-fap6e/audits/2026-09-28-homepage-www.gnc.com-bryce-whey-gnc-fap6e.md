# Homepage snapshot · www.gnc.com · 2026-09-28
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- Okay this is very much GNC's wheelhouse — protein tubs front and center, Creatine Monohydrate, Mega Men Vitapak, big red 30% OFF banner. But I literally can't read half the hero because a cookie banner AND a sign-in popup are both stacked on top of it at the same time. Feels like a supplement brand that knows its audience (me) but hasn't QA'd its own site.

## What's working

- **The product lineup in the hero** — Wholybolic protein, Pro Creatine Monohydrate, Mega Men Vitapak, Omega fish oil — that's exactly my stack, no guessing needed.
- **"Sign in to get rewarded" card** — 3% cash back everyday and 150 points = $5 is a real, specific loyalty number, not vague "join for perks" fluff.
- **30% OFF ALL GNC BRANDS banner** — big, bold, unmissable number even through the clutter.
- **"UP TO 60% OFF FAVORITES" rail below** with Lean Shake, Creatine Monohydrate, Men's 30+ Vitapak, Magnesium Glycinate — solid best-seller mix for someone who already knows what they want.

## What's weak

- **Stacked overlays** — cookie consent box bottom-left and the sign-in modal top-right are both open simultaneously, blocking the actual hero headline. I can only see "TH...OU" of what should be the main message.
- **That floating "Shop with a GNC Expert in Arcadia" chat bubble** bottom right adds a fourth competing element on an already crowded first screen.
- **No visible Auto-Deliver / subscribe-and-save callout** on the homepage — that's literally how I buy my protein, and it's nowhere above the fold.
- **Nav row is dense and small** — Protein, Multivitamins, Creatine, Pre-Workout + Performance, etc. all crammed into one thin line, easy to fat-finger the wrong one on mobile.

## Recommendations

- Don't fire the cookie banner and the sign-in modal at the same time — stagger them or combine into one dismiss action.
- Move the "Sign in to get rewarded" card so it doesn't sit directly over the hero headline text.
- Add an "Auto-Deliver and Save 5%" badge or strip near the protein hero image — that's the single fastest way to hook a subscription shopper like me.
- Let the 30% OFF banner and the product shot breathe — right now the giant "30% OFF" numerals crash into the product photo edge.
