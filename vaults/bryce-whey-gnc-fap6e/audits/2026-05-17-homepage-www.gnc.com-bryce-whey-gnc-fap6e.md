# Homepage snapshot · www.gnc.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- GNC comes out swinging with a "Get Ready for Summer Sale" hero and a B1G1 50% off banner that's plastered everywhere — honestly, that hits. But there's a giant cookie consent wall sitting right in the middle of the screen blocking half the content, which is an immediate buzzkill. This page looks like a promotional machine that knows its audience (summer cuts, supplement stacking) but fumbles the execution with friction right at the first touchpoint.

## What's working

- **"Get Ready for Summer SALE" hero** — the sun-burst graphic + "BUY 1, GET 1" copy in big block letters is peak gym-bro energy for May. I immediately understand there's a meaningful deal.
- **"CREATINE" quick-tap category** — seeing creatine as a fast-access button right below the search bar is exactly what I want. One tap to my staple.
- **Persistent BOGO banner** — the top blue strip reinforces the offer so even if I miss the hero, I've seen the deal twice already.
- **Live chat widget** — "Chat with a GNC Expert" in the corner is actually useful for stack questions. That's a trust signal for a supplement nerd like me.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal** — it's covering roughly 40% of the screen and blocks the hero CTA. This is the first thing I interact with and it's not about products at all.
- **No loyalty/rewards callout** — GNC has a membership program and there's zero mention of it above the fold. I auto-deliver protein; I should be seeing a member-pricing badge or points balance.
- **No specific product imagery** — the hero is pure type and color blocks. No jug of protein, no shaker, no athlete. Nothing that says "these are the exact supplements you want."
- **"MULTIVITAMINS" and "CREATINE" only** — two quick-category chips feels incomplete. No protein, no pre-workout, no BCAAs — which is the rest of my stack.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the immediate cookie modal** — delay it to scroll or use a thin bottom bar. Blocking the hero on first load is burning intent from people who arrived for the sale.
- **Add a protein quick-tap category** next to creatine — for a gym audience, whey/protein is the highest-AOV category and it's not here.
- **Drop in a loyalty badge** in the top-right or near the BOGO banner — even a small "Pro Access members save extra" line turns a one-time buyer into a subscriber.
- **Put a product image in the hero** — a lifestyle shot of a guy mid-workout with a visible supplement product would make this feel like a supplement site, not a generic sale flyer.
