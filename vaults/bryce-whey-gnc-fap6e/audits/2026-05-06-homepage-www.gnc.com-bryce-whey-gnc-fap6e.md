# Homepage snapshot · www.gnc.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- First thing I see when I open GNC.com on my phone is a full-screen email capture modal — the actual homepage is completely blocked. The popup is pitching 10% off for signing up, which is fine in concept, but it's the very first thing I hit before I can see a single product, promo, or category. GNC is clearly pushing list growth right now, hard, at the cost of every other first impression.

## What's working

- **The 10% off hook is concrete.** "UNLOCK 10% OFF" with a clear black CTA button — I know exactly what I'd get if I hand over my email. That's better than a vague "Join our community" ask.
- **The CTA button is impossible to miss.** Full-width, high contrast, bold all-caps. On mobile that's the right call — no hunting for the tap target.
- **"DECLINE OFFER" is available.** At least they let me out without burying the dismiss. I can bail without hunting for a tiny X.

## What's weak

- **The modal blocks 100% of the homepage.** I can't see a single product, promo banner, category, or hero. I came here to shop protein — I'm immediately being asked for my email before I know if this site has anything worth buying.
- **"START YOUR JOURNEY TODAY" is embarrassingly generic.** That's Planet Fitness copy, not GNC. There's nothing performance-focused, nothing about gains, stacks, or pre-workout. Zero resonance for me.
- **That red border looks like a CSS bug.** The modal has a thick red outline that does not look intentional — it reads like a dev artifact left in production. Kills trust instantly.
- **No loyalty/rewards mention in the modal.** I'm a returning GNC customer type — is this 10% stackable with my rewards? Does it apply to my Auto-Deliver protein order? No answer visible.
- **"Exclusions may apply" with zero detail.** That's exactly the kind of copy that makes me think the 10% won't apply to anything I actually want to buy.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the generic "Start Your Journey Today" headline for stack-specific copy.** Something like "10% Off Your First Protein Order" or "Build Your Stack — Get 10% Off" immediately signals GNC knows who's visiting.
- **Kill the red border on the modal.** Whatever CSS is causing that thick red outline is a production bug. It looks broken. Fix it before anything else.
- **Add a one-liner loyalty hook inside the modal.** "Already a Pro Access member? Sign in for member pricing" — that keeps returning shoppers from bouncing and positions the signup as additive, not a gate.
- **Delay the modal or trigger it on exit intent.** Let me see the homepage for 5 seconds before the ask. I'll be more likely to hand over my email after I've seen the BCAAs and pre-workout selection that actually interest me.
