# Homepage snapshot · www.gnc.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- Yo, GNC knows why I'm here — the second I land I see "Buy 1, Get 1 50% Off!" splashed across the top bar AND the rotating banner, and there's literally a "CREATINE" quick-link button waiting for me like they read my mind. The whole vibe is summer-prep sale mode, which tracks perfectly for a guy who's been ramping macros since March. One annoyance: a fat cookie-consent modal is blocking half the page before I can even breathe.

## What's working

- **"CREATINE" quick-link button** — that's a one-tap straight to my stack, no digging through categories. Huge.
- **"GET READY FOR SUMMER SALE" hero** — hits the exact mental mode I'm in right now: summer cut, need to restock, motivated to buy.
- **Buy 1, Get 1 50% Off in the sticky top bar** — I see it before I see anything else. Even if I close the modal and scroll away, that offer is burned in.
- **GNC LIVE WELL logo + search bar** — clean, familiar brand mark and an accessible search up top. High-tech comfort persona means I'll search before I browse.
- **"MULTIVITAMINS" + "CREATINE" pill buttons** — smart shortcut layout for mobile. Two taps and I'm in the right aisle.

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal fires immediately** — it's covering the lower half of the hero section including whatever's in the "NEW ITEMS A..." module and the SHOP NOW button. First impression is a wall of legal text, not a product.
- **No loyalty / Pro Access hook above fold** — I'm a repeat buyer with an Auto-Deliver subscription; there should be something acknowledging that. No points balance, no "Member price" badge, nothing.
- **Hero is graphic-only, no actual product shot** — the "SALE" card is bold but it's wordmark + starburst art. I can't see a protein tub, a shaker, a guy lifting. No product aspiration.
- **No urgency / deadline copy** — BOGO 50% is great but "ends when?" There's no timer, no "this weekend only," no scarcity signal to make me act now vs. tomorrow.
- **No free-shipping callout** — I'm already doing Auto-Deliver; free shipping is probably baked in, but a new visitor or a one-time purchase guy has no idea of the threshold from this screen.

## Recommendations

- **Pin a loyalty balance chip in the top nav** — even something like "Pro Points: 2,450" next to the account icon turns returning buyers into active loyalty hunters before they hit the hero.
- **Swap the starburst-art hero for a lifestyle product shot** — a close-up of a protein tub or a gym bag haul would land harder than a generic SALE graphic. Keep the headline, add the product.
- **Add a countdown or end-date badge to the BOGO banner** — "Ends Sunday 5/25" is all it takes. That drives same-session converts instead of tab-closers who plan to "come back later."
- **Delay or minimize the cookie modal** — fire it after 3 seconds or as a slim bottom bar. Blocking the hero on load on mobile is conversion suicide for a high-intent visitor.
