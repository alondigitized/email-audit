# Homepage snapshot · www.gnc.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Alright, so GNC's mobile homepage is basically one big BOGO sign with a cookie wall in the way — very "supplement store" energy, which is fine, but there's not a lot going on beyond the deal. The "Get Ready for Summer Sale" hero is timely and the Creatine quick-chip is a nice touch for me, but the cookie consent banner is literally eating half the above-the-fold content right now, and I can't see a real CTA without tapping OK first.

## What's working

- **"Buy 1, Get 1 50% Off!" banner** — shows up in the top promo strip AND the carousel, so the deal registers instantly even before I read anything else. For someone who bulk-buys protein, this is exactly the hook I need.
- **CREATINE quick-chip** — one tap to get where I'm going. Whoever added that alongside MULTIVITAMINS clearly knows the site's core buyer.
- **"Get Ready for Summer Sale" hero** — the timing is right. May is peak "cut season" for me, so this campaign lands.
- **Search bar placement** — right under the nav, easy to reach with my thumb. I know what I want; let me type it.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent wall** — it's blocking the hero CTA and part of the "NEW ITEMS ADDED" copy. On a 390px mobile viewport this is brutal. I have to dismiss it before I can interact with anything meaningful.
- **No loyalty/rewards hook** — I'm an Auto-Deliver subscriber. There's zero above-the-fold acknowledgment that I'm a returning customer. No points balance, no member-exclusive pricing callout, nothing.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — I always check this before I add to cart. It's not here.
- **No product imagery** — the hero is just a red/orange gradient with text. No shake, no tub, no athlete. For a supplement brand, that's a miss — I want to see what I'm buying.
- **"NEW ITEMS ADDED" copy is obscured** — the cookie banner is cutting off what sounds like it could be a useful announcement. I can't read it.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the cookie banner on scroll or after 3 seconds, not on pageload** — or at minimum size it as a slim bottom bar instead of a full-width overlay that buries the primary CTA.
- **Surface a loyalty/rewards hook in the top promo strip** — swap one carousel slide to "Pro Access members: extra 10% on all BOGO orders" so returning subscribers feel seen immediately.
- **Add a product shot to the Summer Sale hero** — a tub of whey or a shaker bottle beats a gradient. Even a flat-lay of three supplement categories makes it feel like a supplement store, not a generic sale landing page.
- **Pin the free-shipping threshold to the promo strip** — "Free shipping over $49" costs one line of text and saves me a tab-switch to check.
