# Homepage snapshot · www.gnc.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- Okay, GNC is swinging hard on a deal right now — BUY 1 GET 1 50% OFF is plastered everywhere before I even scroll. As a supplement guy, that headline gets my attention fast. The category quick-taps at the top with PROTEIN right there is smart, but the cookie consent wall eating half the hero makes the whole thing feel janky on mobile.

## What's working

- **"PROTEIN" quick-tap button** — it's black, bold, and sitting right below the search bar. One tap to my category without hunting through a nav tree. That's exactly what I want when I'm on my phone between sets.
- **Double-reinforced BUY 1 GET 1 50% Off** — the teal promo strip at the top AND the big red-and-blue hero graphic both say it. Hard to miss. That's the kind of deal I'll actually text to my gym buddy.
- **Search bar prominently placed** — "What can we help you find today?" sits center-stage. If I'm looking for a specific BCAA or pre-workout, I'm not digging through menus.
- **Patriotic/bold graphic design on the hero** — it reads gym-ready, loud, and deal-forward. Not a lifestyle-magazine vibe, which is correct for GNC's audience.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal blocking the hero** — on mobile this thing covers most of the above-fold content. The "OK" button is there but the modal is stealing screen space from the actual offer I came for. Classic conversion killer.
- **No person in the hero** — there's zero human element above fold. No athlete, no gym shot. Just bold text on a textured background. I'm not emotionally connected to anything.
- **No loyalty/rewards hook visible** — I'm a repeat buyer. Show me my points balance or a "Members save more" badge. Nothing here tells me I'm valued as a returning customer.
- **No urgency signal** — BUY 1 GET 1 is great but there's no "ends Sunday" or countdown. I'll close the tab and think about it later.
- **Chat widget crowding the bottom right** — the "with a GNC Expert in Arcadia" store locator/chat bubble is overlapping the bottom of the hero content. It's not a deal-breaker but it adds clutter on a small screen.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the blocking cookie modal on mobile or shrink it to a banner strip** — a one-line "We use cookies. OK." bar at the bottom preserves the hero and doesn't sandpaper the first impression.
- **Add a loyalty member callout next to the BUY 1 GET 1 strip** — even "Pro Access Members: extra 10% stacked" would stop returning buyers from bouncing to Amazon.
- **Put a countdown timer on the deal** — even "Sale ends Sunday" in the promo strip would spike urgency and make me stop second-guessing and hit the cart.
- **Swap the abstract texture background for a gym/athlete lifestyle shot** — keep the BUY 1 GET 1 overlay text but anchor it with a real person who looks like they lift. Instant persona match.
