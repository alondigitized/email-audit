# Homepage snapshot · www.gnc.com · 2026-08-10
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- This reads like a supplement warehouse that's mid-liquidation — BOGO 50% off slapped across the hero, a "LAST CHANCE" nav tab in red, and product tiles stacked with more BOGO badges. That's exactly the kind of aggressive stacking I hunt for, but the page opens with two popups fighting for my thumb before I've even seen the deal clearly.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% off banner** — big, bold, exactly the stacking-promo energy that gets me and my lifting buddies to actually check out together.
- **CREATINE as its own top-nav tab** — that's a category-specific shortcut most stores bury; GNC knows their audience wants a direct line to it.
- **"Best Seller" tag on GNC Mega Men Ultra Testosterone Booster** — a trust signal that speeds up my decision without me needing to cross-reference reviews.
- **PRO Access Member callout in the top bar** — speaks directly to my loyalty-app mentality even before I scroll.

## What's weak

- **Two modals stacked at once** — the sign-in rewards popup and the cookie-consent banner are both open simultaneously, covering part of the hero text and the corner of the product grid. I have to clear two obstacles before I can actually shop.
- **Hero copy is literally cut off** — "IT'S SAV..." trails off behind the sign-in box, so whatever the secondary hook is, I never read it.
- **No serving-size or ingredient-label glimpse anywhere on the tiles** — as someone who actually reads labels before buying, zero label callouts (protein per scoop, mg of caffeine, etc.) on the grid is a missed hook.
- **Generic "SHOP NOW" CTA** — doesn't tell me if it's shopping pre-workout, protein, or the whole BOGO sale; a little ambiguous for a fast tap.

## Recommendations

- Kill the double-modal stack — show the cookie banner OR the sign-in nudge, never both at once, and don't let either crop the hero headline.
- se
- Swap "SHOP NOW" for something specific like "Shop BOGO Deals" so the CTA maps directly to the offer I just read.
- Add a small "24g protein / 150mg caffeine" style label chip on the pre-workout and protein tiles — that's the exact detail that gets a label-reader like me to tap Add to Cart instead of comparing tabs.
- Put an actual countdown or "ends [date]" on the BOGO banner — right now the urgency is implied, not proven, and I've been burned by "limited time" offers that run for a month.
