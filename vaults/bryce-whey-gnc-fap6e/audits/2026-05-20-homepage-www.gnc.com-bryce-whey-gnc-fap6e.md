# Homepage snapshot · www.gnc.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- GNC is screaming summer sale from the jump — I see "Buy 1, Get 1 50% Off!" in the sticky top bar, in a hero carousel, and again in the big "GET READY FOR SUMMER SALE" block, all in the first scroll. It reads like a mass-market supplement deal page, which honestly isn't bad for me, but a massive cookie consent modal is sitting right on top of the hero, blocking whatever product or lifestyle shot they put there. If I didn't already know GNC, this would feel more like a flyer than a brand.

## What's working

- **"Buy 1, Get 1 50% Off!" top banner** — triple repetition locks the deal in my brain before I even process anything else; as someone who auto-buys protein, BOGO is immediately compelling
- **PROTEIN / MULTIVITAMINS quick-link tabs** — I can reach my category in literally one tap; this is the right content for my stack
- **"GET READY FOR SUMMER SALE" messaging** — seasonal relevance is real; I'm already thinking about cutting for summer, so this isn't random noise
- **GNC "LIVE WELL" brand mark** — I know and trust GNC; the logo does credibility work for me as a returning customer
- **Search bar prominence** — "What can we help you find today?" is front and center; if I want to search "whey isolate," I can get there without hunting

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal blocking the hero** — whatever product or athlete shot GNC put in that hero is completely buried; I'm reading a privacy policy instead of feeling motivated to shop
- **No loyalty/rewards callout above the fold** — I'm the guy who stacks GNC Gold Card deals; not seeing my points balance or member perks feels like a miss for a returning customer
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — for supplement orders I often hit $50–75 AOV; I want to see "free shipping over $X" before I commit to filling a cart
- **No urgency on the BOGO** — "Buy 1, Get 1 50% Off" is everywhere but there's no "ends Sunday" or countdown anywhere; it feels permanent, which actually undercuts urgency
- **No product imagery above the fold** — the hero is all typography and a summer-burst graphic; I want to see a protein tub or an athlete, not just wordmarks

## Recommendations

- **Kill the cookie consent takeover on mobile, or auto-dismiss on scroll** — it's blocking the entire hero value prop; move to a slim bottom banner instead
- **Add a returning-member chip near the account icon** — something like "Points: 420 | Gold Card Active" so I know my loyalty status before I even browse
- **Drop a free-shipping bar** — a persistent "Free shipping on orders $49+" strip between the banner and the category tabs would directly influence my AOV behavior
- **Put a deadline on the BOGO** — even "Ends Sunday 5/25" makes the triple-repetition convert instead of just inform; time-box it
