## What's working

- **The 10% offer is concrete and legible** — "UNLOCK 10% OFF" in massive bold type, black CTA button, clear hierarchy inside the modal itself. If I'm a new visitor, that's a real number.
- **"DECLINE OFFER" escape hatch is visible** — at least there's a labeled way out, not a dark-pattern close button I have to hunt for with a magnifying glass.
- **GNC Live Well logo is immediately recognizable** — brand trust on sight, no confusion about where I landed.

## What's weak

- **The modal fires before I see anything** — I have zero context about what GNC is running right now. No protein deals, no BOGO stacks, no pre-workout drops. Nothing.
- **"START YOUR JOURNEY TODAY" is tone-deaf for a repeat buyer** — I'm not starting a journey, I'm restocking my whey and checking if there's a stackable promo. This copy reads like it's for a first-timer who's never touched a barbell.
- **"Exclusions may apply" with no detail** — instant skepticism. Is my auto-deliver protein excluded? Is the pre-workout sampler excluded? I don't know, and that friction makes me distrust the offer.
- **Zero product or category visibility** — the entire homepage behind the modal is blacked out. I can't see protein, supplements, or anything GNC sells. This is a wasted first impression.
- **No loyalty/PRO Access hook** — GNC has a membership program and I'd expect to see it used here to pull me in, not just a generic email grab.

## Recommendations

- **Delay the modal or make it a slide-in** — fire it after 15 seconds or on exit intent, not on page load. Let me see the homepage first so the 10% offer feels like a reward, not a tollbooth.
- **Swap "START YOUR JOURNEY TODAY" for something gym-stack specific** — "Stack smarter. Save 10%." or "Your next PR starts here — save 10% on your first order." Speaks to me, not a wellness newbie.
- **Add a loyalty-member login bypass** — if I'm already a PRO Access member, don't show me this at all, or swap it for "Welcome back — your rewards balance is X."
- **Show "exclusions" inline** — a single line like "Excludes protein bundles over $100" builds more trust than a vague disclaimer and would reduce the gut-check hesitation before I give my email.
