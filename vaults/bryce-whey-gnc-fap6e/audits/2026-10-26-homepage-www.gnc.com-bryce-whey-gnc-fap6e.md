# Homepage snapshot · www.gnc.com · 2026-10-26
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- This looks like a supplement-aisle brand pushing hard on new flavors. A Sour Patch Kids × ABE (All Black Everything) collab is front and center, with pre-workout, creatine and hydration tubs. It's candy-flavored and a little gimmicky, but the products are on-category for me. I couldn't actually read the hero because a "Sign in to get rewarded" popup, a cookie banner and a chat bubble are all sitting on top of it.

## What's working

- **Top nav:** "Protein, Multivitamins, Creatine, Pre-Workout + Performance, Weight Management" puts every category I buy one tap away. It reads like a gym shopper's menu.
- **Rewards popup copy:** "3% Cash Back Rewards Everyday / 150 Points = $5" is clear math I can check. I like that it's not hidden in the footer.
- **ABE product shots:** The pre-workout tub, creatine tub and RTD cans are large and sharp. I can see "30 SERVINGS" and the 144% / 54% / 17% callouts on the creatine label, which is what I check first.
- **"Shop New from ABE Nutrition" rail:** It's a clear new-drop section, so I can see what's fresh without hunting.
- **Nav tags:** "New on the Drop" and "Best Sellers" give me quick shortcuts.
- **"My Store: Monterey Park":** Local store context is useful for pickup.

## What's weak

- **Stacked overlays:** The popup, cookie banner and "Shop with a GNC Expert" bubble all cover content at once. The hero headline reads only "FU…" and the CTA reads "SH…". I can't tell what the main message is.
- **Sign-in popup on first load:** It's the first thing I see. I'd rather browse before being asked to sign up.
- **No visible price offer:** "Deals" is in the nav, but nothing above the fold gives a real discount or sale. I shop promos and stack them, so the page gives me nothing to stack.
- **No Auto-Deliver or subscribe mention:** My protein subscription is my main habit, and I see no hook for it.
- **Sour Patch Kids theme:** Candy mascots and a Sour Patch Kids logo feel a bit juvenile for a label-reader like me. The licensing fine print ("used under license") is tiny.
- **No ratings or review counts:** The ABE products show no stars or social proof.
- **No people:** There's nobody lifting or training, only product and candy.

## Recommendations

- **Delay the sign-in popup:** Show it after a scroll or on exit intent, or shrink it to a small "Earn 3% back" pill in the header. That way the hero headline and CTA are readable on first paint.
- **Add a concrete offer strip under the black banner:** For example "Auto-Deliver: save 10% + free shipping on $49+", or "Stack your rewards with fall flavor bundles". Show it without scrolling.
- **Put star ratings and review counts on the "Shop New from ABE Nutrition" product cards:** Add price and a "30 servings" note too. Make "Shop Now" a full-width button on each card.
- **Collapse the cookie banner into a slim bottom bar, and hide the chat bubble until after the first scroll:** That stops the three overlays from covering the page at once.
