# Homepage snapshot · www.gnc.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- First thing I see when I land on GNC's homepage is a full-screen email capture modal — the actual page is completely hidden behind it. GNC is clearly a serious supplement brand I already know, but right now it's pushing a 10% off sign-up offer before I've even seen a single product. The "LIVE WELL" tagline is there, but the vibe is less "welcome to your supplement HQ" and more "give us your email first."
- **4/10**

## What's working

- **The 10% off hook** — as someone who buys protein on Auto-Deliver and stacks promos religiously, 10% off my first order is actually meaningful. I'd do the math on whether my next whey haul clears the threshold.
- **Clean, bold modal design** — the GNC logo, red accent border, and big bold "UNLOCK 10% OFF" text are easy to read on a phone screen. No visual clutter.
- **"UNLOCK 10% OFF" CTA button** — large, full-width, and dark — stands out immediately. No confusion about what to tap.

## What's weak

- **Modal fires immediately on landing** — I haven't seen a single product, deal, or reason to care yet. Asking for my email before showing me anything is a friction wall, not a welcome mat.
- **"START YOUR JOURNEY TODAY" copy** — this is beginner language. I'm not starting a journey, I'm re-upping my protein stack. This makes the site feel like it's talking to a first-timer at Planet Fitness, not a regular GNC customer.
- **"Exclusions may apply" fine print** — this is a trust killer right out of the gate. It's immediately hedging the offer before I've even decided if I want it.
- **The entire homepage is blocked** — I can't see a hero, a promo banner, new arrivals, or anything product-adjacent. This is a conversion dead zone for a return visitor like me.
- **"DECLINE OFFER" CTA** — using the word "DECLINE" feels slightly shaming, like I'm refusing to save money. "No thanks" or "Skip" would feel less manipulative.

## Recommendations

- **Delay the modal by at least one scroll or 5 seconds** — let me see the homepage for a beat before hitting me with the email capture. I'm more likely to sign up after I've seen a deal I want.
- **Swap "START YOUR JOURNEY TODAY" for something stack-focused** — something like "SAVE ON YOUR NEXT STACK" speaks directly to repeat buyers and gym regulars, not just beginners.
- **Drop "Exclusions may apply" from the modal** — put that in the email confirmation. Right now it deflates the offer before I even consider it.
- **Replace "DECLINE OFFER" with "No thanks, skip"** — small copy fix, but it removes the guilt-trip framing that makes dark patterns feel gross.
