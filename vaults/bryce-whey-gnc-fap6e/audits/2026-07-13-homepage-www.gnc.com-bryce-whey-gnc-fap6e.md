# Homepage snapshot · www.gnc.com · 2026-07-13
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-13**
## Executive summary

- GNC shows up as a supplement-first brand that knows its lane — the quick-access "PROTEIN" and "MULTIVITAMINS" buttons are the first thing I see after the nav, and that BOGO 50% off banner is right at the top where it belongs. The problem is a wall-of-text cookie consent modal is sitting in the middle of my screen, completely blacking out the hero, so I have no idea what they're actually pushing right now. First impression: relevant category, solid promo, but the page feels half-loaded.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% off banner** sits in the prime real estate just below the GNC logo — impossible to miss, and BOGO is the exact kind of deal I stack on a protein reorder
- **PROTEIN and MULTIVITAMINS category shortcuts** are right below the search bar — two taps to where I'm headed anyway, no digging through nav trees
- **Search bar prompt** ("What can we help you find today?") is the right call on mobile; I often land with a specific SKU in mind (like Optimum Nutrition Gold Standard) and a search-first UI is fast for me
- **GNC "LIVE WELL" branding** is clean and doesn't try too hard — it's not embarrassing to buy supplements from a brand that looks like this

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal** is a full-width overlay blocking the hero completely — I can see an underwater/blue-toned background behind it but nothing about what's being promoted; this is the biggest above-fold failure
- **No loyalty / rewards hook anywhere visible** — GNC has a rewards program, but there's zero mention of it above the fold; for a returning buyer like me who tracks points for Auto-Deliver discounts, this is a missed retention signal
- **Hero is invisible** — I can't see the headline, the product being featured, or the primary seasonal campaign; the cookie modal ate it
- **No urgency cue on the BOGO** — "Buy 1, Get 1 50% Off!" doesn't say when it ends; a "Today Only" or countdown timer would move me faster
- **Cart is empty-looking** — no indication of how many items I have, no loyalty balance, nothing that signals GNC knows I've shopped here before

## Recommendations

- **Compress or defer the cookie banner** — show it as a bottom-sheet toast instead of a full-page modal; the hero should be visible the instant the page loads, not buried under legal boilerplate
- **Add a rewards balance chip in the top nav** (e.g., "You have 320 pts" next to the account icon) — high-retention supplement buyers are loyalty-program power users; surface that hook before I decide to shop Amazon instead
- **Add an expiry timestamp to the BOGO banner** — swap "Buy 1, Get 1 50% Off!" for "Buy 1, Get 1 50% Off — Ends Tonight" to create urgency without changing the deal
- **Add a "Best Sellers in Protein" horizontal scroll rail** immediately below the category shortcuts — I want to see Gold Standard, Dymatize, and their house brand at a glance without tapping into a category page first
