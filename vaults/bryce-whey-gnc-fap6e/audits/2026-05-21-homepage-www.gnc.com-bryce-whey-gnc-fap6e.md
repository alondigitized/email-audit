# Homepage snapshot · www.gnc.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Alright, GNC is leading with LIT AF V2 — a pre-workout drop — which is honestly a solid opener for someone like me. The BOGO 50% off banner hits immediately, which I respect. But the whole above-the-fold experience gets wrecked by a cookie consent modal sitting right on top of the hero, so I can barely read the product pitch before I'm being asked to click OK.

## What's working

- **"Buy 1, Get 1 50% Off!" banner** — appears in the sticky top strip AND the carousel, so it's the first thing my eye lands on. As someone who stocks up on protein and pre-workout in bulk, this is exactly the kind of deal that makes me open my wallet.
- **LIT AF V2 hero** — Beyond Raw is a brand I know and respect. Introducing a V2 feels like news. "Introducing" headline signals this is something fresh, not clearance.
- **PROTEIN tab visible in the category strip** — one tap and I'm in my category. That's the right shortcut to have above the fold.
- **Search bar front-and-center** — I'm a label reader. I'll search for specifics. Having "What can we help you find today?" right up top is useful.
- **"LIT AF" copy register** — that's pure gym-speak. It fits. I'm not being talked at by a wellness blogger.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal blocking the hero** — the modal is sitting right over the product copy, covering the "A PRE-WORKOUT..." sub-headline. First impression is friction, not product.
- **No CTA button visible above the fold** — I see a BOGO banner and a product image but no button to actually act on it. The hero has zero tappable call to action visible.
- **No human imagery** — just the product can. As a gym rat, I respond to athletes in action. This feels more like a warehouse product listing than an aspirational brand moment.
- **No loyalty hook** — I'd expect GNC Pro Access points or a "member price" badge. That's a huge retention lever that's missing entirely above the fold.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — I need to know if my BOGO order qualifies for free shipping before I commit.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the modal or shrink it to a banner** — move the cookie notice to a slim bottom bar so the hero is fully readable on load. Losing the product pitch to a compliance notice is a conversion killer.
- **Add a CTA button to the LIT AF V2 hero** — "Shop the Drop" or "Get BOGO Now" with a visible button. The hero is dead real estate without one.
- **Inject a lifestyle shot** — swap the product-can-only hero for a guy in the gym mid-lift with the product in frame. I need to see myself in this.
- **Surface GNC Pro Access above the fold** — a small "Member Price" badge on the BOGO or a "Earn points on every order" line next to the offer would add a retention hook for regulars like me.
