# Homepage snapshot · www.gnc.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- GNC is pushing a summer BOGO sale hard — the "Buy 1, Get 1 50% Off" message hits me literally three times before I even scroll. As a supplement stack guy, GNC is obviously my playground, but this homepage has a massive cookie consent modal eating half the screen before I can see anything useful. It looks like a brand that knows its audience but is getting in its own way with friction.

## What's working

- **"Buy 1, Get 1 50% Off!" top strip and carousel banner** — showing me a real dollar-value deal immediately. BOGO on protein or pre-workout is genuinely useful; I'm doing mental math already.
- **Creatine and Protein quick-category pills** — these are sitting right below the search bar. One tap to my core supplements without digging through nav. That's how it should work.
- **"GET READY FOR SUMMER SALE" hero framing** — summer cut season is live for me right now. The timing lands.
- **Search bar placement** — front and center above everything. I read ingredient labels; I search specific products. This respects how I shop.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal is the first interactive element I see** — it's covering the hero and any CTA. I have to dismiss it before I can do anything. On mobile this is brutal; it fills the screen.
- **No loyalty hook visible** — GNC has a Pro Access membership and a rewards program. None of that is surfaced. As someone on auto-deliver, I expect to see my status or points balance the moment I land.
- **Hero image is a red/orange graphic with text — no product, no person** — "GET READY FOR SUMMER SALE" is just giant type on a background. No protein tub, no athlete, no lifestyle shot. Missed opportunity to make me feel seen.
- **BOGO asterisk with no visible terms** — the fine print is hidden behind the modal. I don't know if this applies to my protein brand or is limited to GNC house brands. That friction costs trust.
- **"Ask a GNC Expert in Arcadia"** chat widget at the bottom — I'm shopping online to avoid talking to someone. This widget feels like a store locator dressed up as a chat tool.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the full-screen cookie modal on mobile** — replace with a slim bottom banner. The current implementation buries your hero and kills conversion intent before it starts.
- **Add a returning-member strip** — show rewards points or Pro Access tier right in the nav or below the search bar. Something like "You have 850 points — that's $8 off your next order." I'm already a customer; act like it.
- **Swap the all-text hero for a lifestyle shot** — put a jacked guy mid-workout with a protein tub or pre-workout in hand. "Get Ready for Summer" copy over that image hits harder than red block letters.
- **Scope the BOGO offer clearly** — one line below the promo banner saying "Applies to all sports nutrition" or "excludes GNC1 brands" would eliminate hesitation and make me tap faster.
