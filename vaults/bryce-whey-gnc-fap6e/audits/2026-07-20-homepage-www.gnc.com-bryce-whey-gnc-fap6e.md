# Homepage snapshot · www.gnc.com · 2026-07-20
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-20**
## Executive summary

- GNC is immediately recognizable as a legit supplements destination — the BOGO 50% Off banner hits you before anything else, and "PROTEIN" is literally the first category chip I see under search. That's all me. But a full-screen cookie consent modal has carpet-bombed the hero, so whatever deal or product they were pushing is completely invisible. As a guy who opens this app ready to stack promos, getting blocked by a cookie wall before I can even see what's on sale is annoying.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% Off banner** — front and center in the sticky header strip, impossible to miss. That's the kind of offer that keeps me in the app.
- **PROTEIN quick-nav chip** — first pill under the search bar. One tap to my category. Whoever ordered those chips knew their audience.
- **Search bar placement** — right below the nav, wide and prominent. When I know exactly what I want (ISO100 chocolate peanut butter, let's be real), I'm typing, not browsing.
- **Partial hero imagery** — I can see an athlete in athletic wear holding something dark. The visual language is right, even if the modal is eating it alive.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal blocks the entire hero** — I literally cannot see the primary offer, the hero copy, or any CTA button. Whatever campaign they're running is invisible. First impression = friction.
- **No loyalty/rewards callout** — GNC Pro Access exists. I'm the exact person who would have it. Seeing my points balance or a "Members save extra" badge would hook me instantly. Nothing.
- **"LIVE WELL" brand line is too soft** — that's spa energy. I'm here for gains. Doesn't match my register at all.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — I always check this before I load up my cart. It's not anywhere I can see.
- **Hero copy completely hidden** — "ONLY" is the only word I can read on the hero. Could be an incredible deal. I'll never know without dismissing the modal first.

## Recommendations

- **Trigger the cookie banner on scroll, not on load** — or shrink it to a bottom bar. A full-screen overlay destroying your hero on first paint is a conversion killer. Move it below the fold or delay it 3 seconds.
- **Add a Pro Access / loyalty badge to the top strip** — put it right next to the BOGO offer: "Members get extra 10% on top." One line. Massive engagement lift for repeat customers like me.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold in the promo bar** — "BOGO 50% Off + Free shipping over $49" in that same top strip. Two reasons to keep going. Right now there's one.
- **Rewrite "LIVE WELL" for the fitness-first audience** — something like "Fuel Your Stack" or "Built for Serious Training" on the hero reads way harder for a gym rat persona and still fits the brand.
