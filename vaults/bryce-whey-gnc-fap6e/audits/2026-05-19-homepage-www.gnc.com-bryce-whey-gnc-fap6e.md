# Homepage snapshot · www.gnc.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- GNC knows exactly what they sell — protein, vitamins, and supplements — and the BOGO 50% off deal front and center is legitimately the kind of thing that makes me stop scrolling. But a massive cookie consent wall swallowing the lower half of my screen on first load is a kill-joy, and outside of the promo banner and two quick-category buttons, there's almost nothing above the fold that speaks to me as a guy who cares about his stack, his goals, or his loyalty status.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% promo bar (top):** Shown in the blue sticky header AND the hero carousel AND the hero copy — impossible to miss. As someone who buys protein in bulk, this deal is legitimately compelling.
- **"PROTEIN" and "MULTIVITAMINS" quick-nav buttons:** Two taps to my category. Clean, labeled, directly above the hero. That's what I need.
- **"Get Ready For Summer" campaign:** Seasonally correct for late May — feels current, not like I'm looking at a sale from three months ago.
- **Search bar prominence:** "What can we help you find today?" is front and center. I know exactly what I want to search. That's a fast path to cart.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal swallowing the hero:** The entire lower 40% of my screen is a gray-text legal wall with an "OK" button. The hero image, the product shot, and likely the primary CTA are all blocked. First impression: annoying friction.
- **Zero loyalty or member hook visible:** I'm a repeat Auto-Deliver customer. Nothing on this screen acknowledges that — no rewards points balance, no member-price badge, no "Your GNC Pro Access" callout. I feel like a stranger.
- **No gym/athlete imagery:** The hero is a red sale graphic. That's fine for urgency but it doesn't show me a guy in the gym, a macro-split, a training session — nothing that says "this is for people like me."
- **"Buy 1, Get 1" with no product specifics:** The offer is screaming but for WHAT? Whey? Pre-workout? Creatine? A BOGO on vitamins doesn't move me the same way a BOGO on my protein stack does. No product context above the fold.
- **No urgency signal:** BOGO is shown but no countdown, no "ends Sunday," no "limited to X units." It feels like it'll just... always be there.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the full-page cookie modal on first load** — or shrink it to a dismissible bottom bar so the hero and CTA aren't buried. This is costing conversions before users even see the product.
- **Swap the red sale graphic hero for a lifestyle shot** — a guy mid-lift with a protein shaker and overlay copy like "Stack More. Pay Less." would land way harder for my demographic than a generic SALE sunburst.
- **Add a returning-shopper loyalty strip above the quick-nav buttons** — something like "GNC Rewards Member? Your points + BOGO = MAX savings" turns a generic promo into a loyalty hook for repeat buyers like me.
- **Make the BOGO specific to top protein SKUs** — show the actual product (Gold Standard, ISO-100, whatever's moving) and add "WHEY • PRE • BCAA" eligible labels. Turn a vague deal into a targeted one.
