# Homepage snapshot · www.quince.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- This is Quince doing exactly what Quince does — clean, minimal, unpretentious. The homepage skews dressy and occasion-ready right now with an "RSVP ready" hero, which feels like a late-spring wedding-season push. For me, a cashmere girl who's basically here to check if there's a new Mongolian knit collaboration, the vibe is pleasant but the hook is off.

## What's working

- **"$50 Cashmere" nav tab** — that's literally my category and it's the very first nav item. I see it and I immediately want to tap. Smart placement.
- **Shipping + returns strip ("Free shipping & easy returns for 365 days")** — I return aggressively when sizing drifts, so seeing this above the fold before I've even thought about buying is exactly the right move to lower my friction.
- **App push banner** — "Exclusive drops & more on the app / Luxury essentials, honestly priced" is on-brand and low-pressure; the "GET" CTA is understated. This is how you ask me to download without annoying me.
- **Category bubbles (Dresses, Bedding, Sweaters, Tops)** — Sweaters is in there. I can see it immediately and it's one tap. That matters.
- **Clean, editorial photography** — the hero model and the overall neutral palette match the aesthetic I associate with the brand. No garish sale graphics.

## What's weak

- **"RSVP ready" hero** — I'm here for cashmere, not event dressing. The polka-dot midi dress is cute but it has nothing to say to me. The hero is doing wedding-season outreach and I am not that customer right now.
- **No loyalty or membership hook** — I've spent real money here. There's no "welcome back," no points balance, no member-pricing badge. I feel like a stranger every time I land.
- **No price anchoring in the hero** — Quince's whole thesis is factory-direct pricing. The hero could say "from $50" or "Mongolian cashmere, $50" and I'd click faster. Right now the only pricing signal is in the tiny nav tab.
- **Hero is cropped / carousel is cut off** — There's clearly a second slide partially visible on the right ("F..." and an orange circle), but there's no visible swipe indicator or dot pagination. I might not know to swipe.
- **No new-arrivals callout above the fold** — I read every email for new collabs. There's nothing here teasing what dropped recently. "New Arrivals" is in the nav but it's passive — a new-drop banner would pull me in.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero creative to a cashmere or knitwear shot with the $50 price anchored in the copy** — even a simple "Mongolian Cashmere. $50." overlay on a cozy lifestyle image converts my persona on the spot.
- **Add a "New this week" horizontal scroll rail immediately below the category bubbles** — I'm a new-arrivals chaser; surface those items before I have to navigate anywhere.
- **Surface a returning-shopper hook** — even a generic "Recently viewed" or "Back in your size" nudge would make the homepage feel personalized and cut the time to purchase.
- **Add carousel navigation dots or a visible swipe affordance** — that cropped second slide is invisible to anyone not already swiping. Dot indicators cost nothing and recover a full content module.
