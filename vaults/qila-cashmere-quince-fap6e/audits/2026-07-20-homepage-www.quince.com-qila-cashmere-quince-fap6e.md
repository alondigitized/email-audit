# Homepage snapshot · www.quince.com · 2026-07-20
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-20**
## Executive summary

- Quince's homepage is doing what it does best — looking expensive without trying too hard. "The Cool Summer Edit styled by Nicole Chavez" reads like a quiet flex rather than a sale, and the $50 Cashmere tab in the nav is exactly the kind of honest anchor that made me swap out half my closet for this brand. Right now the site is pushing a summer editorial collab and category browsing over any hard discount, which fits the brand's whole ethos.

## What's working

- **"$50 Cashmere" as the very first nav tab** — this is the brand's founding promise, and foregrounding it is the right move for anyone who knows what Mongolian cashmere costs everywhere else
- **The Nicole Chavez collab editorial** — "styled by" collabs in a quiet-luxury frame feel aspirational without being loud; I'd tap that card
- **"Free shipping & easy returns for 365 days" persistent strip** — as someone who returns aggressively when sizing drifts, 365 days is the kind of policy that keeps me buying with zero hesitation
- **Category circle grid** — Dresses, Tops, Sweaters, Skirts, Jewelry all visible; I can get to what I want in one tap from here
- **Three-panel editorial hero** — actual humans in actual clothes, not product-on-white. This is how I browse

## What's weak

- **No returning-customer hook whatsoever** — my wishlist is longer than my closet and I can't see if I have anything saved; no "recently viewed," no rewards nudge, nothing that says "welcome back, Qila"
- **Hero CTA is microscopic** — the button on the "Cool Summer Edit" card is so small it barely reads as tappable on mobile; I almost missed it existed
- **No reviews or social proof above the fold** — Quince has obsessive fans but you'd never know it from this screen; not a single rating or badge
- **Sweaters visible in categories but no temperature/context cue** — in July, sweaters feel seasonally odd unless framed as "transition" or "travel knits"; it just sits there unexplained
- **App banner eats prime real estate** — the "Exclusive drops & more on the app / GET" banner at the very top is aggressive as the first thing I see; I'm already on the site, push the value proposition here, not to another platform

## Recommendations

- **Pin a wishlist count bubble to the heart icon in the nav** — even "3 items saved" is enough to pull a returning shopper back into the funnel without any new engineering
- **Enlarge and clarify the hero CTA** — swap the tiny button for a full-width pill button reading "Shop the Edit" so it's actually tappable on mobile with one thumb
- **Add a "Fan favorites" or "4.8★ | 12,000 reviews" trust badge strip between the hero and category grid** — one horizontal text bar would do it; it costs no space and removes the biggest objection for first-time visitors Qila is about to recommend the brand to
- **Reframe the Sweaters category tile for summer** — caption it "Pack Light Knits" or "Carry-On Cashmere" so it doesn't read as a seasonal mismatch in mid-July
