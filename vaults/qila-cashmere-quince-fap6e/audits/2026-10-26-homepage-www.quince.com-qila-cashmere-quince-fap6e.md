# Homepage snapshot · www.quince.com · 2026-10-26
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- Quince is my closet, so I came in warm, and the first thing I got was a gate: a full-screen "$40 OFF YOUR ORDER" email popup sitting on top of the page. Behind it I can see a seasonal "The Cold Weather Shop" hero with a model in a black puffer, plus a nav that leads with "$50 Cashmere", which is exactly why I shop here. The brand looks confident and quiet-luxury-adjacent. Right now it's pushing email capture first and outerwear second.

## What's working

- **"$50 Cashmere" as the first nav item.** It speaks straight to my cashmere habit and puts my category one tap away.
- **Top bar: "Free shipping & easy returns for 365 days."** I return aggressively when sizing drifts, so a year of returns is the line that reassures me most.
- **Category circles (Sweaters, Cashmere, Tops, Dresses, Outerwear, Bedding, Pants).** They're clean, calm and easy to scan. The blue cardigan in the Cashmere circle is the kind of thing I'd tap.
- **"The Cold Weather Shop" hero.** It's seasonally right. The "Mongolian cashmere" line shows they know their audience.
- **The popup's layout.** Two fields and one big button is about as low-friction as these things get.

## What's weak

- **The email popup covers everything on first load.** It hides the hero, half the nav (Best Sellers, Women, Men, Home and more are dimmed or cut off) and the categories. I haven't seen the brand yet and I'm already being asked for my email.
- **The "$40 off" is a bait-and-switch.** It needs $250 or more, it's app-only, and I'm giving my email on a web page to get it. I'd read that as exclusionary.
- **"GENDER" is a required-looking dropdown before I've seen anything.** It feels like data harvesting, not personalization.
- **"CONTINUE" is vague.** It doesn't say what happens next (do I get a code? an app link?).
- **The hero CTA is "SHOP PUFFERS", not cashmere.** The $50 cashmere story is in the nav but not in the hero I can see.
- **No returning-customer acknowledgment.** I've bought a lot here, and the popup treats me like a stranger.

## Recommendations

- **Delay the popup.** Trigger it after about 30 seconds or on exit intent, and use a slim bottom banner on first load so the hero and "$50 Cashmere" are visible immediately.
- **Put the real terms in the headline.** Change it to "$40 off orders $250+ in the app" with a clear "Get the app" button, and drop the email and gender fields for anyone who's already signed in.
- **Add a cashmere hero tile or second CTA** next to "Shop Puffers", for example "Shop $50 Mongolian Cashmere", so the best-known product gets above-the-fold space.
- **Add a returning-shopper strip** for logged-in users ("Welcome back, recently viewed, your wishlist"). It also gives people like me a reason to sign in.
