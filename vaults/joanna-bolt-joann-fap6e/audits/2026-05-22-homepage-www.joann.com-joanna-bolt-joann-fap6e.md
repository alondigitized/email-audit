# Homepage snapshot · www.joann.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- I typed in joann.com and somehow I'm looking at a **Michaels** page — that's the first thing that jumped out at me, and honestly it stopped me cold. Whether there's been a redirect, an acquisition, or something else going on behind the scenes, I don't know, but the logo says Michaels and that's what I'm reviewing. On top of that, a cookie consent wall is sitting right in the middle of the screen blocking whatever the actual hero content is — so most of what I can evaluate is a promo strip, a nav bar, and a "Got it" button.

## What's working

- **The promo code is front and center.** "Code SPRING30" is spelled out in the banner. I can't tell you how many times I've gotten a "Save 30%!" email with no code attached — this is the right call.
- **"Buy Again" in the secondary nav** is a smart returning-shopper hook. I buy the same Kona Cotton colorways repeatedly; one-tap reorder matters.
- **"Savings" as a dedicated nav tab** — that's speaking my language. I'm going to tap that before anything else.
- **"30% Off All Regular Price Purchases"** is a broad, usable offer — not a narrowly scoped "selected items" game.

## What's weak

- **The cookie consent overlay blocks the entire hero.** I can't see what the store is actually pushing right now — all that real estate is wasted. This is the first thing I see and it's a wall.
- **This appears to be Michaels, not JOANN.** If I typed joann.com and landed here without explanation, I'm confused and probably hitting back.
- **No fabric, quilting, or sewing category is visible** in any exposed nav element. For someone who came here to plan a quilt project, the page gives me nothing to grab onto.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible.** I always calculate whether to add a yard of interfacing to hit free shipping — can't do that math if the number isn't on screen.
- **No loyalty/rewards mention above the fold.** Michaels has a rewards program; JOANN had one too. Either way, that's the first thing I want to see.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the cookie modal automatically after 3 seconds or move it to a slim bottom bar** — a full-screen overlay on mobile kills the first impression and buries the hero entirely.
- **Add a "Fabric & Quilting" shortcut to the exposed secondary nav** alongside Shop/Services/Savings — one tap to my category, no digging.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold in the promo banner** alongside the 30% code — "30% off with SPRING30 + free shipping over $49" is a stronger hook than the code alone.
- **If joann.com now resolves to Michaels, add a one-line acknowledgment banner** ("We're now part of Michaels — your account and purchase history are here") so returning JOANN shoppers don't feel lost or misled.
