# Homepage snapshot · www.joann.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- Hold on — I typed joann.com into my phone and I'm looking at a **Michaels** homepage. Red cursive Michaels logo, their nav, their cookie banner. That's either a redirect glitch or something went sideways with the browser. Whatever the cause, as a shopper I'm immediately disoriented — I came for fabric by the bolt and I'm staring at a competitor's craft store. What IS visible is a 20% off coupon-code banner and a giant cookie consent modal eating the entire screen, so even if this were the right site, I can barely see the actual store.

## What's working

- **The promo banner code** — "MAY20NOW" is right there in plain text, no hunting required. I'm a coupon stacker; I noticed it immediately and mentally filed it.
- **Seasonally current** — the hero image peeks through with what looks like a patriotic color palette, so at least it's not showing a Christmas campaign in May.
- **"Buy Again" tab in nav** — that's a returning-customer hook I actually want. If I've ordered fabric before, quick reorder matters.
- **Search bar is prominent** — front and center, which is how I shop a big craft store. I know what I want; let me type it.

## What's weak

- **Wrong website** — I came for JOANN; the logo says Michaels. That's disqualifying before a single product loads.
- **Cookie modal as first experience** — the consent wall covers the entire viewport. I have to make a legal decision before I can see a single product, price, or category. Hostile first impression.
- **"Exclusions apply" on the promo** — no detail on what's excluded. On a crafting site, that usually means patterns, Cricut machines, and half the things I actually came to buy. Frustrating without a link to the exclusions list.
- **No fabric or sewing category visible** — zero quilting, fabric, or notions signals anywhere above the fold. Michaels skews more toward paper crafts and kids' kits. I don't see my department.
- **No loyalty/rewards callout** — Michaels has a rewards program but it's nowhere above the fold. I want to know my balance before I shop.

## Recommendations

- **Fix the redirect / URL issue** — if typing joann.com lands shoppers on Michaels, that's a broken navigation experience that needs to be corrected at the DNS or redirect layer immediately.
- **Dismiss the cookie modal or make it collapsible** — replace the full-screen modal with a slim bottom banner so the homepage content is visible on arrival. The "Got it" button is big enough to find without blocking everything.
- **Add exclusions detail inline** — link "Exclusions apply" to a plain-language list right in the banner. Coupon-savvy shoppers like me will lose trust if the exclusions gut the deal at checkout.
- **Surface a fabric/quilting category tile above the fold** — if this were JOANN, a single "Fabric & Quilting" image tile in the first scroll zone would immediately signal I'm in the right place.
