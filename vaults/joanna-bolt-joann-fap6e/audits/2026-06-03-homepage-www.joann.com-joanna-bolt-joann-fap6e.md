# Homepage snapshot · www.joann.com · 2026-06-03
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-03**
## Executive summary

- Hold on — I typed joann.com into my browser and what loaded is **Michaels**. I can see the red script Michaels logo plain as day at the top of my screen. Right out of the gate the whole page is buried under a cookie consent wall, so I can barely see what this campaign is even about. What I *can* read is a promo strip for 25% off with code EXTRA25 — and a navigation bar that at least includes a "Buy Again" tab, which tells me they know returning shoppers exist.

## What's working

- **The 25% off promo strip** — code EXTRA25 is right there, readable, with "Exclusions apply" out in the open. As someone who lives and dies by coupon codes, I appreciate that I don't have to hunt for it.
- **"Buy Again" in the sub-nav** — that one tab tells me the site recognizes repeat customers. I could theoretically jump to my past orders in one tap.
- **Clean nav labels** — "Shop / Services / Savings / Buy Again" is straightforward; no cutesy jargon that makes me squint.
- **Search bar is prominent** — big, centered, white bar just below the logo. If I know what I want (and I usually do), I can type "cotton quilting fabric" without any fuss.

## What's weak

- **The cookie modal is the entire above-the-fold experience.** I came here to shop for fabric, not read a paragraph about analytics partners. The "Got it" button is at the very bottom of a wall of text. This is the first thing I see and it makes me want to close the tab.
- **No visible sewing or quilting content.** Not a single word about fabric, thread, or patterns is visible before I dismiss the consent wall.
- **No promo code stacking info.** 25% off is nice, but as a coupon stacker I immediately want to know: can I combine this with my military discount? Nothing on screen answers that.
- **No loyalty program mention.** Michaels has a rewards program; there's no badge, balance, or "members earn X" hook anywhere I can see.
- **The offer feels floating.** The promo strip says EXTRA25 but there's no deadline, no "offer ends Sunday," nothing to tell me when this expires or if it's ongoing.

## Recommendations

- **Shrink the cookie banner to a bottom-of-screen strip** — one line plus "Accept" and "Manage," not a full-page overlay. Let me actually see the homepage.
- **Add "stacks with military discount? YES/NO" to the promo banner.** One sentence, right next to "Exclusions apply." Coupon-stackers want this upfront; right now I'd have to dig for it.
- **Surface a rewards-points balance or "sign in to see member price" badge** next to the promo strip. The "Buy Again" tab implies I have an account; show me what that account is worth.
- **Add a deadline to the promo copy** — even "Online only, this week" is better than nothing. Time-boxing a code makes me act instead of saving the tab for later.
