# Homepage snapshot · www.joann.com · 2026-09-30
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- I typed joann.com and landed on Michaels. The big banner says "Welcome to Michaels, JOANN fans!", so my store is gone and they want my business. They're pushing 30% off with code GETMY30 and a "Knit & Sew Shop" built for people like me. It's a decent landing, but it's a little sad. I wish the page did more to show me they understand the coupon-stacking I've done for years.

## What's working

- **"Welcome to Michaels, JOANN fans! It's hard to lose a store you love."** It's a human acknowledgment that feels honest. I'm a little grumpy about losing JOANN, and this doesn't dodge that.
- **The promo strip shows the actual code, "GETMY30".** That is the thing I'm usually annoyed about when emails leave it out. I can copy it without hunting.
- **The "Knit & Sew Shop" section with "Your go-to for all things sewing & stitching."** It's in the nav and again as a big block. The fabric stack photo is the first thing I'd tap.
- **Fabric, Yarn, and Sewing Notions & Supplies cards.** They're big, bright, and match what I buy by the bolt.
- **The nav has "Savings" and "Buy Again."** I'd check Savings first to see where my coupon stack can go.
- **The JOANN × Michaels logo lockup over the gingham and calico fabrics.** It eases the transition and tells me they know who I am.

## What's weak

- **No visible button on the hero or promo strip.** There's nothing like "Shop Fabric" to tap. I have to work out that the cards below are the next step.
- **"Exclusions apply" is vague.** Fabric by the bolt is exactly the kind of thing that gets excluded. I need to know before I load a cart.
- **No mention of a military discount or rewards program.** I stack coupon, sale, and military discount, so I'm left wondering if this code even combines with anything.
- **The cookie box covers the fourth category card.** Its title is cut off and I can't tell what it is. The Feedback tab on the right edge adds more clutter.
- **No free-shipping threshold.** I buy in volume and need to know where shipping kicks in.
- **No reviews, badges, or returns info above the fold.** Hobby Lobby's basics are cheaper, so I'd want a reason to trust Michaels.

## Recommendations

- Add a visible button, "Shop Fabric by the Yard" or "Shop the Knit & Sew Shop," inside the red "Welcome to Michaels, JOANN fans!" block.
- Put "GETMY30" on a tap-to-copy chip and add a line under the promo strip, such as "Fabric, yarn & notions included. See exclusions." That way I can see what qualifies.
- Add a small strip under the hero with the free-shipping threshold, the rewards program, and any military or teacher discount. Say whether they stack with GETMY30.
- Move the cookie notice into a thin bottom bar so it doesn't cover the category cards.
