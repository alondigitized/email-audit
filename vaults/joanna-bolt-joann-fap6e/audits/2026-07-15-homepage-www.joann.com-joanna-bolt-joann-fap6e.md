# Homepage snapshot · www.joann.com · 2026-07-15
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-15**
## Executive summary

- Okay, first thing — this is Michaels, not JOANN. Either my browser redirected me or something went sideways, because the logo up top says "Michaels" plain as day. On top of that, a giant cookie consent wall has eaten the entire screen, so I can barely see anything behind it. What little I can see is a red promo banner promising 30% off with code SUMMER30 — which, fine, that's actually my language — but I can't get to anything because this modal is sitting on top of everything.

## What's working

- **SUMMER30 promo banner** — the red bar at the top with "30% Off All Regular Price Purchases Online only with code SUMMER30" is exactly the kind of thing I scan for the second I land on a page. The code is right there, no hunting required.
- **Header navigation** — "Shop," "Services," "Savings," "Buy Again" are clean and readable even above the modal. "Buy Again" is a smart hook for a repeat buyer like me.
- **"Ask Mike" AI assistant** — visible in the header; I'm medium-tech and might actually tap this if I needed to find a specific fabric or coupon rule fast.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent wall dominates 90% of the viewport** — the entire shopping experience is blocked. I came to browse fabric and instead I'm reading a privacy policy essay. This is a conversion killer before I even see one product.
- **Wrong site entirely** — the logo says Michaels, not JOANN. If I specifically came here for JOANN-exclusive sales or the JOANN rewards program, I'm already confused and trust is damaged.
- **"Exclusions apply" with no detail** — I know from experience that exclusions on fabric-store 30%-off deals almost always knock out regular-priced fabric, name-brand notions, or Simplicity patterns. The fine print isn't visible at all, so I have no idea if this deal is even real for what I buy.
- **No loyalty or rewards mention above the fold** — I stack my JOANN/Michaels rewards carefully. There's nothing here signaling my points balance or member-only pricing.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the cookie banner by default or shrink it to a bottom strip** — a full-page modal before I see a single product is a bounce waiting to happen. Make it a slim bar at the bottom so the hero is immediately visible.
- **Add the promo code's exclusions summary inline** — even one line like "Excludes regular-priced fabric cuts" would build trust with a savvy coupon stacker like me; "Exclusions apply" alone feels like a gotcha.
- **Surface a loyalty/rewards balance prompt in the header** — something like "You have 450 points — redeem today" would keep me on the site and shopping instead of bouncing to check Hobby Lobby.
- **Verify the correct domain is serving the correct brand** — if this is a JOANN URL showing Michaels content, that's a technical issue that needs fixing before any other optimization matters.
