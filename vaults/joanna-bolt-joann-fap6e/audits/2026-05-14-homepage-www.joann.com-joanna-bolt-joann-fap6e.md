# Homepage snapshot · www.joann.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- I typed in JOANN and landed on **Michaels** — so right off the bat I'm confused and a little annoyed. Whether this is a redirect after JOANN's bankruptcy or some mix-up on my phone, what I actually see is the Michaels mobile homepage, almost entirely buried under a cookie consent wall. The one thing I can read clearly is a promo strip: 20% off with a code. Everything else is obscured.

## What's working

- **The 20% off promo strip** is persistent and legible at the top — red background, white bold text, code `SAVE20MAY` is right there. As someone who lives for the promo code, I at least clocked it before the modal hit.
- **Navigation tabs (Shop / Services / Savings / Buy Again)** are visible above the modal. "Buy Again" is actually a nice repeat-buyer hook — I would use that.
- **Search bar** is prominent and accessible above the modal fold — I could bypass the homepage noise and just search for "quilting cotton" immediately.

## What's weak

- **The JOANN redirect to Michaels** — if joann.com now lands here, there is zero acknowledgment of that transition. I came in looking for Joann fabric bolts and instead I'm on a competitor's site with no explanation. That's disorienting.
- **Cookie consent wall obliterates the homepage.** The entire value of the hero, featured categories, and any lifestyle imagery is blocked. I cannot see a single product or campaign.
- **No loyalty/rewards hook visible.** Michaels has a rewards program, but it's nowhere above the fold — lost opportunity for someone who stacks discounts.
- **"Exclusions apply" with no detail.** As a coupon-stacker, I immediately want to know: does this exclude fabric? Sale items? Cricut machines? That fine print is doing a lot of work for three words.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the cookie modal automatically after 3 seconds or shrink it to a bottom strip.** The current implementation kills the entire first impression — no one should see a wall of GDPR text as their homepage.
- **Add an inline "What's excluded?" tooltip or link directly on the promo bar.** Coupon-savvy shoppers like me will abandon if we can't quickly verify the discount applies to what we want.
- **Surface the rewards balance or "Sign In to See Member Pricing" hook in the top nav.** Right now there's nothing drawing me into the loyalty ecosystem above the fold.
- **If this IS a JOANN redirect, add a one-line acknowledgment:** "Welcome, JOANN shoppers — your favorites are here." That one sentence keeps me from bouncing immediately.
