# Homepage snapshot · www.joann.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- I clicked to go to JOANN and this is not JOANN — the logo at the top says *Michaels*, plain as day. Whether there was a redirect, a cached page, or something went sideways with my browser, what I'm actually looking at is the Michaels mobile homepage, almost entirely buried under a cookie consent modal. I can barely see anything above the fold that matters, which is already a bad start for any shopping trip.

## What's working

- **The 30% off banner with code BONUS30** is hard to miss — red background, bold white text, promo code spelled out clearly. As someone who coupon-stacks religiously, I appreciate that the code is right there, not buried three clicks in.
- **The nav bar ("Shop / Services / Savings / Buy Again")** is at least legible and compact for a mobile layout. "Savings" tab is exactly the kind of shortcut I'd use.
- **Search bar** is prominent and immediately usable if I knew what I wanted.

## What's weak

- **Wrong website.** The logo says Michaels. I asked for JOANN. This is a fundamental failure — whether it's a browser glitch or a misconfigured redirect, I'd be confused and suspicious.
- **Cookie modal blocks 80% of the viewport.** I can't see the hero, the featured categories, any seasonal content, or any products. It's the first thing I have to deal with, and it covers everything that would hook me.
- **"Exclusions apply" with no link.** On a site where I stack military, sale, and coupon discounts, vague exclusion language with no tap-through makes me nervous about wasting time filling a cart.
- **No fabric, quilting, sewing, or craft-specific category visible above the fold.** Even if this were JOANN, nothing above the cookie wall signals my shopping category.
- **No loyalty/rewards callout.** JOANN's rewards program is a core reason I shop there over Hobby Lobby — not seeing it immediately is a miss.

## Recommendations

- **Fix the brand/URL mismatch immediately.** If JOANN.com is serving Michaels content (or vice versa), that's a trust-destroying bug that needs to be resolved before anything else. A quilter who lands on the wrong store's homepage just leaves.
- **Reduce the cookie modal footprint.** Make it a slim banner at the bottom — not a full-screen takeover. Every second the modal is up is a second I'm not seeing a sale on Kona cotton.
- **Surface the promo code with fabric/sewing context.** The BONUS30 banner would land harder with a visual of a bolt of fabric or a quilt pattern — speak to me, not a generic shopper.
- **Add a loyalty/rewards balance hook above the fold.** A small "Your rewards: $X available" pill would make me feel seen and push me toward checkout faster than any generic offer.
