# Homepage snapshot · www.joann.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Wait — I opened www.joann.com and I'm looking at **Michaels**. That's the first thing I noticed, the red script logo and "MakerPlace" in the top nav — this is not JOANN. Beyond that navigation mixup, a massive cookie consent popup is eating the entire screen. I can barely see a sliver of whatever hero image they've got going, and there's a promo banner at the top with a coupon code — which is the only useful thing visible right now.

## What's working

- **The promo banner** — "30% Off All Regular Price Purchases" with the code `SPRING30` right at the top is the right instinct. That's where I look first. The code is printed in plain English, not buried.
- **"Buy Again" tab** — as someone who buys the same stabilizer and batting over and over, a quick-reorder shortcut in the primary nav is genuinely useful. JOANN doesn't surface that prominently.
- **Search bar is front and center** — I know what I want (Warm & White batting, 60" muslin, etc.), so a big open search field is exactly how I'd start.

## What's weak

- **Cookie popup blocks the entire page** — This is the whole problem. I literally cannot see the hero, any category tiles, any featured fabric, nothing. On a phone screen this modal takes up 80%+ of the viewport. I'm being asked to consent before I can see a single product.
- **Wrong brand** — The screenshot shows Michaels branding, not JOANN. If I came here from a JOANN link and landed on Michaels, that's a redirect or DNS problem that would make me close the tab immediately.
- **No fabric or quilting imagery visible** — Not a single bolt, not a thread, not a fat quarter. The sliver of hero I can see looks patriotic/red-white-blue but it's mostly obscured.
- **No loyalty/rewards hook** — I stack coupons + sale + military discount religiously. There's nothing telling me my reward points balance or a members-only offer.
- **"Exclusions apply"** — It's there in the banner. I've been burned by exclusions before (fabric is always excluded, naturally). No link to clarify what's excluded.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the cookie modal after 2 seconds or reduce it to a slim bottom bar** — a full-screen consent wall on mobile is a bounce trigger before a customer sees a single product.
- **Pin the promo code and make "Exclusions" a tappable link** — "SPRING30 — see what's excluded" right in the banner would head off my frustration before it starts.
- **Put a loyalty/rewards balance chip in the header** — even a simple "You have 240 points" next to the account icon would keep me from shopping Hobby Lobby instead.
- **Verify the domain redirect** — if www.joann.com is serving Michaels branding, that's a critical infrastructure issue that needs to be fixed before any UX work matters.
