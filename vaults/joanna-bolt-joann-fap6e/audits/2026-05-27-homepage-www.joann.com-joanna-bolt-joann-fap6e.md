# Homepage snapshot · www.joann.com · 2026-05-27
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-27**
## Executive summary

- I typed joann.com into my browser and ended up on **Michaels**. That's the first thing I noticed — the red Michaels logo at the top, not the JOANN scissors. Whether this is a redirect because JOANN folded or some kind of domain acquisition, I don't know, but I'd be confused and probably suspicious I got phished. The one thing I can make out is a promo banner with an actual coupon code, which is the kind of thing I always hunt for — but a full-screen cookie modal is sitting on top of everything else, so I genuinely cannot see what they're selling.

## What's working

- **The promo banner** — "20% Off All Regular Price Purchases — Online only with code HEATUP20" is front and center, pinned just below the nav. A code is printed right in the banner. That's what I need: tell me the code, don't make me dig for it.
- **"Buy Again" tab** in the nav strip — if my past purchases are actually in there, that's a one-tap shortcut to my repeat buys, which as a bolt-buyer I appreciate.
- **"Savings" tab** is a dedicated navigation item — signals the brand knows deal-hunters exist and built a lane for them.

## What's weak

- **Wrong brand entirely** — Joanna Bolt came to JOANN for fabric by the bolt and a quilting-specific experience. Landing on Michaels with zero explanation is disorienting. No banner, no copy, no redirect notice says "JOANN is now part of Michaels family" — nothing.
- **Full-screen cookie modal** — it's blocking the hero image and all category content. I can't see what's on sale, what fabrics are featured, nothing. The "Got It" button is big and red but the modal text is a wall of legal-speak that has nothing to do with why I showed up.
- **"Exclusions apply"** printed right on the promo banner — as someone who coupon-stacks, I know from painful experience that fabric by the yard is almost always an exclusion. This sentence makes me distrust the offer immediately.
- **No fabric or quilting signal anywhere visible** — Michaels sells craft supplies broadly, but nothing in the visible chrome (nav labels, banner copy, partial hero) says "quilting," "fabric," or "sewing."
- **No loyalty/rewards callout** — I carry a JOANN Rewards card (or did). Is any of that transferable? No mention.

## Recommendations

- **Add a transition banner** — one line at the very top: "JOANN is now part of Michaels. Your Rewards account transfers. Here's how." That single sentence would stop me from bouncing immediately.
- **Dismiss the cookie modal on scroll or after 3 seconds**, or at minimum make the hero image visible behind a semi-transparent overlay — the total blackout of above-the-fold content is unnecessary friction.
- **Put fabric/sewing as a visible category in the nav or hero** — if Michaels carries quilting fabric, say so above the fold. One tile that says "Fabric & Quilting" would tell me I'm in the right place.
- **Spell out exclusions for the promo** — link "Exclusions apply" to a short list right there, or clarify "including fabric yardage" vs. not. Don't make me find out at checkout.
