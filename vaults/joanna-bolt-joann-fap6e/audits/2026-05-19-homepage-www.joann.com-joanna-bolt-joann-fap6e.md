# Homepage snapshot · www.joann.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- I typed joann.com and landed on... Michaels? That's disorienting right out of the gate — looks like the domain is redirecting to Michaels now, which tells me JOANN's doors are really shut. What little I can see underneath a giant cookie-consent modal is the Michaels homepage: a 20% off promo code banner, a nav row with Shop / Services / Savings / Buy Again, and a half-visible seasonal image. This is a craft store, but it's not the one I came here for.

## What's working

- **20% off promo code (MAY20NOW):** A named coupon code is exactly how my brain works — I can stack it mentally with my military discount before I've even seen a product
- **Promo banner placement:** Red strip right below the nav, hard to miss, legible on mobile
- **Search bar:** Prominent, full-width, accessible — easy to pivot to a specific search if I know what I need
- **"Savings" nav tab:** That word earns a tap from me; I'm already curious what's in there

## What's weak

- **Michaels redirect with no explanation:** I typed joann.com. I did not type michaels.com. There's no banner, no message, nothing explaining that JOANN inventory now lives here. That's a customer-service failure for every loyal JOANN shopper landing here today.
- **Cookie consent modal covers the entire hero:** The first thing I interact with is a legal wall, not product. The modal copy is dense and the "Got it" button is buried at the bottom — that's friction before I've seen a single yard of fabric.
- **No quilting or sewing signal visible:** "Custom Framing," "MakerPlace," "Digital Downloads" in the top nav — none of those are why I came. Fabric, notions, patterns, batting — invisible.
- **Exclusions apply (no detail):** That phrase on the 20% banner immediately makes me suspicious. Which exclusions? Fabric? Cutting fees? I've been burned by this before and I need to know before I plan a haul.

## Recommendations

- **Add a JOANN transition banner immediately:** A one-line strip above the promo — "Welcome, JOANN shoppers — your fabric & sewing supplies are now at Michaels" — would prevent the brand confusion that's about to drive away every loyal JOANN customer.
- **Dismiss or shrink the cookie modal:** On mobile especially, replace the full-screen takeover with a slim bottom bar — show me product first, legal second.
- **Surface a "Fabric & Sewing" category tile in the hero or immediately below the promo:** A quilter landing here needs to see their category within two seconds or they're gone. "MakerPlace" and "Digital Downloads" don't fill that gap.
- **Spell out coupon exclusions inline:** Even "excl. Cricut, sewing machines, and custom framing" gives me enough to plan. Vague exclusions erode trust.
