# Homepage snapshot · www.joann.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- I typed joann.com into my phone and ended up on a **Michaels** page — that's the first thing I notice, and it's a real head-scratcher. Before I can even register what Michaels is selling, a full-screen cookie consent wall drops on top of everything. I can just barely make out a red promo strip at the top promising 30% off with a code, but the rest of the homepage is buried under a giant "Got it" button. Whatever Michaels is trying to tell me, it's not getting through.

## What's working

- **The 30% off promo strip with code SPRING30** sits right at the top, above even the cookie modal on its z-layer — I can read it instantly, code and all. That's exactly how it should work: promo code front and center, no digging required.
- **"Buy Again" in the secondary nav** is a small but smart returning-shopper signal. I know my staples — 44"-wide quilting cotton, batting — and a quick reorder path matters to me.
- **Promo language is plain** — "30% Off All Regular Price Purchases." No ambiguous "up to" language, no mystery discount tiers.

## What's weak

- **I'm on Michaels.com, not JOANN** — if www.joann.com is redirecting here, the landing page does nothing to acknowledge the transition. I'm confused about whether my JOANN rewards, my cut-fabric order history, any of that carries over.
- **The cookie consent modal covers the entire viewport** below the promo strip. The hero image, any featured categories, any sale artwork — all gone behind a text wall and a "Got it" button. This is the worst possible first impression on mobile.
- **No fabric, quilting, or sewing signal anywhere visible** — I'm a bolt-buyer and I can't tell if this store even sells what I need without dismissing two layers of UI first.
- **"Exclusions apply" with no detail visible** — that phrase makes me nervous as a coupon-stacker. Is apparel excluded? Fabric by the bolt? I can't tell from here.
- **No free-shipping threshold, no loyalty balance, no member pricing** — all the stuff I actually check before I decide whether to put something in the cart.

## Recommendations

- **Acknowledge the redirect** — if joann.com now points to Michaels, put a one-line banner at the very top: "JOANN is now Michaels — your account and rewards transferred." Without that, I'm wondering if I mis-typed and I'm about to waste my time.
- **Move the cookie consent to a slim bottom bar**, not a full-screen modal. The hero and top categories should be visible on load; a bottom-anchored "Accept / Manage" strip is the standard on mobile and doesn't kill engagement.
- **Make fabric/quilting a tappable category chip in the first screen** — something like "Quilting & Fabric" right below the nav so I know in one glance I'm in the right place.
- **Surface the exclusions** — even a short inline note ("excludes fabric by the bolt, Cricut machines") would stop coupon-stackers like me from wasting a trip through checkout only to find the thing I came for isn't covered.
