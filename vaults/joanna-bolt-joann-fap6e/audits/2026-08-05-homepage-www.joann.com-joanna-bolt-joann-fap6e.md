# Homepage snapshot · www.joann.com · 2026-08-05
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- I typed in joann.com and I'm looking at the Michaels logo. That's not a small glitch — that's the wrong store entirely. Underneath the mix-up there's a "20% Off All Regular Price Purchases" banner with a code, but a full-screen cookie notice is sitting on top of everything else, so that's really all I can see.

## What's working

- The "20% Off All Regular Price Purchases" banner is bold, red, and easy to read — if I trusted this was actually my Joann's page, I'd have noted the code MAKER20 right away.
- "Ask Mike" chat bubble and clean search bar up top look tidy and modern, whatever site this turned out to be.
- The cookie banner at least gives me a plain "Got it" button instead of forcing me to hunt through settings.

## What's weak

- I opened joann.com and got the **Michaels** logo, nav (Custom Framing, MakerPlace, Enterprise), and site. That's not my fabric store — that's the competitor I sometimes price-compare against for basics, not where I go to buy bolts of quilting cotton.
- The cookie consent popup swallows almost the entire screen the second the page loads, so I can't see any hero image, any product, or any category — just a wall of legal text.
- No sign of a coupon-stack policy, military discount, or rewards balance anywhere visible — and those are the first things I look for.
- "Exclusions apply" tucked right into the promo banner with no link to see what's excluded — that's exactly the kind of buried fine print that annoys me.

## Recommendations

- Fix whatever redirect or caching issue is serving Michaels content on the joann.com URL — that's a broken storefront, not a style note.
- Make the cookie modal a slim bottom bar instead of a near-full-screen takeover so the hero and offer are visible on first load.
- Surface the JoAnn Rewards or coupon-stacking info above the fold — that's the actual reason regulars like me come back.
- Put an "exclusions" link right in the promo banner so I don't have to dig for what's not included in that 20%.
