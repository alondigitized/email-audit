# Homepage snapshot · www.joann.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- I typed joann.com into my phone and what loaded is — wait, this is **Michaels**. The Michaels logo, Michaels nav, Michaels promo. That is either a very bad redirect or the wrong page got captured, and either way it is a problem. What I *can* see is a 30%-off promo banner and a giant cookie consent wall smothering everything below it, including whatever hero image is hiding underneath. If this is my JOANN experience, I'm already reaching for Google to find the right site.

## What's working

- **Promo banner copy** — "30% Off All Regular Price Purchases / Online only with code BONUS30" is in plain English on a high-contrast red strip. I can read it, I know exactly what to do with it, and as a coupon stacker I appreciate that the code is printed right there and not tucked in a drawer somewhere.
- **Secondary navigation tabs** — "Shop / Services / Savings / Buy Again" are visible and "Savings" would absolutely be my first tap. Having a dedicated savings tab is the right instinct for a value-driven shopper like me.
- **Acknowledgment of exclusions** — "Exclusions apply" is mentioned in the promo, which is honest. I hate landing on a coupon and discovering half the store is excluded at checkout.

## What's weak

- **Wrong website** — This is Michaels, not JOANN. The logo is red script "Michaels." If my phone was supposed to open joann.com and landed here, that is a catastrophic routing or capture failure. A quilter coming to buy fabric by the bolt is not a Michaels customer by default.
- **Cookie consent modal fills the entire viewport** — The overlay covers maybe 70% of the visible screen. Whatever hero is below it — I can only see a sliver of red/white/blue — is invisible. This is the first thing I see and it makes the page feel broken, not welcoming.
- **No fabric, sewing, or quilting signal visible** — Nothing in the nav strip or promo speaks to my specific shopping mission. "Custom Framing / MakerPlace / Digital Downloads" in the top bar skews general craft and maker, not the bolt-of-fabric quilter.
- **No loyalty hook** — I have a Michaels rewards account (I cross-shop there). A returning-customer hook or balance callout would make me feel recognized. Nothing here acknowledges I've ever been before.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — As someone buying heavy fabric by the bolt, shipping cost matters a lot. I shouldn't have to hunt for that number.

## Recommendations

- **Fix the page capture / redirect first** — If users searching for joann.com are landing on Michaels, that is a search-poisoning or misconfigured redirect issue that needs immediate investigation; no UX change matters until the right site loads.
- **Dismiss or minimize the cookie modal on scroll** — The full-screen consent wall is killing the first impression. Move it to a slim bottom banner so the hero and promo are visible immediately; GDPR compliance doesn't require covering the entire page on mobile.
- **Bring the promo code up even higher and add a category anchor** — "30% off fabric by the bolt" or "30% off quilting cotton" would speak directly to me; the generic "regular price purchases" is fine but a category callout would stop my thumb from moving to a competitor.
- **Add a "Savings" or "Weekly Ad" shortcut in the hero** — Instead of hiding deals inside a nav tab, put a persistent "See All Savings" chip in the hero so coupon stackers like me know exactly where to go without digging.
