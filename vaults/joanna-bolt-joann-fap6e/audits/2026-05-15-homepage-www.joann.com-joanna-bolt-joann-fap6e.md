# Homepage snapshot · www.joann.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- Now I'll write the review as Joanna Bolt based on what I actually see in the screenshot.
- Hold on — I opened www.joann.com and I'm looking at **Michaels**. The red cursive logo, the "Custom Framing" and "MakerPlace" tabs, the "Buy Again" nav — this is 100% Michaels.com, not JOANN. Whether it's a redirect, a capture glitch, or something else, what I'm seeing is my main cross-shop competitor's homepage. And the first thing that greeted me was a wall-to-wall cookie consent banner that blocks nearly every pixel of actual content. I can see a sliver of what looks like red-white-blue fabric in the hero — patriotic theme, probably Memorial Day — but I can't engage with anything until I click "Got it."

## What's working

- **Seasonal imagery peek** — I can just barely see a blue gingham and red-and-white stars print in the hero behind the banner. If that's a Memorial Day fabric push, the timing is right for me — I plan seasonal quilting projects and May is when I stock up on patriotic fabric.
- **"Savings" tab in the nav** — visible above the cookie banner in the bottom navigation strip. That word alone makes me want to tap it once this banner clears. For a coupon-stacker like me, a dedicated Savings tab is exactly the right hook.
- **"Buy Again" tab** — that's a returning-shopper feature I actually like. If my repeat purchases are surfaced there, that's useful. It's visible in the nav without scrolling.

## What's weak

- **Wrong website entirely** — I went to joann.com and landed on Michaels. That is a catastrophic UX failure at the entry point, whatever caused it. If JOANN is redirecting traffic to a competitor, someone has a serious problem.
- **Cookie consent banner blocks the entire viewport** — The banner is not a small bottom ribbon; it is a full-height overlay that renders the homepage functionally invisible. I cannot see the hero headline, the featured categories, any promo codes, or any fabric imagery. For a mobile user like me on a phone screen, this is an immediate bounce trigger.
- **No promo code visible** — I came here specifically because I expected to see a stackable coupon. The banner ate it. My biggest annoyance with craft store emails is forgetting the promo code — and this homepage makes it worse.
- **No loyalty/rewards callout visible** — I have no idea if I'm logged in, what my rewards balance is, or if there's a member discount active. None of that is accessible while the banner lives.

## Recommendations

- **Move the cookie banner to a slim bottom bar** — A full-height takeover on mobile is unacceptable. A 60px anchored ribbon at the bottom with "Got it" and a "Preferences" link preserves the entire hero experience. Ship this next week.
- **Surface the promo code in the nav bar above the fold** — Even if the hero is blocked, a sticky top bar with "Use code SAVE20 — ends Sunday" would reach me before I dismiss the cookie modal. I'm here to stack coupons; give me the code the moment I land.
- **Pin the active coupon and rewards balance to the header** — Once I'm logged in, show me my JOANN Rewards points balance right next to the cart icon. That single change would make me feel seen as a returning customer instead of a stranger.
- **Fix whatever is causing joann.com to display Michaels branding** — Seriously. Check the domain config, CDN routing, or screenshot capture pipeline. This should not happen.
