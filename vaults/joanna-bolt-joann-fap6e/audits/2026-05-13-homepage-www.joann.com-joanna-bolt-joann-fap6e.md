# Homepage snapshot · www.joann.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- I typed joann.com and what loaded is Michaels — and then a cookie wall slammed down over almost everything before I could see a single yard of fabric. Whatever redirect or acquisition happened here, I'm looking at the Michaels site, not JOANN, and the first thing it made me do was deal with a legal consent banner the size of my whole screen. Not a great welcome for someone who just wants to check if those fat quarters are on sale.

## What's working

- **Promo banner with actual code:** "SAVE20MAY" is right there in plain text in the red strip — I don't have to hunt for it, which I appreciate. As someone who stacks codes, having it surface-level instead of buried in an email footer is the right call.
- **Clean top navigation:** Custom Framing, MakerPlace, Digital Downloads in the top bar, plus Shop / Services / Savings / Buy Again in the sub-nav — "Savings" as a dedicated tab is exactly the kind of shortcut I would tap on instinct.
- **Search bar is immediately accessible:** Front and center, no scrolling. Good for a focused shopper who knows what she wants.

## What's weak

- **The cookie consent modal covers the entire page:** I cannot see a single product, hero, or category image. This is the worst possible first impression. I'm here to check fabric prices, not sign a legal agreement before I've even seen the store.
- **This is Michaels, not JOANN:** I navigated to joann.com and landed on a Michaels-branded site. Whether that's a redirect, an acquisition, or something else — there is zero explanation on screen. That's disorienting and erodes trust immediately.
- **"Exclusions apply." with zero detail:** The 20%-off banner is promising, but quilting cottons and name-brand notions are historically excluded from these deals. No fine print is visible, so I don't know if this applies to anything I actually buy.
- **No loyalty hook above the fold:** Michaels has a rewards program. It's not mentioned anywhere I can see. A returning buyer like me wants to know what my points balance is, not just that there's a generic sale.
- **"Got it" is doing the work of the primary CTA:** The most prominent button on the page is a cookie dismissal. That's not a shopping experience, that's friction.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress or minimize the cookie modal on mobile** — use a slim persistent footer bar instead of a full-screen takeover. The current implementation buries the entire above-the-fold experience.
- **If joann.com now redirects to Michaels, add a clear transition message** — something like "JOANN is now part of Michaels — all your favorites are here" so returning JOANN customers don't think they've landed somewhere wrong.
- **Surface the exclusions list inline on the promo banner** — even a small "see exclusions" tap link next to "Exclusions apply." would reduce the bait-and-switch feeling for coupon-savvy shoppers.
- **Add a Rewards/Savings shortcut to the above-fold area** — the "Savings" tab exists but a personalized hook ("You have 450 points — redeem today") would convert returning members immediately.
