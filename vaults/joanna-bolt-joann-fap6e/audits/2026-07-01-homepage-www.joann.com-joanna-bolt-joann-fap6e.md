# Homepage snapshot · www.joann.com · 2026-07-01
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-01**
## Executive summary

- Wait — I typed in JOANN and this is Michaels. Something is seriously off, and that's the first thing I have to say. Whether there's a redirect happening or I landed wrong, the site I'm looking at is Michaels — red logo, "Ask Mike" AI helper, MakerPlace in the nav, the whole thing. On top of that, a wall-to-wall cookie consent overlay is blocking almost the entire page, so even if I *wanted* to shop, I'm stuck staring at a privacy disclaimer before I can see a single piece of fabric. The one bright spot: there's a promo banner with an actual code — JULYISHERE, 30% off regular-price purchases — which is exactly the kind of offer I came here for. But I can barely see past the cookie box.

## What's working

- **Promo code banner:** The red strip at the top that says "30% Off All Regular Price Purchases — Online only with code JULYISHERE. Exclusions apply." is exactly what I'm looking for when I open a craft store. Code is right there in plain text, I don't have to hunt for it.
- **"Buy Again" tab:** That's in the top navigation row and I appreciate it. I have a short list of things I re-order constantly — Warm & White batting, Wonder Clips, thread. A quick-reorder path is smart.
- **JULYISHERE timing:** The code implies a July 4th sale, and today is July 1st. That's on-calendar and credible — not a leftover "SPRINGSALE" banner baking in the sun.
- **"Ask Mike" AI button:** I'm 52, not allergic to tech. If I can type "does this coupon work on Kona solids" and get an answer, that's genuinely useful for someone who knows the coupon-stack rules and wants to confirm before checkout.

## What's weak

- **Wrong domain:** I navigated to JOANN.com and landed on Michaels. That is a critical failure before we even evaluate anything else. If this is a redirect because JOANN folded, the site does nothing to explain that — I just land in a different store with no acknowledgment.
- **Full-page cookie consent overlay:** This modal blocks every square inch of the page content below the nav. I cannot see the hero, the category grid, the featured products — nothing. On mobile it's even worse because the modal text is long and requires scrolling just to reach "Got it." This is the very definition of a bouncemaker.
- **No quilting/fabric signal anywhere visible:** Not in the nav tabs, not in the promo copy, not in the barely-visible sliver of hero imagery. "30% off regular price" at a craft store is relevant, but there's nothing saying "fabric," "quilting," "sewing," or "notions" in any text I can read.
- **No loyalty hook above the fold:** I know Michaels has a rewards program. I can't see it. "Savings" in the nav doesn't tell me my balance or my member benefit — it could mean printable coupons, it could mean anything.
- **"Exclusions apply" with no fast path to see what's excluded:** I've been burned before. If Kona solids are excluded (they often are), I want to know before I spend 20 minutes filling a cart.

## Recommendations

- **Add a one-line fabric/sewing callout to the promo banner:** Even just "including fabric, notions & quilting supplies†" would tell me this deal applies to what I came for. Right now "All Regular Price Purchases" sounds like it could mean Cricut machines or custom framing, not my project supplies.
- **Delay or minimize the cookie modal:** Swap the full-page blocker for a slim bottom bar. Let me see the homepage before I have to consent to anything — the current implementation makes me want to close the tab, not tap "Got it."
- **Surface the rewards balance in the nav or under "Savings":** One small chip that says "You have 240 points" or "Member? Sign in for 5% back" would do more to keep me on the page than any hero image.
- **If www.joann.com now redirects here, say so clearly:** A single banner — "JOANN has closed. Welcome to Michaels, your new home for fabric and craft supplies." — would stop me from feeling tricked and might actually convert the wave of displaced JOANN shoppers who typed that URL.
