# Homepage snapshot · www.joann.com · 2026-06-17
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-17**
## Executive summary

- I typed in JoAnn.com and landed on… Michaels? I know JoAnn went through bankruptcy but this redirect is disorienting — I wasn't expecting to be handed off to a completely different brand without any explanation. Now I'm sitting here staring at a full-screen cookie consent wall that's burying every bit of actual content. What I *can* see tells me Michaels is running a spend-$100-save-$25 promotion right now, but beyond that banner and a sliver of what looks like a patriotic hero image underneath the modal, this homepage might as well not exist yet.

## What's working

- **The GETMY25 promo banner** — "Save $25 When You Spend $100+" with the actual code printed on the page is exactly what I want to see. No hunting, no "sign in to reveal," just: here's the code, go spend. A $100 threshold is nothing when I'm buying fabric by the bolt.
- **"Buy Again" in the nav** — as a repeat shopper this is a meaningful shortcut. I know what I bought last time, just let me reorder it.
- **"Ask Mike" AI button** — I'm moderately tech-comfortable, and a quick "do you carry this Kona cotton colorway" assistant is actually useful to me. I'd tap it once to test it.
- **Top utility bar** — Custom Framing, MakerPlace, Digital Downloads are distinct enough that I know this is a craft store immediately, even before I see the logo.

## What's weak

- **The JoAnn → Michaels redirect with zero explanation** — I came to JoAnn.com for a reason. There's no banner saying "JoAnn is now part of Michaels" or "your JoAnn account, loyalty points, and history are here." That's a failure for any former JoAnn loyal customer.
- **The cookie consent modal is a full-screen death wall** — It covers the hero, the featured categories, every piece of content that would tell me whether to keep scrolling. On a phone this is especially brutal.
- **Michaels isn't where I go for fabric first** — JoAnn was my destination specifically *because* it stocked serious quilting fabric, notions, and pattern books. Michaels reads as a general craft store. If they want my loyalty dollars now, they need to lead with fabric and sewing, not generic craft framing.
- **No loyalty or rewards callout visible** — I'm a coupon-stacker. If there's a Michaels rewards program that applies, I can't see it. That's a missed conversion point for someone like me.

## Recommendations

- **Add a transition banner above the fold**: "Welcome, JoAnn shoppers — your favorite fabrics and notions are now at Michaels. Sign in to access your account." One sentence, one button. Ship it today.
- **Dismiss-on-scroll or smaller cookie notice on mobile**: Full-screen cookie walls are conversion killers on phones. Either shrink it to a bottom strip or auto-dismiss after a few seconds of scrolling. Don't make me tap a legal notice before I can see a single product.
- **Surface the fabric/quilting category directly in the nav strip** — right now it's "Shop > ..." with no hint that sewing supplies are even here. A "Fabric & Sewing" tab at the top nav level would signal to me in under a second that I'm in the right place.
- **Show a loyalty badge or rewards balance hook in the header** — even "Earn points on every order" next to the account icon tells me there's a reason to sign in and stay.
