## What's working

- **"Shell Shocker" in the top nav** — as someone who scrapes deals daily, seeing that link front-and-center is an immediate dopamine hit. I know exactly what that is and I'm already tapping it.
- **"PC Builder" in the top nav** — compatibility checking tool right in the header. That's the single most valuable Newegg feature for a builder like me and they're not burying it.
- **AMD combo deal on the right** — bundles a CPU and what looks like an ASRock mobo starting at $325. Combo deals are literally how I shop to save on GPU+mobo stacks. This is the right product type.
- **"Build with it" CTA** — copy is unambiguous and speaks directly to the builder mindset. Not "Shop Now," not "Learn More" — "Build with it."
- **RGB rig + guy at desk imagery** — the guy visible behind the nav drawer looks like he could be posting to r/PCMasterRace. Instantly relatable.
- **"Components & Storage" is the first category in the nav** — exactly where I'd head first.

## What's weak

- **Nav drawer is open over the hero** — half the above-the-fold real estate is eaten by the category list. I can't see the full deal, the full product lineup, or whatever headline was meant to hook me. This is a UX fail on the initial page load state.
- **Price is truncated** — "$325.9" is cut off, I can't see the full price or what the savings are vs. buying separately. That's the exact number I need to decide if it's a Shell Shocker-level deal.
- **No urgency signals visible** — Shell Shocker deals are famously time-limited with countdown timers. I can see "Shell Shocker" in the nav but there's no timer or "X hours left" in the current viewport. That urgency is the whole point.
- **No loyalty/rewards callout** — I have a Newegg account, probably with egg points. No "You have X points" or "EggXpert member" recognition is visible.
- **No review counts on the featured AMD products** — I always check ratings before adding to cart. The combo modules show product images and price but zero social proof stars.

## Recommendations

- **Close the nav drawer on initial page load** — or at minimum don't screenshot the state with the drawer open. The hero deal should own the full viewport. The AMD combo with RGB imagery deserves the whole canvas.
- **Add a countdown timer to the Shell Shocker strip in the main nav or in the hero** — even a "Deal ends in 4:22:17" chip next to "Shell Shocker" would spike urgency without adding layout complexity.
- **Show star ratings + review count on combo deal cards** — drop a "4.7 ★ (2,341 reviews)" line under the AMD Ryzen product card. That's what pushes me from browse to cart.
- **Surface a "Welcome back, Nash" or egg points balance** in the header — even a small "500 egg points" chip next to the account icon signals that I have skin in the game and should keep shopping.
