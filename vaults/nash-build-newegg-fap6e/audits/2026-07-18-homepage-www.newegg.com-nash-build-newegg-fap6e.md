# Homepage snapshot · www.newegg.com · 2026-07-18
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-18**
## Executive summary

- Bro, this is basically my browser homepage — Shell Shocker front and center, PC Builder in the nav, and what looks like a RAM drop as the hero. Newegg is clearly in deal-hunter mode right now, pushing a tax holiday on top of combo savings. It reads exactly like a site built for someone who has the app pinned and checks it before checking their texts.

## What's working

- **Shell Shocker tab in the nav strip** — it's the first thing my eyes go to after the logo. That's exactly how I browse Newegg: I open it, I tap Shell Shocker. Done.
- **PC Builder in the same nav strip** — I use this every single build. Seeing it zero-clicks away is a green flag.
- **"AI Hardware" as a standalone category** — not buried under Components. Newegg knows their audience has moved on to AI accelerators and NPU upgrades. Big W.
- **Tax holiday banner** — specific, time-limited, clear benefit. "Tax-free savings on select products" is the kind of headline that makes me do mental math on that GPU I've been eyeing.
- **AMD Combo deal visible at bottom** — Ryzen + something, savings visible. Combos are how I shave $40-80 off a build without thinking.
- **Components & Storage** is the first category in the left nav — exactly where I live.

## What's weak

- **Hero CTA is cut off** — "RAM PER S..." whatever the rest says, I can't read it. On mobile the hero is clipped and the button/CTA isn't fully visible. That's a miss.
- **No rewards balance or returning-user hook** — I'm a repeat buyer. Show me my Egg Rewards points or my saved list. This feels like I'm a guest.
- **"Hello Select address"** prompt at the top — shipping address still unset. On mobile this takes up prime real estate and makes the page feel like I haven't logged in even if I have.
- **No trust signals visible** — no review counts, no "4.8 stars on 12K reviews" rail, nothing. For a $400 GPU buy I want that social proof.
- **Car imagery in the hero** — the dark scene looks like a racing game or a car ad. If it's a RAM product, the product itself should be more prominent, not the vibe shot.

## Recommendations

- **Show my Egg Rewards balance in the header** — even just "2,450 pts" next to the login icon. Every time I see points I'm closer to spending them.
- **Make the hero CTA full-width and readable on mobile** — "RAM PER S..." being clipped is a layout bug. Ensure the hero text and button don't get cropped at the right edge on smaller viewports.
- **Add a "Today's Shell Shockers" mini-rail above the fold** — show 2-3 deal cards with countdown timers right below the nav strip. I'd never leave without checking them.
- **Surface a "PC Compatibility Checker" nudge near the combo deal** — "Building a rig? Make sure it's compatible →" links to PC Builder. Converts combo browsers into full-build buyers.
