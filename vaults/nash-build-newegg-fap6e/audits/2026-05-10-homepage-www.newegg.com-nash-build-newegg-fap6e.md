# Homepage snapshot · www.newegg.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- This is my literal daily browsing destination — I check Shell Shocker every morning and I've used the PC Builder tool to spec like four builds. Landing on the homepage, the signal is immediate: deal-hunting hub for builders, with a Weekend Doorbusters banner up top and an AMD Ryzen combo deal loading in the bottom-right corner. Newegg knows exactly who it's talking to, even if the offer copy is a bit vague.

## What's working

- **Shell Shocker in the top nav** — I see it immediately and know exactly what it is. Huge hook for deal scrapers like me who come back daily.
- **"PC Builder" tool in the nav bar** — one of the main reasons I use Newegg over Amazon. The fact it's top-level nav and not buried is huge.
- **Hero image of a PC builder at their rig** — blue LED cooler, dual-monitor energy, moody lighting. That's aspirational content for anyone on r/PCMasterRace.
- **AMD Ryzen combo deal appearing in the lower-right** — combo deals are literally the reason I check Newegg first when I'm sourcing a CPU + mobo stack. Even partially visible, it hooks me.
- **Components & Storage at the top of the category list** — not buried below "Appliances" or "Home & Outdoors." My category is first.

## What's weak

- **"Extra Weekend Doorbuster deals during select weekends"** — the "select weekends" qualifier kills urgency. Is today a select weekend? I have no idea. Feels like a hedge that makes me trust the offer less.
- **No visible price or discount percentage on the hero** — "Weekend Doorbusters" tells me nothing about how good the deal is. Is it 10% off? 40%? I need a number.
- **AMD combo deal is cut off** — I can see the Ryzen chip and the AMD logo but the price and details are below the fold. The most interesting thing on the page is getting cropped.
- **No Newegg Premiere hook visible** — I'm a paying Premiere member and I get free shipping + price drops. If that badge showed up somewhere above the fold confirming my status or the threshold, I'd feel rewarded for logging in.
- **"Home & Outdoors" and "Health & Sports" in the category nav** — I know Newegg sells this stuff but seeing it just makes the nav feel diluted. Every pixel spent on that is noise to me.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "Weekend Doorbusters" subtext for an actual number** — "Up to 40% off GPUs & CPUs this weekend" is infinitely more compelling than "Extra deals during select weekends." Give me the hook.
- **Expand the AMD combo deal card so it's not cut off** — push the hero image up or shorten the banner so the combo deal is fully visible. That module is the conversion engine for builders.
- **Add a Newegg Premiere badge or "Free shipping on your order" confirmation** for logged-in members above the fold — even a one-line strip below the banner would land.
- **Pin "Shell Shocker ends in 3h 42m" countdown next to the Shell Shocker nav link** — that urgency timer is already a thing on the Shell Shocker page; surfacing it in the nav itself would make me tap immediately.
