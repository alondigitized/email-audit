- **Surface the Shell Shocker deal timer on the hero itself** — if Shell Shocker has a live countdown, show it on the landing panel, not just in the nav text. That's the #1 conversion hook for my persona.
- **Add an Eggxpert / community badge to the Intel combo card** — "4.7 stars, 1,200 Eggxpert reviews" next to $379.9 would push me to tap immediately.
