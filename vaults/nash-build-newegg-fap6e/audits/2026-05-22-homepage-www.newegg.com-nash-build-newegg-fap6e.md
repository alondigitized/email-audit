# Homepage snapshot · www.newegg.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- Alright, Newegg knows exactly who I am — Shell Shocker and PC Builder are literally the first things in the nav, and "Components & Storage" is sitting at top of the category list like it owns the place. The page is pushing what looks like a Memorial Day Sale and an Intel combo deal, which is peak May content for someone like me. Nav sidebar is open and eating half my screen though, which is a bit of an L on mobile.

## What's working

- **Shell Shocker in the top nav** — I check this literally every day. Having it front-row in the sticky nav on mobile is exactly right. One tap, no hunting.
- **PC Builder tool in the nav** — That's my compatibility check workflow. Seeing it above the fold on mobile means I'd actually use it here instead of switching apps.
- **Intel Combo deal module** — Shows the processor, the companion product (looks MSI), and a bundled price. This is combo-deal UX done right for a builder who's always stacking parts.
- **Category list prioritization** — Components & Storage leads the list, then Computer Systems, then Peripherals. That ordering is correct for someone building a rig, not browsing appliances.
- **Memorial Day Sale banner** — Seasonal timing is on-point. Mid-May GPU hunting is real and Newegg knows it.

## What's weak

- **Nav sidebar open state is hiding the hero** — On mobile, the left-rail nav is expanded and covering roughly half the viewport. The hero banner is clipped to just "MEM SA" and some blue imagery — I can't even read the full offer.
- **No CTA button visible above the fold** — I see a sale banner and an Intel module but there's no "Shop the Sale" or "Build Now" button I can tap without scrolling. First-fold on mobile should have an action.
- **No urgency signal on the Intel combo** — Shell Shocker deals expire, that's the whole point. The Intel combo at $924.97 doesn't show a countdown or "today only" — so there's no pull to act now.
- **No trust layer visible** — No review count, no "X sold today," no secure checkout badge. For a $924 combo deal, I want some social proof before I tap.
- **No free shipping callout** — Newegg's free shipping threshold is a deciding factor for me. Not seeing it above the fold is a miss when I'm about to spend nearly a grand.

## Recommendations

- **Collapse the nav by default on mobile** — Show the hamburger menu closed so the hero banner gets full width. The Memorial Day Sale hero is getting murdered by an open sidebar.
- **Add a countdown timer to the Intel combo module** — Even "Ends Monday" would drive urgency. Shell Shocker has this baked in; the combo deals should too.
- **Pin a free-shipping badge to the hero or sticky header** — "Free shipping on orders $49+" somewhere persistent would remove the price-math friction before I even tap into a product page.
- **Show review star count on the Intel combo** — Two lines of text below the product image saying "4.7★ (1,240 reviews)" would close the trust gap for a sub-$1K combo I've never seen before.
