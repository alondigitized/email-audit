# Homepage snapshot · www.newegg.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- Newegg on mobile hits different — this is literally my homepage. The Weekend Doorbusters countdown is front and center, the category nav goes straight to Components & Storage, and there's an AMD combo deal peeking in below. This brand is running a deal event and making sure deal-hunters know it before anything else loads.

## What's working

- **Countdown timer ("Weekend Doorbusters Start in: 20:49:37")** — this is exactly how you hook a Black Friday GPU hunter. I'm already doing math on when to check back.
- **"Shell Shocker" in top nav** — that's Newegg's legendary daily deal format and it's the first nav item. Any Eggxpert knows what that means.
- **"PC Builder" nav item** — literal compatibility tool, one tap away. That's the primary reason I come to Newegg over Amazon.
- **RGB gaming rig image** — the right-side hero image of someone at a lit-up PC setup is aspirational for me. That's a build I want to post on r/PCMasterRace.
- **AMD combo strip** — "Combo up savin..." + Ryzen 5 visible at the bottom. Combo deals are peak Newegg value and I actively hunt these.
- **Category menu depth** — "Components & Storage," "Gaming & VR," "Server & Components" all visible without scrolling. My whole shopping list is right there.

## What's weak

- **"Preview deals ▸" is too soft** — a text link for the biggest CTA on the page? Make it a button. I almost missed it.
- **No specific discount teased** — "Unlock exclusive savings" tells me nothing. Even "up to 40% off GPUs" would've made me tap immediately.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — Newegg's free shipping cutoff matters for component orders. Not showing it above the fold leaves money on the table.
- **AMD section is cut off** — "Combo up savin..." and "AMD Ry..." — I can see it but can't read it. That partial reveal is frustrating on mobile.
- **No reviews or ratings above fold** — even a "4.8★ from 12k reviews" badge on a featured product would add trust signals.
- **Loyalty/rewards not visible** — I have an Eggxpert account and I have no idea what my rewards balance is or if there's member pricing on the doorbusters.

## Recommendations

- **Make "Preview deals" a full-width button** — yellow or orange background, bold type. Right now it blends into the countdown banner. One visual change, massive CTR lift.
- **Add a teaser % inside the countdown banner** — "Up to 45% off GPUs, SSDs, and more — starting 5/9 at 9am PT." Give me a reason to set an alarm, not just a vague promise.
- **Pin the free-shipping threshold to the top bar** — even a thin strip saying "Free shipping on orders $35+" stays visible as users scroll and reduces checkout friction for component purchases.
- **Expand the AMD combo card** — show the full product name, price, and savings in the card. Don't let it get clipped. A Ryzen 5 combo deal at a good price would make me tap immediately.
