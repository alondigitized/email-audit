# Homepage snapshot · www.newegg.com · 2026-05-07
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- Newegg knows exactly who I am. First thing I see is a gamer hunched over a blue-lit rig, a Shell Shocker tab in the nav bar, and a "Weekend Doorbusters" countdown telling me deals drop in 2 days. This is deal-hunter central — the page is basically my homepage already. The brand is pushing a pre-event hype cycle right now, and it's working.

## What's working

- **Shell Shocker in the nav bar** — Nash knows that tab means today's one-day GPU deal. It's front row, always-on, and immediately recognizable without explanation.
- **"Weekend Doorbusters Start In: 2 Days" countdown banner** — creates pre-event anticipation. I'm already mentally marking 5/9/26 at 9am PT on my calendar.
- **"PC Builder" tab in the nav** — my go-to compatibility checker is one tap away. This is not a generic retailer; they built a tool specifically for me.
- **Gaming PC lifestyle image** — blue LED rig, dark room, guy clearly building/gaming. That's my vibe, not a stock photo of a laptop in a coffee shop.
- **AMD Combo deal section loading in at the bottom right** — Ryzen + ASRock combo visible. Combo deals are exactly how I save on CPU + motherboard bundles.
- **"Components & Storage" is literally the first category in the sidebar** — zero hunting required to get to my aisle.

## What's weak

- **"Preview deals ▸" is a text link, not a button** — for the most important CTA above the fold, that's underselling it. A 22-year-old deal hunter needs a fat orange button screaming "SET A REMINDER."
- **No active price visible above the fold** — the Doorbusters are 2 days out, and there's no Shell Shocker price teaser to make me feel like I'm already winning. Feels like a waiting room.
- **No loyalty / Egg Points callout** — I'm a repeat buyer. Where's my balance? Where's the "you're 200 points from a free $5 card"? That hooks returning users.
- **The AMD combo section is cut off** — whatever pricing or product detail is in that bottom-right card is cropped out, which is exactly where I'd want to see $ savings.
- **No GPU-specific hero** — Newegg's bread and butter for me is graphics cards. The lifestyle image is good but generic; a specific GPU hero (RTX or RX drop) would hit harder.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "Preview deals ▸" text link for a high-contrast countdown button** with a "Set Reminder" micro-CTA underneath — give me a calendar invite or push notification for 5/9/26 at 9am.
- **Add a Shell Shocker price teaser strip below the Doorbusters banner** — show one redacted deal with a "see today's deal" prompt to keep the current-day urgency alive while the weekend event builds.
- **Surface the Egg Points balance or Eggxpert badge in the header for signed-in users** — returning power users like me should see our loyalty status on arrival.
- **Feature one GPU product prominently in the hero or above-fold rail** — even a "Best GPU Under $400 Right Now" tile anchors the page for builders who are always window-shopping cards.
