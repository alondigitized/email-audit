# Homepage snapshot · www.newegg.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay so Newegg on mobile hits different — the nav drawer is wide open showing every category, and off to the right I can see a RAM/performance hero banner and an Intel combo deal with a "Build with it" CTA. This is clearly a PC-parts-first store and that energy is immediately right. The Shell Shocker and PC Builder shortcuts in the top bar are basically my entire personality.

## What's working

- **Shell Shocker and PC Builder in the top nav bar** — these two shortcuts are basically why I use Newegg over Amazon. Seeing them immediately tells me the site knows what it's for.
- **Intel combo deal section** — "Build with it" CTA with a bundled CPU + board combo is exactly how I shop. I always want to see what pairs well together, not just a single SKU.
- **Components & Storage at the top of the nav drawer** — right category, right position, no hunting required.
- **Gaming & VR in the visible nav list** — another one-tap path to my category, right there in the sidebar.
- **"RAM PE—" hero banner** — even half-obscured, the performance/gaming visual signals this is a place for me.

## What's weak

- **Open nav drawer eating the hero** — the left-side category menu is covering the majority of the right-side hero banner. On a phone screen, that's almost the entire viewport gone. I have to mentally look past the drawer to see what's being promoted.
- **Intel combo pricing is cut off** — "Combo up savi…" and the product names ("Intel Co… 270K P…", "AS U…") are truncated. I can't read the actual deal without tapping, which kills impulse-buy energy.
- **No urgency signal visible** — Shell Shocker implies time-limited deals but I can't see any countdown timer or "ends today" copy on screen. That timer is the whole point of Shell Shocker.
- **No Egg Cash / rewards balance visible** — as an Eggxpert I have rewards, and I can't see my balance at a glance. That's a missed hook for a returning shopper.
- **No trust signals in viewport** — no review counts, no "X sold today," nothing to validate the combo deal without tapping deeper.

## Recommendations

- **Auto-dismiss the nav drawer on homepage load** — or at minimum collapse it so the hero occupies the full width. A half-visible "RAM PE—" banner does nothing. Let the hero breathe.
- **Stick a live countdown chip directly on the Shell Shocker nav link** — even "2h 14m left" next to the Shell Shocker text would trigger FOMO without adding any clutter.
- **Show truncated combo deal specs in full** — "Intel Core i9-14900K + ASUS ROG Z790 — $934.99" should fit on two lines. Right now half the product names are cut off and I can't evaluate the deal.
- **Surface Egg Cash balance in the top bar** — replace the location pin with a "🥚 $12 Egg Cash" chip for logged-in users. I check that before I buy anything.
