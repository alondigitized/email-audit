# Homepage snapshot · www.newegg.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- Newegg lands exactly where I need it — Shell Shocker in the top nav, PC Builder one tap away, and an Intel combo deal staring me in the face before I even clear the nav drawer. This feels like a site built for people who already know what they want to buy, not for someone discovering PC gaming for the first time. Right now it's pushing an Intel combo build, which checks out for Q2 upgrade season.

## What's working

- **Shell Shocker in the top nav** — that's my daily ritual. Putting it in the persistent nav bar instead of a buried banner is exactly right; I tap it before I even look at the hero.
- **PC Builder in the nav** — compatibility checking is one tap away. That's a massive trust signal for builders.
- **"Build with it" CTA** — the Intel combo card immediately frames the product as a build ingredient, not just a standalone purchase. That framing is perfectly tuned for me.
- **Components & Storage leads the category list** — not "Appliances," not "Health & Sports" — the thing I actually shop is right at the top.
- **Partial hero shows RAM performance content** — even half-visible behind the nav drawer, it signals that the site is running enthusiast-tier content, not generic consumer deals.

## What's weak

- **Nav drawer is blocking the hero** — whatever the RAM/performance hero banner is selling, I can barely see it. The open nav state in this screenshot kills the first-impression moment.
- **No Egg Points / rewards callout above the fold** — I'm a returning customer. Show me my balance. Loyalty hooks are free retention and they're invisible here.
- **No Shell Shocker countdown timer visible** — Shell Shockers live and die on urgency. If there's a timer, it's off-screen. That's a miss on mobile.
- **Right-side hero content is truncated** — "RAM PE S" is all I can read. If the hero is about RAM performance, it should survive a 390px viewport without being sliced in half.
- **No review counts on the Intel combo card** — I'd want to see star ratings before tapping "Build with it." Trust signal is missing right there.

## Recommendations

- **Close the nav by default on mobile**, or render the hero above it so the first visual impression isn't a menu. The current state makes it look like a nav app, not a deal site.
- **Add an Egg Points balance chip to the header** — right next to the profile icon. One line of text, zero friction, massive retention signal for logged-in users.
- **Surface the Shell Shocker countdown timer above the fold** — even a "Ends in 3h 22m" strip under the nav bar would create urgency without requiring a scroll.
- **Expand the Intel combo card to show star ratings and review count** — "Intel Core i9-14900K — 4.7★ (1,203 reviews)" before I tap "Build with it" would move me from browse to cart.
