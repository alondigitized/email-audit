# Homepage snapshot · www.newegg.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- Newegg is literally built for me — first thing I see is a PC builder hunched over an RGB rig and an AMD combo deal loading in. The Weekend Doorbusters countdown with a hard date (5/9/26, 9am PT) is exactly the kind of drop-time energy I track on Reddit. This is the one retailer where the homepage doesn't make me feel like I accidentally opened a Macy's ad.

## What's working

- **"Shell Shocker" in the top nav** — for deal scrapers like me, that tab is a reflex tap. Newegg knows their audience and leads with it.
- **Weekend Doorbusters countdown ("3 Days")** — specific drop time creates genuine urgency. I'm already setting a reminder.
- **"PC Builder" tool in the nav bar** — one-tap compatibility checker, right there. No digging through menus.
- **Components & Storage listed first** in the category rail — my actual use case is the top result, not buried under Appliances.
- **AMD Combo deal card** partially visible below the hero — combo deals are my jam. Seeing an AMD Ryzen + ASRock pairing right away signals this page knows what I buy.
- **Hero image** — dude at a glowing PC rig. Zero ambiguity about what this site is for.

## What's weak

- **Left nav list is dense and text-heavy** — on mobile, this eats half the screen with a wall of categories. The hero is cramped to the right and partially cut off.
- **No price anchors visible** — I can see "Combo deal saving" but the actual dollar amount is clipped. Tease me with a number, don't make me tap to find out.
- **"Preview deals ▶" is undersized** — this is the main CTA for the Doorbusters push and it's a small text link. Should be a proper button.
- **No trust/review signals in viewport** — no star ratings, no "X sold in last 24 hours," nothing that builds FOMO without scrolling.
- **Hero image is partially obscured** by the nav list on mobile — the guy at the rig is clipped on the right side. Missed opportunity for a full-width impact shot.

## Recommendations

- **Collapse the category list into a two-column icon grid** on mobile so the hero image gets full-width treatment — the battlestation shot is the best brand asset on this page.
- **Add a price anchor to the Doorbusters banner** ("Save up to $XXX — Starting 5/9 at 9am PT") so I know whether to care before I tap.
- **Replace the "Preview deals ▶" text link with a bold pill button** in Newegg orange — the action step needs to be unmissable.
- **Pin a live "Today's Shell Shocker" mini-card** below the Doorbusters banner with actual product + price — converts deal hunters immediately instead of making them hunt.
