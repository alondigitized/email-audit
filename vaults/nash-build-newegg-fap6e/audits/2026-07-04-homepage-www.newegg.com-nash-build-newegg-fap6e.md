# Homepage snapshot · www.newegg.com · 2026-07-04
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-04**
## Executive summary

- Okay so Newegg on mobile hit me with the nav drawer wide open, which is kind of a wild first impression — felt less like a homepage and more like I accidentally tapped the menu. But once I looked past that, the right side of the screen was dropping a 4th of July hero *and* an AMD Ryzen + MSI combo deal with a price already showing, which is exactly the kind of thing that makes me stop scrolling Reddit and open my wallet. This is clearly a deal-first tech retailer that knows what week it is, even if the layout is fighting itself.

## What's working

- **Shell Shocker tab** is right in the top nav strip — that's my daily ritual and seeing it immediately tells me the deals are a tap away
- **PC Builder tab** is pinned alongside Shell Shocker — Newegg knows its audience and surfaces its best tools front and center
- **AMD Combo deal card** in the bottom-right shows a named CPU + motherboard bundle with a real dollar price — that's the exact "combo deal" dopamine hit I came here for
- **4th of July hero banner** is timely and visible — I can tell there's a holiday sale running without having to dig
- **Category nav** has "Components & Storage" as the literal first item — correct, that's where I live

## What's weak

- **Nav drawer dominating the viewport**: The left nav is eating ~65% of the screen on what appears to be an open/default state — it's burying the hero and the deals I actually want to see
- **"Combo up saving" copy** on the AMD card is grammatically awkward — feels like a localization artifact, not sharp deal copy
- **"Hello Select address"**: No personalization beyond a location prompt. No rewards points balance, no recent build history, nothing that says Newegg knows I've been here before
- **Right-side hero is clipped**: The 4th of July banner is cut off — I can only read "4TH OF" and "SA" — the full offer or deal headline is hidden behind the nav
- **No trust signals visible**: No review count, no free-shipping callout, no Egg rewards badge anywhere above fold

## Recommendations

- **Auto-close the nav drawer on homepage load** — the hero and combo deals are the real hook on mobile; don't let a full-width menu bury them on first impression
- **Swap "Combo up saving" for sharp deal copy** like "Save $XX on this AMD Ryzen + MSI bundle" — name the savings, not just the concept
- **Surface an Egg Rewards balance or Shell Shocker countdown** as a sticky strip below the nav tabs — give returning deal hunters a reason to feel VIP immediately
- **Make the 4th of July offer headline fully visible above fold** — even a one-line deal strip ("4th of July PC Sale — Up to XX% off Components") would anchor the visit
