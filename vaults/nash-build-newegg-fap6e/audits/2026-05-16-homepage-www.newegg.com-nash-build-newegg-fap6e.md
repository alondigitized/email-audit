# Homepage snapshot · www.newegg.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Newegg on mobile hits me immediately with the right energy — Shell Shocker right in the nav bar, PC Builder a tap away, Components & Storage the very first category in the left rail. The Intel combo deal card below the hero is exactly the kind of thing I screenshot and drop in my build thread. This homepage knows its audience, even if the mobile layout chops off some key text above the fold.

## What's working

- **Shell Shocker in top nav** — that name carries weight in the PC community. I know instantly there's a timed flash deal waiting for me, and it's one tap from any page.
- **PC Builder shortcut in top nav** — not buried in a menu, right there. First-tap access to the compatibility checker I use every build.
- **"Components & Storage" is literally the first category** in the sidebar — no scrolling past "Appliances" and "Health & Sports" to get to what I came for.
- **Intel combo deal card** — CPU + motherboard bundle with a `Build with it` CTA is catnip. That's exactly the Stack I screenshot and post to r/PCMasterRace.
- **Intel branding on the deal card** — seeing the blue Intel badge gives the combo instant legitimacy.

## What's weak

- **Hero text is clipped on mobile** — "RAM PER" with a dark background is all I get. I can't read the headline, the price, or the CTA for whatever deal that hero is running. It's just vibes.
- **"Combo up savi..." is truncated** — the savings figure is the whole reason I'd tap that card and I can't see it. That's a conversion kill on mobile.
- **No loyalty/rewards hook above fold** — Newegg Premier exists but I'd have no idea from this screen. A returning customer sees zero signal that their status matters.
- **No review counts or ratings on the Intel combo card** — I'm about to consider a $959.97 purchase and there's no social proof visible. That number is scary without a star rating attached.
- **No free shipping threshold** — for a cart that size, free shipping is a deciding factor and it's completely invisible here.

## Recommendations

- **Fix the mobile hero crop** — the RAM banner's headline and CTA are being swallowed by the left nav drawer. Either collapse the nav by default on mobile or resize the hero so the copy is fully legible without tapping anything.
- **Show the combo savings amount, not just the final price** — "Save $120 when you bundle" next to `$959.97` is infinitely more motivating than a truncated "Combo up savi..." label. One line of visible copy.
- **Add a star rating + review count to the Intel combo card** — even "4.7 ★ (2,341 reviews)" on that card converts PC builders who are seconds away from going to Amazon to price-check.
- **Surface a Newegg Premier / free shipping banner** below the top nav — something like "Free 2-day shipping on $49+" in a thin strip would clear the biggest objection for anyone building a cart.
