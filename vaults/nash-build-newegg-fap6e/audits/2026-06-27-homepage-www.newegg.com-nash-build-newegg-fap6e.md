# Homepage snapshot · www.newegg.com · 2026-06-27
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-27**
## Executive summary

- Alright, this is Newegg being Newegg — heavy on the category nav, light on the wow factor. The FantasTech Sale banner is right there and "Up to 71%" on components is the kind of number that gets my thumb to stop scrolling, but the homepage feels like it opened mid-hamburger-menu rather than hitting me with a hero. It's a deal-hunting site doing deal-hunting things, not a brand moment.

## What's working

- **"Up to 71% — Components &..."** — that number hits immediately, even half-cropped. For me that's "stop and investigate."
- **Shell Shocker in the top strip** — I check Shell Shocker daily. Seeing it in the navigation strip without having to dig for it is exactly right.
- **"PC Build..." in the nav strip** — PC Builder tool accessibility from the very top means compatibility checking is one tap away. That matters for build planning.
- **Components & Storage leads the left nav** — first item, top of the list. Newegg knows who its core customer is and didn't bury it.
- **AI Mode label in the top strip** — as a Gen Z builder I'm curious what this is. That's a genuine hook even if it's ambiguous.

## What's weak

- **The hero is getting strangled by the left nav panel** — on mobile the category sidebar is eating half the screen, which means the FantasTech Sale banner is compressed into a sliver on the right. I can barely read "FAN..."
- **No visible countdown timer or end date** for the FantasTech Sale — if 71% off is a time-limited deal, that urgency is completely missing above the fold.
- **"Hello Select address"** — I'm not logged in, there's no EggPoints nudge, no member pricing callout. Newegg has a rewards program; none of it is surfaced here.
- **No product shot with price** — I see a motherboard thumbnail but no SKU, model name, or price visible. One concrete product deal would make me tap immediately.
- **No free-shipping threshold** — Newegg ships free on tons of stuff. Not mentioning it here is a missed conversion signal.

## Recommendations

- **Collapse or minimize the left nav on mobile homepage load** — let the FantasTech Sale banner breathe full-width so the hero can actually function as a hero, not a sidebar afterthought.
- **Add a countdown timer to the FantasTech Sale strip** — even a "Ends in 2d 14h" badge next to the label would create urgency where there's currently none.
- **Surface one Shell Shocker deal inline** — model name, was/now price, a Buy button. Right now the Shell Shocker is a navigation label; make it a product card with a tappable deal.
- **Show EggPoints balance or "Join for member pricing" CTA** — returning users want to see their rewards, new users need a reason to create an account. Neither audience is served here.
