# Homepage snapshot · www.newegg.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Newegg is in full deal mode right now — Memorial Day Sale banner up top, Shell Shocker pinned to nav, Intel combo already staring me in the face. This feels like home. The nav drawer is wide open showing me exactly what I came for: Components & Storage at the very top. It's not fancy, but it's built for people like me, not some random consumer who wandered in from a Google ad.

## What's working

- **Shell Shocker in the top nav** — this is the first thing I check every morning and it's right there, no digging required
- **PC Builder tool in nav** — compatibility checking one tap away; that's a huge deal for someone spec'ing a new build
- **Components & Storage as the #1 category** — the category list is in the right order for me; I'm not scrolling past "Women's Shoes" to find my stuff
- **Intel Combo deal above fold** — "$924.97" with a Core and MSI product visible immediately signals this is a real build deal, not a peripheral accessory push
- **Memorial Day Sale banner** — timely campaign, that sale urgency is real for a deal hunter

## What's weak

- **Nav drawer auto-open on mobile obscures the hero** — I land on the page and the left menu is already expanded, blocking the right side; it's visually cluttered and feels like a layout bug even if it's intentional
- **No countdown timer or urgency signal** — I can see the sale exists but there's no "ends in 2 days" pressure; for a deal scraper this is a missed tap
- **No trust signals on the Intel combo card** — no star rating or review count visible; on Newegg specifically I always check Eggxpert reviews before clicking a bundle
- **"Hello Select address"** feels like an onboarding state, not a returning-customer state — I'm an Eggxpert reviewer with an account and I'm greeted like a guest
- **Free shipping threshold invisible** — for a $900+ combo I want to know immediately if it ships free

## Recommendations

- **Replace the generic patriotic hero with a GPU/component lifestyle shot** — show a lit build, an open case, hands on a GPU, something that signals "this is for builders" not "this is a department store sale"
- **Add a countdown to Memorial Day weekend** — "Sale ends Monday 11:59 PM" with a live timer; deal scrapers respond to that immediately
- **Surface the Eggxpert badge / loyalty status** in the header for logged-in users so returning reviewers like me feel seen, not anonymous
- **Show free shipping threshold on the combo card** — even a small "Free Shipping" badge on the $924.97 deal would reduce hesitation before the tap
