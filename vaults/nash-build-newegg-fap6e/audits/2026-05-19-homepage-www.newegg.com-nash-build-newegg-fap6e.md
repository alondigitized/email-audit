# Homepage snapshot · www.newegg.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Bro, Newegg knows exactly who they're talking to the second I open this page. Shell Shocker and PC Builder are right in the top nav, the left drawer is blown open showing every category I care about, and there's an AMD Ryzen 5 combo deal staring me in the face below what looks like a Memorial Day sale banner. This is a hardware-first retailer in full sale-season mode — no lifestyle fluff, just parts, deals, and a "Build with it" CTA.

## What's working

- **Shell Shocker in the top nav** — that's Newegg's daily doorbusters and it's the first thing I look for. One tap from anywhere on the site.
- **PC Builder in the nav bar** — this is the compatibility checker I use for every build. Having it top-level is chef's kiss for someone who lives in that tool.
- **AMD combo module** — Ryzen 5 + motherboard at ~$340 with a "Build with it" CTA is exactly the kind of bundle deal I hunt. Shows they understand my purchase pattern (CPU + mobo = always bought together).
- **Memorial Day sale banner** — the timing is right, the scale of the sale feels big based on the large bold type visible.
- **Category list breadth** — Components & Storage, Gaming & VR, Networking all visible without scrolling. I can get anywhere in two taps.

## What's weak

- **Full nav drawer open on load** — on mobile this is a brutal experience. It's covering 60% of the screen including most of the hero. If this is the default state on load, that's a major UX problem for a first impression.
- **Hero is almost completely hidden** — I can barely read the Memorial Day sale banner because the nav overlay is eating it. I'm missing whatever deal or imagery is behind that drawer.
- **No loyalty hook** — I'm an Eggxpert reviewer. I've spent real money here. Show me my points balance, my Eggxpert status, *something* that rewards me for coming back.
- **No urgency signal** — the AMD combo is great but there's no countdown, no "X units left," no "ends tonight." Shell Shocker deals die fast and I can't see when this one expires.
- **No review counts on the combo** — I need star ratings on a $340 purchase decision. Where are they?

## Recommendations

- **Close the nav drawer on load** — or at minimum set the default state to collapsed so the hero and deal panels are visible immediately on mobile. The drawer should be triggered by the hamburger tap, not auto-open.
- **Add a countdown timer to the AMD combo panel** — even "Sale ends in 14:32:07" would spike urgency for someone like me who knows these deals go fast.
- **Surface Eggxpert status or EggRewards balance** in the header next to the cart icon — returning buyers want to see their equity instantly.
- **Drop star ratings inline on the combo cards** — even a compact "4.5★ (1,247)" line under the product name would cut hesitation for that price point.
