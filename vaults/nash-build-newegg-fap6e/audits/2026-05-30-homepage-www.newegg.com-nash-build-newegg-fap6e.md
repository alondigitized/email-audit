# Homepage snapshot · www.newegg.com · 2026-05-30
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-30**
## Executive summary

- Newegg straight-up opens on the category sidebar — Components & Storage is literally the first thing I see, which tells you exactly who they're building this for. The hero is some kind of gaming/Minecraft-looking promo and there's already an Intel combo deal card showing $943.97 below the fold. This is clearly a PC hardware and tech retailer in full "let's sell you a build" mode right now.

## What's working

- **Shell Shocker tab in the top nav** — this is the first thing I look for every single time I open Newegg. The fact it's pinned in the persistent nav means I never have to hunt for daily deals.
- **PC Builder tool right next to Shell Shocker** — having my two most-used tools in one tap is genuinely great UX for a builder like me.
- **"Components & Storage" leading the category list** — not buried under "Appliances" or "Health & Sports." The hierarchy respects that core PC parts are why people come to Newegg.
- **Intel Core combo deal card visible** — a $943.97 bundle with a CPU (looks like a Core Ultra i9 270K) immediately speaks to upgrade-mode shoppers.
- **Gaming hero with Minecraft-ish aesthetic** — I'm not 35, this doesn't feel like a stodgy tech catalog. The visual energy is right for my age group.

## What's weak

- **Hero copy is cut off** — "ENJOY NOW" and "WITH AS LO..." is truncated on the right edge. I can't read the full offer and that's a miss. If you're running a deal, let me read the deal.
- **No Egg Points / rewards balance visible** — I'm a logged-in Eggxpert reviewer and I have no idea what my balance is without navigating away. That's a loyalty hook they're leaving on the table.
- **"Hello Select address" feels cold** — if I'm logged in, this should say "Hey Nash" or show my last-used address. This feels like a guest experience on a returning-buyer session.
- **Combo deal pricing is ambiguous** — "AS U... = $943.97" is hard to parse. Is that the combo price? The individual price? The savings? I shouldn't have to squint.
- **Lending terms disclaimer already visible** — showing financing fine print before I've even engaged with a product creates friction and mild distrust right away.

## Recommendations

- **Fix the hero text truncation** — the offer copy is cut off on mobile. Either reduce font size or restructure the layout so the full "ENJOY NOW [DEAL COPY]" is readable without tapping through.
- **Surface Egg Points balance for logged-in users** — replace "Hello Select address" with "Hey Nash · 2,450 Egg Points" to immediately activate my loyalty brain and make me more likely to convert.
- **Make the Intel combo card copy scan in 2 seconds** — rewrite "AS U... = $943.97" to something like "Bundle: Save $120 vs. buying separately" so the value prop lands instantly.
- **Add a "Today's Shell Shockers" preview strip** — even just 2–3 deal thumbnails with countdown timers would get me scrolling immediately. Right now I have to tap the tab to see anything.
