# Homepage snapshot · www.newegg.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- Okay this is literally my site — Newegg, Shell Shocker, AMD combos, PC Builder tool right in the nav. The Memorial Day Sale banner is live which makes total sense for late May, and they're already pushing an AMD Ryzen + MSI motherboard combo deal in the first screen. This homepage knows exactly who it's talking to. Feels like home.

## What's working

- **Shell Shocker in the top nav** — I check this daily. Seeing it pinned immediately tells me this isn't a generic electronics store, it's MY store.
- **PC Builder tool in the nav** — one tap from compatibility checking. That's exactly the journey I take: browse deal → check compatibility → buy.
- **AMD Combo deal module** — AMD Ryzen + MSI board with a combo price is literally the kind of bundle I hunt on here. That's actionable above the fold.
- **Memorial Day Sale banner** — timely, credible, the RGB PC visual in the hero fits the holiday-weekend "treat yourself" build energy perfectly.
- **Category depth in sidebar** — Components & Storage, Gaming & VR, Networking all visible in one pass. No digging needed.

## What's weak

- **Hero is half off-screen on mobile** — the "MEM SA..." text is cropped, I can barely read what the sale is about. That's a real miss for the hero's job.
- **No price-off callout on the hero** — I can see there's a Memorial Sale but no "up to 20% off GPUs" or specific dollar hook. Memorial Day means nothing if I don't know what category is discounted.
- **No account/login state** — "Hello Select address" tells me I'm not logged in, which means no Egg Points balance, no saved wishlists, no member pricing. I have an account — this should surface that.
- **AMD combo module is tiny** — the product image thumbnails for the Ryzen and MSI board are small enough that I can't read the actual model numbers without tapping. For someone checking specs, that friction is real.
- **No urgency on Shell Shocker** — I know Shell Shocker has timers but I can't see a countdown above the fold. That's the whole mechanic — if I can't feel the clock ticking, I don't feel the pull.

## Recommendations

- **Crop the hero for mobile viewport** — either stack the "MEMORIAL SALE" headline above the image or give it a taller dedicated hero block so the copy isn't amputated off the right edge of the phone screen.
- **Add a % callout to the hero** — even just "Up to 25% off CPUs & GPUs" would convert the seasonal banner into a reason to tap.
- **Surface login state on load** — if I've shopped here before, show "Welcome back Nash — 4,200 Egg Points" in the header instead of "Hello Select address." That returning-customer hook is the lowest-effort engagement win on the table.
- **Add a Shell Shocker countdown chip in the nav strip** — just "03:42:17 left" next to the Shell Shocker tab. That single visual triggers the urgency loop that drives my daily check-in behavior.
