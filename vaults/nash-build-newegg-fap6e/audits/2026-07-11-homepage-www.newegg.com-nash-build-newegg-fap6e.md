# Homepage snapshot · www.newegg.com · 2026-07-11
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-11**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Newegg knows exactly who it's talking to. The second I landed here I saw "BUILD UP," Shell Shocker in the nav, and a PC Builder tab — this site is built for me. Right now it's pushing AMD combos and component deals, which is pretty much my entire personality. Classic Newegg energy: dense, parts-forward, no fluff.

## What's working

- **Shell Shocker in the top nav** — I check this daily. Seeing it as a pinned tab on mobile is exactly right. One tap away.
- **PC Builder tab** — For someone building a rig, this is the single most valuable tool on the site and it's front and center. Huge.
- **"BUILD UP" hero copy** — Hits different for the r/PCMasterRace crowd. Not generic "Shop Now" — it speaks to a builder identity.
- **Components & Storage as the first category** — The most important category for me is literally line one. No digging required.
- **AMD Combo deal card** — Combos are how you save money on a build. Showing it prominently is smart.

## What's weak

- **No primary CTA button above the fold** — The hero says "BUILD UP" but there's no visible button to act on it. Where do I tap?
- **Price displayed without clear savings** — "$1,378.5X" tells me the total but the combo savings/discount is cut off. Half the appeal of a combo deal is knowing how much you're saving vs. buying separately.
- **No reviews or ratings on the AMD combo card** — PC buyers live by reviews. Even a star count would build confidence.
- **No loyalty hook** — No egg rewards balance, no member-pricing badge, nothing. As a repeat Newegg shopper I'd want to see my rewards balance immediately.
- **Left nav open by default feels cluttered** — On mobile, the full category sidebar dominating the left half squishes the hero content into a narrow strip on the right.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop Deal" CTA button directly on the BUILD UP hero** — Even a small pill button ("Shop Components") turns a brand statement into an action.
- **Show the combo savings in the AMD card** — Display "Save $X vs. separate" or a crossed-out MSRP so the value is instant, not inferred.
- **Surface Egg Rewards balance in the header for logged-in users** — Even a small "🥚 1,240 pts" badge in the header gives me a reason to stay and spend.
- **Add star ratings + review count to featured combo cards** — One line of social proof on the AMD Ryzen listing would pull hesitant buyers in faster.
