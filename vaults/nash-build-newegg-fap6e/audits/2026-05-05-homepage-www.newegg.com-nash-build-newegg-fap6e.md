# Homepage snapshot · www.newegg.com · 2026-05-05
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-05**
## Executive summary

- Newegg shows up exactly how I expect — a wall of categories on the left, AMD combo deal trying to grab my eye on the right, and Shell Shocker + PC Builder sitting right in the top bar. This is clearly a parts store for builders, not a lifestyle brand, and right now it's pushing a processor/mobo combo which is very on-season for a spring build. Respect the hustle, but the mobile layout is cooked.
- **7/10**
- + Hero imagery speaks to persona: RGB gaming desk setup is visible, clearly aimed at builders not casual shoppers
- + Concrete offer above fold: AMD Ryzen 5 + ASR mobo combo shown at $315 (partially cut but legible)
- + Primary CTA above fold: "Build with it" button is present and visible
- + No render bugs: no broken images, no placeholder text, no overlapping elements

## What's working

- **Shell Shocker in the header bar**: I check this daily for daily-deal CPUs and GPUs — it's one tap from the top, exactly where it needs to be for deal hunters like me.
- **PC Builder in the header**: Newegg's biggest edge over Amazon is compatibility checking. Having it top-of-nav instead of buried three levels deep is the right call.
- **AMD Ryzen 5 + mobo combo with a price shown**: A processor/board bundle at $315 with a "Build with it" CTA is exactly the upsell format that works on r/PCMasterRace recommendation threads. Platform, price, action — all there.
- **Components & Storage listed first in the category nav**: No wading through Appliances or Home & Outdoors to find GPU categories. It's item one.
- **RGB gaming desk imagery**: The partial hero shows someone at an actual build with blue RGB lighting. That's not a stock photo of a laptop in a coffee shop — it's my aesthetic.

## What's weak

- **Nav sidebar crushing the mobile viewport**: The left-side category list takes up roughly 60% of the screen width on a phone. The hero and the AMD combo deal are crammed into the remaining 40%. This is a desktop two-column layout shoved onto a small screen and it's a mess.
- **"Combo up savi..." is truncated**: The main promo headline is literally cut off mid-word. I can't tell if it says "Combo up savings — save $50" or what. The value prop of the featured deal is hidden behind its own layout.
- **No countdown timer on Shell Shocker**: Shell Shockers are supposed to create urgency because they expire. If there's a timer running somewhere I can't see it, and that kills the whole mechanic.
- **Zero ratings on the AMD combo card**: I see a product image and a price but no star count. For a $315 combo buy I need to know 4.7★ (3,200 reviews) before I tap — without it, I'm just guessing.
- **No shipping info anywhere**: Newegg Premier and free shipping thresholds are real buying factors for me. Nothing above the fold tells me what shipping costs.

## Recommendations

- **Fix the mobile layout — collapse the sidebar into a hamburger**: On a phone this should be single-column with a hamburger nav, not a compressed two-column desktop layout. The combo deal and hero need full-width real estate.
- **Show a live countdown next to "Shell Shocker" in the header**: Even a small "Ends in 3h 42m" tag would turn that link from a menu item into an urgency trigger that makes me tap immediately.
- **Add star ratings to the featured combo card**: Drop a `4.6★ (2,104)` line under the AMD Ryzen 5 product image — that single data point moves me from "maybe later" to "add to cart."
- **Expand the "Combo up savi..." headline so it fits**: Show the full savings amount on the card — "Save $45 on this AMD combo" — even a dollar figure I can read without tapping through increases conversion on the featured deal.
