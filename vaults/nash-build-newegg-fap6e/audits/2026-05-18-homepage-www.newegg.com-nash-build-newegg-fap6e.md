# Homepage snapshot · www.newegg.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- Okay so Newegg is clearly in Memorial Day sale mode — I can see the hero is pushing what looks like a "MEM[ORIAL] SA[LE]" with a patriotic stars-and-dark-background aesthetic. The nav menu is fully expanded on load which is kinda aggressive on mobile, but the AMD combo deal section below the fold is exactly the kind of thing I come here for. This is unmistakably a PC hardware store doing a holiday sale push, and they're not shy about it.

## What's working

- **Shell Shocker + PC Builder in the top nav** — these two words are why I open Newegg. Seeing them in the nav bar immediately tells me this page knows its audience. One tap to either.
- **AMD Ryzen + ASRock combo at ~$340** — this is the exact "CPU + mobo bundle" deal I screenshot and post to r/BuildaPC. It has a brand logo, product names, and a "Build with it" CTA all in one compact block.
- **"Build with it" CTA copy** — not "Shop Now," not "Explore." "Build with it" is builder-native language. Whoever wrote that gets it.
- **Components & Storage as the #1 nav item** — first thing in the expanded menu, exactly where I'm headed.
- **Memorial Day hero timing** — seasonal relevance is real. GPU and CPU deals spike on Memorial Day weekend and Newegg knows it.

## What's weak

- **Nav expanded by default on mobile** — the full left-rail category list is open on load, which shoves the hero banner into a narrow right column. The "MEM[ORIAL] SA[LE]" hero is basically unreadable at this size. That's a lost impression.
- **No Shell Shocker deal visible in the viewport** — Shell Shocker is in the nav text but there's no active deal card showing me what today's shocker is. That's the whole reason I check Newegg daily and it's buried.
- **No GPU content visible** — the AMD deal shows a CPU + motherboard, but no GPU. GPUs are the #1 reason I'm here. Nothing calling me toward the graphics card section above the fold.
- **No loyalty/rewards hooks** — no Newegg Egg Points balance, no member-exclusive badge on the combo deal. If I'm a returning customer, there's nothing that acknowledges me.
- **Hero text illegible** — the hero copy is truncated and only "MEM" and "SA" are readable. The value prop of the Memorial Day sale is completely lost in the narrow right column the open nav forces it into.

## Recommendations

- **Default the mobile nav to closed** — let the hero breathe. The Memorial Day sale banner should land as a full-width impression, not a sliver. Hamburger tap opens the nav, that's the standard.
- **Pin today's Shell Shocker as a sticky banner or card** — it's a branded daily deal that I check Newegg specifically for. Surface the live deal SKU, countdown timer, and price right below the nav bar so I see it the second I land.
- **Add a GPU category shortcut above the fold** — a horizontal icon-chip row (GPU, CPU, Motherboard, RAM, SSD) would let me tap into my specific category in under a second without digging through the full nav tree.
- **Badge the AMD combo deal with savings %** — "$340" is good, but "Save $45 vs. individual" or "15% off bundle" would close me faster. Show me the math.
