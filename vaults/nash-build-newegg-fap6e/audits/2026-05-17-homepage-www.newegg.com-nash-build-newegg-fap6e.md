# Homepage snapshot · www.newegg.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Yo, this is like opening Newegg and having the nav drawer straight-up explode in my face — the hamburger menu is fully open, eating half my viewport. Behind it I can see a RAM/performance hero and an Intel combo deal at $959.97 with a "Build with it" CTA, which is exactly my language. This feels like home, but the mobile UX is a mess right now.

## What's working

- **"Shell Shocker" and "PC Builder" in the top nav bar** — I see these before anything else. That's Newegg speaking my dialect immediately. Shell Shocker is the reason I open this app on Black Friday.
- **Intel Combo deal with $959.97 price + "Build with it"** — a combo bundle at a named price with a build-oriented CTA is exactly the kind of deal I'm scanning for. That's a potential i7-13700K + mobo bundle situation and I'm already doing math.
- **"Components & Storage" as the first nav item** — zero hunting. One tap and I'm in my zone.
- **RAM/performance hero aesthetic** — the neon/dark hero with "RAM PER(FORMANCE)" copy is the right visual register. This is r/PCMasterRace energy.

## What's weak

- **Open nav drawer covering the hero** — whoever designed this mobile flow let the drawer stay open over the main content. I can't see the full promo or hero CTA because the category list is blocking it. This is friction before I even start.
- **No EggPoints / rewards balance visible** — I'm a returning Eggxpert reviewer. Where's my balance? A returning user hook should be front and center, not buried post-login somewhere.
- **Truncated promo copy ("Combo up savi...")** — the offer text is cut off. I shouldn't have to tap to know what the saving is. Show me the number.
- **No urgency signal on the Intel combo** — Shell Shocker implies time limits but there's no countdown or "ends tonight" on this deal. That's a missed conversion trigger for me.
- **No star ratings on the featured Intel products** — I live and die by review counts. Two product thumbnails visible, zero rating stars. That's a miss.

## Recommendations

- **Auto-dismiss the nav drawer on page load** — the mobile session should never open with the navigation drawer blocking content. Ship a fix that closes the drawer by default and shows the hero/deal on arrival.
- **Truncate-proof the combo offer copy** — "Combo up to $XX off" needs to fit in the card at mobile viewport. Hard-cap the string or use a shorter format like "Save $120 on Intel Combo."
- **Add EggPoints balance as a sticky pill in the header** — right next to the search icon, a small "🥚 2,340 pts" badge pulls returning users into loyalty engagement without any extra tap.
- **Drop a countdown timer on the Intel deal card** — even a "Deal ends in 14:32:00" under the price line would spike urgency and match the Shell Shocker energy Newegg is already built on.
