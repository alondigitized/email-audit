# Homepage snapshot · www.newegg.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Aight, this is clearly my site — Shell Shocker is right there in the nav, PC Builder tool is front and center, and there's an Intel combo deal already trying to get me to drop a grand. Newegg is flexing hard on the PC building angle, which is exactly what I'm here for. Right now the push is Intel builds with combo pricing, and the gaming/neon hero aesthetic is chef's kiss for the vibe.

## What's working

- **Shell Shocker in the top nav** — this is my daily ritual. The fact it's in the secondary nav bar means I can hit it in one tap without digging through menus. That's exactly how I use this site.
- **PC Builder tool prominent in nav** — it's not buried. For compatibility checks before pulling the trigger on a combo, this being in the header is huge.
- **Intel Combo deal module** — showing two actual products, a bundled price ($961.99), and a "Build with it" CTA is the right move. It's actionable. I can see what I'm getting and immediately know if I want to pursue it.
- **Left nav structure** — "Components & Storage" is the first item. "Gaming & VR" is right there too. My two most-used categories are above the fold without scrolling.
- **Dark/neon gaming hero** — the aesthetic reads PC gaming culture. Not some stock-photo laptop-on-a-desk energy. This looks like it was made for someone who posts build photos.

## What's weak

- **Hero is getting murdered by the nav on mobile** — the left category drawer is dominating the entire viewport. The hero is shoved into maybe 35% of the right side of the screen. The "RAM PER S" text is literally cut off. On mobile this is a bad look.
- **No Shell Shocker deal countdown visible** — the whole point of Shell Shocker is urgency, but from this screenshot there's no timer or "ends in X hours" visible. If I'm a daily deal scraper, I want that clock screaming at me.
- **Intel combo context is thin** — I can see "Intel C... 270K P..." and "AS U..." — the product names are truncated. If I don't already know what 270K means (I do, but casuals won't), this doesn't help conversion.
- **No trust or ratings visible** — no review count, no "Eggxpert reviewed" badge, no rating stars on the featured products. That stuff matters when you're dropping $961.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — as a PC parts buyer I always want to know if I'm hitting the free ship threshold. Not seeing it above the fold is a miss.

## Recommendations

- **Fix the mobile hero clipping** — the left nav and hero need to coexist properly. Either collapse the nav by default on mobile (hamburger only) or make the hero full-width beneath it. The truncated "RAM PER S" text is embarrassing.
- **Put a Shell Shocker countdown clock in the hero or a sticky banner** — a live timer ("Ends in 3h 42m") above the fold would immediately activate the urgency dopamine hit for daily deal hunters. That's the whole brand promise of Shell Shocker.
- **Show product names fully on the combo deal cards** — "Intel Core i9-14900K" is more motivating than "Intel C... 270K P..." Give me the full SKU so I can confirm compatibility in my head before clicking.
- **Add an Eggxpert review badge or star count to the featured combo** — even one "4.7 ★ (2,341 reviews)" line under the product would increase my confidence before tapping "Build with it."
