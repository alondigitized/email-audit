# Homepage snapshot · www.newegg.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Yo, so Newegg pulled up on my phone and honestly the first thing I see is a giant category menu taking up like 80% of the screen — it's like someone left the hamburger nav open. There's an Anniversary Sale countdown (3 days to go, 5/25/26 at 9am PT) and a partially visible Intel combo deal in the corner. This is clearly my store — PC Builder literally in the top nav — but the above-the-fold layout on mobile is a mess right now.

## What's working

- **Shell Shocker tab in the nav** — that's the first thing I look for every morning as a deal scraper; the fact it's a top-level tab tells me Newegg knows their audience
- **PC Builder in the top nav** — compatibility checking is the reason I use Newegg over Amazon; this being two taps from the home screen is exactly right
- **Anniversary countdown banner** — "3 Days — Unlock exclusive savings on 5/25/26 at 9am PT" is specific and credible, I'd actually set a calendar reminder for that
- **Intel Combo deal teaser** — bottom-right shows an Intel Core 270K combo bundle; combo deals are exactly how I stretch a GPU budget, this caught my eye immediately
- **Components & Storage as the first category** — no buried navigation, I can tap it in one shot

## What's weak

- **The entire layout looks like the category drawer is open** — on mobile this giant left-nav list is covering the actual homepage; there's no hero image, no featured product, just a text menu
- **"Learn more" CTA** — for an anniversary sale that's supposedly dropping exclusive savings, this is the blandest possible button; I have zero urgency to tap it
- **No specific discount teased** — "exclusive savings" tells me nothing; even "up to 30% off GPUs" would make me stop scrolling
- **Intel combo content is half off-screen** — the most interesting thing on the page (a real combo deal) is cut off on the right edge; I can see "Combo up saving..." but not the price or the full specs
- **No Egg Points / rewards hook** — I have points on this account and there's zero reminder of that; Newegg's loyalty program is genuinely good but invisible here
- **"Newegg Outl..." in the nav is truncated** — small thing but sloppy on mobile

## Recommendations

- **Collapse the category drawer by default on mobile** — show a hero banner for the Anniversary Sale with the actual lead offer (e.g., "30% off RTX 50-series" or "GPU + Mobo combos from $X"); the category list should live behind the hamburger icon, not auto-open
- **Change "Learn more" to "Shop Anniversary Deals"** — be specific about what I'm clicking into; urgency copy like "Preview drops before 5/25" would kill
- **Surface the Intel combo deal as a full-width card** — right now it's a sliver in the bottom corner; make it the secondary hero with the actual price shown
- **Add Egg Points balance for logged-in users** — even a small chip like "You have 2,400 Egg Points" would pull me toward converting because I'd want to burn them before the sale
