# Homepage snapshot · www.newegg.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- Newegg still feels like home — the Shell Shocker tab and PC Builder link in the nav bar tell me immediately this site knows who its people are. Right now it's pushing an Intel combo deal and some kind of earn-a-reward promo, but the menu drawer is eating the whole hero so most of that context is buried. It's a builder's site trying to be everything at once — Appliances, Health & Sports, Toys — which is a vibe shift I've never loved.
- **8/10**

## What's working

- **Shell Shocker in the top nav** — that's the first thing I clock every time I land here, it's basically Newegg's signature deal format and it's front and center where it belongs
- **PC Builder link** — right in the nav bar, no hunting; this is the compatibility tool I actually use when I'm speccing a build
- **Intel Combo savings card** — "Combo up savi..." with an Intel Core i5 and what looks like an ASUS board at $379.9 is exactly the bundle deal format I scrape for; the "Build with it" CTA is clean and action-oriented
- **Components & Storage at the top of the category list** — whoever ordered this nav knew the audience; I don't have to scroll past Appliances to find my stuff (even though Appliances is ironically also in there)
- **Newegg logo + search icon immediately accessible** — zero friction to jump into a part search

## What's weak

- **The expanded menu drawer is covering the hero** — I can only see a sliver of whatever promo banner is behind it; "Earn a [reward] when you c[heck out?]" is clipped and the image is basically invisible. That's a real content loss above the fold
- **"Offer valid: 4" is cut off** — I can see a date is there but can't read it; that kills urgency cues entirely
- **The hero image behind the menu** — dark background with some kind of neon/tech pattern, but I literally cannot tell what product it's for because the drawer is in the way
- **Appliances, Home & Outdoors, Health & Sports in the nav** — these dilute the builder identity; every time I see this I feel like Newegg is trying to be Amazon and it's not a flex
- **Price on the Intel combo is cut off at "$379.9"** — $379.99? $379.90? Small thing but sloppy on mobile

## Recommendations

- **Default to the collapsed menu on mobile** so the hero promo and Intel combo are actually visible above the fold — the open drawer is burying the two most conversion-relevant elements on the page
- **Pin the Shell Shocker timer** as a persistent sticky strip at the top with a countdown, like "Shell Shocker ends in 3:42:17" — that's the kind of urgency that gets me to stop scrolling Reddit and open a new tab
- **Collapse or deprioritize non-PC categories** (Appliances, Health & Sports, Home & Outdoors) into a single "More" bucket so the nav reads as a builder-first destination, not a general retailer
- **Make the Intel combo card full-width** with the complete price and board model visible — I need to see the full SKU at a glance to know if it's worth tapping
