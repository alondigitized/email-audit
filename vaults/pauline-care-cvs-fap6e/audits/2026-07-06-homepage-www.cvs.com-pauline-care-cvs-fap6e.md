# Homepage snapshot · www.cvs.com · 2026-07-06
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-06**
## Executive summary

- Well, I barely got to see the homepage because a big privacy notice wall slammed down right over the hero the second the page loaded. What I can see is the CVS I know — the red heart logo, "Extra Big Deals" in the nav, and a tagline that says "Healthier happens together." That tagline actually speaks to me a little as someone who's always running to CVS for my mom and myself, but I can't tell you what's on sale because the site is busy telling me about data collection instead of showing me a reason to shop.

## What's working

- **"Extra Big Deals" nav label** — as an ExtraCare devotee, that's the first word I'm looking for and it's right there in the nav strip. I'd tap it immediately.
- **"Manage prescript..." nav item** — I pick up prescriptions every week for my mom and myself. The fact that it's top nav (not buried in a hamburger) tells me CVS knows their customer.
- **Location banner ("816 E. Main Street — Open 24 hours")** — I genuinely appreciate knowing my store hours before I even scroll. That's useful at 10pm when I'm running out for mom's meds.
- **Hero tagline "Healthier happens together®"** — it's soft, but as a caregiver the word "together" registers. It doesn't feel like it's selling to a 25-year-old.
- **Search bar prominence** — it's front and center. When I know what I need, I search. Good placement for a task-oriented shopper like me.

## What's weak

- **The consent/privacy modal blocks the entire hero** — I opened the site to check deals, not read about data-sharing partnerships. This wall is the first thing I see and it makes me want to close the tab.
- **No ExtraBucks balance or ExtraCare member hook above the fold** — I check my ExtraBucks every week. If I'm logged in (or prompted to sign in with a benefit), that's the #1 thing that would hook me. Instead I get a generic "Sign in" link with no reason to bother.
- **"Use the CVS app" button takes my eye but sends me nowhere useful** — I'm already on the site on my phone. Pushing me to the app mid-browse feels like a detour, not a perk.
- **Zero promotional offer visible** — no "Save $10 on your $50 purchase," no ExtraBucks booster, nothing. The hero headline is warm but it doesn't tell me why today is the day to shop.
- **No caregiver-forward imagery** — the hero image is hidden under the modal, but nothing in the visible portion acknowledges that a lot of CVS shoppers are buying for someone else, not just themselves.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the modal on page load for signed-in users (or shrink it to a slim banner)** — if I've already accepted terms on a previous visit, don't cover the hero again. A thin "we use cookies — OK" strip at the bottom is enough and doesn't bury what I came to see.
- **Add an ExtraBucks CTA in the top bar for signed-in users** — something like "You have $4 ExtraBucks to redeem → Shop now" would make me tap immediately instead of bouncing.
- **Replace "Use the CVS app" button with a concrete deal CTA** — swap it for "This week's ExtraCare deals →" or "Weekly Ad →"; that's what brings me back every Sunday.
- **Surface a caregiver or family-care category strip above the fold** — a "Caring for a loved one?" module with links to pharmacy, vitamins, and personal care would speak directly to a customer like me without having to guess what I need.
