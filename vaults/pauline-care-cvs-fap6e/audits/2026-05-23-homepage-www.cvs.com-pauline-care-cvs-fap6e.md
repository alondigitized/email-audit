# Homepage snapshot · www.cvs.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- I opened the site and immediately got a data-privacy popup covering half the screen — before I could even see what CVS is offering me today. What I *can* see is the CVS logo, a search bar, "Manage prescriptions" across the top nav, and the first word of a hero that says "Embracing" before the modal cuts it off. CVS clearly knows I'm here for my prescriptions, but they haven't given me a single ExtraBucks hook or offer to make me feel like a valued ExtraCare member.

## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" in top nav** — that's the first thing I come for every week. It's right there, one tap. That's muscle memory for me.
- **"Schedule a Vaccine" shortcut** — as someone managing care for a parent, this is genuinely useful. Good placement.
- **Store info row** — 816 E. Main Street, Open 24 hours. Knowing my local store is open around the clock matters when I'm picking up a late prescription for my dad.
- **"Use the CVS app" button** — clear, specific, not buried. I use the app for ExtraBucks tracking, so this is a relevant nudge.

## What's weak

- **The consent modal fires immediately and blocks the hero** — before I can even read what's on sale, I have to deal with a wall of legalese about data sharing. That's a terrible first impression.
- **Zero ExtraCare presence above the fold** — I print those long receipts because I love my ExtraBucks. There's no rewards balance, no "You have $X in ExtraBucks," nothing. For an ExtraCare devotee, this page treats me like a stranger.
- **No offers, no prices, no promotions visible** — nothing that would make me feel like coming to CVS is smarter than running to Walgreens today.
- **"Embracing" — embracing what?** The hero headline is cut off. What I can see isn't a hook; it's a fragment.
- **Signed-out state with no incentive to sign in** — if you want me in my account so you can show me personalized deals, give me a reason. "Sign in to see your ExtraBucks" would do it.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss or delay the consent modal** — fire it after 3 seconds or on scroll, not as the literal first thing I see. Let me see the homepage first.
- **Add an ExtraCare balance strip at the top** (for signed-in users) or a "Sign in to see your ExtraBucks balance" CTA for signed-out — I should feel rewarded for being a loyal member the moment I land.
- **Surface at least one concrete offer above the fold** — "20% off vitamins for ExtraCare members this week" would have me tapping immediately.
- **Complete the hero headline** — "Embracing" isn't a hook. Whatever follows should be visible, and it should speak to health, caregiving, or savings — not fashion or beauty.
