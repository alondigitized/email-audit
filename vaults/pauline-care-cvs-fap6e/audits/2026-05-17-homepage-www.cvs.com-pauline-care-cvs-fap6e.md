# Homepage snapshot · www.cvs.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Oh, CVS — I know this store like the back of my hand, but right now I can barely see it. The first thing I get is a big gray privacy notice blocking nearly the entire page, and behind it I can just make out the word "Embracing" starting to load. The navigation up top is familiar — Manage Prescriptions, Schedule a Vaccine, my local store at 816 E. Main Street — so the bones are right for someone like me, but that modal wall is doing this homepage no favors.

## What's working

- **"Manage Prescriptions" shortcut** — that's the first thing in the quick-action row and it's exactly why I'm here half the time. One tap to my refills. That's smart placement.
- **"Schedule a Vaccine" with the syringe icon** — as a caregiver I am always thinking about my parent's shots. Nice that it's surfaced immediately without digging.
- **Local store address + hours** — "816 E. Main Street, Open 24 hours" is immediately reassuring. I like knowing my store is right there before I even scroll.
- **CVS branding + search bar** — clean, familiar, and the search bar is front and center for a shopper who knows exactly what she came for.

## What's weak

- **The consent/privacy modal is the entire above-the-fold experience** — "Improving your site experience" is a long block of legal text that shoves everything else off screen. I'm 55, not a lawyer. I just want to see what's on sale.
- **"Embracing" — that's all I get of the hero** — one word peeking out from behind the modal. Zero context, zero offer, zero incentive to stay.
- **No ExtraCare or ExtraBucks anywhere visible** — I check my bonus bucks balance every single week and there is not one loyalty callout above the fold. That is a miss for your most loyal shoppers.
- **"Use the CVS app" button** — I'm already on the website. Pushing me to download an app before I've seen a single product feels like the brand is trying to route me away from where I already am.
- **No offer, no promo strip, no %/$ off** — for a weekly shopper who plans her trip around ExtraBucks deals, a completely blank promo slate (thanks to the modal) gives me no reason to stay.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the cookie modal faster or make it a slim bottom banner** — a full-page overlay before I see a single product is a conversion killer. A one-tap bottom bar keeps it legal without hijacking my screen.
- **Pin an ExtraCare balance or "You have $X in ExtraBucks" nudge to the top nav for logged-in users** — I open this app specifically to track rewards; surface it the second I land.
- **Move a concrete promo strip above the hero** — even a single "This week: 20% off vitamins for ExtraCare members" would give me a reason to scroll past the fold instead of heading to the app.
- **Replace "Use the CVS app" with a loyalty sign-in hook** — "Sign in to see your ExtraBucks" speaks to me; "Download the app" while I'm already on the site just creates friction.
