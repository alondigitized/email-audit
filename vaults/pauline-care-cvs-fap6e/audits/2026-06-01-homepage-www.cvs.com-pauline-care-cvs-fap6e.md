# Homepage snapshot · www.cvs.com · 2026-06-01
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-01**
## Executive summary

- I opened CVS.com and immediately got smacked with a privacy consent wall that ate the entire screen. Behind it I can just barely make out the words "Healthy, stocked" — that's it. CVS is clearly trying to be a full health-and-wellness destination, and I do see "Manage prescriptions" sitting right there in the top nav which made my heart leap a little — that tab is basically my lifeline — but any goodwill evaporated the second that modal popped up blocking everything I came to see. This feels like a pharmacy that knows me but decided to put a clipboard in my face before I could even walk through the door.

## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" tab** is front and center in the shortcut strip — I can find my Rx workflow in one tap, which is exactly what gets me to CVS.com in the first place.
- **Store locator strip** ("816 E. MAIN STREET — Open 24 hours") is surfaced immediately. As a caregiver on unpredictable schedules, knowing my store is open 24 hours matters at 11pm.
- **Search bar with conversational placeholder** ("Search CVS or ask a question") feels approachable for a medium-tech user like me who might type "when does my prescription refill."
- **"Use the CVS app" CTA** is unambiguous — button is clearly labeled and tappable, even if it's asking me to go somewhere else entirely.

## What's weak

- **Full-screen consent modal** blocks the hero, the offer strip, the product rail, and presumably the ExtraCare callout — everything I came to see is gone. This is the single biggest problem on the page.
- **No ExtraBucks balance or ExtraCare sign-in prompt visible** — I check my bonus bucks every time I hit the site, and there is zero loyalty acknowledgment above the fold once the modal fires.
- **Hero text is truncated at "Healthy, stocked"** — I can't read the rest. Whatever campaign message CVS invested in is invisible.
- **"Summer" tab is present but vague** — what does Summer mean at CVS? Sunscreen? Allergy? Bug spray for Dad's porch? No imagery or descriptor to tell me.
- **Sign In is a bare text link**, not a prominent button — for a store whose whole value proposition is ExtraCare personalization, making login nearly invisible is a self-inflicted wound.

## Recommendations

- **Delay the consent modal by 3–5 seconds or trigger it on first scroll** — let me see the page before the clipboard comes out. A slim persistent banner at the bottom (GDPR-style) would accomplish the same legal goal without nuking the hero.
- **Pin an ExtraCare balance chip in the header for signed-in users** — something like "You have $3 ExtraBucks — redeem today" next to the cart icon would hook returning devotees like me instantly.
- **Promote "Manage prescriptions" from a tab to a hero-level CTA** — feature it in the hero with copy like "Your refills are ready" or "Refill in 60 seconds" during the weekly Rx window; that's the job I hire this site to do.
- **Give the Summer tab a one-line descriptor** — "Sunscreen, bug spray, first aid" tells me immediately if I need to tap it; the bare word "Summer" does not.
