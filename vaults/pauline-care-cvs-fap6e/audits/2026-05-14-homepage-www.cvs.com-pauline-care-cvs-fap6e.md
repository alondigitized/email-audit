# Homepage snapshot · www.cvs.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- I open CVS.com and the first thing that fills my screen is a cookie consent wall — I can barely see anything behind it except half a word and a quick-link row. CVS is clearly a health and pharmacy brand and I can see my store address and prescription shortcuts right at the top, which is great, but honestly the popup is doing everything it can to make me close the tab. Where's my ExtraBucks? Where's the sale? I shouldn't have to wrestle a legal notice to find out if my shampoo is on special this week.

## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" shortcut** — that horizontal quick-link row is the single best thing on this screen for someone like me. It's the main reason I open CVS.com in the first place.
- **Store locator with hours** — "101 E. Alameda Ave. Open, Closes 12:00 AM" is right there in a labeled bar. I appreciate knowing my store's status without hunting.
- **Search bar is prominent** — big, full-width, "Search CVS or ask a question" is approachable language, not techie.
- **"Sign in" is visible** — at least I know I can authenticate and presumably get to my ExtraCare account.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal kills the entire above-the-fold experience** — it covers the hero, any offers, any imagery. As a mobile user I can see almost nothing useful.
- **No ExtraCare / rewards hook visible** — this is my whole reason for brand loyalty. I want to see my Bonus Buck balance or an "ExtraCare members save X%" before I do anything else.
- **"Use the CVS app" button** — I already came to the website; pushing me to the app when I'm already here feels like misdirection.
- **No active promotional offer in sight** — no "this week's deals," no "save 25%," nothing that gives me a reason to keep scrolling.
- **Third quick-link tab is cut off** — I can see "Manage prescriptions" and "Schedule a Vaccine" but whatever is to the right is clipped. Nav shouldn't be truncating like this on mobile.

## Recommendations

- **Auto-dismiss or soft-present the cookie banner** — slide it up from the bottom as a slim bar, not a full-screen takeover. Every second it sits there is a second I'm not seeing your deals.
- **Surface ExtraCare balance or a member-pricing badge immediately after sign-in** — if I'm already signed in, show me "You have $3.00 in ExtraBucks to redeem" above the fold. That single line would make me stay.
- **Add a "This week's deals" strip directly below the quick-links row** — a horizontal scrollable tile row (Vitamins 30% off, BOGO shampoo, etc.) should live right below Manage Prescriptions so I don't need to scroll.
- **Fix the truncated quick-link nav on mobile** — make the tab row horizontally scrollable with a visible affordance, or consolidate to fit 3 links cleanly.
