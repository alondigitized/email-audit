# Homepage snapshot · www.cvs.com · 2026-05-05
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-05**
## Executive summary

- I open the CVS site and the first thing I get is a cookie-consent wall practically covering the whole screen — that is not the welcome I was hoping for as someone who checks in here at least once a week for my ExtraBucks and my dad's prescriptions. Once I look past the modal, the page is doing Mother's Day in a soft floral way that I actually appreciate, but there's not a single ExtraCare balance, bonus buck, or weekly deal in sight. It feels like CVS forgot its most loyal customers are standing right here at the door.
- **5/10**
- + Hero imagery (Mother's Day florals) explicitly speaks to a 55-year-old Gen X woman in early May — criterion 1
- + "Sign in" link is visible, technically a returning-shopper hook — criterion 2
- + "Use the CVS app" is a real button with real copy above the fold — criterion 4
- + Mother's Day navigation tab confirms the site is current for May 5 — criterion 8

## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" navigation tab** — it's the second pill in the top strip, right where I can see it without hunting. That's my single most common reason to visit CVS online and it's one tap away.
- **Mother's Day floral imagery** — soft pinks and greens above the hero text. It's May, I'm 55, and whether I'm being honored or buying for someone, that imagery lands emotionally.
- **Location + store hours bar** — showing "101 E. ALAMEDA AVE. · Open, Closes 12:00 AM" tells me my local store is open, which matters when I'm deciding whether to order online or just swing by.
- **"Use the CVS app" CTA** — I actually use the app specifically for ExtraBucks tracking, so prompting me to the app is not wrong-headed, even if this moment feels premature.

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal eating the hero** — it pops up immediately and swallows the headline and any below-the-fold content. I'm closing this before I even register what the page is about. That is real friction.
- **Zero ExtraCare visibility above the fold** — I am a rewards-first shopper. No ExtraBucks balance, no "Earn 2× on Mother's Day gifts," no "Your rewards: $X." CVS's biggest loyalty asset is invisible.
- **Hero tagline "Here for what" is incomplete** — the modal cuts off the rest of the sentence. Even if I'm generous and assume it says something meaningful, I can't read it. That's a dead first impression.
- **No offer, deal, or price signal** — not a single percent-off, BOGO, or ExtraBucks earn rate anywhere in this viewport. That's the whole reason I come to the site.
- **The third nav tab is cut off** — I can only read "Sch…" which might be Schedule a vaccine or MinuteClinic. As a caregiver, that matters to me, but I can't confirm it from this view.

## Recommendations

- **Surface ExtraCare immediately** — even a slim sticky bar below the nav that says "Sign in to see your $X ExtraBucks" would pull loyal customers like me in within seconds. Put the reward front and center.
- **Swap the cookie modal for a dismissible banner** — move the privacy notice to a slim bottom bar with a one-tap "Got it." Don't wall off your own homepage on the first interaction.
- **Add a concrete weekly deals callout above the fold** — a single line like "This week: 30% off vitamins + earn $5 ExtraBucks on $20 beauty" next to the hero would give me a reason to scroll. Right now there's nothing anchoring me.
- **Complete the hero headline** — even if it wraps to a second line, let me read the whole tagline. "Here for what" followed by nothing readable is not a hook.
