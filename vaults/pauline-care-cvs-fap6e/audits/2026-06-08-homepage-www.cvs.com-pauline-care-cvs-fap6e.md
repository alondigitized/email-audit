# Homepage snapshot · www.cvs.com · 2026-06-08
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-08**
## Executive summary

- I come to CVS.com every single week — I need to manage my mom's prescriptions, track my ExtraBucks, and grab household sundries — and the first thing I see is a big legal wall asking me to agree to data sharing. Behind it, there are flip flops and the headline "Here for it all," which tells me absolutely nothing about what I actually came for. CVS is clearly pushing a generic summer vibe, but there's no ExtraCare hook, no rewards balance, no "hey, welcome back" — nothing that says this store knows me at all.

## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" tab** — visible right in the nav strip. That's the one thing that makes me feel seen. One tap and I'm where I need to be for my mom's meds.
- **Store address + "Open 24 hours"** — I always check if the pharmacy is open before I drive over. Seeing it right there, with the hours, saves me a call.
- **"Use the CVS app" CTA** — it's blue, it's obvious, and since I already use the app for ExtraBucks tracking, it's not wrong, just not exciting.
- **Search bar** — big, easy to find, "ask a question" copy is friendly without being cutesy. I can work with this.

## What's weak

- **The cookie consent modal** — it's enormous, it's blocking the page, and it opens the session with a wall of legal text. I just want to check my ExtraBucks. This makes me want to close the tab immediately.
- **"Here for it all" hero** — this is the most non-committal headline I've seen. It says nothing about why I should stay on this page right now.
- **Flip flops as hero imagery** — I'm here for a caregiver's pharmacy run, not a beach vacation. This imagery is not for me.
- **No ExtraCare callout** — I have a card. I have bonus bucks. There is no acknowledgment that I exist as a loyalty member anywhere above the fold. For a program that CVS built its entire customer retention on, this is a real miss.
- **No offers, no savings, no urgency** — nothing says "there's a reason to shop today." Not even a banner strip.
- **"Sign in" is cold** — just text, no "Sign in for your ExtraBucks" or "Check your rewards." It doesn't motivate me.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the generic "Here for it all" hero with a loyalty-first message** — something like "Your ExtraBucks are waiting" with a visible rewards balance prompt or a "Sign in to see your deals" CTA. I come back because of ExtraCare; lead with it.
- **Surface a concrete offer strip above the fold** — a "20% off vitamins this week" or "Earn 10x ExtraBucks on prescriptions" banner below the nav would give me an immediate reason to stay and click.
- **Suppress the full-page privacy modal or shrink it to a bottom banner** — this is a known dark pattern; a slim footer bar would satisfy compliance without killing the entire first impression.
- **Swap flip flops for a lifestyle image that includes a 50-something woman** — maybe someone at the pharmacy counter or organizing a medicine cabinet. The imagery is currently speaking to someone half my age.
