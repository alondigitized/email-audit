# Homepage snapshot · www.cvs.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- I opened CVS.com and immediately got hit with a privacy cookie modal that practically eats the entire screen — I can barely see the top of a hero that says "Embracing" and nothing else. CVS feels like my pharmacy and I trust it, but right now this page is telling me nothing: no ExtraBucks balance, no deals, no reason to stay. If I weren't already a loyal customer I'd bounce.

## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" quick link** — that's the first thing I tap every week; it's right there at the top, which is exactly right for someone like me
- **Store locator with hours** — seeing "816 E. Main Street / Open 24 hours" immediately tells me my store is close and I can run in anytime; useful for a caregiver's unpredictable schedule
- **"Schedule a Vaccine" shortcut** — I manage my parent's immunizations; having that in the nav bar without digging is smart
- **CVS logo and branding** — clean, recognizable, I know where I am immediately

## What's weak

- **Cookie/privacy modal dominating the viewport** — this is the single biggest problem; it covers the hero, the offers, the products — everything I came to see is behind a wall of legalese I have to dismiss before I can shop
- **No ExtraBucks callout anywhere** — I am a devotee; the second I open this app I want to see my balance or at least a "Sign in to see your ExtraBucks" hook; there's nothing
- **"Sign in" is plain text, not a loyalty prompt** — it reads like a generic account link, not "Welcome back, check your bonus bucks"
- **Hero says "Embracing" and then nothing** — whatever that campaign is, it's invisible; I have no idea what CVS is pushing right now
- **No visible offers, promos, or beauty/health deals** — I came to check what's on sale this week; the page tells me absolutely zero

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss or shrink the cookie modal to a bottom banner** — a slim bar at the bottom that doesn't block content is the standard; this full-screen overlay is killing the entire above-the-fold experience
- **Add an ExtraBucks prompt in the sticky nav** — even signed-out, show "Sign in to see your ExtraBucks balance" next to the cart icon; that one line converts loyal shoppers instantly
- **Surface the weekly ad or one concrete promo before any other content** — I shop CVS for the deals; lead with "This week: 40% off vitamins + 2x ExtraBucks on beauty" and I'm already in my cart
- **Move "Manage prescriptions" to the top utility nav as an icon + label** — it's already there, but make it equal in visual weight to the search bar since it's the #1 job-to-be-done for a significant share of CVS's customer base
