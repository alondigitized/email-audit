# Homepage snapshot · www.cvs.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- CVS is clearly my pharmacy — I'm there every week for prescriptions and sundries — but this homepage is not exactly rolling out the welcome mat. What I'm seeing is a privacy consent modal eating up half my screen before I can even read what the hero is trying to say, a location card for my local store, and a Mother's Day tab in the nav. It feels like a utility more than a destination, and whoever decided to lead with a data-collection disclaimer needs to hear from me directly.

## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" tab** — Right there in the nav bar, first thing I see after the search box. That's me, every Tuesday. One tap.
- **Mother's Day nav category** — Timely. I need a gift for my mother-in-law, and it's top of mind.
- **Local store card ("101 E. Alameda Ave. — Open, Closes 12:00 AM")** — CVS knows where I am and confirms my store is open. That's actually useful when I'm doing a quick pickup run at 11pm after my dad's evening meds.
- **Search bar front and center** — I know what I need most of the time. "Search CVS or ask a question" is a reasonable invite.

## What's weak

- **The privacy consent modal** — "Improving your site experience" takes up most of the visible screen and shoves my actual shopping experience out of frame. I do not want to read three paragraphs about third-party data partners before I can see a single product. I'm here to check my ExtraBucks, not sign a GDPR waiver.
- **Hero copy is unreadable** — "Here for what" is all I get. The modal cuts it off. Whatever CVS paid to write that headline, I can't benefit from it.
- **Zero ExtraCare presence** — I live for my ExtraBucks. My card is linked. Where's my balance? Where's "Welcome back, Pauline"? Where's the ExtraCare tab? This is the most loyalty-heavy pharmacy in America and I see nothing about my rewards status above the fold.
- **"Use the CVS app" CTA** — I already have the app. This button is wasted real estate pushing me somewhere I already am.
- **No offer, no hook** — Nothing says "this week, $5 off $20 in health" or "ExtraBucks bonus on vitamins." Totally flat on promotions.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the modal or make it a banner, not a full overlay.** Move the privacy notice to a slim bottom bar so the hero is immediately visible when I land. One tap to close, not a wall of legalese.
- **Add an ExtraCare sign-in prompt in the hero zone.** Something like "Sign in to see your ExtraBucks balance" with my rewards total after auth. I open this site for that exact number. Make it the first thing I see.
- **Swap the "Use the CVS app" CTA for a concrete weekly offer.** "This week: earn 2× ExtraBucks on vitamins — ends Saturday." That's what gets me to tap.
- **Surface a "Manage Rx refills" shortcut card** below the hero — not buried in the nav tab. A button that says "Refill a prescription" with my last-filled meds pre-populated would make this page a daily habit.
