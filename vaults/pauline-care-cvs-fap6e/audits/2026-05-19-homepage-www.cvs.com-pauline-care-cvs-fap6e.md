# Homepage snapshot · www.cvs.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- I opened CVS.com and immediately hit a wall — a full-screen privacy/data consent modal is sitting right on top of whatever the homepage is trying to say. The only thing I can actually read below that popup is the word "Embracing," so I have no idea what the hero campaign is. The top chrome is all business: pharmacy shortcuts, a vaccine scheduler, my nearest store hours — which is honestly more useful to me than any banner sale. But CVS knows I'm here every week, and there is not a single ExtraBucks number, rewards balance, or "Welcome back" anywhere on this screen.

## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" shortcut bar** — I can tap straight to my Rx refills without digging through a menu. For a weekly pharmacy run, that is the most valuable pixel on this page.
- **"Schedule a Vaccine" shortcut** — Caregiver life means I'm booking flu shots and COVID boosters for my mom too. Seeing this immediately signals CVS understands that health management — not just beauty deals — is what brings me here.
- **Store location with "Open 24 hours"** — It auto-detected my store and surfaced the hours. That's practical and reassuring, especially on a night I'm running a late pickup.
- **Prominent search bar** — "Search CVS or ask a question" is clean and prominent. When I know exactly what I need (a specific OTC med, a brand of compression socks for my mom), I go straight there.
- **"Sign in" in the top nav** — It's where I expect it, and it's not buried.

## What's weak

- **Full-screen consent modal blocking the entire page** — This is the biggest problem. I opened CVS.com and I'm reading a legal disclosure about third-party data partners before I can see a single product or offer. I'm not a 22-year-old who clicks "Close" immediately; I actually read these things, and now I'm annoyed before I've seen anything.
- **Zero ExtraBucks or ExtraCare hook** — I have a loyalty card number I know by heart. I print the long receipt. I track my Bonus Bucks like a coupon binder. There is nothing on this screen that acknowledges I exist as a member — no balance, no "You have $X to redeem," no member-only pricing badge. That's a missed layup.
- **Hero is completely invisible** — The word "Embracing" is the only thing peeking out below the modal. I don't know if there's a Memorial Day sale, a skincare promotion, or a photo of a 30-year-old influencer that has nothing to do with me.
- **"Use the CVS app" CTA is self-defeating** — I already have the app. CVS keeps pushing the app on people who are already on their website. If I wanted the app, I'd be using the app.
- **No deals, no offers, no ExtraBucks events visible** — Weekly CVS shoppers come for the deals. I clip coupons. I wait for the 40%-off cosmetics week. There is nothing here tempting me to browse.

## Recommendations

- **Make the consent modal dismissible with one tap and shrink it to a bottom banner** — A full-screen overlay on mobile is hostile. Move it to a slim bottom strip with a single "Got it" tap; the hero should be the first thing I see, not a data-sharing disclosure.
- **Surface my ExtraCare balance and any expiring Bonus Bucks immediately after sign-in** — A personalized "You have $7.00 in ExtraBucks — expires Sunday" strip above the fold would make me convert on the spot. This is table stakes for a loyalty program this size.
- **Add a "Weekly Ad" or "This week's deals" shortcut tile in the same bar as "Manage prescriptions"** — Put it right alongside the pharmacy shortcuts. That is the second reason I open CVS.com.
- **Replace or caption "Use the CVS app" CTA with a contextual action** — If I'm already on the website, send me to the Weekly Ad, or to my ExtraCare dashboard. "See your ExtraBucks" is a better button than "Use the CVS app."
