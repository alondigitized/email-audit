# Homepage snapshot · www.cvs.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- CVS is leading with "Here for all the ways you care" — and honestly, as someone who is literally the caregiver in my household, that tagline hits closer to home than most. The pharmacy tab is right up front, and Mother's Day is the first featured category, which feels timely. But before I could even look at any of that, a big privacy consent wall slammed down and took over my whole screen, which is a rough first impression when I just want to check ExtraBucks.
- **7/10**

## What's working

- **"Here for all the ways you care" hero headline** — that's me. Caregiver, ExtraCare member, weekly pharmacy run. CVS is one of very few retailers that could actually write a headline I take personally, and they did.
- **"Manage prescriptions" shortcut in the nav** — that's the first thing I actually came here for. Seeing it right there in the horizontal tab strip without having to dig into a menu is genuinely useful.
- **Mother's Day tab prominently featured** — I'm 55, which means I either am a mother, have one, or both. Relevant right now. Good timing on the merchandising.
- **Store location and hours shown up top** — knowing my local store closes at midnight and I can click "Use the CVS app" to go deeper is the kind of practical info I appreciate. I don't need lifestyle, I need logistics.
- **Search bar is front and center** — "Search CVS or ask a question" — the "or ask a question" framing is friendly and low-pressure. Good for someone like me who's often searching for a specific medication or product name I'm not sure how to spell.

## What's weak

- **The privacy consent modal hijacks the entire above-the-fold experience** — A giant "Improving your site experience" overlay with dense legal text about data sharing with third parties and research partners is the first thing that ate my screen. I'm a weekly CVS shopper. I don't need to feel surveilled before I can see a single product.
- **No ExtraCare / ExtraBucks callout visible at all** — I came here partly to track my bonus bucks before my next in-store trip. There's zero loyalty hook visible on this viewport. That's a miss for a rewards program as strong as ExtraCare.
- **Hero image is almost entirely hidden** — Whatever lifestyle or product visual is below the headline is totally obscured by the privacy modal. I have no idea what they're actually promoting.
- **"Sign in" is tiny text in the top right** — For someone like me who wants to sign in immediately to see my ExtraCare account, this feels undersized and easy to miss. A pill button or badge showing my rewards status would serve me much better.
- **No visible ExtraBucks balance or loyalty prompt** — Once you dismiss the modal (which I'd need to do first), there's still no visible rewards hook in this viewport. CVS's biggest loyalty differentiator isn't surfaced at all.

## Recommendations

- **Move the ExtraCare sign-in prompt to a sticky banner or inline card below the hero** — Something like "Sign in to see your ExtraBucks balance" with a visible dollar amount placeholder. I came here for that. Make it front and center.
- **Reduce the privacy modal to a slim bottom banner** — Move the consent notice to a slim, dismissible strip at the bottom of the screen the way most modern apps handle it. The full-screen takeover is killing the hero before I even see it.
- **Show the hero image** — Whatever is partially visible under that modal (looks like an in-store or lifestyle shot) should be front and center. Swap any generic runway image for a warm caregiver/family scene that mirrors the "all the ways you care" copy.
- **Add a "Refill a prescription" quick-action button above the fold** — "Manage prescriptions" in the nav is good, but a tappable pill button with that label right under the hero would eliminate a tap for my most frequent task.
