# Homepage snapshot · www.cvs.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- I opened CVS.com and what I got was a wall of legal text before I could see anything else. There's a cookie-consent popup taking up the bottom half of my screen, and all I can see of the actual homepage is the word "Embracing" peeking out above it. For a weekly Rx customer who just wants to check her ExtraBucks balance or refill a prescription, this is not a warm welcome — it feels like the lawyers own the front door.

## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" in the top nav strip** — that's the first thing I came here for, and it's right there, one tap. Good.
- **"Schedule a Vaccine" link** — relevant for me as a caregiver managing an aging parent's health. Practical, not fluffy.
- **Store locator showing "816 E. Main Street / Open 24 hours"** — I like knowing my store is open right now. That's useful context above the fold.
- **"Use the CVS app" CTA** — clear button, easy to read, not buried. I already have the app but a new customer would know what to do.

## What's weak

- **The consent modal is eating the homepage.** It's the first thing I see. It's dense legal prose about data sharing and third-party marketing partners. I shouldn't have to read a privacy disclaimer before I see a single product or offer.
- **No ExtraBucks or ExtraCare visibility.** I'm a loyalty devotee — my first question is always "what bonus bucks do I have?" Not a whisper of it above the fold.
- **No offer, no deal, no reason to stay.** Zero promotional content visible. I don't know if there's a sale, what's on clearance, or what my Extra Savings this week are.
- **"Embracing" — and then nothing.** The hero headline is cut off and covered. Whatever message CVS is trying to send, it's not landing.
- **Sign In has no rewards context.** It just says "Sign In" — no "Sign in to see your ExtraBucks" or "X rewards waiting." That's a missed hook for someone like me.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the consent modal faster — or don't block content with it.** Put a persistent banner at the bottom (GDPR-style) instead of a modal that covers the hero. I came to shop, not sign legal forms.
- **Surface the ExtraBucks balance in the sign-in CTA.** Change "Sign In" to "Sign In — See Your ExtraBucks" or add a loyalty chip next to it. That's the number one reason ExtraCare members open the site.
- **Add a weekly-deal strip above the fold**, even if it's just "This week: 25% off vitamins + earn 2x ExtraBucks." One concrete, scannable offer banner would give me a reason to keep scrolling.
- **Make the hero visible on load.** Whatever "Embracing…" completes to, let me see it before the consent overlay fires — or at minimum reduce the modal to a slim bottom banner so the hero imagery can breathe.
