# Homepage snapshot · www.cvs.com · 2026-07-20
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-20**
## Executive summary

- CVS is leaning into a generic summer campaign, which is fine, but I opened this expecting to see my ExtraBucks balance or at least a loyalty nudge — and instead I got a wall of privacy-policy text before I could even see what they're selling. The hero line is pleasant but it doesn't say anything to me as someone who's here every week for my dad's prescriptions and my own ExtraCare points. Feels like the site forgot I exist.

## What's working

- **"GLP-1 Support" nav tab** — I notice this immediately as someone managing a parent's health. That's a smart pharmacy hook in the horizontal nav, even if it's small.
- **"Manage prescripti..." nav item** — the tab is cut off but I can tell it's Rx management. That's my weekly errand, so I'd tap that.
- **Store locator with "Open 24 hours"** — seeing my actual nearby store address and hours builds immediate local trust. Useful.
- **Search bar is prominent** — "Search CVS or ask a question" is approachable copy; the search bar is front-and-center which works for me when I know exactly what I need.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal** — it's eating the bottom half of my screen and it's walls of legal text. For someone my age who just wants to see if Boost is on sale this week, this is a speed bump that makes me want to put my phone down.
- **No ExtraCare / ExtraBucks hook above the fold** — this is CVS's biggest differentiator and it's completely absent. My loyalty balance should be the first thing I see when I land here.
- **Hero copy "Here for where summer takes you"** — generic to the point of meaningless. Where summer takes me is the pharmacy drive-through. This copy is aimed at a 28-year-old road-tripper, not me.
- **"Sign in" with no loyalty framing** — it just says "Sign in." If it said "Sign in to see your ExtraBucks," I'd tap it before anything else.
- **No visible offer** — not one sale, not one deal, not a free shipping threshold. The entire above-the-fold is navigation and a modal. Nothing is pulling me into the store.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the hero copy** with something that acknowledges repeat shoppers: "Your ExtraCare perks are waiting — sign in to see this week's deals" and put a clear ExtraBucks login prompt where the generic summer headline is now.
- **Move the "Manage Prescriptions" tab to the first position** in the horizontal nav — or give it a persistent icon in the header alongside cart — since refills are the primary reason weekly shoppers like me open this site.
- **Reduce the cookie modal to a slim banner** at the bottom of the screen instead of a mid-page takeover. The current version blocks discovery for users who haven't dismissed it yet.
- **Add a visible offer strip** directly under the nav — even one line: "ExtraCare members save 20% on vitamins this week" — so there's a reason to keep scrolling.
