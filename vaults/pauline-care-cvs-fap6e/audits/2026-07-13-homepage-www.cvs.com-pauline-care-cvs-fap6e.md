# Homepage snapshot · www.cvs.com · 2026-07-13
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-13**
## Executive summary

- This is CVS — my pharmacy, my Saturday routine — but right now my first impression is a wall of legal text. A privacy consent modal has taken over practically the whole screen before I can even see what they're promoting. Behind it I can just make out the headline "Let's make healthier happen together™" and some quick-nav pills, but honestly I can't shop from a cookie disclosure. CVS looks clean and organized in the sliver I can see, and I notice a "Manage prescriptions" shortcut which I use constantly as a caregiver, but the modal is doing them no favors right now.

## What's working

- **"Manage prescripti[ons]" quick-nav pill** — as someone juggling my own Rx plus my mother's, this shortcut earns its real estate immediately. One tap and I'm in the right place.
- **Store locator row ("816 E. MAIN STREET · Open 24 hours")** — knowing my store is open all night matters when I'm doing a late-night caregiver run for supplies.
- **"Use the CVS app" button** — I already use the app for ExtraBucks tracking, so this cross-promotes the tool I rely on most.
- **"GLP-1 Support" shortcut** — health-forward navigation shows CVS is positioning as a full healthcare destination, not just a convenience store.
- **Clean search bar** — "Search CVS or ask a question" is a practical entry point; I know exactly what I'm looking for most of the time.

## What's weak

- **The privacy consent modal eating the entire viewport** — this is the worst possible first impression. I'm 55, I'm not patient with legal walls that block the thing I came to do. The "Close" button is small and easy to miss.
- **Zero ExtraCare visibility** — I am literally an ExtraCare devotee. My whole relationship with CVS is built on that program. There is no rewards balance, no "Your ExtraBucks," no member-pricing badge anywhere I can see.
- **No offer above the fold** — not a single price, percentage, or promotion is visible. The whole page above the scroll line is brand messaging and friction.
- **"Let's make healthier happen together™" is thin** — it's warm but totally generic. It could be Walgreens, it could be a health insurance company. Nothing here says "your weekly ExtraCare run starts here."
- **No hero imagery at all** — the modal covers whatever product or lifestyle visual was supposed to anchor this page. I'm reading a tagline with no context.

## Recommendations

- **Shrink or delay the privacy modal** — surface it as a slim bottom banner that doesn't block content, or trigger it after the first scroll. Don't lead with legal copy.
- **Put my ExtraCare balance in the header** — I'm signed in (or sign-in is one tap away). Show me "You have $4.00 in ExtraBucks" right next to the cart icon; that's the hook that makes me stay and shop.
- **Swap the generic tagline hero for a loyalty-first hero** — something like "Your ExtraBucks are ready. Here's what's on sale this week." with a specific weekly offer would convert people like me immediately.
- **Surface a caregiver or "Family Health" quick-nav pill** — alongside Summer and GLP-1, a "Caregiving Essentials" or "Senior Care" shortcut would signal that CVS sees me and understands my weekly mission.
