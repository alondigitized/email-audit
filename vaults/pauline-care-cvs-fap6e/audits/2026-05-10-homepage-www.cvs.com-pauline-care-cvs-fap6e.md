# Homepage snapshot · www.cvs.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- I came to CVS.com to check my ExtraBucks and maybe pick up my dad's refills online, and the first thing I'm greeted with is a full-screen data-consent wall sitting on top of whatever they're trying to sell me. CVS is a brand I trust — I'm in the store every single week — but this homepage is burying itself before it even says hello. Whatever "Embracing…" means, I can't finish reading it.

## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" quick link** — it's right there in the top nav strip, no hunting required. As someone juggling my own prescriptions and my dad's, this is the one thing I immediately recognized as mine.
- **"Schedule a Vaccine" shortcut** — Gen X caregivers are exactly the audience for this. It belongs right where it is.
- **Store location + hours bar** — "101 E. Alameda Ave., Open, Closes 12:00 AM" is exactly what I'd glance at before a late-night run. Useful at a glance.
- **CVS brand mark** — the red heart logo is immediately recognizable. No confusion about where I am.

## What's weak

- **The cookie consent modal** — it covers almost the entire above-the-fold experience. As someone with medium tech comfort, my first instinct is "what is this pop-up, is something wrong?" Not a good first impression.
- **No ExtraCare hook visible** — I live for my ExtraBucks. If your most loyal customer segment (weekly in-store shoppers tied to the card) can't see a single rewards mention on load, you've missed your moment.
- **"Embracing…" hero is truncated and invisible** — whatever the campaign message is, it's completely inaccessible because of the overlay. That's a wasted hero slot.
- **"Use the CVS app" is the only real CTA visible** — and it's asking me to leave the browser I'm already in. That's friction, not conversion.
- **Sign in is in the corner, not prominent** — for a returning ExtraCare member, there's no warm welcome or "sign in to see your ExtraBucks" nudge. The page treats me like a stranger.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the full-page cookie modal on return visits** — if I've been to CVS.com before, there's no reason to wall me off again. At minimum, make the "Close" button the obvious primary action, not a small text link in the corner.
- **Surface ExtraBucks balance as the hero hook for returning members** — a banner like "You have $4.00 ExtraBucks ready to redeem" above the fold would get me to tap immediately. That's free, personalized, and deeply relevant.
- **Add a "Refill a prescription" CTA button** alongside "Manage prescriptions" — verb-first, action-oriented copy converts better than a noun label for this audience.
- **Move "Sign In" into a more prominent above-the-fold position** with a loyalty callout: "Sign in to see your ExtraBucks & prescription history." That's the single highest-value action for weekly regulars like me.
