# Homepage snapshot · www.cvs.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- I came here to check my ExtraBucks and maybe pick up something for my mom's next visit, and the first thing I see is a big privacy consent box blocking the whole page. The hero only gets out the word "Embracing" before the modal covers everything. CVS is my pharmacy — I trust it — but this welcome mat is a mess right now.

## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" shortcut** — it is right below the search bar, one tap. As someone picking up Rx every week, that is exactly where it should be.
- **"Schedule a Vaccine" link** — practical, visible, very on-brand for a 55-year-old who tracks flu shots and shingles boosters for herself and her parent.
- **Store address + "Open 24 hours"** — I appreciate knowing my location is already recognized. The green "Open 24 hours" is genuinely reassuring for a caregiver who sometimes makes late runs.
- **Search bar prominence** — large, easy to tap, says "Search CVS or ask a question," which feels helpful rather than clinical.

## What's weak

- **The privacy consent modal** — it takes up roughly two-thirds of the visible screen and completely buries the hero, any offer, and any loyalty messaging. This is the single biggest friction point. I cannot see the rest of the page until I tap "Close."
- **Hero is truncated to one word** — "Embracing" is all I get. I have no idea what the campaign is, what product is being pushed, or why I should care.
- **No ExtraCare / ExtraBucks visibility** — I came here partly to check my balance. There is not a single loyalty signal above the fold — no "Sign in to see your ExtraBucks," nothing. For a member like me, that feels like the site doesn't know me.
- **"Sign in" is a plain text link** — it is easy to miss. For a returning ExtraCare member, this should be a button with a rewards hook, not a quiet corner link.
- **Cart shows 0 with no nudge** — a "You have items saved" or "Your ExtraBucks expire soon" tooltip here would pull me in immediately. It is a wasted touch point.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the full-bleed modal or shrink it to a slim banner** — a one-line cookie notice at the bottom (like most modern sites use) would stop blocking the hero entirely. Ship this next week.
- **Add an ExtraCare balance chip near "Sign in"** — even a prompt like "Sign in to check your ExtraBucks balance" in a teal pill next to the cart icon would immediately signal that this site knows me.
- **Make "Embracing [whatever]" visible** — if the hero copy is "Embracing caregiving" or "Embracing wellness," that lands perfectly for me. Just let me see it.
- **Swap the generic "Use the CVS app" button for a loyalty hook** — something like "App: track ExtraBucks + digital coupons" tells me *why* to download it, not just that it exists.
