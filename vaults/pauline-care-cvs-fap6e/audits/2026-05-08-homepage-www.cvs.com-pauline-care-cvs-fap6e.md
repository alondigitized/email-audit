# Homepage snapshot · www.cvs.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- CVS feels like home the second I see that red heart logo — I know exactly where I am. Right now they're leaning into Mother's Day, which makes sense for early May, but before I can even read the hero I get smacked with a full-screen data-consent modal that buries whatever they were trying to say. It's a pharmacy brand that clearly knows its digital audience is there for prescriptions and deals, but this first impression is a mess of competing priorities.

## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" tab** — it's the second item in the horizontal quick-link strip, right below the search bar, one tap away. For someone like me who's picking up meds for my dad every week, that's exactly where it needs to be.
- **Store address + hours** — "101 E. ALAMEDA AVE. · Open, Closes 12:00 AM" with the "Use the CVS app" button right next to it. I can confirm my store is open before I even scroll. Practical and useful.
- **Mother's Day tab** — seasonally correct, and as someone shopping for both my aging parent and myself, gift ideas from the pharmacy/beauty aisle are genuinely relevant.
- **Search bar prominence** — "Search CVS or ask a question" is front and center. For medium-tech-comfort me, a big obvious search box reduces anxiety.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal covering ~40% of the viewport** — it obliterates the hero before I even know what they're selling. I'm 55, not a privacy lawyer. I just want to find my ExtraBucks.
- **Hero copy "Here for what"** — cut off mid-sentence. I can't tell what they're promising me. That's a bad first impression on mobile.
- **Zero ExtraCare presence above the fold** — I am their loyalty program's ideal customer. I print the long receipt. I redeem every Bonus Buck. And there is not one mention of ExtraCare, ExtraBucks, or my rewards balance before scrolling. That's a miss.
- **Floral image** — pretty but completely generic. It could be from any greeting card company. Nothing here says "pharmacy" or "your health" or "we know you."
- **No offer, no price signal** — not a single deal is visible. CVS runs promotions constantly. Why can't I see one right now?

## Recommendations

- **Surface the ExtraBucks balance in the header for signed-in users** — even a small "You have $4.00 in ExtraBucks" badge next to the Sign In link would make me feel seen and immediately push me toward conversion.
- **Dismiss or minimize the cookie modal** — move it to a slim bottom banner or a one-tap drawer. Right now it covers the hero and makes the page feel broken on mobile.
- **Complete the hero headline** — whatever "Here for what" is supposed to say, make sure the full line fits in the viewport. On a phone screen, a truncated tagline looks like a layout bug.
- **Add a visible deal strip** — even a simple "ExtraCare member deal: 25% off vitamins this week" banner between the search bar and the hero would give me a reason to keep scrolling immediately.
