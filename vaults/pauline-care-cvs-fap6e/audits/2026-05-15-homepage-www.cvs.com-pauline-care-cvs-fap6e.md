# Homepage snapshot · www.cvs.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- I pulled up CVS on my phone and the first thing I see is a big privacy notice covering half the screen — I can barely make out the word "Embracing" behind it, which tells me nothing. CVS is clearly a pharmacy-first brand, and I can see the prescription shortcuts right away which matters to me, but the cookie modal is eating my whole above-the-fold experience before I even get to whatever they're promoting.

## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" shortcut strip** — this is the first thing relevant to me as a weekly Rx customer and caregiver; it's right at the top, one tap
- **"Schedule a Vaccine" shortcut** — at 55, I track boosters for both me and my parent; seeing it in the top strip without digging is genuinely useful
- **Store locator / "Open 24 hours"** — I check this regularly; knowing my CVS is open overnight is real peace of mind
- **"Sign in" placement** — top right, easy to find when I want to check my ExtraBucks balance

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal blocking everything** — this is the biggest problem on the screen; it's covering the hero, the offers, all of it; I have to deal with this before I see a single product
- **No ExtraCare / ExtraBucks hook visible** — I'm a devotee; showing me my rewards balance or a "Clip your ExtraBucks" CTA would have me tapping immediately; there's none of that
- **No visible offer or promotion** — not a single "% off," "bonus bucks," or deal is showing; for a weekly shopper like me that's what gets me to move
- **"Use the CVS app" CTA feels like a deflection** — I came to the website; sending me to an app download isn't what I want right now, I want deals or my Rx status
- **Hero text "Embracing" is orphaned** — whatever campaign this is, I can't read it; the word alone means nothing

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss or shrink the consent modal** — show it as a slim bottom banner instead of a full-screen takeover; I should see the hero and at least one offer the moment the page loads
- **Surface ExtraCare balance and expiring bonus bucks in the signed-out state** — a "Sign in to see your ExtraBucks" badge above the fold would have me tapping immediately
- **Replace "Use the CVS app" with a deals or pharmacy CTA** — something like "View this week's circular" or "Refill a prescription" maps to why I actually came here
- **Add a caregiver-oriented offer strip** — even a small "Stock up on health essentials — ExtraBucks rewards on vitamins & supplements" banner would feel like CVS sees me
