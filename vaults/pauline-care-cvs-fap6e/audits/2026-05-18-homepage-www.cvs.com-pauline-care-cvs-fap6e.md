# Homepage snapshot · www.cvs.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- I open CVS.com on my phone and the first thing I see — before I even get to whatever they're promoting — is a wall of legal text about data collection covering almost the entire screen. The word "Embracing" is peeking out from behind it like a shy kid at a school play. This is a pharmacy site I visit weekly and I can't even see the deal of the day without closing a modal first. At least "Manage prescriptions" is right there in the quick-strip, which is the one thing that makes me feel like they know who they're selling to.

## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" quick-link** — This is the first thing I actually need every week. It's right there above the fold in the action strip, and for a caregiver managing refills for a parent, that's a genuine gift.
- **"Schedule a Vaccine" shortcut** — Smart. Health-forward framing in the quick-strip feels relevant to someone in my life stage who's thinking about mom's flu shot and my own.
- **Store locator with hours** — "816 E. MAIN STREET — Open 24 hours" is exactly the kind of information I need when I'm running a late errand. No hunting for it.
- **Search bar is prominent and welcoming** — "Search CVS or ask a question" — the conversational copy is friendly without being cutesy. Medium-tech me appreciates that.

## What's weak

- **The cookie modal is the whole page** — A wall of dense legal language ("we may record your site interactions... trusted third parties... marketing, analytics and research partners") is the first thing I read. I came here for ExtraBucks, not a privacy seminar.
- **Zero ExtraCare presence above the fold** — I am a loyalty devotee. I clip coupons in the app and I print the long receipt on purpose. There is not a single mention of ExtraBucks, ExtraCare, or my rewards balance anywhere I can see. That is a miss for a returning customer.
- **No visible offer at all** — No percentage off, no "20% for CarePass members," no weekly deal. Nothing. A pharmacy-chain homepage that shows me zero promotions above the fold is leaving money on the table.
- **"Use the CVS app" redirect feels like a deflection** — I'm already on the website. Pushing me to the app as the main CTA before I've seen a single product feels like a funnel designed around someone else's KPIs, not my shopping.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the cookie modal for returning sessions** — If I have cookies from a prior visit (which I likely do as a weekly shopper), skip the modal. Show it only to net-new visitors. This alone would dramatically improve first-impression scores.
- **Surface my ExtraCare balance in the header** — If I'm signed in (or immediately after I sign in), show me "You have $4.00 in ExtraBucks — redeem today." That's my favorite thing about CVS. Make it visible without scrolling.
- **Replace the "Use the CVS app" hero button with a weekly-deal CTA** — Something like "This week: 25% off vitamins + earn bonus ExtraBucks" gives me a reason to stay. The app redirect is fine in the footer, not as the dominant CTA.
- **Add a "Caregiving" or "For Your Family" quick link** to the action strip alongside prescriptions and vaccines — I'm buying for my aging parent, not just myself. One tap to incontinence supplies, blood pressure monitors, or senior vitamins would make me feel seen.
