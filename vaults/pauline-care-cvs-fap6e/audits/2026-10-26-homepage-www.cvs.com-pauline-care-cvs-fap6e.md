# Homepage snapshot · www.cvs.com · 2026-10-26
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- I opened CVS on my phone and the first thing I got was a big blue COVID vaccine strip, then a glowing "Haunt this way" Halloween banner. It feels like a store pushing seasonal fun and a $5 coupon, with the pharmacy stuff squeezed into the top nav. The cookie notice covers the bottom of the screen, so I can't tell what the tiles are selling.

## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" in the nav:** that's my weekly errand, and it's one tap away.
- **"Coupons & Rewards" in the nav:** this is where my ExtraBucks live, so I know where to go.
- **Store line "816 E. MAIN STREET, Open 24 hours":** it knows my store, and the hours help when I'm picking up for my mom late.
- **"Schedule a Vaccine" in the nav, plus the blue vaccine banner:** useful as a caregiver, and the plain wording is easy to read.
- **"Get a $5 off $20" badge:** a clear, big number I can understand quickly.

## What's weak

- **"Haunt this way" hero:** it's pretty, but it has nothing to do with my prescriptions, my mom's supplies or the cosmetic aisle.
- **Vaccine banner:** the text touches the left screen edge and looks unfinished. "Some restrictions and state limitations apply" is vague.
- **"$5 off $20*" tile:** I can't tell what it applies to, or whether it's an ExtraCare deal or for everyone. The asterisk has no explanation I can see.
- **Cookie panel:** it takes up a big chunk of my phone screen. "Close" is tiny, grey and hard to hit, and the text is dense legal paragraphs.
- **No ExtraBucks balance:** it doesn't show what I've earned, even though I'm a devoted ExtraCare user.
- **Halloween house tile:** it's cropped so I can only see a ghost figure. I don't know what's for sale.

## Recommendations

- Put a signed-in strip under the vaccine banner that says "Your ExtraBucks: $X.XX — expires [date]" with a visible "Redeem" button. Show a "Sign in to see your ExtraBucks" button when signed out.
- Swap the Halloween script banner for a split hero: a refill-ready tile ("Refill prescriptions in 2 taps", with a visible button) next to the Halloween tile.
- Put the offer terms under the badge in plain words, for example "Get $5 off $20 on beauty & health, ends 10/31, ExtraCare card required", instead of a bare asterisk.
- Shrink the cookie notice to a slim one-line bar with a big "Accept" button, and add side padding to the vaccine banner text.
