# Homepage snapshot · www.cvs.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- First thing I see when I land on CVS.com is a big legal pop-up asking me to agree to their data collection — not a deal or my ExtraBucks balance or even a "welcome back." CVS is clearly a full-service pharmacy chain trying to push me to their app, but right now it's hard to know what they're actually promoting because the privacy modal is eating the entire hero. Feels like showing up at the store and being stopped at the door to sign paperwork before you can even grab a basket.

## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" quick-access tab** — right in the top nav where I need it, zero hunting. That's the whole reason I come to CVS in the first place.
- **Store location + hours** — "101 E. Alameda Ave., Open, Closes 12:00 AM" tells me instantly my local store is pulling up. That's useful grounding.
- **"Schedule a Vaccine" shortcut** — as a caregiver for an aging parent, I notice this immediately. Flu shot appointments matter to me.
- **Privacy modal has a visible "Close" button** — at least I can get out of it without hunting. The "X" isn't hidden.

## What's weak

- **Privacy modal blocks the entire hero** — "Improving your site experience" is the first full sentence I read on CVS.com. Not a deal. Not my rewards. A data-collection disclaimer. I came to shop, not to do paperwork.
- **No ExtraCare hook whatsoever** — I collect ExtraBucks religiously. My loyalty to CVS is real and they're not acknowledging me at all. No balance, no "member pricing," no ExtraBucks offer in sight.
- **"Use the CVS app" button above the fold, but I'm already on the website** — pushing me off the browser to download an app as the primary CTA feels backwards for a returning customer.
- **Hero copy "Embracing..." is a tease that goes nowhere** — I can see one word before the modal cuts it off. What am I embracing? I have no idea what they're selling right now.
- **No visible prices, no offers, no product imagery** — I can't see a single product or price point. For a weekly shopper, that's a cold open with nothing to grab onto.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the privacy modal or reduce it to a slim bottom banner** — the full-screen takeover on mobile kills the first impression. A bottom cookie bar with an "Accept & Close" chip leaves the hero and offer visible.
- **Add an ExtraCare welcome strip directly below the nav** — even a static "Sign in to see your ExtraBucks balance" banner would signal that CVS knows who I am and values my loyalty.
- **Make the hero or a banner strip show the week's circular deal** — weekly savings (like the Sunday circular) is why ExtraCare devotees come to the site. Show me the deals, not the app pitch.
- **Move "Use the CVS app" to a secondary position** — keep it accessible but don't let it outrank a "Shop deals" or "Refill Rx" primary CTA for people already on the website.
