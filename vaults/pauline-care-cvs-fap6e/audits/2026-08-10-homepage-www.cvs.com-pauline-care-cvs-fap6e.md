# Homepage snapshot · www.cvs.com · 2026-08-10
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- This looks like the CVS I use every week — pharmacy front and center, ExtraCare deals right there, easy to find "Manage prescriptions." Right now they're pushing GLP-1 weight-loss drug pricing pretty hard, with the "Extra Big Deals" and a "Summer Shop" bag squeezed in below. It reads like a pharmacy that also sells beach totes, which is a little odd but not unfamiliar.

## What's working

- The store info bar up top ("816 E. Main Street, Open 24 hours") — I always check this first before heading out, and it's right there.
- "Manage prescriptions" in the main nav — one tap and I'm where I need to be for my mom's refills.
- The "Extra Big Deals with extracare" banner — that's my rewards program, I recognize the branding immediately.
- The pharmacist photo — feels warm and trustworthy, like someone I'd actually talk to at the counter.

## What's weak

- That privacy/cookie banner at the bottom is covering up whatever comes after "Keep your routine with easy Rx management" — I can't see the rest of that section or what's below it.
- The GLP-1 purple box dominates the top-right — I'm not on that medication, so it's a lot of real estate for something that doesn't apply to me.
- No clear single "shop now" button — my eye bounces between the GLP-1 box, the Extra Big Deals tile, and the Summer Shop tote before landing anywhere.
- The "Summer Shop" beach bag and kids-on-beach photo feel like a different store entirely — not what I come to CVS for.

## Recommendations

- Swap the beach-tote "Summer Shop" tile for something a weekly Rx shopper actually uses — cold & flu, vitamins, or caregiver supplies.
- Give me one clear button, like "Refill Your Prescription" or "Check ExtraBucks Balance," instead of a wall of text links.
- Push that cookie banner into a small footer strip so it stops blocking content — right now it's eating a quarter of my screen.
- If GLP-1 is the big campaign, fine, but balance it with a rewards-balance callout for those of us who are here for the ExtraCare card, not weight-loss drugs.
