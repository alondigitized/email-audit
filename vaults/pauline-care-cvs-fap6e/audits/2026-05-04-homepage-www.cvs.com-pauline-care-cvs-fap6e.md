## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" in the top nav strip** — that's my primary reason for coming to CVS.com and it's one tap away without any digging.
- **Hero headline "Here for what matters"** — lands emotionally for a caregiver. Understated but I feel it.
- **Store locator showing 101 E. Alameda Ave. as open** — I appreciate knowing my store is actually available before I go further.
- **Mother's Day nav tab** — seasonally right, and caregivers buy for aging parents on Mother's Day just as much as kids do.
- **Clean render, no clutter** — the page isn't screaming at me with seven pop-ups, which at my age is a relief.

## What's weak

- **No ExtraCare / ExtraBucks hook above the fold** — that's my whole relationship with CVS. I check my balance the way some people check their bank account. The fact that I have to hunt for it is a miss.
- **No offer, deal, or promotional strip visible** — CVS's brand DNA is the deal. Weekly circular. Bonus Bucks. This homepage feels like it forgot that.
- **Hero image demographic is off** — the woman in the photo looks like she's 35 with a toddler. I'm 55, my "child" concern is my dad's blood pressure medication, not a baby's sippy cup.
- **"Use the CVS app" as the main CTA** — I already have the app. Pushing me to download it when I'm already on the site feels like the wrong ask.
- **No prescription-refill shortcut** — "Manage prescriptions" is in the nav tab, but there's no quick "Refill Rx" button in the hero, which for a weekly pharmacy customer is a missed conversion.

## Recommendations

- **Surface the ExtraBucks balance or a "Sign in to see your ExtraBucks" prompt in the hero** — even a ghost-state badge ("You have X ExtraBucks — sign in") would pull returning ExtraCare members in immediately.
- **Add a pharmacy-focused hero CTA** — something like "Refill your prescriptions" as a secondary button alongside the app push; prescription refills are the #1 reason Gen X women come to CVS.com.
- **Swap the hero image for a Gen X / caregiver-coded lifestyle shot** — a 50-something woman helping an older parent, or at minimum someone my age, not a millennial mom with a 2-year-old.
- **Drop a thin weekly-deal banner between the nav and the hero** — even one line ("This week: 40% off vitamins, ExtraBucks on select health brands") would signal that the weekly deals I rely on are live.
