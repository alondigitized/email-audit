# Homepage snapshot · www.cvs.com · 2026-09-28
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- Well, this doesn't look like my CVS at all this morning — it's all GLP-1 pricing boxes and a big fall leaves banner. I get that everybody's chasing the weight-loss drug dollar right now, but where's my ExtraBucks balance, where's my prescription refill button front and center? It reads like a pharmacy trying to be a wellness startup rather than the CVS I run into three times a week for Mom's meds.

## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" right in the top nav** — that's exactly what I come here for, glad it's not buried
- **COVID-19 vaccine banner** — as the one taking Mom to her appointments, this actually catches my eye
- **Fall leaf graphics and "Everything fall calls for"** — feels timely, not a leftover summer banner
- **"Coupons & Rewards" visible in the nav bar** — good, I know where to find my bonus bucks even if the balance itself isn't shown

## What's weak

- **GLP-1 box dominates the whole right side** — not my need, and it's crowding out anything about my regular prescriptions or the cosmetic aisle
- **No ExtraBucks balance or rewards total visible** — I like to see my number, that's half the reason I use the app
- **The cookie consent banner at the bottom cuts off text** — the line under the GLP-1 box literally gets chopped off mid-sentence ("CVS is your one spot for everything GLP-1...")
- **The hero photo caption is truncated too** — "Stay w..." — looks unfinished, sloppy for a big brand
- **No obvious "Refill your prescriptions" button** — that should be the biggest button on the page for someone like me

## Recommendations

- Put a big "Refill Prescriptions" or "Manage Meds" button in the hero, not just a small nav link
- Show my ExtraCare rewards balance right up top if I'm signed in — that's my whole reason for opening the app
- Shrink the GLP-1 promo or move it lower — it's taking over real estate that weekly-Rx shoppers like me need
- Fix that cookie banner so it doesn't slice off the sentence underneath it
