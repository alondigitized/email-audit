# Hours left for 30% off sitewide
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the final-call version of the Black Friday early-access push, and it follows the same playbook as the 11/22 "30% off early access is almost up ⏱️" send. A black "LAST CHANCE!" bar sits over a maroon hero with a huge "30% OFF SITEWIDE" numeral and a single "SHOP NOW" button. The eye lands on the offer immediately and the hierarchy is clean.
- The body is competent but generic. Four category tiles (Slip-ins, Cozy Fit, Court & Classics, Performance) and a Doorbuster block ("starting at $49.99") are followed by size chips, a nav list, Klarna/Afterpay, and an app download. It is a complete retail template with no product-level detail, no prices on any item, and no reviews.
- There is also a credibility problem. The subject says "Hours left," but the hero fine print says "Valid online only thru 11/22," and the footer says 11/18–11/22/26. If this lands on 11/23, the deadline has already passed. A subscriber who notices that loses trust. Judgment: strong hero, weak and slightly contradictory urgency, and the fourth near-identical member-exclusive 30% send in a week.

## What's working

- The "30% OFF SITEWIDE" numeral is enormous, high contrast, and unmissable.
- "LAST CHANCE!" in a black bar sets the urgency before any other copy.
- One offer and one primary "SHOP NOW" CTA keep the hero focused.
- The four category tiles with individual shop links let a reader go straight to their interest.
- The size chips for women and men are a useful shortcut.
- The Doorbuster block at "$49.99" adds a concrete price anchor and a second reason to click.

## What's weak

- The urgency contradicts itself. "Hours left" in the subject sits against "thru 11/22" in the fine print, so the offer may already be expired.
- The tiles show no products with prices, ratings, or names, only lifestyle shots and a style label.
- There are no reviews or social proof anywhere in the email.
- The legal copy is dense, tiny, and repeated twice, and "Exclusions apply" next to the offer is buried fine print.
- The loyalty ask ("Join Skechers Plus…") in the header is small and sits above the hero, while the body assumes you are already a member.
- The email is long, with a lot of footer and utility content below the real offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the deadline so the subject, hero, and legal copy agree. Add a specific time, for example "Ends tonight at 11:59 PM PT," so "hours left" is credible.
- 2. Replace at least two category tiles with specific hero products, each with a name, price, and star rating. The "Slip-ins" tile should link to a named best-seller.
- 3. Show a "member price" line, for example "$XX → $XX with 30% off," so the savings read as dollars, not just a percentage.
- 4. Move the Doorbuster block directly under the hero as the second offer, and cut or collapse the nav list and app block.
- 5. Cut the legal text to one line with an "Exclusions" link.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: 30% off every pair`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call, members: 30% off ends at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus Doorbusters from $49.99. Discount applies automatically at checkout.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Slip-ins, Cozy Fit and more. Your member price is waiting in your cart.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is mostly standard and responsive, but it has two non-HTTPS images (one a tracking pixel carrying a recipient ID), missing alt text on both, and zoom/accessibility restrictions in the head. The header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be confirmed because the relay did not capture the headers. The HTML is truncated, so the body links, footer and unsubscribe link were not reviewed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Not verified:** 45 tracking links were skipped because they use click-redirect domains. Their destinations, status codes and redirect chains are unknown.
- **Plaintext tracking pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108862811&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_SITESELECT_FULL_EN_11222026_PM`
  - It loads over `http://`, which can be blocked or flagged as mixed content.
  - `mi_u=8108862811` looks like a per-recipient identifier sent in cleartext.
- **Favicon:** `<link rel="icon" href="http://image.emails.nl.skechers.com/lib/fe3515…/70bf5f91….png">`
  - It uses `http://`, a different subdomain (`emails.nl`) and a different library ID (`fe3515…` vs `fe3115…`).
  - Most email clients ignore favicons, so this is a low-severity template leftover.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:**
  - `o.gif` has no alt. It is a tracking pixel, so `alt=""` would be correct.
  - `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115…/m/1/dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` has no alt.
- **`http://` image:** `dde00662….png` loads over `http://`, although the same host serves `https://` (the JSON-LD logo URL). Change the scheme to HTTPS.
- **Zoom restriction:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text scaling:** `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }` prevents iOS text scaling.
- **Focus outline:** `a { outline: none; }` removes the keyboard focus indicator.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Skechers&reg;</title>` doesn't reflect the subject line.
- **Mobile/desktop toggle:** `.mobile-content { display:block }` and `.desktop-content { display:none }` are the defaults, and they swap only at `min-width:768px`. Clients that strip media queries will show only the mobile block.
- **Preheader:** hidden with `display:none !important` and `font-size:1px`. This is standard and correct.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible HTML. The only per-recipient value seen is `mi_u=8108862811` in the pixel URL. The rest of the body was truncated, so token coverage is not fully verified.

## 5. Compliance

- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found, with the same caveat. This matters for Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **Authentication-Results:** absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment could not be checked. The sender is `no-reply@emails.skechers.com`.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address:** not visible in the truncated HTML, so I can't confirm CAN-SPAM compliance.
