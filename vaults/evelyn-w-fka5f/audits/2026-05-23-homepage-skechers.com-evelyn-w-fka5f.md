# Homepage snapshot · skechers.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Skechers is coming in hot with a Memorial Day patriotic play — the Red, White & Blue Collection is front and center, and I respect the timing. It reads like a lifestyle brand that knows the holiday weekend is a shopping moment, and they're leaning into it. The BOGO offer and members reward hit before I even get to the hero, which honestly is exactly how you earn a second look from me when I'm toggling between this and my grocery app.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF banner** — It's the first thing I see. No scrolling, no hunting for a code. That kind of instant clarity makes me feel like the visit is already worth it.
- **Member reward with a deadline** — "Now until 5/25! Members Only - Earn a $5 Reward on $75+" tells me exactly what I get and when it expires. That urgency actually works on me.
- **"Slip-ins" in the top nav** — That single word is doing heavy lifting. I'm here for the slip-ons and Skechers put it in the first tap position. Smart.
- **Memorial Day timing** — The Red, White & Blue Collection is on-brand for the long weekend. It doesn't feel like a leftover sale that forgot to flip the sign.
- **Woman in the hero** — She's in a casual navy dress, active and put-together, which reflects something close to how I see myself on a Saturday.

## What's weak

- **No "Shop Now" button in the hero** — The collection name is big and bold but there's no tappable CTA button attached to it. On mobile, I shouldn't have to guess where to go.
- **Hero speaks to the holiday, not to me** — There's nothing about comfort, easy-on/off, or the chaos of daily life. The patriotic campaign is fine but it doesn't earn my personal loyalty.
- **No free shipping mention above the fold** — That's one of my top purchase triggers and I can't see it yet. I don't know if they offer it or what the threshold is without scrolling.
- **Cart is empty (0) but no "start here" nudge** — As a returning visitor type, I'd love a "pick up where you left off" or recently viewed signal. Nothing there.
- **Man is half the hero** — I'm not the target of that half of the image. It dilutes the relevance for me personally.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop Slip-ins" button directly in the hero** — The patriotic collection image can stay, but layer in a tappable button for the category I'm already navigating toward. One tap from hero to Slip-ins category = higher conversion for shoppers like me.
- **Swap the hero sub-copy to include a comfort hook** — Instead of just "The Red, White & Blue Collection," add something like "Comfort for the whole long weekend" underneath. Five words that make the patriotic angle feel relevant to my actual life.
- **Surface free shipping threshold in the second banner** — That strip already has the member reward; add "Free shipping on $X+" right next to it. Two reasons to stay, same real estate.
- **Make the member banner tap directly to a sign-in modal** — Right now "Login | details" reads like two separate links. A single "Log in to earn" button would convert better on a phone screen.
