# Homepage snapshot · skechers.com · 2026-05-27
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-27**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so right away I can see Skechers is running a BOGO deal and a spring-into-summer apparel sale — those banners hit me immediately, which is good. But I'm honestly a little confused about what this page is actually trying to sell me, because the most prominent block below the nav is a "Win the Ultimate Soccer Fan Prize Package" sweepstakes, and I'm here to buy shoes for my busy life, not enter a contest. The brand feels approachable and familiar, but the homepage is pulling in too many directions at once.

## What's working

- **The BOGO 50% banner at the very top** is impossible to miss — that's the kind of offer that stops me from scrolling past. As someone who shops for the whole family, stacking a BOGO deal is genuinely motivating.
- **"Slip-ins" as the first quick-nav tab** — it's right there, labeled exactly what I'd type in a search bar. One tap and I'm in my category. That's efficient for a busy mom opening this on her phone between drop-offs.
- **The secondary sale bar ("25% OFF Apparel")** reminds me Skechers sells more than shoes, which I sometimes forget — could catch me for a gym top while I'm browsing.
- **The woman in the hero** looks like someone I'd pass at a school pickup — age-appropriate, casual, not intimidating or overly trendy. That registers as "this brand is for me."

## What's weak

- **The soccer sweepstakes block dominates prime real estate** — "WIN THE ULTIMATE SOCCER FAN PRIZE PACKAGE" means nothing to me as a mom shopping for comfy slip-ons. It's the biggest designed element on the page and it's irrelevant to my visit.
- **No hero copy at all** — the main image section has no headline, no copy, no CTA button. I'm just looking at a split photo of two people walking around looking stylish. I don't know what they're promoting or where to tap.
- **Free shipping threshold not visible** — that's one of my biggest purchase triggers and it's not anywhere above the fold.
- **No trust signals** — no "free returns," no star ratings, nothing that would reassure me before I commit to tapping deeper. I'm on mobile; I need that confidence quickly.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the soccer sweepstakes block with a "Slip-ins for every moment" lifestyle module** — show a mom in the school pickup lane, or someone juggling groceries. Tie it to the BOGO offer with a "Shop Slip-ins" button. That copy + CTA combo would convert me immediately.
- **Add a free-shipping threshold strip** — even a simple "Free shipping on orders over $50" line under the BOGO banner would push me toward filling my cart instead of hesitating.
- **Put hero copy on the main image** — the split photo of the two lifestyle models is pretty, but it says nothing. A two-word headline like "Comfort. All Day." over the woman's panel with a "Shop Women's" button would give me something to act on.
- **Surface one SKECHERS Plus benefit** — swap "WIN THE ULTIMATE SOCCER FAN PRIZE PACKAGE" or put below it a one-liner like "Members earn 1 point per $1 — sign up free." That would actually make me care about the loyalty program.
