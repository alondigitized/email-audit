# We've missed you! Check out what's new.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean win-back send, and a near repeat of the 2026-08-10 "We've missed you! Check out what's new." email, which has the same subject and the same "Hello, Stranger!" hero. The offer is clear: 20% off your order with a visible code, WBC-FBL7GCMWVQ, in a light-blue band right under the hero. The hierarchy works. Logo, hero, offer, then category tiles.
- The weakness is that it is generic and timing-blind. It is November 9, yet the creative has no holiday or Black Friday framing. Our own history shows Skechers sending early Black Friday and Singles Day mail around this date. A lapsed customer would wonder why the promo is 20% off with no seasonal hook. The offer is also valid until 12/24, so nothing forces action now. The body is long, with a dozen sections after the offer (category tiles, Casuals, Athletics, a banner, a link list, app, Afterpay, text club, Plus), and it dilutes the one offer that matters.
- Verdict: competent and honest, but a recycled, low-urgency send. I'd call it a mid-5 email.

## What's working

- The "Hello, Stranger!" hero names the lapse and gives a reason to return: new looks and more comfort.
- "20% OFF YOUR ORDER" is the largest text in the email, in a contrasting blue band, with the code written out in plain text.
- Two "SHOP NOW" buttons in navy are unambiguous and sit above the fold.
- The Walking / Casuals / Running tiles let a lapsed buyer pick a category quickly.
- The footer fine print is legible and states the expiry (12/24/2026), so the offer reads as honest.

## What's weak

- The subject and hero are the same as the 2026-08-10 send, so this feels like a recycled automation rather than a fresh message.
- There is no seasonal or holiday context in a week when Skechers is running Singles Day and Black Friday promos.
- Both "SHOP NOW" buttons go to a generic destination. The hero shoe collage shows no specific product, price or benefit.
- The Casuals and Athletics modules have large headlines but no prices, no reviews and no product names.
- After the offer there are roughly ten more sections: the category list, app, curbside, BNPL, text club, Plus and social. The single offer gets lost.
- A code of the form WBC-FBL7GCMWVQ must be copied by hand, and there is no "applied automatically" cue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the offer seasonal and deadline-driven. Swap "20% off your order" for "20% off before Black Friday" with a real end date. The 12/24 expiry gives no reason to act now.
- 2. Replace the generic "SHOP NOW" with a specific CTA such as "Shop my 20% off". Link it to a page that applies the code automatically.
- 3. Put one hero product with price and a "was/now" figure in the first screen, instead of an abstract collage.
- 4. Cut the module count to the offer, three categories and one product row. Move the app, BNPL and text club into a slim footer.
- 5. Add social proof, such as a star rating or "most-loved" label, on the Casuals and Athletics products.
- 6. Vary the subject across sends. The August subject was identical.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Still thinking about new shoes? 20% off inside`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 20% off is waiting, ends before Black Friday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code WBC-FBL7GCMWVQ saves 20% on comfort picks for the holidays.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New Slip-ins and everyday sneakers, plus 20% off your whole order.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has a confirmed broken personalization slot ("Hi ,"), no plain-text part, and an unresolvable tracking pixel (`pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif`, no scheme). Most images load over `http://`. The HTML provided is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and most CTAs could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Scheme-less pixel:** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…" width="1" height="1" />` has no protocol. Clients resolve it as a relative path, so it 404s or is dropped and the Return Path/Validity seed tracking never fires.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108862811&mi_ecmp=TRG_US_EN_WINBACKENGAGE_1_09182026` (MovableInk) is `http://` and may be blocked or rewritten.
- **Probe not run:** 35 click-tracking links were skipped by the QA check (redirect domains), so destination health is unverified. No broken links were observed, but none were confirmed working either.
- **Stale campaign ID:** the `…_09182026` campaign ID appears in both the MovableInk and `beacon.krxd.net` ad-impression URLs. This is informational. It suggests a 2026-09-18 build, sent 2026-11-09.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Insecure images:** 11 images load over `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115…/m/1[1]/…`. They include the logo `dde00662-…png`, the App Store and Google Play badges, the Afterpay image, and the social icons. Clients may block them or show mixed-content warnings.
- **Missing alt text:** the content image `49468f73-4651-4af3-bea2-61d1ae5db486.png` has no `alt`. The `o.gif` pixel also has none, though it carries `aria-hidden="true"`, which mitigates it.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 issue.
- **No focus outline:** the CSS rule `a { outline: none; }` removes the default keyboard focus indicator.
- **No language:** `<html xmlns=…>` has no `lang` attribute.
- **Malformed style:** `style="background-color: #ffffff;!important"` on the outer `<div align="center">` is invalid, so the declaration is likely ignored.
- **Pixel markup:** the `krxd.net` pixels use unquoted `width=0 height=0` and have no `alt`. They are inside a `display:none` container, so the impact is low.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting:** QA detected `Hi ,`, an empty first-name slot. The merge field resolved to blank with no fallback value. This was not confirmed in the truncated HTML, so treat the QA check as the source.
- No raw, unrendered merge tokens (e.g. `%%first_name%%`) were seen in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] No plain-text part:** the text version is 0 chars. This hurts deliverability and accessibility, and many spam filters score HTML-only mail down.
- **Unverified headers:** `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found, and `Authentication-Results` is missing, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The QA tool notes that the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so I can't confirm a real defect.
- **Unverified footer:** the unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated portion of the HTML, so I can't confirm them.
- **From domain:** `no-reply@emails.skechers.com` and the click domain `click.emails.skechers.com` share the `emails.skechers.com` subdomain, which is consistent. Image hosting is split between `image.emails.skechers.com` and `image.emails.nl.skechers.com` (the favicon). This is a minor inconsistency.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters and landing page alignment could not be assessed. The visible HTML contains only wrapped click-tracking URLs and pixels, and the 35 tracking links were not resolved.
- The only campaign identifier visible is `TRG_US_EN_WINBACKENGAGE_1_09182026`, which is in the ad-pixel URLs. No UTM values were visible.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a fallback to the first-name merge field (e.g. "Hi there,") or suppress the greeting when the name is empty.
2. Fix the Return Path pixel to `https://pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=…`.
3. Switch all `image.emails.skechers.com` assets and the `ink1000.com` pixel to `https://`.
4. Add a multipart `text/plain` alternative.
5. Add descriptive `alt` to `49468f73-…png`, and `alt=""` to decorative images and pixels.
6. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta, and add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
7. Restore a visible focus style on links, and fix `;!important` so it sits inside the declaration (`background-color: #ffffff !important;`).
8. Re-run the audit against the raw `.eml` to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. Also verify the footer unsubscribe link and address, and resolve the tracking links to check destinations and UTMs.
