# Homepage snapshot · skechers.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I'm actually kind of impressed right away — the first thing I see is a BOGO 50% deal plastered at the top, and then boom: Slip-ins front and center. That's literally what I came here for. This feels like a comfortable, lifestyle-y brand that knows exactly what its core customer wants, and right now it's pushing its hands-free slip-on line hard for spring.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF banner** — It's the very first thing I see, and "Applied at Cart" means I don't have to fumble with a promo code. As someone who shops on my phone while my kid is napping, this is huge.
- **"HANDS FREE Slip-ins GLIDE-STEP" hero** — The product name does the selling for me. "Hands free" is the exact promise a busy mom needs. I didn't have to read anything extra to understand the pitch.
- **The woman in the floral dress** — She looks like she's living her best life at the pool, not running a 5K. That's the energy I'm shopping with. She's wearing slip-on sandals that look stylish AND comfortable.
- **"Recently Viewed" tab** — Small but meaningful. I shop in tiny windows between errands. Coming back and finding my stuff quickly matters.
- **"SHOP NOW" button** — Big, white, clear. No ambiguity about what to tap.

## What's weak

- **No free shipping callout** — I'm motivated by free shipping, and I don't see a threshold anywhere on this screen. That's a miss for someone like me who weighs shipping costs before hitting checkout.
- **"SHOP NOW" is vague** — Slip-ins for women? Slip-ins for men? The hero has both genders but I want a "Shop Women's Slip-ins" button, not a generic one.
- **The Spring Apparel Sale strip feels secondary** — 25% off apparel is also a solid deal, but it's sandwiched in a small gray strip and easy to overlook. I almost missed it.
- **No trust signals visible** — No star ratings, no "free returns," no review count. I'm about to spend money — a little reassurance goes a long way.
- **Man is equally prominent in the hero** — I don't need the men's imagery competing for my attention when I'm shopping for myself. It slightly dilutes the relevance.

## Recommendations

- **Split the CTA by gender:** Replace the single "SHOP NOW" with "Shop Women's Slip-ins" so it feels like it's talking directly to me — takes one word to double relevance.
- **Add a free-shipping threshold to the promo strip:** Even "Free shipping on orders $50+" tucked into that gray bar would seal the deal for me before I even start browsing.
- **Give the woman model more screen real estate:** Crop or reframe the hero to lead with her — she's the one I identify with, and right now the layout splits attention 50/50.
- **Add a star-rating snippet to the hero:** Even a "4.7 ★ from 12,000 reviews" line under the Slip-ins headline builds instant trust for a first-timer or a returning shopper who's been comparison shopping.
