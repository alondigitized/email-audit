# You've still got 20% off!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-03**
## Executive summary

- A re-engagement/reminder email from Skechers surfacing a 20% off first-purchase offer with a clear promo code. The hero does its job — the discount is unmissable — but everything below it is generic catalog browsing rather than deepening the deal. The email reads as a broadcast, not a targeted send: no product specificity, no persona-matching models or categories front-and-center, and the loyalty upsell (Skechers Plus) competes for attention midway through the body. Solid mechanics, shallow targeting.

## What's working

- **Hero is unambiguous.** "There's still time! 20% OFF YOUR FIRST PURCHASE" with the promo code printed in full is exactly what a discount email should lead with. No guessing, no click required to reveal the code.
- **Clean render.** Every image loads, no overlapping text, no empty containers. Layout survives well.
- **CTA is visible without scrolling.** "SHOP NOW" sits directly under the code — zero friction to the click.
- **Collection tiles are visually organized.** Hands Free Slip-Ins, Cozy Fit, Max Cushioning, Arch Fit — gives someone scanning a reason to stay.

## What's weak

- **No product specificity.** The featured shoes in the hero carousel (5–6 thumbnail products) are small, hard to distinguish, and feel decorative rather than curated. No prices, no product names readable at a glance.
- **Skechers Plus upsell dilutes the offer.** The mid-email "Not a member? Sign up" section competes with the 20% off CTA and introduces a second ask before the first has converted.
- **Category links are placeholder-level.** "WOMEN, MEN, KIDS, CLOTHING, NEW ARRIVALS, SALE" — this is a nav bar, not email content. It adds zero value over just clicking through to the site.
- **No demographic or persona signal.** Models visible are diverse but tiny; there's no "walking shoe," "running shoe," or style-specific angle to match any particular buyer intent.
- **"Still got 20% off" implies a prior email.** For a re-engagement, the creative should probably acknowledge the lapse or add urgency — there's no expiry date visible.
- **Texting opt-in and app download** add further noise below the fold with no compelling reason to act.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a deadline.** "Offer expires Sunday" or a countdown makes the urgency real. Right now there's no reason to act today vs. next week.
- 2. **Feature 1–3 specific products with prices.** Pull a hero shoe from the persona's most-browsed or top-selling category. Small thumbnails with no names/prices are wasted real estate.
- 3. **Move Skechers Plus below the fold or into a footer strip.** It's a separate conversion goal — don't ask for two things at once.
- 4. **Cut the nav-link section.** Replace with curated category blocks ("New in Walking," "Best in Slip-Ins") with actual product images and direct links.
- 5. **Echo the "still got" message.** If this is a reminder, say "Your code is still waiting" and give an explicit expiry — lean into the time pressure rather than hinting at it.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**CRITICAL — Doubled-domain merge error**
URL recorded by QA: `http://www.skechers.com/www.Skechers.com`
A template variable (likely a `%%SITE_URL%%` or `%%baseURL%%` merge token) failed to resolve and was concatenated directly onto the hardcoded domain prefix. The resulting URL is non-functional — depending on server routing it produces a 404 or loops back to root. This is the sole `[FAIL]`-rated finding.

**26 tracking links skipped**
All click links route through `click.emails.skechers.com` redirect. HTTP probe could not validate final destinations — UTM pass-through and redirect chain health are unverified (see §6).

**Tracking pixel over HTTP**
MovableInk pixel: `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif` — served over plain HTTP; will be blocked by email clients that enforce mixed-content policies.

**Return Path pixel**
`pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif` also uses HTTP (no `https://` prefix in source).

**Krxd cross-site beacons (3 pixels)**
`beacon.krxd.net` pixels perform user-matching and ad impression firing against Salesforce DMP (`advertiserid=100007688`). These fire on open, not click — relevant to consent scope for EU/CA recipients.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**13 images served over HTTP**
`image.emails.skechers.com` subdomain uses `http://` for the majority of content images including the header logo, footer icons (Instagram, Facebook, YouTube), AfterPay badge, and app store badges. Many corporate and security-hardened clients will block non-HTTPS image sources silently, collapsing the visual layout.

Affected assets (partial list from QA):
- `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` (Skechers logo)
- `49468f73-4651-4af3-bea2-61d1ae5db486.png`
- `00100b23-0afa-4715-a8cd-23bb46f9d2b2.png`
- `fc08601a-fcce-4c96-8b90-39e5a55d6c05.png`
- Social icons: Instagram, Facebook, YouTube
- AfterPay badge, app store badges

**4 images missing `alt` text**
`bcc16007...jpg`, `49468f73...png`, `00100b23...png`, `fc08601a...png` — no `alt` attribute. Screen readers skip these entirely; images-off clients render blank blocks with no fallback.

**Favicon uses wrong subdomain + HTTP**
`<link rel="icon" href="http://image.emails.nl.skechers.com/...">` — note the `nl.` subdomain (Netherlands regional CDN?) vs. the `image.emails.skechers.com` used elsewhere. Also HTTP.

**`text-size-adjust: none` on `*` selector**
Applied globally in CSS. This overrides user-level accessibility font scaling across all platforms — not a rendering failure but a non-standard accessibility tradeoff.

**Dark mode CSS is cosmetic only**
The `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` block forces `background-color: white` on `body` and `.bg-white`. No dark-adapted text colors or image swaps are present — dark mode recipients get a harsh white-on-dark shell.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Failed token — root cause of §2 critical bug**
The URL `http://www.skechers.com/www.Skechers.com` is consistent with a merge token like `%%SITE_BASE_URL%%` resolving to the literal string `www.Skechers.com` instead of a path, then being appended to a hardcoded `http://www.skechers.com/` prefix. No other unresolved tokens are visible in the available HTML — the truncation prevents full assessment of all merge fields.

### 5. Compliance

| Check | Status | Evidence |

| `List-Unsubscribe` header | **MISSING** | Not captured by AgentMail relay |
| `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) | **MISSING** | One-click unsubscribe not supported |
| SPF/DKIM `Authentication-Results` | **UNKNOWN** | Header not found via relay — pass/fail unconfirmed |
| CAN-SPAM physical address | **UNVERIFIED** | HTML truncated; footer not visible in provided source |

The absence of `List-Unsubscribe-Post` means Gmail and Apple Mail will not render the native one-click unsubscribe button — recipients must follow the in-body unsubscribe link. Gmail began requiring `List-Unsubscribe-Post` for bulk senders (>5k/day) in February 2024.

The Krxd impression pixel (`beacon.krxd.net/1x1_ad_impression.gif`) fires on open without click interaction. If this email is sent to EU recipients, consent basis for third-party DMP impression tracking on email open should be confirmed.
