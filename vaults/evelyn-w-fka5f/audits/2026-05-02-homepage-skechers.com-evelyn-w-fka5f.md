# Homepage snapshot · skechers.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- Okay, first impression — this is a comfort-focused shoe brand that's clearly trying to appeal to everyday people who want something practical and stylish without trying too hard. The BOGO deal hits me immediately, which I appreciate, and the Hands Free Slip-ins headline below the fold is *literally* made for me as a mom who's always got her hands full. It's not flashy, but it feels real.
- **8/10**

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF banner at the very top** — I notice deals first, and this one's hard to miss in that blue bar. Auto-applied at cart? Yes, please. That removes friction I hate.
- **Hands Free Slip-ins headline** — as someone who gravitates toward slip-ons for the school-run chaos, this speaks directly to me. Didn't even need to scroll much.
- **25% OFF Apparel secondary promo strip** — spring sale energy, layered savings. I feel like I'm getting a good deal just by landing here.
- **Brooke Burke as the face** — she's a relatable mom figure, roughly my generation. Not a twenty-year-old model I can't identify with.
- **Split lifestyle hero image** — the woman's look (casual pink sweater, jeans, clean sneakers) matches exactly how I dress day-to-day.

## What's weak

- **No visible CTA button above the fold** — I see the hero image with two celebrities but I don't see a "Shop Now" or "Shop Women's" button actually on the hero itself. I have to find the "Shop | details" text link in the promo strip, which is easy to miss.
- **Two different promos competing at once** — BOGO 50% on shoes AND 25% off apparel in the same breath feels slightly cluttered. Which one do I click?
- **No free shipping callout visible** — I'm heavily motivated by free shipping and I don't see it anywhere in this view. That's a missed hook.
- **The Tony Hawk pairing feels off-target for me** — splitting the hero 50/50 between a mom-coded woman and a male celebrity skater dilutes the message. Who am I shopping for here?

## Recommendations

- **Add a tappable "Shop Women's Slip-ins" button directly on the hero image** — right now there's no obvious next step after you look at the celebrities; a clear CTA on the photo would capture people like me immediately.
- **Surface the free shipping threshold in the top banner** — swap or add alongside the BOGO copy: "BOGO 50% + Free Shipping on Orders $50+" since that's a major motivator for online shoppers like me.
- **Separate the hero by audience** — show me the woman's side full-width on mobile, not a 50/50 split with Tony Hawk. A/B test a mom-only hero vs. the split.
- **Add a "Just for you: Slip-ins" quick-shop strip below the hero** — surface 3–4 bestselling slip-on styles right under the fold so I can shop without navigating.
