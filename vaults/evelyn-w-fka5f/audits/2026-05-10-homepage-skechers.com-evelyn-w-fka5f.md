# Homepage snapshot · skechers.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I actually stopped scrolling the second this loaded — a mom kissing her daughter surrounded by pink flowers with "The Mother's Day Shop" right there? That's me. Skechers is clearly running a full Mother's Day push, and on May 10 it feels perfectly timed, not stale. The vibe is warm, accessible, and deal-forward — exactly the kind of page that makes me think I might find something for myself while I'm technically shopping "for the holiday."

## What's working

- **The mother-daughter hero image**: I immediately see myself in that picture — a mom in pink sneakers sitting with her little girl. That's not an accident and it works.
- **BOGO 50% OFF banner**: That top blue banner is the first thing my eye hits, and BOGO is the kind of deal that makes me do math in my head and stay on the page.
- **"The Mother's Day Shop" headline**: The script font feels warm and giftable without being cheesy. It signals this is a curated experience, not just a generic sale.
- **Double-stacked promos**: BOGO on shoes + 25% off apparel means there's something for multiple cart combos — I'm already thinking about buying for myself AND the kids.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA**: Full-width, high contrast, no ambiguity. I know exactly what tapping it will do.

## What's weak

- **No free shipping callout**: Free shipping is a major purchase trigger for me — I buy online constantly and the threshold is what gets me to add one more thing to cart. It's nowhere above the fold.
- **No navigation to Women's slip-ons or comfort styles**: I have to tap the hamburger menu to find my category. There's no "Women's | Slip-ins | Comfort" quick-link bar visible, which slows me down.
- **"SHOP NOW" is vague**: I'd tap it, but "Shop Slip-ins" or "Shop Women's Styles" would feel more like Skechers knows what I want specifically.
- **No social proof anywhere visible**: No star ratings, no "10,000+ reviews," no "as seen on" — nothing that reassures me before I commit to clicking through.
- **No urgency**: The Mother's Day timing is obvious, but there's no "Offer ends tonight" or countdown to create that little push to act now vs. later.

## Recommendations

- **Add a free-shipping threshold to the secondary banner strip**: Swap "Shop the Spring Apparel Sale! 25% OFF Apparel Shop | details" for something like "Free shipping on orders $50+ · BOGO 50% OFF" so I can see both deals and know shipping is handled.
- **Add a horizontal quick-nav strip below the hero**: A scrollable pill row — "Women's · Slip-ins · Sneakers · Kids'" — so I can get to my category in one tap instead of drilling through the hamburger menu.
- **Make the CTA copy category-specific**: Change "SHOP NOW" to "Shop Women's Styles" or "Shop Mother's Day Picks" — low-lift change, much higher relevance signal.
- **Add a trust strip above the footer**: Three small icons — "Free Returns · 5M+ Reviews · Secure Checkout" — to answer the questions I'd ask before entering payment info.
