# We've missed you! Check out what's new.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a win-back email done reasonably well: it names the lapse ("Hello, Stranger!"), gives a real reason to return (new collections), and backs it with a legible, unambiguous 20% off code — no fine-print scavenger hunt required. The visual hierarchy is clean: hero collage → offer → nav bar → loyalty/app cross-sell, in a logical top-down order. Compared to the brand's recent BOGO 50%-anchored blasts (5/8's "Styles Taking Over Your Feed," 5/4's welcome offer), this one trades the flashier BOGO for a flat 20%, which is honest but slightly less punchy for a win-back moment where you'd expect the bigger incentive.
- The subject line undersells it — "Check out what's new" is generic filler that doesn't hint at a 20% code, and the win-back angle only shows up once you open. Overall this is a solid, on-brand execution let down by a subject/preview mismatch with the actual offer.

## What's working

- The "HELLO, STRANGER!" headline immediately signals this is a win-back/re-engagement send, not a generic blast.
- The 20% off code is large, high-contrast, and impossible to miss — no scrolling needed to find the offer.
- Two clear "SHOP NOW" CTAs (hero card + offer block) reinforce the same action without redundant clutter.
- Full nav ladder (Women/Men/Kids/Clothing/New Arrivals/Sale) gives multiple entry points if the hero offer isn't a match.
- Skechers Plus and Afterpay/curbside modules add real utility below the fold without competing with the primary offer.

## What's weak

- Subject line doesn't mention the 20% off or win-back angle at all — a missed opportunity to lead with the strongest asset.
- The hero collage models/product shots (blazer pants, running shoes) don't clearly signal who this is for — no obvious gender/category focus.
- All-caps styling ("HELLO, STRANGER!", "GIVE US ANOTHER TRY...", "SHOP ADDITIONAL STYLES") throughout feels shouty rather than warm, undercutting the "we missed you" emotional tone.
- No urgency device (countdown, "expires in X days") despite fine print revealing the offer actually runs through 2026-09-24 — that's a long window that could've been used as a "you have all month" nudge or tightened for urgency.
- No product-level imagery/pricing near the offer block itself — it's all typography, so there's nothing concrete to click toward besides "SHOP NOW."

## Recommendations

- 1. Rewrite the subject to lead with the offer, since that's the strongest asset and it's currently buried: pair a win-back tone with the 20% code up front.
- 2. Add a visible expiration date/urgency line near the coupon code — the fine print already has 9/24/2026, surface it as "Ends 9/24" near the CTA.
- 3. Drop or soften some all-caps blocks (especially body copy under the headline) to make the "we missed you" tone feel more human than commanding.
- 4. Add 2-3 product thumbnails under the offer block (e.g., "Trending now") so there's something concrete to click besides a text-only CTA.
- 5. Consider swapping the flat 20% for a BOGO-style offer or bundling both, consistent with the brand's stronger-performing sends (5/8 "Styles Taking Over Your Feed" scored 8/10 with BOGO 50%).

# Technical Audit: "We've missed you! Check out what's new." (SKECHERS)

## 1. Technical Summary
This is an SFMC-generated win-back email with multiple third-party tracking pixels and largely legacy (HTTP, non-responsive-image) markup; QA automation flags a 0-char plain-text part and missing unsubscribe/authentication headers as the most material issues.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Tracking infrastructure is heavy: an open-tracking pixel (`click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx?...`), two Krux/Salesforce DMP pixels (`beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif`), an ad-impression pixel (`beacon.krxd.net/1x1_ad_impression.gif`), a Return Path pixel (`pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif`, note: missing `https://`/`http://` scheme prefix — malformed `src`), and a MovableInk personalization pixel (`ink1000.com/p/up/...`).
- 30 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the automated HTTP prober (expected/by design for redirect domains — not itself a defect, but means click-through destinations were not verified in this pass).
- The Return Path pixel's `src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?..."` lacks a protocol; in an HTML `<img>` tag this will resolve as a relative path from the render context rather than fetch the intended host, silently breaking that pixel.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 8 images confirmed served over `http://` instead of `https://` (Skechers logo, hero image, App Store/Google Play badges, AfterPay badge, and social icons for Instagram/Facebook/Pinterest/YouTube — full list in QA data above). Mixed-content blocking in modern mail clients (Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail) may prevent these from rendering.
- 4 images missing `alt` text: `o.gif` (MovableInk pixel — cosmetic since it's 1x1), and three content images (`49468f73-...png`, `00100b23-...png`, `fc08601a-...png`) whose semantic role isn't visible in the truncated source — if these are non-decorative content/CTA images, missing alt text is a real accessibility gap for screen-reader users.
- Markup targets legacy Outlook/mso and Gmail-specific hacks (conditional comments, `mso-table-lspace`, `.ExternalClass`), consistent with an SFMC template; no structural rendering errors found in the visible source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No merge-tag syntax (e.g., `%%FirstName%%`, `{{...}}`, AMPscript `%%=...=%%`) visible in the truncated source — no issues found in the reviewed portion.
- MovableInk pixel includes `mi_u=8108862811` (user/subscriber ID) and `mi_ecmp=TRG_US_EN_WINBACKENGAGE_1_06302026_B` (campaign ID) as personalization/targeting parameters — functioning as expected for MI dynamic content, no defect.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL/WARN]** `List-Unsubscribe` header not found — either not sent or not preserved by the AgentMail relay. CAN-SPAM does not strictly require this header (a body-level unsubscribe link suffices), but Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements (effective 2024) mandate one-click unsubscribe via `List-Unsubscribe` + `List-Unsubscribe-Post` for bulk senders — both are absent here.
- **[FAIL/WARN]** `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found — one-click unsubscribe unsupported per current header data.
- **[WARN]** `Authentication-Results` header not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be confirmed from available data (may be an AgentMail relay artifact rather than a true auth failure; recommend confirming against the original inbound headers, not just the relay-captured copy).
- Truncated source does not show the physical mailing address or unsubscribe link required by CAN-SPAM — cannot confirm compliance without the full footer HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Campaign identifier `TRG_US_EN_WINBACKENGAGE_1_06302026_B` is consistent across the Krux ad-impression pixel and the MovableInk pixel (`mi_ecmp`), indicating coherent cross-vendor campaign tagging.
- No UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) visible on any link in the truncated source — all outbound CTAs route through the SFMC click-tracking redirector (`click.emails.skechers.com`), so UTM attribution, if present, would be applied server-side at redirect resolution and is not verifiable from the raw HTML alone.
- Cannot confirm landing-page alignment since destination URLs are masked behind tracking redirects (30 links skipped by prober, per QA data).

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the malformed Return Path pixel `src` (`pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?...`) by adding `https://`.
2. Migrate all `http://` image assets (logo, hero, badges, social icons) to `https://` — 8 confirmed instances — to avoid mixed-content blocking.
3. Add `alt` attributes to the 3 non-decorative content images missing them.
4. Populate the plain-text MIME part — it is currently 0 chars, which hurts deliverability/spam scoring and breaks accessibility for text-only clients.
5. Add `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers to meet Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements for one-click unsubscribe.
6. Verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status directly against the original send (not just the AgentMail-relayed copy) since `Authentication-Results` wasn't captured here.
7. If UTM parameters are required for downstream analytics, confirm they're appended at the SFMC redirect layer (`click.emails.skechers.com`) since they aren't visible in the raw link markup.
