# Early access is open! 30% off sitewide for members
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- This is the strongest send in the recent Black Friday run. The "30% OFF SITEWIDE" numeral is huge, on a maroon "Skechers Plus Member Exclusive" hero, and the eye lands on it immediately. It beats the 11/13–11/17 sends, which repeated "BOGO 50% off or 20% off one pair" with a fallback condition. A flat, simple discount for members is a real escalation and feels like an earned perk.
- The body is a standard category grid (Women, Men, Kids, Apparel) plus a $49.99 Doorbuster block. It's clean and renders without bugs. But nothing in it is a specific product. Every tile goes to a category, and the "Shop Additional Styles" list repeats the same destinations. The fine print is dense and sits well below the hero. The email says "Early access" but the deadline is 11/22, so the exclusivity is weak.
- Verdict: a good, honest, high-contrast promo. It gets the offer right and the product merchandising wrong. It's a solid 7 to 8, held back by the lack of hero products and the repetitive navigation.

## What's working

- The 30% OFF numeral is oversized and high contrast, so the offer reads in one second.
- The "Member Exclusive" and "Skechers Plus" branding makes this a loyalty perk, and the subject delivers on it.
- "Discount auto-applied at cart" removes the code-hunting friction, and the deadline "Valid online only thru 11/22" is visible right under the CTA.
- The four-tile category grid with real models and clear "SHOP WOMEN'S / MEN'S / KIDS' / APPAREL" buttons lets different shoppers self-select.
- The $49.99 Doorbuster block adds a second, concrete price anchor.

## What's weak

- No individual shoe or product is shown, only lifestyle models with category words overlaid. Nothing tells the reader what the discount buys.
- The "SHOP ADDITIONAL STYLES" list repeats Women, Men and Kids right after the grid, which adds length without new information.
- "Exclusions apply" and "not stackable" sit in small text under the CTA, and the long fine print at the bottom is hard to read. That slightly undercuts the "sitewide" claim.
- The "Early access" framing is thin. The window runs 11/18 to 11/22 with no public sale date, so the urgency is generic.
- There are no reviews or social proof, which the 11/16 bestseller roundup did include.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace one or two category tiles with three or four named bestsellers (for example Slip-ins) showing the original price and the 30% price. That is the biggest lift to click-through.
- 2. Add a real countdown or "ends Sunday 11/22" in the hero, and say when everyone else gets the sale.
- 3. Cut the duplicate "Shop additional styles" block, or turn it into a Sale/Gift Guide pair.
- 4. Move a one-line "Exclusions: gift cards, select styles" up under the CTA and shorten the fine print.
- 5. Add a social-proof strip, such as a star rating or "most-loved Slip-ins".

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

The issues the QA data and source confirm are two non-HTTPS images without alt text, accessibility-hostile viewport and CSS rules, and unverifiable compliance headers. The source is truncated and 33 tracking links weren't probed, so body-level checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 33 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped, so none of their destinations are verified.
- **Third-party pixel over HTTP:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108862811&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_SITESELECT_FULL_EN_11182026`.
  - It is a plain-HTTP open pixel on a domain different from the sender (`emails.skechers.com`).
  - Clients that block mixed or insecure content won't load it, so opens will be undercounted.
  - `mi_u` is a per-recipient identifier sent in cleartext. I can't tell from the source what it encodes.
- **Favicon link:** `<link rel="icon" href="http://image.emails.nl.skechers.com/...">` is HTTP and on a different subdomain (`emails.nl.`) than the other assets (`image.emails.`). Favicons have no effect in email clients, so this is hygiene only.
- **Gmail annotation URLs:** All three `PromotionCard` entries point to `https://www.skechers.com/black-friday-cyber-deals`, with no tracking parameters.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):**
  - `o.gif` is a tracking pixel, so `alt=""` is acceptable but should be explicit.
  - `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` is a content-hosting image, so a missing alt is a real defect.
- **Mixed content (QA-confirmed):** `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-...png` is non-HTTPS and may be blocked. The same host serves the JSON-LD images over HTTPS, so HTTPS is available.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1" />` disables pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Focus outline:** `a { outline: none; }` removes keyboard focus indicators.
- **Text sizing:** `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }` blocks user text scaling on iOS.
- **Language:** The `<html>` element has no `lang` attribute. Only `xmlns`, `xmlns:v` and `xmlns:o` are present.
- **Title:** The `<title>` is the generic "Skechers&reg;" rather than the subject line. This is minor.
