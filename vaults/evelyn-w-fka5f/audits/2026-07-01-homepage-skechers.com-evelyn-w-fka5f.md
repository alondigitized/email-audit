# Homepage snapshot · skechers.com · 2026-07-01
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-01**
## Executive summary

- Okay, first thing I see walking into skechers.com is a big, bold 4th of July BOGO deal slapped right in my face — and honestly that's kind of working on me because I've been eyeing another pair of Slip-ins for summer. This feels like a deal-first brand right now, which I get, but there's zero lifestyle energy here — no one who looks like me, no "mom on the go" vibes, just a patriotic coupon banner. It's effective in a blunt-instrument way.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% sticky bar** — the deal is declared before I've even scrolled a pixel; as someone who checks sale prices first, this immediately grabs me
- **Skechers Plus "Member Exclusive" callout** — I actually am in the loyalty program and seeing "Extra 15% OFF with Code: SALE15" on top of BOGO makes me feel rewarded, not just marketed to
- **"Slip-ins" as the first quick-nav category** — this is my lane and it's one tap; whoever arranged that nav order knew what they were doing
- **"Automatically Applied at Cart"** — I hate hunting for promo codes, so spelling out that the BOGO activates itself removes a friction point I've rage-quit on before
- **"Recently Viewed" tab** — smart for repeat visitors like me; I can pick up right where I left off

## What's weak

- **Zero lifestyle imagery** — the entire hero is a deal graphic with no people, no product shots, nothing that shows me what I'm actually buying; as a mom scrolling during nap time, I want to *see* the shoes
- **No free shipping mention** — free shipping is a huge motivator for me and it's completely absent from this viewport; I know Skechers usually offers it but I can't see it here
- **No CTA button in the hero** — the deal is screaming at me but there's no "Shop Slip-ins" or "Shop the Sale" button; "Shop" as a tiny text link next to the promo code bar is undersized for this much offer energy
- **"Exclusions Apply*"** — no asterisk breakdown visible; I've been burned by exclusions before and this makes me a little suspicious
- **No product previews or rails** — everything below the hero is cut off; I have no idea what's actually on sale without scrolling deeper

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop Slip-ins" button directly inside the BOGO hero** — the deal is compelling but there's no obvious next step; a button that drops me into the Slip-ins sale filter would close that gap
- **Swap at least half the hero real estate for a lifestyle shot** — a mom in Slip-ins at the playground or farmers market communicates comfort *and* the deal at the same time; pure coupon graphics feel a little impersonal
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold in the secondary bar** — something like "Free Shipping on $50+ | BOGO 50% Applied" in that same banner bar would catch my attention immediately
- **Expand the "Exclusions Apply*" to a tooltip or inline line** — even "Excludes Clearance" shown inline would eliminate the trust hesitation
