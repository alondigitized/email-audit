# Our biggest sale of the year is now live!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the full-price Black Friday launch, and it works as a billboard. A black hero reads "30%-50% OFF SITEWIDE" in huge red type. Under it sits a dashed-border member strip: "Extra 15% off everything, code BF2026". The eye lands on the offer first, and the stacked member discount is the best thing in the send.
- The email is also very busy. A subscriber has seen this campaign for over a week: the 11/18 early access, the 11/22 "almost up" send, and this morning's "Hours left" final call. The hero is strong, but below it come four category tiles, a doorbuster block, a product row, a nav list, and a wall of fine print. The "30%-50%" range blurs the message, and the extra 15% code needs a manual copy-paste.
- Judgement: a solid, well-built promotional send that mostly repeats beats we've already seen. It will convert existing intent but adds little new reason to click.

## What's working

- The black and red hero with "30%-50% OFF SITEWIDE" and a "SHOP THE SALE" button is unmissable and clean.
- The member stack ("EXTRA 15% OFF EVERYTHING", code BF2026) gives Plus members a concrete reason to log in.
- The four category tiles (Shoes 30%, Boots/Slippers/Outdoor 40%, Apparel 50%, Sale up to 50%) have red badges that show the discount by category at a glance.
- The "Doorbuster Deals starting at $49.99" block gives a specific price anchor with a clear button.
- The deadline is stated ("thru 11/29"), and there are no broken images or layout bugs.

## What's weak

- The "30%-50%" range plus separate category percentages means the shopper has to work out which discount applies to them.
- Every tile uses a generic "SHOP NOW" button, and the product row has no prices and tiny captions.
- The fine print is huge and dense, and the "valid online only" caveat sits in small grey text under the hero.
- The member promo code must be typed manually, which adds friction right where the best offer is.
- The send is the fourth near-identical creative in a week. The 11/23 "Hours left" email already carried the same deadline pressure.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one number. Either "Up to 50% off" or "30% off + extra 15% for members", not both in the hero.
- 2. Auto-apply the member extra for logged-in Plus members, or add a one-tap "copy code" button.
- 3. Put prices and savings on the product row ("$49.99, was $90") and link each shoe to its own product page.
- 4. Give the category buttons distinct copy ("Shop 30% off shoes", "Shop 50% off apparel").
- 5. Cut the fine print to two lines and link out to terms.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30–50% off everything + extra 15% for Plus members`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Black Friday is live: sneakers from $49.99`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code BF2026 for an extra 15% off. Online through 11/29.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Doorbusters from $49.99, plus 50% off apparel. Sale ends 11/29.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's head markup and tracking pixel have a few minor rendering and accessibility gaps. The compliance and authentication checks could not be verified, because the relay did not capture the headers. The QA run scored 57% with 0 failures and 3 warnings. The HTML source is truncated at `<body…`, so body-level findings are limited to what the QA scanner reported.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 42 tracking links were skipped as click-redirect domains, so their destinations and status codes are unverified.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108862811&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_BLACKFRI_FULL_EN_11232026` loads over plain HTTP, so it may be blocked or rewritten. If it is, open tracking from this pixel is lost. The `mi_ecmp` value matches the `utm_campaign` value in the JSON-LD URLs, so the campaign ID is consistent.
- **Mixed-domain assets:** The favicon uses `image.emails.nl.skechers.com`, while the other assets use `image.emails.skechers.com`. This is low impact, since favicons aren't used in email clients.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `o.gif` (the tracking pixel) and `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` have no alt attribute. The pixel should have `alt=""`. The PNG is unconfirmed, because its role in the body isn't visible in the truncated source. If it is content, it needs meaningful alt text.
- **HTTP image source:** `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` uses HTTP and may be blocked. The other assets on that host are served over HTTPS, so this one is an inconsistency.
- **Missing `lang`:** The `<html>` tag has no `lang` attribute, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile.
- **Focus outline removed:** `a { outline: none; }` removes keyboard focus indicators.
- **Charset declaration late:** `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" … charset=utf-8>` comes after about 1.5 KB of JSON-LD. Some parsers expect the charset declaration within the first 1024 bytes. The MIME `Content-Type` header normally covers this, so the risk is low.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Skechers&reg;</title>` doesn't describe this campaign.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The body is truncated, so this isn't a full check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No evidence of a problem:** `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results` were not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip or not capture them. These are unverified gaps, not confirmed defects. Check the raw headers in the original delivery. The sender is `no-reply@emails.skechers.com`.
- **Footer not checked:** The physical address and unsubscribe link in the footer could not be checked, because the source is truncated.
- **Reply-to:** A `no-reply` From address is not a CAN-SPAM violation by itself.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **JSON-LD URLs are consistent:** Both PromotionCard entries point to `https://www.skechers.com/black-friday-cyber-deals/?srule=top-sellers&src=2026Q4BLACKFRIDAY&utm_source=trig&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=PRO_US_NA_U_BLACKFRI_FULL_EN_11232026&utm_content=annotation&utm_term=googleannotation1`. UTM naming matches the campaign ID, and the destination matches the sale theme.
- **Identical URLs:** Positions 1 and 2 use the same URL, so the two annotation cards can't be told apart in attribution.
- **Offer window dates:** The DiscountOffer has `availabilityStarts: 2026-11-23T00:11:00Z` and `availabilityEnds: 2026-11-29T23:11:59Z`. The odd minute values (`00:11`, `23:11`) look like possible typos, and they don't align to a clean midnight boundary.
- **Body links unverified:** The 42 body links were not probed, so UTM presence and landing-page alignment there are unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve `o.gif` and `dde00662-….png` over HTTPS, and add `alt=""` to the pixel and meaningful alt text to the PNG if it is content.
2. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, remove `maximum-scale=1`, and restore focus styles (`a:focus { outline: … }`).
3. Move `<meta charset>` above the JSON-LD script so it sits within the first 1024 bytes.
4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC from the original message headers, not the relay copy.
5. Re-run the QA with the full HTML and HEAD-probe a sample of the 42 tracking links through the redirect chain to confirm final URLs and UTMs.
6. Check the JSON-LD offer timestamps, and give the two PromotionCards distinct `utm_content` values.
