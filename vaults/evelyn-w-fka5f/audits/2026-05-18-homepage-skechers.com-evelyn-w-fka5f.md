# Homepage snapshot · skechers.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- OK so I opened Skechers and honestly my first thought was — oh, they *know* me. Hands Free Slip-ins is literally the category I live in as a mom who needs to get out the door fast. The BOGO 50% banner at the top plus a members-only reward offer made me stop scrolling immediately. This feels like a brand that's leaning hard into convenience and savings right now, which is exactly my language.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF top banner** — this is the first thing I see and it's exactly the kind of deal that makes me stop. Applied at cart is low friction.
- **Hands Free Slip-ins hero** — as a mom who's always rushing, slip-on tech speaks directly to me. No laces = sold.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA** — big, high-contrast, centered button. No guessing where to tap.
- **Members-only $5 reward strip** — even though it requires login, the "Now until 5/25!" deadline is a real urgency signal I notice.
- **Women / Men / Kids / Recently Viewed tab row** — I can get to my category in literally one tap. Love that.
- **Lifestyle imagery** — the woman by the pool in a summer dress wearing Skechers feels aspirational but realistic for someone like me, not a runway model.

## What's weak

- **"SHOP NOW" CTA is too generic** — it could say "Shop Slip-ins" or "Shop Women's Slip-ins" and it would convert way better for me specifically.
- **Members reward requires login to unlock** — putting "Login | details" in that strip adds steps right up front; I'm already half-gone if I have to create an account just to understand the offer.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — I always look for this before I commit. It's not above the fold, which is a miss for someone like me who shops online for convenience.
- **No product shown prominently** — the hero is lifestyle/campaign imagery, not a shoe I can actually tap into. I want to see the specific slip-in style they're selling.
- **Both male and female models share the hero** — dilutes the message a bit; a tighter women's-only frame would feel more targeted to me.

## Recommendations

- **Rename the CTA button** from "SHOP NOW" to "Shop Women's Slip-ins" — one small copy change, immediately more relevant to me and the majority of Skechers' online buyer base.
- **Show free shipping threshold in the top bar** alongside the BOGO — something like "BOGO 50% OFF + Free Shipping over $50" in that blue strip removes a big hesitation before I even start browsing.
- **Add a product carousel below the hero** — three to four actual slip-in styles I can tap directly from the landing screen; right now there's nothing shoppable above the tab row.
- **Simplify the members reward strip** — lead with the benefit ("Earn $5 on $75+") and move the Login CTA to a smaller inline link so it doesn't feel like a gate.
