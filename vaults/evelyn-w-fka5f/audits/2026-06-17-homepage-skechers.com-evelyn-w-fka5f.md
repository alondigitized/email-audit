# Homepage snapshot · skechers.com · 2026-06-17
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-17**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so I open Skechers and the first full-screen image I see is the Statue of Liberty at sunset — and the big button says "SHOP BASKETBALL." I'm a mom who came here to look at Slip-ins for school pickup and weekend errands, not NBA fan merch. The BOGO 50% off banner caught my eye immediately, so it's not a total miss, and I do notice "Slip-ins" is the very first tab in the horizontal category strip — but the hero completely ignores me.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF banner** — it's the very first thing I read and it instantly makes me want to keep going. Applied at cart = no code hunting = perfect for busy me.
- **"Slip-ins" is the first nav pill** — one tap to my category from right here, I don't need to dig through a mega-menu.
- **"25% OFF Apparel" secondary strip** — I'm always grabbing a hoodie or leggings too; this actually applies to me.
- **"Recently Viewed" tab** — signals the site knows me and makes it easy to pick up where I left off.
- **Clean render** — no janky layout shifts or broken images; the video autoplay has visible pause/mute controls which I appreciate on mobile data.

## What's weak

- **The hero image is completely off-target for me** — the Statue of Liberty at sunset is cinematic but tells me nothing about shoes, comfort, or anything related to why I'm here. It reads like a campaign I wasn't invited to.
- **"SHOP BASKETBALL" CTA** — I almost tapped it by reflex because it's so dominant, then realized it would send me somewhere I don't care about. That's a near-miss frustration.
- **No free shipping callout** — I specifically came here partly hoping for a free shipping threshold. It's not visible anywhere above the fold.
- **Zero trust signals** — no review counts, no "free returns," no "4.7 stars from X customers." For a first or infrequent visit, this would make me hesitate.
- **No lifestyle imagery I relate to** — I see architecture, not a mom running errands in slip-ons. Zero representation of my actual life.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the basketball/Statue of Liberty hero for a Slip-ins lifestyle shot** — a mom at the farmers market or doing school pickup in Slip-ins would land for me (and probably a huge chunk of Skechers' actual customer base).
- **Rename or swap the primary CTA to match what I came for** — a "Shop Slip-ins" or "Shop Women's Comfort" button right beneath the hero would convert me in seconds.
- **Add a free-shipping threshold to the BOGO banner or a second strip** — "Free shipping on $75+" is the kind of detail that tips me from browsing to buying.
- **Drop a star-rating badge or trust callout near the CTA** — even just "4.8 stars · 2M+ reviews" would give me the push to tap confidently.
