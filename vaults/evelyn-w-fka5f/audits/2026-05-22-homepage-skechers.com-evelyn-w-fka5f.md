# Homepage snapshot · skechers.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- Skechers is clearly leaning into Memorial Day weekend right now — everything is red, white, and blue with a patriotic campaign front and center. As a brand it reads as friendly and accessible, not intimidating or fashion-snobby, which I appreciate. The BOGO deal and the "Slip-ins" nav tab are doing real work for me, but the hero imagery is pulling me in a pretty broad direction rather than speaking directly to what I actually came here for.

## What's working

- **"BOGO 50% OFF" top banner** — this is the first thing I see and it immediately makes me want to stay. A buy-one-get-one is exactly the kind of deal I'd text my sister about.
- **"Slip-ins" as the first nav tab** — whoever put Slip-ins first in the nav deserves a raise. That is my category and I didn't have to hunt for it.
- **Members Only strip with a real dollar amount ($5)** — "$5 Reward on $75+" is concrete. It's not "earn points," it's actual money, and a deadline (5/25) makes it feel real.
- **Patriotic campaign timing** — Memorial Day is three days away, so this doesn't feel stale at all. It makes the whole page feel like something is happening *right now*.

## What's weak

- **Hero doesn't speak to me** — "The Red, White & Blue Collection" is a broad seasonal campaign. As a busy mom who came here for comfortable, easy-footwear, there's nothing in that headline that says "this is for you."
- **No "Shop Now" CTA button above the fold** — The nav tabs are there but I have to already know to tap "Slip-ins." A clear hero button (even "Shop Slip-ins") would have me moving faster.
- **Female model reads fashion/resort, not relatable** — She's in a sleek navy dress, looks early-to-mid 20s and very fashion-forward. I'm a 32-year-old mom running errands; I need to see myself in the product, and right now I don't quite.
- **Cart shows 0 items** — Fine, it's a first visit, but no "recently viewed" or personalized hook means I'm starting cold with no momentum.

## Recommendations

- **Add a hero CTA button** — overlay a "Shop Slip-ins →" button on the hero image so tap destination is obvious, not implied by the nav.
- **Swap the female hero model or add a second lifestyle shot** — show someone closer to 30-35 doing something recognizably "life busy" (gym bag, kid's backpack, coffee in hand) wearing Slip-ins. The current model sells aspirational fashion, not relatable comfort.
- **Surface the free shipping threshold** — I can see BOGO and the $5 reward, but I don't know when shipping kicks in free. Adding "Free shipping on orders $65+" to the top banner (alongside BOGO) would close a mental loop I always have while shopping.
- **Pin the membership earning rate into the hero** — "BOGO 50% + earn $5 toward your next pair" in one line would collapse two separate strips into one compelling compound offer.
