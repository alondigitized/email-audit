# Homepage snapshot · skechers.com · 2026-06-10
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-10**
## Executive summary

- Okay, first impression? Skechers is clearly in summer-sale mode and they're coming in hot with that BOGO banner right at the top — hard to miss. The site feels friendly and accessible, like a brand that gets busy people who want comfortable shoes without a bunch of fuss. The fact that "Slip-ins" is literally the first nav category tells me they know who their customer is.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF banner** — it's the very first thing I see, in blue, no ambiguity. BOGO is the kind of deal that makes me think "I should grab a second pair for my kid while I'm here."
- **"Slip-ins" as the lead nav item** — whoever put that first knows that Slip-ins are the hero product for people like me. One tap and I'm exactly where I need to be.
- **Member Month strip** — "Earn 2X Points now thru 6/13" creates a soft deadline without feeling like a pressure tactic. I appreciate that it shows the actual date instead of vague "limited time."
- **Hero model** — the woman is wearing a casual but put-together look with what clearly look like slip-on flats. That's a relatable outfit for a school-run morning or a casual errand day. I see myself in that.
- **Arch Fit in the nav** — comfort tech callout right in the top nav signals that this brand gets my priorities beyond just aesthetics.

## What's weak

- **No explicit shop button above the fold** — the BOGO banner links to "details" (fine print vibes), and the hero has no overlaid CTA. I have to figure out on my own that I should tap the nav categories.
- **Split hero dilutes the message** — the man in the upper-right of the hero is just as prominent as the woman. If I wasn't already a Skechers shopper, I'd wonder who this homepage is actually for.
- **"Summer" headline is half-visible** — the campaign headline is getting cut off at the bottom of the viewport. On mobile, that's a missed opportunity to anchor the whole page in a clear seasonal message.
- **No free-shipping mention visible** — for an online shopper like me, free shipping is a huge motivator. I don't see it above the fold at all, which is a gap given Skechers almost certainly offers it.
- **No product imagery** — it's all lifestyle, no product close-ups. I can't tell what exact shoes they're wearing without squinting.

## Recommendations

- **Add a clear hero CTA button** — something like "Shop the BOGO Sale" overlaid on the hero image, in the same blue as the top banner. The eye gets to the hero and then has nowhere to go right now.
- **Pin a free-shipping callout to the Member Month strip** — something like "Member Month: 2X Points + Free Shipping over $X thru 6/13" makes that strip do double duty and gives me the shipping answer before I have to hunt for it.
- **Let the Summer headline fully load above the fold** — either shift the hero image up a bit or shorten the top strips so that the campaign headline is fully visible on a standard phone screen. Half a word is worse than no word.
- **Swap the dual-gender split hero for a single female lifestyle shot** — or at minimum make the woman the clear focal center. Evelyn-type shoppers are Skechers' bread and butter; the hero should reflect that without sharing the frame.
