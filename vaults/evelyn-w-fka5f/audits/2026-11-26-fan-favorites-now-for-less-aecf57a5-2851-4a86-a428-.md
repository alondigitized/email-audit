# Fan favorites, now for less
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth or fifth Black Friday send in a week from Skechers, and it reads like one. The red "30-50% OFF SITEWIDE" banner and the "Extra 15% off, code BF2026" member strip are the same offer structure as the 11/23 "Our biggest sale of the year is now live!" email. The new part is a social-proof angle: a family unwrapping gifts under "LOVED BY THOUSANDS," then four product tiles with customer quotes.
- The structure is clean and the offer is easy to find. The subject, "Fan favorites, now for less," promises a price cut on best sellers. The body doesn't deliver that. No product shows a price, a discount or a named hero, and the tiles are generic category entry points (Slip-ins, BOBS, Cozy Fit, Arch Fit). The quotes are a good trust move, but they are faded grey italic and hard to read.
- Overall it's a competent, safe, and forgettable send. It also repeats the 11/24 "coziest deals" structure with a different hero. Because the sale ends 11/29, the email needs a deadline, and it has none.

## What's working

- The red "30-50% OFF SITEWIDE" banner sits at the very top, so the offer is the first thing you see.
- The member strip ("Extra 15% off everything, CODE BF2026") gives Plus members a clear stacking incentive.
- The four-tile grid with customer quotes adds real social proof and splits shoppers by need (easy on, comfort, support).
- Each tile has paired Women's and Men's buttons, which are clear and specific.
- The footer has Klarna/Afterpay, app download and text signup, which cover several conversion paths.

## What's weak

- Nothing says "fan favorites" or "for less." There are no prices, no before and after, and no product names.
- The hero image is a family holiday scene with no product in focus. The shoes are tiny and hard to pick out.
- There is no deadline in the body. The offer end date (11/29) appears only in the fine print at the bottom.
- The quote text is light grey italic with weak contrast, and the quote marks are barely visible.
- The footer legal copy is dense, and the 30-50% range is vague about what is actually 50% off.
- The "SHOP BEST SELLERS" button, four tiles and eight buttons compete with each other, so there is no single hero CTA.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put real products and prices in the tiles ("Slip-ins $59.99, was $85") so the subject's promise is visible.
- 2. Add a deadline near the top: "Ends Sunday 11/29" or a countdown. This is a late-week send, and urgency is the cheapest lift.
- 3. Replace the family hero with a product hero, or overlay a hero shoe with its price.
- 4. Darken the quote text and trim it to one punchy line per tile.
- 5. Cut the tile buttons from eight to four, with a Women's/Men's toggle below.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "Fan favorites, now for less" (SKECHERS)

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is an SFMC-sent XHTML table layout with responsive CSS, a hidden preheader and several tracking pixels. Confirmed issues are a zoom-blocking viewport tag, two images without alt text on non-HTTPS sources, and unprobed tracking links. The compliance and authentication checks could not be verified because the headers weren't captured.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links not probed:** 41 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped, so none of them were checked for status or final destination.
- **Third-party pixels:** These sit in the hidden `<div style="display:none !important;">` after the preheader:
  - `https://beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif?partner=LprH63a7_sfmc_100007688_sha25&partner_uid=f03aefa3…` (the `partner_uid` looks like a SHA-256 hash of the recipient identifier)
  - `https://beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif?partner=…_subid&partner_uid=123064971`
  - `https://beacon.krxd.net/1x1_ad_impression.gif?confid=so3yexmpm&…`
  - These are third-party user-matching and ad-impression pixels. They add external dependencies and share recipient identifiers with a data partner, and some clients will block or flag them.
- **HTTP tracking pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108862811&mi_ecmp=MKG_US_NA_U_MULTI_ENGAGEDL14D_EN_11262026` loads over plain HTTP. It also carries a subscriber ID (`mi_u`) in the clear.
- **Open pixel:** The SFMC open pixel (`click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx?…`) is HTTPS, with `width="1" height="1" alt=""`. No issue.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` stops pinch-zoom on mobile, a WCAG 1.4.4 failure. Remove `maximum-scale=1`.
- **Missing alt text (confirmed by QA):**
  - `o.gif` (the ink1000 pixel above)
  - `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`
  - The second image is content-hosted, not a pixel, so a missing `alt` means screen readers and images-off clients get no text.
- **Mixed content:** Both images above use `http://`. The second one is on the brand's own image domain and may be blocked or flagged as insecure in some clients. The `<link rel="icon">` in `<head>` also uses `http://image.emails.nl.skechers.com/…`, which is a different subdomain (`emails.nl.`) from the one used for content images (`emails.`).
- **Tracking pixel markup:** The two `beacon.krxd.net` `usermatch` images use unquoted `width=0 height=0` and have no `alt`. This is minor because they are hidden.
- **Other markup:** `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }` disables iOS text resizing. The `.mobile-content`/`.desktop-content` swap at 768px leaves 640–767px viewports on the mobile variant. Neither could be checked against the full DOM because the source is truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible portion of the source. The source is truncated, so the body and footer were not checked. Resolved identifiers appear only inside tracking URLs: `mi_u=8108862811`, `partner_uid=123064971`.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** Both were not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed, not a verified failure**. The sender's own message headers would settle it. If they are really absent, RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe, which Gmail and Yahoo require for bulk senders, is not supported.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. Again this is most likely a relay artifact. Check the original delivery.
- **Footer:** The unsubscribe link and physical address are not in the truncated source, so they couldn't be verified.
- **From address:** `no-reply@emails.skechers.com` is a no-reply sender. This is not a violation, but replies go nowhere.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
UTM parameters and landing-page destinations can't be verified. All 41 links are SFMC click-redirect URLs that were not resolved, and the source is truncated. The campaign ID is consistent across pixels: `MKG_US_NA_U_MULTI_ENGAGEDL14D_EN_11262026` appears in both the krxd `campaignid` and the ink1000 `mi_ecmp`. The preheader promise ("Save 30-50% sitewide, plus an extra 15% off everything for members") can't be compared to the landing page without resolving the redirects.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
2. Add `alt` text to `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`, with descriptive text if it carries content or `alt=""` if it is decorative. Add `alt=""` to `o.gif` and the krxd pixels.
3. Serve `dde00662-….png`, the favicon and the ink1000 pixel over HTTPS, or drop the pixel if the vendor offers no HTTPS endpoint.
4. Review the krxd and ink1000 third-party pixels for privacy and consent, since they pass hashed and plain recipient IDs.
5. Re-run the audit against the raw sender message, not the relay copy, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC).
6. Resolve the 41 click-redirect links, for example by following them with a HEAD or GET request, to check status codes and that UTM parameters and landing pages match the offer.
7. Re-run with the full, untruncated HTML to check for merge-token leaks, the footer unsubscribe link, the postal address and the rest of the body's alt text.
