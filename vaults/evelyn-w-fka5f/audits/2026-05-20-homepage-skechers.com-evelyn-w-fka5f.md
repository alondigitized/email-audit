# Homepage snapshot · skechers.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- Opening Skechers on my phone, I'm immediately hit with a patriotic Red, White & Blue Collection push — clearly gearing up for Memorial Day. The BOGO 50% off banner is front and center, which honestly caught my attention right away, and there's a members-only $5 reward nudge that makes me want to log in. It reads like a brand that's trying to serve everyone — couples, moms, active people — but the hero skews a bit young and male for my taste.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF banner** — it's the very first thing I see, applied automatically at cart. As someone motivated by discounts, that's a real hook. No hunting for a promo code.
- **Members-only $5 reward callout** — time-bound ("until 5/25") and specific ($75+ threshold). That's a number I can calculate against.
- **"Slip-ins" in the nav** — I can see it's the first category listed, which tells me Skechers knows that's a high-demand product. One tap to get where I want.
- **Sandals in nav** — as a busy mom heading into summer, sandals are absolutely on my list, and they're right there.
- **Clean mobile layout** — no overlapping text, no broken images, icons are tappable-sized. This renders well on my phone.

## What's weak

- **Hero imagery is male-leaning** — the man in the foreground is larger and more prominent; the woman is cropped toward the right edge. I'm the primary shopper here and I'm not the focus.
- **"The Red, White & Blue Collection" hero copy** — it tells me what it IS, not why I care. There's no value prop ("perfect for the long weekend," "all-day comfort for every backyard moment").
- **Members login wall** — the $5 reward requires me to log in to see details. I'm in browse mode; friction this early could lose me.
- **No comfort/lifestyle copy in the hero** — as someone who values comfort and ease, there's nothing in this above-the-fold view signaling that Skechers is the comfort brand. It's all Americana aesthetic, no "feel-good all day" messaging.
- **Cart icon shows 0 items** — missed opportunity for a returning-shopper hook or loyalty balance display.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero to lead with a mom/lifestyle scene** — a woman with kids or in motion, not a couple posed separately. A line like "Comfort for every summer moment" under the collection name would bridge the patriotic theme with the actual benefit.
- **Surface the Slip-ins visually above the fold** — the nav label exists, but a small product thumbnail or "Shop Slip-ins →" quick-link strip just below the nav would reduce friction for the shopper who came specifically for that.
- **Remove the login gate on the $5 reward** — show the reward as already earned or earnable, and put the login prompt after. Reward first, friction second.
- **Add a deadline to the BOGO banner** — "BOGO 50% OFF — Ends 5/26" would create urgency without being manipulative, and it matches the Memorial Day framing.
