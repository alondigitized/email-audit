# 30% off early access is almost up ⏱️
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the final-call version of the 11/18 early-access send, and it works. The "LAST CHANCE!" black bar sits over a maroon hero with a huge "30% OFF SITEWIDE" numeral. The eye lands there first, and one deadline ("Valid online only thru 11/22") explains why the email is going out today. The 11/18 send ("Early access is open! 30% off sitewide for members") scored 8 on the same offer. This one drops the novelty and adds real urgency.
- Below the hero it becomes a generic catalog. Four category tiles (Slip-ins, Cozy Fit, Court & Classics, Performance) are followed by a Doorbuster block ("starting at $49.99"), size grids and a text nav. None of that pushes the deadline. The second offer competes with the first, and the dense legal copy undercuts the "honest offer" read. For a subscriber who has had 7+ Black Friday sends in a week, it's a clean reminder and nothing more.
- Verdict: strong top third, forgettable rest. It's a solid last-chance send that reads as more of the same promotion.

## What's working

- The "30% OFF SITEWIDE" numeral is oversized and high-contrast on maroon. It is unmistakable.
- The "LAST CHANCE!" bar plus "thru 11/22" gives credible, dated urgency.
- "Black Friday Early Access" and the Plus Member Exclusive branding make the member benefit clear.
- The "SHOP NOW" button and "Discount auto-applied at cart" remove checkout friction.
- Category tiles with model photography and labeled buttons give easy browsing paths.

## What's weak

- The Doorbuster block ($49.99) is a second headline offer. It splits attention from the 30% hero.
- The CTA buttons are small black bars with generic copy ("SHOP NOW"). The hero button is tiny next to the giant numeral.
- The size grids are a lot of space for a sitewide sale and add scroll weight. No product, price or review is shown.
- The legal text at the bottom is dense and tiny. Exclusions ("Exclusions apply") appear right under the hero, which makes a "sitewide" claim feel qualified.
- The email is at least the 8th Black Friday-themed send in a week. The creative is largely recycled, with no new product or news.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a countdown, or "Ends tonight at 11:59 PM PT", in the hero. The deadline is currently small grey text and should be the second-largest element.
- 2. Enlarge the hero CTA and make the copy specific, e.g. "SHOP 30% OFF NOW".
- 3. Merge the Doorbusters into one clearly secondary strip below the tiles, or cut them. Keep a single hero offer.
- 4. Replace the four generic category tiles with 3–4 actual bestseller products showing price and rating.
- 5. Drop the size grids, or move them lower, and shorten the footer legal copy to a short summary with a link.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's structure is mostly sound (table-based layout, `role="presentation"`, hidden preheader, responsive media queries). The issues I can confirm are two insecure `http://` images, missing alt text on two images, a viewport setting that blocks zoom, and JSON-LD that uses non-standard schema types. The HTML source is truncated, and the relay didn't capture the authentication and unsubscribe headers, so several compliance checks can't be confirmed either way.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracking pixel over HTTP from a third-party domain.** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108862811&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_SITESELECT_FULL_EN_11222026`
  - It uses plain HTTP, so clients that block or rewrite insecure content may drop it, which undercounts opens.
  - It also has no alt text.
- **44 tracking/click-redirect links were not probed (QA).** Their destinations and validity are unverified.
- **Mixed image hosts.** The `<link rel="icon">` uses `http://image.emails.nl.skechers.com/...`, a different subdomain (`emails.nl`) from the `image.emails.skechers.com` host used elsewhere. It is also plain HTTP. A favicon has no effect in email clients, so this is low impact, but it suggests leftover template config.
- The one fully visible link, the JSON-LD `PromotionCard` URL, is well-formed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA, confirmed).**
  - `o.gif` is the tracking pixel, so this is low impact.
  - `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` is a content image, so this is a real accessibility gap.
- **Insecure image source (QA, confirmed).** The `dde00662-...png` image loads over `http://` and may be blocked or flagged as mixed content in some clients and webmail. Other images in the same account use `https://`, so the HTTPS version of this host evidently exists.
- **Zoom restricted.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **No `lang` attribute.** The `<html>` tag has only the xmlns attributes (`xmlns`, `xmlns:v`, `xmlns:o`), so screen readers have no language hint.
- **Generic title.** `<title>Skechers&reg;</title>` doesn't match the subject line. It is minor, since most clients ignore it.
- **Mobile-first CSS.** `.mobile-content {display:block}` and `.desktop-content {display:none}` are the base styles, and the desktop override sits in `@media screen and (min-width:768px)`. In clients that strip or ignore media queries, only the mobile variant would render. I haven't confirmed this against the full body, which is truncated.
- **Preheader hidden correctly.** `div.preheader` uses `display:none !important` and `font-size:1px`. It shows "Don't miss doorbusters starting at $49.99". No issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[[…]]`). `mi_u=8108862811` is a resolved subscriber identifier. The body is truncated, so tokens further down can't be ruled out.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found (QA warning).** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture it. This is unconfirmed, not a proven defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found (QA warning).** Same caveat. One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules, so verify it on the raw original message.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sender is `no-reply@emails.skechers.com`, and `emails.` is the same subdomain that hosts the images.
- **Visible footer not verified.** The physical address and unsubscribe link are in the truncated portion, so CAN-SPAM body requirements can't be confirmed.
- **No-reply sender.** A `no-reply@` From address is not a CAN-SPAM violation by itself, but the message needs another working opt-out mechanism.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The `PromotionCard` URL is `https://www.skechers.com/black-friday-cyber-deals/?srule=top-sellers&src=2026Q4BFEARLYACCESS&utm_source=trig&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=PRO_US_NA_U_SITESELECT_FULL_EN_11222026&utm_content=annotation&utm_term=googleannotation1`.
  - UTM parameters are complete.
  - `utm_campaign` matches the `mi_ecmp` value on the tracking pixel (`PRO_US_NA_U_SITESELECT_FULL_EN_11222026`).
  - The `11222026` date matches the send date.
- **JSON-LD markup problems.**
  - `DiscountOffer` and `PromotionCard` are not valid schema.org types, and `availabilityEnds` is not a property of them.
  - Gmail's promotion annotations expect a `DiscountOffer` with specific fields (and are only supported for Promotions-tab messages), so non-standard markup is likely ignored.
  - `availabilityEnds: 2026-11-22T23:11:59Z` looks like a typo for `23:59:59`. If it is, the annotation would expire about 48 minutes early. This is an inference, not confirmed.
- **Not checked.**
  - The 44 tracked links were skipped, so their landing page alignment is unverified.
  - The annotation's `utm_source=trig` / `utm_content=annotation` differ from whatever the body links use, which is typical, but the body links were not visible.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Change both `http://` image sources, `dde00662-…png` and the `ink1000.com` pixel, to `https://`. Confirm the tracking vendor supports HTTPS.
2. Add a descriptive `alt` to `dde00662-…png`. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
4. Inspect the raw original message, not the relay copy, for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass and alignment).
5. Fix the JSON-LD: use valid schema.org types and verify the `availabilityEnds` time.
6. Re-run QA on the full, untruncated HTML. Include probes for the 44 tracked links, footer unsubscribe and address checks, and a merge-token scan.
7. Check the `mobile-content`/`desktop-content` pattern in clients that strip media queries, such as Gmail with non-Google accounts.
