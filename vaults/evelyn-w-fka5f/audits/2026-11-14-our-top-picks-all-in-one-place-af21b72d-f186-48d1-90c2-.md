# Our top picks, all in one place
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- This is a holiday gift guide that sits under a Black Friday countdown banner. The BOGO 50% off banner is the strongest element, and the offer is clear and prominent. The green "Holiday Gift Guide" hero then takes over visually with a vague "Get Shopping" CTA, so the offer and the hero compete for attention. The price-point tiles ($75, $100, $125 & under) are the most useful gifting tool in the email, but they sit below the hero.
- The body is a long, evenly weighted scroll: new arrivals, four category tiles, a gifts block, a nav list, then app and Klarna filler. Nothing in it says why a given product is worth buying. The new-arrivals shoes have no names, prices or reviews. They also sit in large white gaps that look like missing imagery, even though the shoes themselves render.
- Compared with the 2026-11-13 send ("Our #1 style is calling your name", 7/10), which led with one clear hero style, this one is a catalog. It's fine for browsing and weak for converting. Judgement: competent and seasonal, but generic and undifferentiated.

## What's working

- The navy BOGO 50% off banner at the top gives a concrete, scannable offer, with a 20% single-pair fallback.
- The price-point tiles ($75, $100, $125 & under) are a practical gifting filter.
- The seasonal hero is on-theme for the holidays and sets the tone immediately.
- The four category tiles (Cozy Fit, Slip-ins, Apparel, Accessories) are cleanly laid out with consistent black CTAs.
- The footer fine print discloses the offer dates (11/12–11/17) and its exclusions.

## What's weak

- The hero CTA, "Get Shopping", is generic, and the hero doesn't mention the BOGO offer, so the two messages are disconnected.
- The New Arrivals block has six unlabeled shoes, with no names, prices or links to specific products. The large white gaps between the rows look like missing content.
- The offer has no urgency. The "11/17" end date appears only in the fine print and not in the banner or hero.
- The "Join Skechers Plus" strip at the top is tiny, and its 1000-point bonus competes with the main offer.
- The email is very long, and the app, Klarna and "Sign up for texts" sections dilute the close.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the end date in the banner ("Ends 11/17") and make the hero CTA "Shop BOGO 50% Off Gifts". This is the single change that would lift the email most.
- 2. Move the price-point tiles directly under the banner and above the hero, or into the hero itself.
- 3. Replace the unlabeled new-arrivals grid with 3 named hero styles. Each should show a price and a star rating, and link to its product page.
- 4. Cut the filler sections (app download, Klarna, text sign-up) down to one line.
- 5. Give each category tile a benefit-led CTA, such as "Shop Slip-ins for Her".

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop by price: $75, $100, $125 & under. Ends 11/17.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based SFMC email with VML/MSO fallbacks. The automated QA found no hard failures, only warnings: two insecure, alt-less images, and header checks that couldn't run because of the AgentMail relay. The source is truncated, so the footer and body links were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links not verified:** 43 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains (likely `click.emails.skechers.com`). Destinations, redirect health and final landing URLs are unconfirmed.
- **Third-party beacons in the hidden block:**
  - `https://beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif?partner=LprH63a7_sfmc_100007688_sha25&partner_uid=f03aefa3…8628` (a SHA-256 value)
  - `https://beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif?partner=…_subid&partner_uid=123064971`
  - `https://beacon.krxd.net/1x1_ad_impression.gif?confid=so3yexmpm&advertiserid=100007688&campaignid=MKG_US_NA_U_MULTI_ENGAGEDL30D_EN_11142026`
  - These fire on open and pass a hashed identifier and subscriber ID to a third party. Each adds a request and a failure point.
- **Non-HTTPS tracking pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108862811&mi_ecmp=MKG_US_NA_U_MULTI_ENGAGEDL30D_EN_11142026` is plain HTTP. Clients that proxy images over HTTPS (Gmail, Apple MPP) may block or rewrite it, so open and attribution data from it is unreliable.
- **Unquoted attributes:** `width=0 height=0` on the krxd `<img>` tags is invalid XHTML 1.0 Transitional, which is the declared DOCTYPE. This is minor, since clients tolerate it.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):**
  - `o.gif` (ink1000 pixel). It has no alt attribute, though it is a tracking pixel.
  - `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`. It has no alt attribute and appears to be a content image.
- **Mixed content (QA-confirmed):** `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-….png` is served over HTTP and may be blocked. The `<link rel="icon">` also uses `http://image.emails.nl.skechers.com/...`. Email clients ignore it, but it shows the same HTTP default.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom on mobile, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Focus outline removed:** the `a { outline: none; }` rule removes the focus indicator on links in clients that honor `<style>`.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" …>` has no `lang`, so screen readers can't pick the language.
- **Text resizing disabled:** `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }` blocks text resizing on iOS.
- **Layout tables:** the first layout table has `role="presentation"`. The rest could not be checked because of the truncation.
- **Responsive CSS:** it is duplicated across three media-query breakpoints (375/480/640/680px). It is redundant but not broken.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source. Subscriber identifiers (`mi_u=8108862811`, `partner_uid=123064971`) are resolved.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found [WARN]:** the QA notes the relay may strip it. It is unconfirmed either way, and the sender can't be cleared on this.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found [WARN]:** RFC 8058 one-click support is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it, so this matters if it is truly absent.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found [WARN]:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The relay is the likely cause, not a sender fault. The From domain `emails.skechers.com` does differ from the image domain `image.emails.nl.skechers.com`, which is worth checking alignment on.
- **Footer unverified:** the visible unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are not in the truncated source, so they could not be confirmed.
- **Third-party data sharing:** the krxd pixels send a hashed email and subscriber ID to a third-party DMP. Confirm this is disclosed in the privacy policy.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters not verifiable:** all 43 links are click-redirect URLs that were not followed, so UTM presence and landing-page alignment are unconfirmed.
- **Campaign ID visible:** `MKG_US_NA_U_MULTI_ENGAGEDL30D_EN_11142026` appears in the pixel URLs and encodes the 2026-11-14 send date. Whether the same ID is passed to the site cannot be determined.
- **Offer alignment:** the preheader says "Double up and save with BOGO 50% off". Landing-page agreement could not be tested.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` to `dde00662-….png` (descriptive) and to `o.gif` (`alt=""`).
2. Move the `ink1000.com` pixel, the `image.emails.skechers.com` asset and the favicon link to HTTPS.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag, and drop `outline: none` from `a`, or add a `:focus` style.
4. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
5. Re-run the audit against the raw `.eml` or the sender's ESP preview to check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC, since the relay hides them.
6. Run the audit on the untruncated HTML to confirm the unsubscribe link, the postal address and the UTM parameters on the click-redirect links. Resolve a sample of the 43 redirects to check destinations.
7. Review whether the three krxd beacons are needed, given the extra requests and the hashed identifier they share.
