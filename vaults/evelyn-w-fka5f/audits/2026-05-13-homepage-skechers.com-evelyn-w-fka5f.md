# Homepage snapshot · skechers.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay, first impression: Skechers knows what I'm here for. Slip-ins are literally their whole hero right now, and as someone always hunting for shoes I can jam my feet into while chasing a toddler, that lands. The BOGO 50% banner at the very top is the kind of thing that makes me stop scrolling Instagram mid-nap-time. It feels like a practical, deals-forward brand having a decent seasonal moment — not flashy, but functional.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF banner at the very top** — this is exactly what gets me to stay. It's specific, it's applied automatically, and it's front and center before I even see the logo.
- **Hands Free Slip-ins hero** — the product name does the job. "Hands Free" is basically the tagline of every mom's shoe wishlist.
- **Woman in the hero wearing Slip-ins casually** — she's poolside in a sundress, not doing a 10K. That's relatable.
- **"SHOP NOW" button** — full-width, hard to miss, no ambiguity.
- **Spring Apparel Sale strip** — secondary deal means two reasons to stick around.
- **Category tabs (Women / Men / Kids' / Recently Viewed)** — I can get to Women's in one tap.

## What's weak

- **No free shipping mention above the fold** — this is a big motivator for me and it's nowhere on this screen. If there's a threshold, I want to know immediately.
- **"SHOP NOW" is generic** — the hero is clearly Slip-ins, but the CTA doesn't confirm I'm shopping Slip-ins specifically. A button that said "Shop Slip-ins" would remove all friction.
- **No trust signals** — no review count, no star ratings, no "free returns" badge. For a first-time or lapsed visitor, that's a gap.
- **Man in the hero** — the left half of the hero features a man prominently. I get that it's a mixed campaign, but for me it slightly dilutes the "this is for you" signal.
- **No urgency on the BOGO** — the banner doesn't say when it ends. A soft deadline ("this week only") would push me to act now instead of "I'll come back later."

## Recommendations

- **Change "SHOP NOW" to "Shop Women's Slip-ins"** — takes two seconds to ship, directly addresses the product shown in the hero, and reduces the click-to-category friction for women shoppers.
- **Add free shipping threshold to the top banner or just below the hero** — even "Free shipping on $50+" would noticeably lift conversion. I make budget decisions around this.
- **Swap the dual-model hero for a solo woman-with-kid lifestyle shot** — a mom slipping on her Slip-ins at the door or school pickup would speak directly to my use case and differentiate from the generic "couple by the pool" feel.
- **Add a star rating snippet to the hero area** — even just "4.7★ from 50,000+ reviews" under the CTA would give me the confidence nudge to tap instead of browse.
