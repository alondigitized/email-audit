# Comfort for every budget
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a price-tier gift send under a members-only Black Friday banner. The top bar reads "30% OFF SITEWIDE FOR MEMBERS" in white on maroon, with a "SHOP NOW" link. Below it, a hero shows a woman holding wrapped gifts, with red gift-tag graphics reading "Great Gifts, Great Prices." The body then splits into three tag-led price tiers: "Styles $50 and under," "$75 and under," and "$100 and under." Each tier has its own lifestyle photo and its own CTA.
- The tiered structure is a good idea. It tells the shopper what they can spend before they scroll. But the email carries two offers at once: 30% off for members and the "Styles $X and under" price points. The two don't connect. Three nearly identical photo-plus-button blocks make the email long and repetitive. The best-seller grid near the bottom has blank white space where product images should be, and I can't tell if those images failed to load or just haven't rendered.
- This lands as a competent but flat send. It's the third members-driven 30% message in four days, after 11/18 "Early access is open! 30% off sitewide for members" and 11/19 "Holiday Ready, from party to pajamas." The 11/18 numeral-led hero had more impact. Here the discount is a small banner and the gift-tag price points dominate.

## What's working

- The "30% OFF" numeral in the top banner is large and easy to read, and it names the member condition.
- The three price-ceiling tiers ($50, $75, $100) give a clear way to browse by budget, which matches the "Comfort for every budget" subject.
- The gift-tag graphics are seasonal and consistent across the hero and all three tiers.
- Each tier has its own green or red CTA button, so the next step is always visible.
- The footer is complete: Klarna/Afterpay, app download, and store locator.

## What's weak

- The best-seller grid has large blank gaps. Only the shoe thumbnails render, with no names, prices, or ratings.
- The email is very long. Three full-width hero photos for tiers that all say the same thing push the best-sellers well below the fold.
- The 30% member offer and the price-tier tags aren't connected. It's unclear whether 30% off applies on top of the "$50 and under" styles.
- The CTAs are all a generic "SHOP NOW." None of them says which tier or category it leads to.
- The AI-generated model labels sit on the photos, which cuts against the warm gift mood. The hero and the men's tier both carry them.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fold the member offer into the tier copy, e.g. "Styles $50 and under, plus 30% off for members," so there is one clear story.
- 2. Replace the three tall hero blocks with a compact three-tile row, or cut them to two, so the best-sellers appear sooner.
- 3. Fix or remove the best-seller grid. If it stays, show product names, prices, and a "members save 30%" line under each.
- 4. Rewrite the CTAs to be specific: "Shop styles under $50," "Shop under $75," "Shop under $100."
- 5. Add a deadline to the banner. The fine print says the offer ends 11/22, so the top banner should say it too.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Gifts under $50, $75, $100 + 30% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Members: 30% off gifts that fit your budget`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cozy slippers from $50, sneakers under $75. Member 30% off ends 11/22.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Early access ends tomorrow. Shop comfort by price.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` has no alt. If it is a content image, it is invisible to screen readers and to clients that block images. `o.gif` is a tracking pixel, so its missing alt is minor.
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **No document language:** `<html xmlns=...>` has no `lang` attribute, so screen readers can't select the correct pronunciation.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Skechers&reg;</title>` does not describe the message. This is minor.
- **Text-size override:** `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none }` prevents iOS text resizing.
- **Layout tables:** Only the first table visible has `role="presentation"`. I could not confirm the rest because the source is truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- The tracking pixel carries `mi_u=8108862811`, a resolved numeric recipient identifier, in a plaintext URL. It is a persistent ID passed to a third-party host over HTTP, so it can be seen in transit.
