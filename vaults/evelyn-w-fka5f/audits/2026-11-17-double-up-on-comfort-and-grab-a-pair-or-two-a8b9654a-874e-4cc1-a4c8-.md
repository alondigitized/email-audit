# Double up on comfort and grab a pair (or two)
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth Black Friday countdown send in a row from Skechers. The "LAST CHANCE" black bar sits above a maroon hero that reads "BUY ONE, GET ONE 50% OFF or 20% OFF 1 PAIR*" with a "SHOP NOW" button. The offer is strong and the hierarchy is clear. The urgency is real too, because the fine print says "Valid online only thru 11/17", which is today.
- The hero has a visible render bug. The "50% OFF" numeral sits in a dark flip-clock tile and looks garbled, so it reads like "00% OFF" or "10% OFF". This is the one number the whole email depends on, and the render makes it ambiguous.
- Below the hero the email falls back to a generic Women/Men/Kids split, price tiers and a best-sellers strip. The best sellers are small and unlabeled, with no prices, names or links I can see. It is a competent template, but it sells a category rather than one product, and it repeats a promo the subscriber has seen all week. Verdict: strong offer, weak execution of its key number, and heavy fatigue.

## What's working

- The "LAST CHANCE" bar plus "thru 11/17" gives credible same-day urgency.
- The BOGO 50% off with a 20% single-pair fallback is a clear, tiered offer.
- The maroon hero with white type and a black "SHOP NOW" button is high contrast and easy to find.
- The Women/Men/Kids blocks each have a large product shot and their own button, so routing is clear.
- The "Pick your price. Then double up." tiles ($50/$75/$100/$125) tie the offer to a budget, which is a useful shopping shortcut.

## What's weak

- The "50% OFF" numeral is garbled in the flip-clock graphic and reads like "00% OFF" or "10% OFF". It is the key number and it looks broken.
- The best-sellers row is small, has no prices, names or CTA, and leaves a large empty gap beneath the shoes.
- The offer language is cluttered: "BOGO 50% off OR 20% off 1 pair*" plus asterisked fine print makes the deal harder to read.
- This is the fifth near-identical countdown send in five days, after 11/13, 11/14, 11/15 and 11/16, so the creative is stale.
- The bottom third is crowded with a nav list, Klarna/Afterpay, an app download block and social icons, and it dilutes the close.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix or replace the flip-clock graphic with a plain, large "50% OFF" so the key number reads cleanly. This is the biggest single lift.
- 2. Rebuild the best-sellers row with larger images, names, prices and a "Shop best sellers" button.
- 3. State one offer in the hero, "Buy 1, get 1 50% off", and move the 20% fallback to a secondary line.
- 4. Vary the hero per send in a countdown series, or cut the cadence to avoid fatigue.
- 5. Trim the footer: keep the app and text sign-up, and drop the rest.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: buy 1, get 1 50% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: BOGO 50% off shoes`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Pick any pair under $50, $75, $100 or $125. Online only, ends 11/17.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Second pair half off, or 20% off one. Today is the last day.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "Double up on comfort and grab a pair (or two)"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has no hard failures: the QA run reported 0 issues and 4 warnings. The confirmed findings are two non-HTTPS, alt-less images (one a third-party tracking pixel) and accessibility-hostile viewport/CSS settings. The header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be verified, because the relay did not expose those headers. The HTML source was truncated, so footer and body checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **41 tracking links were skipped.** They are click-redirect domains, so the HTTP probe never ran. Final destinations and redirect health are unverified.
- **Third-party pixel over plaintext HTTP:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108862811&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_BUNDLE_FULL_EN_11172026`
  - It is not HTTPS and can be blocked or rewritten (mixed-content handling, proxy rewrites).
  - `mi_u=8108862811` is a 10-digit per-recipient identifier sent in cleartext. Confirm it is not a phone number or other PII.
  - It is hosted on a different domain (`ink1000.com`) from the sending domain (`emails.skechers.com`).
- **Possible typo in the JSON-LD:** `"availabilityEnds":"2026-11-17T23:11:59Z"`. This looks like a mistyped `23:59:59Z`. It could end the Gmail promo annotation about 48 minutes early.
- **Inconsistent image hosts:** the `<link rel="icon">` points at `http://image.emails.nl.skechers.com/...`. The body images use `image.emails.skechers.com`. This is a second subdomain served over HTTP. It is low impact, since favicons are ignored by most clients.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed by QA):**
  - `o.gif` (tracking pixel). Add `alt=""` and a `width="1" height="1"` attribute.
  - `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`, which is a content image and needs descriptive alt text.
- **HTTP image source:** `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`. Other images on the same host appear to use HTTPS in the JSON-LD, so this one is likely a template inconsistency.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. The `maximum-scale=1` setting prevents pinch zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resizing blocked:** `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }` disables user text scaling in WebKit clients.
- **Focus indicators removed:** `a { outline: none; text-decoration: none; }` removes keyboard focus visibility and link affordance.
- **Mobile/desktop content split:** `.mobile-content { display:block }` is the default. `.desktop-content` only shows under `@media (min-width:768px)`. Clients that ignore media queries, such as Outlook desktop (Word engine), would likely show only the mobile variant. This is inferred from the CSS, not tested.
- **Generic `<title>`:** it reads "Skechers®" and does not differ per campaign. This is a minor issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FirstName]` appear in the captured portion. The only recipient-specific value seen is `mi_u` in the tracking pixel (see section 2).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them. This is **unverified, not a confirmed defect**. Check the raw `.eml` from the original send. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment could not be verified. It is expected to be missing via the relay. Check `emails.skechers.com` DNS and a direct-delivery sample.
- **Footer not captured.** The source is truncated, so the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address cannot be confirmed.
- **Plain-text part:** 4763 of 6621 characters (72%) are URLs, which exceeds the 70% heuristic. This is a minor spam-filter risk, caused by long tracking URLs.
- **Sender address:** `no-reply@emails.skechers.com`. This is not a violation, but it removes a reply path.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters in the JSON-LD URLs.** They are `https://www.skechers.com/m/sitewide-offer/?prefn1=gender&prefv1=W`, `…prefv1=M` and `…prefv1=G%7CB`. Attribution probably relies on the ESP click-redirect wrappers. That cannot be confirmed, because the 41 tracked links were skipped.
- **Landing URLs are consistent with the BOGO 50% offer:** all three point to `/m/sitewide-offer/` and are filtered by gender (W, M, G|B). The actual page content and the in-body hrefs were not probed.
- **Campaign ID in the pixel:** `mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_BUNDLE_FULL_EN_11172026` is embedded in the pixel only. It was not seen on any landing link.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve `o.gif` and `dde00662-….png` over HTTPS. Update the `<link rel="icon">` host too.
2. Add `alt=""` to the pixel. Add meaningful alt text to `dde00662-….png`.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`. Restore `:focus` outlines for `a`.
4. Verify `availabilityEnds` in the JSON-LD (`23:11:59Z`, probably meant to be `23:59:59Z`).
5. Confirm what `mi_u` contains. If it is PII, stop passing it in plaintext through a third-party pixel.
6. Re-test from a direct-delivery copy of the message to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC). Capture the full footer.
7. Resolve the 41 tracking redirects in a follow-up pass. Confirm final URLs, UTM/campaign parameters and HTTP status.
8. Add a text-alternative trim, such as shorter URLs or fewer links, to bring plain-text URL density under 70%.
