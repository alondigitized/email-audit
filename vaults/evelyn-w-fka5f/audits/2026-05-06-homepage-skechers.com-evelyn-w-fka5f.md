# Homepage snapshot · skechers.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- OK so I opened this and honestly — they got me immediately. Mother's Day is *days away* and there's a mom and her little girl right in the hero, pink flowers everywhere, BOGO deal screaming at me from the top. This feels like a brand that knows exactly who's shopping right now. It's warm, it's timely, and it doesn't feel like they're trying too hard.

## What's working

- **The Mother's Day hero image** — a mom kissing her daughter's cheek, both in casual comfort wear and pink Skechers, surrounded by flowers. I see myself in this. It's not a runway model, it's a real-looking mom moment.
- **BOGO 50% OFF banner** — it's the very first thing on the page, auto-applied at cart. I don't have to remember a code. That removes friction I hate.
- **"Shop the Spring Apparel Sale! 25% OFF Apparel"** — two deals stacked means I feel like I landed at the right time. That feeling keeps me on the page.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA** — large, high-contrast black button. Zero ambiguity about what to do next.
- **"Recently Viewed" at the fold edge** — tiny but meaningful. It tells me the site remembers me, which nudges me toward logging in.

## What's weak

- **"SHOP NOW" is too generic** — it doesn't tell me *what* I'm shopping. "Shop Mother's Day Styles" or "Shop Women's Comfort" would send me somewhere relevant instead of a broad landing page.
- **No free shipping mention anywhere** — as someone who shops online primarily for convenience, I want to know the threshold upfront. Its absence makes the BOGO feel less complete.
- **No category shortcuts visible** — I have to tap the hamburger menu to find Slip-ins or Women's. One extra tap is one more chance I bounce, especially on mobile in a busy moment.
- **"Applied at Cart details" fine print link** — the word "details" right next to the BOGO makes me nervous. What are the exclusions? Right now I don't know, and that vague asterisk energy chips away at trust.

## Recommendations

- **Rename the CTA to "Shop Her Favorites"** or "Shop Women's Comfort Styles" — ties the button directly to gift-giving context and steers me into a relevant product set.
- **Add a free-shipping callout to the top banner strip** alongside the BOGO — even "Free shipping on orders $50+" next to the deal would lock in my add-to-cart decision.
- **Add two or three quick-tap category pills below the hero** (e.g., "Slip-ins · Sneakers · Wide Width") so I can get to my category without opening the full menu.
- **Swap or supplement the "details" link with a one-liner exclusion summary** right in the banner (e.g., "Applies to equal or lesser value item") so the offer reads as honest instead of hiding something.
