# Homepage snapshot · skechers.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- OK, I am actually into this. They put "Hands Free Slip-ins" front and center, which is basically screaming my name — I live in slip-ons. The BOGO deal in the top banner caught my eye immediately, and the Spring Apparel Sale strip right below it makes the page feel current and worth staying on. This feels like a casual-lifestyle brand that knows it has a comfort-first bestseller and is leaning into it hard right now.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF top banner** — I see that before anything else and it immediately makes me feel like I'm already winning. That's the kind of deal that makes me tap through.
- **"Hands Free Slip-ins" hero** — This product is made for someone like me. Being able to slip shoes on without bending over while holding a kid is genuinely useful, and the brand is naming it plainly.
- **The woman model near the pool** — She looks like a peer, not a runway model. Floral dress, relaxed setting, practical sandal-style slip-in. I see myself in that image.
- **"Recently Viewed" tab** — Small, but it tells me the site remembers me. That frictionless return experience matters when I'm shopping on my phone in five-minute windows.
- **Spring Apparel Sale strip** — Two separate promos (BOGO shoes + apparel sale) in the first scroll means there's something for multiple trip purposes.

## What's weak

- **"SHOP NOW" CTA is too generic** — This button leads... somewhere. I'd tap it but with zero confidence about where I land. "Shop Women's Slip-ins" or even "Shop the BOGO" would convert me faster.
- **Man takes up the larger share of the hero** — He's on the left side and occupies more visual real estate than the woman. Since I'm shopping for myself, it's slightly off that the dominant figure isn't the one I identify with.
- **Background watermark text ("SLIDE GLIDE")** — The large repeated letters behind the models create visual noise. The eye keeps snagging on the background instead of the models and product copy.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — I shop online for convenience, and free shipping is a big motivator for me. I don't see anything about it here, which leaves an incentive gap.
- **No trust signals or reviews above the fold** — No star ratings, no "free returns," no security badge. For a first visit on mobile, that's a missed reassurance.

## Recommendations

- **Change "SHOP NOW" to "Shop Women's Slip-ins"** — Simple copy swap, routes me directly into my category and ties back to the exact product in the hero. Zero dev work.
- **Swap or rebalance the two-model split** — Either lead with the woman (she's the lifestyle match for the slip-ins buyer) or use a single-model hero of a mom-in-motion type scene to reduce the visual confusion.
- **Add a free-shipping callout to the top banner or hero** — Even "Free shipping on orders $50+" as a third badge in the promo strip would tip my decision. It's a purchase motivator the page is currently leaving on the table.
- **Drop the "SLIDE GLIDE" watermark background or reduce its opacity significantly** — It's competing with the product messaging and making the hero feel cluttered on a small phone screen.
