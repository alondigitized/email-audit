# Homepage snapshot · skechers.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I have to be honest — opening Skechers right now kind of felt like the algorithm read my mind. It's Mother's Day season and there is literally a mom-and-daughter image front and center with a BOGO deal staring me in the face. This looks like a family-friendly comfort brand that is fully leaning into the gifting moment, and I am here for it.

## What's working

- **Mother's Day hero image** — a woman who looks like she could be my age kissing her daughter, both wearing matching pink shoes. I felt seen instantly. This is not a stock-runway model moment; it's a real-life lifestyle vibe.
- **BOGO 50% OFF top banner** — bright blue, first thing I read, and it's the kind of deal that makes me think "I should pick up a pair for myself and my kid." That's exactly how my brain works when I'm buying shoes.
- **"25% OFF Apparel" secondary strip** — layered promotions. Even if I came for shoes, I'm now curious about apparel. Good upsell bait.
- **"Mother's Day Shop" campaign framing** — the floral design and warm pink palette feel intentional and seasonal, not generic. It signals someone put thought into this moment.
- **"Recently Viewed" tab** — barely peeking in at the bottom but I noticed it. As someone who shops in multiple short sessions between school drop-off and work, that is genuinely useful.

## What's weak

- **"SHOP NOW" CTA is too generic** — I'm in the Mother's Day Shop, so what am I shopping? For myself? For my mom? As a gift? Swapping to "Shop Her Styles" or "Shop Slip-ins" would immediately filter my intent and reduce friction.
- **No free-shipping callout above the fold** — as someone who is heavily motivated by free shipping, the absence of that info is a missed opportunity. I shouldn't have to scroll or guess.
- **No product visibility at all above the fold** — I can't see a single shoe. I know I'm in a shoe store, but I don't know if there's something I'd actually want before I tap deeper.
- **No sizing or comfort language** — words like "memory foam," "arch support," or "slip-on" would catch my attention immediately and reinforce why I'm here. The hero is pure lifestyle; there's zero product claim.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "SHOP NOW" for intent-specific CTA copy** like "Shop Women's Slip-ins" or "Shop Her Gift" to reduce the decision tax and route me faster.
- **Add a free-shipping threshold line** directly under the BOGO banner — something like "Free shipping on orders $50+" would keep me from bouncing to check shipping at checkout.
- **Pull one hero product forward** — a small "featured shoe" tile with a name and one comfort keyword ("Slip-in, Memory Foam") alongside the lifestyle image would bridge emotion to purchase intent.
- **Surface the loyalty/rewards hook** in the top banner or below the hero — even just "Skechers Plus members earn points on this purchase" gives returning shoppers a reason to log in.
