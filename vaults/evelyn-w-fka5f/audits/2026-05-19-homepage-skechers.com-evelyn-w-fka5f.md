# Homepage snapshot · skechers.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay, first impression: Skechers knows what they're doing this week. The Slip-ins are front and center, there's a solid BOGO deal screaming at me from the top, and even a little loyalty perk for members — all before I even scroll. It's a comfortable, practical brand that's clearly pushing a comfort-tech hero product right now, which honestly is right up my alley.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF banner**: It's the first thing I see, in a bold blue strip. No decoding required — buy two pairs, save half on the second. Done.
- **Hands Free Slip-ins hero**: As a mom who's putting on shoes while telling someone to grab their backpack, slip-ons with zero hands needed? Yes, please. The product/copy alignment is genuinely good.
- **Members-Only strip**: "Now until 5/25! Members Only - Earn a $5 Reward on $75+" creates a soft nudge to log in before shopping. The deadline makes it feel real.
- **"Recently Viewed" tab in the bottom nav**: That's a convenience feature I'd actually use on my phone when I want to pick up where I left off.
- **Mobile layout**: Clean single-column, big tap targets, nothing crammed. Feels built for phone-first shopping.

## What's weak

- **"SHOP NOW" CTA is too generic**: I'm looking at a Slip-ins hero — why doesn't the button say "Shop Slip-ins"? A one-word tweak would pull me closer.
- **No free shipping callout**: That's one of my top motivators and it's nowhere above the fold. Even a "$X ships free" line in the promo banner would help.
- **Woman in the photo doesn't feel like me**: She's gorgeous and by a pool, which is nice, but she looks like she's on vacation with no kids in sight. A quick "school pickup to yoga" lifestyle vibe would hit harder for where I am in life.
- **No trust signals above the fold**: No "free returns," no review counts, no "easy exchanges" — nothing that would reassure me before I tap through to spend money.

## Recommendations

- **Rename the hero CTA**: Change "SHOP NOW" to "Shop Hands Free Slip-ins" — it directly connects the product promise to the action and removes ambiguity.
- **Add free shipping threshold to the promo banner**: Slide "Free shipping on $X+" right next to the BOGO callout in that top blue strip. For me, that alone could tip the decision.
- **Swap or augment the hero lifestyle shot**: Add a second image of a woman in a casual, relatable real-life moment — errands, school drop-off, a walk with a stroller — not just a pool scene. The Slip-ins story is a busy-life story; the imagery should say that.
- **Surface a "Free Returns" badge near the CTA button**: One small trust line under "SHOP NOW" reduces the hesitation I'd have before tapping through.
