# Homepage snapshot · skechers.com · 2026-05-07
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I genuinely stopped scrolling when I saw this — a mom kissing her daughter with both of them in cute pink sneakers, surrounded by flowers. That's literally my life. Skechers is clearly pushing a Mother's Day campaign right now, and they went all in on the emotional angle. It feels warm, relevant, and like they actually thought about who's opening this app.

## What's working

- **The hero image** — a mom and daughter in matching pink Skechers is aspirational and emotionally resonant for me. I felt seen immediately.
- **"The Mother's Day Shop" headline** — timely, warm, written in a soft script font that fits the moment. Not salesy, just inviting.
- **BOGO 50% OFF banner** — leading with the deal at the very top means I registered the discount before I even consciously read it.
- **25% OFF Apparel strip** — double-stacking offers makes the value feel substantial without feeling desperate.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA** — big, clear, takes up the full width. On mobile I can't miss it.

## What's weak

- **No free-shipping callout visible** — as someone who shops primarily for the free shipping perk, I immediately scan for that threshold. It's not here.
- **"SHOP NOW" is generic** — I don't know if I'm being taken to women's shoes, gifts, or the whole site. For a comfort-shoe focused mom, "Shop Women's Slip-ins" or "Shop Gifts for Her" would feel more targeted.
- **No trust signals** — no review counts, no "free returns," no fit/comfort badges. For footwear I'm buying without trying on, those matter.
- **No urgency cue for Mother's Day** — the campaign is time-sensitive by nature but there's no "ends May 11" or countdown. It feels soft.

## Recommendations

- Add a free-shipping threshold line in the top banner ("Free shipping on orders $50+") right next to the BOGO tag — that's the combo that makes me tap.
- Change the CTA copy from "SHOP NOW" to "Shop the Mother's Day Gift Guide" or "Shop Comfort Styles for Mom" — it earns the click better and sets expectation.
- Add a deadline to the hero — even a subtle "Offer ends May 11" in the floral area would create real urgency without breaking the warm aesthetic.
- Swap or supplement the generic "details" fine-print links with a one-line trust badge row ("Free returns · Size guide · Secure checkout") just below the CTA.
