# Homepage snapshot · skechers.com · 2026-06-03
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-03**
## Executive summary

- OK so I open Skechers and the first thing that jumps out is a huge glowing neon shoe that looks like it belongs in a Fortnite ad — definitely not the vibe I came for. That said, the BOGO 50% Off banner at the very top is hard to ignore, and I can see "Member Month" perks happening right now which actually makes me want to poke around a bit. It reads more like a performance-athletics brand in this moment than the practical, comfort-first brand I associate with Skechers.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF banner** — it's the very first thing I see, bold blue stripe, impossible to miss. As someone who is absolutely motivated by a deal, this makes me want to stay.
- **"Slip-ins" as the first nav item** — whoever ordered that nav is my hero. I came here for slip-ons and I can get there in one tap without any hunting.
- **Member Month 2X Points through 6/13** — the deadline makes this feel real, not evergreen fluff. The fact that it shows a Login link means I can actually act on it right now.
- **Clean mobile layout** — no overlapping text, no broken images, icons are tap-friendly, everything is legible on a small screen.

## What's weak

- **"AERO BURST" hero** — a neon-streaked performance running shoe is the opposite of what I'm here for. I want slip-ons I can throw on for school pickup, not something that looks like it runs the 400m.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA** — this tells me nothing about where I'm going. Tap it and I'm presumably on the Aero Burst page, not the slip-ons I actually want. The CTA should match the product it's attached to.
- **No free shipping callout** — I specifically came here motivated by free shipping offers. There's no threshold visible, no mention of it at all above the fold. That's a miss.
- **No reviews or social proof on the hero** — even a "4.7 stars, 12K reviews" badge under the product would give me confidence to tap through.
- **Lifestyle imagery is absent** — the legs on the running track are anonymous. I want to see someone who looks a little like me, not just a disembodied shoe floating over grass.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the Aero Burst hero for a Slip-ins or comfort lifestyle hero** at least on mobile — show a mom in a kitchen or at a playground in comfortable Skechers, not a track athlete. The performance segment is real but it's not the whole brand.
- **Make the SHOP NOW button category-specific** — "Shop Slip-ins" or "Explore Member Deals" would both convert better than the generic prompt, especially since the nav literally shows Slip-ins first.
- **Add a free-shipping threshold to the BOGO banner or just below it** — "BOGO 50% OFF + Free Shipping on orders $50+" would make that banner nearly perfect for deal-seekers like me.
- **Surface a star rating or quick review count** under the hero product — even a single line like "★★★★½ · 8,400 reviews" would add the trust signal this viewport currently lacks.
