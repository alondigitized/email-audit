# Homepage snapshot · skechers.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Skechers is clearly leaning hard into Memorial Day weekend right now — the Red, White & Blue Collection is front and center, and the patriotic stars-and-wood-plank backdrop is very much giving "backyard cookout energy." As a brand they come across as accessible and family-friendly, which honestly fits my life pretty well. The BOGO deal at the top immediately caught my eye, which, yes, is exactly how you get me to keep scrolling.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF banner** — It's blue, bold, and the first thing I see. As someone who is absolutely motivated by a deal, this is a strong hook. BOGO especially lands because I can buy a pair for me and one for the kids.
- **"Slip-ins" as the first nav tab** — I didn't have to hunt. My category is right there, first tap, no digging through a mega-menu.
- **Members-only reward with a deadline** ("Now until 5/25!") — The deadline makes it feel urgent without being obnoxious. The Login link is right there, which nudges me to sign in before I shop.
- **Seasonal hero** — The Red, White & Blue Collection feels very "right now." It's what I'd expect a brand to be pushing this week, which makes the page feel alive and not stale.
- **Woman model in the hero** — She's about my age, casual summer dress, relaxed vibe. That's genuinely relatable compared to a lot of athletic brands that plaster elite runners everywhere.

## What's weak

- **No shop button above the fold** — The hero is a big gorgeous patriotic spread but there is no tappable CTA button on it. I have to know to tap the category nav or the hero image itself. For a mobile user distracted by my toddler, I might just scroll past and forget to tap.
- **"The Red, White & Blue Collection" doesn't tell me what's in it** — Is it slip-ons? Sneakers? Sandals? I can't tell from this hero. For me, the collection name alone isn't enough motivation to click if I don't know it includes what I actually want.
- **No free-shipping info visible** — I am heavily motivated by free shipping offers and I can't see any shipping threshold above the fold. That's a missed nudge.
- **The man gets more real estate in the hero images** — As a woman shopping for herself and her family, I'd rather see the woman model featured more prominently, not split 50/50 with a men's lifestyle shot.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop Slip-ins" button overlay on the hero image** — Give me something to tap right on the hero. Even a small pill button in the lower third of the woman's image would convert browsers to clickers.
- **Swap to a woman-forward hero or a mom-and-kid lifestyle shot** — The 50/50 male/female split is fine but a shot of a woman in Slip-ins doing real-life stuff (running errands, at the park) would resonate more with who actually buys comfort footwear online.
- **Add free-shipping threshold to the top banner or directly below it** — A second line like "Free shipping on $50+" costs nothing and would keep people like me from bouncing to check shipping costs elsewhere.
- **Surface at least one product image in the hero** — Show me a specific shoe — ideally a Slip-in or a Sandal — with the star styling. Lifestyle is nice but I need to see the product to know I'm in the right place.
