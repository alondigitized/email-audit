# Homepage snapshot · skechers.com · 2026-09-30
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- This looks like a big, confident sporty brand. Right now it's pushing the neon green "Skechers Aero Series" running shoe with "Outrun the night." It's slick, but it's aimed at a night runner, not a mom running errands and school pickup. The member discount and the "Slip-ins" link in the nav are what made me feel like the site might still be for me.

## What's working

- **"Members Save 20% OFF" top strip.** It's the first thing I see, and it tells me right away there's a discount to be had.
- **Nav row (Slip-ins, Wide Fit, Arch Fit, Max Cushioning, Aero Burst).** I can get to slip-ins in one tap. The comfort terms are what I actually shop for.
- **Hero layout.** It's bold and easy to read, with one big button. On my phone I don't have to think about where to tap.
- **"Recently Viewed" tab.** It's handy for a repeat shopper like me who comes back to check a pair.
- **"25% off Apparel & Bags" banner.** I could use it for the kids' or my own gym stuff, and it comes with a "Shop" link.

## What's weak

- **Hero.** It's all night-running and neon carbon-plate shoes. Nothing says comfort, easy on/off, or all-day wear.
- **No people.** I don't see a single mom, or anyone else, so I can't picture myself in these shoes.
- **Discounts.** Every one is "applied at cart," so I can't see a real price or what I save until I'm deep in checkout.
- **Stacked offers.** Three promo messages compete at the top (Members 20%, 25% apparel, "Save up to 20% off, 3 available offers"). It's confusing and doesn't feel like one clear deal.
- **Product rail.** It's mostly empty white space, and the sticky offers bar sits on top of it. I can't see prices or star ratings.
- **Missing basics.** I see no free-shipping threshold, no returns info, and no reviews. Those matter a lot when I'm buying online in a hurry.

## Recommendations

- Swap or add a second hero for **Slip-ins**: a real mom-type person stepping into a pair with a bag in one hand and a kid nearby, with the button "Shop Slip-ins."
- Merge the three promos into one clear line, something like "Members: 20% off, applied at cart. Free shipping over $X." Show the member price on each product card.
- Fix the product rail so images and prices load right under the hero. Add star ratings and a "Slip-in" badge, and let the offers bar collapse so it doesn't cover products.
- Put a small trust strip under the nav: "Free returns · Free shipping over $X · 4.8★ from X reviews."
