## What's working

- **The mom-and-daughter hero**: I don't care that it's a marketing photo — seeing a woman my age in jeans and pink sneakers kissing her kid made me smile before I even read the copy. That's rare.
- **Dual-banner offer stack**: BOGO 50% at the top then 25% off apparel below it — I noticed both within two seconds. As someone who always checks for deals before I start browsing, this is exactly what I want to see immediately.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA placement**: Full-width, high-contrast black button right below the hero. No hunting around.
- **"Recently Viewed" tray peeking at the bottom**: Signals I can pick up where I left off, which matters for me since I often browse in bursts between kid stuff.

## What's weak

- **No navigation shortcuts above the fold**: The only way to get to Women's or Slip-ins is to tap the hamburger MENU. On mobile when I'm in the pickup line at school, I want a "Women's" chip right there — not a full menu open.
- **"SHOP NOW" is too vague**: It could mean shoes, apparel, or gifts. I'd feel more confident clicking "Shop Her Shoes" or "Shop Slip-ins for Mom."
- **Zero trust signals above fold**: No star ratings, no "free returns," no "free shipping on orders over $X" — nothing that makes me feel safe pulling out my card. This is the one thing that would actually slow me down.
- **Apparel sale feels slightly off-brand for me**: I'm here for shoes. The 25% off apparel banner is fine but it dilutes the footwear focus I came for.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Women's | Slip-ins | Sale" quick-link row** directly below the header so I can reach my category in one tap without opening the hamburger menu.
- **Swap "SHOP NOW" for "Shop Her Slip-ins"** or "Shop Mother's Day Shoes" — the specificity removes any hesitation about where the button leads.
- **Inject a free-shipping badge** (e.g., "Free shipping on $75+") somewhere in the hero area — even a small text line — because that's the one thing that tips me from browse to buy.
- **Pull one product card with a star rating** just below the hero: a single featured Slip-in with "4.8 stars · 12,000+ reviews" would give me the social proof I need to feel confident fast.
