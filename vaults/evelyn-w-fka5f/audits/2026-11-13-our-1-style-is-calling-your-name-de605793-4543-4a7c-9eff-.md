# Our #1 style is calling your name
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a Black Friday countdown send that leads with a strong offer. The dark navy banner reads "BUY ONE, GET ONE 50% OFF" with a "20% OFF A SINGLE PAIR" fallback, and it's the first thing the eye lands on. It's a clear anchor, similar to the BOGO 50% that headlined the 2026-05-08 "The Styles Taking Over Your Feed" send.
- The body is a different story. The subject promises "Our #1 style", but the email never says which style that is, what makes it #1, or what it costs. The Arcade appears across four stacked modules (hero, Slip-Ins, Arch Fit, "Find Your Favorite"), and each repeats the same message with a different button. The result is a long scroll of lifestyle photography with no price, no review count and no product name beyond "Arcade." The BOGO offer is also pushed up into the banner and never reinforced next to a product.
- Judgement: a competent, on-season promotional blast with a real offer. The subject-to-content link is weak and the body is repetitive. It's a solid 5 to 6, not a standout.

## What's working

- The BOGO 50% banner is high-contrast and sits at the very top, so the offer registers instantly.
- The hero ("Classic Look, Lasting Comfort") uses two models in a clean studio setting, and the "SHOP ARCADE" button is clearly visible.
- Each module has its own colour-matched button, which gives clear, scannable CTAs.
- The size-shortcut grids (women's and men's) are a useful friction reducer for shoppers who already know what they want.
- The "Black Friday Countdown" framing is seasonal and fits the date (11/12 to 11/17 validity).

## What's weak

- The subject promises "Our #1 style", but nothing in the body labels the Arcade as the bestseller. There are no "#1" badges, no ratings and no sales claim.
- There is no price, sale price or product-level savings anywhere. The only offer is the generic banner.
- Four modules push the same shoe family, which dilutes the message. Slip-Ins and Arch Fit are different features, and none of them gets a specific product or colourway link.
- The fine print under the footer is small and dense. The banner also says "See below for details", which pushes the terms to the bottom.
- The member perk ("Join Skechers Plus and earn 1000 Bonus Points") is a tiny grey strip that is easy to miss. The footer also stacks a lot of utility content (Klarna/Afterpay, app download, social) after the product modules.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pay off the subject. Add a "#1 Best Seller" badge and a star rating to the hero, then show the actual BOGO math (for example "2 pairs from $X") right next to the product.
- 2. Cut the four near-identical modules to two: one hero product with price, and one grid of 3 to 4 distinct styles.
- 3. Move the loyalty perk up into the banner or directly under it, with a clearer "Join Plus" button.
- 4. Link each image to a specific product page rather than a collection.
- 5. Add a real deadline to the banner ("Ends 11/17") instead of relying on fine print.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a standard SFMC-sent table layout with a working preheader and media queries. The QA data shows two `http://` images without alt text, several undocumented third-party beacons, and viewport and CSS settings that hurt accessibility. Header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be verified, and 42 tracked links were not probed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified click destinations:** the QA run skipped 42 links as tracking/click-redirect domains. No destination, status code, or redirect chain was validated, so broken or mismatched links can't be ruled out.
- **Insecure image sources:**
  - `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108862811&mi_ecmp=MKG_US_NA_U_CATEGORY_ENGAGEDL60D_EN_11132026`
  - `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`
  - Both are plain HTTP. They can be blocked or rewritten by clients, which risks mixed-content warnings and lost pixel fires. The `<link rel="icon">` also uses `http://image.emails.nl.skechers.com/...`.
- **Inconsistent image hosts:** the source uses `image.emails.skechers.com` and `image.emails.nl.skechers.com` (the favicon). Mixing the two subdomains adds a second host to monitor for TLS and DNS.
- **Stacked third-party beacons in the hidden `<div>`:** three `beacon.krxd.net` pixels (`1x1_usermatch.gif` ×2, `1x1_ad_impression.gif`) sit alongside the SFMC open pixel (`click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx?qs=…`).
  - They carry a SHA-256 hash of the recipient address (`partner_uid=f03aefa3…8628`) and a subscriber ID (`partner_uid=123064971`).
  - They also expose the segment name `MKG_US_NA_U_CATEGORY_ENGAGEDL60D_EN_11132026` to third parties.
  - They are written `width=0 height=0` with unquoted attributes. Some clients and privacy proxies drop zero-dimension images.
- **Multiple open-tracking pixels:** the SFMC pixel, the krxd beacons, and `ink1000.com/o.gif` all report opens. Open counts will be unreliable and may be inflated.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):** `o.gif` (ink1000) and `dde00662-…png`.
- **Missing alt text (source-confirmed):** the three `krxd.net` beacons have no `alt` attribute. They are hidden, so the impact is low, but they are invalid markup.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resizing disabled:** `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }` blocks text scaling on iOS.
- **Link affordance and focus removed:** `a { color: inherit; outline: none; text-decoration: none; }` removes the focus indicator and underline. Links rely on surrounding styling to be identifiable.
- **Desktop content hidden by default:** `.desktop-content { display: none; }` is set outside any media query and only shown at `min-width: 768px`. Clients that strip `<style>` blocks or media queries will show only `.mobile-content`. Whether the content is duplicated or unique is not visible in the truncated source.
- **Layout tables:** the visible tracking-pixel table has `role="presentation"`. I can't confirm it for the rest of the tables because the source is truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The tail of the HTML is truncated, so this is unconfirmed for the full body.
- Subscriber-level identifiers (`mi_u=8108862811`, `partner_uid=123064971`) are resolved correctly and sent to third-party domains. Hashing the email address is a mitigation, but the ID is not hashed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` missing (QA WARN):** the header was not found.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` missing (QA WARN):** this means no RFC 8058 one-click support. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unverified, not confirmed**. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` missing (QA WARN):** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `emails.skechers.com`, which is not checked here.
- **Footer not visible:** the unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated source, so they can't be verified.
- **Third-party identifier sharing:** the `krxd.net` user-match pixels pass a hashed email to a data partner. This is a privacy-disclosure item worth confirming against the stated policy. I can't determine from the HTML whether it is disclosed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not assessable:** all 42 tracked links were skipped, and SFMC click-wrapping hides the real landing URLs.
- **No UTM evidence:** no UTM parameters are visible in the provided source. Attribution likely relies on SFMC redirect parameters, and the destination can't be confirmed.
- **Campaign ID:** `MKG_US_NA_U_CATEGORY_ENGAGEDL60D_EN_11132026` appears only in third-party pixel URLs, not on any visible landing link.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve both `http://` images over HTTPS: the `ink1000.com` `o.gif` (or drop it as redundant) and `image.emails.skechers.com/.../dde00662-…png`. Also fix the `http://` favicon link.
2. Add meaningful `alt` text to `dde00662-…png` (`alt=""` if decorative) and add `alt=""` to `o.gif` and the krxd beacons.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1`, and drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` and `a { outline: none }`. Restore a visible focus style.
4. Consolidate open-tracking pixels and audit the `krxd.net` beacons. Quote the `width`/`height` attributes and confirm the hashed-email sharing is disclosed.
5. Re-run the QA against raw `.eml` headers (or direct from SFMC) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. Don't treat the missing headers as defects until that's done.
6. Resolve the SFMC click URLs (follow redirects with a header-aware probe) to check the 42 skipped links for status codes, UTM presence, and landing-page alignment.
7. Re-run with the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer (unsubscribe link, postal address), merge tokens, and `role="presentation"` coverage.
