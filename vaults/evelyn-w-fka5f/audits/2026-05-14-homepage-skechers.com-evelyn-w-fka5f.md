# Homepage snapshot · skechers.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Skechers definitely knows what they're doing right now — the Slip-ins hero is front and center the moment the page loads, and that's basically exactly what I came here for. The BOGO deal is impossible to miss, which as a busy mom on a budget is the first thing I look for. It reads like a brand that's fully leaning into the comfortable, effortless lifestyle crowd — my crowd.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF sticky banner** — it's the first thing I see and it's in high-contrast blue. As someone who shops with discounts in mind, this immediately validates me coming here.
- **Slip-ins hero with female model** — she's wearing a floral dress, looks my age, standing by a pool. This is summer-mom energy and I relate to it immediately.
- **"HANDS FREE" product label** — that copy speaks directly to the slip-on convenience I care about. As a mom grabbing shoes on the go, "hands free" is a real selling point, not just a style claim.
- **Women tab already active** — the bottom navigation defaults to Women with it underlined, so I don't have to hunt for my section.
- **"Recently Viewed" tab** — small thing, but it signals the site remembers me and won't make me start from scratch.

## What's weak

- **"SHOP NOW" is too vague** — this button could take me anywhere. After a hero specifically about Slip-ins, the CTA should say "Shop Slip-ins" or "Shop Women's Slip-ins." I don't want to land on a generic category page and have to filter down.
- **No free shipping callout** — the BOGO is great but I always check whether shipping is free before I commit. That threshold is nowhere visible here.
- **The male model is distracting** — about half the hero real estate goes to a guy sitting on stairs. The woman on the right is who I identify with, but she's split across two panels. The layout dilutes her impact.
- **No social proof above the fold** — no star ratings, no "4.5M+ reviews," no trust badge. If I'm new to Skechers or haven't bought in a while, nothing here reassures me beyond the pretty photo.

## Recommendations

- **Replace "SHOP NOW" with "Shop Women's Slip-ins"** — one word change, massive conversion lift for anyone who arrived because of that hero.
- **Add a free-shipping threshold to the BOGO banner or the secondary strip** — even something like "BOGO 50% OFF + Free Shipping over $50" on one line keeps both hooks visible without adding a new element.
- **Give the woman in the hero 80% of the frame** — she's the demographic doing the buying here; the male model could slide to a separate Men's module below.
- **Drop a trust strip just above the category tabs** — a single row with "Free Returns · 4.5M Reviews · Secure Checkout" takes almost no vertical space and closes the credibility gap before I scroll.
