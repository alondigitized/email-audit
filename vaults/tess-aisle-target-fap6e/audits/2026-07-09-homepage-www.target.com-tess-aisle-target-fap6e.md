# Homepage snapshot · www.target.com · 2026-07-09
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-09**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Target is giving me summer-girl energy right off the bat — the sage green, the crochet dress, the "effortless trend-forward" language. This feels like a brand that knows exactly who I am. The upper fold is clean and aesthetically on-point, but it's doing more mood-boarding than selling — there's no deal, no button, no "your Circle savings are waiting" nudge, which is a miss for someone like me who came primed to spend.

## What's working

- **"Endless summer style" hero** — The headline is clean and the model is casting gold. She looks like a person I'd follow on Instagram, wearing something I'd buy. Sage green background feels current and Pinterest-worthy.
- **Subtext copy register** — "Find effortless, trend-forward outfits." is millennial-fluent without being try-hard. Target knows not to say "slay" to me.
- **Clean mobile layout** — No visual clutter, no auto-playing video noise, no pop-up modal blocking my view the second I land. That counts for a lot.
- **Second module teaser** — "Refresh your summer beauty" starting to peek below the fold with product shots gives me a reason to scroll — beauty is absolutely in my basket.

## What's weak

- **No CTA button** — I see soft descriptive copy but no red button saying "Shop Now" or "Shop Summer Looks." That's a real miss on a commerce homepage.
- **Zero Circle / rewards presence** — I'm a Circle loyalist. My order history is basically a Target autobiography. There's no rewards balance, no "your 5% is active," nothing that acknowledges I exist as a repeat customer.
- **No deal above the fold** — Not a percent off, not a free-shipping callout, not even a "prices as low as $X." I came primed to spend and there's no hook to trigger urgency.
- **"Commerce" / "Store Mode" labels in the top bar** — This looks like an internal mode banner leaking into the consumer view. A civilian shouldn't see that.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Circle acknowledgment to the hero** — A small "5% off with Circle. Active." badge under the headline is all it takes. I want to feel seen as a loyalist the moment I land.
- **Swap the soft subtext for a tappable button** — Make "Shop Summer Style →" a proper red button with contrast. The subtext line is nice but it reads editorial, not commercial.
- **Surface a concrete threshold offer** — "Free shipping on orders $35+" in a persistent strip below the nav turns browsing into converting. It's already true — just show it.
- **Move the "Refresh your summer beauty" module up** — Beauty + fashion in the same scroll is the Target trap at its best. Tighten the gap so both categories hit in the first scroll.
