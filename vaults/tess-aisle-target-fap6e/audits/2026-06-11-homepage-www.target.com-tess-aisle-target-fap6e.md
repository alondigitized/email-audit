# Homepage snapshot · www.target.com · 2026-06-11
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-11**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Target is doing Target things — the homepage is clean, red, and immediately screaming Father's Day at me. I respect the seasonal hustle, and honestly I'm already mentally adding a charcuterie board and a cologne set to a gift basket for my dad. That said, this is 100% a gifting push, not the "treat yourself to a new Stanley and three throw pillows you didn't need" energy that usually gets me.

## What's working

- **"Father's Day gifts he'll love / from $5"** — the price floor is smart. $5 anchors me low and makes the whole gifting task feel approachable. I'm already doing mental math on a gift bundle.
- **The product tile trio** (camp chair, mug/Keurig setup, tablet/planner) at the top actually has range — those three items tell me "we have something for every kind of dad," which I appreciate.
- **Page is genuinely clean**: no pop-up, no modal asking for my email before I even look around, no layout jank. As someone who checks Target on my phone weekly, I'm grateful.
- **Father's Day timing is on-point**: June 11, Father's Day is days away. This is exactly what I'd expect and want to see.

## What's weak

- **Zero Circle rewards presence**: I have Circle. I spend a lot at Target with Circle. The fact that there's no rewards hook, no member-exclusive teaser, no balance nudge above the fold is a miss. That's the thing that makes me feel like Target *knows me*.
- **No CTA button**: "Father's Day gifts he'll love" is a headline, not a tap target with clear button copy. I'm guessing it's clickable but I shouldn't have to guess.
- **Cologne and a baseball hat** are fine, but they're not speaking to me as the buyer. Show me the gifter — a woman putting together a basket, styling a gift — not just the product on a table.
- **No navigation shortcuts**: The hamburger menu is there but no quick-tap category rail (Home, Decor, Kitchen, Clothing). One of Target's best features is how many categories I browse — hiding them behind a hamburger is friction.
- **No Drive Up or free shipping callout**: Both are huge reasons I choose Target over Amazon. Neither is visible above the fold.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Circle rewards nudge** ("Your Circle savings: $X" or "Members get an extra 5% off") right under the search bar — it's the number-one reason loyal shoppers feel seen.
- **Swap the pure product-on-table tiles** for a lifestyle shot of someone (a woman, my age) wrapping or curating a Father's Day gift — make the gifter the hero, not just the gift.
- **Add a visible Drive Up / free shipping strip** — even a thin banner saying "Free shipping on $35+ or free Drive Up" would close a conversion loop that's currently invisible.
- **Give the hero a button**: change the Father's Day copy block so it has a tappable CTA like "Shop Father's Day" — make it undeniable.
