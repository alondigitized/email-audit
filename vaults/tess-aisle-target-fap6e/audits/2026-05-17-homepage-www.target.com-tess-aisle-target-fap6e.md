# Homepage snapshot · www.target.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Target is fully in summer mode and they're not being subtle — "Hello Summer Sale" with a giant patriotic popsicle pool float hits the right seasonal note for me. It reads cheerful, intentional, and very on-brand Target, but above the fold it's all vibes with almost no shopping infrastructure: no button, no category chips, no Circle check-in. I have to do the work to actually buy anything.

## What's working

- **"HELLO SUMMER SALE" headline** — bold, big, warm — tells me instantly there's a reason to be here
- **"20% off" badge** — concrete number, prominent placement, no ambiguity. I see it immediately and it registers as real money
- **Popsicle pool float illustration** — nostalgic, playful, unmistakably Target-fun. This is the exact energy I show up for
- **Red gingham scalloped banner** — that styling detail signals intentionality. Someone made a mood board. I respect it
- **Clean mobile nav** — bullseye centered, search bar full-width and prominent, no visual noise crowding the header

## What's weak

- **No CTA button** — the hero has real momentum and then just... stops. No "Shop the Sale," no "Shop Summer," nothing to tap
- **No Circle/Cartwheel hook** — they already know my zip code, I'm presumably logged in, and there's zero "your rewards" nudge. That's a missed personalization gift
- **No category entry points** — zero shortcuts to Home, Patio, Outdoor, Kitchen above the fold. I have to go hunting via hamburger nav
- **No free shipping threshold** — I know Target does free shipping at $35 but it's not here to motivate me
- **Category scope of the sale is totally ambiguous** — 20% off what? Pool toys? Everything? Home? Apparel? The imagery doesn't tell me

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop the Sale" CTA button** — even a small pill below the 20% badge. The hero needs a tap target or all this creative energy goes nowhere
- **Surface a Circle member teaser line** — one sentence: "Circle members, save an extra 5%" would make me feel seen and reward my loyalty
- **Drop 2-3 category chips below the fold break** — Home, Outdoor Living, Kitchen — quick-tap entries for people like me who know exactly what we came for
- **Make the 20% off scope explicit** — "20% off outdoor & patio" or "20% off home" turns a vague promo into a specific reason to tap
