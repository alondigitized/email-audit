# Homepage snapshot · www.target.com · 2026-06-04
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-04**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I love Target, I live at Target — but opening this on my phone and immediately hitting a full-screen sign-in wall before I can even see what's on sale? Not the warm Cartwheel welcome I was hoping for. The branding is instantly recognizable and the glimpse of that summery patriotic family scene below the modal tells me there's a Fourth of July campaign hiding under there, but right now the homepage is doing the most for account creation and the least for actually getting me to throw pillows.

## What's working

- **Family lifestyle hero**: even at half-crop, the warm sunny family scene with a kid in a USA top lands immediately — this is Target's patriotic summer campaign and it feels on-brand and timely
- **Brand recognition**: the bullseye logo, the red header, the red CTA button — I know exactly where I am in 0.2 seconds, no ambiguity
- **"See past orders and manage returns"**: this hooks me as a returning shopper. I have Circle rewards and a purchase history — the modal is at least relevant to me even if it's interruptive
- **Zip code personalization**: "Ship to 91754" at the very top tells me Drive Up and delivery are already localized, which I appreciate as a weekly Target-runner

## What's weak

- **The forced sign-in modal is the entire above-fold experience**: I can't see the hero copy, the banner deals, the navigation categories — the modal ate the whole page. This is friction at exactly the wrong moment
- **No deal is visible**: I came for the Thursday weekly ad energy, but there's no "30% off," no "free shipping on $35+," no Circle price callout anywhere in sight — zero purchase motivation above the fold
- **No Circle rewards hook**: the modal mentions "check out faster" but doesn't tell me my rewards balance or offer a Circle-member-only deal. That's a missed loyalty play for someone like me who is already a member
- **Hero copy completely hidden**: I literally cannot read the campaign headline — the modal blocks it. If Target is running a Fourth of July sale, I have no idea what the offer is

## Recommendations

- **Swap the forced modal for an inline sign-in chip or banner**: show a subtle "Hi, sign in for your Circle deals" strip at the top instead of a blocking overlay — let me see the campaign first, then nudge me to log in
- **Surface a concrete Circle deal on the modal itself**: change "Check out faster" to "Sign in to unlock 20% off home decor for Circle members" — now the modal is a value prop, not a toll booth
- **Make the free-shipping threshold the first thing I see**: "Free shipping on orders $35+" pinned to the header bar would immediately reduce my hesitation to browse
- **Put the seasonal campaign headline above the fold**: if it's Fourth of July, say so loudly — "Shop the Red, White & Blue Sale" with a deadline converts browsers like me into shoppers
