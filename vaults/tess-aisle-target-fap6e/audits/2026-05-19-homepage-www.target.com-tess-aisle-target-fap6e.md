# Homepage snapshot · www.target.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay, the bullseye never misses me and this is not the exception — I opened the app and instantly felt that classic Target dopamine hit. They're deep in Hello Summer Sale energy with a bomb-pop popsicle float and a red-white-plaid picnic vibe, and for a millennial who will absolutely walk past the patio section on the way to checkout, this is a whole mood. The 20% off above the fold is the hook; I just wish there were a button to actually *tap into* the sale.

## What's working

- **"HELLO SUMMER SALE" headline** — punchy, fun, seasonally exact. May 19 + summer sale = yes, send it.
- **20% off badge** — left-anchored, immediately legible. I don't have to squint or scroll to find the deal.
- **Bomb-pop float imagery** — charming and nostalgic. It's distinctly Target-coded summer fun, not generic stock art.
- **Red plaid / picnic top trim** — the tablecloth motif ties the whole hero together into a real art direction moment, not just a product dump.
- **Clean mobile render** — search bar accessible, nav icons crisp, no layout jank. Drive-Up queen can open this in the parking lot with zero friction.

## What's weak

- **No visible CTA button** — "20% off" is great but where do I tap? The whole hero is presumably tappable but there's no button label telling me "Shop the Sale" or "See Deals." That's a miss.
- **No Circle / Cartwheel hook above fold** — I'm a loyalty regular and there's zero rewards balance, Cartwheel coupons, or "you're X points away" nudge visible. That's a huge retention lever left idle.
- **No category tiles or product rails above fold** — I can see the hero and nothing else. One scroll and I'm gone if I don't find home/decor/Stanley content fast.
- **"Commerce" label in the top bar** — unclear what this means in context (store mode? location?). Slightly jarring on first glance.
- **No urgency cue** — "20% off" with no "ends Sunday" or countdown leaves money on the table.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop the Sale" button** inside the hero — any color-popped pill button with clear copy would instantly lift tap-through; the current hero relies on the user already knowing the whole image is tappable.
- **Surface Circle Rewards balance at the top** — a simple "Hi Tess, 🎯 342 pts" chip in the header or a one-line Cartwheel banner below the hero would keep me engaged and loyal instead of just transactional.
- **Add a "Sale ends Sunday" or countdown** — even a subtle "this week only" line turns a passive offer into an urgency moment; costs nothing to ship.
- **Add a product category strip below the hero** — Home, Patio, Kitchen, New Arrivals — one horizontal scroll rail would capture me in the aisle I actually overspend in.
