# Homepage snapshot · www.target.com · 2026-08-06
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- This reads like Target leaning hard into back-to-school/dorm season with a "College Haul" push — cute banner styling, a stack of coordinated bedding, and pastel headphones. It's very on-brand aesthetically (that mix of preppy collegiate colors and cozy textiles is very Target), but as someone who's well past dorm shopping, it's not really talking to me. Feels like I opened the app during someone else's shopping moment.

## What's working

- The "College Haul" banner trio (Bullseye University, '62 megaphone) is genuinely cute styling — that's the kind of playful graphic design that makes me stop scrolling even if the topic isn't mine.
- The bedding/pillow/blanket stack is a nice soft, coordinated flatlay — pastel headphones as the styling accent is a smart touch that shows off more than one department at once.
- "Shop college picks" is a dead-simple, unambiguous button — I know exactly what tapping it gets me.

## What's weak

- Zero Target Circle presence above the fold — no rewards balance, no member-pricing badge, nothing that says "hey, we know you're a regular." That's a missed layup for a loyalty-heavy shopper like me.
- No actual offer anywhere — no % off, no "% off with Circle," nothing. Just a themed collection with no price hook.
- The whole hero is dorm/college-specific, which isn't my life stage — I'd love this energy if it were "Fall Refresh" or "New Room, New You" pointed at general home decor instead of specifically college kids.

## Recommendations

- Swap (or A/B test) the college-specific hero for shoppers outside that segment — a "Fall Home Refresh" version of this same styled-flatlay concept would pull me in immediately.
- Add a Circle Rewards strip right under the search bar — even just "You have $X in Target Circle Bonus" would make me feel recognized on open.
- Put a real number on the CTA — "Shop college picks — up to 20% off with Circle" converts way better than a bare "Shop college picks."
- Surface a home/decor category tile (throw pillows, Studio McGee, Hearth & Hand) right below the hero so non-college shoppers have a fast lane to their thing.
