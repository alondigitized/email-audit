# Homepage snapshot · www.target.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- Okay, it's Mother's Day and Target showed up wearing pink — I respect it. The homepage is doing exactly one thing: funneling me toward Mother's Day gifting with a warm, giftable flat-lay and zero ambiguity about the occasion. It feels like the brand knows I'm already here weekly, so it's skipping the "why Target" pitch entirely and going straight for the cart.

## What's working

- **The Mother's Day hero headline** is massive, legible, and emotionally on-key — "HAPPY MOTHER'S DAY" in that chunky white type on pink is basically a gift tag
- **"Fast & ready today"** is the line that gets me every time — it quietly says Drive Up without saying Drive Up, and that's exactly why I'm not at the mall right now
- **The gift flat-lay imagery** (heart clutch, chocolate box, plaid wrapped gift, lotion, MOM mug) shows me the actual product range in one glance — I can immediately picture my cart
- **Saved zip code in the utility bar** confirms the app/site knows me — no friction to get to fulfillment mode
- **"Trending picks for…"** teased at the bottom creates just enough scroll pull without feeling desperate

## What's weak

- **No Circle rewards hook above the fold** — I have 3,000+ Circle points right now and I'd love to see my balance before I start adding chocolates to my cart; the prompt would convert me faster
- **No actual button CTA** — "Find thoughtful gifts she'll love, fast & ready today." is underlined text doing the job of a button; a proper Shop Now or Shop Mother's Day button would be cleaner and more tappable on mobile
- **No urgency copy** — this is the actual day before (or the day of) Mother's Day and there's no "Order by [time] for same-day pickup" countdown; that's a huge missed conversion moment
- **No visible deal or price hook** — even a "Circle members save extra today" badge would give me a reason to move from browsing to buying

## Recommendations

- **Add a Drive Up cutoff timer** — "Order by 5 PM for Drive Up pickup today" would drive immediate action on Mother's Day itself; this is the single highest-leverage add right now
- **Surface Circle rewards balance** in the hero band — even "You have $12 in rewards — use them today" as a persistent banner would pull Cartwheel loyalists like me straight to checkout
- **Make the CTA a tappable button**, not underlined text — "Shop Mother's Day Gifts" in a white pill button over the pink hero would be more scannable on mobile and add clarity to the visual hierarchy
- **Add a second editorial module above "Trending picks"** that segments by gift recipient (Gifts for Mom, Gifts for Grandma, Gifts for Her) — the flat-lay is pretty but doesn't tell me where to start if I'm buying for three people
