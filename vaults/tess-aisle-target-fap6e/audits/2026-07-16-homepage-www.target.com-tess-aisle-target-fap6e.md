# Homepage snapshot · www.target.com · 2026-07-16
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-16**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I open Target dot com and the first thing that greets me is a sign-in modal sitting directly on top of whatever deal they're running — I can barely make out "on kids' back-to-school clothing\*" peeking out behind it like a shy kid on the first day of school. To their credit, it's Target, so the bones are good and the aesthetic is clean, but this homepage is running a back-to-school kids campaign right now, which is decidedly not where my Thursday ad-scan brain is at (I'm thinking throw pillows and maybe a new Stanley colorway, not elementary school fits).

## What's working

- **The sign-in modal's benefit copy** ("See past orders and manage returns," "Check out faster") speaks directly to a returning Circle member — this is genuinely useful framing, not just a gate
- **"Sign in or create account" CTA** is full-width, red, unmissable — the action is crystal clear
- **Back-to-school timing** is spot-on for mid-July; the kid in the light-blue hero looks genuinely cute and the campaign feels seasonally current
- **Top utility bar** (Ship to 91801 / Alhambra / Store Mode) is exactly the kind of Drive Up–friendly detail I actually want on a mobile visit

## What's weak

- **The modal eating the hero** is the biggest UX offense on this page — I cannot see the actual offer amount, the headline, or any Shop Now CTA for the campaign itself
- **No home/lifestyle category shortcut** above the fold — if I'm here for throw pillows or Magnolia decor, there is no visual signal that this place has me covered
- **The asterisk on "clothing\*"** is already planting doubt before I've read a single selling word
- **Zero Circle rewards context** — no "You have X points" or "Members save extra 5%" — a loyal Circle member deserves to see her balance, not just a generic sign-in prompt
- **Hero imagery skews young child**, which doesn't match my cohort at all; I don't see myself anywhere on this screen

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the modal on scroll or after 3 seconds** — or at minimum, shrink it to a sticky banner so the back-to-school hero and offer copy are readable immediately
- **Surface a Circle balance or member savings badge** in the utility bar for signed-in users (e.g., "Hi Tess — 1,240 points") to make loyal shoppers feel seen before the campaign even starts
- **Add a secondary category strip below the hero** with icons for Home, Kitchen, Beauty, and Seasonal so shoppers who aren't here for back-to-school can route themselves in one tap
- **Show the full discount claim above the fold** — whatever "\_\_\_ on kids' back-to-school clothing\*" completes to, that number needs to be visible; a mystery offer is not an offer
