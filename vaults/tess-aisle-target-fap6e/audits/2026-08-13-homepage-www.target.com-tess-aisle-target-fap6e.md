# Homepage snapshot · www.target.com · 2026-08-13
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay so I open the app expecting my usual Thursday weekly-ad scroll and instead I land smack in the middle of "Dorm Deals Are Here" with a pennant flag and a guy in a checkered cardigan. This reads like Target's talking to my little cousin heading off to freshman year, not to me and my cart full of throw pillows. It's a clean, well-branded page — just not built for a 33-year-old who's already furnished her apartment twice over with Target home decor.

## What's working

- **Target Circle nav row** — Target Circle™, Target Circle Card, and Target Circle 360™ all sitting right up top is a nice reminder my rewards are always one tap away, even if I didn't come here for that.
- **The storage & organization tile** — bins and a laundry bag, "up to 40% off." That's genuinely adjacent to my thing. I organize everything in cute Target bins, so this tile alone almost pulled me in.
- **Clean layout, no clutter** — no popups, no janky overlapping text, easy scroll rhythm from hero to offer tiles.

## What's weak

- **"Dorm Deals Are Here" hero** — I am not moving into a dorm. This whole banner, pennant flag and all, just isn't speaking to me.
- **Models skew college-age** — the girl with the flowing hair and the guy in the cardigan read early-20s, not "mom who just redid her living room with Magnolia vases."
- **No home/decor rail above the fold** — for a page I open hoping to find throw pillows or seasonal decor, there's zero decor merchandising visible without scrolling further.
- **Vague asterisks** — "women's & men's tees, tanks & bottoms*" and "select storage & organization*" leave me wondering what's actually excluded before I even tap in.

## Recommendations

- Swap the dorm hero for a home-refresh or "new season, new decor" lifestyle shot — same layout, different life stage.
- Add a "Home & Decor" or "New This Week" tile in that first row of offer cards so people like me have an immediate landing spot.
- Surface my Target Circle rewards balance or personalized offers right in the hero strip instead of just nav links — I want to see what's mine, not just that the program exists.
- Spell out what the asterisk excludes right on the tile (even a tiny "some exclusions apply" link) so the 40% off doesn't feel like a maybe.
