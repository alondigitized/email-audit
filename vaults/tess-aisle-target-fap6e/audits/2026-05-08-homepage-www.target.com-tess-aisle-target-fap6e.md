# Homepage snapshot · www.target.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I genuinely love Target and this homepage knows its moment — Mother's Day in two days is front and center, and the pink-and-chocolates flatlay is giving exactly the kind of "thoughtful but I grabbed it at Target" energy I live for. But as someone who's been scanning the weekly ad every Thursday since college, I'm a little annoyed there's no Circle deal above the fold, no percent-off banner, nothing to make me feel like I'm getting the insider scoop my membership supposedly earns me.

## What's working

- **The headline timing:** "MOTHER'S DAY IS MAY 10" with two days to go creates urgency without being pushy — I feel the pull to buy *now*
- **The flatlay styling:** the heart-shaped pink bag, chocolates, fragrance, "BEST MOM EVER" mug — this is curated exactly like a Target end-cap I'd stop and photograph for my stories
- **"Fast & ready today":** Drive Up is literally how I live my life; this speaks directly to my "order from the parking lot" behavior without me even having to read between the lines
- **Clean render:** not a single broken image or layout glitch — the page loads like a page should
- **The "Trending picks for..." peek at the bottom:** teases more content and makes me want to scroll

## What's weak

- **No Circle rewards hook:** I have a Target Circle card and I want to see my savings before I buy anything — the complete absence of any loyalty signal above the fold feels like a missed layup
- **Zero price anchoring:** not one "%off" or "$X off $X" anywhere — for a store I associate with affordable design, this homepage is oddly price-agnostic
- **No CTA button:** the underlined link text "Find thoughtful gifts she'll love, fast & ready today" is doing button-work without button-confidence; a big red "Shop Mother's Day Gifts" button would be cleaner
- **No people in the hero:** the flatlay is pretty, but showing a mom actually receiving this stuff would hit harder emotionally — I want to see myself in the moment, not just the products

## Recommendations

- **Add a Circle member exclusive callout** directly in the hero — even "Circle members save 5% today" in a badge would make me feel rewarded for my loyalty before I even tap
- **Swap the underlined link for a high-contrast red button** with copy like "Shop Mother's Day Gifts" so the primary action is impossible to miss on mobile
- **Inject one deal into the hero subtext** — "Same-day pickup + free shipping over $35" would give me the price hook that currently sends me to the weekly ad tab instead
- **Add a lifestyle photo option** — a quick A/B with a mom-and-daughter moment alongside the flatlay would bring the emotional payoff that makes me convert instead of just browse
