# Homepage snapshot · www.target.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Full review
Okay, full-on Mother's Day mode the second I land. The "Made for Mom" hero is very pink, very purple, very *Target knows their moment* — and honestly I'm here for it. The "from $5" callout is doing its job because I immediately started doing the mental math on how many little gifts I could stack. The Lindt pralines box in that purple ribbon presentation looks giftable without trying too hard, and that flower vase situation is giving me Sunday centerpiece ideas for myself, not even for my mom.

What catches my eye second is how gift-friendly the price anchor feels. "From $5" on a Mother's Day page is very much the reason I tell myself I'm "just stopping in" and then end up with a full Circle rewards haul. Target knows exactly how to open the wallet gradually. No sticker shock, just a soft invitation.

What feels slightly off is that I'm not immediately seeing a path to the stuff I actually want — the home decor, the Stanley drops, the Threshold finds. It's all Mom gifts right now and I get it, it's May 2nd, but my inner Cartwheel-checker is already wondering where the "treat yourself while you're here" lane is. The Store Mode toggle at the top is interesting — I use Drive Up constantly so I'm curious if that's going to route me somewhere useful, but it feels a little tucked away.

The vibe is warm and approachable, the color is on-brand seasonal, and the merch shown (chocolates, candle, mug) hits the $15-and-under impulse buy sweet spot perfectly. I would not leave this page — I'd scroll.

**7/10**
