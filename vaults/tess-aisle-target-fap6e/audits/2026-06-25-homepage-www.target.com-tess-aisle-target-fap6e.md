# Homepage snapshot · www.target.com · 2026-06-25
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-25**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Target, I see you — very much in summer mode, very much pushing the daily deals energy. The bullseye is doing its thing with a cute kid-in-summer-clothes hero and a big, punchy 40% off splash, which reads like a Thursday ad drop come to life on my phone screen. It's a clean, familiar experience, but nothing here is making me stop mid-scroll and go "wait, I need to tap that RIGHT NOW" for the stuff I actually came to browse — home, decor, my beloved random-aisle finds.

## What's working

- **The 40% off daily deals callout** — that teal circle with the big red percentage is pure Target visual language, and I clocked it in under a second. Exactly how I want to feel when I open the app: there's a deal happening RIGHT NOW.
- **"2 days to save" urgency badge** — small red pill beneath the hero, subtle but effective. It creates a timer without feeling aggressive or manipulative.
- **Circle loyalty banner below the hero** — "Don't miss 40% off deals + more — join ⊙circle for free today. Ends Friday, 6/26." This is doing three things at once: reinforcing the deal, pitching the loyalty program, and giving me a deadline. Efficient use of screen real estate.
- **Seasonal coherence** — beach ball, pastels, summer shorts. It's cohesive and current. No stale winter content bleeding through.
- **Search bar is prominent** — that "What can we help you find?" bar is right under the logo. For a Drive Up girlie who already knows what she wants half the time, this is the fastest path to conversion.

## What's weak

- **Hero model is a child** — I don't hate it, but as a 33-year-old who came here to mentally redecorate her living room and maybe impulse-buy a new Stanley colorway, a grade-schooler in a tank top is not my entry point. It reads kids/back-to-school prep, not "affordable style for YOU."
- **"Learn more" is a nothing CTA** — that red pill button under the Circle callout says "Learn more." Where is the "Shop deals" or "See today's offers" button? I want to be sent somewhere, not learn more about learning more.
- **Zero home/decor signal above the fold** — not a single throw pillow, not a candle, not a Magnolia piece. My category is invisible here. Target sells SO much beautiful home stuff and this screen is entirely kidswear + beach gear.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — I need to know if my $48 cart qualifies. That's usually a banner at the top and it's not here, which is a mild friction point.
- **No product-level specifics** — "daily deals" is vague. I want to see what's actually 40% off — is it baby gear? housewares? fashion? Give me a teaser category so I know it's worth tapping.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero creative for a lifestyle scene that spans categories** — a bright, styled kitchen or living room vignette with a summer entertaining angle would speak to me and still feel seasonal. Keep the kid imagery for a secondary tile below the fold.
- **Change "Learn more" to "Shop 40% off deals"** — direct action copy, takes me to the deals page immediately. One word swap, measurable conversion lift.
- **Add a horizontal category shortcut rail directly under the search bar** — Home, Clothing, Beauty, Kitchen — one tap to wherever. I shouldn't have to open the hamburger menu to route myself.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold** — even a small strip "Free shipping on orders $35+" above the hero would remove a micro-friction point that pops into my head every single time I'm filling a cart.
