# Homepage snapshot · www.target.com · 2026-07-23
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay so Target is going full back-to-school mode right now — "Fresh year starts here" with the cutest little girl by some purple lockers. As a Cartwheel loyalist who knows this store like the back of her hand, I recognize the campaign energy immediately, but honestly? This above-the-fold isn't doing much for me personally since I'm not shopping for a second-grader's folder set. No Circle rewards badge, no price callout, no CTA button — just vibes and a very photogenic kid.

## What's working

- **"Fresh year starts here" headline** — it's big, bold, confident. The typography is clean and very Target. Works as brand moment even if it doesn't hit my bucket.
- **Clean layout** — no clutter, no banner stack, no pop-up modal begging me for my email on arrival. That's a dark-pattern win I deeply appreciate.
- **Location context at the top** — "Ship to 91801 / Alhambra / Store Mode" is genuinely useful. Drive Up lives or dies by knowing my store is right there.
- **Back-to-school timing is on point** — late July is exactly when this campaign should be running. They're not asleep.

## What's weak

- **No Circle rewards hook** — I have 3,200 points sitting there and Target didn't say a word about it. That's free engagement they left on the table.
- **No CTA button visible** — the hero is all mood, no action. I don't know where to tap. "Fresh year starts here" → …and then what?
- **Zero offer copy** — not even "Free shipping on $35+" which is the most basic trust/conversion signal Target could surface. Nothing.
- **Child imagery doesn't speak to me** — if I'm in my home-decor era, this is actively the wrong image. It's a great photo but it's not my photo.
- **Search bar is the de facto CTA** — which means Target is offloading navigation work onto me. Not great when I could get distracted or bounce.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Circle rewards balance chip under the nav** — even "You have $X in rewards — use it now" would stop me in my tracks and make this feel personal. That's one line of personalization.
- **Swap the hero to a lifestyle split** — run back-to-school on the left tile, home refresh / "New at Target" on the right tile so they're catching parents AND the home-obsessed crowd simultaneously. One hero image is leaving money on the floor.
- **Put a tappable CTA button on the hero** — "Shop the List" or "Shop Back to School" with a red pill button. Right now there's no invitation to go anywhere.
- **Surface a concrete offer in the sub-hero band** — "Free shipping on orders $35+" or "Earn 1% back with Circle" takes up one line and converts browsers into tapper-inners.
