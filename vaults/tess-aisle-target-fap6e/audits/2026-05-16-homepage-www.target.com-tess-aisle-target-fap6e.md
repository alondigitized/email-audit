# Homepage snapshot · www.target.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Target is fully in vacation mode and I am *here* for it. The homepage is pushing a travel-ready fashion edit with that effortlessly aspirational aesthetic they nail every summer — think resort dressing, tropical greenery, and a woman who looks like she has her life together. It's editorial, it's pretty, and it's 100% speaking to me — but it's also holding back the practical stuff (deals, Circle savings, free shipping) that would actually make me tap through and buy something instead of just sighing appreciatively.

## What's working

- **"The Vacation Edit is here" headline** — three seconds on this page and I already know the story. Bold, legible white text over tropical greenery reads instantly on mobile.
- **Lifestyle photo of the woman in the knit top** — she looks like someone I'd see at a beach town restaurant. Gold jewelry, natural hair, relaxed knit — this is aspirational without being alienating or overly editorial.
- **Clean mobile layout** — the bullseye logo, hamburger nav, search bar, and hero all stack in a way that doesn't feel cramped. No weird overflow, no junky ad strip at the top (just the useful "Ship to 91754" localization bar).
- **Tropical aesthetic is cohesive** — the palm-leaf background behind the headline carries into the photo below it. It feels like a campaign, not a clearance bin.
- **"Store Mode" toggle visible** — as a Drive Up regular, seeing that top-bar utility function confirms Target knows how I actually shop.

## What's weak

- **Zero deals above the fold** — I'm a Circle member who specifically scans the Thursday ad. There's not a single dollar sign or percentage on this screen. That's a miss.
- **"Explore the collection" CTA is soft** — "Explore" is the retail equivalent of "discover." Give me "Shop the Vacation Edit" or even "Shop Resort Looks." The vague verb doesn't trigger my finger.
- **No Circle/loyalty hook** — RedCard savings, Circle week teaser, rewards balance — nothing. For someone like me who has the app and earns points religiously, this feels like Target forgot I exist.
- **Subtext is underlined like a hyperlink but doesn't feel clickable** — "Discover versatile, resort-ready staples." reads as a link style but it's just decorative italic-underline. Slightly confusing on mobile.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Circle loyalty strip directly under the search bar** — even a one-liner like "Circle members: extra 5% off this week's Vacation Edit" would light me up and make me feel seen before I even hit the hero.
- **Swap "Explore the collection" for "Shop Resort Styles"** — stronger verb, category-specific noun, same one button. Ships in an afternoon.
- **Surface a concrete price signal in the hero subtext** — something like "Styles from $15" or "Free shipping on orders $35+" tucked under the CTA costs nothing and adds real conversion gravity.
- **Tighten the subtext underline styling** — if "Discover versatile, resort-ready staples" is not a tappable link, remove the underline. If it is, make it feel like one. This is a five-minute CSS fix.
