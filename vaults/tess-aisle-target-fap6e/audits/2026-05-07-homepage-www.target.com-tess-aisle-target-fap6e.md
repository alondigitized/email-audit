# Homepage snapshot · www.target.com · 2026-05-07
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is immediately giving "I walked in for one thing and Target already knows me." The whole above-the-fold is a big pink Mother's Day push — totally seasonally correct since it's three days out — with a cute gift flatlay that has the exact vibe I'd actually send to my mom. It reads warm, it reads fast, and it reads like a brand that knows I'm shopping under a time crunch right now.

## What's working

- **"MOTHER'S DAY IS MAY 10" headline** — the date stamp does real urgency work without a countdown timer. Three days is short enough to make me feel it.
- **The gift flatlay** — heart-shaped pillow, chocolates, a "MOM" card, a pink spray, a plaid-wrapped box. This is a Target flatlay and it is *doing its job*. Everything there reads under $30 and giftable, which is exactly my mental model walking into this run.
- **"fast & ready today" subtext** — speaks directly to the panicking last-minute shopper in me, and "Drive Up" is always in the back of my head when I see that copy.
- **Clean pink/purple hero palette** — seasonal color is on point, not generic. This doesn't feel like a leftover template.
- **Search bar is prominent** — when I already know I want something specific, it's right there, not buried.

## What's weak

- **No Circle/rewards callout above the fold** — I am a Cartwheel-loyal shopper. Not seeing my rewards balance or a member benefit banner is a miss. That's the hook that keeps me on the app.
- **No concrete offer** — "BOGO 50% on gifts" or "Free shipping on orders $35+" would have me tapping immediately. Right now it's vibes, not deals.
- **CTA is a text link, not a button** — "Find thoughtful gifts she'll love, fast & ready today" is underlined but it doesn't visually scream "tap me." A red Target button would convert harder.
- **No lifestyle person in the hero** — the flatlay is cute, but no human. A Millennial woman handing her mom a Target gift bag would have landed closer to home.
- **"Trending picks for" rail is cut off** — I can see the beginning of it at the bottom of the fold, which means I have to scroll to see what category it's even trending *for*. Tiny thing, but it's dangling.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Circle rewards nudge in the hero or just below it** — even "Earn 5% back with Circle" in a small red badge would activate the loyalty habit. I check my Target Circle balance like a slot machine, and they're leaving that dopamine hit on the table.
- **Drop one concrete offer into the hero** — "Free shipping on all Mother's Day gifts" or a dollar-off threshold. The campaign is warm; a deal would close the gap between browsing and tapping.
- **Swap the text CTA for a red Target button** — something like `Shop Mother's Day Gifts →` in the standard red pill button. This page has a clear call to action conceptually, but it's not button-shaped.
- **Bring one lifestyle person into the hero** — a 30-something handing a wrapped box to a beaming 60-something mom. The flatlay is fine but the human moment converts better for an emotional holiday like this one.
