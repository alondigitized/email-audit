# Reserve the Jordan Retro 6 'Black & White'
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- This is Foot Locker sending the *same* Jordan Retro 6 'Black/White' reservation email two days in a row — 2026-08-04 was "Reserve the Jordan Retro 6 'Black/White'" (7.0/10), and today's send is "Reserve the Jordan Retro 6 'Black & White'," differing only by an ampersand instead of a slash. The Kids' Jordan Retro 3 'Chalk & Track Red' pairing also already appeared as one panel in the sprawling five-section 08-04 catalog send. So structurally this is a tighter, better-focused re-edit of yesterday's content — two launches instead of five — but it's still the same product being pitched on back-to-back days with no new information.
- On production quality alone, it's clean: bold "LAUNCH RESERVATION" black hero blocks, real countdown timers tied to a specific "8/7 at 4PM ET" deadline, unambiguous "RESERVE NOW" buttons, and an FLX Rewards free-shipping callout up top. No broken images, no overlapping text, no lorem ipsum.
- The real miss is the "Looks You'll Love" cross-sell grid at the bottom — it's 100% kids' toddler product (Boys Toddler, Girls Toddler labels throughout) directly under an adult Jordan Retro 6 launch, which reads like a templating mismatch rather than a deliberate merchandising choice. Combined with the near-duplicate subject from yesterday, this email is well-built but poorly differentiated — solid execution undercut by lazy cadence.

## What's working

- Two focused launches (adult Jordan Retro 6, kids' Jordan Retro 3) instead of the five-panel scroll from 08-04 — much easier to parse.
- Real, specific deadline ("Reservations close 8/7 at 4PM ET") with a live countdown timer under each product — credible urgency, not fake "ends today" language.
- CTA buttons ("RESERVE NOW") are large, high-contrast, and placed directly under each product with no ambiguity.
- FLX Rewards free shipping/returns and app-download reservation tip are surfaced without cluttering the hero.

## What's weak

- Subject line is a near carbon copy of yesterday's "Reserve the Jordan Retro 6 'Black/White'" — same product, same colorway, one punctuation mark different. A subscriber who opened yesterday gets nothing new today.
- "Looks You'll Love" grid is entirely kids' toddler apparel and shoes, mismatched against the adult Jordan Retro 6 hero — no adult sizes/styles cross-sold at all.
- No product detail (sizing, release price, "why reserve" context) shown for either hero sneaker — just name, colorway, and a countdown.
- No social proof (reviews, ratings) on either hero launch, even though ratings appear on some of the cross-sell tiles below.

## Recommendations

- 1. Kill the day-over-day duplicate: if the Retro 6 reservation window is still open, don't resend an near-identical email 24 hours later — consolidate into a single "reservation reminder" cadence (e.g., day 1 launch, day-of-deadline reminder) instead of two full sends.
- 2. Swap the "Looks You'll Love" grid to adult sneakers/apparel that complement the Jordan Retro 6, or split into two mini-grids (one adult, one kids) so it doesn't read as a templating error.
- 3. Add one line of hero copy with a "why reserve" hook — retail price, release date, or scarcity signal ("Limited pairs per store") to give the CTA more weight than a bare countdown.
- 4. Add star ratings/social proof under at least the primary hero product, matching what's already used lower in the email.
