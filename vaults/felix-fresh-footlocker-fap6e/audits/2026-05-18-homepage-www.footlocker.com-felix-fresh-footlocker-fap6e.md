# Homepage snapshot · www.footlocker.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- Foot Locker is coming through with the NB Grey Days 2026 launch banner up top and a Jordan section loading in below — those two things alone tell me the sneaker calendar is still alive here. But the main hero they're pushing right now is a **kids' sale**, and as a 23-year-old dude deep in the SNKRS app ecosystem, that hero is basically invisible to me. The brand looks solid, the bones are there, but the targeting on this visit is just off.

## What's working

- **New Balance Grey Days 2026 ticker** — That scrolling top banner with the sneaker emoji is exactly how I track drops. It tells me Foot Locker knows launch culture exists.
- **Jordan module loading below** — The Jumpman logo and "World's Best" Air Jordan 3 visual is genuinely eye-catching for someone like me who lives on the Jordan calendar.
- **Men's / New Arrivals category pills** — Having those one-tap shortcuts directly under the search bar means I can escape the kids sale and get to my category in a single tap.
- **Clean mobile layout** — No cumulative layout shift, no overlapping text, renders fast. The orange sale banner is bold and readable on a small screen.

## What's weak

- **Hero is a kids' sale** — "Save on top kids styles from New Balance, Nike, and more!" is the dominant message above the fold. As a 23-year-old raffle-entering sneakerhead, this is a complete miss. The hero is doing zero work for me.
- **No FLX rewards hook** — I specifically wait for FLX double-up weekends. There's no mention of my points balance, no "member exclusive" badge, nothing. That's a retention miss.
- **No urgency on the NB Grey Days drop** — The top banner teases the launch but gives me zero scarcity signal. No countdown, no "available now," no raffle CTA. Just "Shop New Balance" — that's weak for a launch event.
- **Sale CTA goes to kids section** — "Shop the Sale" presumably dumps me into kids' shoes. If I tap that by accident, I'm bouncing immediately.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero to the NB Grey Days launch** — Pull that ticker content into a full hero with a launch countdown, a direct men's colorway image, and a raffle or early-access CTA. That's the story I came to see.
- **Add a FLX rewards strip above the fold** — "You have X points — FLX double-up weekend starts Friday" would hook me instantly. Right now the loyalty program is completely invisible.
- **Segment the sale banner by gender** — Instead of "kids styles," show "Men's Nike + Jordan up to 30% off" for me. The personalization data is there; use it.
- **Put a product-level CTA on the Jordan module** — "Shop Air Jordan 3" with the Jumpman image right there is one tap away from a conversion. Don't make me dig through Men's → Jordan → Air Jordan 3.
