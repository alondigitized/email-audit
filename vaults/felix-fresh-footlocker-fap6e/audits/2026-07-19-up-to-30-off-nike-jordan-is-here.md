# Up to 30% off Nike & Jordan is here!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-19**
## Executive summary

- Foot Locker is back on a straight-discount playbook after the adidas x Bad Bunny collab sends on July 14–15. The offer is a clear step up from the 20% off Nike & Jordan promo that ran July 2–5 — subscribers who remember that run will register 30% as a meaningful escalation, and the hero communicates it in about one second. The creative is clean, the brand rail works, and the FLX free-shipping callout is a genuine member perk well-surfaced.
- The single biggest problem is the "BASED ON YOUR STYLE" personalization block: every product tile in that 6-item grid carries a **Boys' Preschool** label. For an adult subscriber, this is a credibility-killing targeting miss. The hero builds click intent; the recs block destroys it. An adult shopper seeing preschool sneakers "based on their style" does not convert.
- The email would be an easy 9/10 with correct personalization. As-sent, the broken recs drag it to solid-but-wasted.

## What's working

- **Hero clarity**: "UP TO 30% OFF NIKE & JORDAN" in oversized type with an Air Jordan 1 Low hero image — zero ambiguity about what the email is offering.
- **Offer escalation**: 30% is a visible step up from the 20% off that ran July 2–5; returning subscribers register this as a better deal.
- **FLX member perk surfaced**: the magenta FLX Rewards strip explicitly calls out free shipping stacking on this promo — a tangible benefit for loyalty members.
- **Strikethrough pricing on tiles**: the crossed-out-original / now-price format reinforces deal depth at the product level even before a click.
- **Clean nav bar**: Men's / Women's / Kids' / Sale tabs at top give immediate wayfinding without cluttering the hero.

## What's weak

- **Boys' Preschool product recs for an apparent adult subscriber**: every tile in "BASED ON YOUR STYLE" shows a preschool size — wrong demographic segment, kills downstream click intent regardless of how strong the hero is.
- **No deadline**: "For a limited time only" is placeholder urgency copy. The July 2–5 promo sequence used "FINAL HOURS" and "LAST CHANCE" that manufactured real tension; this send has nothing equivalent.
- **"SHOP SALE" CTA undersells the 30% headline**: the action point doesn't carry the offer forward; a subscriber who skimmed the hero gets a generic button.
- **Subject is informational, not motivating**: "is here!" is filler — the offer is stated but there's no pull or deadline to act on it now.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the personalization segment first** — this is the single highest-ROI fix. Correct the audience segment feeding the recs block to the subscriber's actual purchase tier (Men's, Women's, etc.), or fall back to top-selling adult styles in the sale. No creative change needed; this is a data/segmentation fix.
- 2. **Add a hard deadline**: "Ends Sunday" or a specific date in both the hero subhead and the subject would activate urgency this email currently lacks.
- 3. **Rewrite the CTA**: change "SHOP SALE" to "GET 30% OFF" or "SHOP NIKE & JORDAN SALE" to close the loop between headline and action.
- 4. **Lift the subject and preheader**:
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off Nike & Jordan — ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 30% off Nike & Jordan deal is live`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `FLX members: free shipping stacks on top — shop before it's gone`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Select Nike & Jordan styles, up to 30% off at checkout`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Confirmed issues:**

**CSS selector collision (fluid grid):**
```css
/* Line targeting col-8 under md-fluid-row — appears twice */
.fluid-row .col-8,.md-fluid-row .col-8 { width: 66% !important; }
.fluid-row .col-9,.md-fluid-row .col-8 { width: 74.25% !important; }  /* BUG */
```
The second rule re-targets `.md-fluid-row .col-8` (should be `.md-fluid-row .col-9`). Result: `.md-fluid-row .col-8` gets width `74.25%` at mobile (wrong), and `.md-fluid-row .col-9` gets no mobile width rule at all — it will render at its desktop width on small screens.

**External font CSS blocked by major clients:**
```html
<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://partner-fonts.bluecore.com/footlocker/footlocker_fonts_1776712244.css">
```
Outlook (all desktop versions), Gmail (non-Google-account), and some iOS Mail configurations strip or ignore external `<link>` stylesheets. Custom fonts will silently fall back to system fonts — confirm fallback stack in inline styles is acceptable.

**Missing `lang` attribute on `<html>`:**
```html
<html>  <!-- should be <html lang="en"> -->
```
Screen readers cannot determine document language; fails WCAG 3.1.1.

**Viewport meta incomplete:**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width">
```
Missing `initial-scale=1`. Some Android WebView-based clients will apply automatic zoom without it.
