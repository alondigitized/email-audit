# Save Now: Up to 40% Off ️
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-28**
## Executive summary

- This is Foot Locker falling back to the same generic "sitewide markdown" playbook the brand history already flagged — no single hero product, no named colorway, just "500+ styles on sale" dressed up in back-to-school red. It's the same beat as the 2026-07-26 "LAST CHANCE: up to 30% off" send (5.00/10), just re-skinned with a backpack emoji and a bumped-up 40% ceiling, echoing the 2026-07-27 "BIG sale" send it most closely resembles structurally.
- The hero itself is well-built — bold red field, two clean product renders, oversized type, a clear bordered CTA button — and the "Picks for You" grid does real work with visible pricing, star ratings, and a markdown callout on the New Balance 530. But there's a hard render bug sitting right under the CTA: four solid black rectangles where icons or a promo strip should be, which reads as broken on delivery, not polish.
- Net: solid production values undercut by a real visual defect and a subject line that's pure filler compared to what this brand can do when it leads with an actual product (see the 9.00/10 Air Max 95 "Solar Red" and Jordan Retro 4 "Comic Con" sends). Middle-of-the-pack send, and the broken image blocks alone should have blocked this from going out.

## What's working

- The red "BACK-TO-SCHOOL BIG SALE / 500+ STYLES / ON SALE" hero stack has strong type hierarchy and is impossible to miss.
- "SHOP THE SALE" is a clear, high-contrast bordered button with obvious placement.
- FLX Rewards membership benefit (free shipping, no minimum) is called out twice — top nav and a dedicated magenta banner.
- The "Picks for You" grid shows real product data: price, star rating, and one item with a struck-through markdown ($85 → $49.99).
- Footer has full compliance/trust elements — address, unsubscribe link, "add to address book," multiple payment logos.

## What's weak

- Four solid black rectangles render directly below the "Exclusions apply" line — a clear broken-image/render bug in the hero panel.
- No single hero product or colorway is named, despite the brand's strongest recent sends (Air Max 95 "Solar Red," Jordan Retro 4 "Comic Con") proving that's what drives opens and clicks.
- The "Picks for You" grid skews heavily toward kids'/Grade School sizing (Boys, Girls) mixed with one adult style, so it doesn't clearly target one shopper demographic.
- Subject line has zero urgency or product specificity beyond a generic percentage — a step down from the brand's "today!" and "reserve now" language.
- This is at least the third generic sitewide-sale send in three days (07-26 "LAST CHANCE," 07-27 "BIG sale," now this one), which risks fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the broken black image blocks under the CTA before this goes out again — that's a hard delivery defect, not a style note.
- 2. Anchor the send on one hero product/colorway instead of "500+ styles" — the brand's own history shows named-colorway sends (Solar Red, Comic Con) score 9/10 vs. this generic beat's 5-8/10 range.
- 3. Add a real deadline ("Ends Sunday," "48 hours only") to convert "Save Now" from filler into actual urgency.
- 4. Curate "Picks for You" toward one consistent demographic per send rather than mixing kids' and adult sizing in the same six-product grid.
- 5. Strip the stray trailing character in the subject line — it's rendering as visual noise, not an emoji.
