# Homepage snapshot · www.footlocker.com · 2026-06-09
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-09**
## Executive summary

- Foot Locker's mobile homepage is going full sneaker museum right now — three heat pieces (New Balance 530, Air Max 95, AJ1) stacked on a red background, zero context. It reads like a legit sneaker retailer but the homepage feels like it's showing me product without actually telling me why I'm here or what the drop is. The "New Markdowns Added!" banner is the only real hook and it's doing heavy lifting with no muscle behind it.

## What's working

- **The shoe stack hero** — NB 530, Air Max 95, AJ1 in one frame on a clean red background is actually fire. That's three grails in one shot. I'm not bouncing immediately.
- **Category chip nav** — "Men's," "Women's," "Kids'," "New Arrivals" chips right below the search bar means I can hit Men's in one tap without digging through a hamburger menu.
- **"New Markdowns Added!" banner** — any markdown signal gets my attention. I'm always looking for a deal on carry-overs before the next drop.
- **Clean mobile layout** — no pop-up, no cookie modal blocking the view, no layout shift. The screen is usable immediately.

## What's weak

- **Zero hero copy** — the product image has no headline, no context, no "Now Available" or "Shop the Classics" overlay. I don't know if these are on sale, newly dropped, or editorial fluff.
- **Vague markdown banner** — "New Markdowns Added!" with no percentage is basically noise to me. If it said "Up to 40% off" or even "Jordan 1s just marked down," I'd tap immediately. Right now I have to go hunting.
- **No FLX rewards callout** — I check my FLX points balance constantly, especially around double-up weekends. Not a single mention of FLX above the fold. That's a miss for any returning customer.
- **No SNKRS/hype context** — is this editorial? Is it a raffle? Is the AJ1 a specific colorway I should know? No colorway name, no SKU signal, nothing for someone who tracks drops.
- **No person, no lifestyle** — just product floating in space. Shoots like this work on StockX because I'm buying blind. On the Foot Locker homepage I expect some energy — a fit check, a street scene, something.

## Recommendations

- **Add a headline to the hero**: Even one line like "Classic Heat, Marked Down" or "AJ1 | Air Max 95 | NB 530 — Shop the Sale" over the product stack instantly converts curiosity into a tap.
- **Put a specific markdown % in the announcement banner**: Swap "New Markdowns Added!" for "New Markdowns — Up to 40% Off" so I know it's worth clicking before I even look at the shoes.
- **Add an FLX points callout near the top nav**: A small "FLX Members earn 2× this weekend" chip next to the account icon costs nothing and turns every returning member into a convert.
- **Name the products in the hero**: Even a small "Jordan 1 Retro High OG | Air Max 95 | NB 530" label on the image stack gives me the signal I need — I want to know the colorway, not just the silhouette.
