# Reserve the adidas Adistar Jellyfish today!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-31**
## Executive summary

- This is the same "reservation calendar" hero Foot Locker ran on 2026-07-28 and 2026-07-29 (both 9.00/10), but stripped down to a single launch — the adidas Adistar Jellyfish — and it's weaker for it. The jet-ski hero image is striking and on-brand for the "Jellyfish" name, but the countdown timer underneath reads **00 : 00 : 00 : 00**, which either means the clock has already expired or the render is broken — either way it kills the urgency the whole hero is built around.
- Below the fold this reverts to a full grab-bag: FLX Fridays cash-back tiers, Jordan Retro 4 "Comic Con," Converse/Vans "Refresh the Rotation," and a Jordan Retro 3 "True Blue" — four more offers stacked under a single-product subject line. That's consistent with the brand's pattern of promising one hero in the subject and then burying it under three unrelated pushes.
- Net: strong single-sneaker concept, undercut by a broken-looking countdown and the now-familiar four-more-things overload. It's a mid-pack send — solid visual craft, but the timer bug and subject/body mismatch cap it.

## What's working

- The jet-ski/water visual for "Adistar Jellyfish" is a clever, on-theme hero image — one of the more distinctive visuals in this brand's recent sends.
- "RESERVE NOW" is a clear, unambiguous, single primary CTA directly under the hero.
- FLX Fridays tiers (Spend $125/Get $10, Spend $250/Get $20, Spend $400/Get $50) are a genuinely specific, honest offer with visible terms.
- Section dividers (pink Jordan block, dark "Refresh the Rotation" block, blue Jordan Retro 3 block) give clean visual separation between the four product pushes.

## What's weak

- The countdown timer shows **00 Days 00 Hours 00 Minutes 00 Seconds** — this reads as broken/expired, not urgent, and undercuts the entire "reservations close 7/31 at 4PM ET" premise.
- Subject line promises one product (Adistar Jellyfish), but the email immediately pivots to FLX Cash, Jordan Retro 4, Converse/Vans, and Jordan Retro 3 — four more offers competing for attention.
- No price or member-savings shown anywhere near the hero reservation — it's a reservation, not a purchase, so there's no offer to click toward in the primary CTA.
- This is Foot Locker's third "reservation" format email in four days (7/28, 7/29, now 7/31) — cadence fatigue is real for a subscriber who's seen this exact template twice already this week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix or remove the countdown timer — a live, correctly-ticking clock would restore urgency; a static/expired-looking one actively damages trust in the "reservations close" deadline.
- 2. Cut the email to just the Adistar Jellyfish reservation plus FLX Fridays — drop Jordan Retro 4, Converse/Vans, and Jordan Retro 3 into a separate send; a subject that names one shoe should deliver mostly that shoe.
- 3. Add a specific reservation window in the hero copy itself (e.g., "Reservations open 7/31, 9AM–4PM ET") rather than relying solely on the countdown.
- 4. Vary the format from the 7/28 and 7/29 reservation-calendar sends — same subscriber is now seeing near-identical layouts three times in a week.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call — reserve the Adistar Jellyfish before 4PM ET`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `One of 2026's most-hyped drops. Reserve your size before time runs out.`
