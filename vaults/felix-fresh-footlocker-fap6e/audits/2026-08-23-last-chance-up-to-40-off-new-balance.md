# Last Chance: up to 40% off New Balance!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth New Balance send in six days, but it's finally the right template for it — "Last Chance" urgency plus a genuine deadline claim ("Today's your last day") gives this recycled purple hero a reason to exist that 8/17, 8/18, and 8/20 didn't have. The hero is clean: bold "LAST CHANCE / UP TO 40% OFF / NEW BALANCE" stacked typography, one CTA ("SHOP THE SALE"), FLX 3X points as a secondary hook. Below the fold it sprawls into four redundant category buttons and a "Based on your style" product grid that's only half New Balance (Brooks, Puma, adidas x3 mixed in) — which undercuts the single-minded promo above it.
- The core problem isn't this specific email, it's the pattern: this is the same asset recycled a fourth time in under a week, now with "last chance" slapped on. If a subscriber saw 8/17, 8/18, or 8/20, this reads as the brand crying wolf on urgency it didn't mean the first three times.

## What's working

- Clear single hero message with strong visual hierarchy — "LAST CHANCE" and "40% OFF" dominate immediately.
- Copy explicitly states a deadline ("Today's your last day"), which is a real urgency signal, not vague language.
- FLX 3X points callout gives loyalty members a concrete incremental reason to act now.
- One unambiguous primary CTA ("SHOP THE SALE") directly under the offer, no scrolling needed.
- No render bugs — clean layout, images load, no overlapping text.

## What's weak

- "Based on your style" recommendations are mostly not New Balance (Brooks, Puma, adidas ×3) in an email whose entire premise is a New Balance sale — dilutes the offer.
- Four separate "Shop Men's/Women's/Kids'/All" buttons stacked below the hero add friction/choice paralysis right after a single clear CTA already existed.
- "Exclusions apply" appears twice with zero specifics — no indication of what's excluded, which reads as generic fine print.
- This is the same purple-gradient New Balance creative used on 8/17, 8/18, and 8/20 — a subscriber who's opened those will recognize instant reuse, undermining "last chance" credibility.
- No pricing/percentage shown on any of the actual New Balance product (the one NB item in the grid, "New Balance Lace...", shows no price at all next to items that do).

## Recommendations

- 1. Make "last chance" true and specific — swap "Today's your last day" for a real cutoff time (e.g., "Ends tonight at 11:59pm PT") and drop the recycled hero art in favor of a new creative so repeat subscribers register it as a genuinely different, final message rather than send #4 of the same graphic.
- 2. Cut the four stacked category CTAs down to one or two — they compete with the primary "Shop the Sale" button and add decision fatigue right after the hero.
- 3. Fix "Based on your style" to actually be New Balance-only, or relabel it "You might also like" if cross-brand — as-is it looks like a merchandising bug in a single-brand promo email.
- 4. Add the excluded categories/brands inline near "Exclusions apply" (even a short parenthetical) — bare disclaimer text with no info reads as evasive.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: up to 40% off New Balance`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Final hours — New Balance sale + 3X FLX points`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sale ends at midnight. FLX members earn 3X points on every pair.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your last shot at 40% off — shop before it's gone.`
