# All black styles for back-to-school.
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-12**
## Executive summary

- This is the same scattered-multi-section problem as the 8/10 "Head back to class in these all-time classics" send (4.00/10) — a subject line promising one specific thing ("All black styles") paired with a hero that delivers, then five more unrelated sections (court classics, "Set the Tone" lifestyle, kids back-to-school, "Hot Picks Under $100") that have nothing to do with all-black anything. The hero itself is genuinely solid — "HEAD BACK IN STYLE," a clean all-black sneaker tying laces, "SHOP TRIPLE BLACK" CTA — but it's one section out of six, buried under a wall of scroll.
- There's no discount, no price, no urgency anywhere in this email — just a rotating carousel of style categories with generic "Shop X" buttons. Compare to the "Big Sale" reds Foot Locker has been running since 8/8 (6.00/10 average) — those at least had a number attached. This one has none.
- Verdict: the hero delivers on the subject, which is an improvement over 8/10's total mismatch, but the email dilutes its own promise by page 2 and offers zero competitive reason to click over just browsing the site.

## What's working

- The hero directly matches the subject — "HEAD BACK IN STYLE" + "trending all-black kicks" + "SHOP TRIPLE BLACK" is a rare case of subject/hero alignment for this brand.
- Clean render — no overlapping text, no broken images, all six sections display correctly.
- Nav bar and "FLX Members get free shipping & returns" is visible above the fold, giving loyalty members a reason to stay.
- Color-blocked sections (black, maroon, brown, blue) create visual rhythm even if the content doesn't cohere.

## What's weak

- Five of six sections (court classics, "Set the Tone," kids back-to-school, "Hot Picks Under $100") have nothing to do with "all black styles" — the subject's promise expires after one scroll.
- Zero offer anywhere — no %, no $, no BOGO. Every CTA is a generic "Shop X" with no incentive attached.
- No urgency or timing signal at all, unlike the brand's own sale sends this week.
- "Hot Picks Under $100" buries the one concrete price signal in the email at the very bottom, disconnected from the black-styles theme.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the one section that matches the subject. If "all black styles" is the hook, the whole send should be all-black product — not a hero plus five unrelated categories. This is the single biggest lift available.
- 2. Attach a real number to the hero CTA — even "20% off Triple Black styles" beats "SHOP TRIPLE BLACK" with no price context.
- 3. If the multi-category format must stay, retitle the subject to match reality (e.g., "Back-to-school shop: black kicks, court classics & kids") instead of promising one thing and delivering six.
- 4. Move "Hot Picks Under $100" — the only concrete price signal — up near the top, since price is the strongest click driver you have and it's currently the last thing seen.
