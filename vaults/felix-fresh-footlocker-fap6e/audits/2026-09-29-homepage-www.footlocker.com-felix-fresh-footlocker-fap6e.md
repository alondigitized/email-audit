# Homepage snapshot · www.footlocker.com · 2026-09-29
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- Foot Locker is running a big "Up to 40% Off Sale" hero with a Bred Jordan 1 Low front and center. The top strip repeats the same message. It reads as a sneaker retailer in promo mode, not launch mode, which is a bit of a bummer since I came here for the calendar and raffles. Under the hero there's a Nike x NBA "Worn With Obsession" collection teaser, but I only see the headline and the top edge of the product tiles.

## What's working

- **Bred Jordan 1 Low on the "SALE" pattern:** It's a shoe I actually recognize, and it makes the promo feel like it includes real heat, not just clearance.
- **"Turn your wishlist into your everyday rotation":** The copy speaks my language. "Rotation" is exactly how I think about my closet.
- **Nav includes "Releases" and "New & Trending":** I can get to launches in one tap.
- **"Sign In | Join FLX" plus the purple FLX Rewards link:** The color makes it the one nav item that stands out.
- **Carousel pause/prev/next controls:** I can stop the auto-advance, so it doesn't feel pushy.

## What's weak

- **Four same-size CTA buttons:** They're small, with washed-out gray backgrounds on the orange gradient. No single button says "this one."
- **No FLX detail anywhere above the fold:** I'm FLX-points-aware on every cart, and I see no points multiplier, no double-up weekend and no member price.
- **No raffle or launch-calendar teaser:** For a "Releases" person, the hero is a discount, not a drop.
- **The hero shoe has no name, price or link:** I can't tell if that Jordan is in the 40% off, and I can't tap straight to it.
- **Nike x NBA module is apparel-first:** The visible tiles are hoodies and tees, and I shop sneakers.
- **No deadline on the sale:** "Up to 40% off" with no end date means no reason to act now.

## Recommendations

- Make "Shop the Sale" one solid, high-contrast button (white fill, black text, bigger). Move Men's, Women's and Kids' into a small text-link row underneath.
- Add an FLX line in the hero, such as "FLX members: double points this weekend" with a "Join FLX" button. It gives me a loyalty reason to buy now.
- Put a name, a price and a "Shop" link on the hero Jordan (for example "Air Jordan 1 Low OG 'Bred' – $X"), and say what's actually discounted.
- Move a "Releases / Upcoming Drops" strip or raffle-entry module up under the hero, and add an end date to the sale copy ("Ends Sunday").
