# Reserve the Nike Ja 4 'Nightmare'.
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a subject-line/body mismatch problem dressed up as a launch email. The subject promises "Reserve the Nike Ja 4 'Nightmare'" — but the hero that opens the render is "DON'T SLEEP ON JA 4" with a plain "SHOP NIKE" button, not a reservation CTA at all. The actual reservation mechanic ("A CLASSIC REBORN," Retro 3 "Sports Renaissance," "RESERVE NOW," deadline 8/14 4PM ET) is the *second* section, for a completely different shoe.
- After that it sprawls further: a red "Hot Picks Under $100" multi-brand section, then a cyan HOKA Clifton 11 running-shoe pitch. Four unrelated offers stacked in one email, none of which is the thing the subject told me to open for. This is the same scattered-multi-section pattern flagged in the 8/10 and 8/11 "classics" sends — Foot Locker keeps building single-subject emails on multi-offer bodies.
- The one genuine strength: the Jordan Retro 3 section is clean, has a real deadline, and a real product shot. If this had been the whole email — with the subject matching it — it'd score well. As sent, it's a confusing bait email that undercuts its own reservation urgency.

## What's working

- The Retro 3 "Sports Renaissance" section has a specific, credible deadline ("Reservations close 8/14 at 4PM ET").
- Clean full-bleed product photography throughout — no broken images, no layout breakage.
- Color-blocked sections make each offer visually distinct and easy to scan individually.
- FLX Rewards banner and footer member benefit ("FREE SHIPPING. NO MINIMUM.") are visible for loyalty context.

## What's weak

- Subject says "Reserve the Nike Ja 4 'Nightmare'" but the Ja 4 hero has no reservation language or deadline — it's a generic "SHOP NIKE" push, and "Nightmare" (the colorway name) never appears anywhere in the visible copy.
- The actual "RESERVE NOW" CTA belongs to a different shoe (Jordan Retro 3), not the one named in the subject — a reader clicking in expecting to reserve the Ja 4 gets redirected mid-scroll to a different product.
- Four distinct offers (Ja 4, Retro 3, Hot Picks Under $100, HOKA Clifton 11) crammed into one send with no unifying theme — this is the same scattered-section issue flagged in the 8/10 and 8/11 audits.
- HOKA Clifton 11 section has zero connection to a Jordan/Nike basketball-sneaker-reservation subject line — it reads like a bolted-on cross-sell.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the subject-to-hero mismatch first.** Either lead the hero with the actual Ja 4 "Nightmare" reservation mechanic (deadline, "RESERVE NOW" CTA) to match the subject, or rewrite the subject to match what the hero actually sells ("SHOP NIKE" / general Ja 4 push).
- 2. **Cut this to one offer.** Split Retro 3, Hot Picks Under $100, and HOKA into separate sends — Foot Locker already has a working single-drop template (the 8/11 "Reserve the Jordan Retro 3" solo send scored 7/10).
- 3. **Put a real deadline on the Ja 4 section** the way the Retro 3 section has one — the whole "reserve" premise is toothless without urgency visible in the hero.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Reserve the Nike Ja 4 'Nightmare' — closes 8/14`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Ja 4 'Nightmare': last call to reserve your size`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Reservations close 8/14 at 4PM ET — don't miss your size.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Be first in line for Ja Morant's next signature drop.`
- 4. **Drop "Nightmare" into visible copy** — right now the colorway name that's doing all the work in the subject line never shows up on screen.
