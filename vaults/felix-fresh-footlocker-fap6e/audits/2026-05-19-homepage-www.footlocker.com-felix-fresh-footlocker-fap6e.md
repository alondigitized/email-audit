# Homepage snapshot · www.footlocker.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Foot Locker is out here pushing a kids' sale as the main hero — that's the first thing I see, not Jordans, not SNKRS-adjacent drops, not FLX rewards. The New Balance Grey Days banner up top is actually the most relevant signal for me, but it's a tiny promo strip you'd easily scroll past. Feels like the homepage was set for a different audience than me.

## What's working

- **New Balance Grey Days 2026 banner** — Grey Days is a legit sneakerhead calendar moment. Having it pinned at the very top says the brand is aware of the drop cycle I track.
- **"Shop the Sale →" CTA** — Clean arrow button, impossible to miss, no ambiguity about what happens when I tap it.
- **Horizontal category tab strip** (Men's / Women's / Kids' / New Arrivals) — I can get to Men's in literally one tap. That's the fastest path to something I care about.
- **Lifestyle photo below the fold** — The young dude in the beanie and wide-leg pants is exactly my aesthetic. That image would slow my scroll.

## What's weak

- **Hero is a kids sale** — "Save on top kids styles from New Balance, Nike, and more!" is a hard miss for me. I'm here for my pairs, not a back-to-school rack.
- **Zero FLX visibility above the fold** — I check double-up weekends before I cart anything. If I don't see my points or a member-pricing badge, I feel like a basic guest.
- **No Jordan presence whatsoever** — Nothing above the fold hints at any Jordan or Nike Dunk content. That's my main reason to open Foot Locker over SNKRS.
- **No urgency or scarcity signals** — Grey Days has limited colorways. The banner mentions it but doesn't communicate "limited" or "ends X date," so there's no reason to tap now vs. later.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero to a men's sneaker drop** — Push the kids sale to a secondary slot. Make the primary hero a New Balance Grey Days exclusive colorway or a Jordan restock with "FLX members get early access" copy.
- **Add an FLX balance/rewards strip directly under the nav** — A one-line "You have 450 FLX points — double-up weekend this weekend" bar would stop me mid-scroll every time.
- **Add a countdown or "limited" badge to the Grey Days banner** — Even "Only X left" or "Ends Sunday" would create the same urgency as the SNKRS app that I'm already wired to respond to.
- **Surface a New Arrivals product rail above the fold** — A horizontal scroll of Men's new drops with actual shoe images and a price tag keeps me on the page; a generic sale hero doesn't.
