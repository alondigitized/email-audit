# Up to 40% off NOW .
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-04**
## Executive summary

- Foot Locker returns to the "Up to 40% Off" markdown well for the fifth time in nine days (2026-07-28, 08-01, 08-02 twice, 08-03, now 08-04), and this time it's wrapped in "Back-to-School Big Sale / First Day Fresh" branding instead of the BTS-generic framing from earlier sends. The hero is bold and legible — big red "40% OFF" numerals dominate the fold — but the bottom of that hero has an obvious render bug: four solid black rectangles sit stacked on the red gradient like a broken image grid that never loaded.
- The product grid underneath is the real value here — six specific SKUs with prices, one with a strikethrough discount, several with star ratings — which is more concrete than the pure-hero sends this brand has been running. But the subject line itself is a retread of 2026-08-03's "Up to 40% off has been extended!", now stripped of the "extended" honesty and just repackaged as new urgency days later. That's the same extend-then-re-announce pattern flagged in the 08-03 audit, and by day five subscribers will notice.
- Net: solid product real estate, competent visual hierarchy, but a visible rendering defect and a subject line that's now cried wolf on urgency one too many times this week.

## What's working

- Large, high-contrast "UP TO 40% OFF" numerals are the first thing the eye hits — impossible to miss.
- "SHOP THE SALE" CTA button is outlined, centered, and unambiguous.
- Product grid shows six real SKUs with names, colorways, prices, and one visible discount ($77 → $61.60) plus star ratings — more concrete than several recent Foot Locker sends.
- FLX Rewards free-shipping banner reinforces a loyalty benefit right below the grid.
- Clean nav bar and footer with store locator, app/SMS links, and payment options — no layout breakage there.

## What's weak

- Four solid black rectangular blocks sit at the bottom of the hero banner — looks like a broken/missing image asset, a clear render bug.
- Subject "Up to 40% off NOW ." is nearly identical to yesterday's "Up to 40% off has been extended!" — same offer, no new specifics, and the stray period before the space reads like a template error.
- "Exclusions apply" sits directly under the CTA with no detail — the offer's honesty signal is undercut right where the eye lands next.
- No urgency element in the hero itself (no countdown/deadline) despite the subject implying immediacy with "NOW."
- Five product-grid items show no star rating at all, inconsistent with the two that do — reads unfinished.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the broken black-box graphic at the base of the hero before this ships again — it's the single most visible defect in the render and undermines an otherwise strong hero.
- 2. Stop re-announcing the same 40%-off offer under a new subject every day — either commit to one sustained BTS campaign subject for the week or genuinely escalate the offer (deeper %, added category) so repeat opens feel earned.
- 3. Clean up the subject line typo/spacing ("NOW .") and add a concrete anchor — a category or deadline — so it doesn't read as a copy-paste of yesterday's.
- 4. Move "Exclusions apply" into smaller, less prominent placement, or replace with a one-line summary of what IS included, to protect the offer's honesty signal.
- 5. Add star ratings consistently across all six grid products, or drop the ones that show inconsistently.
