# Secure the Jordan Retro 6 ‘Black & White’ today!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-08**
## Executive summary

- Foot Locker is now on its fourth consecutive day pushing the Jordan Retro 6 'Black and White' — "Reserve" on 8/4, "Reserve" again on 8/5, "Reserve" again on 8/6, and now "Secure" on 8/8. The hero itself is strong and on-brand: bold stacked typography, a clean product shot, a specific "SHOP JORDAN" CTA above the fold. But the repetition is the story here — a subscriber who's seen this shoe hyped three times already this week has little reason to open a fourth near-identical send, and "Secure" reads as a thin subject-line swap rather than a fresh hook.
- Below the hero, the email sprawls into three more unrelated product pushes (Animal Instinct Pack, Reebok x Barbie, a "Looks You'll Love" grid) with no unifying theme, which dilutes the Jordan focus the subject promised. It's the same "strong hero, cluttered body" pattern seen across recent Foot Locker sends.
- Net: solid execution on the top module, but cadence fatigue and a diluted body drag this down from what could be a genuinely good targeted send.

## What's working

- Hero delivers exactly what the subject promises — "JORDAN RETRO 6 'BLACK AND WHITE'" headline directly over a clean product shot.
- Primary CTA "SHOP JORDAN" is a bordered, unambiguous button placed right under the copy.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text is legible, no overlap.
- FLX Rewards membership banner is visible near the top and reinforced again in the footer band.

## What's weak

- This is the fourth Jordan Retro 6 'Black/White' send in five days (8/4, 8/5, 8/6, and now 8/8) — the offer/urgency ("Secure...today!") feels manufactured given the shoe has been "about to drop" all week.
- The body abandons the Jordan focus almost immediately — Animal Instinct Pack, Reebok x Barbie, and a generic "Looks You'll Love" grid crowd out the hero's momentum.
- "Looks You'll Love" grid is mostly comfort/lifestyle product (UGGs, Birkenstocks, adidas kids shoes) with no connection to sneakerhead/Jordan intent signaled by the subject.
- No release date, price, or "reserve vs. buy now" distinction shown in the hero — just "can finally get a taste," which is vague for a release this hyped.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending near-identical Jordan Retro 6 emails daily — either escalate with real new information (drop date, price, limited quantity count) or skip a day. Repetition without new information trains subscribers to stop opening.
- 2. Cut the email to two modules max: Jordan hero + one complementary sneaker drop (Animal Instinct Pack), and drop Reebok x Barbie and the mixed lifestyle grid — they don't serve the subject's promise.
- 3. Add a concrete detail to the hero copy — release date, price, or "limited pairs" — to justify "Secure...today" urgency.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: Jordan Retro 6 'Black and White' still available`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sizes are going fast on the most-wanted release of the week.`
