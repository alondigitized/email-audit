# Shop now to get up to 40% off top styles.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-08**
## Executive summary

- Foot Locker leads with a genuinely bold, single-focus hero — "BACK-TO-SCHOOL BIG SALE / BASKETBALL DEALS GALORE" over a black-and-teal basketball shoe — which is a stronger creative moment than the scattered six-section catalog scrolls this brand has been sending most days this week (see 2026-08-05, "Get first day fresh with Vans, Converse & more," 9.00/10, six sections). The problem is the subject line doesn't match what's actually in the render: "up to 40% off top styles" promises a specific discount, but nowhere in the hero, the category grid, or the product tiles does a "40%" or any percentage badge actually appear. The only visible markdown in the whole email is a single Under Armour sports bra at $29.99 (was $35) — roughly 15% off, not 40%.
- That gap between subject and delivered offer is the email's core weakness. It's the same pattern flagged on 2026-08-07's "Stay fresh with top back-to-school styles" (5.00/10) — subject promises one thing, hero delivers something adjacent but different. Here the hero at least nails a coherent theme (basketball/BTS), but the subject's specific "%" number is unsupported by anything visible, which reads as bait when a reader actually opens looking for the discount.
- Net: a visually clean, on-brand send with the year's usual FLX loyalty banner and solid product-grid ratings, undercut by a subject line making a promise the creative doesn't keep.

## What's working

- Hero is a single clear theme (basketball + back-to-school) with strong typographic contrast (red/black/white) — no competing messages at first glance.
- Two clearly differentiated CTA buttons ("SHOP BASKETBALL STYLES ON SALE" / "SHOP ALL SALE") sit right under the hero copy, no scrolling needed.
- "Shop Sale by Category" section gives four clean, tappable category buttons (Footwear/Apparel/Accessories/All Sale).
- Product grid ("Looks You'll Love") shows star ratings on every item (4.2–4.8), which builds trust.
- FLX Rewards free-shipping-no-minimum banner appears twice (top and mid-email), reinforcing the loyalty value prop.

## What's weak

- Subject promises "up to 40% off" but no percentage discount is visible anywhere in the rendered email — only one product (Under Armour) shows a strikethrough price, and it's roughly 15% off, not 40%.
- Five of six featured products in "Looks You'll Love" show full price with no discount at all, directly contradicting the subject's sale framing.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere in the render — "Shop now" in the subject isn't backed by a countdown, end date, or "today only" cue in the body.
- Product tiles show no sizing/fit/availability info, just name, price, and rating.
- Hero-linked CTAs go to category pages ("Shop Basketball Styles," "Shop All Sale"), not to a single hero product — reduces click confidence for someone drawn in by that specific shoe.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the subject/creative mismatch first — either put an actual "40% OFF" badge in the hero (or at minimum on the discounted products shown) or rewrite the subject to match what's really there (a basketball/BTS sale, not a specific 40% claim). This is the single biggest lift since it's what determines whether the open converts to trust or bounce.
- 2. Add a visible discount % or "Save up to 40%" callout directly on the hero banner or the first CTA button so the promised number is verifiable within one screen.
- 3. Swap at least 2-3 of the six product-grid items for genuinely discounted styles so "Looks You'll Love" reinforces the sale premise instead of undercutting it.
- 4. Add a credible deadline ("Ends Sunday," "This week only") near the hero CTA to convert "Shop now" urgency into something real.
- 5. Link the hero product image itself to that specific shoe's PDP rather than only to the category page.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Basketball deals galore — save on Nike, Jordan & adidas`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Back-to-school hoops sale: prices you'll actually love`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `FLX Members get free shipping — no minimum, ends soon.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Top hoops styles from Nike, Jordan, adidas & more inside.`
