# Earn FLX points today during FLX Fridays when you shop.
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-07**
## Executive summary

- This is the rare Foot Locker send where the hero copy actually matches the subject line — "FLX Fridays / Spend More Earn More" delivers on "Earn FLX points today during FLX Fridays when you shop." That's a real improvement over the disconnect this brand has shown in other recent sends (2026-08-05's Birkenstock hero was the last time subject and hero lined up this cleanly).
- But look closer and the promise cracks: the subject says "points," the entire body only ever says "FLX Cash." That's a literal terminology mismatch a subscriber will notice mid-scroll. And once you get past the FLX block, it's the same five-section catalog scroll this brand keeps sending — the Jordan Retro 6 'Black/White' reservation is back for what's now its fourth-plus consecutive day (8/4, 8/5 twice, 8/6, and now 8/7), followed by Salomon, a BTS sale banner, and a Converse/Vans push.
- Net: solid hero execution undercut by a copy inconsistency and the same reused catalog-scroll structure and stale Jordan creative that's dragged recent sends down.

## What's working

- Hero directly matches the subject line — the FLX Cash spend tiers ($125→$10, $250→$20, $400→$50) are legible and specific, sitting right at the top with no scroll needed.
- Clean, bug-free render — no overlapping text, no broken images, sharp product photography on the Jordan 6 and Converse/Vans pairing.
- FLX Rewards footer banner reinforces the loyalty hook (free shipping, no minimum) right where a member would look for it.
- Nav and footer (store locator, app download, payment options) are complete and functional-looking.

## What's weak

- Subject promises "FLX points" but the body exclusively uses "FLX Cash" — never once says "points." That's a real wording mismatch, not just nitpicking.
- Two competing buttons stacked immediately under the hero (SHOP NOW, JOIN FLX FOR FREE) blur which action is actually primary.
- The Jordan Retro 6 'Black/White' reservation block is running for its fourth-plus straight day with the same product shot and "Reservations close 8/7 at 4PM ET" urgency line — this is the last day it can even claim urgency.
- Still a long catalog scroll (FLX Fridays → Jordan → Salomon → BTS sale → Converse/Vans) rather than a focused single-offer send built around the subject's actual topic.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the subject/body mismatch — say "FLX Cash" in the subject, not "points," since that's what the email actually delivers.
- 2. Collapse the hero to one primary CTA (SHOP NOW) and demote "Join FLX" to a smaller secondary link — two stacked buttons of equal weight competes for the click.
- 3. Retire the Jordan Retro 6 creative or give it a genuinely new angle — it's been the second section four sends running and today's the deadline, so tomorrow it needs to be gone or replaced.
- 4. Write an actual preheader that previews the $ tiers ("Spend $125, earn $10 back") — none is showing here, wasting the inbox line the subject alone should not have to carry.
- 5. Cut the send to three sections max (FLX Fridays, Jordan close-out, one more) — the Salomon/BTS/Converse tail dilutes the FLX Fridays focus the subject sets up.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Get $10-$50 FLX Cash today — FLX Fridays is here`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Spend $125+, earn FLX Cash back (today only)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $125, $250 or $400 — stack up to $50 in FLX Cash.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Today only: shop and get FLX Cash back automatically.`
