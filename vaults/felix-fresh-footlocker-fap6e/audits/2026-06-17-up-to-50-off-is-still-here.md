# Up to 50% off is still here!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-17**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Summer Savings send in four days (June 14 opener → June 15 "still got time" → June 17 "still here"), and it shows. The same neon yellow-green hero with the oversized "50% OFF / SUMMER SAVINGS HEATING UP" treatment runs unchanged from the June 15 send — no creative refresh, no escalation, no new reason to act. The "still here" subject echoes June 15's "still got time" almost word for word, which means subscribers who ignored the last one get no new hook to grab.
- What's genuinely strange: the hero promises "Sneakers, apparel & more" but the entire product block is UGG — Disquette slides, Scuffette II, Tasman Hoodie, Classic Short boots, Pluff Yeah slippers, Classic Slip On. Six products, zero Nikes, zero Jordans, zero Adidas. For Foot Locker's core sneakerhead audience this is a weird mismatch that probably mutes clicks.
- The underlying offer (up to 50% off) is real and competitive. The email is technically clean. But without a deadline and with creative fatigue this deep into the run, the email is coasting on the offer's gravity rather than doing any marketing work of its own.

## What's working

- The neon yellow-green hero delivers the "50% off" message instantly — zero ambiguity on the offer.
- "SHOP THE SALE" CTA is prominent and unambiguous, placed directly below the hero copy.
- The six-product grid gives something to browse even if the category selection is off.
- FLX Rewards section and multi-payment footer (Klarna, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal) add genuine utility.
- No render bugs — clean layout, no broken images, no overlapping text.

## What's weak

- "Hot Styles" is six UGG products — slides, slippers, and boots — directly contradicting "Sneakers, apparel & more" in the hero copy. This is a trust-eroding bait.
- Zero deadline anywhere in the email — "still here" has no end date, so the urgency is vague aspiration rather than actionable scarcity.
- The creative is identical to June 15 — same template, same headline, same color. Third time in four days; the neon is wearing out its welcome.
- "Still here" subject is the same beat as June 15's "still got time" — same energy, no escalation.
- No FLX tier personalization or member pricing surfaced in the body despite FLX being a core Foot Locker retention lever.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a hard deadline to the hero and subject.** "Ends Sunday" or "48 hours left" converts passive awareness into urgency. This is the single highest-leverage change — the offer is good but it reads as evergreen right now.
- 2. **Swap the product block for sneakers.** If the hero says "Sneakers, apparel & more," the six hot styles should be Nikes, Jordans, or Adidas — not UGG. If UGG is the actual story, rewrite the hero to match.
- 3. **Refresh the hero creative.** The neon Summer Savings template has run three times in four days. Introduce a countdown timer graphic or a new colorway to signal "this is different from the last one you ignored."
- 4. **Inject FLX tier in the subject or preheader.** "Gold member: your 50% off window closes soon" outperforms a generic blast.
- 5. **Name a specific hero product.** One featured shoe with a struck-through original price in the hero would anchor the offer and pull a click.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your last 48 hours: Summer Savings up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Jordan, Nike, Adidas and more — savings expire this weekend.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The sale is almost over. Here's what's still in stock.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Confirmed issues:**

**CSS selector typo — wrong column width applied at tablet breakpoint**
```css
/* Line in the truncated source: */
.fluid-row .col-9,.md-fluid-row .col-8 { width: 74.25% !important; }
/*                              ^^^^^^ should be .col-9, not .col-8 */
```
The `.md-fluid-row .col-9` selector is missing; instead `.md-fluid-row .col-8` gets the 74.25% rule applied a second time, overriding the correct 66% value defined earlier. A 9-column fluid block on tablet will render at the wrong width.

**Missing `lang` attribute on `<html>`**
```html
<html>  <!-- no lang="en" -->
```
Screen readers default to the OS language; required for accessibility compliance (WCAG 2.1 §3.1.1) and some spam filters flag it.

**Incomplete `viewport` meta tag**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width">
<!-- missing initial-scale=1 -->
```
iOS Safari may render at an unexpected zoom level on first load.

**`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none !important`**
Applied globally to `div, p, a, li, span, td` in the mobile media query. This prevents users from scaling text to their preferred size — an accessibility concern and a violation of WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).

**External font with no fallback**
```html
<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://partner-fonts.bluecore.com/footlocker/footlocker_fonts_1776712244.css">
```
No `font-family` stack fallback is declared in the inline styles shown. If this CDN is unavailable or blocked (common in some corporate email clients), the email falls back to the client's default serif — confirm fallback stacks are defined in the font CSS itself.
