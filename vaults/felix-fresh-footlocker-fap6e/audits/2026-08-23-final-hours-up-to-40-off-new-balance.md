# FINAL HOURS: up to 40% off New Balance.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-23**
## Executive summary

- Same purple-gradient New Balance hero Foot Locker has run at least three times in the past week (8/17, 8/18, 8/20-ish variants), now with "SALE ENDS TODAY" swapped in for genuine urgency. The urgency line is a real upgrade — "FINAL HOURS" in the subject plus "Exclusions apply" honesty note reads more credible than the last two New Balance sends that scored a flat 5. The template itself is clean: single dominant hero, one CTA, product carousel below with real pricing and star ratings.
- The problem is fatigue, not execution. This is the fourth-plus New Balance push in six days using the same jacket-and-sneaker photography, same purple gradient, same layout skeleton. A subscriber who's seen 8/17, 8/18, and 8/22 versions of this exact creative will recognize it instantly, and recognition without novelty erodes urgency claims — "sale ends today" reads less credible the fourth time you've heard it. Net: solid single-send craft, weak as part of a sequence.

## What's working

- Bold, unambiguous hero hierarchy — "SALE ENDS TODAY / UP TO 40% OFF / NEW BALANCE" reads instantly at a glance.
- Single clear CTA ("SHOP THE SALE") with no competing buttons above the fold.
- "Exclusions apply" is small but present — feels more honest than a bare percentage claim.
- FLX loyalty tie-in (3X points on New Balance) is specific and relevant, not generic loyalty filler.
- Product carousel below ("Based On Your Style") shows real prices, strikethrough discounts, and star ratings — good social proof and personalization signal.

## What's weak

- This is the same purple-gradient New Balance hero used repeatedly this week (8/17, 8/18, 8/22) — zero new creative for a "final hours" moment that should feel special.
- "Sale ends today" urgency is undercut by brand history — a subscriber has heard near-identical urgency language from Foot Locker on New Balance sends multiple times this week already.
- No explicit end time (e.g., "ends 11:59pm PT") — "today" without a clock creates vague urgency, not credible urgency.
- Carousel recommendations mix in non-New-Balance brands (Brooks, PUMA, adidas) right under a New Balance-exclusive hero, diluting focus.

## Recommendations

- 1. Retire this exact hero creative for at least one cycle — same photography four times in a week trains subscribers to skip it regardless of copy changes.
- 2. Add a hard deadline stamp to the hero ("Ends tonight at 11:59pm PT") instead of the vague "today" — turns a repeated claim into a verifiable one.
- 3. Swap the "Based On Your Style" carousel to New-Balance-only items to reinforce the single-brand offer instead of splitting attention across four brands.
- 4. Consider a countdown timer or last-call badge treatment to visually differentiate this from the prior New Balance sends since the layout is otherwise identical.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Score New Balance before the clock runs out — plus 3X FLX points.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Few hours left. Shop 40% off New Balance now.`
