# Homepage snapshot · www.footlocker.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Foot Locker is running their Summer Kickoff Sale and leading with Nike Air Max energy — the pool vibes and that white AM270 hovering over water are clean, I'll give them that. The New Balance Grey Days 2026 banner at the very top shows they're plugged into the launch calendar, which hits different for me. But above the fold it's basically a vibes page — no FLX balance, no "ends Sunday," no specific deal number — just a sale announcement that doesn't tell me why to tap right now.

## What's working

- **New Balance Grey Days 2026 banner** — that's the kind of launch-calendar awareness I respect. It signals Foot Locker knows the culture, not just the clearance rack.
- **Nike Air Max hero** — shoe-first imagery with actual product floating in frame keeps it relevant to what I'm here for.
- **SUMMER KICKOFF SALE headline treatment** — the yellow bold type on pool-water blue is loud in the right way. It punches through my scroll pattern.
- **Men's / Women's / Kids' / New Arrivals nav strip** — one-tap access to my section without hunting through a hamburger menu.

## What's weak

- **No FLX hook anywhere above the fold** — I'm FLX-aware on every cart and there's zero mention of points, double-up weekends, or my balance. This is the biggest miss for a returning member like me.
- **"SUMMER KICKOFF SALE" with no number** — Is it 20% off? 40%? BOGO? A sale without a figure reads like marketing, not a deal. I'm trained to skip these.
- **No CTA button in the hero** — the big bold SALE headline has no tap target. Where do I go from here? It just ends at the bottom of the screen.
- **No Jordan mention anywhere** — my main focus is Jordan + Nike drops and Jordan doesn't appear in any visible copy or imagery. FL carries Jordan hard but you'd never know from this screen.
- **No urgency signals** — no countdown, no "ends Sunday," no "limited pairs." For a launch-culture shopper, "sale" without scarcity is noise.

## Recommendations

- **Add an FLX double-up callout in the hero or banner strip** — even a small "FLX members earn 2x points this weekend" badge would make me feel like this page was built for me, not just anyone with a credit card.
- **Put a specific deal figure in the hero** — swap "SUMMER KICKOFF SALE" for "Up to 40% Off" or "Members Save 30%" so there's a reason to tap now instead of later.
- **Drop a tappable CTA button below the hero copy** — "Shop the Sale" or "See All Deals" with a pill button so the hero actually converts instead of just looks cool.
- **Rotate the top banner to flag a Nike or Jordan exclusive** — the NB Grey Days banner is great cultural flavor but I'm here for Jordan and Nike SNKRS cross-shop. A "Nike exclusives" or "Just Dropped" callout would keep me on site.
