# Reserve the Jordan Retro 8 'Chrome' today.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-24**
## Executive summary

- This is the same Jordan Retro 8 "Chrome" reservation send from earlier today (8/24), and it's still Foot Locker's strongest recent creative — moody grayscale hero, "OG-LIKE AURA" headline, single dominant CTA above the fold, no clutter in the reservation block itself. Where it falls apart is everything after: five more product blocks (Hoka Bondi 9, Bad Bunny F50 cleats, "Classroom Classics," "Hot Picks Under $100") turn what should be a focused drop announcement into the same kitchen-sink scroll Foot Locker has run all week under different subject lines.
- The Jordan hero alone would score well — clean layout, credible urgency ("Reservations close 8/28 at 4 PM ET"), collector-relevant copy about the 11-year vault gap and MJ Face Box packaging. But a recipient opening for Jordan info scrolls into HOKA running shoes and adidas cleats that have nothing to do with the subject line's promise, which dilutes the send's focus and repeats a structural problem flagged in the 8/22 "back-to-school" and 8/22 "Laser" audits.
- Verdict: good hero, bad follow-through — ship the top block as its own email and this jumps two points.

## What's working

- Grayscale hero with bold "OG-LIKE AURA" wordmark is visually distinct from the purple New Balance sends and the neon Jordan "Chrome" hero used elsewhere this week.
- Copy is specific and collector-relevant: "After 11 years in the sneaker vault," "drops inside the timeless MJ Face Box," with a hard reservation deadline (8/28, 4 PM ET).
- Single, unambiguous "RESERVE NOW" button directly under the hero copy, no scroll needed for the primary CTA.
- No render bugs — images loaded cleanly, no overlapping text or broken layout anywhere in the send.

## What's weak

- Five unrelated product blocks (Hoka, Bad Bunny F50, Classroom Classics, Hot Picks Under $100) bury the Jordan-specific subject-line promise in a long, unfocused scroll.
- A second "Reservations close 8/28 at 4PM ET" reservation CTA appears for the Bad Bunny F50 cleats, competing directly with the Jordan reservation for attention and diluting the "one hero" framing implied by the subject.
- Generic section headers ("Classroom Classics," "Hot Picks Under $100") carry no urgency or specificity and read like filler inventory dumps, not a curated release email.
- No visible product image of the actual Jordan Retro 8 "Chrome" colorway itself — only a dark, hard-to-parse grayscale shoe silhouette that doesn't clearly show the "Chrome" colorway being sold.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to just the Jordan reservation block — move Hoka, F50, Classroom Classics, and Hot Picks into a separate weekly-roundup send. This is the single biggest lift: it turns a diluted 6-product scroll into a focused drop announcement that matches its own subject line.
- 2. Show an actual clear product shot of the Chrome colorway (not just a moody grayscale silhouette) so buyers can confirm this is the pair they want before clicking Reserve.
- 3. Remove or relabel the second "Reservations close 8/28 at 4PM ET" CTA on the F50 block — identical deadline language for a second product confuses which reservation is time-sensitive.
- 4. Add a countdown clock to the Jordan hero, consistent with the reservation drop template used in yesterday's Chrome send, to reinforce urgency visually rather than just in text.
