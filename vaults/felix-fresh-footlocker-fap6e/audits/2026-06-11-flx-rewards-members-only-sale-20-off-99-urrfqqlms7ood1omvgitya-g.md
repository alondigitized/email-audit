# FLX Rewards Members Only Sale: 20% off $99.
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-11**
## Executive summary

- This is the payload email for the FLX Rewards Members Only Sale, arriving June 11 — sandwiched between the June 10 teaser and a near-identical send on June 12 ("You can get 20% off purchases of $99!"). Three emails in three days on the same offer with the same magenta-on-black template is the biggest strategic problem here, not the email itself. At some point the member just starts tuning out the drum.
- The email itself is well-executed. The hero lands cleanly: MEMBERS ONLY SALE / 20% OFF $99 is the first thing the eye lands on, the dual CTAs bifurcate the funnel for members vs. non-members correctly, and dropping Jordan, Nike, and New Balance by name in the body copy immediately signals relevance. The product grid below with star ratings gives the curious member something to browse.
- What undermines the execution is the absence of any deadline. "Limited time only" is not an offer end date. When a member knows the same email will arrive again tomorrow (and it did), there is no reason to click today. That is the one change that would move the needle most.

## What's working

- **Hero hierarchy is clean**: MEMBERS ONLY SALE → 20% OFF $99 stacks in the right order and reads instantly on mobile
- **Dual CTA covers both funnel stages**: "SHOP & SAVE" for active members, "JOIN FOR FREE" for fence-sitters — both visible above the fold
- **Brand names in body copy**: "Nike, Jordan, New Balance" named explicitly gives the sneaker buyer immediate confirmation this offer covers their wishlist
- **FLX Rewards branding is consistent throughout**: loyalty framing reinforces the exclusivity of the offer
- **Product grid with star ratings**: the "LOOKS YOU'LL LOVE" section adds genuine browse content and social proof below the hero

## What's weak

- **No hard deadline anywhere**: "limited time only" is functionally no urgency; without an end date the member has zero reason to act now rather than tomorrow — or never
- **Third send on the same creative**: the June 10 teaser already used this template; by June 11 it is already on borrowed attention, and the June 12 re-send confirms it was overdeployed
- **"SHOP FOR FULL FAMILY" section kills momentum**: four generic department buttons interrupt the offer narrative without adding any proof that 20% off applies to anything specific
- **Preheader echoes the subject**: the top-of-email strip mirrors the MEMBERS ONLY / 20% OFF language rather than offering a complementary hook

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put a deadline in the hero** — this is the single change that would lift conversion most; "Ends Sunday midnight" or "72 hours left" belongs directly under "20% OFF $99," not buried in fine print
- 2. **Cut the "SHOP FOR FULL FAMILY" row** — it pads the email without reinforcing the offer; replace with a second editorial product shot or a tighter "trending this week" carousel
- 3. **Break the creative template** — after three deployments in three days, the next send in this campaign needs new imagery; even a single Jordan colorway hero shot would reset subscriber attention
- 4. **Rewrite the preheader to add new information**, not repeat it — a deadline or specific product category not in the subject would increase the open-to-click bridge
- **Subject Alt A:** `Members Only: 20% off your next order — ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your FLX perk: 20% off $99 through this weekend`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Jordan, Nike, New Balance — valid on $99+ orders through June 15`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Don't leave this on the table — offer ends Sunday at midnight`

## 1. Overview

This is the payload email for the FLX Rewards Members Only Sale, arriving June 11 — sandwiched between the June 10 teaser and a near-identical send on June 12 ("You can get 20% off purchases of $99!"). ### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Confirmed CSS selector bug — mobile column mismatch:**

```css

```

The `.col-9` rule for `fluid-row` is paired with `.md-fluid-row .col-8` instead of `.md-fluid-row .col-9`. Any layout using `md-fluid-row col-9` will receive no mobile width override and default to unintended behavior. All other column pairs (`col-1` through `col-8`, `col-10`, `col-11`) are correctly paired.

**External font stylesheet:**

```html
<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://partner-fonts.bluecore.com/footlocker/footlocker_fonts_1776712244.css">
```

Gmail (WebKit and non-WebKit), Outlook (all desktop versions), and Yahoo strip or block external `<link>` stylesheets. Fonts will silently fall back to system defaults in a large share of the recipient base. This is a known Bluecore pattern but should be validated against fallback font stacks in the inline CSS.

**`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none !important`** applied globally to `div, p, a, li, span, td`. This disables iOS auto-sizing across all elements rather than just problematic ones. Small body copy (e.g. legal footer text) may become unreadably small on older iOS devices without compensating `font-size` values.
