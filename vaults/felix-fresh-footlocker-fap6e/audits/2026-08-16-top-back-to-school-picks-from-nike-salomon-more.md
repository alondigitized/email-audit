# Top back-to-school picks from Nike, Salomon & more.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-16**
## Executive summary

- This is Foot Locker's multi-drop lifestyle template again — the same shape as the Ja 4/Kobe V/Jordan Retro 3 sends this week — but with a subject line that actually fits it for once. "Top back-to-school picks from Nike, Salomon & more" sets up a curated roundup, and the body delivers exactly that: a lifestyle hero, Jordan Retro 3, Vans Old Skool "36 Souvenir," and a Nike Ja 4 spot. No mismatch this time.
- The problem is the top banner. A red "UP TO 50% OFF EXTENDED BACK-TO-SCHOOL 🎒 BIG SALE" strip sits above a completely different "SPARK YOUR LOOK" narrative, and it's the same red sale banner this brand has run on every send since 8/8 (see 8/16's "LAST CHANCE" audit, 6.00/10, and 8/15's "still here" send, 7.00/10). It reads as bolted-on urgency rather than integrated into the roundup concept below it, and it's competing with the subject line for what the email is actually about.
- Below the banner, execution is clean — no render bugs, good color-blocking between sections, clear single CTA per product. But three of four product blocks (Jordan, Vans, Ja 4) have no visible price or discount, so despite the subject promising "picks," most of the email is pure browse content with the actual offer parked at the top, disconnected from any specific product.

## What's working

- Subject and body are aligned — this is genuinely a "top picks" roundup across Nike, Vans, and Jordan, matching the promise.
- Clean, distinct color-blocking per product section (charcoal, navy, green) makes scanning the email easy on mobile.
- Vans Old Skool section has real urgency ("Reservations close 8/19 at 4PM ET") that's specific and credible, not vague.
- Each section has one unambiguous CTA button ("SHOP JORDAN," "RESERVE NOW," "SHOP NIKE").
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, no overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The red 50%-off banner is disconnected from "SPARK YOUR LOOK" and the picks below it — it's the same recycled sale strip from every recent send, not tailored to this roundup.
- No visible pricing or discount on the Jordan Retro 3, Vans, or Ja 4 blocks — the "picks" are just browse links with zero purchase incentive shown.
- "Salomon" is called out by name in the subject line but never appears anywhere in the visible body — a subject/content gap.
- Ja 4 copy ("Keep the defense tossing and turning") is vague marketing language with no product detail (colorway, price, sizes) to act on.

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop "Salomon" from the subject if it's not in the email — right now it's a subject-line promise the body never pays off, which trains readers to distrust future subjects.
- 2. Add a price or "up to 50% off" flag directly on at least one hero product (Jordan or Vans) so the top banner's discount connects to something specific instead of floating alone.
- 3. Replace vague Ja 4 copy with a concrete detail — colorway name or price — matching the specificity Vans already has with its reservation deadline.
- 4. Merge the red banner concept into "SPARK YOUR LOOK" instead of stacking two competing headlines at the top.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Back-to-school picks: Jordan, Vans & Nike are up to 50% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New Jordan Retro 3, Vans, and Nike Ja 4 — all in one drop`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus: Vans Old Skool reservations close 8/19 at 4PM ET.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Gear up before the bell rings — deals inside.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a Bluecore-templated Foot Locker promotional email with a Bluecore click-tracking layer (`trk.bc.footlocker.com`) and Bluecore-hosted fonts/pixels (`mi.footlocker.com`). Automated QA shows a 38% pass rate driven by a broken tracked link, a missing plain-text MIME part, and absent compliance/authentication headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL]** One probed link timed out: link text "Display images to show real-time content" → `https://trk.bc.footlocker.com/ss/c/u001.udUFY2RF1c3A6...` (Bluecore SafeSend redirect). The link text itself is anomalous — it reads as an image-blocking fallback label rather than a real CTA, suggesting a broken/placeholder `alt` or conditional block was serialized into a live anchor. Combined with the timeout, this link should be re-verified for both destination validity and correct source markup.
- 12 of 35 total links were probed (23 skipped per click-likelihood ranking); no other broken links reported among the probed set.
- All tracked links route through the Bluecore SafeSend redirector (`trk.bc.footlocker.com/ss/c/...`), consistent with expected redirect wrapping — no open-redirect or malformed-token issues observed in the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **[WARN]** 3 images missing `alt` text:
  - `ho.gif` — `https://trk.bc.footlocker.com/ss/o/u001.../ho.gif` (open-tracking pixel)
  - `o.gif` — `https://mi.footlocker.com/p/up/.../o.gif?mi_u=...&mi_ecmp=suba` (Bluecore open pixel)
  - `fa53ea8871ffa0e2.png` — `https://mi.footlocker.com/p/rp/....png?mi_u=...` (retargeting pixel)
  These are all 1x1 tracking pixels, so the missing `alt` is low-impact, but best practice is `alt=""` (not omitted) to avoid screen readers announcing the raw filename/URL.
- Preheader (hidden `div` before body content) contains an unusually long run of `&#160;&#8204;` (non-breaking space + zero-width non-joiner) sequences used for preheader padding. This is a known technique to push Gmail/Apple Mail's auto-preview snippet past the intended text, but the entity count here is excessive (100+ repetitions) and can cause screen readers to verbalize whitespace entities or bloat the DOM unnecessarily.
- Responsive CSS (`max-width: 600px` media query) is present and structurally standard (flex-image, flex-button, mobile-hide/desktop-hide classes) — no malformed selectors observed in the truncated block.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`, Bluecore `{{...}}` syntax) are present in the visible/truncated HTML. No issues found in the reviewed portion; full-source confirmation would be needed to rule out unrendered tokens further down the template.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN]** `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — may be a relay capture gap (AgentMail) rather than a true absence; needs confirmation against raw SMTP headers.
- **[WARN]** `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe) not detected — if genuinely absent, this email doesn't support one-click unsubscribe, which major mailbox providers (Gmail/Yahoo) increasingly require for bulk senders.
- **[WARN]** `Authentication-Results` header not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unverifiable from available data; also likely a relay-capture limitation rather than confirmed auth failure.
- QA confirms an unsubscribe link is present in the footer (deprioritized/skipped in link probing, but noted as always included) — visible unsubscribe mechanism exists in-body regardless of header status.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
The visible tracked link uses Bluecore's opaque SafeSend token format (`u001.<hash>/4t8/<hash>/h10/h001.<hash>`) rather than plain query-string UTM parameters — this is expected for Bluecore-managed sends, as UTM tagging is typically applied server-side after redirect resolution rather than in the raw href. Cannot confirm final landing-page UTM alignment or destination-page match from the truncated source/QA data; the one link that could be evaluated failed to resolve (see §2), which also blocks continuity verification for that CTA.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Investigate and fix the timed-out link (`u001.udUFY2RF1c3A6JL9Jxc...`) — confirm both that the destination resolves and that the anchor text isn't a leaked fallback string.
2. Add `alt=""` to the three tracking pixels (`ho.gif`, `o.gif`, `fa53ea8871ffa0e2.png`) for cleaner accessibility handling.
3. Trim the preheader's zero-width/nbsp padding to a reasonable length (typically 10–15 repetitions suffice); excessive entities add no benefit and slightly bloat the DOM.
4. Regenerate/attach a non-empty plain-text MIME part — a 0-char text version fails CAN-SPAM's practical readability expectations and hurts deliverability scoring with mailbox providers.
5. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` directly against raw SMTP headers at the sending MTA, since AgentMail relay capture may be dropping them rather than the sender omitting them.
