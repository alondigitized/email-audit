# Homepage snapshot · www.footlocker.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- Foot Locker's mobile homepage is reading as a clearance event first and a sneaker culture brand second — the above-the-fold experience is basically a big orange "Up to 40% Off" banner. That said, the New Balance Grey Days callout in the marquee and the Nike name-drop in the sub-copy tell me they know what's moving right now. The lifestyle shot below the fold is doing more personality work than the hero itself.

## What's working

- **"New Balance Grey Days 2026" marquee** — that's a real event with actual hype. Calling it out by name signals the brand is plugged in, not just posting stock banners.
- **"Save on top styles from New Balance, Nike, and more!" sub-copy** — Nike name is the hook. The second I see Nike I stop scrolling past.
- **Quick-nav category pills** (Men's, Women's, Kids', New Arrivals) — one tap to my section. No buried mega-menu nonsense.
- **Lifestyle shot of young dude in streetwear** — gray crewneck, baggy denim, black beanie. That's my mirror. Not a stock photo vibe.
- **Clean mobile rendering** — header, hero, and nav all load without any obvious jank or layout breaks.

## What's weak

- **No FLX rewards hook anywhere above fold** — I literally plan my cart around FLX double-up weekends. If you're not showing me my points balance or a "members get extra 20% off" badge, I'm not feeling seen.
- **"Up to 40% Off" is doing heavy lifting for a sale that might only be 10% on anything I want** — the "up to" qualifier is a trust killer. Show me 30% off Air Force 1s and I'm in. Show me a vague ceiling and I'm suspicious.
- **No Jordan mention anywhere** — Foot Locker is a key Jordan retailer. Nothing. Not even a category chip.
- **Hero is sale-mode, not drop-mode** — I came here thinking there's new heat. The orange gradient screams clearance event, not limited release. That mismatch hits different when you're a SNKRS-calendar guy.
- **No countdown or urgency signal** — "Shop the Sale" with no deadline means I'll bookmark it and forget.

## Recommendations

- **Add a FLX member strip directly under the hero** — "FLX Members: Extra 10% Off Sale. Check your points →" costs one small banner slot and converts loyalty members immediately.
- **Swap the abstract orange gradient for actual product imagery** — hero showing a pair of Air Max or a Jordan 1 colorway gets me to tap faster than a blurred warm blob. Show the shoe.
- **Add a Jordan-specific chip** to the quick-nav pill row — Men's, Women's, Kids', New Arrivals, **Jordan**. One chip, massive signal.
- **Put a real deadline on the sale** — "Ends Sunday 5/25" turns a browse into a conversion. The urgency doesn't need to be fake, just present.
