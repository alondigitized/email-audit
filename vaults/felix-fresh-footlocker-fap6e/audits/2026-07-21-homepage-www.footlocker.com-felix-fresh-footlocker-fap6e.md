# Homepage snapshot · www.footlocker.com · 2026-07-21
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-21**
## Executive summary

- Okay so the second I landed on this page, I clocked the Jordan 1 Mid immediately — that red/white/black colorway floating on the icy blue background is clean. Foot Locker is running Back to School energy right now and pushing Nike discounts, which is exactly the move mid-July. The overall vibe says "Nike house" with a Jordan focus, which works for me — but the page cuts off right before the offer lands, which is lowkey frustrating.

## What's working

- **Jordan 1 Mid hero image** — Floating on that blue background with the Nike swoosh embossed in the BG? That's art direction that hits. I recognized the shoe in under a second.
- **"Up to 30% Off Nike" offer** — Even half-visible at the fold, the Nike discount is the exact kind of hook that makes me keep scrolling instead of bouncing to SNKRS.
- **Quick-nav tabs (Back to School / Men's / Women's / Kids')** — "Men's" is one tap away. No hunting through mega-menus on mobile.
- **Back to School ticker banner** — Seasonal and actually relevant. Late July is when I'm thinking about gear for the fall semester grind anyway.

## What's weak

- **No FLX rewards or points callout above the fold** — I budget my purchases around FLX double-up weekends. If I don't see my balance or a member perk, I might as well be on StockX. This is table stakes for a returning customer and it's missing.
- **Hero CTA is completely off-screen** — The "Up to 30% Off Nike" copy is cut off and there's no button visible. The most important action on the page is invisible unless I scroll.
- **No raffle or launch teaser** — I check Foot Locker specifically for SNKRS-adjacent launches and raffles. Zero signal above the fold that there's anything hyped incoming.
- **No urgency on the offer** — "Up to 30% Off Nike" with no deadline is just a number. Slap "Ends Sunday" on that and it changes behavior.

## Recommendations

- **Surface the FLX balance/points CTA in the sticky header** — One line: "You have 340 FLX points. Shop now →". I check this constantly and it's a retention hook for every visit.
- **Extend the hero down 40px or bring the CTA button above the fold** — Right now the offer and the button live in scroll purgatory. Even showing the top half of a "Shop the Sale" button would drive tap-through.
- **Add a launch calendar teaser strip below the hero** — Something like "🗓 Upcoming Drops: Air Max Dn8 | July 26" pulls sneakerheads in and differentiates from SNKRS.
- **Add a deadline line to the Nike offer** — "Up to 30% Off Nike — Ends Sunday 7/27" costs nothing to ship and meaningfully lifts urgency.
