# Reserve the Vans Old Skool 36 Souvenir today!
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-18**
## Executive summary

- This is a near carbon-copy of yesterday's 8/17 send — same hero product (Vans Old Skool 36 Souvenir), same reservation-launch template, same "JORDAN LASER PACK" second drop, same back-to-school and Retro 3 storytelling blocks below the fold. The one change to the subject line is a regression: yesterday's "Reserve the Vans Old Skool 36 Souvenir 'Navy' today!" called out the colorway; today's drops "Navy," making the subject slightly less specific and reinforcing the sense that this is a re-send rather than fresh news.
- The render itself is clean and well-structured — big bold hero type, a live countdown timer, an unambiguous "RESERVE NOW" button — and the FLX Rewards membership banner and app-download reservation nudge are smart adds that support urgency. But there's no visible discount, no price on either launch product, and the back half of the email (back-to-school block, Retro 3 block, recommendations grid) feels bolted on rather than building toward the reservation hook up top.
- Given this is essentially the same creative Foot Locker ran 24 hours ago with a slightly weaker subject, this lands as a solid-but-not-improved repeat: strong execution, poor cadence discipline.

## What's working

- Hero hierarchy is immediate: "VANS OLD SKOOL 36 SOUVENIR" headline, full product shot, live countdown ("01 Days 01 Hours 08 Min 48 Sec"), single "RESERVE NOW" CTA — no ambiguity about what to do.
- The dual-drop structure (Vans then Jordan Laser Pack) with matching countdown/CTA treatment keeps the reservation theme consistent through the top half.
- FLX Rewards banner ("Members Get Free Shipping & Returns") and the "Secure Hot Sneakers" app-download reservation callout both reinforce a real, credible loyalty/exclusivity angle.
- No render bugs — every image loads, type is crisp, buttons are legible, nothing overlaps.

## What's weak

- This is functionally the same email as yesterday's top-scoring 8/17 send, just missing the "Navy" colorway detail in the subject — a step backward in specificity, not forward.
- Neither reservation drop shows a price, size run, or availability detail — a sneaker persona deciding whether to reserve needs at least a price point.
- Two competing countdown timers (Vans closes 8/19, Jordan closes 8/21) split urgency instead of reinforcing one clear deadline.
- The back half of the email (back-to-school, Retro 3 "Classic Reborn") is generic lifestyle filler that doesn't connect back to the reservation hook — it reads like a separate email pasted underneath.

## Recommendations

- 1. Don't send the same hero creative two days running with a weakened subject — either escalate the offer (add colorway, add a price/reservation deposit detail) or skip a day. Repeating identical creative trains subscribers to skim past it.
- 2. Add a price or "starting at $X" line under each launch product — a reservation with zero pricing info is a weak click driver.
- 3. Pick one countdown to lead with; demote the second drop below a fold break so the two deadlines don't compete.
- 4. Cut or shorten the back-to-school/Retro 3 storytelling blocks — they dilute the reservation urgency that's supposed to be the whole point of this send.
