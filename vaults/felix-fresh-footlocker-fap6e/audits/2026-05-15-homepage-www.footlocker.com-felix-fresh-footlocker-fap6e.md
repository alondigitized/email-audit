# Homepage snapshot · www.footlocker.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- Foot Locker's leading with a New Balance Grey Days event at the top banner and a "Hoops Lives Here" lifestyle block lower down — so there's sneaker-culture DNA in here, which I respect. But the biggest above-fold promo is a 30% off sale that turns out to be for kids' styles, and I see zero FLX points, zero Jordan drops, zero SNKRS-adjacent urgency. Feels like they forgot who actually opens this app first.

## What's working

- **"New Balance Grey Days 2026" top banner** — this is an actual sneaker-culture event. Someone at FL knows the release calendar. That earns a nod.
- **"HOOPS LIVES HERE" lifestyle block** — the three-person cast looks my age, the energy is right for basketball culture, and it ties sneakers to a lifestyle moment rather than just a discount rack.
- **Men's / Women's / Kids' / New Arrivals tab row** — clean, one-tap access to my category. No friction getting to men's.
- **Search bar immediately below nav** — I know what I want; this lets me get there fast.

## What's weak

- **"Up to 30% Off" promo is for kids** — the headline punches hard, but the sub-copy clarifies "top kids styles." As a 23-year-old men's shopper I mentally bounced the second I read that.
- **Zero FLX hooks** — no rewards balance, no "earn points on this drop," no double-up weekend callout. For someone who literally schedules shopping around FLX events, this is a dead zone.
- **No Jordan or Nike launch content** — if there's anything in the SNKRS calendar right now, FL isn't surfacing it. My competitive cross-check instinct kicks in immediately.
- **Hoops section is cut off** — "HOOPS LIVES HERE" is partially cropped at the bottom of the viewport, so I can't read the CTA or the full play without scrolling. Strong block, weak placement.
- **No urgency anywhere** — no countdown, no "limited pairs," no raffle entry window. For a raffle-lurker like me, urgency is a buy signal. None here.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the kids sale hero for a men's sneaker launch or NB Grey Days product tile** — the Grey Days event is already in the top banner; build on that momentum with an actual men's product above the fold instead of a kids promo.
- **Add an FLX rewards strip** — one thin banner below the nav saying "FLX members earn 3x points this weekend" would make me stop scrolling and think about my cart.
- **Surface a Jordan or Nike raffle/launch CTA** — even a "Enter the AJ1 Raffle — closes Sunday" module would convert someone like me in 2 seconds flat.
- **Pull the Hoops section fully above the fold** — either shorten the kids promo block or reorder so the lifestyle content doesn't require a scroll to resolve.
