# The Jordan Retro 4 'Comic Con' is now here!
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth "Comic Con" beat in the Jordan Retro 4 saga this brand has been running since July 20, and it's the payoff send — "is now here!" replaces the "reserve today" urgency with an actual launch. The comic-book halftone dot background (orange-to-magenta gradient) is the strongest visual treatment this brand has used in the whole recent run, and it actually echoes the shoe's own pink Ben-Day-dot panel, which is a nice bit of design coherence you don't usually get from a retail ESP template.
- Below the hero, though, this reverts to the exact three-block stacked format (Jordan → Hoop Like Her → Air Max 95) seen in prior sends — it's functionally a rerun of the July 22-24 emails with the top block swapped. That's efficient production, not insulting, but it caps how fresh this can feel to a subscriber who's opened four of the last five.
- Overall this is a solid, on-brand launch email with the best hero art of the cycle, dragged down only by an inflated, repetitive body. Good open, good click on the top offer, mediocre on everything after the fold.

## What's working

- The halftone/Ben-Day dot gradient background directly mirrors the shoe's own pink dot panel — genuine visual coherence, not just a generic color block.
- "SHOP JORDAN" CTA button is bold, outlined, and impossible to miss against the gradient.
- The shoe product photography is large, unobstructed, and angled to show the full silhouette including the pink accent panel that makes this colorway distinct.
- "FLX Members get Free Shipping & Returns" is stated twice (top utility bar and bottom banner), reinforcing loyalty value without being pushy.
- No render bugs — clean text, no overlapping elements, no broken images anywhere in the scroll.

## What's weak

- Body content is a rerun of the same three-section structure (Jordan hero → basketball editorial → Air Max 95) already sent July 22-24; a subscriber who's been opening regularly will recognize the skeleton immediately.
- No price or percent-off is shown anywhere in the email — this is a full-price launch push with zero discount signal, which will underperform against the "30% Off" emails in the same rotation for anyone price-motivated.
- "Hoop Like Her" section has no clear single CTA target — "SHOP SIGNATURE BASKETBALL" is generic compared to the specific "SHOP JORDAN" and "SHOP NIKE" copy elsewhere.
- Three unrelated product pushes (Jordan 4, signature basketball, Air Max 95) in one email dilutes the "is now here" launch moment — the email doesn't feel like a single-product event despite the subject promising one.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the Air Max 95 block entirely (or move it to its own send) — this email's subject and hero promise one specific launch, and stacking two more products contradicts that focus and buries the actual news under the fold.
- 2. Add a size-availability or "shop now before it sells out" line near the Jordan CTA — comic/collab colorways sell out fast and that urgency is currently unstated anywhere in the copy.
- 3. Give "Hoop Like Her" a sharper CTA than "SHOP SIGNATURE BASKETBALL" — name a player or shoe the way the Jordan and Nike sections do.
- 4. Test a subject with a size/stock urgency hook instead of a flat announcement.
