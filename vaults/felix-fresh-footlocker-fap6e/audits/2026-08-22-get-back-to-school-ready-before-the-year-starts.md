# Get back-to-school ready before the year starts!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a kitchen-sink send — six distinct hero blocks (back-to-school lifestyle, New Balance sale, running, triple-black essentials, "fresh heat" discount, kids' back-to-school) stacked with no single dominant offer. "SPARK YOUR LOOK" leads, which nails the back-to-school subject line, but by the third module the email has already pivoted to running shoes and triple-black essentials that have nothing to do with school. It reads like six separate campaigns pasted into one template rather than one coherent back-to-school push.
- The one save here is that the actual back-to-school-relevant content — the opening hero and the kids' section near the bottom — is genuinely well composed: clean product shot, readable type, unambiguous CTA. But sandwiching that between running and all-black-everything content dilutes the subject line's promise and forces anyone who's actually shopping for a kid's first-day-of-school fit to scroll past irrelevant modules.
- Verdict: on-brand production quality, off-target content curation. This needed to be edited down to 2-3 back-to-school-specific modules, not six generic ones stitched together under a back-to-school subject.

## What's working

- Opening hero ("SPARK YOUR LOOK") directly matches the subject line with a clean product flat-lay (sneakers, backpack, jacket).
- Kids' back-to-school module near the bottom has an actual lifestyle photo of kids walking to school — the most on-brief image in the whole email.
- Consistent FLX Rewards branding and nav bar at top, single clear CTA button per module throughout.
- No render bugs — every image loads, text is legible, no overlap.

## What's weak

- Five of six modules (New Balance sale, running, triple-black, fresh heat, and even parts of the hero) have zero connection to "back to school," diluting the subject's promise.
- No concrete offer percentage visible anywhere except the small "up to 10%" text under "WEEK OF NEW BALANCE" — everything else says "shop the sale/deal" with no number.
- Email is extremely long (six full hero sections) with no clear single primary CTA — a scanning reader has to choose among six competing "SHOP X" buttons.
- The kids' back-to-school section — arguably the most relevant content to the subject — is buried near the very bottom, after four unrelated modules.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to 2-3 back-to-school-specific modules (hero + kids' + maybe one accessory/backpack push) and drop the New Balance/running/triple-black content into a separate, honestly-subject-lined send.
- 2. Move the kids' back-to-school module up to the #2 slot, right after the hero — it's currently orphaned at the bottom where most readers won't scroll.
- 3. Put an actual number on the hero offer ("Up to 30% off back-to-school essentials") instead of the vague "must-have trends" copy.
- 4. Reorder or trim to give this one unambiguous primary CTA instead of six competing ones.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Back-to-school fits: up to 30% off Nike, adidas & Salomon`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New school year, new kicks — shop back-to-school now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Backpacks, sneakers, and first-day fits for the whole family`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `FLX members shop free shipping on every back-to-school pick`
