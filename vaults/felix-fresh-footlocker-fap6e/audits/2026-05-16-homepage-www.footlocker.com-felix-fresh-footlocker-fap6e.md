# Homepage snapshot · www.footlocker.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Foot Locker's mobile homepage is running two different conversations at once right now — a kids' clearance sale front-and-center and a Jordan/New Balance drop culture teaser right underneath it, and those two things are not the same audience. As a SNKRS lurker who tracks every Grey Days drop, I can see there's something for me here, but I have to work to find it. The brand reads like a general athletic retailer trying to flash streetwear cred while moving units.

## What's working

- **New Balance Grey Days 2026 marquee** — that top announcement bar immediately signals that Foot Locker is tracking the same release calendar I am. That's the right handshake with a sneakerhead.
- **Jordan section below the fold** — I can see the Jumpman logo and what looks like a classic Jordan 3 silhouette with "WORLD'S BEST" on the heel. That image is enough to make me scroll down, full stop.
- **Men's tab first in the nav strip** — "Men's" is the first pill in the horizontal category row, which means I'm one tap from where I actually want to be.
- **Clean mobile layout** — no layout shift, no overlapping elements, search bar is accessible and not buried. Page loaded fast enough that I'm not bouncing for performance reasons.

## What's weak

- **Kids' sale is the hero, not the sneak peak** — "Save on top kids styles" is the first sentence I read in the promo body copy. That's not me. The Jordan drop below the fold is way more relevant but I have to scroll to get there.
- **No FLX hook above the fold** — I am FLX-aware on every cart. If I saw "Double FLX points this weekend" or even my current balance, I'd be in a completely different headspace. It's invisible here.
- **No launch calendar or raffle entry CTA** — Grey Days is in the marquee but there's no "Enter the Raffle" or "Drop Goes Live [date]" urgency attached to it. It's just a banner pointing to a brand shop, not a launch event.
- **"Up to 30% Off" sale reads as clearance** — for a sneakerhead who's cross-shopping SNKRS, a markdown sale on kids gear doesn't generate hype. It makes the homepage feel like a mall store moving old inventory.
- **No social proof** — zero review counts, no "X sold in the last hour," no collab badge or heat indicator on the Jordan below.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the kids' sale hero for a Grey Days drop module** — show the actual NB silhouettes dropping under Grey Days 2026, with a countdown timer and "Notify Me" or "Enter Raffle" CTA. Move the kids' sale to a secondary banner.
- **Surface the FLX rewards widget in the nav area** — a single line like "You have 1,240 FLX points — Double-up ends Sunday" converts loyalists who are already logged in. This is a one-week ship.
- **Give the Jordan section an actual headline** — "WORLD'S BEST" is flavor copy but it doesn't tell me the shoe name, release date, or price. Add a text overlay: `Air Jordan 3 'White Cement' — Shop Now`.
- **Add a launch ticker or carousel to the top marquee** — right now it's rotating (I can see the `< >` arrows) but it's all announcement copy. Include one image thumbnail per upcoming drop so I can see what's coming without tapping through.
