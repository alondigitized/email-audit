# Get up to 40% off - now extended.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- This is Foot Locker's fourth "Big Sale" send in three days, and it's the same red-gradient, oversized-outline-type hero they've been running since 8/8 — just with "EXTENDED" swapped in for "ENDING SOON." The creative formula works (bold, single-focus, high-contrast, easy to read at a glance) and the Dunk hero shoe is a strong visual anchor, but there's a real render bug here: four solid black rectangle blocks sit directly under the CTA button with no visible content, image, or alt text — that reads broken to a recipient's eye.
- The "Picks for You" personalization grid is a nice touch and the strongest structural element, giving concrete product/price/rating data six-deep. But the cadence problem is now unavoidable: this is the same red sale block for the third time this week with only the headline word changed, and a subscriber who opened Friday's "Ending soon" email will feel this is the same email again.
- Net: competent execution of a tired creative with a visible layout bug drags it down. This lands mid-pack — the offer and hero are fine, but nothing here differentiates it from the last two sends, and the black-box glitch actively hurts trust.

## What's working

- Hero is legible at a glance — "BACK-TO-SCHOOL BIG SALE / UP TO 40% OFF / EXTENDED" reads clearly without scrolling.
- Single hero product (Nike Dunk) is well-lit and centered, giving the eye one clear landing spot.
- "Picks for You" grid shows six specific products with price, discount (Reebok Workout at $59.99 from $85), and star ratings — genuine personalization signal.
- FLX Rewards banner (free shipping, no minimum) reinforces loyalty value right after the product grid.

## What's weak

- Four solid black rectangular blocks appear directly below "Exclusions apply" with no visible image or text — this is a clear render bug that looks broken to a recipient.
- This is essentially the same red "Big Sale" hero as 8/8 and 8/9 with only the sub-headline swapped ("Ending soon" → "Extended") — creative fatigue is now visible across three consecutive opens.
- "Extended" as the news hook is weak urgency — it signals the deadline wasn't real the first two times, which undercuts future urgency claims from this brand.
- CTA button ("SHOP THE SALE") is an outlined ghost button on red — lower contrast/visibility than a solid white or black fill would give.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix or remove the four black placeholder blocks under the hero CTA — that's the single biggest issue; a broken-looking image block right below the primary CTA erodes trust before the reader even scrolls.
- 2. Retire the red "Big Sale" hero template for at least one send — three near-identical hero blocks in a week trains subscribers to skip without opening. Rotate to a different color/layout or lead with "Picks for You" instead.
- 3. Drop "Extended" as the headline hook — it reads as "the last two deadlines were fake." Replace with a specific new detail (a category, a brand, a real end-date/time) if this genuinely is a final extension.
- 4. Make the CTA button a solid fill (white or black) instead of an outline — it'll pop harder against the red background and read as more clickable.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns just added to the sale.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Dunks, New Balance, and more — up to 40% off.`
