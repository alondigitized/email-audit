# Homepage snapshot · www.footlocker.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Foot Locker's showing up as a legit sneaker retailer right now — the NB Grey Days 2026 event banner at the top tells me they know what's happening in the sneaker calendar, and the Nike/New Balance name-drop in the hero copy is on target. But the main hero energy is a clearance-sale vibe, not a drop-culture vibe, which honestly feels a little off for how I actually use this site.

## What's working

- **"New Balance Grey Days 2026" top banner** — this is the single best thing on the page for me. NB Grey Days is a real cultural moment and it signals Foot Locker is tracking the sneaker calendar, not just pushing clearance.
- **Brand name-drop in hero subtext** — "New Balance, Nike, and more" is doing work. It immediately tells me the brands I care about are here.
- **Lifestyle model** — young guy, grey sweatshirt, beanie, sitting casual. That's visually my world.
- **Clean tab navigation** — Men's / Women's / Kids' / New Arrivals is one tap to my section, no friction.
- **"Shop the Sale →"** — the arrow CTA is direct and unambiguous. No "Discover the possibilities" energy here.

## What's weak

- **Zero FLX presence above fold** — I'm FLX-aware on every single cart. Not seeing my points balance or even a "FLX Members get extra X%" banner is a meaningful miss. SNKRS shows me my pass status on launch day; Foot Locker should show me my FLX status when I open the app.
- **Sale hero when there's a drop to promote** — NB Grey Days is RIGHT THERE in the top banner, but the main hero is a generic "Up to 40% Off" sale. These two things are fighting each other. The drop should be the hero.
- **No sneaker imagery** — the lifestyle photo shows a guy in a sweatshirt. I can't see any actual shoes. For a sneaker retailer, that's weird.
- **No raffle/drop cadence visible** — no "upcoming drops," no SNKRS-equivalent launch calendar, no "enter to win" module. That's the whole reason I check Foot Locker.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — I need to know if my cart hits the free-ship number before I add stuff. Not seeing it makes me hesitate.

## Recommendations

- **Make the NB Grey Days event the hero** — swap the generic sale banner to a full-width drop-culture hero. Show the silhouette of the colorway dropping, the drop date, and a "Set Reminder" or "Enter Raffle" CTA. That's what puts Foot Locker on par with the SNKRS app.
- **Add a persistent FLX rewards strip** — a thin banner just below the nav showing "You have X FLX points • Double-up weekends every [date]" would hook returning members immediately. Even logged-out users should see "Earn FLX on every purchase."
- **Swap sweatshirt model for a shoe-forward lifestyle shot** — put an actual sneaker in the frame. A close crop of the NB Grey Days colorway on a pair of feet would drive intent.
- **Add a "Launching Soon" or "Drop Calendar" widget** — even a two-row rail with upcoming drops and their dates would differentiate this homepage from a generic e-commerce sale page.
