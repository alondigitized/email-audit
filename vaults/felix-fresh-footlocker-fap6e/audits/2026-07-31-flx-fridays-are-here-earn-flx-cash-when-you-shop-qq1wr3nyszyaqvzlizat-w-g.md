# FLX Fridays are here! Earn FLX Cash when you shop.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-31**
## Executive summary

- This is the loyalty play, not a markdown play — and it's a genuinely different beat than anything else in the recent Foot Locker cadence. FLX Fridays is a spend-tiered cash-back program ($125→$10, $250→$20, $400→$50 FLX Cash), and Foot Locker leads with it clearly: hot-pink "SPEND MORE EARN MORE" banner, a legible three-tier table, and two stacked CTAs ("SHOP NOW" / "JOIN FLX FOR FREE") right up top. That's a strong, unambiguous hero — better structured than the 2026-07-31 Adistar Jellyfish reservation send (6.00/10) that led with a single weak product hero.
- Where it falls apart is everything after the fold. The email pivots hard into a five-section "back to school" scroll (First Day Feels → 40% Off BTS Sale → Trends → Best Foot Forward running → Strapped In Style backpacks) that has nothing to do with FLX Cash and buries the actual subject-line offer under four unrelated CTAs. This is the same problem flagged on 2026-07-30 ("All-black styles are here!") — too many competing concepts in one send — except here it's worse because the subject promises a specific loyalty mechanic and then abandons it after one screen.
- Net: strong top, unfocused body. A subscriber who opens for FLX Cash gets what they came for in the first fold, then is asked to context-switch into a generic BTS sale five separate times.

## What's working

- The three spend tiers are laid out as a clean, scannable table — exact dollar-in/dollar-out math, no vague "earn rewards" language.
- Two CTAs up top serve both existing FLX members ("SHOP NOW") and prospects ("JOIN FLX FOR FREE") without forcing a choice.
- Hot pink FLX branding is consistent and immediately distinguishes this from Foot Locker's usual red/black promo sends.
- Legal/terms line ("Bonus cash issued within 5 days... valid for 30 days... Terms apply") is visible and not hidden in a footer scroll, which supports the "offer feels honest" signal.
- Top nav bar (Mens'/Womens'/Kids'/Sale) plus free shipping/returns line gives the email standard, expected structure.

## What's weak

- Five distinct campaign concepts in one email (FLX Cash, First Day Feels, 40% Off BTS Sale, Trends, Running, Backpacks) — the same overstuffed pattern called out on 2026-07-30's "All-black styles" send (8.00/10 despite the strain).
- The 40% Off BTS banner and its own "SHOP THE SALE" CTA compete directly with the FLX Cash offer for attention, diluting the one thing the subject line promised.
- No FLX Cash callback anywhere below the first fold — a recipient who scrolls past the hero has no reminder of why they opened the email.
- Product photography (PUMA sneakers, Air Max, backpack) is generic lifestyle/product shots with no pricing, sizing, or specific offer attached — pure browse-through filler.
- "First Day Feels" section header has zero connective tissue to FLX Rewards; it reads like a separate email spliced in.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email in half. Ship FLX Fridays as its own send (tiers + CTA + terms) and let the BTS trends/running/backpack sections be a separate, subject-appropriate email — this single change would do more than any copy tweak, since right now two campaigns are fighting for one inbox slot.
- 2. If the BTS sections must stay, insert a recurring "Don't forget: Spend $125+ today, get FLX Cash" sticky reminder banner between sections so the offer isn't abandoned after the first fold.
- 3. Replace the generic "SHOP NOW" top CTA with something that reinforces the mechanic, e.g. "START EARNING" — "Shop Now" doesn't signal the cash-back angle the subject led with.
- 4. Add a countdown or explicit "today only" framing near the tier table — FLX Fridays implies a single-day window, but nothing in the hero visually reinforces urgency beyond the section title.
- 5. Drop the 40% Off banner's competing CTA color/size down a tier visually (smaller button, less saturated red) so it doesn't out-compete the FLX pink hero for eye draw.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Today only: Spend $125, get $10 FLX Cash`
- **Subject Alt B:** `FLX Fridays: up to $50 back on your order`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend more, earn more — FLX Cash stacks up to $50 today.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free to join. Cash back in 5 days. Ends tonight.`

# Technical Email Audit — "FLX Fridays are here!" (Foot Locker)

## 1. Technical Summary
Email is served via Bluecore (`bc.footlocker.com` sender domain, `partner-fonts.bluecore.com` fonts) with Movable Ink personalization/tracking pixels (`mi.footlocker.com`). Core structural issue: missing plain-text MIME part and no captured unsubscribe headers; one CSS selector bug affects mobile column widths.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Tracking infrastructure present and consistent with a Bluecore/Movable Ink stack: `trk.bc.footlocker.com/ss/o/...` (Bluecore open pixel), `mi.footlocker.com/p/up/...?mi_u=...&mi_ecmp=suba` and `mi.footlocker.com/p/rp/fa53ea8871ffa0e2.png` (Movable Ink personalization/render pixels).
- QA probed 12 of 39 total links (27 skipped per click-likelihood ranking methodology); this is expected behavior, not a defect.
- HTML was truncated before the body CTA/link block, so individual CTA `href` values (and their query strings) are not visible in this source — cannot confirm destination URLs or click-tracking wrapper integrity beyond the header/tracking pixels shown.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **CSS selector bug**: in the mobile media query, the `.col-9` rule incorrectly targets `.col-8`:
  `.fluid-row .col-9,.md-fluid-row .col-8 { width: 74.25% !important;}`
  This duplicates the `.md-fluid-row .col-8` selector (already set to `66%` earlier in the same block) instead of declaring `.md-fluid-row .col-9`. Net effect: `.md-fluid-row .col-8` elements render at `74.25%` (last-declared wins on equal specificity) instead of the intended `66%`, and `.md-fluid-row .col-9` elements receive no mobile-width override at all, falling back to desktop width on mobile clients.
- Responsive scaffolding (`viewport` meta, `max-width:600px` media query, `.mobile-hide`/`.desktop-hide`, `.flex-image`, `.flex-button`) is otherwise standard and well-formed.
- Missing `alt` text on 3 images per QA: `ho.gif`, `o.gif`, `fa53ea8871ffa0e2.png` — all three are 1x1 tracking/personalization pixels (Bluecore/Movable Ink), not content images. Low accessibility impact but best practice is `alt=""` to explicitly mark them decorative/non-content for screen readers.
- Preheader (`display:none` div) is padded with an extremely long run of repeated `&#160;&#8204;` (nbsp + zero-width joiner) entities — functions as intended (prevents body text leaking into preview text) but adds significant markup weight to the `<head>`/preview region; a shorter, standard hidden-preheader technique would reduce payload.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{...}}`, `*|...|*`) are present in the visible source.
- Movable Ink personalization parameters (`mi_u`, `mi_ecmp=suba`) are populated with real-looking values, suggesting personalization resolved correctly for this send.
- Full body content (product blocks, CTAs) was truncated in the provided source, so token resolution in the main content area could not be verified.
