# Get up to 40% off New Balance for a limited time only.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-20**
## Executive summary

- This is the same purple-gradient New Balance hero Foot Locker already ran on 8/17 ("Get up to 40% off top New Balance styles!") and again as a mismatched send on 8/18 ("Tax free weekend is here"), now recycled a third time with a matching subject. The template itself is clean and works — big "UP TO 40% OFF," clear single CTA, product grid with real ratings and prices below — but this is a straight rerun, not a new creative, and there's zero urgency or differentiation from the 8/17 send.
- The "Hot Styles" grid is the weak spot: it's titled New Balance but every single product shown is a Vans or adidas sneaker — not one New Balance shoe appears anywhere in the six-item grid. That's a category-tagging miss that undercuts the entire premise of the email.
- Solid execution, stale idea, and a product-grid mismatch that actively confuses the offer. Middle-of-the-pack send.

## What's working

- Hero is unambiguous: "MORE NEW BALANCE STYLES," "UP TO 40% OFF," single "SHOP THE SALE" button, no scrolling needed to get the offer.
- Specific style callouts (1906, Abzorb 2000, track jackets) plus an FLX 3X-points loyalty hook for New Balance purchases.
- Secondary shop-by-category buttons (Shoes/Apparel) give a fast path past the hero.
- Product tiles show real star ratings, review-style specificity (color, size category), and pricing — no lorem-ipsum or broken images.
- Clean top nav and consistent purple/black Foot Locker branding throughout.

## What's weak

- "Hot Styles" section is titled under a New Balance sale but shows Vans and adidas products exclusively — no New Balance shoe is visible anywhere below the hero.
- This is a near-repeat of the 8/17 "Get up to 40% off top New Balance styles!" send — same hero, same purple gradient, same 40% claim, no new angle or escalated offer.
- No deadline or urgency signal despite subject saying "limited time only" — nothing in the body states when the sale ends.
- "Exclusions apply" sits directly under the CTA with no detail on what's excluded, which reads as vague fine print undercutting the offer's honesty.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the "Hot Styles" grid — swap in actual New Balance SKUs (the 1906R, Abzorb 2000, etc. mentioned in the hero copy) so the featured products match the promoted brand. This is the single highest-impact fix; right now the email undermines its own subject line.
- 2. Add a real deadline (date/time) near the CTA to back up "limited time only" — right now it's an empty claim.
- 3. Differentiate this send from the 8/17 near-twin — new hero photography, a different lead style, or an escalated/varied discount tier so subscribers don't feel like they're seeing the same email again.
- 4. Trim or specify the "Exclusions apply" line — link it to a details page rather than leaving it as unexplained fine print.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `FLX members get 3X points on every New Balance buy.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop 1906, Abzorb 2000, track jackets & more before it's gone.`
