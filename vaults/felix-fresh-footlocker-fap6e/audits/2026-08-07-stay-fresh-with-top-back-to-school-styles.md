# Stay fresh with top back-to-school styles.
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-07**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises "top back-to-school styles" but the actual hero — the first thing rendered — is FLX Fridays cash-back tiers, not a single back-to-school product. That's a mismatch a scrolling reader notices immediately: the eye lands on "SPEND MORE EARN MORE" and a rewards ladder before anything resembling a "style" appears. It isn't until the second screen ("BACK-TO-SCHOOL BIG SALE EXTENDED") that the stated theme even shows up, and even then it's a banner, not a hero shot.
- Once you get past the FLX block, the email does deliver on back-to-school: all-white sneakers, kids' outfits, and a backpack, each with its own clean section and clear CTA. This is a fairly typical Foot Locker catalog-scroll structure — three or four themed blocks stacked vertically, each with a bordered white CTA button. Compared to 2026-08-05's Birkenstock send (8.0/10, single clean hero matching the subject), this one buries its subject-line theme under an unrelated loyalty promo first.
- Net: solid back-to-school content once you scroll, but the subject/hero mismatch costs opens and costs the first-impression click. This lands mid-pack.

## What's working

- Three distinct back-to-school sections (all-white sneakers, kids' outfits, backpacks) each with its own bordered CTA button, giving the reader multiple entry points.
- Clean, well-lit lifestyle photography — the crosswalk shot of the two kids in back-to-school outfits is a strong, relatable demographic cue.
- FLX Rewards is reinforced twice (top nav strip and bottom banner) without feeling redundant, and "FREE SHIPPING, NO MINIMUM" is a concrete, visible member benefit.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, buttons are legible, text doesn't overlap.

## What's weak

- The hero space is spent entirely on FLX Fridays cash-back tiers, which has nothing to do with "back-to-school styles" — the subject's promise isn't visually paid off until well into the scroll.
- No actual price/percent-off callout for back-to-school product — "BIG SALE EXTENDED" banner has no number attached to it, just "New Markdowns Added."
- The "FRESH IN ALL-WHITE" section touts sneakers "from Nike, New Balance & more" but the shot only shows one New Balance-style pair, undercutting the "from Nike... & more" promise.
- Three near-identical CTA button treatments (white outline box) in a row reduce visual hierarchy — nothing signals which section is the priority.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap the hero: lead with the back-to-school sale/product imagery first, and move FLX Fridays to a secondary block below it — the subject line sets the expectation and the layout should pay it off first, not on scroll 2.
- 2. Put a real number on the back-to-school offer ("Extra 20% off markdowns" instead of just "New Markdowns Added") — right now there's no size of the deal anywhere in the sale banner.
- 3. Either show multiple brands in the all-white sneaker shot or drop the "from Nike, New Balance & more" line so copy matches image.
- 4. Vary the CTA button style (fill vs. outline, or a color accent) across sections so one clearly reads as the primary action.
