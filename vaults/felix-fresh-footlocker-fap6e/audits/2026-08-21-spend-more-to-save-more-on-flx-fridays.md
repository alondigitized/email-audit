# Spend more to save more on FLX Fridays!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-21**
## Executive summary

- This is Foot Locker's FLX loyalty/spend-tier promo, and it's a clean, legible execution — the hero is unambiguous: "SPEND MORE EARN MORE" in hot pink on a diagonal-striped background, with a clear tiered breakdown ($125→$10, $250→$20, $400→$50 FLX Cash). The offer mechanic is genuinely well laid out, which is rare for tiered spend promos that usually bury the math in fine print.
- Where it falls apart is everything after the hero. This single email tries to be six emails at once — FLX Fridays spend-tiers, Jordan "Laser" reservation, New Balance sale, running energy, free gift with purchase, "fresh heat," and triple-black essentials — with zero narrative thread connecting them. The subject promises FLX Fridays spend-and-save, but a scrolling reader hits five unrelated campaigns before reaching the bottom, which dilutes the one clear, well-built offer at the top. Compare to the 8/17 Vans send that scored 8/10 for hero-to-subject match: this one nails the hero but then abandons the subject's promise entirely for the rest of the body.
- Verdict: strong top, unfocused everything else. This reads as a "stuff every current promo into Friday's send" email rather than a purpose-built FLX Fridays email.

## What's working

- Tiered offer table (spend $125/$250/$400 → get $10/$20/$50 FLX Cash) is genuinely easy to parse at a glance.
- Hero color-blocking (magenta on black diagonal stripes) creates strong contrast and is immediately eye-catching.
- Two clear CTA buttons in the hero ("SHOP NOW" and "JOIN FLX FOR FREE") cover both existing and prospective members.
- Terms/expiration disclosure ("valid within a five day period... valid for 30 days") is visible in-body, not hidden in a footer wall of text.
- FLX Rewards banner near the footer reinforces free shipping as a member perk.

## What's weak

- Subject says "FLX Fridays" but 80% of the email body (Jordan reservations, New Balance, running, gift-with-purchase, triple-black) has nothing to do with FLX Fridays spend tiers.
- No product imagery tied to the actual FLX Fridays offer — the hero is text/graphic only, so there's no sense of what you'd actually buy to hit $125.
- The "UNMISTAKABLE ENERGY" running section has upside-down/mirror-flipped shoe product shots, which reads as a render error even if intentional.
- Six distinct campaign sections stacked back-to-back with no visual pacing — it's a fatigue scroll, not a curated read.
- This is the fourth New Balance banner variant this week (8/17, 8/18 twice, now 8/21) with the same purple/gradient treatment — pure repetition per the brand history.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to two sections max: FLX Fridays hero + one supporting "what to buy" product carousel that actually hits the spend tiers. Everything else (Jordan, NB, running, gift, triple-black) belongs in separate sends or a "more from Foot Locker" footer strip, not full-width banners.
- 2. Add product imagery under the hero showing 3-4 items near the $125 threshold — right now there's no visual anchor for what "spend $125" buys.
- 3. Fix or re-shoot the upside-down running shoe images in the "UNMISTAKABLE ENERGY" section — it looks broken regardless of intent.
- 4. Replace the repeated New Balance purple-gradient banner with new creative — it's now appeared 4x in 5 days per brand history and is clearly stale.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your FLX Fridays bonus cash is live`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack up to $50 in bonus cash on today's purchase.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Members: bigger baskets, bigger bonus cash.`
