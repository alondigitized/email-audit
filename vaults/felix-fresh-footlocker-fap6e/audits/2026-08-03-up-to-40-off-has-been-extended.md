# Up to 40% off has been extended!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-03**
## Executive summary

- This is Foot Locker doing the oldest trick in the markdown playbook: manufacture urgency, then quietly extend it. On 2026-08-02 subscribers got "⏰ SALE STOPS TODAY ⏰" and "LAST CHANCE... ENDS TODAY" — twice in one day. Now, one day later, the same "Up to 40% Off" sale is back with "SALE EXTENDED" stamped across the hero. Anyone who opened both emails now knows the "ends today" deadline was fake, which quietly erodes the urgency lever for every future FL send.
- Visually this is a clean, on-brand execution — the black/red BTS hero with the Jordan 9 is bold, the "SHOP FOR THE FULL FAMILY" category block is a nice added utility layer, and the Top Recommendations grid with star ratings gives real product signal. But the offer itself is the same "Up to 40% off" markdown Foot Locker has been running since 2026-07-28, just re-skinned. There's no new product story here (the Jordan 9 hero is generic BTS art, not a specific launch like the Adistar Jellyfish sends).
- Net: solid production values, weak news value. A subscriber who's been paying attention will read "extended" as "we lied about the deadline," which is a worse outcome than just not having urgency copy at all.

## What's working

- Hero hierarchy is immediate — "BACK-TO-SCHOOL BIG SALE" and the Jordan 9 dominate the first screen with no scrolling needed.
- "SHOP SALE FOR THE FULL FAMILY" with dedicated Men's/Women's/Kids'/All buttons is genuinely useful navigation, not just decoration.
- Top Recommendations grid shows real prices and star ratings (4.6, 4.3, 5.0, 4.0) across 6 varied products — sneakers, apparel, sandals — giving concrete reasons to click beyond the generic sale.
- FLX Rewards banner ("FREE SHIPPING, NO MINIMUM") is loud and clearly placed, reinforcing loyalty value.
- Clean render throughout — no broken images, no overlapping text, no lorem ipsum.

## What's weak

- "SALE EXTENDED" directly contradicts the "ENDS TODAY" urgency Foot Locker pushed twice on 2026-08-02 — this is the kind of whiplash that trains subscribers to ignore future deadlines.
- The offer itself is stale: "Up to 40% off" is the same markdown beat running since 2026-07-28, just re-wrapped in BTS branding with no new hero product or launch story.
- "Exclusions Apply" sits directly under the CTA button — small, but it's the one piece of fine print visible right where the eye lands after the button.
- The hero CTA ("SHOP THE SALE") routes to a generic sale page, not a specific product, despite showing one specific shoe (Jordan 9) as hero art — a missed link between image and destination.
- No specific deadline given for the "extended" sale, so the one piece of urgency copy in the email is actually the least credible thing in it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop "extended" framing entirely, or replace fake urgency with a real one. If the sale is genuinely still running, give it a hard new end date and say so — don't reuse "ends today" language that already burned trust one day earlier.
- 2. Make the hero CTA product-specific. If the Jordan 9 is the hero image, link "SHOP THE SALE" to that shoe (or a "shop this style" secondary CTA) instead of a generic category page.
- 3. Move "Exclusions Apply" further from the CTA, or make it link to a details page rather than floating as loose fine print under the button.
- 4. Rotate the hero product — sneakerheads on this list have now seen "40% off" markdown copy for a week straight; a genuinely new item (like the Adistar Jellyfish launches) would break the fatigue.
