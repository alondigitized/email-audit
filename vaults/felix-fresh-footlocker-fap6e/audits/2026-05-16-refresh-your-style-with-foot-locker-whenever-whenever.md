# Refresh your style with Foot Locker whenever, whenever!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a browse/discovery promotional email dressed in lifestyle clothing but arriving naked — no discount, no exclusive, no reason to shop today rather than next week. Foot Locker's design team did the work: clean product grid, star ratings on individual items, and a thoughtful size-filter section. But the subject line has an apparent copy error ("whenever, whenever!" instead of any coherent phrase), there's no concrete offer anywhere in the body, and four competing CTAs fight for attention with no clear winner. The hero is handsome and the brand renders flawlessly — this is a well-built engine with no fuel in the tank.
- The deeper problem: this email lands the same day as the welcome send ("Thanks for joining the Foot Locker fam! 🙌🏽" audited earlier today). Hitting a fresh subscriber with a promotional browse email hours after the welcome email is an aggressive cadence choice that burns goodwill before the relationship has been established.
- Nothing in this email answers "why should I click today?"

## What's working

- Product grid shows real, named products — HOKA Bondi 9, adidas Columbia, New Balance — with visible star ratings under each tile, which adds credibility and browsability
- "SHOP SHOES IN YOUR SIZE" section with Men's/Women's/Kids' tabs is a smart segmentation signal that earns attention
- FLX Rewards section is clearly laid out with a low-friction "JOIN FOR FREE" CTA
- Hero lifestyle photography is polished, on-brand, and renders cleanly with no broken images or layout artifacts
- No render bugs anywhere in the email — solid production quality

## What's weak

- Subject line "whenever, whenever!" reads like a copy-paste QA miss — it's the first thing the subscriber sees and it undermines credibility immediately
- No concrete offer anywhere in the email — no % off, no dollar threshold, no free shipping, nothing actionable
- Four CTAs (SEE WHAT'S NEW, SHOP CLASSICS, DOWNLOAD THE APP, JOIN FOR FREE) compete at equal visual weight; the hero section has no CTA of its own
- Second Foot Locker email today on a subscriber who just got the welcome email — cadence is hostile for a new relationship
- Prices are not visible in the product grid, removing any purchase-readiness signal

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the subject line first** — "whenever, whenever!" is a credibility hole before the email opens; it should have caught in any copy review pass.
- 2. **Add one concrete offer to the hero** — even "Free shipping on orders $75+" gives a reason to act today vs. later.
- 3. **Pick one primary CTA and make it the hero's button** — the SEE WHAT'S NEW or SHOP NEW ARRIVALS button belongs on the hero image itself, not buried below.
- 4. **Suppress this email for same-day welcome recipients** — a 24–48 hour delay after the welcome send is the minimum for brand safety.
- 5. **Show prices in the product grid** — even a "from $XX" range creates urgency and helps buyers self-qualify.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fresh styles for men, women, and kids — shop the latest now`

### 5. Compliance

**[WARN] List-Unsubscribe header not captured**
The AgentMail relay did not surface a `List-Unsubscribe` header. If absent at send time (not just relay-invisible), this violates Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender requirements (enforced since February 2024 for senders >5K/day). Foot Locker at this volume almost certainly sends this header — likely a relay capture gap, but must be confirmed against raw SMTP headers.

**[WARN] List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found (RFC 8058)**
One-click unsubscribe (`List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`) is required for bulk senders under Google's 2024 mandate. Same caveat — may be relay-invisible, but the absence must be explicitly verified.

**[FAIL] No plain-text alternative part**
QA reports `0 chars` for the text version. A missing multipart/alternative text part:
- Increases spam score across multiple filters (SpamAssassin, Postini)
- Violates CAN-SPAM best practices (though not strictly legally required)
- Causes some corporate mail clients to reject or quarantine the message

**[WARN] SPF/DKIM/DMARC authentication status unknown**
`Authentication-Results` header not captured by relay. `bc.footlocker.com` is a Bluecore sending domain and should have SPF + DKIM alignment. Cannot confirm without raw header access.
