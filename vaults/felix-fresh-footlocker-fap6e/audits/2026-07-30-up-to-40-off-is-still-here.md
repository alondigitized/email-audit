# Up to 40% Off is Still Here 😱
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-30**
## Executive summary

- This is Foot Locker leaning on the exact "Save Now: Up to 40% Off" markdown beat it already ran on 2026-07-28 (9.00/10 that day), just re-titled "Still Here" to signal the sale is aging out. The hero is bold and legible — "BIG BRANDS BIGGER SAVINGS" reads clearly at a glance, the CTA is unmissable, and FLX member benefits are surfaced twice. But this send has a real production defect: four black, content-less boxes render directly under the "Exclusions apply" line in the hero, which reads as either a failed image load or an unfinished creative element sitting in a live send.
- Compared to the brand's recent single-hero "reservation calendar" sends (Jordan Retro 1, adidas Adistar Jellyfish — both 8-9/10), this is a step down in specificity: no named product, no colorway, no launch date, just a generic sitewide percentage-off banner dressed in back-to-school red. It's competent execution wrapped around a lazy offer, undercut by a visible rendering bug.
- Judgement: solid craft, weak idea, broken execution in one visible spot. This is a mid-pack send that the black-box glitch alone should have caught in QA before it went out.

## What's working

- Hero typography ("BIG BRANDS BIGGER SAVINGS") is bold, high-contrast, and immediately readable without scrolling.
- "SHOP THE SALE" CTA is a clear, outlined button that stands out against the red background.
- "Picks for You" product grid shows real prices, colorways, and star ratings (e.g., Under Armour HG leggings at 4.7 stars) — concrete, scannable detail.
- FLX Rewards free-shipping/no-minimum banner is repeated near the top and again above the footer, reinforcing the loyalty hook for members.
- Category shortcuts (Footwear / Apparel / Accessories / All Sale) give a fast path past the generic hero into a relevant department.

## What's weak

- Four solid black rectangles render below "Exclusions apply" in the hero — a visible, unresolved image/asset failure that makes the email look broken on open.
- The offer is the same undifferentiated "up to 40% on Nike, New Balance & more" sitewide markdown the brand has run repeatedly in the last week (7/26, 7/27, 7/28 x2) — no new hook beyond "Still Here."
- No specific deadline anywhere in the visible creative — "Still Here" implies urgency without ever stating when the sale actually ends.
- "Picks for You" mixes men's, boys', and generic apparel/footwear with no clear single-persona targeting, diluting relevance.
- Subject's urgency ("Still Here") is vague continuation copy rather than a fresh, standalone reason to open.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the four black boxes under the hero before anything else ships — this is a visible defect that will read as broken to every recipient, regardless of how good the rest of the design is.
- 2. Replace generic "Still Here" framing with a hard, credible deadline (e.g., "Ends Sunday") to convert vague urgency into real urgency — the brand's best-performing recent sends (Jordan Retro 1, 8-9/10) all used explicit reservation/deadline language.
- 3. Anchor the hero on one specific hero product or category instead of "Nike, New Balance & more" — even swapping in the top "Picks for You" sneaker would sharpen the offer.
- 4. Vary the creative from the near-identical 7/28 "Save Now: Up to 40% Off" send — reusing the same percentage and brand list back-to-back reads as recycled, not refreshed.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last Call: Up to 40% Off Ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Nike & New Balance Picks Are Still 40% Off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop before back-to-school stock runs out.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `FLX members ship free — no minimum.`
