# Last day for our Back to School Big Sale
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-09**
## Executive summary

- This is Foot Locker closing out the same "Big Sale" creative they ran on 8/8 ("Ending soon: Back to School Big Sale," 7/10), and it lands in the same territory — a bold, single-focus hero with a real urgency hook that's actually earned this time: "Final Day to Save" on the literal last day of the sale isn't manufactured urgency, it's true. The red-to-black gradient with the sneaker lineup (New Balance, Nike Dunk, and an On running shoe) is clean and on-brand, and "up to 40% off" plus "Shop the Sale" gives a reader exactly one thing to do above the fold.
- Where it loses points is everything after the hero. "Shop Sale by Brand" and "Based on Your Style" are two more scroll-stopping sections competing for attention, and the personalized product grid — Air Max 95, Vans Old Skool, Saucony, boys' sneakers, a Nike Knicks tee, Timberland boat shoes — reads like a generic catalog dump rather than anything tied to back-to-school or "your style." It's not as scattered as the four-section "Find Your Style" send from earlier today (5/10), but it's not as disciplined as 8/8's version either.
- Judgement: solid, honest urgency hook undercut by a bloated back half. This is a 7 — same tier as the other "Big Sale" sends this week, not better or worse.

## What's working

- "FINAL DAY TO SAVE" is bold, unambiguous, and true — today genuinely is the last day, so the urgency isn't manufactured.
- Single hero CTA ("Shop the Sale") with a concrete offer ("up to 40% off") sits clearly above the fold with no competing button.
- "Exclusions apply" is small and honest rather than buried in illegible fine print.
- FLX Rewards free shipping/returns messaging appears twice (top banner and bottom band), reinforcing loyalty value without being pushy.
- Product grid shows real specifics — price, color, star rating — which supports a click once a reader gets that far.

## What's weak

- No back-to-school visual cue anywhere in the hero — no backpacks, kids, classroom styling — despite the subject and header text leading with "Back-to-School."
- "Shop Sale by Brand" and "Based on Your Style" stack three distinct sections after the hero, diluting the single-minded urgency the top of the email establishes.
- The "Based on Your Style" grid is generic (a men's tee, boys' preschool shoes, boat shoes) and doesn't read as personalized despite the header claiming it is.
- Truncated product names ("Nike Air Max 95 ...", "Nike Knicks Fina...") look sloppy in a render this polished.
- This is Foot Locker's third "Big Sale" urgency beat in three days (8/8 "Ending soon," now "Final Day") — repetition is starting to blunt the deadline's credibility.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut "Shop Sale by Brand" or fold it into a single row beneath the hero — the email doesn't need three separate sections between the hero and the footer on a last-day urgency send. Focus wins urgency emails; browsing options undercut them.
- 2. Add one small back-to-school visual anchor in the hero (a backpack, a locker graphic, kids in the lineup) so the subject's promise shows up in the first thing a reader sees.
- 3. Rename "Based on Your Style" to something honest like "Trending Now" unless the grid is actually personalized — the mismatch between claim and content erodes trust over repeated sends.
- 4. Fix truncated product titles so nothing renders with a trailing "...".
- **Subject Alt A:** `Final day: up to 40% off back-to-school styles`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Sale ends tonight — up to 40% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop New Balance, Nike, Jordan & adidas before it's gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `FLX members get free shipping, no minimum, all day today.`
