# Reserve the Jordan Retro 1 and the adidas adistar Jellyfish today!
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-29**
## Executive summary

- This is the same "reservation calendar" format Foot Locker ran on 2026-07-28 (9.00/10) — four named launches (Nike Air Zoom Flight "Glove," adidas Adistar Jellyfish, Jordan Retro 1 "Love of the Game," and a fourth Nike GT/ST Charge) stacked with individual countdown clocks and "RESERVE NOW" CTAs. The bones are strong: bold black-and-white typography, one hero product per section, clean product photography, hard deadlines (7/30–7/31 at 4PM ET).
- The execution has a real flaw this time: every single countdown timer across all four products reads "00 Days 00 Hours 00 Minutes 00 Seconds." That's not a minor nit — a countdown showing zero looks either broken or expired, which directly undercuts the "reserve before it's gone" urgency the whole email is built around. The subject also mismatches the visual hierarchy: it calls out the Jordan Retro 1 and adidas Adistar Jellyfish specifically, but the email leads with Nike Air Zoom Flight "Glove" first — so the two products promised in the subject aren't what the reader sees first.
- Solid format, undermined by a broken-looking urgency mechanic and a subject/hero mismatch. Call it a 9-out-of-10 concept executed at a 7.

## What's working

- Four distinct, named hero products with colorway callouts ("Glove," "Love of the Game") — no generic sitewide filler.
- Consistent, unambiguous "RESERVE NOW" CTA repeated after every hero, so there's no confusion about the action.
- Hard reservation deadlines are printed in plain text (7/30, 7/31 at 4PM ET) independent of the broken countdown graphics.
- FLX Rewards membership benefits (free shipping, no minimum) are visible twice — top banner and bottom footer.
- "Picks for You" carousel at the bottom adds a personalization layer with star ratings and visible sale pricing (e.g., $61.60 vs $77).

## What's weak

- All four countdown timers display "00 00 00 00" — this reads as broken or already-expired, which is a real render bug that damages trust in the urgency claim.
- Subject promises Jordan Retro 1 + adidas Adistar Jellyfish, but the hero position (top of email, largest treatment) goes to Nike Air Zoom Flight "Glove" instead — the two products named in the subject are buried second and third.
- No discount/price signal anywhere in the hero content — all four launches are reservation-only, so shoppers scanning for "% off" get nothing until the unrelated Picks for You carousel far below.
- Subject line runs ~68 characters, well past mobile-friendly length, so it likely truncates on phone inboxes before naming both products.
- Third reservation-themed send in four days (following 2026-07-26 and 2026-07-28) risks fatigue — the format is starting to repeat rather than feel like a fresh drop.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the countdown timers first.** A static "00 00 00 00" is worse than no countdown at all — it looks like the launch already ended. This is the single biggest lift available here since it directly touches the credibility of the CTA.
- 2. **Match hero order to subject line.** If the subject leads with Jordan Retro 1 and adidas Adistar Jellyfish, put those two products first in the layout — right now Nike Air Zoom Flight gets the prime real estate the subject didn't promise.
- 3. **Shorten the subject and pick one hero product to name**, letting the second product live in the preview text instead of cramming both into the subject.
- 4. **Add a loyalty or reservation-priority hook to the subject/preview** (e.g., "FLX members reserve first") to differentiate this send from the two prior reservation emails this week.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Reserve the Jordan Retro 1 'Love of the Game' before 7/31`
- **Subject Alt B:** `4 launches, 1 reservation window: Jordan, adidas & more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus adidas Adistar Jellyfish, Nike Air Zoom Flight & GT Charge — all close this week.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `FLX members: reserve now, free shipping on every pair.`
