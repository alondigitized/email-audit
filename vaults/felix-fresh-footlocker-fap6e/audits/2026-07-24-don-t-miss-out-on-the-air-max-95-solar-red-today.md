# Don't miss out on the Air Max 95 'Solar Red' today!
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-24**
## Executive summary

- The Air Max 95 'Solar Red' hero is the sharpest single-product lead Foot Locker has served this week. A specific named colorway in the subject, a bold red shoe render that fills the hero panel, and a "today!" urgency that actually maps to a real drop — this is a competent product-launch email on first impression.
- Everything after the fold undercuts it. What could have been a tight, one-product drop alert immediately fragments into four separate sections: "Hoop Like Her" (already the editorial centerpiece of yesterday's July 23 send "Top signature sneakers from Sabrina, A'ja & more."), a Jordan Retro 3 "Triple Brand" block, and an Adidas Hyperboosd Euphoria section tacked on at the bottom. The urgency the subject creates — "today!" — dissolves into a weekly-digest structure that asks the reader to care about four unrelated products at once.
- This is also the second Foot Locker send on July 24, on top of five others since July 19. The Air Max 95 creative is genuinely good. The surrounding decisions work against it.

## What's working

- Air Max 95 Solar Red shoe render is bold and immediately readable — the colorway reads as "Solar Red" at a glance without needing the text overlay
- Subject "Don't miss out on the Air Max 95 'Solar Red' today!" is the most product-precise and colorway-specific subject in the recent rotation — a real upgrade over July 24's other send ("Super Hot Deals on Nike & Jordan!")
- FL Unlocked banner persists at top, keeping membership front of mind before the hero
- Clean render throughout — no broken images, no layout breakage, no overlapping text visible
- "SHOP SIGNATURE HOOP" CTA in the Hoop Like Her section is actually the correct verb+noun pattern; the primary hero CTA should learn from it

## What's weak

- "Hoop Like Her" block is lifted directly from yesterday's July 23 editorial email — any subscriber who opened both sees identical content in back-to-back sends
- Primary hero CTA "SHOP NOW" is generic — "SHOP AIR MAX 95" would cost nothing and reinforce the drop before the click
- No visible price, discount, or member pricing anywhere in the email — "don't miss out" without a number is pressure without payoff
- Four-product structure fully dissolves the "today!" urgency by the time the reader hits Adidas; the email can't decide if it's a drop alert or a browse digest
- Second email of the same calendar day from the same sender — cadence fatigue is compounding across the week

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut to one product.** Run the Air Max 95 Solar Red as a standalone drop email: full-bleed hero, one value line (price, member price, or "limited sizes"), one CTA. "Hoop Like Her" already had its send.
- 2. **Rewrite the hero CTA** from "SHOP NOW" to "SHOP AIR MAX 95" — verb + noun, reinforces the colorway, specific.
- 3. **Add a member-exclusive hook** — if FL Unlocked members get early access or a sizing priority, put that in the hero copy. That's the one value prop that makes "today!" feel earned rather than boilerplate.
- 4. **Surface a visible deadline or sizing signal** — "Sizes going fast" or a "Today only" badge in the hero image anchors the urgency the subject promises.
- 5. **Hold the Adidas block** — running a competitor product in the same email as your hero Nike colorway drop confuses the send's reason for being.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Air Max 95 'Solar Red' — FL Unlocked members shop first`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The Solar Red Air Max 95 just dropped. Sizes going fast.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Members: exclusive early access before it hits the floor.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Limited colorway. One day. Shop the Air Max 95 Solar Red now.`
