# Reserve the Jordan Retro 8 'Chrome' today!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-24**
## Executive summary

- This is Foot Locker's cleanest launch send in the recent run — a proper reservation drop for the Jordan Retro 8 "Chrome," with a live countdown clock and a single dominant hero above the fold. It's a genuine change of pace from the New Balance purple-gradient hero that's now run four times this week (8/17, 8/18, 8/20, 8/22-ish) and the six-block kitchen-sink send from 8/22.
- The problem is that after the strong hero, this becomes another kitchen-sink email — Ja 4 running shoe, back-to-school essentials, and Hoka Speedgoat 2 all stacked underneath with zero connection to the Jordan reservation that got you to open. This isn't as bad as the 8/22 six-block send, but it's the same instinct: one email, four unrelated offers, no single reason to keep scrolling once you've clicked (or not clicked) Reserve Now.
- The core offer — reserve a sneaker before a launch — is legitimate urgency (a real countdown, a real deadline) rather than the "SALE ENDS TODAY" recycled-purple move from 8/23. That's a step up in honesty. Score this one solidly above the brand's recent average, driven almost entirely by the top block.

## What's working

- Live countdown timer (04 Days 01 Hours 11 Minutes 37 Seconds) creates credible, specific urgency tied to a real deadline ("Reservations close 8/28 at 4PM ET").
- Single, unambiguous hero: "JORDAN RETRO 8" in huge bold type, product shot of the actual shoe, colorway named ('Chrome'), one clear "RESERVE NOW" button.
- Clean black hero background makes the white typography and shoe product shot pop — best visual hierarchy of any recent Foot Locker send reviewed.
- Sneaker silhouette is sharp and unobstructed, no render bugs, no overlapping text.

## What's weak

- Three unrelated product blocks (Ja 4, back-to-school, Hoka Speedgoat 2) dilute the launch focus after the strong hero — this is a reservation email that turns into a general catalog blast.
- "SECURE HOT SNEAKERS" app-download block interrupts the flow immediately after the primary CTA, competing with Reserve Now rather than reinforcing it.
- No FLX loyalty tie-in near the Jordan hero itself (loyalty banner only appears at the very bottom, disconnected from the launch offer).
- Subject promises one product; a scrolling reader gets three additional pitches with no thematic thread (basketball, back-to-school, lifestyle) — feels like slots to fill rather than a curated drop email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the Ja 4 / back-to-school / Hoka blocks entirely (or move to a separate send) — let this be a dedicated one-product reservation email; that focus is the whole reason it outperforms the brand's recent multi-block sends.
- 2. Move the FLX Rewards line ("FLX Members get Free Shipping & Returns") up near the Reserve Now button — tie loyalty perks directly to the launch action instead of burying it in the footer.
- 3. Drop or shrink the "Secure Hot Sneakers" app-download block, or move it after all reservation content so it doesn't split attention from the single CTA.
- 4. Add a line of product context under the hero (retail price, sizes available at launch) — persona cares about fit/availability and currently sees zero product detail beyond the name.
