# Reserve the Jordan Retro 1 'Love of the Game' today.
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-26**
## Executive summary

- This is a strong single-hero send built around the Jordan Retro 1 "Love of the Game" — a specific colorway, an emotional hook ("Inspired by the GOAT's farewell letter to basketball"), and a hard reservation deadline ("Reservations close 7/31 at 4PM ET"). The hero panel does everything a Jordan drop email should: bold two-line product name, atmospheric court-side product shot, one clear "RESERVE NOW" button, no scroll needed to get the offer.
- Where it loses points is everything after the hero. The email stacks four more full-width product blocks (New Balance ABZORB 2010, Nike Air Max 95 Solar Red, and a Kids' Back to School push) with zero visual pause between them — it reads as one long undifferentiated feed rather than a hero-plus-supporting-picks layout. Compared to the July 24 "Solar Red" solo send that scored 9/10 on the strength of a single focused CTA, burying that same shoe as block #3 here dilutes its impact.
- Net: a genuinely well-executed hero (on par with the Comic Con and Solar Red launch sends), dragged down by an unfocused, kitchen-sink body that will cost clicks on the actual named product in the subject line.

## What's working

- Hero product name is large, bold, and matches the subject line exactly — no ambiguity about what this email is for.
- The "GOAT's farewell letter to basketball" line gives real narrative hook instead of generic hype copy.
- A specific, credible deadline ("Reservations close 7/31 at 4PM ET") creates real urgency without feeling manufactured.
- "RESERVE NOW" is a precise, unambiguous CTA — not a vague "Shop Now."
- Product photography is clean and on-brand (court background for a basketball launch shoe).

## What's weak

- Four back-to-back product blocks after the hero (New Balance, Nike Air Max, Kids' BTS) compete with and dilute the Jordan launch the subject promised.
- No social proof, sizing info, or "limited quantities" style detail near the hero to reinforce urgency beyond the date/time.
- The Kids' Back to School block is a jarring category jump in a Jordan-launch email — it belongs in a separate send.
- Visual hierarchy resets with every color-block section (brown, red, teal) — each looks like its own email, so nothing besides the top hero reads as "primary."
- No loyalty/FLX-specific benefit tied to this specific reservation (top banner mentions free shipping generically, not reservation perks).

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to a true single-product reservation email — move New Balance, Air Max, and Kids' BTS into separate sends. A dedicated Jordan Retro 1 send would likely match the 9/10 performance of the July 24 Solar Red and July 25 Comic Con drops.
- 2. If secondary products must stay, demote them below a clear "More drops this week" divider so they don't compete visually with the hero.
- 3. Add a scarcity/proof detail near the hero — "Limited pairs available" or size-range callout — to back up the urgency already set by the deadline.
- 4. Tie the reservation to FLX member benefit explicitly ("FLX members reserve first") since the top nav already flags rewards.
