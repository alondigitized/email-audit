# Reserve the Jordan Retro 3 'Sports Renaissance' today!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a near-identical twin of the Nike Ja 4/Kobe V/Jordan Retro 3 multi-drop email already audited today at 6.00/10, just re-cut to lead with the Jordan Retro 3 "Sports Renaissance" alone. The hero — "A CLASSIC REBORN," a single clean product shot, one countdown deadline (8/14 at 4PM ET), one "Reserve Now" button — is the strongest, most focused hero Foot Locker has run in this brand history so far, and it matches the subject line exactly. That's a real improvement over the scattered, subject-mismatched sends flagged on 8/10.
- Where it falls apart is everything below the fold: immediately after the reservation hero comes a "50% OFF BACK-TO-SCHOOL BIG SALE" banner (the same creative running since 8/8), then "Bring the Energy," then HOKA Clifton 11, then a backpack module. That's four unrelated offers stacked under a launch-reservation subject line — the classic Foot Locker pattern this history keeps calling out. A subscriber who opens for the Jordan reservation gets one clean screen, then a wall of disconnected sale/lifestyle noise.
- Judgment: solid, on-brand hero; wasted potential because the email doesn't stay in its lane after the first fold.

## What's working

- Hero headline "A CLASSIC REBORN" and subcopy name the exact shoe and deadline (8/14, 4PM ET), matching the subject precisely.
- Single hero product shot, no clutter — the eye lands on the sneaker immediately.
- "RESERVE NOW" is a clear, unambiguous, verb-forward CTA with visible button.
- No render bugs — clean gradient, sharp product photography, buttons render correctly.
- FLX Rewards nav bar and free-shipping line at top add a credible loyalty signal.

## What's weak

- Four more offers (Big Sale, Bring the Energy, HOKA Clifton 11, backpacks) crowd in right after the reservation CTA, diluting the single-purpose promise of the subject line.
- The "UP TO 50% OFF" sale banner uses near-identical creative to the 8/8–8/10 sends already flagged as "showing wear" — repeated creative fatigue continues.
- No reservation-specific urgency reinforcement (e.g., "only X pairs," size-run preview) beyond the date/time — feels generic for a hyped retro drop.
- Email is long for a single-intent subject line; a shopper interested only in the Jordan has to scroll past unrelated categories to reach the footer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the Jordan Retro 3 hero + reservation CTA and one supporting module (e.g., "why this drop matters" or size availability) — stop bundling four unrelated sale sections under a launch-specific subject.
- 2. If Foot Locker insists on cross-selling, move the Big Sale/Energy/HOKA/backpack blocks to a separate send — this brand history already shows those get their own dedicated emails.
- 3. Add a sizes/availability teaser near the CTA (e.g., "Limited sizes — GS and Men's available") to raise perceived scarcity for a retro drop.
- 4. Retire the red Big Sale banner creative — it's now run unchanged across four consecutive days per this history.
