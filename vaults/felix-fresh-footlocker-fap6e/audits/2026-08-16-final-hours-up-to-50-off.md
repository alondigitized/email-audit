# FINAL HOURS: up to 50% off!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-16**
## Executive summary

- Foot Locker runs the identical red "BIG SALE" hero it's used every single day since 8/8 — same New Balance 550 in blush pink, same white block lettering, same "SHOP THE SALE" outline button — with the label rotated once more, this time to "IT'S ALMOST OVER!" and "It ends today! Get up to 50% off now!" The subject, "FINAL HOURS: up to 50% off!," at least agrees with the hero for once, unlike the 8/16 "LAST CHANCE" send that clashed with its own "LAST DAY" banner.
- But this send has a real defect the others didn't: four solid black rectangular blocks sit directly under the hero button, clearly broken/unrendered image placeholders. That's a hard flag regardless of how good the copy is.
- Cadence is now the dominant problem. This is at least the sixth near-identical red "BIG SALE" hero this week, and the fourth or fifth "final/last chance" urgency claim — after "LAST DAY TO SAVE" on 8/16 and multiple "still here" sends earlier. Urgency copy that's been wrong four times in a row stops being urgency and starts reading as noise. Combined with the visible render bug, this is a below-average send.

## What's working

- Subject and hero finally align — "FINAL HOURS" up top matches "IT'S ALMOST OVER" / "It ends today" in the creative, unlike the 8/16 LAST CHANCE/LAST DAY mismatch.
- "Up to 50% off" is a concrete, visible offer with a clear "SHOP THE SALE" CTA button.
- "Shop Sale By Brand" grid (Nike, New Balance, Jordan, adidas) gives an easy second path to browse if the hero doesn't land.
- "Based on Your Style" personalized product row shows real products with prices, ratings, and visible markdowns (e.g., $56.25 from $74.99, $30 from $60).
- FLX Rewards banner (free shipping, no minimum) is visible and relevant for members.

## What's weak

- Four black rectangular blocks render directly below the hero CTA — a visible broken-image/layout bug that looks unfinished and unprofessional.
- "It ends today" / "FINAL HOURS" urgency has now been claimed on multiple consecutive days (8/16 "LAST DAY," 8/16 "LAST CHANCE") — repeated false deadlines erode trust in the claim.
- No actual countdown, timestamp, or date to back up "ends today" — it's asserted, not shown.
- "Exclusions apply" sits directly under the CTA in small text — a soft caveat that undercuts the "up to 50%" headline.
- Same red hero template, same shoe styling as multiple prior sends this week — zero creative differentiation in the inbox.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the broken image blocks under the hero CTA before this ships again** — four black boxes is the single biggest credibility hit in this render, bigger than any copy issue.
- 2. **Retire "final/last chance" language until it's true** — after LAST DAY (8/16) and LAST CHANCE (8/16), a fourth false deadline this week will train subscribers to ignore it. Save it for an actual final day and back it with a visible countdown or explicit end date/time.
- 3. **Break the red hero template** — same shoe-on-red-block layout run daily; swap to a different color story or lifestyle photography to stand out in an inbox already trained to skim past this exact shape.
- 4. **Move "Exclusions apply" or make the actual exclusion clear** — either state what's excluded or drop the vague caveat near the CTA.
