# Get up to 40% off top New Balance styles!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-17**
## Executive summary

- This is the strongest Foot Locker send in the batch this week, and it breaks the "BIG SALE" red-hero rut it's been stuck in since 8/8. Purple gradient hero, a single hero product (the New Balance 1906R in white/silver), a themed headline ("NEW BALANCE WEEK IS HERE!"), and a subject line that matches the creative exactly — subject, hero, and CTA are all telling the same story for once.
- Below the fold it does the standard Foot Locker multi-category-plus-picks-for-you layout, which works fine but is now visually identical to half the sends in the brand history (purple outline buttons, "PICKS FOR YOU" grid). The "Get up to 40% off" framing is honest and specific compared to the vague "up to 50% off... somewhere" sends from 8/16, but there's no visible end date, so the urgency is soft.
- Solid, on-brand, targeted send — a clear step up from the recycled red BIG SALE creative, though it still leans on the generic post-hero template rather than anything New Balance-specific past the top banner.

## What's working

- Subject line and hero headline match word-for-word ("New Balance... up to 40% off") — no bait-and-switch like 8/16's "LAST CHANCE" vs. "LAST DAY" mismatch.
- Single hero product (New Balance 1906R) rendered cleanly and large, easy to identify at a glance.
- FLX loyalty incentive is called out twice — top banner ("Free Shipping & Returns") and inside the hero copy ("3X points on New Balance purchases") — relevant if I'm a member.
- Clear unambiguous CTA button ("SHOP NEW BALANCE") sitting right under the offer.
- No render bugs — images loaded, text is legible, pricing/ratings on the picks-for-you grid all display correctly.

## What's weak

- No specific deadline for "New Balance Week" — is it 3 days or 7? Without an end date the urgency reads as filler.
- "Picks for you" grid mixes Detroit Tigers apparel, UGGs, Birkenstocks, and Vans/Nike — none of which are New Balance, which dilutes the single-brand focus the hero promised.
- "Up to 40% off" is never anchored to a real product/price anywhere in the email — no strikethrough pricing on the hero item itself, so the discount is a claim, not a demonstrated value.
- This is the same purple-outline-button, "SHOP FOR THE FAMILY" template Foot Locker has run repeatedly — the structure below the hero doesn't feel distinct even though the top does.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a visible end date/countdown to "New Balance Week" in the hero (e.g., "Ends Sunday 8/23") — this is the single biggest lever since the offer is otherwise strong but time-agnostic.
- 2. Show actual before/after pricing on the hero 1906R (e.g., "$160 $96") so "up to 40%" is demonstrated, not just asserted.
- 3. Swap at least 2 of the 6 "Picks for You" items for New Balance products to keep the whole email on-theme instead of diluting the hook halfway down.
- 4. Vary the post-hero module layout (e.g., a horizontal NB product carousel) instead of reusing the same purple-outline category-button block from prior sends.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New Balance Week: up to 40% off starts now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your New Balance pick is up to 40% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The 1906R and more — plus 3X FLX points this week only`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop New Balance for the family before the deal ends`
