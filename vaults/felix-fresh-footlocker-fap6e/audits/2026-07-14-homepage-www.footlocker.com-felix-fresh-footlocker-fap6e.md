# Homepage snapshot · www.footlocker.com · 2026-07-14
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-14**
## Executive summary

- Foot Locker is running a Back to School campaign and the hero is a sneaker flat-lay stacking what looks like Adidas Sambas and Nike Air Force 1s — both certified Gen Z staples right now, so the product curation checks out. But this is a vibe board, not a homepage: no price, no CTA button, no FLX hook above the fold. It looks like a curated sneaker mood board more than a store trying to get me to buy something.

## What's working

- **Sneaker selection in the hero** — whoever picked that flat-lay knows their audience. Sambas, AF1s, and the black Nike swoosh peeking in? That's my sneaker shelf right there.
- **"Back to School" campaign banner** — the top sticky strip with "Shop Back to School" links directly to the campaign, and the quick-filter pill reinforces it. Consistent.
- **Category pills (Men's, Back to School, Women's, Kids')** — one tap to Men's is efficient, especially on mobile where nav hamburgers are friction.
- **Film strip border on the hero** — it's a creative choice that feels culturally aware. Not your dad's Foot Locker grid layout.

## What's weak

- **Zero hero copy** — there is no headline, no sub-copy, no offer text overlaid on the hero image. It's just sneakers floating in a black void. I have no idea if there's a deal, why I should care right now, or what page I'm even on beyond the logo.
- **No CTA button on the hero** — I have to use the nav pills or scroll to do anything. That's extra friction the page doesn't need to create.
- **FLX rewards are invisible** — I specifically time purchases around FLX double-up weekends. If that's live right now, I have zero signal of it. That's a massive miss for a loyalty-driven shopper.
- **No price signal anywhere** — not a "starting at," not a "members save X%," nothing. For Back to School, I expect markdown racks, not a mood board.
- **The hero image is cropped awkward on mobile** — the sneaker pile is cut off at the bottom, which makes the composition feel incomplete rather than intentional.

## Recommendations

- **Put a headline and CTA on the hero** — even "Sambas are here. Shop now." with a tappable button over the image. The visual is doing the heavy lifting alone and it's not enough.
- **Add a FLX banner or loyalty callout** — a thin strip under the nav ("FLX Members: Earn double points this weekend") would hit me immediately and give me a reason to log in before I even browse.
- **Show one concrete discount above the fold** — "Back to School deals from $39.99" or "20% off select kicks" gives me a conversion hook. Right now I don't know if there's a deal at all.
- **Pin a lifestyle shot with a person wearing the Sambas** — the flat-lay is cool but I want to see how they style on-foot. One model shot in the rotation would close the "do I want these on me" gap fast.
