# Homepage snapshot · www.footlocker.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Foot Locker is trying to do a lot at once — there's a New Balance Grey Days 2026 collab drop teased at the very top, a kids' sale blasting 30% off as the main hero, and a Hoops Lives Here section starting to peek in below. As a 23-year-old who checks SNKRS every morning, the New Balance callout at the top is the most interesting thing on this screen, but they buried it in a tiny marquee banner and then hit me with a kids' sale as the actual hero. Mixed signals.

## What's working

- **"New Balance Grey Days 2026" top banner** — the Grey Days event is legit on the sneaker calendar and the fact that they're announcing it is a signal for someone like me. That's the right info at the right time.
- **Navigation pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', New Arrivals)** — single-tap access to my section, no hunting needed.
- **"Hoops Lives Here" secondary module** — the vibe is right. Young guys, basketball aesthetic, streetwear energy. That's my lane. If that were the hero instead of the kids' sale, I'd be way more engaged.
- **Clean render** — no layout jank, no broken assets. The page just works on mobile, which I appreciate.

## What's weak

- **Kids' sale as the primary hero** — I'm 23, I'm here for Jordans and SNKRS drops. Leading with "Save on top kids styles" is a scroll-past for me. Wrong audience, wrong placement.
- **No FLX rewards callout** — I've got FLX points and I always check if there's a double-up event before I buy. Nothing visible here. That's a missed hook for a loyal customer like me.
- **"Up to 30% Off" headline without clarifying who it's for** — until you read the small subtext, you assume it's sitewide. The kids' qualifier feels buried, which makes it feel like a bait-and-switch.
- **No urgency on the New Balance Grey Days banner** — it's just "Shop New Balance" with no "limited," no date, no "while supplies last." For a raffle-mindset guy, I need to know if this is a drop window or just evergreen.
- **No product imagery** — the orange hero module is pure text and color, no actual shoe shown. For a sneakerhead, the shoe IS the hook.

## Recommendations

- **Make the Hoops Lives Here module the hero and put an actual Nike or Jordan product in it** — that section has the right energy, it just needs to be first.
- **Add an FLX rewards strip directly below the nav** — even a one-liner like "FLX Members: Double Points This Weekend" would instantly change my engagement posture.
- **Give the New Balance Grey Days banner a date or urgency signal** — "Grey Days: May 15–18 only" would make me tap immediately instead of treating it as ambient noise.
- **Show a shoe in the 30% Off hero** — replace the blurred orange gradient background with an actual product shot of whatever's on sale, even if it's a kids' shoe. Visual over verbal.
