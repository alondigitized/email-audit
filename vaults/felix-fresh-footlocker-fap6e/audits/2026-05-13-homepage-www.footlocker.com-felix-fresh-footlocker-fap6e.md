# Homepage snapshot · www.footlocker.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Foot Locker is leading with sneaker culture energy — the NB Grey Days 2026 callout at the top is exactly the kind of launch-event language I track, and the HOOPS Lives Here module peeking in at the bottom speaks my language. But the primary hero real estate is burning on a kids' sale, which completely derails the vibe for a 23-year-old who's here to check drops, not cop Jordan 11s for a toddler.

## What's working

- **NB Grey Days 2026 announcement bar** — putting a named drop event in the top banner is exactly how you talk to someone who has a launch calendar. I see that and I don't immediately bounce.
- **"HOOPS Lives HERE" module** — the crew shot with the dark background and bold typography reads like streetwear editorial. That's the visual register I respond to.
- **Navigation tabs** — Men's, Women's, Kids', New Arrivals are pill-shaped and easy to tap on mobile. "New Arrivals" being front and center is the right call for drop-chasers.
- **Search bar prominence** — it's the first thing under the header. If I want to look up a specific model, I can get there instantly.

## What's weak

- **Kids' sale as the primary hero** — the largest above-fold canvas is selling me on kids shoes. That's not just irrelevant, it actively signals that the page wasn't built for me right now.
- **Zero FLX loyalty visibility** — I came here partly because I'm tracking double-up weekends. Not seeing any points balance, member pricing, or FLX callout is a miss for a returning member.
- **"Up to 30% Off" is ambiguous from the button** — the fine print says kids styles but the "Shop the Sale" button doesn't differentiate. If I tap it expecting a men's sale and land on kids shoes, I'm out.
- **HOOPS module is clipped** — the most relevant-to-me content is cut off at the fold. The headline reads "HOOPS Lives HERE" but the bottom is cropped, so it's teasing without paying off.
- **No Jordan / SNKRS-competing language** — no "members get early access," no raffle entry callout, no reason to pick Foot Locker over just camping the SNKRS app this weekend.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the kids' sale hero with a men's sneaker drop module** — specifically something that ties to NB Grey Days inventory. The announcement bar already set the hook; the hero should close it.
- **Surface FLX points in the header or hero** — even a small "You have X FLX points — double-up ends Sunday" badge converts returning members like me who are already primed to buy.
- **Give the HOOPS module more above-fold real estate** — push the kids' sale below the HOOPS editorial or stack it after. The basketball crew shot is the strongest Gen Z signal on the page.
- **Add "Members Get Early Access" copy to the NB Grey Days banner** — that one phrase turns a passive announcement into a reason to log in and convert.
