# Reserve the Jordan Retro 1, adidas adistar Jellyfish today.
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-28**
## Executive summary

- This is Foot Locker returning to a multi-hero "reservation calendar" format — bundling four separate launches (Nike Air Zoom Flight "Glove," adidas Adistar Jellyfish, Jordan Retro 1 "Love of the Game," and what's meant to be the Nike G.T. Charge) into one send, each with its own headline, hero shot, deadline, and CTA. Structurally it's a step up from the flat sitewide-markdown sends this brand fell into on 7/26 and 7/27 ("Up to 30/40% off"), since it reuses the single-hero treatment that worked well when the Jordan Retro 1 ran solo on 7/26 at 8/10.
- But two real production bugs undercut it: the intended "G.T. Charge" headline renders as "ST CHARGE" (clipped), and the "Upcoming Drops" marquee band is cut off on both edges, reading "OMING DROPS // UPCOMING DROPS // UPCOMING DR." Given this brand already ran the Jordan Retro 1 as a clean, high-scoring single-product hero on 7/26, seeing it demoted to one of four modules — with a subject line that drops two of the four products entirely — is a step backward for that specific product.
- Solid bones, sloppy execution. Fix the cropping and this is a genuinely strong send.

## What's working

- Four distinct named products each get full hero treatment: headline, product shot, colorway name, hard deadline, and dedicated "Reserve Now" CTA.
- Reservation deadlines are specific and credible (7/30 4PM ET, 7/31 4PM ET) rather than vague urgency copy.
- FLX Rewards / free-shipping messaging appears at both header and footer, reinforcing loyalty value.
- A personalized "Picks for You" grid at the bottom adds a secondary conversion path with visible prices and star ratings.
- Consistent dark, bold Foot Locker visual identity throughout — no brand-voice whiplash between modules.

## What's weak

- Text-cropping bug: "G.T. Charge" renders as "ST CHARGE," and the "Upcoming Drops" ticker is clipped on both sides.
- Subject only names 2 of the 4 featured products — the Nike Air Zoom Flight and the Nike G.T. Charge get zero subject-line credit.
- No price is shown anywhere for the four hero sneakers, despite this being a reservation email where shoppers will want to know the number before committing.
- All four countdown timers render frozen at 00:00:00:00, which reads as expired rather than urgent.
- Subject is a comma-spliced product list ("Jordan Retro 1, adidas adistar Jellyfish today") that reads as a run-on rather than a clean urgency hook.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the cropped headline rendering — "G.T. Charge" and the "Upcoming Drops" ticker are both getting clipped; this looks like a template-width or font-scaling bug worth a QA pass before the next send.
- 2. Rewrite the subject to lead with the strongest single hero — per brand history, "Reserve the Jordan Retro 1 'Love of the Game' today" alone scored 8/10 solo; diluting it with a second product via comma splice weakens it.
- 3. Add a visible price to each hero module — reservation emails convert better when the number is known before the click.
- 4. Replace the frozen 00:00:00:00 countdown with a static "Closes in X hours" line if the live-countdown asset can't render reliably across clients.
