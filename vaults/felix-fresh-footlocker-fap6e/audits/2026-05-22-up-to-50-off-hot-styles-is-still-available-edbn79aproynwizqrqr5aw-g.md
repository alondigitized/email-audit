# Up to 50% off hot styles is still available! 🔥
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the Day 2 "still available" follow-up to the Summer Kickoff Sale Foot Locker launched yesterday (May 21, 9/10). The hero is essentially unchanged: same blue pool-party backdrop, same oversized "50% OFF" in yellow, same silver Nike silhouette center-stage. The real addition is "CURATED FOR YOU" — a six-product grid with strikethrough pricing, star ratings, and shipping callouts — which adds decision-support that Day 1's launch email didn't have.
- The weakness is that any subscriber who opened yesterday's email recognizes this immediately. The subject says "still available" without delivering a hard deadline, and nothing in the email body enforces the urgency claim. No end date appears anywhere. The offer, the hero, and the discount percentage are identical to 24 hours ago. For engaged subscribers, the implicit message is "we didn't sell out and we want you to know it" — useful, but not compelling.
- Execution is clean and the personalization signals are running: the FLX footer surfaces a specific Pasadena, CA store address, the curated grid has real pricing, and the loyalty section is present. The bones are there; the email just needed a deadline.

- "CURATED FOR YOU" grid is the genuine differentiator from Day 1: six products with visible strikethrough pricing, star ratings, and "Ships Free" callouts provide real purchase context that yesterday's launch never had.
- FLX Rewards section anchors the bottom with an "exclusive pricing" call-to-action and a geo-targeted store address (Pasadena, CA) — both digital and physical touchpoints in one module.
- Hero visual hierarchy is immediately legible: yellow "50% OFF" at oversized scale with a single CTA button beneath, nothing competing for attention.
- Render is clean — no broken images, no overlapping text, no empty placeholder content.

## What's weak

- Hero creative is recycled directly from May 21's sale launch; subscribers who opened that email get no visual signal that this send contains new content.
- No end date anywhere in the body — "still available" implies scarcity but never closes with a deadline, which defuses the urgency.
- All six "CURATED FOR YOU" products appear to be variations of the same silhouette family (Nike LaFrance colorways in different tones), which reads more like a color-option push than genuine taste-based curation.
- Preview text is likely leaking a "View in Browser" header from the top of the template, which would undercut the subject line's work in inbox.
- FLX loyalty pricing is buried below the product grid — it's the only member benefit and it's arriving after most readers have already decided.

- 3. **Diversify the curated grid** — at least two different silhouette families or brands would make "curated for you" feel like a recommendation engine rather than a colorway matrix.
- 4. **Promote FLX pricing into the hero area** — a single line like "FLX Members: see your exclusive price" above the CTA button drives loyalty acquisition and engagement simultaneously, rather than making members hunt for their benefit at the bottom.
- 5. **Fix the preheader** — suppress whatever is leaking at the top and replace with deadline copy or a product callout.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Bug — CSS selector typo (confirmed):**
```css
/* Line in @media (max-width: 600px) block */
.fluid-row .col-9, .md-fluid-row .col-8 { width: 74.25% !important; }
```
The second selector should be `.md-fluid-row .col-9`, not `.col-8`. As written:
- `.md-fluid-row .col-9` receives no mobile width override.
- `.md-fluid-row .col-8` receives **two conflicting rules** — 66% (correct, set two rules earlier) is overridden by 74.25% (incorrect). Any 8-column `md-fluid-row` block will render 8.25% too wide on mobile.

**External font dependency:**
```html
<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://partner-fonts.bluecore.com/footlocker/footlocker_fonts_1776712244.css">
```
Gmail (web), Outlook (all), and Apple Mail with "Block All Remote Content" will not load this. No `font-family` fallback stack is visible in the truncated CSS. If the font fails to load and no fallback is defined, clients will substitute the system default — visually inconsistent but not broken.

**Accessibility — text scaling disabled:**
```css
div,p,a,li,span,td { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none !important; }
```
Applying `none` globally prevents iOS from scaling small text for low-vision users. WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) is at risk. Change to `100%` if browser zoom support is the actual goal.

**Accessibility — missing `lang` attribute:**
The `<html>` tag has no `lang="en"`. Screen readers will default to the OS language, which may cause incorrect pronunciation for international recipients.
