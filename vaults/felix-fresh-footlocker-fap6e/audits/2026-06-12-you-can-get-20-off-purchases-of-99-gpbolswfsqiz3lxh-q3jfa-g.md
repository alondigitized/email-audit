# You can get 20% off purchases of $99!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-12**
## Executive summary

- This is the payload email delivering on the June 10 teaser — same magenta-on-black "MEMBERS ONLY" creative, now with the actual number: 20% off orders of $99+. The follow-through from teaser to reveal is clean and the visual identity is consistent. That's the high point.
- The offer itself is structurally weaker than subscribers were primed for. Six days ago Foot Locker was shouting "UP TO 50% OFF"; two days ago the teaser promised an exclusive members benefit. Landing on 20% with a $99 spend floor and a "must be logged in" friction gate feels like a letdown relative to that setup. The fine print — online only, exclusions apply, log in required — does real damage to click confidence.
- There is also a clear price display bug in the product grid: the Nike P-6000 SE shows a price of **$2.22**, which is obviously wrong and erodes trust in the whole product section. This email would have benefited from a QA pass before send.

## What's working

- **Teaser-to-send continuity.** The magenta/black "MEMBERS ONLY SALE" hero directly mirrors the June 10 tease — the creative promise is paid off visually.
- **Hero legibility.** "20% OFF" is enormous on the screen; no scrolling needed to know the offer.
- **Dual CTA at the top.** "SHOP SALE" and "JOIN FLX FOR FREE" in the same frame converts both existing members and prospects without separate sends.
- **Product grid with social proof.** Star ratings and review counts on six products add credibility and browsability below the fold.
- **Subject line length.** 37 characters — clean on mobile, no truncation risk.

## What's weak

- **$2.22 Nike P-6000 SE price.** Obvious data error in the product grid; makes the section look broken and makes you wonder what else is wrong.
- **"For a limited time" without a deadline.** The hero copy implies urgency but provides no date. After the May/June drumbeat of "FINAL HOURS," vague urgency reads as noise.
- **$99 minimum is buried until after the CTA.** The hero reads "20% OFF" before the $99 threshold appears in smaller type below — sets up a fine-print gotcha experience.
- **"Must be logged in for discount to be applied in cart"** is a real conversion killer that appears only in grey fine print below the full-family grid — this should be called out clearly near the CTA.
- **Weak subject line phrasing.** "You can get 20% off purchases of $99!" reads like boilerplate. No personality, no urgency signal, no hint of exclusivity despite this being a members-only event.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with exclusivity in the subject.** The members-only framing is the real hook — the subject buries it. "Members Only: 20% off your $99+ order — today only" does more work.
- 2. **Add a specific deadline to the hero.** "Ends Sunday" or "3 days only" would rescue "for a limited time" from generic promo blur.
- 3. **Surface the $99 minimum and login requirement in the CTA row**, not hidden in fine print. A note like "20% off $99+ | Sign in at checkout" next to the SHOP SALE button prevents cart abandonment.
- 4. **Fix the Nike P-6000 SE price before any future send resurfacing this product grid.** $2.22 is an obvious data pipeline error.
- 5. **Re-sequence the fine print.** Move exclusion language above the fold (right under the offer callout), not after four CTA buttons — trust is better built with transparency than buried asterisks.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Members Only: 20% off orders $99+ — ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your FLX perk is live: 20% off $99+`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop the sale online today — log in at checkout to apply your discount.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Hundreds of styles on sale. Exclusions apply — see what qualifies.`

### 1. Technical Summary

Bluecore-rendered email using a table-based responsive framework with dynamic product recommendation widgets. One confirmed CSS selector bug causes layout corruption on medium breakpoints; the `<html>` element is missing a `lang` attribute, a WCAG Level A failure.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Cannot fully assess** — HTML is truncated before link hrefs are visible. The following is flagged based on what is present:

- Sending subdomain is `bc.footlocker.com` (Bluecore MTA). All click-tracking will route through Bluecore's redirect layer. Confirm that redirect chain terminates on `footlocker.com` and not a generic Bluecore domain, which can affect spam filter reputation scoring.
- External stylesheet loaded from `partner-fonts.bluecore.com` — a third-party CDN. If this origin is unavailable at render time, font fallback behavior is undefined and may shift layout.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Confirmed bug — CSS selector collision at mobile breakpoint:**

```css
.fluid-row .col-9, .md-fluid-row .col-8 { width: 74.25% !important; }
```

The selector `.md-fluid-row .col-8` appears twice — once correctly in the col-8 rule and again erroneously paired with `.fluid-row .col-9`. The result: `md-fluid-row .col-9` is **never defined** (falls back to 100% via the `#content-table` catch-all), and `md-fluid-row .col-8` receives a conflicting width. Any medium-breakpoint grid using `.col-9` in an `md-fluid-row` will collapse to full-width unexpectedly.

**Missing `lang` attribute on `<html>`:**

```html
<html>  <!-- no lang="en" -->
```

This is a WCAG 2.1 §3.1.1 (Level A) failure. Screen readers cannot determine the document language, and some spam filters penalize emails with no declared language.

**`-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` applied globally:**

```css
div,p,a,li,span,td { -webkit-text-size-adjust:none !important; }
```

Disabling text-size adjustment globally can make small text inaccessible on mobile (particularly in product name and price cells, which have explicit pixel heights like `height: 24px`). If the font is already sized correctly, use `100%` instead of `none`.

**Fixed-height product cells:**

Cells like `.name`, `.gender_age`, `.price` are set to fixed pixel heights (24px, 25px). If Bluecore injects product names longer than one line, text will overflow or clip with no visible truncation indicator.
