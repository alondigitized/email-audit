# Are you back-to-school ready?
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-15**
## Executive summary

- This is Foot Locker's most thematically coherent back-to-school send in the batch — "DAY ONE ESSENTIALS" hero, then Jordan Retro 3 drop, a 50%-off banner, apparel, and backpacks, all loosely orbiting the same seasonal idea instead of the grab-bag mismatches seen in the 8/12 "All black styles" send (5.0/10). The subject "Are you back-to-school ready?" is honest about what's inside, even if it's vague and offers zero hook.
- The problem is structural, not thematic: five stacked hero sections with five separate CTAs means there's no single dominant offer for the eye to land on. The 50% off sale — arguably the most clickable thing in this email — is buried as a thin red banner between the Jordan drop and the apparel section, easy to scroll past.
- This is a solid, on-brand seasonal email dragged down by a flat subject line and a lack of visual hierarchy. Mid-pack for this brand: better than the scattered sends, worse than the sharpest single-offer ones like the 8/14 FLX Fridays send (7.0/10).

## What's working

- Hero and subsequent sections all genuinely tie back to "back to school" — sneakers, apparel, backpacks — unlike prior sends that promised one thing and delivered another.
- The Jordan Retro 3 "Sports Renaissance" section reuses a strong, already-validated format (clean product shot, specific colorway name, clear "SHOP JORDAN" CTA).
- FLX Rewards membership benefits (free shipping/returns, no minimum) appear twice — top nav and a dedicated footer banner — reinforcing loyalty value.
- Clean render: no overlapping text, no broken images, good spacing throughout.

## What's weak

- No single visual anchor — five hero sections in a row means the actual best offer (up to 50% off) is easy to miss on the scroll.
- Subject line "Are you back-to-school ready?" is a generic question with no product, price, or urgency — weak pull off an already-crowded inbox from this sender.
- The 50% off banner uses vague "up to" framing with tiny "more styles added" fine print, echoing the honesty gap flagged in the 8/8-onward "BIG SALE" sends.
- No sizing, fit, or availability info anywhere, despite five separate product categories being pitched.
- Cadence fatigue: this is at least the fourth Foot Locker send in the past week hitting BTS/sale themes with near-identical mechanics.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero: lead with the 50% off sale banner up top (it's the most concrete, clickable thing in the email) instead of burying it mid-scroll behind two other sections.
- 2. Tighten the subject line to include the actual offer instead of a rhetorical question.
- 3. Add a sizing/availability cue near at least the Jordan drop, since it's a limited release and urgency around stock would drive clicks.
- 4. Cut from five sections to three — Sale, one hero sneaker drop, one accessory (backpack) — to reduce scroll fatigue and sharpen hierarchy.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 50% off back-to-school starts now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your back-to-school list, up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sneakers, backpacks, and apparel — all in one stop.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The Jordan Retro 3 just landed. Grab it before size runs out.`
