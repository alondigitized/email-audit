# Up to 50% off is still here!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-15**
## Executive summary

- This is Foot Locker running the exact same "BIG SALE" red hero playbook it's used since 8/8 — swapped this time for a pink Nike Dunk instead of the generic sneaker stack — paired with a subject that outright admits the fatigue: "Up to 50% off is still here!" Compared to the 8/13 "extended" version (5.00/10), this send is at least structurally cleaner: one hero, one CTA, no scattered multi-section mess like the 8/12 back-to-school email.
- But it ships with a real defect: four solid black rectangles sit directly under the "SHOP THE SALE" button where promo tiles or a countdown module were clearly meant to render. That's the kind of broken-image gap that reads as sloppy in an inbox, right at the moment the eye is scanning for a next action. Below the fold, the "Looks You'll Love" product grid is the strongest part of the email — real prices, star ratings, a markdown callout (PUMA MB.05 $109.99 vs $135) — but it's buried after a hero that's now running its third consecutive week with diminishing "sale is still on" messaging.
- Net: functional but tired creative, dragged down by a visible render bug and zero urgency. This lands in the same mid-tier band as the brand's other recycled-hero sends.

## What's working

- Clean single-purpose layout: one hero, one clear CTA ("SHOP THE SALE"), no competing messages fighting for attention.
- Product grid below the fold shows real signal — star ratings, a visible markdown (PUMA MB.05 $109.99, was $135), and specific colorways.
- FLX Rewards loyalty banner ("FREE SHIPPING, NO MINIMUM") is prominent and legible right above the footer.
- "Exclusions apply" is disclosed visibly under the CTA rather than buried in tiny footer print.

## What's weak

- Four black rectangles render directly beneath the CTA — a clear broken-image/placeholder bug that undercuts trust right at the point of conversion.
- This is the same red "BIG SALE" hero treatment the brand has run since 8/8 and "extended" on 8/13 — by 8/15 it reads as stale, not urgent.
- "Up to 50% off is still here!" has no deadline or new tier — no reason to act today versus tomorrow.
- Hero CTA routes to a generic sale, not the specific pink Dunk it showcases — the strongest visual asset in the email isn't directly shoppable.
- Subject and preheader offer nothing new versus the last two sends in this campaign — cadence fatigue is visible to a subscriber who's seen the 8/13 version.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the broken black-box render before this goes out again — that's a visible, no-excuses bug that erodes trust in every other email in the campaign.
- 2. Replace "still here" framing with a hard deadline or new escalation (e.g., a specific end date or a deeper markdown) — reusing the same creative for a third week needs a reason to re-engage, not a reminder it hasn't ended.
- 3. Make the hero shoe (pink Nike Dunk) directly clickable to its product page instead of a generic sale link — that specific shoe is the strongest visual asset and should get its own CTA.
- 4. Pull one line from the product grid (e.g., the PUMA MB.05 price drop) up into the hero or subject to give the offer teeth beyond "up to 50%."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Pink Dunks are up to 50% off — today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your back-to-school list, up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `PUMA MB.05 now $109.99 — plus free shipping for FLX members.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New markdowns just added to the sale — shop before sizes go.`
