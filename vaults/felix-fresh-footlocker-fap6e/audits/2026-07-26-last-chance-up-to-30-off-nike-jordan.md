# LAST CHANCE: up to 30% off Nike & Jordan
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the generic "sitewide markdown" beat Foot Locker falls back to when there's no hero product to push — and it shows. After a two-week run of sharp, single-SKU sends (Air Max 95 "Solar Red," Jordan Retro 4 "Comic Con," the Sabrina/A'ja signature editorial), this drops back to the same "30% Off Nike & Jordan" discount drumbeat that was already stale on July 24's "Super Hot Deals on Nike & Jordan!" send. The "LAST CHANCE" framing gives it real urgency, but the creative underneath is a straight reskin of a promo this list has seen at least twice in the last week.
- The bigger miss is the "Picks for You" grid: four of the six products shown are adidas Samba, adidas Originals, and adidas Adilette slides — in an email whose subject and hero both say "Nike & Jordan." That's not a matching-recommendation problem so much as a trust problem — a subscriber scanning for the Nike/Jordan discount lands on adidas product almost immediately below the fold.
- Judgement: functional, on-brand, no visual bugs, but it's the weakest creative in this brand's last five sends — recycled urgency copy plus an off-category product feed drag it well below the Air Max 95 and Jordan Retro 4 sends.

## What's working

- Hero is unambiguous: "UP TO 30% OFF NIKE & JORDAN" fills the frame in huge type, impossible to miss even on a quick scroll.
- "LAST CHANCE" + "Time's Running Out" stacks two credible urgency cues without tipping into ALL-CAPS spam territory.
- Single clear CTA ("SHOP SALE") sits directly under the offer with good whitespace around it.
- "Shop Sale By Brand" gives a fast Nike vs. Jordan split for subscribers who already know what they want.
- FLX Rewards banner (free shipping, no minimum) and footer are clean, on-brand, and bug-free — no broken images or overlapping text anywhere in the render.

## What's weak

- "Picks for You" shows adidas Samba, adidas Originals, and adidas Adilette slides in a Nike & Jordan discount email — a jarring category mismatch right under the fold.
- Subject/hero copy ("Nike & Jordan") isn't reflected in the actual recommended products, which undercuts the "last chance" urgency since half the visible product isn't even on offer.
- This is a near-identical creative template to the July 24 "Super Hot Deals on Nike & Jordan!" send — same brand, same blue Jordan-jumpman background, same discount framing, just swapped urgency copy.
- No specific colorway, model, or dollar amount anywhops in the hero — "up to 30%" is vague compared to the Air Max 95 "Solar Red" or Jordan 4 "Comic Con" sends that named an actual product.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the product feed mismatch first.** Swap "Picks for You" to actually surface Nike/Jordan product — showing adidas in a Nike & Jordan sale email is the single biggest trust-eroding issue here and the easiest to fix.
- 2. **Name a specific product or category leader in the hero** (e.g., "up to 30% off Jordan 4s") instead of "Nike & Jordan" broadly — this brand's best-performing sends (Air Max 95, Jordan 4 Comic Con) all named a specific product.
- 3. **Break the template repetition** — this is visually near-identical to July 24's send; rotate background art or layout so back-to-back sitewide-discount emails don't blur together in the inbox.
- 4. **Add a real deadline** ("Ends tonight," "Ends Sunday 11:59pm") next to "Last Chance" — right now the urgency is emotional, not concrete.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last chance: 30% off Jordan ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Final hours — up to 30% off Nike & Jordan`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sale ends tonight — new styles just added.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `FLX members get free shipping, no minimum, all week.`
