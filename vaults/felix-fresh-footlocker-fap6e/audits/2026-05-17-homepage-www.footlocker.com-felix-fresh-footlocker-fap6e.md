# Homepage snapshot · www.footlocker.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Yo, first thing I see is a New Balance Grey Days banner at the top — okay, that's actually sneaker-culture aware, they know the drop calendar. But then the hero hits me with a kids' sale in orange. That's not me. The Jordan 3 section peeking at the bottom is the real hook, and it's getting buried under a misdirected promo. Foot Locker is showing me it knows sneaker culture but put it in the wrong slot.

## What's working

- **New Balance Grey Days 2026 banner** — that top ticker shows they're plugged into the drop calendar. I've got Grey Days on my radar and seeing it here signals Foot Locker is paying attention to sneaker culture the way I do.
- **Jordan section just below the fold** — Air Jordan 3s with the Jumpman logo and "WORLD'S BEST" tongue detail. That's the visual that almost makes me stay. Right product, right brand, good photography.
- **Men's / Women's / Kids' / New Arrivals tab row** — one tap to Men's. Clean. No friction getting to my section.
- **Search bar front and center** — if I already know what I want (say, AJ3 Cement), I can go straight there. Native behavior for me.

## What's weak

- **Kids' sale hero as the dominant viewport** — I'm 23. I'm not buying kids' shoes. Making a kids' sale the biggest thing on the screen for a returning adult sneakerhead is a hard miss. The FLX personalization should have swapped this for the Jordan module.
- **Zero FLX rewards hook above the fold** — this is the one thing that makes Foot Locker different from GOAT or StockX for me. No points balance shown, no "double-up weekend" teaser, nothing. That's the loyalty lever and it's completely invisible.
- **No urgency on the New Balance Grey Days banner** — it's there but it's just a link. No "drops this weekend," no countdown, no "limited pairs." Grey Days should be treated like a SNKRS drop, not a site-nav link.
- **Orange kids' sale color palette vs. Jordan section** — the warm blurry orange feels totally disconnected from the dark, clean Jordan aesthetic right below it. The visual tone is inconsistent and the kids' sale is stealing the spotlight from the premium content.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero for the Jordan or New Balance drop content** — the Jordan 3 module is the most relevant thing on this page for a men's sneaker shopper. Move it above the fold or build a full hero around the Grey Days launch with a launch-date callout.
- **Pin an FLX rewards chip to the nav bar** — even a "You have 450 FLX points" badge next to the account icon would make me feel seen and keep me from bouncing to SNKRS.
- **Add a countdown or "limited" tag to the Grey Days banner** — "Grey Days — drops May 22" or "Limited Pairs" turns a passive link into urgency I recognize from the drop calendar.
- **Separate the kids' sale strip into a thinner banner below the fold** — it's a legit promo but it doesn't need hero real estate when your most loyal segment is adult sneakerheads. Demote it to a slim promotional strip and give Jordan the stage.
