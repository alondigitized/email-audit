# You've still got time to get up to 50% off!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- This is Foot Locker running back the same red "BIG SALE" hero it's used since 8/8, now tagged "EXTENDED" and paired with the subject "You've still got time to get up to 50% off!" — a subject that's honest about what this is: an urgency wrapper on stale creative, not a new push. The hero itself is clean and on-brand (bold "UP TO 50% OFF," a visible "SHOP THE SALE" button, "Exclusions apply" disclosed right under it), but directly beneath the CTA sit four solid black boxes where images clearly failed to load — a real render bug that undercuts an otherwise tidy layout.
- Below the hero it's the standard Foot Locker template: category shop-by tiles, a "Hot Styles" grid with six products (prices, star ratings, a couple of markdowns like Nike V5 RNR $71.25 from $95), then the FLX Rewards footer. Nothing here is broken or off-brand, but nothing distinguishes it from the last five sends in this brand history either — nearly every one of them has been red-hero-plus-grid.
- Compared to 8/11's "Extended: Up to 50% off select styles" (6.00/10), this is a near-repeat with a weaker, vaguer subject and a visible render defect. It lands lower.

## What's working

- Hero offer is unambiguous: "UP TO 50% OFF" is the largest element on the page, impossible to miss.
- CTA is clear and visible — a bordered "SHOP THE SALE" button sits directly under the offer copy.
- "Exclusions apply" is disclosed inline near the CTA rather than buried in footer fine print.
- Hot Styles grid shows real pricing, discounts, and star ratings (e.g., Jordan Retro 11 $56 was $70, 4.8★), giving shoppers enough info to click with intent.

## What's weak

- Four black/broken image boxes render directly below "Exclusions apply" — a clear visual defect that makes the hero look unfinished.
- The subject "You've still got time to get up to 50% off!" is generic urgency with no specific deadline, product, or category — it could be reused indefinitely and offers nothing the 8/11 "Extended" send didn't already say better.
- This is the same red BIG SALE hero running since 8/8 with only the word "EXTENDED" bolted on — fourth+ near-identical creative in under a week, and the brand history already flags fatigue on this exact pattern.
- No true urgency signal (no countdown, no specific end date) despite "still got time" implying a ticking clock.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the broken image render — those four black boxes are the single biggest drag on this send; nothing else matters if the hero looks broken in-inbox.
- 2. Retire the red BIG SALE creative for at least one cycle — five-plus sends of the same hero in a week is fatigue the subscriber will notice even if the offer changes.
- 3. Add a real deadline to justify the "still got time" framing — a countdown clock or explicit "ends Sunday" date would make the urgency credible instead of generic.
- 4. Tighten the subject to reference a category or specific brand (Nike/adidas/New Balance) to differentiate from the 8/11 near-twin subject.
