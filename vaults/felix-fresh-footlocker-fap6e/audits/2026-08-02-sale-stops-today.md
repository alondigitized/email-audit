# ⏰ SALE STOPS TODAY ⏰
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-02**
## Executive summary

- This is Foot Locker's second "ENDS TODAY" urgency send of the same day — the brand history shows "LAST CHANCE TO SHOP THE BTS BIG SALE 😱 ENDS TODAY" also went out on 2026-08-02, and this "⏰ SALE STOPS TODAY ⏰" is functionally the same message with a fresh subject line and a slightly cleaner hero. The visual is strong: a bold red field, two models in Jordan/Nike gear, a clean "SHOP THE SALE" button, and "up to 40% off" spelled out plainly. Below the fold it does the standard Foot Locker things well — shop-by-brand tiles, a "Top Recommendations" grid with prices and star ratings, and the FLX Rewards banner.
- The problem is credibility and cadence, not creative. Telling a subscriber the sale "ends today" for the second time in one day (after already saying it in the prior send) reads as manufactured urgency rather than a real deadline, and it burns trust for the next genuinely time-bound offer. There's also a clear render bug — four solid black bars where imagery should load, sitting right under the "Exclusions apply" disclaimer, which looks broken on the screen.
- Net: good execution of a tired trick, undercut by a visible bug and a redundant same-day resend.

## What's working

- Hero is unmissable — big red field, bold "ENDS TODAY" stamp, single unambiguous CTA button ("SHOP THE SALE").
- Concrete discount stated in the hero copy ("up to 40% off") rather than buried in fine print.
- FLX Rewards / free shipping banner appears twice (top nav strip and bottom footer band), reinforcing loyalty value without extra clutter.
- "Top Recommendations" grid gives real product names, prices, and star ratings (e.g. Birkenstock Tokio 4.7★, Brooks Adrenaline 4.5★) — genuine product detail, not filler.
- Shop-by-brand tiles (Nike, New Balance, Jordan, adidas) give a fast path to a specific brand without forcing a full scroll.

## What's weak

- Broken image block: four solid black vertical bars render directly below "Exclusions apply" — a visible layout bug that looks unfinished or broken.
- Same-day duplicate urgency: this is the second "ends today" claim in one day per the brand history, which cheapens the deadline and trains subscribers to ignore it.
- "Up to 40% off" with unspecified exclusions is vague — no indication of what's actually included at 40% vs. a token markdown.
- Hero pushes to the generic sale, not a specific hero product — despite featuring a Jordan 1 Mid prominently in the image, the CTA doesn't link to that shoe specifically.
- No preview/preheader text is visible at all — the inbox line right under the subject is wasted space.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop reusing "ends today" same-day — if the sale genuinely ends today, say it once, in one send, and let this slot cover a different angle (e.g., the featured Jordan 1 Mid from the hero, or a specific brand callout) so the deadline still carries weight.
- 2. Fix the broken image block under "Exclusions apply" before this goes out again — it's the first visual thing a scrolling reader hits after the CTA.
- 3. Replace "up to 40% off" with a floor number or named category ("30-40% off sneakers") so the offer doesn't read as bait.
- 4. Link the hero directly to the Jordan 1 Mid shown in the image, or add a second CTA under it, since the product is doing the visual work but isn't clickable.
- 5. Add real preheader copy — right now the inbox preview is blank, wasting prime real estate next to the subject.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: up to 40% off ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your BTS sale cart is about to expire`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Jordan, Nike, adidas & more — before the discount resets.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `FLX members: free shipping on everything, no minimum.`
