# Reserve the Jordan Retro 6 'Black & White' today!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- This is the third day in a row Foot Locker has sent a Jordan Retro 6 'Black/White' reservation email — 2026-08-04 ("Reserve the Jordan Retro 6 'Black/White' today!", 7.0/10) and 2026-08-05 ("Reserve the Jordan Retro 6 'Black & White'", 9.0/10) both hit inboxes with nearly identical hero treatment, and now this is a third consecutive touch on the exact same sneaker with the exact same deadline (8/7, 4PM ET). The hero itself is clean and well-produced — dramatic black background, product shot front and center, unambiguous "RESERVE NOW" button — but it's buried in a six-section catalog scroll (Jordan 6, Jordan 3, adidas Handball loafer, Salomon, Havaianas) that dilutes the reservation urgency the subject line promises.
- The bigger problem is cadence, not execution. Three days of the same product with the same close date reads as either a broken send schedule or a brand assuming subscribers didn't open the first two. Anyone who already reserved on day one gets no differentiation here — no "reminder" framing, no scarcity update ("only X pairs left"), just a repeat.
- Judgement: well-built individual send, but the third identical beat this week drags it down. On execution alone this is a 7-8; on fatigue it's a 6.

## What's working

- Hero section delivers exactly what the subject promises — Jordan Retro 6 'Black/White' with a clear reservation deadline (8/7 at 4PM ET) and a bold "RESERVE NOW" button.
- Product photography is sharp and well-lit throughout — the Jordan 3, adidas loafer, Salomon, and Havaianas shots all look professional, not stocky.
- FLX Fridays banner is a nice mid-scroll break that adds a second, distinct offer without hijacking the hero.
- No render bugs — every image loads, no overlapping text, no broken layout blocks.
- Bottom nav (store locator, app download, SMS signup, payment methods) is complete and on-brand.

## What's weak

- Third consecutive day promoting the identical Jordan Retro 6 'Black/White' reservation with the same 8/7 4PM deadline — zero new information for anyone who already saw 08-04 or 08-05.
- Six unrelated product sections (Jordan 6, Jordan 3, loafer, Salomon, sandal) stacked under one subject line about a single sneaker reservation — the promise narrows, the email doesn't.
- No urgency escalation despite this being the closest send yet to the 8/7 deadline — no "reservations close tomorrow" countdown framing.
- FLX Rewards membership benefit (free shipping/no minimum) is buried at the very bottom instead of reinforced near the hero CTA where it could lift conversion.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop resending the same reservation email three days running — segment: suppress or replace the Jordan 6 module for anyone who already opened/clicked the 08-04 or 08-05 versions, and reserve this slot for a genuinely new drop or a "last chance" version.
- 2. If sending again is intentional, reframe as urgency, not a repeat: "Reservations close tomorrow at 4PM" instead of restating the same static date three times.
- 3. Trim the catalog scroll — a reservation-deadline email should be 1-2 sections (the sneaker + FLX Fridays), not five unrelated categories that undercut the urgency.
- 4. Move FLX free-shipping messaging up near the hero CTA to reinforce the reservation action.
