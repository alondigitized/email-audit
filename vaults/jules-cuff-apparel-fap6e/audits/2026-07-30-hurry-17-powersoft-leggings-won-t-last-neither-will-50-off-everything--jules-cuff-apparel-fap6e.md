# Hurry — $17 PowerSoft leggings won't last (& neither will 50% OFF everything*!)
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-30**
## Executive summary

- Same purple 50% off everything banner, same "Haul Into Fall" — I could've screenshotted this from Tuesday's send and nobody would know the difference. This is the seventh-ish "50% off everything" email from Old Navy in under a week, and at this point I'm not even reading the banners, I'm scrolling past them looking for the one thing that's actually new. That thing turns out to be the $14/$17 PowerSoft leggings block and the $6/$8 activewear tops — those I'd actually stop for, because leggings and tees are my low-stakes restock items and a flat dollar price is easy math on my phone.
- The low-rise jean section further down is where it falls apart for me. Sketch-style illustrations of jeans, a "Why You Need Low Rise" headline, a quote from a denim designer about "short torsos" — this is not Old Navy's lane for me and it's not mine either. I'm the person who buys five-packs of leggings and matching family pajamas, not someone shopping a fashion trend deep-dive. I'd click the leggings tile if I were already annoyed enough to want $17 off my cart, then close the tab before I hit the jeans essay.
- Verdict: skim, maybe tap the leggings price, don't forward. This isn't a "share with a friend" email — it's background noise I've been trained to ignore because Old Navy sends it daily.

## What's working

- The $14/$17 PowerSoft leggings tile has a real photo, a real price, and reads instantly on mobile — no math required.
- $6 tops / $8 seamless activewear is a genuinely good price point for a stock-up buy, and it's segmented Girls vs. Women's so I know which is for me and which is for my kid.
- "TODAY ONLY" on the leggings creates real urgency, unlike the evergreen "50% off everything" banner that's been running all week.
- The top grid of $5-and-under basics (tees, tanks) is exactly the volume-shopping category I go to Old Navy for in the first place.

## What's weak

- The purple "50% OFF EVERYTHING! Even New Arrivals" banner is now the fourth or fifth time I've seen this exact graphic this week — I registered it as wallpaper, not an offer.
- The entire low-rise jeans section (sketches, "Two Leg Shapes, Unlimited Outfits," the torso quote) reads like a completely different, younger-skewing brand dropped into my Old Navy email — nothing here says mom-of-two uniform shopper.
- No kids' matching-set or family-pajama callout anywhere, which is the thing that actually gets me to click as a parent buying in volume for two kids.
- Zero loyalty/rewards mention even though I'm a Navyist member — no points balance, no member-exclusive tier, just generic "Encore Member" language at the very top that I almost missed.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the whole email with the $14/$17 leggings and $6/$8 tops deal — that's the only genuinely new, time-bound thing here, and it's currently buried below a repeated banner I've already tuned out.
- 2. Cut or drastically shrink the low-rise jeans editorial section — it's off-persona for a parent buying uniforms in bulk, and it's eating space that could go to a kids'/family bundle callout.
- 3. Add a Navyist rewards line near the top (points balance or "member pricing applied") — Hanna Andersson's welcome series earns loyalty trust by naming the 20% code outright; this email doesn't even nod at my member status.
- 4. Retire the purple "50% Off Everything" hero graphic for at least a week — I've now logged it in five separate sends and it's actively working against open rates for me.
