# Don't miss it — 50% off everything*, 60% off all shorts & swim* + $5 dresses
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-21**
## Executive summary

- Day five. I'm staring at the same sale I've been staring at since Tuesday and my brain has officially filed it under "noise." The 50% off everything, 60% off shorts and swim, $5 dresses — I could write this email myself at this point. I almost swiped it without opening, and honestly the only reason I didn't is because I have a conditioned Pavlovian response to the words "Old Navy" and "clearance" appearing together.
- Here's the thing, though: once I was actually inside, there's a "HOT! NEW! GEAR!" section lower down showing what looks like colorful backpacks and kids accessories, and *that* is the first genuinely new thing I've seen in this campaign. I have two kids who need to be outfitted before school starts, and if Old Navy had led with back-to-school gear instead of the fifteenth iteration of swim clearance, I'd have been much more engaged. The "SHOP CLEARANCE IN YOUR SIZE" CTA is also a smarter hook than the generic "SHOP NOW" — size-specific clearance actually reduces friction for me.
- Would I click? Probably, but only because I had already started a clearance cart on Monday and wanted to see if anything new dropped. I would not forward this to a friend. The campaign has burned out its own urgency.

## What's working

- **"SHOP CLEARANCE IN YOUR SIZE"** is a genuinely useful CTA — clearance by size is how I actually shop, and naming it directly tells me there's a filter waiting for me
- **The "HOT! NEW! GEAR!" section** is the freshest visual in five days; colorful kids items signal family shopping and that's my lane
- **Woman model in the casual green tee** reads like my weekend uniform — demographic alignment is solid, she doesn't look like she's going to a yacht club
- **Three stacked offers are all clearly legible** — no render bugs, hierarchy is clean, prices are easy to parse at a glance
- **"Summer's almost over"** urgency framing is at least honest seasonal reasoning vs. manufactured scarcity

## What's weak

- **This is send five of the exact same sale** — the cadence has destroyed any urgency the creative tried to build; "Don't miss it" rings hollow when I've "missed it" four times and the sale is still here
- **The subject line is 75 characters** — on my phone, everything after "50% off everything*" gets cut off, so the additional offers I'm supposedly here for don't even register at inbox level
- **The asterisks on "everything\*"** are doing a lot of heavy lifting and I've learned to read the fine print — it's never actually everything
- **Nothing for kids is called out above the fold** — for a brand that's my primary kids-clothing volume play, not mentioning kids until deep in the scroll is a missed opportunity for someone like me
- **No loyalty callout** — Hanna Andersson's welcome email opened with my discount code front and center; Old Navy knows I'm a repeat customer and treats me like a stranger every time

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead the subject with what's actually new** — the gear/back-to-school angle is the only fresh beat in this campaign and it should be driving opens, not buried below the fold
- 2. **Add a kids-specific hook above the fold** — a single sentence like "Kids clearance by size is live" would have me tapping immediately; I spend more on my kids at Old Navy than on myself
- 3. **Retire the 60% off swim creative** — it's appeared in every single email this week; any urgency it carried is gone and now it's just visual clutter
- 4. **Surface loyalty status or a cart-reminder** — something like "you left items in your cart" or "Navyist member pricing inside" would make me feel like a known customer instead of a cold lead
- 5. **Tighten the subject to under 50 characters** — everything past the first offer is invisible on mobile anyway
- **Subject Alt A:** `New gear dropped + clearance in your size`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Kids gear + 50% off — stock up before school`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Backpacks, basics, and clearance filtered to your size — shop before it's gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Back-to-school gear just landed. Plus 60% off swim while sizes last.`
