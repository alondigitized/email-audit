# Major news: $12 & under summer deals + 50% off everything else!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- I saw "Major news" in my inbox and thought — really? After the GASP email Saturday morning, the "(yes, really!)" double-tap Saturday afternoon, and the $6 tees emails from Friday? Old Navy has sent me a variation of this same stacked-discount story so many times this week that "Major news" reads as the email equivalent of crying wolf. I'm numb to it.
- That said, I opened it, and the email itself is actually cleaner than I expected. "TODAY ONLY!" lands first, "$12 & Under Summer Faves" lands second — that hierarchy is tight. But here's the thing: I shop Old Navy for my kids, and this email is almost wall-to-wall women's apparel. Dresses. Women's clearance by size. More dresses. There's not a single kids' item in sight, which is the whole reason I'm in this list. If Hanna Andersson sent me their welcome email (which I audited last week and gave an 8) and showed me nothing but adult loungewear, I'd feel the same way.
- I'll click, reluctantly, because $12 & under is the Old Navy trigger I'm trained to respond to and I'll hunt for kids' tees myself once I'm in. But I wouldn't forward this. A friend asking "are there any deals for the kids?" would get a "yeah but the email is all grown-up stuff" from me, which is not a great referral.

## What's working

- **"TODAY ONLY!" + "$12 & Under" hierarchy is crisp** — my eye lands on the offer in under a second, no hunting required.
- **Clean render** — no broken images, no overlapping text, layout holds on the narrow mobile view. More than I can say for some of the tangled Hanna Andersson emails I've seen.
- **"Shop Clearance In Your Size" section** — listing actual size buckets (XS through 4X) feels like they see me as a real-bodied woman, not a size 4 sample. That's a genuine win.
- **Summer timing is right** — the season is correct, the offer makes sense for a stock-up moment, and the dress hero is appropriate for May.
- **$12 price anchor is doing real work** — it's specific, concrete, and it's the exact dollar-number that makes me check what's in the grid.

## What's weak

- **Zero kids content in a kids-family email list** — Old Navy is my kids' uniform brand. This email could have been sent by any mass women's retailer. Hanna Andersson wouldn't do this to me; their emails stay laser-focused on who I bought from them last.
- **"Major news:" is bankrupt as a hook** — I've had four nearly identical emails from Old Navy in three days. "Major news" is the boy-who-cried-wolf subject opener right now.
- **The 50% Off section feels bolted on** — it's the same "50% Off Everything." block from Saturday's sends, just appended below the $12 hero. It doesn't feel curated; it feels like they ran two modules in the same wrapper.
- **"New Dresses Are Popping Up"** — I like a good dress but this framing is so generic. It's the same language Gap and Banana Republic use when they have nothing interesting to say. After the specificity of "$6 tees and $5 tanks" from last week's sends, this reads lazy.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Surface kids content in the first scroll** — a single kids' tees/bottoms module would flip this from "women's email I happened to receive" to "my Old Navy." Even a "Stock the kids' summer drawer: $12 & under" callout in the $12 hero would do it.
- 2. **Retire "Major news:" immediately** — this hook had maybe one use. After five near-identical sends this week, it lands as noise. Use urgency you've earned: "Today only" in the subject beats "Major news" any day.
- 3. **Consolidate the stacked offer into one clear headline** — "$12 & under + 50% off everything" is the actual sell. Stop splitting it into two separate hero modules that both fight for attention. One hero, two lines of copy.
- 4. **Give the dresses section a reason beyond "popping up"** — show the dress with a price. "$28 summer dresses, today only" beats a vague category tease every time.
- 5. **Add a loyalty/rewards balance callout** — I know I have ON Cash or points; showing my balance here would make the "TODAY ONLY" urgency land harder as a "use it or lose it" moment.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Today only: $12 & under + 50% off everything`
- **Subject Alt B:** `$12 summer faves for the whole family — today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Tees, tanks, dresses — kids included. Ends tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fill the summer drawer before this one expires.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[WARN] Missing alt text on CDP tracking pixel**
`<img src="https://cp.oldnavy.com/o/7665bff9-...">` has no `alt` attribute. Tracking pixels conventionally use `alt=""` (empty string); omitting the attribute entirely causes some screen readers to announce the full URL.

**[INFO] Duplicate CSS selector in media query**
`.MspaceTop` and `.MspaceBot` are each defined twice inside `@media only screen and (max-width: 639px)`. The inline comment in the source acknowledges this: `/* MspaceTop was duplicated, kept one */`. The second rule silently overrides the first; confirm the final padding values are intentional.

**[INFO] Outlook conditional `line-height:0px` on `td`**
The MSO conditional block sets `td { line-height:0px; mso-line-height-rule:exactly }`. This is a known Outlook height-fix pattern but can collapse text cells if a `td` lacks an explicit `mso-line-height-rule` override. No confirmed rendering breakage from the truncated source, but worth regression-testing in Outlook 2019/365.

**[INFO] Invalid `<meta>` inside `<div>` for schema.org**
```html
<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">
  <meta content="Old Navy" itemprop="name">
```
`<meta>` is not a valid child of `<div>` per HTML spec. Gmail's schema.org parser is tolerant of this pattern, but it may fail in stricter parsers.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header absent from relay capture**
The QA relay did not capture a `List-Unsubscribe` header. Gmail and Yahoo (as of Feb 2024 bulk sender requirements) require this header for >5k/day senders. May be a relay capture gap, but should be confirmed against raw SMTP headers.

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) absent**
One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender policy. Absence of this header means Gmail will not render its native one-click unsubscribe UI, degrading deliverability scores.

**[WARN] Physical mailing address not detected**
CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid physical postal address in the body. The HTML was truncated; if the footer containing the address was cut off, this may be a false positive. Verify the rendered footer includes a street address.

**[WARN] `Authentication-Results` header not found**
SPF/DKIM pass/fail status is unknown from this capture. `From` domain is `oldnavy.com`; sending domain is `email.oldnavy.com`. A DKIM alignment mismatch between these two domains would cause Gmail to show a "via" qualifier, reducing trust signals.
