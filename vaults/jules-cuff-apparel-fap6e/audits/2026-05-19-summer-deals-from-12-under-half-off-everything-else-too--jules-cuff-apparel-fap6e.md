# Summer deals from $12 & under + half off everything* else, too
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- I'm going to be honest: I hesitated before I even opened this one. Between the "GASP 😮" email Saturday morning, the "(yes, really!)" follow-up Saturday afternoon, the $6 tees sends from Friday, and yesterday's "Major news: $12 & under" — this is the fifth variation of the same stacked deal in four days. When I finally did open it, I got exactly what I expected: TODAY ONLY! at the top, the same $12 & under hero I've been staring at since Thursday, the 50% off everything block, and then a long scroll through clearance and dresses I've already mentally filed. The "TODAY ONLY!" urgency feels almost comical at this point because Old Navy has been running some version of this event since Friday and it keeps being "today only" again.
- That said — mechanically, the email works. The Clearance callout is loud and visual, the Dream Dresses under $35 section is actually a fresh hook I haven't seen in this run, and the layout is clean with zero broken images. If I weren't subscriber-fatigued, the clearance-plus-dresses combo would've been a quick tap to shop. But I am, so it wasn't.
- I'm going to mark this as read and maybe check the site tonight if I'm shopping anyway. I wouldn't forward it — Hanna Andersson's welcome email from two weeks ago I actually saved. This one I'm just trying to survive.

## What's working

- **"TODAY ONLY!" at the very top** — the urgency placement is right even if the credibility is shot after four days of the same deal.
- **$12 & under hero lands immediately** — the price point is at the top, no scrolling required, exactly how it should be on mobile.
- **Dream Dresses Under $35 is a genuinely new hook** in this campaign run — that section actually made me pause because I haven't seen it framed this way before.
- **Clearance repeated four times is loud and intentional** — feels very ON-brand Old Navy, almost playful, and it works as a visual signal that there's stuff to clear out.
- **Zero render bugs** — clean layout, real product images, no broken tiles or lorem ipsum anywhere.

## What's weak

- **The "TODAY ONLY!" credibility is completely gone.** Old Navy ran "today only" effectively the same deal on May 16, 17, 18, and now 19. At this point it's ambient noise.
- **No kids or family section visible** — the entire email is adult women's dresses, clearance tops, and sandals. I'm here for the whole family, and there's not a single kids' signal in this scroll.
- **The email is exhaustingly long** — four full sections competing for attention: $12 hero, 50% off, clearance, dream dresses, sandals, new dresses. Every section is "the important one."
- **"Summer deals from… else, too" subject line is doing a lot of heavy lifting with weak words** — "else, too" is the kind of copy that sounds like someone typed it at 11pm to avoid duplicating yesterday's subject line.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill the daily resend. Consolidate.** The actual single-biggest lift here is campaign discipline — three sends of the same deal in 72 hours trains me to ignore the fourth. If the TODAY ONLY deal has to extend, change the hook (new category, new price tier, new product) not just the subject line.
- 2. **Put one kids/family callout in the hero or at section two.** I am subscribed to Old Navy because I outfit two kids. If you know that (and you do — I've ordered kids' sizes for years), show me the $12 under kids' tees next to the women's row.
- 3. **Cut the email by half.** Pick three sections max: the hero deal, clearance, and one featured category. Right now it's a scroll maze.
- 4. **Move time-bounded urgency into the subject line**, not just the email body. "TODAY ONLY" sitting in the hero helps me once I've opened, but it doesn't make me open.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Today only: $12 & under + 50% off (for real this time)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Clearance just dropped. $12 & under, today only.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Kids, adults, dresses, sandals — everything's in the mix.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Clearance is deep and the dresses are under $35. Go.`

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**[FAIL] Broken consent-platform redirect**
The Cheetah Digital consent pixel (`cp.oldnavy.com/o/daf3d99c-c730-4678-addf-8c259244e2d1?...`) resolves to a redirect that the QA crawler cannot follow — it returns an unknown URL type: `/g/daf3d99c-c730-4678-addf-8c259244e2d1?txn=5d091380-5324-11f1-b5b1-4e61e507eb6e`. This is a relative-path redirect issued by `cp.oldnavy.com` that lacks a host, making it unresolvable outside the browser. Likely benign for end users (browsers handle it), but signals a misconfigured redirect on the consent platform side that should be confirmed with the Cheetah/CP team.

**[INFO] 23 click-tracking links skipped**
All CTAs route through `click.email.oldnavy.com`. Not an error, but means destination URL health is unverified by automated checks.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[WARN] Missing `alt` text on consent/tracking pixel**
The `cp.oldnavy.com` tracking pixel at the top of the email has no `alt` attribute:
```html
<img src="https://cp.oldnavy.com/o/daf3d99c-..." width="1" height="1">
```
For a 1×1 pixel, `alt=""` should be explicitly set. Some screen readers will announce the full `src` URL when `alt` is absent.

**[INFO] VML/Office namespace declarations present** — correct for Outlook 2013+ rendering. MSO conditional comments are well-formed.

**[INFO] Duplicate CSS class selectors** — `.MspaceTop` and `.MspaceBot` each appear twice in the responsive block with different property values. The second declaration wins; this is harmless but indicates copy-paste debt.

No issues found with charset declaration (`UTF-8`), viewport meta, or IE-compat meta.

### 5. Compliance

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header not confirmed**
The AgentMail relay did not capture a `List-Unsubscribe` header. Gmail and Yahoo require this for bulk senders. If the header is present at the MTA level but stripped by the relay, this is a monitoring gap — confirm directly against raw SMTP headers. If absent, this is a deliverability and compliance defect.

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not confirmed**
One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender policy (effective Feb 2024). Same caveat as above — verify at MTA level.

**[WARN] Physical mailing address not detected by automated scan**
CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid physical postal address. The truncated HTML does not include footer content, so this may be a scan artifact. Must be confirmed against the full email footer before flagging as a real defect. If absent, it is a CAN-SPAM violation.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All CTA links route through `click.email.oldnavy.com` redirects, which are not followed by the QA crawler, so UTM parameter presence on landing page URLs is unverified. Standard Gap Inc. practice is to append `?icid=` parameters; this should be spot-checked manually for 2–3 CTAs against the resolved destination URLs to confirm UTM/ICID attribution is intact.

No issues found with the sender domain alignment (`oldnavy.com` → `email.oldnavy.com` → `click.email.oldnavy.com` — consistent subdomain chain).
