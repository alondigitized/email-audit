# 📲 Move that thumb! You have $6 tees and Wow jeans are 50% off

- That subject line made me stop scrolling for a second — "$6 tees" is basically the Bat-Signal for anyone stocking a school-week drawer. But then I open and the first big section is a couple in Wow jeans. ## What's working

- **TODAY ONLY tees at $4 and $6** — first section, clear price, hard deadline. This is the only reason I'm clicking and it earns its place above the fold.
- **Back-to-School $5/$8/$12 price ladder** — the tiered pricing at the bottom is exactly how I shop: pick a price tier, grab multiples. Smart structure, just in the wrong position.
- **Clearance from $3.99 banner** — the bright yellow makes it pop and signals genuine markdown depth, not just a sale-page redirect.
- **Clean render** — no broken images, no overlapping text, everything loaded correctly on mobile.
- **TODAY ONLY urgency** — credible, specific, and a welcome change from the rolling "limited time" language that's been in every other Old Navy send this week.

## What's weak

- **Wow jeans as the hero** — I'm in back-to-school mode and the above-fold real estate went to adult couple in denim. Misread the room.
- **Run shorts section** — a completely separate audience. After yesterday's clean kids-first send scored an 8, this splice feels like two emails got merged at the last minute.
- **Four-plus promos competing for hierarchy** — tees, jeans, clearance, run shorts, back-to-school. The eye doesn't know where to land, and neither does my wallet.
- **No family/kids signal in the hero** — Hanna Andersson put kids front and center from the first pixel of their welcome email. Old Navy hid the back-to-school angle below the scroll break.
- **Subject line is too long** — over 60 characters clips on most phones, and it's trying to sell two things at once.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Move back-to-school to the hero** — if you have a $5/$8/$12 kids section in late July, that is the email. The Wow jeans couple can slot in below; they're not going anywhere.
- 2. **Cut the run shorts or send separately** — it dilutes the urgency and signals Old Navy didn't know who they were emailing. The July 17 dress repeat was the same mistake.
- 3. **Tighten the subject to one offer** — at 63 characters it clips on mobile and asks the inbox to process two separate products simultaneously.
- **Subject Alt A:** `TODAY ONLY: Tees from $4 + back-to-school starts now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `$6 tees + $5/$8/$12 back-to-school — today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Wow jeans 50% off too — but the tees go first`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stock the whole closet while the TODAY ONLY price holds`
- 4. **Give back-to-school section its own CTA to the kids landing page** — not a generic "shop now" that drops me at the homepage and makes me filter back.

## Technical Audit — Old Navy "Move that thumb!" Email

### 1. Technical Summary
One confirmed broken redirect in a Cordial/cp.oldnavy.com URL causes an invalid resolution path; compliance headers are absent or unverifiable at the relay layer. Otherwise the HTML structure is standard legacy-email XHTML with no critical rendering failures.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**[FAIL] Broken redirect — cp.oldnavy.com tracking URL**
The Cordial engagement-tracking pixel resolves to an invalid relative path:
```
Error: unknown url type: '/g/0bb8938d-fd88-46bb-8a4b-b76185b85524?txn=84c14880-8447-11f1-bdc0-7685877ed2a2'
Source URL: https://cp.oldnavy.com/o/0bb8938d-fd88-46bb-8a4b-b76185b85524?mi_mid=...&cp_cid=1bc829e71098f268
```
The `/o/` endpoint is returning a redirect to `/g/` with a `txn` parameter that the checker cannot follow — likely a Cordial open-tracking redirect misconfiguration or a dead transaction ID. This pixel appears twice in the `display:none` preheader block.

**[WARN] 23 click-tracking links unverified**
All CTAs route through `click.email.oldnavy.com`. HTTP probing was skipped; destination URLs and UTM state are unconfirmed.

**Open pixel** (`click.email.oldnavy.com/open.aspx?6XXNVUSU46CEXLBTJ3MIN4K6AM.60269`) — structurally valid, not probed.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[WARN] Missing alt text on tracking pixel**
```html
<img src="https://cp.oldnavy.com/o/0bb8938d-fd88-46bb-8a4b-b76185b85524?..." width="1" height="1">
```
No `alt` attribute present. Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` explicitly to satisfy strict XHTML 1.0 Transitional validation and suppress screen-reader noise.

**[INFO] Duplicate CSS class definitions**
`.MspaceTop` and `.MspaceBot` are each defined twice in the responsive block (the source even comments on the duplication). Last-write wins, so behavior is correct, but the dead declarations add noise and complicate future edits.

**[INFO] `td { line-height: 0px }` Outlook conditional**
The MSO-conditional style sets `line-height: 0px; mso-line-height-rule: exactly` on all `td` elements. This is a known Outlook height fix but can clip single-line cells if any content `td` lacks an explicit line-height override.

No missing responsive breakpoint issues detected in the truncated CSS; `@media (max-width: 639px)` covers the standard mobile stack.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens visible in the provided HTML. `mi_mid` and `mi_cid` parameters in the Cordial URL are populated with live values (`019f7f2e-c780-7000-...`, `67859151839b71d0`), indicating the send-time token substitution completed.

No issues found in the visible portion.

### 5. Compliance

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header absent at relay**
QA could not confirm the header via AgentMail. Gmail and Yahoo enforce one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058 `List-Unsubscribe-Post`) for bulk senders; absence at the relay layer may mean the header is stripped or was never injected by the ESP.

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent**
Required for one-click unsubscribe compliance under Gmail/Yahoo 2024 bulk sender rules. Must accompany a `List-Unsubscribe: <https://...>` mailto-less header.

**[WARN] No physical mailing address detected**
CAN-SPAM §7 requires a valid physical postal address in the message body. The truncated HTML ends before the footer — if a Gap Inc. address block exists below the fold this may be a false positive, but it must be confirmed.

**[INFO] `Authentication-Results` header absent**
SPF/DKIM pass/fail status is unknown at the relay. `oldnavy@email.oldnavy.com` should be covered by Gap Inc.'s SPF record and DKIM signing on `email.oldnavy.com`, but relay interception prevents verification here.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All 23 CTAs pass through `click.email.oldnavy.com` — the click-redirect layer is responsible for appending UTM parameters before forwarding to `oldnavy.com`. Because probing was skipped, UTM injection cannot be confirmed from this audit. Known risk: if the redirect chain omits `utm_source` / `utm_campaign`, site analytics will attribute sessions to direct rather than email.

The Cordial `cp_tp` payload (base64 + deflate-encoded) carries campaign context internally but is not a substitute for URL-level UTM parameters visible to GA4/Adobe.
