# Today only: 50% off everything* in women's, accessories included
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-29**
## Executive summary

- Okay. Twelfth Old Navy email in roughly two weeks — I'm actually keeping count now because it's become a minor hobby. And yet I'll admit: this one is the most targeted they've managed since the $3 tanks countdown on May 25th. Women's styles plus accessories, one day, clear offer. For someone who buys for herself *and* the family, a women's-specific frame is actually a signal I respond to — it's not the usual "whole family + flag tees in-store" dragnet.
- What genuinely caught my eye was the "Shop Clearance in Your Size" grid below the hero. That's the first time all campaign this brand has treated me like a person with an actual size rather than just a body with a credit card. The Kids & Baby Sale section is useful context for me too — I'm not just shopping for myself. The $10 shorts callout at the bottom is a specific, honest price anchor.
- But the asterisk on "everything*" is doing a lot of quiet damage. Old Navy exclusions are famously comprehensive — jeans, denim, anything you actually wanted probably has a small asterisk next to it. Hanna Andersson offered 20% off in their welcome email, called it exactly that, and it felt earned. Old Navy says "everything*" and I'm already bracing for the fine print. I'd click through, probably buy something from the clearance grid, and not forward this to anyone who doesn't already have this brand in their inbox daily.

## What's working

- **"Shop Clearance in Your Size" grid** is the single smartest thing in this email — it's actionable, browse-friction-reducing, and actually personal. First time this campaign run I felt like Old Navy remembered I have a specific size.
- **Women's + accessories in one ask** matches how I actually shop for myself — outfit thinking, not category thinking.
- **"TODAY ONLY!" hero** is visually immediate; my eye went to the offer before anything else. Hierarchy is doing its job.
- **Kids & Baby Sale section** is relevant for a family shopper without hijacking the lead message — right placement, right proportion.
- **$10 shorts callout** at the bottom is a specific, honest price point for activewear basics — the kind of thing I'll actually buy multiples of.

## What's weak

- **The asterisk on "everything*"** is the subject line doing a soft bait-and-switch. If jeans, denim, or Old Navy Money are excluded, say so. Hanna Andersson would never write "everything*" and expect trust to survive it.
- **Twelfth email, same urgency architecture.** "Today only" stopped meaning anything around email eight. The May 25th countdown email used a literal clock emoji and it worked — this version is the same lever pull with less theater.
- **No loyalty callout anywhere I can see.** I have an Old Navy account. Hanna Andersson put the member relationship front and center in their first email to me; Old Navy is treating me like a cold lead after twelve sends.
- **Models read as generic catalog, not millennial mom.** Nothing in the visual says "this is for someone doing school pickup and a dinner out on the same day."

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead the hero with the size-clearance frame.** "50% off in your size, today only" is more personal than a blanket women's banner and uses the grid that's already in the email.
- 2. **Kill the asterisk in the subject or immediately qualify it.** "50% off most women's styles" is honest. "Everything*" sets up a gotcha I'll remember next time I almost don't open.
- 3. **Add one NavyCard/rewards line.** Even a badge or a single sentence — it signals you know I'm a repeat customer, not a stranger.
- 4. **Rest the cadence.** If the next send is three days from now, "today only" means something again.
- 5. **Update model direction.** Real styling for real life — not just wide-leg jeans on a 24-year-old in a studio.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Today only: 50% off women's (your size is in stock)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `50% off women's + accessories — one day, no extensions`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Clearance in your size + $10 shorts — today only`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop the whole outfit for less, accessories included`
