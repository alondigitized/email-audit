# $6 tees & tanks are going FAST
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-13**
## Executive summary

- Twenty-seven. Maybe twenty-eight. This is Old Navy's second blast today — same $4 and $6 hero, same tees-and-tanks angle, just with "going FAST" bolted onto the end of the subject line. Classic send-to-non-openers urgency bump, and honestly I understand the tactic. I just wish I couldn't see the seams. If I missed the morning send and this one lands cold, the deal lands: the "2 DAYS ONLY! $4 & $6" hero is big, readable, and speaks exactly to the semi-annual stockpile mentality I'm fully on board with. But I didn't miss the morning send. I was in it.
- The email itself renders cleanly. The mustard wide-leg lifestyle shot is aspirational without being alien to my life. The multi-section layout below the fold covers clearance, summer pricing, and accessories — more than I need, but at least it's browsable. The problem is none of this feels like it was sent to me, specifically. It was fired at a list. Hanna Andersson's welcome email — scored an 8 when I reviewed it — felt like a brand handing me something. This feels like Old Navy clearing inventory before the weekend.
- I'll mark it read. If I hadn't already opened the morning send, I'd have clicked straight to the tees grid. That's the honest answer.

## What's working

- **$4 & $6 pricing is the whole point and it's front and center** — massive, unambiguous, and priced exactly where I stockpile basics for the family.
- **"2 DAYS ONLY!" creates a real window** — specific deadline, not just "limited time," which is what Old Navy needed after burning through every synonym for "top-rated."
- **Summer lifestyle imagery is cohesive and current** — the mustard pants shot feels like a real person's Instagram, not a catalog page.
- **Clean render throughout** — no broken images, no overlapping text, layout holds on what appears to be a mobile-optimized view.
- **Clearance callout at the bottom is a legitimate pull** — for a deals-first shopper, knowing clearance is right there removes one search step.

## What's weak

- **Same-day double send is the cardinal sin here** — I already opened "👕 $6 TEES & TANKS" this morning. Sending version B to my same inbox the same afternoon doesn't feel urgent; it feels like my inbox is being used as a testing surface.
- **Zero family or kids signal in the visible hero** — I'm buying tees for two kids and a husband. The women's-only lifestyle imagery never tells me whether the $4 price extends to the kids section, which is actually where I'd spend the most.
- **"Going FAST" is soft urgency with no data behind it** — after "Yes, really," "Top-rated for a reason," and "Customer faves," Old Navy's tone tricks have stopped landing. Give me a number: colors selling out, sizes going, something real.
- **No NavyCard or loyalty hook visible** — Hanna Andersson led its welcome email with a code within the first scroll. Old Navy hasn't surfaced member benefits in any of the last several sends I've reviewed.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Build suppression logic for same-day openers** — anyone who opened the morning blast should not receive this. Or if they must, change the hero offer: add a "plus kids tees from $4" callout that morning-openers haven't seen.
- 2. **Put a kids/family line in the hero copy** — one sentence: "Family tees too — $4 across kids, adults & baby." That's the segmentation signal that turns a blast into a targeted send for me.
- 3. **Replace "going FAST" with a real inventory signal** — "Sizes selling out — shop by Sunday night" or "Popular colors nearly gone" is concrete urgency that earns the click instead of manufacturing it.
- 4. **Add a loyalty stripe** — even a thin NavyCash banner between the hero and the lifestyle shot would make member subscribers feel like the cadence is working for them, not against them.
- **Subject Alt A:** `$4 kids + adult tees: 2 days left to stock up`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Sizes going fast — $4 & $6 tees, today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Kids, adults & baby all included. Shop before Sunday night.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Select colors nearly gone in XS–XL. NavyCard holders: check your cash.`
