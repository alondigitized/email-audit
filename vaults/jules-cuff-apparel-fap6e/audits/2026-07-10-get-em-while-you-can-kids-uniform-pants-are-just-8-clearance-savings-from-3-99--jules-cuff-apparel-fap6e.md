# Get 'em while you can — kids uniform pants are just $8 + Clearance savings from $3.99
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-10**
## Executive summary

- Look, I almost didn't even open this because yesterday's send was *also* $8 uniform pants plus clearance — and the subject line reads almost word-for-word the same to my eye. I had to do a double take to confirm this wasn't the July 9th email still sitting in my inbox. That said, once I'm in, the hero delivers: "2 DAYS ONLY!" in big bold type with uniform pants and a tight countdown is exactly what I need when I'm mentally building the back-to-school cart right now. The clearance from $3.99 underneath is a nice pull for a volume-shopper like me.
- But then the email goes sideways. Below the BTS section there's a "NEW TREND" block that looks like it belongs in a teen influencer mood board — pink puffer vibes, adult-forward denim at $40 and $36, lifestyle imagery that has nothing to do with getting three kids into khakis by September. It's the Old Navy identity crisis playing out in real time: great BTS opener, then the brand pivots to trying to dress me like a Millennial OOTD account.
- Mark as read? Probably not — I'd actually click through for the uniform pants because the urgency is credible and I know the $8 price point is real. But forward to a friend? No. This is the same email I *already* told my sister about yesterday.

## What's working

- **"2 DAYS ONLY!" hero is loud and believable** — the urgency doesn't feel manufactured, it matches the back-to-school timeline
- **$8 uniform pants front and center** — the product and price are visible before any scroll; no hunt required
- **Clearance from $3.99 is a strong secondary hook** — stacks well on top of the hero for a volume buyer, feels like a bonus rather than a distraction
- **Layout is clean, no render bugs** — product photography is sharp, text isn't overlapping anything, CTA buttons appear consistently placed
- **"Extra 30% Off Clearance" reinforces the value thesis** at the bottom without burying it in fine print

## What's weak

- **This is essentially yesterday's email** — the July 9th send featured the exact same $8 uniform pants + clearance combo; running nearly identical creative two days in a row kills the urgency "2 DAYS ONLY!" is trying to manufacture
- **The adult denim/trend section is dead weight for me** — $40 "The Late Blue Laws" jeans and a pink fashion block read as a different brand's email got spliced in mid-scroll
- **"NEW TREND TICK" (whatever that section is) actively confuses the hierarchy** — I'm here for uniform pants and clearance, not a teen style moment
- **Subject line is 82 characters** — way too long for mobile; the "$3.99" part almost certainly gets chopped before most people see it

## Recommendations

- 1. **Don't send the same hero two days in a row.** If this is genuinely a 2-day countdown, make the second day *feel* like a countdown — tighten copy to "24 hours left" or "Final day," not a repeat of the opener. Hanna Andersson would never recycle a hero like this; their sends feel considered even when they're promoting the same stripe.
- 2. **Cut the adult denim/trend section entirely from this send.** Segmentation fix, not a layout fix — if Old Navy has my kids' uniform shopping history, this block should not appear in a BTS send. Save the trendy teen pink block for a separate campaign.
- 3. **Lead with the clearance floor** in the preheader to complement the subject, not echo it.
- 4. **Lift the $3.99 clearance headline visually** — make it a callout badge or price-forward block, not a secondary section that competes with the fashion content below.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last 2 days: $8 uniform pants + clearance from $3.99`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Final call — $8 uniform pants before they're gone`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stock up now before the BTS rush hits — sizes going fast`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Grab extras while clearance is still from $3.99`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[WARN] Missing alt text on content image**
The image served from `https://cp.oldnavy.com/o/34c398b7...` has no `alt` attribute. This image appears to be a dynamic/personalized asset — missing alt text degrades the experience when images are blocked (common in Outlook by default) and fails WCAG 2.1 AA.

**[INFO] Duplicate CSS class selectors**
`.MspaceTop` and `.MspaceBot` are each defined twice in the mobile `@media` block. The comment in source acknowledges this (`/* MspaceTop was duplicated, kept one */`). While browsers handle duplicate rules gracefully, it indicates template drift and should be cleaned up.

**[INFO] Outlook line-height zero override**
The MSO conditional block sets `td { line-height: 0px; mso-line-height-rule: exactly }` globally. This is a known Outlook fix but can suppress whitespace in unexpected ways if any `td` lacks an explicit `line-height` override.

No issues with charset (`UTF-8`), viewport meta, or IE compatibility shims.

### 5. Compliance

**[FAIL] List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post headers absent**
Neither `List-Unsubscribe` nor `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers were captured. Gmail and Yahoo have required one-click unsubscribe (`List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`) for bulk senders since February 2024. Absence increases spam classification risk and violates platform sender policies. Note: the QA checker acknowledges these may not be captured through the AgentMail relay — confirm directly against raw SMTP headers before treating as definitive.

**[WARN] Physical mailing address not detected**
CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid physical postal address in every commercial email. The HTML source provided is truncated — verify the address appears in the footer of the full template. If absent, this is a CAN-SPAM violation.

**[WARN] SPF/DKIM authentication status unknown**
`Authentication-Results` header was not captured by the relay. Sending domain is `email.oldnavy.com` (subdomain of `oldnavy.com`). Cannot confirm DMARC alignment without raw headers. Verify SPF pass and DKIM signature on `email.oldnavy.com` independently.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**No UTM parameters visible**
All click tracking is handled through SFMC's own redirect chain (`click.email.oldnavy.com` → destination). UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) are not visible in the pre-redirect URLs in the truncated source. If SFMC is appending UTMs at the redirect layer, confirm this is configured — otherwise GA4/analytics will attribute resulting sessions as direct traffic.
