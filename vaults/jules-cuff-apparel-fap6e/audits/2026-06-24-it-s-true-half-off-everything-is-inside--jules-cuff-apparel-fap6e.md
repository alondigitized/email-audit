# IT'S TRUE! HALF OFF everything* is inside
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-24**
## Executive summary

- Day eight. I did not count on purpose — my brain just did it automatically at this point. The subject line came in with the energy of someone trying to convince me they have exciting news, but I already know the news because the news has been the same news since June 16. "IT'S TRUE!" is doing a lot of heavy lifting for a fact I have internalized to the point where I could recite it in my sleep. The all-caps is giving me late-night infomercial, not semi-annual sale from a brand I actually like.
- That said, I did open it — because Old Navy's semi-annual is legitimately the one sale I plan my fall basics haul around, and "2 DAYS ONLY" in the body finally tells me something I didn't know: the clock is actually ticking now. The FormTech launch buried in the hero felt like a brand pivot dressed in sale clothes. I came for the kids clearance section at the bottom and the "TIME TO CHILL" loungewear — those are the Old Navy I'm here for.
- I would not click through from the top. I'd scroll to clearance, look at the baby section, and maybe tap the chill basics. I would not forward this. If a friend asked me if Old Navy was running a sale, I'd say yes, but I'm not volunteering it unprompted.

## What's working

- **"2 DAYS ONLY" urgency block** — this is the first new information in the whole campaign streak and it finally gave me a reason to think about *when*, not just *whether*
- **Baby and kids section visible mid-scroll** — as a family-first Old Navy shopper, seeing that content tells me the email is at least trying to cover my actual use case
- **"TIME TO CHILL" section** — the lounge/easy-summer-basics angle is exactly why I keep coming back to Old Navy between Hanna Andersson runs; it's the quiet competency
- **50% off banner locked at the top** — no confusion about what the sale is; the hierarchy is doing what it's supposed to
- **Clearance-on-clearance at the bottom** — "Go, Go, Go! Clearance" is exactly the chaos energy that makes the semi-annual feel like an event

## What's weak

- **"IT'S TRUE!" in all caps is a spam-brain trigger** — the minute I see that in my inbox I'm already half-checked-out before I even open; Hanna Andersson's welcome email opened with warmth, not a shout
- **FormTech as the hero feels like a category mismatch for me** — I'm not coming to Old Navy for activewear tech; I'm coming for the six-pack of jersey knit tees and the baby basics; leading with performance gear pushes me past the fold before I hit what I actually want
- **"Discover More" is a dead CTA** — every section has it, none of them earn it; compare this to how Hanna structures a single focused CTA per section with actual specificity ("Shop Girls' Stripes")
- **The asterisk on "everything\*" is doing quiet damage at this point** — eight days of "everything\*" with exclusions I've never been able to predict has eroded whatever trust the word "everything" used to carry

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the countdown, not the offer** — "2 days left" should be the subject, not body content buried mid-email; I already know about the 50% off, I need a reason to act *today*
- 2. **Kill "IT'S TRUE!" entirely** — it reads like a spam filter test; replace with something that respects the eight-day attention I've already given
- 3. **Move FormTech out of the hero slot** — put the clearance-level urgency or the kids section first; that's where my money actually goes at Old Navy
- 4. **Give the CTAs verbs that mean something** — "Shop Clearance Now," "Grab Baby Basics," "Stock Up on Tees" — anything with a noun attached
- 5. **Acknowledge the send cadence** — Hanna's welcome email felt like it was talking *to* me; a "last chance" acknowledgment ("We know you've been watching — this is the final push") would feel honest after eight days
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last 2 days: 50% off everything — kids, clearance, all of it`

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Clearance-on-clearance + baby basics — this is the good stuff`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `2 days to grab the keepers before they're gone`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Duplicate CSS class selectors**
`.MspaceTop` and `.MspaceBot` are each defined twice in the `@media` block (lines covering both `space-top5` and `space-top10`, same for bottom). The second declaration silently overwrites the first. The comment in source acknowledges this: `/* MspaceTop was duplicated, kept one */` — but the duplicate was not actually removed. Last-write wins in CSS, so `space-top5` and `space-top10` both resolve to `padding-top: 10px`.

**Outlook conditional stylesheet**
```css
<!--[if gte mso 9]>
td { line-height: 0px; mso-line-height-rule: exactly }
```
Setting `line-height: 0px` globally on all `td` elements in Outlook 2013+ will collapse any text-only cells with no explicit height. This is a known Outlook rendering pitfall — should be scoped to layout-spacer cells only, not a blanket `td` reset.

**Missing alt text on tracking pixel**
The CP pixel `src=https://cp.oldnavy.com/o/5f68e675...` has no `alt` attribute. For a 1×1 tracking image `alt=""` is acceptable per WCAG; the absence of the attribute entirely (rather than `alt=""`) may trigger strict accessibility validators.

### 5. Compliance

**[WARN] List-Unsubscribe header absent**
Neither `List-Unsubscribe` nor `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers were captured by the relay. Gmail and Apple Mail use these headers to render the native unsubscribe button. Their absence increases spam classification risk, and Google's February 2024 bulk sender requirements mandate one-click unsubscribe for senders exceeding 5k/day to Gmail.

Note: the QA tool flags these as "may not be captured by AgentMail relay" — headers may exist at SMTP level but not be forwarded. Verify against raw `.eml` headers before treating as a hard failure.

**[WARN] No physical mailing address detected**
CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid physical postal address in the email body. Not visible in the truncated source — confirm it exists in the footer of the full HTML. If absent, this is a statutory violation.

**[WARN] Authentication-Results header not found**
SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown. Sender domain `email.oldnavy.com` is a dedicated ESP subdomain; DMARC alignment requires DKIM signing with a `@oldnavy.com` or `@email.oldnavy.com` `d=` value, or SPF alignment via `oldnavy.com` envelope sender. Cannot confirm without raw headers.
