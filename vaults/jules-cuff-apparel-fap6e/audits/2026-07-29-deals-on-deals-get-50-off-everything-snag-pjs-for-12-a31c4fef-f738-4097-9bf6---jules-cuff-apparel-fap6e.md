# Deals on deals! Get 50% off everything* + snag PJs for $12
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-29**
## Executive summary

- Old Navy again. This is what, the sixth "50% off everything" email in five days? I opened this one mostly out of morbid curiosity to see what new wrapper they'd put on the same promo, and yep — "Today Only!" Palomas at $8/$10/$12 up top, then the same purple "50% OFF EVERYTHING" banner I've seen on repeat since 7/24. At least this time there's real product density below the fold — jeans, kids fleece, the baby one-piece — which is more than some of their recent sends managed.
- The Kids Favorite Fleece and Baby Ribbed One-Piece blocks are the only parts that made me pause, because that's literally my job as the family shopper. But by the time I scrolled past yet another purple "50% OFF EVERYTHING" banner mid-email, my eyes had glazed over. Hanna Andersson's welcome email felt like it was talking to me; this feels like it's talking at everyone, five times a week.
- Verdict: I'm not clicking. I've clicked a version of this before and it's the same site-wide sale I can hit anytime this week. Not forwarding — my sister's already rolling her eyes at how much Old Navy mail I get.

## What's working

- The kids fleece and baby one-piece sections are genuinely in my lane — I buy exactly this stuff for exactly this reason (durability, family sizing).
- "3 Activewear Names To Know" breaks up the sale monotony with product-specific content instead of another discount banner.
- Price points are dead simple — $8, $10, $12, $13, $15 — no math required, which I appreciate when I'm scrolling one-handed with a kid on my hip.
- Clearance "FROM $3.99" at the bottom is a real, distinct offer, not just a rehash of the 50% banner.

## What's weak

- Three separate "50% OFF EVERYTHING" purple banners in one email — I counted them. That's not urgency, that's just filling space.
- "TODAY ONLY!" at the top contradicts the "become an Elavate member to get free shipping this order or first order, applicable again" fine print I can barely read — small, gray, crammed under the header.
- No kids-specific hero, no "matching family" callout despite having both kids and baby product below — feels like three separate emails stapled together, not one thought-through send.
- I've now seen this exact 50%-off-everything promise so many times this week the "Deals on deals" subject just reads as more of the same, not new news.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the redundant purple banners down to one — mid-email, right before the sale picks — and give the real estate back to product. Three copies of the same graphic is the single biggest waste in this email.
- 2. Lead with a kids/family hero if you're going to feature kids fleece and baby onesies this heavily — I open Hanna Andersson emails specifically because they lead with "matching" and "keepers," and Old Navy has the inventory to do that here and just... doesn't.
- 3. Fix the top fine print — either make the membership/shipping terms legible or cut them; right now they read like an afterthought nobody proofed.
- 4. Vary the subject line language — I've mentally tuned out "50% off everything" from Old Navy. Try leading with the $12 PJs or the kids fleece instead, since that's the part of this email that's actually differentiated.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Kids fleece from $15, baby onesies from $8 — today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `$12 PJs just dropped (plus the sale you already knew about)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New activewear, kids favorites, and clearance from $3.99 — all today`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Low-rise jeans, cozy fleece, $8 onesies. One email, no repeats.`
