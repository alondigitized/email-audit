# $5 TEES (Yes, $5!) + 40-70% OFF DEEP END DEALS
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-04**
## Executive summary

- Day eight — or whatever we're calling this — of the Old Navy Summer Grind, and I open my inbox on the Fourth of July to find... a $5 tee email. Again. Literally yesterday was a $5 tee email. The "(Yes, $5!)" in the subject isn't playful — it's Old Navy winking at me like they know I've seen this bit before. I appreciate the self-awareness, but it doesn't make the repeat feel any less like they're recycling the creative.
- That said, the email itself actually has more going on than yesterday's. The "DEEP END DEALS 40-70% off" second section adds real range, and the child model in the hero hits me where I live — I'm shopping for two kids plus myself, and a $5 tee with a color swatch row is genuinely useful. The "3 DAYS ONLY" urgency on a holiday weekend is smart calendar work. The ALL-DAY STRETCH / POWERSOFT section at the bottom is a gear shift that loses me a little — by the time I scroll that far, I've already made my decision.
- I'd click the $5 tees row. I would not forward this. The Hanna Andersson welcome email from May made me want to share it with my mom group. This one I'm using as a shopping utility.

## What's working

- **Child model in the hero** — a girl in a pink tee as the lead image is basically a direct signal to me that this is family-stocking territory. Hanna would never lead with a kid in a $5 anything, but for Old Navy this is exactly right.
- **$5 tees + color swatch row** — the variety strip below the hero does the work of telling me this isn't one-color-take-it-or-leave-it. Useful at a glance.
- **"3 DAYS ONLY" urgency on July 4th weekend** — anchoring the clock to a holiday when families are together and thinking about summer shopping is a smart placement.
- **"DEEP END DEALS starting at $2"** adds meaningful depth below the hero — it's not just a tee pitch, it's a discovery send, which makes the email feel worth scrolling.
- **No visible render bugs** — email lays out cleanly, hierarchy is readable even at thumbnail size.

## What's weak

- **Repeat of yesterday's lead** — "Tees are $5 (and going fast!)" was July 3rd. "$5 TEES (Yes, $5!)" is July 4th. The "(Yes, $5!)" parenthetical even telegraphs they know they're saying the same thing twice. That erodes urgency — if they're still $5 today, they'll probably be $5 tomorrow too.
- **ALL-DAY STRETCH / POWERSOFT section is a tonal detour** — I'm in deal-stacking mode by the time I hit the activewear block. That's a different purchase mindset and it splits the email's energy.
- **"Discover More" section at the bottom is vague** — after two strong deal blocks, an unanchored "discover more" CTA is where momentum goes to die. Hanna's welcome email landed a single tight CTA and I remembered it weeks later. This one scatters.
- **No family/matching-set callout** — for a holiday weekend, a "coordinate the crew" frame would have made this feel like it was written for me. The deep deals section just runs individual SKUs, no family-set thinking.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Retire the $5 tee lead — or at least dress it differently.** The offer is still good, but two identical leads in a row kills urgency credibility. Lead with "4th of July weekend = 3 days to stock up" framing and let the $5 tees be part of a family stack, not a solo hero. Show a mom + kid in matching colors.
- 2. **Cut the ALL-DAY STRETCH section or move it to its own send.** Activewear is a different shopping mode; it's diluting the deal-stacking energy that the first two sections built.
- 3. **Add a "build the family haul" bundle callout.** "$5 tees × 3 kids = $15 for the whole crew" — that math is exactly why I open semi-annual emails and it's nowhere in this email.
- 4. **Sharpen the "Discover More" CTA.** Make it "Shop All $5 Finds" or "Grab Your Colors Before They're Gone" — something tied to the offer, not a generic explore prompt.
- **Subject Alt A:** `4th of July: $5 tees + up to 70% off — 3 days left`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Stock up the whole crew for $5 a tee this weekend`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Deep End Deals start at $2 — shop the summer haul before it's gone`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Grab colors for everyone — tees, tanks, and more on sale now`
