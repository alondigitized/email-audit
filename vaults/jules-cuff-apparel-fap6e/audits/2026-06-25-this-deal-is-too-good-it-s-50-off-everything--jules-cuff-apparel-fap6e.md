# This deal is TOO good — It's 50% off EVERYTHING*
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-25**
## Executive summary

- Nine days. I've been counting, and apparently so has Old Navy's subject line writer, because "this deal is TOO good" reads like someone who knows you're onto them. The defensive enthusiasm is real. The email actually earns some goodwill this send though, because two things are new: a "HOT NEW GEAR" section that gives the eye something fresh to land on, and — finally — a "2 DAYS ONLY!" urgency block that makes me think *oh, okay, so this one actually ends.* That deadline is the first credible reason to click I've had all week.
- That said, "TIME TO CHILL" as hero copy above a rack of basics is doing the absolute minimum. It's summer, I get it. The baby clearance section at the bottom is genuinely useful to me — I always need the next size up in onesies and the window for that deal is real. But this is the same sale, same offer, same cadence for a third week running, and even my loyalty to Old Navy volume buys is wearing thin.
- Would I click? Yes — specifically on the clearance section. Would I forward it? Not a chance. Not when Hanna Andersson's welcome email last month had me screenshot the offer for my group chat and this one barely registers as new information.

## What's working

- **"2 DAYS ONLY!" urgency block is the first real deadline signal I've seen in nine sends** — it cuts through my scan-and-skip reflex in a way that "50% off everything" alone stopped doing around day four.
- **"HOT NEW GEAR" section breaks the scroll monotony** — dark background, different energy, gave my eyes a reason to keep going instead of muscle-memory-closing the tab.
- **Baby/clearance section at the bottom is legitimately relevant** — as a stocking-up shopper, knowing clearance baby sizes are at 60% off is actionable information I'll actually use.
- **No visible render bugs** — the layout reads clean on what appears to be a mobile-first template; no broken images, no overlapping text.
- **50% off offer is immediately readable** in the banner strip before I even get to the hero — zero hunting required.

## What's weak

- **"TOO good" in the subject is now the brand's tell** — after a week of "yes, really!" and "it's TRUE!" disclaimers, every intensifier signals fatigue, not excitement. The Hanna Andersson welcome email from May had zero defensive energy and still made me feel like I'd found something.
- **"TIME TO CHILL" hero copy is doing nothing** — it's vague, it's generic, it's not telling me what's new or why I should shop *this* email versus yesterday's.
- **No family or multi-kid framing anywhere in the hero** — I'm here for the volume buys across two kids. The hero model is a solo young woman. Nothing targets me as a mom who's stocking up.
- **The asterisk on "everything\*" is load-bearing and invisible** — I know the exclusions exist, Old Navy always has them, but nine days in I still have no idea what's actually excluded. Feels like fine print that should have been surfaced by now.
- **CTA copy (likely "Shop Now") doesn't differentiate** — I'd guess every button in this email is generic. Hanna Andersson's welcome led with "Shop Girls" / "Shop Boys" splits. That one change would do more for my click rate than any hero copy tweak.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead the subject with the deadline** — "2 days left: 50% off everything" is more honest and more urgent than "TOO good." The sale ending *is* the news. The discount hasn't been the news since day one.
- 2. **Make the hero family-specific** — swap the solo young woman for a mom-and-two-kids shot or at minimum a matching-set callout. I buy in multiples. Acknowledge that.
- 3. **Split CTAs by age/gender** — Hanna does this in their onboarding and it instantly makes the email feel like it was sent to me, not the database. "Shop Girls" / "Shop Boys" / "Shop Baby" above the fold.
- 4. **Surface the exclusion list once, plainly** — put a single line of small copy under the offer block: "Excludes: [3 things]." Nine days of asterisks without explanation erodes trust.
- 5. **Retire "TIME TO CHILL"** — if the sale ends in two days, the hero copy should say so. "Last call" does more work.
- **Subject Alt A:** `2 days left: 50% off everything (yes, even that)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Final 48 hrs — 50% off + 60% off clearance`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Baby clearance, new gear, summer basics — all 50% off through Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop before it's gone: kids, baby & adults all on sale`

## Technical Audit — Old Navy "50% off EVERYTHING" Email

### 1. Technical Summary

One confirmed broken link in the preference center flow and missing one-click unsubscribe headers are the critical issues. Authentication relay capture gaps leave SPF/DKIM verification incomplete.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**CRITICAL — Broken preference center redirect**
The Cheetah/SFMC preference center pixel at `cp.oldnavy.com/o/dab33465...` resolves but returns an application-level error:
```
unknown url type: '/g/dab33465-7ac1-4a74-99c0-16f457041b2e?txn=2c14dc00-720a-11f1-aa2c-aea38baf8e0c'
```
This indicates the internal routing within the preference center app cannot handle the resolved path. If this pixel doubles as the preference/unsubscribe redirect destination, the unsubscribe flow is broken for recipients routed through it.

**23 click-tracking links skipped**
All click links route through `click.email.oldnavy.com/r/...` — correct pattern for Cheetah ESP click-redirect, but HTTP probing was skipped so destination URL health is unverified.

**Two open-tracking pixels present**
- `click.email.oldnavy.com/open.aspx?PHG7FQNDNSZUJN5FVEFXIINC34...` — standard open pixel
- `cp.oldnavy.com/o/dab33465...` — secondary pixel (broken, see above)

Both are hidden in a `display:none` div. The broken secondary pixel will silently fail on every send.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Missing alt text — tracking pixel**
```html
<img src="https://cp.oldnavy.com/o/dab33465...">
```
No `alt` attribute present. The open-tracking pixel has `alt=""` (correct); this secondary pixel has none. Screen readers will announce the raw URL.

**Duplicate CSS rule keys**
In the `@media (max-width: 639px)` block:
- `.MspaceTop` is defined twice (bound to both `.space-top5` and `.space-top10`)
- `.MspaceBot` is defined twice (bound to both `.space-bot5` and `.space-bot10`)

The second definition silently overrides the first. Any element relying on `.space-top5` / `.space-bot5` spacing on mobile will render at 10px instead of 5px.

**Outlook `line-height: 0px` blanket rule**
The MSO conditional injects:
```css
td { line-height:0px; mso-line-height-rule:exactly }
```
This resets all `<td>` line-height to zero in Outlook 2013+. Correct behavior depends on every content `<td>` having an explicit override. Any `<td>` missing one will collapse vertical spacing in Outlook.

**XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype**
`<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN">` is appropriate for broad email client compatibility; no issue.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens visible in the provided HTML. The `cp_tp` parameter in the Cheetah pixel contains a properly encoded (Base64 + zlib) subscriber payload — no raw `{{token}}` or `%%token%%` strings detected.

---

### 5. Compliance

**CRITICAL — List-Unsubscribe header absent**
`List-Unsubscribe` was not captured by the AgentMail relay. Gmail and Yahoo Mail have required this header for bulk senders (>5,000/day) since February 2024. Absence risks inbox demotion or bulk folder routing on both providers.

**CRITICAL — List-Unsubscribe-Post absent (RFC 8058)**
One-click unsubscribe (`List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`) is required alongside `List-Unsubscribe` under the same Gmail/Yahoo enforcement. Not detected.

**Note:** Both header warnings may be relay capture artifacts — AgentMail may not be surfacing headers set at the MTA layer. Confirm directly against raw SMTP headers before treating as a production defect.

**Physical mailing address not detected**
CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid physical postal address in the message body. The HTML is truncated; the address may exist in the footer. Verify in the full source. If absent, it is a legal violation.

**SPF/DKIM status unknown**
`Authentication-Results` header not captured by relay. Cannot confirm whether `email.oldnavy.com` passes SPF alignment or DKIM signature validates for the `oldnavy.com` From domain. Requires raw header inspection from a delivered copy.
