# 50% off everything* ENDING SOON (+ get $17 PowerSoft leggings for the workout girlies!)
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-29**
## Executive summary

- Same purple flat-lay, same "Haul Into Fall," same "50% OFF Everything! Even New Arrivals" banner I've now seen more times than I can count from Old Navy this week — this is at minimum the sixth send in five days with that exact promise. The one new hook is the PowerSoft leggings dropping from $17 to $14 "Today Only," which at least gives me a reason to open beyond pure numbness, and I'll admit the jeans-fit lookbook at the bottom ("Your Fave Jeans As Told By You" — OG Loose, Full Length Wide Leg, Bootcut, High Rise Wide Leg) is genuinely useful content I don't usually get from this brand.
- But the fatigue is real. I scrolled past three near-identical "50% OFF EVERYTHING" walls in one email — the leggings-and-tank teaser, the full graphic banner, and then the category grid — before hitting anything resembling new information. Hanna Andersson's welcome email felt like it was written for one person; this feels like it's blasted to everyone on the list with a subject-line word shuffled. I'm not clicking, I'm not forwarding, and I'm getting close to just filtering these into a folder I check once a week.

## What's working

- The $14 PowerSoft leggings (down from $17) is a real, specific number I can act on — better than the vague "50% off" wall around it.
- The jeans-fit-by-name section (OG Loose, Bootcut, High Rise Wide Leg) speaks to an actual shopping problem I have — finding my cut — and no one else in my inbox does this as directly.
- "TODAY ONLY!" on the leggings creates real urgency, unlike the vague "Limited Time Only" banner up top that's said nothing new in a week.
- Clean, on-brand green/purple palette — at least it's instantly recognizable as Old Navy in a crowded inbox.

## What's weak

- Three separate "50% off everything" graphics stacked in one email is just noise — I said the same thing about the 7/28 and 7/27 sends, and it's still not fixed.
- The category grid (Tees, Jeans, Activewear, Pants, Dresses, Sweatshirts) is the exact same 50%-in-a-box layout I've screenshotted from three prior emails — nothing distinguishes today's send from yesterday's.
- Model in the hero legging shot doesn't give me any real sense of fit on an actual body in motion — it's a static crop, not "workout girlie" energy the subject line promised.
- No loyalty/Navyist points messaging anywhere, and I am a member — Hanna Andersson's welcome series leads with a 20% code tied to signup; Old Navy just assumes anonymous mass-blast.
- Zero kids/family framing in an email going to a uniform-shopper household — if they know anything about my purchase history, none of it shows here.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the redundant 50%-off banners — I don't need to see the same offer three times in one scroll. Lead with the leggings deal, follow directly with the jeans-fit lookbook, and put the category grid last (or drop it).
- 2. Segment this: if I've bought kids' sizes before, show me a kids activewear or matching-set callout instead of another generic "everything" wall — Hanna Andersson does this kind of relevance well even in a cold welcome email.
- 3. Add a "Navyist member" line or point-earning note near the offer — give me a reason this deal is mine, not everyone's.
- 4. Show the leggings on a body actually moving (running, stretching) since the subject explicitly calls out "workout girlies" — right now the visual doesn't match the copy's energy.
- 5. Vary the creative day-to-day — even a different flat-lay color or hero crop would help distinguish this from the last five sends.
