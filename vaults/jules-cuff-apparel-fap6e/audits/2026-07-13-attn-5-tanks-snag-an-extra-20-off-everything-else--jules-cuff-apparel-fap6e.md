# ATTN: $5 tanks + snag an extra 20% off everything else*
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so I've been watching Old Navy do this $4 polo thing for four days straight — subject after subject with kids uniform polos as the hero — and today they pulled a hard pivot to women's tanks and clearance. I'm not mad at the $5 price point; that stopped my thumb. But opening to find a six-section email that goes tanks → denim washes → $3.99 clearance → hot weather gear → then uniforms at the very bottom? That's a lot of Old Navy at once. The uniforms are technically in there, which I appreciate, but after yesterday's "dress code, handled" which was the tightest execution of the polo series, this email feels like the brand hit reset and threw everything against the wall.
- The "ATTN:" opener in the subject reads like a phishing template, not a welcome deal from a brand I actually like. Hanna Andersson doesn't do that — when their welcome email landed it felt like a real letter, not a mass blast with a fake urgency prefix. The asterisk on "everything else\*" also gives me pause because I immediately wonder: are uniforms excluded? If yes, this whole send is kind of irrelevant to my primary Old Navy shopping mission right now.
- I'd probably click for the 20% off to see what's excluded, but I wouldn't forward this to anyone. I've already texted my sister-in-law about the $4 polo promo — that one was worth sharing. This one is just... a Tuesday email.

## What's working

- **$5 tanks in the hero are hard to ignore.** It's a clear, single-digit price point that lands before I have to think.
- **"2 DAYS ONLY!" urgency in the email body** is visually prominent and feels credible — short window, specific copy.
- **The uniforms section is there.** Buried at position six, but present. For a back-to-school mom in July, I noticed it and that's worth something.
- **Multiple price drops in one scroll** ($5, $4, $6, $3.99) signal value-stacking — Old Navy knows I'm doing math while I shop.
- **Clean rendering throughout.** Nothing is broken, images load, layout holds. Low bar but it clears it.

## What's weak

- **"ATTN:" is a dead opener.** It reads like a financial alert, not a promo from a brand I trust. Hanna Andersson's welcome email opened with warmth and earned attention — Old Navy is demanding it.
- **Six content sections is too much.** Tanks, denim washes, clearance, hot weather gear, uniforms, and whatever else is in the footer — the hierarchy collapses. I don't know what Old Navy wants me to buy today.
- **The asterisk on "everything else\*"** feels like a gotcha waiting at checkout. If uniforms are excluded from the 20% — and they often are — the whole email bait-and-switches the exact category I'm shopping.
- **After four consecutive polo emails, the pivot is jarring.** No acknowledgment that I've been eyeing school uniforms. Just: new day, new hero, you're a stranger again.
- **Uniform section is buried last.** This is peak uniform-shopping season; that section should be at least third, not sixth.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the 20% off as a stacking offer on the polo promo.** "Still building your uniform pile? Grab the $4 polos — plus 20% off the rest of the cart." That connects this email to last week's momentum instead of abandoning it.
- 2. **Kill "ATTN:" entirely.** It signals spam before I even open. Concrete and direct beats fake urgency every time.
- 3. **Move uniforms to section two or three.** It's July. Back-to-school is the shopping mission. Don't make me scroll past denim washes to find it.
- 4. **Clarify the exclusions upfront.** If uniforms are out, say so. If they're in, say so explicitly — that's the copy that would actually drive my click.
- 5. **Collapse to three sections max.** Hero offer → uniforms → clearance. The hot weather gear and denim washes dilute everything.
- **Subject Alt A:** `$5 tanks today — plus 20% off your uniform haul`
- **Subject Alt B:** `2 days: $5 tanks + extra 20% off (yes, uniforms count)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack it: $4 polos in your cart, now take 20% off the whole order.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Tanks are $5. Uniforms are waiting. Your cart does the math.`
