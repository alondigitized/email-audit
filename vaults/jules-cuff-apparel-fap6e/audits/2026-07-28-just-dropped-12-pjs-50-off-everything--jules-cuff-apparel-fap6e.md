# JUST DROPPED — $12 PJs + 50% OFF everything*
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-28**
## Executive summary

- Another "50% off everything" wall of banners from Old Navy — and I mean that literally, this is the second one they've sent me today. Yesterday it was "You've officially got 50% off everything*," today it's "JUST DROPPED — $12 PJs + 50% off everything*," and neither the subject line nor my inbox preview told me anything that actually differentiates the two. The one thing that did catch my eye scrolling through: the "Baggy's Big Sale Debut" section with a mom, dad, and two kids all in the same baggy denim silhouette — that's the one beat that speaks to me as the person buying jeans for four humans at once.
- Here's my real gripe though: the subject promised "$12 PJs" and I scrolled this entire email top to bottom and never saw a single pajama. The hero is "Palomas" at $8/$10/$12 styled with a cardigan, boots, and shorts — that's not pajamas, that's daywear. So either the product naming is doing something I don't get, or this is a classic subject-line bait to get me to open, and I don't love being baited by a brand I already buy from four times a week.
- Would I click? Maybe, on the "Shop All Girl's/Boy's Jeans" links, because I do genuinely need denim for the kids before fall. Would I forward it to a friend? No — there's nothing here worth sharing, it's just Tuesday-Old-Navy-noise. Mark as read and move on.

## What's working

- The "Baggy's Big Sale Debut" family lineup (mom, dad, two kids, all in baggy fits) is the one section that reads as "shop for your whole crew," which is exactly how I buy.
- Fit callouts are actually there — "Low Rise," "Super Baggy," "Wide Leg" — so I know what I'm clicking into before I click, which Old Navy doesn't always bother with.
- CTA buttons are specific ("Shop All Women's Jeans," "Shop All Men's Jeans") instead of a vague "Shop Now," so I know exactly where each link goes.
- Rewards early-access line up top is a small nod that I'm a member, not a cold lead.

## What's weak

- No PJs. Anywhere. The subject line sold me $12 pajamas and the hero shows a cardigan-and-boots outfit called "Palomas" — that's a mismatch I noticed and didn't love.
- This is the second "50% off everything" email from Old Navy today, and the sixth-ish variant in under a week (7/24, twice on 7/25, twice on 7/26, 7/27, and now two on 7/28). Hanna Andersson's welcome series earned an 8/10 from me by saying one clean thing once — Old Navy is saying the same thing five times a day and hoping volume substitutes for a real hook.
- Three separate "50% off Everything!" banners stacked through one email dilutes any sense of a single hero offer — my eye doesn't know where to land first.
- No preview text came through in my inbox at all, so I opened this blind on nothing but the subject.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the subject/content mismatch — either put actual pajamas in the hero or stop promising them. Right now it reads as bait, and I'll start discounting Old Navy subject lines the way I already discount their fifth "50% off everything" of the week.
- 2. Cut the redundant "50% off Everything!" banners down to one — say it once at the top, let the jeans and fleece sections do the selling.
- 3. Lead with the family denim debut instead of burying it below the fold — that's the actual differentiated content in this send, not another 50%-off callout.
- 4. Write a real preheader — right now nothing shows up next to the subject, which wastes free real estate every competent sender (Hanna Andersson included) is using.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Baggy denim, the whole family — 50% off today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New: $12 pajamas + baggy jeans for everyone, 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Mom, Dad, and the kids — same baggy fit, half the price.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New low-rise and wide-leg baggy jeans just dropped, today only.`
- 5. Space out send cadence — two "50% off everything" emails in one calendar day is the kind of thing that gets a brand marked as spam by someone like me, not just ignored.
