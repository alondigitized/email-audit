# 🦘 JUMP ON IT! Get 50% off ALL activewear
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-28**
## Executive summary

- Twelve days. I've been counting. And this is now the third time in roughly 48 hours I've been asked to "JUMP ON IT" for 50% off activewear from Old Navy. The kangaroo emoji is cute exactly once. The "2 DAYS ONLY" hero is punchy and the "HOT NEW GEAR!" section does introduce fresh product — there's genuine visual energy in the middle of this email with the colorful drops and the neon colorways. I appreciated that they're not just running the same static grid again.
- But then I got to "SORRY PLUS SIZE FRIENDS." I had to stop. That copy is sitting right there in the email body as an actual section header — and it is not the way you talk to your customers. Even if you're flagging an exclusion in a promotion, the word choices matter. I don't wear plus sizes but I have eyes, and that phrasing feels dismissive and lazy in a way that reflects on the whole brand. Hanna Andersson would never. Not even in a rushed send.
- Mark as read. I've already filed this beat in my brain as "Old Navy activewear sale is perpetual." I would not forward this.

## What's working

- **"2 DAYS ONLY! 50%" hero lands immediately** — the orange-on-bold layout puts the offer front and center before a single scroll.
- **"HOT NEW GEAR!" section genuinely adds something** — colorful new product imagery breaks the recycled-promo feeling; this is what the June 27 TrekTech send did right, and it's happening again here.
- **Product grid is well-organized** — the lower section shows a clean mix of basics in neutral tones; easy to scan, no image overflow or layout breaks.
- **Brand voice is consistent** — Old Navy's high-energy, punchy tone is exactly what I expect from them; the visual identity is cohesive.
- **No render bugs** — images loaded, CTAs are tappable-looking, no broken layout or lorem ipsum visible.

## What's weak

- **"SORRY PLUS SIZE FRIENDS" is a hard miss** — exclusionary section headers are not marketing copy; it signals the brand didn't think carefully about who they're talking to.
- **The urgency is spent** — "2 DAYS ONLY" reads as background noise when it's the fourth near-identical send in two days; the deadline doesn't feel real anymore.
- **No family or kids hook anywhere** — I buy Old Navy because it's good for volume family shopping; this email is 100% adult activewear with zero signal for the parent half of their customer base.
- **Hero model skews male** — the top-of-email model is a guy in athletic shorts; for a 36-year-old woman who is the primary household purchaser, that's an immediate signal this wasn't built for me.
- **No loyalty or NavyRewards callout visible** — after twelve days of emails, I'd expect at least a "Cash Rewards members get early access" line; Hanna Andersson's welcome email (8/10 in my history) put a benefit code front and center and it worked.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill "SORRY PLUS SIZE FRIENDS" immediately** — reframe as "Note: This offer excludes plus sizes" in fine print at the bottom if you must communicate it, but do not make it a section header. This single line does more brand damage than the 50% off does good.
- 2. **Put a real deadline in the subject line** — "ends tonight" or "ends Tuesday" beats "JUMP ON IT" every time because it answers "why now?" from inside the inbox.
- 3. **Add a family/kids module** — even one row of kids' activewear with "dress the whole crew" copy would bring me back into the fold as the household buyer.
- 4. **Surface a NavyRewards callout** — one line: "NavyRewards members, your Cash is stacking." Costs nothing, gives me a reason to feel like a known customer instead of a cold prospect.
- 5. **Lead with the female model** — swap the hero image; demographic-match your primary decision-maker for activewear purchases.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends Tuesday: 50% off all activewear + hot new gear`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New activewear drops — 50% off, 2 days only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Colorful new styles just landed. Shop before the sale ends.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fresh TrekTech styles + your favorite basics, all 50% off through Tuesday.`
