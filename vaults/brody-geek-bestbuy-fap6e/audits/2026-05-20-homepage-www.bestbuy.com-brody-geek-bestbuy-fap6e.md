# Homepage snapshot · www.bestbuy.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- First impression on mobile: Best Buy is running a hard Memorial Day push, and "big tech deals" is the entire pitch above the fold. As someone who plans purchases weeks out and actively tracks Best Buy sales calendars, the timing is right — but there's almost nothing here beyond the banner itself. No product tiles, no gaming or audio callouts, no sign my Plus membership even exists. It's a billboard, not a homepage.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day Sale urgency block** — the clock icon plus "Ends 5/25" is clean and credible. As someone who literally calendars Best Buy sale windows, this registers immediately as "act this week."
- **"Big tech deals" copy** — generic but correctly positioned. Tells me this isn't a fashion sale or a food delivery play, it's my category.
- **Single dominant hero** — no competing banners fighting for attention below it (at least above the fold). The blue-to-teal gradient keeps the layout uncluttered and the yellow CTA button pops against it.
- **Search bar always visible** — as a high-intent researcher, I often skip browsing and go straight to search. It's right there in the header without scrolling.

## What's weak

- **Instacart banner squatting at the very top** — that's prime real estate being handed to a grocery delivery ad. As a Plus member I'm coming here for tech, not same-day bananas. It also has a visible text clipping bug on the right side.
- **Zero product imagery in the hero** — "big tech deals" is vague. Show me a Sony WH-1000XM6, an LG OLED, a Steam Deck, anything. Abstract gradients don't make me want to shop.
- **No Best Buy Plus / Totaltech acknowledgment** — I pay for this membership specifically for the perks. If the homepage can't show me my rewards balance or member-exclusive pricing, the loyalty investment feels invisible.
- **Nav is hamburger-gated** — the top bar shows "Gift Ideas / Top Deals / Deal of the Day / More" but there's no direct path to Gaming, TVs, or Audio without tapping Menu first. One extra tap on mobile is a real friction point for a category browsing session.
- **No specific deal dollar amounts** — "Memorial Day Sale" is a label, not an offer. As someone who comparison-shops obsessively, I need to see the actual number to decide if this is worth my time versus Amazon or Costco.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the Instacart top banner with a Plus/Totaltech member callout** — show me my rewards points balance or a "member exclusive" badge. If I'm logged in, personalize that slot. Turn the squatter ad into a retention signal.
- **Add a product image to the hero** — swap the gradient-only background for an actual hero product shot (a flagship TV, ANC headphones, or a gaming monitor). One image would make "big tech deals" concrete and browsable.
- **Surface a specific offer number in the hero subhead** — "Save up to 40% on TVs, soundbars & gaming gear" would be infinitely more actionable than "Bring on summer with big tech deals."
- **Add a horizontal category quick-link strip below the nav** — Gaming / TVs / Audio / Laptops / Open-Box as icon chips. One tap to my category without touching the hamburger menu.
