# Homepage snapshot · www.bestbuy.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- This is basically a ghost town — the nav chrome loaded but the entire body of the page is a blank white void. No hero, no deals, no product rails, nothing. Best Buy is a brand I genuinely respect as a place to do serious research before a big purchase, but right now I'm looking at a skeleton that never got dressed. Whatever campaign they're running, I can't see a single pixel of it.

## What's working

- **Search bar** — prominently placed top-left in the header; for a guy who already knows what he wants (Sony WH-1000XM6, a 4K OLED, a GPU), search-first is my actual workflow. That box being front-and-center is the right call.
- **"Top Deals" nav link** — visible in the secondary nav bar. As someone who has a Black Friday calendar and actively hunts open-box deals, this is exactly the kind of shortcut I want one tap away.
- **"Gift Ideas" with a gift icon** — it's May, could be graduation/dad gifting season. The highlighted yellow icon draws attention and contextually makes sense for this time of year.
- **Store locator in header** — as someone who does a Geek Squad consult before big-ticket purchases, the "Find a store" link being in the persistent header is genuinely useful.

## What's weak

- **The entire hero / body didn't render** — this is a catastrophic failure. There is no below-the-fold content, no product imagery, no promotional banners, no category tiles. I'm looking at white space for the bottom ~85% of the viewport.
- **No loyalty/member signal above the fold** — I have Best Buy Plus for the free shipping benefit; the nav says "Sign in" but there's no "Plus member" badge, rewards balance, or member-pricing callout anywhere visible.
- **No price anchoring or urgency** — the nav has "Top Deals" but not a single dollar amount, percentage off, or deadline is visible in the loaded portion of the page.
- **"More ▼" as a collapsed nav item** — on mobile this buries the actual category taxonomy (TVs, Audio, Gaming, Computers) behind an extra tap. For a guy looking to jump straight to headphones or graphics cards, this is friction.
- **Zero campaign personality** — the brand could be running an incredible audio event or a gaming peripheral sale right now and I'd have zero idea. The page communicated nothing.

## Recommendations

- **Fix the render failure first** — whatever is causing the body not to load (lazy hydration crash, JS bundle failure, image-heavy hero blocking paint) needs to be diagnosed and fixed immediately. This is not a UX tweak; it's table stakes.
- **Surface Best Buy Plus perks in the persistent header** — replace generic "Sign in" with "Plus member · 3,420 points" for logged-in users, or a "Join Plus, get free shipping" teaser for guests. One line of copy, massive signal for deal-hunters like me.
- **Pin a category strip below the nav** — a horizontally scrollable icon row for TVs · Audio · Gaming · Computers · Cameras · Appliances. No hero needed; even if images fail, text icons survive. That's my fastest path to product.
- **Add an inline deal badge to "Top Deals"** — even something like "Top Deals · Up to 40% off" in the nav link copy gives me a reason to tap before the hero loads.
