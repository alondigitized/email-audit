# Homepage snapshot · www.bestbuy.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- I opened Best Buy's homepage on my phone and immediately got hit with a sign-in interstitial that swallowed basically the entire screen — no hero, no products, no deals, just a "Hi there!" modal sitting on a giant white void. The brand looks like it's running a blue-and-white utility play, but right now I can't even tell what they're selling. This is a first-impression disaster.

## What's working

- **"Recently Viewed" nav link** — I can see it in the top nav bar, which tells me the site at least has session/browse history infrastructure. Good for a returning shopper like me.
- **"Sign In" + "Create Account" dual CTA** — the button hierarchy is clean: solid blue for Sign In, outlined for Create Account. Correct priority.
- **Store Locator in nav** — I always scope open-box inventory before going in-store; having this one tap away in the header is exactly what I need.
- **Search bar prominent above the fold** — I'm a search-first shopper when I know what I want. It's right there.

## What's weak

- **Full-page content void** — Below the sign-in interstitial, the entire lower 60% of the viewport is blank white. The page clearly failed to render content behind the modal. That's a broken load state, not a design choice.
- **Sign-in gate before any value delivery** — Asking me to log in before showing me a single deal or product is backwards. I came to browse; you haven't given me one reason to care yet.
- **No hero, no offer, no product imagery** — There is literally zero merchandise or promotion visible above the fold. A Best Buy homepage that shows no tech products is wild.
- **My Best Buy Plus is invisible** — I'm a paying member. Not a single badge, balance, or member-exclusive deal is surfaced. The nav is cut off on the right ("Save" is truncated) so I can't even confirm what's there.
- **"Hi there!" is generic** — That greeting copy is a nothing-burger. No personalization, no urgency, no hook.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the interstitial or load content behind it** — The modal should overlay a rendered hero/homepage, not a white abyss. Even a guest view of current deals gives me something to want before asking me to sign in.
- **Surface a My Best Buy Plus member benefit in the interstitial itself** — Replace "For the best experience, be sure to sign in" with something like "Sign in to see your Plus member pricing" — now the login gate is a value prop, not a friction bump.
- **Show one concrete deal in the nav strip** — A thin promo banner ("Open-Box TVs up to 40% off") above or below the search bar would give me a reason to engage before the modal even resolves.
- **Fix the blank-page load state** — This is a critical mobile bug. Whether it's a slow JS bundle or a z-index issue trapping the modal on a white layer, it needs to be resolved so content renders behind the overlay.
