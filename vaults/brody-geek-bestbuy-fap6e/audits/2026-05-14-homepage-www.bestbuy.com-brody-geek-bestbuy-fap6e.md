# Homepage snapshot · www.bestbuy.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Classic big-box retailer energy — recognizable blue-and-yellow brand, no surprises. What I'm seeing above the fold is basically a gated experience pushing me to sign in before I can even browse, followed by a Memorial Day appliance sale that has zero relevance to me as a gamer and audio nerd. Best Buy has all the right inventory for someone like me, but this mobile homepage is hiding it behind a login wall and pushing fridges.

## What's working

- **Clean mobile shell** — the header is tight: Best Buy logo, hamburger menu, search bar. One tap to search for a specific product is always available, which saves me as a research-heavy buyer.
- **Sign-in value prop copy** — "free shipping, recommendations, easy order tracking" is a solid pitch for a member like me (Best Buy Plus). The functional benefits list is clear and not bloated.
- **"Sign in" CTA button** — high-contrast blue on white, unmissable, well-sized for thumb tap. No ambiguity about what it does.
- **Deal of the Day nav link** — I clock that in the nav bar. That's a hook for me; I check those habitually for open-box opportunities.

## What's weak

- **Appliance hero is dead wrong for me** — "Memorial Day Sale / Appliances" is the dominant visual message. I don't buy appliances. This could be anyone's homepage for a first-time fridge shopper, not a returning tech loyalist.
- **Zero gaming, AV, or audio signal above the fold** — not one product image, category chip, or copy fragment signals that Best Buy carries the gear I actually care about. I have to mentally bridge to "they have that stuff somewhere."
- **Login wall feels like friction, not benefit** — leading with "sign in to get basic things" before I even see products reads as the site needing something from me before it gives me value.
- **No Best Buy Plus / membership branding** — if I'm paying for Best Buy Plus, where's my member badge, rewards balance, or exclusive pricing callout? It's invisible above the fold.
- **"Terms and conditions apply" asterisk on the appliance promo** — small, gray, buried. Not confidence-inspiring on first impression.
- **No product imagery** — the entire viewport is text + color blocks. For a retailer with incredible product depth, that's a missed visual opportunity.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the appliance hero for a tech-category carousel** — a Memorial Day sale on gaming monitors, soundbars, or laptops would speak to the same seasonal moment without alienating the core tech buyer. Personalize by login state if possible.
- **Surface Best Buy Plus perks front and center** — if I'm already a member (or close to becoming one), show my rewards balance or an exclusive member deal in the hero area. "Member Deal: $50 off Sony headphones this weekend" beats a generic sign-in pitch.
- **Add a category quick-nav strip below the header** — chips like "Gaming," "Home Theater," "Headphones," "Laptops" get me one tap from the homepage to my aisle. The current "Gift Ideas / Top Deals / Deal of the Day / More" strip is too generic.
- **Lead with a product image** — even a hero showing a gaming setup or a soundbar lifestyle shot communicates instantly that Best Buy is my store. Right now the page looks like it could belong to any retailer.
