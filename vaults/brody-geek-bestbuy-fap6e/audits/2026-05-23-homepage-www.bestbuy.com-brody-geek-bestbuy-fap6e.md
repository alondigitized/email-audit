# Homepage snapshot · www.bestbuy.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Loaded the page and immediately got gate-crashed by a sign-in modal — the entire hero is either blocked or never loaded, leaving a massive blank white void behind it. Best Buy is clearly pushing account creation hard, but right now this feels less like a tech retailer and more like a half-loaded app with a pop-up problem. If this is my first impression, I'm already hunting for the X button.

## What's working

- **Sign In / Create Account modal:** The two-button layout is clean, high contrast, easy to tap on mobile. If account creation is the goal, the UI itself is well-executed.
- **Nav bar utility icons:** "Your store / Find a store," "Account / Sign in," and cart icon are all legible and tappable-sized for mobile — basic utility is present.
- **X dismiss button:** Visible in the top-right corner of the modal, so I'm not trapped. Small win.

## What's weak

- **Blank hero / failed content load:** The majority of the above-the-fold viewport is a white void. No product, no offer, no campaign — nothing. This is the most critical piece of screen real estate on the entire page and it's dead.
- **Modal blocking what little is there:** Even if something had loaded behind it, the sign-in modal would be sitting right on top of the first scroll-position content.
- **No Best Buy logo visible:** The nav bar is cropped — the logo and search bar (presumably to the left/right) are cut off. I don't even get brand confirmation in this viewport.
- **No category navigation visible:** No hamburger menu, no "TVs / Gaming / Audio" quick-links — I have no one-tap path to headphones or gaming gear.
- **Zero deal signal:** As a Best Buy Plus member, I'm used to seeing member pricing or a deal banner the second I open the app. Seeing nothing is a conversion killer.

## Recommendations

- **Lazy-load the hero above the modal trigger** — whatever is causing the blank render needs to fire before the sign-in prompt does; showing a blank page then asking me to log in is backwards.
- **Swap the full-screen blocking modal for a persistent sticky banner** — "Sign in for member pricing" as a slim top bar lets me see the actual homepage while still nudging account creation.
- **Add a "Gaming & Entertainment" or "Audio" quick-link icon row** in the nav or just below it — one tap to my category on first screen, no modal required.
- **Show a Best Buy Plus member deal teaser even pre-login** — e.g., "Plus members save an extra 10% on open-box" keeps me engaged and gives me a reason to actually sign in.
