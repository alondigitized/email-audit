# Homepage snapshot · www.bestbuy.com · 2026-08-08
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-08**
## Executive summary

- This reads like classic Best Buy: a paid ad up top, then two big blue "shopping event" tiles, then a ranked trending-deals rail. Nothing here is speaking directly to me as a gamer/AV guy — it's Apple and TCL, not PC Gaming or headphones front and center — but the bones are there: real deadlines, a real ranked rail, and the categories I actually care about (PC Gaming, Headphones) are one tab away. Feels like a store that knows tech shoppers exist, just hasn't pointed the hero at me specifically today.

## What's working

- **"Ends Sunday" timer on the Apple Shopping Event** — a real deadline, not vague urgency copy. That's the kind of thing that gets me to actually check back before I forget.
- **"Deal of the Day" on TCL** — "today only" framing is honest and specific, no fake countdown gimmicks.
- **Trending deals rail with a ranked #1–#6 list and category tabs (PC Gaming, Headphones, Wearable Tech)** — this is exactly the browsing pattern I use: skim what's hot, then dive into my category.
- **Sign-in module up top with "recommendations, easy order tracking"** — speaks to me as a returning member even before I've logged in.

## What's weak

- **Skylight Calendar ad as the very first thing I see** — it's a paid banner for a family organizer product, not remotely my lane. Feels like ad inventory, not curated homepage real estate.
- **No Best Buy Plus / Totaltech badge or member-pricing callout above the fold** — I pay for Plus specifically for free shipping and extra return windows; the homepage doesn't remind me what I'm getting for it.
- **Trending deals thumbnails are generic laptop wallpaper art** — no spec callouts (GPU, RAM, refresh rate) that would actually get a research-first shopper like me to stop scrolling.
- **No open-box or clearance CTA visible above the fold** — that's half of how I actually buy big-ticket stuff at Best Buy, and it's nowhere in this first screen.

## Recommendations

- Swap the Skylight Calendar ad slot (or move it below the fold) for a Best Buy-owned hero tied to PC Gaming or home theater — that's higher-margin, higher-relevance real estate for a tech-first audience.
- Add a small Best Buy Plus/Totaltech ribbon under the sign-in module ("Members get free shipping + extended returns") so the loyalty value is visible without a tap.
- Put a spec chip (e.g. "RTX 4070 · 16GB") on the Trending Deals thumbnails instead of bare product art — helps a config-comparing shopper decide whether to tap in.
- Surface an "Open-Box Deals" tile alongside Apple/TCL — it's a core Best Buy differentiator and currently invisible above the fold.
