# Homepage snapshot · www.bestbuy.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- Best Buy's mobile homepage opens with an account sign-in nag before it even shows me what's on sale — and the one promo that's actually visible is a **Memorial Day APPLIANCES** sale. I'm a 34-year-old who spent last weekend comparing AV receivers and hunting open-box GPU deals, so leading with refrigerators and dishwashers is a swing-and-a-miss right out of the gate. That said, the bones are clean and I recognize the brand instantly — this is still the place I trust for big-ticket tech.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day Sale banner** — Yellow-on-blue with bold "MEMORIAL DAY SALE" text is high contrast and legible on mobile; I immediately clock that there's a major promotional event happening
- **"Up to 45% off" copy** — Concrete number, not vague "great deals" language; a 45% ceiling is actually compelling even if it's for appliances
- **Search bar always visible** — For someone like me who knows exactly what I want (Sony WH-1000XM6, RTX 5080, specific SKUs), having search pinned in the header means I can skip the browse experience entirely
- **Top nav is useful** — "Top Deals" and "Deal of the Day" are exactly the tabs a price-conscious research nerd like me would tap first

## What's weak

- **Sign-in box as hero** — This is prime real estate and it's being spent on account acquisition, not product inspiration. I came here to shop, not to be reminded I'm not logged in yet
- **Appliances lead** — The only visible promotional category is "IANCES" (Appliances). I have zero interest in washing machines; this feels like the homepage was built for my parents, not me
- **No category shortcuts above fold** — There's no Electronics / Gaming / Audio / TV quick-tap rail visible without scrolling; I'd have to hunt through the hamburger menu
- **"Free shipping*" asterisk** — The asterisk with fine-print exclusions immediately after the sign-in pitch undercuts trust right before the ask; feels like bait
- **Truncated banner** — The sale banner is cut off on the right edge — I can read "SAL" not "SALE," "IANCES" not "APPLIANCES," "delive" not "delivery." On a 390px-wide viewport this suggests the layout isn't fully optimized for narrow screens

## Recommendations

- **Replace the sign-in nag with a personalized category strip** — If I'm not logged in, show me "Trending in Gaming," "New in Audio," "Open-Box Deals" as quick-tap tiles; save the sign-in ask for checkout depth
- **Add a tech/gaming Memorial Day banner variant** — Slot an A/B hero: same "up to 45% off" offer but with a gaming peripheral or OLED TV as the hero image, not an implied appliance context
- **Fix the truncated banner on narrow viewports** — The right-side clipping on "MEMORIAL DAY SALE" and the offer copy is a real polish issue; either constrain the text container or scale the font down one step
- **Surface Best Buy Plus perks in the sign-in hook** — Instead of generic "free shipping*," lead with "Best Buy Plus members get free 2-day shipping + exclusive member pricing" — I already have Plus and that would actually make me tap Sign In faster
