# Homepage snapshot · www.bestbuy.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- I land on Best Buy's homepage and immediately get cockblocked by a "Hi there! For the best experience, be sure to sign in." interstitial before I can see a single product, deal, or hero image. Below the sign-in prompt is a completely blank white void — the actual homepage hasn't loaded. This feels less like a retailer and more like a velvet-rope bouncer at a club I'm already a member of.
- **2/10**

## What's working

- **The nav bar structure** — Search, Gift Ideas, Store Locator, Recently Viewed, Order Status are all visible and sensibly placed for a mobile header. That's clean utility navigation I can actually use if I can get past the gate.
- **"Gift Ideas" CTA in the top nav** — that pill-button treatment with the gift icon stands out against the blue bar; it's a smart surface for discovery browsing.
- **"Sign In" / "Create Account" button pairing** — the two-button layout is clear and the blue/outline contrast is good. No dark-pattern tricks here, at least.

## What's weak

- **The sign-in interstitial is eating the entire above-the-fold viewport.** There is literally nothing below it except white space. No hero. No deals. No products. I can't see what I came for.
- **"For the best experience, be sure to sign in"** — this copy is friction dressed up as a favor. I know what's happening: they want my account cookie. Don't pretend it's for my benefit.
- **Zero merchandising is visible.** No promo banner, no deal of the day, no category tiles — nothing to hook my interest before demanding a login. A gamer/tech shopper like me opens Best Buy to browse GPUs or check open-box deals, not to log in first.
- **The homepage content itself appears to have failed to render.** Below the modal is a blank white area. This could be a render/JS loading failure combined with the interstitial, which makes the page look broken even if I dismiss the prompt.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the sign-in prompt by default on first visit** — show the homepage hero and let the content sell itself. Surface the sign-in nudge as a persistent but unobtrusive banner or after a scroll event, not as a full-viewport gate.
- **Load and render the hero content behind the modal** so dismissing it reveals something immediately — right now closing "Hi there!" would expose a blank white page, which is a broken experience.
- **Replace the generic "best experience" copy** with something membership-specific: "See your Plus member pricing" or "Track your open-box alerts" — give me an actual reason tied to my account benefits.
- **Surface a deal or featured category in the nav strip** (e.g., a "Today's Deals" pill alongside Gift Ideas) so even the locked-out state communicates value.
