# Homepage snapshot · www.bestbuy.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so I land on Best Buy's mobile homepage and the first thing I see is a Mother's Day gift push — gradient purple-to-blue hero, a stock photo of a woman and kid on a couch, and "Mom's favorite? These deals." That's... fine, I guess. It's May 8, Mother's Day is in two days, so the timing is legit. But I came here to check open-box TV deals or maybe scope some audio gear, and this homepage is not talking to me at all right now. Best Buy feels like a big-box retailer doing a seasonal campaign, not a tech destination.

## What's working

- **Seasonal relevance:** The Mother's Day countdown ("is May 10") is correctly timed. If I were shopping for my mom, I'd register the urgency.
- **Clear CTA button:** "Shop now" is high-contrast white on a dark gradient — easy to tap on mobile, no ambiguity about what it does.
- **Top nav utility:** "Top Deals," "Deal of the Day," and the search bar are immediately accessible — if I want to route around the hero, I can do it in one tap.
- **Search bar above the fold:** As a researcher who already knows what I want (e.g., "Sony WH-1000XM6 open box"), I can skip the hero entirely and just search.

## What's weak

- **Left-edge text clipping:** The hero headline — "Mom's favorite? These deals." — is visually chopped on the left margin. The "M" reads as "I", the "f" is half-gone. That's a mobile render bug, full stop.
- **Zero tech/gamer relevance above the fold:** No gaming, audio, home theater, or PC category is surfaced. For my persona, the entire above-the-fold is a skip.
- **No offer specificity:** "These deals" tells me nothing. Best Buy runs 20% off open-box TVs and member-pricing on headphones — none of that is surfaced.
- **No Best Buy Plus / loyalty hook:** My membership is the reason I use Best Buy over Amazon. That benefit is invisible above the fold, which makes the homepage feel like it doesn't know me.
- **Hero lifestyle imagery:** The mom-and-child couch scene reads as a gift-giving campaign. I'm not in gift mode — I'm in "buy something for myself" mode, which is where Best Buy earns most of its revenue from tech enthusiasts.

## Recommendations

- **Add a persistent loyalty/member ribbon:** A thin strip just below the header — "Plus member? Your exclusive deals are here" — keeps me anchored to the reason I chose Best Buy over Amazon.
- **Swap the hero for a dual-lane layout:** On mobile, a split or tabbed hero (e.g., "Shop for Mom | Shop for Yourself") lets the Mother's Day campaign run without alienating every non-gifter who hits the page.
- **Make the offer concrete:** Change "Mom's favorite? These deals." to something like "Up to 40% off top gifts — headphones, tablets, TVs." Give me a number. Vague copy bounces researchers like me.
- **Fix the left-edge text clip:** Whatever CSS or viewport overflow is clipping the hero headline needs a patch — it looks broken on mobile and undermines trust in the brand at first glance.
