# Homepage snapshot · www.bestbuy.com · 2026-05-04
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-04**
## Executive summary

- I opened Best Buy expecting to see something about OLED TVs, the new GPU drop, or at minimum a headline deal on headphones — and I got a Mother's Day gradient with a stock photo of a family on a couch. Best Buy is running a straight holiday gift push right now, which makes total sense as a business move, but as a dude shopping for himself it's a hard miss. The shell of the site (search bar, nav, "Deal of the Day") is still recognizably Best Buy, but the above-the-fold real estate is 100% hijacked by a campaign I have zero stake in.
- **6/10**
- + Unmistakable primary CTA visible above the fold — "Shop now" button is clearly rendered
- + Visual hierarchy is clean — big headline, lifestyle image, CTA, eye reads it in order
- + No render bugs — layout is crisp, no broken elements, no overflow
- + Page reflects current campaign/season — "Mother's Day is May 10" and today is May 4, so it's timely
- + Offer feels honest — no buried fine print, no fake countdown, no obvious bait-and-switch
- − Hero copy/imagery does not speak to me at all — Mom gifting campaigns are not Brody territory
- − No returning-shopper hook — no rewards balance, no "Welcome back," no recently-viewed rail
- − No concrete offer above the fold — "these deals" is not a number; I see no % off, no dollar threshold
- − Demographic signals are wrong for my persona — the lifestyle image is a mom-and-kids scenario
- − Loyalty/membership benefits not visible — no Best Buy Plus badge, no free-shipping callout

## What's working

- **"Deal of the Day" in the nav bar** — that tab is a reflex tap for me; glad it survived the holiday takeover
- **Search bar prominently placed** — I can skip the hero entirely and go straight to "Sony WH-1000XM6" or whatever I'm hunting; the search UX isn't buried
- **"Top Deals" nav link** — one tap to a deals page is the escape hatch when the hero is off-target, and it's right there
- **Clean render** — no layout jank, the gradient hero loads fast, no overlapping text despite the large font size on mobile
- **Timely campaign** — Mother's Day is six days out; if I were gift shopping, the urgency strip "Mother's Day is May 10" is a legit nudge

## What's weak

- **Hero is 100% Mother's Day gifting** — "Mom's favorite? These deals." does nothing for a guy who came here to price-check a receiver or grab an open-box gaming monitor
- **Zero loyalty/rewards signal above the fold** — I pay for Best Buy Plus specifically for free shipping and exclusive deals; none of that is visible here, which feels like a missed retention play even on a holiday campaign page
- **No concrete offer** — "these deals" is marketing vapor; I want to see "up to 40% off" or a specific dollar-off badge before I'm tapping anything
- **Hero image is generic stock-photo energy** — mom, kids, tablet on a couch; there's no tech being shown at all, which is weird for Best Buy
- **No category shortcut tiles** — on mobile I expect a row of quick-tap categories (TVs, Gaming, Laptops, Audio) right under the hero; I don't see that in the viewport

## Recommendations

- **Add a thin deal strip below the hero** — a horizontal scroll rail with "Open-Box Picks" or "Top Deals in Gaming / Audio / TVs" sits below the Mother's Day hero and catches everyone who isn't there to buy mom a tablet; ship in a day, keeps the campaign intact
- **Surface the Best Buy Plus benefit in the header** — a single line "Best Buy Plus: Free shipping + member deals" next to the logo recaptures loyalty customers who tuned out the Mother's Day hero immediately
- **Replace the stock-photo family with actual product in context** — even keeping the Mother's Day theme, swapping the generic couch scene for a real product (noise-canceling headphones, a smart display) makes the hero feel like Best Buy, not a Hallmark card
- **Add a concrete savings claim to the CTA** — change "Shop now" to "Shop deals — up to 40% off" or pin a badge to the button; vague CTAs cost clicks
