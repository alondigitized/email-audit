# Homepage snapshot · www.bestbuy.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- I just loaded Best Buy's homepage on my phone and got... almost nothing. The nav bar rendered — search, Find a Store, Sign In, cart icon, Gift Ideas, Top Deals, More — and then a completely blank white void where the entire page body should be. This isn't a branding critique, it's a broken page. From a pure UX standpoint, Best Buy looks like it crashed mid-load, which is embarrassing for a retailer that sells the tech people use to browse it.

## What's working

- **Search bar** is front and center in the nav — if I already know what I want (e.g., "Sony WH-1000XM6"), I can bypass the broken content entirely and go straight to search.
- **"Top Deals" nav link** is visible and labelled in plain English — that's my language as a deal-calendar guy.
- **"Find a store" utility** is prominent, which matters for me since I do my final buy in-store after a Geek Squad consult.

## What's weak

- **The entire hero section failed to render** — there is nothing below the navigation bar. No promotional image, no headline, no CTA button, no product rail. The page is a white rectangle.
- **No loyalty or My Best Buy Plus hooks** — I'm a Plus member and I get zero acknowledgment of that. No "welcome back," no rewards balance, no member-exclusive deal callout.
- **"Gift Ideas" is the primary nav highlight** — a gift-bow icon in yellow is the most visually prominent element on the page, which feels completely off-calendar for late May. Memorial Day deals should own this moment, not gift guides.
- **"More ▼" hides all category navigation** — as a mobile user I can't see TVs, Audio, Gaming, Computers, or any category without an extra tap. The collapsed nav is a one-tap dead-end.
- **Zero seasonal context** — it's May 22nd, Memorial Day weekend. There is no sale banner, no countdown, no "Biggest Sale of the Year" copy that I'd expect Best Buy to be running right now.

## Recommendations

- **Fix the render failure first** — whatever is causing the hero/body content to not load on mobile is the only thing that matters. Could be a lazy-load JS timeout, an A/B test gone wrong, or a CDN issue. I shouldn't be writing recommendations on content that isn't there.
- **Surface My Best Buy Plus membership above the fold** — a signed-in member should see their points balance or a "Member Deals" badge in the nav area, not a generic "Sign in" prompt. I'm already a Plus subscriber; make me feel it immediately.
- **Swap "Gift Ideas" as the nav hero for a Memorial Day sale link** — it's a holiday weekend, that should be the boldest tap target in the nav row, not a gift bow.
- **Expand the category nav or add a chip rail** — a horizontal scroll of category chips (TVs · Headphones · Gaming · Laptops · Cameras) directly below the main nav would let me get to Audio or Gaming in one tap instead of hiding everything behind "More ▼."
