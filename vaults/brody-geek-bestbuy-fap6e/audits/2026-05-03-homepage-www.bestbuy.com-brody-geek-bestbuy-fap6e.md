- Best Buy's mobile homepage is leading with an entry-level budget TV pitch — Insignia Fire TV, "as low as $79.99 after savings" — which tells me they're in clearance-push mode right now, not innovation mode. For me, a guy who will spend three weeks reading AVSForum threads before pulling the trigger on an OLED, this feels like they're shouting at the wrong end of the market. It's clean and functional, but completely misses the high-consideration buyer.
- **5/10**
- + Hero imagery is TV-category relevant — cinephile / home theater is squarely my domain
- + Concrete price anchor visible above the fold: "$79.99 after savings"
- + Primary CTA ("Shop now") is present and tappable above the fold

## What's working

- **Single-focus hero:** One product category, one price, one button. On mobile that's the right call — no cognitive overload.
- **Concrete dollar anchor:** "$79.99" is a real number. Even if it's not my budget tier, it signals "there are deals here," which gets me into browse mode.
- **Clean render:** No broken images, no overlapping text, no jank. The gradient background behind the TV looks intentional and sharp.
- **"Deal of the Day" tab in the secondary nav:** That's a tap I'd actually make — I bookmark Best Buy's daily deal page. It's right there.

## What's weak

- **Insignia Fire HD is the wrong hero for me:** I'm not a $79 TV shopper. I want to see what's happening with Sony X90L, LG C4, or at minimum a mid-tier Samsung. Showing me Insignia immediately signals "bargain bin run," not "you came to the right place for your next big purchase."
- **Zero loyalty/Plus visibility:** I pay for Best Buy Plus specifically for the perks. My membership status, any member pricing, or rewards balance is nowhere on this screen. That's a missed hook on a returning customer.
- **No category rails below the fold visible:** From the screenshot, the entire above-the-fold is one hero. I can't see if there's a gaming/audio/home-theater rail waiting for me below — I'd have to scroll to find out if my categories even exist.
- **"Shop now" is the laziest CTA in the playbook:** For a TV deal, even "Shop TVs" or "See all TV deals" gives me a stronger sense of what's behind the tap.
- **No trust signals:** No review count, no "free shipping," no Geek Squad mention. For a $79 TV, I'd still want to see those before I tap.

## Recommendations

- **Swap Insignia for a tiered TV narrative:** Show the Insignia at $79 *and* a Samsung/LG at $499 in the same hero — a "from $79 · up to 85 inches" spread. Catches budget shoppers AND pulls me in as a premium researcher.
- **Pin my Best Buy Plus balance to the top bar:** Even a small "👋 Brody — 2,400 points" strip above the logo would make me feel like this page knows me and keep me from bouncing.
- **Change "Shop now" to "Shop TV deals" with a sub-label like "OLED · QLED · 4K Fire TV":** Tells me immediately there's range here, not just bargain basement.
- **Add a "Deal of the Day" urgency badge to the nav tap:** If today's deal is actually worth my time, put a red dot or a countdown there. That's the one nav item I'd click first.
