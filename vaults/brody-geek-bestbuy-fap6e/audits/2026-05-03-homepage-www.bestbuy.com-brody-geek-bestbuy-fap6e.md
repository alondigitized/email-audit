The actual site — hero, deals, category rails, featured products — is nowhere to be seen; the bottom two-thirds of the viewport is blank white. As a Best Buy Plus member who knows exactly what I'm here for (checking open-box TV deals or new GPU drops), this modal-first experience reads as a friction machine, not a welcome mat.
- **3/10**
- + Returning-shopper hook: "Recently Viewed" dropdown is visible in the top nav — that's a genuine hook for someone like me who browses across sessions
- + Primary CTA above the fold: "Sign In" button is a clear, blue, full-width button with unambiguous copy — passes the visual sniff test even if it's an auth gate, not a shop CTA

## What's working

- **"Recently Viewed" nav link** — small but meaningful signal for a returning shopper; I can at least see it's there without signing in
- **Modal CTAs have clear hierarchy** — "Sign In" (filled blue) vs "Create Account" (outlined) is a good primary/secondary button treatment; no confusion about what each does
- **Search icon is immediately tappable** — if I want to bypass the gate entirely and just search for a Sony headphone model number, I can; the magnifying glass is right there in the top nav
- **Close (X) button is visible** on the modal — they're at least letting me dismiss it, which is better than a forced hard gate

## What's weak

- **The modal is eating the entire homepage** — there is zero product, deal, or category content visible. A Best Buy Plus member landing here sees nothing that rewards their loyalty or justifies staying
- **No Best Buy Plus / My Best Buy rewards mention** — as a paid member, I expect to see my rewards balance or at least a "sign in to see your member pricing" message; this generic "Hi there!" copy could be sent to any stranger off the street
- **Blank white lower half** — either the page content failed to render, or the modal is blocking lazy-loaded content; either way it looks broken to me
- **"Gift Ideas" in the primary nav feels seasonally misaligned** — this isn't December; it reads like a stale nav item that wasn't swapped out for spring
- **No category shortcuts visible** — gaming, TV, audio, computing — the entire reason I'd be here is invisible
- **"Hi there!" copy is lowest-common-denominator** — for a site that knows my purchase history and membership tier, this is an embarrassingly generic opener

## Recommendations

- **Show the homepage behind the modal, not a blank page** — render the hero and at least one category rail in the background so I can see what I'm being asked to sign in *for*; the modal floats above content, it doesn't replace it
- **Personalize the sign-in gate for known Best Buy Plus members** — swap "Hi there!" for "Welcome back, Brody — sign in to see your member pricing and rewards balance"; you have the cookie data to do this
- **Move "Gift Ideas" out of the primary nav** until Q4 — replace it with high-traffic categories like "Gaming & VR" or "Audio" that reflect the actual current shopping season
- **Add a one-line loyalty benefit hook inside the modal** — something like "Sign in to unlock My Best Buy member pricing and open-box alerts" gives me an actual reason to tap that blue button instead of hitting X
