# Homepage snapshot · www.bestbuy.com · 2026-07-11
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-11**
## Executive summary

- Man, I open Best Buy's homepage on my phone and the first thing I get is a modal that body-slams the entire page — "Hi there! For the best experience, be sure to sign in." Meanwhile, everything behind it is a complete white void, not a single pixel of product or promo visible. I have a Best Buy Plus membership and they're treating me like a random first-timer who needs to be converted before I'm allowed to see what's on sale.

## What's working

- **"Sign In" button** is a clean, high-contrast blue filled button — visually unambiguous as the primary action, easy to tap on mobile
- **"Create Account" outline button** gives a clear secondary path for new visitors without competing visually with the primary CTA
- **Header utility bar** (Find a store, Account, Cart) is compact and appropriately placed — the store locator is relevant since I do buy in-store after Geek Squad consults

## What's weak

- **Full-page blank beneath the modal** — the entire body of the homepage failed to render or is hidden; there is literally nothing to see if I dismiss the modal, which kills any reason to stay
- **Modal text is clipped on the left edge** — "i there!" and "or the best experience" are truncated, a visible layout bug that makes the brand look sloppy on mobile
- **No loyalty/member hook in the modal copy** — I'm a Best Buy Plus subscriber; if you're going to gate me, at least tell me I'll see my rewards balance or member pricing. "Best experience" is vague nonsense
- **Zero product, promo, or category content above the fold** — as a guy who researches for weeks before buying, I came here to scan deals and new drops. There's nothing to scan
- **Dark pattern energy** — forcing me to authenticate before I can browse is friction I don't expect from a retailer I already buy from

## Recommendations

- **Show the homepage behind the modal or make it dismissible without a blank page** — even a half-opacity backdrop showing hero content would keep me oriented and curious
- **Fix the modal left-edge truncation** — the "Hi there!" and "For the best experience" text is getting clipped; needs a left-margin fix in the mobile stylesheet
- **Replace generic copy with a member-value pitch** — swap "For the best experience, be sure to sign in" for something like "Sign in to see your Plus member pricing and rewards balance" — that actually gives me a reason to tap
- **Surface one product teaser or deal banner above the modal fold** — even a glimpse of "4K TVs — Members Save Extra 10%" would anchor me while the auth prompt does its thing
