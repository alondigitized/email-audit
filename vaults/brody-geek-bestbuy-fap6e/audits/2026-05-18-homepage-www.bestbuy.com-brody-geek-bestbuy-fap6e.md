# Homepage snapshot · www.bestbuy.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- I opened Best Buy's homepage on my phone ready to eyeball some deals — maybe scope out a new DAC or check if there's an open-box GPU worth grabbing — and instead I got a full-screen modal demanding I sign in before I can even see the store. The actual homepage content below it is completely blank white. As someone who spends serious money here and has Best Buy Plus, this is a terrible first handshake.

## What's working

- **"Sign In" CTA is visually unambiguous** — the filled blue button against the white modal is high contrast, easy to tap on mobile. At least the modal itself is clean and legible.
- **"Create Account" secondary CTA is properly deprioritized** — outlined vs. filled correctly signals hierarchy within the modal.
- **Close (X) button is present** — it's top-right and dismissible, which at least gives me an escape route.

## What's weak

- **The modal is a content blackout** — nothing below it has rendered. I can't see a hero, deals, categories, or anything that would give me a reason to stay. This is a catastrophic mobile first impression.
- **No value prop for signing in** — the copy just says "for the best experience, be sure to sign in." As a Best Buy Plus member, I actually *do* have reasons to sign in (member pricing, free shipping), but this modal doesn't name a single one of them.
- **The header is clipped / cut off on the left** — "[Hi] there!" is truncated, and text is running off the left edge of the modal. That's a layout bug.
- **Zero personalization signal** — as a returning customer with Plus membership, I should see something that acknowledges I've been here before. Instead I get the same cold gate as a first-time visitor.
- **No offer, no urgency, no reason to care** — I came to check deals. There's nothing to look at.

## Recommendations

- **Show something behind the modal** — blur or dim the actual homepage content rather than rendering a blank white void. Give me a glimpse of what I'm signing into so there's pull, not just a wall.
- **Add a value prop line to the modal copy** — something like "See your My Best Buy Plus rewards and exclusive member pricing" would make me actually *want* to tap Sign In instead of dismissing it.
- **Fix the left-edge text truncation** — "i there!" instead of "Hi there!" is a visible layout bug that erodes trust on first contact.
- **Reconsider the trigger timing** — defer the sign-in prompt until I tap something that requires an account (cart, wishlist, member price), not before I've seen a single pixel of the store.
