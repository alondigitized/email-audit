# Homepage snapshot · www.wholefoodsmarket.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- First glance and I immediately clocked the Prime deal — two pints for eight dollars, Ben & Jerry's and Talenti, right at the top. That's exactly the kind of thing I keep my Prime membership active for. The page feels clean, very much Whole Foods — that dark green, the airy overhead shot — but it's basically a single hero and some nav tabs. Not a lot of depth visible, but the one thing it's showing me is something I'd actually act on.

## What's working

- **"2 for $8 with Prime" headline** — this is exactly the Yellow-Tag-deal-literacy I bring to every WF run. They named the Prime price explicitly, didn't bury it. I see it, I clock it, I'm interested.
- **Product selection in the hero** — Ben & Jerry's and Talenti are not random. Those are WF staples, premium-tier pints that regularly sit in my freezer. They picked brands that fit the store's identity AND the customer's pantry.
- **Overhead flat-lay photography** — the ice cream bowls, sprinkles, scoops on the linen napkin — it's clean, aspirational, editorial. Not a box-on-white-background grocery shot. It matches my aesthetic expectations for WF.
- **Quick nav tabs** (Grocery Pickup & Delivery, Weekly Sales, Order Catering) — three taps I'd actually use, especially Weekly Sales. That tab is precisely where I go first every week.
- **Location line** ("Delivering to 91201") — the app already knows where I am. That's one fewer friction point before I start shopping.

## What's weak

- **No CTA button copy visible** — "Sale: 2 for $8" is a headline, not a call to action. I shouldn't have to guess whether tapping the hero takes me to the deal. Give me a button that says "Shop the Deal" or "Add to Cart."
- **Sign In is just a nav icon** — I'm a returning shopper. Show me something relevant to my account: my rewards, my Fresh credit, my repeat order. The generic sign-in icon does nothing for me.
- **No 365 brand presence above the fold** — I use 365 for at least a third of my cart. It's the whole reason I'm loyal to WF over other organic-ish stores. Not even a ghost of it in this viewport.
- **No Free Shipping / Fresh delivery threshold visible** — I use Amazon Fresh for the heavy stuff specifically because of delivery economics. That info isn't surfaced anywhere in this viewport, which is a missed conversion trigger for how I shop.
- **No Weekly Sales tease** — the "Weekly Sales" tab has a yellow SALE badge, which is good, but nothing tells me *what's* on sale this week. A two-line preview ("10 new deals added") would make me tap it immediately.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop Deal" button under the hero copy** — even a small ghost button. Right now the deal is announced but there's no clear action step. Button copy like "Shop 2 for $8 →" costs nothing and closes the loop.
- **Swap the generic Sign In icon for a personalized greeting or Prime badge** — if I'm a known Prime member, show "Hi Willa — Prime deals ready" or a green Prime checkmark. That hook takes ten seconds to recognize and would instantly improve returning-shopper engagement.
- **Add a thin "This week's deals" preview strip below the hero** — two or three deal pills (e.g., "Organic strawberries $3.99 / lb · 365 oat milk 2 for $7") give me a reason to keep scrolling and signal that the Weekly Sales tab is worth tapping.
- **Surface the Amazon Fresh delivery threshold explicitly** — something like "Free delivery on orders $35+" pinned under the location line. That's the number I'm always calculating in my head anyway; make it visible and I'll shop to hit it.
