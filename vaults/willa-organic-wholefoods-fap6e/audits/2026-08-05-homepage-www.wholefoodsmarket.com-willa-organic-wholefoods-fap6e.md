# Homepage snapshot · www.wholefoodsmarket.com · 2026-08-05
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- This reads like standard Whole Foods homepage furniture — clean, on-brand, nothing broken, but it's playing it safe with a generic "Back-to-School" lunchbox push instead of anything that says "we know you." I don't see a single dollar figure, a Prime discount callout, or a 365 mention anywhere on this screen, which for a store built around member pricing feels like a missed layup.

## What's working

- The "Weekly Sales" tile with the yellow SALE badge is exactly the kind of shortcut I look for — I go straight there every visit to check what's Yellow-Tagged this week
- The flat-lay hero image uses recognizable staple brands (Organic Valley Grassmilk, Once Again peanut butter, Perfect Bar) — these are things that are actually in my cart most weeks
- "Delivering to 91801 / Update Location" up top is smart — I always want to confirm my store/delivery zone before I start browsing
- Three-tile quick nav (Pickup & Delivery / Weekly Sales / Order Catering) gets me to the modes I actually use in one tap

## What's weak

- The primary CTA button is literally cut off at the bottom of the screen — I can't even read what it says without scrolling, which is a basic above-the-fold miss
- No mention of Prime member pricing anywhere, which is bizarre for Whole Foods — that's half the reason I check this site in the first place
- "Save on Back-to-School Favorites" has zero specificity — no percentage, no dollar amount, just vague "savings"
- No sign-in personalization visible — it just says "Sign in," no rewards balance, no "welcome back," nothing that acknowledges I'm a repeat shopper

## Recommendations

- Put an actual number in that hero — "20% off Organic Valley & Perfect Bar for Prime members" beats "Save on Back-to-School Favorites" every time
- Pull the CTA button up so it's fully visible without scrolling — right now it's teased and cut off
- Add a Prime badge or member-pricing strip near the top, even a small one — I'm scanning for that signal within the first two seconds
- Swap "Sign in" for something that shows I'm known once I am logged in — recently-ordered items or a rewards nudge would keep me scrolling
