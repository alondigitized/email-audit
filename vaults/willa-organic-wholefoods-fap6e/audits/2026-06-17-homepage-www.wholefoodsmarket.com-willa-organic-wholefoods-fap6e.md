# Homepage snapshot · www.wholefoodsmarket.com · 2026-06-17
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-17**
## Executive summary

- This feels exactly like what it is: Amazon's grocery arm reminding me it exists and that Prime Day is coming. The 365 brand flat-lay in the hero is basically my pantry shelf photographed from above, so the product relevance is real. But the above-fold experience is more "entry lobby" than "let's shop" — it's routing me to a category rather than landing me in one.

## What's working

- **365 brand hero flatlay** — Eggs, marinara, pasta, whole wheat bread all in one shot. That's not aspirational grocery imagery, that's my actual cart. It earns credibility fast.
- **"This Prime Day" sweepstakes banner** — I track every Prime grocery promo. The green dismissible banner catches my eye without being obnoxious, and "win a year of FREE groceries" is genuinely compelling copy.
- **"Weekly Sales" tile with the yellow SALE badge** — As someone who knows the difference between a real Yellow-Tag deal and just a yellow sticker, having this surfaced as a primary nav tile is the right call.
- **Location-aware delivery strip** — "Delivering to 91801" is personalized context. I know I can order for pickup or delivery without hunting for it.
- **Search bar copy** — "Search Grocery Pickup & Delivery" is functional and accurate. No fluff.

## What's weak

- **The "Save 40%" offer is literally cut off** — Text reads "Limited-Time Offer: Save 40% on a Year of $0" then disappears below the fold. That's a teaser that makes me anxious, not excited. I need to see what I'm saving 40% on before I care.
- **No signed-in state / loyalty hook** — I'm a weekly shopper. There's no "Welcome back," no Prime member badge, no saved-items nudge. The header treats me like a stranger.
- **No Prime member pricing visible** — I'm Amazon Prime and I shop WFM for the member deals. Nothing above the fold acknowledges that relationship exists.
- **"Order Catering" as a top-three tile** — That's not how I shop weekly. Feels like it's stealing real estate from something I'd actually tap, like "365 Everyday Deals" or "Your Usual Items."
- **No people in the imagery** — The flatlay is beautiful product photography, but there's no aspirational lifestyle scene with anyone who looks like me. It's a catalog, not a community.

## Recommendations

- **Surface Prime member pricing above the fold** — A small badge or strip that says "Your Prime member price: save X% on 365 items" would immediately make me feel seen and give me a reason to tap.
- **Replace "Order Catering" with a "Your Usual Items" or "365 Deals" tile** — Weekly shoppers don't think about catering. Give that tile to a replenishment or staples shortcut.
- **Reveal the full "Save 40%" offer** — Either move it up above the fold or rewrite the hero copy so it doesn't orphan the offer. Cut-off price copy kills momentum.
- **Add a "signed-in" personalization layer** — Even a "Hi Willa, your order from last week →" strip in the location bar zone would dramatically increase my engagement and skip the "do I even have an account here?" mental tax.
