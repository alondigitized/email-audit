# Homepage snapshot · www.wholefoodsmarket.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Opened the site and the first thing I see is a 2 for $8 Ben & Jerry's and Talenti deal — with Prime, spelled out right in the headline. That's my language. This page is doing a clean summer ice cream push and using the Prime hook as the price gate, which is smart because I already have Prime and I know exactly what "with Prime" means. It's transactional, not warm, but for a weekly grocery run that's fine by me.

## What's working

- **"2 for $8 with Prime" headline:** Specific price, specific brands, specific membership qualifier — all three in one sentence. I know immediately whether this deal is for me (it is) without doing any math.
- **Overhead flat-lay food photography:** The Ben & Jerry's / Talenti spread with the cherry bowls and vintage scoops looks genuinely appetizing. This is the kind of shot that makes me add something that wasn't on the list.
- **"Weekly Sales" tab in the nav strip:** Second position, clearly labeled, one tap. I use this every single week and it's not buried under three menus.
- **"Delivering to 91801" delivery bar:** Shows local delivery is confirmed before I even start browsing. I'm not guessing whether they serve my zip code.

## What's weak

- **No CTA button visible above the fold:** The deal copy is strong but there's no "Shop this deal" button in frame. The offer sells itself — the tap target should match.
- **"Sign in" header on a returning shopper:** I'm in the app with a Prime account. Showing me "Sign in" like I'm a stranger is friction that shouldn't exist in 2026.
- **No visual Prime badge on the hero price:** "With Prime" is text-only. A green badge or member-price pill would communicate the deal faster than reading the full headline copy.
- **No delivery threshold or free-delivery callout:** That's usually my tipping point for adding one more item. It's not visible anywhere above the fold.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop this deal" button directly on the hero image** — not below the fold. The offer copy is already doing the selling; the button just needs to catch up.
- **Surface a Prime member badge or personalized "Hi Willa" state in the header** — Amazon knows exactly who I am; the Whole Foods site should behave accordingly.
- **Pin a "Free delivery on orders over $X" strip** below the nav tabs — that nudge is the difference between a 2-item cart and a full weekly haul.
- **Add an expiry or "through Sunday" line under the deal headline** — the lack of any deadline makes me wonder if this is actually a sale or just a permanently re-tagged shelf price.
