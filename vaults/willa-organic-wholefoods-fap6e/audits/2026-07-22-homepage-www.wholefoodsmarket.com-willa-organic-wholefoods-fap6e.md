# Homepage snapshot · www.wholefoodsmarket.com · 2026-07-22
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-22**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is exactly what I expect when I open Whole Foods on my phone — clean, green-branded, very "farmer's market but make it app." Right now they're pushing a summer savings story around meal prep, which honestly tracks with where my head is in July. It's a grocery brand doing grocery things, no surprises, but also no wow.

## What's working

- **The hero food spread** — multiple bright dishes, summer colors, fresh fruit and protein — this reads as "aspirational Tuesday dinner," which is exactly why I shop here instead of Safeway.
- **"Weekly Sales" tile with the yellow SALE badge** — as a WF regular I know this is my Yellow-Tag discount section and I go there first; surfacing it in the top nav row is smart.
- **Subhead copy** — "Simplify summer meal prep with savings on easy fruit-forward dishes. Save on made-in-house proteins, ready-to-heat sides and best-of-season fruit" — this is the sentence I would write to describe why I order from here. Genuinely resonant.
- **Grocery Pickup & Delivery tile** — as an Amazon Fresh user, confirming delivery mode is right there without hunting.
- **Location bar** — "Delivering to 91801 · Update Location" is small but I notice it. I appreciate that the app already knows where I am.

## What's weak

- **No Prime/member pricing hook** — I'm a Prime member and I pay for that. The page doesn't acknowledge me at all. No "Your Prime savings this week" banner, no member badge, nothing. That's the biggest miss for a returning customer like me.
- **No specific offer amount** — "Sweet Savings" is a headline, not a deal. What's 25% off? What's the buy-2-get-1? I can't tell without tapping.
- **No CTA button** — the subtext copy cuts off mid-sentence ("to lighten…") and I have to scroll to find out what action to take. There's no visible "Shop Now" or "See Deals" button above the fold.
- **No urgency cue** — Weekly Sales presumably expire, but there's no "ends Sunday" or countdown visible here. I'd feel more compelled to tap now if I knew.
- **Sign in is cold** — "Sign in" with an icon is not the same as "Hey Willa, your rewards." The sign-in entry point exists but offers no incentive to actually do it from this screen.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Prime member savings strip** directly under the location bar — one line: "Prime members save extra this week · See your deals →" — I should know immediately that my membership is working for me.
- **Put a specific deal callout in the hero subtext** — "Save up to 30% on summer proteins" or "365 brand items from $2.99" — anchor it with a number so the value proposition is concrete, not just vibes.
- **Add a visible CTA button** at the bottom of the hero block before the scroll — "Shop Weekly Sales" in WF green, right below the headline. One button, one action.
- **Show a deal deadline** on the Weekly Sales tile — even just "Ends Sun 7/27" next to the SALE badge would make me tap it right now instead of bookmarking and forgetting.
