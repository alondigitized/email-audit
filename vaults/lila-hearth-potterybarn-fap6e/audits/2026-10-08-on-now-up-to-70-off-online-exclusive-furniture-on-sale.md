# On now! UP TO 70% OFF + Online exclusive: Furniture ON SALE
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a furniture-led sale email that repeats the same-day "The Fall Sale is ON 🍁 Up to 70% off" blast. The dark-green banner shouts "The Fall Sale: Up to 70% off", and right under it a second headline, "Online Exclusive: Select Furniture Now on Sale", takes over. The four "NOW ON SALE" collection tiles (Aubrey, Toscana, Livingston, Farmhouse) are clean and shoppable. The offer is clear, but the email gives you no price, no deadline and no sense of what "select" covers.
- It's competent but redundant. I'd expect a subscriber who got the 70%-off send this morning and the "Heritage quilts" send the same day to see this as the third promo in one day. The "Online exclusive" angle is the only new idea. It's undercut by the lack of any concrete price or discount per collection. The long tail (Recommended, New Arrivals, Design Crew, app, credit card, Affirm) turns a focused furniture push into a general catalog.
- Judgement: solid execution, weak differentiation. It's a mid-tier send that works as a reminder and would do better with a deadline and real prices.

## What's working

- The "Up to 70% off" banner at the very top puts the offer in the first screen.
- The "Shop Furniture Sale" button is clear and sits right under the headline.
- The red "NOW ON SALE" tags on each collection image reinforce the sale without extra copy.
- The four lifestyle collection shots are high quality and show room context.
- The layout is clean, and I can't see any broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- Two stacked headlines ("Fall Sale: Up to 70%" and "Select Furniture Now on Sale") compete, and "select" quietly limits the offer.
- No prices, percentages or "from $" appear on any collection tile, so the 70% claim is never tied to a product.
- There's no deadline or end date, so "On now!" carries no real urgency.
- The email is very long. The recommendations, New Arrivals, Design Crew, app, credit-card and Affirm modules dilute the furniture message.
- The legal block at the bottom is a dense wall of small text. It sits close to the offer language and makes the exclusions feel buried.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a specific price or discount on each collection tile (e.g. "Aubrey Collection: up to 40% off" or "from $X"). This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Merge the two headlines into one, such as "Up to 70% off furniture, online only", and state the end date.
- 3. Cut the lower modules (Design Crew, app, credit card, Affirm) or move them to a slim footer strip, so the email stays about furniture.
- 4. Add one hero product with price and a star rating or review count to give it social proof.
- 5. Space out sends. Fold this into the earlier sale email or differentiate it, instead of sending a third near-identical promo in one day.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Aubrey, Toscana, Livingston & more — online-only savings on now.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, 650px XHTML Transitional template with working VML/MSO fallbacks, but about 45 images have no alt text. The source also contains stray server-side code in a `<style>` block, and it sends the recipient's address in plaintext HTTP pixel URLs. QA data can't confirm authentication or one-click unsubscribe, because the relay may have stripped those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 49 tracking links (for example `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Imti…`) were skipped by the HTTP probe. Their destinations are unverified.
- **Plaintext tracking pixel with PII:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is served over HTTP and carries the recipient's email address in the query string. This is a privacy leak, and clients may block the pixel.
- **PII in other image URLs:** `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is also loaded as an image with the address in the URL.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEv…/cat/1` is a Turn (Amobee) ad-tech pixel with no alt text.
- **Mixed content:** two non-HTTPS creatives, `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`. Some clients may block them.
- **Stale asset paths:** the 2022 banner path above, and a body folder named `2025/US/1016_THU_AM_DTCOnlyFurnitureSale` (an October 2025 campaign) in an email sent 2026-10-08. This suggests template reuse, so check that the creatives are current.
- **Cross-brand asset:** the spacer image is `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`, hosted on a West Elm domain. It is a minor dependency risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** roughly 45 images have no alt text. Examples:
  - Hero and body slices: `H26_TOP_FallSale.jpg` and `slices_01`–`07.jpg` in `1016_THU_AM_DTCOnlyFurnitureSale/`.
  - Product images: `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`, `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-1245-1-l.jpg`, `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg` and others.
  - Footer: `Email-Footer/slices_01`–`18.jpg`.

  With images blocked, the sale offer, product names and footer links all disappear. The logo image does have `alt="POTTERY BARN"`.
- **Stray code in CSS:** the second `<style>` block ends with literal PHP text: `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store…"); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. It is invalid CSS and likely a templating leak. It can corrupt the rule that follows in strict parsers.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility issue.
- **No document language:** `<html xmlns=…>` has no `lang` attribute.
- **Missing table roles:** some layout tables, such as the 20px spacer table, lack `role="presentation"`. Others have it.
- **Preheader:** the text is present and hidden correctly (`display:none; mso-hide:all`). No issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unresolved merge tokens (such as `%%…%%` or `{{…}}`). The recipient email is injected correctly into the two URLs above. The source was truncated, so the rest of the body wasn't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** both were not found. The QA output attributes this to AgentMail relay stripping, so it is unconfirmed rather than a proven sender defect. Large mailbox providers require both for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is also likely a relay artifact. The sender domain `e.potterybarn.com` can't be verified from this data.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** the footer is in the truncated portion of the source, so I couldn't verify either.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…` links are opaque, encoded redirects. UTM parameters and landing destinations can't be confirmed, and the probe skipped them.
- Product images use `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/…` hosts, which suggests the product links share that domain, but no landing-page URLs were visible.
- No evidence of a mismatch with the subject's "UP TO 70% OFF + Online exclusive" claim was found.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to every content image, and `alt=""` to spacers and tracking pixels. Prioritize the hero and sale slices and the footer links.
2. Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP lines from the `<style>` block.
3. Serve `svopn.potterybarn.com` and the two `edm.potterybarn.com` assets over HTTPS. Stop putting the recipient's email address in image and pixel URLs, and use an opaque ID instead.
4. Fill in `<title>`, add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and drop `maximum-scale=1`.
5. Retire the 2022 and 2025-dated asset paths, or confirm they're intentionally evergreen.
6. Re-run the QA on the raw `.eml`, not the AgentMail copy. That would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC).
7. Resolve the tracking redirects in a browser or with a redirect-following probe, to check UTM tagging and landing-page alignment.
