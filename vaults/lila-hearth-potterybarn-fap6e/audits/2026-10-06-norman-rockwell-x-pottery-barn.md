# 🎄 Norman Rockwell x Pottery Barn
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- This is a brand-collaboration launch, and the hero does its job. A festive Norman Rockwell holiday table, with a Santa plate, a "Merry Christmas" mug and red-ribboned napkins, sits under a "SHOP NOW" button. It's the most giftable and seasonal creative Pottery Barn has sent this week. It also follows the 10-04 "Inspired by your favorite holiday movies..." send, which was another licensed collaboration. Two licensed-collab sends in three days start to feel like a pattern.
- The email has no price, no deadline, no free-shipping hook and no reason to act today. After the hero, the body drifts into generic modules: "Recommended Just For You" (curtain rod, ghost collection, platform bed, dining table, rug), a Christmas Shop banner, Design Crew, the app, a credit card and Affirm. Those modules dilute the one strong idea. The collab gets about four images before the email turns into a catch-all.
- Verdict: strong visual hook and a credible seasonal moment, but it's all mood and no motive. It's a solid 6, and it could be a 7 or 8 with one concrete offer or one hero product.

## What's working

- The hero is on-theme and warm. The Rockwell plate, mug and napkins read as nostalgic and giftable.
- The "Norman Rockwell x Pottery Barn" lockup is clear, so the collab is obvious at a glance.
- The "SHOP NOW" button sits right on the hero, and the "SHOP THE FULL COLLECTION" button repeats the CTA after the product montage.
- The italic serif line, "Step into the nostalgic world of a Norman Rockwell Christmas...", sets the tone cleanly.
- The render is clean. I see no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- There is no offer. No price, percentage, shipping perk or deadline appears in the creative.
- The "Recommended Just For You" block is off-theme. The curtain rod, ghost collection and dining table dilute the collab story.
- The collab montage doesn't name or price any products, and nothing is linked individually. Everything funnels to a collection page.
- The footer stack is long and heavy: the app, the credit card with its fine print, Affirm, social links and a legal wall. The email is long for one message.
- The Christmas Shop and Design Crew banners add competing CTAs after the hero has made its pitch.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add one concrete hook to the hero, such as "Limited-edition," "Ships free," or a "Starting at $X" tag on the plate set. The Heritage Quilts send on 10-04 ("from $199", "Quilts Ship Free") scored a 7 on exactly that.
- 2. Replace the generic montage with three or four named, priced collab products, each linked to its own page: the plate, mug, napkins and quilt.
- 3. Cut the "Recommended Just For You" block, or fill it with collab-adjacent items, so the email stays on one story.
- 4. Trim the footer to one secondary CTA, either the credit card or the app, not the whole stack.
- 5. Make the subject say what the collection is and add a reason to open now.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Santa plates, mugs and quilts, exclusive to Pottery Barn.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML Transitional layout that is heavily image-sliced (hero, product and footer images), with about 50 alt-less images, three plain-HTTP image requests, and the recipient address in cleartext in image URLs. SPF/DKIM and List-Unsubscribe could not be verified because the AgentMail relay did not expose those headers. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer and the unsubscribe link were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links not verified.** 51 tracking links were skipped by the probe (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`, an ESP redirect domain). Destination status and redirect chains are unknown. No broken links were confirmed.
- **Plain-HTTP tracking pixel.** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is not HTTPS, so clients may block it and open tracking may be undercounted. It also sends the address unencrypted.
- **Third-party and secondary pixels.** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` is a third-party ad-network pixel. `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/27ff0ffb-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...` is a second open pixel. Neither is a defect, but there are three separate open-tracking beacons.
- **Cross-brand asset host.** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a Pottery Barn email loading a spacer from the West Elm domain, which adds a dependency on another property's CDN.
- **Stale asset path.** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` is a 2022 asset reused in a 2026 send, with no HTTPS version.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 50 images.** This covers the whole Norman Rockwell hero (`H26_B1_Collab_NormanRockwell/slices_01–08.jpg`), the product images (`cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`, `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-1245-1-l.jpg`, and others) and 18 footer slices (`Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`). With images off, or in a screen reader, the content and links are unreadable. The plain-text part is also more than 70% URLs (9,900 of 14,057 characters).
- **Tracking pixels without `alt=""`.** `coreemail.html`, `d.turn.com/.../cat/1`, `svopn.potterybarn.com` and `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/...` are flagged. Because they are decorative, they should carry `alt=""` rather than none.
- **Plain-HTTP images:** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` and the `svopn` pixel. These may be blocked or show mixed-content warnings.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **No `lang` attribute** on `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" ...>`.
- **`maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta** (`width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1`) disables pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Server code leaked into CSS.** The second `<style>` block ends with `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store..."); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. This is invalid CSS from a template or build artifact. It sits after the last rule, so it probably does not break rendering, but it is a hygiene issue.
- **Layout tables without `role="presentation"`.** The spacer table (`<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><tr><td height="20">&nbsp;`) and the outer wrapper tables lack it, although the content tables have it.
- **Other.** The Google Fonts `<link>` (Montserrat) is dropped by many clients, and the CSS has no fallback beyond system fonts. Attribute-selector media queries (`table[class="wrapper"]`) are old-style and unsupported in some clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source. The recipient address is substituted in `coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` and in `sv_sveme=`.
- **PII in cleartext.** The email address appears as a plain query parameter in image URLs, and one of them (`svopn`) is over HTTP. This leaks the address to network observers and into proxy and CDN logs. It is a privacy weakness, not a rendering bug.
- The `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` values on the `clickcp` pixel are populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA notes say this may be an AgentMail relay artifact, so it is unconfirmed. If the headers are truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The relay is the likely cause. The From domain `e.potterybarn.com` and the click domain `click.e.potterybarn.com` are the same organizational domain, which is favorable for alignment, but nothing was verified.
- **Footer not auditable.** The unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated source. No violation is confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All links are ESP-wrapped (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=<opaque token>`), so the final landing URLs and any UTM or campaign parameters cannot be inspected. Continuity cannot be assessed.
- `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` is set. It only affects the HTML document's own referrer behavior and does not change click attribution.
- Product images load from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`, so the images depend on the live site CDN. A site-side change could break them.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the hero slices, product images and footer slices. Use `alt=""` on spacers and pixels. Add live text or a bulletproof button for the key CTAs so they survive image blocking.
2. Move the three `http://` assets to HTTPS (`Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, the 2022 `slices_02.jpg`, the `svopn` pixel). Replace the 2022 asset with a current one.
3. Stop putting the raw email address in image query strings. Use an opaque recipient ID or a hashed token, and serve it over HTTPS.
4. Fill in `<title>` and add `lang="en"` to `<html>`. Remove `maximum-scale=1`. Add `role="presentation"` to the remaining layout tables.
5. Remove the stray PHP `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block and fix the template.
6. Host `spacer_0.gif` on `edm.potterybarn.com` instead of `edm.westelm.com`.
7. Re-run the audit against the raw message with full headers (not the relay copy) to confirm List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Also probe the SFMC redirect chains to capture final URLs and UTM parameters.
