# LAST DAY❗️EXTRA 20% OFF SELECT STYLES
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-09**
## Executive summary

- Same burgundy Labor Day banner Pottery Barn has run for two weeks straight — "Extra 20% off / Select styles. Plus new deals up to 70% off" — now with a "last day"-repeating marquee at the very top and a "LAST DAY❗️" subject to manufacture urgency on the way out. It's a legitimate final call, but visually it's indistinguishable from the 6+ near-identical sends already logged in this brand history, right down to the same dual CTAs and category grid.
- Below the fold it's the standard Pottery Barn wall: a category grid (Bedding, Furniture, Sofas & Chairs, Lighting, Tabletop & Bar, Bath, Rugs, Outdoor), a "Recommended Just For You" personalized product grid, a Halloween Shop teaser, Design Crew and app/rewards footer modules. Competent, on-brand, zero render bugs — but it's coasting on a stale template at the exact moment (last day) when a brand should be sharpening the offer, not repeating it verbatim.
- Verdict: functional deadline email, but it under-leverages true last-day urgency — no countdown timer, no visual difference from prior sends, and the subject's urgency isn't echoed anywhere prominent in the hero itself beyond the scrolling ticker.

## What's working

- The scrolling "last day / last day" ticker at the very top is a genuine (if subtle) urgency device not seen in prior sends.
- Hero offer (Extra 20% off) and both CTAs (Shop Extra 20% Off, Shop All Sale) are large, legible, and unmissable.
- "Recommended Just For You" personalized grid is a real signal of segmentation, not just generic merchandising.
- Clean category grid with clear photography and no layout breakage anywhere in the render.

## What's weak

- The hero itself doesn't say "last day" or "ends today" anywhere in the burgundy banner — all urgency is confined to the tiny scrolling ticker text, easy to miss.
- No countdown clock or time-of-day cutoff, which is the single easiest way to make a true last-day deadline feel real.
- Visually identical to at least four prior burgundy Labor Day sends in this history — subscriber fatigue risk is high by this point in the cadence.
- "Exclusions apply. Prices as marked." sits directly under the CTA, a small honesty-eroding note right at the moment of decision.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline inside the hero banner itself (e.g., "Extra 20% off — ends tonight at midnight") instead of relying on the scrolling ticker alone; this is the highest-leverage fix since it's the true last chance and currently reads like every other day.
- 2. Add a simple countdown timer graphic under the CTA — proven to lift last-day conversion vs. static banners.
- 3. Differentiate the visual template from the prior 6+ sends (new color accent, new hero photography) so loyal openers don't pattern-match this as "yet another burgundy banner" and skip it.
- 4. Move "Recommended Just For You" higher, above the generic category grid, since personalized picks are more likely to convert on a deadline day than browsing.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: Extra 20% off everything on sale`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call — your 20% off expires at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus new markdowns up to 70% off — but only for a few more hours.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Don't wait — Labor Day savings close tonight.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 100% of `<img>` tags in the parsed source are missing `alt` text (48 instances flagged by QA) — screen readers and blocked-image fallback both degrade to nothing; given this is an image-heavy layout, blocked-image users see a blank email.
- Two images load over `http://` instead of `https://`: `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` (edm.potterybarn.com) and `slices_02.jpg` (edm.potterybarn.com, 2022 legacy asset) — likely to be blocked outright by Gmail/Outlook image proxies, breaking footer rendering.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header: not found.** No RFC 2369 header captured — one-click unsubscribe may not be exposed to mailbox providers, increasing spam-foldering risk at Gmail/Yahoo (both require this for bulk senders).

- **Authentication-Results header: not found.** SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from this capture — noted as likely an artifact of the AgentMail relay not preserving/exposing the header rather than confirmed auth failure; recommend verifying directly at the receiving MTA.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Primary CTAs route through `click.e.potterybarn.com` tracking redirects rather than direct UTM-tagged landing URLs — final destination and UTM parameters are not visible in this truncated source (redirect resolution needed to confirm landing-page alignment).
- Plain-text version is >70% URL characters (9,924/14,067 chars) — indicates the plain-text alternative is dominated by tracking/redirect link boilerplate rather than readable content, which can hurt spam-filter scoring and provides a poor fallback experience for plain-text readers.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers (RFC 2369 / RFC 8058) to meet Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements and reduce spam-folder placement risk.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content-bearing images (product shots, hero banners); decorative spacers/pixels can use `alt=""`.
3. Migrate the two remaining `http://` image sources to `https://` to prevent client-side blocking.
4. Remove the stray PHP `header()` calls from the `<style>` block in `<head>` — dead code suggesting a template-rendering leak upstream.
5. Serve the open-tracking pixel (`svopn.potterybarn.com`) over HTTPS to avoid mixed-content blocking of open metrics.
6. Slim the plain-text alternative to reduce URL-to-text ratio; excessive tracking-link boilerplate in plain-text can depress deliverability scoring.
7. Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status directly at the MTA, since `Authentication-Results` wasn't captured via the AgentMail relay.
