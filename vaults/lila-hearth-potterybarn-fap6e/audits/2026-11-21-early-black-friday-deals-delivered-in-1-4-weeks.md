# EARLY BLACK FRIDAY DEALS delivered in 1-4 weeks
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is the same Early Black Friday Pottery Barn template that has run since 11/16, with a new hero. The headline is "In Stock & On Sale," and the new angle is availability and fast delivery. It follows the 11/21 send "UP TO 70% OFF ❤️ Top Early Black Friday picks." That makes at least a dozen near-identical sends in six days.
- The subject says "delivered in 1-4 weeks," a delivery-time promise. The body never mentions it. The hero says "In Stock & On Sale," and the only price signal is "UP TO 50% OFF" in the Christmas Shop block, far down the page. The 70% figure from the earlier sends is gone from the visible hero. The email has no single offer, and the subject promise and the body don't match.
- The layout is clean, with a clear hero, a category grid and a "Recommended Just For You" product block. But the unlabeled products, the buried discount and the stacked secondary modules (Design Crew sweepstakes, app, credit card, Affirm) dilute it. It reads as inventory rather than an offer.

## What's working

- The hero has a clear "SHOP NOW" button over a warm, seasonal living-room scene.
- The category grid (Furniture, Sofas & Chairs, Bedding and so on) gives fast, tappable navigation.
- "Pick Up Thanksgiving Essentials in Store" with "BUY ONLINE, GET IT TODAY" is a useful, time-relevant angle.
- The product grid has six distinct items with names, which gives specific click targets.
- No render bugs are visible. Images load and the layout is clean.

## What's weak

- The subject's "delivered in 1-4 weeks" is not echoed anywhere in the body, so it reads as a delay warning rather than a benefit.
- No price or discount is shown on the six recommended products.
- The headline offer, "UP TO 50% OFF," sits in the Christmas Shop block near the bottom. It is lower than the 70% from earlier sends, and it conflicts with the hero's "On Sale" claim.
- The product grid has no ratings or reviews.
- The bottom third piles on the sweepstakes, app download, credit card and Affirm modules, plus dense legal text. The email feels long and the primary action gets lost.
- Cadence fatigue: this is the same template as the 11/20 and 11/21 sends, so it adds nothing new.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one hard offer in the hero, such as "Up to 70% off, in stock and ships in 1-4 weeks," so the subject and body agree.
- 2. Show sale and original prices on every product tile, and add star ratings.
- 3. Cut the app, credit card and Affirm modules, or collapse them into a single footer line.
- 4. Replace "SHOP NOW" with category-specific CTAs such as "Shop in-stock sofas."
- 5. Cut the send frequency, or segment by category interest so repeat recipients get different content.
- **Subject Alt A:** `In stock + up to 70% off: ships in 1-4 weeks`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Early Black Friday: furniture that ships fast`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sofas, rugs and dining tables, ready to ship at up to 70% off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Order today and get it before the holidays. Online pickup available.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML layout with image-slice content, 61 click-tracking links that were not probed, and widespread missing alt text. The QA run found no hard failures (0 issues, 4 warnings). Header-level compliance and authentication could not be verified because the AgentMail relay did not expose the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links were not validated.** 61 links on `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…` were skipped as tracking/redirect domains. Destination status and landing-page alignment are unverified.
- **Plain-text part is more than 70% URLs.** 12,041 of 17,070 characters are URLs, which is a spam-filter risk. The tracked URLs are long and opaque.
- **Several tracking pixels are loaded as images.**
  - `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` is a third-party ad-network pixel.
  - `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/29ab4c13-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` is the open pixel.
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=…` is a plain-HTTP open pixel.
  - Using three or more pixels adds third-party requests and privacy exposure.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text is pervasive.** The QA run flags about 60 images, including:
  - `slices_01.jpg` to `slices_16.jpg` in `/pb/2026/US/1121_SAT_AM2_InStock&OnSale/`
  - `slices_01.jpg` to `slices_18.jpg` in `/Email-Footer/`
  - the product images `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-125-l.jpg`, `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-l.jpg`, `faux-fur-ruched-throw-l.jpg` and the stocking images
  - the `H26_Bottom_*` banners
  - `spacer_0.gif`, which should have `alt=""`
  - With images off, the sliced content is unreadable.
- **Three images use `http://`.** These are `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `slices_02.jpg` in `/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/`, and the `svopn` pixel. Mixed content may be blocked or rewritten by clients.
- **The `<title>` is empty** (`<title></title>`).
- **The `<html>` element has no `lang` attribute.**
- **`maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta** disables pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem.
- **A stray PHP snippet sits inside a `<style>` block:** `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store…")`. It is invalid CSS, and it could break parsing of the rules that follow in strict clients.
- **Layout tables are inconsistent.** Some have `role="presentation"`, but others, such as the bare `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0">` spacer, do not.
- **A remote webfont is declared but may be unused.** `fonts.googleapis.com` Montserrat is linked, and most clients strip `<link>` tags anyway.
- **`meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer"` and `robots` meta tags** have no effect in email.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **The recipient email address is exposed in the clear in image URLs.**
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?…&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - The second is over plain HTTP, so the address travels unencrypted. Both can end up in proxy and server logs.
- **No unresolved merge tokens were found** in the visible source. The HTML is truncated, so this is not exhaustive.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` was not found,** per the QA warning. This may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay and is unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was not found.** One-click unsubscribe support is unverified, and Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unverifiable. The sending domain is `e.potterybarn.com`, with a tracking domain on the same parent.
- **The visible unsubscribe link and physical address are not verifiable.** They sit in the footer, and the supplied HTML was truncated before it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters cannot be confirmed.** Destinations are hidden behind opaque `qs=` redirect tokens, and the HTTP probes were skipped.
- **Direct `potterybarn.com` image URLs are the only visible site references,** and they are not landing pages.
- **Campaign identifiers are present on the open pixels** (`sv_cid=0053_00597`, `mi_cid=677b44e6831b79f3`). They are not verifiable against the site.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all sliced images and product images, and `alt=""` to spacers and pixels.
2. Move the open pixels and the `coreemail.html` image to HTTPS. Stop putting the recipient email address in query strings and use an opaque ID instead.
3. Remove the PHP `header()` text from the `<style>` block, add a `lang` attribute, add a meaningful `<title>`, and drop `maximum-scale=1`.
4. Re-run the audit against the raw RFC 822 message to check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, and to inspect the full footer.
5. Resolve a sample of the `click.e.potterybarn.com` links to confirm landing-page alignment and UTM parameters.
6. Reduce the plain-text URL density with shorter links, or a plain-text part that carries fewer links.
