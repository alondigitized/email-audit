# Just in! Our new holiday catalog is here 🎄
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a brand-moment send, not a promotion. "The Holiday Catalog" hero, with a hand holding the "A Nostalgic Holiday" cover, is clean and on-brand, and the seasonal timing is right. Compared with the offers from this week, it has nothing to act on. On Oct 4, "Heritage holiday quilts, from $199" had a price anchor, and on Oct 3, "All tabletop now ships free!" had a shipping hook. This one has neither, and the "YOUR FIRST LOOK" button doesn't say where it leads.
- Below the hero the email sprawls into about six modules: category nav, design classes, a recommended grid, the Christmas Shop, Design Crew, the app, the rewards card and Affirm. The tall render has no single next step. The strongest read is "pleasant, well-made, low-urgency". It will do well with people already in a holiday-shopping mood and poorly with anyone who needs a reason to click today.

## What's working

- The hero is a strong, scannable opener. "THE HOLIDAY CATALOG" on a warm wood backdrop, with the catalog photo, sets the season instantly.
- The dark green category block (Pillows & Decor, Armchairs, Bedding, Bedroom Furniture, Dining Furniture, Tabletop) gives easy entry points.
- The "Recommended Just For You" grid links to named products (Lit Ghost Collection, Scarlett Upholstered Bed, Reeva Handwoven Rug, Hydrocotton Quick-Dry Towel).
- The "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks" banner at the top is a useful reassurance for holiday timing.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text, and the brand voice matches earlier sends.

## What's weak

- There is no concrete offer anywhere: no percent off, no free shipping, no dollar threshold. The only money line is buried near the bottom ("Earn 10% back in rewards") and applies only to a credit card.
- "YOUR FIRST LOOK" is vague, and "SHOP NOW" under the Christmas Shop is generic.
- The email is very long, with a dense legal block at the end. The hierarchy fragments into competing modules (classes, Design Crew, app, card, Affirm).
- The recommended grid shows no prices, reviews or ratings, so there's nothing to compare or trust at a glance.
- The "Lit Ghost Collection" sits oddly in a Christmas catalog email. Both "free design" modules (in-store classes and Design Crew) overlap.
- The cadence is heavy. This is roughly the sixth Pottery Barn send in four days (Oct 2 to Oct 5).

## Recommendations

- 1. Add one dated, concrete incentive to the hero, such as "Free shipping on holiday décor through Sunday" or a member price, and make it the subject's second half.
- 2. Rewrite the hero CTA to name the destination: "Browse the Holiday Catalog" or "Shop Holiday Décor".
- 3. Cut the lower modules to two (recommended products and one service module). Merge the in-store classes and Design Crew blocks, and move the app, card and Affirm blocks into a slim footer strip.
- 4. Add prices and star ratings to the recommended products. Replace the Halloween ghost item with a Christmas best-seller.
- 5. Space the catalog send away from the other promos, or fold it into a single weekly digest.
- **Subject Alt A:** `The holiday catalog is here: shop it now, ships free`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your first look: 2026 holiday collection 🎄`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Quilts, tabletop and trees, with free shipping on holiday décor through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `In stock and delivered in 1-4 weeks. Browse the pages and shop the favorites.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is mostly table-based and has a preheader and Outlook conditionals. It has about 40 content images with no `alt` text, 3 `http://` resources, stray PHP code inside a `<style>` block, and the recipient address in the URLs of several image and pixel requests. Authentication and unsubscribe checks could not be confirmed. The QA run reported 0 hard failures and 3 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracking links not probed:** 52 links on `click.e.potterybarn.com` (e.g. `/u/?qs=ABB7InYiOjEs…`) are opaque redirects. QA skipped them, so destinations and redirect health are unverified.
- **Recipient address in pixel and image URLs:**
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
- **Plaintext email in a query string:** it can end up in proxy and CDN logs and in referrers. The `svopn` request is also cleartext HTTP.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` is an ad-tech beacon, loaded alongside the first-party `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/…` open pixel.
- **Cross-brand asset:** the spacer `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on the West Elm domain. It is a minor dependency risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** 44 `<img>` elements have no `alt`. About 40 are content images: `slices_02`–`slices_10` in the `1005_MON_AM_HolidayCatalog` set, `slices_01`–`18` in `Email-Footer`, the product images such as `scarlett-upholstered-bed-1-l.jpg`, and the banners `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg` and `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`. Because many are wrapped in `<a>`, those links have no accessible name, and the email is unreadable with images blocked. The other four are tracking pixels, where an empty `alt=""` is acceptable.
- **Non-HTTPS images:** three images use `http://`: `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, the 2022 `0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`, and the `svopn` pixel. They may be blocked or rewritten by clients, which breaks the layout or the open tracking.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Stray PHP in a style block:** the second `<style>` block contains `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: …"); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. It is invalid CSS and can corrupt the rule that follows it in strict parsers, and it indicates a template-generation leak.
- **Zoom disabled:** the viewport meta has `maximum-scale=1`, which blocks pinch-zoom.
- **Fixed widths:** the layout uses 650px-wide fixed tables. The mobile rules rely on attribute selectors (`table[class="wrapper"]`, `img[class="photo"]`), which are poorly supported in Gmail and some clients. No element in the visible HTML uses those classes, so they may be dead rules.
- **Leftover CSS:** it includes commented-out viewport meta tags and a stray `.createBrk`.
- **Web font:** the Google Fonts `<link>` for Montserrat is stripped by most clients and falls back silently.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible HTML.
- The recipient email is injected correctly into the `svopn` and `coreemail` URLs, but see section 2 for the PII concern.
- The HTML was truncated, so tokens in the body or footer cannot be ruled out.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** QA did not find it. The QA note says the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post:** QA did not find the RFC 8058 header either, with the same caveat.
- **Authentication-Results:** QA did not find it. SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unverified. The From domain `e.potterybarn.com` matches the tracking subdomains, but that is not proof of alignment.
- **Footer content:** the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address fall past the truncation point and could not be checked.
- **Referrer policy:** `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` has no effect in most email clients and does not offset the PII exposure in section 2.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** the visible links are all wrapped in `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…` tokens. UTM parameters cannot be verified from the source, and the QA run did not resolve them.
- **Product images:** they come from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/…`, on the same domain as the site. The 2022 and 2026 asset paths are mixed, which suggests stale reused modules (see the `http://` banner above).
- **Landing alignment:** it could not be checked, because the destinations were not probed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, and `alt=""` to the pixels and spacers.
2. Serve every asset over HTTPS, including `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, the 2022 `slices_02.jpg` and the `svopn` pixel. Replace the 2022 banner module if it is stale.
3. Remove the PHP `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block.
4. Fill in `<title>`. Drop `maximum-scale=1` so users can zoom.
5. Replace the plaintext `email=` and `sv_sveme=` values with an opaque hashed ID, and review whether the `d.turn.com` beacon is needed.
6. Check the full raw message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. The relay may have stripped them, so look at the original source before treating them as defects.
7. Resolve a sample of the redirect links end to end to confirm UTM parameters and landing pages.
