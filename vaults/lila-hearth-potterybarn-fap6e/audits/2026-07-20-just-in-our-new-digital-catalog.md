# Just in! Our new digital catalog
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-20**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's third catalog-adjacent send in three days — after July 18's "Your first look at the NEW catalog" scored 9/10 here, this July 20 reprise arrives with the claim "Just in!" attached to something that debuted 48 hours ago. The freshness hook lands hollow immediately for any subscriber who opened the earlier send.
- The body is architecturally overloaded: Fall Catalog hero, Summer Sale 70% off banner, six category navigation tiles, in-store design events, personalized product recommendations, a New Arrivals section, an app download prompt, a loyalty rewards block, and Affirm financing — all in one scroll. None of these modules gets enough breathing room to compel action. The hero CTA is "GET INSPIRED," one of the lowest-converting action phrases in email marketing.
- The catalog itself is visually strong. The problem is campaign architecture, not creative execution. This send needed to pick one story and commit — instead it split eight stories and let them dilute each other.

## What's working

- Summer Sale "Up to 70% off" banner at the very top ensures the discount registers immediately even for skimmers.
- "RECOMMENDED JUST FOR YOU" personalization section is a genuine click driver; relevant product cards above the fold feel curated, not generic.
- Fall Catalog hero photography is rich and evocative — warm lifestyle tones that photograph well in email.
- "Earn 10% Back in Rewards" and Affirm "Buy Now, Pay Over Time" both appear near the close as rational purchase justifiers.
- Clean render: no broken images, no overlapping text, no visible layout issues.

## What's weak

- "Just in!" is a credibility problem — the catalog debuted July 18 in an email already scored here; subscribers who opened that send will recognize the recycled pitch immediately.
- "GET INSPIRED" as the primary catalog CTA is action-dead; it signals browse, not buy, and stalls click intent at the hero.
- Six competing content modules with four different CTAs (SHOP NOW, GET INSPIRED, SEE WHAT'S NEW, LEARN MORE) fragment the click and confuse the session's purpose.
- The Fall Catalog and Summer Sale are tonally in conflict — "clearance season is ending" and "fall preview has arrived" send opposite seasonal signals in the same scroll.
- No time-bounded deadline anywhere: after three weeks of identical "up to 70% off" banners, the discount no longer reads as urgent.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill the in-store events, app download, and Affirm blocks from this send.** The catalog is the news — every additional module competes with it and dilutes recall.
- 2. **Replace "GET INSPIRED" with a transactional CTA.** "Shop the Fall Catalog" or "Explore Fall 2026" give subscribers a clear next action tied to what they're looking at.
- 3. **Put a deadline on the Summer Sale banner.** "Ends Sunday" restores urgency that's been flattened by 20+ identical promo emails in July.
- 4. **Wait 4–5 days minimum between catalog sends.** The July 18 → July 20 cadence trains subscribers to skip the first touch; consecutive sends in 48 hours devalue both.
- 5. **Resolve the seasonal frame.** Either lead with "Fall is here" and subordinate the sale, or lead with "Final summer sale days" and subordinate the catalog — not both at equal weight.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Fall 2026 is here — shop the new catalog`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New season, new room. See the Fall Catalog.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Earthy textures, layered bedding, and the pieces we can't stop styling.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: Summer Sale up to 70% off — this weekend only.`

### 1. Technical Summary

Two links are confirmed broken (403), 40+ images lack alt text, three image assets load over HTTP, and a block of PHP `header()` calls is erroneously embedded inside a `<style>` tag — a server-side template leak. Authentication and one-click unsubscribe headers were not captured by the relay.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**[FAIL] Two broken links returning 403:**

- **Credit card apply/buy CTA** — `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/c/da01b407-b21c-4618-a71b-f83bef0dd682?brandCode=POTTERYBARNCB&marketingChannelCode=APPLYANDBUY…` — renders 403. Any recipient clicking this CTA hits a dead end.
- **West Elm spacer GIF** — `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` — cross-brand asset served from the wrong domain, returning 403. This also leaks a West Elm CDN dependency into a Pottery Barn send.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**PHP `header()` calls inside `<style>` block (template artifact):**

```html
<style>
  ...
  header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
  header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
  header("Cache-Control: post-check=0, pre-check=0", false);
  header("Pragma: no-cache");
</style>
```

This is server-side PHP that was not stripped before render. It will not execute in any email client, but it is visible in source, exposes implementation details, and is invalid CSS that some clients may choke on.

**40+ images missing `alt` text**, including all primary content slices (`slices_01.jpg` through `slices_18.jpg`), all product images, and promotional banners (`F26_TOP_SummerSalePartC_NoUrgency.jpg`, `F26_Bottom_NewArrivals.jpg`, `S26_Bottom_UpTo60OffOutdoor.jpg`). ### 5. Compliance

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected** — may not be captured by AgentMail relay, but if genuinely absent, Gmail and Yahoo enforce bulk-sender requirements (>5k/day) that mandate this header. Status should be verified at the ESP level.

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not detected** — one-click unsubscribe (required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender policy since 2024) may not be supported. Needs ESP-level confirmation.

**[WARN] `Authentication-Results` header not captured** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown from this relay. Should be verified against raw headers at delivery.

**[WARN] Plain-text alternative is 70% URLs** (9,742 of 13,870 chars) — spam filters treat high URL-density plain-text as a signal; this ratio is above typical safe thresholds (~40%).

CAN-SPAM physical address and unsubscribe mechanism appear to be present in the footer (standard Pottery Barn template), though the truncated source prevents full confirmation.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

The `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer" />` tag is set in the `<head>`. In webmail clients that render meta tags (Gmail web, Yahoo), this strips the HTTP `Referer` header from outbound clicks. Since the email relies on click-redirect tracking via `click.e.potterybarn.com`, UTM parameters are embedded in the redirect URL itself — so GA4/analytics attribution should survive. However, any analytics relying on the referrer header (rather than UTM params) will receive null referrers.

No raw destination URLs are visible in the truncated source to verify UTM parameter consistency across all CTAs.
