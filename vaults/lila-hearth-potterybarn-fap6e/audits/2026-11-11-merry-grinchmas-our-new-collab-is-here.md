# Merry Grinchmas! Our new collab is here
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a strong concept with a muddled top. The Grinch × Pottery Barn collab is the best hook in the recent run: a licensed, seasonal, shareable idea, and the hero plate-setting photo with red plaid and the Grinch plate does the work. The subject line sells the collab. The first thing in the render, though, is a green "The Fall Sale: Up to 70% off" banner. It pulls attention away from the collab and sits oddly in a Christmas-themed email on Nov 11.
- Below the hero, the email does its job: a short story paragraph, then a run of lifestyle shots (towels, bedding, "Merry Grinchmas" pillow, stockings, wreath). It closes with a "SHOP THE FULL COLLECTION" button. After that it fades into the usual Pottery Barn tail: Recommended Just For You, The Christmas Shop, the Design Crew $3,000 sweepstakes, app, rewards card, Affirm and a very dense legal block. That tail repeats the "Now on sale & shipping free" and "$3,000 gift card" sends from Nov 10. A subscriber now sees the same sweepstakes module and the same sale banner for the third time in two days.
- Judgement: a better-than-average send, held back by banner clutter, a long tail, and a high-frequency week. The collab should be the whole email.

## What's working

- The Grinch collab is a concrete, seasonal, novel hook that stands apart from the generic sale sends in the brand history.
- The hero image is on-brand and cozy, and the "The Grinch" logo lockup is legible with a clear SHOP NOW button.
- The copy line "make your heart grow three sizes, at least" is playful and fits the Grinch voice.
- The mid-email image grid shows many product types (towels, pillows, stockings, linens), so readers can see the range of the collab.
- The full-width "SHOP THE FULL COLLECTION" button is clear and specific, with a verb and a noun.

## What's weak

- The "Fall Sale: Up to 70% off" banner is the first element and competes with the collab hero. "Fall" also reads stale next to a Christmas collab.
- The collab section has no price, no hero product name and no individual product links, so the only click path is a generic collection page.
- The tail is long and generic: Recommended Just For You, The Christmas Shop, the sweepstakes, the app and the rewards card all dilute the collab.
- The $3,000 Design Crew sweepstakes was already the lead of the "Want a $3,000 gift card for a home refresh?" send on Nov 10, so it is repeated.
- The legal and fine-print block is very large and shows exclusions and sweepstakes terms in tiny type, which undercuts the "honest offer" feel.

## Recommendations

- 1. Remove or shrink the Fall Sale banner so the Grinch hero is the first thing the eye lands on. Put the sale in a small secondary strip, or drop it for this send.
- 2. Make the offer concrete for the collab. Name one hero product with a price, such as "Grinch Dinner Plate, $X", and add a deadline or limited-run note ("Limited edition. While supplies last").
- 3. Link individual products in the lifestyle grid (towel, pillow, stocking) so the reader clicks straight to the item they liked, not a collection page.
- 4. Cut the tail down. Keep one secondary module (Recommended or The Christmas Shop) and move the app, rewards and Affirm blocks to a short footer row.
- 5. Skip the sweepstakes in this send. It was just the lead of Nov 10, and rotating it out reduces fatigue.
- 6. Cadence: this is the fifth or sixth Pottery Barn email in about three days. Space the sends, or give each a clearly distinct reason to open.
- **Subject Alt A:** `The Grinch is at Pottery Barn 💚`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Grinch plates, towels & stockings: new today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Limited-edition Grinch tableware, bedding & stockings, plus up to 70% off the home.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Make your heart grow three sizes. Shop the full collection before it sells out.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Table-based XHTML 1.0 email with a 650px layout. The 57% QA pass rate comes almost entirely from missing `alt` attributes (about 45 images) and four plain-HTTP image sources. The header-based compliance checks could not be verified, because the relay did not capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracking links not probed:** 55 links were skipped as click-redirect domains, so destination validity and redirect chains are unverified. Example: `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7ImQi...` (logo link). The `qs=` blob is opaque, so no UTM parameters are visible.
- **Plain-HTTP tracking pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`. The recipient address is in the query string over unencrypted HTTP, so it is exposed in transit. Clients that block mixed or HTTP content will also drop this open-tracking pixel.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` is an ad-network beacon with no `alt`. This is a privacy and deliverability consideration, not a break.
- **Email address in a URL:** `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is rendered as an `<img>`, apparently another tracking or beacon call. It also puts the address in a URL that is likely logged by intermediaries.
- **Beacon without alt:** `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/732be545-...?mi_cid=...` is a tracking image with no `alt`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 45 images are flagged, including:
  - Hero slices: `H26_Collab_B2_Grinch/slices_01.gif` through `slices_09.jpg`.
  - Product images: `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`, `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`, `classic-velvet-stockings-l.jpg`, and others.
  - Banners and footer: `H26_TOP_FallSale.jpg`, `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg`, and `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`.
  - The spacer `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`, which should carry `alt=""`.
  
  With images off or a screen reader, the offer content is not available.
- **HTTP image sources:** three non-pixel images use `http://` and may be blocked: `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`, and the `svopn` pixel.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>`.
- **Stray PHP in CSS:** the second `<style>` block contains literal `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ...")` text after `.yshortcuts`. This is a template leak. It is invalid CSS and could break parsing of rules that follow it in strict clients.
- **Viewport:** `maximum-scale=1` prevents pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility issue.
- **Mobile selectors:** `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` only match when the class is exactly that value. The `.contenttable` tables use a different class, so the 480px rule may not apply. I can't confirm this from the truncated source.
- **Sliced layout:** the hero and footer are built from sliced images (`slices_01–09`, footer `slices_01–18`). Combined with the missing alt text, this makes the email fragile when images are blocked.
- **Hidden preheader:** the preheader is hidden with `display:none; mso-hide:all` and padded with `&zwnj;`/`&shy;` filler. This is standard and no issue.

- **Visible unsubscribe link and physical address:** these sit below the truncation point, so I can't verify them. No issues confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- All links go through `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirects, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be assessed. Resolve a sample of `qs=` links manually to check for UTM parameters and landing-page match (collab page versus homepage).
- Product images load directly from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`. These are not tracked links.
- A West Elm asset (`edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`) is used in a Pottery Barn email. This is minor and probably shared template infrastructure.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, and `alt=""` to spacers and tracking pixels (about 45 images).
2. Move `svopn.potterybarn.com`, `edm.potterybarn.com/.../Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, and `.../0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` to HTTPS.

4. Remove the stray PHP `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block.
5. Fill in `<title>` and drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
6. Re-run the audit against the raw message to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results`.
