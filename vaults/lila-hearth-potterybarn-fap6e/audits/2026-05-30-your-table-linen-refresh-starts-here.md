# Your table linen refresh starts here
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-30**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's third email hitting inboxes on May 30, and it follows the same editorial-over-promotional playbook as the Coastal Washed Quilt send earlier today. The hero is a colorful grid of table linens — tablecloths, runners, placemats — visually appetizing for anyone who hosts, with genuine seasonal appropriateness. But like the quilt send, there is no price incentive, no discount, and no urgency. After the knockout outdoor sale (May 28, 50% OFF, 9/10), this lands as a category browse page that wandered into your inbox.
- The "RECOMMENDED JUST FOR YOU" module below the category grid is a genuine asset — behavioral data is at least being used. But the table linen browse is thin on reasons to click: no specific product is being spotlighted, no price is being offered, and the half-dozen CTAs dilute focus. The email loads cleanly and looks correct, which is damning with faint praise at this point in the cadence.
- Verdict: competent execution, weak premise. A no-offer browse email as the third send of the day is a hard ask.

## What's working

- Colorful product swatch hero immediately communicates the category — warm, summery patterns that read well at thumbnail size
- Sub-category grid (Table Runners, Placemats, Runners) gives browse-minded recipients multiple entry points without requiring scrolling
- "RECOMMENDED JUST FOR YOU" module signals behavioral personalization rather than raw category blasting
- "Earn 10% Back in Rewards" and Affirm financing in the footer offer soft incentives without cluttering the hero section
- Clean layout, no visible render bugs, legible typography throughout

## What's weak

- No offer whatsoever — not even "free shipping on linens today" — makes this feel like a homepage redirect in email form
- Third Pottery Barn email on May 30 compounds fatigue; subscribers who already engaged with the quilt or outdoor emails are primed to skim or unsubscribe
- Six-plus CTAs (three "SHOP NOW" labels, "SHOP ALL TABLE LINENS," "SHOP YOUR TABLE," plus Recommended product links) scatter attention with no clear primary action
- The hero is a grid of small swatches — visually busy on mobile and undersells the actual product beauty; one aspirational lifestyle table setting would land harder
- No occasion anchor — "table linen refresh" could drop any week of the year; nothing ties it to summer entertaining, a holiday weekend, or a specific moment

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a hook offer** — even "free shipping on table linens through Sunday" converts this from a browse push to an action email. No incentive means no urgency means no click from anyone not already in-market.
- 2. **Lead with a lifestyle hero** — one aspirational outdoor table setting (brunch, summer dinner party, floral cloth in natural light) sells the aspiration before the category. Swap the swatch grid to a secondary position.
- 3. **Cut to two CTAs** — one primary ("SHOP TABLE LINENS") and one secondary ("SEE YOUR RECOMMENDATIONS"). The swatch grid of SHOP NOWs is noise that trains the eye to ignore buttons.
- 4. **Anchor the subject line to the occasion**, not the category — "table linen refresh" is evergreen in the wrong way; it has no seasonal urgency.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Host better this summer — new table linens are in`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Fresh table linens for every summer meal`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Tablecloths, placemats & runners in bright new summer patterns.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New arrivals in the category you've been browsing.`
- 5. **Promote the Recommended module above the fold** — it is the most personalized content in the email; burying it below category CTAs wastes the signal entirely.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**PHP code in `<style>` block**
The following PHP `header()` calls appear literally inside the client `<style>` block and will be emitted as raw text to the CSS parser:
```
header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
header("Cache-Control: post-check=0, pre-check=0", false);
header("Pragma: no-cache");
```
This is a template/build error. It does not execute, but it corrupts the stylesheet and may cause unexpected rendering artifacts in strict parsers.

**Empty `<title>` tag**
`<title></title>` — blank title can cause issues in some preview and tab environments.

**45 images missing `alt` text**
All sliced content images (`slices_01.jpg` through `slices_13.jpg` in both the body and footer), all product recommendation images, both bottom banner images, and all three open/tracking pixels lack `alt` attributes. This breaks screen reader access to the entire email body and triggers accessibility flags in Outlook and Apple Mail.

**3 images served over HTTP (mixed content)**
- `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`

All three will be blocked or display broken in Gmail and most modern clients due to mixed-content policy.

**Open pixel served over HTTP**
`http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` — the open-tracking pixel uses plain HTTP, which will be blocked or proxied by Gmail Image Proxy, invalidating open-rate data for Gmail recipients.

**`maximum-scale=1` in viewport meta**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
Prevents user zoom on mobile — an accessibility violation (WCAG 1.4.4).

### 5. Compliance

**WARN — `List-Unsubscribe` header not captured**
The AgentMail relay did not surface a `List-Unsubscribe` header. If absent from the actual SMTP envelope, this violates Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender requirements (enforced since 2024) and may cause deliverability penalties.

**WARN — `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not captured (RFC 8058)**
One-click unsubscribe is unverifiable. Required by Gmail/Yahoo for senders above 5k/day threshold.

**WARN — `Authentication-Results` not captured**
SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status cannot be confirmed. The sending domain `e.potterybarn.com` should have all three configured; inability to verify through the relay is a monitoring gap, not necessarily a sender-side failure.

**WARN — Plain-text body is 70.7% URLs**
9,924 of 14,044 characters in the plain-text part are URL strings. Spam filters (SpamAssassin, Proofpoint) penalize high URL-to-text ratios in plain-text alternatives.

**`<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer" />`**
Referrer suppression is intentional for privacy, but confirms that any analytics relying on HTTP `Referer` headers at the landing page will receive no upstream referrer signal — UTM params in the click URL must carry the full attribution load.
