# Outdoor furniture & more ON SALE
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-19**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's outdoor-furniture sale send, and it's the most legible sale email in this brand's recent run — "Savings for Outdoor Living / UP TO 60% OFF" is a real, quantified offer stacked over a genuinely appealing patio-dining hero, and it stays on that single message through three product shots (Torrey, Indio Metal, Indio Wood) before the "SHOP ALL SALE" button. That's a tighter, more disciplined structure than the lighting or seating sends from the past two days, which meandered into trendspotting callouts and grids before ever asking for a click.
- Where it falls apart is everything after the fold: a sprawling "Recommended Just for You" grid unrelated to outdoor furniture (curtain rods, a wool rug, a swivel chair), then the now-familiar Design Crew sweepstakes bait, then a full Halloween Shop plug, then app/rewards/Affirm blocks. The sale message wins the first screen and then gets buried under four unrelated pitches — a subscriber skimming past the hero has no reason to believe this is still "the outdoor sale email" by the time they hit Halloween decor.
- Solid top, bloated bottom. Score reflects a real offer and clean hero undercut by scope creep and a recycled sweepstakes trap already seen in the 08-18 send.

## What's working

- Clear, specific offer up top: "UP TO 60% OFF" with a legible red banner-style hero, no ambiguity about the deal.
- Hero image is well-composed — styled outdoor tablescape with real product context (Westport bistro chairs), matches the seasonal outdoor-living moment.
- Product cadence (Torrey → Indio Metal → Indio Wood) reads as a coherent "shop the collection" sweep rather than a random grid.
- Single unmistakable CTA button ("SHOP ALL SALE") in brand red, well after enough visual buildup to justify it.
- No render bugs — images load, text is legible, no overlap or broken layout.

## What's weak

- "Recommended Just for You" grid is off-category (curtain rods, sheets, a wool rug) and dilutes the outdoor-sale focus right after the primary CTA.
- The Design Crew sweepstakes block reappears verbatim in structure from the 2026-08-18 "$1500" send — same lead-gen-dressed-as-giveaway mechanic, now showing up two days running.
- Halloween Shop cross-promo has zero connection to outdoor furniture and competes for attention in an already-long email.
- No urgency or end-date on the "up to 60% off" — no sense of when this expires, which matters for a sale message specifically.
- "Up to 60%" is vague enough to feel like a placeholder ceiling most items won't hit — no per-category or per-item price anchor visible in the sale section itself.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after "SHOP ALL SALE." Move the recommended-products grid, Design Crew sweepstakes, Halloween plug, and app/rewards blocks to separate sends — this email should be the outdoor sale and nothing else. That's the single biggest lift available here.
- 2. Add a real deadline to the hero ("Ends Sunday" / "Through 8/24") — turns a soft "up to 60% off" into something with urgency.
- 3. Replace "up to 60% off" with a concrete anchor price on at least one hero SKU (e.g., "Indio Wood Sectional, now $X") so the offer feels priced, not aspirational.
- 4. If cross-sell is required, keep it to one on-category line (e.g., outdoor rugs/planters) rather than curtain rods and organic percale sheets.
- 5. Drop or clearly relabel the Design Crew sweepstakes — running the same "book an appointment, win $1500" mechanic two sends in a row reads as a pattern subscribers will start to distrust.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your patio refresh, up to 60% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Torrey, Indio Metal & Indio Wood sets — all reduced.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop the collection before it sells out this weekend.`

# Technical Audit: Pottery Barn — "Outdoor furniture & more ON SALE"

## 1. Technical Summary
This email is a standard multi-table EDM build with heavy tracking/redirect infrastructure and multiple open-tracking pixels; automated QA passed only 57% of checks, driven almost entirely by missing `alt` attributes and a handful of insecure (`http://`) image sources.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Header/nav CTA routes through a click-tracking redirector: `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABB7InYiOjEsImQiOjQ5NzN9AAsAAAAABAdTU4RavlKf2WOK2697FDqxKhK1Dvb21aCxoRspDKnE6pb1ro1KSxwPvr-D2MCalG-QMzjVcPk4px46Pj2bengEQjahltFbmvFeM7rYSSAYgAAz` — opaque payload, not independently verifiable from source alone.
- 54 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the automated HTTP probe (by design — redirect domains aren't dereferenced), so destination validity for the bulk of CTAs is unconfirmed.
- Additional third-party tracking calls present in source: `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` (Turn/Amobee data pixel) and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/c4f7d21a-362e-4217-96bb-25c175356a5c?mi_mid=01a01908-c400-7000-b34a-3e0bb3dc4df7&cp_tp` (open/click tracking, MailIQ/Silverpop-style params).
- Open-tracking pixel served over plaintext HTTP: `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` — the recipient email address is passed in cleartext in a GET query string over an unencrypted connection, which is both a security/PII-leak concern and a likely image-blocking target for corporate mail gateways.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **33 images missing `alt` text**, including primary content banners (`F26_Top_NewArrivals.jpg`, `slices_01–07.jpg`, `F26_Top_DesignCrewSweeps_01a/b.jpg`, `F26_Bottom_HalloweenShop.jpg`), product images (`banks-extendable-dining-table...`, `remmy-swivel-chair...`, `toscana-extendable-dining-table...`, `400-thread-count-organic-percale-sheet-set...`, `custom-capitola-hand-tufted-wool-rug...`), and all footer slice images. With images blocked by default in most mail clients, the email will render as a near-blank shell with no fallback text for screen-reader or images-off users.
- Head contains malformed/invalid CSS: raw PHP-style `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');`, `header("Cache-Control: ...")`, `header("Pragma: no-cache")` statements are embedded inside a `<style>` block (not inside a `<?php ?>` tag or comment). These are not valid CSS and will be ignored by mail clients, but indicate a templating/build artifact that leaked into the shipped HTML.
- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` are present — expected for transactional/marketing EDMs, not a defect.
- Responsive behavior relies on two overlapping/conflicting `@media` breakpoints (`min-width:700px` and `min-width:650px`) both targeting `img{max-width:...}` — functional but redundant; the 650px rule's `!important` will always win over the 700px rule at widths ≥700px, making the 700px block dead code.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `%%`, `{{`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are present in the visible truncated source.
- Recipient identity is passed as a literal, resolved email address in tracking URLs (`sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`, and in `coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`) rather than an opaque token/hash — this is a personalization implementation, not a broken merge, but it does mean PII travels in plaintext query strings (see §2/§5).

- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found (RFC 8058)** — one-click unsubscribe (required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender rules) is not confirmed present.
- **Authentication-Results header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC validation status is unknown from the captured headers via the AgentMail relay; cannot confirm authentication passed at receipt.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Header nav CTA uses an opaque tracking-redirect wrapper (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`) rather than a directly inspectable UTM-tagged URL — landing page and UTM parameters cannot be verified without following the redirect (out of scope for this static-source audit; 54 such links were skipped by design).

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive (or explicitly empty `alt=""` for purely decorative slices/spacers) `alt` attributes to all 33 flagged images, prioritizing primary content banners and product shots.
2. Migrate the three remaining `http://` asset URLs (`edm.westelm.com/spacer_0.gif`, `edm.potterybarn.com/.../Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `.../slices_02.jpg`) to HTTPS.
3. Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP statements from the `<style>` block in `<head>` — dead code from a leaked build artifact.
4. Confirm at the originating MTA (not just the AgentMail relay) that `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually sent, given Gmail/Yahoo's bulk-sender one-click-unsubscribe requirement.
5. Move the open-tracking pixel off plaintext HTTP (`svopn.potterybarn.com`) to HTTPS to stop transmitting the recipient's email address in cleartext.
6. Consolidate the two overlapping `img{max-width}` media queries (650px vs 700px breakpoints) to remove dead CSS.
