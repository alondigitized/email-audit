# Sconces & picture lights that set the mood
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's lighting category email. It does the job cleanly. The "Set the Mood" hero shows brass sconces, and the copy underneath ("Sconces and picture lights give your home that warm glow") matches the subject. Two lifestyle blocks follow: sconces, then picture lights. Each has its own "Shop" link. A "Shop by Finish" row (Black, Brass, Nickel) is a useful touch.
- The trouble is the length. After the lighting content, the email turns into a full-catalog newsletter. It carries a "Recommended Just for You" grid of curtain rods, rugs, and a dining table, then a Halloween Shop banner, a Design Crew banner, App Store, rewards card, Affirm, and social blocks. The sconce message gets diluted, and the email never states an offer. I don't see a percent off, free shipping, or a deadline anywhere. It's a mood email with no reason to act today.
- Judgement: good on-brand creative, weak on urgency and focus. Like the other 6/10 sends this week ("Nightstands that add storage & style," "Furniture & more for your cozy bedroom"), it's a competent category template with no incentive.

## What's working

- The hero copy and subject line agree, so the promise is delivered right away.
- The brass sconce and picture-light imagery is styled and cohesive, and the warm tones suit the theme.
- The "Shop by Finish" swatches (Black, Brass, Nickel) give a quick way to browse.
- There are separate CTAs for sconces and picture lights, plus a "Shop All Lighting" button that catches everyone else.
- The subcategory row (Chandeliers, Table Lamps, Pendants, Outdoor) extends the lighting theme sensibly.

## What's weak

- No visible offer, price, discount, or deadline, so there's no reason to click today.
- The hero headline is light text over a busy, pale image, and it looks low-contrast.
- The lower half is off-topic: Halloween, Design Crew, a rug, a dining table, and a ghost collection. It competes with the lighting story.
- The "Recommended Just for You" grid shows no lighting products, which undercuts the personalization.
- The footer stack is long and heavy: rewards card, Affirm, app download, and dense fine print.
- The email says nothing about product specifics such as bulb type, plug-in vs. hardwired, or sizing. Lighting buyers care about these.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a concrete hook to the hero, such as a tiered "Up to 25% off lighting" or "Free shipping on lighting through Sunday." This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Fix the hero headline contrast with a solid text panel or a darker overlay.
- 3. Show 4-6 priced lighting products in the "Recommended" grid, with plug-in options and ratings.
- 4. Cut the Halloween and Design Crew banners, or move them to a separate send. Keep the email to one story.
- 5. Add practical guidance, such as "No electrician needed: plug-in sconces."
- 6. Trim the footer blocks to one loyalty or financing message.

## Technical Audit

1. **Technical Summary**
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with click-tracked links. It has widespread missing alt text and three `http://` image sources. No `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers were captured, which may be a relay artifact.

2. **Link & Tracking Issues**
- 58 tracking links on `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` were skipped by the HTTP probe, so their destinations and status are unverified. The QA report gives no evidence of a broken link.
- The recipient address `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` appears in plaintext in URL query strings, for example `coreemail.html?email=...` and `svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...`. This is PII in URLs and is likely intentional for personalization.
- `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` is set. Most clients ignore it.
- Third-party pixel: `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` is an ad-tech beacon. Its alt text is the string "1".
- Open-tracking pixels: `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=...` is served over HTTP, and `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/7616e895-...` has no alt text.
- The QA finding that plain text is more than 70% URLs (10466 of 14653 chars) results from the long tracking URLs. It raises spam-filter and readability risk for text-only clients.

3. **Rendering & Accessibility**
- About 50 images have no `alt` attribute. Examples:
  - Sliced hero and banner images: `F26_Lighting_B9_Sconces_PictureLights/slices_01.jpg` through `slices_15.jpg`.
  - The footer image set: `Email-Footer/slices_01.jpg` through `slices_18.jpg`.
  - Product images: `reeva-handwoven-rug-3-l.jpg`, `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`, `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`, `emery-linen-curtain-l.jpg` and others.
  - Banner images: `F26_Bottom_HalloweenShop.jpg` and `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`.
  - Because the hero is sliced into 15 images with no alt text, the promotional content is unavailable with images off or in a screen reader.
- Three images load over `http://`, so mail clients may block them:
  - `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - `svopn.potterybarn.com/...` (the open pixel)
- `<title></title>` is empty.
- No `lang` attribute is set on `<html>`.
- The spacer `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on a West Elm domain. This is a cross-brand asset dependency.
- Some layout tables lack `role="presentation"`. For example, the spacer table `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><tr><td height="20">&nbsp;</td></tr></table>` has none.
- `<style>` contains stray PHP code: `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ...")`. It is invalid CSS and a template-leak defect, though harmless in rendering.
- The Google Fonts `<link>` (Montserrat) is stripped by Gmail and Outlook, so a fallback stack is needed. The truncated source doesn't show whether one exists.
- Selectors `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` depend on class names. The `wrapper` and `photo` classes are not confirmed in the visible source.
- Global `img {max-width: 650px}` at min-width 700px conflicts with `img {max-width:100% !important}` at min-width 650px. The rules overlap and the cascade result is unclear.
- Preheader "Small fixtures, major impact" is present. It is followed by many `&#847;`/soft-hyphen padding characters, which is standard.

4. **Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. The recipient email is resolved in `coreemail.html?email=` and `sv_sveme=`. The visible source shows no unrendered merge tokens, though the HTML was truncated.

5. **Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers were not found. QA notes the relay may strip them, so this can't be confirmed as a sender defect. If the sender really omits them, that fails Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- `Authentication-Results` was not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `e.potterybarn.com` is aligned with the click domains, but this is not proof of authentication.
- The footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not visible in the truncated source, so I can't verify them. The footer is built from image slices (`Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`). If the legal text is baked into images, it is unreadable to screen readers and with images blocked. Verify that the unsubscribe and address text are live HTML.

6. **Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Destination URLs are hidden behind `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` encoded redirects, so UTM parameters and landing pages can't be audited from the source. The only visible identifiers are `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` on the pixel.
- The QA data has no HTTP probe result for the landing pages. Continuity is unverified.

7. **Recommendations**
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, and `alt=""` to spacers and tracking pixels. Also replace the hero slices with a single image plus alt, or give each slice alt text.
2. Change the three `http://` image sources to `https://`.
3. Confirm that `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` are sent at the origin, for example by checking the raw message outside the relay.
4. Confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment from the original headers.
5. Remove the leaked PHP `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block.
6. Set a `<title>` and `<html lang="en">`.
7. Make sure the unsubscribe link and postal address in the footer are live text.
8. Re-run with the redirect-resolving probe on a sample of the 58 tracking links to check UTM parameters and landing page status.
9. Consider shortening tracking URLs or adding a fuller plain-text part to lower the URL ratio.
