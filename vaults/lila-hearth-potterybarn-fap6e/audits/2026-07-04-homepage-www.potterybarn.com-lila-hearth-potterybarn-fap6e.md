# Homepage snapshot · www.potterybarn.com · 2026-07-04
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-04**
## Executive summary

- I open Pottery Barn's site and the very first thing I see is a full-screen signup modal blocking everything. The brand image in it — a gorgeous cream sofa draped with floral and geometric throw pillows — is *exactly* my aesthetic, but I'm immediately being asked to hand over my email and phone number before I can browse a single product. It feels like the host greeting me at the door before I've even stepped inside.

## What's working

- **The modal hero image** — cream sofa, colorful floral pillows, natural light, layered textures — is aspirational in the exact right direction for a home decorator. I want to live in that room.
- **"Find out first"** — the headline framing speaks to someone who shops seasonally and loves getting early access to new arrivals. That's me around holiday catalog season.
- **Clean modal layout** — Pottery Barn logo, tight headline, two fields, fine print. No clutter, no visual noise competing with the lifestyle image.
- **Williams-Sonoma family acknowledgment in the fine print** — I'm a W-S rewards member, so seeing that ecosystem mentioned is a subtle trust signal.

## What's weak

- **The modal itself is the problem** — blocking the entire homepage on first visit is aggressive. I came here to browse, not to fill out a form. High bounce risk.
- **"Exclusive savings & more" is hollow** — no number, no category, no deadline. If I'm giving up my phone number, tell me what I'm actually getting. "20% off your first order" would convert me. This won't.
- **No loyalty hook for existing members** — I may already be a Pottery Barn Visa or W-S rewards member. There's no "sign in" option or member acknowledgment visible, so the modal treats me like a stranger.
- **Homepage content is 100% hidden** — I can't see navigation, featured categories, seasonal merchandise, or any product. The sliver of nav bar visible says "Baby, Kids, Teen, Dorm" — none of that is why I'm here.
- **No urgency** — no deadline, no "limited time," no countdown. The offer is standing and therefore easy to dismiss.

## Recommendations

- **Lead with a specific offer in the modal headline** — swap "exclusive savings & more" for something like "20% off your first order + early access to new arrivals." Give me a reason to hand over my info.
- **Add a "Already a member? Sign in" link** — returning loyalists shouldn't be treated as strangers. A simple sign-in link inside the modal respects the existing relationship.
- **Reduce modal friction with a single field** — asking for both email AND phone on first contact is greedy. Email first; follow up for mobile. Lower the barrier.
- **Surface a "Skip for now / browse freely" button more visibly** — the X to close the modal is there but small. Let me into the store. I'll sign up after I see something I love.
