# Your bedroom design checklist ✔️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-03**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, editorial, holiday-guest-room send, and it is the best Pottery Barn email in this run. It drops the oxblood "Extra 20% off" banner that has led almost every send since Oct 26. The hero is a soft "ready for guests / Bedroom Updates" card over a plaid-bedding photo, with a single SHOP BEDS button. The five-item numbered checklist (bedding, nightstand, pillows, table lamps, frames) delivers on the subject line. It is the same service-and-inspiration mode as the Nov 3 "New arrivals to invite the season in" and "Book your FREE design services" sends.
- The weakness is commerce. No price, percentage or deadline appears above the fold. "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks" is the only top-line hook, and it is a thin utility message. The "Ships Free" tags on the sheet set and pillow are the only visible offer. Each item gets a category link (BEDDING, NIGHTSTANDS, PILLOWS, TABLE LAMPS, FRAMES) rather than a product link. Below the checklist the email sprawls into a "Recommended Just For You" grid, a Christmas Shop banner, a design-services banner, an app promo, a credit card promo and an Affirm promo. The focus dilutes quickly.
- Overall it is a good concept with soft execution. It reads as pleasant, not urgent. It is likely to get opens from the subject but modest clicks, because the email never gives a reason to act today.

## What's working

- The subject and hero agree: "Bedroom Updates" and a numbered checklist deliver on "design checklist."
- The 1–5 numbered layout alternates image left and right, which gives a scannable rhythm.
- The plaid and holiday styling is seasonal and cohesive across the hero, pillows, lamp and frames.
- "Ships Free" tags on the Patchwork Plaid Sheet Set and Stewart Plaid Pillow add a concrete perk.
- A single clear SHOP BEDS button sits in the hero.
- It breaks the oxblood banner repetition from earlier in the week.

## What's weak

- There is no price, percent-off or deadline in the hero or checklist. The "Extra 20%" promo is gone but nothing replaces it.
- Each checklist item links to a category, not a specific product, and the underlined text links are small.
- The lower half piles on six or seven unrelated modules (Recommended grid, Christmas Shop, Design Crew, app, credit card, Affirm), and the bedroom story gets buried.
- The "Recommended Just For You" grid is mostly stockings, a dining table, a desk and rugs. Almost none of it is bedroom-related.
- The "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks" banner is small and gets lost above the hero.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one real offer in the hero, such as "Ships free" or a member price, with a date. A checklist with no reason to act today reads as inspiration only.
- 2. Link each checklist item to a specific hero product, and add a price and a button ("Shop the sheet set") instead of a category word.
- 3. Cut the lower stack to one secondary module, either Christmas Shop or Design Services. Drop the app and credit-card blocks from a bedroom-focused send.
- 4. Replace the generic "Recommended" grid with bedroom-relevant items (headboards, bedding, bedside lighting) to match the story.
- 5. Move the delivery-window message into a stronger callout under the hero.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, image-sliced build with correct basics (UTF-8, `role="presentation"` on the main tables, MSO conditionals). It has widespread missing alt text, stray non-CSS code inside a `<style>` block, and the recipient's email address in plaintext in tracking and asset URLs. The QA run scored 50% pass (0 failures, 4 warnings). Most warnings concern headers that the relay probably stripped, so they are unconfirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverifiable links:** 61 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect URLs. Example: `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...`. Destination validity, redirect chains and UTM handling are unverified.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEv.../cat/1` is an ad-network pixel with no alt text.
- **Non-HTTPS tracking pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` loads over plain HTTP. The open signal can be blocked or sent in cleartext.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a spacer hosted on a different brand's domain. This is a minor dependency risk.
- **Stale asset path:** `/pb/2025/US/H25_TOP_InStockFurniture/...` sits in a 2026 campaign (`H26_...`), which suggests a reused module.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 60 images flagged, including:
  - all 19 body slices (`.../H26_Furn_B6_BedroomChecklist/slices_01–19.jpg`)
  - all 18 footer slices (`.../Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`)
  - six product images (e.g. `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`)
  - banners such as `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`
  
  Because the body is sliced images, the email is unreadable with images off and to screen readers. Tracking pixels such as `d.turn.com/.../cat/1` and `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/5ac37a33-...` also lack `alt=""`.
- **HTTP image sources:** three images use `http://` and may be blocked or trigger mixed-content warnings:
  - `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - the `svopn` pixel
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>`.
- **No language attribute:** `<html xmlns=...>` has no `lang`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom.
- **Stray code in CSS:** the second `<style>` block contains `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store..."); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. This is invalid CSS from leaked template code. It can break parsing of any rules that follow it in some clients.
- **Presentation roles:** some spacer tables, such as `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><tr><td height="20">&nbsp;</td>`, lack `role="presentation"`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved merge tokens** (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[%`) in the visible source.
- **Recipient email in URLs:** it appears in plaintext in `.../customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` and `...&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`. These are PII leaks into URLs, which end up in logs and referrers. The `svopn` one is also over HTTP.
- **Truncated source:** the HTML was truncated, so footer and body tokens could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both not found. The QA tool itself notes AgentMail may not capture these headers, so this is **unconfirmed, not a confirmed violation**. Verify against raw headers.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same relay reason. The From domain `e.potterybarn.com` and the click domain `click.e.potterybarn.com` align.
- **Body unsubscribe link and postal address:** not visible in the truncated source, so they could not be verified.
- **Plain-text part:** 11,744 of 15,996 characters (73%) are URLs. This is a mild deliverability and readability risk, driven by long tracking links.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters:** none can be confirmed. The links are opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` redirects, and the probe skipped them.
- **Landing alignment:** unverifiable for the same reason.
- **`potterybarn.com` asset URLs:** the only direct ones are image URLs and the `coreemail.html` pixel, so none give evidence on landing pages.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content slice and product image, and `alt=""` to pixels and spacers.
2. Remove the leaked `header(...)` PHP lines from the `<style>` block.
3. Move the `http://` images and the `svopn` pixel to HTTPS.
4. Stop putting the raw email address in URL query strings. Use an opaque token or hash.
5. Set a non-empty `<title>` and `<html lang="en">`. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
6. Check the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. Check that the body has a working unsubscribe link and a postal address.
7. Resolve a sample of the `click.e.potterybarn.com` links manually to confirm the destination and any UTM parameters.
8. Replace the cross-brand spacer and the 2025 banner reference with campaign-correct assets.
