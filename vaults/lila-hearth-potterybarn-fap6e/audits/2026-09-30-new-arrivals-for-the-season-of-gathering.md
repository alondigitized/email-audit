# New arrivals for the season of gathering
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's seasonal "Gather Together" new-arrivals email. It's moody, editorial and well styled. A dark library hero with a set table leads into Thanksgiving tableware, a cozy bedroom and guest-ready furniture. The imagery is the strongest thing about it. It's polished, but it gives me no reason to click today.
- There's no hard offer. The only concrete hook is the small "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks" line, which is easy to miss. This is the 7th Pottery Barn send in about three days. The earlier ones were the 2026-09-29 sconces email and the 2026-09-28 "These designs are on sale & ship free!" email. Against those, a no-offer mood email feels like filler. The body then runs long: a category tile grid, "Recommended Just For You", a Christmas Shop banner, design services, the app, a credit card and Affirm. The hero message gets buried under all of it.
- Judgement: a good-looking brand piece and a mediocre performance email. It's a 5-6 send that would do better with one clear reason to act.

## What's working

- The "GATHER TOGETHER" hero is atmospheric and on-season, with a clear **SHOP NEW ARRIVALS** button above the fold.
- The Thanksgiving table, cozy bedroom and guest-ready sections have a consistent styled look and fit the copy.
- The "Touches to welcome the season" grid (Pillows, Lighting, Rugs, Armchairs, Decor, Christmas) is easy to scan and gives people several ways in.
- I see no broken images or overlapping text, and the layout is clean.
- The "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks" banner gives a real practical benefit for holiday hosting.

## What's weak

- There's no price offer, deadline or member perk in the hero, so nothing creates urgency.
- The email is very long and stacks eight or more competing modules (Recommended, Christmas Shop, Free Design Services, app, credit card, Affirm), which dilutes the single message.
- The hero text is small and low-contrast over a busy dark photo, so the "In-Stock" banner reads as an afterthought.
- Three near-identical "Shop" style CTAs (hero, "Shop all new arrivals", "Shop now") compete without a clear priority.
- The "Recommended Just For You" products look generic and show no prices or ratings, so nothing signals personalization or social proof.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one concrete hook in the hero, such as a % off, a free-shipping threshold or the in-stock delivery promise as a headline. This would lift the email more than any other change.
- 2. Cut the body to three or four modules. Drop the credit card, Affirm and app blocks into a slim footer strip.
- 3. Add prices and star ratings to the recommended products, and link the hero to a specific collection rather than the general new-arrivals page.
- 4. Increase contrast and size on the hero and section headlines.
- 5. Space out sends. Fold this mood content into fewer, stronger emails instead of a 7th in three days.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Get the house guest-ready, delivered by the holidays`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Tables, bedding and lighting that ship fast, so you're ready to host.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New arrivals for every room, with in-stock furniture arriving in 1-4 weeks.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a slice-based, fixed-width (650px) table layout run through an SFMC-style click wrapper. Its main technical problems are about 45 images without `alt` text, three plain-HTTP image requests, and personal email addresses in tracking-pixel query strings. Source truncation and relay header stripping mean compliance and UTM checks cannot be confirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Destinations unverified.** 55 tracking links were skipped as click-redirect domains (e.g. `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABB7Iny...`). None of their final destinations were probed, so dead or mis-targeted links can't be ruled out.
- **Plaintext tracking pixel.** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=<recipient email>` loads over HTTP. It may be blocked by clients, and open tracking can silently fail.
- **Third-party pixel.** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` is an ad-tech (Turn) beacon.
- **Legacy asset.** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` is a 2022 banner reused in a 2026 send.
- **Stale or cross-brand assets.** `.../2025/US/H25_TOP_InStockFurniture/...jpg` is a prior-season path. `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on a different brand's domain.
- **Plain-text part.** URLs make up 9,920 of 14,098 characters (70%+). This is expected with long wrapped tracking URLs, but it is a spam-scoring risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** About 45 images are flagged, mostly the `slices_01–13.jpg` hero and `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg` slices. Also flagged:
  - Product images such as `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg` and `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`.
  - The banners `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg` and `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`.
  - With images blocked, or in a screen reader, most of the content is empty. This is significant because the layout is sliced images. The pixels and `spacer_0.gif` are legitimately decorative, and the QA check can't tell `alt=""` from a missing attribute.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** There is also no `lang` attribute on `<html>`.
- **`maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta.** This blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility failure.
- **Fixed width with no small-screen rule.** Tables are `width="650"` with `class="contenttable"`. The only `img { max-width:100% }` rule is inside `@media (min-width: 650px)`, and the `.contenttable` width override is commented out. Below 650px, horizontal overflow is likely. This can't be confirmed because the source is truncated.
- **Stray code in the `<style>` block.** PHP-style `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ...")` text is left inside the CSS. It is invalid CSS and comes from a template leak, though it sits at the end of the block, so impact is probably nil.
- **Dead selector.** `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` rules are defined, but no matching elements appear in the visible source.
- **Web font.** The Google Fonts `<link>` for Montserrat is ignored by most email clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source. The recipient address is populated in `coreemail.html?email=` and `sv_sveme=`. This category covers only the truncated portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers.** QA did not find either, so one-click unsubscribe can't be confirmed. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a proven defect.
- **SPF/DKIM/DMARC.** `Authentication-Results` was not captured, so alignment is unknown.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and physical address.** Both sit in the truncated footer, so they can't be verified.
- **PII exposure.** The recipient email is in cleartext query strings, including over HTTP in the `svopn` pixel. That exposes it to network observers and to proxy or server logs. This is a privacy concern rather than a CAN-SPAM violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs not verifiable.** No `utm_*` parameters are visible. Links are opaque `qs=` tokens and the probes were skipped, so campaign attribution and landing-page alignment can't be confirmed.
- **Direct product image links.** Assets on `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...` are served from the main site domain, not a CDN, which puts load on the storefront.
- **Campaign path.** Asset paths carry the campaign name, `0930_WED_AM_HolidayMarketingLaunch`.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, using `alt=""` for spacers and pixels. Add `<title>`, `lang="en"`, and remove `maximum-scale=1`.
2. Serve every image and pixel over HTTPS. This means `svopn.potterybarn.com`, `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, and the 2022 `slices_02.jpg`. Replace or retire stale assets.
3. Stop passing the raw email address in pixel and image query strings. Use a hashed or opaque ID.
4. Add a mobile rule such as `.contenttable { width:100% !important }` and `img { max-width:100%; height:auto }` outside the min-width query. Remove the leaked `header(...)` text from the CSS.
5. Send the message to a raw-header-capturing inbox to check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Then re-run link probes by following redirects, and check the footer unsubscribe link and address.
