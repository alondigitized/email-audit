# The Halloween Shop 🦇🕷️ Up to 50% off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- This is a seasonal send that finally breaks the pattern. After a week of near-identical "Extra 20% off" blasts, the Halloween Shop gives the subscriber a real theme. The hero is a moody porch scene with witch figures and pumpkins, and a "Get it in time! Order by Tue 10/27, 6 AM PT with standard shipping" line adds credible urgency. Two category blocks follow, "Create an eerie ambiance" (decor) and "Sip in spine-chilling style" (tabletop & bar), and each is clear and has its own link.
- The weakness is the same one the brand's recent sends have. The oxblood "Extra 20% off Select Styles / Plus savings up to 70% off" banner sits on top of the email again, with SHOP ALL SALE, and competes with the "Up to 50% off" Halloween offer. A reader sees two different discount claims in the first screen. The bottom half then drifts into Christmas Shop, Design Crew, Affirm and the credit card, which dilutes a send that is about a holiday three days away.
- My judgement: this is better creative than the 10/26 "In case you missed it" and 10/24 "Discover deals" sends, but it still isn't focused. A decent send held back by a banner that never changes.

## What's working

- The Halloween hero is on-theme and visually strong. It has a clear title lockup, "Up to 50% off", and a real room scene.
- The shipping-deadline line (order by Tue 10/27, 6 AM PT) gives believable, time-bound urgency.
- The two category sections have specific CTAs ("SHOP DECOR", "SHOP TABLEWARE & BAR") and show real products.
- The subject carries bat and spider emojis plus a concrete "Up to 50% off", so it signals both theme and offer.
- The photography is consistent and high quality, with a clean grid of product close-ups.

## What's weak

- The oxblood "Extra 20% off Select Styles" banner leads again. It is the same banner as the previous sends, and it muddies the Halloween offer.
- "Up to 50% off" and "Extra 20% off / up to 70% off" are two competing claims. The reader can't tell which one applies to Halloween.
- The "Recommended Just For You" block (dining tables, curtain rod, bed) has nothing to do with Halloween, and the Christmas Shop and Design Crew sections are off-message.
- The email is very long. The footer is dense with legal copy, and the Affirm and rewards promos add more clutter.
- The only hero-level product detail is the imagery. There are no prices, no named hero SKU, and no reviews or social proof.

## Recommendations

- 1. Remove the sitewide "Extra 20% off" banner from Halloween-themed sends, or move it below the hero, so one offer owns the top.
- 2. Put the shipping deadline in or directly under the hero as a countdown-style line, since it is the strongest urgency in the email.
- 3. Cut the below-the-fold content to Halloween: best sellers with prices and ratings, then one "Shop all Halloween" button. Drop the Christmas and Design Crew blocks, or move them to a different send.
- 4. Add 3-4 named hero products with sale prices, so a click lands on a specific item.
- 5. Fold the Affirm and rewards promos into the footer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Halloween ends Tuesday: up to 50% off decor`
- **Subject Alt B:** `🎃 Last call: skeletons & spooky bar sets`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Order by 10/27, 6 AM PT with standard shipping.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Candelabras, skeleton goblets and more, up to 50% off.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style table layout (650px, `click.e.potterybarn.com` wrapping). It has no hard failures, but about 45 images lack `alt`, 3 images load over `http://`, and the recipient's email address is passed in image URLs. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the relay stripped the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 53 tracking links were skipped as click-redirect domains (e.g. `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...`), so none of them were probed. The QA data can't confirm that destinations resolve or whether any of them 404.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` loads over plain HTTP, so clients may block it. Opens would be under-counted, and the address travels unencrypted.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` is an ad-network beacon with no `alt`.
- **Stale or cross-brand assets:**
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` is a 2022 asset, also served over HTTP.
  - `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a West Elm host serving a Pottery Barn email, so the template likely carries cross-brand dependencies.
- **Plain-text part:** URLs make up 10,192 of 14,379 characters (about 71%). The tracking-URL bloat is likely to hurt spam scoring and readability of the text alternative.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt`:** about 45 `<img>` tags have no `alt` attribute. Examples:
  - The 11 hero slices `F26_Seasonal_B2_HalloweenLaunch2/slices_01–11.jpg`.
  - The 18 footer slices `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`.
  - Product images such as `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-1245-1-l.jpg`.
  - Banners `H26_TOP_Extra20CLR.jpg`, `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg` and `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`.
  - The logo is fine (`alt="POTTERY BARN"`).
  - The sliced-image layout means that with images off, the whole offer is invisible.
- **Pixels and spacers:** `coreemail.html?email=...`, the `svopn` pixel, `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/cd7cbdc3-...` and `spacer_0.gif` should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Empty `<title></title>`:** this is an accessibility and client-display defect.
- **Leaked PHP in `<style>`:** the block contains `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store..."); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. These are invalid CSS. Parsers will discard them, but they can break the rule immediately after them in strict clients.
- **Viewport:** `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom, an accessibility issue (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Responsive rules:** the mobile CSS targets only `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]`. The 650px `.contenttable` tables use fixed `width="650"`, and the `.contenttable` override is commented out. Because the source is truncated, I can't confirm whether mobile reflow works.
- **Webfont:** the Google Fonts `<link>` for Montserrat is stripped by Gmail and Outlook, so the fallback stack matters. The truncated source doesn't show it.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Merge tokens:** none are unresolved. The email address is injected correctly in `coreemail.html?email=` and `sv_sveme=`.
- **PII in URLs:** the recipient's address (`lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`) is placed in plaintext query strings. The `svopn` one is over HTTP, so the address is exposed in transit and in third-party server logs.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** neither header was found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may have stripped them, so treat this as unverified. If the headers are truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements aren't met.
- **Authentication-Results:** not present, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain is `e.potterybarn.com`, and alignment couldn't be verified.
- **Footer elements:** the visible unsubscribe link and physical address can't be confirmed because the HTML is truncated before the footer. The `Email-Footer/slices_*.jpg` sliced images may hold the footer text as images with no `alt`. That would hide the legally required text from image-blocking clients and screen readers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** none are visible. Links are opaque `qs=` blobs, and the one probed host (`www.potterybarn.com`) appears only in image and pixel URLs. Campaign attribution is presumably handled server-side after the redirect, but the QA data can't confirm it.
- **Landing alignment:** unverifiable, since none of the 53 click-redirects were followed.
- **Extra promo link:** the email is a Halloween campaign but carries `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg`, a Christmas banner. Landing destinations for the secondary modules should be checked in a live run.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, especially the hero and footer slices. Use `alt=""` on pixels and spacers.
2. Serve `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` and the `svopn` pixel over HTTPS. Replace or retire the 2022 asset.
3. Stop putting the raw email address in `coreemail.html?email=` and `sv_sveme=`. Use a hashed or opaque subscriber ID.
4. Fill in `<title>`, remove the PHP `header(...)` text from `<style>`, and drop `maximum-scale=1`.
5. Re-check the compliance items against raw `.eml` headers captured before the relay: `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Also confirm the footer has text-based unsubscribe and address content.
6. Run a follow-up click-through on a sample of the 53 redirects to verify landing pages and UTM or attribution carry-through.
7. Trim the plain-text part, using shorter or unwrapped URLs and more descriptive text.
