# Bedding bestsellers now on sale
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent but diffuse bedding send. The hero, "bedding bestsellers / Now on Sale," matches the subject, and the three product tiles (Pick-Stitch Quilt, Cloud Quilt, Cloud Duvet) are clean and aspirational. The problem is the discount. The only number is "Up to 70% off" in the top banner, which is a sitewide Fall Sale message. The bedding hero never says how much off, so the subject's promise of "on sale" has no price attached.
- The email also loses focus. After the bedding block it adds a four-category deal grid, a "Recommended Just For You" product grid, a Christmas Shop banner, Design Crew, an app promo, a credit card promo and Affirm. That is seven or eight competing messages in one scroll, and most of them have nothing to do with bedding. It reads as a template with a bedding module dropped in, not a bedding campaign.
- Against the brand history, this is the third bedding-adjacent send in three days. "Now on sale: Our bestselling Cozy Cloud Quilt" ran on 11-06, and today's email features the same Cloud Quilt again. A subscriber would see this as a repeat. Overall it is a solid 6: good photography and a clear category, undercut by a missing offer and a cluttered footer-heavy layout.

## What's working

- The hero typography ("bedding bestsellers / Now on Sale") is elegant, on-brand and matches the subject line.
- The three large product photos are well styled, and each has a labeled link, so the eye moves down the page easily.
- The "Shop All Bedding" outlined button gives a clear category-level CTA after the product tiles.
- The top banner's "Up to 70% off" is a strong, specific number, and the "Shop Now" button under it is visible.
- The secondary category grid (Furniture, Lighting, Pillows & Decor, Bath) is a useful, low-friction way to redirect people who aren't shopping for bedding.

## What's weak

- The bedding hero has no discount. "Now on Sale" with no percentage or price leaves the offer vague.
- There are two "Shop Now" buttons stacked at the top, one for the Fall Sale banner and one for the bedding hero. That splits the primary CTA.
- The product tiles have no prices, sale prices, ratings or review counts, so nothing supports a click.
- The "Recommended Just For You" grid shows stockings, a dining table, curtain rods and a rug. This dilutes the bedding message and is the opposite of "just for you" for someone who clicked in for bedding.
- The lower half of the email (Christmas Shop, Design Crew, App Store, credit card, Affirm, long legal text) is long and heavy. Most readers will never scroll to it, and it makes the email feel bloated.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a number in the bedding hero, such as "Bedding up to 40% off", and show a sale price on each product tile (for example "Cloud Quilt, from $X"). This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Remove the Fall Sale banner or merge it into the hero, so there is one headline and one primary button.
- 3. Add star ratings or a "Bestseller" badge to each product to back up the "bestsellers" claim.
- 4. Replace the generic recommendation grid with bedding-adjacent items (sheets, pillows, throws), or cut it.
- 5. Move the Christmas Shop, app, credit card and Affirm modules out of this send. Keep the email to one idea plus a single "more deals" row.
- 6. Vary the hero product. The Cloud Quilt was the lead on 11-06, so lead with the duvet or the pick-stitch quilt to avoid repetition.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 40% off bestselling bedding`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Cloud Quilt & Duvet, now on sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Supersoft quilts, duvets and sheets, marked down through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our most-loved bedding, from $149. Free shipping on quilts.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard table-based XHTML layout with image-slice content and ESP click-wrapped links. Its main issues are widespread missing alt text, three non-HTTPS image sources, a stray PHP snippet inside a `<style>` block, and subscriber email addresses in image and tracking URLs. Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified. The QA run scored 50% (0 failures, 4 warnings), and the HTML source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque tracking links:** 53 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains, e.g. `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7ImQi...`. Destination validity and redirect behavior are unverified.
- **Non-HTTPS tracking pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is served over plain HTTP, so it may be blocked or flagged as mixed content. The subscriber email also travels in the clear.
- **Other tracking pixels:**
  - `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` is a third-party pixel.
  - `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/97bcbe53-...?mi_cid=...` is a first-party open pixel.
  - The multiple pixels add redundancy but also weight.
- **Email address in URLs (PII):** The subscriber address appears as a query parameter in `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` and in the `sv_sveme` parameter above. Both are exposed to any proxy, log, or image-caching layer.
- **Plain-text bloat:** 10,192 of 14,376 plain-text characters (about 71%) are URLs, which exceeds the 70% threshold. The long redirect strings are the cause.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images:**
  - The QA run flagged about 45 images.
  - Image-slice sets with no alt text: the `0727_SUN_PM_BestsellersOnSale/slices_01–11.jpg` set and the `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg` set.
  - Banners with no alt text: `H26_TOP_FallSale.jpg`, `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg`, and `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`.
  - Product images with no alt text: `holiday-icons-crewel-stockings-2-l.jpg`, `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`, `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg`, and others.
  - With images off, the email may render as blank space, and screen readers get no content from these images.
  - The tracking pixels and `spacer_0.gif` are not real accessibility defects, but they should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
- **HTTP image sources:** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` may be blocked by some clients.
- **Stray PHP code in CSS:** The second `<style>` block ends with `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store..."); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. This is invalid CSS. It is harmless in most clients but can break parsing of adjacent rules in strict clients.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Zoom restriction:** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom, an accessibility concern.
- **Mixed `role="presentation"` usage:** Some layout tables have `role="presentation"` and others don't, e.g. the spacer table `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><tr><td height="20">&nbsp;</td>`.
- **Webfont link:** The Google Fonts `<link>` (Montserrat) is stripped by many clients, so the fallback stack matters.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on a West Elm domain, which adds a dependency on a different property.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (e.g. `%%name%%`, `{{...}}`) were found in the visible source. The recipient email is populated correctly in the `coreemail.html?email=` and `sv_sveme=` parameters. The truncated HTML limits coverage.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
These items are unconfirmed, not confirmed defects.
- **List-Unsubscribe:** The header was not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture it, so this may be a capture artifact.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Also not found, so one-click unsubscribe is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** The header is absent, so SPF, DKIM, and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `e.potterybarn.com` can't be checked from the relay copy.
- **Footer:** The truncated source doesn't show the physical address or the in-body unsubscribe link. These need verification in the full HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** None are visible. Product image URLs are on `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`, and click destinations are hidden behind `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`. Landing-page alignment and campaign parameters can't be verified without resolving the redirects.
- **Stale asset path:** The hero slices come from `/pb/2025/US/0727_SUN_PM_BestsellersOnSale/`, a 2025 July campaign folder. The banners are 2026 (`H26_*`, `F26_*`). If the "Bestsellers on sale" offer is no longer active, the creative and the landing page may not match. Confirm that the destinations are still live.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, especially the 11 bestseller slices, the 18 footer slices, and the product images. Set `alt=""` on spacers and pixels.
2. Switch the three `http://` images, including the `svopn` pixel, to HTTPS.
3. Remove the stray PHP `header()` calls from the `<style>` block.
4. Stop passing the raw email address in `coreemail.html?email=` and `sv_sveme=`. Use an opaque subscriber ID instead.
5. Populate `<title>` and drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
6. Confirm that the full message carries `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and that SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass. Check the raw headers at the source, not the relay copy.
7. Resolve the click-wrapped URLs and verify that they land on live bedding pages with UTM parameters.
8. Shorten the tracking URLs, or add more real text, to bring the plain-text URL ratio under 70%.
