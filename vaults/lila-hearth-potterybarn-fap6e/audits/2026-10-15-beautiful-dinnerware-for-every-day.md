# Beautiful dinnerware for every day
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-15**
## Executive summary

- This is a long, category-led dinnerware and tabletop send with a holiday-hosting angle. The hero reads "A Timeless Table" with a "Dinnerware ships free" pill. It's a better-targeted send than the recent Fall Sale blasts, because it has a concrete theme (hosting) and one concrete benefit (free shipping). The hero shows food and place settings, so the topic is clear within a second.
- The email then keeps going. Flatware, glassware, napkins, table throws, barware and serveware tiles come first. Next are two more promos (The Holiday Table, Caterer's Box), a dining-room link grid, six "Recommended just for you" tiles, a "Shop our Latest Designs" banner, a Design Services banner, and the credit card, app and Affirm blocks. Some of those later tiles are curtain rods and beds, not dinnerware. By the end the email has lost the "beautiful dinnerware for every day" idea.
- Verdict: a strong top third and a bloated rest. The hero and free-shipping hook are good. The subject says "every day", but the body is a holiday-hosting pitch. That mismatch and the six-plus competing CTAs hold it back.

## What's working

- The hero is clear, with seasonal food photography and a "Dinnerware ships free" pill that gives a tangible offer.
- The tile grid (Flatware, Glassware, Napkins, Table Throws, Barware, Serveware) lets a shopper pick a category quickly.
- The Holiday Table and Caterer's Box ("serves up to 12 guests with ease") add practical, occasion-based storytelling.
- The photography is warm and consistent, and it looks on-brand for Pottery Barn.
- It's a break from the repetitive "Up to 70% off" Fall Sale sends of 10-13 and 10-14.

## What's weak

- The subject promises "every day" dinnerware, but the hero and sections are holiday hosting. The preview and subject don't match the email.
- The "Shop All Tabletop" button, two "Shop Now" buttons and the "Book a Free Appointment" button all compete.
- The "Recommended just for you" block includes curtain rods, beds and a swivel chair, which dilutes the dinnerware focus.
- The email is very long, so the dinnerware story is buried under apps, credit card, Affirm and fine print.
- The free-shipping offer has no visible deadline or detail near the hero, so there's no urgency.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut everything below the Caterer's Box except one recommendation row of dinnerware only, then the footer. This single change would lift the email most.
- 2. Make the hero and subject agree. Either lead with "every day" dinnerware or lead with holiday hosting.
- 3. Add a deadline to the free-shipping pill, such as "Ends Sunday".
- 4. Link the hero to a specific dinnerware collection, not just the category.
- 5. Show a price or a "from $X" on at least one hero product.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a sliced-image template with a good structure (preheader, VML/MSO namespaces, `role="presentation"` on most layout tables). It has widespread missing alt text, three non-HTTPS images (one leaks the recipient's address in cleartext), and several head-level accessibility gaps. The unsubscribe and authentication checks are inconclusive, not failed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverifiable destinations:** 60 links go through click-redirect domains (e.g. `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...`). The QA probe skipped them, so I can't confirm they resolve or where they land.
- **Cleartext PII in a tracking pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` puts the recipient's email in a plain-HTTP query string. It can be intercepted or logged in transit, and it is also blocked or rewritten by some clients.
- **Email in an image URL:** `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is used as an `<img>` src and also carries the address in the URL.
- **Other tracking pixels:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEv.../cat/1` (a third-party ad-tech pixel) and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/62afcb14-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...` have no alt.
- **Cross-brand asset:** the spacer `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on a different brand domain. If that path is removed, this email breaks.
- **Stale asset paths:** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` is a 2022 asset, and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` is also served over HTTP.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** the QA report lists about 52 images with no alt attribute.
  - Content-bearing images: the 18 `H26_Ttop_B1_CoreDinnerware/slices_01–18.jpg` slices, `H26_Top_ChristmasShop.jpg`, `F26_Bottom_NewArrivals_V1.jpg`, `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`, the 6 product images (e.g. `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`) and the 16 `Email-Footer/slices_*.jpg` slices. Since the layout is image slices, the email is largely unreadable with images blocked or in a screen reader.
  - Pixels and spacers (`spacer_0.gif`, the turn.com, svopn and clickcp pixels): these should have explicit `alt=""`.
- **Insecure images:** 3 images use `http://`, which may be blocked or flagged as mixed content: `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, 2022 `slices_02.jpg`, and the `svopn` pixel.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **No language attribute:** the root element is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" ...>` with no `lang`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile.
- **Stray code in CSS:** the second `<style>` block ends with PHP-style text (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store...`). It is invalid CSS and harmless in most clients, but a strict parser may drop it or the rules around it.
- **Layout tables:** the spacer tables (e.g. `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><tr><td height="20">&nbsp;</td>`) lack `role="presentation"`, unlike the content tables.
- **External font:** the Google Fonts `<link>` for Montserrat is stripped by Gmail and some other clients. No fallback stack was visible in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%...%%` or `{{...}}` appear in the visible source. The recipient email is resolved in the `coreemail.html` and `svopn` URLs.
- The source is truncated, so tokens in the body or footer can't be fully ruled out.
- The only issue here is the privacy exposure from putting the email address in URLs (see section 2).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe headers:** `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is inconclusive. Confirm against the raw message headers before treating it as a sender defect.
- **Authentication:** `Authentication-Results` is absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown (relay-related).
- **Plain-text part:** 11,550 of 15,788 characters (73%) are URLs, which is over the 70% threshold. It is link-heavy and may score worse with spam filters.
- **Footer:** the visible unsubscribe link and postal address are cut off by the source truncation, so I can't verify them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No UTM parameters are visible. Links are opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` tokens, so campaign attribution and landing pages can't be checked from the source or from QA.
- Product images load from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`, so the image CDN domain matches the site.
- The subject, "Beautiful dinnerware for every day", and the `H26_Ttop_B1_CoreDinnerware` assets match the dinnerware theme. The click-through destinations are unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to all content and product images. Add `alt=""` to pixels and spacers.
2. Switch the `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, 2022 `slices_02.jpg` and `svopn` URLs to HTTPS.
3. Stop putting the raw email address in tracking and image URLs, and use a hashed or opaque ID instead.
4. Set `<html lang="en">` and a meaningful `<title>`. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport.
5. Remove the stray `header(...)` text from the `<style>` block.
6. Add `role="presentation"` to the spacer tables. Host the spacer on the Pottery Barn domain instead of westelm.com.
7. Check the raw headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Run a full-source check of the footer and tokens.
8. Resolve the click-redirect tokens to confirm the landing pages and UTM tagging.
