# Introducing the Big Daddy's Antiques collab
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's collab launch with Shane Brown of Big Daddy's Antiques. It's a real editorial story, and the best-looking send in recent weeks. The hero is a portrait of Brown beside a dark dining room, with the "BIGDADDY'S ANTIQUES" co-brand lockup and a single **SHOP NOW** button. Two pull quotes ("Patina. It's what gives a piece its soul.") sit between moody, wreath-dressed room shots. A dresser and bed vignette leads into a **SHOP THE COLLECTION** button and an "about our partner" blurb.
- The story is strong, but the commerce is weak. No price, percentage or offer appears anywhere. No individual collab product is named or linked in the body. The sequence of hero, quote, image, quote, image, button is long, and a shopper has to scroll a long way before any collab piece is identified. The lower half (Recommended Just For You, Christmas Shop, Design Services, app, rewards card, Affirm) is generic template filler and weakens the collab story. It reads as a brand-awareness send, not a conversion send.
- Judgement: it's a good brand-building email with no reason to click today. It deserves a point above the brand's usual 6 for the creative, but the missing offer and product specificity hold it there.

## What's working

- The collab has a real hook: a named partner, a human face in the hero, and a co-brand lockup.
- Pull quotes from Shane Brown give the email a point of view and break up the images.
- The styled room photography is cohesive. The dark wood, wreaths and warm lighting fit early-fall and holiday nesting.
- The hero has one clear **SHOP NOW** CTA, and **SHOP THE COLLECTION** repeats it mid-email.
- The "about our partner" block adds credibility (30+ years of sourcing).

## What's weak

- There is no offer, price or incentive anywhere in the collab story.
- No collab product is named or labeled. The hero and lifestyle images don't say what the pieces are or what they cost.
- The top half is very long and quote-heavy. The first product-level detail only appears in the generic "Recommended Just For You" grid, which is not collab product.
- The footer stack of Christmas Shop, Design Services, app, rewards and Affirm dilutes the focus and repeats what recent sends pushed (the 2026-09-29 "100% free interior design help" send).
- The "about our partner" block sits after the CTA, at small scale, so it is easy to miss.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a product-forward module right after the hero: 4–6 named collab pieces with prices, each linked to its product page. This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Cut one quote-and-image pair and move the partner bio up next to the hero, so the story lands in the first two scrolls.
- 3. Add a reason to act now: a launch-week perk, limited quantities, or an early-access window for Rewards members.
- 4. Drop the generic "Recommended Just For You" grid and the Design Services, app and Affirm blocks from this send, or collapse them into the footer.
- 5. Make the CTA specific, for example "Shop the Big Daddy's Antiques collection" instead of "SHOP NOW".

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Patina, history and character, reimagined for your home. Shop the collab.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Meet the collection: dressers, beds and dining pieces with a story.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Pottery Barn, "Introducing the Big Daddy's Antiques collab"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, image-sliced template with heavy tracking instrumentation. The QA run gave a 57% pass rate with 0 errors and 3 warnings. The main code-level issues are widespread missing `alt` attributes, three `http://` image sources (one carrying the recipient's address in plaintext), and stray server-side code pasted into a `<style>` block. The missing-header warnings are probably artifacts of the AgentMail relay, so they are not confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracking links were not verified.** 53 links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, for example `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABB7InYi...`. Destinations and redirect health are unconfirmed.
- **Mixed-content tracking pixels.**
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` is an open-tracking pixel over plain HTTP. It may be blocked or rewritten by clients.
  - The same URL has no `alt`.
  - Three third-party or secondary tracking beacons are also present: `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/...`, `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/0b0a7766-...?mi_cid=...`, and `coreemail.html?email=...`. All load without `alt`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` text on about 45 images.**
  - This covers every slice in the `0930_WED_PM_BigDaddysAntiques/slices_01–10` hero set. With images off or a screen reader, the campaign's main content is unreadable.
  - It also covers the product images (`reeva-handwoven-rug-3-l.jpg`, `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`, `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`, and others) and `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`.
  - Banners `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg` and `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg` are also missing it.
  - The QA output flags tracking pixels (`d.turn.com`, `svopn`, `clickcp`, `coreemail.html`) as well. For those, `alt=""` with a 1×1 size is the correct fix, not descriptive text.
- **Non-HTTPS images (3).**
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - The `svopn` pixel above.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Stray non-CSS code in the `<style>` block.** The text `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store, ..."); header("Pragma: no-cache");` sits inside the second `<style>` block. It looks like leaked PHP. Parsers will treat it as invalid CSS. It is harmless in most clients, but it can break the rule that follows it in strict ones.
- **Commented-out viewport meta tags.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=320, initial-scale=0.29">` and `width=1024` are left in comments. This is cosmetic.
- **Possible dead mobile selectors.** The selectors `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` have no matching elements in the visible, truncated source. I can't confirm whether they match anything later in the document.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved merge tokens** (such as `%%...%%` or `{{...}}`) in the visible source.
- **Recipient email exposed in URLs.**
  - `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is appended in plaintext to `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=...`.
  - It is also appended to the HTTP-only `svopn` pixel as `sv_sveme=`.
  - Sending it over `http://` exposes PII in transit and in proxy or server logs.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found (WARN).** The QA note attributes this to the relay possibly not capturing the header. It is unconfirmed, not a verified defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found (WARN).** The same caveat applies. One-click unsubscribe support is unknown. Gmail and Yahoo's bulk-sender rules require it, so check the raw message headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found (WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment could not be assessed. The From domain `e.potterybarn.com` and the click domain `click.e.potterybarn.com` are consistent, which is a good sign but not proof.
- **Physical address and unsubscribe link not assessable.** The HTML source is truncated before the footer, so I can't confirm either.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters cannot be verified.** All outbound links go through `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=<opaque>` redirects, and the HTTP probe was skipped. Whether UTM parameters are applied at redirect time, or whether the landing pages match the "Big Daddy's Antiques" creative, cannot be confirmed from this data.
- **Hotlinked product images.** They load from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...` at 2026-dated paths. `edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a cross-brand asset dependency. Neither is an error, but the spacer has no `alt`.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, starting with the `0930_WED_PM_BigDaddysAntiques/slices_01–10` hero set and the product tiles. Use `alt=""` plus explicit dimensions on the tracking pixels.
2. Move the three `http://` image sources to `https://`. Prioritize the `svopn` pixel because it carries the recipient email.
3. Stop putting the recipient's raw email address in query strings (`coreemail.html?email=`, `sv_sveme=`). Use a hashed or opaque ID instead.
4. Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP lines from the `<style>` block, and fill in the `<title>`.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
6. Inspect the raw `.eml` headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass. The AgentMail relay may be stripping them.
7. Follow a few `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirects to check the UTM parameters and landing pages.
