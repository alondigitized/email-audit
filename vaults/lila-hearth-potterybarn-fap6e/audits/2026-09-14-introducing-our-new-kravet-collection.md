# Introducing our NEW Kravet Collection
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-14**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's brand-collaboration launch — introducing a new Kravet textile collection, and it's the most editorially ambitious send in the recent run. The "kravet. x POTTERY BARN" hero lockup, the layered floral bedroom shot, and the designer-archive framing ("Layer pattern like an interior designer with new pieces inspired by Kravet's storied textile archive") give this real point-of-view, distinct from the seating/rug/dining category emails that have dominated this brand's last six sends.
- But it's a pure aesthetics play with zero commercial hooks. There's no price, no % off, no "new arrivals" urgency — just mood, quote cards, and a long scroll of lifestyle photography before a "Recommended Just For You" grid finally surfaces buyable SKUs. For a subscriber skimming on a commute, this reads as a lookbook, not an email designed to convert.
- Verdict: beautifully produced, on-brand storytelling, but it under-performs on the fundamentals that actually drive opens and clicks — no offer, generic CTA copy, and a subject line that assumes the reader already cares about Kravet as a name.

## What's working

- The "kravet. x POTTERY BARN" hero lockup immediately signals this is a designer collaboration, not a routine sale email.
- The layered floral bedroom hero is well-lit, styled, and on-message with the "layer pattern like an interior designer" copy directly beneath it.
- Two italicized quote cards ("Patterns tell the story of a home..." / "Start small, think in layers, and build over time.") add editorial voice consistent with recent sends like the Molly Sims influencer email.
- The "Recommended Just For You" product grid at the bottom finally gives concrete, clickable SKUs with visible thumbnails (curtain rods, rugs, platform beds).
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text doesn't overlap, layout holds together across the full scroll.

## What's weak

- No visible price, discount, or any concrete offer anywhere in the email — this is pure mood/inspiration copy.
- Primary CTA is just "SHOP NOW," generic and undifferentiated from every other Pottery Barn send in this history.
- It takes a long scroll (multiple full lifestyle photo blocks and two quote cards) before any actual product appears — high friction for someone who wants to shop fast.
- Subject line "Introducing our NEW Kravet Collection" assumes reader familiarity with Kravet as a brand name; most consumers won't recognize it as a textile-industry name.
- This is the second unpriced, pure-editorial send in three days (holiday preview, seating story) — cadence is leaning heavily on inspiration over conversion.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move a "SHOP THE COLLECTION" product strip (with visible SKUs/prices) up to right after the hero, instead of making readers scroll past two quote cards to reach it.
- 2. Replace the generic "SHOP NOW" CTA with something specific like "Shop the Kravet Collection" so it's clear what clicking leads to.
- 3. Add a concrete hook — new-arrival badge, limited-run framing, or launch pricing — since collaborations create natural urgency that isn't being used here.
- 4. Rewrite the subject to explain what Kravet is, not just name-drop it, since most subscribers won't recognize the brand.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New: A designer textile collab, straight from Kravet's archive`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Layered patterns, storied fabrics — the Kravet collection is here`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Bedding, pillows & more inspired by Kravet's decades of textile design`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop the new collaboration before it's gone`

- Open-tracking pixel present via SocialVine: `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` — served over plain HTTP (see §3) and includes the recipient email address in the URL query string in cleartext, which will leak the address to any intermediary logging that request (proxies, CDN logs, browser history).
- Second tracking pixel: `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` (Turn/Amobee) — third-party ad-tracking pixel embedded in a marketing email; confirm this is intentional and disclosed in the privacy policy.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 46 `<img>` tags are missing `alt` text (QA-confirmed), spanning hero banners, product slices, footer navigation, and both tracking pixels. Screen-reader and images-off users will see no fallback text for any content image in this email.
- Two images use insecure `http://` sources rather than `https://`, which many mail clients (Outlook, Gmail image proxy) will block or flag as mixed content:
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`

- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow" />` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer" />` are present — correct practice for transactional/marketing email markup.
- Responsive breakpoints via `@media` queries and MSO conditional comments (`<!--[if gte mso 9]>`, `<!--[if mso 16]>`) are structured correctly for Outlook/desktop fallback.
