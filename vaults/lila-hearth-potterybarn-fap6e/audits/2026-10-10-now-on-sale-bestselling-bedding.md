## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with 58 redirect-wrapped tracking links. It has no confirmed hard failures. The main technical issues are pervasive missing `alt` text on sliced images, three `http://` image sources, and stray non-CSS code in a `<style>` block. Header-level compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified because the relay didn't capture the headers.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 58 tracking links on click-redirect domains were skipped for HTTP probing, so destination validity is unverified. Example: `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7ImQi...`. This is a QA coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- Tracking pixels are embedded as images with no alt text:
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=<recipient email>`
  - `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1`
  - `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/fbecbbbd-...?mi_cid=...`
- The `svopn` pixel is served over plain `http://`, so it may be blocked or downgraded. That undercounts opens and exposes the recipient address in cleartext (see section 4).
- `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a cross-brand asset dependency. It loads from a West Elm host inside a Pottery Barn send.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Missing alt text:** about 50 images have no `alt`. The affected set:
  - Hero and banner slices: `H26_TOP_FallSale.jpg`.
  - Main body slices: `1010_SAT_AM_BeddingSale/slices_01–16.jpg`.
  - Product images: `standard-curtain-rod-wall-bracket-l.jpg`, `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`, `sausalito-8-drawer-dresser-66-l.jpg`, `york-slope-arm-deep-seat-slipcovered-sofa-60-108-l`, `chunky-wool-jute-rug-l.jpg` and `lit-ghost-collection-l.jpg`.
  - Bottom banners: `F26_Bottom_NewArrivals_V1.jpg` and `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg`.
  - Footer slices: `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`.
  - Other: `3ceb0c96-f305-4f56-a6bf-1ce072abc871.jpg`.
  
  The sale offer is carried by the sliced images, so with images off or in a screen reader the email may convey little. The logo does have `alt="POTTERY BARN"`.
- **Insecure image sources (`http://`):**
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/...`
- **Empty `<title>`:** `<title></title>` is present.
- **Invalid CSS in `<style>`:** the last `<style>` block contains PHP-style statements as literal text: `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store..."); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. Clients parse this as invalid CSS. It's harmless in most clients but could break rule parsing after that point in strict ones.
- **Attribute-selector media queries:** `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` only match when `class` is exactly that value. They are also unsupported in some clients, such as Gmail app variants.
- **Image width rules:** `img {max-width: 650px}` and `img {max-width: 100%!important}` at the 700px and 650px breakpoints overlap. The result is OK but redundant.
- **Layout and sizing:** the layout tables lack `role="presentation"` in places. For example, the spacer `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0">` after the logo has none.
- **Web font:** the Google Fonts `<link>` for Montserrat is stripped by most clients. It adds no value and no fallback stack was visible in the truncated source.
- **Viewport:** `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch zoom, which is an accessibility concern in clients that honor it.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- No unresolved merge tokens were found in the visible source.
- The recipient email (`lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`) is placed in plaintext query strings:
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=...`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/...&sv_sveme=...`
  
  The `svopn` URL is plain HTTP, so the address is exposed in transit. This is a privacy and security concern, not a rendering bug.
- The `coreemail.html` URL is used as an `<img>` source, which makes it a tracking pixel.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` was not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture it, so this is unconfirmed.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was not found, with the same caveat. One-click unsubscribe is unverified.
- `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The relay is the likely cause. The From domain `e.potterybarn.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain.
- The footer was truncated, so the visible HTML can't confirm a physical postal address or an in-body unsubscribe link. Verify both in the full source.
- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow" />` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer" />` are present. They are harmless in email and ignored by clients.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- The visible links use opaque redirect tokens (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`). UTM parameters can't be verified without resolving them, and the probe was skipped.
- Product image URLs point at `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`, which is the same brand domain as the site.
- The subject says "NOW ON SALE: Bestselling bedding" and the preheader says "Get cozy with up to 70% off". The assets are in `1010_SAT_AM_BeddingSale`. The product images shown are a curtain rod bracket, a swivel chair, a dresser, a sofa, a rug and a "ghost collection" light. Most are not bedding. Landing-page alignment can't be confirmed from the HTML alone.
- The plain-text part is more than 70% URLs (11265 of 15484 characters). It consists mostly of long tracking URLs, which suggests no meaningful text alternative.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, especially the `slices_*` offer images. Use `alt=""` for spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Move the three `http://` images (`Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `slices_02.jpg` from `0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner`, and the `svopn` pixel) to HTTPS. Stop putting the raw recipient email in query strings, and use a hashed or opaque ID instead.
3. Remove the PHP `header(...)` statements from the `<style>` block and fill in `<title>`.
4. Drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta to allow pinch zoom. Replace the exact-match attribute selectors with class selectors.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF, DKIM and DMARC results from raw headers at the originating MTA, not through the relay. Confirm the footer unsubscribe link and postal address in the full HTML.
6. Resolve a sample of the `click.e.potterybarn.com` links to check UTM parameters and landing pages. Also check that they align with the bedding offer.
7. Improve the plain-text alternative so it has real copy rather than tracking URLs, and remove the West Elm spacer dependency.
