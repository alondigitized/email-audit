# The 4th is gone but our sale is STILL ON ❤️
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-05**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Pottery Barn email today and somewhere around send twenty-plus in the July 4th campaign — and the subject line is the most self-aware thing the brand has written in this entire sequence. "The 4th is gone but our sale is STILL ON" actually acknowledges the world the subscriber is living in rather than pretending yesterday's holiday urgency is still fresh. That's a meaningful creative pivot. The email itself, though, doesn't live up to the subject: it's the same Extra 20% off hero, the same SHOP SALE button, the same Open Box Outlet module, the same category nav — clone-stamped from the last dozen sends.
- The one structural bright spot is the "RECOMMENDED JUST FOR YOU" personalized product grid, which surfaces specific bedroom furniture with real product names and pricing. That module does more selling than everything above it. The rest is campaign creative that has been mailed into the ground.
- At three emails in a single day — following "Ends tomorrow" and "ONLY 48 HOURS LEFT" — this send lands with diminishing returns no matter how clever the subject line is. The brand is training its list to wait for markdowns rather than act.

## What's working

- **Subject line is fresh and honest.** "The 4th is gone but our sale is STILL ON" is the most situationally aware copy in this entire sequence — it meets the subscriber where they are on July 5th.
- **"RECOMMENDED JUST FOR YOU" personalization module** surfaces specific bedroom product tiles with visible product names — the closest this email gets to one specific linked product.
- **Open Box Outlet callout** ("Extra 20% off Open Box Outlet Deals") is a distinct value proposition not buried at the bottom.
- **Category navigation bar** is comprehensive and scannable — Outdoor, Sofas & Chairs, Pillows & Throws, Bed + Bath all visible at a glance.
- **Loyalty program block** ("Earn 10% Back in Rewards") appears in the email body, not just the footer.

## What's weak

- **Third email today, same creative skeleton.** The hero module is identical to the prior two sends — same banner, same SHOP SALE button, same 20% off framing — which completely undercuts the novelty of the subject line.
- **No specific deadline anywhere in the body.** The subject implies the sale will end, but there is no "ends Sunday" or "through July 7" call-out visible — urgency evaporates the moment you scroll past the subject.
- **Hero CTA sends to a sale category page, not a specific product.** "SHOP SALE" is a warehouse door, not a product recommendation.
- **Large lifestyle photo of outdoor seating mid-scroll** is beautiful but adds nothing — no price, no CTA, no product link visible.
- **"Discover New Arrivals" module contradicts the clearance-sale urgency** — mixing sale-closeout messaging with new-arrival browsing is a mixed signal.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a specific end date to the body hero** — "Sale ends Sunday July 7 at midnight" turns the "still on" subject into an actual countdown rather than vague reassurance. This is the single highest-leverage change.
- 2. **Collapse the email to three modules max** — subject is doing the creative lifting; the body should be: (1) the offer with a deadline, (2) the personalized product grid, (3) Open Box Outlet. Cut the lifestyle photo and the New Arrivals module.
- 3. **Make the CTA category-specific** — swap "SHOP SALE" for "Shop Outdoor Sale" or "Shop Bedroom Sale" matched to the personalized product grid above it; it's the only place in this email where personalization is actually applied.
- 4. **Do not send a fourth email today.** The cadence has crossed from urgency into noise.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Sale ends Sunday — Extra 20% off still yours`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 20% off window closes this weekend`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus up to 70% off select styles — including Open Box Outlet deals.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Open Box Outlet deals + Extra 20% off. Ends July 7.`

### 1. Technical Summary

One confirmed broken link returns 403, 47 images lack alt text, three image sources use unencrypted HTTP, and compliance headers (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) are absent from what was captured. The email is otherwise structurally standard for a Williams-Sonoma Brands ESP send.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**FAIL — Broken link (403)**
The credit card apply-and-buy banner link returns HTTP 403:
```
https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/c/da01b407-b21c-4618-a71b-f83bef0dd682?brandCode=POTTERYBARNCB&marketingChannelCode=APPLYANDBUY...
```
This is the primary Key Rewards / credit card CTA. Any click on the credit card banner goes nowhere. Needs an immediate redirect fix or link replacement before further sends.

**INFO — 23 tracking links skipped**
All `click.e.potterybarn.com` and `clickcp.potterybarn.com` redirect links were not probed. The 403 above was one that resolved; others may also be broken. Manual spot-check of high-value CTAs (hero, sale category buttons) is warranted.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**47 images missing `alt` text** — the entire image grid has empty or absent `alt` attributes. Affected modules include:

- Hero urgency banner: `S26_MemorialDaySale_UrgencyTopBanner_EndsTomorrow.jpg`
- All 13 slices in `S26_Sale_July4Sale_9GridHeroLinkFarm/`
- All 11 slices in `Updated26_xBrand_Bottom_Banner_Red/`
- All 18 footer slices in `Email-Footer/`
- Product recommendation images (curtain rod, bed, quilt, dining table, towels, mattress)
- Open box deals banner, outdoor sale banner, new arrivals banner

With images off (Gmail Promotions tab default on mobile, many corporate proxies), the email renders as a blank white column with no readable content or CTAs.

**Three HTTP (non-HTTPS) image sources** — will be blocked by Gmail, Apple Mail, and Outlook when loaded over HTTPS email views:
```
http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif
http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg
http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg
```
The `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` is the credit card footer banner — doubly broken (also references a 2022 asset path).

**PHP `header()` calls in CSS block** — the `<style>` block contains literal PHP header directives:
```html
<style>
  ...
  header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
  header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
  header("Pragma: no-cache");
</style>
```
These are server-side PHP statements rendered verbatim into the HTML. They do nothing in an email client but indicate a template rendering failure — a dynamic asset CDN snippet was pasted into the wrong block. No functional breakage in current form, but signals a template hygiene issue.

**`<title>` is empty** — `<title></title>` renders as blank in clients that display it (some desktop clients show the title in tab/preview).

**Plain-text body is 74% URLs** (12,195 of 16,472 chars). Spam filters that score plain-text/HTML ratio will flag this. The plain-text version appears auto-generated from click-redirect URLs rather than readable copy.

### 5. Compliance

**WARN — `List-Unsubscribe` header not captured**
The AgentMail relay did not surface this header. Could be relay stripping or genuine absence. CAN-SPAM and Gmail/Yahoo 2024 bulk sender requirements both mandate this. Needs verification against raw SMTP headers.

**WARN — `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not captured**
One-click unsubscribe (required by Google/Yahoo for bulk senders >5k/day) is either absent or stripped. Same caveat — confirm from raw headers.

**WARN — `Authentication-Results` not captured**
SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status unknown through the relay. `PotteryBarn@e.potterybarn.com` should align with a DMARC policy on `potterybarn.com` — unverifiable from this capture.

Physical address and unsubscribe link are present in the footer (standard for WSB emails) — CAN-SPAM footer requirement appears satisfied at the HTML level.
