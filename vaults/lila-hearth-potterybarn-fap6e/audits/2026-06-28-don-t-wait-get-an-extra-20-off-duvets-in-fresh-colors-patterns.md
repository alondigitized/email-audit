# Don't wait! Get an extra 20% off + Duvets in fresh colors & patterns
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-28**
## Executive summary

- Eight sends in, and Pottery Barn finally made a meaningful editorial call: put Duvet Covers at the center and lean into the "fresh colors & patterns" angle rather than just re-serving the same stacked-discount template. The hero executes this well — a clean bed vignette leads the eye, the duvet category tiles give a real sense of the assortment breadth, and the Extra 20% off offer is still stacked on top of the 4th of July Sale. The subject line is legitimately the strongest of this entire campaign run — it combines urgency, a specific discount, and a specific product category with a styling benefit in one read.
- That said, the structural offer is unchanged. Anyone who has been in this inbox since June 26 has seen the same Extra 20% off + tiered-sale setup across seven prior sends. The creative refresh is real, but the underlying deal is not new, and the "Don't wait!" urgency framing has been devalued by repetition. A subscriber who has waited this long has already decided this deal isn't for them — this email won't change that.
- Clear judgment: the best subject line of the run, a useful category pivot, but arriving one or two sends too late. The duvet angle should have led the campaign, not closed it.

## What's working

- **Subject line is the strongest of the entire campaign run** — urgency ("Don't wait!") + offer ("extra 20% off") + specific product + styling hook ("fresh colors & patterns") in a single line, more informative than any prior send.
- **Duvet hero is specific** — calling out a single product category with fresh lifestyle imagery is a genuine differentiation from the generic "4th of July Sale" template that dominated June 26–27.
- **Color/pattern variety in the tile grid** is visible and compelling — the assortment shots actually make the category feel shoppable, which prior sends with broader category mixes didn't achieve.
- **"Recommended for you" section** signals personalization and gives the email a second purchase hook below the hero.
- **Clean render** — no broken images, no text overlap, no layout failures visible.

## What's weak

- **Cadence fatigue is now critical.** This is the eighth send in approximately three days with the same stacking offer. The "Don't wait!" urgency reads hollow against that backdrop — they've been "not waiting" since June 26.
- **No visible deadline date.** "Don't wait!" without an explicit "through July 4th" or countdown makes the urgency feel manufactured, not credible.
- **Subject is 68 characters** — clips on most mobile clients before "& patterns," losing the styling hook that differentiates this send.
- **The New Arrivals furniture section at the bottom is a category non-sequitur** — the email opens on Duvet Covers focus and then pivots to sofas and living room pieces, diluting the editorial thread.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill one or two sends from this run and concentrate the duvet creative earlier.** This is editorial planning feedback, not a copy fix — the category angle is strong enough to anchor a send on its own, ideally mid-campaign as a refresh moment, not as the eighth send.
- 2. **Add a hard deadline to the urgency.** Replace "Don't wait!" with "Ends July 4th" — makes the deadline real and stops the urgency from feeling like boilerplate.
- 3. **Trim the subject to under 55 chars** so the full product hook survives mobile truncation.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Duvets in fresh colors — extra 20% off, ends July 4th`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Extra 20% off duvet covers in new colors & patterns`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop the sale before it wraps — free shipping on orders $149+`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Linen, percale, and prints in colors you haven't seen this season`
- 4. **Remove or relocate the New Arrivals furniture module.** A Duvet Covers email should end on a bedding upsell (shams, sheets, pillow inserts) — not pivot to sofas.
- 5. **Add member pricing or loyalty-exclusive framing** if Pottery Barn Key members get a better rate — that signal is absent and would genuinely lift conversion.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[HIGH] PHP `header()` directives inside `<style>` block**
The following raw PHP code appears verbatim inside a `<style>` tag in `<head>`:
```css
header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
header("Cache-Control: post-check=0, pre-check=0", false);
header("Pragma: no-cache");
```
These are server-side PHP function calls that were never executed — they were copy-pasted into the stylesheet as literal text. Email clients will attempt to parse them as CSS (and fail silently), but their presence indicates a broken template assembly step.

**[HIGH] 3 images served over HTTP (not HTTPS)**
Email clients with mixed-content policies (Gmail, Apple Mail on iOS 15+) may block these:
- `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
- `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&...` (open-tracking pixel)

**[HIGH] 50+ images missing `alt` text**
Affects nearly every content image including all 17 duvet slices (`slices_01.jpg`–`slices_17.jpg`), promo banners (`S26_Top_July4Sale_NoUrgency.jpg`, `S26_Bottom_UpTo60OffOutdoor.jpg`, `F26_Bottom_NewArrivals.jpg`), and all 18 footer slices. Images-off rendering will be a blank experience.

**[LOW] `maximum-scale=1` in viewport meta**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
Locks pinch-zoom on mobile; fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).

**[LOW] Global `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`**
```css
*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; -ms-text-size-adjust:none;}
```
Applied via the universal selector — prevents OS-level text scaling for low-vision users.

**[LOW] Empty `<title>` element**
```html
<title></title>
```
Screen readers announce an empty subject line when the email is opened in browser-view.
