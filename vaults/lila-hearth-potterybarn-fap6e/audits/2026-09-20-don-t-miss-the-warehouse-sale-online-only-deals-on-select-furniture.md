# Don't miss The Warehouse Sale + Online-only deals on select furniture
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-20**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's warehouse sale doing a straightforward furniture-collection crawl — "Select Furniture Now on Sale" leads into four named collections (Aubrey, Toscana, Livingston, Farmhouse), each with a lifestyle hero shot and a red "Now on Sale" flag. It's clean and legible, but it's also nearly a repeat of the 2026-09-18 "Shop The Warehouse Sale + Create a cozy year-round outdoor space" send — same "up to 60% off" banner, same warehouse-sale mechanic, five sends in ten days touching this promo. The fatigue is starting to show.
- The header banner claims "up to 60% off" but not a single product in the body shows a price, a percentage, or a strikethrough — that's the biggest miss. A shopper has to click through blind to find out if the discount applies to the piece they're looking at. The "Recommended Just for You" grid and Halloween Shop cross-sell are reasonable adds, but they dilute an already-long email rather than reinforcing the sale.
- Net: professionally produced, no render bugs, clear navigation — but it's the same warehouse-sale beat with no visible offer specificity, running for the fifth time this window.

## What's working

- Four distinct collections (Aubrey, Toscana, Livingston, Farmhouse) each get their own hero image and a dedicated "Collection" link, making it easy to jump to a style.
- Consistent "Now on Sale" red flag tag repeated across every hero reinforces the promo without needing bold pricing copy.
- "Recommended Just for You" section shows real personalization intent with a distinct product grid.
- No overlapping text, broken images, or layout breakage anywhere in the render.
- Clear top-of-email CTA ("Shop Furniture Sale") sits right under the headline, above the fold.

## What's weak

- No visible price, percent-off, or strikethrough anywhere in the body — the "up to 60% off" only appears once, in the top banner.
- This is the second warehouse-sale email in three days (following 2026-09-18) with near-identical framing — cadence fatigue risk.
- The email is very long (four collections + recommended grid + new arrivals + Halloween shop + app + credit card + Affirm) before reaching the footer, burying the core offer under cross-sells.
- "Select Furniture" in the hero copy is vague — it never tells the reader whether their category (bedroom, office, dining) is included until they scroll.
- The Halloween Shop insert is a jarring, off-topic detour in the middle of a furniture sale email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add actual discount percentages or sale prices on at least the top 1-2 collection heroes (e.g., "Aubrey Collection — up to 40% off") — this is the single biggest lever since the current version relies entirely on the top banner for the discount claim.
- 2. Trim the email — cut or move the Halloween Shop and App download blocks to a separate send; they compete with the sale message rather than support it.
- 3. Vary the creative from the 09-18 warehouse sale send (different hero collections, different subject structure) to reduce repeat-promo fatigue.
- 4. Move "Recommended Just for You" higher or make it feel more distinct from the static collection crawl, since generic recs this deep may go unseen.

## Technical Audit — Pottery Barn "Warehouse Sale" Email

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**

- Multiple redundant open-tracking pixels present: `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/...`, `svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=...&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...`, and `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/5003d64d-...`. The `svopn.potterybarn.com` pixel is served over **http://**, not https.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` are present — expected for email but confirm intentional if this markup is ever reused on a web-hosted version.
- 40 `<img>` tags missing `alt` text (full list in QA output), spanning hero banners (`F26_Top_WarehouseSale2.jpg`), product shots (`remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`, `farmhouse-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`, `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`, etc.), and footer slice images (`slices_01–18.jpg`).
