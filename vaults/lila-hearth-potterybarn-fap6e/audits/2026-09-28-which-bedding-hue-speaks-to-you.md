## What's weak

- The "Up to 15% off Belgian Flax Linen Bedding" discount is visually buried — small caption text under the hero photo, not a banner or badge, so most scanners will miss the only real price incentive.
- Email keeps going well past the color-sort concept: "More Bedding Essentials" text grid, "Recommended Just For You," Halloween Shop, Design Crew — none tie back to the subject's hue-picking hook, diluting focus.
