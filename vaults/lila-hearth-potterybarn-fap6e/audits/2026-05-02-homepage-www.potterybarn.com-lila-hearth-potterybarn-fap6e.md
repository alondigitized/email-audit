# Homepage snapshot · www.potterybarn.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn feels like exactly the brand I'd expect to find when I'm dreaming up what the back deck of the lake house could look like — aspirational, lifestyle-forward, and clearly seasonal right now with an outdoor push. The second I landed, though, a sweepstakes modal hijacked the entire screen, which is a jarring way to start a browse. The brand underneath reads sophisticated and warm, but the entry experience undercuts it.
- **7/10**

## What's working

- **The sweepstakes hook itself** — "$1,500 toward your outdoor refresh" is a real number and a real fantasy for someone who just priced out a new patio sectional. That speaks directly to me.
- **The lifestyle photo inside the modal** — wicker seating, pool backdrop, lush garden greenery — this is 100% my second-home mood board. It's warm and editorial, not just a product shot.
- **"Our Design Crew can help bring it to life"** — the mention of design service is a trust signal I genuinely respond to. I've used the in-store consult before and this reminds me it exists.
- **The chat button** is visible and accessible even with the modal up, which is a nice safety net for someone mid-decision.

## What's weak

- **The modal blocks everything.** I cannot see the homepage at all. The navigation (Baby, Kids, Teen, Dorm) is partially visible at the top and it's already wrong — those aren't my categories. The entire above-the-fold homepage is hidden behind this gate.
- **The legal fine print is enormous and front-and-center.** Two full paragraphs of FCC wireless carrier boilerplate sit between the offer and the email field. That's not fine print treatment — it's the visual centerpiece of the modal, and it kills the vibe completely.
- **No urgency or deadline on the sweepstakes** — "Enter for a chance" with no end date gives me no reason to act now vs. closing it and coming back never.
- **The navigation categories I can partially see (Baby, Kids, Teen, Dorm)** suggest this top nav isn't personalized at all — it skews young/family when I'm here to outfit a second home, not a dorm room.

## Recommendations

- **Shrink the legal text.** Move the FCC/SMS boilerplate into a collapsed "Terms & conditions" link — one line max. Give the offer the visual space it deserves.
- **Add a soft deadline** — "Sweepstakes ends May 15" or a simple countdown chip would convert fence-sitters like me who need a nudge.
- **Add a "No thanks, take me to Outdoor" link** at the bottom of the modal so dismissing it routes me to the outdoor collection rather than dumping me on a generic homepage.
- **Surface the Design Crew CTA more prominently** — make it a tappable link in the modal body, not just a throwaway phrase. That's a genuine differentiator from Wayfair and I'd click it.
