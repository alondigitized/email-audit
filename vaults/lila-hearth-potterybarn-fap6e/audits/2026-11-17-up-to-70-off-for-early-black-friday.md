# Up to 70% off for EARLY BLACK FRIDAY
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth or sixth send in a row on the same burgundy "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY / up to 70% OFF" template. The subject, "Up to 70% off for EARLY BLACK FRIDAY," is close to a copy of "Early Black Friday is HERE + Timeless pieces UP TO 70% OFF" and "For the early shopper: UP TO 70% OFF" from yesterday and earlier today. The hero is strong. The eye lands on the big "70% OFF" and the white SHOP NOW button, and a Santa-mug lifestyle photo sits beneath it.
- The body is a long category stack: bedding, pillows and throws, sofas, furniture, gifts, rugs, decor and robes. Each block has its own "up to X% off" line, and the "SHIPS FREE" badges are a nice touch. The email then keeps going with "Recommended just for you," the Christmas Shop, a sweepstakes, the app, a rewards card and Affirm. That's about eight screens of content. The offer is clear, but the email has no single story and no new angle.
- My judgement: it's competent and clean, and it's also the fourth near-identical send this week. The creative is fine. The cadence and the lack of a distinct hook are the problem.

## What's working

- The hero is clear. "up to 70% OFF" is the largest type in the email, with a single white SHOP NOW button on burgundy.
- Category tiles carry their own discount ranges (bedding, pillows & throws, furniture at 60%, gifts at 50%), so a reader can scan for their area.
- "SHIPS FREE" badges on several tiles add a second incentive without cluttering the layout.
- The seasonal imagery is on point: Santa mugs, tartan candles and stockings. It matches the Early Black Friday moment.
- The "Shop All Sale" bar partway down gives a second CTA for readers who don't want to browse categories.

## What's weak

- The subject and hero repeat what the brand sent all week, including yesterday's three near-identical Early Black Friday sends. A subscriber would see nothing new.
- The email has too many modules. The sale grid, Recommended Just For You, the Christmas Shop, the Design Crew sweepstakes, the app, rewards and Affirm compete with each other and dilute the core offer.
- "Up to 70%" is a ceiling, and the tiles drop to "up to 20% off" for rugs and "up to 50%" for gifts. The headline overstates what most categories offer.
- Few tiles show a product with a was/now price. The candle and the robe have prices, but most tiles show only a percentage.
- The legal and fine print at the bottom is long and dense, and the headline discount is not clearly tied to its exclusions.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one category or one hero product and send it alone, as the Nov 15 bedding send did (8/10 in the history above). It was the best send of the week because it stayed on one thing.
- 2. Add a real deadline, such as "Ends Sunday" or a countdown, so the urgency is credible.
- 3. Cut the lower modules (sweepstakes, Affirm, app, rewards) or collapse them into one footer strip. Keep the "Recommended" row only if it is personalized.
- 4. Show was/now prices on at least four product tiles, so the savings are visible.
- 5. Give each send a new hook, such as a gift guide, a best-seller or a stock warning, so it reads as a new email and not the same template.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a standard 650px XHTML table layout, but it has widespread missing `alt` attributes (~50 images), several non-HTTPS image requests, and malformed CSS and head markup. The recipient's email address is also exposed in plaintext in tracking-pixel URLs. The HTML source is truncated, so the footer unsubscribe block and the header-level compliance checks could not be confirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque redirects, unverified:** 57 links go through `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` and were skipped by the HTTP probe. Their destinations and UTM parameters can't be confirmed, and this isn't a defect by itself.
- **Cleartext tracking pixel with PII:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is served over HTTP. The recipient address travels unencrypted in the query string, and clients may block the request.
- **Address in HTTPS URL:** `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is loaded as an `<img>` and also carries the raw address.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` is an ad-network pixel with no alt text. A further tracker is `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/e40f5c1d-...`.
- **Cross-brand asset host:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a spacer served from the sister brand's domain, which is a dependency on an unrelated host.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** About 50 images are flagged, including all 12 `Grid2/slices_01–12.jpg`, all 18 `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`, the six product images (e.g. `faux-fur-ruched-throw-l.jpg`) and the `H26_Bottom_*` banners. If images are blocked, the content, and possibly footer links, would be invisible. Only the logo has alt text (`alt="POTTERY BARN"`).
- **HTTP image sources:**
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - These may be blocked or trigger mixed-content warnings. The second one is also a stale 2022 asset path.
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in the head.
- **No `lang` attribute** on `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" ...>`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Malformed CSS:** Stray PHP text sits inside the second `<style>` block: `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store, ...");`. It is invalid CSS and may break parsing of rules that follow it in strict clients.
- **Layout tables:** Only some tables carry `role="presentation"`. The spacer table `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><tr><td height="20">&nbsp;</td>` and the outer wrappers do not.
- **Web font:** `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Montserrat...">` is stripped by Gmail and Outlook. No font stack is visible in the truncated source, so the fallback can't be confirmed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) appear in the visible source. The address is resolved correctly in the `sv_sveme=` and `?email=` parameters.
- The risk is the plaintext PII exposure described in section 2, not a broken token.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe:** Not found. The QA check says the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is inconclusive.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Not found, and equally inconclusive. If it is genuinely absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found. SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment could not be verified because of the relay. The From domain is `e.potterybarn.com`.
- **Body unsubscribe and postal address:** Not visible in the truncated HTML. The footer is built from image slices without alt text (`Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`), so if unsubscribe is image-based it has no text fallback. This can't be confirmed from the available source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- No UTM parameters are visible in the sampled source, because all 57 click URLs are wrapped in opaque `qs=` redirects. Landing-page alignment could not be verified.
- Direct `potterybarn.com` URLs appear only as image sources (`/pbimgs/...`), not as click targets.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, and `alt=""` to spacers and tracking pixels. Give the footer slices alt text, especially any that hold links.
2. Change the two `http://edm.potterybarn.com/...` images and the `http://svopn...` pixel to HTTPS. Replace the stale 2022 `BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` path.
3. Remove the plaintext email from the `sv_sveme` and `?email=` parameters, or replace it with a hashed or opaque ID.
4. Delete the stray `header(...)` PHP lines from the `<style>` block.
5. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and a meaningful `<title>`. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Add `role="presentation"` to the remaining layout tables.
6. Host the spacer on `edm.potterybarn.com` instead of `edm.westelm.com`.
7. Check the raw message headers (not the relay copy) for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, and check the full HTML footer for a text unsubscribe link and postal address.
8. Resolve a sample of the `click.e.potterybarn.com` links to confirm UTM tagging and landing-page alignment.
