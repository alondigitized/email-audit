## What's weak

- **No offer anywhere visible.** No %, no $, no free shipping — nothing to convert browse intent into a click decision.
- **Cadence fatigue.** Third send from Pottery Barn on one calendar day; subscribers who opened the dining table email two hours ago are already fatigued.
- **CTA copy indistinct.** Button text is too small to read at email-client scale — can't tell if it says "Shop Rugs" or "Explore" — which means it's likely bland filler copy.
