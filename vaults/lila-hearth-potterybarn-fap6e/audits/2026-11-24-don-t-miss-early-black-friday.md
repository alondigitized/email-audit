# Don't miss EARLY BLACK FRIDAY
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- This is the same burgundy "Early Black Friday, up to 70% off" Pottery Barn template that has run since 11/16. It is the eleventh or so send in about nine days, following yesterday's "Early Black Friday deals SELLING FAST" and "1000s of Early Black Friday deals + Must-have furniture deals". The hero is clean and the offer is clear. Nothing here is new, and a subscriber who has seen ten near-identical sends will probably scroll past.
- The structure is solid: a hero with a "SHOP NOW" button, a burgundy category grid, two priced product callouts with was/now pricing (Boucle Pillow $49.50 → $39.50, Whitetaker Chair $899 → $449), then a "Recommended Just For You" block. The page is very long, with a large legal and sweepstakes footer. The Christmas Shop tile ("up to 50% off") and the Design Crew sweepstakes compete with the Black Friday message in the lower half.
- The subject "Don't miss EARLY BLACK FRIDAY" is generic urgency with no number or product, and the all-caps phrase reads as shouty. The email is competent but fatigued. Its main problem is cadence and sameness, not execution.

## What's working

- The hero states a concrete offer ("up to 70% off") with one obvious SHOP NOW button.
- The category grid (Furniture, Bedding, Rugs, and so on) lets the reader jump straight to their interest.
- The was/now pricing on the Boucle Pillow ($49.50 → $39.50) and the chair ($899 → $449) makes the savings tangible. The chair is a 50% cut.
- "Ships Free" badges add a second incentive on the featured products.
- The "In-Store Savings / Find Your Store" block gives a useful omnichannel route.

## What's weak

- The creative is essentially unchanged from the last ten or more sends, which causes fatigue and weakens urgency.
- The hero says "up to 70%" but the featured products show only about 20–50% off, so the headline looks like a stretch.
- The lower half is cluttered: the Christmas Shop ("up to 50% off"), the Design Crew sweepstakes, the credit card, Affirm and the app promo all dilute the single message.
- The legal and sweepstakes footer is very long and dense. It adds to the feel of buried fine print.
- The "Recommended Just For You" items (stockings, quilt, dining table) are products with no prices or offers, so they give little reason to click.

## Recommendations

- 1. Change the creative between sends, or reduce the cadence. Leading with a different hero, such as a price-drop on one hero item or a countdown to Black Friday, would do more than any copy tweak.
- 2. Make the subject specific and lower-case. Name the number or the deadline.
- 3. Cut the lower-half promos (sweepstakes, card, Affirm, app) down to one, so the Black Friday offer stays the focus.
- 4. Add prices or discount badges to the "Recommended Just For You" products.
- 5. Show a real best-case example of "70% off" in the hero area, so the headline claim is backed up.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, image-sliced build. It is mostly sound structurally, but about 53 images have no alt text and three are served over `http://`. The relay captured neither the authentication headers nor `List-Unsubscribe`, so compliance can't be fully verified from this capture. The HTML source is truncated, so the footer, body links and later markup were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 64 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are redirect-tracked. Examples are `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...` on the logo link, and `clickcp.potterybarn.com`. Destination validity and status codes are unverified.
- **Third-party pixels:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` is an ad-network pixel. `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=...&sv_emopen=true` and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/4186897d-...` are separate open-tracking pixels. That makes three beacons, and the `svopn` one is plain HTTP.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a West Elm CDN asset in a Pottery Barn send. It's harmless unless that path is retired.
- **Stray code in CSS:** the second `<style>` block contains PHP-style text (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ...")`). It is ignored by renderers but is a template defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 53 images are flagged. They include:
  - the full hero slice sets (`H26_Sale_EBF_9GridHeroLinkfarm/slices_01–12.jpg`, `1119_THU_AM_EBF_GraphicHeroLinkFarm+WasNowGrid/slices_01–06.jpg`, `1125_WED_AM_EBF_Grid2/slices_13.jpg`)
  - all six product images (e.g. `classic-velvet-stockings-l.jpg`, `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-125-l.jpg`)
  - the three `HolidayBanners/H26_Bottom_*` banners
  - `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`

  Because the offer content is image-only, image-blocking clients and screen readers get nothing for the sale. The only alt text confirmed is on the logo (`alt="POTTERY BARN"`).
- **Non-HTTPS images:** three images use `http://`, which may be blocked or trigger mixed-content warnings.
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/...`
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="...maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, an accessibility regression.
- **Layout-table roles:** the outer layout tables (`<table width="100%" ...>` and the 20px spacer table) lack `role="presentation"`. The inner tables have it.
- **Stale asset:** one footer banner path is dated 2022 (`/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/`), which suggests reused template slices.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** not found (WARN). It may be dropped by the AgentMail relay, so absence at the sender is unconfirmed.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post:** not found, so RFC 8058 one-click support is unverified.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. This is a capture limitation, not a confirmed failure.
- **Footer content:** the footer is built from 18 image slices with no alt text (`Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`). If the unsubscribe link and postal address live only in those images, that is a CAN-SPAM and accessibility risk. It can't be confirmed from the truncated source, so check the full HTML.
- **Plain text:** 12,512 of 17,526 characters (71%) are URLs, mainly long tracking redirects. This is a minor spam-filter signal.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not verifiable:** every click goes through `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`, an opaque encoded redirect, and the probe skipped all 64. I can't confirm final destinations, UTM parameters or landing alignment with "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY".
- **Product images:** these are hosted on `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`, which shows the product-to-asset mapping but not link targets.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content slices (hero, product, banner and footer images). Use empty `alt=""` for spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Serve the three `http://` assets over HTTPS, and remove or replace the stale 2022 banner slice.
3. Populate `<title>`, drop `maximum-scale=1`, and add `role="presentation"` to the remaining layout tables.
4. Remove the stray PHP `header(...)` text from the `<style>` block.
5. Consolidate the three tracking pixels, and avoid putting the recipient's email in query strings. Use an opaque ID instead.
6. Re-run the audit against raw `.eml` headers, or through a relay that preserves them, to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
7. Follow the redirect chains with a tracking-aware probe to check final URLs, UTMs and landing pages.
8. Confirm the footer carries a live-text unsubscribe link and physical address.
