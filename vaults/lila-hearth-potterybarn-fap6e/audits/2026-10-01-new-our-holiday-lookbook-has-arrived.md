# New! Our Holiday Lookbook has arrived
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-01**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's holiday lookbook launch. It is a long editorial scroll of three styled rooms: Cottage Christmas, Evergreen Traditions and Classic Holiday. The hero is "THE HOLIDAY LOOKBOOK" with a "GET INSPIRED" button over a plaid-draped bedroom. The imagery is warm and moody, and it sells a mood well.
- It sells nothing concrete. I see no price, percent off, free-shipping hook or deadline anywhere in the visible render. Every CTA is a soft "Shop the Look" or "Shop Now." The "Recommended Just for You" grid and the Christmas Shop tile are products with no price attached. The Design Crew, app, Affirm and credit-card blocks pile onto an already very long email.
- Judgement: a pretty brand-builder that is weak at driving clicks. It is a 5, in line with the recent 6.0 sends but a notch lower because it has no offer and no urgency. Compared with the 9/30 "Big Daddy's Antiques" story, it has less news and less reason to act today.

## What's working

- The hero is on-brand, with a clear title and a "GET INSPIRED" button above the fold.
- The three themed rooms each have a named concept ("Cottage Christmas") and a one-line descriptor.
- The "Recommended Just for You" grid adds product-level entry points, such as the Cayman Platform Bed and the Lit Ghost Collection.
- The Christmas Shop and "SHOP ALL NEW ARRIVALS" CTAs give a clear path into the catalog.
- The seasonal timing is right for early October, and the subject says "New!"

## What's weak

- There is no offer or price anywhere in the visible render, so there is no reason to click now.
- The email is extremely long, and the lower sections (Design Services, app, Affirm, credit card) dilute the lookbook story.
- The "Shop the Look" links are small and sit on top of the photos, so they are easy to miss.
- The "Recommended Just for You" grid is generic: curtain rod, ghost, dining table and rug have no holiday coherence, and the products have no prices.
- Several CTAs compete: Get Inspired, Shop the Look ×3, Shop All New Arrivals, Shop Now, Book a Free Appointment, Download Now and Learn More.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add one concrete hook, such as a price, a free-shipping threshold, a member perk or a ship-by date for holiday delivery, and put it in the hero. This single change would lift the email most.
- 2. Cut the Affirm, app and credit-card blocks, or collapse them into a single slim line. Keep the lookbook and the shoppable products.
- 3. Make each room's CTA a real button with product-specific copy (for example "Shop Cottage Christmas") and add prices to the recommended grid.
- 4. Make the recommendations holiday-relevant instead of generic.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop the rooms, then order early for holiday delivery.`

# Technical Audit: Pottery Barn "New! Our Holiday Lookbook has arrived"

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a table-based, image-sliced email with ~57 opaque click-tracking links, ~55 images missing alt text, and several third-party tracking pixels. Two of those pixels (one over plain `http://`) put the recipient's email address in the URL. SPF/DKIM and List-Unsubscribe could not be verified, because the relay did not expose those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracking links not probed:** 57 links on click-redirect domains were skipped. Example: `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABB7InYi...`. Destination URLs and their HTTP status can't be confirmed from this data.
- **PII in pixel URLs:** the recipient address `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` appears in plain text in two pixel/URL parameters:
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=<email>` (an open-tracking pixel over unencrypted HTTP)
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=<email>` (loaded as an `<img>`)
- **Other pixels:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` (third-party ad-tech) and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/c159a8fa-...?mi_cid=...` (open pixel).
- **Cross-brand asset:** the spacer `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is served from the West Elm domain. It is harmless but creates a cross-domain dependency.
- **Stale asset path:** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` is a 2022 asset reused in a 2026 send.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 55 images are flagged. This covers all 16 body slices (`.../1001_THU_AM_HolidayLookbook/slices_01–16.jpg`) and 18 footer slices (`.../Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`). It also covers product images such as `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg` and `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`, and banners such as `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg`. With images blocked, the email will be largely blank and unreadable to screen readers. Only the nav logo carries alt text (`alt="POTTERY BARN"`).
- **Non-HTTPS images (3):**
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - the `svopn` pixel
  These may be blocked or trigger mixed-content warnings.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Zoom restricted:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, an accessibility issue.
- **Stray code in CSS:** PHP statements (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ...")`) are pasted inside the final `<style>` block. They are invalid CSS. They sit after the valid rules, so they likely have no effect.
- **Data tables:** the layout tables carry `role="presentation"`, but some, such as the `height="20"` spacer table, do not.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens are visible in the supplied source. The recipient email is resolved in pixel URLs (see section 2). The source is truncated, so the body copy could not be fully checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** neither header was found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a proven defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both, so the original message headers should be checked.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown.
- **Body unsubscribe link and postal address:** both are in the footer, which was truncated from the supplied source. They could not be verified.
- **Plain-text part:** 10,284 of 14,472 characters (71%) are URLs, a spam-filter signal. It is driven by the long tracking URLs.
- **Header values:** `From: "Pottery Barn" <PotteryBarn@e.potterybarn.com>` uses a branded subdomain, which is consistent.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
UTM parameters and landing-page alignment cannot be verified. Every link is wrapped in an opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=` redirect, and none of the 57 were resolved. The `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` tag also removes referrer data in the browser. Attribution will depend on the redirect's server-side parameters rather than visible UTMs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, and `alt=""` to spacers and pixels. Add background colors and live text for the key headline and CTA slices.
2. Move the `svopn`, `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and 2022 banner URLs to HTTPS.
3. Stop putting the recipient's plain-text email in pixel and image URLs. Use an opaque subscriber key instead.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag, fill in `<title>`, and delete the stray PHP lines from the `<style>` block.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` against the raw headers, plus SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `e.potterybarn.com`. Don't treat the relay's missing headers as a confirmed failure.
6. Resolve a sample of the tracking links to check destinations and UTM parameters. Also confirm that the footer unsubscribe link and postal address are present.
7. Replace the 2022 banner and the West Elm spacer with current Pottery Barn-hosted assets.
