# ⭐️ Amazing Labor Day Sale finds ⭐️
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-31**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's fourth "up to 70% off" Labor Day send in as many days, and it's the least differentiated of the bunch. The burgundy hero banner, the "100s of new deals added!" claim, and the 8-tile category grid are near-identical to the 08-31 "It's ON 🎉" send — same color, same layout, same generic sitewide framing. Below the fold it pivots into a "Recommended Just For You" product grid (bed, curtain rod, dining tables, swivel chair, rug) that reads as algorithmic filler rather than curated picks, then tacks on a Halloween Shop banner and Design Crew promo that have nothing to do with the Labor Day framing at all.
- Render is clean — no broken images or overlapping text — but the email has lost any sense of occasion. There's no product-level specificity in the hero, no urgency beyond "sale," and the mid-email swerve into Halloween dilutes whatever momentum the 70%-off banner built. Judgment: a functional but fatigued sale email that's cannibalizing its own prior sends this week.

## What's working

- Hero banner is legible at a glance: "labor day sale / Up to 70% off / 100s of new deals added!" with a clear SHOP NOW button.
- Three-image lifestyle collage under the hero gives some visual texture before the category grid.
- Category grid (Furniture, Sofas & Chairs, Bedding, Pillows & Decor, etc.) is a low-friction way to route different shoppers.
- No render bugs — images load, text is crisp, layout holds together.

## What's weak

- Near-identical creative to the 08-31 "It's ON 🎉" send (same burgundy banner, same "Up to 70%" framing, same grid pattern) — this is now the third or fourth repeat of this exact beat this week.
- "Recommended Just For You" grid feels generic/algorithmic — a platform bed, a curtain rod bracket, two dining tables, a chair, a rug — with no thread tying them to Labor Day or to each other.
- The Halloween Shop banner mid-email breaks the Labor Day narrative entirely; it's a jarring seasonal non-sequitur sandwiched in a sale email.
- No specific product or category is hero'd — it's all "sitewide," so there's nothing concrete pulling a specific shopper in.
- Subject line stars (⭐️) and "Amazing" read as generic filler compared to a concrete number or category callout.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill the repeat creative.** Four near-identical "up to 70%" burgundy-banner sends in one week trains subscribers to skim and archive. Rotate in a genuinely different hero (e.g., a single hero product/vignette) or consolidate to one strong send per week.
- 2. **Cut or relocate the Halloween Shop block.** It contradicts the Labor Day framing and dilutes the sale message — save it for its own send or a "coming next" footer mention only.
- 3. **Replace "Recommended Just For You" with an actual curated cluster** (e.g., "Top Bedroom Picks Under $500") so the grid has a reason to exist beyond filler.
- 4. **Add a real deadline** ("Sale ends Monday at midnight ET") to the hero — nothing here signals when this expires.

- **Subject Alt B:** `70% off furniture, bedding & more — today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns just added across furniture, bedding, and decor.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sale ends Monday — shop before it's gone.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard ESP-templated (Cheetah/Bronto-style MSO+VML) HTML build with heavy legacy code artifacts; automated QA passed 57% of checks, flagging missing image alt attributes, mixed-content image sources, absent unsubscribe/authentication headers, and PII exposure in open-tracking URLs.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 57 tracking/redirect links (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`, `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/...`) were present but skipped by the automated HTTP probe since they are wrapped redirects — final destinations are not verifiable from source alone.
- Open-tracking pixel served over **plain HTTP** with the recipient's email address embedded in the query string: `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`. Sending PII (email address) over unencrypted HTTP is a data-exposure issue, not just a deliverability one.
- Third-party ad-tracking pixel present: `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` (Amobee/Turn data-broker pixel).
- A second open pixel resolves recipient identity in the clear: `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 40+ `<img>` elements are missing `alt` text (all `slices_NN.jpg` grid/footer images, product images such as `farmhouse-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`, `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-1245-1-l.jpg`, `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`, `custom-capitola-hand-tufted-wool-rug-l.jpg`, plus the nav banner and footer slices). Since this template is entirely image-sliced (no live text), images-blocked or screen-reader users get no content at all.
- Two image sources use **http://** instead of https: `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`. Mail clients that enforce HTTPS-only image loading (e.g., Outlook, some Gmail configs) may block these, leaving blank slices.
- The `<style>` block in `<head>` contains malformed, non-functional PHP-style code: `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0"); ...` sitting inside a CSS `<style>` tag. This is dead/broken code leaked from a server-side template into the client HTML — it has no effect in an email client but indicates a build/templating error worth flagging to the ESP.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- Merge tokens resolved correctly for the sample recipient (`lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` appears consistently in both tracking pixel URLs), so no broken/unresolved token syntax (e.g. stray `{{...}}`) was found.
- However, resolving the email address directly into unauthenticated, cleartext (and in one case HTTP) pixel URLs is a personalization **implementation** risk — it exposes recipient PII to any intermediary or log capturing that URL. See Section 2.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not found in captured headers.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058, one-click unsubscribe) header not found.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC outcome) not found.
- Caveat: per QA notes, these may reflect the AgentMail relay not preserving/forwarding original headers rather than the sender omitting them — cannot confirm sender-side absence from this data alone. Recommend re-checking against raw source headers at the receiving MTA.
- CAN-SPAM footer (physical address, unsubscribe link text) is not visible in the truncated HTML provided — unable to confirm compliance; flag for follow-up with full source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- All outbound links are wrapped in opaque redirect tokens (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABB7...`), so UTM parameters and final landing-page URLs cannot be verified from the HTML alone.
- One direct (non-wrapped) link to `www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=...` was found — no UTM parameters present on this one, so it would land untracked in analytics if clicked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content-bearing images, particularly the numbered `slices_NN.jpg` grid tiles and product shots — this template has zero fallback text otherwise.
2. Serve `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `slices_02.jpg` over HTTPS to avoid client-side blocking.
3. Route open-tracking pixels (`svopn.potterybarn.com`, `coreemail.html`) over HTTPS and avoid placing raw recipient email addresses in unauthenticated query strings.
4. Remove the stray PHP `header()` calls from the `<style>` block in `<head>` — dead code suggesting a templating/build error.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent at the MTA (not just dropped by the relay), and verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment via raw headers.
6. Unwrap sample redirect links to confirm UTM parameters and landing-page alignment; add UTM tracking to the untracked `coreemail.html` link if analytics attribution is desired.
