# FREE (yes, free!) design help
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-15**
## Executive summary

- This is a services email dressed as a promo. The subject promises "FREE (yes, free!) design help." The hero delivers it plainly: "RSVP for Your Free Design Services," with a what/when/where block and a green "BOOK A FREE APPOINTMENT" button. The offer is easy to grasp, and the button is the first thing the eye lands on.
- It's also a near-repeat. On 10-13 Pottery Barn sent "RSVP for FREE design services," with the same Design Crew pitch. A subscriber sees the same idea two days later, and the repeat makes the "yes, free!" line read as trying too hard. Below the hero, the email turns into a long inspiration catalog (Shades of Green, High Contrast Neutrals, Rosewood), and then a "Recommended Just For You" grid, an app ad, a rewards card and an Affirm pitch. The booking CTA gets diluted by the time you scroll to the bottom.
- Overall it's competent and on-brand, but it adds little new. It's a 6, below the Grinch and Ashley Stark collab sends from 10-14, which were fresher.

## What's working

- The hero is clear. A headline, a what/when/where list and one dark-green button give a single obvious action above the fold.
- Pottery Barn's usual editorial photography is warm and seasonal (sage, olive, rosewood), and it fits the fall timing.
- The "Book a free appointment" CTA repeats mid-email, after the three style sections.
- The in-store class module ("Design a Guest-Ready Bedroom," Sunday, Oct 25th, 10AM, RSVP TODAY) gives a concrete date.
- The email renders cleanly. I see no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The subject's "FREE (yes, free!)" has all-caps and exclamation spam cues and reads as a bit desperate for a premium brand.
- It repeats the 10-13 "RSVP for FREE design services" send almost verbatim, so cadence suffers.
- "Spots go fast!" and "You choose a time" give no real deadline or scarcity. The only date is the Oct 25 class, which sits far down the email.
- The style sections ("Get the Look") and the "Recommended Just For You" grid pull attention from the booking goal. The product grid has no prices or reviews.
- The footer stacks rewards, Affirm, app and credit card pitches, plus dense fine print, on a services email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the lower half. End the email after the hero, one style row and the class RSVP, so the appointment CTA is the whole story.
- 2. Add real urgency and proof: a booking deadline, "X slots left this week," or a one-line testimonial about a Design Crew result.
- 3. Move the Oct 25 class date into the hero as a second option, such as "Or join our free class."
- 4. Drop the product grid and the Affirm and card blocks from this send. They don't serve a services CTA.
- 5. Differentiate from the 10-13 send by showing a before/after room or naming the designer.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a sliced-image layout built from nested tables. The main problems are unusable alt text on linked images, recipient email addresses in tracking-pixel URLs, malformed head and markup, and responsive CSS that matches nothing in the visible source. Compliance and authentication could not be verified because the headers were not captured and the source is truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque tracking links:** 46 `click.e.potterybarn.com/?qs=…` links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destinations and redirect health are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Recipient email in pixel URLs:** two tracking images put the recipient's address in the query string in plaintext:
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `https://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`

  The address is exposed to any proxy, CDN or log that sees these URLs.
- **Third-party ad pixels:** `secure.leadback.advertising.com/adcedge/lb?...` and `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/...` are retargeting pixels loaded from the email.
- **Dead code with a live link:** a commented-out credit-card banner still ships in the HTML, including a `potterybarn.com/pages/credit-card/?...` URL. It is harmless but adds bytes.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a West Elm-hosted spacer. If that host removes it, the layout can shift.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Linked images with empty alt:** the hero slices (`slices_01.jpg` to `slices_07.jpg`) are wrapped in `<a href="https://click.e.potterybarn.com/...">` with `alt=""`. These links have no accessible name, and with images blocked the content is blank. The footer slices (`Email-Footer/slices_01.jpg` to `slices_18.jpg`) are flagged for missing alt for the same reason. The product images (`banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`, `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`, `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`, etc.) have no alt at all.
- **HTTP images:** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` may be blocked or trigger mixed-content warnings.
- **Responsive CSS matches nothing visible:** the media query targets `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]`. The visible markup has neither class. The slice tables and images use fixed `width="650"`, so they won't scale below 650px.
- **Malformed head:** the stray PHP statements `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ...")` sit inside the second `<style>` block as literal text. The browser treats them as invalid CSS and may drop the rule that follows (`a[x-apple-data-detectors]`).
- **Comment before the DOCTYPE:** `<!-- begin dynamic ampScript slot --> <!-- end … -->` appears before `<!DOCTYPE>`. That can push some clients into quirks mode.
- **Other markup problems:**
  - The XHTML 1.0 Strict doctype is used with deprecated `bgcolor`, `align`, `border` and `<center>`.
  - There is no `<title>` and no `lang` on `<html>`.
  - The data tables lack `role="presentation"`.
  - Raw `&` appears in the preheader text and in the Google Fonts URL.
  - The Montserrat font is loaded but never referenced in the visible CSS.
- **Flagged alt warnings that are not real defects:** the tracking pixels (`lb`, `1`, `svopn…`, `coreemail.html`) and `spacer_0.gif` are decorative. They should have `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"`, but this is low priority.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[%…%]`) were seen, and the recipient address resolved in the pixel URLs. The ampScript slot at the top rendered empty.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unverified, likely a capture artifact:** the List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) and Authentication-Results headers were not found. The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so don't treat it as a sender defect. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- **Not verifiable from the truncated source:** the body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address. Because the footer is 18 image slices with no alt text, a text-based unsubscribe link may be missing. Confirm this against the full HTML.
- **Visible in the source:** the From domain `e.potterybarn.com` matches the click-tracking domain `click.e.potterybarn.com`, which is good alignment.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not assessable:** all 46 clicks go through opaque `qs=` redirects, so destination URLs and UTM parameters can't be confirmed. The only plain-text campaign parameters (`cm_ite`, `marketingChannelCode`) are in the commented-out block, which is dead code.
- The campaign path `/pb/2026/US/1015_THU_AM2_DC_SBS/` suggests a Thursday 10/15 AM send, which matches today's date. I did not verify the landing pages.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every linked hero, product and footer image. Keep `alt=""` only for true pixels and spacers.
2. Stop putting the recipient email in plain-text pixel URLs. Use a hashed or opaque ID.
3. Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP lines from the `<style>` block.
4. Move the ampScript comments after the DOCTYPE, or strip them at render time.
5. Serve the two `http://` images over HTTPS.
6. Add `class="wrapper"` and `class="photo"` to the actual tables and images, or rewrite the media query so slices go fluid on mobile.
7. Add `<title>`, `lang="en"` and `role="presentation"`, and escape `&` as `&amp;`.
8. Delete the commented-out credit-card block.
9. Re-run the audit with the full raw message source to verify List-Unsubscribe, authentication headers, the unsubscribe link and postal address, and the resolved destination URLs and UTMs.
