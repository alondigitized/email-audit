# ☀️ SUMMER WAREHOUSE SALE ☀️
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-07**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's latest Summer Warehouse Sale email — arriving mid-campaign after at least five consecutive warehouse sale sends since June 5. The body is competently executed: a clean dark-navy hero with "Up to 60% off" clearly stated, a personalized product grid of six SKUs, and an outdoor lifestyle image anchoring the primary SHOP NOW button. The structural problem isn't the template — it's the subject. "☀️ SUMMER WAREHOUSE SALE ☀️" in all-caps with emoji bookends looks like a blast trigger firing on a schedule, not a targeted send. Subscribers who opened "Our Summer Warehouse Sale is ON" on June 5, "Dive into our Summer Warehouse Sale" on June 6, and "Discover deals UP TO 60% OFF" also on June 6 are now looking at a fourth variation of the same creative in 48 hours.
- The "Recommended Just For You" product grid is the standout element — six specific SKUs (Platform Bed, Extendable Dining Table, quilts, towels) give the email real browsability. But the design services pitch, app download block, and Affirm financing section below the fold compete with the core conversion goal and have no place in a hard-promo send.
- The biggest conversion leak: no deadline anywhere in the email. A sale without a stated end date removes all reason to act today over tomorrow.

## What's working

- Hero leads immediately with the offer — dark navy background, "Summer Warehouse Sale / Up to 60% off" is the first thing the eye hits, no hunting required
- Category navigation inside the hero (FURNITURE, OUTDOOR, SOFAS & CHAIRS, BEDDING, DECOR, RUGS, etc.) is genuinely useful for browse-intent readers
- "Recommended Just For You" grid adds specific, named SKUs — this is the most actionable block in the email
- "Earn 10% Back in Rewards" surfaces a loyalty incentive for members, adding a secondary conversion layer
- Clean render — no broken images, no overlapping text, no layout issues visible

## What's weak

- ALL CAPS subject line with sun emojis on both sides reads as a promotional blast and triggers every inbox spam-signal heuristic
- No sale deadline anywhere in the email body — "up to 60% off" without an end date creates zero urgency to click now
- This is the fourth or fifth warehouse-sale-themed send in three days; the creative has burned through subscriber attention
- Design services (BOOK NOW), app download, and Affirm payment blocks dilute focus and belong in editorial or service-oriented emails, not a hard promo
- The SHOP NOW CTA on the hero lifestyle image links to a general destination — no category or product anchoring

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a deadline.** "Sale ends Sunday" or "48 hours left" is the single highest-impact change this email could make. No other fix matters as much as giving readers a reason to act today.
- 2. **Rewrite the subject line.** Drop the ALL CAPS and emoji brackets; add the percentage and a deadline. The brand has room to be specific here — they haven't used a deadline-anchored subject once this campaign.
- 3. **Cut the non-sale modules.** Design services, app install, and Affirm are content diversions. One email, one conversion goal.
- 4. **Anchor the hero CTA to a category.** "Recommended Just For You" products skew toward bedroom and dining — the SHOP NOW button should match ("Shop Bedroom Sale" or "Shop Outdoor") rather than pointing to a generic sale page.
- 5. **Rotate the visual.** At least three consecutive emails in this campaign have used the same warm-tone outdoor lifestyle photography. A product-first hero — the quilt, the platform bed — would break pattern and signal a fresh send.
- **Subject Alt A:** `60% off ends Sunday — your warehouse sale picks`
- **Subject Alt B:** `3 days left: up to 60% off furniture, bedding & more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your recommended pieces from the sale — before they sell out.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sale ends Sunday. Six picks we think you'll want first.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Pottery Barn "Summer Warehouse Sale" (2026-06-07)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Two hard-broken links (HTTP 403) block a key CTA and a cross-brand image asset; server-side PHP code was incorrectly emitted into a `<style>` block indicating a template rendering failure. Compliance headers are absent or unverifiable, and 40+ images carry no alt text.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**FAIL — CTA link returns 403**
The credit-card/loyalty banner link is broken at the point of click resolution:
```
https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/c/da01b407-b21c-4618-a71b-f83bef0dd682
  ?brandCode=POTTERYBARNCB&marketingChannelCode=APPLYANDBUY
  &external_id=PB_Email_MemberBanner&cm_ite=PB_header_nonmember
```
This is linked from the `KeyCC_Banner_Certs_Amp_Updates_v2` module. Any subscriber clicking the credit card/loyalty CTA gets a 403.

**FAIL — Wrong-brand image asset (West Elm spacer, 403)**
```
http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif
```
A West Elm CDN asset was referenced in a Pottery Barn email. The request 403s and the image is also over plain HTTP (see §3).

**INFO — 25 click-tracking links not probed**
All `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirect URLs were skipped. UTM passthrough and destination health cannot be confirmed from this audit alone.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**PHP server-side code leaked into `<style>` block**
The following lines appear verbatim inside a `<style>` tag — they are PHP `header()` calls that were never executed server-side and instead rendered as CSS text:
```css
header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
header("Cache-Control: post-check=0, pre-check=0", false);
header("Pragma: no-cache");
```
This is a template rendering failure. These lines are inert in the rendered email but indicate a broken build/compilation step.

**3 images served over HTTP (mixed-content)**
Gmail, Outlook.com, and Apple Mail proxy or block non-HTTPS image sources. Affected assets:
- `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` (wrong brand, 403)
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
- `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&...` (open-tracking pixel)

**40+ images missing `alt` text**
Every content slice (`slices_01.jpg` through `slices_12.jpg`), all footer slices (`Email-Footer/slices_01.jpg` through `slices_18.jpg`), all product images (`cayman-platform-bed-l.jpg`, `hydrocotton-quick-dry-towel-l.jpg`, etc.), and both tracking pixels have empty or absent `alt` attributes. Subscribers with images disabled see a fully blank email.

**Empty `<title>` tag**
```html
<title></title>
```
Minor, but some clients surface the title in tab/notification contexts.

**`no-referrer` meta tag suppresses analytics referrer**
```html
<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer" />
```
Outbound clicks from this email will not pass a `Referer` header, stripping channel attribution from server-side analytics that depend on it (separate from click-tracking URLs).

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `[%first_name%]`) are visible in the truncated source. The audit persona email `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is correctly reflected in tracking pixel URLs:
```
sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app
```
Token substitution appears functional for at least the email-address field.

---

### 5. Compliance

**List-Unsubscribe header absent**
The `List-Unsubscribe` header was not detected. This is required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender policies (enforced since 2024) for senders above 5,000 msgs/day. Absence increases spam folder placement risk.

**List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) absent**
One-click unsubscribe is not supported. This is a hard requirement under the same Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender rules.

**Authentication-Results not captured**
SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown due to AgentMail relay not forwarding the header. Cannot confirm authentication posture from this audit. Should be verified at the MTA level.

**CAN-SPAM physical address** — present in footer (not shown in truncated source but implied by standard footer module). Confirm it resolves to a deliverable postal address.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

The 25 tracked CTAs all pass through `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirects, which prevents confirming UTM parameter presence or landing-page alignment without following the redirect chain. The `no-referrer` meta (§3) means server-side referrer-based attribution will be absent even if UTMs are appended.

The broken `clickcp.potterybarn.com` credit card CTA (§2) breaks the loyalty acquisition flow entirely — clicks go nowhere.
