# Selling fast: Open Box Outlet Deals
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-07**
## Executive summary

- This send has a clear hero and almost no substance behind it. "Selling Fast / Open Box Outlet Deals" on an oxblood block with a "SHOP NOW" button tells you the category and creates some urgency. It never says how much you save. "Save big with on-sale items that have been opened and returned" is vague, and no percentage or price appears anywhere in the email.
- The body then loses focus. A Free Design Services banner comes right after the hero, and it is the same program the brand pushed on 11/06 ("Free design appointments! Spots go fast"). Below it sits a six-item "Recommended Just For You" grid (stockings, dining table, curtain rod, rug, quilt) with no prices, no sale callouts, and nothing marking any item as open-box. The Christmas Shop tile is a rerun of the 11/05 launch. The subscriber ends up with three or four competing messages and no reason to click any one of them.
- Verdict: a decent outlet concept with weak execution. The subject has real urgency, but the body doesn't deliver on it. Showing real open-box items with their markdown prices would fix most of the problem.

## What's working

- The hero is clean and readable: a short "SELLING FAST" eyebrow, a large serif headline, and a single "SHOP NOW" button on a bold red block.
- Outlet and open-box is a distinct angle. It stands apart from the pillow, quilt and Christmas Shop sends earlier in the week.
- The "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks" bar at the top is useful, and it reads as honest about delivery.
- The product grid is tidy, with consistent photography and clear labels.
- The footer carries the rewards card and Affirm financing, so there are real benefits for members.

## What's weak

- No discount, price, or percentage appears anywhere, so the "Save big" promise is unsupported.
- The product grid is generic and "recommended," with no sale prices and no open-box marking. It reads as a standard catalog block, not outlet deals.
- Free Design Services repeats the 11/06 send and pulls attention away from the outlet offer.
- The Christmas Shop tile repeats the 11/05 launch creative, which adds to the sense of recycled content.
- The legal and footer text is long and dense, and the outlet terms are buried in it. "Sales final" appears only in the fine print. That conflicts with the "Offer feels honest" signal.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the generic grid with 4–6 real open-box items. Show the original price crossed out, the outlet price, and a "Only 2 left" cue.
- 2. Put a concrete number in the hero, such as "Up to 60% off open-box finds".
- 3. Cut the Design Services banner and the Christmas Shop tile from this send, or move them below the outlet content.
- 4. State the key terms (final sale, limited quantities) near the hero in plain language, so they aren't hidden in the footer.
- 5. Make the CTA more specific, for example "SHOP OUTLET DEALS".

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Returned, inspected, and marked down. Sofas, rugs and more, final sale.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard ESP-built (Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style) HTML template with fully wrapped tracking links. The main confirmed problems are widespread missing `alt` attributes, cleartext HTTP assets, the recipient's email address exposed in pixel URLs, and leaked server-side code in a `<style>` block. Header-level compliance and authentication checks could not be verified. QA: 57% pass rate, 0 errors, 3 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **43 links could not be probed.** They all use click-redirect domains, e.g. `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...`. Destinations, redirect chains and status codes are unverified.
- **Mixed-protocol tracking pixel.** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` is loaded over plain HTTP, so some clients may block it and open tracking may be undercounted.
- **Third-party pixel.** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` is an ad-tech pixel with no `alt`.
- **Cross-brand asset dependency.** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is served from a West Elm host. If that asset is removed, the layout spacing breaks.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on about 35 images.** This includes content images:

  - `slices_01.jpg` (Open Box Deals)

- **No `lang` attribute.** `<html xmlns=...>` has no `lang`.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="..., maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
