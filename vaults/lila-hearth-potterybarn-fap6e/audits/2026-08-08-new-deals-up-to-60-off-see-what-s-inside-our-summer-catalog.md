# New deals UP TO 60% OFF + See what's inside our summer catalog
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-08**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's "everything, all at once" catalog send, and it's the least focused of the week — a "Warehouse Sale up to 60% off" banner leads into a Summer Catalog hero, then New Arrivals, In-Store Design Classes, a personalized rug/furniture recommendation grid, another "Discover New Arrivals" banner, and then — in August — a full Halloween Shop promotion. The subject line promises "New deals UP TO 60% OFF" but the actual hero content is the catalog, not a sale; the discount only shows up as a thin top banner, which is a mismatch that will frustrate anyone who opens for the deal.
- Compared to the tighter category sends this week — "Must-see dining chairs" (8/10) or "Coffee table styling 101" (8/10) — this one reads like five emails stapled together. There's a personalized recommendation block (a genuine strength, and rare for this brand this week) but it's buried below three other big content sections, and the Halloween insert this early in August feels jarring against a "Summer Catalog" headline just above it.
- Judgement: a scattered, kitchen-sink send that will underperform on click-through despite decent individual pieces — the subject over-promises on the deal, and the layout buries the one section (personalized recs) that could have driven a click.

## What's working

- Top banner clearly states "The Warehouse Sale Up to 60% off" with a visible link, giving at least one honest offer signal.
- "Recommended Just For You" section shows six real, distinct SKUs with names and (partial) pricing context — genuine personalization, uncommon in this week's other sends.
- Clean section separation with consistent gray dividers and bold sans-serif headers keeps a very long email visually organized despite its length.
- "New Arrivals" category grid (Pillows & Decor, Outdoor, Bedding, etc.) is legible and gives clear, distinct entry points.
- No visible render bugs — no overlapping text, broken images, or placeholder content anywhere in the render.

## What's weak

- Subject promises "New deals UP TO 60% OFF" but the hero is "The Summer Catalog" — the discount is reduced to a one-line top banner, a bait-and-switch feel.
- The Halloween Shop section ("Enter If You Dare") appears directly under a Summer Catalog and New Arrivals framing — seasonally incoherent for an August 8 send.
- Six content blocks (Warehouse Sale, Summer Catalog, New Arrivals, Design Classes, Recommended For You, Discover New Arrivals, Halloween Shop) with no single dominant CTA — visual hierarchy never resolves to one offer.
- The "Discover New Arrivals" banner duplicates the earlier "New Arrivals" grid almost verbatim, wasting scroll real estate that could reinforce the sale instead.
- Pricing/percentage-off specifics are never shown next to actual products — the 60% claim is disconnected from anything visually tied to it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the sale, not the catalog: put "Up to 60% Off" as the hero with 2-3 actual discounted products and prices, then push the Summer Catalog to a secondary card — right now the subject's best hook is minimized to a banner.
- 2. Cut either the "New Arrivals" grid or the "Discover New Arrivals" banner — they're redundant and this email is already too long.
- 3. Move "Recommended Just For You" up near the top, right after the sale hero — it's the strongest personalization signal in the email and it's currently buried past four other sections.
- 4. Pull the Halloween Shop into its own dedicated send — mixing "Summer Catalog" and "Enter If You Dare" Halloween in the same email undercuts both messages.
- 5. Add visible % or $ savings on at least the top 2-3 hero products so the subject's "60% OFF" claim has something concrete to point to.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 60% off is here — plus the new Summer Catalog`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Warehouse Sale picks, up to 60% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Save on rugs, bedding & more before the sale ends`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Handpicked for you, all on sale now`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Standard EDM-platform (potterybarn.com via Salesforce/Cheetah-style edm.potterybarn.com + click.e.potterybarn.com tracking domains) marketing email; automated QA shows no critical failures but flags missing alt text across nearly all images, several plain-HTTP asset/tracking URLs, and unverifiable compliance headers.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 57 tracking/click-redirect links detected (click.e.potterybarn.com, clickcp.potterybarn.com) — skipped by automated HTTP probe, so destination validity is unconfirmed; manual click-through testing recommended.
- Open/tracking pixels present: `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` served over plain HTTP (non-HTTPS), and `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/...` third-party pixel.
- No issues found with link structure itself (standard redirect-domain pattern for ESP click tracking).

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 40+ `<img>` elements missing `alt` attributes (banners, product slices, footer legal/social slices, tracking pixels) — see QA list; screen readers and blocked-image fallback will show nothing.
- Two images served over `http://` instead of `https://`: `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` — mixed content may be blocked or flagged by mail clients enforcing TLS.
- `<style>` block contains stray PHP-style `header(...)` calls (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); ...`) embedded as literal text inside a CSS `<style>` tag — inert/dead code, harmless to rendering but indicates a templating/build artifact that leaked into the HTML source.
- Legacy DOCTYPE (XHTML 1.0 Transitional) and MSO/Outlook conditional comments present — standard for cross-client email compatibility, no issues found.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- Recipient email is embedded in plaintext in multiple tracking/unsubscribe URLs (e.g., `coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`, `sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`) — functional merge substitution executed correctly (no unresolved `{{token}}` or `%%FIELD%%` placeholders observed in the truncated source).
- No issues found with broken/unresolved merge tags in the visible source.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe support at the header level (may be relay-stripped rather than absent at origin; caveat per QA note).
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not detected — same caveat.
- `Authentication-Results` header not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from available data.
- In-body unsubscribe/footer link (`coreemail.html`) is present, satisfying CAN-SPAM's requirement for a visible opt-out mechanism regardless of header-level gaps.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Header/logo link routes through `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirect wrapper rather than a direct potterybarn.com URL with visible UTM parameters — actual landing destination and UTM tagging can't be confirmed since tracking links were skipped by the probe.
- Product-image `src` URLs point directly to `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...` (not the redirect domain), consistent with standard PB image-hosting; no mismatch found there.
- Unable to fully verify UTM parameter consistency across the 57 skipped tracking links — flagged as unverified rather than confirmed broken.

7. Recommendations
- Add descriptive (or explicitly empty `alt=""` for purely decorative) alt text to all `<img>` tags, especially the product-slice and legal-footer images, to fix accessibility/QA fails.
- Migrate the three `http://` asset/pixel URLs to `https://` to avoid mixed-content blocking in strict mail clients.
- Remove the dead `header(...)` PHP-style lines from the `<style>` block — likely a leaked build/template artifact.
- Confirm at the ESP/send level that `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent (not just stripped by the relay) to ensure RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe compliance.
- Manually spot-check a sample of the 57 tracking-redirect links to confirm correct destinations and UTM tagging, since automated probing was skipped for this domain class.
