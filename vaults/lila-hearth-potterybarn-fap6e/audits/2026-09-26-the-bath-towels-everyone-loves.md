# The bath towels everyone loves
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-26**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's most straightforward hero-plus-catalog-crawl in the run: "Our Most-Loved Towels," a starting price ($24.50), monogram detail, and "Towels Ship Free" all stacked in the first fold. It's the cleanest towel-specific send I've logged — four named lines (Cloud, Dream, Hydrocotton, Addison Striped), each with a one-line material callout and its own product shot, which actually helps a shopper self-sort by fiber/feel rather than just browsing generic white towels.
- Where it falls apart is scope creep: after the towel block it pivots into vanities, hardware, bath mats, bath accessories, then a full "Recommended Just For You" grid (curtain rods, a bed, a wool rug, a dining table), then the Halloween Shop, then Design Crew, then the app pitch, then Affirm/rewards. By the time a reader scrolls past "RECOMMENDED JUST FOR YOU" the email has stopped being about towels entirely — it's a generic PB catalog with a towel hero bolted on, which dilutes what was actually a decent, differentiated opening.
- Verdict: strong hero, bloated body. If this were trimmed to the towel section plus a "shop the rest of your bath" add-on, it'd be one of the better sends in this history; as sent it's a middling 5.5–6.

## What's working

- Clear, specific hero offer: "Towels Ship Free" + "Starting at $24.50" — both visible without much scrolling.
- Product differentiation is genuinely useful: Cloud (cotton terry), Dream (silk/cotton blend), Hydrocotton (ultra-absorbent, 15+ colors), Addison Striped (striped terry) — each gets its own line of positioning, not just a name.
- "Shop All Bath Towels" and per-line "Shop Now" links give both a broad and a narrow path to purchase.
- Clean, consistent product photography — neutral tones, well-lit, no render bugs in the towel section.

## What's weak

- Severe scope creep: towels, vanities, hardware, mats, accessories, curtain rods, a bed, a rug, a dining table, Halloween, Design Crew, and the app pitch all in one email — no single CTA dominates by the end.
- "Recommended Just For You" claims personalization but shows unrelated big-ticket furniture (bed, dining table) with zero connection to a towel-intent open.
- No urgency or time-bound element anywhere — "Ships Free" is a standing benefit, not a deadline, so nothing pushes same-day action.
- The NEW badges on Hydrocotton and Addison Striped aren't explained — unclear if "new colorway" or "new to lineup."

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after "Shop All Bath Towels" plus one small "complete your bath" module (mats/accessories/hardware) — drop curtain rods/bed/rug/dining table entirely; those belong in a separate furniture send, not a towel email.
- 2. Turn "Towels Ship Free" into a bounded promo if possible (e.g., "Ships Free This Week") to give a reason to act today instead of bookmarking.
- 3. Add a one-line differentiator under each towel name that ties to a use-case ("Dream — hotel-soft, best for guest baths") rather than just fiber content, to speed decision-making.
- 4. Move Halloween Shop and Design Crew to a separate seasonal/services email — they compete with the towel CTA for attention.
