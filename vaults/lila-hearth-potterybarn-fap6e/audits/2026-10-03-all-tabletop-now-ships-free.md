# All tabletop now ships free!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-03**
## Executive summary

- This is a clear free-shipping send with a good opening and a diluted body. The hero, "Table Essentials Ship Free," is a moody shot of white dinnerware with SHOP DINNERWARE and SHOP SERVEWARE buttons. It matches the subject and lands the offer in the first screen. The "everything you need for holiday hosting, with shipping on us" line ties it to the season.
- After the hero, the "tabletop" promise drifts. The grid starts with table linens and flatware, which are tabletop. It then moves to throws, bedding, towels and pillows, which are not. The headline offer stops being specific. Below that sit a "Recommended Just For You" block, a Christmas Shop banner, Design Crew, the app, the credit card and Affirm. That is seven or so messages after the one the subject sold. The dense fine print at the bottom also hides the free-shipping conditions.
- It's also the third free-shipping or sale beat in two days. "Ships FREE: Your new favorite throw" (Oct 2, 7/10) used the same hook, and "Your next great find is ON SALE" (Oct 2, 6/10) used the same generic energy. This one is better than the sale blast because it names a category. I'd call it a solid 7: strong top, weak middle, no deadline.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer, "Ship Free," in the first screen and matches the subject.
- The two category buttons, Dinnerware and Serveware, are specific, and the hero photo is appetizing.
- "Everything you need for holiday hosting" ties the offer to the season.
- The table linens tile and the "Shop All Free Ship" button give a clear second path.
- Nothing is visibly broken. Images load, and the layout is clean and on-brand.

## What's weak

- Throws, bedding, towels and pillows aren't tabletop, so the "all tabletop" promise gets muddy.
- There is no deadline and no visible minimum or conditions. "Now" is weak urgency, and the terms are buried in the fine print.
- The lower half has too many competing blocks: recommendations, the Christmas Shop, Design Crew, the app, the credit card and Affirm. The free-shipping story gets lost.
- Every tile links to a category, not a product. Nothing shows a price, a set size or a review.
- The cadence is heavy. This is the third promo in two days and the second free-shipping hook (see the Oct 2 throw send).

## Recommendations

- 1. Keep the body on tabletop. Show dinnerware, serveware, flatware, glassware and linens, and cut the throws, bedding and towels block. If those categories ship free too, say "Free shipping on tabletop, bedding & more" in the subject and hero.
- 2. Add a real end date and state the terms above the fold, for example "Free shipping through Sunday, no minimum."
- 3. Feature two or three hero products with prices or set sizes, such as a 12-piece dinnerware set.
- 4. Cut the footer promos (app, card, Affirm) to one, and move "Recommended Just For You" below the offer or drop it from this send.
- 5. Space out free-shipping sends so the offer doesn't feel routine.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, image-sliced build with a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style click-redirect setup. Confirmed issues are widespread missing alt text, `http://` image sources (one carrying the recipient's plaintext address), stray server-side code in the CSS, and a URL-heavy plain-text part. Header-level compliance and authentication could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque redirects:** 54 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe. They are `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` redirects, so destinations and redirect health are unverified. The logo link (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABB7InYi...`) is one example.
- **Plain-text part:** 9,738 of 13,889 characters (~70%) are URLs, which is a spam-filter risk.
- **Tracking pixels over HTTP:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` is an open pixel on plain HTTP and may be blocked or flagged as mixed content. `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` is a third-party pixel and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/7afcdacc-...` is another open pixel. All three lack alt.
- **Cross-brand dependency:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a West Elm-hosted asset in a Pottery Barn send. If that path changes, the layout could break.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 50 images have none. The hero and body slices (`1003_SAT_AM_FreeShipDinnerware/slices_01–11.jpg`) have none, and neither do the product images (`cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`, `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`, `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`, etc.). The same goes for the footer slices (`Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`). The free-shipping offer appears to live entirely in images, so with images blocked the offer is invisible to screen readers and to clients that block images. The 1×1 tracking pixels should carry `alt=""`.
- **Insecure image sources:** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` may be blocked by clients.
- **Stray code in CSS:** the second `<style>` block contains PHP: `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store, ...");`. It is invalid CSS that is not executed. It looks like a copy-paste leak and can corrupt the CSS rule that follows it in some parsers.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>`.
- **Viewport zoom:** `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta disables pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Attribute selectors:** `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` only match when the class attribute is exactly that value. These rules break if other classes are added.
- **Layout tables:** the logo and banner tables have `role="presentation"`, but the spacer tables, such as `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><tr><td height="20">`, do not.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens were seen in the visible source.
- **PII in URLs:** the recipient's address appears in plaintext as a query parameter in `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`. It is also in `sv_sveme=` on the **HTTP** pixel, so it is sent unencrypted. This is a privacy and security exposure.
- `coreemail.html` is loaded as an `<img>` source but is an HTML page, not an image. This looks like a tracking or personalization hack and has no alt.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. QA notes this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay, so it is unconfirmed. If real, it would conflict with Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a limitation of the relay, not confirmed sender misconfiguration.
- The visible body unsubscribe link and the physical address were not confirmed. The source is truncated before the footer, so I can't verify either.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** unverifiable. Every click goes through the `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` redirect, with tracking encoded in the `qs` blob.
- **Landing-page alignment:** unverifiable for the same reason, because redirects were not followed. The subject says "All tabletop now ships free!" and the hero asset is named `1003_SAT_AM_FreeShipDinnerware`. They are consistent, but the destination is unconfirmed.
- **Stale assets:** a 2022-era banner is still in use (`/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/`).

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add meaningful `alt` to hero, product and footer images, including the free-shipping offer text, and `alt=""` on spacers and pixels.
2. Move `svopn.potterybarn.com` and the two `http://edm...` assets to HTTPS.
3. Stop putting the plaintext email in URL query strings, or use a hashed or opaque identifier.
4. Remove the PHP `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block, fill in `<title>`, and drop `maximum-scale=1`.
5. Reduce URL density in the plain-text part.
6. Check the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the authentication results outside the relay, and confirm the footer unsubscribe link and address.
7. Follow the redirects in a browser to verify destinations and UTM parameters.
