# Artfully stitched quilts starting at $169
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-29**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises "Artfully stitched quilts starting at $169," and the body delivers a clean, on-theme quilt hero. It's the most product-specific Pottery Barn send in this week's run. But it's still wrapped in the same oxblood "Extra 20% off Select Styles / Plus savings up to 70% off" banner as the Oct 26–29 sends. The $169 price point is never shown in the render, so the subject's headline number has no visible payoff.
- The "Beautifully Crafted Quilts" hero with a "Quilts ship free" button is a good offer. The six-quilt grid (Cozy Cloud, Velvet Scalloped Stewart Plaid, Heritage Medallion, Classic Patchwork, Corinna, Winter Spruce) is tidy and shoppable. The lower half then drifts into a generic "Recommended just for you" block, a Christmas Shop banner, Design Crew, app, credit card and Affirm modules. Those dilute the quilt story.
- Verdict: a decent, focused subject and a competent body, held back by banner fatigue and by no visible prices on the quilts. A solid 6.

## What's working

- The subject names a specific category and price anchor, which is more concrete than the "Extra 20% off" subjects earlier this week.
- The "Beautifully Crafted Quilts" hero is on-season, and the "Quilts ship free" button copy is a clear incentive.
- The six-quilt grid has consistent photography and clear product names, so it's easy to scan.
- "Shop All Bedding" gives a clear next step after the grid.
- The Christmas Shop and Design Crew modules reflect the current season and keep the brand voice consistent.

## What's weak

- The $169 starting price from the subject doesn't appear on any quilt tile, so the promise is never confirmed.
- The oxblood "Extra 20% off" banner sits on top again, competing with the quilt hero and repeating the Oct 28 and Oct 29 creative.
- The email runs very long, and the Recommended block (dining tables, curtain brackets, rugs, stockings) is unrelated to quilts and breaks focus.
- The dense fine-print and legal footer, plus the app, credit card and Affirm blocks, add clutter.
- The primary CTA "Quilts ship free" is a shipping button, not a product-action verb. The quilt tiles have no prices or reviews.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put prices on every quilt tile ("from $169") and lead the hero with that number.
- 2. Drop the recurring 20% banner on category sends, or shrink it to a thin strip below the hero so the quilt story owns the top.
- 3. Cut the Recommended and secondary modules, keeping at most the Christmas Shop and Design Crew.
- 4. Add a review count or "bestseller" tag to one or two quilts.
- 5. Change the hero button to "Shop quilts" and state the free-shipping terms in the line above it.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Quilts from $169 + free shipping`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your bed's cozy upgrade starts at $169`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Velvet, plaid and patchwork quilts, plus an extra 20% off select styles.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Six handcrafted quilts we love. Free shipping on every one.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

This is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style email (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…`) that is mostly sliced images. The automated QA run shows a 50% pass rate with no hard failures. The real issues are widespread missing alt text, three plain-HTTP image requests, and the recipient's address leaking into tracking-pixel URLs. The header-related warnings are likely artifacts of the AgentMail relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **Opaque tracking links:** 51 links were skipped as click-redirect domains (for example `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im…`). Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified, not confirmed broken.
- **Plain-HTTP open-tracking pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`.
- **Other tracking pixels:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEv…/cat/1` (third-party ad-network pixel) and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/6322bd15-…?mi_cid=…`. Neither has alt text. Both are 1×1 pixels and should carry `alt=""`.
- **Plain-text part:** 9,804 of 13,963 characters (70%+) are URLs. This is the long tracking-URL problem and can hurt spam scoring.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

## 7. Recommendations

1. **Alt text:** add meaningful alt text to every linked image (hero, product, footer slices) and `alt=""` to spacers and pixels.
2. **HTTPS:** move the three `http://` assets, including `svopn`, to HTTPS.
3. **Email address in URLs:** stop putting the plaintext email in pixel and image URLs. Use an opaque subscriber key.
4. **Template cleanup:**
   - Remove the PHP `header()` text from the `<style>` block.
   - Fill in `<title>`.
   - Add `lang` to `<html>`.
   - Drop `maximum-scale=1`.
5. **Headers:** verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` against the raw message source rather than the relay copy, and confirm the unsubscribe link and address in the footer.
6. **Landing pages:** follow the redirect chain on a sample of links to confirm UTM parameters and landing pages, since the HTTP probe skipped all 51.
