# Our top 100 gifts 🎁
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a gift-guide catalog send that is tidy and on-season but has no clear offer. The hero reads "Our Most-Loved Gifts" with a "TOP 100 GIFTS" pill over a stack of faux fur throws. The "Loved" copy appears to be clipped or overlapped by the hero text. After the hero, six product tiles run in a zigzag: Stewart Plaid Scent Collection, Cozy Eyelash Chenille Throw, Cozy Retreat Robe, Acacia Wood Cheese Board, Grosgrain Picture Frame, and Quinn Leather Jewelry Storage. A "SHOP ALL GIFTS" button and a "gifts under $30 / $50 / $100" price-tier band follow.
- The price tiers are the most useful shopping aid in the email, but they sit about two-thirds of the way down. They are not linked to a specific headline offer. Three "SHIPS FREE" tags on individual products are the only incentive. Under the rubric, the email is a curated browse with no percent or dollar hook.
- Below the gift content, the email drifts into a long tail: "Recommended Just For You" (curtain rod, dining tables, swivel chair, towel), a Christmas Shop banner, Design Crew, app, credit card and Affirm blocks. That tail dilutes the gift story. It comes one day after "The Halloween Shop is OPEN" (up to 50% off) and "FREE design help for the holidays", so a subscriber would see this as a softer, offer-less follow-up. My judgement: a good brand voice and clean layout, but it needs a stronger reason to click now.

## What's working

- The "TOP 100 GIFTS" pill and "Our Most-Loved Gifts" headline state the email's purpose immediately.
- The product tiles are large, well-styled and easy to scan. Names are clear and each has its own "SHOP NOW" link.
- The "gifts under $30 / $50 / $100" band gives a fast, budget-led way into the gift range.
- "SHIPS FREE" tags on three products add a small incentive at the product level.
- The Christmas Shop banner fits the season and keeps the brand voice consistent.

## What's weak

- There is no headline offer. No percent off, dollar off or deadline is visible near the top.
- The hero text looks clipped or overlapped ("…oved Gifts" behind "Our Most-"), which makes the headline hard to read.
- The price tiers sit too low. They should come right after the hero.
- The "Recommended Just For You" block of curtains, dining tables and towels has nothing to do with gifting and weakens focus.
- The email is very long, with app, credit card and Affirm modules stacked at the bottom. Fine print is dense and small.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the price-tier band ("under $30 / $50 / $100") directly under the hero, and link each tier to its own page.
- 2. Add one concrete incentive to the hero, such as a free shipping cutoff date or a gift-with-purchase. Use it as the single call to action.
- 3. Fix the hero so the headline is not clipped, and make "TOP 100 GIFTS" a real button that says "Shop the top 100".
- 4. Cut the "Recommended Just For You" block and the secondary banners, or move them below a gift-focused footer.
- 5. Add social proof to the tiles, such as star ratings or "Best seller" badges, to support the "most-loved" claim.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Gifts people keep buying: top 100 list`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Throws, robes and jewelry boxes. Many ship free.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop by budget: under $30, $50 or $100.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard table-based, SFMC-style send with no unresolved merge tokens in the visible source. It has three confirmed problems: roughly 44 content images with no `alt`, the recipient's email address exposed in image and pixel URLs (one over plain `http://`), and malformed CSS in the head. The compliance and authentication warnings come from header capture and a truncated source, so I couldn't confirm them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque tracking:** 56 links were skipped as tracking redirects, for example `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...`. Destination health is unverified, not failing.
- **Email address in pixel URLs:**
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is a cleartext, non-TLS open pixel carrying the recipient's address.
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is loaded as an `<img>`, so the address sits in the URL and in any proxy or CDN logs.
- **Third-party ad pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEv.../cat/1` is a cross-domain ad-tech beacon. It is not necessarily a defect, but it adds a third-party call.
- **Pixel hosts:** `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/d6025af6-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...` is a normal first-party open pixel.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt:** 48 QA entries have no `alt`. Four are tracking pixels, leaving about 44 content images.
  - They include `slices_01–14.jpg` (`/H26_Seasonal_B4_OFGiftIdeas/`), the six product images (e.g. `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-1245-1-l.jpg`, `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`), the banners, and `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`.
  - Sliced images without alt render as blank or broken blocks when images are off, and screen readers get nothing. This is an inference from the file naming, because the source is truncated.
  - Only the header logo has alt (`alt="POTTERY BARN"`).
- **Non-HTTPS images:** these are flagged and may be blocked or downgraded in some clients.
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` (a 2022 asset)
  - the `svopn` pixel
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Stray PHP in CSS:** the second `<style>` block ends with `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store..."); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. This is server-side code leaked into CSS. It is invalid, and strict parsers may discard the preceding rule.
- **Missing roles:** layout tables such as the spacer `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><tr><td height="20">&nbsp;</td>` and the outer wrappers lack `role="presentation"`, while the content tables have it.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on a sister-brand domain with no alt. A change on that host would break this.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[[…]]` tokens appear in the visible source. The recipient address is substituted correctly in the pixel URLs (see section 2 for the exposure).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unverified (QA warnings):** `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found, and `Authentication-Results` was missing, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. QA attributes the headers to the AgentMail relay, so these may be capture artifacts and not sender defects.
- **Not confirmable:** the unsubscribe link and physical postal address aren't visible in the truncated source. The footer is built from 18 alt-less image slices, so if either lives inside an image, it is invisible to image-blocked and screen-reader users. This is plausible but unconfirmed.
- **Alignment:** the From domain `e.potterybarn.com` matches the click domain `click.e.potterybarn.com`, so branding is aligned.
- **Plain-text part:** over 70% URLs (10,651 of 14,857 chars), a mild spam-filter signal, caused by the tracking-wrapped links.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs unverifiable:** every CTA is wrapped in `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…`, and the probe skipped them. UTM and landing page alignment can't be confirmed from this data.
- **Product images:** they hotlink the site CDN (`https://www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/ab/images/dp/wcm/2026xx/...`), so imagery matches the site. Their click destinations are unknown.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` to all content images, using `alt=""` for decorative slices and spacers. Put real text for the offer and footer links in live HTML, not in image slices.
2. Move the recipient email out of query strings. Use an opaque ID for `coreemail.html` and `svopn`.
3. Serve `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, the 2022 `slices_02.jpg` and the `svopn` pixel over HTTPS. Replace the 2022 asset.
4. Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP lines from the `<style>` block.
5. Fill `<title>`, drop `maximum-scale=1`, and add `role="presentation"` to the remaining layout tables.
6. Re-verify `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC against the raw message, bypassing the relay, before treating them as defects.
7. Resolve a sample of the `click.e.potterybarn.com` links to confirm UTM tagging and landing pages.
