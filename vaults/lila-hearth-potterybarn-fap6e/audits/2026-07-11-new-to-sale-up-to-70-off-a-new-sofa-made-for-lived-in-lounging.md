# New to sale & UP TO 70% OFF + A new sofa made for lived-in lounging
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-11**
## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn is running a hybrid product-launch-plus-sale email for the third day in a row — this time introducing the Bellevue Collection sofa while simultaneously pointing at the Summer Sale. The hero is clean: a large, aspirational beige sectional in a lived-in room, with a tight "introducing The Bellevue Collection" framing that actually earns the "lived-in lounging" line in the subject. Below that, the email pivots hard into sale grids, a "Recommended Just for You" personalization block, and a "Brand New Arrivals" module — four distinct objectives in a single send.
- The tension is real and familiar. The subject promises "UP TO 70% OFF" but the first thing you see is a brand-new collection at what is almost certainly full price. That bait-and-switch energy isn't egregious, but a subscriber who opened for the sale has to scroll past a premium new-product pitch to find it. The reverse is also true: someone drawn in by the Bellevue story gets immediately pulled away into a sale grid. Neither audience gets a clean experience.
- This is the third "+" compound subject this week from PB (see July 10's "100s of NEW sale styles + Style a beautiful dining table"). The formula is becoming a recognizable tic — and not a flattering one. The July 8–9 focused furniture-sale emails (both 9/10) landed better precisely because they committed to one idea.

## What's working

- **Bellevue hero is strong.** Full-bleed sofa shot with a warm, livable room context sells the "lived-in lounging" positioning immediately.
- **Sofa variant row.** Showing multiple configurations/colorways below the hero is a good commerce move for a new collection launch — it signals range without requiring a click.
- **"Recommended Just for You" block.** Personalization signals mid-email give a reason to keep scrolling and increase click surface for subscribers who aren't sold on the Bellevue.
- **No render bugs.** Clean layout, no broken images, no overlapping text visible.

## What's weak

- **Dual-purpose subject collapses both stories.** "New to sale & UP TO 70% OFF + A new sofa made for lived-in lounging" at 67 characters combines a sale tease with a product launch — neither lands cleanly in the inbox.
- **Hero and offer are misaligned.** The 70% off claim is the inbox hook; the new full-price sofa is the hero. The subscriber who opens for the deal has to scroll to find it.
- **"+" compound subject is now a weekly pattern.** Third use in five days — subscribers will start pattern-matching and ignoring it as noise.
- **Loyalty/rewards buried in footer.** PB Key appears only in the bottom legal block, not as a CTA-adjacent nudge in the body where it could motivate a click.
- **Four-section email with no connective thread.** Bellevue launch → sale grid → recommendations → new arrivals reads like four mini-emails stitched together.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Split this into two sends.** The Bellevue Collection launch deserves a standalone email — one hero, one story, one CTA. The "new to sale" content belongs in a sale-focused send. Trying to do both mutes both.
- 2. **If you must combine, reconcile the hero with the promise.** If the subject leads with 70% off, the hero scroll-stop should be a sale item, not a full-price new arrival. Alternatively, frame the Bellevue launch as a discovery moment and drop the sale language to a secondary banner.
- 3. **Retire the "+" compound subject for now.** Three uses in five days has trained subscribers to read it as clutter. Pick one storyline per send.
- 4. **Lift the PB Key call-out into the body.** A "Members save more" line adjacent to a CTA button drives login and conversion — the footer placement wastes it.
- 5. **Add a deadline.** "New to sale" with no end date removes urgency entirely. Even "through Sunday" would help click rates.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Meet the Bellevue — our most lived-in sofa yet`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New to sale: up to 70% off furniture, rugs & more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sink-in comfort, stays-beautiful style. Explore the full collection.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Just added to our Summer Sale — shop before it's gone.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[CRITICAL] PHP `header()` calls inside `<style>` block**
The final `<style>` tag in `<head>` contains raw PHP function calls:
```
header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
header("Cache-Control: post-check=0, pre-check=0", false);
header("Pragma: no-cache");
```
These are server-side PHP statements that were not executed — they leaked as literal text into the rendered CSS. Email clients will parse this as invalid CSS and may reject or mangle the entire `<style>` block, breaking responsive layout rules that follow.

**[FAIL] 34 images missing `alt` text**
Every content image in the email has no `alt` attribute or an empty one. This includes hero banners (`F26_TOP_SummerSalePartC_NoUrgency.jpg`), all 18 Bellevue collection slices, all 18 footer slices, product images, and tracking pixels. Images-off rendering (common in Outlook, Apple Mail default, corporate proxies) produces a completely blank email.

**[WARN] 3 image sources served over HTTP (not HTTPS)**
- `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` — cross-brand, also 403
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` — footer credit card banner
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` — dated 2022 asset path

Gmail, Apple Mail (iOS 16+), and most modern clients block mixed-content images. These will render as broken.

**[WARN] Open tracking pixel over HTTP**
`http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
Open tracking served over HTTP. Will be blocked by clients that enforce HTTPS for remote content, silently undercounting opens.

**[WARN] 18-slice image layout for Bellevue collection**
The hero section is assembled from 18 individual `slices_01.jpg`–`slices_18.jpg` GIF slices. A single failed image request or a 1px table gap renders the entire composition as broken strips. No fallback text.

**[WARN] `maximum-scale=1` in viewport meta**
`<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` — prevents user zoom on iOS, an accessibility violation (WCAG 1.4.4 Resize Text).

**[INFO] Empty `<title>` tag**
`<title></title>` — not critical but surfaces as blank in some clients' tab/preview UI.

### 5. Compliance

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header not captured**
The QA relay did not observe a `List-Unsubscribe` header. If absent at delivery, CAN-SPAM §5(a)(3) and Gmail/Yahoo 2024 bulk sender requirements are not met. Likely a relay artifact — verify against raw headers at the MTA.

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not captured**
One-click unsubscribe (`POST` method) required by Gmail/Yahoo for senders over 5K/day. Same caveat — verify at MTA level.

**[WARN] `Authentication-Results` header not found**
SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status unknown via this relay path. Cannot confirm sender alignment for `e.potterybarn.com`. If DMARC is in `p=reject` and alignment fails, delivery to Gmail/Yahoo will silently drop.

**[INFO] `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` present** — correct, prevents archival indexing.

**[INFO] `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` present** — suppresses referrer on outbound clicks. This is intentional for privacy but means analytics on destination pages will show "direct" traffic rather than email source for any non-tracked link.
