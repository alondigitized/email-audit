# ❤️ Take an EXTRA 20% OFF select styles
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the third "Extra 20% off select styles" send in two days (10/22 "STARTS NOW 📣", "Holiday NEW ARRIVALS", "ON NOW!"), and today's version repeats the same oxblood banner. The offer is clear, but a subscriber has seen it three times, so it has stopped being news. Beneath the banner is a handsome Plaid Shop hero: a tartan table setting, a "THE PLAID SHOP" title, and a "SHOP NOW" button. It is beautiful, but it is a second message. The banner sells a sale and the hero sells a theme, so they compete.
- The lower half has a lot going on. It runs through a plaid mosaic, a "The Christmas Shop" button, category links (Bedding, Decor, Furniture, Rugs), a "Recommended Just For You" product grid, a second Christmas Shop banner, Design Crew, app, rewards and Affirm blocks. The page is long, with a lot of footer content. None of the lower blocks reinforces the 20% off, and the product grid shows no prices.
- Judgement: a competent, on-brand holiday send with a credible discount. Its main weakness is fatigue and diffusion. It reads as a template re-sent daily, not as a reason to act today.

## What's working

- The offer sits at the very top: "Extra 20% off Select Styles / Plus savings up to 70% off" with a clear "SHOP ALL SALE" button.
- The Plaid Shop hero is seasonal and polished, and it gives a clear visual theme for the holidays.
- The mosaic of plaid pillows, throws, bedding and table settings is cohesive and on-season.
- The "Recommended Just For You" grid adds named products such as the Banks Extendable Dining Table and the Reeva Handwoven Rug, which suggests some personalization.
- The category links (Bedding, Decor, Furniture, Rugs) give easy ways into the site.

## What's weak

- The oxblood banner has run on every send since 10/22, so this is the third near-identical promo in two days and the cadence feels heavy.
- Two heroes compete: the sale banner and the Plaid Shop. Both have a button, and it is unclear which is primary.
- The "Exclusions apply. Prices as marked." line is tiny, and the long fine print at the bottom is dense and hard to read. This weakens how honest the offer feels.
- The recommended products show no prices or sale markers, so the 20% off is not tied to any item.
- There are too many secondary modules: the second Christmas Shop banner, Design Crew, app download, rewards and Affirm. They dilute the email and bury the CTA.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero. Fold the Plaid Shop into the sale message, for example "Plaid Shop: extra 20% off" over the tartan photo, and drop the separate banner.
- 2. Show prices in the product grid, with the sale price next to the original, so the discount feels real.
- 3. Add a deadline, such as "Ends Sunday". It would add urgency and set today's send apart from the earlier ones.
- 4. Cut the second Christmas Shop banner and the Design Crew, app and Affirm blocks, or collapse them into one slim row.
- 5. Move the exclusions into a visible line right under the CTA, and shorten the footer legal text.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Plaid Shop: extra 20% off, ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tartan pillows, throws & bedding — 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Extra 20% off select styles, plus up to 70% off. Our plaid favorites are included.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fresh holiday plaid for every room. Sale ends Sunday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is mostly standard table-based XHTML. It has a stray server-side code fragment in a `<style>` block, many images without `alt`, and three plain-HTTP image requests. Two of those requests expose the recipient's address. The header-level checks (List-Unsubscribe, SPF/DKIM) could not be evaluated, because the AgentMail relay doesn't capture those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified tracking links:** 57 links were skipped because they use click-redirect domains, for example `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...` on the logo. Destinations, redirect health and UTM parameters are therefore unconfirmed.
- **Recipient email in a cleartext URL:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is an open-tracking pixel that sends the recipient address over unencrypted HTTP.
- **Recipient email in an `<img>` URL:** `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is loaded as an image, and the address is in the query string.
- **Third-party pixels:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` is an ad-tech pixel, and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/eea06183-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...` is a tracking pixel. Both are expected for this kind of email but add third-party requests.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is loaded from a different brand's domain.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on content images:** About 50 images are flagged. These include the plaid story slices (`1023_FRI_AM_PlaidStory/slices_01–15.jpg`), the hero banner (`H26_TOP_Extra20CLR.jpg`) and the product images (`banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`, `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg`, etc.). The footer slices (`Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`) are also flagged. If these carry text, such as the offer or footer links, they are unreadable with images off or in a screen reader. The `alt` flags on tracking pixels and `spacer_0.gif` are benign.
- **Plain-HTTP images:** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` and the `svopn` pixel may be blocked or flagged as mixed content.
- **Stray code in CSS:** The last `<style>` block contains `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store, ...`. This looks like leaked server-side code, and clients just discard it as invalid CSS.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>`.
- **No language attribute:** `<html xmlns=... xmlns:v=... xmlns:o=...>` has no `lang`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile.
- **Layout tables:** The spacer table `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><tr><td height="20">&nbsp;</td>` has no `role="presentation"`, unlike the other tables. I can't confirm the others in the truncated source.
- **Font link:** The Google Fonts `<link>` is ignored by many clients, so the layout depends on fallbacks. This is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source.
- The recipient address is injected into pixel and image URLs, as noted in section 2.
- The footer is truncated, so any greeting or name tokens there are unverified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Neither was found. The QA tool notes the relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules expect both.
- **Authentication-Results:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sender domain is `e.potterybarn.com`.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address:** Both sit in the truncated footer, so I can't confirm them.
- **Plain-text part:** 10,899 of 15,119 characters (about 72%) are URLs. That is mostly long tracking links, and it raises the spam-filter risk.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The sampled links go through `click.e.potterybarn.com` (encoded `qs=` tokens) and the pixels use `mi_cid`/`mi_mid` and `sv_cid` parameters. No UTM parameters are visible.
- Because the redirects weren't resolved, I can't verify that the landing pages match the "Extra 20% OFF select styles" offer. The product images point to `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the offer banner, the product images and the footer link slices. Use `alt=""` on the pixels and spacers.
2. Switch the three `http://` image sources to `https://`. Stop putting the recipient email in plain-text query strings, especially over HTTP (`svopn`, `coreemail.html`).
3. Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP fragment from the `<style>` block.
4. Set `<title>` and `<html lang="en">`, and drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag.
5. Add `role="presentation"` to the remaining layout tables.
6. Re-run the compliance checks against the raw message source to confirm List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
7. Resolve a sample of the tracking links with redirect-following to verify landing pages and UTM parameters.
8. Shorten or consolidate the tracking URLs in the plain-text part.
