# Worth a look 🚨 Up to 70% off + New curtains ship free! Florals, neutrals & more
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-25**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's curtains-and-window-treatment send, arriving mid-blitz as roughly the eleventh Summer Sale touchpoint in five days. The hero executes well — a sunlit room with billowing patterned curtains is aspirational and category-specific, and "SHOP CURTAINS" is one of the cleanest, most specific CTAs in the entire campaign. The free-shipping-on-curtains angle is the right utility hook: concrete, product-linked, and differentiated from the relentless "UP TO 70% OFF" drum.
- The subject line blows it. "Worth a look 🚨 Up to 70% off + New curtains ship free! Florals, neutrals & more" packs four separate pitches into 79 characters, topped with an alarm siren emoji on a brand whose entire equity is taste and calm aspiration. Compare this directly to the June 24 handcrafted rugs send — "Handcrafted rugs starting at $299" — which scored an 8/10 precisely because it committed to one specific number. This subject tries to say everything and lands nowhere.
- The body is stronger than the subject deserves. The layout is clean, the personalization block ("RECOMMENDED JUST FOR YOU") is prominent, and the "Discover New Arrivals" section adds browse breadth. But cadence fatigue at send #11 is a real tax, and no executional strength fully offsets being the fourth email this week with "UP TO 70% OFF" in the hero.

## What's working

- **Hero image is on-brand and category-specific** — the curtains lifestyle shot is elevated and aspirational, not catalog-flat.
- **"SHOP CURTAINS" is a clean CTA** — verb + noun, category-clear, none of the "Discover More" vagueness that plagued the June 23 bestsellers send.
- **Free shipping tied to a specific category** — "new curtains ship free" is more concrete and actionable than a blanket shipping banner.
- **"RECOMMENDED JUST FOR YOU" personalization block** — visible and prominent; adds a sense of purchase-relevant targeting.
- **Layout renders cleanly** — no overlapping text, no broken images, no empty gaps.

## What's weak

- **Subject line is dangerously overloaded** — four distinct value props + a 🚨 emoji in 79 characters; mobile clients truncate well before "free shipping."
- **🚨 is a brand mismatch** — an alarm siren on a refined home-goods brand reads as desperation, not urgency.
- **Cadence fatigue is at maximum** — send #11+ in five days with essentially the same hero claim; the subscriber relationship absorbs real damage from this.
- **No credible urgency anchor** — "Summer Sale" is live but no end date is visible; the 🚨 promises urgency but delivers nothing to back it up.
- **Secondary sections dilute the curtains story** — "Polished Platform" and "Discover New Arrivals" split focus across three unrelated categories in one scroll.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead the subject with the specific utility, not the siren.** Free shipping on curtains is the differentiator — that earns the open.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New curtains ship free — florals, linens & neutrals`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Free shipping on new curtains, now through the Summer Sale`
- 2. **Kill the 🚨 outright.** If an emoji is needed, ✨ at the tail is as far as Pottery Barn should go; the siren actively degrades brand equity.
- 3. **Anchor urgency with a deadline.** "Ends Sunday" or "Free shipping this weekend only" converts empty urgency signals into believable ones.
- 4. **Set the preheader to complement, not repeat, the subject** — the free-shipping hook or a style-specific tease earns an extra second of inbox attention.
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New florals and airy linens, delivered on us.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Window refresh — fresh curtains, no shipping cost.`
- 5. **Consolidate the body to one story.** Drop either "Polished Platform" or "Discover New Arrivals" to keep the curtains narrative coherent through the full scroll.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[FAIL] 47 images missing `alt` text**
Nearly every content image, all footer slices (`slices_01.jpg` through `slices_18.jpg`), product images, promotional banners (`S26_TOP_SummerSale_NoUrgency.jpg`, `F26_Bottom_NewArrivals.jpg`), and tracking pixels have empty or absent `alt` attributes. Recipients with images disabled see a blank email; screen readers get no content.

**[FAIL] 3 image sources served over HTTP (not HTTPS)**
- `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
- `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&...` (open-tracking pixel)

Gmail, Outlook.com, and Apple Mail all proxy or block mixed-content HTTP images. The open-tracking pixel in particular being HTTP means open tracking may be silently lost on HTTPS-enforcing clients.

**[WARN] PHP `header()` calls embedded in CSS `<style>` block**
```html
<style>
  ...
  header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
  header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
  ...
</style>
```
These are server-side PHP directives that have leaked into the HTML output verbatim. They are no-ops as CSS but indicate a template rendering bug — the PHP was not executed before send. No runtime impact in the email client, but it signals a broken build/render pipeline.

**[WARN] `<title>` element is empty**
`<title></title>` — some clients display the title in tab or preview contexts.

**[WARN] `max-scale=1` in viewport meta**
`<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` — `maximum-scale=1` prevents user zoom, which is an accessibility violation (WCAG 1.4.4).

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)

The AgentMail relay did not surface a `List-Unsubscribe` header. Gmail and Apple Mail use this header to render the native unsubscribe button; its absence increases spam classification risk. May be a relay capture gap — should be verified against raw SMTP headers.

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found (RFC 8058)**
One-click unsubscribe (required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender rules since Feb 2024) cannot be confirmed. If this header is genuinely absent, the send is non-compliant with current Gmail bulk sender policy.

**[WARN] `Authentication-Results` header not captured**
SPF and DKIM pass/fail status is unknown. Sending domain is `e.potterybarn.com`; DKIM alignment should be verified via raw headers.

**[INFO] Physical address and unsubscribe link**
An unsubscribe mechanism is present in the footer (standard for Williams-Sonoma ESP sends). CAN-SPAM physical address presence could not be confirmed from the truncated source — should be verified in the full footer.
