# Celebrate mom with the perfect gift
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-05**
## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn sends a well-produced Mother's Day gift guide anchored by a "Celebrate Mom" hero and three curated gifting categories: spa essentials, homewares, and memory/moment gifts. The visual execution is clean and on-brand — elegant, aspirational, whitespace-forward. The email's weakness is strategic, not aesthetic: there's no discount, no urgency, and no clear single offer to convert on. It reads like a catalog browse, not a conversion send.

## What's working

- **Visual design is strong.** The white-and-natural palette, elegant product photography, and generous whitespace are exactly on-brand for Pottery Barn. First impression is premium.
- **Seasonal relevance is tight.** Sending a Mother's Day guide on May 5 (six days out) is well-timed — enough runway to order and receive before the holiday.
- **Multi-category structure reduces bounce.** Spa Essentials, Relaxing Homewares, and Gift of Moments let different buyer intents self-select.
- **"Recommended Just For You" module is a trust signal.** A personalized product grid at the bottom adds specificity without cluttering the hero.

## What's weak

- **No offer.** There's no discount, free shipping threshold, or promotional hook anywhere visible. For a gifting occasion, where competitors almost certainly have a promo, this is a meaningful conversion gap.
- **No urgency mechanism.** Nothing communicates "order by X to guarantee Mother's Day delivery." That's the single most actionable piece of copy for a gift send six days before the holiday.
- **CTAs are category-level, not product-level.** Each module links to a category page, not a specific hero product. That's one extra click before the buyer sees anything to add to cart.
- **Hero copy is generic.** "Celebrate Mom" is the category-level brief. Without a specific product, exclusive item, or price point, there's no reason to click vs. just closing the email.
- **"Recommended Just For You" feels disconnected.** Positioned after three generic category modules, the personalized section lands as an afterthought rather than the email's sharpest hook.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a shipping cutoff.** A single line — "Order by [date] for Mother's Day delivery" — in the hero would create real urgency without a discount.
- 2. **Lead with one hero product.** Pick a bestselling gift item, show the price, and make that the first CTA. Category guides are warm-up content; a specific product drives clicks.
- 3. **Surface a threshold offer.** Even "Free shipping on all orders" reduces conversion friction for gifters who haven't committed yet.
- 4. **Move "Recommended Just For You" to position two.** Personalized content is the most compelling hook — bury it below three generic sections and it's wasted.
- 5. **Tighten the subject line** to include the shipping deadline or a specific product ("Get mom the gift that ships free — order by Thursday").

### 1. Technical Summary

Two broken links produce HTTP 403 responses, blocking CTA paths; 50+ images render without alt text. A PHP `header()` block was pasted verbatim into a `<style>` tag, producing invalid CSS that mail clients must parse around.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Critical — broken CTAs (HTTP 403):**

- Credit card apply-and-buy banner: `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/c/da01b407-b21c-4618-a71b-f83bef0dd682?brandCode=POTTERYBARNCB&marketingChannelCode=APPLYANDBUY…` — returns 403. Any recipient clicking the Key Rewards / credit card CTA hits a dead end.
- West Elm cross-brand spacer: `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` — 403 and HTTP. This asset belongs to West Elm and should not be present in a Pottery Barn send; indicates a copy-paste error from a shared template.

**HTTP (non-HTTPS) asset sources — 4 images:**

| Asset | URL |
|---|---|
| `spacer_0.gif` | `http://edm.westelm.com/...` |
| `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` | `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/...` |
| `slices_02.jpg` | `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/...` |
| Open-pixel | `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true...` |

Gmail and Outlook proxy HTTP images through their own servers; some mobile clients block them outright. The open-tracking pixel being HTTP means opens may go unrecorded on strict clients.

**22 click-tracking links** through `click.e.potterybarn.com` were not probed (expected). No issue flagged for those.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Invalid CSS — PHP code in `<style>` block (high severity):**

```css
/* inside <style> tag — this is not CSS */
header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
header("Cache-Control: post-check=0, pre-check=0", false);
header("Pragma: no-cache");
```

Server-side PHP `header()` calls were pasted into the client-facing `<style>` block. Mail clients will encounter a CSS parse error here. Depending on the client's error recovery, subsequent CSS rules may be silently dropped. This needs to be removed entirely.

**Empty `<title>` tag:** `<title></title>` — some clients (Outlook, Apple Mail) display the document title in tab bars or previews.

**Alt text missing on 50 images**, including all 18 content slices (`slices_01.jpg` through `slices_18.jpg`), all 18 footer slices, hero images, and tracking pixels. Subscribers with images disabled see a blank email. Screen readers get no content.

**`max-scale=1` in viewport meta** (`maximum-scale=1`) prevents user zoom on mobile — an accessibility regression.

**`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`** applied globally via `* {}` overrides user agent text scaling on iOS, which conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).

No unfired merge tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `%%email%%`) visible in the truncated source — no hard failures here.

### 5. Compliance

| Check | Status | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| `List-Unsubscribe` header | **MISSING** | Not captured by AgentMail relay |
| `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) | **MISSING** | One-click unsubscribe not detectable |
| `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM) | **UNKNOWN** | Not captured by relay |
| Physical mailing address | Not verified (source truncated) | CAN-SPAM requires postal address in body |
| Unsubscribe link in body | Not verified (source truncated) | Assumed present given ESP send |

**`List-Unsubscribe` absence is the highest compliance risk.** Gmail and Yahoo both require it for bulk senders (>5K/day). Without it, Gmail's "Unsubscribe" button does not appear, and Yahoo may route to spam. This may be a relay-capture gap rather than a true omission — confirm against raw SMTP headers at the ESP.

**Plain-text version URL density:** 72% of plain-text chars are URLs (10,652 / 14,800). This ratio will trip Bayesian spam filters that score plain-text parts independently. The plain-text version likely needs a human-readable rewrite.

The West Elm asset (`edm.westelm.com`) signals the footer module was copied from a West Elm template without updating the asset domain — worth auditing whether any West Elm destination links also leaked in.
