# Happening now! Up to 70% off + Ready-to-ship outdoor essentials to start summer
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-25**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's outdoor essentials entry in an already-saturated Summer Sale blitz — by my count, the thirteenth or fourteenth touchpoint in roughly five days. The hero earns its keep: a warm, dappled-light patio scene with natural-wood furniture is genuinely aspirational, and "Ready-to-Ship" is a smarter hook than yet another raw percentage. Outdoor furniture has long lead times, so availability as a purchase trigger is real.
- But the email undermines itself twice. The "Recommended Just For You" section inserts curtain panels — indoor window treatments — directly into an outdoor-focused send. That's almost certainly the browse-history algo misfiring after the June 25 curtains email went out the same day, but to a recipient it just reads as the email not knowing what it wants to be. And the subject line opens with "Happening now!" — a panic-urgency opener that neither adds information nor feels earned this deep into a prolonged sale event.
- The bones are solid. The execution is scattered. A focused outdoor-only send with a real ship-date deadline would be meaningfully stronger than what landed here.

## What's working

- **Hero photography is the best in the blitz so far** — natural light, layered texture, and an actual patio context make the lifestyle case without a single word of copy.
- **"Ready-to-Ship" is a genuine differentiator** — it answers the real objection for outdoor furniture (will it arrive before summer's over?) and no prior send in this blitz has led with availability.
- **"Shop Outdoor" CTA is clean and specific** — verb + noun, contrasting button, no ambiguity.
- **Product grid with labeled categories** (Pillows, Cushions, Covers + Awnings, Sofas & Sectionals) gives scope quickly without forcing a scroll commitment.

## What's weak

- **"Recommended Just For You" fires curtain panels inside an outdoor essentials email** — the algo is cross-selling from the same-day curtains send, breaking the entire thematic frame.
- **"Happening now!" is exhausted urgency** — no end date, no credible scarcity signal, just a hollow noise-maker fourteen sends into a blitz.
- **Subject line is 79 characters** — far past mobile truncation; the most valuable part ("Ready-to-ship outdoor essentials") gets clipped on most phones.
- **"Up to 70% off" returns** — the June 24 handcrafted rugs send proved that a specific anchor price ("starting at $299") outperforms the vague ceiling in this blitz; this email regresses to the ceiling.
- **No ship deadline visible** — "ready to ship" is a promise, but there's no date anchoring it; "arrives by July 4th" would have made this actually actionable.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with a ship deadline in the subject, not "Happening now!"** — "Arrives by July 4th" or "In time for summer" turns the inventory hook into urgency the reader can actually use. This is the single highest-leverage change.
- 2. **Pull the curtains out of "Recommended Just For You"** — suppress cross-category algo recs or replace with outdoor accessories (lanterns, planters, outdoor rugs) that reinforce the send's theme.
- 3. **Add a floor price to the hero** — "Up to 70% off, sofas from $499" anchors the discount to something real; it worked for the rugs send (June 24, 8/10) and would work here.
- 4. **Trim the subject to under 50 characters** — the outdoor-availability story is the news; lead with it.
- 5. **Remove the app download block** — it competes with the purchase CTA and adds nothing at the bottom of an already-long scroll.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ready to ship before July 4th — 70% off outdoor`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Outdoor furniture that ships now — up to 70% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sofas, sectionals, pillows — ready to leave our warehouse today.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your patio isn't going to furnish itself. Ships now, arrives fast.`

Accessibility coverage is severely degraded by missing alt text across ~40 images and three HTTP image sources that will be blocked by modern email clients enforcing HTTPS.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**PHP header() calls inside `<style>` block — templating error**
The following literal PHP code appears inside a `<style>` tag:
```
header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
header("Cache-Control: post-check=0, pre-check=0", false);
header("Pragma: no-cache");
```
This is invalid CSS. It will either render as a parse error (silently ignored by most clients) or, in strict parsers, corrupt adjacent style rules. This is server-side code that escaped into the template output unexecuted — likely from a dynamic image-serving module.

**3 images served over HTTP (will be blocked)**
| Image | URL |
|---|---|
| `spacer_0.gif` | `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` |
| `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` | `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` |
| `slices_02.jpg` | `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` |
| Open pixel | `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=…` |

Gmail, Outlook.com, and Apple Mail block mixed-content image loads. The open-tracking pixel over HTTP will produce false negatives on open-rate reporting.

**~40 images missing alt text**
All product slices (`slices_01.jpg` through `slices_18.jpg` in footer, `slices_01–12` in outdoor block), hero (`S26_TOP_SummerSale_NoUrgency.jpg`), and multiple product thumbnails have empty or absent `alt` attributes. Screen readers will skip these entirely; clients with images off show blank spaces.

**Empty `<title>` tag**
`<title></title>` — no document title. Minor, but shows as blank in some preview surfaces.

**`maximum-scale=1` in viewport meta**
Suppresses user-initiated zoom on mobile — fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).

### 7. Recommendations

1. Repair or replace the `clickcp.potterybarn.com` Apply & Buy link — it is actively blocking a conversion path.
2. Remove or upgrade the West Elm spacer to an HTTPS Pottery Barn-hosted asset.

**P1 — Fix in next send**
