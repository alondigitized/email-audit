# RSVP for your FREE design appointment
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a Design Services appointment email wearing the Early Black Friday template. The subject promises one thing, a free design appointment. The body then delivers the same "up to 70% off" sale grid Pottery Barn has sent since 11/16, with the appointment pitch stacked on top. The top of the email works well. A dark green band offers a chance to win a $3,000 gift card for booking, and a plaid hero below it carries "Free Design Services" with a clear what/when/where structure and a "Book a free appointment" button.
- After that the email gets muddled. It repeats the CTA, then runs straight into a full sale block (sofas, bedding, furniture, tabletop, pillows) and a "Shop all sale" button. Below that sit a product carousel, an app promo, a credit-card promo, Affirm and a very long legal block. This is a rare non-duplicate concept in a week of near-identical sends like "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY DEALS that do it all ✔️". Even so, it dilutes its own premise by giving the reader two competing jobs.
- Verdict: a fresh angle with a good top third, undercut by a bloated body. The sweepstakes is a hook but is buried in small copy. The email would convert better if it picked one goal.

## What's working

- The Design Crew sweepstakes banner ($3,000 gift card) at the very top gives a concrete incentive to book.
- The "what / when / where" hero copy is scannable, and "Free Design Services" reads instantly.
- "Book a free appointment" is a clear red button and appears twice.
- The sale grid has strong category labels with specific "Up to X% off" figures and "Ships free" tags.
- The plaid hero and serif typography keep the brand voice consistent.

## What's weak

- The sale block competes with the appointment ask, so the email has two goals and loses focus.
- The "Find your store" and "Book a free appointment" CTAs sit close together in the hero and split the click.
- The "Recommended just for you" grid has no prices or reviews, and its captions are small and plain.
- The footer stack of app, credit card, Affirm and legal text is long and heavy. Sweepstakes rules and promotional disclaimers take up a large share of the render.
- The sweepstakes details are tiny and buried, so the $3,000 hook is easy to miss or distrust.
- Cadence is fatigued. This is yet another Early Black Friday-branded send, after multiple per day on 11/19.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the sale grid and recommended products from this send. Keep the email to the appointment: hero, three-step "how it works", designer photo or room before/after, one CTA.
- 2. If the sale stays, shrink it to one strip below the CTA so the booking button is the only primary action.
- 3. Make the sweepstakes the headline of the subject and hero, and link the rules clearly from the banner.
- 4. Add social proof: a designer portrait, a review quote, or a "10,000 rooms designed" stat.
- 5. Drop the app, credit-card and Affirm modules for this send, and shorten the legal text.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Book a free design appointment, enter to win $3K`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Pick a time in-store, virtual, or at home. Sweepstakes entry included.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a fixed-width 650px table layout built from image slices. Its main confirmed defects are missing or empty alt text on nearly every image, stray PHP code inside a `<style>` block, and the recipient's email address embedded in third-party tracking pixel URLs. The header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be verified from the captured data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque tracking links:** 53 links were skipped because they are ESP click-redirects (`https://click.e.potterybarn.com/?qs=ABp7Im...`). Destinations and redirect health are unverified.
- **PII in third-party pixels:** the recipient address appears in plaintext query strings.
  - `https://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
- **Third-party ad pixels:** `secure.leadback.advertising.com/adcedge/lb?site=695501...` and `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` both load as images.
- **Cross-brand dependency:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on the West Elm domain, so a change on that domain could break this email.
- **Stale asset:** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` comes from a 2022 campaign folder.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text:** the QA check flags about 45 images with missing alt text.
  - The campaign slices `slices_01.jpg` to `slices_11.jpg` are wrapped in links but carry `alt=""`. The source confirms this, so those links have no accessible name.
  - The product images `holiday-icons-crewel-stockings-2-l.jpg`, `classic-velvet-stockings-l.jpg`, `faux-fur-ruched-throw-l.jpg`, `standard-curtain-rod-wall-bracket-l.jpg`, `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-125-l.jpg` and `westin-heavenly-bed-mattress-1325h-l.jpg` have no alt.
  - The footer slices `Email-Footer/slices_01.jpg` to `slices_18.jpg` have no alt.
  - The tracking pixels also have no alt.
- **Image-only content:** if images are blocked, the empty alt text leaves nothing readable.
- **Invalid CSS:** the second `<style>` block begins with stray PHP: `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ..."); header("Pragma: no-cache");`.
  - These lines sit in the selector position of the next rule, so `a[x-apple-data-detectors] {...}` is likely dropped. Apple's auto-linking override then won't apply.
- **Responsive rules likely unmatched:** the media query targets `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]`.
  - The visible tables have no `class="wrapper"` and the images have no `class="photo"`. They use fixed `width="650"`.
  - The mobile rules appear not to apply, so 650px images may overflow a 320px viewport.
  - This is based on the truncated source only.
- **Mixed content:** `http://` images may be blocked or rewritten.
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
- **Markup:** the doctype is XHTML 1.0 Strict, but the body uses `<center>`, `bgcolor` and `border` attributes, which are not valid under Strict. This is low impact.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The AMPscript slot comments are empty, and no unresolved `%%...%%` or `{{...}}` tokens appear in the visible source. The footer and later content were truncated, so they are unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect. Verify against the raw message.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA tool attributes this to the relay.
- **Unsubscribe link and physical address:** the source is truncated before the footer, so neither could be checked.
- **Recipient email in third-party URLs:** the address is sent to ad-network pixels (see section 2). This is a privacy and compliance concern.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Every live link is wrapped in `click.e.potterybarn.com/?qs=...`, so UTM parameters and landing pages cannot be verified from the HTML.
- The only visible plain URL is in a commented-out block (`...?brandCode=POTTERYBARNCB&marketingChannelCode=APPLYANDBUY&external_id=PB_Email_MemberBanner&cm_ite=PB_header_nonmember`). It is not live.
- The campaign's own images are hosted on the `edm.potterybarn.com` CDN, while the product images are hot-linked from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`. A change to the site's image paths would break those images.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to every image, starting with the linked slices. Linked images need meaningful alt text so the links have a name.
2. Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP lines from the `<style>` block so the `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` rule parses.
3. Add `class="wrapper"` and `class="photo"` to the tables and images, or rewrite the media queries to match the real markup.
4. Move the `http://` images to HTTPS, and remove the 2022 asset and the `westelm.com` spacer.
5. Stop putting the raw email address in `sv_sveme=` and `?email=`. Use a hashed or opaque ID, and review the `advertising.com` and `turn.com` pixels.
6. Check the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Then check the footer for an unsubscribe link and a physical address.
7. Resolve the redirect chains of the tracking links to confirm landing pages and UTM parameters.
