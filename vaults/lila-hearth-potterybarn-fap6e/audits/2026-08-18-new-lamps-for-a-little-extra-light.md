# New lamps for a little extra light✨
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-18**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's lighting-category launch — "Illuminate Every Corner / Shop Table Lamps" hero, followed by a "trendspotting" callout on petite table lamps, then a six-tile "More Lighting By Type" grid (Chandeliers, Floor Lamps, Pendants, Sconces, Picture Lights, Outdoor). It's a clean, well-shot, on-brand execution — moody warm-toned lamp photography that actually earns the subject line's "extra light" hook, which is more than most of this week's sends have managed.
- The problem is depth, not craft. After the lighting content wraps, the email tacks on curtain rods, a dining table, a rug, a bed, duvet covers, and a Halloween Shop banner before finally landing on the Design Crew sweepstakes and app/rewards boilerplate. This is the same "one clean hero, then kitchen-sink cross-sell" structure as the 8/16 "Now on Sale" and 8/15 "Beautifully coordinated beds" sends — a recognizable Pottery Barn template at this point, not a lighting-specific email once you scroll past the fold.
- Verdict: strong top, diluted bottom. If you clicked from the subject expecting lamps, you get lamps for two screens, then a different store.

## What's working

- Hero photography is genuinely strong — warm brass/glass table lamps styled on a console with clear "SHOP TABLE LAMPS" CTA directly under the headline.
- "Illuminate Every Corner" ties directly to the lighting category and subject line, unlike some recent PB sends where hero copy and subject diverge.
- The "More Lighting By Type" grid is a smart, scannable way to cover six lighting subcategories without needing six separate emails.
- "trendspotting" petite-lamps callout adds a point of view / editorial angle rather than pure product dump.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text is legible, layout holds together end to end.

## What's weak

- The email drifts off-topic fast: curtain rods, dining tables, rugs, beds, and duvet covers all appear before the email ends, none of which is "lighting."
- No visible price or discount anywhere in the lighting section — pure inspiration copy, no reason to buy today.
- The Halloween Shop banner and Design Crew sweepstakes both compete for attention against the stated lamp theme, further diluting focus.
- "SHOP ALL LIGHTING" is the only lighting CTA after the hero — the six category tiles aren't individually clickable-looking (no per-tile buttons), just labels under images.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the "Recommended Just For You" cross-sell block (curtain rods, dining table, rug, bed, duvet) entirely, or move it to a separate send — it turns a focused lighting email into a generic catalog scroll and buries the actual lamp CTA.
- 2. Add a price anchor to the hero or trendspotting section (e.g., "Table lamps from $XX") — right now there's zero purchase urgency, just mood.
- 3. Make the six lighting-type tiles individually clickable with visible "Shop" micro-links, not just category names, so the grid does more selling work.
- 4. Move "Shop All Lighting" higher, or duplicate it right after the hero, so a fast scroller doesn't have to reach the grid to convert.
