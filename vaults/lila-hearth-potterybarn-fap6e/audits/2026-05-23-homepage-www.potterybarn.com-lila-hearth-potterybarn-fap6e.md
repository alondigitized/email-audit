# Homepage snapshot · www.potterybarn.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- I came here to browse sofas and throw pillows for my lake house and instead I'm staring at a golf sweepstakes for Dad. Pottery Barn clearly knows its moment — Father's Day, U.S. Open, very on-season — but this modal completely erases the homepage for anyone who isn't shopping for a man who golfs. It looks polished, which honestly makes the mismatch sting more.

## What's working

- **Seasonality is spot-on** — a Father's Day U.S. Open sweepstakes in the third week of May is exactly the right campaign window; the brand knows its calendar.
- **$500 Pottery Barn credit as prize** — even as a sweepstakes entry, that number is large enough to catch my eye for half a second.
- **Clean modal execution** — the layout is uncluttered, the "Golf Courses" book and bar accessories photo is genuinely beautiful product photography.
- **Transparent fine print** — "No purchase necessary," state exclusions, and consent language are all surfaced, not buried. That earns a little trust.
- **Williams-Sonoma brands opt-in** — as a WS rewards member, the cross-brand email consent checkbox is at least a nod toward the ecosystem I already live in.

## What's weak

- **Full viewport hijack** — the modal blocks 100% of the actual homepage; I cannot see a single piece of furniture, bedding, or decorating content. I landed here to shop, not enter a raffle.
- **Zero relevance to me** — "For the Golf Lover" and "#1 Dad" signals exclude me immediately; I'm not shopping for a Dad, I'm shopping for my second home.
- **No rewards acknowledgment** — I'm a PB Key Rewards member and there's no balance, welcome-back moment, or member pricing visible anywhere.
- **No clear shop-now CTA** — the modal's email field is the only interactive element I can act on; if I dismiss this, I have no idea where to tap next.
- **Text-marketing consent block** — four paragraphs of SMS legal copy underneath the entry form is a lot of friction for what should be a quick, delightful homepage arrival.
- **Navigation barely legible** — the top bar shows "Baby, Kids, Teen, Dorm, Business to Business" — none of those are my categories, and everything behind the modal is invisible.

## Recommendations

- **Auto-target the modal by gender/cookie signal** — if I'm logged in as a PB Key Rewards member with a purchase history in bedding and furniture, serve me a sweepstakes or offer that reflects that, not a golf Father's Day entry.
- **Add a "No thanks, take me shopping" dismiss link** — replace the bare X close button with something like "Skip to shop" so the transition from modal to homepage feels intentional, not abandoned.
- **Surface the rewards balance inside the modal** — even a single line like "You have 1,240 Key Rewards points — shop now" would make me feel recognized and drive me deeper into the site instead of bouncing.
- **Show one lifestyle hero image at 50% opacity behind the modal** — a blurred-but-visible shot of a beautifully styled lake-house living room tells me what's waiting if I close the popup, and that alone increases my likelihood of dismissing instead of leaving.
