# Inspired by your favorite holiday movies...
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a licensed-collaboration holiday email, and it's a good one. "Holiday Movie Magic" gives three licensed worlds (National Lampoon's Christmas Vacation, Elf, Peanuts) their own full-width photo block. The tablescape and pillow photography is charming and gives the brand a story beyond a discount. The hero is tidy and the nostalgia hook is the right angle for November.
- The offer is the weak part. "The Fall Sale: Up to 70% off" sits in a thin green banner above the fold, but the real hero below is pure theme with no price. Each movie block links to "Shop Now" with no product or price attached. The long "Recommended Just For You" grid (stockings, dining table, curtain rod, quilt, rug) and the design-services banner dilute the movie story. A subscriber who has seen the Nov 8 furniture and bedding sales will read this as a fourth sale-adjacent send in a week.
- Verdict: strong creative concept and brand voice, but the offer is buried and the CTAs are generic. It will get clicks from nostalgia-driven holiday shoppers, not from deal hunters.

## What's working

- The three movie blocks (Griswold's plates, Elf pillows, Peanuts dinnerware) are distinct, visual, and emotionally specific.
- The italic taglines ("All for a fun, old-fashioned family Christmas") fit the brand voice and the season.
- The sale banner at the top puts "Up to 70% off" in view immediately.
- The layout is clean, with generous whitespace and no visible broken images or overlapping text.
- The "Earn 10% Back in Rewards" and Affirm blocks give loyalty and financing options without crowding the hero.

## What's weak

- The 70% offer is in a thin banner, then disappears. The movie blocks carry no price or discount.
- All three "Shop Now" links are generic, and the large "Explore the Christmas Shop" button is just as vague.
- The "Recommended Just For You" grid dilutes the theme. The dining table, curtain rod and rug have nothing to do with holiday movies.
- Stacking the design services banner, app download, rewards and Affirm makes the email too long. The footer fine print is heavy.
- It repeats earlier sale beats. The sale banner echoes the Nov 8 "Up to 60% off furniture" send, and the cadence is dense.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move a concrete offer into the hero ("Movie-themed décor, up to 70% off") and put a price or "from $" under each movie block.
- 2. Replace "Shop Now" with specific CTAs: "Shop Elf Pillows," "Shop Griswold Dinnerware," "Shop Peanuts Dishes."
- 3. Make the "Recommended" grid holiday-only (stockings, throws, tableware) and cut the unrelated furniture and hardware.
- 4. Cut the app, Affirm and design-services modules, or move them to the footer, to shorten the scroll.
- 5. Add a deadline, since the banner doesn't show one.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Elf pillows, Griswold plates & Charlie Brown dishes. Up to 70% off.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit: Pottery Barn, "Inspired by your favorite holiday movies..."

### 1. Technical Summary
Table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with no confirmed blocking defects. It has pervasive missing alt text on slice images, three non-HTTPS image sources, a leaked PHP snippet inside a `<style>` block, and recipient email addresses in plaintext query strings. The header and authentication checks are inconclusive because of the relay, and 47 tracking links went unprobed.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 47 click-redirect links (e.g. `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7ImQi...`) were skipped by the QA probe. Destination, status and redirect chain are unverified. Their opaque `qs=` payloads also hide any UTM parameters from inspection.
- **Non-HTTPS images (3):**
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` (open pixel)
  - These may be blocked or rewritten by clients that enforce HTTPS. A blocked open pixel also undercounts opens.
- **Stale or shared assets:** a 2022 path (`/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/`) and a West Elm host (`https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`) are referenced from a Pottery Barn send. That is cross-brand asset coupling, so an outage or removal on that host would break the spacer.
- **Multiple tracking pixels:** `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1`, `svopn.potterybarn.com`, `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/63c7b19b-...` and `coreemail.html?email=...` all load as images. Several third-party calls add latency and privacy surface.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 40 images are flagged. This includes:
  - the hero `H26_TOP_FallSale.jpg`
  - `F26_Collabs_B11_HolidayXMASCollabRoundUp/slices_01–06.jpg`
  - all product images, e.g. `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`
  - `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`
  - With images off or a screen reader, the sliced content and footer links are unreadable.
- **Empty `<title></title>`:** this is a minor accessibility gap.
- **No `lang` attribute** on `<html xmlns=...>`.
- **Viewport:** `maximum-scale=1` in `<meta name="viewport">` disables pinch-zoom on some clients, which is an accessibility concern.
- **Leaked server-side code:** `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store..."); header("Pragma: no-cache");` sits inside the second `<style>` block. Browsers ignore it, but a CSS parser will treat it as garbage and may drop the rule that follows it (`html { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; ... }` precedes it, so that rule is fine).
- **Unusual media-query syntax:** `table[class="wrapper"]` / `img[class="photo"]` attribute selectors only work if those classes exist as the sole class value. Behavior is unconfirmed from the truncated source.
- **Global `img {max-width: 650px}` / `100%!important`** rules are applied by breakpoint to all images, including 1×1 pixels and spacers.
- **Very long hidden preheader padding** uses `&zwnj;`-style characters and soft hyphens. This is a standard technique and is fine.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) found in the visible source or QA output.
- **PII in URLs:** the recipient's address is placed in plaintext query strings:
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `...&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - This exposes the address in proxy, CDN and server logs, and in referrers if the pixel is intercepted. `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` mitigates the referrer leak only in viewers that honor it.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both are not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **inconclusive**. Verify against the raw message at the origin. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. This is likely a relay artifact. The From domain `e.potterybarn.com` is aligned with the click domain `click.e.potterybarn.com`.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** these could not be confirmed because the HTML was truncated before the footer. The footer is built from 18 image slices with no alt text. If the unsubscribe and address text exist only as image slices, they are invisible when images are blocked and fail the plain-text requirement.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters could not be verified. Tracked links are redirect-wrapped (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`) and were not probed.
- Product images load from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`, which shows direct dependence on live site assets. They will break if SKUs are retired. Two filenames, `open-box-cozy-cloud-...` and `holiday-icons-crewel-stockings-2-l.jpg`, suggest product/promo items that may have changed availability.
- Landing-page alignment is not assessable from the available data.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content and slice images. Use `alt=""` plus `role="presentation"` on spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Change the three `http://` image sources to `https://`. Prioritize the `svopn` open pixel to avoid undercounted opens.
3. Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP statements from the `<style>` block.
4. Remove recipient emails from query strings, or replace them with a hashed or opaque ID. Move `coreemail.html?email=` to a tokenized identifier.
5. Add `lang="en"` and a meaningful `<title>`. Drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
6. Replace the `edm.westelm.com` spacer and 2022-era assets with Pottery Barn-hosted current assets.
7. Re-run the header checks against the raw `.eml` (not the relay copy) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
8. Resolve the redirect chains of the 47 tracking links with a tracking-aware probe, and confirm UTM and landing-page parity.
9. Extend the QA to cover the footer, which the truncated source did not reach, to confirm the unsubscribe link and postal address exist as live text.
