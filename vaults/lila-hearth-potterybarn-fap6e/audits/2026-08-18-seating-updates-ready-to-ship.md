# Seating updates, ready to ship
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-18**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's "ready-to-ship" seating send — a plaid armchair hero in a moody library setting under "Ready-to-Ship Seating / Less waiting, more unwinding." It's a legitimate, differentiated angle for the category: the copy leads with a real customer pain point (long furniture lead times) rather than generic "shop now" filler, and the four-tile category grid (Armchairs, Sofas, Sectionals, Swivel/Leather) gives a clear, scannable path into in-stock inventory.
- Where it falls apart is length and focus. After the strong hero and a legitimate "shop all in-stock seating" CTA, the email keeps going for another dozen screens — round-out-the-room categories, a "Recommended Just For You" grid unrelated to seating (curtain rods, dining tables, rugs, bedding, curtains), a full Halloween Shop promo, and a sweepstakes block, before finally landing on app/rewards/financing footers. The seating message that earns the open gets diluted fast.
- Solid concept, bloated execution. Trim it to seating and this is a 7; as sent, it's a 6.

## What's working

- Hero headline "Ready-to-Ship Seating" paired with "Less waiting, more unwinding" directly answers a real furniture-buying anxiety (long lead times), which is more compelling than a generic sale banner.
- "Delivered in just 1-4 weeks" gives a concrete, credible timeframe — rare specificity for a lifestyle brand email.
- The four-category grid (Armchairs, Sofas, Sectionals, Swivel/Leather) with clean product photography makes it easy to jump straight to a subcategory.
- "SHOP ALL IN-STOCK SEATING" is a clear, unambiguous mid-email CTA that reinforces the hero's promise.

## What's weak

- No price or discount anywhere in the seating section — this is an inventory/availability message, not an offer, so it scores weak on urgency/value.
- The email sprawls well past its stated subject: "Recommended Just For You" pushes curtains, dining tables, rugs, and bedding — none of which are seating — undermining the "ready to ship" focus.
- The Halloween Shop and Design Crew Sweepstakes blocks are unrelated cross-promotions stacked onto an already-long email, which is exactly the "lead-gen trap" mechanic flagged in yesterday's "$1500" sweepstakes send — same brand reusing the same gimmick two days running.
- No urgency or scarcity signal ("limited stock," "selling fast") to reinforce the "ready-to-ship" hook, even though that would fit naturally.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after "SHOP ALL IN-STOCK SEATING" — drop the unrelated "Recommended Just For You," Halloween Shop, and Sweepstakes blocks entirely, or move them to a separate send. A seating-lead-time message diluted by curtains and a sweepstakes will underperform a tight, single-topic email.
- 2. Add a stock/urgency cue near the hero — e.g., "Limited quantities, ships this week" — to convert the availability hook into urgency.
- 3. Surface a price anchor on at least the hero chair ("From $XXX") so there's a concrete reason to click beyond curiosity.
- 4. Swap "Round Out The Room" into seating-adjacent-only items (throws, ottomans, accent tables) instead of leaving it generic, if kept at all.
- **Subject Alt A:** `In-stock seating, no wait required`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your new chair, home in 1-4 weeks`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Armchairs, sofas & sectionals ready to ship now`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Skip the backorder — shop seating in stock today`

# Technical Audit: Pottery Barn — "Seating updates, ready to ship"

**1. Technical Summary**
Standard PB/EDM legacy template with heavy MSO/VML conditional markup; passes basic structural checks but fails several deliverability and accessibility hygiene rules — 3 mixed-content (HTTP) image sources, near-universal missing `alt` attributes, and no visible unsubscribe/authentication header data in the captured payload.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 59 tracking/redirect links (`click.e.potterybarn.com`, `clickcp.potterybarn.com`) were skipped by the HTTP prober — expected for click-redirect domains, not a defect, but means link destinations are unverified in this pass.
- Two open-tracking pixels present: `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` and `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1`. The svopn pixel fires over **plain HTTP**, exposing the recipient email address (`sv_sveme=`) in a cleartext query string — a PII-over-HTTP leak.
- Personalized email address is also embedded in a clear URL param on the customer-service image link: `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` (HTTPS here, so lower risk, but still logs PII in URL/referrer chains).

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- 44 of ~46 images are missing `alt` text — affects every content image (product shots, banners, footer slices, tracking pixels). Screen readers and images-off clients get no fallback text.
- 3 images load over `http://` instead of `https://`: `edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`, `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`. Mixed content on an otherwise-HTTPS mail template can trigger client-side blocking/broken-image icons.
- The `<style>` block includes literal PHP `header(...)` calls (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');` etc.) sitting inside a CSS `<style>` tag — dead/inert code that does nothing in an email client but suggests a copy-paste error from a server-side asset script into the template source.
- No `<img>` `width`/`height` mismatches or broken conditional blocks observed in the truncated source; VML/MSO fallbacks and Outlook `xml` blocks are present and well-formed.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- No unresolved merge-tag placeholders (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) found in the truncated source.
- Recipient identifier is populated correctly across tracking/link URLs (`lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`), confirming personalization merge succeeded — but see §2 for the plaintext-over-HTTP exposure this creates.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` header: not found. Could be a relay-capture gap (AgentMail) rather than a true absence — cannot confirm from HTML source alone since one-click unsubscribe is header-based, not body-based.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058): not found — one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC): not found — authentication status of this send is unknown from available data.
- No unsubscribe link/footer visible in the truncated HTML source provided; full source would need to be reviewed to confirm a body-level unsubscribe link and physical mailing address (CAN-SPAM requires both regardless of header state).

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Cannot assess: no UTM parameters or resolved landing-page URLs are visible in the truncated source — links use the `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirector, which was skipped by the prober. Full-source review needed to verify UTM tagging and destination-page alignment for the "Quick Ship Seating" campaign.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images (product/banner images), or empty `alt=""` for purely decorative/tracking assets, to satisfy accessibility and images-off rendering.
2. Migrate the 3 HTTP image sources (`edm.westelm.com`, `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey`, `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/...`) to HTTPS to avoid mixed-content blocking.
3. Move the `sv_sveme` open-tracking pixel to HTTPS to stop transmitting recipient email addresses in cleartext.
