# Take an EXTRA 20% OFF select styles
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-28**
## Executive summary

- This is the same oxblood "Extra 20% off Select Styles / Plus savings up to 70% off" banner Pottery Barn has led with all week, and the subject line repeats it. The Oct 27 "Don't forget your EXTRA 20% OFF…" and Oct 26 "In case you missed it: EXTRA 20% OFF select styles" sends opened the same way. Only the body changes, and here it's a lighting story. The "All Is Bright" table-lamp hero is moody and well shot, and the "a little more glow" copy is warm. But the subject promises a discount and the hero sells lamps, so the two don't connect.
- Below the lighting block the email turns into a catalog. It has a "Recommended Just For You" grid, a Christmas Shop banner, Design Crew, an app promo, a credit-card pitch and Affirm. That's at least five competing asks after the hero. The 20% offer sits in a banner with "Exclusions apply. Prices as marked." under it, with no deadline and no sign of which styles qualify.
- Verdict: a polished but undifferentiated send, and probably the eleventh near-identical "Extra 20%" message in about five days. Beyond fatigue, nothing sets it apart from the rest of the week.

## What's working

- The "All Is Bright" lamp hero is atmospheric and seasonal, and its SHOP TABLE LAMPS button is clear.
- The lighting block is well organized. Floor lamps, chandeliers, sconces and pendants each get a tile, and a SHOP ALL LIGHTING button closes the block.
- The 20% and "up to 70%" offer is the first thing you see, in a high-contrast banner with a SHOP ALL SALE button.
- The Christmas Shop banner is on-season and breaks up the page with a bold red block.
- The render is clean: no broken images or overlaps, and the brand voice is consistent.

## What's weak

- The offer has no deadline and no way to tell which styles are "select". The fine print reads "Exclusions apply. Prices as marked."
- The subject sells the discount and the hero sells lamps, so the click path is muddled.
- The body is long, and the app, rewards card, Affirm and brand-family footers all dilute the main message.
- "Recommended Just For You" shows a dining table, curtain rod, rug, bed and stockings. It looks generic, not personalized, and it has no prices or review stars.
- The legal block at the bottom is dense, tiny and hard to read.

## Recommendations

- 1. Give the email one job: the lighting story. Put an offer line such as "20% off lamps through Sunday" under the hero and cut the app, credit-card and Affirm modules.
- 2. Add a real deadline to the banner, because "Extra 20% off" has now run for a week with no end date.
- 3. Show sale prices and a rating on the recommended grid so the "Just For You" tiles earn a click.
- 4. Pause the identical banner for a send or two. The Oct 27 "Free design help!" email scored well partly because it broke the pattern.
- 5. Make the subject name the product so it matches the hero.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Lamps for the long nights: 20% off ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Warm up your room: lighting + extra 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Table lamps, sconces and pendants, now with extra savings.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our petite lamps add glow to any corner. Plus savings up to 70%.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a standard table layout with a valid logo alt, but it has widespread missing alt text, three non-HTTPS image sources, an unencoded space in image URLs, and the recipient email address in plaintext tracking-pixel URLs. Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified because the relay didn't capture those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque tracking links:** 49 links go through click-redirect domains (e.g. `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...`), so the HTTP probe skipped them. Destination validity can't be confirmed.
- **Unencoded space in image URLs:** `https://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2026/US/1028_WED_AM_Table LampsAndMoreLighting/slices_01.jpg` (slices_01 to 07). A raw space in the path is invalid per RFC 3986. Most clients encode it, but strict parsers or proxies may fail to load the image.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is served from a West Elm host in a Pottery Barn email. It's a shared-template leftover and a minor dependency risk.
- **Pixels without alt:** these are flagged only because `alt` is absent:
  - `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&...`
  - `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/3cc6c89d-...`
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=...`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 40 images have no `alt`, per QA. Examples:
  - Hero and promo banners: `H26_TOP_Extra20CLR.jpg`, `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg`, `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`.
  - Product images: `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`, `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg`, `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`.
  - Image-sliced sections: `Table LampsAndMoreLighting/slices_01–07.jpg` and `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`.
  - With images off or a screen reader, the offer and CTAs are unavailable, because they appear to be image-only.
  - Tracking pixels and spacers should use `alt=""` rather than omit the attribute.
- **HTTP image sources (3):**
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=...`
  - These may be blocked or trigger mixed-content warnings.
- **Empty `<title></title>`:** it's present in the head.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" ...>` has no `lang`, which affects screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Stray PHP in CSS:** the last `<style>` block contains `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ...")` etc. It's invalid CSS. It sits after the valid rules, so impact is limited, but it indicates a template leak.
- **Layout tables:** only some have `role="presentation"`. The outer wrapper tables don't.
- **Web font:** the Google Fonts `<link>` in `<head>` is ignored by many clients, so it falls back to system fonts.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source, and the recipient email is populated in the tracking URLs. The source was truncated, so lower sections weren't reviewed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** neither was found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed, not a confirmed sender failure. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The `From` domain `e.potterybarn.com` can't be validated from the data.
- **Footer content:** the in-body unsubscribe link and postal address are outside the truncated HTML, so they can't be confirmed.
- **Plaintext recipient address in URLs:**
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - The second is sent over cleartext HTTP, which exposes the address in transit and in logs.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
UTM parameters and landing pages can't be verified. All 49 tracked links are wrapped in `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirects (encoded `qs=` payload) that weren't resolved. Product images point to `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`, which is consistent with the brand domain. No other issues are confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, especially the banners, product tiles and sliced sections. Use `alt=""` for pixels and spacers.
2. Switch the three `http://` image sources to `https://`, especially the `svopn` pixel.
3. URL-encode the space in `1028_WED_AM_Table LampsAndMoreLighting` (or rename the folder).
4. Stop passing the plaintext recipient email in `coreemail.html` and `sv_sveme`. Use a hashed or opaque ID instead.
5. Set `<title>` and `<html lang="en">`. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
6. Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP text from the `<style>` block.
7. For our pipeline:
   - Capture raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) before the relay strips them, so compliance checks stop returning "unknown".
   - Resolve redirect chains on `click.e.potterybarn.com` links to audit UTMs and landing pages.
   - Stop counting tracking pixels as missing-alt content images in QA.
