# Happening now! EXTRA 20% OFF select styles + Storage that’s meant to be seen
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- This is the same "Extra 20% off Select Styles" oxblood banner Pottery Barn has sent all week (Oct 26–30), now with a storage editorial under it. The "Everything in Its Place" hero is well composed. The console, mirror and baskets are on-theme for hosting season, and the subject's "Storage that's meant to be seen" is the best line in the send. But the banner owns the top of the email and the storage story only gets about half the body.
- After the storage section the email runs out of focus. A "Recommended Just For You" grid mixes curtain hardware, dining tables, a pendant, a rug and a mattress. Below that sit Christmas Shop, Design Services, app, rewards card and Affirm blocks. Each is fine alone, but together they make a catalog that dilutes the one idea the subject sold. "Happening now!" is also weak urgency when the same sale has been "happening" for six days.
- Judgement: competent and on-brand, but it's a fatigue send. It's the sixth near-identical promo this week, and the storage angle is the only differentiator. A middling 5.

## What's working

- The "Everything in Its Place" hero is warm, aspirational and matches the subject.
- The storage tiles (Bookcases, Baskets, Shelving, Buffets & Cabinets) are specific and easy to scan, with one clear "Shop All Storage & Organization" button.
- The "More Ways to Stay Organized" links (Jewelry & Beauty, Office, Luggage & Travel, Laundry & Closet) widen the storage theme sensibly.
- The oxblood banner is bold and has a visible "Shop All Sale" button, so the offer is clear.
- The Christmas Shop tile gives a seasonal pivot that fits the calendar.

## What's weak

- The sale banner is the same one as the Oct 26–30 sends, which feels stale. Sale and editorial also compete for the hero position.
- "Exclusions apply. Prices as marked." and the dense legal block make the 20% feel conditional. "Select styles" is vague, and no product shown has a visible price or discount.
- The "Recommended Just For You" grid has no prices, ratings or reviews. It reads as generic, not personalized.
- The lower half stacks Christmas, Design Services, app, rewards card and Affirm, which pulls attention away from storage.
- "Happening now!" doesn't carry real urgency. No end date is visible.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the footer-stack modules down to one. Keep storage, with Christmas as a single secondary tile.
- 2. Put prices on the storage tiles, for example "Bookcases from $X", and give the recommended grid prices and star ratings.
- 3. State an end date on the banner ("Ends Sunday") and drop the "Happening now!" opener.
- 4. Vary the top banner. Rotate the creative, or fold the 20% into the storage hero so the email has one message.
- 5. Make the CTAs specific: "Shop Bookcases" instead of generic "Shop All" buttons.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Bookcases, baskets and console tables, plus extra 20% off select styles through Sunday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based layout with ESP click-wrapping (`click.e.potterybarn.com`). The main issues are widespread missing image alt text, a stray PHP snippet inside a `<style>` block, three non-HTTPS images, and the recipient's email address in cleartext in tracking and image URLs. The header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified from the data supplied.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links not verified:** The QA run skipped 55 tracking links (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`) because they're redirect domains. Destination validity and redirect chains are unconfirmed.
- **Cleartext HTTP tracking pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`. The plain-HTTP request exposes the recipient address and may be blocked or rewritten by clients.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` is an ad-network pixel (Turn). A second open pixel is at `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/7f306768-...?mi_cid=...`.
- **Recipient email in URL query strings:** `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` (loaded as an `<img>`) and the `sv_sveme=` parameter above. This is a PII-in-URL exposure that appears in server and proxy logs.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (about 50 images):**
  - Hero banners: `H26_TOP_Extra20CLR.jpg`, `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg`, `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`.
  - Sliced content: `H26_Furn_B3_StorageAcrosstheHome/slices_01–13.jpg`.
  - Footer slices: `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`.
  - Product images, for example `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg` and `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg`.

  With images off or in a screen reader, the offer and navigation content would be unavailable.
- **Non-HTTPS images (3):**
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - the `svopn` pixel above
- **Invalid code in `<style>`:** The second `<style>` block contains PHP text (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store...` ). This is not CSS. It can corrupt parsing of the rules that follow in some clients.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns=...>` has none, which affects screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom.
- **Image-heavy sliced layout:** The plain-text part is more than 70% URLs (10,580 of 14,808 characters), so the text alternative is poor.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `spacer_0.gif` loads from `edm.westelm.com`, so a West Elm host failure would affect this email.
- **Some layout tables lack `role="presentation"`**, for example the `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0">` spacer table. The source is truncated, so the full extent is unknown.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source, and the recipient address is populated in the URLs. The PII-in-URL concern is covered under section 2.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA notes AgentMail may strip these, so this is unconfirmed. If they are truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- **Authentication-Results absent.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `e.potterybarn.com` can't be validated from this data.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address:** Neither appears in the truncated source, so they can't be confirmed or denied.
- **`<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">`** is harmless. It has no compliance impact.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters:** None are visible. Links use opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=` tokens, and the one `potterybarn.com` URL seen (`coreemail.html?email=...`) carries no UTMs. The campaign tags may be applied at redirect, but that can't be verified because the probe was skipped.
- **Landing page alignment:** Unverifiable for the same reason. This covers the "Extra 20% off" and storage CTAs and whether the offer appears on the landing pages.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images. Use `alt=""` for spacers and pixels, and put the offer text (for example "Extra 20% off select styles") in the hero banner alt.
2. Remove the PHP `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block.
3. Move the three `http://` images, including the `svopn` pixel, to HTTPS.
4. Stop putting the raw email address in image and pixel query strings. Use hashed or opaque IDs instead.
5. Add `<title>` and `lang="en"`, and remove `maximum-scale=1`.
6. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results from the raw message headers. If the relay is stripping them, fix the capture first.
7. Resolve the 55 click-tracked links with a redirect-following probe. That would verify destinations, UTMs, and landing-page alignment.
8. Host the spacer image on the sender's own domain instead of `edm.westelm.com`.
