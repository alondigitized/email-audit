# So thankful for you ❤️ Enjoy up to 70% off
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the same burgundy "Black Friday, up to 70% off" Pottery Barn template that has run since 11/16. The only new things are a "So thankful for you ❤️" subject and a "New deals added" kicker above the headline. It's the fourth or fifth send in 48 hours, following "NEW BLACK FRIDAY DEALS" and "First look 📣 NEW Black Friday deals" earlier today. The creative is clean and the offer is clear. A subscriber has seen this hero many times by now.
- The hero does its job: one big "70% off" and one "Shop Now" button. The rest of the email is a long scroll with a dense category grid, a store locator, six recommended products, a family-of-brands block, an app promo, a credit card pitch, Affirm, and a wall of legal copy. The gratitude angle in the subject never shows up in the body. Nothing in the render says "thankful," so the subject and the creative don't match.
- Judgement: a competent, repetitive send. It isn't broken, but it adds no news beyond the subject line, and the cadence is its biggest weakness.

## What's working

- The hero has a strong hierarchy: a huge "70% off" in a burgundy block, with a single "Shop Now" button.
- The category button grid (Furniture, Bedding, Rugs, and so on) gives fast routing for shoppers who already know what they want.
- The "In-Store Savings" and "Find Your Store" block gives omnichannel shoppers a useful second path.
- "Recommended Just For You" shows named products (Classic Velvet Stockings, Faux Fur Ruched Throw), which gives specific things to click.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The subject promises gratitude, but the body has no thank-you messaging. The hero only says "New Deals Added."
- This is the same hero as the 11/16–11/26 sends, so it feels like the fourth or fifth identical promo this week.
- The "Recommended Just For You" products show no prices or sale callouts, so nothing ties them to the 70% off.
- The email is very long. The brand-family links, app promo, credit card, Affirm, and dense fine print dilute the hero.
- "Up to 70% off" has no visible exclusions or deadline near the hero, and the legal copy is buried at the bottom. There is no urgency such as "ends Monday."

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the creative match the subject. Add a short Thanksgiving thank-you line or a one-day "thank you" offer in the hero, so the email gives a reason for the new send.
- 2. Cut the cadence. Suppress or consolidate repeat Black Friday sends to engaged subscribers instead of mailing five near-identical versions in 48 hours.
- 3. Add prices and "was/now" or "% off" tags to the recommended products so the discount is visible on the items.
- 4. Add a deadline such as "Ends Monday 11/30" next to the hero button.
- 5. Shorten the body by removing the app, card, and Affirm blocks from promo sends, or moving them below a tighter footer.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, image-sliced Salesforce Marketing Cloud send (65 `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirects) with no unresolved merge tokens. Its main technical problems are accessibility (about 50 images with no alt text, including all promo and footer slices), invalid CSS, mobile-responsive rules that match nothing in the visible source, and compliance headers that couldn't be confirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** All 65 tracking links (`https://click.e.potterybarn.com/?qs=ABp7Im...`) were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destinations and redirect health are unverified.
- **Mixed content:** Two images use `http://`: `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`. Clients may block or rewrite them. The second path is dated 2022, which suggests a stale reused asset.
- **Cross-brand asset:** The spacer `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on a West Elm domain. It adds a third-party dependency, and a failure there would be silent.
- **Third-party pixels:** `secure.leadback.advertising.com/adcedge/lb?...` and `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` are ad-network pixels. The QA flagged them as "missing alt text", but that is a false positive, because they are tracking beacons, not content images.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text:** About 50 images have missing or empty alt text. This covers every hero slice (`1126_THU_PM_BF_9GridHeroLinkfarm/slices_01–12.jpg`, several with explicit `alt=""`), the bottom banners, product images such as `classic-velvet-stockings-l.jpg`, and all 18 `Email-Footer/slices_*.jpg`.
  - Each slice sits alone inside an `<a>`, so those links have no accessible name.
  - With images blocked, the body and footer render blank.
  - Only the header logo has alt text (`alt="POTTERY BARN"`).
- **Invalid CSS in `<style>`:** The second `<style>` block begins with stray PHP, `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ...")`. It sits directly before `a[x-apple-data-detectors] {...}`. A CSS parser reads it as part of that rule's selector, so the rule is probably dropped.
- **Mobile rules that match nothing:** The media query targets `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]`. Neither class appears on any table or image in the visible source. The tables are fixed at `width="650"` and the images at `width="650"` or `325`, so the layout probably won't scale on mobile. This is based on the truncated HTML, so the unseen portion could differ.
- **Document structure:**
  - There is no `lang` attribute on `<html>` and no `<title>`.
  - The ampScript comments sit before the XHTML 1.0 Strict `<!DOCTYPE>`.
  - Strict-invalid markup is used: `<center>`, `bgcolor`, `border`.
- **Web font:** The Google Fonts `<link>` (Montserrat) is stripped by Gmail and several other clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens or ampScript fragments are visible in the HTML. The `begin/end dynamic ampScript slot` comments are empty and harmless. The recipient address is embedded in plain text in query strings:
- `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
- `https://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`

This puts raw PII in URLs, where it can be logged. Both URLs are first-party.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** Both are absent from the captured headers. The QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed, not a confirmed violation**. These headers matter for Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is likely a relay artifact.
- **Unsubscribe link and postal address:** The truncated source doesn't show them. The footer is 18 image slices with no alt text, so any unsubscribe or address text is probably baked into images. I can't confirm that a live text unsubscribe link exists.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
This can't be assessed. The click URLs are opaque `qs=` tokens with no visible UTM parameters, and the probe skipped them, so the landing pages and campaign parameters are unknown. The only direct `potterybarn.com` URL with a tracking parameter (`cm_ite=PB_header_nonmember`) is inside an HTML comment and isn't live. The hero folder name `1126_THU_PM_BF` matches the send date.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every linked image. Use `alt=""` only on spacers and pixels, and add real text for the footer unsubscribe and address.
2. Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP lines from the `<style>` block so the `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` rule applies.
3. Put `class="wrapper"` and `class="photo"` on the tables and images, or rewrite the media query. Add `max-width:100%` and `height:auto` on images.
4. Switch the two `http://` image sources to `https://`, and replace the 2022 banner and the `westelm.com` spacer with current, same-brand assets.
5. Add `lang="en"` and a `<title>`.
6. Move the recipient email out of URL query strings, or use an opaque ID.
7. Confirm the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results against the raw message at the original receiving MTA, not through the relay.
8. Resolve the 65 click URLs with a redirect-following check to verify the destinations and UTM or campaign parameters.
