# EARLY BLACK FRIDAY DEALS + Up to 70% off for your cozy home
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's standard Early Black Friday template, sent again. The burgundy hero reads "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY / up to 70% off" with a white SHOP NOW button. It is clean, and the eye lands on it first. The subject line, "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY DEALS + Up to 70% off for your cozy home," also matches the hero well.
- Below the hero, the email loses focus. After a six-tile product grid and a SHOP ALL SALE button, it adds a category link block, a "Recommended just for you" grid, a Christmas Shop banner, a $3,000 sweepstakes, an app promo, a credit card pitch and Affirm financing. That is eight or more competing messages, and the offer is diluted well before the footer. A subscriber who got the bedding-only send earlier today (2026-11-15, "Early Black Friday bedding deals + FREE SHIPPING") would see this as a step back to the generic version.
- The cadence is the biggest problem. This is at least the fifth near-identical burgundy "up to 70% off" send in three days, going back to "Just in! NEW Early Black Friday deals" on 11-13. The creative is competent, but fatigue is real.

## What's working

- The burgundy hero with a large "70%" and a white SHOP NOW button is unambiguous and hard to miss.
- The offer is specific and consistent: "up to 70% off" appears in both the subject and the hero.
- The product tiles name individual items (Reeva Handwoven Rug, Remmy Swivel Chair), so the click goes to something concrete.
- The "More Early Black Friday Deals" category block (Bedding, Furniture, Sofas & Chairs and so on) gives easy routes into a category.
- The seasonal Christmas Shop banner (up to 50% off) fits the moment.

## What's weak

- The lower half is overloaded. The sweepstakes, app download, credit card and Affirm sections each pull attention away from the sale.
- The hero has no deadline, so there is no credible urgency despite the "early" framing.
- The "cozy home" theme in the subject isn't carried into the hero or the tiles, so the personalization is weak.
- The tiles have no prices or discounts. The "up to 70%" headline is never tied to a real product price.
- The tiles show no reviews or social proof, and the fine print at the bottom is long and dense.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the lower modules (sweepstakes, app, card, Affirm) from sale sends, or move them to a separate cadence. Keep hero, tiles, categories and the Christmas banner.
- 2. Show real price and discount on each tile ("$399 → $199") to make the 70% concrete.
- 3. Add a deadline to the hero, such as "Ends Sunday night."
- 4. Vary the creative day to day. Lead with one category, as the bedding send did, rather than repeating the generic hero.
- 5. Add star ratings to the top products.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Rugs, chairs & throws: up to 70% off ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Cozy-home picks, now up to 70% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Handwoven rugs, swivel chairs and holiday bedding, marked down for early Black Friday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our biggest savings of the season, plus the Christmas Shop at up to 50% off.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Pottery Barn, "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY DEALS + Up to 70% off for your cozy home"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with MSO/VML conditionals. It is structurally sound, but it has widespread missing `alt` text on sliced images, an empty `<title>`, a zoom-blocking viewport tag, and the recipient's email address in plain-text tracking-pixel URLs. The truncated HTML and the relay-stripped headers mean unsubscribe and authentication could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All links are opaque redirects.** The 60 tracking links skipped by the probe are on click-redirect domains, e.g. `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...`. Destinations can't be verified from source, so landing-page health is unconfirmed rather than failing.
- **Three tracking pixels exist:**
  - `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...`
  - `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/ee46844f-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...`
- **One pixel is plain HTTP.** The `svopn.potterybarn.com` open pixel is `http://`, so some clients will block it and the open will go unrecorded.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 12,041 of 17,044 characters (70.6%) are URLs, which is consistent with long tracking redirects inflating the text alternative. This is a spam-filter risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` is widespread.** About 45 content images have no `alt`:
  - Hero slices: `H26_Sale_EBF_MinkStudio/slices_01–10.jpg`
  - Product tiles: `faux-fur-ruched-throw-l.jpg`, `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-125-l.jpg`, and others
  - Banners: `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop_50off.jpg`, `H26_Bottom_DesignCrewSweeps_01/02.jpg`
  - Footer: `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`

  With images blocked, or in a screen reader, the offer and CTAs are unreadable. The header logo is the one image with alt (`alt="POTTERY BARN"`).
- **Empty `<title>`.** The markup has `<title></title>`.
- **Zoom is disabled.** The viewport is `width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1`, which blocks pinch-zoom and fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Mixed-content images.** Two content images use `http://`: `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `.../2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`. Both may be blocked.
- **Stray code in the stylesheet.** The second `<style>` block contains leaked PHP text: `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store...")`. It is invalid CSS. It may break parsing of the rules that follow it in some clients.
- **Reliance on slice images.** The hero and footer are image slices, `.mobile-hide` and `.showmobile` carry fixed `width:414px`, and `max-width:650px` is applied only at `min-width:700px`. Rendering is fragile when images are off.
- **Web font is ineffective.** `fonts.googleapis.com` Montserrat is loaded via `<link>`, which most clients (Gmail, Outlook) strip, so the fallback stack applies.
- **Stripped-attribute selectors.** `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` only match if those exact classes exist on the elements. They are not visible in the truncated source.
- **Cross-brand asset.** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a spacer served from the West Elm domain, with no `alt`. It is harmless but a dependency on another brand's host.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved tokens** (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `[[...]]`) are visible in the provided source.
- **The recipient's email appears in plain text in URLs:**
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?...&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`

  The `svopn` request is over HTTP, so the address is exposed in transit and in proxy/server logs. This is a privacy and compliance concern.
- **Conflicting meta tags.** `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` and `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` are present. Both are ignored by nearly all email clients, so they offer no real protection.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may have stripped them, so this is unconfirmed. If they are really absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules (2024+) are not met.
- **`Authentication-Results` is absent.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC can't be assessed because the relay removed it. This is unverifiable, not a confirmed failure.
- **Footer unsubscribe and postal address.** These are not visible in the truncated HTML, so they can't be confirmed. The footer is image slices (`Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`) with no alt text. If the unsubscribe text and address are baked into images, they are inaccessible and may fail CAN-SPAM's clear-and-conspicuous requirement. Verify against the full source.
- **From domain.** `PotteryBarn@e.potterybarn.com` is on a dedicated subdomain, which is good for alignment but not verifiable without headers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters are visible.** Every link is wrapped in an opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` token, so campaign attribution parameters can't be checked, and no `utm_*` parameters appear on the product or `coreemail.html` URLs.
- **Landing alignment is unverifiable.** Probes were skipped (60 links), so there is no confirmation that "Up to 70% off" or "Early Black Friday" destinations resolve or match the offer.
- **Product image hosts.** Product images load from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`, the main site CDN, so they are exposed to site-side changes and caching.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all slice and product images. Use `alt=""` for spacers and pixels, and put the offer text and CTAs in `alt`.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag and fill in `<title>`.
3. Remove the leaked PHP `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block.
4. Switch `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `slices_02.jpg` and the `svopn.potterybarn.com` pixel to HTTPS.
5. Stop passing the plain-text email address in URL query strings (`coreemail.html?email=`, `sv_sveme=`). Use a hashed or opaque ID.
6. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC against raw headers captured before the relay. If unconfirmed, don't treat the warnings as findings.
7. Confirm the footer contains a live-text unsubscribe link and a physical postal address, rather than image-only slices.
8. Resolve a sample of the redirected links to check landing-page status and for `utm_*` presence, since the probe skipped all 60.
9. Reduce the URL share of the plain-text alternative by using shorter tracking links or a curated text version.
