# Love pattern? This bedding is for you
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a well-built, category-led bedding send. "Sleeping Patterns" is a clear editorial hero with a "Quilts ship free" pill. Three named hero products follow: Greer Botanical Quilt, Cordelia Toile Reversible Quilt and Lilah Suzani Duvet Cover. The subject, "Love pattern?", matches the toile, botanical and suzani creative, so what you open is what you were promised. That's better than the recent Fall Sale blasts.
- The weakness is urgency. Free shipping on quilts is the only incentive, and there's no deadline, price cut or reason to buy today. The second half of the email loses focus. It runs from "Complete Your Retreat" through generic "Recommended Just For You" (curtain rod, dining table, swivel chair) to "Shop our Latest Designs", Design Crew, the app, Affirm and rewards. A shopper who wanted bedding gets pulled into a catalogue.
- Verdict: a solid, pretty send that earns an open and a browse, but not much of a purchase. It's in line with the 6–7 range of this week's editorial sends ("Introducing the Ashley Stark collab", "Beautiful dinnerware for every day"). A single dated incentive would lift it.

## What's working

- The subject and hero agree. "Love pattern?" pays off with toile, botanical and suzani prints right away.
- The hero is clean and aspirational. The serif "Sleeping Patterns" headline sits over a layered bed, with a "Shop the Look" tag.
- Each of the three hero products gets its own image, name and "SHOP NOW" link, so the clicks go to specific products.
- The "Quilts ship free" pill and the "All bedding ships free" bar repeat one offer in two places.
- The "Complete Your Retreat" grid (Duvets, Sheets, Beds, Nightstands, Dressers, Pillows) gives easy cross-sell paths.
- There are no visible render bugs. The layout is tidy, with consistent spacing and brand typography.

## What's weak

- The only incentive is shipping. It has no price cut, no deadline and no stated minimum, so there's no reason to act today.
- The "SHOP NOW" links are small, thin text links. The one real button is "ALL BEDDING SHIPS FREE", and its copy is a statement, not a verb.
- The lower half dilutes the message. Generic recommended products, "Shop our Latest Designs", Design Crew and app, Affirm and rewards modules all compete with the bedding story.
- The product names say nothing about size, fabric, price or reviews, so a shopper has nothing to decide on.
- The header strip, "Explore Our Holiday New Arrivals", points to a different campaign from the bedding hero. It's a mixed signal at the top.
- Cadence is heavy. Pottery Barn sent "Get your living room ready for guests", "From our Kravet Collection" and "FREE (yes, free!) design help" in the past two days.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a dated, specific incentive to the hero, such as "Free shipping on quilts through Sunday" or a bedding percent-off. This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Turn the three "SHOP NOW" text links into real buttons with product-specific copy, such as "Shop the Greer Quilt". Add starting prices and a star rating under each product name.
- 3. Cut the lower half to one bedding-adjacent module. Drop the generic recommended grid, or make it bedding-only (sheets, shams, nightstands). Move the app, Affirm and rewards modules to the footer.
- 4. Remove or retire the "Explore Our Holiday New Arrivals" header strip so the bedding message isn't contradicted.
- 5. Add the member angle ("Earn 10% back with the Pottery Barn credit card") right under the hero. It's currently buried near the bottom.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Pottery Barn, "Love pattern? This bedding is for you"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is image-sliced (about 14 hero slices and 18 footer slices) and nearly all of them lack alt text. It has legacy-markup problems: leaked PHP in a `<style>` block, an empty `<title>`, no `lang`, and a zoom-locking viewport. It also puts the recipient's email address in plaintext, non-HTTPS pixel URLs. The HTML source is truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and link-destination checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click tracking can't be verified.** 56 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe. The one visible example is the logo link `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...`, an opaque redirect. Destination, redirect chain and expiry are unconfirmed.
- **Insecure pixel.** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` loads over HTTP. It may be blocked by clients or proxies, and it breaks open tracking when it is.
- **Third-party and cross-domain dependencies.**
  - `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` is a third-party pixel.
  - `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/a6e7df41-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...` is a dynamic-content/open pixel.
  - `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a spacer hosted on a different brand's domain.
- **Leaked server-side code.** The last `<style>` block contains `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store..."); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. These are PHP statements pasted into CSS. They are invalid CSS, and some clients may mis-parse the rules around them.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** About 50 images are flagged by QA. Examples:
  - Hero slices `.../H26_Bedding_B2_PatternedBedding/slices_01.jpg` through `slices_14.jpg`.
  - Footer slices `.../Email-Footer/slices_01.jpg` through `slices_18.jpg`.
  - Product images such as `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg` and `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-1245-1-l.jpg`.
  - Banners `H26_Top_ChristmasShop.jpg`, `F26_Bottom_NewArrivals_V1.jpg` and `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`.
  - With images blocked, these sections render blank. Screen readers get filenames or nothing.
- **Tracking pixels without `alt=""`.** `d.turn.com`, `svopn`, `clickcp` and `coreemail.html` are flagged as missing alt. They should carry `alt=""`, and `width="1" height="1"` or `display:none`.
- **Non-HTTPS images.** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`. They may be blocked or trigger mixed-content warnings.
- **Empty title.** `<title></title>`.
- **No language attribute.** `<html xmlns=...>` has no `lang`.
- **Zoom locked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Table roles.** The outer layout tables use `role="presentation"`. Some spacer tables (`<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><tr><td height="20">&nbsp;</td>`) do not. Coverage of the rest can't be confirmed from the truncated source.
- **Web font.** The Google Fonts `<link>` for Montserrat is stripped or ignored by many clients. No fallback behavior can be confirmed from the visible source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unrendered merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) in the visible source.
- **PII in URLs.**
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/...&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - The raw address is in plaintext query strings. The second is sent over unencrypted HTTP, so it is exposed to network observers and proxy logs. Hashed or opaque IDs would be safer.
- `coreemail.html` is loaded as an `<img>`, so it is likely a tracking/beacon endpoint.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058).** Both headers were not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed, not a verified sender defect.
- **Authentication-Results.** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `e.potterybarn.com`.
- **Footer.** The visible source is truncated before the footer, so the in-body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address could not be verified.
- **Plain-text part.** 10,875 of 15,062 characters (about 72%) are URLs, which is over the 70% warning threshold. This is a minor spam-filter signal, driven by long tracking URLs.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters.** None are visible. All clicks go through `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`, and the final landing URLs and any UTM/campaign parameters can't be verified because the probes were skipped.
- **Product images.** They are hotlinked from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...` with weekly path IDs (`202640`, `202634`, ...). They will break if the site CDN rotates assets.
- No landing-page alignment issues can be confirmed from the data provided.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, and `alt=""` to spacers and tracking pixels. Add background colors or live text to the sliced hero and footer so the email degrades when images are blocked.
2. Move the `svopn`, `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `slices_02.jpg` URLs to HTTPS.
3. Stop passing the raw email address in the `sv_sveme` and `email=` parameters. Use hashed or opaque identifiers.
4. Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP lines from the `<style>` block.
5. Set a non-empty `<title>`, add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
6. Re-test against raw `.eml` headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. The AgentMail relay may be hiding them.
7. Re-run the QA on the full, untruncated HTML to verify the footer unsubscribe link, postal address, and final landing URLs with their UTM parameters.
8. Host shared assets such as the spacer on the sender's own domain, not `edm.westelm.com`.
