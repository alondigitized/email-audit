# Holiday lovers, this one’s for you 🎁
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-23**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's earliest holiday-signal send of the season, and it's a genuine break from the six-week textile/furniture crawl I've been logging — "Christmas Bestsellers Are Back" leads with a tight stocking/wreath/ornament grid instead of another duvet or rug lifestyle shot. The hero copy ("Holiday lovers know it's never too early... they sell out every year") is a smart scarcity hook that actually earns its place in late September.
- The problem is everything after the fold undercuts the premise. The "Recommended Just For You" section reverts to generic non-holiday SKUs (curtain rods, dining tables, a swivel chair), then the email pivots hard into a Halloween Shop banner and a Design Crew pitch — three competing seasonal/service messages stacked in one send with no offer or discount anywhere. It reads like three merchandising teams each got a slot in the same template.
- Net: strong subject/hero, weak follow-through, zero concrete offer. This lands as a mid-pack awareness email, not a converter.

## What's working

- The Christmas product grid (stockings, wreaths, ornaments, a bear) is visually cohesive and immediately reads "holiday" without needing copy.
- "Christmas Bestsellers Are Back" + "they sell out every year" is a credible, low-cheese urgency line for a September send.
- "THE (EARLY) CHRISTMAS SHOP" CTA button is clear, on-brand, and matches the hero promise exactly.
- Clean visual hierarchy at the top — eye lands on the holiday grid, then the headline, then the button, in that order.

## What's weak

- No discount, price, or concrete offer anywhere in the entire email — pure browse/discovery messaging.
- "Recommended Just For You" breaks the holiday narrative with non-seasonal furniture (dining table, swivel chair, sheet set), diluting the hero's momentum.
- Three unrelated CTAs compete for attention (Christmas Shop, Halloween Shop, Design Crew) with no visual priority signal — it's unclear what the brand wants me to click.
- The Halloween Shop banner mid-email is a jarring seasonal whiplash right after the Christmas-forward hero and subject line.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the Design Crew and Halloween Shop blocks from this send entirely, or move them to a separate email — a "Christmas is early" email shouldn't immediately pitch Halloween two scrolls down.
- 2. Add one concrete offer to the hero band (e.g., "Up to 30% off stockings, wreaths & trim") — right now there's nothing to click for besides curiosity.
- 3. Swap "Recommended Just For You" to holiday-adjacent SKUs (holiday bedding, dining/entertaining) so the personalization block doesn't contradict the seasonal hook.
- 4. Give the Christmas Shop button a second placement further down, since the Halloween/Design Crew blocks currently end the seasonal thread before a repeat CTA.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Stockings & wreaths are selling out early this year`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop the styles that sell out every December — before they're gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Get first pick of stockings, wreaths & ornaments while stock lasts.`
