# Up to 70% off & going fast + On sale & shipping free
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-11**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth or sixth Fall Sale send in three days, and it reads like a catch-all. The dark-green "Up to 70% off" bar and the "Save on Easy Updates" hero are clean. The hero is a pillow-and-throw scene with a clear SHOP NOW button, and a tidy text grid below it covers Pillows, Throws, Quilts, Duvets, Table Linens, Sheets, Towels and Curtains. The offer is easy to find. But the email then goes on to a six-product "Recommended Just for You" block, a New Arrivals banner, a Christmas Shop banner, and a stack of app, credit card and Affirm promos. A recipient who opened for the sale ends up with at least six competing destinations.
- Next to the focused sends of 10/10 and 10/9, such as "NOW ON SALE: Bestselling bedding" and "Table lamps & more beautiful lighting", this one is diluted. The "just for you" block mixes a curtain rod, dining tables, a bed, a ghost decoration and a rug, and none of it connects to the soft-goods hero. The Christmas Shop block is also early and pulls attention away from the sale.
- Verdict: a competent template with a clear offer, but not a targeted one. It's more of a repeat of the same Fall Sale than a fresh reason to click.

## What's working

- The dark-green "The Fall Sale: Up to 70% off" bar puts the offer first, matching the brand's recent sends.
- The hero image is warm and on-brand, and its SHOP NOW button is clear.
- The eight-category text grid gives fast routes to specific products, plus a SHOP ALL SALE button.
- "Ships free" sits in the hero kicker, so the shipping offer is visible alongside the discount.
- The footer legal copy is complete and the layout shows no render bugs.

## What's weak

- The email has too many competing CTAs: hero, grid, six product tiles, New Arrivals, Christmas Shop, App Store, credit card and Affirm.
- The "Recommended Just for You" tiles have no prices, no sale callouts and no reviews. They show a "70% off" email with nothing to back it up.
- The "Discover New Arrivals" text overlaps the busy photo and is hard to read. It also pushes full-price items in an email about markdowns.
- The Christmas Shop block is early and has no offer attached.
- The subject line has two competing messages ("going fast" and "shipping free"), joined with a "+" that reads like a template.
- This is another near-identical Fall Sale send in a run, and the brand history shows the same banner every day since 10/9.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut everything below the category grid except one product row with sale prices, "was/now" pricing and star ratings. Move the app, credit card and Affirm promos to a single slim footer line.
- 2. Make the "going fast" urgency real. Give it a deadline or a stock signal such as "ends Sunday" or "low stock on quilts."
- 3. Pick one hero product and give it a concrete callout, such as "Quilts from $X, ships free."
- 4. Hold the Christmas Shop banner until the sale ends, or send it as its own email.
- 5. Rewrite the New Arrivals overlay with a solid text panel so it's legible, or drop it.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a table-based XHTML layout with a hidden preheader and 52 opaque click-tracked links. QA shows 0 failures and 4 warnings, but the source has real defects: about 45 images with no alt text, 3 plain-HTTP image requests that carry the recipient's address, and stray PHP code inside a `<style>` block. Authentication and unsubscribe-header checks are inconclusive, not failed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified destinations:** 52 links on `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` were skipped by the HTTP probe, so no destination, status code or redirect chain could be checked.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` is a third-party ad-network beacon. It adds a tracking domain outside the sender's own.
- **Open-tracking pixels:** `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/1572ef2a-...` and `svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true` are two separate open pixels. One of them, `coreemail.html?email=...`, is an HTML URL loaded as an `<img>`.
- **Cross-brand asset:** the spacer `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is served from the West Elm domain. It will break if that asset is removed.
- **Reused campaign assets:** images come from `/pb/2025/US/0822_FRI_AM_LTOSale_FreeShip1/` (an Aug 2025 path) and `/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/`. These are evergreen slices in an October 2026 send, which is a QA risk if those assets are ever retired.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 45 `<img>` tags have no alt. They include the main banner `H26_TOP_FallSale.jpg`, all `slices_*.jpg` files, and every product image such as `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg` and `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-1245-1-l.jpg`. Because the footer is built from `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg` with no alt text, image-blocked clients will show no readable footer content.
- **Non-HTTPS images (3):** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`, and the `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/...` pixel. These can be blocked or flagged as mixed content.
- **Empty title:** the document has `<title></title>`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>` stops pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Stray PHP in CSS:** the second `<style>` block contains `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store..."); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. This is server-side code leaking into the CSS. It is invalid, and the parser may drop the rule that follows it.
- **Unused commented viewports:** the `<meta name="viewport" content="width=320, initial-scale=0.29">` and `width=1024` lines are commented out. They do no harm.
- **Tracking pixels with no alt:** the `d.turn.com`, `clickcp` and `coreemail.html` pixels have no alt. They should use `alt=""`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Email address exposed in plain text:** the recipient address appears in clear text in two image URLs. One is `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`. The other is `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?...&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`.
- **Leak over HTTP:** the `svopn` URL is plain HTTP, so the address travels unencrypted. It can also leak through proxies and logs.
- **Merge tokens:** no unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) appear in the truncated source. The rest of the HTML was not available to check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **Unsubscribe headers:** `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. QA says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed, not a confirmed defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers.
- **Authentication:** the `Authentication-Results` header was not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `e.potterybarn.com`.
- **Footer:** the truncated source does not show an unsubscribe link or postal address. The footer is image slices with no alt text, so it cannot be confirmed whether that required text is live or baked into images.
- **Plain-text part:** 10,093 of 14,247 characters (71%) are URLs. This is a mild spam-filter signal, caused by the long tracking URLs.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters:** none can be confirmed. Every CTA is wrapped in an opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=` token, and the probe was skipped.
- **Landing-page alignment:** it cannot be verified from the HTML. Product images point at `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...` paths, and the destinations behind them were not checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` to all content images, especially the footer slices and the hero banner. Use `alt=""` on spacers and pixels.
2. Switch the 3 `http://` image sources to `https://`.
3. Stop putting the raw recipient email in image URLs. Use a hashed or tokenized ID instead, and never send it over HTTP.
4. Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP lines from the `<style>` block.
5. Fill in `<title>`, and remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
6. Replace the West Elm spacer and the 2022 and 2025 reused slices with current assets from the sender's own domain.
7. Re-run the checks against raw message headers, not the AgentMail relay copy. That would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC and the footer unsubscribe link and address.
8. Resolve a sample of the `click.e.potterybarn.com` links manually to confirm destinations and UTM parameters.
