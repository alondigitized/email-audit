# Up to 60% off summer blooms & botanicals 🌼
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-10**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's Summer Warehouse Sale fatigue email number thirteen-or-so — but with a category pivot to "The Bloom Shop." The floral/botanical angle is a legitimate creative lever, and it's executed with a well-structured grid of eight flower product thumbnails that gives the email a catalog feel. The hero is clean and the brand marks a soft urgency with "In-Stock Furniture delivered in 1-3 weeks" at the top, which feels oddly mismatched against a florals-focused email. The overall impression is mid-effort: the category is appealing, the product grid is legible, but the personalization layer ("Recommended Just For You") shows bedroom furniture and rugs — categories completely unrelated to the bloom shop above. That disconnect undercuts the editorial coherence this email almost achieves.
- The bottom half loses the thread entirely. Free Design Services, an Affirm financing module, and a generic social footer all compete for attention below the product grid. This is a catalogue dump masquerading as a focused botanical feature. Compare to the June 9 George Kittle editorial send, which committed to a single narrative top-to-bottom — this one hedges and ends up diluted.
- On balance: a good category idea, half-executed. The open is earned by the subject (florals + warehouse-sale % off is a concrete combo), but the body doesn't deliver a coherent botanical experience.

## What's working

- **Subject specificity lands** — "summer blooms & botanicals" is a concrete sub-category call-out inside the broader 60%-off sale, meaningfully more targeted than yesterday's generic "UP TO 60% OFF + Pieces ON SALE for every room."
- **Product grid is clean and scannable** — eight floral SKUs displayed at consistent scale with visible pricing; the eye moves naturally across the grid.
- **"The Bloom Shop" hero framing** — naming the sub-shop creates a slight editorial identity and separates this from yet another raw warehouse blast.
- **No major render bugs visible** — images load, text doesn't overlap, layout is intact throughout.
- **Soft urgency at top** — "In-Stock Furniture delivered in 1-3 weeks" at the very top is a credible, non-hypey urgency signal (though misplaced for a florals email).

## What's weak

- **"Recommended Just For You" shows rugs and bedroom furniture** — completely breaks the florals narrative; these product cards belong in a different send.
- **Hero copy is generic** — "Bring the outside in with our handcrafted floral arrangements" is filler; there's no specific offer or price anchor in the hero itself.
- **Bottom-of-email noise** — Free Design Services, Affirm module, and app download block add three competing CTAs below the product grid with no visual hierarchy separating them.
- **No visible price callout on individual flower SKUs** — the grid thumbnails show flowers but pricing is very small; no "from $X" anchor in the hero to stop the scroll.
- **Flower-to-furniture ratio is off** — the "Recommended" block is as large as the bloom shop grid itself, visually halving the botanical focus.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Remove or relocate the "Recommended Just For You" block** — the furniture/rug cards shatter the botanical story. Either cut them or swap for additional botanical SKUs to fill out the Bloom Shop grid. This is the single highest-impact change.
- 2. **Add a price anchor to the hero** — put "From $29" or "Up to 60% off arrangements, stems & botanicals" directly under "The Bloom Shop" headline so the offer is visible without scrolling the grid.
- 3. **Replace the mismatched hero subhead** — "Bring the outside in with our handcrafted floral arrangements" is vanilla; tie it to the season or an occasion ("Summer tablescapes. Weddings. Or just because.").
- 4. **Collapse the bottom modules** — Design Services and Affirm don't belong in a florals-focused send; move them to a digest or a dedicated send.
- 5. **Fix the top banner copy** — "In-Stock Furniture delivered in 1-3 weeks" is a furniture message; swap for a florals-relevant trust signal ("Ships in water-safe packaging" or similar).
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your summer flowers are up to 60% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Blooms, botanicals & stems — up to 60% off now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Handcrafted arrangements, faux florals & stems — shop The Bloom Shop sale`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fresh picks for summer tablescapes, gifting, or just because — up to 60% off`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**PHP `header()` calls verbatim in `<style>` block — template artifact:**
```html
<style>
  ...
  header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
  header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
  header("Cache-Control: post-check=0, pre-check=0", false);
  header("Pragma: no-cache");
</style>
```
These are server-side PHP calls that were never executed — they ended up as literal text inside a CSS block. Harmless at render time but confirms a broken template compilation step. The source file was likely a PHP partial included without being run through the PHP interpreter.

**40 images missing `alt` text.** Key offenders:
- All 18 `slices_0*.jpg` hero/content images (2026/US/0610_WED_AM_BotanicalsPipV2/)
- All 18 Email-Footer `slices_0*.jpg`
- Product images: `farmhouse-platform-bed-l.jpg`, `reeva-handwoven-rug-3-l.jpg`, `H25_TOP_InStockFurniture.jpg`, etc.
- Banner images: `S26_Bottom_UpTo50OffOutdoor.jpg`, `W26_Bottom_GeneralDCBottomBanner.jpg`

Renders as blank in image-off environments (Gmail desktop default, many corporate clients).

**3 HTTP image sources** (will be blocked by HTTPS-enforcing clients):
```
http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif           ← also 403
http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg
http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg
```

**Empty `<title>` tag** — affects tab/window label in webmail clients that render the email in a browser frame.

**`maximum-scale=1` in viewport meta** — suppresses pinch-zoom on mobile, an accessibility regression (WCAG 1.4.4).

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header absent** — not captured by AgentMail relay. Gmail and Yahoo require this header for bulk senders (>5k/day); absence increases spam folder placement probability and may violate bulk sender policies effective 2024.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent** — RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe not supported. Gmail bulk sender requirement as of February 2024.

**`Authentication-Results` header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC status unverifiable via relay. If DMARC alignment is failing on `e.potterybarn.com`, this send is at risk for Gmail/Yahoo rejection.

**Plain-text body is 72% URLs** (10,937 of 15,131 chars) — above the ~70% threshold commonly used by spam filters. Likely caused by auto-generated plain-text that echoes all tracking redirect URLs verbatim rather than using the destination text.

CAN-SPAM physical address and unsubscribe mechanism appear present in the footer (based on standard Pottery Barn template) — not contradicted by QA data.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

The click URLs route through `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` (opaque base64 parameters), so UTM preservation depends entirely on what the redirect resolves to — not verifiable from this source. No raw `utm_source` / `utm_medium` / `utm_campaign` parameters are visible in any href, which means either (a) they're encoded in the `qs` payload or (b) they're absent.
