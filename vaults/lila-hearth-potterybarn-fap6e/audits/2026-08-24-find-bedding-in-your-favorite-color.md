# Find bedding in your favorite color
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-24**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's third color-sort bedding email in three days (following 2026-08-24's "Comfort Meets Color" quilt send and 2026-08-22's "Bedding Bestsellers" sale), and the fatigue is starting to show. "Layers of Color" leads with a nicely stacked-linens hero shot and a real number — "Up to 15% off Belgian Flax Linen Bedding" — but that discount is buried under the hero as a small caption, not the headline. The bulk of the email is a 4x2 grid of color-swatch tiles (blues, pinks, greens, neutrals) that reads more like a category-navigation page than a promotional send.
- It's competently produced — good photography, clean grid — but there's no urgency, no single hero product, and the discount is the least visually prominent element on the page. Compared to yesterday's cleaner "Bedding Bestsellers" send, this one trades focus for browse-by-color convenience, which is a weaker pitch to open on a commute and skim.

## What's working

- The hero "Layers of Color" stacked-linens shot is well-composed and immediately communicates the color-sort concept.
- "Up to 15% off Belgian Flax Linen Bedding" is a concrete, specific offer with a real percentage.
- "It all ships free!" is a clear, friction-removing line placed right under the section header.
- The color-tile grid (blues, pinks, greens, neutrals) is clean and consistent, no render bugs or broken images visible.
- "SHOP BEDDING BY COLOR" gives one clear rollup CTA if a shopper doesn't want to pick a single tile.

## What's weak

- The 15% off discount is small, secondary caption text under the hero — it should be the headline, not an afterthought.
- No single hero product or bestseller is called out; six competing color tiles with no visual hierarchy dilute the primary offer.
- This is the third bedding/color email in three days from this sender — cadence is repetitive and will read as noise to anyone who opened the last two.
- The email keeps going well past the offer into unrelated categories (Design Crew Sweeps, Halloween Shop, App promo, credit card pitch) — it's long and unfocused for a single-offer email.
- Subject "Find bedding in your favorite color" has zero mention of the 15% discount, so the strongest asset in the email is invisible until open.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the discount in the subject line and as the dominant hero headline — "Up to 15% off Belgian Flax" is a real number that's currently whispered, not shouted.
- 2. Cut the color-tile grid from 6 tiles to 3-4, or replace with a single hero linen set plus a "shop by color" link below it — right now nothing anchors the eye.
- 3. Space this out from the last two bedding sends — three near-identical color/bedding emails in three days trains subscribers to skip.
- 4. Trim the email — Design Crew Sweeps, Halloween Shop, and the credit card pitch dilute a send that should be a single-minded linen offer.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your color, 15% off: new Belgian Flax linens`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Blues, pinks, greens, neutrals — free shipping on it all`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard Salesforce Marketing Cloud (ExactTarget-style) HTML email with tracking pixels and click-redirects; no rendering-breaking code errors found, but several compliance headers are missing and image alt attributes are absent throughout.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All CTAs route through `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirect wrappers (e.g. `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABB7InYiOjEs...`) — standard SFMC click-tracking, not independently verifiable without following redirects (65 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destination validity is unconfirmed).
- Open-tracking pixel uses plaintext HTTP: `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` — the recipient email address is passed in cleartext in a GET parameter over an unencrypted connection.
- Secondary pixel `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` also load over HTTP, as does `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`. Mixed-content HTTP images risk being blocked by mail clients enforcing HTTPS-only image loading.
- Additional third-party pixel present: `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` (Amobee/Turn data-tracking pixel) — confirms this send is instrumented for third-party ad-audience tracking, not just internal analytics.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 46 `<img>` elements are missing `alt` text (all `slices_*.jpg` grid/spacer images, product photography, banners, and both tracking pixels). This degrades the experience for image-blocked and screen-reader clients — recipients get no fallback text for any content image.
- Preheader text is correctly hidden via `display:none !important; mso-hide:all` with zero-width-space padding — implemented correctly, no issue.
- MSO/Outlook conditional comments (`<!--[if mso 16]>`, `<!--[if gte mso 9]>`) and `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` are present — standard defensive markup, no issue.
- Stray `header()` PHP-style calls (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); ...`) are embedded inside a `<style>` block. These are inert as CSS (rendered as invalid property declarations, ignored by parsers) but indicate a template artifact/leftover from a PHP asset script that was pasted into the wrong context — worth flagging to the ESP/template team even though it has no functional rendering impact.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `%%member%%`, `{{first_name}}`) found in the truncated source — personalization appears to have resolved correctly.
- Recipient identifier is embedded in tracking/pixel URLs as a literal resolved email (`lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`), confirming merge fields rendered correctly for this send, but this also means the email address is exposed in plaintext GET parameters over HTTP (see §2/§6 security note).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header: not found.** No RFC 2369 `List-Unsubscribe` header was captured — cannot confirm one-click/mailbox-level unsubscribe support.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header: not found.** RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe (required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender rules) cannot be confirmed as supported.
- **Authentication-Results header: not found.** SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown from this capture — cannot confirm sender authentication compliance.
- Note: all three are flagged by the automated QA as relay-side gaps ("may not be captured by AgentMail relay"), so these may be a capture/proxy limitation rather than the sender's actual header configuration — recommend verifying against raw headers from the original SMTP transaction before treating as a sender-side compliance failure.
- In-body unsubscribe/footer links were not visible in the truncated HTML provided, so footer-level CAN-SPAM elements (physical address, unsubscribe link text) could not be verified from this excerpt.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Header logo and CTA links route through the `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirector rather than direct UTM-tagged URLs, so UTM/landing-page alignment cannot be verified without resolving the redirect chain (skipped by automated probe).
- Customer-service link resolves directly (not through the click-tracker) to `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` — again passes the recipient's plaintext email as a URL parameter, this time over HTTPS (lower risk than the HTTP pixel in §2, but still logged in server access logs/referrer headers).

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` attributes to all content images (46 instances flagged), or at minimum empty `alt=""` on purely decorative spacers/slices to satisfy accessibility parsers.
2. Migrate the three HTTP-only image/pixel sources (`edm.westelm.com`, `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/...`, `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/...`) to HTTPS to avoid mixed-content blocking in modern mail clients.
3. Switch the `sv_emopen` open-tracking pixel from HTTP to HTTPS — it currently transmits the recipient's email address in cleartext.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually present in the raw SMTP send (this may be a relay-capture artifact rather than a true gap) — RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is required by major mailbox providers' bulk sender policies.
5. Verify `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) against the original transaction rather than the relayed copy.
6. Remove the orphaned `header('Content-Type: ...')`/`header('Cache-Control: ...')` PHP snippet embedded in the `<style>` block — harmless today but suggests a broken template-include and should be cleaned from the source.
7. Resolve the 65 `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirect links to confirm final landing-page URLs carry correct UTM parameters before this becomes a client-facing continuity issue.
