# LAST DAY 🚨 Memorial Day Sale
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-26**
## Executive summary

- "LAST DAY 🚨 Memorial Day Sale" is the twentieth-plus Memorial Day email from Pottery Barn in roughly a week — but unlike the mid-campaign filler sends, the urgency here is finally accurate. May 26 is the day after the holiday itself, and the sale legitimately ends today. The previous day's "ENDS TOMORROW" email (scored 8/10) was the peak of the sequence; this one arrives with every signal in place but a subscriber base that has been systematically desensitized. The red banner, the "SHOP EXTRA 20% OFF" CTA, the "Recommended Just For You" personalized grid, loyalty rewards, and Affirm financing all execute correctly.
- The structural problem is that Pottery Barn has cried wolf across the entire campaign window. "ENDS SOON," "ENDS TOMORROW," "Time is running out" — subscribers have been conditioned to expect an extended sale email tomorrow regardless of what today's subject line claims. The "LAST DAY" signal, which should land hardest, is landing softest because the brand trained the audience not to believe it. The body compounds this by never reinforcing the deadline — the hero just says "MEMORIAL DAY SALE" with no countdown or cutoff time visible inside the email itself.
- If the sale genuinely closes tonight, this email has the mechanical execution to convert buyers who are still in-market. But Pottery Barn's cadence has eaten its own urgency.

## What's working

- **"LAST DAY" is finally credible**: unlike most prior sends, the claim in this subject line is actually accurate for May 26 — the first time in the sequence the urgency is genuine rather than stretched.
- **"Recommended Just For You" personalization**: a product grid with visible prices appears below the fold, adding the kind of individual targeting that differentiates this send from the bulk-template middle of the campaign.
- **Dual CTA is specific and action-oriented**: "SHOP EXTRA 20% OFF" and "SHOP ALL SALE" both use verb + noun and are clearly visible as buttons above the fold.
- **Loyalty and financing both surface**: "Earn 10% Back in Rewards" and the Affirm pay-over-time block are visible, reducing conversion friction for hesitant buyers right at the sale deadline.
- **Category grid covers full breadth**: FURNITURE, OUTDOOR, SOFAS, BEDDING, RUGS, BATH — buyers who want to browse their own category can do so without clicking through to search.

## What's weak

- **The body never says "LAST DAY"**: the deadline urgency that drives the open lives entirely in the subject line; the email body just reads "MEMORIAL DAY SALE" with no deadline timestamp or cutoff reinforcement inside.
- **Twenty identical red banners**: the same "Extra 20% off Select Styles" hero block has run every day this campaign. At send 20+, it registers as background noise regardless of the subject-line escalation.
- **Asterisked exclusion erodes trust**: "Extra 20% off*" is visible with an asterisk but no inline clarification — "Select Styles" is doing real damage to offer credibility at this point in the campaign.
- **Family of Brands grid dilutes last-day focus**: seven sibling brand tiles (West Elm, Williams-Sonoma, PB Kids, etc.) appear mid-email on a send that should funnel all urgency into a single Pottery Barn cart.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Plant a deadline inside the email body**: "Sale ends tonight at midnight PT" as a one-line subhead under the hero offer — the subject drives the open, but the body needs to close the conversion with the same urgency.
- 2. **Kill the Family of Brands section on last-day sends**: every module below the primary CTA should reinforce the deadline or the personalized picks, not scatter the reader to six other storefronts.
- 3. **Surface the asterisk exclusions inline**: "Extra 20% off Select Styles — exclusions apply, see details" with a visible link costs nothing and kills the fine-print distrust that "Select Styles" currently manufactures silently.
- 4. **Elevate the personalized recommendations above the category grid**: the "Recommended Just For You" section is the most differentiated module in the email — on a last-day send it should be second, not fifth in the scroll order.
- **Subject Alt A:** `LAST DAY: Extra 20% off ends at midnight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your sale ends tonight — Extra 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop your recommended picks before the sale closes tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Extra 20% off Select Styles + 10% back in rewards — today only.`

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**[FAIL] Broken CTA — 403 response**
The "Apply and Buy" / non-member credit card banner link returns HTTP 403:
```
https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/c/da01b407-b21c-4618-a71b-f83bef0dd682
  ?brandCode=POTTERYBARNCB
  &marketingChannelCode=APPLYANDBUY
  &external_id=PB_Email_MemberBanner
  &cm_ite=PB_header_nonmember
```
Parameter `cm_ite=PB_header_nonmember` and `external_id=PB_Email_MemberBanner` confirm this is the header acquisition module. The destination is dead — any click on that element results in an error page.

**[WARN] 23 click-tracking links not probed**
All `click.e.potterybarn.com` and `clickcp.potterybarn.com` redirects were skipped by the HTTP probe. Only the one above surfaced a hard failure; others may have additional 4xx/5xx responses not captured.

**[WARN] Coremetrics `cm_ite` parameters in use**
Links use IBM/Coremetrics `cm_ite` attribution rather than standard UTM parameters. Not a defect, but downstream analytics will not appear in GA4 campaign reports without a Coremetrics-to-GA connector.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[FAIL] PHP `header()` calls embedded literally in `<style>` block**
The following server-side PHP statements appear as raw text inside a `<style>` tag — they will not execute and will be parsed as invalid CSS:
```css
header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
header("Cache-Control: post-check=0, pre-check=0", false);
header("Pragma: no-cache");
```
This is a template assembly error where a PHP include was output into the wrong context. It is inert but signals a build pipeline defect.

**[FAIL] 3 HTTP (non-HTTPS) image sources — mixed content**
Gmail, Outlook 365, and Apple Mail block HTTP assets in HTTPS contexts. Affected images will render as broken:
- `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` — cross-brand West Elm CDN domain
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`

Additionally, the open-tracking pixel fires over HTTP:
- `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...`

**[WARN] ~40 images missing `alt` text**
Every image in the primary content area, product grid (`slices_01.jpg` through `slices_14.jpg`), cross-brand footer, and email footer has an empty or absent `alt` attribute. This includes the hero urgency banner (`S26_MemorialDaySale_UrgencyTopBanner_LastDay.jpg`) and all product tiles. Subscribers with images disabled will see a blank email.

**[WARN] `maximum-scale=1` on viewport meta**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
Prevents user-initiated zoom on mobile. Violates WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text, Level AA).

**[INFO] Empty `<title>` tag**
`<title></title>` — no value. Some email clients display the title as a fallback; leaving it empty is a minor but addressable gap.

### 5. Compliance

**[WARN] List-Unsubscribe header not confirmed**
`List-Unsubscribe` was not captured by the AgentMail relay. If absent from the actual SMTP envelope, this violates Google and Yahoo's 2024 bulk sender requirements (mandatory for >5k/day senders). Likely a relay capture gap, but must be verified against raw headers from a direct inbox.

**[WARN] List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not confirmed**
One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail for bulk senders. Without `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, Gmail's unsubscribe button degrades to mailto fallback, increasing spam complaints.

**[WARN] Authentication-Results header not captured**
SPF and DKIM pass/fail status is unknown due to relay interception. Cannot confirm alignment between `From: PotteryBarn@e.potterybarn.com` (sending domain: `e.potterybarn.com`) and DKIM `d=` tag. Should be verified via a direct inbox header inspection.

**[INFO] `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow" />`**
Standard and correct for email HTML — no issue.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**[WARN] `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer" />`**
This meta tag is present in the email `<head>`. When subscribers click through to `potterybarn.com`, the browser will send no `Referer` header. Any server-side or GA4 referrer-based attribution for this campaign will be suppressed. If referrer attribution is expected, this tag should be removed. If intentional (privacy), document it.
