# NEW prints & patterns with a storied past
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-25**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's Kravet archive collab — "New Print & Pattern" — leading with a genuinely differentiated creative hook: licensed heritage textile patterns, not another quilts-and-throws crawl. The hero bedroom shot (moody florals, layered pillows, warm wood tones) is the strongest visual moment in this run of sends, and the 3x3 grid below it does real work showing the print across pillows, drapery, upholstery, and wall art in different rooms — that's useful for someone actually trying to picture it in their home.
- Where it falls apart is everything after the fold. This is a kitchen-sink email: the pattern story gets one screen, then it dumps into "Recommended Just For You" (six unrelated SKUs — curtain rods, a dining table, a jute rug, Halloween ghosts), then a full Halloween Shop module, then a Design Crew pitch, then app download, then credit card, then legal. There's no offer anywhere — no %, no free shipping, nothing — so this is a pure discovery/inspiration play riding on a co-brand story that's strong enough to carry maybe a third of what's actually here.
- Judgement: good creative concept, badly overloaded execution. The hero and pattern grid alone would have been a 7+; bolting on four more unrelated pitches drags it down.

## What's working

- Hero visual is genuinely premium — layered floral bedding, warm lighting, no render bugs.
- "Kravet x Pottery Barn" co-brand lockup is legible and gives the send a reason to exist beyond routine merchandising.
- The 3x3 pattern-application grid shows the print across multiple product types and rooms, which is the right way to sell an archival-print collab.
- Clean, unambiguous CTA button ("Shop the Collection") repeated at top and bottom of the hero block.

## What's weak

- No visible offer or price signal anywhere in the email — pure inspiration, no reason to act now.
- "Recommended Just For You" is generic and disconnected from the pattern story — a jute rug and a dining table sitting right under a Kravet pillow grid breaks the narrative.
- The email keeps going for what looks like 4-5 more distinct sales pitches (Halloween Shop, Design Crew, App, Credit Card) after the hero — no single clear focus once you scroll past the first screen.
- Subject line undersells it: "storied past" is vague copy that doesn't say "designer collab" or name Kravet, so the differentiation gets lost before open.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the pattern grid + one supporting module (e.g., just the Halloween Shop OR Design Crew, not both plus recommended products plus app plus credit card). One story, one CTA path.
- 2. Name the collab in the subject line — "Kravet" is a recognizable design-world name to the target shopper and it's buried in the hero image only.
- 3. Add a concrete reason to click now — even "Limited-run patterns" or ship-date language would beat pure inspiration copy.
- 4. Drop "Recommended Just For You" from this send entirely — it dilutes a genuinely good creative idea with random catalog filler.
