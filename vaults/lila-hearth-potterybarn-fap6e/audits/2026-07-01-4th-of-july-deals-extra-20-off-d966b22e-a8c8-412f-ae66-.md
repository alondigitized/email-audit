# 4th of July deals + EXTRA 20% OFF
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-01**
## What's working

- **Open Box Outlet Deals block** is a new angle — discounting already-reduced inventory frames this as a separate, additive offer rather than just repeating the hero.
- **Category grid navigation** (Furniture, Outdoor, Dining, Decor, Lighting, Rugs, Bath) gives highly scrollable quick-jump access to specific interests with no friction.
- **"Recommended Just For You" product grid** is the single strongest scroll incentive — showing named products with thumbnails and individual Shop Now buttons personalizes an otherwise generic blast.
- **Loyalty points callout** ("Earn 100 Points in Rewards") visible without scrolling past the recommended block — reinforces member value without a separate email.
- **Render is clean** — no overlapping text, no broken image placeholders, layout holds correctly.

## What's weak

- **Subject is the weakest of the entire campaign run** — "4th of July deals + EXTRA 20% OFF" is vague where June 29's "Our TOP 4th of July Sale picks (up to 70% off)" and June 28's "Don't wait! Duvets in fresh colors & patterns" at least had an editorial hook.
- **No explicit deadline** — the holiday is 3 days out but the email never says "ends July 4" — the one thing that would justify another send in this campaign.
- **Hero copy is identical to prior sends** — "Extra 20% off / Select styles / Plus take deals up to 70% off" has appeared verbatim across most of this campaign's emails.
- **Open Box section is undersold** — a "Shop Now" CTA with minimal copy; no specific product callout, no sense of how deep the discount is on top of the already-reduced price.
- **Affirm placement at the bottom** is buried; it belongs closer to the hero for high-AOV furniture consideration.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add an explicit end date to the hero** — "Ends July 4" or a live countdown element would make this send feel necessary instead of redundant. That single change justifies the entire blast.
- 2. **Lead the Open Box section with a product** — show one hero Open Box item (a sofa or bed, specific name + was/now pricing) instead of just the category label. The June 28 duvet send proved product-first outperforms category-first.
- 3. **Move Affirm up to the hero or just below the CTA** — high-ticket furniture buyers factor financing into the click decision; burying it below recommended products loses the persuasion moment.
- 4. **Cut the Halloween-adjacent creative entirely** — nothing like that appears here, which is the right call after the June 30 audited send that mixed July and October creative.
- 5. **Subject Alt A:** `Ends July 4: Extra 20% off + Open Box deals from $X`
- 6. **Subject Alt B:** `3 days left — up to 70% off + Outlet prices drop more`
- 7. **Preheader Alt A:** `Open Box furniture at already-reduced prices, now extra 20% off. Ends Friday.`
- 8. **Preheader Alt B:** `Your recommended picks are on sale — plus Open Box deals you won't see again.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**PHP code in `<style>` block (confirmed in HTML source)**
The following PHP server-side directives appear verbatim inside a `<style>` tag:
```css
header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
header("Cache-Control: post-check=0, pre-check=0", false);
header("Pragma: no-cache");
```
These are not CSS. They will not execute; they will be parsed as malformed CSS rules and discarded. However, their presence signals a template assembly error (PHP output that was meant for HTTP headers ended up concatenated into the HTML body) and may affect spam scoring.

**3 HTTP (non-HTTPS) image sources** — may be blocked by Gmail, Outlook, and iOS Mail:
