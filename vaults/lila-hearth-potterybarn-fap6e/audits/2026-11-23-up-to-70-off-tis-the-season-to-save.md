# UP TO 70% OFF 🎁 ‘Tis the season to save
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the same burgundy "Early Black Friday, up to 70% off" Pottery Barn template that has run since 11/16. It is at least the eighth send in about five days, after 11/22's "Early Black Friday deals you'll love + Holiday bedding, pillows & more SHIP FREE" and "Shop early, save BIG (UP TO 70% OFF)". The offer is clear and the category tiles are easy to scan. A subscriber who has seen seven near-identical versions has no reason to treat this one as new.
- The body is a clean stack of category tiles: Furniture, Sofas & Chairs, Bedding, Tabletop & Bar, Decor, Pillows & Throws, Bath and Gifts. Each has a tiered "up to X% off" line and a was/now price on one hero product. That price anchoring is strong. The email is also very long. A "Recommended just for you" block, an "In Stock & On Sale" tile, a Design Crew sweepstakes, app, rewards and Affirm modules, and heavy legal copy all follow the tiles, so the real message gets buried.
- Overall it is a competent, honest promo with a clear offer, but it is cadence fatigue with nothing fresh in it. The "Tis the season" subject is generic, and nothing in the render says why this send differs from yesterday's.

## What's working

- The hero banner leads with "up to 70% off" in large serif type, and a visible "Shop Now" button sits directly under it.
- Every category tile has a tiered discount, a named product and a was/now price (Aubrey Desk $2,297 → $2,067, Cozy Cloud Quilt $229 → $222, Dream Robe $129 → $103). That makes the discount concrete.
- The layout is a consistent two-column alternation of image and text. The eye moves down it easily and every tile has its own "Shop Now" link.
- A Gifts tile (up to 50% off) fits the season.
- The "Earn 10% Back in Rewards" module surfaces member benefits.

## What's weak

- The template is reused again, with the same banner and the same tiles. Nothing new, such as a Christmas Shop push or a delivery-date angle, is visible.
- "Up to 70%" in the hero is contradicted by the tiles, which show 50–60% for most categories. Only Tabletop, Decor and Pillows reach 70%. That reads as bait-and-switch.
- The email is very long, with a recommended-products grid, a sweepstakes, the app, rewards, Affirm and dense legal text. The sweepstakes and Affirm modules dilute the sale message.
- The "Recommended Just for You" grid has no prices or CTAs, only product names, so it carries no urgency.
- The "In Stock & On Sale" tile is stranded below the recommendations. It has no deadline and no context.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one new reason to open at the top. Examples are a countdown to Black Friday, a delivery-by-Christmas cutoff, or a single hero product. Drop the banner that has run since 11/16.
- 2. Cut the body to the top four or five categories and add a clear end date to the offer.
- 3. Move the sweepstakes, app, Affirm and recommendation modules to a secondary email, or at least below a final CTA.
- 4. Match the headline to the real depth of discount. For example, "Up to 70% off decor, pillows & tabletop".
- 5. Add prices and CTAs to the recommendations grid, or remove it.
- 6. Reduce the send frequency or segment the list, so recent clickers don't get a near-duplicate daily.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: up to 70% off decor, pillows & tabletop`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Order by Dec 15 for Christmas delivery + 70% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Quilts, robes and stockings, with free shipping on festive favorites.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Early Black Friday pricing on in-stock bedding, decor and gifts.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with a 650px layout. The QA pass rate is 57%. The main problems are widespread missing `alt` attributes, three non-HTTPS image sources (one of them a pixel that sends the recipient's email address in plaintext), and stray server-side code in the `<style>` block. The HTML source is truncated, so the footer and unsubscribe markup could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links:** 55 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they use click-redirect domains (e.g. `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Imdi...`). Destinations are unverified. This is a probe limitation, not a confirmed defect.
- **Non-HTTPS tracking pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` loads over plain HTTP. It can be blocked or rewritten by mail clients, so open tracking is unreliable. It also sends the recipient address in cleartext (see section 4).
- **Other pixels:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEv.../cat/1` and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/c43b008a-0f34-4af8-8394-0629477f2a4b?mi_cid=...` appear to be third-party pixels. Both are flagged for missing alt text.
- **Cross-brand asset host:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a Pottery Barn email loading a spacer from a sister brand's host. This is a minor dependency risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** About 45 images are flagged. Examples:
  - `slices_01.jpg` through `slices_10.jpg` under `/pb/2026/US/1123_MON_AM_EBF_ZigZag1/`
  - `slices_01.jpg` through `slices_18.jpg` under `/pb/2026/US/Email-Footer/`
  - the product images (`classic-velvet-stockings-l.jpg`, `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-125-l.jpg`, etc.)
  - `H26_Bottom_InStockAndOnSale.jpg` and `H26_Bottom_DesignCrewSweeps_01.jpg` / `_02.jpg`

  If these slices carry the offer and navigation text, image-blocking and screen-reader users get no content. Pure tracking pixels should use `alt=""`, but they are flagged too.
- **HTTP images:** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` may be blocked as mixed content.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **No language attribute:** `<html xmlns=...>` has no `lang` attribute.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom.
- **Stray code in CSS:** The second `<style>` block contains PHP-style statements: `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store...")`. These are invalid CSS. They are harmless in most clients but may corrupt the rule that follows in strict parsers.
- **Selector issue:** `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` are attribute selectors that only match an exact class value. I did not see either class in the visible portion of the HTML, so these rules may be dead.
- **Web font:** The Google Fonts `<link>` to Montserrat is stripped by many clients (Gmail, Outlook). The fallback stack was not visible in the truncated source.
- **Preheader:** It is correctly hidden with `display:none; mso-hide:all`, so no issue there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) were visible in the provided source or QA output.
- **Email address in URLs:** The recipient address is embedded in plaintext in query strings:
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?...&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`

  The `svopn` request goes over HTTP, so the address is exposed in transit and in proxy logs. Combined with `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">`, this does limit referrer leakage on click-through. It does not protect the HTTP pixel request itself.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe:** The header was not found. The QA tool notes it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed rather than a verified absence.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** The header was not found, so one-click unsubscribe is not confirmed. The same relay caveat applies.
- **Authentication-Results:** The header was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA tool attributes this to the relay.
- **Body footer:** The footer is not in the truncated source, so I can't confirm a visible unsubscribe link or physical postal address.
- **Sender:** `PotteryBarn@e.potterybarn.com` is a branded subdomain, which is consistent with proper alignment. This is not verifiable from the data given.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters:** All links in the visible source use opaque redirect tokens (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`). UTM or campaign parameters can't be assessed from the email alone.
- **Image paths:** Image paths encode the campaign (`1123_MON_AM_EBF_ZigZag1`). The product images load from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`, which is the live site CDN.
- **Landing pages:** Destinations behind the 55 tracked links weren't resolved, so landing-page alignment is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content slices (`ZigZag1/slices_01-10`, `Email-Footer/slices_01-18`, `H26_Bottom_*`, product images). Use `alt=""` on spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Serve `svopn.potterybarn.com` and the two `edm.potterybarn.com` banner images over HTTPS.
3. Stop putting the raw email address in `coreemail.html?email=` and `sv_sveme=`. Use a hashed or tokenized identifier.
4. Remove the PHP `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block.
5. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, fill in `<title>`, and drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
6. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` against the raw MIME source from the sending side. The relay may be stripping them. Also check SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment the same way.
7. Resolve the tracked redirects in a follow-up pass to verify landing pages and UTM parameters. Also check the footer for the unsubscribe link and postal address, using the untruncated HTML.
