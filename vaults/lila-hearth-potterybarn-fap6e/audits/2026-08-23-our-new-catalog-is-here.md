# Our NEW catalog is here
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-23**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's fall catalog announcement — a literal print-catalog cover ("Heritage Home") photographed as the hero, under "The Fall Catalog / Your first look has arrived." It's a category-agnostic teaser: no discount, no specific product push, just "Discover 1600+ New Arrivals" and a "Get Inspired" CTA. Compared to the run of straightforward sale sends this week (Bedding Bestsellers 5/10, On Sale AND Shipping Free 5/10), this one trades a concrete offer for pure inspiration/catalog-launch framing, which is honest but weak on urgency.
- Below the fold it's a long, well-organized scroll: category nav grid, an in-store Design Classes callout, a solid "Recommended Just For You" product grid (bed, curtain rod, dining table, dresser, sheet set, platform bed), then a strong seasonal pivot into "The Halloween Shop" with a genuinely well-composed ghost-decorated porch hero. The Halloween section is arguably the most compelling visual moment in the whole email and is buried two-thirds down.
- No offer, no urgency, no loyalty callout up top — this reads as a brand-awareness/catalog-drop email, not a conversion email. Fine for cadence variety, but it will underperform on clicks against Pottery Barn's own sale sends.

## What's working

- Clean, on-brand hero with the physical catalog cover — reinforces "new catalog" concept literally and clearly.
- Category grid (Pillows & Decor, Armchairs, Bedding, Bedroom Furniture, Dining Furniture, Tabletop) gives multiple clear entry points for different shoppers.
- "Recommended Just For You" product grid has real personalization framing with six distinct, well-photographed SKUs and prices-adjacent detail (dimensions/thread count).
- The Halloween Shop section is a strong, seasonally relevant visual — genuinely eye-catching porch/ghost styling.
- No render bugs — images all load, text is legible, layout is clean end to end.

## What's weak

- No offer anywhere in the email — zero %/$ off, no free shipping, nothing actionable for a deal-motivated shopper.
- Primary CTA "Get Inspired" is vague, not a verb+noun action tied to a product or category.
- The strongest visual (Halloween Shop) is stranded far down the scroll, past a design-classes ad and six product tiles — most readers won't reach it.
- "Design Classes" in-store callout is low-relevance filler for an email subscriber base that's mostly shopping online.
- Preview text just repeats "Discover 1600+ New Arrivals" already implied by hero copy — no complementary info.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with an offer or at minimum a single flagship new-arrival product shot instead of the abstract catalog cover — "new catalog" alone doesn't convert; pair it with "Free Shipping on Your First Order from the Catalog" or similar.
- 2. Move the Halloween Shop section up, directly under the hero — it's seasonally timely and the best creative in the email; don't let it get lost.
- 3. Replace "Get Inspired" CTA with something concrete: "Shop New Arrivals" or "Browse the Catalog."
- 4. Cut or shrink the Design Classes block — it's a distraction between the hero and the product-relevant content.
- 5. Add a loyalty/rewards teaser near the top (there's already a "10% Back in Rewards" card near the footer — surface it higher, it's more actionable than the catalog framing).

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit — Pottery Barn "Our NEW catalog is here"

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard ESP-templated HTML email (multi-table layout, MSO conditionals, hidden preheader) with a broad set of accessibility and compliance gaps: no image alt text anywhere in the source, several image assets loaded over plain HTTP, and missing unsubscribe/authentication headers at the transport layer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 56 tracking/redirect links (`click.e.potterybarn.com`, `clickcp.potterybarn.com`, `d.turn.com`) were skipped by the automated HTTP probe since they are click-tracking redirects, not final URLs — destination integrity is unconfirmed.
- Multiple open/tracking pixels present:
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` — served over **HTTP**, and the recipient's email address is passed in cleartext as a URL query parameter.
  - `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` — third-party tracking pixel (Turn/Amobee).
  - `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/ca6dd656-343d-4ef7-b8b5-efa8883042f6?mi_mid=...` — additional open-tracking beacon.
- No issues found with primary CTA link structure itself (standard `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=` redirect wrapper).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **0 of ~50+ `<img>` elements carry `alt` text** (QA flagged every image, including navigation banner, product shots, and footer social icons) — screen readers and image-blocked clients will render no fallback content.
- 3 images load over **plain HTTP**, which mixed-content policies in modern mail clients may block or flag:
  - `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
- **Malformed `<style>` block**: the head contains literal PHP-style code inside a CSS `<style>` tag —
  ```
  header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
  header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
  header("Cache-Control: post-check=0, pre-check=0", false);
  header("Pragma: no-cache");
  ```
  This is invalid CSS (parsed as unrecognized selectors/declarations and ignored by clients), and strongly suggests server-side code was accidentally pasted into the template rather than executed — a build/template hygiene defect worth flagging to the ESP even though it's currently inert.
- Table-based layout with MSO conditional comments and `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` is correctly structured for Outlook/Apple Mail rendering — no structural issues found.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- Recipient identifier `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is correctly resolved (not a raw `%%EMAIL%%`/`{{merge}}` placeholder) in both the customer-service link (`coreemail.html?email=...`) and the open-tracking pixel (`sv_sveme=...`).
- No unresolved or broken merge tags found in the visible source.
- Note: personalized email address being passed in a **plaintext, HTTP** tracking-pixel URL (`svopn.potterybarn.com`) is a minor PII-exposure concern (see §2).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** — may be a relay capture limitation (AgentMail) rather than a true sender omission; cannot confirm CAN-SPAM one-click compliance from headers alone.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found** (RFC 8058) — one-click unsubscribe support is unconfirmed.
- **`Authentication-Results` header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified via this relay.
- Footer does contain a `customer-service/coreemail.html` link (visible in source), suggesting an in-body unsubscribe/preference-center path exists, but the QA pipeline did not independently confirm unsubscribe functionality since tracking links were skipped.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All outbound merchandising links route through the `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=<opaque token>` redirector rather than exposing direct `potterybarn.com` URLs with UTM parameters — final landing-page destinations and UTM tagging cannot be verified from the HTML alone (redirects were excluded from the HTTP probe per §2).
- Static image assets do resolve to a consistent `potterybarn.com` / `edm.potterybarn.com` domain structure, consistent with the brand.
- No direct evidence of broken or mismatched landing pages — this category is **unverified**, not confirmed clean, due to redirect-skip limitations.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all `<img>` tags, particularly product images and the top nav banner — currently 100% missing.
2. Migrate the 3 HTTP-only image/pixel URLs to HTTPS (`edm.westelm.com`, `edm.potterybarn.com/thekey`, `edm.potterybarn.com/2022/...BrandBanner`).
3. Remove or fix the stray `header(...)` PHP snippet embedded inside the `<style>` block in `<head>` — verify with the ESP/template owner whether this was pasted in error.
4. Confirm with the sending platform whether `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent (vs. dropped by the AgentMail relay) to ensure RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe compliance.
5. If feasible, re-run link verification against the resolved (post-redirect) destinations to confirm landing-page/UTM alignment, since the current pipeline skips all `click.e./clickcp.` tracking redirects.
6. Consider whether passing the recipient's raw email address in a plaintext HTTP tracking-pixel query string (`svopn.potterybarn.com`) meets current data-handling standards.
