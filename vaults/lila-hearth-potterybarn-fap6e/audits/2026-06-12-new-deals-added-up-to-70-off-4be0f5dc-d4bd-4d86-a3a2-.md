# NEW DEALS ADDED! Up to 70% off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-12**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's nth warehouse-sale refresh email — a full-width hero banner announcing 70% off, a 10-category grid, a "Recommended Just For You" product rail, then a stack of utility modules (design services, app download, rewards, Affirm, social). The bones are solid: the hierarchy is clean, the personalization rail is genuinely useful, and the loyalty callout is visible. But after the smart editorial pivots on June 10–11 (lightweight quilts, quick-ship furniture, rugs by color, free design services), landing back on a raw "NEW DEALS ADDED!" shout feels like a regression. The June 9 bedding email scored 8/10 by pulling a single category out of the sale and making it feel curated — this email does the opposite, scattering attention across all ten categories at once and letting the discount do all the heavy lifting.
- The "RECOMMENDED JUST FOR YOU" section is the most valuable thing in this email and it's buried below the category grid. Flip the order and this email is meaningfully more effective. As it stands, it reads like an automated blast wearing a personalization accessory.
- Verdict: competent execution of a tired template. The discount depth (70%) is the only real differentiator from the June 9–10 sends, and "up to" disclaimers soften that considerably.

## What's working

- **Hero is immediate and legible** — "Summer Sale / Up to 70% off" reads in one glance; no ambiguity about what this email is.
- **Loyalty reward visible** — "Earn 10% Back in Rewards" is present in the body; reinforces membership value for enrolled subscribers.
- **"Recommended Just For You" rail** — actual personalized product thumbnails with names is a genuine lift over a pure-discount grid; rare to see this in a PB sale send.
- **Clean render** — no broken images, overlapping text, or layout failures visible in the screenshot.
- **Design services cross-sell held over** — the free design services block from the June 10 email continues to appear, keeping that value proposition alive.

## What's weak

- **Subject-line ALL CAPS is the biggest inbox-level mistake** — "NEW DEALS ADDED!" triggers soft spam signals and reads as an automated retailer blast, not a curated send.
- **Category grid scatters attention** — ten categories listed (Furniture, Outdoor, Sofas, Bedding, Decor, Pillows, Tabletop, Rugs, Lighting, Bath) means no category gets focus; contrasts badly with the targeted approach that made the June 9 bedding email and June 11 quilt email work.
- **No deadline creates fake urgency** — "New Deals Added" implies scarcity but there's no end date, so the urgency evaporates.
- **Asterisk on "NEW DEALS ADDED*"** — implies fine print exclusions; undermines the headline claim before the subscriber even scrolls.
- **Personalization buried** — the "Recommended Just For You" rail lives below the fold, after the category grid; the most compelling element is in the worst position.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the personalized rail, not the category grid.** The "Recommended Just For You" products should be the hero unit, with the 70% off banner as a supporting label above them — not the other way around. This is the single highest-impact structural change.
- 2. **Kill the ALL CAPS and exclamation in the subject.** Reframe as specificity, not volume.
- 3. **Collapse the category grid to 3–4 hero categories** (or eliminate it entirely in favor of the personalized rail). Covering all ten at once means nothing stands out.
- 4. **Add a concrete deadline.** Even "ends Sunday" creates real urgency without requiring a countdown timer.
- 5. **Add social proof to the product rail** — a star rating or "bestseller" label on the recommended products gives a reason to click beyond the discount.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your picks just dropped to 70% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Prices cut: summer furniture, bedding & rugs up to 70% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns on your top categories — ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Rugs, sofas, bedding: your recommendations are on sale now.`
