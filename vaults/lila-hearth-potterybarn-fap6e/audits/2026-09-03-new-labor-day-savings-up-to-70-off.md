# NEW Labor Day savings ☀️ UP TO 70% OFF
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-03**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's eighth burgundy "Up to 70% off" Labor Day banner in eight days, and the render offers nothing to distinguish it from the 2026-09-02 and 09-01 sends — same deep burgundy hero, same "100s of new deals added!" line, same three-tile bedding/sofas/furniture stack, same 8-tile category grid, same "Shop All Sale" button placement. Anyone who's opened even one of the prior seven emails has already seen this exact layout.
- The one new element worth noting is the "Halloween Shop / Enter if you dare" module about two-thirds down — a legitimate seasonal beat that breaks from the sale monotony — followed by a "Discover New Arrivals" banner. Both are buried below a very long sale stack, so most recipients scrolling on mobile will never reach them.
- Judgement: technically clean, zero render bugs, honest offer — but creatively this is a rerun, and the brand is now deep into promo fatigue territory with this exact template.

## What's working

- Hero renders cleanly with clear visual hierarchy: "Labor Day Sale" → "Up to 70% off" → "Shop Now" button, no overlapping text or broken images.
- "Ships Free" badges are visible on the Bedding and Rugs tiles, adding a concrete secondary incentive.
- Category-specific percentage callouts (70% bedding, 30% sofas & chairs, 60% furniture) give more specificity than a single blanket "up to 70%" claim.
- "Recommended Just For You" section signals personalization/browsing history, a step up from pure batch-blast content.
- App download and 10% rewards card modules at the bottom add legitimate incremental value beyond the sale push.

## What's weak

- Visually and structurally identical to the last several days of sends — same burgundy hero, same tile grid, same button copy, same order of sections.
- Extremely long single-column scroll (three sale tiles, an 8-tile grid, a recommended grid, Halloween module, New Arrivals module) before reaching anything transactional like rewards or app info — high mobile fatigue.
- The Halloween Shop and New Arrivals modules — the only genuinely fresh content — are pushed so far down most recipients won't scroll to them.
- "Up to 70% off" / "Up to 60%" / "Up to 30%" are all unqualified — no indication of what's actually included at the top discount tier, continuing the buried-specifics pattern from prior sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. Break the burgundy-hero template entirely for at least one send this week — lead with the Halloween Shop or New Arrivals content instead of another "Up to 70% off" banner; the brand has now run this same visual eight days straight and subscribers are almost certainly tuning it out.
- 2. Move "Recommended Just For You" up near the hero — it's the most personalized, highest-intent content in the email and it's currently buried under two full screens of generic sale tiles.
- 3. Trim the category grid from 8 tiles to 4-5; the current length guarantees most mobile readers never reach Halloween or New Arrivals.
- 4. Attach a real number to at least one "Up to X% off" claim (e.g., name the specific bedding collection at 70%) to reduce the generic-blast feel.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Labor Day picks: up to 70% off bedding`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New fall arrivals + the Halloween Shop just launched`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Bedding, sofas, and furniture — all on sale through Labor Day`
