# Grinchy mischief for your holiday home
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-24**
## Executive summary

- This is a Grinch collab send, but the top of the email undersells it. The oxblood "Extra 20% off Select Styles / Plus savings up to 70% off" banner, with its SHOP ALL SALE button, sits above the Grinch hero. That is the same banner from the last five sends, so the new collection has to fight the sale for attention. The Grinch hero is strong. It has a styled holiday table, a "SHOP NOW" button and the line "sure to make your heart grow three sizes, at least!" That line is on-brand and fun.
- The body is a long, polished lifestyle scroll: Grinch linens, a "Merry Grinchmas" pillow, wreaths and stockings. It ends in "SHOP THE FULL COLLECTION". Below that, the email turns into a generic catch-all: "Recommended Just For You" (curtain rod, dining table, rug, pendant), the Christmas Shop, Design Crew, the app, the credit card and Affirm. The Grinch idea gets diluted. The email has about eight competing modules and almost no prices on products.
- Judgment: the creative is good and the theme is a real change from the "Extra 20%" run (10/22 "ON NOW!", 10/23 "❤️ Take an EXTRA 20%", 10/24 "Save MORE"). The structure still leads with the old discount, and the email runs too long.

## What's working

- The Grinch collab is a fresh, seasonal story after four near-identical sale blasts.
- The hero photography is rich, with a red and green table setting and the Grinch plate, and it reads as holiday at a glance.
- The copy "grow three sizes" has personality and fits the brand voice.
- The collection scroll shows variety: linens, bedding, pillows, wreaths and stockings.
- The 20% off / up to 70% off banner is clear and honest, with "Exclusions apply. Prices as marked."

## What's weak

- The sale banner is first, so the new collection is second. It is the same banner as in the previous sends.
- Two CTAs, "SHOP ALL SALE" and "SHOP NOW", compete in the first screen.
- The Grinch collection images carry no product names or prices, so nothing is concrete.
- "Recommended Just For You" is unrelated to the Grinch theme and has no prices or offers.
- The email ends in a pile of modules (Christmas Shop, Design Crew, app, rewards card, Affirm) plus a long legal block.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the Grinch hero to the top and shrink the sale banner to a thin strip. That change would lift the email the most.
- 2. Add three or four named Grinch products with prices, such as the plate, pillow and stocking, and link each one.
- 3. Cut the generic recommendations, or make them holiday-only. Drop Affirm and the app block to a single footer line.
- 4. Say whether the Extra 20% applies to the Grinch collection. If it does, say so in the hero.
- 5. Keep one CTA above the fold.
- **Subject Alt A:** `The Grinch is at Pottery Barn 💚`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New: Grinch plates, pillows & stockings`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Limited collection of Grinch-inspired holiday decor, with extra 20% off select styles.`

# Technical Audit: Pottery Barn, "Grinchy mischief for your holiday home"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout. It has widespread missing alt text, three `http://` image sources, a stray PHP snippet inside a `<style>` block, and the recipient address in plain text in several URLs. The compliance and authentication headers could not be verified because the relay didn't capture them. The HTML source was truncated, so footer and unsubscribe markup was not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Destinations unverified.** 51 tracking links were skipped as click-redirect domains, for example `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...`. No HTTP probe was done, so the final destinations and status codes are unknown.
- **Plain-text part.** 9,804 of 13,990 characters (70%) are URLs. This is a spam-score risk for the text alternative.
- **Insecure tracking pixels.**
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is served over HTTP.
  - `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` is a third-party pixel with no alt text.
  - `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/b9f72e3e-...?mi_cid=...` is another pixel with no alt text.
- **Recipient email in URLs.** The address is sent in plain text in the query string of `coreemail.html?email=...` and of the `svopn` pixel. Anyone who sees the URL (proxies, logs) sees the address.
- **Cross-brand asset.** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on a West Elm domain. A failure on that host would affect this send.
- **Stale asset paths.** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` is a 2022 asset path reused in a 2026 send. It was not probed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** About 45 images are flagged. Many are the content itself:
  - The Grinch collab slices `slices_01.gif` to `slices_09.jpg` under `/H26_Collab_B2_Grinch/`.
  - The top and bottom banners `H26_TOP_Extra20CLR.jpg`, `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg` and `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`.
  - The product images, including `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg` and `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg`.
  - The 18 footer slices `Email-Footer/slices_01` to `slices_18`.
  - With images blocked or read by a screen reader, those sections convey nothing. The footer slices are the most serious case, because footer content (legal text, links) may be image-only.
- **Mixed content.** Three `http://` images may be blocked by some clients: `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `slices_02.jpg` (the 2022 brand banner) and the `svopn` pixel.
- **Stray code in CSS.** The second `<style>` block contains PHP: `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store, ...");`. Some clients may drop or mis-parse the rules that follow it.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Viewport.** `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Responsive rules.** `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` are used in the 480px media query. The visible markup uses `class="contenttable"` rather than `wrapper`, so those rules may not match anything. This is unconfirmed because the source is truncated.
- **Preheader.** The preheader div uses `font-size:9px` and is hidden with `display:none` and `mso-hide:all`. It is properly hidden.
- **Image max-width.** `img {max-width: 650px}` applies at min-width 700px, then `max-width:100%!important` at min-width 650px. These conflict in the overlapping range.
- **Google Fonts.** The `<link>` to `fonts.googleapis.com` is stripped by most clients and has no fallback stack beyond what is set inline.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens were visible. The recipient address resolved correctly in `coreemail.html?email=` and `sv_sveme=`. The only concern is that the address is exposed in plain text (see section 2).

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found.** The QA tool notes this may be a relay artifact, so it is unverified rather than confirmed missing.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found.** Without it there is no RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders, so confirm it on the original message.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The `From` domain is `e.potterybarn.com`.
- **Unsubscribe link and postal address not confirmed.** The truncated HTML doesn't show them. If they are inside the unlabeled footer slices (`Email-Footer/slices_*.jpg`), they are unreadable to text-only clients and screen readers.
- **Privacy meta tags.** `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` and `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` are present and harmless.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- No UTM parameters are visible in the shown links. Links go through the opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=` wrapper, so campaign attribution and landing-page alignment can't be confirmed from the source.
- Product images load from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`, so the images match the shop domain.
- The cross-brand `edm.westelm.com` asset is the only mismatch.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to all content images. Prioritize the Grinch collab slices, banners, product images and footer slices, and use `alt=""` for `spacer_0.gif` and the tracking pixels.
2. Make sure the unsubscribe link and postal address are live text, not image-only footer slices.
3. Move the three `http://` assets (`Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, the 2022 `slices_02.jpg`, and the `svopn` pixel) to HTTPS.
4. Remove the PHP `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` on the raw message, and check SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `e.potterybarn.com`.
6. Stop putting the recipient email in query strings. Use an opaque token instead.
7. Fill in `<title>`, remove `maximum-scale=1`, and fix the 480px media query selectors so they match the real classes.
8. Host `spacer_0.gif` on the sender's own domain, or remove it.
9. Reduce the URL ratio in the plain-text part.
10. Re-run the audit against the full HTML to check UTM parameters and the footer.
