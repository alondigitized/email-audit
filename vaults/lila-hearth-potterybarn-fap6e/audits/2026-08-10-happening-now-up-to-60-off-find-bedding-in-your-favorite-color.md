# Happening now! Up to 60% off + Find bedding in your favorite color
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's "shop by color" bedding send, and it's actually one of the cleaner sends this week — the theme is genuinely maintained top to bottom: "Layers of Color" hero, a color-swatch grid (Blue, Rosewood, Green, Neutral), then "More Bedding Essentials," "Recommended Just For You," and a New Arrivals/Halloween Shop tail. Unlike 2026-08-09's "UP TO 60% off furniture, bedding & more" catalog dump, this one earns its subject line — it's actually about bedding in different colors, and "it all ships free" is a nice honest touch buried in the hero subhead.
- The subject line itself is doing too much work, though — "Happening now! Up to 60% off + Find bedding in your favorite color" is two subject lines stapled together, and the exclamation point plus vague "Happening now!" opener reads as filler rather than urgency. The visible hero banner only promises "up to 60% off" and "up to 15% off Belgian Flax Linen Bedding," while the color-grid section has no visible discount at all — so the "60% off" in the subject isn't clearly the same offer being led with in the body. Still, this is a tighter, more coherent execution than most of this week's Pottery Barn sends, closer to the 2026-08-08 "Now on sale: Bestselling bedding" send (8/10) than to the scattershot catalog dumps.

## What's working

- Clear, single visual theme carried the whole top half — "Layers of Color" hero into four color-coded shop tiles (Blue, Rosewood, Green, Neutral), each with two named products.
- "It all ships free" in the hero subhead is a concrete, honest secondary hook that doesn't require a promo code.
- Color-tile grid is scannable — each tile has a distinct hue swatch, a clear "SHOP [COLOR]" button, and two labeled products beneath it.
- "Shop Bedding by Color" CTA button anchors the section before moving into cross-sell.
- No render bugs — images loaded cleanly, text is legible, no overlap.

## What's weak

- Subject line's "Up to 60% off" isn't reflected anywhere near the top of the email — the hero only shows 15% off linen bedding; the 60% figure doesn't surface until deep in the footer's "Warehouse Sale" language, which is a bait-and-switch mismatch.
- Subject crams two offers into one line ("Happening now!" + "60% off" + "find bedding in your favorite color"), diluting focus and reading as promo-copy soup rather than one clear hook.
- After the strong color-grid section, the email sprawls into curtain rods, rugs, a platform bed, New Arrivals, and a full Halloween Shop promo — same scope-creep pattern flagged in 2026-08-08's "living room" send.
- No pricing, sale badges, or "% off" shown on any individual product tile in the color grid — a shopper can't tell if "Coastal Washed Quilt" or "Cozy Cloud Quilt" is actually discounted.

## Recommendations

- 1. Align hero copy with the subject — either put "Up to 60% off" directly in or near the hero banner where the shopper can see it, or drop that phrase from the subject entirely so it doesn't feel like a broken promise this deep into a scroll.
- 2. Split the subject into one clean idea instead of two stapled ones — lead with the color-picker hook, which is the section that's actually well-executed.
- 3. Add price/discount badges to the individual color-tile products (duvets, quilts) so the offer is visible at the point of decision, not just in the hero.
- 4. Trim the tail — New Arrivals, Halloween Shop, curtain rods/rugs/beds is a lot after a focused bedding-color section; consider a separate send for Halloween given it's mid-August.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Which color speaks to you? New bedding, up to 15% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Layers of Color: shop bedding by hue — free shipping`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Blue, rosewood, green, or neutral — find your bedding palette.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New Belgian Flax Linen bedding, up to 15% off. Ships free.`
