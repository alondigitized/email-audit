# Our Memorial Day Sale STARTS NOW 🔔
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn lands the Memorial Day sale launch cleanly: "Extra 20% off" is the hero, the category grid follows logically, and the "Recommended Just for You" personalization block is a smart value-add below the fold. This is a functional, competently executed promotional email — the visual hierarchy works, the photography is on-brand, and the category-level CTAs (Bedding, Sofas & Chairs, Pillows & Decor, Outdoor) give different intent signals a place to land.
- That said, this email plays it safe to the point of feeling generic. "Memorial Day Sale STARTS NOW" is the same subject line structure every major retailer uses this week, and the offer — 20% off select styles, plus broader clearance — is buried under hedging language rather than led with the sharpest number. The "Recommended Just for You" section is a genuine differentiator but shows up well below the fold, after the app download block and Affirm pitch have already eaten attention.
- Compared to last week's brand history — the Dormify collab, the celeb sofa hook, the personalized gift curation — this reads like Pottery Barn switched from editorial voice to blast-promo mode. The subscriber who's been getting aspirational lifestyle content for five days straight may notice the tonal shift. It's effective, but it's playing at a lower ceiling.

## What's working

- **Hero offer is immediate and clear** — "Extra 20% off" dominates the top fold, with a second CTA ("SHOP ALL SALE") giving browsers a softer on-ramp.
- **Category grid is well-structured** — Bedding, Sofas & Chairs, Pillows & Decor, and Outdoor each get their own lifestyle image and SHOP NOW, making it easy to self-segment.
- **"Recommended Just for You" personalization** — even if it's algorithmic, naming it explicitly signals relevance and earns a second scroll.
- **No render bugs** — layout is clean, images load, text doesn't overlap, CTAs are visible.
- **Timing is credible** — Memorial Day is real, seasonal, and this week; the urgency doesn't feel manufactured.

## What's weak

- **No deadline visible** — "STARTS NOW" implies urgency, but no end date or "through Monday" copy is visible; the clock has no face.
- **"Select styles" qualifier softens the headline** — "Extra 20% off select styles" trains the eye to immediately discount the discount; bury that caveat or cut it from the hero.
- **App download and Affirm blocks interrupt the purchase path** — they appear between the category grid and the recommended products, fragmenting momentum at the worst moment.
- **Subject line is undifferentiated** — every home brand in the inbox this week says "Memorial Day Sale STARTS NOW." Nothing in subject or preheader quotes the actual offer to stand out.
- **Recommended products are small and hard to parse** — the product tiles in that section appear thumbnail-sized and text-light; likely a missed upsell opportunity.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put a deadline in the hero** — "Ends Monday" or "Through May 26" next to the offer transforms "starts now" urgency into a real countdown and closes the commitment loop.
- 2. **Lead the subject with the number** — "Extra 20% off" is the strongest signal; it should be in the subject line, not buried in the preview or hero subhead.
- 3. **Collapse the app + Affirm interruptions** — move them to the footer; let the recommended products flow immediately after the category grid.
- 4. **Amplify the clearance story** — "up to 60% off" is a stronger anchor than "extra 20%"; flip the hierarchy or make them equally visible.
- 5. **Expand recommended product tiles** — larger images, visible price before/after, and a clear "for you" rationale ("Based on your recent views") would lift click rate on that block meaningfully.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 20% off starts now — Memorial Day Sale`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 60% off: Memorial Day Sale is live`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sofas, bedding, outdoor & more — ends Monday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `60% off clearance + extra 20% on select styles. Ends May 26.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**PHP header() code injected into a `<style>` block (template defect)**
The following appears verbatim inside a `<style>` tag in the `<head>`:
```css
header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
header("Cache-Control: post-check=0, pre-check=0", false);
header("Pragma: no-cache");
```
These are PHP HTTP response header calls that were never executed server-side — they ended up rendered as raw text inside CSS. Email clients ignore them as invalid CSS, but this confirms a template preprocessing failure. Whichever conditional or partial outputs these cache headers did not fire correctly.

**43 images missing `alt` text** — All content images, product images, footer slices, and both open-tracking pixels have empty or absent `alt` attributes. Images-off rendering (common in Outlook, corporate environments) produces a fully blank email. Screen readers receive no content.

**3 image assets served over HTTP (will be blocked)**
```
http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif
http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg
http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg
```
Gmail, Outlook.com, and Apple Mail proxy or block mixed-content image requests. The CC banner footer image (`Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`) is a visible content block, not a spacer — it will silently drop for a significant share of recipients.

**Open-tracking pixel served over HTTP**
```
http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true
  &sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app
```
HTTP open-tracking pixels are blocked by most modern clients. Open-rate data for this send will be artificially deflated.

**`maximum-scale=1` in viewport meta** — Prevents pinch-to-zoom on mobile. WCAG 1.4.4 violation for low-vision users.

**Empty `<title>` element** — `<title></title>` is blank. Assistive technologies announce document title on load; screen reader users get no context.

### 5. Compliance

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header absent** — Not captured by the AgentMail relay. CAN-SPAM requires a functional unsubscribe mechanism; Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender guidelines additionally require a `List-Unsubscribe` header and one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) for high-volume senders. Missing both increases spam-folder placement risk.

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) absent** — One-click unsubscribe is not supported. Gmail began enforcing this for senders >5k/day in Feb 2024.

**[WARN] `Authentication-Results` header not captured** — SPF and DKIM pass/fail status is unknown for this send. Cannot confirm whether `e.potterybarn.com` is properly aligned. If DMARC is enforced on the root `potterybarn.com` domain, misalignment on the `e.` subdomain would cause failures.
