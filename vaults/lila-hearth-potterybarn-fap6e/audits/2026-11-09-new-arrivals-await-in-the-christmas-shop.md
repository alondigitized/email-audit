# New arrivals await in The Christmas Shop
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a good Christmas-shop launch with a crowded top. The hero, "The Christmas Shop," is a rich tartan-ornament photo with a "SHOP NOW" button, and the "warmth, nostalgia, and time spent together" line sets the tone. The category grid below it (pillows & throws, holiday bedding, stockings, botanicals, tabletop, ornaments) is the kind of browsing layout that works for an early-November gifting and decorating push.
- The weak spot is the top banner. "The Fall Sale: Up to 70% off" has its own "SHOP NOW" button and sits above a Christmas hero. Two promotions compete for the first screen, and the more concrete offer (70% off) belongs to a season that is ending. The Christmas hero itself carries no price or offer, only "Ships Free" tags on some tiles. Those tags echo the Nov 7 and Nov 9 sends ("Ships free 🎄 Festive pillows & more decor", "Now shipping free 🎄 Holiday essentials"), so this is the third near-identical Christmas push in three days.
- The body then drifts into a long "Recommended just for you" block, a design-services promo, an app download, a credit card pitch and Affirm. Dense legal text closes it out. Judgement: attractive and on-season, but unfocused. It would convert better with one clear offer and a shorter tail.

## What's working

- The Christmas hero is evocative, with tartan ornaments and a warm palette. It signals the season right away.
- The tiles are clean and editorial, each with a one-line label and a "Ships Free" tag where it applies. They are easy to scan.
- The copy "a season of warmth, nostalgia, and time spent together" sounds like a real brand and fits the product.
- "Recommended just for you" suggests personalization, with stockings and a dining table likely matched to browsing.
- The Christmas Shop button after the grid gives a clear catch-all path.

## What's weak

- The "Fall Sale: Up to 70% off" banner competes with the Christmas hero. It is also a seasonal mismatch for Nov 9, and its offer is never connected to the Christmas content.
- Two "SHOP NOW" buttons at the top point at different campaigns, so there is no single primary action.
- The Christmas section has no price or discount, and "Ships Free" is only on some tiles. The reader can't tell what the offer is.
- The tail is overloaded: design services, the app, a rewards card, Affirm and social icons. Dense fine print follows, which makes the offer feel heavier with exclusions.
- The cadence is repetitive. This is the third Christmas-themed send in three days, with similar imagery and tiles.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one message for the top. Drop the Fall Sale banner, or move it below the hero as a secondary strip. Put one clear offer on the Christmas hero, such as "Up to 30% off" or "Ships free on holiday décor."
- 2. Make the hero button specific, for example "Shop Christmas Decor" or "Shop Stockings," in place of a generic "SHOP NOW."
- 3. Apply "Ships Free" to every tile or to none, so it reads as an offer and not a decoration.
- 4. Show prices or sale tags in the "Recommended" grid.
- 5. Cut the tail to one service module (the app, the card or Affirm) and shorten the legal block.
- 6. Vary the creative from the Nov 7 and Nov 9 sends, for example with a "new this week" angle that matches the subject's "new arrivals."
- **Subject Alt A:** `New this week: The Christmas Shop is open`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tartan, candlelight & stockings — new for Christmas`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stockings, ornaments and tabletop, plus free shipping on select décor.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fresh holiday styles just landed, and the Fall Sale ends soon.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with 56 wrapped click-tracking links. Its main technical gaps are widespread missing `alt` text on image slices, three plain-`http://` images (one carrying the recipient address in the query string), and a stray block of PHP text inside a `<style>` tag. The automated QA run scored 57% pass, with 0 hard failures and 3 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** All 56 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains, e.g. `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...`. Destination validity and redirect health are unconfirmed.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is a plaintext HTTP open pixel. It can be blocked as mixed content.
- **Multiple tracking vendors:** The email loads three separate tracking pixels:
  - `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1`
  - `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/20e78e77-...?mi_cid=...`
  - the `svopn` pixel above

  Each is an extra third-party request, and none has alt text.
- **Shared asset host:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on a sibling brand's domain, which couples this email's rendering to another property's CDN.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** About 45 `<img>` elements are flagged, so the email is largely unreadable with images off or in a screen reader. Affected files:
  - the entire `.../1101_SUN_AM_ChristmasShopLaunch/slices_01–12.jpg` hero set
  - `.../Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`
  - the product images (`toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-125-l.jpg`, `classic-velvet-stockings-l.jpg`, etc.)
  - `H26_TOP_FallSale.jpg`
  - `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`

  The missing `alt` attribute (rather than `alt=""`) means screen readers may announce filenames.
- **Pixels flagged as missing alt:** `spacer_0.gif` and the tracking pixels are flagged too. For these, `alt=""` is the correct fix.
- **Non-HTTPS images:** Two content images use `http://`:
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>`.
- **Missing language:** No `lang` attribute is visible on `<html xmlns=...>`.
- **Stray code in CSS:** The second `<style>` block ends with `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ...` PHP text. It is invalid CSS and harmless today because it follows the last real rule, but it is a template defect.
- **Table roles:** Some layout tables have `role="presentation"` and the spacer table `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><tr><td height="20">` does not.
- **Stale asset path:** `/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/` suggests a reused 2022 module.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) were found in the visible source or QA output.
- **Email address in URLs:** The recipient address is embedded in plaintext query strings, with no hashing:
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - the `sv_sveme=` parameter on the `http://` pixel above

  The `http://` case exposes it unencrypted in transit.
- **Wrong element:** `coreemail.html` is loaded as an `<img>`, but the path suggests an HTML page. It is probably a tracking beacon, and it is flagged for missing alt.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** Not found, and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) is not found either. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed**. It is a real issue only if the headers are absent at origin. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not captured, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. It is a relay limitation, not evidence of failure.
- **Footer unsubscribe and address:** Not verifiable, because the supplied HTML is truncated before the footer. The footer is built from `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`, all missing alt text. If the unsubscribe link and postal address exist only as image slices, they are not accessible and are not detectable as text.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM visibility:** No `utm_*` parameters are visible. Links are wrapped in opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` redirects and none were resolved, so campaign attribution and landing-page alignment **cannot be confirmed** from this data.
- **Direct links:** Product images link to `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...` asset paths. Whether their `href`s deep-link to PDPs was not testable.
- **Cross-brand asset:** The `edm.westelm.com` spacer is a cosmetic mismatch only.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content slices and product images, and `alt=""` to spacers and tracking pixels. Hero and footer slices need text equivalents.
2. Move the 3 `http://` assets (`Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `slices_02.jpg`, `svopn` pixel) to HTTPS.
3. Stop passing the raw recipient email in URL query strings (`coreemail.html?email=`, `sv_sveme=`). Use a hashed or opaque ID.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` against the raw origin message, bypassing the relay. Likewise confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `e.potterybarn.com`.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1`, add a `<title>` and `lang="en"`, and delete the stray `header(...)` PHP text from the `<style>` block.
6. Resolve a sample of `click.e.potterybarn.com` links to verify destinations and UTM presence. Also confirm the unsubscribe link and postal address exist as live text in the footer.
7. Consolidate the redundant tracking pixels (`turn.com`, `clickcp`, `svopn`) where possible.
