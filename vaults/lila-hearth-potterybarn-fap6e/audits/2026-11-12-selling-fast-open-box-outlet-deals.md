# Selling fast: Open Box Outlet Deals
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- The "Selling fast: Open Box Outlet Deals" send has a good hook and a thin payoff. The red hero is clear: "SELLING FAST / Open Box Outlet Deals" with a white "SHOP NOW" button. It's the only part of the email that matches the subject. After that the email turns into a generic Pottery Barn catch-all: Design Crew, "Recommended Just For You", the Christmas Shop, the sweepstakes, the app, the credit card and Affirm.
- The email never shows an open-box item. There is no product, no price, no "was/now" and no condition note. The only hard number is "Up to 70% off" in the top banner, and that is the Fall Sale, not the outlet. A reader who opens for outlet deals gets stockings, curtain rods and dining tables at full price.
- It's also the same stack as earlier today. The "Fall Sale: Up to 70% off" banner opened the "Thoughtful gifts for everyone on your list" send. The $3,000 Design Crew sweepstakes block repeats the "Get a chance to win a $3,000 gift card" email from the same day. This reads as the fourth or fifth template-sharing send in about 48 hours. Judgement: a clean render and a decent subject, but it overpromises and then recycles.

## What's working

- The red hero block is the strongest visual on the page, and its "Selling fast" eyebrow carries the subject's urgency into the body.
- The "SHOP NOW" buttons are high-contrast and easy to find.
- "Up to 70% off" sits in the very first banner, so a concrete discount is visible immediately.
- The 2x3 "Recommended Just For You" grid is tidy and well photographed, with product names and sizes, such as the 76"–128" extendable table.
- The email renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlapping text, and the holiday imagery fits mid-November.

## What's weak

- No open-box product, price or condition detail appears anywhere, so the subject's promise isn't delivered.
- Two sale messages compete at the top: the Fall Sale banner and the Open Box hero. The reader has to work out which discount applies to which.
- The "Recommended Just For You" grid shows no prices and no open-box items, so it doesn't support the hero.
- The back half runs long: Christmas Shop, sweepstakes, app, credit card, Affirm and a wall of fine print. It dilutes the one idea the subject set up.
- The sweepstakes block and fine print repeat the earlier "win a $3,000 gift card" send from the same day, which adds to the cadence fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put real open-box products in the hero or directly under it. Each needs a photo, a "was $X / now $Y" price and a short condition note such as "open box, like new". Give the outlet one dominant button.
- 2. Drop the Fall Sale banner from this send, or move it below the outlet content. One offer per email.
- 3. Replace the generic recommendation grid with 4–6 live outlet items, labelled with the percent saved.
- 4. Cut the sweepstakes, app, card and Affirm blocks from outlet sends, or collapse them into a one-line footer.
- 5. Add a credible deadline such as "while quantities last" with a real stock cue, for example "Only 3 left" on individual items.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Open-box deals: up to 70% off, limited stock`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Returned, not used: outlet finds from $49`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Open-box tables, chairs and decor, marked down and ready to ship.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Once they're gone, they're gone. See what just landed in the outlet.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Pottery Barn, "Selling fast: Open Box Outlet Deals"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional with an MSO fallback and a hidden preheader. It has widespread missing alt attributes, three cleartext `http://` image requests (one carrying the recipient's email address), and leaked server-side code in a `<style>` block. Authentication and unsubscribe headers could not be assessed because the AgentMail relay does not expose them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links.** 48 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are on click-redirect domains. Examples are `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7ImQi...` and the open pixel at `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/c1b66afe-...?mi_cid=...`. Destination resolution and status codes are unverified.
- **Cleartext tracking pixel.** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` is loaded over HTTP. Clients that block mixed content will not fire it, so open tracking is lost.
- **Third-party pixel.** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` is an extra ad-network beacon with no alt text.
- **Cross-brand asset host.** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a West Elm host in a Pottery Barn send. This is low severity, but it adds a dependency on another brand's CDN.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on content images.** About 40 images flagged by QA have no alt attribute. Examples:
  - Product images: `holiday-icons-crewel-stockings-2-l.jpg`, `classic-velvet-stockings-l.jpg`, `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`, `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-125-l.jpg`, `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`, `standard-curtain-rod-wall-bracket-l.jpg`.
  - Banners: `H26_TOP_FallSale.jpg`, `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`, `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg`, `H26_Bottom_DesignCrewSweeps_01.jpg`, `H26_Bottom_DesignCrewSweeps_02.jpg`.
  - Footer slices: `slices_01.jpg` through `slices_18.jpg` under `/pb/2026/US/Email-Footer/`. With images blocked, the footer, which probably holds links, is unreadable and unlabelled to screen readers.
  - Open Box hero: `/pb/2025/US/F25_Sale_B2_OpenBoxDeals/slices_01.jpg`.
- **Tracking pixels without alt.** These are `coreemail.html?email=...`, `d.turn.com`, `svopn`, and `clickcp`. A 1x1 beacon should carry `alt=""`.
- **HTTP image sources.** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`, and the svopn pixel are non-HTTPS and may be blocked.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1 />` prevents pinch-zoom.
- **Leaked server code.** The second `<style>` block contains `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store, ...")`, which is PHP. It is invalid CSS and is ignored by renderers, but it points to a template or build defect.
- **Web font.** The Montserrat `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/...">` is stripped by many clients, so the fallback stack matters. I could not check the fallback in the truncated source.
- **Truncated source.** The HTML was cut off, so MSO/VML button handling and the footer markup could not be assessed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved tokens.** No `%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, or `${...}` placeholders were found in the visible source.
- **Recipient email in URLs.** The address is exposed as a plaintext query parameter in two places:
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?...&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`

  The second is sent over unencrypted HTTP, so the address can be seen in transit and in proxy or server logs. This is a privacy and PII leakage issue.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058).** Both are not found. QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay not capturing the headers, so it is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so verify against the raw message at the origin.
- **Authentication-Results.** Not found, so SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown. The relay is the stated cause. The sender domain `e.potterybarn.com` is aligned with the click domain `click.e.potterybarn.com`.
- **Footer unsubscribe and postal address.** These could not be verified in the truncated source. The footer is built from image slices, so a text unsubscribe link and physical address cannot be confirmed from the HTML shown.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Redirect tokens.** Links use opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` tokens, so landing URLs and any UTM parameters cannot be seen from source. Direct product image URLs on `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...` suggest the product links lead to potterybarn.com, but this is unconfirmed.
- **Stale asset paths.** `/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/` and `/pb/2025/US/F25_Sale_B2_OpenBoxDeals/` are in a 2026 holiday send. If the 2025 hero is reused, check that the offer and landing page still match.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to all product and banner images. Add `alt=""` to spacers and pixels. Give the footer slices alt text or replace them with live text links.
2. Move `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `slices_02.jpg`, and the `svopn` pixel to HTTPS.
3. Stop putting the raw email address in `coreemail.html?email=` and `sv_sveme=`. Use a hashed or opaque subscriber ID instead.
4. Check the raw message at the origin ESP for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, and for SPF, DKIM, and DMARC alignment. Do not rely on the relay copy.
5. Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP from the `<style>` block, set a real `<title>`, and drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport.
6. Resolve the 48 tracking links in a controlled click-through run to confirm destinations, redirect chains, and UTM parameters.
7. Retest on the full HTML. The source was truncated before the body content and footer.
