# More to love 🧡 EXTRA 20% OFF select styles
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-29**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth or sixth "Extra 20% off" send in about four days, and it's the least distinctive of them. The oxblood banner ("Extra 20% off Select Styles / Plus savings up to 70% off") is the same one used on Oct 26, 27 and 28. The only new element is the "On Sale & Shipping Free" hero, a draped fringe-throw photo. It's pretty and cozy, but it's pushed down by the banner and a wall of category links.
- The body is a grab-bag. A dining table, curtain hardware, a rug, curtains and stockings sit under "Recommended Just For You," followed by the Christmas Shop, Free Design Services, the app, rewards and Affirm. Nothing leads, and the Oct 28 send already ran the Christmas Shop and design-help ideas. The "just for you" grid reads as generic, and the offer is vague ("select styles") with a long fine-print block.
- Verdict: a competent but fatigued template send. It's fine as a reminder, but it adds nothing a subscriber hasn't seen all week.

## What's working

- The throw-blanket hero is warm and seasonal, and the "Shipping Free" line adds a second concrete benefit.
- The Pillows / Throws / Quilts / Duvets / Sheets category grid lets a shopper jump straight to bedding and textiles.
- The "Shop All Sale" button is clear and appears twice.
- The Christmas Shop block (tree and stockings) fits the late-October timing.
- The Free Design Services block with "Book a Free Appointment" is a distinct, specific CTA.

## What's weak

- The oxblood "Extra 20% off" banner is now stale after a week of identical use.
- "Select styles" with "Exclusions apply" offers no clear sense of what's discounted, and the fine print at the bottom is long and dense.
- The email has no single hero or priority. It stacks about eight modules (sale, recommended, Christmas, design, app, rewards, Affirm, social).
- The "Recommended Just For You" grid mixes unrelated categories (dining tables, curtain rods, a rug, stockings), so it doesn't feel personalized.
- The product tiles show no prices, no sale prices and no reviews, so nothing shows the discount.
- The bottom third (app, rewards card, Affirm) is clutter that dilutes the promo.

## Recommendations

- 1. Retire the identical banner. Lead with one hero (the throws, or a specific product at a visible sale price) and put the "Extra 20%" claim in a smaller strip.
- 2. Add sale prices and a star rating to the recommended tiles, and trim the grid to 4 products in one category.
- 3. State the deadline and the code or auto-apply logic ("Ends Sunday, applied at checkout") so the urgency is credible.
- 4. Cut the app, rewards and Affirm modules to a single footer row, and don't repeat the Christmas Shop and design blocks from Oct 28.
- 5. Move the fine-print exclusions into a short linked line.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Throws, quilts and duvets, plus free shipping on sale styles through Sunday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with 52 redirect-tracked links. Its main technical problems are about 45 images with no alt text, the recipient's email address in plaintext in pixel and image URLs, three `http://` image sources, and malformed `<head>` markup. The header-based compliance checks could not be confirmed from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 52 links on `click.e.potterybarn.com` (and similar redirect domains) were skipped by the QA probe. Destinations, redirect health and UTM handling are unconfirmed.
- **Mixed content:** Three images use `http://`:
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=…`

  These may be blocked or rewritten by clients. The last one is the open-tracking pixel, so a block means lost open data.
- **Third-party pixels:** The email loads `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/…/cat/1` (Turn ad network) and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/038a4c37-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. These are expected tracking pixels, but they are extra third-party calls.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on a West Elm domain. It works, but it is a dependency outside the sender's own brand domain.
- **Stale asset:** The footer banner path `/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/` dates from 2022, which suggests reused legacy modules.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, about 45 images:** The QA check flagged these. Examples:
  - Hero banner: `H26_TOP_Extra20CLR.jpg`
  - Sliced content: `1029_THU_AM_FreeShipOnSale/slices_01–10.jpg`
  - Product images: `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`, `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg`, `belgian-flax-linen-curtain-1-l.jpg`
  - Footer slices: `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`

  The sliced and product images carry content and links, so with images off the email is blank and the links have no accessible name. Tracking pixels and spacers are fine without alt text, but they should still use `alt=""`.
- **Empty `<title>`:** The source has `<title></title>`.
- **No `lang` attribute:** The `<html>` tag has none, which matters for screen readers.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` prevents pinch-zoom, an accessibility failure.
- **Stray code in the stylesheet:** A `<style>` block contains PHP-style text (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store…")`). It is invalid CSS. Parsers will drop it, but it can corrupt the rule that follows it in strict clients.
- **Missing `role="presentation"`:** The wrapper tables near the top and the spacer table (`<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><tr><td height="20">&nbsp;</td>`) don't have it. Other tables do, so the usage is inconsistent.
- **Fragile attribute selectors:** `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` fail if a client appends classes.
- **Web font:** The Montserrat Google Fonts `<link>` is stripped by most clients, so it needs a safe fallback stack.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Recipient email in URLs:** The address `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` appears unencoded in image and tracking URLs:
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=…`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?…&sv_sveme=…`

  This exposes PII in a query string. The pixel is also plain `http://`, so the address travels in cleartext and can appear in proxy and server logs.
- **Unrendered tokens:** None are visible in the truncated source, and the visible portion shows no raw `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` tokens.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** The QA check flagged both as not found. The check itself says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed, not a verified defect**. The RFC 8058 one-click header cannot be assessed from this data.
- **SPF/DKIM/DMARC:** `Authentication-Results` was not captured, so authentication status is unknown.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** The HTML is truncated before the footer, so neither can be confirmed.
- **Plain-text part:** The plain-text alternative is 9,998 of 14,183 characters URLs (about 70%), which is a spam-filter risk. It is likely long tracking URLs, not an error.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters:** None are visible. Links go through opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…` redirects, so campaign attribution depends on the ESP's redirect layer.
- **Product images:** They load directly from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/…`, which ties email rendering to site CDN availability.
- **Landing pages:** They could not be checked because the probe skipped all tracked links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to every content and linked image. Use `alt=""` for spacers and pixels. This covers the sliced banners, the product images and the 18 footer slices.
2. Remove the recipient email from the `coreemail.html` and `svopn` URLs, or replace it with a hashed or opaque ID. Serve these over HTTPS.
3. Move the three `http://` assets to `https://`.
4. Delete the PHP `header(...)` text from the `<style>` block.
5. Fill in `<title>`, add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
6. Re-run the audit against raw `.eml` headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Also check the full footer for the unsubscribe link and postal address.
7. Probe a sample of the 52 tracked links through the redirect to confirm destinations and UTM carry-through.
8. Retire the 2022 footer module and the cross-brand `westelm.com` spacer.
