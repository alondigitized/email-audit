# Now open: The Holiday Botanical Shop
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-04**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, editorial holiday send with no discount. "Holiday Botanicals" sits over a wreath-and-garland photo, with a "SHOP NOW" button and a "SHOP ALL DECOR" link below it. It follows the same no-promo pattern as the Nov 4 "New arrivals with timeless holiday charm" send and the Nov 3 new-arrivals send. The big oxblood "Extra 20% off" banner is gone.
- The body is well organized: Floral Stems & Branches, Floral Arrangements, then Wreaths & Garlands, each with a short italic line of copy. The photography is strong and seasonal. The weakness is that nothing creates a reason to act today. The only top-line message is "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks", which is unrelated to botanicals. The email then runs long, with a "Recommended Just For You" grid, a Christmas Shop banner, Free Design Services, the app, rewards and Affirm. That dilutes the botanical story.
- Verdict: pretty, on-theme and easy to browse, but it is a catalog with no offer or deadline. It is also the third near-identical editorial send in two days, so it reads as brand content more than a prompt to buy.

## What's working

- The "Holiday Botanicals" hero is seasonal and has a visible "SHOP NOW" button.
- The three-part structure (stems, arrangements, wreaths) is easy to scan.
- The italic line under each heading ("mix and match or display a single bough") sets the tone.
- The photography is consistent and well styled, and the red and green palette suits the season.
- The "More Festive Touches" tiles (Vases, Pillows & Throws, Frames, Decorative Objects) give secondary paths without clutter.

## What's weak

- There is no offer. No % off, no free shipping, and no deadline for holiday buyers who are comparison shopping.
- The top banner, "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks", has nothing to do with the botanical theme and competes with the hero.
- The email is very long. The "Recommended Just For You" grid (stockings, curtain rod, dining table, bed, quilt) pulls attention away from botanicals.
- Product labels are tiny, and no prices or reviews are visible. Several product captions look small and hard to read.
- Several CTAs compete with each other: Shop Now, Shop All Decor, Christmas Shop, Book a Free Appointment, Download Now, Learn More.

- 1. Put one concrete reason to buy now in the hero, such as free shipping on botanicals or a dated deadline for holiday delivery. The email currently has neither.
- 2. Replace the furniture-delivery banner with a botanical-relevant one, for example "Order by Dec 10 for holiday delivery".
- 3. Cut the "Recommended Just For You" block and the Free Design Services block, or move them below a single secondary CTA, so the botanical story stays the focus.
- 4. Add prices ("from $XX") and star ratings under the hero products.
- 5. Use specific CTA copy such as "Shop Wreaths & Garlands" instead of the generic "Shop All Decor".

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional with MSO conditionals and resolved personalization. QA flagged about 60 images with no alt text, 4 images over plain `http://` (one of them a tracking pixel that puts the recipient's address in the URL), and no captured compliance or authentication headers. The HTML source is truncated, so the footer unsubscribe link and the UTM parameters could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracking links:** 63 links on `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` redirect domains were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destinations and redirect health are unverified.
- **Insecure pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is an open-tracking pixel served over HTTP. It may be blocked by clients, and it sends the recipient address unencrypted.
- **Other pixels:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/42ba413e-...?mi_cid=...` are third-party beacons with no alt text. The `d.turn.com` one is an ad-network pixel.
- **Query-string email:** `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is loaded as an image and also exposes the recipient address.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on a West Elm domain. It is low risk, but it adds a dependency on another brand's CDN.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** QA reports about 60 images, for example:
  - `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2026/US/H26_Seasonal_B6_HolidayBotanicalShop/slices_01.jpg` through `slices_21.jpg`
  - `Email-Footer/slices_01.jpg` through `slices_18.jpg`
  - the product images (`classic-velvet-stockings-l.jpg`, `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`, and others)
  - `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg` and `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`

  Only the logo has alt text (`alt="POTTERY BARN"`). With images blocked, the sliced content is unreadable and screen readers get nothing for these images.
- **HTTP images:** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` may be blocked as mixed content.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` has no content.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom.
- **PHP in CSS:** the second `<style>` block ends with stray PHP, `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store..."); ...`. Parsers will ignore or choke on it, but it shows a templating leak.
- **Stale assets:** `H25_TOP_InStockFurniture.jpg` (H25 path) and the 2022 `0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner` slice are old assets in an H26 send.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved tokens** appear in the visible source. The recipient address is substituted in the `coreemail.html?email=` and `sv_sveme=` parameters.
- **PII exposure:** the substituted address is placed in plaintext URL parameters, and one of them is over HTTP (see section 2).
- **Empty `<title>`:** it carries no personalization or subject.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe`:** not found. QA notes this may be an AgentMail relay artifact, so it is unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** not found, with the same caveat.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. The From domain is `e.potterybarn.com`.
- **Body unsubscribe link and postal address:** both are likely in the truncated footer, so they could not be checked.
- **Plain text part:** 12,132 of 16,412 characters (about 74%) are URLs. That is above the 70% threshold and is a minor spam-filter risk.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters:** none are visible in the source I received. All tracked links go through the `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=` redirect, so UTMs may be added server-side after the redirect. That could not be verified because the probe skipped them.
- **Landing pages:** the product images point to `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`, but the click destinations were not resolved. Alignment with the "Holiday Botanical Shop" landing page is unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to the content images. Use `alt=""` for spacers and pixels.
2. Switch the `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, 2022 `slices_02.jpg` and `svopn` pixel URLs to HTTPS. Stop passing the plaintext email address in image and pixel query strings.
3. Fill in `<title>` and remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Remove the stray PHP `header()` lines from the `<style>` block.
5. Replace stale assets (the H25 and 2022 images and the `edm.westelm.com` spacer).
6. Re-run the audit against the raw `.eml` to check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Also capture the full HTML to check the footer unsubscribe link and address.
7. Follow a sample of the redirect links to confirm the final URLs and UTM parameters.
