# Homepage snapshot · www.potterybarn.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- I landed on Pottery Barn's homepage and immediately got hit with a full-screen popup asking for my email and phone number before I could see a single product. The modal has a beautiful white sofa image and the Pottery Barn wordmark, which tracks with the brand I know — clean, aspirational, livable luxury. But right now it's pushing lead capture first and shopping second, which is exactly backwards for someone who came here to browse new arrivals for the lake house.

## What's working

- **Sofa lifestyle image** — that cream upholstered sofa with the floral and striped throw pillows is exactly the kind of styling inspo I pin. It makes me think Pottery Barn still gets my aesthetic.
- **Clean modal design** — the Pottery Barn logo, the serif headline, the neutral palette all feel on-brand. Nothing looks cheap or off.
- **Williams-Sonoma brand acknowledgment** — the fine print confirming this is a Williams-Sonoma brand actually builds trust for me as a WS rewards member. I appreciate knowing these are connected.
- **CHAT button visible** — I actually use live chat when I'm comparing large furniture pieces, so seeing it immediately is reassuring.

## What's weak

- **Full-screen modal as first impression** — I haven't seen one product, one price, or one category before being asked for my personal info. This is the homepage equivalent of a hostess asking for your email before she seats you.
- **"Exclusive savings & more" is nothing** — that copy could be from any retailer in 2014. No number, no threshold, no category, no season. I need a reason to hand over my phone number.
- **Navigation is completely buried** — I can barely see "Baby, Kids, Teen, Dorm, Business to Business" behind the modal. The Furniture / Outdoor / Bedding categories I came here for? Invisible.
- **Dense legal fine print** — two full paragraphs of TCPA compliance language before the form fields. I understand why it's there, but it visually screams "we are going to text you forever" more than "here's a great deal."
- **No returning-customer recognition** — if I'm already a WS rewards member, there's zero acknowledgment of that relationship here. No "Member? Sign in to see your savings."

## Recommendations

- **Swap the gated modal for an anchored bottom banner** — let me see the homepage first, then invite me to sign up. A sticky "Get 15% off your first order — Enter email" bar converts better than a roadblock.
- **Give the offer a number** — "15% off your first order" or "Free shipping on orders over $99 when you join" is a reason to subscribe. "Exclusive savings & more" is not.
- **Add a loyalty/rewards hook to the modal** — a single line like "Already a Pottery Barn Key member? Sign in to unlock your rewards" would make me feel seen and might actually get me to log in instead of closing.
- **Surface one category shortcut inside the modal** — a small "Shop New Arrivals" or "See What's New in Outdoor" link below the form gives me an exit ramp if I don't want to sign up, keeping me on-site rather than sending me to a competitor.
