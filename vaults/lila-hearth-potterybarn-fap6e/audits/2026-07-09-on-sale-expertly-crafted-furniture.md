# ON SALE: Expertly crafted furniture
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-09**
## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn is running a second straight furniture sale email — yesterday's "Online exclusive: Furniture ON SALE" (9/10) was a tight, well-positioned send; today's "ON SALE: Expertly crafted furniture" is the same premise without a new hook. The hero "Summer Sale: Up to 70% off" sounds punchy until the sub-copy beneath it reveals the context is clearance and floor models at local stores — a qualifier that deflates online urgency and edges into bait-and-switch territory. The category grid is thorough, the Rewards section is present, and the render is clean, but both feel like infrastructure copy-pasted from every Pottery Barn send rather than anything specific to today's email.
- The bigger issue is cadence. This is also the second send of July 9th — the "New designs in shades of indigo" editorial dropped earlier today. That makes three Pottery Barn emails in roughly 24 hours. The most engaged subscribers will start tuning out or unsubscribing; the sale audience they're trying to reach was already hit yesterday.

## What's working

- **"Up to 70% off" is the biggest discount number in the current July cycle** — it dominates the hero and earns a glance even from fatigued subscribers.
- **Category grid covers the full sale breadth** — Outdoor, Sofas, Bedding, Rugs, Lighting, Clearance in one tight module reduces navigation friction.
- **"Recommended Just For You" product rail** — personalization gives a browse reason to scroll beyond the hero.
- **Rewards overlay at the bottom** — "Earn 10% Back in Rewards" gives card members an extra conversion signal.
- **Render is clean** — no broken images, overlapping text, or layout bugs.

## What's weak

- **Clearance/floor-model sub-copy undercuts the 70% hero** — leading with the biggest discount and burying the caveat in the next line reads as bait-and-switch; it chips away at trust right in the first fold.
- **Subject line wastes the 70% figure** — "Expertly crafted" is evergreen brand filler; the most compelling number in the email doesn't appear in the subject at all.
- **No urgency deadline anywhere** — "Summer Sale" has no end date; without one, there's zero reason to act today over next week.
- **All CTAs read "SHOP NOW"** — every module uses identical copy; nothing is category-specific or action-differentiated.
- **Cadence problem** — second furniture sale email in 24 hours; open rates for today's send will be hit by yesterday's nearly identical message.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put 70% in the subject** — this is the single highest-ROI change. "Summer Sale: Up to 70% off furniture" wins on every inbox signal vs. "Expertly crafted."
- 2. **Add a credible deadline** — "Ends Sunday" or "This week only" converts browsers into buyers; without it, urgency is theoretical.
- 3. **Differentiate from yesterday or skip the send** — if this must go out, reframe it around a specific category (clearance-only, outdoor closeout) so it doesn't read as a copy-paste of yesterday's email.
- 4. **Rewrite the hero sub-copy** — instead of revealing the clearance qualifier upfront, reframe it as urgency: "While supplies last — biggest discounts of the season."
- 5. **Give category CTAs specific labels** — "Shop Outdoor," "Shop Sofas," "Shop Rugs" instead of six identical SHOP NOW buttons.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Summer Sale: Up to 70% off furniture`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 70% off — our biggest furniture sale yet`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sofas, outdoor, bedding and more — while supplies last`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Clearance prices + earn 10% back with Rewards`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Pottery Barn "ON SALE: Expertly crafted furniture"

**1. Technical Summary**

Two hard-broken links (403) will cause dead clicks in the credit card banner and a cross-brand spacer request. The email also carries PHP header directives injected into a `<style>` block — invalid CSS that will be ignored by clients but signals a template authoring defect. Accessibility coverage is severely deficient, with 40+ images lacking `alt` text.

| Severity | Evidence |
|---|---|
| FAIL | `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/c/da01b407-b21c-4618…` returns **403** — the Key Rewards / credit card CTA is dead for all recipients |
| FAIL | `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` returns **403** — cross-brand West Elm asset pulled into a PB send; likely a copy-paste template error |
| WARN | 23 click-redirect links on `click.e.potterybarn.com` were not probed — if the ESP click-tracking domain has any misconfiguration, failures would be invisible to this audit |

---

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**

**Injected PHP headers in `<style>` block (template defect):**
```css
header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
header("Cache-Control: post-check=0, pre-check=0", false);
header("Pragma: no-cache");
```
These are PHP `header()` calls sitting inside a `<style>` tag. They produce no visible output in email clients (silently ignored), but indicate the template was assembled by string-concatenating a PHP image-serving script with the HTML shell — a fragile build pipeline that could break if the PHP block expands.

**Missing `alt` text — 40 images** across banner, product grid, and footer slices (full list in QA findings). Worst offenders by user impact:
- `F26_TOP_SummerSalePartC_NoUrgency.jpg` — hero banner; images-off subscribers see a blank above the fold
- All 14 `S25_Sale_B12_JuneExtraOffClearanceFurniture/slices_*.jpg` — product grid completely unreadable without images
- All 18 `Email-Footer/slices_*.jpg` — footer nav inaccessible

**HTTP (non-HTTPS) image sources — 4 confirmed:**
- `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
- `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true…` (open-tracking pixel)

All four will be blocked in Outlook, Apple Mail (Link Tracking Protection), and any client enforcing mixed-content rules.

**Open-tracking pixel is HTTP:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/` — open-rate data will be under-counted in any HTTPS-enforcing client.

**`<title>` element is empty** — affects tab/window labeling and some screen reader announce flows when the email is opened in a browser.

**`maximum-scale=1` on viewport meta** — suppresses user zoom on mobile; considered an accessibility violation under WCAG 1.4.4.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity**

The plain-text body is **70.7% URLs** (10,037 of 14,198 chars) — nearly all are raw click-redirect URLs with no human-readable anchor text. This degrades plain-text rendering to near-unusable and triggers spam heuristics on some filters.

UTM parameter coverage could not be confirmed — all 23 content links route through `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirects, which were not probed. If UTMs are appended post-redirect at the ESP layer, they should be verified against at least one resolved destination URL.

The hero banner filename `F26_TOP_SummerSalePartC_NoUrgency.jpg` (Fall 2026 naming convention) is inconsistent with the product grid path `S25_Sale_B12_JuneExtraOffClearanceFurniture/` (Spring/Summer 2025). This suggests the email assembles assets from at least two different campaign cycles, which may produce landing page mismatches if sale prices differ between the hero campaign and linked PDPs.
