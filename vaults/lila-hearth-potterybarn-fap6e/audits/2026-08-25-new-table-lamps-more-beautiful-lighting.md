# New! Table lamps & more beautiful lighting
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-25**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuine category launch, not a rehash — Pottery Barn's shift from bedding to lighting is a real reset, and "Illuminate Every Corner" with a clean shelf-of-lamps hero photo actually reads as a distinct new-arrivals moment rather than another color-sort exercise. The "trendspotting" petite-lamps callout right below the hero is a nice touch — it signals editorial intent, not just a product dump.
- But the email quickly loses focus. After the hero, it sprawls into a seven-category lighting grid (chandeliers, floor lamps, pendants, sconces, picture lights, outdoor), then a "more details to inspire" rug/decor/furniture/bedding nav, then a "Recommended Just For You" bedroom furniture block that has nothing to do with lighting, then a sweepstakes CTA, then a full Halloween Shop teaser. By the time you hit the Halloween section you've forgotten the subject line was about lamps. It's Pottery Barn's default catalog-scroll format applied to a category that could have sustained a tighter, punchier send.
- Net: strong hero, weak follow-through. Worth opening for the lamp photography, unlikely to hold attention past the first two screens.

## What's working

- The hero image is well-composed — a styled shelf vignette with multiple lamp styles, warm consistent lighting, clear "SHOP TABLE LAMPS" CTA button.
- "Illuminate Every Corner" is a genuinely on-topic, benefit-oriented headline tied to the subject line.
- The "trendspotting" petite-lamps insert adds a point of view instead of just more product tiles.
- The lighting-by-type grid (chandeliers/floor lamps/pendants/sconces/picture lights/outdoor) is a legitimately useful category map for someone actually shopping for lighting.
- No visible render bugs — images load cleanly, text is legible, buttons are intact.

## What's weak

- The email dilutes its own premise: "Recommended Just For You" pivots hard into bedroom furniture (platform beds, duvet covers, curtain rods) with zero lighting tie-in.
- The sweepstakes ("Design Crew Sweeps") and Halloween Shop sections are unrelated bolt-ons that push the lighting content further from the top of mind by the time you scroll past them.
- No actual offer or price call-out anywhere — this is pure browse/discovery content, which is fine for a launch email but scores low on urgency/conversion pull.
- Seven sub-category tiles plus a "more details to inspire" nav plus a recs block is a lot of scrolling before any concrete product or price appears.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the lighting-by-type grid and "SHOP ALL LIGHTING" — drop the bedroom furniture recs, sweepstakes, and Halloween teaser entirely, or move them to a separate send. A focused lighting email converts better than a scroll-everything catalog.
- 2. Add one concrete price or promo signal (e.g., "Lamps from $89" or "Save 20% on lighting") near the hero — right now there's no reason to act now versus later.
- 3. If cross-sell is required, make it lighting-adjacent (nightstands, console tables to pair with the lamps) rather than beds/curtains, which reads as a non-sequitur.
- 4. Move "trendspotting" (petite lamps) higher or make it the hero — it's the most distinctive, POV-driven content in the email and it's currently buried as a secondary block.

## Technical Audit — Pottery Barn "New! Table lamps & more beautiful lighting"

**1. Technical Summary**
Standard Pottery Barn/Williams-Sonoma EDM template with functional tracking and unsubscribe infrastructure, but broad accessibility gaps (missing alt text on nearly every image) and several unencrypted legacy asset/pixel URLs.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- Primary nav link routes through `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirect wrapper (`https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABB7...`) — standard ESP click-tracking, not an issue on its own.
- 61 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the automated HTTP prober (redirect domains excluded by design) — link destinations were not validated for HTTP status; manual spot-check recommended before send if not already QA'd upstream.
- Multiple concurrent open-tracking mechanisms present: `svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true` pixel, `d.turn.com/r/dd/...` beacon, and `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/e7a2f028-...` — this is redundant but not broken; flagging only because triple-pixel tracking increases payload/privacy surface.
- Recipient email address is exposed in plaintext in multiple URLs (e.g., `sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`, `coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`) — standard for personalization/open pixels but worth noting as PII-in-URL.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- `alt` attribute missing on effectively every `<img>` tag in the QA scan (40+ instances), including hero banners, product slices, and even 1x1 tracking pixels. Screen readers and blocked-image fallback states will render no descriptive text — significant accessibility gap for a purely image-based layout (no visible fallback text found in the sampled source).
- Layout is table-based with MSO conditional comments (`<!--[if mso 16]>`, `.mso-font-fix-*`) — correct legacy pattern for Outlook rendering.
- `<style>` block contains stray PHP-style `header()` calls (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ...")`) embedded as literal text inside a CSS `<style>` tag — these are inert (not executed, just dead text sitting in CSS) but indicate a code artifact/copy-paste error from the template source that should be cleaned up.
- Preheader text uses zero-width joiner padding (`͏` sequences) — correct technique to control preview-text length across clients.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- No unresolved merge tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) found in the truncated source — personalization appears limited to backend-injected tracking IDs (`mi_mid=`, `sv_cid=`) rather than visible on-page merge fields.
- Recipient identifier consistently populated (`lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`) across pixel/link parameters — no broken/empty token values observed.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not found in the captured headers — may be a relay capture limitation (per QA note) rather than absence in the original send; cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe compliance from this data alone.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) also not found, consistent with the above.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not present in the captured data — authentication status is unknown, not necessarily failing; flagged as a QA/visibility gap via the AgentMail relay rather than a confirmed compliance failure.
- Footer/unsubscribe imagery (`Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`) and customer-service link (`coreemail.html`) are present in-body, consistent with CAN-SPAM's visible-unsubscribe-mechanism requirement, though the actual unsubscribe link target wasn't visible in the truncated source provided.
- **Recommendation:** re-verify header capture with a raw MIME source (not the relay-processed version) to confirm List-Unsubscribe/Authentication-Results are genuinely absent vs. just not passed through.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Outbound links use ESP redirect wrappers (`click.e.potterybarn.com`, `clickcp.potterybarn.com`) rather than direct UTM-tagged URLs to potterybarn.com — final landing-page UTM parameters can't be confirmed from the wrapped/tracking URLs alone.
- Product images link to `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...` CDN paths directly (not tracking-wrapped) for several PDP thumbnails (e.g., `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`, `farmhouse-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`) — these are image assets, not click targets, so no continuity concern.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images (hero banners, product slices) at minimum; decorative/tracking pixels can use `alt=""` to satisfy accessibility without adding noise.
2. Replace the four `http://` asset/pixel sources (`edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`, `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/.../Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/.../slices_02.jpg`, `svopn.potterybarn.com` open pixel) with HTTPS equivalents — non-HTTPS assets risk being blocked by mail clients enforcing mixed-content policies.
3. Remove the dead PHP `header(...)` lines sitting inside the `<style>` block — harmless today but a sign of template contamination worth cleaning at the source.
4. Confirm List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post / Authentication-Results headers against a raw (pre-relay) copy of the message before treating their absence as a compliance issue.
5. Spot-check a sample of the 61 skipped tracking links manually, since the automated prober intentionally does not validate redirect-wrapped URLs.
