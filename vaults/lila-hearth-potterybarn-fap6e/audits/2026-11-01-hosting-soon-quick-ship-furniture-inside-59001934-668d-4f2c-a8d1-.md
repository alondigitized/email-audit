# Hosting soon? Quick-ship furniture inside
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- This is the best of Pottery Barn's recent sends because it's about one idea: hosting season and quick-ship furniture. The "Hosting soon?" subject matches the "get guest-ready / Quick-Ship Furniture, delivered in 1-4 weeks" hero. The 1-4 week delivery window is a concrete reason to act now, and the room-by-room tiles (dining, tabletop, bedroom, lighting, bedding, living room, seating) are easy to scan.
- It still opens with the same oxblood "Extra 20% off Select Styles / Plus savings up to 70% off" banner that has led the brand's sends since Oct 26. That makes it the fourth or fifth near-identical promo in a week. The banner competes with the quick-ship hero, and the offer is hedged with "Exclusions apply. Prices as marked." The long tail of recommended products, the Christmas Shop, design services, app, rewards and Affirm blocks dilutes the focused message. It is a solid send that spends its good idea on the same tired banner.

## What's working

- The subject and hero are tightly aligned: "Hosting soon?" leads to "get guest-ready Quick-Ship Furniture."
- "Delivered in 1-4 weeks" is a specific, credible value that answers the real worry about holiday delivery.
- The tiles are clearly labeled by room and category with strong lifestyle photography, so the eye moves down the email easily.
- "Ships Free" badges on tabletop and bedding add a second concrete incentive.
- "Shop All In-Stock" is a clear category-level CTA, and the seasonal Christmas Shop block fits the date.

## What's weak

- The oxblood "Extra 20% off" banner repeats the same top block as the Oct 26-Nov 1 sends and overshadows the quick-ship idea.
- "Exclusions apply. Prices as marked." and the dense fine print undercut how honest the offer feels.
- The email is very long. Recommended Just For You, Christmas Shop, Design Crew, app, rewards and Affirm all compete after the hero message is delivered.
- The tiles are labels without prices, so nothing shows what a quick-ship item costs or which ones qualify for the discount.
- The hero says "Quick-Ship" but doesn't show a specific date or a "order by" cutoff, so the urgency is soft.

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop or shrink the generic 20% banner. Lead with the quick-ship hero and put one line below it: "In stock, ships in 1-4 weeks. Extra 20% off select styles."
- 2. Add a real deadline, such as "Order by Nov 12 for pre-Thanksgiving delivery," so the urgency is credible.
- 3. Show starting prices or a hero item (for example, a sofa or dining table) in the tiles, so the click has a product behind it.
- 4. Cut the tail. Keep one block, either Recommended or Christmas Shop, and move rewards, Affirm and app promotion to a slim footer strip.
- 5. Add a rating or review count to the featured pieces.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Guest-ready furniture, delivered in 1-4 weeks`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Hosting this season? In-stock pieces ship fast`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Dining, bedroom and seating that arrive before the holidays. Extra 20% off select styles.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Order by Nov 12 for guest-ready delivery. Free shipping on select tabletop and bedding.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The Pottery Barn email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 layout. Its main problems are about 45 `<img>` elements with no `alt` attribute, the recipient's email address sent in plain text over `http://`, and three non-HTTPS image or pixel sources. The HTML was truncated and the relay stripped headers, so unsubscribe and authentication status can't be confirmed from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque tracking links:** 51 links go through `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…`. The HTTP probe skipped them, so destinations are unverified. The `click.e` host is a subdomain of the From domain `e.potterybarn.com`, which is good for alignment.
- **Mixed content:** three sources use `http://`, and the QA check says they may be blocked:
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=…`
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/…/cat/1` is an ad-network pixel, a separate tracking domain.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` ties this send to another brand's CDN. A failure there would leave a layout gap, though it's low risk.
- **Open pixels:** `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/0b1af8b4-…` and `svopn.potterybarn.com` are open pixels. The 1px tracking images need `width`/`height` and `alt=""`; the QA scan found neither.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, about 45 images.** The QA check flagged all of these:
  - Hero slices `H26_InStock_B4_InStockDiningHero/slices_01–11.jpg`.
  - Product images: `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`, `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`, `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg`, and three others.
  - Banners: `H26_TOP_Extra20CLR.jpg`, `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg`, `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`.
  - Footer `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`.

  Because the content is sliced images, the email is largely unreadable with images blocked or in a screen reader. The pixels and spacer should use `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Stray PHP in CSS:** the last `<style>` block contains `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: …")`. These are server-side calls leaked into the CSS. They're invalid, likely harmless, but they signal a template defect.
- **Web font:** the Google Fonts `<link>` for Montserrat is stripped by Gmail and Outlook. Check that a fallback font stack is set in the inline styles.
- **Selector support:** `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` attribute selectors are supported inconsistently.
- **Slice and spacer layout:** the many slice images and the `<td height="20">&nbsp;</td>` spacer depend on images loading to preserve the layout.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved tokens** were visible in the truncated source.
- **PII in URLs:** the recipient's address is in query strings.
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_…&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
- The `svopn` request is plain `http://`, so the address is exposed in transit. It also appears in server logs and Referer-style paths, although `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` limits that last one.
- **Marker image:** the `coreemail.html?email=…` item is a tracking or marker image with no `alt`. Its `.html` extension is a mismatch worth checking.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** both are missing. The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay stripping headers, so I can't confirm a violation. Verify against the raw original message.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not present, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. The From domain `e.potterybarn.com` aligns with the click domain.
- **Footer content:** the HTML is truncated, and the footer is made of image slices (`Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`) with no `alt`. If the unsubscribe link, physical address, or legal text exists only as image text, that is a CAN-SPAM accessibility risk. Confirm there's a text unsubscribe link in the full source.
- **Plain text:** 9,747 of 13,920 characters (70%) are URLs, which is a deliverability signal. It comes from the tracking-wrapped links.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters:** no visible `utm_*` parameters. All CTAs are wrapped in opaque `qs=` redirects, so campaign parameters and landing pages can't be verified from the source.
- **Product images:** these point directly at `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/…`, which are image assets. The click targets are unknown.
- **Links to check:** the "Hosting soon? Quick-ship furniture" subject lines up with the `InStockDiningHero` asset path. Click each tracked link and confirm it lands on an in-stock, quick-ship page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to hero, product and footer images. Add `alt=""` and explicit dimensions to the tracking pixels and spacers.
2. Switch the three `http://` assets, including the `svopn` pixel, to HTTPS.
3. Stop putting the raw email address in `coreemail.html` and `sv_sveme`. Use an opaque token instead.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag and populate `<title>`.
5. Delete the stray PHP `header()` lines from the `<style>` block.
6. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF, DKIM and DMARC results from the raw message. Confirm a text-based unsubscribe link and postal address are in the footer.
7. Resolve a sample of the `click.e.potterybarn.com` links to check destinations and UTM parameters.
8. Host the `spacer_0.gif` on the sender's own CDN instead of `edm.westelm.com`.
