# Shipping free! NEW throw blankets
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a "free shipping" send dressed as a throws launch — "Cozy Throws Ship Free" hero, then a category grid ("More to love, with shipping on us.") spanning pillows, table linens, sheets, duvets, quilts, towels, flatware, curtains. It's honest and clean: warm, styled hero shot, a clear mechanic (free shipping across home categories, not just throws), and a tidy 8-tile grid that's easy to scan. No render bugs, no lorem ipsum, brand voice consistent with prior Pottery Barn sends I've seen this week.
- The problem is this is the sixth Pottery Barn email in three days and the fatigue that started with the color-sort bedding trilogy (2026-08-24, 2026-08-25) is compounding. "Free shipping sitewide, framed around throws" is a real, distinct mechanic vs. the bedding-color and in-stock-shipping sends — but the subject undersells it ("Shipping free! NEW throw blankets" reads like two half-thoughts stapled together), and the actual content is 80% generic category grid, not throws. This is a decent execution of a mediocre idea — solid 5/10 territory, consistent with the run of sends this week.

## What's working

- Hero image is well-styled — stacked throws on a wood shelf with warm lighting, reads as a real seasonal lifestyle shot, not stock.
- Free-shipping offer is unambiguous and appears twice (hero CTA "Shop Now" and "Shop All Free Ship" button below the grid).
- Category grid is well-organized with consistent tile sizing and labels (Pillows, Table Linens, Sheets, Duvets, Quilts, Towels, Flatware, Curtains) — easy to scan on mobile.
- "Recommended Just For You" section adds personalization signal with named, priced-ish products (Remmy Swivel Chair, Toscana Extendable Dining Table).
- No visual bugs — clean render top to bottom.

## What's weak

- Subject promises "NEW throw blankets" but the email is a sitewide free-shipping promo — throws only get the hero, then the email pivots entirely to unrelated categories (dining tables, rugs, curtain rods). Bait-and-switch feel.
- No price or percentage in the offer — "free shipping" alone is a weak, low-urgency incentive compared to the sweepstakes and color-sort sends earlier this week.
- Sixth email in three days from the same brand with overlapping content (bedding/linens categories repeat almost every send this week) — cadence fatigue is real.
- "Recommended Just For You" products (dining tables, curtain rods, an accent chair) have nothing to do with throws or the free-shipping framing — feels bolted-on rather than curated.
- Subject line is grammatically awkward ("Shipping free! NEW throw blankets") — reads like two subject lines merged, not a deliberate phrase.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rewrite the subject to lead with the actual mechanic and match the hero: something like "Free shipping on cozy throws (and everything else)" — don't force "NEW" onto a shipping promo.
- 2. Cut or clearly separate the "Recommended Just For You" block — right now it dilutes the throws/shipping message with unrelated dining and lighting products.
- 3. Add a threshold or deadline to the free-shipping offer (e.g., "free shipping on $99+" or "ends Sunday") — flat, unconditional-sounding free shipping with no date reads low-urgency and slightly suspicious.
- 4. Space this out — given five near-identical bedding/linens sends since 2026-08-24, this should have been folded into one of those rather than issued as a standalone.
- 5. If throws are the real hero, show throws styled in 2-3 more contexts (bed, sofa, chair) instead of pivoting straight to an 8-category catch-all grid.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free shipping starts now — shop cozy throws`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your throws (and everything else) ship free`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New fall throws, plus free shipping across the home shop.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Layer up for less — free shipping, no minimum, this week only.`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard MSO/webmail-compatible HTML template using ESP redirect links, an open-tracking pixel, and Turn.com third-party tracking; QA found no broken links but flagged accessibility, compliance-header, and mixed-content issues.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Primary CTA and nav links route through `click.e.potterybarn.com` (e.g., the header logo link `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABB7InYiOjEs...`), a standard ESP click-tracking redirect. 57 such tracking/redirect links were skipped by the automated HTTP probe (expected behavior for redirect domains, not itself a defect).
- Open-tracking pixel present: `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` — served over **http://**, not https. Mixed-content/non-TLS tracking pixels can be blocked by mail clients enforcing HTTPS-only image loading.
- A second open pixel is loaded from a third-party ad-tracking domain: `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` (Turn.com/Amobee) — cross-domain conversion/retargeting tracking embedded in the email body.
- Recipient PII (raw email address `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`) is passed in cleartext as a query parameter in both the open pixel and the `coreemail.html` customer-service link. Since the open-tracking pixel is unencrypted (http), this exposes the recipient's email address to anyone able to observe the network request.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- All 40+ content images lack `alt` attributes (banners, product shots, slice/spacer images, footer). This fails WCAG 1.1.1 and means the email is unreadable to screen-reader users or when images are blocked (the common default in most mail clients), leaving only the pixel-string filenames as fallback (e.g., `slices_01.jpg` through `slices_18.jpg`).
- Two images load over plain **http://**, which will be blocked or flagged by TLS-enforcing clients:
  - `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
- Layout relies on nested tables with nonstandard MSO/VML conditional comments and nonstandard `header()` PHP-style calls embedded inside a `<style>` block (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); ...`) — this is invalid CSS/dead code left in the template; it has no effect in-client but indicates unclean template hygiene (worth flagging even though it's inert, since it could be a leftover server-side snippet accidentally pasted into the email source).
- Spacer GIFs and 1px-style tracking images (`spacer_0.gif`, pixel images) are used for layout/tracking, both standard for this template era.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No visible unresolved merge tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) in the truncated source.
- Personalization is limited to the recipient email being appended as a literal string to tracking/service URLs (see Link & Tracking Issues) rather than a merge-tag — functions correctly but is a privacy consideration, not a rendering defect.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected by QA — either absent or not surfaced through the AgentMail relay. CAN-SPAM does not strictly require this header if a working unsubscribe link/mechanism exists in the body, but its absence removes one-click unsubscribe support in clients that rely on it (Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements as of Feb 2024 require List-Unsubscribe + List-Unsubscribe-Post for bulk senders).
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header also not found — combined with the above, this email does not appear to meet Gmail/Yahoo one-click unsubscribe requirements for bulk senders if these headers are genuinely absent at the source (vs. stripped by the relay).
- `Authentication-Results` header not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be confirmed from this data — flagged as unknown, not a failure.
- Truncated source does not include the footer/unsubscribe body section, so physical address and unsubscribe link presence in-body cannot be confirmed from available data.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Sampled destination link uses ESP-native redirect tracking (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` opaque token) rather than visible UTM parameters — standard for this ESP (Salesforce Marketing Cloud/Bronto-style pattern based on `qs=` token format), but means UTM/campaign tagging cannot be verified from the link itself since the destination is opaque prior to redirect resolution.
- Customer-service link resolves to `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` — direct site domain (not tracked redirect), consistent domain alignment with the potterybarn.com brand.
- No broken cross-domain references observed between edm.potterybarn.com (asset host), click.e.potterybarn.com (ESP), and potterybarn.com (site) — domain structure is internally consistent.

7. Recommendations
- Add descriptive `alt` text to all content-bearing images (banners, product shots); decorative/spacer images should use `alt=""` explicitly rather than omitting the attribute.
- Migrate the three `http://` image/pixel sources to `https://` to prevent blocking under TLS-enforcing mail clients and to stop transmitting the recipient's email address in cleartext via the open-tracking pixel.
- Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent at the source (not just dropped by the AgentMail relay) — required for Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender compliance.
- Remove the inert `header('Content-Type: ...')` PHP-style lines from the `<style>` block; confirm this wasn't accidentally pasted from a server script during template assembly.
- Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment independently (outside this relay) since `Authentication-Results` could not be verified here.
