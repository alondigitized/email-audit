# New arrivals—classic, comfortable, collected
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-21**
## Executive summary

- After a week-plus of hammer-blow summer sale emails, Pottery Barn pivots here to an editorial launch: new arrivals anchored by a dark-indigo, warm-wood heritage aesthetic. The hero image earns its real estate — moody, aspirational, well-composed — and the alliterative subject ("classic, comfortable, collected") lands the creative tone it's reaching for. That's the upside.
- The downside is a structural contradiction that runs top to bottom. The top banner screams "Summer Sale: Up to 70% off" while the entire body is about new arrivals. New arrivals don't go 70% off — subscribers know this — so the banner reads as an ambient leftover rather than a real offer, and it undercuts both messages. Yesterday's lifestyle email ("An effortlessly styled space starts here," scored 8/10 here) made the same bet on soft-sell, but without the sale-banner contradiction muddying the read.
- The "Recommended Just for You" block is the email's hidden strength — it adds a personalization signal that the editorial section completely lacks. The overall execution is competent but the dual-message architecture holds it back from the top tier.

## What's working

- Hero image is genuinely evocative — deep indigo, warm wood, layered textiles — the visual matches the "classic, comfortable, collected" subject line perfectly.
- "SHOP ALL NEW ARRIVALS" CTA is specific and appropriately prominent below the editorial copy.
- "Recommended Just for You" personalization block adds a relevant pull that pure editorial emails miss.
- "Earn 10% Back in Rewards" loyalty callout and Affirm payment option are both visible — reduces friction on a higher-consideration purchase.
- No render bugs; clean layout with strong typographic hierarchy in the editorial section.

## What's weak

- The "Summer Sale: Up to 70% off" banner contradicts the new arrivals body — new arrivals aren't 70% off, so the banner creates false expectations and dilutes both messages.
- Editorial copy ("turn a house into a heritage home") is pretty but abstract — no specific product, no collection name, no price anchor.
- Hero links to new arrivals generally, not a single featured piece — the image is strong enough that a specific product link would convert better.
- After multiple sends in 72 hours (July 18–20 included at least five emails), cadence fatigue is real; this softer subject is doing extra lift to earn the open.
- No urgency — no deadline, no "limited quantities," no seasonal endpoint on the new arrivals push.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Separate the sale banner from the new arrivals editorial, or remove it.** If new arrivals are in the sale, say so explicitly ("New arrivals, now in the Summer Sale"). If they're not, kill the banner — it actively misleads.
- 2. **Anchor the editorial copy to one hero product.** Name the collection ("Introducing the Harlow Collection"), show a price, and link the hero image to that specific product page.
- 3. **Add a light deadline or scarcity signal.** "Just landed — limited quantities" would add urgency without resorting to the worn "SUMMER SALE" drum.
- 4. **Move the "Recommended Just for You" block above the fold.** It's doing more personalization work than the editorial; it deserves a higher position.
- 5. **Subject rewrite:** the alliteration is pleasant but zero urgency and zero category specificity.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New arrivals: warm wood, deep indigo, just landed`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Just in: the pieces we've been building toward`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Classic shapes, updated for summer — see what arrived this week`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Heritage style meets new-arrival freshness — shop now`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**PHP `header()` calls inside `<style>` block — template bug**
The second `<style>` block in `<head>` ends with:
```
header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
header("Cache-Control: post-check=0, pre-check=0", false);
header("Pragma: no-cache");
```
These are PHP cache-busting directives that have leaked into the HTML output verbatim. They are invalid CSS and will be silently ignored by email clients, but they indicate template file corruption — a PHP script's `header()` calls were copy-pasted or interpolated into the wrong section. This poses no rendering failure today but is a template integrity defect.

**38 images missing `alt` text**
Virtually every content image — product grid (`slices_02.jpg` through `slices_12.jpg`), hero banner (`F26_TOP_SummerSalePartC_NoUrgency.jpg`), product shots (bed, chair, towel, rug, quilt), footer slices, and tracking pixels — has no `alt` attribute. When images are blocked (default in Outlook, many corporate clients), the email renders as a column of empty boxes with no readable content.

**3 images served over HTTP — will be blocked in secure contexts**
- `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` — footer Key Rewards image
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` — CC banner slice

Gmail, iOS Mail, and most modern clients will block mixed-content images. The Key Rewards footer image will not render for a significant portion of recipients.

**Open tracking pixel over HTTP**
`http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
The open-rate pixel is served over HTTP. It will be blocked by Gmail proxy rewriting and iOS MPP, causing open tracking to undercount or fail entirely.

`<title></title>` — no title set. Minor: some clients display the title in tab/notification UI.

### 5. Compliance

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header absent**
Not captured by the relay. If missing in the actual envelope, major ISPs (Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook) will not render the native unsubscribe button in the UI — increasing spam complaints and reducing deliverability. Required under Yahoo/Google 2024 bulk sender requirements for >5k/day senders.

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) absent**
One-click unsubscribe (the machine-readable POST variant) is absent. This is now enforced by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders. Without it, senders risk deferral.

**[WARN] `Authentication-Results` header not captured**
SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status is unknown through the AgentMail relay. Cannot confirm authentication chain. If DKIM is failing or misaligned, inbox placement at major providers will be affected.

**CAN-SPAM physical address** — not verifiable from truncated HTML, but footer structure is present; assume compliant pending full source review.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

The credit card apply CTA (`cm_ite=PB_header_nonmember`) is fully broken (403), severing the conversion path for the non-member Key Rewards segment.

All product and content links route through `click.e.potterybarn.com` or `clickcp.potterybarn.com` redirectors, which should append UTM parameters at redirect time — cannot confirm parameter presence or correctness since those 24 links were not probed. The `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer" />` tag suppresses referrer headers for any clicks that bypass the click redirector. This will cause direct traffic to appear unattributed in GA4/Adobe Analytics for any edge cases where users navigate without the redirect firing.
