# Early Black Friday deals to make spirits bright 🥂✨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the same burgundy "Early Black Friday, up to 70% off" Pottery Barn template that has run since 11/16, with a new subject line. After the 11/24 "FREE design help in stores" send, which briefly broke the pattern, it is back to the standard sale layout. The hero is clear, the "SHOP NOW" button is visible and the category grid is easy to scan. But a subscriber has now seen this banner roughly a dozen times in nine days. Today's creative adds almost nothing new.
- The body is a long scroll: seven or eight category tiles, a "Recommended just for you" block, an in-store promo, a Design Crew sweepstakes, Affirm, the app and rewards. Each module has its own offer ("up to 50%", "up to 60%", "up to 70%", "up to 30%"). The pitch is strong, but nothing is urgent and no single product is promoted.
- The judgement is that it is competent and on-brand, and it is also stale. The best parts are the clear hero and the "Ships free" tags. The weakest part is a campaign that has stopped giving subscribers a reason to open the next one.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer plainly: "Early Black Friday, up to 70% off", with a visible "Shop Now" button.
- Every category tile carries its own discount, such as "Pillows & Throws up to 70% off" and "Sofas & Chairs up to 50% off". This makes the tiles easy to scan.
- The "Ships free" badges on several tiles add a second reason to buy.
- The "Recommended just for you" block gives a sense of personalization and shows real products with names.
- The photography is warm and seasonal (stockings, robes, a decorated table), so it fits the holiday mood.

## What's weak

- The template is a near-duplicate of the sends since 11/16. It is repetitive and fatigues the reader.
- The ranges ("up to 30% to 70%") are wide and vague. Nothing says what a customer will actually pay.
- The email is very long (about 8,000px). Modules like Design Crew, Affirm and the app promo dilute the main CTA.
- There is no deadline or end date for the sale, so the urgency is generic.
- The in-store, sweepstakes and rewards sections compete with the "Shop All Sale" CTA and give the eye too many places to go.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one hero product at a specific price, for example "Cozy quilt $149, was $249", instead of a blanket "up to 70%". Specific prices convert better.
- 2. Add a real deadline with a countdown, such as "Early access ends tonight at midnight". The sale has run for nine days, so a deadline is the only fresh urgency left.
- 3. Cut the email to the hero, four category tiles, one recommended row and the footer. Move Affirm, the app and the sweepstakes into a separate send or a short strip.
- 4. Reduce cadence or rotate the creative. Alternate between category-led sends (for example "Gifts under $50") and the sitewide sale.
- 5. Use the personalization block earlier, as in the second module.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: 70% off Pottery Barn`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Stockings, robes & throws: up to 70% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping on select styles + price drops on our best-selling quilts.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Last call before Black Friday pricing changes. Shop sofas, decor and gifts.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based layout (650px) behind Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style redirect tracking. QA reports 0 errors and 3 warnings, but the HTML shows widespread missing alt text, three cleartext HTTP image requests that carry the recipient's address, and several markup defects. The HTML was truncated, so unsubscribe, footer and later link checks could not be completed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **58 tracking links were not probed.** They sit on click-redirect domains (e.g. `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`), so destinations and redirect health are unverified.
- **Cleartext tracking pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is HTTP, not HTTPS. Clients may block it, which would undercount opens. It also sends the recipient address unencrypted.
- **Third-party pixels:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` (Turn/Amobee ad-tech) and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/f8e4cb68-...` are open or beacon pixels. Both are flagged as missing alt text.
- **Mixed content:** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` are HTTP. The same host serves HTTPS elsewhere, so these are likely stale 2022-era assets that can be switched to HTTPS.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is loaded from the West Elm domain. It is a minor dependency risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** QA flags about 50 images. This includes the full-width content slices (`.../1125_WED_AM_EBF_Grid2/slices_01–13.jpg`, `.../HolidayBanners/H26_Bottom_*.jpg`, `.../Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`). It also includes product images such as `classic-velvet-stockings-l.jpg` and `faux-fur-ruched-throw-l.jpg`. With images blocked or read by a screen reader, the offer and CTAs are not conveyed. Only the header logo has alt text (`alt="POTTERY BARN"`).
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **No language attribute:** `<html>` has no `lang` attribute. It only declares the `xmlns` namespaces.
- **Code in a style block:** The second `<style>` block contains stray PHP such as `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ...")`. Parsers will ignore it, but it is a template defect. It can also invalidate the CSS rule that follows it in strict parsers.
- **Quirks mode risk:** A `<!-- -->` comment comes before the `<!DOCTYPE>`. This can push older Outlook and IE-based renderers into quirks mode.
- **Layout tables:** Some layout tables have `role="presentation"` but others do not (e.g. the outer wrapper tables and the spacer table).
- **Possibly dead CSS:** The mobile rules `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` match no element in the visible HTML. The source is truncated, so this is unconfirmed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved merge tokens** appear in the visible HTML.
- **Email address exposed in URLs:** `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is a tracking pixel (flagged as an image). The `sv_sveme=` parameter on the HTTP pixel carries the same address. Putting the plaintext address in image URLs leaks it to proxies and logs. The `svopn` request does this over unencrypted HTTP.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)** were not found. QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly dropping them, so the result is inconclusive.
- **SPF, DKIM and DMARC** are unverifiable because `Authentication-Results` was absent.
- **Footer checks not completed:** The HTML is truncated, so the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be confirmed. The footer exists as images (`Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`). Those images lack alt text, so the unsubscribe text would be invisible with images off.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters could not be assessed.** Visible links are opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` redirects, and the final landing URLs and any UTM parameters are unknown.
- **Product images** are hotlinked from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`. One filename is truncated (`open-box-cozy-cloud-handcrafted-qui-white-cozy-cloud-whi`), and its "open-box" naming suggests a clearance item. The product links were not checked for in-stock status.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to every content and footer slice, and `alt=""` to the pixels and spacers. Make sure the offer, CTAs and unsubscribe text are available as live text or alt.
2. Serve all images and pixels over HTTPS, specifically `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, the 2022 `slices_02.jpg`, and `svopn.potterybarn.com`.
3. Stop putting the plaintext email address in pixel URLs (`coreemail.html?email=`, `sv_sveme=`). Use a hashed or opaque ID.
4. Remove the `maximum-scale=1` viewport restriction. Fill in `<title>`, add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and add `role="presentation"` to all layout tables.
5. Delete the stray `header(...)` PHP lines from the `<style>` block, and move the comment after the DOCTYPE.
6. Re-run QA with raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) and the full untruncated HTML. Resolve the redirect links to confirm landing pages and UTM parameters.
