# Homepage snapshot · www.potterybarn.com · 2026-08-08
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-08**
## Executive summary

- This reads like classic, elevated Pottery Barn — the beamed-ceiling living room with the kilim rug and leather chair is exactly the "polished but lived-in" look I'm chasing for the lake house. Problem is, the first thing I actually get to interact with isn't the store — it's an email pop-up parked right in the middle of my screen. Right now the push feels split between "sign up for our list" and "Warehouse Sale, up to 60% off," which isn't the strongest first impression.

## What's working

- The background hero (that vaulted-ceiling living room with the chandelier and the kilim rug under the sectional) is genuinely aspirational and on-brand for what I'm decorating right now.
- The category bar — Furniture, Bedding, Bath, Rugs, Pillows & Decor, Tabletop & Bar — gets me to my usual shopping lanes in one tap, no digging.
- "The Warehouse Sale — Shop New Deals Up to 60% off" is a real, specific offer I'd actually click.
- "Zero interest promo financing on purchases of $750 or more" at the very top is smart — that's realistic language for someone furnishing a second home, not a $40 impulse buy.
- The scroll-past tagline, "Sofas made for sinking into and tables built for gathering together," reads warm and editorial, not salesy.

## What's weak

- The email signup modal ("Find out first: New arrivals, exclusive savings & more") swallows almost the entire hero the second the page loads — I can't even see the actual homepage before I'm asked for my email and phone number.
- No loyalty/rewards presence up top — I'm a Williams-Sonoma rewards person, and there's no member-pricing badge or "sign in for your rewards balance" anywhere visible.
- There's a second overlay in the corner (the QR code "Scan to Download Our App") stacked on top of the email modal — two competing pop-ups is a lot for one screen.
- No people in any of the imagery, so nothing here signals "this is styled by and for someone like me" — it's all still-life rooms.
- No reviews, ratings, or trust badges visible anywhere above the fold.

## Recommendations

- Delay the email pop-up by a scroll or a few seconds so I actually see the hero and nav first — right now it fires before I've seen anything.
- Don't stack the QR-download bubble with the email modal at the same time — pick one ask per visit.
- Surface a Williams-Sonoma Key Rewards strip near the account icons (points balance or "member pricing" badge) since that's a huge loyalty lever for someone like me.
- Add a specific dollar threshold to "Free Shipping" (e.g., "Free Shipping on orders $99+") instead of the vague asterisk — I want to know if I've already cleared it.
