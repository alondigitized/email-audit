# HAPPENING NOW: Extra 20% off + Collectibles for new holiday traditions
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- This is a better-looking email than its recent siblings, but it still opens with the same oxblood "Extra 20% off Select Styles / Plus savings up to 70% off" banner. That makes it at least the ninth "Extra 20%" send in about four days. Right under the banner, the "Nostalgic Collectibles" hero, with vintage Santa and Mrs. Claus figures and a lit tree, finally gives a subscriber a reason to scroll. The creative is cozy and seasonal, and the script captions ("for a season of tradition and delight," "whimsical decor you can't wait to pull out") carry the holiday mood well.
- The email is split between two jobs. The banner pushes a discount and the hero pushes a nostalgic-decor story. Neither is tied to a product, price, or deadline. "Happening now" in the subject promises urgency, but the body never says when the offer ends. The lower half is a generic "Recommended Just For You" furniture grid (dining tables, rod, bed, rug, curtain) that has nothing to do with collectibles.
- Judgment: a nice holiday mood piece sitting on top of a worn-out promo. It will do fine for people already shopping for decor and will blur into the pile for everyone else.

## What's working

- The "Nostalgic Collectibles" hero is a clear, emotional departure from the sale-banner sameness, with a visible SHOP NOW button.
- The imagery is cohesive and seasonal: stockings, a gingerbread village, vintage Santas, and a red-and-gold table setting.
- The "Explore the Christmas Shop" button is a full-width dark bar, so it is easy to spot and tap.
- The "Festive essentials ship free" row (stockings, ornaments, tabletop & bar, bedding, pillows, throws) gives useful category shortcuts and a shipping perk.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The top banner repeats the same "Extra 20% off Select Styles" message as the last week of sends, so the offer reads as background noise.
- No deadline or end date is visible, so "HAPPENING NOW" feels empty.
- The three decor image blocks have no visible product names, prices, or individual links, so nothing concrete draws a click.
- The "Recommended Just For You" grid is off-theme furniture, with no prices and no tie to collectibles.
- The footer is heavy: the fine print, rewards, Affirm, and app blocks run long and dilute the ending.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the collectibles story and move the sale banner into a thin strip or the footer. Give the hero a single message, such as "Collectibles + extra 20% off through Sunday."
- 2. Add 3–4 hero products with names and prices (a Santa figure, stockings, the gingerbread village) in place of the generic furniture block.
- 3. Put a real deadline in the hero, such as "Ends Sunday at midnight PT," so the urgency in the subject is credible.
- 4. Cut the footer to the essentials and put the fine print behind a link.
- 5. Tell the same story in the subject line and the preheader, and do not repeat "Extra 20% off" in every send.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Vintage Santas are back (plus extra 20% off)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Start a new holiday tradition: collectibles, 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Santa mugs, gingerbread villages and stockings, ready to ship before Thanksgiving.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Extra 20% off ends Sunday. Free shipping on festive essentials.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, Marketing Cloud-style send with tracked links throughout and the usual MSO fallbacks. The main technical problems are widespread missing `alt` text on image-sliced content, subscriber PII in query strings (including over plain HTTP), and compliance headers that couldn't be verified. The HTML source was truncated, so findings are limited to what the source and QA output show.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **58 tracking links were not probed.** They are on click-redirect domains such as `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`, so destination, redirect chain and UTM presence are unverified.
- **Insecure tracking pixel.** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=<email>` loads over HTTP. It may be blocked or rewritten by clients, which would undercount opens.
- **Third-party pixels.** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/236cc4a1-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...` are extra tracking beacons. Both lack `alt` text, and the `d.turn.com` host adds an external dependency.
- **Mixed content.** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` use HTTP and may not render. Both hosts already serve HTTPS elsewhere (`https://edm.potterybarn.com/...`).
- **Stale or cross-brand assets.** `.../pb/2025/US/H25_Bottom_InStock_V2/H25_Bottom_InStock_V2.jpg` (2025) and the 2022 brand banner are reused in a 2026 send. `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on another brand's domain.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` text.** The QA run flagged about 55 images, so most of the email's image content is inaccessible with images off.
  - Hero: `H26_TOP_Extra20CLR.jpg`.
  - Collectibles section: `slices_01.jpg` to `slices_17.jpg` under `H26_Seasonal_B3_NostalgicCollectiblesShop/`.
  - Product tiles: `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-...`, `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-...`, `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`, `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg`, `belgian-flax-linen-curtain-1-l.jpg` and `standard-curtain-rod-wall-bracket-l.jpg`.
  - Footer: `Email-Footer/slices_01` to `slices_18`.
  - Pixels: `d.turn.com`, `svopn`, `clickcp` and `coreemail.html`.
  - Only the logo (`alt="POTTERY BARN"`) is confirmed to have `alt` in the visible source.
- **Sliced-image layout.** The 17 and 18 numbered slices suggest image-only sections. That means an images-off view loses the offer and CTAs, and the footer may lose its legal and navigation text.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **No `lang` attribute** on `<html>`.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="..., maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, an accessibility concern.
- **Stray server code in CSS.** The second `<style>` block ends with `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store..."); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. It sits after the last valid rules, so it is probably harmless, but it is invalid CSS and shows a template leak.
- **Spacer table.** `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><tr><td height="20">&nbsp;</td>` has no `role="presentation"` (other layout tables do).
- **Plain-text part.** About 72% of it is URLs (11091 of 15314 chars), which is a deliverability risk.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved tokens** appeared in the visible source.
- **Email address in URLs.** The recipient's address is appended to two URLs in cleartext:
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?...&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
- The second one is sent over HTTP, so the address is exposed in transit and in logs. This is a privacy and security issue.
- **`coreemail.html` is loaded as an `<img>`**, so it is a tracking beacon rather than content.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them. This is unconfirmed, but if the headers are absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unverified. The From domain is `e.potterybarn.com`, which matches the link and image domains.
- **Footer unverified.** The visible source doesn't show a text unsubscribe link or a physical postal address. The footer is built from 18 image slices without `alt`, so those required elements may be image-only or sit in the truncated portion. Confirm against the full source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters can't be confirmed.** Every link is wrapped in a redirect (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`) and was not resolved, so destinations are unverified.
- **Product images link to `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`.** The products shown (Toscana and Banks tables, Cayman bed, Reeva rug, Belgian flax curtain) are untested against the landing pages.
- **Offer mismatch can't be checked.** The "Extra 20% off" offer and the "Collectibles" section can't be tied to a landing page without resolving the redirects.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images and the footer slices. Use `alt=""` for spacers and pixels.
2. Switch the `http://` assets and the `svopn` pixel to HTTPS.
3. Stop putting the raw email address in `coreemail.html?email=` and `sv_sveme=`. Use an opaque subscriber ID.
4. Verify that `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` are present on the original send, and check SPF, DKIM and DMARC on the raw message.
5. Confirm a text unsubscribe link and a postal address in the full HTML. Don't rely on image-only footer slices.
6. Remove the `header(...)` text from the `<style>` block. Add a `<title>`, `lang="en"` and `role="presentation"` on the spacer table.
7. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
8. Improve the plain-text version, which is about 72% URLs.
9. Resolve the redirects to check for UTMs and landing-page alignment.
10. Replace the stale 2022 and 2025 assets and the cross-brand `edm.westelm.com` spacer.
