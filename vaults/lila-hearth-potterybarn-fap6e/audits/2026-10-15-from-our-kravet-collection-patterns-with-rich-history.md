# From our Kravet Collection: Patterns with rich history
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-15**
## Executive summary

- This is a pretty, editorial brand-collab send, and it has no offer. The Kravet hero is moody and well shot: a floral-patterned bed in dark blue and gold, with a "Kravet × Pottery Barn" lockup and a SHOP NOW button. The two serif pull-quotes ("Patterns tell the story of a home…" and "Start small, think in layers…") fit the "rich history" angle. As a brand moment it works, and it sits in the same lane as the Ashley Stark collab (10-14) and the Grinch collab (10-14).
- It is also very long, and it loses focus fast. After the Kravet content there is a "Recommended Just For You" grid (curtain rod, Banks dining table, Cayman bed, Toscano table, Remmy swivel chair, Farmhouse bed), then "1400+ New Arrivals," then Design Crew, the app, rewards and Affirm. Most of those modules have nothing to do with patterns. The single "SEE THE FULL COLLECTION" CTA is a small bar buried well down the page. Nothing in the email gives a reason to act today.
- Judgement: a good mood piece that works as brand building and is weak as a driver of sales. After the Fall Sale blasts it is a welcome change of pace, but it asks for no action.

## What's working

- The hero is strong: dark, textured and premium, with the Kravet co-brand lockup and a visible SHOP NOW button.
- The pull-quotes give the "rich history" subject line a story, and the layered-pattern styling fits it.
- Lifestyle photography is consistent and warm, and fits the fall mood.

- There are no visible render bugs: no broken images and no overlapping text.

## What's weak

- No offer, price, member benefit or deadline appears in the Kravet section, so there is no reason to click now.

- The Recommended grid (a curtain rod, dining tables, beds) doesn't connect to the pattern and textile theme.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional, built almost entirely from sliced images. Roughly 50 images have no `alt` text, three images load over `http://`, and stray server-side code is printed inside a `<style>` block. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because of how the email was captured, not because they failed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverifiable destinations:** 58 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect URLs, so none of their destinations were checked. Example: `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7ImQi...`. The `qs` payload is opaque.
- **Plaintext email in tracking pixels:** three pixel or beacon URLs carry the recipient address in the query string:
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/b19ed638-...?mi_cid=...`
- **Insecure open pixel:** the `svopn` pixel loads over `http://`, so it can be blocked and it exposes the address in transit.
- **Third-party beacon:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` is an ad-network pixel with no alt text.
- **Cross-brand asset:** the spacer `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on a different brand's domain.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 50 images are flagged. They include all 16 `0814_FRI_AM_FallKravetLaunch/slices_01–16.jpg` images, all 18 `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg` images, the product images (e.g. `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`, `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`) and the banners (`H26_Top_ChristmasShop.jpg`, `F26_Bottom_NewArrivals_V1.jpg`, `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`). The sliced content is unreadable with images blocked and for screen readers. The only text equivalent is the logo (`alt="POTTERY BARN"`).
- **`http://` images:** `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` and the `svopn` pixel are non-HTTPS and may be blocked or flagged as mixed content.
- **Leaked code in CSS:** the last `<style>` block contains `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store..."); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. This is invalid CSS from a templating error. Some clients may drop the rules that follow it.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>`.
- **Missing language:** `<html>` has no `lang` attribute.
- **Blocked zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>` prevents pinch-zoom.
- **Layout tables:** some layout tables lack `role="presentation"`. For example, the spacer `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><tr><td height="20">` has no role.
- **Content before the doctype:** `<!-- -->` precedes `<!DOCTYPE>`, which can trigger quirks mode in older Outlook/IE-based renderers.
- **Plain-text part:** 11,265 of 15,493 characters are URLs, which is over the 70% threshold. The plain-text version is mostly raw tracking links.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens are visible in the truncated source. The recipient address is merged correctly into the `sv_sveme=` and `?email=` parameters. That is a privacy concern (see section 2), not a rendering bug.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** both are not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**. Verify against the raw message before treating it as a sender defect.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** absent. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same reason (relay). The From domain `e.potterybarn.com` and the click domain `click.e.potterybarn.com` are aligned.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** the source is truncated, so they could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** none are visible. All links go through the opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` redirector, so UTM and landing-page alignment could not be verified.
- **Redirect probe gap:** the probe skipped every tracked link, so no landing-page status codes or redirect chains are available.
- **Product images:** they load from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`, so asset paths are first-party.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all sliced and product images, and `alt=""` to spacers and tracking pixels. Give the sliced hero images meaningful text, or live text for key copy.
2. Move `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` and the `svopn` pixel to HTTPS.
3. Remove the leaked `header(...)` calls from the `<style>` block.
4. Set `<title>` and `<html lang="en">`. Remove `maximum-scale=1`. Add `role="presentation"` to the remaining layout tables.
5. Stop putting the plaintext email address in image and beacon query strings. Use a hashed or opaque ID.
6. Check the raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results, because the relay may have hidden them.
7. Re-run the link check with the full HTML and a redirect-following probe to confirm destinations and UTMs.
8. Improve the plain-text part with real copy rather than mostly URLs.
