# Don’t miss your last chance to win $1500!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-28**
## Executive summary

- This isn't a product email at all — it's a design-consultation lead-gen pitch wearing a sweepstakes headline. The subject promises a $1500 giveaway, but the actual body barely mentions it: one banner line at the very top ("Meet with a designer before August 30th and you'll be entered for a chance to win a $1500 Gift Card") and then the entire rest of the email pivots to "RSVP for Free Design Help." That's a bait-and-switch structure — the thing that got you to open isn't the thing you're being sold once you're in.
- Once past the sweepstakes hook, it's a competent enough consultation-booking email: clear "what/when/where," a floral hero, five "book now" service touchpoints (decor, lighting, curtains, gallery walls), an in-store class RSVP, then the usual recommended-products grid and account/app footer. It's well-organized for what it is, just misaligned with what the subject sold.
- Judgement: the subject line will pull opens, but the reveal that it's actually a design-services upsell — with an entry deadline of August 30 buried in fine-print-sized text — will feel like a letdown. This reads as a 4-5, dragged down specifically by subject/body mismatch.

## What's working

- Clear repeated CTA pattern ("BOOK NOW") across every service block makes the desired action unambiguous.
- The "what/when/where" mini-format for the design appointment is genuinely scannable and useful.
- Clean, on-brand floral/neutral visual styling consistent with prior Pottery Barn sends.
- Distinct in-store class callout (Sept 13, 10AM) adds a concrete, dated event beyond the vague sweepstakes.

## What's weak

- The $1500 sweepstakes — the entire reason for the subject line — gets one line at the top and is never mentioned again; no rules link, no visible odds, no clear entry mechanism beyond "book a designer."
- No explicit CTA that says "enter to win" — you have to infer that booking an appointment is the entry method.
- Deadline (August 30) is only in that top banner, in small type, easy to miss on a scroll-heavy layout like this.
- The email is very long (multiple stacked service blocks + product grid + app promo + footer) before ever circling back to reinforce the offer that got the open.
- No image of the actual $1500 gift card or prize to reinforce the promise visually.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rebuild the top third around the prize, not the consultation — make "$1500 Gift Card Sweepstakes" the hero with a single clear CTA like "Book & Enter to Win," and repeat that framing at least once further down (e.g., near the in-store class or before the product grid).
- 2. Add a visible deadline treatment (badge or bolded line) near the top hero, not just inside dense banner text.
- 3. State the entry mechanism explicitly in one sentence: "Every completed design appointment = one sweepstakes entry" (or whatever the real rule is) — right now it's implied, not said.
- 4. Cut one or two of the five near-identical "decor touches / lighting / curtains / gallery walls" blocks — they're redundant with each other and dilute urgency toward the sweepstakes deadline.
- 5. **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: book a designer, win $1500`
- 6. **Subject Alt B:** `$1500 gift card sweepstakes ends Sunday`
- 7. **Preheader Alt A:** `Free design appointment = your entry. Ends August 30.`
- 8. **Preheader Alt B:** `Book your free design session before the deadline.`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Standard SFMC-templated transactional/marketing email with heavy click-tracking redirection; QA surfaced no critical failures but several compliance-header and accessibility warnings worth fixing.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All primary CTAs route through `click.e.potterybarn.com/?qs=...` SFMC click-tracking redirects (51 tracking links total, per QA — skipped for HTTP probing, so live destination validity is unconfirmed).
- Secondary tracking pixels/beacons present: `secure.leadback.advertising.com/adcedge/lb?site=695501...`, `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/...`, and an open-tracking pixel at `svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` — note this pixel passes the recipient email address in a plaintext query parameter, which is a PII-in-URL exposure risk (logged in server/proxy logs, referrer headers, etc.).
- Malformed `<style>` block in `<head>`: raw PHP-style `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ...");` statements are embedded directly inside a CSS `<style>` tag (not inside a script or server context). This is invalid CSS and will either be ignored or cause style-parsing errors in some clients — appears to be a copy/paste artifact from a server script, not functional code.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- Every content image (37 instances) is missing `alt` text, including product images (`remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`, `westin-heavenly-bed-mattress-1325h-l.jpg`, etc.), hero slices, and footer legal/nav images. This breaks accessibility for screen readers and image-blocked clients (Outlook default).
- Two images load over `http://` instead of `https://`: `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`. Mixed-content over http:// may be blocked or flagged by mail clients enforcing TLS.
- Media queries target both `max-width: 480px` and `max-width: 650px` with overlapping `.mobile-hide`/`.mobile-show`/`table[class="wrapper"]` rules — no functional conflict detected in the truncated source, but worth visual QA on mobile clients given the dual breakpoints.
- Standard Outlook/Apple Mail resets present (`mso-*`, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, `.ExternalClass`) — no issues found here.

Cannot confirm personalization logic executed correctly from this source alone.
