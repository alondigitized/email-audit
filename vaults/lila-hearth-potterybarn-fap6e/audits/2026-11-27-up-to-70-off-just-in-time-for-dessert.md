# UP TO 70% OFF 🥧 Just in time for dessert
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the same burgundy-plaid "Black Friday, up to 70% off" Pottery Barn template that has run since 11/16. It also follows the 11/26 "So thankful for you ❤️" send by one day. The hero is strong: a big "70% off" and a single "Shop Now" button. Below it is a clean grid of category tiles with their own "up to" percentages, so the offer is easy to read.
- The new element is the subject line, "Just in time for dessert 🥧". Nothing in the render supports it. I see no pie, no baking or hosting content, and no dessert tableware beyond a generic tabletop tile. The email hero still says "New deals added", which was already stale on 11/26. The body is the same as every prior send, so the subject sets up a payoff the email doesn't deliver.
- Overall it is a competent, on-brand sale email with no render problems. A subscriber who has seen this layout a dozen times in 11 days has no new reason to open or click. It is polished but repetitive.

## What's working

- The "70% off" hero is large and high-contrast, and the single "Shop Now" button sits directly below it.
- Category tiles (Furniture, Decor, Sofas, Tabletop, Pillows & Throws, Bedding, Gifts, Bath, Rugs) each carry their own "up to X%" figure, which makes the offer scannable.
- "Ships Free" flags on the tabletop, pillows and bedding tiles add a concrete incentive.
- A "Recommended Just For You" product block adds some personalization.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text visible.

## What's weak

- The "dessert" subject has no payoff in the body, so the email feels mismatched to its subject.
- The "New deals added" kicker is carried over from 11/26 and is no longer news.
- The email is very long and ends in a dense block of legal fine print, which buries the offer terms.
- There is no stated deadline in the hero, so urgency is weak for the last Black Friday send.
- The "Recommended" products show no prices or sale callouts, so there is no reason to click a specific item.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the creative match the subject. Lead with a tabletop, hosting or dessert hero, such as serveware, baking or pie dishes, and put a specific price on it.
- 2. Add a real deadline to the hero, such as "Ends tonight" or "Black Friday only". Drop "New deals added" unless something is actually new.
- 3. Add prices and sale badges to the recommended products, and cut the length of the email.
- 4. Reduce the legal block, or push it behind a link.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Black Friday is here: up to 70% off` (no emoji)
- **Subject Alt B:** `Pies, platters & more: up to 70% off tabletop`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Serveware, throws and furniture, plus free shipping on select styles.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Today only: our biggest savings of the season, in stores and online.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based and sliced-image HTML with SFMC click-tracking, and it renders. The main defects are PHP `header()` text leaking into a `<style>` block, responsive CSS that targets classes the visible markup doesn't use, linked image slices with no accessible name, and two `http://` images. Compliance and authentication could not be verified from the captured data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified tracking links:** All 64 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe (`click.e.potterybarn.com/?qs=…`), so destinations, redirect chains and 4xx/5xx status are unconfirmed.
- **Insecure images:** Two assets load over plain HTTP and may be blocked or trigger mixed-content warnings:
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
- **Stale asset:** The second asset's path (`/pb/2022/0311_…`) points to a 2022 creative reused in a 2026 send.
- **Dead markup:** The commented-out credit-card banner still contains a link (`…brandCode=POTTERYBARNCB&…cm_ite=PB_header_nonmember`). It does not render, but it adds weight to the HTML.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Stray PHP in CSS:** The second `<style>` block begins with `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: …"); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. This is invalid CSS. Those tokens become part of the prelude of the next rule, so the `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` reset is likely dropped. That means iOS may auto-link and restyle dates and addresses.
- **Responsive CSS doesn't match the markup:** The media query targets `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]`. The visible body tables are fixed `width="650"` with no `class="wrapper"`, and the slice images have no `class="photo"`. In the visible source, the 320px mobile rules therefore have nothing to apply to.
- **Linked images with no accessible name:** The slice images inside `<a>` tags carry `alt=""` (for example `slices_01.jpg` and `slices_02.jpg` under `1126_THU_PM2_BF_Grid`). The anchors then have no text and no alt, so screen readers announce a bare link. QA flags 11 grid slices, 18 footer slices, 9 `H26_xBrand_Bottom_Banner_Red` slices, 6 product images and 2 banners. If images are blocked, none of that content is available as text.
- **QA wording vs. source:** QA reports "missing alt", but the slices that appear in the truncated HTML have an empty `alt=""` attribute. I can't confirm the rest from the truncated source.
- **Tracking pixels:** These have no alt text (`secure.leadback.advertising.com/adcedge/lb…`, `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/…`, `svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=…`, `coreemail.html?email=…`). Low severity, but they should be `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Doctype position:** AMPscript comments sit before the `<!DOCTYPE>`, so the doctype is not the first node. It is minor, and only older clients are affected.
- **Webfont:** The Montserrat `<link>` in `<head>` is stripped by Gmail and Outlook. This is low impact.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved tokens:** No `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `%%=…=%%` strings are visible in the captured source.
- **Recipient address in URLs:** The address is written in clear text into the query strings of two third-party-facing URLs:
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `https://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  
  This is not a rendering bug, but it puts PII in URLs that may be logged by proxies and CDNs.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe:** The header was not found (QA WARN). QA notes it may be stripped by the AgentMail relay, so the failure is unconfirmed.
- **One-click unsubscribe:** `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was not found, with the same caveat. Gmail and Yahoo require both headers for bulk senders.
- **Authentication:** `Authentication-Results` was missing, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `e.potterybarn.com` and the click domain `click.e.potterybarn.com` are aligned.
- **Footer checks:** The body unsubscribe link and the postal address are in the truncated section, so I couldn't verify them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM/tracking params:** None can be evaluated. The captured links are opaque SFMC `qs=` tokens, and the probe skipped them. I found no UTM parameters in the visible source.
- **Landing pages:** The destination pages could not be checked because the links weren't resolved. The only readable parameters are `cm_ite` and `external_id`, and both are in the commented-out block.
- **Cross-brand asset:** A spacer is hosted on `edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`. That creates a dependency on another brand's CDN, and the image has no alt text.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Delete the `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block so the `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` reset applies.
2. Add `class="wrapper"` and `class="photo"` to the tables and images, or rewrite the media queries to target the real markup, and test at 320px.
3. Give linked slices descriptive alt text, such as the offer or the destination. Keep `alt=""` only on spacers and pixels.
4. Move the two `http://` assets to HTTPS and replace the 2022 banner.
5. Re-run QA against the raw `.eml` from the origin (not the relay) to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
6. Resolve a sample of the 64 `click.e.potterybarn.com` links with a follow-redirects probe, then check UTMs and landing-page status.
7. Stop passing the plain-text email address in pixel and `coreemail.html` query strings. Use an opaque subscriber key instead.
8. Remove the commented-out block and the cross-brand spacer to reduce HTML weight.
