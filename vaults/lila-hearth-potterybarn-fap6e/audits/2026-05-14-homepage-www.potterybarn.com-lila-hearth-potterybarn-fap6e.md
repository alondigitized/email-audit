## What's working

- **On-calendar campaign:** Father's Day sweepstakes with a May 24 deadline is timely — at least the brand isn't running a winter clearance in spring
- **Clean modal design:** The lifestyle shot (golf book, crystal decanters, bar accessories) is well-composed and high-production; Pottery Barn's visual standard is intact
- **Transparent fine print:** The SMS opt-in terms, Williams-Sonoma brand disclosure, and state exclusions are all surface-level visible — I actually trust a brand more when they're upfront like that
- **Williams-Sonoma brand mention:** I'm a WSom rewards member, so seeing "Williams-Sonoma brands" in the body copy reminds me this is my ecosystem

## What's weak

- **Modal completely blocks the homepage:** I cannot see a single room, product, category link, or editorial story — the entire shopping surface is behind this overlay
- **Wrong audience, wrong product:** Golf accessories and whiskey are not why I came to Pottery Barn; nothing in this modal is relevant to decorating a second home
- **No dismiss affordance in the hero zone:** The X is there but small and tucked in the upper right — the modal feels more like a toll gate than an invitation
- **No immediate escape to my category:** The navigation bar is barely visible at the top ("Baby, Kids, Teen, Dorm…") and truncated — I can't even one-tap to Furniture or Bedding
- **Sweepstakes is passive:** Entering to win doesn't make me want to shop; it just makes me close the modal and scroll

## Recommendations

- **Add a "No thanks, let me shop" text link** directly under the email field — low cost, keeps me in the funnel instead of annoyed-and-gone
- **Swap the modal trigger to exit-intent or timed delay** (15–20 seconds) so I see the homepage first and have a reason to care before being interrupted
- **Introduce a loyalty hook into the modal** — something like "Already a Pottery Barn Key member? Your entry also earns 200 points" would convert this interruption into a benefit for returning shoppers like me
- **Run a split experience:** Show this Dad sweepstakes to sessions that have browsed gifts or male giftee profiles; show me a home refresh editorial if my browsing signals second-home intent
