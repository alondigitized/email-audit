# Unlock early access to new arrivals, sales & more
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-23**
## Executive summary

- This is not a product email — it's a text-signup acquisition pitch wearing a promotional email's clothes. The hero asks you to sign up for SMS alerts, not to shop anything, and it burns the entire top fold (the only part most subscribers will see before bouncing) on a phone-icon graphic and "SIGN UP FOR TEXTS" button with zero product or offer in sight.
- Below the fold there are three nice lifestyle shots (cabinet, collaborations vignette, hurricane candles) each paired with generic category links ("Shop Now," "Shop Sale") but no price, no percentage, no named sale — just soft aspirational copy. It closes with another CTA to sign up for texts, then a wall of app/credit-card/Affirm cross-sell blocks before the footer.
- Compared to the brand's recent cadence — "Warehouse Sale up to 60% off," "NEW pillows, all shipping free!" — this send has no offer at all. It reads as a list-growth email slotted into the regular promotional cadence, and for a shopper opening expecting a deal, it's a letdown. Weak send: pretty photography, no reason to click through to a product.

## What's working

- Clean, on-brand visual hierarchy — three distinct lifestyle categories are easy to scan.
- Photography is high quality and consistent with Pottery Barn's aesthetic (warm wood tones, styled vignettes).
- No render bugs — images load, text is legible, layout is clean on both hero and body.
- Footer/legal and unsubscribe blocks are present and complete, no missing compliance info.

## What's weak

- The hero — the only guaranteed-visible section — has no product, no price, no offer, just an SMS opt-in pitch.
- All three body sections use vague CTAs ("Shop Now," "Shop Sale") with no specific product, discount, or category name attached.
- No concrete offer anywhere in the email, a sharp departure from recent sends like the 60%-off warehouse sale or free-shipping pushes.
- Subject promises "early access to new arrivals, sales & more" but the email never shows a new arrival or a sale — it's bait for an SMS signup.
- Heavy cross-sell clutter (App, credit card, Affirm) competes with whatever thin merchandising exists.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with an actual product or sale in the hero, then move the SMS pitch to a secondary module — right now the highest-value real estate is spent on a non-purchase ask.
- 2. Replace generic "Shop Now"/"Shop Sale" links with named products or percentages (e.g., "Shop the Cassie Cabinet," "20% Off Lighting") to give a reason to click.
- 3. Align subject/preview with actual content — if this is fundamentally an SMS-acquisition email, don't frame it as a new-arrivals/sales email; if it's meant to merchandise, add the merchandising.
- 4. Trim the cross-sell stack (App + credit card + Affirm) to one module max so it doesn't dilute the shopping CTAs.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New arrivals just landed — shop the edit`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Text us for first dibs on new arrivals + sales`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fresh fall pieces for every room, starting today`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sign up for texts and never miss a sale`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based HTML render from Salesforce Marketing Cloud (Pottery Barn's `e.potterybarn.com` domain), with functional click-tracking and open-pixel infrastructure, but several images lack alt text and two legacy footer assets still load over unencrypted HTTP.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All primary CTAs route through `click.e.potterybarn.com/?qs=...` (SFMC CloudPages tracked-link format) — consistent and expected for ExactTarget/SFMC sends.
- Open tracking present via `svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` — note the recipient email address is passed in plaintext in the pixel URL query string.
- Third-party tracking pixels present: `secure.leadback.advertising.com/adcedge/lb?site=695501&srvc=1&betr=...` and `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/...` (AOL/Xandr audience tracking) — functioning as expected for retargeting, flagged only for visibility.
- QA skipped HTTP probing on 31 tracking/redirect links (expected behavior for click-redirect domains, not a defect).

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 24 of 24 content/footer images are missing `alt` attributes (slices_01–slices_18, Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg, and others) — screen readers and blocked-image states will show nothing.
- Legacy vendor CSS injection bug: the `<style>` block contains literal PHP `header()` calls (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); ...`) leaked into CSS — these are inert as CSS but indicate a template/build artifact that should not ship in production HTML.
- Mobile responsive rules target `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]`, but no elements in the visible source actually use `class="wrapper"` or `class="photo"` — the media query may be dead code for this send.
- Outlook/Apple Mail resets (`x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, `ExternalClass`, `-webkit-text-size-adjust`) are present and standard.
