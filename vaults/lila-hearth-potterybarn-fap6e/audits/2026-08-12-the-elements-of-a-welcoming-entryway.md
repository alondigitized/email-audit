# The elements of a welcoming entryway
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-12**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's "How to Style Your Entryway" send — a numbered, editorial styling guide (vase → candles → decorative objects → botanicals → baskets) with a moody, warm-toned entryway hero. It's cut from the same cloth as 2026-08-11's "Coffee table styling 101" — another decor-education format rather than a sale dump — and it's the more polished of the two, with better photographic consistency and a genuinely coherent numbered structure that a reader can actually follow top to bottom.
- The problem is that "styling guide" only works as a hook if the reader wants styling inspiration in general — there's no discount, no urgency, and no entryway-specific promise beyond the subject line itself. It's a pleasant browse, not a compelling open. Buried below the fold is a Halloween teaser and a Design Crew sweepstakes CTA that have nothing to do with entryways, diluting the one clear theme the email actually earns.
- Competent execution, weak commercial pull. This will perform like a content/nurture email, not a conversion email — fine for brand affinity, unlikely to move revenue this send.

## What's working

- Clean numbered 1-5 structure makes the "how to" concept easy to scan and mentally follow.
- Photography is consistent — warm, moody, lived-in entryway aesthetic carried through every product shot.
- Each step pairs a lifestyle photo with a tight sentence and a specific shop-this-category link (Vases, Candles, Decorative Objects, Botanicals, Baskets).
- "Recommended Just for You" section adds personalization signal with real product images and names.
- No render bugs — clean grid alignment, no overlapping text or broken images visible.

## What's weak

- No offer anywhere in the primary content — zero %/$ off, no free shipping callout, nothing that creates urgency to act now.
- Halloween shop banner and Design Crew sweepstakes are bolted onto the bottom with no visual or thematic connection to entryway styling — the email loses focus right when it should be closing strong.
- Subject and hero promise "your entryway" but the content is generic decor categories (vases, candles, baskets) that could apply to any room — the entryway framing is thin.
- "Recommended Just for You" mixes curtain rods, dining tables, and bedding into an entryway email — undercuts the single-room premise.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a concrete incentive tied to the entryway theme — e.g., "20% off entryway furniture" or free shipping on the featured categories — a styling guide alone won't move a subscriber who's already seen two of these this week.
- 2. Cut or clearly separate the Halloween/Design Crew blocks with a visual break (divider, different header treatment) so they don't read as part of the entryway narrative.
- 3. Tighten "Recommended Just for You" to entryway-adjacent items only (consoles, mirrors, rugs) instead of dining tables and bedding — reinforces rather than dilutes the theme.
- 4. Consider adding a small "shop the whole look" bundle CTA near the hero for readers who don't want to scroll through five steps.

## Technical Audit

Technical Audit — Pottery Barn "The elements of a welcoming entryway"

**1. Technical Summary**
Standard EDM-platform (Bronto/Bronto-style) HTML email with heavy legacy table markup; core rendering hooks are present, but the send is missing RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe headers, has several HTTP (non-TLS) image sources, and near-universal missing `alt` attributes across content and tracking images.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 64 tracking/click-redirect links (`click.e.potterybarn.com`, `clickcp.potterybarn.com`) were skipped by the automated HTTP probe — these are redirect-through links by design, not a defect, but they weren't independently validated for final destination.
- Open-tracking pixel served over plaintext HTTP: `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` — mixed-content risk in HTTPS-rendered mail clients; may be blocked, silently failing open tracking.
- Second tracking beacon `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` present and served over HTTPS — no issue.
- Header nav link uses a `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirect with an opaque `qs=` param — consistent with platform tracking, not itself a defect.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- 47 of the flagged images are missing `alt` text, including primary content banners (`F26_Top_NewArrivals.jpg`, `F26_Bottom_HalloweenShop.jpg`), all 18 header "slices" images, all 18 footer "slices" images, and 5 product shots (curtain rod bracket, dining table, curtain, sheet set, bed, rug). Screen-reader/image-blocked fallback will render blank.
- 3 images served over `http://` instead of `https://`: `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`, `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` — mixed content, likely to be blocked or flagged by mail clients enforcing HTTPS.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**

- No issues found beyond the above.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not found — cannot confirm mailbox-level one-click unsubscribe is offered; CAN-SPAM requires *a* working opt-out mechanism (in-body link may satisfy this, not confirmed in truncated source) but modern deliverability best practice (Gmail/Yahoo 2024 bulk-sender rules) requires the header.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found — RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe unsupported; combined with missing `List-Unsubscribe`, this is a deliverability compliance gap for bulk senders.
- `Authentication-Results` header not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from available data; noted as a QA/relay visibility gap (per automated findings, may be an AgentMail relay capture limitation rather than a sender-side failure).

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Outbound links route through `click.e.potterybarn.com` and `clickcp.potterybarn.com` redirect wrappers rather than direct UTM-tagged URLs — actual landing-page/UTM parameters are only visible post-redirect and weren't captured in this scan (link probe was skipped for these domains, see §2).
- No direct `potterybarn.com` deep links with visible UTM parameters were present in the truncated source to verify against landing-page content.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers to the send to meet RFC 8058 and current Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images (banners, product shots) at minimum; decorative slice/spacer images can use `alt=""`.
3. Migrate the three `http://` image sources to `https://` to avoid mixed-content blocking, including the `svopn.potterybarn.com` open-tracking pixel (currently likely non-functional in HTTPS mail clients).
4. Remove the stray PHP `header()` calls leaked into the `<style>` block — indicates a template/build artifact that should be cleaned at the source template level.
5. Confirm `Authentication-Results` visibility with the relay provider to validate SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status independent of this scan's limitation.
