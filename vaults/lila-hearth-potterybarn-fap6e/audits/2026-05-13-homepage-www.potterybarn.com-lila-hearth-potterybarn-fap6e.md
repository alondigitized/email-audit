# Homepage snapshot · www.potterybarn.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- I came to Pottery Barn to browse for the second home — maybe new throw pillows, a dining refresh, something for the porch — and instead the entire site is buried under a golf sweepstakes pop-up for Dad. As a Williams-Sonoma family brand I genuinely love, I expected better than this. The modal reads like a mass-email blast that somehow got promoted to the homepage.

## What's working

- **Seasonal relevance:** The Father's Day angle (U.S. Open, golf, whiskey) is timely for mid-May — it's not a stale "Summer Refresh" from August.
- **Clear prize value:** "$4,000 grandstand seats + $500 to spend" communicates the prize stakes immediately, no ambiguity.
- **Williams-Sonoma brand tie-in in fine print:** Seeing "Williams-Sonoma brands" in the opt-in copy gives me a flicker of recognition — I'm already in their ecosystem.
- **Modal renders cleanly:** No broken images, no overlapping text, the hierarchy from image → headline → body copy → CTA is legible.

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is hidden:** This modal is a full-blotter — I cannot see a single product, category, or navigation item relevant to what I came here to buy.
- **Golf/whiskey/bar imagery:** Completely off-brief for someone shopping home furnishings. I feel like I landed on a men's gifting page by accident.
- **SMS dark pattern:** The consent language ("By entering your mobile number you will receive automated marketing text messages") is buried below the offer copy and before the submit button — classic pressure opt-in.
- **No loyalty hook for me:** I'm a Williams-Sonoma Key Rewards member. There is zero acknowledgment of that relationship or any personalized returning-shopper signal.
- **No path to my category:** The nav is almost entirely obscured. I can barely read "Baby, Kids, Teen, Dorm, Business to Business, William[s-Sonoma]" at the very top edge — nothing about furniture, bedding, or outdoor.
- **Sweepstakes ≠ a promo I can use:** Entering to *maybe* win $500 is not the same as a 15%-off-your-next-order offer I'd actually apply to a duvet purchase today.

## Recommendations

- **Make the X button larger and immediately obvious** — right now it exists but feels like an afterthought; users who aren't the target (most of them) should be able to escape in one tap without searching.
- **Serve this sweepstakes modal only to users browsing gift or registry contexts**, not cold visitors landing on the homepage — behavioral or referral targeting could narrow this to gift-intent sessions.
- **Add a rewards/loyalty banner below the modal dismiss** — even a slim "Welcome back, Lila — you have 1,240 Key Rewards points" strip would make a returning member feel seen the moment they close this pop-up.
- **Replace the golf bar-cart imagery with a lifestyle shot of the featured prize product** — if the $500 prize is meant to excite people about Pottery Barn's catalog, show a beautiful room they could shop, not a whiskey glass on a dark wood table.
