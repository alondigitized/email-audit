# Early Black Friday deals to beat the holiday rush
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- This is the same burgundy "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY / up to 70% off" template Pottery Barn has sent all week. It is the sixth or seventh near-identical send since 11/16. The hero is clear: a big 70% number over a Christmas-red and neutral product collage, with a single SHOP NOW button. Nothing here is new, though. If you opened "Up to 70% off for EARLY BLACK FRIDAY" yesterday, this adds nothing.
- The body is competent but crowded. A burgundy category grid, a "More Early Black Friday Deals" block with real was/now prices (Cole Swivel $1,749 → $874, Aubrey Cabinet $3,694 → $3,324), then a "Recommended Just for You" grid, an in-stock furniture banner, a sweepstakes ad, an app promo, a credit card promo and Affirm. That is too many competing asks below the fold. The "holiday rush" angle in the subject is only echoed lightly in the body, via the "In-stock furniture… delivered in 1–4 weeks" block.
- Verdict: a solid but fatigued promo. It would work as a first touch. As the Nth send this week it is wallpaper.

## What's working

- The 70% off hero is the first thing the eye lands on, with a clear SHOP NOW button.
- The category grid (Furniture, Sofas & Chairs, Bedding, Rugs and so on) makes the offer easy to navigate.
- The priced product tiles show real was/now figures. The Cole Swivel at 50% off is a believable proof point.
- "Ships Free" badges on some tiles add a second incentive.
- The "In-stock furniture, delivered in 1–4 weeks" block speaks to the holiday-rush promise in the subject.

## What's weak

- Creative fatigue: it is the same hero and layout as the 11/16 and 11/17 sends, with no new reason to open.
- The Aubrey Cabinet is only $370 off ($3,694 → $3,324), about 10%. That undercuts the "up to 70%" headline.
- Too many tail modules (sweepstakes, app, credit card, Affirm) dilute the shopping intent.
- The "Recommended Just for You" tiles have no prices or offer, so there is little reason to click them.
- The fine print is a long, dense block of exclusions that makes the offer feel less honest.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop re-sending the same hero. Rotate in a new hero each day, such as a "final days" or "ships by Dec 24" angle, so the subject and creative match.
- 2. Put the strongest single-item deal (the $874 swivel chair) in the hero instead of a generic collage.
- 3. Cut the app, credit card and Affirm modules, or collapse them into one footer line.
- 4. Show prices or percent-off on the "Recommended" tiles.
- 5. Move the in-stock delivery promise higher, since it matches the "beat the rush" subject.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Our $874 swivel chair + more early deals`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `In-stock furniture arrives in 1–4 weeks. Free shipping on select styles.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, image-slice layout with about 50 images missing `alt` text, three non-HTTPS image sources, and stray server-side code inside a `<style>` block. The QA header warnings (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) are probably relay artifacts and can't be confirmed from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 12,318 of 17,306 characters (about 71%) are URLs. This comes from the long tracked URLs, and it can raise spam-filter scrutiny.
- **Tracking pixels are over HTTP and carry the address.** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is a non-HTTPS pixel with the recipient address in the query string.
- **Third-party pixel.** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` is an ad-network beacon.
- **Address in image URL.** `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is loaded as an image.
- **Stale assets.** `H25_Bottom_InStock_V2.jpg` (2025) and `2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` (2022) are old creative.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 50 images.** The main ones are:
  - `H26_Sale_EBF_9GridHeroLinkfarm/slices_01–12.jpg`
  - `1118_WED_AM_EBF_9GridHeroLinkfarm/slices_01–06.jpg`
  - `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`
  - product images such as `classic-velvet-stockings-l.jpg` and `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`
  - `H26_Bottom_DesignCrewSweeps_01/02.jpg`
  - the 1×1 pixels (`d.turn.com`, `svopn`, `clickcp`), which should carry `alt=""` and are benign.

  The 9-grid "linkfarm" slices are likely wrapped in `<a>` tags. With images off or a screen reader, those links have no accessible name.
- **Non-HTTPS images.** Three sources use `http://`: `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `slices_02.jpg` from the 2022 banner, and the `svopn` pixel. They may be blocked or rewritten by clients.
- **Stray code in CSS.** The second `<style>` block contains `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store…"); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. This is leaked PHP and is invalid CSS. It may break the rules after it in strict parsers.
- **Empty title.** `<title></title>` is present.
- **Zoom disabled.** The viewport meta has `maximum-scale=1`, which blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Web font.** `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Montserrat…">` is stripped by Gmail and Outlook. The fallback fonts are not visible in the truncated source.
- **Outlook fallback.** A VML/MSO block is present. The source is truncated, so I can't confirm bulletproof buttons.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The recipient address resolved in `coreemail.html?email=` and `sv_sveme=`, and no unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) are visible. The source is truncated, so the rest of the body is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes AgentMail may not capture these headers. Treat this as unconfirmed rather than a sender defect. If the real message lacks them, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both.
- **Authentication-Results absent.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment can't be assessed. The From domain is `e.potterybarn.com`.
- **Footer not visible.** The truncated HTML doesn't show the visible unsubscribe link or the physical address, so CAN-SPAM body requirements can't be confirmed.
- **Address in third-party URLs.** The recipient email is passed in pixel and image URLs, including to `d.turn.com` via the beacon. This is a privacy consideration.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Nothing is confirmable. All links use opaque `qs=` tokens (for example `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7ImQiOjUwNjQs…`), and they were not resolved. UTM parameters, landing-page match and sale-offer consistency need a redirect-following pass.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images and linked slices. Use `alt=""` for spacers and pixels.
2. Move the `http://` images and the `svopn` pixel to HTTPS.
3. Remove the PHP `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block.
4. Fill in `<title>` and drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Resolve the tracked links with redirect-following to check final URLs, UTMs and status codes.
6. Check the raw `.eml` for List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post and Authentication-Results, since the relay likely dropped them.
7. Check the full HTML footer for the unsubscribe link and postal address.
8. Remove the stale 2022 and 2025 assets and the West Elm-hosted spacer.
9. Consider not putting the raw recipient address in pixel and image query strings.
