# 100% free interior design help
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- This is a service email, not a promo. Pottery Barn is pushing free design appointments, not product. The hero, "RSVP for Your Free Design Services," is clear. The what/when/where block is scannable, and the dark green **BOOK A FREE APPOINTMENT** button is the obvious next step. It's a real change of pace from this week's run, which was mostly category and sale sends like "Nightstands that add storage & style" and "These designs are on sale & ship free!"
- The middle of the email weakens it. After the hero it becomes a heritage-look mood board with four unlabeled, unlinked-looking photos, then a second identical CTA. A "Prep for Fall Hosting" class banner and a six-item "Recommended Just For You" grid follow. Two different asks (book a consult, RSVP for a class) plus a product grid split the focus. The footer runs long, with credit card, Affirm and app promos.
- Verdict: a solid, low-friction concept with a clean hero. It's undersold by a vague "spots go fast" deadline and a bloated back half. It's a better send than the average mood-board email, but it's still a mid-pack email.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer plainly in the headline: "Free Design Services."
- The what / when / where trio makes the format concrete: styling tips, pick a time, at home, virtual or in-store.
- The CTA button is high-contrast and repeated after the mood board, so a scroller doesn't have to go back up.
- The fall mood-board imagery (dark wood, plaid bedding, layered dining) is on-season and on-brand.
- The dated class banner ("Oct 4 | Sunday, 10AM") is the one credible deadline in the email.

## What's weak

- The urgency is vague: "spots go fast" has no date, no slot count and no cutoff.
- The middle is four large photos with no captions, product names or per-image links. It's pretty but doesn't say what the designers actually do.
- The class banner's "RSVP TODAY" competes with the main "BOOK A FREE APPOINTMENT" ask.
- The "Recommended Just For You" grid (curtain rod, rug, ghost collection) is generic. It has no prices or reviews and doesn't connect to the design-services message.
- The footer stacks a credit card offer, Affirm and the app promo under the service pitch. That dilutes a clean message.
- There are no testimonials or before-and-afters to show why a free consult is worth the time.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real deadline and scarcity, e.g. "Book by Oct 12" or "Limited appointments this month," in the hero and on the button's sub-line.
- 2. Replace the mood-board photos with one before/after or a designer quote. That gives social proof and shows the outcome.
- 3. Pick one primary CTA. Move the class banner below the second button, or send it separately.
- 4. Cut the recommended grid to three items tied to the mood board and put shoppable labels on the photos. Move the card and Affirm blocks out of this send.
- 5. Show a designer's face and name. It makes "free" feel human rather than like a sales funnel.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Pick a time: at home, virtual or in-store. Appointments are limited.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, image-sliced layout with roughly 47 ClickThrough-wrapped links. It has widespread missing alt text on `<img>` elements, two non-HTTPS images, and stray server-side code inside a `<style>` block. Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be confirmed because the relay didn't capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque tracking links.** 47 links use `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/?qs=...`. The QA probe skipped all of them, so destination, redirect chain and UTM presence are unverified.
- **Mixed-content images.** Two images load over `http://`, which some clients block or rewrite:
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
- **Third-party pixels.** These are ad-network beacons, and the last two carry the recipient address in the URL:
  - `secure.leadback.advertising.com/adcedge/lb?site=695501...`
  - `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1`
  - `svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
- **Plaintext address in query strings.** The recipient address is exposed unhashed in the `svopn` and `coreemail.html` URLs. That leaks it to logs and proxies.
- **Cross-brand asset.** The spacer `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on a West Elm domain, which is an unnecessary external dependency.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** About 40 images have no alt text, and some have no `alt` attribute at all.
  - **Hero slices:** `slices_01.jpg` to `slices_08.jpg` under `/0929_TUE_AM2_DC_ColorEditGreen/`. These have `alt=""`, and each is the only content inside an `<a>`. With images off or a screen reader, those links have no accessible name and the email has no readable content.
  - **Product images:** `reeva-handwoven-rug-3-l.jpg`, `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`, `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`, `emery-linen-curtain-l.jpg`, and others.
  - **Footer:** `/Email-Footer/slices_01.jpg` to `slices_18.jpg`.
  - **Tracking pixels:** these should use `alt=""` explicitly. The QA tool reports the `svopn` and `d.turn.com` pixels as missing alt text.
- **Stray code inside `<style>`.** The second `<style>` block begins with PHP:
  - `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store, ...`
  - This is invalid CSS. It is likely a template leak, and it can cause the parser to drop or mis-handle the next rule, `a[x-apple-data-detectors]`.
- **Content before the DOCTYPE.** `<!-- begin dynamic ampScript slot --> <!-- end dynamic ampScript slot -->` comes before `<!DOCTYPE ...>`. In some clients this can trigger quirks mode.
- **Mobile selectors may not match.**
  - The responsive rules target `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]`.
  - The visible source shows fixed `width="650"` tables and images with no `wrapper` or `photo` class.
  - If no elements carry those classes, the 480px media query does nothing. The truncated HTML means I can't confirm this fully.
- **Font link.** `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Montserrat...">` is stripped by Gmail and Outlook. No issue if a fallback stack exists, but I didn't see one.
- **Commented-out block.** A large HTML block (credit-card banner) sits in a comment. It is inert but adds size.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The AMPscript slot is empty and no unresolved `%%...%%`, `{{...}}` or `${...}` tokens appear in the visible source. The recipient address renders correctly in the pixel URLs (see the PII note in section 2).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found.** The QA tool notes this may be an AgentMail relay artifact. It is unverified, not confirmed absent.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found.** The same caveat applies. Without it, RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is not confirmed, and Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **`Authentication-Results` not captured.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `e.potterybarn.com`, and click and image hosts differ (`click.e.potterybarn.com`, `image.e.potterybarn.com`, `edm.potterybarn.com`).
- **Footer not visible.** The visible source is truncated, so I can't confirm a physical postal address or a visible unsubscribe link.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not verifiable.** UTM parameters and landing URLs sit behind the `qs=` payloads. All 47 tracking links were skipped, so campaign attribution and landing-page alignment couldn't be checked.
- **Confirmed direct URLs.** The only ones I could see are in the commented-out block (`...?brandCode=POTTERYBARNCB&marketingChannelCode=APPLYANDBUY&external_id=PB_Email_MemberBanner&cm_ite=...`), and it is inert.
- **Product images.** They point to `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`. That is fine, but it couples email rendering to the live site's asset paths.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content image, especially the linked hero slices `slices_01` to `slices_08`. Use `alt=""` on tracking pixels and spacers.
2. Remove the PHP `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block, and move the AMPscript comments after the DOCTYPE.
3. Switch the two `http://` image URLs to `https://`.
4. Stop putting the plaintext recipient address in the `svopn` and `coreemail.html` query strings. Use a hashed or opaque ID.
5. Check whether the `wrapper` and `photo` classes exist on the tables and images, or change the selectors so the mobile rules apply.
6. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` on the raw message. Capture the raw headers, not the AgentMail-relayed ones, to verify SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
7. Resolve a sample of the `click.e.potterybarn.com` links manually to confirm UTMs and landing pages. Re-run the QA probe with redirect-following for tracking domains.
8. Host the spacer image on `potterybarn.com` infrastructure instead of `edm.westelm.com`.
