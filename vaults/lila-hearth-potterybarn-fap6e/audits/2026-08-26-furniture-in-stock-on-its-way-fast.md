# Furniture in stock & on its way FAST
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-26**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's in-stock/fast-shipping send — "Beautiful Designs, Delivered Fast," "Delivered in 1-4 Weeks," and a "In-Stock Living Room Furniture" hero, built to address the real pain point of furniture lead times. It's a genuinely different value prop from the recent run of color-sort bedding and catalog-teaser emails, and the hero copy leads with the actual differentiator (speed) rather than a generic seasonal hook. The category grid — Pillows & Decor, Lighting, Bedroom, Quick-Ship Seating, Rugs, Dining, Office, Bedding — is comprehensive but that's also the problem: there's no single hero product or price, just a wall of category tiles.
- The email is long and repetitive in structure (rooms → tiles → rooms → tiles → recommended-for-you → sweepstakes → Halloween → app promo → footer), and there's no discount anywhere — this is a logistics/availability message, not a savings message. For a furniture shopper that's fine, but it undercuts urgency-driven click behavior. Overall this is a solid category-refresh idea, competently executed, but it's buried under too much scroll and no price incentive.

## What's working

- Hero headline and "DELIVERED IN 1-4 WEEKS" badge immediately communicate the differentiator without needing to scroll.
- Clean, consistent room photography (living room, bedroom, dining, office) gives the grid a cohesive, on-brand look.
- "Recommended Just For You" section signals personalization/segmentation.
- Free shipping ("SHIPS FREE") is called out on the bedding tile.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text is legible, layout holds together end to end.

## What's weak

- No concrete offer/discount anywhere in the body — this is purely a shipping-speed message, weaker for click-through than a %-off send.
- Extremely long scroll (8 room/category sections plus sweepstakes, Halloween teaser, and app promo) dilutes the "fast delivery" hook before you reach "Shop All In-Stock."
- Primary CTA ("Shop All In-Stock") is generic and sits far down the page — no clear single hero product to click into.
- The Design Crew Sweepstakes and Halloween Shop blocks are off-message clutter in a fast-shipping email, diffusing focus.
- Subject/hero promise "delivered fast" but no delivery date, cutoff, or urgency deadline is shown to make that concrete.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email roughly in half — keep hero + 3-4 top in-stock categories + one CTA, drop the Halloween and sweepstakes blocks (already run this week) to protect the fast-shipping message.
- 2. Add a concrete secondary offer (e.g., "extra 15% off in-stock furniture" or a specific delivery date) since speed alone with no price signal is a soft click driver.
- 3. Move "Shop All In-Stock" (or a room-specific CTA) up near the hero so there's an immediate, unambiguous action.
- 4. Replace the generic room-grid opener with one hero SKU + price + "arrives by [date]" to give shoppers something concrete to click.
- **Subject Alt A:** `In stock now — furniture that arrives in weeks, not months`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Skip the wait: 1600+ pieces ready to ship`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `No backorders. Just fast, beautiful furniture.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sofas, beds & dining sets — delivered in 1-4 weeks.`

# Technical Audit: Pottery Barn "Furniture in stock & on its way FAST"

**1. Technical Summary**
Standard MI-platform (Merkle/Digital River) HTML email with a 650px table layout and Sailthru/Cordial-style click tracking. Structurally sound for rendering, but fails several accessibility and header-level compliance checks, with two mixed-content (HTTP) image sources.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- Primary nav link routes through `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` (standard ESP click-tracking redirect) — 58 total tracking/redirect links detected across the document; all skipped automated HTTP probing (expected behavior for click-redirect domains, not itself an error).
- Open-tracking pixels present via multiple vendors: `svopn.potterybarn.com` (Sailthru-style open beacon), `d.turn.com/r/dd/...` (third-party ad/data pixel), and `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/...?mi_mid=...` (MessageIQ/Message Systems open pixel). Redundant multi-vendor open tracking is present but not a functional defect.
- No issues found with link structural formatting (all `<a href>` values are well-formed absolute URLs).

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- 45 of ~46 images missing `alt` attributes, including all content banners (`F26_Top_NewArrivals.jpg`, `F26_Top_DesignCrewSweeps_01a/b.jpg`, `F26_Bottom_HalloweenShop.jpg`) and all footer/legal slice images. This breaks screen-reader/image-blocked rendering — recipients with images off (default in Outlook) will see a blank email with no fallback text.
- Two images served over plain HTTP instead of HTTPS: `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` (plus one more `slices_02.jpg` from `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/...`) and the Sailthru open pixel `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/...` — non-HTTPS resources are commonly stripped or blocked by mail clients enforcing mixed-content policies (Gmail, Outlook.com).
- Stray `<style>` block contains raw PHP-style `header(...)` calls (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store...")`) — these are inert leftover code inside a CSS `<style>` tag, not executable, but indicate a templating/build error where server-side code leaked into the client HTML. Should be stripped; no functional rendering impact since browsers/mail clients ignore invalid CSS content, but it's dead code worth flagging to the ESP.
- Responsive breakpoints defined at both 650px and 700px min-width media queries with overlapping `img {max-width}` rules — not contradictory but redundant.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- Recipient email is hard-coded/interpolated correctly into tracking URLs (e.g., `...email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`, `sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`) — merge token resolved correctly, no unresolved `{{}}` or `%%%%` placeholder tokens found in the visible source.
- No issues found with broken/unresolved merge syntax.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — note this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not surfacing the header rather than the sender omitting it; recommend confirming directly against raw SMTP headers before treating as a sender-side defect.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe) not detected — same relay caveat applies. If genuinely absent, this is a Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirement gap (mandatory since Feb 2024 for high-volume senders).
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not detected — same relay caveat; cannot confirm authentication posture from this data alone.
- In-body unsubscribe/customer-service link present and resolves to `potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=...`, satisfying the visible CAN-SPAM unsubscribe-mechanism requirement, though that image tag is also missing `alt` text.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Outbound links use ESP-native click-tracking wrappers (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`) rather than plain UTM-tagged URLs — final destination/UTM params are not visible in the truncated source (obscured behind the encoded `qs` param), so landing-page alignment can't be verified from this data.
- Customer-service link uses direct (non-wrapped) URL to `potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html`, with the recipient email appended as a query param rather than a tracking token — consistent with a support/self-service link pattern, not flagged as an issue.
- No issues found with domain mismatch — all commerce/content links target `potterybarn.com`/`e.potterybarn.com`/`edm.potterybarn.com`, consistent with sender domain.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images (banners, product shots) — currently 45+ images have none, which is the highest-impact fix for accessibility and images-off rendering.
2. Migrate the remaining HTTP image/pixel sources to HTTPS (`edm.westelm.com`, `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/...`, `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/...`, `svopn.potterybarn.com`) to avoid mixed-content blocking in Gmail/Outlook.com.
3. Remove the dead PHP `header(...)` statements embedded in the `<style>` block — leftover server-side code with no function in delivered HTML, likely a template-export artifact worth reporting to the ESP/build pipeline.
4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` directly against raw SMTP headers (bypassing the AgentMail relay) to confirm whether these are real compliance gaps or relay-side visibility gaps before escalating.
5. If pursuing full email-to-site continuity checks, resolve the `click.e.potterybarn.com` wrapped redirects to their final destination URLs to confirm UTM parameters and landing-page match.
