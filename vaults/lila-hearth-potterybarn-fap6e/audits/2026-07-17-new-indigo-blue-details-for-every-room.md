# NEW indigo blue details for every room
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-17**
## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn breaks from the July discount grind with a color-story editorial — indigo blue new arrivals anchored by the richest hero photography of the entire month. After seven or eight compound-subject sale emails (the July 14–16 run alone hammered "UP TO 70% OFF" three times), this is the right strategic pivot. The INDIGO hero is dark, dramatic, product-cohesive, and the subject line earns its 38 characters without a discount crutch.
- The execution, however, betrays the premise. That "ANOTHER SALE" bar planted at the very top of an editorial email creates immediate cognitive dissonance — if this is a curated color moment, commit to it. Instead the brand flinches, slapping a vague sale label on a send that has no actual offer to back it up. Scroll past the hero and the lower half is the familiar pile-on: Free Design Services (third appearance in four days), the app download module, Rewards, Affirm, and now Snapchat. Each module competes for the click; none of them win.
- Net read: this is the most creatively coherent email Pottery Barn has sent in July, but the operational clutter and that one cowardly "ANOTHER SALE" bar stop it from being the editorial statement it could have been.

## What's working

- Hero INDIGO photography is the strongest single visual asset of the entire July cycle — dark, atmospheric, and product-cohesive across every frame.
- Subject line breaks the compound-subject formula and lands under 40 characters — a clear, color-specific hook with no spam noise.
- Editorial hero copy ("Deep waters and dusty denim, bold, yet serene…") matches the visual mood and gives the send a coherent voice.
- "RECOMMENDED JUST FOR YOU" personalization grid is a smart below-fold addition that pulls the email back toward individual relevance.
- 10% Back in Rewards loyalty callout is visible and unburied.

## What's weak

- "ANOTHER SALE" banner at the top immediately undercuts the editorial confidence — it's vague (no %/$) and contradicts the color-story premise.
- Free Design Services module reappears for the **third time in four days** (July 14, July 16, July 17) — subscriber fatigue on this module is already severe.
- Both hero CTAs ("SHOP NEW ARRIVALS" and "EXPLORE THE LOOKBOOK") are category-level; there is no single anchor product with a price to give the click real purpose.
- App download + Snapchat modules below the fold are brand-diluting noise on what should be a clean editorial scroll.
- The "ANOTHER SALE" label with no offer attached reads as mildly deceptive — the worst of both worlds (neither editorial nor discount).

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill the "ANOTHER SALE" bar.** This is an editorial color send. Own it fully — drop the vague sale signal or replace it with a real one (e.g., "Up to 40% off indigo favorites"). Mixing editorial and discount without committing to either is weaker than either alone.
- 2. **Add one anchor product with a price.** A single hero indigo piece ("The Gabrielle Throw Pillow, $49") gives the CTA weight and makes the click a decision, not just browsing.
- 3. **Rest Free Design Services for at least 5 sends.** This module has now appeared in 3 of the last 4 Pottery Barn emails audited. Its effectiveness is near zero at this cadence.
- 4. **Cut the App and Snapchat modules.** They compete with the editorial voice and dilute scroll momentum past the hero.
- 5. **Give the Lookbook CTA a preview.** "EXPLORE THE LOOKBOOK" is a dead-end if the reader doesn't know what's inside — add one line ("12 room looks in the season's defining shade") to justify the second CTA.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Indigo new arrivals — the color of right now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Something new in indigo`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `From throw pillows to dinnerware — shop the full indigo look`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New arrivals in the season's most-wanted shade`

**`<title>` tag is empty**

**PHP header directives inside `<style>` block**

The following fragment appears inside a `<style>` tag and is inert HTML, but indicates a template preprocessing error — these are PHP `header()` calls that were never executed server-side and leaked into the HTML output:

```html
header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
header("Cache-Control: post-check=0, pre-check=0", false);
header("Pragma: no-cache");
```

This is a template rendering bug. The directives do nothing in a `<style>` block but expose internal implementation detail.

### 5. Compliance

**List-Unsubscribe header — not found**

The AgentMail relay did not capture a `List-Unsubscribe` header. Without it, Gmail and Yahoo (per 2024 bulk sender requirements) cannot surface the one-click unsubscribe UI. If the header is present upstream but stripped by the relay, that needs relay configuration review; if it is genuinely absent, this is a deliverability and compliance risk.

**List-Unsubscribe-Post header — not found**

RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe (`List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`) is absent or not captured. Required by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders since February 2024.

**Authentication-Results — not found**

SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown. The relay did not surface an `Authentication-Results` header. Sending domain is `e.potterybarn.com` (Salesforce Marketing Cloud subdomain); alignment should be verified directly against DMARC records for `potterybarn.com`.

**CAN-SPAM physical address**

Not verifiable from truncated source — assumed present in footer (standard for Pottery Barn sends). Confirm unsubscribe link is in the rendered footer and resolves.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All destination links are routed through `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/` redirects.
