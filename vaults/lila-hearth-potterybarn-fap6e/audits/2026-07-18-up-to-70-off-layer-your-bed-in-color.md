# 💙 Up to 70% off + Layer your bed in color
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-18**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's "Layer of Color" bedding editorial — same summer sale scaffold they've been running all July, but the editorial hook here is genuinely visual: a modular color-swatch grid of duvet/sheet options in sage, olive, terracotta, and blush. The hero reads "Summer Sale: Up to 70% off" with "Layers of Color" as the editorial frame, and the body fans out into category-shop tiles (Shop Sage, Shop Green, Shop Terracotta, etc.) before pivoting to a "More Bedding Essentials" row (comforters, sheets, pillows, blankets) and then a personalized "Recommended Just For You" product grid.
- The color-story execution is the strongest thing here. After the July 14–16 cadence that was all percentages and no personality, showing me actual fabric colors in a browsable grid makes this feel like a magazine feature I'd clip, not a flyer I'd recycle. The editorial energy is real.
- The weakness is formula fatigue. This is at minimum the eighth compound-subject email in July ("70% off + editorial hook"), and the subject line — "💙 Up to 70% off + Layer your bed in color" — is structurally identical to three prior sends. A subscriber who has been paying attention to this brand's July output will recognize the formula immediately and discount the sense of freshness the creative is working hard to earn. The "Recommended Just For You" section near the bottom is a genuine differentiator — personalization in a sea of batch-and-blast — but it's buried below the fold.
- Clear judgement: a well-executed editorial-sale hybrid that would score higher if the subject line didn't telegraph formula-fatigue and if personalization surfaced earlier.

## What's working

- **Color-swatch navigation grid** — the Shop Sage / Shop Olive / Shop Terracotta / Shop Green / Shop Blush tile layout is genuinely useful; it turns browsing intent into a one-tap decision.
- **Hero photography is rich** — real room settings with styled beds in each color; not flat product shots.
- **Personalized product row** — "Recommended Just For You" with visible product thumbnails adds a retention hook that most of July's sends lacked.
- **"More Bedding Essentials" category bar** — comforters, sheets, pillows, blankets as scannable tiles gives the email utility beyond the color story.
- **No render bugs** — layout is clean, images load, text is legible throughout.

## What's weak

- **Subject line is formula-identical to prior sends** — "💙 Up to 70% off + Layer your bed in color" is structurally the same as the July 16 send ("Sale styles UP TO 70% OFF + Your guide to styling patterned rugs") and the July 18 morning send ("Up to 70% off ON NOW + Your first look at the NEW catalog"). A subscriber is parsing this as noise by now.
- **Personalization is buried** — "Recommended Just For You" appears well below the fold; if that's the differentiation, it should be closer to the top.
- **No specific price anchors in the body** — the hero promises 70% off but no individual product shows a was/now price; "up to" framing without a visible example feels vague.
- **CTA copy is generic** — every tile says "SHOP [COLOR]" or "SHOP NOW"; no verb variation, no urgency language, no deadline.
- **Cadence pressure** — this is the second Pottery Barn email arriving on July 18 alone; the July 18 morning send already led with "Up to 70% off ON NOW."

## Recommendations

- 1. **Rotate the subject line formula entirely** — lead with the color story, not the sale percentage. The creative is strong enough to carry a curiosity-first subject. The 70% signal can live in the preheader.
- **Subject Alt A:** `💙 Sage, olive, blush — your bed in color`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your bed wants a color story`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Summer Sale: up to 70% off bedding. Shop by color.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Find your palette — duvet sets, sheets & more up to 70% off`
- 2. **Surface one was/now price in the hero** — anchor the "up to 70% off" claim with a visible example ("Organic Linen Duvet Cover — was $289, now $89") to make the discount feel real rather than aspirational.
- 3. **Lift "Recommended Just For You" above the Bedding Essentials row** — personalization is the single strongest reason to open email over visiting the site; don't make the subscriber scroll past generic category tiles to find it.
- 4. **Add a deadline to at least one CTA** — "Shop Sale — Ends Sunday" on the primary hero button costs nothing and materially increases urgency without bait-and-switch.
- 5. **Consider suppressing this segment for 48 hours** — two "up to 70% off" sends in one day to the same list is the fastest way to train subscribers to ignore both.

### 1. Technical Summary

Two hard-broken links block live CTAs, a cross-brand West Elm asset has leaked into production, and PHP `header()` directives were accidentally injected into a `<style>` block — indicating a template composition failure. Accessibility posture is poor across the entire content section due to universal missing alt text.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**[FAIL] Credit card member-banner CTA returns 403**
- URL: `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/c/da01b407-b21c-4618-a71b-f83bef0dd682?brandCode=POTTERYBARNCB&marketingChannelCode=APPLYANDBUY&external_id=PB_Email_MemberBanner&cm_ite=PB_header_nonmember&...`
- This is the primary above-fold acquisition link (Apply & Buy / Key Rewards banner). The destination is unreachable; all clicks on this module produce an error page.

**[FAIL] Cross-brand West Elm spacer GIF returns 403**
- URL: `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`
- A layout spacer from a sibling Williams-Sonoma brand is referenced directly. It is HTTP-only, returns 403, and exposes internal template reuse practices. Most clients will silently suppress it, but the broken asset can affect layout integrity in strict rendering environments.

**22 tracking links not validated** — `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirect domain was skipped by the HTTP probe. These should be spot-checked manually in a pre-send QA pass.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[CRITICAL] PHP `header()` directives embedded in CSS block**

Inside the final `<style>` tag in `<head>`:
```css
header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
header("Cache-Control: post-check=0, pre-check=0", false);
header("Pragma: no-cache");
```
These are PHP cache-control directives that were inadvertently pasted into a CSS block, likely during template assembly from a server-side image-serving script fragment. Email clients will ignore them as invalid CSS, but this confirms a template composition bug in the build pipeline.

**[HIGH] 50+ images missing alt text**
Every content image — hero banners, all 19 bedding story slices (`slices_01.jpg` through `slices_19.jpg`), 6 product images, footer slices, and promotional banners — has no `alt` attribute. Recipients with images disabled (Apple Mail default, corporate Outlook) see a fully blank email.

**[HIGH] 4 images served over HTTP**
Clients with HTTPS-only image loading (Gmail, iOS Mail, many corporate proxies) will block these:
- `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
- `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` ← open-tracking pixel

The open pixel being HTTP means open tracking is unreliable — any client enforcing HTTPS will drop it silently.

**[LOW] Empty `<title>` tag** — Some clients render this in the tab/window bar. A descriptive title aids accessibility tooling.

**[LOW] `maximum-scale=1` in viewport meta** — Prevents user zoom on mobile, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).

### 5. Compliance

**[WARN] List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post headers not confirmed**
- Neither `List-Unsubscribe` nor `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was captured by the AgentMail relay.
- One-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058 `POST` method) has been required by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders since February 2024. Absence increases spam-folder placement risk.
- Note: headers may be present on the actual SMTP envelope but stripped by the relay — this should be confirmed against raw SMTP headers.

**[WARN] Authentication-Results not captured**
- SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status cannot be confirmed from the relay. Sending domain is `e.potterybarn.com`; authentication posture for this subdomain should be verified directly against DNS records and SMTP logs.

**CAN-SPAM structural requirements** — physical address and unsubscribe mechanism are standard components of PB footer templates and expected to be present in the truncated portion of the HTML. Cannot confirm from the provided source slice.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All destination URLs are wrapped in the `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirect layer, so UTM parameters cannot be directly inspected from the HTML source. The one resolvable destination that was not redirect-wrapped (the broken 403 credit card link at `clickcp.potterybarn.com`) contains `external_id=PB_Email_MemberBanner&cm_ite=PB_header_nonmember` — these appear to be internal Salesforce Marketing Cloud attribution parameters, not standard UTM params. Whether standard `utm_source/utm_medium/utm_campaign` are present downstream of the redirect layer is unverified.
