# Early Black Friday is ON! Save up to 70%
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent, conventional Pottery Barn sale email. The burgundy hero reads "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY / up to 70% off" with a white SHOP NOW button, and the eye lands on it first. Below it, a stack of category tiles (furniture, sofas, tabletop, bedding, gifts, bath, rugs) each carry their own "up to X% off" line, so the offer is easy to scan. A "Recommended just for you" grid, the Christmas Shop banner and a rewards-card pitch follow.
- The problem is cadence. This is the fourth near-identical Early Black Friday send in about two days, after "STARTS NOW 📣", "100s of NEW…", "Just in! NEW…" and "Our favorite EARLY BLACK FRIDAY DEALS ❤️". Nothing in the creative is new: same hero, same 70% headline, same tile stack. The email is also very long, and the back half (Design Crew sweepstakes, app, rewards card, Affirm) dilutes the sale message.
- Judgement: well executed but redundant. It works for someone who hasn't seen the earlier sends and does little for someone who has.

## What's working

- The hero is clear. "up to 70% off" in large serif on burgundy with a high-contrast SHOP NOW button.
- Each category tile pairs a lifestyle photo with its own percentage (Furniture 60%, Sofas 50%, Tabletop 70%, Bedding 70%, Gifts 50%), so the reader can pick a room quickly.
- "SHIPS FREE" badges on the pillows, tabletop and bedding tiles add a second incentive without clutter.
- Some product callouts show real was/now prices (the Toscana Dining Table, the Stewart Plaid Candle), which makes the discount concrete.
- It is seasonal and on-brand. Holiday stockings, plaid and the Christmas Shop banner fit mid-November.
- I saw no render bugs: no broken images, overlapping text or empty gaps.

## What's weak

- It's the fourth near-identical Early Black Friday send in two days. The subject and hero add no new reason to open.
- The "Discover savings for every room of the home" line is generic, and the second SHOP ALL SALE button repeats the hero.
- The back half runs long and shifts focus: the Design Crew gift-card sweepstakes (also sent on 11/12), the app download, the rewards card and Affirm all compete with the sale.
- The fine print is a dense wall of exclusions at the bottom, and the headline "up to 70%" is only reached in a few categories.
- No deadline is visible in the body, so the urgency in the subject ("is ON") has no date behind it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Give each send a distinct angle instead of re-running the same hero. For example, lead with one category's deepest discount (Bedding up to 70% off, ships free) or with a real deadline.
- 2. Add a visible end date and countdown in the hero ("Ends Sunday 11/16").
- 3. Cut the sweepstakes, app, rewards and Affirm blocks from sale sends, or collapse them into one slim row.
- 4. Make the hero CTA specific ("Shop 70% Off Deals") and link the tiles to the exact sale categories.
- 5. Add social proof such as star ratings or "best seller" tags on the recommended products.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Bedding, rugs & tabletop: up to 70% off, ships free`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Early Black Friday ends Sunday: up to 70% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping on bedding, pillows & tabletop. Deals end Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stockings, rugs & dining tables: see the deepest markdowns first.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit: Pottery Barn "Early Black Friday is ON! Save up to 70%"

### 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with a hidden preheader and fully sliced image content. It has widespread missing alt text, a few insecure (`http://`) tracking and banner images, one of which sends the recipient's email address in cleartext, and leaked server-side code inside a `<style>` block. Header-level compliance and authentication could not be verified (QA pass rate 57%, 0 errors, 3 warnings).

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **57 tracking links were not probed.** They are all `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…` redirects (for example the logo link), so destinations and UTM parameters can't be confirmed from this data.
- **Three images load over `http://`**, which clients may block or rewrite:
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=…` (open-tracking pixel)
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/…/cat/1` (Turn/Amobee ad-tech) is in addition to the ESP pixel `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/86aaebc9-…`.
- **Cross-brand dependency:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on a sister brand's domain. If that asset is moved, the spacer breaks.
- **Stale asset path:** `/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/` is a 2022 banner reused in a 2026 send. This is minor, but worth checking that it still resolves.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 50 images** (QA-confirmed). This covers every `slices_01–12.jpg` in `1114_SAT_AM2_EBF_Grid1`, `slices_01–18.jpg` in `Email-Footer`, and the product images:
  - `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`
  - `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-125-l.jpg`
  - `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg`
  - `classic-velvet-stockings-l.jpg`
  - `holiday-icons-crewel-stockings-2-l.jpg`
  - `standard-curtain-rod-wall-bracket-l.jpg`

  With images off, or in a screen reader, the sliced content has no text equivalent. Tracking pixels (`d.turn.com`, `svopn`, `clickcp`, `coreemail.html`) should carry `alt=""`, and the spacer `spacer_0.gif` should too.
- **Leaked server-side code in `<style>`:** the text `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store…"); header("Pragma: no-cache");` sits inside the second `<style>` block. It is invalid CSS. It may corrupt the rule that follows it in strict parsers, and it points to a template-assembly bug.
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in the head.
- **No `lang` attribute** on `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" …>`, which screen readers need to pick a language.
- **`maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta** blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Layout tables:** the preheader and content tables have `role="presentation"`. The outer wrapper tables and the spacer tables (`<td height="20">&nbsp;</td>`) in the visible source do not.
- **Dead code:** commented-out viewport metas and `.createBrk{display:none}` remain in the head.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved merge tokens** (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source.
- **The recipient's email address is embedded in URLs:**
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?…&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is cleartext HTTP, so the address is exposed in transit.
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is HTTPS but puts the address in a query string, so it can land in server and proxy logs.

### 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA flags these as possibly dropped by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed. It needs verification against the raw original message.
- **Authentication-Results is absent.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown for the same reason. The sender is `PotteryBarn@e.potterybarn.com`, and the links and images use `click.e.`, `image.e.`, `edm.` and `clickcp.` subdomains of `potterybarn.com`.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address:** the source is truncated, so neither could be checked. They are most likely in the footer slices.
- `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` and `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` are harmless.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM and campaign parameters can't be audited.** Destinations sit behind opaque `qs=` redirect tokens, and the 57 links were not resolved.
- Product images load from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/…`, so the email depends on live site assets for their availability.
- No landing-page mismatch can be confirmed from this data.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the content slices and product images, and `alt=""` to tracking pixels and spacers.
2. Switch the three `http://` assets to `https://`. Move the `svopn` pixel to HTTPS and stop putting the raw email address in the pixel and `coreemail.html` URLs; use an opaque ID or hash.
3. Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP text from the `<style>` block and fix whatever template step emits it.
4. Populate `<title>`, add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, remove `maximum-scale=1`, and add `role="presentation"` to the remaining layout tables.
5. Re-check the compliance items against the raw `.eml`: `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC in `Authentication-Results`.
6. Follow a sample of the `click.e.potterybarn.com` links to confirm landing URLs and UTM parameters.
7. Host the spacer on the sender's own domain and retire the 2022 banner path.
