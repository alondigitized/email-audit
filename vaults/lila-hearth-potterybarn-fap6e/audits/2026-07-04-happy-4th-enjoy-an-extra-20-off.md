# Happy 4th ❤️🤍💙 Enjoy an EXTRA 20% OFF
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-04**
## Executive summary

- The seventeenth-plus send in Pottery Barn's July 4th campaign lands on the actual holiday, and it earns exactly one new line of copy: an "Extra 20% off Open Box Outlet Deals" callout midway down the email. Everything else — the hero layout, the SHOP NOW button, the "Save on furniture, bedding & more" subtext, the product grid, the "Recommended Just for You" rail — is the same template subscribers have been staring at for two-plus weeks. Sending a holiday greeting on the holiday itself ("Happy 4th") is the right instinct; it's just stapled to a subject line that still leads with the same EXTRA 20% OFF hook that's appeared in every send since mid-June.
- The Open Box Outlet tier is the only real creative news. If Pottery Barn wanted July 4th to feel distinct from the sixteen emails before it, outlet-pricing on returned and display merchandise was the hook to lead with — not the same evergreen 20% offer recycled one more time. Instead it sits below the fold, underpowered. The July 3rd "Last weekend" and "Final weekend" sends at least tried urgency framing; this one retreats to the same generic boilerplate and wraps it in flag emojis.
- Net judgment: competent execution of a stale brief. The email renders cleanly, the offer is real, but by send seventeen "EXTRA 20% OFF" has lost any remaining power over this subscriber.

## What's working

- **Open Box Outlet Deals** is a genuinely new offer tier — outlet pricing on top of the 20% off is the most differentiated thing in the entire campaign, and it's appearing here for the first time.
- **Clean render**: no broken images, no overlapping text, no layout gaps anywhere in the scroll.
- **"Recommended Just for You" rail** below the fold adds relevant personalization and a secondary click surface.
- **Holiday hero greeting** ties the send to the actual day, giving it contextual relevance the mid-week sends couldn't claim.

## What's weak

- **Campaign fatigue is severe**: by any subscriber's count this is the 17th+ send in under three weeks — "EXTRA 20% OFF" in the subject line is wallpaper at this point.
- **Open Box Outlet is buried below the fold**: the most compelling and differentiated offer in the campaign is treated as a secondary module while the hero repeats copy seen in every prior send.
- **No explicit deadline visible in the body**: July 4th is the implicit cutoff, but nothing in the email says "today only" or "offer ends tonight" — urgency is left entirely implicit.
- **CTA copy is generic**: "SHOP NOW" was vague in send one; in send seventeen it's effectively invisible.
- **Subject doesn't earn the holiday**: "Happy 4th" is a greeting pasted in front of the same promotional boilerplate — nothing in the line promises anything holiday-specific that the prior sixteen sends didn't.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the Open Box Outlet** — make it the hero, not the footnote. "Extra 20% off already-discounted outlet furniture" is a genuine deal that justifies the email and has never been the lead in this campaign. Move it above the fold now.
- 2. **Add a body-copy hard deadline** — "Offer ends midnight July 4th" in the hero section. The subject implies today; the body should confirm it.
- 3. **Rewrite the hero CTA to match**: "Shop Outlet Deals" or "Shop Open Box" instead of "SHOP NOW."
- 4. **Drop the all-caps EXTRA in the subject** — it reads as spam noise in an inbox already saturated by this campaign.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Happy 4th: outlet furniture + extra 20% off, today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Open Box deals + extra 20% off — ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Outlet prices on sofas, lighting & more — offer ends at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Already-reduced pieces, now an extra 20% off. Today only.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Pottery Barn "Happy 4th ❤️🤍💙 Enjoy an EXTRA 20% OFF"

---

### 1. Technical Summary

One confirmed broken CTA link (403), PHP server code leaked into a `<style>` block, 4 HTTP (non-HTTPS) image sources, and 50+ images missing alt text. Compliance header capture is incomplete via the AgentMail relay, making SPF/DKIM and one-click unsubscribe status unverifiable.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**[BROKEN — HIGH]** The Apply & Buy credit card banner link returns HTTP 403:
```
https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/c/da01b407-b21c-4618-a71b-f83bef0dd682
  ?brandCode=POTTERYBARNCB&marketingChannelCode=APPLYANDBUY
  &external_id=PB_Email_MemberBanner&cm_ite=PB_header_nonmember
```
This is a revenue-bearing CTA (credit card acquisition). The 403 indicates either a misconfigured redirect rule or an expired campaign token in the `cp_tp` payload.

**[INFO]** 23 click-redirect links via `click.e.potterybarn.com` and `clickcp.potterybarn.com` were not probed. UTM append behavior is assumed server-side but unverified.

**[WARN]** Open-tracking pixel uses HTTP, not HTTPS:
```
http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app
```
Gmail, Apple Mail, and Outlook proxy images over HTTPS — the HTTP pixel may be blocked or silently proxied, corrupting open-rate data.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[HIGH — Template Bug]** PHP `header()` calls are embedded verbatim inside a `<style>` block:
```css
header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
header("Cache-Control: post-check=0, pre-check=0", false);
header("Pragma: no-cache");
```
This is server-side PHP code from an image-serving partial that was incorrectly included during template composition. Email clients parse this as invalid CSS and discard it, but it signals a broken template include path. The underlying partial should not be injected into `<style>`.

**[HIGH]** 4 image sources use `http://` — will be blocked or proxied by modern clients:
- `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` — also cross-domain (West Elm CDN in a PB email)
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
- `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/...` (open pixel, noted above)

**[MEDIUM]** 50+ images have no `alt` text, including all product grid slices (`slices_01.jpg` through `slices_14.jpg` across three modules), the urgency banner, the Open Box Deals banner, and all footer images. Subscribers with images off see a blank email.

**[LOW]** `<title></title>` is empty. Some clients (e.g., Samsung Mail) display the title tag as the email name.

**[LOW]** `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta tag prevents pinch-to-zoom on mobile — accessibility concern for low-vision users.

**[WARN — Asset Mismatch]** The urgency banner is a reused Memorial Day asset:
```
https://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2026/US/MemorialDayBanners/S26_MemorialDaySale_UrgencyTopBanner_FinalWeekend.jpg
```
Filename and path confirm this is not a July 4th creative. If the image renders "Final Weekend" Memorial Day copy, it directly contradicts the July 4th offer framing.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**[MEDIUM — PII Exposure]** The subscriber's email address is passed as a plain query parameter in two tracking pixels:
```
https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app
http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?...&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app
```
Email addresses in URLs are logged in server access logs, CDN logs, and any intermediate proxy — a privacy risk under GDPR/CCPA. Standard practice is to pass a hashed or opaque subscriber ID instead.

No broken merge tokens (e.g., `{{FIRST_NAME}}`, `[FNAME]`) visible in the truncated HTML — personalization appears either absent or resolved correctly.

---

### 5. Compliance

**[WARN]** `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers were not captured by the AgentMail relay. If these headers are absent at the MTA level (not just relay-invisible), Gmail and Yahoo will not show the one-click unsubscribe UI — a requirement for bulk senders under Gmail/Yahoo 2024 sender policy.

**[WARN]** `Authentication-Results` header absent via relay — SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status cannot be confirmed. Sending domain is `e.potterybarn.com`; DKIM signing domain alignment should be verified directly against MX records.

CAN-SPAM physical address and unsubscribe mechanism are expected in the footer (not visible in truncated HTML) — cannot confirm from this sample.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**[WARN]** Plain-text version is 73.7% URLs (11,840 of 16,071 characters). This is above spam-filter thresholds that treat URL-dense plain text as a signal of low content quality. The plain-text alternative should contain readable prose, not just link dumps.

**[WARN]** The preheader copy reads "Final weekend to shop summer's best deals" — this is Memorial Day language, inconsistent with the July 4th subject line ("Happy 4th — Enjoy an EXTRA 20% OFF"). If preheader is dynamically pulled from a campaign template field, the field was not updated for this send.

UTM parameters are not visible in the raw click URLs (they route through `click.e.potterybarn.com`). Server-side UTM injection cannot be confirmed from HTML alone.
