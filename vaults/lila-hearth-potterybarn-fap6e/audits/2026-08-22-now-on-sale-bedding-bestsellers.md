# Now on sale 🚨 Bedding bestsellers
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-22**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's "Bedding Bestsellers Now on Sale" send, and it's the most straightforward, well-composed email in the recent run. Unlike yesterday's "On sale AND shipping free" send (5.00/10) that buried the actual discount, this one leads clean: "Bedding Bestsellers / Now on Sale / Supersoft favorites you'll reach for again and again" over a serene neutral bedroom shot, with a clear "Shop Now" button and "Shop All Sale" link right below the hero. No dollar amount or percentage is shown anywhere, but at least the framing (bestsellers, now on sale) is unambiguous about what's happening.
- Below the hero it does the format Pottery Barn does well: three named products (Pick-Stitch Quilt, Cloud Quilt, Cloud Duvet) each with a clean full-width lifestyle shot, then a "More Deals You'll Love" category nav, then a "Recommended Just For You" grid of six cross-sell items with names and presumably prices (not fully legible at this resolution). It closes with the now-familiar Design Crew Sweepstakes banner and Halloween Shop teaser, both recycled from prior sends.
- It's competent, well-photographed, and legible, but it's also completely generic — there's no offer number, no urgency, no reason this needs opening today versus next week. Judgement: solid execution, weak marketing muscle. This lands squarely mid-pack, better than the vague sends but missing the concrete-offer teeth of the free-shipping pillow send (6.00/10, "Starting at $39.5...").

## What's working

- Clean, singular hero message — "Now on Sale" with one clear CTA ("Shop Now") that's visible without competing elements.
- Strong, consistent product photography — each quilt/duvet gets its own full-bleed lifestyle shot in a cohesive neutral palette.
- Logical scroll structure: hero → named bestsellers → category nav → personalized recs → account/app upsell.
- "Recommended Just For You" signals personalization, which builds on the "bestsellers" framing.
- No render bugs — images load, text is crisp, spacing is even throughout.

## What's weak

- Zero visible discount — no percentage, no dollar figure, no "up to X% off" anywhere in the hero or product callouts.
- Subject line's 🚨 emoji clashes with the calm, upscale product photography — it reads as clickbait for a brand that doesn't need it.
- The three "bestseller" product names (Pick-Stitch Quilt, Cloud Quilt, Cloud Duvet) have no visible price or sale price under them, so there's no way to gauge the deal at a glance.
- Recycled bottom modules (Design Crew Sweepstakes, Halloween Shop) are now appearing across multiple consecutive sends this week — this is the third time in five audited emails.
- "Now on Sale" is generic enough that a subscriber who saw yesterday's sale email may assume this is the same promo.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real number to the hero — "Bedding Bestsellers, Now 20% Off" (or whatever the actual figure is) converts this from a vague nudge into an actionable offer; this is the single biggest lift available.
- 2. Put sale prices under each of the three featured items (Pick-Stitch Quilt, Cloud Quilt, Cloud Duvet) — right now the reader can't tell if this is a $10 or $100 savings.
- 3. Drop or soften the 🚨 emoji — it doesn't match brand voice and adds false urgency to what's actually a calm, evergreen sale.
- 4. Rotate out the Design Crew Sweepstakes and Halloween Shop blocks — they've appeared in back-to-back sends and are starting to feel like filler rather than curated content.
- 5. Add a deadline ("Sale ends Sunday") somewhere near the hero to justify opening now versus later.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Favorite Quilts, Now on Sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Supersoft quilts and duvets, marked down for a limited time.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The bestselling bedding you keep coming back for — now discounted.`

# Technical Email Audit: Pottery Barn — "Now on sale 🚨 Bedding bestsellers"

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard ESP-generated (Salesforce/Marketing Cloud-style) HTML email with heavy table-based layout and MSO conditionals; core structure renders correctly, but the message fails multiple accessibility and header-level compliance checks.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 57 tracking/click-redirect links (`click.e.potterybarn.com`, `clickcp.potterybarn.com`, `d.turn.com`) were skipped by automated HTTP probing — link destinations are not independently verified in this pass.
- Multiple tracking pixels present: `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` (open tracking) and `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/...` (third-party pixel, likely retargeting/analytics).
- Open-tracking pixel `svopn.potterybarn.com` is served over **plain HTTP**, not HTTPS — flagged below under Rendering.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **44 images missing `alt` text**, including primary content images (`toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-1245-1-l.jpg`, `farmhouse-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`, `400-thread-count-organic-percale-sheet-set-l.jpg`, etc.) and structural banner/footer slice images. Screen readers will announce these as unlabeled or fall back to filenames.
- **3 image sources use `http://` instead of `https://`**, which mixed-content policies in some mail clients will block or flag:
  - `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/...` (open-tracking pixel, noted above)
- `<style>` block in `<head>` contains stray PHP-looking `header(...)` calls (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ...")`) sitting inside a CSS `<style>` tag — these are inert/dead code (invalid CSS, will be ignored by renderers) but indicate a templating artifact that shouldn't have shipped in production HTML.
- `<title></title>` is empty — no subject fallback for clients that display the HTML title.
- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` present — expected for transactional/marketing email, not a rendering defect.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- Recipient email is embedded in plaintext query strings across multiple URLs (e.g. `...coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`, `sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`) — merge token resolved correctly, no unresolved `%%...%%` or `{{...}}` tokens found in the visible source.
- No broken/unresolved personalization tags observed in the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** — required for one-click unsubscribe support in Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender rules; may be a relay-capture artifact rather than a true absence (QA notes AgentMail relay may not surface it).
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found** (RFC 8058) — one-click unsubscribe (POST) cannot be confirmed as supported.
- **`Authentication-Results` header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from available headers.
- Note: all three above are flagged WARN, not confirmed failures — the sending domain `e.potterybarn.com` is a dedicated subdomain typical of authenticated bulk senders, so this is most likely a header-capture limitation of the relay rather than a genuine compliance gap. Cannot confirm either way from this data.
- No footer/unsubscribe link content was visible in the truncated HTML provided, so body-level CAN-SPAM elements (physical address, unsubscribe link text) can't be assessed here.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Primary CTAs route through `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirect tracking (not directly inspectable — skipped by probe), so destination UTM parameters and landing-page alignment cannot be confirmed from available data.
- Customer-service link resolves to a live on-domain path: `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=...` — correctly on `potterybarn.com`, not a third-party domain.
- No issues found in domain consistency for the one directly-visible non-redirect link.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 44 flagged product/banner images (empty `alt=""` is acceptable for purely decorative slices, but product images should have descriptive text).
2. Migrate the remaining `http://` image/pixel sources (`edm.westelm.com`, `edm.potterybarn.com/thekey`, `edm.potterybarn.com/2022/...`, `svopn.potterybarn.com`) to HTTPS to avoid mixed-content blocking.
3. Remove the dead PHP-style `header(...)` statements from the `<style>` block in `<head>` — harmless today but indicates a leaked template fragment worth cleaning at the source template level.
4. Populate `<title>` with the subject line or brand name for clients that surface it.
5. Confirm via raw SMTP headers (outside this relay) that `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) are actually present and passing — this audit cannot confirm or deny compliance status, only that the relay didn't capture them.
