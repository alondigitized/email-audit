# Ships free 🎄 Festive pillows & more decor
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-07**
## Executive summary

- The hero is the strongest part. "Festive Favorites Ship Free" sits over a tartan-ribbon and tree photo, with eight category buttons (Stockings, Ornaments, Bedding, Table Linens, Pillows, Throws, Dinnerware, Candles) plus "All Free Ship." It tells you the offer and lets you pick a category in one glance. The subject promises "Festive pillows & more decor," and Pillows is only one of eight buttons.
- Below the hero the email loses focus. The Christmas Shop block, the "Recommended Just For You" grid, Free Design Services, the app, the rewards card and Affirm all compete for attention. Several of these repeat beats from the last few days. The Christmas Shop launched on 11/05, and Free Design Services was the whole email on 11/06. The "Ships free" angle also echoes 11/05's "Colorful pillows, shipping free."
- Overall it's a decent, well-built seasonal send with a clear free-shipping hook, but it's a catch-all. The "recommended" grid is generic: a curtain rod, a dining table and a rug sit next to stockings. None of the products shows a price, and the free-shipping terms are only in the fine print.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer in four words ("Festive Favorites Ship Free") and sits over a seasonal, on-brand image.
- The eight category buttons let the reader go straight to what they want, and pillows, bedding and throws are all there.
- The "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks" banner at the top adds a useful reassurance for holiday timing.
- The Christmas Shop block (red panel plus tree photo) is a strong, clean seasonal visual.
- The render is clean. I see no broken images, overlapping text or gaps.

## What's weak

- The free-shipping offer has no deadline and no visible conditions above the fold. The exclusions are only in the fine print at the bottom.
- The "Recommended Just For You" grid shows no prices, no sale badges and no CTA beyond the product captions. A curtain rod and a rug dilute the festive theme.
- Free Design Services repeats 11/06's entire send, and The Christmas Shop repeats 11/05's launch.
- The lower half stacks the app, the credit card and Affirm, which pushes the reader away from shopping.
- The subject leads with pillows, but the pillows button is just one tile among eight, and no pillow is featured.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero match the subject. Feature one hero pillow or a pillow trio with a "Shop Free-Shipping Pillows" button, and keep the category tiles beneath it.
- 2. Add a deadline to the free-shipping offer, for example "Free shipping through Sunday," and put the key condition in the hero.
- 3. Put prices and sale badges on the recommended products. Cut the curtain rod and rug so the grid stays festive.
- 4. Drop the Free Design Services block, since it ran yesterday. Cut the app, rewards and Affirm stack down to one line.
- 5. Space out Christmas Shop and free-shipping sends so the same shop isn't promoted two or three times in a week.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Ends Sunday: holiday decor ships free`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stockings, pillows, table linens and more. Shop the Christmas favorites before they sell out.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with opaque redirect links and about 48 images flagged for missing `alt`. The visible source has three code-level defects: stray PHP text in a `<style>` block, an empty `<title>`, and the recipient's address in plain-text query strings. Header checks (List-Unsubscribe, SPF/DKIM) could not be verified from this capture.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque redirects, not probed:** 52 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains. Example: `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7ImQi...`. Destinations and redirect health are unverified.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` loads over HTTP. It may be blocked or rewritten by clients, which would undercount opens.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` is a separate ad-network beacon with no alt text.
- **Additional pixel:** `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/1573065e-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...` is an open pixel with no alt text.
- **Mixed HTTP assets:** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` use HTTP. The same host serves HTTPS elsewhere in the email.
- **Cross-brand asset dependency:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is loaded from a West Elm host, which is a fragile cross-domain dependency.
- **Stale asset path:** the 2022 path (`/pb/2022/0311_...`) points to a stale asset reused in a 2025/2026 send.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on about 48 images:**
  - the hero slices `1110_MON_AM_FreeShipHolidayTTOP/slices_01–11.jpg`;
  - `H25_TOP_InStockFurniture.jpg`;
  - the product images (`holiday-icons-crewel-stockings-2-l.jpg`, `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`, `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg`, and others);
  - `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`;
  - `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`.
- **Image-only content:** with images blocked, these will render blank and the content will be inaccessible to screen readers. This is more serious for the sliced hero and footer images, which carry the message and navigation.
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in the head.
- **Code leakage in CSS:** the second `<style>` block contains literal PHP: `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store..."); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. It is invalid CSS that appears to be a template or server-side leftover. It may break parsing of any rules that follow it in that block in some clients.
- **Viewport constraints:** `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility concern. The `html` rule also sets `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`, which disables text resizing on iOS.
- **Responsive limits:** `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` use attribute selectors. The `.contenttable` mobile rule is commented out, and the tables have fixed `width="650"`, so mobile scaling depends on the `img` max-width rules.
- **Other markup:** `.showmobile` is set to a fixed `width:414px`. The hero tables use `role="presentation"` correctly.
- **Plain-text version:** more than 70% of the plain-text part is URLs (9,939 of 14,098 characters). That is a spam-filter and readability risk.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (such as `%%…%%` or `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source.
- **PII in URLs:** the recipient's email appears unencrypted in query strings: `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` and `...&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`. The `coreemail.html` request is loaded as an image, and the other is an HTTP pixel. Both leak the address through logs, proxies and referrers.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **List-Unsubscribe not found:** the QA flags this as possibly an AgentMail relay capture limitation, so it is unconfirmed.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found:** the same caveat applies. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results not captured:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `e.potterybarn.com` can't be verified from this data.
- **Body unsubscribe link and postal address:** both are likely in the footer, but the HTML was truncated, so they are unverified. The footer is built from image slices (`Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`), so the unsubscribe and address text may be images with no alt text.
- **Missing `alt` on footer slices:** if these slices hold the legal text, the unsubscribe and address wording is invisible when images are off.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters:** none are visible. Every link is wrapped in `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…`, with destination parameters encoded in the opaque `qs` token. UTM presence, landing-page alignment and destination status can't be verified from the source or the skipped probes.
- **Direct site hits:** product images load directly from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`, so they are hosted on the retail site and tied to its CDN. This is not a defect, but the URLs are generated per product (`dp/wcm/2026xx`) and could break if products rotate.
- **`<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">`:** this suppresses referrer data when links are opened in a web client, which may reduce attribution on the landing page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images (hero slices, product images, footer slices), and `alt=""` to spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Remove the PHP `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block.
3. Move the three HTTP assets (`Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, 2022 `slices_02.jpg`, the `svopn` pixel) to HTTPS.
4. Stop passing the plaintext email in query strings. Use a hashed or opaque subscriber ID.
5. Add a non-empty `<title>`. Drop `maximum-scale=1`, and use `-webkit-text-size-adjust:100%`.
6. Confirm the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and the SPF, DKIM and DMARC results from the raw message at the original mailbox. Re-run with the full, untruncated HTML to verify the unsubscribe link and postal address.
7. Resolve a sample of the redirect links to check UTM tagging and the landing pages.
8. Make the plain-text part less URL-heavy (more descriptive text per link).
