# Curated new looks for a heritage home
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn opens August with a straight "Fall Lookbook" showcase — three moodboard vignettes (Heritage Autumn, Indigo Blues, Rustic Lodge), each with a hero image and a single "Shop the Look" CTA, followed by a "Recommended Just For You" product grid. It's clean, well-photographed, and on-brand, but it's also the same playbook the brand has now run five-plus times in this stretch (08-02 heritage design, 08-03 dining room, 08-03 Halloween door, 08-04 new arrivals, 08-04 sale). There's no discount, no urgency, and no differentiator versus the 08-02 "heritage design" send that scored a 6 for being generic — this one is more tightly art-directed with three distinct vignettes instead of one loose collection, which lifts it, but the underlying formula is wearing thin.
- The "Recommended Just For You" grid is the most interesting element here — it's the first real personalization signal in this run of sends, even if it reads as templated cross-sell rather than true 1:1 targeting. Overall this is a solid mid-tier lifestyle send: better executed than the 08-02 generic drop, but without the single unifying concept that made 08-03's dining room and Halloween emails stand out at 8/10.

## What's working

- Three visually distinct vignettes (Heritage Autumn, Indigo Blues, Rustic Lodge) each get their own hero shot and CTA, avoiding the "one big moodboard" flatness of the 08-02 send.
- "Recommended Just For You" grid names actual SKUs (Farmhouse Platform Bed, Belgian Flax Linen Duvet Cover) with visible price context, giving shoppers something concrete to click.
- Clean render — no overlapping text, broken images, or layout breaks anywhere in the scroll.
- Consistent brand voice and photography quality throughout; the Rustic Lodge vignette in particular has strong seasonal styling (antlers, leather, wood).

## What's weak

- No offer anywhere in the primary content — no %/$ off, no free shipping call-out, nothing to create urgency to act now versus later.
- Subject line ("Curated new looks for a heritage home") is generic and interchangeable with 08-02's "New arrivals inspired by heritage design" — a repeat subject theme within days invites banner blindness.
- This is at least the fifth "new arrivals/lookbook" style send in six days per brand history — cadence is heavy and undifferentiated for anyone not actively furniture-shopping right now.
- Halloween Shop promo mid-email feels like a bolted-on cross-sell that breaks the "heritage fall home" narrative the rest of the email built.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a concrete hook to the subject/hero — even a soft one ("new this week" count, a specific room type, or a rewards-tie-in) would differentiate this from the near-identical 08-02 subject and give a reason to open now.
- 2. Cut or relocate the Halloween Shop block — it interrupts the fall/heritage narrative; move it to its own dedicated send (which the brand already proved works well on 08-03 at 8/10).
- 3. Lead the "Recommended Just For You" section higher, right after the hero — it's the only genuinely personalized element and currently sits below three vignettes most people won't scroll to.
- 4. Test a light urgency or loyalty hook in the subject/hero since there's a "10% Back in Rewards" program already buried in the footer — surface it higher to give a reason to click beyond aesthetics.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the leaked PHP `header()` statements from the `<style>` block in `<head>` — indicates a template render step that isn't being executed correctly.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to all product and banner images, particularly the ~15 product-detail images in the lookbook grid.

4. Move the recipient-email open-tracking pixel (`svopn.potterybarn.com`) to HTTPS to avoid transmitting the address in cleartext.
5. Confirm List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post headers are actually being sent at the MTA level (may be a relay-capture gap rather than a true omission) and verify SPF/DKIM via a raw header capture outside the AgentMail relay.
6. Sample-resolve a few `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirects to confirm they 302 to matching, live `potterybarn.com` product pages and carry UTM parameters for attribution.
