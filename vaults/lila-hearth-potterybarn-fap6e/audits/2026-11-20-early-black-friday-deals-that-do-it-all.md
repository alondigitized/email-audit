# EARLY BLACK FRIDAY DEALS that do it all ✔️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- This is the same burgundy "Early Black Friday, up to 70% off" template Pottery Barn has sent since 11/16. Recent examples are "Early Black Friday is UP TO 70% OFF" and "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY SAVINGS for every room". The render is clean and the hierarchy is clear. A large "up to 70% off" sits over a Shop Now button, then category tiles with their own percentages, then a Recommended Just for You grid. Nothing is broken. The screenshot is tall and downscaled, so small text like the product price captions was hard to read.
- Nothing here is new. A subscriber who has seen about nine near-identical sends in five days will skim past this one. "Deals that do it all ✔️" says nothing about what the deals are, and the all-caps start plus a checkmark emoji reads as filler.
- Judgement: well built, but it is fatigue-bait. Its only real selling point is the stacked category discounts, and the subject line doesn't mention them.

## What's working

- The hero is strong. The oversized "up to 70% off" on burgundy under the Pottery Barn wordmark is the first thing the eye lands on.
- Each category tile carries its own discount (60%, 50%, 70% off). That makes the offer feel specific.
- The lifestyle photography is warm and seasonal (buffet with holiday styling, a quilted bed), and it fits the brand.
- Two "Shop All Sale" buttons plus a 2x3 grid of "More Early Black Friday Deals" give plenty of ways to click.
- "Ships free" flags on the bedding, tabletop and pillows tiles remove a purchase worry.
- The "Earn 10% back in Rewards" and Affirm blocks add price-softening options near the bottom.

## What's weak

- The subject is vague. "Deals that do it all" gives no discount, category or deadline.
- The email is very long, with about five stacked modules after the first Shop All Sale button. The design crews, app, credit card, Affirm and legal blocks dilute the offer.
- There is no deadline. "Early" implies urgency but never says when it ends, which is weak given the daily cadence.
- Every discount is "up to", and the fine print at the bottom is dense and tiny.
- The hero CTA is a generic "Shop Now", and the tile links read as generic category names.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the all-caps subject with a specific hook that names the offer and a deadline. Cut the emoji.
- 2. Reduce frequency, or make each send visibly different. Lead with a single category per send instead of the full recap.
- 3. Add a real countdown or end date to the hero, such as "Ends Sunday".
- 4. Make CTAs specific, for example "Shop sofas up to 50% off" instead of "Shop Now".
- 5. Move the Rewards, Affirm and design-sweepstakes blocks below the product grid, or cut one of them.
- 6. Put one "best price" product with a was/now price above the first fold.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Early Black Friday: ends Sunday, up to 70% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping on bedding, pillows & tabletop. See this week's biggest markdowns.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sofas up to 50% off, rugs up to 20% off. Prices drop again Sunday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Image-slice email from Pottery Barn (SFMC-style `click.e.potterybarn.com` tracking). About 60 images have no alt text, the viewport blocks zoom, and the recipient's address is sent over plaintext HTTP in a tracking pixel. Header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be verified because AgentMail didn't capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracking links not verified.** 62 links on tracking/redirect domains were skipped by the HTTP probe, for example `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...`. Destination validity and redirect behavior are unconfirmed.
- **HTTP tracking pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is non-HTTPS. It is likely to be blocked or proxied, which undercounts opens. It also exposes the recipient's email address in cleartext.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` is an ad-tech (Turn) beacon with no alt text.
- **Other pixels:** `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/bf9a21da-...?mi_cid=...` and `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` are also tracking beacons. The second one carries the plaintext email in its query string.
- **Cross-brand asset host:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a Pottery Barn email loading a spacer from a sister-brand domain. This is a minor dependency risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 60 images:**
  - the full-width grid `.../1120_FRI_AM_EBF_FullWidthsGrid/slices_01–17.jpg`;
  - the footer `.../Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`;
  - the product images (`faux-fur-ruched-throw-l.jpg`, `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-125-l.jpg`, and others);
  - the banners `H25_Bottom_InStock_V2.jpg` and `H26_Bottom_DesignCrewSweeps_01/02.jpg`.

  With images blocked, the email is effectively blank. Screen readers will announce filenames.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Likely mobile overflow:** the header image is `width="650"` with fixed pixel tables (`width="650"`). The only fluid-image rule, `img {max-width:100% !important}`, sits inside `@media (min-width:650px)`. The `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` rules at ≤480px target classes not seen in the visible markup, which uses `class="contenttable"`. Below 650px, images may not scale. The HTML is truncated, so this isn't fully confirmed.
- **Stray PHP in CSS:** the second `<style>` block contains `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store...` as literal text. It's invalid CSS from a templating leak. It appears at the end of the block, so it probably doesn't break the rules before it.
- **Mixed content:** these images use `http://` (flagged by QA):
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/...`
- **Web font:** the Google Fonts `<link>` for Montserrat is ignored by many clients (Gmail and others). It's harmless but adds a third-party request.
- **Plain-text part:** 12,236 of 17,271 characters (71%) are raw tracking URLs. This is poor for a text-only reader and slightly negative for spam scoring.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) were seen in the provided source.
- The recipient email is correctly resolved in the pixel and `coreemail.html` URLs, as noted above. Putting PII in query strings, especially over HTTP, is the only issue here.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe:** not found. QA notes the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found, with the same caveat. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM/DMARC):** not captured, so status is unknown.
- **Unsubscribe link and physical address:** these are not visible in the truncated HTML, so they can't be confirmed. The footer is image slices (`Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`) without alt text. If the legal text and unsubscribe link are baked into images, that would be a CAN-SPAM and accessibility concern. This needs checking against the full source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **No UTM parameters visible.** All links go through `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` with an opaque encoded token. Campaign parameters and final landing URLs are not verifiable because the probe skipped the redirects.
- **Product image URLs** point to `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`. These are static assets, not landing pages.
- **Preheader/subject:** the hidden preheader "Up to 70% off room updates, holiday gifts & more" renders correctly, followed by the zero-width padding block.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images and set `alt=""` on spacers and pixels (`spacer_0.gif`, `d.turn.com`, `clickcp`, `svopn`).
2. Move the `svopn.potterybarn.com` pixel and the two `edm.potterybarn.com` images to HTTPS. Stop passing the plaintext email in the pixel and `coreemail.html` query strings.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Add a non-empty `<title>`.
4. Add `max-width:100%; height:auto` inline or at all breakpoints for images, and make the 650px tables fluid below 650px. Remove the stray `header(...)` text from the `<style>` block.
5. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the auth headers against the raw message at the sending ESP, since AgentMail dropped them. Also confirm a text or alt-bearing unsubscribe link and postal address exist in the footer.
6. Resolve the redirect chains with a tracked-link follower to confirm UTM parameters and landing pages.
7. Trim the plain-text alternative so URLs make up less of the content.
