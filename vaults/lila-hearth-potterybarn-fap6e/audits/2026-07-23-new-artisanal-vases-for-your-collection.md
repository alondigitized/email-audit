# NEW artisanal vases for your collection
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-23**
## Executive summary

- This is a tightly scoped new-arrivals email for Pottery Barn's artisanal vase category — narrow brief, cleaner than most of the July sends. The hero opens on warm, cream-toned vases in a lived-in lifestyle setting and flows directly into a named-product grid: six distinct styles, each with its own image, descriptive name, and link. That product specificity is meaningfully stronger than the brand's recent category-dump format — compare this to the coffee tables send earlier today (same date, 5/10), which showed products without naming them individually.
- The weakness is friction to action: no pricing anywhere in the vase section, no discount, no urgency signal. The email asks me to browse, not buy. For a "NEW" launch send that's defensible — you're building desire, not clearing stock — but the sale module injected below the vase grid (a dark, colorful cross-category banner that appears to advertise a different product line at a discount) creates tone whiplash. New arrivals full-price above, aggressive discount below: the brand is talking to two different customers in the same send.
- On balance this is a well-formed editorial email that earns genuine interest in the vase section. The dilution from below-fold furniture modules and the mismatched sale injection pull it back from being a truly focused send.

## What's working

- **Named product grid**: Six individual vases with descriptive names gives this email specificity that the brand's category-only sends routinely lack.
- **Visual hierarchy**: Hero lifestyle shot → product grid → cross-sell modules. The eye moves naturally down the page.
- **Consistent aesthetic**: Cream, natural, earthy tones from hero to product tile — not a kitchen-sink shot.
- **Clean render**: No broken images, no overlapping text, layout holds correctly.
- **Subject length**: 39 characters — well within mobile display limits and doesn't get clipped.

## What's weak

- **No pricing in the vase section**: New arrivals with zero price anchoring is a tougher click to justify; even "starting at $X" would help.
- **Generic CTAs throughout**: Every product link says "SHOP NOW" — no differentiation between the six distinct pieces shown.
- **Cross-category sale module mid-email**: The darker promotional banner below the vase grid creates a tonal mismatch — it interrupts the editorial flow and reframes the send as a sale email it isn't.
- **Below-fold furniture modules dilute the vase story**: Sofa recommendations and new arrivals sectionals tell me the email doesn't trust the vase category to carry the send on its own.
- **No urgency signal in body**: "NEW" in the subject gets no reinforcement inside — nothing about how long the launch window is open.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add price anchoring in the hero or product grid**: "Starting at $XX" or individual prices on product tiles. This single change is the highest-leverage lift — it converts passive browsing into a buying consideration.
- 2. **Make CTAs product-specific**: Replace generic "SHOP NOW" with the product name as the link ("Shop The Woven Vase"). Specificity pulls more individual clicks and surfaces which styles actually resonate.
- 3. **Move the cross-category sale module to the footer**: If it must stay, it belongs after the vase content completes — not mid-email where it resets the reader's intent.
- 4. **Cut or collapse the personalized furniture recommendations**: The sofa module below the fold adds scroll length without serving the vase shopper.

## Technical Audit — Pottery Barn "NEW artisanal vases" (2026-07-23)

### 1. Technical Summary
Two hard-broken links (403) will cause click failures for the credit card banner and a cross-brand tracker. Pervasive missing alt text across ~40 images and three HTTP (non-HTTPS) image sources compound rendering and deliverability risk. Authentication header data was not captured by relay, leaving SPF/DKIM status unconfirmed.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**BROKEN — 403 on credit card apply CTA:**
```
https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/c/da01b407-b21c-4618-a71b-f83bef0dd682?brandCode=POTTERYBARNCB&marketingChannelCode=APPLYANDBUY...
```
This is the Key Rewards / credit card apply-and-buy link in the header banner (`cm_ite=PB_header_nonmember`). Any recipient clicking it gets a 403. The URL contains a CP token (`cp_tp=v3.eJy...`) that appears malformed or expired at time of QA scan.

**BROKEN — 403 on West Elm cross-brand spacer/tracker:**
```
http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif
```
This is a 1×1 tracking pixel sourced from a West Elm domain over HTTP. It returned 403, meaning the pixel will fail to fire and open tracking via this mechanism is broken. It is also a mixed-content (HTTP) resource.

**25 tracking links skipped** — click.e.potterybarn.com redirects were not probed. No broken destination URLs can be confirmed from QA data, but the credit card CTA is confirmed broken independent of the click-tracking wrapper.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**HTTP image sources (will be blocked in many clients):**
| Image | src |
|---|---|
| spacer_0.gif | `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` |
| Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg | `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` |
| slices_02.jpg (footer) | `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` |
| Open-pixel | `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&...` |

Gmail, Outlook, and Apple Mail all block mixed-content HTTP image loads. `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` is a visible footer creative — subscribers with images enabled may still see a broken image placeholder.

**Missing alt text — 40 images flagged.** Critical functional images with no alt text include:
- `F26_TOP_SummerSalePartC_NoUrgency.jpg` — top hero banner
- `slices_01` through `slices_10` (vases product grid) — the primary content block
- `F26_Bottom_NewArrivals.jpg` and `S26_Bottom_UpTo60OffOutdoor.jpg` — secondary CTAs
- All 18 footer slice images

Screen readers will receive no content description for any product image. When images are blocked (common in Outlook), there is no fallback text for any section.

**PHP header directives in CSS block:**
```html
<style>
  ...
  header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
  header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
  header("Cache-Control: post-check=0, pre-check=0", false);
  header("Pragma: no-cache");
</style>
```
PHP `header()` calls are inert inside a `<style>` block but indicate a template rendering failure — these lines should have executed server-side before HTML output. They will appear as junk text in some rendering environments and suggest the email was assembled incorrectly from a PHP template.

**`<title>` tag is empty** — minor, but some clients display this as a tab/window title.

### 5. Compliance

**List-Unsubscribe header not captured** — QA relay did not surface `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058). If absent from actual delivered headers, this violates Google/Yahoo bulk sender requirements (enforced since 2024) and disables one-click unsubscribe in Gmail. Requires verification against raw headers from the ESP (not relay artifact).

**Authentication-Results not captured** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail is unknown. Sending domain is `e.potterybarn.com` (Salesforce Marketing Cloud). Should be confirmed at ESP level.

**CAN-SPAM physical address** — not verifiable from truncated HTML. Assumed present in footer based on standard PB template; should be confirmed in full source.

**Unsubscribe link** — present in footer based on standard template structure; not flagged as broken by QA.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

The open-tracking pixel embeds subscriber email (`lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`) directly as a query parameter in a `coreemail.html` image src — this is a site analytics / session stitching mechanism, not a compliance issue per se, but exposes PII in HTTP logs and referrer headers.

UTM parameters on destination URLs cannot be confirmed (tracking links were skipped during QA probe). The broken credit card CTA (`cm_ite=PB_header_nonmember`) means attribution for that click path is lost entirely.

Cross-brand asset reference (`edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`) indicates a shared template fragment that was not updated for this PB send — West Elm tracker should not appear in a Pottery Barn email.
