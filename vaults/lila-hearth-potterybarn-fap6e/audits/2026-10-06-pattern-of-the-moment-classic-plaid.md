# Pattern of the moment: Classic plaid
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- This is a mood-board email: a seasonal pattern story with no price, deadline, or reason to act today. The "Classic Plaid" hero is moody and well styled, and the plaid bedding, plates, and napkins are a coherent fall look. It reads as brand-building, similar to the 2026-10-04 "Heritage holiday quilts" send. It has none of that email's "from $199" anchor or "Quilts Ship Free" hook.
- The weakness is in the body and the cadence. Below the plaid grid, the email turns into a generic template: a "Recommended Just For You" block of curtain rods, ghost décor, a platform bed, and a rug. Then come the Christmas Shop, Design Crew, app, credit card, and Affirm modules. None of these relate to plaid, and each one pulls attention from the hero. This is also roughly the second or third Pottery Barn send in a day. It follows "The right furniture to organize your entryway" and the holiday catalog email on 2026-10-05, so it adds to a noisy inbox.
- Verdict: a pretty, on-brand, low-urgency send. It will get a few browsers, but it gives no one a reason to act now. It's a solid 5-6 that could be a 7 with a single offer.

## What's working

- The "Classic Plaid" hero is clear and atmospheric, with a readable serif headline and a "Shop New Arrivals" button right under it.
- The 2x3 photo grid shows plaid across bedding, a chair, plates, and napkins, so the theme is easy to grasp.
- The "Shop All New Arrivals" button after the grid is a clear second CTA.
- The Heritage Autumn module ("Get the Look") extends the seasonal story with a lifestyle image.
- No broken images or overlapping text are visible.

## What's weak

- There is no offer anywhere in the main story: no percentage, price, free shipping, or deadline.
- The "Recommended Just For You" products (curtain rod, ghost collection, platform bed, dining table, linen curtain, rug) have nothing to do with plaid. They dilute the theme.
- The bottom half is long and crowded: Christmas Shop, Design Crew, app download, rewards card, Affirm, social, and brand logos. The eye has no single place to land.
- The photo grid is hard to act on. It shows no product names, prices, or individual links I can tell apart, so the plaid story doesn't become specific items.
- The email follows another Pottery Barn send from the same day, so the cadence feels heavy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add one concrete hook to the hero, such as "Plaid bedding from $X," "Free shipping on plaid," or a member price. That alone would lift click intent the most.
- 2. Label the photo grid with product names and prices (for example, "Plaid Flannel Duvet, from $X") and link each tile to its own product page.
- 3. Replace the generic recommendations with plaid-adjacent items, such as flannel sheets, plaid throws, and matching pillows, so the email stays on theme.
- 4. Cut the footer promo stack from five modules to two, such as Design Crew and the rewards card. Move the rest below the fold or drop them.
- 5. Space this send away from the same-day Pottery Barn emails, or merge it with the Heritage Autumn story.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Cozy plaid is here: new for fall`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Flannel bedding, tabletop, and throws in classic heritage plaid. Shop the new arrivals.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plaid for every room, with free shipping on bedding.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based, XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with a 650px layout. It is image-heavy (about 50 images lack alt text) and loads three third-party or plain-HTTP tracking pixels. The header-level compliance and authentication warnings are likely relay artifacts rather than sender defects. The QA run scored 50% with 0 failures and 4 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque redirects:** 51 links go through click-redirect domains, e.g. `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7ImQi...`. The QA probe skipped them, so destination health and final landing URLs are unverified.
- **Plain-HTTP open pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is served over HTTP. It may be blocked and exposes the recipient address in transit.
- **Plain-HTTP images:** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` use HTTP. The second is a 2022 asset path reused in a 2026 send.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEv.../cat/1` is an ad-tech pixel with no alt text. It also adds an external dependency.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on the West Elm domain. A West Elm outage would only affect a spacer, but it is a brittle dependency.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 50 images have no alt text. They include the whole hero (`.../0911_FRI_AM_PlaidStoryHeritageAutumn/slices_01.jpg` to `slices_10.jpg`), the product images (`cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`, `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-1245-1-l.jpg`, etc.) and `Email-Footer/slices_01` to `slices_18`. With images off, the content and links are unreadable.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Stray code in CSS:** the `<style>` block contains PHP code (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store...")`). It is invalid CSS, can corrupt the rule that follows it in some parsers, and indicates a template leak.
- **Mobile CSS:**
  - Mobile selectors rely on attribute matching (`table[class="wrapper"]`, `img[class="photo"]`).
  - The `wrapper` and `photo` class names don't appear in the visible, truncated source, so those rules may be dead.
  - The `.showmobile` rule hard-codes `width:414px`.
- **Role attributes:** some spacer tables (`<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><tr><td height="20">&nbsp;`) lack `role="presentation"`, while others have it.
- **Plain-text part:** 9900 of 14025 characters (~71%) are URLs, which is above the 70% threshold the QA check flags.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved tokens:** none are visible in the truncated source.
- **PII in URLs:**
  - The recipient email appears unencoded in `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` and in the `svopn` pixel's `sv_sveme=` parameter.
  - Both are embedded as images, so the address could leak through proxy logs and referrers.
  - The `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` tag only partly mitigates this.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Missing headers:** `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found, and neither was `Authentication-Results`. QA notes these headers may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is therefore unknown, and the headers should be checked on raw delivery before treating them as sender defects.
- **Footer:** the unsubscribe link and physical address weren't visible in the truncated source, so they can't be confirmed. The footer is built from `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg` images, so any footer text may be image-only.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** none are visible. Click-throughs go via opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=` tokens, and the QA probe skipped all of them. UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be confirmed from this data.
- **Product images:** they come from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...` and match the campaign path `0911_FRI_AM_PlaidStoryHeritageAutumn`, which is consistent with the subject "Classic plaid".
- **Date mismatch:** the creative folder name is `0911` while the email arrived 2026-10-06. That could just be an internal naming convention, but it can't be verified here.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to all content images, starting with the hero slices, product images and footer slices.
2. Switch all `http://` assets and pixels to HTTPS (`Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `slices_02.jpg` from the 2022 campaign, and `svopn.potterybarn.com`).
3. Remove the stray PHP `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag and fill in `<title>`.
5. Stop putting the raw recipient address in the `coreemail.html` and `sv_sveme` query strings. Use a hashed or opaque ID instead.
6. Capture raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) on a direct, non-relay delivery to confirm authentication and one-click unsubscribe.
7. Re-run the audit against the full, untruncated HTML. Then verify the redirect destinations, the UTM parameters, and the unsubscribe link and address in the footer.
