# Extra 20% off is ON (but not for long) + Furniture in stock & on its way FAST
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the tenth-ish "Extra 20% off" send in about five days, and the subject spends its first half on that same line. The body is a competent furniture-and-delivery email, with the oxblood "Extra 20% off Select Styles / Plus savings up to 70% off" banner on top again. Under it, "Beautiful Designs, Delivered Fast" and a "DELIVERED IN 1-4 WEEKS" tag make the real angle in-stock furniture, which the subject buries behind the recycled promo.
- The layout is clean. A living-room hero is followed by clearly labeled category tiles (Lighting, Bedroom, Quick-Ship Seating, Dining, Office, Bedding) and a "Shop All In-Stock" button. The "Recommended Just For You" product grid and the Christmas Shop tease sit below. Nothing is broken, but the email is long and has no deadline, so "not for long" is only a claim.
- Judgement: well built and on-season, but it carries the fatigue of a promo that has run all week. The in-stock furniture story is the only fresh idea here, and the email leads with the tired one.

## What's working

- The "Beautiful Designs, Delivered Fast" headline with the "1-4 weeks" pill answers a real furniture objection (lead time).
- Category tiles are clearly labeled and visually rich, so the shopper can pick a room quickly.
- The "Quick-Ship Seating" and "Ships Free" tags add concrete reasons to click.
- The Christmas Shop block ties the email to the current season after the Halloween Shop send on 2026-10-26.
- Nothing is visibly broken: images load and the layout is intact.

## What's weak

- The banner is the same "Extra 20% off Select Styles / Plus savings up to 70% off" creative that has topped every send since about 10/23.
- "Not for long" has no date or time anywhere, so the urgency isn't credible.
- "Exclusions apply" sits right under the offer, and the fine print at the bottom is long and dense.
- The hero is a lifestyle image with a tiny caption, "In-Stock Living Room Furniture", and no product or price.
- Below the hero there are three CTA layers (Shop All Sale, Shop All In-Stock, Shop Now), which competes for the primary click.
- The email is very long for a single message: Recommended, Christmas Shop, Design Services, app, rewards and Affirm blocks all follow the furniture content.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the differentiator: put "In stock, ships in 1-4 weeks" in the hero and demote the recycled 20% banner to a slim strip.
- 2. Add a real deadline, such as "Ends Sunday at midnight", to the banner and subject.
- 3. Make the hero a specific hero product with a price or "from $X" and a verb CTA like "Shop quick-ship sofas".
- 4. Cut the footer stack (app, Affirm, rewards, Design Services) or collapse it to one line.
- 5. Vary the banner creative from send to send so the discount doesn't become wallpaper.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Furniture in stock now: ships in 1-4 weeks`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Extra 20% off ends Sunday + sofas that ship fast`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Quick-ship seating, dining and bedroom pieces, plus extra 20% off select styles.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Skip the wait: these in-stock favorites arrive in as little as 1-4 weeks.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Image-heavy sliced layout with a clean click-tracking setup, but about 45 images lack alt text, the recipient's email address is passed in plain-text image URLs (three images are cleartext `http://`), and the source has an empty `<title>` and stray PHP text inside a `<style>` block. The header-based compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because of the relay (see §5).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified destinations:** 54 links on `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` (opaque Salesforce-style tokens) were skipped by the HTTP probe, so redirect targets, status codes and dead links are unconfirmed.
- **Mixed-protocol tracking pixels:**
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` loads over plain HTTP.
  - `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` is a third-party ad-network pixel.
  - `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/286c600d-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...` is the open pixel.
  - Together that is three separate open or ad pixels.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a spacer served from a West Elm domain. This is a dependency on another brand's CDN. If it is removed, layout spacing breaks.
- **Odd image source:** `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=...` is used as an `<img>` source. It points to an HTML page, not an image, so it is probably a tracking beacon.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (about 45 images):**
  - The promo banner `H26_TOP_Extra20CLR.jpg` has no alt, and it likely carries the "Extra 20% off" offer.
  - `F26_InStock_B8_InStockLivingFurnitureHero/slices_01–12.jpg` all lack alt. The hero is split into 12 slices, so with images blocked the email is blank in the main body.
  - The product images lack alt: `toscana-extendable-dining-table…`, `banks-extendable-dining-table…`, `cayman-platform-bed…`, `belgian-flax-linen-curtain…`, `reeva-handwoven-rug…` and `standard-curtain-rod-wall-bracket…`.
  - `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg` and the bottom banners `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg` and `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg` also lack alt.
  - Only the logo (`alt="POTTERY BARN"`) has alt text.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Invalid CSS:** the second `<style>` block contains PHP text (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store…")`). Parsers will treat it as malformed rules, and some clients that strip or validate CSS may drop the whole block, including the `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` rules.
- **Non-HTTPS images:** `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` use `http://`, and the `svopn` pixel does too. These may be blocked or show mixed-content warnings.
- **Web font:** `fonts.googleapis.com` Montserrat is loaded via `<link>`. Gmail and Outlook ignore it, so rendering falls back to the stack.
- **Mobile CSS:** `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` are attribute selectors. They match only if the tables or images carry those exact classes, and the visible source shows `contenttable`, not `wrapper`. I can't confirm they match anything, because the HTML is truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source.
- **Email address in plain-text query strings:** `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is embedded in `coreemail.html?email=...` and in `sv_sveme=` on the `http://svopn…` pixel. The second one is sent unencrypted, which exposes the recipient address to network observers and to any proxy logs. Hashing the address or using an opaque ID would avoid this.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both are flagged missing. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed** and not proven to be a sender defect. Check the raw message headers before treating it as a failure.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This can't be assessed from the available data.
- **Physical address and unsubscribe link:** they would be in the footer, which falls in the truncated HTML, so I can't confirm either way.
- **Plain-text part:** 10,323 of 14,492 characters (71%) are URLs. It sits just above the 70% threshold, which is a minor spam-filter risk.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Links are wrapped in opaque `qs=` tokens, so UTM parameters and landing pages can't be verified. The probe skipped all 54 links.
- Direct `www.potterybarn.com` URLs appear only as image sources and have no campaign parameters.
- Continuity is unverified and not confirmed broken.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to the promo banner, the 12 hero slices, the product images and the footer slices. Use `alt=""` on spacers and tracking pixels. Consider live text for the offer.
2. Stop putting the raw recipient email in URLs. Use a hashed or opaque ID, and move the `svopn` pixel to HTTPS.
3. Move the three `http://` images to HTTPS.
4. Remove the PHP `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block.
5. Fill in `<title>` and remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
6. Host `spacer_0.gif` on the Pottery Barn CDN, not `edm.westelm.com`.
7. Re-run QA with the raw `.eml` headers to verify List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post and SPF, DKIM and DMARC. Resolve a sample of the 54 click-tracking redirects to check destinations and UTM carry-through.
8. Raise the plain-text part's text-to-URL ratio.
