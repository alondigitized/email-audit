# New arrivals to invite the season in
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-03**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, editorial new-arrivals send, and it is the first Pottery Barn email in this week's run without the oxblood "Extra 20% off Select Styles" banner. It opens on a fireplace hero with "NEW ARRIVALS", a "SHOP NOW" button, and a thin "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks" strip above it. Compared with the Nov 1–2 sends ("EXTRA 20% OFF select styles—going fast!", "Don't miss your chance for EXTRA 20% OFF"), the creative is calmer and more brand-led.
- The cost of dropping the promo is that there is no concrete offer. The body is a long scroll of tiles, and nothing in it says why to buy today. "SHIPS FREE" badges appear on only two tiles (pillows and throws, tabletop and bar), and the "Earn 10% Back in Rewards" block sits near the bottom. The photography is warm and seasonal, but the email works as a lookbook, not a conversion tool. The subject is on-brand but low in urgency.
- Verdict: good mood and a clear hierarchy, but weak on commercial intent. It will perform as a browse-and-save email, not a driver of purchases.

## What's working

- The fireplace hero with the "NEW ARRIVALS" serif headline sets a clear seasonal tone, and the "SHOP NOW" button is visible.
- "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks" above the hero gives a practical reason to buy now. It echoes the Nov 1 "Hosting soon? Quick-ship furniture" send, which scored best that week.
- The category tiles are tidy: Pillows & Throws, The Christmas Shop, Furniture, Lighting, Armchairs, Bedding, Decor, Tabletop & Bar. Each has a plain label, so skimming is easy.
- The "SHIPS FREE" badges on the pillows and tabletop tiles are small, specific incentives.
- The "Recommended Just for You" product grid adds named items (Banks Extendable Dining Table, Reeva Handwoven Rug) and a "Free Design Services" block with a "BOOK A FREE APPOINTMENT" button.

## What's weak

- There is no headline offer. No % off or dollar amount appears in the hero, so the eye lands on mood instead of value.
- The email is very long, with 8 category tiles, 6 recommended products and several footer promos. Several tiles, such as Furniture, Lighting and Armchairs, are image-plus-label only.
- The "Recommended Just for You" items show no prices, ratings or reviews, so a shopper has no reason to click through.
- Calls to action are spread across "Shop Now," "Shop All New Arrivals," "Book a Free Appointment," "Download Now" and "Learn More." The last two are generic.
- The dense legal and rewards fine print at the bottom is a wall of small text. The 10% back in rewards is only available on a new Pottery Barn credit card.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one concrete offer in the hero, such as free shipping on all new arrivals or a dated member perk. The email currently has none.
- 2. Cut the category grid to about four tiles and make each one name a hero product with a price.
- 3. Add star ratings or "Bestseller" tags to the recommended products.
- 4. Rewrite the CTAs to verb-and-noun copy, such as "Shop new pillows" and "Get the Banks table."
- 5. Move the credit-card rewards promo out of this send and shorten the legal block.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New for the season: free shipping on pillows & throws`
- **Subject Alt B:** `In stock now: furniture in 1-4 weeks`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cozy pillows, plaid throws and quick-ship furniture, all just landed.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Get holiday-ready fast: new arrivals ship free on select styles.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Pottery Barn, "New arrivals to invite the season in"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout built from sliced images, and it renders structurally. The main problems are widespread missing `alt` text (about 45 images), three `http://` image sources, and a recipient email address sent in cleartext in an `http://` tracking pixel URL. SPF/DKIM and List-Unsubscribe could not be verified because of the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 52 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. The logo link `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...` is an example. Destinations, redirect chains and 404s are unconfirmed.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is served over plain HTTP. It can be blocked, and it exposes the recipient address in cleartext.
- **Address in image URL:** `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is loaded as an `<img>`. The address ends up in server logs and referrers. This is a pixel-style request, not a real image.
- **Third-party pixels:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/bc97280c-...?mi_cid=...` add extra tracking dependencies.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a Pottery Barn email depending on a West Elm host, a minor fragility.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 45 images have no `alt`, per QA. This includes:
  - product images (`banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`, `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg`, `classic-velvet-stockings-l.jpg`, and others)
  - hero slices (`H26_NewArrivals_B6_NewArrivalsGridCottageChristmas/slices_01–11.jpg`)
  - `H25_TOP_InStockFurniture.jpg`
  - the footer slices (`Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`)
  - `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`
  
  With images blocked, the email will show little or no content, and screen readers get no usable text. Only the logo (`alt="POTTERY BARN"`) is labeled.
- **HTTP image sources:** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` and the `svopn` pixel may be blocked or flagged as mixed content.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns=...>` has no `lang`, so screen readers can't pick the correct language.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, an accessibility failure.
- **Missing table roles:** some layout tables lack `role="presentation"`, for example the spacer `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><tr><td height="20">` and the outer wrapper tables. Screen readers may announce them as data tables.
- **Stray code in CSS:** the second `<style>` block contains leaked PHP: `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ...")`. It is invalid CSS. It sits after the valid rules, so it is probably harmless, but it risks parse errors in strict clients and points to a template build problem.
- **Web font:** `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Montserrat">` is stripped by many clients (Gmail, Outlook). I couldn't verify fallback stacks in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (for example `%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) are visible in the supplied source. The recipient address is populated correctly in the pixel URLs. The source is truncated, so the body copy and footer were not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** the header was not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture it, so this is unconfirmed, not a confirmed sender defect.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found. One-click unsubscribe support is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **Authentication:** `Authentication-Results` was not present, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The from domain `e.potterybarn.com` aligns with the click domain `click.e.potterybarn.com`.
- **Footer:** the visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked because the HTML was truncated before the footer.
- **Plain text:** 9,998 of 14,151 characters (about 71%) are URLs, mostly tracking redirects. That is above the 70% threshold and slightly raises spam-filter risk.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The links use opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=` tokens, and the probe skipped them, so UTM parameters and landing-page destinations are unknown. The only visible `potterybarn.com` URLs are image assets and the `coreemail.html` pixel. No continuity issue was confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add descriptive `alt` text** to all product and hero images. Use `alt=""` for spacers and tracking pixels. The sliced hero and footer images need alt text that reconstructs the full message.
2. **Move all assets to HTTPS**: `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, the 2022 `slices_02.jpg`, and the `svopn` pixel.
3. **Stop putting the recipient email in image or pixel query strings.** Use an opaque token instead, especially over HTTP.
4. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta. Also add `lang="en"` and a meaningful `<title>`.
5. **Add `role="presentation"`** to every layout table, and delete the leaked `header(...)` PHP from the `<style>` block.
6. **Confirm the headers on the original message** (not the relay copy): `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass.
7. **Test the tracking-link destinations** with a redirect-following check and record the final UTM parameters.
8. **Add a visible footer unsubscribe link and postal address check** to the QA run, since the truncated source hid them.
