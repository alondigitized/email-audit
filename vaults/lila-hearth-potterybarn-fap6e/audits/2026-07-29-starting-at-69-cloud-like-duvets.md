# Starting at $69: Cloud-like duvets ☁️
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-29**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn running a tight, well-anchored hero ("Most-Loved Duvets," "Starting at $69," free shipping) that then sprawls into the same kitchen-sink tail every recent send has had — essentials grid, a cross-category "Recommended Just For You" block that includes a curtain rod and a platform bed, an Outdoor sale banner, an app pitch, and a credit-card/Affirm pitch. The top third is genuinely strong: four named duvets with prices and clear product photography, capped by a real "Shop All Bedding" CTA.
- Compared to the July 28 "Quilts to make any bed more beautiful" send (9/10, called out as the cleanest quilt-specific email in the recent stretch), this one covers nearly identical territory — cloud-like, bestseller bedding — just one day later. A subscriber who opened both is seeing back-to-back "our best bedding" pitches, which dulls the urgency of either.
- Above the fold this earns the open and probably the click on the hero duvet. Below the fold it's the same everything-email tail Pottery Barn keeps bolting on, which caps how good this can score.

## What's working

- The hero section is single-purpose and price-anchored: "STARTING AT $69" badge sits directly on the Sunset Toile Duvet image, no ambiguity about the offer.
- "DUVETS SHIP FREE" is a second concrete incentive stacked right under the hero headline — a real reason to click beyond price.
- Four duvets (Belgian Flax Linen, Nola Songbird, Lilah Suzani, Cloud) are shown full-bleed with named links, giving a real "pick your favorite" browsing path rather than one static hero.
- Clean render — no overlapping text, no broken image placeholders, no lorem ipsum anywhere in the visible email.
- "Earn 10% Back in Rewards" and Affirm financing are visible and legible, giving loyalty members and budget shoppers a reason beyond the sale price.

## What's weak

- The email runs long and drifts off-category fast: "Recommended Just For You" mixes a curtain rod, a platform bed, a bath towel, and a rug into what opened as a duvet-only pitch.
- No single item in the hero four-pack shows a price except the top hero duvet — Belgian Flax Linen, Nola Songbird, Lilah Suzani, and Cloud Duvet are all price-less links, forcing a click just to see cost.
- This lands one day after the July 28 quilt-specific email covering nearly the same "cloud-like bedding" territory — a subscriber gets bedding fatigue fast.
- "Up to 70% off" at the very top and "Up to 60% off Outdoor" further down are generic banner offers layered on top of the specific $69 duvet price, muddying which number is the actual hook.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere in the visible copy — nothing tells the reader why to act on the duvets today versus next week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after "Shop All Bedding" — everything from "Recommended Just For You" onward (curtain rods, platform beds, Outdoor sale, app pitch, credit card) belongs in a separate send. This alone would turn a diluted grab-bag into a focused 8-9/10 duvet email like the July 28 quilt send.
- 2. Put a price under each of the four featured duvets (Belgian Flax, Nola Songbird, Lilah Suzani, Cloud), matching the hero — the missing prices are the single biggest click-friction point in the body.
- 3. Add a real deadline ("Ends Sunday," "This week only") near the $69 anchor — right now there's a specific offer but zero urgency to act on it now.
- 4. Space this out from the July 28 quilt send in the calendar — two "our best bedding" emails 24-48 hours apart trains subscribers to skim rather than open.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Duvets from $69 — free shipping, ends soon`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your bed, upgraded: cloud-like duvets from $69`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `4 bestselling duvets, all ship free — through Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cloud-like comfort starting at $69, shipping's on us`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
Standard multi-table HTML email (XHTML 1.0 Transitional, VML/MSO conditionals) with ~40+ tracking/analytics pixels and a broken embedded code snippet; core issues are missing alt attributes, mixed HTTP/HTTPS image sources, and unverifiable compliance headers.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 57 tracking/redirect links (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`) were skipped by the automated HTTP probe — link destinations and final landing pages cannot be verified from source alone since the `qs` parameter is an opaque encoded token.
- Multiple third-party open-tracking pixels present: `svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=...&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` and `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/...` (Amobee/Turn data-broker pixel), plus a `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/{uuid}?mi_mid=...` open/click tracker.
- **PII exposed over unencrypted HTTP**: the `svopn.potterybarn.com` open-tracking pixel transmits the recipient's email address (`sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`) as a plaintext GET parameter over `http://`, not `https://` — this is interceptable in transit.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Broken/leaked server code in `<style>` block**: the CLIENT styling `<style>` tag contains literal PHP `header()` calls (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ...")`) as raw text inside CSS. This is invalid CSS and indicates a template that failed to execute/strip PHP source before the email was sent — evidence of a broken build step in the ESP template, not a rendering-client issue.
- 38+ images missing `alt` text (all `slices_*.jpg` divider images, product photography under `/pb/2026/US/...`, footer banners) — screen readers get no fallback content if images are blocked, which is the default in most mail clients.
- 3 images loaded over `http://` instead of `https://`: `spacer_0.gif` (edm.westelm.com), `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, and `slices_02.jpg` — non-HTTPS resources are commonly blocked by Gmail/Outlook image proxies, which would show broken spacers/footer graphics.
- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` are present and appropriate for transactional/marketing email — no issue.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{FirstName}}`, `%%TOKEN%%`) found in the truncated source.
- Email-address personalization resolved correctly in the customer-service link (`coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`) and in the tracking pixel noted above — token substitution is functioning, though see §2 for the plaintext-HTTP exposure of that same resolved value.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not found — RFC 2369 one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found — cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe compliance.
- `Authentication-Results` header not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status unknown.
- Note: QA flags all three as possibly stripped by the AgentMail relay rather than genuinely absent at origin — inconclusive from this data; recommend checking raw headers at the ESP/inbox level directly.
- Body footer (physical mailing address, visible unsubscribe link) was not present in the truncated HTML, so CAN-SPAM body-content requirements can't be verified from the supplied source.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- All primary CTAs route through the `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirect wrapper with opaque `qs=` tokens rather than human-readable UTM parameters, so UTM presence/consistency with landing pages cannot be verified without resolving the redirects (which the automated probe skipped).
- Static product images link directly to `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...` asset paths (these are `<img src>` references, not anchor hrefs) — not a continuity concern.

**7. Recommendations**
- Remove the leaked PHP `header()` snippet from the `<style>` block — indicates a template/build defect that should be fixed at the source template level.
- Add `alt` text to all content-bearing images (product/divider slices); tracking pixels can use `alt=""`.
- Migrate the 3 remaining `http://` image sources to `https://` to prevent client-side image blocking.
- Move the `sv_sveme` email parameter off plaintext HTTP, or drop the raw email address from the pixel URL entirely.
- Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Unsubscribe-Post`/`Authentication-Results` at the raw SMTP header level (outside the relay) to close out the compliance warnings definitively.
