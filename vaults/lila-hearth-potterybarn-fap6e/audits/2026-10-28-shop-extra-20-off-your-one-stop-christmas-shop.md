# 🧡 Shop EXTRA 20% OFF + Your one-stop Christmas shop
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-28**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent seasonal send. The Christmas creative is the best part: the "Deck the Halls" hero of needlepoint stockings, plaid bedding and vintage dinnerware reads as warm and on-season. But it sits under the same oxblood "Extra 20% off Select Styles / Plus savings up to 70% off" banner Pottery Barn has led with all week. That banner also led the Oct 28 "Take an EXTRA 20% OFF select styles" send earlier today and the Oct 27 "Don't forget your EXTRA 20% OFF…" send.
- The email works as a gift and decor browse. It doesn't work as an event. Nothing tells me why to act now, because there's no deadline and no countdown. A subscriber who has seen this banner for days will skim past it.
- Verdict: a good-looking catalog page on a stale promotional frame. It will earn some clicks from people already in Christmas-shopping mode, but it adds to the fatigue.

## What's working

- The "Deck the Halls" hero with the red-and-green stocking mantel sets the Christmas mood immediately and looks premium.
- The alternating image and text modules (Stockings, Bedding, Dinnerware, Pillows & Throws, Botanicals) are easy to scan. Each category has a single underlined link.
- "Ships Free" tags on the plaid stockings, the Velvet Scallop Plaid Quilt and the Nostalgic Wreath Pillow add a concrete benefit at the product level.
- The "Recommended Just For You" grid and the Design Crew "Book a Free Appointment" module give a second reason to click for non-Christmas shoppers.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The oxblood "Extra 20% off Select Styles" banner is the same one used all week, so it reads as noise.
- There is no deadline or end date anywhere in the render. "Extra 20% off" has no urgency, and the "select styles" and "exclusions apply" language weakens trust in the offer.
- The one-stop Christmas shop has no price anchor. None of the Christmas modules show a price, a percent off, or which items are in the 20% off.
- The email is long, with an app promo, a credit card offer, Affirm and a dense legal block. These dilute the Christmas message and push the primary CTA further from the finish.
- "Shop All Sale" and "The Christmas Shop" compete as primary CTAs.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the offer specific and time-bound. State an end date in the banner, for example "Ends Sunday", and name the Christmas categories it applies to.
- 2. Retire the identical banner for this send. Let the Christmas hero carry the offer with a short overlay such as "Extra 20% off Christmas decor."
- 3. Add prices or "from $X" to the product tiles, plus one best-seller or review cue.
- 4. Cut the secondary promos (app, rewards card, Affirm) or move them below the legal block, so the Christmas CTA is the final strong action.
- 5. Use one primary button, "Shop Christmas Decor", and drop the second shop button.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Christmas stockings, quilts & tableware — extra 20% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Christmas shop is open: extra 20% off ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plaid stockings, velvet quilts and nostalgic tableware. Many ship free.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Everything for a cozy Christmas, plus free design help when you need it.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with mostly sliced-image content. Automated QA found 0 errors and 4 warnings (50% pass rate). The confirmed defects are widespread missing alt text, three `http://` image sources (one carrying the recipient's email address), and stray server-side code in the `<style>` block. Authentication and unsubscribe headers could not be verified because the relay doesn't expose them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links can't be verified.** 54 tracking links on `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…` (opaque redirects, e.g. the logo link) were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destinations and status codes are unchecked.
- **Insecure tracking pixel.** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is loaded over plain HTTP. It may be blocked by clients, so opens may be undercounted.
- **Pixels depend on remote content.** Other pixels are `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/…/cat/1` (a third-party ad-network pixel) and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/36938ca0-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. Both depend on image loading.
- **Cross-brand asset.** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on a sister brand's domain. It works, but it adds an external dependency.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (about 48 images).** Examples:
  - `H26_TOP_Extra20CLR.jpg`, the lead promo banner
  - `slices_01`–`slices_13.jpg` in `/H26_B1_XCAT_ChristmasShopZigZag/`
  - `slices_01`–`slices_18.jpg` in `/Email-Footer/`
  - Product images such as `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg` and `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`
  - `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`

  With images off, or in a screen reader, the offer and the product content are not available. Only the logo (`nav0.jpg`, alt="POTTERY BARN") has alt text.
- **Tracking pixels without alt.** The turn.com, svopn and clickcp pixels also lack alt. This is low severity, but `alt=""` is the correct pattern for decorative pixels.
- **Non-HTTPS images.** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` may be blocked or trigger mixed-content handling.
- **Empty title.** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta disables pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility failure.
- **Stray code in CSS.** The last `<style>` block ends with leaked PHP: `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store…"); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. This is invalid CSS, and it comes after the real rules so it likely does no harm. It does show a template-build defect.
- **Outer layout tables.** The outer `<table width="100%">` wrappers and the spacer table (`<td height="20">&nbsp;</td>`) lack `role="presentation"`, though later content tables have it.
- **Plain-text part.** It is more than 70% URLs (10,386 of 14,583 chars), which is a spam-filter and readability risk.

- **List-Unsubscribe not found.** It is likely stripped by the AgentMail relay, so this is unverified rather than confirmed absent.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post not found.** The same caveat applies. RFC 8058 one-click support can't be confirmed, and Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain is `e.potterybarn.com`.
- **Footer not confirmed.** The visible footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated source, so I can't confirm them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters unverifiable.** All click-throughs use opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…` redirects, and the probe skipped them. Landing-page alignment with the "EXTRA 20% OFF" and Christmas shop offers is unconfirmed.
- **Direct product-image URLs.** These point to `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/…`, but their click targets were not resolved.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the promo banner and product images, and `alt=""` to pixels and spacers. For image-only slices, include the offer text (e.g. "Extra 20% off") in the alt.
2. Switch the three `http://` assets to HTTPS. Stop placing the recipient email in URL query strings, especially over HTTP.
3. Remove the leaked `header(...)` PHP from the `<style>` block, and fill in `<title>`.
4. Drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results against the raw message, not the relay copy. Also check the footer unsubscribe link and postal address in the full HTML.
6. Resolve a sample of the `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirects to confirm destinations and UTM/`mi_cid` tagging.
7. Shorten the plain-text part's URL load by using fewer tracked links, or add more descriptive text.
