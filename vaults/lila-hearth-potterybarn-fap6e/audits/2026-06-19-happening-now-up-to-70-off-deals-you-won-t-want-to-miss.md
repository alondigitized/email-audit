# Happening now! Up to 70% off + Deals you won’t want to miss!
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-19**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's third email on June 19 — following "Looking for FREE design advice?" and "It's ON! The Summer Sale is UP TO 70% OFF" — and the subject line makes that painfully obvious. "Happening now! Up to 70% off + Deals you won't want to miss!" is two FOMO phrases sandwiching the only real signal in the subject, stretching to 62 characters with no category hook and no deadline. The inbox sees this and says: I've already opened two Pottery Barn emails today.
- The body is a different story. The leather armchair hero is strong, the category grid — Furniture, Bedding, Rugs, Outdoor, Pillows & Decor — is clean and well-photographed, and the "Recommended Just For You" algorithmic product block meaningfully elevates click potential above a generic sale blast. The Free Design Service module tucked mid-email is the same one Pottery Barn ran as the anchor of the June 19 service push (scored 8/10); here it reads as a quiet upsell rather than a centerpiece, which is actually the right use.
- The verdict: a well-built email body undermined by poor send-day discipline and a subject written by committee. The brand's best June sends — "NEW finishes for pieces you love" (June 18, 8/10), the celebrity cabin feature (June 17, 7/10) — earned their scores by leading with a story. This one leads with FOMO and never pivots.

## What's working

- **Personalization block ("Recommended Just For You")** surfaces specific product imagery that lifts click-through well above a generic category grid — the strongest element in the email.
- **Clean visual hierarchy** — armchair hero → Summer Sale headline → SHOP THE SALE button → category rows — eye moves without friction.
- **No render bugs** — all product images load, layout is tight, no overlapping text or broken sections visible.
- **Free Design Service module** provides a second CTA pathway for higher-intent subscribers who want a consultation, not just a discount.
- **Affirm 0% financing** callout at the footer reduces purchase friction on big-ticket furniture.

## What's weak

- **Subject line is a two-filler sandwich**: "Happening now!" and "Deals you won't want to miss!" are pure padding — the 70%-off claim is the only real hook, buried in the middle at 62 characters.
- **Third Pottery Barn email on June 19**: subscribers who already opened the service push and the sale hammer have no reason to open a third nearly-identical send.
- **No hard deadline anywhere**: "Happening now" implies urgency without giving a cutoff — the body has no end-date banner, no countdown, nothing that says "act by X."
- **Category CTAs are fully generic**: "SHOP THE SALE" and "SHOP ALL SALE" appear multiple times with no product specificity, no price anchor, no hero item called out by name.
- **Social proof absent in the RJFY block**: algorithmically surfaced products appear without star ratings or review counts, leaving conversion on the table.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Send one email today, not three** — or differentiate hard by category. The cadence alone is killing open rates across the batch.
- 2. **Lead the subject with a category and a deadline** — the hero is a leather armchair; make the subject say so, and add an end date.
- 3. **Add a hard deadline in the hero banner** — even "Ends Sunday" or "48 hours left" would materially lift urgency without changing the offer.
- 4. **Anchor the hero product** — name the chair, show the original price struck through, show the sale price. One named product outperforms "up to 70% off everything."
- 5. **Add star ratings under the RJFY product tiles** — the section is the email's best feature; give it social proof to close the loop.
- **Subject Alt A:** `This leather chair is 70% off — sale ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Furniture. Bedding. Outdoor. Up to 70% off today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Named pieces, real prices, 48 hours to act.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop your shortlist before it sells out.`

## Technical Audit — Pottery Barn "Up to 70% Off" (2026-06-25)

### 1. Technical Summary

Two links return HTTP 403 at send time, including the primary credit card CTA, creating a broken conversion path for Key card holders. Multiple HTTP (non-HTTPS) image sources and pervasive missing alt text add compounding deliverability and accessibility risk.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**BROKEN — KeyCC apply/buy CTA (403)**
```
https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/c/da01b407-b21c-4618-a71b-f83bef0dd682
  ?brandCode=POTTERYBARNCB&marketingChannelCode=APPLYANDBUY
  &external_id=PB_Email_MemberBanner&cm_ite=PB_header_nonmember
```
This is the credit card acquisition link in the header banner. A 403 at probe time means the destination URL is either misconfigured on the click-processor side or the campaign token is invalid. Any subscriber clicking this gets a dead end.

**BROKEN — Cross-brand spacer GIF (403 + HTTP)**
```
http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif
```
A West Elm asset embedded in a Pottery Barn email. Both 403 and served over plain HTTP. Likely a stale evergreen footer module; should be replaced with a PB-hosted HTTPS equivalent or removed.

**25 tracking links skipped** — `click.e.potterybarn.com` and `clickcp.potterybarn.com` redirect domains were not probed. The 403s above came from the resolved destinations, not the redirectors themselves, so additional broken destinations may exist among the remaining 25.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**PHP header() calls leaked into `<style>` block**
```html
<style>
  ...
  header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
  header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
  header("Cache-Control: post-check=0, pre-check=0", false);
  header("Pragma: no-cache");
</style>
```
Server-side PHP code was injected literally into the CSS block rather than being executed. Email clients will treat this as malformed CSS and may throw parse errors; strict clients could reject the stylesheet entirely, breaking all inline style overrides.

**3 HTTP image sources** — will be blocked by Gmail, Apple Mail, and most modern clients when image proxying or HTTPS enforcement is active:
- `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` (also 403)
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
- `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` ← open-tracking pixel also HTTP

**~40 images missing `alt` text** — the full `slices_0N.jpg` grid (clearance section, footer), all product images, all footer logos, both tracking pixels, and the open-tracking beacon have no `alt` attribute. When images are blocked (default in Outlook, common in Gmail), the email renders as a blank page with no fallback text.

**`maximum-scale=1` in viewport meta** — `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom on mobile, which is an WCAG 1.4.4 failure.

**Empty `<title>` tag** — `<title></title>` sends no document title to screen readers and some email client tab previews.

**Stale campaign asset in live send**
```
https://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2025/US/W26_Bottom_GeneralDCBottomBanner/W26_Bottom_GeneralDCBottomBanner.jpg
```
Filename prefix `W26` indicates a Winter 2026 evergreen banner; it is embedded in a Summer sale email. If the image contains winter-season messaging it is a continuity error; if it is a generic DC banner being reused it should be renamed to avoid confusion.

### 5. Compliance

**List-Unsubscribe header absent** — not captured by the AgentMail relay. Gmail and Yahoo require this header for bulk senders (>5k/day); absence risks inbox demotion or filtering.

**List-Unsubscribe-Post absent (RFC 8058)** — one-click unsubscribe (`POST` method) is required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender policy since February 2024. Missing this header is a deliverability compliance gap.

**Authentication-Results absent** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be confirmed from relay headers. Sending domain is `e.potterybarn.com`; DMARC alignment should be verified directly at the MTA.

`<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` are present and correct for email (prevents archiving, strips referrer on click-through to protect subscriber privacy).

CAN-SPAM physical address and unsubscribe link presence cannot be confirmed from the truncated source — footer must be verified in full HTML.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

The 25 un-probed tracking links all route through `click.e.potterybarn.com` (ESP click-redirect). UTM parameter carrythrough into final destination URLs cannot be confirmed without resolving the redirects. The 403 on the KeyCC link means that particular conversion path delivers zero attribution data even if UTMs are present.

`cm_ite=PB_header_nonmember` is visible as a campaign parameter on the broken KeyCC URL — if the link is fixed, this value will populate the email campaign report; verify it matches the intended placement label in the analytics schema.
