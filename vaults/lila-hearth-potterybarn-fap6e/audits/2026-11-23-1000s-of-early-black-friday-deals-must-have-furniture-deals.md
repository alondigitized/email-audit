# 1000s of Early Black Friday deals + Must-have furniture deals
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the same burgundy "Early Black Friday, up to 70% off" Pottery Barn template that has run since 11/16. It's at least the ninth send in about a week, after this morning's "UP TO 70% OFF 🎁 'Tis the season to save" and 11/22's "Early Black Friday deals you'll love + Holiday bedding, pillows & more SHIP FREE". The hero is strong: a big 70% number and a single SHOP NOW button. The only new element is a "Must-Have Furniture Deals" tile.
- Below the hero the email turns into a long catalog: a 10-tile navy category grid, a "Recommended Just For You" block, a Christmas Shop banner, a Design Crew sweepstakes, and app, credit card and Affirm promos. Each section has its own CTA, so the offer gets diluted. Furniture is the subject's second promise, but it gets only one small tile and nothing specific, such as a price, a hero piece or a percentage.
- My verdict is a competent, familiar sale email that a subscriber will skim past. The cadence is the biggest problem. Nothing in this send explains why it exists, and the subject doesn't differentiate it from this morning's.

## What's working

- The hero is clear. "Early Black Friday up to 70% OFF" is large, high-contrast, and has one SHOP NOW button above the fold.
- The navy category grid (Outdoor, Sofas & Chairs, Bedding, Rugs and others) gives easy browse paths, and "Shop All Sale" is a clean catch-all.
- The furniture tile matches the subject, with lifestyle photos of a dining set, bed, armchair and dresser.
- The Christmas Shop "up to 50% off" block adds seasonal relevance. The stockings and tree imagery fit late November.
- The render is clean. I see no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The cadence is relentless: the same template as this morning's "'Tis the season to save" send and the same banner as every send since 11/16. Subscribers learn to ignore it.
- The email is too long, with roughly 15 CTAs competing. The sweepstakes, app download, credit card and Affirm blocks pull attention away from the sale.
- "Must-Have Furniture Deals" has no specifics. There is no discount depth, no hero price and no named product.
- The "Recommended Just For You" products show no prices, ratings or sale flags. Stockings and a throw aren't furniture, so the block doesn't follow the subject.
- The legal footer is dense and runs very long. The 70% claim also depends on fine print about exclusions, and a skeptical reader will notice it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Break the repeat pattern. Lead with the one thing that is new today, such as a furniture deadline or a delivery-by-Christmas cutoff, instead of re-running the identical banner.
- 2. Cut the lower half. Remove the sweepstakes, app, credit card and Affirm blocks, or move them to a single slim row, so the sale remains the only story.
- 3. Make the furniture tile concrete: one hero sofa or dining table with a "from $X" or "up to X% off" tag, and name the category in the CTA.
- 4. Replace "Recommended Just For You" with furniture products carrying prices and star ratings, so the content matches the subject and adds social proof.
- 5. Change the CTA copy from "Shop Now" to "Shop furniture deals".

- **Subject Alt B:** `Sofas, beds & dining tables: Early Black Friday prices`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus free shipping on holiday favorites. Sale ends at midnight PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our biggest furniture savings of the season are open now.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with Pottery Barn's own tracking links. The main confirmed problems are widespread missing `alt` text on images, 16 images served over plain `http://`, and the recipient's address passed in image URLs, one of them unencrypted. The automated pass rate is 50% (0 failures, 4 warnings).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracking links not verified:** 59 links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, for example `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...`. Destinations are opaque, so their validity is unconfirmed.
- **Mixed-content images:** 16 images use `http://`, which clients may block or flag:
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2026/US/W26_Sale_B13_FurnSale9Grid/slices_01.jpg` through `slices_13.jpg` (13 images)
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - the open pixel `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...`
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is loaded from a West Elm host. This is a shared-template dependency that could break if that path is retired.
- **Third-party tracker:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` is an ad-network pixel. It lacks `alt`, and its presence is worth noting for privacy review.
- **Several beacons:** the email carries `svopn.potterybarn.com`, `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/61c345ea-...` and `d.turn.com` pixels at once.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt:**
  - Nearly every content image has no `alt`. This includes the hero `H26_Top_EBF_NoUrgency.jpg`, all 13 `FurnSale9Grid/slices_*.jpg`, all 18 `Email-Footer/slices_*.jpg`, the product images (`classic-velvet-stockings-l.jpg`, `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-125-l.jpg` and others) and the `H26_Bottom_*` banners.
  - With images off, or for screen-reader users, the deal content is not available. Many images are sliced, so the links around them are unlabelled too.
- **Empty `<title>`:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Stray PHP in CSS:** the second `<style>` block contains leaked server-side code (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store..."); header("Pragma: no-cache");`). It is invalid CSS and may corrupt the rule that follows it in some parsers.
- **Plain-text part:** QA reports 11651 of 16627 characters (about 70%) are URLs. The plain-text alternative is mostly raw tracking links, which is a spam-score and readability risk.
- **Slice-heavy layout:** the `slices_*` image grids depend on images loading and add many requests.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Address in URLs:** the recipient address appears in query strings:
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
- **Plaintext exposure:** the second URL is unencrypted `http://`, so the address goes over the network in the clear.
- **Merge tokens:** no unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) were seen in the visible source, but the source is truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`:** neither header was found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. If they are really absent, the email does not support RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe, which Gmail and Yahoo require for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is the same relay limitation and cannot be confirmed from this data.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and postal address:** the truncated HTML does not show them, so they cannot be assessed.
- **Header meta tags:** `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` are present. The latter can hide the referrer from landing pages, which affects attribution.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** none are visible, because links are wrapped in `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` redirects. Attribution depends on the ESP's redirect layer and could not be verified.
- **`no-referrer` meta:** this may strip referrer data on click-through and reduce analytics visibility on the landing site.
- **Landing pages:** destinations were not checked, since all 59 tracked links were skipped. Whether the "Early Black Friday" and furniture deals match the landing pages is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the hero, product and banner images, and `alt=""` to spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Serve all assets over HTTPS. This covers the `FurnSale9Grid` slices, `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` and `svopn.potterybarn.com`.
3. Stop placing the email address in query strings. Use an opaque token, and never over `http://`.
4. Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP lines from the `<style>` block.
5. Fill in `<title>`, and remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
6. Rewrite the plain-text part with shorter or cleaner URLs and more descriptive text.
7. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, plus SPF, DKIM and DMARC, from the raw message rather than the relay output.
8. Resolve a sample of redirect links to check landing pages and UTM parameters.
9. Host `spacer_0.gif` on `edm.potterybarn.com` instead of `edm.westelm.com`.
