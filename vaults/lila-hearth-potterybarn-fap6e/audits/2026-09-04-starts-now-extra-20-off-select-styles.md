# STARTS NOW 📣 EXTRA 20% OFF SELECT STYLES
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-04**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's ninth burgundy-hero Labor Day send in nine days, and it swaps "up to 70% off" for a new headline discount — "Extra 20% off select styles" — while still stacking "up to 70% off" underneath as the secondary hook. It's a slightly different offer structure (an incremental 20% on top of the sale, rather than another straight percentage-off banner), but visually it's the same burgundy hero, same "starts now" ticker scroll at top, same category grid below. A subscriber who's seen 09-01 through 09-04's prior sends will register this as the same email with a new number on it.
- The category grid is clean and functional — furniture, bedding, sofas, pillows, outdoor, each with its own clear CTA — and the render is bug-free. But nine days into an identical creative template, novelty is gone and the "Extra 20%" framing does at least offer a genuinely new mechanic (stackable discount) that's worth surfacing more than it currently is.

## What's working

- Clean grid layout with distinct product photography per category (furniture, bedding, sofas, pillows, decor, outdoor) — no overlapping text or broken images.
- "Extra 20% off" is a genuinely new offer mechanic versus the prior week of flat "up to 70% off" sends, giving this one a small point of differentiation.
- Primary CTA "SHOP EXTRA 20% OFF" is bold, high-contrast, and impossible to miss in the hero.
- "Recommended Just for You" section signals personalization with distinct SKU-level product shots (curtain rods, platform bed, rug, dining table).
- Halloween shop teaser and rewards/Affirm blocks give scroll-depth engagement beyond the sale push.

## What's weak

- The burgundy hero template, "starts now" scrolling ticker, and category-grid layout are identical to at least five prior sends this week — visual fatigue is real at this cadence.
- "Extra 20% off select styles" is undercut immediately by "Plus new deals up to 70% off" in smaller text — two competing offers in the hero dilute which one the eye should act on.
- No indication of what "select styles" actually includes — the exclusion is baked into the headline claim with no visual cue (rug icon, category tag, etc.) pointing to eligible categories.
- "Exclusions apply. Prices as marked." fine print sits directly under the CTA button, undermining the urgency of "STARTS NOW" with an immediate discount caveat.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a single, unambiguous offer in the hero — either "Extra 20% off" or "Up to 70% off," not both stacked. Two percentages competing for attention weakens both.
- 2. Add a visual anchor (icon or small badge) showing which categories qualify for "select styles" so the eye doesn't have to guess before clicking.
- 3. Retire or meaningfully re-skin the burgundy hero template — a new background color or hero image would signal "this is different" after nine straight sends of the same look.
- 4. Move "Exclusions apply" fine print below the fold or shrink its visual prominence relative to the CTA.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 20% off is live — plus up to 70% off sale`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Stack an extra 20% off today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns just landed across furniture, bedding & decor`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your extra 20% off select styles ends soon`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard multi-client Pottery Barn ESP template (Cheetah Digital/Salesforce-style) with heavy MSO/Outlook conditional support; primary technical gaps are missing image alt text at scale and absent unsubscribe/auth headers at the relay layer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Header logo link routes through `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` (Cheetah Digital click-tracking redirect) — standard ESP wrapper, resolves correctly.
- 67 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped from HTTP probing (redirect domains such as `click.e.potterybarn.com`, `clickcp.potterybarn.com`) — not independently validated for destination/200 status in this pass.
- Open-tracking pixel uses **HTTP, not HTTPS**: `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` — plaintext request, may be blocked by mail clients enforcing HTTPS-only image loads, silently degrading open-rate data.
- A second HTTP-only asset: `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` (mixed-content risk / client image blocking).
- Footer image `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` also served over HTTP (`http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`).
- Recipient email address (`lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`) is exposed in cleartext in two tracking pixel URLs (open pixel and `coreemail.html?email=...` customer-service link) — not encrypted/hashed in the query string.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **~55 `<img>` tags missing `alt` attributes**, spanning hero banners, product slices, footer slice images, and tracking pixels (full list in QA findings). This affects screen-reader users and clients with images-off (common default in Outlook/Gmail), leaving blank space with no fallback text for promotional/product imagery.
- Head contains an invalid, non-functional block inside a `<style>` tag: `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ...");` — this is raw PHP code mistakenly left inside a CSS `<style>` block. It has no effect (parsed as invalid CSS and ignored by renderers) but indicates a templating/build artifact that should be removed.
- Legacy XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype with MSO/VML conditional comments (`xmlns:v`, `xmlns:o`, `<!--[if gte mso 9]>`) — expected for Outlook desktop compatibility, no issue.
- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` present — correct for transactional/marketing email (prevents indexing if web-viewed).
- Responsive breakpoints defined at 480px, 650px, and 700px via multiple overlapping media queries — functional but redundant (three breakpoint rules for essentially two layout states); not a rendering bug, just excess CSS.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g., `%%FIRSTNAME%%`, `{{...}}`, `[FNAME]`) found in the truncated source — personalization tokens appear to have resolved correctly.
- Recipient identifier is passed as a literal email address in multiple tracking URLs (see Section 2) rather than an opaque token — a personalization-token design choice worth flagging to the ESP team, not a rendering defect.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header: not found.** Per QA, this may be a relay-capture limitation (AgentMail) rather than a true absence — cannot confirm from HTML body alone, since one-click unsubscribe is a header-level mechanism, not HTML.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header (RFC 8058): not found** — one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed.
- **Authentication-Results header: not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified via the AgentMail relay in this test.
- Footer unsubscribe/customer-service link is present in-body (`coreemail.html?email=...`), satisfying CAN-SPAM's requirement for a visible opt-out mechanism in content, independent of header-level unsubscribe support.
- Sender domain `e.potterybarn.com` is a dedicated ESP subdomain (standard for bulk-sending domain separation) — no issue.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All promotional CTAs route through `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirect wrapper rather than direct UTM-tagged links to `potterybarn.com` — cannot confirm final landing-page UTM parameters or destination-page alignment since these 67 links were skipped from HTTP probing.
- Product images link directly to `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/.../*.jpg` CDN paths (image assets only, not page links) — consistent, no broken-domain concerns.
- No direct evidence of URL mismatches between email CTAs and landing pages in the truncated source; full validation requires resolving the redirect chain on the 67 skipped tracking links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all ~55 images, particularly product slice images and the `startsnow.gif` hero — highest-volume, easily automatable fix.
2. Serve the open-tracking pixel and both flagged banner images over HTTPS instead of HTTP to avoid client-side blocking and mixed-content warnings.
3. Remove the stray PHP `header(...)` lines embedded in the `<style>` block — dead code from a templating error.
4. Confirm with the ESP (or re-test via a relay that surfaces raw headers) whether `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` are actually present on the wire — current absence may be a test-harness limitation, not a real compliance gap.
5. Resolve the 67 skipped tracking-redirect links to confirm final landing-page URLs and UTM parameter consistency.
6. Consider hashing/tokenizing the recipient email address in tracking-pixel query strings instead of passing it in cleartext.
