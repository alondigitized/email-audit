# Homepage snapshot · www.potterybarn.com · 2026-06-06
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-06**
## Executive summary

- The first thing I see when I open Pottery Barn is a full-screen modal blocking the entire homepage — an email/SMS capture form with a sofa-and-pillows lifestyle photo and the headline "Find out first: New arrivals, exclusive savings & more." I can't actually *see* the homepage at all; it's completely obscured. Pottery Barn reads like a well-styled, upper-middle brand, but they've buried their first impression under a signup gate before I've had a single second to browse.

## What's working

- **Lifestyle hero image inside the modal** — white linen sofa, layered throw pillows, warm natural light, woven rug. That's my aesthetic. I instantly recognize it as "my" brand.
- **Clean modal design** — the Pottery Barn wordmark, simple serif font, restrained color palette. It looks elevated, not spammy.
- **"New arrivals, exclusive savings & more"** — the copy hierarchy is clear inside the modal; the promise of first-access feels relevant to how I shop (I'm the type who wants the catalog before it hits stores).

## What's weak

- **Modal fires immediately on load** — I haven't seen a single product, category, or deal. I'm being asked to commit before I've been seduced. For a brand that depends on aspiration, this is backwards.
- **"Exclusive savings" is vague** — no percentage, no dollar figure, no category. This is the oldest dark-pattern trick: promise a reward but withhold specifics until after you've captured the data.
- **Homepage is entirely hidden** — I have zero idea what's on promotion, what's new, or where to navigate. The hero, featured collections, seasonal campaign — all invisible.
- **Dense legal fine print inside the modal** — two full paragraphs of TCPA/carrier disclaimers immediately under the headline. On a small phone screen this is visual noise that undercuts the premium feel.
- **No loyalty callout** — I'm a Williams-Sonoma rewards member. There's no "Already a member? Sign in" or "Earn points" hook. They're treating me like a cold stranger.

## Recommendations

- **Delay the modal by at least 10 seconds or one scroll** — let me see the homepage first. An email gate on arrival is friction before value; show me one aspirational room scene, then ask.
- **Replace vague "exclusive savings" with a concrete offer** — e.g., "Get 15% off your first order" or "Free shipping on orders over $99 for members." Specifics convert; promises don't.
- **Add a loyalty/rewards sign-in shortcut to the modal** — "Already a Pottery Barn Key member? Sign in to unlock your savings" would instantly feel more personal for returning shoppers like me.
- **Cut or collapse the TCPA fine print** — move it behind a "See terms" toggle so the modal reads like a premium invitation, not a legal contract.
