# On sale AND shipping free
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-22**
## Executive summary

- This is another home-textiles sale send, but it's got a real problem: the hero doesn't say what the discount actually is. "On sale & shipping free" and "Save on Easy Updates" are both vague enough to be meaningless — there's no percentage, no dollar figure, no starting-at price anywhere in the hero. Compare this to the 2026-08-19 outdoor-furniture send ("UP TO 60% OFF") or even the 2026-08-20 pillow send ("Starting at $39.50") — both gave a concrete number up top. This one asks you to click "Shop Now" on faith.
- The category grid (Pillows, Throws, Quilts, Duvets, Table Linens, Sheets, Towels, Curtains) is clean and legible, and the "Recommended Just For You" module below it is a nice personalization touch with real product photography. But the email is long and loses focus fast — a sweepstakes module, a Halloween teaser, an app download pitch, and a credit card offer all get bolted on after the sale content, diluting whatever urgency the subject line implied.
- Net: this is a serviceable browse-the-catalog email, not a sale email that closes. Fine for a low-intent Saturday send, but it undersells itself given the subject line promises "on sale."

## What's working

- Clean, uncluttered hero photo (neutral living room, styled pillows/throw) that matches the "Easy Updates" home-textile theme.
- Category grid is well-organized and scannable — 8 clear category tiles plus a "Shop All Sale" catch-all.
- "Recommended Just For You" section shows real, varied SKUs (curtain rod, dining table, dresser, bed, sheet set) with visible product names — feels personalized, not generic.
- No render bugs — images loaded, text is legible, no overlapping elements.

## What's weak

- No concrete discount anywhere in the hero — "on sale & shipping free" never states a %, $, or starting price, so there's no reason to feel urgency.
- Subject line ("On sale AND shipping free") is essentially restated verbatim as the hero copy — no new information once opened.
- Email overstays its welcome: sweepstakes, Halloween shop, app download, and credit card pitch all compete with the sale for attention after the fold.
- No urgency/deadline signal (no "ends Sunday," no countdown) despite this being framed as a sale.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an actual number in the hero. "Up to 40% Off Home Textiles + Free Shipping" beats "on sale & shipping free" — this is the single biggest lift available here, since right now there's no discount depth signal anywhere above the fold.
- 2. Trim the back half. Move the sweepstakes, Halloween teaser, app, and credit card modules to a separate send or footer-only placement — they're diluting a sale email's one job.
- 3. Add a deadline chip near the hero CTA ("Ends Monday") to borrow urgency the way the 08-19 outdoor sale did.
- 4. Tighten the subject/hero redundancy — use the hero to reveal the specific offer the subject only teased.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 40% off pillows, throws & more — free ship`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your home refresh, on sale this week only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns on bedding, curtains & table linens — no minimum for free shipping.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Score fall-ready textiles before this sale wraps up.`

# Technical Email Audit — Pottery Barn "On sale AND shipping free"

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style HTML email template (`<!--V2-->`, MSO conditional comments) with heavy use of tracking redirects and open pixels; primary issues are missing image `alt` attributes (39 instances) and mixed-content (HTTP) image sources.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All navigable links route through `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` and `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/{uuid}?mi_mid=...` redirect wrappers — final destinations could not be verified; QA skipped HTTP probes on 56 tracking/redirect links by design.
- Open-tracking pixel `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` transmits the recipient's email address as a plaintext query parameter over unencrypted HTTP — PII exposed in transit and in server logs.
- A second open pixel, `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1`, is a third-party (Turn/Amobee) tracking beacon embedded as an `<img>`.
- `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` suppresses referrer data on outbound clicks, which limits click-source attribution.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **39 images missing `alt` text** across hero banners, product tiles (`toscana-extendable-dining-table...`, `sausalito-8-drawer-dresser...`, etc.), footer slices, and tracking pixels — confirmed by QA scan. Screen readers and images-blocked clients will render no fallback text for any of these.
- Malformed embedded code inside a `<style>` block in `<head>`:
  ```
  header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
  header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
  header("Cache-Control: post-check=0, pre-check=0", false);
  header("Pragma: no-cache");
  ```
  These are PHP `header()` calls, invalid as CSS. They will be silently ignored by rendering engines but indicate a template-authoring error (server-side snippet leaked into client markup) — no functional impact but worth cleanup.
- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` is present — inert for email clients, no impact.
- Mobile responsive rules are duplicated/redundant: two conflicting `@media` breakpoints for `img { max-width }` (700px and 650px) in the MI styling block.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- Recipient identifier resolves correctly and consistently across tracking URLs as `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` (seen in `coreemail.html?email=...` and the `svopn.potterybarn.com` pixel) — no unresolved merge tags (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) found in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not found by QA — noted as possibly an artifact of the AgentMail relay rather than the sender's omission; cannot confirm compliance status from header data alone.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)**: not found — one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed.
- **Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM)**: not found — cannot confirm authentication status; QA notes this is expected to be stripped by the relay.
- A visible unsubscribe link/footer was not present in the truncated HTML sample provided — cannot confirm CAN-SPAM footer compliance (physical address, opt-out link) from available source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Cannot verify UTM parameter presence or landing-page alignment: all product/CTA links are wrapped in opaque tracking redirects (`click.e.potterybarn.com`, `clickcp.potterybarn.com`) rather than direct `potterybarn.com` URLs with visible query strings, and QA explicitly skipped probing these.
- One direct (non-redirected) link is present: `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` — no UTM parameters attached.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 39 flagged images, particularly product images (dresser, bed, table, sheet set) where alt text aids both accessibility and image-blocked rendering.
2. Convert the 4 HTTP-only image sources to HTTPS: `edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`, `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`, and the `svopn.potterybarn.com` open-tracking pixel.
3. Move the `svopn.potterybarn.com` open pixel to HTTPS to stop transmitting the recipient email address in cleartext.
4. Remove the stray PHP `header()` statements from the `<style>` block in `<head>` — dead code with no functional effect but indicates a template hygiene issue.
5. Confirm List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post / Authentication-Results headers directly at the sending MTA (outside the relay) to validate actual compliance, since the relay may be stripping them before this audit sees them.
