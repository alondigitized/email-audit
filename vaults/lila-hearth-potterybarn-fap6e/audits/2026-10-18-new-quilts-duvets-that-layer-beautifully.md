# New quilts & duvets that layer beautifully
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-18**
## Executive summary

- This is a tidy, editorial bedding send with one small offer. It opens on a "Beautifully Layered" hero with a "BEDDING SHIPS FREE" pill. Four "Shop the Look" room scenes follow, each captioned with a named collection: Heritage Medallion, Lilah Suzani, Cozy Clouds and Lydia Palampore. It matches the subject's quilts-and-duvets angle and looks like a real seasonal push. It's close to the Oct 16 "Love pattern? This bedding is for you" send, which had the same structure and a "Quilts ship free" pill.
- The weakness is that the reason to act today is thin. Free shipping on bedding is a perk, not a deadline. The four collections sit behind lifestyle photos and small "Shop the Look" links, with no prices and no product detail. The bottom half is a generic "Recommended Just For You" grid (curtain rod, dining tables, a bed, a rug) plus a Christmas Shop banner, free design services, the app, the rewards card and Affirm. ## What's working

- The hero names the category and carries a concrete perk (bedding ships free) right under the headline.
- Each collection has a short evocative line, such as "Plush chenille in soothing tones," and a named pattern, which gives the email a consistent editorial voice.
- The room photography is warm, cohesive and seasonal, with neutral, olive and cream palettes that suit cozy season.
- A "Shop All Bedding" button and a "More ways to layer a cozy bed" row (Blankets, Pillows, Duvet Inserts, Comforters) give clear next steps.
- The "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks" strip at the top addresses a real purchase worry.

## What's weak

- No price, percent off or deadline appears anywhere in the bedding section, so there's no reason to act now.
- The "Shop the Look" links are small and low-contrast, and the CTA is repeated four times with identical copy. The only real button is "Shop All Bedding" near the bottom.
- The hero headline sits over a busy photo and is hard to read at this size. The pill and strip above it are also small.
- The email is very long. The recommended-products grid, Christmas Shop banner and design-services promo dilute the quilts-and-duvets message, and the app, rewards and Affirm blocks add more clutter below that.
- The recommendations don't match the subject. Dining tables and curtain hardware don't follow from a quilt-and-duvet email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one real offer in the hero, such as "Quilts & duvets: free shipping through Sunday" or a tiered discount, with an end date. This would lift the email more than any other change.
- 2. Replace the four "Shop the Look" links with a specific product and a price per collection, for example "Shop Heritage Medallion from $X."
- 3. Make the hero text legible with a solid or darkened text panel, and give it one large button with a verb and noun, like "Shop Quilts & Duvets."
- 4. Cut the recommendation grid, or limit it to bedding-adjacent items (shams, sheets, throws), and drop the Christmas, design, app and Affirm blocks into a slimmer footer.
- 5. Add reviews or a rating to at least one hero collection for social proof.

## Technical Audit

1. **Technical Summary**
The Pottery Barn email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with a hidden preheader, and its core structure is sound. It has widespread missing alt text (about 55 images), three non-HTTPS image sources, and stray PHP and CSS debris in a `<style>` block. The header-related compliance warnings can't be confirmed because the AgentMail relay may not capture those headers.

2. **Link & Tracking Issues**
- 59 tracking links point to click-redirect domains (e.g. `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...`). The QA run skipped the HTTP probe on these, so their destinations and validity are unverified. This is not a confirmed defect.
- The tracking pixel `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is served over plain HTTP. It may be blocked or rewritten by clients, which would undercount opens. The recipient email address is also passed in clear text in the query string.
- `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is loaded as an `<img>`, so it is another tracking beacon that exposes the address in the URL.
- Two further beacons are present: `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/b376e046-...?mi_cid=677b44e6831b79f3&mi_mid=...`. Both lack alt text.
- Image paths point to `edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`, a West Elm host, from inside a Pottery Barn email. This is a cross-brand asset dependency.
- Stale asset paths are in use: `/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` is a 2022 asset and `/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` is a legacy path. These are over HTTP, and the 2022 path is a likely leftover from template reuse.
- The folder name `0924_AM_THU_NewBeddingLooksEvergreen` suggests a Sept 24 send, but the email was received October 18. This is only an inference from the path naming.

3. **Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Missing alt text:** about 55 images have no `alt`. These include all 17 body slices (`.../0924_AM_THU_NewBeddingLooksEvergreen/slices_01–17.jpg`), all 18 footer slices (`.../Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`), the product images (`banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`, `emery-linen-curtain-l.jpg`, `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`, etc.), and the hero and banner images (`H25_TOP_InStockFurniture.jpg`, `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg`, `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`). With images off or in a screen reader, the email is effectively blank. The `<img>` for `coreemail.html` also gets flagged, but that's a tracking beacon, so empty alt is appropriate there.
- **Non-HTTPS images:** three images are served over `http://` (`Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, the 2022 `slices_02.jpg`, and the `svopn` pixel). These risk mixed-content blocking.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Stray code in CSS:** the second `<style>` block contains literal PHP (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ...")`) after the CSS rules. Parsers will ignore it, but it is invalid and shows that a server-side snippet leaked into the template.
- **Viewport:** `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility concern (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Attribute selectors:** `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` only match elements whose class is exactly that value. They are brittle and may never match if the markup uses other classes.
- **Decorative image roles:** layout tables use `role="presentation"`, which is good. The spacer table near the logo does not, which is a minor inconsistency.
- **Preheader:** the visible preheader text is "All bedding ships free". It sits in a hidden div with `mso-hide:all`, and the padding characters (`͏` and `&shy;`) are applied correctly.
- **Google Fonts link:** the Montserrat `<link>` is unsupported in many clients and is not needed as a fallback.

4. **Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- No unresolved merge tokens (such as `%%...%%`, `{{...}}` or `[[...]]`) appear in the truncated source.
- The recipient address is correctly resolved in the beacon URLs. This is the intended personalization, but see the PII exposure point in section 2.
- **Caveat:** the source was truncated, so I could not check the rest of the body for tokens.

5. **Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** the QA check did not find either header. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe for senders of this size, so the original message headers should be checked.
- **Authentication-Results:** the header was not found, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain is `e.potterybarn.com`. Verify it with a direct DNS lookup (`dig TXT e.potterybarn.com`, `_dmarc.e.potterybarn.com`) rather than inferring it from the relay.
- **Body unsubscribe link and postal address:** both would be in the footer, which falls beyond the truncated source. I could not confirm either. The footer images have no alt text, so if the unsubscribe or address text is baked into an image slice, it is invisible to text-only clients.
- **Plain-text part:** 11356 of 15578 characters (73%) are URLs. This is above the 70% threshold. Long tracking URLs inflate the ratio, which is a minor spam-score risk.
- The `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` and `robots noindex, nofollow` tags are not an issue.

6. **Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- No standard UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) are visible. Attribution is handled by the opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=` redirect and the `mi_cid`/`mi_mid` parameters. This is a design choice, but it means site-side analytics can't attribute sessions from the URLs alone.
- Product images link to `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`, which are image CDN paths. Click-through destinations for the product tiles are behind the 59 skipped tracking redirects and can't be verified for landing-page alignment with the "quilts & duvets" subject.
- Two themes in the creative don't match the subject: the `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop` banner and the `H25_TOP_InStockFurniture` banner (furniture) appear in a bedding-focused email. This only matters for continuity if their links go to the wrong place, which I could not check.

7. **Recommendations**
1. Add meaningful `alt` text to all content images (hero, product and footer slices). Use `alt=""` only for spacers and pixels.
2. Move the three `http://` assets (`Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, the 2022 `slices_02.jpg`, the `svopn` pixel) to HTTPS.
3. Remove the PHP `header(...)` code from the `<style>` block and add a `<title>`.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Stop putting the recipient address in plain-text URLs in beacons. Use an opaque token.
6. Replace the cross-brand `edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` with a Pottery Barn–hosted asset, and retire the stale 2022 and `thekey` assets.
7. Check the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` before treating the compliance warnings as real. Then audit the footer, which the truncation hid, for the unsubscribe link and physical address.
8. Check the 59 tracking redirects in a follow-up probe (follow redirects) to confirm landing pages and that UTMs are appended.
