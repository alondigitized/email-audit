# Early Black Friday deals SELLING FAST
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the same burgundy "Early Black Friday, up to 70% off" Pottery Barn template that has run since 11/16. It is at least the tenth send in about a week. It followed this morning's "1000s of Early Black Friday deals + Must-have furniture deals" and "UP TO 70% OFF 🎁 'Tis the season to save". The hero is strong: a big "70% OFF" with a single SHOP NOW button. After that the email loses focus.
- The category button grid (Furniture, Sofas & Chairs, Bedding and so on) is a useful navigation aid. Below it sits a "Recommended Just For You" grid of stockings, a throw, a dining table, a robe and a quilt. No prices, no discounts and no ratings are shown. The Design Crew sweepstakes, app promo, credit card and Affirm blocks pile on further asks. A subscriber who has already seen nine near-identical emails this week gets no new reason to click.
- Judgement: competent but fatigued. The offer is clear, and the cadence and the lack of anything new are the problems. "SELLING FAST" is the only new element, and nothing in the body backs it up.

## What's working

- The hero offer, "Early Black Friday up to 70% OFF", dominates the top of the email, and the SHOP NOW button is clear.
- The category button grid lets readers jump straight to their room or product type.
- The branding is consistent: the wordmark, burgundy palette and photography look unmistakably Pottery Barn.
- The "In Stock & On Sale" banner with the sideboard adds some availability reassurance.
- The seasonal products (stockings, throws, robes) fit the holiday timing.

## What's weak

- The "Recommended Just For You" products have no prices, no sale callouts and no reviews, so nothing shows the discount.
- The body has too many competing asks: sweepstakes, app download, credit card, Affirm. These dilute the Black Friday message.
- The "SELLING FAST" urgency is only in the subject. The body shows no low-stock cues, no countdown and no end date.
- Cadence fatigue: the creative is nearly identical to the earlier sends this week, with a different grid order.
- The legal fine print is very long and dense. "Up to 70%" has a lot of exclusions behind it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the footer promos (app, card, Affirm) from sale sends and keep one secondary CTA at most. Put price-and-discount product tiles (for example "$399 $799") in the recommended grid.
- 2. Add real urgency in the body. Use a countdown or a stated deadline, plus "only X left" tags on the products that are actually low.
- 3. Reduce the send frequency or segment it. Send different creative to people who clicked earlier in the week.
- 4. Make the "Just For You" block personalized by category, or rename it if it isn't.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 70% off sofas ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your saved items are going fast`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Up to 70% off furniture, bedding & decor. Free shipping on festive favorites.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Top sellers are low in stock. Shop before they're gone.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a fixed 650px table layout, mostly image-sliced content, and opaque SFMC-style click redirects. The main findings are widespread missing alt text, three plain-HTTP image requests (two carry the recipient's email address), and compliance headers that the QA run could not verify. The source was truncated, so the footer and most of the body were not available.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **57 tracking links were not probed.** The links are on `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`, and QA skipped them as click-redirect domains. Destinations, redirect health and UTM presence are unverified.
- **Three tracking pixels have no alt text.** They are not marked as decorative (`alt=""`, 1x1) either.
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=...`
  - `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1`, a third-party ad-network pixel
  - `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/3a45b40d-...`
- **Cross-brand asset dependency:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on a sibling brand's domain. It has no alt text and will break if that asset moves.
- **Stale asset path:** one footer image is `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`, a 2022 creative reused in a 2026 send.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text is widespread.** QA flagged about 45 images:
  - `H26_Sale_EBF_LinkfarmOverSolidColor/slices_01.jpg` to `slices_12.jpg`
  - `Email-Footer/slices_01.jpg` to `slices_18.jpg`
  - Product images such as `classic-velvet-stockings-l.jpg` and `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-125-l.jpg`
  - `H26_Bottom_*` banners

  The 12-slice "LinkfarmOverSolidColor" block is likely the primary navigation and offer content. Without alt text, it is unreadable when images are blocked or with a screen reader.
- **Mixed content:** three images load over `http://`.
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&...`

  These may be blocked or rewritten by clients and proxies.
- **`<title></title>` is empty.**
- **`<html>` has no `lang` attribute.** The tag is `<html xmlns=... xmlns:v=... xmlns:o=...>`.
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` stops pinch-zoom on supporting clients (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Stray PHP in CSS:** the second `<style>` block contains `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ...")`. This is invalid CSS, and some parsers may drop rules that follow it. It also suggests a template-build artifact.
- **Dead mobile rules:** `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` appear in the visible source only as selectors. No matching elements are visible in the truncated portion, so these rules may be dead code.
- **Layout tables are inconsistent:** the spacer tables (for example `<td height="20">&nbsp;</td>`) lack `role="presentation"`, while others have it.
- **External font:** the Montserrat Google Fonts `<link>` is unsupported in many clients. No fallback stack was visible in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) are visible in the truncated source.
- **The recipient email address appears in URLs, two of them over plain HTTP:**
  - `.../coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`

  The `svopn` request is unencrypted, so the address is exposed in transit and in server logs. The `coreemail.html` URL is HTTPS, so it is not exposed in transit.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** (QA WARN).
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found** (QA WARN).
- **Authentication-Results not found,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown (QA WARN).
- All three warnings may be artifacts of the AgentMail relay, which QA itself notes. They are unconfirmed, not proven failures.
- The unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked because the HTML was truncated before the footer.
- The sender domain `e.potterybarn.com` is consistent with the click domain `click.e.potterybarn.com`, which is good for alignment.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters cannot be verified. The `qs=` blobs are opaque and the redirects were not followed.
- Product images point to `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...` and resolve as `-l.jpg` assets. Their click targets were not resolved, so landing-page alignment cannot be confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, especially the 12 LinkfarmOverSolidColor slices and the product images. Add `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"` to tracking pixels.
2. Move the `http://` assets (`Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `2022/...slices_02.jpg`, `svopn` pixel) to HTTPS. Stop passing the plaintext email in `sv_sveme` and `coreemail.html?email=`, using a hashed or opaque ID instead.
3. Add `<title>` and `<html lang="en">`. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Remove the stray PHP `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block. Delete the unused `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` selectors if no markup uses them.
5. Re-run QA against the raw `.eml` or the full original headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Check the footer for an unsubscribe link and a postal address.
6. Resolve a sample of the `click.e.potterybarn.com` links to confirm UTM parameters and landing-page alignment.
7. Host the spacer GIF on the brand's own domain instead of `edm.westelm.com`.
