# Extra 20% off select styles won't last forever + Ships free! Pillows in every palette
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-02**
## Executive summary

- This is a pillow-led send with the usual "Extra 20% off Select Styles" banner on top. The pillow half works. "Made for Mixing / Pillows ship free" sits over a stacked-pillow photo. Below it, a tidy 2x3 grid splits into shop neutrals, blues and greens, then "Our Favorite Pillow Pairings" and a "Shop All Pillows" button. It's the same pillow idea as the 11/2 "Pillows in all your favorite hues (ship free!)" send, now with the markdown banner bolted back on.
- The subject tries to carry two messages: a vague deadline and a shipping perk. At 86 characters it will truncate on mobile, so the pillow half may never show. "Won't last forever" is weak urgency. The oxblood banner has run since Oct 26, and the second 11/2 send ("Don't miss your chance for EXTRA 20% OFF") used it too. A repeat subscriber will read this as one more identical promo.
- The body is also long. After the pillow story come a "More for your living room" nav, a six-item "Recommended just for you" grid, a Christmas Shop block, design services, the app, a credit card and Affirm. It looks polished, but the second half dilutes the pillow message. Overall it's a solid send, held back by cadence fatigue and an overstuffed subject.

## What's working

- The pillow hero and "Pillows ship free" pill agree with the subject's second half.
- The shop neutrals / blues / greens grid lets a shopper pick by color, with large photos.
- "Shop All Pillows" is a clear verb-plus-noun button after the grid.
- The Christmas Shop block and stockings fit the early-November season.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The oxblood "Extra 20% off" banner is the same top block as every send since Oct 26. It competes with the pillow hero for the first read.
- The subject is 86 characters and truncates, and "won't last forever" gives no real deadline.
- The "Recommended just for you" grid shows a dining table, a curtain rod, stockings, a rug, a bed and a mattress. None are pillows, and no prices or reviews are shown.
- Exclusions and fine print sit in a small "Exclusions apply" line under the banner, with more at the very bottom. The "up to 70% off" claim reads vague.
- The footer stacks the app, the credit card, Affirm and the design appointment, a lot of secondary asks after the main message.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one lead idea. Either run the pillow story with "Pillows ship free" as the hero and shrink the 20% banner to a thin strip, or lead with the markdown and drop the pillow subject.
- 2. Give the deadline a real date or time, such as "ends Sunday." Put it in both the subject and the banner.
- 3. Cut the lower body. Keep the pillow grid, then a single Christmas block, and drop the unrelated recommended grid.
- 4. Add a price or review count to the pillow tiles, for example "from $39" or a star rating.
- 5. Move the exclusions closer to the offer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Pillows ship free + extra 20% off, ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Mix and match pillows: free shipping today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Neutrals, blues and greens, all shipping free. Extra 20% off select styles through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Find your palette: pillow pairings from $39, plus 20% off select styles.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a table-based, image-sliced ESP template (Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style click wrapping). The visible source has no broken merge tokens. The QA data and source show widespread missing alt text, three non-HTTPS images, and an email address exposed in plaintext tracking URLs. The compliance and authentication headers could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque redirects:** QA skipped 58 tracking links. The logo link is `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...`, so destinations can't be validated from this data.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEv.../cat/1` is an external ad-network beacon with `alt="1"`.
- **Plaintext tracking pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=<email>` loads over HTTP. It may be blocked, and the recipient email travels unencrypted.
- **Plain-text part:** 11,162 of 15,399 characters (about 72%) are URLs. This is a spam-score risk and a poor plain-text experience, caused by the long wrapped links.
- **Odd image source:** `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=...` is used as an `<img src>`, so it is an HTML endpoint serving as a pixel. It may not render as an image.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** QA flagged about 50 images. They include the top banner `H26_TOP_Extra20CLR.jpg`, all 16 `F26_Decor_B6_PillowPairings/slices_01–16.jpg`, all 18 `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`, six product images (e.g. `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`), and the bottom banners. Because the content is sliced images, it is blank with images off and unreadable to screen readers.
- **Decorative and tracking images:** `spacer_0.gif` and the pixels need `alt=""` at minimum.
- **Non-HTTPS images (3):**
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - the `svopn` pixel above
- **Document-level problems:**
  - `<title></title>` is empty.
  - `<html>` has no `lang` attribute.
  - `viewport` uses `maximum-scale=1`, which blocks pinch-zoom.
  - The outer layout tables lack `role="presentation"`. The inner `contenttable` tables have it.
- **Stray code in CSS:** The last `<style>` block contains literal PHP, `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store...")`. It is invalid CSS and likely a templating leak. It sits after the real rules, so impact is probably limited.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on another brand's domain, which is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible (truncated) source.
- **PII in URLs:** The recipient address `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` appears in clear text in the `coreemail.html?email=` and `svopn ... sv_sveme=` query strings. The `svopn` URL is HTTP. This is a privacy and leakage risk through logs, proxies and referrers.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both were not found. QA notes this may be an AgentMail relay capture artifact, so I can't confirm a real defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers.
- **Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM/DMARC):** Not captured, so authentication status is unknown.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and physical address:** The truncated source doesn't reach the footer, so I can't verify them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters:** Every link is wrapped in `click.e.potterybarn.com` with an encoded `qs` payload, so UTM parameters and final landing pages can't be verified from this data.
- **Image URLs:** Product images load from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`, with campaign-style asset paths like `HolidayBanners/H26_...`.
- **Stale asset:** The 2022 `0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` is reused in a 2026 send.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content slices (pillow and footer slices) and `alt=""` to spacers and pixels.
2. Serve `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `slices_02.jpg` and the `svopn` pixel over HTTPS.
3. Stop putting the raw email address in URLs. Use a hashed or opaque subscriber ID.
4. Fix the stray PHP in the `<style>` block. Add a `<title>` and `lang`, and remove `maximum-scale=1`.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` on the raw message, and capture `Authentication-Results`, outside the relay.
6. Resolve the redirect chains to verify landing pages and UTM carry-through.
7. Reduce plain-text URL density by shortening the tracked links.
