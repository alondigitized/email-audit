# Shop early, save BIG (UP TO 70% OFF)
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the same burgundy "Early Black Friday, up to 70% off" Pottery Barn template that has run since 11/16. It is the sixth send in roughly three days: 11/20 "Don't miss Early Black Friday", 11/21 "UP TO 70% OFF ❤️ Top Early Black Friday picks", 11/21 "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY DEALS delivered in 1-4 weeks", and the two 11/22 sends. The hero is strong: a large "up to 70% off" with a clear SHOP NOW button, and a lifestyle collage below it. But a subscriber has seen it all several times by now.
- The render holds up. I see no broken images or overlapping text. The category button grid (Furniture, Sofas & Chairs, Bedding and so on) makes it easy to jump to a category. The "Recommended Just For You" product block is the only part that looks personalized. It leads with Christmas stockings and a throw, which suits the season.
- The email is competent but a repeat, and it adds nothing new for someone already on the list. The subject's "save BIG" and the capitalized "BIG" read as filler next to the real offer, which is the 70% figure.

## What's working

- The hero is unambiguous. "Early Black Friday, up to 70% off" sits in large type over solid burgundy with a single SHOP NOW button.
- The eight-button category grid gives fast, scannable paths for different shoppers.
- The "Recommended Just For You" block with named products (Classic Velvet Stockings, Toscana Extendable Dining Table) is more specific than a generic promo.
- The seasonal imagery, with stockings and cozy bedding, fits late November.
- The Design Crew sweepstakes ($3,000 gift card) and the credit-card and Affirm modules add secondary reasons to engage.

## What's weak

- The creative is a near-exact repeat of the 11/20–11/22 sends, so it adds to the fatigue.
- The email is very long. The sweepstakes, app, credit card, Affirm and legal blocks pile up below the offer and dilute the one message.
- The hero collage has no product names, prices or sale prices. "Up to 70%" gives no sense of what is actually discounted.
- The "In Stock & On Sale" block is recycled from the 11/21 delivery-speed send. It repeats that message without a deadline.
- The offer has no deadline or end date visible in the render. "Early" implies urgency but gives no end.
- The legal fine print is dense and long, and it sits right under the offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cap the cadence. Send one Black Friday email a day, or suppress people who clicked or opened the previous send. This would lift performance more than any copy change.
- 2. Add a real deadline to the hero, for example "Ends Monday 11/24 at midnight PT", with a countdown.
- 3. Put two or three hero products with sale prices above the category grid, such as "Velvet stockings $X, was $Y".
- 4. Cut the lower modules (app, credit card, Affirm) to a single line, and move the sweepstakes to a separate send.
- 5. Segment the product block by browse history instead of showing the same recommendations to everyone.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Early Black Friday prices on furniture, bedding and holiday decor. Delivered in 1-4 weeks.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your picks are on sale. Shop before Monday midnight PT.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Pottery Barn, "Shop early, save BIG (UP TO 70% OFF)"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with opaque SFMC-style click wrappers. QA gave a 57% pass rate (0 issues, 3 warnings), and the HTML source adds more. The main problems are about 50 images without alt text, the recipient address leaking in image and tracking URLs (one over plain HTTP), and header-level compliance checks that could not be confirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **57 tracking links were not probed.** They are on the click-redirect domain, for example `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...` on the logo link. Destinations and redirect health are unverified.
- **Non-HTTPS tracking pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` loads over HTTP, so it may be blocked or flagged as mixed content.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` is an ad-network pixel with no alt text.
- **Cross-brand asset:** the spacer `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on a West Elm domain.
- **Stale, non-HTTPS assets:** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` is a 2022 asset, and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` is also HTTP.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 50 images.** Examples:
  - `slices_01.jpg` to `slices_13.jpg` under `/H26_Sale_EBF_BannerOver6Grid/`
  - `slices_01.jpg` to `slices_18.jpg` under `/Email-Footer/`
  - product images such as `classic-velvet-stockings-l.jpg`, `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-125-l.jpg` and `faux-fur-ruched-throw-l.jpg`
  - banners such as `H26_Bottom_InStockAndOnSale.jpg`
  
  The sliced-image layout makes this worse. With images off, large areas are blank and the offer is unreadable.
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in `<head>`.
- **Viewport:** `maximum-scale=1` disables pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility concern.
- **Stray PHP code inside a `<style>` block:** `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store...` sits after the `html {…}` rule. It is invalid CSS and will be dropped, but it signals a template leak.
- **Table roles:** some tables carry `role="presentation"`, but the outer wrapper tables and the spacer table (`<td height="20">&nbsp;</td>`) do not. Screen readers may treat them as data tables.
- **Mobile selectors:** `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` are exact-match selectors. They cannot be confirmed to match anything in the visible source, which is truncated.
- **`<html>` has no `lang` attribute.**

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) are visible in the truncated source.
- **The recipient's address is exposed in plaintext in URLs:**
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` (used as an image, also without alt text)
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?...&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  
  The second is sent over unencrypted HTTP, which leaks PII in transit and in proxy logs.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` was not found** (WARN). The QA note says the AgentMail relay may have dropped it, so this is unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was not found** (WARN). One-click unsubscribe support is unverified.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found** (WARN). SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, and the relay is the stated likely cause.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and physical address are not visible in the truncated source, so footer compliance cannot be confirmed. The footer appears to be composed of `/Email-Footer/slices_*.jpg` images, so any footer text would be image-only.
- The sender domain `e.potterybarn.com` aligns with the tracking domains `click.e.potterybarn.com` and `image.e.potterybarn.com`.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No UTM parameters are visible. The `qs=` click wrappers are opaque, and the 57 tracked links were not resolved, so UTM presence and landing-page alignment cannot be verified.
- Product images point directly to `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`, which is fine for assets.
- The `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` parameters on `clickcp.potterybarn.com` suggest campaign and message IDs are carried in the tracking layer.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images. Use `alt=""` for spacers and pixels (`spacer_0.gif`, `turn.com`, `svopn`, `clickcp`). For sliced banners, put the offer text in the alt of the main slice.
2. Move the `svopn` pixel, `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and the 2022 `slices_02.jpg` to HTTPS.
3. Stop placing the recipient's email in query strings, especially over HTTP. Use an opaque subscriber ID.
4. Fill in `<title>`, add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, remove `maximum-scale=1`, and add `role="presentation"` to the remaining layout tables.
5. Remove the PHP `header(...)` text from the `<style>` block.
6. Re-run the QA against the raw message, not the AgentMail relay copy. This would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
7. Check the unsubscribe and address text in the footer. Make sure it is live HTML text, not just inside `Email-Footer/slices_*.jpg`.
8. Resolve the 57 click-redirect links with a redirect-following probe to check UTMs and landing pages.
