# STARTS NOW 📣 Extra 20% off select styles
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a loud, clear sitewide sale email. The oxblood hero says "starts now / Extra 20% off Select Styles / Plus savings up to 70% off" with a white "SHOP ALL SALE" button, so the offer lands in about a second. The 10 category tiles under it (Furniture, Sofas & Chairs, Pillows & Decor, Rugs and so on) give a home shopper an easy way in.
- It's weaker than it first looks. "Select styles" is vague, and there's no end date, so "starts now" has nothing behind it. The tiles are plain text on dark red with no imagery, and the product grid below them shows no prices, so the sale never becomes concrete. Compare the Oct 20 Halloween Shop send, which had a hard "Order by…" deadline. This one has none. It's also roughly the seventh Pottery Barn send in four days, after the Oct 21 "Decor that instantly makes the room feel styled" and "Our top 100 gifts" sends. Verdict: solid and competent, but generic and easy to ignore.

## What's working

- The hero is unmistakable. The "Extra 20% off" headline is large, and a single white CTA sits right under it.
- The category tile grid is scannable, and it includes decor and pillows, which match this persona's interest.
- The "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks" bar at the top adds a practical reason to buy now.
- The "Recommended Just For You" grid has clean, well-lit product photography, and the render has no broken images or layout breakage.
- The Christmas Shop and Design Services modules are on-season and give extra routes in.

## What's weak

- "Select styles" with "Exclusions apply" is vague, and the long fine print below makes the offer feel less honest.
- There's no deadline. "Starts now" gives no end date, so the urgency isn't credible.
- The recommended products have no prices, no sale pricing and no reviews, so nothing ties them back to the 20% off.
- The hero and its 10 tiles are all dark red and text-only, so it feels heavy and repetitive. The "Shop All Sale" button also appears twice.
- The footer is crowded with the credit card, Affirm, app download and a wall of legal text. All of that competes with the sale CTA.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real deadline and a clear definition of the offer, such as "Ends Sunday" and "Extra 20% off 1,000+ styles." Urgency and clarity would lift this email more than anything else.
- 2. Put product photography with sale prices (was/now) in the hero or directly beneath it. Replace some of the text-only tiles with image tiles for rugs, sofas and pillows.
- 3. Cut the repeated CTA and trim the tile grid to 6 categories.
- 4. Move the "Recommended Just For You" items above the Christmas Shop and Design Services modules, and show a price on each.
- 5. Shorten the footer so the credit card and Affirm blocks don't compete with the sale.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your extra 20% off starts now (up to 70% off)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Up to 70% off furniture, bedding & lighting. In-stock pieces ship in 1-4 weeks.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sale prices on rugs, pillows & dining tables. Ends Sunday at midnight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with sliced-image content and roughly 54 opaque click-tracking links. The main technical problems are 46 images with no alt text, three plaintext-HTTP images (one carries the recipient's email address), and compliance headers and authentication results that can't be verified from this capture.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Destinations not verified.** 54 links were skipped as tracking redirects. The header logo link, `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7ImQi…`, is typical of these. Destination, status and redirect chain are unconfirmed.
- **Plaintext tracking pixel.** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is served over HTTP. It can be blocked or rewritten, so open tracking is unreliable.
- **Third-party pixel.** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEv…/cat/1` is a Turn (ad-network) beacon. It is a third-party data-sharing path in a first-party email.
- **Plaintext part is mostly URLs.** 10,386 of 14,568 characters (71%) are URLs, which crosses the QA threshold of 70%. Long tracking URLs inflate the text part and can look spammy to filters.
- **Cross-brand asset.** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a West Elm-hosted spacer in a Pottery Barn send. It adds a dependency on a second host for no function.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 46 images.** About 41 are content images and the rest are pixels or spacers.
  - The hero is sliced as `…/1022_THU_AM_Extra20StartsNowLinkFarmOverRed/slices_01–12.jpg`, with none carrying alt. With images off, the offer content is blank, and screen readers announce filenames.
  - The same applies to all six product images (e.g. `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`, `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg`), the banners, and the 18 footer slices (`Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`).
  - Tracking pixels and spacers should carry `alt=""` to mark them decorative.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** There is no title for assistive tech and web-view fallback.
- **No `lang` attribute.** `<html xmlns=…>` has no `lang`.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Inconsistent `role="presentation"`.** Content tables have it, but the outer wrapper tables and the spacer table (`<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><tr><td height="20">&nbsp;</td>`) do not.
- **Stray server-side code in the CSS.** The last `<style>` block ends with `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store…"); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. This is leaked PHP text inside CSS. It is harmless today but a malformed trailing rule.
- **Possible mobile overflow.** Layout tables are fixed `width="650"`, and the only mobile override, `.contenttable{width:320px !important}`, is commented out. Mobile behavior depends on the `.mobile-hide` / `.showmobile` swap and client scaling, and I did not render-test it.
- **Overlapping `img` max-width rules.** `min-width:700px` sets `max-width:650px` and `min-width:650px` sets `max-width:100%!important`, so the rules overlap.
- **Web font.** The Google Fonts `<link>` is stripped by Gmail and many clients, so the fallback stack matters.
- **Unescaped ampersand.** The preheader reads `stores & online`, which is invalid XHTML. It is cosmetic.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved merge tokens** (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) are visible in the captured source.
- **Recipient email in URLs.** The address `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` appears in plaintext query strings in two image requests:
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=…`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?…&sv_sveme=…` (unencrypted)

  Both leak PII into server logs and proxies. The second is also exposed in transit.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA note says AgentMail may strip these, so this is unverified, not confirmed missing. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both.
- **Authentication-Results not found**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `e.potterybarn.com`.
- **Unsubscribe link and postal address not verifiable.** The captured HTML is truncated before the footer. The footer is 18 alt-less image slices, so if the unsubscribe text or address is baked into the images, it is invisible with images off. Confirm that a live-text unsubscribe link and physical address exist.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters can't be assessed.** Every captured link is a redirect (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…` or `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/…`) with the destination encoded in `qs`. Landing-page alignment with the "Extra 20% off select styles" offer is unverified.
- **Product images link to the site directly.** They are hosted at `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/…`, so they will break if SKUs or image paths change.
- **Dynamic image.** `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/e019516c-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` is a Movable Ink dynamic image with no alt. If it fails to load, there is no fallback text.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt to all content images, starting with the hero slices (carry the offer text) and the footer slices (carry the legal and unsubscribe text). Use `alt=""` on pixels and spacers.
2. Move `svopn.potterybarn.com` to HTTPS and stop putting the raw email address in query strings (`sv_sveme`, `coreemail.html?email=`). Use a hashed or opaque ID instead.
3. Serve `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` over HTTPS.
4. Fill in `<title>`, add `lang="en"`, and remove `maximum-scale=1`. Delete the leaked `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block.
5. Replace the West Elm spacer with a same-host asset or table spacing.
6. Check the raw message headers in the source mailbox for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Confirm a text unsubscribe link and postal address in the full, untruncated HTML.
7. Re-run the link check with redirect following, so the destinations and UTMs behind the `click.e.potterybarn.com` links are validated.
8. Render-test the mobile layout, since the 650px fixed tables rely on an unverified `.showmobile` swap.
