# Homepage snapshot · www.potterybarn.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- I opened Pottery Barn expecting to browse furniture and textiles for my second home, and instead I got a full-screen sweepstakes popup about winning U.S. Open golf tickets for Dad. It's a nice-looking modal — clean imagery, readable copy — but it has absolutely nothing to do with why I'm here. I have to actively dismiss this before I can see a single piece of furniture, and that is not a warm welcome.

## What's working

- **Seasonal relevance:** The Father's Day tie-in is timely — May 15, sweepstakes ends May 24. The brand is at least living in the right moment.
- **Polished flatlay photography:** The golf book / bar accessories / whiskey glass image is well-lit and feels premium. Pottery Barn's production quality is evident even in a promotional modal.
- **Prize value is compelling on paper:** "Two tickets to the U.S. Open + $500 to spend at Pottery Barn" is a real hook — for someone shopping Father's Day gifts for a golf-loving husband. That's just not me today.
- **Clean modal structure:** Headline, subhead, prize detail, fine print, email field — logically organized, no clutter.

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is blocked.** I came to browse. I can't see a single sofa, pillow, or rug. This modal is doing maximum damage to my intent-driven session before I've seen anything.
- **Zero connection to my use case.** I'm here for second-home décor — throws, bedding, accent furniture. There's nothing in this popup that acknowledges that.
- **SMS consent block is intimidating.** The legal copy takes up nearly as much vertical space as the entry form itself. That's a trust friction point even for users who do want to enter.
- **No close-and-shop affordance.** I see the X button, but there's no "no thanks, take me to the site" link that acknowledges I might actually want to buy something right now.
- **Navigation is almost entirely cut off** — I can barely read "Baby · Kids · Teen · Dorm · Business to Business · Willia…" — the modal is eating the whole chrome.

## Recommendations

- **Set a first-visit condition on the sweepstakes modal** — if I'm a known loyalty member or have browsed before, skip the interstitial entirely and drop a small persistent banner at the top instead.
- **Add a contextual "Shop the Prize Look" link inside the modal** — the flatlay has Pottery Barn bar accessories and books; link directly to those categories so browsers like me still get pulled in.
- **Shrink or collapse the consent block** — move it below the fold inside the modal or behind a "see details" toggle so the form-to-legal ratio doesn't feel like a contract signing.
- **Surface the loyalty/rewards hook** — even a "Key Rewards member? Log in for an extra entry" line would make this modal work harder for existing customers while triggering account recognition.
