# Homepage snapshot · www.potterybarn.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- I opened Pottery Barn on my phone and the very first thing I see — filling my entire screen — is a sweepstakes popup for "#1 Dad Sweepstakes." Golf courses book, whiskey decanters, grandstand seats to the U.S. Open. I came here to think about throw pillows and a linen duvet for the lake house, not to enter a contest for my husband. This is Pottery Barn — one of my brands — and it just made me feel like a stranger at a party I wasn't invited to.

## What's working

- **Williams-Sonoma brand affiliation copy** — the fine print mentions "Williams-Sonoma brands" and I can unsubscribe. That WS ecosystem connection builds low-key trust for me; I already have that loyalty card.
- **Clean modal render** — the popup itself is polished, image is crisp, type is readable on mobile. At least it doesn't look broken.
- **Stated deadline** — "Ends 5/24/26" in the fine print means there's a real expiration, even if it's buried.

## What's weak

- **Modal blocks the entire homepage** — I literally cannot see a single product, category, or piece of the actual store. No navigation, no hero, no seasonal collection. Pottery Barn might as well not exist right now.
- **"#1 Dad / Golf Lover" framing** — I am not the target of this sweepstakes. The imagery (whiskey decanters, golf courses book, bar tools) signals a male gift-giver persona. I'm the one decorating the house.
- **No loyalty hook** — I'm a Williams-Sonoma Key Rewards member. Not a single mention of my points balance, member-only offer, or "as a member, you get extra entries." Missed layup.
- **SMS consent wall** — three paragraphs of legal text before I even see a submit button. That's a lot of friction and fine print for a sweepstakes I didn't come here to enter.
- **$500 prize requires winning** — this isn't a coupon. It's a lottery. For someone ready to buy throw pillows today, this offers nothing actionable.

## Recommendations

- **Let me dismiss and land on the homepage immediately** — if you must run the sweepstakes popup, a single tap-to-close should surface the actual seasonal shop. Right now the X button is small and I'm stuck.
- **Add a loyalty-member layer to the modal** — swap the generic email field for "Log in for your exclusive member offer" or surface my rewards balance. If I'm already a Key Rewards member, stop treating me like a stranger.
- **Segment the interstitial by browsing behavior** — someone who typed "potterybarn.com" on a Thursday in May is likely not shopping for a golf dad. Show me new arrivals in bedding or outdoor entertaining, not a sweepstakes.
- **Surface the actual catalog above the fold** — one scroll-strip showing "New: Summer Bedding" or "Outdoor Dining Now" behind the modal dismiss would tell me there's something worth staying for.
