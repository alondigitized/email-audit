# Up to 70% off ON NOW + Your first look at the NEW catalog
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-18**
## Executive summary

- Another compound-subject send from a brand that has been running this exact formula for most of July — but this one earns slightly more grace because the catalog debut is genuinely fresh content. "Your first look at the NEW catalog" could carry an entire email on its own; instead it's the junior partner to the now-threadbare "70% off ON NOW" hook that subscribers have seen at least four times this month. The two hooks pull the reader in opposite directions: one signals sale urgency, the other signals editorial discovery, and neither gets enough room to land.
- Below the dual hero, the email fractures into six or seven competing sections — new arrivals grid, design classes, personalized rug recommendations, a second "Discover New Arrivals" hero, an app download pitch, and a rewards/Affirm block. The personalized product row ("Recommended Just For You," featuring named rugs and a dining table) is the smartest move in the email and a shame it's buried halfway down.
- Judgment: better than the mid-July discount grind purely because the catalog launch gives subscribers something new, but worse than yesterday's indigo-blue editorial (9/10) because everything gets diluted. The catalog reveal deserved its own clean send.

## What's working

- The Summer Catalog section is the freshest content in the July cycle — a genuine catalog debut rather than another reprise of the sale.
- "Recommended Just For You" with named products (Moroccan Cream Oval Rug, Capulet Terracotta Rug) signals real personalization and gives the click a clear destination.
- Sale hero is first-fold on mobile with a visible SHOP NOW button — no ambiguity about the offer.
- "Earn 20% Back in Rewards" loyalty section adds member-specific value to a send that would otherwise feel generic.
- Render is clean — no broken images, no overlapping text, no layout breakage visible.

## What's weak

- The compound subject formula returns for the fourth-plus time in July; the "70% off ON NOW" half is fully exhausted as an attention signal.
- "ON NOW" with no deadline is not urgency — subscribers have heard this phrase continuously for two weeks.
- Six distinct CTA tracks (sale, catalog, category grid, design classes, new arrivals v2, app) compete equally below the hero, destroying hierarchy.
- The catalog section gets a narrow, mid-email slot when it had enough novelty to anchor a standalone send — the Chris Loves Julia and indigo blue editorial emails from July 17 proved exactly that format works.
- The Design Classes section breaks the commercial momentum mid-scroll with no clear connection to the email's stated hooks.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Split the email — run the catalog as its own standalone send.** The July 17 indigo blue email (9/10) and the Chris Loves Julia reveal (7/10) show that editorial-only emails outperform when they have a single focus. The catalog launch is that moment.
- 2. **Kill "ON NOW" — replace with a hard deadline.** "Ends Sunday" or "Through July 20" restores the urgency the word "now" has lost after two weeks of continuous use.
- 3. **Collapse to two CTAs in the body:** Shop the sale → Browse the catalog. Everything else moves to the footer or a separate send.
- 4. **Promote the personalized product row above the fold.** The rug recommendations are the highest-intent click in the email; they're wasted at scroll position four.
- 5. **Let the catalog hero breathe** — give it full-width image treatment instead of splitting it with sale copy.
- **Subject Alt A:** `The Summer Catalog is here — shop up to 70% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your first look at our Summer Catalog`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New inspiration inside — plus sale prices through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop the new looks before the sale ends.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Critical — PHP code injected into CSS block:**
The following server-side directives appear verbatim inside a `<style>` tag:
```
header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
header("Cache-Control: post-check=0, pre-check=0", false);
header("Pragma: no-cache");
```
These are invalid CSS. They will cause CSS parser errors in strict email clients and represent a template assembly failure — server-side code that was not stripped before rendering.

**Missing `alt` text:** 39 images across the email have no `alt` attribute set. This includes all content slices (`slices_01.jpg` through `slices_18.jpg`), hero banners (`F26_TOP_SummerSalePartC_NoUrgency.jpg`, `F26_Bottom_NewArrivals.jpg`, `S26_Bottom_UpTo60OffOutdoor.jpg`), and product images (bed, rug, towel, curtain, curtain rod). Renders as a blank email when images are blocked.

**HTTP image sources (3 assets):** Will be blocked by Gmail, Outlook 365, and Apple Mail with image proxying active:
- `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`

**Open pixel tracker also HTTP:**
`http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` — open tracking may fail in clients that enforce HTTPS-only image loading.

**Viewport `maximum-scale=1`:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents user zoom on mobile. Accessibility violation (WCAG 1.4.4).

**Empty `<title>` tag:** `<title></title>` — affects web-view rendering and any browser tab display.
