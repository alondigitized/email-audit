# UP TO 70% OFF ❤️ Top Early Black Friday picks
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is the same burgundy "Early Black Friday, up to 70% off" Pottery Barn template that has run since 11/16, including "Don't miss Early Black Friday + UP TO 70% OFF" on 11/20. The only change is a product-grid block under the hero, with named hero items and real was/now prices. That makes it more concrete than the pure-banner sends before it. A subscriber who has seen five of these this week gets nothing new.
- The hierarchy is clean. The burgundy banner with a huge "70%" gets the eye first, then a zigzag of six category tiles with price anchors: Toscana Buffet $2,099 to $1,679, Corinna Quilt $299 to $150, Fireside Throw $59 to $41. The "Recommended just for you" block below is mostly generic holiday stockings and dining tables, so it doesn't read as personal. The long legal and sweepstakes footer is heavy but sits where footers belong.
- Verdict: a competent, well-built promo, but it's near-duplicate fatigue. The subject line is fine, and the creative is what drags it down. Roughly a 6.

## What's working

- The hero "up to 70% off" is large and unmistakable, with a visible SHOP NOW button.
- Tiles show was/now pricing on named products, which makes the discount feel real. The Corinna Quilt at $299 to $150 is the best example.
- "SHIPS FREE" badges on bedding and throws add a concrete incentive.
- The zigzag grid with one CTA per category is easy to scan and has no visible render bugs.
- The subject names the offer and the season, and the heart emoji adds some warmth.

## What's weak

- It is a near-clone of the 11/19 and 11/20 sends, so the cadence is exhausting. Nothing signals a new reason to open.
- The "up to 70%" headline is only reached by bedding, throws and bath. Furniture is 60% and sofas and gifts are 50%, so the hero oversells.
- "Recommended just for you" shows generic seasonal items with no visible personalization.
- Every tile has the same "Shop Now" link. There is no verb and noun pairing, and no urgency or end date is visible above the footer.
- The footer is very long, with sweepstakes, Affirm, credit card and exclusions. The Design Crew sweepstakes and the app and credit card blocks dilute the single sale message.

## Recommendations

- 1. Break the sameness. Send a distinct creative with an end-date countdown, or use a different hero such as "Last call: bedding 70% off". Pottery Barn is sending too many identical emails.
- 2. Make the hero honest and specific. Lead with the best-selling product at its real price, not a blanket "up to".
- 3. Make the recommendation block actually personal, based on browsing or category affinity, or cut it.
- 4. Replace the repeated "Shop Now" with specific CTAs such as "Shop quilts" or "Get the throw".
- 5. Move the sweepstakes, app and credit card blocks out of this send, or collapse them.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Throws $41, Teddy robe $47, bedding up to 70% off. Early access ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your early Black Friday picks, with free shipping on bedding and throws.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Pottery Barn's table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email has about 42 images without alt text, three `http://` image sources, and the recipient's address in image URLs. The List-Unsubscribe and Authentication-Results headers could not be checked because the relay didn't capture them. The truncated HTML and the skipped tracking links limit what can be confirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque click redirects:** The logo link is `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7ImQi...`. QA skipped 53 links as tracking/redirect domains, so destinations were not probed or verified.
- **Recipient address in pixel URLs:**
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  
  The plaintext address is exposed in URLs that can show up in proxy and server logs. The `svopn` pixel is also plain HTTP.
- **Multiple tracking pixels:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` (a third-party ad network pixel) and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/a85eee67-...`. The Turn pixel is likely blocked by privacy clients.
- **Cross-brand asset host:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a Pottery Barn email loading an asset from the West Elm domain, which is a template dependency on a sister brand's CDN.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (42 images per QA):** This covers most content images.
  - `slices_01–08.jpg` in `/pb/2026/US/1121_SAT_AM_EBF_ZigZag2/`.
  - `slices_01–18.jpg` in `/Email-Footer/`.
  - Six product images, e.g. `faux-fur-ruched-throw-l.jpg` and `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-125-l.jpg`.
  - The `H26_Bottom_*` banners.
  
  With images blocked, the email is largely blank to screen-reader and image-off users. Some of the 42 are tracking pixels where `alt=""` would be acceptable, so I can't confirm all are real defects.
- **Non-HTTPS images:** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`, and the `svopn` pixel. These may be blocked or flagged as mixed content.
- **Empty `<title></title>`** and no `lang` attribute on `<html>`.
- **Viewport:** `maximum-scale=1` prevents pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Stray PHP code in the CSS:** The second `<style>` block contains `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store...` as literal text. It is invalid CSS and a template leak. It sits after valid rules, so it likely only corrupts the trailing declarations, but it is sloppy.
- **Non-standard attribute selectors:** `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` match only when the class attribute is exactly that value, which is fragile.
- **Webfont:** The Google Fonts `<link>` for Montserrat is stripped by Gmail and several other clients, so a fallback stack is needed. I couldn't confirm one from the truncated source.
- **Missing table roles:** Some layout tables lack `role="presentation"`, e.g. the 20px spacer table and the outer wrappers. Others have it.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) are visible in the available source.
- The recipient address is resolved correctly into the two URLs above. See §2 for the privacy implication of embedding it in query strings.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Not found. QA notes the relay may strip them, so this is unverified rather than a confirmed defect. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check the raw message.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found. SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown from this data. The sending domain is `e.potterybarn.com`.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address:** The truncated HTML doesn't reach the footer, so I can't confirm either.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- No UTM parameters are visible. Links are wrapped in `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…` redirects, so campaign attribution is likely handled server-side inside the `qs` payload. I can't verify that.
- Product images are hosted on `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/…` rather than the email CDN (`edm.` or `image.e.`). Product-page changes could break those images, or the site's cache headers could affect them.
- Landing destinations were not verified because the redirect probes were skipped.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, starting with the `ZigZag2/slices_*` hero slices and the product images. Use `alt=""` for spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Move the `svopn` pixel, `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and the 2022 `slices_02.jpg` to HTTPS.
3. Remove the stray PHP `header(...)` text from the `<style>` block.
4. Fill in `<title>`, add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Stop putting the plaintext email in query strings. Use an opaque or hashed ID.
6. Host the `spacer_0.gif` on the Pottery Barn domain or replace it with CSS spacing.
7. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the authentication results from the raw message, bypassing the AgentMail relay. Verify the in-body unsubscribe link and postal address in the full HTML.
