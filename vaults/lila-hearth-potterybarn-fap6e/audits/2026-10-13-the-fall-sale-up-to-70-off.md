# The Fall Sale 🧡 UP TO 70% OFF
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-13**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth Pottery Barn Fall Sale send in roughly two days, and it is the plainest version so far. A dark-green hero says "The Fall Sale / Up to 70% off" with one SHOP NOW button. Below it sits a ten-tile category grid, then a "Recommended just for you" product block. The offer and CTA are clear and the layout is tidy. Nothing in it is new.
- The back half dilutes the message. It runs from the Christmas Shop banner to Design Crew free appointments, the app download, a credit card pitch and Affirm, and each one pulls attention from the sale. The hero is a lifestyle table shot with no price, product or deadline attached. This is a catch-all blast, the same shape as the 10/12 "Don't miss The Fall Sale (UP TO 70% OFF)" send. Without an urgency hook it reads as noise to anyone who got the earlier ones.
- Judgement: competent and on-brand, but it adds nothing over this week's earlier sends. It will get a modest open from brand recognition and a modest click from the grid.

## What's working

- The hero offer is unmistakable. "Up to 70% off" is large white type on dark green, with a visible SHOP NOW button.
- The category grid (Furniture, Sofas & Chairs, Bedding, Rugs and so on) gives easy entry points and ends with a "Shop All Sale" link.
- "Recommended just for you" shows named products such as the Banks Extendable Dining Table and the Remmy Swivel Chair, which gives some personalization and specificity.
- The top nav strip ("Discover 1400+ New Arrivals", "Shop In-Stock Furniture") offers a useful secondary path.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- Cadence is a problem. This is the fourth Fall Sale send in about two days, after "Don't miss The Fall Sale" and "Fall deals UP TO 70% OFF + Deals you won't want to miss!" on 10/12.
- There is no deadline or urgency anywhere in the render. The offer reads as open-ended.
- The Christmas Shop, Design Crew, app, credit card and Affirm blocks compete with the sale and dilute the primary CTA.
- Recommended products show no prices or sale markdowns, so the 70% claim never turns into a concrete deal.
- The legal and exclusions block is long and dense, which makes "up to 70%" feel less honest.
- The hero image is a generic living room with no product callout.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real deadline to the hero, such as "Ends Sunday", with a countdown or end-date line. This is the single biggest lift for an offer that has run all week.
- 2. Show sale prices on the recommended products ("Was $2,299, now $1,379"). That turns the percentage into something concrete.
- 3. Cut the footer promo stack to one secondary block, either Design Crew or the credit card. Move the Christmas Shop banner to its own send.
- 4. Reduce the number of near-identical sale sends, or give each a distinct angle such as category, deadline or in-stock.
- 5. Make the hero a specific product or category with a price point rather than a generic room.

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email is a table-based 650px XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with 55 tracked links, mostly image-slice links. The main gaps are pervasive missing alt text, three `http://` image sources, and no captured List-Unsubscribe or authentication headers. Header gaps may be relay artifacts rather than sender faults.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 55 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe (QA INFO). They go through `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` and `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/...`. Their destinations and liveness are unverified.
- Third-party tracking pixel: `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` (Turn/Amobee) is loaded as an image. It is an extra data-sharing endpoint.
- The open-tracking pixel `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is served over plain HTTP. It also carries the recipient's email address in cleartext in the query string.
- The `coreemail.html` image also carries the recipient's email in the query string (`https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`).

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Missing alt text:** about 50 images are flagged. Examples:
  - Hero link-farm slices `slices_01–12.jpg` under `1013_TUE_AM_FallSale_GraphicHeroLinkfarm/`.
  - Footer slices `slices_01–18.jpg` under `Email-Footer/`.
  - Product images such as `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg` and `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`.
  - Banners `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg` and `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`.
  - The spacer `spacer_0.gif`.
  - Image-only links with no alt text are unreadable to screen readers and to clients with images blocked. The hero and footer are sliced images, so most navigation would disappear with images off.
- **Insecure image sources (`http://`):**
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/...`
  - Some clients block or warn on mixed content.
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in the head.
- **Stray PHP code inside the `<style>` block:** `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store..."); header("Pragma: no-cache");` is literal text. It is invalid CSS and appears to be a template or server-side leak. It may break parsing of the following rules in strict clients.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom, an accessibility concern.
- **Cross-brand asset:** the spacer is hosted at `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`. This is a minor dependency on another brand's CDN.
- **Legacy asset:** `/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/` is a 2022 asset reused in a 2026 send.
- **Minor markup issues:**
  - Hidden preheader text uses `display:none` with `mso-hide:all`, which is fine.
  - The `<meta name="x-apple-disable-message-reformatting">` tag is not self-closed in an XHTML doctype.
  - The `max-width:650px` rule on `img` in the min-width: 700px query may not apply in all clients.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No unresolved merge tokens were found in the visible HTML. The recipient email is merged into the tracking-pixel and `coreemail.html` URLs. See section 2 for the PII-in-URL concern.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)** were not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Verify against the raw message. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers.
- **Authentication-Results:** it was not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The cause is likely the relay.
- The truncated HTML did not show the footer unsubscribe link or the physical postal address. They could not be verified from the supplied source.
- Plain-text part: 10,680 of 14,868 characters (about 72%) are URLs. The long tracking URLs produce a text alternative that spam filters may score poorly.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Links use opaque redirects (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`). UTM parameters and landing pages could not be verified from the source, because the QA tool skipped the redirect probes.
- Product image URLs point directly to `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`. These are not tracked links and do not affect landing continuity.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, especially the hero link-farm and footer slices. Use `alt=""` for spacers.
2. Switch the three `http://` image sources to `https://`, including the open pixel.
3. Remove the stray PHP `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block.
4. Fill in `<title>`, and drop `maximum-scale=1` so users can zoom.
5. Remove the recipient email from the URL query strings, or use an opaque ID.
6. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results against the raw message. Don't treat these as sender faults until then.
7. Resolve a sample of the `click.e.potterybarn.com` links to check destinations and UTM parameters.
8. Shorten the plain-text version, or reduce its URL density.
