# NOW ON SALE✨ Furniture, decor & more
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-16**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's broadest, least differentiated send of the week: a generic storewide "Now on Sale" banner sitting over a lifestyle shot of a leather chair, followed by a six-tile category grid (Furniture, Tabletop, Bedding, Bath, Outdoor, Pillows & Decor) with zero specific discounts anywhere in the render. Compare that to the brand's other recent sends — the Kravet collection launch or even the dining-table drop — which at least name products and tell a story; this one is pure category-catalog filler dressed up as a "sale."
- The execution is clean — no broken images, no overlapping text, consistent PB branding throughout — but the offer itself is vague to the point of being non-committal. "Now on Sale" with no percentage, no dollar figure, no BOGO, nothing time-bound. It reads like a placeholder for an actual promotion rather than the promotion itself.
- Given this is at least the sixth near-identical promotional Pottery Barn send in the recent history window, and it's the weakest on offer specificity of the bunch, this lands below the brand's already-middling average.

## What's working

- Hero section is visually clean — warm, well-lit lifestyle photography with a clear "Now on Sale" headline and an obvious "Shop Now" button.
- The "Recommended Just for You" module shows real, named products (Balboa Swivel Chair, Reeva Handwoven Rug, Capitola Wool Rug), which is more concrete than the category tiles above it.
- Loyalty/rewards messaging ("Earn 10% Back in Rewards") and Affirm financing are both surfaced, useful for a repeat PB shopper.
- No rendering defects — images load, layout is intact, footer/legal renders correctly.

## What's weak

- No concrete offer anywhere in the render — not one percentage, dollar amount, or BOGO callout despite "sale" being the entire premise of the email.
- The category grid (Furniture/Tabletop/Bedding/Bath/Outdoor/Pillows & Decor) is undifferentiated boilerplate — it could be reused for any week of the year with no edits.
- The Halloween Shop banner and Design Crew Sweepstakes insert feel bolted on and dilute the "sale" narrative the subject line promised — three competing messages in one email.
- CTA copy is generic throughout ("Shop Now," "Shop All Sale") rather than pointing to a specific category or product.
- This is functionally a repeat of the brand's recent cadence — a fourth-plus "browse everything" send this week with no new creative angle.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an actual number in the hero — "Up to 30% Off Furniture, Decor & More" instead of "Now on Sale." This is the single biggest lever; a vague sale headline suppresses both open and click intent.
- 2. Cut the category grid down to 2-3 tiles and attach a discount range to each (e.g., "Bedding — up to 25% off") instead of six identical unlabeled tiles.
- 3. Move or drop the Halloween Shop and Design Crew Sweepstakes blocks into separate sends — they compete with the sale message rather than support it.
- 4. Add specific pricing or "was/now" pricing to at least the "Recommended Just for You" products to give the click-through a reason to convert.
- 5. Add a deadline ("Ends Sunday") to create urgency that's currently absent entirely.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 30% off furniture & decor — today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Sale alert: your fall room refresh, discounted`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Save on the pieces on your list — furniture, bedding, and more, all discounted.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Storewide savings end soon. Shop before it's gone.`

# Technical Email Audit — Pottery Barn "NOW ON SALE" Campaign

## 1. Technical Summary
Email renders via a standard Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style ESP template (click.e.potterybarn.com redirects, MID-tagged tracking) with no blocking HTML errors, but automated QA flags gaps in unsubscribe/authentication header capture, widespread missing `alt` text, and several mixed-content (`http://`) image sources.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All primary CTAs route through the `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` redirect wrapper (Salesforce Marketing Cloud tracking format) — consistent, expected pattern for this ESP.
- QA skipped HTTP probing on 55 tracking/click-redirect links (expected behavior for redirect-domain links, not itself an error).
- Two open-tracking beacons present: `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` and `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1`. The `svopn.potterybarn.com` pixel is served over **plain HTTP**, which mail clients commonly block, causing this open-tracking pixel to silently fail.
- Secondary click tracker `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/7dbc40f6-69ed-4669-885a-c27c52e27d29?mi_mid=...&cp_tp` present and well-formed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **34 images missing `alt` text**, including all navigation/banner creative (`F26_Top_NewArrivals.jpg`, `F26_Bottom_HalloweenShop.jpg`, `F26_Top_DesignCrewSweeps_01a/b.jpg`), full product slice sets (`slices_01–18.jpg` in both the sale and footer modules), and product photography (`balboa-swivel-chair-l.jpg`, `reeva-handwoven-rug-3-l.jpg`, `belgian-flax-linen-curtain-l.jpg`, `400-thread-count-organic-percale-sheet-set-l.jpg`, `custom-capitola-hand-tufted-wool-rug-l.jpg`, `standard-curtain-rod-wall-bracket-l.jpg`). With images blocked by default in most mail clients, this leaves the email with no accessible fallback text for the majority of its content.

  - `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`

## 7. Recommendations

4. Move the open-tracking pixel `svopn.potterybarn.com` to HTTPS — it is currently non-functional in HTTPS-only mail clients.
5. Remove the stray PHP `header(...)` snippet embedded in the `<style>` block — dead code artifact from template source control.
6. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers exist in the actual delivered message (outside this relay) to confirm RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe compliance.
