# Don't miss your chance for EXTRA 20% OFF select styles
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-02**
## Executive summary

- This is the weakest kind of Pottery Barn send: the same oxblood "Extra 20% off Select Styles / Plus savings up to 70% off" banner as every other email this week (Oct 26–Nov 1). Only the subject line is new. "Don't miss your chance" implies a deadline, but nothing in the render states an end date, so the urgency is empty.
- The render itself is clean. The banner leads, the category grid is easy to scan, and a "Recommended Just for You" block follows with six products. The Christmas Shop tile and the Design Crew appointment banner give it some seasonal and service value. It is competent but undifferentiated, and a subscriber who got "Extra 20% off" on Nov 1 will see no reason to open this one.
- Verdict: a safe, reliable template send with no new idea. It is a 6–7 in execution but adds little over the Nov 1 sends, such as "A little holiday cheer!" and "Hosting soon?".

## What's working

- The hero offer is unmistakable: "Extra 20% off" in large serif type, with a single white "Shop All Sale" button.
- The eight-tile category grid (Furniture, Sofas & Chairs, Bedding, Rugs, and so on) lets a shopper jump straight to what they need.
- "Recommended Just for You" is a real product block with named items and sizes, such as the extendable dining table (76"–128") and the 13" mattress.
- The Christmas Shop tile fits early-November timing.
- The Design Crew "Book a Free Appointment" banner adds a useful non-discount CTA.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- No deadline appears anywhere in the render, so "Don't miss your chance" has nothing behind it.
- The banner is the same oxblood block seen in at least four prior sends this week, which breeds banner blindness.
- "Select styles", "Exclusions apply" and "Prices as marked" leave the shopper unsure which products qualify.
- The recommended grid has no prices, no sale badges and no ratings, so the 20% off never connects to a specific item.
- Below the hero, the email is long and generic: app download, credit card, Affirm and a dense legal block. The tail is all utility and dilutes the click path.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real end date and time to the banner and the subject, such as "Ends Tuesday at midnight PT". This single change would lift opens and clicks most.
- 2. Show sale prices and "was/now" on the six recommended products, with star ratings, so the discount reaches the item level.
- 3. Rotate the hero creative. A lifestyle or product hero would break the week-long run of the same banner.
- 4. Say which categories are included at the top, for example "Extra 20% off furniture, rugs, bedding".
- 5. Cut the app, card and Affirm blocks down to a single slim row.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: extra 20% off furniture & rugs`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call for your extra 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Dining tables, rugs and bedding are included. Sale ends at midnight PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus up to 70% off, and free design help if you book this week.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is an image-sliced, table-based layout with ESP-wrapped click tracking. The QA run shows 50% pass, 0 errors and 4 warnings. The main code-level problems are widespread missing alt text, cleartext HTTP image requests that carry the recipient's email address, and stray server-side code inside a `<style>` block. The supplied HTML is truncated, so the footer and unsubscribe link couldn't be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 54 tracking links were skipped as click-redirect domains (for example `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Imci...`). Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Cleartext pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is an HTTP open pixel. Clients may block or rewrite it, and open tracking is unreliable.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` is an additional ad-tech beacon (Turn) with no alt text.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is loaded from a West Elm host. This is template reuse that adds a dependency on another domain.
- **Opaque query strings:** The `qs=` tokens are opaque, so UTM parameters can't be confirmed (see section 6).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** About 45 images have no alt, per the QA data.
  - Hero slices: `slices_01–12.jpg` under `1102_MON_AM_Extra20StartsNowLinkFarmOverRed/`.
  - Product images: `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`, `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg`, `farmhouse-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`, and others.
  - Banners: `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg`, `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`.
  - Footer slices: `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`.
  - These are the only content carriers in an image-sliced layout, so with images blocked the email is blank and unreadable to screen readers. The logo does have `alt="POTTERY BARN"`.
- **HTTP image sources:** Three images use `http://` and may be blocked (mixed content or privacy proxies): `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`, and the `svopn` pixel.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility defect.
- **Stray PHP in CSS:** The second `<style>` block contains literal `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store..."); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. This is server-side code that leaked into the CSS. It is invalid CSS and can break parsing of the rules that follow it in some clients.
- **Legacy selectors:** `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` are attribute selectors that may not match elements with additional classes.
- **Plain-text part:** QA flags that 10,386 of 14,570 characters are URLs, about 71%. A text part that is mostly tracking URLs is a spam-filter and readability risk.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unrendered tokens:** No merge tokens (`%%`, `{{`, `${`) were observed, and the email address is substituted correctly.
- **PII in URLs:** The recipient's email appears in plain text in two image URLs:
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?...&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`

  The second one is sent over unencrypted HTTP, so the address is exposed in transit and in proxy logs.
- **Misused image slot:** `coreemail.html` is loaded as an `<img>` with no alt. It is an HTML page, not an image, so it is probably a tracking pixel.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** Both headers were not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unverified rather than a confirmed defect. If they are really absent, the email lacks RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe, which Gmail and Yahoo require for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** The header is missing, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The relay is the likely cause.
- **Footer:** The footer unsubscribe link and physical address are not in the truncated source, so they couldn't be checked.
- **Sender domain:** The From domain `e.potterybarn.com` matches the click domain `click.e.potterybarn.com`, which is good for alignment.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs unverifiable:** Links are wrapped in opaque `qs=` tokens, and the 54 wrapped links were not resolved. UTM presence and landing-page alignment can't be confirmed.
- **Product image hosts:** Product images load from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`, so they depend on the main site's availability. This is a minor risk.
- **Preheader code:** `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` and `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` are harmless in email.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, especially the hero slices, product images and banners. Use `alt=""` for spacers and pixels.
2. Serve all images over HTTPS, specifically `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` and the `svopn` pixel.
3. Stop putting the raw email address in URLs, especially over HTTP. Use a hashed or opaque subscriber ID.
4. Remove the leaked `header(...)` PHP lines from the `<style>` block.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta and fill in `<title>`.
6. Replace the `edm.westelm.com` spacer with an image on the sender's own host.
7. Check the raw message source for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, since the relay may have stripped them.
8. Resolve the tracking links to verify destinations and UTMs.
9. Add a proper text alternative to the plain-text part, so it isn't mostly tracking URLs.
