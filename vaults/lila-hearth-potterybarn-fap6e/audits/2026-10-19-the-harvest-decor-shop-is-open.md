# The Harvest Decor Shop is open
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a moody, well-art-directed seasonal launch. The dark "Harvest Decor Shop" hero with candles, pumpkins and a duck decoy looks like a magazine spread, and the brand voice is consistent. The problem is that it has no offer at all. It is also a diffuse send. It opens on harvest decor, then pivots to the Thanksgiving Shop, a "Recommended Just For You" grid of unrelated products, the Christmas Shop, Design Crew, the app, a credit card and Affirm. A subscriber who clicked for pumpkins ends up in a department-store directory.
- Compared with the last few sends, such as "Nightstands that add storage & style" (10/19) and "Just in! New arrivals for the holidays" (10/17), the pattern is the same: handsome catalog, no price hook. This one is slightly weaker on focus because the product grid has nothing to do with harvest. Fall decor ends up competing with Christmas, which is already pulling attention in mid-October.
- Verdict: strong mood, weak commerce. A real 5/10.

## What's working

- The dark hero and "THE HARVEST DECOR SHOP" headline set a clear seasonal mood.
- The 3x2 lifestyle photo grid (pumpkins, lanterns, dried florals) is cohesive and on-brand.
- The "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks" strip at the top is a useful, honest delivery promise.
- The quick-link tiles for Coffee Tables, Throws, Armchairs and Rugs give an easy route into other categories.
- The footer ties in Rewards, the app and Affirm, which gives members and financing-minded shoppers an option.

## What's weak

- There is no price, discount or shipping offer anywhere. The only reason to click is the mood.
- The 3x2 grid is unlabeled and has no prices, so a reader can't tell which items to click.
- "Recommended Just For You" shows a curtain rod, a dining table, a towel, a bed and a curtain. None of them are harvest or fall decor, and none feel personalized.
- Thanksgiving, Christmas, Design Crew, the app, the credit card and Affirm all compete after the hero. There are too many CTAs and no priority among them.
- The legal and fine-print block is dense and runs long, and the rewards fine print makes the "10% back" claim feel less honest.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add one concrete hook to the hero, such as "Free shipping on harvest decor" or "Up to 25% off fall accents", with a visible pill like the one used in the 10/18 pillows send.
- 2. Label the six grid images with product names and prices, and make each one a separate link.
- 3. Replace the generic recommendations with fall-relevant picks, such as faux stems, candle holders and table runners.
- 4. Cut the Christmas Shop, Design Crew and credit-card modules from this send. Keep one secondary CTA at most.
- 5. Add a deadline, such as "Fall decor ships free through Sunday".

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a sliced-image layout in XHTML 1.0 Transitional tables, with all links routed through click-redirect tracking. About 50 images have no `alt` text, 3 resources load over plain `http://`, and the recipient address appears in plaintext in pixel and image URLs. Compliance headers could not be verified (the QA warnings point to relay capture), and the HTML source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 58 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect URLs. One example is `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7ImQi...` on the logo. Destinations, redirect health, and final status codes are unconfirmed.
- **Non-HTTPS tracking pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` loads over HTTP. Clients may block it, which makes open tracking unreliable, and it exposes the address in transit.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` is an ad-network pixel with no `alt`. It is a privacy and deliverability consideration.
- **Multiple open pixels:** three trackers are present, `svopn.potterybarn.com`, `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/7417e2c2-...?mi_cid=...`, and `d.turn.com`. The `coreemail.html` image URL also carries the email address.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a spacer hosted on a different domain (West Elm). A failure there would only affect spacing.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both are absent in the captured message. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM, and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sender is `PotteryBarn@e.potterybarn.com`, and the links and pixels use the matching `click.e.`, `clickcp.`, and `svopn.` subdomains.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** not visible in the truncated source, so they could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** none are visible, because links are opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=` tokens. The only campaign identifiers are `sv_cid=0053_00597` and `mi_cid=677b44e6831b79f3`, which are on pixels, not links.
- **Landing pages:** these could not be assessed because the redirects were not resolved. Product images point to `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`, which is consistent with the brand domain.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the content slices, products, and banners, and `alt=""` to spacers and pixels. Add live text for key content, or at least the hero message.
2. Move the 3 `http://` assets (`Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, the 2022 brand banner `slices_02.jpg`, and the `svopn` pixel) to HTTPS.
3. Stop putting the recipient address in plaintext in image and pixel URLs. Use an opaque ID instead.
4. Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP text from the `<style>` block.
5. Fill in `<title>` and add `lang` to `<html>`. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
6. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` plus SPF, DKIM, and DMARC against the raw message at the source, not through the relay. Also confirm the footer unsubscribe link and postal address.
7. Resolve a sample of the tracking links, for example with a redirect-following probe, to check destinations and UTM carry-through.
8. Trim the plain-text part, or add real copy, to bring the URL share well under 70%.
