# Just in! 1400+ NEW ARRIVALS
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-26**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's broadest catalog crawl yet in this run — "Just in! 1400+ NEW ARRIVALS" abandons any single-category hook in favor of a category index: Pillows, Throws, Bedding, Lighting, Armchairs, Furniture, Decor, Tabletop & Bar, all stacked vertically with "Ships Free" badges scattered through. It's the opposite move from the Halloween Movie Magic send (2026-09-24, 7/10) that scored well specifically because it committed to one differentiated idea — this is width instead of a hook.
- The hero is clean (bright living room shot, "SHOP NOW" button, no render bugs), and the category-crawl format does let a subscriber self-select into whatever they care about. But "1400+ new arrivals" is a warehouse-inventory number, not an offer — there's no price, no percentage, no urgency anywhere in the visible fold. It reads as a housekeeping email ("here's everything we added") rather than a campaign.
- Verdict: competently produced, zero friction, but it's the least targeted, least offer-driven send in the run — a scroll-and-browse email for someone already loyal, not a click-driver.

## What's working

- Hero renders cleanly — bright, on-brand living room shot with unmissable "SHOP NOW" CTA.
- "Ships Free" badges appear on Pillows, Throws, and Bedding sections, giving at least a soft incentive per category.
- "RECOMMENDED JUST FOR YOU" module shows personalized-feeling product grid (curtain rods, platform bed, rugs, dining table) — a genuine segmentation signal.
- Halloween Shop and Design Crew banners at the bottom cross-sell without cluttering the main crawl.
- No overlapping text, broken images, or layout breaks anywhere in the render.

## What's weak

- No concrete offer anywhere in the visible email — no %, no $, no promo code; "1400+ new arrivals" is a volume claim, not value.
- Eight sequential category sections (Pillows→Throws→Bedding→Lighting→Armchairs→Furniture→Decor→Tabletop) is a long, undifferentiated scroll with no visual hierarchy pulling the eye to one thing.
- "Just in!" subject/hero copy is generic — it could describe literally any weekly inventory refresh, no seasonal or persona hook.
- Coming one day after the quilts/bedding-heavy sends (2026-09-24) and Halloween hub (2026-09-25), this reads like more inventory noise layered on top of an already-busy week.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with one hero category and one offer, then link out to the rest.** Pick the single strongest new-arrivals category (Furniture looks like the visual standout — that Chesterfield sofa) and give it the top fold with a real price or discount; downgrade the other seven categories to a compact grid below instead of full-width sections each.
- 2. **Add a number that means something to the reader, not the warehouse.** "1400+ new arrivals" → swap for a benefit-framed number ("Free shipping on 200+ new bedding styles") or drop it in favor of a specific hero product.
- 3. **Tighten the scroll.** Collapse Pillows/Throws/Bedding/Lighting/Armchairs/Furniture/Decor/Tabletop & Bar into a single 2x4 category tile grid — same information, a third of the scroll.
- 4. **Cadence check** — this is the third bedding/furniture-adjacent catalog send in four days; if the calendar allows, space this out from the 9/24 quilts email.
