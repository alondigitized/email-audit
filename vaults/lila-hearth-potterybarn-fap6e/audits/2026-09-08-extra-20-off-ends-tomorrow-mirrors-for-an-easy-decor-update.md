# Extra 20% off ENDS TOMORROW + Mirrors for an easy decor update
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-08**
## Executive summary

- This is the strongest execution I've seen from Pottery Barn's burgundy Labor Day run — same banner up top ("Extra 20% off / Select styles. Plus new deals up to 70% off"), but this time it's paired with an actual dedicated category moment: "Reflect Your Style / Shop Mirrors," followed by five distinct design-tip modules (standout shapes, contrast in tones, texture as statement) each with real named products (Bondi, Newport, Carmel, Stowe, Malibu, Marble, Seabrook). It reads like a genuine mirror lookbook, not a generic sale wrapper.
- The subject line is doing real work too — "ENDS TOMORROW" is a credible, time-bound urgency claim (a shift from the vaguer "48 hours left" framing on 2026-09-08's other send), and pairing it with "Mirrors for an easy decor update" gives it a concrete secondary hook instead of just another sale repeat. This is the best-targeted subject in the recent run for someone whose focus is home decor.
- Where it falls down is cadence and scroll depth: this is at minimum the thirteenth burgundy Labor Day banner in two weeks, and the mirror content — while good — is buried under a long scroll before you even reach "Recommended Just For You" and the Halloween shop. A subscriber who's seen this banner daily will tune out before reaching the good part.

## What's working

- Clear category focus: "Reflect Your Style / Shop Mirrors" hero with a full-bleed lifestyle shot of leaning floor mirrors.
- Five well-organized design-tip modules, each with two-across product callouts and named SKUs (Bondi, Newport, Carmel, Stowe, Metal Cladded, Malibu, Marble, Seabrook).
- Subject line urgency ("ENDS TOMORROW") feels more concrete and credible than this week's vaguer "48 hours left" framing.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text doesn't overlap, layout is consistent top to bottom.
- Strong bottom-of-funnel structure: More Inspiring Decor cross-sell, Recommended Just For You carousel, Halloween teaser, Design Crew CTA, app/loyalty footer.

## What's weak

- The burgundy sale banner is still the exact same template run daily for two weeks — a subscriber has seen this dozens of times now.
- Primary hero CTA is "Shop All Sale," not "Shop Mirrors" — the mirror content (the actual differentiator) doesn't get top billing until the second module.
- Very long single-scroll email (8+ modules deep before Halloween/footer) with no anchor nav or "jump to" — mobile users likely won't reach the Recommended or Halloween sections.
- "Labor Day Sale" banner copy is now stale relative to the calendar — Labor Day itself has passed, and running it into a second week reads like leftover promo language rather than a live campaign.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap hero CTA priority: lead with "Shop Mirrors" as the primary button and push "Shop All Sale" to secondary — the mirror content is what differentiates this send from the prior dozen, so it should own the top fold.
- 2. Retire "Labor Day Sale" banner copy — reframe as "End of Summer / Extra 20% Off" to stop signaling a stale, passed holiday.
- 3. Trim the module count — five design-tip pairs is a lot of scroll for one email; cut to three tightest pairings and move the rest into a follow-up send.
- 4. Move the Halloween teaser higher (or spin it into its own send) — it's currently buried under mirrors, dressers, beds, and dining tables, most subscribers won't scroll that far.

## Technical Audit: Pottery Barn — "Extra 20% off ENDS TOMORROW" Email

### 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard ESP-generated (Bronto/Cheetah-style, `e.potterybarn.com`) HTML template with functioning redirect-based click tracking, but it fails baseline unsubscribe/authentication header capture, ships two plaintext HTTP image assets, and is missing `alt` text on effectively every image in the document.

  - `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` — third-party ad-retargeting beacon (Amobee/Turn), fires on open with no visible disclosure in the payload.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **40+ images are missing `alt` text**, including primary content banners (`F26_Top_LaborDayEndsTomorrow.jpg`, all 20 `slices_XX.jpg` mirror roundup images, footer slices, product images). Screen readers and blocked-image fallbacks will render no descriptive text for nearly the entire email.

This has no effect in an HTML/CSS context and won't render or execute; it's leftover copy-paste debris from a server-side template, not a functional bug, but indicates unclean template hygiene.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- The recipient identifier `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is embedded in plaintext in at least two URLs (the HTTP open pixel and `coreemail.html?email=...` customer-service link). This is standard ESP personalization-via-querystring behavior, but combined with the unencrypted HTTP pixel above, the address is transmitted without TLS.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** — one-click unsubscribe via mail client UI is not available; only a body-level unsubscribe link (not visible in the truncated source) may be relied upon.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found** — RFC 8058 one-click POST unsubscribe is not supported, which Gmail/Yahoo increasingly require for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be confirmed from this capture; this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not preserving the header rather than an actual authentication failure, but it cannot be verified either way from available data.
- Physical mailing address / footer compliance elements are not visible in the truncated HTML and cannot be assessed.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No standard `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` parameters are visible on any link; all tracked links use opaque ESP redirect tokens (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`), so campaign attribution and landing-page alignment cannot be verified from the HTML alone — this depends on server-side redirect mapping not present in this capture.
- The customer-service link (`www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=...`) does resolve to the brand's live domain, consistent with expected destination.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` attributes to all content-bearing images (banners, product shots, mirror roundup slices) — currently a full accessibility gap.
2. Migrate the two HTTP-only image sources and the `svopn.potterybarn.com` open-tracking pixel to HTTPS to avoid client-side blocking and to stop transmitting recipient email addresses unencrypted.
3. Implement `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers to support one-click unsubscribe — increasingly required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender rules.
4. Remove the inert `header(...)` PHP-style statements from the `<style>` block in `<head>` — dead code with no functional effect, but should be cleaned from the template source.
5. Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status independently (e.g. via a raw header capture outside the AgentMail relay), since `Authentication-Results` was not observable here.
