# Nightstands that add storage & style
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's nightstand category email: a single-product hero, then a tidy 8-item grid. It is clean and easy to scan, and the "Do-It-All Nightstands" headline matches the subject. The layout is competent but low-energy. The only price cue is a small "STARTING AT $349" tag tucked into the hero corner. There is no sale, deadline or member benefit to pull the click.
- The cadence works against it. Yesterday, 9/28, this brand sent "Furniture & more for your cozy bedroom", which already led with the Cayman bed and a SHOP BEDS button. Today Cayman shows up again as a nightstand tile, so a subscriber sees the same bedroom story twice in two days. It also lands amid the 9/27–9/28 run of sale, rug, bedding and Thanksgiving sends, so it reads as another routine category push.

## What's working

- The hero headline "Do-It-All Nightstands" matches the subject's storage-and-style promise, so subject and email agree.
- The 8-tile grid (Sausalito, Farmhouse, Cayman, Oakleigh, Reed, Rylee, Calistoga, Summerville) gives clear product choice. The uniform white-background photography keeps it easy to compare.
- The "SHOP ALL NIGHTSTANDS" button after the grid is clear verb-plus-noun copy with a visible button.
- The "Complete Your Retreat" text grid (Beds, Dressers, Mattresses, Sheets, Duvets, Quilts) gives useful cross-sell paths without clutter.
- The layout renders cleanly, with no broken images, overlapping text or gaps.

## What's weak

- There is no real offer. "STARTING AT $349" is a price floor, not a savings, and it is small and low-contrast in the hero's bottom corner.
- The primary hero CTA is generic "SHOP NOW". The "SHOP BEDROOM FURNITURE ›" link right below it competes with the button.
- The product tiles are name-only. There are no prices, materials, dimensions or ratings, so nothing helps a shopper decide before clicking.
- There is no urgency or deadline anywhere in the email.
- The bottom third is heavy: the recommended grid, Halloween Shop, Design Crew, app download, rewards card, Affirm and a wall of fine print. It dilutes a focused nightstand message and hides the one loyalty perk (10% back) far down the page.

- 1. Add a concrete, time-bound hook to the hero, such as a nightstand price drop, free shipping, or "In stock, ships by [date]". Make it large and put it beside the button, not in a corner tag.
- 2. Change the hero button to "SHOP NIGHTSTANDS" and drop the competing "SHOP BEDROOM FURNITURE" link.
- 3. Add a price and one differentiator to each tile, for example "$349 · 2 drawers · solid wood", plus star ratings on the best sellers.
- 4. Cut the lower blocks to one, or move the 10% back rewards mention up near the grid. Right now it is buried below Halloween and Design Crew.
- 5. Vary the creative from the 9/28 bedroom send. Drop the repeated Cayman look, or make the nightstands lead a genuinely different angle such as small-space storage.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Pottery Barn, "Nightstands that add storage & style"

## 1. Technical Summary
Table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with hidden preheader, all links wrapped in click-redirect tracking, and no critical failures (0 issues, 4 warnings, 50% pass rate). The main problems are pervasive missing alt text, three `http://` image sources, and the recipient's email address appearing in plain-text query strings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 62 tracking links (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`) were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destination validity and redirect chains are **unverified**. No broken links are confirmed.
- Some tracking pixels are served over plain HTTP: `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...`. HTTPS-only clients may block or rewrite it, which would undercount opens.
- There are also third-party pixels: `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` (alt="1") and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/0aa988b6-...?mi_cid=...`.
- The plain-text part is more than 70% URLs (11,194 of 15,418 chars). This is a spam-filter and readability risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text** is flagged on roughly 60 images, including:
  - Nearly all sliced content images in `W26_Furniture_B11_NightstandRoundUp/slices_01–09, 12–21.jpg`.
  - All 18 `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`.
  - Product images: `reeva-handwoven-rug-3-l.jpg`, `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`, `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`, and others.
  - With images blocked, the sliced layout renders as blank space and the CTAs are unreadable to screen readers.
- **Non-HTTPS images:**
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - Both may be blocked as mixed content.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **`<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>`** disables pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Stray non-CSS in `<style>`:** PHP-style lines (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ...")`) sit inside a `<style>` block. They are invalid CSS and could break parsing of the rules that follow in strict clients.
- **Attribute selectors:** `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` are exact-match and fragile.
- **Font import:** the Google Fonts `<link>` (Montserrat) is dropped by most clients, including Gmail, so this is harmless but has no effect.
- **`spacer_0.gif` cross-brand host:** it loads from `edm.westelm.com`, which is a hosting dependency on a different brand's domain.
- **Stale asset path:** `/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/` is a 2022 asset in a 2026 send.
- The layout tables use `role="presentation"`, which is correct.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) are visible in the truncated source.
- The recipient email is resolved into URLs as plain text:
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `...svopn.potterybarn.com/?...&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
- Because the second URL is over HTTP, the address is exposed in cleartext. Email-in-URL leaks to logs and proxies.
- `coreemail.html` is loaded as an `<img>` (a tracking or core-email pixel) without alt text.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe** was not found, and the QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip it. It is **unconfirmed, not proven absent**.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)** was not found, so one-click unsubscribe is unconfirmed. This matters under the Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **Authentication-Results** was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is **unknown**. The From domain `e.potterybarn.com` matches the tracking domains, which suggests aligned sending, but this is not verified.
- The body unsubscribe link and physical address are in the footer region, which the truncated HTML doesn't reach, so they can't be confirmed here.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters cannot be verified. All links are opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=` redirects that were not resolved, so campaign tagging and landing-page alignment are **unconfirmed**.
- Product images do resolve to `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...` on the production domain.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to all content and footer slices, and `alt=""` to decorative and tracking images.
2. Serve all images over HTTPS: `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, the 2022 `slices_02.jpg`, and `svopn.potterybarn.com`. Remove the 2022 legacy asset.
3. Remove the PHP `header()` text from the `<style>` block, and fill in `<title>`.
4. Drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Stop putting the raw recipient email in URL query strings, especially over HTTP. Use an opaque ID.
6. Trim the plain-text part, or shorten the URLs in it, to lower the URL ratio.
7. Verify the sending-side headers (List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, SPF, DKIM, DMARC) against the raw original message, not the relay copy.
8. Resolve a sample of the tracked links to confirm UTM tagging and that the destinations are live.
