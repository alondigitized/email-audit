# On sale & delivered for free
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a soft-sale home-textiles send. The hero reads "on sale & shipping free / Save on Easy Updates" over a cozy pillow-and-throw photo. Its best move is the "shipping free" promise, which it repeats in the subject and in the top banner "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks ›". That is a real, if modest, benefit. It also differs from the oxblood "Extra 20% off" banner that led nearly every send from Oct 26 to Nov 3.
- The offer is vague. There is no percentage, no dollar amount and no deadline, so "on sale" asks the reader to take it on faith. The eye lands on the hero headline, but nothing there quantifies the value. The category grid (Pillows, Throws, Quilts, Duvets, Table Linens, Sheets, Towels, Curtains) is clean and scannable. The "Recommended just for you" block then drifts into Christmas stockings, curtain rods, a dining table and a bed. That undercuts the textile-refresh message.
- Overall it is a competent, tidy send with weak urgency. It sits in the middle of the pack, close to the recent 5 to 6 scores. The "free shipping" angle is the hook. The execution doesn't back it up with a number, a date or a single hero product.

## What's working

- "Delivered for free" in the subject and "shipping free" in the hero give one concrete, easy-to-grasp benefit.
- The hero is warm and on-season, with a single "SHOP NOW" button.
- The eight-tile category grid plus "SHOP ALL SALE" lets a shopper jump straight to their category.
- The "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks" banner addresses a real delivery-time concern.
- The Christmas Shop and Design Crew modules reinforce the seasonal and service story, and tie back to the Nov 3 free design services send.

## What's weak

- The "on sale" claim has no percentage or dollar amount, so the offer is not quantified.
- There is no deadline or urgency language anywhere, so there is no reason to act today.
- The "Recommended just for you" grid mixes stockings, curtain hardware, a dining table and a bed. It doesn't follow the pillows-and-linens hero, and four of the six items show no prices.
- The email is long, with several secondary modules (app, credit card, Affirm) stacked below the fold. The legal footer is dense, and the "promotional exclusions" fine print sits at the very bottom.
- The "shipping free" condition is unclear. The footer exclusions suggest limits that the hero doesn't mention.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the actual offer in the hero, for example "Up to 30% off bedding & decor + free shipping", and add an end date.
- 2. Make the recommendations match the hero. Use five or six sale textiles with sale prices visible, instead of mixed furniture and stockings.
- 3. Add a credible deadline such as "Ends Sunday" or "Free shipping through 11/8".
- 4. Cut the stacked app, credit card and Affirm modules to one line, so the sale stays the focus.
- 5. Put the shipping conditions (for example a minimum spend) right under the hero CTA, so the free-shipping claim feels honest.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit: Pottery Barn, "On sale & delivered for free"

### 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, image-sliced layout with no unresolved merge tokens in the visible source. It has widespread missing alt text, three insecure `http://` image calls (one carrying the recipient's email address), and leaked server-side code in a `<style>` block. The unsubscribe headers and authentication results could not be verified.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link validation:** 52 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, so destinations and redirect chains are unverified. Example: `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...`. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` loads over plain HTTP. It may be blocked by clients, so opens could be undercounted. The recipient email is also sent unencrypted in the query string.
- **Third-party pixels:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/1d5bb17e-...?mi_cid=...` are additional beacons. They are noted, not defects.
- **Email in a URL:** `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is loaded as an `<img>` and puts the address in the URL.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 45 images are flagged. Examples:
  - Hero and body slices: `H25_TOP_InStockFurniture.jpg` and `0822_FRI_AM_LTOSale_FreeShip1/slices_01–07,10–12.jpg`.
  - Product images: `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg` and `farmhouse-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`.
  - Footer slices: `Email-Footer/slices_01, 03–18.jpg`.
  - With images off or in a screen reader, the offer and navigation are unreadable.
- **Insecure image sources:**
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - Both may be blocked as mixed content. The second also points to a 2022 asset path, so it is likely stale.
- **Leaked PHP in CSS:** the second `<style>` block ends with `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store..."); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. This is invalid CSS and shows a templating or build artifact. It is harmless to rendering today but is fragile.
- **Empty `<title>`:** `<title></title>`.
- **Viewport:** `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on another brand's domain, so it is a dependency risk. It also has no alt.
- **Structure:** `role="presentation"` appears on some layout tables but not all, for example the bare `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0">` spacer. The plain-text part is more than 70% URLs (9939 of 14111 characters), which is a minor deliverability signal.
- **Fonts:** Montserrat is loaded via a Google Fonts `<link>`, which many clients strip. No issue if a fallback stack is defined.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved tokens (`%%`, `{{ }}`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible source. The tokens that did render show the recipient address in three URLs (`sv_sveme=` and `?email=`). See Section 2.
- The HTML source is truncated, so the footer and body are not fully verifiable.

### 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** neither header was found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. The From domain `e.potterybarn.com` aligns with the click and image subdomains.
- **Footer:** the visible source is truncated before the footer. The unsubscribe link and physical address could not be verified.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- All 52 tracked links are opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=` redirects, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment cannot be checked from the source. Direct `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...` references are image assets, not landing URLs.
- No UTM parameters are visible in any exposed URL. They may be applied inside the redirects.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to all content images, especially hero and offer slices and footer navigation. Use `alt=""` for spacers and pixels.
2. Move `svopn.potterybarn.com` and the two `edm.potterybarn.com` `http://` assets to HTTPS.
3. Stop passing the recipient email in plain-text query strings on pixels and image URLs. Use hashed or opaque IDs.
4. Remove the stray PHP `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block.
5. Populate `<title>`, remove `maximum-scale=1`, and add `role="presentation"` to the remaining layout tables.
6. Replace the stale 2022 banner and the West Elm-hosted spacer with Pottery Barn-hosted current assets.
7. Re-run compliance checks against the raw MIME message to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Also check the footer unsubscribe link and postal address in the untruncated HTML.
8. Resolve a sample of the `click.e.potterybarn.com` links with a browser or HTTP trace to confirm landing pages and UTMs.
