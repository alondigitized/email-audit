# Just in! New arrivals for the holidays 🎁
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, seasonal new-arrivals send with no offer. The "NEW ARRIVALS" hero is a plaid-bedding room shot with a SHOP NOW button, and a strip above it says "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks." The subject's gift emoji and "for the holidays" match the Christmas styling. It looks like Pottery Barn and reads as the start of holiday season.
- It is also a long catalog scroll with seven or eight competing destinations and no reason to click today. The tile grid (Pillows & Throws, The Christmas Shop, Bedding, Lighting, Armchairs, Furniture, Tabletop & Bar, Decor) is pretty but undifferentiated. A "Recommended Just for You" block, a second Christmas Shop banner, Design Crew, the app, a credit card and Affirm follow. Compared with the Oct 16 "Love pattern? This bedding is for you" send, which had a "Quilts ship free" pill and named hero products, this one is softer. There are only two small shipping signals ("Ships free" on a couple of tiles and the in-stock strip), and they are easy to miss.
- Verdict: a competent brand-building send, but it is a middling driver of action. It is a 5 or 6 because the offer is absent and the focus is diffuse.

## What's working

- The plaid-bedding hero is warm and on-season, and the SHOP NOW button is clear.
- The subject's "Just in!" and the 🎁 emoji give novelty and a holiday cue.
- The "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks" strip answers a real holiday-timing worry.
- The category tiles are consistently styled, and the "Ships free" labels on pillows/throws and bedding add a small incentive.
- "Recommended Just for You" gives named products: Cayman Platform Bed, Toscana Extendable Dining Table, Banks Extendable Dining Table, Emery Linen Curtain and Reeva Handwoven Rug.

## What's weak

- There is no headline offer, no percentage or dollar-off, and no deadline.
- The "Ships free" and "In-Stock" signals are tiny and sit on only some tiles, so shoppers can't tell what qualifies.
- The email is too long, with eight category tiles, a recommendations grid and then four more promo blocks. The Christmas Shop appears twice.
- The recommendations show no prices, reviews or ratings, and the products feel unrelated to the holiday hero.
- The footer is dense with legal copy, and the credit card and Affirm modules dilute the close.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one concrete incentive in the hero, such as "Free shipping on holiday arrivals" or "Order by Dec X for Christmas delivery." This would lift the email most.
- 2. Name two or three hero products in the first screen instead of a generic category grid.
- 3. Cut the modules after the grid: the second Christmas Shop banner, the app and the Affirm blocks. Keep Design Crew only if it is tied to holiday hosting.
- 4. Add prices or star ratings to the recommended products, and make the "Ships free" pills larger and consistent across tiles.
- 5. Make the CTA specific: "Shop new holiday arrivals" rather than "SHOP NOW."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard table-based, 650px ESP template. The main defects are widespread missing `alt` attributes on sliced images, several plain-`http://` image sources, and stray PHP code inside a `<style>` block. Compliance and authentication could not be verified, because the headers weren't captured by the relay and the HTML source is truncated before the footer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link health is unverified.** 53 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, e.g. `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7ImQi...`. Their destinations are opaque tokens, so I can't confirm they resolve.
- **Insecure tracking pixel.** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` is loaded over plain HTTP. Clients that block mixed or insecure content will drop it, and the open event won't be recorded.
- **Third-party pixel.** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` is an ad-network beacon (Turn/Amobee), alongside the first-party `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/d389b8b7-...` open pixel. This is not a defect, but it adds third-party requests.
- **Cross-brand asset.** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on the West Elm domain. If that asset is removed, the spacer breaks.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` text (about 45 of the QA warnings).** This covers all hero and product slices, for example:
  - `.../1017_SAT_AM_NewArrivalsClassicHoliday/slices_01.jpg` through `slices_11.jpg`
  - `.../Email-Footer/slices_01.jpg` through `slices_18.jpg`
  - product images such as `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg` and `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-1245-1-l.jpg`

  With images blocked or read by a screen reader, the email is blank or unintelligible. The header logo does have `alt="POTTERY BARN"`.
- **Tracking pixels without `alt`.** `coreemail.html`, `svopn`, `turn.com` and `clickcp` have no `alt`. These should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Non-HTTPS images.** Three images use `http://`:
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - the `svopn` pixel

  They may be blocked or flagged as mixed content.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** Some clients and screen readers use the title.
- **Stray PHP in CSS.** The second `<style>` block contains `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store, ...");` and similar lines. This is server-side code that leaked into the CSS. It is invalid, and it can break rule parsing in strict clients, since the parser may swallow the next declaration.
- **Pinch-zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1">` blocks zooming on mobile, which is an accessibility failure (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Heavy slicing.** Roughly 30 stacked image slices make rendering depend on image loading. That worsens the alt-text problem above.
- **Google Fonts `<link>`.** `fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Montserrat` is stripped by Gmail and Outlook, so there will be a font fallback. This is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved merge tokens** (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) in the visible source.
- **Recipient email in cleartext URLs.** The address `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` appears in query strings: `.../customer-service/coreemail.html?email=...` and `...svopn...&sv_sveme=...`. The `svopn` URL is also plain HTTP, so the address travels unencrypted. This is a privacy and data-leak concern.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes the relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. If they are really absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not captured**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `e.potterybarn.com` aligns with the click domain `click.e.potterybarn.com`, which is a good sign but not proof.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and physical address are unverified.** The source is truncated before the footer. The only footer evidence is the sliced `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg` images, which may carry the text as images, with no `alt` and no plain-text equivalent. If so, that would also be an accessibility problem.
- **Plain-text part:** 10,192 of 14,362 characters (71%) are URLs, which is slightly above the 70% threshold. This is a minor spam-filter risk.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters can't be verified.** Every CTA is wrapped in an opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=` token, and the probe skipped them all. Campaign tags and landing-page alignment can't be confirmed from the source.
- **Product images** point to `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`, a first-party host that is consistent with the brand. The destinations are not checkable.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content slices and product images. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels and spacers.
2. Move the three `http://` assets to HTTPS, in particular the `svopn` pixel.
3. Remove the stray PHP `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta and fill in `<title>`.
5. Stop putting the recipient email address in image and pixel query strings. Use an opaque hashed ID instead.
6. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results from raw headers rather than the relay copy. Re-run the audit on the full HTML to check the footer unsubscribe link and postal address.
7. Resolve a sample of the redirect links with a follow-redirect probe to confirm UTM tags and landing pages.
8. Host the spacer on `edm.potterybarn.com` instead of the West Elm domain.
