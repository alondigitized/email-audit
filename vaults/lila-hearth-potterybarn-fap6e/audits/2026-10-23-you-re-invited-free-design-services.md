# You're invited: FREE design services
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-23**
## Executive summary

- This is a service-invite send, and it's a welcome break from the sale blast. Over the last two days Pottery Barn sent three near-identical "Extra 20% off select styles" emails (10/22 "STARTS NOW 📣", "ON NOW!", and 10/23 "❤️ Take an EXTRA 20% OFF"). "RSVP for Your Free Design Services" with a single "BOOK A FREE APPOINTMENT" button gives a different reason to open. The hero is clean and the what/when/where structure is easy to scan.
- The email then spends most of its length on something else. Three "Bring this look to life" room vignettes are decent inspiration, but they dilute the appointment ask. They are followed by a Holiday Open House banner, a "Recommended just for you" product grid with no prices, and card and Affirm promos. It reads as the standard Pottery Barn template with a design-services hero bolted on. The result is a good concept with a mushy middle.
- My verdict is that this is a solid, relevant send that undersells its own offer. The free service is the only differentiator, and the email never says what you get, how long it takes, or why it's worth the time.

## What's working

- The hero is a clear three-part pitch (what, when, where) with one dark, high-contrast "BOOK A FREE APPOINTMENT" button.
- The "Where" line (in your home, virtually, or in-store) removes a real objection to booking.
- The room vignettes (green living room, dining room, gallery wall) show the kind of outcome a designer could deliver, and the "BRING THIS LOOK TO LIFE" links are specific.
- The CTA is repeated after the vignettes, so a scroller doesn't have to go back to the top.
- It breaks the 10/22-10/23 run of repeated 20%-off sends with a different message.

## What's weak

- The hero text sits on a busy, washed-out photo. The gray "what / when / where" copy is low-contrast and hard to read on a phone.
- The "Holiday Open House" banner has pale, low-contrast headline text over a bright image. It gives a date and time (Saturday, November 7th, 10AM) but no location, and the "RSVP TODAY" button competes with the main appointment CTA.
- The urgency is vague. "Choose a time—but spots go fast!" has no deadline, no number of slots, and no date.
- The "Recommended just for you" grid has no prices or offers, so it adds length without adding a reason to click. The card and Affirm promos and the long legal footer also weigh the email down.
- The email never says what the service includes (a free consult, a room plan, product recommendations) or whether there's any purchase requirement.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the vague hero copy with a concrete value statement, for example "Free 1:1 room plan from a Design Crew pro, 45 minutes, no purchase required." Name the deliverable and keep the same button.
- 2. Give the hero real urgency. "Book by Nov 7" or "Only X slots left this month" is better than "spots go fast."
- 3. Cut or relocate the Holiday Open House block. It's a second event with a second RSVP. If it stays, add the location and use a solid overlay so the headline is readable.
- 4. Drop the price-less product grid, or add prices and a tie-in to the offer. "Pieces our designers pick most" would be on-theme.
- 5. Put a dark scrim behind the hero text, or set it in solid white or black, so it's legible on mobile.
- 6. Move the card and Affirm promos below the footer links, or send them separately.

- **Subject Alt B:** `RSVP: free design help, in-home or virtual`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `45 minutes with our Design Crew. At home, virtual, or in-store. Book before Nov 7.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Room refresh, total makeover, or just a few styling tips. Pick a time that works.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, 650px fixed-width SFMC send. Its main code-level problems are junk text inside a `<style>` block, empty alt text on linked images, and recipient email addresses in plaintext in third-party pixel URLs. The header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be confirmed from the data given.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Destinations unverified.** 46 links go through `click.e.potterybarn.com/?qs=…`, an opaque SFMC redirect. The QA probe skipped all of them, so destinations, redirect status and 404s are unconfirmed.
- **Dead code in the markup.** A commented-out block (the "10% back in rewards" credit-card banner) still ships a direct `potterybarn.com/pages/credit-card/?…&cm_ite=PB_header_nonmember` URL. It isn't rendered, but it adds weight and exposes internal tracking params.
- **Third-party pixels.** The email loads `secure.leadback.advertising.com/adcedge/lb?…`, `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/…/cat/1` and `svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=…&sv_emopen=true`. The first two are ad-network retargeting beacons embedded in an email, which clients with tracking protection will block.
- **Stale and cross-brand assets.**
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` is a 2022 asset.
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` is a legacy "thekey" path.
  - `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on the West Elm domain.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid CSS in the second `<style>` block.** It contains literal PHP text: `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store…"); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. This sits directly before `a[x-apple-data-detectors] {…}`, so a CSS parser will likely read the junk and the selector as one invalid prelude and drop the rule. Apple's auto-linking override (phone numbers, addresses) would then not apply.
- **Responsive rules appear dead.** The media query targets `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]`. In the visible source, the layout tables and images (`slices_01.jpg` and others) carry no `class` attribute, and every table is hard-coded `width="650"`. The email likely won't scale below 650px except through client-side scaling. The source is truncated, so I can't rule out classes further down.
- **Empty alt text on linked images.** `slices_01.jpg` through `slices_07.jpg` (`/1023_FRI_AM2_DC_MoodBoard/`) are each the sole content of an `<a href="https://click.e.potterybarn.com/…">` and have `alt=""`. The links have no accessible name, and with images off the hero body is blank. The same applies to the footer `Email-Footer/slices_01`–`18.jpg`, and the product images lack alt entirely (`banks-extendable-dining-table-…`, `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`, `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg` and others). The header logo is fine (`alt="POTTERY BARN"`).
- **Mixed content.** Two images use `http://` (`Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, the 2022 `slices_02.jpg`) and may be blocked or flagged.
- **Markup validity.** The doctype is XHTML 1.0 Strict, but the body uses `<center>`, `bgcolor`, `align` and `border`, which are invalid under Strict. Comments precede the `<!DOCTYPE>`. Both are low-impact.
- **Webfont.** The Google Fonts `<link>` for Montserrat is stripped by Gmail and other clients, and the visible text relies on images anyway.
- **QA alt warnings that are not real a11y issues.** The tracking pixels (`coreemail.html`, `svopn…`, `lb`, `1`) don't need alt text.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found. The QA tool says AgentMail may not capture these headers, so this is **unconfirmed, not a confirmed defect**. Verify against the raw message.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sending domain `e.potterybarn.com` can't be assessed from this data.
- **Body unsubscribe link and postal address:** not visible in the truncated source, so they can't be confirmed either way.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- UTM params can't be assessed. All CTAs use opaque `?qs=` tokens, and the probe skipped them.
- The commented-out block uses `cm_ite=` and `marketingChannelCode=` rather than UTMs, which suggests the brand's own tracking scheme. Landing-page alignment with the "FREE design services" subject is unverified.
- The asset path `1023_FRI_AM2_DC_MoodBoard` matches the 10/23 send date, so no stale-campaign mismatch there.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the PHP `header(...)` text from the `<style>` block so the `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` rule parses.
2. Add real alt text to the linked hero slices, footer slices and product images. A short descriptive alt on each CTA slice gives the link an accessible name.
3. Stop putting the raw email address in the `coreemail.html` and `svopn` pixel URLs. Use an opaque subscriber key or hash.
4. Move the two `http://` images to HTTPS and retire the 2022 and `thekey` assets. Host the spacer on the sender's own domain.
5. Add `class="wrapper"` and `class="photo"` to the actual tables and images, or rewrite the media queries so the mobile rules apply.
6. Drop the commented-out credit-card block from production sends.
7. Check the raw message for List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post and Authentication-Results, and resolve a sample of the `click.e.potterybarn.com` links to check destinations and UTM/`cm_` params.
