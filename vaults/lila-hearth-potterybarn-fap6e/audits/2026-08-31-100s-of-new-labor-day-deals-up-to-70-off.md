# 100s of NEW Labor Day deals up to 70% off
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-31**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's third "up to 70% off" Labor Day send in three days — same burgundy sale-banner treatment as the 08-31 "It's ON 🎉" email and roughly the same offer as 08-31's "Save up to $800" send. The hero is clean and functional: dark burgundy block, "Up to 70% off," "100s of new deals added!", a white SHOP ALL SALE button, then an 8-tile category grid (Furniture, Sofas & Chairs, Bedding, Pillows & Decor, Outdoor, Lighting, Rugs, Curtains, Tabletop, Bath). No overlap, no broken images, nothing lorem-ipsum — it renders fine.
- Past the fold it sprawls: a 6-item "Recommended Just For You" product grid, then a Halloween Shop banner, then a "Discover New Arrivals" sofa banner, then app-download, credit card, and Affirm modules before the footer. That's four distinct calls to action competing after the sale banner — Halloween and New Arrivals dilute the Labor Day message rather than reinforcing it. Given this is the brand's third near-identical Labor Day banner this week, fatigue is a real risk; this send doesn't do enough to differentiate itself from the other two.

## What's working

- Hero offer ("Up to 70% off") is immediately visible with a single unambiguous white CTA button.
- Category grid is scannable and gives shoppers a clear path into their subcategory of interest.
- "Recommended Just For You" personalizes the mid-body with named, priced-adjacent product tiles (bed, curtain rod, dining tables, chair, rug).
- No visual bugs — clean rendering, consistent brand typography and burgundy/cream palette throughout.
- Clear exclusions disclosure ("Exclusions apply. Prices as marked.") sits directly under the hero rather than buried in fine print only.

## What's weak

- Third near-identical "up to 70%" Labor Day banner in as many days (following 08-31's two prior sends) — no new creative angle, just a reshuffled category grid.
- No visible prices or dollar amounts on any "Recommended" product tile, weakening the offer's concreteness compared to the 08-31 "$800" send.
- Halloween Shop and "Discover New Arrivals" banners crowd the bottom of the email, diluting the single Labor Day CTA with two competing, off-topic prompts.
- No urgency/deadline anywhere in the visible copy (no "ends tonight," no countdown) despite this being a holiday sale email.
- Product recommendations lean skilled/investment-furniture (extendable dining tables, wool rug) with no lower-priced or accessory items for browsers who aren't buying big-ticket furniture.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the Halloween Shop and New Arrivals banners from this specific send (or move them into a separate email) — a sale email should end near the sale content, not pivot to two unrelated campaigns.
- 2. Add a real deadline to the hero ("Ends Monday at Midnight ET") — none of the last three Labor Day sends have shown urgency, and it's the single easiest lift for click-through.
- 3. Show sale prices or "% off" callouts on the "Recommended" product tiles instead of just product names — right now the personalization block has no offer signal at all.
- 4. Differentiate this send's subject/creative from the two prior Labor Day emails this week — vary the hero visual or lead category so it doesn't read as a duplicate to an inbox that's seen "Up to 70% off" twice already.
