# Introducing our NEW Kravet Collection
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-14**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's launch send for a new designer textile collaboration — Kravet × Pottery Barn — and it's a much more premium, editorial execution than most of this week's sends. The "kravet. x POTTERY BARN" lockup over a richly patterned canopy bed hero immediately signals "new, exclusive collection" rather than another discount push, and it's a clean break from both the "UP TO 60% OFF" catalog dumps and the styling-guide format the brand ran on 2026-08-12. Editorial pull-quotes ("Patterns tell the story of a home...", "Start small, think in layers...") reinforce a design-authority voice that's distinct and well-executed.
- The tradeoff is that this is a brand-building email, not a conversion email — there's no discount, no urgency, and the primary CTA ("SHOP NOW" / "SEE THE FULL COLLECTION") points to a browse experience, not a specific product. For a subscriber who already loves Pottery Barn's aesthetic this is a genuinely nice send; for anyone shopping on price or urgency, it gives them nothing to act on. Combined with a long scroll before any concrete product info appears, this reads as a strong brand/loyalty play but a weaker direct-response email.

## What's working

- The Kravet x Pottery Barn lockup at the top is an immediate, clear "new collaboration" signal — distinct from anything else in the recent brand history.
- The hero bed styling (layered florals, warm wood tones) is cohesive and on-brand, with no render bugs or broken images anywhere in the scroll.
- Editorial pull-quotes break up the product grid nicely and reinforce a designer-collection narrative rather than a generic sale pitch.
- "RECOMMENDED JUST FOR YOU" section with named products and prices (Standard Curtain Rod, Emery Linen Curtain, Westin Heavenly Bed Mattress at 10.20% callout) gives concrete, clickable specificity lower in the email.
- Clean footer utility stack (app download, Rewards/Affirm financing, social) is well organized and not cluttered.

## What's weak

- No discernible discount, price anchor, or offer anywhere in the top two-thirds of the email — just "Discover 1600+ New Arrivals" and lifestyle imagery.
- Primary CTAs ("SHOP NOW", "SEE THE FULL COLLECTION") are generic and lead to a browsing experience, not a specific hero product.
- It's a very long scroll (7+ full-width lifestyle images) before hitting anything transactional — high risk of drop-off on mobile.
- The Halloween Shop and Design Crew Sweepstakes banners near the bottom feel bolted-on and dilute the Kravet launch focus — same pattern of banner-stacking seen in the 08-13 Peanuts collab send.
- No visible member/loyalty pricing tied to this specific collection despite Rewards being promoted in the footer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a concrete hook to the hero — even something modest like "New arrivals from $49" or "Now available" with a ship/availability date would give price-sensitive openers a reason to click beyond aesthetics.
- 2. Move one "RECOMMENDED JUST FOR YOU" product card (with price) up near the hero, so there's a specific, clickable product before the long lifestyle scroll.
- 3. Cut or move the Halloween Shop and Design Crew Sweepstakes banners to a separate send — they compete with the Kravet launch narrative rather than supporting it.
- 4. Shorten the lifestyle-image cascade — 7 full-bleed images before the CTA button is a lot of mobile scroll for a single collection launch.

# Technical Audit — Pottery Barn "NEW Kravet Collection" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard PB/Williams-Sonoma-platform HTML email with heavy legacy table markup and third-party tracking (Turn.com, MI/SVOPN pixels). Primary defects are widespread missing `alt` attributes, two hardcoded `http://` (non-TLS) image sources, and absent authentication/unsubscribe headers in the captured relay data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All navigational/CTA links route through `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` (Salesforce Marketing Cloud–style opaque tracking tokens) — 62 tracking/redirect links were skipped by the automated HTTP probe (unverifiable reachability, not necessarily broken).
- Open-tracking pixels present: `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/...` (Turn/Amobee) and `svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` — the latter is served over **plain HTTP**, exposing the recipient's raw email address (`sv_sveme=`) in an unencrypted query string.
- `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/ca9d8afc-6576-4810-ae13-a03c9fb4da5d?mi_mid=...` — additional tracking/personalization endpoint (Movable Ink), HTTPS.
- No evidence of broken/malformed href syntax in the visible source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **43 images missing `alt` text** across hero banners, product slices, footer, and legal/compliance imagery (see QA list) — screen readers and image-blocked clients will render nothing meaningful; several are content-bearing (e.g., `F26_Top_NewArrivals.jpg`, product photography like `farmhouse-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`).
- **2 images served over `http://` instead of `https://`**: `edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` and `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, plus the `sv_emopen` tracking pixel and `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` — mixed content risk; may be blocked or flagged by mail clients enforcing TLS-only image loading.
- CSS `<style>` block contains stray PHP-style `header(...)` calls (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg')`, cache-control headers) embedded as literal text inside a `<style>` tag — these are inert/dead code (won't execute in an email client) but indicate a template artifact/copy-paste error from a server-side script.
- Legacy table-based layout with MSO conditional comments and `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` — standard defensive pattern for Outlook/Apple Mail, no issues.
- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` present — appropriate for transactional/marketing email markup.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `%%FirstName%%`, `{{...}}`) found in the visible source.
- Recipient identifier is embedded in plaintext across multiple URLs: `email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` (customer-service link) and `sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` (open pixel) — the latter over unencrypted HTTP, meaning the email address is exposed in cleartext network traffic.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header: **not found** in captured headers — may be a relay-capture limitation (AgentMail) rather than absence from the original message; cannot confirm CAN-SPAM one-click compliance from this data alone.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058): **not found** — same caveat.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC): **not found** — sender authentication status is unknown from this capture.
- Footer unsubscribe/compliance imagery present (`Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, footer slices) suggesting a visible unsubscribe link exists in-body, but it cannot be verified from the truncated source provided.
- **Recommendation caveat**: all three header findings are QA warnings tied to relay-capture limits, not confirmed violations — flag for re-verification against raw SMTP headers if compliance certainty is required.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` parameters are present on any visible link — PB uses opaque SFMC tracking tokens (`qs=ABB7...`) instead of standard UTM query params, so campaign attribution on the landing side depends entirely on SFMC-side decoding, not GA-style UTM parsing.
- Landing destinations are masked behind `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirects; actual destination URLs (and whether they resolve to live PB product/category pages) could not be verified — the QA probe explicitly skipped these 62 links.
- One direct (non-redirected) outbound link to `potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=...` — passes recipient email as a live URL parameter to the customer service page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 43 flagged images, prioritizing content-bearing product/banner images over decorative spacers/pixels.
2. Migrate the two `http://` image sources (`edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`, `edm.potterybarn.com/.../Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`) and the `svopn.potterybarn.com` open-tracking pixel to HTTPS to eliminate mixed-content blocking risk and stop transmitting the recipient's email address in cleartext.
3. Remove the dead PHP `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block — harmless in rendering but indicates a template/build artifact worth cleaning at the source template level.
4. Re-verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` against raw SMTP headers (bypassing the AgentMail relay) to confirm actual CAN-SPAM/RFC 8058/SPF-DKIM status, since the current warnings may reflect relay capture gaps rather than sender-side omissions.
5. Consider supplementing SFMC opaque tracking tokens with standard UTM parameters on outbound links to support GA/analytics-side attribution independent of SFMC decoding.
