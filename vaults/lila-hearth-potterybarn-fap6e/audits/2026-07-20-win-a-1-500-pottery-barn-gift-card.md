# Win a $1,500 Pottery Barn gift card
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-20**
## Executive summary

- A clean departure from the July grind. After eleven consecutive discount-forward sends, Pottery Barn breaks the formula with a sweepstakes: Win $1,500 toward "Newlywed Must-Haves." The registry angle is a genuine pivot — it targets a completely different intent state (bride/groom, gift-giver) rather than the bargain-hunter segment they've been pounding. The structural bones are solid: hero, CTA, registry-feature tiles, personalized product recommendations, and a loyalty footer.
- That said, the audience mismatch is glaring. If you're not currently engaged in a wedding registry, this email has almost no pull — the $1,500 carrot is the only hook keeping a general subscriber reading. The "Recommended Just For You" grid at the bottom does useful work for non-newlyweds, but by that point most people have already bounced.
- Verdict: smart segmentation idea, wrong or too-wide list deployment. Should have been targeted narrowly at registrants or recent registry-page visitors.

## What's working

- **Clean creative break** — after a week of summer-sale templates, the sweepstakes visual reads fresh; the airy linen bedroom hero photograph is polished and consistent with PB's aesthetic.
- **Registry feature tiles are well-organized** — the three-column benefit grid (Merch Checklist / App / Free Expert Advice) communicates utility quickly.
- **"Recommended Just For You" section adds relevance** — product row with real bed/bedding images gives the non-newlywed a reason to keep scrolling.
- **Pottery Barn Rewards footer** — the 10% back callout is a genuine loyalty signal, and the app store badge provides a conversion pathway.
- **CTA is visible and unambiguous** — "Register Now" button copy is specific and action-oriented.

## What's weak

- **Audience mismatch is severe** — for anyone not actively planning a wedding registry, a "Newlywed Must-Haves" sweepstakes is immediately irrelevant; the list should have been filtered to registry-intent users.
- **The $1,500 prize doesn't translate to a guaranteed discount** — unlike every other July email, there's no concrete savings offer for the reader today; feels like a bait-and-switch if you're expecting a sale.
- **No urgency or deadline visible** — sweepstakes entry without a "Ends July 31" or "Enter by Sunday" signal reads as low priority, easy to defer and forget.
- **Hero copy "Newlywed Must-Haves" is narrow** — it excludes anyone buying for an existing home or gifting for a non-wedding occasion; opportunity to reframe more inclusively.
- **Bottom legal/unsubscribe block is dense** — the fine print section is visually prominent at the bottom, which is typical but especially noticeable in an email that lacks other busy visual elements.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a hard deadline to the sweepstakes** — "Enter by July 31" in the hero subhead immediately upgrades urgency from zero to credible. This single change is the highest-lift.
- 2. **Segment the list** — send the registry-forward version only to users who have visited /registry, engaged with prior registry content, or are in a "life milestone" segment. Send the "Recommended Just For You" product block to everyone else as the main event.
- 3. **Reframe the headline for wider appeal** — "Win $1,500 for Your Dream Home" captures both newlyweds and general home-upgraders without excluding your largest subscriber cohort.
- 4. **Reinforce with a consolation offer** — "Plus, enjoy 20% off your registry order this week" underneath the CTA gives people who don't win a reason to act today.
- 5. **Move the deadline/entry count to the CTA area** — "2,400 entries so far — add yours before July 31" creates social proof + urgency in one line.
- **Subject Alt A:** `You could win $1,500 — enter by July 31`
- **Subject Alt B:** `$1,500 dream home giveaway — enter now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `One quick entry. Your entire registry, covered.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Enter before it closes — plus see what we picked for you.`

**Priority 2 — Fix before next send cycle:**
