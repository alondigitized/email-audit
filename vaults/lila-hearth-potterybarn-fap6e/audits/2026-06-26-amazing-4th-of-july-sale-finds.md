## Executive summary

- This email is, body-wise, functionally identical to the June 26 "ON NOW: Extra 20% off select styles" send — same Extra 20% off hero, same tiered category tiles (70%, 79%, 68% off), same Open Box section, same Recommended Just For You block. The only meaningful difference is the subject line, and it is a straight downgrade. "Amazing 4th of July Sale finds" swaps a concrete offer ("ON NOW: Extra 20% off select styles") for filler copy: "Amazing" signals nothing, "finds" signals nothing, and bookending with star emojis signals a little desperation. - **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 79% off — our best 4th of July deals`

- 3. **Collapse three category tiles into one** — pick the single strongest number (79% off furniture) and anchor the body on that. Multiple moderate percentages diffuse attention.
- 4. **Move "Recommended Just For You" above the first SHOP NOW CTA** — personalization is the highest-intent section and it's at the bottom where attrition has already taken 60%+ of openers. Swap it with the Open Box module at minimum.
