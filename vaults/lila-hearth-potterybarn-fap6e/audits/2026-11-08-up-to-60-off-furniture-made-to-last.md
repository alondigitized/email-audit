# Up to 60% off furniture made to last
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a clear, headline-led sale email. "Up to 60% off Furniture" is the first thing the eye lands on, and a bedroom hero photo and two buttons ("Furniture Sale", "Shop All Sale") sit right under it. The "in-stock furniture delivered in 1-4 weeks / get it in time for the holidays" banner adds a real reason to buy now. For a brand I'm subscribed to, that opening is strong.
- The body is where it weakens. After the hero, the email becomes a long, general catalog. It has a sale category grid, a Festive Favorites shipping banner, a six-item "Recommended Just For You" block, a Christmas Shop tile, Design Crew, the app, the credit card and Affirm. The 60% off furniture promise gets diluted. Nothing in the recommendations shows a price or a markdown, and half of them are stockings and a quilt, not furniture. That's a mismatch with the subject. It also overlaps heavily with the 11/07 "Festive Favorites Ship Free" send and the 11/06 Cozy Cloud Quilt send, so this reads as one more rotation of the same creative.
- Verdict: a solid send with a strong top and a bloated bottom. It would perform better if it were about 40% shorter and every product tile showed a sale price.

## What's working

- The "Up to 60% off Furniture" headline is large and unambiguous, and it matches the subject.
- The urgency line, "in-stock furniture delivered in 1-4 weeks / get it in time for the holidays", is credible and time-aware.
- The hero bedroom photo is on-brand and shows what the sale category looks like.
- The "Explore More on Sale" grid (Bedding, Rugs, Lighting, Art & Mirrors, Pillows & Decor, Tabletop & Bar, Curtains, Bath) lets me jump straight to my category.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The recommended products have no prices, no sale badges and no star ratings, so the sale offer never gets reinforced.
- Many tiles aren't furniture (stockings, curtain rod, rug), and the "Furniture" hero only links to a generic category.
- The email is very long. The festive ship-free banner, Christmas Shop, Design Crew, app, credit card and Affirm modules compete with the main offer.
- "Up to 60%" gives no sense of which furniture is actually that deep, and the terms are in dense fine print at the bottom.
- It repeats the Festive Favorites Ship Free banner from the 11/07 send and the Cozy Cloud Quilt from 11/06.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the recommended block furniture-only, and show was/now prices with a "% off" badge on each tile. This is the biggest lift.
- 2. Cut the email below the recommendations to one secondary module, for example Design Crew or the Christmas Shop, and move the app, credit card and Affirm modules into a slim footer strip.
- 3. Add a specific hero proof point such as "Sofas from $X" or "Extra 10% in rewards" under the headline, so "up to 60%" becomes concrete.
- 4. Replace the generic "Furniture Sale" button copy with a more specific line like "Shop sofas, beds & tables".
- 5. Vary the creative across the week so this send doesn't repeat the 11/07 festive ship-free banner.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 60% off sofas, beds & tables`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Furniture sale: in stock, home by the holidays`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `In-stock pieces delivered in 1-4 weeks. Shop before your favorites sell out.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sofas, beds, dining tables and more, up to 60% off through Sunday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML layout with image-sliced content. It has widespread missing alt text, three non-HTTPS image sources, and an empty `<title>`. Authentication and unsubscribe headers could not be verified because of the relay, and none of the 56 click-tracking links were probed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified destinations.** 56 links were skipped as tracking redirects, for example `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Imxk...`. The final landing URLs, status codes and redirect chains are unconfirmed.
- **HTTP open-tracking pixel.** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` is non-HTTPS, so clients or proxies that block mixed content may drop it. This undercounts opens.
- **Recipient email in image and pixel URLs.**
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - Both put the recipient's address in plaintext in a query string, where it can be logged by proxies and CDNs.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 10,774 of 14,978 characters (about 72%) are URLs, which exceeds the 70% threshold. The long `qs=` redirect tokens are the likely cause.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 48 images.** The QA check does not separate absent `alt` from empty `alt=""`.
  - All 15 hero slices `…/1108_SUN_AM_LTOFurnAllSale/slices_01–15.jpg`
  - Footer slices `…/Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`
  - Banners `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg` and `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`
  - Six product images, for example `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg` and `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg`
  - `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, the 3ceb0c96… image and the `spacer_0.gif` spacer
- **Image-only content.** The hero is 15 sliced images with no alt text. With images off or in a screen reader, the content renders blank or unreadable.
- **Four tracking pixels also flagged.** `d.turn.com`, `svopn`, `clickcp` and `coreemail.html` should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`. This is low severity.
- **Non-HTTPS images.** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` may be blocked. The second is a 2022-era asset path.
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in `<head>`.
- **No `lang` attribute** on `<html xmlns=…>`.
- **`maximum-scale=1`** in the viewport meta tag blocks pinch-zoom.
- **Stray PHP in a `<style>` block.** The `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: …")` text is invalid CSS and looks like a template leak. It is probably dropped by the parser, but it is unclean.
- **Cross-brand asset.** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is served from the West Elm host.
- **Web font.** The Google Fonts `<link>` for Montserrat is stripped by many clients, and I can't confirm a fallback stack from the truncated source.
