# NEW collections for your bedroom
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- This is a category-focused send, and a better one than the "Extra 20% off" emails Pottery Barn has sent all week (Oct 26–31). The "Bedroom Foundations" hero with a plaid-dressed canopy bed leads into a tidy stack of collection tiles: Campaign, Scarlett, Summerville. It's on-theme for late October, with the plaid bedding and the Christmas Shop tile near the bottom.
- The weak point is that the oxblood "Extra 20% off Select Styles / Plus savings up to 70% off" banner sits on top again. It is the same block as in the Oct 29, 30 and 31 sends, so it competes with the bedroom story. The subject promises "NEW collections" but carries no offer, price or deadline. The body also runs very long, with Recommended Just For You, the Christmas Shop, Design Services, the app, rewards and Affirm all stacked below the bedroom content.
- Judgement: a solid editorial send with a clear category, undermined by promo fatigue and a vague subject. It is a 6 in a week full of 6s.

## What's working

- The "Bedroom Foundations" hero with a "Shop Beds" button states the category immediately.
- Collection tiles (Campaign, Scarlett, Summerville) give named, scannable entry points, and the NEW and BESTSELLER tags add some social proof.
- A "Shop All Bedroom Furniture" button and a "Complete Your Retreat" grid (Quilts, Duvets, Pillows, Sheets) offer a sensible cross-sell.
- The plaid and green bedding and the Christmas tile fit the season.

## What's weak

- The "Extra 20% off Select Styles" banner repeats from earlier in the week and diverts attention from the bedroom hero. "Select styles" is also vague.
- The subject says "NEW collections," but the body shows a mix of new, bestseller and classic items, and no pricing.
- The body is too long. After the bedroom section, the Recommended grid (dining tables, rug, pendant), Christmas Shop, Design Services, app, credit card and Affirm modules dilute the message.
- Product tiles show no prices, so the discount can't be tied to any bedroom item.
- The legal and exclusions block at the bottom is dense and small, so the "up to 70% off" claim reads as less than fully honest.

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop the generic sale banner. Put one bedroom-specific offer in the hero, such as "Bedroom Foundations, up to X% off bedding," with a deadline.
- 2. Add prices ("from $…") and star ratings to the collection tiles.
- 3. Cut everything after "Complete Your Retreat" down to one secondary module, and move the Design Services and card promos to other sends.
- 4. Make the CTA copy more specific, for example "Shop Canopy Beds" instead of "Shop Beds."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Bedroom refresh: new beds + extra 20% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Plaid, brass & carved wood for your bedroom`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Three new collections, plus extra 20% off select bedding through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Canopy beds and nightstands that ship in 1-4 weeks. See the Campaign collection.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with 57 ESP-wrapped tracking links and a lot of missing image alt text. It also puts the recipient's email address in plaintext in two image URLs, one of them over HTTP. The automated QA run scored 50% pass with 0 failures and 4 warnings. The HTML source was truncated, so the unsubscribe/footer markup and the middle of the body were not reviewed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 57 tracking links on click-redirect domains (e.g. `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`) were skipped by the HTTP probe. Their destinations and status codes are unconfirmed.
- **Opaque redirects:** The `qs=` payloads are encoded, so no destination can be read from the source.
- **Plain-text bloat:** 10,968 of 15,215 characters (about 72%) of the plain-text part are URLs. This comes from the long tracking URLs and can hurt spam scoring and readability.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` is a Turn (Amobee) ad-network beacon. It has no alt text.
- **Click-pixel image:** `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/85677843-...?mi_cid=677b44e6831b79f3&mi_mid=...` is an image with no alt text.
- **Cross-brand asset host:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a spacer served from the West Elm domain. It adds a dependency on another brand's CDN.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** About 55 images are flagged. Most are the sliced banner images: `slices_01–15.jpg` in `H26_Furn_B2_NewBestsellingBedroomFurniture/`, `slices_01–18.jpg` in `Email-Footer/`, and the `H26_TOP_Extra20CLR.jpg` and `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg` banners. The product images are also flagged, for example `standard-curtain-rod-wall-bracket-l.jpg` and `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg`. With images blocked, the email is effectively blank for screen readers and image-off clients.
- **Non-HTTPS images:**
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...`

  These may be blocked or flagged as mixed content in some clients.
- **Stray code in a style block:** The second `<style>` block contains PHP: `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store..."); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. It is invalid CSS and can break the rules that follow it in strict parsers.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **No language attribute:** `<html xmlns=...>` has no `lang` attribute.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom.
- **Layout tables:** Some layout tables, such as the spacer `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0">`, lack `role="presentation"`. Others have it.
- **Late-applied image CSS:** The responsive image rules `img {max-width: 650px}` and `img {max-width: 100%!important}` only apply at 650px and above. Below that, images rely on fixed `width="650"` attributes, so verify them on narrow viewports.
- **Web font:** The Google Fonts `<link>` (Montserrat) is stripped by many clients. Confirm the fallback stack covers the cases where it is.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved tokens:** The visible source has no raw merge tokens such as `%%...%%` or `{{...}}`.
- **Email address in URLs:** The recipient address `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is passed in plaintext as a query parameter in two places:
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`, which is also an `<img>` with no alt text
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`

  The second is over unencrypted HTTP, so the address is exposed in transit and in proxy and server logs.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** The QA run found no `List-Unsubscribe` header. It notes the AgentMail relay may not be capturing it, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed sender defect.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post:** The RFC 8058 one-click header was also not found, with the same caveat. This matters for Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **Authentication-Results:** The header was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status could not be verified. This is expected through the relay.
- **Footer content:** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address were not visible in the truncated HTML, so they could not be checked.
- **Sender identity:** The From address `PotteryBarn@e.potterybarn.com` is on a subdomain of the brand's domain, which is consistent.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No readable UTMs:** The `qs=` parameters hide any `utm_*` values, and the only product and asset URLs visible are image URLs. UTM tagging and landing-page destinations cannot be confirmed.
- **Session IDs:** The `mi_cid` / `mi_mid` parameters on the `clickcp.potterybarn.com` pixel suggest ESP-side attribution instead of query-string UTMs.
- **Link probing:** Because all 57 click links were skipped, redirect chains and landing-page status codes were not checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content image. Use `alt=""` for spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Replace the three `http://` image sources with `https://`, and move the `svopn` open pixel to HTTPS.
3. Stop putting the raw email address in `coreemail.html?email=` and `sv_sveme=`. Use an opaque subscriber ID or a hash.
4. Remove the PHP `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block.
5. Fill in `<title>`, add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
6. Add `role="presentation"` to the remaining layout tables.
7. Serve the spacer from the sender's own CDN instead of `edm.westelm.com`.
8. Re-run the compliance checks against raw headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. If the relay is stripping them, fix the capture rather than flagging the sender.
9. Resolve a sample of the tracking links with a redirect-following probe to confirm destinations, status codes and UTM presence.
10. Check that the plain-text part gives readable link labels instead of full tracking URLs.
