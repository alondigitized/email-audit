# Classic plaid, now in season
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-11**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn stepping fully off the burgundy Labor Day banner and doing a genuine editorial launch: "Classic Plaid," a tartan-patterned hero with a moody deep-brown headboard/upholstery photo, no discount callout, no urgency marquee. It's the strongest departure yet from the two-week discount treadmill — compare to 2026-09-10's "Ready-to-Ship Furniture" and "Classic Comforts" sends, which also went pure-editorial. The plaid pattern is genuinely well-executed visually: the hero photography, the "Heritage Autumn" secondary module, and the tight product grid all read as a coherent seasonal story rather than a sale blast.
- The problem is the same one that's dogged every non-discount Pottery Barn send this cycle: there's no offer, no urgency, and the subject line gives a subscriber zero concrete reason to open over the dozen other retail emails competing for that same inbox slot. This is a beautiful catalog page pretending to be an email. It'll perform fine on brand affinity and repeat customers who like plaid bedding, but it will not pull incremental opens or clicks from someone scanning a crowded inbox.
- Judgement: gorgeous, on-brand, zero urgency — a keep-warm send, not a revenue send.

## What's working

- Hero photography is genuinely striking — the tartan pattern fills the frame and "CLASSIC PLAID" reads immediately as a seasonal moment, not a generic banner.
- Two clear CTAs in the hero ("SHOP NEW ARRIVALS" / "EXPLORE THE LOOKBOOK") give both a shopping path and a discovery path.
- Product grid is well-curated and thematically tight — all plaid/heathered/autumn textiles, no filler.
- "Recommended Just for You" section adds personalization signals (named products, prices implied by category) beyond the static merchandising blocks.
- Clean footer with loyalty (10% Back in Rewards), Affirm financing, and app download — full-funnel coverage without feeling cluttered.

## What's weak

- No offer anywhere in the visible render — no %/$ off, no free shipping, nothing time-bound. Compare to the "Extra 20% off" sends that dominated the first two weeks of September.
- Subject line "Classic plaid, now in season" is pure editorial with zero urgency or specificity — nothing compels an immediate open.
- "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 4-6 Weeks" pre-header banner undercuts urgency rather than building it — it's a fulfillment disclaimer, not a hook.
- Halloween Shop banner appears mid-email with no visual transition from the plaid/heritage theme — a jarring seasonal whiplash between autumn textiles and haunted-house decor.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a concrete, time-bound incentive to the hero itself — even a modest "Free shipping on plaid, this week only" would give the subject line something to promise and the reader a reason to act now instead of bookmarking for later.
- 2. Rewrite the subject to name a category or urgency hook rather than a pure trend statement — "now in season" is vague and low-curiosity.
- 3. Move or visually separate the Halloween Shop module — right now it breaks the autumn-plaid narrative the rest of the email built carefully.
- 4. Add a preview/preheader that complements the subject with something concrete (a product, price point, or delivery promise) instead of leaving it to default inbox junk.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Plaid is back — new arrivals in cozy tartan`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The plaid edit: bedding, seating & decor for fall`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Heathered chenille, warm neutrals, and tailored plaid — shop the full collection`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Standard MI-generated multipart email with a tracking pixel array, 52 click-redirect links, and CSS-only responsive breakpoints; automated QA could not confirm unsubscribe/authentication headers because they weren't captured by the ingesting relay, and nearly every image lacks alt text.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 52 tracking/click-redirect links (e.g., `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`, `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/cff0ccbf-...`) were skipped by the automated HTTP probe since they are redirect endpoints, not final destinations — not independently verified as live.
- Multiple non-HTTPS (`http://`) resources present: `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`, and `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...`. These may be blocked or flagged by mail clients enforcing mixed-content/TLS policies.
- Multiple open-tracking pixels present (`svopn.potterybarn.com` open pixel, `d.turn.com/r/dd/...` third-party pixel, `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/...`), consistent with standard MI engagement tracking — no anomaly, noting for completeness.
- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` are present — expected for email HTML, not an issue.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 39 `<img>` tags are missing `alt` attributes, including all content slices (`slices_01.jpg`–`slices_18.jpg` across two modules), product images (toscana-extendable-dining-table, remmy-swivel-chair, reeva-handwoven-rug, balboa-swivel-chair, belgian-flax-linen-curtain), and the header nav image (`nav0.jpg`). Screen readers and images-disabled clients (Outlook default) will render this email with no usable text content.
- Stray `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ...")` PHP-style function calls are embedded as literal text inside a `<style>` block in `<head>`. These are inert as CSS (browsers will ignore them as invalid rules), but their presence indicates a template artifact/leftover server code that wasn't stripped from the source.
- Conditional MSO blocks (`<!--[if gte mso 9]>`, `<!--[if mso 16]>`) and Outlook font-fix classes are correctly structured.
- Responsive breakpoints rely on two overlapping/conflicting media queries (`min-width: 700px` sets `img{max-width:650px}`, `min-width:650px` sets `img{max-width:100%!important}`) — the `!important` in the second will win at ≥700px, making the 650px cap in the first query dead code. Not a functional bug but redundant/contradictory rules.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- Recipient email is hard-coded/merged into tracking URLs as plain text, e.g. `sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` and `coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`. No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%EMAIL%%`) were found in the truncated source.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- List-Unsubscribe header: not found by automated check. QA notes this may be a relay-capture limitation (AgentMail) rather than confirmed absence — cannot confirm compliance status from available data.
- List-Unsubscribe-Post header (RFC 8058, one-click unsubscribe): not found — same caveat as above.
- Authentication-Results header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC): not found — same relay-capture caveat; sender authentication status is unverified, not confirmed failing.
- No visible unsubscribe link/footer text was present in the truncated HTML provided — full footer beyond the truncation point was not available to confirm physical address and unsubscribe mechanism required by CAN-SPAM.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Header nav link uses an MI tokenized redirect (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABB7...`) rather than a directly inspectable UTM-tagged URL — final landing page and UTM parameters cannot be confirmed without following the redirect (skipped by probe per QA findings).
- Customer service link resolves directly to `potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html` (bypassing the click-tracking domain) — inconsistent tracking treatment vs. other CTAs, though not inherently an error.

7. Recommendations
- Add descriptive `alt` text to all 39 flagged images, particularly product images and primary CTA banners, for accessibility and images-off rendering.
- Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP-style lines from the `<style>` block in `<head>` — dead template code that should be cleaned from the source.
- Upgrade the three `http://` resources to `https://` to avoid mixed-content blocking in modern mail clients.
- Consolidate the two conflicting `img { max-width }` media queries into a single non-contradictory rule.
- Confirm List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post / Authentication-Results header presence at the raw-SMTP level (outside the AgentMail relay) to close the compliance gap the automated check couldn't resolve.
