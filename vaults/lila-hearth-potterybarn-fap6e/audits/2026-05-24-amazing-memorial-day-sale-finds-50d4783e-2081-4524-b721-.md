# ⭐ Amazing MEMORIAL DAY SALE finds ⭐
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-24**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's seventeenth Memorial Day email in about a week, and the template has now ossified into pure wallpaper. Same red hero, same "Extra 20% off" headline, same category grid (Bedding / Furniture / Sofas / Pillows / Outdoor), same "Recommended Just For You" rail underneath. The subject line — "⭐ Amazing MEMORIAL DAY SALE finds ⭐" — is the laziest framing of the campaign so far: no category, no urgency, no number, just star emojis around the word "amazing."
- The render itself is clean. Hierarchy works: eye lands on the red 20% block, then steps through the category tiles. But after sixteen prior sends, "finds" promises curation and the email delivers the same catalog tour. The one Final-Weekend urgency Pottery Barn finally earned 48 hours ago is now gone — replaced by generic adjective-noun filler.
- Verdict: technically functional, creatively bankrupt. This is the send where a subscriber starts right-click-unsubscribing.

## What's working

- Hero hierarchy is clean — "Extra 20% off" reads in under a second.
- Category tiles cover the whole catalog with specific %-off ceilings (up to 40/50/60% off).
- Render is tidy: no broken images, no overlap, no layout breakage.
- "Recommended Just For You" rail adds a personalization beat the prior templates often skipped.

## What's weak

- Subject is the weakest of the entire 17-email run — "Amazing finds" says nothing.
- Hero is identical to the May 22 and May 23 sends; recipients can't tell this from yesterday's email.
- Memorial Day is now ~36 hours away and the email contains zero countdown / deadline.
- "Up to 60%" is buried inside category tiles instead of led with — the strongest number in the email is the smallest one on screen (20%).
- Star emojis ⭐...⭐ flanking "MEMORIAL DAY SALE" in ALL CAPS reads spammy in the inbox row.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the deadline, not "amazing."** Memorial Day is Monday — the subject should say so.
- 2. **Pick one category and commit** — the last winning send (May 22 "NEW furniture deals") proved category-specific subjects outperform catalog tours.
- 3. **Surface the 60% in the hero**, not in a sub-tile. "Extra 20% off" is the smallest discount in the email; lead with the biggest.
- 4. **Kill the star emojis** — they're the only spam signal in an otherwise clean subject.
- 5. **Add a real countdown band** ("Ends Monday 11:59 PT") above the hero — every prior send in this campaign would have benefited and this one needs it most.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Memorial Day ends Monday — up to 60% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last 48 hours: Extra 20% off outdoor + bedding`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus free shipping on the patio sets selling out fastest.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sale ends Monday at midnight — shop the deepest markdowns now.`

2. Strip the PHP `header(...)` block from the `<style>` tag — template-include is leaking server code into the rendered HTML.

5. Verify outbound headers include `List-Unsubscribe` + `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and a passing `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC); if these are present at the MTA but stripped by the AgentMail relay, instrument the relay to preserve them.
6. Reconsider `meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer"` — it kneecaps landing-page attribution.
7. Remove dead/commented viewport metas to reduce source-size noise.
