# 📞 Last call for EXTRA 20% off select styles + Now on sale: Your next favorite furniture find
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-26**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's third Memorial Day email on May 26 alone — following the "LAST DAY 🚨" send and the "Less than 24 hrs / Bloom Shop" send already audited at 9/10 and 8/10 respectively. The creative is functionally identical to both: same red hero banner, same "Extra 20% off select styles," same SHOP SALE button, same category grid underneath. The only differentiator is the subject line swapping in a phone emoji and a tacked-on "your next favorite furniture find" hook that the body doesn't really pay off.
- Below the fold there's a "Recommended Just For You" row of four product tiles — bedding, a dining setup, a bedroom scene, and a headboard — which is the email's most useful piece of content. It's buried under a sea of category links and above a stack of value-prop modules (Free Design Service, App download, Rewards, Affirm) that dilute any remaining urgency. By the time you reach those product tiles, the "last call" framing has already evaporated.
- The judgement: this is a filler send that should not exist. Both heavier sibling emails on the same day already covered the last-day urgency with better execution. This one adds fatigue without adding value.

## What's working

- **Offer is above the fold and unambiguous** — "Extra 20% off" and the red LAST DAY banner land immediately; no hunting required.
- **Category grid is comprehensive** — Outdoor through Clearance covers the full breadth of what's actually discounted, which is useful for a browsing subscriber.
- **Loyalty and financing both visible** — "Earn 10% Back in Rewards" and Affirm appear below the fold; members have their reasons to click reinforced.
- **Clean render** — no broken images, no overlapping elements, no lorem ipsum anywhere in the email.

## What's weak

- **Third email, same day, identical creative** — the red banner and "Extra 20% off select styles" line has appeared verbatim in every send for a week; opening this one adds zero new information over what was already in the inbox.
- **Subject line is 90+ characters** — nearly double the mobile-safe limit; it will truncate badly on every phone and the second half ("Now on sale: Your next favorite furniture find") disappears entirely.
- **"Recommended Just For You" is buried** — the only personalized content in the email sits below three separate category modules; most subscribers never scroll there.
- **No time stamp on the deadline** — "Last call" and "LAST DAY" are vague; "Ends tonight at midnight PT" would create actual urgency rather than the ambient anxiety of the tenth "last" send.
- **Value-prop stack below fold is crowded** — Free Design Service + App promo + Rewards + Affirm + Social icons all in sequence turns the bottom half into a generic brand brochure that undercuts the supposed urgency.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill this send.** Both prior May 26 emails already fulfilled the last-day message more effectively. A third send the same day trains subscribers to ignore the brand. If it must go, consolidate the Bloom Shop, the furniture "find," and the last-call urgency into one unified email — not three.
- 2. **Lead with the personalized product tiles** — move "Recommended Just For You" to the top, immediately under the hero banner. That's the one signal that this isn't a broadcast blast.
- 3. **Add a hard deadline** — "Ends tonight at 11:59 PM PT" everywhere the vague "last call" appears, including the hero banner.
- 4. **Cut the subject in half** — the first 50 characters should carry the full message; the second clause after the "+" is invisible on mobile.
- 5. **Collapse the value-prop stack** — pick one below-fold module (Rewards, since loyalty members are the most likely converters), drop the rest.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: Extra 20% off ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your furniture picks — 20% off, final hours`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus up to 60% off select styles. Deadline: midnight PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Recommended for you — and on sale through tonight only.`

### 1. Technical Summary

Two hard-broken links (403) will silently fail for affected users — one is the credit card banner CTA, the other a cross-brand tracking pixel from West Elm. Widespread missing alt text and three HTTP (non-HTTPS) image sources are the next highest-priority class of issues.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**FAIL — Credit card apply/buy CTA returns 403**
`https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/c/da01b407-b21c-4618-a71b-f83bef0dd682?brandCode=POTTERYBARNCB&marketingChannelCode=APPLYANDBUY&...`
The Key Rewards credit card banner CTA is dead. Users who click receive an HTTP 403; the offer is unreachable.

**FAIL — West Elm cross-brand spacer pixel returns 403**
`http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`
This is a layout spacer pulled from a West Elm CDN domain. The 403 may cause a broken layout row in image-on clients, and the HTTP source compounds the issue (see §3).

**WARN — 24 tracking links unverified**
All click-redirect links through `click.e.potterybarn.com` and `clickcp.potterybarn.com` were skipped by the HTTP probe. The one confirmed 403 above came from `clickcp` — additional broken links in that domain cannot be ruled out without manual spot-checks.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**HTTP image sources — may be blocked by clients**
Three `src` attributes use `http://` rather than `https://`:
- `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` (also 403)
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
- `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&...` (open-pixel tracker)

Gmail, Outlook.com, and Apple Mail all proxy or block mixed-content HTTP sources. The footer credit card banner (`Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`) and brand banner slice will likely render as broken images in major clients.

**Missing alt text — 44 images**
Every product image, all footer slices (slices_01–18), the main hero banner (`S26_Top_MemorialDayLastDay.jpg`), and the open-tracking pixel have empty or absent `alt` attributes. With images disabled (common in B2B inboxes), the email renders as a wall of blank space with no text fallback for any product or CTA.

**`<title>` element is empty**
`<title></title>` — some clients surface this as the email tab label; leaving it blank is a minor but avoidable gap.

**PHP header directives in `<style>` block**
```html
<style>
  ...
  header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
  header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, ...");
  header("Pragma: no-cache");
</style>
```
Server-side PHP `header()` calls were erroneously included inside a CSS `<style>` block. They are inert in HTML but indicate a template assembly bug — a server-side snippet was pasted into the wrong template section.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)

RFC 2369 `List-Unsubscribe` and RFC 8058 `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (one-click) headers were not captured. If these are set at the ESP and stripped by the AgentMail relay, this is a monitoring gap. If genuinely absent, Gmail and Yahoo's bulk sender requirements (enforced since 2024) flag the send as non-compliant — one-click unsubscribe is mandatory for senders above 5K/day.

**CAN-SPAM physical address** — not verifiable from the truncated HTML provided; assume present in footer (standard for PB).

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM Params, Landing Page Alignment)

UTM parameters are embedded inside the ESP click-redirect URLs (via `click.e.potterybarn.com`) rather than as bare query strings, so direct inspection is not possible without resolving the redirects. The 24 unverified tracking links represent the full CTA set — if any redirect chain is misconfigured, UTM attribution will silently drop.

The credit card CTA (`APPLYANDBUY` / `PB_header_nonmember`) that returns 403 delivers zero attribution and zero conversion — it is the highest-priority landing page continuity failure.
