- The hero commits to one category and one number, so the eye lands on "70% off Pillows & Throws" first.

## What's weak

- Cadence is tired. It reuses the burgundy Early Black Friday frame from the week's earlier sends.
- The email is very long, with a category grid, recommendations, in-stock furniture, design services, an app promo, rewards, Affirm and dense legal text. It dilutes the pillows-and-throws story.
- Every CTA is a generic "SHOP NOW" or "SHOP ALL SALE", with no product-specific verb.

- 3. Make CTAs specific, for example "Shop pillows" and "Shop throws".

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, image-slice layout (23 `slices_NN.jpg` in the hero plus 18 in the footer) with no alt text, so it is effectively blank when images are blocked. Subscriber email addresses are exposed in plain-text query strings, including on `http://` pixel requests. Header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be confirmed (see section 5).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 68 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe because they are click-redirect domains (e.g. `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...`). Link health, destinations and redirect chains are unverified.
- **PII in URLs:**
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`

  Both put the recipient address in the query string. The second is an open pixel over unencrypted HTTP, so the address can be exposed in transit and in proxy or server logs.
- **Third-party pixels:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/da9d21f4-...?mi_cid=...` are tracking pixels. Neither has alt text, and `d.turn.com` is a third-party ad-tech domain.
- **Stray `http://` image sources:** the pixel above, `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`. These can be blocked or rewritten, or trigger mixed-content warnings in some clients.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 80 images, including every hero slice (`.../1116_MON_PM_EBF_PillowsThrows/slices_01.jpg` to `slices_23.jpg`), all footer slices, and the product images (`faux-fur-ruched-throw-l.jpg`, `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-125-l.jpg`, etc.). The offer and CTAs are image-only, so they are inaccessible to screen readers and invisible with images off. The logo is the only image with alt text (`alt="POTTERY BARN"`).
- **Empty `<title></title>`** and no `lang` attribute on `<html>`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>` prevents pinch-zoom, a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Stray PHP code in the CSS:** the second `<style>` block contains `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store..."); ...`. This is invalid CSS and leaked server-side code. It is harmless in most clients but can break parsing of rules that follow it.
- **Layout and tracking images:**
  - `spacer_0.gif` is hosted on `edm.westelm.com`, another brand's domain.
  - Layout tables such as `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><tr><td height="20">&nbsp;</td>` lack `role="presentation"` (some other tables have it).
  - Pixels such as `d.turn.com/.../cat/1` carry no alt text.
- **Plain-text part:** 13,406 of 18,476 characters (about 73%) are URLs. That ratio is spam-filter-sensitive and poor for plain-text readers.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `${...}`) appear in the visible (truncated) source, and the recipient address resolves correctly in the `email=` and `sv_sveme=` parameters. The source was truncated, so the rest is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** neither was found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is not confirmed as a sender defect. The raw headers need checking at the original receipt.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown for the same reason.
- **Unsubscribe link and postal address:** neither appears in the truncated HTML, so neither can be confirmed. There is no evidence of a violation.
- **Sender:** `PotteryBarn@e.potterybarn.com` sits on a subdomain consistent with the click and image domains (`click.e.`, `image.e.`), which is a good sign for alignment.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** these are not verifiable. All CTAs go through `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` redirects that were not resolved, so UTMs and landing-page parity with the "UP TO 70% OFF cozy pillows & throws" offer are unconfirmed.
- **Stale and cross-brand assets:** the email reuses the 2022 banner `.../2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`. It also loads `edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` from another brand's domain.
- **Product images:** these are hot-linked from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...` rather than the `edm.` or `image.e.` CDN. They are subject to site-side changes.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, especially the 41 sliced hero and footer images. Use `alt=""` on spacers and pixels.
2. Remove the recipient email address from URL query strings (`coreemail.html?email=`, `sv_sveme=`). Use an opaque subscriber ID, and serve all pixels over HTTPS.
3. Convert the remaining `http://` assets to `https://`, and replace the 2022 `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `slices_02.jpg` assets with current ones.
4. Remove the stray PHP `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block, and drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Fill in `<title>`, add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and add `role="presentation"` to the remaining layout tables.
6. Add more live text to the HTML and plain-text parts to reduce the URL ratio.
7. Re-run QA against the original message headers, bypassing the relay, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Follow redirects to confirm UTM presence and landing-page parity.
