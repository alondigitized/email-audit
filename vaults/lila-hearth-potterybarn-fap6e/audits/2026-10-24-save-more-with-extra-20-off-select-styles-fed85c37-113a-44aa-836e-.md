# Save MORE with EXTRA 20% OFF select styles
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-24**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth "Extra 20% off" send in about three days. It follows 10/22 "STARTS NOW 📣", "ON NOW!", 10/23 "❤️ Take an EXTRA 20%" and "Sale on sale!". The offer is clear: an oxblood hero reading "Extra 20% off Select Styles / Plus savings up to 70% off" with a "SHOP ALL SALE" button. It is also the same banner I've now seen five times, so the novelty is gone.
- Below the hero, the email loses focus. A category grid sits above a "Recommended just for you" block of curtain rods, a rug, a pendant, and dining tables. After that come the Christmas Shop, Free Design Services, the app, a credit card, and Affirm. That is seven or eight competing asks for one email. The credit card and Affirm blocks dilute the sale message.
- Overall it's a competent but fatigued send. The offer reads instantly. Cadence and the long tail of secondary pitches hold it back.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer in one glance: "Extra 20% off Select Styles" with "up to 70% off" and a white "SHOP ALL SALE" button.
- The "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks" strip adds a useful reassurance for furniture buyers.
- The category grid (Sofas & Chairs, Bedding, Rugs, Curtains, etc.) gives easy entry points by room or product type.
- "Recommended just for you" shows named products with sizes, such as "Banks Extendable Dining Table (76"-128")" and "Reeva Handwoven Rug". That is more concrete than a generic grid.
- The lifestyle hero photo of the dining table is warm and on-brand.

## What's weak

- Five near-identical "Extra 20%" sends in three days is clear over-mailing. The banner is the same one as 10/22 and 10/23.
- The body has no prices, discount amounts, or deadline on the recommended products. "Select styles" and "exclusions apply" make the offer feel vague.
- The email stacks too many secondary pitches: Christmas Shop, Free Design Services, App Store, credit card, and Affirm. Each pulls attention from the sale.
- No urgency or end date is visible. "Save MORE" has no deadline behind it.
- The legal text is a dense wall of small type at the bottom, and the offer exclusions are buried there.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending the same banner five times. Consolidate into one sale email per day at most, and vary the hero with a different category or a fresh deadline.
- 2. Add a real end date to the hero, for example "Ends Sunday". There is currently no time-bound element.
- 3. Put prices on the recommended products, with sale price next to original, so the 20% feels tangible.
- 4. Cut the footer promos down to one. Keep the Christmas Shop or Design Services and drop the app, credit card, and Affirm blocks from sale sends.
- 5. Move the category grid below the recommendations, or shorten it, so the product images come sooner.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a nested-table layout with a heavily image-sliced body and a click-tracking wrapper on all 55 links. The main confirmed issues are missing alt text on nearly all content images, three `http://` image sources (one is a tracking pixel that carries the recipient's address in cleartext), and leaked PHP code in a `<style>` block. Authentication and unsubscribe headers could not be assessed because the relay didn't capture them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracked links not probed.** All 55 links were skipped because they use click-redirect domains such as `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`. Destinations, redirect status and link health are unverified.
- **Opaque redirects.** The `qs=` payload is an opaque blob, so UTM parameters can't be read from the HTML (see section 6).
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 10,515 of 14,733 characters (71%) are URLs, which is the cause of the QA warning. The long tracked URLs dominate the plain-text alternative and make it hard to read.
- **Multiple tracking pixels.** There are three, on three different hosts, plus an HTML endpoint used as an image:
  - `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1`: a third-party pixel on the Turn/Amobee domain.
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...`: an open pixel over plain HTTP.
  - `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/d5f51b59-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...`: an open pixel.
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=...`: an `.html` page loaded as an `<img>`. It's unlikely to return image content, so it may not count as a valid pixel.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content images (confirmed by QA):**
  - `slices_01.jpg` to `slices_13.jpg` in `/pb/2026/US/1024_SAT_AM_Extra20ImageLinkFarm/`.
  - `slices_01.jpg` to `slices_18.jpg` in `/pb/2026/US/Email-Footer/`.
  - `H25_TOP_InStockFurniture.jpg`, `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg`, `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`.
  - Product images: `standard-curtain-rod-wall-bracket-l.jpg`, `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`, `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg`, `emery-linen-curtain-l.jpg`, `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-1245-1-l.jpg`, and the open-box pendant.
  - With images blocked or read by a screen reader, the offer and its link targets are unavailable. Because the images are sliced, this is more severe than for a single hero image.
- **Missing alt text, non-content images:**
  - `spacer_0.gif` and the tracking pixels should carry `alt=""`.
  - `spacer_0.gif` is hosted on `edm.westelm.com`, a different brand's asset domain. This is a cross-brand dependency.
- **Insecure image sources.** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` and the `svopn` pixel use `http://`. These can be blocked or flagged as mixed content in some clients and proxies.
- **Leaked server-side code in CSS.** The second `<style>` block ends with `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store, ...`. It's likely harmless because it's an invalid rule with no braces, but it's a template defect.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `maximum-scale=1` in `<meta name="viewport">` stops pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Responsive selectors may not match.** The `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` rules target classes that don't appear in the visible, truncated source, where tables use `class="contenttable"`. The `.contenttable` width override is commented out. I can't tell whether responsive scaling relies only on `img {max-width:100%}`.
- **Incomplete `role="presentation"`.** Some layout tables have it. The outer wrapper and spacer tables (for example the `<td height="20">&nbsp;</td>` table) do not.
- **Web font dependency.** The Google Fonts `<link>` for Montserrat is stripped by several clients, so those clients fall back to the default font. This is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%first_name%%` or `{{...}}` appear in the visible source or QA output.
- **Recipient email exposed in URLs.** The recipient's address (`lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`) appears as a plain query parameter in:
  - `.../coreemail.html?email=...`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?...&sv_sveme=...` (this one is unencrypted).
- This is a confirmed privacy and security concern. The `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` doesn't help because the address is in the request URL itself.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058).** Both are not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so I'm treating this as unverified rather than a confirmed sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe support.
- **Authentication-Results.** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sending domain `e.potterybarn.com` can't be checked from this data.
- **Unsubscribe link and postal address.** These are outside the truncated HTML, so I can't confirm either.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters can't be verified.** All links go through `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`, and none of the tracked links were resolved. The visible source shows no UTM parameters on any destination. Direct `potterybarn.com` URLs appear only as image sources (`/pbimgs/...`).
- **Landing page alignment can't be assessed.** The `Extra20ImageLinkFarm` slices have no alt text and their link targets are hidden behind redirects.
- **Stale asset paths.** Some assets use older paths (`/pb/2022/...`, `/pb/2025/...`). This isn't necessarily broken, but it suggests reused modules.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, especially the `Extra20ImageLinkFarm` slices, which carry the offer. Use `alt=""` on spacers and pixels.
2. Move the `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, 2022 `slices_02.jpg` and `svopn` pixel URLs to HTTPS.
3. Stop putting the raw recipient email in pixel and image query strings. Use an opaque subscriber ID instead.
4. Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP lines from the `<style>` block. Fill in `<title>`, and drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Make the `.contenttable` and `img` responsive rules match the actual class names, or confirm that scaling works as intended in the full HTML.
6. Replace the `coreemail.html` image reference with a real image or pixel endpoint, and host `spacer_0.gif` on the sender's own domain.
7. Re-test with raw headers (not via the relay) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
8. Follow a few tracked links to check destinations and UTM parameters, since the `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirects were skipped.
9. Reduce the length of tracked URLs, or give the plain-text part cleaner links, to bring the URL share of the text below 70%.
