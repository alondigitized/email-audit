# 100s of NEW Early Black Friday furniture deals
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, furniture-led Early Black Friday send with a real offer. The burgundy hero says "NEW DEALS ADDED / UP TO 60% OFF FURNITURE" with a white "SHOP FURNITURE DEALS" button, and the eye lands there first. The four room tiles below (living, bedroom, dining, home office) each show a was/now price, so the reader can see the discount on a real product. That is better than a bare percentage.
- It loses focus in the back half. After the room tiles it adds a "More Early Black Friday Deals" block, a "Recommended just for you" grid, an in-stock furniture banner, a Design Crew sweepstakes, an app promo, a credit-card promo and Affirm. That is about seven competing messages. It also repeats the "$3,000 gift card" sweepstakes from the 2026-11-12 send, "Get a chance to win a $3,000 gift card." The result is a competent sale email that stops being a furniture email halfway down. The 60% headline is also lower than the "UP TO 70% OFF" in the earlier "STARTS NOW 📣 Early Black Friday" send today. That makes this one feel like a category follow-up and not an escalation.

## What's working

- The hero is unambiguous: "UP TO 60% OFF FURNITURE" with a visible white button on burgundy.
- "NEW DEALS ADDED" gives a reason to open a second Black Friday email in one day.
- The room tiles show was/now prices (for example the Cayman bed and Banks dining table), so the discount is concrete.
- Each tile has a clear room-category label ("LIVING ROOM FURNITURE" and so on), which makes browsing easy.
- "SHIPS FREE" badges on the pillows and bedding tiles add a second concrete benefit.
- The lifestyle photography is on-brand and consistent.

## What's weak

- The back half dilutes the message. Stockings, curtain rods, the sweepstakes, app, credit card and Affirm all compete with the furniture offer.
- The "SHOP ALL SALE" buttons are generic and sit below mixed content, so they don't tie to a product.
- The sweepstakes was already sent yesterday, which feels repetitive.
- The legal fine print at the bottom is long and dense. It sits near the offer language, and the exclusions are hard to read.
- The "Recommended just for you" tiles (stockings, curtain rods) are not furniture, so they break the subject's promise.
- The hero doesn't say when the deal ends. There is no credible urgency.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero, the four room tiles and one in-stock furniture banner. Move the sweepstakes, app, credit card and Affirm modules to a separate send or the footer.
- 2. Add a real deadline to the hero, such as "Ends Sunday," next to the percentage.
- 3. Replace "SHOP ALL SALE" with specific CTAs like "Shop Sofas & Sectionals" or "Shop Dining Tables."
- 4. Make the recommendation block furniture-only so it matches the subject.
- 5. Put the loyalty or credit-card benefit in one line near the hero, with the fine print linked and not shown in full.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Sofas, beds & dining tables: 60% off now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Was $2,999, now $2,179: the Cayman bed and more room-by-room deals.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus free shipping on pillows and bedding. In-stock pieces arrive in 1-4 weeks.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based, XHTML 1.0 Transitional, 650px wide, and mostly built from sliced images. Its main confirmed problems are missing alt text on about 48 images, three non-HTTPS image sources, and the recipient's email address in plaintext in pixel and image URLs. The compliance, authentication and UTM checks couldn't be confirmed from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click tracking can't be verified.** 59 links were skipped as tracking/redirect domains, so none were probed. The one visible example is the header logo link, `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...` (an opaque `qs` token). Destinations and redirect health are unknown.
- **Mixed-content tracking pixel.** The open pixel `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` uses plain HTTP and may be blocked or rewritten by clients.
- **Several third-party and secondary pixels.** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` (a third-party ad-network pixel) and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/a58493a6-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...` are both flagged as images without alt.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 48 images.** The count is from the QA warnings. It covers:
  - hero slices `slices_01`–`slices_14` under `/pb/2026/US/1113_FRI_AM2_EBF_FurnitureSale/`
  - six product images (e.g. `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`, `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`)
  - banners `H25_Bottom_InStock_V2.jpg`, `H26_Bottom_DesignCrewSweeps_01/02.jpg`
  - footer `slices_01`–`slices_18` under `/Email-Footer/`
  - `3ceb0c96-...jpg` and `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`

  The QA output doesn't say whether the attribute is absent or just `alt=""`. For tracking pixels and `spacer_0.gif`, an empty `alt` would be acceptable. For the content slices it isn't. Because the layout is image-sliced, a client that blocks images will show little or no readable content.
- **HTTP image sources:**
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - the `svopn` pixel above
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in the head.
- **Stray PHP code inside a `<style>` block.** The text `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store...` sits after the CSS rules in the second client `<style>` block. It is invalid CSS, and the PHP was never executed server-side. It's probably harmless where it sits, but a strict parser may discard the rule that precedes it.
- **`maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta** disables pinch-zoom on some clients, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **No `lang` attribute** on `<html>`.
- **Inconsistent table semantics.** Most content tables have `role="presentation"`, but the outer wrapper tables and the spacer table (`<td height="20">&nbsp;</td>`) don't.
- **Stray `<!-- -->` comment before the DOCTYPE.** This can trigger quirks mode in some older Outlook/IE-based renderers.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) appear in the visible source or QA data.
- **Recipient email exposed in URLs.** The plaintext address `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` appears in:
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=...`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?...&sv_sveme=...` (over HTTP, so visible in transit)

  This is a PII leak into server logs, proxies and referrers. The `no-referrer` meta tag partly mitigates referrer leakage but does nothing for the HTTP pixel.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed rather than a proven defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment can't be assessed. The From domain is `e.potterybarn.com`.
- **Footer not verifiable.** The HTML was truncated before the footer, so the visible unsubscribe link and postal address (CAN-SPAM) couldn't be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All links visible in the provided source go through `click.e.potterybarn.com` or `clickcp.potterybarn.com`, and the 59 tracking links were not resolved. Whether the final landing pages carry UTM parameters, and whether they match the "Early Black Friday furniture" offer, is unknown. The only direct site URL seen (`coreemail.html`) is an image URL, not a CTA.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content slice and product image. Use `alt=""` explicitly on pixels and spacers.
2. Switch the three `http://` assets, including the `svopn` pixel, to HTTPS.
3. Stop putting the raw recipient email in query strings (`coreemail.html`, `sv_sveme`). Use a hashed or opaque ID.
4. Remove the stray PHP `header(...)` text from the `<style>` block, and populate `<title>` and `<html lang="en">`.
5. Drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
6. Re-run QA against the raw `.eml` to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Then resolve the tracking links to verify UTM parameters and landing-page alignment.
7. Minor hygiene: replace the stale 2022 and 2025 assets, and the cross-brand `edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`, with current Pottery Barn assets.
