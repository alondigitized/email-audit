# The Halloween Shop is OPEN
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a strong seasonal hero on a weak email. The dark "The Halloween Shop" banner, with ghost, bat and trick-or-treat pillow props, makes a clear "UP TO 50% OFF" pill the first thing you see. The deadline, "Order by Tue 10/27, 6 AM PT with standard shipping", is credible and lands right in the hero. It is a better-armed send than the 10/19 "The Harvest Decor Shop is open", which had no offer.
- The middle undercuts the hero. The 3x3 product grid is unlabeled: no names, no prices, no per-item links. The only action is a single "SHOP ALL THINGS HALLOWEEN" button. Below it, "Recommended Just for You" shows curtain rods, dining tables, towels and a bed. None of those are Halloween, so the email loses its theme halfway down. A full-width "The Christmas Shop" banner then tells you the next season is already competing for attention.
- Judgement: a good hero and deadline, but an unlabeled, price-free grid and a drifting second half. It lands in the middle, around 6 to 7, close to the brand's recent sends. The subject is also the weakest part. It announces a shop opening and leaves the 50% off and the 10/27 deadline out.

## What's working

- The hero puts "UP TO 50% OFF" directly under the title, so the offer is the first thing you see.
- The deadline "Order by Tue 10/27, 6 AM PT" gives a real reason to act now.
- The dark, moody styling is on-brand and clearly a Halloween campaign.
- The "SHOP ALL THINGS HALLOWEEN" button is high-contrast, black on white, and its copy is a clear verb plus noun.
- The images render cleanly, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The 3x3 Halloween grid has no product names, prices or sale markers, so nothing is shoppable from the grid. It is only a mood board.
- "Recommended Just for You" is off-theme: curtain rod, dining table, towel and bed. It dilutes the Halloween push and reads as generic.
- The "The Christmas Shop" banner mid-email competes with the Halloween CTA and makes the first send feel rushed.
- "Up to 50% off" with no hint of which items or how deep most discounts go reads vague. The very long promotional-exclusions fine print adds to that.
- The footer is long and cluttered (app, Pottery Barn card, Affirm, a brand wall, legal text). A near-identical stack followed the 10/19 and 10/20 sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Make the grid shoppable.** Add name plus sale price under each item and link each tile to its product. This is the biggest single lift.
- 2. **Lead the subject with the offer and deadline.** The subject currently has neither.
- 3. **Replace "Recommended Just for You" with Halloween bestsellers or low-stock items,** or move it below the Christmas block.
- 4. **Add a second CTA mid-email,** such as "Shop Halloween décor" and "Shop costumes & kids", so the deeper categories are reachable.
- 5. **Hold the Christmas banner for a later send,** or shrink it to a text link. Pottery Barn has sent six emails in three days (10/18 to 10/20), so cadence is already a risk.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Ghosts, bats & pumpkins are here, up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Order by 10/27 to get it in time. Décor, pillows, tabletop & more.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Bewitching décor for your most spirited season yet, ships in 1-4 weeks.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Image-heavy, table-based email with no hard failures (QA: 57% pass, 0 errors, 3 warnings). The main problems are widespread missing `alt` text, three `http://` image or pixel requests, and the recipient address placed in plain-text query strings. The unsubscribe and authentication headers could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverifiable links:** 46 links were skipped by the QA probe because they use click-redirect domains. Example: `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7ImQi...` on the logo link. Destinations and link health can't be confirmed from this data.
- **Plain-HTTP tracking pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`. It is non-HTTPS, so it may be blocked and the open may not register. It also sends the recipient address unencrypted.
- **Recipient address in URLs:** `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is an `<img>` request. The plaintext address in a query string can end up in server and proxy logs.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` is an ad-tech beacon on an external domain.
- **Extra pixel:** `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/323d41f8-...?mi_cid=677b44e6831b79f3&mi_mid=...` is a second open pixel alongside the others.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 38 images are flagged. Every hero and footer slice is affected: `F26_Seasonal_B1_HalloweenShop/slices_01–04.jpg`, `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`, `H25_TOP_InStockFurniture.jpg`, `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg` and `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`. The product images (`toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-1245-1-l.jpg`, `farmhouse-platform-bed-1-l.jpg` and others) are also affected. With images blocked, the email has no readable content. The only `alt` text confirmed in the source is "POTTERY BARN" on the logo. Spacers and tracking pixels (`spacer_0.gif`, `d.turn.com`, `clickcp`) should have `alt=""`, which is acceptable for decorative images.
- **HTTP images:**
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  
  These may be blocked or flagged as mixed content.
- **Empty `<title></title>`:** there is no document title.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns=...>` has no `lang`.
- **`maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta:** this blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Server-side code in CSS:** the final `<style>` block contains `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store...")`. The browser will ignore it, but it shows a template or build error.
- **Missing `role="presentation"`:** the outer wrapper tables and the 20px spacer table lack it, while the content tables have it.
- **Web font:** the Google Fonts `<link>` for Montserrat is ignored by many clients. The fallback stack can't be checked in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source. The recipient address is substituted correctly into the pixel URLs. The source is truncated, so the rest of the body is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. If they are really absent, that is a gap against Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.

- **Footer not verifiable:** the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address fall in the truncated section.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM params not verifiable.** Every CTA is wrapped in an opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…` redirect, so the final URLs and any UTM parameters can't be seen.
- **Product images hotlinked from the site CDN:** `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/ab/images/dp/wcm/…`. They load at send time, so a product being delisted could break an image.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` loads from a sister-brand domain. It works, but it is an extra dependency on another domain.
- **Landing-page alignment** can't be assessed without resolved destinations.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, starting with the hero slices (`F26_Seasonal_B1_HalloweenShop/slices_01–04.jpg`) and the product images. Use `alt=""` for spacers and pixels.
2. Move `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, the 2022 `slices_02.jpg` and the `svopn.potterybarn.com` pixel to HTTPS.
3. Stop putting the raw email address in query strings (`coreemail.html?email=`, `sv_sveme=`). Use a hashed or opaque ID instead.
4. Fill in `<title>`, add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and remove `maximum-scale=1`.
5. Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP lines from the `<style>` block.
6. Re-run the QA against raw `.eml` source to check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Resolve the 46 redirects to check UTM parameters and landing pages.
