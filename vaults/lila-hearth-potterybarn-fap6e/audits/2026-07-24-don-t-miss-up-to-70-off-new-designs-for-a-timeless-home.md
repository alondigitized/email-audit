# Don't miss UP TO 70% OFF + NEW designs for a timeless home
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-24**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn trying to thread two competing needles in one email — a 70%-off sale pitch and a new-arrivals editorial — and it doesn't fully commit to either. The subject line leads hard with "UP TO 70% OFF" but the rendered body opens on a new-arrivals lifestyle hero that reads almost identically to the "Your first look: 1600+ NEW arrivals" send from earlier today. The 70% off discount, which is the actual hook in the subject, isn't visibly prominent in the hero frame — it's buried downstream, making the first scroll feel like a bait-and-switch.
- The visual execution is competent: warm lifestyle photography, clean grid below the hero, the "Indigo Blues" collection section is a nice edit, and the "Recommended Just for You" module adds personalization weight. But none of that compensates for the fundamental structural problem: the subject and the body are telling two different stories.
- This is also Pottery Barn's third email on July 24 — following "Your first look: 1600+ NEW arrivals" and "Work with our designers for FREE." That cadence creates real fatigue risk, and subjects like "Don't miss" explicitly acknowledge it (don't miss what you already got twice today).

## What's working

- **Lifestyle photography is strong** — warm neutrals in the hero living room shot are aspirational and on-brand.
- **"Indigo Blues" collection section** gives the email a product-story anchor beyond generic new arrivals.
- **"Recommended Just for You" module** is the right personalization play and sits where a subscriber would actually scroll to.
- **Clean layout** — no broken images, no overlapping text, no visible render bugs.

## What's weak

- **Subject-body disconnect** — "UP TO 70% OFF" is the subject hook but the hero reads as a pure new-arrivals send; the discount isn't prominent above the fold.
- **"Don't miss" opener is a cadence tell** — this phrasing only makes sense when you're worried subscribers are tuning out (and after two prior sends today, they should be).
- **"Timeless home" is evergreen copy, not seasonal** — no urgency or summer/fall transition hook visible in the body to make this feel time-relevant.
- **No visible deadline or expiry on the 70% off** — without a "ends Sunday" or similar, the offer reads as perpetual and therefore low-urgency.
- **Third send in one day** from the same sender; cadence is burning goodwill and suppression risk rises.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pick one story and commit** — either lead the body with a 70%-off hero banner (full-bleed, countdown timer, "Ends Sunday") or send a clean new-arrivals email. Mixing the two halves the effectiveness of both.
- 2. **Add a visible expiry to the 70% off** — a bold "Ends July 27" line under the hero discount call-out would convert the casual scroller who needs a nudge.
- 3. **Replace "Don't miss" with the actual news** — the subject is doing damage control; rewrite it around the single strongest hook (either the discount OR the arrivals, not both).
- 4. **Rest the cadence** — three sends in one day is a deliverability and unsubscribe accelerant; the July 24 "1600+ NEW arrivals" already did the catalog reset job.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 70% off ends soon — shop before it's gone`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Indigo Blues + 70% off select styles`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New arrivals just landed — now with our biggest summer discount`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sale ends Sunday. New Indigo Blues collection is in.`

## Technical Audit — Pottery Barn "UP TO 70% OFF + NEW designs" Email

### 1. Technical Summary
Two hard-broken links (403) will result in a failed credit card banner experience and a missing tracking pixel. The email also contains PHP header injection artifacts in a `<style>` block, 3 non-HTTPS image sources, and 44 images with no alt text.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**BROKEN — Credit card apply banner (403)**
```
https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/c/da01b407-b21c-4618-a71b-f83bef0dd682?brandCode=POTTERYBARNCB...
```
The "Apply & Buy" / Key Rewards member banner CTA returns HTTP 403. The redirect target is inaccessible; recipients clicking this will hit a dead end.

**BROKEN — West Elm spacer pixel (403)**
```
http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif
```
Cross-brand pixel from West Elm's domain returns 403. This is a stale asset reference — also served over HTTP (see §3).

**Tracking links skipped (24)**
All `click.e.potterybarn.com` and `clickcp.potterybarn.com` redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe. Destination URLs and their HTTP status codes are unverified. The two confirmed 403s above both go through `clickcp.potterybarn.com`, suggesting a systematic issue with that click-tracking subdomain.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**PHP `header()` calls inside a `<style>` block**
The following literal PHP code appears inside a `<style>` tag in `<head>`:
```css
header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
header("Cache-Control: post-check=0, pre-check=0", false);
header("Pragma: no-cache");
```
This is an unrendered server-side template artifact — the PHP was never executed, leaving raw source in the CSS. It will not crash rendering, but it is invalid CSS and represents a template/build pipeline defect.

**3 image sources served over HTTP (not HTTPS)**
Modern email clients (Gmail, Apple Mail) block mixed-content images:
- `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`

`Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` is a visible Key Rewards footer banner — if blocked, subscribers see a broken image where a CTA should be. The 2022-dated path on `slices_02.jpg` suggests a stale footer asset.

**Open pixel also served over HTTP**
```
http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...
```
The open-tracking pixel uses HTTP — will be blocked in secure clients, undercounting open metrics.

**44 images missing `alt` text**
The full grid of content images (`slices_01–slices_13` across multiple modules), all product images, and the footer slices (`slices_01–slices_18`) have empty or absent `alt` attributes. Recipients using screen readers get no content. In image-blocking clients (Outlook default), the email renders as blank space with no fallback text.

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header absent**
The header was not captured by the AgentMail relay. If it is genuinely missing from the envelope, Gmail and Yahoo (per their 2024 bulk sender requirements) will surface their own unsubscribe UI — and sustained absence can trigger deliverability penalties for senders at this volume.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) absent**
One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail/Yahoo for bulk senders. Without this header, one-click unsub is non-functional. This may be a relay capture gap, but should be confirmed at the MTA level.

**Note:** Both header warnings may be artifacts of the AgentMail relay not forwarding them. Verify against raw SMTP headers before treating as a production defect.

The plain-text alternative is URL-dense with no readable prose. Spam filters weight the plain-text part; this ratio is a deliverability risk and offers zero fallback reading experience.
