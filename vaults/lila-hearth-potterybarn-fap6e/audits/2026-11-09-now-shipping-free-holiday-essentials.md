# Now shipping free 🎄 Holiday essentials
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a solid seasonal send with a mixed message. The "Festive Favorites Ship Free" hero is strong: a tartan-ribboned tree, stockings and a clear "SHOP NOW" button. It matches the subject, and the three stacked tiles (ornaments & tree toppers, keepsake stockings, tree skirts & collars) are easy to scan. The free-shipping hook is the right one for the week.
- It's undercut by the first thing you see. A "The Fall Sale: Up to 70% off" banner sits above the holiday hero. The subject promises free shipping on holiday essentials, while the banner leads with a different offer on a different season. On Nov 9 the "Fall" label feels stale. Two offers compete for the eye, and neither gets full weight.
- The lower half is a long scroll of generic modules: bedding, pillows, recommended products, design services, app download, Affirm and a credit card. Fine print fills the footer. It's a competent, on-brand Pottery Barn send, but it's diffuse after the hero. It follows the brand's recent pattern (Nov 7 "Ships free 🎄 Festive pillows & more decor") with little new.

## What's working

- The hero ties directly to the subject: "Festive Favorites Ship Free" over a tartan tree photo.
- The three category tiles each have a distinct image and a lowercase script label, so the hierarchy is clear.
- The "SHOP ALL FREE SHIPPING" button gives a single catch-all destination.
- The recommended-products grid shows named items with sizes (e.g. Toscana Extendable Dining Table 60"/125"), which adds useful product detail.
- Photography is rich and consistent with the brand voice.

## What's weak

- The "Fall Sale: Up to 70% off" banner above the hero competes with the free-shipping message and reads as the wrong season.
- The small italic overlay text on the hero ("ornaments, stockings & more") has low contrast against the busy photo.
- There is no deadline or urgency on the free shipping, so there is no reason to click today.
- The bottom third is bloated: app download, Affirm, a credit card promo and a dense fine-print block dilute the holiday message.
- The CTAs are generic "Shop Now" and the tile links give no price or offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Remove or demote the "Fall Sale" banner, or merge it into one headline such as "Holiday sale: up to 70% off + free shipping". One offer should own the top.
- 2. Add a real deadline to the free shipping, such as an order-by date for delivery before Christmas.
- 3. Give the hero text a solid or gradient backing so the overlay is legible.
- 4. Make the tile CTAs specific, e.g. "Shop stockings from $X", and show a price or discount on each tile.
- 5. Cut the footer modules (app, Affirm, credit card) down to one line, or move them below the fine print.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus up to 70% off. Get tartan stockings and tree toppers delivered before Christmas.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders through opaque ESP click-redirects (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…`) and sliced, image-based layouts. The QA run reported 0 hard failures and 4 warnings. The main code-level concerns are widespread missing `alt` text, HTTP-sourced images and pixels that carry the recipient's email address, and stray non-CSS code inside a `<style>` block. Header-level compliance and authentication checks could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverifiable links:** 52 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, so none were probed for status or destination. Example: `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7ImQi…`. Their liveness and final URLs are unconfirmed.
- **Cleartext HTTP tracking pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`. It is non-HTTPS, so it may be blocked or flagged as mixed content. It also sends the recipient's email address in plaintext.
- **Email address in image URLs over HTTPS:** `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is loaded as an `<img>`. The address ends up in URL logs.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEv…/cat/1` is a third-party ad-network pixel (Turn). It is missing `alt` and adds a tracking dependency.
- **Another pixel:** `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/7e075588-…?mi_cid=…` is an open-tracking pixel with no `alt`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 45 images lack `alt`.
  - Hero and body slices: `slices_01.jpg`–`slices_11.jpg` (`/1109_MON_AM_HolidayFreeShip/`) and `H26_TOP_FallSale.jpg`.
  - Product images: for example `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-125-l.jpg` and `classic-velvet-stockings-l.jpg`.
  - Footer slices: `/Email-Footer/slices_01.jpg`–`slices_18.jpg`.
  - Banners: `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`.
  - With images blocked, the offer content and navigation are blank to users and screen readers. Only the header logo has `alt="POTTERY BARN"`.
- **Mixed-content images (HTTP):**
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/...` (the pixel above)
- **Stray code in CSS:** the final `<style>` block contains PHP-style statements: `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store…")`. This is invalid CSS that can break parsing of rules after it in strict clients.
- **Empty `<title>`:** `<title></title>`.
- **Viewport:** `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility concern.
- **Web font:** the Google Fonts `<link>` (`Montserrat`) is unsupported or blocked in many clients, such as Gmail and Outlook.
- **Image width rule:** `img {max-width: 650px}` and `img {max-width: 100%!important}` overlap across the 650/700px media queries.
- **Layout markup:** the layout uses nested tables, and the preheader uses `role="presentation"`.
- **Stale or cross-brand assets:**
  - `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a spacer hosted on the West Elm domain.
  - `/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/` is a 2022 asset in a 2026 send.
  - `H26_TOP_FallSale.jpg` is a "FallSale" banner in a holiday-shipping email.

- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) were found in the visible source.
- Recipient email is correctly substituted into the pixel URLs (`lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`). See Section 2 for the PII exposure.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** not found. QA notes this may be stripped by the AgentMail relay, so it is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found, with the same relay caveat. One-click support is unverified.
- **Authentication-Results:** absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `PotteryBarn@e.potterybarn.com`.
- **Body footer:** the HTML source was truncated before the footer. The unsubscribe link and physical address could not be assessed.
- **Plain-text part:** 9,998 of 14,193 characters (about 70%) are URLs, which the QA check flags as a deliverability signal.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All 52 tracked links go through `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…`, so no UTM parameters or final landing URLs are visible or verified.
- Product images point to `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/…`, which is the right domain. Click-through targets could not be checked.
- Stale assets (see Section 3) suggest the "Free Shipping" offer may not match the landing banners, but that can't be confirmed from the code.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content image, and `alt=""` to spacers and pixels.
2. Switch the three `http://` image and pixel sources to `https://`.
3. Remove the PHP `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block.
4. Stop passing the plain recipient email in image and pixel query strings. Use an opaque ID instead, or at least keep it on HTTPS.
5. Add a non-empty `<title>`.
6. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
7. Host the spacer on the sender's own domain, and remove the 2022 and fall-sale assets.
8. Resolve a sample of the redirect links manually to confirm the landing pages and UTMs.
9. Re-check List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post and Authentication-Results against the raw message. That should be done before the relay, which may be stripping them, rather than through it.
10. Improve the plain-text part's text-to-URL ratio.
