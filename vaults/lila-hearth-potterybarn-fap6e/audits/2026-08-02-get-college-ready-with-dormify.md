# Get college-ready with Dormify✨
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-02**
## Executive summary

- This is a "Spotlight On" collab email pairing Pottery Barn (via Williams-Sonoma, Inc.) with dorm-decor brand Dormify — a category showcase, not a sale. It's organized cleanly by dorm need (bedding, storage, furniture, bath/laundry), each with a lifestyle shot, a one-line hook ("Sleep better," "Store smarter," "Feel at home," "Stay fresh"), and its own shop button.
- The execution is tidy — consistent typography, no broken images, no overlapping text — but there's no actual offer anywhere in the body. The only monetary hooks are a generic "10% Back in Rewards" credit-card pitch and small "SHIPS FREE" badges tucked into two product photos. For a back-to-school send in peak August timing, that's a missed chance to lead with urgency or savings.
- The bigger issue is cadence: this lands the same day PB apparently also sent a "Back to School Styles up to 50% Off" email (per the vault history). Two college-themed sends on one day, one with a real discount and one without, is redundant and risks training subscribers to skip the non-discount version.

## What's working

- Clean, single-purpose layout — each section pairs one lifestyle photo with one clear category and one button, no clutter.
- "SHOP TWIN XL BEDDING" and similar CTAs are specific enough to set expectations before the click.
- The dorm-sizing callout (Twin XL) is a real product detail a college shopper actually needs, not generic marketing copy.
- "Pick up near campus" with a Williams-Sonoma, Inc. store finder is a genuinely useful, differentiated CTA for this audience.
- No visual bugs — imagery, badges, and text all render as intended.

## What's weak

- Zero concrete offer in the entire body — no %, no $ off, no BOGO; only a buried credit-card rewards pitch far down the email.
- Six competing CTAs (Shop Essentials, Shop Twin XL Bedding, Shop Storage, Shop Furniture, Shop Bath & Laundry, Start Shopping) with no single primary action — the eye has nowhere obvious to land first.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere, despite back-to-school being an inherently time-boxed shopping window.
- No social proof (reviews, ratings) on any product section, which matters for a first-time dorm buyer deciding what to trust.
- Likely same-day overlap with a discount-led "back to school" send, undercutting this one's reason to exist.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real, time-bound offer to the hero (e.g., "20% off dorm essentials through 8/15") — right now there's nothing to click for, only to browse for.
- 2. Pick one primary CTA for the hero fold (e.g., "Shop the Dorm Shop") and demote the five category buttons to a secondary grid below — six equal-weight buttons dilute intent.
- 3. Coordinate send timing with other back-to-school campaigns so this doesn't land the same day as a discount-led college email — merge the two into one send with Dormify as the creative hook and the discount as the offer.
- 4. Surface a rating/review snippet on at least the bedding and furniture sections — dorm buyers lean heavily on social proof for unfamiliar SKUs.
- 5. Move "Pick up near campus" higher — it's the most differentiated, useful CTA in the email and it's buried near the footer.

# Technical Audit: Pottery Barn / Dormify Email

## 1. Technical Summary
This is an SFMC-style (ExactTarget) HTML email using `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirect wrappers for links and `edm.dormify.com`/`edm.potterybarn.com` for images; automated QA passed at 57% with 0 hard failures but 3 warning categories (compliance headers, HTTP image sources, missing alt attributes).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All 39 outbound links route through the `click.e.potterybarn.com/?qs=...` redirect wrapper (base64/opaque `qs` param) — QA skipped HTTP probing on these by design, so final destination and HTTP status are unverified.
- Multiple third-party tracking pixels are embedded as `<img>` tags rather than proper 1x1 beacons in markup: `secure.leadback.advertising.com/adcedge/lb?site=695501&srvc=1&betr=...`, `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/...`, and `svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...`. These are functioning as expected for open tracking but inflate the "missing alt text" count below.
- No broken/malformed href syntax found in the visible source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid CSS/code leakage**: the `<style>` block in `<head>` contains raw PHP function calls (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store...")`) sitting inside a `<style>` tag. This is not valid CSS and appears to be a template/build artifact (likely copy-pasted from a PHP image-serving script). Most mail clients will ignore it as inert text, but it should be stripped — it signals a broken build/merge step upstream.
- 27 images missing `alt` text per QA, including all footer "slice" images (`slices_01.jpg` through `slices_18.jpg`), `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, and the tracking-pixel images noted above. Screen readers and images-off clients will render these as blank.
- 13 images served over `http://` instead of `https://`: all `edm.dormify.com/dm/2026/0810_OBO/*.jpg` hero/nav images plus `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `slices_02.jpg` on `edm.potterybarn.com`. Mixed-content/non-HTTPS sources are commonly blocked or flagged by Outlook, Gmail image proxies, and corporate mail filters, risking broken hero images for recipients.
- Mobile responsive rules exist (`@media (max-width:480px)` and `@media (max-width:650px)` with `.mobile-hide`/`.mobile-show`), and `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` is present — standard defensive markup, no issues there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- Merge field resolved correctly: recipient email `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is properly substituted into both the `coreemail.html?email=...` preference-center link and the `svopn.potterybarn.com` open-tracking pixel (`sv_sveme=...`). No unresolved `%%...%%` or `{{...}}` tokens found in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — may be a relay capture gap (AgentMail) rather than a true sender omission, but cannot be confirmed from headers alone.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058, one-click unsubscribe) not detected — if genuinely absent at the sending MTA, this is a compliance gap for Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender requirements.
- `Authentication-Results` header not detected — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from this capture.
- Body-level unsubscribe link/physical address cannot be confirmed or refuted — the HTML source provided is truncated before the footer/legal block.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Links are wrapped in SFMC redirects (`click.e.potterybarn.com/?qs=...`) with opaque encoded payloads rather than visible UTM query params, so campaign-parameter alignment with the landing page cannot be verified from source alone (requires following the redirect, which QA explicitly skipped).
- Cross-domain asset sourcing: email is sent from `e.potterybarn.com`/links wrapped under `click.e.potterybarn.com`, but creative images are hosted on `edm.dormify.com` — a different brand's email domain. This is an infrastructure/domain-alignment detail worth confirming with ESP config, since it means Dormify creative assets depend on Dormify's CDN uptime for a Pottery Barn-sent campaign.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Strip the stray PHP `header()` calls from the `<style>` block in `<head>` — dead code from a broken build/merge, not functional CSS.
2. Migrate all `http://edm.dormify.com` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com` image sources to `https://` to prevent client-side blocking.
3. Add descriptive (or explicitly empty `alt=""` for decorative/pixel) `alt` attributes to the 27 flagged images, particularly the footer slice images which likely convey policy/legal info.
4. Confirm with the ESP/relay whether `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers are actually being sent at the MTA level — re-check with full raw headers rather than the AgentMail-relayed capture, since one-click unsubscribe is a Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirement.
5. Verify `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) directly against the sending ESP's logs, since relay capture may not preserve it.
6. Spot-check a sample of the 39 wrapped tracking links by following redirects manually to confirm they resolve to live, correctly-tagged landing pages.
