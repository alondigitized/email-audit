# Up to 70% off is happening now + Decor details that make all the difference
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-13**
## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn sends a second email on July 13 — and does it again with the familiar compound subject formula: a sale anchor on the left of the "+" and an editorial hook on the right. The execution is cleaner than yesterday's double-send: the "Easy Style Updates" section (pillows, throws, botanicals, vases) is a genuine effort to translate the editorial hook into shoppable category tiles, and the visual hierarchy from hero to grid to "Recommended Just for You" is coherent. The Summer Sale banner with "Up to 70% off" and "100s of new styles added" is prominent and believable.
- What drags this down is cadence and structural repetition. This brand has now sent a sale-heavy email every single day of July (plus multiple on July 11 and now a second on July 13). The "Recommended Just for You" product rail looks algorithmically generic — full-size sofa shots don't scream décor-impulse purchase. And the subject line at 72 characters is a mobile truncation guarantee; the "Decor details that make all the difference" half disappears before most subscribers ever read it.
- Solid mid-range execution. Familiar enough to feel like a brand, tired enough to feel like a conveyor belt.

## What's working

- **Hero is immediate.** "Summer Sale: Up to 70% off" is the first readable element; no banner, no nav bar eating real estate above the offer.
- **"Easy Style Updates" category grid is thematically tight.** Pillows, throws, botanicals, vases — four high-impulse, low-commitment décor categories that match the editorial subject hook better than most of their hybrid sends.
- **Affirm + Key Rewards visible.** Both lower-funnel purchase objections are surfaced — instalment option and loyalty upside — without crowding the main creative.
- **No render bugs.** Layout holds up cleanly; images load, text doesn't overlap.

## What's weak

- **Second email of the day is a cadence risk.** Yesterday's double-send (July 12) was already flagged; doing it again on July 13 trains the subscriber to mute or filter.
- **"Recommended Just for You" is clearly not personalized.** Wide living-room sofa shots don't feel like décor impulse items — they feel like a generic furniture grid that undermines the editorial positioning.
- **Subject line is 72 characters.** The editorial second half ("Decor details that make all the difference") is invisible on mobile. The send leads with the stronger hook but buries it.
- **No deadline on the sale.** "Is happening now" implies urgency but delivers none — there's no end date, countdown, or even a season-end reference to anchor the 70% claim.
- **"Discover New Arrivals" section at bottom competes with the sale.** Two distinct narratives (sale + new arrivals) in one send after already doing a pure new-arrivals email earlier today is confusing.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Drop to one email today.** This is the highest-leverage fix: merging the new-arrivals send from this morning into this one, or holding this until tomorrow, eliminates cadence fatigue and subscriber burnout that's building across the July run.
- 2. **Cut the subject to the editorial hook; make the sale the preheader.** The "Decor details" angle is more scroll-stopping than "Up to 70% off" (subscribers have seen 70% off every day this week). Swap them.
- 3. **Add a sale end date.** "Ends Sunday" or "through July 20" converts "happening now" into real urgency without any creative lift.
- 4. **Replace the generic sofa grid in "Recommended Just for You" with décor accessories.** If the category tiles are pillows/throws/botanicals/vases, the product recommendations beneath should be items in those categories — not full-room furniture.
- 5. **Consolidate the New Arrivals section into the "Easy Style Updates" grid** rather than running it as a separate module — reduces scroll length and eliminates the competing narrative.
- **Subject Alt A:** `The décor details subscribers buy first in summer`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Pillows, throws & botanicals — all on summer sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Up to 70% off, now through Sunday — shop the refresh.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `100s of new styles added. Sale ends soon.`

### 5. Compliance

If the header is present at the MTA but stripped by the AgentMail relay, this is a monitoring gap rather than a send defect — confirm against raw SMTP headers.

**WARN — `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** One-click unsubscribe via POST is required by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders as of 2024. If absent at the MTA level, this is a compliance gap.

**WARN — `Authentication-Results` not captured.** SPF/DKIM pass status unknown from relay. Sending domain `e.potterybarn.com` should have SPF and DKIM aligned to `potterybarn.com` for DMARC pass. Cannot confirm without raw headers.
