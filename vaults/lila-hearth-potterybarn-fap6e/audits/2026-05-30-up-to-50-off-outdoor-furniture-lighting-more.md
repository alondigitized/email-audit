# Up to 50% off outdoor furniture, lighting & more
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-30**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, competent outdoor sale email — well-produced hero image, logical category breakdown, and a genuinely useful "Recommended Just For You" personalization module that the May 28 outdoor sale email lacked. On execution alone it's solid. The problem is context: this is at minimum the third outdoor-sale email in three days, and arguably the weakest reason to open it. The subject "Up to 50% off outdoor furniture, lighting & more" is identical in spirit to the May 28 send that already scored 9/10. If the sale ends Sunday (tomorrow), nothing in this email says so — the last-chance urgency that would justify a repeat send has been left on the table.
- The personalization module is the only material upgrade over the prior sends, and it's buried below the fold. Swap its placement with the category grid and this email has a real reason to exist. As-is, it reads like a resend with a slightly different hero crop.

## What's working

- Hero image is aspirational and seasonally on-point — white outdoor sofa, bright airy setting, pulls the eye immediately.
- "Up to 50% off Outdoor" headline is large, readable, and unambiguous above the fold.
- "SHOP ALL OUTDOOR FURNITURE" CTA is specific and action-oriented — better than a vague "Shop Now."
- "Recommended Just For You" personalization block is a genuine differentiator vs. the May 28 send; shows real behavioral awareness.
- Clean render — no broken images, no layout breaks, no lorem ipsum.

## What's weak

- No visible deadline anywhere above the fold — if the sale ends Sunday this is the last-chance send, but nothing communicates it.
- "Up to 50% off" is the broadest possible discount framing; no anchor product with a specific before/after price to make the offer feel real.
- Personalization module is buried well below the fold — the highest-relevance content is the least visible.
- Cadence: third outdoor-sale email in three days from the same brand, and this one doesn't give subscribers a new reason to care.
- "Pottery Barn Sale" sender display name signals promotional rather than editorial, making it easier to skip in a crowded inbox.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a visible deadline in the hero** — if the sale ends Sunday, say "Ends Tomorrow" or "Last Day" in the hero band. The May 28 email had "through Sunday" and scored 9/10 partly for that. This one doesn't have it.
- 2. **Pull "Recommended Just For You" above the category grid** — it's the most relevant content for a returning subscriber; make it the second thing they see after the hero.
- 3. **Feature one specific hero product** with a dollar price, not just "up to 50% off" — even one SKU ("The Chatham Sofa — now $XXX") makes the offer feel honest and clickable.
- 4. **Change sender display name** from "Pottery Barn Sale" to "Pottery Barn" — the "Sale" label reads as a promotional filter trigger.
- 5. **Subject Alt A:** `Last day: up to 50% off outdoor furniture`
- 6. **Subject Alt B:** `Outdoor sale ends tomorrow — chairs, dining & more`
- 7. **Preheader Alt A:** `Dining sets, lounge chairs, lighting — all on sale through tonight`
- 8. **Preheader Alt B:** `Your picks are waiting — sale ends Sunday`

## Technical Audit — Pottery Barn "Up to 50% off outdoor furniture" (2026-05-30)

### 1. Technical Summary

Two hard-broken links (403) will silently fail for all recipients, and PHP header directives are embedded verbatim inside a `<style>` block — a template assembly defect with no runtime effect but a clear indicator of a broken build process. Overall QA pass rate is 33%.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**[FAIL] Credit card banner CTA — 403**
`https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/c/da01b407-b21c-4618-a71b-f83bef0dd682?brandCode=POTTERYBARNCB&marketingChannelCode=APPLYANDBUY&external_id=PB_Email_MemberBanner&cm_ite=PB_header_nonmember`
The "Apply and Buy" / Key Rewards card banner is dead for every recipient. The `clickcp` redirect domain is returning 403 rather than the expected redirect.

**[FAIL] West Elm spacer pixel — 403 + cross-brand asset**
`http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`
A West Elm (sibling brand) asset is embedded in a Pottery Barn email and is currently returning 403. This is a shared-template inheritance artifact — a stale cross-brand include that should have been removed or replaced.

**23 tracking links skipped:** All click-redirects through `click.e.potterybarn.com` were not probed. Link integrity on those destinations is unverified.

**Third-party ad pixel present:**
`https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk5MzMvdC8y/cat/1`
Turn/Amobee (now Sizmek) retargeting pixel fires on open. Introduces a third-party data dependency with no `async` or failure isolation.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**PHP header directives in `<style>` block — template defect**
The following literal text appears inside a `<style>` tag:
```
header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
header("Cache-Control: post-check=0, pre-check=0", false);
header("Pragma: no-cache");
```
These are PHP `header()` calls that were not executed server-side and were emitted as CSS text. They are inert in email clients but confirm a template rendering failure in the build pipeline for this module.

**48 images missing `alt` text**
All 16 `slices_N.jpg` content images, all 16 footer `slices_N.jpg`, plus product images (`H25_TOP_InStockFurniture.jpg`, `cayman-platform-bed-l.jpg`, `hydrocotton-quick-dry-towel-l.jpg`, etc.) have empty or absent `alt` attributes. When images are blocked (Gmail Promotions tab, Outlook default), recipients see a blank email.

**3 HTTP (non-HTTPS) image sources — blocking risk**
- `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` (also 403)
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`

The CC banner footer asset (`Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`) and a 2022 brand banner slice are served over plain HTTP. Modern email clients and corporate proxies will block mixed-content image loads. The 2022 asset path suggests this module has not been refreshed in 4 years.

**Open-tracking pixel over HTTP**
`http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true`
The Silverpop/Watson open-tracking beacon is HTTP, not HTTPS. This pixel will be blocked by any client enforcing HTTPS-only image loading, causing open-rate undercounting.

**Empty `<title>` tag**
`<title></title>` — no impact on deliverability but causes accessibility tools and some preview panes to display nothing.

### 5. Compliance

Not detected by AgentMail relay. If genuinely missing from the envelope, Gmail will suppress the native unsubscribe UI for Gmail users, and the send is non-compliant with Google/Yahoo 2024 bulk sender requirements.

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) absent**
One-click unsubscribe (required by Google/Yahoo for >5k/day senders) is not confirmed present. Without this header, Gmail shows a two-step unsubscribe flow and may throttle delivery.

**[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not captured**
SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown. Cannot confirm alignment between the `From:` domain (`e.potterybarn.com`) and the signing domain.

**CAN-SPAM:** Physical address and unsubscribe mechanism appear present in footer (standard for PB templates). No issues flagged from visible source.

`<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow" />` is correctly set.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**Plain-text body is 71% URLs (10,217 / 14,366 chars)**
The multipart/alternative text part is essentially a raw link dump with no readable prose. This is a known spam signal for Outlook and aggressive corporate filters. A text part should summarize offer copy, not enumerate every redirect URL.

**UTM parameter verification blocked**
All 23 outbound CTAs route through `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirects, which were skipped by the probe. UTM parameter presence on landing pages could not be confirmed. No structural issues detected in the HTML link markup itself.

**Stale asset from 2022 campaign**
