# NEW BLACK FRIDAY DEALS
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the same burgundy-plaid "Black Friday, up to 70% off" Pottery Barn template that has run since 11/16. The only new element is a small "New deals added" kicker above the headline. It lands one day after the 11/25 "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY + Open Box Outlet" send and the same day as "First look 📣 NEW Black Friday deals." That is roughly the thirteenth near-identical send in eleven days.
- The hero is clear. A big "70% off" and a single "Shop Now" button sit at the top. Below it, a category stack of Furniture, Sofas, Bedding, Decor, Pillows, Tabletop and Gifts gives each category a clear percentage and a hero product with was/now pricing. That is the strongest part of the email.
- The email is competent but stale. A subscriber who has seen a dozen of these has no reason to treat "NEW deals" as news. The subject is a shouty, all-caps near-duplicate of this morning's "First look" send. The render is clean and well organized, but it is fatigue-prone and very long.

## What's working

- The hero offer is unmistakable: "up to 70% off" in large type with one "Shop Now" button.
- Each category block pairs a percentage with a named hero product and was/now pricing. Examples are the Benchwright Dining Table, Cloud Quilt and Gingerbread Plates at $24.
- "Ships Free" tags on several tiles add a second incentive without clutter.
- The "In-Store Savings / Find Your Store" band gives a clear omnichannel path.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlaps. The seasonal plaid and holiday styling are on-theme.

## What's weak

- The template is unchanged since 11/16, and the "New deals added" kicker is the only visible update. Cadence fatigue is real.
- Discounts range from "up to 20%" (Ornaments) to "up to 70%," and the headline number only applies to a few categories. This reads as a mild bait-and-switch.
- The email is extremely long, with seven category tiles, a recommended grid, a brands strip, Christmas Shop, app, credit card and Affirm sections, then dense fine print. The primary CTA is repeated, and the lower sections dilute it.
- Every category has its own "Shop Now" link, so the eye has no single priority after the hero.
- There is no visible deadline or urgency date, despite "Black Friday" being the premise.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the repetition. Make this send visibly different from this morning's "First look" email, or skip it. Consolidate the same-day sends into one.
- 2. Add a credible deadline to the hero, such as "Ends Friday at midnight."
- 3. Trim the body to three or four top categories and move the brand strip, app, card and Affirm sections to a lighter footer.
- 4. State the real range in the hero ("20–70% off") so the 70% headline feels honest.
- 5. Rotate the hero imagery. A fresh lead product would signal that the deals really are new.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Fresh Black Friday drops, ends Friday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Added today: dining tables from $2,429 and bedding up to 70% off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Ships free on select styles. Sale ends Friday at midnight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, image-sliced layout with working click-tracking wrappers. The source has a malformed `<style>` block, responsive selectors that match no elements, linked images with empty `alt`, and the recipient's address in plaintext in third-party pixel URLs. Header-level compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because of the relay. The HTML source and several QA checks were also truncated, which limits what can be confirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links can't be verified.** 64 links were skipped because they go through `click.e.potterybarn.com/?qs=<opaque token>`. Destinations and status codes are unconfirmed.
- **Third-party pixels in the body.**
  - `secure.leadback.advertising.com/adcedge/lb?site=695501…`
  - `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/…/cat/1`
  - `svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
- **Plaintext recipient address in URLs.** The address appears in `sv_sveme=` and in `potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`. These are image requests, so the address is exposed to any proxy, log or third party that sees the URL.
- **Dead code shipped to recipients.** The commented-out credit-card banner block contains a live-looking link with `cm_ite=PB_header_nonmember`. It is harmless but adds bytes and leaves stale tracking params in the source.
- **Cross-brand asset.** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is loaded from another brand's host.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed `<style>` block.** The second `<style>` block contains PHP-style text before the first rule: `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store…"); …`. CSS error recovery will likely discard the next rule, `a[x-apple-data-detectors] {…}`, which breaks the iOS auto-link suppression it was meant to provide.
- **Responsive rules match nothing visible.**
  - The media query targets `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]`.
  - The visible tables are plain `<table width="650">` and the slice images have no `class` attribute.
  - The layout will therefore stay fixed at 650px on mobile.
  - Elements further down the truncated source may use these classes.
- **Empty `alt` on linked images.** Hero slices such as `slices_01.jpg` to `slices_03.jpg` have `alt=""` inside `<a>` elements. Those links have no accessible name, and QA flags about 50 more images across the product, footer and banner blocks.
- **Pixels without alt.** The three tracking pixels (`lb`, `1`, `svopn…`) also lack `alt`. This is low priority, but they should carry `alt=""` and 1x1 sizing.
- **Mixed content.** Two images use `http://`:
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` (a 2022 asset in a 2026 send)
- **Document structure.**
  - Ampscript comments come before the `<!DOCTYPE>`.
  - The XHTML 1.0 Strict doctype is used alongside `<center>`, `bgcolor` and `align`, which are invalid under Strict.
  - There is no `lang` attribute on `<html>`, no `<title>`, and no `role="presentation"` on layout tables.
- **Preheader.** No issues found.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Merge tokens resolved.** The recipient address is correctly substituted in the pixel and `coreemail.html` URLs, and no unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `%%[…]%%` tokens appear in the visible source.
- **Source truncated.** Tokens later in the body or footer can't be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Headers not captured.** `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results` were all not found. QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so these are unconfirmed rather than confirmed defects. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- **Footer not visible.** The unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated source, so they can't be confirmed.
- **From address.** `PotteryBarn@e.potterybarn.com` is a branded subdomain and is consistent with the click domain `click.e.potterybarn.com`.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Tracking is opaque.** The visible click URLs carry only `qs=` tokens, with no `utm_*` parameters, so campaign attribution can't be inspected from the source.
- **Landing pages not checked.** The HTTP probe was skipped, so destination alignment with "NEW BLACK FRIDAY DEALS" is unverified.
- **Direct links.** The only direct `potterybarn.com` links visible are the commented-out banner (`cm_ite=…`) and `coreemail.html`.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the stray `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block and confirm that `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` applies.
2. Add `class="wrapper"` and `class="photo"` to the layout tables and images, or rewrite the media query for the actual markup.
3. Give the linked slices meaningful `alt` text (offer or destination). Use `alt=""` only on decorative or tracking images.
4. Switch the `http://` assets to HTTPS and retire the 2022 `0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner` slice.
5. Stop putting the raw email address in pixel and `coreemail.html` query strings. Use a hashed or opaque ID.
6. Strip commented-out blocks at send time. Move ampscript comments below the doctype, and add `lang`, `<title>` and `role="presentation"`.
7. Re-run the header checks against the raw MIME from the original sender to confirm `List-Unsubscribe` (with `-Post`) and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
8. Resolve a sample of the `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirects to confirm landing URLs and UTM or `cm_*` parameters.
