# Our most-loved bedroom collections
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, well-organized bedroom catalog send, and it has no offer. "Our Bestselling Beds" opens on a large hero with a SHOP NOW button. Three named beds follow: the Sausalito Cane Bed, the Farmhouse Bed and the Cayman Bed. Each has finish swatches and its own SHOP NOW, then a SHOP ALL BEDS button. The subject and hero match, and the layout is easy to scan.
- The weakness is motivation. Compared with the 2026-10-16 "Love pattern? This bedding is for you" send, which at least had a "Quilts ship free" pill, nothing here gives a reason to click today. After the bed section the email drifts into generic modules: an Upholstered Beds tile, "More for your bedroom" links, a "Recommended just for you" grid, "Shop our Latest Designs," Design Crew, the app, a card and Affirm. The grid mixes in dining tables and a curtain rod, so the email stops being about bedrooms. At about eight screens long, it reads as a template.
- Judgement: a good browse email with no hook. Next to the more specific category sends this week, it's a mid-pack 6.

## What's working

- The hero is on-theme. A warm, well-lit bed photo sits under "Our Bestselling Beds," and the subject delivers on it.
- The three bed modules are consistent. Each has a product name, a short descriptor ("weathered wood meets airy cane"), swatch dots and a button.
- The finish swatches show product detail (wood and upholstery options) without a click.
- The "More for your bedroom" row (Nightstands, Dressers, Bedding, Rugs) gives a sensible cross-sell path.
- The "Explore Our Holiday New Arrivals" strip at the top keeps the email seasonal.

## What's weak

- No offer, shipping perk or deadline is visible anywhere above the fold. The one credit-card incentive ("Earn 10% Back in Rewards") is buried near the bottom.
- Every button is a generic "SHOP NOW" or "SHOP ALL BEDS." None says what the click gets you.
- The "Recommended just for you" grid is off-topic for a bedroom email. It has dining tables and a curtain rod, and it splits attention.
- The email is far too long. The app, card, Affirm and Design Crew blocks stack up after the real content.
- The dense fine print and footer block at the bottom is hard to read.
- There are no reviews, ratings or prices on the bed modules, so there's nothing to justify clicking a high-consideration purchase.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add one visible reason to act in the hero, such as a "Free shipping on beds" pill or a dated event, as the 10/16 and 10/15 sends did. This would lift the email most.
- 2. Put prices or "from $X" and a star rating under each bed, and make the CTAs specific: "Shop the Cayman Bed," "Shop cane beds."
- 3. Cut the email to the hero, three beds, upholstered beds, and one cross-sell row. Move the app, card, Affirm and Design Crew blocks into a slim footer.
- 4. Keep "Recommended just for you" on-theme (bedding, nightstands, rugs) or drop it.
- 5. Merge the Upholstered Beds tile into the product flow so it doesn't look like a fourth competing hero.

# Technical Audit: Pottery Barn, "Our most-loved bedroom collections"

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email built from sliced images. It has widespread missing alt text, three non-HTTPS image sources, and the recipient's email address in cleartext in tracking-pixel URLs. The header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified, because the relay probably didn't capture those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque tracking links:** 54 links were skipped because they use click-redirect domains, for example `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7ImQi...` on the logo. Destination, status and redirect chain are unverified.
- **Third-party and legacy pixels:**
  - `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` is a third-party ad-network pixel.
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` is an open-tracking pixel over plain HTTP.
  - `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/d8dd8140-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...` is a second open pixel.
  - That makes three separate open-tracking pixels. Any of them blocked or slow adds no visible content but adds requests.
- **Stale or cross-brand assets:**
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` is a 2022 asset.
  - `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on another brand's domain. If that host changes, the spacer breaks.
  - Product images load from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`, the production site, instead of the EDM CDN.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** roughly 55 images are flagged. Examples:
  - `F26_FURN_B12_CoreBedroomCollections/slices_01.jpg` through `slices_13.jpg`
  - `Email-Footer/slices_01.jpg` through `slices_18.jpg`
  - `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`
  - `H26_Top_ChristmasShop.jpg`

  The core content is image slices with no alt text, so it is effectively invisible with images blocked or in a screen reader. Tracking pixels also lack `alt=""`, which should be explicit.
- **Mixed content:** three images use `http://`, which clients may block or flag:
  - `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - the `svopn` pixel
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility issue.
- **Stray PHP in CSS:** the second `<style>` block contains `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store...")`. It is invalid CSS and can break rule parsing in strict clients.
- **Global CSS:** `*{line-height:100%; max-height:1000000px}` and `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` are overly broad.
- **Layout and external dependencies:**
  - Spacer tables lack `role="presentation"`, while others have it.
  - The Google Fonts `<link>` for Montserrat is stripped by many clients, so it is a dependency with no benefit there.
  - Media queries use attribute selectors (`table[class="wrapper"]`) and a `min-width: 700px` rule, `img {max-width: 650px}`. Support for these varies by client.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content slice and product image. Use `alt=""` on spacers and pixels.
2. Move all `http://` assets to HTTPS, including `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `0311_..._slices_02.jpg` and the `svopn` pixel.
3. Stop putting the raw email address in URLs, and use an opaque or hashed subscriber ID instead. Do this first for the HTTP pixel.
4. Remove the stray PHP `header()` lines from the `<style>` block, set a non-empty `<title>`, and drop `maximum-scale=1`.
5. Host all creative assets on the owned EDM CDN, not `edm.westelm.com`, the production site or the 2022 folder.
6. Consolidate the three open-tracking pixels, and drop the legacy `d.turn.com` one if it is unused.
7. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results against the raw message at the original receiving mailbox, not through the relay.
8. Follow a sample of the tracked links manually to confirm destination status and UTM parameters.
