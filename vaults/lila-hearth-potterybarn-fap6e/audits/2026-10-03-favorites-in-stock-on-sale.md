# Favorites in stock & on sale
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-03**
## Executive summary

- This is a generic "on sale" blast with no number attached. The hero reads "On Sale & In Stock / Save on Furniture & More / Delivered in 1-4 weeks." The photo is a bed in gingham bedding and is attractive. But "save" never says how much, so nothing in the hero gives me a reason to act today. The 1-4 week delivery line is the most useful concrete detail, and it's small.
- The body is a tidy category grid (Bedding, Outdoor, Living Room, and so on), a six-product "Recommended Just For You" block, then Christmas Shop and Design Crew banners. The rewards card, Affirm and app modules follow. It's clean and renders without bugs. But it carries four or five competing messages and no single reason to click. After this week's free-shipping, open-box, and design-help sends, it feels like another undifferentiated Pottery Barn promo.
- Verdict: competent and on-brand, but with no hook. It sits at the median of the brand's recent sends, and a stated discount in the hero would be the biggest lift.

## What's working

- The hero image is warm and aspirational, and the bedding and bed frame match the "Furniture & More" headline.
- "Delivered in 1-4 weeks" answers the in-stock question up front.
- The category grid is easy to scan and lets a subscriber jump straight to their room.
- "Recommended Just For You" shows six named products (Cayman Platform Bed, Remmy Swivel Chair, Reeva Rug), so the clicks go to specific items.
- The Christmas Shop banner fits the season and gives early shoppers a second path.

## What's weak

- The hero never states a discount. There's no %, $ or "up to," so "Save" and "On Sale" are empty.
- The subject says "Favorites," but the body never explains whose favorites these are or why they're in stock.
- The sale CTA is buried. "Shop All Sale" sits in plain text at the bottom of the grid, and there's no button in the hero.
- The Lit Ghost Collection sits next to a Christmas banner and a bed, so the recommendations feel scattered.
- The lower half is cluttered. The Design Crew banner, App Store, rewards card, Affirm and fine print dilute the sale message and add nothing to the click.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the real offer in the hero, for example "Up to 30% off furniture, ready in 1-4 weeks," with a solid SHOP THE SALE button. This is the biggest lift.
- 2. Cut the body to the category grid, one recommended-products block and one seasonal banner. Drop the Design Crew banner, since design help was already sent today.
- 3. Add an end date, such as "Sale ends Sunday," if the promotion has one.
- 4. Make the "Delivered in 1-4 weeks" line larger and tie it to a specific benefit, such as "Order now, in your home before the holidays."
- 5. Give the recommended grid a point of view, such as "Best sellers in stock now," instead of a mixed set that includes ghosts.

## Technical Audit

## Pottery Barn "Favorites in stock & on sale" – Technical Audit

### 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with 51 opaque click-redirect links and many unlabeled images. The QA run scored 57% with 0 errors and 3 warnings. The HTML has several real code-level defects: no `lang` attribute, an empty `<title>`, stray PHP in a `<style>` block, and a plaintext-HTTP tracking pixel that carries the recipient's address. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer and unsubscribe markup could not be inspected.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque redirects.** 51 links on `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` were skipped by the HTTP probe, so their destinations are unverified. This is an ESP redirect pattern, not a defect.
- **Plaintext tracking pixel.** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` loads over HTTP. Clients may block it, which undercounts opens. It also sends the recipient's address in cleartext (see section 5).
- **Third-party pixel.** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` is an ad-network pixel (Turn/Amobee). It is a third-party tracking surface in a first-party email.
- **Cross-brand asset.** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a spacer hosted on the West Elm domain. If that CDN path is removed, this spacer breaks.
- **Stale assets.** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` is a 2022 asset served over HTTP.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** QA flagged about 45 images with no `alt`:
  - Content slices: `.../0620_SAT_AM_InStockOnSale/slices_01–10.jpg`.
  - Product images: `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`, `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`, `reeva-handwoven-rug-3-l.jpg`, `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`, and others.
  - Banners: `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg`, `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`.
  - Footer: `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`.
  - Some are tracking pixels or spacers (`coreemail.html`, `d.turn.com/.../cat/1`, `clickcp.../02bf1282-...`, `spacer_0.gif`) and should carry `alt=""`. The rest are meaningful images with no text equivalent, so with images off the email is largely blank.
- **Empty `<title>`.** `<title></title>` is present in the source.
- **No `lang` attribute.** The root is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" ...>` with no `lang`.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom, an accessibility failure.
- **Stray PHP in CSS.** The second `<style>` block ends with `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store, ...");`, which is server-side code pasted into the stylesheet. It is invalid CSS and can corrupt the parse of following rules in some clients.
- **Missing table roles.** Spacer tables such as `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><tr><td height="20">&nbsp;</td></tr></table>` lack `role="presentation"`, unlike the content tables.
- **Attribute selectors.** `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` only match when the class is exactly that value, so they are fragile. They are probably unused, because no `class="wrapper"` or `class="photo"` appears in the visible source.
- **Web font.** The Google Fonts `<link>` for Montserrat is stripped or ignored by Gmail and Outlook. No fallback stack was visible in the truncated source.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unresolved merge tokens. The recipient address is populated in the tracking URLs.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post.** Both were not found. QA attributes this to AgentMail relay stripping, so it is unconfirmed. Treat the absence of one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) as unverified, not as a confirmed sender defect.
- **Authentication-Results.** Not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. This is also a relay limitation. Verify against raw headers from the original delivery.
- **Footer.** The in-body unsubscribe link and postal address could not be checked because the HTML was truncated. The `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg` footer is image-sliced, so any legal text in it is not machine-readable and has no alt text.
- **Address in a URL.** Putting the recipient's address in query strings (`sv_sveme=`, `coreemail.html?email=`) is a privacy exposure. Over the HTTP pixel it travels in cleartext, and it can leak via logs and proxies. `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` is set, which limits Referer leakage but does not protect the URL itself.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 51 links sit behind `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`, an opaque token, so UTM parameters and landing pages can't be read from the source. Run a redirect-following probe (or capture the Location headers) to audit them.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Add meaningful `alt` text to product and banner images, and `alt=""` to spacers and pixels. This is the highest-volume issue (about 45 images).
2. Serve `svopn.potterybarn.com` and the three `http://edm.potterybarn.com/...` assets over HTTPS. Stop passing the plaintext address in the pixel URL, or use a hashed or opaque ID.
3. Remove the PHP `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block.
4. Add `<html lang="en">` and a populated `<title>`. Drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Add `role="presentation"` to the remaining layout tables. Replace the cross-brand `edm.westelm.com` spacer and the 2022 banner with current assets hosted on the Pottery Barn domain.
6. Re-run the compliance and authentication checks against raw delivery headers. The three QA warnings may be relay artifacts, so don't treat them as confirmed until then.
7. Re-run the audit with the full, untruncated HTML plus a redirect-following link check, so the footer, unsubscribe link, and UTM parameters can be audited.
