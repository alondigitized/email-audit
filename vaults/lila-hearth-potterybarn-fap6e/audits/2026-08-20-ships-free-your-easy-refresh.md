# Ships FREE: Your easy refresh
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-20**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's free-shipping pillow send, and it's a solid, legible hero: "Your Easy Refresh Ships Free / Shop Pillows" over a well-composed flatlay of plaid, floral, and textured pillows, with "Starting at $39.50" anchoring a real price point. It's a cleaner single-category focus than the brand's recent multi-room "Get the Look" or lighting sends — one hero product, one clear offer, one CTA.
- Where it falls apart is the sprawl beneath the fold. After the pillow hero, the email pivots into a six-tile "must-haves that ship free" grid (duvets, quilts, throws, table linens, curtains, towels), then a second "Recommended Just for You" grid, then a sweepstakes module, then a Halloween Shop banner, then app/rewards/Affirm blocks. The pillow offer is essentially abandoned after one screen — this reads less like a pillow campaign and more like a category dump with free shipping as the loose organizing theme.
- It's a middling send: strong top, unfocused everything else, and the Design Crew Sweepstakes reappearing again dilutes an otherwise reasonable offer. Same pattern flagged in the 2026-08-18 sweepstakes audit — this brand keeps stapling that lead-gen unit onto unrelated sends.

## What's working

- Hero has a real, specific offer: "Ships Free" + "Starting at $39.50" gives both an incentive and a price anchor.
- Hero image is well-styled and on-category — the pillow flatlay is the clear visual focal point.
- Single unambiguous CTA at the top ("Shop Pillows") with no competing button in the hero zone.
- Section CTAs are consistent and specific (Duvets, Quilts, Throws, Table Linens, Curtains, Towels all link with ">" affordance).
- "Shop All Free Shipping" button gives a clean exit for anyone who wants the broader offer, not just pillows.

## What's weak

- The email loses its own subject/hero promise almost immediately — "Your Easy Refresh" is about pillows, but 80% of the body is other categories with no easy visual tie-back to "refresh."
- Design Crew Sweepstakes module reappears yet again (this is at least the third recent instance) — same lead-gen-disguised-as-giveaway pattern flagged before, and it's jarring dropped between product recommendations and Halloween content.
- Halloween Shop banner is a hard tonal left turn in an email that opened on cozy neutral pillows — no seasonal thread connects the two.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere in the email — "ships free" is evergreen, so nothing compels acting today vs. next week.
- No social proof/reviews on the hero or grid products despite plenty of scroll real estate being spent on lower-priority modules (app download, Affirm, rewards).

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the pillow hero + "Shop All Free Shipping" and the first recommendation grid. Drop the sweepstakes and Halloween modules from this send entirely — they belong in their own dedicated emails, not bolted onto a pillow refresh.
- 2. Add a real urgency element to the hero (e.g., "Free shipping ends Sunday") — right now there's no reason to act now versus bookmarking it.
- 3. Replace the generic six-category grid with 2-3 curated pillow-adjacent items (throws, quilts) that actually build the "easy refresh" story, instead of unrelated furniture (dining table, swivel chair, curtain rods).
- 4. Add a star rating or review count under the hero pillow price — this category lives and dies on "does it look as good as the photo," and social proof closes that gap.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Pillows from $39.50 — ships free`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your refresh, delivered free`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New pillow arrivals, no shipping fee, ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Layer in fresh pattern — free shipping on every pillow`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

Standard Responsys/Oracle Marketing Cloud email template with MSO/Outlook conditionals and mobile media queries; automated QA passed 57% (0 issues, 3 warning categories) but surfaced multiple unauthenticated tracking pixels, missing alt attributes, and mixed-content (`http://`) image sources.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- 55 tracking/redirect links were skipped by the automated HTTP probe (click-redirect domains) — cannot confirm destination validity or redirect health from this pass.
- Nav banner link uses a `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` Responsys-wrapped redirect (evidence: `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABB7InYiOjEsImQiOjQ5NzR9AAsAAAAABBBRFBGM7fZuOriDWCYhEsJbzl_dJb9eIYPnNPgrvMGEbXmvYRbEN8gFOgWpWfO250vOfsYKri7sYVnjQ9xL_7Tdwofy-OXgDr2DYuvMS-rYMRpV`) — destination URL is opaque and can't be validated for landing-page correctness.
- Open-tracking beacon over plaintext HTTP leaks the recipient's email address in the query string: `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`. Sending PII (email address) unencrypted is a data-exposure issue independent of tracking function.
- Third-party pixel to `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` — external ad-tech tracker with no alt text and unresolvable destination in this audit.
- `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/2a957d4c-a421-4bab-8882-2358a5cf3ae7?mi_mid=...&cp_tp` appears to be another tracking/open pixel using Cordial (`cp_tp`) alongside the Responsys pixel — two separate ESP tracking systems present in the same send, suggesting a platform migration in progress or duplicate instrumentation.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- 40+ `<img>` elements are missing `alt` text (full list in QA data), including primary merchandising banners (`F26_Top_NewArrivals.jpg`, `F26_Top_DesignCrewSweeps_01a/b.jpg`, `F26_Bottom_HalloweenShop.jpg`) and all footer legal/social slice images. This fails WCAG 1.1.1 and leaves the email with no fallback content when images are blocked.
- Three images load over non-HTTPS `http://`, which will be blocked or flagged by mail clients enforcing mixed-content/TLS policies:
  - `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`

- Cross-brand asset dependency: a Pottery Barn email references an image hosted on `edm.westelm.com` (West Elm's domain) — an infrastructure/asset-hosting anomaly, not a branding judgment call.
- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` are present — standard for email, no issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- Merge token resolved correctly in the customer-service link and open-tracking pixels: `email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` / `sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` — token substitution is functioning, no unresolved `%%FIELD%%` or `{{token}}` placeholders found in the visible source.
- Flag: the resolved email address is embedded in a plaintext-HTTP tracking pixel URL (see §2), which is a personalization/PII-handling issue as much as a tracking one.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- `List-Unsubscribe` header not found in the captured headers (QA WARN) — may be a relay capture gap (AgentMail) rather than a true absence; cannot confirm from HTML alone since the truncated source doesn't include the footer unsubscribe block.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058, required for one-click unsubscribe under Gmail/Yahoo 2024 bulk-sender rules) not found — same caveat as above.
- `Authentication-Results` header not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from available data.
- No visible unsubscribe link or physical mailer address in the truncated HTML segment provided — cannot confirm CAN-SPAM footer compliance either way; flagging as unverified rather than a confirmed defect.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

- All primary CTAs route through the `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirect wrapper, which obscures any UTM parameters until resolved — could not verify UTM presence/consistency since these links were skipped by the HTTP probe.
- The customer-service link resolves directly to a live site path with the email merge token as a query param: `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` — no UTM source/medium/campaign params present on this link, so site-side attribution for footer/service traffic from this email will not be tracked.

## 7. Recommendations

1. Add `alt` text to all content-bearing images, particularly the four hero/promo banners and the CTA/tracking pixel images — prioritize over decorative footer slices.
2. Convert the three `http://` image sources to `https://` (`edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`, `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `slices_02.jpg`) to avoid client-side blocking and mixed-content warnings.
3. Move the open-tracking pixel (`svopn.potterybarn.com`) to HTTPS — it currently transmits the recipient's email address in cleartext.
4. Confirm with the ESP/deliverability team whether `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent (vs. dropped by the AgentMail relay capture) — required for Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender compliance.
5. Investigate the dual tracking stack (Responsys `sv_sveme` pixel + `clickcp.potterybarn.com` Cordial-style pixel) for redundancy or migration cleanup.
6. Fix the cross-domain asset reference to `edm.westelm.com` — likely a copy-paste template artifact from a West Elm send.
