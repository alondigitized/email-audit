## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a slice-based, image-heavy template with about 45 content images that have no alt text, several `http://` assets, and the recipient's address embedded in image and pixel URLs, one of them over cleartext HTTP. The HTML is truncated, so the footer and unsubscribe markup could not be checked. Authentication and unsubscribe headers were not captured by the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 54 links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, for example `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...` on the logo. Destination validity, redirect chains and status codes are unverified.
- **Cleartext tracking pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` loads over HTTP. Clients may block it, so opens will be undercounted there. It also exposes the recipient's address in cleartext.
- **Third-party pixels:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` (Turn) and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/09c4362a-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...` are also present.
- **Leaked server code:** the last `<style>` block contains PHP-style statements. Examples are `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store, ...")`. This is invalid CSS and a template leak. It sits after the last real rule, so the impact is probably limited.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 45 images have none. This includes the full hero and body slice sequence (`S26_Outdoor_B11_OutdoorInStock/slices_01–12.jpg`), the footer slices (`Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`), the banners (`H26_TOP_FallSale.jpg`, `F26_Bottom_NewArrivals_V1.jpg`, `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg`) and the product images. With images off, or in a screen reader, the content is effectively blank. Only the logo has alt text ("POTTERY BARN").
- **Tracking pixels:** `spacer_0.gif`, the Turn pixel, `svopn` and `clickcp` also lack alt text. For 1×1 pixels this is minor, but `alt=""` is the correct value.
- **HTTP image sources:** three images use `http://`, which may be blocked or flagged as mixed content.
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - the `svopn` pixel
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>`.
- **No language attribute:** `<html xmlns=...>` has no `lang`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom.
- **Table roles:** outer layout tables, such as `<table width="100%" ... bgcolor="#ffffff">`, lack `role="presentation"`. Later tables have it.
- **Web font:** the Google Fonts `<link>` for Montserrat is stripped by Gmail and several other clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved tokens:** nothing like `%%name%%` or `{{...}}` is visible in the truncated source.
- **Recipient address in URLs:** it appears in two places, which is a privacy exposure and needs proper URL-encoding.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** missing. The relay may have stripped it, so this is unconfirmed.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** missing, so one-click unsubscribe may not be supported. This is also unconfirmed.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain is `e.potterybarn.com`.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** none can be verified. Every link is an opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=` redirect, so the final destination and any UTM tags are unknown.
- **Possible theme mismatch:** the subject is "Quick-ship essentials for your outdoor space", but several assets are non-outdoor or seasonal.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` to all content slices and banners, and `alt=""` to pixels and spacers.
2. Move `svopn` and the other `http://` assets to HTTPS.
3. Remove the recipient address from image and pixel URLs, or switch to a hashed or opaque ID.
4. Remove the leaked `header(...)` statements from the `<style>` block.
5. Set `<title>`, add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and drop `maximum-scale=1`.
6. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, plus SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment, from the raw message headers, not the relay copy.
7. Reduce the plain-text URL ratio, and put the unsubscribe link and postal address in live text, not footer images.
8. Resolve a sample of the redirect links to check for UTMs and for landing-page alignment with the "outdoor" subject.
