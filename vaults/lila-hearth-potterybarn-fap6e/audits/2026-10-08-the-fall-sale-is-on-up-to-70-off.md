# The Fall Sale is ON 🍁 Up to 70% off
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a straight-ahead sale blast, and it works. The dark-green hero says "The Fall Sale / Up to 70% off", with a SHOP NOW button above an olive swivel chair. The offer is the first thing you see. A 10-tile category grid follows (Furniture, Sofas & Chairs, Bedding, and so on), so a shopper can get to a category in one tap. After the 10-06 "Pattern of the moment: Classic plaid" and the 10-07 "Small poufs & stools" sends, which had no price or reason to act, this finally gives a subscriber a concrete reason to click.
- The weak spots are urgency and focus. There is no end date, so "is ON" carries no deadline. Below the grid, the email runs a recommendations block, a Christmas Shop banner, a Free Design Services banner, the app, the credit card and Affirm. That is five or six competing asks after the sale message. The "up to 70%" headline also sits above a long block of fine print, and the recommended products show no sale prices. The email says "sale" but never shows what a sale item costs.
- My judgment: it is a solid, scannable promo and the strongest offer of the week. It is a generic mass blast with no deadline and no price proof, and it is the fourth or fifth send in three days.

## What's working

- The hero puts the offer first: "Up to 70% off" in large serif type on dark green, with a high-contrast SHOP NOW button.
- The category grid is easy to scan. Ten large tiles ("SOFAS & CHAIRS", "RUGS", "OUTDOOR") and a "SHOP ALL SALE" bar let people jump straight to what they want.
- The top utility bar ("Discover 1400+ New Arrivals" and "Shop In-Stock Furniture") gives a useful second path.
- The "Recommended Just For You" grid names real products and sizes, such as the Cayman Platform Bed, Toscana Extendable Dining Table (60"–125") and Westin Everfeel Bed Mattress (13" h). That makes the email feel less like a pure blast.
- The sale-first framing of the Fall Sale is more promotional than the plaid and pouf emails earlier this week, and it fits the season.

## What's weak

- There is no deadline or end date, so nothing makes the click urgent.
- The recommended products show no prices, no "was/now" pairs and no discount badges, so the "70% off" is never backed up.
- The lower half is overloaded. The Christmas Shop, Free Design Services, App Store, rewards card and Affirm blocks all compete, and the Christmas banner undercuts the Fall Sale message.
- The fine print is a dense wall of text, and the "up to" claim comes with many exclusions. It feels buried.
- Cadence is heavy. The 10-06 RSVP send, three emails on 10-07 and now this one make it the fourth or fifth send in three days, and the sale hero is another high-volume push.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a deadline to the hero, for example "Ends Sunday" under the headline. This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Put sale prices on the product grid ("$1,299, was $2,199 — 41% off") and add a "% off" badge to each tile. Show proof of the offer, not just the claim.
- 3. Replace the generic "SHOP NOW" with "Shop sale furniture". Link the hero to the featured chair's sale page rather than the sale homepage.
- 4. Cut the lower modules to one. Keep Free Design Services or the Christmas Shop, and drop the app, card and Affirm blocks, or fold them into the footer.
- 5. Pull one real best-seller with a rating ("4.8★, 2,100 reviews") into the hero area for social proof.
- 6. Space this out from the earlier sends this week.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Fall Sale ends Sunday: up to 70% off furniture`
- **Subject Alt B:** `🍁 Up to 70% off sofas, beds & rugs`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Swivel chairs from $X, bed frames from $X, plus free shipping on select styles.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our biggest fall savings on furniture, bedding and decor, through Sunday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, image-slice-heavy build with about 50 images missing alt text, three non-HTTPS image sources, an empty `<title>`, and a leaked server-side snippet inside a `<style>` block. The auth and unsubscribe-header warnings come from the AgentMail relay and can't be confirmed as sender defects. The HTML was truncated, so footer and compliance content could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Destinations unverified.** 55 tracking links were skipped by the probe. Examples are `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im…` (logo) and the `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/…` pixel. The `qs` blob is opaque, so redirect health and landing destinations are unconfirmed.
- **Insecure tracking pixel.** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` loads over plain HTTP. It may be blocked, which would lose open tracking in clients that enforce HTTPS.
- **Third-party pixel.** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEv…/cat/1` is an ad-network beacon with no alt text. It loads from a third-party domain and may be blocked by privacy filters.
- **Plain-text bloat.** The plain-text part is 10,680 of 14,876 characters URLs, about 72%. That is above the 70% threshold the QA check flags and is a minor spam-filter risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, about 50 images.** This includes content-bearing hero and footer slices:
  - `…/1008_THU_AM_FallSale_GraphicHeroLinkfarm/slices_01.jpg` through `slices_12.jpg`
  - `…/Email-Footer/slices_01.jpg` through `slices_18.jpg`
  - `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg`
  - `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`
  - Six product images, for example `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg` and `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`

  With images blocked, the sale offer, navigation, and product content all disappear. Screen readers get nothing.
- **Insecure image sources.** Three images use `http://`:
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - the `svopn` pixel above

  These may be blocked or trigger mixed-content warnings.
- **Empty title.** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **No document language.** `<html xmlns=…>` has no `lang` attribute.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility failure (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Leaked server code.** The second `<style>` block ends with PHP-style text: `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store…"); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. It is invalid CSS and shows that template or server code leaked into the output. Parsers should drop it, but it is unclean.
- **Spacer table without presentation role.** `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><tr><td height="20">&nbsp;</td>` has no `role="presentation"`. The outer layout tables don't either. Other tables do have it, so the use is inconsistent.
- **Cross-brand and live-site asset dependencies.**
  - `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on a sister brand's domain.
  - Product images load from the live site at `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/…`. They could change or break if the site's image paths change.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found.** The QA tool notes these headers may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. Treat them as unverified, not as a confirmed sender failure.
- **`Authentication-Results` missing.** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown for the same relay reason. The From domain `e.potterybarn.com` does align with the click domain `click.e.potterybarn.com`.
- **Footer not auditable.** The visible HTML does not include the footer. The unsubscribe link and postal address could not be confirmed. The footer appears to be 18 image slices (`Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`) with no alt text. If the legal text and unsubscribe link are image-only, they would be inaccessible when images are blocked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

- **Only visible parameters are on tracking pixels.** Those are `sv_cid=0053_00597` and `mi_cid=677b44e6831b79f3`. No campaign-naming issue can be confirmed.
- **Landing alignment not verified.** The probe skipped all tracking links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to every content image. Prioritize the 12 hero slices, the 18 footer slices, and the product images. Use `alt=""` on spacers and pixels.
2. Switch the three `http://` image URLs to HTTPS, and move `svopn` and `clickcp` tracking to HTTPS.
3. Remove the PHP `header(...)` text from the `<style>` block.
4. Fill in `<title>`, add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Stop putting the raw email address in query strings of image and pixel URLs. Use a hashed or opaque ID.
6. Host the spacer GIF on the sender's own domain instead of `edm.westelm.com`.
7. Add `role="presentation"` to all layout tables.
8. For the audit pipeline:
   - Capture raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) before the relay strips them.
   - Pass the full, untruncated HTML so the footer and compliance content can be checked.
   - Resolve tracking redirects (follow with GET) so the landing pages and UTMs can be validated.
