# 💙 Up to 70% off + Labor Day savings ⭐️ Bedding & more
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-02**
## Executive summary

- This is the seventh consecutive "up to 70% off" burgundy-banner Labor Day email from Pottery Barn in seven days, and it's now barely distinguishable from 2026-09-02's earlier "UP TO 70% OFF" send and 09-01's "Just in!" email — same deep burgundy hero, same "100s of new deals added!" copy, same bedding hero shot, same 12-tile burgundy category grid, same "Shop All Sale" button.
- The one real bright spot is that this send actually adds something new below the fold: a "Recommended Just for You" personalized product grid, a Halloween-themed seasonal teaser, and a "Discover New Arrivals" section — none of which appeared in the prior six sends I've seen. That's a genuine attempt to break the monotony, even if it's buried under a wall of identical sale furniture.
- For a subscriber who has now seen this exact hero treatment seven times running, this reads as fatigue-inducing rather than urgent. The offer itself is fine — but the creative has completely stopped signaling "new," and nothing in the subject or hero differentiates this send from the six before it.

## What's working

- Clean, unambiguous hero with "Up to 70% off" and a visible "SHOP NOW" button above the fold.
- Two-column category grid (Furniture, Bedding, Rugs, etc.) makes navigation clear and scannable.
- The "Recommended Just for You" section shows real product photography with names and appears personalized, adding variety not seen in recent sends.
- Halloween teaser and "Discover New Arrivals" sections break up an otherwise sale-only email and hint at what's coming next.
- No render bugs — images, text, and buttons all display cleanly with no overlap or broken assets.

## What's weak

- This is the seventh straight burgundy "up to 70% off" hero in seven days — the creative is now fully interchangeable with prior sends.
- Subject line offers zero new information versus 09-01's and 09-02's earlier "UP TO 70% OFF" subjects — same claim, same emoji-bookending pattern.
- "100s of new deals added!" is the same unverifiable stale claim used in at least four prior sends.
- The email is extremely long (13+ sections) before reaching anything genuinely new, burying the Halloween/New Arrivals content that could differentiate it.
- No urgency element (no countdown, no "ends tonight") despite Labor Day being a hard deadline — a missed opportunity for real time-boxing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop running the identical burgundy hero — rotate in the Halloween or New Arrivals creative as the lead visual for at least one send this week to break the pattern before subscribers tune out entirely.
- 2. Add a real countdown or explicit "Sale ends [date]" in the hero — Labor Day sales have a natural deadline that isn't being used anywhere in this email.
- 3. Move "Recommended Just for You" higher, right under the fold — it's the only truly personalized, differentiated content and it's currently buried below a 12-tile static grid.
- 4. Cut the category grid from 12 tiles to 6 — most of these categories have appeared unchanged for a week and are likely being scrolled past.
- 5. Vary subject line structure (not just emoji swap) so brand-history dedup tools and subscribers alike can tell this apart from yesterday's send.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your picks are on sale + new for fall`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Handpicked for your home, plus first look at Halloween`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New arrivals just landed — see what's trending`

- Primary nav link and 58 total tracking/click-redirect links route through `click.e.potterybarn.com` (ExactTarget click-tracking) — not individually probed per QA config, but structurally consistent with standard redirect wrapping.
- Multiple parallel tracking pixels/beacons present: `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1`, `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`, and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/fc71bff5-e6cf-44c5-a67b-0e873d74473d?mi_mid=...&cp_tp=...`. The `svopn.potterybarn.com` open-tracking pixel is served over **http://**, not https.
- Recipient email address is exposed in plaintext in tracking URLs (`sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`, and in `coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`) — a PII-in-URL pattern worth flagging even though it's vendor-standard for ExactTarget.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
