# Fall deals UP TO 70% OFF + Ships free: Layers for your coziest bed
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a bedding-led product email with the Fall Sale banner on top. The dark-green header reads "The Fall Sale: Up to 70% off" with a SHOP NOW link. Below it, "Layer a Cozy Bed" and a "QUILTS SHIP FREE" button sit over a neutral bedroom shot. The body is four category tiles: duvets, sheets, blankets and bedding essentials. A "Recommended just for you" grid, a "Discover New Arrivals" banner and a Christmas Shop banner follow.
- The email has a clear theme and the photography is good. But the offer is split. "Up to 70% off" sits at the top, "Quilts ship free" is in the hero, and "Up to 15% off Belgian Flax Linen Bedding" is on the duvet tile. A reader can't tell which deal applies to what they're looking at. The sale and the quilt hero also don't match. The hero says quilts, but the tiles show duvets, sheets and blankets.
- The email is tidy but it's the third Pottery Barn sale send in two days. On 2026-10-08 there was "The Fall Sale is ON 🍁 Up to 70% off", the furniture "On now! UP TO 70% OFF" send, and the "Heritage quilts made for layering" send. This one mostly repeats that quilts-plus-sale mix. I'd call it competent but redundant.

## What's working

- The sale banner is the first thing you see, so the headline offer lands fast.
- The bedding photography is warm and cohesive, and it fits the fall season.
- The tile layout is easy to scan, with one underlined "SHOP …" link per category.
- The "Recommended just for you" grid adds some personalization.
- The App Store, rewards card and Affirm modules give loyalty and financing options near the bottom.

## What's weak

- Three offers compete: up to 70% off, quilts ship free, and up to 15% off linen. None of them leads.
- The hero says quilts, but no quilt tile follows, which makes the email feel inconsistent.
- The CTAs are small underlined text links. Only SHOP NOW and the QUILTS SHIP FREE button look like buttons.
- Past the bedding tiles, the email has many modules: the rec grid, New Arrivals, the Christmas Shop, the app, rewards and Affirm. That dilutes the focus and makes a very long scroll.
- It's the same sale message as the previous two days, so it risks reading as spam to a frequent subscriber.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one lead offer and make every tile support it. Either show the 70% sale items or show quilts with free shipping, not both.
- 2. Make the tile CTAs real buttons and put prices or "% off" on each tile.
- 3. Cut the bottom modules (Christmas Shop, app, Affirm) or move them to a slimmer footer.
- 4. Match the hero to the tiles. If the hero says quilts, lead with a quilt tile.
- 5. Space out sale sends. Run the quilt message on a separate day from the broad sale.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Duvets, sheets and blankets in warm neutrals, plus free shipping on quilts.`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email is built on a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with 59 click-tracked links. It has widespread missing alt text, three `http://` image sources, and unverified compliance headers. QA shows 0 failures and 4 warnings, and several of those are probably artifacts of the AgentMail relay.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 59 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`). Their destinations are unverified, not confirmed broken.
- The logo link uses an opaque `qs=` token, so the landing URL and UTM parameters can't be checked from the source.
- Several images and trackers are loaded over plain HTTP:
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
- A third-party pixel, `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1`, is also loaded. A `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/...` open pixel is present too.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Missing alt text:** about 55 images lack `alt`, including:
  - the top banner `H26_TOP_FallSale.jpg`;
  - all 17 body slices, `F26_Bedding_B10_LayerAFallBed/slices_01–17.jpg`;
  - all 18 footer slices, `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`;
  - the product images (`york-slope-arm-deep-seat-slipcovered-sofa...`, `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`, etc.).
  
  The offer and CTA content appears to be baked into sliced images, so with images blocked the email is unreadable and screen readers get nothing.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Mixed content:** the three `http://` sources above may be blocked or flagged by clients.
- **Stray CSS:** PHP-style code (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ...")`) sits inside a `<style>` block. It is invalid CSS and may corrupt the rule that follows it in some parsers.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on a West Elm domain.
- **Responsive CSS:** the attribute selectors `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` only apply to elements carrying those classes. The `@media (min-width: 700px) img {max-width: 650px}` rule combined with `img {max-width: 100% !important}` at 650px is redundant and conflicting.
- **Positive:** the preheader is hidden correctly with `mso-hide:all`, and the layout tables use `role="presentation"`.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) are visible in the truncated source.
- The recipient address `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` appears in plaintext in the query strings of `coreemail.html?email=` and `svopn...sv_sveme=`. Putting an email address (PII) in URLs exposes it to logs, referrers and proxies.
- `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` partly mitigates this for web views.
- The source was truncated, so the full body could not be checked.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` is not found. QA notes this may be dropped by the AgentMail relay, so it is **unconfirmed**, not a confirmed sender failure.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) is not found, so one-click support is unverified. This matters for Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- `Authentication-Results` is not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `e.potterybarn.com` is aligned with the tracking domains.
- The footer's unsubscribe link and physical address are in the truncated portion, so they could not be verified. The footer is built from images (`Email-Footer/slices_*`) with no alt text, so any legal text in it is not machine-readable.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Destinations are hidden behind `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirects, so UTM parameters and landing alignment can't be checked from the source.
- Product images point directly at `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`, which shows the product-to-site mapping but not the link targets.
- The subject refers to "UP TO 70% OFF" and "Layers for your coziest bed". The module path `F26_Bedding_B10_LayerAFallBed` is consistent with that. The landing pages could not be tested.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add meaningful `alt` text to every image, starting with the hero, the offer slices and the footer slices. Use `alt=""` only for spacers and pixels.
2. Move all `http://` assets to `https://`: `svopn`, `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`.
3. Remove the PHP `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block and fill in `<title>`.
4. Stop putting the recipient email address in URL query strings. Use an opaque ID instead.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` in the raw message headers. Also check SPF, DKIM and DMARC at the receiving MTA, since the relay may be stripping them.
6. Resolve a sample of the tracked links in a browser or with a redirect-following probe to confirm landing pages and UTM parameters.
7. Host the spacer GIF on the same brand's domain instead of `edm.westelm.com`.
