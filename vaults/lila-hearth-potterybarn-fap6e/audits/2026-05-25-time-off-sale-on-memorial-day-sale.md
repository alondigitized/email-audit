# Time off, sale ON ❤️ Memorial Day Sale
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-25**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's nineteenth-or-so Memorial Day email in about a week, and at this point the brand is just clocking in. The hero is the exact same red "MEMORIAL DAY SALE — Extra 20% off Select Styles" banner I've now seen on every send since 2026-05-18, followed by the exact same Bedroom / Living / Dining / Bedroom-or-Office category grid I scored 4/10 yesterday. The only thing that "Time off, sale ON ❤️" gives me as a recipient is a slightly cuter subject line — the body has not been refreshed.
- Worse, the subject promises a Memorial Day vibe ("Time off") but the email doesn't deliver any seasonal narrative. No outdoor, no holiday styling, no entertaining moments — just the same furniture grid that was there for "FINAL WEEKEND!" and "Amazing MEMORIAL DAY SALE finds." The heart emoji is the only differentiator.
- Net: the offer is real and recognizable, the render is clean, but for a subscriber on send nineteen the diminishing returns are brutal. This is wallpaper.

## What's working

- Branded "POTTERY BARN" wordmark sits cleanly above the red sale banner — instantly identifiable.
- Hero offer ("Extra 20% off") is bold, legible, and visible above the fold.
- Category grid is well-organized with clear labels (Bedroom, Living, Dining, Bedroom).
- "Recommended Just For You" section gives the email a personalization beat further down.
- Render is clean — no broken images, no overlapping text, no layout breakage.

## What's weak

- This is the 19th near-identical Memorial Day email in ~7 days — cadence fatigue is severe.
- Subject says "Time off" but the creative shows zero holiday/outdoor/entertaining context.
- Category grid duplicates "Bedroom" twice (top row + second row), which is a sloppy curation tell.
- No specific hero product, no price anchor, no real "deal" — just the generic 20% off lever again.
- The heart emoji in the subject is the only differentiator between this and yesterday's send.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Commit to the "Time off" promise in the creative.** If the subject leans into a Memorial Day weekend mood, the hero needs to show an outdoor table, a patio, a guest bedroom — something seasonal. Right now the disconnect is the biggest miss.
- 2. **Drop the duplicate Bedroom tile.** Two "Bedroom" tiles in a 4-tile grid signals laziness. Swap one for Outdoor or Entertaining.
- 3. **Anchor a hero product with a real price.** Replace the generic "Extra 20% off" hero with one curated bed/sofa/table, original price + sale price, so the recipient sees an actual deal not a coupon code.
- 4. **Cool the cadence.** Pause one day, then send a final "last 24 hours" with escalating urgency. Sending the same template 19 times in 7 days is teaching subscribers to mute the sender.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Beds, sofas, dining — final hours of the holiday event.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit — Pottery Barn "Time off, sale ON ❤️ Memorial Day Sale"

## 1. Technical Summary
Email is a legacy table-based template with significant accessibility, security, and compliance defects: stray PHP `header()` calls leaked into a `<style>` block, ~57 images missing alt text, mixed HTTP/HTTPS asset references, a 403 on a primary CTA, and missing one-click unsubscribe headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA (403)**: `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/c/da01b407-b21c-4618-a71b-f83bef0dd682?brandCode=POTTERYBARNCB&marketingChannelCode=APPLYANDBUY&external_id=PB_Email_MemberBanner` — Apply-and-Buy/Member Banner click redirect returns 403, blocking conversion path.
- 23 additional tracking/redirect links via `click.e.potterybarn.com` and `clickcp.potterybarn.com` were not probed (skipped by design); deliverability of those URLs is unverified.
- Open-pixel tracker `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` served over HTTP — likely to be blocked by Gmail/Apple Mail image proxies, skewing open-rate data.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **PHP code leaked into `<style>` block** — the following raw PHP appears inside a CSS block in `<head>` and will render as visible/garbled CSS text or be silently ignored:
  ```
  header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
  header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
  header("Cache-Control: post-check=0, pre-check=0", false);
  header("Pragma: no-cache");
  ```
  This is a server-side template bug — PHP that should have executed got serialized into the HTML output.
- **57 images missing `alt` attributes**, including the logo, all 13 hero/grid slices in `S26_Sale_MemorialDay_Grid2/`, all 18 footer slices in `Email-Footer/`, the bottom outdoor banner, and 6 product thumbnails (cayman-platform-bed, toscana-extendable-dining-table, etc.). Fails WCAG 1.1.1; renders as blank/URL filenames when images are blocked.
- **Mixed content (HTTP assets)** — will be blocked or downgraded by many clients:
  - `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&...` (open pixel)
- `<title>` element is empty.
- External Google Fonts `<link>` (`fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Montserrat`) is included; most email clients strip `<link rel="stylesheet">`, so the request is wasted/leaks the open.
- `meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer"` will strip referrer on all click-throughs — intentional, but worth flagging since it breaks any referrer-based analytics on the landing pages.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `[FNAME]`) detected in the captured source. The recipient address (`lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`) is correctly substituted into the customer-service image URL and open-pixel parameters. No issues found.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header**: not present in captured headers (may be a relay-capture gap, but flagging).
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`** (RFC 8058): not present — one-click unsubscribe required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender rules (Feb 2024+) is not detectable.
- **`Authentication-Results`** header not captured — SPF/DKIM/DMARC status unverified via the relay.
- From-domain `e.potterybarn.com` is a recognized ESP subdomain; alignment cannot be confirmed without auth headers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- All click-through links route through `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirectors with opaque `qs=` payloads; UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) are not present on the email side and presumably injected after redirect — cannot be verified from source alone.
- Tracking attribution uses proprietary params (`cm_ite`, `cp_tp`, `mi_cid`, `cp_cid`, `mi_mid`, `external_id=PB_Email_MemberBanner`) consistent with the Cheetah/CrossPixel stack. No standard GA4-compatible UTMs observed — analytics teams relying on UTM-based reporting will see this traffic as direct/referral.
- The broken Apply-and-Buy link (§2) is the most material continuity failure.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the 403 on the `clickcp.potterybarn.com/c/da01b407-...` Apply-and-Buy CTA** before next send — primary credit-card acquisition path is dead.
2. **Remove the stray PHP `header()` block from `<style>`** in `<head>` — server-side templating is leaking executable code into the output.
3. **Add `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`** alongside the `List-Unsubscribe` mailto/https header to satisfy Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
4. **Upgrade all `http://` image sources to `https://`** (4 assets: westelm spacer, Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg, 0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner slice, svopn open pixel) to prevent client blocking.
5. **Populate `alt` attributes** on all 57 flagged images — at minimum the logo, hero banner, grid CTAs, and product thumbnails. Use `alt=""` on purely decorative slices/spacers to mark them as such.
6. **Populate `<title>`** element (currently empty) for accessibility and browser-preview rendering.
7. **Add UTM parameters** to redirect destinations (or expose them in the `qs=` payload) so landing-page analytics aren't dependent on the proprietary `cm_ite`/`mi_cid` schema.
8. **Remove the unused `fonts.googleapis.com` `<link>`** — email clients strip it and it adds a tracker hit.
