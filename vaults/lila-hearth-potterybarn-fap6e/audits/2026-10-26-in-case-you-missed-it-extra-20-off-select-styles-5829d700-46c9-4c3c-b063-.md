# In case you missed it: EXTRA 20% OFF select styles
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the eighth "Extra 20% off" send in roughly four days, and it looks like the other seven. The oxblood hero ("Extra 20% off Select Styles / Plus savings up to 70% off") sits on top again with a SHOP ALL SALE button. It is clean and unambiguous, but there is nothing new in it. Compared with the focused furniture sends on 10/25 ("Online SALE exclusive: Bestselling furniture" and "New in furniture: Antique-inspired designs"), this one goes back to a generic catch-all.
- The "In case you missed it" subject is honest about what this is: a resend. A subscriber who already saw the 10/22 to 10/25 blasts has no reason to open it. The body does have a decent product block ("Recommended just for you"), and the Christmas Shop and Free Design Services modules give some seasonal and service variety. The best content is buried below a long category-button stack.
- Verdict: competent execution, tired concept. It would be fine as a first touch and is weak as the eighth. Expect fatigue and unsubscribes more than lift.

## What's working

- The hero offer is instantly readable: large serif "Extra 20% off Select Styles" with a white SHOP ALL SALE button on oxblood.
- The 2×N category button grid (Sofas & Chairs, Bedding, Rugs, Curtains and others) gives quick routing by category.
- The "Recommended just for you" product grid shows named products with sizes, such as "Toscana Extendable Dining Table (60"-125")", so it feels curated.
- The Christmas Shop module is seasonal and visually strong, with a styled tree photo.
- The layout renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The creative is the same oxblood banner that has led every recent send, so it reads as a repeat.
- There is no deadline or end date. "In case you missed it" implies urgency without giving any, and the "Exclusions apply" line is tiny.
- The category button stack (about 8 solid oxblood blocks) pushes the actual products below the fold. The block is heavy and visually monotonous.
- The product tiles show no prices, no sale prices and no reviews, so the "Extra 20% off" is never tied to a real number.
- The footer is crowded with credit card, Affirm and legal text, and the fine print is dense. The 10% rewards offer competes with the sale message.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the repeated banner. Lead with one hero product and its sale price (for example, the Toscana table at "$X, now $Y with extra 20% off"), and move the category grid below it.
- 2. Add a real deadline ("Ends Sunday") to the hero and the subject line. Without one, a resend has no reason to exist.
- 3. Show prices and strike-through savings, plus star ratings, on the product tiles.
- 4. Collapse the eight category buttons into a slim text nav row or a two-row strip.
- 5. Suppress or segment this send for recipients who got the 10/22 to 10/25 emails and opened or clicked them.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends Sunday: Extra 20% off furniture`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your picks are still 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Dining tables, rugs and curtains, all on sale through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Savings up to 70% off, plus free design help. Final days.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based XHTML Transitional with 650px fixed-width sliced images. The QA data and visible source show widespread missing alt text, three non-HTTPS image sources, stray PHP code inside a `<style>` block, and the recipient's address in plaintext in pixel URLs. The header-based compliance warnings are most likely relay artifacts, not sender defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Not probed:** 54 tracking links on `click.e.potterybarn.com` (Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style `/u/?qs=` redirects) were skipped by the HTTP probe. Link validity and destinations are unverified.
- **Insecure pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is an open-tracking pixel over plain HTTP. It may be blocked or rewritten by clients, and it sends the email address in clear text.
- **Third-party pixels:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` (Turn/Amobee ad-tech) and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/94921f8d-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...` are also embedded, so tracking comes from multiple vendors.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a West Elm-hosted spacer in a Pottery Barn send. It is a dependency on another brand's host and has no alt text.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** 46 images are flagged. 42 are content images:
  - hero slices `slices_01–12.jpg` under `1026_MON_AM_Extra20GraphicHeroLinkfarm/`
  - six product images, e.g. `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-1245-1-l.jpg` and `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`
  - banners such as `H25_TOP_InStockFurniture.jpg`, `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg` and `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`
  - `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`
  - The other four are tracking pixels.
- **Blank alt fallback:** If images are blocked, the sliced hero and footer render as empty space. The hero linkfarm and footer navigation would then have no readable link text.
- **Non-HTTPS images:** There are three:
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - the `svopn` pixel above
- **Stray code in CSS:** The second `<style>` block contains PHP statements: `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store...")`. They are invalid CSS. A client that parses strictly could drop the rule that follows them, though the rules before them, such as `a[x-apple-data-detectors]`, are unaffected.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Zoom disabled:** `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility issue.
- **Attribute selectors:** `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` only match when the class attribute is exactly that value, so they are brittle.
- **Preheader hidden text:** The `display:none` block is padded with `͏` and `­` characters. This is a standard preheader pattern, not a defect.
- **Plain-text part:** 10,386 of 14,579 characters (71%) are URLs, which exceeds the 70% threshold. This is a spam-filter risk.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved tokens:** None were visible in the provided source.
- **Address in URLs:** The recipient address `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` appears in the query strings of `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=...` and the `svopn` pixel. This is correctly populated personalization, but it puts PII in cleartext in URLs, and the `svopn` one is HTTP.
- **Address embedded as an image:** `coreemail.html` is served as an `<img>`, i.e. a tracking beacon, not a page.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** Both are absent from the captured headers. The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. RFC 8058 one-click support cannot be verified from this data.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not present. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, again likely due to the relay.
- **Footer content:** The visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address were not in the truncated HTML, so they can't be confirmed or faulted.
- **Sender domain:** `PotteryBarn@e.potterybarn.com` is on the same organizational domain as the link and image hosts, which is consistent.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM:** All 54 links go through opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` redirects, so UTM parameters and final destinations could not be verified. The links were not resolved.
- **Direct site assets:** Product images load from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...` and banners from `edm.potterybarn.com`, so asset hosting is on-brand.
- **Stale asset:** One footer asset is from 2022 (`/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/`) and is still served over HTTP.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 42 content images, especially the hero linkfarm and footer slices. Use `alt=""` on spacers and `width="1" height="1"` pixels.
2. Move the `svopn` pixel and the two `http://edm.potterybarn.com/...` images to HTTPS.
3. Remove the PHP `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block and fill in `<title>`.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Stop sending the recipient email address in cleartext query strings. Use an opaque ID.
6. Replace the West Elm spacer with a Pottery Barn-hosted or inline `<td>` spacer.
7. Trim the plain-text alternative so URLs make up less than 70% of it.
8. To close the QA gaps, capture raw headers (List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, Authentication-Results) from a direct SMTP receipt, not through the relay. Also resolve a sample of the `click.e.potterybarn.com` links to check UTM parameters and landing pages.
