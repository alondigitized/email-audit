# FREE design help for any room & any project
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-12**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn Design Services' second dedicated send in 48 hours — the first landed June 10 as "FREE design services for any project" (scored 8/10 in brand history). The creative is nearly identical: same hero image register, same room-category grid, same "Book a Free Appointment" module, same loyalty footer. Swapping "services" for "help" and adding "for any room &" is not differentiation, it's copy drift. A subscriber who opened the June 10 send will recognize this immediately and feel like they're being spammed.
- Inside the email, the execution is genuinely solid. The hero is clean, the visual hierarchy is strong, the three-mode booking grid (In Store / Phone Calls / Video Chat) is a smart specificity move, and the "Recommended Just For You" product rail at the bottom gives it a transactional safety net. But the fundamentals are undermined by the cadence problem: this email would've scored 8+ as a standalone. Sent two days after an effectively identical send, it craters open likelihood.
- Clear verdict: great email, wrong send date.

## What's working

- **Hero visual hierarchy is excellent** — "Free Design Services" headline lands immediately above a bright, aspirational living-room photo; eye goes straight to the offer.
- **Booking mode specificity** — "In Store / Phone Calls / Video Chat" tells the subscriber exactly how to engage, reducing friction that generic "Book Now" CTAs leave.
- **Room-category grid is well-executed** — Bedroom, Living Room, Dining Room, Outdoor each get a lifestyle image and a specific CTA ("Create a Cozy Bedroom," "Design Your Living Room"), making the service feel broad but targeted.
- **Loyalty module is present and specific** — "Earn $75 Back in Rewards" gives members a concrete financial reason to act beyond the free service hook.
- **No render bugs** — clean layout, no broken images, no overlapping text, no lorem ipsum.

## What's weak

- **Near-duplicate of the June 10 send** — "FREE design services for any project" → "FREE design help for any room & any project" is not a refresh; it's a copy-paste with minor word-swaps.
- **Zero urgency** — "Free" is always available at Pottery Barn; without a deadline or slot-scarcity signal, there's no reason to act today vs. next month.
- **No social proof** — no review count, no "X appointments booked this week," no customer quote; a service email especially needs trust scaffolding.
- **"RECOMMENDED JUST FOR YOU" feels appended** — the product rail at the bottom breaks the editorial coherence of a services send; it reads as a default module dropped in rather than a curated tie-in.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Hold this email** — the June 10 send hasn't expired. This should've been suppressed for subscribers who opened or clicked the June 10 version. Send the refresh to non-openers only, or push it out to June 15+.
- 2. **Add a scarcity/urgency layer** — "Appointments filling up this week" or "Limited spots for June" turns a soft service offer into something that needs a click today.
- 3. **Replace the generic product rail with a design-project tie-in** — if someone is about to book a bedroom consultation, show the top-3 bestselling bedroom pieces. Make the "Recommended" section earn its place.
- 4. **Lead with social proof** — one customer quote ("I booked a phone call and had a full room plan in a week") above the booking module would materially lift conversions on a services email.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Book a free design call — rooms of any size`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your free designer, any room, no pressure`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `In-store, by phone, or video — your choice. Spots are limited.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Bedroom, living room, outdoor — one free appointment covers it all.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[CRITICAL] PHP code injected into `<style>` block**
The following server-side PHP appears verbatim inside a CSS `<style>` tag:
```
header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
header("Cache-Control: post-check=0, pre-check=0", false);
header("Pragma: no-cache");
```
This is a template assembly error — a PHP snippet was concatenated into the wrong template slot. Most email clients silently discard invalid CSS, so this is unlikely to break rendering, but it confirms a build pipeline defect that should be investigated.

**[FAIL] 37 images missing alt text**
Every content image in both the body (`slices_01.jpg` through `slices_08.jpg`, product images, footer slices) and several tracking pixels carry `alt=""` or no `alt` attribute. Images-off rendering (default in Outlook, common in corporate inboxes) produces a completely blank email.

**[FAIL] Two images served over HTTP**
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`

Mixed-content HTTP assets are blocked by default in Gmail, Apple Mail, and most modern clients over HTTPS connections. Both are footer/banner images from a 2022 campaign path — likely stale template carry-over.

**[WARN] Google Fonts `<link>` tag will be stripped**
`<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Montserrat&display=swap">` — Gmail, Outlook (all versions), and Yahoo strip external stylesheet `<link>` tags. Montserrat will not load in the majority of clients. System fallbacks (`verdana, arial`) apply.

**[WARN] Doctype mismatch**
Document declares `XHTML 1.0 Strict` but contains HTML5-incompatible patterns. Non-blocking in practice for email clients but signals a legacy template base.

### 5. Compliance

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header absent**
QA did not detect this header via the AgentMail relay. If it is genuinely missing (not just relay-suppressed), this is a Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender policy violation for high-volume sends (>5k/day), and it disables the inbox-level unsubscribe UI.

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) absent**
One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender requirements since February 2024. Missing `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` means automated one-click flows will not function.

**[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not captured**
SPF and DKIM pass/fail status is unknown through the relay. Cannot confirm deliverability posture. The sending domain is `e.potterybarn.com` (ESP subdomain); DMARC alignment depends on whether the root `potterybarn.com` policy covers this subdomain.
