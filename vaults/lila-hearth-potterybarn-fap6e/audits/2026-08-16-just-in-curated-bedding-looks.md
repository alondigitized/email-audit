# Just in! Curated bedding looks
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-16**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's bedding-lookbook send, essentially the same editorial template they've been running all week — "New Bedding Inspiration" hero, then four named duvet stories (Cascina, Linden, Everett, and a flannel/charcoal look), each with a full-bleed lifestyle shot, a one-line material descriptor, and a "Complete the Look" link. There's no discount, no percentage off, no price anywhere — the only concrete offer is "All Bedding Ships Free," buried as a single text line between the duvet cascade and the "Our Favorite Bedding Looks" module.
- It's competently shot and on-brand, but it's pure content marketing with zero urgency, and it's the third near-identical cascading-collection format this week after the dining tables (8/14) and the bed/nightstand pairing (8/15). Subscribers who've seen those two sends are looking at a fourth "browse our aesthetic" email in three days with nothing new to differentiate it. Judgement: fine as an inspiration/engagement email, weak as a conversion driver — it needs an actual offer or urgency hook to earn a click over the last two sends.

## What's working

- Hero is clean and immediately legible: "New Bedding Inspiration" with a clear "Shop Duets" (Duvets) CTA button above the fold.
- Consistent named-collection format (Cascina, Linden Reversible, Everett) with material call-outs ("crisp plaid in warm neutrals," "antique-inspired indigo toile") gives each section a distinct identity.
- "All Bedding Ships Free" is a real, if minor, incentive that's at least visible without heavy scrolling.
- No visible render bugs — images load cleanly, text is legible, spacing is consistent throughout.
- Good category cross-sell block (Sheets/Quilts/Duvet Inserts/Pillows/Blankets/Throws) gives multiple entry points for browsers.

## What's weak

- No discount, promo code, or time-bound offer anywhere in the body — this is the weakest conversion lever of any recent Pottery Barn send in this history.
- Four consecutive near-identical "hero + named collection cascade" emails in four days (dining tables 8/14, bedroom furniture 8/15, this one 8/16) — the format is now visibly repetitive and easy to tune out.
- "Complete the Look" CTA repeats three times with identical copy — no variation or specificity to differentiate which duvet story it's attached to.
- Free shipping line is easy to miss — it's a single small text row, not styled as an offer/badge the way a real incentive should be.
- The bottom "Recommended For You" and Halloween shop modules dilute an already-long email further, pushing any real urgency (Design Crew sweepstakes, rewards) below six other content blocks.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add an actual offer — a %/$ off duvets or a threshold discount ("20% off new bedding") would give this email a reason to click over yesterday's and the day before's near-identical sends. Content-only lookbooks should be occasional, not a four-day-in-a-row cadence.
- 2. Promote "All Bedding Ships Free" into a visible badge/banner near the hero instead of a buried text line — it's currently the only tangible incentive and it's underused.
- 3. Vary the "Complete the Look" CTA copy per collection ("Shop the Cascina Look," "Shop Linden Indigo") to make each section feel distinct and trackable.
- 4. Trim the email — cut either the "Recommended For You" or the Halloween Shop block; the current length pushes the Design Crew sweepstakes and rewards program too far down to be seen.
- 5. Break the cadence pattern — alternate format types (lookbook vs. single-product spotlight vs. offer-led) rather than three consecutive cascading-collection sends.

- **Subject Alt B:** `4 bedding looks we can't stop styling`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Layered duvets, quilts & throws — all bedding ships free.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `From crisp plaid to indigo toile: this week's bedding edit.`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Mostly clean B&B/EDM template; primary defect is compliance-header related — no List-Unsubscribe/List-Unsubscribe-Post found and widespread missing alt text on image-only content.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 64 tracking/click-redirect links (e.g. `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`) were skipped by HTTP probe per QA, so destination validity is unconfirmed.
- Open-tracking pixels present: `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` (HTTP, not HTTPS) and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/24b13fe2-...`.
- A third-party pixel `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` is loaded (data/ad-tech tracker).

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 40+ `<img>` elements missing `alt` text (product images, banner slices, footer slices, header nav) — screen readers will announce nothing for these; flag as accessibility gap.
- Two images served over plain `http://`: `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` — mixed content may be blocked by mail clients enforcing HTTPS.
- Stray `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ...")` PHP-style function calls appear inside a `<style>` block (not valid CSS) — dead/leftover code, harmless but indicates template hygiene issue.
- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` present — appropriate for email, no issue.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- Recipient email is hard-embedded in plaintext across several URLs rather than an opaque token, e.g. `...coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` and `sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` — no broken/unresolved merge tags (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`) observed in the truncated source.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- List-Unsubscribe header not found (QA WARN) — cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe support at the header level; body-level unsubscribe link not visible in truncated HTML.
- List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found (RFC 8058) — one-click unsubscribe (Gmail/Yahoo requirement) unconfirmed.
- Authentication-Results header not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from this capture.
- Plain-text part is 70%+ URLs (11653/16607 chars) — spam-filter risk factor, may reduce inbox placement.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Primary click links route through `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirect domain rather than direct UTM-tagged potterybarn.com URLs — actual landing page/UTM parameters are not visible pre-redirect (skipped by QA probe), so continuity cannot be verified from source alone.

7. Recommendations
- Add `alt` attributes to all content-bearing images, particularly the 18 `slices_*.jpg` promo images and product shots.
- Migrate the 3 remaining `http://` image sources to `https://` to avoid mixed-content blocking.
- Confirm List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post headers are actually being sent by the originating ESP (may be a capture artifact of the relay rather than a true absence) and validate via a raw SMTP/header capture outside the relay.
- Remove dead `header(...)` PHP calls from the `<style>` block.
- Reduce plain-text URL density or add more descriptive text to lower spam-filter risk.
- Spot-check a sample of the 64 redirect links manually to confirm destination and UTM parameters land correctly on potterybarn.com.
