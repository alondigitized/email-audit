# NEW! TODAY-ONLY DEALS + 20% off all ornaments in stores & online
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the same burgundy-plaid "Black Friday, up to 70% off" template that has run since 11/16, with a new "Today Only Deals" block added under the hero. The hero is still the strongest part: a big "70% off" and one "Shop Now" button. The new module is the only fresh content. It shows four products with Was/Now prices, such as sheets at $119 and a bath towel at $111. That is more concrete than the usual category tiles.
- Below that the email turns into a long scroll. It runs through category tiles, a store locator, a recommendations grid, a "Christmas Shop" banner, brand links, app and rewards promos, Affirm, and several blocks of dense fine print. The subject promises "20% off all ornaments", but I can't see an ornament-specific module or CTA in the render. The ornament offer looks buried in the "Decor" and Christmas Shop tiles.
- This is the 6th or so send in 3 days, following 11/26 "So thankful for you ❤️" and 11/27 "UP TO 70% OFF 🥧". Subscribers will read it as the same email again. The product-price module is a real upgrade, but the subject stacks two offers and doesn't commit to one.

## What's working

- The hero has a clear hierarchy: a "NEW DEALS ADDED" kicker, a huge "70% off" and one "Shop Now" button.
- The "Today Only Deals" module shows four specific products with Was/Now prices. That is concrete and credible.
- The seasonal and urgency framing is current: Black Friday, "Today Only", and a Christmas Shop banner.
- The email renders cleanly. I see no broken images or overlapping text.
- It offers loyalty and payment angles: "Earn 10% Back in Rewards" and Affirm.

## What's weak

- The subject stacks "TODAY-ONLY DEALS" and "20% off all ornaments", but the ornament offer has no obvious hero or CTA in the body.
- The email is very long, with at least 10 distinct modules, so the primary action gets diluted.
- The template and "up to 70%" creative are unchanged from 11/16, which makes the cadence feel repetitive and promotion-fatigued.
- Many category tiles use the "up to X% off" pattern, which is vague. They dilute the specific Today Only prices.
- The dense fine-print blocks at the bottom hurt the "honest offer" read. Exclusions are visible but small.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add an ornament hero module directly under the main hero, with a "Shop Ornaments 20% Off" button. Delivering the subject's headline offer in the body would lift this email the most.
- 2. Cut the body to roughly 5 modules by dropping the brand-family links, the Affirm block and the duplicate rewards promo. Keep the Today Only products at the top.
- 3. Put a real countdown or "Ends tonight at midnight" line near the Today Only block.
- 4. Use different creative from the 11/26 send, and send only one email per day during the sale.
- 5. Make the CTAs specific. Change "Shop Now" to "Shop Today-Only Deals" and "Shop All Sale" to "Shop Ornaments".
- **Subject Alt A:** `Today only: 20% off all ornaments`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Sheets from $119 + 20% off ornaments, today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus up to 70% off Black Friday deals, ending tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Thousand-count sheets, bath towels and more, today-only prices.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based, image-sliced SFMC email that sends 68 opaque click-tracked links. It has widespread missing or empty alt text, two non-HTTPS images, and stray PHP code in a `<style>` block. The QA run could not verify authentication, unsubscribe headers or link destinations. The source was truncated, so the footer was not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Destinations unverified.** All 68 links go through `click.e.potterybarn.com/?qs=…`, an SFMC redirect with an opaque token. The QA probe skipped them (`68 tracking link(s) skipped`), so there is no evidence of 200 status, redirect chain or destination.
- **Third-party ad pixels.** These are `secure.leadback.advertising.com/adcedge/lb?site=695501…` and `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/…/cat/1`. They are extra external dependencies and are not first-party tracked.
- **Cross-brand asset.** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on another brand's domain, so it breaks if that path changes.
- **Dead code in source.** A large commented-out credit-card banner is left in the HTML, with a live-looking `potterybarn.com/pages/credit-card/?…` URL. This adds payload but has no functional impact.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** About 60 images are flagged by QA. Examples are `slices_01.jpg`–`slices_15.jpg` in `1127_FRI_AM_BF_Grid`, `H26_InStoreSavingsBanner.jpg`, `H26_Bottom_XmasShop_50off.jpg`, and product images such as `faux-fur-ruched-throw-l.jpg`.
- **Empty alt on linked images.** The visible hero slices use `alt=""` inside `<a href="https://click.e.potterybarn.com/…">`. Each link therefore has no accessible name, and with images blocked the email shows no text content or CTAs. The header logo is the only image with alt text (`alt="POTTERY BARN"`).
- **Mixed content.** Two images load over `http://`:
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`

  Some clients block or rewrite these.
- **Stray PHP in CSS.** The second `<style>` block begins with `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: …"); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. This is invalid CSS. It will probably corrupt the selector of the next rule, so `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` is likely dropped. Apple data-detector links such as addresses and dates would then be restyled.
- **Mobile selectors may not match.** The media query targets `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]`. In the visible source, the layout tables are fixed `width="650"` with no `class="wrapper"`, and the slice `<img>` tags have no `class="photo"`. The responsive rules likely never apply, so the fixed 650px layout would scroll or scale down on mobile. Later markup was truncated, so this is not fully confirmed.
- **Markup structure.**
  - Layout tables lack `role="presentation"`.
  - Content (`<!-- begin dynamic ampScript slot -->`) precedes the `<!DOCTYPE>`, which can trigger quirks mode in some legacy clients.
  - The XHTML 1.0 Strict doctype is used with deprecated attributes (`bgcolor`, `align`, `<center>`).
- **Unlabelled tracking pixels.** QA also flags `svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=…` and the `coreemail.html` image for missing alt text.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens or AMPscript were found in the visible source. The ampScript slot at the top is empty.

The recipient address is resolved and embedded in two image URLs (see section 5).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No List-Unsubscribe header.** QA found no `List-Unsubscribe` and no `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058). The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed, not a confirmed sender failure. It matters for Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **Authentication unknown.** No `Authentication-Results` header was captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `e.potterybarn.com` matches the click-tracking domain, which is consistent with aligned sending, but this is not verified.
- **Recipient email in URLs.** The recipient address is passed in plain text in two image URLs:
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `https://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`

  The `svopn` pixel is a third-party open-tracking pixel. Both leak the address into server logs and proxies.
- **Footer not verified.** The physical address and unsubscribe link are in the truncated portion and were not checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs unverifiable.** The click URLs carry only the opaque `qs` token, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment cannot be confirmed from the source. The skipped HTTP probe means there is no resolved destination data.
- **Subject-to-landing check not possible.** There is no resolved landing page to check against "20% off all ornaments in stores & online".

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to every linked slice and CTA, with text that carries the offer. Use `alt=""` only for spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Remove the PHP `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block and verify that `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` is applied.
3. Move the two `http://` images to HTTPS (`Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and the 2022 `slices_02.jpg`).
4. Add `class="wrapper"` and `class="photo"` to the layout tables and images, or rewrite the media queries to match the real markup, and test at 320px.
5. Stop putting the raw recipient email in image query strings. Use an opaque subscriber key instead.
6. Self-host the spacer GIF instead of pointing at `edm.westelm.com`, and add `role="presentation"` to the layout tables.
7. For the QA pipeline:
   - Capture the raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from the original message, not only the relay copy.
   - Resolve the redirect chain for a sample of `click.e.potterybarn.com` links to check UTMs and landing pages.
   - Inspect the footer by ingesting the full HTML.
