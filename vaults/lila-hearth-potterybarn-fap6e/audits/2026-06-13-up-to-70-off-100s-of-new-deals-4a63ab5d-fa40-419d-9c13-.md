# Up to 70% off 100s of NEW DEALS 🎉
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-13**
## Executive summary

- This is the third "Up to 70% off / new deals" email Pottery Barn has fired in roughly 48 hours — the June 12 "NEW DEALS ADDED! Up to 70% off" send (scored 7/10) was essentially this same template, and this one barely updates the hero copy beyond swapping in "Summer Sale" and "100s of new deals added." The structural bones are identical: full-width sale hero, 10-category icon grid, personalized product rail, Design Services plug, app download, rewards module, Affirm. A real subscriber at this point is trained to ignore the hero and skim for the personalized rail.
- The hero image — a soft cream sofa in a warm living room — is clean and on-brand, and the category grid is genuinely useful for navigating a large sale. The "Recommended Just For You" rail is the best thing in the email because it converts a generic 70%-off blast into a semi-targeted unit. But that rail is buried below the fold; the hero doesn't earn the scroll.
- Verdict: competent execution of a fatigued campaign. The personalization layer saves it from being pure noise, but the brand is burning subscriber goodwill by repeating the same creative week-over-week without meaningful differentiation.

## What's working

- **Personalized product rail** — "Recommended Just For You" with individual product images is the only element that gives the subscriber a reason to prefer this email over the identical June 12 send.
- **Category icon grid** — clean, scannable navigation to 10 sale categories (Furniture, Outdoor, Bedding, Sofas & Chairs, Pillows & Throws, Decor, Tabletop, Bath, Lighting, Rugs) is genuinely useful for a large sale.
- **Rewards callout** — "Earn 10% Back in Rewards" and the Affirm module add real purchase-decision value for a high-AOV category.
- **No render bugs** — layout is clean, no broken images, no overlapping text.
- **Seasonal label** — "Summer Sale" framing anchors the email to a moment rather than a perpetual clearance.

## What's weak

- **Creative fatigue** — this hero is a near-duplicate of the June 12 "NEW DEALS ADDED! Up to 70% off" email; a subscriber who opened that one has zero reason to engage differently here.
- **No deadline visible** — "100s of new deals added" implies urgency but gives no expiry; the fear of missing out has no anchor.
- **Hero product is too generic** — the sofa in the hero image is aspirational but links to "SHOP ALL SALE," not the specific featured piece; a missed conversion opportunity.
- **Design Services module out of place** — embedding the "Free Design Service / Any project & any budget" unit here is the same recycled cross-sell block from the June 12 dedicated design-services email (scored 8/10). It reads like filler.
- **"SHOP ALL SALE" as sole hero CTA** — four instances of "SHOP ALL SALE" in the email; no CTA differentiation for category-level or product-level entry points in the hero.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Differentiate the hero with a category or product hook** — instead of "100s of new deals added," name the specific categories that got refreshed. "New deals in Outdoor Furniture + Bedding" tells the subscriber something the June 12 email didn't.
- 2. **Add a visible deadline** — a single line of copy ("Ends Sunday, 6/15") immediately converts this into an urgency play without redesigning anything.
- 3. **Elevate the personalized rail above the category grid** — if PB has the data to recommend products, that recommendation should appear before the generic icon grid, not after.
- 4. **Kill the Design Services cross-sell module** — this block ran in a dedicated email two days ago. It reads like padding here and dilutes the sale focus.
- 5. **Vary the subject from the June 12 comp** — the June 12 subject was "NEW DEALS ADDED! Up to 70% off"; this one is "Up to 70% off 100s of NEW DEALS 🎉" — they're the same claim in a different word order.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Summer Sale: new outdoor & bedding deals — ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `We added more: 70% off now includes [category]`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your recommended picks are in the sale. See what's new.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Ends Sunday — new deals in furniture, outdoor, and more.`
