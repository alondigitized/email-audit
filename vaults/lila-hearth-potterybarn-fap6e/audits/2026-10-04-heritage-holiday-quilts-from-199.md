# Heritage holiday quilts, from $199
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-04**
## Executive summary

- This is a well-built seasonal product-story email, and the clearest of Pottery Barn's recent sends. The hero, "Heritage Quilts," has a "Quilts Ship Free" banner and a "Shop Holiday Bedding" link. A "Starting at $199" tag sits just below it. After Oct 3's vague "Favorites in stock & on sale" and Oct 2's "Your next great find is ON SALE," a real price anchor is a clear improvement. The subject's "$199" is also backed up in the body.
- The body then splits into three quilt stories: Classic Patchwork Plaid, Heritage Medallion, and Velvet Scallop. Each has lifestyle photography, an italic editorial caption, and a "Shop the Look" link. It looks good, but the CTAs are small text links rather than buttons, and "Shop All Quilts" only appears at the end. Below that, the "Recommended Just For You" grid of curtain rods, a ghost collection, a rug and a dining table dilutes the quilt message. A Christmas Shop banner, a Design Crew block, an app promo, a rewards card and an Affirm block follow. That makes the email very long and gives the shopper many exits.
- Overall it's a solid 6-7 send: on-theme, honest about price, and polished. It spends its strong opening on a long tail of unrelated modules, and "from $199" is a starting price, not a deal.

## What's working

- The subject's "from $199" is echoed by the "Starting at $199" tag, so a price anchor is visible.
- The "Quilts Ship Free" banner in the hero adds a second concrete benefit.
- Each quilt gets its own lifestyle shot, a product name, and a short caption. The copy sells the plaid, velvet and heritage story.
- The seasonal timing is right. Holiday plaids in early October suit the "Holiday Movie Magic" send earlier today.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text. A "Complete Your Retreat" category grid (Duvets, Sheets, Pillows, Blankets, Beds, Nightstands) adds easy cross-sell navigation.

## What's weak

- The per-product CTAs are small "Shop the Look" text links, not buttons. Only "Shop All Quilts" is a real button.
- The "Starting at $199" tag is small and sits below the hero, so it may not be seen on a mobile first screen.
- There's no discount, deadline or member benefit. "From $199" is a price floor, so urgency is zero.
- The long tail of recommendations, the Christmas Shop banner, Design Crew, the app promo, the rewards card and Affirm bury the quilt focus and make the email very long.
- The fine print at the bottom is a dense wall of exclusions, which undercuts the free-shipping promise.
- Today's earlier send, "Inspired by your favorite holiday movies...", was also holiday-themed, so the cadence feels crowded.

## Recommendations

- 1. Turn each "Shop the Look" link into a full-width button with a specific verb and noun, such as "Shop the Patchwork Plaid Quilt." Put the price on each module (for example, "Full/Queen from $199").
- 2. Cut everything after "Shop All Quilts" except a short "Complete Your Retreat" row. Drop the generic Recommended grid, the app promo and the Affirm block, or move them to a minimal footer.
- 3. Enlarge the "Starting at $199" tag and put it in the hero, next to the free-shipping line, so the offer is the first thing seen.
- 4. Add a deadline or member perk, such as "Free shipping through Sunday" or "Members save extra."
- 5. Space out the holiday sends. Don't run two holiday-themed emails on the same day.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Plaid quilts for the holidays, from $199`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Quilts ship free: heritage plaids from $199`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cozy patchwork, velvet and reversible plaids, with free shipping on every quilt.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Three heritage quilts to bring out before guests arrive. Free shipping, from $199.`

## Technical Audit: "Heritage holiday quilts, from $199"

### 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, image-sliced build behind ESP click-wrapping. The main confirmed problems are widespread missing alt text (about 52 images), three plain-HTTP image requests (one carrying the recipient's email address in the query string), and stray server-side code inside a `<style>` block. Header-level compliance and authentication checks couldn't be confirmed because the relay stripped the headers.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque click tracking.** All 60 tracked links go through `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`, for example the logo link. The HTTP probe skipped them, so destination status, redirect chain and UTM parameters are unverified.
- **Plain-HTTP tracking pixel.** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is served over HTTP and may be blocked or downgraded by clients.
- **Third-party pixel.** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` is an ad-network pixel with `alt="1"`.
- **Stale and cross-brand assets.**
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` is a 2022 asset, also on HTTP.
  - `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a West Elm-hosted spacer inside a Pottery Barn send, a cross-domain dependency.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, about 52 images.** Examples:
  - Every slice in `H26_Bedding_B1_QuiltsOfTheSeason/slices_01–17.jpg`.
  - All of `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`.
  - Product images (`reeva-handwoven-rug-3-l.jpg`, `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`, etc.).
  - The banner images `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg` and `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`.

  Because the content is sliced images, image-blocking clients and screen readers get little or no content.
- **Mixed content.** Three `http://` images are flagged: `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, the 2022 `slices_02.jpg`, and the `svopn` pixel.
- **Empty `<title></title>`** and no `lang` attribute on `<html xmlns=...>`.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, an accessibility failure.
- **Leaked server-side code in CSS.** The last `<style>` block contains `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store...")`. Clients may discard it, but it is malformed CSS.
- **Missing table roles.** Spacer tables such as `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><tr><td height="20">&nbsp;</td>` have no `role="presentation"`, unlike the content tables.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 10,830 of 15,038 characters (72%) are URLs, which is a spam-filter risk and poor for text-only readers.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Email address in clear-text URLs.** The recipient address is appended to `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`. It also appears in `sv_sveme=` on the plain-HTTP `svopn` pixel, which exposes it in transit.
- No unresolved merge tokens are visible in the truncated source.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may be stripping them. This is unconfirmed as a sender defect, but one-click unsubscribe support can't be verified. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown, which is likely a relay artifact. From-domain `e.potterybarn.com` matches the click and image subdomains, which is consistent but not proof.
- **Footer not verifiable.** The truncated source doesn't show the footer, which is built from image slices (`Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`). The visible unsubscribe link, physical postal address and sender identification can't be checked, and image-only footers without alt text would fail accessibility there.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Destination URLs are hidden behind `qs=` tokens, and no UTM parameters are visible in the source.
- Product images resolve to `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`, the same brand domain, so there is no domain mismatch.
- Landing-page alignment for the quilts hero can't be verified because no link was resolved.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all flagged images, with empty `alt=""` for spacers, and carry the hero text and offer ("Heritage holiday quilts, from $199") in live text or alt text.
2. Move `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, the 2022 `slices_02.jpg` and the `svopn` pixel to HTTPS. Stop passing the plain email address in query strings, and use a hashed or opaque ID instead.
3. Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP lines from the `<style>` block.
4. Add a `<title>`, `lang="en"` and `role="presentation"` on all layout tables. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` against a raw-header capture that bypasses the relay.
6. Resolve a sample of the click-wrapped links with a browser-like client to verify destinations and UTM tagging.
7. Replace the 2022 banner and the West Elm spacer with current Pottery Barn-hosted assets.
