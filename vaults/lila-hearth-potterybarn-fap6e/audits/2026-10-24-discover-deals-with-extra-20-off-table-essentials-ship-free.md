# Discover deals with EXTRA 20% off + Table essentials ship FREE
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-24**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth "Extra 20% off" send in about three days (10/22 "STARTS NOW 📣" and "ON NOW!", 10/23 "❤️ Take an EXTRA 20%" and "Sale on sale!", and earlier today "Save MORE with EXTRA 20% OFF"). The oxblood banner at the top is the same one again. The real news is the "Holiday Tabletop Ships Free" hero, and it sits second. The subject leads with the stale offer, so the fresh hook gets buried.
- The tabletop creative is good: a styled place setting, a clean category grid (Dinnerware, Serveware, Table Linens, Glassware, Barware, Flatware), and a "Shop all free shipping" button. It's seasonal and easy to scan. Below it the email sprawls into Caterer's Box, a personalized product row, the Christmas Shop, in-stock furniture, app, rewards and Affirm modules. That dilutes the message, and the long legal block at the bottom adds to it.
- Verdict: a decent, seasonal email held back by cadence fatigue and a hierarchy that puts the old offer ahead of the new one. Free shipping on table essentials is a credible hook for hosting season, but I can't see a minimum spend or deadline in the render.

## What's working

- The "Holiday Tabletop Ships Free" hero is seasonal, visually rich and has a clear "Shop Now" button.
- The six-category grid with underlined labels is easy to scan and lets the reader self-select.
- The "Prep for the season of gathering with shipping on us" line ties the offer to a real occasion.
- The "Recommended just for you" row gives some personalization.
- The "Shop all free shipping" button gives the free-shipping offer its own clear action.

## What's weak

- The oxblood "Extra 20% off" banner is the sixth near-identical one in three days and takes the top spot from the new free-shipping news.
- The email stacks too many offers: 20% off, up to 70% off, free shipping, rewards and Affirm. No single message wins.
- The free-shipping offer shows no deadline or minimum spend, so there's no credible urgency.
- The lower modules (Christmas Shop, In-stock furniture, app, rewards card) make it a long scroll with several competing CTAs.
- The footer fine print is dense and hard to read.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the free-shipping tabletop hero and demote or drop the sale banner. The 20% banner has run all week and is what causes the fatigue.
- 2. Add a deadline and any minimum spend to the free-shipping offer, for example "Free shipping ends Sunday."
- 3. Cut the lower modules to one secondary hero, such as the Christmas Shop or Caterer's Box, and drop the app and Affirm blocks.
- 4. Put a single hero product or set above the category grid, so the first scroll has one concrete item.
- 5. Tighten the subject to one offer instead of two.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Holiday tabletop ships free, today through Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Set the table: free shipping on serveware + linens`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Dinnerware, glassware, flatware and more, delivered free. Plus extra 20% off select styles.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Host-ready table essentials, shipped free. Shop before Sunday.`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The HTML is a table-based, 650px XHTML 1.0 Transitional email. It is built almost entirely from sliced images and routed through click-tracking redirects. The automated QA confirms widespread missing alt text, several non-HTTPS image sources, and a broken CSS block. It could not verify authentication headers or List-Unsubscribe, because the relay didn't capture them.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 52 tracking links went through `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…` and were skipped by the HTTP probe. Their destinations are unverified. No broken links are confirmed.
- The logo link uses an opaque `qs=` token (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7ImQi…`). It can't be matched to a destination from the source.
- A third-party tracking pixel is present: `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1`. It is an ad-network (Turn/Amobee) pixel with no alt text. The `cat/1` path suggests a retargeting beacon.
- Open-tracking pixels:
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is plain HTTP and may be blocked.
  - `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/a5be7888-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` is a second open pixel.
- The recipient email address appears in plaintext in the `sv_sveme=` query string and in `coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`. This is PII in URLs. The `svopn` call is unencrypted HTTP, so the address is exposed in transit.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- Missing alt text is flagged on about 45 images. The main groups are:
  - Hero and banner slices: `H26_TOP_Extra20CLR.jpg`, `H26_TTOP_B3_TTOPShipsFree/slices_01–10.jpg`, `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg`, `H25_Bottom_InStock_V2.jpg`.
  - Product images: `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`, `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg`, `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-1245-1-l.jpg`, `emery-linen-curtain-l.jpg`, `standard-curtain-rod-wall-bracket-l.jpg`, `open-box-cooper-ribbed-glass-pendant…`.
  - Footer slices: `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`.
  - Tracking pixels and `spacer_0.gif`.
- Several of these are the only carriers of the offer text, such as "EXTRA 20% off" and "ships free". With images off, the offer won't show. The `<title>` is also empty (`<title></title>`).
- Three images use `http://`, which may be blocked or flagged as mixed content:
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/…`
- The second `<style>` block contains stray PHP-style text: `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store…"); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. This is invalid CSS. It is probably a template leak and can break parsing of the rules that follow it in some clients.
- `<html>` and `<body>` have no `lang` attribute.
- `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` are in the media queries, but the visible markup uses `class="contenttable"`. Those mobile rules may never match.
- The hero tables have fixed `width="650"` with `class="contenttable"`, and the commented-out `.contenttable{width:320px}` rule means they won't shrink on mobile. Responsive behavior is limited to `img{max-width:100%}` at ≥650px.
- A Google Fonts `<link>` (Montserrat) is included. Many clients strip it, so it needs a fallback stack.
- The preheader text is "Serveware, linens, glasses & more", which doesn't match the subject line's offer. It is followed by a long run of filler characters (`͏`, `­`).
- Stale asset paths are reused: `2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner`, `H25_Bottom_InStock_V2` (2025), and `edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` (a cross-brand West Elm asset on a Pottery Barn send).

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the truncated source or the QA output.
- The email address is merged correctly into the `sv_sveme` and `coreemail.html?email=` parameters.
- The source is truncated, so I can't check body-level personalization. No other issues found.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- QA flags a missing `List-Unsubscribe` header and a missing `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unverified, not confirmed**. If they are truly absent, that is a gap against Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules.
- The `Authentication-Results` header wasn't captured, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC are **unverified**. The From domain `e.potterybarn.com` and the tracking domains are aligned subdomains of potterybarn.com, which suggests the setup is sound.
- The truncated source doesn't show the footer, so the unsubscribe link and physical address can't be confirmed. The footer is image slices (`Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`) with no alt text. If the unsubscribe text and postal address exist only as images, they are invisible with images off and to screen readers, which is a risk.
- The plain-text part is 70% URLs (9,887 of 14,066 characters), a spam-filter signal.
- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` are present. They are harmless but unusual for email.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- No UTM parameters are visible in the source. Every link goes through `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…`, so the final landing URLs and any UTM tagging aren't verifiable without following the redirects. The QA tool skipped them.
- Product images are hotlinked from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/…`, the production site CDN, not an email CDN (`edm.` or `image.e.`). Image availability is therefore tied to the live catalog. A product update or SKU removal could break the images. The pendant filename, `open-box-cooper-ribbed-glass-pendant-large-14-diam-vinta…`, is an "open box" item, so the image or product may disappear quickly.
- Whether the "EXTRA 20%" and "Table essentials ship FREE" offers match the landing pages can't be confirmed from the source.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all images, including the offer banners ("EXTRA 20% off clearance", "Table essentials ship free"), the product images, and each footer slice. Use `alt=""` for the spacer and pixels.
2. Move `http://` assets (`Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`, `svopn` pixel) to HTTPS. Retire the 2022 and 2025 assets.
3. Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP text from the `<style>` block.
4. Stop putting the plaintext recipient email in URL query strings (`sv_sveme=`, `coreemail.html?email=`). Use a hashed or opaque ID, and never send it over HTTP.
5. Fill in the `<title>`, add `lang="en"` and `role="presentation"` on layout tables, and align the `wrapper` and `photo` class selectors with the actual markup.
6. Confirm that `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` are present in the raw message. Test against a direct inbox, not just the AgentMail relay. Make sure the footer has a text-based unsubscribe link and postal address.
7. Make the plain-text part carry real copy, not mostly URLs.
8. Replace the cross-brand West Elm spacer and the production-site product hotlinks with email-CDN-hosted assets.
9. Follow the 52 redirect links and confirm that final URLs resolve with consistent UTM tags and offer-matching landing pages.
