# Starting at $299: Handcrafted rugs in wool & jute
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-30**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's tightest category-specific send in the recent stretch — a genuine rug catalog, not a kitchen-sink dump. "Handcrafted Rugs, Starting at $299" leads clean, followed by a well-organized four-product ladder (Emerson Wool, Drift Jute, Calliope Wool) each with clear pricing, styled room shots, and its own "Shop the Look" CTA. It reads like a real editorial rug guide, complete with a "Rug Guide" sizing tool and a swatch-ordering CTA — genuinely useful, non-discount content that most of these sends skip.
- Where it falls apart is the back half. After "Shop All Rugs," the email sprawls into the exact kitchen-sink tail flagged in the July 29 duvet email and the July 28 table-essentials email — "Recommended Just For You" (curtain rods, a bed frame, a dining table, towels, a duvet cover, a desk), then "Discover New Arrivals," then a second unrelated "Up to 40% Off Outdoor" promo block, then an app pitch and a credit card pitch. A subscriber who came in for rugs gets four different unrelated category pushes before the footer.
- Solid top-of-funnel execution again undercut by an unfocused tail — score reflects a strong hero with real follow-through diluted by scope creep.

## What's working

- Hero is unambiguous: "Handcrafted Rugs" + "Starting at $299" + a single visible "Shop Now" CTA on a clean product shot.
- Four named rugs (Emerson, Drift, Calliope, plus the hero) each carry a distinct price point and material callout (wool vs. jute), letting a shopper self-select by budget or style.
- Room-context photography (living room, dining room, entryway) helps a buyer picture scale and styling, which matters for a considered purchase like a rug.
- "The Rug Guide" and "Order Rug Swatches" blocks add real utility — sizing guidance and a low-risk swatch order are smart adds for a category where fit/color anxiety kills conversion.
- No render bugs — images, prices, and buttons all load cleanly with no overlap or placeholder text.

## What's weak

- The email pivots hard after "Shop All Rugs" into unrelated categories (curtain rods, bedding, dining table, towels, desk) — the same tail-sprawl problem flagged in the July 28 and July 29 sends.
- A second, fully unrelated "Up to 40% Off Outdoor" promo is stapled in near the bottom, competing with the rug narrative instead of reinforcing it.
- "Summer Sale: Up to 70% off" in the top banner is a different, vaguer offer than the specific "$299" rug pricing below it — two competing offers in the first screen.
- No rug-specific social proof (review stars, "bestseller" badge) on any of the four featured products, despite this being a considered, cost-heavy purchase.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after "Shop All Rugs" / "The Rug Guide" / "Order Rug Swatches." A focused rug send converts better than a rug send that dissolves into curtain rods and desks — this is the single highest-leverage fix, and the brand has heard it three sends in a row now.
- 2. Drop the "Summer Sale: Up to 70% off" banner or make it rug-specific ("Rugs up to 70% off") so the first screen has one offer, not two.
- 3. Add a review count or star rating under at least the hero rug — a $299–$699 purchase benefits more from social proof than a $25 candle does.
- 4. If cross-sell is required for revenue reasons, cap it to one adjacent block (e.g., just "Discover New Arrivals" in the rug/home category) instead of four stacked unrelated pushes.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Rugs from $299: wool, jute & new arrivals`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New rug drop — 4 styles, starting at $299`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Handcrafted wool and jute, sized for every room`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping on rugs through Sunday`
