# Don't wait! Shop Early Black Friday + Our sale is UP TO 70% OFF
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the same burgundy "Early Black Friday, up to 70% off" template Pottery Barn has sent since 11/16. It is at least the eighth near-identical send in four days, and the third on 11/19 alone, after "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY SAVINGS for every room" and "🎄 Save BIG on holiday decor 🎄". The hero is clear: a big "70%" in script type, a white SHOP NOW button, and a 12-tile category grid right under it. But a subscriber who has seen this banner all week has no new reason to open it.
- The body is a "Recommended just for you" grid of six products: stockings, a faux fur throw, a curtain rod, a dining table and a quilt. It then adds a Christmas Shop banner (up to 50% off), a Design Crew sweepstakes, an app download, a credit card pitch and Affirm. That is seven or more competing asks below one clear offer. The result reads as a catalog, not a campaign.
- Verdict: competent and on-season, but fatigued. The subject is the weakest part. It stacks "Don't wait!", "Early Black Friday" and "UP TO 70% OFF" into one long line with no new news.

## What's working

- The hero is instantly legible. "70%" dominates, and the eye lands on the offer first.
- The category grid (Furniture, Bedding, Rugs, Curtains and so on) lets a shopper jump straight to their interest in one tap.
- The product grid uses clean, well-lit photography with named items and sizes (e.g. the Toscana table, 60"–125").
- The Christmas Shop banner gives a seasonal secondary hook with a distinct 50% offer and a red/green holiday look.
- The sale exclusions and sweepstakes terms are in the footer, not hidden in the hero.

## What's weak

- The template is a near-exact repeat of the earlier sends, so there is no new reason to open or click.
- The "Recommended just for you" grid shows no prices, no sale prices and no reviews. Shoppers can't see the savings, and the "just for you" label is unconvincing.
- Too many competing CTAs sit below the hero: Christmas Shop, Design Crew, App Store, Rewards card and Affirm.
- The hero has no deadline. "Don't wait!" implies urgency, but nothing in the render says when the sale ends.
- The footer is a long wall of dense legal text. The "up to 70%" claim sits above a lot of fine print, and the Affirm APR and sweepstakes copy add to it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real deadline or a new twist to the hero, such as "Ends Sunday" or "Today only: extra 10% off rugs". Without that, the send is just the same banner again.
- 2. Cut the page to one hero, one category grid and one product grid. Move the app, card, Affirm and sweepstakes blocks to a separate lifecycle email.
- 3. Show sale prices with the original price struck through under each product. Add star ratings on the best sellers.
- 4. Replace "Shop Now" with category-specific copy such as "Shop sofas up to 70% off".
- 5. Throttle the cadence. Three sends in one day with the same template trains people to ignore it. Segment by recent browse category instead.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Early Black Friday picks are on sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus free shipping on select furniture. Sale prices end Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stockings, throws & dining tables, all marked down.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout sent through Salesforce Marketing Cloud (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`). The main confirmed issues are about 46 images without alt text, three `http://` image sources (one of them carrying the recipient's email address), and stray PHP code inside a `<style>` block. Compliance and authentication could not be verified because the headers were not captured.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **55 tracking links were not probed.** The QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and landing pages are unverified. The only visible example is the logo link: `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7ImQiOjUwNjUs...`.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`. It loads over plain HTTP, so it may be blocked or rewritten, and open tracking may be undercounted.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEv.../cat/1` is an ad-network beacon with no alt text. It is not a defect on its own, but it is an extra data-sharing endpoint.
- **Second open pixel:** `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/63f923dd-...?mi_cid=677b44e6831b79f3&mi_mid=...` is a separate open pixel. Two or three tracking beacons in one message is redundant.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 46 images.** This includes:
  - the entire hero, `…/H26_Sale_EBF_GraphicHeroLinkfarm/slices_01–12.jpg`;
  - the product images (`faux-fur-ruched-throw-l.jpg`, `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-125-l.jpg`, and others);
  - the bottom banners (`H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop_50off.jpg`, `H26_Bottom_DesignCrewSweeps_01/02.jpg`);
  - footer `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`.

  The offer and navigation appear to be image-only. With images blocked, the hero and footer links render as empty linked images, and screen readers get no label.
- **HTTP image sources:** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`. They may be blocked or flagged as mixed content.
- **Stray PHP in CSS:** the second `<style>` block ends with `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store..."); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. This is leaked server-side code. It is invalid CSS and may corrupt the rule that follows in strict parsers.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** Some clients and screen readers use the title.
- **No `lang` attribute** on `<html>`. The tag declares `xmlns` only.
- **Viewport and text sizing:** `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom. `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` and `-ms-text-size-adjust:none` disable text scaling, a WCAG 1.4.4 resize-text concern.
- **Layout tables:** the preheader and header tables use `role="presentation"`, but the spacer table (`<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><tr><td height="20">&nbsp;</td>`) does not.
- **Cross-brand asset:** the spacer image `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on a sister brand's domain, which is a fragile dependency. It needs `alt=""`.
- **Web font:** the Google Fonts `<link>` for Montserrat is stripped by most clients, and Gmail and Outlook ignore it. Confirm the fallback stack renders acceptably.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) are visible in the truncated source.
- **The recipient email is exposed in URLs:**
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/...&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`

  The second is the pixel over plain HTTP, so the address travels unencrypted and can appear in proxy and server logs. Also, `coreemail.html` is reported as an image with no alt text, so it is a pixel or beacon.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found. The QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unverified, not a confirmed defect**. Check the raw headers at the origin.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. The QA attributes this to the relay. The visible From domain is `e.potterybarn.com`, and its alignment cannot be confirmed from this data.
- **Unsubscribe link and postal address:** the source is truncated before the footer, so they could not be checked. The footer is composed of image slices (`Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`). If the unsubscribe link and address are image-only, they are unreadable with images off. Verify against the full HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** none are visible because every link goes through the opaque SFMC `qs=` redirect, so UTM presence and campaign naming cannot be confirmed.
- **Landing pages:** unverified because the 55 tracking links were skipped. Product images point to `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`, which is consistent with the brand domain.
- **Stale footer asset:** `…/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` dates from 2022, so the footer likely reuses an old banner.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the hero slices, product images and footer slices (about 46). Use `alt=""` for spacers and pixels.
2. Move all three `http://` image sources to HTTPS. Move the `svopn` pixel first.
3. Stop putting the recipient email in query strings. Use an opaque subscriber ID, especially on non-HTTPS endpoints.
4. Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP lines from the `<style>` block.
5. Add a `<title>`, `lang="en"`, and `role="presentation"` on the remaining layout tables. Drop `maximum-scale=1` and `text-size-adjust:none`.
6. Consolidate the redundant open pixels.
7. Re-run QA against the raw `.eml` to check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Resolve the redirects for the 55 links to confirm landing pages and UTMs.
8. Check that the footer's unsubscribe link and physical address exist as live text, not only as image slices.
