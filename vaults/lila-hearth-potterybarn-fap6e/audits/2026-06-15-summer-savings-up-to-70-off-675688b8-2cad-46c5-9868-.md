# Summer savings UP TO 70% OFF 🌻
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-15**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's sixth or seventh "Up to 70% off" email since June 12 — the branding is polished, the hero image is genuinely lovely (soft blue floral bedding that reads "Summer" immediately), and the cadence has now crossed from aggressive into numbing. The "100s of new deals added!" sub-copy tries to manufacture freshness, but subscribers who've seen this subject line variant four times this week have already made their peace with the sale.
- Where this send earns its keep: the in-email personalization play ("Recommended Just For You") is the strongest structural move in the batch. It breaks the scroll pattern and gives a reason to engage beyond the hero CTA. The loyalty widget (Earn 10% Back), the Affirm module, and the Design Service block add real utility depth below the fold.
- The problem is the subject line. "Summer savings UP TO 70% OFF 🌻" is so close to the June 14 "100+ new sale styles UP TO 70% OFF" that it will land as a duplicate to any subscriber who's been paying attention. The sunflower emoji is doing the heaviest differentiation work here — and that's not enough.

## What's working

- **Hero image earns the open.** The blue floral bedding is genuinely beautiful and screams Summer without a single word — the brand's best visual in this run.
- **"100s of new deals added!"** — small copy under the hero headline gives a concrete reason to re-engage over prior sends.
- **"Recommended Just For You"** — product-level personalization grid breaks the template feel and provides scroll momentum.
- **Loyalty widget is above the fold-ish.** "Earn 10% Back in Rewards" is surfaced early enough that a KEY member will clock it.
- **Category grid is fast and scannable** — Furniture, Outdoor, Bedding, Sofas, etc. cover the real purchase intent verticals cleanly.

## What's weak

- **Cadence fatigue is real.** This is send #6-7 in a near-identical format over three days; the "UP TO 70% OFF" subject is now a pattern the subscriber eye glazes past.
- **"SHOP ALL SALE" CTA is too broad.** It dumps you on the full sale page — a missed chance to tie the hero's bedding visual to a specific category landing.
- **No deadline.** "Up to 70% off" with no end date undercuts urgency. Without a "Ends Sunday" or "3 days left" signal, the subscriber can rationally defer.
- **Design Service and Affirm blocks dilute focus.** Below the recommended products, the email becomes a three-module brand brochure (Design Service → App download → Rewards → Affirm) that fragments the conversion path.
- **Subject has no differentiation** from the June 14 send — "Summer savings" vs. "100+ new sale styles" is the same offer dressed in different adjectives.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pin a deadline in the hero and subject.** Even "3 days left" or "Ends Sunday" would make this send stand out from the prior six and force a decision. This is the single highest-leverage change.
- 2. **Swap "SHOP ALL SALE" for a bedding-specific CTA** ("Shop Bedding Sale") to match the hero imagery — align visual and click destination.
- 3. **Collapse the below-fold module stack.** Cut the Affirm block or move it to footer; let Recommended Products → Design Service → Rewards flow cleanly without the payment module interrupting.
- 4. **Give the subject a hook the previous six didn't use** — a deadline, a product shoutout, or a member-exclusive angle.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends Sunday: up to 70% off Summer Sale 🌻`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Members earn 10% back on 70% off — this weekend only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New deals in bedding, outdoor + furniture — shop before it's gone`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `100s of new styles added — your picks are waiting`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**CRITICAL — PHP `header()` calls embedded inside a CSS `<style>` block**
The final `<style>` tag in `<head>` contains:
```
header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
header("Cache-Control: post-check=0, pre-check=0", false);
header("Pragma: no-cache");
```
This is server-side PHP that was not executed — it was passed through raw into the HTML output. Several email clients (notably Outlook and some Gmail views) will render unknown CSS text as visible content. This is a template build/rendering pipeline defect.

**HIGH — 43 images missing `alt` text**
Affected images span all major content zones:
- Main content: `slices_01.jpg` through `slices_12.jpg`
- Product tiles: `farmhouse-platform-bed-l.jpg`, `hydrocotton-quick-dry-towel-l.jpg`, `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-1245-l.jpg`, and 6 others
- Footer: `Email-Footer/slices_01.jpg` through `slices_18.jpg`
- Key CC banners: `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `slices_02.jpg` (CC block)

Images-off rendering (common in Outlook, preview pane) will show blank rectangles for the entire email body.

**HIGH — 3 image assets served over HTTP (mixed content)**
| Image | URL |
|---|---|
| `spacer_0.gif` | `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` |

### 5. Compliance

**WARN — `List-Unsubscribe` header absent**
Not captured by AgentMail relay. If genuinely missing from the envelope, this violates RFC 2369 and CAN-SPAM best practices. Gmail and Yahoo enforced this header as a hard requirement for bulk senders (>5k/day) starting February 2024.

**WARN — `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent**
RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe. Same Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender mandate. Without this, Gmail shows a manual unsubscribe flow that increases friction and spam complaints.

**WARN — `Authentication-Results` header not found**
SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status is unknown via the AgentMail relay. If the relay strips these headers, the underlying authentication cannot be confirmed. Sending domain is `e.potterybarn.com` — should be verifiable via direct MX/SPF lookup against that domain.

**INFO — `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer" />`**
This suppresses HTTP `Referer` headers on all outbound link clicks from webmail clients that render the email in a browser context. Any analytics that rely on referrer (rather than UTM params) will show traffic as direct.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

UTM parameters are not directly inspectable — all destination URLs are behind the `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirect wrapper. No issues are confirmed, but the following create risk:
