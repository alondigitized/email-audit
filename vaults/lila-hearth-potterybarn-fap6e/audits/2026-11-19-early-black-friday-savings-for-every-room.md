# EARLY BLACK FRIDAY SAVINGS for every room
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the same burgundy "Early Black Friday, up to 70% off" template Pottery Barn has sent since 11/16, including "Early Black Friday is UP TO 70% OFF" and "Amazing Early Black Friday deals + Up to 70% off throws & pillows" on 11/18. The hero is clean and the 70% number dominates. The category grid under it is a useful shortcut. Below that, the email turns into a long catalog of product tiles, a Christmas Shop banner, a sweepstakes, a rewards card, Affirm and a large fine-print block.
- Nothing is broken, but nothing is new. A subscriber who has seen this banner seven or eight times in four days has little reason to open or click. The subject, "savings for every room," is the vaguest framing of the week. The best content is lower down: a Whitechair at $449 (was $899) and a dining table at $1,599.
- Judgement: competent and on-brand, but fatigued. It is a 6 to 7 email whose main weakness is cadence and a generic subject, not execution.

## What's working

- The hero is unambiguous. A large "up to 70% off" sits over a single "Shop Now" button on a seasonal burgundy background.
- The 8-tile category grid (Furniture, Bedding, Rugs and so on) lets a shopper jump straight to their room.
- Product tiles show "Was / Now" pricing, for example the Whitechair at $899 → $449 and the Bouclé Pillow at $49.50 → $39.50. The "Ships Free" badges add a concrete benefit.
- The Christmas Shop (up to 50% off) tile and the stocking products keep the email seasonally relevant.
- The 10% back in rewards card and the Affirm block give a payment-side reason to buy.

## What's weak

- The campaign is highly repetitive. It uses the same banner and the same "up to 70%" claim as the 11/16–11/18 sends, with no escalation and no deadline.
- No end date appears anywhere in the body, so the urgency is weak.
- The back half is cluttered. The sweepstakes, the app download, the rewards card, Affirm and the dense legal block compete with the offer and dilute the one clear CTA.
- "Recommended just for you" shows stockings, a throw, a curtain rod and a quilt with no prices or CTAs, so it works as inspiration and does little to drive clicks.
- The fine print is a wall of exclusions around the "up to 70%" claim. This hurts how honest the offer feels, because the best discount probably applies to only a few items.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a deadline to the hero, such as "Ends Sunday" or "Ends tonight," and put the real hook above the fold, for example the $449 chair at 50% off. Replace "up to 70%" as the lead claim.
- 2. Cut or reorder the lower modules. The sweepstakes, app download and Affirm blocks should drop below a single best-sellers row, or be removed from sends that have already featured them this week.
- 3. Vary the creative day to day. Rotate the hero by category (seating, bedding, rugs) instead of reusing the same banner.
- 4. Add prices and star ratings to the "Recommended just for you" tiles, and give the CTA a verb and a noun, such as "Shop Sofas & Chairs."
- 5. Collapse the legal block into a short "See details" link.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: up to 70% off furniture & rugs`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Our $449 chair + 70% off decor, today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping on select items. Sofas, bedding, rugs and more, all marked down.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Early access ends soon. Plus 10% back with the PB card.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Pottery Barn "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY SAVINGS for every room"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, image-sliced layout with a Marketing Cloud-style redirect-wrapped link farm. The main source-level problems are widespread missing alt text, cleartext HTTP tracking and image requests that carry the recipient's address, and stray server-side code inside a `<style>` block. The header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be verified through the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **63 tracking links were not probed.** They are on click-redirect domains (e.g. `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...`), so destination health, redirect chains and UTM presence are unverified.
- **Cleartext open pixel.** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is HTTP, not HTTPS. It may be blocked by clients, and it sends the recipient's address in cleartext.
- **Several tracking beacons, no alt text.** The QA tool flags these as images missing alt text:
  - `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1`
  - `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/4095accc-...?mi_cid=...`
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=...`
  - They are pixels, so this is low severity, but they should carry `alt=""`.
- **Third-party pixel.** `d.turn.com` is a third-party ad-tech beacon. It is also a deliverability and privacy surface.
- **Plain-text part is link-heavy.** 12,431 of 17,459 characters (71%) are URLs, which exceeds the 70% threshold. This comes from the long `qs=` tokens and is a spam-filter signal.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images.** About 50 images are flagged. The main ones:
  - Hero link-farm slices `slices_01–12.jpg` under `/pb/2026/US/H26_Sale_EBF_GraphicHeroLinkfarm/`.
  - `slices_01–06.jpg` under `/1119_THU_AM_EBF_GraphicHeroLinkFarm+WasNowGrid/`.
  - Footer `slices_01–18.jpg` under `/Email-Footer/`.
  - Product images such as `faux-fur-ruched-throw-l.jpg` and `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-125-l.jpg`.
  - Banners such as `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop_50off.jpg`.
- **Image-only navigation.** With images blocked, or for screen readers, the hero link farm and footer links have no accessible names.
- **Mixed content.** Two images load over HTTP and may be blocked:
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - The second one is a 2022 asset path, so it is likely legacy.
- **Empty title.** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **No language attribute.** `<html>` has no `lang` attribute.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, an accessibility failure.
- **Stray PHP code in CSS.** The second `<style>` block contains `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store..."); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. It is invalid CSS and can break parsing of the rules around it in strict clients.
- **Missing `role="presentation"`.** Some layout tables lack it, e.g. the spacer `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><tr><td height="20">&nbsp;</td>`.
- **Web font.** The Google Fonts `<link>` for Montserrat is stripped by Gmail and Outlook, so those clients fall back. Confirm the font stack has fallbacks.
- **Cross-brand spacer.** `spacer_0.gif` is hosted on `edm.westelm.com`, a different brand's host, with no alt text.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved merge tokens** (such as `%%...%%` or `{{...}}`) are visible in the truncated source.
- **Recipient address in URLs.** The plain address appears unencoded in two URLs: `coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` and the `sv_sveme=` parameter above. The `svopn` one is over HTTP, so the address is exposed in transit and in proxy logs. This is a privacy and PII-in-URL issue.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly stripping headers. It is **unconfirmed**, not a verified defect.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment could not be assessed through the relay. Check the raw `.eml` upstream.
- **Body footer not verified.** The visible HTML is truncated, so an in-body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address could not be confirmed. The footer is built from `/Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`, which are image slices without alt text. If the unsubscribe text is baked into an image, it is inaccessible with images off.
- **Robots meta tag.** `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` is appropriate for email.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Not verifiable.** All 63 CTAs sit behind `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirects that were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment could not be checked.
- **Direct URLs.** The only non-redirect links in the source are image sources on `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`. These hotlink the production site's CDN, so product images depend on site availability.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content slices and product images. Use `alt=""` on spacers and tracking pixels. Keep the unsubscribe and legal text as live HTML, not image slices.
2. Move `svopn.potterybarn.com` and the two `edm.potterybarn.com` images to HTTPS.
3. Stop putting the plain email address in URL query strings (`coreemail.html?email=`, `sv_sveme=`). Use an opaque subscriber ID or a hash.
4. Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP lines from the `<style>` block.
5. Fill in `<title>`, add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and drop `maximum-scale=1`.
6. Add `role="presentation"` to all layout tables.
7. Re-run the compliance and authentication checks against the raw message (`.eml`) rather than the relay copy, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
8. Resolve a sample of the redirect-wrapped links to confirm UTM parameters and landing pages.
9. Shorten the plain-text part and reduce URL density if possible.
