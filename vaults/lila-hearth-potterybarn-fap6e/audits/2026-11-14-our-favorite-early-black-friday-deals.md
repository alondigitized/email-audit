# Our favorite EARLY BLACK FRIDAY DEALS ❤️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, conventional Pottery Barn sale email, and it's the third near-identical Early Black Friday send in two days. The burgundy hero reads "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY / up to 70% off" with a white SHOP NOW button, and the eye lands there first. Below it, eight category tiles each carry a "Was/Now" price on a named product, so the offer is concrete.
- The weakness is that it adds almost nothing new. The subject promises "our favorite" deals, but the body is a standard category grid with no editorial pick or "staff favorite" framing. That makes it a repeat of the 11/13 "STARTS NOW" and "NEW deals added" sends. It's solid on execution and weak on distinction.
- Two caveats. The render was shown very small, so I couldn't read fine print. The preheader isn't visible in the render either.

## What's working

- The hero is unambiguous: "up to 70% off" in large type with a single SHOP NOW button.
- Category tiles pair a named product with "Was $2,199 / Now $874" style pricing, which makes the discounts tangible.
- "SHIPS FREE" flags on several tiles add a second reason to buy.
- Alternating image and text tiles keep the long scroll easy to scan.
- A second SHOP ALL SALE button after the "More Early Black Friday Deals" block gives a second chance to click.

## What's weak

- The subject promises "our favorite" deals, but the body is a generic category grid with no curation.
- Almost every tile says "up to X% off". The discount range is vague, and the actual savings only show on one product per tile.
- The email is very long. The "Recommended just for you" block, the Design Crew appointment pitch, the app and rewards promos and the Affirm block all dilute the sale focus.
- There is no visible deadline or end date, so "early" is the only urgency. That's weak after three sends in two days.
- The footer is dense, with heavy fine print that I couldn't read at this size.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make "favorite" real. Put 3–4 hero products at the top with Was/Now prices and a "most-loved" or "best-selling" tag, then drop the category grid lower.
- 2. Add a real deadline to the hero, such as "Ends Sunday", with a clear end time.
- 3. Cut the lower promo stack (Design Crew, app, rewards, Affirm) to one secondary module.
- 4. Space out sends. A new angle, like "last chance" or a single category, beats a fourth identical "Early Black Friday" email.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with many accessibility and hygiene gaps: about 50 images lack alt text, three assets load over `http://`, and the `<title>` is empty. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the relay doesn't expose those headers. The HTML supplied was truncated, so the footer and any unsubscribe link couldn't be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links not verified:** 60 tracking links on `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` were skipped as redirect domains. No link validity or destination data exists for this email.
- **Mixed-content tracking pixels over HTTP:**
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` is a non-HTTPS open pixel and may be blocked.
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` is non-HTTPS.
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` is non-HTTPS.
- **Extra pixels:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/e3a1f571-...?mi_cid=...` are additional tracking pixels with no alt text.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a West Elm-hosted spacer inside a Pottery Barn send. It is a shared-template dependency and has no alt text.
- **Stale asset paths:** `/pb/2025/US/H25_Bottom_InStock_V2/` and `/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/` suggest reused modules. They render now, but the paths are old.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 50 images are flagged.
  - Content images: `slices_01–15.jpg` in the `1114_SAT_AM_EBF_ZigZagGrid_WasNow` grid, the product images (`remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`, `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-125-l.jpg`, `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg`, and others), and the `H26_Bottom_DesignCrewSweeps_01/02.jpg` banners.
  - Footer slices: `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`.
  - With images blocked, the email has little or no readable content.
- **Empty `<title></title>`:** this affects the web-view and accessibility name.
- **Viewport:** `maximum-scale=1` in `<meta name="viewport">` blocks pinch-zoom, an accessibility (WCAG 1.4.4) concern.
- **Stray server code in the CSS:** the second `<style>` block contains `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store...` PHP text. It is invalid CSS and can break parsing of the rules that follow in some clients.
- **Wrong meta tags for email:** `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` have no effect in most email clients. `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Montserrat">` is stripped by Gmail and Outlook, so the font falls back.
- **Outlook-incompatible CSS:** `*{max-height:1000000px; line-height:100%}` and the `table[class="wrapper"]` / `img[class="photo"]` attribute selectors are fragile in some clients.
- **Plain-text part:** 11,846 of 16,828 characters (70%) are URLs, mostly long tracking strings. That hurts readability and spam scoring.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** neither header was captured. This is likely an AgentMail relay artifact and is not confirmed as a sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers, so verify against the raw original message.
- **Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM/DMARC):** unavailable via the relay, so status is unknown. The `From` domain `e.potterybarn.com` matches the link domains, which is consistent with alignment, but that is unverified.
- **Physical address and in-body unsubscribe link:** not assessable because the HTML was truncated before the footer.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM and landing-page alignment can't be verified.** Every link is an opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` redirect (the logo link is one example), and none were resolved. Only the `potterybarn.com` image paths and `coreemail.html` are visible.
- **Footer pixel:** `coreemail.html` is loaded as an `<img>`, not a landing page.
- **Consistency:** the subject says "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY" and the grid folder is `1114_SAT_AM_EBF_...`, so asset naming matches the 11/14 send date.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add alt text** to all content images, product images and footer slices. Use `alt=""` for spacers and tracking pixels.
2. **Change the three `http://` assets and the `svopn` pixel to HTTPS.**
3. **Remove the email address from `sv_sveme` and the `coreemail.html` `?email=` parameters.** Use an opaque recipient ID.
4. **Remove the stray PHP `header(...)` lines** from the `<style>` block.
5. **Fill `<title>`** and drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
6. **Verify the raw message headers** (List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, Authentication-Results) outside the relay before treating these as defects.
7. **Resolve the redirect chain for a sample of the 60 tracked links** to check UTM parameters and landing pages.
8. **Capture the full HTML** so the footer's unsubscribe link and postal address can be checked.
