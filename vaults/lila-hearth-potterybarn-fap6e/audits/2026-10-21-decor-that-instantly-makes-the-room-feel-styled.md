# Decor that instantly makes the room feel styled
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a polished, on-brand catalog send with no reason to click today. The "Effortless Updates" hero is a moody, styled shelf vignette with a "Shop Decor" button. Below it, a tidy mosaic covers candles, vases, pillows, throws and wall art, and several tiles carry "Ships Free" tags. It looks like Pottery Barn, but it has no price, no percentage off and no deadline.
- Pottery Barn has sent five or six near-identical category emails in three days. That includes the 10/20 barstools send ("from $99") and the 10/19 nightstands send. This one is the weakest of them because it has no price anchor. The modules after the mosaic (Living Room Inspiration, Recommended Just For You, Christmas Shop, Design Crew) dilute the message into a very long scroll.
- Verdict: a decent brand-building send, but a mediocre conversion driver. It would work better if it led with one product and one price or perk.

## What's working

- The hero is atmospheric and instantly reads as home decor styling, which matches the subject.
- "Ships Free" tags on candles, pillows and throws give a concrete perk without cluttering the layout.
- The in-stock banner ("In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks") adds a practical reason to buy now.
- The "Shop All Pillows & Decor" button is clear and well placed after the mosaic.
- The Christmas Shop tile and the red-tree imagery are seasonally on point.

## What's weak

- No price, percent off or deadline appears anywhere, so there is no reason to click today.
- The category mosaic has many equal-weight tiles and no single hero product.
- The email is very long, with at least four extra modules after the main message, including an app promo, a credit card promo, Affirm and a legal block.
- The "Shop Decor" hero button is generic, and the tiles are labeled by category ("Candles", "Vases") rather than by benefit.
- The "Recommended Just For You" grid shows no prices, so there is no personalization payoff.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with one hero product and a price or perk.** For example, a candle-stick set "from $39" with free shipping. This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Cut the mosaic to 4 tiles and move the Design Crew and Christmas modules into their own sends.
- 3. Add prices to the "Recommended" grid.
- 4. Add a real deadline tied to the Christmas shop or shipping cutoffs.
- 5. Change the CTA from "Shop Decor" to "Shop candles, from $39".

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with about 55 click-tracked links. The main problems are widespread missing alt text on image-sliced content, three `http://` image sources, recipient email addresses in image URLs, and stray PHP code in a `<style>` block. Authentication and unsubscribe headers couldn't be verified from the capture.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracking links not probed.** 55 links on click-redirect domains were skipped, so their destinations are unverified. One example is the logo link `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7ImQi…`. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Non-HTTPS tracking pixel.** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=…` loads over plain HTTP. Clients that block mixed or insecure content will drop the open pixel, so opens are undercounted.
- **Third-party pixel.** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` is an ad-network beacon. A third-party tracker like this is a privacy and deliverability consideration.
- **Cross-brand asset dependency.** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on a sister brand's domain, so removing it there would break this email.
- **Stale asset paths.** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` is a 2022 asset in a 2026 send. This is minor, but it is a maintenance risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 45 images.** The QA check flags `slices_01–13.jpg` (`H26_Decor_B2_EasyDecorUpdates`), `slices_01–18.jpg` (`Email-Footer`), `H25_TOP_InStockFurniture.jpg`, `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg`, `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`, `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and every product image, such as `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-1245-1-l.jpg` and `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`. Most of these are likely linked images, so with images blocked the links have no accessible name. The email would be unusable in that case. The preheader text ("Baskets, candles, botanicals & more") does not make up for this.
- **Insecure image sources.** `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `slices_02.jpg` (2022 banner) and the `svopn` pixel use `http://`, and the first two are content images that may be blocked.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** There is no document title for assistive tech or web-view rendering.
- **No `lang` attribute.** `<html xmlns=…>` has no `lang`, so screen readers can't pick the right language.
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `maximum-scale=1` prevents pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Stray PHP in the CSS.** Inside the `<style>` block there is `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store…")`. This is invalid CSS and looks like a template-build leftover. It may cause the following rule to be dropped in strict parsers.
- **Possibly ineffective media query.** The rules `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` only match if those exact class values exist. I could not confirm that in the truncated source.
- **Layout.** Layout tables are mostly marked `role="presentation"`, which is good. The logo spacer table (`<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0">`) lacks it.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens are visible in the captured source.
- **Recipient email in URLs.** The address `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` appears in plaintext query strings in two places. One is `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=…`. The other is `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/…&sv_sveme=…`, which is not encrypted in transit. This is PII in a URL: it ends up in server logs, and in the `svopn` case it is sent unencrypted.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these, so this is unconfirmed. If they really are absent, the sender is out of line with Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, so none can be confirmed.
- **Footer not visible.** The truncated HTML does not show an unsubscribe link or physical address, so those can't be verified from the supplied source.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 10,580 of 14,787 characters (about 72%) are URLs, mostly tracking URLs, which can raise spam-filter scores.
- **Sender domain.** The From address is `PotteryBarn@e.potterybarn.com`, and the click and image subdomains share the `potterybarn.com` root, so alignment looks consistent. DKIM alignment can't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs not inspectable.** Every sampled link is an opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…` redirect, so UTM parameters can't be read or validated from the HTML. The final landing URLs are unknown because the redirects weren't probed.
- **Product images.** Product images come from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/…`, which at least points to the live site's catalog.
- **`mi_cid` / `mi_mid` parameters.** The `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` pixel carries campaign IDs, which suggests an ESP-side attribution system. This is not confirmable as site-side continuity.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content and product images, and `alt=""` to pixels and spacers (`spacer_0.gif`, `svopn`, `clickcp`, `d.turn.com`).
2. Switch the three `http://` image sources to `https://`, especially the `svopn` open pixel.
3. Remove or hash the recipient email in `coreemail.html?email=` and `sv_sveme=` query strings.
4. Delete the stray `header(...)` PHP lines from the `<style>` block.
5. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, fill in `<title>`, and drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
6. Re-run the check against the original message (not the relay copy) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF, DKIM and DMARC. Also resolve the redirect chains to verify landing pages and UTMs.
7. Host the spacer on the sender's own domain instead of `edm.westelm.com`, and retire the 2022 banner path.
8. Shorten the plain-text part's tracking URLs, or reduce the link count, to improve the text-to-URL ratio.
