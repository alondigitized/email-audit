# Heritage quilts made for layering
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a quilt-led product email with a sale banner stuck on top. The subject promises "Heritage quilts made for layering", and the body delivers on it. The hero, "Made for Layering" with "QUILTS SHIP FREE", sits over a stack of velvet and floral quilts. A second hero shows a hand-quilted velvet scallop quilt, followed by a "More Keep-Forever Quilts" grid. The creative is strong, and the ship-free hook gives it a concrete offer.
- The problem is the first thing you see. A dark-green banner reads "The Fall Sale: Up to 70% off", with its own SHOP NOW button. It is the same Fall Sale creative as this morning's "The Fall Sale is ON 🍁 Up to 70% off". It competes with the quilt story and muddies the offer: is this about quilts, free shipping, or a sitewide sale? The back half is generic template filler: "Recommended Just For You", Discover New Arrivals, the Christmas Shop, app, credit card and Affirm modules. A dense legal block follows.
- Judgement: good merchandising, weak focus. A same-day second send that re-runs the sale banner reads as cadence fatigue. It scores average-to-good, below this morning's clean sale blast.

## What's working

- The "Made for Layering" hero is cozy, seasonal and on-theme, and it matches the subject line.
- "QUILTS SHIP FREE" gives a concrete, category-specific benefit.
- Named products such as "Lydia Palampore Embroidered Quilt" and "Cordelia Toile Quilt" link to specific items, not the homepage.
- The "Essential Bedding Layers" category row (Duvets, Sheets, Comforters, Pillows, Duvet Inserts, Blankets) invites cross-sell.
- The photography is consistent and high quality. I see no broken images or layout bugs.

## What's weak

- The Fall Sale banner on top repeats this morning's send and dilutes the quilt message.
- The sale banner and the hero each carry their own CTA ("SHOP NOW" and "QUILTS SHIP FREE"), so the primary action is unclear.
- The "Recommended Just For You" grid mixes beds, curtain rods, a dining table and a swivel chair. It has nothing to do with quilts and shows no prices or offers.
- The Christmas Shop, New Arrivals, app, credit card and Affirm modules pile on after the quilt story. The email is very long and the focus fades.
- The dense fine print at the bottom is tiny, and it undercuts the "up to 70% off" and free-shipping claims.

## Recommendations

- 1. Remove the Fall Sale banner from this send, or shrink it to a thin strip. Let the quilt hero be the first thing seen.
- 2. Make the CTA explicit: "Shop Quilts" with a visible button, plus a short line stating the ship-free terms.
- 3. Show prices or a "from $X" on the featured quilts.
- 4. Cut the unrelated recommendations and the Christmas, app and card modules. End with quilts, then bedding layers.
- 5. Tell the member benefit and the sale terms plainly above the fold, and leave the legal copy at the bottom.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Hand-quilted velvet and classic florals. Free shipping on every quilt.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, image-sliced layout. Nearly every content image lacks alt text, and three images load over plain `http://`, one of which puts the recipient's address in the URL. Compliance and authentication could not be verified: the QA run did not capture the headers, and the HTML source was truncated before the footer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque tracking.** 57 links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains. Example: `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...`. Destinations and UTM parameters can't be confirmed from this data.
- **Cleartext PII in a tracking pixel.** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is loaded over HTTP with the recipient address in the query string.
- **Address in an image URL.** `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is used as an `<img>` source, so the address is exposed in the URL.
- **Third-party pixel.** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEv.../cat/1` (Turn/Amobee ad-tech) is embedded and has no alt attribute.
- **Insecure images.** Three images use `http://`: `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`, and the svopn pixel. Clients may block or warn on these.
- **Cross-brand and stale assets.** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a West Elm-hosted spacer in a Pottery Barn email. `/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/` is a 2022 asset reused in a 2026 send.
- **Open-tracking pixel.** `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/c2afd8e4-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...` is present. It is fine in itself, but it has no `alt=""`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** About 55 images have none. They include the full `F26_B12_Bedding_SpotlightOnVelvetQuiltV2/slices_01–15.jpg` hero, the `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg` set, and product images such as `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`. With images off or in a screen reader, the body content is unreadable. Only the logo has alt text (`alt="POTTERY BARN"`).
- **Empty `<title>`.** The tag is `<title></title>`.
- **No `lang` attribute.** `<html xmlns=...>` has none.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Leaked server code.** The second `<style>` block contains PHP-style text: `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store..."); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. It isn't valid CSS, and parsers may drop the rules after it or fail to render them.
- **Fonts.** `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Montserrat...">` is ignored by Gmail and several other clients. No fallback stack is visible in the truncated source.
- **Image-only sections.** The fixed 650px sliced images depend on the `img {max-width: 100%!important}` media-query rules. There is no text fallback.
- **Plain-text part.** 11070 of 15299 characters (72%) are URLs, which is above the 70% warning threshold.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source, and the recipient address is populated in the pixel URLs. The only concern is the exposure of the address in URLs, covered in section 2.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058)** were not found. The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect.
- **`Authentication-Results`** was not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain is `e.potterybarn.com`.
- **Footer content.** The unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated source, so they could not be checked. The footer is image-sliced (`Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`), and none of those slices have alt text. If the unsubscribe and address text are baked into images, they are invisible with images off.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters and landing-page alignment could not be verified. Every CTA goes through `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`, and the HTTP probe skipped those links.
- Product images point directly at `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`, so there is no tracking evidence for them.
- The `coreemail.html?email=` request and the svopn pixel pass the recipient identity to the site's domain.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to all content slices, with `alt=""` on spacers and tracking pixels. Make sure the footer's unsubscribe and address text exists as live HTML text.
2. Serve the three `http://` assets over HTTPS. Stop putting the recipient address in pixel and image URLs. Use an opaque ID instead.
3. Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP text from the `<style>` block.
4. Set a non-empty `<title>` and `lang="en"`, and remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Replace the West Elm spacer and the 2022 banner with current Pottery Barn assets.
6. Re-run QA with the raw message headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
7. Follow the tracking redirects with a full-source capture to verify UTM parameters and landing-page alignment.
