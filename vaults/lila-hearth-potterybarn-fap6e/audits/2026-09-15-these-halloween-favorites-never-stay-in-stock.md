# These Halloween favorites never stay in stock
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's Halloween seasonal push — "Selling Scary Fast" / "The Halloween Shop" — and it's the first genuinely holiday-themed send in the recent brand history after a run of dining, seating, and card-signup emails. The ghost-lantern porch hero is atmospheric and on-brand, and the urgency angle ("never stay in stock," "gone for the season") is a real, credible hook for decor shoppers who've been burned by sellouts before.
- Where it falls apart is depth and focus: the email is a long, undifferentiated scroll of Halloween decor tiles with no single hero product, followed by a completely unrelated "Recommended Just For You" furniture block, a Design Crew pitch, an app download pitch, and a credit-card pitch — four separate asks bolted onto one seasonal email. It reads like several campaigns stitched together rather than one focused Halloween moment.

## What's working

- The ghost-lantern hero image is genuinely eye-catching and clearly signals "Halloween" at a glance.
- "Selling Scary Fast" is a strong, on-theme urgency headline that ties directly to the scarcity message.
- "(Trick or) treat yourself to these favorites. But act fast, once they vanish, they're gone for the season" is well-written, brand-voiced copy.
- Clear "SHOP ALL HALLOWEEN" CTA button appears mid-email with good contrast.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text is legible, layout holds together.

## What's weak

- No single hero product or price point — it's a wall of decor thumbnails (skeletons, candlesticks, bar cart, trays, cocktail napkins) with no anchor item to click toward.
- Zero pricing or discount anywhere in the visible Halloween section — pure browse appeal, no offer.
- The email overstays its welcome: after the Halloween shop, it pivots into unrelated furniture ("Recommended Just For You"), then Design Crew, then app download, then credit card — four bolt-on pitches dilute the Halloween focus.
- "Halloween Headquarters" guide module and "RECOMMENDED JUST FOR YOU" section fight each other for attention right after the primary CTA, undercutting the one clear action the email built toward.
- The scarcity claim ("Selling Scary Fast") isn't backed by any stock indicator, countdown, or specific item callout — it's an assertion, not a visible signal.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after "SHOP ALL HALLOWEEN" — drop the furniture recommendations, Design Crew, and app-download blocks from this send; they belong in separate emails and are actively pulling focus from the seasonal urgency angle.
- 2. Add a price or "starting at $X" on at least one hero decor item so the scarcity message has a concrete reason to click now.
- 3. Feature one specific best-selling item above the fold (e.g., "the ghost lanterns everyone's buying") instead of an undifferentiated grid — give the eye one landing spot.
- 4. Add a stock-status cue near the top ("Back in stock — won't last") to make "Selling Scary Fast" feel earned rather than generic urgency copy.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Back in stock: the Halloween decor that sold out last year`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Our best-selling Halloween pieces are already going fast`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Skeletons, lanterns & bar accessories — most sell out by October`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop before your favorites are gone for the season`
