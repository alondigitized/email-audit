# ON NOW✨ The Warehouse Sale
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's most sale-forward, offer-clean send in the recent stretch: "Up to 60% off" is stated once, huge, above the fold, with a single "SHOP NOW" button and a clean category grid (Furniture, Sofas & Chairs, Bedding, Pillows & Throws, Decor, Tabletop, Lighting, Rugs, Outdoor, Bath). No render bugs, no clutter — it's a textbook clearance email and reads faster than the 08-04 "Online SALE exclusive" send that had to compete with a lookbook narrative.
- Where it loses points is length and focus creep after the fold. Past the category grid it stacks a "Recommended Just For You" product grid, a New Arrivals banner, a full Halloween Shop banner, app-download promo, credit card promo, and Affirm promo — five distinct asks after the sale pitch already landed. For a "Warehouse Sale" email, that's a lot of real estate handed to things that aren't the warehouse sale.
- Subject line "ON NOW✨ The Warehouse Sale" is solid and on-brand for urgency, but the emoji plus ALL CAPS "ON NOW" nudges toward a soft spam signal, and there's no visible percentage in the subject even though 60% off is the hero visual — a missed opportunity to pull that number forward.

## What's working

- Hero is unambiguous: "THE WAREHOUSE SALE / Up to 60% off" with one SHOP NOW button, no competing copy.
- Category grid (10 tiles) lets a shopper self-select their room/category before clicking, reducing bounce to an irrelevant landing page.
- Clean product-recommendation grid with real (non-lorem) named products and prices implied by category (bed, curtain rod, wool rug, duvet, towel) — feels personalized even if generic.
- No overlapping text, broken images, or placeholder content anywhere in the render.
- "SHOP ALL SALE" reinforces the primary CTA a second time before the eye drifts to secondary content.

## What's weak

- No actual discount percentage or dollar figure shown for any individual product — "up to 60%" is the only number, and "up to" language is the classic bait-and-switch flag from the rubric.
- Email tries to do five jobs at once (sale, new arrivals, Halloween shop, credit card, app, Affirm) — this dilutes the one job it should do, which is move the warehouse sale.
- The Halloween Shop banner right after New Arrivals is a seasonal non-sequitur sitting inside a clearance email — it doesn't reference savings and breaks the sale narrative.
- No urgency/deadline anywhere in the body (no "ends Sunday," no countdown) despite "ON NOW" implying time-sensitivity in the subject.
- No loyalty/rewards-member pricing called out near the hero, even though a credit card rewards block appears later — those two elements aren't connected.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after "SHOP ALL SALE" or immediately follow it with a *sale-specific* recommended grid (showing struck-through original price vs. sale price) instead of a generic "Recommended Just For You" grid — right now nothing in the body proves the 60% claim with a real number.
- 2. Remove or relocate the Halloween Shop banner — it doesn't belong inside a clearance-sale email and it visually competes with the sale for attention.
- 3. Add a real deadline under the hero ("Ends Sunday 8/9" or similar) to convert "ON NOW" urgency into something actionable.
- 4. Move the credit-card 10% rewards mention up near the hero as a stacking offer ("+10% back with your PB card") — that's a stronger click driver than burying it near the footer.
- 5. Tighten subject/preheader to lead with the number since that's the actual hero asset.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 60% off: The Warehouse Sale is on now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Warehouse Sale: furniture up to 60% off, ends soon`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sofas, rugs, bedding & more — before it's gone`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop 10 categories, all marked down for the warehouse sale`

Three code-level issues stand out: leftover PHP `header()` calls embedded in a `<style>` block, four assets/pixels served over plain HTTP (one leaking the recipient's email address in cleartext), and missing `alt` text on nearly every image.

- 56 tracking/redirect links (`click.e.potterybarn.com`, `clickcp.potterybarn.com`, etc.) were skipped by the automated HTTP probe — validity/destination not confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- `<title></title>` is empty — no inbox tab/preview title.
- Four images load over `http://` instead of `https://` (see §2 and §7), risking mixed-content blocking in HTTPS-only mail clients.
- MSO conditional comments (`<!--[if gte mso 9]>`, `<!--[if mso 16]>`) and Gmail/Apple resets (`.ExternalClass`, `a[x-apple-data-detectors]`) are present and correctly structured — no issues found here.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers not detected — QA tooling itself notes this may be a capture gap in the AgentMail relay rather than a confirmed sender-side omission; recommend verifying at the source MTA.

- The truncated HTML provided does not include the footer/unsubscribe block, so CAN-SPAM body requirements (physical address, visible unsubscribe link) cannot be verified from this excerpt.
- Separately: transmitting the recipient's email address over unencrypted HTTP (`svopn.potterybarn.com` pixel, §2) is a PII-handling concern worth flagging alongside compliance review.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

1. Remove the stray `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ..."); header("Pragma: no-cache");` PHP calls sitting inside the `<style>` block in `<head>` — inert as CSS today, but evidence of server-side template code leaking into compiled output; fix at the build/compile step.
2. Add `alt` text to all 39 flagged images, prioritizing product/CTA images.
3. Move the four HTTP resources to HTTPS — especially `svopn.potterybarn.com`, which currently exposes the recipient's email address in a cleartext query string.
4. Retire the stale 2022-dated asset (`edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`) and confirm the cross-brand `edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` reference is intentional.
5. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` directly at the sending MTA rather than relying on relay capture, since QA flagged these as relay-side unknowns.
6. Confirm the `d.turn.com` third-party pixel's data use is covered by current privacy disclosures.
