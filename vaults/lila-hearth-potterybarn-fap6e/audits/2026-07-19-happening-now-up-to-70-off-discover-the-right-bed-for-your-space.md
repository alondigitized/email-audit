# Happening now: Up to 70% off + Discover the right bed for your space
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-19**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's second email of the day on July 19 — the morning's "Don't miss our SUMMER SALE" blast already went out, and this one follows the now-worn compound formula: discount hook + editorial category angle. The editorial pivot here — "Discover the right bed for your space" — is more consultative than a straight sale push, and the body execution reflects that: Wood Beds and Upholstered Beds get their own category blocks with dedicated hero shots and Shop Now buttons, a "Recommended Just for You" personalized grid appears mid-email, and "Brand New Arrivals" rounds out the bottom. The bed-finder framing is a reasonable creative direction.
- The problem is the setup. This subject line is 68 characters long and is the third time this week the "Up to 70% off" language has led. The "Happening now" urgency opener is hollow without a visible deadline. By the time a subscriber hits this email, they've already seen the same offer framing from PB multiple times today and across the prior week. The cumulative effect of this cadence undermines what is, at body level, a reasonably well-structured category send.
- Judgment: the bones are good — the personalization signal and category segmentation are real value — but the send timing and subject line are self-defeating.

## What's working

- **Category-split layout** (Wood Beds / Upholstered Beds) gives the subscriber a clear decision frame rather than a generic "shop beds" dump.
- **Hero photography** is consistent with Pottery Barn's aspirational-neutral aesthetic — clean, white, uncluttered bedroom shots that let the product breathe.
- **"Recommended Just for You" module** is a meaningful personalization signal mid-email; it elevates this above a pure blast.
- **Clean render** — no broken images, no layout collapse, no overlapping text visible anywhere in the frame.

## What's weak

- **Double-send fatigue**: this is the second PB email today, arriving after "Don't miss our SUMMER SALE" scored 8/10 this same morning. Subscriber patience for a second inbox hit on the same day is low.
- **Subject line is too long at ~68 characters** — it will truncate on most mobile clients before reaching "Discover the right bed for your space," which is the more differentiated half.
- **"Happening now" urgency is hollow** — no visible end date, no countdown, no "sale ends Sunday" anchor. The urgency signal doesn't close the loop.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA copy is generic** — the category-specific framing (Wood Beds, Upholstered Beds) is doing the specificity work, but the button copy itself doesn't reflect it.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Skip this send entirely or merge with the morning blast.** Two emails on one day from the same brand with the same "70% off" lead erodes list health. The bed-finder angle could have been the morning's editorial hook instead of a standalone follow-up.
- 2. **Put the consultative angle in the subject line and drop the discount lead.** "Which bed is right for your space?" is more clickable than the 10th "Up to 70% off" subject line this month.
- 3. **Add a visible deadline.** Even "Sale ends Sunday" in the hero or CTA context converts better than "Happening now" with no anchor.
- 4. **Upgrade CTA copy** from "SHOP NOW" to something category-specific: "Shop Wood Beds" / "Shop Upholstered Beds" — the category blocks already set this up, the buttons don't follow through.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Which bed is right for your space? (Up to 70% off)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Wood beds, upholstered beds: sale ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Find your match — four styles, every size, all on sale now.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The right bed changes a room. Shop wood, upholstered, and canopy — up to 70% off.`

## Technical Audit — Pottery Barn Email (2026-07-19)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Two broken links will cause click failures for credit card CTA and cross-brand tracking pixels. The email also has widespread missing alt text (50+ images), three HTTP image sources that will be blocked by modern clients, and unconfirmed SPF/DKIM authentication status via the relay.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**BROKEN — Hard failures:**

- **Credit card apply CTA** returns HTTP 403:
  `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/c/da01b407-b21c-4618-a71b-f83bef0dd682?brandCode=POTTERYBARNCB&...`
  This is the `PB_header_nonmember` / "Apply and Buy" banner link. Any click on this CTA results in a dead page.

- **West Elm spacer pixel** returns HTTP 403:
  `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`
  Cross-brand tracking pixel is also HTTP (not HTTPS) — double failure.

**Skipped (unverified):** 22 tracking/click-redirect links through `click.e.potterybarn.com` were not probed. These should be spot-checked in staging before send.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**HTTP image sources — will be blocked by Gmail, Apple Mail, and Outlook with image security settings:**

| Image | URL |
|---|---|
| `spacer_0.gif` | `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` |
| `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` | `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` |
| `slices_02.jpg` (footer) | `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` |
| Open-pixel | `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&...` |

**Missing alt text:** 50+ images have no `alt` attribute, including every content slice in the "Find Your Perfect Bed" section (`slices_01.jpg` through `slices_16.jpg`), all footer slices, and product images. Images-off rendering will show a completely blank email for screen-reader users and clients that block images by default.

**Inline PHP headers in `<style>` block:** The `<style>` tag in `<head>` contains literal PHP header calls:
```
header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache...");
header("Pragma: no-cache");
```
These are dead strings inside CSS — they do nothing — but indicate a template rendering error where server-side code was not executed and leaked raw into the HTML. This is a build/template pipeline bug.

**`<title>` is empty.** Minor but affects some accessibility tools.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unfired merge tokens (`{{`, `%%`, `[%`) visible in the truncated HTML. The persona email address `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is correctly substituted into the open-pixel URL and the `coreemail.html` confirmation link — personalization fired correctly for this recipient.

### 5. Compliance

**List-Unsubscribe header:** Not found via the AgentMail relay. This is likely a relay capture gap rather than a true omission (Pottery Barn's ESP almost certainly sends this header), but it cannot be confirmed from available data. **Must be verified against raw SMTP headers** before treating as clean.

**List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Not found. Without this, Gmail's one-click unsubscribe button will not appear — Gmail shows the header-based one-click button only when both `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` are present.

**CAN-SPAM:** An unsubscribe mechanism and physical address appear to be present in the footer (inferred from standard Pottery Barn templates; not contradicted by the HTML excerpt). No explicit violation confirmed.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**UTM parameters:** The 22 tracked click links route through `click.e.potterybarn.com` (Salesforce Marketing Cloud click-tracking). UTM decoration on final destination URLs cannot be verified without resolving the redirect chain — skipped by QA. This should be manually validated for at least the primary CTAs (sale hero, bed finder, product tiles).

**Plain-text version is URL-heavy:** 10,470 of 14,622 characters (71.6%) are URLs. This is a spam signal for plain-text filtering and indicates the plain-text version is auto-generated from HTML without cleanup rather than human-authored.

**Cross-brand asset bleed:** A West Elm domain (`edm.westelm.com`) is used for a Pottery Barn spacer image. This is a template hygiene issue — the asset should be hosted on `edm.potterybarn.com`.
