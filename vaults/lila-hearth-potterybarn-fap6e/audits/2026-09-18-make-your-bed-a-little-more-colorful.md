# Make your bed a little more colorful
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-18**
## Executive summary

- This is a straightforward color-and-material bedding push — "Comfort Meets Color" hero into a genuinely useful "shop by color" grid (greens, browns, blues, whites) that lets a shopper self-select by the one thing that actually varies in bedding: hue. It's a clean, well-organized send with real texture photography and a sensible "ships free" hook up top.
- The problem is length and focus. This is one of the longest, most scroll-heavy Pottery Barn sends in the recent history — quilts, then Print & Pattern Bedding, then a full recommended-for-you grid spanning curtain rods, dining tables, rugs, and towels, then New Arrivals, then Halloween, then the loyalty card, then Affirm. By the time a reader reaches "Recommended Just for You" they've left bedding entirely. The "Warehouse Sale up to 60% off" banner at the very top also promises a discount that never shows up again in the body — the whole rest of the email is full-price color/pattern browsing, which is the same bait-and-switch pattern flagged in the 2026-09-17 "Warehouse deals... NEW catalog" send.
- Solid creative, undermined by scope creep and a top banner that doesn't pay off. Score in the middle of the pack.

## What's working

- The color-grid concept (shop greens / browns / blues / whites) is a clean, useful organizing principle unique to this send vs. recent history.
- "It all ships free!" is a concrete, visible incentive right under the hero.
- Product photography is consistent, warm, and well-lit — no render bugs, broken images, or overlapping text anywhere in the scroll.
- "SHOP QUILTS" / "SHOP ALL BEDDING" CTAs are clear, high-contrast buttons.

## What's weak

- Top banner promises "Up to 60% off" but nothing in the visible body offers a discount — pure bait, same pattern already flagged in the 2026-09-17 catalog send.
- The email sprawls well past bedding: curtain rods, a dining table, a rug, a towel, a swivel chair, New Arrivals, and a full Halloween shop all appear before the footer — no single clear "primary" CTA once you're past the fold.
- "MORE FOR YOUR BEST NIGHT'S SLEEP" is a plain text table (Duvets/Sheets/Comforter/Pillows/etc.) with zero visual treatment — it reads like a placeholder nav block, not real content.
- Print & Pattern Bedding section repeats the same "shop bedding" idea as the color grid just above it, adding length without adding a new decision point.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email roughly in half — end after "SHOP ALL BEDDING" or the Print & Pattern block, and move curtain rods/dining table/rug/chair/Halloween into a separate send. A focused bedding email will out-click a sprawling catalog dump.
- 2. Either put a real, specific discount on quilts/bedding to justify the top banner, or drop the "60% off" banner from this send entirely — right now it sets an expectation the body doesn't meet.
- 3. Give the Duvets/Sheets/Comforter/Pillows table real visual weight (icons or thumbnails) or cut it — as plain text it looks unfinished next to the photography around it.
- 4. Move "Recommended Just for You" to its own email or at minimum filter it to bedding-adjacent items only (drop the dining table and curtain rod).

- **Preheader Alt A:** `New colorways just landed across quilts, duvets, and shams`
