# Your next great find is ON SALE
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-29**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn's third email landing on May 29 — and it reads like the weakest of the three. The hero is visually warm (a cognac leather armchair in a bright room), the sale has a real deadline ("through Sunday"), and the "Recommended Just For You" personalization module is the one genuine differentiator from the May 28 sitewide sale email. But the subject does the offer no favors: "Your next great find is ON SALE" is aspirational vagueness when the May 28 equivalent led with "UP TO 50% OFF" in the subject line itself. No specific discount percentage appears anywhere in the hero — you have to click to find out how much off, which is a conversion killer.
- The Free Design Services module below the fold is now appearing for the third time in three days. What was a smart editorial pivot on May 27 has become wallpaper. The category tile grid (Furniture, Tabletop, Bedding, Bath, Outdoor, Pillows & Decor) is clean and navigable, but it's the same modular playbook recycled across every sale email in the brand history.
- Judgment: competent execution of a mid-tier template, undermined by a subject line that refuses to commit to a number and a hero that lets the offer breathe too much oxygen.

## What's working

- The cognac leather armchair hero is genuinely eye-catching — warm, aspirational, seasonally right for late spring/early summer.
- "SALE NOW THROUGH SUNDAY" is a credible, specific deadline — one of the few concrete details in the email.
- The "Recommended Just For You" personalization block is the best differentiation this send has; it treats the subscriber as an individual rather than a broadcast target.
- The category tile grid gives multiple entry points without being chaotic — any of six categories gets a click path.
- No render bugs: clean layout, images load, nothing broken.

## What's weak

- No discount percentage anywhere in the hero — "SALE" alone is the weakest possible discount call-to-action, especially compared to the May 28 "UP TO 50% OFF" email that hit the same list.
- Subject line "Your next great find is ON SALE" is soft and non-specific — zero category signal, zero percentage, zero urgency in the subject itself.
- The Free Design Services module is a direct copy-paste of the May 27 and May 29 "Expert design services" sends — three appearances in 48 hours turns a value-add into noise.
- Loyalty/rewards visibility is buried in the footer in small type — "Earn 8% Back in Rewards" deserves to be in or near the hero for a sale email.
- Seven Pottery Barn emails since May 27 is unsustainable cadence; this send arrives fatigued.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with a number in the hero.** Rewrite the hero banner to "UP TO X% OFF — THROUGH SUNDAY" in large type. The cognac armchair is great context; it needs a price-reduction headline to close.
- 2. **Put the discount in the subject.** The current subject is evasive. Any A/B with a specific number will win.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 40% off furniture, decor & more — ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The sale ends Sunday — your finds are waiting`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Furniture, bedding, outdoor and more — shop your personalized picks now`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Ends Sunday: save on the pieces you've been watching`
- 3. **Retire the Free Design Services module for at least a week.** Replace it with a "Top-rated picks in the sale" or "Members-only early access" block to reinvigorate the scroll-down section.
- 4. **Surface the 8% rewards benefit near the hero**, not the footer — especially for a sale email where incremental savings tip the decision.
- 5. **Reduce send cadence.** Three emails on May 29 alone undermines all three. Even holding this send for May 30 — a gap day — would lift open rates by reducing fatigue.
