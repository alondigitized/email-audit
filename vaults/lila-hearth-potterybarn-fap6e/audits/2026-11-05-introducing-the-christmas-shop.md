# Introducing The Christmas Shop 🎄✨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand launch email for The Christmas Shop. It has no discount, and it's built to inspire. The hero ("now open / THE CHRISTMAS SHOP" over a decorated tree, with a "DECK THE HALLS" button) sets the tone, and four category tiles follow: stockings, bedding, tablesetting and pillows. Each tile has the same small "SHOP NOW" link, and a "SHOP HOLIDAY NEW ARRIVALS" button closes the section.
- The weakness is that it gives no reason to act today. It's the third no-promo, editorial holiday send in about 36 hours. Nov 4 had "New arrivals with timeless holiday charm" and "Now open: The Holiday Botanical Shop", and Nov 5 also had "Spotlight on: West Elm Kids". "Introducing" also oversells it, because the earlier Christmas Shop and Botanical sends already announced this territory. The "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks" bar is the only practical hook, and it's easy to miss at the top.
- Verdict: pretty and well executed, but low on urgency and repeated from recent sends. It reads as a brand beat, not a driver of purchases.

## What's working

- The hero is strong and seasonal. The tree and mantel image with a centered "DECK THE HALLS" button makes the entry point obvious.
- Visual hierarchy is clean. Four full-width tiles with italic script captions have consistent spacing and no layout breakage.
- The tiles span several categories (stockings, bedding, tablesetting, pillows), so the send serves different shoppers.
- The "10% Back in Rewards" credit-card block and the Affirm block add real purchase incentives lower down.
- The "Recommended Just For You" grid gives concrete products with sizes, such as the dining tables with "(60"-125")".

## What's weak

- No offer appears above the fold. The only price-adjacent message is the small "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks" line, and the rewards perk is buried near the bottom.
- The "SHOP NOW" links under each tile are tiny, low-contrast text, so the tiles lean on the image alone to drive clicks.
- There's no deadline or scarcity cue. "Now open" gives nothing to act on this week.
- The legal and fine-print block at the bottom is dense and long, and the footer is heavy for a no-offer email.
- The cadence is repetitive. This is the third near-identical editorial holiday send in two days.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one concrete reason to act in the hero. Examples are a time-bound perk, such as free shipping on Christmas Shop orders through a date, or a reminder of the 1-4 week delivery window and the order-by date for Christmas delivery.
- 2. Make the tile CTAs real buttons or bolder links, and name the product, for example "Shop stockings".
- 3. Add a member or rewards line near the top, such as "Earn 10% back with the Pottery Barn card", instead of leaving it near the footer.
- 4. Space out the editorial sends and give each a distinct angle, such as gifting, hosting or a guest room. Don't repeat the "new arrivals" framing.
- 5. Shorten the footer and legal text where possible.
- **Subject Alt A:** `The Christmas Shop is open: stockings to tablesetting 🎄`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Order by Dec 10 for Christmas delivery`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stockings, bedding, tabletop and more, with in-stock pieces delivered in 1-4 weeks.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Earn 10% back in rewards on holiday decor with the Pottery Barn card.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML layout built from sliced images, and its click links go through an opaque ESP redirect domain. The confirmed code-level problems are widespread missing alt text, three `http://` image sources, an empty `<title>`, and stray PHP text inside a `<style>` block. The compliance and authentication checks came back inconclusive because the headers weren't captured, so they don't show a defect. The QA pass rate is 57% (0 failures, 3 warnings).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque click tracking:** 47 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they use tracking or redirect domains. Example: `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7ImQiOjUwNTEs...` on the logo. Destination validity, redirect chain and final status for those 47 links are **unverified**.
- **Recipient email in tracking-pixel URLs:** the plaintext recipient address appears in query strings:
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`

  The address is unhashed and travels in a URL, so it can end up in proxy and server logs. The svopn pixel is also over HTTP.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEv.../cat/1` is an ad-platform beacon with no alt text.
- **Open pixel:** `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/a7e3d06d-5c6a-4450-81e6-3370981ea48a?mi_cid=...` is likely the ESP open pixel and is flagged for missing alt.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 40 images are flagged. This covers the main creative (`F26_Seasonal_B10_XmasShop/slices_01–06.jpg`), all product images (`classic-velvet-stockings-l.jpg`, `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`, and others), and `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`. Because the content is sliced images, the email is unreadable with images blocked. Screen readers will announce filenames. The logo image does have `alt="POTTERY BARN"`.
- **Non-HTTPS images (3):** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`, and the `svopn` pixel. These risk mixed-content blocking or stripping.
- **Empty `<title></title>`:** this is an accessibility and metadata defect.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Stray server-side code in CSS:** the second `<style>` block contains literal text, `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store..."); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. This is a template leak. It is invalid CSS and may break parsing of rules that follow it in some clients.
- **Cross-brand and legacy assets:** a spacer is hosted on `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`, a different brand's domain. A 2022 banner is also still referenced.
- **Layout tables:** `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0">` spacer tables have no `role="presentation"`. Other tables do.
- **Web font:** the Google Fonts `<link>` for Montserrat is ignored by many clients. This is minor and has no fallback issue by itself.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, or `alt=""` on decorative images and spacers. Prioritize `F26_Seasonal_B10_XmasShop/slices_01–06.jpg` and the product images.
