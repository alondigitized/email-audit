# 🔔 UP TO 70% OFF + Which bedding hue speaks to you?
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-26**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn stacking its two most-used levers into one send: the "UP TO 70% OFF" blast and the color-led editorial format the brand has run well before (the July 25 "well-styled table" guide, the July 24 "1600+ new arrivals" lookbook). Structurally it's the strongest hybrid of the two moves so far — the "Which hue speaks to you?" quiz framing gives the 8-swatch grid (Blue, Rosewood, Green, Neutral) a real organizing principle instead of just being another discount wall, and "It all ships free!" is a clean, low-friction hook sitting right under the question.
- Where it stumbles is the same place the July 24 "Don't miss UP TO 70% OFF + NEW designs" send stumbled (5.00/10 in that history): it's trying to be a sale email and a discovery/editorial email at once, and the subject line inherits that split. "UP TO 70% OFF + Which bedding hue speaks to you?" reads like two subject lines mashed together rather than one clear hook.
- Below the fold this becomes a kitchen-sink send — bedding grid, "More Bedding Essentials" nav, "Recommended Just For You," New Arrivals banner, Outdoor sale banner, app promo, credit card promo — which dilutes the color-quiz idea that made the top half strong. Net: a well-executed hero concept buried under too many competing CTAs by the time you scroll halfway.

## What's working

- The "Layers of Color" hero with stacked quilts across the full color range is genuinely appealing and on-brand.
- The color-block grid (Shop Blue, Rosewood, Green, Neutral) turns a generic sale into a browsable, low-effort decision — each swatch is a specific, named product ("Belgian Flax Linen Duvet," "Cozy Cloud Quilt," "Coastal Washed Quilt").
- "It all ships free!" removes a real point of friction right at the moment of highest interest.
- "Recommended Just For You" and the "Earn 10% Back in Rewards" module are visible personalization/loyalty signals.
- Clean render throughout — no broken images, no overlapping text, consistent Pottery Barn branding top to bottom.

## What's weak

- Subject line stacks a sale pitch and a quiz hook into one clunky line, echoing the same two-messages-in-one problem that dragged the July 24 "70% OFF + NEW designs" send down to 5.00/10.
- "Up to 70% off" is a ceiling claim with no anchor product or price shown near it — no specific item, no specific discount tier, just the headline number.
- The email keeps going long after the color-quiz payoff: bedding essentials nav, recommended products, new arrivals banner, and an Outdoor sale banner all compete for the same click by mid-scroll.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere — "Summer Sale" with no end date makes the offer feel evergreen rather than time-sensitive.
- No preview/preheader text was captured for this send, so the inbox line is doing zero work to complement the subject.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split the subject into one idea. Lead with the quiz hook alone (it's the more original, higher-curiosity asset) and let the discount live in the preheader instead of fighting for subject-line space.
- 2. Cut the email after "Shop Bedding By Color" + "More Bedding Essentials." Everything from "Recommended Just For You" down (New Arrivals, Outdoor, app promo, credit card) should be its own send — this is three emails glued together.
- 3. Anchor the "up to 70%" claim to one real number near the hero (e.g., "Quilts from $49") so it's not just a bare ceiling stat.
- 4. Add a soft deadline ("Sale ends Sunday") to convert the browsing-friendly quiz format into something with a reason to act today.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Which bedding color are you? Take a peek 👀`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Free shipping + up to 70% off: shop bedding by color`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Blue, rosewood, green, or neutral — find your palette, all ships free.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New colorways just dropped across quilts, duvets & sheets.`

# Technical Audit — Pottery Barn "UP TO 70% OFF" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard multi-table HTML email built on the "MI" ESP template framework with MSO/Outlook conditionals, image-heavy layout, and third-party open/click tracking. Core structure is sound, but alt text is absent across nearly all images, several assets load over plain HTTP, and a block of raw PHP `header()` calls is embedded inside a `<style>` tag — a leftover server-side snippet that has no effect but signals a templating error.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 61 tracking/redirect links (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`, `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/...`) were skipped by automated HTTP probing since they resolve through opaque redirect tokens — destination URLs cannot be verified from source alone.
- Open-tracking pixel served over **plain HTTP**: `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` — mixed-content pixel, may be blocked by mail clients that enforce HTTPS-only image loading, silently undercounting opens.
- Secondary tracker present: `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` (Turn/Amobee ad-retargeting pixel) — third-party audience-tracking beacon outside PB's own domain.
- Plain-text alternative is **72.6% URLs** (11,016 of 15,185 chars) — a strong spam-filter signal at major providers; typically driven by unshortened/verbose redirect tokens repeated for every product link.
- Header link and CTA link both route through the same `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirect chain — consistent, no dead/malformed href syntax observed in the visible source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **~40 images missing `alt` text**, spanning hero banners, all 19 "BeddingColorStory1" color-swatch slices, product shots (curtain rod, towel, curtain, beds, duvet), footer slice images, and tracking pixels. Screen readers and images-blocked inboxes (default in Outlook/many corporate clients) will render this email as a wall of blank space with no fallback copy.
- **3 images load over `http://` instead of `https://`:**
  - `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` — note this is a **West Elm domain referenced inside a Pottery Barn email**, presumably shared Williams-Sonoma Inc. infrastructure for a spacer gif; still non-HTTPS and cross-brand, worth confirming intentional.
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` (also a stale 2022-dated asset path, suggesting an outdated shared footer/banner module)
  
  Non-HTTPS images risk being blocked outright by clients that enforce mixed-content policies (notably iOS Mail, Gmail proxy in some configurations), causing broken layout.
- Markup includes legitimate MSO conditional comments (`<!--[if gte mso 9]>`, `<!--[if mso 16]>`) and Apple/Outlook-specific resets (`x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, `.ExternalClass`, `.ReadMsgBody`) — standard and correctly applied.
- **Invalid CSS / leaked server code:** inside the second `<style type="text/css">` block, alongside `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` rules, there are four literal PHP `header()` calls:
  ```
  header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
  header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
  header("Cache-Control: post-check=0, pre-check=0", false);
  header("Pragma: no-cache");
  ```
  These are PHP statements, not valid CSS — they'll be ignored/no-op by mail client CSS parsers but indicate a server-side snippet (likely meant to set cache headers on a dynamically-generated tracking image) was pasted into the client-facing template by mistake. Cosmetically harmless but a build-process defect worth flagging to the ESP/dev team.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- Recipient identifier is consistently populated and correctly merged across tracking/service links: `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` appears in `coreemail.html?email=...`, the `sv_sveme` open-tracking param, and is implied via `mi_mid=019fa003-5200-7000-b2a4-ac18da47ee74` on the click-tracking domain. ## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not present** in the captured headers — QA flags this may be an AgentMail relay capture limitation rather than a true absence; cannot be confirmed as a sender-side omission from this data alone.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not present** — one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) cannot be confirmed as supported.
- **Authentication-Results header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown from this capture; cannot confirm authentication posture.
- Body-level unsubscribe link/footer could not be evaluated — the provided HTML source is **truncated before the footer/CC_Banner section**, so physical-address and unsubscribe-link presence (required by CAN-SPAM) is not verifiable from this excerpt.
- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` are present — standard practice to prevent tracking-link leakage via referrer headers, not a compliance issue.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All product and CTA links route through the `click.e.potterybarn.com` / `clickcp.potterybarn.com` redirect layer rather than direct UTM-tagged URLs to potterybarn.com — actual landing-page UTM parameters are only visible after redirect resolution, which was not performed (61 links skipped HTTP probing per QA).
- One direct (non-redirected) link to the main site was observed: `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=...` — a service/support link, not campaign-tagged.
- Cannot confirm UTM-to-landing-page alignment (e.g., `utm_campaign`, `utm_source` matching the site's expected campaign tracking) without following the opaque redirect chain — recommend a follow-up pass that resolves the `click.e.potterybarn.com` tokens to their final destinations.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all ~40 images, prioritizing the CTA/hero banners and product shots (color-swatch slices and spacers can use `alt=""` if purely decorative).
2. Migrate the 3 remaining `http://` image sources (`edm.westelm.com`, `edm.potterybarn.com/thekey`, `2022/...slices_02.jpg`) to HTTPS, including the non-HTTPS open-tracking pixel.
3. Remove the leaked PHP `header()` block from the `<style>` tag — flag to the ESP/template team as a build-pipeline defect (likely a copy-paste from a server-side image script).
4. Confirm with the ESP/relay provider whether `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` headers are genuinely absent or simply not captured by the AgentMail relay — one-click unsubscribe is increasingly enforced by Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender rules.
5. Investigate the plain-text part's 72.6% URL density — consolidate/shorten redirect tokens or trim redundant repeated links to reduce spam-filter risk.
6. Resolve a sample of the 61 tracking redirects to confirm final landing-page UTM parameters match campaign expectations.
