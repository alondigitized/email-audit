# Don't miss The Fall Sale (UP TO 70% OFF)
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a catch-all Fall Sale blast, and it reads that way. A dark-green box carries "The Fall Sale / Up to 70% off / Plus free shipping on 1000s of items" with a SHOP NOW button. Below it is a ten-tile category grid: Furniture, Sofas & Chairs, Bedding, Pillows & Throws, Decor, Tabletop, Lighting, Rugs, Outdoor, Bath, plus Shop All Sale. The offer is clear and the hierarchy is clean. Nothing in it is new, though.
- The subject, "Don't miss The Fall Sale," is the same pitch as the last several days. The 10/11 "Up to 70% off & going fast" send was already described as the fifth or sixth Fall Sale email in three days. The 10/09 through 10/11 category sends were at least specific, with lamps, bedding, botanicals and outdoor each getting a send. This one drops back to the generic banner.
- The "Recommended just for you" block is the only personalization. Six products appear with names but no prices, no sale callouts and no ratings. A sale email that never shows a sale price undercuts its own headline. The Christmas Shop and Design Services banners below pull attention away from the sale. The footer is a wall of legal text. Judgement: competent but forgettable, and a middling fourth or fifth repeat.

## What's working

- The hero is unambiguous: "Up to 70% off" in large serif type, free shipping noted, and a clear SHOP NOW button.
- The category grid gives fast, scannable routes into the sale, with Bedding, Lighting and Outdoor matching the recent sends.
- The "Recommended just for you" grid has real product imagery with named items, which signals personalization.
- The Christmas Shop banner is seasonally timely and visually strong, with the red background and tree.
- The brand look is consistent: dark green, serif type, and a clean layout with no visible render bugs.

## What's weak

- No product in the recommendations shows a price, a discount or a "was/now" figure, so the 70% claim is never proven.
- There is no deadline anywhere. "Don't miss" implies urgency, but the body has no end date or countdown.
- The subject repeats a message subscribers have seen almost daily, with no new angle.
- The Christmas Shop, Design Services, app, credit card and Affirm modules compete with the sale after the product grid. The email has no single focus.
- The fine print in the footer is very long and dense, and "up to 70%" gives no sense of typical savings.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add sale prices (was/now) and a "% off" tag to every recommended product. That alone would lift click-through the most.
- 2. Add a real deadline to the hero, such as "Ends Sunday," so the urgency in the subject is credible.
- 3. Make the hero more specific by featuring one best-seller with its actual discount instead of a generic banner.
- 4. Cut the footer promotional modules (credit card, Affirm, app) from sale sends, or move them below a clear break.
- 5. Vary the cadence. If this is the 5th send in 4 days, pull back and let category emails carry the message.

# Technical Audit: Pottery Barn "Don't miss The Fall Sale (UP TO 70% OFF)"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML has no hard failures (QA: 0 issues, 4 warnings). It has three real code-level problems: widespread missing `alt` attributes, plaintext HTTP tracking pixels that carry the recipient's email address, and server-side code left inside a `<style>` block. Header-level compliance and authentication could not be verified from the captured data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link destinations unverified.** 55 links on `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` were skipped as tracking redirects, so none were probed. Landing URLs, redirect chains and 404s are unconfirmed.
- **Recipient email in tracking URLs.**
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` sends the address in cleartext over HTTP.
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is loaded as an `<img>` and also carries the address in its query string.
- **Third-party pixel.** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` is an ad-network pixel with an unusual file-less path. The QA report listed it under "missing alt" with `alt="1"`.
- **Open pixel with no alt.** `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/e7698a46-971e-4549-bd4c-a1cf208e37d4?mi_cid=...` is an open-tracking pixel.
- **Redundant tracking.** Three separate open or tracking pixels (svopn, turn.com, clickcp) fire per open. Each is an extra request and a chance for a blocked or failed load.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt`: about 50 images.**
  - Sliced banners: `1012_MON_AM_FallSale_LinkFarm/slices_01–12.jpg`, `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg` and `F26_Top_NewArrivals+InStock/slices_01–02.jpg`.

  - Banners: `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg` and `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`.
  - With images blocked, the linked slices and product images have no accessible name. Spacers and pixels should carry `alt=""`.
- **Insecure image sources.** Three images load over `http://`:
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - the `svopn` pixel

  These can be blocked as mixed content or proxied. The 2022 asset path also suggests stale module reuse.
- **Server-side code in CSS.** The last `<style>` block ends with `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store, ...");`. This is invalid CSS from a leaked PHP snippet. Parsers drop it, but it is malformed and exposes backend code.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Zoom blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="..., maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom, an accessibility issue.
- **Dead mobile selectors (visible portion only).** The mobile rules `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` have no matching elements in the visible source. The visible tables use `class="contenttable"`. The source is truncated, so this is unconfirmed.
- **Table roles.** The spacer table (`<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><tr><td height="20">`) and the outer layout tables lack `role="presentation"`.
- **Web font.** The `fonts.googleapis.com` Montserrat `<link>` is stripped by Gmail and Outlook and gives no benefit there.
- **Plain-text part.** About 72% of it is URLs (10680 of 14866 characters), so it is nearly unreadable.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source. The recipient address is resolved correctly in the pixel URLs, though see the privacy concern in section 2. The source is truncated, so tokens in the body or footer can't be ruled out.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so treat this as unconfirmed rather than a sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both.
- **`Authentication-Results` not captured.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `e.potterybarn.com` matches the link and image subdomains (`click.e`, `image.e`), which is consistent but not proof.
- **Footer not verifiable.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated source. This is not flagged as missing.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs unverifiable.** Every CTA is an opaque `qs=` redirect, and the probe was skipped, so UTM parameters and the final landing URL can't be confirmed. The redirect may append them server-side.
- **Product links.** The product images on `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...` are hot-linked from the main site. The product page targets behind the redirects were not checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` text to all content images: product names for the product tiles, the offer text for the slices. Use `alt=""` on spacers and pixels.
2. Move the `svopn` pixel and the two `http://edm.potterybarn.com` images to HTTPS.
3. Stop putting the raw email address in the `svopn` and `coreemail.html` URLs. Use an opaque subscriber ID instead.
4. Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP lines from the `<style>` block.
5. Set a real `<title>`, and drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
6. Add `role="presentation"` to the layout tables, and remove or fix the unused `wrapper` and `photo` selectors.
7. Check the raw `.eml` headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, to separate relay stripping from a real sender gap.
8. Run a manual follow-up on a sample of the `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirects to confirm landing URLs and UTMs.
9. Include a readable text alternative with more prose and fewer URLs.
