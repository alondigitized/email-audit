# A little holiday cheer! EXTRA 20% OFF select styles
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- This is a Christmas Shop send with the usual "Extra 20% off Select Styles" banner on top. The holiday creative is strong. A plaid-and-ornament hero leads into six tidy category tiles: pillows and throws, candles, bedding, stockings, botanicals, and tabletop. The tiles carry "Ships Free" tags, and the whole thing reads as seasonal and cohesive.
- The subject's "A little holiday cheer!" promises a holiday email, but the first thing you see is the same oxblood sale banner Pottery Barn has sent all week (Oct 26–31). The sale banner and the Christmas Shop compete for the top slot. Below the tiles, the email tacks on a "Recommended Just For You" row, a "Latest Designs" banner, a "Free Design Services" banner and an app promo. The back half dilutes the holiday message, and the long footer legalese stacks on top.
- Judgement: this is a good seasonal catalog email, better than the pure "Extra 20% off" sends, but it has no single hook. It is a browse email with a discount sticker on top, and the repetition fatigue from the week shows.

## What's working

- The Christmas Shop hero (plaid pillows, ornaments) is on-season and visually rich.
- The six category tiles are clean and scannable, with a consistent two-column layout.
- "Ships Free" badges on several tiles add a concrete incentive.
- The "Recommended Just For You" row adds a hint of personalization, with a mix of products like the Banks Extendable Dining Table.
- The "Book a Free Appointment" CTA gives a service option for higher-intent shoppers.

## What's weak

- The "Extra 20% off Select Styles" banner is the same one sent all week, so it reads as stale.
- The "Exclusions apply. Prices as marked." fine print sits right under the headline offer and undercuts it.
- The email has too many sections. The Latest Designs, Design Services, app promo, Affirm and rewards blocks all dilute the Christmas message.
- Most CTAs are generic ("Shop Now", "Shop All Sale"). The tiles carry no prices or specific offers.
- The footer has dense legal text that is long and hard to read.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the Christmas hero and drop the sale banner to a slim strip, or merge the two ("Christmas Shop: Extra 20% off select styles"). That gives one clear message.
- 2. Cut the back half to one module, either recommendations or Design Services, and drop the app and Affirm blocks.
- 3. Put prices or "from $X" on the category tiles.
- 4. Replace "Shop Now" with specific copy such as "Shop Christmas Decor".
- 5. Add a clear end date for the 20% off.
- **Subject Alt A:** `The Christmas Shop is open: extra 20% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Stockings, plaid & candlelight, 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Select styles are extra 20% off, and many holiday pieces ship free.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Pillows, stockings, tabletop and more, now through Sunday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Table-based 650px Salesforce Marketing Cloud email (`click.e.potterybarn.com`). It has no unresolved merge tokens and a working preheader. The main problems are about 50 images flagged for missing alt text, three `http://` image sources (one a tracking pixel that puts the recipient's address in the query string), and an unzoomable viewport. The HTML source is truncated, so the footer and unsubscribe markup could not be reviewed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverifiable links:** 54 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe because they are click-redirect domains. The redirect targets and any UTM parameters are not visible, for example `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7ImQi…` on the logo link.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` loads over plain HTTP. It also carries the recipient's address unencrypted in the URL.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEv…/cat/1` is an ad-network beacon, which adds a third-party tracking dependency.
- **Email in an image URL:** `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is served as an `<img>` and also exposes the address in the URL.
- **Open pixel without alt:** `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/8602653c-…?mi_cid=…` has no alt text.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (about 50 images):** this covers the hero `H26_TOP_Extra20CLR.jpg` and `slices_01–12.jpg` (1101_SUN_AM_ChristmasShopLaunch). It also covers the product images (`banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`, `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg`, `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg` and others) and the footer `slices_01–18.jpg`. With images blocked, the offer and CTAs are unreadable. Only the logo has alt text ("POTTERY BARN").
- **Non-HTTPS images (3):**
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/…`

  These may be blocked or trigger mixed-content warnings. The 2022 asset also looks stale.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>`.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns=…>` has none.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom.
- **Fixed mobile width:** `.showmobile{width:414px !important}` in the `max-width:650px` media query can overflow viewports narrower than 414px.
- **Stray PHP in CSS:** `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control…")` sits inside a `<style>` block. It is invalid CSS and harmless where it falls, but it is a template leak.
- **Missing `role="presentation"`:** the spacer table (`<td height="20">&nbsp;</td>`) and the outer wrapper tables lack it, while the content tables have it.
- **Web font:** the Montserrat `<link>` is unsupported in many clients. Fallback stacks can't be confirmed from the truncated source.
- **Cross-brand spacer:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on a West Elm domain and has no alt text.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source, and the recipient address is populated in the pixel URLs. The PII-in-URL concern is covered in section 2.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe:** the header is not found. The QA tool notes this may be stripped by the AgentMail relay, so it is unconfirmed.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found, with the same caveat.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. This is expected via the relay.
- **Footer content:** the visible HTML is truncated before the footer, so the physical address and unsubscribe link could not be verified.
- **Plain-text part:** it is 71% URLs (10,386 of 14,581 chars), driven by long tracking URLs. This is a mild deliverability risk.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not verifiable. The links are opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…` redirects, so UTM parameters and landing pages could not be checked. Product images come from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/…`, which points to the correct brand domain. No other continuity issues were found.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, and `alt=""` to spacers and pixels. Prioritize the hero and the slice images that carry the offer and CTAs.
2. Move the three `http://` assets to HTTPS. Replace the 2022 brand banner. Remove the PII from the `svopn` and `coreemail` URLs or hash the identifier.
3. Add a `<title>` and `lang="en"`. Remove `maximum-scale=1`. Change `.showmobile` to `max-width:100%`.
4. Remove the stray PHP `header()` lines from the `<style>` block.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and SPF/DKIM/DMARC from the raw message at the original receiving mailbox, not through the relay.
6. Resolve the redirect chains with a follow-redirects probe to verify UTM tagging and landing-page alignment.
