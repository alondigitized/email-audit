# Free design appointments! Spots go fast
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- This is a services-and-events send, not a promo. The hero, "RSVP for Your Free Design Services" with a "BOOK A FREE APPOINTMENT" button, puts one clear action at the top. The what/when/where framing (styling tips and room refreshes, in your home, virtually or in-store) is easy to scan. The "spots go fast" urgency is credible.
- The body then dilutes the hero. After the appointment pitch come a Holiday Open House on Saturday, November 7th, a "Home for the Holidays Classes" block, a "Newest Arrivals" block, a "GREAT GIFTS" banner and a six-item "Recommended Just For You" grid. The appointment CTA repeats mid-email, but it competes with four other asks (RSVP TODAY twice, FIND YOUR STORE, SHOP NOW). The email also carries no price or discount, so it reads as brand-building. For a bedding shopper like the ones in this audit history, it's relevant but not compelling.
- Judgement: a solid, polished send with a good hero, and a mid-tier result because it asks for too many things. Compared with the Nov 6 "Cozy Cloud Quilt" send, which had one offer and one hero, this one has less focus.

## What's working

- The hero states a single free service with a clear "BOOK A FREE APPOINTMENT" button, and the eye lands on it first.
- The what/when/where triplet makes the service concrete and low-friction.
- The booking CTA repeats after the Newest Arrivals block, so a deep scroller gets a second chance.
- The Saturday, November 7th open house is a dated, time-bound event (10 AM–2 PM), which gives credible urgency.
- The photography is warm and seasonal (tree, fireplace, plaid bedding), and the render is clean with no visible broken images.

## What's weak

- There are too many competing CTAs: RSVP TODAY (twice), FIND YOUR STORE, BOOK A FREE APPOINTMENT (twice), SHOP NOW, plus product tiles.
- The "GREAT GIFTS" banner looks washed out, and its text is hard to read against the photo.
- The "Recommended Just For You" grid is a generic mix of dining tables, stockings, curtain rods and a bed, with no visible price or review. It doesn't connect to the design-services theme.
- Nothing in the email carries a price, member benefit or discount, so shoppers motivated by savings have no reason to click.
- The footer is long, with credit card, Affirm, app and legal blocks. The "Earn 10% Back in Rewards" credit card pitch distracts from the appointment ask.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the appointment the only primary CTA. Cut the Great Gifts banner and shrink the product grid to three items or fewer.
- 2. Merge the open house and classes into one "This weekend in stores" row. Use a single RSVP button there instead of two text links.
- 3. Add proof under the hero: a designer photo, a "1M+ rooms designed" line or a short testimonial.
- 4. Add a time frame to the appointment pitch, such as "Book by Sunday for holiday install."
- 5. Link each product tile to a price-bearing page, and show price and star ratings.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, image-sliced SFMC send. Its main technical problems are leaked PHP text inside a `<style>` block, many linked images with empty or missing alt text, plain-text recipient email addresses in image URLs, and two `http://` image sources. Because every link goes through click.e.potterybarn.com, destination and UTM alignment can't be verified from the source.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Untestable links:** 46 links were skipped because they are `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/?qs=…` redirects. The `qs` values are opaque, so destinations and campaign parameters can't be confirmed.
- **Third-party pixels:** `secure.leadback.advertising.com/adcedge/lb?site=695501…` and `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/…/cat/1` are ad-network pixels. I couldn't check whether they still resolve. If they are dead, they add failed requests and tracking noise for nothing.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a Pottery Barn email loading a spacer from the West Elm domain. It works if the infrastructure is shared. Otherwise it's an extra external dependency.
- **Commented-out markup:** The credit-card banner (`cm_ite=PB_header_nonmember`) is commented out but still ships in the HTML. It adds bloat but has no functional impact.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Leaked PHP in CSS:** The second `<style>` block begins with `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store…"); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. CSS parsers treat this text as part of the next selector, so the `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` rule that follows is likely dropped. iOS will then auto-link and restyle detected dates, addresses and phone numbers.
- **Empty alt on linked images:** `slices_01.jpg` to `slices_07.jpg` in `/1106_FRI_AM2_DC_InviteHeroMoreinStore/` are each wrapped in an `<a>` and have `alt=""`. The links have no accessible name, and with images blocked the email would be blank apart from the header logo. The header image has `alt="POTTERY BARN"` and is fine.
- **Missing alt elsewhere:**
  - Product images: `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`, `holiday-icons-crewel-stockings-2-l.jpg`, `standard-curtain-rod-wall-bracket-l.jpg`, `classic-velvet-stockings-l.jpg`, `farmhouse-platform-bed-1-l.jpg` and `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-125-l.jpg`.
  - Banner: `3ceb0c96-f305-4f56-a6bf-1ce072abc871.jpg`.
  - Footer: `Email-Footer/slices_01.jpg` to `slices_18.jpg`.
- **`http://` image sources:** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`. These can be blocked or flagged as mixed content, and the 2022 path suggests a stale reused asset.
- **Responsive CSS doesn't match the markup:** The media query targets `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]`. In the visible source, the tables have no `class="wrapper"` and the images have no `class="photo"`. Images are fixed at `width="650"` with no `max-width:100%`, so the 480px rules don't apply and the layout will likely overflow at 320px. The source is truncated, so this holds only for the part I can see.
- **Missing structure:** `<html xmlns=…>` has no `lang` attribute, the head has no `<title>`, and the layout tables lack `role="presentation"`.
- **Web font:** The Google Fonts `<link>` for Montserrat is stripped by Gmail and some other clients. Nothing in the visible source uses that font.
- **Comment before the DOCTYPE:** `<!-- begin dynamic ampScript slot --> <!-- end dynamic ampScript slot -->` comes before `<!DOCTYPE>`. This is minor, but it can trigger quirks mode in some renderers.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens or AMPscript leakage were found. The AMPscript slot comments are empty.
- **Email address in URLs:** The recipient address `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` appears in plain text in image URLs. The first is `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=…` and the second is `https://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=…`. This exposes the address in logs and proxy caches. The `svopn` pixel also shares it with a third-party tracker (the QA scan didn't cover that domain's ownership).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Missing List-Unsubscribe headers:** `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may have stripped them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a sender defect.
- **Footer may be image-only:** The footer is 18 image slices (`/Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`), all without alt text. The truncated source doesn't show a text unsubscribe link or physical address. If they exist only inside images, there's no text fallback. This is a risk, not a confirmed violation.
- **Authentication:** `Authentication-Results` is absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status can't be assessed. This is a relay limitation. The From domain (`e.potterybarn.com`) and the click domain (`click.e.potterybarn.com`) are aligned.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All visible CTAs use SFMC click wrappers, so UTM parameters and landing URLs can't be confirmed. The only plain `potterybarn.com` URLs visible are product images and the `coreemail.html` pixel.
- Continuity can't be assessed without resolving the `qs` redirects. That requires following a live click, which the QA run skipped.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP lines from the second `<style>` block so the `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` rule applies.
2. Give every linked image a descriptive `alt` (for example "Book a free design appointment"). Add `alt=""` to pure pixels and spacers, and alt text to the footer slices or replace them with live text.
3. Switch the two `http://edm.potterybarn.com/…` images to `https://`, or drop the 2022 banner.
4. Add `class="wrapper"` and `class="photo"` to the tables and images (or rewrite the selectors). Add `max-width:100%` to images so the layout is responsive.
5. Add `lang="en"`, a `<title>`, and `role="presentation"` on layout tables.
6. Use a hashed or opaque identifier instead of the raw email address in the `coreemail.html` and `svopn` pixel URLs.
7. Check whether the `leadback.advertising.com` and `d.turn.com` pixels still resolve, and remove them if they don't.
8. Run a seed test against a mailbox that preserves raw headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Follow the `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirects to check UTM and landing-page alignment.
