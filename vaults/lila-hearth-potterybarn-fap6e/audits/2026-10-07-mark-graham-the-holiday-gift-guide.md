# Mark & Graham 🎁 The Holiday Gift Guide
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-07**
## Executive summary

- This is a brand-spotlight send for Mark & Graham, the Williams-Sonoma Inc. sister brand, under Pottery Barn's sender name. The hero is strong: a navy-and-green plaid tote with a red ribbon and a monogram, under "The Holiday Gift Guide". The three red price-tier bars ("Gifts Under $50 / $100 / Next-Level Gifts") are the best idea in the email. They give a shopper a fast way in.
- The rest is a catalog scroll. It has five category tiles (Her, Him, the Host, Pets, New Arrivals), a monogram banner, then a long tail of app, credit card, Affirm and legal blocks. There is no price, deadline or incentive anywhere. Compare the 10-06 "RSVP: 100% free design services" send, which had a concrete reason to act. This one only says "browse our gifts".
- The sender and subject also disagree. The inbox shows "Pottery Barn" while the subject leads with "Mark & Graham". Pottery Barn's own cadence this week (furniture, Norman Rockwell, plaid, holiday catalog) has been editorial and offer-light. This is another mood send, and a decent one, but it is not a driver.

## What's working

- The hero is polished and on-season, and the monogrammed tote makes the personalization angle obvious.
- The "Gifts Under $50 / $100 / Next-Level Gifts" bars are full-width, high-contrast red and unambiguous, and they sort shoppers by budget.
- The category tiles (Her, Him, Host, Pets) follow a standard gifting logic and are easy to scan. The dog tile is a nice emotional break.
- The "Gifts They'll Love. Beautifully Monogrammed." banner restates the brand's differentiator and has a clear "Shop the M&G Gift Guide" button.
- The red-and-green holiday palette is consistent from header to footer, so the email reads as one unit.

## What's weak

- No offer is visible: no discount, free-shipping threshold, or deadline. Everything sits in the fine print, including a dense block of promotional exclusions at the bottom.
- The "Shop now" links on the Host and Pet tiles sit over busy, dark imagery, and the "Gifts for the Host" text looks partly lost against the dark backgammon-board photo. The tiles are hard to read quickly.
- The sender is "Pottery Barn" but the subject and hero push Mark & Graham, which dilutes recognition.
- The long footer stack (app download, credit card, Affirm, Join Our Email List) pushes the email toward bloat. The "Join Our Email List" prompt is also odd for people already receiving the email.
- The product tiles have no names, prices or ratings, so there is nothing to attach interest to.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a concrete reason to click, such as "Free monogramming through 10/15" or "Order by 12/10 for holiday delivery". A dated shipping or personalization deadline is the natural urgency for a monogram brand.
- 2. Put 3–4 specific products with names and prices under each budget tier, instead of generic category tiles.
- 3. Make the sender and subject agree: use "Mark & Graham" as the sender, or lead the subject with Pottery Barn.
- 4. Cut the footer stack by at least half, and drop "Join Our Email List" for existing subscribers.
- 5. Put dark-image tile titles on a solid or gradient label so they stay legible.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Pottery Barn "Mark & Graham 🎁 The Holiday Gift Guide"

## 1. Technical Summary
This is an image-sliced, fixed-width (650px) ESP (Salesforce Marketing Cloud) email. The visible HTML has invalid CSS, responsive selectors that match nothing, and many images with no alt text. The recipient's email address is also written into third-party tracking-pixel URLs. The HTML was truncated, so the unsubscribe footer could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified click links.** 39 links on `click.e.potterybarn.com/?qs=...` were skipped by the QA probe because they are redirect domains. Destinations, redirect chains and status codes are unverified.
- **Third-party ad pixels.** QA found pixels at `secure.leadback.advertising.com/adcedge/lb?site=695501...` and `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1`. They are in addition to the first-party open pixel at `svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true`. They are unaffected by the email's own consent or unsubscribe handling and are slow to load.
- **Mixed content.** Two images load over `http://`, which some clients may block:
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
- **Dead commented-out block.** An HTML comment in the header still contains a live-format link (`potterybarn.com/pages/credit-card/?...cm_ite=PB_header_nonmember`). It is harmless but adds bytes and leaks internal campaign parameters.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid CSS in `<style>`.** Stray PHP statements sit inside the second `<style>` block: `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ...");`. Per CSS parsing rules, they merge with the following selector into one invalid prelude. That likely drops the `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` rule, so Apple auto-linking of dates, addresses and numbers will not be neutralized.
- **Dead responsive rules.** The media query targets `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]`. In the visible source, no table has `class="wrapper"` and no image has `class="photo"`. Tables are hard-coded `width="650"` and images `width="650"`, so the layout won't scale at ≤480px. The `viewport` meta tag is present but has no effect.
- **Missing alt text.** QA flagged these images:
  - `obo-holiday-gg-650px--slice-06.jpeg` and `--slice-12.jpeg`. These are body content slices inside links, so those links have no accessible name and show blank when images are blocked.
  - `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `slices_02.jpg`, and footer `slices_01.jpg` through `slices_18.jpg` (20 images in all).
  - The tracking pixels (`coreemail.html`, `leadback`, `d.turn.com`, `svopn`) also lack alt, which is low impact.
- **Structure.** The `<html>` tag has no `lang` attribute, `<head>` has no `<title>`, and layout tables have no `role="presentation"`.
- **Markup.** Comments (`<!-- begin dynamic ampScript slot -->`) come before the `DOCTYPE`, and XHTML 1.0 Strict is declared alongside deprecated attributes (`bgcolor`, `align`, `border`, `<center>`). This can trigger quirks mode in some clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved tokens** (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) in the visible source.
- **Recipient email exposed in pixel URLs.** The email is in plaintext in query strings of two image requests:
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `https://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  
  This puts PII in URLs that can end up in proxy and server logs. The address is also unhashed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post** (RFC 8058) were not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed.
- **Authentication-Results** was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is likewise attributed to the relay.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address** could not be checked because the source is truncated.
- **Net.** No confirmed violations, but the header-level compliance status cannot be verified from this capture.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters can't be verified.** Every click URL is an opaque SFMC `qs=` blob. Any UTM or `cm_` parameters sit behind the redirect and were not resolved (the probe was skipped for all 39 links).
- **Landing pages not checked.** The header logo link and the two body slice links each have a distinct `qs=` token. Whether they land on Mark & Graham or Pottery Barn pages was not tested.
- **Domain mismatch.** Images are hosted on `image.e.markandgraham.com` and `edm.potterybarn.com`, while all clicks route through `click.e.potterybarn.com`. This is not itself a defect, but cross-domain redirects should be confirmed to resolve cleanly.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the stray `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block so the `x-apple-data-detectors` and `.yshortcuts` rules parse.
2. Add `class="wrapper"` and `class="photo"` to the tables and images, or rewrite the media query to target the actual markup. Use fluid `max-width:100%` images.
3. Add alt text to the content slices (`slice-06`, `slice-12`) and the footer links. Use `alt=""` on decorative images and pixels.
4. Switch the two `http://edm.potterybarn.com/...` images to `https://`.
5. Add `lang`, `<title>` and `role="presentation"` to the document.
6. Stop sending the plaintext email in the `coreemail.html` and `sv_sveme` pixel URLs, or hash it. Review whether the Advertising.com and Turn pixels are needed.
7. Re-capture the raw message with headers, using a direct-to-inbox path rather than the relay, to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Also resolve the `qs=` redirects with a follow-redirect probe to check UTMs and landing pages.
