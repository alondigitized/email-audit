# Homepage snapshot · www.microsoft.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- This is Microsoft going full "store mode" — hero copy is "Microsoft Store Deals" with an AI chat bar front and center, which honestly I respect as a move. The quick-filter chips (Consoles under $500, Refurbished Surface, Trade-in offers) feel weirdly well-targeted for me personally. But there's a literal "Placeholder text" dropdown sitting on screen that makes me question whether this page is actually live or if I caught it mid-deploy — that kills confidence fast.

## What's working

- **"Consoles under $500" chip** — I'm immediately calibrated. That's the Xbox Series S price band and it's where I actually shop.
- **"Refurbished Surface" chip** — as someone skeptical of launch supply and markup, refurb is my gateway drug. They surfaced this (pun intended) without me having to dig.
- **"Trade-in offers" chip** — I have old hardware. This is the one prompt that could get me from browse to transaction.
- **The Xbox + Surface laptop composite image** — a Surface running Game Pass content, with the $500+ in added value callout, is exactly the cross-sell I'd entertain.
- **AI "Ask a question" bar** — as a tech-native I'll actually use this instead of navigating a mega-menu. Smart placement.

## What's weak

- **"Placeholder text" dropdown** — this is not a vibes complaint, it is literally the words "Placeholder text" with a chevron, sitting on the live page. That's either a staging artifact or a broken component and it looks sloppy regardless of which.
- **No Microsoft Rewards hook** — I have points. I actively use the Rewards program. There's zero mention of it above the fold, which is a missed upsell for a returning Microsoft shopper.
- **No specific discount signal** — "Deals" in the headline but nothing quantified. No "save $150," no "15% off education," no dollar amount except the $500+ value bundle claim below the fold. Vague.
- **AI bar copy "Ask a question"** — too passive. "Find your next Surface" or "What are you shopping for?" would work harder. This reads like a support widget, not a shopping assistant.
- **No price visible on the product tile** — the Surface laptop image with "$500+ in added value" doesn't tell me what the laptop costs. That's a friction point.

## Recommendations

- **Fix the placeholder dropdown immediately** — ship a hotfix or pull the component. A broken UI element on the homepage of the Microsoft Store destroys credibility faster than any competitor ad.
- **Add a Microsoft Rewards balance nudge** — signed-in users should see "You have X points — use them today" above the fold. This is table stakes for a loyalty-driven shopper.
- **Make one chip a limited-time offer** — change "Trade-in offers" to "Trade-in: up to $400 back, ends May 31" and watch click-through go up. Specificity converts.
- **Rename the AI bar CTA** — swap "Ask a question" placeholder copy for "Find your Surface or Xbox deal →" so it reads as a shopping tool, not a help center widget.
