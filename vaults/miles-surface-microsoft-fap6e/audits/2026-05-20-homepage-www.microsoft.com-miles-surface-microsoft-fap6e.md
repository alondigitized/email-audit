# Homepage snapshot · www.microsoft.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so Microsoft is leading with an AI-powered deals page — feels like they're trying to make the Store feel smarter and more personal than just a product grid. The Surface + Xbox imagery below the fold is genuinely relevant to me, but someone shipped this with a literal "Placeholder text" dropdown still visible on the page, which makes the whole thing feel unfinished. Right brand for me, rough execution.

## What's working

- **"Consoles under $500" chip** — one tap to Xbox hardware, perfectly calibrated for my budget-conscious back-to-school buying pattern
- **"Refurbished Surface" chip** — I always check refurb before buying new; this being front-and-center saves me nav time
- **"Trade-in offers" chip** — I have old Surface gear lying around; this is a hook I'd actually tap
- **Surface + Xbox game art product image** — the combo shot visually captures exactly why I'm in the Microsoft ecosystem; it's not Surface *or* Xbox, it's both together
- **AI "Ask a question" box** — as a tech-native, I'm not intimidated by this interaction model; I'd actually try typing "best Surface deal under $1200"

## What's weak

- **"Placeholder text" dropdown** — this is just a bug that shipped to prod. Kills trust immediately; makes me wonder what else is broken
- **No Microsoft Rewards callout** — I have points. This homepage has no idea I exist as a returning customer. A Rewards balance or member-pricing badge would have me engaged before I even scrolled
- **"Explore savings and more with personalized help from AI"** — subhead is doing too much; "personalized help from AI" competes with "savings" and lands as neither
- **Zero urgency** — no deadline, no countdown, no "ends tonight." "Deals" is a destination, not an event. I have no reason to act now vs. next Tuesday
- **No specific price anchoring in hero** — "$500+ in added value" is marketing math that sounds like fine print, not a real number I can act on

## Recommendations

- **Kill the placeholder:** That "Placeholder text" dropdown needs to be removed or replaced before any campaign runs — one line of copy, one hour of work
- **Surface the Rewards balance for signed-in users:** Put my Microsoft Rewards points total next to the account icon in the nav — even just "1,240 pts" would make me feel like a known customer instead of a random visitor
- **Swap "Deals" hero framing for a time-bound event:** "Memorial Day Sale — Ends Monday" with a single specific offer ("Save $300 on Surface Pro") outperforms a generic deals page every time
- **Make the Xbox + Surface bundle the hero CTA:** That product image at the bottom is the most compelling thing on the page — promote it above the AI search box with a direct "Shop the Bundle" button
