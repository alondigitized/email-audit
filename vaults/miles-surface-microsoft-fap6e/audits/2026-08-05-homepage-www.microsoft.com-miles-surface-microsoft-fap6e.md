# Homepage snapshot · www.microsoft.com · 2026-08-05
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- This screams "we know exactly who's on this page" — back-to-school messaging, an AI-powered search bar, and the Surface Pro front and center with an education-discount icon sitting right on the product card. It reads like a legit tech retailer pushing hardware deals, not some generic corporate landing page. Feels like Microsoft actually built this screen for someone shopping laptops right now.

## What's working

- The "Explore back-to-school tech, deals, and more with personalized help from AI" subhead — hits the exact seasonal window I'd actually be shopping in.
- The Surface Pro, 13-inch (12th Edition) card with the graduation-cap badge — that's the education-discount signal I always hunt for before I buy a Surface.
- "Refurbished Surface" pill — smart, because I'm cautious about launch-day supply and stock issues, a refurb option tells me there's inventory sitting around, not a phantom pre-order.
- "Trade-in offers" — I've got an old Surface kicking around, this is the first thing that'd get a tap from me.

## What's weak

- No actual price or percentage-off anywhere on this screen — "Deals" is the headline but there's zero dollar figure to anchor against.
- The "New" badge on the Surface Pro card doesn't tell me what's actually new about the 12th Edition — spec bump? Chip? I'd want that up front given how skeptical I am about early stock/launch hype.
- That floating chat bubble in the bottom right is half-covering the product description text ("Get more done every day with a devi—" cut off) — mild layout friction right where I want to read more.
- No Xbox/Game Pass presence at all above the fold — for a guy who's as much Xbox loyalist as Surface buyer, that's a miss on this screen, though "Consoles under $500" at least nods at it.

## Recommendations

- Put an actual number on the Surface Pro card — "$150 off with student ID" or similar — instead of leaving "Deals" to imply savings with no math shown.
- Move or shrink that chat bubble so it's not clipping the product copy under the Surface Pro card.
- Add a one-line spec callout under "New" (e.g., "Copilot+ PC, up to 20hrs battery") so I know why this edition matters before I tap in.
- Surface an Xbox/Game Pass tile in this same row as Consoles/Refurbished/Trade-in — I'd expect a peer chip like "Game Pass Ultimate" sitting right next to Trade-in offers.
