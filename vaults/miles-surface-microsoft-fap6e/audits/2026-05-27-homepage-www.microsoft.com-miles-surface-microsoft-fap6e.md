# Homepage snapshot · www.microsoft.com · 2026-05-27
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-27**
## Executive summary

- This is Microsoft's deals hub, and on a pure portfolio-fit basis it's almost exactly aimed at me — Surface hardware, Xbox, trade-in hooks, even an AI-powered search bar front and center. But there's a "Placeholder text" dropdown sitting right in the middle of the page that destroys any sense of polish, which is embarrassing for a company selling $2,000 laptops. The brand vibes are correct; the execution is half-baked.

## What's working

- **"Consoles under $500" + "Refurbished Surface" quick-filter pills** — these are one-tap shortcuts straight to my two most likely purchase paths. Smart.
- **"Trade-in offers" pill** — as a Surface loyalist who upgrades every few cycles, this is exactly the hook that would make me pause.
- **Surface laptop with Xbox Game Pass art visible below the fold** — the "$500+ in added value" framing sells the ecosystem, not just the hardware. That's the right message for me: I already know the specs, sell me the bundle value.
- **AI search bar ("Ask a question")** — for a native-tech millennial, the conversational search UI feels natural and I'd actually try it.
- **"Refurbished Surface" pill** — shows Microsoft is thinking about education-discount and back-to-school shoppers (my buying window) who want Surface at a discount.

## What's weak

- **"Placeholder text" dropdown** — this is a live production bug visible to every visitor. A literal debug string on the Microsoft Store homepage is humiliating and immediately undermines the premium brand positioning.
- **No primary shop CTA button** — "Ask a question" is the only interactive element besides the pills. There's no "Shop Surface," "View Deals," or "Buy Now" button with a visible tap target. I shouldn't have to interrogate an AI to start shopping.
- **Zero loyalty / Microsoft Rewards visibility** — I've accumulated Rewards points from years of Xbox purchases. Not showing me a balance or member-exclusive pricing is leaving money on the table.
- **No trust signals above the fold** — no review counts, no free-shipping callout, no "free returns" badge. For a considered hardware purchase, that silence creates hesitation.
- **No urgency on the deals** — "Microsoft Store Deals" is static. No "ends Sunday," no countdown, nothing to make me act today rather than check back tomorrow.

## Recommendations

- **Nuke the "Placeholder text" dropdown immediately** — this is a P0 bug. Replace it with a real category selector or just remove it entirely. Nothing tanks credibility faster than visible dev scaffolding on a flagship commerce page.
- **Add a hard CTA button below the AI search bar** — something like "Shop All Deals →" or "Browse Surface" as a tappable button for people who don't want to type a query.
- **Surface a Microsoft Rewards balance or member-exclusive badge** — even a generic "Earn Rewards on every purchase" chip next to the search bar would give returning shoppers a reason to stay logged in and transact.
- **Put an urgency signal on the deals strip** — add "Deals through June 1" or a simple countdown chip on the hero. Deals pages without deadlines feel like permanent markdown, which devalues the brand.
