# Homepage snapshot · www.microsoft.com · 2026-07-22
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-22**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is actually kind of speaking my language — Microsoft led with "back-to-school tech, deals" right as that window opens, and they dropped a Surface + headphones product shot right in the middle of the page. The AI "Ask a question" search bar as the hero interaction is a bold swing — feels like they're betting I'll converse my way to a purchase instead of browse. It's clean and organized for a mobile experience, but the conversion scaffolding is almost entirely missing.

## What's working

- **"Back-to-school tech, deals" hero copy** — this is the exact seasonal peg I operate on; that subhead about "personalized help from AI" also lands as a differentiator from Amazon's dumb search
- **"Consoles under $500" chip** — I'm a Game Pass loyalist and this chip makes me stop scrolling immediately; it's one tap to a relevant category
- **"Refurbished Surface" chip** — I'm skeptical of launch-day supply and this gives me a credible in-stock alternative that I'd actually click
- **Surface + headphones product shot** — rose gold Surface with black headphones against dark background is aspirational but not pretentious; it's a realistic desk setup, not a fashion shoot
- **"Trade-in offers" chip** — I have old hardware and this is the value hook that makes upgrading feel lower-risk

## What's weak

- **The AI search bar as the primary hero interaction** — asking me to type a question before I even know what I'm shopping for is backwards on mobile; I want to browse, not prompt
- **Zero trust signals above the fold** — no review counts, no free-returns badge, no delivery estimate, nothing that would close the gap between "interested" and "buying"
- **The "Limited-time offer / Buy the latest Surface, get free..." is cut off at the bottom** — the most conversion-friendly element on the page and it's half-visible; that's a wasted hook
- **No member or education pricing callout** — I specifically wait for education deals and there's no signal that any of these chips lead to discounted pricing vs. MSRP
- **No human in the imagery** — product-only shot is clean but doesn't tell me anything about who this is for or what using it feels like

## Recommendations

- **Move the "Limited-time offer" Surface bundle above the AI search bar** — the AI chat is a novelty, a Surface bundle with a freebee is a reason to buy; swap the stack order
- **Add an education/student badge to the back-to-school hero** — a small "Education pricing available" tag next to the headline would immediately validate this as the right window to buy
- **Surface chip on the search bar** — pre-populate the "Ask a question" field with "Which Surface fits my workflow?" to show me what the AI is good for instead of leaving me to figure out how to use it
- **Surface Headphones bundle callout beneath the product image** — the image already shows headphones; add a "Included in select Surface bundles" label to pull the partially-visible offer up above the fold
