# Homepage snapshot · www.microsoft.com · 2026-07-08
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so Microsoft is going full education-season mode — "Microsoft Store Deals" with a back-to-school hook front and center, plus a student/teacher discount card lower on screen. As a Surface loyalist who literally times my purchases around edu discounts and back-to-school cycles, this is genuinely hitting me where I live. It's not flashy, but it's pointed.

## What's working

- **"Microsoft Store Deals" headline with back-to-school framing** — I literally plan my Surface upgrades around this window. Seeing it confirmed in the hero tells me I'm in the right place at the right time.
- **"Refurbished Surface" quick-chip** — As someone skeptical of Surface launch supply constraints, this chip is actually a relief valve. Refurb is how I've hedged bad launch allocation before.
- **"Trade-in offers" chip** — Visible and tappable. I have an old Surface Studio I've been sitting on. This gets me thinking.
- **Student/parent/teacher 10% off card** — Parents count. I'm 37, likely with kids in school. This framing is broader than just students, which catches me.
- **AI search bar with "Ask a question" prompt** — Genuinely useful for a native-tech guy. I'd actually type "best Surface for video editing under $1,500" here.

## What's weak

- **No Surface product imagery above the fold** — I came here as a Surface loyalist and I see zero hardware. A Search bar and text chips don't give me anything visual to lust after.
- **"Consoles under $500" chip placement** — Xbox is my other category, but leading with it above Refurbished Surface feels off on a homepage competing with Apple hardware ambitions. It de-premiums the page.
- **No account/member hook** — I have a Microsoft account. There's a profile icon in the nav but zero personalization signal. No "Welcome back, Miles" or loyalty/rewards prompt.
- **"Personalized help from AI" subhead doesn't deliver** — The AI bar looks like a generic search box. If it's personalized, show me it's personalized — my account, my history, something.
- **10% off is underwhelming for a $1,500–$3,500 Surface Studio purchase** — The discount feels thin for the premium hardware audience. Apple education is also 10%. No differentiation here.

## Recommendations

- **Swap generic stock-photo education card for an actual Surface product shot** — Show the Surface Pro or Surface Studio in a back-to-school context. Hardware desire starts with imagery.
- **Add a "Microsoft Rewards balance" or signed-in account nudge** — Even a "Sign in to see your personalized deals" CTA would make the AI search bar feel earned rather than decorative.
- **Elevate the trade-in chip to a full module** — "Trade in your old Surface, get up to $X" with a concrete dollar amount converts fence-sitters like me who already own Microsoft hardware.
- **Put a price anchor in the hero** — "Surface Laptop starting at $799" or "Surface Pro from $1,099" — something that anchors before I have to dig into nav. Apple always has this on their edu page.
