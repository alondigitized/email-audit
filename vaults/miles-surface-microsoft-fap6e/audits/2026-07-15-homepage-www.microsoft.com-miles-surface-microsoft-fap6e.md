# Homepage snapshot · www.microsoft.com · 2026-07-15
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-15**
## Executive summary

- Alright, Microsoft knows who they're talking to — back-to-school framing, a "Consoles under $500" chip, and "Refurbished Surface" front and center. That's not an accident. The vibe is a clean, AI-first store portal, which makes sense for where the company is going. That said, this thing doesn't tell me a single actual price difference, and there's a big black rectangle below the fold that's either a hero product image that didn't load or the most minimalist Surface ad I've ever seen.

## What's working

- **"Refurbished Surface" chip** — I cross-shop refurb because I know launch supply is sketchy and I hate paying full price for a first-run unit. One tap from the hero to the refurb Surface catalog is exactly the shortcut I want.
- **"Consoles under $500" chip** — Xbox Game Pass loyalty runs deep, and "under $500" is a credible budget anchor for a Series X. No ambiguity about what you get for that money tier.
- **Back-to-school framing** — That's my buying window. Seeing it called out in the hero subhead tells me the deals are real and timed, not evergreen.
- **AI "Ask a question" box** — As a tech native I'd actually use this. Asking "which Surface is best for a creative workflow under $1,200" and getting a filtered answer beats navigating category trees.
- **"Trade-in offers" chip** — I have an older Surface device. The fact that this is above-the-fold tells me Microsoft knows their upgrade loop.

## What's weak

- **That black rectangle** — Below the chips there's a product image slot that's entirely dark. If that's a Surface Studio or an Xbox console, I can't tell. It kills the visual momentum right when I'm about to scroll.
- **No Microsoft Rewards callout** — I have points. I'd redeem them. The fact that there's no rewards balance or "Earn X points" hook anywhere above the fold is a missed shot for a returning buyer like me.
- **"Personalized help from AI" is vague** — I trust the AI box enough to try it, but "personalized help" doesn't tell me if it's going to surface actual deals or just funnel me to search results. Could be one specific sentence like "Ask about Surface specs, Xbox bundles, or today's deals."
- **Zero price anchoring** — Not one dollar figure in the hero. "Consoles under $500" is close, but that's still a ceiling, not an offer. Back-to-school is when I expect to see "Save $150 on Surface Pro" styled callouts.
- **No urgency signal** — If this back-to-school event ends August 15th or there's limited refurb stock, I don't know it. As someone skeptical of Surface supply, a "limited quantities" note on refurb would actually move me faster.

## Recommendations

- **Fix or replace the black hero image** — Swap whatever isn't loading for a product shot of a Surface Laptop or Xbox Series X with a white/light background. One clear product image is better than an empty black void.
- **Add a Microsoft Rewards teaser** — A single line like "Earn 1,500 Rewards points on back-to-school purchases" directly under the subhead would give returning buyers a reason to log in and convert.
- **Swap "personalized help from AI" for a concrete example** — Change the subhead to something like "Ask: 'Which Surface handles video editing under $1K?'" to signal exactly what the AI box does.
- **Surface the back-to-school end date** — Add a light "Deals end Aug 17" line under the chip row. Deadlines close tabs faster than any headline.
