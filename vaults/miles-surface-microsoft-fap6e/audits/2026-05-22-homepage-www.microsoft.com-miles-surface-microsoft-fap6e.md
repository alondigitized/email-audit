# Homepage snapshot · www.microsoft.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- Microsoft is clearly running a deals-first homepage right now, which tracks — "Microsoft Store Deals" is the hero headline and the chip filters go straight to consoles, refurbished Surface, and trade-ins. The Surface + Xbox game art lower on the page speaks directly to someone like me who lives in both ecosystems. That said, there is a literal "Placeholder text" accordion element sitting right in the middle of the page, and that single bug does real damage to the brand perception for a company trying to compete with Apple's polish.

## What's working

- **"Consoles under $500" chip** — as someone with a standing Xbox Game Pass sub and an eye on hardware, this chip lands immediately. One tap to browse is the right friction level.
- **"Refurbished Surface" chip** — I am perpetually skeptical of new Surface supply constraints and launch-day availability. Refurb as a featured path is smart and feels like Microsoft actually understands its core audience.
- **"Trade-in offers" chip** — upgrade path thinking. For a Millennial who's been in the Microsoft ecosystem since Surface Pro 3, this resonates.
- **Surface laptop + Xbox game art image** — the "$500+ in added value" product visual directly shows the Surface / Xbox bundle pairing I actually think about when evaluating hardware value. Strong dual-ecosystem signal.
- **AI "Ask a question" bar** — as a tech-native I might actually use this instead of drilling through nav. Novel enough to try.

## What's weak

- **"Placeholder text" accordion** — this is a production bug sitting between the filter chips and the hero product image. It's a gray accordion element with literal placeholder copy. For a brand competing against Apple's "it just works" reputation, this is brand damage in a screenshot.
- **No Xbox Game Pass mention** — I have an active sub. Showing me bundle value or Game Pass pricing right here would be the highest-relevance hook Microsoft could throw, and it's absent above the fold.
- **No Microsoft Rewards hook** — I accumulate Rewards points on every purchase and they're a real retention lever. Zero mention on this screen.
- **No specific deal figures** — "Microsoft Store Deals" is the hero but I don't see a specific percentage or dollar amount. "Up to 30% off Surface" or "$200 off Surface Pro" would convert me faster than a vague deals umbrella.
- **AI search bar as primary CTA is risky** — asking me to type a question before showing me what's on sale requires effort I haven't committed to yet. Most users — including me — want to see the deal first, then ask questions.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the "Placeholder text" element immediately** — ship a hotfix today. Nothing else matters if a production bug is visible on the homepage to every mobile visitor. Replace it with the most compelling current deal module.
- **Add a Game Pass bundle callout directly under the hero** — "Xbox Game Pass Ultimate included — $X value" tied to a Surface purchase would be the single highest-relevance hook for someone like me. One line, one chip, massive signal.
- **Swap "Ask a question" as the primary CTA** — lead with a bold deal card ("Surface Laptop 7 — now $849, was $1,099") before the AI search bar. Let the search bar be secondary for people who want to dig deeper.
- **Add a Microsoft Rewards balance teaser for signed-in users** — even a small "You have 4,200 points — redeem today" banner would close the loop for returning shoppers and give me a reason not to cross-shop Best Buy.
