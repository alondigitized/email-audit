# Homepage snapshot · www.microsoft.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Microsoft's store homepage right now reads like it's trying to be a deals hub and an AI concierge at the same time, and honestly it's not quite either. The "Microsoft Store Deals" banner tells me there's something worth clicking, and the Xbox gaming visual lower on the page is squarely in my wheelhouse — but a fat "Placeholder text" dropdown sitting in the middle of the above-the-fold experience is embarrassing for a trillion-dollar company. I came here as a Surface loyalist half-expecting a Surface Pro launch push; instead I get a search box asking me to "Ask a question."

## What's working

- **"Refurbished Surface" pill** — as a buyer who watches supply and waits for deals, this pill is a direct hit. One tap away from my primary shopping lane.
- **"Consoles under $500" pill** — I'm a Game Pass subscriber; console pricing is absolutely in my consideration set.
- **"Trade-in offers" pill** — I've traded in Surface hardware before. This is legitimately useful to me and it's right there above the fold.
- **Xbox gaming imagery on the laptop** — the lifestyle shot of what looks like a Surface with Xbox game art is the most on-brand thing on screen; it visually confirms this isn't just an enterprise IT page.
- **"$500+ in added value" call-out** — bundle framing lands for someone who calculates total value rather than sticker price.

## What's weak

- **"Placeholder text" dropdown** — this is a broken component sitting in the critical viewport and it kills credibility instantly. I'm a native tech user; I notice this immediately and it makes me wonder what else is broken.
- **"Ask a question" as primary interaction** — leading with an AI search box instead of a clear shop CTA assumes I know what I want. When I'm browsing deals I want the deals surfaced to me, not an empty input field.
- **No loyalty / account hook** — I almost certainly have a Microsoft account. There's zero "Welcome back" or "Your rewards" signal above the fold. That's a miss for a returning shopper.
- **No specific product image above the fold** — the hero is text + pill buttons; the actual product image doesn't appear until I nearly scroll. For a deals page I want to see the deal product, not just the category label.
- **No urgency anywhere visible** — "Deals" implies limited time, but there's no countdown, no "ends tonight," nothing that creates a reason to act now.

## Recommendations

- **Hotfix the "Placeholder text" element before anything else** — swap it with real deal copy or remove it entirely; every minute that bug is live costs credibility with exactly the buyers Microsoft wants to retain.
- **Replace the "Ask a question" field with a featured deal card** — lead with the actual Surface or Xbox bundle offer at the top. Move the AI search to a secondary drawer or top nav icon so deal-hunters see product first.
- **Add a returning-account hook in the hero area** — even a small "Sign in to see member pricing" or "Your Microsoft Rewards: X pts" nudge would change my behavior from browse to buy.
- **Put a time-bound urgency signal on the "Consoles under $500" and bundle offers** — "This week only" or an end date gives me a reason to not close the tab and come back tomorrow (which usually means never).
