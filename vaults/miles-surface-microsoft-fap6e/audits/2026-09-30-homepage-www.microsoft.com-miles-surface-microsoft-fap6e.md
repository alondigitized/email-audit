# Homepage snapshot · www.microsoft.com · 2026-09-30
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- This is Microsoft Store's Deals landing page, and it opens on an AI "Ask a question" box instead of a product or an offer. Under it are three new Surface models with prices. As a Surface loyalist I like seeing the hardware right away. The deal itself is thin, though: 5% for students and $150 off one model.

## What's working

- **Three Surface cards with "New" badges:** they show me what shipped this cycle without any hunting, and they're the products I care about.
- **Prices shown on the card:** "From $1,149.99 / $1,199.99 / $1,349.99" lets me compare before I tap. I don't have to open a configurator to find out.
- **Quick chips ("Consoles under $500", "Refurbished Surface", "Trade-in offers"):** these are how I actually shop Microsoft, and refurbished and trade-in are the smart routes for me.
- **Top nav with Surface, Xbox, Windows and Deals one tap away:** I can get to my categories immediately.
- **Strikethrough on the Surface Pro 13-inch:** the "Save up to $150.00" pricing is honest and easy to read.

## What's weak

- **AI search box as the hero:** "Explore savings and more with personalized help from AI" makes me type a question before I see any deal. I'd rather browse.
- **"Up to 5% off for students*":** it's small, it has an asterisk, and 5% is barely a discount. I buy at education pricing precisely because it's normally better than that.
- **No Xbox or Game Pass presence:** the Deals page shows only Surface. My other big Microsoft category isn't visible, apart from a chip and a nav link.
- **Confusing product naming:** "Surface Pro, 12-inch (2nd Edition)" next to "Surface Pro, 13-inch (12th Edition)" is hard to parse. I can't tell which one is actually the better buy.
- **No buy or shop button in view:** the cards have descriptions and prices but no visible "Shop now" or "Add to cart" above the fold.
- **No urgency, reviews, shipping or returns info:** nothing tells me why to buy today or that it's safe to. I'm already skeptical of Surface launch supply, and there's no stock or availability signal.
- **Floating AI sparkle button (bottom right):** it's a second AI entry point that competes with the search box and covers the corner of the page.

## Recommendations

- **Flip the hero:** put the three Surface cards and a real "Shop Surface" button first, and shrink the AI box to a slim search bar under the nav.
- **Make the education offer prominent:** replace the small yellow "Up to 5% off for students*" tag with a banner like "Education pricing: verify in 2 min" that links to the terms.
- **Add a "Game Pass + Xbox" strip:** put a card or chip row right under the Surface cards, for example "Xbox Series X bundles with 3 months Game Pass".
- **Add trust lines to each card:** a star rating, "Free shipping & 30-day returns", and an "In stock / ships by [date]" note. That answers my supply worry.
