# Homepage snapshot · www.microsoft.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- This is Microsoft leaning hard into the "we're a store now" pivot — deals-forward, AI-assisted, with a chat box as the hero interaction. For me, a Surface loyalist who lives on Xbox Game Pass and watches every new hardware drop, the homepage actually has some relevant chips right up front. But that "Placeholder text" dropdown sitting in the middle of my screen is a big red flag — this feels like a staging build that escaped to prod.

## What's working

- **"Refurbished Surface" chip** — I buy at a discount or education pricing, and refurb is exactly where I shop. One tap, no nav maze.
- **"Trade-in offers" chip** — I upgrade on a cycle and trade-in value is always part of the math. Showing this immediately is smart.
- **AI "Ask a question" hero** — I'm native-tech, I'll use a chat interface. Framing the store as AI-assisted is actually appealing to me more than a static banner.
- **Gaming laptop imagery with Xbox controller** — hits both my Surface loyalty and my Game Pass habit in one visual.
- **"$500+ in added value" callout** — partially visible but bundles are how I justify a new Surface. Speaks to my value-calculation brain.

## What's weak

- **"Placeholder text" dropdown** — this is an unfinished UI element sitting center-screen above the fold. For a brand that's supposed to be polished and developer-trusted, this is embarrassing.
- **No Microsoft Rewards hook** — I'm a Rewards member. Showing me my points balance or "earn X points today" would be instant engagement. Zero loyalty signal above the fold.
- **Xbox Game Pass is invisible** — I'm a Game Pass loyalist and the hero doesn't surface it at all. The gaming imagery implies it but never lands the punch.
- **"Ask a question" as the PRIMARY CTA** — I get the AI angle but for someone ready to buy, this adds a step. A direct "Shop Surface" button alongside the chat box would serve both intents.
- **No launch-supply signal** — I'm chronically skeptical about Surface availability at launch. Even a "currently in stock" badge or "shipping now" note would reduce my purchase anxiety.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the placeholder dropdown immediately** — this is a production bug above the fold on Microsoft's flagship store. Fix before any other optimization.
- **Add a Microsoft Rewards balance chip** next to "Ask a question" — even a generic "Earn Rewards on every purchase" badge gets me thinking about my points.
- **Surface the Xbox Game Pass bundle** as a chip or inline callout — I'm already here for hardware; remind me the $15/month sub is included on new devices.
- **Pair the AI chat CTA with a hard "Shop Surface" button** — let me choose my own adventure without forcing the AI path.
