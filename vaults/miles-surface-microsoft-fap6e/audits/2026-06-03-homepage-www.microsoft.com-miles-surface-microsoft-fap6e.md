# Homepage snapshot · www.microsoft.com · 2026-06-03
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-03**
## Executive summary

- Alright, so Microsoft's homepage is leading with "Microsoft Store Deals" and an AI-powered search prompt — it's a deals-first experience with a little bundle teaser below the fold. The quick-links are honestly built for someone like me: "Refurbished Surface" and "Consoles under $500" are exactly the two channels I shop. That said, there's a literal "Placeholder text" accordion on screen right now, which is a rough look for a trillion-dollar company.

## What's working

- **"Refurbished Surface" quick-link** — for a loyalist who knows Surface launch supply is notoriously thin and overpriced at launch, refurb is where I actually buy. Surfacing (pun intended) this on the homepage is smart.
- **"Consoles under $500" quick-link** — speaks directly to the Xbox/Game Pass side of my life. One tap to my ecosystem.
- **"Trade-in offers" pill** — I have old hardware and this is exactly how I offset upgrade costs. It's visible, it's clickable, it doesn't need an explanation.
- **AI search box as hero UX** — as a native tech user, I'd actually use "Ask a question" to navigate directly to what I want rather than browsing nav menus. This is a reasonable bet on a power-user behavior.
- **$500+ in added value** bundle teaser — the Xbox Game Pass + Surface combo visible in the card below hits both my identities simultaneously. That's a tight dual-signal.

## What's weak

- **"Placeholder text" accordion** — this is inexcusable on a live page. It's front and center in the viewport, it's collapsed but clearly labeled with the default dev string. If I weren't already a brand loyalist, I'd bounce and assume this company ships sloppy software.
- **No concrete offer above the fold** — "Explore savings and more" tells me nothing. What percent off? What products? I need a number to anchor my expectations before I commit to scrolling.
- **AI search as primary CTA feels presumptuous** — the prompt field is the biggest interactive element on the page, but it doesn't tell me what kinds of questions it can actually answer. As a tech-native I'll try it, but the value prop of the AI concierge is completely unexplained.
- **No account/loyalty hook** — I have a Microsoft account, a 365 subscription, Game Pass, and an active Surface. None of that is acknowledged. The homepage treats me like a stranger.
- **Hero image is below the fold** — the Surface + Xbox bundle card is only partially visible. I can see colorful gaming imagery and the "$500+ in added value" badge, but it's cut off, making it feel unresolved.

## Recommendations

- **Replace "Placeholder text" with the actual accordion label immediately** — this is a P0 bug; it ships a broken experience to every mobile visitor.
- **Add a specific deal anchor to the hero subhead** — change "Explore savings and more" to something like "Save up to $400 on Surface + Xbox bundles" to give deal-seekers a reason to engage rather than bounce.
- **Show a logged-in state hint** — even just "Sign in to see your member pricing" below the search box would activate loyalists like me who have purchase history and active subscriptions on the account.
- **Move the bundle card fully above the fold on mobile** — the $500+ value story is the most compelling thing on the page; right now it's teasing without delivering. Compress the search section slightly so the bundle card is fully visible at first load.
