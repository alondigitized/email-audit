# Homepage snapshot · www.autozone.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- I opened AutoZone's site on my phone and got a brick wall — "Access is temporarily restricted" with a bot-detection error. No homepage loaded at all. What I'm looking at is a white screen, the AutoZone logo, a generic block message, and an IP address. As someone who uses AutoZone's app regularly to check inventory before I drive over, hitting this kind of wall is a really bad experience that would make me immediately open the app instead — or just call the store.

## What's working

- **AutoZone logo** is displayed at the top — at minimum I know I'm in the right place before the wall hits.
- **"Submit feedback" link** is present — there's at least a visible path to report the problem, which is marginally better than a raw 403 page.

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage failed to render** — I'm staring at "Access is temporarily restricted" on a white background. Nothing to browse, nothing to buy.
- **Bot-detection block is accusatory** — the page lists reasons like "automated bot activity" with my actual IP address (75.140.9.0) printed on screen. That's jarring for a real customer who just tapped a link.
- **No fallback content** — there's no "try again," no link to the app, no store-finder, no phone number. The page is a dead end.
- **No Rewards / loyalty prompt** — as a Rewards cardholder, I'd expect some kind of session recognition or sign-in nudge. Instead I got flagged as a bot.
- **Zero navigation** — not even the top nav bar rendered. I can't reach Parts, Deals, or the Loan-A-Tool page from here.

## Recommendations

- **Add a human-verification escape hatch** — a simple "I'm not a robot" button or CAPTCHA solve path directly on the block page so legitimate customers can get through without abandoning the session.
- **Surface the AutoZone app deep-link** — if the mobile web experience is going to block users, at least show a prominent "Open in the AutoZone App" button so you don't lose the transaction entirely.
- **Replace the accusatory IP-display copy** with something softer — showing a customer their raw IP address feels technical and weird; swap it for plain language like "We had trouble verifying your request — tap below to try again."
- **Add a store-finder or phone number as a fallback** — even on a block page, give me a way to accomplish my goal (finding a part, checking stock) without the full site.
