# Homepage snapshot · www.autozone.com · 2026-09-27
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-27**
## Executive summary

- This isn't a homepage — it's AutoZone's bot-detection wall. I typed in the URL like normal and got an "Access is temporarily restricted" page blaming rapid taps, JavaScript issues, or automated activity on my network. As someone who just wants to look up a part on my phone, this is a dead end before I've even started.

## What's working

- The AutoZone logo at the top is at least crisp and correctly branded, so I know I'm on the real site and not a phishing page.
- The block page is legible and not visually broken — text renders cleanly, no overlapping elements.

## What's weak

- The entire homepage content is replaced by "Access is temporarily restricted" — I can't see a hero, a category, a promo, or a single product.
- The reasons listed ("Rapid taps or clicks," "JavaScript disabled," "Automated bot activity on your network (IP 47.229.200.33)") read like a developer console dump, not customer-facing copy — it exposes my IP address on screen, which feels like a weird thing to show a regular customer.
- There's no "try again" or "reload" button — just a "Submit feedback" link and a UUID-style error ID, which is a dead end for someone standing in their driveway trying to look up a part.
- No path back to the real site is visible at all.

## Recommendations

- Add a simple "Try again" or "Reload page" button so a falsely-flagged customer isn't stuck.
- Drop the IP address from the visible message — that's backend diagnostic info, not something a customer needs to see.
- Soften the copy — "Rapid taps or clicks" and "developer tools" language sounds like it's accusing me of doing something wrong when I just opened the app/browser normally.
- Give a fallback contact option (phone number or "call your local store") since I might need a part right now.
