# Homepage snapshot · www.autozone.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- I pulled up AutoZone on my phone and got slapped with a block page before I even saw the homepage. All I'm looking at is their logo, "Access is temporarily restricted," and a list of reasons why they think I'm a bot — including my IP address sitting right there on screen. I can't tell you what AutoZone is pushing right now because they never let me through the door.

## What's working

- The AutoZone logo renders cleanly at the top — at least I know I'm on the right domain and haven't been phished.
- The "Submit feedback" link is present, so there's a theoretical escape hatch if someone hits this as a false positive.

## What's weak

- The entire homepage is missing — I got a bot-block interstitial instead. Zero merchandising, zero navigation, zero reason to stay.
- The page exposes my IP address (47.41.15.19) in plain text. That's an odd and slightly unsettling thing to show a regular customer.
- "Automated (bot) activity" and "Use of developer or inspection tools" reads like I'm being accused of something. That's not a great way to greet someone who uses your app regularly and has a Rewards card.
- No retry button, no CAPTCHA, no "try again" flow — just a dead end with a feedback link.
- The vast majority of the screen is blank white space below the restriction message, which makes it feel even more broken.

## Recommendations

- Add a CAPTCHA or "I'm not a robot" challenge so legitimate customers can self-serve their way through instead of hitting a permanent wall.
- Replace the cold "access restricted" language with something warmer — "We hit a snag, let's verify you're human" — and provide a clear retry path on the same screen.
- Remove the raw IP address from customer-facing copy; it adds no value for the user and can feel invasive.
- Invest in bot detection that better differentiates app/mobile traffic from headless scrapers — a Rewards member checking parts inventory on their phone should never see this.
