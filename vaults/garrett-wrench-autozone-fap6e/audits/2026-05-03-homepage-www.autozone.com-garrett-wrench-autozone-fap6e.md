AutoZone's bot-detection flagged the request before a single product, promo, or nav item could load, so there is literally nothing to shop here.

## What's working

- **AutoZone logo** renders correctly in the brand's orange/red. At least I know I'm on the right domain.
- **"Submit feedback" link** is present and functional — gives me one escape hatch if I think the block is a mistake.

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is blocked.** I see "Access is temporarily restricted" and nothing else. No parts, no categories, no deals — I can't do anything.
- **Error copy is cold and technical.** "Automated (bot) activity on your network (IP 47.41.15.19)" reads like a sysadmin log, not a message for a real customer who just opened the app.
- **No recovery path for a legitimate user.** The feedback link goes somewhere vague. There's no CAPTCHA challenge, no "try again" button, no redirect to the app — just a dead end.
- **The large empty white space** between the headline and the footer text makes the page feel broken, not intentional.

## Recommendations

- **Add a CAPTCHA or "I'm not a robot" challenge** instead of a hard block. Let me prove I'm human and get back to shopping in 10 seconds rather than losing me entirely.
- **Rewrite the error message in plain English.** Something like "We hit a snag loading the page — tap below to try again" is far less alienating than flagging my IP address.
- **Link directly to the AutoZone app** as a fallback. If the mobile web is having issues, push me to the app where I can still check inventory and get my Rewards points.
- **Surface a minimal fallback header** — just the search bar and a "Find a store" link — so I can at least do something while the block is active.
