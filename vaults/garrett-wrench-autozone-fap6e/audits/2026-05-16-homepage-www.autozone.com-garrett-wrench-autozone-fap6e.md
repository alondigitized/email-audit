# Homepage snapshot · www.autozone.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Well, this is a rough start. I pull up AutoZone on my phone and instead of the homepage I get a "Access is temporarily restricted" error screen — just the logo, a block message, and a wall of reasons why they think I'm a bot. I didn't even get to see a product, a promo, or a nav bar. As far as shopping experiences go, this is about as useful as a locked store door on a Saturday morning.

## What's working

- **AutoZone logo** renders correctly in the brand's signature orange/red — I know I'm in the right place, at least.
- **"Submit feedback" link** is present, so there's technically a path forward if I want to fight with their support team.

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is inaccessible.** I get a bot-block wall with the message "Access is temporarily restricted" — no content, no products, no navigation whatsoever.
- **Bot-detection false positive.** The page lists "Rapid taps or clicks," "JavaScript disabled," "Automated bot activity," and "Use of developer or inspection tools" as possible reasons. I'm just a regular guy on my phone trying to find a brake pad. This is humiliating.
- **No fallback content.** Below the error text there's a massive blank white space — nothing. Not even a link back to a search bar or a store-finder.
- **The error copy is cold and accusatory.** "We detected unusual activity from your device or network" is not the tone you want with a loyal Rewards card holder. It reads like a parking ticket, not a retailer.
- **IP address is exposed in the error message** (`IP 75.140.9.0`) — that's weird and slightly unnerving to see on a shopping site.

## Recommendations

- **Tune the bot-detection threshold for mobile browsers.** A real customer on a phone shouldn't hit this wall. If my normal tap triggered it, the sensitivity is way too aggressive.
- **Add a human-verification fallback (CAPTCHA, SMS verify) instead of a dead end.** Right now the only out is "Submit feedback" — give me a way to prove I'm human and let me shop.
- **Show cached or static content below the restriction notice.** At minimum, render a store-finder or a search bar so a blocked user can still accomplish something.
- **Rewrite the error copy in plain, non-accusatory language.** Something like "We're having trouble loading your page — tap here to try again" is miles better than listing reasons you think I'm a bot.
