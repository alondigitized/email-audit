# Homepage snapshot · www.autozone.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- Man, I didn't even get in the door. AutoZone's site hit me with a bot-block wall — "Access is temporarily restricted" — before I could see a single part, promo, or anything else. The logo renders, some plain text renders, and that's it. This isn't a homepage experience; it's a digital bouncer that just told me to kick rocks.

## What's working

- **The logo renders.** AutoZone's orange-and-white wordmark is there, so at minimum I know I hit the right URL and didn't fat-finger it.
- **The error message is readable.** Plain text, decent font size on mobile — I can actually read why I'm blocked, even if the reason is frustrating.

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is missing.** I got a bot-detection wall (IP: 47.41.15.19 flagged as suspicious). No hero, no parts search, no categories, nothing.
- **"Submit feedback" is the only actionable link.** That's a dead end for a guy who came to find brake pads. There's no "try again," no link to the app, no customer service number.
- **The error copy is technical and cold.** "JavaScript disabled or not working," "Automated (bot) activity" — this reads like a server log, not a customer-facing message. Doesn't feel like the AutoZone I know from the store.
- **Massive white void in the middle of the screen.** There's a huge empty gray-white area between the heading and the footer text block. Looks broken and unfinished.
- **No recovery path.** A real customer hitting this — maybe on a VPN, maybe in a parking lot on spotty cell — has no way forward except to bail.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Try Again" or "Go to App" button on the restriction page.** If you're going to wall someone off, give them a ladder. A deep-link to the AutoZone app would save the session.
- **Humanize the error copy.** Replace the bot-detection bullet list with one plain sentence and a phone number or store locator link. "Something went wrong on our end — try again or call 1-800-AUTOZONE."
- **Shorten the dead space.** The massive blank area between the heading and the footer copy looks like a broken layout. Compress it so the page feels intentional, not crashed.
- **Tune the bot-detection threshold.** If a mobile user on a standard carrier IP is tripping this, the sensitivity is too high. That's real revenue walking out the door.
