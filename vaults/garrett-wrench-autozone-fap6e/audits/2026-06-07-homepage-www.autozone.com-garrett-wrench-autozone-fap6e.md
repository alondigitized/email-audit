# Homepage snapshot · www.autozone.com · 2026-06-07
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-07**
## Executive summary

- I opened AutoZone's site and got a wall. Not a homepage — a bot-detection block page that says "Access is temporarily restricted." The logo loaded, the red-and-orange AutoZone branding is there, and that's it. The entire below-fold area is a gray error explanation with my IP address in it. Whatever I came here to buy — brake pads, filters, a scan-tool rental — I can't get to any of it.

## What's working

- **AutoZone logo** rendered correctly in the brand's signature red/orange — at least I know I'm on the right domain.
- **"Submit feedback" link** is present and functional-looking, which is at least something to do when you're stuck.

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage** — nothing loaded. No nav, no hero, no categories, no deals. Zero content.
- **"Access is temporarily restricted" headline** is the coldest possible welcome for someone who just opened the app to look up a part number.
- **Bot-detection message lists my IP address** (75.140.9.0) publicly on screen — that's weird and a little unsettling as a user.
- **No retry button or alternative path** — there's no "tap here to verify you're human," no CAPTCHA, just a dead end and an error ID.
- **Huge dead whitespace** between the error headline and the explanation block — the page looks broken even beyond the restriction logic.

## Recommendations

- **Add a human-verification flow (CAPTCHA or tap-to-confirm)** instead of a flat block — I came here to buy something, give me a way back in without closing the app and trying again cold.
- **Replace the IP-address disclosure** with something more user-friendly — seeing my IP printed out feels invasive, not helpful.
- **Put a direct phone number or store-finder CTA** on the block page — if I can't shop online right now, at least route me to the nearest store or the app.
- **Tighten the bot-detection threshold on mobile** — a real customer opening the app shouldn't hit this wall on a first visit.
