# Homepage snapshot · www.autozone.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Well, this is not a great start — I opened AutoZone's site and got hit with a wall saying "Access is temporarily restricted." No hero, no parts search, no deals, nothing. The only thing I'm looking at is a security block page with an error ID and a "Submit feedback" link. As a guy who just needs to know if the store near me has a starter for a '16 Tacoma, this is a dead stop.

## What's working

- The AutoZone logo renders at the top, so I at least know I'm on the right domain.
- The "Submit feedback" link is present — at least there's a path to report the problem.

## What's weak

- The entire homepage is blocked. I see zero products, zero categories, zero search bar. I came to check parts inventory and got locked out.
- The error copy reads: "We detected unusual activity from your device or network" — accusatory tone that makes me feel like I did something wrong when I literally just opened the app.
- The listed reasons include "JavaScript disabled or not working" and "automated (bot) activity" — neither of those is my fault as a normal mobile user, and seeing my IP address (75.140.9.0) exposed in the message feels weird and invasive.
- No fallback whatsoever — no "try the app," no store locator link, no phone number. If I need a part today, I'm stuck.
- The page is mostly white space with a dense text block at the bottom. Terrible layout, looks broken.

## Recommendations

- **Add a graceful fallback CTA** — even a simple "Call your local store" button with a tap-to-call link would give me an exit ramp when the main page blocks.
- **Remove the raw IP address from the user-facing error message** — showing "IP 75.140.9.0" to a regular shopper adds zero value and feels like a surveillance red flag.
- **Rewrite the block copy in plain language** — something like "We're having trouble loading the page — try refreshing" is less accusatory than listing bot-detection reasons at a paying customer.
- **Implement a CAPTCHA or challenge page** instead of a dead block — let me prove I'm human and get through to the actual site rather than leaving me at a wall with no recourse.
