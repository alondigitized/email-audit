# Limited-Time Offer: Earn 4 Free Nights
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-offer credit card acquisition email. It is a repeat of the 2026-11-06 "Limited-Time Offer: Earn 4 Free Nights" send, and the subject line is identical. The hero is strong. "EARN 4 FREE NIGHTS" sits in large type over a balloon-at-dusk Santa Fe shot, with the card pictured below it. The offer is obvious in about two seconds.
- The weak point is the fit with the persona. Helena is a Globalist member with 0 current points, and the email still treats her as a prospect for a $95-fee card. The Globalist badge in the header shows the email knows her status. The body never uses it. The spend requirement ($5,000 in 3 months) and the "Offer Details / $95 Annual Fee" disclosures are small and sit below the CTA. The offer is clear but not especially honest-feeling.
- Overall this is a competent but undifferentiated resend. It is the same creative and same subject as 18 days ago, and the cadence is noticeable next to the Delta, Milestone and 20,000-point sends in between. I'd call it a 6, in line with the earlier send.

## What's working

- The hero is big and unmistakable: "4 FREE NIGHTS" with a "LIMITED-TIME OFFER" tag, and the card is visible.
- The primary CTA is a single blue "APPLY NOW" button, and the "Offer ends December 17, 2026" deadline sits directly above it.
- The personalized header ("Hi Helena Globalist · Member | 0 Current Points") plus a "Book Now" link makes it feel like a member email.
- The benefits section is well structured. Free night, Discoverist status and 5 Tier-Qualifying Night Credits each get an icon and a short line.
- The 9X / 2X / 1X earn tiers are scannable and easy to compare.
- The render is clean, with no broken images and no overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The $5,000 spend in 3 months is buried in body copy below the hero, and the "Offer Details" and "$95 Annual Fee" notes are in small gray text.
- The email shows she is a Globalist and has 0 points, but pitches her a Discoverist benefit ("Discoverist status, included"). That is a lower tier than the one she already holds, so it reads as irrelevant or tone-deaf to her.
- The body copy is generic ("a relaxing resort stay, a long weekend somewhere you've never been"), and the Santa Fe hero is not tied to anything she has shown interest in.
- The lower half is long and repeats itself. The disclosure block appears twice, and the brand logo grid adds length without adding a reason to click.
- There is only one CTA, and it appears only once, mid-email. Nothing at the bottom invites a second click after the benefits section.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment the send by status. For Globalist members, drop the Discoverist benefit and lead with what a card adds on top of elite status: 5 Tier-Qualifying Night Credits per year and the free night.
- 2. Put the spend condition in the hero or directly beside the CTA, for example "after $5,000 in 3 months".
- 3. Add a second "APPLY NOW" button after the earn tiers, and make the copy specific, such as "Get my 4 free nights".
- 4. Don't resend the identical subject. Vary the subject and hero on each send, and add urgency tied to the December 17 deadline as it gets closer.
- 5. Cut the logo grid, or move it below the footer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `4 free nights end Dec 17, Helena`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Globalist status + 4 free nights`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $5,000 in 3 months, get 4 nights at any Category 1–4 hotel.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus a free night every year and 5 tier credits. Apply by Dec 17.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, responsive layout with `role="presentation"` tables, a `lang="en"` attribute and a viewport meta tag. The main technical gaps are an empty `<title>`, an unverifiable tracking and unsubscribe setup, and an image with no alt text. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and merge-token checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified destinations:** 25 links go through `click.hyatt.com/q/...~~/...~/...` click-redirect URLs, and the QA run skipped the HTTP probe on all of them. I can't confirm that any of them resolves, or where it lands.
- **Plain-text bloat:** 10,902 of 14,947 plain-text characters (about 73%) are URLs. The long tracking URLs inflate the text part, which can hurt spam scoring.
- **Image through the click domain:** the image flagged by QA has a `click.hyatt.com/q/pHtZwmxknL6m7JkWEfh5SA~~/AAQRxRA~/ek5WkMc1...` src. That is a tracking-redirect URL, not a normal image asset, so it may be an open-tracking pixel. If it is, the missing alt is harmless. If it is a visible image, it depends on the redirect service to render.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty, which hurts accessibility and the browser-view tab title.
- **Missing alt text:** the QA-flagged image (src `click.hyatt.com/q/pHtZwmxknL6m7JkWEfh5SA~~/...`) has no `alt`. If it is a pixel, use `alt=""`. If it is content, give it descriptive alt text.
- **External font:** `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Noto+Sans">` sits inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. Gmail and several other clients strip or ignore it, so those clients fall back to the system font.
- **Good practice noted:** `role="presentation"` is on the layout tables, the hidden preheader uses `mso-hide:all`, and there are mobile breakpoints at 620px and 480px. I found no problems there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unresolved tokens such as `{{...}}`, `%%...%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The source is truncated, so tokens further down can't be ruled out.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe:** QA found no `List-Unsubscribe` header, and no `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click). The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. Treat this as unconfirmed, not as a sender defect.
- **Authentication:** `Authentication-Results` wasn't captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain (`loyalty.hyatt.com`) and tracking domain (`click.hyatt.com`) share the `hyatt.com` parent domain, which is consistent.
- **Footer:** the visible, truncated source doesn't show the footer, so I can't verify the in-body unsubscribe link or the physical postal address.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters:** can't be assessed. All links are wrapped in opaque redirects, and the QA run skipped probing them, so final URLs and any UTM parameters are unknown.
- **Landing page alignment:** unverified for the same reason.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Resolve a sample of the `click.hyatt.com` links (follow the redirect chain) to check destinations, UTM parameters and status codes.
2. Fill in `<title>` with a meaningful value, such as the subject line.
3. Add `alt` to the flagged image: `alt=""` if it's a tracking pixel, descriptive text otherwise.
4. Check the raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, since the relay may have dropped them.
5. Confirm the footer has a working unsubscribe link and a postal address.
6. Make sure the plain-text part reads sensibly despite the long tracking URLs.
7. Make sure Noto Sans has a web-safe fallback stack, because Gmail ignores the Google Fonts link.
