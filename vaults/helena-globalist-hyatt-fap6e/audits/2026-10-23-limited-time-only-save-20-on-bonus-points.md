# Limited Time Only: Save 20% on Bonus Points
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-23**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-offer loyalty send: 20% off Bonus Points, one blue "Buy Points Now" button, and a "Fall for somewhere new" award-night showcase below it. The pitch works because the 0-point header ("Hi Helena Globalist, Member | 0 Current Points") gives a reason to buy, and the redemption tiles show what the points are for.
- The weak spot is urgency and novelty. This is the fourth 20%-off-points beat in about three months, after 2026-08-07, 2026-09-03 and 2026-10-02. The "Limited Time Only" subject overstates a deadline of December 21, which is two months out. The hero is a generic "Be More Inspired" beach shot that doesn't mention the offer, so the 20% headline sits below the first scroll on mobile. The 55K annual cap and the 60-day membership rule are only in the fine print.
- Verdict: competent and on-brand, but a mid-pack send. It would score higher if the offer sat in the hero and the deadline were real.

## What's working

- The headline "Save 20% when you buy Bonus Points" is plain and specific, and the single blue "BUY POINTS NOW" button is unambiguous.
- The header bar shows member status and a 0-point balance, which personalizes the pitch.
- The four redemption tiles (35K, 30K, 25K, 15K points) show concrete "free nights" values, so the points feel worth buying.
- The gifting callout adds a second reason to buy without competing with the main CTA.
- The layout renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlapping text. The brand voice and look are consistent.

## What's weak

- The hero image carries no offer copy, so the first screen is a pretty beach scene and the 20% message is below it.
- "Limited Time Only" against an "Offer ends December 21, 2026" deadline reads as manufactured urgency, especially after the 2026-10-02 "Last Chance To Save 20% on Points" send.
- The destination tiles are mostly unrelated to the offer. The "Explore >" links are generic, and the tiles don't say what 20% off actually costs in dollars.
- The caps are buried in the terms: 1,000 to 55,000 points per year, a 60-day membership minimum, and the non-refundable and non-transferable rules.
- Cadence fatigue: this is the same mechanic as three earlier sends, with no new angle.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the offer into the hero. Put "20% off Bonus Points" on the image, or replace the generic "Be More Inspired" art with a headline-plus-CTA hero, so the offer is visible on the first mobile screen.
- 2. Add a price anchor, such as "10,000 points: $X, now $Y". This makes the 20% tangible.
- 3. Use a real deadline: a countdown or a date-driven cutoff, or a tiered bonus for buying early. Drop "Limited Time Only" if the deadline stays at December 21.
- 4. Tie the tiles to the offer, for example "Buy 35K points and sleep free at Alila Mayakoba," with the points-to-cash math.
- 5. Surface the key limits (55K cap, 60 days of membership) in one line near the CTA.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Helena, 20% off points through Dec 21`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 35K-point beach stay costs 20% less`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Buy 10,000 points for less and book a free night at Alila Mayakoba.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Top up before year-end. Gift points to someone too.`
