# Get Ready: More Possibilities, More Adventures
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a program-change announcement, not a promotion. Hyatt tells members that on January 1, 2027, Milestone Rewards will start at 10 nights instead of 20. It also adds Tier-Qualifying Night Credits, a 365-day award validity window and an enhanced Pay My Way. The "10 qualifying nights (previously 20 nights)" block is the best part. It is large, scannable and concrete. The email has no deadline and no action to take, and the primary CTA is a generic "LEARN MORE".
- The header strip shows "Hi Helena Globalist | Member | 0 Current Points." Greeting a Globalist, the top tier, with a zero-point balance looks odd. It undercuts the personalization and may be a render quirk. The hero says "BE MORE Here" over a couple sharing food. It is warm and on brand, but it says nothing about the news. The headline "Coming to World of Hyatt in 2027" doesn't name the benefit either.
- Compared with the 2026-11-14 send ("Surprise Helena! You're Eligible to Earn Up to 20,000 Bonus Points"), which had one clear offer, this is informational and slower to read. It is also the third loyalty-mechanics message in a week, after the Delta partnership send on 2026-11-18. The brand logo wall near the bottom is long and adds little. Judgement: a solid announcement that is poorly packaged, and it should be a quick teaser pointing to one page.

## What's working

- The "10 qualifying nights (previously 20 nights)" callout is large and shows the before and after clearly.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.
- The blue "LEARN MORE" button is clear and sits right after the intro copy.
- Setting Milestone Rewards and Tier-Qualifying Night Credits in big type gives the eye two clear stops.
- The pairing of lead-in labels with bold benefit lines ("Accelerate status with new…") is easy to skim.

## What's weak

- The email has no urgency and no action for the member to take. It is purely informational.
- The "Hi Helena Globalist … 0 Current Points" header looks inconsistent for a top-tier member.
- The hero image and "BE MORE Here" copy say nothing about the news.
- Both "LEARN MORE" links go to the same generic destination, and neither says what the reader gets.
- The brand logo wall is large and irrelevant to the news, and its tiny logos and footnote are hard to read.
- The body copy is vague ("greater access, options and value") before the specifics arrive.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the news in the hero. Replace "BE MORE Here" with "Milestone Rewards now start at 10 nights" and use the "previously 20" contrast above the fold.
- 2. Add a personal angle: "You're X nights from your first Milestone," so it feels relevant and prompts action.
- 3. Make the CTA specific, such as "See Your 2027 Benefits" or "Check Your Milestone Progress."
- 4. Shorten the intro paragraphs to two lines and move the details into the benefit block.
- 5. Cut or shrink the logo wall and give the space to a "Book a stay to start earning" module.
- 6. Check the header: hide the points balance if it is zero, or fix the data.

# Technical Audit: World of Hyatt, "Get Ready: More Possibilities, More Adventures"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a standard table-based, Stensul/BEE-style build with `lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag, MSO conditional comments and mobile media queries. The confirmed defects are one image with no alt text and an empty `<title>`. Several QA warnings (unsubscribe headers, auth results) come from the AgentMail relay and can't be confirmed as sender faults.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Not verifiable:** All 22 tracking links on `click.hyatt.com` were skipped, so redirect targets, status codes and dead links weren't tested.
- **Anomaly:** The flagged image's `src` is `https://click.hyatt.com/q/mF7d8dH4Okyuk-8h5tf6uw~~/AAQRxRA~/YH1l9xdSspabcXGWsqBiSyMa4DskAPkwLO_vyjS4roBmg_98xvO8Fx_VaEvy…`. That is a click-redirect path (`/q/…`), not an image asset path. Either the image is served through the tracking domain, or a click URL is wrongly used as an image source. The truncated source doesn't show which.
- **Plain-text part:** 9060 of 12364 characters (~73%) are URLs, over the 70% threshold. This is probably caused by the long wrapped tracking URLs, and it can raise spam-filter scores on the text part.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt (confirmed):** The image with `src` starting `https://click.hyatt.com/q/mF7d8dH4Okyuk-8h5tf6uw~~/AAQRxRA~/YH1l9xd…` has no `alt` attribute. Screen readers will announce the URL, and the image will show nothing if blocked.
- **Empty title (confirmed):** `<title></title>` is empty. This is minor, but some clients use it as a fallback label.
- **Web font dependency:** `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Noto+Sans">` is wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. It is ignored by Gmail and several other clients, so the font stack must have a fallback. The truncated source doesn't show whether it does.
- **Positives:** `lang="en"` is set, a viewport meta tag is present, MSO/VML namespaces and `PixelsPerInch` are declared, and mobile breakpoints exist at `max-width:620px`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The truncated source can't rule out later sections.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post:** Both are reported missing. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. If they are really absent, the email is missing RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe, which Gmail and Yahoo require from bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found. SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown for the same relay reason. The From domain `loyalty.hyatt.com` couldn't be checked for alignment.
- **Body footer:** The visible unsubscribe link and postal address are not in the truncated source, so they couldn't be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. Every link is wrapped in a `click.hyatt.com` redirect, and the probe was skipped. UTM parameters and landing-page destinations would be applied after the redirect and can't be seen in the source.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a descriptive `alt` to the image at `click.hyatt.com/q/mF7d8dH4Okyuk-8h5tf6uw~~/…`, or `alt=""` if it is decorative. Check whether a click-tracking URL is being used as an image source.
2. Populate `<title>` with the subject or a short descriptor.
3. Re-run the QA against the raw message, not the relay copy, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
4. Follow the 22 tracking redirects with a real HTTP client to confirm final URLs, status codes and UTM parameters.
5. Shorten the tracked URLs or add more plain-text copy to bring the URL share of the text part under 70%.
6. Fetch the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer (unsubscribe link, physical address), font fallbacks and any merge tokens.
