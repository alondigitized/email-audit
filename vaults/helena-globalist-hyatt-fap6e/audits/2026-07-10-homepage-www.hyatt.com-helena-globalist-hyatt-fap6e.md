# Homepage snapshot · www.hyatt.com · 2026-07-10
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-10**
## Executive summary

- The page is completely blank — a white void, nothing rendered. No header, no hero, no navigation, no loyalty hook, no anything. For a brand where I'm checking my points balance and scoping out a Park Hyatt redemption, this is an immediate credibility hit. Whatever Hyatt is pushing right now, it did not reach my phone.

## What's working

- Nothing. The page delivered a blank white screen. There is no element to commend.

## What's weak

- **Complete render failure** — every pixel on screen is white. No hero, no nav, no footer, nothing. Whatever caused this (JS bundle error, failed network request, bot-gate, geo-block) the end result for me is zero content.
- **No fallback state** — a resilient mobile homepage should at minimum render a static shell or an error message. There is not even a spinner or skeleton visible.
- **Zero loyalty presence** — as a Globalist I expect to see my status acknowledged or at least a "Sign in" prompt. Neither exists.
- **No search bar or navigation** — my standard entry point on Hyatt.com is the search-by-dates flow. It's gone.

## Recommendations

- **Fix the render failure first, full stop** — before any creative work, engineering needs to identify why the mobile viewport is returning a blank page. A blank homepage is a P0 incident, not a UX note.
- **Implement a static HTML fallback** — if the JS bundle fails, render at minimum a logo, a "Search hotels" link, and a "Sign in" button so Globalists aren't staring at white.
- **Surface the loyalty sign-in shell in the static layer** — even a cached header with my points balance would tell me the site knows who I am when JS recovers.
- **Add a visible loading / error state** — a skeleton screen or "Having trouble loading? Tap to retry" message costs almost nothing and saves the bounce.
