# Limited-Time Offer: Earn 4 Free Nights
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- This is a credit card acquisition email, and it's a departure from Hyatt's usual loyalty-promo cadence. The hero is strong: "EARN 4 FREE NIGHTS" in large type over a balloon-at-dusk Santa Fe shot, with the card pictured below. The offer is concrete. Spend $5,000 in the first 3 months and get 4 free nights at any Category 1-4 hotel, with the offer ending December 17, 2026.
- The recipient is "Helena Globalist" with 0 current points, so this is a points-light member being pitched a $5,000 spend threshold. That's a hard ask for this audience. The $95 annual fee appears only in small print under the button. The body is clear and well organized, but it stacks three benefit blocks and an earn table before the footer, and the only button is the single "Apply Now" near the top.
- Judgement: a clean, credible card pitch with a good hero. It's undercut by the $5,000 spend gate, which isn't acknowledged in the hero, and by the lack of any personalization beyond the name. Compared with the 2026-10-23 "Save 20% on Bonus Points" send (7/10), this one is a harder sell to a 0-point member.

## What's working

- The hero is bold and instantly readable: "EARN 4 FREE NIGHTS" with the card image anchors the offer.
- There is one primary CTA, a blue "Apply Now" button that stands out against the white body.
- The offer has a credible deadline: "Offer ends December 17, 2026."
- The benefits are scannable: free night every year, Discoverist status, and 5 Tier-Qualifying Night Credits.
- The 9X / 2X / 1X earn table is easy to parse at a glance.

## What's weak

- The $5,000 spend in 3 months is buried in the body paragraph, not the hero. A 0-point member will see a gap between the headline and the reality.
- The $95 annual fee and "Offer Details" are tiny gray text under the button, which hurts honesty.
- The "Book Now >" link in the top banner competes with "Apply Now" and points to a different intent.
- The brand logo grid at the bottom is long and adds no conversion value.
- There is only one CTA button, and it sits above the benefits. After the benefit and earn sections, the reader has no second button.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the spend threshold in the hero: "Spend $5,000 in 3 months, earn 4 free nights." This sets expectations and filters unqualified clicks.
- 2. Add a second "Apply Now" button after the earn table, where the reader has finished the benefits.
- 3. Lift the $95 fee into the body in readable type, next to the offer.
- 4. Remove "Book Now" from the banner on acquisition sends so there is one action.
- 5. Cut the logo grid down to a single line.
- **Subject Alt A:** `4 free nights with the World of Hyatt Card`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Helena, 4 free nights end Dec 17`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $5,000 in 3 months. Redeem at any Category 1-4 hotel.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus a free night every year and Discoverist status.`
