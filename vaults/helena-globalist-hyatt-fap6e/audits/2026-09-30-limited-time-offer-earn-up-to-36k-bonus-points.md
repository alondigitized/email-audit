# Limited-Time Offer: Earn up to 36K Bonus Points
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- This is a solid, clean loyalty promo with one clear mechanic. Register, then earn 3,000 Bonus Points for every three eligible nights, capped at 36K, on stays from September 29 to December 15, 2026. The hero is a moody Dream Nashville bar shot with the "Be More Here" script. The bordered offer box and the blue "REGISTER WITH ONE CLICK" button put the eye on the offer quickly.
- The email is weaker on relevance. The member bar reads "Hi Helena Globalist | 0 Current Points", so personalization is present but adds nothing for a zero-balance member. The hero shows a bar rather than a destination or a stay. The second module pushes the credit card at 60K points, which competes with the main offer. This is the same card upsell Hyatt has been running since August 11's "Last Chance: Earn Up to 75K Bonus Points" send. The brand-portfolio logo grid at the bottom is decorative and doesn't help anyone click.
- Judgement: competent and honest, but a generic 3-night-stay promo. It is stronger than the recent Account Summary digests (September 26, 5/10) because it has a single clear action. It stays mid-range because nothing in it is tailored to the reader.

## What's working

- The offer box is clear: "Earn 3,000 Bonus Points for every three eligible nights, up to 36K Bonus Points."
- The dates are spelled out: "Stay between September 29 and December 15, 2026."
- The "REGISTER WITH ONE CLICK" button is high-contrast and its copy is specific. It says what the action is and that it is easy.
- The headline, "Take the trip by December 15", gives a deadline that feels credible.
- The blue member bar with a "Book Now >" link fits the brand and keeps a booking path visible.

## What's weak

- The hero is a bar interior with "Be More Here" script. It says nothing about earning points or travel, so the offer isn't visible until you scroll.
- The 36K in the subject is a cap that takes three-night stays to reach. That means 36 nights, which is unrealistic for most members. The body copy hides this in "up to."
- The credit card module ("Earn up to 60,000 Bonus Points") is a second offer that pulls attention away from the register CTA.
- The "0 Current Points" line is a weak personalization signal. It reminds the reader they have nothing.
- The logo grid is long and adds no offer or product detail.
- "Terms apply" is small and there is no visible list of exclusions.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in the hero. Overlay "Earn 3K Bonus Points every 3 nights" on the image, or replace the "Be More Here" script with it. Right now the hero and the offer are separate.
- 2. Reframe the cap. Lead with the first milestone ("Your first 3 nights = 3,000 points") instead of the 36K ceiling, which looks like bait.
- 3. Remove or demote the credit card module in a stay-driven send. It cuts the register button's click share.
- 4. Add a real destination or a "Where to stay" link to a hotel search. "Book Now" in the top bar is the only booking path.
- 5. Cut the logo grid to a single row or a link.
- 6. Handle the 0-points state with a message like "Start earning today" instead of showing the zero.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Register: 3,000 bonus points per 3-night stay`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stay Sept 29–Dec 15 and earn 3K points every third night. Register in one click.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `One click to register, then every 3 nights earns you 3,000 points.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Braze-built, table-based layout (600px, `role="presentation"`, `lang="en"`) with click tracking through `click.hyatt.com`. The confirmed issues are three images without alt text and an empty `<title>`. The compliance and authentication warnings come from relay capture limits, so they can't be confirmed as sender defects. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer and unsubscribe markup could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 23 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are tracking/click-redirect domains. Their destinations are unverified.
- Links are wrapped as `https://click.hyatt.com/q/<token>~~/AAQRxRA~/...`. This is normal ESP click-wrapping, but the final landing URLs can't be seen in the source or QA data.
- The plain-text part is 9026/12055 characters URLs (about 75%), which trips the >70% URL warning. This is a side effect of the long wrapped tracking URLs and is low risk.
- No broken links were confirmed.

  - An image whose `src` is the tracked URL `https://click.hyatt.com/q/wr_Ry2OlUcHZ-ZvESfL4wg~~/AAQRxRA~/shvJqAD_fpV07nn2tAnQxP2fiwH1n_4ZZVjedUn_-am_Kb4bGP...`.

- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** neither header was found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture them. This is unconfirmed rather than a sender defect. Check the raw original message. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. It is expected to be stripped by the relay. Verify from the raw headers or a seed test.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address:** these are outside the truncated HTML, so they could not be verified.
- **Sender:** `worldofhyatt@loyalty.hyatt.com` uses a subdomain of the brand domain, which is consistent with authenticated sending.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters and landing pages could not be verified. Every CTA is wrapped in a `click.hyatt.com` redirect and skipped by the probe.
- Nothing in the source or QA data contradicts the offer. The preheader references "eligible nights completed by Dec. 15, 2026", which is a claim to check against the landing page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the two `braze-images.com/.../69e418e8dbb9f600aee21673/original.png` instances. Use `alt=""` only if they are purely decorative. Also add alt text to the tracked `click.hyatt.com/q/wr_Ry2OlUcHZ-ZvESfL4wg~~` image.

3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` on the original message, outside the relay. Also confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment.
4. Resolve the tracking redirects manually or with a follow-redirect probe. Confirm the final landing pages, UTM parameters and the Dec. 15, 2026 offer terms.
