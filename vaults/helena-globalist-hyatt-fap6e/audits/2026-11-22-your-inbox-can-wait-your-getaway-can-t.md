# Your Inbox Can Wait—Your Getaway Can’t
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a roundup email that restates four Hyatt promos from the past six weeks. It has a lifestyle subject line, a generic "Jet-Setter" hero, and four equal-weight offer cards. The render is clean and on-brand. But nothing is the star, and a subscriber who got Hyatt's Nov 13, Nov 14 and Nov 6 sends will recognize every card here.
- The subject, "Your Inbox Can Wait—Your Getaway Can't," is charming but says nothing about an offer. The body then asks the reader to choose among four: 36K points, a Delta tier credit, a personal 20K points offer, and the credit card's 4 free nights. That is a catalog, not a campaign. The personalized 20,000-point offer is the strongest asset, and it sits third.
- Judgment: competent and polished, but it adds nothing new. It reads as a cadence filler between the Nov 18 Delta send and the Nov 20 program-change announcement.

## What's working

- The member bar ("Hi Helena Globalist, Member | 0 Current Points" with "Book Now") personalizes the top and gives a persistent booking path.
- The Kotor Bay hero is a high-quality image, consistent with the Oct 30 "Be More Jet-Setter" creative.
- Each offer card uses the same layout: image left, bold headline, short copy, blue text link. They scan quickly.
- Real deadlines are stated (Dec 15 and Dec 31, 2026), so the urgency is credible.
- The "exclusive offer just for you" card is the only one framed as personal.

## What's weak

- Four offers with four different CTAs split attention. "Explore All Offers," "Register Now" (twice), and "Learn More" (twice) compete with the header "Book Now."
- The hero carries no offer. The first real value, 36,000 points, doesn't appear until about a quarter of the way down.
- "Stop scrolling, start planning" is filler. It restates the subject without adding specifics.
- The 36K promo is a recycled send (Sep 30, Oct 22, Nov 13), and the card offer repeats Nov 6. This is fatigue, not news.
- "0 Current Points" in the member bar is deflating for a Globalist-tier member. It also undercuts the points-earning pitch.
- The "Learn More" links are weak, and the Delta card gives no hint of why linking accounts matters.
- The long brand-logo grid adds length without adding action.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer. Make the personalized "earn 2,000 points per night, up to 20,000" the hero and headline, and demote the others to a single "More ways to earn" row.
- 2. Put the offer in the hero image or directly below it, with one primary button such as "Register for 20,000 Points."
- 3. Cut or relabel the "0 Current Points" display, or hide it when the balance is zero.
- 4. Replace "Learn More" with specific verb-and-noun CTAs: "Register for Bonus Points," "Link Delta SkyMiles," "Apply for the Card."
- 5. Drop the logo grid below the fold, or collapse it into a one-line link.
- 6. Suppress or rotate the 36K card when the recipient has already received it.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Helena, earn up to 20,000 bonus points`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your getaway pays 2,000 points a night`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your personal offer: 2,000 bonus points per qualifying night through Dec 15.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus a free Tier-Qualifying Night credit when you link Delta.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Hyatt (World of Hyatt) template with a responsive 620px breakpoint, `lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag and a hidden preheader. The only confirmed source issues are an empty `<title>` and 5 images without alt text. The compliance and authentication warnings come from header capture, so they can't be confirmed from this data. The HTML is truncated, so the footer and body links were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **29 links were not probed.** They go through the click-redirect domain `click.hyatt.com/q/...`, so destinations, redirect chains and 404s are unverified. This is an audit coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **The plain-text part is 75% URLs** (10,450 of 13,845 chars). This follows from the long encoded `click.hyatt.com/q/<token>~~/...` URLs being repeated in the text alternative. It makes the text part hard to read and can add mild spam-filter risk.
- **A tracking-pixel-style image is in the body.** The `<img>` with src `https://click.hyatt.com/q/6-78HGKzdMlabQbhnXPvdg~~/AAQRxRA~/BCzHfDbT_...` appears to be an open-tracking or redirect asset. It has no alt attribute.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** It is confirmed in the source.
- **Missing alt text on 5 images.** Four are content images on `assets.hyatt.com`:
  - `CUNIF-P0479-Aerial-Hotel-Pool.4x3.jpg`
  - `WOH-P0616-Front-Desk-Check-In-Coworkers-Luggage.4x...`
  - `WOH-P0046-Lifestyle-City-Couple.4x3.jpg`
  - `TAMAY-P255-Signature-Hot-Air-Balloons.LargeCard.4x...`

  The fifth is the `click.hyatt.com` image above. With images off or a screen reader, the four content images have no text equivalent. Each should have a descriptive `alt`, and the tracking image should have `alt=""`.
- **Stray empty `<p>` elements.** The source has an empty `<p>` wrapping only template comments, plus leftover comments such as `<!-- Name Salutations Script -->` and `<!-- Colors and Background Image based on Tier Code -->`. This is cosmetic and adds page weight.
- **Positives:** MSO/VML namespaces and `OfficeDocumentSettings` are present. The `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` and `#MessageViewBody a` resets are present. The preheader div uses `display:none; mso-hide:all`, and the zero-width-character padding div follows it.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible source. Name, tier and points logic appear only as comments (for example "Member Vs Non Member"), which suggests it was resolved server-side. The truncated portion is not covered.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes this may be an AgentMail relay artifact, so it is **unconfirmed**. It needs checking against the raw original message. If the headers are genuinely absent, the email doesn't meet Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender one-click unsubscribe requirements.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** It is expected to be stripped by the relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The `From` domain is `loyalty.hyatt.com`. Confirm alignment from the original headers.
- **Footer not inspected.** The physical address and the unsubscribe link are beyond the truncation point, so CAN-SPAM body requirements are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not verifiable. All 29 links are wrapped in `click.hyatt.com` redirects, and the final destinations and any UTM parameters are not exposed. No broken or mismatched landing pages are confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four `assets.hyatt.com` images, and use `alt=""` on the `click.hyatt.com` tracking image.
2. Set a meaningful `<title>`, for example the subject line.
3. Check the raw original message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass for `loyalty.hyatt.com`). Treat the QA warnings as unresolved until then.
4. Follow the redirects on the 29 tracking links, in a headless or manual run, to confirm the destinations resolve (HTTP 200) and carry the intended UTM parameters.
5. Review the plain-text alternative. Shorter link tokens or fewer repeated URLs would improve its readability.
6. Strip empty `<p>` elements and unused template comments from the production output.
