# Surprise Helena! You’re Eligible to Earn Up to 20,000 Bonus Points
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, personalized, single-offer loyalty send. The subject promises "Up to 20,000 Bonus Points," and the render delivers it: a bold blue banner reading "Earn 2,000 Bonus Points per night up to a total of 20,000," followed by one button, "REGISTER WITH 1-CLICK." Compared with the 2026-11-13 "Earn up to 36K Bonus Points" send, the mechanic is easier to read (2,000 per night, capped at 20,000), and the "just for you" framing makes it feel targeted.
- The weak spot is the hero. It's a generic "Be More Here" lifestyle shot of two women with umbrellas. It has no offer, no destination and no link to the promo. The member bar also shows "0 Current Points," which undercuts a points-earning pitch. The brand-portfolio logo wall below the CTA is long and adds nothing. A subscriber who has seen three near-identical bonus-point sends since September (the 2026-09-30 and 2026-10-22 "Earn up to 36K" emails, then 2026-11-13) will read this as the fourth in the series.
- Overall it's solid but forgettable: the offer is strong, the execution is mostly clear, and the creative is brand wallpaper. I'd call it a 6.

## What's working

- The offer block is the visual anchor: a large "20,000" on a saturated blue field, and the eye lands on it right after the hero.
- A single clear CTA, "REGISTER WITH 1-CLICK," states the action and the low friction.
- Personalization is visible in two places: "Hi Helena Globalist" in the member bar and "Helena, here's an exclusive offer, just for you."
- The deadline is concrete and bolded: "February 10, 2027."
- The "Free nights start at only 3,000 points" and "Suite upgrades start at 6,000 points" callouts give the points a tangible meaning.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or layout bugs.

## What's weak

- The hero doesn't carry the offer. It reads as brand campaign art, and the promo sits about 330px lower.
- "0 Current Points" in the member bar is a poor look for an earn-points pitch, and it sits next to a "Book Now >" link that competes with the main CTA.
- The offer's key condition, that points come only from stays at participating hotels, is buried in the legal block. The logo wall reads as decoration, and the "participating hotels" link is tiny.
- The "Register and complete the qualifying activity" copy is vague. It never says "stay 10 nights."
- The footer legal copy is dense and long, and it carries the real terms (the 2,000 per Eligible Night mechanic and the 20,000 cap).

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in the hero: overlay "Earn up to 20,000 Bonus Points" on the image, or replace the generic shot with the offer, so the value is visible without scrolling.
- 2. Replace the vague "qualifying activity" line with a plain one: "Stay up to 10 eligible nights by Feb 10, 2027 and earn 2,000 points each night."
- 3. Handle the "0 Current Points" state. Either hide the balance or reframe it, for example "Start earning today."
- 4. Cut or shrink the logo wall, and make "participating hotels" a visible button-style link.
- 5. Change the CTA to something more specific, such as "Register for 20,000 Points."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Helena, 20,000 bonus points are waiting`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Earn 2,000 points a night through Feb 10`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Register in one click, then earn 2,000 points on every eligible night.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your offer ends Feb 10, 2027. Free nights start at 3,000 points.`

The source is also truncated, so the footer and most links were not reviewed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 19 links on `click.hyatt.com` (a click-redirect domain) were skipped by the HTTP probe, so their destinations and redirect chains are **unverified**. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- The tracked links use opaque `/q/<token>~~/AAQRxRA~/...` paths. Destination URLs and UTM parameters can't be read from the source.
- No broken or malformed links were found in the visible HTML.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed by QA):** the `<img>` with `src="https://click.hyatt.com/q/cN-ITm6l9wFwbMB79CTb_w~~/AAQRxRA~/BwkFXu9Uny3rpLksljfiojECZFA9wzQClKq3ASrQxs1ShBSfZg..."` has no `alt` attribute. If it's a content image, screen readers will announce the long URL. If it's a tracking pixel, it should have `alt=""`.
- **Empty `<title></title>`:** it is present in `<head>` with no text. This is a minor accessibility and metadata gap.
- **Leftover template comments:** the HTML block contains empty comment stubs such as `<!-- Assigning Variables -->`, `<!-- Name Salutations Script -->` and `<!-- Link Tracking -->`. The script content has been stripped, but the comments add payload weight and expose internal template structure.
- **Web font:** `fonts.googleapis.com` Noto Sans is loaded inside a `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` wrapper, with an Arial/Sans-serif fallback in the inline styles. This is handled correctly.
- No other rendering defects are visible in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The subject renders "Surprise Helena!" with the name resolved. No unresolved `{{ }}`, `%%`, or `[[ ]]` tokens appear in the visible source. The remaining body was truncated, so tokens further down could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe** and **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)** were not found. The QA tool notes that AgentMail may not relay these headers, so this is **unverified, not confirmed missing**.
- **Authentication-Results** was not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The QA tool again attributes this to the relay.
- The footer (unsubscribe link, physical postal address) falls outside the truncated HTML, so CAN-SPAM body elements could not be checked.
- The sender is `worldofhyatt@loyalty.hyatt.com`, and the tracking domain `click.hyatt.com` is on the same parent domain, so there is no domain mismatch.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
This could not be assessed. All 19 tracked links were skipped and the redirect targets are hidden behind `click.hyatt.com` tokens. No UTM parameters or landing page URLs are visible in the source.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` to the `click.hyatt.com/q/...` image: descriptive text if it's content, `alt=""` if it's a pixel.
2. Populate `<title>` (for example with the subject line) so the web view and assistive tech have a title.

4. Re-run QA with the raw `.eml` or full headers, bypassing the relay, to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
5. Resolve a sample of the 19 tracked links with a redirect-following probe to check destinations, UTM parameters and landing page alignment.
6. Re-audit with the untruncated HTML to check the footer, the unsubscribe link, the postal address and the remaining merge tokens.
