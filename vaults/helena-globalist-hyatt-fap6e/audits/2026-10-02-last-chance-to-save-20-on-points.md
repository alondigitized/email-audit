# Last Chance To Save 20% on Points
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-purpose deadline nudge. It's the third 20%-off-points send in about two months, after the 2026-08-07 launch and the 2026-09-03 "There's Still Time to Save 20% on Points" reminder. The hierarchy works. A clock icon and "Offer Ends Soon" lead to the headline "Save 20% on Bonus Points", then the hero, a short paragraph and a solid blue "BUY POINTS NOW" button. The deadline (October 5, 2026) sits right under the button.
- The weak spot is the middle of the email. The "Make the most of your points this fall" award-night block (30K, 25K, 15K, 10K points) competes with the buy CTA. The member bar also reads "0 Current Points". Those four EXPLORE links push someone with an empty balance toward spending points they don't have, rather than buying them. The card pitch and the logo wall below add more noise.
- Verdict: a competent reminder with a clear offer and honest dates, but not fresh. The creative is largely recycled, and the subject line adds nothing the September 3 send hadn't already said.

## What's working

- The offer is unambiguous: 20% off, a visible "Offer ends October 5, 2026" line, and a matching deadline in the terms.
- The "BUY POINTS NOW" button is a strong verb-and-noun CTA, and it sits well above the fold on desktop.
- The blue member bar ("Hi Helena Globalist, Member | 0 Current Points") makes the email feel personal and gives the loyalty context.
- The gifting line ("Want to share the journey? Gift points...") adds a second use case without adding a second button.
- The render is clean: no broken images or overlapping text, and the hero is on-brand.

## What's weak

- The award cards (30K, 25K, 15K, 10K points, each with an EXPLORE link) compete with the purchase CTA. They also feel disconnected from a member who shows 0 points.
- The Hyatt Credit Card block ("Earn up to 60,000 Bonus Points") sits below the points offer and pitches a different way to earn points.
- The hero is a decorative property shot ("Commune by the Great Wall"). It says nothing about points or the discount, so the offer isn't visible in the image itself.
- The cap and conditions (55,000 points per year, increments of 1,000, 48-hour posting) sit in dense fine print at the bottom. A buyer would want to see the cap near the button.
- The send is the third near-identical 20% points push in two months, so the urgency feels manufactured.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one job. Remove the award-night grid and the credit card block, or collapse the grid to one line of "what 10K points gets you". Keep the buy CTA as the only button above the fold and add a closing repeat of it.
- 2. Put the math in the hero. For example, "10,000 points for $X instead of $Y" next to the button, plus the 55,000-point cap in one line. A concrete price beats a bare percentage.
- 3. Adapt the content for a 0-balance member. Lead with "Start your balance" or show what a first purchase would cover, instead of a generic fall-trip pitch.
- 4. Replace the decorative hero with a headline graphic that carries the offer and the deadline: "20% off points · ends Oct 5".
- 5. Vary the cadence. If this is the last send, the subject should say so credibly: name the exact day and time the offer ends.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends Sunday: 20% off Bonus Points`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your last 3 days to buy points at 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Offer ends Oct 5 at 11:59 PM ET. Buy or gift up to 55,000 points.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Top up your balance for your next stay. Gifting works too.`
