# Ends July 27: 20% More Bonus Points on Points Purchase
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a "buy points" promotional email — a clean departure from the relentless Chase credit card acquisition drumbeat that's dominated Hyatt's sends since early June (three credit card pitches in six weeks, peaking July 13 at 75K bonus points). The offer itself is straightforward: purchase Hyatt points before July 27 and receive 20% more. The execution is solid — specific deadline, prominent CTA, destination inspiration tiles below the fold — but the email leaves performance on the table by not personalizing the ask to the member's current balance or a specific points gap.
- The "gift points" secondary option is an underrated touch. It opens a social sharing angle ("help someone take their summer getaway") without cannibalizing the primary CTA. Most loyalty brands never think to surface this.
- The weakest moment is the destination tile section. Four beautiful properties — Sydney, Lake Tahoe, Dominican Republic, Napa Valley — but each resolves to a generic "EXPLORE >" link. It's an inspiration section that doesn't close the loop: it motivates you to want a trip without connecting that desire back to the buy-points action.

## What's working

- **Specific deadline creates real urgency** — "Ends July 27" in the subject and "Last chance" hero copy is credible and concrete, not the vague "limited time" many brands use.
- **Primary CTA is unambiguous** — "BUY POINTS NOW" is a clean verb-noun button; no guessing what happens when you click.
- **Gift-points secondary path is clever** — surfaces a social use case ("Prefer to share the discount?") without fighting the main CTA for attention.
- **Visual hierarchy is clean** — hero image → headline offer → CTA flows naturally without competing elements above the fold.
- **Destination tiles add aspirational context** — makes the abstract act of buying points feel connected to a real trip.

## What's weak

- **"EXPLORE >" CTA on every destination tile is generic** — four properties, four identical links; none of them completes the buy-points narrative.
- **No points balance or gap anchor** — the most persuasive buy-points email would say "You're X points away from a free night at Napa." This one doesn't.
- **Subject is 54 chars** — slightly over the mobile-safe ~50 threshold; "on Points Purchase" at the end is redundant given the earlier context and will clip on some devices.
- **Dense T&C block at the bottom** — not a render bug, but the paragraph of fine print is visually heavy and will trigger skepticism for first-time offers.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Anchor the ask to a specific gap.** Add one line above the CTA: "You're 4,200 points away from a free night at a Category 1 property. Today's 20% bonus closes that gap." This converts an abstract transaction into a clear goal.
- 2. **Replace "EXPLORE >" with property-specific CTAs.** "Book Lake Tahoe →" does more work than a generic explore link and ties the destination tile back to the buy-points action.
- 3. **Trim the subject line.** Drop "on Points Purchase" — the offer context is already set by the sender name and "Bonus Points."
- 4. **Shorten the T&C block or collapse it.** A "See full terms" link above a collapsed summary would reduce the visual noise without hiding anything.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends July 27: Get 20% More When You Buy Points`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last chance: 20% bonus on points — expires July 27`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Boost your balance before summer plans lock up — one week left.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Buy now, travel later — 20% more points through July 27.`
