# Earn up to 36K Bonus Points. Unlock More Travel.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-13**
## Executive summary

- This is the third send of the same 3K-per-three-nights promo, after "Limited-Time Offer: Earn up to 36K Bonus Points" on 2026-09-30 and again on 2026-10-22. The render is clean. A blue hero reads "EARN 3K BONUS POINTS EVERY 3 NIGHTS," a white card gives the mechanic, and one blue "REGISTER WITH ONE CLICK" button follows. The offer is easy to understand, and it's consistent with the brand's better single-offer sends, like 10-23's 20% off Bonus Points (7/10).
- The weakness is freshness and urgency. This recipient has seen this exact mechanic twice already, and the hero never states the December 15 deadline. It only appears in the headline below the fold of the hero and in the bold line above the button. The header also shows "0 Current Points," which makes a 36-night earn cap feel remote. The "36K" in the subject is also a stretch against a hero that says 3K.
- Verdict: competent and clear, but a recycled promo with no new reason to act. Expect a modest open and a below-average click.

## What's working

- Single offer and single CTA. The "Register with one click" button is the only action in the body.
- The hero type is large and legible: "3K BONUS POINTS EVERY 3 NIGHTS" reads instantly.
- The callout box ("Earn 3,000 Bonus Points… up to 36K") states the math plainly.
- The member header bar ("Hi Helena Globalist, Member | 0 Current Points") reminds the recipient they're in the program.
- The date window is explicit: "Stay between September 29 and December 15, 2026."

## What's weak

- The hero is a washed-out blue-on-blue photo with no property, destination or travel imagery. It feels generic compared with the "Be More Jet-Setter" Kotor hero on 10-30.
- The deadline is missing from the hero. Urgency only shows up in the card headline and the bold line near the button.
- The subject says "up to 36K," but the visible hero says 3K. The 36K cap means 36 eligible nights, and that is buried in a small callout.
- The brand-logo grid below the CTA is dense and tiny, and it adds little. The fine print is a very long wall of text.
- The "Book Now >" link in the header competes with the real CTA, which is "Register."
- "0 Current Points" in the header undercuts the "unlock more" pitch.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero ("Through Dec 15") and swap in a destination photo with real contrast. Reframe the pitch as "3 nights = 3K points" with a progress-style visual (for example, "Night 1 of 3").
- 2. Suppress or vary this send for people who got the 10-22 version. A third identical resend trains people to ignore it.
- 3. Add a concrete redemption example, such as "36K = X free nights at Category Y." That makes the points feel worth earning.
- 4. Cut or shrink the logo grid and move the fine print behind a terms link. Keep the CTA above the fold on mobile.
- 5. Drop "Book Now" from the header on promo sends, so "Register" is the only action.
- **Subject Alt A:** `3K points every 3 nights. Ends Dec 15`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Helena, your next 3 nights = 3K bonus points`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Register once, stay through Dec 15, and earn up to 36K Bonus Points toward free nights.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Still time to register. Every third eligible night earns 3,000 points.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Braze/Beefree-built template with tracked links on `click.hyatt.com`. The QA data and the truncated HTML confirm three accessibility defects: missing alt text on 3 images and an empty `<title>`. The header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified, and the HTML was truncated before the footer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Destinations not verifiable.** 23 tracking links on `click.hyatt.com` were skipped by the HTTP probe, so redirect chains, final URLs and 4xx/5xx status are unknown.
- **Tracking URL used as an image source.** One `<img>` has `src` on `https://click.hyatt.com/q/DOFwBv0Jl_wjbona4LCEiA~~/AAQRxRA~/oFDptV8Zcw0t6-...`. Images served through a click-redirect host depend on that redirector being available. They also can't be cached or verified like static assets.
- **No broken links confirmed.** The QA run reported 0 issues.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, 3 images** (confirmed by QA):
  - `https://braze-images.com/appboy/communication/assets/image_assets/images/69e418e8dbb9f600aee21673/original.png?177655626` appears twice. It is the same asset, so it is either repeated or a duplicated block.
  - The `click.hyatt.com/q/DOFwBv0Jl_...` image.
  - Without `alt`, screen readers may announce the filename or URL. If the images are decorative, they need `alt=""`. If not, they need descriptive text. Image-blocked clients show nothing useful in either case.
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in `<head>`. This is minor for email, but it affects the web-view and accessibility title.
- **Good practice present:** `lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, a `viewport` meta tag, MSO conditionals, and a hidden preheader with Arial/sans-serif fallback for the Noto Sans web font.
- Only the top of the document was available, so the rest of the body was not assessed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unrendered merge tokens such as `{{ }}`, `%%`, or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The only leftovers are empty HTML comments from the template's script blocks ("Name Salutations Script", "Link Tracking", "Current Points…"). These expose internal template structure, including the typo "VARIALBES", but they have no functional impact. Personalization further down the email could not be checked because of truncation.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** (WARN).
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found** (WARN, RFC 8058 one-click).
- **`Authentication-Results` not found** (WARN), so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown.
- QA notes that all three headers may simply not be captured by the AgentMail relay. They are unconfirmed, not confirmed defects. Verify against raw headers from a direct delivery. The From domain `loyalty.hyatt.com` is a subdomain of the sender's own domain, which would support alignment if DKIM and SPF are configured.
- The footer (unsubscribe link, physical postal address) was cut off in the truncated source, so CAN-SPAM body requirements could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. Every link is wrapped in an encoded `click.hyatt.com/q/...` redirect, so UTM parameters and landing pages can't be read from the HTML. They would need to be checked by following the redirects. The preheader mentions "eligible nights completed by Dec. 15, 2026", but whether the landing page matches that can't be checked here.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` text to the three flagged images, using `alt=""` for decorative ones. Check whether the duplicated `original.png` is intentional.
2. Populate `<title>` with the subject line or email name.
3. Obtain raw headers from a direct send, bypassing the relay. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass with alignment.
4. Follow the 23 `click.hyatt.com` links with a redirect-following check. Confirm 200 status on the final URLs, UTM or campaign parameters, and consistency with the Dec. 15, 2026 offer.
5. Host images on a static CDN (such as the existing `braze-images.com`) rather than through the click-redirect domain.
6. Strip the template-script HTML comments before send to reduce payload and avoid exposing internals.
7. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to cover the footer, unsubscribe link, postal address and remaining personalization.
