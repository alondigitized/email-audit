# Earn More—and Enter To Win a Trip—With Delta Air Lines®
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- This is a co-branded Hyatt and Delta SkyMiles partnership send. It tries to do four things at once: get members to link accounts, push two linked-member offers, run a sweepstakes for a Riviera Maya trip, and tease Milestone Rewards for January. The render is clean and the hero is handsome. But the email never settles on one ask, and the actual offers sit in small gray text blocks well below the hero.
- The "Link Your Accounts" button is the right primary CTA. The reward for clicking is vague, though. The real value, 1,000 Bonus Points or a free Tier-Qualifying Night Credit, is buried in two gray cards under a "Terms apply" caveat. The sweepstakes is the only emotional hook, and it sits fourth from the top. Compared with the clean single-offer sends from 11/13 and 11/14 ("Earn up to 36K Bonus Points", "Surprise Helena!"), this is a diffuse, partner-driven message. The header also reads "0 Current Points", which undercuts any points-based pitch for this recipient.
- Judgement: a solid brand execution, but a mid-tier performer. It needs one lead offer and a shorter body.

## What's working

- The hero is polished. The Park Hyatt New York shot with the traveling couple gives a premium, aspirational feel.
- The co-brand lockup (World of Hyatt | Delta SkyMiles) is clear, and both brands are credible.
- The primary CTA, "LINK YOUR ACCOUNTS", is a clear verb plus noun in a solid blue button.
- The sweepstakes block has a strong image and a real prize: a Riviera Maya escape for two, with dates.
- The email renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- Too many asks: link accounts, two offers, a sweepstakes, and a Milestone Rewards tease. No single offer leads.
- The two offer cards are small centered gray text with a tiny "Register now" link. The highlight, 1,000 Bonus Points, doesn't stand out.
- Two generic "LEARN MORE" buttons don't say what they lead to. This is the same weak CTA copy the rubric warns about.
- The header shows "0 Current Points" next to the headline. That hurts the points-earning pitch for this recipient.
- The "Coming soon" Milestone Rewards block can't be acted on until January 1, so it dilutes the send.
- The sweepstakes disclaimer and the "Terms Apply" notes add friction and make the offer feel less direct.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer, either 1,000 Bonus Points or the Riviera Maya sweepstakes. Put it in the hero as large type or a banner, with the link CTA directly under it.
- 2. Rebuild the two offer cards with the number as the visual anchor ("1,000 Bonus Points" large, "+1 Tier-Qualifying Night Credit" large), then one line of detail. Drop the "Learn more" buttons from this section.
- 3. Replace both "LEARN MORE" buttons with specific copy, such as "Link & Earn 1,000 Points" and "Preview Milestone Rewards".
- 4. Move the Milestone Rewards tease out of this send or demote it to a single text line. It isn't actionable until January.
- 5. Segment the points-balance header, or hide the "0 Current Points" line for members with low balances in promo sends.
- 6. Put one honest deadline near the top. The sweepstakes ends 11/23 and the Delta flight offer runs through 2/15, so the sweepstakes date gives the real urgency.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Win a Riviera Maya trip—link Delta by Nov 23`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Link Delta, earn 1,000 bonus points`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Enter to win a getaway for two at Alila Mayakoba. Ends Nov 23.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fly Delta after linking and earn 1,000 Bonus Points.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The markup is well-formed: a responsive 600px table layout with `lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag and `role="presentation"` layout tables. The source was truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and most hrefs could not be checked. The QA run flagged an empty `<title>`, one image with no alt text, and header and plain-text deliverability warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracking links not verified.** 29 links were skipped because they sit on a click-redirect domain. They use `https://click.hyatt.com/q/<token>~~/AAQRxRA~/...`, so the HTTP probe didn't run and their final destinations are unconfirmed.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 12,090 of 15,913 characters (about 76%) are URLs, which exceeds the 70% threshold. This is a side effect of wrapping every link in long `click.hyatt.com` tokens. It can hurt text-part spam scoring and readability.
- No broken links were confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty title.** `<title></title>` is in `<head>`. Some clients and web-view versions use it as the page title.
- **Missing alt text.** One image has no `alt`. It is wrapped in the tracking URL `https://click.hyatt.com/q/kcmMv-sgA9jpSGYHCGftGg~~/AAQRxRA~/08BB5KVxalGxEGBAcbftVeeqe0rAy_hrcEECVaMod8mlCQ_hOe…`. If it is linked or meaningful, it is unreadable to screen readers and to clients that block images.
- **External web font.** `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Noto+Sans">` is wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, and the inline font stacks fall back to `Arial, Sans-serif`. Most clients ignore the link, but the fallback is in place, so this is minor.
- The preheader (`Access limited-time offers and more when you link accounts.`) is hidden correctly, and the padding characters are also hidden.
- Nothing else is confirmed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source.
- **Template comments left in the output.** These are present in the delivered HTML: `<!-- Email Level Code -->`, `<!-- Assigning Header Variables -->`, `<!-- Name Salutations Script -->`, `<!-- Korean and English Characters Identification -->`. They are harmless to rendering but expose internal template structure and add payload.
- The source was truncated, so the salutation output could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found.** The QA tool notes the relay may strip it, so this can't be confirmed as a sender defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The same caveat applies. One-click unsubscribe support is unverified.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture. The From domain `loyalty.hyatt.com` and the tracking domain `click.hyatt.com` are both Hyatt-owned and aligned.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated source, so they couldn't be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Link destinations and UTM parameters can't be confirmed. The probe was skipped and the source was truncated, so the redirect tokens hide the final URLs.
- No continuity issues are confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run the audit with the full raw message (headers plus untruncated HTML) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, and to check the footer unsubscribe link and postal address.
2. Add `alt` text to the image at `click.hyatt.com/q/kcmMv-…`. Use `alt=""` if it is purely decorative.
3. Populate `<title>` with the subject line or a short descriptor.
4. Add a hand-built plain-text part with fewer and shorter URLs to bring the URL ratio below 70%.
5. Strip template and developer comments from the production HTML.
6. Follow a sample of the `click.hyatt.com` redirects with a tracking-aware probe to verify the landing pages and UTM parameters.
