# Your Account Summary—Your Next Trip Could Be Even More Rewarding
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-02**
## Executive summary

- This is the second "Account Summary" email World of Hyatt has sent in five weeks — the first landed May 28 with the subject "The Getaway Everyone Will Remember." Same template, same activity module, same digest structure of special offers → brand spotlight → locations → wellbeing. The seasonal hook ("Save and earn more with this season's offers") gives it a fresh layer, and the concrete 30K bonus points registration offer is the most actionable thing in the send. But the email undermines itself immediately: the activity module displays "0 Save Points" and "0 Qualifying Nights" in large, prominent numbers, which tells an inactive subscriber they've done nothing — the opposite of aspirational.
- The multi-section layout is clean and on-brand. Great Scotland Yard Hotel and Miraval Austin are distinct spotlights that add genuine editorial interest. The problem is structural: by the time those properties appear, the subscriber has already scrolled past a demoralizing activity widget, a 30K points offer with no deadline, and a "Hyatt Bound" tile that's hard to read at scroll speed.
- Competent execution of a familiar template, held back by a single data-display misstep and a subject line that promises more than it delivers.

## What's working

- **Hero + seasonal headline are strong**: "Save and earn more with this season's offers" pairs well with the lush destination photography and anchors the email's purpose immediately.
- **30K bonus points offer is specific**: "Register to earn up to 30K Bonus Points" is the clearest value statement in the send and the most action-worthy tile.
- **Dual CTAs in the activity module**: "MY OFFERS" and "BOOK NOW" are unambiguous and placed where the eye rests after reading the account data.
- **Editorial variety in the lower sections**: Two distinct hotel spotlights (Great Scotland Yard, Miraval Austin) and a wellbeing module give the email a magazine feel that's different from the Chase card pushes that dominated June.
- **No visible render bugs**: Layout, images, and text appear clean throughout.

## What's weak

- **"0 Qualifying Nights / 0 Save Points" is deflating**: Surfacing zero activity to an inactive subscriber is a motivational hole in the center of the email — it highlights disengagement rather than inspiring action.
- **Subject line is too long and vague in the back half**: At 63 characters, it's over the mobile-optimal threshold, and "Could Be Even More Rewarding" is filler — no offer, no number, no urgency.
- **No specific deadline on the seasonal offer**: "This season's offers" is ambiguous. Is it through July? August? The absence of a date removes the only urgency lever.
- **Repeating the Account Summary format**: This is the same structural DNA as the May 28 email. Two Account Summary digests in five weeks risks training subscribers to skim-or-archive without reading.
- **"Explore Hyatt Bound" tile is unclear**: Even at desktop rendering, the copy for the second special-offer tile is too small to parse without squinting — a conversion opportunity lost to tight layout.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Suppress or reframe the activity widget for zero-activity subscribers.** Replace "0 Qualifying Nights" with something aspirational — "Your summer is just getting started" — or skip the module entirely and lead with the 30K offer. Zero metrics communicate the wrong thing.
- 2. **Add a hard deadline to the seasonal offer.** "Register by July 31" transforms a soft consideration into a specific action. The June 6 "Explore Limited-time Summer Offers" email suffered the same vagueness problem.
- 3. **Rewrite the subject — move the concrete offer to the front.** The current back half ("Could Be Even More Rewarding") is a placeholder, not a hook.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Register Now: Earn Up to 30K Bonus Points This Summer`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Summer Offers Are Live — 30K Bonus Points Inside`
- 4. **Write a preheader that complements instead of repeating.** The inbox pairing should add specific detail the subject only teases.
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New spotlights: Great Scotland Yard Hotel + Miraval Austin. Register by July 31.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Earn 30,000 bonus points on summer stays — plus new hotels and seasonal offers.`
- 5. **Vary the Account Summary cadence.** Consider gating this format to members with actual activity or alternating it with a format that doesn't require a widget — the template is starting to feel mechanical.

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is structurally sound (valid DOCTYPE, MSO/VML conditionals, responsive breakpoints) but has significant compliance gaps: missing List-Unsubscribe headers violate Google/Yahoo 2024 bulk sender requirements, and SPF/DKIM authentication status is unverifiable through the relay. Six images lack alt text and the plain-text part is predominantly raw URLs.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**25 tracking links via `click.hyatt.com`** — all were skipped during HTTP probing because the click-redirect domain returns non-terminal redirects. Destination URLs and UTM parameter integrity cannot be verified from the source alone.

One tracking pixel uses a click-redirect URL as its `src`:

This is a Braze open-tracking pixel routed through the click domain rather than a dedicated pixel domain — not a bug, but worth noting for analytics separation.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Empty `<title>` tag** — `<title></title>` renders as a blank string in clients that surface it (some webmail previews, accessibility tools).

**6 images missing `alt` text:**

The lifestyle/hotel images should carry descriptive alt text for screen readers and image-blocked clients. The Braze asset (`original.png`) and tracking pixel should carry `alt=""` to suppress screen-reader announcement.

**Google Fonts loaded via `<link>` tag** (Noto Sans, inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`): blocked in Gmail (strips `<head>` content), Apple Mail with remote content disabled, and many corporate clients. **Plain-text part is 70.5% raw URLs** (11,178 of 15,858 chars). Auto-generated plain text that dumps redirect URLs is a spam signal for content filters and unreadable for plain-text clients.

### 5. Compliance

**[CRITICAL] `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected.** Google and Yahoo require this header for bulk senders (>5,000/day) as of February 2024. Absence risks Gmail/Yahoo filtering or blocking.

**[CRITICAL] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not detected (RFC 8058).** One-click unsubscribe (Gmail's "Unsubscribe" button in the sender line) requires this header. Its absence means Gmail will either surface a less prominent unsubscribe path or suppress the option, increasing spam complaints.

**[WARN] `Authentication-Results` header not found via AgentMail relay.** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status cannot be confirmed. If loyalty.hyatt.com is not DMARC-aligned with the sending IP, deliverability to major providers will degrade.

CAN-SPAM physical address and unsubscribe link are expected in the email footer (common for Hyatt loyalty emails) but not visible in the truncated source — cannot confirm.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All 25 outbound links route through `click.hyatt.com` redirects. Because HTTP probing was skipped on redirect domains, UTM parameter presence (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) on final landing URLs cannot be verified. This is a gap in the QA pipeline, not confirmed missing — but worth a spot-check on a sample redirect chain.

No mismatched domains or suspicious redirect destinations observed in link hostnames.
