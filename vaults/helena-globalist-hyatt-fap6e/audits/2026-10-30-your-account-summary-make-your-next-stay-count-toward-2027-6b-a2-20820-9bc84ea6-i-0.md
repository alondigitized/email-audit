# Your Account Summary—Make Your Next Stay Count Toward 2027 
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-30**
## Executive summary

- This is Hyatt's fifth "Account Summary" digest in about five months, and it's a weak one for this recipient. The subject promises "Make Your Next Stay Count Toward 2027". The hero is a moody terrace shot with "Be More Inspired" script, which doesn't mention status or 2027. The real hook is a headline further down, "Keep your 2027 status goals in sight". It carries a December 31 deadline and a restatement of the 36K Bonus Points promo from 2026-09-30 and 2026-10-22.
- The personalized block undercuts the pitch. It shows 0 Base Points and 0 Qualifying Nights beside copy about "progress toward status". Nothing in the email makes a zero balance feel like a reason to act. Below that it becomes a long catalog: Chase card, Bahia Principe, Seville, Hua Hin, Masterpiece Hotels and a brand-logo wall. It reads as a newsletter, not a send with one job.
- Judgement: a generic, low-urgency digest. It has a solid status-year angle and a recognizable sender, but it is buried under eight content blocks. Expect roughly 4-5/10 performance.

## What's working

- The "Keep your 2027 status goals in sight" section has a clear headline and a concrete December 31, 2026 deadline.
- The single blue "START PLANNING" button and the "MY ACCOUNT / BOOK NOW" pair are visible and unambiguous.
- The activity box (Base Points, Qualifying Nights) gives a personalized, scannable status snapshot.
- Imagery is high quality and the layout renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlap.
- The 36K Bonus Points mechanic (3K per three eligible nights, Sept 29 to Dec 15) is clearly stated.

## What's weak

- The hero has no copy about the offer, status or 2027. Its "Be More Inspired" script is brand fluff, and the real message sits below the fold.
- Both counters read 0, and the email doesn't acknowledge that. For a zero-activity member, "status goals" feels unreachable.
- The 36K Bonus Points offer is the same one sent on 2026-09-30 and 2026-10-22, so it reads as stale repetition.
- The email carries too many competing modules: gifting, Chase card, 36K, Under Canvas, Bahia Principe, Seville, Hua Hin and Masterpiece. Most are low-priority, text-heavy and bury the primary CTA.
- The "START PLANNING" button doesn't say what it does. It's a vague verb with no specific destination such as "Book a stay".
- The Under Canvas 15% back offer expires November 30 and is buried mid-email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rebuild the hero around the single message "Lock in 2027 status by Dec 31". Put a status-progress mechanic and a "Book a stay" button above the fold, and cut the generic "Be More Inspired" art.
- 2. Handle the 0-night case explicitly. Use a line such as "Start your status run: 3 nights = 3K bonus points".
- 3. Cut the lower modules to one or two (36K promo plus one destination). Move the rest to a web-view link.
- 4. Rename "START PLANNING" to a verb + noun such as "Book Qualifying Nights".
- 5. Surface a real countdown: "X days left in 2026 status year".
