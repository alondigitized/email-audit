# Your Account Summary—Vacation Your Way With Stunning Resorts 
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-26**
## Executive summary

- This is Hyatt's fourth "Account Summary" digest in three months (following 2026-09-01's near-identical send, 2026-07-31, and May 28's "The Getaway Everyone Will Remember"), and it's the weakest of the bunch — 0 Base Points and 0 Qualifying Nights on display is a bleak, unpersonalized hero moment for anyone reading this as a real member. The "BE MORE HERE" hero banner is moody and on-brand but generic — it's a lifestyle image with no discernible offer, forcing the reader to scroll past a full screen before hitting anything actionable.
- Below the fold this sprawls into an exhausting kitchen-sink digest: credit card pitch, bonus points offer, anniversary promo, brand spotlight, two property spotlights, and a "Masterpieces" collection — eight distinct CTAs competing for attention with zero prioritization. There's no single reason to open this today; it reads as an omnibus newsletter, not a targeted send. Judgement: skippable, and the zeroed-out stats box actively works against the brand here.

## What's working

- Clean, consistent visual system — Hyatt blue, consistent card layout, legible typography throughout.
- The "Your activity year to date" stats box is a smart personalization mechanic when populated — the structure is good even if the data isn't.
- Partner hotel logo wall at the bottom reinforces brand scale/trust for a loyalty-program audience.
- Multiple property spotlights (Sunscape Cancun, Alila Mayakoba) include real amenity detail (splash park, cenote immersions) rather than generic stock copy.

## What's weak

- Stats box shows "0 Base Points / 0 Qualifying Nights" — a glaring, demotivating render of a real data field with no engagement.
- Hero banner has no offer, price, or deadline — just mood photography and a vague "Save on your next resort escape" subhead.
- Eight+ competing CTAs (credit card, bonus points x2, brand spotlight, two properties, Masterpieces) with no visual hierarchy indicating which one matters most.
- Credit card and bonus-points offers are the same "Earn up to X Bonus Points" mechanic Hyatt has run repeatedly (per brand history) — no new angle.
- "Register Now" bonus offer (Sept 1–Oct 26) buried below three other sections instead of leading.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix or suppress the zeroed stats box.** Never render "0 Base Points / 0 Qualifying Nights" to a member — either suppress the module for members with no YTD activity, or replace with a soft CTA ("Start earning toward your next free night").
- 2. **Pick one hero offer and lead with it.** Drop the vague "Be More Here" mood shot as the sole hero; pair it with a specific, dated offer (the 30K Bonus Points registration deadline is concrete and time-bound — promote that to the top).
- 3. **Cut this from 8 modules to 3-4.** Digest format is fatiguing recipients — consolidate credit card + bonus points into one "Ways to Earn" block, and pick one property spotlight instead of two.
- 4. **Vary the bonus-points mechanic.** Three sends in a row leaning on "earn up to X bonus points" (this one, 09-01, 08-07) risks fatigue — try a redemption-focused angle ("Here's what 30K points gets you") instead.
