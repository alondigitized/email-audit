# Your Account Summary—Explore What Your Points Can Unlock
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-31**
## Executive summary

- This is the third "Account Summary" digest World of Hyatt has sent in two months — following May 28's "The Getaway Everyone Will Remember" and July 2's near-identical "Your Next Trip Could Be Even More Rewarding." Same template, same activity module, same waterfall of sections (rewards, special offers, brand spotlight, locations, Aeroplan). The one new wrinkle this time is that the account being summarized has **zero** activity — 0 Base Points, 0 Qualifying Nights — which undercuts the entire "account summary" premise; there's nothing to summarize.
- Buried in the "Rewards for the way you spend" section is the same 75K Bonus Points credit card offer that anchored the July 13 send and got a "Reminder:" resend on July 20. Seeing it a third time in three weeks, now folded into an unrelated digest, reads as recycled inventory rather than a fresh push, and it's competing for attention against two more bonus-point registration offers plus a Hyatt Select spotlight plus two hotel tiles plus an Aeroplan cross-promo — six distinct asks in one email.
- The render itself is clean and on-brand, and the point-cost transparency on the hotel tiles (6,000 pts/night, 10,000 pts/night) is a genuine strength. But this is a template running on autopilot for a member with no stay activity — competent execution, weak targeting.

## What's working

- Clean, bug-free render — no broken images, no overlapping text, consistent brand blue/white styling throughout.
- Point-cost transparency on the "Locations to love" tiles (Krakow: 6,000 pts/night, Kuala Lumpur: 10,000 pts/night) gives a concrete, actionable number a member can act on immediately.
- Member status bar at the very top ("Member | 0 Current Points | 0 Lifetime Base Points") establishes loyalty context before the fold.
- Deadline dates are explicit on every registration offer (Aug 20, Sept 1, Sept 30, 2026), so urgency is at least legible even if not emphasized visually.

## What's weak

- The "Account Summary" is summarizing nothing — 0 Base Points, 0 Qualifying Nights on display undercuts the entire premise of the email and is a discouraging thing to show a member, not a motivating one.
- Hero image/copy ("Where will your points take you next?") is generic aspirational lifestyle content, not tied to any specific offer — the eye lands on a pool photo, not the 75K bonus points headline.
- Six competing offers/sections (credit card, 2K registration, 30K registration, Hyatt Select, two hotel tiles, Aeroplan) with no visual hierarchy signaling which one actually matters this send.
- The 75K Bonus Points credit card offer is now appearing for the third time in under three weeks (July 13 original, July 20 "Reminder:", now folded into this digest) — cadence fatigue.
- Primary CTA is diluted: "START PLANNING," "MY ACCOUNT," "BOOK NOW" all compete near the top with no single dominant action.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Suppress or reframe the "0/0" activity module for zero-activity members.** Showing a blank stat block is worse than showing nothing — swap it for a re-engagement hook ("You haven't stayed with us this year — here's 2,000 points to get started") rather than a literal zero.
- 2. **Lead the hero with the actual best offer, not generic lifestyle copy.** Put the 75K bonus points card offer or the 30K stay bonus in the hero fold with a specific number, since it's the strongest concrete offer in the email and currently doesn't appear until well past the fold.
- 3. **Cut the section count.** Six offer types in one send is noise. Pick one primary offer + one secondary and move the rest (Hyatt Select spotlight, second hotel tile, Aeroplan) to a follow-up send.
- 4. **Retire or meaningfully escalate the 75K card offer before using it a fourth time** — three appearances in three weeks (July 13 → July 20 reminder → this digest) risks the recipient tuning it out entirely.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your next stay could earn double the points — see how`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Krakow from 6,000 points a night. See what's new.`
