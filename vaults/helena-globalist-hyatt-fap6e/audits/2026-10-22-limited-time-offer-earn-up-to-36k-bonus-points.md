# Limited-Time Offer: Earn up to 36K Bonus Points
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a near-exact resend of the 2026-09-30 "Limited-Time Offer: Earn up to 36K Bonus Points" email. The subject is the same, and so are the mechanic (3K points per three eligible nights, capped at 36K) and the window (September 29 to December 15). The hero is clean: a big "3K BONUS POINTS" over a blue-tinted resort photo, with a single "Register with one click" button. The eye lands on the offer first.
- The weak spot is the personalization strip. It greets "Helena Globalist" and shows "Member | 0 Current Points," so the sender is telling a Globalist-tier member they have no points. That undercuts the "unlock more" pitch. The 22 days since the first send also remove any real urgency. The legal block is long and dense, but it sits at the bottom where it belongs.
- Verdict: a competent loyalty promo that is stale on arrival. It works as a reminder, but a second identical send with no new angle is lazy, and for a recipient who already registered it is noise.

## What's working

- The hero reads instantly: "EARN 3K BONUS POINTS EVERY 3 NIGHTS" is large and high contrast.
- There is one mechanic and one CTA. "Register with one click" is a clear verb plus object in a solid blue button.
- The callout box ("Earn 3,000 Bonus Points… up to 36K") states the cap and the earn rate plainly.
- The stay window is spelled out in bold: September 29 to December 15, 2026.
- The brand portfolio grid adds trust and shows the range of properties that count.
- The Book Now link and the member bar at the top give a second path to action.

## What's weak

- This is the same offer and subject as the 2026-09-30 send, so a subscriber sees a repeat with nothing new.
- The member bar shows "0 Current Points" for a Globalist member. It looks like a data glitch and weakens the "earn more" framing.
- The hero image is washed out in blue and the resort is hard to make out. It sells no destination or feeling.
- The body copy is generic ("Escape for a weekend or go on a far-flung adventure"). Nothing is tied to the member's tier or travel history.
- The December 15 deadline is mentioned but not pushed. There is no countdown and no "X weeks left" framing.
- The brand grid is a lot of tiny logos that are barely legible on mobile, and it adds nothing toward the click.

## Recommendations

- 1. Vary the creative and angle on the resend. Use a "you haven't registered yet" or "X weeks left" message, and suppress the send for people who already registered.
- 2. Fix or hide the "0 Current Points" display for members where it looks wrong, or replace it with tier status or nights toward the next bonus.
- 3. Replace the hero with a vivid, specific destination image and keep the "3K" type.
- 4. Add a tangible redemption example, such as "36K = X free nights at a Category 4 hotel," to make the payoff concrete.
- 5. Put a visible deadline near the CTA, such as "Stay by Dec 15 · 54 days left."
- 6. Collapse the logo grid to a single row or a link.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Register once, then earn 3,000 points per three eligible nights through Dec 15.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `36K points can mean free nights. Stay by December 15 to earn them.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, 600px Braze/Beefree-style build with a mobile media query, a preheader and `lang="en"`. The QA run found no hard failures. Three warnings need follow-up: the unsubscribe headers and authentication results could not be verified, three images have no alt text, and the `<title>` is empty. The HTML source was truncated, so findings below cover only the visible portion.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 23 links on tracking/click-redirect domains were skipped by the HTTP probe, so their destinations and status codes are unverified. They use `click.hyatt.com/q/<token>~~/…` redirect wrappers.
- **Broken links:** No broken links were confirmed.
- **Tracking-wrapped image:** The image `src` `https://click.hyatt.com/q/a_9i5rQntEvOEvZ8Hoi3sw~~/AAQRxRA~/U91LtzGZB-…` goes through the click-redirect domain. Images normally load directly from a CDN. If this is an image `src` and not a linked `href`, each load goes through the redirect, which adds latency and a possible failure point. The QA data doesn't show which attribute it came from, so this is worth checking.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (3 images):**
  - `original.png` at `https://braze-images.com/appboy/communication/assets/image_assets/images/69e418e8dbb9f600aee21673/original.png` appears twice with no `alt`. If it's meaningful content, images-off clients and screen readers get nothing. If it's decorative, it still needs `alt=""`.
  - The `click.hyatt.com/q/a_9i5rQntEvOEvZ8Hoi3sw~~/…` image has no `alt`.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty. Some clients and the web-view version use it.
- **Leftover template comments:** The first content row (`html_block block-1`) holds many empty HTML comments from the template's logic blocks, such as `<!-- Name Salutations Script -->` and `<!-- Link Tracking -->`. This is dead markup and exposes internal template structure, including a typo, "VARIALBES". It has no rendering impact.
- **Positive checks:**
  - `role="presentation"` is on the layout tables.
  - The viewport meta tag is present.
  - Outlook (MSO) conditional comments are present.
  - The hidden preheader uses `mso-hide:all`.
  - Google Fonts is loaded only for non-MSO clients, with an `Arial,Sans-serif` fallback.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source contains no unrendered tokens such as `{{ }}` or `%%`. The Name Salutations and Points script comments are empty, which suggests the logic was resolved at send time.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header:** QA could not find it. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this is **unconfirmed, not a confirmed defect**.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** Also not found, with the same caveat. The header is needed for one-click unsubscribe, which Gmail and Yahoo require from bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sending domain `loyalty.hyatt.com` could not be verified from the data.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** Not visible in the truncated source, so I can't assess them. Re-run the check on the full HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All destinations are hidden behind `click.hyatt.com` redirect tokens, and the probe skipped them. UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the "Earn up to 36K Bonus Points" offer can't be confirmed without following the redirects.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the three flagged images, using `alt=""` for any that are decorative.
2. Set a meaningful `<title>`.
3. Verify the unsubscribe headers and authentication results using the original message headers from a direct-to-inbox send, not the relay copy. Confirm both `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` are present, plus SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment.
4. Resolve the 23 tracking links with a redirect-following check (e.g., `curl -IL`) to confirm final destinations, status codes and UTM parameters.
5. Check whether the `click.hyatt.com` image is an image `src`. If so, serve it directly from a CDN.
6. Strip the empty template-logic comments at build time.
7. Re-run QA on the untruncated HTML to cover the footer, unsubscribe link and postal address.
