# Homepage snapshot · fabfitfun.com · 2026-07-24
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-24**
## Executive summary

- FabFitFun is coming in hot with the annual subscription pitch — "BEST SALE OF THE YEAR" plus a free $250 bonus box stacked on top of a bottom modal also pushing the annual plan. As someone who literally times my rejoins around bonus offers, this page is doing almost everything right. It reads like a brand that knows exactly who it's talking to: subscription-curious shoppers who need a big headline number to pull the trigger.

## What's working

- **"$250 FREE Bonus Box" hero headline** — the crossed-out dollar amount next to "FREE" is a classic value-anchor move and it works on me. I immediately think "that's what I'd sell the extras for on Mercari anyway."
- **Five-star badge in the hero** — "★★★★★ BEST SALE OF THE YEAR" stacks social proof right on top of the value claim. Efficient.
- **The Annual Plan bottom modal** — as someone who rejoins annually on a deal, having "Get a FREE bonus box ($250 value) when you choose Annual Plan / Claim Offer" sitting right there below the hero is exactly the prompt I need. It removes one step from the decision.
- **Product pile imagery** — I can see what looks like a tumbler, skincare, beauty brushes, and some branded packaging. That's enough to make me curious what's actually in the box without giving it all away.
- **Sweepstakes banner up top** — "sign up for your chance at over $36,000" adds a lottery-ticket dopamine hit on top of the already good deal.

## What's weak

- **"GET STARTED" CTA is too generic** — for someone rejoining, "GET STARTED" sounds like a new-user onboarding flow. "Claim My Bonus Box" or "Rejoin & Save" would feel more specific and urgent.
- **No box contents preview** — I cannot tell what's actually in this season's box from this screenshot. That's the #1 thing that would move me from "interested" to "converted." The product pile is teasing but not disclosing.
- **No deadline on the "BEST SALE OF THE YEAR" claim** — there's no countdown timer, no "ends Sunday," nothing. If it's the best sale of the year, when does it end? Without a deadline it feels like always-on marketing copy.
- **No trust signals for the annual commitment** — I'm being asked to choose an annual plan but I don't see anything about cancellation policy, skip/swap options, or what happens if I hate the box. That friction matters when you're asking for a year's commitment upfront.

## Recommendations

- **Replace "GET STARTED" with "Claim My Free Bonus Box"** — match the button copy to the value prop literally above it. The user should feel like they're claiming something, not starting a process.
- **Add a box-contents teaser strip** — even 3 product tiles with brand names ("MALIN+GOETZ · Kiehl's · Stanley") directly under the hero would let me pre-validate whether this season's curation is worth rejoining for. That's the real conversion lever for lapsed subscribers.
- **Put a deadline on "BEST SALE OF THE YEAR"** — "Offer ends July 27" or a 3-day countdown timer would convert the already-warm visitor who's been sitting on the fence. Right now there's no urgency to act today vs. tomorrow.
- **Add a one-liner on the annual plan flexibility** — something like "skip, swap, or cancel anytime" near the "Claim Offer" modal button would remove the commitment anxiety for someone who subscribes and cancels seasonally like me.
