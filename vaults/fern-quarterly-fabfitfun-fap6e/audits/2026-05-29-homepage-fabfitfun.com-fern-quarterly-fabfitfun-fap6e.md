# Homepage snapshot · fabfitfun.com · 2026-05-29
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-29**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is extremely on-brand for where I live in the subscription box world — FabFitFun is leading with their biggest financial hook of the year and not wasting any of my screen real estate. The hero is screaming "annual plan" energy, which honestly hits because I know exactly how to game this system. It's a clean, high-contrast sell, though I wish I could see what's actually *in* the box without tapping deeper.

## What's working

- **The "$250 FREE Bonus Box" hero** — strikethrough pricing is crack for box subscribers. I see "$250" crossed out and "FREE" and my brain immediately calculates the arbitrage.
- **"Best Sale of the Year" star badge** — positions this as a moment, not a permanent offer. That urgency framing works on me even when I know the game.
- **The bottom sticky banner** — "Get a FREE bonus box ($250 value) when you choose Annual Plan / Claim Offer" reinforces the hero message and answers the "how do I get this?" question without making me scroll.
- **Product pile imagery** — I can see actual product silhouettes (skincare, wellness bottles, what looks like a tote) which gives me enough to mentally customize my spoilers already.
- **Clean, minimal nav** — hamburger + wordmark, nothing to distract from the one job this page has.

## What's weak

- **No visible box contents breakdown** — I want to see the actual brands or items. "Curated boxes of your favorite brands" is vague; I need an Eve Lom or a Revlon drop-name to get excited.
- **"GET STARTED" CTA copy is generic** — for a subscriber like me who already knows FabFitFun, this reads as new-member language. A "Rejoin & Claim Bonus" version would hit harder.
- **Zero returning-member acknowledgment** — no loyalty balance, no "member exclusive," nothing that signals they know I've been here before. I'm not a cold lead.
- **The sweepstakes banner at the top is cropped** — "stakes is NOW LIVE! Sign up today for your chance at over $36,000 in pri[ze]" is partially cut off. That's a missed urgency signal.
- **No social proof visible** — five-star badge is generic decoration, not tied to real review counts or press mentions.

## Recommendations

- **Name-drop three to five brands in the hero subtext** — replace "your favorite brands" with "Eva NYC, Revlon, Elemis, and 15 more" so box subscribers can immediately start mental spoiler math.
- **Add a "Rejoin & Save" CTA variant** — detect returning visitors or lapsed members and swap "GET STARTED" for "Welcome Back — Claim Your Bonus Box" to close the reacquisition loop faster.
- **Show the full sweepstakes banner** — either expand the top strip or move the "$36,000 in prizes" message into the hero as a secondary hook; cropped copy is wasted urgency.
- **Drop a review count under the five-star badge** — even "★★★★★ Rated by 200,000+ members" would give the social proof teeth and validate the "Best Sale of the Year" claim.
