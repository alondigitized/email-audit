# Homepage snapshot · fabfitfun.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Okay, FabFitFun is going full send on new-subscriber acquisition right now — the "BEST SALE OF THE YEAR" framing is hard to miss, and the free bonus box push is obviously designed to pull in fresh annuals. As someone who cycles in and out, this page hits me right where I live, but it's treating me like a total stranger rather than someone who already knows the drill and wants to customize her fall box spoilers.

## What's working

- **"Get a FREE Bonus Box ($250 Value)"** — that dollar value anchor is everything for me. I instantly do the math: annual plan cost vs. what I'm getting back. This is exactly how I justify rejoining.
- **Sticky popup at the bottom** — "Get a FREE bonus box ($250 value) when you choose Annual Plan" with a "Claim Offer" button is smart double coverage. Even if I dismissed the hero, this catches my scroll hesitation.
- **"BEST SALE OF THE YEAR" label** — feels like a real event, not a permanent banner. That framing creates the sense that I'd be missing something if I left now.
- **Clean mobile layout** — nothing is broken, text is legible, and the CTA button is appropriately sized for a thumb tap. No fighting with the UI.
- **Sweepstakes ticker** — partially visible at the top ("chance at over $36,000 in pr…") adds another hook for someone like me who subscribes on value-maximizing behavior anyway.

## What's weak

- **"GET STARTED" CTA copy is new-subscriber language** — I've been here before. I know the box. "Rejoin" or "Claim My Bonus Box" would feel more personal and reduce the cognitive gap of "does this apply to me as a returning subscriber?"
- **No person in the hero image** — a striped tote and partial beauty products don't tell me what kind of woman is a FabFitFun person. I'd love to see a lifestyle shot that looks like me — unboxing at a kitchen counter, Mercari tab open in the background.
- **No urgency mechanism** — "BEST SALE OF THE YEAR" promises a big event, but there's no countdown, no "ends Sunday," nothing that makes me feel like I need to act now vs. in two weeks.
- **No trust signals** — not a single review count, star rating, or "X million subscribers" stat above the fold. I know the brand, but someone new (or me being skeptical after a cancellation) would want some social proof before clicking.
- **Category navigation invisible** — the hamburger menu is there, but I can't see what's inside. As someone who immediately goes to the customize spoilers page when I rejoin, there's no shortcut to that workflow.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "GET STARTED" for "Claim My Bonus Box"** — orients the CTA around the offer rather than the onboarding process; feels like I'm claiming something, not beginning a churn-heavy sign-up flow.
- **Add a countdown timer under the hero copy** — even "Offer ends in 3 days" would transform "BEST SALE OF THE YEAR" from a marketing claim into a credible deadline I'd act on.
- **Swap the product-only hero image for a lifestyle unboxing shot** — a woman in her 30s pulling items out of the FFF box, maybe with a phone showing the Mercari app, would speak directly to the audience that monetizes the subscription.
- **Add a one-line trust snippet under the CTA** — something like "Join 1M+ subscribers • Cancel anytime" would address the re-subscription anxiety without adding visual clutter.
