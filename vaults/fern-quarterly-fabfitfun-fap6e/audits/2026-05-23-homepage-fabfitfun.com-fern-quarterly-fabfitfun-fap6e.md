# Homepage snapshot · fabfitfun.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay, FabFitFun is going all-in on acquisition right now — "BEST SALE OF THE YEAR" with a free $250 bonus box dangled front and center. As someone who's been in and out of this subscription for a few years, I can see exactly what they're doing: they want my annual plan sign-up and they want it today. The page is clean and the offer lands, but it's treating me like I've never heard of FabFitFun before.

## What's working

- **The headline combo** — "BEST SALE OF THE YEAR" + "Get a FREE Bonus Box ($250 Value)" stacked together creates immediate value framing before I even read the subtext. As someone who evaluates every box for resale and keep value on Mercari, "$250 Value" is a number I actually want to interrogate.
- **The subtext does the job** — "curated boxes of your favorite brands up to 70% off" tells me exactly what the product is in one line. Zero ambiguity about what I'm buying into.
- **Sticky bottom bar** — The "Claim Offer" modal reinforces the Annual Plan benefit without interrupting the hero. I can dismiss it (X is visible) or act on it. That's good UX, not a dark pattern.
- **Live sweepstakes ticker** — Seeing "$36,000 in prizes" at the very top signals something is actively happening. Even if I miss the details (it's truncated), it creates a "something's going on" pull.
- **Clean visual hierarchy** — On mobile, this hero is basically perfect. One focal point, one CTA, no clutter.

## What's weak

- **Zero returning-subscriber recognition** — I have literally subscribed to FabFitFun before. This page has no idea. No "rejoin for X off," no referral code prompt, no "log in to see your member offer." For someone like me who times my rejoins around promos, this is a missed conversion.
- **No urgency mechanism beyond copy** — "BEST SALE OF THE YEAR" is a claim, not a countdown. No timer, no "offer ends May 26," nothing that makes me act *today* vs. tomorrow. As a high-tech-comfort user I know this is a soft signal.
- **No box content preview** — I want to know what's *in* the box I'm supposedly getting $250 of value from. The product shot shows a tote bag and what looks like a coffee tumbler, but I can't tell if that's the actual box or just lifestyle set dressing. Big miss for a habitual customizer like me.
- **Hamburger-only nav** — One tap to get anywhere? Nope. If I want to browse what's in the shop or see past box contents, I have to open the menu first. The page gives me exactly one path: sign up or leave.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Returning member? Rejoin here" secondary CTA below "GET STARTED"** — I know this brand, I've bought this subscription. Give me a lapsed-member flow with a referral or win-back code. It's a one-week build and it probably converts churned members at a much higher rate.
- **Swap the lifestyle product shot for a flat lay of actual current box items with prices** — Show me the $48 tote, the $32 serum, the $22 sunglasses all laid out with retail price tags. Make the "$250 Value" feel real and itemized, not abstract.
- **Add a countdown to the "BEST SALE OF THE YEAR" claim** — If this sale ends May 31, say so. A "Sale ends in X days" pill next to the headline would do more work than any copy change.
- **Surface a spoiler-customization hook** — Even a line like "Customize your box before it ships" below the CTA would resonate deeply with me. That feature is FabFitFun's biggest differentiator and it's nowhere on this page.
