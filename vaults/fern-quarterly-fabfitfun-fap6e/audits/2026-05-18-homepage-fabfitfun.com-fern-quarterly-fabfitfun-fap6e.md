# Homepage snapshot · fabfitfun.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is practically my homepage — FabFitFun is speaking directly to me in every pixel. The hero is pushing hard on a bonus box acquisition offer ("BEST SALE OF THE YEAR"), and the sticky overlay is doing double-duty trying to lock me into the Annual Plan specifically. This is a conversion-focused acquisition page wearing a lifestyle aesthetic, and honestly? It's working.

## What's working

- **The $250 free bonus box headline** lands immediately — concrete value, specific number, and I know FabFitFun boxes well enough to trust that figure's in the ballpark.
- **The "BEST SALE OF THE YEAR" label** above the hero copy gives me a reason to act now vs. waiting; feels like a real campaign moment, not evergreen copy.
- **The Annual Plan overlay at the bottom** is speaking directly to how I actually subscribe — I go annual every time I re-up, and this is the right nudge at the right moment.
- **The product image** — striped tote, sunglasses, water tumbler, pink bag — is styling the box contents rather than just stacking logos, which makes me curious what's inside and want to click.
- **"GET STARTED" CTA** is clean, high-contrast, above the fold with no competition from secondary buttons.

## What's weak

- **No referral or re-subscribe hook visible**: I'm literally the person who cancels and rejoins for the referral bonus, and there's zero surface for that journey. A "Refer a friend" or "Rejoining? Get an extra bonus" callout would catch me.
- **No social proof above the fold**: I can't see a single review count, subscriber number, or "as seen in" badge. For someone evaluating the rejoining math on Mercari resale value, peer validation matters.
- **The overlay modal competes with the hero CTA**: I'm looking at two conversion asks simultaneously — "GET STARTED" and "Claim Offer" — with slightly different framings. It creates a micro-pause of "wait, which one do I tap?"
- **Sweepstakes banner is clipped**: The top banner cuts off mid-sentence ("your chance at over $3...") and I can't see the prize or deadline. That's a missed urgency hook.

## Recommendations

- **Surface a "returning member" path in the nav or hero subtext**: even one line — "Already a member? Sign in for your loyalty offer" — catches re-subscribe cycles like mine without cluttering the page.
- **Swap the overlay timing**: let the hero CTA breathe for 3 seconds before the Annual Plan modal slides in, so both asks don't compete on first paint.
- **Add a subscriber count or top-line social proof chip** directly beneath the hero copy — "Loved by 1M+ subscribers" takes one line and removes the "is this legit?" hesitation for a re-evaluation visit.
- **Fix or expand the sweepstakes banner**: full prize value and a hard end date would turn a clipped teaser into a genuine urgency trigger.
