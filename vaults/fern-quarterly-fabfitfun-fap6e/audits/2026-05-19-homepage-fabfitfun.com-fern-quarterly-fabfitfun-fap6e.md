## What's working

- **The bonus box headline** — "Get a FREE Bonus Box ($250 Value)" is the most box-opener-brained CTA possible. I read it and my Mercari brain immediately starts calculating what I'd flip.
- **The stacked offer** — "up to 70% off" AND a free bonus box is double value. Smart. That's the reason I rejoin every other year on referral deals, and this feels like that energy.
- **The bottom sticky modal** — reinforcing the Annual Plan bonus specifically, without being obnoxious (has an X). It caught my eye right when I was about to process the hero.
- **The top banner sweepstakes hook** — "Dream Sweepstakes is NOW LIVE" creates a secondary reason to engage even if someone's on the fence about subscribing. I'd tap that.
- **Clean layout on mobile** — nothing is broken, text is readable at thumb size, button is easy to tap. No complaints there.

## What's weak

- **No member-returning hook** — I subscribe, cancel, and rejoin. This page has zero "welcome back" energy — it reads like a cold acquisition page. No rewards balance, no "last box was X, here's what's coming" tease. As a returning annual sub, I feel invisible.
- **No one in the photo** — the hero image is a product flatlay (bag, sunglasses, products), no lifestyle shot with a person. I'd engage more with a shot of someone who looks like me actually opening one of these boxes.
- **Vague "GET STARTED" CTA** — where does this go? Subscription configurator? Spoiler page? For a spoiler-customizer like me, "Build Your Box" or "Pick Your Box" would feel more specific and exciting.
- **No urgency mechanism** — "BEST SALE OF THE YEAR" is a bold claim but there's no countdown timer or end-date anchor to make me act now rather than later.
- **Trust signals missing** — no review counts, no star ratings, no "as seen in" badges. For a brand pushing a $250 value claim, some social proof would close skeptics fast.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "GET STARTED" for "Pick My Box" or "Build My Spring Box"** — much more specific, matches the spoiler-customizer mindset, and sets expectations for the next step.
- **Add a countdown timer or end-date under the hero** — even "Offer ends May 25" would spike urgency. Right now the "BEST SALE OF THE YEAR" claim floats without a deadline.
- **Add a lifestyle photo with a person** — even a single frame of someone opening the box would humanize the page and make the value feel real, not like a product catalog shot.
- **Add a thin trust strip below the CTA** — something like "⭐ 4.7 stars · 500K+ members · Cancel anytime" takes one line of space and eliminates the biggest objection for new-to-FFF visitors.
