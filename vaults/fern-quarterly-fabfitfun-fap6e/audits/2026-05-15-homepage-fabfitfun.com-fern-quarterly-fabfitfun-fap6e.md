# Homepage snapshot · fabfitfun.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so I immediately clock this as the FabFitFun annual subscription push — the whole page is basically one big "sign up now" funnel dressed up in a warm sand palette. For me specifically, this is relevant-as-hell: a free $250 bonus box is exactly the kind of offer that makes me hit rejoin after a cancel year. It's clean, it's focused, and it doesn't waste my time pretending to be something it's not.

## What's working

- **The free $250 bonus box headline** — dollar-valued hooks convert me better than percentage-off. $250 is a number I can weigh against my Mercari resell math.
- **"Up to 70% off" subtext** — gives me permission to keep reading and understand the value equation before I commit.
- **The product pile imagery** — the green-striped tote, rose water bottle, and sunglasses all look resaleable, which is genuinely relevant to how I think about boxes.
- **Annual plan popup** — yes, it's a layer, but it maps directly to how I actually subscribe (annual plan, cancel, rejoin) so it doesn't feel irrelevant noise.
- **Sweepstakes banner at the top** — partially visible but it signals "there's even more going on right now," which makes me curious to scroll.

## What's weak

- **"GET STARTED" is vague** — I'm a returning canceler; I don't need onboarding framing, I need "Rejoin Now" or "Claim Your Box." Start feels like I'm a brand-new subscriber they don't know.
- **No social proof anywhere above the fold** — no review count, no "join X million members," nothing. For a subscription I've already paid for before, I want validation that the curation hasn't slipped.
- **Hamburger-only nav** — I can't see categories, shop the market, or browse past box contents without tapping into a hidden menu. As someone who spoiler-customizes, I want shortcuts.
- **Annual plan popup timing** — the "Claim Offer" sheet is live before I've even finished reading the hero. It undercuts the main page message rather than reinforcing it.
- **No seasonal specificity** — "BEST SALE OF THE YEAR" is every quarter. I'd engage more with "Spring Box revealed" or a spoiler teaser.

## Recommendations

- **Replace "GET STARTED" with "Claim Your Free Box"** — mirrors the headline promise and removes the new-user framing that alienates lapsed subscribers like me.
- **Add a 3-item spoiler strip below the hero** — show me three items in the upcoming box with a "customize before cutoff" tag. That's the feature that differentiates FabFitFun and it's nowhere above the fold.
- **Delay or anchor the popup** — fire it on scroll-past-50% instead of immediately on load so the hero has a chance to land first.
- **Drop a trust line under the CTA** — something like "Over 1M subscribers • Cancel anytime" in small gray text. One line, huge conversion impact for skeptical rejoins.
