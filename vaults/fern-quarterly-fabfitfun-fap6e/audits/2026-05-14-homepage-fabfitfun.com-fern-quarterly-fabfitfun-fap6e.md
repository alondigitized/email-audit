# Homepage snapshot · fabfitfun.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- FabFitFun is going hard on a new-subscriber acquisition push — the whole above-the-fold is one big "join now for a free bonus box" pitch. As someone who has canceled and rejoined this exact brand multiple times for the referral bonus, I recognize the playbook immediately, and honestly? The $250 value claim hits different when you already know what's actually in those boxes. The warm tan aesthetic and curated product pile look clean enough, but there's zero acknowledgment that returning members exist.

## What's working

- **"BEST SALE OF THE YEAR" + FREE Bonus Box headline combo** — stacking a superlative campaign label on top of a tangible product value ($250) is exactly how you get Millennial box people to stop scrolling
- **The Annual Plan modal** — timing it right after the hero loads is smart; for someone like me who already knows I'd pick Annual anyway for the cost math, this is a shortcut, not an annoyance
- **Product pile imagery** — the striped tote, the skincare, the pink accessories all read "curated lifestyle haul," which is the whole brand promise made visual
- **Sweepstakes banner at top** — a NOW LIVE sweepstakes hook above everything else is a low-cost way to add urgency; I'd tap that to see what $3K+ in prizes looks like

## What's weak

- **"GET STARTED" CTA is vague** — for a returning subscriber rejoining on a new referral, "GET STARTED" reads like a brand-new onboarding flow, not a seamless rejoin. I don't know if this goes to a sign-up or a login
- **No trust signals above the fold** — zero review counts, no "as seen in," no star ratings anywhere visible; for a first-timer this matters, even if I personally know the brand
- **No urgency deadline** — "BEST SALE OF THE YEAR" implies scarcity but there's no "ends Sunday" or countdown timer. The claim floats without teeth
- **Hamburger-only nav** — on mobile I have zero category visibility without tapping into a menu, which adds friction if I want to check spoilers or box contents before committing
- **No returning-member pathway** — the whole page assumes I'm new. If I'm rejoining for a referral bonus, where do I even go?

## Recommendations

- **Swap "GET STARTED" for split CTAs**: "Join Now" for new members and a secondary "Sign In / Rejoin" link — takes one design sprint and would immediately reduce rejoin friction for subscribers like me
- **Add a deadline line under the hero copy**: something like "Offer ends May 18" turns "BEST SALE OF THE YEAR" into an actual reason to act today instead of bookmarking for later
- **Drop two trust badges below the CTA**: star rating aggregate ("4.7 stars, 200K+ members") and a press logo strip — visible without scrolling, costs nothing, moves conversion
- **Anchor the product imagery to a spoiler teaser**: instead of a generic beauty pile, label one item "Spring Box Preview" with a "see what's inside →" tap target — that's the content box openers actually want
