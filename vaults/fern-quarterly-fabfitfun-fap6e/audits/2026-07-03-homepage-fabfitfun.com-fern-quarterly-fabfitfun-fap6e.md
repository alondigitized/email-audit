# Homepage snapshot · fabfitfun.com · 2026-07-03
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-03**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is *very* me. FabFitFun is doing exactly what I expect from them — a big, bold annual subscription push timed to what they're calling their "Best Sale of the Year." The hero is laser-focused on getting new or returning subscribers to commit to an annual plan with a free bonus box dangled right at the top. Classic FFF acquisition energy, and honestly? It's working on me a little.

## What's working

- **The free bonus box callout** — "$250 FREE Bonus Box" with the strikethrough makes the value feel tangible and urgent. As someone who does the annual plan math every single year, this is exactly the hook I need.
- **"BEST SALE OF THE YEAR" badge with the 5-star row** — social proof framing up top builds credibility before you even read the headline. It reads like a community-validated moment, not just brand copy.
- **The bottom sticky modal** — "Get a FREE bonus box ($250 value) when you choose Annual Plan / Claim Offer" with a gift box emoji is a smart secondary nudge. I would normally be annoyed by modals but this one is small, dismissible, and reinforces the exact offer in the hero without repeating it verbatim.
- **"GET STARTED" CTA** — pill button, high contrast, clear action. No ambiguity about what happens next.

## What's weak

- **Zero product variety shown** — the flatlay in the hero is small and partially cropped. I can barely make out what's in the box. As someone who obsesses over spoilers and curates my preferences before every box, I want to see actual products, not a tan blob of items.
- **No returning member hook** — I cancel and rejoin every other year on referral bonuses. There's nothing here acknowledging that I might have a history with them, no "Welcome back" energy, no member pricing, no referral CTA.
- **No urgency signal** — "Best Sale of the Year" implies it's time-sensitive, but there's no countdown, no "ends Sunday," nothing. For someone who runs the annual plan math and waits for the best deal, I want a reason to act *now* rather than wait another week.
- **Navigation is a hamburger menu** — one tap doesn't take me anywhere useful; I'd have to drill into a menu to find customization options, spoilers, or seasonal box details. That's friction.

## Recommendations

- **Show actual products in the hero** — swap or expand the small flatlay to a grid of 4–6 identifiable full-size products with brand names visible. Quarterly box openers make decisions on curation quality; let the product do the selling.
- **Add a countdown timer** — "Offer ends July 7" with a live clock would push the fence-sitters (me) to commit now rather than screenshot and come back in three days.
- **Add a "Returning member? Rejoin here" secondary CTA** — even a small text link below the main button would capture the churn-and-return segment without cluttering the hero for new subscribers.
- **Surface one or two brand name drops in the sub-copy** — "Get curated boxes from brands like Tarte, Stanley, and Elemis up to 70% off" hits differently than the generic version. Name recognition closes.
