# Homepage snapshot · fabfitfun.com · 2026-08-07
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-07**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is very "we know exactly why you're here" energy — I land on fabfitfun.com and it's not even pretending to sell me individual products, it's going straight for the box hook with a fake-crossed-out $250 and a giant "FREE Bonus Box." Feels like a subscription brand in full acquisition mode, probably mid-promo push, and honestly it's the exact kind of screen that gets me to reconsider resubscribing on a deal.

## What's working

- **The "$250 FREE Bonus Box" strikeout price** — this is catnip for me. I already flip stuff I don't use on Mercari, so "free extra box" reads as pure profit to my brain.
- **"GET STARTED" button** — big, black, impossible to miss, sits right under the offer.
- **The product flatlay image** — real, textured, lots of little bottles and boxes, which is exactly what sells me on a curated box (I want to see stuff, not lifestyle fluff).

## What's weak

- **Top banner text is literally cut off** ("akes is NOW LIVE! Sign up today for your chance at over $39,000 in prize...") — that's a sweepstakes teaser I can't even read fully, feels sloppy.
- **The Annual Plan popup at the bottom is stacked on top of the sweepstakes banner** — two competing promos fighting for my attention before I've even scrolled.
- **No mention of what's actually IN a box or which season's box this is** — I'm a repeat/lapsed subscriber type, I want to know if this is the Fall box before I commit mentally.
- **"Best Sale of the Year" with no date or countdown** — I've seen that claim enough times that it doesn't create real urgency for me anymore.

## Recommendations

- Fix the sweepstachce banner so it's not truncated off-screen — either shorten the copy or make it fit the viewport.
- Put the Annual Plan condition ("$250 bonus box requires Annual Plan") right in the hero subcopy instead of a separate popup — I don't like discovering the catch after I'm already excited.
- Add a small "Fall 2026 Box" or season tag near the hero so returning shoppers like me know this is fresh, not a recycled banner.
- Swap "GET STARTED" for something more specific like "Build My Box" or "Shop This Box" — tells me exactly what tapping gets me.
