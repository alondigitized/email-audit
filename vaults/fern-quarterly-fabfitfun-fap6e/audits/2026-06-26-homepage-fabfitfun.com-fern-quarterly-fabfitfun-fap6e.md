# Homepage snapshot · fabfitfun.com · 2026-06-26
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the FabFitFun homepage and it is doing one thing hard: selling the annual subscription with a $250 bonus box hook. For me as someone who already subscribes, cancels, and rejoins on referral bonuses, this is practically a personalized ad — the "best sale of the year" framing + free bonus box is exactly the kind of re-join bait I fall for. It reads like a brand that knows its acquisition loop cold.

## What's working

- **Strike-through price anchor ($250 → FREE):** This is textbook loss aversion. I immediately calculated what I'd be "leaving on the table" and felt the pull.
- **"BEST SALE OF THE YEAR" five-star badge:** That phrasing triggers my deal-seeker brain — I'm conditioned to treat this like a Black Friday moment.
- **Sticky bottom banner ("Get a FREE bonus box ($250 value) when you choose Annual Plan — Claim Offer"):** Smart double-tap on the same offer. Keeps the conversion hook visible even as the hero draws the eye.
- **Product haul image:** The pile of recognizable lifestyle products (Stanley-style tumbler, beauty products, teal tote) reads as aspirational unboxing content, which is literally what I make.
- **Top sweep banner:** "Sweepstakes is NOW LIVE! Sign up today for your chance at over $36,000 in prizes" — that's an extra layer of urgency I hadn't even clicked into yet.

## What's weak

- **No returning-member login or "Welcome back" hook:** If I'm a lapsed subscriber or someone who cancelled after last year, there's nothing here acknowledging that I exist. This screen treats everyone as a cold lead.
- **No urgency signal on the offer itself:** "Best Sale of the Year" is a claim, not a deadline. No end date, no countdown, no "ends Sunday" — so I can come back tomorrow without feeling like I missed it.
- **Zero social proof on the hero:** No "1.2M+ members" callout, no review snippet, nothing that tells a first-time skeptic this is legit. The five stars are decorative, not attributed.
- **No visible category browsing:** As someone who reads every spoiler and researches what's in the box before committing, I want a path to "see what's inside" — it's not here above the fold.

## Recommendations

- **Add a countdown timer to the hero:** "BEST SALE OF THE YEAR ends in 2d 14h 32m" would convert my "I'll do it later" into "okay, right now."
- **Add "See what's in the Summer Box →" beneath the CTA:** I don't sign up blind — I spoiler-hunt obsessively. One tap to the box contents page would move me from interested to converted.
- **Swap the decorative ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ for a real attribution:** "★★★★★ 4.8 from 42,000 reviews" costs two words and earns the trust signal.
- **Add a returning-member re-join path:** A small "Already a member? Rejoin and keep your bonus" link under the CTA would capture the churned-subscriber reactivation cohort — which, based on my own behavior, is probably huge.
