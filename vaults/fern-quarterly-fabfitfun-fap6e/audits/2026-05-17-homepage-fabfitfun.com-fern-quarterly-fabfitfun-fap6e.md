# Homepage snapshot · fabfitfun.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Oh, this is literally MY brand. FabFitFun is leading with a "Best Sale of the Year" push and dangling a free $250 bonus box for new annual subscribers — and I've absolutely been that person who cancels, waits for a deal like this, and rejoins. The homepage is clean, the offer is loud, and the vibe screams "lifestyle curation for people who love a good unboxing moment."

## What's working

- **The "$250 Value" framing** — that's the exact psychological hook that works on box-openers. We do the math in our heads immediately.
- **"BEST SALE OF THE YEAR" label** — risky claim, but it's bold. It made me stop scrolling mentally and go "oh, is it though?"
- **The bottom Annual Plan modal** with the gift-box graphic and "Claim Offer" CTA is actually well-placed. It doesn't hijack the whole screen and has an easy X — I don't hate it.
- **Warm tan background** gives the page a curated, editorial feel rather than shouty e-commerce. It feels like a brand I trust to pick nice things.
- **Product imagery** — the tote, the sunglasses, the hint of beauty products at the bottom — communicates lifestyle without a model, which actually works here.

## What's weak

- **"GET STARTED" is generic.** As someone who has subscribed before, this CTA reads like they don't know I exist. "Claim My Bonus Box" or "Rejoin + Get the Free Box" would hit harder.
- **No existing member path visible.** Zero "Sign In" prominence. If I'm a lapsed subscriber, I don't know if this offer applies to me or if I need a new account. That friction will make me bail.
- **Sweepstakes banner is clipped.** The top announcement bar is cut off on both ends — "...am Sweepstakes is NOW LIVE! Sign up today for your chance to over $3..." — I can't read the prize amount or brand name. That's a miss on potentially exciting copy.
- **No social proof above the fold.** No "4.8 stars from 50,000 subscribers" or a reviewer quote. For new-ish visitors, trust signals are absent entirely.
- **No urgency mechanism.** "Best Sale of the Year" is a claim, not a deadline. A countdown or "ends Sunday" would actually motivate me to act now vs. tab away.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "GET STARTED" for "Claim My Free Bonus Box"** — action-specific, value-forward, no ambiguity about what tapping does.
- **Add a "Returning member? Sign in here" micro-link** beneath the main CTA — captures lapsed subscribers like me without disrupting the acquisition flow.
- **Fix the sweepstakes banner** — either make it wider or shorten the copy so the prize amount is readable. A cut-off "$3..." is worse than no banner.
- **Drop a single trust line under the CTA** — e.g. "Join 1M+ members · Cancel anytime · Ships free" — costs nothing and answers the objections I have before I bounce.
