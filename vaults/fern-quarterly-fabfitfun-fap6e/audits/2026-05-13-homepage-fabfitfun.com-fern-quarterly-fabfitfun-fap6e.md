# Homepage snapshot · fabfitfun.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- FabFitFun is literally my brand — I subscribe annually for the referral bonuses and this page feels immediately familiar. Right now it's all-in on acquisition mode: the hero is screaming "free box" and a sticky modal is double-dipping on the same offer before I've even scrolled. Clean hierarchy, familiar palette, but it completely ignores the fact that I'm a returning member.

## What's working

- **"BEST SALE OF THE YEAR" + $250 bonus box framing**: The dollar-value anchor is exactly the kind of hook that makes me rationalize re-subscribing every other cycle. This is how they get me every time.
- **"GET STARTED" CTA**: Black pill button on a warm beige background has strong contrast and occupies prime real estate. Hard to miss.
- **Product teaser imagery**: The striped tote, sunglasses, tumbler, and skincare products peeking below the CTA are doing smart work — they're showing me the box without overwhelming me with it.
- **Top sweepstakes banner**: "NOW LIVE" creates a time-sensitive layer on top of the base offer. Didn't expect it and it made me pause.

## What's weak

- **Zero returning-member recognition**: I've subscribed to this brand. There's no "Welcome back," no rewards balance teaser, no "Your next box ships soon" hook. The page treats me like a cold stranger, which feels like a missed opportunity on something like a subscription box where loyalty is everything.
- **Sticky modal is redundant**: "Get a FREE bonus box ($250 value) when you choose Annual Plan / Claim Offer" — this is the exact same offer already in the hero, just repeated in a blue overlay that covers the bottom 30% of my product image. It feels desperate, not persuasive.
- **Zero social proof above the fold**: No "Join 2M+ members," no star rating, no "as seen in" badge. For a subscription box that lives or dies on community trust, this is a real gap.
- **"up to 70% off" is a hedge**: That "up to" qualifier is classic subscription box language and I notice it. Not enough to make me leave, but it creates a beat of skepticism.

## Recommendations

- **Rename the CTA**: Swap "GET STARTED" for "Claim My Free Box" or "Get My Annual Box" — anchor the button to the specific offer so I know exactly what I'm clicking into.
- **Add a social-proof strip below the hero**: A single line — "4.8 stars · 2,000,000+ members" with a trust badge — would do more for conversion confidence than the second offer modal.
- **Surface a member state for returning visitors**: Even a cookie-recognized "Welcome back, Fern — check your box customization options" banner would flip this from cold acquisition to relationship marketing.
- **Convert the sticky modal into an urgency clock**: I've already seen the free box offer. Use that sticky real estate for something new — a countdown to when the Annual Plan bonus expires — rather than repeating the hero.
