# Homepage snapshot · fabfitfun.com · 2026-06-05
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-05**
## Executive summary

- Okay, FabFitFun is swinging for the fences — this is a full-on acquisition play, leading with a "$250 FREE Bonus Box" offer that's hard to ignore. It reads like a brand that knows exactly what it is: a curated subscription box with a strong value story. Right now they're pushing an annual plan conversion hard, which, as someone who genuinely cycles through FFF every other year for the referral bonus, is basically speaking my language.

## What's working

- **The "$250 FREE Bonus Box" hero** — the strikethrough price next to "FREE" is a classic anchoring trick and it works. I felt the pull immediately.
- **The floating Annual Plan banner** — this is a smart persistent layer for lapsed subscribers like me. "Claim Offer" is low-commitment copy. The gift-box emoji softens it.
- **5-star badge + "BEST SALE OF THE YEAR" label** — gives the offer social proof and campaign legitimacy above the fold without being loud.
- **Sweepstakes ticker at the top** — "$36,000 in prizes" over a scrolling banner is a great reason to linger. I want to know what that is.
- **Product flat-lay** — I can actually see what's in the box. Skincare, supplements, a bag. That's how I evaluate whether a box is worth rejoining for.

## What's weak

- **"GET STARTED" CTA is vague** — started on what? Joining? Customizing? As someone who's been through this flow before, I know what it means, but a first-timer might hesitate. "Claim My Free Box" would convert better.
- **No urgency deadline** — "BEST SALE OF THE YEAR" implies scarcity but there's no "ends June 8" or countdown. I'm not sprinting to act.
- **No visible review count** — the 5 stars appear but there's no "(14,823 reviews)" underneath. That's free social proof left on the table.
- **No spoiler/customization mention** — spoiler customization is a huge retention and acquisition hook for this brand and I can't see it anywhere above the fold. That's my favorite thing about FFF and it's invisible here.
- **Navigation is hamburger-only** — for a returning subscriber who wants to log in and check their spoilers, there's no fast path visible.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "GET STARTED" for "Claim My Free Bonus Box"** — more specific, matches the hero offer, and gives the click more intent.
- **Add a spoiler teaser in the hero subhead** — something like "Customize your box before it ships" would differentiate FFF from generic subscription boxes instantly, and it's the thing that keeps me subscribed.
- **Drop a hard deadline on the sale** — even "offer ends Sunday" creates urgency without feeling manipulative. Right now "BEST SALE OF THE YEAR" is floating untethered.
- **Surface the review count under the 5 stars** — one small text addition that significantly raises conversion trust, especially for prospects who don't know the brand.
