# Homepage snapshot · fabfitfun.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- Okay, FabFitFun gets it — this is a subscription box brand that immediately leads with its product promise, and as someone who literally lives for unboxing day, I felt seen before I even read a word. The hero is clean, the value prop is front and center, and the free bonus box angle is exactly the kind of hook that made me sign up the first time (and the second, and the third after I rejoined on a referral). Right now it's pushing hard on annual-plan conversion, which I respect — they know the LTV math.

## What's working

- **"BEST SALE OF THE YEAR" label above the headline** — I know an urgency play when I see one, and calling it the best sale of the year is a bold claim that makes me stop scrolling to see if they're serious.
- **The $250 value bonus box anchor** — This is exactly how I think about FFF deals: what's the per-box value vs. what I paid. They're speaking my math.
- **"Up to 70% off" in the subtext** — Specific enough to feel real, and 70% is a number that makes me do mental math about my Mercari resale margin instantly.
- **Annual Plan sticky modal at the bottom** — Two CTAs working together (hero "GET STARTED" + "Claim Offer" modal) means wherever my thumb lands, something converts. Smart layering.
- **Warm, cohesive art direction** — The tan/gold background with the tote and beauty products feels intentional and premium, not cluttered. I'd screenshot this for inspo.

## What's weak

- **"GET STARTED" is vague CTA copy** — For a returning subscriber who's debating re-joining, "Get Started" feels like it's talking past me. "Rejoin & Claim Bonus" or "Start My Annual Plan" would close harder.
- **No people in the hero** — The product flat-lay is cute, but I want to see *someone* opening this box. Lifestyle shots of women my age doing the unboxing ritual are what convert me personally.
- **No countdown or deadline visible** — "BEST SALE OF THE YEAR" is a claim without a clock. If it ends Sunday, show me the Sunday. The urgency banner is doing all the heavy lifting without the actual mechanism.
- **No navigation categories above fold** — Hamburger menu only means I have zero idea what's inside without tapping. If I want to browse the shop (for items I'd resell), I have no shortcut.
- **The sweepstakes banner at the top is cut off** — "stakes is NOW LIVE! Sign up today for your chance to over $36,000 in pri..." — that's a big hook that's basically unreadable at first glance.

## Recommendations

- **Fix the top sweepstakes banner** — A $36,000 sweepstakes is genuinely exciting; make the full sentence visible or give it its own dedicated row that doesn't get clipped at mobile viewport. This is money left on the table.
- **Swap "GET STARTED" for plan-specific copy** — "Claim My Free Bonus Box" or "Start Annual Plan" ties the CTA directly to the offer and removes ambiguity for someone landing cold.
- **Add a lifestyle shot of a woman unboxing** — Even a circular inset over the product flat-lay. Show the experience, not just the haul.
- **Add a visible deadline to the Annual Plan modal** — "Offer ends May 26" next to the Claim Offer button would turn that sticky widget from passive to urgent.
