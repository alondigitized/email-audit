# Homepage snapshot · fabfitfun.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Okay, FabFitFun knows exactly what they're doing here — this is a full-court press acquisition page dressed up as a "best sale of the year" moment. As someone who has rejoined this exact brand twice already, the bones of this pitch are very familiar to me: big bonus box, big dollar value, one button to start. It's a subscription-in-a-box brand and the homepage makes zero effort to be anything else, which I actually respect.

## What's working

- **"BEST SALE OF THE YEAR" label** — sets the stakes before I even read the headline; creates FOMO without being manipulative
- **The $250 value anchor** — that number appears twice (headline + floating modal), which is smart reinforcement for a purchase-consideration moment
- **The curated product shot** — the tote, the beauty jars, the sunglasses instantly communicate "curated lifestyle box" without me having to read a word of copy; I get it in one glance
- **The floating "Claim Offer" modal** — annoying in principle, but the X is visible and the message is clear; it doubles down on Annual Plan without blocking the primary CTA
- **The sweepstakes banner** — a sweepstakes on top of a bonus box offer on top of a discount is layered value that actually lands for my shopping brain

## What's weak

- **"GET STARTED" CTA copy** — vague. Started what? I'd rather see "Claim My Free Box" or "Join Annual Plan" so I know what the tap commits me to
- **No returning member path** — I have rejoined this brand before. There is nothing on this screen that acknowledges I might be coming back, no "already a member? Log in for your deal" hook
- **No referral bonus visibility** — I literally rejoin on referral bonuses; that is my entire relationship with this brand, and it is nowhere above the fold
- **No trust signals** — no review count, no "join X million members," no press badges; for someone on the fence, that absence is notable
- **No countdown or deadline** — "Best Sale of the Year" implies urgency but doesn't deliver it; a timer would have converted me faster

## Recommendations

- **Change "GET STARTED" to "Claim My Free Box"** — the current copy makes me think I'm starting a quiz; the replacement tells me exactly what I'm getting
- **Add a returning-member module or login CTA** below the hero — something like "Already subscribed? Log in to see your member deal" with a secondary link; loyal-churners like me are a real segment
- **Surface the referral bonus in or near the hero** — even a line like "Know someone who's a member? Get an extra $X off" turns my existing network into a conversion layer
- **Swap the product flatlay for a lifestyle shot with a person** — a woman my age unboxing on a couch or at her kitchen table would make this feel aspirational instead of transactional
