# Homepage snapshot · www.priceline.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- I opened Priceline and immediately got hit with three layers of interference before I could see a single hotel or flight deal: a "10% OFF" promotional banner, a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" consent notice, and a bot-check modal demanding I press and hold to prove I'm human. As someone who knows Booking Holdings owns this whole family — Booking.com, Kayak, OpenTable, the lot — I expect them to have figured out how to let a real customer land on the homepage by now. Right now this feels less like a travel site and more like a bouncer at a club I'm not sure I want to get into.

## What's working

- **"10% OFF" banner** — Even behind the stacked modals, I can see it teasing a discount. As someone who lives for the reveal, this does make me want to dismiss the junk and find out what it applies to.
- **VIP JOIN badge in the nav** — The gold "VIP" pill is visible at a glance. I know this program, I'm in it, and seeing it confirmed tells me I'm at least on the right site.
- **Priceline logo + hamburger nav** — Clean, instantly recognizable brand mark. No identity confusion.

## What's weak

- **Bot-check modal ("Before we continue… Press & Hold")** — This is the most damaging element on the page. I'm a real customer on my phone and I'm being made to prove my humanity before I can see a single deal. Hostile first impression.
- **Privacy consent layered *under* the bot check** — So I can't even dismiss the consent notice without first solving the captcha. The stacking order is backwards and makes both feel even more adversarial.
- **"10% OFF" is teasing but unfinished** — I can see the number but I can't see what it applies to. Is it hotels? Flights? Express Deals only? "Up to 10%"? This is exactly the headline-vs-fine-print game I hate.
- **"JOIN VIP" framing** — I'm already VIP Diamond. Showing me a join prompt instead of recognizing me (even without a cookie) is a miss.
- **Zero homepage content visible** — No hero imagery, no search bar, no deal tiles, no category navigation. Nothing to evaluate below the fold because I can't get past the fold.

## Recommendations

- **Fix the modal stacking order** — Dismiss the bot-check first, *then* surface the consent notice. Better yet: detect mobile browsers and swap the Press & Hold captcha for a tap-based alternative that doesn't feel like a test.
- **Reveal the 10% off terms immediately on dismissal** — When the banner is the first thing a returning customer sees, the second thing should be exactly what it applies to. "10% off Express Deals for VIP members, this weekend only" is infinitely more compelling than a naked percentage.
- **Add a "Welcome back" signal in the nav for logged-in / cookied users** — Even a "Sign In to see your rate" hook would acknowledge I'm a repeat visitor, not a cold acquisition target.
- **Swap the generic consent interstitial for a footer link** — CCPA/CPRA notice doesn't need to be a full-page modal. Put it in the footer with a one-tap opt-out and get out of my way.
