# Homepage snapshot · www.priceline.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Oh, they brought The Negotiator back — I actually felt a flicker of nostalgia before I caught myself. This is Priceline in pure search-engine mode: one massive form, no deals surfaced, no prices teased. They're banking on brand recognition doing the heavy lifting while quietly burying everything that used to make this site interesting — like, where are my Express Deals? Where's my VIP Diamond balance?

## What's working

- **"The Negotiator is back"** — I know exactly what this means. Priceline = deal hunting. One headline does the brand positioning without a paragraph of corporate copy.
- **"Hotel prices now shown with fees"** — This is the trust line I needed. The old Priceline used to hide resort fees until checkout. Calling it out upfront earns a point with me.
- **Bundle + Save checkbox** — Visible right in the form, no digging. I always check whether bundling a car actually saves anything, and at least the option is surfaced.
- **Tab navigation (Hotels / Flights / Packages / Cars / Cruises)** — Category switching in one tap, exactly where it should be.
- **Date pre-filled to today** — Removes friction; the form already knows I might be searching for tonight.

## What's weak

- **No deal surfaced above the fold** — This is Priceline. You built your entire brand on price aggression. Show me something. A "Tonight's Express Deals from $49" strip would do more than The Negotiator's ghost.
- **VIP Diamond is invisible** — If I'm already a member with status, "JOIN VIP" is an insult. I want to see my tier, my points balance, maybe a member-rate badge. Right now the logged-out state treats me like a stranger.
- **No chain transparency** — I know Priceline, Booking.com, Kayak, and Agoda are all Booking Holdings. But the homepage gives me no signal about which properties I'll be booking through, or whether I can stack a portal like Chase Travel on top. That fine print matters to me.
- **No imagery whatsoever** — Not a single hotel photo, destination shot, or lifestyle image. It is a form on a white background. That is not inspiration; that's a utility widget.
- **No Express Deals or Name Your Own Price callout** — These were the differentiators. If they still exist, they're completely hidden from the homepage.

## Recommendations

- **Add an Express Deals strip directly below the search form** — "Tonight: NYC from $79, Miami from $64" pulls in exactly the kind of deal-seeker who came to Priceline specifically for that. One horizontal scroll rail, live inventory.
- **Show logged-in VIP state in the header** — Replace "JOIN VIP" with tier + points balance for recognized members. Even a cookie-matched "Welcome back, VIP Diamond" would signal that this site knows me.
- **Add a transparent "How pricing works" tooltip next to the fees line** — I read the small print twice anyway; make it easy and I'll trust you more, not less.
- **Swap the blank white background for a destination thumbnail grid** — Six city tiles behind the search widget costs nothing and gives the eye somewhere to land while I'm thinking about where to go.
