# Homepage snapshot · www.priceline.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- I opened Priceline and immediately got hit with a bot-check wall, a privacy consent modal, AND a half-blocked promo banner all at once — I can barely tell I'm on a travel site. As a VIP Diamond member who knows exactly how this ecosystem works (hi, Booking Holdings), I expect to land on deals, not a human-verification quiz. This feels like a site that prioritizes legal cover and anti-scraping over the actual customer standing at the door.

## What's working

- **VIP badge + "Sign In / JOIN VIP"** in the top-right header: I see it immediately and it signals this site recognizes returning members — that's the right instinct for a loyalty-stacker like me
- **"10% OFF" banner**: Even partially obscured, a numeric discount is more honest than "up to X% off" — I can at least see a real number

## What's weak

- **Triple-modal pile-up**: A bot-verification overlay, a CCPA privacy notice, AND a promo banner are all fighting for the screen at the same time — I cannot see the actual homepage at all. This is a conversion killer, full stop
- **"Press & Hold to confirm you are a human"**: On a phone, this is clunky and vaguely insulting. I'm a VIP Diamond member — I've been giving Priceline my data for years, and this is how I'm greeted?
- **"10% OFF" — off what?**: I can see the number but not the category, conditions, or whether my VIP stacking applies. Classic bait framing I've been burned by before
- **Zero travel context above the fold**: No destination imagery, no "Hotels / Flights / Cars" nav, nothing that tells me I'm on a travel site until the modals clear

## Recommendations

- **Suppress bot-check and privacy modals for returning/signed-in users** — if I have a VIP session cookie, skip the human verification entirely; it destroys the loyalty experience you're supposedly investing in
- **Serialize the modals**: Show one thing at a time — promo banner first, then privacy consent on a second visit; never stack three overlays simultaneously
- **Make the 10% off copy earn its keep**: Add "on hotels" or "for VIP members" directly on the banner so I don't have to tap through to find out it's only for Pay Now bookings on specific chains
- **Pin the search/booking bar above all overlays** so that even mid-modal, I can see the familiar "Where are you going?" field and know I'm in the right place
