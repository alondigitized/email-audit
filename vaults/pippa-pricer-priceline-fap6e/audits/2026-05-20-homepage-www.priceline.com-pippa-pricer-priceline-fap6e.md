# Homepage snapshot · www.priceline.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- I opened Priceline and immediately got hit with three things stacked on top of each other: a bot check, a cookie consent wall, and a 10% off promo banner — and I cannot see a single hotel, car, or flight. As someone who knows this whole family tree (Booking Holdings, Kayak, OpenTable, the whole roster), I expect friction, but this is a new low. The page is essentially a hostage situation before I've even seen what I'm supposedly getting 10% off of.

## What's working

- **"JOIN VIP" nav prompt** — it's there, top right, and for a returning Diamond member like me, at least the brand remembers it has a loyalty tier
- **10% OFF banner** — the number is legible and blue-on-blue high-contrast; something is being promoted even if I can't verify it
- **Priceline wordmark** — instantly recognizable, no identity confusion about what site I'm on

## What's weak

- **Bot-verification modal ("Press & Hold")** takes over the entire viewport — I cannot see the homepage at all; I'm being interrogated before I've done anything
- **Cookie/privacy modal ("Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information")** is layered on top of the homepage mid-scroll, adding a second full-screen interrupt
- **10% OFF — off what?** No category, no product type, no minimum spend visible; this is exactly the "up to X% off" headline I read the fine print on twice
- **No search widget visible** — Priceline's entire value prop is the search/Name Your Own Price mechanic, and I can't see it at all
- **Sign In is visible but no personalized returning-customer hook** — I have VIP Diamond status and nothing on this screen acknowledges it past the generic JOIN CTA
- **Three competing interrupts simultaneously** — bot check + cookie wall + promo banner is too much friction before I've seen one hotel card

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the bot-check modal on organic mobile traffic** — or at minimum show it *after* I've seen the hero; greeting me with "prove you're human" kills trust immediately
- **Sequence the interrupts** — never show cookie consent + promo + bot verification at the same time; pick one, resolve it, move on
- **Unblind the 10% off offer instantly** — add "on hotels this weekend" or "on car rentals with code X" directly on the banner so skeptics like me don't dismiss it as a bait headline
- **Surface the VIP Diamond balance/status on load for signed-in users** — I stack portal cashback on top of Diamond; showing my balance before I even search is the one personalization signal that would make me feel seen
