# Homepage snapshot · www.priceline.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Priceline threw two walls in my face before I could see a single hotel or flight deal — a CCPA privacy modal and a "Press & Hold to prove you're human" bot check, both active at the same time. I know Booking Holdings owns this whole property, I've been a VIP Diamond member for years, and this is the welcome I get? I can barely see the "10% off" banner hiding behind the chaos. This feels less like a travel booking homepage and more like a security checkpoint at a budget airport.

## What's working

- **"JOIN VIP" CTA in the nav bar** — as a VIP Diamond stacker, I clocked this immediately; the P rewards icon next to it tells me my program is front-and-center in the hierarchy
- **"10% OFF" in the hero** — even buried behind a modal, I can see a real number, not "up to 60% off select items if you stand on one leg"; that's progress for Priceline

## What's weak

- **Double-modal assault** — a CCPA "Do Not Sell" notice AND a bot-verification "Press & Hold" challenge are both live simultaneously, completely blocking the homepage; I cannot see product, navigation, or any deal content
- **Bot check wording** — "Press & Hold to confirm you are a human (and not a bot)" is jarring on a travel site I've used for two decades; it reads like I've been flagged as suspicious, not welcomed back
- **Privacy modal placement** — it fires at the same time as the bot check, creating visual chaos with no clear sequence; which one do I answer first?
- **10% off with no context** — I can see the number but not what it applies to; is it hotels? flights? rental cars? Booking Holdings properties only? As someone who reads the small print twice, no context = no trust

## Recommendations

- **Serialize the interstitials** — resolve the bot check first, then surface the CCPA notice; never stack two full-screen modals simultaneously
- **Add "VIP Diamond, welcome back" personalization** in the nav or hero for signed-in loyalty members; I should not be asked to "JOIN VIP" when I already am one
- **Show the 10% off terms inline on the banner** — two lines of fine print ("on hotels, for VIP members, through Sunday") would neutralize my skepticism before I even dismiss the modal
- **Replace the generic "Press & Hold" bot check** with a less confrontational flow (silent fingerprinting or a lighter CAPTCHA) that doesn't make a returning Diamond member feel like a criminal
