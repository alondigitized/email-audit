# Homepage snapshot · www.priceline.com · 2026-06-11
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-11**
## Executive summary

- OK, they brought back The Negotiator. I'll admit that got a tiny smirk out of me — very deliberate Gen X bait, and I caught it. But here's the thing: Priceline is promising me a hotel deal without actually showing me one. The whole above-the-fold is just a search box. If you're going to invoke William Shatner, you better have an Express Deal with a real number attached. Right now this feels like a placeholder dressed up as a homecoming.

## What's working

- **"The Negotiator is back"** — This headline is doing real work for someone my age. It's a cultural shortcut that says "we remember you, we're still in the deal business." I felt it.
- **VIP / JOIN VIP in the header** — I'm a VIP Diamond member and it's visible without digging. It signals the loyalty program is still alive.
- **Search widget is front and center** — No carousel noise, no lifestyle photo getting in my way. The utilitarian layout actually suits a deal-hunter who knows where she's going.
- **"Bundle + Save" with Add a flight / Add a car checkboxes** — This is the feature I actually use to stack savings. It's right there in the form, not buried.
- **Date pre-filled with today** — Small detail, but it means the page is live and current. I don't have to clear stale placeholder dates.

## What's weak

- **No actual deal visible** — The promise is "deal awaits" but I see zero dollars off, zero percent, no Express Deal teaser, no "Hotels from $79 tonight." The Negotiator metaphor without a number is just marketing.
- **VIP has no teeth above the fold** — My Diamond status should show member pricing or an exclusive badge on something. Right now "JOIN VIP" looks like a sign-up CTA aimed at strangers, not a returning member's dashboard.
- **No Express Deals entry point** — That's the product I came here for. Name Your Own Price is gone (I've accepted it), but Express Deals was the successor and it's invisible above the fold.
- **No trust signals** — No "100,000+ hotel reviews" badge, no partner logos, no secure-checkout mention. For a site where I'm handing over real money before I see the hotel name, that's an oversight.
- **Zero imagery** — The whole viewport is text and form fields. No aspirational travel photo, no lifestyle shot. "Deal awaits" with a white background is not exactly inspiring.

## Recommendations

- **Add one concrete teaser number to the hero** — "Hotels from $59 tonight — Express Deals" or "VIP members save an extra 10%" placed directly under the headline. The Negotiator landing with a real number would close the loop.
- **Personalize the VIP badge for signed-in members** — Show "Hi Pippa — Diamond status" or at minimum "VIP Members: exclusive rates inside" so returning users feel recognized, not pitched at.
- **Surface an Express Deals shortcut tab** — Right next to Hotels / Flights in the tab bar, add "Express Deals" as a sixth option. Power users are hunting for it and currently have no fast path.
- **Add a single trust strip above the CTA button** — One line: "Trusted by 100M+ travelers · Best Price Guarantee · 24/7 support." Costs nothing to ship, addresses the biggest hesitation for first-time and returning bookers alike.
