# Homepage snapshot · www.priceline.com · 2026-07-02
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-02**
## Executive summary

- I opened Priceline and immediately got three things fighting for my attention: a half-visible "10% OFF" banner, a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" cookie wall sliding up from the bottom, and then a bot-check modal screaming "Press & Hold to confirm you are a human" plastered over all of it. I haven't seen an actual hotel deal yet. This feels less like a travel booking site and more like an obstacle course designed by a compliance team and a fraud-prevention vendor who've never spoken to each other.

## What's working

- **VIP header treatment** — the "P" loyalty icon and "Sign In / JOIN VIP" text show up immediately in the nav. As someone who stacks VIP Diamond, I at least know this site acknowledges I exist.
- **10% OFF banner** — before it gets buried, that blue flash of a number is the right instinct: lead with a real number, not "up to savings you won't believe."
- **Clean nav bar** — hamburger, logo, loyalty icon, sign-in. It's stripped down and doesn't try to do too much. That part I respect.

## What's weak

- **Bot-check modal ("Before we continue… Press & Hold")** — this is a hard wall on first load. I'm a 43-year-old Gen X woman trying to check hotel rates for a weekend trip, not a credential-stuffing script. Starting our relationship with an accusation is not great.
- **Simultaneous privacy modal stacking on top of the bot modal** — two interrupt dialogs at once is not UX, it's chaos. The privacy notice is also truncated mid-sentence ("to remember your device and…") — I can't even finish reading what I'm consenting to before the bot wall covers it.
- **Zero visible deals or destinations below the fold** — because there is no below the fold. Everything is blocked. I have no idea what Priceline wants me to do on this site today.
- **"10% OFF" with no chain revealed** — I know Booking Holdings runs the whole family (Kayak, Booking.com, Hotels.com). This 10% — off rack rate? Off a member rate? Off a rate that was inflated before the discount? The chain is not revealed.

## Recommendations

- **Sequence the interruptions** — bot check first, then privacy notice, never both simultaneously. Better yet, move bot verification to a lighter passive fingerprint and surface the privacy opt-out in the footer where it legally needs to live anyway.
- **Put the VIP deal in the hero with the chain spelled out** — "VIP Diamond members save 10% off published rates on 800K+ properties" is a sentence I'll actually read. "10% OFF" floating in a blue box tells me nothing.
- **Give me one actionable tap above the fold** — a search bar pre-filled for this weekend, or a "Your saved hotels" shortcut for returning members. I signed into VIP for a reason; surface something that proves you know me.
- **Kill the bot modal or move it to suspicious-signal-only triggers** — a mobile user on a fresh page load should not be asked to prove they're human on the first pixel.
