# Homepage snapshot · www.priceline.com · 2026-08-13
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- Well, look who's still kicking — "The Negotiator" himself, up on the hero pointing at his phone screaming "up to 60%." That's the Priceline I remember, the one that made its whole brand on Name Your Own Price theater. Right now they're pushing hotel deals and a "Rental Car Week — 25% off" strip, but no mention of the actual chain I'll get, and no VIP status pricing shown up top, which is the part I actually care about.

## What's working

- The Negotiator hero is a strong nostalgia play — that's literally why I still use this site over Expedia
- "Hotel prices now shown with fees included" fine print under the search box — a small honesty nod I appreciate, since buried resort fees are my number one pet peeve
- "JOIN VIP" badge sits right next to Sign In, so the loyalty tier isn't hidden in a hamburger menu somewhere
- The search widget defaults to today's date and 2 adults/1 room — saves me a few taps, which matters on my phone

## What's weak

- "Up to 60%" on the phone mockup graphic — up to 60% off *what*, exactly? No hotel name, no chain reveal, no minimum. That's the vague headline number that makes me squint
- "Rental Car Week: 25% off, no detours" — cute copy but zero specifics on which brands or blackout dates, so I don't trust it yet
- No VIP member pricing or cashback stacking messaging visible anywhere above the fold — for someone who stacks portal cashback, that's a missed above-the-fold moment
- The hero photo dominates the right third of the screen with padding-heavy circles that add nothing functional — feels like space that could've shown an actual deal card

## Recommendations

- Put a real member-price example next to "JOIN VIP" — e.g., "VIP Diamond members save an extra 10%" — so the value of signing in is obvious in one glance
- Swap the vague "Up to 60%" phone graphic callout for one real, specific example: hotel name, city, discount, so the Negotiator bit has teeth
- Add a one-line "unblinded chain reveal" trust note near the search box for people like me who've been burned by mystery-hotel bookings before
- Tighten the decorative blue circles behind the hero photo — that's dead space that could hold a second CTA or a "Recently viewed" rail
