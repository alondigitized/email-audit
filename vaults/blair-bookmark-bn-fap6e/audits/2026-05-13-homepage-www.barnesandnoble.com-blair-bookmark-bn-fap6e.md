# Homepage snapshot · www.barnesandnoble.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- This is exactly what I'd expect to see from B&N right now — they lead hard with the Book Club pick and dangle the Premium Membership front and center. It reads like a brand that knows its audience is readers who want curation and a deal, not a scrollable warehouse. The hierarchy is clean and the book choice is doing real work for me specifically, but I immediately notice that the membership banner is talking to people who haven't joined yet, not to me — a card-carrying member who just wants to see my rewards and get to the shelves.

## What's working

- **Book Club hero**: "Good Joy, Bad Joy" by Mikki Brammer with its painted floral cover is genuinely beautiful and the subtitle copy ("friendship, self-discovery and finding peace") is doing emotional heavy lifting for exactly my demographic. I'd stop scrolling here.
- **Membership banner placement**: Putting "Extra 10% Off & Earn Rewards" immediately below the nav means even impulse visitors see the value prop before anything else. That's smart real estate.
- **"Pick Up in Store: Ready in Two Hours" top bar**: As someone who walks in monthly, this is a genuine differentiator over Bookshop.org. Love that it's the first thing I read.
- **Bestsellers rail starting below the fold**: I can see the edge of it, which creates a natural pull to scroll. B&N knows I'm going to want to see that list.
- **Search bar is prominent and labeled correctly**: "Search by Title, Author, Keyword or ISBN" — this is how I actually think when I'm hunting a specific book.

## What's weak

- **"JOIN NOW" is aimed at a stranger, not me**: I'm already a member. There's no "Your membership is active" signal, no rewards balance, nothing that makes me feel seen as a returning customer. Bookshop.org doesn't have a loyalty program and yet I still end up there partly because it feels less like a pitch.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible**: This is relevant for me every single time — I'm calibrating my cart to hit the threshold. Not showing it above the fold is a missed conversion nudge.
- **Carousel indicators but no urgency**: Four dots at the bottom of the hero mean there are more offers, but there's zero reason shown to advance through them. No "Ends Tonight" or scarcity copy to make me swipe.
- **No star ratings or review counts**: The bestsellers covers are peeking in at the bottom but I can't see any social proof — no "4.6 stars / 2,400 ratings" badges that would shortcut my decision.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "JOIN NOW" for a returning-member state**: If I'm logged in, show me "Your Rewards: 847 pts — redeem toward your next order." That's the homepage I want. If I'm not logged in, show both states with a clear sign-in prompt alongside the join CTA.
- **Add free-shipping threshold to the membership banner**: Change "Extra 10% Off & Earn Rewards" to "Extra 10% Off + Free Shipping on orders $40+ for members." One line, major conversion lift.
- **Put a deadline on the Book Club pick**: "Available now through May 31" or "This month's pick" with a subtle calendar icon. Turns a browse into a this-week purchase for me.
- **Surface star ratings on the Bestsellers rail**: Even tiny "★ 4.7" badges on the cover thumbnails would make me tap into that section immediately instead of treating it as ambient wallpaper.
