# Homepage snapshot · www.barnesandnoble.com · 2026-06-06
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-06**
## Executive summary

- This is unmistakably Barnes & Noble — clean, literary, and leading with books rather than deals. Right now they're pushing a site redesign announcement and a spotlight on their Debut Author Prize finalists, which is exactly the kind of editorial curation I expect from B&N. The membership recruitment banner is front and center, which is slightly annoying given that I'm already a member and the page doesn't know that.

## What's working

- **Debut Author Prize Finalists hero**: This is editorial gold for me. B&N discovering and spotlighting debut authors is exactly the kind of thing that differentiates them from Amazon, and it's the reason I don't just use Bookshop.org.
- **Membership 10% off banner**: Even if it's pitched at newcomers, the "Extra 10% Off & Earn Rewards" copy reminds me why I pay for Premium — it's reinforcing the value I already get.
- **Search bar prominence**: Large, up top, with "Title, Author, ISBN" — I know exactly what to type and I often come in with a specific title in mind.
- **Bestsellers rail starting below the fold**: "Yesteryear," "Whistler," "Land" — recognizable titles that signal this is a curated, current list, not last year's leftovers.
- **Clean mobile layout**: No clutter, no pop-up modal hijacking the screen on load — I can actually see the page.

## What's weak

- **"JOIN NOW" banner is wrong message for me**: I'm a Premium Member. Seeing a membership recruitment pitch without any acknowledgment that I'm already logged in (or that I should log in to see my benefits) is a mild slap. It wastes above-the-fold real estate on a pitch that doesn't apply to me.
- **"Welcome to the New BN.com!" announcement bar**: This reads like a maintenance message, not a marketing hook. It takes up the prime top-of-screen slot and gives me nothing useful. I don't care about a site redesign; I care about what's new in fiction.
- **Hero carousel dots visible but no context**: I can see I'm on slide 2 of 3 in the hero, but the other slides are invisible. The debut author content is good — I have no idea what I'm missing by not swiping.
- **No price or free-shipping threshold visible**: As a monthly shopper, I'm always calculating whether my cart clears the free-shipping bar. I can't find that number anywhere above the fold.
- **No reviews or ratings on featured books**: The book covers in the debut finalists grid and the bestsellers rail show zero stars, zero ratings. Even a "4.2 ★" under a title would increase my confidence before I tap.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the "JOIN NOW" banner for a signed-in member strip**: Show returning members their rewards balance and a "Your 10% member discount is active" confirmation — that's actually useful and converts existing members into buyers right now.
- **Replace the redesign announcement bar with a seasonal editorial hook**: "New June Arrivals" or "Our Debut Author Prize Winner Announced" — something that pulls me in with content, not infrastructure news.
- **Add a free-shipping callout**: One line, anywhere above the fold: "Free shipping on orders $40+" — this is table stakes and I have to dig for it.
- **Put ratings on the hero book thumbnails**: Even just star icons with a count under each cover in the debut finalists grid would meaningfully increase tap-through for those titles.
