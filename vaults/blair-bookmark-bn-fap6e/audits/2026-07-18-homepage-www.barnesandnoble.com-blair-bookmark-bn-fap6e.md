# Homepage snapshot · www.barnesandnoble.com · 2026-07-18
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-18**
## Executive summary

- This is a bookstore homepage that actually remembers I come here to buy books — "Book Your Summer" is warm and reader-coded without being saccharine about it. The Rebecca Yarros pre-order module is doing real work for members like me: a $5 bonus reward plus stamps on a book I was almost certainly going to pre-order anyway. Feels current, feels seasonal, feels like they know their audience, even if the top hero is a bit generic.

## What's working

- **Rebecca Yarros pre-order module**: Calling out Premium & Rewards perks ($5 bonus + stamps) directly on a high-demand title is exactly what I want to see. I was probably going to pre-order *Threshing Day* anyway — this just closes it.
- **"Free Shipping on Orders of $60 or More" top banner**: Clear, prominent, immediately resolves the friction I always feel with online book orders. Two hardcovers gets me there.
- **Bestsellers rail beginning to peek at the bottom**: Real title covers (*Yesteryear*, *Whistler*, *Theo of Golden*) below the fold are browser-candy — the kind of thing that makes me forget I came here for one book.
- **"SHOP NOW" and "PRE-ORDER" CTAs**: Both are unambiguous and contrast well against their backgrounds. No "Discover More" or "Explore the Season" vagueness.

## What's weak

- **Hero is anonymous**: "Book Your Summer" with a SHOP NOW button could belong to any summer retail campaign. There's nothing in it that signals "for the person with a Membership card and three books stacked on her nightstand." No book covers, no personalization, no differentiation from, say, Target's summer splash.
- **No member welcome or rewards balance visible**: I'm a monthly customer — nothing at the top saying "Welcome back — 420 Rewards points" or my current member tier. The loyalty mention is buried inside a product pre-order module, not on the account level.
- **Hamburger-only navigation on mobile**: To reach Fiction or Staff Picks, I have to tap the hamburger first. For someone who visits monthly and knows exactly where she's going, that's unnecessary friction.
- **"See page for details" on the rewards offer**: I read fine print. That line makes me feel like there's a catch. If the terms are simple, just say them; if they're not, that's also information.

## Recommendations

- **Add a personalized thin bar for logged-in members** just below the main nav: "Hi Blair — 420 points | My B&N" — zero extra scroll, immediately separates BN.com from Bookshop.org for returning members.
- **Give the hero a destination, not just a season**: Swap "Book Your Summer" for something like "Your Summer Reading: Bestsellers, Staff Picks & New Releases" — same warmth, actual wayfinding.
- **Surface 2–3 category quick-links below the search bar**: "Fiction · Nonfiction · Staff Picks" as tappable pills — reduces hamburger dependency for people who already know where they're going.
- **Inline the reward condition in the Yarros module**: Cut "See page for details" and write "Earn $5 after release (9/29), pre-orders only" inline — same information, no anxiety.
