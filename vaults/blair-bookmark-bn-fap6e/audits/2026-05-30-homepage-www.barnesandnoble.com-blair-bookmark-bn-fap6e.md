# Homepage snapshot · www.barnesandnoble.com · 2026-05-30
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-30**
## Executive summary

- Okay, they've redesigned and they want me to know about it — that announcement banner at the top is the first thing I see. Right now B&N is squarely in Father's Day gift mode, which makes total sense at the end of May. The homepage reads like a solid neighborhood bookstore putting its best table display forward: a big seasonal push, a membership reminder that actually benefits me, and real book covers I recognize without having to scroll.

## What's working

- **Premium Membership strip placement** — "Extra 10% Off & Earn Rewards" lands second on screen, before the hero. As a member, that's my loyalty hook delivered immediately.
- **Father's Day book-cover collage** — a dense grid of real titles I know (Sedaris, Crichton, Weir, James Holland) communicates "books for smart adult readers" without any explanation. Visually persuasive.
- **Bestsellers rail** — *Yesteryear*, Andy Weir, *Theo & Golden* are already peeking into frame. The moment I land here, there are specific products to lust after.
- **Search bar prominence** — title/author/ISBN search front and center. When I already know what I want, that's the fastest path in.
- **June 21 date anchor** — the Father's Day deadline creates soft urgency without a countdown timer screaming at me. Respects my intelligence.

## What's weak

- **"Welcome to the New BN.com! | Find Out More"** — this announcement bar costs me vertical space and says nothing useful. I'm already here; I don't need a ribbon telling me the site got a facelift.
- **No navigation tabs visible** — I have to tap a hamburger menu to reach Fiction, Nonfiction, or any other category. One extra tap to find my section is friction I notice.
- **"JOIN NOW" framing on the membership banner** — I'm already a member. That CTA is pitched at strangers. I'd rather see "Your 10% is active" or a rewards balance.
- **No cafe or in-store hooks** — I do most of my browsing from my phone while commuting, then buy in-store with my membership. There's zero "find a store" or "your store" signal anywhere above the fold.
- **Fine print on the membership offer** — "Some exclusions apply" sitting directly under the headline undercuts the lead benefit before I've had a second to feel good about it.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the top announcement bar for a personalized "Welcome back, Blair" or rewards-balance chip** — a signed-in member should not see the same first message as a first-time visitor.
- **Expose at least two or three category chips under the search bar** ("Fiction," "Nonfiction," "New Releases") so I can reach my section in one tap without opening the hamburger.
- **Replace "JOIN NOW" on the membership strip with "Member pricing applied" or a visible rewards tally** — turn a recruitment message into a retention signal for existing members.
- **Add a small "Find my store" or "Pick up in-store today" line** somewhere above the fold — B&N's biggest competitive advantage over Bookshop.org is the physical location, and it's completely invisible here.
