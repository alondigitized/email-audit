# Homepage snapshot · www.nordstrom.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I did not get a homepage — I got a security wall. Nordstrom's bot-detection flagged the visit and served me a plain white interstitial that says "We've noticed some unusual activity," points me to a customer service phone number, and suggests I apply to the Nordstrom Affiliate Network if I want data access. There is no hero, no product, no promo, no navigation — just a locked door. As a Nordy Club loyalist who has spent thousands on this site, this is a deeply jarring first impression.

## What's working

- The Nordstrom wordmark in bold black at the top is instantly recognizable — brand ID is not lost even on this error state.
- The page renders without layout breakage, overlapping text, or visual glitches — it is technically clean.
- The customer service phone number (1.888.282.6060) is prominently placed and tappable — at least there's a lifeline.

## What's weak

- **The entire shopping experience is absent.** No hero, no nav, no categories, no offer, no CTA — I landed on a dead end.
- **"Unusual activity" language feels accusatory.** As a long-time Nordy Club member, being treated like a bot is genuinely off-putting. The copy has zero warmth.
- **The Affiliate Network link is completely off-target.** I am a customer, not a content partner — this is noise at best, confusing at worst.
- **No self-service recovery path for real customers.** The only option is calling a phone number, which is a high-friction ask just to load the homepage.
- **© 2026 Nordstrom, Inc. at the bottom** is the only other visible element — the page is nearly empty, which amplifies how broken it feels.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "I'm a real customer" quick-verify CTA** — a CAPTCHA or a "Sign in to continue" button that routes me directly into my Nordy Club account would recover the session gracefully instead of dead-ending me.
- **Rewrite the headline.** "We've noticed some unusual activity" reads as a fraud alert. Swap to something brand-appropriate: "Let's make sure it's you — tap below to continue shopping" keeps the warmth Nordstrom is known for.
- **Include a direct sign-in link.** Authenticated sessions bypass bot-detection; surfacing a login button here would immediately resolve the block for most real customers without a phone call.
- **Do not show the Affiliate Network link on consumer-facing blocks.** It creates confusion and dilutes the brand voice — save that for developer/partner flows.
