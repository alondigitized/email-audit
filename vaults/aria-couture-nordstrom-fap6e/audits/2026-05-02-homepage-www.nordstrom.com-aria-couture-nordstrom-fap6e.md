# Homepage snapshot · www.nordstrom.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I didn't even get a homepage — I got a block screen. Nordstrom flagged my visit as "unusual activity" and served me a white wall with a customer service number instead of, I don't know, *anything* to shop. For a brand that prides itself on best-in-class service, this is the digital equivalent of a bouncer turning away your best customer at the door.
- **1/10**

## What's working

- The NORDSTROM wordmark at the top is clean and recognizable — at least I know whose wall I just hit.
- The customer service phone number (1.888.282.6060) is clearly visible and linked, so there's technically a recovery path if I'm determined enough to call.

## What's weak

- The "We've noticed some unusual activity" hero message is the entire above-the-fold experience — no product, no editorial, no reason to stay.
- Zero personalization, zero merchandising, zero loyalty acknowledgment — my Nordy Club status is completely invisible here.
- The body copy pivots immediately to explaining bot policy and affiliate network access, which is language aimed at developers and scrapers, not a loyal customer browsing on her lunch break.
- No navigation bar, no search, no category links — there is literally no path forward except calling a phone number.
- The page is mostly empty white space below the message, which communicates abandonment, not luxury.
- © 2026 Nordstrom, Inc. footer is the only other element — this page has *three* content blocks total.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "I'm a real customer" verification flow** — a CAPTCHA or "confirm you're human" button with a redirect back to the actual homepage, so loyal customers aren't dead-ended at a phone call.
- **Surface a fallback navigation strip** below the error message — at minimum, links to Women's, Beauty, Sale, and Account so someone determined can still shop.
- **Rewrite the body copy** away from bot/affiliate language toward customer-first framing: "We're protecting your account security — tap below to continue shopping" instead of explaining the Affiliate Network program.
- **Inject Nordy Club status acknowledgment** if session/cookie data is available: "We see you, Aria — let's get you back in" makes this feel like a brand moment instead of a security rejection.
