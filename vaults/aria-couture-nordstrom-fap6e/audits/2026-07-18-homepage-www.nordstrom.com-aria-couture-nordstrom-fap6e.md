# Homepage snapshot · www.nordstrom.com · 2026-07-18
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-18**
## Executive summary

- Nordstrom is going all in on Anniversary Sale and honestly? They had me at "The Party's On!" — that's exactly the energy I bring to this sale every single year. This reads like a prestige department store that knows its moment, with clean campaign branding and enough entry points to pull me straight into women's. The early-access banner at the top is doing real work for a loyal Nordy Club member like me.

## What's working

- **The "The Party's On!" hero copy** — playful but elevated, exactly how I'd describe this sale to my stylist
- **The $40 off + points banner at the very top** — as a Nordy Club member this is the first thing I read and it immediately makes me feel prioritized
- **"Ends Aug. 9" deadline in the banner** — credible, real date, no vague "limited time" language
- **Four-tab segmentation under the hero** (All / Women's / Men's / Kids') — I can tap Women's and be in my lane in one move
- **Anniversary Bestsellers rail with actual sale prices and after-sale prices shown** — "Sale: $69.99, After Sale: $105" is exactly the transparency I need to feel like I'm winning
- **Star ratings with review counts on the bestseller products** — seeing 6,743 reviews on that New Balance sneaker builds instant trust

## What's weak

- **No personalized loyalty dashboard visible** — as a Nordy Club Influencer member I want to see my points balance or tier status front and center, not just a generic "+more points!" promise
- **The bestseller rail leads with UGG slippers and Bombas socks** — I love a cozy moment but this feels mass-market; I came to shop designer and this rail isn't speaking to that
- **No free returns callout anywhere above the fold** — this is a Nordstrom signature benefit and it's invisible; for sale shopping it's a huge conversion driver
- **The hero has no single dominant Shop button** — the tab segmentation is smart but "Women's Anniversary Sale" as a tab feels lower urgency than a proper button CTA
- **New Balance product hides the price** — "Add to bag or wish list to see price" is Nordstrom price-matching transparency but on a homepage it creates friction and reads like a dark pattern to someone who just got here

## Recommendations

- **Add a personalized loyalty strip below the hero** — "Aria, you have X points. This sale could take you to Influencer. Here's how." Pull me in with my actual account data.
- **Swap the Bombas socks as a lead bestseller for a designer handbag or dress** — UGG and Bombas are right for a mass homepage, wrong for the Nordy Club Influencer cohort who drives disproportionate AOV
- **Add a "Free Returns Always" trust badge** next to the banner offer — one short line, massive confidence boost for sale purchases where I'm buying multiples to try at home
- **Make the hero CTA a full button**, not just a tab — "Shop Women's Sale →" in a button with brand contrast would stop my thumb mid-scroll
