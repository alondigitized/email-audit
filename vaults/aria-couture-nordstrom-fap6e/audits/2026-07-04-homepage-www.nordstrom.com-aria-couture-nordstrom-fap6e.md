# Homepage snapshot · www.nordstrom.com · 2026-07-04
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-04**
## Executive summary

- I literally cannot believe what I'm looking at right now. I opened nordstrom.com like I do every other day and instead of the homepage I got a "we think you're a bot" block page — white screen, zero imagery, zero products, zero vibe. As a Nordy Club member who has DM'd their stylists and has the Anniversary Sale on my actual Google Calendar, this is not the first impression a brand of this caliber should be serving. This is a security interstitial, not a homepage.

## What's working

- **Clean render:** The block page itself loads without layout errors — text is readable, no overlapping elements, footer copyright is properly placed. At minimum the technical bones work.
- **Customer service number visible:** `1.888.282.6060` is surfaced. If I were genuinely locked out, I know exactly who to call without hunting.

## What's weak

- **Zero commerce, zero brand:** No hero, no imagery, no products, no navigation — I am staring at a white page with a paragraph of legal-sounding copy. The Nordstrom brand equity is invisible.
- **No path forward for real customers:** There is no "sign in to verify it's you," no CAPTCHA, no "try again" button — just a dead end with a phone number. As a Nordy Club member I have no way to self-serve my way past this.
- **Copy feels clinical and cold:** "We don't allow unidentified, automated traffic" reads like a firewall alert, not a premium department store talking to its most loyal shoppers.
- **Complete loss of seasonal moment:** It's July 4th — the Anniversary Sale lead-up is one of the biggest retail moments of the Nordy calendar. This page captures zero of that energy.
- **No loyalty recognition:** I've spent real money to hit Nordy Club status and I'm being treated like a threat, not a VIP.

## Recommendations

- **Add a member sign-in bypass CTA** — a visible "Sign in to continue" button would let Nordy Club members authenticate themselves past the block without calling customer service. One button, ships next week.
- **Surface a human-friendly bridge message** — swap "We've noticed some unusual activity" for something like "Just a quick check — sign in to get back to shopping." Tone should match the brand, not a firewall log.
- **Never show a blank brand shell** — at minimum, load a static marketing image (no JS required) so the Nordstrom visual identity stays present even when the dynamic page is blocked.
- **Add a CAPTCHA or challenge flow** — if bot detection triggers, give real users a solvable path forward rather than a phone number dead-end.
