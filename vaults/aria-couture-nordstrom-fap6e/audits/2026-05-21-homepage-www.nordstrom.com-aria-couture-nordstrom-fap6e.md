# Homepage snapshot · www.nordstrom.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- This is not a homepage — it's a bot-block wall. I opened nordstrom.com on my phone and instead of the brand I've been loyal to for years, I got a security error page telling me they've "noticed some unusual activity." No hero, no product, no Anniversary Sale teaser, nothing. This is a broken experience, full stop, and it tells me absolutely nothing about what the brand is pushing right now.

## What's working

- **The Nordstrom wordmark** renders correctly in the top-left — at least the brand is identifiable before I bounce.
- **Customer Service phone number (1.888.282.6060)** is linked and visible — if I'm determined enough to call, there's a path forward.
- **"Nordstrom Affiliate Network" link** is present for data-access requests — not relevant to me, but technically it's a functional element.

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is missing.** No hero, no products, no navigation, no offers — I landed on an error interstitial instead of the store.
- **The error copy is cold and accusatory.** "We've noticed some unusual activity" reads like I did something wrong. I'm a Nordy Club member who has spent thousands — this framing is insulting.
- **No recovery path for real customers.** The only option given is "call us." There's no "sign in to verify," no CAPTCHA to pass, no retry button. I'm just blocked.
- **Zero loyalty recognition.** If I were signed in, a real returning-customer experience would know who I am. This page treats me like a bot.
- **Completely blank lower half of the page.** The bottom two-thirds is dead white space, making the experience feel broken and abandoned.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Sign In to Continue" button directly on this page** — a logged-in Nordy Club member with purchase history is trivially distinguishable from a scraper; let authentication be the unlock instead of a phone call.
- **Soften the copy.** Replace "We've noticed some unusual activity" with something like "Let's make sure it's you — sign in to keep shopping." Same security outcome, dramatically less alienating for a loyal customer.
- **Surface a CAPTCHA or one-tap human verification flow** so the real homepage loads immediately after passing, rather than routing me to a phone queue.
- **At minimum, render the nav bar and a static promotional banner** so even blocked users see the brand is alive and running a current campaign — right now this looks like the site is down.
