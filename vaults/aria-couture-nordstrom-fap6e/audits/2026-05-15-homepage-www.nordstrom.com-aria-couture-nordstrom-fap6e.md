# Homepage snapshot · www.nordstrom.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- Nordstrom is leading with the MANGO x Hailey Bieber collab and I am immediately interested — that's the kind of influencer-tier drop I screenshot and send to my stylist before I've even had coffee. Right now the site is straddling two stories (newness vs. markdowns) and it works better than it should, but there are some glaring gaps for a returning Nordy Club member at my tier. Clean, aspirational, seasonally on point — just not fully personalized for someone who's been shopping here for a decade.

## What's working

- **MANGO x Hailey Bieber hero**: The collab headline lands immediately — celebrity, brand, lifestyle imagery all in one shot. That's an attention-stopper.
- **"Top Picks for You" personalized rail**: Nordstrom acknowledging I'm a returning shopper with a curated section is meaningful. The tab structure gives me options without forcing a path.
- **3X Nordy Club points on beauty**: Concrete, specific, no math required. I know exactly what I'm getting.
- **Designer tab in the nav bar**: One tap to my category — that's as frictionless as it gets.
- **Tory Burch card partially visible**: Even cropped, seeing a brand I actually buy signals the algorithm is partially tracking my taste.

## What's weak

- **"Join The Nordy Club" banner**: I am already a member. Showing me acquisition copy is a miss — replace it with my points balance and tier name.
- **"New Markdowns" as the default tab**: I open Nordstrom to discover what's new, not what didn't sell. Leading with clearance before newness is backwards for a loyalty-tier customer.
- **Mix & Wisdom and Golden Hour prominent**: Neither brand is in my mental wardrobe. My spend history should be surfacing Vince, Totême, or Stuart Weitzman, not mid-market basics.
- **No free returns / alterations callout**: Free alterations is a Nordstrom exclusive and a key reason I stay off Net-a-Porter. It's invisible here.
- **No Personal Stylist hook**: I DM my Nordstrom stylist. That feature drives real loyalty and it doesn't exist anywhere above the fold.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the acquisition banner for a logged-in member state**: Show my points balance, tier ("Influencer"), and a nudge like "Your Nordy Club points expire in 30 days." Lose "Join" entirely for authenticated sessions.
- **Make "New Arrivals" the default tab in "Top Picks for You"**: I scroll to discover; lead with discovery. Markdowns can be a secondary tab I opt into.
- **Add a single trust strip above the product rail**: "Free shipping. Free returns. Free alterations." — one line, no decoration. Nordstrom can say this honestly; almost no one else can.
- **Insert a Personal Stylist card in the product rail**: One card — "Message [Stylist Name]" — with a direct DM link. That's the retention hook that keeps me from drifting to competitors.
