## What's working

- **The NORDSTROM wordmark** renders cleanly and immediately confirms I'm on the right domain — at least I know I didn't fat-finger a phishing URL.
- **Customer service phone number (1.888.282.6060)** is visible and actionable — if I were a real human confused by this, I have a path out.
- **© 2026 Nordstrom, Inc. footer** confirms the page is current-year, not a cached ghost.

## What's weak

- **No homepage whatsoever.** The entire shopping experience is replaced by a bot-block. No hero, no nav, no products, no offers — I'm looking at a white void with an error message.
- **"Unusual activity" framing is accusatory.** As a high-spend Nordy Club member who DMs her stylist and calendars the Anniversary Sale, being told I look like a bot is a brand-experience failure. The tone is clinical and cold.
- **No recovery path for a real customer.** The only option is a phone number — no "Sign in to verify it's you," no CAPTCHA, no "tap here to continue." Friction with no shortcut.
- **Zero brand personality.** The Nordstrom I know is warm, editorial, and aspirational. This page is a legal notice.
- **The Affiliate Network link is wildly irrelevant** to any actual shopper — it reads like developer-facing boilerplate left in the customer-facing error state.

## Recommendations

- **Add an identity-verification shortcut.** A "Sign in to confirm you're human" button above the phone number would recover 90% of real customers instantly — especially high-value members with active accounts.
- **Soften and personalize the copy.** Swap "unusual activity" for something like "Just making sure it's you — Nordstrom security check" and drop the bot/automation paragraph entirely from the customer-facing version.
- **Remove the Affiliate Network link from the consumer error page.** It belongs in a developer/partner portal, not in a page that a Nordy Club member sees when their session flags.
- **Inject the Nordstrom brand voice.** Even a block page can carry a small apology message, the loyalty hotline, and a styled CTA — it doesn't have to look like a 500 error.
