# Homepage snapshot · www.nordstrom.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Nordstrom is clearly in full Anniversary Sale warm-up mode and I am here for it — the cardmember banner at the very top, the Hailey Bieber MANGO collab hero, and a "Top Picks For You" rail that is already showing me marked-down Tory Burch? That is not an accident. This feels like a brand that knows exactly who opens their app religiously, and it is pandering to me in the best possible way.

## What's working

- **"Top Picks For You" personalized rail** — this is the homepage doing its job. Seeing a Tory Burch bag knocked from $490 to $218.40 with a "NEW MARKDOWN" badge and 274 five-star reviews stops my scroll immediately.
- **Hailey Bieber × MANGO hero** — celebrity designer collabs are exactly the kind of event I block off calendar time for. The slide indicator (1/4) tells me there's more to explore without making me hunt for it.
- **"New Arrivals" CTA on the hero** — unambiguous, correctly placed, matches my reflex to tap into a new collab drop.
- **Anniversary Sale teaser in the banner** — I have this sale calendared. Seeing "Ends Aug 9" while it's still May means I'm being primed early, which is exactly how I want Nordstrom to treat me.
- **Star ratings in the product rail** — Tory Burch at ★★★★★ (274) and Mix & Wisdom at ★★★★ (326) give me instant social proof without leaving the homepage.
- **Full-category nav visible** — Women, Sale, Designer, Shoes — one tap to wherever I want to go.

## What's weak

- **Cardmember banner is wasted space for me** — "$65 off for new cardmembers" means nothing to an existing Nordy Club Influencer member. That prime banner slot should show my points balance, upcoming member early-access date, or a personalized reward.
- **MANGO is not designer-tier** — I am loyalty to Tory Burch, McQueen, and above. Leading with MANGO (even Hailey Bieber's MANGO) signals Nordstrom is hedging toward accessible, not speaking to the customer who drops $590 on sneakers without blinking.
- **McQueen product card is cropped** — the price is visible ($354–$411, $590 crossed out) but the shoe image is cut off on the right edge. That is the most expensive product in the rail and it gets clipped.
- **No loyalty summary for signed-in members** — I am clearly signed into a personalized experience ("Top Picks For You"), but my Nordy Club tier, points, or next-reward milestone is completely absent above the fold.
- **Wellness tabs dominate the "Top Picks" filter bar** — "Wellness Products for Women" and "Wellness Products for Men" are two of the four visible tabs. I am a designer-fashion shopper, not a wellness buyer. The tab personalization feels off.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the cardmember banner for a signed-in loyalty summary bar** — show me my Nordy Club tier, current points, and "You're X points from your next reward" in that top strip. Acquisition banners belong on the logged-out experience.
- **Give McQueen the hero slot on carousel slide 2** — the product rail is already surfacing it as a markdown. Make the editorial carousel match. A McQueen or Tory Burch lifestyle hero would land harder for my tier.
- **Fix the product card crop on the right rail** — the Nike card and McQueen card are both getting clipped. If the rail is showing four items, give them equal visual breathing room or add an arrow affordance earlier.
- **Replace the wellness tabs in "Top Picks" with Designer, New Markdowns, and Sale** — I am signed in, you have my purchase history. Lead with the tabs that reflect what I actually buy.
