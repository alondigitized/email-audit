# Homepage snapshot · www.nordstrom.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- Okay, not what I expected to open on a Wednesday afternoon. Instead of the Nordstrom homepage I know and love — Anniversary Sale countdowns, new arrivals, my stylist's picks — I got a white screen telling me I'm suspicious. The brand I associate with white-glove service just flagged me as a bot. That is not a premium experience, full stop.

## What's working

- The Nordstrom wordmark at the top is clean and loads instantly — brand recognition is immediate even on a broken state.
- The error copy is at least plain English. "We've noticed some unusual activity" is friendlier than a cold 403 page.
- A customer service phone number (`1.888.282.6060`) is provided, which is marginally better than a dead end.

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is missing.** No hero, no nav, no products, no offers — nothing I came here to see.
- **The bot-block fires on a normal mobile browser visit.** If this is what a real customer on mobile sees, that's a catastrophic acquisition failure.
- **No "sign in to continue" path.** A Nordy Club member like me should be able to authenticate and bypass this — instead I'm offered a phone call or an affiliate program signup, neither of which I want.
- **Zero loyalty acknowledgment.** Nordstrom knows who I am when I'm signed in. This page doesn't even offer a login prompt — it just dumps me at a wall.
- **The affiliate program link is tone-deaf** in this context. Showing a business partnership CTA to a blocked shopper reads as bureaucratic noise.

## Recommendations

- **Surface a "Sign in to continue" button immediately** — authenticated Nordy Club members should never hit this wall; a login flow would both unblock legit users and satisfy the bot-detection requirement.
- **Replace the dead-end block page with a CAPTCHA or browser-challenge** so real customers on mobile pass through in 10 seconds rather than bouncing forever.
- **Add a one-tap "Open in Nordstrom App" CTA** — if the site suspects mobile automation, redirect me to the verified app experience rather than stranding me.
- **Log this session for re-targeting** — at minimum, if someone hits the block page and then lands on the real site later, show them a "Sorry for the trouble — here's 10% off" loyalty moment.
