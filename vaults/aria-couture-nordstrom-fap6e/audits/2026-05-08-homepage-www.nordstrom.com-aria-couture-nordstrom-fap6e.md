# Homepage snapshot · www.nordstrom.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I literally cannot shop right now — Nordstrom hit me with a "We've noticed some unusual activity" block page the second I landed. There's no hero, no product, no offers, just a white screen with the logo and a customer service phone number. This isn't a homepage experience; it's a dead end that tells me nothing about what's in store or why I should be excited to be here.

## What's working

- **The Nordstrom wordmark** is clean, correctly rendered, and instantly legible — at least I know whose site ate my session.
- **The copyright footer** ("© 2026 Nordstrom, Inc.") is present, confirming the page didn't completely fail to load.

## What's weak

- **The entire shopping experience** is absent — no nav, no hero, no category links, no offers, nothing. I can't tap a single product-adjacent element.
- **"We've noticed some unusual activity"** is a cold, clinical message that makes me feel accused rather than welcomed. As a loyal Nordy Club member this is jarring.
- **Zero loyalty recognition** — if my account cookie was flagged, there's no reassurance that my Nordy Club status or rewards are safe, which is anxiety-inducing.
- **No path forward on-page** — the only option is calling 1.888.282.6060 or applying to an Affiliate Network, neither of which is relevant to a regular shopper trying to browse new arrivals.
- **No app deep-link or "try the app" fallback** — a high-tech-comfort millennial like me would expect at minimum a redirect option to the app or a CAPTCHA solve.

## Recommendations

- **Serve a CAPTCHA or one-click "I'm not a bot" challenge** instead of a full dead-end block — let me prove I'm human and get back to shopping in under 5 seconds.
- **Personalize the error if I'm signed in** — show my name and Nordy Club tier ("Hey Aria, we just need to verify it's you") to cut the accusatory tone and reassure me my account is intact.
- **Add a direct "Open in the Nordstrom App" button** so high-app-usage shoppers like me have an immediate unblocked path to browse and buy.
- **Surface a basic nav strip** (Women / Sale / New Arrivals) even on the error page — at minimum give me something to tap while the bot check resolves.
