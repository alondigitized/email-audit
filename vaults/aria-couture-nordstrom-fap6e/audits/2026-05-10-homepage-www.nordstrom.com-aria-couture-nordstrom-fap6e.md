# Homepage snapshot · www.nordstrom.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is genuinely embarrassing — I opened nordstrom.com and got a bot-detection wall instead of a homepage. No hero, no products, no Nordy Club welcome, nothing. As a loyalty member who has spent actual thousands on this app, being greeted with "We've noticed some unusual activity" is jarring and honestly a little insulting. The only thing this page is pushing right now is a phone number.

## What's working

- The Nordstrom wordmark renders cleanly in the top-left — at least I know I'm in the right place.
- The error message is legible, in plain English, and includes a real phone number (1.888.282.6060) so a human path to resolution exists.
- The © 2026 Nordstrom, Inc. footer confirms the page is current — no cached stale content.

## What's weak

- **The entire shopping experience is absent.** No hero, no categories, no products, no offers — a total dead end for any shopper intent.
- **"Unusual activity" copy reads accusatory.** As a Nordy Club member who browses on my phone regularly, being flagged as suspicious feels like a terrible brand moment. This is the opposite of the VIP treatment I expect.
- **No sign-in CTA to resolve the block.** If this is a bot-check, offering me a "Sign in to continue" would recover the session for a legitimate user — instead I'm told to call a 1-800 number.
- **Zero personalization or recovery path.** A recognized loyalty member should get a softer gate — maybe a CAPTCHA or a sign-in prompt, not a wall that implies I'm automated traffic.
- **The Nordstrom Affiliate Network link is completely irrelevant** to a retail shopper and adds confusion about what I'm supposed to do next.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hard block for a sign-in gate.** Logged-in Nordy Club members should authenticate through a session check, not a bot wall — "Sign in to continue shopping" preserves the session and the relationship.
- **Add a CAPTCHA or challenge flow** as the intermediate step before a full lockout page. Most shoppers on mobile with unusual traffic patterns (VPN, shared IP) are real humans, not bots.
- **Soften the copy.** Replace "We've noticed some unusual activity" with something like "Let's make sure it's you — sign in to continue" — same security outcome, zero brand damage.
- **Surface a Nordy Club–aware recovery path.** If the site detects a loyalty cookie or recognized device, automatically route to a lighter verification step, not the generic error page.
