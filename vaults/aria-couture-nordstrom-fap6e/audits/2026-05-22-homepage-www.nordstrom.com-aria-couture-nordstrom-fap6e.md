# Homepage snapshot · www.nordstrom.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- I opened nordstrom.com and got a blank white wall with a security block — zero hero, zero nav, zero product. This is not a homepage; it's a bot-detection interstitial, and as a real Nordy Club member who literally has my stylist's DM on speed-dial, being treated like automated traffic is both jarring and honestly a little insulting. The brand I know for white-glove service just door-slammed me on arrival.

## What's working

- **Brand wordmark** — "NORDSTROM" in the correct typeface is the one thing that loads, so I at least know I'm in the right place and didn't land on a phishing page.
- **Customer service number is clearly legible** — 1.888.282.6060 is easy to read if I actually need to call, which at this point I might.

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is missing** — I see a security interstitial, not a shopping experience. Zero products, zero nav, zero imagery.
- **The copy is cold and accusatory** — "We've noticed some unusual activity" reads like a fraud alert, not a premium retailer greeting a loyal customer. As a Nordy Club member, this tone is completely off-brand.
- **No path forward for a legitimate shopper** — there's no "continue anyway," no CAPTCHA, no retry button, no sign-in link. My only options are to call a phone number or apply to the Affiliate Network, which is absurd if I just want to browse new arrivals.
- **No navigation whatsoever** — not even a hamburger menu. I cannot reach Women's, Designer, or my account from this screen.
- **The "Nordstrom Affiliate Network" link feels like a non-sequitur** — presenting that to a consumer shopper is confusing and slightly off-putting.

## Recommendations

- **Add a sign-in / "I'm a real customer" path** — a "Sign in to your account" button would let me authenticate and bypass the block immediately, rewarding loyal members instead of punishing them.
- **Soften the copy** — swap "We've noticed some unusual activity" for something warmer like "Just checking in — let's get you back to shopping" to preserve the Nordstrom service tone even in a security context.
- **Surface a minimal nav strip** — even during a bot-check, show the top-level nav so a real user can manually navigate to Women's or Designer without needing a phone call.
- **Add a CAPTCHA / quick human-verification widget** — a one-click "I'm not a robot" checkbox resolves this in seconds and gets me back to the Anniversary Sale without the friction of a phone call.
