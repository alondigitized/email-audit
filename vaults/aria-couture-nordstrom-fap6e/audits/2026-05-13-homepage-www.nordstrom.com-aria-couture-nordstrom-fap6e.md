# Homepage snapshot · www.nordstrom.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- I opened Nordstrom.com and got a wall of text telling me I look like a bot. No hero. No imagery. No sale. No "Welcome back, Aria." As a Nordy Club member who has literally DMed stylists through this app, being greeted with *"We've noticed some unusual activity"* and a customer service phone number is a genuinely bad first impression. The page communicates exactly one thing: distrust.

## What's working

- **Clean, legible typography** — the wordmark and body copy are crisp and on-brand. If I squint, it still looks like Nordstrom.
- **Phone number is visible** — `1.888.282.6060` is right there if I want to escalate, which is the one thing this page actually needs to offer.
- **Copyright footer** — `© 2026 Nordstrom, Inc.` confirms I'm on the real domain, not a phishing clone.

## What's weak

- **Zero shopping content** — no hero, no categories, no products, no sale banners. The entire homepage is blocked.
- **Accusatory tone** — "We've noticed some unusual activity" reads as a accusation before I've done anything. As a returning customer I feel immediately penalized.
- **No signed-in state recovery** — if I were logged into my Nordy Club account, there's no acknowledgment of that. No "Sign in to confirm you're human." No frictionless escape hatch for real customers.
- **No session-recovery CTA** — the only links are a phone number (who calls anymore?) and an Affiliate Network application — neither relevant to me wanting to shop.
- **No imagery whatsoever** — the page is entirely white space and serif text. Feels like an error state, not a brand moment.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Sign in to continue" button** — a logged-in Nordy Club member should be able to verify their identity in-app without calling a 1-800 number. One tap auth bypass is table stakes.
- **Replace the accusatory headline with a neutral one** — "Just a quick security check" lands softer than "unusual activity," which implies I did something wrong.
- **Surface a minimal navigation strip** — even a locked-down state should let me see categories and search so the brand doesn't feel completely offline.
- **Show a reassuring brand moment** — one editorial image behind the message keeps the Nordstrom aesthetic alive and signals "we'll be right back" rather than "you're blocked."
