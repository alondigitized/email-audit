# Homepage snapshot · www.nordstrom.com · 2026-07-11
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-11**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is very much *my moment* — Anniversary Sale preview is live and Nordstrom knows it. The whole homepage is structured around the sale with CTAs, preview-priced product cards, and a Lancôme banner at the bottom that makes me want to start a wishlist immediately. Brand looks premium, campaign-focused, and intentional — but the hero models skew early-20s when this sale's biggest spenders are squarely in their 30s.

## What's working

- **"Party Starts July 18" hero countdown framing** — this is exactly how Nordy Club members experience the sale. I feel invited, not marketed to.
- **Anniversary Preview price tags on products** — showing "Sale: $69.99 / After Sale: $105" on the UGGs is the conversion moment. It makes the math visceral.
- **$40 off top banner with hard deadline (Aug. 9)** — specific dollar amount, real date, easy to act on.
- **"Anniversary Items on Everyone's List" with Women/Men/Beauty/Kids tabs** — immediately filterable, no extra navigation required.
- **Lancôme banner at bottom** — Beauty exclusives are a major Anniversary Sale draw and this teases it well.
- **Review counts visible on product cards** — 6,743 reviews on those New Balance sneakers? That's a trust signal I can see at a glance.

## What's weak

- **Hero models look 22, not 36** — I love the energy but the casting doesn't reflect who actually spends the most at this sale. A lifestyle shot of women my age styling their Anniversary Sale haul would hit differently.
- **No Nordy Club rewards balance or tier callout** — I am Influencer-tier. Show me my points. The "Sign In" button is there but nothing signals *why* I should sign in right now.
- **"Add to bag or wish list to see price" on New Balance and Vuori** — this is a friction point. Even as a member, that mystery price is mildly annoying on a browsing session. It rewards sign-in but punishes discovery.
- **No free shipping mention** — for an Anniversary Sale hero moment, the free shipping threshold should be visible. I'm spending $500+ and I want confirmation shipping is covered.
- **Hero image is dark** — the black background and spotlight treatment feels more party-editorial than shoppable. I want to see what I'm buying, not a vibe.

## Recommendations

- **Cast models 32–42 in the hero** — the Anniversary Sale skews toward established Nordy Club members; a lifestyle shot of two friends (stylish, 30s, effortlessly put-together) trying on designer pieces would speak directly to the buyer.
- **Add a Nordy Club tier/points callout in the nav bar** — even a "Your points: 2,450 | Influencer" line next to Sign In would make returning shoppers feel seen and create an immediate reason to log in.
- **Surface free shipping confirmation prominently** — put "Free shipping, always" or the threshold as a persistent bar or in the hero subhead so high-AOV shoppers stop wondering.
- **Swap one hero CTA to "My Early Access Preview"** — reframe the CTA as a membership perk, not a general invitation. It signals exclusivity without adding any design complexity.
