# Homepage snapshot · www.nordstrom.com · 2026-06-06
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-06**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Nordstrom — I see you leaning hard into the Nordy Club right now, which honestly is smart because that program is the whole reason I stay loyal. But the homepage is doing a lot of work for *new* members when I'm already here, already spending, already have my Anniversary Sale date blocked on my calendar. The top picks are algorithmically decent (On and Birkenstock are definitely in my rotation), but nothing above the fold is screaming "designer" to someone like me who came here for a Totême moment, not a sandal scroll.

## What's working

- **The Nordy Club hero** — making my loyalty program the literal centerpiece of the homepage? That's the right bet. The "Unlock Bonus Notes" angle is clever and feels rewarding without being salesy.
- **"Top Picks for You" personalization rail** — the label alone tells me the algorithm is trying. On sneakers and Birkenstock are genuinely in my Nordy cart right now, so it's clearly working off real signal.
- **"New Markdowns" tab** — the word "markdowns" makes me twitch in the best way. Strikethrough pricing is the fastest way to get my thumb moving.
- **"Designer" in the nav** — one tap. It's right there. That's all I need.
- **Father's Day banner** — timely, relevant, and it's got me thinking about my partner, which is bonus conversion territory.

## What's weak

- **"Join The Nordy Club" CTA — directed at strangers, not me**: I've been a Nordy Club member for years. Showing me "Join" instead of "Your Rewards" or my current points balance is a personalization miss that feels tone-deaf.
- **No designer brand callout above fold**: I'm designer-loyal and the entire above-the-fold experience is On and Birkenstock. Both are great, but they're casual-premium, not the tier I associate with why I choose Nordstrom over Net-a-Porter.
- **The hero image is a hand and a notepad** — it's fine, it's Nordy, but it's not aspirational for a 36-year-old who wants to feel styled, not enrolled.
- **Parachute and "Come Home to Comfort" at the bottom** — sponsored home goods content when I'm here for fashion feels like a context break. It also has a visible "Sponsored" label, which can undercut trust.
- **No free-returns or free-shipping callout** — that's one of Nordstrom's biggest differentiators and I can't see it anywhere on this view.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the Nordy Club CTA from "Join" to member-aware copy**: If I'm logged in or recognized as a returning user, show "Your Notes: 450" or "Redeem Rewards" — turn the hero into a loyalty moment for existing members, not a recruiting pitch.
- **Add one designer brand hero tile or editorial callout above the fold**: A single "New in Designer" module or a Valentino / Brandon Maxwell lifestyle image would signal this is still the premium house I trust.
- **Surface free returns prominently**: A simple "Free returns, always" strip between the nav and the hero would remove a micro-hesitation that's probably killing mobile conversion.
- **Give the Father's Day banner a date**: "Father's Day is June 15 — shop gifts" creates urgency without a timer. Right now it's floating with no deadline.
