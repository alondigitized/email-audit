# Homepage snapshot · www.nordstrom.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- I opened Nordstrom.com on my phone and instead of seeing the Anniversary Sale countdown or the new arrivals my stylist DM'd me about, I got a blank white page accusing me of being a bot. This is not a homepage — it's a security block screen, and Nordstrom just called one of their most loyal Nordy Club members suspicious. For a brand that prides itself on service, this is a genuinely embarrassing first impression.

## What's working

- **Brand wordmark** renders cleanly in the correct Nordstrom typography — at least I know I'm in the right place.
- **Customer service number** (1.888.282.6060) is prominently displayed and hyperlinked, which is a small grace note — if I were truly locked out, I'd know who to call.
- **Affiliate Network link** is present for data partners, which shows Nordstrom thought about legitimate access paths even in the block state.

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is missing.** No hero, no nav, no categories, no promotions — I literally cannot shop.
- **"Unusual activity" framing is accusatory.** As a high-frequency Nordy Club shopper who browses the app constantly and visits the site, being flagged as a bot is insulting and disorienting.
- **Zero loyalty recognition.** There is no "sign in to continue" CTA, no member bypass, nothing that would let a verified Nordy Club member prove they're human and proceed.
- **No recovery path for a real shopper.** The only options are call customer service or apply to become an affiliate. Neither option gets me to the cashmere I was looking for.
- **White void aesthetic.** The massive empty whitespace below the message makes the page feel broken and abandoned.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Sign In to Continue" CTA** directly on the block page — a verified account login should immediately bypass the bot gate and restore the full homepage experience for real members.
- **Soften the copy** from "We've noticed some unusual activity" to something like "Just making sure it's you — sign in to keep shopping." Same security outcome, zero brand damage.
- **Surface the app download link** as an alternative: "Get a smoother experience on the Nordstrom app" with a link, so blocked web traffic converts to app sessions rather than bouncing entirely.
- **Trigger a Nordy Club email** to any logged-in member whose session was blocked, so I don't think the site is down — give me a "Sorry for the interruption, here's 5 bonus points" moment that turns the friction into a loyalty beat.
