# Homepage snapshot · www.nordstrom.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is not a homepage — this is a bot-block wall. I opened Nordstrom and got hit with a "We've noticed some unusual activity" interstitial instead of anything resembling a shopping experience. The brand presents itself here as a security gate, not a retailer. There is literally nothing to shop, browse, or engage with.

## What's working

- The Nordstrom wordmark renders cleanly in the top-left — at minimum I know I'm on the right domain.
- The phone number `1.888.282.6060` is legible and hyperlinked — so if I were a human who got blocked by mistake, the path to resolution is one tap away.
- The copyright line `© 2026 Nordstrom, Inc.` confirms the page is current-year, not a cached stale block.

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is missing.** No imagery, no categories, no hero, no nav, no offers — nothing I came to see.
- **"Unusual activity" messaging is cold and accusatory.** Being greeted like a bot when I'm a loyal Nordy Club member feels like a slap. This is the opposite of the white-glove Nordstrom brand voice.
- **No sign-in prompt or loyalty-member rescue path.** If Nordstrom suspects automation, the obvious fix is "sign in to confirm it's you" — that's not offered here.
- **No secondary navigation or search bar.** Even a blocked homepage could keep the nav header intact so I could try to get through another way.
- **Affiliate Network link feels completely out of place.** Directing a confused shopper to apply for affiliate access is bizarre UX — that copy belongs in a developer portal, not a consumer error page.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Sign In to Continue" CTA immediately** — let a verified Nordy Club member bypass the block with a single tap rather than dialing a 1-800 number.
- **Soften the copy** — swap "We've noticed some unusual activity" for something like "Just checking it's you — tap to sign in and keep shopping." Maintain the brand's service-first voice even on an error screen.
- **Keep the nav header alive** — logo + search bar + account icon should persist even on block pages so the path to the site isn't totally severed.
- **Remove the Affiliate Network callout** from this consumer-facing page entirely — it's confusing, off-brand, and not relevant to 99.9% of the people landing here.
