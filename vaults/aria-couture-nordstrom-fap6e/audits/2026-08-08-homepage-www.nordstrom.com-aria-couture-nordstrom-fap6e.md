# Homepage snapshot · www.nordstrom.com · 2026-08-08
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-08**
## Executive summary

- This reads like Nordstrom mid-Anniversary-Sale crunch mode — big yellow urgency banner, a "prices go up August 10" countdown, and a "Last Chance" product rail. It's exactly the moment I have circled on my calendar, so the timing lands, but the hero art (two guys goofing around on a couch) feels like it's talking past me rather than to me.

## What's working

- **"Prices Go Up August 10" deadline** — a real, dated urgency hook instead of a vague "ends soon," which is exactly the kind of thing that gets me to act before I lose the sale price.
- **"Book an Appointment" banner up top** — this is catnip for me, I basically live by my stylist DMs, and an in-store expert tie-in to the sale is very on-brand for how I actually shop.
- **The quick-shop button row** (Women's Anniversary Sale, Men's Anniversary Sale, Bestsellers, Beauty Exclusives) — one tap gets me exactly where I want to go, no digging.
- **Estée Lauder Advanced Night Repair in the Must-Haves rail** — that's a prestige staple I'd actually reorder, so seeing it front and center is smart merchandising.

## What's weak

- **Hero photo is two guys jumping on a couch** — cute energy, but it's not speaking to a 36-year-old woman who's here for the Anniversary Sale. Feels like a Men's campaign borrowed the whole hero slot.
- **No Nordy Club presence anywhere above the fold** — I'm status-loyal, I want to see my points or member pricing called out, not just a generic "Sign In."
- **No actual discount number visible** — "Anniversary Sale" is a vibe, not an offer. I don't know if I'm looking at 20% off or 40% off until I click in.
- **Product rail feels generic** — a slipper, PJs, a serum, a sneaker — nothing screams "designer" the way my shopping habits would want.

## Recommendations

- Swap one hero panel for a lifestyle shot of a woman trying on/carrying designer pieces — keep the playful energy but represent the customer actually funding this sale.
- Add a small Nordy Club rewards strip ("You have $45 in Nordy Notes" or "2x points during Anniversary Sale") right under the top banner.
- Put the actual discount range ("Up to 40% off") into the hero copy instead of just "Anniversary Sale Ends Sunday."
- Swap one Must-Haves tile for a recognizable designer name/logo product to reinforce the premium tier.
