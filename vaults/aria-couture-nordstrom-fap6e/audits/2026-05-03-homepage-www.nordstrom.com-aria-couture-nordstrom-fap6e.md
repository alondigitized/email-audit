## What's working

- **The Nordstrom wordmark** is clean and renders correctly — brand identity is at least intact at the top left.
- **The phone number (1.888.282.6060)** is a clickable link, so if I were genuinely locked out, I know exactly how to escalate. That's a small but real customer-service signal.
- **The footer copyright line** (`© 2026 Nordstrom, Inc.`) confirms the page is live and current, not a cached ghost.

## What's weak

- **The entire shopping experience is missing** — no hero, no nav, no categories, no products, no promotions. I'm a Nordy Club member who Anniversary Sale-calendars months in advance, and I got a security interstitial instead of a homepage.
- **"We've noticed some unusual activity"** is alarming copy. It reads like my account is flagged, not like a routine bot check. For a loyalty-tier shopper like me, that creates instant brand anxiety.
- **Zero personalization recovery path** — there's no "Sign In to continue" button, no "Try again" CTA, no app download redirect. If I'm a real customer, I'm just... stuck.
- **The affiliate-network paragraph** is completely off-tone. Explaining the bot-detection policy and linking to the Nordstrom Affiliate Network is developer/partner messaging dropped straight onto a consumer-facing block page. Wrong audience, wrong moment.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Sign In" or "Continue as Member" CTA** immediately below the error message — Nordy Club members should be able to authenticate past the block in one tap rather than being told to call a phone number.
- **Soften the copy** from "unusual activity" (sounds like fraud) to something like "Let's verify you're human — just a quick check" with a CAPTCHA or device confirmation flow.
- **Strip the affiliate-network paragraph** entirely from the consumer-facing version of this page — route that to a developer/partner subdomain.
- **Surface a minimal brand holding state** (logo + one lifestyle image + "We'll be right back") rather than a blank white wall, so the page still communicates luxury retail even during a block.
