## What's working

- The Nordstrom wordmark in the top-left renders cleanly — at least the brand identity survived.
- The customer service phone number (1.888.282.6060) is clearly visible and readable if I genuinely needed support.
- The copyright line "© 2026 Nordstrom, Inc." confirms the page is live and served, not a DNS failure.

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is missing.** I'm looking at a bot-block interstitial — no navigation, no hero, no products, nothing shoppable.
- **"We've noticed some unusual activity"** — as a loyal Nordy Club member browsing on my phone, this message makes me feel accused and unwelcome. That is a catastrophic tone for a loyalty-first brand.
- **No way forward.** There's no "prove you're human," no CAPTCHA, no sign-in prompt to verify my membership. My only option is to call a 1-800 number, which I am absolutely not doing.
- **Zero personalization.** The whole Nordstrom value prop is knowing who I am — stylist DMs, my wishlist, Nordy Points. This page treats me like a bot.
- **Blank lower third of the page** — enormous dead whitespace below the error text before the footer. Visually unfinished and alarming.

## Recommendations

- **Add a sign-in CTA to the block page** — "If you're a Nordy Club member, sign in to confirm your identity" with a visible button. I should be able to unlock access in one tap, not a phone call.
- **Soften the copy.** "We've noticed some unusual activity" reads accusatory. Swap to something like "Just a quick security check — sign in to continue shopping" to preserve the brand relationship.
- **Add a CAPTCHA or browser-verify flow** so legitimate mobile shoppers aren't dead-ended. A phone call in 2026 is a conversion killer.
- **Surface the Nordy Club login explicitly** — if the block is triggered by my session state, recognizing a logged-in member token and bypassing the block entirely would eliminate the problem for loyal customers.
