# Homepage snapshot · www.nordstrom.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- Oh. I did not expect to open my go-to app and get a robot-suspicion wall — this is a bot-block page, not the Nordstrom homepage. There's zero merchandising, zero Nordy Club love, and absolutely nothing that looks like the brand I've been loyally swiping through since the Anniversary Sale became a personal holiday for me. This is a dead end, full stop.

## What's working

- **The NORDSTROM wordmark** is clean and correctly renders — at minimum I know I'm on the right domain.
- **Customer Service phone number (1.888.282.6060)** is clearly linked — if I were a real user locked out, that's at least actionable.
- **Affiliate Network link** is present for anyone who actually needs it — legible, underlined, easy to spot.

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is blocked** — I see zero products, zero promotions, no nav, no hero imagery. As a loyal Nordy Club member, this is a jarring, trust-eroding experience.
- **No logged-in recovery path** — there's no "sign in to verify you're human" option, no CAPTCHA, no soft gate. It just… kicks me out.
- **No brand warmth** — the tone is clinical and slightly accusatory ("unusual activity"). For a brand built on white-glove service, this copy is completely off-voice.
- **Dead white space** — below the three paragraphs, there's an enormous empty white expanse before the copyright line. It screams "something went wrong" even louder than the copy already does.
- **Zero personalization or loyalty acknowledgment** — not even a generic "Welcome back" or Nordy Club prompt to re-authenticate.

## Recommendations

- **Add a soft-gate CAPTCHA or "Verify you're human" button** — let real customers prove identity and pass through in one tap instead of sending them to a call center.
- **Inject brand-voice warmth into the error copy** — something like "Hey, we just want to make sure it's really you before we show you the good stuff" keeps the Nordstrom tone intact.
- **Add a direct sign-in CTA** — a prominent "Sign in to continue" button would recover the majority of legitimate loyalty members immediately.
- **Reduce the dead white space** — even a minimal footer with store hours or the app store badge would signal "this is still Nordstrom" rather than "you've hit a wall."
