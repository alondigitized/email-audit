# Homepage snapshot · www.costco.com · 2026-05-04
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-04**
## Executive summary

- This is Costco's mobile homepage and it is doing the bare minimum — I can see it knows I'm in Burbank, it's pushing Mother's Day flowers, and there's a May Online Savings section just starting to peek in at the bottom. The site reads like a functional warehouse directory, not a destination for someone who plans her monthly stock-up around this place. For how central Costco is to running my household, the homepage feels weirdly thin.
- **7/10**
- + Returning-shopper hook: "My Warehouse: Burbank, Open until 8:30PM" is personalized — the site knows where I am
- + Primary CTA visible: "Shop" button is clearly present above the fold
- + Visual hierarchy is clear: banner → logo/nav → search → location → tiles flows without confusion
- + No render bugs: page looks clean, no broken elements
- + Demographic signals OK: Mother's Day banner is relevant for a 42-year-old mom
- + Current campaign/season: "May Online Savings" + Mother's Day = this is actually May, not stale
- + Offer feels honest: nothing manipulative or buried visible in what's shown
- − No concrete offer: "May Online Savings" has zero specifics — no %, no $, no threshold
- − Hero doesn't speak to household-operator persona: nothing about bulk, family size, or Kirkland
- − Loyalty/membership benefits invisible: my 2% Executive reward is nowhere on this screen

## What's working

- **"My Warehouse: Burbank, Open until 8:30PM"** — I actually appreciate knowing my warehouse is open when I'm browsing on my phone Saturday afternoon. That's useful, not decorative.
- **"Don't Forget Mom! Pre-Order Mother's Day Flowers Today"** — Seasonal, timely, and as a mom of five I notice this. Pre-ordering flowers through Costco is exactly the kind of errand I'd consolidate here.
- **"May Online Savings" module starting at the bottom** — The section heading plus the product images beginning to load tells me deals are one scroll away, which keeps me from bouncing.
- **Clean mobile layout** — Search is front-and-center, the "Shop" button is tappable, nothing is crammed. For a site I visit on a phone while in the parking lot, this matters.

## What's weak

- **"This Week's Featured Items" tile** — That's just a photo of a Costco building exterior. I clicked into this page to shop, not look at the store I'm about to drive to. Zero product signal.
- **"What's New" tile with a megaphone graphic** — Generic clip-art energy. What's new in what category? For whom? This tells me nothing.
- **No Kirkland Signature anywhere** — Kirkland is the reason I'm loyal. It should be leading, not absent.
- **No Executive member or 2% reward callout** — I use my rewards check like a quarterly bonus. Not seeing it above the fold on mobile is a missed hook for a returning member.
- **No specific savings numbers** — "May Online Savings" with no amounts is a tease, not an offer. Show me $8 off the Kirkland olive oil or 20% off paper towels and I'm scrolling.
- **Dot navigation with 4 slides** — I can't tell what the other three slides even contain. No teaser, no label. I might not swipe.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the Costco-building-photo tile with a "Top Picks This Week" grid** — Show 3–4 actual product thumbnails with member pricing. That's what gets someone like me to tap through instead of opening the app.
- **Add a persistent Executive member bar** with my current rewards balance (e.g., "Your 2% reward: $47.80") — I use this as a check, and seeing it on the homepage reinforces the membership value every visit.
- **Swap the megaphone "What's New" tile for a category shortcut strip** — Give me one-tap access to Household, Grocery, Electronics, and Fresh — the four categories that drive my Saturday run. I shouldn't need to open the full nav for the things I buy every month.
- **Surface one specific Kirkland Signature deal above the fold** — Even a "Kirkland Spotlight: Organic Chicken Broth, 6-pack" would speak directly to how I shop and reinforce why I'm loyal.
