# Homepage snapshot · www.costco.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- Costco.com is doing what Costco always does — leading with function over flash. The Memorial Day push on appliances and mattresses lands right in my wheelhouse (that king mattress is happening this month), and I appreciate that the site already knows I'm an Executive Member at Alhambra without me touching a thing. But the mobile viewport gives me almost nothing to sink my teeth into — two category tiles and a half-rendered Member-Only Savings block isn't exactly the warehouse-style sensory overload I showed up for.

## What's working

- **Executive Member hours callout** — I see "Executive Member hours start at 9AM" the second the page loads. That's the site recognizing me by tier. It's the digital equivalent of the early-bird warehouse lane, and I notice it.
- **Personalized warehouse + ZIP** — "My Warehouse: Alhambra" and "Delivery Location: 91801" are both surfaced without me doing anything. For a Saturday-run shopper who switches between in-store and .com, this is exactly right.
- **Memorial Day banner** — Appliances and mattresses in late May? Correct. I've been eyeing a new washer/dryer pair. This banner stops my thumb.
- **Member-Only Savings teaser** — Even half-rendered at the bottom of the viewport, that red-and-white flap signals "there's a deal catalog here waiting for you." I'm going to scroll.
- **"This Week's Featured Items" tile** — Rotisserie chicken aside, I live by the weekly featured rotation. This tile is exactly how I discover what I'm adding to the Saturday cart.

## What's weak

- **No specific discount figures** — "Memorial Day Savings" without a number is a missed opportunity. Every other retailer is screaming "UP TO 40% OFF." Costco just says "Savings!" and expects me to trust them. I do trust them, but a number would make me tap faster.
- **Only two category tiles visible** — Two tiles (Featured Items + Grocery) in the carousel is a thin content payload for a shopper who also needs paper towels, olive oil, and a 48-pack of juice boxes. I can't see Household, Bulk, or Electronics from here.
- **No rewards balance / 2% check status** — As an Executive Member, I track my reward accrual. A line that says "Your 2% Reward: $XX" would be a retention hook AND a spend motivator. Not visible here.
- **Zero imagery with someone like me** — No families, no full carts, no Saturday-run lifestyle. The page is functional but cold. A single lifestyle shot of a loaded flatbed cart in a warehouse would do more than a dozen banners.
- **Search bar is passive** — It says "Search Costco" but there are no suggested search terms, trending items, or "Members are searching for: rotisserie chicken, paper towels…" prompts. Medium-tech-comfort me would use that prompt.

## Recommendations

- **Add a specific number to the Memorial Day banner** — "Save up to 30% on Appliances & Mattresses — Memorial Day Only" would stop thumbs and communicate the magnitude of the sale without any redesign.
- **Surface the 2% reward balance in the logged-in header** — Replace the generic "Account" icon text with "Hi Mara • $47.28 earned" for Executive Members. It costs nothing and makes me feel seen every single time I open the app.
- **Expand the category carousel to 4–5 tiles** — Grocery, Household, Electronics, Kirkland Signature, and Current Deals should all be one tap away above the fold. Two tiles on a carousel that requires swiping is lazy product organization for a mobile-first user.
- **Add a trending-searches chip strip below the search bar** — "Kirkland toilet paper · rotisserie chicken · outdoor furniture · air purifier" — four chips, no commitment required. Medium-tech-comfort shoppers browse by prompt, not by intention.
