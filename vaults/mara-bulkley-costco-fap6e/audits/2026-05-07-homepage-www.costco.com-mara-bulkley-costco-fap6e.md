# Homepage snapshot · www.costco.com · 2026-05-07
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- This feels like Costco knows I'm here — my Burbank warehouse is right there at the top and the $140 off Dyson Airwrap is exactly the kind of deal that makes me open the app on a Wednesday night. The brand feels like it always does: no-nonsense, deal-first, member-aware. Right now it's pushing the May online savings push and a couple of high-ticket appliance/beauty grabs.

## What's working

- **The Dyson Airwrap banner**: I have been watching that thing for two years. $140 off is not a vague "save big" — it's a number I can act on immediately. That banner earns its top-of-screen real estate.
- **Personalized warehouse callout**: "Burbank, Open until 8:30PM" makes this feel like my app, not a generic homepage. I know if I order before close I can grab it same-day. That's useful.
- **"May Online Savings" section**: The thumbnail strip at the bottom shows luggage and what looks like patio furniture — bulky items I'd buy online instead of hauling through the warehouse. That's the right channel for that product type.
- **"This Week's Featured Items" + "What's New" tiles**: Clean, direct labels. No jargon. I know exactly what I'm clicking into.
- **Search bar prominence**: Middle of the screen, labeled "Search Costco." With five kids I usually know what I need — I'd use this immediately.

## What's weak

- **No Executive member rewards balance**: My 2% check is one of the reasons I renewed my Executive membership. There's no badge, no balance, no "You have $X in rewards" hook anywhere. That's a miss — it's the loyalty signal that would make me feel seen as a heavy user vs. a casual browser.
- **"What's New" tile is vague**: A red megaphone graphic tells me nothing about what's actually new. If it's new electronics or a Kirkland item, say so. I'm not tapping a mystery button.
- **No Kirkland signal**: Kirkland Signature is my primary brand and I see zero mention of it above the fold. For a member-heavy household operator that's a gap.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible**: I do a lot of .com orders for bulky items. If I knew "free shipping on orders over $X," I'd be building a cart right now. That number isn't here.
- **Thumbnail images in May Online Savings are too small**: On mobile I can barely tell what those products are. I can make out luggage and something furniture-adjacent, but I shouldn't have to squint.

## Recommendations

- **Add an Executive member widget above the fold**: A small chip showing "Executive Member | $X earned this year" next to the Account icon would make the rewards program feel real and give me a reason to load up a cart to hit the next tier.
- **Surface a Kirkland Signature deal in the carousel**: Replace one of the generic carousel slides with a Kirkland Signature featured item — paper towels, olive oil, protein bars. It signals you know your audience.
- **Show the free-shipping threshold in a sticky bar or under the search**: One line — "Free shipping on orders $75+" — anchored near the search bar would directly address the .com-for-bulky-items use case I'm already in.
- **Make the "May Online Savings" thumbnails larger or add labels**: Either increase the tile size or put a category label ("Luggage," "Outdoor Furniture") under each image so I can see at a glance whether this sweep has anything for my Saturday list.
