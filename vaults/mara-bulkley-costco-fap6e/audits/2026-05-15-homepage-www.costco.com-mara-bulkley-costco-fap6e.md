# Homepage snapshot · www.costco.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- This is unmistakably Costco — the logo, the warehouse localization, and the blunt "$300 OFF" headline feel exactly like the brand I know. Right now they're pushing Memorial Day mattress savings hard, which is fine, but I opened this app to restock my pantry and check what's in this week's featured items, and the homepage is spending most of its real estate selling me a king mattress. It's a solid Costco experience, just not quite calibrated to my Saturday-morning grocery mission.

## What's working

- **Top promotional banner** — "After $300 OFF" with a real price ($999.99) is the kind of blunt deal communication Costco does best; I trust it instantly
- **My Warehouse localization** — seeing "Alhambra, Open until 8:30PM" tells me this isn't a generic session; it knows I'm a member with a home warehouse
- **Grocery category tile** — it's small but it's there in the category carousel, and it's the one tap I actually need
- **Memorial Day campaign timing** — we're replacing the kids' beds this summer; the timing is honestly relevant even if I didn't come here for it
- **Clean mobile layout** — no clutter, no overlapping text, no ads fighting for space; very easy to scan even on a phone screen

## What's weak

- **Zero rewards/membership signal above the fold** — I am an Executive Member and the 2% reward check is my flex. Nothing on this screen acknowledges that I'm not just any customer; that's a miss
- **The Grocery tile is tiny** — it's a small thumbnail competing with "This Week's Featured Items" in a five-dot carousel; for a household that drops $400–$600 per run on food, this deserves more prominence
- **No Kirkland callout** — Kirkland Signature is the reason I'm Kirkland-loyal, and there's no brand mention, no "Member's Mark"-style callout, nothing
- **Category carousel requires swiping** — I can see only two tiles ("This Week's Featured Items" and "Grocery") and then three more dots I have to swipe to reach; on a busy Saturday morning that's friction
- **No free-shipping threshold or delivery info** — I do .com for bulky items specifically; the homepage doesn't tell me what the threshold is or that delivery to 91801 is active

## Recommendations

- **Persist a slim Executive Member ribbon** under the location bar ("Executive Member · Your 2% reward accrues on this order") — it costs one row of screen space and directly speaks to members who drive the highest AOV
- **Swap the category carousel for a persistent 2×3 category grid** with Grocery, Household Essentials, Kirkland Signature, Featured Items, Electronics, and Health — so power users can reach their aisle in one tap instead of swiping through dots
- **Add a delivery confidence bar** ("Free delivery on orders over $75 · Ships to 91801") directly under the hero; this is the one piece of info that gets me to order bulky items online instead of hauling them from the warehouse
- **Anchor the hero banner with a visible "Shop Sale" button** in the Memorial Day section — right now it's a beautiful banner that goes nowhere obvious on mobile
