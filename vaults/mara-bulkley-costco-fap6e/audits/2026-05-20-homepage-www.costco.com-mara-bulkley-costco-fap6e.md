# Homepage snapshot · www.costco.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- This is the Costco site doing what it does best in the first few inches — it already knows I'm in Alhambra, it knows I'm an Executive Member, and it's showing me the early-access hours I actually plan around on Saturday mornings. The big push right now is mattresses, which isn't why I'm here, but I see a Grocery tile and "Member-Only Savings" creeping into frame at the bottom, so I know the stuff I care about is a scroll away. Functional, branded, and very obviously built for people who already drink the Kirkland Kool-Aid.

## What's working

- **Personalized warehouse block** — "My Warehouse: Alhambra / Executive Member hours start at 9AM" is the first thing I read after the banner and it immediately tells me this site knows me. That early-hours perk is the whole reason I have the Executive card.
- **Grocery tile** — it's small but visible, and for me that's the tent-pole category. One tap to get where I'm going.
- **"Member-Only Savings & Everyday Values" section loading in** — even half-visible, that coupon-book framing is the Costco ritual. I know exactly what that is and I want to see it.
- **Clean, uncluttered mobile layout** — no pop-up modal, no permission wall, no cookie banner blocking the screen. I can actually see the page on my phone.
- **"This Week's Featured Items" tile** — as a regular, "what's in this week" is exactly the dopamine hit that makes me browse longer.

## What's weak

- **Mattress hero is an off-target opener for a grocery/bulk run** — a $60–$700 range is so wide it tells me nothing about whether my household can realistically use this right now. I'm here for the Kirkland paper towels, not an adjustable bed today.
- **No rotisserie, no Kirkland, no food imagery in the hero** — this is where my brain lives in Costco-land and it's not acknowledged at all in the top half of the page.
- **No visible 2% reward balance or Executive reward progress** — that check is the reason I pay for the upgrade. If it were here, I'd tap immediately.
- **Only two category tiles visible, with a five-dot carousel** — three more slides are hiding behind those dots and I have no idea what they are. The pagination gives no hint. Medium-tech-comfort me might not think to swipe.
- **No free-shipping signal above the fold** — I use .com specifically for bulky items. If there's a threshold, it needs to be visible or I'm second-guessing my cart before I even start.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the mattress banner for a rotating household hero** — lead with a "Stock-up Saturday" or "Family of 5 Favorites" frame; mattresses can live in a secondary slot and let the food/household essentials lane do the heavy lifting for most visits.
- **Surface the Executive 2% reward balance in the location/membership block** — one line: "Your 2026 reward: $XX.XX" right under the Executive Member hours. That number is the emotional anchor of the whole membership for people like me.
- **Label the carousel dots or add a swipe hint** — even a tiny "→ See more" nudge would help medium-tech users discover the other three category tiles they're currently ignoring.
- **Add a free-shipping threshold chip near the search bar** — something like "Free shipping on orders $75+" pinned near the search field so it's always visible as I browse.
