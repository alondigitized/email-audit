# Homepage snapshot · www.costco.com · 2026-05-30
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-30**
## Executive summary

- This is unmistakably Costco — the red logo, the warehouse locator, the pragmatic layout. It's leading with the one-two punch of "Online Only Savings End Tomorrow" and "Member-Only Savings & Everyday Values," which is exactly the language I show up for. For me specifically, the Executive Member callout in the warehouse hours block is a nice recognition signal, but the actual product content above the fold is thin — two tiles (Featured Items and Grocery) don't give me much to grab onto on a Saturday morning with a cart to fill.

## What's working

- **"My Warehouse: Alhambra" with Executive Member hours** — I immediately know this page knows I'm a member. That executive-hours callout is low-key satisfying. It's not "welcome back" fluff, it's actually useful information.
- **"Select Online Only Savings End Tomorrow" top banner** — blue strip is impossible to miss, and "End Tomorrow" creates real urgency without being obnoxious. I'll check what those are.
- **"Member-Only Savings & Everyday Values" banner** — the red ribbon at the bottom of the screen is on-brand and confirms this trip will be worth it. That language is my love language when it comes to Costco.
- **Delivery Location 91201** — zip code-level delivery context is useful for the bulky items I order online. Confirms the site knows where to send my 50-lb dog food.
- **Search bar is prominent** — when I know what I want (and I usually do — rotisserie chicken, Kirkland olive oil, the usual suspects), I'll search. The bar is front and center.

## What's weak

- **"This Week's Featured Items" tile leads with a storefront exterior photo** — that's not merchandise, that's architecture. I came here to shop, not admire the building. Show me what's inside.
- **No product imagery above the fold** — I scroll Costco.com to see deals, not building photos and a produce still life. There are zero products with prices, no Kirkland callouts, nothing to make my trigger finger itch.
- **Carousel dots indicate more content but give no preview** — I see four more dots below the two tiles. What's in those? I have no idea. On mobile, unlabeled carousel navigation is a dead zone.
- **No rewards balance or 2% cashback teaser** — as an Executive Member, the 2% reward is a real behavioral hook. Not a hint of it. That's a missed retention signal.
- **"Member-Only Savings" is partially cut off** — the banner at the bottom is barely visible; it's doing half a job without a clear CTA ("Shop Now," "See Deals," anything).

## Recommendations

- **Replace the storefront hero tile with a high-velocity item at a Kirkland price** — show me a 48-pack of Kirkland water or the Costco rotisserie chicken with "$4.99 members only" and I'm already tapping.
- **Add a visible 2% reward balance chip near the Member-Only Savings banner** — something like "Your Reward Balance: $84.20 — Shop to grow it." That's genuinely motivating for Executive Members.
- **Label the carousel dots or replace with a horizontally scrollable category strip** — "Household," "Grocery," "Electronics," "Clothing" — one tap to where I'm headed, not a mystery swipe game.
- **Surface a free shipping threshold or online-only exclusive prominently** — I order bulky items online specifically to avoid loading them into my car. Tell me upfront ("Free shipping on orders $75+") and I'll build a cart around it.
