# Homepage snapshot · www.costco.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- I know this site better than most people know their own pantry, so I came in ready to shop — and the homepage delivers the basics without embarrassing itself. It knows I have a warehouse, it knows my zip, and there's a "May Online Savings" banner that at least signals bulk-season mode. But the top offer is $150 off jewelry at a $999 minimum, which is about as useful to me on a Tuesday morning as a cashmere throw is to a kindergartner. I want the rotisserie chicken signal, the paper-towel pallet, the Kirkland badge — I'm not here to buy a necklace.

## What's working

- **My Warehouse personalization** — Burbank location with today's hours and my delivery zip 91201 is the right handshake for a returning member. I trust this site knows who I am.
- **"Ends Today!" urgency banner** — I clock deadlines. Even if I don't want the jewelry, the banner format trains my eye to look for time-bound deals, which I do act on.
- **"May Online Savings" section** — the thumbnail strip at the bottom shows what looks like luggage, appliances, and home goods. That's my category neighborhood. Good signal that bulk-buy season is on.
- **Search bar front and center** — I know exactly what I want (Kirkland paper towels, 30-pack Ziploc, Better Than Bouillon). One tap to search is how I actually shop .com.
- **"This Week's Featured Items" card** — as a loyal member I check this every week. The placement is right.

## What's weak

- **$999 jewelry offer** — this is my primary above-the-fold message and it's completely off-target. I'm here for household consumables; a high-ticket jewelry threshold feels like it was pasted in from a different site.
- **No loyalty / rewards surface** — I use my 2% Executive reward check as a budget line item. The fact that my balance isn't visible anywhere above the fold is a missed retention hook Costco should own.
- **Hero images are institutional, not aspirational** — a photo of a warehouse exterior and a clip-art megaphone don't make me feel anything. No family, no kitchen, no giant pallet of goods landing at a doorstep.
- **Carousel pagination dots with no visible content** — there are four dots below the featured cards but I can only see one state. On mobile I have no idea what else is there; I'm not going to swipe for it.
- **"What's New" is too vague** — that could mean anything. For a household operator, "New in Kirkland" or "New Bulk Packs" would make me tap. Generic "What's New" does not.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the jewelry banner for a member-relevant offer** — replace "Spend $999 on jewelry" with something like "May Savings: Members Save Big on Household Essentials" with a realistic threshold ($150+ spend) that matches how I actually shop.
- **Surface the Executive reward balance** — put a small "Your 2% Reward: $XX.XX" chip next to the Account icon or in the warehouse row. This is retention gold and Costco isn't using it.
- **Replace the warehouse-exterior hero card with a lifestyle shot** — a full cart, a packed pantry, a family unloading from an SUV. Something that says "this is for people running a household," not "this is a building."
- **Label the carousel** — add a preview of slide 2 peeking in from the right edge so I know swiping is worth my time, or replace the dots with visible category tiles.
