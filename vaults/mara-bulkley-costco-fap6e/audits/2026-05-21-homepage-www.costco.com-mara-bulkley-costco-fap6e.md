# Homepage snapshot · www.costco.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Okay, first glance — it knows I'm an Executive Member in Alhambra, and it's pushing patio furniture in May, which, fine, I do have a backyard and five people who need somewhere to sit. But I came here to stock up on Kirkland olive oil and three flats of sparkling water, not browse deck chairs. The Grocery tile is right there, which is genuinely useful, but this opening screen is doing more for a casual browser than for the person who has a running mental list of 14 items before she even opens the app.

## What's working

- **Location + tier recognition** — showing my warehouse (Alhambra) and calling out Executive Member hours immediately signals this experience is personalized. That matters to me. I'm not a Gold Star shopper and I like knowing that's acknowledged.
- **Grocery category tile** — it's above the fold, image is recognizable (food), one tap away. For my use case this is the most important real estate on the page and it's right there.
- **"This Week's Featured Items" tile** — I run my household on the weekly featured cycle. Seeing this named explicitly is a reminder of why I opened the app.
- **"Member-Only Savings & Everyday Values" module** — even partially visible, this is the anchor promise of Costco membership. Good that it's early in the scroll.
- **Top banner is seasonally accurate** — May patio push is legit. I will eventually need new outdoor chairs.

## What's weak

- **No specific offer above the fold** — "Save Today" is filler copy. Tell me it's 20% off, tell me it's an instant rebate, give me a number. Without it, there's zero urgency.
- **No bulk/household signal in the hero** — A family of five does not identify with a dreamy backyard lifestyle shot. Show me a warehouse floor, a loaded flatbed cart, something that says "volume."
- **No cart or list shortcut** — I often come back mid-week to add to an existing order. There's a cart icon in the nav but no visible cart count or "continue shopping" nudge for returning users.
- **Carousel dots with no labels** — there are five carousel dots below the category tiles and I have no idea what's in slides 2–5. On mobile that's friction — I'm not going to swipe blind.
- **No Kirkland brand callout** — not a single mention of Kirkland Signature anywhere in the visible viewport. That's my loyalty anchor and it's invisible.

## Recommendations

- **Put a specific dollar or percent figure in the banner** — "Save up to $150 on Patio Sets Today" beats "Save Today" every single time. Ship it next week, test it against the vague version.
- **Add a returning-member shortcut strip** — one horizontal row with "Grocery," "Household," "Kirkland Signature," "Your Warehouse Deals" — the four things I come for. Takes zero hero real estate, dramatically cuts time-to-category.
- **Show cart item count on the cart icon** — if I have 8 items staged, show me "Cart (8)" so I know to go finish the order. This is a basic returning-shopper hook that's missing.
- **Label or thumbnail the carousel slides** — swap the generic dot indicators for small labeled thumbnails (e.g., "Electronics," "Appliances," "Clothing") so I know whether slide 3 is worth a swipe.
