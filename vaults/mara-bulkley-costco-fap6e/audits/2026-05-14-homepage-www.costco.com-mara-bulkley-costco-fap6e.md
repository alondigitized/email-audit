# Homepage snapshot · www.costco.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- This is Costco doing what Costco does — efficient, no-frills, and built around the warehouse run. Right now it's pushing Memorial Day appliance deals hard, which honestly isn't a bad angle for me, but I came here expecting to see bulk grocery staples and Kirkland deals, not a showroom floor. It reads like a competent warehouse site, not a homepage that knows I've been a member for eleven years.

## What's working

- **"My Warehouse: Burbank – Open until 8:30PM"** — This is the single best thing on the page. I know exactly where I'm going and when I need to leave. That's real utility.
- **Grocery tile** — It's immediately visible in the carousel and the imagery shows food products. One tap and I'm where I need to be.
- **Top banner appliance offer** — "$300-$500 off" is specific enough to be credible. My fridge is seven years old. This actually lands.
- **Memorial Day Appliance Savings hero** — Timely, seasonal, and visually clean. Appliances are a genuine household-of-five purchase category.
- **"Search Costco" bar** — I know what I want (Kirkland olive oil, paper towels, rotisserie chicken strips). The search bar is prominent and easy to tap.

## What's weak

- **No Kirkland brand callout anywhere above the fold** — For someone who plans her entire pantry around Kirkland Signature, this is a miss. It's the whole point.
- **No Executive Member / 2% reward hook** — I use that annual check like a bonus. The site should be reminding me that every dollar I spend here compounds. It's not.
- **Carousel pagination dots** — Five dots, and I can only see slide one (Featured Items + Grocery). I have no idea what's on slides two through five without swiping. That's engagement left on the table.
- **"See Item Page For Details"** on the appliance offer — Classic fine-print deflection. How many items actually qualify? "Select" is doing a lot of work there.
- **No bulk/quantity messaging** — Nothing says "jumbo," "case," or "palletized price." This could be any generic retailer's homepage.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Kirkland Signature strip directly below the Grocery tile** — Even a "Shop Kirkland" button with the K-shield logo would tell me this site knows who I am.
- **Surface the Executive Member 2% reward in the nav bar or beneath My Warehouse** — One line: "You've earned $X toward your reward check." That's a conversion driver every time I log in.
- **Replace the "Featured Items" carousel tile thumbnail** — It shows a warehouse exterior. That doesn't tell me anything. Swap it for a flat-lay of the week's actual deals — rotisserie pack, paper towel pallet, the Kirkland protein bars everyone's buying.
- **Put an end date on the Memorial Day deal** — "Ends May 26" costs nothing and makes me act sooner instead of clicking away and forgetting.
