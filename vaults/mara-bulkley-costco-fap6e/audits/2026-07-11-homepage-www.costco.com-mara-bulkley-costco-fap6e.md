# Homepage snapshot · www.costco.com · 2026-07-11
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-11**
## Executive summary

- Costco's mobile homepage is exactly what I'd expect from a brand that knows I'm already sold — it's utilitarian, membership-forward, and built for the member who just wants to get in and get out (digitally or physically). Right now it's pushing a tire deal up top and member savings below, which tracks for mid-month replenishment mode. It's not trying to wow anyone; it's trying to serve existing members efficiently, which honestly is on brand.

## What's working

- **"My Warehouse: Alhambra" with Executive Member hours** — I plan my Saturday run around these exact hours. Seeing it personalized to my warehouse, front and center, is genuinely useful and saves me a Google search.
- **"$80 OFF Any Set of 4 New Bridgestone Tires" banner** — My van has 87,000 miles on it. This registers immediately as a household-budget item, not just noise.
- **"Grocery" tile one tap from the homepage** — My primary mission at Costco is stocking the kitchen. That shortcut being visible without menu-diving is exactly right.
- **"Member-Only Savings & Everyday Values" banner** — The Kirkland/coupon-book framing is my language. This is why I'm an Executive Member.
- **Delivery Location set to 91801** — Remembered my location. Small thing, but it signals the app knows me.

## What's weak

- **Hours block dominates above-the-fold real estate** — Almost a third of my screen is warehouse hours and delivery location. Useful once a week, but today I want to shop, not check operating hours.
- **Grocery tile is tiny** — On mobile, the two category tiles are small thumbnails. "Grocery" — arguably the #1 reason most members visit the site — gets the same visual weight as the warehouse exterior shot for "Featured Items." That's a hierarchy problem.
- **Tire deal is relevant but not my mission** — Leading with a tire banner is fine for my household in aggregate, but if I'm in meal-planning mode on a Thursday night, it's a context mismatch. No grocery or pantry promo above the fold.
- **Carousel with 4 slides but no visibility into slides 2–4** — Those three extra dots are invisible effort. I'm not going to swipe-discover what's there; I'll tap Grocery and move on.
- **No urgency on member savings** — "Member-Only Savings & Everyday Values" is a permanent state of affairs at Costco. No end date, no "coupon book expires X" callout. Wastes the hook.

## Recommendations

- **Lead with a grocery/bulk-stocking hero instead of tires for non-tire-season traffic** — A "Stock Your Pantry" or "This Week's Kirkland Deals" tile in the hero slot would convert browsers like me faster than a tire banner for Thursday-night mobile sessions.
- **Collapse the warehouse hours block** — Move it behind a single "Alhambra — Executive hours 9AM" chip that expands on tap. Free up that real estate for a promotional tile above the fold.
- **Add a coupon-book expiration date to the Member-Only Savings banner** — "Expires July 14" turns a static banner into urgency. It's already the most compelling hook for Executive Members; give it a deadline.
- **Make the Grocery tile full-width** — On mobile, grocery is the dominant use case. Give it a full-width banner row with a lifestyle image (think: a packed refrigerator, a pantry wall, a rotisserie chicken) instead of a thumbnail competing with the warehouse storefront.
