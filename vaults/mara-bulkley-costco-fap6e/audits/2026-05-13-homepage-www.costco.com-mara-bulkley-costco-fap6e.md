# Homepage snapshot · www.costco.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay, it's Costco — so the bones are solid and I know exactly what I'm here for. But opening the app right now, the first thing they're leading with is graduation gift ideas, which, fine, I do have a junior in high school, but that is not why I'm here on a Tuesday morning. I'm here because we're out of Kirkland olive oil and I need to check if the patio furniture deal from last week is still live. This homepage feels like a functional warehouse app more than a curated shopping destination — which, honestly, is kind of on-brand, but they're leaving money on the table by not surfacing the Member Savings harder.

## What's working

- **"My Warehouse: Burbank, Open until 8:30PM"** — I didn't have to hunt for this. Knowing my store hours before I even start shopping is exactly the kind of small thing that keeps me in the app.
- **"Grocery" carousel tile** — immediately visible, one tap, right where I need to go. This is probably the most-used category for a household runner like me.
- **"This Week's Featured Items" tile** — that weekly deal rotation is half the reason I even open the site. Good that it's first in the carousel.
- **"Member-Only Savings — & Everyday Values" banner** — validates my executive membership the second I scroll a half-inch. That's the right signal for a loyal member.
- **Search bar prominent above the fold** — when I need Kirkland Signature Olive Oil 3-liter, I'm not browsing. Search front and center is the right call for a power user.

## What's weak

- **"Gift Ideas for Grads" hero banner** — this is fine for a college parent, but it's not my Costco reason. Leading with this instead of a "Member Savings This Week" or "Stock Up Now" frame wastes the hero slot on a Tuesday run.
- **No rewards balance anywhere on screen** — I'm an executive member and I use that 2% annual reward like a savings account. There's zero acknowledgment that I've earned anything. A "Your Rewards: $XX" nudge here would make me feel seen and keep me browsing longer.
- **No specific discount numbers** — "Member-Only Savings" is a promise, not an offer. Show me $8 off Kirkland detergent or 20% off patio sets. Numbers convert; labels don't.
- **Carousel with five dots but I only see two tiles** — I shouldn't have to swipe to discover what's on slides 3, 4, and 5. Thumbnail strip or a "view all" link would help.
- **No product imagery below the carousel** — the "Member-Only Savings" banner cuts off. There's no pull to keep scrolling. Dead zone.

## Recommendations

- **Replace "Gift Ideas for Grads" with a "Member Savings This Week" banner that shows a specific dollar or percent off** — even one anchor deal ("Kirkland Signature Paper Towels, Member Price $24.99") gives me a reason to tap through instead of just checking hours and leaving.
- **Add an executive member rewards balance chip near the top** — a small "You've earned $142 this year" callout next to Account would take ten seconds to ship and would make every executive member feel like the homepage was built for them.
- **Show at least 3 carousel slides visually at once** (partial-peek layout) — right now the carousel looks like it has two items. Partial peeking on the third tile signals "there's more here" and increases swipe rate.
- **Surface one bulk-buy or household staple deal below the carousel** — a single featured product tile (rotisserie chicken, paper goods, olive oil, laundry) with member pricing visible anchors me before I even search.
