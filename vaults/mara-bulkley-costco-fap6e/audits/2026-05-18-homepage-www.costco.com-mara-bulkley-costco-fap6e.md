# Homepage snapshot · www.costco.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- Opening Costco.com on my phone, I'm immediately hit with a washer-and-dryer push and then a whole block of warehouse hours — which is fine intel, but not why I'm here. This feels like the digital equivalent of walking into the warehouse and stopping at the entrance map when I already know my route. It's Costco, so the bones are solid, but the homepage is doing location-awareness housekeeping before it does any selling.

## What's working

- **My Warehouse block ("Alhambra"):** I know instantly this is personalized to my location. Executive Member hours called out separately — I notice that every single Saturday morning.
- **"Grocery" tile:** It's right there, two tiles on the main content row. One tap and I'm in my lane.
- **"This Week's Featured Items" tile:** That's the Costco carousel I actually use. Scanning weekly deals is part of my ritual before the Saturday run.
- **"Member-Only Savings & Everyday Values" module:** Even partially visible at the bottom, it signals the page is going to deliver on the membership value I'm paying for.
- **Clean mobile render:** No jank, no overlapping elements, search bar is accessible and large enough.

## What's weak

- **Appliance banner as the hero:** Leading with washer/dryer delivery is a fine offer for *someone*, but it's not where a bulk grocery operator starts. I came for Kirkland paper towels and Greek yogurt by the flat, not a Maytag.
- **Warehouse hours block as visual centerpiece:** This is useful info buried in the wrong place. It's not driving me to shop — it's telling me when the doors open. That belongs in a smaller utility strip, not the prime content zone.
- **No specific offer in the hero banner:** "Save Today!" is not a number. A household-of-five budget manager needs to see a figure — 20% off, free delivery on orders over $75, something I can act on.
- **No 2% reward balance or Executive reward callout:** I use that check every year like clockwork. Even a "Your rewards are growing" nudge would land.
- **No Kirkland branding anywhere above the fold:** Kirkland is the reason I'm loyal. Showing zero Kirkland presence on load is a missed signal.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the hero banner copy with a member-specific offer that has a real number:** Something like "Executive Members: Free delivery on orders $75+" or "Member-Only Price: Kirkland Signature 2-pack — $X today." Make me feel the card in my hand.
- **Demote the warehouse hours to a collapsed utility bar:** Accordion it under "My Warehouse" so it's there when I need it but not consuming prime content space.
- **Add a Kirkland-branded or household-staples shortcut tile** alongside the Grocery and Featured Items tiles — "Household Essentials" or "Kirkland Picks" would send me exactly where I'm going without friction.
- **Surface a rewards nudge above the fold:** Even "Your 2% Executive Reward is accumulating" with a chevron to view balance keeps the loyalty flywheel spinning on every visit.
