# Homepage snapshot · www.costco.com · 2026-05-05
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-05**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so Costco's site knows I exist — it's showing me my Burbank warehouse hours and my zip — which is exactly the kind of "we know you" thing that keeps me coming back. But then the hero pushes a restaurant eGift card at me, and I'm standing here with a list of Kirkland paper towels and 48-count protein bars. The site feels like a solid, functional warehouse trip rather than anything that makes me stop scrolling and go "oh, I needed that."
- **7/10**
- + returning-shopper hook: "My Warehouse — Burbank, Open until 8:30PM" tells me I'm recognized and logged in
- + concrete offer: top banner "$64.99 After $10 OFF" on a $100 eGift card is a specific dollar figure, not vague
- + primary CTA visible: "Shop" button is above the fold, clearly labeled
- + visual hierarchy clear: banner → nav → location row → content tiles → savings — eye knows where to go
- + no render bugs: page looks clean and crisp, no broken layout
- + current campaign/season: "May Online Savings" is timely, we're in May
- + offer feels honest: no asterisks or buried fine print visible
- − hero does NOT speak to my persona: restaurant eGift card is not what a bulk-buying mom of five opens the app for on a Saturday
- − no loyalty/rewards balance visible: I want to see my 2% sitting there motivating me, and it's nowhere
- − no demographic signals: no imagery of a family stocking a garage shelf or a kitchen island buried in Kirkland goods

## What's working

- **"My Warehouse — Burbank, Open until 8:30PM"** — this tiny row earns a lot. It tells me the site knows me, knows where I shop, and saves me a step. That's exactly how I want to start a .com session before Saturday's run.
- **Top banner with a real number** — "$64.99 After $10 OFF" is the kind of math I respect. I can immediately decide if I want it (and honestly a restaurant eGift card for a family of five isn't crazy).
- **"This Week's Featured Items" tile** — rotisserie chicken logic: I come back weekly because the rotation changes. This tile speaks to that habit even if the name is bland.
- **"May Online Savings" section** — I can see furniture and what looks like luggage below, which signals this is going deeper than one promo and is worth a scroll.
- **Search bar** — front and center, high contrast. I usually know what I want and I'll search it directly. Medium-tech-comfort me appreciates not hunting for it.

## What's weak

- **Restaurant eGift card as the hero** — it's fine for someone impulse-browsing, but it doesn't say "stock your house for the next three weeks." I opened the app to reorder, and the first thing I see is a dining gift card. Mild whiplash.
- **Zero rewards/membership balance** — my 2% reward is a running motivator. Not showing it on the authenticated homepage is a missed layup. I literally use it as a proxy for how much I've spent; show it to me and I'll shop more.
- **No category navigation visible** — the "Shop" button presumably expands a menu, but until I tap it, the page gives me almost no category entry points. A horizontal scroll of top departments (Groceries, Electronics, Household) would get me moving faster.
- **Carousel dots with no visible context** — four dots, but I can only see two tiles. What's on slides 3 and 4? The dots don't give me a reason to swipe. Low information density.
- **"What's New" tile** — that megaphone icon with a red background feels like a clearance-rack sign, not a premium warehouse moment. The icon does nothing for me; I want to know *what* is new, not just that something exists.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the eGift card hero with a "May Stock-Up Savings" banner** showing Kirkland essentials or bulk categories — coffee, protein, cleaning supplies. Give me a number ("Save up to $30 on pantry staples") so I feel the pull immediately.
- **Add a persistent rewards-balance chip** near "My Warehouse" row — something like "2% Reward: $84.12" with a "View Details" link. That number alone would make me add one more thing to cart before checkout.
- **Expose top 5 categories as icon tiles below the location row** — Grocery, Household, Electronics, Health, Outdoor. One tap to any of them instead of opening a full nav drawer. I'm on mobile, I want direct lanes.
- **Make the carousel dots tell a story** — swap them for small label pills ("Featured," "New," "Savings," "Kirkland") so I know what's waiting without swiping blind.
