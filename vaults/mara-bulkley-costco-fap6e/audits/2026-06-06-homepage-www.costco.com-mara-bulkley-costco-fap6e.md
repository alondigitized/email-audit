# Homepage snapshot · www.costco.com · 2026-06-06
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-06**
## Executive summary

- This is peak Costco mobile — functional, member-aware, and dressed up for an end-of-savings push. It knows I'm an Executive Member at Alhambra, it's flashing urgency all over the place, and yet somehow the main content block between the header and that red button is doing almost nothing for me. I'm here to restock my house, and I don't see a single product.

## What's working

- **Personalized warehouse header** — "My Warehouse: Alhambra / Executive Member hours start at 9AM" tells me the site sees me as a real member, not a guest. That matters for a loyalty-first shopper.
- **Dual urgency signals** — the top banner AND the bottom red button both pound "End Tomorrow." Repetition works. I feel the clock.
- **"Get the Most Out of Your Membership" carousel card** — relevant to an Executive Member who actively tracks her 2% reward; at least one content block speaks to membership value.
- **"This Week's Featured Items" card** — the weekly featured rotation is exactly how I mentally organize my Costco run. Surfacing it in the carousel is right.

## What's weak

- **Zero product imagery above the fold** — I came to shop. There are no products, no prices, no categories visible without scrolling. The main carousel is two small text cards.
- **"Instant Savings" with no amount** — a savings event without a headline number ("Up to $200 off," "Save on 500+ items") is a missed conversion moment. I need a reason to tap.
- **Carousel feels like filler** — five pagination dots for what appears to be informational cards, not deals or products. I'm not going to swipe through that.
- **No Kirkland/bulk/household signal** — nothing on this screen tells me I can get 2.5lbs of mixed nuts or a flat of sparkling water. My actual shopping list is invisible.
- **No membership reward balance** — my 2% reward check is a real motivator for me. It's not here.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the carousel with a 3-up category shortcut grid** ("Pantry & Bulk," "Paper & Cleaning," "Frozen & Fresh") so I can get to what I need in one tap — that's my Saturday pattern.
- **Put a dollar figure on the savings banner** — "Select Savings Up to $150 Off End Tomorrow" converts harder than the generic copy. It's a small copy edit with big tap-through impact.
- **Surface the Executive Member reward balance** in the member header block (e.g., "You've earned $47.80 toward your reward check") — this is the hook that turns a browse into a cart.
- **Add at least one rotisserie chicken or Kirkland Signature anchor product** to the hero or a sticky strip — it's the single most-searched item and a pure retention signal for members like me.
