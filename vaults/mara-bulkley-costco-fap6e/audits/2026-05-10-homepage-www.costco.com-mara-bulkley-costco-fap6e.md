# Homepage snapshot · www.costco.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- Costco.com is doing what Costco does — no-nonsense, functional, a bit bland on the outside, and trusting its brand to carry the weight. Opening this on my phone I can see they're pushing gift cards at the top and kicking off "May Online Savings," but honestly the first thing I'm thinking is: where's the food? Where's the Kirkland? This looks like a generic homepage that could belong to any big-box dot-com, and for someone like me who's here specifically to restock the house and snag the bulk deals, it's making me do extra work to find my reason to stay.

## What's working

- **"Set My Warehouse" and "Set Delivery Location" prompts** — I appreciate that they're surfacing warehouse selection early. My Saturday run is to the same location every time; if I had this set it would instantly make the experience feel like mine.
- **"This Week's Featured Items" tile** — this is exactly how I think. I come in looking for the weekly rotation. Having it as a prominent entry point is smart.
- **"MAY ONLINE SAVINGS" header** — calendar-aware labeling. I know the cadence of Costco savings events and "May" tells me this is fresh, not recycled.
- **Search bar prominence** — for a medium-tech user like me, a big obvious search box is a relief. I know what I want; let me type it.
- **Clean mobile render** — no clutter, no overlapping elements, everything has breathing room.

## What's weak

- **Gift card promo as the hero banner** — "$5–$10 off select gift cards" is the first thing I see and it's not for me right now. I'm here to fill the garage with paper towels and restock the freezer, not buy a gift card.
- **"May Online Savings" thumbnails show luggage** — I can see a suitcase and what looks like outdoor patio furniture. Neither of those are in my monthly shopping list. If I didn't already know Costco carried everything, I'd think this was a travel and furniture site today.
- **No loyalty signal at all** — I have an Executive membership. I get a 2% reward check every year and it matters to me. There's zero reference to membership perks, member pricing, or my rewards status in this viewport. That's a miss.
- **"What's New" tile is vague** — megaphone icon on a red background, generic label. Doesn't tell me what's new or why I'd care.
- **No product-level content above the fold** — I have to tap twice minimum to see an actual item. For a monthly restock mindset, showing me three Kirkland items at deal prices would close me faster than anything else.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the gift card banner for a member-specific hook** — something like "Executive Members: Your May 2% Savings Add Up Here" or just a visible member-savings badge. I am the audience; remind me why I pay the fee.
- **Put one food/grocery/household item in the "May Online Savings" thumbnail strip** — even a single Kirkland paper towel or olive oil image tells me this is my site. Luggage and patio chairs in the hero slot is leaving money on the table for the buyer who came here to stock a household.
- **Add urgency copy to the savings section** — "Ends May 31" or "Online only through Sunday" would make me tap immediately instead of bookmarking and forgetting.
- **Give "What's New" a category label** — even "What's New in Grocery" or "New Kirkland Items" would pull me in. A megaphone and a vague label gets scrolled past.
