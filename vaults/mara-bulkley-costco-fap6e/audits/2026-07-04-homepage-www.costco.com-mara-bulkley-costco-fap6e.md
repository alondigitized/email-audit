# Homepage snapshot · www.costco.com · 2026-07-04
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-04**
## Executive summary

- This is exactly what I expect when I pull up Costco on my phone — no frills, no pretense, just "here's the seasonal savings, go find your stuff." It's pushing 4th of July deals front and center, which makes sense today, but the whole thing is vague enough that I'm not sure if they actually have anything worth my time or if it's just a holiday banner slapped over the usual homepage. It reads like a warehouse operator doing the bare minimum on mobile, which, honestly, is pretty on-brand.

## What's working

- **"My Warehouse — Alhambra" location block** — I'm logged in and it knows my warehouse. That's a small but real signal that this is my Costco, not a generic landing page.
- **Grocery carousel tile** — One tap to Grocery is exactly what I need when I'm mentally running through my Saturday list mid-week.
- **"This Week's Featured Items" tile** — This is the one I'll always tap. I time my .com orders around the weekly rotation.
- **4th of July hero timing** — It's July 4th today, so this is right. Seasonal accuracy matters to me; I hate when a site is still running Valentine's promos in March.

## What's weak

- **"Great Online Values" is not an offer** — I need to know if that means 20% off or $15 off or a Kirkland bundle. "Great values" tells me nothing and makes me do more work.
- **No Executive member hook above the fold** — I'm a 2% Executive member. My rewards check is real money every year. If the homepage doesn't acknowledge that when I'm logged in, it's leaving motivation on the table.
- **Warehouse shown as "Closed"** — Today is a holiday, I get it, but seeing "Closed" in red right at the top makes the whole experience feel slightly deflating when I'm trying to get into a shopping mindset.
- **No price, no product, nothing to tap in the hero** — The 4th of July hero takes up real estate but sends me nowhere specific. There's no "Shop the 4th of July Deals" button.
- **Carousel with four dots but only two tiles visible** — I can see dots for four items but only two tiles, and I have no idea what the other two are. That carousel is doing very little work for me.

## Recommendations

- **Add a concrete offer to the hero:** "Save up to $X on select items through July 4th" with a Shop Now button. "Great Online Values" is not a CTA — it's a tagline.
- **Inject a visible Executive member callout:** At my logged-in state, show "Your 2% reward balance: $X" or "Executive members save an extra 10% today" right below the location block.
- **Surface a Kirkland or grocery-forward product rail immediately below the hero:** A row of four Kirkland staples with member prices is something I'd scroll and tap without any convincing.
- **Suppress or soften "Closed" on holiday weekdays:** Either remove it or reframe as "Warehouse re-opens [date]" so it doesn't short-circuit the shopping mood before I even get to a product.
