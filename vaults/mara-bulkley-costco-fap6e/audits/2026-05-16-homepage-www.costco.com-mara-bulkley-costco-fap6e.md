# Homepage snapshot · www.costco.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- OK, so Costco.com opened to what I'd call a perfectly functional warehouse of a homepage — clean, no drama, exactly what I'd expect from the brand. Right now it's pushing Grocery and This Week's Featured Items up top, and the Member-Only Savings banner is peeking in below the carousel. No frills, but also no wow — which honestly tracks for Costco.

## What's working

- **"My Warehouse: Alhambra, Open until 7PM"** — I love this. I do not need to remember my zip code or reselect my warehouse every time. That little detail makes me feel like the app knows me.
- **Grocery carousel tile** — One tap to Grocery is exactly how I want to start a Costco session. If I'm doing a Saturday stock-up mentally in my head, that tile is the on-ramp.
- **Member-Only Savings banner** — Even half-visible, that red ribbon is instantly recognizable and it reminds me why I pay $65 a year. Good placement as a below-the-fold hook.
- **Search bar is front and center** — I often know exactly what I'm coming for (paper towels, Kirkland olive oil, the 3-lb bag of spinach). Search-first design respects that.
- **Clean render** — Nothing is broken, no placeholder text, no layout shift. For a household manager running this in between making lunches, a fast-loading clean page matters.

## What's weak

- **T-Mobile banner at the top** — I came here for rotisserie chicken and bulk toilet paper. A wireless carrier partnership is the first thing my eyes hit, and it costs Costco their own premium real estate to cross-sell someone else's product. I'm scrolling past without a glance.
- **"This Week's Featured Items" carousel tile** — The copy is vague. What's featured this week? Luggage? Lawn mowers? Patio furniture? A Costco member in warehouse-stock mode wants a clue before tapping.
- **No price signal anywhere above the fold** — Not a single dollar amount or percentage is visible in this view. I'm a 2% Executive Member who budgets around my annual reward check. Show me something that makes my membership feel worth it right now.
- **No Kirkland-specific callout** — Kirkland is why millions of us are members. It doesn't appear anywhere in this viewport. That's a missed loyalty signal.
- **No people in the imagery** — Both carousel tiles use illustrated icons and a warehouse exterior. Nothing that looks like a family or a mom with a cart. The imagery is utilitarian, not aspirational or relatable.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the T-Mobile top banner with a member savings flash** — Even "Members save $X this week on Grocery essentials" would immediately speak to me and justify the membership tap before I've done anything else.
- **Add a line of copy to the "This Week's Featured Items" tile** — Something like "New Costco.com deals on pantry, appliances & more" gives me a reason to tap instead of just floating a vague label.
- **Surface a Kirkland Signature spotlight module** — It could be as simple as a strip that says "Kirkland Signature | Members' Favorite Brands" with 3-4 product thumbnails. That alone would make me feel seen and keep me scrolling.
- **Add a "Your Recent Buys" or "Buy Again" rail** — For a household-of-five running on repeat purchases, this would be the highest-value feature on the page and I'm stunned it's not visible here.
