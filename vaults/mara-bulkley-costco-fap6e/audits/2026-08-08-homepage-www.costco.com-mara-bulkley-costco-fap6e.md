# Homepage snapshot · www.costco.com · 2026-08-08
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-08**
## Executive summary

- This still reads like Costco to me — no-nonsense, price-forward, warehouse-club energy right from the top banner. But today it's leading with an appliance financing push and a wildfire relief banner instead of anything about the actual Saturday grocery run, so as someone who's here for the rotisserie chicken and the Kirkland restock, I'm scrolling past the top third without much reason to stop.

## What's working

- **"My Warehouse: Alhambra" with Executive Member hours listed** — this is exactly the kind of thing I check before I load the kids in the car. Immediate and useful.
- **The category tile row (Featured Items, Grocery, What's New, End of Summer, Back to School)** — fast, thumb-friendly way to route myself without hunting through the nav.
- **"Buy Again" and "Lists" up top** — as someone restocking the same 15 things every month, that's a real time-saver if I ever tap it.
- **Spend-and-save tiers on appliances ($1,999→$300 off, $2,499+→$500 off)** — clear math, no guessing games, which is very on-brand for Costco.

## What's weak

- **Hero real estate going to appliances, not grocery** — I'm not in the market for a fridge every Saturday. My routine is chicken, produce, Kirkland paper goods — none of that shows up above the fold.
- **No CTA button anywhere in this view** — everything is a tile or a link. Fine for browsing, but there's nothing that says "tap here and go."
- **Wildfire relief banner placement** — worthy cause, but sandwiched between the promo carousel and the appliance deal it just reads as visual clutter, not a clear ask.
- **Zero people in any of this imagery** — just product shots of washers and fridges. Doesn't feel like it's talking to a household, it feels like a warehouse catalog page.

## Recommendations

- Swap the top hero banner rotation so grocery/fresh (rotisserie chicken, produce, Kirkland staples) gets equal or first billing alongside appliances — that's what brings me back weekly, not what brings me back twice a year.
- Add one actual "Shop Grocery" button with real button styling near the top, not just a nav link buried in the header row.
- Move the wildfire relief banner below the fold or into a dedicated community/giving section so it doesn't compete with the appliance offer for attention.
- Drop in a lifestyle shot of a family loading a cart — something that visually says "this is for households," not just "this is for appliance shoppers."
