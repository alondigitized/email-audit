# Homepage snapshot · www.costco.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- OK, so Costco's mobile site knows I exist as a member — it's already showing my Burbank warehouse and my zip code, which is more than most retailers bother to do. Right now it's pushing a restaurant gift card deal and a "May Online Savings" module that I can only partially see, plus the standard "Featured Items" and "What's New" entry tiles. Functionally solid, but nothing on this screen is screaming "stock the house for five people" at me the way I need it to.

## What's working

- **My Warehouse + Delivery Location block** — Burbank / 91201 is already set, which means I'm not starting from scratch. That's genuinely useful when I'm doing a Saturday-morning drive-by check before loading up the car.
- **"This Week's Featured Items" tile** — direct, one tap to what I actually care about, no hunting through a mega-menu.
- **"What's New" tile** — I always check this to see if Kirkland has launched anything I need to rotate into the pantry.
- **May Online Savings module** — I can see the start of it (furniture and what looks like luggage), which reminds me the .com channel exists for big-ticket items I don't want to haul from the warehouse.
- **inKind eGift card offer** — specific discount is clear, and I do buy restaurant gift cards here for date nights when I can justify the math.

## What's weak

- **Zero Executive member context** — my 2% reward balance should be the first thing I see when I'm logged in. That number drives my purchase decisions every single visit and it's buried somewhere I can't see.
- **The "May Online Savings" thumbnail strip is cut off** — I can barely see the products. For a household operator who plans purchases, I need to know what's on sale before I decide if it's worth tapping.
- **No bulk or household imagery** — the May banner appears to show patio furniture and luggage. Those aren't bad categories, but they don't put me in "monthly haul" mode the way a Kirkland paper towels feature or a diapers deal would.
- **The inKind gift card feels like house advertising, not member value** — a restaurant eGift card as the top banner feels like Costco is monetizing my attention rather than helping me stock my house.
- **No Kirkland callout anywhere** — the most loyalty-driving brand in the building is invisible on the homepage.

## Recommendations

- **Surface the 2% reward balance in the location/account bar** — right next to "My Warehouse: Burbank," show "Your Reward: $XX.XX." That one number makes me feel seen and raises my perceived urgency to spend before the cycle closes.
- **Swap or augment the top banner with a bulk-household hook** — "Stock Up: Members Save on Kirkland Essentials" with a cleaning-supplies or pantry visual would hit my actual use case. The restaurant gift card can live one scroll down.
- **Expand the May Online Savings preview** — show three or four product tiles I can actually read, not a cropped peek. If patio furniture is on sale, I want to see the price before I tap.
- **Add a "Reorder Your Staples" quick-access tile** — pull from my purchase history and drop it right between Featured Items and What's New. I buy the same 40 SKUs every month; let me do it in two taps.
