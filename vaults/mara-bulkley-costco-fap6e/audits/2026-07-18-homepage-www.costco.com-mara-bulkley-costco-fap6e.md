# Homepage snapshot · www.costco.com · 2026-07-18
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-18**
## Executive summary

- This is exactly the app I open on my phone on a Friday night before I mentally plan my Saturday run — Costco knows me. The blue urgency banner screaming "End Tomorrow" is the first thing I see, which is fine, honestly I've been trained to read those. It's pushing this week's in-warehouse savings, and the site has already pulled my Alhambra warehouse and Executive Member hours without me doing anything. Functional and familiar, but it's not going to blow anyone away visually.

## What's working

- **"My Warehouse — Alhambra" card:** It already knows where I shop, shows my Executive Member start time (9AM), and has my delivery zip saved. This is the app being smart, and I notice it.
- **Grocery tile:** Second tile in the carousel, immediately visible. Colorful spread of food products. For my cart, that's the whole point of the trip.
- **"Select Offers End Tomorrow!" red banner:** That red against white snaps my attention and, honestly, it works — I will check what's ending.
- **"Member-Only" teased at the bottom:** Starting to peek into view below the fold, which keeps me scrolling.
- **Search bar front and center:** I almost always know what I'm going for. Having search immediately accessible is correct.

## What's weak

- **"This Week's Featured Items" tile with a warehouse exterior photo:** A picture of the building's parking lot facade is not inspiring. I'm not here to look at architecture — show me what's inside worth buying.
- **No specific savings number in the top banner:** "Select In-Warehouse and Online Instant Savings" tells me exactly nothing about whether it's worth my Saturday. Thirty percent off? Five dollars? I have no idea.
- **Zero human imagery:** Not one lifestyle shot, not a family, not a kitchen. For a store I literally feed my family from, that's a miss.
- **Four carousel dots, only two tiles visible:** I don't know if I'm missing something important behind dots three and four. On mobile, that navigation is not obvious.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the warehouse exterior tile with a "This Week's Top Deals" product collage** — four to six actual items with prices crossed out and member pricing shown. That's what I'd tap.
- **Put a dollar amount in the urgency banner:** Change "Select In-Warehouse and Online Instant Savings" to something like "Save Up to $X In-Warehouse — Ends Tomorrow." Give me a reason to go in tomorrow.
- **Add an Executive Member / 2% Reward balance widget** to the My Warehouse card — even just "Your current rewards: $X." That's the one thing that would make me feel seen as a top-tier member rather than just a location lookup.
- **Surface the Kirkland Signature section or a "Pantry Staples" quick-shop tile** as a third visible carousel option — that's a real category for household-of-five shoppers and it would pull me in immediately.
