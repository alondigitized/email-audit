# Homepage snapshot · www.costco.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- This is Costco doing exactly what Costco does — lean on membership loyalty and trust that I already know why I showed up. The page is pushing Memorial Day refrigerator deals in the hero banner, the Grocery tile is sitting right there ready to go, and the Member-Only Savings section is just starting to peek in at the bottom. It's functional, it's on-calendar, but it's coasting on my existing loyalty rather than selling me on anything new.

## What's working

- **Personalized warehouse callout**: "My Warehouse: Alhambra / Open until 7PM" is the first thing I needed to know before a Saturday run — no hunting, no guessing if it's open.
- **GROCERY tile**: One tap directly into my highest-spend category; colorful food imagery makes it visually findable immediately.
- **Memorial Day timing**: Surfacing a major appliance deal over a long weekend when I'd actually have time to coordinate a delivery to the house is smart.
- **Clean, uncluttered mobile layout**: No forced modals, no pop-ups, no interstitials. I can get where I'm going without fighting the page.

## What's weak

- **Hero leads with appliances, not my core use case**: My monthly Costco mission is restocking a household of five with consumables — olive oil, paper towels, Kirkland coffee. An appliance banner doesn't confirm I'm in the right place for that.
- **"This Week's Featured Items" tile is completely dead weight**: It shows a photo of the warehouse exterior. That tells me nothing. What is actually featured? That tile is supposed to create urgency and it's just an image of a building.
- **No Executive Member acknowledgment**: I'm an Executive Member. I'm tracking my 2% reward check. There's zero indication anywhere on this page that my membership tier exists or that I'm earning anything today.
- **Zero product-level content above fold**: No price, no item name, no Kirkland label, no "jumbo" flag. The page hands me a category directory and asks me to do all the work.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the "This Week's Featured Items" warehouse photo with 3 actual product thumbnails and prices** — show me what's on this week's list (paper towels, Kirkland olive oil, whatever's on the floor promo). That tile should create a pull, not describe a building.
- **Add a small Executive Member reward chip near the "My Warehouse" row** — something like "2% Reward: $XX" or an Executive Member badge next to the personalized location would immediately acknowledge my tier and give me a reason to keep spending.
- **On non-appliance weeks, rotate the hero to bulk grocery or household consumables** — refrigerators are legitimate but they're not the message that brings me back every month; a "Stock up on Kirkland" or "Household Essentials" hero would hit harder for my profile.
