# Homepage snapshot · www.costco.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so I open Costco.com and the first thing I see is a gazebo deal — a $1,000 off Yardistry 12' x 24' gazebo. Not what I came here for, but I appreciate the hard number. The page is straightforward, very utilitarian, which honestly is very Costco. It knows what it is — warehouse shopping digitized — and it's not trying to be fancy about it.

## What's working

- **"$1,000 OFF" announcement bar** — hard dollar amount, "Starts Today!" urgency, pulls my eye immediately. I'm a numbers person; this is the right language.
- **"My Warehouse: Alhambra" + delivery ZIP** — this is genuinely useful. I know immediately whether stock-relevant items will actually be at my warehouse. Huge for Saturday planning.
- **Executive Member hours callout** — "Executive Member hours start at 9AM" is personalized service info. I'm an Executive member and I use that early-access window. Seeing it front and center makes me feel like the site knows I exist.
- **"This Week's Featured Items" + "Grocery" category tiles** — these two are the most relevant above-fold taps for someone running a household. Grocery especially.
- **"Member-Only Savings & Everyday Values" banner** — even though it's cut off, the glimpse of it confirms the membership payoff is coming. Keeps me reading.

## What's weak

- **Gazebo hero is a swing and a miss for me** — I came here to restock Kirkland olive oil and paper towels. A 12' x 24' outdoor structure has nothing to do with my immediate intent or typical session behavior.
- **No rewards balance or account personalization** — I'm logged in (presumably), but I don't see my 2% reward accumulation anywhere. That number is a massive motivator for me and it's completely absent from this view.
- **Four hero carousel dots with nothing differentiated** — there are four dot navigation indicators under the category tiles but I can only see one active slide. I have no idea what else is in that carousel; there's zero teaser to make me swipe.
- **Search bar is passive** — it just says "Search Costco." A hint like "Search 4,000+ items" or even a trending search like "Kirkland protein bars" would help me start a session faster.
- **No cart count visible** — there's a cart icon but no item count badge. When I've been building a .com order over a few days, I want to see "3 items" right there.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the gazebo hero for a pantry-stocking or family-bulk lifestyle banner** — show a real family loading up on Kirkland essentials with the "Member-Only Savings" headline front and center. That's the homepage for 80% of returning members.
- **Surface my 2% reward balance in the header** — even a small "$47.23 earned" line next to Account would immediately remind me why I'm here and push me toward the spending threshold.
- **Add a "Back in Stock" or "Your Warehouse Deals" personalized rail** — I'm giving you my warehouse and my ZIP, use it. Show me what my Alhambra location has on promotion this week.
- **Hint at carousel content** — label the dots or show partial thumbnail edges so I know slides 2–4 aren't just more big-ticket outdoor items.
