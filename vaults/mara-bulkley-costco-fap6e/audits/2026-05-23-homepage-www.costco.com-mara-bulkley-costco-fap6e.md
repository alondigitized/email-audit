# Homepage snapshot · www.costco.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- I've been a Costco member for twelve years and this is the homepage I get on my phone — functional, clean, and clearly knows I'm an Executive Member, which I appreciate. But if you didn't already know Costco, this page wouldn't tell you much: there's almost nothing here that makes me want to stop scrolling TikTok and actually shop. The Memorial Day delivery banner is timed right, and the Member-Only Savings strip at the bottom is doing real work, but between those two the page is basically empty real estate.

## What's working

- **Personalization block ("My Warehouse: Alhambra" + "Executive Member hours start at 9AM")** — I know instantly my warehouse is recognized and what time I can walk in. This saves me a search.
- **Memorial Day delivery banner** — Same-day delivery for a holiday weekend? That's me scrambling Thursday to pick up hot dogs, chips, and a case of La Croix. Relevant.
- **Grocery tile** — One tap from here to everything I actually came to restock. I need this visible immediately.
- **Member-Only Savings & Everyday Values banner** — Even partially cut off, the red ribbon tells me there's a warehouse circular live. That's my savings layer, and I want to see it.
- **Clean mobile layout** — No horizontal scroll, no squished text, no mystery gestures needed. Medium tech comfort, and this passes with zero friction.

## What's weak

- **"This Week's Featured Items" tile shows a warehouse exterior photo** — That's the building I already drive to every Saturday. Why is a photo of the parking structure my featured-item hero? Show me the Kirkland protein bars or the canned chicken, not the facade.
- **No specific offer with a number anywhere above the fold** — "Member-Only Savings" means nothing to me without a dollar sign next to it. Is it 15% off? $20 off? I need a hook to tap.
- **Zero product imagery in the main content area** — Two tiles and a banner. A household of five shops by product recognition. I should be seeing a rotisserie chicken or a Kirkland signature label, not abstract category labels.
- **No rewards balance or next-check teaser** — My 2% Executive reward is my favorite number on this site. If it showed me "Your reward is tracking at $X toward your annual check," I'd feel seen and I'd shop more.
- **Pagination dots under the tile carousel** — There appear to be three more slides I can't see. No visual hint what they contain. I'm not swiping blind.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the warehouse exterior in the Featured Items tile with an actual in-season product grid** — three to four items, photos + prices. Make it look like the first page of the warehouse circular, not a real estate listing.
- **Add the rewards balance or "X% toward your check" to the personalization block** — it's already showing my warehouse and tier, adding one more line doubles the stickiness of that section for Executive Members like me.
- **Put a concrete $ or % in the Memorial Day banner** — "Same-Day Delivery of Memorial Day Favorites — Shop now and save up to $X" converts better than a category label. Even "Free delivery over $75" gives me something to act on.
- **Show peek labels on the carousel slides** — if slides 2–4 have a deal I want (bulk paper towels, Kirkland supplements), I need a reason to swipe. Add a bottom strip showing what's on the next card.
