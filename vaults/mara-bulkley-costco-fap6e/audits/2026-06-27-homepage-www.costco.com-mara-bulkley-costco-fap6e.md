# Homepage snapshot · www.costco.com · 2026-06-27
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-27**
## Executive summary

- Costco knows I'm an Executive Member and even pulls up my Alhambra warehouse hours — that part I respect. But the first real content push is a $1,600 LG OLED TV with a soccer tagline, and that's not why I'm here on a Friday. This feels like Costco's homepage hedging toward the big-ticket electronics shopper instead of the woman who runs through here once a month to load a flatbed with Kirkland laundry pods and a 3-pack of rotisserie chickens.

## What's working

- **Executive Member personalization** — Seeing "Executive Member hours start at 9AM" right in the location block tells me Costco knows who I am. That's the kind of small detail that makes me feel like a known customer, not an anonymous visitor.
- **"GET THE MOST OUT OF YOUR MEMBERSHIP" carousel tile** — As someone who uses the 2% reward check to fund the next run, seeing a membership optimization tile front and center is genuinely useful.
- **"THIS WEEK'S FEATURED ITEMS" carousel tile** — The label "This Week's" implies fresh, rotated inventory, which is exactly how I think about my Costco trip planning.
- **Clean mobile render** — No layout chaos, no overlapping text, no broken images. Everything is where you'd expect it.
- **Announcement banner** — "Member-Only Savings and Everyday Values" is the right framing for a Costco member. It's not a fake hype message — it's the actual value prop.

## What's weak

- **LG OLED TV as the hero** — $1,599.99 is a real purchase for some people, but I'm here to plan my Saturday stocking run. Leading with a premium electronics ad on a mobile homepage means I have to scroll past something irrelevant to get to what I need.
- **No grocery, food, or household goods imagery above the fold** — Zero. Not a single Kirkland logo, no bulk produce, no food category shortcut. For the core Costco use case (food, household, pantry staples), the homepage completely ignores it.
- **Location block is text-heavy and hard to scan** — "Executive Member hours start at 9AM, Gold Star & Business hours start at 9:30AM" is useful info buried in a dense paragraph. Two hours stacked as prose on mobile is hard to skim.
- **Carousel with no visible CTA buttons** — The two carousel tiles ("MEMBERSHIP," "FEATURED ITEMS") have no clear tap affordance. I don't know if I'm supposed to tap them, swipe, or what.
- **No visible free-shipping threshold** — I order bulky items online all the time. Not showing the shipping bar above the fold is a missed conversion moment for .com shoppers like me.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the LG TV hero for a seasonal bulk value banner** — Show me what's on the flatbed this weekend: a bulk protein deal, a Kirkland promo, or a fresh food highlight. "This weekend only: $5.99 rotisserie chicken" would make me tap immediately.
- **Add a one-tap category shortcut strip below the location block** — Four icons: Groceries, Household, Electronics, Pharmacy. That strip gets me to my category in one tap instead of routing me through the "Shop" dropdown.
- **Make the membership tile CTA explicit** — Put a visible "See My Rewards" button on the "GET THE MOST OUT OF YOUR MEMBERSHIP" tile. Right now it's a tile with no action, which is wasted real estate for Executive Members like me.
- **Add free delivery threshold to the announcement bar** — Something like "Free delivery on orders over $75 for members" alongside the savings messaging sets up the .com conversion before I even start browsing.
