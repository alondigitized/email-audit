# STARTS TODAY: earn 2X points on your purchase and 5X points on ALL Sephora Collection 😍
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-01**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is Sephora's version of a points event, and it works better than their usual wall of tiles. The giant red "2X" is the first thing I see, and the "STARTS TODAY" above it is honest. I know in two seconds what's happening. After that, the email gets busy. A same-day-delivery code, a "See What Your Points Can Do" ladder, a cardmember box, six product tiles, Klarna and Afterpay, Hair Faves at 50% off, then a scroll of legal text. It's a lot for one send.
- My real issue is the math. 2X on a $405 Tom Ford fragrance is 810 points. By the email's own ladder, 500 points is $10 off. So I'm getting back about $16 on a $405 purchase. The email shows "810 points" in huge type, and I notice that the big number is more flattering than the value behind it. Neiman Marcus' tier table last week at least showed me dollars saved. Sephora shows me points and makes me do the conversion.
- Would I click? Yes, if I already had a basket in mind, because I need that code in my pocket. Would I forward it? No. And since the code is the whole mechanism, I'd want it as a real button, not a line of small text.

## What's working

- The "2X" hero and "STARTS TODAY" are unmissable. This is a clear event, not a mystery.
- The "See What Your Points Can Do" ladder (250 / 500 / 750+) shows what points are worth, which most brands skip.
- Each product tile shows price and points earned together, like "EADEM $24, 48 points." That's easy to read.
- The Cardmembers section is a real loyalty cue for someone like me who won't use a card without points back.
- The "Spot it. Shop it." and "Founders Face to Face" modules feel more editorial and less shouty than the tile wall.

## What's weak

- The conversion is buried. 810 points looks great until I realize it's about $16, and nothing says so.
- "USE CODE: SAYPOINTS" appears under every product tile. Why do I need a code at all? It reads like friction and clutter.
- The legal block at the bottom is a wall of tiny type. For an offer this simple, that much fine print makes me suspicious.
- The ending is confusing. There's a barcode and a "For use in Sephora at Kohl's stores" line, and I can't tell if I need it or if it's a leftover.
- There are too many competing offers: points, free Same-Day Delivery, 50% off Hair Faves "TODAY ONLY," and Buy Now Pay Later. The Hair Faves block competes with the main event.
- This is the fifth Sephora email in a few days. The 9/28 Living Proof send and the 9/29 "From your feed to your cart" send both ran their own "today only" urgency. The urgency is wearing thin.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the value in dollars in the hero. "2X points = about 4% back" (or whatever it really is) tells me what I'm getting before I scroll. Kohl's leads with Kohl's Cash amounts, and I know exactly what I earn.
- 2. Make "SAYPOINTS" a single clear step at the top with one button. Don't repeat "USE CODE" under every tile.
- 3. Cut the Hair Faves and Get Gifting blocks from this send. One event, one email.
- 4. Move the legal text behind a "Details" link, or at least shorten it. Macy's states its terms more plainly than this.
- 5. Clarify the barcode and the "Kohl's" line, or remove them.
- 6. Space out the sends. Four promos in five days trains me to wait for the next one.
- **Subject Alt A:** `2X points starts today, 5X on Sephora Collection`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your points event is on: Oct 1–7`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code SAYPOINTS through 10/7. Insiders only, exclusions apply.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `810 points on Tom Ford? See what your points are worth.`
