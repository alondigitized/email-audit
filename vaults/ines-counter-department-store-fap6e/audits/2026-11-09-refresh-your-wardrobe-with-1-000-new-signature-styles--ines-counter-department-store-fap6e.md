# Refresh your wardrobe with 1,000+ new signature styles
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-09**
## Executive summary

- This is the eleventh Neiman's email since Thursday, and it's the quietest one yet. It has no offer, no InCircle mention and no points. This morning's "InCircle Members: Earn 3x Points for a limited time" at least gave me a reason to put a card down. This one is a catalog page with a black banner on top. It looks lovely and tells me nothing I can act on.
- The grid itself is well edited. Brunello Cucinelli, Ferragamo and LOEWE are designers I actually shop. But there are no prices and no "new" detail beyond the headline. Nothing tells me why today is the day to click. Sephora's "ENDS TODAY" send had two big discount circles and a deadline. This one has neither. I'd scroll it once, admire the Cucinelli sweater, and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The designer mix in the women's grid (LOEWE, Ferragamo, Brunello Cucinelli, A.L.C.) reads as real designer access. It's not a Macy's-style pile of everything.
- The restrained black hero and clean white grid feel calm. Nothing shouts, which I appreciate after ten emails this week.
- Brand names sit under every product in bold. I can scan by designer, which is how I shop.
- The split into Women's and Men's with separate "Shop All" buttons is easy to follow.
- The personal style advisor link in the footer is a nice nod to my kind of customer.

## What's weak

- There are no prices anywhere and no offer. After this morning's 3x points send, why would I click without a reason?
- There is no InCircle or loyalty signal at all. I won't put a card down without points back, and the email knows nothing about that.
- The hero CTAs, "Shop Women's" and "Shop Men's", are small underlined text links on black. They're easy to miss and awkward to tap on a phone.
- Half the email is menswear: Burberry, Santoni, Paul Smith and others. That's not me, and it makes the email feel generic and mass-blasted.
- The "1,000+ new signature styles" claim from the subject isn't in the body. The hero says only "Brand New Styles Just Landed", so the number never pays off.
- It's the eleventh send in five days. Even a pretty one adds to the fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add an InCircle line under the hero, such as "Earn points on every new arrival." That would lift this email more than anything else.
- 2. Make the hero CTAs real buttons, and lead with one for women's.
- 3. Add prices, or at least "from $" on the products.
- 4. Use the 1,000+ number in the body, and say which designers are new this week.
- 5. Cut the cadence. Skip this send on a day when the 3x points email already went out.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible head of the HTML is mostly well-formed, with a table-based layout, a mobile media query and an Apple-link reset. It does have two confirmed code-level defects: an unstripped ESP template comment, and a viewport meta tag that blocks zoom. The compliance and deliverability checks are inconclusive, because the relay didn't capture the headers and the source is truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **30 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped** by the QA probe, so none of them are verified. Destination validity, redirect chains and expiry are unknown.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs:** 5649 of 6455 characters (about 87%) are URLs. This usually means long tracking URLs are being printed in the text alternative, which is a spam-filter signal and makes the text version hard to read.
- No broken links were confirmed. The truncated source shows no `<a>` tags.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom is disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile and fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang`, so screen readers can't select the correct pronunciation.
- **Weak `<title>`:** `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` has a trailing space and doesn't match the subject line. This is minor.
- **CSS checkbox hamburger menu:** the selectors `*[class].navbar-checkbox:checked~*[class].menu` and `.mobileCont:checked~table` rely on `:checked` support. Gmail and most Outlook versions don't support this, and the truncated source doesn't show whether a fallback exists. I'm treating this as a risk to verify, not a confirmed defect.
- **Exact-match class selectors:** `*[class=contenttable]`, `*[class=mobile]` and similar only match when the `class` attribute is exactly that value. They will silently fail on any element that carries a second class. This is fragile, but I can't confirm a failure from the truncated source.
- **Not verifiable:** image alt text, the preheader and the `role="presentation"` attribute on layout tables all sit in the truncated portion.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Template documentation comment shipped in the delivered HTML.** It exposes internal Salesforce Marketing Cloud data extension and field names (`NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`, `uEm`, `cmdid`, `EMAIL_ID`, `@base_refid`, `@refid`), along with a `HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256` scenario note. It should be stripped before send. It's an information-disclosure and hygiene issue, and it sits before the `<!DOCTYPE>`, which is non-standard placement.
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) were found in the visible source. The `@variable` names appear only inside that comment.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** (QA WARN). The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture it, so this is unconfirmed, not a confirmed failure.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found** (QA WARN). Same caveat. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so this needs to be checked against the raw message headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture. The From domain is `e.neimanmarcus.com`.
- **Not verifiable:** the footer unsubscribe link and the physical postal address (CAN-SPAM) are not in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- No UTM parameters or landing URLs are visible in the truncated source. The template comment suggests attribution runs through `@refid`/`@base_refid` and a hashed `@user` identifier instead of UTMs, but I can't confirm this. The 30 tracking links were not followed, so landing-page alignment is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Strip the SFMC variable comment from the production template, or move it to a server-side comment that is removed at send.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`. Optionally tighten `<title>` to match the subject.
4. Pull the raw message headers from the original send, not the relay copy. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
5. Shorten the plain-text alternative: use readable link labels and fewer or shorter URLs, so URLs are well under 70% of the text.
6. Probe the 30 tracking links with a click-tracking-aware check. Resolve the redirect chain and confirm the final landing URLs and any attribution parameters.
7. Confirm a fallback exists for the checkbox hamburger nav in Gmail and Outlook. Check for dark-mode handling and image alt text in the full, untruncated source.
