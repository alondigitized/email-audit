# Last day for up to 60% off during our Designer Sale
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-07**
## Executive summary

- Eight Neiman Marcus emails since July 2nd, and we have arrived at "Last day." I know this arc — yesterday they emailed me "up to 60% off designer styles today," and today the copy is the same offer wearing a deadline costume. That's not a new email. That's a drip-close. The thing is, I believe this is actually the last day, because I've been watching the cadence all week: 40% off, then 60% off, then the accessories pivot, now the death knell. The arc is coherent. My skepticism isn't about credibility — it's about fatigue.
- What I can see in this email is actually cleaner than I expected after days of that typographic "S/ale" block. A red hero, white type, sixty percent, a button. Then eight designer pods: Jacquemus, Chiara Boni, Stella McCartney, Polo Ralph Lauren, Veronica Beard, Valentino Garavani, Etro, Chloe. These are the brands. This is the edit. At a different point in the week this would have moved me immediately. Today I'm mostly clicking to check whether the Valentino slides I've been eyeing finally hit the 60% tier.
- I'd click. I would not forward to a friend — they've already gotten the same seven emails I have.

## What's working

- The designer brand grid is exactly right for NM — Jacquemus, Chloe, Valentino, Stella McCartney. This is the edit I come to Neiman Marcus for, and it's more focused than anything Macy's puts in a comparable sale email.
- "Last day" is doing real urgency work because the campaign arc earned it. Unlike Macy's "LAST CHANCE: confirm now" email — which was a triggered confirmation masquerading as urgency — this deadline is the natural conclusion of a week-long progression.
- The bottom CTA grid (SHOP WOMEN'S CLOTHING, SHOP WOMEN'S SHOES, SHOP HANDBAGS, SHOP DRESSES) is smart wayfinding. Let me self-select rather than guessing where to point me.
- Layout is clean. No broken images, no overlapping text, all brand names correctly populated under each product pod. No render issues I can see.

## What's weak

- Not a single InCircle mention. I have an InCircle account specifically because I refuse to spend at NM without earning. This email treats me like a stranger walking in off the street, and Saks would have found a way to layer in a loyalist hook.
- The offer hasn't moved from yesterday. Sixty percent was yesterday's number too. Without an escalation — double points, extended through midnight, an exclusive InCircle early-access moment — there's no new reason to act today versus just feeling nagged.
- No beauty. I cross-shop NM across departments, and this email forgot about La Mer, La Prairie, and the fragrance counter entirely. Sephora would never send an end-of-sale email that ignored my primary category.
- "Save on these designer styles before they're gone" is filler connector copy. It adds nothing between the hero and the product grid.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with an InCircle hook in the hero.** Even a one-liner — "InCircle members earn triple points today only" — makes this feel targeted to a known customer rather than a broadcast to the list.
- 2. **Surface prices on the product pods.** Seeing "was $1,250, now $500" under the Valentino pod is a harder sell than the brand name alone. Sephora shows me the stacked discount math; NM is making me do the work.
- 3. **Add one beauty pod.** If I buy across departments, show it. A La Mer or La Prairie card at 30% off would have made this email mine.
- 4. **Tighten the hero copy.** "THE DESIGNER SALE / UP TO 60% OFF" is informational. "Eight designers. One last day." is a campaign.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ines — designer sale ends tonight, 60% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: Valentino, Chloe + more at 60% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Jacquemus, Stella McCartney, Chiara Boni — final hours`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `InCircle members: earn points on every designer piece today`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Neiman Marcus "Last Day Designer Sale"

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The template is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud AMPscript build using XHTML 1.0 Transitional. One critical merge-token assignment is dead code (wrapped in an HTML comment), and compliance headers required for modern inbox one-click unsubscribe are absent or unverifiable.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**26 tracking links skipped by probe** — all redirect through a click-tracking domain (presumably `e.neimanmarcus.com` or a vendor subdomain). No broken destinations were confirmed, but none were verified either.

**Plain-text version is 87% URLs** — QA measured 5,148 of 5,916 plain-text characters as URLs. This ratio exceeds the 70% threshold that commonly triggers spam filters (Spamhaus, Gmail classifier). The plain-text alternate should contain readable prose with links inline, not a raw URL dump.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**No `lang` attribute on `<html>`** — `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` omits `lang="en"`. Screen readers (NVDA, VoiceOver) use this to select the correct pronunciation engine; WCAG 2.1 §3.1.1 requires it.

**Three conflicting commented-out `<meta name="viewport">` tags** — the live tag is correct, but the dead variants are evidence of copy-paste from multiple template versions:

```html
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, user-scalable=no" />-->
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=320, initial-scale=0.29">-->
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=1024">-->
```

These are inert but increase payload size and indicate template hygiene debt.

**Dual breakpoints (480px and 520px) in separate `<style>` blocks** — the main responsive block targets `max-width: 600px`; a second block (for the 3-up product grid) re-declares rules at 480px and 520px in a separate tag. This causes specificity races in Samsung Internet and older Android WebViews. The 3-up grid rules should be consolidated into the primary `<style>` block.

**`title` has trailing whitespace** — `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>`. Minor; affects tab/preview display in some clients.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**`@user` assignment is dead code — high risk.**

The template comment declares `@user` as a required variable and lists two resolution paths. The chosen path appears here:

```html
<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->
```

This is AMPscript wrapped inside an HTML comment (`<!-- -->`). **AMPscript inside an HTML comment does not execute** — the SFMC renderer strips the comment before AMPscript evaluation. If `@user` is not assigned elsewhere in the full template body (not visible in the truncated source), every link or block referencing `%%=v(@user)=%%` will render as empty or throw a runtime error, potentially breaking personalized URLs or suppressing content blocks.

**Evidence:** The comment block explicitly instructs developers to "CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW" and marks the SHA256 path as the selected one — yet the assignment is inside `<!-- -->`, not standalone AMPscript.

**All five other required variables** (`@email`, `@base_refid`, `@refid`, `@emailid`, `@uEm`, `@cmdid`) are declared only in HTML comments. Their actual assignments must exist in the non-truncated template body; confirm each is present and outside comment wrappers before deployment.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header absent** — QA found no `List-Unsubscribe` header captured via the AgentMail relay. Gmail and Apple Mail use this header to surface the one-click unsubscribe button in the UI. Without it, complaint rates rise and the sending domain is at risk of losing Gmail's "Subscribed" classification (required for bulk senders per Google's 2024 enforcement).

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent (RFC 8058)** — One-click POST unsubscribe (required for Google/Yahoo bulk sender compliance as of February 2024) is either missing or was not relayed. Both headers must be present and consistent.

**`Authentication-Results` header unknown** — SPF and DKIM pass/fail status could not be confirmed through the relay. Before attributing delivery issues, verify SPF alignment for `e.neimanmarcus.com` and that the DKIM signature's `d=` domain matches the `From:` header domain. DMARC policy for `neimanmarcus.com` should be at least `p=quarantine`.

**CAN-SPAM physical address and unsubscribe link** — not verifiable from the truncated source; confirm both are present in the footer.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**UTM parameters unverifiable** — 26 links were skipped; no destination URLs could be inspected for UTM coverage. Standard risk: if any CTA bypasses the click-tracker and links directly to `neimanmarcus.com`, it will arrive without `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, or `utm_campaign`, making attribution blind in GA4/Adobe Analytics.

**Landing page alignment** — subject line promises "up to 60% off during our Designer Sale." If any linked landing page does not prominently surface the sale or has expired pricing, bounce rate and complaint rate will spike. This should be verified at send time, especially given the "Last day" urgency framing.
