# HOURS LEFT for 30% off every brand by Kohl's 🙌
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-08**
## Executive summary

- Forty-seven. That's my count, and yes, I'm keeping it. This is the *second* Kohl's in my inbox today — the first one hit me this morning with "ENDS TODAY" and now they're back six hours later with "HOURS LEFT" as if I've somehow forgotten that the sale they've been running since Memorial Day is still wrapping up. The email is clean enough — "ENDS TODAY" reads loud and clear, the 30% off on the house brands is plainly stated — but they're leaning on APT9 and Sonoma as if those are the labels I'm breathlessly refreshing my inbox for. I cross-shop Nordstrom and Saks. I own Sonoma linen shirts. They are not the same conversation.
- The clearance block at the bottom screams desperation. Seventy percent off clearance tacked onto a same-day "HOURS LEFT" send feels like the store is rolling sale racks into the parking lot. And there's zero beauty content — not a serum, not a fragrance sampler, nothing. I spend real money at Kohl's beauty. They have no idea.
- Mark as read. I already opened the earlier one today, and it said the same thing.

## What's working

- **ENDS TODAY hierarchy is clean** — the eye lands exactly where it should, the offer reads in two seconds without hunting.
- **30% off with no coupon needed is genuinely easy** — Macy's would've made me dig for a code; Kohl's gets credit for frictionless.
- **Clearance reveal lands visually** — the red block breaks up the monotony and the 70% number is big and readable.
- **No layout breaks** — renders cleanly, no clipped images, tap targets are usable on a phone at my age.

## What's weak

- **Second email in a single day with the same offer** — Neiman Marcus ran nine sends since July 2nd and at least each one had a distinct creative angle; this is a copy-paste with the clock ticking five hours later.
- **"Styles that say designer. Prices that say otherwise."** — APT9 is a fine basic. It does not say designer. That line would embarrass itself inside a Saks email and it doesn't land here either.
- **No Kohl's Cash anywhere** — The July 5th send explicitly mentioned Kohl's Cash in the subject and it was the one thing that actually gave me a reason to open. It has vanished from this email entirely, and that's the one loyalty signal that actually moves me.
- **Zero beauty** — I'm a known beauty buyer. Not one product in this email speaks to that spend.
- **Clearance right behind the hero is a cadence miscalculation** — clearance next to an urgency-close feels like they're fire-selling everything; it undercuts the 30% offer by making the whole brand feel marked down.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Never send the same promotional urgency twice in one day** — the second email erases the credibility of the first. If the sale truly ends tonight, one send at 7am was enough. A second send at 2pm signals that nobody bought, which is the last signal you want.
- 2. **Put Kohl's Cash back in the subject or the hero** — that's the one hook that ties into how I actually shop. "Earn Kohl's Cash on everything" changes the conversion math for me completely.
- 3. **Pull beauty into the email** — even one skincare or fragrance callout acknowledges I exist as a customer. Sephora mixed editorial with product on their summer send and it still felt more cohesive than this.
- 4. **Retire "Styles that say designer"** — lean into value without the apology. "Great brands, real price drop" is more honest and I'd respect it more.
- 5. **Move clearance to a standalone send or email footer teaser** — don't dilute the 30% close with a 70% blowout in the same scroll.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off Kohl's brands + earn Kohl's Cash — ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 30% off ends at midnight — Kohl's Cash counts too`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `APT9, Sonoma, Tek Gear + beauty — no code, just savings`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stack it: 30% off now, Kohl's Cash for your next trip`

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "30% off every brand" Email

### 1. Technical Summary

Email uses a standard table-based HTML4 Transitional layout with MSO conditional comments and dual Google Fonts loading (both `<link>` and `@import`). Multiple compliance and accessibility gaps are present, primarily around alt text on tracking pixels and missing CAN-SPAM physical address.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 click-tracking links skipped** — all routed through `click.s.kohls.com` and `click.chp.kohls.com` redirect domains; liveness of destination URLs is unverified.

Three distinct tracking pixel systems fire on open:
- `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` (CoherentPath)
- `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...` (MessageGears)
- `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager)
- `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...` (Salesforce Marketing Cloud open pixel)

**Malformed HTML** at the SFMC open pixel: `</custom>` closing tag appears instead of `</div>`:
```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..."> </custom>
```
This is invalid HTML and will trigger parser error recovery in all clients.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**11 images missing `alt` text** — QA confirmed. Critically, none of the tracking pixels use `alt=""` with `aria-hidden="true"` consistently. Only `o.gif` has `aria-hidden="true"`; the others do not, meaning screen readers may attempt to announce them.

Affected pixels:
- `click.chp.kohls.com/o/d9ebf22c-...` — no `alt`, no `aria-hidden`
- `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` — no `alt`, no `aria-hidden`
- Multiple `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/...` content images — no `alt`

**Dual Google Fonts loading** is redundant and adds unnecessary DNS/render-blocking requests:
```html
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Roboto...">  <!-- in <head> -->
@import url("https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Roboto..."); <!-- in <style> -->
```
Both Roboto and Montserrat are loaded twice each.

**`<title>` tag is empty** — degrades accessibility in some email clients that surface it as a document label.

**`user-scalable=0`** in the viewport meta tag blocks pinch-to-zoom on mobile, an accessibility concern for users with low vision.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unfired/exposed merge tokens visible in the truncated source (e.g., no `{{first_name}}` literals). MessageGears subscriber ID `mi_u=604230016` and campaign ID `mi_ecmp=1016989_202678` are baked into pixel URLs, indicating recipient-level personalization is correctly applied at send time.

No issues found with token rendering based on available source.

---

### 5. Compliance

**CAN-SPAM — physical address not detected** by QA scan. HTML is truncated; address may exist below the fold in the footer, but QA did not confirm it. Needs manual verification of the full HTML footer.

**`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** — could be a relay capture gap (AgentMail), but if genuinely absent, violates Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender requirements (enforced since February 2024 for senders >5k/day).

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found** — one-click unsubscribe (required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender policy) may not be supported. This is a high-priority gap if Kohl's volume qualifies them as a bulk sender (it does).

**`robots` meta tag** is correctly set to `noindex, nofollow` and `referrer` to `no-referrer` — standard and appropriate for email.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All 22 tracked links are click-redirect wrapped; UTM parameter presence cannot be confirmed without resolving the redirects. The `mi_ecmp=1016989_202678` and `email_name=260708_DG_Email_Wed_PM` parameters on pixel URLs suggest campaign-level tagging is in place, but whether UTM params (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) are appended to final destination URLs is unverifiable from this source.

No broken or obviously mismatched destination URLs detectable from the truncated HTML.
