# Save 20% | Semi-Annual Intimates Sale = perfect fits at perfect prices.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- Okay — Kohl's, again, but this time there's an actual reason to open it: the Semi-Annual Intimates Sale is a real event, not another "ULTIMATE Clearance" reshuffle. $19.99 bras (reg. $42-$54) with a clean model shot and a "select styles" grid on the right is the most focused this brand has looked all week. That said, by the time I scroll past the bra section it collapses right back into the same navy-and-red 20%-off-plus-random-clearance formula I flagged on the 4th, 5th, and yesterday — a slushi machine, a bath towel, a mattress topper, and jeans, thrown in under "picked with you in mind" like Kohl's doesn't actually know what I'm in the market for.
- The "Only Twice a Year — Semi-Annual" framing at the top is genuinely good copy — it signals real scarcity instead of the fake urgency Kohl's usually leans on. But then it gets buried under the same 20%-off hero banner, the same "Ultimate Clearance Event 70%" block, and the same random grab-bag of home goods I've now seen three separate ways this week. Compare that to Sephora's "art of body care" send — one category, no scavenger hunt. Kohl's had one good idea here (bras) and diluted it in under a screen's worth of scrolling.
- I'd click through on the bra sale specifically — $19.99 with fit-type tabs (Wire Free, T-Shirt, Push-Up, Strapless) is exactly the kind of concrete, my-size-matters info I want. I would not forward this to a friend; it's still a Kohl's kitchen-sink email underneath the good intimates header. Mark as read, maybe tap through to bras, skip the rest.

## What's working

- "Only Twice a Year — Semi-Annual" is real, credible urgency — not the manufactured "act now" tone Kohl's usually runs.
- $19.99 (reg. $42-$54) is a specific, honest number with the regular price shown for comparison — no vague "up to" language.
- The fit-category buttons (Racerback, Wire Free, Minimizer, T-Shirt, Push-Up, Strapless) tell me exactly what I can shop by, which matters more to me at 47 than a generic "shop bras" button.
- The model diversity across the four thumbnail shots (different ages, body types, skin tones) actually reads like Kohl's tried, versus the single hero-model approach Macy's and Neiman's use.
- Kohl's Rewards status bar up top ("KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER / Earn 5% REWARDS") acknowledges me as an existing member right away — Neiman's "1,000+ styling ideas" send never did that for anyone.

## What's weak

- The "Picked with you in mind" section is a lie — a slushi machine, bath towels, a mattress topper, and jeans is not curation, it's inventory clearance with a friendly label slapped on.
- This is the same navy-circle-20%-plus-red-70%-clearance layout I called out on 8/4 and 8/5 — the intimates section is new, the scaffolding around it isn't.
- Coupon code GET20 with a barcode and PIN buried near the bottom feels like in-store friction dressed up as a perk — I have to scroll past two more sale blocks to even find it.
- "Coupons do not apply to select brands" with a vague "details" link is exactly the kind of buried fine print that makes me trust the 20% less than I would a flat, no-asterisk discount.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the "Picked with you in mind" grab-bag entirely from this send — it undercuts the one good, focused idea (intimates) with four unrelated categories. Split it into its own email or drop it.
- 2. Move the coupon code/barcode up near the bra offer instead of burying it after a second clearance banner — I shouldn't have to scroll through 70%-off towels to find the code that applies to the bras I actually want.
- 3. Lead the subject and preheader with the bra price point, not the generic 20% — "$19.99 bras" beats "Save 20%" for stopping my scroll.
- 4. Turn "select brands excluded" into a plain-language line ("excludes Nike, Under Armour") instead of a "details" link — Sephora and Neiman's don't make me click to find the catch.
