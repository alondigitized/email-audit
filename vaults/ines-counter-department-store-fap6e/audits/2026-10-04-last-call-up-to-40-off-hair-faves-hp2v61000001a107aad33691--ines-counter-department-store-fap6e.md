# LAST CALL: up to 40% off hair faves 👀
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-04**
## Executive summary

- Sephora again, and the subject says "LAST CALL" on hair. But the body is mostly about mini fragrance. The top is a small "Up to 40% off Hair Faves" block with "TODAY ONLY" in the corner. Below it is a big pink "Minis Make It Easy" hero with a Miu Miu spray, and then a grid of eight minis. The email I opened isn't the one the subject promised. Hair gets a thin strip at the top and a "Now Starring: Glossy Hair" tile near the bottom.
- This is the fifth Sephora send since 10/1. On 10/2 I got the brow email in the morning and the hair-and-brow bazaar that afternoon, and on 10/3 I got "Hot drops." Now the same SAYPOINTS "Ends 10/7" points block is back, and the same hair offer is back with "last call" on it. Neiman Marcus at least had the decency to say "last chance" about the exact same 15% beauty offer and then stay quiet and elegant. I'd read this one for the code and skip the minis. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The "TODAY ONLY" flag and "Up to 40% off" at the very top state the hair offer plainly. The urgency matches the subject.
- The P.S. line with the DELIVERED code for same-day delivery is a small, useful perk, and it appears twice.
- The 2X and 5X points graphic, with "Ends 10/7" and "Use code SAYPOINTS," is clear. It's the same clean idea as the 10/1 "2X" send I liked.
- The minis grid shows brand names and prices ($20 to $58), so I can scan it quickly. Estée Lauder and Prada are names I'd actually buy.
- The Beauty Insider block is there, with the VIB and Rouge tiers. That's the member signal I want.

## What's weak

- The hair offer is a cramped banner with four tiny product images. The "up to 40%" has no named hero product and no prices. The subject promises hair, and the body spends most of its length on minis.
- The email has two jobs, hair and minis. Macy's confirm-now email at least had one deadline. The pink Miu Miu hero is large and unrelated to "LAST CALL."
- The eight mini tiles carry "NEW · ONLY AT SEPHORA · LIMITED EDITION" labels that are small and repetitive. Several are only "$35 ($51 value)" style claims, which I can't verify.
- Three offers compete: hair 40% off, minis under $60 and 2X/5X points. Nothing tells me which one to act on first.
- The fine print and the barcode at the bottom run very long, and I can't read them on a phone without zooming. It's the fifth send in four days, so the "last call" urgency feels manufactured.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make hair the hero. Put one named product with a price, or a "40% off" tile row, above the fold. Move the minis to a separate send.
- 2. Cut the points block, or cut the hair block. Pick one deadline per email.
- 3. Cap the cadence. Two emails a day is why "LAST CALL" gets ignored.
- 4. Shorten the fine print and put a "Details" link in the footer so it isn't a wall of text.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Hair sale ends tonight: up to 40% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day: 40% off Pattern, Ellis Rose & more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus same-day delivery with code DELIVERED. Ends 10/7 PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Pattern by Tracee Ellis Ross, Vegamour and more, today only.`
