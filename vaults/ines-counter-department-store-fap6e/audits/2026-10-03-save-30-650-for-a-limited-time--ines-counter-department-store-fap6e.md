# Save $30-$650 for a limited time
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-03**
## Executive summary

- Neiman's again, and it's the same tier table I got on 10/1 ("The more you buy, the more you save"). This time it comes with a mustard hero and a code, JUST4U. It's the fourth offer from them in four days: Friends & Family on 9/30, the tier table on 10/1, 15% off beauty on 10/2, and now the tier table again. I like that it's calm and the type is serif. But I've already seen this offer, and the subject line calls it "limited time" without saying when. The deadline is only in the fine print: 10/4 at 10:00 PM CT.
- I'd probably open it out of habit and give the table a quick read. Spend $150 and save $30 is a 20% discount. That's a real number, but it doesn't beat Friends & Family. Nothing here says points or tier benefits to me, and I won't put a card down without points back. I'd click to look at the boots, but I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The hero is clean. "Up to $650 off" is the first thing I see, and the black "Shop Now" button sits right under it.
- The Spend/Save table is easy to read at a glance, and I did the math in seconds. Macy's "extra 25% off" confirmation email made me work harder than this.
- The designer lineup matches how I shop: Akris punto, Jimmy Choo, Tod's, STAUD. It reads like real access, not a clearance rack.
- The serif logo and the quiet mustard color keep the tone grown-up. Kohl's red "EXTRA" shouting would never get past me.

## What's weak

- The deadline isn't in the hero. "Limited time" with no date feels vague, and the real end date, 10/4 at 10 PM CT, is buried in the small print.
- The product grid has no prices and no sale markers. I can't tell what a $400 spend actually buys, or whether any of these items qualify.
- Loyalty is missing. Nothing mentions InCircle, points, or tier benefits. Sephora Insider puts its rewards front and center, so this feels like a stranger's email.
- The six "Shop" buttons at the bottom are a lot, and they're the same pale tone with no hierarchy. Beauty is excluded, which is my main category, and I only find that out in the fine print.
- The tier table is a repeat of 10/1, so this send feels like recycled creative.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the end date in the hero, for example "Ends Sunday 10/4, 10 PM CT," and make it more prominent than "Limited time."
- 2. Add an InCircle line, such as "Earn points on top of your savings."
- 3. Say upfront that beauty is excluded, or show one qualifying hero product with the savings worked out.
- 4. Cut the six buttons down to two or three, or tie each one to a spend tier.
- 5. Space the sends out. Don't repeat the same table within a week.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Save up to $650, ends Sunday night`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your $30–$650 savings code, through 10/4`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code JUST4U on regular-price Akris, Tod's and more.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Spend $150, save $30. Online only, ends 10/4 at 10 PM CT.`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The HTML source is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) template with a responsive hamburger-nav and a 3-product mobile reflow. The source was truncated, so findings are limited to the visible head and the QA warnings. The QA run found 0 hard failures and 4 warnings, and none of those warnings is confirmed as a defect.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- QA reports 30 tracking links skipped because they are on click-redirect domains, so none were HTTP-probed. Their destinations and status are **unverified**, not broken.
- The plain-text part is 5315/6754 characters URLs (79%). This is consistent with long tracked URLs and is a deliverability signal, not a link defect.
- I can't check the link, UTM or refid parameters because the truncated source doesn't include the body anchors.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` has a trailing space and carries no campaign context. It's minor.
- The viewport meta is `width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom in some clients, which is an accessibility concern (WCAG 1.4.4).
- The mobile CSS uses attribute selectors such as `*[class=contenttable]` and `*[class=hide]`. These are exact-match, so they fail on elements with multiple classes. Some clients also strip them.
- The hamburger nav depends on `:checked` checkbox CSS. It won't work in Gmail or Outlook, and the source doesn't show whether there's a non-CSS fallback.
- I can't check `alt` text, `lang` or `role="presentation"` from the truncated source.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- The header comment block is left in the production HTML. It exposes internal SFMC data extension and variable names: `NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`, `@uEm`, `@cmdid`, `@refid` and `@base_refid`. This reveals internal schema to anyone who views source.
- The same comment reads "CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW", and the `SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256` line is commented out. That suggests a template scenario selection was left in place. I can't confirm from the visible source which scenario is active.
- I found no unresolved merge tokens in the visible portion.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` was not found. QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly not capturing it, so this is **unconfirmed**.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was not found. It is required for one-click unsubscribe under Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules. It has the same capture caveat.
- `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain is `e.neimanmarcus.com`.
- I can't check the footer for a visible unsubscribe link or postal address, because the source is truncated before the footer.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- I can't assess UTM parameters or landing-page alignment from the available data. The 30 tracking links weren't resolved, and the source is truncated.
- `@refid` and `@base_refid` suggest campaign attribution via a refid parameter rather than UTMs. I can't confirm that.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and raw headers, not the relay-processed message. That would settle the `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` warnings.
2. Resolve the 30 redirect links with a follow-redirects check, and verify the final URLs, UTM or refid parameters and landing-page status.
3. Don't treat the missing headers as real defects until step 1 is done.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta so users can zoom.

6. Confirm that the hamburger-nav fallback works in clients without `:checked` support.
