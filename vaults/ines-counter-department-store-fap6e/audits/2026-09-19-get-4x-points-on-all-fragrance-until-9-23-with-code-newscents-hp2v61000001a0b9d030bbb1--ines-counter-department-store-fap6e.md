# Get 4X points on all fragrance until 9/23 with code NEWSCENTS
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is a marathon like the "Beauty's most wanted" send from 9/16, but at least it opens with a real headline: 4X points on fragrance for a code named NEWSCENTS, and I can actually see a PRADA green bottle up top with a proper Beauty Insider "4X" badge. That's a legible hook — I collect points, I know what tier I'm in usually, and a men's fragrance push ahead of gifting season makes sense. But then it just keeps... going. "His New Scent Lineup" for YSL, Jean Paul Gaultier, Prada, Rabanne is fine as a section, but by the time I hit Benefit, Peter Thomas Roth, Khloé Kardashian Fragrances, Olaplex, Glossier, Fresh, DAE, and Beautyblender, I've forgotten this was supposed to be a fragrance-points email.
- The "Get a Mini" Burberry GWP up top before I even see the main offer is classic Sephora clutter — I have to scroll past a whole secondary promo to get to the headline offer. Compare that to Kohl's 9/17 "40% off + Kohl's Cash" send, which led with one clean offer stack and didn't bury it under a second promo first.
- Would I click? Yes, probably on the PRADA tile specifically, since that's a scent I'd actually consider gifting my husband. Would I forward it? No — it's too sprawling to send a friend without them getting lost in the ten-product catalog dump below.

## What's working

- The 4X points badge and PRADA hero shot is a real, specific offer I can see clearly without hunting.
- "His New Scent Lineup" framing gives me a gifting angle — I know exactly who this is for (a guy in my life) and when to buy (before 9/23).
- Loyalty language ("BEAUTY INSIDER 4X POINT PER DOLLAR") is front and center, which this brand doesn't always nail — the 9/15 "0 points" email was tone-deaf by comparison.
- New/Limited Edition/Only-at-Sephora tags on products give me a reason to act now instead of waiting.

## What's weak

- The Burberry "Get a Mini" GWP banner sits above the actual fragrance-points offer, so the hierarchy is backwards — my eye lands on the wrong thing first.
- The email sprawls into a full catalog after the fragrance section — Benefit, Olaplex, Glossier, Fresh, DAE, Beautyblender have nothing to do with fragrance or the NEWSCENTS code.
- "BEAUTY FOR ALL / These picks will on be repeat" is a vague, generic banner in the middle that adds nothing — no offer, no product, just filler.
- The insider tier grid at the bottom (INSIDER / VIB / ROUGE) is a nice touch but it's stranded at the very bottom after I've already scrolled through ten unrelated products — most people won't get there.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email in half — this should be a fragrance-only send. Drop Benefit, Olaplex, Glossier, Fresh, DAE, and Beautyblender entirely; if Sephora wants to cross-sell, that's a separate email.
- 2. Move the 4X points/PRADA hero to the very top, above the Burberry GWP — the fragrance offer is the actual subject line, so it should be the first thing I see.
- 3. Cut the "Beauty For All" filler banner — it interrupts the fragrance narrative with nothing actionable.
- 4. Bring the tier grid (Insider/VIB/Rouge) up near the top offer so members immediately see how the 4X applies to their tier.
- **Subject Alt A:** `4X points on fragrance ends 9/23 — shop PRADA, YSL & more`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Beauty Insider bonus: 4X points on every fragrance`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code NEWSCENTS at checkout — men's and women's scents included`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Ends Wednesday: stack 4X points on PRADA, YSL, Rabanne and more`
