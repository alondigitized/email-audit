# Don’t miss out on an extra 25% off Last Call styles
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-01**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this one actually stops my scroll — a big red "EXTRA 25% OFF" banner with "UP TO 70% OFF" underneath and "LAST CALL" logo, that's a real number on a real sale, not the vague designer name-drop I've been getting from Neiman's the last two sends. Compare this to the 07-30 "1,000+ new designer styles" email that scored a 5 from me because there wasn't a single percentage sign in it, or the 07-31 matching-sets email that read like an editorial spread with no price anywhere — this one leads with the deal up top, no hunting required.
- Where it loses me is the merchandise grid underneath. Isabel Marant, Balmain, Bugatchi, Stuart Weitzman, Theia, Manolo Blahnik, Weekend Max Mara — that's a real spread of designer names, which I appreciate, but not one product tile has a price or shows the 25% actually applied. I'm staring at a suede hobo bag and a pink tweed jacket with zero idea if they're $200 or $2,000 after markdown. Neiman's Last Call sale is genuinely the moment I'd normally click through for — I've bought at this exact kind of event before — but the fine print at the bottom ("Interim markdowns may have been taken. No adjustments for prior purchases") is doing a lot of quiet work to protect the brand from what "up to 70% off" actually means at checkout.
- Verdict: I'd click SHOP SALE, mostly because the hero is genuinely strong and Last Call is a category I trust from this brand — but I'd go in skeptical about the real discount, and I would not forward this to a friend. It's a solid transactional email, not a "look at this" moment.

## What's working

- The red hero banner is unmissable — "EXTRA 25% OFF" in huge type, no scrolling needed to find the offer, unlike the 07-30 and 07-31 sends that buried any dollar signal entirely.
- "LAST CALL" branding front and center tells me exactly which event this is — I know that name, I've shopped it before, it reads as a known sub-brand not a generic sale.
- Nine-item merchandise grid gives me real browsing options across categories (bags, dresses, shoes, menswear) rather than one hero product I may not want.
- "SHOP SALE" button is a clean white-on-red block, easy tap target, obvious primary action.
- Six category shortcut buttons (Women's Clothing, Men's, Handbags, Shoes, Kids, Women's Dresses) at the bottom respect that I might want to jump straight to my category instead of scrolling the whole grid.

## What's weak

- Not a single price or discounted price shown on any of the nine product tiles — I have no idea if 25% off makes that Balmain jacket a steal or still four figures.
- "Exclusions Apply" sits right under the CTA button in tiny text, which is the Saks-style bait-and-tease move — flash a big number, then immediately hedge it before I even click.
- No loyalty or InCircle member callout anywhere — Neiman's has a rewards program and I'm exactly the customer who'd want to know if my points multiply during this event, and it's just absent.
- Generic "Save on these designer styles before they're gone" subhead — no real urgency date until you hit the tiny fine print at the very bottom (8/31/26), which should be near the hero if it's actually meant to create urgency.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a price (or "starting at" price) under at least the hero products in the grid — Sephora's product grids always show price, and it's the single biggest reason I click through there versus abandoning a Neiman's tile cold.
- 2. Move the end date ("Ends 8/31/26") up near the hero banner in real type size — right now it's buried in 8pt legal copy at the bottom, so there's zero credible urgency while I'm actually looking at the offer.
- 3. Add an InCircle rewards line — even a small "Plus earn double points" banner would make this feel like it's talking to me as a member instead of a stranger.
- 4. Soften or reposition "Exclusions Apply" — either move it smaller into the fine print block below, or pair it with what's actually excluded, instead of it puncturing the offer right under the CTA.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last Call: extra 25% off ends 8/31`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your extra 25% off Last Call styles is live`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Balmain, Manolo Blahnik & more — up to 70% off, plus 25% extra`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop Last Call before your favorites sell out`
