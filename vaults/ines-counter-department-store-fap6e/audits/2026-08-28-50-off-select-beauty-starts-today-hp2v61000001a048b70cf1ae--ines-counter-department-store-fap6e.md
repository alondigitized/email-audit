# 50% off select beauty starts today 💋
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-28**
## Executive summary

- "0 points" again, right up top — that's five sends now in eleven days (8/16, 8/19, 8/20, 8/24, and this one) reminding me I'm an Insider with nothing to show for it. At this point it doesn't read as a nudge to shop, it reads as a receipt of how little Sephora's loyalty math has done for me. That said, "GET 50% OFF" as the actual hero, with real prices crossed out under Clinique, Lawless, and Skinfix, is more honest than the vague "App Fest" nonsense from 8/24 — I can see exact dollars saved before I even scroll.
- Where it falls apart for me is the "Week 1 / Week 2 / Week 3" daily-deals calendar taking up half the email with zero product names or prices attached to most of the tiles — just thumbnails. That's the same overwhelming-grid problem I flagged in the 8/26 "Clean at Sephora" email, just repackaged as a calendar instead of a grid. Compare that to Neiman Marcus's tiered $250-off promo, which at least keeps the math dead simple even when it's repetitive — here I'd have to tap through three separate "weeks" to find out what's actually in them.
- I'd click through on the Clinique lipstick or the Skinfix serum since those prices are visible and reasonable. I would not forward this — it's a decent daily-deal email dragged down by bloat, and my thumb is already trained to scroll past Sephora's mid-email filler at this point.

## What's working

- The hero is unambiguous — "GET 50% OFF" with a white SHOP NOW button, no guessing what today's deal is.
- "Today Only" section shows real before/after pricing (Clinique $25→$12.50, Lawless $47→$23.50) — that's the honesty Neiman Marcus's tiered promo has, applied to single items instead.
- Afterpay's 10% off callout is a genuinely new offer, not another repeat of the same promo language.
- Clean layout breaks (divider lines, color blocks) make it easy to tell sections apart even scrolling fast on my phone.

## What's weak

- "0 points" banner at the very top — fifth time in under two weeks, and it's starting to feel like a taunt rather than a status update.
- The Week 1/2/3 grid shows nine product photos with no names or prices — I have to tap in blind, which is more friction than Macy's confirm-and-save flow even with its urgency-costume problem.
- "Deals Drop Daily" plus three weeks of teased products crammed into one email is the same overload Kohl's Friends & Family emails give me daily — too much, not enough payoff per scroll.
- Olive Young K-beauty block at the bottom feels bolted on, no connection to the 50% off hero above it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the Week 1/2/3 calendar down to one row with actual product names and prices — if I can't see what it costs, I'm not tapping.
- 2. Move "0 points" language out of the top banner or replace it with something that isn't a fifth reminder of zero progress — even a "you're X points from Rouge" would read better.
- 3. Give the Olive Young section its own send or at least a transition line tying it to "more brands, same 50% off" so it doesn't feel like a different email pasted on.
- 4. Lead with the Afterpay 10% stack since it's the freshest offer in the email — right now it's buried two sections down.

- **Subject Alt B:** `50% off + 10% with Afterpay — today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Clinique, Lawless, Skinfix — new markdowns drop daily`
