# Field notes from a new season
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-01**
## Executive summary

- Gorgeous, and completely useless to me. This is a straight-up editorial spread — horses, linen, "Field Notes," a model who's clearly 24 dressed for a countryside shoot — and there is not one price, one percentage, one word about a sale anywhere on the page. I get that Neiman's is going for aspirational, but I'm 47 and I have a Neiman Marcus InCircle account I actually use, and this email doesn't acknowledge that at all. It's a lookbook, not an invitation to buy.
- I scrolled the whole thing waiting for the catch — a "shop the sale," a member preview, anything — and it never comes. Compare that to the "Don't miss out on an extra 25% off Last Call styles" email this same brand sent me on 08-01: that one had a red banner and a real number, and I actually remember it. This one I'll forget by tomorrow. Sephora's Beauty Insider Celebration email talks to me like I'm a member with points on the table; this talks to me like I wandered into a magazine.
- Verdict: not clicking, not forwarding, marking as read and moving on.

## What's working

- The horse-and-linen visual is genuinely beautiful — Neiman's photography always is, and it's on-brand for pre-fall.
- Clean layout, no render bugs, nothing overlapping or broken.
- Nav bar up top (Women, Men, Shoes, Sale) at least gives me a way out if I want to go hunting for a deal myself.
- "Shop Countryside" and "Shop Designer Collections" links are visible and clickable, even if vague.

## What's weak

- Zero pricing, zero percentage, zero offer — this is a bigger miss than even the 07-30 "1,000+ new designer styles" send, which at least had product tiles with designer names.
- No InCircle mention at all — I'm a loyalty member and this email doesn't know it, unlike Sephora's Rouge-tier language that made me feel seen.
- The model looks mid-20s in a linen slip dress with a plunging neckline next to a horse — cute for a fashion editorial, meaningless for how I actually dress or shop.
- No urgency, no season-specific CTA beyond vague "Shop" links — feels like a brand catalog mailer, not a marketing push.
- Subject "Field notes from a new season" tells me nothing concrete before I even open it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an actual offer or InCircle perk in the hero — even "InCircle members: early access to Pre-Fall" would beat silence entirely.
- 2. Add a price point or "starting at $X" under at least one product shot so this doesn't read as a pure editorial.
- 3. Swap one lifestyle model shot for a flat-lay or on-body shot of the actual garment with size range — I can't tell what these clothes look like off a horse.
- 4. Give me a reason to act now — a preview window, a countdown, a "shop before it sells out" — anything time-bound.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- Standard defensive CSS is present and correctly implemented: `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, iOS blue-link override (`a[x-apple-data-detectors]`), Outlook table resets (`mso-table-lspace/rspace`), `.ExternalClass` line-height fixes for Outlook.com, and mobile breakpoints at 480px/520px/600px with a hamburger-nav checkbox pattern.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The HTML ships with an unstripped AMPscript developer comment block at the very top of the source, e.g.:
  `<!--REQUIRED variables: @email ... @user Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", "EMAIL_ID", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email)) ... @uEm = Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "uEm", ...) @cmdid Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "cmdid", ...) CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW -->`
  followed by `<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->`.
- This is a real code-quality/leakage issue: it exposes internal Data Extension names (`NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`) and field names (`EMAIL_ID`, `uEm`, `cmdid`) in view-source, and leaves an ambiguous, unresolved "choose one scenario" toggle for how `@user` is set (raw email vs. `HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256`) in the production payload. Recipients/anyone viewing source can see this internal CRM/DE schema.
- Recommend stripping all AMPscript authoring comments from the compiled/sent HTML at deploy time.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058, required for one-click unsubscribe) not found — same caveat applies.
- `Authentication-Results` header not found, so SPF/DKIM pass/fail status could not be verified via this relay.

- No issues found in the visible source; full assessment requires the untruncated body with actual `href` values.
