# Don’t forget: Last Call is up to 70% off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-03**
## Executive summary

- Now this is what I'm talking about. No editorial nonsense, no linen-and-horses fantasy — a big red banner, "EXTRA 25% OFF," "UP TO 70% OFF," LAST CALL logo, done. This is the same energy as the "Don't miss out on an extra 25% off Last Call styles" email I gave a 7 on yesterday, and honestly it's basically the identical creative again — which makes me wonder if Neiman's just has one Last Call template they cycle every 24 hours.
- The designer grid underneath is genuinely nice — Isabel Marant, Balmain, Manolo Blahnik, Weekend Max Max Mara — real names I recognize and would actually consider for a fall wardrobe refresh. But not one single price on any of them. I have to click through and gamble on whether "up to 70% off" applies to that Balmain jacket or if it's one of the token pieces holding the "up to" ceiling up.
- I'd click SHOP SALE, because the top banner earned it. I would not forward this to a friend — it's a sale email, not a moment. And I'm getting a little fatigued seeing this same red-box format two days running.

## What's working

- The red banner is unmissable — 25% OFF in giant type, no scrolling needed, that's the Sephora-style clarity I want.
- "LAST CALL" branding at the top tells me exactly what tier of sale this is before I even scroll.
- Designer name-drops (Isabel Marant, Balmain, Manolo Blahnik, Theia) read like actual product, not stock photography strangers.
- Six clean "SHOP" category buttons at the bottom (Women's Clothing, Handbags, Shoes, Dresses) make it easy to jump straight to my category instead of hunting.
- A real deadline — "Ends 8/31/26 at 10:00 PM CT" — sits in the fine print, so this isn't fake urgency.

## What's weak

- Zero prices on any of the nine product tiles — I don't know if that Bugatchi shirt is $40 or $140, and Macy's "confirm now" email at least gave me a clear mechanic even if it was gimmicky.
- This is nearly a repeat of yesterday's Last Call send — same red block, same "up to 70%," same "Shop Sale" button. Sephora would never run back-to-back near-identical creative on their Insider Celebration cadence.
- "Exclusions Apply" sits small and easy to miss under the button — not hostile, but I'd like it acknowledged a beat more honestly given how much fine print follows below.
- No loyalty tie-in anywhere — I've got an Neiman's InCircle-adjacent expectation from department stores at this point (Macy's Star Rewards, Sephora Rouge), and there's not a single mention of points or member pricing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a price or percentage on at least the hero product tile — even "Balmain jacket, now $XXX" under one image would answer the question the whole email raises and not answer.
- 2. Rotate the creative — if this is genuinely the same banner as yesterday's "extra 25% off" send, swap the color block or headline treatment so loyal openers don't feel like they're rereading yesterday's email.
- 3. Surface InCircle status or points-back messaging somewhere in this email — I refuse to swipe a card without points back, and Neiman's has a loyalty program, so use it.
- 4. Move "Exclusions Apply" or a one-line summary of the fine print closer to the button, not buried at the bottom in a paragraph.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 25% off Last Call — up to 70% off, ends soon`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Last Call reminder: 70% off before it's gone`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Balmain, Manolo Blahnik, Isabel Marant — up to 70% off, plus an extra 25%`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sale ends 8/31 at 10PM CT — shop before markdowns are gone`
