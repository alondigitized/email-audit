# Wait, you’re going to want in on these Cyber Week deals 😅
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- Fifteenth Sephora email in about two weeks, and this one actually has a point. The hero is four stacked purple panels, each with a giant "30% OFF" for a brand: Eilish Fragrances, Lancôme, Living Proof and Viktor&Rolf. I can read the offer from across the room. That's a lot cleaner than the 11/24 "Cyber Week is really bringing it ON 🤩" send, which made me work to find the deal.
- It's still the same Cyber Week story I've been told since the 11/21 app-exclusive send and the 11/22 "PSA" email. The 30% brand panels are new, but the 4X points banner at the top and the "Handpicked for You" grid are the same furniture. Lancôme at 30% off is the one that would make me stop. I'd click through to check it, and I would not forward it. I'd also want to see the real price before I buy anything here. Nordstrom and Saks don't send this much to get the same click.

## What's working

- The 30% OFF panels are huge and readable. Each one has its own "SAVE NOW" button, so I know exactly what I'm getting.
- Lancôme and Viktor&Rolf are brands I actually shop. That's much closer to my counter than Eilish.
- The 4X points banner at the top, with code CYBERSCENT, respects that I care about points. Macy's "confirm now" email never gave me a clear reward like that.
- "24 hours-only" gives me a deadline I can believe, since it's tied to the day.
- The barcode at the bottom for Sephora at Kohl's is useful, and I can scan it in the store.

## What's weak

- The first thing I see is a "FREE* Sample Bag" with a promo code, GRABMYBAG, and the 4X points banner. Both sit above the real offer, so the hero is competing with itself.
- Four near-identical 30% panels in a row are repetitive. It reads like a stack of coupons, not a curated edit. Neiman's does the same thing with its black box, and I'm tired of it there too.
- Fine print is everywhere: "*Exclusions/terms apply" under the points banner, the sample bag and the "in-stock merchandise only" notes. I read all of it and it shows how many conditions the deals carry.
- The bottom half is a Macy's-style pile of products, "Handpicked for You," Fragrances, Value Sets, a Holiday Gift Guide and a Credit Card banner. By the time I get there I've stopped scrolling.
- Most of the grid is skin and makeup items for a younger shopper. Nothing says it's for a woman who already has a routine, and I see no Beauty Insider tier recognition.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one hero. Put Lancôme 30% off first, and move the sample bag and points banner below it or into a slim top strip.
- 2. Cut the four panels to two, chosen by what I've bought before. Kohl's has the same stacking problem, and Sephora should do better.
- 3. Show my tier. Say "Rouge: 4X points" so it feels like it was sent to me, not to the list.
- 4. Pull the fine print into one clear line under the offer.
- 5. Slow the cadence. A fifteenth send in two weeks teaches me to ignore the sender.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off Lancôme, 24 hours only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Cyber Week 30% off: Lancôme, Viktor&Rolf`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 4X points on fragrance through 11/30, with code CYBERSCENT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Today only: four brands at 30% off, in store and online.`

## Technical Audit: "Wait, you're going to want in on these Cyber Week deals 😅"

### 1. Technical Summary
The email has two FAILs: an empty greeting slot (`Hi ,`) and no plain-text part (0 chars). The header-based checks (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) came back inconclusive because of the AgentMail relay. The HTML source was truncated, so the body links, footer and unsubscribe markup could not be inspected.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links reported.** 12 material links were probed and none were flagged. 42 were skipped as footer, utility or social links, so those are unverified.
- **Open-tracking pixel 1:** `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=4970269939971072&mi_ecmp=20261125_Cyber_Week_Daily_Brand_Deals_Day_3` is a 1x1 image with a campaign ID in `mi_ecmp`. It is working as intended.
- **Open-tracking pixel 2:** `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…` is a second pixel with no `alt` attribute. It is hidden (`display:none; max-height:0; mso-hide:all`).
- Both pixels also carry a per-recipient ID (`mi_u`, or the long path token). Anyone with the file can match it to the test inbox.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **QA false positive:** The `o.gif` alt warning is wrong. The source has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, which is the correct way to mark a decorative image.
- **Real alt issue:** The `app.sephora.com/O/…` pixel has no `alt` attribute. The impact is low because it is hidden, but `alt=""` would be cleaner.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` prevents pinch-zoom on mobile, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Fixed width:** The wrapper uses `min-width:700px; width:700px`. Clients that ignore the `@media (max-width:480px)` block will get horizontal scroll.
- **Malformed CSS:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…}<![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` block. Non-MSO parsers will probably discard the rule after it, `.mobileHeaderTierRouge`, and `img.mso-size` itself. I'm inferring this from the CSS parsing rules and haven't tested it in a client.
- **Minor:**
  - `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` is invalid. The value should be `noindex`, and the tag has no effect in email anyway.
  - `<title>Sephora</title>` is generic.
  - The JSON-LD `<script>` (`"discountCode": ""`, a bare `PromotionCard`) is empty and will be stripped by most clients.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **FAIL: empty greeting slot.** The QA check found `Hi ,`, so the first-name field rendered blank. The truncated source doesn't show the selector, so I can't point to the exact node. The seed address may have no first name on file, in which case this is a missing fallback rather than a send-wide bug. Either way the template needs a fallback (for example `Hi there,`).
- No other unresolved tokens were reported.

### 5. Compliance
- **FAIL: no plain-text part.** The text version is 0 chars. A missing `text/plain` alternative hurts deliverability and accessibility. If the relay only exposes the HTML part, this could be a capture artifact, but treat it as real until confirmed in the raw MIME.
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both are not found. The QA note says the relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**. Check the raw message headers at the sending ESP, because Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM/DMARC):** Not captured, so authentication is unknown. The From domain is `beauty.sephora.com`.
- The visible unsubscribe link and physical address are in the truncated footer. QA says an unsubscribe link was probed, but there was no result I could cite.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Not verifiable.** The truncated source shows no CTA hrefs, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment couldn't be checked.
- The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=20261125_Cyber_Week_Daily_Brand_Deals_Day_3` on the open pixel.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Add a fallback for the greeting token (`Hi there,`). This is the only confirmed content-level bug.
2. Add a `text/plain` alternative part.
3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` in the raw headers, and check SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `beauty.sephora.com`.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Move the `<!--[if mso]>` block out of the `@media` rule, so the mobile CSS isn't dropped in non-Outlook clients.
6. Add `alt=""` to the `app.sephora.com/O/…` pixel.
7. In our own QA, treat `alt=""` as valid for decorative images, since it flagged `o.gif`. Also capture the full HTML for link and UTM audits.
