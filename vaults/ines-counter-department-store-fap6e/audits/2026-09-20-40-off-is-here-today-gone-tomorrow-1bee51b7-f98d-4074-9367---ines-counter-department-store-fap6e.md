# 40% off is here today, gone tomorrow ...
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-20**
## Executive summary

- So this is the fourth Kohl's send I've logged this week, and it's the same 40% off coupon banner from yesterday's "Rewards Member Exclusive" — they've just bolted on a full home-goods-meets-fashion catalog underneath: boho jackets, kids' flannel, a Ninja slushie maker, bath towels, Gloria Vanderbilt jeans, a mattress topper. It's a warehouse flyer wearing a beauty-counter dress. I clicked into this expecting something might've changed since the 19th's near-identical Kohl's Cash stack, and nope — same coupon, same barcode, same "Ends Sept. 20" urgency, now buried under six unrelated product categories I have to scroll past.
- What actually caught my eye, briefly, was the "Beauty | Sephora" tile — that's the one category where Kohl's could talk to me directly the way Sephora Insider does in "Your fall beauty routine is coming together," which at least opened on a real woman's face and a specific ritual. Here, Beauty is one square icon of lipstick-and-lotion products wedged between Home and Shoes & Boots, no product names, no ingredients, nothing. It's an afterthought, not a pitch.
- I'm not clicking, and I'm definitely not forwarding this. It's the same coupon I've already seen twice, dressed in more merchandise photography. Mark as read and move on — this is Macy's "confirm now" energy without even the fake urgency mechanic to make it interesting.

## What's working

- The 40% off hero is unmissable — big magenta type, clear "Ends Sept. 20," a real Shop Now button right under it.
- "KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER" status bar at the very top acknowledges I'm a known customer, which Sephora and Neiman's don't always bother doing this cleanly.
- The barcode-ready coupon at the bottom means I could actually use this in-store without hunting for a code, which matters to someone like me who still shops the physical counter.
- Category navigation (Women/Men/Kids/Home) is clean and tappable, decent-sized buttons for my thumb.

## What's weak

- This is a straight repeat of the 40% off Rewards Member offer from the 19th — same discount, same deadline, just re-skinned with more merchandise, which makes the brand feel like it's stalling rather than escalating.
- Beauty gets one generic tile ("Beauty | Sephora") with no product, no name, no reason to click — compare that to Sephora Insider's actual model-led hero; Kohl's clearly doesn't see beauty as a real category for someone like me.
- The email is enormous — I counted at least eight distinct merchandising blocks (boho, kids, "just one thing," picked-for-you deals, coupons again) before the footer. Neiman's Friends & Family stays tight and on-brand; this sprawls like a circular.
- "Your 'just one thing' plan had a good run" is a cute line but it's copy filler sitting on top of yet another Women/Men/Kids block I already saw twice above the fold.
- Nothing here signals designer access or event tone — it reads like a big-box flyer, which is fine for Kohl's identity but does nothing to earn my attention over Nordstrom or Saks.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop re-running the same 40% off coupon three sends in a row — if the offer hasn't moved, change the creative angle entirely or hold the send. I'm registering fatigue, not urgency.
- 2. Cut this down to two or three merchandising blocks max. If beauty is going in, give it a real hero moment like Sephora does — a name, a product, a face — not a single tile.
- 3. Lead with something that isn't identical to the last two emails — even reordering categories or swapping the hero art would help distinguish this from the 19th's send in my inbox.
- 4. If Kohl's Cash is stacking again (it was in the 19th's version), say so in the subject/hero — don't drop that lever entirely, it was the strongest number I've seen from this brand all week.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 40% off + $10 Kohl's Cash, ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 40% off before it resets`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus new fall arrivals in Women, Home & Beauty`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Rewards members: your coupon expires at midnight`

## Technical Audit

1. **Technical Summary**
Standard ESP-generated (Coherent Path/CHEETAH platform) promotional email with heavy tracking instrumentation; automated QA flags missing unsubscribe/authentication headers and widespread missing alt text (43% pass rate).

2. **Link & Tracking Issues**
- Redirect chain uses `click.chp.kohls.com`, `click.s.kohls.com`, and `mi.kohls.com` domains; QA skipped HTTP probing on 65 tracking/redirect links (expected for click-wrapped URLs, not itself a defect).
- Multiple parallel tracking pixels present: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/e60641f3-...` (1x1), `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif` (open pixel), `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?R6XFGBTVIIIEDK3MMZVZDVAVI4.60264&d=60264&bmt=0` (second open pixel), and `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager beacon) — four separate open/tracking beacons is redundant but not broken.
- Malformed markup: `<img ... aria-hidden="true" /></custom>` — a stray closing `</custom>` tag with no matching opening tag, inside the `click.s.kohls.com` open-pixel `<div>`. This is invalid HTML and should be removed.
- CTA link uses long opaque query-string token (`?qs=ABB7InYiOjEsImQiOjUwMDV9...`) typical of ESP click-tracking; cannot verify destination from truncated source.

3. **Rendering & Accessibility**
- Nine images confirmed missing `alt` attributes per QA (tracking pixels are exempt/expected, but content images `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png` appear to be creative/promo images based on filenames under `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/`, not just beacons — these need alt text for screen-reader users).
- `<title></title>` is empty — no fallback title for webmail clients that display it in tab/preview.
- MSO conditional comments and media query fallback (`max-width: 500px`) present — standard Outlook/mobile handling, no issues found.

4. **Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No merge tokens (e.g. `%%FIRSTNAME%%`, `{{...}}`) visible in the truncated source. No issues found based on available markup.

5. **Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not found in QA scan — required by CAN-SPAM/Gmail-Yahoo bulk sender rules; may be a relay-capture artifact rather than a true absence, but flag for verification.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)**: not found — one-click unsubscribe non-compliant with 2024+ Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender requirements if genuinely absent.
- **Physical address**: not detected in scanned content — CAN-SPAM requires a physical mailing address in every commercial email; not visible in the truncated HTML (may exist in an untruncated footer, but QA did not find it).
- **Authentication-Results header**: not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status unknown; cannot confirm authentication via this scan.

6. **Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**

- CTA destination URL is opaque (click-tracked `qs=` token); cannot verify it resolves to a live, matching landing page from the truncated source.

7. **Recommendations**
- Remove stray `</custom>` closing tag in the open-pixel block.
- Add descriptive `alt` text to the seven `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` creative images.
- Populate `<title>` with a non-empty subject-line fallback.
