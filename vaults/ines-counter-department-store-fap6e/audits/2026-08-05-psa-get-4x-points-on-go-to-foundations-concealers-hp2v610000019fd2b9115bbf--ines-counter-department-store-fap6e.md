# PSA 📣 Get 4X points on go-to foundations & concealers.
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is Sephora doing the thing I actually want them to do — a straightforward "here's your multiplier, here's exactly which products qualify" email, and for once the offer is the whole email instead of buried under ten "NEW" product drops. "Get 4X points on go-to foundations & concealers" does what it says: eleven products, each with a clear points payout (96 to 232 points), each with a use-code line right under it. That's the kind of math I can do in three seconds while standing in line.
- That said, it's a wall. Eleven nearly-identical product cards stacked vertically with tiny swatch photos is a lot of scroll for a Tuesday PSA, and by product number seven I've stopped reading and I'm just skimming for a brand I already own. Compare this to Sephora's own "STARTS TODAY: Beauty Insider Celebration" from Monday, which led with my tier status and felt like it knew who I was — this one is generic "everyone gets 4X points," no Rouge/VIB/Insider tier callout at all, which is a miss given how hard Sephora usually leans on that.
- I'd click, but only to scroll-hunt for Dior or Charlotte Tilbury in my own cart rather than because the email sold me on anything new. I wouldn't forward this — it's useful, not shareworthy.

## What's working

- The "4X points, use code GETMORENOW" mechanic is dead simple and repeated consistently under every product, no scavenger hunt required — a real improvement over Macy's confirm-for-25%-off nonsense I flagged this week.
- Point values are prominent in red, right-aligned, easy to scan even skimming fast — better information hierarchy than Sephora's own "So Hot Right Now" grid from yesterday.
- Familiar names in the mix I actually shop — Dior, Charlotte Tilbury, Merit, Make Up For Ever — so there's a real chance my go-to is on this list.
- The end-of-email $5 off 250 points redemption reminder is a nice loyalty touch that rewards people who are already Beauty Insiders, not just new shoppers.
- Clean, on-brand black/cream/red palette, no render bugs, everything loaded fine.

## What's weak

- Eleven stacked product cards with near-identical layout is fatigue-inducing — I lose the thread by the middle of the scroll and this becomes list-blindness, not persuasion.
- Zero tier acknowledgment — no "as a Rouge member" or "Insider exclusive," which feels like a step backward after Monday's Celebration email that led with exactly that.
- Product photos are thumbnail-small; I can't actually judge shade range or texture, which matters more for foundation/concealer than almost any other category.
- "Free Samples in Store" banner up top is a nice line but has zero visual weight — it's the kind of value-add Nordstrom would put front and center, here it's an afterthought strip.
- The New Summer Fridays / Hot Hair Accessories mini-promos jammed in mid-scroll break the foundation/concealer focus the subject line promised — feels like they couldn't resist tacking on unrelated inventory.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the list from eleven products to a curated "top 5 bestsellers" or let me filter by shade/skin type — this is the single biggest lever, since right now it's an inventory dump, not a curation.
- 2. Add tier-specific framing at the top ("Rouge members: your 4X points start now") the way Monday's Celebration email did — Sephora clearly has this data, use it.
- 3. Enlarge the product images or add a shade-range strip so foundation shoppers can actually evaluate fit before clicking.
- 4. Move "Free Samples in Store" up into the hero banner — it's a stronger hook than the tiny strip treatment it got.
- 5. Pull the Summer Fridays/hair accessory blocks out of this send entirely — they dilute a otherwise-focused offer.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus free samples in store through 8/31 — no purchase required.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Dior, Charlotte Tilbury, Merit & more all qualify. Ends 8/5.`
