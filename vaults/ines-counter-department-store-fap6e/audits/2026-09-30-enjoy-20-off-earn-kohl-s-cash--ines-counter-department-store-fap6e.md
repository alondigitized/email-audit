# Enjoy 20% off + earn Kohl's Cash 👏
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again, and I only opened it out of habit. The header status bar says "Kohl's Rewards' Member" with "Earn 5% Rewards," so at least it knows me. Then comes a giant "EXTRA" with nothing under it. That's the same one-word hero from the 29th, when I had to scroll to find out what I was getting extra of. The 20% off is buried in a barcode block at the very bottom. The top is a green Kohl's Cash strip and four red category buttons (Women, Men, Kids, Home).
- The body is a baby and toddler "Stock-Up Sale," then Carter's and toys, then a workwear and boots section, then Halloween and Christmas decor. It ends with a "Picked with you in mind" product grid. That's five campaigns in one email. It's also the fifth Kohl's send in a week for me, after the 27th "ENDS TONIGHT" one and the 28th and 29th "Extra 20%" ones. I'm a beauty-counter woman, and there isn't a single beauty item in it. Sephora is only mentioned in the fine print about exclusions.
- Would I click? Maybe for the boots at $54.99, and only if I had a Kohl's Cash balance to burn. I wouldn't forward it. I'd skim it and archive it.

## What's working

- The header shows my Rewards status and earn rate up front. That's the one moment it treated me like a known customer.
- The Women's boots at "Starting at $54.99 + save with coupon" show a clear price and a clear "Shop Now."
- The "$10 off your purchase of $50 or more" coupon tiles are simple and easy to read.
- The barcodes and codes at the bottom mean I can redeem in store or online without hunting for anything. I like that.
- The "Elevated Everyday" workwear section is calmer and looks more grown-up than the red banner wall I saw earlier in the week.

## What's weak

- "EXTRA" with no number under it is a wasted hero. The subject promises 20% off, and I can't find it above the fold.
- The email tries to sell baby, toddler, toys, workwear, boots, decor and mattresses all at once. Nothing tells me what it's for.
- There is no beauty content anywhere. Nothing here speaks to a 47-year-old who shops the counter at Nordstrom or Macy's.
- The baby and Grandma-texting section is aimed at someone else. I'm not that shopper.
- The fine print and exclusions at the bottom are dense and tiny. The "extra 20% off" only becomes clear down there, which doesn't feel honest.
- This is the fifth send in about four days, and it repeats the same coupons and "earn Kohl's Cash" line as the 28th and 29th.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the real offer in the hero: "20% off, code YOUSAVE20, ends Oct 4." Drop the lonely "EXTRA."
- 2. Pick one lead story and cut the rest. Send the baby, workwear and decor stories as separate segmented sends.
- 3. Segment by category. If I've never bought baby items, don't lead with them for me. Show me beauty or apparel instead.
- 4. Cut the frequency. Two or three strong sends a week beat five near-duplicates.
- 5. Move the coupon and Kohl's Cash details up and put the fine print in a collapsed block.

# Technical Audit: Kohl's, "Enjoy 20% off + earn Kohl's Cash 👏"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based with a responsive `@media (max-width:500px)` block and a MIME-safe structure. It has several code-hygiene defects (invalid head markup, a stray closing tag, an empty title) and heavy pixel tracking. Most QA warnings are relay artifacts rather than real defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque links:** 71 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe. The visible link is `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABB7InYi...`, with an encrypted `qs` blob, so destinations can't be verified from source.
- **Four open or analytics pixels:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/bd0e2366-...`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`

  Redundant open tracking has no functional impact, but the demdex call is a third-party data-sync request.
- **Stray closing tag:** `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." ...></custom>` closes an element that was never opened. It is invalid HTML and some parsers may mishandle it.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on content images:** QA flagged `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` images, including `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png` and `916d6fa08d185233.png`. These are content or promo images, so images-off clients and screen readers get no content.
- **QA false positive:** `o.gif` is flagged but already has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, which is valid for a decorative pixel. The `click.chp.kohls.com` and `demdex` pixels genuinely lack `alt`, but they are hidden 1x1 or 0x0 images.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` gives no accessible document title.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. Browsers will move it into `<body>`, and the schema microdata has no effect in email clients.
- **Empty icon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href.
- **Broken CSS selector:** `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` is missing its leading dot, so it targets a nonexistent element.
- **Missing table roles:** the 1x1 tracking table and the AAM table lack `role="presentation"`, though the layout tables have it.
- **Web fonts:** Google Fonts are loaded via `<link>` and `@import`, inside the `<!--[if !mso]>` guard. The stack falls back to `Montserrat, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`, so this is acceptable.
- **Legacy doctype:** HTML 4.01 Transitional is used with an `xmlns:v` VML namespace, which is fine for Outlook.
