# Save up to 50% on select lip must-haves ⭐
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-26**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction: this is a bait-and-switch dressed up as a lip sale. The subject promises "up to 50% off select lip must-haves," but the second I open it, the hero banner says 30% off Charlotte Tilbury — not 50%, and not exclusively lip. Then the whole rest of the email is a "New Arrivals" grab bag: a cushion foundation, a blush stick, an eyeliner, a contour stick, a highlighter — maybe three of the nine featured products are actually lip products. Sephora, we've been here before with your 7/25 "This is what your hair wants" email that turned out to be 50% off Makeup Forever for National Lip Day. You're recycling the exact same subject/hero mismatch trick, just flipped.
- What I do like: the top banner is clean, the offer is legible, "Today only!" creates real (if modest) urgency, and the Beauty Insider points bar up top ("you have 0 points") at least acknowledges I'm a member — something Neiman Marcus and Kohl's rarely bother with in their generic spend-tier blasts. But visually this reads more like a "look what's new" catalog scroll than a targeted lip sale, and at eight-ish scrolls deep with a dozen SKUs, it's Macy's-level overload dressed in Sephora's cleaner aesthetic.
- Would I click? Maybe — the Charlotte Tilbury 30% off is a real, recognizable name and I do restock lip product there. But I'd click past the subject's promise, not because of it. Forward to a friend? No. Mark as read and move on, same as I did with three of your last four sends this week.

## What's working

- The top banner is the first thing my eye hits — pink block, bold "30% off," clear "SHOP NOW" button. No hunting required.
- "Clean at Sephora" badges on several products (Sarah Creal eyeliner, Fara Homidi compact) are a trust cue I actually register — I care about that certification more than most shoppers.
- Beauty Insider tier bar at the bottom (INSIDER / VIB / ROUGE) is a nice touch that Neiman Marcus and Kohl's just don't offer — it reminds me my status has weight here.
- Prices are shown right under every product ($49, $35, $38...) — no click-to-reveal games, unlike Macy's "confirm now" nonsense I flagged last week.

## What's weak

- The subject says "50% on select lip must-haves," the actual banner says 30% off Charlotte Tilbury — that's not a rounding error, that's two different numbers and two different scopes. Feels like a bait-and-switch, not a translation issue.
- Beyond the hero, this isn't a lip email at all — it's foundation, blush, eyeliner, contour, then a lip balm or two buried mid-scroll. If I clicked in chasing "50% off lip," I'd be annoyed within three scrolls.
- No models, no faces, nothing that signals this collection is for someone my age vs. a 22-year-old TikTok shopper — just product flat-lays. Fine for efficiency, but it doesn't say "we know who you are."
- Zero social proof — no star ratings, no "customer favorite" tags anywhere in the whole scroll, which is unusual for Sephora and makes we-know-best product with no reviews visible feel like a gamble.
- This is at least my fourth Sephora email in five days (following the Juice Bar, the sunscreen all-caps one, and the hair/lip mismatch on 7/25) — the fatigue is real and this one didn't earn its spot by being different enough.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the subject/hero mismatch first** — either make the hero genuinely 50% off lip products, or change the subject to match the actual 30% off Charlotte Tilbury offer. This is the single biggest trust-eroder in the email.
- 2. **Cut the SKU count** — nine "New" products in one send is Macy's-style overload. Pick three hero items max and let them breathe.
- 3. **Add one social proof element** — a star rating or "customer favorite" badge on at least the hero product would do more for click-through than another "Clean" badge.
- 4. **If it's really a lip sale, show only lip products** — the current mix (foundation, blush, contour, eyeliner) dilutes the promise and confuses the shopper who opened specifically for lip.
- 5. **Space this out from the rest of the week's cadence** — four sends in five days is starting to numb me to all of them, this one included.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus new arrivals in foundation, blush & liner — all Clean at Sephora certified.`
