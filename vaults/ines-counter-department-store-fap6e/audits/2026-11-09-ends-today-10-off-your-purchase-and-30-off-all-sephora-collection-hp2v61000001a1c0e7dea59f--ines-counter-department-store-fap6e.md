# ENDS TODAY 🚨 10% off your purchase and 30% off ALL Sephora Collection
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-09**
## Executive summary

- Sephora again, and today is the last day. I opened it because the subject has a clear deadline. The top of the email delivers on it: "Insider, Last Chance to Save on Everything," with two big circles, 10% off and 30% off Sephora Collection, both "Ends 11/9." That's the cleanest hero Sephora has sent me this week. On 11/8 the countdown clock and the SPEEDY shipping code crowded the offer, and this one doesn't.
- Below the hero, it falls apart the way these always do. I count at least three different codes: ALLONSALE online, SCSAVE in store, and SPEEDY for same-day delivery. Then come a 500-point challenge, a credit card pitch, a "Beauty on Demand" strip, a product grid, a gift-with-purchase block, a Kohl's store finder, holiday gift categories and a wall of fine print. Neiman Marcus's InCircle triple-points email gave me one idea and let me act on it. This one gives me about nine.
- Would I click? Yes, but only because of the deadline and the Insider 10%. I'd skip the product grid. I wouldn't forward it. It's a transaction, not a discovery.

## What's working

- The hero is two big numbers with "Ends 11/9" under each. I understood it in two seconds.
- "Ends today" in the subject matches "Ends today" in the email. Kohl's "STARTS TODAY" subject against its "Ends Nov. 21" header was the opposite.
- The "Shop Now" button sits right under the codes, so I don't have to hunt for it.
- The Insider tier framing and the 500-point challenge show me they know I'm a member. Sephora knows who I am, which Macy's "confirm now" email didn't manage.
- The prices are readable, with strikethrough and sale price (EADEM, Dr. Jart+, Summer Fridays). Brands I'd actually buy.

## What's weak

- Three codes in the first screen (ALLONSALE, SCSAVE, SPEEDY) feels like homework. The 11/8 email had the same clutter.
- The Kohl's block with store hours is long and low in the email. Ten-plus Sephora emails this week, and I still get a store locator I don't need.
- The Sephora Collection 30% is the new news, but the product grid below doesn't show any Sephora Collection items. I can't see what I'd save 30% on.
- The credit card offer, challenge, gift-with-purchase and holiday gift guide all compete. The gift with $100 spend, using code UNBEAUTY or similar, is buried halfway down.
- The fine print runs several screens and ends in barcodes. It's honest, but it's heavy for a "last chance" email.
- The hero shows no people. I can't tell who this is for, and nothing says it's aimed at me over a 25-year-old.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email by half. Hero, one code, a Sephora Collection product row, then the footer. Move the Kohl's block and the credit card pitch to a different send.
- 2. Put one code per channel in the hero and drop SPEEDY from it entirely. Shipping belongs in a thin banner.
- 3. Show Sephora Collection products in the grid, since that's the new 30% news. Add a bestseller with a review count.
- 4. Move the gift-with-purchase up, because it's a spend lever I'd act on.
- 5. Show the end time ("midnight PT") next to "Ends today."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last day: 10% off + 30% off Sephora Collection`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tonight at midnight: your Insider 10% ends`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `One code online, one in store. Stack with Sephora Collection 30% off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sale ends 11/9 at 11:59 PT. Your Insider points still count.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed personalization failure (empty greeting) and no plain-text part. Several head-level markup problems also affect rendering and accessibility. The HTML source is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and link hrefs could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage:** 12 of 53 links were probed and 41 skipped. No broken links were reported, but coverage is about 23%.
- **Tracking pixels:** There are two.
  - `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=4970269939971072&mi_ecmp=20261109_HSE_Insider_Ends_Today` is 1x1 and has `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"`. This is valid decorative markup, so the QA "missing alt" warning for `o.gif` is a **false positive**.
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…` is hidden with `display:none; max-height:0; font-size:0; mso-hide:all`. It has no `alt` and no width/height. Because it is hidden and decorative, this is low severity, but it can be given `alt=""`.
- **Per-recipient ID:** The `mi_u` parameter in the pixel URL carries a per-recipient identifier. This is normal for open tracking, but it means the pixel URL is recipient-identifying if forwarded.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Fixed width:** The outer table has `style="min-width:700px; width:700px"`. The `@media (max-width:480px)` block does not override it, so a 700px minimum on mobile forces horizontal scroll or client-side scaling.
- **Malformed CSS:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…} <![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` rule in the `<style>` block. Conditional comments are not valid CSS, and some parsers may drop the rules that follow it (`.mobileHeaderTierRouge`, `.mobileIcon`, `.mobileDisc` and others).
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Invalid meta value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` is not a valid directive. It should be `noindex`.
- **Missing language and table roles:** `<html>` has no `lang` attribute, and the layout tables have no `role="presentation"`. Both affect screen readers.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` is not descriptive.
- **Structured data:** The JSON-LD block has `"discountCode": ""` and a bare `PromotionCard` type, so it carries no usable data. Most clients ignore or strip `<script>`, so it adds weight for no benefit.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** The greeting renders as "Hi ,". The first-name merge field resolved to empty with no fallback. I could not locate it in the truncated source, so this relies on the QA check.
- **Unresolved tokens:** None were visible in the available source.

## 5. Compliance
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is empty (0 chars):** This is a deliverability and accessibility issue, since multipart/alternative with no text body is a spam signal.
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** Both were not found. QA notes that AgentMail may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed** and not a verified sender defect. Verify against the raw message before treating it as a bug. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require RFC 8058 one-click.
- **Authentication-Results:** It was not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. Sender `shop@beauty.sephora.com` could not be validated.
- **Unsubscribe link and postal address:** Neither was in the truncated source. QA says an unsubscribe link was probed, but no result was reported. I can't confirm either way.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Link hrefs, UTM parameters and landing pages were not visible in the truncated source. The only campaign identifier seen is `mi_ecmp=20261109_HSE_Insider_Ends_Today` on the pixel. I can't assess UTM or landing-page alignment without the full href list.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a fallback to the first-name merge field (for example "Hi there,") or suppress the greeting when the name is empty.
2. Include a text/plain part with the offer, key links and unsubscribe URL.
3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` in the raw headers. Also confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `beauty.sephora.com`.
4. Make the layout responsive: use `width:100%; max-width:700px`, and remove `min-width:700px` from the mobile path.
5. Move the MSO conditional out of the `@media` block and fix `content="noindex"`.
6. Remove `maximum-scale=1`, and add `lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` on layout tables and a descriptive `<title>`.
7. Drop the empty JSON-LD block, or populate it with real offer data.
8. Add `alt=""` to the hidden `app.sephora.com` pixel. Disregard the `o.gif` alt warning.
9. Rerun the audit with the full HTML and a raw `.eml` to cover link probing, UTMs and the footer.
