# Friendly reminder: 25% off + 20% off jewelry
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-20**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the "friendly reminder" version of the Friends & Family email I've already gotten, and it's telling on the brand a bit — same forest green hero, same "LIMITED TIME" stacked type, just now doing the nudge-nudge routine because I didn't shop it the first time. The merchandising grid is still doing real work for me though: Dolce&Gabbana bag, Roger Vivier crystal clutch, Gianvito Rossi in that orange patent — that's a curated edit, not a dump, and it reads like someone who knows my closet actually picked it.
- Where it loses me a little is that fine-print notice up top — beauty and fragrance excluded from the 25% — buried in gray text under the discount block instead of stated where I'd see it before clicking. I check for that every single Friends & Family because Neiman's has burned me on it before, and here they make me hunt.
- I'd click through to browse the jewelry — 20% off jewelry paired with Double Points for InCircle is genuinely the combo that gets me moving — but I wouldn't forward this one. It's a reminder email, not news; nothing here is more exciting than the CHANEL fine jewelry send from the 18th, which is still the best thing they've sent me this month.

## What's working

- The green-on-cream Friends & Family banner is instantly recognizable as Neiman's own event, not a generic sale reskin — unlike the Kohl's 40%-off banner I keep seeing recycled.
- 25% off + 20% off jewelry is one clean stacked offer, no confusing tiers.
- InCircle Double Points calls out my loyalty tier by name — I'm a member, and it's front and center, not buried.
- The product grid (Theia gown, FRAME denim, Dolce&Gabbana, Gianvito Rossi) is genuinely aspirational and matches my taste — not the Macy's kitchen-sink approach.
- Clean single "SHOP THE EVENT" CTA button — no ambiguity about what to click first.

## What's weak

- "Exclusions Apply" sits right under the CTA in tiny type with no specifics — I have to scroll to the legal block to learn beauty/fragrance is excluded, which feels like the Saks-style fine-print dodge I don't trust.
- This is a repeat send of the same Friends & Family creative I've likely already seen this week — the "friendly reminder" framing doesn't disguise that it's the identical hero and grid.
- No urgency countdown visible above the fold — the "Ends 9/27/26" deadline is only in the legal footer, not near the offer where it'd actually move me to act today.
- The category buttons below (Shop Women's, Shop Handbags, etc.) are a bit much — six green blocks in a row starts to feel like the Kohl's-style link wall rather than Neiman's usual restraint.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the actual exclusion ("beauty & fragrance not included") right next to "Exclusions Apply" in the hero, not three screens down — I want to know before I click, not after I've picked a product.
- 2. Add the countdown ("Ends Sunday 10PM CT") directly under the CTA button — that's the one thing missing that would make this reminder feel urgent instead of routine.
- 3. Since this is a reminder send, differentiate it visually from the original — even a small "Last chance" ribbon so it doesn't read as a duplicate of the first Friends & Family email.
- 4. Trim the six category buttons to three — Women's, Handbags, Jewelry — since jewelry is the actual incremental offer here and it's not even called out as its own button.
