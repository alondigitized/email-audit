# Get an extra 20% off + earn Kohl's Cash 🤩
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again. I opened it out of habit, and I got the same hollow red "EXTRA" header I've now seen on 9/29, 9/30 and 10/1. The subject says "extra 20% off," but nothing under that word says 20%. The 20% only shows up near the bottom, as "Take an extra 20% off. Code: YDUSAVE20." I had to scroll through a whole toy catalog to find the offer I was promised.
- What I see instead is a "Top Toys" Grinch hero with $10 off $50 in toys, a Lego block that says "ARRIVING September 21" (it's October 2), a cider-doughnut home section, and Nike fleece. It's a flea market. The header does say "Kohl's Rewards' Member" and "Earn 5% Rewards," so it knows me, but then it shows me toys. I'm 47 and shop the beauty counter. I'm not buying a Grinch plush in the first week of October.
- Would I click? Probably not. Would I forward it? No. This is the fourth near-identical send in a week, so I'm marking it read.

## What's working

- The status bar at the top says "Kohl's Rewards' Member" with "Earn 5% Rewards." That's the one moment it treated me like a known customer.
- The green "Kohl's Cash" strip says "Plus, earn Kohl's Cash!" Points back is what I buy for.
- The coupon block at the bottom is clear. Each coupon has a code, a barcode, an end date (Oct. 4) and a "details" link. That's honest.
- The "Picked with you in mind" product row shows real prices ($29.99 jeans, $349.99 Ninja SLUSHI). I can see what things cost.
- The "View coupons in Wallet" button is easy to find and a decent size to tap.

## What's weak

- The "EXTRA" hero has no number under it. It's one red word with nothing to back it up, and it's the same header as the last three sends.
- The real 20% offer is buried near the bottom. Sephora's giant "2X" and "STARTS TODAY" told me the deal in two seconds. Kohl's makes me hunt for it.
- The Lego banner says "ARRIVING September 21." That date has already passed, so it reads stale and sloppy.
- There's no beauty anywhere. The category tiles are Women, Men, Kids and Home. Sephora is Kohl's own beauty partner, but it's just a footnote about "Sephora purchases" in the fine print.
- Six or seven competing modules mean nothing leads. The Nike fleece and the Grinch toys fight each other for attention.
- The coupon exclusions are heavy ("Coupons do not apply to select brands"), so the "extra 20%" feels like it has strings.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the actual offer in the hero: "Extra 20% off" with the code and the end date (Oct. 4), above everything else. Neiman Marcus put its tiered table at the top and I did the math right there. Kohl's makes me do a scavenger hunt.
- 2. Cut the toy-heavy middle. Segment by what I buy. If I'm a beauty and apparel shopper, show me that and drop the Lego block.
- 3. Fix the stale Lego date and stop sending creative that's already expired.
- 4. Space out the sends. Four "extra 20%" emails in a week tells me the discount is permanent, so I don't feel any urgency.
- 5. Show the Kohl's Cash earn rate as a number in the hero: "$10 Kohl's Cash for every $50."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source has several markup defects (a stray `</custom>` tag, a `<div>` inside `<head>`, an empty `<title>`) and a viewport meta that blocks zoom. QA flagged 13 images with missing alt text. The compliance and authentication WARNs come from header capture through the AgentMail relay, so they are unconfirmed. The source is truncated, so link destinations, UTMs and the footer could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverifiable links:** QA skipped 76 tracking links (HTTP probe skipped on tracking/redirect domains). Every visible CTA is wrapped in `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABB7InYi…`, which is an opaque token. Final destinations, redirect health and broken links cannot be confirmed.
- **Four 1×1 tracking pixels before the main content:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/a53e583a-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1023029_2026102`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?ETSHDPZGGZ6EZHI22LL7XS6E5I.60269&d=60269&bmt=0`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (width/height 0)
- **Redundant opens tracking:** Three separate open pixels from three vendors are present (the demdex pixel is an audience-manager event). This adds requests and a privacy surface.
- **Malformed pixel markup:** `<img src=".../open.aspx?…" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>` has a stray `</custom>` closing tag with no opener. Parsers ignore it, but it is invalid HTML.
- **Per-recipient ID in image URLs:** `mi_u=604230016` appears on every `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` image. These URLs can be proxied, cached and logged by third parties. This is a privacy concern, not a functional break.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (13 images flagged by QA):**
  - `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`
  - `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`
  - `58125190_2_2x`, `58125190_23_2x`, `58125190_25_2x`
  - The first pixel `a53e583a-…` has no alt attribute at all.
  - The `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` and `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/58125190_*` images look like content (rendered or product imagery). Screen readers will announce filenames or URLs.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` disables pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Empty `<title></title>`:** No document title.
- **Invalid head content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">…</div>` sits inside `<head>`. Browsers and webmail parsers end the head at that point, so the closing `</head>` is orphaned. Schema.org microdata is also not used by most email clients.
- **Empty icon href:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` resolves to the document itself and has no purpose in email.
- **Redundant font loading:** Both `<link href="…fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Roboto…">` and `@import url(…)` load the same fonts. Roboto is declared but the body uses Montserrat. This is a wasted request. It is wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, and the Arial/Helvetica fallback exists.
- **Missing `role="presentation"`:** The pixel table (`width="1" height="1"`), the AAM table and the preheader table (`width='640'`) lack it, while the main layout tables have it.
- **Stray CSS:** `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` has no `.` or `#` selector, so it matches nothing.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FirstName]`) are visible in the supplied source. Personalization appears to be server-rendered through `mi_u` and `mi_ecmp` image parameters. The footer and offer sections were truncated and not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **No physical address detected.** The source is truncated before the footer, so this cannot be confirmed from the data. A body-level unsubscribe link also could not be verified.
- **`Authentication-Results` not captured.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The envelope is `From: "Kohl’s" <Kohls@s.kohls.com>`, and the `s.kohls.com` subdomain alignment cannot be validated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No UTM parameters are visible. Click URLs are opaque `?qs=` tokens, and QA skipped probing all 76.
- Campaign identity appears only on image URLs (`email_name=261002_DG_Email_Friday`, `mi_ecmp=1023029_2026102`), so site-side attribution depends on redirect-time parameters that cannot be checked here.
- The landing page match for the "extra 20% off + Kohl's Cash" subject cannot be assessed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 10 content images (the `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` and `58125190_*` assets). Add `alt=""` to the three tracking pixels.
2. Fix `<meta name="viewport">` by removing `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0`.
3. Move the schema.org `<div>` out of `<head>` or remove it, remove the stray `</custom>`, drop `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`, and give `<title>` a value.
4. Remove the duplicate font loading (keep either the `<link>` or the `@import`) and drop unused Roboto.
5. Consolidate the open pixels to reduce vendor requests.
6. Re-run QA against the raw message, not the relay copy, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) and the footer address and unsubscribe link.
7. Resolve a sample of the `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=` links in a headless session to confirm final URLs, UTMs and landing-page status.
