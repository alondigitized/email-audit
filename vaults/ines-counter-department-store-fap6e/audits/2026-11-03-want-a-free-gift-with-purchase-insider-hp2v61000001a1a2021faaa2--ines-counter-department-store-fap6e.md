# Want a FREE gift with purchase, Insider? 😍
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-03**
## Executive summary

- This is the twenty-third Sephora email I've had in about a week. The subject promised a free gift with purchase, but the top of the email is a big "Insider, everything is on sale!" banner with two percentage badges: 10% for Insiders and 30% off Sephora Collection. The free gift is below that, in a red "Stack Your Savings" block, and it's a headband and washcloth set with a spend threshold and a code. I had to scroll to find the thing I was told about. That's the same subject-and-body mismatch I flagged on the 11/02 "10% off EVERYTHING" send.
- It's well built, though. The dates are clear and the codes are right there (ALLONSALE, SPEEDY). The 500-point challenge and the credit card perk are real loyalty content. Sephora also treats me like a member by calling me Insider. Kohl's, by contrast, gave me a sleigh and a "Shop Now" button and left the 20% off for me to hunt down.
- Would I click? Probably, to check the free gift and the points challenge. I wouldn't forward it. After this many sends in a week the sale feels like a permanent condition, not an event.

## What's working

- I like that the dates sit right under each badge (11/3–11/9 and 10/30–11/9). I can plan my trip around them.
- Codes are printed in the open. Macy's makes me "confirm" before I get my 25%, and Sephora doesn't.
- Calling me "Insider" and showing the points challenge and the card benefits tells me they know I'm in the program.
- The "Beauty on Demand" strip (pickup, curbside, same-day delivery, free shipping) is useful and sits where I'd look for it.
- The store locator for Sephora at Kohl's, with hours, is handy for how I actually shop. I'm in a store on weekends.

## What's weak

- The free gift is in the subject, but the hero sells percentages. The gift is buried in a red block, so the open and the body don't match.
- Two different percentages and two date ranges in one banner make me do math. The "Use as many times as you want" line is tiny and easy to miss.
- The fine print under the offers is small. I can see the asterisked exclusions and I can't read them without zooming.
- The product grid is long and generic. It has no hero product and no ratings or reviews.
- The email is very long, with a store finder, a gift guide, barcodes and a wall of legal text. It feels like Macy's-style overload on a phone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the free gift the hero, with the product image, the spend threshold and the code, so the subject and the first screen say the same thing.
- 2. Pick one offer for the top banner. Move the Sephora Collection event to a smaller secondary block.
- 3. Shorten the email. Keep the offers, the loyalty section and one product row. Move the store locator and the legal text to a link.
- 4. Label the buttons by what they do, like "Get my free headband" instead of "Shop Now" or "Learn More."
- 5. Space out the sends. Twenty-three in a week trains me to wait for the next deal.
