# Save an extra 20% 🤗  Time to fill your cart 🛒
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-10**
## Executive summary

- Forty-eight. That's my Kohl's count since Memorial Day, and today they come in with *another* 20% off — the same number they've been running since yesterday, and a step down from the 30% they were shouting at me on Tuesday. I've now seen five Kohl's emails in three days, and the offer went backwards. That's not a cadence problem, that's a trust problem.
- But the thing that actually got me was the hero. A man in a plaid short-sleeve. I'm 47, I know every Clinique bonus date at the Macy's beauty counter, and Kohl's opened this send with a male model and a home-decor section featuring outdoor toys. The "Shop Women's" link exists, but it's so small it reads like an afterthought below the shoe callout. Kohl's Cash always keeps me in the tent — I won't spend anywhere without points back — but the Kohl's Cash mention here is buried in the footer, not leading the conversation.
- I'll probably glance at the shoes on my way to delete. I won't click through, I won't forward this to anyone, and I'll feel slightly less warmly toward Kohl's than I did this morning. Compare that to Neiman Marcus's new-arrivals send from two days ago — no countdown, no panic, clean editorial hierarchy, a quiet confidence that felt like they knew exactly who I was. Kohl's is shouting into a crowd; Neiman's was speaking to a subscriber.

## What's working

- **Offer is concrete and unambiguous.** 20% off, code KLSBE720, expires July 16 at 9pm — no guessing, no fine print you need a magnifying glass for. That's the right move.
- **Wallet coupon with barcode is genuinely frictionless.** I can flash that in-store without digging for a paper coupon. That's a real convenience signal that Sephora still hasn't solved as cleanly for in-store.
- **Rewards member status acknowledged in the header.** "KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER | Earn $6 REWARDS" tells me I'm a known customer, not a cold lead. Small thing, but it lands.
- **Kohl's Cash earn rate is visible.** "$10 for every $50 spent" in the footer speaks directly to my points-or-nothing rule — it just needed to be higher up.
- **No visual render bugs.** Layout holds, images load, buttons are tappable. Clean execution.

## What's weak

- **Wrong hero for me.** Male model, men's plaid shirt, men's summer essentials — this email opened talking to someone else. The "Shop Women's" link reads as a correction, not a welcome.
- **No beauty content whatsoever.** I'm a counter regular. Kohl's carries beauty. Not even a teaser row. Macy's figured out how to segment their beauty subscribers; Kohl's is carpet-bombing me with home goods.
- **Offer went backwards.** The last two sends were 30%. Now it's 20%. That pattern actively teaches me to wait for the better deal, not act on this one.
- **"Fill your cart" is too casual for how I shop.** I buy deliberately. I'm not a cart-stuffer. The subject line's 🤗 emoji reads like Kohl's trying to be my friend when what I actually want is to feel like a valued customer.
- **Home goods section is a miss for me personally.** Bedding, patio, outdoor toys — nothing here connects to why I open Kohl's emails.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Segment beauty subscribers into their own send.** Even a single beauty tile above the home-goods section would have changed my read of this email. Sephora's summer editorial (6/10 last week) felt more relevant to me *from a competitor* than this did from a brand I actively shop.
- 2. **Lead with Kohl's Cash earlier.** Move the earn-per-$50 callout from footer to immediately under the 20%-off banner. For people like me, points-back is the reason to act *now* rather than wait.
- 3. **Use a women's model in the hero or swap to a category-appropriate image.** If the shoe callout is your product story, show the women's shoe. The men's plaid is a quick unsubscribe signal for Gen X women.
- 4. **Acknowledge the discount step-down honestly.** You ran 30% Tuesday. Running 20% Thursday with no context is confusing. "New week, new savings" framing at least acknowledges the calendar turn.
- 5. **Tighten the subject.** Kill the 🤗, add a deadline.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 20% off ends July 16 — shop your list`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 20% coupon + Kohl's Cash starts now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Earn $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 — use code KLSBE720 through Thursday`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Women's, beauty, home — all 20% off. Coupon in your Wallet.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "Save an extra 20%" (2026-07-10)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Well-structured HTML email with standard multi-client compatibility patterns (MSO conditionals, VML namespace, responsive breakpoints). Primary technical concerns are missing alt text on tracking pixels, unverifiable compliance headers through the AgentMail relay, and a malformed HTML tag.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **22 click-redirect links skipped** by the QA HTTP probe — all route through `click.chp.kohls.com` and `click.s.kohls.com`. Link reachability is unconfirmed.
- **Three separate open-tracking mechanisms** in use simultaneously:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` (CoherentPath pixel)
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/.../o.gif` (Movable Ink open pixel)
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...` (ESP open tracker)
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?...` (Adobe Audience Manager sync pixel)
- **Malformed HTML tag** at the open-tracker block: `</custom>` is not a valid closing tag. The surrounding `<div>` opens, the tracker `<img>` renders, then `</custom>` appears — no matching opening tag. Low rendering risk in practice but invalid markup.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **11 images missing `alt` text** (QA confirmed). Affected images include all Movable Ink personalization assets (`mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png`) and all three tracking pixels. For tracking pixels, `alt=""` with `aria-hidden="true"` is the correct pattern — two of three pixels lack both attributes.
  - `o.gif` has `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"` — correctly handled.
  - `c434c940...` (CoherentPath pixel) — no `alt`, no `aria-hidden`.
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event` pixel — no `alt`, no `aria-hidden`.
  - All `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` content images — missing `alt` text entirely; these appear to be visible product/promo images, not pixels.
- `user-scalable=0` in the viewport meta tag prevents pinch-zoom — an accessibility concern (WCAG 1.4.4).
- `<title>` element is empty. Screen readers announce the tab/window title; a blank title is a minor but confirmed gap.
- Google Fonts loaded three ways simultaneously: `<link>` tag + `@import` in a `<style>` block (both for Roboto and Montserrat). Redundant requests; MSO conditional wraps the `<link>` tags correctly, but the `@import` duplicates fire on all clients.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- Movable Ink real-time personalization assets use `mi_u=604230016` — this appears to be a recipient-specific UID embedded at send time. No unresolved merge token syntax (`{{`, `%%`, `*|`) detected in the truncated source.
- `mi_ecmp=1017230_2026710` and `email_name=260710_DG_Email_Friday` are consistent across all MI asset URLs — campaign tagging looks correctly applied.
- `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` on one asset URL (`916d6fa08d185233.png`) has an **empty value** — the redemption end date parameter was not populated. If MI uses this to render a countdown or expiry date, it may render blank or throw a fallback.

---

### 5. Compliance

- **List-Unsubscribe header not detected** by QA relay. CAN-SPAM does not mandate this header, but Gmail and Apple Mail use it for the native unsubscribe prompt. Its absence increases spam folder risk on those clients.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not detected** — one-click unsubscribe (required by Gmail/Yahoo for bulk senders >5k/day since Feb 2024) is unconfirmed. If these headers are set by the ESP and stripped by the AgentMail relay, this is a relay artifact; if genuinely absent, it is a deliverability compliance gap.
- **No physical mailing address detected** in the truncated source. CAN-SPAM §7(a)(5) requires a valid physical postal address in the message body. This may exist in the unshown footer — cannot confirm or clear from available source.
- `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` suppresses the HTTP Referer header on any outbound navigation. This is intentional for privacy but will cause UTM attribution loss if any destination reads the referrer instead of query params (see §6).

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

- All 22 tracked links route through `click.chp.kohls.com` or `click.s.kohls.com` redirects — UTM parameters (if any) are embedded in the redirect destination, not verifiable from the truncated source. The QA probe skipped all 22, so landing page UTM alignment is unconfirmed.
- `mi_ecmp=1017230_2026710` is present on MI asset URLs but is an internal MI campaign ID, not a standard UTM parameter. Whether the click-through URLs carry `utm_campaign`, `utm_source`, and `utm_medium` is unknown from available data.
- The `no-referrer` meta (noted above) is a belt-and-suspenders concern: if UTM params are correctly on destination URLs, referrer suppression is harmless.
