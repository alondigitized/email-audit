# This one’s just for you 😘
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-12**
## Executive summary

- Well, this is addressed to someone named "Rosie" — I am not Rosie, and I don't appreciate finding out Sephora's "just for you" personalization is running on the wrong customer's name. That's not a small glitch, that's the kind of thing that makes me trust the brand less, not more. Underneath the sloppy greeting, this is at least a cleaner layout than the last few Sephora sends I've reviewed — the Prada Paradoxe hero has real breathing room, and I like that it's tagged NEW rather than just another "dropped" banner.
- But once you get past the hero, it's the same overstuffed Sephora grid I've seen three times this week: eight products crammed two-across with no real narrative thread — clean makeup, a peony perfume, hair serum, lipstick, hand sanitizer, moisturizer. That's not curation "for you," that's just inventory. And "you have 0 points" is sitting right up top again, same complaint I had about the 8/11 "winning" email — if you're going to lead with my Insider status, don't lead with a null.
- I'd skim this, maybe glance at the Prada bottle since it's genuinely pretty, and archive it. Not forwarding it — nothing here rises to "you have to see this."

## What's working

- The Prada Paradoxe hero has real white space around it and a clear single CTA — a relief after Sephora's usual grid-first layouts.
- "NEW" tag and full product name/price under the hero gives me enough to decide without clicking.
- "Spot it. Shop it." mystery reward banner (250 points, get 1 of 5) is at least a concrete, gamified offer, not vague copy.
- The $10 bonus card and free same-day shipping strip up top is honest and specific, same strength I noted in the 8/10 email.

## What's weak

- Addressed to "Rosie" in the hero subhead — that's not my name, and a wrong-name personalization miss is worse than no personalization at all.
- "You have 0 points" front and center again — this is the second time this week Sephora's led an Insider with a null instead of my tier or last purchase.
- The 8-product grid below the hero has zero editorial logic — cleansing balm next to a $380 perfume next to hand sanitizer, no attempt to group by category or occasion.
- "Must-Have Fragrance Minis" and "New in Skincare" banners stacked at the bottom feel like leftover inventory dumped in after the real pitch already happened.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the merge-field bug before anything else — sending me someone else's name in a "just for you" email is the single biggest trust-breaker here, worse than any layout issue.
- 2. Replace "You have 0 points" with tier status or "You're 250 points from Rewards Bazaar" — turn the null into forward motion, the way the mystery-reward banner already tries to.
- 3. Cut the 8-product grid to 3-4 items grouped by theme (e.g., "New Fragrance," "Clean Beauty Picks") instead of a scattershot catalog dump.
- 4. Move the mystery-reward banner up near the hero — it's the strongest offer on the page and it's buried past the fold.
