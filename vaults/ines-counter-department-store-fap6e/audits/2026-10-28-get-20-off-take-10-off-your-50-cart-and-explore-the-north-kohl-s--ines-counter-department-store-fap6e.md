# 🚂 Get 20% off + Take $10 off your $50+ cart AND explore The North Kohl's 🚂
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-28**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's again, and it's the third "20% + $10 off $50+" stack in three days. I got it on 10/26, again on 10/27 ("holiday gifting made easy"), and again this morning with the home section on top. The elves and the train are cute, but I opened this and recognized the offer before I finished the first screen. At Nordstrom or Saks, a sale that repeated that often would have lost its credibility.
- The email is also a very long scroll. It opens with a coupon graphic, then "Welcome to the North Kohl's," then four themed "stops" on the map, a Top 100 gifts grid and a product block. The coupons don't show up until the very bottom, as barcodes. I'd have to scroll about 20 screens to find the actual codes.
- I'd click once, maybe, for the Ninja SLUSHi or a towel set. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and wait for the weekend, because I know the same offer is coming back.

## What's working

- The top has my status ("KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER") and the "Earn 5% Rewards" line. That's the loyalty acknowledgment I look for, and it makes me feel known.
- The "Ends Nov. 1" date sits right by the hero offer, so the urgency is believable and specific.
- The product block at "Picked with you in mind" shows real prices: $299.99 (original $349.99), $2.99–$9.99, $52. I can judge the value without clicking.
- The barcode coupons are clear once I get to them: SAVE20, $10 off $50, $10 off jewelry and watches.
- The "North Kohl's" illustration is pretty and seasonal. It's a real creative idea, not another maroon sale template.

## What's weak

- The offer I came for is a small coupon graphic at the top. The huge illustrated world takes over from there, and the eye lands on elves, not the savings.
- There are five or six "Shop Now" buttons and none of them name a category. "Shop Now" under a map vignette doesn't tell me what I'm shopping.
- The "Plus, earn Kohl's Cash" block and the "$5 Kohl's Cash" box add a second offer on top of the first. I count at least four stacked offers, and that's Macy's-style overload.
- The product picks are a slush machine, towels, a mattress topper and jeans. Nothing in the email says beauty, and Kohl's Sephora presence is buried. The "Coupons do not apply to certain brands" line is also easy to miss.
- This is the same stack as 10/26 and 10/27. It feels like shouting.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in one clear headline block at the very top, with a single "Use code SAVE20" button, and move the barcodes up near it. Right now they're the last thing in the email.
- 2. Cut the four themed "stops" to one hero and one product row. Fewer buttons, each with a specific verb and noun: "Shop cozy gifts," "Shop kitchen deals."
- 3. Rotate the offer, or change what the offer is built around. Three days of the identical stack trains me to wait, and Sephora at Kohl's would be a more relevant hook for me.
- 4. Use my status in the offer: tell me how much Kohl's Cash I could earn on a typical $50 order.
- 5. State the exclusions right next to the offer, not at the bottom.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Rewards member: 20% off, plus Kohl's Cash`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Codes are inside. Earn Kohl's Cash on top through Nov. 1.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):**
  - Content-style images on `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/`: `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`.

  - I'd treat the `rp/*.png` images as the real problem, since they likely carry the offers. Screen readers and image-blocked clients get nothing from them.
- **QA false positive:** `o.gif` is flagged, but the source shows `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, so it is already handled correctly.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Empty favicon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` can trigger a request back to the document URL in some clients.
- **Invalid markup in `<head>`:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will usually move it into the body, which can add an unintended element.
- **Web fonts:** Google Fonts `@import` and `<link>` are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, and the stacks fall back to `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`. No issue found.
