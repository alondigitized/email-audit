# Take 20% off | Fall home finds at can’t-miss prices 🏡
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again — and this one's a mess. My status bar up top does say "KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER" and "Earn: 5% REWARDS," which is at least consistent with what I've seen this week, but two paragraphs into "A little fall. A lot less spending," I'm already scrolling past bedding, decor, bath, then suddenly I'm staring at a "WE ARE SO BACK (to school)" banner with kids in backpacks — so which is it, cozy fall home refresh or back-to-school? Then there's a sweepstakes module, and then a "Picked with you in mind" grid that tries to sell me a towel, a slushie maker, jeans, AND a mattress topper in the same four boxes. Nothing here was picked with anyone in mind — it's a clearance dump with my name stapled on.
- This is worse than yesterday's "Take 20% off | Find fall home must-haves for less" send I gave an 8 to, because that one at least stayed on-theme start to finish. This just falls apart by the middle. Compare it to Neiman Marcus's Fendi eyewear email — moody, over-scrolled, but at least it commits to one idea. Kohl's can't even commit to one category in a single email.
- I'd open it because the subject is fine, but I'm marking it read after one scroll. Not forwarding — nobody I know needs to see a slushie maker next to a mattress topper.

## What's working

- The 20% coupon code (GET20) and end date (Aug. 16) are right up top and unmissable — no hunting for the deal.
- Rewards status and 5% earn rate shown immediately, so at least I know where I stand as a member.
- Individual product tiles (bedding, decor, bath) have clear percentage-off callouts and "Shop Now" links.
- Photography on the bedding and gallery-wall shots is warm and actually fall-appropriate — that part I'd pin to a mood board.

## What's weak

- The back-to-school section completely derails the fall-home theme — it's a jarring pivot with kids and backpacks shoved into a home-decor email.
- The "Picked with you in mind" grid is a random product dump (towel, slushie machine, jeans, mattress topper) — nothing about that says "your great taste," it says "we didn't segment this."
- Way too many CTAs and category buttons (Home/Women/Kids/Men) stacked before I even get to a single product — the eye has nowhere to land first.
- A sweepstakes ad wedged between school shopping and product tiles adds more noise, not more relevance.
- Nothing here reads as beauty-counter or Saks/Nordstrom-tier curated — this is Macy's-style overload, but messier, since even Macy's usually keeps one theme per email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one theme and cut the rest — either fall-home or back-to-school, not both stitched into one scroll. That's the single biggest fix.
- 2. Delete or heavily trim the "Picked with you in mind" grid — if it's not actually personalized, don't claim it is; that's a trust-breaker for anyone paying attention.
- 3. Move the sweepstakes module to its own send — it's stealing real estate from the fall-home offer this email is supposed to be about.
- 4. Cut the four category buttons down to just Home-related subcategories (Bedding, Decor, Bath) since that's what the email is actually selling.
- 5. Lead with one hero product shot (the bedding set) instead of four competing 20-50% callouts before I've even scrolled once.
