# Fall’s bold new arrivals
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- This is the Neiman's I like, with no shouting. After a week of the $200-off ladder (10/6 "Grab it now," 10/7 "You could save $50–$200," and this morning's "Save today: $50–$200 off your purchase"), a plain black hero and a tidy grid of product is a relief. It looks like a boutique window, not a clearance table. Macy's "LAST CHANCE: confirm now" email is the opposite: it's all urgency, and this one has none.
- But it's also a bit empty. There's no offer, no price, no deadline and no mention of my Neiman Marcus InCircle points. I can see a Saint Laurent heel, a Zimmermann blouse, the Chloé bag and a Theory jacket, and nothing tells me what any of it costs or where to tap for that item. The only links are two small underlined text lines in the black block and two outlined buttons.
- Would I click? Probably, for the Chloé bag or the Saint Laurent sandal. I'd browse, not buy. I wouldn't forward it, since there's nothing to forward except pretty pictures. It's a nice read-and-keep-scrolling email.

## What's working

- The black hero with "BRAND NEW STYLES JUST LANDED" is clean and quiet. It reads as designer access, not a promo.
- Brand names sit under every item (Saint Laurent, Zimmermann, Chloé, Balmain, Theory). That's how I shop, brand first.
- The women's block comes first, then men's, with separate "Shop All" buttons. I don't have to wade through menswear to get to mine.
- The grid is evenly spaced and the white backgrounds are consistent. Nothing is broken or overlapping.
- It's a relief after the discount-ladder week. Sephora's "The Chat" was just as quiet, but this one at least has product.

## What's weak

- There are no prices anywhere. For a $1,000 bag or a $900 sandal I want at least a "from" price.
- The hero CTAs, "Shop Women's" and "Shop Men's," are tiny underlined text on black. On a phone those are hard to tap, and they don't look like buttons.
- Nothing mentions InCircle points, a gift card event or a beauty tie-in. A points-driven shopper like me gets no reason to pick Neiman's over Nordstrom or Saks today.
- The product photos don't appear to link to specific items from what I can see, and the "Shop All" buttons are thin gray outlines with light gray text.
- Half the email is menswear, which isn't for me. Kohl's at least put a clear "Save 20%" banner first.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one sentence under the hero tying it to loyalty, such as "InCircle members: earn bonus points on new arrivals through Sunday." That single line would lift this most.
- 2. Make the hero CTAs solid white buttons, not underlined text.
- 3. Add "from $" prices under each brand name.
- 4. Segment by gender so I don't get a half-menswear email.
- 5. Replace the pale gray "Shop All" outlines with high-contrast buttons.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `The bags, boots and blouses everyone will want this season`

# Technical Audit: "Fall's bold new arrivals" (Neiman's New Arrivals)

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML source shows a table-based email with a leaked internal ESP template comment, a viewport setting that blocks zoom, and no `lang` attribute. Most automated QA warnings (headers, auth) are relay-capture limits, not confirmed defects. The source is truncated, so body links, alt text, and the footer could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified:** 30 tracking links were skipped from HTTP probing because they are on click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chain, and 404s are unknown.
- **Plain-text part:** QA reports 5,693 of 6,477 characters (~88%) are URLs. This suggests long tracking URLs in the text alternative, which spam filters may weigh against it. It is a warning, not a confirmed failure.
- No broken links are confirmed (QA: 0 issues).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom on mobile, which is an accessibility problem for low-vision users.
- **No language attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang`/`xml:lang`, so screen readers can't pick the correct language profile.
- **Comment before the DOCTYPE:** Several HTML comments precede `<!DOCTYPE>`. Some older Outlook and IE-based engines can fall into quirks mode when anything precedes the DOCTYPE.
- **Fragile mobile selectors:** The media queries use exact-match attribute selectors such as `*[class=contenttable]`, `*[class=hide]`, and `*[class=mobile]`. These break if a client appends or rewrites class names (for example, multiple classes on one element). The `*[class].hamburger` and `.navbar-checkbox:checked` nav relies on the checkbox hack, which Gmail and several clients don't support. The mobile menu may be non-functional there.
- **Title:** `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` has a trailing space and doesn't reflect the subject. This is minor.
- **Not verifiable:** Image alt text and the dark-mode meta tags are outside the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The comment block at the top of the delivered HTML exposes ESP/AMPscript-style template notes. It names Data Extensions (`NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`), fields (`EMAIL_ID`, `uEm`, `cmdid`), and variables (`@email`, `@base_refid`, `@refid`, `@user`, `@emailid`). It also shows a hashing option (`SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256`).
- The comment is unstripped template scaffolding. It discloses internal data-model names to any recipient who views the source.
- It also contains a syntax slip, an unbalanced `))` in the `@user Lookup(...)` line. The comment is inert, but it shows the scaffold was shipped as-is.
- No unresolved visible merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the supplied source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** Both are not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip or not capture them, so absence is unconfirmed. Check the raw message headers at the source.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** Not found, expected via the relay. SPF, DKIM, and DMARC alignment are unknown. Sender is `NeimanMarcus@e.neimanmarcus.com`, a dedicated subdomain, which is good practice, but alignment is unverified.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** Not visible in the truncated source, so unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be assessed. All 30 links are click-redirect tracking URLs that weren't resolved, and the body is truncated.
- The `@refid` / `@base_refid` variables suggest campaign IDs are appended server-side. Whether they reach the landing pages is unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Strip the template/variable comment block from the sent HTML (ESP comment syntax, such as server-side comments, so it never ships). Fix the `))` typo in the source template.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Add `lang="en"` (and `xml:lang`) to `<html>`, and move the DOCTYPE to the first line.
4. Switch `*[class=x]` selectors to `[class~="x"]` or `.x` so they survive multi-class elements. Offer a non-checkbox fallback for the mobile nav.
5. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC on a raw copy received directly, not through the relay.
6. Shorten or simplify the plain-text part's URLs (or reduce link count) to lower the URL ratio.
