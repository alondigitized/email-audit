# It’s Hispanic Heritage Month—let’s give these brands their flowers 🌺🌼🌸
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-26**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "let's give these brands their flowers" is a nice sentiment and I get what Sephora's doing here — spotlighting Aora Mexico, Touchland, Ceremonia, and Rare Beauty for Hispanic Heritage Month. But the top of this email is a fragrance/haircare promo banner and a wall of "Founders" tiles before I ever see why I should care as a shopper, not just as someone getting a diversity-and-inclusion newsletter. It reads more like a company blog post than a shopping email — pretty, well-intentioned, scroll-forever, and light on any actual reason for me to click today.
- I'd skim it, maybe pause on the Rare Beauty tile because I already own two of her blushes, and then close it. This isn't a forward-to-a-friend email — it's a "noted, moving on" email. Compare that to Sephora's own "Hair Daily Deals" send from Thursday (9/23) that opened with up to 50% off and free same-day delivery — that one gave me a reason to act in the first three seconds. This one gives me a reason to feel good about Sephora, which isn't the same as giving me a reason to buy.

## What's working

- The "Founders Face to Face" video tile with Dez Perkins and Denise Vasi is a nice trust cue — real founders, real faces, not just product shots.
- Rare Beauty by Selena Gomez gets top billing near the bottom with two clear "ONLINE AT SEPHORA"/"NEW" product tags — I actually recognize and buy this brand, so that's the one relevant touchpoint for me.
- The founder quote callouts ("Aiste is a brand that invites you to celebrate...", "Selena Gomez: our cultural commitment to family") give some warmth instead of pure sales copy.
- The bottom banner clearly ties back to my Beauty Insider tier progress bar, which I always look for.

## What's weak

- The hero banner is "Up to 30% off Hair Faves" — that's a completely different campaign bolted onto a Hispanic Heritage Month email, and it's confusing which offer I'm even supposed to act on.
- Six back-to-back brand spotlights (Aora, Touchland, Ceremonia, Rare Beauty) with no discount, no bundle, no single CTA is a lot of scrolling for zero urgency — this is the same "wall of new products" fatigue I flagged in the 9/25 "New beauty to obsess over" send, just with a cultural theme slapped on instead of a seasonal one.
- Touchland's hand sanitizer and phone spray don't read as Hispanic Heritage Month product picks to me at a glance — feels like filler to round out the tile count.
- By the time I hit "Spot it. Shop it." with yet another 4X points/20% off haircare banner, I've lost the thread of what this email was even about.
- Nordstrom's Friends & Family reminder at least keeps hammering one clear offer even when it's repetitive; this email can't decide if it's a cause marketing piece or a promo blast, and ends up weak at both.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one lane — if this is a heritage-month spotlight, drop the hair-deal banner entirely and lead with one specific, shoppable bundle (e.g., "Shop the Rare Beauty + Ceremonia edit") instead of six standalone brand cards.
- 2. Give me an actual incentive tied to the theme — a % off these specific brands, or a GWP, would turn browsing into buying. Right now there's no discount anywhere near the featured brands.
- 3. Move Rare Beauty (the brand I actually shop) higher, not buried after four brands I don't know.
- 4. Cut the Touchland inclusion or explain the tie-in — it reads like a mismatched filler brand in this lineup.
- 5. Trim to 3 brands max with one clear CTA button instead of "SHOP AND SUPPORT" repeated six times, which starts to feel like a rubber-stamped label rather than a real ask.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Rare Beauty, Ceremonia & more — plus a beauty tier bonus inside`
