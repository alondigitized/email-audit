# Doorbusters + 20% off + $15 Kohl's Cash = JACKPOT 🏆
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- My first reaction is that this is Kohl's, and it's a long one. The image I see is about 12,000 pixels of stacked tiles, and at the size it renders I'm reading shapes and big numbers, not fine print. I can make out "Stores open today at 5 AM," a "$15 Kohl's Cash" badge, a "Free shipping with a $25 purchase" strip, a "Win up to $500 Kohl's Cash" sweepstakes block, then a black-and-gold "Doorbusters" banner and a long run of product tiles. Those include Nike, adidas, Levi's, Tek Gear, Shark, Garmin, KitchenAid and Lego.
- This is the fifth Kohl's email I've had since 11/25, including this morning's "Doorbusters 🛍️ Extra 20% off 🛍️ $15 Kohl's Cash for every $50 spent." It's the same offer in a new outfit, and the subject line's "JACKPOT" trophy is a casino costume on a coupon.
- Would I click? Probably not. I'm a beauty-counter person who buys at the brand event and wants points back. Beauty is one small tile near the bottom, and I'd have to scroll through puffer jackets and charcoal-grill boards to find it. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read.

## What's working

- The "Stores open today at 5 AM" line at the top is a real, credible urgency cue. It's specific and tied to the day.
- The "$15 Kohl's Cash" badge and the Kohl's Cash/Rewards callouts show up early, so the loyalty angle is there if you care about points.
- The "Free shipping with a $25 purchase" strip is a clear, honest benefit on its own line.
- The doorbuster tiles lead with big numbers like 60%, 40% and 30%, so the price signal is easy to catch even when the image is shrunk.
- The black-and-gold palette is consistent and clearly Kohl's.

## What's weak

- There's no single hero. The sweepstakes, Kohl's Cash, free shipping and doorbusters all compete in the first screen, so my eye has nowhere to land.
- The email is far too long, and nearly every tile carries its own "Shop Now" button, so the buttons turn into noise.
- Beauty is nearly invisible. The one "Beauty" tile in the Gift List Lane block is a tiny afterthought, and nothing signals a beauty event or designer access.
- The stacked "+20% w/coupon" claims make me suspect conditions I can't see. The "Coupons do not apply" and "Select styles" notes under many tiles confirm it. The honesty is there, but only in small type.
- It's the same promo as 11/25, 11/26 and this morning. The 🏆 "JACKPOT" framing feels like shouting, not respect.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to one hero and four or five tiles. Lead with "Open 5 AM: 60% off doorbusters + $15 Kohl's Cash per $50," then one button. Sephora's 50% OFF send made the offer the headline and used a single SHOP NOW. This should do the same.
- 2. Add a beauty or "your category" module near the top for subscribers who've clicked beauty before. Neiman Marcus' one-offer, one-button email earned its 7 from me this week.
- 3. Spell out the Kohl's Cash mechanics in plain words in one line, including the earn and redeem dates. Macy's "extra 25%" email was pushy, but it at least said what I had to do.
- 4. Space out the sends. One strong email on 11/27 beats three near-duplicates in 48 hours.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML has several markup-validity and accessibility defects: a stray `</custom>` tag, an `<itemscope>` div inside `<head>`, missing alt text on content images, and zoom disabled in the viewport meta. The automated compliance and authentication warnings come mostly from relay and truncation limits, so they are unconfirmed. The source also shows an empty dynamic parameter (`mi_kc_redm_end_dte=`) on a Kohl's Cash image.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **102 tracking links were not probed.** All are redirect wrappers, such as `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7ImQi…`. Destination status, redirect chains and expiry can't be verified from this data.
- **Four tracking beacons load before any visible content**, from four separate vendors:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/d5545e9e-…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`
  
  This is redundant open tracking, and one failing host could delay image loading.
- **Plain-text part is 75% URLs** (18,012 of 23,864 chars) because of long `qs=` tokens. This is a spam-filter risk, and it is the QA tool's `>70%` threshold, not a fabricated figure.
- **The recipient-level ID `mi_u=604230016` is embedded in image URLs.** The same identifier is sent to every image host, which is a privacy and caching consideration.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Stray `</custom>`:** the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx…` `<img>` is followed by `</custom>`, which has no opening tag. This is malformed HTML.
- **`<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`.** It is invalid there, and parsers will close `<head>` early and move the div and metas into `<body>`.
- **Empty `<title></title>`** and **`<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`** (empty href).
- **Zoom is disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0>` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Missing alt text on content images**, per QA:
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`
  - `…/f441d45d2128ae53.png`
  - `…/c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`
  - `…/415c8a18fc2950ea.png`
  - `…/69494b48a79b4240.png`
  - `…/78308d1d9adb0751.png`
  - `…/916d6fa08d185233.png`
  - `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/60699446_4_2x`
  - `…/60699446_3_2x`
  
  If these carry offers or CTAs, the content is invisible when images are blocked.
- **Beacon images lacking `alt`:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/…` and `kohls.demdex.net/event` have no `alt` attribute and should get `alt=""`.
  - The QA flag on `o.gif` looks like a false positive, because the source has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`.
- **Doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional but the markup uses XML-style `/>`.** This is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the stray `</custom>` tag, and move the `itemscope` div and `<meta itemprop>` tags out of `<head>` (use JSON-LD or drop them).
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images. Add `alt=""` to the `click.chp.kohls.com/o/` and `demdex` beacons.
3. Drop `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta. Populate `<title>` and remove the empty `rel="shortcut icon"` link.
4. Fix the empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` value in the personalization feed, and check that the Kohl's Cash expiry renders.
5. Consolidate the four open-tracking pixels to reduce load and privacy surface.
6. Verify the unsubscribe headers, authentication results and footer address from a direct, non-relay send. Resolve a sample of the 102 tracked links to confirm UTM carry-through and 200 status.
