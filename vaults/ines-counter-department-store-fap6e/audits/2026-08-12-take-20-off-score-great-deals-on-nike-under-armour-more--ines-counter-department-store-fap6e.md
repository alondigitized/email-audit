## What's weak

- Five nearly identical "20% off + Kohl's Cash" emails this week and this one still opens with the same red starburst and green Kohl's Cash banner I've seen on repeat — the cadence is wearing me out.
- The email sprawls from activewear into home goods into a slushi machine into jeans with no throughline — Sephora Insider's "just for you" mess at least tried to feel personal; this just feels like a firehose.
- Under Armour/Nike/TEK GEAR is a younger, more athletic demographic than me — nothing here signals "beauty counter regular" or even "Gloria Vanderbilt jeans lady," even though those jeans are buried at the very bottom.
- "Coupons do not apply to select brands" shows up twice — once in the hero, once near the footer — which is exactly the buried-fine-print feeling that made me side-eye Macy's "confirm now" email.
