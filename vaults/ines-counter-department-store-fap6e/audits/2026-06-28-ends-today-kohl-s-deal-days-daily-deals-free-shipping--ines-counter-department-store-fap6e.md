# Ends today: Kohl's Deal Days + Daily Deals + FREE shipping ⏳
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-28**
## Executive summary

- Thirty-four. That's my Kohl's count for June, and I barely flinched when this one hit. "Ends today" — which is exactly what landed in my inbox on the 27th, and something functionally identical on the 26th twice, and before that the "Say less 🤗" version on the 25th. I opened this the way I open all of them now: out of folder-sorting muscle memory, not intent.
- What I found when I opened was the full Kohl's parade: red-and-gold Deal Days hero, five category tiles, six product deals stacked like a flyer, then a crock pot and an outdoor chair at the bottom. I am a woman who spends real money at the Nordstrom beauty counter and times her Saks purchases to the gift-with-purchase event. There is not a single beauty product in this email. Not one. Sephora ran "last chance to save up to 50% on select beauty" and knew exactly who they were talking to. This email was written for everyone who shops all of Kohl's, which means it was written for no one in particular.
- I won't click. I won't forward it. I already filed it.

## What's working

- The Kohl's Cash banner at the very top is the one thing that held me — I never use a card without points back, and at least this surfaces the loyalty mechanic before I've scrolled anywhere.
- The 60% off signal is real and it pops; that's the strongest number in the email and it deserved more visual weight than it gets buried mid-grid.
- Clean render — no broken images, no overlapping text, no lorem ipsum. Given how content-dense this template is, that's not nothing.
- "Ends today" in the subject is at least unambiguous; I know the window, even if I've heard it every day this week.
- Category nav tiles (Denim, Women, Men, Kids, Home) let me triage fast if I were in a specific mindset — functional choice.

## What's weak

- Zero beauty. I am the target customer for beauty-at-mass-retail and Kohl's left me empty. They carry Bliss, Neutrogena, even some prestige adjacents — and the category didn't make the cut. The June 27 send had the same gap.
- This email shouts. Neiman Marcus ran a leopard-print editorial a few days ago that felt like it trusted my judgment. This stacks Deal Days + Daily Deals + FREE + ⏳ in the subject, then matches that energy in the body with red banners, block percentages, and no negative space. I'm being yelled at, not spoken to.
- Six product deals with no through-line — a men's striped shirt, women's clothing, a crock pot, a patio chair, some sub-$6 home good. A Macy's extra-25%-off email at its most chaotic still builds toward one action. This presents six separate shopping decisions.
- Cadence has obliterated any urgency this brand has left. Thirty-four emails in June. "Ends today" has zero credibility at this point — I've opened too many tomorrows.
- No personalization whatsoever. Kohl's has my purchase history. They know I've bought women's and skipped home goods. They didn't use any of it.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Surface one beauty deal, front and center.** Not buried in a tile — a hero. Even one prestige-adjacent skincare or fragrance item signals that Kohl's knows who I am. This single change would make the email relevant to me.
- 2. **One urgency signal, not four.** "Ends today" works. "Ends today: Kohl's Deal Days + Daily Deals + FREE shipping ⏳" is four things fighting each other. Choose the strongest and stop.
- 3. **Make the 60% off deal the hero.** It's the best offer in this email and it's buried in the second product card. Lead with it. One CTA. Support it with two secondaries below.
- 4. **Earn the Kohl's Cash banner.** Tell me what I'd *earn* on today's buys, not just that Cash exists. "Spend $50 today, earn $10 Kohl's Cash" is actionable. A small banner that says "Instant Kohl's Cash" is furniture.
- 5. **Pre-route me by past behavior.** The category tiles are a fine triage tool — but pre-selecting Women's for a subscriber who's opened women's content would save the scroll entirely.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Deal Days: today's best is 60% off women's`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping, no minimum. Stack your Kohl's Cash before midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Today's hero deal: 60% off — plus earn Kohl's Cash on every dollar you spend.`

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "Ends Today: Deal Days + FREE Shipping"

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is a standard table-layout HTML build using Kohl's CoherentPath/MessageGears stack with functional responsive breakpoints. The primary technical concerns are missing alt text on 10+ images (including tracking pixels), unverifiable compliance headers through the relay, and one malformed HTML tag in the open-tracking block.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 tracking links skipped by QA probe** — all click links route through `click.chp.kohls.com` (CoherentPath) and `click.s.kohls.com` (MessageGears). Destination URLs could not be verified. No broken links confirmed, but no live resolution was possible.

**Three separate open-tracking pixels fired:**
- `click.chp.kohls.com/o/9d649013-...` (CoherentPath open pixel)
- `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif` (MessageGears open pixel)
- `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?P7QAXPZVK7CEXJBQO6JOQVLVWM...` (MessageGears second open pixel)

Three concurrent open-tracking pixels is redundant and inflates open rate metrics across platforms. Likely a dual-platform send artifact.

**Additional tracking pixel:** `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` — Adobe Audience Manager segment sync pixel firing on open.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Malformed HTML tag** in the open-tracking block:

```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>
```

The `</custom>` closing tag is invalid and unclosed. In lenient parsers this is silently ignored, but it can cause downstream DOM parse errors in strict validators and some ESPs.

**10 images missing alt text** (QA confirmed). Affected assets include:
- All three open-tracking pixels (`9d649013-...`, `o.gif`, `event`)
- Six content/rendition images at `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/` (hero and product images)

Tracking pixels with `alt=""` is acceptable — but the `aria-hidden="true"` attribute is only present on one of the three pixels (`o.gif`). The CoherentPath pixel (`9d649013-...`) and the MessageGears open pixel (`open.aspx`) lack both `alt=""` and `aria-hidden`, which means screen readers may announce them.

The `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/` content images missing alt text are a real accessibility gap — these appear to be visible product/promotional images based on their naming convention (`260628_DG_Email_Sunday`).

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` prevents pinch-to-zoom on mobile. This is a WCAG 2.1 SC 1.4.4 failure (Resize Text).

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**`mi_u=604230016`** — subscriber ID is present and populated in all `mi.kohls.com` tracking URLs. No unfired/raw merge tokens detected in the truncated source.

**`mi_kc_redm_end_dte=`** — empty merge token in one image URL:

```
https://mi.kohls.com/p/rp/916d6fa08d185233.png?...&mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_pe
```

The `mi_kc_redm_end_dte` parameter is blank. If this is a redemption end date field, it fired unfilled. This is low-risk for an image URL parameter but indicates a suppressed or missing data value in the send context.

### 5. Compliance

**CAN-SPAM physical address:** Not detected in the truncated HTML source. Full source would need verification, but QA did not find it — flag for confirmation.

**List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post headers:** Not captured by the AgentMail relay. These are expected to be present on Kohl's commercial mail (MessageGears supports RFC 8058 one-click). The absence here is likely a relay capture gap rather than a true header omission, but cannot be confirmed from available data.

**`<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">`** and **`<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">`** — both present and correct for email context.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
