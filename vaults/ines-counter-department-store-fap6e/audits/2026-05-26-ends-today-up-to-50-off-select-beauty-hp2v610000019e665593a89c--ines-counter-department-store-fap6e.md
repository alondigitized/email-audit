# ENDS TODAY‼️Up to 50% off select beauty.
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-26**
## Executive summary

- Two emails in one day. Same offer. Different subject line cadence on the same promotion — "LAST CHANCE 🥺" arrived this morning, and now "ENDS TODAY‼️" lands this afternoon. At this point I'm not reading, I'm just cataloguing volume. Four sends of this 50%-off stacking offer in less than a week, and this is the second one today. That's not urgency — that's a brand that forgot it already yelled at me.
- That said, if I zoom out from the cadence fatigue, the email itself is mechanically solid. "Stacking Weekend" is the best thing Sephora has named this campaign across all four sends — it tells me exactly what the mechanic is before I scroll. The 50% badge paired with the 500-point add-on is clean and the credit card callout speaks directly to how I shop. And I'll say this: not seeing "Rosie" in the greeting is a genuine relief after nine days of misfires. Small bar to clear, but cleared.
- What kills the goodwill is the tone. "ENDS TODAY‼️" in all caps with a double-bang is the marketing equivalent of a salesperson grabbing your elbow on the way out. I've been a Beauty Insider long enough to have lived through VIB Rouge. Sephora doesn't need to scream at me. I would likely click — the deal is real — but I feel like I'm caving, not being served. Mark as read, probably act, definitely annoyed.

## What's working

- **"Stacking Weekend" hero naming** earns trust — it explains the mechanic instead of just re-screaming the discount, which is more than the May 22 send managed
- **50% + 500 points dual-badge layout** makes the combined value land at a glance; the stacked-circle visual shorthand works
- **"ENDS TODAY" is at least actionable** — even fatigued, I know the window closes now, which is more functional than a vague "hurry"
- **Sephora Credit Card callout is directly relevant to me** — I won't swipe without points back, and seeing it called out reminds me which card to use
- **Beauty Insider anchoring at the footer** confirms I'm in the program, even without a personalized greeting

## What's weak

- **Second send of the day, same offer** — "LAST CHANCE 🥺" was already in my inbox when this arrived; two triggers in a day for identical promotional content is volume abuse, not urgency management
- **ALL CAPS + ‼️ in the subject** — Sephora is a luxury-adjacent brand and this reads like a closeout rack announcement; Neiman Marcus never shouts at me like this, even in their Designer Sale emails
- **Auto-Replenish section mid-sale email** — dropping a subscription service upsell in the middle of a "buy now before it ends" flow is a completely different mindset and it breaks the momentum
- **"Limited-Edition rhoé" launch buried in an urgency send** — I don't know that brand, and introducing an unfamiliar label mid-fire-drill makes me trust neither the campaign nor the launch
- **Product grid thumbnails are too small to read** — the "Must-Haves" section shows maybe eight products at postage-stamp scale; Macy's, even in their cluttered sale sends, at least makes the brand names legible

## Recommendations

- 1. **Hard cap at one send per day for the same promotion.** If the morning send says "LAST CHANCE," the afternoon send is suppressed for anyone who received or opened it. Segment the afternoon hit to non-openers only.
- 2. **Kill the ALL CAPS and ‼️.** "Ends tonight: 50% off select beauty + 500 bonus points" is every bit as urgent and respects me as a customer.
- 3. **Enlarge the Must-Haves grid.** Three hero products with readable names beats eight thumbnails I have to squint at — especially on mobile.
- 4. **Move Auto-Replenish below the fold or to its own send.** Sale mindset and subscription mindset are not the same session.
- 5. **Remove the rhoé launch from this email entirely.** New brand introductions deserve their own real estate, not a cameo in an expiring-sale email.
- **Subject Alt A:** `50% off + 500 points ends tonight — stack your savings`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Stacking Weekend closes tonight, Ines`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use your Sephora Credit Card to layer on even more — here's how to stack today`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Select beauty up to 50% off. 500 bonus points with $75 spend. Tonight only.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`maximum-scale=1` on viewport meta — confirmed issue**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
Prevents user zoom on mobile. Fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text, Level AA). Should be removed or replaced with `initial-scale=1` only.

**Duplicate `display` declarations on `.show` and `.appear` — minor**
```css
*[class=show], .show { display: block!important; display: table!important; }
*[class=appear], .appear { display: block!important; display: table!important; }
```
The first `display: block` is immediately overridden by `display: table` in the same rule block. Likely intentional for cross-client compatibility, but the `block` declaration is dead weight.
