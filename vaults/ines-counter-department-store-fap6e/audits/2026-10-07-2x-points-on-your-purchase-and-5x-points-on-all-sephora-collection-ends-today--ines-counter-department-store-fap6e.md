# 2X points on your purchase and 5X points on ALL Sephora Collection ends today❗
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-07**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction was "yes, I know, SAYPOINTS." This is the same 2X and 5X promo that told me it "ends tomorrow" yesterday, and Sephora has now sent it to me about seven times since 10/2. The giant red "2X INSIDER" at the top is clean, and "ENDS TODAY" is honest this time. But the code is the same, the deadline is the same, and I've had days to act on it already.
- The body does what I'd hoped. It shows how many points specific products earn: 810 on the Tom Ford, 350 on the Jo Malone, 276 on the Versace. That's useful for someone who won't use a card without points back. Neiman Marcus's $50–$200 ladder is easier to read, because it shows dollars off. Sephora shows points without saying what they're worth. Below that, the email turns into a very long scroll of holiday sets, a Klarna/Afterpay block and legal text. Macy's would bury the code too, but not under this much.
- Would I click? Probably, if I were already planning a purchase today, because the Tom Ford and Jo Malone point counts are tempting. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and use the code at the counter.

## What's working

- The big "2X INSIDER" with "ENDS TODAY" above it is unmissable. I know the deadline in a second.
- Showing the points per product (810 for Tom Ford, 350 for Jo Malone) lets me do the math on what I'd earn.
- The Cardmember callout ("Earn an extra point per dollar using your Sephora Credit Card") speaks to my "no points, no card" habit.
- The "Beauty Insider" tier strip near the bottom (INSIDER / VIB / ROUGE) is a quiet nod to my status.
- The "See What Your Points Can Do" ladder is helpful: $5 off at 250 points, $10 off at 500, and so on. Points feel like money here.

## What's weak

- This is the same promo I got yesterday, "ends tomorrow," and on 10/3 ("Hot drops coming through"). I'm numb. The urgency is fair today, but the repetition has used up its power.
- The "SAYPOINTS" code is the only action. There's no "Shop now" button on the top block, so I have to remember a code and enter it myself, which is more friction than it should be.
- The points-per-product tiles look like a catalog. I'm shown what I'd earn on a $405 fragrance but not what I'd get back in dollars.
- It's a very long scroll to the bottom, with holiday sets, Klarna/Afterpay and dense legal copy. The "Spot it. Shop it." and holiday blocks feel like a different email stuffed into this one.
- Nothing speaks to me as a 47-year-old Rouge member. The Insider/VIB/Rouge strip sits at the bottom with no recognition of my tier, so I feel treated like a stranger.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real "Shop 2X points" button with the code pre-applied or visible. Stop making me type it.
- 2. Convert points to dollars in the hero, for example "That Tom Ford earns you $40 back."
- 3. Cap the product grid at 3–4 items and cut the Klarna and holiday blocks. One send, one story.
- 4. Space out the sends. Seven emails with the same code in six days is too many. Send one reminder that says "ends today."
- 5. Recognize my tier, such as "Rouge, you're one purchase from..." Neiman Marcus's NMCIRCLE emails at least feel built for a specific customer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last day: 2X points on everything, 5X on Sephora Collection`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 2X points end tonight, Rouge`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code SAYPOINTS before midnight. See what your points are worth.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `A $405 Tom Ford earns 810 points today. Ends tonight.`
