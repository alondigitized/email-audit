# Our LOWEST prices + Kohl's Cash = 🛒 🛍️ 🤑
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction is that this is a catalog, not an email. It's the fifth Kohl's send I've had in about two weeks. The "ENDS TONIGHT" banner stack from 10/18 is gone, and I'll give them credit for that. The maroon "Lowest Prices of the Season" header is calm. A Clearance box says "Save up to 85% after an extra 50% off," and I can read it without squinting. Then it turns into a very long scroll of "Up to 50% off, Shop Now" blocks. Bedding, fall decor, floor care, luggage, handbags, coats, boots and towels all get the same treatment. Nothing tells me what this email is actually for.
- I'm a beauty-counter person. The only beauty or Sephora nod is a tiny "Beauty | Sephora" tile near the bottom, below the gift-guide section. The Kohl's Cash line is a green strip near the top, but it's cramped. The real terms are in the footer, "Earn $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 spent," with redemption dates in small print. I had to hunt for that.
- Would I click? Probably not today. I might click the handbags block out of habit, and I'd probably look at the boots at $17.99. I wouldn't forward it. I'd read it as a bargain-bin flyer and mark it read.

## What's working

- The "Lowest Prices of the Season" header is clean and readable. It's a real improvement on the shouting from 10/18.
- The Clearance badge ("85% after an extra 50% off") states one clear, big number.
- Each block has a clear price anchor. "Starting at 17.99, Reg. $29.99–$69.99" on the boots shows the savings honestly.
- The "Shop now. Decide later. Free returns through January 31, 2027" strip is the best thing here. I return things, and a long return window respects that.
- The category chips at the top (Home, Women, Men, Kids, $10 & Under, $20 & Under) let me skip the scroll.

## What's weak

- Ten near-identical "Up to X% off" blocks flatten everything. The eye has no hero to land on.
- The Kohl's Cash offer is buried. The strip at the top says "Plus, earn Kohl's Cash!" but gives no amount, and the footer is where the dates live. I won't use a card or offer without knowing exactly what I get back.
- The coupon exclusions are everywhere: "Coupons do not apply," "Not valid on Sephora," "select brands." Each block carries fine print, and it makes the "Lowest" claim feel conditional.
- There's almost nothing for me on the beauty side. Sephora at Kohl's is a tiny tile near the bottom, with no hero and no product shown.
- The gift-guide section ("Checking off your gift list early") is early-holiday filler in late October, with a toy and a "Jammies for Your Families" tile that aren't for me.
- Nothing says "member." The header has a Kohl's Rewards line, but no tier or points status.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one hero and one offer. Pick the handbags, the Kohl's Cash, or the Clearance 85%, and put it above the fold with a real button. Cut the ten category blocks to three.
- 2. Put the Kohl's Cash amount and dates in the top strip, for example "Earn $10 Cash per $50 · spend Oct 20–25, redeem Oct 26–Nov 1." Don't send me to the footer.
- 3. Segment by what I buy. If I've clicked on beauty, show me Sephora at Kohl's first. Sephora, Ulta and Macy's all open with a segmented, relevant hero. This opens with everything.
- 4. Bring the free-returns line up near the top. It's the strongest trust signal here.
- 5. Give the exclusions one line at the bottom, not one per block.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Earn $10 Kohl's Cash per $50 — 5 days only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Handbags up to 50% off + Kohl's Cash, ends Oct. 25`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus free returns through Jan. 31. Coats, boots, bedding and more.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your Kohl's Cash earns Oct. 20–25. Here's where to spend it.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has several markup defects: a stray `</custom>` tag, a `<div>` inside `<head>`, an empty `<title>` and an empty favicon link. Accessibility and compliance checks are partly inconclusive because the HTML was truncated and the relay stripped headers. The QA run passed 38% of checks with 0 errors and 5 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Heavy tracking stack:** The email contains 78 click-redirect links, which QA skipped for HTTP probing, so none could be checked for resolution. Trackers seen in the source:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` (open pixel)
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/.../o.gif` (open pixel)
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` (open pixel)
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager, third party)
- **Stray tag:** The `open.aspx` pixel is followed by `</custom>`, which has no opening tag. The markup is invalid.
- **Empty favicon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty `href`. Some clients may request the page URL as a result.
- **Campaign data in image URLs:** `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` URLs carry `email_name=261020_DG_Email_Tuesda…` and `mi_ecmp=1024215_20261020`. They also carry per-recipient `mi_u=604230016`, so the image requests are individually tracked.
- **Plain-text part:** 13,866 of 19,587 characters (about 71%) are URLs, which trips the QA warning. The tracking wrappers are the likely cause.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt attributes:** QA flagged 15 images, but some of those flags are false positives:
  - Real failures: the `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` pixel and the `demdex.net/event` pixel have no `alt` attribute in the source.
  - False positives: `o.gif` has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"` and `open.aspx` has `alt=""`. Both are correct for decorative pixels.
  - Probably real: the product images `58966978_{1,2,11,14}sa_2x` and the `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` images were flagged. The truncated source doesn't show them, so I couldn't confirm. If they are content images, such as offers or banners rendered as images, missing alt hides the message from screen readers and from clients that block images.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility failure.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Invalid head content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will close the head early, and the `<meta itemprop>` elements inside it are ineffective in email.
- **Missing table roles:** The preheader table and the tracking tables lack `role="presentation"`, while the main layout tables have it.
- **Font loading:** Google Fonts are loaded through `<link>` and `@import`. Many clients strip these, and the fallback stack (`Montserrat, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`) is present.
- **Doctype:** HTML 4.01 Transitional is paired with an `xmlns:v`/`xmlns:o` declaration. This is common for Outlook and not a defect.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[First Name]`. The remainder of the body was truncated and could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** Both were not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is not confirmed as a sender defect. Verify against the raw message.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** It was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same reason. The sending domain is `s.kohls.com`.
- **Physical address:** QA found none. The footer was cut off in the source I received, so I can't confirm this. Kohl's footers normally include one, so check the full HTML before calling it a violation.
- **Referrer policy:** `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` is present. It has no compliance impact, but it suppresses referrer data on landing.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not verifiable. Every link is wrapped in `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=<encrypted blob>`, so destination URLs and UTM parameters are opaque. The 78 redirects were not followed, so landing-page alignment with the offers ("Kohl's Cash", "lowest prices") is unconfirmed. The only visible campaign identifiers are `mi_ecmp=1024215_20261020` and `email_name=…` on image URLs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run QA with the full HTML and raw headers. Then confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results` and the footer address.
2. Follow a sample of the `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=` links to verify final URLs, HTTP status and UTM or `mi_*` parameter carry-through.
3. Remove the stray `</custom>` tag and move the `schema.org` `<div>` out of `<head>`. Remove or populate the empty `<link href="">`.
4. Add `alt=""` to the `click.chp.kohls.com` and `demdex.net` pixels. Add descriptive alt text to the product and offer images.
5. Replace `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` with a standard viewport, and fill in `<title>`.
6. Add `role="presentation"` to the preheader and tracking tables.
7. Adjust the QA alt-text check so it stops flagging pixels that already have `alt=""`.
