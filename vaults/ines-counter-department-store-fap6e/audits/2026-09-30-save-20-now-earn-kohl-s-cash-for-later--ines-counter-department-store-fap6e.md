# Save 20% now 😍 Earn Kohl's Cash for later 🤩
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- It's Kohl's again, and I opened it out of habit. The subject says "Save 20% now," but the hero is a single word, "EXTRA," with nothing under it. That's the same hollow header I got on 9/29 ("Get an extra 20% off + earn Kohl's Cash") and on 9/30 earlier ("Enjoy 20% off + earn Kohl's Cash"). The status bar does say "Kohl's Rewards' Member" and "Earn 5% Rewards," so they know me. After that it turns into a long scroll of home decor, Halloween, Lego, and a recommendations block.
- The 20% code doesn't show up until the very bottom, near the barcodes. By then I've scrolled past about eight offers. Neiman's Friends & Family put one calm "25% off" and a plain "Shop the Event" button up top, and I knew what to do. Here I have to hunt. Macy's at least made the confirm-now step the obvious ask.
- Would I click? Maybe the $10 off $50 home purchase, if I were redoing a room. Would I forward it? No. I'd leave it unread and wait for the next one, which will be tomorrow.

## What's working

- The status bar with my Rewards membership and "Earn 5% Rewards" tells me they recognize me.
- The pumpkin-spice home tile has a clear "$10 off $50 or more" offer with a visible "Shop Now."
- The "Picked with you in mind" block shows real prices, like the Serta topper at 137⁹⁹–209⁹⁹ with the original price crossed out. That's useful.
- The code blocks at the bottom (YOUSAVE20, HOME10) are easy to use once I find them.
- The seasonal theme is on point: pumpkins, cozy bedding, Halloween.

## What's weak

- "EXTRA" on its own is a headline with no noun. Extra what? The subject promised 20%, and the hero doesn't say it.
- The Kohl's Cash strip is a thin green band with no amount. Earn how much, and when can I spend it?
- There are too many competing offers: 30% off bedding, 30% off bath, 20% off Lego, $10 off home, $10 off toys, $10 off baby. Nothing is the hero. It feels like a Macy's-style overload.
- The fine print is a wall of tiny gray text. I can't read it without zooming, and "Not valid on Sephora at Kohl's" is buried in it.
- Kohl's has sent me roughly the same email every day this week ("Extra 20% off" on 9/28, 9/29, and now today). I can't tell what's new.
- Nothing speaks to beauty or designer shopping, which is where I spend. Sephora at Kohl's gets no mention in the body.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put "Extra 20% off" in the hero with one button, "Shop the Sale," and move the code above the fold. Make the expiry date (Oct. 4) visible there too.
- 2. Show one clear Kohl's Cash line: "Earn $10 for every $50 spent. Spend it Oct. 5–18." Put it up top.
- 3. Cut the tile count by half, or rotate the categories so it isn't the same mix every day.
- 4. Give Rewards members a real perk line, like "Your 5% back, plus $10 Kohl's Cash."
- 5. Add a Sephora at Kohl's tile to segment me. I'd click that.

# Technical Audit: Kohl's "Save 20% now 😍 Earn Kohl's Cash for later 🤩"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based layout with MSO conditionals, a mobile `@media (max-width: 500px)` block and heavy third-party tracking. The source has several markup defects (empty `<title>`, invalid `<head>` content, a stray closing tag), and many images have no alt text. The HTML is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and header-based compliance checks can't be confirmed either way.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Links not verified:** 64 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe. The links use opaque redirects such as `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABB7InYiOjEsImQiOjUwMTV9...`. Their destinations and validity are unconfirmed.
- **Four tracking pixels in the first few lines of the body:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/8c494d5a-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...` (1x1, no `alt`)
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&...`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?EFJRVKYYR5PUJFIILFPXVCVW2U.60269&d=60269&bmt=0`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager, `width="0" height="0"`)
  
  This is redundant open tracking across three systems. It adds third-party requests and depends on each one resolving.
- **Malformed markup:** the Salesforce open pixel is followed by a stray `</custom>`, which has no matching opening tag: `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** the QA check flags 8 or more images. Two are tracking pixels (`8c494d5a-...`, `o.gif`) and one is the demdex `event` pixel. At least seven are content images served from `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/` (`2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`). Without alt text, images-off and screen-reader users get no content.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` with `<meta itemprop>` children sits inside `<head>`. A `<div>` isn't allowed there, and parsers will close `<head>` early at that point. Gmail and other clients ignore schema microdata in email anyway.
- **Empty favicon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`.
- **Web fonts:** Roboto and Montserrat load via both `<link>` and `@import` inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. The body stack falls back to `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`, so this is acceptable. Gmail and Outlook will ignore the web fonts.
- **Inconsistent layout semantics:** the outer tables use `role="presentation"`, but the first tracking-wrapper tables (`width="1" height="1"`) and the preheader table don't.
- **Dated doctype:** HTML 4.01 Transitional is paired with `xmlns:v`/`xmlns:o` attributes. It works, but it's mixed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unrendered merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) appear in the visible source.

Image URLs carry per-recipient parameters (`mi_u=604230016`, `mi_mid=01a0f379-...`, `d_sid=13245196`). Those parameters look like working personalization, not failures.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Physical address:** QA reports "No physical address detected". The source is truncated, so the footer isn't visible. Treat this as unconfirmed, and check the footer of the full HTML.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** QA could not find either header. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not preserve them, so this is likely a capture limitation, not a sender defect. Check the raw message headers to confirm.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not present, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. QA attributes this to the relay. The `From` domain `s.kohls.com` is an aligned subdomain, but alignment can't be verified from this data.
- **In-body unsubscribe link:** not visible in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** none are visible in the truncated source, and the click URLs are encoded inside the `qs=` token.
- **Image parameters:** the image URLs carry `mi_u`, `mi_ecmp=1022810_2026930` and `email_name=260930_DG_Email_Wed_PM`. These are campaign identifiers for Coherent Path, not UTMs.
- **Landing pages:** the destinations can't be checked because the probe skipped the tracking links. Continuity with the 20% offer and Kohl's Cash messaging is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` content images. Add `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"` to the decorative pixels (`click.chp.kohls.com/o/...`, `kohls.demdex.net/event`).
2. Fill in `<title>` and remove or populate the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon" href="">`.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
4. Delete the stray `</custom>`. Move the `itemscope` div and its `<meta>` tags out of `<head>`, or drop them.
5. Consolidate the open-tracking pixels, with only one open pixel per system that is actually consumed.
6. Re-run QA against the full HTML and the raw headers to confirm the physical address, unsubscribe link, `List-Unsubscribe(-Post)` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC status before treating the compliance warnings as defects.
7. Resolve a sample of the `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=` links in a browser to check landing-page alignment and any UTM parameters.
