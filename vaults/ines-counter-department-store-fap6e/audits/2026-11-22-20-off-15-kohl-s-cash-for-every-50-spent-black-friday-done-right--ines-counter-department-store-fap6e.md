# 20% off + $15 Kohl's Cash for every $50 spent = Black Friday done right 🎉
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction was "that's a lot of email." The subject promises a clean formula: 20% off, plus $15 Kohl's Cash for every $50 spent. The body then turns into a scroll of about twenty tiles, each with its own percentage. I see 40%, 65%, 30% and 50% off, plus "+20% off w/coupon" stacked on top. That's the Macy's-style overload I try to avoid. The formula is clear, but I have to hunt for where it applies.
- This is the Kohl's send I've stopped counting. "HOURS LEFT" and "ENDS TONIGHT" on 11/21, "STARTS TODAY" this morning, and now a "done right" celebration. Nothing here tells me why today is different, so I'd likely skim it for the Sephora at Kohl's tile and move on. I wouldn't forward it. I'd probably click once, and only if I were already planning a Black Friday run.

## What's working

- The $15 Kohl's Cash hero at the top states the real hook in a big, readable number.
- The category chips under the hero (Women, Men, Kids, Toys, Home, Beauty) let me jump to my aisle.
- The "Free shipping with a $25 purchase" band is easy to find and costs me nothing to understand.
- The Sephora at Kohl's and Shark beauty tiles show Kohl's knows beauty shoppers exist.
- The Kohl's Cash sweepstakes and the "Earn 2% Kohl's Cash" footer line signal loyalty, which is the only reason I'd use a card there.

## What's weak

- The offers are stacked: "up to 50% off" plus "+20% off w/coupon." On many tiles the text says "Select styles" and "Coupons do not apply." That is exactly the buried fine print I notice.
- Dozens of "Shop Now" buttons give me no single path. Sephora's Cyber Week email at least picks one story.
- The email runs very long, with Klarna, same-day delivery and a Black Friday Deals promo near the bottom. It reads like a circular, not a note to a known customer.
- There's no sign of my name, tier or Kohl's Cash balance. I'm treated like a stranger, while Macy's at least tied its last send to my own confirmation and deadline.
- The hero never shows a real deadline. After the "ENDS TONIGHT" sends this week, I don't believe any urgency.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one mechanic, such as "$15 Kohl's Cash per $50," and show one hero product. Cut the grid to six tiles or fewer.
- 2. Add a true end date and my own Kohl's Cash balance or tier status near the top.
- 3. Say plainly which tiles allow coupons. Don't make me read "Coupons do not apply" tile by tile.
- 4. Give beauty its own short block. Neiman Marcus's "Roundup" format shows one themed edit can feel more curated than 20 percentages.
- 5. Space the sends out. After this week's cadence, a quiet day would make the next one feel more credible.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a table layout with a mobile media query and three tracking and personalization stacks (Salesforce Marketing Cloud, Movable Ink, Adobe Audience Manager). The source has several malformed-markup issues and incomplete alt-text coverage. The compliance and authentication warnings come from header capture, so most can't be confirmed from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No link validation was possible.** 91 tracking links were skipped (the QA tool doesn't probe click-redirect domains). Landing-page status, redirect chains and expiry are unverified.
- **Many tracking domains.** `click.s.kohls.com` (SFMC click and open), `click.chp.kohls.com` (Coherent Path), `mi.kohls.com` (Movable Ink) and `kohls.demdex.net` (Adobe AAM) all appear. That is four third-party-style hosts, so one failure can break a click or an open count.
- **Three open-tracking pixels** fire per open: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/d57489bb-…`, `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif` and `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`. Counts will be inflated or inconsistent between systems.
- **Empty parameter.** The Movable Ink image URL carries `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` with no value. If this is a dynamic "redemption end date", the field may have resolved to blank.
- **Plain-text part is 16,167 of 21,943 characters URLs (74%).** This is the cause of the QA warning. The tracking URLs are long and not shortened.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed markup.**
  - A stray `</custom>` closes nothing, directly after `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?…">`.
  - `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. This is invalid, and parsers may close the head early.
  - `<title></title>` is empty.
  - `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href.
- **Zoom disabled.** The viewport meta is `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0`, which blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Missing alt text on content images.** The QA tool flags these:
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png` and `916d6fa08d185233.png`
  - `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/60479404_1_2x`, `60699446_4_2x` and `60699446_3_2x`
  - If these carry offer or product content, they are unreadable with images blocked or in a screen reader.
- **Tracking pixels.**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/…` and `kohls.demdex.net/event` have no `alt` attribute and no `aria-hidden`.
  - `o.gif` already has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, so its QA flag looks like a false positive.
- **Layout and fonts.**
  - There is `role="presentation"` on the layout tables, a `lang="en"` attribute, and MSO conditional comments.
  - The Google Fonts `@import` has Arial/Helvetica fallbacks, so it degrades acceptably.
  - The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional but the `<html>` tag also carries XML namespaces. This is harmless but inconsistent.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[[…]]`) appear in the visible source. Per-recipient identifiers are resolved: `mi_u=604230016` and `d_sid=13245196`. The source is truncated, so the rest of the body is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. Check the raw message headers from the sending MTA before treating it as a real gap.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from the relay. The From domain `s.kohls.com` is a sending subdomain, so alignment should be verified on the original message.
- **No physical address detected.** The check ran on truncated HTML, and the address is normally in the footer. It is not confirmed missing. Verify against the full source.
- **`<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `referrer=no-referrer`.** These are not compliance issues, but `no-referrer` removes referrer data for web-view opens.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters cannot be verified.** `click.s.kohls.com` hides destinations in the opaque `qs=` blob, and the HTTP probe was skipped.
- **Tracking parameters visible.** The Movable Ink URLs use `mi_ecmp=1026459_20261122` and `email_name=261122_DG_Email_Sun_PM`. The `20261122` date matches the send date. No other campaign identifiers or landing-page alignment are visible in the supplied portion.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Check the raw headers from the sending MTA for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) to settle the three header warnings.
2. Re-run the physical-address check on the full, untruncated HTML.
3. Add meaningful `alt` text to the `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` and `images.am.kohls.com/…` content images. Add `alt=""` to the two decorative pixels that lack it.
4. Remove the stray `</custom>`, move the `itemscope` div out of `<head>`, fill in `<title>`, and drop or fill the empty `rel="shortcut icon"` link.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
6. Investigate the empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` value in the Movable Ink URLs.
7. Probe a sample of the redirect chains through a real-click test to confirm the final landing URL and the UTMs.
8. Consider consolidating the three open pixels to reduce clutter and the inconsistent open counts.
