# Save 15% | Set the scene for summer get-togethers ☀️
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Four emails in eight days from Kohl's — May 8th jewelry for Mom, May 11th summer style, May 15th activewear, and now this — and every single one has come in at 15% off. At this point the discount isn't an offer, it's just the Kohl's price. The creative team rotated the lifestyle hook to summer entertaining, and honestly this one is better thought-out than the activewear blast from yesterday. "Your home gets a summer refresh. Your budget gets a summer break." is actually charming — the best copy I've seen from this brand in this run.
- But I'm a Nordstrom and Saks shopper. I'm at the beauty counter on Saturdays. The primary content of this email is patio sectionals and outdoor furniture, with a women's fashion section buried so far below the fold I almost missed it. Nothing here signals that Kohl's knows who I am. Neiman Marcus's welcome email made me feel like I was on a list. This makes me feel like I'm a zip code.
- Mark it read, move on. Not forwarding this to anyone.

## What's working

- "Your home gets a summer refresh. Your budget gets a summer break." — genuinely good copywriting. First time a Kohl's headline earned a second read from me.
- The summer get-together theme is coherent start to finish — home, patio, hosting, then fashion. Better editorial discipline than the scattered May 11th send.
- Kohl's Cash earn is clearly visible at the bottom. I refuse to use a card without points back, so seeing that mechanic explicitly called out always lands.
- Two concrete offers (up to 40% off home + 15% sitewide) are both visible without hunting — offer clarity is not the problem here.
- Clean render, no layout breaks, each section flows without friction.

## What's weak

- This is the fourth consecutive 15% off in eight days. The offer is now background noise, not a reason to click. The urgency burned off around send number two.
- Home and patio are not my category. I'm in beauty and fashion. The email didn't even try to segment — it feels like a zip code blast, same as the others.
- Every CTA says "Shop Now" — nothing more. Nordstrom would write "Shop Outdoor Entertaining." Macy's at least sometimes writes "Shop the Sale." Kohl's gives me a button that goes nowhere specific.
- The women's fashion section ("Dress for the Occasion") is buried below home, patio product tiles, and the 15% stack. If that's the hook for someone like me, it needed to come up.
- No personalization whatsoever. Sephora got my name wrong (called me Rosie, which is jarring), but they tried. This email doesn't know I exist.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Split home and fashion into separate sends, or lead with whichever category the subscriber has actually browsed.** Asking me to self-sort through patio sectionals to find the dress section is a conversion killer.
- 2. **Retire the 15% offer for at least two sends.** Run a Kohl's Cash bonus event, a category-specific event, or a loyalty-tier exclusive. Four identical percentage-off blasts in eight days trains subscribers to wait for the next one, not click the current one.
- 3. **Make the CTAs earn their real estate.** "Shop Patio & Outdoor," "Shop Summer Hosting," "See What's 40% Off" — any of those tells me where I'm going. "Shop Now" tells me nothing.
- 4. **Elevate the fashion section.** Put it in the second slot, directly below the hero, with a specific CTA like "Dress the Host." Right now it's an afterthought.
- 5. **Add a personalization token or segment signal.** Even "Based on your recent browsing" or a loyalty tier callout would tell me this email was meant for me specifically.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 40% off | Hosting season is officially open`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Get the space (and the outfit) ready — up to 40% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Outdoor furniture, table settings, and the dress to wear while it all comes together.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus Kohl's Cash on every $50 — because the host deserves something too.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "Save 15% | Summer Get-Togethers" (2026-05-16)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is a standard ESP-rendered promotional send with no merge token anomalies or broken links detected. Primary technical concerns are compliance gaps (CAN-SPAM physical address absent, unsubscribe headers not captured), missing alt text on tracking pixels, and unauthenticated delivery attribution due to AgentMail relay stripping or not forwarding authentication headers.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 click-redirect URLs skipped** — all links route through `click.s.kohls.com` or `click.chp.kohls.com`. HTTP probes were skipped as expected for tracking redirects; no broken destination links were confirmed, but destination resolution was not verified.

**Three distinct tracking pixel domains in use:**
- `click.chp.kohls.com` — Coherent Path behavioral pixel
- `mi.kohls.com` — MessageGears open/render pixels (3 instances with `mi_u=604230016`)
- `kohls.demdex.net` — Adobe Audience Manager sync pixel (`d_sid=13245196`)
- `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` — ESP open tracker (malformed closing tag: `</custom>` instead of `</div>`)

**Malformed tag evidence:**
```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt="">
</custom>   <!-- ← invalid closing tag; should be </div> -->
```
This stray `</custom>` is a minor but real HTML error that could cause rendering inconsistencies in strict parsers.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Alt text absent on all tracking pixels** — 10 images flagged:

| Image | Domain | Issue |
|---|---|---|
| `c2dd5842-...` | `click.chp.kohls.com` | Behavioral pixel, no `alt` |
| `o.gif` | `mi.kohls.com` | Open pixel, no `alt` |
| `event` | `kohls.demdex.net` | AAM sync pixel, no `alt` |
| `2a5adc5a...png` through `916d6fa0...png` (7 images) | `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/` | Render pixels, no `alt` |

Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"`. The `o.gif` pixel has `aria-hidden="true"` but no `alt`; the others have neither. The Coherent Path pixel (`c2dd5842`) has no `alt` or `aria-hidden`.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />` blocks pinch-to-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text). Common in email but worth flagging.

**`<title>` is empty** — `<title></title>` provides no context for screen readers that announce document title.

**`<html lang="en">`** — correctly set; no issue.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens detected in the truncated source. `mi_u=604230016` is consistent across all MessageGears pixel calls, indicating a resolved subscriber ID. No `{{...}}` or `%%...%%` placeholders visible.

One render pixel carries a potentially unresolved parameter:
```
mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_pe
```
The `mi_kc_redm_end_dte` value is empty — this may be an unfilled optional merge field or a truncation artifact. If this field is supposed to carry an expiry date, it silently failed.

### 5. Compliance

| Check | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `List-Unsubscribe` header | **Not captured** | AgentMail relay did not forward; unsubscribe mechanism unverifiable at header level |
| `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) | **Not captured** | One-click unsubscribe compliance cannot be confirmed |
| Physical mailing address | **Not detected** | CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid physical postal address in the email body; not found in truncated source |
| `robots` meta | Present | `noindex, nofollow` — correct for transactional email |
| `referrer` meta | Present | `no-referrer` — prevents referrer leakage on click-through |

**CAN-SPAM physical address** is the only actionable compliance issue that cannot be attributed to relay stripping. If the address is present below the truncation point (e.g., footer), this finding is moot — but it was not detected by the automated parser.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM Params)

All 22 links route through `click.s.kohls.com` or `click.chp.kohls.com` redirectors. UTM parameters on destination URLs were not resolvable without following redirects. The `mi_ecmp` parameter (`1012666_2026516`) serves as a campaign identifier within MessageGears but does not replace standard `utm_campaign` / `utm_source` / `utm_medium` attribution for GA4.

If destination URLs do not carry UTM params post-redirect, campaign traffic will be attributed as direct/none in web analytics. This is unverifiable from the source alone but is a common failure mode in ESP redirect chains.
