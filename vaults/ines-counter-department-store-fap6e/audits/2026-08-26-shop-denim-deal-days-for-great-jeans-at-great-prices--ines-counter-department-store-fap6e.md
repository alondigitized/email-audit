# Shop Denim Deal Days for great jeans at great prices 👖
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-26**
## Executive summary

- At least it's not another kid at the lockers — that's an actual improvement over the last week of Kohl's sends I've opened. This one's genuinely about denim, and the "Find an outfit these jeans don't work with. We'll Wait." line is the first bit of copywriting personality Kohl's has shown me all week. But then it's the same playbook underneath: Friends & Family 20%, Kohl's Cash, and by the time I hit "Picked with you in mind" I'm looking at a slow cooker— sorry, a Ninja slushie maker and a mattress topper wedged between the jeans. That's not "picked with you in mind," that's picked with whatever's overstocked in mind.
- I'd scroll past the Women's/Men's/Kids denim block because it's at least on-topic, but I'm not opening my wallet for this. Compare it to Macy's "LAST CHANCE: confirm now" email I looked at — that one had real urgency mechanics even if the costume didn't fit. This one doesn't even try urgency beyond a vague "through Sept. 2." I'd mark as read and move on, not forward it — there's nothing here that feels like it was made for me specifically, just a denim theme slapped onto the same Friends & Family chassis I've seen seven days running.

## What's working

- The "Denim Deal Days" hero has actual visual identity — script font, folded-jeans texture strip — more effort than the flat banners Kohl's usually sends me.
- "Find an outfit these jeans don't work with. We'll Wait." is a genuinely fun line, first bit of brand voice all week.
- Women's/Men's/Kids denim sections are laid out cleanly with real product photography, not stock-y filler.
- Rewards status "KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER · Earn 5% REWARDS" is up top again, so at least they're consistent about acknowledging I'm a member.

## What's weak

- The second half completely abandons denim — a slushie maker, bath towels, and a mattress topper under "Picked with you in mind" is the opposite of personalization, it reads like a random end-cap.
- Clearance banner ("Save up to 70%") competes with the Denim Deal Days offer for hierarchy — I don't know which sale I'm supposed to care about.
- No specific dollar or percent off on the jeans themselves — just "on sale," while Neiman Marcus's Prada Bonnie email got dinged by me for the same sin, no pricing shown at point of interest.
- Same Friends & Family 20% banner and Kohl's Cash mechanic I've now seen four days straight — the cadence fatigue is real.
- This has none of the beauty-counter tone I actually respond to — no tie-in to Sephora at Kohl's, which is odd given I shop that counter.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the household goods block entirely from a denim-themed send, or retitle it honestly as "you might also like" instead of "picked with you in mind" — that phrase actively undercuts trust when the picks are this random.
- 2. Put an actual price cue on the denim hero (e.g., "Women's jeans from $19.99") instead of a bare "Shop Now" button with no anchor price.
- 3. Pick one offer to lead with — either Denim Deal Days or Clearance 70% — not both stacked, it splits attention.
- 4. Vary the Friends & Family creative treatment — I've now seen this same banner four sends running and it's starting to blur together.

- **Subject Alt B:** `New denim, 20% off — Women's, Men's & Kids`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Straight, wide-leg, cropped — find your fit before Sept. 2`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus earn Kohl's Cash on every pair`

- No issues found with malformed/broken URLs in the visible source.
