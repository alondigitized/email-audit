# ▶ We’re treating you to something new
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-29**
## Executive summary

- After four days of Sephora hammering me with "the sale is ENDING, ENDS TODAY, LAST CHANCE" — June 21 through 23 — landing on "We're treating you to something new" is actually a palate cleanser. I exhaled a little. No more manufactured urgency. But then I opened it and... it's a lot. A trial-size sampling offer up top, a Stacking Weekend deal in the middle, "Just Arrived" new launches below that, and an Insider tier callout near the footer. This email doesn't know what it wants to be. It's a new-product story, a loyalty-value story, AND a promotional offer, all fighting for the same scroll.
- The trial-size sampling mechanic in the hero is the thing that actually interests me. I'm a beauty counter regular precisely because I like to touch, test, and then decide. Getting three trial sizes of something I haven't tried yet is exactly the kind of offer that converts me. But Sephora buried the "what" — I can see the "SOMETHING NEW IS HERE" hero and the trial offer, but I can't immediately tell me what brand or product this is about. Compare this to last week's Neiman Marcus "Designer Sale up to 60% off" — that email led with the goods. This one makes me work for it.
- I'd click through for the trial offer specifically, but I wouldn't forward this to a friend. It's too scattered to pitch to anyone else with a clean headline.

## What's working

- **Free same-day delivery banner** catches me immediately at the top — that's a genuine perk I care about and it doesn't cost me anything to claim.
- **"Something New Is Here" is a tonal reset** after the sale panic of June 21–23; the discovery angle actually fits how I shop beauty (I want to find something, not just mark down something I already knew about).
- **Stacking Weekend with a visible 30% offer** is a real number — after vague "treat yourself" language, seeing a concrete discount figure in the middle of the email does land.
- **Beauty Insiders tier callout at the footer** tells me this email knows I'm a member, and that still matters to me; I don't use a card without points back, so loyalty visibility keeps me engaged.
- **No render bugs, clean layout** — images load, text isn't overlapping, nothing looks broken.

## What's weak

- **The subject "We're treating you to something new" tells me nothing** — no brand, no category, no offer. After Neiman Marcus's "up to 60% off during our Designer Sale" last week, which told me everything I needed to know from the subject line alone, this feels lazy.
- **The hero product identity is unclear** — I can see a trial-size sampling mechanic but I can't immediately identify the brand or hero product without squinting; that's a miss for someone like me who moves fast through my inbox.
- **Too many sections competing for attention** — trial-size hero, product grid, Stacking Weekend, Spot It Shop It, Just Arrived, Insiders callout. This is Macy's-level visual overload, and I expect cleaner from Sephora. Even the June 22 "chapter closing" email had a tighter single focus.
- **"Treating you" copy reads condescending** — I'm 47, I've been a Sephora Insider for years; I don't need to be told I'm being "treated." Ulta does this too and it always reads like I'm five years old at a birthday party.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Name the hero product or brand in the subject** — if this is a new brand launch or a specific trial-size set, lead with that. "3 free samples with your next order" or "New from [Brand]: try it free" converts people like me.
- 2. **Collapse to two sections max** — hero/trial offer and one supporting section (Stacking Weekend OR Just Arrived, not both). The multi-section scroll punishes mobile readers and dilutes the message.
- 3. **Make the trial-size CTA copy specific** — "SHOP NOW" is too generic. "Choose Your 3 Samples" or "Pick Your Trial Set" tells me exactly what clicking does.
- 4. **Move the Stacking Weekend 30% offer higher** — a concrete discount number is the most motivating thing in this email and it's buried past the fold.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 3 free samples are ready — pick now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New in: try before you buy (free samples inside)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Choose any 3 trial sizes on your next order. Plus, Stacking Weekend starts now.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free trial sizes + 30% stacking deals — this week only.`
