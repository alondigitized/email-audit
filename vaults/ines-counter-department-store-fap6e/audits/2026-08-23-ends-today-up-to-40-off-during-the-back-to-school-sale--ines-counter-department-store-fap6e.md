# ⌛ENDS TODAY: Up to 40% off during the Back-to-School Sale 🎒
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-23**
## Executive summary

- Same locker hallway, same kid, same "Back-to-School Sale up to 40% off" banner Kohl's has now run five days straight — 8/19, 8/20, 8/21, 8/22 twice, and now today. At this point I could draw that kid from memory. But I'll give Kohl's this: buried about a third of the way down, there's a "Beauty up to 50% off Sephora at Kohl's" tile and, further down, a black "Sephora + Kohl's: Beauty Insiders Get 25% off Select Brands" banner — that's the first time all week this brand has actually spoken my language.
- The rest is a wall of category tiles — bras, panties, towels, a coffee maker, jeans — stacked like a circular you'd get in a Sunday paper, not a curated email. Compare that to Sephora's App Fest send I looked at, which at least led with one clear hero product even if it was a "who?" celebrity brand. Kohl's doesn't even try for a hero here beyond the kid — it's just tile after tile after tile.
- I'd scroll straight to the Sephora Beauty Insider line, note the 25% off, and close the email. I would not forward this — there's nothing here worth sharing, and I already have Kohl's Cash fatigue from this week's giveaway send.

## What's working

- The Sephora + Kohl's Beauty Insider 25% off banner is the one thing in this whole email built for someone like me — clean black-and-gold treatment, product icons, a real segmented offer.
- "ENDS TODAY" urgency on the hero band feels credible since it's paired with a real date (through August 23) rather than vague fear-of-missing-out language.
- Kohl's Cash callout ($10 for every $50 spent) is a genuine loyalty mechanic and I do actually track this — Kohl's is consistent about surfacing it, unlike Neiman's Fashion Passport send that never mentioned points at all.
- Pricing is specific everywhere (bras $19.99, dresses up to 30% off) — no vague "shop now" mystery pricing.

## What's weak

- Fifth straight day with a kid at school lockers as the hero — I have no kids in school, so that entire top third is dead space to me again, same complaint as 8/19 through 8/22.
- The layout is an undifferentiated grid of 10+ unrelated categories (towels, sneakers, tees, dresses, bras, panties, a coffee maker, jeans, mattress topper) — nothing tells my eye where to look first besides "everything is on sale."
- The beauty offer, the only genuinely relevant content, is sandwiched between a "So-Back Sweepstakes" banner and a "Sew Annual" bra sale — it needed top billing, not a mid-scroll tile.
- "Picked with you in mind (Your great taste is easy)" is a lie of a headline sitting over a coffee maker, bath towels, jeans, and a mattress topper — that's not "picked for me," that's just more inventory.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the Sephora Beauty Insider 25% off banner as the hero, not a mid-scroll afterthought — that's the one audience-fit signal in the whole send, and Sephora's own emails know to put beauty first.
- 2. Retire the locker-hallway kid creative — five sends in a row is creative fatigue even for parents; for childless Gen X shoppers like me it's five straight days of irrelevant real estate.
- 3. Cut the tile count. Pick 4-5 categories max and let them breathe instead of the current circular-ad grid.
- 4. Drop the "Picked with you in mind" headline unless there's actual personalization behind it — right now it reads as false advertising over a mattress topper.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Beauty Insiders: 25% off Sephora brands ends today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: up to 40% off + 25% off beauty at Kohl's`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus earn Kohl's Cash on every purchase, in-store and online`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sephora at Kohl's beauty deals end tonight — don't miss out`

## Technical Audit

Technical Audit — Kohl's "ENDS TODAY: Back-to-School Sale" Email

**1. Technical Summary**
Standard Kohl's/Cheetah Digital (CoherentPath) transactional template with heavy multi-vendor tracking (Kohls click redirects, mi.kohls.com opens, Adobe demdex). QA pass rate is 43%, driven mainly by missing compliance headers and alt-text gaps.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 78 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP prober (expected behavior for `click.chp.kohls.com`, `click.s.kohls.com` redirect domains — not itself a defect, but means link-destination validity is unverified in this pass).
- Open/beacon tracking present via three separate systems: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` (1x1 img), `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...` and `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/...` (open pixels), and `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` (open pixel), plus Adobe Audience Manager (`kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`). No functional issue, but confirms redundant/layered tracking infrastructure.
- Malformed markup: `<img ... width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>` — stray closing `</custom>` tag with no matching opening tag, inside the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` pixel block. This is invalid HTML and should be removed.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- 8 images flagged with missing `alt` text (QA findings), all tracking/beacon pixels (`o.gif`, `event`, and multiple `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png`). Low practical impact since these are 1x1 invisible pixels, but screen readers/HTML validators will still flag them — recommend `alt=""` (already present on some) applied consistently and `role="presentation"` where missing.
- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` present — intentional for transactional/promo email, no issue.
- Uses legacy HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype with MSO conditional comments and media queries for responsive stacking (`max-width: 500px`) — standard email-client-compatibility pattern, no issues found.
- Google Fonts (`Roboto`, `Montserrat`) loaded via `<link>` and `@import` — most email clients (Outlook desktop, Gmail app) strip these and fall back to the `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` stack declared on `<body>`; not a bug, but font fallback should be assumed as the actual rendered experience for most recipients.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- No unresolved merge tokens (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) found in the truncated source.
- Tracking URLs are recipient-specific (`mi_mid=01a03035-6200-7000-9f98-22edeea542a3`, `mi_cid=67791fba63db790b`, `cp_cid=8ba359f132e3c109`) — personalization is being carried via query params/tracking IDs rather than inline merge tags. No issues found.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** (QA WARN) — could be a gap in the actual email, or an artifact of the AgentMail relay not surfacing/preserving the header. Cannot confirm from HTML body alone since unsubscribe headers live in message headers, not HTML source.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found** (RFC 8058, one-click unsubscribe) — same caveat as above.
- **No physical mailing address detected** — flagged by QA scan of the truncated HTML; the source provided cuts off before the footer, so this cannot be independently confirmed as a true absence vs. truncation artifact. Flag as unconfirmed pending full-source review.
- **Authentication-Results header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC status unknown; likely an AgentMail relay capture gap rather than a sender-side authentication failure, but cannot be confirmed either way from available data.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Beacon URLs carry `mi_ecmp=1020372_2026823` and `email_name=260823_DG_Email_Sunday` campaign identifiers — consistent internal campaign tagging.
- No standard `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` query params observed on the visible tracking/redirect links; Kohl's appears to rely on its own `mi_*`/`cp_*` parameter scheme (Cheetah Digital / CoherentPath platform) instead of GA-style UTMs. This is a platform convention, not a defect, but means UTM-based attribution tooling on the landing-page side won't pick these up — worth noting only if UTM continuity is an expected requirement.
- Landing page destinations were not resolved (all 78 links skipped per QA), so actual landing-page alignment cannot be verified in this pass.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Remove the stray `</custom>` closing tag in the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` pixel block — invalid HTML.
2. Add `alt=""` consistently to the remaining tracking pixel `<img>` tags flagged by QA (8 instances).
3. Confirm List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post / Authentication-Results header presence directly from raw SMTP headers (not just the relay capture) to rule out a relay-side capture gap vs. an actual compliance gap.
4. Confirm presence of a physical mailing address in the full (non-truncated) footer HTML.
5. If UTM-based attribution is required for landing-page analytics, consider appending standard `utm_*` params alongside the existing `mi_*`/`cp_*` scheme.
