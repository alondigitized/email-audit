# Ends today: Earn a $100–$1,250 gift card with your purchase
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**
## Executive summary

- This is the third gift-card ladder I've had from Neiman's in three days. Yesterday's "Don't miss out on a $100–$1,250 gift card" and this one are nearly the same email. The only change is the "Ends today" tag. The plum hero is handsome and the spend/earn table reads in about three seconds. I respect a brand that shows me the math up front. But I've already seen it.
- The offer isn't built for me. It pays out in a gift card, not points on my card, and I don't buy anything for the purpose of a future gift card. The fine print is long. Sale and reduced-price items don't count, and returns can claw the card back. The hero does say "Includes Beauty & Fragrance," which is the one line that made me look twice. Then the grid is all clothes and shoes: Moncler, Zimmermann, Louboutin, Jimmy Choo. There's not one lipstick or serum in it.
- Would I click? Probably not today. This is the tenth or eleventh Neiman's email in a week, and I'm numb to it. I'd mark it read. I wouldn't forward it, except maybe to a friend who's been eyeing a Moncler jacket.

## What's working

- The spend/earn table is clear: $500 gets $100 back, up to $5,000 for $1,250. No hunting for the mechanics.
- "Includes Beauty & Fragrance" in the hero speaks to me directly, since beauty is where I shop there.
- The designer grid is tidy and uncluttered, with each brand labeled. It feels like Neiman's, not a flyer. Macy's would have crammed six more banners above this.
- The code **OURGIFT** and the deadline (10/20 at 10 PM CT) are in the hero and the fine print, so the urgency is believable.

## What's weak

- It's nearly a repeat of yesterday's gift-card email, and the "First Call" and Tom Ford sends before it. I'm getting hammered with near-duplicates.
- "Includes Beauty & Fragrance" is small, and then there's no beauty in the grid. The email promises something it doesn't show.
- The exclusions are heavy. Sale items don't count, only pre-shipping and pre-tax spend counts, the card arrives in 8 weeks, and returns can forfeit it. That's a lot to bury under a "buy more, earn more" headline.
- The 9-tile grid links to nothing specific that I can tell, and the six category buttons are generic. Nothing says "start here."
- There's no sign they know me. There's no tier status, no points mention, and nothing about my beauty purchases.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one or two beauty products in the grid, such as La Mer, Tom Ford or Chanel, and give them a "counts toward your gift card" tag. That would be the single biggest lift for me.
- 2. Cut the frequency. Don't send me the same ladder three days running. Send "Ends today" only to people who haven't opened the first two.
- 3. Pull a two-line summary of the exclusions up under the table: "Regular-price items only. Card arrives in 8 weeks."
- 4. Show my tier status or point balance, so it feels like a message to a known customer.
- 5. Replace the six generic buttons with one dominant CTA, like "Shop beauty & fragrance."

- **Subject Alt B:** `Tonight 10 PM CT: your gift card ladder closes`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $500, get $100. Regular-price beauty and fragrance count. Code OURGIFT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Ends at 10 PM CT. Here's what qualifies, and what doesn't.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Neiman Marcus, "Ends today: Earn a $100–$1,250 gift card with your purchase"

**Scope note:** The HTML source was truncated inside the `<style>` block. The body, links, footer and unsubscribe markup were not available, so I only flag what the visible source and QA data support.

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible source is a standard table-based XHTML Transitional email with a media-query-driven responsive layout. The QA run found 0 hard issues and 3 warnings, all of them header-capture gaps. The one confirmed defect is that internal ESP/CRM merge logic ships in the delivered HTML.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 30 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so redirect health, destination status codes and link-level UTMs are **unverified**.
- No broken links are confirmed. The link markup was truncated, so I can't audit `href` values, empty hrefs or `mailto:` handling.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on clients that honor it. This conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **No document language:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` attribute, so screen readers can't select the right pronunciation rules.
- **Weak `<title>`:** `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` is brand-only and has a trailing space. It does not reflect the subject line.
- **Exact-match class selectors:** The mobile rules use `*[class=contenttable]`, `*[class=mobile]`, `*[class=hide]` and similar. These match only when `class` is exactly that string, so any element with a second class silently loses its mobile override.
- **Hamburger menu:** The mobile nav uses a `:checked` checkbox hack (`.navbar-checkbox:checked~.hamburger`). It is unsupported in Gmail and several other clients. I can't tell from the truncated source whether a fallback exists.
- **Leftover legacy CSS:** `.ReadMsgBody`, `.ExternalClass` and `-webkit-animation: bugfix` are Outlook.com and iOS 9-era hacks that add weight without benefit.
- **Comments before the DOCTYPE:** The ESP comment block precedes `<!DOCTYPE>`. Most modern clients tolerate this, but some parsers can fall into quirks mode.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Internal logic exposed in the sent HTML:** The pre-DOCTYPE comment block was not stripped at send. It contains `REQUIRED variables: @email … @base_refid … @refid = CONCAT(@base_refid,"") … Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", "EMAIL_ID", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email) … Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "uEm", …) … @cmdid … CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW --> <!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->`. This leaks internal data extension names (`NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`), field names (`EMAIL_ID`, `uEm`, `cmdid`) and the hashing approach to anyone who views source. It also shows a template-hygiene failure: the "choose one scenario" instruction was left in unresolved.
- **No unresolved tokens in the visible portion.** I can't check the truncated remainder.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Auth Headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** (WARN).
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found** (WARN).
- **`Authentication-Results` not found** (WARN). SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown.
- The QA tool itself attributes all three to the AgentMail relay stripping or not capturing headers, so these are **likely capture artifacts, not confirmed sender defects**. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so verify against the raw message at the sending ESP or in a direct-inbox test.
- The footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated section and are **unverified**.
- The `From` address is `NeimanMarcus@e.neimanmarcus.com`, a dedicated subdomain, which is good practice for reputation isolation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Not assessable.** The source shows a `@refid` and `@base_refid` tracking scheme in the comments, which suggests campaign IDs are appended to links. The actual UTM or `refid` parameters and landing URLs were truncated, and all 30 tracking links were unprobed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Strip the ESP/CRM comment block before send** (pre-DOCTYPE comment). Use AMPscript or SFMC comment syntax (`%%[ ]%%` blocks, or `<!--- --->`-style server-side comments) so it never renders to the recipient. Resolve the "CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO" instruction in the template.
2. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag, and add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
3. **Make the `<title>` meaningful**, for example the subject line, and trim the trailing space.
4. **Confirm unsubscribe headers and authentication on the raw message.** Check for `List-Unsubscribe` plus `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and for SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment on `e.neimanmarcus.com`. Do this outside the AgentMail relay.
5. **Rerun the audit with the full HTML.** The footer, unsubscribe link, postal address, alt text, link `href`s and UTMs can't be audited from this truncation.
6. **Switch the mobile selectors** from `*[class=x]` to `.x` (or `[class~=x]`) so multi-class elements still pick up the overrides, and confirm the hamburger menu has a fallback for Gmail.
