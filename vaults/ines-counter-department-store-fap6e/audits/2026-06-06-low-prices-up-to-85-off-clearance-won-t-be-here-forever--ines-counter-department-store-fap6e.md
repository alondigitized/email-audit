# LOW PRICES + up to 85% off clearance won't be here forever 👀
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-06**
## Executive summary

- Sixteen. That's where my Kohl's counter sits now — sixteen emails in roughly two weeks — and this one has the audacity to add "won't be here forever" to the exact same 85% off clearance promise from the June 1 send. I remember June 1. The subject literally said "up to 85% off clearance." Five days later you're staging a faux deadline on the same number? That's not urgency. That's a tell.
- I opened the email and found the full Kohl's department-store scroll: outdoor furniture at 50% off, women's intimates, a $19.99 beauty block that turned out to be mass personal care (think CVS aisle, not Lancôme counter), kids' clothing, more 40% off sections, and a Kohl's Cash close at the bottom. Every section is competing at full volume. Neiman Marcus sent me a vacation mood board last week with zero offer — frustrating for different reasons — but at least that was one coherent story. This is six stories shouted simultaneously.
- The Kohl's Cash mention at the end kept me from closing immediately. That's the only thing keeping this brand in my inbox at all. But I would not click, would not buy, and would not forward this to a single person I respect.

## What's working

- **Kohl's Cash close is visible** — as someone who refuses to buy without points or earnings back, this is the one brand-loyalty signal that makes me pause before deleting.
- **85% is a real, concrete number** in the hero — beats Neiman Marcus's zero-offer editorial send from earlier this week on pure information value.
- **No render bugs** — the email loads cleanly, every section and image intact, which is more than can be said for some of the broken renders I've gotten from smaller brands this month.
- **Clear visual hierarchy at the top** — LOW PRICES + the clearance badge hit first, which is correct sequencing even if the offer itself is tired.

## What's weak

- **The urgency is manufactured and I can see the seams.** June 1 said "85% off clearance." June 6 says "85% off clearance won't be here forever." Adding a vague threat to an unchanged claim is not urgency — it's a tell that nothing has changed.
- **ALL CAPS in the subject line is a spam signal and just rude.** Macy's doesn't shout in their subject lines when they're running 25% off storewide. Neither does Saks. There is no reason to do this.
- **The beauty section is mass personal care, not beauty.** A $19.99 block of what appears to be body wash and soap is not a counter event. If you want my attention in the beauty aisle, you need a brand name I recognize, a specific product, and a reason to care beyond a price point.
- **Sixteen emails in two weeks.** This is inbox abuse. I'm actively filing this sender into the "Sunday scroll only" category. No email, however well-executed, can survive this cadence.
- **The email has no editorial point of view.** Six categories at full volume with no hierarchy of intent — it reads as a mailer, not a message.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut to one story per send.** Clearance OR Kohl's Cash event OR a category launch. Not all of them at once. One thing done with intention beats six things done at volume.
- 2. **Put a real date on the urgency.** "Clearance ends Sunday" is honest and specific. "Won't be here forever" is the email equivalent of a shrug.
- 3. **Give the beauty section a reason to exist.** Name a brand, show one product, link to beauty clearance specifically — not a generic $19.99 personal care cluster buried in block four.
- 4. **Move Kohl's Cash higher.** It's the one loyalty signal that keeps me subscribed. Burying it in the footer is leaving the best conversion tool on the table.
- 5. **Kill the ALL CAPS subject prefix.** Lowercase works. We are adults.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Clearance ends Sunday — up to 85% off + earn Kohl's Cash`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 85% off clearance + $10 Kohl's Cash before the weekend's over`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Outdoor, intimates, beauty — real clearance prices with a real end date.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop clearance + earn $10 Kohl's Cash. This one closes Sunday.`

### 1. Technical Summary

Standard retail ESP build (Coherent Path / MessageIgnite stack) with no critical rendering blockers. Primary concerns are missing compliance signals in captured headers and pervasive missing `alt` text on tracking pixels and content images.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 click-redirect links skipped by QA probe** — all route through `click.s.kohls.com` (ESP click-tracker). Not a bug, but means destination URL health is unverified for this send.

**Three distinct tracking pixel systems active simultaneously:**
- `click.chp.kohls.com` — Coherent Path open pixel
- `mi.kohls.com` — MessageIgnite open/render pixels (`o.gif`, `rp/*.png`)
- `kohls.demdex.net` — Adobe Audience Manager sync pixel (`d_sid=13245196`)
- `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` — ESP open pixel (Salesforce Marketing Cloud pattern)

No broken pixel URLs detected. No issues with link structure itself, but the `<custom>` tag at line:

```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>
```

contains an invalid `</custom>` closing tag with no matching open tag — this is malformed HTML and will generate a parse error in strict validators, though most email clients will silently ignore it.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Alt text missing on 10+ images** per QA, including non-decorative content images:

| Image | URL Pattern | Issue |
|---|---|---|
| Open pixel | `click.chp.kohls.com/o/644bc273...` | Missing `alt` |
| Render pixel | `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...o.gif` | Missing `alt` |
| AAM sync | `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=...` | Missing `alt` |
| Content images | `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5a...png` (×5+) | Missing `alt` |
| Product image | `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/54462797_2s_2x` | Missing `alt` |

Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` (already present on the `o.gif` pixel — that one is correct). Content/product images need descriptive `alt` text for screen readers and images-off rendering.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — disables pinch-to-zoom on mobile. Accessibility anti-pattern; violates WCAG 1.4.4.

**Google Fonts loaded via three mechanisms** (two `<link>` tags + two `@import` rules for the same families). Redundant — doubles font load requests in clients that support external CSS. Non-blocking but wasteful.

**`<title>` element is empty** — `<title></title>`. Most email clients ignore this, but it surfaces in some accessibility trees and tab labels.

**HTML 4.01 Transitional DOCTYPE** — expected for broad email client compatibility; not a bug, noted for context.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)

**[WARN] List-Unsubscribe header absent** from captured headers. CAN-SPAM does not mandate this header, but Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender requirements (enforced since 2024) require it for senders above 5K/day threshold. Kohl's volume almost certainly triggers this requirement. May be a relay-capture gap rather than a true omission — needs verification against raw SMTP headers.

**[WARN] List-Unsubscribe-Post absent** — RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe. Same caveat: may be stripped by AgentMail relay. Gmail enforces this for bulk senders.

**[WARN] No physical mailing address detected in captured HTML** — CAN-SPAM §7(a)(3) requires a valid physical postal address. The truncated source does not include it; likely present in the footer (below truncation). Needs confirmation against full HTML.

**[WARN] SPF/DKIM/DMARC authentication status unknown** — `Authentication-Results` header not captured. Sending domain `s.kohls.com` should be checked via DNS for DMARC policy alignment. Not confirmable from available data.
