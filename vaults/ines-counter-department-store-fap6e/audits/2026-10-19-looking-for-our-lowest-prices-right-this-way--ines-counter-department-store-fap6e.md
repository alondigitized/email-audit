# Looking for our LOWEST prices? Right this way 👉
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction is relief, then fatigue. The "ENDS TONIGHT" banner stack from the 10/18 send ("40% off ends TONIGHT ⏳") is gone. This one opens with a clean maroon "Lowest Prices of the Season" header and a clearance tile saying "up to 85% off after an extra 50% off". I'm still a Kohl's Rewards member, and the "Status: Kohl's Rewards Member | Earn: 5% Rewards" bar at the top is a nice nod to that. But this is the fifth Kohl's email since the 10/8 "Ends Oct. 18" run, and the new "Lowest Prices" theme is just a different coat of paint on the same discount machine.
- Below the header it turns into a catalog. I count ten maroon-and-white modules: LEGO, activewear, Nike-style sneakers, a slow cooker, bras, underwear, towels, boots, sweaters, and kids' tees. Every one is labeled "Lowest Prices of the Season" and "Shop Now." There's no hero and no point of view. Sephora's "These hot sets + you" email was busy too, but it at least led with one idea.
- Would I click? Maybe the boots at $17.99, or the sweaters. I wouldn't forward it. I'd skim it, shrug, and go back to the counter at Nordstrom or Macy's, where the beauty offers actually mean something to me.

## What's working

- The Rewards status bar ("Kohl's Rewards Member, Earn: 5% Rewards") at the top treats me like a known customer. Neiman's didn't do that in its New Arrivals send.
- The price points are specific and big: "Starting at 7.79," "59.99," "19.99," "5/$30." Reg. prices sit right beside them, so I can see the savings without doing math.
- The modules are consistent. Maroon block, white headline, product photo and a Shop Now button make it easy to scan on a phone.
- The sale window is stated plainly in the header: "Select styles on sale now through Oct. 25." That's honest and calm after last week's shouting.
- The "Shop now. Decide later. Free returns through January 31, 2027" strip is a real reassurance. I return at the brand event, so I notice return terms.

## What's weak

- Nothing leads. Ten equal modules mean the eye has no landing spot, and the 85% clearance badge competes with "Lowest Prices" for the top billing.
- The "Shop Now" buttons are all the same, generic and small. Macy's at least gave me a verb and a deadline in "LAST CHANCE: confirm now."
- Nothing here is beauty, and nothing is a designer or event moment for a woman my age who cross-shops Saks. The Ninja drink maker, LEGO and kids' tees say household inventory, not me.
- "Select styles," "Coupons do not apply to select brands," and "Coupons do not apply to select items" repeat all the way down. That's the fine-print hedging I notice, and the dense legal block at the bottom is tiny.
- The cadence is exhausting. I got four "Ends Oct. 18" Kohl's emails, and now a "lowest prices" reset the day after. That's a lot of urgency for a brand that isn't telling me what's new.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one hero. Pick the best offer, such as the clearance 85% or the boots, and give it a full-width block. Cut the grid from ten modules to four or five.
- 2. Make the loyalty angle the offer. Put "You have X Kohl's Cash/Rewards" up top instead of a generic "earn Kohl's Cash" banner. Sephora Insider does this better.
- 3. Make the CTAs specific: "Shop boots from $17.99" instead of "Shop Now."
- 4. Cut the cadence. One reset email at the start of a sale window, not five sends of the same promo.
- 5. Add a beauty or women's-focused module. Kohl's sells Sephora at Kohl's, and I didn't see it in the rendered email.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The source is a standard ESP-built (Salesforce Marketing Cloud and Movable Ink) email with five tracking beacons, 71 wrapped links, and some malformed head markup. The automated compliance and authentication warnings come from the relay, so they are unconfirmed. The HTML was truncated, so the footer and most links could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Links not verifiable:** 71 links were skipped as tracking redirects. All sampled hrefs use opaque `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7ImQi...` tokens, so destinations and 404/redirect behavior are unconfirmed.
- **Malformed markup after a pixel:** `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?YSL4CO5TQYBU7PZKBIAZ4DLFWQ.60264&d=60264&bmt=0" ...></custom>` has a stray closing `</custom>` tag with no opener.
- **Five beacons:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/3f8f468a-...` sits outside any table, directly under `<body>`.
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager)
  - Movable Ink `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` render images
- **Duplicate open tracking:** three independent open pixels will inflate or skew open metrics. This is a data-quality concern, not a defect.
- **Subscriber ID in image URLs:** `mi_u=604230016` appears in every Movable Ink image URL. Anyone who forwards the email or whose client proxies the images exposes it.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Empty icon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href.
- **Invalid head content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. A `<div>` is invalid there, and parsers will implicitly close the head. Gmail and most clients ignore schema.org microdata in this form.
- **Mixed doctype and syntax:** the HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is paired with `xmlns:` attributes and XHTML-style `/>` self-closing tags.
- **Content images missing alt (QA-confirmed):**
  - Product images `58966978_1sa_2x`, `_2sa_2x`, `_11sa_2x` and `_14sa_2x` (`images.am.kohls.com`).
  - Movable Ink images `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png` and `916d6fa08d185233.png`.
  - With images blocked or read by a screen reader, these render as empty content. If they carry offers, that content is inaccessible.
- **Likely false positives in the QA alt check:** `o.gif` and `open.aspx` already have `alt=""`. `o.gif` also has `aria-hidden="true"`, which is correct for decorative pixels. The `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` and `demdex.net/event` pixels have no `alt` attribute at all, so adding `alt=""` there is a valid fix.
- **Missing table roles:** the pixel wrapper tables (`width="1" height="1"`, and the AAM table) lack `role="presentation"`. The main layout tables have it.
- **Web fonts:** Google Fonts `@import` and `<link>` are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, and the stack falls back to `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`. No issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unrendered tokens:** none were visible in the available source, such as `%%...%%`, `{{...}}` or `[FirstName]`.
- **Empty dynamic field:** one Movable Ink URL contains `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_p...`, so the Kohl's Cash redemption end date is empty in this send. This may be expected for this recipient. If that image renders Kohl's Cash dates, it could show a blank or default.
- **Coverage:** the truncated source prevents a complete check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unconfirmed:** the three QA warnings are the relay dropping headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`) and `Authentication-Results`, which the QA output itself says is expected via AgentMail.
- **Physical address:** the footer was truncated and the "No physical address detected" check may have missed it, so this is not confirmed. Kohl's is a major sender on `s.kohls.com`, so I would not infer a violation.
- **Verification needed:** check the raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `s.kohls.com`), and the footer's unsubscribe link and postal address.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs not verifiable:** every sampled link is wrapped in `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=...`, and no `utm_*` parameters appear in the visible source. Final landing URLs would need a redirect trace, which the probe skipped.
- **Campaign IDs consistent:** the Movable Ink images carry `email_name=261019_DG_Email_Mon_PM` and `mi_ecmp=1024151_20261019`. Both match the send date, 2026-10-19.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the head markup:** remove the stray `</custom>`, move the `itemscope` div into `<body>` or use JSON-LD, set a real `<title>`, and drop the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon" href="">`.
2. **Restore zoom:** remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
3. **Add alt text:** give the four product images and seven Movable Ink images descriptive `alt`. Add `alt=""` to the `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` and `demdex.net/event` pixels.
4. **Re-run QA on the raw message:** capture the original `.eml` with full headers, an untruncated body, and redirect-chain tracing of the 71 links. Use it to confirm the compliance and UTM items above.
5. **Check the Kohl's Cash dates:** inspect the render with the empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` parameter to confirm it is intended.
6. **Review the beacon stack:** confirm all three open-tracking pixels and the demdex beacon are needed.
