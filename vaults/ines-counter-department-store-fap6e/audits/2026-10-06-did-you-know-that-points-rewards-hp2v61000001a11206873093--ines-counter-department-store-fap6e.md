# Did you know that points = rewards?
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction was "Sephora, you've emailed me six times this week." The subject asks if I *knew* that points equal rewards, which is a bit patronizing for someone who has been a Rouge-level member for years. Then I opened it and found something better than the subject promised. This is a Rewards Bazaar, with a real catalog of what my points buy and how many are left of each item.
- The top is a green "Don't Miss Out on up to 500 Points" Beauty Insider Challenge banner. Under it is a big "October rewards are here!" hero with a SHOP NOW button. Below that come a Laura Mercier brand spotlight and a 750+ point rewards grid, with a point cost and a "X available now" count under every item. I'd click, because I actually want to see whether the caviar clutch is worth 4,000 points. I'd probably not forward it, though. It's a long scroll and it's aimed at people already in the program.

## What's working

- The Rewards Bazaar is a useful email. Each reward lists its point price and a scarcity line like "12 available now" or "1 available now." That is real information, and it respects my time.
- The Laura Mercier spotlight lands for me. Setting powder, a brow and cheek set, and a branded makeup bag are the kind of prestige-brand rewards I'd spend points on. Nordstrom's beauty events don't give me this tier of access.
- The layout is clean and easy to scan. Products sit on light backgrounds with the cost right under them, so nothing shouts. Compare that to Kohl's sixth Deal Days siren this week.
- There's a Beauty Insider tier bar near the bottom (INSIDER / VIB / ROUGE) and a "Rewards Bazaar" link. It reminds me where I stand without lecturing.

## What's weak

- The subject line is condescending and vague. "Did you know that points = rewards?" is a tagline, not a reason to open. My own 10/6 read of the "2X points… ends tomorrow 🚨" send had a clear deadline. This one has none.
- There are two competing asks at the top: "Join the Challenge" for up to 500 points, and "Shop Now" for rewards. I can't tell which one matters most.
- "Up to 500 points" is a hedge. I'd have to complete tasks I can't see, and the fine print below is a wall of tiny gray type I'd need to zoom to read.
- Nothing here is addressed to me. There's no point balance, no tier name and no "you have X points." Neiman Marcus's spend ladder ($200 off, up to $1,000+) at least gave me a number I could do math on.
- It's another send in a very crowded week. After SAYPOINTS, the 10/5 App Early Access send and the 10/4 hair last call, I'm tired.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put my own point balance and tier in the hero ("You have 1,240 points. Here's what they get you"). That would lift this email more than any other change.
- 2. Pick one top CTA. Make "Shop Rewards" the primary button and drop the Challenge lower, or send it separately.
- 3. Add a real deadline to the Bazaar. Say when October rewards stop, so I have a reason to act now.
- 4. Sort the rewards by point tier (250 / 500 / 750+), with a "within reach for you" row.
- 5. Cut the fine print down, and don't run another points send in the next 48 hours.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your October rewards just dropped`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Laura Mercier for 350 points. 12 left.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New Rewards Bazaar picks, with limited quantities, from 250 points.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `See what your points buy before the best ones are gone.`
