# ENDS TONIGHT: 40% off is on its way out ...
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-22**
## Executive summary

- Sixty-five. Or four. I stopped counting exactly when Kohl's stopped meaning what they said. Yesterday this sale was "EXTENDED" — I gave that one an 8, mostly on the novelty of them admitting the extension openly. Today it's "ENDS TONIGHT." My thumb knows this is a lie. The clearance rate doesn't lie. That 85% is a real number, and if they'd led with it instead of the 40% they've been circling for a week, I might actually have scrolled to the bottom with intent.
- What kills me is the layout. There are three separate discount offers stacked in this email — 40% off the main event, up to 50% on activewear, 85% clearance — and each one wants to be the hero. None of them get to be. It's the same problem I have with the Macy's "LAST CHANCE" email I reviewed last week: dress urgency up in too many costumes and it starts looking like a rack of mismatched separates. Compare that to how Neiman Marcus ran "Fashion's power pieces" — one editorial angle, one clear place to land. Different price point, yes. But the discipline translates down-market.
- I'll mark this read. I won't click, and I won't forward. If a friend asked me what was on sale at Kohl's right now, I genuinely could not tell her, because the answer appears to be "everything, tonight, forever."

## What's working

- **Kohl's Cash is front and center.** The rewards strip in the header and the "Plus, earn Kohl's Cash!" secondary hook are where my eye stops — I won't spend a dollar without points back, so that framing has real pull.
- **85% clearance is a credible number.** That section stops the scroll. It reads like actual endgame inventory pricing, not a manufactured "sale."
- **No broken images, no render garbage.** Kohl's emails always render clean on my phone — no overlapping text, no missing hero. Table stakes, but they clear them.
- **"Even the ones who never sit still."** That line under the family section is the one moment of actual voice in the whole email. A copywriter earned their rate there.
- **Brand logos in the activewear tier.** Seeing FLX and what looks like Under Armour gives me a brand anchor — I know what I'm shopping, not just a generic "activewear."

## What's weak

- **"ENDS TONIGHT" has no credibility left.** Yesterday it was extended. The day before it was "going, going." This deadline is crying wolf for the fourth time in a week.
- **Three competing offers kill the visual hierarchy.** The eye lands on 40% off, then immediately gets pulled to 50% off, then 85%. I can't click what I can't prioritize.
- **Zero beauty content.** I'm at the Clinique counter or browsing fragrance at Nordstrom as often as I'm on Kohl's. Macy's always carves beauty into its own panel. Sephora built their whole model on it. Kohl's carries real beauty brands — they just didn't invite them to this email.
- **"Shop Now" on every single CTA.** Neiman Marcus gives me a destination. Kohl's gives me a verb and a wish.
- **The barcode Wallet section at the bottom is friction.** If the sale is live, I shouldn't have to scan a code to access it. That section belongs in a dedicated loyalty email, not stacked under three competing offer banners.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the 85% clearance, not the 40%.** It's the most credible offer and the most specific. A "CLEARANCE" hero email once a quarter works harder than "40% off" for the fifth time this week.
- 2. **Kill "Shop Now" everywhere.** Replace with destination-specific copy: "Shop Clearance," "Shop Activewear," "Use Your Kohl's Cash."
- 3. **Add one beauty module.** A single skincare or fragrance row — even a 2-up — keeps me engaged. Sephora's 4X points email last week kept me reading because it spoke directly to what I spend on. This email does not.
- 4. **Retire "ENDS TONIGHT" for at least one full send cycle.** Try scarcity over deadline: "Only 300 styles left at clearance prices" is more honest and more believable right now.
- 5. **Cut the Wallet barcode section or move it.** It's visual noise that makes this email feel twice as long.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Clearance hits 85% off — these won't last`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Kohl's Cash + 40% off ends at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Clearance from $4. Kohl's Cash stacks on everything.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `85% off clearance — no code needed, no tricks.`

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "ENDS TONIGHT: 40% off" Email

### 1. Technical Summary

Well-structured HTML4 email with standard multi-client compatibility patterns, but carries multiple compliance gaps and pervasive missing alt text across tracking and content images. Auth header visibility is obscured by the AgentMail relay layer.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 tracking/click-redirect links skipped** — all external links route through `click.s.kohls.com` or `click.chp.kohls.com`. No broken destination URLs were confirmed, but redirect chain depth is unverified.

Three distinct pixel/tracking systems are firing simultaneously:
- `click.chp.kohls.com/o/9c5b74ba...` — CoherentPath behavioral pixel
- `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...o.gif` — MessageGears open pixel
- `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?E5AL66...` — Salesforce Marketing Cloud open pixel
- `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` — Adobe Audience Manager sync pixel

Redundant open-tracking pixels across three vendors is unusual and increases page weight; confirm all three are intentional and contractually required.

**Malformed tag detected:**
```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>
```
The stray `</custom>` closing tag is invalid HTML and may cause parser errors in strict renderers (though most email clients will recover silently).

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Missing alt text — 11 images flagged**, including content images (product shots from `images.am.kohls.com`) and all tracking pixels. Tracking pixels should carry `alt="" aria-hidden="true"` (the MessageGears pixel does this correctly; the others do not). Content images require descriptive alt text for screen readers and image-blocked rendering.

Confirmed missing alt on:
- `9c5b74ba-7a6d-47f4-b168-3f33051f56e0` (CoherentPath pixel)
- `event?d_sid=13245196` (Demdex sync pixel)
- `55869996_root2_2x` (product image via `images.am.kohls.com`)
- 8 additional `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/` content render pixels

**`<title>` tag is empty** — `<title></title>` — some clients (notably Gmail web) display this in the tab or preview area.

**`user-scalable=0` set** in viewport meta — blocks zoom on mobile, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).

**Google Fonts loaded three times** for the same families (one `<link>`, one `@import` each for Roboto and Montserrat) — redundant fetches on clients that render external CSS, minor load overhead.

**MSO conditional targets `gte mso 9`** — covers Outlook 2000+, which is broader than necessary; `mso 16` would suffice for any live Outlook version, though this causes no functional harm.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge token literals (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `%FIRSTNAME%`) detected in the truncated source. MessageGears user ID is populated: `mi_u=604230016`. CoherentPath and MessageGears `ecmp` campaign IDs are present and resolved.
