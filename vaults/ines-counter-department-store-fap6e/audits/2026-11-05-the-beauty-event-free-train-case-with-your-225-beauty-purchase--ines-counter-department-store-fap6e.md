# The Beauty Event: free train case with your $225 beauty purchase
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- This is the Neiman's I'd actually open. It's a beauty event with a gift and Triple Points, which is the language I shop in. The three quilted train cases in red, brown and green sit on a clean grey hero, and the offer is plain: free train case with a $225+ beauty purchase. After five days of 25% black boxes and gift-card ladders, a send about a real brand event feels like someone remembered who I am.
- The weak spot is that the Triple Points line is tucked into a black strip under the hero. For me that's the headline. I don't use a card that doesn't pay me back, and "InCircle members earn Triple Points" should be as big as the case. The nine-brand grid is a nice counter-style edit, but it has no prices and no hint of which products count toward the $225. The fine print is a wall of text, and the gift has a limit on top of "while supplies last."
- I'd click, probably to Dior or La Mer, and I'd check the points terms first. I wouldn't forward it. It's a good send, not a send-to-a-friend one.

## What's working

- The hero is clean. "THE BEAUTY EVENT" and one line of offer copy, with the three cases doing the selling. It's easy to take in.
- The Triple Points strip is on-brand for loyalty, and Neiman's is the only one in my inbox this week pointing at the InCircle program.
- The brand grid reads like a beauty counter: Maison Francis Kurkdjian, La Mer, Creed, Cartier, Parfums de Marly, Tom Ford, La Prairie, Dyson, Dior. It signals designer access without shouting.
- The four category buttons (Beauty, Fragrance, Skincare, Makeup) let me go straight to my aisle.
- It's much calmer than Sephora Savings Event, which sent a "10% off AND FREE gift ✨" subject with a body that didn't match. This one's subject and body agree.

## What's weak

- Triple Points is buried in a dark strip with small grey "See terms & conditions" text. The thing I care about most is the quietest thing on the page.
- The grid has no prices, no "what gets you to $225," and no hero product. Every tile is a brand name, so it's a catalog with no guidance.
- The fine print is dense and long. "One tote and one pouch per customer, while supplies last, exclusions apply" is disclosed under the hero in tiny type, and the full terms sit way down the page. Does the gift stack with Triple Points? I'd have to read paragraphs to find out.
- The "SHOP NOW" button is a pale outline box on a grey field and almost disappears. Macy's, for all its noise, makes its button impossible to miss.
- The cadence is heavy: this is the sixth Neiman's send in six days, after "Magical moment: Holiday 2026 Bejeweled" and the $500 gift card ladder yesterday.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put Triple Points in the hero, at the same weight as the gift: "Free train case + Triple InCircle Points."
- 2. Add a spend guide to the grid, such as "Gift-eligible from $225: La Mer set, Dior Jadore," plus a "from $" price on each tile.
- 3. Make the "SHOP NOW" button solid black with white text.
- 4. Move a three-line terms summary above the fold and link out to the full text. Say whether the gift and points stack.
- 5. Space the sends. One beauty event email, then a reminder in the last 48 hours before 11/14.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $225 on La Mer, Dior, Tom Ford and more. Gift added at checkout.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a standard table-based Salesforce Marketing Cloud (AMPscript) template with a mobile media-query block and a checkbox-hack hamburger nav. It has a few code-hygiene and accessibility issues. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the headers weren't captured, not because they failed. The source is truncated, so the body, links, footer and images could not be audited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **28 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and the offer landing page (the free train case at $225) are unverified.
- **No link-level defects are confirmed.** The truncated source shows no `href` values, so I can't confirm broken, malformed or missing links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom is disabled on mobile.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on some clients, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **No `lang` attribute.** The tag is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">`. Without `lang`, screen readers can't pick the right pronunciation.
- **Weak `<title>`.** It is `Neiman Marcus ` (trailing space) and has no campaign context.
- **Checkbox-hack nav.** `.navbar-checkbox:checked~.hamburger/.close/.menu` depends on `:checked` support, which is unreliable in Gmail and Outlook. Keyboard and screen-reader access to this menu is likely poor. Whether the nav has a non-CSS fallback can't be confirmed from the truncated source.
- **Legacy attribute selectors.** `*[class=contenttable]` and `*[class=hide]` match only an exact class value. An element with multiple classes would silently skip the mobile rule.
- **Small text.** `.footerText` and `.disclaimerText` are set to 12px, and the mobile override targets only `.f12`.
- **Alt text and image dimensions are unverified.** The images fall after the truncation point.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Unrendered template documentation is in the delivered HTML.** The leading comment contains `@email`, `@base_refid`, `@refid = CONCAT(@base_refid,"")`, `@emailid`, `@uEm`, `@cmdid`, and "CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW". It also has the commented-out line `SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256`. These are authoring notes, and they show that the scenario selection was left unresolved.
- **Internal data-extension names are exposed.** The comment names `NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated` and `NM_CRM_Feed`, along with the lookup fields `EMAIL_ID`, `EMAIL_ADDRESS`, `uEm` and `cmdid`. This is minor information leakage, and it adds bytes.
- **No visible unresolved tokens.** I found no `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` strings in the visible portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found (WARN).** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may be stripping it, so this is not a confirmed sender defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found (WARN).** One-click unsubscribe under RFC 8058 can't be confirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found (WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain `e.neimanmarcus.com` is a subdomain, but alignment can't be verified here.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and physical address are unconfirmed.** Both sit in the footer, past the truncation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters can't be verified.** The 28 tracking links were skipped and no destination URLs are visible, so I can't confirm UTM presence or naming, or that the landing page matches the "$225 beauty purchase / free train case" offer.
- **The campaign is tagged through a `@refid` variable.** The comment suggests an internal refid scheme (`@base_refid` from the Campaign Calendar) rather than standard UTMs. Whether the refid survives the redirect is untested.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Strip the AMPscript authoring comment block, including the `NM_*` data-extension names and `CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO`, from the production template or the send-time output.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and a campaign-specific `<title>`.
4. Provide a non-CSS fallback for the hamburger menu, such as plain text links.
5. Re-run the QA with the full raw `.eml` or MIME headers instead of the relay copy, to check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
6. Resolve the 28 tracking links through a redirect-following probe and check:
   - destination status codes
   - UTM/refid retention
   - that the landing page shows the $225 threshold and the train-case offer
7. Audit the footer in the untruncated source for the unsubscribe link, the physical address and the image alt text.
