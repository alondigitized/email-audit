# 3 ways to save: up to $1,500 off + extra 25% off + The Designer Sale: up to 60% off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- My first thought was that this is the Black Friday box again. It's the same black panel with the red border I've had from Neiman's since 11/19, and this one stacks three offers inside it. The $1,500 off, the extra 25%, and the Designer Sale at 60% all arrive at once. "Three ways to save" sounds generous, but I had to read it twice to work out how they fit together. Is it three separate offers, or do they stack?
- Below the box it settles down. The nine-product grid has Maison Francis Kurkdjian, Jimmy Choo, Chloé and Givenchy, with no shouting, and that's the Neiman's I actually shop. I'd click once for the Kurkdjian fragrance, but I'd want a real deadline first. That deadline is buried in the fine print at the bottom. I wouldn't forward it. I'd leave it unread until I need a gift.

## What's working

- The product grid is clean and the brand names are labeled. Kurkdjian, Jimmy Choo and Chloé say designer access far better than the box above them.
- "Includes Beauty | Code | THANKFUL" tells me beauty counts toward the $1,500 offer. That's the first thing I check, and Sephora doesn't hide it either.
- "Time to check off your wish list—and gift list" is a calm line after all that black.
- The tiles for Women's Clothing, Beauty, Shoes and Handbags are big and easy to tap with a thumb.
- The "Match with a personal style advisor" link feels like a store that knows me.

## What's weak

- The black box with the red border is the same hero I've seen all week. It's now a fixture, not an event, and 11/22's "Last day!" box is exactly what I mean.
- Three nested red boxes and a coral Designer Sale block turn the hero into a cluttered mess. It's Macy's-style overload with better fonts.
- There's no deadline anywhere above the fold. "Ends 11/28" and "Ends 12/2" only appear in the tiny print, so the urgency isn't credible.
- "Exclusions Apply" is the last thing you read in the hero, and the $250 minimum and the THANKFUL code are also in the small print. For something shouting "up to $1,500," that feels like a bait-and-switch.
- There's no sign of my tier or any points. I won't pay for a card without points back, and nothing here says what I earn.
- "Shop All" is a generic button, and the nine products don't say whether they're on sale or by how much.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show one lead offer, not three. Make it the $1,500-off THANKFUL tier and push the other two below the fold, with a plain explanation of whether they stack.
- 2. Put the dates in the hero ("Ends 11/28, 10 PM CT") and move the code out of the fine print.
- 3. Add member language, something like "Your tier earns X points on top." Sephora's app-first Cyber Week send at least made me feel like a member.
- 4. Put sale prices or "% off" on each product, and make the button say "Shop the Designer Sale."
- 5. Stop re-running the same black box. The 11/21 Etro and the 11/22 Roundup sends felt better, and Kohl's at least changes its hero between sends.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Comment before the DOCTYPE:** The file opens with a long `<!-- REQUIRED variables … -->` block before `<!DOCTYPE …>`. Content ahead of the doctype can trigger quirks mode in some legacy engines, including Outlook/IE-based renderers.
- **Zoom restriction:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom where honored, which conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4. Most email clients ignore this tag, so it is a minor issue.
- **Exact-match class selectors:** Mobile rules such as `*[class=contenttable]`, `*[class=hide]` and `*[class=f12]` only match when `class` is exactly that single value. Any element that gets a second class silently loses its responsive rule. I can't confirm this occurs because the body is truncated.
- **Checkbox hamburger nav:** The `.navbar-checkbox:checked ~ .menu` pattern is unsupported in Gmail and several other clients. A fallback can't be verified from the truncated source.
- **`<title>`:** It is `Neiman Marcus ` with a trailing space and no campaign context. This is minor.
- **Alt text, `lang` and `role="presentation"`:** These can't be assessed from the truncated source.
