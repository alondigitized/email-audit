# Score 50% off select beauty 😎
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "GET 50% OFF" in that huge sunset-gradient hero is at least honest and impossible to miss — my eye landed right there before anything else. But the second I hit "Today Only" with a benefit tin, a Supergoop tube, and Rare Beauty blush stacked like a tiny catalog, my brain went "here we go again" — this is the third Sephora send in three days basically running the same 50%-off play, just reshuffled. By the time I scrolled through eight more full-price-crossed-out products I'd already mentally filed this as background noise.
- Sephora at least doesn't waste my time with vague lifestyle copy the way that "point of view" launch email did on 9/3 — that one made me scroll a full screen just to figure out what brand I was even looking at. This one tells me the deal and the brands in the first three inches. But compared to their own 9/4 "Stacking Weekend" send, which layered the sale with Beauty Insider bonus points and samples, this one feels thinner — it's just a product grid with a discount slapped on, no reason to act today versus next week.
- Would I click? Maybe, out of habit, to see if the Charlotte Tilbury foundation is really $52. Would I forward it? No — nothing here is surprising enough to text a friend about.

## What's working

- The red-to-purple hero gradient is genuinely eye-catching in an inbox full of white boxes — it stopped my thumb for a second.
- "FREE shipping† for Beauty Insiders" is a real, relevant loyalty nod for a Rouge/VIB shopper like me.
- Product tiles show clear before/after pricing (strikethrough plus the sale number), so I don't have to do math at checkout.
- The tiered Insider bar (Insider / VIB / Rouge) at the bottom at least visually acknowledges tier status exists, even if it's generic.

## What's weak

- This is the same "50% off select beauty" beat Sephora already ran on 9/2 and teased again on 9/4 — the cadence is wearing thin, three near-identical sends inside a week.
- No product images render for the top three "Today Only" items visually distinct from the grid below — Benefit, Supergoop, Rare Beauty just sit as flat squares with no lifestyle or texture shot, which feels cheap for a beauty-counter brand.
- Nothing here says how many points I have or what my Rouge status actually gets me beyond "you have 0 points" flatly stated at the top — that reads like a jab, not a benefit.
- "Exclusions/terms apply" appears twice before I've even seen a real product, which is the same fine-print-up-front fatigue Kohl's gets dinged for with its stacked $10-off-$25 sends.
- No urgency beyond "Today Only" on the top block — the rest of the grid has no deadline at all, so it doesn't feel like an event, just a permanent sale wearing an event's clothes.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop repeating the flat "50% off select beauty" hero three sends running — rotate in the points/sample stacking mechanic from the 9/4 email, since that one actually scored better with me.
- 2. Replace "you have 0 points" with something constructive — even "You're $25 from Rouge" would turn a null-state into a nudge.
- 3. Give the "Today Only" trio real lifestyle photography instead of flat product-on-white — Charlotte Tilbury and Rare Beauty deserve better than a stock square.
- 4. Add a real countdown or end-date instead of "Today Only" sitting next to a scroll of items with no deadline at all.
- 5. Cut the grid to 4-5 curated picks instead of 10 — a shorter list reads as curated for me, a Rouge shopper, versus a dump of whatever's on markdown.
