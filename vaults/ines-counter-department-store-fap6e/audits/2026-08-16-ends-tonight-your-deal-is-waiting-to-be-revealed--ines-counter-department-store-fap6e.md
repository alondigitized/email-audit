# ENDS TONIGHT: Your deal is waiting to be revealed 👀
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-16**
## Executive summary

- Well, the subject promised a "deal waiting to be revealed" and a "reveal" this is not — it's the intimates Semi-Annual sale, and I've now seen some version of "20% off Kohl's Cash" or a bra promo from this brand basically every day this week. My gut reaction opening this: another wall of red starbursts and "Reg. $42-54, save more with coupon" math homework before I even see a real dollar number. The one thing that did catch me was "Only Twice a Year: Semi-Annual" — that's an actual event name with a real cadence, not the usual generic percentage-off filler, and the "Ultimate Clearance Event... up to 70%" banner further down has some teeth to it.
- That said, between the "$0 Rewards" spend tracker up top, the multiple coupon-stacking math problems, and then a random detour into bath towels, a slushie machine, jeans, and a mattress topper, this email can't decide if it's a lingerie sale or a clearance everything-store. I'd skim past this, not click. I'm not forwarding it — there's nothing here I'd tell a friend about, unlike Macy's "confirm now for extra 25%" which at least had a clean, single ask.

## What's working

- "Only Twice a Year — Semi-Annual" gives this a real occasion, better than the recycled "20% off" starburst Kohl's has run on me for four straight days.
- The bra/intimates models are age- and body-diverse enough to not feel like they're selling to my daughter.
- "Ultimate Clearance Event — Save up to 70%" is a genuinely bigger number than anything else Kohl's has thrown at me this week.
- Rewards status bar up top ("KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER, Earn 5% REWARDS") at least acknowledges I'm a member, which Neiman Marcus's "Last Call" blast never bothers to do.

## What's weak

- The subject says "deal waiting to be revealed" but the email opens straight into intimates pricing — no reveal, no mystery, just bait.
- "Spend $0 to score your next reward" reads like a broken personalization token, not a benefit — it actually made me suspicious something didn't render right for my account.
- Every single price needs a coupon stacked on top of a "Reg. $X-Y" range to get to the real number — I have to do math to know what I'm actually paying, which is more friction than Sephora's flat "0 points" gut-punch honesty.
- The pivot from bras and shapewear straight into a bath towel, a slushie machine, and a mattress topper is Macy's-style overload — I came for intimates, not small appliances.
- This is at minimum my fifth Kohl's email this week (following the 8/13 intimates one, the 8/14 home-goals one, and the 8/14 active-gear one) with the same red-starburst-and-coupon format — I'm fatigued.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject line honest — either lead with the Semi-Annual bra sale specifics or drop the "reveal" gimmick entirely; it's a wasted click driver when the open just shows a normal sale.
- 2. Cut the unrelated home/appliance/jeans block from an intimates-focused send, or split it into its own email — right now it dilutes a decent Semi-Annual hook.
- 3. Show one final price per item instead of "Reg. $42-54 + save with coupon" — Nordstrom and Saks don't make me do subtraction to know what I'm paying.
- 4. Fix or remove the "$0 to score your next reward" line — it reads like a merge error, not a perk.
- 5. Space out the cadence — five near-identical sends in a week is Neiman-Marcus-"ICYMI" territory; I stop opening after three.
