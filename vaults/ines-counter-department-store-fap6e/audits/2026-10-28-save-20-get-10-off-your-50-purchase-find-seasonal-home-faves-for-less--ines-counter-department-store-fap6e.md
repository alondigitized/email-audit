# Save 20%, get $10 off your $50+ purchase & find seasonal home faves for less 👏
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-28**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again, and I've stopped counting. This is the same "20% + $10 off $50+" stack I got yesterday and on 10/26, with the home section moved up top. The hero is a coupon graphic with "Plus, earn Kohl's Cash." The home hero reads "Hosting Headquarters." Below that is a very long scroll: the North Kohl's world, then a Sephora at Kohl's banner, then a "Picked with you in mind" grid.
- The one thing that got my attention is the Sephora + Kohl's Savings Event banner ("30% off Sephora Collection, 20% off Rouge, 10/30–11/9"). That's the first thing in this email that speaks to a beauty-counter person. It's buried about two-thirds of the way down, under the elves and the toy box. The Rouge tier is the only loyalty content I could pick out. I wouldn't forward this. I'd probably click once for the Sephora event, and I'd already have found it myself.

## What's working

- The Sephora + Kohl's banner has a black-and-gold look, real percentages (30% and 20%) and dates. It's the only block that felt like it respected my category.
- The top strip shows my Kohl's Rewards member status and 5% rewards, so Kohl's knows who I am.
- The bottom coupon section lists codes (SAVE20, GOSHOP10) and "Ends Nov. 1" next to each one. I can see exactly what I'm getting and when it ends.
- "Free returns through January 31, 2027" and the fast store pickup block are the kind of assurance I notice as someone who returns at the brand event.

## What's weak

- Beauty is buried. Neiman Marcus's recent "$50 gift card for every $200 spent" put the number in the hero. Kohl's makes me scroll through bakeware and bath towels first.
- The email is far too long, and I counted at least six separate themes. Sephora's own sends are shorter. Macy's does overload, but its "confirm now for 25% off" email at least had one job.
- It repeats the 10/27 send ("holiday gifting made easy") and the 10/26 "North Kohl's" welcome. The elves and toy box are the same creative a third time in three days.
- The product grid is a slush machine, a bath towel, a mattress topper and Gloria Vanderbilt pants. Nothing in it is aimed at a 47-year-old beauty shopper.
- The hero text is small and crowded, and some of the tap targets (the category pill buttons) are tiny on a phone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the Sephora savings event in the hero, with "30% off 10/30–11/9" visible in the first screen, and send beauty-lapsed shoppers a segment version.
- 2. Cut the email to one offer plus one category. Drop the North Kohl's block, since it's already run twice this week.
- 3. Segment by category. I buy at the beauty counter, so don't send me a mattress topper.
- 4. Make the Rewards and Kohl's Cash value specific, for example "You have $X Kohl's Cash."

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 20% + $10 off code ends Nov 1`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus Kohl's Cash on every $50 you spend, through Nov 1.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has several markup defects: a stray `</custom>` tag, a `<div itemscope>` inside `<head>`, an empty `<title>`, and a viewport meta that blocks zoom. It also has many content images with no alt text and a plain-text part that is mostly URLs. The compliance warnings (unsubscribe headers, physical address, SPF/DKIM) could not be confirmed from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links were not probed.** 77 tracking links on `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=...` and `click.chp.kohls.com` were skipped as redirect domains. Landing-page status and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Four tracking pixels load at the top of the body:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/77561aa4-...` is a 1x1 `<img>` with no `alt`.
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif` has `alt=""` and `aria-hidden`.
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=...` is closed with a stray `</custom>` instead of a valid `<img>` ending.
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` is a 0x0 Adobe Audience Manager pixel.
- **The `</custom>` tag is invalid HTML.** It follows the `open.aspx` pixel `<img>`.
- **`<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href.** Some clients resolve it to the document URL and make a spurious request.
- **A recipient identifier is in image URLs.** `mi_u=604230016` appears in the `mi.kohls.com` image URLs. If the message is forwarded or its images are proxied, the ID travels with it.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Content images have no alt text.** The QA check flagged these, and they are not tracking pixels:
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/f441d45d2128ae53.png`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/415c8a18fc2950ea.png`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/69494b48a79b4240.png`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/78308d1d9adb0751.png`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/916d6fa08d185233.png`
  - `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/59205032_3_2x`
  - `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/59205032_1_2x`

  With images blocked, or for screen readers, the offer and product content is lost.
- **The tracking pixels are also missing `alt`.** The `click.chp.kohls.com` and `kohls.demdex.net` pixels should carry `alt=""`.
- **`<title></title>` is empty.**
- **The viewport meta blocks zoom.** `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` fails WCAG 1.4.4 (resize text).
- **`<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`.** This is invalid. Parsers will move it into `<body>`, and it also gets stripped or ignored by most clients.
- **The Montserrat and Roboto web fonts load via `@import` and `<link>`.** Most clients ignore these. The `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` fallback stack is present, so this is only a note.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unrendered tokens such as `%%...%%`, `{{...}}` or `[FirstName]`.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA note says AgentMail may not capture these headers, so this is unconfirmed. Without them, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender one-click unsubscribe requirements may not be met.
- **No physical address was detected.** The source is truncated before the footer, so I can't tell whether it is missing or just cut off. Verify against the full HTML.
- **`Authentication-Results` is missing.** The relay doesn't expose it, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sender is `Kohls@s.kohls.com` and the tracking domains are `click.s.kohls.com`, `click.chp.kohls.com` and `mi.kohls.com`.
- **The plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 13,470 of 19,225 characters (70%) are URLs. This is a spam-filter risk.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters are visible in the available source.** Links are opaque `qs=` redirects, so the final landing URLs and any UTMs can't be checked.
- **Campaign identifiers are only on image URLs.** They are `mi_ecmp=1024679_20261028` and `email_name=261028_DG_Email_Wednes...`. They show no evidence of carrying through to the site.
- **Landing page alignment is unverified.** The 77 skipped click links mean the "Save 20%", "$10 off $50+" and seasonal home offers could not be matched to destination pages.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the product and offer images. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
2. Remove the stray `</custom>`. Close the `open.aspx` pixel `<img>` properly.
3. Move or remove the `itemscope` div from `<head>`. Fill in `<title>`, and remove the empty `rel="shortcut icon"` link.
4. Drop `maximum-scale=1` and `user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` against the raw message headers, not the relay copy.
6. Verify the footer for a physical address and an unsubscribe link using the untruncated HTML.
7. Improve the plain-text part by adding more readable copy and trimming long tracking URLs.
8. Follow a sample of the redirect links manually to confirm destinations and UTM carry-through.
9. Check SPF, DKIM and DMARC on the original message, bypassing the relay.
