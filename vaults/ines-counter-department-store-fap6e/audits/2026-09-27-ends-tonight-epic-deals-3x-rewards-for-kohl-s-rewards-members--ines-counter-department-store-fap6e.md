# ENDS TONIGHT 🏃‍♀️‍➡️ Epic Deals + 3x rewards for Kohl's Rewards members ➡️
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-27**
## Executive summary

- Ugh, "EPIC DEALS" again — that's the same shrieking red banner I flagged on the 23rd, 24th, 25th, and twice on the 26th, now dressed up with "ENDS TONIGHT" urgency like it's brand new. The handbag hero is fine, I guess — pleather satchel, fall-appropriate — but this is a wall-to-wall tile catalog of kids' fleece, LEGOs, jeans, mattress toppers, and floor care. Nothing in here says "Ines," it says "everyone, buy something, anything."
- The one thing that did catch my eye is the 3x Rewards banner up top — I'm a Kohl's Rewards member, so that's real value, not noise. But it's competing with about fifteen other tiles for my attention and gets buried by the fourth scroll. Compare that to Neiman Marcus's Bottega Veneta send — one hero, one bag, one mood, "Precious Cargo" — that's editorial styling that respects my time. This is the opposite: a garage sale flyer with my name nowhere on it.
- Would I click? No. Would I forward it? Absolutely not — I'd feel embarrassed sending a friend the sixth identical "EPIC DEALS" email of the week. This gets scanned for the Rewards multiplier and archived.

## What's working

- The 3x Rewards + $10 Kohl's Cash banner is genuine loyalty value I'll actually use as a member.
- "ENDS TONIGHT" at least gives a real deadline instead of vague "hurry" language.
- The handbag hero photo is clean and in-season for fall.
- Category nav bar (Home/Men/Women/Kids) up top is a small mercy — at least I can jump past the noise if I want to.

## What's weak

- This is the same red "EPIC DEALS" tile wall I've now logged five-plus times this week — zero creative variation, just reshuffled products.
- It's a Macy's-style catalog dump — floor care, mattress toppers, LEGO toys, kids' fleece, jeans — nothing curated, nothing that reads "beauty counter regular."
- No beauty, no designer access, no tier-program specifics beyond the generic Rewards banner — this brand clearly isn't built for someone like me.
- Visual hierarchy is nonexistent — everything is the same size tile, so my eye doesn't land anywhere specific.
- Zero personalization — this reads like it went to every subscriber on the list, stranger treatment, not known-customer treatment.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the tile count in half and lead with ONE hero offer above the fold — right now nothing wins because everything's shouting equally.
- 2. Stop reusing the same red "EPIC DEALS" banner creative every single day — rotate the visual language or subscribers like me start filtering the whole sender.
- 3. Segment by category history — if I've never bought a LEGO set or floor care product, don't put it in my top-of-email real estate.
- 4. Move the 3x Rewards/Kohl's Cash callout higher and make it visually distinct from the deal tiles — it's the one thing with real loyalty value and it's getting lost.
- 5. Add real urgency copy in the hero, not just a small red "ENDS TONIGHT" corner badge — if it really ends tonight, say so boldly once, not as wallpaper.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Standard ESP-generated marketing email (Kohl's/CoherentPath platform) with tracking pixels and click-redirect links; automated QA flags gaps in compliance headers and image accessibility.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Multiple tracking/analytics endpoints present: `click.chp.kohls.com` (1x1 pixel), `mi.kohls.com` open/click pixels, `kohls.demdex.net` (Adobe Audience Manager), `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` (open tracking beacon).
- 70 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by automated HTTP probing (expected behavior for redirect domains, not itself a defect — flagged here for transparency, not as an issue).
- Malformed markup: `<img ... aria-hidden="true" /></custom>` in the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` tracking block — stray closing `</custom>` tag with no matching opening tag.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 11 images missing `alt` text, per QA output, including both tracking pixels (`efa38477-d44a...`, `o.gif`, `event`, and 7 `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` beacons) and one content image (`57746756_2_2x` product image at `images.am.kohls.com`). Tracking pixels are low-impact, but the product image lacking alt text is a genuine accessibility gap for screen reader users.
- Legacy HTML4 Transitional doctype with MSO conditional comments and `<style>` resets — standard for cross-client email rendering, no issues found.
- Truncated source prevents full accessibility audit of remaining body content.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- `mi_cid`, `mi_mid`, `mi_ecmp`, `mi_kc_redm_end_dte`, `mi_kc_event_pe` query params observed in tracking URLs — appear to be populated (no unresolved `{{merge_tag}}`-style placeholders visible in the truncated source).
- No issues found in visible source.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- List-Unsubscribe header not detected — may be an AgentMail relay capture limitation rather than a true absence; cannot confirm from HTML source alone.
- List-Unsubscribe-Post header (RFC 8058) not detected — one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed.
- No physical mailing address detected in the truncated HTML — CAN-SPAM requires this; cannot confirm absence definitively since source is truncated, but flagging per QA output.
- Authentication-Results header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not found — authentication status unknown, likely a relay capture gap rather than a sender-side failure.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Tracking/redirect URLs (`click.chp.kohls.com`, `click.s.kohls.com`) use opaque tokenized IDs (`mi_cid`, `mi_mid`, `qs=` encoded blob) rather than standard UTM parameters — consistent with ESP click-tracking redirects, not a defect, but prevents direct verification of destination/landing-page alignment from source alone.
- Cannot confirm final landing page match since redirect targets were not resolved (skipped per QA probe settings).

7. Recommendations
- Add `alt` text (or empty `alt=""` for purely decorative/tracking images) to the 11 flagged images, prioritizing the content image `57746756_2_2x`.
- Remove the stray `</custom>` closing tag in the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` tracking block.
