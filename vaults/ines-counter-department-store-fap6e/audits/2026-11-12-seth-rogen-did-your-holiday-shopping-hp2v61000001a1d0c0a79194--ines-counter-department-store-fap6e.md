# SETH ROGEN DID YOUR HOLIDAY SHOPPING
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- This is the seventh Sephora email in about a week. I saw "Seth Rogen" in caps and nearly deleted it as noise. The red-and-gold collage is charming, I'll give it that. It reads like a magazine spread, not a flyer. But I scrolled the whole thing looking for a price, a percentage or a deadline, and I couldn't find one. It's a gift guide dressed up as celebrity content.
- Compare it to Neiman Marcus, whose designer sale email put "Exclusive Access, 40% Off" in the hero with a code. I knew what I was getting in two seconds. Here I get Seth Rogen's quips about setting spray and blush, and I have to work out the offer myself. My 11/10 "points = rewards" email from Sephora at least talked to me as a Rewards Bazaar member. This one treats me as a stranger who wants a laugh.
- Would I click? Maybe once, for the Charlotte Tilbury-style glam picks, but nothing here is urgent. I wouldn't forward it. I'd skim it, smile at "I'm so good at this, they should change the name to Sephora," and mark it read.

## What's working

- The "Great Gifts According to Seth" hero is warm and funny. It's a real break from the shouting I get from Macy's.
- Each product has its own quote and a named brand and item, like Kérastase Nutritive Magic Night serum and the Patrick Ta blush duo. That tells me what I'd actually buy.
- The Beauty Advisor section is a decent trust cue. It tells me a human at the counter can help me, which is how I like to shop.
- The red and gold palette feels seasonal without being garish.
- The footer carries the useful perks: free shipping or in-store pickup, 25% off on the first Credit Card purchase, and Sephora Texts.

## What's weak

- There's no price, no percentage and no deadline anywhere in the body. Compare that to Kohl's, which hits me with "extra 20%" in the subject line.
- The email is very long, and the product tiles carry no price or visible shop buttons. I can't tell what is clickable.
- The Dancing with the Stars block is a long detour. It's a sponsorship plug that has nothing to do with my holiday list.
- Rewards and tier status are missing. I'm a member, but there's no points angle, which is the main reason I'd pick Sephora over Nordstrom.
- The subject is in all caps and the sender name is "Sephora: The Chat." After six sends this week, I can't tell this apart from the others.
- The celebrity styling skews younger and male. It doesn't say "47-year-old woman buying skincare" to me.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one concrete offer in the hero. For example, a points multiplier or a gift-with-purchase with an end date, plus a visible "Shop Seth's picks" button.
- 2. Add prices and small "Shop" links under each product.
- 3. Cut the Dancing with the Stars section down to a single line, or drop it.
- 4. Add a member line like "Rewards Bazaar members: earn extra points on these picks."
- 5. Space out the sends. Seven in a week trains me to ignore them.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has two confirmed failures: an empty greeting slot (`Hi ,`) and a 0-character plain-text part. Compliance and authentication headers could not be verified because they were not captured. The HTML has a few markup problems, listed below.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links were reported. QA probed 12 material links and skipped 33, which were mostly footer, utility and social links, so coverage is partial.
- All visible links go through the ESP redirect wrapper, e.g. `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a1d0c0a791947ded6e96c569c8/.../?__dU__=...`. The final destinations can't be seen in the truncated source.
- The open-tracking pixel is `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...`. It is hidden with `display:none; max-height:0; mso-hide:all`, which is expected. QA flags it for missing `alt`, but that is low impact for a hidden pixel.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Fixed-width layout:** the wrapper table has `style="min-width:700px; width:700px"`. The only mobile rules in the visible `@media (max-width:480px)` block adjust fonts and logo sizes. None override the 700px table, so horizontal scroll or shrink-to-fit is likely on mobile. The source is truncated, so this isn't fully confirmed.
- **Malformed CSS:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {...} <![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` block in `<style>`. Conditional comments are invalid in CSS there, and strict parsers may drop the rules that follow it (`.mobileHeaderTierRouge`, `.mobileIcon`, `.mobileBelong`, and so on).
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid meta value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. This has no functional impact.
- **Missing alt text (QA WARN):**
  - `20261112_The_Chat_06.jpg`
  - `20261112_The_Chat_12_02.jpg`
  - Both are content images on `images.harmony.epsilon.com`. If they carry messaging, that content is lost when images are blocked or read by a screen reader.
- **Empty structured data:** the JSON-LD has `"@type":"DiscountOffer","discountCode":""` and a bare `"@type":"PromotionCard"`. Both are empty and may fail schema validation.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Sephora</title>`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** the greeting renders as `Hi ,`, so the first-name merge field resolved to empty with no fallback. This is a confirmed broken experience. It may be because the test inbox has no profile data, but there is no fallback or conditional to handle it.

## 5. Compliance
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is 0 chars:** the email has no `text/plain` alternative. That hurts deliverability and spam scoring and breaks text-only clients. It may be a relay capture artifact, but as received it is absent.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` not found:** this may be stripped by the AgentMail relay, so it is unverified.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found:** one-click unsubscribe is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it for high-volume senders.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because of the relay. It can't be confirmed from this data.
- The physical address and visible unsubscribe link are not in the truncated HTML, so they can't be assessed. QA says an unsubscribe link was included in probing.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- No UTM parameters are visible, because links are wrapped in opaque `app.sephora.com/T/...` redirects and the source is truncated.
- Landing-page alignment can't be assessed from this data. No probe failures were reported for the 12 links checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a fallback to the first-name merge field, e.g. `Hi there,`. Otherwise suppress the greeting when the field is empty.
2. Include a `text/plain` multipart alternative.
3. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` on the original message. Also check SPF, DKIM and DMARC on the raw headers, outside the relay.
4. Add descriptive `alt` text to `20261112_The_Chat_06.jpg` and `20261112_The_Chat_12_02.jpg`. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixel.
5. Move the `<!--[if mso]>` block out of the `@media` rule and fix the CSS.
6. Replace the fixed `min-width:700px` with a fluid width (`width:100%; max-width:700px`). Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
7. Populate or remove the empty JSON-LD `DiscountOffer` and `PromotionCard` entries, and correct `robots` to `noindex`.
8. Re-run with the full HTML to check UTM parameters, the footer address and unsubscribe link, and the remaining 33 links.
