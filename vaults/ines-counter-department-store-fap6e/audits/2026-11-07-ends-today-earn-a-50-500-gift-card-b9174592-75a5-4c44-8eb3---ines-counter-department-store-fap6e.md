# Ends today: Earn a $50–$500 gift card
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-07**
## Executive summary

- This is the Neiman's I actually wanted, and it arrived about a week late. The gold hero with a clear spend-and-save ladder ($200 gets $50, $2,000+ gets $500) is honest and easy to read. I did the math in two seconds, which is more than I can say for most department store emails. It also comes from a sender who has already hit my inbox five or six times in the last day or two (Refined Knits, the "top styles up to 40% off" send, Dior Cruise). I'm tired of the cadence, but this one has a real reason to exist.
- The gift card is a spend-and-get-back deal, not a discount. I don't mind that, but I shop the brand event, and this isn't points. Beauty is included, which is the only reason I'd look. Then I get to the middle of the email. The "Shop Women's / Shoes / Men's" button block is broken. The Shoes button floats beside a big grey blank with a blurry NM monogram in it. That looks unfinished, and it's the kind of thing Neiman's shouldn't let out the door.
- Would I click? Probably, once, for a fragrance, if I was already planning to spend $200 or more today. I'd skip forwarding it. The Maison Francis Kurkdjian tile is the only thing in the email that speaks to me.

## What's working

- The spend/save table is clear. I can see exactly what I get at each level without hunting.
- "Includes beauty & fragrance" in the hero is what a counter regular needs to see. Sephora would have buried that line.
- The single gold SHOP & EARN button is easy to find, and the NOVTGC code sits right above it.
- The Maison Francis Kurkdjian Baccarat Rouge tile feels like a real designer-access signal for me.
- It's quieter than Kohl's. The hero is calm, with no emoji and no stacked "extra 20% off."

## What's weak

- The button block is broken. A blurry NM placeholder takes up the whole right-hand column, so "Shop Shoes" has nothing next to it and the layout looks half-built.
- The 3x3 grid is a random mix of Moncler, Veronica Beard, Polo and Tom Ford. It doesn't tell me which products qualify, and the fine print says select designers are excluded.
- "Exclusions Apply" under the table is vague, and the real terms sit in a wall of small print at the bottom. It's a gift card that arrives within 8 weeks and expires. That's not much like cash.
- This is the sixth Neiman's email in about two days. After the Beauty Event email with Triple Points and the train case, this one reads as the same message resent.
- A gift card for a later purchase is weaker than the points back I'd get at Macy's or Sephora.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the broken button block and the NM placeholder. Use a real image or make it a plain button row.
- 2. Put one beauty product and one price point above the spend table, so I can see what $200 buys.
- 3. Show the deadline in the hero ("Ends tonight, 10 PM CT"). It's only in the small print now.
- 4. Say "gift card arrives by email within 8 weeks" near the button, not only in the legal copy.
- 5. Cut the send frequency, or tie it to a loyalty tier so it reads as a known-customer email.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Beauty counts: up to $500 gift card, ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fragrance and beauty included. Use code NOVTGC at checkout.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The truncated source is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with an exposed ESP template comment and a zoom-blocking viewport tag. All three automated QA warnings are about missing headers or unprobed links, and the headers may simply not survive the AgentMail relay. The QA run reports 0 hard issues, a 62% pass rate and 3 warnings. The HTML is cut off, so the body, footer and link `href`s could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **29 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them because they are click-redirect domains, so none of their destinations are verified (dead links, redirect chains and landing-page status are unknown).
- **No broken links were confirmed.** The visible source contains no `href` values to inspect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom is blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` restricts pinch-zoom, which conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **No language is declared.** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` or `xml:lang` attribute, so screen readers can't pick a pronunciation profile.
- **The title is generic.** `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` has a trailing space and doesn't reflect the subject ("Ends today: Earn a $50–$500 gift card").
- **The CSS has a minor error.** `.disclaimerText { font-family: Arial, Helvetica, 'sans-serif'; }` quotes the generic keyword, so it is treated as a font name, not the generic fallback.
- **The source is mixed markup.** The XHTML DOCTYPE is paired with unclosed `<meta ... >` tags, and the DTD URL uses `https://www.w3.org/TR/xhtml1/DTD/xhtml1-transitional.dtd`. Clients tolerate this, but it is inconsistent.
- **Mobile selectors are brittle.** The media queries use exact-match `*[class=contenttable]`, `*[class=hide]` and similar. They break if an element carries more than one class.
- **Hamburger menu support is unconfirmed.** The `.navbar-checkbox:checked~` menu pattern only works in clients that support `:checked` and `<input>`. Gmail and Outlook generally don't, and the fallback can't be verified in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Internal template comments are exposed.** The HTML starts with `<!--REQUIRED variables: @email ... @base_refid ... @refid = CONCAT(@base_refid,"") ... Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", "EMAIL_ID", ...) ... Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "uEm", ...) CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW -->`, followed by `<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->`. This discloses Data Extension names, lookup field names (`uEm`, `cmdid`) and the hashed-email scheme to every recipient. It also adds unneeded bytes, and "CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW" suggests the template wasn't cleaned before send.
- **No unresolved tokens were found in the visible text.** The variables above are in comments only, so rendered output isn't affected as far as the visible source shows.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` was not found (WARN).** QA notes the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` was not found (WARN).** Without it, RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe can't be confirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found (WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `e.neimanmarcus.com`.
- **The footer is not visible.** The unsubscribe link and physical postal address can't be checked because the source is truncated.
- **These header warnings are probably relay artifacts.** They are unconfirmed, not confirmed defects.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM and landing-page alignment can't be assessed.** The visible source has no `href`s, and the 29 tracking links were unprobed. The only attribution clue is the `@refid` / `@base_refid` variables in the template comment, which suggests tracking via a `refid`-style parameter rather than UTMs. That is an inference from the comment, not verified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Strip the ESP variable and scenario comments (`<!--REQUIRED variables ... -->`, `<!--/* SET @user ... */ -->`) at send time.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Add `lang="en"` and `xml:lang="en"` to `<html>`, and give `<title>` a meaningful, trimmed value.
4. Change `'sans-serif'` to `sans-serif` in `.disclaimerText`.
5. Confirm from a raw send (not the relay copy) that `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and DKIM, SPF and DMARC pass.
