# Ends tonight ⏰ Shop the Back-to-School Sale & save up to 40% 🚌
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-23**
## Executive summary

- Sixth day in a row now — locker kid on 8/19, 8/20, 8/21, 8/22 (twice), and today's another kid at the lockers, this time in a school hallway with a backpack. Kohl's has officially run out of new ideas this week, and "ends tonight" doesn't hit the way it should when the last four sends all said some version of "ends soon" too. I don't have a kid going back to school, so that whole top third — hero, backpack banner, the works — is dead space before I've scrolled past my second sip of coffee.
- What does stop my thumb, buried down near the bottom under "Picked with you in mind": the Beauty | Sephora tile. That's the one category tag in this entire email that's actually mine, and it's the sixth item down in a six-across product grid. Sephora's own "Psst, something new just arrived" send buried its offer too, so at least Kohl's isn't alone in that mistake — but Kohl's makes it worse by stacking eight category sections (Activewear, Jeans, Basics, Uniforms, Bedding, Kitchen & Dining) ahead of it, none of them beauty, none of them me.
- I'm not clicking, not forwarding. This is a mark-as-read, and honestly a candidate for "stop sending me the school-hallway one," the way I'd tell a Saks SA to stop pushing me the same trunk show invite twice.

## What's working

- The "Beauty | Sephora" tile in the mixed grid is a real category match, even if it's an afterthought — Kohl's does sell Sephora, and that's worth surfacing higher.
- Kohl's Rewards status banner and "Earn 5% Rewards" is up top and legible — I know exactly what my member status gets me, which Saks emails routinely fumble.
- The Ninja Slushi and mattress topper "picked for you" tiles have actual prices with strikethroughs ($349.99, $91.99–$139.99 vs $225.99–$349.99) — that's honest, visible math, not vague "shop now" language.
- Kohl's Cash reminder at the bottom ($10 per $50 spent) is a real, quantifiable loyalty mechanic I can act on.

## What's weak

- This is the same back-to-school hallway/kid photography I've now seen five days running — 8/19 through 8/22 twice, and now 8/23. I could sketch this kid from memory at this point.
- Eight straight product sections — Activewear, Jeans, Basics, Uniforms, Bedding, Kitchen & Dining — before anything beauty-adjacent shows up. That's Macy's-style category overload, and Macy's at least segments differently email to email.
- "Ends tonight" urgency is undercut by the fact that four of the last five subject lines all claimed some version of ending soon. Neiman Marcus did the same "ends soon" recycling on its gift-card email, but at least varied the framing device (reminder → deadline). This is just the same graphic, different day.
- Nothing here signals "we know you're 47 and shop counters, not backpacks" — nothing to compare to Nordstrom's or Saks's designer-access tone. It's undifferentiated mass-market blast.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap the hero for the Beauty | Sephora tile on any recipient tagged non-parent/beauty-shopper — don't make me scroll past six unrelated categories to find the one thing I'd buy.
- 2. Retire the school-hallway/locker creative — five-plus uses this week is fatigue, not urgency. Rotate in a different visual even if the offer stays the same.
- 3. Cut the category grid for segments like me down to 2-3 relevant sections instead of eight — less scroll, sharper hierarchy.
- 4. Add a beauty-specific subject line variant for known Sephora-at-Kohl's shoppers instead of one generic back-to-school subject for the whole list.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Beauty faves up to 40% off — plus Kohl's Cash`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Sephora-at-Kohl's picks, up to 40% off (ends tonight)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New drink maker, jeans, and mattress toppers — picked for your cart`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Kohl's Cash still stacking — see what's in your wallet`
