# 🔔 Don’t miss 30% off ALL Sephora Collection, Rosie
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-05**
## Executive summary

- Sephora sent me this deal two days ago. Same percentage, same collection, different subject-line wrapper — "Don't miss" instead of "Ends 7/7." I know when it ends because the July 3rd email told me explicitly. I know the offer because the July 1st Stacking Weekend launch spelled it out. What I don't know is what's new here, because "Don't miss" plus my first name is not a new argument — it's the same argument in a fresh envelope.
- The email itself is well assembled. Clean render, Beauty Insider visible at the bottom, Paula's Choice earning a spotlight I genuinely appreciate. The hero — "Beauty with a Following" — is a mood I'd buy on a different send. But it's doing brand-building work when this email needs to be closing a sale. On the third variation of the same offer in five days, lead with the discount and the deadline, not the aesthetic.
- Mark as read. I bought what I wanted from Sephora Collection when the price first dropped. I'm not forwarding this — I sent my friend the July 3rd email because *that* one had the "Ends 7/7" hook baked in.

## What's working

- **Name in the subject.** "Rosie" is the one signal that made me pause before I auto-swiped. Sephora has earned that data. It works even on the third send.
- **Beauty Insider section at the bottom.** I scroll to confirm I'm earning points before I commit to anything. It's there — the minimum I expect from a brand I'm loyal to.
- **Paula's Choice module.** Gives me a reason to read past the hero. A specific brand I trust, with its own editorial lane. Smarter than what Neiman Marcus has been doing — same typographic "Sale" hero three emails in a row.
- **Clean render.** No broken images, no overlapping copy. After Macy's "LAST CHANCE: confirm now" triggered email showed up looking like a form wearing a costume, basic execution quality registers.

## What's weak

- **Same offer, third send.** This is 30% off Sephora Collection email number three since July 1st. "Don't miss" tries to manufacture urgency my inbox has already processed twice. Kohl's does "LAST DAY" across forty sends — I expected Sephora to know better.
- **Lifestyle hero buries the offer.** "Beauty with a Following" is fine copy for a brand moment. It's wrong copy for a closing-argument email. When you're on day five of a five-day promotion, the hero needs a number and a deadline — not a vibe.
- **No explicit deadline in the body.** The July 3rd email said "Ends 7/7" loud and clear. This one says "Don't miss" and makes me do the date math myself. That date is the conversion lever. Using it is not optional at this point in the sale calendar.
- **Too many modules after the hero.** Paula's Choice, Spot & Shop It, New Launch & Clean Mode, All Over Your Feed — four editorial sections before I hit Beauty Insider. That's discovery-mode volume for a closing-mode email.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Replace the lifestyle hero with a deadline headline.** "Two days left: 30% off ALL Sephora Collection" does what "Beauty with a Following" refuses to do — it tells me where I am in the sale clock and makes SHOP NOW meaningful.
- 2. **Put "Ends July 7" in bold above the CTA.** Not in the banner. Not in the subject. In the email body, readable without effort, above the fold.
- 3. **Cut to two modules.** Keep Paula's Choice (earns its space) and Beauty Insider (I need the loyalty confirmation). Drop the other three — they're traffic for a product-discovery email, not a close.
- 4. **Feature one hero SKU with a before/after price.** Give me a reason to click *this* send that I didn't have on July 3rd. A single Sephora Collection bestseller with strikethrough retail and the 30% price makes this email stand apart from its predecessors.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends Monday: 30% off Sephora Collection, Rosie`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Beauty Insider discount wraps 7/7`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Two days left — stock up on everything Sephora Collection at 30% off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stacking Weekend closes Monday. Points + 30% off, one last time.`
