# ’Tis the season for luxe beauty gifts 💎
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- This is the ninth Sephora email in about a week. After "Meet the best of the best🏆" on 11/13 and "SETH ROGEN DID YOUR HOLIDAY SHOPPING" the day before, I'm tired. My gut reaction: this is a catalog, not a message. It opens with a free Dior Addict Lip Maximizer trial size, which is the one thing I'd actually want. Then it quickly turns into a long scroll of gift sets.
- The Dior gift is the strongest thing here, and it's squeezed into a small header strip. The headline is "Gifts for Everyone," which doesn't speak to me. The cheerful "To: Me, From: Me" block comes closer, because I do buy for myself. Below it, eight products sit in a grid. Most are $30–$110 sets with "value" prices, and the tiers and codes only show up in the lower half.
- Would I click? Maybe, for the Dior sample if I'm already near a store. I'd skim the rest. I wouldn't forward it. Nordstrom would send me one clean message, and this feels like six stacked into one.

## What's working

- The Dior Addict Lip Maximizer trial size is a real gift and a luxe name. It's the first thing I see.
- "To: Me, From: Me" is the one line that sounds like it knows I shop for myself.
- Prices are on the tiles ($79, $35, $110), with "$114 value" and "$55 value" next to them, so I can judge the deal quickly.
- The "NEW" and "ONLY AT SEPHORA / LIMITED EDITION" tags work as exclusivity cues. Oribe, Tatcha and Fara Homaidi make it feel more upmarket than a drugstore flyer.
- The Sephora Credit Card block offers 4X points on gift sets with code 4XGIFTS, ending 11/20. That's the kind of points deal I care about.

## What's weak

- Too many things compete for attention: a trial size, a hero, a luxe section, eight tiles, a card promo, Gisou, Jumbo Sizes, category buttons, and a footer strip. Macy's would be proud, and not in a good way.
- The points offer is buried below the product grid and requires a credit card. It's a good deal, but I'd have to scroll about two-thirds of the way down to find it. As a points-first shopper, that's the wrong order.
- "Gifts for Everyone" is generic. The gift-set tiles have no ratings, no reviews, and no hint of shade or size.
- The Dior freebie has a $40 minimum and "exclusions/terms apply" in tiny print. The fine print at the bottom is dense and hard to read on a phone.
- The "SHOP NOW" buttons are small and repeated, with nothing specific in the copy. The Fara Homaidi and Peter Thomas Roth tiles feel disconnected from the headline.
- A few of the tiles have lots of white space and thin product shots, so some read as filler.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the Dior gift with purchase the headline of the whole email. Put the $40 threshold and the tier benefit in the first screen. Cut the "Gifts for Everyone" block.
- 2. Move the 4X points offer right under the hero, and state the deadline (11/20) in the headline.
- 3. Cut the grid from eight tiles to four, and add star ratings.
- 4. Change "SHOP NOW" to something specific, like "Shop luxe gift sets" or "Get my Dior gift."
- 5. Space these sends out. One strong send a week would beat nine similar ones.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free Dior lip gift with $40 — ends soon`
- **Subject Alt B:** `4X points on gift sets, through 11/20`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus Tatcha, Oribe and Fara Homaidi sets from $35.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Earn 4X points with your Sephora card on every gift set.`

- **Broken-link findings:** none shown in the QA output.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Fixed-width layout:** the wrapper table has `style="min-width:700px; width:700px"`. The visible `@media (max-width:480px)` block has no rule that overrides this min-width, so mobile clients may scroll horizontally. The truncated source may contain an override.
- **Malformed CSS:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…}<![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` block. Non-Outlook parsers will treat it as junk tokens, so the `img.mso-size` rule, and possibly the adjacent `.mobileHeaderLogo` rule, can be dropped. Move the conditional comment outside the `<style>` block.
- **Missing language:** `<html xmlns=… >` has no `lang` attribute. Layout tables lack `role="presentation"`, so screen readers may announce them as data tables.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. It is also pointless in email.
- **JSON-LD:** the `DiscountOffer` has `"discountCode": ""` and the `PromotionCard` has no properties. Most clients strip `<script>` in email, so this is dead markup.
- **Weak title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` is generic and doesn't match the subject line.
- **Plain-text part:** QA reports 0 chars. This is a FAIL for deliverability and accessibility (no non-HTML fallback).
