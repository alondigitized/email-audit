# Shop Epic Deals + earn 3x rewards with Kohl's Rewards 😊
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-21**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is Kohl's again — sixth email logged from them this week, and it's the same "Epic Deals" wall I flagged on the 21st, just with a Sephora banner sandwiched in the middle now. There is not one square inch of white space in this thing; it's baby clothes, jeans, towels, luggage, a lipstick tube, a drink mixer, and a mattress topper all fighting for my eye in the same red-tag grid. I have to scroll past six "EPIC DEAL" tiles before I even hit the Sephora-at-Kohl's line, which is the one thing in here that's actually relevant to me.
- The Sephora Insider $5-off-$49 banner is genuinely the only beat worth my time, and it's buried below the fold like an afterthought instead of leading the email. Compare that to Sephora Insider's own "Clean at Sephora" send — cluttered in its own way, but at least it opens with a beauty hero instead of a bedding sale. I'm not clicking this, I'm not forwarding it, and honestly I'm getting closer to unsubscribing given how many near-identical "Epic Deals" sends I've gotten in seven days.

## What's working

- The Kohl's Rewards 3x banner up top is at least clear and legible — I know what the loyalty ask is without squinting.
- The Sephora + Kohl's $5-off-$49 tile has a real, honest, simple offer with a visible promo code (SAVE5).
- Nothing looks visually broken — images load, text doesn't overlap, it's just relentlessly busy.

## What's weak

- This is a catalog, not an email — jeans, towels, luggage, a coffee maker, a mattress topper, and a lipstick all crammed into one send with zero curation for who I actually am.
- The one thing I care about — Sephora at Kohl's — is buried six tiles deep instead of leading.
- "EPIC DEALS" is doing the same job "40% off is here today, gone tomorrow" did on the 20th — different words, same generic blast, no sign anyone segmented this for a 47-year-old beauty-counter shopper.
- No designer or beauty-event tone anywhere near the top — this reads like Macy's on its most overloaded day, not even Macy's "LAST CHANCE 25%" send, which at least had one clear offer and urgency mechanic.
- Cadence fatigue is real: this is the third near-identical Kohl's blast I've logged since Saturday, and I'm starting to just mark these read without opening.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the Sephora Insider offer at the top of the email, not buried mid-scroll — that's the one segment-relevant hook you have for a beauty shopper like me.
- 2. Cut this down to 3 curated deals max instead of a dozen unrelated categories; a wall of tiles reads as spam, not personalization.
- 3. Slow the cadence — three "Epic Deals" variants in a week trains me to stop opening at all.
- 4. If you know I've engaged with beauty content before, put my category first and make the subject line say so.
