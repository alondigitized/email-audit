# Save 30% on ALL Sephora Collection 💄
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- This is the twenty-first Sephora email I've had in about a week, and it doesn't match its subject line. The subject sells me 30% off Sephora Collection. The first thing I see is a small "Savings Event" banner with a code, ALLONSALE. Right under it, a huge "WOW-WORTHY GIFTS" headline takes over, followed by a long scroll of holiday sets. The offer I was promised is stuck at the top, shrunk to a thumbnail-sized badge.
- The Jo Malone London Holiday Collection block is the nicest part. It's quiet and it reads as designer access, which is how I like to be spoken to. After that it turns into a catalogue. There are a dozen small product tiles, "Spot it. Shop it.", skincare gift sets, a holiday gift guide, a Sephora Credit Card pitch and an app download. That's Macy's-style overload from a counter I normally trust.
- Would I click? Maybe once, for the Jo Malone block, and only if I were already shopping for gifts. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read, because I'll see the same offer again tomorrow.

## What's working

- The Sephora logo sits cleanly at the top, so there's no doubt who sent it.
- "Use code ALLONSALE" plus the date range, 10/30–11/9, gives me a credible deadline and a usable code.
- The Jo Malone hero feels aspirational, and "scratch of cardamom, vanilla, and amber" is the kind of copy a counter regular responds to.
- The barcode at the bottom with "Present this barcode at Sephora to receive 30% off" is useful. It's a real in-store option, and I buy in store.
- Product tiles carry the price and the "(value)" comparison, like "$68 ($92 value)". That's honest, easy to compare and appreciated.

## What's weak

- The 30% off Sephora Collection offer is buried in a tiny banner while "Wow-Worthy Gifts" takes the hero. The subject and the body argue with each other.
- It's about 7,000 pixels of scroll with at least four competing CTAs: Shop Now, Shop Now, Build Your Loves List and Download Now.
- The 30% applies only to Sephora Collection, but the hero is Jo Malone and other brands. That feels like a bait-and-switch against the subject.
- Nothing about my Beauty Insider tier, points or member perks stands out. I only get a "Hi, Rosie" in the corner, which isn't even my name, so the personalization feels off.
- The fine print is a dense grey block at the bottom, and the type is too small to read on a phone without zooming.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the 30% Sephora Collection offer the hero, full width, with the code in a button. Move the gift guide below it.
- 2. Cut the product tiles to four or six, and keep one clear CTA per module.
- 3. Add a visible Beauty Insider line, such as "Your points on this event," so it feels like it's from my counter.
- 4. Space out sends. Twenty-one in a week trains me to ignore you.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Sephora Collection event ends Nov 9`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use ALLONSALE at checkout or show the barcode in store. Ends 11/9.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus Jo Malone's new holiday edition, with Insider points on top.`

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders as a fixed 700px table layout and its link tracking appears intact. QA flagged an empty greeting name slot (`Hi ,`) and a 0-character plain-text part. Header-level compliance and authentication could not be verified from the captured data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- All links in the visible source are wrapped redirects (`https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a1976eebfeaa7a386e96c660c0/...?__dU__=...`). The final destination URLs can't be checked from the truncated source.

  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...`. This is the open pixel, hidden with `display:none; mso-hide:all`.
  - `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=...&mi_ecmp=20261101_Holiday_Showstoppers_BI...`. Nothing in the visible source hides this one.
  Both are non-content images, so `alt=""` would be correct.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Fixed width:** the wrapper table has `width:700px; min-width:700px`. The `@media (max-width:480px)` block only adjusts font sizes and image dimensions, so the layout can overflow on narrow viewports.
- **Malformed CSS:** an `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {...} <![endif]-->` conditional sits inside the `@media` block in `<style>`. Outside Outlook, parsers will likely drop `img.mso-size` and probably the `.mobileHeaderTierRouge` rule that follows it. This is inferred from CSS error recovery, not observed.
- **Invalid meta value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`.
- **JSON-LD:** the block includes `"@type":"DiscountOffer","discountCode":""` and a bare `"@type":"PromotionCard"`. The empty code and incomplete promotion card are invalid or no-op markup.
- **Title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` is generic.
- **Plain text:** the text part is 0 characters (QA FAIL). The email is HTML-only, which hurts accessibility and deliverability. The relay may have dropped it, so check the raw message.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- QA FAIL: the greeting has an empty name field (`Hi ,`).
- The preheader renders the name correctly ("Rosie, now's the time to stock up."). The name data exists, so the greeting slot likely uses a different merge field or a missing fallback.
- I couldn't confirm the greeting markup because the HTML source is truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` not found (WARN).
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found (WARN).
- `Authentication-Results` not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown (WARN).
- QA notes the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers, so these are unverified, not confirmed failures.
- An unsubscribe link is always probed, and QA reported no failure for it. The visible portion of the source doesn't show the physical address or footer, so I can't comment on those.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters can't be assessed. Links are wrapped in `app.sephora.com/T/...` redirects and the final URLs aren't in the captured source.
- The only visible campaign identifier is `mi_ecmp=20261101_Holiday_Showstoppers_BI` on the `mi.sephora.com` pixel. Its name differs from the subject's "Savings Event" framing, but that is an internal naming difference, not a defect.
- No landing page data was provided, so alignment can't be verified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the greeting merge field and add a fallback, for example "Hi there,". This is a confirmed FAIL.
2. Add a plain-text MIME part. Confirm in the raw message whether it is truly absent or lost in the relay.
3. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` on the raw message, not the relay copy.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Move the MSO conditional out of the `@media` block and fix `content="noindex"`.
6. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
7. Capture the full HTML and resolve the redirects, so UTM and landing page checks can run.
