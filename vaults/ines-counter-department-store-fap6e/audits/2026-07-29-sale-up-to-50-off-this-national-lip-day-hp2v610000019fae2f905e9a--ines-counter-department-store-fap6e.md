# SALE: up to 50% off this National Lip Day 💄
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-29**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "0 points" right under my Insider badge at the top is not the greeting I want, but the rest of this actually delivers what the subject promised for once. Real 50% off banner, real product grid with real strikethrough prices, eight lip items laid out cleanly two-across — I can see exactly what I'm getting before I even scroll. This is the third National Lip Day send this week from Sephora and easily the most coherent of the bunch.
- What pulls me in: Charlotte Tilbury liner at 30% off, the Gisou and Summer Fridays "Clean" badges (I care about that tag, I look for it), and prices I don't have to hunt for — they're right under each product, no fine-print maze. Compare that to Neiman Marcus's Last Call email, which just shouted "EXTRA 25% OFF" with no products attached — at least here I can see what I'm actually buying before I click.
- Where it loses me: the "Buy More, Save More" banner buried near the bottom with a mismatched purple gradient that looks like a different email got pasted in, and the Beauty Insider tier bar at the very bottom shows my tiers (Insider/VIB/Rouge) but doesn't tell me which one I actually am or what I'm missing to move up. I'd click on the lip grid — probably the Charlotte Tilbury liner — but I wouldn't forward this. It's a solid transactional sale email, not a "you have to see this" moment.

## What's working

- The 50% off hero banner is unmissable and the pink gradient actually reads as festive without looking like a Kohl's clearance sticker.
- Eight products with real names, real strikethrough pricing, and clean square photography — I don't have to guess what's on sale.
- "Clean" badges on Gisou and Summer Fridays speak directly to how I shop — I check ingredient/clean claims before I click add-to-bag.
- "Same Day Delivery, free shipping, in-store pickup" banner up top respects that I'm a cross-shopper who sometimes wants it today, not Tuesday.

## What's weak

- "You have 0 points" at the very top is a bad opener — I don't want to be reminded I haven't spent enough to matter before I've even seen the sale.
- The Beauty Insider tier bar at the bottom (Insider/VIB/Rouge) doesn't highlight which tier I'm in — a real missed chance to make me feel recognized as a member, not just marketed at.
- The "Buy More, Save More" purple block feels like a bolted-on second email — different palette, different offer logic ("build your basket" vs. the clean sale grid above), and it dilutes the one clear ask.
- "STARTS 7/31" on that same banner is confusing directly under a sale that's live "today" — now I don't know what's active right now versus later.

## Recommendations

- 1. Kill or reposition "You have 0 points" — lead with something that flatters me as a member (my tier name) instead of my empty balance. This is the single biggest fix; a 47-year-old Rouge or VIB shopper doesn't want to open to a zero.
- 2. Fix the tier bar at the bottom to actually mark my current status (bold/highlight my tier) instead of showing all three neutrally — that's the tier-program clarity a real department-store regular expects.
- 3. Cut the "Buy More, Save More" purple banner from this email entirely, or move it to its own send — it competes with the lip sale instead of supporting it.
- 4. Clarify the "STARTS 7/31" date relative to the live 50% off sale so I'm not doing math mid-scroll.
