# Just in: 1,000+ new pre-fall arrivals
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-17**
## Executive summary

- Now THIS is the Neiman's I subscribe for. No crimson "LAST CALL" banner shouting at me, no calligraphic "S" milking its umpteenth curtain call — just a clean black hero, "BRAND NEW STYLES JUST LANDED," and then a genuinely well-edited grid: McQueen, Prada, LOEWE, Ralph Lauren Collection. That's a lineup that respects that I know what these names mean and don't need convincing.
- That said — I'm 47, this is a "just landed" email, and there is not one word of copy telling me why any of it matters right now. It's pure catalog. No "your size," no "picks based on what you browsed," no beauty-counter mention at all despite my whole reason for shopping Neiman's being the counter experience. Compare this to Sephora's App Fest email, which at least led with a concrete stacked offer (10% off + gift) — here there's zero pricing signal, so my brain quietly filed this as "browse later," not "act now."
- Would I click? Maybe, lazily, scrolling past McQueen and the Loewe sandals with mild interest. Would I forward it? No — there's nothing here worth sending a friend, no single hero piece, no "you have to see this." I'd mark it read and maybe tap "Shop Women's" if I had five idle minutes, which is a soft outcome for a "just landed" send.

## What's working

- The black "BRAND NEW STYLES JUST LANDED" hero is calm and confident — no crimson, no urgency gimmick, a real change of pace after this week's "LAST CALL" barrage.
- The two-tier grid (Women's then Men's) is genuinely well-curated — McQueen, Prada, LOEWE, Ralph Lauren Collection all read like items I'd actually consider, not filler.
- "Shop All Women's/Men's New Arrivals" buttons are clean outlined CTAs, easy to spot, no visual clutter.
- Product images are consistent, well-lit, and evenly cropped — feels like a real editorial catalog, not a stock-photo dump.

## What's weak

- There's no offer, no price, no member benefit anywhere — as a beauty-counter loyalty holder, I got zero acknowledgment I'm a InCircle member, which Neiman's absolutely knows.
- Zero copy beyond the hero headline — no "why these," no styling note, nothing that makes this feel curated for me versus a generic blast.
- It's exclusively apparel/accessories/shoes — no beauty, no fragrance, nothing at the counter I actually shop, so half my loyalty to this brand goes unaddressed.
- The utility footer (app download, personal stylist, find a store) is nice but generic — I'd rather that space had one "recommended for you" module.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add one line of real copy under the hero — even "Fall's first drop, picked for you" — because right now this reads like a catalog dump, not a curated arrival.
- 2. Slot in a beauty/fragrance category alongside Women's/Men's — I open Neiman's emails for the counter as much as the racks, and this ignores that entirely.
- 3. Surface InCircle points or member perks somewhere, even a small footer line — Macy's at least leans into loyalty mechanics even when clumsy about it; here there's nothing.
- 4. Give me one hero product with a "shop this" callout instead of an undifferentiated 6-up grid — right now my eye has nowhere specific to land.
