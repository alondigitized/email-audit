# Ends today: 25% off select regular-price styles
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-07**
## Executive summary

- There it is again — the big black "S" banner, fourth send in a row now. I flagged it Friday on "25% off select regular-price styles ends soon" and again on the two before that, and Neiman's just... kept running it. At this point I could sketch that swash from memory, and it's eating the entire top third of the email that could've shown me an actual product. The good news is the discount is explicit and the exclusions note is right there under the CTA, not buried three screens down like Kohl's does it.
- Below the fold it redeems itself a little — nine products, real designer names (Chiara Boni, Theia, L'AGENCE, Canali), and that floral Sachin Babi piece actually caught my eye scrolling past. But nothing is tagged "beauty" or "fragrance" for me, which stings since the fine print explicitly excludes beauty/fragrance from this offer — so as a counter regular, this email has functionally nothing for me despite being from the store where I buy my beauty.
- I wouldn't click. I'd skim the grid for two seconds, notice fragrance is excluded, and archive it. Not forwarding — there's no single hero piece worth sending a friend, just a wall of thumbnails.

## What's working

- The 25% off and "Shop Now" button are unmissable — no hunting required, unlike Macy's confirmation-dressed-as-urgency email I opened Thursday.
- Nine real product shots with brand names attached (not generic "New Arrivals" filler) — more specific than Kohl's category-dump approach.
- The exclusions line sits directly under the CTA in the hero, not buried in a footer wall like some retailers do.
- "Ends 9/7/26 at 10:00 PM CT" in the fine print gives a real, credible deadline, not vague "limited time" fluff.
- Clean nav bar and footer utilities (Find a store, personal style advisor) — feels like Neiman's, not a mass blast.

## What's weak

- That black "S" hero is the fourth consecutive send using it — it's officially wallpaper, and it's crowding out any actual product photography where the eye should land first.
- Beauty and fragrance are explicitly excluded per the fine print, and there isn't a single beauty item in the nine-product grid — as a counter shopper this offer isn't really for me.
- No loyalty/InCircle points messaging anywhere — I won't touch a card without points back, and Neiman's has a points program, so leaving it off is a missed trust cue.
- The product grid is undifferentiated — dresses, jeans, a men's shirt, all crammed into identical gray boxes with no "for you" curation signal.
- No social proof or "back in stock" / bestseller framing on any of the nine pieces — just static tiles.

## Recommendations

- 1. Retire the black "S" hero for at least the next few sends — swap in an actual styled product shot (like the sequin jacket from Friday's "Fall's chic jackets" email, which I scored highest of this run). That one stopped my thumb; this one doesn't.
- 2. Add an InCircle points callout near the CTA — "Earn double points" or similar — since that's the single biggest lever for a card-loyal shopper like me.
- 3. Curate the grid by category instead of a flat 3x3 — a "Beauty & Fragrance excluded, but here's what's included for you" note would be more honest than silence.
- 4. Give one hero product its own callout above the grid — a "Editor's Pick" treatment — rather than nine equally-weighted tiles.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: 25% off dresses, denim & designer pieces`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last hours: 25% off select styles (beauty not included)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Nine pieces we'd grab before 10PM CT tonight`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Chiara Boni, L'AGENCE, Canali & more — 25% off ends today`
