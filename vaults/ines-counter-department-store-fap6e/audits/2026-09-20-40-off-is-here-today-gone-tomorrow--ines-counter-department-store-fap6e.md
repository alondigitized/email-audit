# 40% off is here today, gone tomorrow ...
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-20**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is Kohl's escalating the same play I flagged on the 19th — that 40% off coupon banner is still front and center, "Rewards Member Exclusive," ends Sept 20, and now they've dressed it up with a full home-goods lookbook underneath. It's more visually ambitious than the last four sends I logged this week, I'll give it that — the Martha Stewart collection intro is a genuine surprise and the only thing here that made me pause.
- But this is a home/kitchen catalog wearing a beauty-adjacent subject line, and nothing about "here today, gone tomorrow" told me it was going to be stand mixers, bedding, and a mattress topper. I'm a beauty-counter regular who cross-shops Nordstrom and Saks — Neiman Marcus's "Peak Sleek" send at least commits to one styled hero image and a clean designer-name drop; this is eleven different categories crammed into one scroll with a barcode coupon at the bottom like a Sunday circular.
- Would I click? Only if I were already mid-project on my kitchen, and even then I'd scroll straight past the jewelry-store levels of red banners to get there. Not forwarding this — it's not personalized enough to feel like it was made for me, just another Kohl's everything-sale.

## What's working

- The Martha Stewart collection reveal is a legit "ooh" moment — recognizable name, clean product shot, stands out from the usual Kohl's red-box noise.
- The 40% off banner is unmissable at the top, no scrolling needed, with a real deadline (Sept 20) and a visible "Shop Now" CTA.
- Kohl's Cash stacking is called out early and reinforced again near the coupon at the bottom — consistent with every recent send, so at least it's not a bait-and-switch.
- The tablescape and bedding photography is genuinely pretty — warm, autumnal, aspirational in a way Macy's usually fumbles.

## What's weak

- Zero beauty content. I'm a counter regular — Sephora's NEWSCENTS fragrance push at least led with a product I'd actually shop; this is home goods top to bottom.
- Way too many categories stacked vertically — kitchen, dining, bedding, clearance, then a random product grid (a coffee maker, bath towel, jeans, mattress topper) that reads like a clearance dump, not a curated pick.
- The nav bar repeats twice (Home/Women/Men/Kids) with zero personalization — I've been buying at brand events for years and this still treats me like a stranger with no clue what I shop.
- "Picked with you in mind" over a coffee maker and a mattress topper is almost insulting — that's not picked for me, that's generic overstock.
- Barcode coupon crammed at the very bottom next to Kohl's Cash terms feels like an afterthought, not the hero the subject line promised.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this into two sends — one home/kitchen email with Martha Stewart as the hero, one beauty/apparel email — instead of forcing everything under one urgency subject line. Right now the "gone tomorrow" urgency gets buried under six unrelated categories.
- 2. Move the actual coupon code and barcode up near the top banner where the urgency lives, not scrolled to the bottom where I've already lost interest.
- 3. Replace "Picked with you in mind" with real personalization — if I'm a Rewards member with purchase history, show me categories I've actually bought, not a random product grid.
- 4. Trim the nav/category block duplication — one Home/Women/Men/Kids row is enough; two feels like padding.
