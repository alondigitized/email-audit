# Don't miss out on up to $250 off your purchase today
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-28**
## Executive summary

- Here we go again — the exact same "spend $400/save $100, spend $1,000/save $250" tiered promo Neiman's ran on 8/26 and 8/27, just with a new subject line and the code moved into the hero box this time. I've said it before and I'll say it again: the math is genuinely clear and I appreciate that, but running the identical mechanic three sends in four days makes it feel less like an exclusive Neiman's event and more like Kohl's Friends & Family, which I've now seen "20% off" from nine days running. The nine-tile "shop styles you love" grid below is pretty — Mac Duggal, Jimmy Choo, Amiri, rag & bone, nice mix of price points — but it's the same generic just-landed wall I already saw in yesterday's "Insider's look" email, no curation tied to what I actually buy.
- The fine print explicitly excludes beauty and fragrance, which stings a little given I'm a counter regular — that's the one category I'd cross-shop for, and it's carved out. No mention of Circle or InCircle points anywhere, no personal-shopper callout beyond a generic "match with a style advisor" icon-link buried at the bottom.
- I'd skim this, note the code, and probably not click today — I already redeemed the mental note from Tuesday's version. Not forwarding, not deleting either, just leaving it for whenever I'm actually ready to buy something over $400.

## What's working

- The spend/save table is dead simple to read at a glance — no squinting required.
- Code SHOPFALL is right in the hero, not buried in body copy.
- Nine-item grid gives me real designer names (Jimmy Choo, Amiri, Cinq à Sept) I recognize and trust.
- Clean single-CTA hero — no competing banners stacked on top of each other like Macy's tends to do.

## What's weak

- This is the third near-identical tiered-discount email from Neiman's this week — 8/26 "Limited time: Get up to $250 off" and 8/27's version both had the same $400/$1,000 tiers.
- Beauty and fragrance are explicitly excluded in the fine print — irrelevant to my actual shopping habit.
- No InCircle/loyalty points messaging anywhere, and no personal-shopper note that references anything about me specifically.
- The product grid is generic "new arrivals," not curated to anything I've bought or browsed before.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop repeating the identical tier promo three sends running — rotate in something else or at minimum vary the creative meaningfully, not just the subject line.
- 2. Add InCircle points value next to the discount — "Save up to $250 + earn X points" would actually pull a loyalty-driven shopper like me.
- 3. Swap two of the nine generic grid tiles for a "picked for you based on your last order" callout — even one personalized tile beats an anonymous wall.
- 4. Lead the fine print with what's included, not just excluded — right now beauty/fragrance exclusion is the first thing I clock as a counter regular.
