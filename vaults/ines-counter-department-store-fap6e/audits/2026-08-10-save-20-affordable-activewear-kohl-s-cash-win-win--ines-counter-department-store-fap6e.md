# Save 20% | Affordable activewear + Kohl's Cash ... win-win 🏆
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again — number eight this week if I'm counting right, and I am, because this is starting to feel like they're testing how many near-identical emails I'll tolerate before I unsubscribe. The status bar's back — "KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER · Earn: 5% REWARDS" — fine, consistent, I'll give them that. But then it's straight into "Take an extra 20% off" with a coupon code, then a Kohl's Cash banner, then a giant activewear hero screaming "UP TO 40% OFF," then a K-Pop Demon Hunters backpack, then a sweepstakes for a $200 gift card, then a random grid of a bath towel, a slushie maker, jeans, and a mattress topper under "Picked with you in mind." None of that is picked with me in mind — that's just clearance inventory dressed up as personalization.
- The activewear hero is actually decent — Nike, Under Armour, New Balance logos stacked on the left, a model in a red hoodie, "up to 40% off" in a clean red block. That's the one moment this email knows what it's selling. Everything after it is noise. I'm not buying a Ninja slushie maker or a mattress topper because Kohl's told me my "great taste" points there — that line is almost insulting given what's under it.
- Would I click? Maybe the activewear tile, since New Balance is one of the few things in my closet from this brand. Forward to a friend? No. Mark as read and move on — this is Kohl's on autopilot, and I've now seen this exact playbook, activewear sale plus Kohl's Cash plus grab-bag "picked for you" tile, at least twice this week already.

## What's working

- The activewear hero is genuinely legible — brand logos, a real model, "up to 40% off" in a color-blocked callout that's easy to scan.
- The rewards status bar up top is consistent with every other Kohl's send this week, so at least that's a reliable anchor.
- "Shop Active Sale" is a clear, specific button sitting right under the offer.
- The 20% off coupon code and expiration date (Aug. 16) are both visible without digging.

## What's weak

- The "Picked with you in mind. (Your great taste makes it easy.)" section is a towel, a slushie machine, jeans, and a mattress topper — that's not curation, that's clearance filler with a flattering headline slapped on it.
- Way too much crammed in: activewear sale, K-Pop Demon Hunters backpack tie-in, a sweepstakes, then the random product grid, then coupons again at the bottom. My eye has nowhere to land after the hero.
- This is the same "20% off + Kohl's Cash" combo I've now seen from Kohl's four times in the last two days — the cadence is exhausting and it's training me to stop opening.
- The K-Pop backpack banner feels like it wandered in from a completely different email — jarring next to "Serta Gel Memory Foam Mattress Topper."

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email down to one offer. Lead with the activewear sale since it's the only section with real visual discipline, and kill the towel/slushie/jeans/mattress grid entirely — it dilutes the one good idea in here.
- 2. Stop calling random clearance items "picked with you in mind" — either build real personalization off my purchase history or drop the pretense. Nordstrom and Saks don't insult me with fake curation; Kohl's shouldn't either.
- 3. Space this campaign out. Four sends in 48 hours with the same coupon code is the fastest way to get marked as read on sight.
- 4. Move the K-Pop tie-in and sweepstakes into their own dedicated send — they're competing with the sale message, not supporting it.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Activewear up to 40% off + your 20% coupon`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New Balance, Nike, Under Armour — all on sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus earn Kohl's Cash on every $50 you spend`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Coupon ends Aug. 16 — activewear starts at 40% off`

# Technical Audit: Kohl's "Save 20%" Promotional Email

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard MSO/legacy-doctype marketing template with heavy tracking instrumentation (Movable Ink, Adobe AAM, coherentpath click tracker); several CAN-SPAM/authentication signals were not detectable in the provided source and header data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 63 tracking/redirect links (`click.chp.kohls.com`, `click.s.kohls.com`, `mi.kohls.com`) were present but skipped by automated HTTP probing since they are click-redirect domains — destination validity is therefore unconfirmed and should be manually click-tested.
- Multiple 1x1 tracking pixels present: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...`, `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`, `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx...`, `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`, plus several `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` retargeting pixels — consistent with expected ESP open/click/AAM instrumentation, no anomaly found.
- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow" />` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer" />` are present — expected/intentional for transactional marketing HTML, not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 9 images flagged with missing `alt` text, all tracking pixels/beacons (0×0 or 1×1 dimension): `ed3bfb1d...`, `o.gif`, `event` (demdex), and six `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` beacons. Low severity since these are non-visual tracking assets (`aria-hidden="true"` is present on at least one), but should still carry `alt=""` for strict HTML validation/spam-filter scoring.
- Legacy `<!DOCTYPE HTML PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD HTML 4.01 Transitional//EN">` with MSO conditional comments (`<!--[if gte mso 9]>`) — standard for Outlook desktop compatibility, no issue.
- Malformed markup: `<img ... aria-hidden="true" /></custom>` — a stray closing `</custom>` tag with no matching opening tag, immediately after the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` pixel. Not rendering-critical (browsers/clients will ignore it) but indicates unclean template generation.
- Responsive breakpoint at `max-width: 500px` with `.hide-sm`/`.show-sm` classes present — mobile-responsive scaffolding in place, no issues found in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{firstName}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) visible in the truncated source.
- `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` (in the `916d6fa08d185233.png` beacon URL) has an empty value — a Movable Ink personalization/countdown field that failed to populate. Worth flagging to the ESP/Movable Ink integration if this field drives dynamic content (e.g., offer end-date countdown).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not found — one-click unsubscribe via mailbox provider UI will not be offered; may be a capture gap in the relay rather than the sender's actual headers.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)**: not found — one-click POST unsubscribe unsupported per this data.
- **Physical mailing address**: not detected anywhere in the provided source — CAN-SPAM requires this; likely present in the truncated footer (source was cut off before the footer/unsubscribe block), so this should be re-verified against the full HTML rather than treated as confirmed absent.
- **Authentication-Results header**: not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status could not be verified from available data; expected via AgentMail relay per QA tooling.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- Not verifiable from the truncated source — no visible destination URLs with UTM parameters were present outside the skipped tracking-redirect links (`click.chp.kohls.com`, `click.s.kohls.com`). Since the underlying destination URLs are wrapped by click-tracking redirects, UTM/landing-page alignment cannot be assessed without resolving the redirects.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run QA against the full (untruncated) HTML to confirm whether a physical address and unsubscribe link exist in the footer before treating this as a compliance failure.
2. Resolve the 63 skipped tracking-redirect links to confirm final destination URLs and UTM parameter alignment with landing pages.
3. Add `alt=""` to the 9 flagged tracking-pixel `<img>` tags for markup hygiene.
4. Remove the stray `</custom>` closing tag near the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` pixel.
5. Confirm with ESP/relay why `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` headers weren't captured — verify at the raw SMTP/MIME header level, not just rendered HTML.
6. Investigate the empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte` parameter if it feeds a live countdown/personalization element.
