# Your exclusive access ends tonight!
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Three emails from Neiman Marcus in under 48 hours. I want to name that before I say anything else, because it shapes everything that follows. Yesterday's welcome — "You're on the List" — was earned. This morning's brand editorial was a stretch. And now, same day, I'm getting a sale clock. The sequence isn't wrong exactly; it's just wearing me out before I've even opened.
- But here's the thing: the content itself is actually the most compelling of the three. This is a genuine designer sale — Jimmy Choo, Brunello Cucinelli, Carolina Herrera, Jacquemus in a single grid — not a basket of department-store clearance the way Kohl's fills its emails with everything they need to move. The "exclusive access before the public" framing is event language, and I am an event shopper. That line worked on me. The 40% off in the hero is real enough to stop the scroll.
- What I can't forgive is the loyalty gap. I have an InCircle account. I carry the Neiman Marcus card specifically because of the points. Not once in this email does NM acknowledge that. Saks Fifth Avenue's SaksFirst sends consistently open with your tier status before they drop the sale — that one detail tells me I'm a known customer, not a cold prospect. This email handed me a promo code like I walked in off the street. I'll probably click. But I'll feel like a stranger doing it.

## What's working

- The brand grid is perfectly calibrated for someone like me — Brunello Cucinelli, Carolina Herrera, and Jacquemus together says "we know what you're shopping," and that's enough to earn a scroll
- "Exclusive access before the public" is the right register for Neiman Marcus — event-first, quiet confidence, not a markdown shout
- The hero earns its keep: "EXCLUSIVE ACCESS" lands the luxury signal, "40% OFF" closes the deal, and the code NMSALE is right there — no second click needed to learn the terms
- Clean render, no broken images, type is readable without zooming — this passes the basic phone test
- "THE DESIGNER SALE" label above the hero is understated but positions it correctly as a curated event, not a clearance run

## What's weak

- No InCircle mention anywhere — the loyalty program that's supposed to differentiate NM from Nordstrom Rack is completely absent, and for a card-carrying member that's a missed conversion lever
- "Up to 40% off" + "Exclusions Apply" beneath the CTA is the Macy's playbook, not the Saks playbook — it immediately makes me wonder what's actually 40%
- The urgency "ends tonight" lives in the subject but vanishes inside the email body — no deadline date, no countdown, no time anchor anywhere in the visual hierarchy
- SHOP MEN'S gets full equal weight in the footer CTA row alongside SHOP WOMEN'S and SHOP HANDBAGS — I have no reason to be in the men's category, and it signals mass send, not segmentation
- Third email of the day from one brand is activating my unsubscribe reflex, not my credit card

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put the deadline inside the email.** "Offer ends midnight tonight, May 17" near the CTA or as a banner above the hero. The subject set up urgency; the body has to deliver it or the click drops off.
- 2. **Open with InCircle.** Even a single line above the hero — "InCircle members earn points on sale purchases" — closes the gap between "stranger with a promo code" and "valued customer with a reason to use her NM card." That line could meaningfully move conversion among cardholders.
- 3. **Kill the MEN'S CTA for this segment** or at minimum stack it below a widened WOMEN'S button. If NM knows enough to show me Carolina Herrera and Gianvito Rossi, they know I'm not cross-shopping menswear.
- 4. **Swap "up to 40% off" for a floor.** "40% off hundreds of designer styles" reads more honestly and doesn't invite the Macy's comparison this brand is trying to escape.
- 5. **Slow the send cadence.** Three emails in two days from a luxury brand erodes the exclusivity signal faster than any competitor could.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Designer sale: 40% off Jimmy Choo, Brunello & more — tonight only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `InCircle exclusive: your designer sale access ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Brunello Cucinelli, Carolina Herrera, Jacquemus — before the public gets in. Code NMSALE.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `40% off select designers, ends midnight. Your InCircle points apply.`

### 1. Technical Summary

The email uses a Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) AMPscript template with several unresolved variable patterns visible in the source comment block. Automated QA flagged authentication header gaps and a plain-text part that is overwhelmingly URL-heavy, both of which carry deliverability risk.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**27 tracking links skipped (unprobed)**
All click links are redirect-wrapped (likely `click.e.neimanmarcus.com` or equivalent). QA could not verify destination reachability or UTM integrity for any of them. If any redirect chain is broken, clicks silently 404 with no alert.

**Plain-text body is 83% URLs**
4,790 of 5,787 characters in the plain-text alternative are raw URLs. This is a known spam-filter signal (Spamhaus, Barracuda). A properly generated plain-text part should strip redirect URLs and use human-readable labels or bare destination paths.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Commented-out conflicting viewport meta tags**
Three `<meta name="viewport">` directives are commented out directly above the active one. These are dead code but indicate prior breakage; no active rendering bug, but worth cleaning.

**`<title>` has a trailing space**
```html
<title>Neiman Marcus </title>
```
Minor but affects preheader rendering in some clients that surface the title tag.

**No `alt` text verifiable from truncated source**
The source is truncated before `<img>` tags appear. Cannot confirm alt-text coverage — this should be verified against the full source.

**CSS animation hack shipped to production**
```css
body { -webkit-animation: bugfix infinite 1s; }
@-webkit-keyframes bugfix { from { padding: 0; } to { padding: 0; } }
```
This is a known iOS 9 hamburger-nav fix. iOS 9 is end-of-life (2019). Recommend removing to reduce render jank on current clients.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**AMPscript comment block exposes internal variable schema**
The comment at the top of the HTML is rendered verbatim in the delivered source:

```
<!--REQUIRED variables: @email ... @base_refid ... @refid ... @user ... @emailid ... @uEm ... @cmdid ... CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW -->
```

This discloses internal data-extension names (`NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`), lookup logic, and field names (`uEm`, `cmdid`) to any recipient who views source. Should be stripped at render time or moved to a server-side AMPscript block with no HTML comment wrapper.

**SHA-256 hash token commented out but present**
```
<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_SHA256_EMAIL */-->
```
If this block is ever accidentally uncommented in a variant, the hashed PII goes into every link. Current state is inert but the comment should be removed.

**`@refid` is a no-op concatenation**
```
@refid = CONCAT(@base_refid,"")
```
Concatenating with an empty string does nothing. Either a placeholder that was never completed or dead code — either way it indicates the refid may not be set correctly.

---

### 5. Compliance

**List-Unsubscribe header not detected**
QA found no `List-Unsubscribe` header. Gmail and Apple Mail require this for the native unsubscribe UI; Google enforces it for bulk senders (>5k/day) under its 2024 policy. Missing this header risks placement in the Promotions tab or triggering the "unsubscribe" prompt that bypasses the sender's flow.

**List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not detected**
One-click unsubscribe (the machine-readable `POST` variant) is absent. Required for Gmail bulk sender compliance as of February 2024.

**Authentication-Results header not captured**
SPF and DKIM pass/fail status is unknown due to AgentMail relay not forwarding the `Authentication-Results` header. Cannot confirm DMARC alignment. This is a monitoring gap, not a confirmed failure, but it must be resolved to audit deliverability.

**Sending domain:** `e.neimanmarcus.com` — DKIM/SPF should be verified directly against this subdomain's DNS records if header capture is unavailable.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**UTM parameters unverifiable**
All 27 CTA links are redirect-wrapped and were skipped by the HTTP prober. Cannot confirm whether UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content`) are appended before or after the redirect resolves. If they are appended to the redirect target rather than the redirect URL itself, some analytics platforms will lose attribution on the first hop.

**No issues found** with landing page alignment (cannot assess without probing redirect destinations).
