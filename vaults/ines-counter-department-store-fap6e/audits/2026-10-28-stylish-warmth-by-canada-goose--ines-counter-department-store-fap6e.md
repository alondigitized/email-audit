- The nav (Women, Men, Shoes, Sale) is simple, and the logo is crisp and trustworthy.

## What's weak

- There's no offer, price or deadline anywhere. Macy's at least gave me "extra 25% off" with a clock on it.
- The button text is thin gray on white, so it's hard to read on a phone. The tap targets feel light too.
- There's no mention of the gift-card promo or any loyalty benefit. I won't use a card that doesn't give me points back, and nothing here says what I'd earn.
- The model and styling skew much younger than me. One jacket in one colorway also gives me no range to browse.
- The footer is crowded with small text and underlined links, and it has no product detail like warmth rating, fit or sizes.

- 2. Show three or four Canada Goose styles with prices, including a longer parka and a range of ages and colors. Add warmth and fit detail.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom. This fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Comment before DOCTYPE:** a large `<!--REQUIRED variables…-->` block precedes `<!DOCTYPE …>`. Some legacy engines can drop into quirks mode when anything but whitespace comes before the DOCTYPE.
- **Fragile mobile selectors:** `*[class=contenttable]`, `*[class=mobile]`, `*[class=hide]` and similar are exact-match attribute selectors. They stop matching if an element carries more than one class, so the mobile overrides fail silently.
- **Hamburger menu:** the nav depends on the checkbox hack (`.navbar-checkbox:checked~.menu`). Gmail and Outlook don't support it, so those clients need a fallback.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` is the same for every send and has a trailing space.
- **Alt text and `lang`:** not assessable because the source is truncated.
