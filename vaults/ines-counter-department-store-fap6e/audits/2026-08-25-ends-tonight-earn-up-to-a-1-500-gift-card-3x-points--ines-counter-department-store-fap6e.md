# Ends tonight: Earn up to a $1,500 gift card + 3x Points
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-25**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the third act of the same play — Neiman's has now run this gift-card-and-Triple-Points offer three days running: "Reminder: Earn up to a $1,500 gift card" on the 21st I'm inferring, then "Ends soon" yesterday (8/23), and now "Ends tonight" today. I recognize the mint-green tiered table, I recognize the La Mer and Manolo Blahnik and Jimmy Choo grid, I recognize the InCircle Triple Points banner. At this point it's less "limited time offer" and more "we really need you to buy something before month-end."
- That said, the mechanics are still genuinely good — a real spend/earn table ($500→$75 up to $10,000+→$1,500), a promo code, InCircle Triple Points stacked on top, and a product grid that's speaking my language: La Mer moisturizer, Burberry knit, Versace loafers, a Manolo kitten heel. This is a legitimate beauty-and-fragrance-inclusive event, which matters to me specifically.
- Would I click? Probably, because "ends tonight" with a real deadline (10:00 PM CT) does create urgency, and I do want to know if my La Mer purchase would clear the $500 tier. But I wouldn't forward this to a friend — it's the same email I've now seen three times this week with a new subject slapped on. I'd mark it read, maybe screenshot the spend table for myself, and move on without much emotional lift.

## What's working

- The spend/earn table is dead simple — I can do the math on my own cart in two seconds, no clicking required.
- "Includes Beauty & Fragrance" called out explicitly — a lot of these promos exclude beauty, so naming it up front respects that I'm a counter shopper.
- Triple Points banner for InCircle members is a real loyalty tie-in, not a generic "join now" ask.
- Product grid mixes accessible (Mac Duggal) with aspirational (La Mer, Manolo Blahnik, Burberry) — feels like they know I browse both ends.
- Clean editorial black nav bar and mint palette — same visual DNA as yesterday's "$1,500 gift card" send, so it reads as trustworthy and on-brand, unlike some of Macy's more chaotic templates.

## What's weak

- This is the third near-identical send on this exact offer in three days — Sephora's App Fest emails at least varied subject urgency across sends, but repeated the "0 points" problem four times; Neiman's is now doing its own version of promo fatigue.
- "Ends tonight" buried at the very bottom in fine print says "Ends 8/25/26 at 10:00 PM CT" — that specific deadline should be bigger and closer to the hero, not four screens down in legal text.
- No personalization anywhere — no "Ines" or "as an InCircle member you've already earned X points," just generic broadcast copy, even though Neiman's clearly has my loyalty tier data (they used it for the Brunello Cucinelli send on 8/24, which scored an 8 with me specifically because it named my tier).
- Exclusions are vague at point of scan ("Exclusions Apply" small text) — I have to dig into the bottom legal block to find out select brands, prior purchases, and Bergdorf don't qualify.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the actual countdown ("Ends tonight at 10 PM CT") in the hero banner, not buried in the footer — that's the single biggest miss given the subject line promises urgency the body doesn't visually deliver.
- 2. Personalize the send the way the 8/24 Brunello Cucinelli email did — even a line like "You're $340 from your next gift-card tier" using my purchase history would make this seven times more relevant than generic broadcast copy.
- 3. Space this campaign out — three sends in three days on the same offer is Sephora-level fatigue; if InCircle members are tracked, suppress repeat sends to people who already opened and didn't convert.
- 4. Move "Includes Beauty & Fragrance" up into the subject line itself — that's the detail that gets a counter-regular like me to open before I even see the body.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: Your beauty & fragrance purchase earns up to $1,500`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Final hours: $1,500 gift card + Triple Points on your next order`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `10 PM CT tonight — La Mer, Burberry & more all qualify`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Beauty, fragrance, and fall arrivals all count toward your gift card`
