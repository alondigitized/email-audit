# LAST DAY for Epic Deals & 3x rewards for Kohl's Rewards members 👀
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-27**
## Executive summary

- Same red tile wall, sixth or seventh time this week — I clocked it on the 23rd, 24th, 25th, and 26th (twice), and here it is again dressed up as "LAST DAY." The handbag hero at top is a nice enough prop, and I'll give Kohl's credit for putting the burgundy quilted bag front and center instead of another fleece pile, but by now I know this template better than my own closet. Nothing here says "you, specifically" — it's toddler tees, bedding, jeans, Huggar slacks, jewelry, floor care, and a toy sale all stacked one after another like a Sunday circular.
- I'm not clicking. There's no single offer that pulls me in — it's ten different departments each shouting their own percentage, and my eye just slides down without landing anywhere. Compare this to Macy's "LAST CHANCE: confirm now" email I logged — that one at least had a specific mechanic (confirm for an extra 25%) even if it was dressed up urgency theater. This is just... more Kohl's. Not forwarding it, not flagging it as read-worthy, just closing the tab.

## What's working

- The handbag hero photo is genuinely a nicer visual anchor than the recycled fleece shots I've seen all week.
- "ENDS TODAY" in the top banner is at least a real, credible deadline rather than manufactured urgency.
- The Kohl's Rewards 3x banner with the shield icon is a clear loyalty callout — I am a member, so that's the one section that acknowledges it.
- The $10 off $50 baby/toddler/kids coupon nested inside the family-photo block is a concrete, specific offer with real numbers.

## What's weak

- This is the same red "EPIC DEALS" tile wall I've now logged five-plus times this week — Sept 23, 24, 25, 26 (twice), and now the 27th. The fatigue is real.
- Zero personalization — nothing here reflects that I'm a 47-year-old beauty-counter shopper, not a parent buying toddler tees and toy sets.
- The layout is an undifferentiated scroll: tees, bedding, towels, sweaters, jeans, Huggar pants, jewelry, handbags, a turntable, floor care, toys, more towels, mattress toppers. Nothing tells my eye where to land first.
- "Coupons do not apply" appears repeatedly right under the big percentages — that's the fine-print bait-and-switch feeling Saks would never let through.
- No product that speaks to my actual category (beauty, fragrance, designer accessories) — the jewelry and handbag tiles are the closest, and they're buried mid-scroll.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick ONE hero offer and lead with it — right now ten departments compete and none win. Neiman Marcus's boot roundup at least had a single strong visual moment even if it was thin on offer; Kohl's has the opposite problem, too many offers and no focal point.
- 2. Segment this — if I've never clicked toddler or toy content, stop showing it to me. Show jewelry, handbags, home if that's my click history.
- 3. Cut the redundant "Coupons do not apply" repetition or bundle it into one disclosure at the bottom instead of stamping it under every third tile.
- 4. Retire this red-tile template for at least two weeks — I've seen it enough times to recite the font choice from memory.
- 5. Move the loyalty 3x rewards banner higher and make it visually distinct from the discount tiles, since that's the actual member benefit, not just another red box.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Kohl's Rewards are about to expire — last call`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Members: stack 3x rewards with today's markdowns`
