# 15% off fragrance, beauty & more
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- This is the Neiman's I expect: calm, a pink block, a serif headline, and no shouting. After yesterday's "The more you buy, the more you save" tier table, a flat 15% on beauty and fragrance is easy to read. The offer is stated once and plainly. There's no countdown clock and no emoji, which is why I trust Neiman's more than Kohl's. Kohl's sent me the same hollow red "EXTRA" header three days running.
- But 15% is thin for a beauty counter regular like me. I buy at brand events because I get gifts with purchase and points back. Nothing here mentions Neiman's InCircle points, a gift with purchase, or a points multiplier. Sephora's 2X/5X points email told me in two seconds what I'd earn. This one tells me I'll save a bit on "select" items, and I don't know which ones until I click. The real end date, 10/4 at 10 PM CT, is in the fine print at the bottom, while the hero says "limited time."
- Would I click? Probably, because La Mer, La Prairie and Guerlain are my lane. I'd go in checking whether my products are excluded, and I'd be annoyed if they were. I wouldn't forward it. A friend wants a deal that sounds like one.

## What's working

- The hero is calm. "15% OFF BEAUTY" in big serif on blush pink reads as luxury, not clearance. It's much quieter than Macy's "LAST CHANCE" push.
- The product grid leads with La Mer, La Prairie, Guerlain, Dr. Barbara Sturm and Chantecaille. That's real skincare, not filler, and it signals this is the counter I shop.
- Brand names only under each product, with no prices or starbursts, keeps the Neiman's restraint I noticed in the 9/29 Roundup.
- "Discount auto-applied at checkout" under the button is useful. I don't have to hunt for a code.
- The four category buttons at the bottom (Shop Beauty, Fragrance, Makeup, Skincare) are specific and tap-friendly.

## What's weak

- There's nothing for members. I get no InCircle points, no tier perk and no gift with purchase. For someone who won't put a card down without points back, that's the big miss.
- The end date (10/4, 10 PM CT) is only in the fine print. The hero says "Limited Time," which tells me nothing.
- "Select beauty" is vague, and the exclusions are dense and tiny. I can't tell whether La Mer is in or out. That's exactly the thing I'd want to know before I click.
- The hero button and the "Discover must-have skin care and scents" line are set in a light monospace type that's hard to read on a phone. The hero's "SHOP NOW" is generic.
- It's the third discount email from them in three days, after the 9/30 "Ends today" send and the 10/1 tier table. The pink-on-pink bottom buttons also look faded next to the black hero button.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put my loyalty benefit in the hero, for example "15% off + 2X InCircle points on beauty." Sephora does this well, and it's what makes me buy now instead of waiting for a brand event.
- 2. Move the real deadline into the hero ("Ends Sunday, 10/4") and drop the vague "limited time."
- 3. Add a one-line list of what's included and what's excluded, or at least a "See what's included" link. Right now I have to guess.
- 4. Link each product to its own page, and add a price or a "from" price on two or three of them so I can see the value.
- 5. Use a stronger, specific button such as "Shop 15% off skincare" in a readable sans-serif, and make the lower buttons the same contrast as the hero button.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a standard table-based XHTML Transitional layout. It has a leaked ESP (AMPscript) template comment, a zoom-blocking viewport tag, and a checkbox-hack mobile nav. The automated QA could not confirm unsubscribe headers, SPF/DKIM, or any of the 28 tracked links, and the HTML was truncated, so footer and link-level checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Not verifiable:** 28 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so redirect destinations and status codes are unconfirmed. This is a coverage gap, not a defect.
- **Plain-text ratio:** 4965 of 6102 plain-text characters (81%) are URLs, which exceeds the 70% threshold. This points to long click-tracking URLs rendered inline in the text part, and it is a possible spam-filter signal.
- No broken or malformed links are confirmed in the visible source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing language attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang`, so screen readers can't select the correct pronunciation.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 resize-text concern.
- **Checkbox-hack nav:** the mobile hamburger relies on `.navbar-checkbox:checked~.menu`, with `.menu{display:none}` as the default. Gmail and several Outlook clients don't support `:checked`, and the pattern is poor for keyboard users. In those clients the nav is unreachable, though the visible source can't show whether a fallback exists.
- **Fragile selectors:** `*[class=contenttable]`, `*[class=hide]` and similar are exact-match attribute selectors, so they fail if an element carries more than one class. They also depend on client support for attribute selectors.
- **Comment before the DOCTYPE:** the leaked comment block precedes `<!DOCTYPE>`. Some older IE-based and Outlook rendering engines drop into quirks mode when anything precedes the doctype.
- **Minor:** `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` has a trailing space and is generic.
- **Not assessable:** image `alt` text and any text-in-image issues fall in the truncated portion.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Leaked template comment:** the sent HTML begins with `<!--REQUIRED variables: @email … @base_refid … @refid = CONCAT(@base_refid,"") @user Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", …) … @uEm = Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "uEm", …) @cmdid … CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW -->`.
  - It exposes internal Data Extension names (`NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`), field names (`uEm`, `cmdid`), and a SHA-256 hashed-email scenario note (`SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256`).
  - It also shows that the template was shipped with the "choose one scenario" instruction unresolved. This is information disclosure and template hygiene debt.
- No unrendered `%%…%%` or `@var` tokens are confirmed in the visible body text.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe not found:** QA flagged this with a note that the AgentMail relay may strip it. It is unconfirmed, and it should be checked against the raw message at the sending MTA.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post not found:** RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it, so verify it before treating it as a pass.
- **Authentication-Results not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `e.neimanmarcus.com` is a subdomain, but alignment can't be confirmed from the data given.
- **Footer not seen:** the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address fall in the truncated HTML, so they are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not verifiable:** the tracked links were skipped, and no destination URLs or `utm_*` parameters appear in the visible source.
- **Likely `refid`:** the template variables `@refid` / `@base_refid` suggest campaign attribution runs through a proprietary `refid` parameter rather than `utm_*`. This is an inference from the comment, not confirmed from link output.
- Landing page alignment with the "15% off fragrance, beauty & more" subject can't be checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Strip the AMPscript/template comment block from the rendered output, for example by using `%%[ ]%%` server-side blocks rather than HTML comments. Resolve the "choose one scenario" step in the template.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag, and add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
3. Add a non-`:checked` fallback for the hamburger nav, such as a visible "Shop" link row or a link to the web version.
4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` against the raw message, bypassing the relay. Confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC from the original headers.
5. Resolve the 28 redirect URLs through a headers-only follow-up probe to check final destinations, status codes and any `utm_*` or `refid` parameters.
6. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to cover the footer, `alt` text, and the unsubscribe and address checks.
