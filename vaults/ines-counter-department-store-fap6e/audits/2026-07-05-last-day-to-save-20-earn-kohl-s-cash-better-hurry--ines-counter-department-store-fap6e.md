# LAST DAY to save 20% + earn Kohl's Cash ... better hurry 🏃‍♀️‍➡️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-05**
## Executive summary

- Forty-three. That is my rough running count of Kohl's in my inbox since Memorial Day, and this one arrives with "LAST DAY" in full capitals like that's going to snap me out of the very numbness their own cadence created. I opened it — I always do, because I am a loyalty-points person and Kohl's Cash is real money — but I was already skeptical before the email finished loading. The July 4th send did the same mechanic in a flag wrapper. The July 3rd send did it with two checkmarks. Today it is a running-woman emoji.
- Once I was inside, the email is functional and clean. The TEK GEAR hero is legible, the 70% clearance callout in orange reads fast, and the specific timestamp — "Ends July 6, 05:00" — is the most credible piece of urgency I've seen from Kohl's this week. Sephora pulled this off in their "Ends 7/7" send and it worked on me; Kohl's finally figured out that "limited time" is not the same as a clock. That's the one win here.
- But I am a beauty-counter woman. I cross-shop Nordstrom, Macy's, Saks. And this email sent me to activewear and kids' go-mode styles without a single nod to beauty or skincare or fragrance — all of which Kohl's sells. I would mark this as read. I would not forward it. If I needed athletic shorts, I'd click; I don't, so I won't.

## What's working

- **Specific deadline is credible**: "Ends July 6, 05:00" shown near the barcode section — a timestamped close beats the vague "hurry" in the subject line.
- **Kohl's Cash earning terms are visible**: The $50-per-earn threshold and code (KCHKFRD) are surfaced together — as someone who tracks points obsessively, I'd screenshot this even if I don't click today.
- **TEK GEAR hero is clean**: Price, savings range, and a single Shop Now button without paragraphs of copy — that section would actually convert if I were the target.
- **70% clearance callout is punchy**: The orange block reads fast, "New Markdowns Taken" implies fresh rotation, and that number stops a scroll.
- **No render problems**: Clean load, nothing broken, no layout collapse — baseline but not guaranteed in every send.

## What's weak

- **Zero beauty content**: Kohl's has a beauty section; I am a beauty-counter regular. Neiman Marcus tied their "Summer-break style" send to fashion and beauty in the same scroll. Kohl's didn't try. I don't exist in this email.
- **"LAST DAY" has been spent**: This is at minimum the fourth urgency-tier subject in one week from this address. The signal has been trained down to white noise — I scan past it the way I scan past grocery store "sale" tags.
- **Manual coupon code is friction**: KCHKFRD needs to be typed or copy-pasted. My Nordstrom Anniversary, my Macy's Star Rewards, my Sephora Rouge — all discount at checkout automatically. A code in 2026 feels like a brand that doesn't trust its own CRM.
- **Three "Shop Now" sections compete**: TEK GEAR, family styles, and clearance all want the same click. Nothing wins; I scroll past all three looking for what they actually want me to do.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Segment by purchase history and surface beauty**: If my Kohl's Card account has ever touched beauty or fragrance, lead me to that. A beauty + Kohl's Cash angle would have had me clicking, not reading.
- 2. **One "LAST DAY" per campaign, then retire it**: Use the deadline trigger on day one of the final send only. Daily LAST DAY burns the word out of the dictionary within a week.
- 3. **Apply the discount at the account level**: Ditch the coupon code. Premium-channel brands — Saks, Nordstrom — don't ask you to remember a code. Auto-apply 20% for Kohl's Card holders and stop making me work for my own discount.
- 4. **Collapse to one primary CTA above the fold**: One hero, one button. Let clearance and categories live below. Right now, everything shouts and nothing converts.
- 5. **Subject Alt A:** `Tonight: 20% off + Kohl's Cash — ends at 5am`
- 6. **Subject Alt B:** `Kohl's Cash deadline: tonight at 5am`
- 7. **Preheader Alt A:** `New clearance markdowns + your 20% off — no code needed at checkout`
- 8. **Preheader Alt B:** `70% off clearance + Kohl's Cash for every $50 spent`

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "LAST DAY 20% + Kohl's Cash" Email

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is a standard ESP-rendered HTML 4.01 Transitional build with no merge token or rendering blockers. The primary technical concerns are missing compliance signals (unsubscribe headers, physical address) and pervasive missing alt text on tracking pixels and content images.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 click-redirect links skipped by HTTP probe** — all links route through `click.s.kohls.com` (Salesforce MC) and `click.chp.kohls.com` (CoherentPath). No dead-link confirmation possible; monitor ESP click-tracking dashboard for 4xx redirects post-send.

**Three distinct tracking pixel domains in use simultaneously:**
- `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` — CoherentPath open pixel
- `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...` — MessageGears open pixel
- `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...` — Salesforce MC open pixel
- `kohls.demdex.net/event?...` — Adobe Audience Manager sync pixel

Three separate open-pixel vendors firing on render is unusual and indicates a multi-ESP / DMP stack with no consolidation. Each pixel is a redundant round-trip and a potential cross-device fingerprinting vector. Not a deliverability blocker, but worth confirming all are intentional.

**Malformed HTML tag:** `</custom>` closing tag present in the pixel block (`...alt=""></custom>`). This is invalid HTML and has no matching open tag — browsers will silently ignore it, but strict parsers and some ESPs may flag it.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`<title>` is empty** — `<title></title>`. Several email clients (Outlook on Windows, Apple Mail preview) surface the title tag in threading or accessibility trees. An empty title is a minor screen-reader gap.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` prevents pinch-zoom. This is an accessibility regression for low-vision users on mobile and fails WCAG 2.1 SC 1.4.4.

**Alt text missing on 9 flagged images**, including content images (not just tracking pixels):
- `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png` — likely promotional/product images based on `email_name=260705_DG_Email_Sunday` param context. If images are blocked, these render as empty boxes with no text fallback.
- The three tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `event`, UUID pixel) are missing `alt=""` — they should carry an explicit empty alt to suppress screen-reader announcement.

**HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype** — not a bug, this is standard defensive email practice for Outlook compatibility.

**Google Fonts loaded three times** (two `<link>` tags + one `@import` block for each family). This is redundant but unlikely to cause rendering failures.

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** — QA relay did not capture this header. Major inbox providers (Gmail, Yahoo, Apple Mail) require `List-Unsubscribe` + `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) for one-click unsubscribe on bulk mail. Missing RFC 8058 support will suppress the native unsubscribe UI in Gmail for senders above the 5k/day threshold, directly impacting complaint rate.

**No physical mailing address detected in HTML source** — CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid physical postal address in the message body. This may be present in the truncated portion not provided; confirm before flagging as a live violation.

**`Authentication-Results` header not captured by AgentMail relay** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status unverifiable from the audit data. Kohl's sends from `s.kohls.com` (Salesforce subdomain); DKIM alignment to `kohls.com` should be confirmed via a live header inspection.

**`<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` are present** — correct practice for suppressing indexing and referrer leakage on email-viewed-in-browser versions.
