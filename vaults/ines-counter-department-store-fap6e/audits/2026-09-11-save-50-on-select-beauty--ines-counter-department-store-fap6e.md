# Save 50% on select beauty ❤️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-11**
## Executive summary

- Well, at least this one leads with an actual offer — "50% off Select Beauty" on Kiehl's, Bobbi Brown, and Azzaro, with a "TODAY ONLY" badge sitting right up top. That's a relief after this week's parade of stacking-weekend mush. But then it immediately pivots into the Beauty Insider Rewards Bazaar — a whole separate module with its own hero, its own "NEW" badge, its own "shop now" button — and I'm two screens deep before I've even processed the first offer. This is the same overstuffed habit Sephora's been running all week, just with a fresh coat of paint.
- The points catalog is actually kind of nice to scroll — Benefit brow pencil, K18 hair oil, Versace EDT, all at 100 points, then a 250-point tier with Anastasia Beverly Hills and Juliette Has A Gun. That's real Beauty Insider utility, something Neiman's loyalty program doesn't even attempt. But by the time I hit "Spot It. Shop It." with yet another two-tile promo for Black-owned brands and Prada, then a THIRD banner for "First Access: Holiday Drops," I've lost the thread entirely. Would I click? Maybe on the 50% off tile since it named actual brands I recognize. Forward to a friend? No — this is Sephora's inbox, not a curated one.

## What's working

- The "50% off Select Beauty" hero names real brands (Kiehl's, Bobbi Brown, Azzaro) instead of vague "select items" language — more concrete than Sephora's 9/9 and 9/8 sends.
- The points-reward catalog with product photography and point costs (100/250) is genuinely useful for someone like me who tracks my Beauty Insider balance.
- "You have 5 points" at the very top personalizes the header — a small nod that I'm a known member, not a stranger.
- Product photography throughout is clean and shoppable, no broken images or render bugs.

## What's weak

- Way too many competing CTAs — 50% off, Rewards Bazaar, Try It Love It, New From Prada, First Access Holiday Drops — five separate "shop now" moments in one email, worse than even Kohl's three-tier discount pileup.
- "Exclusions/terms apply" sits directly under the 50% off banner in tiny print — same buried-fine-print habit that's dinged Sephora every day this week.
- The "NEW REWARDS ARE HERE!" ticker text repeats itself like a scrolling marquee, which reads as filler, not information.
- No sense of hierarchy on what Sephora actually wants me to do first — Neiman Marcus's beauty event this week nailed one clear ask ($150 spend, free tote); this reads like five emails duct-taped together.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this down to one primary offer per send. If today's push is 50% off Kiehl's/Bobbi Brown/Azzaro, lead with it and stop — push the Rewards Bazaar, Prada launch, and holiday drops to separate sends or at minimum below a hard fold.
- 2. Move "Exclusions/terms apply" out of 8pt gray-on-red into something legible, or at least link it clearly.
- 3. Drop the repeating "NEW REWARDS ARE HERE!" ticker — it adds visual noise without adding information.
- 4. Give the points catalog its own dedicated email on a Rewards Bazaar cadence — it's genuinely good content getting buried under three other promos.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 100-point rewards just refreshed`
