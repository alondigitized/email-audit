# Kohl's Deal Days + Kohl's Cash are here today, gone tomorrow 👋
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-08**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again. The subject swaps in a waving hand, and I've now had about a dozen Deal Days sends since 10/4. The last two (the checkmarks on 10/7 and the 👀 one) were close to identical. At the top I get the same hollow maroon band I complained about on 10/5, with a speck of a logo and nothing in it. Below that it's a long scroll of percent-off tiles. Nothing here tells me the sender knows who I am.
- The offers are real: 40% off activewear, 50% off bedding, and a $10-off home coupon for Rewards members. But the subject says "gone tomorrow" while the body says "Ends October 8," which is today. That mismatch costs trust. I'm the woman who won't use a card without points back, so the "Kohl's Rewards Member / Earn 5%" strip at the very top did get a flicker out of me. The rest is sweaters, towels and toys, not what I shop Kohl's for.
- I wouldn't click, I'd mark it as read, and I wouldn't forward it. If I needed towels, I'd open it. I'd never open this for beauty.

## What's working

- The member status strip ("Kohl's Rewards Member · Earn 5%") at the top is a real loyalty cue. It's the one place I feel recognized.
- Each tile pairs a big percentage with a short category line, so I can scan it in about ten seconds.
- "Shop Now. Decide later. Free returns through January 31, 2027" is a good line. Returns policy matters to me, and it's stated plainly.
- The "Picked with you in mind" row shows real products with prices, like the Ninja SLUSH at $229.99 and the Gloria Vanderbilt jeans at $52. That reads more personal than the tiles above it.
- The promo codes are printed out in the footer (YOUSAVE25, YOUHOME), so I don't have to hunt for them.

## What's weak

- The hero is an empty maroon block with a tiny logo. It's the same hollow top I flagged on 10/5, 10/6 and 10/7, and it hasn't been fixed.
- The subject says "gone tomorrow," but the body says "Ends October 8." That's today. The urgency is fuzzy, and I've heard "last day" from Kohl's all week.
- The fine print wears me out: "Coupons do not apply to select brands," "Select styles," "+25% off w/coupon," and "$10 off $50 with coupon." The offer isn't honest at a glance.
- It's a wall of tiles with no hierarchy. Sweaters, Nike socks, NFL tees, toys and a mixer all get equal weight. Nothing here is aimed at a 47-year-old woman who shops the beauty counter.
- The preheader line at the very top is too small for me to read on my phone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the hero first. Put one real image and one offer in the top block, like "40% off + $10 Kohl's Cash, ends tonight." Right now the most valuable spot in the email is dead space.
- 2. Make the deadline match. Either say "ends tonight" in the subject, or stop saying "ends October 8" in the body.
- 3. Lead with the member benefit. A line like "You've got 5% back on everything today" would make me feel like a customer instead of a stranger.
- 4. Cut the tiles from about 11 to 4–5. Sephora Insider's "2X points" email is repetitive too, but it has one giant red offer and I know in a second what it is. Kohl's makes me hunt.
- 5. Segment. Show me beauty, since Kohl's has Sephora inside, or women's apparel, instead of kitchen appliances and toys.
- 6. Take a breather on cadence. Neiman Marcus's quiet Burberry send ("Forecast calls for Burberry") felt like someone lowering the volume. After a week of Deal Days, I'd welcome that.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day: $10 off your home order, Rewards members`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Activewear, bedding and towels, plus free returns through January 31.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your Kohl's Cash and the code YOUSAVE25. Deals end at midnight.`

## Technical Audit: Kohl's "Deal Days + Kohl's Cash" email

### 1. Technical Summary
The template is a table-based layout with a mobile breakpoint, but it has several markup defects: a stray `</custom>` tag, a `<div>` placed inside `<head>`, an empty `<title>`, and an empty icon `href`. It also carries four separate open-tracking pixels. Compliance and authentication could not be confirmed because the relay dropped the headers and the source is truncated.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **74 click-tracking links were not probed.** They go through `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=<opaque token>`, so destination URLs can't be checked from the source.
- **Four tracking pixels fire on open:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/5f7e5480-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` (1x1)
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1023415_2026108` (1x1)
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?25A3HQ3ISP7ENKKX57QZS526EE.60266&d=60266&bmt=0` (1x1)
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (0x0)
  
  These add request overhead and give inflated or duplicate open counts. The `demdex.net` call is a third-party Adobe Audience Manager endpoint.
- **Recipient and campaign identifiers sit in image URLs.** `mi_u=604230016` and `email_name=261008_DG_Email_Thursda…` are in the `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` URLs, so they are in plain text for any proxy or forwarded copy.
- **Stray closing tag:** `<img src="…open.aspx?…" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>` has no matching `<custom>` opener. That's invalid markup, and some parsers may mishandle it.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text, 12 images flagged by QA:**
  - **Tracking pixels (4):** the `click.chp.kohls.com/o/…` and `kohls.demdex.net/event` images have no `alt` attribute at all. The `o.gif` pixel already has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`. - **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **`<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` is inside `<head>`.** That's invalid, and parsers will close the head at that point.
- **`<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`** has an empty `href`.
- **Missing `role="presentation"`** on the preheader table (`<table width='640' … class="container">`) and on the 1x1 tracking tables. Other layout tables have it.
- **CSS typo:** `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` is missing the leading `.`, so it matches nothing. The Outlook block also has an empty rule, `td,th,div,p,a {}`.
- **Fixed 640px width against a 500px breakpoint.** The preheader table has `width: 640px`, but the mobile override only applies at `max-width: 500px`. Viewports of 501–640px may scroll horizontally.
- **Web fonts:** Google Fonts are loaded for non-Outlook clients only, and the font stack has `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` fallbacks. No issue.
