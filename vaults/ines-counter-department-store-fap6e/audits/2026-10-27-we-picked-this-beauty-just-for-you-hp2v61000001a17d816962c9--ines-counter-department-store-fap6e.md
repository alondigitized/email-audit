# We picked this beauty just for you! 🍒
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifteenth Sephora email I've had in about a week, and "chosen just for you" is a stretch. The hero is a big orange gradient with "Chosen Just for YOU" in a fancy script, and underneath it is a Gisou lip oil and honey mask. Everything below that is the usual catalog grid. Nothing in it says Sephora knows me. It doesn't mention my points, my tier, or anything I've bought. The Insider bar at the top says "You have 0 points," which is the one place it should have known better.
- The real offer is the Topicals trial size at the top, free with code HISKINLOVE on a $30 purchase. It sits above the hero and gets outshouted by it. The Gisou block is pretty, and "New • Only at Sephora • Limited Edition" is a decent hook. But the hero has no price, no offer and no reason to act today. The Sephora Credit Card banner near the bottom, "Get 25% off your first purchase," is the only real discount here, and it asks me to open a card.
- I'd skim it, maybe tap the Gisou lip oil if I were already shopping, and not forward it. Neiman Marcus at least put a number in the hero this week. Sephora just shows me a mood board.

## What's working

- The Gisou "Only at Sephora, Limited Edition" labels give me a reason to buy here instead of at Nordstrom or Saks.
- Prices are printed right on the products ($28, $26, $35, $45, $79). I don't have to click to find out whether something is $26 or $126.
- The Insider banner and the Beauty Insider tier bar near the bottom show the program exists, and "Rewards Bazaar" is a nice nod to points people.
- The Clean at Sephora badges are clear, and the Clarins serum and Versace fragrance are items I'd actually recognize at a counter.

## What's weak

- "Just for you" isn't backed up by anything. It's the same Gisou and fragrance grid everyone gets, so it reads like a mass send.
- The free Topicals offer, the best deal here, is squeezed above the hero in small type. The code and the $30 minimum are easy to miss, and the fine print at the bottom is tiny and dense.
- The email is long, with six sections and a credit card pitch tacked on near the end. It's the same catalog feel as "These gifts = 🌶️🌶️🌶️" on 10/26 and "This beauty is the talk of the town 📞" on 10/25.
- The lead product pictures are close-up smears of lip oil and mask. They're pretty, but I can't tell what the products are or who they're for.
- Nothing speaks to my age. There's no skincare for mature skin beyond the Clarins and Topicals mentions, and nothing about points multipliers or a Rouge event. Kohl's at least puts its math in the subject line.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in the hero. Make the Topicals trial size or a points multiplier the headline, and move the "Chosen just for you" script into a smaller line.
- 2. Show the member's real status. "You have 0 points" is a wasted chance, and a tier-based perk would make "for you" true.
- 3. Cut the grid to one hero product plus four picks, and move the credit card ad out of this send.
- 4. Add a deadline. Even "Code HISKINLOVE ends Sunday" would help.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free Topicals trial size with $30 🍒`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Gisou's new lip oil, only at Sephora`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code HISKINLOVE at checkout. Ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Limited edition Gisou, plus 25% off your first Sephora Card purchase.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible source and QA data show one confirmed failure (no plain-text part) and several accessibility gaps. Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified because the relay didn't capture the headers. The HTML is truncated, so footer, link and merge-token checks are limited to what's shown.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Only 12 of 59 links were probed, and no broken links were reported among them. The 47 skipped links, mostly footer and social, are unverified.
- Tracking infrastructure seen in the source:
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…` is an open-tracking pixel.
  - `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=4970269939971072&mi_ecmp=20261027_CS_Curated_For_You` is a second pixel carrying a user ID (`mi_u`) and a campaign ID (`mi_ecmp`).
  - Images are served from `images.harmony.epsilon.com`.
- Two separate open pixels in one message is redundant. It isn't a defect, but it doubles the third-party calls.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (3 images, QA-confirmed):**
  - `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg` is a header/nav image. If it carries nav text, that content is invisible to screen readers and to clients that block images.
  - The two tracking pixels (`app.sephora.com/O/…` and `o.gif`) should have `alt=""` rather than no attribute.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom in clients that honor it, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index" />` should be `noindex`. It has no practical effect in email.
- **Text sizing:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` is applied to `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td`. This disables user and OS text-size scaling in WebKit clients.
- `<title>Sephora</title>` is generic. This is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear in the visible source. The truncated portion wasn't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is 0 chars.** The email has no multipart/alternative text version. That hurts deliverability and accessibility. Confirm against the raw MIME, because the relay may have dropped it.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found.** The QA tool itself says AgentMail may not capture these headers, so this is unconfirmed. If they are truly absent, the message fails the Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender one-click requirement (RFC 8058).
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The relay is the likely cause, so no failure can be asserted.
- The physical address and visible unsubscribe link are in the footer, which the truncated HTML doesn't show. The QA data says an unsubscribe link was probed, but no result is reported.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=20261027_CS_Curated_For_You`, on the pixel. The truncated source shows no CTA hrefs, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be assessed. No mismatches were reported by the link probe.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a plain-text alternative, and verify the raw MIME to rule out a capture artifact.
2. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` from the original message headers, not the relay copy. Do the same for the SPF, DKIM and DMARC results.
3. Add descriptive alt text to `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg`, and `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta, and drop `text-size-adjust: none` where it isn't needed.
5. Fix `content="no index"` to `noindex`, or remove it.
6. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to cover CTA UTMs, the footer address and the unsubscribe link. Extend link probing beyond the top 12 links.
