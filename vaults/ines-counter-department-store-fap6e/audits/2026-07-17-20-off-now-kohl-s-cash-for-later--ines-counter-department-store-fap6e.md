# 20% off now + Kohl's Cash for later 🛍️
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-17**
## Executive summary

- Fifty-nine. That's where I am in my Kohl's count since Memorial Day — and this one arrives as the *second* email today, a few hours after the running-woman emoji send that already said the same thing in different shoes. "Like a beach day for your budget" is the hero, which is fine as summer framing goes, but by now that 20%-off-plus-Kohl's-Cash structure is a wallpaper pattern in my inbox and my eyes have learned to skip it.
- Here's what almost got me, though: buried in the Kohl's Cash block at the bottom is the line "Use cash on Sephora at Kohl's, July 20–29." That's the most relevant sentence in this entire email for someone like me, and it's sitting below a barcode, below a clearance banner, below sandals. If I'd scrolled past the barcode — which most people don't — I would have missed it entirely.
- I'm marking this read. I won't click, and I definitely won't forward it. If a friend asked me about Kohl's Cash redemption at Sephora, I'd tell her from memory — not because the email communicated it well, but because I happened to catch it on the way out.

## What's working

- **Concrete price anchors in the body.** $7.99 tops and $29.99 shorts with the reg prices crossed out — I can do the math. That's more honest than the vague "up to X% off" that Saks leans on in their summer event banners.
- **Sephora redemption window is visible** — even if buried. The fact that Kohl's Cash can go toward Sephora is a genuine loyalty benefit and it's the one detail that puts this email in my world at all.
- **No render chaos.** The email loaded cleanly, the grid held together, the barcode didn't break. After Macy's sent me that "confirm now" email last week that looked like it was stitched together at 11 PM, clean is something to appreciate.
- **Clearance call-out is clear.** "New markdowns taken / up to 70%" with a distinct section break reads fast — I can triage that as "maybe" without hunting for the offer.

## What's weak

- **Two emails in one day is one email too many.** Sephora Insider ran 10 emails in 16 days and I still noticed. Kohl's ran 4 emails in 3 days with the same creative skeleton. I'm developing email blindness — and I *buy here*.
- **The Sephora hook is on page two of scrolling.** Beauty redemption is the single most relevant signal for someone like me, and it's after the barcode. That is a burial, not a placement.
- **"Shop Now" tells me nothing.** Every button says Shop Now. Where? Tops? Sandals? Women's? Neiman Marcus uses editorial CTAs ("Explore Summer Aisle") that at least gesture at something. "Shop Now" is the retail version of a shrug.
- **The model and merch aren't speaking to me.** The woman in the hero is young, casual, beachy. I cross-shop Nordstrom and Macy's beauty. Basic cotton tees and flat sandals feel like they belong to a different subscriber list — not mine.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the Sephora redemption.** Make the Kohl's Cash earn/redeem block the hero or second block, not the postscript. "Earn $5 toward Sephora for every $50 you spend" is the one sentence that makes me feel like Kohl's knows I exist as a beauty buyer. Put it above the barcode.
- 2. **Kill the second daily email or differentiate it hard.** If you're going to send twice in one day, the second send needs to be a different category or a different angle entirely — not the same 20% off headline in beach clothing.
- 3. **Swap "Shop Now" for category CTAs.** "Shop Women's" / "Shop Sandals" / "Shop Clearance" — anything directional. It costs nothing and reduces bounce.
- 4. **Pull one beauty-adjacent product into the grid.** Even a Sephora-at-Kohl's skincare item at a featured price point gives me a visual reason to stay. Right now I see tops, shorts, sandals — none of which are my entry points.

- **Subject Alt B:** `20% off today + Sephora Cash starts now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `$5 in cash per $50 spent. Tops from $7.99, sandals to 40% off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Clearance up to 70% off + beauty Cash waiting for you.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "20% off + Kohl's Cash" (2026-07-17)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Standard retail HTML email built on an HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype with MSO conditional blocks and a dual-stack font-loading approach (Google Fonts via both `<link>` and `@import`). No critical rendering blockers found; flagged issues are compliance gaps and pervasive missing alt text on tracking pixels.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **22 click-tracking links skipped** by the QA probe — all route through `click.chp.kohls.com` and `click.s.kohls.com`. Destination URLs and redirect chains could not be validated; broken destination links would not surface in current QA coverage.
- **Three separate pixel/open-tracking systems** are present simultaneously:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` (CoherentPath)
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/.../o.gif` (MessageGears)
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` (Salesforce Marketing Cloud)
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event` (Adobe Audience Manager)
- Stacking four independent open-tracking pixels inflates open-rate counts and increases payload; expected for large retail but worth noting for accuracy of any downstream analytics.
- `<custom>` is an unclosed, non-standard tag wrapping the third open-pixel `<div>`: `</custom>` — this is invalid HTML and could cause parser errors in strict-mode clients.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **Eleven images missing `alt` text**, confirmed by QA — includes tracking pixels (acceptable) but also what appear to be content images (`2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`). If those are editorial/product images, screen readers receive no fallback.
- Tracking pixel images use `width="1" height="1"` but lack `aria-hidden="true"` on all instances — only the MessageGears pixel (`o.gif`) includes it. The CoherentPath and Demdex pixels do not.
- `<meta name="viewport" content="..., user-scalable=0">` disables pinch-zoom — an accessibility regression for low-vision users on mobile.
- `<title></title>` is empty. Some clients (notably Gmail desktop) display the title in the tab; screen readers may also announce it.
- Google Fonts are loaded via both `<link rel="stylesheet">` **and** `@import url()` for the same two families (Roboto, Montserrat) — this is a duplicate request that adds latency in clients that honor external CSS.
- Responsive breakpoint uses `max-width: 500px` with fixed pixel overrides (e.g., `.w_50pc { width: 207px }`). The class names imply percentage-based intent but the values are hard-coded — will break on devices between 500–641px if `.hide-lg` / `.show-sm` toggling doesn't compensate.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- No unresolved merge tokens visible in the truncated HTML source (no `{{`, `%%`, `[%`, or similar unfired placeholders).
- `mi_u=604230016` appears across all MessageGears pixel URLs — consistent subscriber ID parameter, no anomalies.
- `mi_ecmp=1017786_2026717` is a campaign ID that includes today's date (`2026717`) — correct for send date.
- `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` in one pixel URL (`916d6fa08d185233.png`) has an **empty value** — if this is a Kohl's Cash redemption end-date parameter, a blank value may indicate a template rendering failure or intentional omission. Flag for sender-side verification.

---

### 5. Compliance

- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** — QA confirms the header was not captured by the AgentMail relay. This header is required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk sender policies (enforced since February 2024) for senders above 5,000/day. Its absence via relay may be a relay capture artifact, but should be verified against raw headers at the MTA.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found** — RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is not confirmed. Same caveat applies re: relay capture.
- **`Authentication-Results` header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown from this capture. Kohl's sending domain `s.kohls.com` is a dedicated ESP subdomain and is almost certainly properly authenticated, but the gap means this audit cannot confirm it.
- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` is present — correct practice to prevent email HTML from being indexed if forwarded/published.
- `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` suppresses referrer on outbound clicks — consistent with privacy-forward practice but means destination sites cannot identify the email as a traffic source without UTM parameters (see §6).

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

- All 22 destination URLs route through click-tracking redirects — UTM parameter presence on final landing URLs cannot be confirmed without resolving the redirect chains.
- `email_name=260717_DG_Email_Fri_PM` appears in several MessageGears pixel URLs — campaign naming convention is consistent and date-stamped, which is good for analytics attribution.
- Given `no-referrer` meta tag (§5), UTM params on click-through URLs are **required** for GA4 / attribution to work. If any CTA links lack `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, and `utm_campaign`, those sessions will land as direct/none in analytics. Cannot verify without resolving click redirects.
