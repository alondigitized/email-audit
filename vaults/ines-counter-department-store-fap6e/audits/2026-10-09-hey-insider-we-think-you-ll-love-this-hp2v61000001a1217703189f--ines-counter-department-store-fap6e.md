- The header shows "You have 0 points," which reads like a stranger's email, not a Rouge-tier regular's. ## What's working

- The Huda Beauty hero with the "4X Beauty Insider" badge is the strongest moment. Points are my currency, and "until 10/14" gives me a real deadline.
- Chanel Coco Mademoiselle, Tom Ford Figue Erotique, Jo Malone and Prada, all with prices, feel like designer access I'd shop. Macy's would bury that under 40 coupons.
- The layout is clean and the type is readable on a phone, with generous white space and large "Shop Now" buttons.
- "First Access: Holiday Drops, 24 hours only" feels seasonal and gives me a reason to look today.
- The "Easy Bake" shade names and "only at Sephora" copy make the Huda product feel exclusive.

## What's weak

- Two competing offers sit at the top and middle: free trial sizes with a $105 spend, and 4X points. The eye has no single place to land. Kohl's at least leads with one number.
- The terms are huge and tiny at once. "*Exclusions/terms apply" shows up three times, and the real conditions, app-only and a code, are in dense paragraphs at the bottom. That doesn't respect me.
- "You have 0 points" at the top is either a bug or an insult. I'm a returning customer.
- "Your Personalized Beauty Edit" is a Chanel, an amika mask, and a Tom Ford. It doesn't feel personal, and Tom Ford Figue Erotique shows up twice.
- Every CTA says "Shop Now," so there's no verb plus noun telling me what I'm getting. The "Beauty Insider / VIB / Rouge" tier bar near the bottom is too late to help me understand my own status.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer. Put either 4X Huda or the trial sizes in the hero, not both, and move the other below.
- 2. Put the code and the deadline in plain text next to the offer, not in the fine print.
- 3. Fix the points header so it shows my real tier and balance, or remove it.
- 4. Make the personalized edit actually personal: lean on my past purchases and don't repeat a product.
- 5. Replace the "Shop Now" buttons with specific copy like "Earn 4X on Huda."

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA WARN, 2 images):**
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2gAndjYxMDAw…`: the path and host look like a tracking or open-pixel image. It should have `alt=""` at minimum.
  - `https://images.harmony.epsilon.com/ContentHandler/images/de0a3226-…/2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg`: a nav header image. Without alt text the nav is unreadable when images are blocked and for screen readers.
- **Zoom restricted:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Text resizing disabled:** the CSS sets `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` and `text-size-adjust: none` on `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td`. This stops users from scaling text on iOS and similar clients.
- **No document language:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" xmlns:v=… xmlns:o=…>` has no `lang` or `xml:lang` attribute, so screen readers must guess the language.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">`. The valid token is `noindex`, and the space makes it invalid. It is also a no-op in most email clients.
