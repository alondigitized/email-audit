# Welcome to The North Kohl's 🎁 It's a perfect time to shop & save.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's again. The Kohl's Rewards member bar and "Earn 5% Rewards" at the top are the only part that feels like it knows me. Everything under it is a holiday-village hero with elves and a ski-lift scene, and the actual deal is a coupon graphic crammed up top next to a Kohl's Cash badge. I got the same 20% + $10 off $50 stack on 10/28, 10/29 and 10/30, with a train, then checkmarks, then clapping hands on the subject lines. Today's version is a "welcome" that welcomes me to something I've been getting for a week.
- The story-telling mid-section ("Hosting finds so good, even Mrs. Claus would approve") is cute. But I came for a reason to buy, and the reasons are buried at the bottom: the barcodes, SAVE20, DOSHOP10 and JEWELRY10. Neiman Marcus at least gave me one Moncler story with nothing to decode. Sephora's 30% off Sephora Collection led with a number a person could read from across the room. Kohl's makes me scroll the whole length to find the codes.
- Would I click? Maybe the jewelry block, since 60% off plus 20% with the coupon is a real number. Would I forward it? No. Mark as read.

## What's working

- The rewards status bar at the very top ("Kohl's Rewards Member | Earn 5% Rewards") treats me like a known customer.
- "Ends Nov 1" next to the hero coupon is a credible, specific deadline.
- The jewelry block shows 60% off plus 20% with coupon, and the "$10 off $50+ jewelry & watches" callout beside it is a concrete offer.
- The "Picked with you in mind" grid shows real prices with originals (the Ninja at $299.99, orig. $349.99, and the Gloria Vanderbilt jeans at $52), so I can judge value.
- The "Shopping made fast" strip is a good trust cue: free returns through January 31, 2027, plus store pickup and same-day delivery. As someone who returns at the brand event, I notice that.

## What's weak

- The top of the email is cluttered. There's a coupon graphic, a "+", a Kohl's Cash badge and a "Shop Now" in a small space, and I couldn't tell which one was the offer.
- The "North Kohl's" elf world is a full screen of scenery before any product. It reads as a theme-park map, not a sale.
- The coupon codes and barcodes are at the very bottom. The "details" links and "coupons do not apply to certain brands" make me feel I'll be told no at checkout.
- It's the fourth near-identical 20% + $10 send in a row since 10/28. I've seen this offer so many times that the novelty is gone.
- Nothing here is aimed at a 47-year-old beauty shopper. There's no beauty section, and Sephora at Kohl's is a footnote at the bottom ("Not valid on Sephora at Kohl's purchases"), so my category is excluded from the headline offers.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one offer in the hero, "Extra 20% off with code SAVE20," with the code as live text and a single button. Move the elf scene below it.
- 2. Put a member-specific line in the top module, such as "Your Kohl's Cash: earn $10 for every $50." Rewards members should see the tier benefit before the scenery.
- 3. Break the sameness. Each send in this run should have a different lead item and a different reason to buy, not a new emoji on the same offer.
- 4. Add a beauty or Sephora module for shoppers like me, and say plainly which brands are excluded.
- 5. Shorten the subject to under 50 characters and make it concrete.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 20% off ends Nov 1 + $10 off $50`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Kohl's Cash: $10 back for every $50`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code SAVE20 inside, plus free returns through Jan 31.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Jewelry up to 60% off, and the coupons are in your Wallet.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Kohl's "Welcome to The North Kohl's"

## 1. Technical Summary
The markup is a fixed 640px table layout with Movable Ink dynamic images and four tracking pixels. All 66 links are opaque redirects, so the destination, UTM and compliance checks below couldn't be confirmed. The confirmed issues are malformed head and body markup, missing alt text on content images, and a viewport tag that blocks zoom.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque redirects:** QA skipped 66 links (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7Im...`). The destination and link health are unverified.
- **Four tracking pixels:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/4fa19983-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1024910_20261031`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=...&d=60274`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager)
- **Pixel markup problems:**
  - The first pixel sits bare in `<body>` with no `alt` and no hiding wrapper. Some clients will render a 1x1 gap, and screen readers may announce the filename.
  - The AAM pixel uses `width="0" height="0"`. Some clients drop or mis-size zero-dimension images.
- **Stray closing tag:** the `open.aspx` pixel is followed by `</custom>`, which has no matching open tag. It is invalid HTML.
- **Subscriber IDs in URLs:** `mi_u=604230016` and `d_sid=13245196` are embedded in image URLs. This is a privacy consideration, not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images (QA-confirmed):**
  - `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`
  - `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`
  - `59199073_Product_hdgt30_HolidayGifting_Hero_GIF`

  If these carry offers or the hero message, that content is lost when images are blocked. The pixel images flagged in QA need `alt=""` only.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="..., maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Empty `<title></title>`:** no document title.
- **Empty icon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`.
- **Invalid head content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will close the head early at that point.
- **Missing `role="presentation"`:** the 1x1 table, the AAM table and the preheader table (`width='640'`) have none. The main wrapper tables do have it.
- **Web fonts:** Montserrat and Roboto load via `<link>` and `@import`. Gmail and Outlook ignore both, but the `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` fallback is present, so this is acceptable.
- **Not assessed:** the source is truncated, so the remaining body, CTA buttons and footer markup weren't reviewed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) are visible in the available source. The personalization present is Movable Ink query parameters (`mi_u`, `mi_ecmp`, `email_name=261031_DG_Email_Saturd...`). The truncated portion wasn't checked.

## 5. Compliance
All three QA warnings are unconfirmed. They may come from the capture path rather than from the email.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) missing:** QA notes the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers. Check the raw message. Kohl's is a large sender and most likely sends them.
- **No physical address detected:** the footer is truncated from the provided HTML, so this can't be confirmed or ruled out.
- **`Authentication-Results` missing:** SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `s.kohls.com`, and the tracking domains are on different subdomains (`click.s`, `click.chp`, `mi`). Alignment couldn't be verified.
- **Unsubscribe link:** it isn't visible in the available source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Unverified. Every click goes through `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=...` with an encoded payload, and QA skipped all probes. No UTM parameters appear in the visible source. They may be applied at redirect time, so landing-page alignment and UTM consistency can't be assessed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the eight content images above. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
2. Remove `</custom>` and move the `itemscope` div out of `<head>`. Use `<meta>` or JSON-LD instead.
3. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag.
4. Populate `<title>` and remove or fill the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon" href="">`.
5. Add `role="presentation"` to the remaining layout tables. Consider consolidating the four pixels.
6. Re-run QA against the raw `.eml` (not the relay copy) with the untruncated HTML. That will confirm the unsubscribe headers, authentication results and footer address.
7. Resolve a sample of the redirects (browser or HEAD with a real UA) to confirm the final URLs and UTMs.
