# STARTS TODAY: Save an extra 20% ... the perfect reason to shop 🛒
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-11**
## Executive summary

- Eighteen. That's where the Kohl's folder sits in my inbox, and this one shows up with "STARTS TODAY" in all caps, a shopping cart emoji, an ellipsis, and a tagline that tells me absolutely nothing — "the perfect reason to shop." That's not a reason. That's a placeholder someone forgot to fill in.
- The bones of the send are fine. Father's Day is June 21, there's a legitimate 20% off coupon, and Kohl's Cash is spelled out at the bottom — I'll give them that. But from top to bottom this email is golf shirts starting at $24.99, athletic sneakers at $39.99, and what appears to be Looney Tunes-adjacent character merchandise for $10. Not a single fragrance. Not a grooming kit. Not one thing I'd actually pull out my card for. Macy's sent a Father's Day email last week that at least had recognizable brand names and a clear gift story. This one feels like it went to every address on file and no one asked who was on the other end.
- I won't click. I might keep the code for later. I will not forward this to anyone.

## What's working

- **The 20% off coupon is unambiguous** — I see the code, I see the discount, it shows up twice. No "up to" hedging, no tiny-asterisk exclusion visible from where I'm standing.
- **Kohl's Cash is clearly stated** — $10 for every $50 spent through June 21 at the bottom. I don't spend without points back, so that line actually matters to me.
- **Timing is legitimate** — Father's Day Is June 21 is right in the hero. This email has an actual calendar reason to exist, which is more than I could say for the "LAST DAY" send from June 7 that came two days before the June 8 "budget-friendly Father's Day" send.
- **Clean render** — No broken images, no overlapping text, no lorem ipsum. After Sephora called me "Rosa" twice this month on a triggered send, a clean load is not nothing.

## What's weak

- **None of this merchandise is for me** — I'm the buyer, not the gift recipient, and nothing here speaks to a 47-year-old woman shopping for someone she loves. No fragrance section, no grooming, no quality goods I'd feel good giving. All I see is FLX activewear and licensed character apparel.
- **The character merchandise kills the tone** — That $10 novelty section near the bottom reads like a clearance endcap, not a curated Father's Day gift guide. Neiman Marcus dropped the Adidas x Willy Chavarria collab email this week. The contrast is not subtle.
- **The subject line is doing five jobs at once** — ALL CAPS opener, ellipsis pause for drama, a tagline that means nothing, and a shopping cart emoji. Every one of those elements is trying to be the hook and none of them are.
- **Eighteen emails this month** — Cadence has completely burned out the urgency. "STARTS TODAY" means nothing when the email three days ago said "LAST DAY" and the send before that said the prices "won't be here forever."

## Recommendations

- 1. **Segment the Father's Day file by buyer, not recipient.** I am not the person receiving golf shirts — I'm the person buying the gift. Show me a polo I'd feel good gifting, a fragrance, a shoe that's worth $40. The navigation bar has a Women tab; the entire email ignores that she exists.
- 2. **Build one real gift story instead of four category rows.** Pick a hero product — a shoe, a shirt, something with a story — and let the 20% off do the work. The Macy's format of one clear gift narrative with a "confirm your extra 25% off" mechanic is more respectful of my time than four scrolling rows of thumbnails.
- 3. **Cut the subject line to its essential claim.** The "the perfect reason to shop" clause is pure filler. The coupon code is the reason. Say that.
- 4. **One coupon placement is enough.** Showing 20% in the hero and again at the footer doesn't compound the offer — it signals that the brand is nervous the first one didn't land.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Father's Day is June 21 — extra 20% off starts today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Gifts for Dad, 20% off. Ends June 21.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `FLX, shoes, home + Kohl's Cash — use code at checkout`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Earn $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 through June 21`

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "Save an extra 20%" (2026-06-11)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is structurally sound but has a malformed HTML tag, an empty `<title>`, and a high volume of accessibility violations from untagged tracking pixels. Compliance signals (List-Unsubscribe, physical address) are absent from what the relay captured, which is the most significant deliverability risk.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Tracking infrastructure identified:**
- CoherentPath click tracking: `click.chp.kohls.com`
- MessageGears open/render tracking: `mi.kohls.com`
- Adobe Audience Manager sync pixel: `kohls.demdex.net`
- Responsys open pixel: `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx`

**Issues:**
- **22 click links unverifiable** — all wrapped in redirect domains; destination URLs and UTM parameters cannot be confirmed from source. QA correctly skipped HTTP probing.
- **`email_name` parameter appears truncated** in several `mi.kohls.com` render-pixel URLs: `email_name=260611_DG_Email_Thursda` (cut off at 30 chars). Evidence: `src="https://mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1014914_2026611&email_name=260611_DG_Email_Thursda"`. Low impact for recipients but breaks campaign reporting if the field is used for segmentation.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Malformed HTML tag:**
- `</custom>` appears as a stray closing tag immediately after the Responsys open pixel `<div>`:
  ```html
  <img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>
  ```
  `<custom>` is not a valid HTML element. Most clients will silently ignore it, but it is an invalid construct and could cause issues in strict parsers or future-proofing.

**Empty `<title>` tag:**
- `<title></title>` — no document title set. Screen readers announce the title when a message is opened; an empty title degrades accessibility.

**Empty favicon href:**
- `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` — empty `href` triggers a redundant HTTP request to the current document URL in some environments. Should be removed or set to a valid resource.

**Missing alt text — 10 images flagged by QA:**

| Image | Type | Issue |
|---|---|---|
| `click.chp.kohls.com/o/9bd80fde...` | CoherentPath open pixel | No `alt` attribute, no `aria-hidden` |
| `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` | AAM sync pixel | No `alt` attribute |
| `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png` | Render pixel | No `alt` attribute |
| `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/f441d45d2128ae53.png` | Render pixel | No `alt` attribute |
| `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/c2f81e9ac8daee44.png` | Render pixel | No `alt` attribute |
| `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/415c8a18fc2950ea.png` | Render pixel | No `alt` attribute |
| `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/69494b48a79b4240.png` | Render pixel | No `alt` attribute |
| `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/78308d1d9adb0751.png` | Render pixel | No `alt` attribute |
| `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/916d6fa08d185233.png` | Render pixel | No `alt` attribute |

Note: `mi.kohls.com/p/up/o.gif` correctly has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"` — that pattern should be applied to all the above.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta:**
- `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` disables pinch-to-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) and is blocked entirely on iOS 10+.

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header — not captured**
QA confirms the header was not found via the AgentMail relay. This may be a relay-layer capture gap rather than a genuine absence, but it cannot be verified. Gmail and Apple Mail use this header for one-click unsubscribe; if missing in transit, bulk sender compliance (Google/Yahoo 2024 requirements) is at risk.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) — not captured**
Same caveat as above. Without this header, one-click unsubscribe (as opposed to mailto-based) is not supported at the protocol level.

**Physical mailing address — not detected in HTML source**
CAN-SPAM §7 requires a valid physical postal address. QA did not detect one in the truncated source provided. This must be confirmed against the full HTML — if the footer was cut off before rendering, it may still be present further down. If absent, this is a legal compliance gap.

**Authentication-Results — not captured**
SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown for this message as received. The sending domain is `s.kohls.com` (Responsys subdomain); authentication configuration for this subdomain should be verified independently.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All 22 links are wrapped in CoherentPath (`click.chp.kohls.com`) or Responsys (`click.s.kohls.com`) redirectors, so destination URLs and UTM parameters are not inspectable from source. Cannot confirm whether landing pages carry consistent UTM attribution. This should be spot-checked by following at least one tracked CTA link manually and verifying `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` on the destination.
