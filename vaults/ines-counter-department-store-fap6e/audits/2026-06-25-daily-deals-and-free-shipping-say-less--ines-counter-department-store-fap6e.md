# Daily Deals AND free shipping? Say less 🤗
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-25**
## Executive summary

- Thirty-one. That's where my Kohl's count lands for June, and I didn't even need to check — I knew the moment "Say less 🤗" landed in my subject line that this was another Deal Days clone dressed up in Gen Z slang I don't use and honestly find a little embarrassing coming from a brand that wants my credit card number. The email opens to a purple Deal Days banner, a navigation strip, Kohl's Cash thresholds I can barely read, and then a cascade of percentage-off blocks — $14.99 tops, 50% off family apparel, 40% off Lego, a 5/$35 bra offer buried near the bottom. It is everything I already knew it would be.
- The structure isn't broken — it renders cleanly, the Shop Now buttons are findable, the offers are real. That's about as far as the praise goes. Nothing here tells me Kohl's knows I exist as a specific person. Neiman Marcus sent me "1,000+ new arrivals" this same week and the editorial framing made it feel like a letter. This feels like a flyer shoved under my windshield wiper.
- I'd mark this read, skip the click, and not forward it to anyone. The only scenario I act on it is if I'm already heading to Kohl's for something else and I pull up the app on the way out.

## What's working

- **Free shipping is above the fold and in the subject** — that combination is genuinely useful; I don't want to math out shipping thresholds at checkout.
- **Kohl's Cash earning is visible** — the $10/$15 threshold blocks remind me the transaction has upside, which matters to me; I don't buy without points back.
- **Clean render, no broken images** — the email loads as intended; I don't have to squint around white boxes or placeholder text.
- **Beauty is the first nav tab** — it's a small gesture but putting Beauty before Women in the navigation acknowledges that I might be here for skincare, not swimwear.

## What's weak

- **"Say less" is not how I talk** — I'm 47, not 22; the Gen Z closer in the subject line signals Kohl's isn't writing to me, they're blasting everyone and hoping the slang feels friendly.
- **Lego as the 6 Days of Deals hero** — a Lego 40%-off block prominently featured in what's supposed to be my inbox tells me this email is for someone's husband shopping for his kid, not for me cross-shopping beauty.
- **No single hero moment** — Sephora's "Last chance to save up to 50%" email last week had one clean offer with a clear deadline; this email has nine competing deal blocks fighting for my attention and I end up clicking nothing.
- **Kohl's Cash terms are illegible** — the threshold amounts are there but the rules are in fine print I can't read without zooming; that's exactly the kind of buried detail that makes me distrust whether the math is real.
- **This is the fifth Deal Days email I've gotten this week** — by the June 24th "New day, new deals" send I had already tuned out the format entirely; this one benefits from nothing except showing up one more time.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with a single beauty or women's offer instead of a category nav strip** — if I'm a known beauty buyer, the hero image and first CTA should be skincare or fragrance at X% off; the Lego deal should not exist in my inbox variant.
- 2. **Surface a concrete Kohl's Cash earn statement** — "Earn $15 Kohl's Cash on today's deals" in the hero copy is a sentence I actually care about; right now that information is in a small threshold block I almost scrolled past.
- 3. **Kill the "Say less" slang in the subject** — it's trying to be casual and landing as out-of-touch for anyone north of 35.
- 4. **Add a deadline** — "Today only" or "Ends Sunday" on the featured daily deal gives me a reason to act now rather than close the email and forget it; right now there's zero urgency beyond "daily."
- 5. **Cut at least four deal blocks** — Macy's "LAST CHANCE" triggered email this week had one offer and one CTA; this email has nine deals and I converted on zero.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Earn $15 Kohl's Cash when you spend $50 — today's featured deals inside`

**Google Fonts double-loaded** — both `<link>` tags and `@import` rules fetch Roboto and Montserrat. In clients that honor `<link>`, this fires two separate HTTP requests per font family.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Malformed HTML — stray `</custom>` tag:**
```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?…" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>
```
The `</custom>` closing tag has no matching open tag. Strict-mode parsers (Outlook, some webmail) may bubble parse errors from this point forward.

**Zoom disabled via viewport meta:**
```html
maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0
```
Prevents users from pinching to zoom — a WCAG 1.4.4 violation and a friction point for low-vision recipients.

**Missing alt text on content images** — 6 personalized content images from `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/` have no `alt` attribute. The three 1×1 tracking pixels correctly carry `alt=""`, but the rendered product/promo images do not. Screen readers will announce raw filenames (e.g. `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`).

Affected srcs (sample):
- `https://mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`
- `https://mi.kohls.com/p/rp/f441d45d2128ae53.png`
- `https://mi.kohls.com/p/rp/415c8a18fc2950ea.png`

**Empty `<title>` tag** — `<title></title>` renders as a blank tab label in webmail clients that display it (Gmail web, Yahoo). Not critical but low effort to fix.

**DOCTYPE is HTML 4.01 Transitional** — expected for broad email client compatibility; not flagged as an issue.

### 5. Compliance

**Physical address absent from visible HTML source.** CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid postal address in the message body. It may exist below the truncation point, but the QA checker did not detect it — confirm it is present in the full render.

**List-Unsubscribe header not captured** — the AgentMail relay did not surface this header. If Kohl's ESP sends it (standard for reputable senders), the relay is stripping or not forwarding it. Without it, Gmail and Apple Mail cannot render their native one-click unsubscribe UI — increasing spam complaint rate.

**List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not captured** — same root cause. Google and Yahoo's 2024 bulk sender requirements mandate one-click unsubscribe for senders above 5,000/day. Absence here is a deliverability risk flag, not necessarily a Kohl's defect — depends on whether the relay is suppressing the header.

**SPF/DKIM/DMARC unverifiable** — `Authentication-Results` header absent from relay capture. Cannot confirm pass/fail for `s.kohls.com` sending domain. This is a relay instrumentation gap, not a confirmed auth failure.

**`no-referrer` meta tag present:**
```html
<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer" />
```
Suppresses the HTTP Referer header on all outbound clicks. This is intentional for privacy but means GA4/Adobe Analytics on landing pages will receive direct/none traffic instead of email attribution — unless UTM params in the click URLs compensate.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All CTAs pass through click-redirect domains (`click.chp.kohls.com`, `click.s.kohls.com`), so final landing page URLs and their UTM parameters cannot be inspected without live redirect resolution. The `no-referrer` policy (noted above) makes UTM params on destination URLs the only attribution mechanism — if any CTA link omits `utm_source`, that session will be unattributed.

The `mi_ecmp=1015935_2026625` parameter on pixel URLs encodes campaign identity in MessageGears format, but this does not substitute for standard `utm_*` params on CTA hrefs.

No mismatched or placeholder offer copy detected in the truncated source; full source review would be needed to confirm landing page offer alignment.
