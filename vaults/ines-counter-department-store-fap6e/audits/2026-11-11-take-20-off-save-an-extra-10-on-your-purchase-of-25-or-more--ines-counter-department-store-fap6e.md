# Take 20% off | Save an extra $10 on your purchase of $25 or more 👉
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- It's Kohl's again, and this is the same email in a different order. My gut reaction was fatigue. The subject says "Take 20% off | Save an extra $10," but the top of the email leads with a $10-off-$25 coupon that "Ends Nov. 11." The 20% shows up much further down, as a barcode block. I came in for a stack and found a scavenger hunt. I counted at least eight separate offers: $10 off $25, Kohl's Cash, 30% off for military, up to 40% off coats, boots at $29.99, up to 20% off accessories, $10 off $50 for toys, and $10 off $50 for home.
- The email does have a clean barcode section near the bottom: "Take an extra 20% off," "Take $10 off your purchase of $25 or more," and the Kohl's Cash earn offer. I'd screenshot that section and skip everything above it. Compared with the 11/10 "Stack 20% off + $10 off" send, this one is no clearer. It's the thirteenth or fourteenth Kohl's email this week by my count, so I'm not rushing.
- I'd probably mark it read. I wouldn't forward it. If I did click, it would be for the coupons in Wallet and only because I already planned to shop.

## What's working

- The header shows "Status: Kohl's Rewards' Member" and "Earn 5% Rewards." That is the only place I feel recognized as a points customer, and I notice it.
- The barcode section at the bottom has codes like SAVEMORE and SHOP10, a PIN, and "Ends Nov. 11." That is real, usable detail. Macy's makes me chase the promo code, so this is better.
- The Kohl's Cash earn line gives dates: earn through Nov. 18, redeem Nov. 20-25. That is concrete, and it's what a points-minded shopper like me looks for.
- The product tiles show prices: the Ninja SLUSHI at $249.99 and the Big One blanket at $2.99-$9.99. Prices let me judge value quickly.

## What's weak

- The hero is a muddle. The $10 coupon, a Kohl's Cash graphic, and a "Plus, earn" line all sit on top of each other. Sephora's "November rewards are here!" email opened with one calm idea, and this doesn't.
- The 30% military discount and "Thank you for your service" blocks are tucked into the middle of a sales email. That is a respectable gesture, but placing it between promos makes it look like another banner.
- The fine print is everywhere: "Coupons do not apply to select brands," "Select styles," and "Coupons do not apply." Every discount has an exclusion attached. That erodes trust, and it reads like the shouting I try to avoid.
- Nothing here speaks to a 47-year-old who buys beauty. Sephora at Kohl's gets one small tile in a category grid. Neiman Marcus at least commits to a mood, even without an offer.
- The "Preview the Deals Now" block pushes me to the Nov. 22-26 sale. That tells me to wait, which undercuts the Nov. 11 urgency above it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer. Either the stacked 20% plus $10, as the subject promises, or the Kohl's Cash earn. Put its code and button in the hero, and move the barcodes up.
- 2. Cut the tile count by at least half. Drop the boots, accessories, and nice-list grid, and keep two or three hero products.
- 3. Give Sephora at Kohl's a real beauty module with a rewards angle, so I have a reason to shop it over Nordstrom or Macy's.
- 4. Put the exclusions in one footnote line rather than on every tile.
- 5. Give the military offer its own send, or at least its own strip, instead of mixing it into a general promo.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 20% off + $10 off $25 codes (ends tomorrow)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Ends Nov. 11: 20% off and $10 off, codes inside`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code SAVEMORE for 20% off, plus earn Kohl's Cash through Nov. 18.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Two coupons, one deadline. Your Kohl's Rewards member codes are inside.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is mostly structurally sound (`lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` on the main layout tables, media-query fallbacks). It has several verifiable defects: content images without alt text, malformed head and tracking-pixel markup, zoom disabled, and a 70% URL plain-text part. Compliance items and UTM alignment can't be confirmed because the HTML is truncated and the relay stripped the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracking domains.** 77 links go through `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…` (opaque, SFMC-style) and were not probed, so no destination or status is verified.
- **Stray closing tag.** The open pixel is `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=60274&bmt=0" …></custom>`. `</custom>` has no matching opening tag and is invalid markup.
- **Pixel stack.** There are four separate tracking or open pixels:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/668dfd5e-…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`
- **Identifier in URLs.** Image URLs carry `mi_u=604230016`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid`. These are recipient-level identifiers sent to the image host on every render.
- **Possible empty dynamic value.** `916d6fa08d185233.png` has `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` with an empty value. A Kohl's Cash redemption date may have failed to populate. The URL is truncated, so this needs a check.
- **Plain-text part.** 13,612 of 19,388 characters (about 70%) are URLs, which is a spam-filter risk. It follows from the 77 long tracking links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on content images.** `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/{2a5adc5afb647aad, f441d45d2128ae53, c2f81e9ac8daee44, 415c8a18fc2950ea, 69494b48a79b4240, 78308d1d9adb0751, 916d6fa08d185233}.png` have no alt text. If these carry the "20% off" or "$10 off $25" offer, it is invisible with images blocked and to screen readers.
- **Pixel alt text.**
  - The QA flag on `o.gif` is a false positive, because it has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`.
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/…` (1×1) and `demdex.net/event` (0×0) have no `alt` attribute. `demdex` is only a pixel, so give it `alt=""`.
  - The first pixel is also a bare `<img>` directly in `<body>`, outside any table.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility failure.
- **Empty title and icon link.** `<title></title>` is empty, and `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href.
- **Invalid markup in head.** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will implicitly close the head at that point.
- **CSS typo.** `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` is missing the leading `.`, so the rule matches nothing.
- **Missing table roles.** The preheader table (`width='640' … class="container"`) and the 1×1 tracking tables have no `role="presentation"`.
- **Fonts.** Roboto and Montserrat load from Google Fonts via `@import`, wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. The fallback stack (`Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`) is present, so this is fine.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the seven `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` content images. Put the offer copy in the alt of any image carrying it.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.

   - Move the `schema.org` div out of `<head>`.

   - Change `ReadMsgBody` to `.ReadMsgBody`.
