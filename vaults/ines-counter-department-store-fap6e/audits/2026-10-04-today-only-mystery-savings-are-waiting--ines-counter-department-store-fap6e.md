# Today only! Mystery savings are waiting 👀
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-04**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again, and the subject promised "mystery savings." There's no mystery in the body. This isn't the hollow red "EXTRA" header I complained about on 9/29 through 10/3, and I'll give them that. The top is a "Elevated Everyday" workwear hero with a Great Price burst, "Starting at 34.99," and a "Reg. $36.99–$39.99" line. But the "Today only!" in the subject isn't echoed anywhere I can find. The 20% off is buried near the bottom, in a code block under "You get an extra 20% off." The Kohl's Cash earn line is down there too.
- Nothing here treats me like a beauty-counter regular. The one beauty nod is a small perfume bottle and lipstick in the "outfit" collage, with a Fragrance button. A Sephora at Kohl's line sits in the fine print. I'd skim it and close it. I wouldn't forward it. I might click Fragrance out of curiosity, but my points-back habit doesn't kick in until I scroll past about nine sections to find the coupon.

## What's working

- The "Elevated Everyday" hero is the best top I've seen from Kohl's this week. It's a real image and a real price, with no empty red word.
- Status: Kohl's Rewards Member and "Earn 5% Rewards" sit at the top, so I know I'm recognized as a member.
- The category buttons (Women, Men, Kids, Home) are big and easy to tap on a phone.
- Barcodes and codes (KJF5EKN8NLB6P, HOME10, TOYS10) are clearly labeled with end dates of Oct 4.
- The Fragrance button gives me a beauty-adjacent door, which is more than the last five sends gave me.

## What's weak

- "Today only! Mystery savings" in the subject is never paid off. I see no reveal, no scratch-off, nothing mystery.
- The 20% off is far below the fold, after workwear, handbags, home, and a Ninja SLUSH machine. I had to scroll to find the actual offer.
- The email is long and has too many offers: 20% off, $10 Kohl's Cash per $50, $10 off $50 home, toys, and baby. This is the Macy's-style overload I dislike.
- Two kinds of savings are stacked in the product section: "2.99–9.99" towels and a "137.99–209.99" mattress topper. Ranges with no clear discount make it hard to trust the price.
- "Coupons do not apply to select brands, details" is easy to miss. Fine print like this makes me wary.
- The fine print at the bottom is tiny and unreadable without zooming.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the 20% off code and the Kohl's Cash earn line right under the header. Neiman Marcus left no doubt about what I was getting. Kohl's makes me hunt for it.
- 2. Make the subject match the email. If there's a mystery, show it. If there isn't, say what the deal is.
- 3. Cut the number of offers. Sephora Insider ran SAYPOINTS and "Ends 10/7" at the very top. Kohl's should lead with one deal and let the rest go.
- 4. Give beauty and fragrance its own tile, with a real product and a price. The Sephora at Kohl's shop is a draw for someone like me.
- 5. Make the exclusions visible near the offer, not in 6-point type at the bottom.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML has several markup defects: an empty `<title>`, a stray `</custom>` tag, an invalid element inside `<head>`, and 7 content images with no `alt` attribute. Compliance and authentication findings can't be confirmed, because the headers weren't captured by the relay and the HTML is truncated before the footer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 69 links are wrapped in `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=...` redirects. The QA probe skipped all of them, so destinations and redirect health are **unverified**, not broken.
- There are 4 tracking pixels in the body, all before the main content:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/1ae4cb0e-...` (1×1, no `alt`)
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (`width="0" height="0"`)
- The `open.aspx` pixel is followed by a stray `</custom>` closing tag. That tag is invalid and has no matching opener.
- `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty `href`. Some clients will request the page URL as the icon.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** Some webmail and archive views show nothing or fall back to a default.
- **Invalid `<head>` content.** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will close the head early, so the `<meta itemprop>` tags and anything after them can end up in the body. Most mail clients ignore microdata anyway.
- **Viewport meta.** `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem on mobile.
- **Missing `alt` attributes.** The QA scan flagged 7 content images in `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png`:
  - `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`
  - `f441d45d2128ae53.png`
  - `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`
  - `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`
  - `69494b48a79b4240.png`
  - `78308d1d9adb0751.png`
  - `916d6fa08d185233.png`
- If these images carry the offer content, that content disappears with images blocked and is unreadable to screen readers. Three more flagged images are tracking pixels, where a missing `alt` has minimal impact. Only `open.aspx` has `alt=""`.
- **Missing `role="presentation"`.** The 1×1 pixel table and the preheader table (`width='640'`) don't have it. The main layout tables do.
- Web fonts load via `<link>` and `@import`, both inside `<!--[if !mso]>`. Fallbacks (`Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`) are declared, so this is fine.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[%`) appear in the visible source. Image URLs carry per-recipient parameters (`mi_u=604230016`, `mi_ecmp=1023150_2026104`) and a campaign tag (`email_name=261004_DG_Email_Mystery`). The rest of the HTML is truncated, so body-level tokens can't be fully ruled out.

- **List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post and Authentication-Results:** not found. The QA tool itself attributes this to the AgentMail relay, not the sender, so these are unconfirmed and shouldn't be treated as sender violations.
- **Physical address:** not detected. The HTML is truncated, so the footer wasn't inspected. This is unconfirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- The wrapped click URLs hide destinations and UTM parameters, and the probe skipped them. UTM presence and landing-page alignment **can't be verified**.
- The only visible campaign identifier is `email_name=261004_DG_Email_Mystery` on image URLs. Several images (`69494b48...`, `78308d1d...`, `916d6fa0...`) omit it, so tagging is inconsistent across assets. This is low severity.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a descriptive `alt` to the 7 `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` content images, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
2. Populate `<title>`, and remove or fill the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon" href="">`.
3. Move the `itemscope` div out of `<head>`, or drop it, and remove the stray `</custom>` tag.
4. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Add `role="presentation"` to the preheader and pixel tables.
6. To close the open compliance and continuity questions, re-run with raw `.eml` headers and the untruncated HTML. Then resolve a sample of the `click.s.kohls.com` redirects to check UTMs and landing pages.
