# Get 10% off when you stock up on new palettes 💖
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-07**
## Executive summary

- My first thought was that Sephora has a 30% sale and a 10% Insider sale running at once, and the subject only tells me about the 10%. The 30% is in the banner at the top, which I'd have to scroll to find on my phone. The sale itself is good news. The email is just badly organized around it.
- The palette lineup is the strongest part. Huda, Patrick Ta, Danessa Myricks, Dior, Charlotte Tilbury, Nars and Makeup by Mario are what I'd shop at a beauty counter, and the prices are printed ($55, $72, $82). The email also stacks free same-day delivery, a Parachute gift with $100, a Kohl's store barcode and a holiday gift guide on top of the palettes. It reads like a shopping mall, not a beauty counter.
- Would I click? Yes, probably into Huda or Charlotte Tilbury. Would I forward it? Only to a friend who wants a palette. It's a competent send that's too crowded to feel special.

## What's working

- The 30% and Insider 10% date windows (through 11/9) are clear, and "Last weekend to save" feels like a real deadline.
- The palette brands are ones I know, and the Dior and Charlotte Tilbury names signal prestige.
- Prices sit right next to each palette, so I can decide without clicking.
- "Limited edition" and "new, only at Sephora" tags give me a reason to buy now.
- The Insider percentage is visible, so my membership counts for something.

## What's weak

- Two discounts, free delivery and a gift offer all compete before the hero. This is the Macy's-style overload I dislike.
- The subject says 10% off, but the banner leads with 30%. Neither my 11/3 nor my 11/6 Sephora email matched its subject either, so this is the same problem again.
- "Shop Now" doesn't say what I'm shopping for.
- The bottom of the email has too much going on: gift guide, Sephora at Kohl's, store hours, barcodes and a wall of fine print. I can't tell whether the 30% has exclusions without squinting.
- It's another Sephora email in a week of near-daily sends, after "10% off our most-searched beauty" and the Nov 6 "250-point bundle" send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make one offer the headline and name it in the subject. If the 30% applies to palettes, say so. If it doesn't, don't put it in the top banner.
- 2. Move the free-delivery and Parachute gift offers below the palettes.
- 3. Change "Shop Now" to "Shop palettes" and link the three hero brands directly.
- 4. Shorten the bottom and put the exclusions near the offer. Neiman Marcus' gold spend-and-save ladder, which I could read in two seconds, is a better model.
- 5. Cut the cadence. Kohl's and Sephora both send almost daily, and the volume is what makes the sales feel routine.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Huda, Dior, Nars palettes, 10% off for Insiders`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Last weekend: 30% off select brands, plus your Insider 10%.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Limited-edition palettes from $55. Sale ends 11/9.`

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage gap:** QA probed 12 material links and skipped 47. The skipped links, mostly footer, utility and social, were not validated. No broken links were reported among the 12 that were probed.
- **Tracking pixels (two):**

- **Empty structured data:** the JSON-LD block has `"@type": "DiscountOffer", "discountCode": ""`. The empty code carries no value. The `PromotionCard` entry has no properties.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:**
  - The `app.sephora.com/O/…` pixel has no `alt` attribute at all (confirmed). It is hidden, so the impact is minimal.
  - The `o.gif` warning looks like a false positive. The source shows `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, which is correct for a decorative pixel.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Fixed-width layout:**
  - The wrapper is `width:700px; min-width:700px` on the `tbl tbl_cntr` table.
  - The visible `@media (max-width:480px)` rules don't override that width. This may cause horizontal scroll on mobile, but I can't confirm it from the truncated source.
