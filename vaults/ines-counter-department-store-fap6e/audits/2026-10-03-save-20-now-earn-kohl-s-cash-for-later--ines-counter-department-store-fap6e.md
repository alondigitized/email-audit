# Save 20% now 😍 Earn Kohl's Cash for later 🤩
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-03**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again. This is the fifth "EXTRA" send in a week, counting 9/29, both on 9/30, 10/1 and 10/2. I opened it out of habit, and the top of the email is the same hollow red "EXTRA" with nothing under it. The subject promises 20% now, but I scrolled past family pajamas, a baby sale and toys before the 20% showed up as a barcode coupon near the bottom. The status bar does say "Kohl's Rewards Member" with "Earn 5% Rewards," so it knows who I am.
- I'm a beauty-counter person. Nothing here speaks to me: no beauty, no designer access, and the only beauty mention is a fine-print line saying Kohl's Cash isn't valid on Sephora at Kohl's. I'm 47, and the imagery is toddlers in Santa hats and car seats. Nordstrom and Saks would never put a mattress topper next to a pajama set and call it a point of view.
- Would I click? No. Would I forward it? Only to my sister, who has grandkids. I'd mark it read and move on. Kohl's has the loyalty machinery, but it keeps hiding the point.

## What's working

- The Rewards status bar ("Earn 5% Rewards") at the very top tells me they know me. It's more than Macy's gave me on its "LAST CHANCE: confirm now" send.
- Deadlines are real. The coupons say "Ends Oct. 4," and the Kohl's Cash earn window and redeem window (Oct. 5–18) are spelled out.
- Prices are shown honestly on the product tiles, with the original price crossed out on the jeans ($29.99 vs. $52.00). I can do that math.
- The barcode coupons at the bottom are scannable and each has a code, so the offer is usable once I find it.
- "Picked with you in mind. (Your great taste makes it easy.)" is a friendly line, even if the products don't match me.

## What's weak

- The "EXTRA" hero is a one-word banner with no number under it. The subject says 20%, but the email doesn't say it where my eye lands. Sephora Insider opens with one number and one idea, and this opens with a word.
- Too many offers: $10 off $25 baby, 50% Carter's, 25% baby gear, $10 off $50 toys, $10 off $50 home, and $10 Kohl's Cash per $50. By the third one I stopped reading. This is the Macy's overload problem.
- The content has nothing for me. Family pajamas, car seats, a Ninja SLUSH machine and a mattress topper don't match a beauty-counter shopper.
- The fine print is heavy: "Coupons do not apply to certain brands," "Not valid on Sephora at Kohl's," and a wall of legal text. The "extra 20% off" feels conditional.
- Every button is "Shop Now" or similar, and there are many of them. I can't tell which one matters.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the real offer in the hero: "20% off, today through Oct. 4. Code YOUSAVE20," with one button. That single change would do the most.
- 2. Cut the offer count to two, with one clear headline and one backup.
- 3. Segment by what I actually buy. I'd show me beauty, and show the Sephora at Kohl's shop instead of excluding it in the fine print. Neiman Marcus's no-offer shoulder-bag roundup earned a 7 from me by being quiet and specific. Kohl's could do that for beauty.
- 4. Rewrite the buttons to say what I get ("Shop 20% off beauty").
- 5. Space out the sends. Five near-identical promos in a week trains me to ignore them.
- **Subject Alt A:** `20% off through Oct. 4, plus $10 Kohl's Cash`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Rewards: extra 20% off ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code YOUSAVE20. Earn $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 you spend.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Beauty, home and more, all marked down. Your Rewards status applies.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a fixed-width (640px) table layout with a mobile media query at 500px. The source shows malformed markup, four tracking pixels, and content images with no alt text. The compliance and authentication warnings come from header capture limits and a truncated source, so they can't be confirmed as real failures.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** The QA probe skipped 69 links as tracking redirects (e.g. `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABB7InYi…`). Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are untested.
- **Four tracking pixels before the first content block:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/d4c330db-…` (bare `<img>` directly under `<body>`)
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?HSDUC34N7VPETDYNGGHZGEVTTY.60265`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager, third-party)
- **Redundant opens:** Three separate open trackers fire. They add third-party requests and can inflate or skew open metrics.
- **Broken tag:** The `open.aspx` pixel is followed by a stray `</custom>` close tag with no matching opener (`<img … alt=""></custom>`).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images:** These QA findings are valid. Images `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png` and the product image `58125192_2s_2x` have no `alt`. If they carry offer text (as the "Save 20%" subject suggests), that text is lost to screen readers and with images blocked.
- **Tracking pixels without `alt`:** The `click.chp.kohls.com` and `demdex.net` pixels have no `alt` attribute. They should carry `alt=""`.
- **QA false positive:** `o.gif` is flagged, but the source has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, which is valid for a decorative pixel.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` disables pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Empty `<title></title>`**.
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will close `<head>` early at that point, which can push the later `<style>` and `<link>` handling into unpredictable territory.
- **Empty icon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href, which can trigger a request to the document URL.
- **Missing `role="presentation"`:** The 1×1 tracking table, the AAM table and the preheader table (`width='640'`) have no `role="presentation"`. The main layout tables do have it.
- **CSS errors:**
  - `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` is missing its leading `.`, so it matches nothing.
  - `max-height: auto !important` is not a valid value.
- **Duplicate font loading:** Both `<link>` and `@import` request Montserrat and Roboto. Roboto isn't in the visible `font-family` stacks, and the duplicate loads add blocking requests in clients that support them.
- **DOCTYPE mismatch:** The HTML 4.01 Transitional DOCTYPE is paired with XHTML-style `/>` self-closing tags. This is low impact.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source shows no unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[[…]]`). The source is truncated, so personalization further down is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unverifiable, not confirmed failures:**
  - `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are missing, and the QA note attributes this to the AgentMail relay not capturing headers.
  - `Authentication-Results` is missing, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown.
  - "No physical address detected" most likely reflects the truncated HTML, since footers sit at the end of the document.
- **Sender alignment:** The From address is `Kohls@s.kohls.com` and tracking runs on `click.s.kohls.com`, so the sending and tracking subdomains look aligned.
- **Action needed:** Check the raw `.eml` and the full HTML footer before concluding anything.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters visible:** Tracking uses `mi_cid`, `mi_mid`, `mi_u`, `mi_ecmp` and `email_name` (e.g. `261003_DG_Email_Saturda…`) on image URLs. Click links use opaque `?qs=` tokens, so destination URLs and any UTM tagging can't be assessed.
- **Landing page alignment:** Not checkable because the redirects were skipped.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run QA on the full HTML and the raw `.eml` to settle the unsubscribe, address and authentication items.
2. Resolve a sample of the `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=` links, following redirects, to check destinations, offer match and UTMs.
3. Add meaningful `alt` text to the 8 content images and `alt=""` to the `click.chp` and `demdex` pixels.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
5. Fix the markup:
   - Delete the stray `</custom>`.
   - Move the schema.org `<div>` out of `<head>`.
   - Fill the `<title>` and drop or populate the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon">`.
   - Correct `.ReadMsgBody`.
   - Add `role="presentation"` to the remaining layout tables.
6. Remove duplicate font loading, and consider consolidating the tracking pixels.
7. Tune the QA checker: treat `alt=""` as valid, and flag a truncated source as inconclusive for footer checks instead of emitting WARNs.
