# Extra 20% off starts today & your cart is so ready 🛒
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-02**
## Executive summary

- My first reaction was that the email feels like a mall directory. I opened it and kept scrolling past holiday decor, a gift shop, Top 100 gifts, clearance, delivery options and a Ninja slow cooker. The subject said "your cart is so ready," but there's no cart in the email, just a general catalog. If I had something waiting in my cart, I didn't see it here, and that kind of line is a small broken promise.
- The bar at the top ("Status: Kohl's Rewards Member | Earn: 5% Rewards") is the only part that treats me like a known customer. The 20% off code, SAVEBIG, is down by the barcode at the very bottom, and it's the actual offer. This is the Kohl's I've been hearing from since 10/26. On 11/01 it was "Last day ⏰ Save 20% + get $10 off your $50+", and then "Ends soon ⏳" the same day. Today is a new offer, "Extra 20%", but the layout is just as busy. Would I click? Maybe if I needed a gift. I wouldn't forward it.
- Sephora would have put the code and the offer in the first screen. Neiman's "Roundup: Wild Things" sends six bags and no shouting, and it respects me more than this does. Macy's at least tells me what I'm confirming and why.

## What's working

- The member bar at the top shows my status and the 5% Rewards earn rate. It's a small bit of respect.
- "Clearance, save up to 70%" is a big, clear block with a real number.
- The "Fast & Free Store Pickup" strip with "Get $5 Kohl's Cash on orders $49+" gives me a reason to go in store. I like buying at the counter.
- The product tiles at the bottom show real prices, like the Ninja at 119.99 and the Sertа topper at 229.99–349.99. They also show the original prices.
- The "Style Square / Holiday Party Edit" block is the one section that looked aimed at a woman my age: a polished couple, a heel and a bag.

## What's weak

- The "Extra 20% off" in the subject isn't in the hero. The top of the email is a sled image with "Shop Now," and the code is buried at the bottom.
- "Your cart is so ready" has no cart behind it. I see no product I left behind.
- There are too many sections: decor, gift shop, Top 100, Toy Box, clearance, delivery, picks. It's Macy's-style overload, and nothing tells me where to start.
- The fine print is dense and tiny. There's a "Coupons do not apply to select brands, details" line. I also read an exclusion note about Sephora at Kohl's purchases. I don't want to hunt for the catch.
- Nothing beauty-related stands out for me, and there's no tier or event signal beyond that top bar.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in the hero: "Extra 20% off, code SAVEBIG, ends Nov. 5," with a single button. Don't make me scroll to the barcode.
- 2. If the subject says "cart," show the cart. If there's no cart data, drop the claim.
- 3. Cut the email to three blocks: the offer, one gift guide and clearance.
- 4. Make the exclusions legible, in plain sentences next to the offer.
- 5. Use my member status. Show my Rewards earn rate in the hero or a "your Kohl's Cash" line.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 20% off through Nov. 5, code SAVEBIG`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Rewards: extra 20% off + $5 Kohl's Cash`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code SAVEBIG on holiday gifts and home. Ends Nov. 5.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus clearance up to 70% off, and free store pickup.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based Movable Ink and Adobe Audience Manager-instrumented template with several markup defects: an empty `<title>`, a stray `</custom>` tag, an `itemscope` div inside `<head>`, and zoom-blocking viewport settings. Compliance and authentication can't be confirmed. The relevant headers weren't captured, and the source is truncated before the footer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 74 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe, and their destinations are unverified. The visible example is `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7ImQi…`, an opaque `qs` blob.
- **Four tracking pixels fire before any content:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/a5c71099-…` (1x1, no `alt`, no hiding style)
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`
- **Visible pixel risk:** The first pixel sits directly in `<body>` with no `display:none` or wrapper. Some clients may render it as a stray inline element.
- **Malformed markup:** The `open.aspx` pixel is followed by a stray `</custom>` closing tag with no matching opener, inside `<div style="font-size:0; line-height:0;">`.
- **Empty icon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty `href`, which some parsers resolve to the document URL.
- **Movable Ink query strings:** Image URLs (`mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1025026_2026112&email_name=261102_DG_Email_Monday&…`) carry a subscriber-level ID (`mi_u`) in the URL.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 on supporting clients.
- **Invalid head markup:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">…</div>` is inside `<head>`, where `div` is invalid. Parsers will implicitly close `<head>` at that point. Any `<head>` content after it, including `<meta>` and `<style>`, will be treated as body content. The schema.org microdata is also unlikely to be honored in email clients.
- **Missing alt text (QA, 11 images):**
  - Likely content images, which need descriptive `alt`: `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/{2a5adc5afb647aad, f441d45d2128ae53, c2f81e9ac8daee44, 415c8a18fc2950ea, 69494b48a79b4240, 78308d1d9adb0751, 916d6fa08d185233}.png`, plus `59551394_1_2x` and `59551394_Product_hdcu16_Apparel_Ftw_Hero_GIF`. Whether they are decorative can't be determined from the truncated source.
  - Tracking pixels: `a5c71099-…` and `demdex.net/event` truly lack `alt`.
  - QA false positive: `o.gif` already has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"` in the source and is correctly marked decorative. The QA check appears to treat empty `alt` as missing.
- **Fonts and Outlook:** Google Fonts are loaded in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` and Outlook gets the Arial fallback, so there is no issue there. The `<style>` block doing `@import` will be stripped by Gmail, which is also handled by the font stack.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Merge tokens:** No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[[…]]`) appear in the visible source.
- **Possible empty value:** One Movable Ink URL contains an empty parameter, `…&mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_pe…`. This may be intentional if the recipient has no Kohl's Cash, but it's worth confirming that the dynamic image degrades correctly.
- **Truncation:** The source is truncated, so cart and product content can't be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** QA flags `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) as not found. QA itself attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly not capturing headers, so it's inconclusive. It can't be treated as confirmed non-compliance.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown. Sending domain is `s.kohls.com`.
- **Footer:** The footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address aren't visible in the truncated source, so they can't be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** None can be verified. All CTAs route through the `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…` redirector, and its destination URLs weren't resolved because the probe skipped them.
- **Landing pages:** The destination and any promo-code carry-through for the "extra 20% off" offer are untested.
- **Identifiers:** The email carries `email_name=261102_DG_Email_Monday` and `mi_ecmp=1025026_2026112`. These could be used for attribution, but nothing confirms they reach the site.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fill in `<title>`, and remove the stray `</custom>` tag and the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon" href="">`.
2. Move the schema.org `<div itemscope>` out of `<head>`, or drop it.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
4. Add descriptive `alt` text to the content images listed in section 3. Use `alt=""` plus `aria-hidden="true"` on the tracking pixels, and wrap the first pixel in a hidden container.
5. Re-run QA with the full source and raw headers (not the relay copy) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, the footer unsubscribe link and the postal address.
6. Follow a sample of the redirect links with a headless fetch to check final URLs for UTM parameters and promo carry-through.
7. Check the checker: treat explicit `alt=""` as valid so decorative images like `o.gif` stop showing up as warnings.
