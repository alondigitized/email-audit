# Prices so low, filling your cart is a little too easy 🛒
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-21**
## Executive summary

- This is the seventh Kohl's email I've had in about two weeks. It's the same maroon "Lowest Prices of the Season" template as the 10/19, 10/20 and earlier 10/21 sends ("Lowest prices 🤝 Top finds"). I scrolled a very long way, and it reads like a flyer, not a note to a customer I know. The "ENDS TONIGHT" shouting from 10/18 is still gone, and I'll credit them for that.
- The top does one thing I like. There's a status bar with my Rewards membership and earn rate, a "Plus, earn Kohl's Cash" strip, and a clear 85% clearance box. Then it turns into a tile-by-tile product dump: bedding, a roaster oven, electronics, fleece, dresses, activewear, sweaters, boots, towels. Nothing in it is for me. There's no beauty up top, only a "Beauty / Sephora" tile buried in the gift section. I shop the counter, not the towel aisle.
- Would I click? Only if I needed boots or towels. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- The Rewards status and earn bar at the very top tells me they know I'm a member. Neiman Marcus's InCircle email made points the hero. Here they're a thin strip, but they're there.
- The "Plus, earn Kohl's Cash" band and the 85% clearance box give me one concrete number to look at.
- The price tiles are clean. Each shows a starting price with the "Reg." price beside it, such as $9.99 fleece against $14.99–$34.99. That reads honest, and I can compare it to what Macy's would charge.
- "Free returns through January 31, 2027" is a real trust cue. I return things, and a long window respects me.
- The Sephora at Kohl's tile is the one thing close to my lane.

## What's weak

- There are ten near-identical maroon tiles in a row. The eye has no hero, so nothing stands out. Sephora opens with one clear offer, and this is the opposite.
- It's the seventh Kohl's email in two weeks. I'm tired of it, and the cadence now feels like a megaphone.
- Beauty is almost invisible. Sephora at Kohl's is the only place I'd earn points and play with product, and it's a small tile near the bottom.
- The category chips at the top (Home, Women, Men, Kids, $10 & Under, $20 & Under) are small. The brand has all of those, but I don't see anything for the woman in her late 40s.
- The legal fine print at the bottom is tiny and dense. "Coupons do not apply to select brands" sits under the picks section, and I can barely read the rest on a phone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the ten tiles down to three, and lead with a Rewards or Kohl's Cash hero. Make the member benefit the headline, not a thin strip.
- 2. Segment by category. Send me a Sephora-first version with beauty points or a gift-with-purchase. Ulta would have put that first, and Sephora's sample bag email did.
- 3. Space the sends out. Combine the daily "lowest prices" emails into one weekly one.
- 4. Make the Kohl's Cash dates and amounts explicit in the hero, so I know what I earn and when it expires.
- 5. Make the fine print readable.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your Rewards + Kohl's Cash: lowest prices of the season`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Beauty at Kohl's: earn points on Sephora this week`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Earn $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 spent, through Oct. 25`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fleece from $9.99, boots from $17.99, plus free returns to Jan. 31`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML has several markup defects (stray `</custom>`, `<div>` inside `<head>`, empty `<title>`, zoom disabled) and widespread missing `alt` attributes. The compliance and authentication warnings come from header and footer data this run couldn't capture, so they are unconfirmed. The QA run's own tally (38% pass rate, "0 issues, 5 warnings") is internally inconsistent.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **78 tracking links were not probed.** All were skipped as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and landing-page validity are unverified.
- **Opaque redirects.** Links use `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7ImQi…`, an encoded blob with no plain-text destination or UTM parameters.
- **Four separate tracking domains** fire on open:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/ec1ece2f-…` (1x1 pixel)
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager, `width="0" height="0"`)
- **Redundant pixels.** Three first-party open pixels (the first two above plus `open.aspx`) are redundant. Each adds a request and a failure point.
- **Subscriber identifier in image URLs.** `mi_u=604230016` appears in every `mi.kohls.com` image URL, along with `email_name=261021_DG_Email_Wed_PM`. This is a persistent ID in plain-text query strings, which is a privacy concern if images are proxied or logged.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 13,644 of 19,341 characters (about 70.5%) are URLs, which is a spam-filter and readability risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on content images.** Examples from QA:
  - `58966978_2sa_2x`, `58966978_1sa_2x`, `58966978_11sa_2x`, `58966978_14sa_2x` (`images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/…`)
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` (`2a5adc5afb647aad`, `f441d45d2128ae53`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44`, `415c8a18fc2950ea`, `69494b48a79b4240`, `78308d1d9adb0751`, `916d6fa08d185233`)
  - The `rp/` PNGs look like dynamically rendered content blocks. If they carry text or offers, that content is invisible with images off or to screen readers. The QA list is truncated, so the real count may be higher.
- **Tracking pixels with no `alt`.** The first `click.chp.kohls.com` pixel and the `kohls.demdex.net` pixel have none, and the first lacks `aria-hidden`. The `o.gif` pixel does have `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, so the pattern is inconsistent.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, an accessibility failure under WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Empty `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`** triggers a request to the base URL in some clients.
- **Stray `</custom>`.** It follows `<img src="…open.aspx…" alt="">` with no matching opening tag, which is malformed markup.
- **`<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` inside `<head>`.** Block elements are invalid there. Parsers may close `<head>` early and push the `<meta>` tags and any later head content into the body.
- **Fonts loaded from Google** via both `<link>` and `@import`, duplicated. The `Montserrat,Arial,Helvetica,sans-serif` fallback stack is adequate.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the truncated source. The personalization present is the per-recipient `mi_u` and `mi_ecmp` query values in the `mi.kohls.com` image URLs, and they appear resolved. Only the beginning of the HTML was available, so the full body isn't covered.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
All four items below are unconfirmed, not confirmed defects.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058)** weren't found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them. This matters for Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements if the headers are truly absent.
- **`Authentication-Results`** wasn't found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `s.kohls.com` can't be assessed.
- **Physical address** wasn't detected. The footer is outside the truncated source, so the QA flag may be a truncation artifact.
- **In-body unsubscribe link.** The footer wasn't visible, so its presence couldn't be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters** are visible on any link. Attribution appears to depend on the opaque `qs=` token and `mi_*` / `email_name` parameters.
- **Landing-page alignment can't be verified.** All 78 tracked links were skipped, so link-to-destination matching, price and promo consistency, and 404s were not checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the product images (`58966978_*_2sa_2x`) and the `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` blocks. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
2. Remove `</custom>`, move the `itemscope` div out of `<head>`, populate `<title>`, and delete the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon" href="">`.
3. Drop `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Consolidate the redundant open pixels and review whether `demdex.net` needs to fire on open.
5. Re-run QA on the raw `.eml`, or with a link resolver that follows redirects. This would settle `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, the footer address and unsubscribe link, and the destination and UTM checks.
6. Fix the QA summary logic. "0 issue(s), 5 warning(s)" doesn't match the 15+ warnings listed or the 38% pass rate.
