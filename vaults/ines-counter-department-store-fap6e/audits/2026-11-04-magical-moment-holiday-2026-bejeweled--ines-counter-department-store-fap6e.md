## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The truncated source is a standard SFMC-built table email with a responsive stylesheet and a checkbox-hack mobile nav. The only confirmed code-level issue is internal template scaffolding left in the delivered HTML. The compliance and authentication warnings are most likely relay-capture artifacts, and the 18 tracking links were never probed. The source is cut off before the body, footer and links, so most of the email is unaudited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **18 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and landing-page validity are unverified. No broken links are confirmed.
- **The plain-text part is URL-heavy.** 3404 of 3914 characters (about 87%) are URLs. This is typical of long tracked URLs, but it adds spam-filter and readability risk for text-only clients.
- **The template comment points to `@refid`/`@base_refid` tracking.** It appears in the comment only, so I can't confirm that the refid is appended to links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Template comment before `<!DOCTYPE>`.** The first node is a multi-line HTML comment. Some clients treat anything before the doctype as a trigger for quirks mode, which can alter table and box-model rendering. Move it after the doctype or strip it at send time.
- **`maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta** (`width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1`). This blocks pinch-zoom where the client honors it, which conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4. Three commented-out viewport variants are also left in the source.
- **Exact-match class selectors.** Rules like `*[class=contenttable]`, `*[class=mobile]` and `*[class=hide]` only match when `class` is exactly that value. Any element with a second class silently skips the rule.
- **Checkbox-hack hamburger nav.** The `.navbar-checkbox:checked~.menu` rules are unsupported in Gmail and several other clients. I can't see the fallback markup in the truncated source, so this is a risk to check rather than a confirmed failure.
- **`<title>` has a trailing space** (`Neiman Marcus `). This is trivial.
- **Alt text, image dimensions and the `lang` attribute can't be assessed.** The source is truncated before the body, and `<html>` has no `lang` attribute (only `xmlns`).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No rendered or unresolved merge tokens are visible in the available source. The `@email`, `@user`, `@emailid`, `@uEm` and `@cmdid` references appear only inside the leading comment.
- **Internal data-extension names are exposed to recipients.** Anyone who views the source can see `NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated` and `NM_CRM_Feed` in the comment, along with the lookup keys (`EMAIL_ID`, `EMAIL_ADDRESS`, `uEm`, `cmdid`). This is information disclosure of internal schema.
- **Authoring instructions were left in.** `CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW` and `SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256` suggest the template-selection step wasn't cleaned up before send.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool flags that the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers, so this is unconfirmed rather than a defect. Large senders are expected to include both, and Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results` is missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown because the relay doesn't expose it. The From domain (`e.neimanmarcus.com`) is a sending subdomain, but alignment isn't verified.
- **The unsubscribe link and physical postal address can't be checked.** They are most likely in the truncated footer.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be evaluated.** All 18 links sit behind tracking redirects that were not resolved, and no final URLs appear in the truncated source. No mismatches are confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Strip the leading SFMC comment, including the DE names and the "CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO" text, at send time. If it stays, move it after the doctype.
2. Re-run the QA against a direct SFMC test send or raw `.eml` with full headers. That would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
3. Resolve the tracking redirects to check final URLs, HTTP status and UTM/refid parameters. Then audit the full, untruncated HTML for alt text, the unsubscribe link, the postal address and the nav fallback.
4. Drop `maximum-scale=1` and add `lang` to `<html>`.
5. Shorten the tracked URLs in the plain-text part, or add more prose to it, to reduce the URL ratio.
6. Switch the mobile media-query selectors to `[class~=...]` or plain class selectors so they survive multi-class elements.
