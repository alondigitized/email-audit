- There's no offer, no percent off, no Circle/InCircle points callout anywhere — as a member who refuses to use a card without points back, I got nothing acknowledging my loyalty tier.

- The model is styled beautifully but skews young and editorial rather than acknowledging a 47-year-old shopper's actual fit questions (no sizing or fabric info visible anywhere).
