# Save an extra 20% during our Customer Appreciation Event 🎉
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-10**
## Executive summary

- Alright, this one opens with a three-way discount race right at the top — 30% for Kohl's Card holders, 20% for Rewards members like me, 15% for nonmembers — and I'll admit, seeing my tier called out first with the coupon math already done is more respectful than most of what lands in my inbox. But then it just... keeps going. Free Spirit Boho, Kids, Men, Bedding, Bath, Kitchen, Decor, Storage, Floor Care, a Ninja Slushi machine, memory foam mattress toppers — by the time I hit "Picked with you in mind" I've forgotten what "you" even means to this brand anymore.
- This is Kohl's fifth or sixth send this week and it's the same three-tier discount math I already saw in the 09-10 "20% off with Kohl's Rewards ☀️" email and the same "ENDS TODAY" energy dressed slightly nicer than the 09-07 Labor Day panic version. Neiman's "1,000+ arrivals" email I read this week didn't need a single coupon code stacked on a coupon code to make me want to look — Kohl's is still leaning on "REWARDS20, ends Sept 20" like the product isn't allowed to speak for itself.
- I'm not clicking through this whole catalog. I might tap "Shop Now" on the boho jacket because that model actually looks like she could be a friend of mine, but honestly this is a skim-and-archive, not a forward.

## What's working

- My rewards tier is front and center with real math (20% + coupon) instead of buried fine print — better than making me hunt for my status.
- The Free Spirit Boho hero with the denim jacket and ankle boots is genuinely styled for someone my age, not a 22-year-old in a crop top.
- "Take an extra 20% off" appears again near the bottom with the actual coupon barcode and code — easy to screenshot and use in-store, which is how I actually shop Kohl's.
- Earn $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 spent is a real loyalty mechanic I recognize and value, unlike Sephora's confusing four-banner points stack.

## What's weak

- This email tries to be six emails at once — apparel, home, kitchen, mattress toppers — and by the third category block I've completely lost the thread of what I opened this for.
- The product grid at the bottom (towel, slushi maker, jeans, mattress topper) has zero connective tissue — that's not "picked with you in mind," that's "picked at random from the warehouse."
- Three separate discount tiers stacked in the first screen is a math problem before I've even scrolled, and it's the same overload trick Sephora pulled with its 50%/4X/5X stacking.
- "New Trending: Free Spirit Boho" only to pivot into cowboy boots and a slouchy purse feels like a trend chaser, not a curated point of view.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this into two sends — apparel/beauty one day, home/kitchen the next. Nobody needs a mattress topper and a boho jacket in the same scroll.
- 2. Lock the hero to one category and let it breathe — the Free Spirit Boho block alone, without Kids/Men/Home crammed underneath it, would actually hold my attention.
- 3. Drop to one clear offer instead of three tiers — Rewards members already know their rate; showing me Cardholder and Nonmember pricing just adds noise I have to filter past.
- 4. Replace "Picked with you in mind" with real personalization — if I'm a Rewards member with purchase history, show me items near my past categories, not a random towel-to-mattress-topper grid.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Standard Kohl's promotional HTML template using nested tables/MSO conditionals for Outlook compatibility, with multiple tracking pixels and click-redirect links; compliance headers (unsubscribe, authentication) are absent from the captured payload.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 67 tracking/click-redirect links detected and skipped by automated probing (redirect domains, e.g. `click.chp.kohls.com`, `click.s.kohls.com`) — link destinations not verified.
- Three separate open/tracking pixels present: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` (1x1), `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif` (1x1), and `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...` (1x1) — redundant multi-vendor open tracking (Kohl's CHP + Kohl's mi.kohls.com), not a functional defect but worth noting for load overhead.
- Adobe Audience Manager pixel (`kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`) fires unconditionally on open.
- Malformed markup: `<img ... aria-hidden="true" /></custom>` — a stray, unopened `</custom>` closing tag follows the tracking pixel `<img>` inside the `<td>`. Invalid HTML; may cause unpredictable DOM handling in strict renderers.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- All 8 flagged images (tracking pixels + `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` rendering-pixels) are missing `alt` text. Tracking/rendering pixels are non-content (1x1) so this is low-impact, but confirm the same holds for any content/hero images further down the truncated source (not visible in this excerpt).
- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` present — expected for transactional/marketing email, not a defect.
- Legacy DOCTYPE (HTML 4.01 Transitional) with MSO conditional comments — standard practice for Outlook table-based rendering, no issue.
- Responsive breakpoint at 500px via media query with `.hide-sm`/`.show-sm` classes — structurally sound as far as visible.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) visible in the truncated source.
- `mi_mid`, `mi_cid`, `mi_u` query params on tracking links appear populated (not literal placeholders), consistent with correct merge/personalization resolution at send time.

7. Recommendations
