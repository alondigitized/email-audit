# Low prices + Kohl's Cash 🤩 Best combo ever!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-24**
## Executive summary

- This is the eleventh Kohl's email in about two weeks, and it's the same maroon "Lowest Prices of the Season" template I've seen since 10/19. The subject promised a "best combo ever" of low prices plus Kohl's Cash. The top of the email gives that combo one small green "Plus earn Kohl's Cash" strip tucked under the hero. The "Clearance, up to 85% off" box is still up there from yesterday's send ("Low prices AND up to 85% off clearance? Win, win").
- Below that it's a very long scroll of maroon tiles: towels from $3.99, boots, sweaters, throws, and Sephora at Kohl's up to 75% off. There are some real prices in there, and the Sephora tile at the bottom is the first thing that spoke to me. But it's a catalog, not a point of view. I'd skim it and probably not click. I'd only forward it to a friend who lives for Kohl's Cash.

## What's working

- The Sephora at Kohl's tile (up to 75% off beauty) is the one thing in here for me. I'd check that and nothing else.
- Specific prices like "Starting at 3.99" towels and "17.99" boots show the real number and the "Reg." price. That's honest.
- The "Earn 10 Kohl's Cash" line with dates (through Oct 25, redeemable Oct 26–Nov 1) is clear, credible urgency.
- I liked the "Free returns through January 31, 2027" banner. As someone who returns at the brand event, I notice that.
- The Kohl's Rewards member status line at the top treats me like a known customer.

## What's weak

- The subject said "best combo ever," but the Kohl's Cash is a small green strip, not the hero. The email doesn't deliver on its own subject.
- Eleven nearly identical emails in two weeks is fatigue. The 10/22 send ("Your budget is going to love these low prices") and the 10/23 send ("Check these out ➡️") already had this exact layout.
- Nine maroon tiles with the same "Lowest Prices of the Season" tag all look alike. Nothing stands out, so my eye has no place to land after the hero.
- The "Coupons do not apply to select brands" and "Coupons and Kohl's Cash do not apply" notes sit on several tiles. That undercuts the "best combo" claim for me, since the combo doesn't work on the brands I'd want.
- The mix of towels, boots, activewear and kitchen is unfocused, and nothing signals beauty or designer access.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud send (`click.s.kohls.com`) with Movable Ink dynamic images (`mi.kohls.com`). The visible HTML has several markup defects, five open and tracking beacons, and an empty Kohl's Cash date parameter. The compliance items flagged by QA (unsubscribe, physical address, authentication) can't be confirmed because the source is truncated and the relay strips headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **74 tracking links skipped:** The HTTP probe skipped them as click-redirect domains, so none of the destinations were checked. `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…` uses opaque SFMC tokens, so landing pages can't be validated from the source.
- **Five open/tracking beacons in the top of the body:**
  1. `click.chp.kohls.com/o/70cdfa61-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
  2. `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&…`
  3. `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  4. `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager)
  5. The Movable Ink `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` render calls
- **User identifier in image URLs:** `mi_u=604230016` appears in every Movable Ink image URL. Treat these URLs as personal data, since they can end up in proxy logs and caches.
- **Third-party call from the email body:** The `demdex.net` request is a third-party call, and its presence should be reviewed.
- **Malformed tag:** `<img src="…open.aspx?…" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>` has a stray `</custom>` closing tag with no matching opener.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Empty `<title>`:** `<title></title>` leaves the document without an accessible name.
- **Missing `alt` attributes:**
  - The first beacon (`click.chp.kohls.com/o/…`) has no `alt`.
  - The demdex image has no `alt`.
  - The QA scan lists 58966978_2sa_2x, _1sa_2x, _11sa_2x and _14sa_2x as missing alt text (`images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/…`). Those look like product images.
  - Several `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` dynamic images are also flagged: 2a5adc5afb647aad, f441d45d2128ae53, c2f81e9ac8daee44, 415c8a18fc2950ea, 69494b48a79b4240, 78308d1d9adb0751 and 916d6fa08d185233. If these carry offer content, they need alt text.
  - The `o.gif` flag looks like a false positive. The source shows `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, which is correct for a pixel.
- **Invalid markup in `<head>`:**
  - `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` with `<meta itemprop>` children is not valid in `<head>`. Parsers move it into the body, and mail clients ignore the microdata.
  - `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href.
- **Invalid CSS:**
  - `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` is missing its leading dot, so the selector matches nothing.
  - `max-height: auto !important` is not a valid value.
  - `td,th,div,p,a {}` in the MSO block is an empty rule.
- **Missing `role="presentation"`:** The 1×1 table, the AAM table and the preheader table (`width='640'`) lack it. The main layout tables have it.
- **Duplicate meta:** `<meta name="format-detection">` appears twice. This is harmless.
- **Font loading:** Fonts load through `fonts.googleapis.com` `<link>` and `@import`. Both are inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, and the stack falls back to Arial/Helvetica, so the impact is low.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Empty Kohl's Cash parameter:** `…916d6fa08d185233.png?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1024441_20261024&mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_p…` has an empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=`. The subject promises "Kohl's Cash", so the redemption end date rendered blank in this copy. If this recipient has no Kohl's Cash event, that's expected. Otherwise it's a dynamic-content failure.
- **No unrendered tokens:** No visible `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[[…]]` tokens appear in the portion of the source I could see. The rest of the source is truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** Neither header was found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may not capture them, so this is unconfirmed. If they are really absent, the email lacks RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe, which Gmail and Yahoo require of bulk senders.
- **Physical address:** QA found none. The HTML is truncated before the footer, so this is unverified. Check the full source.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `s.kohls.com` shares a parent with the click domain `click.s.kohls.com`, which is consistent with alignment, but alignment can't be confirmed without the header.
- **In-body unsubscribe link:** Not visible in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters in the visible links:** Campaign identity is carried in Movable Ink parameters instead (`mi_ecmp=1024441_20261024`, `email_name=261024_DG_Email_Sat_PM`) and in opaque SFMC `qs=` tokens. Whether these map to analytics campaign tags on the Kohls.com landing pages can't be checked, because the 74 redirect links were not probed.
- **Inconsistent `email_name`:** `email_name=261024_DG_Email_Sat_PM` appears on some `mi.kohls.com` image URLs and not on others, such as 69494b48a79b4240 and 78308d1d9adb0751. This may make attribution inconsistent.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run the audit against the full, untruncated source and raw headers to confirm the unsubscribe link, `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, physical address, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
2. Resolve a sample of the `click.s.kohls.com` redirects to record final landing URLs and check for UTM or analytics parameters.
3. Investigate the empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` by confirming whether this seed address has a Kohl's Cash event.
4. Fix the markup defects:
   - Remove the stray `</custom>` tag.
   - Correct `ReadMsgBody` to `.ReadMsgBody`.
   - Drop `max-height: auto`.
   - Remove the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon" href="">`.
   - Move or remove the schema.org `<div>` and `<meta>` tags from `<head>`.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta, and give the email a non-empty `<title>`.
6. Add `alt=""` to the first beacon and the demdex image. Add descriptive alt text to the product images (`58966978_*`) and any Movable Ink images that carry offer content.
7. Add `role="presentation"` to the 1×1, AAM and preheader tables.
8. Review whether the `demdex.net` call and the five beacons are all needed, given the per-user ID in `mi_u`.
