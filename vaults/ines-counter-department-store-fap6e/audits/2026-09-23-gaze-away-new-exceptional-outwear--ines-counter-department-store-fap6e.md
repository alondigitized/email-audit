# Gaze away: New exceptional outwear.
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-23**
## Executive summary

- Well, this is a nice surprise — Neiman's actually gave the outerwear editorial room to breathe instead of stapling a forest-green "FRIENDS & FAMILY" banner over it for the fourth time this month. The hero shot — that shearling-cuffed coat with the model striding in sunglasses — is genuinely covetable, and the second look, that chocolate shearling moto with the cream trim, is exactly the kind of investment piece I'd actually try on at the counter before buying. But here's the catch: there's no price, no percentage off, nothing. This is pure brand-building, not a reason to click today.
- I like it more as a magazine page than as an email that's supposed to sell me something. It's better than the third "Friends & Family" repeat I flagged on 9/22 and 9/23, and it beats Kohl's seventh identical "Epic Deals" grid this week hands down on taste. But compared to Sephora's Hair Daily Deals — 20-50% off, code DELIVERED, opens strong and closes with a real offer — this is all mood, no mechanism.
- Would I click? Maybe, out of curiosity about the coat. Would I forward it to a friend? Only if she's shopping for a splurge coat and needs inspiration, not if she wants a deal. This gets read, admired, and closed without action.

## What's working

- The hero image actually respects the product — full-length shot, clean styling, no clutter competing for my eye.
- "MAKE WAY FOR FALL'S ENTRANCE COATS" has a little wit to it, which is more than I can say for Neiman's usual "LIMITED TIME" stack.
- Two distinct outerwear looks (grey wool/fur mix, then chocolate shearling) give me a real sense of the range without overwhelming me Macy's-grid style.
- "Match with a personal style advisor" is a nice touch for someone like me who actually uses that service in-store.

## What's weak

- Zero pricing, zero percentage off, zero loyalty tie-in — as an InCircle-adjacent shopper, I want to know if there's a member perk here, and there isn't one visible.
- "Shop the Story" and "Shop Exceptional Outerwear" are both vague-verb CTAs — neither tells me where I land or what I'll pay.
- The second product block ("Embrace bold elegance in designs of sheer perfection") is copy soup — sheer perfection describing a heavy shearling coat doesn't parse.
- No urgency, no season framing beyond "fall," so there's nothing pulling me to act today versus next week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a price or "New Arrivals from $XXX" under at least one hero look — right now this reads like a lookbook, not a sales email, and I need a reason to click now, not later.
- 2. Swap "Shop the Story" for something concrete like "Shop the Shearling Coat" — Sephora's specificity (hair care, 20-50% off) is why that email scored higher than this one will.
- 3. Add an InCircle callout if there's a member benefit on outerwear — right now a known customer isn't being treated any differently than a stranger.
- 4. Fix the "sheer perfection" line — either cut it or rewrite for the actual fabric (shearling, wool) so it doesn't sound AI-generated.
