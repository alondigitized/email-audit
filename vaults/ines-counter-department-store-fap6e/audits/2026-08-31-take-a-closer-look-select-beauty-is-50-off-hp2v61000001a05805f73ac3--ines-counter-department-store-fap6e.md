# Take a closer look—select beauty is 50% off 👀
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-31**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "select beauty is 50% off" got a half-glance from me, but the real hook here is up top — $5 off when I redeem 250 points, plus 4x points on top brands, "ENDS TODAY." That's the first time in a while Sephora's led with something that actually acknowledges I'm sitting on a points balance instead of just reminding me it's empty. Compare that to the 8/28 send where "0 points" was slapped at the top like a scolding — this one at least gives me a reason to use the app instead of shame me for not having.
- Past that banner, though, it's back to the standard Sephora sprawl: a "Next Big Thing" hero for two brands I don't know (Natasha Denona, Violette_FR), then eight-plus product tiles in a grid before I even get to the 50% off box mid-scroll. Nordstrom's Beauty Loves You events and even Macy's confirm-your-discount emails (7/10 from me on 8/28) at least keep one offer front and center — this buries its best asset three scrolls down.
- Would I click? Probably, for the points redemption specifically — that's real value I can act on today. Forward to a friend? No, this is routine Sephora noise, not something worth sharing.

## What's working

- The $5-off-250-points + 4x points banner at the very top is the first genuinely loyalty-forward move I've seen from Sephora in this string of sends — it's not just "you have 0 points," it's "here's what to do with them."
- "ENDS TODAY" and "TODAY ONLY" give the urgency actual teeth instead of vague "hurry."
- The Tower 28 before/after for the Swipe Serum Foundation is smart — that's exactly the product proof I want before I click through on a new foundation.
- Clean product photography throughout, no broken images, no lorem-ipsum — production quality is solid, on par with the 8/30 "Iconic Beauty" send.

## What's weak

- The 50% off Select Beauty box — the thing named in the subject line — doesn't show up until well past the halfway point of the email, buried under a wall of "NEW" product tiles I have to scroll through first.
- "THE NEXT BIG THING" hero leads with two brands I've never heard of before I even see a dollar figure — that's asking a lot of my attention up front.
- This is the fifth near-identical "select beauty X% off" send in eleven days by my count (8/26, 8/28, 8/29, 8/30, now 8/31) — the cadence is genuinely wearing me down, more than Kohl's clearance-dump approach even, because at least Kohl's varies what's on sale.
- "Exclusions/terms apply" shows up twice, plus "While supplies last" on the 50% off box — fine print stacking that echoes the same complaint I had about the 8/29 send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the entire email with the 50% off Select Beauty box and the points banner combined into one hero — right now the subject line's promise is buried under a new-brand launch nobody asked for first.
- 2. Cut the "Next Big Thing" hero down to a single small module lower in the email — new-brand discovery isn't why I opened this.
- 3. Stop sending "select beauty 50% off" as the headline promo five times in two weeks — rotate in something else or consolidate into one stronger weekly event, the way Macy's ties its discounts to a confirm-and-claim mechanic.
- 4. Move the Beauty Insider tier bar (Insider/VIB/Rouge) up near the top banner so the points offer and my tier status reinforce each other instead of living at opposite ends of the email.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 4x points day is here — plus 50% off select beauty`
- **Subject Alt B:** `$5 off with your points, ends today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Redeem 250 points for $5 off, then stack 50% off Clinique, Patrick Starrr & more`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `4x points + half-off favorites — both end tonight`
