# FREE foundation and concealer sample set with $45 min. spend 🤩
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-30**
## Executive summary

- My first reaction: finally, an offer that does what the subject says on the tin. "Free Foundation and Concealer Sample Set" is right there as the hero, no bait-and-switch, no "wait, where's my 50% off" scavenger hunt like Charlotte Tilbury pulled on me back on 7/26. This is a gift-with-purchase play, not a sale, and it's dressed reasonably well — purple hero, clean SHOP NOW button, real product tiles for the sample set itself.
- But then it just... keeps going. After the GWP block I get eight unrelated "new arrivals" — a $182 Viktor&Rolf perfume, a $62 Kérastase serum, a $25 MAC lipstick, a $172 Miu Miu fragrance — followed by "Spot it. Shop it." with Charlotte Tilbury blush and hair oils, then ANOTHER "Just Arrived" fragrance block. That's Kohl's-level sprawl dressed up in nicer typography. And right at the very top, under my Insider badge, it's telling me "You have 0 points" again — same tone-deaf greeting I flagged on the 7/29 lip sale email. Sephora, I know I have zero points, you don't need to lead with it.
- Would I click? Yes, on the sample set itself — the offer is real, the threshold ($45) is reasonable, and the points multiplier is a genuine loyalty perk. Would I forward it? No — nothing here is remarkable enough to share, and the back half reads like Sephora threw everything at the wall. I'd click through to the SHOP NOW and then close the tab before I got to the fragrance overflow.

## What's working

- The hero matches the subject line exactly — free sample set, $45 min spend, no surprise substitution. That's a relief after Charlotte Tilbury's 30%-not-50% stunt on 7/26.
- 4X/5X points on foundation and concealer is a real, quantified loyalty benefit, and I can actually use it in-store — the barcode at the bottom for GETMORENOW works at physical Sephora and Kohl's-hosted counters, which matters to someone who buys and returns at the counter like I do.
- The sample set grid shows actual product photography (MERIT, tarte, PATRICK TA callouts) instead of a stock lifestyle shot — I know exactly what I'm getting.
- Dates are printed plainly (July 30–August 5, 2026), so I'm not guessing when this expires.

## What's weak

- "You have 0 points" front and center, again — this is the second time this month Sephora has opened with a reminder that I'm not earning anything. Neiman Marcus at least keeps its fine print buried below the fold instead of at the greeting.
- Two banners (delivery perks, then "Want up to 500 points?") stack above the actual hero offer, so my eye doesn't land on the free sample set until the third scroll-stop, not the first.
- The email sprawls into eight unrelated new-arrival products after the GWP pitch — perfume, haircare, skincare, lipstick — with no thread tying them to the sample set. That's the same overload problem I called out in Kohl's Friends & Family send.
- "SHOP NOW" is the CTA on nearly every block — generic, repeated five separate times. Macy's at least varies its button copy by section.
- "*Exclusions/terms apply" shows up three separate times before I even hit the real terms block — starting to feel like a hedge, not a hero offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Kill the "0 points" callout at the top, or replace it with something that isn't a reminder of what I don't have — this is the single biggest fix, and I've now flagged it twice.
- 2. Move the GWP hero above both promotional ribbons so it's the first thing I see, not the third.
- 3. Cut the eight-product new-arrivals grid down to two or three items that actually relate to the sample set theme (more foundation/concealer, not a $172 fragrance).
- 4. Vary the CTA copy — "Shop the Sample Set," "Redeem My Points" — instead of five identical "SHOP NOW" buttons.
- 5. Move the exact offer dates (July 30–Aug 5) up near the hero instead of burying them at the barcode.
