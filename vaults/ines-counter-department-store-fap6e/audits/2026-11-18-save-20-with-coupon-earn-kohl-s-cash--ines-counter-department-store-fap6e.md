# Save 20% with coupon 😍 Earn Kohl's Cash 🤑
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again, and this is the fourth different promo I've gotten from them since the weekend. The top is a tiny "Take an extra 20%" banner with a Kohl's Cash stamp beside it. It's crammed into a strip above a wall of red category buttons (Home, Women, Men, Kids). The header does say "Kohl's Rewards Member" and "5% Rewards," so they know me. But the body is a catalog that treats me like any shopper.
- The page is very long and busy. Gift Lift Lane, For Her, For Him, For Kids, Stocking Stuffers, Online Only Daily Deals, a Preview the Deals tease, Klarna, DoorDash, Instacart, Uber Eats, then a product grid. Only at the bottom do I find the actual coupon codes: SAVEMORE for 20% off, plus two "$10 off $50" Rewards codes. The offer I came for is the last thing I see. I'd skim it, maybe screenshot the barcode for later, and not forward it.

## What's working

- The member strip at the top ("Status: Kohl's Rewards Member / Earn 5% Rewards") tells me I'm recognized. That matters to me.
- The coupon block at the bottom is clear: code, PIN, barcode, expiry (Nov. 21 for the 20% code). I can use it in-store without hunting.
- The "Kohl's Rewards members take $10 off your toys purchase of $50 or more" line is a real member perk, not a generic discount.
- Pricing on the product picks is easy to read, with the original price struck through (Ninja SLUSHI at $299.99, Levi's 505 at $44.97).

## What's weak

- The 20% offer is buried. The hero banner is small and the real code sits a mile down the page, under delivery partners and appliances.
- The subject's 😍 and 🤑 emojis, plus the "Earn Kohl's Cash" push, feel shouty to me. I notice when an email respects me versus when it shouts, and this one shouts.
- Nothing here is aimed at a 47-year-old beauty buyer. It's toys, puffers and a slushie machine. The fine print even says Sephora at Kohl's is excluded, which is the one category I'd care about.
- The gift-guide tiles, the "Next stop" tease and the DoorDash/Instacart/Uber Eats row make it feel like a Macy's-style overload. Too many messages, none winning.
- The fine print and exclusions are a dense gray block I can't read without zooming.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the code and the deadline in the hero. "Take 20% off with code SAVEMORE, ends Nov. 21" should be the first thing I see, with a "Shop now" button, and the barcode coupons should come right after it.
- 2. Cut the logistics section (Klarna, DoorDash, Instacart, Uber Eats) or move it to the footer. It's noise when I'm trying to decide.
- 3. Name the exclusions up top. If Sephora and certain brands are excluded, say so before I get my hopes up.
- 4. Vary the creative. This is the fourth send in a row at this offer level, the same as 11/15's "HOURS LEFT for 40% off" and 11/16's "Get 20% off" gift guide. Give me one new reason to open.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Show your code at checkout. Rewards members get an extra $10 off.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is mostly standard ESP output with a bulky tracking stack and several HTML validity and accessibility defects. The compliance and authentication warnings can't be confirmed from the data provided, because the HTML is truncated and the relay appears to have dropped the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **70 tracking links were skipped.** They are all on redirect domains (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…`, `click.chp.kohls.com`). Destination resolution and link health are unverified, and the HTTP probe was skipped by design.
- **There are four separate open/tracking pixels before the visible content:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/6e1e02f1-…` (1×1, no `alt`)
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1026045_20261117`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (the Adobe Audience Manager sync, `height="0" width="0"`)
- **Stray `</custom>` closing tag:** it follows the `open.aspx` `<img>`. It is invalid HTML and has no matching opening tag.
- **Internal naming is exposed in image URLs:** `email_name=261117_DG_Email_Tue_PM` appears on `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png`. This is minor, but it is metadata visible in client-side URLs.
- **Fonts load from an external host:** `fonts.googleapis.com` is loaded via `<link>` and `@import`. It is wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, so Outlook desktop is unaffected. This is an extra third-party request and a privacy-relevant fetch.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Images missing `alt`, flagged by QA:**
  - Content images: `60699446_4_2x` and `60699446_3_2x` (`images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/…`).
  - Likely content images on `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/`: `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png` and `916d6fa08d185233.png`. If these carry the offer or coupon text, screen readers and image-blocked clients get nothing.
  - The 1×1 and 0×0 tracking pixels should use `alt=""`, and `role="presentation"` where applicable. Only the `o.gif` pixel has `aria-hidden`.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Viewport meta blocks zoom:** `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` fails WCAG 1.4.4 in clients that honor it.
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** a `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` with `<meta itemprop>` tags sits inside `<head>`. `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href.
- **Doctype mismatch:** HTML 4.01 Transitional is declared alongside `xmlns:o`/`xmlns:v` and `lang="en"`. This is generally tolerated by clients.
- **Layout tables:** the outer layout tables use `role="presentation"`, but the tracking-pixel tables (`width="1" height="1"`) and the AAM table don't.
- **Stray `<style>` rule:** `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` has no `.` or `#` selector prefix, so it is ineffective.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens are visible in the truncated source. The per-recipient identifier `mi_u=604230016` is populated in the pixel URLs. The rest of the body wasn't available, so I can't check the greeting or Kohl's Cash fields.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) are not found.** The QA note says AgentMail may strip these, so this is unconfirmed. Verify against the raw message source.
- **Authentication-Results is not found,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is the same relay limitation. The sender is `Kohls@s.kohls.com`, and its alignment can't be assessed.
- **No physical address detected.** The HTML is truncated before the footer, so this can't be confirmed or refuted. It is a likely false positive for a retailer of this size.
- **`<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">`** is present. It is harmless in most email clients.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- All visible links are opaque `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…` redirects, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be checked from the source.
- Click-wrap hops weren't followed, so there's no data on whether the coupon or Kohl's Cash offer carries through to the landing page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, especially `60699446_*` and the `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` set. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
2. Remove the stray `</custom>` tag. Move the `schema.org` `<div>` and the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon">` out of `<head>`. Fix the `ReadMsgBody` selector.
3. Set a meaningful `<title>`, and remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1`.
4. Consolidate the four tracking pixels where possible.
5. Re-run the checks on the untruncated source and raw `.eml` to confirm the List-Unsubscribe headers, the physical-address footer and the auth results.
6. Resolve a sample of the 70 click-wrapped links to check UTM and landing-page continuity.
