# Extra 20% off savings are waiting ...
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-11**
## Executive summary

- Fifty. That's my mental count of Kohl's emails since Memorial Day, and today's lands with a linen hero and the exact same 20%-off mechanic they ran at me yesterday and the day before. I'm a regular. I have the app. I know this brand. And right now I'm experiencing something I'd call promotional fatigue — the kind where the subject line barely registers because I already know what's inside before I tap. I opened it anyway, because I always check Kohl's when I'm mentally shopping for something casual, and linen has been on my list.
- The hero caught me — green wide-leg linen trousers, white top, $34.99 with coupon. Actually not bad. But that "with coupon" language does what it always does: it adds a little errand to what should be a frictionless click. Sephora just drops the discount at checkout. Macy's, for all its confirmation-email clumsiness (I audited that one recently — the "confirm now for extra 25% off" that dressed up a trigger email in urgency clothing), at least buried the friction one level deeper. Kohl's puts the coupon caveat right in the hero copy and then asks me to go find my wallet. Not a dealbreaker, but it's a tax on goodwill I'm running low on.
- I won't click today. I'll forward this to my sister who is actively shopping summer casualwear. For me? Marked as read.

## What's working

- **The linen hero is genuinely seasonal and relevant** — wide-leg pants in sage green, white top, $34.99 price clearly visible; a 47-year-old woman can see herself in that outfit.
- **Kohl's Cash call-out in the header** — "Plus: earn Kohl's Cash" is the one loyalty signal that gives me a reason to care, since I refuse to transact without points back.
- **20% off is front-and-center from pixel one** — the badge-style treatment in the top banner means I don't have to hunt for the offer.
- **No render bugs** — the email displays cleanly: images load, text doesn't overlap, layout holds.
- **The 40% off sandals section** is the single best offer in the email and deserved more real estate; sandals are actually a summer buy for me.

## What's weak

- **No beauty content whatsoever** — I am a beauty-counter regular. There is not a single skincare, fragrance, or cosmetics callout in this email. Sephora sent me a Patrick Starr setting spray as its hero this week; this is linen and sandals. Different audience, and Kohl's doesn't seem to know that about me.
- **"With coupon" is friction tax at the exact wrong moment** — the offer is conditional and the fix requires me to open the app and dig around. That's one more step than Neiman Marcus asks when they drop a last-call price.
- **The cadence is exhausting** — same 20% offer as July 10, July 9, and the other July 9 send. From my July 10 review: they stepped *down* from 30% to 20% and have been sitting here for days. It doesn't feel like a deal anymore; it feels like the price.
- **Men's and Kids sections are noise for me** — I don't have school-age kids and I don't buy men's clothing; these sections eat scroll space without earning it.
- **"Savings are waiting" CTA copy is inert** — "View coupons in Wallet" is the only action in the bottom section and it does not make me want to move.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Make the beauty section exist.** I have been buying beauty at Kohl's — they carry Neutrogena, Yes To, and the occasional prestige brand. One beauty product in the hero, or even a sidebar, would have changed my read entirely. This is a segmentation failure.
- 2. **Drop the coupon mechanic or pre-apply it.** Show me the price with the discount already reflected, then confirm at checkout. "Extra 20% off with coupon" creates a question mark at the moment I should be reaching for my card.
- 3. **The 40% sandals section should be the hero**, not a third scroll. That's the strongest offer in the email.
- 4. **Retire this subject line pattern.** "Savings are waiting" is the ninth variation of the same holding-pattern phrase. Try something that names a product or a reason.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Linen under $35 — and Kohl's Cash stacks`
- **Subject Alt B:** `These sandals are 40% off. The summer-closet edit.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus your 20% coupon is already in the app — no hunting required.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Wide-leg linen, clearance sandals, and Cash that doesn't expire until August.`

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "Extra 20% off savings are waiting"

### 1. Technical Summary

Structurally sound HTML4 email with standard Kohl's ESP infrastructure (Coherent Path + MessageMedia). Primary concerns are missing authentication header passthrough via the AgentMail relay, absent one-click unsubscribe compliance headers, and pervasive missing `alt` text on tracking and content images.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

22 click-tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe — all routed through `click.chp.kohls.com` and `click.s.kohls.com`. This is expected behavior; no broken destination URLs were detected, but destination integrity cannot be confirmed without resolving the redirects.

Three separate pixel/beacon systems are active in this send:
- `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` — Coherent Path open pixel
- `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...` — MessageMedia open pixel
- `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...` — secondary open tracker
- `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` — Adobe Audience Manager sync pixel

**Issue:** The HTML contains a malformed tag on the third open pixel:
```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>
```
The `</custom>` closing tag is invalid HTML and will produce a parse error in strict renderers. It is likely harmless in practice but indicates a templating artifact that should be cleaned.

---

Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` with `aria-hidden="true"` (the `o.gif` pixel does this correctly — the others do not). Content images must have descriptive alt text for screen reader users and images-off rendering.

**`<title>` is empty.** The `<title></title>` tag is blank, which some clients (notably older Outlook) use as the preview or window label.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** prevents users from zooming, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure and blocked in iOS 10+.

**Google Fonts** loaded via both `<link>` and `@import` for both Roboto and Montserrat — redundant double-load, though the `!mso` conditional guard means Outlook skips it correctly.

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header not detected** via AgentMail relay. CAN-SPAM and Gmail/Yahoo sender requirements (effective Feb 2024) mandate this header for bulk senders. The header may exist on the original SMTP envelope but is not passing through the relay — this needs verification at the MTA level.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058) not detected.** One-click unsubscribe is required by Google and Yahoo for senders above 5K/day. Absence here either means the header is being stripped by the relay or was never set. If stripped, Gmail may demote deliverability.

**`Authentication-Results` header not found.** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status cannot be confirmed from the captured message. The sending domain `s.kohls.com` is an ESP subdomain — alignment with `kohls.com` DMARC policy depends on DKIM signing by Kohl's key, not the ESP's. This should be verified at the relay.

**Physical address** — not visible in truncated source; assumed present in footer per CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5). Needs confirmation against full HTML.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

The `mi_ecmp` parameter (`1017307_2026711`) is used consistently in MessageMedia tracking URLs but functions as an internal campaign ID, not a UTM parameter. It is not human-readable and will not surface in Google Analytics/GA4 without a UTM mapping layer.

No `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, or `utm_content` parameters are visible in the tracking pixel URLs. Whether these are appended to destination click URLs cannot be confirmed without resolving the 22 skipped tracking links. If UTM params are absent from destination URLs, site analytics will attribute Kohl's email traffic to direct/none.
