# Save 20% | Fill your cart with deals on active gear & more 🛒
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-14**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again, and I've genuinely lost count at this point — eleven, twelve of these since the intimates one yesterday? — but I'll say this for it: it's not as narrow as some. It opens with the same red "20% off" starburst and Kohl's Cash banner I could draw from memory now, but then it actually goes wide — activewear, kids' Paw Patrol bedding, fall home goods, a mattress topper, jeans — like someone dumped the whole store into one email. That's not targeting, that's a catalog with a coupon code stapled to the top.
- The Nike/Adidas/Under Armour "up to 40% off" activewear block is the one section that has any real hook for me, and the model at least looks age-appropriate rather than a 19-year-old in a sports bra. But by the time I scroll past kids' bedding, bath towels, a drink maker, and a mattress topper, I've completely forgotten this was supposed to be about "active gear." Compare that to Neiman Marcus's Last Call emails — at least those stay in one lane (even if it's the same shouty crimson lane four times a week). This one can't decide what it's selling.
- I'm marking as read, not clicking, definitely not forwarding. There's no beauty-counter or designer-access signal here at all — this is warehouse-clearance energy, not somewhere I'm spending my Nordstrom-level attention.

## What's working

- The 20% off coupon code (GET20) and expiration date (Aug 16) are clearly stated near the top and repeated at the bottom — no hunting for it.
- Kohl's Rewards status line ("KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER, Earn: 5% REWARDS") is right up front, so at least they know I'm a member.
- The activewear section has real brand names I recognize (Nike, Adidas, Under Armour, New Balance) with a legible "up to 40% off" callout.
- Category nav buttons (Home/Women/Men/Kids) up top give me a fast way to skip past stuff I don't want.

## What's weak

- This is not an "active gear" email past the second screen — it sprawls into kids' bedding, bath towels, a Ninja drink maker, a mattress topper, and jeans, which buries the subject line's promise.
- "Picked with you in mind (your great taste makes it easy)" sitting directly above a random grid of a blender, a towel, a mattress topper, and jeans is almost funny — nothing there was picked for me, it's just inventory.
- Zero beauty, zero designer-tier language, zero events — nothing here treats me like the counter-hopping Nordstrom/Saks shopper I actually am.
- The "Coupons do not apply to select brands" fine print sits right under the 40% off headline — same bait-and-switch pattern I flagged on the clearance email a few days back.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the email in half.** Pick either "active gear" or "fall home refresh" — not both plus kids' bedding plus a blender. A focused send would actually earn the subject line.
- 2. **Move the exclusions out of hiding.** If "coupons do not apply to select brands," say which brands right there, not buried under "details."
- 3. **Drop or relabel the "picked with you in mind" line** — it reads as false personalization when the grid underneath is generic housewares.
- 4. **Give activewear its own send.** It's the one section with genuine relevance to a shopper like me; diluting it with a dozen other categories wastes it.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 20% off activewear — Nike, Adidas & more`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 20% off code expires Aug 16 — activewear inside`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Up to 40% off Nike, Adidas, Under Armour and New Balance — plus earn Kohl's Cash.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stack your 20% off code with clearance activewear pricing before Aug 16.`

# Technical Audit: Kohl's "Save 20%" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a table-based HTML email built on the CoherentPath/Kohl's ESP stack (`s.kohls.com`, `chp.kohls.com`, `mi.kohls.com`) with legacy MSO conditional markup; automated QA returned a 43% pass rate driven by missing accessibility attributes and unconfirmed compliance/authentication headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 74 tracking/click-redirect links were present but skipped by the automated HTTP prober (expected behavior for redirect-chain domains like `click.chp.kohls.com`, `click.s.kohls.com`) — not independently verified as resolving.
- Multiple beacon/pixel calls fire in sequence: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/447c5722-...` (open/click tracking), `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif` (open pixel), `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...&d=60265` (secondary open tracker), and `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager). This is a heavy multi-vendor tracking stack (CoherentPath + Adobe AAM) but structurally normal for a large retailer ESP.
- One malformed tag: `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...&d=60265" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>` — stray `</custom>` closing tag with no matching opener. Not a functional break (browsers/clients will ignore it), but it's invalid markup that should be cleaned up at the source template.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 9 images flagged with missing `alt` text, including all tracking/beacon pixels (`o.gif`, `event`, multiple `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` re-engagement pixels) and the primary `click.chp.kohls.com` open-tracking image.
- For 1x1 tracking pixels, empty/absent alt is standard and low-risk; however these should use `alt=""` with `aria-hidden="true"` consistently — only the `o.gif` pixel has `aria-hidden="true"` set, the others (including the primary tracking image and demdex event pixel) have neither attribute.
- Responsive CSS includes standard mobile breakpoints (`max-width: 500px`) with column stacking and font-size overrides — no rendering issues detected in the visible markup.
- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` are present — correct for transactional/marketing email, not an issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- One re-engagement pixel URL contains an apparently unresolved/empty merge token: `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` (empty value) and a truncated parameter `mi_kc_event_pe` in `916d6fa08d185233.png?...&mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_pe`. This looks like a redemption-end-date personalization field that failed to populate — worth confirming with the ESP whether this is expected (e.g., only populated for specific offer types) or a broken merge tag.
- No visible `{{merge}}` / `%%TOKEN%%` placeholder leakage in the truncated HTML reviewed.
- `mi_mid` (message ID) and `cp_cid`/`cp_tp` (encoded personalization payload) are present on the primary click tracker, consistent with 1:1 personalization infrastructure — cannot verify payload correctness from encoded value alone.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not found by automated check. Flagged as a warning only — may be a relay-capture limitation (AgentMail) rather than a true absence; cannot confirm from HTML body alone since unsubscribe headers are SMTP-level, not HTML.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)**: not found — same caveat; if genuinely absent, one-click unsubscribe (required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender rules) would not be supported.
- **Physical mailing address**: not detected in the truncated HTML reviewed. CAN-SPAM requires this in the email body/footer — flagged as a warning since the footer was not present in the truncated source; needs confirmation against the full email.
- **Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM)**: not found — flagged as unknown status due to AgentMail relay, not a confirmed authentication failure.
- No unsubscribe link visible in the truncated markup either, but this is likely because the footer is beyond the truncation point.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Cannot fully assess: the tracking links use CoherentPath's opaque redirect format (`click.chp.kohls.com/o/{uuid}?mi_mid=...&cp_tp={encoded}&cp_cid=...`) rather than plain UTM query params, so destination URLs and UTM tagging are not visible in the source and were skipped by the automated prober.
- Campaign identifiers are present and consistent across pixels: `mi_ecmp=1019780_2026814` and `email_name=260814_DG_Email_Friday` appear on the re-engagement pixels, suggesting consistent campaign tagging for internal analytics — but this does not confirm UTM alignment on the actual landing page.
- Recommend a manual click-through test on a sample CTA to confirm the resolved landing page matches the "active gear" promotion and carries expected UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`).

## 7. Recommendations
1. Confirm with the ESP/deliverability team whether `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent — if the AgentMail relay is stripping them, that's a monitoring gap, not necessarily a sender problem.
2. Verify the physical mailing address is present in the email footer (likely just outside the truncated source) — required for CAN-SPAM regardless of header status.
3. Add `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"` consistently to all tracking/beacon pixel `<img>` tags for accessibility hygiene.
4. Remove the stray `</custom>` closing tag near the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` pixel.
5. Investigate the empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` parameter on the `916d6fa08d185233.png` pixel to confirm it's not a broken merge token.
6. Manually resolve 2-3 sample tracking redirect URLs to confirm they land on the correct page with expected UTM tagging, since automated probing was skipped for all 74 tracking links.
