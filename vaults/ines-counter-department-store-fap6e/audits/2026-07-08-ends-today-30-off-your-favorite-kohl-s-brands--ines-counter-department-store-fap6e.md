# ENDS TODAY: 30% off your favorite Kohl's brands ⏳
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-08**
## Executive summary

- Forty-six. That's where I've lost count of Kohl's in my inbox since Memorial Day, and today they walk in with an hourglass emoji and "ENDS TODAY" — which I know is true because yesterday's email told me I had two days. The countdown is real. My willingness to care is not.
- The email is visually fine. Clean mosaic hero, the 30% number lands clearly, Sonoma and Apt.9 are named so I know exactly which brands are included. But then my eye slides straight to the red CLEARANCE banner at the bottom screaming 70% off, and suddenly I'm confused about what this email is actually about. Two promotions, one send, zero commitment to a single idea. Macy's pulls this same trick and I find it just as annoying there. You've got one hero spot — use it.
- What really stings is "Styles that say designer. Prices that say otherwise." I shop Nordstrom and Saks specifically *because* I want the actual designer. This tagline is winking at me about the gap instead of making me feel good about the choice. It's not aspirational — it's apologetic. No click, no forward, straight to marked-read.

## What's working

- **30% number is immediately visible in the hero** — the offer amount isn't buried, I see it before I scroll.
- **Brands are called out explicitly** — APT.9, TEK GEAR, SONOMA logos tell me exactly what's included, which is better than "select styles."
- **Visual hierarchy holds up** — ENDS TODAY → 30% → Shop Now flows without fighting itself.
- **Category grid is organized** — Women/Men/Kids/Home with image panels; nothing broke on my phone render.
- **Clean email render** — no broken images, no overlapping type, no layout collapse.

## What's weak

- **Dual-offer confusion** — a 30% hero and a 70% CLEARANCE banner are competing for my attention. I don't know which one to act on.
- **No Kohl's Cash anywhere above the fold** — I will not spend money at Kohl's without stacking the Cash, and it's invisible in this email. That's the actual reason I'd click.
- **"Styles that say designer"** — this line showed up in at least two prior sends this cycle. It reads as a verbal shrug, not a headline.
- **Zero beauty content** — I'm a beauty counter regular. Sephora at Kohl's exists. It is completely absent from this email. Sephora's own July 1st send gave me a specific offer with product imagery; Kohl's doesn't even acknowledge the category.
- **Urgency fatigue is real** — ENDS TODAY follows LAST DAY which followed HOURS LEFT. The hourglass emoji doesn't fix what 46 consecutive promo emails have broken.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a beauty module** — even a two-product block from the Sephora at Kohl's shop would tell me this email knows who I am. That single change lifts click likelihood more than any copy tweak.
- 2. **Kill one of the two offers** — commit to the 30% Kohl's brands story OR lean into clearance 70%. Two lead offers split the message and the click.
- 3. **Put Kohl's Cash in the hero** — "Earn Kohl's Cash" is the loyalty mechanic I respond to. If it's active today, that's the reason to act now, and it's invisible.
- 4. **Retire the "styles that say designer" line** — it's appeared multiple times this summer and it subtly apologizes for the brand every time it runs.
- 5. **Impose a rest cadence** — one week between "ENDS TODAY" subject lines, minimum. The next one will actually land.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off Sonoma, Apt.9 + earn Kohl's Cash — ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Kohl's brands are 30% off — plus beauty picks inside`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sonoma, Apt.9, Tek Gear — stack the deal with Kohl's Cash before midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `30% off house brands + clearance up to 70% — one day to grab both.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "ENDS TODAY: 30% off" (2026-07-08)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email uses a multi-ESP stack (CheetahMail/CHP, MessageMedia/mi.kohls.com, Demdex/Adobe Audience Manager) with standard table-based layout. The primary technical concerns are missing alt attributes on all tracking pixels, unverifiable compliance headers through the AgentMail relay, and a malformed custom HTML tag in the open-tracking block.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 click-tracking links skipped by QA prober** — all routed through `click.chp.kohls.com` and `click.s.kohls.com`. Destination URLs could not be validated; broken destinations would go undetected.

**Three independent open/impression pixels fired on load:**
- `click.chp.kohls.com/o/2fe131f8...` (CheetahMail open pixel)
- `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbb...o.gif` (MessageMedia open pixel)
- `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?5ZZAS35U...` (secondary ESP open pixel)

Triple-counting opens across systems will inflate reported open rates and make attribution unreliable across platforms.

**Malformed tag in open-tracking block:**
```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt="">
<custom>   ← unclosed, non-standard tag
```
`</custom>` closes a tag that was never opened with a matching element. Some parsers will insert repair nodes; behavior in Outlook is undefined.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**11 tracking/pixel images missing `alt` text** (confirmed by QA). All are 1×1 pixels or decorative tracking assets — they should carry `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"` to be screen-reader neutral. Currently only the `o.gif` pixel has `aria-hidden="true"`.

Affected assets (sample):
- `2fe131f8-ad78-4345-92fe-4ce436942f45` (open pixel)
- `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png` (content renders from mi.kohls.com)
- `916d6fa08d185233.png` — URL contains unfilled merge token fragment: `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_pen` (value is empty — see §4)

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — disables pinch-to-zoom on mobile, a WCAG 1.4.4 violation. Modern mobile email clients may ignore it, but it is still a risk for recipients with low vision.

**Empty `<title>` tag** — `<title></title>` provides no document label; screen readers announce the email as untitled.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Unfilled token in image URL:**
```
mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_pen
```
Found in `src` of `916d6fa08d185233.png`. The `mi_kc_redm_end_dte` parameter has an empty value, suggesting a merge token that evaluated to null. If this controls a promotional end-date render, the image may be serving incorrect or fallback content silently.

No other visible merge token failures in the truncated source, but 22 unprobed click links may also carry unfilled parameters.

---

### 5. Compliance

| Check | Status | Evidence |

| List-Unsubscribe header | WARN | Not captured by AgentMail relay; cannot confirm presence |
| List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058 one-click) | WARN | Not captured; one-click unsubscribe required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender policy |
| Physical mailing address | WARN | Not detected in body; CAN-SPAM §7 requires a valid postal address |
| SPF/DKIM/DMARC | WARN | `Authentication-Results` not available via relay; deliverability posture unverifiable |
| `robots` meta | Pass | `noindex, nofollow` present — correct for email |

The three WARN compliance items are relay-artifact ambiguities — they may exist in actual SMTP headers but cannot be confirmed from this capture. The physical address absence in the HTML body is a harder flag; CAN-SPAM requires it to be in the message itself, not only in headers.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

UTM parameters cannot be verified because all 22 destination URLs are behind click-redirect wrappers (`click.chp.kohls.com`) that the QA prober skipped. No UTM values are visible in the HTML source itself.

The `&email_name=260708_DG_Email_Wednesda` parameter appears on several `mi.kohls.com` image URLs — this is the internal campaign identifier and appears correctly set, though it is truncated in the QA log (likely truncated at capture, not in the actual URL).

**Gap:** Without probing click-redirect destinations, there is no confirmation that UTM params (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) are being passed through to kohls.com landing pages.
