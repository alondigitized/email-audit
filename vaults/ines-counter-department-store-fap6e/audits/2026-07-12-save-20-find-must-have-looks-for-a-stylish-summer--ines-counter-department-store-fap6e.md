# Save 20%  | Find must-have looks for a stylish summer ☀️
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-12**
## Executive summary

- Fifty-one. That is my running count of Kohl's emails since Memorial Day, and this one arrives dressed in a green striped sundress and a coupon I've already seen four days in a row. I opened it — muscle memory — but I knew before the hero loaded exactly what was inside: 20% off, a summer dress, "Shop Now," and a barcode I'm supposed to screenshot in the store.
- The execution is competent. The layout is clean. The Kohl's Cash callout at the top is the single thing that genuinely speaks to me, because I do not shop without points back. But there's no beauty content anywhere in this scroll — not a serum, not a lip kit, not even a mention of the fragrance section that occasionally brings me to the site. And the home goods block stitched below the fashion feels like the email was assembled from a template checklist, not a point of view.
- I'd mark it read. Not delete, not forward. Sephora earned a genuine click from me this week with a Patrick Starr hero and a subject that sounded like a friend's text. This email sounds like a flyer.

## What's working

- **Kohl's Cash is above the fold and immediate.** "Plus, earn Kohl's Cash!" lands right under the 20% banner. I refuse to use a card without points back — this is the one signal that earns my attention.
- **The offer math is done for me.** "$19.99, Reg. $29.99–$39.99, after 20% off with coupon" on the dress hero is the right level of transparency. I don't have to open a calculator.
- **Zero render issues.** Everything loaded clean — no broken images, no overlapping type, no empty blocks. After years of Macy's emails collapsing on mobile, I notice when a layout just works.
- **Summer timing is correct.** Striped dresses, patio decor, outdoor toys — this is a July email. The editorial is at least seasonally honest.

## What's weak

- **No beauty content, anywhere.** I'm in this subscriber list partly because of Kohl's fragrance and skincare deals. This scroll is fashion and home, start to finish. Sephora put a single Patrick Starr product front-and-center last week and it felt personal. Kohl's doesn't even try to differentiate by category.
- **Fourth consecutive 20% off.** Yesterday was "Extra 20% off savings are waiting." Today is "Save 20% | Find must-have looks." Swapping the subject doesn't reset my memory. The mechanic is stale.
- **"Shop Now" three times in the body, once in the header.** That's not hierarchy — it's a lack of editorial courage. Tell me what to shop specifically.
- **The home block dilutes the fashion story.** Bedding, Bath, Kitchen & Dining, Patio, Decor, Outdoor Toys — crammed into a grid below the dress editorial. It reads like a catalog. Neiman Marcus, even when they pivoted to wedding editorial last week, had a single throughline. This email has three.
- **Price point signals a different tier.** When I'm cross-shopping Nordstrom and Saks in the same inbox session, a $19.99 dress hero codes as a different league. Kohl's should lean into the value + loyalty story more aggressively rather than just displaying the number.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Separate fashion and home into distinct sends.** The home grid below the dress editorial is the single biggest drag on focus. Macy's catches heat from me for exactly this pattern — kitchen goods below the beauty counter. Pick a lane per send.
- 2. **Add one beauty or personal care spotlight.** Even a single "your beauty picks" block — dry shampoo, a fragrance, a skincare staple — would retain subscribers like me who are in the list for that reason.
- 3. **Rotate the coupon mechanic.** "Double Kohl's Cash" or "member-exclusive first access to new arrivals" gives me the same savings signal without triggering the déjà vu that four identical 20% offers create.
- 4. **Rename the CTAs.** "Shop Summer Dresses" beats "Shop Now." "Build Your Outdoor Setup" beats "Shop Now." The specificity costs nothing.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your Kohl's Cash is waiting — plus 20% off summer styles`
- **Subject Alt B:** `20% off summer dresses from $19 — and you're earning Cash`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Green stripes, easy prices, and your coupon's already clipped.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Member rewards + 20% off — stack them before the season peaks.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "Save 20% | Find must-have looks for a stylish summer"

### 1. Technical Summary

Email uses a standard table-based HTML 4.01 layout with MSO conditional comments and Google Fonts. Several compliance gaps and accessibility deficiencies are present; no blocking rendering bugs detected in the visible HTML structure.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **22 tracking/click-redirect URLs skipped by HTTP probe.** All CTAs route through `click.s.kohls.com` and `click.chp.kohls.com` — destination URLs and HTTP status codes unverified. Any broken redirect would be invisible to the QA scanner.
- **Three distinct pixel/tracking origins in use simultaneously:** `click.chp.kohls.com` (CoherentPath), `mi.kohls.com` (Marigold/Cheetah), `click.s.kohls.com` (Salesforce Marketing Cloud), and `kohls.demdex.net` (Adobe Audience Manager). Four separate vendor systems firing on open — increases load time and cross-vendor identity reconciliation complexity.
- **Malformed HTML tag:** `</custom>` closing tag present in the pixel block (`<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..."...></custom>`). This is invalid HTML and may confuse parsers in older clients.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **11 images missing `alt` text**, including content images served from `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/`. Tracking pixels omitting `alt=""` is a known screen-reader noise issue; content images omitting `alt` is an accessibility failure (WCAG 2.1 SC 1.1.1).
  - Affected: `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`, plus three tracking pixels.
- **`<title>` tag is empty.** Some clients (notably Gmail on Android) surface the `<title>` as preview text fallback. An empty title degrades the sender experience in those contexts.
- **`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta.** Prevents pinch-to-zoom on mobile — accessibility violation (WCAG 2.1 SC 1.4.4). Should be removed or set to `user-scalable=yes`.
- **`<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer" />`** is present. This suppresses referrer data when recipients click links, which conflicts with UTM attribution (see §6).
- **Google Fonts loaded three times** — once via `<link>` for Roboto, once via `<link>` for Montserrat, and again via `@import` for both inside a `<style>` block. Duplicate requests add latency; `@import` inside `<style>` is also blocked by MSO conditional wrapping, potentially causing font fallback inconsistency.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- No unrendered merge tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `%%first_name%%`) detected in the truncated source — personalization appears resolved at send time or is absent.
- The `mi_u=604230016` parameter across multiple Marigold pixel URLs is a static user ID — consistent and expected for a single recipient capture, not a sign of token failure.

---

### 5. Compliance

- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not detected** by the AgentMail relay. This is required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk sender policies (enforced Feb 2024) for senders above 5k/day. If the header is present at the MTA level but stripped by the relay, this needs relay-level verification; if genuinely absent, it is a deliverability risk.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header not detected.** One-click unsubscribe (required by the same Gmail/Yahoo policy) may not be supported. Absence means recipients must navigate a separate unsubscribe flow — non-compliant with current bulk sender requirements.
- **No physical mailing address detected in the HTML source.** CAN-SPAM §7 requires a valid physical postal address in every commercial email. It may be present below the truncation point, but QA did not detect it — requires manual verification of full source.
- **SPF/DKIM authentication status unknown.** `Authentication-Results` header not captured by relay. Cannot confirm `s.kohls.com` sending domain passes DMARC alignment. This must be verified at the MTA/relay level before any deliverability conclusions can be drawn.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

- **`<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer" />` suppresses HTTP referrer headers** on click-through. Combined with click-redirect wrapping, landing pages will see the click domain (e.g., `click.s.kohls.com`) as referrer rather than the email client — expected behavior given redirects, but the `no-referrer` policy also prevents any referrer reaching downstream pages after the redirect resolves.
- **UTM parameter presence on destination URLs cannot be confirmed** because all 22 CTA links are click-redirect wrappers and the HTTP probe was skipped. If UTM params are not appended at redirect resolution, GA4/analytics attribution will break. This is the highest-priority item to verify manually.
- **`email_name=260712_DG_Email_Sunday`** is present on several `mi.kohls.com` image pixels — confirms campaign tagging at the Marigold layer. Consistent across all tagged images reviewed.
