# Select brands are 50% off 🤑
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-29**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "50% off Select Beauty — TODAY ONLY" up top actually got me for half a second, but then it's the same three brands doing the rounds — Too Faced, Drunk Elephant, MAC — and "exclusions/terms apply" right there next to the badge tells me this isn't the sitewide event it's dressed up as. Below that it's the usual Sephora scroll-fest: Real Luxury, Next-Level Skincare, Feel-Good Fragrance, Hype-Worthy Hair Care — YSL, Caudalie, Jo Malone, Kérastase, all lovely brands, none of them discounted, none of them "select beauty." So I've got a 50%-off headline sitting on top of five sections of full-price prestige merchandise with zero connective tissue between them.
- Compare that to Neiman Marcus's "up to $250 off" reminder from 8/26 — at least NM's tiered math box, annoying as it is, applies to the whole order. Here the offer applies to a slice I have to go find, while the rest of the email quietly sells me full-price YSL and Jo Malone like the 50% banner never happened. And this is Sephora's second "50% off select beauty" swing in two days (8/28 scored a 6 from me for the same "0 points" nagging) — so the cadence is getting repetitive even before I get to the content problem.
- I'd scroll past this one. Not deleting it outright — Beauty Insider muscle memory keeps me opening these — but I'm not clicking through five sections to hunt for what's actually on sale, and I'm not forwarding it to anyone.

## What's working

- The hot-pink "50% off Select Beauty" hero at the very top is legible and the discount is impossible to miss for the first three seconds.
- "TODAY ONLY" badge creates real urgency, more credible than Kohl's stale "Ends August 30" line that sat there unchanged for ten straight days.
- Product photography is genuinely elegant — the YSL blush compact, the Kérastase amber bottles, the Caudalie red backdrop all look like a real prestige catalog, not a clip-art grid.
- Klarna/Afterpay/PayPal payment tile is useful and honest — no hidden fee language, just clear installment options.

## What's weak

- The 50% off hero has zero visual connection to anything below it — no "shop the sale" section, no bordered callout, nothing marking which of these five product stories are actually discounted.
- "Exclusions/terms apply. While supplies last." sits directly under the headline offer, which is exactly the buried-fine-print move that dinged Macy's "confirm now for 25% off" email last week.
- No Beauty Insider points balance or tier callout anywhere — after five straight sends (8/16 through 8/28) hammering "0 points," at least this one skips the insult, but it also skips any loyalty benefit tie-in to the sale itself, which feels like a missed layup given how hard Sephora usually leans on Insider status.
- Six distinct product stories in one email is Macy's-style overload territory — YSL twice, Caudalie, U Beauty, Jo Malone, Kérastase, Oribe, rhode — I'm a beauty-counter regular and even I'm fatigued by section four.
- The "New in Skincare" and "New from rhode" blocks at the bottom feel bolted on, unrelated to the 50% hook that got me to open in the first place.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the discounted brands and products directly under the hero banner — show me Too Faced, Drunk Elephant, and MAC product tiles with strikethrough pricing immediately, don't make me guess which of the six sections below is on sale.
- 2. Cut the email from six product stories to three. Real Luxury and Next-Level Skincare can carry the send; Fragrance, Hair Care, and rhode can wait for a separate email.
- 3. Move "exclusions apply" fine print to the footer, not directly under the 50% badge — right now it reads like Sephora's undercutting its own offer before I've even scrolled.
- 4. Tie a Beauty Insider angle to the sale specifically ("Rouge members: stack this with your birthday gift") instead of leaving loyalty status entirely out of an otherwise loyalty-obsessed brand's email.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Today only: 50% off Too Faced, Drunk Elephant & MAC`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your half-off pass expires tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `See exactly what's included before it's gone`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Insider exclusive: 50% off, today only`
