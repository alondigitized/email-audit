# Save an extra 20% ... it's time to shop 🛍️
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- Oh, it's Kohl's again — sixth time this week in my inbox, and this one's basically every previous send mashed into one scroll: the navy-circle 20%, the red "ULTIMATE Clearance Event 70%," the Active Sale activewear block, home goods, Haggar menswear, a mattress topper. There's no organizing idea here at all, which is actually a step backward from yesterday's Active Sale email that at least picked a lane and stayed in it.
- My eye does land on "Status: KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER" up top, which I'll give them credit for — that's the one line in this whole email that acknowledges I'm not a stranger. But then it's immediately buried under five different sale banners competing for my attention, and "Earn 3% REWARDS" reads like an afterthought stapled next to it rather than a real member benefit callout.
- This isn't a click for me, and it's definitely not a forward. Sephora's 4X-points email last week told me exactly what I get and exactly what qualifies in one clean shot — this is the opposite of that, a wall of coupons and unrelated categories that I'll scroll past and archive.

## What's working

- The "KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER" status line at the very top is a real personalization touch, even if it gets lost fast.
- The 20% off coupon code and expiration date (Ends Aug. 16) are both visible without me having to hunt.
- No render bugs — everything's crisp, images loaded, text is legible.
- "Picked with you in mind" section header is a nice gesture toward relevance, even though a slushie maker and a mattress topper aren't actually picked with a 47-year-old beauty-counter shopper in mind.

## What's weak

- Zero beauty, skincare, or fragrance content anywhere in this email — for someone who lives at the counter, that's a complete miss, and it's the same miss I flagged in every Kohl's send this week.
- The layout is a straight-up grab-bag: activewear, kids' clothes, menswear, bedding, kitchen appliances, jeans — nothing here signals "we know what you buy," it signals "we're clearing a warehouse."
- The models skew younger and more athleisure/family-focused (the Nike/Under Armour guy, the family-of-four shot) — nothing reads Gen X, nothing reads department-store beauty regular.
- "Coupons do not apply to select brands" shows up three separate times — that's the exclusionary fine print that makes an offer feel less honest, and I'm now conditioned to expect it every time Kohl's promises a percentage off.
- This is the same navy/red/cream color-block toolkit I called out on 8/3, 8/4, and 8/5 — nothing here differentiates it from the last five sends other than which SKUs got dropped in.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick ONE hero offer and cut everything else below the fold — Sephora's 4X-points send worked because the offer WAS the email; Kohl's needs to stop trying to be five emails at once.
- 2. If beauty/personal care exists anywhere in Kohl's assortment (Sephora-at-Kohl's counters, for instance), lead with that for shoppers like me instead of activewear and Haggar suits — right now I'm getting the same generic blast as every other customer.
- 3. Move "Earn 3% REWARDS" and my member status into an actual benefit box with a number I can picture (dollars earned, tier progress) — right now it's a throwaway line at the top.
- 4. Drop or consolidate the "coupons do not apply to select brands" caveat into one visible spot instead of repeating it three times — repetition makes it feel like a trap, not a policy.
- 5. Retire the navy/red clearance color-block for at least one send — I've now seen it daily since 8/3 and it's stopped registering as urgent.

### 1. Technical Summary
Email is built on the Cheetah Digital / CoherentPath ESP stack (`chp.kohls.com`, `storage.googleapis.com/coherentpath-contentlibrary`) with standard MSO/Outlook conditional markup; automated QA returned a 43% pass rate driven mostly by missing alt attributes and unverifiable compliance headers rather than rendering defects.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Open-tracking pixel via `click.chp.kohls.com/o/bb147fb0-...?mi_mid=019fd6ba-e380-7000-b928-dd95af783328&cp_tp=v3.eJyF...` (base64/zlib-encoded `cp_tp` payload).
- Secondary open pixel: `https://mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1019194_202686`.
- Third open-tracking beacon: `https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?XXCAHSWAKOOEZDPNO55JYWRUJI.60263&d=60263&bmt=0` — **malformed markup**: the `<img>` tag is immediately followed by a stray closing `</custom>` tag with no matching opening tag (`... alt=""></custom> </div>`). This is invalid HTML and should be removed.
- Adobe Audience Manager beacon: `https://kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`.
- QA flagged 68 tracking/click-redirect links as skipped from HTTP probing (redirect domains not resolved), so final destination URLs and status codes for CTA links could not be verified in this pass.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- Proper MSO conditional block for Outlook (`<!--[if gte mso 9]>`) and non-MSO font loading (`<!--[if !mso]><!-->` guarded Google Fonts `<link>`/`@import`), plus a 500px mobile media query — no structural rendering issues found.
- 9 images flagged by QA as missing `alt` attributes, all tracking/measurement pixels (`bb147fb0-...`, `o.gif`, `event` (demdex), and six `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` re-targeting pixels). Some sibling tracking pixels in the same document do carry `alt=""` (e.g., the `o.gif` beacon at `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...`), so the fix is simply adding empty `alt=""` consistently to the remaining pixels for accessibility-scanner compliance.
- The stray `</custom>` tag noted above is also an accessibility/parsing risk since it can affect how assistive-tech DOM parsers interpret surrounding elements.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- `Authentication-Results` header not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status could not be verified via the relay; recommend checking headers at the receiving MTA directly.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
