# Things We Love
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-07**
## Executive summary

- Pretty, but this is the seventh Neiman's email I've had since Thursday, and it has no offer in it at all. It's a magazine page: velvet dress, Nikos Koulis ring, a men's sweater, a grid of accessories. I enjoyed the scroll, but nothing here tells me why I should open my wallet today. The Beauty Event email with Triple Points and the train case on Nov 6 gave me a reason to act. This one gives me a mood.
- Nothing is wrong with the craft. The hero is gorgeous and the type is restrained. But I just got "Good news—these popular styles from Balenciaga & more are now up to 40% off" and the "Ends today: Earn a $50–$500 gift card" send earlier today. After those, a pure editorial send feels like filler. I'd skim it, maybe tap the dress, and move on. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The hero model in the black velvet dress is a real holiday-party image. It reads as grown-up, not costume-y.
- The restrained layout respects me. Sephora's 30% banner and Kohl's Cash stacking shout, and this doesn't.
- The designer labels under the accessory grid (Saint Laurent, Ramy Brook, Gianvito Rossi, Jade Ruzzo, Ami Paris, Toteme) tell me this is designer access, and that's what I expect from Neiman's.
- The "Match with a personal style advisor" block at the bottom is a nice touch, and the closest thing to service in the email.
- The "Portrait of a Lady" section with feminine fabrics and the burgundy bag feels aimed at me more than the men's sweater does.

## What's weak

- There's no offer, no points, no event and no deadline. After today's gift-card send, I'm asking where the reward is.
- No loyalty or InCircle signal anywhere. I won't use a card without points back, and Macy's at least puts a member-benefit line in its sends.
- The CTAs are small underlined text links ("Shop Now", "Shop Contemporary") with no buttons. They're hard to tap on a phone, and "Shop Now" doesn't tell me what I'm shopping.
- The accessory grid has no prices and nothing is linked to a specific product, so the "brand new styles" are just tiny images with tiny captions.
- The cadence is wearing me out. It's another send within about 36 hours, and I can't tell it apart from the "Fashion Finds: Refined Knits" send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a member line near the top, like "InCircle members: double points on new arrivals through Sunday." That one change would lift this the most, since it gives the pretty pictures a reason to click.
- 2. Turn the text links into real buttons with specific copy, like "Shop the velvet dress."
- 3. Add prices or a "from $" to the accessory grid and link each item to its product page.
- 4. Cut the frequency. Suppress this send for anyone who got the sale and gift-card emails the same day.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Holiday party dressing, edited by our stylists`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Earn points on every new arrival this weekend.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `A personal stylist can pull your look. Book free.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible markup is a table-based, XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with a media-query mobile layer. The truncated source shows an internal Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) template comment shipped in production. The QA run found 0 hard failures. Its 4 warnings are mostly unverifiable header checks, plus a plain-text part that is almost entirely URLs.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed tracking links:** 18 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so their destinations and redirect health are unverified.
- **URL-heavy plain-text part:** The QA check reports 3386 of 3896 plain-text characters are URLs, about 87% against a 70% threshold. Plain-text links are probably long tracking URLs, which hurts text-only readability and can add spam-filter risk.
- **Not assessable:** The HTML is truncated before the body, so individual `href` values, alt-wrapped links and broken-link status can't be checked. No broken links were flagged by QA.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` sets `maximum-scale=1`, which blocks pinch-zoom on some mobile clients. This is an accessibility problem (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Comment before DOCTYPE:** The SFMC variable comment sits before `<!DOCTYPE>`. Some clients and parsers can switch to quirks mode when anything precedes the doctype.
- **Exact-match class selectors:** Mobile rules use `*[class=contenttable]`, `*[class=hide]` and similar. An exact attribute match fails silently if an element carries more than one class, so those responsive rules may not apply.
- **Checkbox-hack nav:** The `.navbar-checkbox:checked~.menu` hamburger depends on CSS checkbox state. Clients that strip `<input>` or `:checked` (Gmail, some Outlook builds) won't show the menu, and the fallback isn't visible in the truncated source.
- **Minor:** `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` has a trailing space and is non-descriptive.
- **Not assessable:** Image `alt` text, `role="presentation"` on layout tables and the preheader are past the truncation point.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Internal template leak:** The top-of-source comment is delivered to recipients. It exposes internal SFMC data-extension names and lookup logic: `NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`, `@uEm`, `@cmdid`, `@emailid`, and `Lookup(... "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email)`. It also leaves authoring instructions in the output: "CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW" and `<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->`.
- **No broken tokens in the visible HTML:** No unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `@var` merge tokens appear in rendered content in the visible portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** The header wasn't found. The QA note says AgentMail may not capture it, so this is **unconfirmed** rather than a verified defect.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Same caveat. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so this needs checking against raw headers.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not present via the relay. SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `e.neimanmarcus.com` can't be assessed from this capture.
- **Footer content:** The postal address and unsubscribe link are not in the truncated portion, so they are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not assessable:** No destination URLs, UTM parameters or `refid` values are visible in the truncated source.
- **Redirect-wrapped links:** The 18 skipped links are click-redirect wrapped, so their final landing URLs and any UTM or `@refid` carry-through weren't validated. The `@refid`/`@base_refid` variables named in the comment suggest campaign tagging is done through `refid` rather than UTM, but that's inference, not confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Strip the SFMC authoring comment** (the `REQUIRED variables…` block) from the production template, or have the build/send step remove HTML comments. It exposes internal DE names and lookup logic.
2. **Move the DOCTYPE to the first line** of the output.
3. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta to restore pinch-zoom.
4. **Verify raw headers** from a direct seed-inbox send rather than the relay. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass and align.
5. **Hand-craft the plain-text part** (or trim tracking parameters from it) so it isn't about 87% URLs.
6. **Re-run the audit with untruncated HTML** and resolve the 18 redirect links to check landing pages, `refid`/UTM values, alt text and footer compliance elements.
7. **Harden the responsive selectors:** switch `*[class=…]` to `[class~=…]` or `.class`, and add a non-checkbox navigation fallback for clients that strip `:checked`.
