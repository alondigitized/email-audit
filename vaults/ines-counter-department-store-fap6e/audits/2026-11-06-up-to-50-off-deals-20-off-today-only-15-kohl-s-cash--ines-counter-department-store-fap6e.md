# Up to 50% off deals + 20% off + TODAY ONLY $15 Kohl’s Cash 🥳
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again. It's the seventh one this week, by my count. The subject stacks up to 50% off, 20% off, and $15 Kohl's Cash, with a party-face emoji on top. When I open it I get a very long scroll of product tiles. The hero is clear: Early Arrival Black Friday Deals, a big 50%, a big 20%, and a $15 Kohl's Cash badge. After that it becomes a flea market. I count roughly 25 tiles, and I'd guess about 10 of them carry fine print that says "Coupons do not apply."
- I shop at the beauty counter, and this isn't built for me. The Sephora at Kohl's tile and the Sephora Savings Event banner are buried about a third of the way down. Sephora's own email from today ("Use your 0 points on a 250-point bundle") at least led with beauty. I wouldn't forward this to anyone. I'd skim the hero, maybe check the Sephora tile, and mark the rest read. The screenshot is shrunk, so I can't read the small print in the tiles.

## What's working

- The hero is easy to read. The big 50% and 20% numbers and the Kohl's Cash badge tell me the deal in one glance.
- The category buttons right under the hero (Women, Men, Beauty, Home and so on) let me skip straight to my section.
- Products are shown with real prices, such as the $11.99 and $14.99 tiles. I can see what I'd pay.
- The "Shop Daily Deals" block adds some structure. It breaks up the grid so the whole thing doesn't read as one wall.
- The Sephora at Kohl's tile with the eyeshadow palette is in my category. It was the only thing I stopped on.

## What's weak

- It's far too long. Macy's buries things too, but this is a 12,000-pixel scroll. Neiman Marcus ran a much quieter "Your top styles could now be up to 40% off." That one respected my time.
- "Coupons do not apply" shows up on tile after tile. I read that as the 20% not being real. The honesty of the offer suffers.
- Beauty is an afterthought. Sephora counts on me noticing a points bundle. Kohl's leaves its Sephora section far down the page with no Rewards or tier message.
- "TODAY ONLY" is carrying a lot of weight. On 11/05 the subject was "20% off won't stick around forever," and earlier today it was "TODAY ONLY earn $15 Kohl's Cash." That's two TODAY ONLY claims in one day, so the urgency isn't credible.
- Some tiles are crowded with badges, and the Gen X shopper in me notices the "Style Square" and "Cozy Corner" headers feel like a catalog, not a curated offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut it to one hero offer, one beauty row, and one "see all" button. Fifteen tiles beat twenty-five.
- 2. Put the Sephora section in the top third for anyone who has bought beauty. Show the Kohl's Cash earn rule right on that tile.
- 3. Put the "coupons do not apply" exclusions next to the headline offer, not at the bottom of each tile.
- 4. Send one "TODAY ONLY" per week, not per day.
- **Subject Alt A:** `$15 Kohl's Cash today, plus 20% off your order`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Beauty + Sephora picks, earn $15 Kohl's Cash today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack your 20% coupon with Kohl's Cash. Exclusions listed up top.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sephora at Kohl's, daily deals, and what's ending tonight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Movable Ink and Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) build with four tracking pixels and 91 click-redirect links. Its HTML has several markup defects, and its content images lack alt text. The compliance and authentication warnings can't be confirmed from the data given (see section 5). Automated QA reports a 38% pass rate with 0 errors and 5 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverifiable links:** 91 links were skipped because they use click-redirect domains (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=...`). The `qs` payloads are opaque, so destination, redirect chain and link health can't be confirmed.
- **Stacked tracking pixels:** There are four before any content:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/bd3e8d7e-...`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=...`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager, third-party)
- **User identifier in URLs:** `mi_u=604230016` appears in query strings on `mi.kohls.com` image URLs. Forwarded or shared copies of the email could expose it.
- **Deliverability:** The plain-text part is 16,076 of 21,872 characters URLs (about 73%). That exceeds the 70% threshold and is a spam-filter risk.
- **Pixel fragility:** The `click.chp.kohls.com` and `demdex` pixels have no `alt` attribute. Only the `open.aspx` and `o.gif` pixels carry `alt=""`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on content images:** At least seven `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` images have no alt text (`2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`). The list is truncated, so there may be more. These look like content or promo images, so the offers are invisible to screen readers and to clients that block images.
- **QA false positive:** The `o.gif` flag is wrong. The source shows `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, which is correct for a decorative pixel.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Malformed markup:**
  - A stray `</custom>` closing tag follows the `open.aspx` pixel `<img>`.
  - `<title></title>` is empty.
  - `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href.
  - A `<div itemscope ...>` containing `<meta>` tags sits inside `<head>`, which is invalid and gets hoisted into `<body>` by parsers.
- **Missing table roles:** The pixel layout tables (`width="1" height="1"`) have no `role="presentation"`. The main wrapper tables do.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unconfirmed warnings:** `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results` were not found. QA attributes these to the AgentMail relay, so the sender's real header state is unknown. Don't treat them as confirmed defects.
- **Physical address:** QA found none, but the HTML source is truncated before the footer, so it can't be confirmed. Kohl's typically includes one.
- **Sender:** `kohls@s.kohls.com` is a consistent first-party subdomain.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters visible:** Attribution runs through SFMC `qs=` tokens and Movable Ink params (`mi_cid`, `mi_mid`, `mi_ecmp=1025351_2026116`, `email_name=261106_DG_Email_Fri_Mid`).
- **Landing pages unverified:** The tracked links weren't probed and the HTML is truncated, so landing-page alignment can't be assessed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` content image. Keep `alt=""` on the decorative pixels, and add it to the `click.chp.kohls.com` and `demdex` pixels too.
2. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Fix the markup: delete the stray `</custom>`, populate or remove `<title>` and the empty `<link href="">`, and move the `itemscope` block out of `<head>`.
4. Consolidate the four open and tracking pixels, and review whether the third-party `demdex.net` call is needed in the email body.
5. Shorten tracked URLs or add more plain-text copy to bring URLs under 70% of the plain-text part.
6. Re-run the audit against the raw message with original headers (not the AgentMail relay). That will settle List-Unsubscribe, one-click unsubscribe and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Also capture the full HTML so the footer address and unsubscribe link can be checked.
