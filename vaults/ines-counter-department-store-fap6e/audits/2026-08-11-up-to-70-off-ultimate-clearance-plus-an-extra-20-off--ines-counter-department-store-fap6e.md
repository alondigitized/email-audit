# Up to 70% off ULTIMATE clearance 😉 Plus, an extra 20% off.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-11**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again — that's eight, nine of these now this week, and this one at least has the decency to open with something other than the same "20% off + Kohl's Cash" banner I've seen on repeat. This time it's a genuine lingerie/intimates sale — Jockey, Fruit of the Loom, Vanity Fair, Warners — stacked with a "Semi-Annual, only twice a year" framing up top, which is the first bit of real urgency language I've seen from them all week instead of the manufactured kind.
- But then it just... keeps going. After four bra brands, we pivot hard into a "Ultimate Clearance Event" banner with a Ninja frozen drink maker, bath towels, a mattress topper, and Gloria Vanderbilt jeans, all under "picked with you in mind" — which is laughable when the mix is a blender, a towel, and a mattress pad. That's not personalization, that's just "everything in the store." Nordstrom would never slap "picked with you in mind" on a random assortment like that; Saks wouldn't either — they'd at least segment.
- Would I click? Maybe on the Vanity Fair or Warners bra, since those are real brands I trust at the counter. Would I forward it? No — this is landfill-tier Kohl's email volume at this point, and my patience from Tuesday's "number eight this week" complaint hasn't recovered.

## What's working

- The "Semi-Annual, only twice a year" framing is the first credible urgency hook I've seen from Kohl's this week, not manufactured scarcity.
- Real brand names I recognize and trust — Jockey, Vanity Fair, Warners — carry more weight than another generic "20% off everything" blast.
- Status bar up top ("KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER," "Earn: 5% REWARDS") is consistent with every other Kohl's send this week, so at least that's stable.
- Individual product cards are clean — price, brand, coupon stack, single CTA — easy to scan on my phone.

## What's weak

- The email tries to be two emails: a lingerie sale and a clearance/home-goods blowout. Neither gets the space it deserves and my eye doesn't know where to land.
- "Picked with you in mind (Your great taste makes it easy)" over a Ninja Slushi maker, bath towels, and a mattress topper is flatly untrue — nothing here reads as curated for a 47-year-old shopping bras.
- The nav bar (Home/Women/Men/Kids) repeats twice, which just feels like padding this email out further.
- Fine print at the bottom is a wall of gray text I'd never read on my phone — Sephora's insider emails at least keep that contained to one line.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this into two sends — one for the Semi-Annual intimates event, one for clearance home goods. Trying to do both in one email is why the "picked for you" line falls apart.
- 2. Cut the duplicate nav bar — one set of category links is plenty.
- 3. Lead with the intimates sale in the subject line since that's the more targeted, higher-intent offer — clearance drink makers won't move me but a Warners bra restock might.
- 4. Trim the legal/fine-print block or collapse it — it's dead weight visually.
