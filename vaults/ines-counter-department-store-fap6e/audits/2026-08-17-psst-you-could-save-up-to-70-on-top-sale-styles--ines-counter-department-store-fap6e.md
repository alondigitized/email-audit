# Psst... you could save up to 70% on top sale styles
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-17**
## Executive summary

- There it is — that crimson block, back for a sixth time in one week. I opened this because "psst" in the subject line was at least trying to be a little coy, but the second I land on the page it's the same "EXTRA 25% OFF... LAST CALL" wall I've now seen on 8/13, 8/15 (twice), and 8/16. At this point I could draw this banner from memory. Below the fold it does settle down a bit — nine designer pieces in a clean grid, Balmain and Manolo Blahnik sitting right next to Cinq a Sept, which is a decent mixed-price ladder — but I'm scrolling past a wall of stacked red "SHOP" buttons to get there.
- Compare this to yesterday's "Pre-fall bags take shape" send, which I actually liked — two bags, cream background, headline that trusted me to read. This is the opposite instinct: shout the number, then bury actual product under it. Sephora's "0 points" emails are annoying in their own way, but at least they don't repeat themselves seven times a week like this.
- I won't click. I've already shopped this exact "up to 70%" sale twice this week from other Neiman's sends — nothing here tells me what's new since Tuesday. Marking read, not forwarding.

## What's working

- The product grid is genuinely good — Isabel Marant, Balmain, Stuart Weitzman, Manolo Blahnik, all logically merchandised by category (bags, then tops, then shoes).
- "Exclusions Apply" is stated plainly instead of buried in six-point gray text — I respect that.
- The fine print at the bottom spells out the math (25-70% off, no adjustments, ends 9/7/26 10PM CT) — that's more honest than most Last Call sends.
- "Match with a personal style advisor" is a nice touch for someone like me who actually uses the counter/personal shopping relationship at Neiman's.

## What's weak

- Same crimson "LAST CALL" hero as 8/13, both 8/15 sends, and 8/16 — this is the least differentiated version yet, just restacking "EXTRA 25%" on "UP TO 70%" again.
- Six stacked red "SHOP" CTA buttons at the bottom (Women's Clothing, Men's, Handbags, Shoes, Kids, Women's Dresses) is Macy's-level button overload, not Neiman's tone.
- No loyalty/InCircle mention anywhere — for a store that knows I'm a member, that's a missed touch given how present Sephora keeps my Insider balance.
- "Save on these designer styles before they're gone" is generic urgency copy I've read from this brand three times this week already.

## Recommendations

- 1. Retire the crimson Last Call banner for at least one cycle — yesterday's cream/quiet "Pre-fall bags" execution scored better with me and it's sitting right there as proof it works.
- 2. Lead with the product grid, not the banner — put Balmain and Manolo Blahnik above the fold instead of stacking discount type six times.
- 3. Cut the six CTA buttons down to two — "Shop Women's" and "Shop Men's" is plenty; the rest is button noise.
- 4. Surface InCircle points or member pricing on this specific sale — I'll open for that before I open for another "up to 70%."
