# Open for 40% off + earn Kohl's Cash 🥳
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-17**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again. This is the same "extra 40% off, ends Oct. 18" header I've seen all week, and today the "2 DAYS ONLY" banner is stacked three times in a row. By this point that reads as shouting. I counted the sends since 10/8 and stopped around eight. The 10/16 "Head-to-toe fall styles" email opened the same way.
- Under the header it's a catalog with a Kohl's logo on it. There's a shoe sale, a Haggar suit block, a Clearance 70% block, and then a grid of "Picked with you in mind." That grid is a Ninja Slushi machine, a bath towel, a mattress topper and khakis. None of that looks like anything I've bought. Nordstrom or Saks would at least pretend to know me.
- Would I click? Probably not. If I did, it would be the "Shop Now" in the top banner, because 40% off plus Kohl's Cash is real money. I wouldn't forward it. I'd skim the pretty boots and archive it.

## What's working

- The "Status: KOHL'S REWARDS' MEMBER | Earn: 5% REWARDS" strip at the very top is the one moment this treated me like a known customer. I notice that.
- The 40% off, the "Ends Oct. 18" deadline and the red "Shop Now" button are all in one block above the fold. The offer is clear and I don't have to hunt for it.
- The Season's Best Shoe Sale photo of the cowboy boots with wide-leg denim is a nice lifestyle shot. It looks current and a bit more grown-up than the rest of the email.
- The "Earn $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 spent" line is the loyalty math I care about. It also gives the dates for redeeming it.
- The footer says "Free returns through January 31, 2027." That kind of reassurance matters to me, since I return things.

## What's weak

- The "2 DAYS ONLY" text is repeated three times in the red box beside the 40% off. It's a visual stutter, and it's the kind of urgency that makes me trust the offer less.
- "Coupons do not apply to select brands" appears in the shoe block, the Haggar block and again below the product grid. I feel the exclusions before I've even picked a product. "Select styles" shows up everywhere too.
- The Haggar suit block ("The budget-friendly way to look like a million bucks") is a men's tailoring ad. A woman on a beauty-counter budget gets nothing from it. "Budget-friendly" in the headline also undercuts the whole thing.
- "Picked with you in mind" has an appliance, a towel and a mattress topper in it. Nothing in it is personal, and it doesn't match what I buy.
- The footer is a wall of tiny legal text. The barcode and PIN coupon blocks are fine, but the fine print under them is unreadable on a phone without zooming.
- There's no beauty, designer or Sephora-at-Kohl's presence at all. The footer even says Sephora is excluded from the Kohl's Cash earning.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pick one story and cut the repeated countdown.** Show "2 DAYS ONLY" once. Put one lead offer first, either the 40% coupon or the shoe sale, and don't stack six modules under it.
- 2. **Segment the grid.** If I've bought beauty or apparel before, show me that. Sephora at Kohl's sends are cleaner than this, and so was Neiman Marcus on the Stuart Weitzman send, which had one photo and one idea. Sephora's makeup-sets email at least made the product the point.
- 3. **Put the exclusions in one visible line near the offer.** Macy's "confirm now for your extra 25% off" mechanic was clear about what I had to do. This is a scavenger hunt by comparison.
- 4. **Show my Kohl's Cash balance or expiry.** That's the real member benefit, and it should sit in the top status strip, not buried in the bottom third.
- 5. **Cut the frequency.** Eight-plus sends in ten days with the same "Ends Oct. 18" header trains me to ignore all of them.

# Technical Audit: "Open for 40% off + earn Kohl's Cash 🥳"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is an ESP-wrapped (`click.s.kohls.com`) send with four stacked tracking pixels and several malformed head and body constructs. Header-based compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because AgentMail doesn't relay those headers. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer and most links are unverified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque click tracking:** 68 links were skipped as tracking redirects, so none of their destinations were probed or verified. Example: `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7ImQi...`.
- **Four tracking beacons fire on open**, which is redundant and adds load and privacy exposure:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/838b4888-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1024046_20261017`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...&d=60268&bmt=0`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager, with `width="0" height="0"`)
- **Stray closing tag:** the `open.aspx` pixel is followed by `</custom>`, which has no opening tag. It is invalid markup left over from the template.
- **Empty parameter:** `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` is blank in the `916d6fa08d185233.png` URL. This is likely an unpopulated dynamic field. The URL is truncated, so I can't confirm it.
- **Subscriber ID in image URLs:** `mi_u=604230016` appears in the `mi.kohls.com` image URLs. This is a persistent identifier in third-party-fetched assets, which is low risk but worth knowing.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images (real impact):**
  - `58379988_Product_ctft01_ShoeSale_Hero_GIF`
  - `58379268_2_2x`
  - `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`
  - `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`
  
  With images blocked, or with a screen reader, these show as filenames or nothing.
- **Pixels without `alt`:** the `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...`, `mi.kohls.com/.../o.gif` and `demdex.net/event` pixels also lack alt. These are low impact, but `alt=""` would be cleaner. The `o.gif` pixel does have `aria-hidden="true"`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Empty favicon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`.
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will close the head early, so the following elements fall into the body and the schema.org microdata is unreliable. Gmail strips it regardless.
- **Duplicate meta:** `format-detection` appears twice.
- **Unused font:** the Roboto stylesheet is loaded but the body font stack is `Montserrat, Arial, Helvetica`. This is a wasted request, though harmless since it sits inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`.
- **Layout tables:** the pixel wrapper `<table width="1" height="1">` and the AAM table lack `role="presentation"`. The main containers have it.
