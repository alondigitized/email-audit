- No render bugs — everything loaded, text is legible, nothing overlapping.

## What's weak

- Nine different brand tiles in one scroll is Macy's-level overload — Sephora usually paces itself better than this, but not today.
- No mention of my Insider tier or points balance at all, so I have no idea where I stand — after four emails obsessing over "0 points," now total silence feels like whiplash, not fixed.
- The hero offer ("Clean at Sephora Must-Haves") has no price, no percentage, nothing time-bound — just vibes and a button.
- Models/hero imagery skew younger-trend (Lady Gaga's Haus Labs, K-beauty hair care later down) — nothing here signals "we know you're 47 and shop for skin-barrier support, not viral TikTok lip liner."
- The AppFest deal requires the app to redeem — that's friction for me; I don't want to download an app for one promo.

- 2. Cut the product grid from nine brands to three or four — curate, don't dump the whole warehouse.
