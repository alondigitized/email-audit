# The Designer Sale: Shop Givenchy, Ferragamo & more
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Six emails in under forty-eight hours. I want to sit with that for a moment before I say a single word about the creative, because that number is doing real damage to how I'm reading everything Neiman Marcus sends me right now. Yesterday's Designer Sale launch — clean red hero, The Row, Louboutin, 50% off front and center — that email earned its place in my inbox. This one is the same email wearing a different subject line. The red hero is the same. The 50% OFF is the same. Even the "LIMITED TIME" framing is the same. What changed is the product grid below the fold: Victoria Beckham, ALAIA, Burberry, Givenchy, Courreges — a genuinely strong designer lineup. That part I respect. I just had to scroll past my own fatigue to get there.
- What I cannot ignore: the subject line promises Ferragamo by name. I opened looking for it. I scrolled the entire grid — Victoria Beckham, Jacquemus, Balmain, ALAIA, Burberry, Givenchy, Courreges, Cult Gaia, Polo Ralph Lauren. No Ferragamo. That is not a creative choice, that is a trust problem. I'm 47, not 22. I know what bait-and-switch feels like on a price tag and I know what it feels like in a subject line. This qualifies.
- I wouldn't click through on this one. I'd mark it read, feel slightly annoyed, and wait to see if tomorrow brings a seventh. I would absolutely not forward it to a friend — I sent her the Designer Sale launch email yesterday, and this version would make her wonder why I'd sent the same thing twice.

## What's working

- **The product grid is legitimately strong.** ALAIA, Burberry, Givenchy, Courreges in one scroll — that's the Neiman Marcus I subscribed for.
- **Red hero reads instantly.** "Up to 50% Off" in large white type on crimson is unambiguous. No mystery about what the email wants me to do.
- **"Save on these designer styles before they're gone"** is the right urgency register for this customer — FOMO, not panic. Macy's would have put an exclamation point on it.
- **No render bugs.** Images load, text doesn't overlap, CTA buttons are distinct and tappable.
- **"Work with an in-person style advisor"** in the footer is a genuine NM brand signal. Kohl's doesn't have that. Macy's doesn't either. It reminds me there's a human behind this brand.

## What's weak

- **Ferragamo is in the subject line. Ferragamo is not in the email.** I counted nine brand tiles — none of them Ferragamo. That's a broken promise, not a teaser.
- **Cadence has crossed the line.** Email six in forty-eight hours. After yesterday's InCircle loyalty push scored so well, this repeat-the-sale email feels like NM squeezing the last drop out of a campaign that already had its moment.
- **No InCircle callout anywhere.** The email immediately before this one was explicitly about joining InCircle. Presumably I'm a member — so why isn't my loyalty status visible? Sephora Insider's "WE CALLED IN THE EXPERTS" email last week wove in Insider status mid-campaign without missing a beat. NM dropped it entirely here.
- **Men's CTAs dilute the grid.** "SHOP MEN'S" and "SHOP MEN'S SHOES" sit side-by-side with the women's options in a six-button row. That space could hold a beauty or fragrance tile — something that actually lands for me.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Remove Ferragamo from the subject or put Ferragamo in the email.** One or the other. A brand with NM's positioning cannot have a subject-line promise the email doesn't fulfill. If Ferragamo inventory exists, swap the Polo Ralph Lauren tile — the green polo reads mass-market for a NM context anyway.
- 2. **Give the hero a new visual signal.** The identical red 50%-off banner is doing heavy lifting for the sixth send in two days. One editorial hero image — one specific piece — makes this a new chapter of the sale, not a redirect.
- 3. **Add a single InCircle line.** "InCircle members earn points on every designer purchase" — that's it. It costs nothing and tells me I'm a known customer.
- 4. **Collapse the CTA grid to women-only for female segments.** Three focused women's CTAs beats six cluttered ones.
- 5. **Give "LIMITED TIME" a timestamp.** "Ends Sunday" turns urgency into action. Even Macy's anchors their LAST CHANCE mechanics to a confirm step.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Neiman Marcus "The Designer Sale" Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) table-layout send with XHTML 1.0 Transitional markup — structurally standard. However, two compliance headers are undetectable via the relay, the plain-text part is nearly all raw URLs, and the AMPscript variable block contains an unresolved scenario fork that could silently misfire in production.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**26 tracking/redirect links were not probed** — all resolved through a click-redirect domain. Broken or mis-parameterized links cannot be confirmed from the QA run. Manual spot-check of at least 3–5 representative links (hero CTA, nav links, footer) is required before send.

**Plain-text body is 87% raw URLs** (5,140 of 5,910 chars). This pattern strongly resembles spam to Bayesian filters. The plain-text part appears to be auto-generated from the HTML without stripping tracking URLs to human-readable anchor text. Inbox providers (especially Gmail) weigh the plain-text part in spam scoring.

No broken link data is available due to the skip — this is a gap in the QA coverage, not a clean bill of health.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Commented-out viewport meta tags (3 variants)** remain in `<head>`:
```html
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, user-scalable=no" />-->
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=320, initial-scale=0.29">-->
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=1024">-->
```
Dead code; no rendering impact, but creates ambiguity about which viewport setting is authoritative. The active tag sets `maximum-scale=1`, which disables pinch-to-zoom — an accessibility regression for low-vision users on mobile.

**iOS 9 hamburger bugfix still present:**
```css
@-webkit-keyframes bugfix { from { padding: 0; } to { padding: 0; } }
```
iOS 9 reached end-of-life in 2016. This payload is dead weight.

**CSS `:checked` hamburger nav** has no support in Outlook, Gmail app (Android), or Samsung Mail. Users on those clients will see no functional nav — acceptable only if fallback desktop layout is fully usable, which cannot be confirmed from the truncated source.

**`<title>` tag has a trailing space:** `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` — harmless but indicates template hygiene issues.

No `alt` text on images is verifiable from the truncated source. Should be confirmed in full HTML review.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

The AMPscript variable block at the top of the template contains an unresolved fork:

```html
<!--REQUIRED variables: ...
    @user Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", "EMAIL_ID", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email))
    ...
    CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW -->
<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->
```

Two mutually exclusive `@user` assignments exist — one sets `@user` to `EMAIL_ID` via a Lookup, the other sets it to a SHA-256 hash. The active scenario is not clearly indicated; the comment block does not show which AMPscript `%%[ ]%%` block is live. If the wrong `@user` value is passed to downstream personalization (e.g., recommendation engine, ESP suppression logic), it will silently produce wrong output rather than erroring.

`@uEm` and `@cmdid` are both looked up from `NM_CRM_Feed`. No fallback values are visible in the truncated source — a null lookup on either field could produce blank values in tracking URLs or personalized content blocks.

---

### 5. Compliance

| Check | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `List-Unsubscribe` header | **WARN — not detected** | QA: "Header may not be captured by AgentMail relay" |
| `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) | **WARN — not detected** | QA: One-click unsubscribe not confirmed |
| Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM) | **WARN — unknown** | QA: "Not found via relay — SPF/DKIM status unknown" |
| CAN-SPAM physical address | Not verifiable (HTML truncated) | Must confirm in footer |
| Unsubscribe mechanism in body | Not verifiable (HTML truncated) | Must confirm in footer |

**Critical:** Gmail and Yahoo Mail require `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (one-click) for all bulk senders above 5,000/day, per their February 2024 enforcement policy. If these headers are absent from the actual SMTP envelope (not just undetected by the relay), this send is non-compliant and subject to deliverability penalties. The ESP (SFMC) should be injecting these headers automatically — verify via raw message headers in a seed inbox before deployment.

Authentication-Results absence means SPF alignment and DKIM signature cannot be confirmed. Confirm DKIM is signing from `e.neimanmarcus.com` and that SPF includes SFMC sending IPs for that subdomain.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All 26 links are click-tracked redirects — UTM parameter correctness cannot be verified without probing. At minimum, confirm:

- `utm_campaign` matches the `@base_refid` value for this specific send
- `utm_medium=email` and `utm_source=neimanmarcus` (or equivalent) are present on all CTAs
- The `@refid` value (which appears to be `CONCAT(@base_refid,"")` — an identity concat that does nothing) is correctly appended to destination URLs

The identity concat `CONCAT(@base_refid,"")` should be audited — it produces no transformation and suggests an incomplete template edit where a suffix was removed but the CONCAT wrapper was not cleaned up.

---

### 7. Recommendations

**Before send:**
1. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are present in SMTP envelope via seed inbox raw headers — do not rely on relay detection alone.
2. Resolve the `@user` scenario fork. Remove the unused assignment entirely and leave only the live AMPscript block. Add a null-check/default on `@uEm` and `@cmdid`.
3. Probe at least 5 tracking links manually to confirm UTM params are correct and landing pages load.
4. Fix or regenerate the plain-text part — strip tracking URLs to human-readable text and destination domain only. Target <30% URL character density.

**Non-blocking / next template cycle:**
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the active viewport meta tag.
6. Replace `CONCAT(@base_refid,"")` with a direct `@base_refid` reference, or add the intended suffix.
7. Remove the three commented-out viewport tags and the iOS 9 keyframe bugfix to reduce template noise.
