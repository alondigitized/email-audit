# JUST IN: Kohl's Deal Days + Free Shipping + Daily Deals 🤗
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-23**
## Executive summary

- Twenty-six. I'm fairly sure that's where my Kohl's count sits for June, and I didn't even have to check — I've been keeping a running tally since the double-send on the 21st. So when "JUST IN" lands in my inbox on the 23rd with three separate value propositions crammed into one subject line, my first reaction isn't excitement — it's fatigue. I skimmed the Deal Days hero, registered there was free shipping, and then immediately felt the scroll anxiety set in as I looked at a wall of product tiles: $4.99 tops, $2.99 something, a slow cooker for $15.99, a Bali bra section, and then a purple Kohl's card box at the bottom. That's five distinct campaigns competing for my attention in one send.
- The Bali section actually stopped me for a second — I'm in the market and $21.99 is real money — and the 25% off for Kohl's card holders is the one signal that speaks my language (I do not spend a dollar without earning points back, ever). But that loyalty reminder is buried deep after I've already decided this email isn't for me.
- I'm marking it read. I won't click. I definitely won't forward it. Compare this to what Neiman Marcus did two days ago with a clean "1,000+ new additions" hero that earned its scroll — Kohl's is doing the exact opposite, throwing everything at the wall and hoping something sticks.

## What's working

- **Free shipping + Deal Days event framing** — an event name gives the discounts a reason to exist, which feels slightly more curated than a naked percent-off blast.
- **Kohl's card 25% off callout** — that purple loyalty block at the bottom is the one moment this email treats me like a known customer rather than a stranger; it's the only thing that almost earned a click.
- **Bali section with a real price** — $21.99 with a brand name attached is a specific, shoppable offer; it's what the entire email should look like.
- **No broken images or layout chaos** — the email renders cleanly, which is more than I can say for some Macy's sends I've suffered through.

## What's weak

- **The subject line is a parking lot of claims** — "JUST IN" + "Deal Days" + "Free Shipping" + "Daily Deals" in 56 characters is not a subject line, it's a press release bullet. Sephora wrote "The chapter is closing ⏲️" and I understood the urgency immediately; Kohl's needs three plus signs to say the same thing.
- **No primary CTA — just ten equal "Shop Now" buttons** — my eye has nowhere to land. The email asks me to care about casual tees, then bedding, then kitchen appliances, then intimate apparel, then my card. I shop one thing at a time.
- **Price points undercut the relationship** — $2.99 and $3.99 are bargain-bin signals. I cross-shop Nordstrom. When I buy at an event I'm spending real money. These numbers say "clearance," not "Deal Days."
- **Cadence has killed the urgency** — "JUST IN" means nothing after 26 sends in 23 days. I watched Kohl's cry wolf with "ENDS TONIGHT" on the 21st twice in one day; I'm not running to click anything labeled "just in."
- **Zero beauty content** — this is my biggest miss. I am at a beauty counter in some store several times a month. Not one skincare, fragrance, or cosmetic product appears in this scroll. Macy's at least nods to the beauty floor occasionally.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Send one section per email, not five** — pick Deal Days OR Bali OR free shipping; the Bali drop alone with a clean hero would have gotten a click from me today.
- 2. **Pull the Kohl's card loyalty callout up to the hero** — if 25% off for cardholders is the real offer, lead with it; don't make me scroll past a slow cooker to find it.
- 3. **Give the hero a deadline** — "Deal Days ends Sunday" changes everything; without it, "JUST IN" feels like filler from a brand that's been in my inbox daily.
- 4. **Add one beauty or fragrance tile** — I'm a Kohl's customer who also shops Sephora; there's no reason Kohl's beauty section shouldn't be in rotation given my behavior.
- 5. **Drop send frequency** — at 26 emails in 23 days, every new send devalues the previous one.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Card members: stack your 25% off through Sunday on hundreds of new deals`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your deal: free shipping + 25% off for Kohl's Card holders, today only`

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 click-redirect links skipped** — all routed through `click.s.kohls.com` and `click.chp.kohls.com`. HTTP probe was not performed; destination URLs and redirect chains are unverified.

**Three distinct tracking pixel domains** observed in the `<body>` before visible content begins:
- `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` — CoherentPath open pixel
- `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...` — Movable Ink open pixel
- `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...` — ESP open pixel (likely Salesforce Marketing Cloud)
- `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` — Adobe Audience Manager sync pixel

Three pixels for a single open event is redundant and adds load latency, though this is not a blocking issue.

**Malformed tag detected:**
```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt="">
<custom>
```
A stray `<custom>` closing tag appears immediately after the ESP open pixel `<img>`. This is invalid HTML and may cause parsing errors in strict clients.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**11 images missing `alt` text** — confirmed by QA. Affected images include what appear to be content/product images (not just tracking pixels), based on filenames like `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, etc., served from `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/`. These are likely Movable Ink-rendered dynamic content blocks.

Tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `event`, CoherentPath open pixel) lack `alt=""` as well. The Movable Ink pixel does have `aria-hidden="true"` correctly set — the others do not.

**`user-scalable=0`** is set in the viewport meta tag:
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />
```
This prevents pinch-to-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) and is increasingly blocked by mobile browsers.

**`<title>` tag is empty** — `<title></title>` — reduces accessibility for screen readers and may affect tab/preview rendering in some clients.

**Google Fonts loaded three times** (two `<link>` tags + two `@import` statements for the same families). Redundant, adds HTTP overhead.
