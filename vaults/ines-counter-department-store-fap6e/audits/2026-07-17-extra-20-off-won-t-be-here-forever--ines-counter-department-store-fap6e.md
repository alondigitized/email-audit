# Extra 20% off won't be here forever … 🏃‍♀️‍➡️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-17**
## Executive summary

- Fifty-eight. That's my count of Kohl's emails since Memorial Day, and this one arrives with a running-woman emoji practically sprinting away from its own exhaustion. "Won't be here forever" would land as urgency if I hadn't read some version of that line since June. The thing is, I *do* open these — I'm a Kohl's Rewards member, I care about Kohl's Cash, and July 20 is a real date I'd mark. So I opened.
- What I found inside is the same home goods cascade that's been scrolling past me all summer: a NutriBullet, white towels, gray bedding. The Simply Vera Wang section gave me a genuine flicker of interest — that's actually why I'm on this list — but the 20% callout there is so small it looks like fine print next to the twin 40%-off banners fighting for my eyes. And "Beauty" is a tiny thumbnail category icon buried near the footer, below the family-of-four lifestyle block. Neiman's recent summer arrivals email made me feel like they knew exactly who I was. This one handed me a blender.
- Mark as read. No click, no forward.

## What's working

- **20% hero is front and center** — the badge, the coupon, the July 20 deadline, all visible without hunting. That part earns its spot.
- **Kohl's Cash callout at the bottom** — the "earn for every $50 spent through July 20" framing is exactly the loyalty nudge that tips me from browsing to buying.
- **In-store barcode is visible** — I screenshot those and show them at the register. Practical, useful, keeps the coupon from being inbox-only.
- **Simply Vera Wang is named** — even buried, it signals that Kohl's remembers I'm not *only* there for the kitchen aisle.
- **Clean render, no broken images** — nothing competing for my eye in a bad way; the layout holds.

## What's weak

- **Email fifty-eight.** The "won't be here forever" urgency is structurally a lie by now. Sephora's "Today Only ‼️ 10% off your $100+ purchase" from two weeks ago actually meant one day. Kohl's "won't be here forever" means "through Sunday, same as last Sunday."
- **Home goods are 70% of this email and I'm a beauty and fashion shopper.** Neiman's last drop led with 1,000+ summer fashion arrivals and felt like it knew my closet. This one leads with a blender.
- **Five "Shop Now" buttons, zero specificity.** Macy's at least differentiates their CTAs by department. Here I have no idea which "Shop Now" is supposed to speak to me.
- **Beauty is an afterthought icon** — a thumbnail label at the very bottom, below the family-of-four block. I came in looking for something worth buying at a counter. I found nothing.
- **Competing discount blocks create visual noise** — the 40% bedding and 40% kitchen banners dwarf the 20% Vera Wang block. The email is fighting itself.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Segment by purchase history.** My Kohl's basket is fashion and Vera Wang, not kitchen appliances. Neiman Marcus leads with category-relevant arrivals. Pull Simply Vera Wang to the first content block after the coupon hero. That one change would have made me click.
- 2. **Retire "Shop Now" on at least three of those CTAs.** "Shop Vera Wang," "Shop Beauty," "Shop Bedding" — give me a destination, not a door.
- 3. **Lead with the Kohl's Cash math, not close with it.** "Earn up to $X Kohl's Cash on your 20% purchase — both run through July 20" should be the subhead under the coupon badge. That loyalty stack is the actual reason I buy.
- 4. **Give the subject line a brand name.** "Vera Wang + 20% off" tells me something. "Extra 20% off won't be here forever" is the same envelope I've opened fifty-eight times.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Vera Wang + 20% off — ends July 20`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 20% coupon expires in 3 days`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Earn Kohl's Cash on every $50 — stack both offers through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Simply Vera, beauty, and more — plus Kohl's Cash through July 20.`

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "Extra 20% off" (2026-07-17)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is built on a standard HTML 4.01 Transitional table layout with multi-ESP tracking (Coherent Path, MessageGears, Adobe Audience Manager/Demdex). All critical compliance and authentication signals are either absent or unverifiable through the AgentMail relay.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 click-redirect links skipped by HTTP probe** — all routed through `click.s.kohls.com` and `click.chp.kohls.com`. Cannot confirm final destination URLs are live or correctly parameterized without resolving redirects.

**Three distinct tracking pixel origins detected:**
- `click.chp.kohls.com` — Coherent Path open pixel
- `mi.kohls.com` — MessageGears open/render pixels
- `kohls.demdex.net` — Adobe Audience Manager sync pixel

This is expected for a retailer of this scale, but creates redundant network requests on open and increases fingerprinting surface.

**Malformed HTML tag** in open-pixel block:
```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>
```
The `</custom>` closing tag is invalid and orphaned. Most clients will silently ignore it, but it will fail strict HTML validators and could cause layout shifts in edge-case parsers.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**11 images missing `alt` text** — confirmed by QA. Breakdown:

| Image | Purpose | Alt Present |
|---|---|---|
| `9d3fadbf...` (click.chp.kohls.com) | Open/click pixel | No |
| `o.gif` (mi.kohls.com) | Open pixel | No (has `aria-hidden="true"` — acceptable for tracking pixels) |
| `event` (kohls.demdex.net) | AAM pixel | No |
| `2a5adc5a...` through `916d6fa0...` (mi.kohls.com/p/rp/) | Render pixels | No |

The `o.gif` pixel is the only one that includes `aria-hidden="true"` — the rest lack both `alt=""` and `aria-hidden`. For tracking pixels, an empty `alt=""` is the correct fix; for content images, descriptive alt text is required.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — prevents users from pinching to zoom on mobile. This is an accessibility violation (WCAG 1.4.4) and is blocked by some mobile clients.

**`<title>` is empty** — screen readers and some preview pane implementations use the `<title>` element. An empty tag degrades the experience for assistive tech users.

**Google Fonts loaded three times** — once via `<link>`, once via `@import` inside a `<style>` block, for both Roboto and Montserrat. This is redundant and adds ~200–400ms latency on first open in image-rendering clients that execute CSS.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens visible in the truncated HTML source. Recipient ID (`mi_u=604230016`) and campaign ID (`mi_ecmp=1017787_2026717`) are correctly populated in pixel URLs. Coherent Path `cp_tp` payload is present and base64/URL-encoded as expected.

No issues found with token resolution in the visible source.

---

### 5. Compliance

**CAN-SPAM — physical address not detected** by QA scan. The HTML is truncated so the footer may be out of scope, but this must be verified in the full source. A missing physical address is a CAN-SPAM violation.

**`List-Unsubscribe` header absent** from captured headers. This is flagged as potentially an AgentMail relay capture gap rather than a confirmed send-side omission — Kohl's at this volume almost certainly sends this header. Verify against raw MIME headers, not relay-captured metadata.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) absent** — same caveat. Gmail and Apple Mail require this for one-click unsubscribe compliance in bulk senders. If genuinely missing server-side, this is a deliverability risk under Google/Yahoo 2024 sender requirements.

**`meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer"`** is set. This suppresses referrer data on any link clicks to external sites, which may conflict with UTM attribution (see §6).

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**`no-referrer` meta conflicts with UTM tracking** — the referrer suppression set in `<head>` means any analytics relying on `document.referrer` at the landing page will see a direct/none source rather than email. If UTM params are present on click URLs (unverifiable since all 22 links were skipped), UTM-based attribution will still work, but referrer-based fallback attribution will not.

**`mi_ecmp=1017787_2026717` format** matches date pattern `260717` (July 17, 2026) — campaign code is date-stamped correctly for today's send.

Cannot audit UTM parameters on destination URLs — all click links were skipped by the HTTP probe. This is the primary gap in site continuity verification.
