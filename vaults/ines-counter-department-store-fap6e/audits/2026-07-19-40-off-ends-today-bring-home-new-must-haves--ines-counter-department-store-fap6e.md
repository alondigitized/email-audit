# 40% off ends today + bring home new must-haves ⏳
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-19**
## Executive summary

- Sixty-one. That's my count since Memorial Day, and this one drops with the same 40% offer that showed up twice yesterday — morning and evening — with slightly different emoji accessories. The hourglass today, shopping bags last night, running woman the day before. Kohl's is rotating emojis the way I rotate lip colors, except I actually feel something when I open a new lipstick.
- Inside: home. More home. Then activewear. Then more home. I'm a Kohl's Beauty section regular — I buy Laneige there, I've grabbed Bliss on a whim, I check the K-Beauty endcap every visit — and this email wouldn't know that if it tried. The entire scroll is rugs, bedding, bath, FLX athletic gear, and a child I cannot fully identify. That's a Macy's-level department-store dump, minus Macy's at least occasionally surfacing a fragrance launch.
- I'd click the 40% link out of muscle memory, not enthusiasm. I would not forward it. There is nothing in here I'd text a friend about.

## What's working

- **The coupon mechanics are clean.** The barcode, wallet link, and promo code are all visible without hunting — that's more than I can say for the last Neiman Marcus "Last Call" email, where the actual discount path required three clicks to understand.
- **Kohl's Cash callout is present.** "Earn Kohl's Cash" and the Rewards Member header remind me there's a points reason to shop, which is the only argument that actually moves me.
- **"Ends today" urgency is explicit in subject.** Even if I've stopped believing it, the signal is there and the 40% number is front and center.
- **Summer framing ("one more hour outside") is seasonally on point.** The outdoor patio lifestyle shot opens the email logically for July.

## What's weak

- **No beauty. Zero.** Sixty-one emails and not once have they shown me Laneige, the Bliss brightening serum I reorder twice a year, or anything from the beauty section I actually visit in-store. Sephora sends me irrelevant soccer puns but at least they're in my category universe; this email isn't even orbiting it.
- **Too many sections competing for attention.** Home patio → 50% off surprise → 40% online only → Bedding/Bath grid → FLX activewear. That's five offers in one scroll. Compare that to the Neiman Marcus "Up to 70% off new sale arrivals" send I reviewed last week — one hero, one number, everything else subordinate. That email scored a 9 for a reason.
- **"Coupons do not apply" appears twice** — once on the 40% section and once on FLX. If the headline offer has exclusions I can spot without reading the fine print, I feel like I'm being misdirected.
- **The preheader leaks what looks like a rewards member code or promo tracking string.** Whatever is showing in that top gray bar before the nav is not copy. It's system bleed, and it's the first thing inbox clients show me next to the subject line.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Surface beauty in at least one content block.** I buy beauty at Kohl's. They have Laneige, Bliss, e.l.f., Neutrogena. One skincare or makeup tile with the 40% applied would make this email actually relevant to me. That's the single highest-lift change.
- 2. **Cut to three sections maximum.** Home patio hero, Bedding/Bath grid, then the coupon close. FLX can have its own send — it's a different shopper mindset.
- 3. **Fix the preheader.** Whatever is leaking at the top should be replaced with copy that earns the open. Something like "Your 40% code is ready — today only."
- 4. **Retire "must-haves" from the subject vocabulary.** It is doing no work. "Rug, bedding, and something for the bath" would be more concrete and almost equally punchy.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 40% ends tonight — home, beauty, + more`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Today only: 40% off + earn Kohl's Cash ⏳`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your code is ready. Shop home, beauty, and more before midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `40% off + Kohl's Cash — everything you've been eyeing.`

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "40% off ends today" (2026-07-19)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

One unrendered merge token (`%%offer_evnt_nm%%`) confirms a broken personalization pipeline that will display raw template syntax to recipients. Compliance gaps (missing physical address, absent List-Unsubscribe headers) and 14 images missing `alt` text are the remaining high-priority issues.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**No broken destination URLs detected**, but all 22 click-tracked links were skipped by the HTTP probe (click-redirect domains: `click.chp.kohls.com`, `click.s.kohls.com`). Destination reachability is unverified.

Three separate open/pixel tracking systems are firing on load:
- `click.chp.kohls.com/o/01bdb5e5...` (CoherentPath)
- `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbb.../o.gif` (MessageInfo)
- `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?3D7CVIZ...` (Salesforce Marketing Cloud)
- `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager)

No functional issue, but triple-pixel stacking means four outbound requests on open — relevant if deliverability filtering is tightening.

**Malformed HTML tag:** `</custom>` appears immediately after the SMC open pixel `<img>` tag. This is invalid HTML and may cause parser errors in strict renderers.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**14 content/product images missing `alt` text** — confirmed by QA. Tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `event`, open-pixel) correctly use `alt=""` or `aria-hidden="true"`, but all `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` renderable images and the product image (`kohlsprod/55405645_root_2x`) lack `alt` attributes entirely.

Notable offenders:
- `55405645_root_2x` — product image with no alt
- `611d2b1debdc5ae0.png`, `b0aad5bed92b660a.png` — images with promo codes embedded in the URL (`left_promo_code=LPJ9Y27GJLXBX`) but no alt text; if images are blocked, offer code is invisible to the recipient

`user-scalable=0` in the viewport meta tag disables pinch-to-zoom on mobile — accessibility violation (WCAG 1.4.4).

`<title>` tag is empty. Screen readers and some clients use this for tab/preview labeling.

Google Fonts are loaded via both `<link>` and `@import` simultaneously for the same families (Roboto, Montserrat) — duplicate requests, no functional breakage but unnecessary overhead.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**[FAIL]** Raw merge token `%%offer_evnt_nm%%` found in rendered HTML source. This token was not substituted at send time. Recipients will see the literal string. Location not pinpointed from truncated source, but the QA scanner confirmed it exists in the delivered message body.

No other unresolved tokens detected.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

The `mi_ecmp` parameter (`1017930_2026719`) and `email_name=260719_DG_Email_Mystery` appear consistently across all `mi.kohls.com` image tracking URLs, indicating campaign-level tracking is wired. However:

- No UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) are visible in the truncated source. If click-redirect URLs do not append UTMs before landing, GA4/analytics attribution will be lost or fall back to direct traffic.
