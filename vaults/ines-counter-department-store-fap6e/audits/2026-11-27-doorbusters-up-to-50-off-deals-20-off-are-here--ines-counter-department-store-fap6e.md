# DOORBUSTERS + up to 50% off deals + 20% off are here 👀
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again. This is the fifth Kohl's send I've seen since Wednesday, and the render is a 12,000-pixel strip of stacked tiles. At the size I see it, I'm reading shapes and big numbers, not fine print. I can make out a black-and-gold "50% off" banner at the top, a "Free shipping with a $25 purchase" strip, a big "DOORBUSTERS" block, "60% off," "$10," "$14," "10.39," and a "Kohl's Cash" tile. Then there's a Black Friday Sweepstakes block near the bottom.
- It's loud, but it's at least organized by category: Home, Men, Women, Kids, Shoes, Beauty. I counted about a dozen "Shop Now" buttons, so no single one is *the* button. Nothing here says "I know who you are." The beauty tile is a Sephora-at-Kohl's shot with a "$14" tag, and it's the only thing aimed at me. I'd skim it, maybe tap the beauty tile out of curiosity, and not forward it.

## What's working

- The category chip row (Kids, Women, Men, Home, Toys, Beauty) lets me jump straight to beauty without scrolling the whole strip.
- "Free shipping with a $25 purchase" is easy to find near the top.
- Large numerals like "60%," "$10" and "$14" read fine even at this size.
- The "Sephora Collection" eyeshadow palette tile at "$14" is a concrete product with a price. It's the one thing in here for me.
- The footer has the Kohl's Cash and Same-Day Delivery info in one tidy block.

## What's weak

- Nothing leads. "Doorbusters," "20% off with coupon," "$15 Kohl's Cash," "10% off with Rewards" and a sweepstakes all compete. It's Macy's-style overload.
- The math is buried. "+20% off w/coupon" and "select styles" and "coupons do not apply" sit in tiny type under almost every tile. That's the fine print I can't read, and it makes the offer feel less honest.
- The models and product mix skew toward fleece, boots, slippers and kids' items. Beauty, the one category I shop, gets a single small tile.
- Kohl's Rewards is there, but only as "10% off w/Kohl's Rewards" and a "Kohl's Cash" badge. There's no tier or status and no "your points." I won't use a card without points back, and this doesn't tell me what I'd earn.
- It's the fifth near-identical Doorbuster email since 11/26. The urgency doesn't feel credible anymore.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut to one hero and one offer. Pick Doorbusters or the 20% coupon or Kohl's Cash, not all three in the header.
- 2. Show the real math once, in readable type: "$50 spent = $15 Kohl's Cash, earned through Dec 1."
- 3. Give me a beauty module that leads with the brand and the price, the way Sephora's Black Friday send opened cleanly on a giant "50% OFF." Add a member line like "Rewards members: extra 10% on Beauty."
- 4. Cap the tiles at six or fewer, and cut the sweepstakes from a shopping email.
- 5. Send fewer of these. Neiman Marcus's "Exclusively for you: 20% off" got my attention precisely because it wasn't the fifth that week.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Beauty doorbusters from $14, plus Rewards bonus`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Earn $15 Kohl's Cash for every $50 you spend, through Dec 1.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Members get an extra 10% off. Free shipping over $25.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, 640px layout with a mobile media query, routed through Salesforce Marketing Cloud (`click.s.kohls.com`) and Movable Ink (`mi.kohls.com`) tracking. Confirmed issues are malformed head markup, missing alt text on content images, and a viewport that blocks zoom. The compliance and authentication warnings come from header capture limits and the truncated source, so they can't be confirmed as defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **94 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped HTTP probing for the click-redirect domains, so destination status codes are unverified. The hrefs are opaque tokens, e.g. `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7ImQiOjUwNzMs…`.
- **Four separate tracking pixels fire on open:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/42066df1-…` (1×1, no `alt`)
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager, `width="0" height="0"`)
- **Malformed pixel markup.** The `open.aspx` image is closed with a stray `</custom>` tag: `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?…" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`. There is no matching opening tag.
- **Empty favicon href.** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` can cause some clients to re-request the document URL.
- **Plain-text part is 74% URLs.** QA reports 16,698 of 22,492 characters are URLs. That is above the 70% threshold and is a spam-filter risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text is missing on content images.** QA flagged 42. Examples:
  - Banner and product images: `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`
  - Product images: `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/60699446_4_2x` and `60699446_3_2x`
  - The `click.chp.kohls.com` and `demdex.net` tracking pixels have no `alt` attribute, but they are not content images.
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` prevents pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Invalid head content.** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will close the head early at that point. The `<meta itemprop>` elements inside it are also invalid there.
- **Inconsistent `role="presentation"`.** The 1×1 pixel table and the AAM table lack it, while the main layout tables have it.
- **Doctype mismatch.** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is paired with `xmlns:o` and `xmlns:v` attributes. This is common in email and low impact.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The pixel URLs carry rendered identifiers (`mi_u=604230016`, `mi_ecmp=1026829_20261127`), which are populated correctly.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed**. Verify against the raw message.
- **No physical address detected.** The HTML source was truncated before the footer, so this is **unconfirmed**. Check the full source.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because of the relay, so no defect is established.
- The sender domain `s.kohls.com` is consistent with the tracking domains (`click.s.kohls.com`).

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM and landing-page alignment can't be verified.** Destination URLs are encoded in SFMC `qs=` tokens, and the 94 links were not resolved. Any UTM parameters or landing-page mismatches are not visible in the source.
- **Campaign ID is in the pixels only.** The `mi.kohls.com` image URLs carry `mi_ecmp=1026829_20261127` and `email_name=261127_DG_Email_Fri_Mi…`, so campaign attribution is present there.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 42 content images, and `alt=""` to decorative images and pixels.
2. Remove the stray `</custom>` tag, move the `schema.org` `<div>` out of `<head>`, remove the empty `href=""` favicon link, and fill in `<title>`.
3. Drop `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Consolidate the four open-tracking pixels where possible to reduce page weight and failure points.
5. Raise the text-to-URL ratio in the plain-text part (for example, by trimming the tracking-wrapped links in it).
6. Confirm the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, the physical address and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results from the raw message or full HTML.
7. Resolve a sample of the redirect links to check destination status and UTM propagation.
