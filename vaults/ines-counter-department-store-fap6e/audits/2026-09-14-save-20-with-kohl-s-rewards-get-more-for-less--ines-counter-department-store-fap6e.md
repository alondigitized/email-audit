# Save 20% with Kohl's Rewards & get more for less 🤗
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-14**
## Executive summary

- Same three-tier discount ladder, same tiny colored boxes — 30% cardholder, 20% Rewards, 15% nonmember — right up top, exactly like I flagged in Wednesday's cozy fall refresh and Sunday's Customer Appreciation blast. At this point I could recite Kohl's discount math in my sleep. Then it just... keeps going: denim guide, style-it outfit grid, a men's/kids'/home button row, then a whole separate Sephora at Kohl's insert for Beauty Insiders, then a clearance banner, then a Ninja blender, a bath towel, some pants, a slow cooker. This is not an email, it's a circular I'd get stuffed in my mailbox next to coupons for the Sunday paper.
- The one bright spot is that Sephora banner — "Beauty Insiders, Get 30% off Benefit Cosmetics" — because that's actually my category, but it's buried past denim guides and a full outfit lookbook, competing with slow cookers for my attention. Neiman Marcus knew to lead with one gorgeous hero image and stop; Sephora's own Daily Deals email is a mess but at least it's a beauty mess, front and center. Kohl's makes me scroll through jeans and cookware to find the one thing I'd actually want.
- Verdict: not opening this on my phone past the first fold, definitely not forwarding, and it's getting archived along with the other five nearly-identical Kohl's sends I've gotten this week.

## What's working

- The Sephora + Kohl's "30% off Benefit Cosmetics" banner is genuinely relevant to me and has a real deadline (Ends September 14).
- Nice clean product photography on the denim guide — the "wide leg / straight / bootcut" labels are a helpful, simple visual sort.
- The "Picked with you in mind" section at least gestures at personalization, even if a Ninja blender and a bath towel don't feel picked for me at all.
- Clearance callout with "Save up to 70%" has real punch as a standalone banner.

## What's weak

- This is the sixth Kohl's email this week with the identical 30%/20%/15% tiered-discount header — I'm numb to it now.
- Way too much crammed in one send: denim, style-it, kids/men/women/home nav, Sephora insert, clearance, home goods, appliances. No focus, no hierarchy.
- The beauty offer I actually care about is buried deep, after multiple unrelated category pushes — a beauty-counter regular shouldn't have to scroll past a slow cooker to find her Sephora discount.
- Barcodes and coupon codes stacked at the bottom feel like a Sunday circular, not a curated retail email — nothing about tier status or "you're a Rewards member, here's what that gets you specifically."
- Models are fine demographically, but nothing here signals "we know you shop beauty" until three-quarters of the way down.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this into two emails: one entirely about the Sephora/Benefit beauty offer for people like me, one for home/apparel. Trying to be everything to everyone means the beauty offer I'd actually click gets lost.
- 2. If it must stay one email, move the Sephora + Kohl's Beauty Insiders banner to the top, above the denim guide — that's the freshest, most personalized offer in the whole send.
- 3. Cut the repeated 30/20/15 tier banner — I've now seen it verbatim six times this week; rotate creative or at least vary the copy.
- 4. Add a line acknowledging my Rewards status specifically ("As a Rewards Member, here's your 30% off Benefit") instead of generic "Beauty Insider" language that doesn't tie back to the Kohl's Rewards tier shown at the top.
- 5. Drop the appliance/home grab-bag section entirely from a beauty-adjacent send — it dilutes the offer and reads like clearance filler.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Beauty Insiders: 30% off Benefit Cosmetics, ends today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Rewards discount + 30% off Benefit at Sephora`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus earn Kohl's Cash on every Benefit purchase — today only`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your 20% Rewards discount is stacking with Sephora savings`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard Kohl's promotional template with heavy tracking/pixel infrastructure; automated QA flags gaps in unsubscribe headers, sender authentication visibility, and missing alt text across nearly all images.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 76 tracking/click-redirect links detected (`click.s.kohls.com`, `click.chp.kohls.com` domains) — HTTP probing was skipped for these per QA policy, so live-destination validity is unconfirmed.
- Multiple tracking pixels present: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/5fcfc489-...`, `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`, `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager), plus several `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` re-permission/tracking pixels.
- Malformed markup: `<img ... width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>` — stray closing `</custom>` tag with no matching opening tag, inside the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` open-tracking `<div>`. This is invalid HTML and should be removed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 10 images flagged missing `alt` text by QA, including the visible product image `57448464_roots_2x` (`images.am.kohls.com`) — this one impacts screen-reader users since it's content-bearing, not just a tracking pixel.
- Tracking/beacon pixels (`o.gif`, `event`, `up/...`, `rp/*.png`) also lack `alt`, though `alt=""` with `aria-hidden="true"` (as used on the `o.gif` pixel) is the correct pattern — the AAM pixel (`kohls.demdex.net/event`) and several `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*` pixels lack even empty `alt`/`aria-hidden`, which is a minor a11y hygiene gap (low impact since they're 0-dimension).
- Legacy DOCTYPE (HTML 4.01 Transitional) and MSO conditional comments indicate template is built for Outlook desktop compatibility — no rendering defects observed in the truncated source itself.
- Google Fonts loaded via `@import` and `<link>` with no fallback web-safe stack declared inline beyond `Montserrat,Arial,Helvetica,sans-serif` on `<body>` — acceptable, low risk.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, `[[...]]`) visible in the truncated source.
- Tracking URLs carry dynamic identifiers (`mi_cid`, `mi_mid`, `mi_ecmp=1021753_2026914`, `d_sid=13245196`) that appear correctly populated (no literal placeholder strings like `undefined` or empty token brackets).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not detected by QA. Note this may be a relay artifact (AgentMail) rather than an actual sender omission — cannot confirm from HTML body alone since unsubscribe headers are SMTP-level, not HTML.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)**: not detected — one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed.
- **Physical mailing address**: not detected in the truncated HTML — CAN-SPAM requires this in the footer; cannot confirm absence definitively since source was truncated before the footer, but flagging per QA output.
- **Authentication-Results header**: not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status unknown via this relay; cannot confirm sender authentication posture from available data.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Campaign identifiers present and consistent across pixels: `mi_ecmp=1021753_2026914`, `email_name=260914_DG_Email_Monday` — naming convention suggests date-coded campaign (2026-09-14, "DG" segment, Monday send).
- Click links route through `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=...` redirect wrapper with opaque base64/encoded query strings — actual landing page destinations and UTM parameters are not visible in encoded form and were not resolved (HTTP probe skipped per QA policy for tracking domains). Cannot confirm landing-page alignment without decoding/following these redirects.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the stray `</custom>` closing tag in the open-tracking pixel block — invalid markup.
2. Add `alt=""` + `aria-hidden="true"` to the Adobe AAM pixel (`kohls.demdex.net/event`) and remaining `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` beacons for consistency with the pattern already used elsewhere in the template.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to the content image `57448464_roots_2x` — this is the one alt-text gap with real accessibility impact.
4. Verify with the sending platform (not derivable from this HTML) that `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and a physical address are actually present at the SMTP/footer level — QA's non-detection may be a relay capture limitation rather than a true compliance gap; confirm rather than assume either way.
