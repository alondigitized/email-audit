# Love Brunello Cucinelli? Earn Triple Points on your purchase today
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-24**
## Executive summary

- Now this is Neiman's speaking my language — and specifically to me, not just "InCircle members." Brunello Cucinelli is exactly the kind of quiet-luxury brand I'd actually swipe my Neiman's card for, and Triple Points on it is a real reason to time a purchase, not just noise. It's basically yesterday's gift-card-and-Triple-Points play ("Ends soon: Up to a $1,500 gift card + 3x Points," 7/10) but swapped from a blunt dollar mechanic to a designer-specific one, which honestly reads more like something a personal stylist would flag for me than a mass blast.
- The black hero block with the serif "TRIPLE POINTS on Brunello Cucinelli" is clean and expensive-looking, no clutter, one CTA. The nine-product grid underneath is a nice touch too — I can see the cashmere, the slingback, the tweed jacket without clicking anywhere. This is miles ahead of Sephora's App Fest email, which buried its one good story (Tower 28 before/after) under endless scroll — Neiman's puts the whole story above the fold in one clean panel.
- I'd click. Not to forward to a friend exactly — my Brunello-buying friends already get their own version of this — but I'd screenshot the "Ends 8/27/26" line and text it to my husband as a hint.

## What's working

- The Triple Points hook is specific to a brand I actually shop, not a generic sitewide push — feels like they know my closet.
- One CTA, one hero, no scroll-fatigue — a relief after Kohl's six-day slog of "Friends & Family" repeats.
- The nine-item grid gives me real product to react to (I want that grey cropped sweater and the slingback) without forcing a click just to browse.
- "Exclusions Apply" is stated right under the CTA, not buried in six paragraphs of fine print at the bottom — though there IS a lot of fine print, more on that below.
- InCircle/Chairman's Circle tier language shows up explicitly in the fine print ("President's Circle and Chairman's Circle members earn 15 points per dollar") — that's the loyalty specificity Fashion Finds and Dress-for-Weekends emails this week completely lacked.

## What's weak

- The fine print block is a wall — I had to hunt for the actual point multiplier and it's honestly Macy's-confirmation-email levels of "read the terms before you shop" energy.
- No price anywhere. I don't need a full price list, but a "starting at" on even one hero piece would help me decide if this is a $400 sweater or a $2,000 coat before I click.
- All nine products just say "Brunello Cucinelli" under the image — no item name, no fabric, no price. I'm squinting at thumbnails to guess if that's cashmere or merino.
- No visible "you're a Chairman's Circle member" personalization — the tier math is in the legal copy, but nowhere does it say MY tier, which is a miss given how deliberately InCircle-flavored the rest of the email is.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a price range or "from $X" on at least the hero look — right now I have zero anchor for whether this is worth timing my spend around.
- 2. Pull one line of the points math out of the fine print and into the hero — "Chairman's Circle: earn 15x points" would beat "Exclusions Apply" as the subhead.
- 3. Add item names/fabric under each product tile (e.g., "Cashmere Crewneck" instead of just repeating "Brunello Cucinelli" nine times) — it's redundant and wastes real estate that could sell me on the piece.
- 4. Show my tier status if they have it — "As a Chairman's Circle member, you earn 15x points" personalizes what's currently a static legal disclosure.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Chairman's Circle bonus: 15x points on Brunello Cucinelli`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop cashmere, tailoring, and boots — points end 8/27/26 at 10pm CT`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Exclusions apply, but the multiplier doesn't — see what qualifies`
