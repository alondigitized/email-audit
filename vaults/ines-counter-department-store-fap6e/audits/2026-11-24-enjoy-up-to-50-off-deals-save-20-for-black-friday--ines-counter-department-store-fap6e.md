## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Kohl's Black Friday (`kohls@s.kohls.com`)

The HTML source was truncated, so the footer, the unsubscribe link and every UTM-bearing CTA are outside what I could see. Statements about those are limited to the QA output.

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, mobile-responsive build with four or more tracking beacons stacked at the top. It has markup-validity defects (a `<div>` inside `<head>` and a stray `</custom>` tag) and widespread missing alt text. The compliance and authentication checks could not be confirmed from the captured data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links.** 92 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe (click-redirect domains), so none of their destinations or status codes were checked. Examples are `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…` and `click.chp.kohls.com/o/…`.
- **Stacked open beacons.** Four separate pixels fire before any content:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/0171509f-…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager)

  This is redundant and adds third-party requests. The `demdex.net` beacon also loads from a third-party domain.
- **Per-recipient ID in image URLs.** `mi_u=604230016` appears in the query string of `o.gif` and the `/p/rp/*.png` images. Those requests carry a per-recipient identifier to the image host. This is not a defect on its own. It does mean the images can't be cached or proxied as generic assets.
- **Malformed markup after the pixel.** `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?…" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>` has an orphaned `</custom>` closing tag. Nothing opens it.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** QA flagged 14 images (the list was cut off after `916d6fa08d185233.png`, so more may exist). The content-bearing ones are:
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png` and `916d6fa08d185233.png`
  - Product images `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/60479404_1_2x`, `60699446_4_2x` and `60699446_3_2x`

  With images blocked, the offer content disappears for sighted users. Screen readers will announce filenames.
- **Tracking pixels without `alt`.** The beacons at `click.chp.kohls.com/o/…` and `kohls.demdex.net/event` have no `alt`. The first sits outside any `role="presentation"` wrapper. Set `alt=""` on them. The `o.gif` image already has `alt=""` plus `aria-hidden`, so it is flagged by QA but is not a real problem.
- **`<div itemscope …>` inside `<head>`.** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits between the `<style>` blocks and `</head>`. A `<div>` is invalid there. Parsers will implicitly close `<head>` and start `<body>`, which orphans the `<meta itemprop>` tags and can disturb the `<body>` styling.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure on supporting clients.
- **Empty `<title>`.** `<title></title>` leaves no document title.
- **Empty icon link.** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty `href`.
- **Doctype mismatch.** The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional, but the markup uses XHTML self-closing syntax (`alt="" />`). This is harmless in practice but inconsistent.
- **Duplicate meta tag.** `meta name="format-detection"` appears twice, once for `telephone=no` and once for `date=no`. Combine them into one tag: `content="telephone=no, date=no"`.
- **Web fonts.** Roboto and Montserrat load via `<link>` and `@import` inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. Many clients ignore them, so the `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` fallback handles those. No issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[%…%]`) appear in the visible HTML. The truncated portion is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
All three findings below are warnings. None is a confirmed failure.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058).** Neither header was found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is inconclusive. Verify against the raw message from the sending side. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers.
- **Physical address.** QA found none, but the footer fell in the truncated section. Confirm manually.
- **Authentication-Results.** The header is absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status could not be determined. The From domain `s.kohls.com` matches the click-tracking host `click.s.kohls.com`, which is good for alignment. That is not proof of passing.
- **Plain-text part.** 16,626 of 22,419 characters (about 74%) are URLs. That is above the 70% threshold and is likely from tracking redirects. It is a spam-filter risk and a poor experience for text-only readers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM data.** The captured HTML shows no `utm_*` parameters and no CTA hrefs, so UTM consistency and landing-page alignment couldn't be assessed.
- **Campaign tags.** Image URLs carry `mi_ecmp=1026616_20261124` and `email_name=261124_DG_Email_Tuesda…` (the value is cut off). Attribution seems to run through `mi_*` parameters and the `click.s.kohls.com` redirect, not UTMs.
- **Unprobed destinations.** All 92 click-redirect links were skipped, so there is no evidence the destinations resolve or match the offer in the subject (up to 50% off, 20% off for Black Friday).

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Confirm compliance headers.** Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` on the raw message, bypassing the AgentMail relay. Check the footer address in the full HTML.
2. **Move `<div itemscope …>` into `<body>`,** or replace it with JSON-LD or `<meta>` tags inside `<head>`. Delete the stray `</custom>` after the `open.aspx` pixel.
3. **Add descriptive `alt` text** to the seven `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` images and the three product images. Use `alt=""` on the tracking beacons.
4. **Drop `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag.
5. **Populate `<title>`** and remove or fill the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon">`. Merge the two `format-detection` tags.
6. **Reduce beacon redundancy.** Consolidate to one open-tracking pixel unless each serves a distinct system (ESP, analytics, Adobe AAM).
7. **Shrink the plain-text part** by shortening or removing tracking URLs in it, to bring URL share under 70%.
8. **Re-run QA on untruncated HTML,** following a sample of the 92 redirects through to confirm destination status and UTM or `mi_*` parameter consistency.
