- The layout is genuinely clean — twelve product tiles, clear brand labels under each, nothing overlapping or broken.
- Dropping the editorial-model fantasy for straight product shots is the right call for someone like me who just wants to see the merchandise.
- Saint Laurent bag and the Prada flats caught my eye immediately — those are pieces I'd actually consider.
- "Shop All Women's New Arrivals" and "Shop All Men's New Arrivals" buttons give me a clear next step instead of vague "Discover" language.

## What's weak

- Zero prices. Not one. Compare that to Neiman's own "EXTRA 25% OFF / UP TO 70% OFF" Last Call send from yesterday that I gave 7/10 — that email respected my time. This one makes me do all the work.
- No InCircle mention anywhere. I'm a rewards customer across three stores, and Sephora's Insider Celebration (9/10) led with "that's my language." Neiman's says nothing about points, tier status, or member perks.
- No urgency, no dates, no "new this week" specificity beyond the vague headline — it could've been sent any day this year.
- "Bright summer days" is the subject line but nothing in the hero or copy reflects that — no seasonal framing, no sun colors, just black-and-white "BRAND NEW STYLES."

## Recommendations

- 1. Put prices under at least the hero pieces — even "from $XXX" would give me a reason to click instead of guess.
- 2. Work InCircle status or points into the copy somewhere — even one line like "InCircle members earn double points on new arrivals" would make me feel like a known customer instead of a stranger browsing a lookbook.
- 3. Tie the subject line's "bright summer days" promise to something visible in the hero — a color story, a beach-adjacent styling note, anything that isn't just black text on black.
