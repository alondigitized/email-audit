# Daily Deals + INSTANT Kohl's Cash + free shipping = WOW 🎉
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-26**
## Executive summary

- Thirty-one. That's where my Kohl's count lands for June, and I didn't need to check my math — I could feel it the moment "WOW 🎉" hit my subject line. This email is built on the same chassis that arrived twice on the 25th, twice on the 24th, and on and on back to at least the 22nd: Deal Days banner, $20 Kohl's Cash with an asterisk attached, a stack of percentage-offs across categories I'm not shopping, and "Shop Now" repeated until it means nothing.
- What I see when I actually open it is a circular, not an email. The $20 Kohl's Cash hero is there at the top with a "TODAY ONLY" badge — fine, that's something — but then it immediately dissolves into featured daily deals (cookware? plaid shirts?), six more day-of deals below that, a lingerie section at 65% off, Docs shoes, a credit card push, and free shipping buried near the footer. Sephora sent me a clean, focused "Last chance to save up to 50%" and I knew exactly what to do with it. Neiman Marcus sent "1,000+ new arrivals" and even that sprawl felt curated compared to this. Kohl's is handing me a catalog and calling it a campaign.
- Would I click? No. The Kohl's Cash mechanic is the one genuine hook for me — I won't spend without something coming back — but nothing in this email is in my category, the fine print asterisk undercuts the hero offer before I've even committed to it, and the sheer length saps any urgency the "TODAY ONLY" badge tried to manufacture. I marked this read and moved on.

## What's working

- The $20 Kohl's Cash hero is legible and above the fold — "to use instantly" with a "TODAY ONLY" badge communicates the key mechanic without making me scroll for it.
- Kohl's Cash is the one loyalty signal in my life that actually speaks to me; as someone who won't run a card without points back, instant-earn is a real value proposition when it's executed clearly.
- The email rendered cleanly — no broken images, no overlapping text, no phantom placeholder copy bleeding through.
- The Deal Days framing is temporally coherent — this isn't a stale promo from February showing up in late June, which is a bar some senders still can't clear.

## What's weak

- "WOW 🎉" in the subject line is doing no one any favors. I am 47 years old. I do not perform emoji enthusiasm about free shipping on a Thursday.
- The asterisk on "$20 Kohl's Cash*" kills the hero offer before I can feel good about it — if there's a spend threshold or exclusion, put it in the copy, not buried at the footer.
- Nothing here is beauty, skincare, or fragrance. Kohl's carries K-Beauty, NYX, Curel — I have bought all three at a Kohl's. This email does not know that about me.
- The section count is punishing: Featured Daily Deals, 6 Days of Deals, Docs Collection, credit card pitch, free shipping callout. This isn't personalization, it's pagination.
- The subject line is 57 characters and functionally identical in structure to the June 25th sends. After thirty-one of these, cadence fatigue isn't a risk — it's already the reality.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill "WOW 🎉" from the subject — the offer sells itself.** Instant Kohl's Cash plus free shipping in a single send is genuinely stacked; it doesn't need game-show punctuation competing with it.
- 2. **Make the Kohl's Cash terms explicit in the hero.** "Spend $50 today, get $20 back instantly" is honest and converts better than a footnote asterisk on a promise.
- 3. **Segment for beauty buyers.** If my purchase history includes beauty, surface it — even one skincare hero image above the apparel block would change this email for me.
- 4. **Cut the email to one featured deal and the Kohl's Cash CTA.** Two sections max. The firehose structure trains subscribers to skim past everything, including the actual offer.
- 5. **Acknowledge the cadence or earn the open.** After thirty-plus sends in a month, give me a reason this specific email is worth stopping for — a category I've bought, a one-hour window, something that differentiates today from yesterday.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Get $20 Kohl's Cash back — today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Today only: earn and spend $20 Kohl's Cash instantly`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping included. No code needed — just shop Deal Days.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Earn $20 instantly when you spend $50 — Deal Days, today only.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "Daily Deals + INSTANT Kohl's Cash" (2026-06-26)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is a well-structured HTML 4.01 table-based build with standard multi-ESP tracking infrastructure (Coherent Path + MessageGears + Adobe Audience Manager). The primary issues are missing alt attributes on tracking/pixel images, unresolvable compliance headers at the relay layer, and a malformed HTML tag in the open-tracker block.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 click-redirect links skipped by QA probe** — all route through `click.s.kohls.com` and `click.chp.kohls.com`. No broken destination URLs were confirmed, but these could not be validated.

**Three separate open-tracking pixels in use simultaneously:**
- `https://click.chp.kohls.com/o/9651f9a6...` (Coherent Path)
- `https://mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif...` (MessageGears)
- `https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?HKAXRARP...` (separate ESP)

Triple-firing open pixels inflates open rate metrics and may cause discrepancies across platforms. Confirm this is intentional deduplication strategy, not a copy-paste error from template assembly.

**Malformed HTML tag in open-tracker block:**
```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>
```
`</custom>` is an invalid closing tag. No matching opening tag exists. Parsers will handle this gracefully, but it signals a template authoring error.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**11 images missing `alt` text** (confirmed by QA). These include both tracking pixels and apparently content images:

| Image | Type | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| `9651f9a6...` (click.chp.kohls.com) | Open pixel | Low — aria-hidden acceptable |
| `o.gif` (mi.kohls.com) | Open pixel | Has `aria-hidden="true"` ✓ |
| `event` (kohls.demdex.net) | AAM pixel | Low |
| `2a5adc5a...`, `f441d45d...`, `c2f81e9a...`, `415c8a18...` (mi.kohls.com/p/rp/) | Likely content images | **High** — if these are product/banner images, screen readers are blind |
| `52819850_roots_2x` (images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/) | Product image | **High** — product name not readable |
| `69494b48...`, `78308d1d...`, `916d6fa08...` | Unknown | Medium |

The `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/52819850_roots_2x` URL pattern confirms at least one is a product image, not a pixel — missing alt text here is a real accessibility failure.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — blocks pinch-zoom on mobile. This is a known accessibility regression; WCAG 1.4.4 requires content to be resizable up to 200%.

**`<title>` tag is empty** — `<title></title>`. Some email clients display this in the tab or subject preview area.

**Google Fonts loaded three times** — `<link>` tag + `<link>` tag + `@import` for the same Roboto and Montserrat URLs. Redundant, adds latency in webfont-capable clients.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)

**`List-Unsubscribe` header not captured** — QA relay did not surface it. If absent at the MTA level, Gmail and Apple Mail will not render the native unsubscribe button. This is a deliverability risk for bulk senders under Google/Yahoo 2024 requirements.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not captured** — RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is required by Google/Yahoo for senders of >5k/day. Absence here is a compliance gap if not present at MTA level.

**No physical mailing address detected in source** — CAN-SPAM §7(d) mandates a valid physical postal address in the email body. This could be in the truncated portion of the HTML, but QA did not find it in what was parsed. Verify it appears above the fold of the footer in the full render.

**`Authentication-Results` header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status is unknown via the relay. Sending domain `s.kohls.com` should have DKIM configured; confirm at MTA.

**`<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow" />` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer" />` are present** — correct practice for email HTML.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM Params, Landing Page Alignment)

All 22 click links route through `click.s.kohls.com` or `click.chp.kohls.com` redirect chains — UTM parameters cannot be confirmed without resolving the redirect chain, which was skipped by QA probe.

The `mi_ecmp` tracking parameter (`1016002_2026626`) is consistently applied to pixel URLs, suggesting a coherent campaign ID, but whether UTM params (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) survive the click redirect to kohls.com cannot be verified from this source alone.

**Recommendation:** Run one sample link through the redirect chain manually to confirm UTM parameters are appended to the final kohls.com destination URL.
