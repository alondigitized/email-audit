# 📣 Epic Deals + 3x rewards for Kohl's Rewards members 📣
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-24**
## Executive summary

- Same red-tile Kohl's wall, eighth or ninth time I'm logging it this week — a handbag hero, "EPIC DEALS" stamped in the same font, and then a scroll of kids' fleece, LEGO, toy trucks, bath towels, jeans, and a floor-care coupon all crammed edge to edge with zero breathing room. I don't shop kids, I don't need a turntable or a Ninja slushie machine, and there's nothing here that reads "beauty counter regular" to me at all — no Sephora at Kohl's tile this time even, which was the one thing giving these sends any relevance to my life.
- The 3x rewards banner up top is the only genuinely useful line in the whole email, and it's buried under the handbag graphic before I even get to it. Compare this to Sephora's "Hair Daily Deals" send from a few days back — that one opened with one specific offer (up to 50% off hair, a real code) and got out of my way. This is the opposite: eleven separate deal tiles, no hierarchy, no "for you" signal anywhere.
- I'm not clicking, I'm not forwarding this to anyone, and honestly at this cadence I'm close to just muting Kohl's notifications altogether — this is a mark-as-read-while-scrolling-past kind of email.

## What's working

- The Kohl's Rewards 3x banner at top is a clear, real loyalty benefit and I did notice it before the tile wall started.
- The handbag/shoes hero image is clean and not overlapping with any text — no render bugs up top.
- "Take $10 off your $50+ purchase" coupon math is spelled out in dollars, which I appreciate over vague "% off select styles" language.

## What's weak

- This is the same "Epic Deals" grid structure I flagged on 9/21, 9/22, and 9/23 — nothing new in layout, just a different product mix, and I'm fatigued on it.
- Zero beauty content this time — even the Sephora at Kohl's tile that showed up in the 9/22 send is gone, so as a beauty-counter shopper there's genuinely nothing here for me.
- Category overload: kids' fleece, LEGO, mattress toppers, floor care, a slushie machine, jeans — this reads like a general-merchandise circular, not a targeted send, and it's the Macy's-style kitchen-sink approach at its worst.
- "Coupons do not apply" shows up twice (athletic shoes, floor care) right next to bold discount numbers — that's the buried-fine-print, bait-and-switch feeling Saks and Neiman would never let through.
- The Hispanic Heritage Month module is sandwiched awkwardly between toy deals and bath towels with no visual transition — feels bolted on rather than intentional.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment me out of the general circular entirely — if Kohl's knows I've engaged with Sephora at Kohl's content before, lead with that, not kids' fleece and LEGO.
- 2. Cut the tile count from eleven down to three or four hero offers — Sephora's single-offer-first approach converts better than this scattershot wall.
- 3. Move "Coupons do not apply" language either off the discount line entirely or into a genuinely small footnote — right now it's undercutting the two boldest numbers on the page.
- 4. Give the Hispanic Heritage Month module its own send or at least a full-width break with padding — right now it looks like it wandered in from a different email.
- 5. Stop reusing the "EPIC DEALS" wall structure send after send — even a different color treatment would signal this isn't the fourth rerun this week.
