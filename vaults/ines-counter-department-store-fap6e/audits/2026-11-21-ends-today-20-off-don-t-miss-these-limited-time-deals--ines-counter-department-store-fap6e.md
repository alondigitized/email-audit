# Ends today: 20% off 👀 Don't miss these limited-time deals ...
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again, and this is the seventh or eighth send since the weekend. Seeing "Ends today" after "HOURS LEFT" on 11/15 and "Take an extra 20% off 🙌" on 11/20, I read it as noise. Every one of these has ended "today" at some point. The image I was given is a tiny, very tall render, so I can read the headlines and prices but not the fine print. Where I say "fine print" below, I mean the part I can see exists but can't read.
- What I can see is a maroon "Super Saturday Deals" banner with a 20% off badge at the top. Under it is a long scroll of small tiles for home, coffee makers, toys, pajamas, bras, jeans, Beats headphones, a Shark face mask and Ninja drinks. It's a flea market with a loyalty card. There's nothing for my beauty-counter habit except one LED face mask. I wouldn't click, and I wouldn't forward it. I'd skim it and mark it read.

## What's working

- The banner is clear: "Super Saturday," a 20% badge, and a Shop Now button above the fold.
- The Kohl's Rewards callouts ("$10 off your home purchase of $50 or more") repeat through the email. That's the closest thing to points back, which I care about.
- Price points are visible and concrete: 21.99, 19.99, 59.99, and the 248.99 Ninja.
- Each tile has its own Shop Now button, so the tap targets look reasonable.
- The "Preview the Deals" block for more Black Friday deals coming soon is a useful signal about what's next.

## What's weak

- It's about 15 stacked tiles, all with the same weight. There's no hero product and no editorial point of view. Nordstrom or Saks would curate five things, not fifty.
- The fine print is everywhere: "select styles," "coupons do not apply," "excludes select brands." That's the kind of exclusion language that makes me feel handled instead of respected.
- It's the wrong category for me. Pajamas, toys, kitchen appliances and a Shark gadget aren't what I shop here for, and there's no beauty section.
- The cadence is exhausting. The 11/20 "extra 20% off" and the 11/21 "Take 20% off | Early Access" sends already used this same 20% hook, so "ends today" has no credibility left.
- The ellipsis at the end of the subject line looks cut off, and the preview text isn't visible.

## Recommendations

- 1. Send fewer emails, each with one real hero. If today really is the last day, say what ends and when, for example "Ends at midnight: 20% off + $10 Kohl's Cash." Then cut the page to 4–5 tiles, not 15.
- 2. Segment by category. Someone who has bought Sephora at Kohl's gets a beauty-led version. Someone who bought home goods gets the Ninja and Shark tiles.
- 3. Put loyalty first: "Rewards members: your extra $10 off is waiting."
- 4. Spell out the exclusions in one visible line near the top, not scattered through the tiles.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Tonight at midnight: 20% off + your $10 Rewards bonus`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day for 20% off at Kohl's, Rewards members get more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your Kohl's Cash and extra 20% both end tonight. Beauty, home and more.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Here's what's actually worth it before the deal ends at 11:59 PM.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Kohl's Super Saturday email

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based layout with `lang="en"` and `role="presentation"` on most layout tables. It has several markup defects (empty `<title>`, a stray `</custom>` tag, an `<div itemscope>` inside `<head>`) and four tracking pixels from different vendors. Many content images lack alt text. The compliance and authentication warnings can't be confirmed from the captured data, because the source is truncated and the relay may have stripped headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage:** 80 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so none of the click destinations were verified. The links use opaque `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7Im...` redirects.
- **Open pixels:** There are four independent open or analytics beacons before the first content table:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/5073203d-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...` (1×1, no `alt`, no `aria-hidden`)
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1026411_20261121`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=...&bmt=0`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager, `width="0" height="0"`)
- **Third-party calls:** The `demdex.net` beacon is a third-party call. Apple Mail Privacy Protection and some clients inflate or suppress these opens, so open data from them is unreliable.
- **Malformed HTML:** The `open.aspx` image is followed by a stray `</custom>` closing tag with no opening tag. This is invalid markup and a parser-quirk risk.
- **Empty favicon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty `href`, which can trigger a request to the document URL in some clients.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** The QA check flagged these images:
  - **Content images:** `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`, plus `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/60699446_4_2x` and `60699446_3_2x`. With images off, or in a screen reader, these are unlabeled.
  - **Tracking pixels:** `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...`, `mi.kohls.com/.../o.gif` and `demdex.net/event` also lack `alt`. These are lower severity, though the `o.gif` pixel already carries `aria-hidden="true"`.
  - The QA list is itself truncated, so there may be more.
- **Empty `<title>`:** `<title></title>` is empty, so the document has no accessible name.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="..., maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 problem.
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. A `<div>` is not permitted there, so parsers will close the head early and move the element and its `<meta>` tags into the body. Email clients don't process schema.org microdata anyway.
- **Missing `role="presentation"`:** The 1×1 tracking tables (`<table width="1" height="1">` and the `<!-- AAM -->` table) have no `role="presentation"`, unlike the main layout tables.
- **Web fonts:** The Google Fonts `@import` and `<link>` are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. Fallbacks (`Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`) are present, so this is fine.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%...%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source.
- `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` in the `916d6fa08d185233.png` URL has an empty value. This may be an unpopulated dynamic field in the Movable Ink image request (redemption end date). If so, the image could render a blank or default state. It can't be confirmed without the rendered image.
- The beacon URLs carry subscriber-level IDs (`mi_u=604230016`, `d_sid=13245196`). This is expected for the platform, but those IDs are exposed in any forwarded or archived copy.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post`:** Both were not found. The QA note says AgentMail may not capture these headers, so this is unconfirmed, not a verified defect.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** Not found. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, and the same relay caveat applies. Header values need to be checked on the raw message.
- **Physical address:** The QA check found none. The HTML is truncated before the footer, so I can't verify this either way.
- **From header:** `Kohls@s.kohls.com` is a branded subdomain, which is consistent with aligned sending.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** None are visible in the captured HTML. The tracked links use opaque `qs=` tokens, so UTM presence and landing-page alignment can't be verified.
- **Campaign ID:** The campaign is identified by `mi_ecmp=1026411_20261121` and `email_name=261121_DG_Email_Saturd...` on the image requests. That is internal, vendor-side attribution, not site-side.
- **Destination checks:** Because 80 links were skipped by the probe, there is no evidence of broken or mismatched destinations. There's also none that the "Ends today" offer's links resolve correctly.
- **Plain-text part:** It is 14,220 of 20,000 captured characters of URLs (about 71%). That is driven by the long encoded `qs=` links and is what trips the >70% warning.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the content images (`mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png`, `60699446_*_2x`). Use `alt=""` plus `aria-hidden="true"` on the tracking pixels.
2. Fill in `<title>`, remove the stray `</custom>`, drop the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon" href="">`, and move or remove the `itemscope` div from `<head>`.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
4. Consolidate the four open beacons, or at least confirm each is needed.
5. Check the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) and the footer's physical address. The current QA warnings can't confirm any of these.
6. Resolve a sample of the `click.s.kohls.com` links with a real redirect-follow, to confirm landing pages and UTM or campaign parameters.
7. Check whether `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` is meant to be empty in the rendered state.
