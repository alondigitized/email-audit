It's the same restraint I liked in the 11/20 "Things We Love" send. It reads like a counter associate saying, "Come see what just landed."

- No countdown clock, no red box, no stacked percentages. After the last week, that respects me.
- The six-tile grid is easy to scan, the images are crisp, and nothing is broken.
- The two outlined buttons, "Shop Etro" and "Shop Designer Collections," are clearly buttons and big enough for a thumb.
- "Match with a personal style advisor" is the one designer-access cue in the email, and I like it.

## What's weak

- There are no prices anywhere. For designer pieces, that's friction, not mystery.
- Every tile caption just says "Etro." I can't tell what any piece is or what it costs.
- Nothing about InCircle points, a gift card, or a member perk. I won't use a card without points back, and this doesn't mention them.
- "Timely Arrival" doesn't say what arrived or why it's timely. The subject promises holiday dressing, but I see no occasion, gifting angle, or dates.

- The footer is cluttered, and "View in a Web Browser" sits at the very bottom, which is odd given the email renders fine.

## Technical Audit: Neiman Marcus, "Etro for the holidays"

### 1. Technical Summary
The visible source has no broken markup, but it ships internal Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) template comments in the delivered HTML and sets `maximum-scale=1`, which blocks pinch-zoom. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the AgentMail relay did not expose the headers. The HTML was truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and body links could not be inspected.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **17 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so none of their destinations, redirect chains or status codes were verified.
- **The plain-text part is mostly URLs.** The QA check reports 3199 of 3691 characters (about 87%) are URLs. That is above the 70% threshold and is consistent with long wrapped tracking URLs in the text alternative. It is a minor spam-scoring risk.
- No broken links were confirmed.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Pinch-zoom is blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables user scaling, which conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **No `lang` attribute.** The tag is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` with no `lang` or `xml:lang`, so screen readers can't select a language.
- **Mobile classes are fragile.** The media queries use exact-match attribute selectors such as `*[class=contenttable]`, `*[class=mobile]` and `*[class=hide]`. They stop matching if an element carries more than one class (for example `class="mobile hide"`). Whether that happens in the body could not be checked because the source was truncated.
- **The hamburger nav depends on the checkbox hack.** It uses `.navbar-checkbox:checked~.menu` and `.mobileCont:checked`. That technique is unsupported in Gmail and several other clients, so the nav and interactive content only work in clients that honor it. Whether a non-interactive fallback exists is not visible in the truncated source.
- **The `<title>` has a trailing space** (`Neiman Marcus `) and carries no email-specific text. This is trivial.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Internal template code is left in the delivered HTML.** The first comment in the source reads `REQUIRED variables: @email … Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", "EMAIL_ID", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email) … Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "uEm", …) … @cmdid …`. It exposes data extension names, field names and the CRM feed structure to anyone who views source. It also contains a stray unbalanced parenthesis (`@email))`) and the line `<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->`, which suggests an unfinished scenario selection.
- **That comment sits before the DOCTYPE.** Comments ahead of the DOCTYPE can trigger quirks mode in some older IE-based renderers.
- **No unresolved tokens in the visible portion.** No `%%…%%` or `@var` tokens appear in rendered content. The rest could not be checked.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` was not found.** The QA tool notes the relay may have stripped it, so its absence is unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` was not found** (RFC 8058 one-click). The same caveat applies. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `NeimanMarcus@e.neimanmarcus.com`, a subdomain, and nothing can be said about its alignment.
- **The footer was not reviewed.** The physical address and in-body unsubscribe link fall outside the truncated source, so no CAN-SPAM conclusion is possible.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The 17 links go through tracking/redirect domains and were not resolved, so no final destination URLs, UTM parameters or landing-page alignment could be checked. The source references `@refid` and `@base_refid` campaign IDs, but the actual link parameters are not visible.

### 7. Recommendations
1. **Strip the SFMC variable-declaration comments** from the delivered HTML (use `%%[ ]%%` blocks or server-side comments), and move the DOCTYPE to the first line.
2. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag. Keep `width=device-width, initial-scale=1`.
3. **Add `lang="en"`** (and `xml:lang`) to `<html>`.
4. **Switch to `*[class~=…]` or `.class` selectors** in the media queries, and confirm the hamburger nav has a fallback for clients without checkbox-hack support.
5. **Re-run the audit against the raw RFC822 message** to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `e.neimanmarcus.com`.
6. **Resolve the 17 tracking links** with a redirect-following check to verify destinations and UTM/refid parameters.
7. **Shorten the URLs in the plain-text part** or add more readable text, to reduce the URL-to-text ratio.
8. **Audit the full HTML**, including the footer, for the unsubscribe link, postal address and unrendered tokens.
