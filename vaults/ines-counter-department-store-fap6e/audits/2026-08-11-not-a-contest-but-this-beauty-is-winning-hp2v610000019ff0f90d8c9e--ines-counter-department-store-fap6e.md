# Not a contest, but this beauty is winning 🏅
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-11**
## Executive summary

- "You have 0 points" sitting right under the INSIDER banner is a rough way to open — I'm a member, remind me of my tier or my last purchase, don't lead with a null. From there this is Sephora doing what Sephora does: a "Hot Right Now" hero for Phlur Vanilla Canyon, then a "Trending Now" grid that's basically eight products with no organizing logic beyond "these exist." It's the same skeleton as the "art of body care" send from August 6th and the "Ready, set, shop" one from August 8th — different SKUs, same bones, and I've now gotten four of these in a week.
- The Free Foundation and Concealer Samples block near the bottom is honestly the most useful thing in here — "pick 3 with $25+ spend" is concrete and matches how I actually shop counters, sampling before I commit to a $60 foundation. If that had been the hero instead of buried past a dozen perfume thumbnails, I'd have opened faster and scrolled less.
- I'm not clicking past a maybe-scroll to the sample offer, and I'm not forwarding this — there's nothing here surprising enough to share. Marking as read.

## What's working

- "Free Foundation and Concealer Samples, pick 3 with $25+ spend" is a real, specific offer I'd actually use — better than anything Kohl's or Neiman's has sent me lately, which just wave percentages around.
- The "CLEAN AT SEPHORA" badge on Vanilla Canyon and The 7 Virtues is a trust cue I actually register — I do check ingredient philosophy before I buy fragrance.
- Product cards are clean — name, description, price, no clutter — easier to scan than Macy's confirm-now email I looked at, which buried the mechanics.
- Loyalty tier bar (Insider/VIB/Rouge) at the bottom is at least present, even if underused.

## What's weak

- "You have 0 points" up top is borderline insulting for a member — it reads like a system default nobody bothered to gate.
- "Hot right now" and "Trending Now" is vague, generic hype copy — no reason given why these are trending, no social proof, no review counts.
- Eight products in a grid with zero personalization — nothing here says "loyalty member" or "based on what you bought," it's just inventory.
- The sample offer, which is the actual best reason to open this email, is stranded near the bottom after a wall of perfume I have to scroll through on my phone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the free sample offer, not "Hot Right Now" — that's the one thing here with a real CTA tied to a real threshold ($25+ spend), move it to the hero slot.
- 2. Fix "0 points" — either hide it for members with a balance or replace it with something like "You're $12 from a reward" so it feels like a real account, not a placeholder.
- 3. Cut the Trending Now grid from 8 to 4 and add one line of why ("loved by Rouge members," "sold out twice this month") — Neiman Marcus at least gave me a striking hero graphic to earn the scroll; this is just a catalog dump.
- 4. Tie the grid to my purchase history if this is meant to be personalized — right now it reads like every other subscriber got the identical email.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Match your shade free with any $25 order — no strings.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The fragrance everyone's asking about, and a gift for you.`
