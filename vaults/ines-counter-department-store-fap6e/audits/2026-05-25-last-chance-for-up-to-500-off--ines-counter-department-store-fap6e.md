# LAST CHANCE for up to $500 off!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-25**
## Executive summary

- Fourteen. That's where the Neiman Marcus count sits in roughly six days, and the well is officially running dry. "LAST CHANCE for up to $500 off" — except I already saw the exact same tiered spend-and-save grid on the 24th with "Kick off summer with 25% off + extra 40% off sale styles," and the 23rd Private Designer Sale, and the China Chow editorial in between. The hero is a flat blue panel with a code (ENJOY) and a spend tier table: $250→$50 off, $500→$100, $750→$150, $1,000→$250, $2,000+→$500. That's an aggressive minimum just to unlock the headline number.
- The product grid below — Jimmy Choo, Lela Rose, WOLF, Stuart Weitzman, LANA, Chloé, Jacquemus, Cinq à Sept — is the same designer carousel they've been recycling. Nothing told me what's NEW today vs. yesterday's send. Sephora's recent "Save up to 50% on select beauty 💄 Plus spend $75 to earn 500 bonus points" was honest about the threshold in the subject; Neiman buries the spend ladder until you're inside, then asks me to drop a grand to get into the meaty discount tier.
- Would I click? No. I've already seen this offer twice. Forward to a friend? Absolutely not — fatigue is fatigue. Mark as read and move on.

## What's working

- The tier table itself is clean and legible — I can scan the spend/save grid in one glance, which is more than Kohl's "$10 off $25" stacking usually gives me.
- Code `ENJOY` is short and memorable; I won't fat-finger it at checkout.
- The "Match with a personal style advisor" footer module is the one piece of real Neiman differentiation — that's white-glove territory Macy's can't touch.
- Designer roster (Jimmy Choo, Chloé, Stuart Weitzman) signals the right tier — no Skechers-adjacent house brands sneaking in.

## What's weak

- Subject says "$500 off" but you have to spend $2,000+ to get it. That's bait. Saks would never lead with the ceiling number without context.
- Fourteenth send in six days. The Private Designer Sale on 5/23 was an 8/10 for me — this is the same audience, lower energy, recycled creative.
- "LAST CHANCE" has lost all meaning. They said the same thing on 5/24 about Memorial Day. Which last chance is the actual last chance?
- The product grid feels like inventory dumping — no story, no edit, no "here's the dress for your Memorial Day cocktail." Sephora at least curates by category.
- Header navigation is WOMEN / MEN / SHOES / SALE — generic, no personalization for a beauty regular like me. Neiman has my purchase history; use it.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Stop the "LAST CHANCE" inflation.** If everything is last chance, nothing is. Reserve it for genuine final-hour sends and put a real countdown timer in.
- 2. **Lead with the achievable tier**, not the ceiling. "$50 off $250" is the entry point most subscribers hit — make THAT the hero, then show the ladder up.
- 3. **Curate the product grid into a story** — "Memorial Day cocktail," "summer wedding guest," "vacation packing list." The designer roster is wasted as a checkerboard.
- 4. **Segment by recent browse.** I'm a beauty-counter regular — show me Chantecaille, La Mer, By Terry, not handbags I never opened.
- 5. **Cut send frequency.** Two thoughtful sends a week beats fourteen forgettable ones.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Ines, your spend-tier sale ends tonight — pick your tier`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack with Memorial Day savings. Designer shoes, dresses & bags.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Code ENJOY at checkout. Five tiers, ending midnight PT.`

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- AMPscript variable block at the top of the source is **leaking into the rendered HTML** — the `REQUIRED variables` block is wrapped in a standard HTML comment (`<!-- ... -->`) rather than an AMPscript comment (`%%[ /* ... */ ]%%`) or block. This means the template documentation/scaffolding is shipped to the inbox inside comments. 2. **Verify auth headers on a raw `.eml`** — confirm whether the missing `Authentication-Results` header is an AgentMail capture gap or a real SPF/DKIM/DMARC issue at the `e.neimanmarcus.com` sending domain.

4. **Move the `REQUIRED variables` scaffolding** out of HTML comments and into an AMPscript block (`%%[ /* ... */ ]%%`) so internal Data Extension field names (`NM_CRM_Feed`, `cmdid`, `uEm`) don't ship to inboxes.
5. **Fix unmatched parentheses and the missing `=`** in the documented `Lookup` calls so the scaffolding is correct if ever activated.
