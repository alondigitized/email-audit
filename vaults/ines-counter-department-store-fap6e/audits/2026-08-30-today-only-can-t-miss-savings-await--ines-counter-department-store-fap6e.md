# TODAY ONLY: Can't-miss savings await 👀
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-30**
## Executive summary

- Denim Deal Days — okay, at least Kohl's swapped out the wallpaper this time. After ten straight days of that blue Friends & Family starburst, this is a genuinely different layout: jeans starting at $21.99, a Levi's callout, a "Denim Guide" module, then the usual back-half sprawl into bedding, bath, a Ninja slushie machine, and mattress toppers. My eye actually landed on the Levi's block for half a second because that's a name with weight — everything else is Kohl's private labels I don't reach for.
- But "TODAY ONLY" screaming at me three times in a giant maroon banner, followed immediately by jeans that are on sale "now through Sept. 2," is the kind of urgency mismatch that makes me trust the brand less, not more. Neiman Marcus's dress email last week worked because it didn't lean on fake urgency at all — just showed me product. This leans on it and then contradicts itself in the very next scroll.
- I'm not clicking. This is a straight archive. Nothing here is beauty-counter, designer, or event-tier — it's basement-level denim and home goods with a 47-year-old woman's Amanda Classic Jeans at $26 tossed in as an afterthought. Not forwarding.

## What's working

- The "TODAY ONLY" hero is at least a genuinely different creative from the ten days of blue Friends & Family banners — some visual variety, finally.
- Levi's up to 30% off is a recognizable name I'd actually stop for, unlike the store-brand jeans surrounding it.
- Rewards status bar at the very top ("KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER · Earn 5% REWARDS") acknowledges I'm a member before I even scroll.
- Clear nav pills (Home/Women/Men/Kids) repeated twice give me an easy tap target if I did want to browse.

## What's weak

- "TODAY ONLY" tripled in giant text, then the jeans sale explicitly runs "through Sept. 2" — that's not urgency, that's a lie I can see with my own eyes.
- The email is scattered across five unrelated categories (denim, home, bath, kitchen appliances, mattress toppers) with no through-line — this is Macy's-style overload, not a curated event.
- The one item actually in my size wheelhouse, Gloria Vanderbilt jeans at $26-$29.99, is buried two-thirds down next to a bath towel and a frozen drink maker.
- "Coupons do not apply to select brands" shows up twice — so the one recognizable name (Levi's) is exactly the one likely excluded from extra savings, which feels like bait-and-switch.
- Extra 30% off code at the bottom with "details" fine print is the same trick Macy's ran in their "LAST CHANCE" confirm email — dangle a bigger number, gate it behind terms.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story and cut the rest — either this is a denim event or a home event, not five departments stapled together. Sephora's Daily Deals at least kept it to one sentence up top even when the offer was thin.
- 2. Fix the urgency lie — don't say "TODAY ONLY" over a sale that runs another three days. Say "Denim Deal Days — through Sept. 2" and save real urgency language for things that are actually ending today.
- 3. Move the Levi's block and the Gloria Vanderbilt jeans up top, since those are the two products with actual brand or size relevance to me — don't make me scroll past mattress toppers to find them.
- 4. Disclose the Levi's coupon exclusion right next to the Levi's offer, not two scrolls later — right now it reads like a switch.
