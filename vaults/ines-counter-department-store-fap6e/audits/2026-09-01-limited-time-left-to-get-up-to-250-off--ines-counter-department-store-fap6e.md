# Limited time left to get up to $250 off
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-01**
## Executive summary

- Well, they're back at it — mango banner and all. This is basically a rerun of the 8/26 tiered box with the same "spend $400, save $100 / spend $1,000, save $250" math homework, just recolored into that pale-yellow "SHOP FALL" wrapper I saw on 8/27. I've now watched this exact offer cycle through at least three creative variations in a week, and my patience for the tiered-math table is wearing thin — I shouldn't have to do arithmetic to know if I'm getting a deal.
- That said, the product grid underneath redeems it a little. Jimmy Choo tote, rag & bone velvet flats, a Chiara Boni gown — that's a legitimate mix that signals "you shop here for real fashion," not just markdowns. But then it's undercut by the fine print: "does not apply to beauty, fragrance... or any other discount offer." So the one category I actually care about — the counter — is explicitly excluded. That's a direct miss for someone like me.
- I'd open it out of habit since I know the sender, but I wouldn't click past the fine print once I clocked the beauty exclusion, and I'm not forwarding this — it's the same saga my friends have probably already seen twice this week.

## What's working

- The SHOP NOW button and code are unmissable — big black button, bold "SHOPFALL" code, no scavenger hunt.
- Nine well-chosen products (Jimmy Choo, Chiara Boni, Akris, Lanvin) keep the luxury tone intact even inside a discount email.
- Clean masthead and nav bar feel like Neiman's, not a blowout retailer — no clutter up top.
- "Ends 9/3/26 at 10:00 PM CT" gives a real, credible deadline instead of vague urgency.

## What's weak

- This is the same tiered $400/$1,000 math table I already saw on 8/26 and again with the mango banner on 8/27 — nothing new, just a color swap.
- Fine print excludes beauty and fragrance entirely, which is the one category that would actually pull me in — Sephora's 8/31 email led with "$5 off at 250 points, 4x points on top brands" and that's a real beauty hook; this has none.
- No loyalty/InCircle tier mention anywhere — Saks Fifth Avenue at least gestures at "designer access" tone even when it misses; this just feels like a generic percent-off blast.
- Tap targets on the six bottom category buttons (Women's Clothing, Men's, Shoes, etc.) are small and stacked tight — easy to fat-finger the wrong one on a phone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Retire the tiered math table — I'm tired of doing homework. Lead with one clean number ("$100–$250 off") and let the code do the rest.
- 2. Either drop the beauty/fragrance exclusion from this send or don't market it to a beauty-counter shopper at all — right now it reads as bait I can't actually use.
- 3. Add an InCircle loyalty callout (points multiplier, member-early-access) the way Sephora did with its 4x points beat on 8/31 — gives me a reason beyond the discount.
- 4. Space out the bottom category CTA buttons for easier mobile tapping.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your last few days for $100–$250 off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Fall favorites, up to $250 off — ends 9/3`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Akris, Jimmy Choo, rag & bone and more — shop before it's gone`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Code SHOPFALL applies in-store and online through Thursday`
