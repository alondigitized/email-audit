# 15% off + summer style upgrades ... we're in 😎
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-11**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Kohl's. I recognize the name, I've probably been in one when I needed a quick throw rug — but it is absolutely not in rotation with Nordstrom and Saks. The moment I opened this I got that familiar "mass blast" feeling: three different customer profiles shoved into one scroll. Women's fashion at the top, fine. Then I hit a section devoted to soccer jerseys and MLB fan gear and I genuinely checked whether I'd opened the wrong email.
- The Kohl's Cash mention grabbed me briefly — I will always clock a points or rewards mechanic — but then I looked at the numbers. Nine dollars and change for a top. Thirty-four for a dress. I'm not mad at a deal, but this is not my store, and nothing in here signals that they know me at all. The subject line with the sunglasses emoji reads like someone on the marketing team is 25 and workshopping a vibe.
- Would I click? Maybe on the 15% off link if I needed something fast and domestic. Would I forward to a friend? Hard no. I'd mark this read and move on.

## What's working

- The Kohl's Cash callout at the bottom — "$10 for every $50 spent through May 12" — is exactly the kind of loyalty mechanic that gets my attention. Real number, real deadline.
- "A full calendar calls for a full closet" is genuinely solid hero copy. It's seasonal, it's aspirational, and it doesn't scream discount.
- The 15% off is a concrete, legible number in the offer strip — not buried in vague language.
- The women's fashion imagery at the top section is clean and appropriately summery. The floral dress and hat read as warm-weather lifestyle without being overdone.

## What's weak

- The sports fan gear section — soccer, MLB, NCAA, NFL — is an enormous section that has absolutely nothing to do with me. It makes the email feel like a Sunday circular, not a curated send.
- The 15% off is the subject line's lead offer, but it's placed at the very bottom of the email. That's a hierarchy mismatch that dilutes the whole message.
- No beauty content whatsoever. I'm a beauty-counter regular; if Kohl's sells makeup or skincare, there's no sign of it here.
- The "we're in 😎" subject line tone skews young and casual in a way that doesn't feel like it's speaking to me — it's the kind of phrasing I'd hear from my daughter, not a brand trying to earn my loyalty.
- The price points ($9.25, $34.99) signal a price tier well below where I shop, and it reads as a mismatch even if the discount math could work in my favor.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the offer in the hero** — move the 15% off strip or a version of that offer to directly under or alongside the fashion hero. The subject promises a deal; the email should deliver it before I scroll past the nav bar.
- 2. **Segment the sports gear into a separate send** — or drop it entirely for any list segment that maps to women's fashion/beauty buyers. Its presence here wrecks the coherence for me.
- 3. **Add a beauty or skincare callout** if Kohl's carries those categories. Even a single section would signal "we know what you shop." The total absence is a missed connection.
- 4. **Soften the emoji** — the sunglasses emoji in the subject reads as performative casual. Drop it or replace with something less age-coded.
- 5. **Personalize the opener** — even "Hi Ines" at the top of the email would shift this from blast to communication.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Summer's here. Take 15% off women's + earn Kohl's Cash`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New arrivals in dresses, tops, and shoes — plus earn $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Offer ends May 12 — shop women's now and stack your Kohl's Cash`

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "15% off + summer style upgrades" (2026-05-11)

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is structurally sound for broad ESP compatibility but carries three compliance gaps (missing unsubscribe headers, unknown authentication status) and a pattern of missing alt attributes on what appear to be content images, not just tracking pixels. The AgentMail relay is obscuring headers that mailbox providers will evaluate for deliverability.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 click-tracked links unverifiable.** All outbound links are wrapped through `click.chp.kohls.com` (Coherent Path) and `click.s.kohls.com` (Dotdigital). Final destination URLs and UTM parameters cannot be confirmed without following the redirect chain.

**Four distinct tracking systems are firing:**

| System | Domain | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Coherent Path | `click.chp.kohls.com` | Open + click pixel |
| Messagegears | `mi.kohls.com` | Open pixel + render pixels |
| Dotdigital | `click.s.kohls.com` | Open pixel |
| Adobe AAM | `kohls.demdex.net` | Audience sync pixel |

**Malformed HTML on Dotdigital open pixel:**
```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?OVYMVIAN4L6E3BWGGIXWBN2CYI.60259..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>
```
Stray `</custom>` closing tag with no matching opener. Technically invalid; most clients will silently ignore it, but it indicates template assembly error.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — disables pinch-to-zoom on mobile. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) violation and is hostile to users who require zoom for readability.
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />
```

**Empty `<title>` tag** — `<title></title>` provides no context in VoiceOver or screen reader previews.

**Seven content images missing alt text** (confirmed by QA, not tracking pixels):
- `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`
- `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/f441d45d2128ae53.png`
- `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`
- `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/415c8a18fc2950ea.png`
- `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/69494b48a79b4240.png`
- `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/78308d1d9adb0751.png`
- `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/916d6fa08d185233.png`

These carry `email_name=260511_DG_Email_Monday` query params, confirming they are rendered content images, not decorative pixels. Gmail image-off mode will render these as blank.

**Google Fonts double-loaded** — both `<link>` tags and `@import` rules request Roboto and Montserrat from `fonts.googleapis.com`. This causes duplicate network requests and is redundant.

**Empty favicon href:**
```html
<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">
```
Will trigger a 404 or no-op. Minor, but indicates template boilerplate was not cleaned up.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens or broken placeholder syntax (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `*|FNAME|*`) visible in the truncated source. The `mi_u=604230016` parameter in Messagegears pixels appears to be a resolved subscriber ID, which is expected. No issues found in the visible source.

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header absent** — QA confirms this header was not captured by the AgentMail relay. CAN-SPAM requires a functional unsubscribe mechanism; the header is also required by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders (≥5k/day threshold). If the header exists on the originating ESP envelope but is stripped by the relay, this needs relay configuration review.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent** — RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe not present. Gmail and Yahoo have enforced this for bulk senders since February 2024. Missing this header degrades spam classification standing.

**`Authentication-Results` header not found** — SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status is unknown due to relay interception. The sending domain `s.kohls.com` should have a published DKIM record; this must be verified at the relay layer. Without confirmed DKIM pass, inbox placement is at risk.

**`meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer"`** — suppresses the HTTP `Referer` header on all link navigations. Any server-side referrer logging on landing pages will receive null. This may be intentional (privacy policy compliance) but should be cross-checked with the analytics team.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All 22 outbound links redirect through `click.chp.kohls.com` or `click.s.kohls.com`. Final landing page URLs are not inspectable from the source. **UTM parameter presence on landing pages cannot be confirmed** without resolving the redirect chains.

The Messagegears render pixels include `email_name=260511_DG_Email_Monday` — campaign naming is consistent and machine-readable, which is good for internal attribution.

The `no-referrer` meta policy (noted above) means organic referrer attribution to `kohls.com` landing pages is suppressed for users who do not click a tracked link.
