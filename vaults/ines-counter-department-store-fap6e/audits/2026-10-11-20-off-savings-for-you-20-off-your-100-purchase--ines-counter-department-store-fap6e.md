# 20% off savings for you 😍 $20 off your $100+ purchase 🤩
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-11**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again. This is the fifth send since 10/8, and the top is the strongest part. It shows "Take an extra 20% off with coupon, ends Oct. 18" beside "$20 off your purchase of $100 or more, ends Oct. 12." Under that sits a green "Plus, earn Kohl's Cash" strip, and then a status line, "Status: Kohl's Rewards Member," with 5% back. I noticed it because it treats me like a known member. Neiman's did the same thing on 10/6 with its $200-off ladder, but this is a little clearer.
- The rest is the usual long-scroll department store dump: pumpkin pillows, bedding, Halloween décor, a slow cooker, Cuddl Duds, a blender and jeans. It's a lot like Macy's on a loud day. Nothing in it is for a beauty-counter person. Sephora at Kohl's gets a tile in the category grid and one line in the footer, and that's all. I'd skim it, maybe screenshot the code, and move on. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The two offers at the top are clear: 20% with a coupon through Oct. 18, and $20 off $100 through Oct. 12. Both have dates.
- The "Status: Kohl's Rewards Member" line and the Kohl's Cash strip tell me I'm known. That's the loyalty acknowledgment I want from a card.
- The coupon codes with barcodes at the bottom are easy to use. They're the same codes I'll need at the register.
- The "Free returns through January 31, 2027" line is a real trust cue. It matters to me because I return things.
- The category buttons (Home, Women, Men, Kids) sit right under the hero, so I can pick a lane fast.

## What's weak

- It's a dozen modules with no single hero. The eye bounces from pumpkin pillows to bedding to Halloween to appliances.
- Beauty is nearly absent. Sephora at Kohl's is one small tile, and the Kohl's Rewards line says "Not valid on Sephora at Kohl's." That's the fine print I read first.
- The 20% and $20 off both hit on Saturday and Sunday, and this is the fifth near-identical send since 10/8. It's the same pitch as "Last chance ⏳ Shop Kohl's Deal Days." I've stopped believing in the urgency.
- Product cards at the bottom show prices, like $299.99 for the Ninja and $29.99 for the jeans, but with no ratings or reviews. I can't tell whether $137.99 for a mattress topper is a deal.
- The fine print is a dense wall at the bottom. "Coupons do not apply to select brands" sits in a small line under the product tiles, where it's easy to miss.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero for a member like me and put the 20% and $20 offers above it. Right now the email has no focal point after the top strip.
- 2. Segment by category. If I've bought beauty, show me a Sephora at Kohl's block with a points-back message, the way Sephora Insider leads with "4X points." Right now there's nothing for me.
- 3. Put the exclusions next to the offers. Say what the coupon doesn't cover in the same line as the 20%.
- 4. Cut the cadence. Five sends in four days teaches me to wait for the next one.
- 5. Add star ratings to the product picks.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Codes inside for members. Free returns through Jan. 31.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based layout with a mostly sound responsive structure. It has several markup defects (invalid `<head>` content, a stray `</custom>` tag, an empty `<title>`) and heavy tracking-pixel stacking. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the relay and the truncated source, so they can't be confirmed as real failures.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Links can't be verified.** QA skipped 69 tracking links (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=...`), so destinations, redirect chains and status codes are untested.
- **Four open or analytics pixels fire before any content:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/775816d5-...`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`
  
  These are redundant and add latency and blocked-image failure points. The `kohls.demdex.net` call is Adobe Audience Manager.
- **Malformed pixel markup:** the `open.aspx` image ends in `alt="">` followed by a stray `</custom>`, which has no matching opening tag.
- **Subscriber ID in URLs:** `mi_u=604230016` appears in image URLs (`mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=604230016&...`). It is an identifier exposed in every image request, and it is forwardable.
- **Possibly empty parameters:** `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` is empty on at least one image URL. It may be an unresolved dynamic value, but the source is truncated, so this is unconfirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` prevents pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>`.
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` with nested `<meta>` tags sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will close the head early and may push it into the body.
- **Empty icon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`.
- **Broken selector:** `ReadMsgBody { width:100% }` is missing its leading `.`, so it matches nothing. It is a legacy Outlook.com rule and low impact.
- **Doctype mismatch:** the HTML 4.01 doctype is mixed with XHTML-style self-closing `/>` tags. This is harmless but inconsistent.
- **Missing `alt`, real:**
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png` and `916d6fa08d185233.png`
  - `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/58379268_2_2x`
  
  These look like content images, and a screen reader or images-off client gets nothing from them.
- **Missing `alt` on tracking pixels:** `click.chp.kohls.com/o/775816d5-...` and `kohls.demdex.net/event` have no `alt` attribute. They should have `alt=""` so assistive tech skips them.
- **QA false positive:** it flagged `o.gif`, but that tag already has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`.
- **Fonts:** Google Fonts `@import` and `<link>` are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]>`, and the font stacks fall back to Arial. No issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens were found in the visible source or the subject line. The only suspect is the empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` parameter noted above. The rest of the body is truncated, so a full token scan wasn't possible.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** QA reports both missing. It attributes this to the AgentMail relay stripping headers, so it is not confirmed as a sender defect. Kohl's is a large sender and likely sends them.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From address is `Kohls@s.kohls.com`, a subdomain. Verify alignment against the raw headers.
- **Physical address:** QA found none. The footer falls outside the truncated source, so this is unverified. Check the full HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- All destinations are wrapped in opaque `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=` tokens, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be assessed from the source.
- Campaign tagging is visible only in image URLs: `email_name=261011_DG_Email_Sunday` and `mi_ecmp=1023674_20261011`.
- The subject promises "20% off" and "$20 off your $100+ purchase". Confirming that both offers land correctly needs a resolved click-through.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the content images listed in section 3. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` and `kohls.demdex.net/event`.
3. Move the `itemscope` div out of `<head>`, delete the stray `</custom>`, fill in `<title>`, and drop the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon">`.
4. Fix `ReadMsgBody` to `.ReadMsgBody`.
5. Consolidate the four tracking pixels, or confirm each is needed.
6. Check the empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` parameter against the full source.
7. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and raw headers. That would confirm List-Unsubscribe, the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results, the footer address, and the resolved click-through URLs with their UTMs.
