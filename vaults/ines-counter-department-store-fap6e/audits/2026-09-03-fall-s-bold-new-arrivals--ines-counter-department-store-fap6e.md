## What's working

- The black "BRAND NEW STYLES / JUST LANDED" hero is elegant and on-brand — reads like an editorial spread, not a shouty sale banner.
- Clear men's/women's split with distinct "Shop All New Arrivals" buttons — easy to skip to what I care about.
- Product grid is clean, well-lit, no render bugs, and designer names (Valentino Garavani, Chloe, Balmain) are legible trust cues for someone who cross-shops Saks and Nordstrom too.
- Restrained, no clutter in the footer — app download and personal style advisor call-out feel appropriately upscale.

## What's weak

- Zero offer, zero price, zero percentage anywhere — after last week's $250-off saga, going cold-turkey to "no offer at all" is jarring, not refreshing.
- No loyalty/InCircle points mention at all, which is a miss for someone who refuses to use a card without points back.
- No beauty content whatsoever — this is 100% apparel/accessories, so if I'm here for the counter, this email has nothing for me.
- "Brand New Styles" is generic — could be any luxury retailer's email any week; nothing here says fall, nothing says event, nothing says exclusive.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a loyalty hook — even a single line like "InCircle members: earn double points on new arrivals this week" would give me a reason beyond aesthetics.
- 2. Put at least one price or "just landed, up to X% off select styles" somewhere near the hero — total blank on pricing reads like window-shopping, not marketing.
- 3. Segment or at least gesture toward beauty/fragrance new arrivals if Neiman's has any — right now this is a men's/women's apparel-only send that ignores half their assortment.
- 4. Give the hero a seasonal or event anchor (a specific fall trend name, a stylist quote, an in-store event date) the way the 9/1 Cleo Wade send did — "just landed" alone has no story.
