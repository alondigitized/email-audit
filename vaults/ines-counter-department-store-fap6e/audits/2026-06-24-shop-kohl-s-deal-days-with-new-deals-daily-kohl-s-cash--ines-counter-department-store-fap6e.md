# Shop Kohl's Deal Days with new deals daily & Kohl's Cash 🥳
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-24**
## Executive summary

- Twenty-eight. I didn't even need to check — I knew this was another Kohl's Deal Days email before I tapped it open. The subject dropped in alongside yesterday's double-send, the day before's double-send, and on and on back through most of June. By the time I'm reading the words "new deals daily," my eyes are already glazing over. The promise of freshness is undermined by the exact same red DEAL DAYS banner I've seen in every send since the 21st.
- What's inside the email is a department-store everything-must-go situation: women's tees, kitchen appliances, children's clothes, patriotic-print bedding, towels, more tees. There's no through-line — just squares of product stacked against other squares of product, each whispering "DAILY DEAL" or flashing a 40% off badge. I'm a Kohl's cardholder. I do cross-shop here. But when an email looks and feels like a circulars insert from the Sunday paper, I treat it like one — I scan for maybe three seconds and move on.
- I won't click. I won't forward this. My friend Sarah, who actually loves a Kohl's home deal, might get a text from me saying "they have bedding at 40%" but she's not getting a forwarded email — there's nothing here with enough editorial lift to be worth sharing.

## What's working

- **Kohl's Cash is called out early.** As a cardholder who literally refuses to shop without points back, seeing "Kohl's Cash" in the subject and presumably in the body is the one hook that keeps me engaged for an extra beat.
- **"8 Days of Deals" sets a clear countdown.** That's a real deadline with a real shape — it doesn't feel like the fabricated ENDS TONIGHT panic from the June 21 send.
- **Pricing is legible.** The $19.99, $9.00, $5.99 price callouts are clearly typeset and easy to scan on mobile — Neiman Marcus could take notes on making prices readable, though obviously they're playing a different game entirely.
- **No render bugs.** Images loaded clean, no overlapping text, layout didn't break — that's a floor, but it's a floor some brands still miss.

## What's weak

- **No beauty, full stop.** I'm a beauty-counter regular. There is not a single skincare, fragrance, or cosmetics product visible in this email. Sephora would never send a 47-year-old beauty subscriber a hero about kitchen appliances and patriotic towels.
- **The visual hierarchy is chaos.** My eye lands everywhere at once — three "Shop Now" buttons visible without scrolling, a "Featured Daily Deals" block competing with an "8 Days of Deals" block competing with individual product pods. Neiman Marcus's "chicest shoulder bags" send earlier this month made me stop because there was *one* thing to look at.
- **The cadence has destroyed the offer's credibility.** This is email 28 (roughly) in June. When I got the "40% off ENDS TONIGHT" message on June 21 and it was followed the same night by another Kohl's send, I stopped believing any of the urgency language. At this rate "daily deals" just means "same deal, new send."
- **The lifestyle photography is generic and demographically scattered.** I see children's models, what looks like a twenty-something in athleisure, and home textiles — nothing that signals this is for a woman like me who shops their Lauren Conrad or Simply Vera lines.
- **Kohl's Cash details are invisible.** The subject promises Kohl's Cash but I can't see the earn rate, spend threshold, or redemption window in the hero — the thing that actually motivates me to add to cart.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Segment this list immediately.** Send me a beauty and women's apparel version. Send the kitchen + kids version to someone else. I will start filtering Kohl's to promotions if I keep receiving circular-style everything-dumps.
- 2. **Lead with Kohl's Cash terms upfront.** "Earn $X Kohl's Cash per $X spent — 8 days only" in a single hero line would make me calculate and commit. Right now it's mentioned and then buried.
- 3. **Cut the email in half.** Pick three hero products for this audience — one apparel, one beauty (add beauty!), one home — and give each breathing room. Macy's drowns you in product pods too, and it's not a compliment.
- 4. **Calm the cadence.** One email per day during Deal Days, one send per type of offer. The June 21 double-send torched my trust — I'm still carrying that.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Earn on every buy. Beauty, fashion & home deals refresh each day.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New deals drop daily — here's what's worth your tap today.`

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "Deal Days" Email

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is a well-structured HTML 4.01 Transitional document with standard ESP infrastructure (Coherent Path + MessageGears). Core issues center on compliance gaps surfaced by the AgentMail relay (missing headers, no physical address detected) and pervasive missing alt text across tracking and content images.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **22 tracking/click links skipped** by HTTP probe — all route through `click.chp.kohls.com` (Coherent Path) and `click.s.kohls.com` (MessageGears). Link resolution correctness is unverifiable without live redirect tracing.
- **Three distinct pixel trackers** in the `<body>` before visible content:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` — Coherent Path open pixel
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...` — MessageGears user pixel
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...` — MessageGears open tracker
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?...` — Adobe Audience Manager segment pixel
- The `click.s.kohls.com` pixel contains a **malformed closing tag**: `</custom>` instead of `</div>`. This is an HTML error that could trip strict parsers but is likely ignored by webmail clients.
- No broken destination URLs confirmed (probe skipped on redirect domains — cannot clear).

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **11 images missing `alt` text**, including:
  - The Coherent Path open pixel (`ce6f77fd...`) — low severity for a tracking pixel, but AT scanners will flag it
  - `o.gif` (MessageGears pixel) — has `aria-hidden="true"` applied, which is correct; however the alt attribute itself is empty (`alt=""`), which is acceptable for decorative/tracking pixels
  - Six content images served from `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/` with `email_name=260624_DG_Email_Wednesd` — these appear to be **personalized recommendation images**; missing alt text means screen readers get no product context
  - The Adobe AAM pixel (`kohls.demdex.net/event`) — no alt, no aria-hidden
- `<title>` element is **empty** (`<title></title>`). Screen readers announce the title on load; an empty title is a minor accessibility gap.
- `user-scalable=0` in the viewport meta prevents zoom on mobile — an accessibility concern (WCAG 1.4.4).
- `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` suppresses referrer on all outbound clicks. Combined with click-redirect links, this is standard practice but means GA4/Adobe Analytics on landing pages will see `(direct)` traffic if click params are dropped.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- Six recommendation images use `mi_u=604230016` — a static user ID baked into the URL, consistent with server-side personalization (no unresolved client-side merge tokens visible in the truncated source).
- `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` and `mi_kc_event_pe` parameters on one image (`916d6fa08d185233.png`) appear **blank/empty** — these look like Kohl's Cash expiration date and event period merge tokens that were not populated at send time. If these drive conditional content or expiry messaging downstream, empty values are a data-pipeline bug.

---

### 5. Compliance

- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not detected** by relay — if absent at the MTA level, Gmail and Yahoo will not render the one-click unsubscribe button in the inbox chrome. As of February 2024 bulk-sender requirements, this is a compliance risk for volume senders.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not detected** — one-click unsubscribe (POST method) is required by Gmail/Yahoo for bulk senders. Without it, machine-initiated unsubscribes may not process correctly.
- **No physical mailing address detected in the HTML source** — CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid physical postal address. Either it appears below the truncation point or it was omitted; must be confirmed against full source.
- **SPF/DKIM authentication results unknown** — `Authentication-Results` header not captured by the relay. Cannot confirm DMARC alignment. Given Kohl's send volume, a DMARC failure would be high-impact.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

- All destination URLs pass through `click.chp.kohls.com` (Coherent Path) before redirecting to `kohls.com`. UTM parameter presence on final destination URLs cannot be confirmed without tracing the redirect chain.
- `mi_ecmp=1015879_2026624` is present on pixel URLs — this is the MessageGears campaign ID, not a UTM parameter. If the click redirects do not append `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, and `utm_campaign` to the final kohls.com URLs, GA4/Adobe Analytics will misattribute sessions.
- `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` (noted above) reinforces the need for UTM params — referrer-based attribution will not work.
