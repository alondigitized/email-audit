# Ends soon ⏳ 20% off + $10 off your $50+ purchase + Kohl's Cash this way ➡️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's again, and it is the eighth "20% off + $10 off $50+" send since 10/26. Yesterday's "Last day ⏰" subject said it ended today. This one says "Ends soon ⏳" and still carries the same coupons. The "ENDS TODAY" tag in the hero is the only honest line. "Ends soon" in the subject undersells it.
- The body is a holiday-village hero with a "Kohl's Cash" card, then a gift guide, a "Top 100 Gifts" banner, and a grid of product cards. The 20% off and $10 off don't show up until the barcode coupons near the bottom. The member bar ("Status: Kohl's Rewards Member | Earn: 5% Rewards") is the one thing that feels like it knows me.
- Would I click? Probably not. I'd skim it and mark it read. I'd forward it to no one.

## What's working

- The member bar at the top shows my status and my 5% earn rate. That's the Kohl's email I can trust.
- "ENDS TODAY" with a "Shop Now" link is a clear deadline.
- The coupons at the bottom have codes, PINs, and "Ends Nov. 1" dates. I can use them in store or online without hunting.
- The "Shopping made fast, easy and on your schedule" band covers free returns through January 31, 2027, same-day delivery, and Sephora at Kohl's pickup. That's useful.

## What's weak

- The offer I was promised in the subject is buried. The hero shows a Kohl's Cash card with no amount. I had to scroll almost to the footer to find 20% off.
- The hero says "Ends today" while the subject says "Ends soon." That mismatch makes the urgency feel invented.
- The images are a village scene and a snow globe. They push a "North Kohl's" theme and don't show anything I'd buy.
- Product prices sit in tiny type. Ninja, Serta, and the mattress topper are not what I'm here for.
- "Coupons do not apply to select brands, details" is the real limit on the offer, and it's in small print under the products. Compare that to Macy's, which put its confirm-now mechanics up front.
- The disclaimer block at the bottom is long and dense, and I can't read it without zooming.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the 20% off and $10 off in the hero as one large offer with a single code button, and move the village art below it.
- 2. Drop the Kohl's Cash tease unless it carries a dollar amount and a date.
- 3. Match the subject to the hero. If it's the last day, say so, as yesterday's send did.
- 4. Cut the number of sends. Eight near-identical emails in a week is a lot, and Sephora's twenty-one in a week shows where that road ends.
- 5. Pull the exclusions up next to the coupons. Neiman Marcus's "The Roundup: Wild Things" shows how a calm, designer-first layout sells without shouting, and I'd want the same restraint here.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is structurally sound for email, with a table layout, a mobile media query and Outlook conditionals. It has several markup defects (stray `</custom>`, `<div>` inside `<head>`, empty `<title>`) and a zoom-blocking viewport meta. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the AgentMail relay and the truncated source, so they can't be confirmed as sender-side failures.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Not verifiable:** 74 links went through tracking redirects and were skipped (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…`, `click.chp.kohls.com/o/…`). Destination, status and redirect chain were not probed.
- **Four tracking beacons are in the first screen of the body:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/04b05e21-…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager)
- **Third-party call to demdex.net:** the `kohls.demdex.net` pixel is a CNAME on a Kohl's subdomain. It still adds an extra request and a cross-system identifier.
- **Recipient identifier in image URLs:** `mi_u=604230016` appears in the `mi.kohls.com` image URLs. This is a per-recipient ID in a URL that proxies and image caches may log. It is not a defect on its own.
- **Malformed markup:** the open-tracking image is closed with a stray `</custom>` tag: `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?…&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`. Some parsers may mis-handle it.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` disables pinch-zoom. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) concern.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>`.
- **Empty icon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href. It is harmless in most clients but is invalid.
- **Invalid placement:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. A `<div>` is not valid there, and parsers may close `<head>` early and move the contents into `<body>`.
- **Duplicate meta tags:** two `<meta name="format-detection">` tags (`telephone=no` and `date=no`). Some clients honor only one.
- **Missing alt on content images:** these look like content rather than pixels, since they have `.png` names under `/p/rp/`:
  - `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`
  - `f441d45d2128ae53.png`
  - `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`
  - `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`
  - `69494b48a79b4240.png`
  - `78308d1d9adb0751.png`
  - `916d6fa08d185233.png`
  
  If these carry offer text, the offer is invisible to screen readers and to image-blocked clients. Alt text may be present in the truncated part of the source, but the QA run flagged all of them.
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels:** `04b05e21-…`, `o.gif` and `event` are 1x1 tracking images. They should have `alt=""`. The `o.gif` pixel already has `alt=""` and `aria-hidden`, so the QA flag looks like a false positive there. The others (the `click.chp.kohls.com` pixel and the `demdex.net` pixel) have no `alt` attribute at all.
- **Web fonts:** the Google Fonts `<link>` and `@import` (Roboto, Montserrat) are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. Gmail and Outlook ignore them, and the stack falls back to `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`. This is acceptable.
- **Fixed widths:** the layout uses `width="640"` containers with `.container {width:100% !important}` at ≤500px. This is fine.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[[…]]`) appear in the visible source. The tracking IDs (`mi_u`, `mi_mid`) are populated. The subject has no tokens.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post:** not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture them, so this is unconfirmed. Validate against the raw message headers before treating it as a sender issue.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found. This is expected through the relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment is unknown. The From domain is `s.kohls.com`.
- **Physical address:** the QA check found none. The source was truncated before the footer, so this can't be confirmed. Check the full HTML.
- **Unsubscribe link in body:** not visible in the truncated source.
- **`<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">`:** these are not compliance issues.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not assessable:** every link is wrapped in an opaque `qs=` redirect, and 74 links were skipped, so destination URLs and UTM parameters can't be seen.
- **Campaign identifiers that are visible:** `mi_ecmp=1024961_2026111` and `email_name=261101_DG_Email_Sun_PM` on `mi.kohls.com` image URLs. These imply a Nov 1, 2026 Sunday PM send. They are Kohl's own parameters, not `utm_*`.
- **To check:** whether the final landing URLs carry `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` matching the email_name, and whether the "20% off + $10 off $50+" and Kohl's Cash offers resolve to a matching page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the stray `</custom>` tag, move the schema.org `<div>` out of `<head>` (use JSON-LD or drop it), and fill in `<title>`. Remove the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon">` and the duplicate `format-detection` meta.
2. Drop `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to the seven `/p/rp/*.png` images if they carry offer content, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels that lack it.
4. Re-run the audit against the full raw message (all headers and untruncated body) to confirm List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, the physical address and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
5. Resolve the redirect chains for a sample of CTAs to verify landing pages, UTM parameters and offer consistency.
