# rhode’s NEW Spotwear has arrived
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-14**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this one I'll actually give Sephora credit for — "0 points" the other day, "Rosie" the day before that, and today they've finally sent something that looks like an actual merchandising email instead of a personalization apology tour. Rhode's Spotwear patches front and center, "JUST DROPPED" in big clean type, a points-code offer up top — this is closer to what I expect from a beauty retailer I've been Insider for years.
- But "closer" isn't "there." I'm 47, I like rhode fine on my daughter's bathroom counter, but a hydrocolloid strawberry-shaped patch is not speaking to me — this is a 22-year-old's TikTok purchase dressed up as my "new arrivals." The grid underneath is the real email: Dior, Saie, YEPODA, BeautyBlender — that's the stuff I'd actually stop scrolling for, and it's buried under a full screen of rhode.
- Would I click? Maybe on the "Beauty Under $45" banner at the very bottom, out of curiosity. Would I forward it? No — nothing here says "for you," it says "for whoever rhode is targeting this quarter." Mark as read and move on.

## What's working

- The "Try rhode Highlight Milk" banner up top with a real redemption code (RHODETREAT) and a points reminder ("You have 0 points" is gone today, thank god) is the kind of concrete Insider perk I want to see.
- "JUST DROPPED" hero is clean, uncluttered, one clear "SHOP NOW" button — no visual noise competing with it.
- The product grid below is legible and well-organized: price, name, NEW/exclusive tags all visible without me squinting.
- Dior Miss Dior and Saie's glowy illuminator sitting in that grid gave me something I'd genuinely consider — that's the email doing its job for once.

## What's weak

- The entire top half is rhode Spotwear patches — three separate placements (banner, hero, and a callout) for one youth-skewing product line. Neiman Marcus repeating that calligraphic "S" three times in three days was annoying; this is the same move compressed into a single email.
- "Get a trial size" language and strawberry-shaped patches read very Gen Z — nothing in the hero visually or verbally acknowledges an Insider like me who's been buying at this counter for a decade.
- "Spot it. Shop it." beauty-event banner is a great beat (in-store events are exactly my thing) but it's shoved near the bottom, small, competing with a "Best of Blush" tile right next to it — buried, when it should've been the second thing I see.
- No mention of my tier (VIB/Rouge) anywhere despite the footer literally showing tier tabs — a missed chance to say "you're Rouge, here's your perk," which is the one thing that would've made this feel personal.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap the hero: lead with the in-store "Celebration" beauty event banner, not rhode patches. That's the single highest-intent CTA for someone like me — I go to counters, I don't buy strawberry patches online.
- 2. Move Dior, Saie, and the BeautyBlender higher in the grid, or pull one into a second "editor's pick" hero — right now the best products in this email are scrolled past in a wall of six equal-weight tiles.
- 3. Personalize the top banner with my actual tier status instead of a generic points reminder — even "You're a Rouge member — here's your exclusive" beats the blank slate they're running now.
- 4. Trim rhode to one placement. Three touches for one SKU in an otherwise broad email is Kohl's-level repetition, not Sephora's usual curation.

- **Subject Alt B:** `New at the counter: Dior, Saie & rhode Spotwear`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus: find a beauty event near you this week`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Miss Dior, Saie illuminator, and more under $45`
