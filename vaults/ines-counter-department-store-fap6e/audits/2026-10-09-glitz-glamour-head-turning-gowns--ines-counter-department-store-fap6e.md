# Glitz, glamour & head-turning gowns
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- This is the Neiman's I like. After five offer emails in four days, with the 10/6 "$200 off your purchase" ladder, the 10/7 "$50–$200" one and this morning's "Up to $200 off + 3x Points" send, a gown editorial with no code, no countdown and no InCircle math feels like someone turned the volume down. It's the same quiet register as "Forecast calls for Burberry" on 10/7 and "Prep for the cold months" yesterday.
- The trade-off is that it's pretty and gives me nothing to act on. The hero is a gorgeous black-and-gold beaded column dress, but the headline is scattered across the model's body ("THE... GLAMOUR... ERA") and the "GLAMOUR" is sitting right on the dress texture. I have to hunt for it. There are no prices, no loyalty hook and no event tie-in. I'd glance at it, enjoy the pictures and probably not click. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The hero model and dress are striking. A dark-skinned woman in a sculptural gold-flecked gown reads as real evening dressing rather than prom, which suits a 47-year-old shopping for an occasion.
- It's calm. After the dollar-off barrage, no shouting is a relief. Sephora's "Hey, Insider" email stacked four things into one send, and this is far easier to take in.
- The designer names under each accessory (Alexis Bittar, Akris, Dries Van Noten, Fernando Jorge, Ben-Amun) tell me this is real designer access, not a promo dump.
- The clutch and jewelry rows are clean, evenly spaced and easy to scan, with short, specific copy like "Shop Clutches" and "Shop Jewelry".
- The "Match with a personal style advisor" footer item is a service cue I actually value.

## What's weak

- The headline is split across the image, and "GLAMOUR" collides with the dress pattern. It's hard to read, and it's the one visible layout flaw.
- The "Shop Gowns" and "Shop Evening Glamour" links are small white underlined text over a photo. They're weak tap targets on a phone and low contrast, and there's no real button.
- There are no prices anywhere, and no InCircle points or tier mention, even though I got "3x Points" hours ago. Macy's, even when it's shouting, tells me what I earn.
- The middle burgundy fringe dress has no brand label, caption or link that I can see. It's just a big picture.
- The product tiles carry no names or prices, so I can't tell which clutch is which without clicking.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the headline in one clean block, or beside the model rather than over her. Turn the hero links into a real "Shop evening gowns" button with a solid background.
- 2. Add one honest line of value, such as "InCircle members: 3x points on gowns through Sunday." That is what would get a click from me instead of a nod.
- 3. Caption the burgundy dress with the designer name and a link. Put "from $" prices under the accessory rows.
- 4. Space this send out from the offer emails so it reads as a real editorial beat and not a lull between promos.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Gowns, clutches & jewelry: your evening edit`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sculptural gowns from Alexis Bittar, Akris and more, plus InCircle points.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML has no confirmed link or rendering failures. It does have two real defects: an internal SFMC template-variable comment is shipped in the production source, and the viewport meta tag blocks pinch-zoom. The QA warnings on unsubscribe and authentication headers are probably relay artifacts and can't be confirmed. The source was truncated after the `<style>` blocks, so body links, alt text and footer weren't available to inspect.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No confirmed broken links. The QA run skipped HTTP probing for all 17 tracking links (click-redirect domains), so their destinations and status are unverified.
- Plain-text part is 3199 of 3708 characters URLs (86%). That points to long, unshortened tracking URLs in the text alternative. It is a deliverability and readability risk, not a failure.
- The comment names `@refid = CONCAT(@base_refid,"")`. This suggests a campaign reference ID is appended to links. That can't be verified because the body is truncated.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` stops pinch-zoom on mobile. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) failure.
- **Comment before the DOCTYPE:** a large HTML comment sits before `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC ...>`. Content ahead of the doctype can push older Outlook/IE-engine renderers into quirks mode.
- **Hamburger menu:** it relies on the checkbox hack (`.navbar-checkbox:checked~.hamburger` / `.menu`) inside the `max-width: 600px` media query. This pattern is unsupported in Gmail and several other clients. Whether the markup has a fallback nav can't be seen in the truncated source.
- **Minor:** `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` has a trailing space and is generic. A global `p { margin: 0 auto; text-align: center; }` forces centering on all paragraphs, so any left-aligned copy needs explicit overrides.
- **Alt text and image dimensions:** not verifiable from the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved tokens are visible in the rendered portion. The `@email`, `@refid`, `@user`, `@emailid`, `@uEm` and `@cmdid` references are only in the leading comment.
- That comment is still a problem. It is a developer note that should have been stripped before send:
  - It exposes internal Data Extension names (`NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`) and lookup field names (`EMAIL_ID`, `uEm`, `cmdid`).
  - It contains an unresolved authoring instruction: "CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW -->" followed by `<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->`.
  - It has unbalanced parentheses in the documented `Lookup(...)` calls.
- Body-level use of `@user`, `@uEm` and `@cmdid` in URLs couldn't be checked. If a hashed email or CRM ID is appended to links, confirm it is a hash and not the plaintext `@email`.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe not found (WARN):** the QA note says it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. This is unconfirmed.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post not found (WARN):** RFC 8058 one-click support is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results not found (WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain is `e.neimanmarcus.com`.
- The footer's unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated section, so they couldn't be checked.
- None of the header findings can be treated as confirmed failures. They need verification against a direct-to-mailbox send or the raw headers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Not assessable. No hrefs, UTM parameters or landing pages are visible in the truncated source, and the 17 tracking links weren't probed.
- The only tracking-related evidence is the `@refid` / `@base_refid` variable pattern in the comment, which suggests a custom `refid` parameter in place of standard `utm_*` parameters.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Strip the SFMC variable-documentation comment** from the production template, or move it above the template into a non-delivered section. This removes the exposed DE names and the "CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO" text, and puts the `<!DOCTYPE>` first.
2. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag. Use `width=device-width, initial-scale=1`.
3. **Verify the unsubscribe headers** on a direct send, not through the relay. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` are present, and check SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment on `e.neimanmarcus.com`.
4. **Check the hamburger nav fallback** in Gmail, where the checkbox hack won't work.
5. **Shorten or trim the URLs in the plain-text part**, or reduce the number of links it carries.
6. **Re-run the audit on the full HTML** with link probing through the redirect chain. That is needed to check link destinations, `refid`/UTM consistency, alt text, and the footer unsubscribe link and address.
