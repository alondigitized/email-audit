# Extra 20% off ✅ Up to 70% off clearance ✅
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-03**
## Executive summary

- Forty-two. That is my rough running count of Kohl's in my inbox since somewhere around Memorial Day, and today's entry leads with not one but two checkmarks — as if the visual tick will do the enthusiasm work the copy isn't bothering to do. I'll give them credit for pairing the 20% off with the clearance-up-to-70% in a single subject line. That's actually more layered than last week's parade of identical GOSHOP codes. But the moment I opened it, the hero section is activewear and athletic shoes. TEK GEAR. I am not a TEK GEAR person. I am a woman who drives to Nordstrom for a Saturday fragrance event and gets her Kohl's Cash for bedding and kitchen towels, not for a teal workout bag.
- The Kohl's Cash mechanic is alive and visible — earn through July 5, redeem July 9-16 — and for that, I do briefly pause. That's the one lever this brand pulls that actually earns my attention, because I will absolutely spend $50 to bank $10, and I know how to stack it. But the creative around it? Activewear starting at $8.99, athletic shoes starting at $39.99. The clearance section gets a sub-header and a blue Shop Now button, but it's buried in the third act.
- I'd mark this as read. I would not click today. I would not forward it — Sephora's "Ends 7/7: 30% off ALL Sephora Collection + 2X points" this week was built for forwarding because it named a deadline and a category I care about. This one is just Kohl's being Kohl's.

## What's working

- **Two concrete offers stacked visibly at the top.** The 20% coupon graphic and the Kohl's Cash earn notice land before I scroll, and the redemption dates (through July 5 / redeem July 9-16) are spelled out in the barcode section. I respect the specificity.
- **Loyalty status visible in the header.** "KOHL'S REWARDS | MEMBER | Earn 5% REWARDS" — treating me like I exist in their system, even if only as a badge line, beats what Macy's did last week where the "LAST CHANCE: confirm now" felt like a CRM trigger fired at a stranger.
- **Clearance lead is genuinely interesting.** "NEW MARKDOWNS TAKEN" with "Save up to 70%" is the kind of clearance signal I'd actually act on for home goods. That has my attention more than the activewear does.
- **No render bugs I can see.** Clean layout, images loaded, no overlapping text, no lorem ipsum, no dead zones. Kohl's at least sends a technically intact email.

## What's weak

- **Activewear hero is wrong for me.** TEK GEAR activewear and athletic shoe inventory, front and center, is not what a 47-year-old beauty-counter regular opens Kohl's for. Compare this to the Neiman Marcus "Pretty in pink for weddings" send I saw this week — that email knew exactly who it was dressing. Kohl's is guessing.
- **"Shop Now" three times is lazy CTA work.** Every section — activewear, shoes, clearance — says Shop Now. Sephora uses "Shop the Sale" or "Shop 30% Off" — specific verbs that tell me what the click resolves to. Generic CTAs erode trust.
- **Same mechanic as the last four Kohl's emails.** I already reviewed the July 1st GOSHOP20 send and the July 2nd "Psst... 20% off" send. The coupon code hasn't changed; only the subject line wrapper has. The checkmark format is a small visual refresh but doesn't mask the repeat.
- **No beauty, no home, no personalization signal.** Not a single mention of fragrance, skincare, or even a "for her" curated section. For a customer who buys at brand events, there is nothing here that feels event-adjacent.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Swap the activewear hero for a home or women's clearance hero.** If you're leading with 70% off clearance in the subject line, the body should open with clearance — not activewear at $8.99. Honor the promise of the subject in the scroll order.
- 2. **Make the CTA buttons category-specific.** "Shop Clearance" / "Shop Women's" / "Shop Home" instead of three identical "Shop Now" buttons. Each click should tell me where I'm landing.
- 3. **Surface the Kohl's Cash earn window earlier.** Right now it's buried in the barcode footer. A single line under the hero — "Earn $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 spent through July 5" — would make me calculate my cart before I even scroll.
- 4. **Retire GOSHOP20 or at minimum vary the code.** I've seen this code across at least four emails now. A new code (CLEAR70, SUMMER20, anything) signals a new send, not a reminder.
- 5. **Add one editorial beat that acknowledges the holiday week.** The July 3rd email I reviewed yesterday at least wore a patriotic hook. Today's send completely abandons it and goes back to generic. Feels like a draft that got scheduled before anyone checked the calendar.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "Extra 20% off / Up to 70% off clearance"

---

### 1. Technical Summary

HTML4.01 Transitional with MSO/VML conditionals targeting Outlook; tracking stack spans four vendors (CoherentPath, MessageGears, Adobe Audience Manager, a Kohl's-owned click domain). One malformed closing tag and several accessibility violations confirmed in source; three compliance headers unverified due to relay capture gap.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Malformed HTML around open-tracking pixel**
The open-pixel `<div>` is closed with `</custom>` instead of `</div>`:
```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?IXUTWN25S44ELPKRVEGM3DEMNQ.60267..." width="1" height="1" alt="">
</custom>  ← invalid tag
```
This is a DOM corruption that may cause downstream layout breakage in strict parsers.

**22 click links unprobed**
All destination URLs route through `click.chp.kohls.com` (CoherentPath) or `click.s.kohls.com`. QA skipped HTTP probes on all 22 — broken redirect targets, destination 404s, and missing UTM params cannot be confirmed.

**Four-vendor tracking pixel stack**
Pixels from `chp.kohls.com`, `mi.kohls.com`, `kohls.demdex.net`, and `click.s.kohls.com` all fire on open. No issue per se, but any single-vendor outage silently drops a tracking signal with no fallback.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`<title>` is empty**
```html
<title></title>
```
Screen readers and some desktop clients (Outlook, Apple Mail) surface the title; leaving it blank provides no context.

**`user-scalable=0` disables pinch-zoom**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />
```
Fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text, Level AA). iOS Mail and Android Gmail clients respect this and block zoom for low-vision users.

**8 images missing `alt` text**
QA flagged all three open/tracking pixels plus five content images (`2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`). Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` (present on the `mi.kohls.com` pixel via `aria-hidden="true"` — correctly handled there); content images require descriptive alt text.

**Redundant Google Fonts loading**
Both a `<link>` tag and a CSS `@import` load the same Roboto and Montserrat families:
```html
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Roboto...">
<style>@import url("https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Roboto...");</style>
```
This doubles the font request overhead and adds ~100–300ms latency on first render in clients that execute `@import`.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved `{{token}}` or `%%token%%` patterns visible in the captured source. `mi_u=604230016` in pixel URLs appears to be a correctly merged subscriber ID (not a placeholder). `email_name=260703_DG_Email_Fri_PM` confirms send-date stamping is functioning.

No issues found in visible source — full merge verification requires the complete HTML.

### 5. Compliance

**List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post headers not confirmed**
QA could not capture these via the AgentMail relay. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender mandates (effective Feb 2024) require `List-Unsubscribe` with `List-Unsubscribe-Post` for one-click RFC 8058 compliance. If the headers exist upstream but are stripped by the relay, this is a monitoring gap; if they are genuinely absent, this is a deliverability risk with Gmail for high-volume sends.

**Physical mailing address not detected**
CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid postal address in every commercial email. The QA check did not find one in the captured HTML. This may be present in the untruncated body — must be verified against the full source.

**SPF/DKIM status unknown**
`Authentication-Results` header was not captured. The sending domain `s.kohls.com` should show `dkim=pass` and `spf=pass`; absence of the header in relay capture means authentication posture cannot be confirmed for this send.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

Campaign ID `mi_ecmp=1016571_202673` is present in pixel URLs, confirming server-side campaign attribution is wired. However, since all 22 click links were proxied through CoherentPath/Kohl's click domains and skipped, UTM parameter presence on destination landing pages (`kohls.com`) cannot be confirmed by this audit. A manual spot-check of one CTA URL is needed to verify `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, and `utm_campaign` pass through the redirect chain intact.
