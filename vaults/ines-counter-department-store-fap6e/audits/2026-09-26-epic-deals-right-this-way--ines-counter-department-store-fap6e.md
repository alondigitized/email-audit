# Epic Deals right this way 👉
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-26**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again — this is the ninth or tenth one of these I've logged this week, and it's the same red "EPIC DEALS" tile wall I flagged on the 23rd, 24th, and 25th, just reshuffled today with sweaters, towels, and a "Baby & Toddler Stock-Up Sale" banner instead of the handbag hero. I'm not the customer for kids' Carter's clothing or LEGO — I don't have little ones anymore — so most of this scroll is dead space to me, and I clock that instantly.
- There's a Kohl's Rewards banner up top ("Status: Kohl's Rewards Member, Earn 2% Rewards") which is the one thing that acknowledges I'm actually a member, but it's buried in tiny gray text under the logo where I'd never see it on a phone without stopping to read. Compare that to how Macy's at least dresses up its urgency mechanics with a clear countdown, or how Sephora leads with an actual hook before dumping tile after tile — Kohl's just opens straight into the grid, no breathing room.
- Would I click? No. Would I forward it? No — this is the kind of email I archive without reading past the first fold, and honestly the cadence itself is starting to annoy me more than any individual tile does.

## What's working

- The "3X Rewards, Members Earn 3x Rewards" banner is at least visually distinct (green bar) so if I were rewards-motivated I'd spot it fast.
- Prices are big, bold, and unambiguous — 60% off bedding, 40% off decor, no squinting required.
- "Shop Now" buttons are consistently placed under every tile, so the tap target is never a mystery.

## What's weak

- This is a straight repeat of the same red-tile-grid template I've now seen five-plus times this week — Kohl's isn't varying the creative, just the SKUs.
- Nothing here speaks to me as a 47-year-old beauty-counter shopper — it's kids' fleece, toy trucks, baby stock-up sales, a Vanderbilt jean, a coffee maker. I'm scanning for a reason to stop and there isn't one.
- The Sephora Collection beauty tile ("Starting at $3.50... Coupons and Kohl's Cash do not apply") is the one category I actually care about, and it's crammed mid-scroll between pajamas and bedding with an exclusion clause attached — that's a Saks-tone-deaf move, burying my category and then telling me the coupon won't work on it anyway.
- Visual hierarchy is just "grid, grid, grid" — my eye doesn't land anywhere specific, unlike Sephora's cleaner top-of-email hook.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment this — if Kohl's has purchase history data, someone like me shouldn't get a baby-and-toddler-heavy send. Lead with beauty or home instead of burying Sephora Collection eight tiles down.
- 2. Cut the tile count in half. Five to six merchandise blocks is already a lot; this has closer to fifteen. Ulta would bury the offer this deep too, but at least they don't send it three days in a row with the same layout.
- 3. Move the Rewards status line into an actual visual badge near the top, not small gray text — if I'm a member, treat me like one I can see.
- 4. Drop or reposition the "coupons do not apply" language on the beauty tile — leading with an exclusion on the one category I care about reads as bait-and-switch.
- 5. Vary the hero pattern day to day — five straight sends with the same red grid is fatigue, not frequency.
