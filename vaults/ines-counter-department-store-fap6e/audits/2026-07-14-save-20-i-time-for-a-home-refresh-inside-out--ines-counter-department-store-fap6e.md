# Save 20%  I Time for a home refresh (inside & out) 🏡
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-14**
## Executive summary

- Fifty-four. That is my count of Kohl's emails since Memorial Day, and today's one lands with a slightly different hook — a blender, white bedding, gray bath towels, and an invitation to refresh my home before the kids go back to school. I'll give them the topic switch. I genuinely did not expect a blender after fifty-three consecutive fashion pushes. But I'm a beauty counter regular. I have the Kohl's card because of the Sephora inside, not because I shop their housewares department. There is not a single skincare product, fragrance, or cosmetic in this entire email. Zero.
- What I'm actually looking at is two emails stitched together with a Kohl's logo on top: a home goods clearance event and a back-to-school outfitter, sharing the same 20% coupon code that has been sitting in my inbox since roughly Memorial Day weekend. The Simply Vera Vera Wang towels are the closest thing to my world in here, and even that's a reach. When Sephora sent me "New products have arrived" last week — scored 9 in my head before I even opened it — they led with products I actually buy. This email leads with a Ninja blender.
- I would not click. I would not forward. I would mark it read, assume the next one comes tomorrow, and wait for one that remembers I have a Sephora punch card in my Kohl's wallet.

## What's working

- The 20% off bar is the first thing my eye hits — they did not bury the offer, and that still earns credit
- Kohl's Cash is called out explicitly, which I actually care about; I refuse to use a store card that doesn't give me something back
- The headline "Home finds that work hard. Prices that take it easy." is the most charming copy Kohl's has sent me in weeks — clean, confident, no exclamation points
- Simply Vera Vera Wang on the towel block is a designer name I recognize; it's the one moment the email feels like it has taste
- The layout is clean — no broken images, no overlapping text, nothing I had to zoom into to understand

## What's weak

- No beauty content whatsoever — as a Sephora-inside-Kohl's shopper, I am completely invisible in this email
- The back-to-school section in the second half is aimed at parents buying kids' sneakers and backpacks; that pivot is jarring after a home refresh pitch, and it is absolutely not aimed at me
- Three different discount depths (40%, 40%, 20%) with "select styles" asterisks under two of them makes the actual value feel slippery — I've been burned by "select styles" before
- Fifty-four emails since Memorial Day, and the coupon mechanic is identical every time; compared to Neiman Marcus, who at least pivoted from gift cards to a 1,000-item new arrivals spread, Kohl's has shown no creative escalation
- The coupon code block in the footer looks like a receipt printout — fine on desktop, nearly unreadable on a phone, which is almost certainly where I opened this

## Recommendations

- 1. **Segment my purchase history.** I have documented Sephora spend inside Kohl's. Open this email with a "refresh your beauty shelf, then your bathroom shelf" angle and the home pivot lands naturally instead of feeling like a wrong number.
- 2. **Pick one discount headline and commit.** "40% off bedding" is a genuinely strong offer. Don't dilute it by stacking it under the generic 20%-off coupon bar and then burying it between two other percentage callouts with asterisks.
- 3. **Split Back to School into its own send.** A parent shopping for their kid's backpack and a woman buying new bath towels are not the same person in the same headspace. Macy's over-stuffed their last email confirmation email too — that "LAST CHANCE" urgency on a confirmation form was awkward — but at least it was one coherent message.
- 4. **Put the coupon expiration date in the subject line.** If this expires tonight, say it. The urgency is legitimate; hiding it in footer fine print wastes it.
- **Subject Alt A:** `40% off bedding + 20% coupon — home refresh, ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 20% off: sheets, towels, blenders, more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus earn Kohl's Cash on every $50 — no code hunting in the app.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Simply Vera, Cuisinart, and more — prices already reduced.`

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "Save 20% / Home Refresh" Email

**Sender:** Kohls@s.kohls.com | **Date:** 2026-07-14

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email uses a well-structured HTML4 transitional skeleton with VML/MSO conditional blocks and a standard responsive breakpoint at 500px. The primary technical concerns are missing compliance signals (unsubscribe headers, physical address) and widespread missing `alt` text on tracking and content images — the latter likely suppressed intentionally on tracking pixels but problematic on content images.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 tracking links skipped by QA probe** — all click-through URLs route via `click.chp.kohls.com` and `click.s.kohls.com`, which is expected for ESP redirect tracking. No broken destination URLs were confirmed, but they could not be validated.

**Three distinct tracking pixel stacks are present:**
- `click.chp.kohls.com/o/{uuid}` — Coherent Path behavioral tracker
- `mi.kohls.com/p/up/` — Movable Ink open pixel
- `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` — ESP open pixel (Salesforce/ExactTarget pattern)
- `kohls.demdex.net/event` — Adobe Audience Manager (AAM) sync pixel

**Malformed custom tag:** Line `</custom>` appears immediately after the `click.s.kohls.com` open pixel `<img>`. This is invalid HTML and may cause parse errors in strict renderers.

```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt="">
</custom>   ← invalid closing tag
```

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**11 images missing `alt` text** per QA findings. Three are intentional tracking pixels (acceptable with `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"` — the open pixel at `click.chp.kohls.com` is missing both attributes). The remaining 8 are content images hosted at `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/` (Movable Ink rendered personalization images):

```
2a5adc5afb647aad.png, f441d45d2128ae53.png, c2f81e9ac8daee44.png,
415c8a18fc2950ea.png, 69494b48a79b4240.png, 78308d1d9adb0751.png,
916d6fa08d185233.png
```

Content images without `alt` text render as broken placeholders in image-blocking email clients (Outlook default, many corporate clients) and fail WCAG 2.1 AA for screen readers.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — disables pinch-to-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) failure and blocked in iOS 10+.

```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />
```

**Empty `<title>` tag** — subject line is not populated into `<title>`, which affects VoiceOver/NVDA announcement on email open.

**Google Fonts loaded via both `<link>` and `@import`** for the same families (Roboto, Montserrat) — redundant double-fetch in clients that support web fonts.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `*|FNAME|*`) detected in the truncated source — tokens appear fully rendered.

Movable Ink `mi_u=604230016` and `mi_ecmp=1017539_2026714` parameters are present and consistent across all `mi.kohls.com` image URLs, indicating the render-time personalization pipeline resolved correctly for this recipient.

One content image URL contains an apparently empty merge field:

This suggests a conditional block with an unfilled date field. If `mi_kc_redm_end_dte` controls a countdown timer or offer expiry display, this recipient may be seeing a broken or default state.

**CAN-SPAM physical address** is a hard requirement. If it's present in an image-only footer (common in Kohl's templates), it is invisible when images are blocked and likely does not satisfy the statutory requirement for plain-text disclosure.

Confirm by checking raw message headers in the originating ESP. If genuinely absent, this is a Gmail/Apple Mail one-click unsubscribe compliance gap as of 2024 Google/Yahoo sender requirements for bulk senders.
