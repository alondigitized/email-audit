- The barcode + coupon code + PIN block near the bottom is genuinely useful if I were actually going to the store — that's a real convenience, more than most department stores bother with in-email.
- Clean category buttons (Women / Men / Kids / Home) give me a fast way to skip past sections I don't care about, even if I ultimately don't use them.

## What's weak

- Air fryers, rice cookers, cookware, bedding, floor care, decor — this is a home-goods circular, not a beauty or apparel email, and nothing here matches how I actually shop.
- The "You came for one thing. (Your house made other plans.)" line is trying to be cute but it just confirms the email itself doesn't know what it's selling.
- A second, unrelated 70% clearance banner stacked right after the home-goods block turns this into two emails glued together — no hierarchy, just more.
- Zero designer-access or beauty-event language anywhere, which is the tone that actually gets me to open an email from a store I cross-shop against Saks and Nordstrom.
- Same navy circle "20% off" badge treatment I flagged on the 08-01 and 07-31 sends — at this point it's wallpaper, not an offer.

- 1. Segment this — send apparel/beauty shoppers an apparel/beauty version and home shoppers a home version. One catalog-dump email for everyone is why I've stopped really looking at these past the top banner.
- 2. Drop the "Your house made other plans" line — either lead with a real category I care about or don't try to be clever about it.
- 3. Cut it down to one clear offer block. Right now Friends & Family 20% + Kohl's Cash + appliance 25% + cookware 25% + clearance 70% is five competing offers in one scroll — nobody's eye lands anywhere.
- 4. Move the coupon barcode/code higher for members who already know they're shopping — don't make me scroll past a dozen product blocks to find my code.

# Technical Audit: Kohl's Friends & Family Email

## 1. Technical Summary
Table-based HTML email with standard MSO/Outlook conditional scaffolding; renders via multiple third-party tracking beacons and a chp.kohls.com click-redirect layer. QA run returned a 43% pass rate driven mainly by missing email-authentication/compliance headers and unlabeled tracking images; one malformed-markup defect was found in the source.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **4 open-tracking beacons fire before any visible content**, in this order: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/e1311830-...` (1×1, no alt), `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif` (1×1, correctly uses `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`), `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager beacon, no alt), and `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...` (1×1, no alt). Implementation is inconsistent — only one of the four pixels follows accessible-pixel convention.
- **69 click-tracking links route through the `chp.kohls.com` redirect domain** with an opaque, encoded `cp_tp` payload param (base64-like blob). QA's automated HTTP probe skipped all 69 by design (redirect domains aren't followed), so final destination URLs and link health are **unconfirmed** — recommend manual click-through QA before send.
- No broken or malformed `href` values found in the visible source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed markup**: a stray closing tag `</custom>` appears immediately after the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` tracking `<img>`, with no corresponding opening tag anywhere in the source. This is invalid HTML, likely a templating/ESP injection artifact — should be stripped.
- **Viewport disables user zoom**: `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />` blocks pinch-to-zoom, a known mobile accessibility failure (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **8 images missing `alt` text** per QA (all tracking pixels: `e1311830-...`, `event`, and six `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` remarketing pixels). Since these are non-visual 1×1 trackers, impact on screen-reader users is minimal, but they should still carry `alt=""` + `aria-hidden="true"` for consistency (as one pixel already does).
- Google Fonts are loaded twice redundantly — once via `<link href="...">` and again via `@import url(...)` inside a `<style>` block, both scoped outside the `[if mso]` block. Harmless but duplicates the font request in clients that honor both.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{firstName}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) or personalization artifacts found in the visible portion of the source. **Not fully verifiable** — the HTML was truncated before body content rendered, so downstream personalization blocks could not be inspected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** (QA) — may be a relay capture gap rather than a true absence; needs verification against the raw MTA headers.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found** (QA) — one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) cannot be confirmed as supported.
- **No physical mailing address detected** (QA) — CAN-SPAM requires one; not visible in the truncated source, so this may be a footer that fell outside the truncation window rather than a true omission. Requires confirmation against the full footer HTML.
- **Authentication-Results header not found** (QA) — SPF/DKIM pass/fail status is unknown via the AgentMail relay; cannot confirm sender authentication for `s.kohls.com`.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Cannot be assessed with confidence: the only fully-visible link is an opaque `chp.kohls.com` redirect carrying an encoded `cp_tp` param rather than a direct landing-page URL with plain UTM parameters, and all 69 tracking links were skipped by the automated probe. No UTM-tagged destination URL or landing-page alignment could be verified from the available evidence.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the stray `</custom>` tag near the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` pixel — invalid markup.
2. Add `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"` to the 8 flagged tracking/pixel images for consistency with existing practice.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta to restore pinch-zoom.
4. Manually resolve a sample of the 69 `chp.kohls.com` redirect links to confirm they land on live pages with correct UTM parameters — automated probing couldn't verify this.
5. Confirm List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post headers and a physical address are present in the actual sent message (verify against raw headers/full footer, since QA relay capture and HTML truncation may both be masking true state rather than indicating absence).
6. Verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status directly against Authentication-Results on the sending domain, independent of the AgentMail relay.
