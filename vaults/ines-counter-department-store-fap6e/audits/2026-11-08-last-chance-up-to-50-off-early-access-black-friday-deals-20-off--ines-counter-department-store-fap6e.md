# LAST CHANCE 🚨Up to 50% off Early Access Black Friday Deals + 20% off 🚨
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again. By my count this is the eleventh one this week. Last night's "Save BIG… Kohl's Cash 🤑" and the 11/7 "Up to 50% off deals + extra 20% off + Daily Deals" send were this exact email with the furniture rearranged. The screenshot is a very long scroll and the text is small, so I'm going by the big shapes. I get a "HURRY, DEALS DEPART TONIGHT" banner, a Black Friday hero with "up to 50%" and "extra 20%", a free shipping line, and then tile after tile after tile.
- I'm not buying a Black Friday pile at Kohl's. I buy at the beauty counter, and Kohl's beauty is a few small tiles way down the page. The Sephora at Kohl's block is the only thing that sounds like me, and it's buried. Macy's at least dresses up its urgency emails with a single clear mechanic. This one just shouts a siren emoji twice and hopes.
- Would I click? Probably not. Would I forward it? No. It's a mark-as-read, and I'd only look for the Sephora block if I were already shopping Kohl's.

## What's working

- The hero is easy to read: big "50%" and "20%" on a dark gold-and-black panel, so I know what the deal is within a second.
- "Shop Daily Deals" is a real verb-and-noun button, and the free shipping line sits right under it.
- The category row (Women, Men, Home, Kids, Beauty and so on) up top lets me skip the circus and go straight to one department.
- Each tile has the same layout (big percent, short label, "Shop Now"), so the long scroll is at least predictable.
- Deadline language like "ends tonight" fits the real Black Friday season.

## What's weak

- It's the same promo I've seen all week, so "LAST CHANCE" and "deals depart tonight" don't read as credible. If it's the last chance, why did I get the same email yesterday?
- The two 🚨 emojis, the caps and the "Early Access" wording make the subject shout. Sephora's "10% off ends TOMORROW ⚠️" has the same problem, but at least that one only has one offer to shout about.
- The fine print is everywhere ("Coupons do not apply," "Select styles," "w/coupon"). The "up to 50%" headline doesn't tell me what I'd actually pay.
- There are far too many tiles for one email, and nothing is the hero. It's candles, boots, rugs, toys and Christmas trees with equal weight.
- Nothing here speaks to a 47-year-old beauty shopper. There's no points-back message for card use, no beauty event signal and no designer tone. Neiman Marcus's quiet Lafayette 148 send at least picked one story.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one hero, a daily deal row and a three-category strip, and drop the 15-tile scroll. Segment by category history so beauty buyers like me see the Sephora at Kohl's block first.
- 2. Drop the second siren and the "LAST CHANCE" if it isn't the last one. If tonight really is the end, say what ends: the extra 20% code.
- 3. Put the Kohl's Cash and card points message right under the hero, in plain numbers. That's what gets me to use the card.
- 4. Move the coupon exclusions next to each tile's discount instead of leaving them as a gray footnote.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Tonight only: extra 20% off, ends at midnight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your extra 20% off expires tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus Kohl's Cash on beauty and free shipping at $49. Code inside.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sephora at Kohl's, daily deals and the full list of exclusions.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has several markup defects: a stray `</custom>` tag, a `<div>` inside `<head>`, an empty `<title>`, and multiple images without alt text. It also stacks four separate open-tracking pixels. The source is truncated, so the footer, the unsubscribe link and the link destinations could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **91 tracking links were not probed.** They are redirect wrappers (e.g. `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7Im...`), so destination, status and redirect chain are unverified.
- **Four tracking pixels fire before the visible content:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/3c45876f-...` (no `alt`)
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=...`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (no `alt`)
- **Per-recipient identifiers appear in image URLs:** `mi_u=604230016` and `mi_ecmp=1025484_2026118` on `mi.kohls.com`. The `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` content images also carry a long query string, including `email_name=261108_DG_Email_Sun_PM`. Content images that depend on per-recipient URLs fail if the personalization service is slow or down.
- **Invalid markup after the `open.aspx` image:** `<img ... alt=""></custom>` closes an element that was never opened.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** QA flagged 11 images. The `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` files (`2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`) look like content images, so screen readers and image-blocking clients get no text equivalent.
  - The `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` and `kohls.demdex.net/event` pixels have no `alt` attribute at all.
  - `o.gif` is flagged by QA, but the source shows `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, which is correct for a pixel. That flag is likely a false positive.
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="..., maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **`<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`.** Parsers will close `<head>` early at that point. The schema markup is also an `http://` reference.
- **`<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href.**
- **Broken selector.** `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` is missing the leading `.` and matches no elements.
- **Web fonts load through `@import` and `<link>` from `fonts.googleapis.com`.** They are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, so Outlook is unaffected. Clients that strip `@import` fall back to `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`, which is declared.
- **The 1×1 tracking table has no `role="presentation"`.** The other layout tables do. The first `<table width="1" height="1">` and the AAM `<table width="100%">` lack it.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unrendered merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `[[...]]`) appear in the visible source.
- The footer and body were truncated, so token rendering there is unverified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders. Verify against the raw message headers.
- **No physical address was detected.** The footer is cut off in the supplied source, so this cannot be confirmed or ruled out.
- **`Authentication-Results` is missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sender domain is `s.kohls.com`, and the tracking domains differ (`click.s.kohls.com`, `click.chp.kohls.com`, `mi.kohls.com`).
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 15,906 of 21,698 characters (73%) are URLs, which can raise spam-filter scores.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Destination URLs are hidden behind `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=...` wrappers, and no `utm_*` parameters are visible in the available source.
- Because the 91 links were not resolved, landing-page alignment with the subject's "Up to 50% off Early Access Black Friday Deals + 20% off" claim cannot be verified.
- The only campaign identifier visible is `email_name=261108_DG_Email_Sun_PM` on image URLs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the stray `</custom>` and move the `itemscope` div and `<meta itemprop>` tags out of `<head>`. Use JSON-LD or the body.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to every `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` content image. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels that lack it.
3. Drop `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Add a `<title>` and remove or fill the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon" href="">`.
5. Fix `ReadMsgBody` to `.ReadMsgBody`, or remove it.
6. Consolidate the four open-tracking pixels to the ones that are actually needed.
7. Check the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results` and the footer address. Re-run QA on the untruncated HTML.
8. Rebalance the plain-text part so it has more readable copy and fewer raw URLs.
9. Resolve a sample of the 91 click-tracked links to confirm the redirect chain, the final landing URLs and the UTM tagging.
