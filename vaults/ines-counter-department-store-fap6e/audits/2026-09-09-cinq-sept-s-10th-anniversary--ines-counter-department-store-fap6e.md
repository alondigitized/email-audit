# Cinq à Sept’s 10th Anniversary
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-09**
## Executive summary

- Well, this is Neiman's back to their better instincts — a single hero shot of a hand-embroidered black tux suit with butterfly appliqués, a clean "Wing It" headline, and a grid of six Cinq à Sept pieces. It's editorial, it's designer-specific, and there's no black "S" banner or countdown clock shouting at me. But here's my problem: the subject line promised a 10th anniversary, and nowhere in this email do I see anything that actually celebrates that — no "10 years" copy, no capsule story, no anniversary framing at all. It just reads as a regular Cinq à Sept shop-the-look email wearing a birthday-party subject line.
- I'd probably click through on the blazer or the caftan-style dress just because the styling is genuinely covetable, but I wouldn't forward this to a friend — there's no "can you believe it's been 10 years" hook to share. This is a scroll-and-maybe-click, not an open-and-forward.

## What's working

- The hero image is gorgeous — one model, one strong look, no clutter, which is exactly the Neiman's-at-its-best mode I noted on 2026-09-08's "Fall's monochromatic moves."
- "Wing It" is a clever, short headline that ties to the butterfly motif without over-explaining.
- Two clear CTAs — "Shop Cinq à Sept" and "Shop Contemporary" — give me an obvious next step without a wall of text.
- The six-item grid all labeled "Cinq à Sept" keeps designer-attribution front and center, which is the thing that actually gets me to click at this price point.
- Bottom module offering a personal style advisor match is a nice loyalty-adjacent touch, even though it's generic across sends.

## What's weak

- The subject says "10th Anniversary" and the email delivers zero anniversary content — no year, no milestone copy, no "since 2016" storytelling. That's a bait-and-switch on the one specific hook that got me to open.
- No price is visible anywhere — not on the hero, not on the grid. I don't even get a starting-at price for a full look this ornate.
- No mention of my Fidelity Club status or any loyalty tie-in on this specific send, despite Neiman's other sends this same week (09/08, 09/08) leaning on gift-card earn tiers.
- The grid items have zero product names, just "Cinq à Sept" repeated six times — I can't tell a blazer from a dress from a bag icon glance, I have to hover or click blind.

## Recommendations

- 1. Actually deliver on the subject line — add one line of copy under "Wing It" naming the anniversary (e.g., "Ten years of Cinq à Sept, one iconic tux"). Right now I opened for a story and got a product grid.
- 2. Add product names under each grid thumbnail instead of the repeated brand tag — I shouldn't have to click to find out what I'm looking at.
- 3. Surface at least a "from $X" price point near the hero — Saks does this even on its editorial sends, and its absence here makes it feel like the brand doesn't trust the price to sell itself.
- 4. Tie in Fidelity Club or InCircle points messaging somewhere, even a small line — Neiman's own 09/08 and 09/09 gift-card emails prove they know how to do loyalty framing, this one skipped it entirely.
