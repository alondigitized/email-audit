# ✏️ Save up to 40% during the Back-to-School Sale 📚
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Back to School" — I don't have kids in school anymore, so the hero shot of the smiling kid at the lockers and the up-to-40% off "essentials" banner is just noise for me. But Kohl's, credit where it's due, doesn't stop there — they scroll me right past the kid stuff into Kitchen, Home, and then that intimates block again with actual bras and panties at real prices. That's the part I'd actually stop for.
- And here's the thing — this is basically the same Semi-Annual intimates sale I already saw in "Stock up & save BIG during the Intimates Sale" yesterday and in "ENDS TONIGHT: Your deal is waiting to be revealed" the day before. Same $19.99 bra, same 5/$35 panty math, same photography. At some point I start to feel like Kohl's is just recycling one creative deck under five different subject lines, and that's more exhausting than persuasive.
- Would I click? Maybe — the "Picked with you in mind" section with the Ninja blender and the mattress topper actually caught my eye more than the back-to-school header did, mostly because it feels like it's finally talking to a grown woman instead of a parent doing school-supply math. Forward to a friend? No. Mark as read and move on — this is a scroll-past, not a save.

## What's working

- The intimates block mid-email has real prices, real product shots, and reads clean — no lorem ipsum, no broken layout.
- "Picked with you in mind (Your great taste makes it easy)" is a decent stab at personalization copy, even generic — it's a nicer line than most of what I get from Macy's triggered emails.
- Kohl's Cash banner up top is at least consistently placed so I know where to look for it every time.
- Category nav buttons (Women, Girls, Boys, Shoes, Home) are big, thumb-friendly, and easy to tap on my phone.

## What's weak

- The whole top third — hero image, Shoes, Activewear, Basics, Kitchen, Home, Back to College — is not for me at all; that's a lot of scrolling before I hit anything relevant.
- This is the same Semi-Annual bra-and-panty sale creative I've now seen three times this week under three different subject lines — the "reveal" and "ends tonight" urgency language from those sends already burned my trust in Kohl's deadlines.
- The sweepstakes banner ("SO-BACK Sweepstakes") is squeezed in between unrelated sections and adds clutter without adding value to me personally.
- No loyalty/Kohl's Card tier visibility anywhere near the top — I have to scroll to the very bottom to even see "Earn $10 Kohl's Cash" mentioned, and there's zero acknowledgment of my card status.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a segmented version for non-parent shoppers — swap the back-to-school hero for the intimates or home goods block up top. Neiman Marcus's "Tantalizing Totes" send worked because it committed to one category and didn't make me wade through irrelevant sections; Kohl's could do the same by just knowing I don't have school-age kids.
- 2. Stop re-running the identical intimates creative under new subject lines within the same week — rotate in different product angles (activewear bras, loungewear) so it doesn't feel like the same email in a new outfit.
- 3. Move "Picked with you in mind" — the actual personalized block — up near the top instead of burying it two-thirds down; that's the section doing the real work.
- 4. Surface Kohl's Card / Kohl's Cash tier status near the hero, not buried in the footer — Sephora's App Fest at least led with the loyalty math up front.
