# LAST CHANCE ‼️ Up to 50% off select lip faves ✨
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-29**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this one delivers — same as last Wednesday's near-identical send, but I'll give credit where it's due. "Up to 50% off Select Lip Picks" up top, real product grid, real strikethrough pricing (MAC $25→$15, Wonderskin $22→$17.60), no bait-and-switch nonsense like the 7/26 email where the hero said 30% Charlotte Tilbury under a "50%" subject line. This is the third lip sale email from Sephora in a week though, and I'm starting to feel like I'm on a lip-product treadmill.
- The "0 points" line under my Insider badge is still an odd flex to lead with — nobody wants to be reminded they haven't earned anything yet, especially not before the offer. And that gray banner up top listing same-day delivery and in-store pickup with a superscript exclusion mark is Macy's-style fine-print energy dressed as a benefit — I have to guess what "exclusions/limits apply" actually excludes.
- Would I click? Yeah, probably — the grid is legible, the discounts are real, and Gisou and Summer Fridays both have "Clean" badges I actually care about. Would I forward it to a friend? No, this is routine maintenance, not a moment. I'd scroll straight to the grid, maybe tap Charlotte Tilbury since I'm out, and ignore the "Buy More, Save More" build-a-basket pitch at the bottom — that one always feels like Sephora doing math I didn't ask for, the same energy Kohl's brings with its "Extra 40%, better hurry" stacking.

## What's working

- The hero banner states the offer once, clearly — 50% off, National Lip Day tie-in, no repeated bait like the 7/26 email had.
- Eight products, eight visible strikethrough prices — I don't have to hunt for the discount, unlike Neiman Marcus's roundup which buries pricing behind styling copy.
- "Clean" badges on Gisou and Summer Fridays are a real trust cue for me — I care about formulation, not just price.
- Product photography is consistent and shoppable — this reads like a real catalog page, not a rushed template.
- Beauty Insider tier bar (Insider/VIB/Rouge) at the bottom at least shows me where I sit, even if the messaging above it undercuts it.

## What's weak

- "0 points" sitting right under my Insider badge at the top is a bad first note — it reads like a scolding, not a welcome.
- Third lip-focused email in about a week (7/25 save-the-date, 7/26, 7/27, now this one on 7/29) — the cadence is wearing thin, and nothing here differentiates it from Tuesday's send.
- The "Buy More, Save More" band with "no code needed, *terms apply" tucked in tiny type is the kind of buried fine print that makes me squint on my phone.
- "Just In From Dae" before/after photo block feels tacked on to a lip sale email — irrelevant to what I opened this for.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the "0 points" line entirely, or replace it with something that doesn't read as a status ding — if I have zero points, don't remind me, incentivize me.
- 2. Trim to one campaign per email — either the lip sale or the "Buy More Save More" Sephora Collection push, not both stacked in one send three days into the same lip promotion cycle.
- 3. Move the "Clean" badge callouts into the hero copy — "Shop Clean Lip Picks" would pull me in faster than a generic "Select Lip Picks."
- 4. Drop or relocate the Dae hair mousse block — it breaks the lip-sale focus and dilutes the one clear CTA I came for.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 50% off lip faves — today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Beauty Insider lip picks, up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `MAC, Charlotte Tilbury, Summer Fridays & more — real markdowns inside`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Clean beauty lip picks now up to 50% off, while they last`
