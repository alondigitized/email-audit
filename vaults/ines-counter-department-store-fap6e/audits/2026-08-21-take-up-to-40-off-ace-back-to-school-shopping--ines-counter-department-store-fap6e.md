# Take up to 40% off & ace back-to-school shopping 💯
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-21**
## Executive summary

- Fourth back-to-school Kohl's email this week, and here we go again — a kid at a locker bank is the hero shot, same beat as 8/20 and 8/19. I don't have kids in school anymore, so that top fold is dead air to me, same complaint I had two days running. But this one keeps scrolling and actually earns some credit: it breaks into real category tiles — Activewear, Jeans, Basics, Uniforms, Bedding, Kitchen & Dining — each with its own percentage off, its own product shot, its own "Shop Now." That's more useful than Tuesday's "Beauty up to 50%, Sephora at Kohl's" single-line save, because Bedding at 60% and Kitchen & Dining at 40% are things I'd actually buy regardless of school season.
- What stops me from clicking is the same thing that's dogged every Kohl's send this week: my status line up top says "5% REWARDS" and there's a green "earn Kohl's Cash" banner, but nowhere does it tell me how much Kohl's Cash I'm sitting on or personalize the offer to my tier. Compare that to Macy's "confirm now to get your extra 25% off" — clunky as that one was, at least it was built around my specific account status. This is a mass blast with my tier logo slapped on top, not a message that knows me.
- I wouldn't click through the hero. I might scroll to Bedding or the Kitchen & Dining tile if I were shopping already, and the "Picked with you in mind" product block near the bottom (Ninja blender, towels, mattress topper) is the only spot that felt remotely curated. Not forwarding this — it's the same promo skeleton as the last three days with a new coat of paint.

## What's working

- The category-tile layout (Activewear, Jeans, Basics, Uniforms, Bedding, Kitchen & Dining) gives me real, scannable percentages instead of one vague banner.
- Bedding at up to 60% off and Kitchen & Dining at up to 40% off are categories I'd shop in any season — smart to include non-school items in a back-to-school send.
- The "Picked with you in mind" strip with actual prices ($349.99 blender, $1.99–8.99 towels, $91.99–139.99 mattress topper) reads like real merchandising, not filler.
- Kohl's Rewards status and "Earn Kohl's Cash" are visible near the top, so at least the loyalty program isn't hidden.

## What's weak

- Fourth day in a row with a kid-at-lockers hero — I said this on 8/19 and 8/20 too, and Kohl's still hasn't rotated the creative for someone without school-age kids.
- My rewards tier is named but not personalized — no dollar amount of Kohl's Cash, no "you have $X to use," just a generic banner, unlike Macy's which at least ties its urgency to my specific account.
- The email is enormous — six product categories, a sweepstakes, a "fresh favorites for fall" block, an instant-Kohl's-Cash callout, AND a picked-for-you strip. By the time I hit Kitchen & Dining I've lost the thread of what the actual headline offer even was.
- "Coupons do not apply to select brands" shows up three separate times without ever saying which brands — that's the exclusionary fine print I flag every time a beauty-counter brand does this, and Kohl's is doing it worse by repeating it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a shoppable-for-everyone hero instead of back-to-school — Bedding or Kitchen & Dining at the top would pull in shoppers like me immediately instead of asking me to scroll past four screens of school gear first.
- 2. Replace the generic rewards banner with my actual Kohl's Cash balance — "You have $12 in Kohl's Cash" beats an unlabeled 5% tier badge every time.
- 3. Cut this email in half — six category tiles plus a sweepstakes plus a second "fresh favorites" grid plus a Kohl's Cash callout is too much; pick the top three offers and stop.
- 4. Name the excluded brands once, plainly, near the top offer instead of repeating vague "select brands" language three times.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Bedding up to 60% off — plus back-to-school savings`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Kohl's Cash is waiting: up to 60% off home + school`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New instant Kohl's Cash drops tomorrow — shop bedding, kitchen, and more now`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Six categories, one sale: activewear to bedding, all up to 60% off`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Standard promotional email built on Kohl's/CoherentPath ESP stack (s.kohls.com), heavily instrumented with tracking pixels and redirect links; core compliance signals (List-Unsubscribe headers, physical address, auth results) were not detected in the captured payload.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 75 tracking/click-redirect links detected and skipped by automated HTTP probe (domain: click.chp.kohls.com, click.s.kohls.com) — destinations unverified, cannot confirm they resolve without 404/loop errors.
- Multiple beacon/pixel endpoints present: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...`, `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...`, `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx...`, `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager) — standard open/click tracking plus third-party Demdex sync, no anomalies found in structure.
- Encoded tracking payload in the primary open pixel (`cp_tp=v3.eJy...`) is a compressed/base64 blob typical of CoherentPath — content not independently verifiable from source alone.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 8 images missing `alt` text (mostly 1x1 tracking pixels and redirect beacons — low visual impact, but two `.png` open/recap-tracking images and content images in the truncated body also lack alt attributes; recommend audit of content images specifically, since decorative pixels are lower priority than hero/promo images).
- MSO conditional comments and legacy DOCTYPE (HTML 4.01 Transitional) present — standard for Outlook desktop compatibility, no issues found.
- Responsive breakpoint at `max-width: 500px` with associated `.w_50pc`/`.w_60pc`/`.w_35pc` fixed-px fallback classes — no issues found in the truncated source.
- Web fonts (Roboto, Montserrat) loaded via `<link>` and `@import` from Google Fonts with no local fallback declared in the snippet shown — fonts.googleapis.com is a well-known email image-blocking risk (many clients strip external CSS/font requests), causing fallback to Arial/Helvetica; not a bug, but a rendering-fidelity risk worth flagging.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) found in the visible source.
- Recipient-specific identifiers present in tracking URLs (`mi_mid=01a022e7-...`, `mi_cid=67791fba63db790b`, `cp_cid=8ba359f132e3c109`) — functioning as expected for individual-level tracking, no malformed tokens observed.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- List-Unsubscribe header not found — QA flags this may be a relay artifact (AgentMail) rather than a true absence; cannot confirm compliance status from this capture.
- List-Unsubscribe-Post header (RFC 8058, one-click unsubscribe) not found — same caveat; if genuinely absent, this fails Gmail/Yahoo's 2024 bulk-sender requirements.
- No physical mailing address detected in the truncated HTML — CAN-SPAM requires this; could be present in the untruncated footer (not visible in this excerpt) — flag for confirmation rather than a confirmed violation.
- Authentication-Results header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not found — QA notes this is expected via AgentMail relay and status is unknown, not necessarily failing.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No standard `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` query params observed on links; tracking instead relies on ESP-proprietary params (`mi_u=604230016`, `mi_ecmp=1020264_2026821`, `email_name=260821_DG_Email_Friday`) — this is consistent with CoherentPath/Kohl's own attribution system rather than GA-style UTM tagging, so absence of `utm_*` is expected, not a defect.
- Cannot verify landing-page alignment: all destination links are wrapped in `click.chp.kohls.com`/`click.s.kohls.com` redirects and were skipped by the HTTP probe, so final landing URLs and page-content match are unconfirmed.

7. Recommendations
- Confirm whether the List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post / Authentication-Results headers are truly absent at the source (vs. stripped by the AgentMail relay) — if genuinely absent, add RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe headers to meet Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- Verify a physical mailing address exists in the (non-truncated) footer; if absent, add one for CAN-SPAM compliance.
- Add `alt` attributes to the two named content images (`916d6fa08d185233.png`, and related recap-tracking images) at minimum; 1x1 pixels can remain empty-alt (`alt=""`) as they already are functionally decorative.
- Re-run the link probe with redirects followed/allow-listed (or sample a subset manually) to confirm the 75 skipped tracking links resolve correctly and land on matching pages.
- Consider adding a local/system-font fallback in case Google Fonts requests are blocked by the mail client, given no `<style>` fallback stack was visible beyond `Montserrat,Arial,Helvetica,sans-serif` at the body level (which is itself a reasonable fallback — confirm it's applied consistently in the truncated sections not shown).
