# ICYMI: Last Call is up to 70% off
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-16**
## Executive summary

- There's that crimson block again. This is the fifth "LAST CALL"-adjacent send from Neiman's this week — 8/13, 8/15 twice, and now 8/16 with "ICYMI" stapled on the front, which is retailer-speak for "you ignored the first four, so here's a sixth." The stacked "EXTRA 25% OFF" on top of "UP TO 70% OFF" is the same math trick they ran on 8/15, and by now I can recite it in my sleep.
- That said, the merchandise grid below the fold is actually decent — Balmain, Manolo Blahnik, Isabel Marant, real designer names with real photography, not stock-photo filler. If I'm going to shop Last Call at all, I'd rather see six specific pieces than a generic "shop now" banner. But "ICYMI" as the whole subject-line hook tells me their segmentation isn't doing much — I opened three of their last four emails, this isn't a re-engagement to someone who's gone dark, it's just cadence fatigue dressed up as a callback.
- I'm not clicking. I already made my Last Call decision on Friday. This gets marked read and, honestly, I'm one more crimson blast away from unsubscribing — Sephora at least varies its visual register between sends, and even Macy's confirmation emails don't repeat themselves this fast.

## What's working

- The product grid has real, recognizable designer names (Balmain, Manolo Blahnik, Isabel Marant, Theia) with clean, consistent product photography — that's the kind of specificity that makes me pause.
- "SHOP SALE" button is unambiguous and sits right under the offer stack, no hunting required.
- The fine print at the bottom is honest and complete — exact end date/time (9/7/26, 10:00 PM CT), "no adjustments for prior purchases," interim markdown disclosure. Saks could learn something here.
- Category shortcut buttons (Handbags, Shoes, Women's Dresses) at the bottom are genuinely useful for someone who wants to jump straight to her lane.

## What's weak

- "ICYMI" as the subject hook is a tell — this is the fifth near-identical crimson "LAST CALL" blast in four days (8/13, 8/15 x2, now 8/16), and slapping "in case you missed it" on top just underlines that they're re-running the same play, not that they've noticed I've already seen it three times.
- Zero personalization or loyalty signal — no InCircle points mention, no "as a member" language, nothing that says they know I'm a regular versus a cold subscriber.
- The hero is 100% offer, 0% product — no hero image, no styling context, just typography on a red field, which is the least "beauty counter" this brand has looked all week.
- Visually identical crimson block to Friday's "Getting set for fall?" send — same color, same layout logic, just the percentages reshuffled.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending near-duplicate Last Call emails within 72 hours — if InCircle members are getting flagged for frequency capping, I'm clearly not on that list, and it's actively pushing me toward unsubscribe.
- 2. Add a loyalty line to the hero — even a small "InCircle members: exclusions may not apply to you" would make this feel earned rather than blasted.
- 3. Swap the "ICYMI" framing for something that acknowledges the grid below — the product mix is the actual asset here, lead with it.
- 4. Give the hero one hero product shot instead of pure type-on-red — Sephora's Gen X trio email proved a single well-chosen visual beats another all-caps banner.
