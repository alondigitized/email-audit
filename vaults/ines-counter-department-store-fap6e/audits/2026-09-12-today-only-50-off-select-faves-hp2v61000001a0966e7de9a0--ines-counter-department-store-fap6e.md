# Today only: 50% off select faves 🌶️
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-12**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is a lot. "50% off Select Beauty," "You have 5 points" at the top, then it swings into "HOT AND TRENDING" for a Kilian Paris cocktail-themed fragrance collection I have zero context for, then eleven — I counted — separate new-launch tiles running straight down the page: Saie, Laneige, Gisou, Obart, Nars, Josie Maran, Armani Beauty, Touchland. Then "Favorites Kits," then "Defined Curls," then "First Access: Holiday Drops." That's not an email, that's a catalog.
- Sephora, this is the fifth one of yours I've opened this week and it's the same problem every time — 9/10's "Stacking Weekend" one had four different math modules, 9/9 buried "50% off select beauty" under a whole Hair Styling Awards bake-off, and now today it's the opposite failure: the 50% off banner is right up top and clean, but then you abandon it entirely for a wall of new-product tiles that have nothing to do with the offer. I don't even know at the end of this email what's actually 50% off besides "Clarins, Estée Lauder, and OUAI" mentioned in tiny text.
- Would I click? Maybe the 50% off banner, since it's honest and up top. Would I forward it? No — there's nothing here personal enough to share, and Macy's "LAST CHANCE: confirm now" email from earlier this week at least felt like it was talking to me specifically about a deal I'd already engaged with. This one just reads as inventory dumped in my inbox.

## What's working

- The 50% off banner at the very top is clean — no fine-print maze, names actual brands (Clarins, Estée Lauder, OUAI) and says "TODAY ONLY," which reads as credible urgency, not manufactured.
- Beauty Insider points balance ("You have 5 points") is a nice touch of "we know who you are" — I clock that immediately as a member.
- Product photography is genuinely pretty — the Kilian bottle against the Parisian rooftop backdrop looks expensive, and that's the kind of aspirational imagery that fits a beauty-counter regular like me.
- "NEW - ONLY AT SEPHORA" and "CLEAN AT SEPHORA" badges give me trust signals I actually use when deciding what's worth trying.

## What's weak

- The 50% off hero completely disconnects from the rest of the email — I scroll five more screens and never see it again or find out which of these eleven new products, if any, are included.
- Way too many product modules stacked with no hierarchy — Kilian, then eight individual new-launch tiles, then two more promo banners, then Holiday Drops. My eye has nowhere to land twice.
- No prices shown on most of the new-arrival tiles beyond the Kilian set — Nars lip liner, Armani eye tint, Touchland sanitizer all show price but Gisou and Obart don't, which is inconsistent and annoying when I'm trying to decide fast on my phone.
- "Holiday Drops" showing up in a September email selling "50% off" today feels like whiplash — pick a season and commit to it.
- This is essentially the same sprawling, multi-offer format I flagged in the 9/10 "Stacking Weekend" send — nothing's changed structurally in two days.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this into two emails: one that's purely the 50% off event (banner + the actual included products, nothing else), and a separate "new arrivals" send for the Kilian collection and the product wall. Trying to do both kills the urgency of the discount.
- 2. If it must stay one email, repeat the 50% off CTA and show 2-3 discounted hero products with strikethrough pricing right under the banner — like your 9/12 "select beauty" send did with Clarins $96→$48 — instead of pivoting to full-price new launches.
- 3. Add consistent pricing to every product tile — Gisou and Obart are missing prices while their neighbors have them.
- 4. Drop "Holiday Drops" from a September 50%-off email; it's competing for attention and undercuts the "today only" urgency.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 5 points + 50% off select beauty, today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Clarins, Estée Lauder, OUAI: 50% off ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop the sale before it's gone — plus new Kilian Paris arrivals`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `50% off select faves, exclusions apply. See what's included.`
