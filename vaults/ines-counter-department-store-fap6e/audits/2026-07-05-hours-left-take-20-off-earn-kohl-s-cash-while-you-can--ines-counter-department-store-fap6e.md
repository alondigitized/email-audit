# HOURS LEFT ⏰  Take 20% off + earn Kohl's Cash while you can.
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-05**
## Executive summary

- Forty-four. That's where I've landed in my Kohl's mental tally since Memorial Day, and this one shows up screaming "HOURS LEFT" in all caps like I haven't been reading that exact energy every other morning for six weeks. I'll say this much: the Kohl's Cash callout up top earns a look from me, because points are the only reason I still open these. But then the email pivots to patio umbrellas, lawn chairs, a "party list" home picks section, and something about dressing like a soccer goal depends on it — and I'm standing there in my Gen X brain going, *who is this for?*
- This is the Kohl's I know: the department store that tries to be everything to everybody in a single send. The July 4th email from yesterday at least had a holiday hook to hold it together. This one's the morning-after hangover — same offer, same structure, none of the occasion. Compared to Sephora's July 3rd send, which used my first name and knew I was there for the beauty collection discount, this email treats me like a zip code, not a customer.
- I wouldn't click. I'd mark it read, maybe tap through to check my Kohl's Cash balance if I remembered it was expiring, and move on. I am absolutely not forwarding this to anyone.

## What's working

- **Kohl's Cash is front and center** — the green badge/button makes it pop visually and that's the one loyalty signal that keeps me from unsubscribing entirely.
- **Concrete percentage discount in the hero** — 20% off is stated clearly; I don't have to hunt for the actual deal.
- **FAST & FREE STORE PICKUP callout** — that matters to me at this point in the summer when I need something this weekend.
- **50% and 30% off tiles are readable** — discount depth is visible without squinting; the hierarchy on individual tiles works even if the overall email is chaos.

## What's weak

- **The "HOURS LEFT" subject shouts, and I stopped believing it two weeks ago** — Kohl's has cried wolf on urgency so many times that the caps register as noise, not action.
- **Zero beauty, zero personal care** — I'm a beauty counter regular and this email has patio furniture, kids toys, and athletic wear. Macy's at least throws a Lancôme tile in the mix when they're running department-wide promos.
- **"Dress Like the Goals Depend on It" section is a complete non-sequitur for me** — I don't know if this is a soccer promo or a motivational poster. Either way, it's not my life.
- **The layout is exhausting** — six distinct content buckets, at least four different visual styles, and nothing is asking me to care about any single thing. Neiman Marcus's recent "in-demand accessories" email was equally promotional but at least had editorial restraint.
- **No name, no segment signal** — I've been a Kohl's Cash earner for years. The July 4th send was warmer in tone; this one feels like a blast to a purchased list.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pick one category and own it for this send** — Beauty and personal care gets a dedicated send with Kohl's-carried brands (Neutrogena, e.l.f., whatever's in the mix). I'd open that. Stop trying to cover Kids + Home + Patio + Sports in one scroll.
- 2. **Retire "HOURS LEFT" from the subject vocabulary entirely** — At send #44, credibility is the only asset left and you're spending it on caps-lock urgency.
- 3. **Acknowledge the Kohl's Cash expiry if that's the real hook** — "Your Kohl's Cash expires Sunday" would get me to open and click immediately, no theatrics required.
- 4. **Surface the 50% off clearance higher** — That's actually a strong deal. It's buried below the fold when it should be the hero.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your Kohl's Cash expires soon — here's 20% to use it`
- **Subject Alt B:** `50% off + Kohl's Cash: make it count before tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Beauty, home, and more — tap to see what's waiting in your cart`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fast & free pickup still available. Offer ends tonight.`

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "Hours Left" Email

**Date:** 2026-07-05 | **From:** Kohls@s.kohls.com | **Subject:** HOURS LEFT ⏰ Take 20% off + earn Kohl's Cash while you can.

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Structurally sound HTML4 email with correct MSO/VML conditionals and responsive breakpoints. Primary concerns are compliance header gaps (likely an AgentMail relay artifact), a malformed HTML tag in the open-pixel block, and pervasive missing alt text on tracking pixels.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 tracking links skipped** — all clicks route through `click.chp.kohls.com` and `click.s.kohls.com` redirect domains; HTTP probes were not executed so link liveness is unconfirmed.

**Three distinct pixel/tracking systems** are present simultaneously:
- `click.chp.kohls.com` — CoherentPath behavioral pixel
- `mi.kohls.com` — MessageGears ESP pixel
- `kohls.demdex.net` — Adobe Audience Manager (AAM) pixel

This is expected for large retailers but creates redundant data collection with no technical issue per se. No issues found with link structure beyond the skipped probes.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Malformed HTML tag** — the open-pixel `<div>` block contains an unclosed/invalid custom tag:

```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>
```

The `</custom>` closing tag is invalid HTML. Most clients will silently ignore it, but it may trigger parser quirks in strict validators or older Outlook versions.

**`<title>` element is empty** — `<title></title>` provides no fallback label for screen readers or tab previews.

**Tracking pixels missing alt text (11 instances):**

| Image | Domain |
|---|---|
| `92424118-dbd6...` | click.chp.kohls.com |
| `o.gif` | mi.kohls.com |
| `event` | kohls.demdex.net |
| `2a5adc5afb647aad.png` + 4 others | mi.kohls.com |
| `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png` | mi.kohls.com |

Tracking/open-pixel images should carry `alt=""` (empty string) and `aria-hidden="true"` to suppress screen reader announcement. The `o.gif` pixel already has `aria-hidden="true"` but is missing `alt=""`. Others have neither.

**`user-scalable=0`** in the viewport meta tag prevents zoom on mobile — an accessibility regression for low-vision users.

**`maximum-scale=1`** compounds the above. Both are common in email but worth flagging if accessibility compliance is a requirement.

**Google Fonts loaded three times** — once via `<link>`, once via `@import` in a `<style>` block, and once more as a duplicate `@import`. This adds ~3 redundant HTTP round-trips on clients that render webfonts (Apple Mail, some Android).

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens detected in the truncated HTML. The `mi_u=604230016` and `mi_ecmp=1016678_202675` query params on MessageGears pixel URLs appear to be correctly populated subscriber/campaign identifiers, not unfired tokens.

`email_name=260705_DG_Email_Sun_PM` is visible in plain text on several image URLs — this is internal naming leaked into pixel params, not a deliverability issue but potentially exposes send cadence metadata externally.

One pixel URL ends with a dangling `&k` parameter with no value:
```
...&email_name=260705_DG_Email_Sun_PM&k
```
This suggests a template variable (`k=<value>`) that failed to render. Confirm whether this is intentional or a dropped token.

### 5. Compliance

| Check | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| List-Unsubscribe header | **WARN** | Not captured by AgentMail relay — may be present at SMTP layer, not surfaced in relay metadata |
| List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) | **WARN** | One-click unsubscribe not confirmed — required for Gmail/Yahoo bulk senders (>5k/day) since Feb 2024 |
| Physical mailing address | **WARN** | Not detected in truncated HTML — CAN-SPAM §7 requires a valid physical postal address |
| `noindex, nofollow` robots meta | Pass | Present — correct for email HTML |
| `no-referrer` referrer policy | Pass | Present |

**The three compliance WARNs are relay-artifact candidates** — AgentMail likely strips or does not forward SMTP-layer headers. Verify against raw SMTP headers from the original message, not the relay copy. The physical address omission is the only one requiring HTML-source confirmation on the full (untruncated) email.

All 22 click links route through `click.chp.kohls.com` redirect infrastructure. UTM parameter population cannot be confirmed without resolving the redirect chain, which was skipped by the QA probe.

The `mi_ecmp=1016678_202675` campaign ID on pixel URLs is consistent across all MessageGears pixels, indicating a single campaign attribution key — correct behavior.

No issues found beyond the unresolved redirect chain.
