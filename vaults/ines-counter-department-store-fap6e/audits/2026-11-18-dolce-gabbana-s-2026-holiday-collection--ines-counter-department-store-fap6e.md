## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source shows an SFMC template with internal developer comments left in the delivered markup, and a viewport meta tag that blocks pinch-zoom. The automated QA run (0 issues, 4 warnings) could not verify unsubscribe headers or authentication, and the source was truncated before the body, links and footer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **15 tracking links were skipped** by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains (QA INFO). Their destinations and status codes are unverified.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs:** 2825 of 3310 characters (about 85%) are URLs, which exceeds the 70% threshold (QA WARN). This usually means long tracked URLs are printed in full, which can hurt spam scoring and readability for text-only clients.
- No broken links were confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom is disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom on mobile. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) failure.
- **Comment before the DOCTYPE:** the large `<!--REQUIRED variables ... -->` block precedes `<!DOCTYPE ...>`. Content ahead of the doctype can trigger quirks mode in some legacy clients, particularly older Outlook/IE-based renderers.
- **Exact-match class selectors:** rules like `*[class=contenttable]`, `*[class=mobile]` and `*[class=hide]` only match elements whose entire `class` attribute equals that string. Any element with a second class will silently miss the mobile rules.
- **Hamburger nav depends on the checkbox hack:** `.navbar-checkbox:checked~.menu` with `.menu{display:none}`. Gmail and several Outlook builds do not support this, so the mobile menu may be unreachable there. I can't confirm the fallback behavior because the source is truncated.
- Minor: `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` has a trailing space, and `.show` declares `width` twice.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Template documentation leaks into the delivered HTML.** The head comment exposes internal Data Extension names and lookup logic: `NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`, the fields `EMAIL_ID`, `uEm` and `cmdid`, and `@user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256`. It also still contains the authoring instruction `CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW`, which suggests the scenario selection was never cleaned up.
- No unresolved `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` tokens are visible in the retrieved portion. The remaining HTML was not available to check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** (QA WARN). The check notes this may be stripped by the AgentMail relay, so it is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found** (QA WARN). Without it, RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is not supported. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders. The same relay caveat applies.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found** (QA WARN). SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture.
- The footer (physical address and unsubscribe link) was cut off by truncation, so I can't assess the CAN-SPAM body requirements.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Unverifiable. The 15 tracking links were not resolved and the visible source contains no hrefs. The `@refid` / `@base_refid` variables in the comment suggest campaign IDs are appended, but I can't confirm UTM presence or that landing pages match the content.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Strip the developer comment block** (the `REQUIRED variables` and `SET @user` comments) at send time, or move it to a server-side comment. It leaks internal DE and field names.
2. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag so users can zoom.
3. **Confirm raw headers** (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC) on the original message or at the ESP, since the relay may be stripping them.
4. **Shorten the plain-text part** by using short link labels or a hosted text version, to lower the URL ratio.
5. **Move the DOCTYPE to the first line** of the document, ahead of any comments.
6. **Relax the exact-match class selectors** to `[class~=...]` or `[class*=...]`, and verify that a fallback exists for clients without checkbox-hack support.
7. **Re-run the audit against the full HTML** and resolve the redirect links to check destinations and UTMs.
