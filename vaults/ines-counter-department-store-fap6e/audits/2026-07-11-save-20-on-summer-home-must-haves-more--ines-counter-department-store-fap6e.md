# Save 20%  on summer home must-haves (& more)🏡
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-11**
## Executive summary

- Forty-nine. That is my count of Kohl's since Memorial Day, and today they walk in as the home edit — soft bedding, pillows, clearance — with the exact same 20% off they have been recycling since yesterday. Let me be precise about what stings: three days ago it was 30%. Two days ago it was 20%. Yesterday it was 20% again. And now 20% is back, dressed up in summer linen. The math is moving the wrong direction and they think I didn't notice.
- The email itself is clean enough. The 20% hero is right at the top, the Kohl's Cash earn window is clearly labeled through July 19 (I will not spend fifty dollars at Kohl's without collecting that cash, ever), and the clearance section does earn some genuine attention — up to 70% on home is a real number. The pivot to a home-specific send is actually the right instinct after a week of generic apparel blasts. But the execution undercuts it. Neiman Marcus showed me "Sea-Breezy Style" without a single percentage sign last week and I opened it immediately. This feels like someone screaming the same word louder and wondering why I stopped listening.
- Mark as read. Forward to no one. I might open the clearance section later if I'm actually in the market for bedding — but that's the task talking, not this email.

## What's working

- **Rewards membership is front-and-center in the header.** Seeing "KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER | EARN: 5% REWARDS" confirms this isn't a cold blast — it knows who I am, at least nominally.
- **The 20% coupon barcode with a visible end date (July 16) is honest.** Date is there, stacking language is noted. I know exactly what I'm getting and when it expires.
- **Kohl's Cash earn window is explicit:** $10 for every $50 spent, through July 19. For someone who refuses to shop without points, that's the only reason I'd click at all.
- **Clearance at up to 70% is a genuine headline number.** That's not the same as "extra 20% off" again — that's actually interesting on its own.
- **Home-specific focus is smart segmentation.** After four generic sends, narrowing to a category is the right instinct.

## What's weak

- **The offer is a downgrade and the cadence proves it.** They went 30% → 20% → 20% → 20%. A customer paying attention — and I am — reads that as the deal getting worse, not better.
- **"Shop Now" is doing all the CTA heavy lifting.** Every button in this email says "Shop Now." Macy's at least tells me *what* I'm shopping. This could say "Shop Bedding" or "Explore Clearance" and actually differentiate the sections.
- **The 50% off section is visually lost.** Between the hero and the giant green CLEARANCE bar, the pillow/soft-home 50% offer gets swallowed. That might actually be the best deal in the email and I nearly missed it.
- **No beauty or personal care anywhere.** I'm a beauty counter regular who also shops home, but this whole send reads like it was built for someone who only shops home. There's no acknowledgment that I'm a multi-category shopper — Sephora at least attempts to give me editorial context. This is a floor display, not a stylist.
- **Four emails in four days.** Kohl's is eroding trust by volume alone. Neiman Marcus ran a beautiful editorial send and I was receptive; Kohl's is the friend who texts me the same meme every day.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Reduce to three sends a week maximum, rotate the discount up not down.** The credibility damage from 30% → 20% over four days is worse than sending less frequently. Save the high number for a real event.
- 2. **Rename every CTA to the section it lives in.** "Shop Bedding Deals," "Explore Clearance," "See All Sale Styles" — anything but four identical "Shop Now" buttons stacked vertically.
- 3. **Surface the Kohl's Cash earn window higher.** Right now it lives below the coupon barcode, which itself is below the clearance section. Lead with "Earn Kohl's Cash through July 19" in the sub-hero or pull it into the header where my rewards status already lives.
- 4. **Give the clearance section a specific product or category anchor.** "Save up to 70%" with a Shop Now button tells me nothing. "Comforters from $19.99" tells me something.
- 5. **Add one cross-category nod.** Even a small footer tile for beauty or personal care signals that Kohl's knows me as a full-basket shopper.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Bedding, pillows, clearance — the soft side of summer is on sale now.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your 20% coupon works on clearance too. Here's what to shop first.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "Save 20% on summer home must-haves" (2026-07-11)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is structurally sound for a legacy HTML4 table-based layout, but has three compliance gaps (unsubscribe headers, SPF/DKIM visibility) and pervasive missing alt text on tracking pixels and content images. No broken links or merge token leaks detected.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

22 click-tracking links were skipped during HTTP probing — all route through `click.s.kohls.com` and `click.chp.kohls.com` redirect domains. This is expected behavior, but means dead-link detection had zero coverage on destination URLs this send.

Three separate tracking pixel systems are firing in parallel:
- `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` — CoherentPath open pixel
- `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...` — MessageGears open pixel
- `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...` — Salesforce Marketing Cloud open pixel
- `kohls.demdex.net/event?...` — Adobe Audience Manager sync pixel

**Issue:** The `<custom>` tag after the third pixel (`</custom>`) is invalid HTML and not a recognized email client construct. It will be ignored by renderers but signals a template assembly error — a closing tag with no matching opener.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Alt text missing on 10 images** per QA — all are tracking pixels or rendered content images hosted on `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/`. Tracking pixels (`width="1" height="1"`) should carry `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"` (the MessageGears pixel does this correctly; the CoherentPath and SFMC pixels do not).

Content images at `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` are missing alt text entirely — this breaks screen reader accessibility and degrades the fallback experience when images are blocked (common in Outlook/corporate environments).

`user-scalable=0` in the viewport meta tag is a known accessibility violation (WCAG 1.4.4) — prevents users who rely on pinch-zoom on mobile.

`<title></title>` is empty. Some clients (Gmail web, iOS Mail) surface this in the tab or preview. It should be set to match the subject line or a descriptive fallback.

Google Fonts are loaded via both `<link>` and `@import` simultaneously — duplicate requests, minor performance waste, no functional impact.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unrendered merge tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`, `%%first_name%%`) detected in the visible HTML fragment. The `mi_u=604230016` parameter in tracking URLs is consistent across all MessageGears pixels, indicating subscriber ID was resolved correctly at send time.

The `mi_ecmp=1017306_2026711` parameter (campaign/send ID) is also consistent. No issues found.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header not present** in captured headers (QA WARN). This header is required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk sender policies (enforced since Feb 2024 for senders >5k/day). Its absence here is likely an AgentMail relay artifact — the header may exist in the actual SMTP envelope but was not forwarded. Confirm against raw SMTP headers from a direct seed account.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not present.** One-click unsubscribe via POST is required by Gmail/Yahoo policy for bulk senders. Same caveat — may be a relay gap, but needs verification.

**`Authentication-Results` header not captured** — SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status is unknown from this relay path. The sending domain `s.kohls.com` should be verifiable independently; this is a monitoring gap, not necessarily a delivery problem.

`<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` are present and correct — these suppress web crawler indexing of the email archive URL and suppress referrer leakage to linked sites.

CAN-SPAM physical address was not present in the truncated HTML fragment — unable to confirm. Must appear in footer.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All 22 tracked links route through `click.s.kohls.com` (SFMC) — UTM parameters, if any, would be appended at redirect resolution and are not inspectable from the source alone. No raw destination URLs are visible in the HTML to confirm UTM coverage.

The `email_name=260711_DG_Email_Saturda` parameter on several `mi.kohls.com` image URLs is truncated (255-char URL limit likely hit). This affects analytics attribution in MessageGears but not delivery.
