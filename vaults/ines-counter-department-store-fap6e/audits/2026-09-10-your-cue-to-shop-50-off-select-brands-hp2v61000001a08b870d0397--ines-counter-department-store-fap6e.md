# Your cue to shop: 50% off select brands 💸
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-10**
## Executive summary

- Sephora, honey. I open this one and I count — subject line, "Stacking Weekend" banner at the top, "50% off Select Beauty" halfway down, "4X points" further down, "5X" on another module — that's at least four different math problems before I've even had my coffee. This is the sixth Stacking Weekend send I've opened this week and by now I could recite the beats in my sleep: code SEPTBONUS, 500 bonus points at $75, ends today, ends today, ends today.
- The one genuinely nice thing buried in this avalanche is the Hourglass "Ambient Lighting Edit Unlocked Palette" — that's a real product with a real name and it's the kind of prestige item I'd actually stop scrolling for. Everything around it is noise: an app-download pitch, eight new-launch tiles I have to squint at, a Korean hair care callout, a "point of view" skincare device I've never heard mentioned once before now. Compare this to Neiman Marcus's gift-card email from 9/8 — one mechanic, one number, done. This is the opposite of that.
- Would I click? Maybe, straight to the Hourglass palette, if I can find it again. Would I forward it to a friend? No — I'd screenshot it and send it to my sister as a "look how unhinged Sephora's gotten" joke, not as a shopping tip.

## What's working

- The Hourglass Unlocked Palette hero shot is genuinely pretty and reads as a real "early access" moment, not just another discount screaming at me.
- Beauty Insider tier bar at the bottom (Insider / VIB / Rouge) at least acknowledges I might be a member — Kohl's does something similar with its rewards banner up top, though Kohl's puts it first, not buried at the footer.
- "50% off Select Beauty" callout has a clean red box with a clear code and "today only" — if I only saw that one module, it'd be fine.
- Barcode at the bottom for in-store use is a nice nod to how I actually shop — I return things at the counter, I don't do everything online.

## What's weak

- Way too many competing offers stacked vertically — Stacking Weekend, App Fun, Hourglass early access, eight new-drop tiles, 50% off, Korean hair care, 4X points, 5X points, a "point of view" device push, a Beauty Chat pitch — I lost track of what I'm actually supposed to do.
- The tiny product tiles ("KERASTASE," "TATCHA," "K18," "DIEUX") are illegible at this size on my phone — I'd have to pinch-zoom to even read what's in the gift sets.
- Nothing here is speaking to a 47-year-old the way, say, Saks does when it leans into "designer access" language — this reads like it's aimed at a 24-year-old TikTok shopper stacking hauls, not someone who buys her Charlotte Tilbury at the counter.
- Same SEPTBONUS/500-points mechanic I've now seen four times this week (9/4, 9/5 twice, 9/7) — the novelty is gone and it's starting to feel like they're just hoping I forget I already said no.
- "4X" and "5X" points shown in two different modules with zero explanation of what they multiply against — that's confusing, not exciting.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick ONE hero offer and lead with it — either the Hourglass early access or the 50% off, not both plus six other things. Right now the eye has nowhere to land.
- 2. Cut the tile count in half. Eight tiny "new launch" products in a grid is a browse page, not an email — send me three, with names I can actually read.
- 3. Retire the SEPTBONUS/500-points combo for at least a week. I've seen it enough times this week that it's numbing, not motivating.
- 4. Move the Beauty Insider tier bar to the top, the way Kohl's does with its rewards status — I want to know what applies to ME before I scroll through eight offers that might not.
- 5. Drop the "point of view" device pitch entirely from this send — it has nothing to do with Stacking Weekend and just adds clutter.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `New Ambient Lighting Edit palette, 24 hours before everyone else`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your tier, your points, your early access — ends tonight`
