# LAST DAY to earn $10 Instant Kohl's Cash when you spend $25 🤑
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-26**
## Executive summary

- I've been getting Kohl's Cash emails almost daily this week, and this one at least has the decency to slap "LAST DAY" on it instead of pretending it's some fresh 40%-off cliffhanger — after the whole "ending tonight, just kidding, extended, no really ending now" carousel I sat through on the 23rd, a straightforward deadline is a small mercy. But then I scroll and it's the same kitchen-sink layout Kohl's always runs: swimwear, sandals, a dress, home goods, and — new twist — a Sephora/Fenty tie-in wedged in the middle like they forgot to plan the email and just stapled two campaigns together.
- The Fenty by Rihanna block is actually the one thing that made me pause — 20% off is a real number and Fenty has enough cachet that I'd consider it, but it's buried between swimwear and blenders with zero context for why it's there. Compare that to what Sephora itself sent me for the Beauty Insider Celebration save-the-date — clean, one idea, no clutter. Kohl's can't resist cramming everything into one send.
- Would I click? Maybe on the Fenty tile out of curiosity, but I'm not opening my wallet app to hunt for a barcode mid-scroll on my phone. Forward to a friend? No — this reads like Kohl's testing how much they can fit in one email, not something that feels curated for me. Mark as read and move on.

## What's working

- The green "LAST DAY" urgency banner at top is at least honest urgency, not the fake extended-deadline game they've played all month.
- The Fenty Beauty tie-in with a real 20% off number and product shots is the one section that felt like it was speaking to an actual shopper, not just inventory clearance.
- Kohl's Rewards member status and "Earn 3x Rewards" callout up top acknowledges me as a member, not a stranger.
- The dress and sandals product photography is clean and seasonally appropriate — no stale spring copy in the middle of summer.

## What's weak

- This email tries to be five emails at once — swim, sandals, dresses, home, and Sephora/Fenty — nothing gets to breathe, and my eye doesn't know where to land first.
- The actual redemption mechanic (barcode + PIN) is dumped near the bottom in tiny text — that's real friction if I'm standing at checkout.
- "Deals for the summer plans you're about to invent" is a weird, try-hard tagline that doesn't match how I actually shop — I'm not inventing summer plans, I'm buying sandals I already need.
- The home goods section (bedding, bath, kitchen, patio, decor, outdoor toys) feels like a completely unrelated email got pasted in below the fashion section.
- Fine print at the bottom ("Not valid on Sephora at Kohl's") right under a Sephora-branded offer is exactly the kind of buried exclusion that makes an offer feel less honest.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this into two emails — one for the Kohl's Cash deadline with fashion, one for the Sephora/Fenty collab. Right now the Fenty offer, the best thing in here, gets lost in a home-goods pileup.
- 2. Move the barcode/PIN redemption info up near the hero, not buried past six product categories — if urgency is the hook, make it frictionless to act on immediately.
- 3. Cut the whimsical copy ("deals for the summer plans you're about to invent") for something concrete — tell me the deal, not a mood.
- 4. Put the Sephora exclusion ("Not valid on Sephora at Kohl's") directly under the Fenty offer, not buried in mouse-print at the very bottom.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Ends today — $10 Kohl's Cash when you spend $25`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 20% off Fenty Beauty by Rihanna, in stores and online through 7/28`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Redeem in-store or online — code and barcode inside`
