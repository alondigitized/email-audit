## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a table-based layout with `role="presentation"` on the main tables, a mobile breakpoint at 500px, and four tracking pixels. Confirmed defects are malformed markup, missing alt text on content images, and a viewport meta that blocks zoom. The compliance and authentication checks could not be confirmed from the data provided (see section 5).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverifiable links:** 68 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination status, redirect chains and UTM params are unverified.
- **Opaque click wrapper:** Visible links use `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABB7InYi…`, with the destination encoded in `qs`. The final URLs cannot be read from the source.
- **Four stacked tracking pixels:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/296c2ded-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1023146_2026104`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?X3RNWW6Z…&d=60265&bmt=0`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`
- **Redundant opens:** Three of these pixels are open or event beacons from three vendors, so open metrics will overlap. The `demdex.net` beacon (Adobe Audience Manager) is a third-party cross-domain call that some privacy proxies will block.
- **Subscriber ID in image URLs:** `mi_u=604230016` appears on every `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` image. These are per-recipient URLs, so they cannot be cached by a CDN or proxy.
- **Invalid markup near a pixel:** `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?…" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>` has a stray `</custom>` closing tag with no opener.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):**
  - Content images with no `alt`: `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`, `58819319_root_2x`. If these carry offer content, it is lost with images off or in a screen reader.
  - Tracking pixels with no `alt`: `296c2ded-…` and `o.gif`. The `o.gif` pixel is hidden with `aria-hidden="true"` but has no `alt`. The `296c2ded-…` pixel has no hiding at all.
  - Some pixels, such as `kohls.demdex.net/event`, have no `alt` and no `aria-hidden`.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty. It gives no accessible name for the web-view version.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Invalid head content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. It is invalid there, and parsers will move it into the body or drop it. Gmail also strips microdata.
- **Empty icon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty `href`.
- **Duplicate meta:** There are two `<meta name="format-detection">` tags.
- **Layout tables without role:** The tracking tables and the 640px preheader table (`<table width='640' …>`) lack `role="presentation"`.
- **Web fonts:** Google Fonts are loaded via `<link>` and `@import`. These are ignored by Gmail and Outlook, but the stack falls back to `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`, so there is no breakage.
- **Doctype:** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is paired with `xmlns` attributes. This is not harmful.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
All four QA warnings are unconfirmed and cannot be treated as defects yet:
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** Both are not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture them.
  - The sender `Kohls@s.kohls.com` is a major brand, so the headers are likely present at origin.
  - Verify against the raw MIME.
  - Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Physical address:** QA reports none detected. The supplied HTML is truncated before the footer, so this cannot be confirmed or ruled out from the data provided.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** Not found. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- **From address:** The `From` display name contains a typographic apostrophe (`Kohl’s`, U+2019). It is valid when RFC 2047-encoded, and nothing here shows a problem.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs unverified:** Campaign tagging could not be checked because destinations are encoded in `qs` and probing was skipped for all 68 links.
- **Campaign identifier:** The only visible one is `email_name=261004_DG_Email_Mystery` (on image URLs) plus `mi_ecmp=1023146_2026104`. Whether the same identifier carries through to link destinations is unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the eight content images. Add `alt=""` to every tracking pixel, with `aria-hidden="true"` and `role="presentation"`.
2. Remove the stray `</custom>` tag. Move the `schema.org` `<div>` out of `<head>` (or drop it) and remove the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon" href="">`.
3. Fill `<title>`. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta. Remove the duplicate `format-detection` meta.
4. Rerun the audit with the full, non-truncated HTML and raw headers. This will confirm the footer address, the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, and the `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) values.
5. Resolve a sample of the `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=` links with a redirect-following check. Confirm each destination returns 200 and carries consistent UTM or campaign params.
6. Consolidate the overlapping open pixels (`click.chp.kohls.com/o/…`, `mi.kohls.com/…/o.gif`, `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx`) if the metrics are redundant.
7. Fix the QA tool: the summary reports "0 issue(s), 4 warning(s)" but lists 14 warnings (3 compliance, 1 deliverability, 10 info). The pass-rate arithmetic needs checking.
