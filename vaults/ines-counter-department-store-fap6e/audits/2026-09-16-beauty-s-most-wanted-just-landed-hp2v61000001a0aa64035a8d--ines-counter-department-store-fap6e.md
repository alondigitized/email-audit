# Beauty’s most wanted just landed 🤩
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-16**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this one's a marathon, not a sprint. I count — what, eight separate "shop now" moments before I even hit the footer? The 50% off Select Beauty banner up top is good, "today only" urgency, real brands named (First Aid Beauty, Sephora Collection, Fenty). But then it just... keeps going. Beauty's Biggest Hits, then Charlotte Tilbury solo spotlight, then a nine-item grid that reads like a catalog dump, then a shampoo deal, then 5X points, then Kérastase. That's not an email, that's the whole store.
- The "You have 0 points" line is back again — same tone-deaf move I flagged on the 9/15 rewards email. If you're going to put my points balance in the header every single time, at least make it not a number that shames me into scrolling to "Rewards Bazaar."
- Would I click? Maybe the Charlotte Tilbury setting powder — that's a real, specific, "only at Sephora" callout with a price. Would I forward it? No. This is a "skim and archive" email, not a "hey look at this" email.

## What's working

- The 50% off hero up top is immediate — no hunting, brand names spelled out, "today only" gives me a real reason to act now.
- Charlotte Tilbury getting its own dedicated block with a price ($50) and "only at Sephora" is the kind of specific, ownable moment Neiman Marcus's clean "up to 40% off A.L.C." send nailed — one hero, one clear reason.
- The nine-product grid actually spans price points and categories I'd shop (lip balm, perfume, foundation, brow pencil) — not just skincare like the K18 send from 9/14.
- 5X points on 9/16 only is a real loyalty lever for someone like me who won't touch a card without points back.

## What's weak

- This email is exhausting. Macy's overload energy — 50% off, then Biggest Hits, then Charlotte Tilbury, then nine products, then shampoo, then points, then Kérastase. Pick one hero and commit.
- "0 points" sitting right under the header, again, is the same miss from yesterday's "Did you know that points = rewards?" email — nobody wants to be reminded they're at zero every single send.
- The nine-product grid has zero hierarchy — Jo Malone at $175 sits next to a $24 lip balm with equal visual weight. My eye doesn't know where to land.
- By the time I hit Kérastase Holiday Sets, I've completely lost the thread of what this email was even about.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this in half. Lead with the 50% off today-only banner, follow with ONE hero product (Charlotte Tilbury), and move the nine-item grid, haircare, and Kérastase into a follow-up send. This is three emails wearing a trenchcoat.
- 2. Fix the points display — show progress toward the next reward tier instead of a bare "0 points," the way Ulta's tier bar at least gives me something to work toward.
- 3. Give the product grid actual hierarchy — feature 2-3 items larger, demote the rest to a smaller row.
- 4. Move 5X points higher — that's my strongest personal motivator and it's buried two-thirds down.
