# Looking for the lowest prices? Check these out ➡️
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the ninth Kohl's email I've had in about two weeks. It's the same maroon "Lowest Prices of the Season" template as 10/19 through 10/22, so my gut reaction was a tired "I know this one." The layout is a long scroll of numbered price tiles. Ten categories in a row, from long-sleeve tees at $7.99 to a Shark vacuum at 35% off, and they all use the same block. I don't shop this way, and it reads like a flyer stuffed in the Sunday paper, not an email addressed to me.
- Kohl's hasn't gone back to the shouting from 10/18. The "ENDS TONIGHT" banners are still gone, and the Oct. 25 end date is stated plainly. The "Shop now. Decide later. Free returns through January 31, 2027" strip also respects the customer. I'd give that line more room, because returns are how I decide whether to buy at all. Still, I wouldn't click. Nothing here is beauty, nothing is designer, and nothing is for someone whose points matter more than a doorbuster. I'd read it, shrug, and move on.

## What's working

- The Kohl's Rewards member/earn strip sits at the very top, so I know I'm recognized as a member.
- The "Shop now. Decide later. Free returns through January 31, 2027" strip is the most customer-respecting thing in the email. Macy's would have buried that.
- The "Earn $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 spent" footer line gives me a points-style reason to buy. It's too low on the page, though.
- Every tile has a consistent price format with a "Reg." comparison, so the math is easy to follow.
- The photography is clean and the tiles are tidy, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- Ten near-identical tiles is a catalog, not a curated send. Nothing tells me which one to care about.
- There's no beauty, no fragrance, and no premium brand. As a counter regular I have no reason to be here. Sephora and Neiman's at least lead with a category I shop.
- The "Kohl's Cash!" banner is a squeezed green strip, and I can't tell if it's an offer or a link. It's the one piece of loyalty value and it's buried.
- The "Shop Now" links are small underlined text. On a phone those are poor tap targets.
- Fine print like "Coupons do not apply" and "select styles" is repeated and hedged. The 85% off clearance line says "up to," so the real number is unclear.
- The footer legal block is dense and tiny, and I can't read it without zooming.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut ten tiles to three or four, and lead with the one offer that matters. Make the Kohl's Cash earn rate the hero, not the footer.
- 2. Move the free-returns promise up to the top. It's the trust cue that earns a click from someone like me.
- 3. Turn the underlined "Shop Now" links into full-width buttons for mobile.
- 4. Give the repeated daily sends a rest. Nine emails in two weeks wears out the "lowest prices" claim.
- 5. Add a beauty or fragrance tile for subscribers who shop those categories. That would be real segmentation.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit: Kohl's "Looking for the lowest prices? Check these out ➡️"

**1. Technical Summary**
The email has real markup defects: a stray `</custom>` tag, a `<div>` inside `<head>`, and a viewport that blocks zoom. It also has missing alt text on content images. Compliance and authentication can't be confirmed because the HTML is truncated and the relay likely stripped headers. QA reported 0 issues and 4 warnings (43% pass rate).

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- QA skipped 73 tracking links because they are redirect domains. No destination was probed, so link health is **unverified**, not confirmed broken.
- Links go through `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7…` with an opaque encoded `qs` token. The final landing URLs can't be read from the source.
- At least four tracking and personalization systems fire on open:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/f4e8cabc-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1024390_20261023`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`
- The first image (`click.chp.kohls.com/o/...`, 1x1) has no `alt` attribute. The others are decorative pixels.
- `<img height="0" width="0" src="https://kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196">` is a third-party Adobe Audience Manager call. It has no `alt`, and it is a privacy and deliverability consideration.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Stray closing tag:** `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?…" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>` closes an element that was never opened, so the HTML is invalid.
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization"><meta itemprop=…></div>` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will end the head early at the `<div>`. Any head content after it, if present, would then be treated as body content.
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` prevents pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Empty `<title></title>`** and **empty `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`**.
- **Mixed syntax:** The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional, but the source uses XHTML-style `/>` self-closing tags.
- **Duplicate font loading:** Roboto and Montserrat are each requested twice, through `<link>` and `@import`. `@import` is unsupported in several clients. The `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` fallback stack is in place.
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):**
  - Product images: `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/58966978_2sa_2x`, `_1sa_2x`, `_11sa_2x`, `_14sa_2x`
  - Dynamic Movable Ink images: `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`
  - These are content images, so with images off or a screen reader the offers are not conveyed.
- **QA false positive:** `mi.kohls.com/.../o.gif` is flagged, but the source has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`. That is correct for a decorative pixel, so no fix is needed.
- **Layout tables:** The main layout tables use `role="presentation"`, which is correct. The preheader table and the tracking tables do not.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found in the visible source. No unrendered tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, or `[FirstName]` appear. `mi_u=604230016` is a resolved subscriber ID in Movable Ink URLs. The truncated HTML means the rest of the body is unchecked.

**5. Compliance**
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both absent. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**.
- **Physical address:** QA did not detect one. The source is truncated before the footer, so this is **unconfirmed**. Kohl's footers normally carry an address.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is **unknown**. The From domain `s.kohls.com` is aligned with the tracking domains, which is consistent but not proof.
- **In-body unsubscribe link:** not visible in the truncated source.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity**
- **UTM parameters:** none are visible in the readable URLs. Attribution relies on `mi_ecmp=1024390_20261023` and `email_name=261023_DG_Email_Friday` on Movable Ink URLs, plus the opaque `qs` tokens. UTM coverage and landing-page alignment **can't be verified** without resolving the redirects.
- **Campaign tags:** The Friday label and the 20261023 date in the campaign tags match the send date, 2026-10-23, which is a Friday.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Remove the stray `</custom>` tag and move the `itemscope` `<div>` out of `<head>`. Use JSON-LD or the body instead.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and `user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four `58966978_*sa_2x` product images and the seven `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` images. Add `alt=""` to the `click.chp.kohls.com` and `demdex` pixels.
4. Set a non-empty `<title>`, and remove or fill the empty `rel="shortcut icon"` link.
5. Drop the duplicate `@import` font declarations.
6. Re-run the audit against the raw message from the original sender, with full headers and untruncated HTML, to confirm List-Unsubscribe, RFC 8058, the physical address, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
7. Follow a sample of the `click.s.kohls.com` redirects to check final URLs, UTM parameters, and landing-page status.
