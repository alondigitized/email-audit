# Up to 70% off new sale arrivals
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-18**
## Executive summary

- Twenty-two. And yes, I'm still counting. After yesterday's "just kidding, one more day" gift card extension, Neiman Marcus woke me up this morning with a pivot to pure clearance — the LAST CALL branding is right there in the hero, big and red, with a clean "EXTRA 25% OFF / UP TO 70% OFF" hierarchy that I actually respect. The layout is the most restrained thing they've sent all month: no countdown clocks, no stacked promo blocks, just a designer product grid underneath the offer. Isabel Marant, Balmain, Manolo Blahnik, Stuart Weitzman — that list makes me sit up.
- The problem is credibility. When a brand tells you "last chance" on Tuesday and then "extended one more day" on Wednesday, and then Thursday morning it's "LAST CALL" again — the urgency has been wrung dry. I've read "Exclusions Apply" under enough Neiman Marcus offers to know it means "not the things you actually want." That small-text line does real damage here.
- I'd mark this as read and maybe open it later. I wouldn't forward it. But honestly? Balmain and Manolo in the product grid might make me click anyway. That's the paradox of a good product roster underneath a fatigued send.

## What's working

- **Red hero with clean offer hierarchy** — "EXTRA 25% OFF" in white above the LAST CALL logo, CTA button immediately below. The eye travels straight down in the right order.
- **Designer brand grid lands well** — Isabel Marant, Balmain, Stuart Weitzman, Manolo Blahnik, Weekend Max Mara. These are my cross-shopping brands; Saks and Nordstrom carry the same names, and seeing them here with a clearance markup is genuinely compelling.
- **No render bugs** — Clean mobile render, no broken image gaps, no overlapping text. After some Macy's email misfires I've seen this season, I notice when things actually load correctly.
- **"Save on these designer styles before they're gone"** — That one line of copy under the hero does more urgency work than the whole gift card extension series did last week.
- **Segmented category nav at the bottom** — SHOP WOMEN'S CLOTHING, SHOP HANDBAGS, SHOP SHOES, SHOP WOMEN'S DRESSES. This is how I actually browse a sale; I don't enter through "SHOP SALE" — I go straight to shoes.

## What's weak

- **"LAST CALL" rings hollow the day after "extended one more day"** — Yesterday Neiman Marcus told me the gift card promo was extended. Today it's LAST CALL on sale styles. I've lost the ability to feel the deadline.
- **"Exclusions Apply" in small print under a 70% claim** — Every time I see this, I assume the 70% is on the things nobody wanted and the good stuff is 20% at best. Kohl's at least tells me what the exclusions are; Neiman just sprinkles doubt.
- **No InCircle mention anywhere** — I have a card. I earn points. This email treats me like a stranger walking in off the street. Sephora's 10% off email last week explicitly acknowledged my Insider tier; that's the bar now.
- **Product thumbnails are small** — The nine-item grid is tight on mobile. Rugchichi and AREA in particular are hard to parse at that size — I genuinely can't tell if those are pieces I'd want without zooming.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with an InCircle hook** — "Your InCircle access: Extra 25% starts now" in the hero subhead would make me feel known. This is the single highest-lift change.
- 2. **Kill the fine print ambiguity** — Either state the key exclusions inline ("Excludes Beauty and Fine Jewelry") or remove the hedge. Buried "Exclusions Apply" poisons every % promise.
- 3. **Rotate the LAST CALL cadence** — After yesterday's extension email, this send needed one beat of breathing room or a genuinely different angle (a specific hero product, a stylist pick, a member-only window) to restore urgency credibility.
- 4. **Make the product grid clickable by brand** — Nine thumbnails with brand labels are fine, but linking each directly to that brand's sale page (not the general SHOP SALE landing) would convert browsers like me who go straight to Stuart Weitzman's markdown.
- 5. **Preheader should name a brand or category** — If the preheader can name one brand from the grid ("Balmain, Manolo, Stuart Weitzman — now at Last Call prices"), that changes what I see in my inbox before I open.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stuart Weitzman, Weekend Max Mara, Cinq à Sept — sale prices drop further today.`

## Technical Audit — Neiman Marcus "Up to 70% off new sale arrivals"

### 1. Technical Summary

The email uses a Salesforce Marketing Cloud AMPscript template with a critical personalization token rendered inert by a misplaced comment wrapper. QA also flags missing RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe support and unverified authentication headers, both of which create deliverability and compliance exposure.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**26 tracking/click-redirect links — not probed**
All outbound links route through a click-redirect domain. QA skipped HTTP probing on all 26. No confirmation that redirects resolve to live landing pages or that UTM parameters are present.

**`@refid` CONCAT is a no-op**
```
@refid = CONCAT(@base_refid,"")
```
Concatenating with an empty string produces an identical value to `@base_refid`. If a campaign suffix was intended (e.g., `CONCAT(@base_refid,"_nm")`), it was never added. All 26 tracking links likely carry an incomplete refid.

**Plain-text body is 86% URLs by character count**
QA: 5,225 of 6,058 plain-text characters are URLs. This ratio triggers spam classifier penalties on most major MTAs (Gmail, Outlook) and is a known cause of inbox demotion.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`maximum-scale=1` blocks user zoom (WCAG 1.4.4)**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">
```
Prevents pinch-to-zoom on mobile. Fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text, Level AA). Apple Mail on iOS partially overrides this, but it remains non-compliant.

**Three commented-out viewport alternatives in `<head>`**
```html
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, user-scalable=no" />-->
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=320, initial-scale=0.29">-->
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=1024">-->
```
Dead code suggesting prior A/B testing or unresolved template cleanup. No rendering impact, but adds parse overhead and signals stale template hygiene.

**iOS9 webkit animation bugfix (stale)**
```css
body { -webkit-animation: bugfix infinite 1s; }
@-webkit-keyframes bugfix { from { padding: 0; } to { padding: 0; } }
```
Targeted a hamburger-nav rendering bug in iOS 9 (EOL 2019). Harmless, but dead weight in the stylesheet.

**`<title>` has trailing whitespace**
```html
<title>Neiman Marcus </title>
```
Cosmetic only; visible in browser tab if the email is viewed in a web version.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Critical: `@user` SHA256 assignment is inside an HTML comment — it will not execute**
```html
<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */-->
```
This is a commented-out HTML block, not an AMPscript comment. AMPscript inside `<!-- -->` is not parsed by SFMC. If `@user` is referenced anywhere in the email body (e.g., for hashed-email tracking pixels, identity stitching, or CDP sync), it resolves to an empty string. The surrounding AMPscript block says "CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW," implying a second scenario should follow — it does not appear in the source.

**Lookup failure risk on `@uEm` and `@cmdid`**
```
@uEm = Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "uEm", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email)
@cmdid = Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "cmdid", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email)
```
Both rely on a separate CRM feed DE. Subscribers not present in `NM_CRM_Feed` will produce empty values with no visible fallback or error handling in the template header. If either token is embedded in tracking URLs, those URLs will silently break for unmatched records.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header — status unverified**
QA warning: header not captured by the AgentMail relay. This may be a relay-capture artifact rather than a true absence, but it cannot be confirmed from available data. If genuinely missing, CAN-SPAM and GDPR compliance is at risk.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) — not found**
One-click unsubscribe as required by Gmail (Feb 2024 sender policy) and Apple Mail is not supported. Gmail's spam rate thresholds apply more aggressively to senders missing this header. For a commercial sender at Neiman Marcus's volume, this is a meaningful deliverability risk.

**Authentication-Results — not captured**
QA: SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status unknown due to AgentMail relay stripping or not forwarding the header. Cannot confirm `e.neimanmarcus.com` is properly authenticated. Absence of this header in relayed form is expected but should be spot-checked against raw header capture from a seed inbox.

**CAN-SPAM physical address** — cannot verify from truncated HTML; footer was not included in the provided source.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**UTM parameters — unverifiable**
All 26 outbound links use click-redirect domains that were not probed. UTM parameter presence on final destination URLs cannot be confirmed. Given the no-op `@refid` CONCAT noted above, campaign attribution is suspect even if UTMs are present.

**`@base_refid` sourced from Campaign Calendar DE**
If this email was deployed outside the normal Campaign Calendar workflow (e.g., manual send, API trigger), `@base_refid` may be empty, producing a null refid across all links. No fallback default is declared in the template header.
