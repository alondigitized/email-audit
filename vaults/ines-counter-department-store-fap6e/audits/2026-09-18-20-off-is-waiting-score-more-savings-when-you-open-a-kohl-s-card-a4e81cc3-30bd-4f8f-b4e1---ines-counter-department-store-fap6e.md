# 20% off is waiting 👀 Score more savings when you open a Kohl's Card ...
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-18**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's, again — this is the fourth near-identical send I've logged this week (the "Customer Appreciation Event" on the 15th, the Rewards member push on the 16th and 17th, and now this one), and the tiered discount ladder at the top — 30% cardholder, 20% Rewards, 15% nonmember — is copy-pasted right down to the little dotted-border boxes. Where this one actually diverges a bit is the middle section: the Martha Stewart collection callout with her actual headshot is a genuinely nice trust cue I haven't seen from Kohl's before, and "up to 25% off kitchen appliances" at least gives me a reason to scroll past the discount ladder I've now memorized.
- But then it falls apart into an endless scroll of category buttons — Women, Men, Kids, Home, Bedding, Bath, Storage, Decor, then ANOTHER row of Women/Men/Kids, THEN Home/Beauty/Shoes again. That's Macy's-level overload, and honestly worse than Macy's "confirm now for 25% off" I flagged last time — at least that one was short. Neiman Marcus's $50-per-$200 gift card mechanic and even Sephora's flawed "free" sample both respected my scroll thumb more than this does.
- I'm not clicking. I've seen this exact discount structure three times in four days and my finger is tired. Marking as read, not forwarding.

## What's working

- The Martha Stewart collection module with her real photo is a legitimate trust/aspirational cue for a beauty-counter, brand-name shopper like me — better than anything Kohl's has shown me this week.
- Kohl's Cash banner ("Earn $10 for every $50 spent") is a clear, concrete loyalty mechanic I understand instantly.
- The three-tier discount box up top is visually clean and color-coded, so even skimming I know exactly which bucket I'm in as a Rewards member (20%).
- Product tile section near the bottom ("Picked with you in mind") shows real prices with strikethroughs (Serta topper $91.99, orig. $229.99) — that's honest, specific pricing.

## What's weak

- This is the same 30/20/15 discount ladder I reviewed on 9/15, 9/16, and 9/17 — the cadence is exhausting and it's starting to numb me to the offer entirely.
- Category button overload — Women/Men/Kids/Home appears TWICE as a button grid, plus Bedding/Bath/Storage/Decor, plus Beauty/Shoes/Sephora — that's nine-plus CTAs competing for my attention with zero prioritization.
- "There's always room for another great deal" and "Your 'just one thing' plan had a good run" are cute copy but say nothing concrete — filler headlines between button grids.
- Nothing here speaks to me as a beauty-counter regular specifically; the Sephora-at-Kohl's tile is buried as one of six equal-weight icons, not elevated the way Sephora or Saks would treat it.
- Subject line ("Score more savings when you open a Kohl's Card") is pushing store-card acquisition, not a product I care about — that's a sales-team goal, not a customer-need goal.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email in half — pick ONE hero offer (Martha Stewart collection or the kitchen 20% off) and drop one of the two redundant category-button grids entirely.
- 2. Stop sending the same 30/20/15 ladder four times a week — rotate creative or at minimum change the hero image so loyal openers don't feel like they're rereading Tuesday's email.
- 3. Elevate the Sephora-at-Kohl's tile with its own section and a specific offer, the way Sephora's own emails lead with fragrance/beauty first — right now it's one icon among nine.
- 4. Replace vague filler headlines ("just one thing plan had a good run") with a specific hook tied to the Martha Stewart launch, which is the actual news here.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Martha Stewart's new kitchen line is here — plus your 20% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 20% off + first look at Martha Stewart's collection`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New cookware, mixers, and more from Martha Stewart — shop before it sells out`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Kitchen essentials up to 25% off, plus your member discount inside`
