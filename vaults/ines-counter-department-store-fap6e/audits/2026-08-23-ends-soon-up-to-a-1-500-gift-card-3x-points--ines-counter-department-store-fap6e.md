# Ends soon: Up to a $1,500 gift card + 3x Points
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the reminder version of the same gift-card-and-Triple-Points play Neiman's already ran yesterday ("Reminder: Earn up to a $1,500 gift card today + Triple Points"), just retitled "Ends soon." I recognized the spend table on sight — $500 gets you $75, $10,000+ gets you $1,500 — because I already read it 24 hours ago. That's not a fresh idea, that's a countdown clock bolted onto yesterday's email.
- What actually got my attention is the merchandising below the fold: La Mer moisturizer, Burberry, Versace loafers, Jimmy Choo, Dolce&Gabbana — that's a real InCircle-caliber assortment, not filler. It signals "we know what you buy" even if the top of the email is pure math. And I'll say it again, like I did yesterday: this is a better-built tiered offer than Macy's "confirm now" nonsense or Kohl's back-to-school kid banner running for the fifth straight day. Neiman's at least shows its work.
- Would I click? Only because the deadline (8/25 at 10PM CT) is now genuinely close and I have real spend coming up for fall. Would I forward it? No — nothing here is new enough to share, I'd just point a friend to yesterday's send if I'd kept it.

## What's working

- The spend/earn table is right up top, unmissable, same clean math as yesterday's version — no clicking through to find out what $2,500 gets me.
- Triple Points banner is called out separately in a bold black bar so InCircle members don't have to hunt for their bonus.
- Product grid is genuinely elevated for me — La Mer, Burberry, Versace, D&G, Jimmy Choo — this is the beauty-and-designer mix I actually shop.
- Exclusions are flagged right under the table ("Exclusions Apply") instead of buried six paragraphs down like some retailers do.

## What's weak

- I already got this exact offer yesterday as "today" urgency — now it's "ends soon" urgency. Running the same table two days running just tells me the first deadline wasn't real.
- No visible reference to Neiman's Fashion Passport or InCircle tier status by name — it says "InCircle Members" generically, not "as a [tier] member, you earn X." Saks does the same tone-deaf blanket-membership thing; I'd like to see my actual tier called out.
- "SHOP NEW FALL COLLECTIONS" header sits oddly above a gift-card offer — it's not clear if I'm shopping new arrivals or chasing points, and the hero doesn't resolve that tension.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop re-sending the identical table two days in a row under a new subject — if the deadline moved, say so explicitly ("now through 8/25") instead of implying new urgency on stale math.
- 2. Name my actual InCircle tier in the Triple Points banner, not just "InCircle Members" — that's the one personalization move Neiman's keeps skipping across every send I've reviewed this week.
- 3. Lead with one hero product from that gorgeous grid (the La Mer or the Versace loafer) paired with the gift-card math, instead of table-first, products-as-afterthought.
