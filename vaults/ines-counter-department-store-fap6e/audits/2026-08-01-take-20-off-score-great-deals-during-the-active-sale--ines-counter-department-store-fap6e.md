# Take 20% off 🙌 Score great deals during the Active Sale 💪
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-01**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again, and I actually recognize the "Friends & Family" banner from the last three sends this week — same blue, same navy circle badge, at least a fresh subject line this time ("Score great deals during the Active Sale"). What's different — and honestly what stops my thumb for half a second — is the Under Armour "Up to 40% Off Active Sale" block halfway down. That's a real, specific offer with an actual dollar-off number attached to an actual brand, unlike the vague "20% off with coupon" that's been the whole pitch all week.
- But then it sprawls into bedding, bath, kitchen, patio, decor, outdoor toys — nine unrelated categories in one email — and I lose the thread completely. This isn't a targeted send, it's Kohl's dumping their entire warehouse into my inbox and hoping something sticks. Compare that to Sephora's "Beauty Insider Celebration" email, which stayed locked on one program and one moment the whole way down. Kohl's can't resist the kitchen sink.
- Verdict: I'd skim past the top banner (seen it, seen it, seen it), pause at the Under Armour block because I might actually need shoes for the gym, and then close the email before I hit the home goods section. Not forwarding this — nothing here is a "look what I found" moment, it's just Tuesday at Kohl's.

## What's working

- The Rewards status bar up top ("Kohl's Rewards Member, Earn 5% Rewards") is a nice touch — it acknowledges I'm a known customer before it asks me for anything.
- The Under Armour "Up to 40% Off" panel is the strongest single offer in the whole email — specific brand, specific number, clear "Shop Active Sale" button.
- Barcode + PIN + code printed right in the email (MYFAMILY20) means I don't have to dig for a coupon if I'm heading to a store — that's a real convenience for someone who shops the counter and returns in person like I do.
- Kohl's Cash callout ("$10 for every $50 spent") is at least concrete math I can do in my head at checkout.

## What's weak

- Nine product categories crammed into one email (Women, Men, Kids, Home, then Bedding, Bath, Kitchen, Patio, Decor, Outdoor Toys) — this is Macy's-style overload with none of Macy's occasional restraint.
- I've now gotten some version of this maroon-or-blue "Friends & Family 20% off" banner four times this week (07-30, 07-31 x2, 08-01) — the fatigue is real, and reusing the identical badge treatment makes it feel like I'm not being read as an individual, just blasted.
- The home goods section has zero connection to "Active Sale" or "Friends & Family" — it reads like two completely different emails stapled together.
- "Coupons do not apply to select brands" buried under the Under Armour offer is exactly the fine-print vagueness that makes an offer feel less honest — which brands? Say it or don't put the asterisk there.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this into two emails: one for the Active Sale (Under Armour, activewear, shoes — that's a real event with real urgency), one for home goods. Mashing them together dilutes both and makes neither feel curated.
- 2. Lead with the Under Armour 40% off block instead of burying it as the third scroll-stop — that's the only offer in here with teeth.
- 3. Retire the maroon/blue "Friends & Family" banner for at least two weeks. I've seen it too many times to register it as anything but wallpaper now.
- 4. Name the excluded brands in the fine print instead of "select brands" — Sephora and Nordstrom are more upfront about exclusions and it reads as more trustworthy.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 40% off Under Armour — Active Sale is on`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 20% off + Kohl's Cash, before Aug. 2`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Active Sale: Under Armour, adidas & more up to 40% off`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stack your 20% off with $10 Kohl's Cash per $50`

# Technical Audit: Kohl's "Active Sale" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
Legacy HTML4.01/MSO-hybrid responsive template (CoherentPath ESP, `s.kohls.com`/`mi.kohls.com`/`click.chp.kohls.com` infrastructure) with four tracking pixels firing before body content. QA run shows a 43% pass rate driven by unverified auth/compliance headers and multiple missing-`alt` warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 4 open/beacon pixels fire pre-content: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/146a6286-...` (CoherentPath open pixel, carries `mi_mid`/`cp_cid`/`cp_tp` payload), `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`, `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?UA2LNOKOTFQULM3WFBRP6SXNFM.60262`, and `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe AAM).
- Malformed markup: the `click.s.kohls.com` open pixel is followed by a stray `</custom>` closing tag with no matching opener — invalid HTML, likely a template artifact. Most clients will silently ignore it, but it should be removed.
- 58 tracking/redirect links were skipped by the automated HTTP probe (click-redirect domains) — their live destinations are unconfirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- HTML4.01 Transitional doctype + MSO conditional comments + `max-width:500px` mobile media query block — standard cross-client hybrid pattern, no issue.
- `meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow"` and `meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer"` are present — correct hardening for a hosted email view.
- Missing `alt` confirmed in source on: the `click.chp.kohls.com` open pixel and the `kohls.demdex.net` AAM pixel (neither has an `alt` attribute).
- QA also flagged `mi.kohls.com/.../o.gif` as missing alt, but the source shows it already has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"` — correctly marked decorative. This is a QA false positive, not a real issue.
- QA additionally flagged 6 `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` tracking images as missing alt; these fall outside the provided HTML excerpt (truncated) and can't be independently verified here, but are taken as confirmed per automated findings.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) appear in the visible source, and tracking params (`mi_mid`, `cp_cid`, `cp_tp`) are populated with resolved values rather than literal placeholders. Body content is truncated, so name-personalization in the visible copy can't be confirmed either way.

## 5. Compliance
- List-Unsubscribe header: not detected — QA notes this may be an AgentMail relay capture limitation rather than a confirmed absence at the source; needs verification against raw headers.
- List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058): not detected, same caveat — one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed.
- No physical mailing address detected in the scanned HTML — CAN-SPAM requires one. Given the HTML was truncated before the footer, this may simply be unscanned rather than absent; needs confirmation against the full body.
- Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM): not detected — QA attributes this to the relay; sender authentication status is unknown from this data.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- Outbound CTA links are wrapped in `click.chp.kohls.com`/`click.s.kohls.com` redirectors; all 58 were skipped by the probe, so destination URLs and UTM parameters on the final Kohls.com landing pages can't be confirmed.
- Campaign identifiers present for cross-referencing this send: `cp_cid=8ba359f132e3c109`, `mi_ecmp=1018841_202681`, `email_name=260801_DG_Email_Sat_PM`.
- No landing page HTML was supplied, so alignment between email offer and landing page can't be assessed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the orphaned `</custom>` tag following the `click.s.kohls.com` open pixel.
2. Add `alt=""` to the two confirmed alt-less tracking pixels (`click.chp.kohls.com` open pixel, `kohls.demdex.net` AAM pixel) and the QA-flagged `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` pixels for HTML validity.
3. Re-verify List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, and Authentication-Results directly against raw send headers (outside the AgentMail relay) before treating these as real compliance gaps.
4. Confirm the physical mailing address is present in the (unscanned) footer of the full email body.
5. Spot-check a sample of the 58 redirect links directly to confirm live destinations and UTM parameter presence on landing pages.
