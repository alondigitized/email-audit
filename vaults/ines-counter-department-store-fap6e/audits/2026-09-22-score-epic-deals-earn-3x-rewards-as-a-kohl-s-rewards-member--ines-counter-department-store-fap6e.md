# Score Epic Deals + earn 3x rewards as a Kohl's Rewards member 🙌
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-22**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is Kohl's again — sixth or seventh one this week, and it's the same "Epic Deals" wall I flagged on the 22nd, just now with a Sephora at Kohl's tile squeezed in between the sweaters and the bath towels. This is not an email, it's a circular. Sweaters, towels, sneakers, fleece, pajamas, sheets, a slow cooker, a mattress topper, jeans — I count at least ten unrelated categories stacked top to bottom with a red "Epic Deal" sticker slapped on each one like it's a garage sale.
- There is exactly one thing here that made me pause: the Sephora Collection tile buried two-thirds of the way down between toddler clothing and bedding, priced "starting at $3.50," with a note that coupons and Kohl's Cash don't apply to it. That's a beauty signal, technically, but it's dressed like an afterthought, not a reason to open the email — and it's the kind of tile Sephora Insider or Ulta would never let get lost in a home-goods pile. I'm not clicking through this, and I'm certainly not forwarding it — there's nothing here that reads as curated for me specifically, it reads as "everything we have, go find your thing."
- Mark as read, maybe skim for the towels since I do need bath towels, but the beauty content is so buried it does nothing for me as a counter regular.

## What's working

- The 3X rewards banner up top is at least a clear, quantifiable loyalty hook — I know what "member exclusive" means and I'm a rewards member, so that part speaks my language.
- The Sephora Collection tile is a real beauty signal, even if it's badly placed — it tells me Kohl's knows I might care about that category.
- The "Picked with you in mind" section header at the bottom is a nice personalization gesture, even though what follows (a slushie maker, bath towels, jeans, a mattress topper) doesn't actually feel picked for me at all.
- The status bar at top ("KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER" / earn 3% rewards) is a decent touch — it acknowledges I'm a known customer rather than a stranger.

## What's weak

- This is the fourth-plus consecutive Kohl's send with the identical "Epic Deals" red-badge grid — I flagged the same structure on 9/20 and 9/21, and nothing has evolved creatively.
- Zero white space, zero visual hierarchy — my eye doesn't land anywhere because everything is shouting "Epic Deal" at the same volume.
- The demographic mix (kids' sneakers, toddler stock-up sale, family portrait) skews family/mom-of-young-kids, not a 47-year-old counter shopper — this doesn't feel built for me.
- The Sephora beauty tile — the one thing relevant to my counter habit — is visually identical to a bath towel tile. No hero treatment, no "new at Sephora" framing, nothing that says "beauty event" the way Sephora Insider's own sends do.
- "Coupons and Kohl's Cash do not apply to select brands / this item" appears twice — that's exactly the buried-fine-print, bait-and-switch feeling that knocks trust down for me.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this down to one category per send. If beauty is the hook, lead with Sephora at Kohl's as the hero — a real product shot, a real offer, not a $3.50 "starting at" line lost in a towel aisle.
- 2. Stop repeating the identical "Epic Deals" grid week over week — I've now logged this exact format four times; even Macy's, which overdoes it, at least rotates its hero art.
- 3. If 3X rewards is the real news, make that the subject and the hero — right now it's a green banner competing with six red badges.
- 4. Move exclusions ("coupons do not apply") out of tiny gray text and either drop the item from the promo or state it plainly near the price.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Sephora at Kohl's: new beauty, 3X rewards this week`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 3X rewards are live — plus Epic Deals through Sun`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New Sephora Collection drops, plus $10 off $50 in-store or online`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Members earn 3X rewards through Sept. 27 — here's what's new`
