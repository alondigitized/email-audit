# Your exclusive code inside: Enjoy 25% off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-02**
## Executive summary

- This is the same black box and the same "25% OFF" I got yesterday, now with a code and a grid of designer pieces underneath. It's still the quiet Neiman's, and the black hero is clean. It hasn't turned into Kohl's, where the "extra 20%" had to fight past holiday decor, a gift shop and a slow cooker. But two nearly identical 25% sends in two days make "exclusive" feel like a standing offer.
- My real problem is the one item. The hero says "one regular-price item," and the fine print then excludes beauty, fragrance, Chanel, Dior, Hermès, Gucci, Louis Vuitton and Saint Laurent. For me, beauty is where the points and the events are, so this offer isn't for my category. Macy's at least confirms an extra 25% with clear mechanics. Here I have to read a paragraph of exclusions before I know whether my item qualifies.
- Would I click? Maybe, if I were already eyeing a coat. Would I forward it? No. I'd leave it unread until the deadline, which I only found in the fine print.

## What's working

- The black hero with "25% OFF" in big type reads at a glance. Nothing competes with it.
- The code is in a cream band right under the hero. I don't have to hunt for it, and Sephora's savings banner buried its code more.
- The nine-item grid with designer names only (Veronica Beard, Miu Miu, Chloe, Ferragamo) feels like a catalog page. It respects me and doesn't shout.
- The six category buttons make it easy to jump to shoes or handbags.
- The app and style advisor links add a little service feel, which I like.

## What's weak

- The hero never shows the deadline. "Limited time" is vague, and the real end, 11/3 at 10 PM CT, is buried in the fine print.
- The exclusions list is huge and tiny. It blocks beauty and most of the big-name designers, and it's the opposite of honest.
- It's the second 25%-off-one-item email in two days, after yesterday's "An exclusive offer just for you." The urgency is hollow.
- There are no prices, and nothing tells me which grid items qualify. The Chanel-adjacent and Chloe pieces may well be excluded, and I can't tell.
- Nothing mentions loyalty or my tier. I wouldn't use a card without points back, and the email doesn't say whether I'd earn them on a discounted item.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the end date in the hero ("Ends tonight, 10 PM CT"). Real urgency beats a vague "limited time."
- 2. Add a one-line "Excludes beauty and select designers" under the button, and show only items that qualify in the grid.
- 3. Don't send the same offer two days running. Space it out or change the creative.
- 4. Add a line on how points and tier apply to the discounted item.
- 5. Change "Shop Now" to something specific, like "Shop 25% off women's."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML has no confirmed rendering or link breakage, but it leaks internal SFMC/AMPscript notes in a pre-DOCTYPE comment. The viewport meta tag also blocks pinch-zoom. The automated QA run was inconclusive on compliance and authentication, because the headers weren't captured and the HTML was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 30 tracking links were skipped by QA as click-redirect domains, so none were HTTP-probed. Their destinations, expiry and redirect health are unverified.
- The truncated source doesn't show any individual `href`, so I can't confirm broken, malformed or non-HTTPS links.
- No confirmed link defects.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` disables pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern. Remove it.
- **Comment before the DOCTYPE:** the leading `<!--REQUIRED variables ...-->` and `<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->` come before `<!DOCTYPE>`. Some clients, especially older IE-based webmail, can drop into quirks mode when anything precedes the doctype. Move the comments inside `<head>` or strip them.
- **Fragile media queries:** selectors like `*[class=contenttable]` and `*[class=hide]` are exact-match. They fail if an element carries more than one class. I can't confirm this from the truncated markup, so verify it against the body.
- **Checkbox-hack menu:** the mobile hamburger menu uses `.navbar-checkbox:checked~.menu`. It is unsupported in Gmail and several other clients, and the menu is `display:none` by default. Confirm the fallback navigation is reachable.
- **Alt text and image sizing:** these can't be assessed from the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved tokens are visible in the visible portion, such as `%%...%%`, `{{...}}` or `@email` in body copy.
- The leaked comment names the template variables `@email`, `@base_refid`, `@refid`, `@user`, `@emailid`, `@uEm` and `@cmdid`. That suggests the template was published with its authoring notes intact. If any of those variables are inlined in link parameters, check that they resolved. That can't be confirmed here.
- The subject promises "Your exclusive code inside", but no promo code appears in the visible source. Check that it isn't dynamically rendered or truncated.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe:** not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip it. This is unconfirmed, not a verified defect.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found, with the same caveat. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules expect both headers, so verify them on the raw message.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `e.neimanmarcus.com` is a subdomain that should be checked at the source.
- **Footer:** the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not visible in the truncated HTML, so they can't be verified.
- **Information disclosure:** the HTML comment exposes internal Data Extension names (`NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`) and field names (`EMAIL_ID`, `uEm`, `cmdid`). Sender-side data schema shouldn't ship to recipients.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- UTM parameters and landing-page alignment are unverifiable. All 30 tracked links were skipped, and the truncated source shows no resolved destinations.
- The `@refid` / `@base_refid` variables suggest campaign attribution is carried in a refid parameter rather than UTMs. This is an inference, not confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Strip the AMPscript and variable-documentation comments from the production send, and move any remaining comments after the DOCTYPE.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Check the raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, since the relay may be dropping them.
4. Resolve a sample of the 30 tracking redirects with a proper follow-redirect check, and record the final URLs and refid/UTM values.
