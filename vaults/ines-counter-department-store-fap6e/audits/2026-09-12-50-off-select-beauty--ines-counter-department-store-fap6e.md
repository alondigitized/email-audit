# 50% off select beauty⏰
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-12**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is at least a clean 50% off with real strikethrough pricing all the way down — Clarins serum $96 to $48, that Gucci Flora down to $94, the OUAI mist to $19 — so credit where it's due, this doesn't make me hunt for the number like yesterday's "Stacking Weekend" mess. But it's the same "GET 50% OFF" starburst hero I've now seen four times this week from Sephora (9/8, 9/9, 9/10, 9/11), just recolored pink-to-purple this time, and my Beauty Insider status is reduced to "you have 0 points" mocking me at the top instead of anything that says Rouge or VIB with actual perks.
- The "Today Only" module with Clarins, Estée Lauder, and OUAI is doing real work — that's a legible, curated three-item pick, not the twelve-tile hair-awards sprawl from 9/9. Below that it turns into a straight product grid, which is fine but generic — no sizing/shade info, no ingredient callouts, nothing that treats me like I've bought serum here before.
- I wouldn't click today. I've already opened five nearly identical "50% off select beauty" emails this week and this one doesn't out-offer the others enough to interrupt what I'm doing. I'd scroll past, maybe screenshot the OUAI mist for myself, and definitely not forward it — there's nothing here worth sending a friend that I haven't already sent from Monday's version.

## What's working

- Real prices are visible up front — Clarins $96→$48, Estée Lauder $63→$32, OUAI $38→$19 — no fine-print treasure hunt.
- "Today Only" curation with three named brands feels more editorial than a dump of everything on sale.
- "SHOP NOW" button is a clear, singular CTA sitting right under the hero copy.
- Grid below has actual before/after pricing per item, not just a blanket "50% off" banner.
- Tier bar (Insider/VIB/Rouge) is at least visible near the footer, even if not personalized to me.

## What's weak

- This is visually the same hero I saw on 9/8, 9/9, 9/10, and 9/11 — different color wash, same starburst "GET X% OFF" treatment, same "Today Only" purple box shape. Nordstrom would never run this many near-identical hero templates in a single week.
- "You have 0 points" front and center feels like a jab, not a benefit — no next-tier progress, no "you're this close to Rouge," nothing Saks-style aspirational about the loyalty program.
- No urgency deadline anywhere except the vague "Today Only" on one module — the main hero has no clock, no countdown, nothing time-bound to make me act now versus next week's identical send.
- Product grid has zero social proof — no star ratings, no "bestseller" flags — Ulta at least slaps a rating under every tile.
- No personalization signal at all — nothing referencing past purchases, skin type, or category I actually shop (I'm a fragrance-and-skincare shopper, this grid is scattershot mascara/concealer/sunscreen with no read on me).

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop reusing the exact same starburst hero shape five sends running — rotate creative or at least the layout, not just the gradient color. Fatigue is real and I can feel it.
- 2. Replace "you have 0 points" with something that works for me — nearest reward threshold, or drop it if I have none to show.
- 3. Add a real deadline to the hero itself (a date/time), not just "Today Only" buried in a sub-module — Macy's LAST CHANCE emails at least commit to urgency even when I've flagged the mechanic before.
- 4. Add one line of social proof or "bestseller" tag on at least the top 2 grid items to build trust into the click.
- 5. Segment the grid toward my actual category history instead of a generic best-of spread.
