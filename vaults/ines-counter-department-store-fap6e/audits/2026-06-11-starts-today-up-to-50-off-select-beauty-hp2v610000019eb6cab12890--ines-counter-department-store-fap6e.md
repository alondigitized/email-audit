# STARTS TODAY: up to 50% off select beauty 🎀
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-11**
## Executive summary

- Here we are again. I know the drill by now — same "50% off select beauty" headline, same Beauty Insider section at the bottom, same cheerful pink layout. At this point I could write the email myself. The May 26 "ENDS TODAY‼️" send, the May 22 bonus points push, the June 1 "LAST DAY" send — this is at minimum the fifth time Sephora has shown me this same promotional scaffolding in under three weeks. The "Major Summer Deals" framing is at least a seasonal reframe, and I appreciate that they swapped "STARTS TODAY" for the opening cadence rather than another LAST CHANCE countdown. But fresh copy on a recycled offer doesn't make it feel new.
- What genuinely stopped me mid-scroll was the "Get 25% Off Your First Purchase" block tucked into the lower half of the email. I have a Sephora credit card. I've been a Beauty Insider long enough to remember when VIB Rouge was a thing. Sephora knows my address. And somewhere in their CRM, they're serving me a new-customer acquisition offer. That's not a small miss — that's the system telling me it doesn't know who I am, which is exactly the opposite of what Beauty Insider is supposed to feel like.
- The Lancôme Balance Mode launch and the 100-point rewards section are the genuinely interesting bits here, buried under the avalanche. I'd click the Lancôme story. I'd ignore everything else. I won't forward this.

## What's working

- **"Major Summer Deals" hero framing** — "Major" with a capital M is a confident swing, and the seasonal anchor (summer) makes this feel newer than the May sends.
- **500-point bonus is prominently positioned** — it sits right below the hero, before the scroll, and that's the right call for a Beauty Insider audience.
- **Beauty Insider tier structure is visible** — showing the 50%/20%/25% tier laddering is smart; it reminds me which bracket I'm in and creates tier envy upward.
- **Lancôme Balance Mode section** — a new product launch buried in an otherwise promotional email is exactly the kind of thing I notice; it breaks the cadence and gives me a reason to actually read.
- **No render bugs I can see** — clean layout, images loading, no overlapping text or broken sections.

## What's weak

- **"25% Off Your First Purchase" in a loyalty member email** — this is the single most damaging element in the email. It signals to me that I'm being treated as an unknown; Neiman Marcus's June 9 send knew who I was down to the designer names in my browse history.
- **Five sections fighting for the headline** — 500 points, 50% off, 20% off, 25% off, first-purchase offer, Lancôme launch, 100-point rewards, Paris — I stopped reading a third of the way down and skimmed the rest. Macy's keeps a single confirmation CTA and still manages to feel more focused than this.
- **Cadence fatigue is real** — the May 26 "ENDS TODAY‼️" scored 8/10 largely because it was closing out a clean promotional arc; this opens another one immediately, and the goodwill is spent.
- **ALL CAPS opener in the subject** — "STARTS TODAY" shouts in a way Kohl's got away with because their 9/10 June send was backed by a $150 trip trigger and a very specific savings amount. This subject has no equivalent hook behind the caps.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Strip the new-customer offer entirely from any send that goes to Beauty Insider members.** This is a segmentation fix, not a copy fix — and it's the single change with the most impact on trust.
- 2. **Lead the Lancôme launch as a story, not an afterthought.** A new product launch in the middle of a sale email is backwards; either give it its own send or make it the emotional anchor of this one.
- 3. **Collapse the offer structure to one number per email.** "50% off" is plenty. The 20%/25% tier laddering below it dilutes the headline and forces me to calculate my actual benefit.
- 4. **Give the subject a rest from "STARTS TODAY / ENDS TODAY / LAST DAY" cycling** — the format has lost all urgency through repetition.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Summer sale: Beauty Insider pricing starts now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Beauty Insider rate: 50% off starts today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Lancôme, skincare, makeup — your member price is live.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `500 bonus points + up to 50% off. No code needed.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`maximum-scale=1` on viewport meta (confirmed)**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
Blocks user-initiated pinch-to-zoom on mobile. WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text, Level AA) violation. Remove `maximum-scale=1`.

**`text-size-adjust: none` applied too broadly (confirmed)**
```css
div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; ... }
```
Suppresses OS-level accessibility font scaling across all body elements. Should be scoped to the outer wrapper `<table>` only, not `body` and all inline elements.

**Missing `lang` attribute on `<html>` (confirmed)**
```html
<html xmlns="..." xmlns:v="..." xmlns:o="...">
```
No `lang="en"` present. Screen readers use this to select the correct voice/language engine. Required for WCAG 3.1.1.

**`robots` meta value malformed (confirmed)**
```html
<meta name="robots" content="no index" />
```
`"no index"` (with space) is not a valid directive. Correct value is `"noindex"`. Harmless in most email clients (which strip meta tags), but invalid if the HTML is ever rendered in a browser context.
