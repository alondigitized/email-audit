# Save 20% | Give your home a cozy fall refresh 🍁
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-11**
## Executive summary

- Well, here's Kohl's again with the same three-tier discount math I've seen from them every single time this week — 30% for cardholders, 20% for me as a Rewards member, 15% for nonmembers, right at the top before I even know what I'm shopping for. This is the "cozy fall home" one at least, so there's a category to it — bedding, kitchen, bath, furniture — but the layout is such a kitchen-sink dump of "I want it all" tiles that nothing actually reads as the hero. Then it randomly throws in NFL and NCAA gear before landing on a jeans-and-mattress-topper grab bag. It's Kohl's on autopilot — I'd skim it in three seconds and archive it.
- Would I click? Maybe on the bedding shot if I actually needed sheets, but nothing here is urgent or curated enough to pull me off my phone. Would I forward it? No — there's nothing here worth sharing with a friend, it's just Kohl's doing Kohl's.

## What's working

- The "Cozy Fall Goals" hero with the actual bedding photo is the one clean visual moment — soft blues, a real styled bed, that's the kind of seasonal mood Kohl's does reasonably well.
- My Rewards tier is called out top-right ("Status: Kohl's Rewards Member... Earn 5% Rewards") so at least I'm recognized as a known customer, not a stranger.
- The 20% coupon code and expiration date (Sept. 20) are both visible near the bottom, so if I did want to shop I could actually find the mechanics.

## What's weak

- Four different offer blocks before I even get to a product — cardholder 30%, Rewards 20%, nonmember 15%, then a Kohl's Card pitch — that's the same stacking-offer fatigue I dinged Sephora's "Stacking Weekend" email for, and Kohl's does it just as badly here.
- The "I want it all" grid (Decor, Kitchen & Dining, Bath, Furniture) is just four stock photos with no actual product or price — compare that to Neiman's "1,000+ arrivals" email, which at least commits to a clean grid of real, specific pieces.
- The NFL/NCAA jersey section has zero relevance to a fall-home-refresh email and completely breaks the theme I clicked in for.
- The bottom grid mixes towels, a slushie machine, jeans, and a mattress topper — no thread connecting any of it, just "stuff we have."
- This is the third home/fall email from Kohl's I've seen this week with nearly the same coupon-first structure (the 9/10 and 9/9 sends both led with the identical tier-discount banner).

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the tiered-discount banner down to one line ("Rewards Members: 20% off, ends 9/20") instead of three competing boxes — Neiman's proves a clean single message beats a math problem.
- 2. Delete the NFL/NCAA section entirely from a fall-home email — it's off-brand-for-this-send and dilutes the "cozy" positioning.
- 3. Replace the generic four-photo "I want it all" grid with 2-3 actual hero products (real SKU, real price) the way the bottom "Picked with you in mind" section already does correctly.
- 4. Lead with the bedding hero and its 30%-off callout since that's the strongest visual — move it above the discount banners, not after them.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 20% off bedding — cozy up your bedroom for fall`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Rewards discount: 20% off fall home refresh`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New waffle-knit bedding, plush throws, and warm bath essentials — all discounted for members.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Ends 9/20: shop the pieces making bedrooms cozier this fall.`

# Technical Audit: Kohl's — "Save 20% | Give your home a cozy fall refresh" (260911_DG_Email_Friday)

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard multi-ESP-vendor Kohl's template (CoherentPath + Adobe/Demdex + Kohl's mi.kohls.com tracking stack) with heavy pixel tracking and MSO/Outlook conditional markup; automated QA shows deliverability-header gaps and widespread missing alt text, but rendering markup itself is well-formed for cross-client compatibility.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Multiple concurrent tracking systems fire on load: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/89540b46-...` (1x1 open beacon), `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif` (open pixel, `mi_ecmp=1021596_2026911`), `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?JPE2URC5PUMUTHWCZ52F2R6H7M.60265` (Sailthru/CoherentPath-style open tracker), and `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager beacon) — four separate open-tracking calls before body content renders.
- All hyperlinks are routed through `click.s.kohls.com` redirect wrappers (63 total tracking-domain links per QA), consistent with standard click-tracking; automated HTTP probing was skipped for these (expected/by design for redirect wrappers, not a defect).
- Malformed markup: `<img ... aria-hidden="true" /></custom></div>` — a stray, unopened `</custom>` closing tag appears after the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` pixel `<img>`. This is invalid HTML; most email clients will silently ignore it, but it should be cleaned up at the source template level.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 10 of 10 flagged images have no `alt` attribute, including the primary content hero image (`57448461_Product_fahm26_Home_Hero_GIF.gif`) — with images blocked (default in Outlook/Gmail on first load), sighted users relying on alt fallback text and screen-reader users both lose all context for the hero visual.
- Tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `event`, `up/88954bbbbcab3c0e`, `rp/*.png`) also lack `alt=""`; these are non-content beacons and should carry empty `alt=""` (some already do via `aria-hidden="true"`, e.g. the `open.aspx` pixel, but most do not) to avoid announcing raw filenames to assistive tech.
- MSO conditional blocks (`<!--[if gte mso 9]>`), `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, and responsive `@media max-width:500px` rules are present and structured correctly for Outlook/mobile fallback — no issues found here.
- `<title></title>` is empty — minor, does not affect rendering but is a missed opportunity for inbox preview/tab labeling in webmail clients that surface it.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- `mi_cid` (`67791fba63db790b`) and `mi_mid` (`01a091d7-4780-7000-be75-232b7c1a2898`) recipient-identifier params are populated (not literal placeholders), indicating personalization tokens resolved correctly at send time.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not detected. Required for one-click unsubscribe support in Gmail/Yahoo (per their 2024 bulk-sender mandates); either genuinely absent or stripped by the AgentMail relay — cannot be distinguished from HTML alone.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)**: not detected — same caveat; without it, one-click unsubscribe cannot function even if `List-Unsubscribe` is present.
- **Physical mailing address**: not detected anywhere in the parsed HTML (truncated source ends before footer, so this may be a truncation artifact rather than a true absence — footer region was not included in the source provided).
- **Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM/DMARC)**: not detected — status unknown via this relay; cannot confirm authentication pass/fail from available data.
- Net: the three header-level warnings are most likely artifacts of the AgentMail relay not surfacing/forwarding raw SMTP headers rather than confirmed sender-side failures — flag as "unknown/unverifiable," not confirmed violations, except the missing footer address, which should be re-checked against the full (non-truncated) HTML.

Cannot confirm final landing-page UTM alignment or working destination URLs from this data; recommend a follow-up unwrapped-redirect check.
