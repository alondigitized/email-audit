# Open for 40% off ✨ Earn Kohl's Cash while you're at it ...
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again, and I'm tired. The hero says "Ends Today, you get an extra 40% off with coupon." That's a real offer. It's the best one I've seen from them this week, and it sits above the fold in red. But this is the same weekend I've been getting emails about since 11/10: "Super Saturday," "Save 20% | Earn Kohl's Cash," and "LAST DAY to stack these limited-time deals." Calling this one the last day after four other "last" days doesn't feel credible.
- The body is a long scroll of gift guides, a Grinch stocking, a Ninja oven, towels, a mattress topper, Doorbuster previews, Klarna, and three barcodes at the bottom. It's a catalog. I'm a beauty-counter person, and I see nothing beauty-related until the "Coupons do not apply to Sephora" line. I'd skim it and close it. I would not forward it.

## What's working

- The hero offer is clear. "Extra 40% off with coupon" is big, and the "Ends today" deadline is right next to it.
- Seeing "Kohl's Rewards Member" and "5% Rewards" at the top tells me they know who I am. That's more than a stranger gets.
- The Ninja Double Oven at $299.99 (reg. $399.99) with "Earn $60 Kohl's Cash" shows the price drop and the points back together. That's how I shop.
- The coupon codes and barcodes at the bottom are usable. I can screenshot one and go.

## What's weak

- There are too many modules. Gifts $25 and under, stockings, Grinch, oven, home categories, Doorbusters, Klarna, delivery, and a product grid all compete. Nothing is the one thing.
- The fine print is everywhere: "Coupons do not apply to select brands," "Coupons do not apply to Sephora." The 40% looks bigger than it really is, and that doesn't feel honest.
- Nothing is aimed at a 47-year-old woman who buys beauty. The Sephora exclusion is the only beauty mention. There's a Grinch stocking and a toy coupon.
- It's the fifth or sixth send since 11/10. The "ends today" urgency is worn out.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to the hero, one category row and the Rewards/Kohl's Cash panel. Neiman Marcus's Balmain send got that right: one tall image and one button.
- 2. Put the tiers and Kohl's Cash earnings in the hero, not buried near the bottom. Macy's confirm-now email at least made the mechanics plain.
- 3. Put the exclusions up front. Say which brands are in the 40%. Don't make me hunt.
- 4. Cap the cadence. Sephora's nine sends in a week taught me that volume wears people out. Kohl's is heading the same way.
- 5. Segment. Show me apparel, beauty and home I've actually browsed, not a Grinch.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Kohl's "Open for 40% off ✨ Earn Kohl's Cash…"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML has several markup-validity and accessibility defects, including a stray `</custom>` tag, `<div itemscope>` inside `<head>`, and viewport zoom locked. The header, compliance and click-link checks are unverified. The source was truncated and the relay did not capture the headers. QA pass rate was 38%, with 0 errors and 5 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **82 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and landing pages are unverified. Example: `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7ImQi…`.
- **Four tracking pixels fire on load**:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/a4e2f200-…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`

  Each is a separate third-party request, and one is an Adobe Audience Manager (demdex) call. Several of them are redundant open-tracking beacons.
- **Malformed markup after the open pixel:** `<img src=".../open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`. `</custom>` has no opening tag. Some parsers may drop or mishandle the tag.
- **`<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">`** is not honored by most email clients. It has no effect, but it could suppress referrer data in clients that do honor it.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">`. This fails WCAG 1.4.4 (resize text) in clients that honor it.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** Screen readers and web-view renderings get no title.
- **`<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` is inside `<head>`.** `<div>` is invalid there. Parsers will implicitly close `<head>`, so the `<link>` and `<style>` content that follows may be misplaced. The schema.org markup is also ineffective in email clients.
- **`<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`** has an empty href. This is invalid and can trigger a request to the document URL in web views.
- **Duplicate `<meta name="format-detection">`** tags (`telephone=no`, `date=no`). Some clients use only the first.
- **Doctype and namespaces:** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is combined with `xmlns:o` and `xmlns:v` declarations. This is harmless in practice but inconsistent.
- **Missing alt on images.** QA flagged 10+ images, including:
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`
  - `images.am.kohls.com/is/content/kohlsprod/59781016_Product_hgdt30_HolidayGifting_Hero_GIF`

  Pixels are acceptable with `alt=""`, though the first beacon (`click.chp.kohls.com`), `o.gif` and `demdex` carry no `alt` at all. The `/rp/*.png` images and the hero GIF look like content images, but I can't confirm that from the truncated source. If they are, they need descriptive alt text, and the whole email is unreadable with images blocked.
- **Web fonts:** Roboto and Montserrat load via Google Fonts `@import` and `<link>`, wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. The stack falls back to `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`, so this is fine. The Google request is an extra third-party call.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 14,497 of 20,251 characters (72%) are URLs, which exceeds the 70% QA threshold. This is a minor spam-filter risk.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unresolved tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[[…]]`. Subscriber identifiers (`mi_u=604230016`) are populated in image URLs. The source is truncated, so tokens further down can't be ruled out.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found, but QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip the headers. This is unconfirmed, not a verified defect.
- **Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM/DMARC):** not captured. Status is unknown, not failed.
- **Physical address:** QA did not detect one. The footer is outside the truncated source, so I can't confirm it is missing. Verify against the full HTML.
- **Unsubscribe link:** not visible in the truncated portion, so unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters can't be verified.** Links are opaque click-redirect URLs (`?qs=ABl7…`), and none were resolved.
- **Campaign context is in the image URLs.** The images carry `mi_ecmp=1025944_20261115`, `email_name=261115_DG_Email_Myster…` and `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` / `mi_kc_event_p…`. Some of these values are empty or truncated, such as `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=`. That may mean the Kohl's Cash redemption dates did not populate, which needs confirmation against the full URL.
- **Landing-page alignment is unverified** for the same reason.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the stray `</custom>` tag and move the `<div itemscope>` block out of `<head>` or delete it.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Add a meaningful `<title>`, and remove the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon" href="">`.
4. Add descriptive `alt` to the content images (`/p/rp/*.png`, the Hero GIF), and add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
5. Consolidate redundant open-tracking beacons if possible.
6. Pull raw headers from the source mailbox rather than the AgentMail relay. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
7. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML. Check the footer for the physical address and unsubscribe link, and check `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` for an empty value.
8. Resolve a sample of the 82 `click.s.kohls.com` links through the redirects to verify the destination, UTM parameters and status codes.
