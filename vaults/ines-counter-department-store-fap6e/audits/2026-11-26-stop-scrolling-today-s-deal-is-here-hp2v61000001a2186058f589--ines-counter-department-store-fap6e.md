# Stop scrolling: Today’s deal is here 😲
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- Sixteenth Sephora email in about two weeks, and I'll give it this: it opens with a real hook. The free sample bag with code GRABMYBAG is above the "Cyber Week Deal" banner, so it's the first thing I see. Then comes "It Just Keeps Getting Better" and three giant purple panels, each with "30% OFF" for Sunday Riley, fresh and skinfix. Yesterday's four-brand stack (Lancôme, Living Proof) at least had names I'd shop. Today's three skew toward skincare I could take or leave. fresh is the only one I'd bother with.
- Then it gets long, and the offer gets cluttered. Below the panels there's a "Shop More Must-Have Deals" grid, a "Your Next Beauty Obsession" grid, a 30% complexion makeup tile, a 4X points on fragrance tile, a lip gift tile and a holiday gift guide. That's a Macy's-style pile-up. Neiman's did its black box with one stacked offer this week, and that was cleaner than this.
- The 4X points on fragrance is the part I care about, since I won't use a card without points back. It's buried near the bottom, and the bottom is also where the barcode and in-store code are. I'd read it, maybe click fresh, and not forward it. After sixteen of these I'm mostly just skimming.

## What's working

- The free sample bag is a real perk. It sits at the top with a code, and it feels like a thank-you.
- The three purple 30% panels can't be missed. The offer reads at a glance, and each has a clear "Save Now" button.
- "24 hour deal—get 30% off these top brands" gives a credible deadline and tells me the deal is for today.
- The 4X points on fragrance tile is a reward I'd actually use.
- The barcode at the bottom for in-store use matches how I shop. I like redeeming at the counter.

## What's weak

- The email is far too long. The deal I came for is in the first three panels, and everything after it is noise.
- Sunday Riley, fresh and skinfix feel generic for a 47-year-old who buys counter skincare. I don't see any Gen X faces. It's product shots only.
- The points offer is hidden in the lower third. Nobody on a phone reaches it, and it competes with five other tiles.
- The 30% panels are identical except for the brand, so it's the same layout three times. I scrolled past a lot of purple.
- The fine print is tiny and dense, and I can't read it without zooming. "Exclusions/terms apply" shows up several times, which doesn't feel honest.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email in half. Keep the sample bag, one hero panel and the points offer. Drop the grids and the gift guide.
- 2. Move 4X points up beside the 30% offer. That's the reward-driven pitch for someone like me, and it's what Sephora has over Nordstrom or Macy's beauty events.
- 3. Name the sample bag contents or show a tier benefit, since I'm a member and the email doesn't acknowledge it.
- 4. Vary the hero brands, and lead with the one most likely to match the reader's purchase history.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off Sunday Riley, fresh & skinfix: today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Free sample bag + 4X points on fragrance, today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code GRABMYBAG gets you a free sample bag. 30% off top brands ends tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Beauty Insiders: 4X points on fragrance through 11/30.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit: "Stop scrolling: Today's deal is here 😲" (Sephora Cyber Week)

### 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed personalization failure (empty greeting) and a missing plain-text part (0 chars). Several compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the AgentMail relay probably stripped the headers. The HTML source we have is truncated, so footer and body findings are limited to what the QA data and the visible head show.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque redirect links.** The CTAs go through `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a…?__dU__=…`. Final destinations can't be seen from the source. The QA run probed 12 material links and skipped 43, and it reported no failures among the probed ones. The 43 skipped links, mostly footer and social, were not verified.
- **Open-tracking pixel.** `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…` is a hidden `<img>` with `display:none; max-height:0; mso-hide:all`. It is placed after the preheader. This is expected behavior, but Apple Mail Privacy Protection will pre-fetch it, so open metrics will be inflated.
- **QA flag on the pixel.** The "image missing alt" warning is this same pixel. It should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` to satisfy validators. The impact is cosmetic.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Fixed-width layout.** The wrapper table uses `min-width:700px; width:700px`. The `@media (max-width:480px)` block only resizes a few classes (`.mobile`, `.mobileHeaderLogo`, and so on). It never overrides the 700px min-width, so mobile clients will likely scale down or scroll horizontally.
- **Zoom disabled.** The viewport meta is `maximum-scale=1`, and the body sets `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none`. Together they block pinch-zoom and text resizing, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Malformed conditional comment.** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…} <![endif]-->` sits inside a `<style>` media query. Outlook does not evaluate conditional comments there, so the MSO sizing rule is ineffective, and the stray `<!--` inside CSS is invalid.
- **Invalid robots value.** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. It is harmless in email.
- **Incomplete JSON-LD.** The `<script type='application/ld+json'>` block has `"discountCode": ""` and an empty `PromotionCard`. Most clients strip scripts, and Gmail would ignore a schema with no properties.
- **Plain-text part missing.** The QA check failed because the text version is 0 chars. A multipart/alternative with no text part hurts spam scoring and accessibility. If the relay dropped the part, this may be a capture artifact, so confirm against the raw MIME.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot.** The greeting renders as `Hi ,`, so the first-name merge field resolved to empty with no fallback. This is confirmed by the QA check. The recipient is a seed or test inbox, which may explain the missing name. Even so, the template needs a fallback such as `Hi there,` or a conditional that omits the greeting.
- **`__dU__` token.** The link parameter is populated, so there is no unresolved-token evidence.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058).** Neither header was found. AgentMail may not pass them through, so this is unconfirmed rather than a proven defect. Check the raw headers at the origin. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results.** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain, `beauty.sephora.com`, is aligned with the tracking domain `app.sephora.com` at the organizational level.
- **Footer.** The physical address and the visible unsubscribe link are not visible in the truncated source, so I can't verify either. The QA note says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result is reported for it.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters.** The visible hrefs carry no UTM parameters. Attribution is handled by the `/T/…` redirect and the `__dU__` token, so campaign parameters can't be checked from the email source. Landing-page alignment is unverified because the probe results don't list final URLs.
- **Empty discount code.** `"discountCode": ""` in the structured data shows no code is declared. If the offer needs a code, that is a gap.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Add a fallback to the first-name merge (`Hi there,`). This is the confirmed P1 issue.
2. Add a plain-text MIME part. Verify against the raw message whether it was dropped by the relay or was never sent.
3. Capture the raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from a direct-to-inbox test, not through AgentMail, to settle the three inconclusive checks.
4. Make the layout fluid: replace `min-width:700px` with `max-width:700px; width:100%`, and add the mobile override to the media query.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and `text-size-adjust:none`.
6. Move the MSO conditional out of the media query, and drop or complete the JSON-LD block.
7. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
8. Re-run the audit on the full HTML, with the footer and unsubscribe link included and the skipped links probed.
