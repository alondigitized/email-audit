# Save up to 40% while the Back-to-School Sale lasts 🕛
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-22**
## Executive summary

- Kid at the lockers, again — that's five for five this week from Kohl's, same beat as 8/19, 8/20, and 8/21. I don't have a kid heading back to school, so that top fold is dead space for me before I've even finished my coffee. But I'll give Kohl's this: right under the mast it says "Kohl's Rewards Member" and "5% Rewards" — that's the one thing that made me pause, because I do care whether a retailer remembers I'm enrolled. Neiman Marcus can't even manage that much; their "Breezy Vacation Looks" email didn't acknowledge I'm Fashion Passport-adjacent at all.
- Once I scroll past the kid, this is actually a decent department-store flyer — Tops, Jeans, Bras, Backpacks, Bedding, Bath, all with real prices and real percentages, not vague "shop now" mush. Bedding at 60% off and the "Picked with you in mind" module with a Vanderbilt jeans/mattress topper pairing is the closest thing to relevance I found. But there's zero beauty content this time — no Sephora-at-Kohl's callout like the 8/19 send had, which is the one thing that would've actually gotten my thumb moving.
- Would I click? Maybe on Bedding, out of habit more than desire. Forward to a friend? No — this reads like a category catalog, not something curated for me. Marking as read and moving on.

## What's working

- The "Kohl's Rewards Member / 5% Rewards" banner at the very top is a real loyalty signal — Neiman's last send didn't even try that.
- Prices are concrete everywhere: $7.99 tops, 30% off jeans, $19.99 bras, 60% off bedding — no vague "up to" mystery pricing hiding the ball.
- Bedding and Bath sections are genuinely in my lane — a 47-year-old redoing a guest room doesn't need a kid at lockers to want a $91 mattress topper.
- Tomorrow's Instant Kohl's Cash callout ("up to $100, in-store only, no purchase necessary") is straightforward, same honest tone as yesterday's dedicated Kohl's Cash email.
- Clearance section up to 70% off is clearly flagged, not buried at the bottom in fine print.

## What's weak

- Kid-at-lockers hero for the fifth straight day — I said this yesterday and the day before: I don't have kids in school, this is dead air for me every single time.
- No beauty or Sephora-at-Kohl's presence anywhere in this send, unlike the 8/19 email that at least gave me a lipstick shot — that's the one category that would've made me stop scrolling.
- The email is long — Tops, Jeans, Bras, Backpacks, Bedding, Bath, sweepstakes, Clearance, "picked for you," coupon wallet reminder — it's Macy's-style overload without Macy's occasional beauty payoff.
- Nothing here signals "we know you're a beauty-counter shopper" or gives me any designer-access or event tone — it's pure mass-market flyer, same as every Kohl's send this week.
- "Coupons do not apply to select brands" shows up twice — small, but it's the kind of asterisk that makes me trust the percentage a little less.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rotate the hero — five straight back-to-school kid shots is fatigue, not strategy. Segment shoppers without school-age kids (like me) into a home/bedding-led hero instead.
- 2. Bring back a beauty module. The 8/19 email proved Kohl's has Sephora inventory to show — leaving it out entirely this send is a missed connection to their one truly relevant-to-me category.
- 3. Lead with the Rewards/Kohl's Cash banner more prominently — it's currently a thin strip at the very top; make it a real module with my accumulated cash balance if you have it, not just a generic "5% Rewards."
- 4. Trim the flyer. Eight-plus product categories plus a sweepstakes plus clearance plus a coupon-wallet nudge is a lot to scroll through on a phone — cut to 3-4 sections and let bedding/bath breathe.
- 5. Clarify the "coupons do not apply to select brands" language — either name the excluded brands or drop the caveat from the headline offer.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Rewards are ready: up to 40% off storewide`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus, Sephora at Kohl's Beauty — new arrivals inside`

# Technical Audit: Kohl's Back-to-School Sale Email

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a standard Coherent Path/Kohl's ESP template with heavy tracking pixel instrumentation; it fails several CAN-SPAM/authentication QA checks and has widespread missing alt text on tracking images.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 73 tracking/click-redirect links present, all skipped by the automated HTTP probe (expected behavior for redirect domains like `click.chp.kohls.com`, `click.s.kohls.com` — not itself a defect, but unverified for dead-link risk).
- Multiple beacon/pixel trackers fire on open: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` (Coherent Path), `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...` and `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/...` (multiple, appears 7x), `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager), `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...` (open tracking). This is a dense first-party + third-party tracking stack but functionally consistent with retail ESP norms.
- No broken/malformed `href` values observed in the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 10 images missing `alt` text (QA-confirmed), all 1x1 tracking pixels: `10c3c5e4-d04b-42e0-9c7a-582387d1de4b`, `o.gif`, `event` (Demdex), and six `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` beacons. Low practical impact since these are invisible tracking pixels, not content images, but worth noting for strict WCAG compliance.
- Template includes standard MSO conditional comments, Outlook `xml:o`/`xml:v` namespaces, and responsive `@media max-width:500px` rules — indicates multi-client rendering support (Outlook, mobile, webmail).
- Google Fonts (`Roboto`, `Montserrat`) loaded via `<link>` + `@import`, gated behind `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` so Outlook falls back to system fonts — correct pattern, no issue.
- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` present — appropriate for transactional/marketing email, no issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) observed in the truncated source. `mi_ecmp`, `mi_kc_redm_end_dte`, `mi_kc_event_pe` query params on beacon URLs appear to be unpopulated/empty (e.g. `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_pe`) — likely inert personalization fields on a tracking pixel, low impact but flagging as a possible unpopulated-token pattern worth spot-checking upstream.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] No `List-Unsubscribe` header detected** — may be an AgentMail relay capture gap rather than a true absence; cannot confirm from HTML alone.
- **[WARN] No `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058)** — one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed.
- **[WARN] No physical mailing address detected** in the truncated HTML — CAN-SPAM requires this; likely present in the untruncated footer but not confirmable from the provided source.
- **[WARN] No `Authentication-Results` header found** — SPF/DKIM pass/fail status unknown; could be a relay-side capture issue rather than an actual auth failure.

All four compliance/deliverability warnings are attributable to either the AgentMail relay's header capture or content truncation — none are confirmed hard failures, but none can be ruled out either.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Tracking params observed use Kohl's/Coherent Path proprietary schema (`mi_mid`, `mi_cid`, `cp_cid`, `cp_tp`, `mi_ecmp=1020324_2026822`) rather than standard `utm_*` params — cannot verify UTM-based landing page attribution from this source.
- `email_name=260822_DG_Email_Sat_PM` present on `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*` beacons, consistent with the send date (2026-08-22) and campaign type — internal naming appears aligned.
- Destination/landing page URLs not visible in the truncated source (all links are wrapped `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` redirects), so landing-page alignment cannot be verified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt=""` (explicit empty alt, not omitted) to all 10 tracking pixel `<img>` tags for accessibility-checker compliance.
2. Confirm with ESP/deliverability team whether `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` / `Authentication-Results` headers are actually absent at the SMTP layer or just not captured by the AgentMail relay — verify with a raw header capture outside AgentMail.
3. Confirm physical mailing address is present in the untruncated footer; if absent, this is a CAN-SPAM violation requiring immediate fix.
4. Clean up empty query params on beacon URLs (e.g., `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=`, `mi_kc_event_pe` with no value) — low priority, but indicates possibly broken personalization feed upstream.
