# Want up to $1,500 off? Plus, extra 25% off & The Designer Sale is on now
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- It's the black box with the red border again. Neiman's has sent me that panel since 11/19, and I'm sick of it. Today it carries three offers, and that's the problem. There's a $1,500 tier, an extra 25% on top of 70% off, and a Designer Sale at 60% off. The subject line adds all three to my inbox preview, so I know the stack before I even open it. Compare that with the 11/24 CHANEL Cruise send, which was the one calm email in a week of shouting.
- Under the panel there's a nine-tile grid of Maison Francis Kurkdjian, Jimmy Choo, FRAME, Chloe and Givenchy. That part respects me. A beauty-counter regular would click the Baccarat Rouge tile, but the tile has no price and no "now" price. I'd still skim it and I'd probably shop. I wouldn't forward it. Sephora would have made the code and the beauty angle the hero. Here beauty is a code condition in tiny type and a plain button in the middle of a list.

## What's working

- The THANKFUL code is visible in the hero, so I don't have to hunt for it.
- The product grid is clean and plain, with named designers and no clutter. That feels like Neiman's, not Macy's.
- Maison Francis Kurkdjian is in the top-left slot. As a beauty person, I noticed.
- The "Includes Beauty" line is there, and that does matter to me.
- The wish list and gift list line is warm and low-pressure.

## What's weak

- Three competing offers in one panel. I can't tell which one applies to me without reading the fine print.
- The "Includes Beauty | Code | THANKFUL" line is tiny, and the red-on-black borders make it hard to read on a phone.
- There's no loyalty or InCircle mention anywhere. I won't use a card without points back, and nothing tells me whether points stack with these offers.
- The fine print is a wall of text. The $1,500 tier needs a $250 minimum, and the deadline of 11/28 only shows up down there.
- "Exclusions Apply" is the first thing under the button, which feels like a hedge.
- The same panel has come back for roughly the sixth time since 11/19. I'm numb to it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer and one deadline. Lift the $1,500 tier, put the code and "Ends 11/28" in the hero, and move the other two lower down.
- 2. Show a real spend ladder like the 11/24 send did, so I can see what I'd save at my cart size.
- 3. Add a beauty-specific module with a clear InCircle points line. That's what would get me to click.
- 4. Put the deadlines in the body, not buried in the footnotes.
- 5. Skip the sixth identical panel. Vary the creative day to day, as Sephora tries to even at fourteen emails.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML source is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) template that has two real defects: internal AMPscript documentation is left in the delivered markup, and the viewport meta tag blocks zoom. The source is truncated, and the QA warnings are mostly relay-capture limits, so link, compliance and UTM checks can't be fully confirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified (not a confirmed defect):** QA skipped 30 tracking or click-redirect links ("skipped HTTP probe"). Destination resolution, redirect health and expiry are untested.
- **Plain-text part:** 5,628 of 7,564 characters (about 74%) are URLs. That is consistent with long wrapped tracking URLs and makes the text alternative close to unreadable.
- No broken or malformed hrefs are visible in the truncated portion.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `lang`:** the root element is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` with no `lang` attribute. Screen readers can't pick the right language (WCAG 3.1.1).
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4). Three commented-out alternative viewport tags remain in the source as dead code.
- **Fragile mobile selectors:** the media query uses exact-match attribute selectors such as `*[class=hide]`, `*[class=mobile]` and `*[class=showmobile]`. These stop matching if any element carries a second class. I can't tell from the truncated source whether that happens.
- **Checkbox-hack hamburger menu:** the CSS (`.navbar-checkbox:checked~.menu`) relies on `:checked` support. Gmail and several Outlook builds don't support it, and the truncated source doesn't show a fallback.
- **Title:** `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` is generic and has a trailing space.
- **Not verifiable:** image `alt` text, table `role="presentation"` and contrast, because the source is cut off before the body.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Template documentation shipped in the delivered HTML:** the first comment block exposes internal SFMC variable logic and data extension names. Examples are `Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", "EMAIL_ID", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email)`, `Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "uEm", ...)`, `@base_refid` and `@cmdid`. It also contains an unresolved "CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW" instruction and `<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->`.
- This is a build-hygiene and information-disclosure problem, not a rendering failure. It also suggests the template wasn't stripped before send.
- The visible portion shows no unresolved `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` tokens in rendered content.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Not confirmed as defects:**
  - `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) weren't found. QA itself says the AgentMail relay may strip them.
  - `Authentication-Results` was not found. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- The footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated section, so I can't verify them.
- The sender domain is `e.neimanmarcus.com`, a subdomain of the brand. That is consistent with aligned sending, but alignment is not confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. The link probes were skipped and no `utm_*` parameters appear in the visible source. The template does set `@refid` and `@base_refid` (campaign-calendar IDs), so campaign attribution is likely handled through a refid parameter rather than UTM. That is unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Strip the SFMC variable and scenario comment block (and the commented-out viewport tags) from the production output. Use AMPscript comments `%%[/* */]%%`, which are removed at render time, instead of HTML comments.
2. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Replace the `[class=…]` exact-match selectors with `[class~=…]` or plain class selectors. Confirm the hamburger menu has a non-`:checked` fallback in Gmail and Outlook.
5. Check the raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) in a direct inbox, not through the relay.
6. Re-run QA on the full, untruncated HTML. Resolve the 30 tracking links with a redirect-following probe and check the landing page parameters.
7. Shorten or restructure the plain-text version so it isn't about 74% URLs.
