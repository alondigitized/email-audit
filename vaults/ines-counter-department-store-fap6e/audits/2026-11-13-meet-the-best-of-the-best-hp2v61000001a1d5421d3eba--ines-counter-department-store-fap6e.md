# Meet the best of the best🏆
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-13**
## Executive summary

- This is the eighth Sephora email in about a week. Yesterday it was "SETH ROGEN DID YOUR HOLIDAY SHOPPING", and before that "Holiday hits, all under $50 🎁". I opened this one because the sender is Sephora, not because of the subject. "Meet the best of the best" says nothing. "Best" of what?
- Inside, the best thing is buried in the top strip: free trial-size fragrances at $50, $80 or $110 spend. That's the actual offer, and it sits in a purple banner above a giant "MOST-LOVED BEAUTY" headline that sells nothing. Then comes a Chanel N°5 hero, which I like. I'm a counter girl at heart, and a Chanel list with real prices ($162–$195 for Coco Mademoiselle Crush Absolu) treats me like an adult. Below that it turns into a grid of rhode, Summer Fridays and OUAI, which is a younger shopper's list, then a Venmo ad, then a credit-card pitch.
- I'd click the fragrance banner, maybe the Chanel. I wouldn't forward it. I'd skim it and mark it read.

## What's working

- The Chanel hero is pure designer-access tone. The bottle shot is clean, and the four named fragrances come with real price ranges.
- "Get one, two, or three FREE trial sizes" is a clear, tiered offer. A spend ladder is how I think about shopping anyway.
- Neiman Marcus's "up to $650 off" email earlier this week showed what a clean offer ladder looks like. This one has the same bones.
- Delivery is spelled out under the Chanel block: "buy online, pick up in store or curbside, or use Same-Day Delivery."
- The "ONLY AT SEPHORA" and "NEW" tags on the product tiles tell me quickly what's exclusive.
- The Insider badge and "Hi, Rosie" in the header tell me they know who I am.

## What's weak

- The real offer is in a thin banner at the very top, while the hero says "MOST-LOVED BEAUTY, Beyond iconic" with a generic Shop Now. That headline spends the most space on the least information.
- The "Shop Top Beauty" grid is mostly rhode, Summer Fridays, EADEM and UNOVE. That's a Gen Z lineup, and nothing in it is for a 47-year-old counter regular.
- The Kayali "Yum Vanilla Cupcake" banner is visibly cut off. The "limited-edition bottle with sprinkles on t…" text runs off the edge.
- The 25% off credit-card pitch is a "your first purchase" offer, and I'm already an Insider. Macy's at least dresses its card offer as a member perk.
- The fine print is a wall of tiny text, and the promo code FRAGSMGM is in a small seal. It looks buried, so it doesn't read as honest.
- It's seven or eight sends in a week with no sign of cadence control.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the free-trial-size ladder the hero, with the code in a real button or text line. Cut the "MOST-LOVED BEAUTY" headline.
- 2. Segment by category. Someone who has bought Chanel or Dior should get the fragrance hero, and the viral rhode and Summer Fridays grid should go to younger shoppers.
- 3. Don't send existing cardholders or Insiders the "first purchase" credit card ad.
- 4. Fix the cropped Kayali banner.
- 5. Show my Insider tier or points balance. Kohl's Cash and Macy's both lead with that kind of benefit.
- 6. Cut the cadence, or at least stop repeating the same holiday-gift hero.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $50, $80 or $110 and pick your free fragrance samples. Code FRAGSMGM.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Chanel, Kayali and more. Free samples, free shipping for Insiders.`
