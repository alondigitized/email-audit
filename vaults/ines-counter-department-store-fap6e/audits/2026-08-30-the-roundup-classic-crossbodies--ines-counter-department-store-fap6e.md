# The Roundup: Classic Crossbodies
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-30**
## Executive summary

- Well, now this is a palm cleanser after that whole Neiman's $250-off tiered saga I've been living through since 8/26. No math table, no code to remember, no "shop to it" mango banner — just a gorgeous editorial hero of a red dress and a navy top-handle bag, and then six crossbodies laid out clean: Loewe, Givenchy, Prada, Ferragamo, Khaite. This is Neiman's doing what Neiman's is supposed to do — curate, not haggle.
- That said, there's not a single price on this page, no member perks mentioned, and no urgency at all — which is fine for a lookbook, but it also means there's zero reason for me to act today versus next week. I'd open it, I'd scroll through it with genuine pleasure because I like crossbodies and I like this brand's eye, and I might screenshot the Ferragamo for myself — but I'm not clicking to buy without knowing what any of these cost, and I'm not forwarding it since there's no offer to share.
- Compare this to yesterday's "Fashion Finds: Daytime Dresses," which pulled the same trick — a clean, discount-free editorial. Two of these back to back tells me Neiman's has two email personalities right now: the relentless $250-off box, and this quieter designer-curation mode. I actually prefer this one, but Saks Fifth Avenue's crossbody roundups usually at least tuck in "as seen on" styling notes or a "shop the muse" line — this one is just product tiles with brand names, no context, no styling hook.

## What's working

- The red-dress hero with the navy Loewe-adjacent bag is genuinely chic — feels like something I'd see in a magazine, not a marketing blast.
- Clean nav (Women/Men/Shoes/Sale) and a simple two-button CTA row ("Shop Crossbody Bags" / "Shop Christian Louboutin") — no clutter.
- The designer roster (Loewe, Givenchy, Prada, Ferragamo, Khaite) is exactly my closet aspiration — this is who I already shop.
- No discount-table fatigue this time — a real break from the tiered-box cadence.

## What's weak

- Zero prices anywhere. I have to click through six times just to know if I'm looking at a $600 bag or a $3,000 one — that's friction, not intrigue.
- No loyalty tie-in — I'm an InCircle member and there's nothing here acknowledging that, unlike Macy's, which at least dangles Star Rewards math even when it's clumsy about it.
- "Christian Louboutin" as the second CTA button is a total non-sequitur next to a crossbody roundup — that's shoes, not bags, and it just looks like a copy-paste leftover.
- No styling context or "why now" — Sephora's "Iconic Beauty" send at least tried a seasonal hook; this is just tiles with brand names underneath.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add prices (or "from $X") under each bag — the single biggest lift here, since right now every click is a leap of faith.
- 2. Swap the "Shop Christian Louboutin" button for something on-theme, like "Shop All Crossbodies" or a specific complementary category (belts, wallets) — the shoe CTA breaks the story.
- 3. Add one line of styling or seasonal framing under "Classic Crossbodies" — even something like "the one bag that goes with everything this fall."
- 4. Surface InCircle points-earning potential somewhere near the CTA — I don't switch cards for nothing, and if Neiman's wants me clicking, remind me what I get back.
- 5. Give this send a companion "shop the edit" moment tied to price tiers — turn the quiet editorial into a nudge without going back to the mango banner.
