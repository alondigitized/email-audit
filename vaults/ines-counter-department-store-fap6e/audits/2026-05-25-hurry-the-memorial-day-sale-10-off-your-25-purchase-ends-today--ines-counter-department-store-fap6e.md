# Hurry, the Memorial Day Sale & $10 off your $25 purchase ends today!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-25**
## Executive summary

- Eleven. That's where the Kohl's count sits as of this morning, and the subject line on this one — "Hurry, the Memorial Day Sale & $10 off your $25 purchase ends today!" — is the brand's polite way of admitting they've been running the same email at me for fifteen days. Same red MEMORIAL DAY SALE block at the top. Same $10-off-$25 coupon. Same waterfall of percentage-off tiles — 30% dresses, 24.99 swim, 30% jewelry, 50% men's tees, 30% kids', 40% luggage, 50% fragrance — Clearance, $5 Kohl's Cash, the barcode coupon. I've now seen every tile in this email at least four times this month.
- The "ends today" hook is the one thing earning its keep. It's a real deadline, and after a week of "shop the sale" emails the urgency finally has somewhere to land. But I already shopped this sale on the 21st when the offer first cleared, and nothing here is different — not the products, not the percentages, not even the photography. Neiman Marcus put China Chow on a picnic blanket yesterday and got a real editorial out of it. Sephora dropped my name entirely and ran a clean 50%-off-plus-bonus-points hero. Kohl's is running tile soup for the eleventh time.
- Would I click? No. Would I forward it? No. Mark as read and move on — and frankly, after Monday this entire series goes to the unsubscribe page if the cadence doesn't reset.

## What's working

- "Ends today" in the subject is finally a credible deadline after a week of the same offer — that's the only honest urgency in this stretch.
- The $10 off $25 coupon plus the $5 Kohl's Cash earning at $25 spend is at least a math-on-math stack a points person like me can parse.
- Layout is readable; no broken images, no overlapping text — Kohl's renders cleanly even if the creative is stale.
- The barcode coupon at the bottom is actually useful — I can pull this up at the register without digging for a code.

## What's weak

- Eleventh email in fifteen days. Every tile, every percentage, every model shot is recycled — this is fatigue, not a campaign.
- No beauty tile, again. Eleven sends and Kohl's still doesn't know I'm here for Sephora-at-Kohl's, not luggage at 40% off.
- "Hurry" plus "ends today" plus a 30% / 24.99 / 30% / 50% / 30% / 40% / 50% / Clearance 70% gauntlet — Macy's-grade overload, and Macy's at least pretends to segment.
- No tier signal, no Kohl's Rewards balance, no "you have $X in Kohl's Cash waiting" — they have my purchase history and they're treating me like a stranger.
- The "ends today" deadline contradicts itself when the next email Tuesday will almost certainly be "extended through midweek."

## Recommendations

- 1. **Stop the cadence.** This is send eleven of an identical promo. Send one closer email today, then go dark Tuesday and Wednesday. Anything else is unsubscribe risk.
- 2. **Personalize the hero by department.** I've never clicked luggage. I've clicked beauty. Put Sephora-at-Kohl's in the hero slot for beauty openers — that's a database query, not a redesign.
- 3. **Show me my Kohl's Cash balance and Rewards tier in the header.** Sephora always shows me my Beauty Insider points. Kohl's has that data and never uses it.
- 4. **Kill three tiles.** Eight is too many. Pick the three best converters and let them breathe.
- 5. **Tighten the subject.** "Hurry, the Memorial Day Sale & $10 off your $25 purchase ends today!" is 71 characters and tries to do three jobs.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: $10 off $25 ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Memorial Day final hours — $10 off $25`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus earn $5 Kohl's Cash on your order. Midnight cutoff.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your coupon expires at midnight. Stack with Kohl's Cash before it's gone.`

# Technical Audit — Kohl's Memorial Day Sale Email

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard table-based HTML4 Transitional layout with multiple tracking pixels (Cordial, Demdex/Adobe AAM, Movable Ink) and Coherent Path personalization parameters. Primary technical issues are accessibility (missing alt text on all images), unverifiable authentication headers, and a malformed `</custom>` closing tag in the body.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 22 tracking/click-redirect links skipped by automated probe — destinations not validated for 200 status.
- Multiple tracking domains in use: `click.chp.kohls.com` (Coherent Path), `mi.kohls.com` (Movable Ink), `kohls.demdex.net` (Adobe Audience Manager), `click.s.kohls.com` (Cordial open pixel).
- Coherent Path `cp_tp=v3.eJyNkL9LAzEU...` parameter is a base64-encoded payload — exposes personalization metadata in URL query string (low risk, but visible if forwarded).
- No UTM parameters detected on tracked links — campaign attribution relies entirely on vendor-side click IDs (`mi_ecmp=1013201_2026525`, `cp_cid=8ba359f132e3c109`).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **All 8+ images missing `alt` attributes** (per QA findings) — fails WCAG 2.1 1.1.1. Tracking pixels are correctly hidden, but content images (`2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, etc.) lack descriptive alt text, breaking screen reader access and degrading rendering when images are blocked.
- Malformed HTML: stray `</custom>` closing tag near the Cordial open pixel (`<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..."></custom>`) — no matching `<custom>` opening tag. Will not break rendering but is invalid markup.
- `<title>` element is empty — preview pane/tab titles fall back to subject line.
- `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` in viewport meta blocks pinch-zoom on iOS, an accessibility anti-pattern (WCAG 1.4.4).
- Google Fonts loaded via `<link>` and `@import` — most email clients (Gmail, Outlook) strip these; Montserrat/Roboto will fall back to Arial/Helvetica.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unrendered merge tokens (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, `[[...]]`) detected in the visible HTML source. Recipient identifier `mi_u=604230016` is hard-coded in pixel URLs, confirming personalization rendered server-side before send.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not found (per QA). May be relay-stripped, but if absent at send, fails Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements (Feb 2024).
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)**: not found — one-click unsubscribe unsupported.
- **Authentication-Results header**: not captured — SPF/DKIM/DMARC status cannot be verified from relay output.
- **Physical mailing address**: not detected in QA scan (likely in truncated footer; verify full HTML).
- `noindex, nofollow` and `no-referrer` meta tags correctly set for web-hosted version.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- Cannot validate landing page alignment — all destination URLs are click-tracker redirects (`click.chp.kohls.com/o/{uuid}`) whose final hops were not probed.
- No UTM parameters on tracked links means downstream analytics (GA4, etc.) depend on Kohl's internal join between Coherent Path click IDs and site sessions — fragile if that pipeline breaks.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add `alt` attributes to all content images**; keep `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"` on tracking pixels (already correct on `o.gif`).
2. **Verify List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post headers** are present at the SMTP layer — critical for Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender compliance.
3. **Confirm physical address** appears in the truncated footer; required by CAN-SPAM §316.5.
4. **Remove orphan `</custom>` tag** near the Cordial open pixel.
5. **Populate `<title>`** with a fallback (campaign name or subject) for web-version rendering.
6. **Drop `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0`** from viewport meta to restore pinch-zoom accessibility.
7. **Add UTM parameters** to click-tracker URLs (or have Coherent Path append them on redirect) so GA4 attribution doesn't depend solely on vendor-side joins.
8. **Capture full Authentication-Results** in the relay so SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail can be audited per send.
