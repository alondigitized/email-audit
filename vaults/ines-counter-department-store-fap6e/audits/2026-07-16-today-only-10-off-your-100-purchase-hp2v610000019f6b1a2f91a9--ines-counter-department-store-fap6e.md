# Today only ‼️ 10% off your $100+ purchase.
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-16**
## Executive summary

- Ten emails from Sephora in sixteen days, and this one finally shows up with something concrete. "Today only" plus a real discount percentage — I'll give them that. The 10% off badge is visible before I even fully open the email, so the main message isn't buried. For someone who regularly crosses $100 at this counter, that's actually a meaningful offer.
- But then I scroll down and the email turns into a department store clearance rack at 6pm on a Saturday. Free hair sample set. Shop these products. Redeem 100-point rewards. Download the app for 8% off instead of 10%. Three competing CTAs fighting for my attention before I even get to the footer. And that app banner at the top is confusing — if I download the app I get *less* off? That's a discount I'm supposed to want?
- I'd open this one, and I'd probably click through to the 10% because I have a routine restock coming anyway. But I'd feel faintly annoyed doing it, the same way I feel shopping a Macy's that's screaming signage at every rack when I just want to find the Lancome counter and leave quietly. Sephora knows I'm a loyal Insider — this email doesn't behave like it.

## What's working

- **The 10% offer is the clearest subject line they've sent me all month.** After "Obsessed? Same." and "These picks are everywhere," a dollar-anchored discount is a relief.
- **"Today only" actually lands** because there's a specific, time-bounded reason to act — I won't sit on this.
- **The 100-point rewards section speaks directly to me.** I refuse to use a card that doesn't kick back points, and this brand knows I have a balance to spend.
- **Free hair sample set is a smart secondary hook** — something tactile and specific, not just "shop our new arrivals."
- **No render bugs.** The layout holds, images load, barcode renders cleanly for in-store use. Basics, yes, but Kohl's broke their layout two emails ago.

## What's weak

- **8% app offer at the top undercuts the 10% in the subject.** I'm being told to download an app to get *worse* terms — that's backwards and confusing.
- **Three competing asks in one scroll.** Free sample redemption, product shopping grid, 100-point reward redemption: I don't know what this email wants me to do first.
- **Visual hierarchy collapses** — the free hair sample set hero is bigger than the 10% discount that got me to open. The star of the show is buried in its own email.
- **No personalization anywhere.** Nordstrom would have led with my category. Sephora knows I'm a skin-first buyer who occasionally crosses into hair. They sent me the same grid they sent every Insider.
- **Cadence fatigue is real.** This is the tenth email since July began. Even a good offer starts to feel like a flyer on my windshield at this frequency.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill the app 8% banner or flip the logic.** Lead with 10% in the email, and note the app gets you *more* than 10% — not less. Right now the math works against clicking.
- 2. **Make the 10% discount the hero, not the free hair set.** The offer that earned the open should dominate the fold. Move the hair sample to a secondary tile.
- 3. **One CTA per email.** Pick: the discount or the sample set or the rewards redemption. Not all three. If the rewards section is valuable, it belongs in a dedicated rewards email.
- 4. **Personalize the product grid** by purchase history — even a rough category filter would separate Sephora from every other blast in my inbox.
- 5. **Drop the exclamation emoji from the subject.** I'm 47, I shop at Saks, and ‼️ reads like a flash sale at a kiosk.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 10% Insider day — today only, no code needed`
- **Subject Alt B:** `10% off your next $100+ haul. Just today.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack it with your rewards. Plus a free hair sample set.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free Hair Sample Set + 10% off — your Insider perks, one place.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-to-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4 violation)**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
Disables user-initiated zoom on mobile. Fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text). Remove `maximum-scale=1` or raise to `maximum-scale=5`.

**No `lang` attribute on `<html>` element**
```html
<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" xmlns:v="..." xmlns:o="...">
```
Missing `lang="en"` prevents screen readers from selecting the correct language profile. Add `lang="en"` (or appropriate locale).

**`text-size-adjust: none` applied to broad selector set**
```css
div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; ... }
```
Disabling text-size-adjust on `body` and block-level elements is overly aggressive. If a mail client overrides this, the cascade is unpredictable. Scope to `td` only, which is the standard email pattern.

**Generic `<title>` tag**
```html
<title>Sephora</title>
```
Not a rendering blocker, but a non-descriptive title reduces accessibility for screen reader users who open the email in a browser tab (e.g., via "view in browser" links).

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)

**Cannot assess unsubscribe link or physical address** — both are required by CAN-SPAM (15 U.S.C. § 7704) and must appear in the email body. The truncated source does not include the footer. Verify:
- One-click or clearly labeled unsubscribe link present
- Sephora's physical mailing address present in footer

**`robots` meta directive has an illegal space**
```html
<meta name="robots" content="no index" />
```
`"no index"` (with space) is not a valid robots directive token. The correct value is `"noindex"`. While email clients ignore this, if the email is scraped or cached by a web renderer, the directive will not be honored. Fix: `content="noindex,nofollow"`.

**Authentication headers** — DKIM, SPF, and DMARC cannot be verified from HTML source alone. These must be confirmed via raw message headers (`shop@beauty.sephora.com` sending domain should pass SPF/DKIM aligned to `sephora.com`).
