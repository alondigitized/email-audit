# Enjoy an extra 40% off | Stacks of Kohl's Cash await ...
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again, and I'm tired. This is the same weekend I've been getting since 11/10. Yesterday it was "Reveal your savings now 👀" and "HURRY, shop limited-time deals," and this morning it was "Open for 40% off ✨." Now there's another one for the same 40% coupon. The hero does say "Ends Today" over a big red 40% with coupon, so I know in three seconds what the deal is. That part is fine.
- Then it keeps going, and it's very long. It has a coat story, a boots story, a gift shop, a toy $10 off, a "Preview the Deals" app promo, Klarna, DoorDash, and Instacart. After that come four products with prices and then two barcodes. It's a catalog, and the actual offer is buried under it. I'd skim it, maybe tap the coupon in the wallet, and move on. I wouldn't forward it to anyone.
- Neiman Marcus sent me one black block, one red "40% OFF," one code, and one button, and I got the message instantly. Kohl's gives me about twelve places to click. Sephora has the same overload problem, but at least it feels like beauty. Here nothing in the email speaks to a 47-year-old who buys at the beauty counter.

## What's working

- The hero gets across "40% off, ends today" at first glance, with the Kohl's Rewards member status bar right above it.
- The "Ends Nov 15" deadline is believable, since it's the actual last day.
- The offer is honest. The coupon code and barcodes are printed at the bottom, and the exclusions ("Coupons do not apply to select brands") are stated.
- The product tiles show price and original price clearly, like $29.99 and $299.99. I can read those without zooming.
- The bottom section lists "Earn $10 Kohl's Cash" with its dates, so I can plan.

## What's weak

- It's far too long. Four offers ("Up to 40%," "Starting at 29.99," "Up to 20%," "$10 off toys") compete with the 40% hero. A shopper who opens this doesn't know what the one deal is.
- Nothing here is beauty. No Sephora at Kohl's, no fragrance, no makeup. The imagery is coats, boots, toys, and gifts, so it reads as a family-and-kids email.
- The Kohl's Cash terms are tucked into small print at the bottom ("Earn $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 spent through November 19"). The subject promised "stacks" of it, and that's where I'd have to hunt for it.
- The 40% hero repeats as "Up to 40%" on the coat tile. I can't tell whether that's the same discount or a different one.
- Cadence is relentless. This is the fourth near-identical send in a week.
- Delivery, Klarna, and app-download blocks sit between me and the products. That's clutter.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut it to the hero, one product grid, and the code. The extras (Klarna, DoorDash, Instacart, the app promo) belong in a footer or in a different send.
- 2. Put the Kohl's Cash earn rule right under the hero. It's in the subject, so it has to be visible in the body.
- 3. Add a beauty or Sephora-at-Kohl's tile. If you want me, show me something I buy.
- 4. Stop sending the same weekend four times. Send one email with a real deadline, and don't repeat it every few hours.
- 5. Make the CTA say what it does, such as "Shop coats at 40% off," not "Shop Now."
- **Subject Alt A:** `40% off ends tonight, code inside`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day: 40% off + Kohl's Cash`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your coupon works on coats, boots, and gifts through 11:59 PM.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Earn $10 Kohl's Cash per $50 spent. Redeem Nov 20–25.`

# Technical Audit: Kohl's "Enjoy an extra 40% off | Stacks of Kohl's Cash await ..."

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is mostly sound for email, but it has several structural defects and accessibility gaps, including a stray `</custom>` tag, a `<div>` inside `<head>`, and zoom-blocking viewport settings. The automated QA reports 38% pass, 0 errors and 5 warnings, and the compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the headers are missing from the capture and the HTML is truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link health is unverified.** 77 tracking links on `click.s.kohls.com` and `click.chp.kohls.com` were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destinations and redirect chains were not checked.
- **Four tracking pixels fire on open.** Each is a separate third-party or first-party beacon:
  - `https://click.chp.kohls.com/o/c4d8c2ea-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...`
  - `https://mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1025936_20261115`
  - `https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=...`
  - `https://kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`
- **Recipient ID in URLs.** `mi_u=604230016` appears in pixel and image URLs. It is a persistent recipient identifier in plain query strings on image requests.
- **Malformed pixel markup.** The `open.aspx` pixel is followed by a stray `</custom>` closing tag (`...&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`), which has no matching opening tag.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Missing alt text.** The QA flags content images served from `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/` with no `alt` attribute, including `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png` and `916d6fa08d185233.png`. Screen readers will announce filenames or nothing, and with images blocked the content disappears. The QA list is truncated, so more may exist.
- **Pixels without alt or `aria-hidden`.** The `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` and `kohls.demdex.net/event` pixels have no `alt` and no `aria-hidden`. The `demdex` pixel also uses `width="0" height="0"`.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Invalid markup in `<head>`.** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will close `<head>` early and push the contents into `<body>`. Schema.org microdata is also ignored by most email clients.
- **Empty icon link.** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty `href`.
- **Duplicate meta tag.** `<meta name="format-detection">` appears twice (`telephone=no` and `date=no`). This is harmless but redundant.
- **Web fonts via `@import`.** The Google Fonts `@import` and `<link>` are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, and the fallback stack is `Montserrat, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`. This is acceptable, but `@import` is stripped by Gmail and some other clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `[[...]]`) appear in the visible source. The HTML is truncated, so tokens further down the body are unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is inconclusive rather than a confirmed defect. Verify against the raw message.
- **No physical address detected.** The HTML source is truncated before the footer, so this cannot be confirmed from the data provided.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `Kohls@s.kohls.com`, and the tracking domains are `s.kohls.com`, `chp.kohls.com` and `mi.kohls.com`. Alignment was not verified.
- **Plain text is mostly URLs.** 13,694 of 19,444 characters (70.4%) are URLs, which is a spam-filter risk driven by the long `qs=` tracking strings.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No landing-page evaluation possible.** Links are wrapped in opaque redirects (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=...`), and the probe skipped all 77. UTM parameters and destination URLs cannot be confirmed.
- **Campaign tagging is in the image URLs only.** `mi_ecmp=1025936_20261115` and `email_name=261115_DG_Email_Myster...` appear on image requests, and the visible part of the source shows no UTM parameters.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Zoom:** Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta tag.
2. **Alt text:** Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images from `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png`. Add `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"` to every tracking pixel.
3. **Malformed HTML:**
   - Delete the stray `</custom>` after the `open.aspx` pixel.
   - Move the `itemscope` div out of `<head>`, or remove it.
   - Populate `<title>`, and remove or fill the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon" href="">`.
4. **Pixels:** Consolidate the four open pixels to reduce load, and consider moving the recipient ID out of image query strings.
5. **Headers:** Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` on the raw message, and confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass and align. Then re-run the audit with full headers.
6. **Footer:** Re-run with the full, untruncated HTML to verify the physical address, unsubscribe link and any merge tokens.
7. **Links:** Resolve a sample of the 77 redirect links to confirm landing URLs and UTM parameters.
