# HURRY, shop limited-time deals + reveal your savings before it's too late ...
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again. This is the fifth or sixth send since 11/10, and I'm tired. The header says "Super Saturday Deals" and "30% off with coupon" for Kohl's Rewards members, and then the email keeps going. I count about eight product blocks, each with its own percentage. There's 30% on dresses, $19.99 on shirts, 60% on Carter's and 40% on kitchen. Further down is a "Preview the Deals" tease, a Klarna and delivery strip, and a "picked with you in mind" product grid. That's a flyer, not a message.
- I shop the beauty counter, and the beauty counter doesn't talk to me like this. Nordstrom or Saks gives me one idea and a clean path to it. Kohl's gives me the whole store. The best thing here is the 30% coupon with the Rewards tie-in, and I only found it because I looked for it. Nothing here is beauty, and nothing is tuned to a 47-year-old who buys at brand events.
- Would I click? Probably only if I already needed towels or a dress. Would I forward it? No. I'd leave it unread with the other four.

## What's working

- The "30% off with coupon" hero, with "2 days only" and the Rewards Exclusive label, tells me what the deal is and how long it lasts.
- The Rewards perk strip ("$10 off your home purchase of $50 or more") repeats under several blocks. I like that, because points and member benefits are how I decide where to put my card.
- The dress and heel blocks near the top are the closest thing to my world. They show women who look roughly my age, in good clothes.
- The pricing is consistent: big numerals, "Starting at 19.99", and "Shop Now" buttons that are easy to tap.

## What's weak

- It's very long, and the top is crowded. By the time I scroll past luggage, slippers and fleece, I've forgotten the hero offer.
- There's no beauty at all. The Sephora Insider send on "luxe beauty gifts" is at least in my category, even if it's the ninth one that week.
- The fine print is dense and tiny. The "Kohl's Cash" and coupon-code section at the bottom is hard to read without zooming, and "Coupons do not apply to select brands" qualifies nearly every block.
- The sender name "Super Saturday" doesn't read as a brand I know. It doesn't differentiate this from the 11/13 "Save 20% | Earn Kohl's Cash" or the 11/12 "extra 20%" sends.
- Too many stacked offers: 30%, $10 off $50, $5 Kohl's Cash, Klarna, and same-day delivery. When everything shouts, none of it feels honest.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body to one hero and two or three supporting blocks, and put the Rewards coupon directly under the hero. Neiman Marcus's "Ends today: up to $650 off" send worked because it was one teal block, one code and one button.
- 2. Pick one deadline. "2 days only" is credible, but the subject, the header and the footer each say something slightly different.
- 3. Segment. A beauty or fashion shopper should see a beauty or fashion block first, not luggage.
- 4. Make the fine print readable. Macy's "confirm now for extra 25% off" at least kept its mechanics plain.
- 5. Slow the cadence. Five sends in four days teaches me to ignore you.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Today + tomorrow: 30% off with your Rewards coupon`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 30% coupon ends Sunday, plus $10 off $50 home`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Dresses, shoes and home. Rewards members get extra Kohl's Cash.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Show your coupon in the app or at the register. Ends Nov. 15.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is mostly sound, but it has several rendering and accessibility defects: stray `</custom>` markup, an empty `<title>`, pinch-zoom disabled, and missing alt text. All three compliance and authentication warnings come from the capture path, so they are unconfirmed. The HTML source is truncated, so the footer and most link `href`s could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link destinations are unverified.** 74 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…`). That is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Four separate open or tracking beacons fire in the first lines of `<body>`:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/baa2440e-…` (1x1)
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (width/height 0)
- **Redundant pixels and a third-party call.** Overlapping open tracking is redundant and adds request overhead. The Adobe Audience Manager (`demdex.net`) call is a third-party request that some clients block.
- **Malformed markup on the Salesforce open pixel.** It ends in `alt="">` followed by a stray `</custom>` closing tag. This is invalid HTML.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text is missing.** The QA flagged 11 images, including:
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`
  - `…/f441d45d2128ae53.png`
  - `…/c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`
  - `…/415c8a18fc2950ea.png`
  - `…/69494b48a79b4240.png`
  - `…/78308d1d9adb0751.png`
  - `…/916d6fa08d185233.png`

  Four of these are 1x1 tracking pixels. The `/p/rp/*.png` files carry `email_name=` and `mi_ecmp=` parameters, which suggests they are content images. Without alt text, these images are unreadable with images blocked or a screen reader. Some pixels, such as `o.gif` and the demdex one, already have `aria-hidden="true"` or `alt=""`, so the QA flag for those is noise.
- **Pinch-zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks zoom on mobile, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **The `<title>` is empty** (`<title></title>`).
- **`<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`.** This is invalid, and parsers will implicitly close `<head>` at that point. The `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` also has an empty `href`.
- **Web fonts depend on `@import` from `fonts.googleapis.com`.** The font stack falls back to `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`, so this is low risk. It is wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, so Outlook is handled.
- **Content tables mostly use `role="presentation"`.** Some layout tables, such as the preheader and pixel tables, do not.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear. Per-recipient identifiers (`mi_u=604230016`, `mi_mid=…`) are populated in the tracking URLs.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unconfirmed, probably a capture artifact:** the QA found no `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) or `Authentication-Results` header. The QA itself attributes this to the AgentMail relay stripping headers. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. Check the raw `.eml` before treating this as a Kohl's defect.
- **Footer not verifiable:** the unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated HTML. Nothing can be confirmed or ruled out.
- **Sender domain looks aligned:** the From address is `Kohls@s.kohls.com`, and the click domain `click.s.kohls.com` is on the same subdomain.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs cannot be verified.** Link `href`s are opaque (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7Im…`), and probing was skipped. No `utm_*` parameters are visible in any URL in the captured source.
- **Campaign ID is in the image URLs.** They carry `mi_ecmp=1025883_20261114` and `email_name=261114_DG_Email_Myster…`, which matches today's send date.
- **Landing pages untested.** Whether the final landing pages match the offers is unknown, because redirects were not resolved.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the content images in `/p/rp/*.png`, and `alt=""` to the 1x1 pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Fix the stray `</custom>` after `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx`, populate `<title>`, and move the `itemscope` div out of `<head>` or remove it.
4. Consolidate the four tracking pixels where possible.
5. For the audit pipeline:
   - Capture the raw `.eml` headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) outside the relay.
   - Store the full HTML so the footer can be checked.
   - Resolve the redirect chains for a sample of the 74 skipped links, so UTMs and landing pages can be checked.
   - Suppress alt-text warnings for 1x1 tracking pixels.
