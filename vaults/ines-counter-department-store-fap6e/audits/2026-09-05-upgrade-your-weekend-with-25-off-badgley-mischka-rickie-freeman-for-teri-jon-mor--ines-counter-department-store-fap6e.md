- Zero InCircle loyalty language anywhere — no points, no tier status, nothing that makes me feel like a recognized regular rather than a cold subscriber.

- "Ends 9/7/26 at 10:00 PM CT" urgency is buried in the legal fine print at the bottom, not up near the button where it'd actually nudge me.
