# Use your 0 points on a 250-point bundle
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- Sephora again, and the subject line misleads me again. "Use your 0 points on a 250-point bundle" makes no sense. If I have zero points, I can't use them on anything. I'm guessing it's a merge glitch. I opened it anyway because the sender name, "Sephora Savings Event," promised a discount.
- The top is busy. There's an "ALONSALE" code for 10% off, a "Want up to 500 points?" banner, and then a "November means new rewards!" Rewards Bazaar hero. That's three messages before I reach the actual reward grid. The grid is clean, with 100-point and 250-point tiers. Even so, it's the same promo layout I've seen all week. On 11/05 the 10% Insider code led, and on 11/04 it was the Mistletoe Must-Haves. This is the same machine with a different headline.
- Would I click? Maybe, if I were at the point-redemption stage. Would I forward it? No. I'd mark it read and wait for the real event.

## What's working

- The 100-point and 250-point reward tiers are labelled clearly. I can see what my points buy without doing math.
- The Insider 10% code is visible right at the top, with the dates (11/3–11/9) next to it.
- The product grid has real brands I know: Saie, Huda Beauty, Moroccanoil, Dior, Fenty, Byredo. That reads as beauty-counter territory.
- The barcode at the bottom for in-store use at Kohl's suits how I shop, since I buy at brand events and in person.

## What's weak

- The subject line reads like a broken template. "0 points" is insulting to a loyal customer and tells me nothing.
- The top is too stacked. The 500-point challenge, the 10% code, the free-gift P.S., the Rewards Bazaar and the new reward option all compete for the first screen.
- Nearly every reward is a $-level sample or small item. Hair oil, a perfume mini and a lip product don't feel like a reward for the points I earn.
- The lower half piles on more promos: a free Parachute gift, Kérastase, the holiday gift guide, and four small CTAs. This is the Macy's-style overload I avoid.
- The fine print is long and dense. I'd need to zoom to read it, and that makes the offer feel less honest.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the subject so it names the actual offer and uses my real point balance, or leave points out when the balance is zero.
- 2. Pick one hero. Make the Rewards Bazaar the lead, and move the 10% code and the free-gift line lower.
- 3. Cut the lower promos down to one secondary offer.
- 4. Put a short, readable summary of the terms near the CTA. Don't bury everything in the long fine print.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New rewards are in: 100 points gets you Dior`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Insider points, 250-point picks inside`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Browse 100- and 250-point rewards before they sell out.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus 10% off for Insiders through 11/9 with code ALONSALE.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** The tracking pixel `<img src='https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...'>` has no `alt` attribute (QA WARN). It is hidden, so the impact is low, but adding `alt=""` would clear the check.
- **No plain-text part:** The text version is 0 chars (QA FAIL). Clients that render plain text, and spam filters that score the text/HTML ratio, get nothing.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>` prevents pinch-zoom on mobile, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Invalid meta value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. The space makes the value invalid. It is harmless in email clients.
- **Malformed CSS:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {...} <![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` block of the `<style>` tag. Conditional comments aren't valid inside CSS, so the MSO sizing rule likely won't apply and may break the rules that follow it.
- **Fixed width:** The outer table is `width:700; min-width:700px`. On narrow viewports this forces horizontal scroll unless the client rescales the email.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` is not descriptive.
- **Unused JSON-LD:** The block has `"discountCode": ""` and a bare `PromotionCard` type with no properties. Gmail won't use either, so they are dead markup.
