# 40% off + Kohl's Cash is worth a look 👀
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-19**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again — and this one's actually got a decent amount going on for a change: it's a "Rewards Member Exclusive" 40% off, there's Kohl's Cash stacked on top, and unlike the last four sends I've logged (the Customer Appreciation Event on the 15th, the Rewards pushes on the 16th and 17th, the Kohl's Cash tease on the 18th), the body actually shows me product this time — flannel shirts, a slow cooker, jeans, a mattress topper. It's kitchen-sink Kohl's, no doubt, more Macy's-style overload than Nordstrom restraint, but at least the merchandising isn't just another colored discount ladder.
- That said, nothing here says "you, specifically." I'm not a mom shopping for a kid in plaid, and the grid jumps from women's coats to kids' overalls to a Ninja Slushi machine to bath towels with zero curation — this is Kohl's shouting at everyone at once, which is exactly the opposite of what I'd want from a beauty-counter brand like Neiman's, where the CHANEL send I logged was two clean product shots and nothing else. I'd skim, maybe screenshot the coupon code for my mom who actually shops Kohl's for the grandkids, but I'm not clicking through myself.

## What's working

- The 40% off banner is unmissable — big red type, clear expiration date (Sept. 20), and a visible "Shop Now" button right under it.
- "Kohl's Rewards Member" status line at the top actually acknowledges I'm a member, which is more than most of their recent sends bothered with.
- Product photography is a step up from prior sends — real styled shots (the flannel-shirt kid, the cardigan woman) instead of just banner graphics.
- The coupon code and barcode are printed right in the email, so I don't need to click anything to redeem in-store — that's a small but real convenience nod.

## What's weak

- The category grid (Home, Women, Men, Kids, then Bedding, Bath, Kitchen, Decor, then random products) is pure Macy's-style shotgun marketing — nothing here is curated for a 47-year-old beauty-counter shopper.
- Featured "picked with you in mind" products are a slushi maker, a bath towel, jeans, and a mattress topper — that's not "picked with me in mind," that's "picked with everyone in mind."
- No beauty, skincare, or fragrance content at all despite Kohl's carrying Sephora in-store — a total miss for someone who cross-shops the beauty counter.
- It's the fifth near-identical promo I've logged from Kohl's this week; the 40% headline number is new, but the format (rewards banner, Kohl's Cash bar, coupon at the bottom) is copy-paste from the 15th–18th.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment the product grid by purchase history — if I've never bought bedding or kids' clothes from them, don't lead with it. Sephora's sends at least stay in-lane with beauty; Kohl's should do the same with whatever category I actually shop.
- 2. Cut the send frequency. Five near-identical emails in five days trains me to stop opening — pick one or two big moments (this 40%-off one is a fine anchor) and skip the daily nudge.
- 3. If Kohl's wants to compete for someone like me, surface the Sephora-at-Kohl's shop-in-shop somewhere in the grid — right now it's invisible.
- 4. Move the coupon code/barcode higher or repeat it near the top CTA — I had to scroll through the entire grid to find the actual redemption mechanism.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 40% off + Kohl's Cash, member — ends Sept. 20`
- **Subject Alt B:** `40% off select styles, just for Rewards members`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus, earn $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 you spend.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fall clothing starts at $14.99 — shop before Sunday.`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Standard Kohl's/CoherentPath marketing template with multiple tracking pixels and click-redirect wrapping; unsubscribe/authentication headers were not observable in the captured payload, and most images lack alt text.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Tracking-domain click wrapper in use: `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=...` (Coherent Path ESP link wrapping) — all CTA links are redirected through this domain rather than direct destination URLs, consistent with the 65 tracking links the automated scan skipped for HTTP probing.
- Multiple beacon/pixel endpoints fire on open: `https://click.chp.kohls.com/o/727255cb-...`, `https://mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?...`, `https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?ZR2FJCF5EOXUVDAK3YYN4M4XR4.60265&d=60265&bmt=0`, and an Adobe Audience Manager call `https://kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`. All are 1x1/0-height, consistent with open-tracking rather than content.
- No broken or malformed `href`/`src` syntax observed in the visible (truncated) source.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 8 of 8 images flagged by QA are missing `alt` attributes: the tracking pixel `727255cb-...`, `o.gif`, the Demdex `event` pixel, and rendering pixels `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`. These are tracking/beacon images, so missing alt text has no meaningful accessibility impact (they should be `alt=""` with `aria-hidden`, which one pixel already correctly does).
- One tracking pixel `<img>` is immediately followed by a stray `</custom>` closing tag with no matching opening tag — invalid markup that some renderers may handle inconsistently.
- MSO/Outlook conditional comments, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, and responsive `@media (max-width:500px)` rules are present — standard cross-client scaffolding, no issues found.
- Web fonts (Roboto, Montserrat) are loaded via `<link>`/`@import` from Google Fonts with no fallback guard for clients that block external CSS (e.g., Outlook desktop); body font-family does correctly fall back to `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (e.g. `{{`, `%%`, `[[`) visible in the truncated source.
- Query params contain per-recipient/tracking identifiers (`mi_cid=67791fba63db790b`, `mi_mid=01a0b840-0b00-7000-b482-e2ae5af2b015`, `mi_u=604230016`) — these are resolved IDs, not raw template tokens. No issues found.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- List-Unsubscribe header not detected — could not confirm one-click unsubscribe capability at the header level (QA WARN). Note per QA this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not preserving the header rather than the sender omitting it — cannot fully confirm from this data alone.
- List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not detected — same caveat.
- No physical mailing address detected in the visible source — CAN-SPAM requires one; the address is commonly placed in a footer not included in this truncated excerpt, so this may be a truncation artifact rather than a confirmed omission. Recommend verifying against the full HTML before treating as a real violation.
- Authentication-Results header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not present in available data — cannot confirm sender authentication status via this relay.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- CTA/tracking links use Coherent Path's `mi_ecmp=1022066_2026919` and `email_name=260919_DG_Email_Mystery` campaign identifiers embedded in pixel URLs rather than standard `utm_*` parameters — final landing page UTM values can't be verified since all outbound links are wrapped in the `click.s.kohls.com` redirector (truncated before resolution).
- Cannot confirm landing-page alignment (destination URL, promo code carry-through) because the redirect targets are not resolved in the available source/QA data.

7. Recommendations
- Confirm List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post headers are actually being sent at the MTA level (check raw headers outside the AgentMail relay, since the relay may be stripping them) — required for RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe support and CAN-SPAM.
- Verify the physical mailing address is present in the full (non-truncated) footer.
- Confirm Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass) directly from the raw MTA headers rather than the relay.

- Remove the orphaned `</custom>` closing tag near the `open.aspx` pixel.
