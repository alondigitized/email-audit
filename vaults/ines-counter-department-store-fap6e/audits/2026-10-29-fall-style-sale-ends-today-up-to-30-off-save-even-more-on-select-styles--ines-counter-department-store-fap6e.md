# Fall Style Sale ends today: up to 30% off + save even more on select styles
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-29**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Neiman's sale email I've opened in two days, and it's the same Fall Style Sale as this morning's "last chance" send. The subject now says "ends today," so the deadline has moved up one more notch. The hero is clean: "UP TO 30% OFF" in big red serif on blush, with a black SHOP NOW button. I know what the offer is in about a second. Nordstrom would do this with less ceremony, and Macy's would bury it under four other promos.
- What lets it down is that the fine print undercuts the hero. "Select regular-price items" and "Exclusions Apply" sit right under the number. The footer says it doesn't apply to beauty or fragrance, and beauty is why I shop at department stores. I'd skim it, maybe click if I were already eyeing a boot, and not forward it. It's a polite email, though, and it doesn't shout.

## What's working

- The hero offer is unmissable: red "30% OFF" against blush, with a single black button.
- It doesn't shout. There's no ALL CAPS subject and no emoji. That's a contrast to Sephora's "🌟 THIS JUST IN 🌟" this week.
- The 3x3 product grid is real designer product, with brand names under each image: Dolce&Gabbana, Balmain, Stuart Weitzman, Polo Ralph Lauren. That tells me this is designer access, not a clearance bin.
- The category buttons (Women's Clothing, Handbags, Women's Shoes, Jewelry) let me jump to my own aisle.
- The footer has a real deadline: "Ends 10/29/26 at 10:00 PM CT." I'd rather see that than a vague "ends soon."

## What's weak

- The grid products have no prices and no sale markdowns. I can't tell what 30% off a Balmain bag actually means.
- The fine print excludes beauty and fragrance. The email never says so up top, and for me that's a bait-and-switch feeling.
- This is the same sale I got this morning in the "last chance" send. After Tuesday's "$50 gift card for every $200" reminder, it's a lot of cadence for one week.
- There's no loyalty or InCircle signal anywhere. Neiman's knows who I am, yet this treats me like a stranger. Macy's at least mentions Star Rewards and extra-percentage tiers.
- The "CLAIM YOUR 15% OFF" text-signup block competes with the main offer. 15% for joining the text list is a second promo that muddies the first.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one line of fine print in the hero itself, such as "Excludes beauty & fragrance," so the exclusion isn't a surprise in the footer.
- 2. Show sale prices on the grid, like "$1,890 → $1,323," so the discount is concrete.
- 3. Add a member line, like "InCircle members: extra points on sale," so I feel recognized.
- 4. Link each product tile to its own product page, not a general sale page.
- 5. Cut the text-signup block from sale-ending sends, or move it below the footer links.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Tonight 10 PM CT: Fall Style Sale ends`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Designer boots, bags and knits, marked down through tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Extra 15% off select sale styles. Ends 10 PM CT.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with media-query responsive CSS. Two confirmed findings: internal ESP template comments are left in the delivered source, and the viewport meta blocks pinch-zoom. The QA flags on headers and tracking links are inconclusive because of the AgentMail relay and the truncated source.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- The QA run skipped 30 tracking/click-redirect links ("skipped HTTP probe"), so none of their destinations, redirect chains or status codes were verified.
- The source I received is truncated, so I can't check individual `href` values for broken, empty or `#` links.
- The plain-text part is 5628 of 6825 characters URLs (~82%, over the 70% threshold). This is probably caused by long tracked URLs and a large link count, and it can hurt spam scoring and text-only readability.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` stops pinch-zoom on mobile, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) and 1.4.10 (Reflow).
- **Checkbox-hack nav:** the CSS builds the hamburger menu with `.navbar-checkbox:checked~.menu`, and `.menu` defaults to `display:none; visibility:hidden`. Gmail and many Outlook builds don't support `:checked`, so the nav may be unreachable there. The visible source gives no evidence of a fallback. I can't confirm this without the body markup.
- **Minor CSS issues:**
  - `*[class].show` declares `width: auto !important` and later `width: 278px`, a redundant duplicate.
  - `body { -webkit-animation: bugfix infinite 1s }` is a perpetual animation hack that runs for the life of the message.
  - `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` has a trailing space.
- The visible portion doesn't let me check `alt` attributes, `lang` on `<html>`, `role="presentation"` on layout tables or image dimensions. `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` attribute.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Template comment leaked into production:** the first node is an HTML comment from the ESP template. It reads `<!--REQUIRED variables: @email ... @base_refid ... Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", "EMAIL_ID", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email)) ... Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "uEm", ...) ... CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW -->`. It exposes internal data extension names (`NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`), field names (`EMAIL_ID`, `uEm`, `cmdid`) and the hashing approach (`SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256`). It also sits above the DOCTYPE. A comment before the DOCTYPE can push some clients into quirks mode.
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `@var`) appear in the visible rendered markup outside that comment.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe missing:** the QA check says the header "may not be captured by AgentMail relay". That is not a confirmed sender defect.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) missing:** same caveat. If it is also absent at the source, the sender doesn't meet the Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender one-click requirement.
- **SPF/DKIM/DMARC unknown:** `Authentication-Results` wasn't captured. This is a relay limitation, so authentication can't be judged from this data.
- The footer (physical address, visible unsubscribe link) is outside the truncated source, so I couldn't verify it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- Not determinable. The visible source contains no resolved link URLs, so I can't check UTM parameters or landing page alignment.
- The comment references `@refid`/`@base_refid` and `@cmdid`, which suggests campaign IDs are appended in links. That is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Strip the ESP template comment block from the delivered HTML, or move it into the authoring layer so it isn't rendered. Keep nothing above the DOCTYPE.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta (use `width=device-width, initial-scale=1`).
3. Add `lang` to `<html>`. Provide a visible fallback for the checkbox-hack nav in clients that don't support `:checked`, such as a linked category list.
4. Re-run header capture against the original message, not the relay copy, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
5. Re-run link validation with the 30 skipped tracking links resolved (follow redirects with a GET) and check UTM/refid on the final landing URLs.
6. Check the text part for a trimmed, human-readable alternative. Shortening tracked URLs would also reduce the URL ratio.
