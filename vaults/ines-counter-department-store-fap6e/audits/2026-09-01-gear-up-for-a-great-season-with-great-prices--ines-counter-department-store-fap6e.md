# Gear up for a great season with great prices 🏈🏆
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-01**
## Executive summary

- Sports jerseys and NFL/NCAA/MLB fan gear — from Kohl's? That's not who I go to for this, and it's not who I am. I'm not shopping team merch, I'm shopping the beauty counter and the shoe wall, so this whole top two-thirds of the email is dead air for me. Then, out of nowhere, there's a Sephora + Kohl's banner promising 50% off select beauty, and that's the one thing that actually made me stop scrolling.
- The "Picked with you in mind" block at the bottom is where it falls apart completely — a slushie machine, a bath towel, jeans, and a mattress topper, side by side, is not "picked with me in mind," that's just an inventory dump with my name slapped on it. Sephora at least led with a real, specific offer last time I looked ($5 off 250 points, 4x points, "ends today") — clean, member-first, easy. This is the opposite: buried, generic, and mostly irrelevant to me.
- Verdict: not clicking past the Sephora banner if anything, marking as read, definitely not forwarding.

## What's working

- The Sephora + Kohl's 50% off beauty banner is the only section that speaks to me directly — clean color block, clear offer, clear CTA.
- The clearance banner up top (85% off after extra 50%) is bold and easy to read at a glance.
- Nav bar (Home/Women/Men/Kids) is at least legible and tappable.
- Rewards status line at the very top ("KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER... Earn 5% REWARDS") is a nice small nod that they know I'm a member.

## What's weak

- Three full screens of NFL/NCAA/MLB jerseys and hats before anything relevant to me — total mismatch for a 47-year-old beauty-counter shopper.
- "Every great season starts with great gear" reads like it was written for someone's teenage son's inbox, not mine.
- "Picked with you in mind" slaps a personalization label on a random grab-bag (drink maker, towels, jeans, mattress topper) — it's the opposite of curated, and it insults the idea of personalization.
- The Sephora deal, the one thing I'd actually act on, is stranded in the middle of the email under a wall of sports gear I've already tuned out of.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the Sephora beauty banner or put it right after the clearance hero — don't make me scroll past three sports categories to find the one relevant offer.
- 2. Segment this send — if I've never clicked sports/team gear, don't build two-thirds of my email around it.
- 3. Fix "Picked with you in mind" — either use real purchase-history logic or drop the personalization language entirely; right now it reads as false advertising.
- 4. Give the Sephora banner a real CTA detail (dollar/point amount like Sephora's own emails do) instead of just "50% Off Select Beauty" with no specifics.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Sephora + Kohl's: 50% off beauty, new deals daily`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Rewards + up to 85% off clearance`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus: Sephora beauty deals refresh daily through Sept 17`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `5% Rewards earned automatically — see what's on sale`

# Technical Audit — Kohl's "Gear up for a great season" (260901_DG_Email_Tuesday)

## 1. Technical Summary
Email is a standard MSO/Litmus-hybrid HTML template (VML + `mso` conditionals, media queries at 500px) with heavy multi-vendor tracking (CHP/Kevel click-tracking, Kohl's `mi.kohls.com` beacons, Adobe AAM). Core compliance signals (unsubscribe headers, sender authentication) could not be verified from headers, and 12 images are missing `alt` text.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- Secondary open pixel: `https://mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1020943_202691`.
- Third open/click beacon: `https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?GK3HNWPRFXJEJFS4E6HBYSED6Y.60266&d=60266&bmt=0`, wrapped in a stray `</custom>` closing tag with no matching opening tag — invalid markup that some strict parsers/clients may mis-render (does not affect major clients but should be cleaned up at the source template).
- Adobe Audience Manager beacon: `https://kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`.
- QA flagged 53 tracking/click-redirect links skipped from HTTP probing (expected — these are wrapped `click.chp.kohls.com` / `click.s.kohls.com` redirect links, not directly resolvable without triggering tracked clicks). No destination-URL validation was possible from this data; flag as unverified rather than broken.
- No standard `utm_*` query parameters observed on any visible link — Kohl's uses proprietary `mi_ecmp`, `mi_u`, `mi_mid`, `cp_tp`, `cp_cid` params instead (see Section 6).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 12 images missing `alt` text per QA scan, including 4 product images (`57841439_1_2x` through `_4_2x` from `images.am.kohls.com`) that carry visible content and should have descriptive `alt`, not just tracking pixels. Tracking-pixel-only images (`o.gif`, `event`, `*.png` beacons under `mi.kohls.com`) are lower priority for `alt` but should still carry `alt=""` with `aria-hidden="true"` for consistency (only one of the beacons, `click.s.kohls.com`, currently does this).
- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow" />` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer" />` are present — correct for a transactional/marketing email, no issue.
- MSO conditional blocks and mobile media query (`max-width: 500px`) are present and structurally sound in the visible source.
- Malformed markup: `</custom>` closing tag with no corresponding opening tag inside the open-tracking `<div>` — minor HTML validity defect, low rendering risk but worth fixing at the template source.
