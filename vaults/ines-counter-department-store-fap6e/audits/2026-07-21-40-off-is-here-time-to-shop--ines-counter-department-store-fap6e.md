# 40% off is here ... time to shop 🛒
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-21**
## Executive summary

- Sixty-two. I stopped counting Kohl's emails around there, and this one lands in my inbox wearing the exact same costume as yesterday's. The subject says "40% off is here" and when I open it, that's exactly what I get — a Friends & Family event with the same percentage, the same "TODAY ONLY" urgency, and a clearance rack thrown in for good measure. After yesterday's 85%-off clearance email that barely moved the needle for me, this feels like a step backward in creativity.
- What this email is: a broad department-store circular jammed into an email frame. Kids. FLX activewear. Bedding. Bath. Kitchen. Shoes. It's the everything-for-everyone approach that Macy's has started to move away from, and Saks would never touch. I'm 47. I shop the beauty counter. There is not a single reference to skincare, fragrance, or cosmetics in this entire scroll. The Kohl's Rewards strip (15% off, Kohl's Cash earning) is the one thing that slows me down because I do care about points — but even that's buried below the fold.
- I would not click. I would not forward. I'd mark it read and move on, mildly annoyed that the Friends & Family offer I might have cared about was wasted on a creative that treats me like I'm shopping for a seven-year-old's soccer cleats.

## What's working

- **TODAY ONLY Friends & Family is a real event signal** — it's different from the rolling 40% they've been pushing all week, and the barcode coupon with an actual code at the bottom gives it credibility.
- **Kohl's Cash earning mechanic is clearly stated** — "Earn $50 Kohl's Cash for every $100 spent through Jul 21, redeemable Jul 22–Aug 10" is explicit and I notice it. Points-back is how I decide whether to spend, so this lands.
- **No render bugs that I can see** — the email loads cleanly, images are intact, text doesn't overlap. Compared to some Macy's emails I've seen with ghost image blocks, this is technically tidy.
- **Clearance 85% off callout is still in the mix** — even though yesterday's version led with it, having it visible here as a secondary beat is a smart retention of that hook.

## What's weak

- **Zero beauty content** — I am a beauty-counter regular. Kohl's carries fragrance, wellness, and beauty brands. None of it appears. Sephora's "bag edit" email last week opened with Charlotte Tilbury above the fold and earned every click. This doesn't even try.
- **Family/kids imagery throughout** — "Outfit the whole family (Even the ones who never sit still)" is aimed squarely at a 35-year-old suburban mom, not me. Demographic mismatch.
- **The 40% offer is worn out** — I audited four near-identical 40% subjects in the last 48 hours from this sender. Even with the Friends & Family wrapper, it reads as the same drum.
- **CTA copy is generic** — every button says "Shop Now." Neiman Marcus at least managed "1,000+ new arrivals" in their subject to create a sense of discovery. "Shop Now" gives me nothing.
- **Visual hierarchy is exhausted by the scroll** — the email is extremely long with six or seven distinct sections all competing for equal weight. Sephora gets one hero, one CTA, and gets out. This is a circular.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Surface beauty or wellness content for known beauty shoppers** — even a single skincare or fragrance module above the fold would transform this email for a segment Kohl's demonstrably has. If they have purchase history, use it.
- 2. **Lead with the loyalty mechanic** — "Earn $50 Kohl's Cash today" is a stronger hook for a member like me than "40% off is here." Put that in the subject line.
- 3. **Cut the email in half** — pick one audience (family vs. beauty vs. home) per send. The FLX activewear, kids, home goods, and bedding sections are all fighting each other.
- 4. **Retire "Shop Now" as button copy** — "Shop Women's" or "Shop Beauty" is one word more and ten times more clickable.
- 5. **Vary the offer framing** — "Friends & Family Exclusive" hits differently than "40% off is here" even when the discount is identical.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Friends & Family: your 40% is here today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Earn $50 Kohl's Cash — Friends & Family starts now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 85% off clearance + Kohl's Cash through tonight`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your coupon code is waiting — today only`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "40% off is here" (2026-07-21)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is structurally sound but carries three compliance gaps (missing unsubscribe headers, no confirmed physical address), a likely unfired merge token in an image beacon URL, and an invalid custom HTML tag. Duplicate Google Fonts loading adds unnecessary overhead.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 tracking links unprobed** — all clicks route through `click.chp.kohls.com` (CoherentPath) and `click.s.kohls.com`; final destinations and UTM passthrough could not be verified by QA.

**Three distinct tracking pixels in play:**
- `click.chp.kohls.com/o/db17c9a5...` — CoherentPath open pixel
- `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbb.../o.gif` — Marigold/Sailthru open pixel
- `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` — Adobe Audience Manager sync pixel

**Invalid closing tag on open pixel:**
```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?2OGBJMZQVIY...">
</custom>   ← invalid element; should be absent or </div>
```
`</custom>` is a non-standard element that will be misinterpreted by strict parsers and may cause rendering issues in some clients.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**11 images missing `alt` text** — flagged by QA across tracking pixels and content images including `55869996_root2_2x` (a product image on `images.am.kohls.com`). Tracking pixels should use `alt=""` explicitly; content images require descriptive text.

**Mobile zoom disabled:**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />
```
`user-scalable=0` fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text). Gmail Android and some accessibility tools will flag this.

**Empty `<title>` tag** — `<title></title>` renders as blank in clients that surface the title (e.g., some webmail preheader fallbacks).

**Duplicate font loading** — Roboto and Montserrat are loaded twice each: once via `<link>` and again via `@import` inside `<style>`. The MSO conditional comment gates the `<link>` tags correctly, but the `@import` rules are outside any conditional, causing double requests in non-MSO clients.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Likely unfired token in image beacon URL:**
```
https://mi.kohls.com/p/rp/916d6fa08d185233.png
  ?mi_u=604230016
  &mi_ecmp=1018062_2026721
  &mi_kc_redm_end_dte=        ← empty value
  &mi_kc_event_pe             ← value missing / truncated in source
```
`mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` has no value. If this is a Marigold personalization field (e.g., offer end date for dynamic content), it did not populate. The truncated `mi_kc_event_pe` param is also suspicious.

**Subscriber ID appears correctly resolved:** `mi_u=604230016` and `mi_ecmp=1018062_2026721` are present and consistent across all beacon URLs. `email_name=260721_DG_Email_Tuesday` is also consistently applied.

No `{{token}}`, `%%var%%`, or `[FIRST_NAME]`-style literals visible in the truncated source.

---

### 5. Compliance

| Check | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| List-Unsubscribe header | MISSING | Not captured by AgentMail relay |
| List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) | MISSING | One-click unsubscribe not confirmed |
| Physical mailing address | NOT DETECTED | QA found no address in parsed HTML; may be in truncated lower body |
| SPF/DKIM/DMARC | UNKNOWN | `Authentication-Results` header absent from relay capture |

**List-Unsubscribe** absence is the most critical: Gmail and Apple Mail use this header for native unsubscribe UI; missing it increases spam complaint rate and risks filtering. **RFC 8058** compliance (`List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`) is now enforced by Gmail/Yahoo for bulk senders.

**Physical address** must be verified in the full HTML below the fold — CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires it in the body. If absent, this is a legal violation.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**UTM passthrough unverifiable** — all 22 destination links are wrapped in `click.chp.kohls.com` redirects which QA skipped. Cannot confirm whether UTM params (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) are appended to final kohls.com URLs.

**Campaign naming is consistent in beacons** (`email_name=260721_DG_Email_Tuesday`, `mi_ecmp=1018062_2026721`) but these only appear in image tracking URLs, not in click-through links — so attribution depends entirely on CoherentPath's redirect behavior.

**`<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer" />`** is set in the head. This suppresses the HTTP Referer header on all link clicks, which will cause kohls.com analytics to show direct traffic rather than email referral for any non-UTM-tagged links.

---
