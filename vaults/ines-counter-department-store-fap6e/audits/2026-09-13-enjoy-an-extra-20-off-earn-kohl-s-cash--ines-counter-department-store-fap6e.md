# Enjoy an extra 20% off + earn Kohl's Cash 🤑
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-13**
## Executive summary

- Well, here we go again — Kohl's, sixth time this week if I'm counting right, and this is basically the same email skeleton I flagged in Wednesday's "cozy fall refresh" and Sunday's Customer Appreciation blast: the 30%/20%/15% discount ladder up top, the same nav pills, and now a "Picked with you in mind" grid that's just throwing a slushy maker, a bath towel, jeans, and a multi-cooker at me like darts at a board. There is one genuinely nice moment — the sweater and clog product shots have decent styling — but it's buried under clearance banners, coupon barcodes, and app-download footers that have nothing to do with me as a "beauty-counter regular" who cross-shops Nordstrom and Saks.
- Compared to Neiman Marcus's wedding-guest email, which led with one aspirational gown and let it breathe, this is chaos — eleven-plus product blocks, three different call-outs before I even see a human model. And unlike Sephora's daily deal (which at least stays in one category, beauty), Kohl's is selling me slushy makers and towels in the same breath as sweaters. This isn't a beauty-counter email at all — there's no cosmetics, no fragrance, no loyalty tier language beyond the generic Rewards line at the top. I'm marking as read and moving on, not forwarding this to anyone.

## What's working

- The maroon sweater shot is clean and well-lit — that's the one product I'd actually consider clicking on.
- "Status: Kohl's Rewards Member / Earn: 5% Rewards" at the very top at least acknowledges I'm a known customer, even minimally.
- The discount tiers (30%/20%/15%) are legible and color-coded so I can spot my own rate fast.
- Clearance banner is bold and easy to scan if I were in a bargain-hunting mood.

## What's weak

- Zero beauty or fragrance content — as someone who shops the counter, this email has nothing for me in my actual category.
- The "Picked with you in mind" grid pairing a slushy maker, a bath towel, jeans, and a multi-cooker feels like randomized inventory, not personalization — nothing here reads like it knows my taste.
- This is the same three-tier coupon math I've now seen from Kohl's five times this week (Sunday, two on Wednesday, Thursday, today) — the cadence is exhausting and the creative isn't evolving.
- No card-linked points-back messaging beyond the generic Rewards line — as someone who won't use a card without points, I need to see that spelled out, not implied.
- The barcode/coupon-in-wallet block at the bottom feels transactional and cold, not like a brand that knows me.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this into a beauty-specific send for members like me instead of the everything-under-one-roof approach — Sephora and Ulta wouldn't dare mix towels and jeans into a beauty push.
- 2. Cut the "Picked with you in mind" grid down to actually reflect purchase history, or drop the personalization claim entirely since it currently undercuts trust.
- 3. Slow the cadence — five nearly identical discount-ladder emails in a week trains me to stop opening them at all.
- 4. Lead with one hero item (like Neiman's did with that gown) instead of eleven scattered product blocks.
- 5. Make the points-back / card benefit explicit near the top, since that's my top purchase driver.
