## What's weak

- Too many ideas in one email. Points, new arrivals, holiday, auto-replenish, Rhode and hair deals all compete. Macy's does the same thing, and it's what I dislike about Macy's.
- The Haus Labs lip liner gets top billing, but I'm not that customer. The lead product skews younger than me.

- The points offer needs a code and the fine print is dense. I can see the footer is a wall of tiny text, and the Insider tier details aren't clear.
- This is the 10/2 hair and brow offer again, and the 10/2 points offer, in new packaging. It feels repetitive.

- 3. Show tier benefits, such as the Insider, VIB and Rouge points multipliers, next to the 2X and 5X badge. I want to know what I earn at my level.
