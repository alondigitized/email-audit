# EXTENDED: Enjoy 40% off for an extra day 👉
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-22**
## Executive summary

- Here we go again. "EXTENDED." That's the word Kohl's chose after telling me last night that 40% off was going, going, gone — the email with the "HOURS LEFT ⏳" subject that I gave a 7 mostly on the strength of the 85% clearance add-on. Now they're resurrecting a deadline that was supposed to be a deadline. I've been counting since Memorial Day and this one lands somewhere in the sixties — wearing the exact same costume as Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday.
- But here's the thing that actually stopped me cold when I opened it: the subject says "EXTENDED for an extra day," and the hero banner inside the email still says **ENDS TONIGHT.** The body template was never updated. So Kohl's is telling me two contradictory things simultaneously — the deal is extended, and the deal ends tonight — and they apparently didn't notice. That's not a strategy problem, that's a production error, and it makes the whole email feel sloppy in a way that no amount of bold typography can fix.
- The rest of it is familiar wallpaper: home goods section with colorful throw pillows, a Kitchen & Dining block, and then a FLX athletic section at the bottom showing a guy in a graphic tee with up to 50% off. I am a 47-year-old woman who shops beauty counters at Nordstrom and Macy's. There is not a single beauty signal in this entire email. I would not click. I would not forward. I'd quietly start reconsidering whether I need Kohl's in my inbox at all.

## What's working

- **The 40% hero is immediately legible** — high contrast, above the fold, I don't have to hunt for the offer number.
- **85% clearance is a genuinely strong secondary number** — it was the hook that got me to open the last send; at least it's still here.
- **Kohl's Cash is acknowledged in the footer** — it's the loyalty signal that keeps me from unsubscribing entirely; at least it's visible somewhere.
- **Clean render** — layout holds, images load, nothing overlapping. Given that Macy's has broken layouts on me more than once this summer, a clean render is not nothing.

## What's weak

- **The body still says "ENDS TONIGHT" while the subject promises an extension** — this is the most damaging thing in the email. Kohl's sent me two contradictory deadlines in the same message, which means neither is credible.
- **"EXTENDED" as a subject word is a credibility kill** — the moment a brand extends a "tonight only" offer, every future deadline I receive from them is meaningless. Sephora's cadence has been intense this week too (five emails in four days), but at least "JUST DROPPED & ONLY HERE" wasn't a retraction.
- **Zero beauty signal** — I cross-shop Nordstrom, Macy's, and Saks specifically for their beauty programs. Kohl's has beauty. None of it is in this email. The FLX guy in a graphic tee is not my demographic, and home décor throw pillows are not why I open a Kohl's email.
- **Cadence burnout** — somewhere in the sixties of emails since Memorial Day. The "welcome to the 'I want it all' section" headline feels like the copy team ran out of ideas.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the body copy immediately** — if you're extending, the hero banner cannot say "ENDS TONIGHT." Update the deadline call-out to match the subject or kill it entirely. This is a production error, not a creative problem, and it has to ship clean.
- 2. **Kill "EXTENDED" framing, full stop** — just don't announce the extension as the subject. "Shop before midnight" or "Your 40% off — still on" is less embarrassing than admitting the deadline wasn't real.
- 3. **Segment beauty subscribers separately** — Kohl's has a beauty section. I should not be receiving the same email as someone browsing for bedding. One beauty hero — fragrance, skincare, a brand name I recognize — and I stay in this email longer.
- 4. **Promote Kohl's Cash above the fold** — it's buried in the footer. The main reason a loyalty member like me clicks on Kohl's over a Nordstrom link is the earn. Make that visible near the CTA, not in the fine print zone.
- 5. **Thin the sections** — home goods, FLX, clearance, Kitchen & Dining, Decor, and a store-locator CTA in one send. Neiman Marcus has the same overload problem, but their "garden party" email at least communicated a point of view. This reads like a circular ad.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Shop your 40% off — before midnight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Still 40% off, and the clearance is real`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus up to 85% off clearance + Kohl's Cash on everything you buy`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Home, FLX, and more — offer ends tonight for real this time`

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is a well-formed HTML 4.01 Transitional document with standard ESP infrastructure (Coherent Path + MessageGears). ### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **22 tracking links skipped** by the HTTP probe — all routed through `click.chp.kohls.com` (Coherent Path) and `click.s.kohls.com` (MessageGears). Redirect chains are expected; no broken destinations confirmed but none verified.
- **3 tracking pixels** present across two separate systems:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/93de04a3...` — Coherent Path open pixel
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...o.gif` — MessageGears open pixel
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...` — secondary MessageGears open pixel
- **Duplicate open-tracking** (two ESP systems) is unusual and may double-count open rates in reporting dashboards.
- **Adobe Audience Manager pixel** present: `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` — third tracking domain in addition to the two ESPs.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **`<title>` is empty** — screen readers and tab previews get no label.
- **`user-scalable=0`** in the viewport meta tag prevents pinch-to-zoom, which is an accessibility failure (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **13 content/tracking images missing `alt` text** — QA flagged all of them. Tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `event`, `open.aspx`) should have `alt=""` (they have none); product images (`55869996_root2_2x`, `55405645_root_2x`) lack descriptive alt, breaking screen reader context.
- **Malformed HTML tag:** `</custom>` appears after the MessageGears open pixel `<img>` — not a standard closing tag, likely a templating artifact left unrendered.
- **`<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`** — empty `href` causes a 404 request to the root domain in some clients.
- **Google Fonts loaded three times** (two `<link>` tags + two `@import` rules for identical families) — redundant round-trips with no rendering benefit.
