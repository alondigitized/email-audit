# 20% off 🤗 New styles for less 🤩 Time to shop 🛍️
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-28**
## Executive summary

- Ninth straight day of "Friends & Family 20%" from Kohl's, and my thumb is basically on autopilot to archive this one before I even register the banner. But I'll give credit where it's due — for the first time this week the email actually leads with something I might use: "Denim Deal Days," jeans starting at $17.59, and real product photography of women, men, girls and boys instead of another kid-at-the-lockers back-to-school shot. That's a genuine step up from yesterday's send.
- Then it falls apart into the classic Kohl's kitchen-sink scroll — a slushy machine, bath towels, a mattress topper, and a Sephora 50%-off-beauty banner all crammed into one email with zero connective tissue. It's like they merged three different department memos into a single send. Compare that to Sephora's own "Clean at Sephora" email I looked at — at least Sephora stays in its lane even when it overwhelms me with product tiles. Kohl's is overwhelming me across five unrelated categories at once.
- Would I click? Maybe on the denim section, purely because the price and the "Starting at $17.59" callout is legible and specific. Would I forward it to a friend? No — nothing here feels personal or curated enough to share.

## What's working

- The "Denim Deal Days" hero has an actual price anchor ($17.59, reg. $29.99-$49.99) — first concrete number I've seen from Kohl's all week.
- Real lifestyle photography for women/men/girls/boys denim, not stock clip-art — easier to picture on myself.
- "Status: Kohl's Rewards Member · Earn 5% Rewards" sits right at the top, so at least they're acknowledging I'm a member before the pitch starts.
- The coupon code and PIN are visible near the bottom without me having to click through — small mercy for someone who just wants the number.

## What's weak

- The Friends & Family 20% banner is the ninth day in a row of identical creative — I could recite the "Ends Aug. 30" copy from memory at this point.
- After denim, it dumps into a Sephora beauty banner, then a slushy maker, bath towels, and a mattress topper — no thread ties these together, it just reads like inventory overflow.
- "Picked with you in mind (your great taste makes it easy)" sitting directly above a frozen drink machine and a mattress topper is almost insulting — nothing about that assortment says "picked for me."
- Fine print at the bottom flags "select brands" excluded from coupons and Sephora exemptions — that's the kind of buried caveat that makes the 20% feel less trustworthy the longer you look at it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this into two emails: one focused entirely on Denim Deal Days (it's strong enough to stand alone), and a separate "picked for you" send that's actually curated by purchase history, not a random four-SKU grab-bag.
- 2. Kill the "Picked with you in mind" line entirely unless the products are genuinely personalized — right now it reads as an empty template, and neither Nordstrom nor Saks would ever pair that language with a bath towel and a drink machine.
- 3. Retire the Friends & Family 20% banner for a few days — I've now seen it daily since at least Aug. 21. Even Macy's confirmation-triggered "LAST CHANCE 25%" email felt fresher than this by virtue of not repeating verbatim.
- 4. Move the Sephora+Kohl's 50%-beauty banner into its own dedicated send for beauty shoppers rather than sandwiching it mid-scroll between jeans and towels.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Standard ESP-templated promo email (Kohl's/Coherent Path platform) with 70 tracking links, six 1x1 tracking pixels, and no image alt text; delivery-header capture appears incomplete on the relay side.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 70 tracking/click-redirect links present, routed through `click.chp.kohls.com` and `click.s.kohls.com` — automated HTTP probing was skipped for these (expected behavior for redirect-domain links, not itself a defect).
- 6 tracking pixels detected: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/d17e69a1-...`, `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`, `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?KP363DUXKLFEPLP2BXCCQQJ4TE...`, `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe AAM), plus two `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` retargeting pixels — consistent with open/click tracking + Adobe Audience Manager sync, no anomalies found.
- `mi_ecmp=1020712_2026828` and `mi_u=604230016` params are consistent across pixels/links, indicating campaign ID tagging is intact.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- All 8 flagged images (1 click-tracker, 1 open pixel, 1 AAM pixel, 5 retargeting pixels) are missing `alt` text. These are 0/1px tracking assets, not content images, so this has no visible accessibility impact — low severity, but trivial to fix (`alt=""` with `aria-hidden="true"`, already applied to one pixel but not the others).
- Malformed markup: `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...KP363DUXKLFEPLP2BXCCQQJ4TE.60269&d=60269&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>` — stray closing `</custom>` tag with no matching opening tag. Not spec-valid HTML; most email clients will ignore it, but it should be removed.
- MSO/Outlook conditional comments, viewport meta, and responsive `@media` breakpoints at 500px are present and structurally standard.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, `[[...]]`) found in the visible source. `mi_mid`, `mi_cid`, `cp_cid`, `d_sid` are populated with resolved values, not raw tokens — no issues found.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)**: not detected, same caveat — one-click unsubscribe compliance unconfirmed.
- **Physical mailing address**: not detected in the visible/truncated HTML. This is a hard CAN-SPAM requirement; if it's genuinely absent (vs. just truncated out of this excerpt, likely in the footer), it's a compliance violation. Recommend checking the untruncated source before flagging as confirmed.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Links use proprietary Coherent Path tracking params (`mi_mid`, `cp_tp`, `mi_cid`, `cp_cid`) rather than standard UTM params (`utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign`) — this is redirect-based attribution, not a UTM scheme, so standard UTM continuity checks don't apply here.

7. Recommendations
