# Up to 50% off hair and brow must-haves? Yes, please 💗
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- My first reaction was "this is the second Sephora email today." The brow send this morning had one idea and one number. This one takes that offer and buries it inside a rewards bazaar, a points multiplier, a holiday teaser and a hair sale. I counted five or six different pitches stacked down a very long scroll.
- The top is fine. "National Brow Day" with "Up to 50% off select brow products" and a LIMITED TIME ONLY badge is easy to read. Then the "Rewards Bazaar" banner takes over, with pink starbursts and "NEW REWARDS ARE HERE!" repeated like a ticker. After that come the 100-point and 250-point tiles, then 2X and 5X points, then "Spot it. Shop it.", and finally the hair deals. The subject promised hair and brow, and the hair doesn't show up until I've scrolled past most of the email.
- I'd open it out of habit because I'm a Beauty Insider. I'd click the brow hero if I needed a pencil. I wouldn't forward it, and I'd probably read it later for the points. Neiman Marcus's calm "15% off fragrance, beauty & more" told me what I was getting in one glance. Sephora still has a brand I trust, but this email makes me do the work.

## What's working

- The brow hero is clear: "Up to 50% off select brow products," a LIMITED TIME ONLY badge and a SHOP NOW button, all above the fold.
- The reward tiles show the actual product, the point cost (100 or 250) and an "online only" tag. I can tell what my points buy without clicking.
- The red-and-gold "2X / 5X" band plus the "Ends 10/7" line gives me a deadline I believe. That's a better points message than Kohl's hollow red "EXTRA" header.
- The Insider / VIP / Rouge tier bar near the bottom reminds me where I stand, and the in-store barcode suits how I shop. I buy at the brand event, so a scannable code is useful.
- "TODAY ONLY" on the hair faves block is honest urgency, in the same way the 10/1 "STARTS TODAY" 2X send was.

## What's weak

- Too many offers compete here. Brow 50% off, three-for-100-points rewards, 2X/5X points, 20% off value-size hair care, up to 50% off hair faves and holiday drops are all in one send. Macy's loves this kind of overload, and Sephora normally does better.
- The subject says "hair and brow," but the hero is only brow. The hair offers sit near the bottom behind a points wall, so the subject and the layout don't match.
- The "NEW REWARDS ARE HERE!" strips repeat so often they read as noise. It feels like shouting, not respect.
- Nothing here is aimed at a 47-year-old. The reward tiles are mostly generic skincare, haircare and fragrance, with no model or voice that speaks to me. There are several tiny-print blocks and a wall of fine print, and I can't read them without zooming.
- It's the fourth Sephora email in four days (9/28 Living Proof, 10/1 2X points, today's brow send and now this). The second brow-50% mention in one day makes the cadence feel pushy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story. Lead with brow or hair, put one number in the hero, and move the Rewards Bazaar to its own send. Merging them is what makes this feel cluttered.
- 2. Match the subject to the hero. If the hair offer goes first, say so. If brow is first, drop "hair" from the subject.
- 3. Cut the repeated "NEW REWARDS ARE HERE!" strips to one, and collapse the 100-point and 250-point tiles into a single row of four.
- 4. Replace the generic SHOP NOW buttons with specific ones, such as "Shop 50% off brow" or "Redeem 100 points."
- 5. Don't send a second brow-50% email on the same day as the dedicated brow send. Segment so I get one or the other.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Brow Day: up to 50% off, today only 🤨`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your points just got more useful 💗`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Brow pencils, gels and more, plus hair favorites up to 50% off. Ends tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Three new 100-point rewards are waiting in your account.`
