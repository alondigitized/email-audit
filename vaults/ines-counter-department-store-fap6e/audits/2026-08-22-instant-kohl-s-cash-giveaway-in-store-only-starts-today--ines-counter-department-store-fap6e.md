- The Kohl's Cash giveaway banner is clear and matches the subject line — no disconnect between what got me to open and what I saw first.
- Fine print ("no purchase necessary, while supplies last") is visible right in the hero, not hidden — feels more honest than yesterday's Kohl's send.
- The "Picked with you in mind" module (blender, towels, pants, mattress topper) is a legitimate attempt at broader demographic relevance, unlike the kid-locker hero.
- Twice-a-year Sew-Annual bra/panty sale has real specificity — $19.99 bras, 5-for-$35 panties, dated through 8/23.

## What's weak

- Kid at lockers, again — this is at least the fifth Kohl's back-to-school hero in a row I've seen this week (8/19, 8/19, 8/20, 8/21, now 8/22). I have zero use for this category and it's the very first thing my eye lands on.
- No Sephora at Kohl's Beauty callout anywhere in this one — the 8/19 email at least gave me a lipstick shot to perk up for; this one has nothing beauty-adjacent until I scroll past a wall of shoes, activewear, and kids' basics.
- Visual hierarchy is a grid of near-identical pink percentage-off tiles (Shoes 40%, Activewear 40%, Basics 40%, Kitchen 35%, Home $3.99, Back to College 50%) — nothing pulls my eye as "made for you," it just reads as inventory dump.
- "Status: Kohl's Rewards Member" sits in 8pt gray text at the very top and never gets referenced again — Nordstrom or Saks would build a whole module around that; here it's an afterthought.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap the hero — lead with the Kohl's Cash giveaway art (which is actually novel) instead of a fifth consecutive back-to-school kid photo. The giveaway is the reason I opened; don't bury my reason to click below dead-air content.
- 2. Bring back a beauty/Sephora module — even one row, since that's consistently the section that gets me to scroll further, per my 8/19 and 8/19 reads.
- 3. Do something with the Rewards status line — a points balance, a tier nudge, anything — instead of leaving it as unused gray text at the top.
