# Instant Kohl's Cash ends tonight + score more DAILY DEALS 🙌
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-26**
## Executive summary

- Thirty-two. That's my June count for Kohl's, and I didn't need to do the math — the "ends tonight" in this subject line is the same phrase that showed up in the "WOW 🎉" send from earlier *today*. Two emails, same day. The Kohl's Cash deadline is real and I know that — I've redeemed it, I value it — but when you're the brand that's been in my inbox every single day since the first of the month, "INSTANT KOHL'S CASH ENDS TONIGHT" stops reading as urgency and starts reading as the boy who cried wolf.
- Structurally, this email is fine. Deal Days hero at the top, a cascade of 40% and 60% off tiles below it, a nine-day countdown so I understand the event isn't over. Clean render, no broken images. The problem isn't what's in the email — the problem is everything that arrived before it. I've been *inside* this deal event for so long that I've lost all sense of what "tonight" means. Sephora sent me one clean "last chance to save up to 50%" email on the same day that actually felt like a last chance, because it wasn't the thirty-second email from Sephora in June.
- I'm not clicking. I'm not forwarding. I'm hovering over "Manage Preferences" and wondering why I haven't done that already.

## What's working

- **Kohl's Cash is still the right headline hook** — a real loyalty mechanic, front and center, with a genuine tonight deadline. As someone who won't pull out a card without points back, this is the one thing that might crack me open on a good day.
- **The visual hierarchy is clean** — hero lands first, daily deals waterfall below it, nothing fighting for attention. Easy to scan even at Kohl's volume.
- **40% and 60% off are plainly stated** — real numbers, not buried "up to X%" teasers. Macy's sometimes hides the actual discount in footnote territory; Kohl's doesn't.
- **"9 Days of Deals" counter** — I actually appreciate knowing there's a defined end. A finish line is better than open-ended rolling promos.
- **No render bugs** — the email loaded clean. After some of the broken-image stretches I've seen from other mid-tier retailers, functional is still worth a point.

## What's weak

- **No beauty. Zero.** I am a beauty counter regular. Kohl's carries CeraVe, Bliss, ELT, e.l.f. — a real beauty aisle. Not one product. Sephora's 6/26 "last chance" email knew *exactly* who it was talking to. This one treated me like a generic household.
- **Two emails in one day breaks the social contract.** I already got a Kohl's email this morning. Sending a second one in the evening — even if it's a legitimate expiration trigger — compounds the cadence problem rather than solving it.
- **The urgency is inverted.** The hero says Kohl's Cash "ends tonight" while the nine-day counter below it says deals keep running. I'm now unsure what's urgent. Isolate the message or lose the message.
- **Every CTA says "Shop Now"** — not a single button tells me *what* I'm shopping for. Neiman Marcus, even on a mediocre "roadtrip style" send, at least segments the click by category. This just dumps me at the top of a category page.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Insert one beauty tile.** One CeraVe or Bliss deal in the first product row doubles my relevance without changing the template structure. If my purchase data is in their system, this should be automatic.
- 2. **Cap sends at three per event, max.** Open day, midpoint, final 24 hours. A Nordstrom sale event communicates that way. Thirty-two sends is not urgency; it's spam with good branding.
- 3. **Isolate the Kohl's Cash expiration.** If Cash ends tonight and deals continue for nine more days, those are two different stories. Send the expiration as a short, single-message alert — not buried inside a six-block daily deals template.
- 4. **Give at least one CTA a noun.** "Shop Beauty Deals" or "Shop Women's Sneakers" beats "Shop Now" every time.
- **Subject Alt A:** `$20 Kohl's Cash: tonight's your last shot`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tonight only — your Kohl's Cash expires at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `40–60% off on beauty, apparel, and home — deals run 9 more days`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Don't let your Cash earn window close without using it`

The stray `</custom>` closing tag is invalid HTML. Most clients will ignore it, but it may trip strict HTML validators or certain ESPs.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**11 images missing `alt` text** — all QA-flagged, including tracking pixels and what appear to be content images (`2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, etc. hosted on `mi.kohls.com`). Tracking pixels should use `alt=""` explicitly; content images require descriptive text for screen readers and image-off rendering.

`aria-hidden="true"` is correctly applied only to the MessageMedia pixel (`o.gif`). The other tracking pixels lack this attribute.

`<meta name="viewport" content="..., user-scalable=0">` — disabling user scaling is an accessibility anti-pattern (WCAG 1.4.4).

`<title></title>` is empty. Some screen readers announce the title on load; leaving it blank is a minor accessibility gap.

`<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` — suppresses referrer on all outbound clicks. This will strip `document.referrer` from landing pages, which may break any server-side attribution that relies on HTTP Referer rather than UTM params.

Google Fonts loaded three ways (two `<link>` tags + two `@import` rules for the same families) — redundant round-trips, minor render-delay risk in webmail clients that do honor external CSS.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge token literals (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIRST_NAME%%`) visible in the truncated source. The `mi_u=604230016` and `mi_ecmp=1016001_2026626` params on MessageMedia image URLs appear to be resolved subscriber and campaign IDs — no obviously raw/unfired tokens.

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header not detected** — QA relay did not capture it. If absent at the MTA level, Gmail and Apple Mail will not render the native unsubscribe button, and bulk sender compliance (Google/Yahoo 2024 requirements) is at risk.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not detected** — one-click unsubscribe unavailable; required for Gmail bulk senders sending >5k/day.

**No physical mailing address detected in source** — CAN-SPAM §7 requires a valid physical postal address in the message body. Not visible in the truncated HTML; if absent from the full render this is a legal violation.

**Demdex pixel fires unconditionally** — cross-site audience sync without explicit consent signal visible in source. Relevant under CCPA/CPRA for California recipients.
