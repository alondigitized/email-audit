# Labor Day Sale + $10 off your $25 purchase ENDS TONIGHT 🚨
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-07**
## Executive summary

- Well, here we are — Kohl's Labor Day email number seven this week, and my thumb genuinely twitched toward archive before I finished the scroll. This one's the "ENDS TODAY" panic-button version: same $10-off-$25, same $5 Kohl's Cash, but now it's stacked with literally every category Kohl's sells — tees, bedding, kids activewear, dresses, vacuums, bath towels, kitchenware, sneakers, fleece, a clearance banner, a Ninja blender, jeans, a mattress topper. It's not merchandising, it's a fire sale in email form.
- Compared to Sephora's stacking-weekend fatigue or Neiman's stuck "25% off" banner, this is worse — at least those brands pick one lane. Kohl's threw six departments and a barcode wall at me in a single scroll. Macy's "confirm now" triggered email at least had a coherent single ask; this has no ask at all beyond "buy something, anything, before midnight."
- Would I click? No. Would I forward it? Only to complain to a friend about inbox overload. This gets archived, unread past the hero.

## What's working

- The red "ENDS TODAY" ribbon at the top is at least honest urgency — I can see the deadline is real (Sept 7).
- $10 off $25 plus $5 Kohl's Cash per $25 is a genuinely good stackable offer if I were already planning to shop.
- The Gloria Vanderbilt jeans and Serta mattress topper "picked for you" block is the one moment that felt like Kohl's tried to personalize rather than blast.
- Barcodes are right there in the email — no digging for a code at checkout.

## What's weak

- Six departments crammed into one scroll (women's, kids, home, floor care, shoes, kitchen, clearance) — no hierarchy, no "this is for you" signal for a beauty-counter shopper like me.
- Zero beauty or fragrance mention anywhere — Kohl's Sephora shop-in-shop is completely absent, which is the one thing that would've made this relevant to me.
- This is my seventh near-identical Kohl's Labor Day send since Wednesday — I flagged the same $10/$25 + $5 Cash combo on 9/3 through 9/6, and it's just gotten louder, not smarter.
- "Coupons do not apply to select styles/brands" shows up three separate times — that's the exclusion fine print the rubric warns about, buried in plain sight rather than upfront.
- The layout is visually exhausting — pink block, cream block, pink block, repeat — nothing tells my eye where to actually look first.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment this — if Kohl's knows I've browsed beauty/Sephora at Kohl's, lead with that category instead of vacuums and mattress toppers. Sephora and Ulta both know to lead category-first; Kohl's doesn't.
- 2. Cut department count from six to two or three max. A department-store-overload email is exactly the "Macy's-style overload" trap — pick winners.
- 3. Stop resending the identical $10/$25 + $5 Cash mechanic seven days running. Vary the hook or at least escalate the number — right now it reads as the same email with a scarier subject line.
- 4. Move the exclusions ("coupons do not apply to select styles") into one clear line near the top offer, not scattered three times through the scroll.
- 5. Trim the clearance banner and barcode wall down — two barcodes stacked with fine print at the bottom reads more "terms and conditions" than "go shop."
