# The Designer Sale exclusive access: 40% off + new styles just added
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- I opened this expecting the 40% off, and it's right there, big and red. It also feels like a reprint of the one I got earlier today ("You get early access to The Designer Sale: up to 40% off"). That's the same sale, the same 40%, and now a different code (NMACCESS instead of NMSALE). Neiman's has sent me something nearly every day since Thursday, so I'm fatigued.
- The layout is clean and it doesn't shout. Black serif type on warm cream, one red number, one button. Compare it to Kohl's, which sent me five emails in three days stacking "extra 20%" on "$10 off $25+", and this looks like a quiet room. But "PRIVATE EVENT" over a code that's printed in the email itself is a bit cute. If anyone with the email can use the code, it isn't private. And the subject promised "new styles just added", yet I see no styles at all. There are no products, no faces and no clothes. That's the opposite of the Valentino and Tom Ford sends, where I at least got something pretty to look at.
- Would I click? Maybe, if I were already shopping the sale. Would I forward it? No. I'd mark it read and go look at the site myself.

## What's working

- The hierarchy is clean. "40% OFF" in red is the first thing my eye lands on, with no hunting.
- The code, NMACCESS, is printed in bold right under the offer. I don't have to open anything to find it.
- It's calm. There's no exclamation-point stacking and no countdown clock. Sephora's "SETH ROGEN DID YOUR HOLIDAY SHOPPING" was louder than this, and this feels more like a counter I'd trust.
- The nav (Women, Men, Shoes, Sale) is simple and the SHOP NOW button is clearly visible.
- The footer has a style advisor and app prompt, which is the service tone I expect from a department store.

## What's weak

- Not one product is shown. "New styles just added" in the subject is unsupported by anything in the body. Macy's at least gave me a clear, mechanical reason to act in its "confirm now" email.
- "Select designer styles" tells me nothing. Which designers? Which categories? I can't tell if my brands are in or out until I click, and "Exclusions Apply" sits there as a shrug.
- The real deadline (11/26 at 10:00 PM CT) is buried in the tiny print. The hero only says "Limited Time," so the urgency is vague.
- There's no sign of loyalty. Nothing about InCircle points, tier or member perks. I won't use a card that doesn't give points back, and Macy's always flags that it's a Star Money or points event.
- It's mostly empty cream space and a generic button. "Shop Now" doesn't tell me what I'm shopping.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put three to four actual products or designers in the hero, with beauty and fragrance represented too, and show the sale price. The subject promised "new styles," so show them.
- 2. Name the designers or categories included (and the big exclusions) above the fold instead of "Select."
- 3. Move the end date up: "Ends Nov 26" beside the code.
- 4. Add an InCircle line, such as "Earn points on sale styles," or drop the "Private" framing if the code is open to everyone.
- 5. Change the CTA to something specific, like "Shop designer sale styles."

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ends Nov 26. See what just landed from your favorite houses.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible source has one confirmed code-level defect: the ESP template's internal variable documentation ships in the production HTML. Everything else is either an accessibility issue or a QA warning that this capture can't settle. The HTML was truncated and 14 tracking links went unprobed, so footer, links and UTMs were not checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **14 tracking links not probed.** The QA run skipped them as click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and expiry are unverified.
- **Plain-text part is more than 70% URLs.** QA measured 2638 of 3579 characters as URLs. This is a spam-filter and readability risk, probably from long tracking URLs in the text alternative. Confirmed by QA.
- No broken links were confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Pinch-zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` prevents user scaling on mobile. This conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Weak `<title>`.** It reads `Neiman Marcus ` with a trailing space and is identical for every send. It gives no per-email context in clients that surface it.
- **Hamburger menu uses a checkbox hack.** The `.navbar-checkbox:checked~.menu` pattern is keyboard- and screen-reader-hostile. It is also unsupported in Gmail and several other clients. The truncated source doesn't show a fallback, so I can't confirm one exists.
- **Exact-match attribute selectors.** `*[class=contenttable]`, `*[class=mobile]` and similar only match when `class` is exactly that value. Adding a second class to any element silently disables its mobile rules.
- I couldn't verify `alt` attributes, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, or `lang`, because the body was cut off.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Unrendered template notes.** The leading comment contains the ESP variable definitions and the instruction `CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW`. It also contains `<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->`. This is a leftover from template authoring and was not stripped at send. It exposes internal data extension names (`NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`) and field names (`EMAIL_ID`, `uEm`, `cmdid`). The comment also sits before the `<!DOCTYPE>`, which can push some older clients into quirks mode.
- No unresolved `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` tokens were visible in the captured portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header missing** (QA WARN).
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header missing** (QA WARN), so RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is not evidenced. Gmail and Yahoo require both for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` header missing** (QA WARN). SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- **Possible capture artifact.** QA notes these headers may have been dropped by the AgentMail relay. Treat all three as unverified rather than confirmed failures, and recheck against the raw message at the receiving MTA.
- The sender is `NeimanMarcus@e.neimanmarcus.com`, a dedicated subdomain, which is good practice.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and postal address were not in the truncated source, so I couldn't check them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. The captured source has no hrefs, and the 14 tracking links were skipped, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment are unknown.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Strip the AMPscript documentation comment from the send-time output or move it out of the HTML. This is the only confirmed defect.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` on the raw message at the receiving MTA. Add them if they are truly absent.
4. Check SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `e.neimanmarcus.com` from a source other than the relay.
5. Shorten the URLs in the plain-text part, or switch to a more concise text alternative, to bring the URL ratio down.
6. Give the hamburger nav a non-checkbox fallback, and use `[class~=…]` selectors instead of exact `[class=…]` matches.
7. Re-run the audit on the full HTML and with tracking-link resolution enabled, so UTMs, footer compliance and `alt` text can be checked.
