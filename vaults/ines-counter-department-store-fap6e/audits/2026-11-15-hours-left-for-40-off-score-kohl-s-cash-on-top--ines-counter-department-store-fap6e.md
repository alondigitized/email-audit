# HOURS LEFT for 40% off ⏰ Score Kohl's Cash on top 💚
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again, and I'm worn out. This is the fifth or sixth version of the same weekend since 11/10. This morning I got "Open for 40% off ✨," and earlier today the "LAST DAY to stack" send. Now it's the "hours left" send. The hero is at least honest: "Ends Today, you get an extra 40% off with coupon," with the Kohl's Cash badge next to it. After that the email just keeps going. I scrolled past stockings, a toy ad, a Klarna block, a barcode and a wall of fine print.
- I shop at the beauty counter and I want to be treated like a known customer. This one treats me like a crowd. Nothing in it is about me. There's no beauty, no Sephora at Kohl's, no tier or points hook beyond the generic Rewards line. I'd glance, take note of the 40%, and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it. Nobody gets forwarded the fourth reminder of a sale.

## What's working

- The hero is clear: "Ends Today," 40% off, with Kohl's Cash tucked beside it. The deadline is real and I can see it.
- The Rewards-member callouts ($10 off $50 home, $10 off $50 toys) tell me what my membership is worth.
- The barcode and coupon codes sit at the bottom, where I can find them at the register. I like that, because I buy at the event.
- The "Ends Nov. 15" repeats in several places, so I can't miss the deadline.

## What's weak

- It's a catalog. I count at least eight blocks: holiday picks, a 30% off tile, gift shop, toys, deals preview, Klarna, pickup, products. Macy's would shout like this too, but Macy's at least stuck to one confirm-now ask in its "LAST CHANCE" email.
- Nothing is about beauty or my category. The products are a slushie machine, a towel, a mattress topper and men's jeans. There's no designer access and no beauty event signal.
- The red nav pills (Home, Women, Men, Kids) take up room at the top, and the real offer gets pushed down.
- The fine print at the bottom is dense and tiny. "Coupons do not apply to select brands" is the line I'd want to read first. It's buried.
- The cadence is exhausting. This is the same weekend I've been getting since 11/10, and the "hours left" urgency feels manufactured by now.

## Recommendations

- 1. Send one email per promo window, not five. Put a clear last-call note on the final one and cut everything else.
- 2. Cut the body to a hero and two or three category tiles. Add a beauty tile for people like me.
- 3. Move the "brands excluded" note up beside the 40% offer. Neiman Marcus's Alice + Olivia email today was quiet and let one photo do the work, and I trusted it more for that. Sephora sends too often, but its hero opens clean and gets to the offer fast.
- 4. Show Kohl's Cash as one line: how much I earn and when I can spend it.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 40% off, Rewards members`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Coupon ends at midnight. Earn $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your Rewards coupon and barcode are inside. Excludes select brands.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Kohl's, "HOURS LEFT for 40% off ⏰ Score Kohl's Cash on top 💚"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a 640px table layout with `lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` on the main tables, and a mobile breakpoint at 500px. It has several confirmed code defects: missing alt text on content images, invalid markup in `<head>` and a stray `</custom>` tag, and zoom disabled in the viewport meta. Header-based compliance checks and link health could not be verified from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverifiable links:** 75 links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…`, `click.chp.kohls.com`). Destination health, redirect chains and 404s are unconfirmed.
- **Four overlapping open and analytics beacons:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/67fc61f8-…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`
  - This adds requests and creates inconsistent open-rate counts across systems.
- **Possible empty dynamic parameter:** the image `916d6fa08d185233.png` carries `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_p…`. The Kohl's Cash redemption end date is empty in the URL. If it feeds a dynamic image, the date may render blank or fall back to a default. The source is truncated, so this is unconfirmed.
- **Stray closing tag:** `<img src="…open.aspx?…" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>` has no matching `<custom>` opener. This is invalid markup and may cause parser quirks in some clients.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA, 10 or more images; the list is truncated):**
  - Content images: `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png` and `916d6fa08d185233.png`.
  - These are dynamic Movable Ink images that likely carry offer text. With images off or in a screen reader, the offer content is lost.
  - The 1×1 tracking pixels (`click.chp…`, `kohls.demdex.net/event`) also lack alt. These are low impact, but `alt=""` is the correct fix.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Empty `<title></title>`:** this gives no accessible name for the web-view version.
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. A `div` is not valid there, and parsers will close the head early. Gmail and Apple Mail ignore the schema markup anyway.
- **Empty favicon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` can trigger a self-referencing request.
- **Broken CSS rule:** `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` is missing the leading `.`, so it targets a nonexistent element. `max-height: auto` is also an invalid value.
- **Redundant font loading:** Google Fonts are loaded twice, by `<link>` and by `@import`. Many clients ignore web fonts, but the `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` fallback is present.
- **Layout tables without `role="presentation"`:** the 1×1 pixel table, the AAM table and the preheader table (`width='640'`) lack it.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) are visible in the truncated source. The per-recipient identifier `mi_u=604230016` appears in the image URLs, and `mi_ecmp=1025937_20261115` is populated. The rest of the body could not be checked. Beyond the empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte` above, no personalization issues were found.

## 5. Compliance
- **Headers:** `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results` were not found. The QA notes say the AgentMail relay may strip them. This is a capture limitation, not a confirmed sender defect, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC and one-click unsubscribe remain unverified.
- **Footer:** the in-body unsubscribe link and physical address are not in the truncated source. They could not be confirmed.
- **Sender:** `Kohls@s.kohls.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain, which is consistent.
- **Referrer policy:** `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` has no effect in most mail clients.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
All visible links are opaque redirects (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…`), so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment cannot be verified. The `email_name=261115_DG_Email_Myster…` campaign identifier is embedded in the image URLs. That suggests tracking is handled by the redirect layer rather than visible UTMs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` content images, with offer text in the alt. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
2. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Fix the markup:
   - Remove the stray `</custom>`.
   - Move or remove the `<div itemscope>` in `<head>`.
   - Remove `<link href="">`.
   - Set a real `<title>`.
   - Change `ReadMsgBody` to `.ReadMsgBody`.
4. Consolidate the four open-tracking pixels and keep only the ones that are needed.
5. Check the Movable Ink fallback when `mi_kc_redm_end_dte` is empty.
6. Re-run the audit with raw headers, the full HTML and resolved redirects. That would confirm authentication, one-click unsubscribe, the footer address and UTM continuity.
