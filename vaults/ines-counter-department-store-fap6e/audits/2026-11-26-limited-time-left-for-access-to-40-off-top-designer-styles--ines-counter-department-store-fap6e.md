# Limited time left for access to 40% off top designer styles
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- Honestly, this is the cleanest Neiman's email I've opened all week. It's cream background, serif type, one red "40% OFF", and one black button. After the black box with the red border three times since yesterday, including this morning's stacked "$1,500 off + extra 25% + Designer Sale" send, I could exhale. It looks like a store event invitation, not a flyer.
- But "Private Event" with "Limited time left" in the subject is a stretch. A promo code anyone can read off the screen isn't private, and I've been getting Designer Sale emails for days. Then the real terms are in 8pt type at the bottom: online only, regular-price only, no stacking, and it ends 12/24. The subject says "limited time" and the date is a month out. I don't buy the urgency.
- I'd click, because I'd want to see which designers are in. I wouldn't forward it. There's nothing to share except a code, and the email gives me no brand names.

## What's working

- The hierarchy is clear. "40% OFF" in red is the first thing I see, and the black SHOP NOW button is hard to miss.
- It doesn't shout. Compared with Kohl's Doorbusters strip or Sephora's sixteenth email in two weeks, this has real breathing room.
- The code NMACCESS is printed right above the button, so I don't have to hunt for it.
- The nav (Women, Men, Shoes, Sale) and the style advisor link feel like the department-store service I expect.
- The fine print is complete and readable if I zoom. It names the end date and the no-stacking rule.

## What's weak

- There's no product and no designer name anywhere. "Select designer styles" tells me nothing. Saint Laurent on the 25th at least showed me a handbag.
- "Private Event" and "limited time" aren't credible. The deadline of 12/24 is only in the fine print, so the email never says when it ends.
- It's online only, and the email doesn't say so until the footer. I buy and return at the counter, so I notice that.
- There's no loyalty or InCircle mention at all. A private event should reward members. Macy's at least makes me feel like a member with its confirm-your-25% email.
- The big empty beige panel below the button wastes the space where designer names could go.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put three or four named designers or product tiles under the button. Without them I have no reason to click.
- 2. Print the end date in the hero, such as "Ends 12/24", and make "online only" visible there too.
- 3. Make "private" true by tying it to InCircle members, or drop the word.
- 4. Cut sends. This is the fourth Neiman's email since yesterday, and the offers contradict each other ($1,500 off, then 25%, now 40%).

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML source has several confirmed problems: an unstripped ExactTarget/SFMC template comment leaks internal data-extension names, the viewport meta blocks zoom, and `<html>` has no `lang`. The automated QA found no hard failures. Its 4 warnings are mostly unverifiable because of the AgentMail relay. The source is truncated, so body-level links, alt text and UTMs could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **14 tracking links not probed.** QA skipped the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains, so their destinations and status codes are unverified.
- **Plain text is 2614/3573 chars URLs (~73%).** The long tracking URLs dominate the text part. That raises spam-filter risk and makes the plain-text alternative close to unreadable.
- **No other link defects confirmed.** The truncated source shows no `href` values.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom is blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4. The commented-out `user-scalable=no` variants are inert.
- **No `lang` attribute.** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang`/`xml:lang`, so screen readers can't pick the right language.
- **Content before the DOCTYPE.** A large HTML comment precedes `<!DOCTYPE ...>`. Some clients, and older IE/Outlook engines, can drop into quirks mode when anything precedes the doctype.
- **Weak `<title>`.** It is `Neiman Marcus ` (with a trailing space) and doesn't match the subject line.
- **Fragile mobile selectors.** The exact-match selectors `*[class=contenttable]`, `*[class=mobile]` and `*[class=hide]` fail if an element carries more than one class.
- **Hamburger menu depends on the checkbox hack.** It uses `.navbar-checkbox:checked~...` and `.mobileCont:checked`. This is unsupported in Gmail and Outlook, so the menu likely has no mobile fallback. I couldn't confirm that from the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Template scaffolding left in the delivered HTML.** The first comment contains `REQUIRED variables: @email ... @base_refid ... @refid = CONCAT(@base_refid,"") ... CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW --> <!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->`.
- **Internal names exposed.** The comment reveals the data-extension names `NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated` and `NM_CRM_Feed`, the fields `EMAIL_ID`, `uEm`, `cmdid`, and the `EMAIL_ADDRESS` lookup key, plus the hashed-email (SHA-256) identity scheme. This is an information leak and a template-QA failure.
- **Unmatched parenthesis.** The lookup syntax in that comment has an extra `))`.
- **No unresolved visible tokens confirmed.** The truncated source shows no `%%...%%` or `@var` text in rendered content.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** (QA WARN).
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found** (QA WARN). Without it there is no RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **Both warnings may be relay artifacts.** QA itself notes the header "may not be captured by AgentMail relay", so I can't confirm the sender omitted them.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The `From` domain is `e.neimanmarcus.com`, and alignment can't be verified from this data.
- **Footer link and postal address not verifiable.** The footer is past the truncation point.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not verifiable.** The 14 tracking links were not resolved, and no destination URLs or UTM parameters appear in the truncated source.
- **Template variables suggest ID-based tracking.** `@refid`/`@base_refid` imply a `refid`-style parameter, but the final URL structure is unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Strip the pre-DOCTYPE comment** (the `REQUIRED variables` block and the `SET @user` line) from the production render. Move it into SFMC-only `%%[ ]%%` blocks or delete it.
2. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta, and set `<html lang="en" xml:lang="en">`.
3. **Replace the exact-match `*[class=x]` selectors** with `.x` or `[class~=x]`, and add a non-checkbox fallback for the nav.
4. **Rewrite `<title>`** to match the subject line, without the trailing space.
5. **Re-test the headers on a direct-to-inbox seed**, bypassing the AgentMail relay. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, plus SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `e.neimanmarcus.com`.
6. **Shorten the plain-text version** by using short tracking URLs or dropping the link-only lines, to bring URL share well under 70%.
7. **Re-run the audit on the untruncated HTML**, following redirects on the 14 tracking links, to validate destinations, UTMs and image alt text.
