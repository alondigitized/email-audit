# Insider’s look: 1,000+ new styles
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, editorial "just landed" grid, which I actually don't hate — it's a relief after the gift-card and tiered-discount barrage Neiman's has been running all week. But there's zero price, zero personal-shopper note, and zero reason given for why *I*, specifically, should care about a Veronica Beard skirt versus a Jacquemus top. It's a catalog page, not a message from a brand that knows I'm a beauty-counter regular who shops the event and expects to be treated like a returning customer.
- The product mix skews younger and trendier than my closet — Casablanca swim trunks, a Louboutin sunglasses cameo, lots of cropped and slouchy silhouettes. Nice styling, but nothing here says "we know what you buy." No loyalty tier mention (InCircle is completely absent), no beauty crossover at all despite this brand doing beauty counters in-store.
- I'd skim it in about four seconds, maybe click one product tile out of curiosity on the Oscar de la Renta dress, and archive. Not forwarding to a friend — there's no single hero moment worth sharing, just a wall of merchandise.

## What's working

- The all-black "BRAND NEW STYLES JUST LANDED" hero is genuinely elegant and on-brand — feels like Neiman's, not Kohl's.
- Clean product grid with real photography, no broken images, no lorem ipsum — professionally executed.
- Separate "Shop Women's New Arrivals" and "Shop Men's New Arrivals" CTAs make navigation obvious.
- Designer names are prominent under each product (Gucci, Valentino Garavani, Oscar de la Renta) — that's the label-recognition cue that gets me to look twice.

## What's weak

- Not one price anywhere. I'm not going to click ten thumbnails just to find out what a Valentino Garavani bag costs — Saks does this same thing sometimes and it's just as annoying there.
- No InCircle points messaging, no event tie-in, no personal-shopper CTA up top even though "Match with a personal style advisor" is buried way down in the footer.
- Nothing here reads as curated *for me* — compare this to Neiman's own 2026-08-25 "Pre-order new designer arrivals now!" send, which at least had an editorial photo and a "BE FIRST" narrative. This is just a spreadsheet of SKUs with nicer formatting.
- Beauty is completely absent, and I'm a counter regular — no lipstick, no skincare, nothing that acknowledges that side of my spending with this brand.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add prices or "starting at" under each item — I shouldn't have to click through to gauge whether something's in my budget.
- 2. Move "Match with a personal style advisor" up near the hero — that's the one thing that actually differentiates Neiman's from Macy's and it's hidden in the footer.
- 3. Work in a beauty or accessories module — even three tiles would signal this isn't just apparel.
- 4. Reference InCircle status/points somewhere — Sephora leads with its loyalty badge ("Clean at Sephora") every send; Neiman's has an even stronger loyalty story and doesn't use it here at all.
