# Get 30% off ALL Sephora Collection 😍
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- This is the nineteenth Sephora email in about a week, and it is the same 30% Sephora Collection event as yesterday's "STARTS TODAY" send. The top banner is the only part that matches the subject: "Sephora Savings Event," a big "30% OFF," code ALLONSALE, 10/30–11/9. Then the email changes its mind. The giant serif "Beauty for You" hero sells Eadem and Yise moisturizers, not the Sephora Collection deal in the subject. I opened it for the discount and landed on a different email.
- The product grid is good. Marc Jacobs, Rare Beauty, Clarins, Maison Margiela and Rejuran are brands I actually shop at the counter. But nothing in the grid ties back to the 30%. It reads as two emails stacked together, and I couldn't tell which one I was meant to act on. I'd skim it and click nothing. I wouldn't forward it, and I'd probably archive it.

## What's working

- The black-and-red banner with "30% OFF" and the code is clear. The dates and the code are right there.
- "We Think You'll Love These" mixes prestige names I know (Clarins, Marc Jacobs, Maison Margiela) with newer ones. The prices are shown, which I like.
- The "Only at Sephora" tags give me a real reason to shop here and not at Nordstrom.
- The barcode at the bottom covers in-store use. That matters to me because I buy at the counter and return at the counter. It also covers the Kohl's locations.
- The product photography is clean and the type is readable on a phone.

## What's weak

- The "Beauty for YOU" hero takes up the first screen and has nothing to do with the subject. The Sephora Collection deal gets about an inch.
- The subject says "ALL Sephora Collection," but not one Sephora Collection product appears anywhere below the banner. It's bait-and-switch.
- It feels like a stranger's email. No tier, no points, and no Beauty Insider status that I can see, only a small "Rosie" in the corner. I want to know what I earn.
- The lower half piles on K18 gift sets, Valentino, a holiday gift guide, a credit card pitch and an app download. That is Macy's-style overload.
- The fine print is a tiny gray block that I can't read without zooming. The offer has exclusions, and they're buried.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero the offer. Show real Sephora Collection products with prices and one "Shop 30% off" button.
- 2. Add a member line near the top, such as "Insiders: your points still count on sale items."
- 3. Cut the lower modules to two. Neiman Marcus sent one Veronica Beard hero and nothing else, and I appreciated it. Kohl's at least puts its Rewards bar at the top, which Sephora skips.
- 4. State the exclusions in plain words near the code, not in the gray footer.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Rosie, your Sephora Collection 30% is waiting`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code ALLONSALE. Brushes, palettes and skincare included.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Beauty Insider points still count. Ends 11/9.`

## Technical Audit: "Get 30% off ALL Sephora Collection 😍"

  - The `mi_u` value looks like a per-recipient identifier.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** Both tracking pixels lack `alt`. The QA tool flagged them, but they are hidden `display:none` images, so the real accessibility impact is negligible. Adding `alt=""` would still clear the warning.
- **Zoom restriction:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on clients that honor it. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Malformed CSS:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {...} <![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` block in `<style>`. Conditional comments don't work inside CSS rules. The block is ignored at best and may break the following `.mobileHeaderTierRouge` rule in some parsers.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. This has no practical effect in email.
- **Fixed width:** The wrapper has `min-width:700px; width:700px`. The `@media (max-width:480px)` rules adjust fonts and logo size only, so the layout is not fluid on narrow clients without scaling.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` is not descriptive.
- **Dead JSON-LD:** The block has `"discountCode": ""` and an empty `PromotionCard`. Most clients strip `<script>`, so it is dead markup.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
