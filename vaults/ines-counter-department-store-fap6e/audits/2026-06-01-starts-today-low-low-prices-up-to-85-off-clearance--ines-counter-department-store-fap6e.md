# STARTS TODAY: Low, low prices & up to 85% off clearance 🙌
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-01**
## Executive summary

- Fifteen. That's where the Kohl's counter sits in my inbox this morning, and this one is pivoting hard — the Memorial Day drum has finally stopped and we've moved to "lowest prices of the season" territory. I'll give them the subject line: "85% off clearance" actually made me look up from my coffee, which almost none of the last four emails managed to do. But then I opened it and it's pure Kohl's: eight separate discount blocks stacked like a Sunday circular, starting with men's shorts and ending — finally, mercifully — with a small beauty sliver near the bottom, just above the Pride Month banner.
- The Kohl's Cash mention at the footer is the one thing that speaks my language. I don't open Kohl's emails for the clothes; I open them for the loyalty math. But $10 Kohl's Cash is buried in the footer when it should be the headline. Compare that to Macy's, which at least tried to put the loyalty confirmation front-and-center on their last send, even if the execution wobbled. Neiman Marcus sent me one hero image — a single Saint Laurent bag — and I felt like a customer. This feels like being handed a stack of coupons at the door.
- Would I click? Maybe on the beauty section if I happen to scroll that far on my phone, which is 50/50 at best after seven other sections compete for my attention. Would I forward it to a friend? No. Mark-as-read, folder, done.

## What's working

- **"85% off clearance" is the sharpest copy Kohl's has written in two weeks** — a specific number cuts inbox noise better than the Memorial Day vagueness that lingered through May 25.
- **The hero clearance badge lands first** — the red "CLEARANCE 85%" badge has enough visual weight that even a two-second skim registers the number.
- **Kohl's Cash is visible** — $10 Kohl's Cash at the footer is clearly stated and it's the signal that makes a Kohl's trip worth doing.
- **Pride Month placement feels earned** — June 1 is exactly the right moment, the rainbow branding isn't tacked-on, and it adds warmth to what is otherwise a pure price-blast.
- **No render bugs** — all images loaded, text is legible on mobile, layout holds together cleanly.

## What's weak

- **Beauty is section eight** — I am a beauty-counter regular and I scrolled through men's shorts, women's casualwear, couples, dinnerware, patio lounge chairs, and lingerie before I found the one block that's relevant to me. That's a structural insult.
- **Eight discount blocks with identical "Shop Now" buttons erases hierarchy** — by section four I've tuned out. Sephora sent me a single "ENDS TODAY" beauty offer and I knew exactly what to do with it. This is the opposite of that.
- **Kohl's Cash is footer content, not headline content** — the one loyalty hook that would get me to open my wallet is the last thing I see.
- **"STARTS TODAY" in all-caps is a mild spam signal** — not egregious, but it edges toward the promotional tab where fourteen prior Kohl's emails are already living.
- **No end date on any offer** — "starts today" without a close date drains the urgency entirely.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the loyalty math.** Reframe the hero: "$10 Kohl's Cash + 85% off clearance — starts today" would earn a hero CTA click without requiring any scrolling at all.
- 2. **Collapse eight blocks to three.** Women's, Beauty, Home. Cut men's from this send segment entirely. Beauty should be block two, not block seven.
- 3. **Give the beauty section a dedicated, named CTA** — "Up to 50% off beauty" as a headline block, not a buried footnote.
- 4. **Add an end date.** "Through June 4" or "this week only" — anything that closes the urgency loop.
- 5. **Lowercase the subject opener.** "Starts today:" reads just as urgent and doesn't trigger ALL CAPS promo-tab signals.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 85% off clearance — starts today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Earn $10 Kohl's Cash + 85% off clearance starts now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Beauty, home & women's — best prices of the season, through June 4`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your $10 Kohl's Cash is waiting. Shop the clearance drop.`

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "Lowest Prices of the Season" (2026-06-01)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is a standard ESP-sent promotional HTML using HTML 4.01 Transitional with legacy MSO/VML compatibility layers. The primary infrastructure concerns are missing compliance signals (List-Unsubscribe headers, physical address) and pervasive missing alt text on tracking pixels and content images.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **22 tracking/redirect links skipped** by HTTP probe — all route through `click.chp.kohls.com` and `click.s.kohls.com`. Destination URL health is unverified.
- **Three separate tracking pixel systems** are active simultaneously:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` — CoherentPath click tracker
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...` — MessageGears open pixel
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...` — a second open pixel (Salesforce Marketing Cloud signature)
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?...` — Adobe Audience Manager sync pixel
- **Malformed HTML tag** at the open pixel block: `</custom>` is a non-standard closing tag with no matching open tag — likely a template artifact left in production HTML.

```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?PLW4PD5SBD3UPOXAHS7R7CNRKU..." ...></custom>
```

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **11 images missing `alt` text** confirmed by QA, spanning both tracking pixels and content images (product images via `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/...`). Tracking pixels should use `alt=""` explicitly; content images require descriptive alt text for screen readers and image-off rendering.
- **`<title>` element is empty** — screen readers announce the email window as untitled; some clients surface this in the tab or preview.
- **`user-scalable=0`** in the viewport meta tag prevents zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 2.1 AA failure (SC 1.4.4).
- **Google Fonts loaded via both `<link>` and `@import`** in the same `<head>` — this is redundant and doubles the font request count on clients that support external CSS.

```html
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Roboto&display=swap" rel="stylesheet">
<!-- AND separately: -->
<style>@import url("https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Roboto&display=swap");</style>
```

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens (`{{`, `%%`, `[%`) visible in the truncated source. The `mi_u=604230016` subscriber ID parameter appears consistently across all MessageGears pixel URLs, indicating merge token substitution completed correctly.

---

### 5. Compliance

- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not detected** via AgentMail relay — header may be stripped or absent. Gmail and Apple Mail require this for bulk sender compliance; Google's February 2024 mandate requires one-click unsubscribe for senders >5k/day.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent** — RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe not confirmed. This is a hard requirement under Google/Yahoo 2024 bulk sender rules.
- **No physical mailing address detected** in the HTML body (truncated source may contain it further down, but QA scanner did not find one). CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid physical postal address.
- **`<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow" />`** — correct for email, prevents indexing if HTML is ever web-viewable.
- **`<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer" />`** — suppresses referrer on outbound clicks. This will strip UTM parameters from referrer headers at the destination, which has downstream analytics implications (see §6).

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

- The `no-referrer` meta policy means destination servers cannot read the HTTP `Referer` header — UTM attribution must be carried entirely in the click URL query string. Whether UTM params are appended to destination URLs is unverifiable since all 22 click links were skipped by the HTTP probe.
- The `cp_tp=v3.eJx9j7FL...` parameter on `click.chp.kohls.com` links is a base64/JWT-encoded payload — this is CoherentPath's personalization token, not a standard UTM param. If GA4/Adobe Analytics is the attribution system, verify that the landing page correctly maps CoherentPath click tokens to sessions.
- Email name tag `email_name=260601_DG_Email_Monday` appears in MessageGears pixel URLs — confirms send date/segment tagging is active for internal reporting.
