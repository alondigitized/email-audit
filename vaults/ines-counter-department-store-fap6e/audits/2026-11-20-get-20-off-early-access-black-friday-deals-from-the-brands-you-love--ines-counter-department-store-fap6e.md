# Get 20% off | Early Access Black Friday Deals from the brands you love 😍
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again. This is the fifth or sixth send since the weekend. I remember the 11/18 "Get 20% off | Perfect time to find new favorites" and the 11/19 "Early Access Black Friday Deals & up to 50% off", and this one is nearly the same email in a new coat. "Early Access" in the subject sounds like an event, but I opened it and found the same long scroll of tiles.
- At the top, "Take an extra 20% off with coupon" with a Shop Now button is clear. Below it is a stack of Tonies, Beats, a Shark face mask, dishes, heaters, gift-price tiles, Levi's and Clearance, all given similar weight. Neiman Marcus's quiet Gorski send this week respected my time. Sephora at least gave me one idea, even if it wasn't the Dyson. This email gives me twenty ideas and no point of view.
- Would I click? Maybe the Shark CryoGlow, since it's the one thing near my beauty world. I wouldn't forward it. I'd skim it and mark it read. One note: I viewed this as a very compressed, tall image, so I can't vouch for the fine print.

## What's working

- The top banner, "Take an extra 20% off with coupon," is the offer, and I see it before anything else.
- Prices are shown on the tiles, such as 99.99 and 299.99. That's honest and easy to scan.
- The Kohl's Rewards callouts ("$10 off your home purchase of $50") acknowledge that I'm a member. That matters to me, because I won't use a card without points back.
- The "Gifts $25 & Under / $50 & Under" tiles are a sensible, budget-respecting way to do holiday shopping.
- Clean category nav (Home / Women / Men / Kids) sits at the top and again lower down.

## What's weak

- The email is endless. There are dozens of tiles and no hierarchy after the first banner. This is the Macy's-style overload I avoid.
- Almost nothing is for me. There's one face device and otherwise vacuums, heaters, toys and Tonies. There's no beauty event and no designer access.
- The repeated "Coupons do not apply" and "Select styles" lines undercut the 20% headline. The offer feels like it has an asterisk on every tile.
- "Shop Now" on every tile is generic, and the buttons look small for a phone tap.
- The Black Friday "Preview the Deals" block, the Klarna/DoorDash/Instacart/Uber Eats strip, and a barcode coupon make the bottom feel like a flyer. It's a lot after a long scroll.
- It's the same promo cadence as the 11/18 and 11/19 sends, so "Early Access" doesn't feel exclusive.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one hero, not twenty. Pick one hero product or category and one deadline, and cut the body to four to six tiles. Neiman's Gorski email shows how much one image can do.
- 2. Segment by interest. Send me a beauty or skincare cut (Shark CryoGlow, fragrance, skincare) instead of heaters and toys.
- 3. State the coupon mechanics once, up top, with the end date. Don't repeat exclusions on every tile.
- 4. Give tier or member value its own prominent block. Show my Kohl's Rewards status and Kohl's Cash earned, the way Sephora Insider speaks to its members.
- 5. Use specific button copy, such as "Get the Shark mask at 299.99".
- 6. Cap the cadence. Fewer sends with real differences would make "Early Access" believable.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shark, Levi's and gifts under $25, plus $10 off $50 for Rewards members.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Use your coupon before the doorbusters start.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based with a mobile media query and a Roboto/Montserrat fallback stack. The source has several markup defects, 4 stacked tracking pixels, missing alt text on content images, and an accessibility-hostile viewport meta. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the header data wasn't captured, and the HTML was truncated before the footer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **82 tracking links were not probed.** They are on `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…` and similar redirect domains, so none of the destinations were validated.
- **Four separate open or analytics beacons fire on load:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/1a099729-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1026205_20261119`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`
  
  This is redundant, and each beacon is another third-party request that can fail or be blocked.
- **Malformed tag:** the `open.aspx` pixel is followed by a stray `</custom>` (`…&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`). It is invalid HTML with no matching opening tag.
- **Per-recipient identifiers in image URLs:** `mi_u=604230016` appears in the query string of content images (`mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png`), so every image load identifies the recipient.
- **Inconsistent campaign parameters on images:** `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png` and others carry `email_name=261119_DG_Email_Thu_PM`. `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png` and the visible `916d6fa08d185233.png` URL do not show it.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text:**
  - The QA scan flags 14 images with missing alt, and the list appears truncated. Content images are affected: `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/{2a5adc5afb647aad,f441d45d2128ae53,c2f81e9ac8daee44,415c8a18fc2950ea,69494b48a79b4240,78308d1d9adb0751,916d6fa08d185233}.png`, `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/60699446_4_2x` and `…60699446_3_2x`.
  - With images blocked, these render as empty gaps.
  - Two tracking pixels also have no alt: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/…` and `kohls.demdex.net/event`.
  - The `o.gif` pixel is a QA false positive: the source shows `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, which is valid for a decorative image.
- **Viewport meta:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` disables pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 on mobile clients that honor it.
- **Empty `<title></title>`** and **empty `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`**.
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will relocate it, and Gmail ignores it.
- **Layout tables:** the outer layout tables use `role="presentation"`, which is correct. The first tracking table (`width="1" height="1"`) does not.
- **Duplicate meta:** two `format-detection` meta tags (`telephone=no`, `date=no`). Some clients honor only one of them.
- **Web fonts:** both `@import` and `<link>` load Google Fonts. Many clients strip these, so Montserrat falls back to Arial.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source: no unrendered `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]` tokens, and none in the subject line. The HTML was truncated, so the body below the preheader was not checked.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe is not detected.** The QA tool notes this may be a relay-capture limitation, so it is unconfirmed rather than proven missing.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) is not detected.** The same caveat applies. If it is truly absent, that conflicts with Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **Physical address is not detected.** The HTML was truncated, so the footer wasn't inspected. This needs a check against the full source.
- **SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.** `Authentication-Results` was not captured. The From domain `s.kohls.com` is a subdomain of `kohls.com`, which suggests alignment is plausible, but that is unverified.
- **Plain-text part:** URLs make up 14,574 of 20,362 characters (about 72%). That tips the text/HTML balance toward spam filtering and reflects the long `qs=` redirect URLs.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- No `utm_*` parameters are visible. Attribution runs through `qs=` blobs on `click.s.kohls.com` and `mi_*` parameters (`mi_ecmp=1026205_20261119`, `email_name=261119_DG_Email_Thu_PM`).
- Final landing URLs can't be verified because the redirects are opaque and weren't probed.
- No broken or mismatched destinations are confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, using `alt=""` for decorative ones. Add `alt=""` to the `click.chp.kohls.com` and `demdex` pixels.
2. Remove the stray `</custom>`, move the `schema.org` div out of `<head>`, and give the page a non-empty `<title>`. Remove or fill the empty `rel="shortcut icon"` link.
3. Drop `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Consolidate the four tracking beacons to one or two.
5. Re-run QA against the raw `.eml` to confirm the List-Unsubscribe headers (including `-Post`), the `Authentication-Results` verdicts, and the footer address.
6. Resolve a sample of the `qs=` redirects (with HEAD or GET follow) to verify the landing pages and look for `utm_*` or equivalent parameters.
7. Standardize `email_name` and the other campaign parameters across all `mi.kohls.com` image URLs.
8. Shorten tracked URLs or add a leaner plain-text part to bring the URL ratio below 70%.
