# 4X points on all hair care ‼️ and styling tools ‼️
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-19**
## Executive summary

- Four-X points on hair care — all right, Sephora, you got me to look. Points are my actual religion at the beauty counter, and a 4X multiplier on a category where I'll easily spend $150 on Olaplex and a Dyson attachment is the right kind of direct. The badge is the first thing I see, the code and deadline are right below it, and "BE YOUR OWN STYLIST" is at least speaking to an adult.
- Then I scroll half an inch and see "Trending looks from Herb Beauty, Britta Byrum, and TikTok Creator." I am 47. TikTok Creator is not a byline that closes me. The email immediately bifurcates into two different audiences — a loyalty send for me, and an influencer editorial for my niece — and that split focus is exactly the kind of Macy's-style overload I've come to expect from brands that don't quite know who they're talking to. Three editorial rails below the fold (Spot It. Shop It. / Maker's Latest Lineup / Best of BeautyInsider) compound the problem: pick one, not all three.
- I'd click "SHOP TO REDEEM" at the top — not because the email earned it, but because I was already mentally restocking. I would not forward this. A friend would get a text: "Sephora 4X points on hair care through the 27th, use HAIRVIDEO at checkout."

## What's working

- The 4X badge is first, biggest, orange — correct hierarchy for a points-forward loyalty email, and Nordstrom Beauty would've buried this same offer in a paragraph.
- "Use code HAIRVIDEO through 7/27" is right there in the banner — if I'm forced to enter a promo code, at least make it impossible to miss before I click away.
- "BE YOUR OWN STYLIST" skews aspirational without being condescending — that's a tone that works for someone like me who wants editorial framing without being talked down to.
- Beauty Insider branding in the footer signals that this is a member email, not a blast — I notice that distinction and it earns a small amount of goodwill.
- No broken images, no overlapping text, nothing catastrophic — Neiman Marcus sent me a hero-image-free email last week, so the bar is low, but Sephora clears it.

## What's weak

- "TikTok Creator" as a style authority is a hard miss for a 47-year-old Gen X shopper who trusts a Sisley counter advisor over any influencer handle — this single line quietly told me the email wasn't really written for me.
- The promo code HAIRVIDEO adds checkout friction that shouldn't exist for a Beauty Insider member — Nordstrom's equivalent loyalty multiplier activates automatically; a code requirement feels like a CRM workaround.
- Three editorial sections below the main hair care message (Spot It. Shop It., Maker's Latest Lineup, Best of BeautyInsider) turn this into a scroll marathon that dilutes the 4X points story — Kohl's does this and I hate it there too.
- The double ‼️ in the subject — the July 16 single-day 10% email skipped the exclamation stacking entirely and felt more credible; two ‼️ read as desperation, not urgency.
- No hero product with a visible price point — I'm looking at a dense product grid with no single recommendation anchoring my attention.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Remove the code requirement for logged-in Insiders, or explicitly say "no code needed when signed in."** The friction of HAIRVIDEO at checkout is the biggest click-to-conversion killer. Fix the back end or add a line that explains who needs the code and who doesn't.
- 2. **Cut two of the three editorial rails — keep "Spot It. Shop It." and drop the rest.** "Maker's Latest Lineup" and "Best of BeautyInsider" compete with the hair care focus and make this feel like three separate emails duct-taped together.
- 3. **Remove the TikTok Creator attribution or replace it with a named salon professional or Sephora Beauty Advisor.** "Looks curated by Sephora's Pro Team" lands for a 47-year-old counter shopper.
- 4. **Surface one hero product with a price, show the points math** — "Earn 800 points on the Dyson Airwrap at $499.99" is more compelling than a grid of twelve unlabeled products.
- 5. **One emoji max in the subject line, and not ‼️** — a single hair-relevant emoji would do more than two loud punctuation marks.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Confirmed issues:**

- **Missing `lang` attribute on `<html>`** — `<html xmlns="..." xmlns:v="..." xmlns:o="...">` has no `lang="en"` attribute. Screen readers cannot infer document language; WCAG 3.1.1 violation.

- **`maximum-scale=1` in viewport** — `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks user zoom on mobile. WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) failure. Remove `maximum-scale=1`.

- **`robots` meta malformed** — `<meta name="robots" content="no index" />` uses a space; the correct token is `noindex` (no space). Unlikely to affect email delivery but indicates a template copy/paste error.

- **`<title>` is generic** — `<title>Sephora</title>`. Not a rendering bug but will appear in browser-preview tabs and some preview panes without campaign context.
