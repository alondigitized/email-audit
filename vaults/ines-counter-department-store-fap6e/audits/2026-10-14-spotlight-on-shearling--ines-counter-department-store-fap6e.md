# Spotlight on Shearling
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-14**
## Executive summary

- This is the Neiman's I like. After a week of "$200 off" ladders, today's "First Call: Up to 40% off starts now" and this morning's loud tomato-red block, a quiet shearling edit felt like a salesperson who waited for me to look up. A dark suede-and-shearling jacket on cream. A calm "Shear Bliss." No countdown clock.
- It's also an email with no offer in it. There's no percent, no event, no points, no reason to act today. I can admire it, but I can't plan a purchase around it. I'd open it and scroll to the grid. I'd probably tap "Shop Shearling," because I do want a good shearling coat this year. I wouldn't forward it. There's no deal to pass along, and at these price points I'm not buying on a mood.

## What's working

- The hero is one clear image: a chocolate shearling jacket with a suede skirt, bag and shoes. It's wearable and polished, and it reads as a real coat.
- "Shear Bliss" is a quiet headline. After Kohl's "Save 20%" and Sephora's "MAKEUP SETS: HERE & NOWHERE ELSE" in capitals, that restraint feels like respect.
- The two outlined buttons, "Shop Shearling" and "Shop Outerwear," are plain, easy to tap and in my category.
- The numbered grid credits the designers: Saint Laurent, Givenchy, Fendi, Balmain. I like seeing who made what without hunting for it.
- The footer has a "personal style advisor" and an app download. That is the designer-access tone I want from Neiman's.

## What's weak

- There's no price anywhere. A $2,000 shearling and a $400 sweater look equally mysterious.
- The grid is numbered 01 to 07, but the items aren't labeled or clickable in any way I can see. The only link is a small underlined "Shop the Look." I'd have to guess which jacket is which.
- Nothing about the NM Rewards program: no points, no tier, no status. I won't use a card that doesn't give me points back, and this email never mentions mine.
- The grid mixes earrings, a red knit, boots and a skirt. The styling is lovely, but the coat is the story and it gets lost.
- The navy bomber with a fur collar in the grid isn't the shearling from the hero. The hero jacket isn't in the grid at all.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the hero jacket in the grid with its brand and price, and link each item to its own product page. Right now the best part of the email goes to a category page.
- 2. Add one member line, such as "Earn bonus points on outerwear this week," or a fit and sizing cue. Sephora Insider and Macy's both put member value up top. Neiman's has a loyalty program and doesn't use it here.
- 3. Make "Shop the Look" a real button, the same weight as the two above it.
- 4. Keep this calm tone. After the 10/6 to 10/14 run of discount sends, this quiet voice is why I still open Neiman's. Run it more often and drop one of the percent-off sends.
