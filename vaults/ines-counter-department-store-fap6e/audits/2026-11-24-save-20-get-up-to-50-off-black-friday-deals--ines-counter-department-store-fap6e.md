# Save 20% & get up to 50% off Black Friday Deals 🤩
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again. I've lost count of the sends since the weekend. After "STARTS TODAY," "HOURS LEFT" and yesterday's "$15 Kohl's Cash for every $50" email, this one adds nothing new. The subject says 20% plus up to 50% off. The body is the same wall of tiles, each with its own percentage and a "w/coupon" footnote.
- I scrolled the whole thing, and it's a very long column. There are about twenty product tiles: cookware, rugs, slippers, pajamas, candles, toys, KitchenAid. Beauty, the only thing I'd actually come for, is buried near the bottom in a small Sephora at Kohl's block. I wouldn't forward it, and I'd click only if I needed towels. I'd skim it, close it, and wait for the Kohl's Cash redemption window.

## What's working

- The green "$15 Kohl's Cash" badge in the hero is the one thing that reads as a reward for me. Earning something back is how I decide where to spend.
- The category strip near the top ($10 & Under, Women, Home, Beauty and so on) lets me jump straight to what I want.
- Specific prices like 7.99, 11.99 and 14.99 are easier to trust than a bare "up to."
- The layout is clean and nothing is broken. It's a loud sale, but it isn't garbled.

## What's weak

- It's too long. Neiman Marcus's "Stride into holiday plans, stylishly" was one dress, one idea, and no countdown. This is the opposite, a flea market.
- The "50% + 20% w/coupon" tiles repeat so often the number stops meaning anything. I can't tell which deals are real and which are "select styles" fine print.
- Beauty is almost an afterthought. Sephora's Cyber Week email leads with the beauty offer. Kohl's puts the Sephora block near the bottom, below toys and Amazon smart-home devices.
- Nothing says "member." I don't see tier status or points, so I'm treated as a stranger even though Kohl's knows my history.
- Macy's at least personalizes with its "confirm now" extra 25%. This feels like the same blast sent to everyone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to about six modules and lead with the Kohl's Cash earn and spend dates, since that's the actual value.
- 2. Put the Sephora at Kohl's beauty block high up for people who buy beauty.
- 3. Show real member status, such as "You have $X in Kohl's Cash expiring [date]."
- 4. Use one clear offer per tile and drop the "w/coupon" stacking. Put the coupon code in one place.
- 5. Throttle the cadence to one or two Black Friday sends a day at most.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The markup has several malformed-HTML defects, and most content images have no alt text. Four stacked tracking pixels and a link set that is almost entirely redirect-wrapped (92 links) leave the click destinations unverifiable. The compliance and authentication warnings are unconfirmed, because the headers weren't captured and the HTML is truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverifiable destinations:** QA skipped the HTTP probe on 92 tracking links (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=...`). Their landing URLs are encoded in `qs`, so link health and redirect behavior are unconfirmed.
- **Four open or analytics pixels:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/3c7b77a4-...`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=...`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager, third-party)
  
  They are redundant, and each adds a request on load.
- **Stray closing tag:** the `open.aspx` pixel is followed by `</custom>`, which has no opening tag. This is invalid markup.
- **Empty favicon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` can resolve to the document URL and trigger a spurious request.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** QA flagged images with no `alt` attribute:
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` (2a5adc5afb647aad, f441d45d2128ae53, c2f81e9ac8daee44, 415c8a18fc2950ea, 69494b48a79b4240, 78308d1d9adb0751, 916d6fa08d185233)
  - `images.am.kohls.com/.../60479404_1_2x`, `60699446_4_2x` and `60699446_3_2x`
  
  With images blocked, or for screen readers, these render as nothing or as a filename. If the Movable Ink images are the main content blocks, there is also no text fallback.
- **Tracking pixels:** `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...`, `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` and `kohls.demdex.net/event` lack `alt`. Only the `mi.kohls.com/.../o.gif` pixel carries `aria-hidden="true"` (with `alt=""`); the others have no `alt` or `aria-hidden`.
- **Empty `<title></title>`:** this fails the document-title accessibility requirement.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will close the head early and move the content into the body.
- **CSS errors:**
  - `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` is missing its leading dot, so it matches an element name instead of a class.
  - `max-height: auto !important` is not a valid value.
- **Font imports:** the Google Fonts `@import` and `<link>` tags are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, so Outlook is excluded. Clients that block external CSS fall back to Arial. This is not a defect.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible HTML, which is truncated. The `mi_u=604230016` subscriber ID resolved in the image URLs.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
All of these are unconfirmed, not verified defects:
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** QA reports both absent. QA itself notes the AgentMail relay may not capture them, so this cannot be attributed to the sender.
- **Physical address:** not detected, but the HTML is truncated before the footer. This cannot be confirmed either way.
- **Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM/DMARC):** status unknown. The header is not exposed by the relay.
- **Visible unsubscribe link:** not confirmed in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
The destinations sit inside encoded `qs` blobs, so UTM presence and landing-page alignment can't be assessed. Campaign identifiers are visible only on the image URLs (`mi_ecmp=1026509_20261123`, `email_name=261123_DG_Email_Mon_PM`), not on the links. Resolving the redirects would be needed to check this.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, including the Movable Ink PNGs and the product images.
2. Add `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"` to the three tracking pixels that lack them.
3. Fill in `<title>`, remove the empty favicon `<link>`, and delete the stray `</custom>`.
4. Move the schema.org `<div>` out of `<head>`, or replace it with JSON-LD in the body.
5. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
6. Fix the `ReadMsgBody` selector and the invalid `max-height: auto`.
7. Consolidate the four open pixels where possible.
8. Reduce URL density in the plain-text part (16,344 of 22,131 characters are URLs).
9. To close the compliance gaps, re-run the audit with the full HTML and raw headers (direct SMTP capture). Resolve a sample of the redirects to check UTM parameters and landing-page alignment.
