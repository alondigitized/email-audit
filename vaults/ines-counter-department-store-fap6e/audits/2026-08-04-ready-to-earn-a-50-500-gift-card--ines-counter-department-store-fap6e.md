# Ready to earn a $50–$500 gift card?
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-04**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this one actually got my attention for a change. After three straight Neiman's sends this week that were all mood board and no math — the "Field Notes" horse-and-linen shoot, the "Day-to-dusk" scroll, the flat product catalog yesterday — this is the first one that talks to me like I'm someone who's going to open her wallet. A spend/earn table with real numbers, right up top, no scrolling required. That's the Neiman's I actually shop at.
- That said, it's still not quite Sephora-level targeted. Sephora's "STARTS TODAY: the Beauty Insider Celebration" (9/10 from me) knew I had a Rouge card in my wallet and spoke directly to my tier. This just says "includes Beauty & Fragrance" in small type under the headline and moves on to gowns and handbags. I'm a beauty-counter regular first — I want to know if my counter purchases count toward this, not just infer it from a subhead.
- Would I click? Probably, yes — I'd at least hover over "SHOP & EARN" to see if my usual Chanel or La Mer spend gets me there. Would I forward it to a friend? Only the one who's already eyeing that Theia gown for a fall wedding. This isn't share-worthy, it's a "keep it in the inbox and check the fine print before I buy" email.

## What's working

- The spend/earn table is dead simple — $200→$50, $500→$125, all the way to $2,000→$500 — I don't have to do math in my head like I did on that Macy's "confirm now" email.
- "SHOP & EARN" is a real button, not a vague "Discover" link, and it's impossible to miss against the burgundy hero.
- The product grid below is genuinely curated to my taste — Theia, Jimmy Choo, Manolo Blahnik, Burberry, Ferragamo — this isn't the 24-year-old-in-a-field energy from "Field Notes," this is grown-up designer.
- It's dated and code-bound ("Code AUGUST," expires 8/9), so it reads like a real, current promotion and not recycled copy.

## What's weak

- Zero mention of InCircle. I have status with this store and this email doesn't acknowledge it once — Sephora remembered my Rouge card, Neiman's forgot I exist as a repeat customer.
- No prices on any of the nine products shown. I have to click through to even know if that Mac Duggal gown is $400 or $4,000 relative to my spend tier.
- The exclusions are heavy and buried at the very bottom in tiny gray text — sale items don't count, gift cards don't count, no InCircle-only qualifying details. For a "buy more, earn more" pitch, that's a lot of asterisks I only find after scrolling past six CTA buttons.
- This is the fourth Neiman's email in my inbox in three days (Last Call 8/3, new arrivals 8/3, Day-to-dusk 8/2, now this). That's aggressive cadence even by department-store standards.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an InCircle callout right under the hero table — even a single line like "InCircle members: this stacks with your points" would move this from generic promo to loyalty play, closing the gap with Sephora's approach.
- 2. Add starting prices under at least the hero product (the Theia gown) so I can judge my spend tier before clicking.
- 3. Move the "Sale and reduced-price merchandise do not qualify" exclusion up near the table, not buried six screens down — I'd rather know that before I get excited about the $500 gift card at the $2,000 tier.
- 4. Ease the send frequency — four emails in three days trains me to skim, not open.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Beauty & fashion gift card, up to $500 back`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Exclusions apply — see which brands qualify before you shop`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Code AUGUST ends 8/9 — earn toward your next Neiman's run`
