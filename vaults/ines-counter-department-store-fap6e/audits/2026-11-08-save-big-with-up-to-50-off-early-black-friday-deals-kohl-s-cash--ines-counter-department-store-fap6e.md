# Save BIG with up to 50% off early Black Friday deals + Kohl's Cash 🤑
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's, again. It's the ninth one I've counted this week, and the 11/7 "Up to 50% off deals + extra 20% off + Daily Deals" send was nearly the same email. The "Early Arrival / Black Friday Deals" hero with "up to 50% off" and "extra 20% off" is clear enough. Then it turns into a very long scroll of 50% tiles for cookware, toys, luggage, sweaters and a KitchenAid. The only thing that changes from tile to tile is the picture.
- I'll be upfront: the screenshot is a tall thin strip, so I could read the headlines and prices but not the fine print. What I can see tells me this is a catalog, not a curated email. I'd skim it, maybe check the Sephora at Kohl's tile since that's my category, and close it. I wouldn't forward it. If I wanted a Kohl's deal, I'd trust the last email as much as this one.

## What's working

- The Black Friday hero at the top is clear. Up to 50% off and extra 20% off are visible right away.
- Prices are shown with the regular price next to them, like the bath towels from $4.79 and the $69.99 vacuum with its "Reg." price. That tells me what I'm saving.
- The "Daily Deal" tiles with a single product and one price are the most honest part of the email.
- There's a Sephora at Kohl's tile with a "Sephora Savings Event" banner. It's the one thing aimed at someone like me.
- The "Shop Now" links under each tile are easy to find, and the layout didn't break anywhere I can see.

## What's weak

- It's the same promo as every day this week, and "early Black Friday" no longer feels early.
- There are too many 50% off tiles. Macy's does overload too, but at least their "confirm now for extra 25%" email had one job.
- "Coupons do not apply" shows up on tile after tile. That's the fine print I've learned to look for, and it takes away from the "extra 20% off" in the hero.
- Beauty is tucked into the middle, and nothing speaks to my age or how I shop. Sephora's own "10% off palettes" email put the category up front. Nordstrom and Neiman Marcus make me feel like a known customer. This treats me like a stranger.
- I couldn't see a clear Kohl's Cash explanation or a loyalty/Card message. It's in the subject but I couldn't find it in the body.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one hero offer and 6–8 products. If I'm a beauty buyer, lead with beauty and the Sephora at Kohl's event.
- 2. Put the Kohl's Cash earn/redeem dates in the hero, with a button that says "Earn Kohl's Cash" and a clear deadline.
- 3. Show the exclusions in the hero rather than repeating them tile by tile.
- 4. Stop sending near-identical emails every day. Space them out, or drop the "early" language once it's been used for a week.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Sephora at Kohl's + 50% off, Beauty picks inside`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Earn $15 Kohl's Cash for every $50 you spend. See what's excluded.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Towels from $4.79, a Shark vacuum for $199.99, and beauty deals.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has several markup defects (stray `</custom>` tag, `<div itemscope>` inside `<head>`, empty `<title>`) and a viewport that blocks zoom. All 91 links go through tracking redirects, so their destinations could not be checked. The compliance and authentication checks came back inconclusive, not failed, because the relay did not capture headers and the HTML source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Destinations unverified.** 91 links were skipped as tracking redirects, for example `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7ImQiOjUwNTQ...`. Link health, landing page status and redirect chains are unknown.
- **Four tracking pixels in the first lines of `<body>`**:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/5f024943-...`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=...`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager, with `width="0" height="0"`)
  
  These are redundant open-tracking calls from several vendors. The 0×0 demdex image may also be dropped by some clients.
- **Malformed tag.** The `open.aspx` pixel is followed by `</custom>`, a closing tag with no opener. Some parsers may mishandle it.
- **Plain-text part.** 16,076 of 21,874 characters (73%) are URLs, which is above the 70% threshold. The long `qs=` tracking strings cause this and can hurt spam scoring.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on images.** The QA check flagged at least 10 images:
  - 3 tracking pixels (`o`, `o.gif`, `event`). These should carry `alt=""`, but the impact is low.
  - 7 or more `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` images (`2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`). If these carry offer content, the email is unreadable with images off or in a screen reader. The list is truncated, so the real count may be higher.
- **Zoom blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` prevents pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Invalid `<head>` content.** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`, so parsers will close the head early. There is also an empty `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`, and `format-detection` is declared twice.
- **Doctype.** HTML 4.01 Transitional is paired with `lang="en"` and VML/Office namespaces. It works, but it is dated.
- **Positive.** The layout tables use `role="presentation"`, the MSO conditional comments are present, and the Google Fonts links are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` with an Arial/Helvetica fallback.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unrendered merge tokens** (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) in the visible source.
- **Empty dynamic parameter.** One image URL contains `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` with no value, and the next parameter `mi_kc_event_pe...` is cut off. If this is the Kohl's Cash redemption end date, it may not have populated, which matters because the subject promises Kohl's Cash. It needs confirming against the full source and the rendered image.
- **Inconsistent image parameters.** Some `mi.kohls.com` image URLs carry `email_name=261107_DG_Email_Sat_PM` and others carry only `mi_u` and `mi_ecmp`. The output is truncated, so this may not be a real difference.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058).** Both were not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. The sender is `s.kohls.com`, and Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Physical address.** The check found none. The HTML was truncated before the footer, so I can't confirm this is a real omission.
- **SPF/DKIM/DMARC.** `Authentication-Results` was not captured, so authentication is unknown.
- **Confirmed in the source.** The sender domain `s.kohls.com` is aligned with the tracking domains `click.s.kohls.com` and `click.chp.kohls.com`. The email also embeds the third-party Adobe demdex pixel.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters can't be assessed.** The links are opaque `qs=` redirects, so no `utm_*` parameters are visible in the source.
- **Landing page alignment can't be assessed.** No destinations were resolved, so I can't compare the "up to 50% off early Black Friday" and Kohl's Cash promise with the landing pages.
- **Campaign ID.** The campaign identifier `261107_DG_Email_Sat_PM` (dated 2026-11-07) appears only in image URLs, and its presence on click links is unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Capture raw headers outside the relay and verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF, DKIM and DMARC. Do this before treating the compliance warnings as real failures.
2. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer for a physical address and an unsubscribe link, and to resolve the `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` value.
3. Resolve the 91 tracking links through a redirect-following check to confirm status codes, final URLs and UTM carry-through.
4. Add descriptive `alt` text to the `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` content images and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
5. Fix the markup:
   - Remove the stray `</custom>`.
   - Move the schema.org `<div>` out of `<head>`, or switch to a `<script type="application/ld+json">` block.
   - Add a `<title>`.
   - Remove the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon">`.
   - Drop the duplicate `format-detection` meta.
6. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
7. Consolidate the redundant open pixels and consider dropping the 0×0 demdex pixel. Shortening the tracking URLs would also bring the plain-text URL ratio below 70%.
