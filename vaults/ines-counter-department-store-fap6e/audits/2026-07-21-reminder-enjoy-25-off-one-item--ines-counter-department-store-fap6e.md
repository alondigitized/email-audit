# Reminder: Enjoy 25% off one item
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-21**
## Executive summary

- Yesterday's "Just for you: 25% off one item" was the best thing NM sent me all month — I genuinely stopped scrolling for it. This email is the same one with the word "Reminder:" pasted on the front. Same code, same nine-product designer grid, same six category shop buttons. That is the entirety of what changed.
- In isolation, the bones are excellent: the 25% OFF hero is clean and immediate, the brands they chose to show me (Miu Miu, Ferragamo, Manolo Blahnik) are precisely the names that make me open my Neiman Marcus app. The email is not broken — it's just redundant. I'm at twenty-six NM emails in July, and sending me my own code back as if I'd forgotten it treats me like an absentminded customer rather than a loyal one. I'd mark this read, wonder if I should check what's left before the code expires, and probably not click.
- I would not forward this. There's nothing here my friend doesn't already have in her inbox.

## What's working

- **25% OFF hero reads in under a second** — type is large, hierarchy is clean, the offer is understood before anything else registers.
- **Designer brand edit is exactly right for me** — Miu Miu sunglasses, Chloe bags, Ferragamo, Oscar de la Renta, Jimmy Choo, Fendi, Manolo Blahnik — this is Neiman Marcus speaking in its own language, not generic clearance.
- **Promo code is front and center** — JULY25-1DGZ-RRLPPT is visible without hunting; I don't have to squint at footer fine print.
- **"Just for you — save on an item you've been eyeing"** — if I hadn't already seen this line yesterday, it would land warmly; it implies personalization and awareness.
- **No render bugs** — fully loaded, no broken image gaps, clean layout on what appears to be a desktop render.

## What's weak

- **"Reminder:" is the laziest word in email marketing** — it signals nothing changed, and that's exactly the problem.
- **No beauty product anywhere in that grid** — the 25% off is theoretically redeemable at the beauty counter, but I'd never know it from these nine items. Every slot is apparel, shoes, or bags. Charlotte Tilbury, La Mer, not a trace.
- **No InCircle mention** — Macy's Star Rewards would have told me what this purchase is worth in points. For someone who won't swipe without earning something back, the silence on loyalty is a real miss.
- **"Online only" in the fine print** — I'm a counter person. I go in-store. This offer excludes my preferred channel and doesn't offer a path to redeem in person.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Reframe the subject entirely** — "Reminder:" is a forfeit. Tell me what I lose if I don't act, not that I forgot to act.
- 2. **Swap one grid product for a beauty hero** — La Mer Crème, a Charlotte Tilbury palette, a Chanel foundation. One slot. That's all it takes to make this relevant to the reason I'm actually a NM customer.
- 3. **Surface InCircle points** — a single line: "Earn InCircle points on your purchase, too." Sephora Insider does this effortlessly and it's the thing that turns a browse into a transaction for me.
- 4. **Give me a real deadline with a timestamp** — "Offer ends tonight at 11:59 PM PT" is credible. "LIMITED TIME" is not. Compare: Macy's confirmation email (which I audited recently) at least creates a functional end-state with its urgency mechanics, even if the execution is clumsy.
- 5. **Add an in-store CTA** — a secondary line: "Shopping in-store? Show this code at the register." I would have used it yesterday if I'd known I could.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 25% off expires soon — don't leave it unused`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last chance: 25% off one item, your code inside`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Designer picks from Miu Miu, Manolo Blahnik, and more — plus beauty.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Code: JULY25. Valid through midnight tonight — in-store and online.`

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud AMPscript send using XHTML 1.0 Transitional. QA catches four warnings: missing unsubscribe headers, unknown authentication status, and a plain-text body with anomalously high URL density — all are actionable infrastructure issues, not rendering problems.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**26 tracking links skipped (QA: not probed)**
All click links route through a redirect domain (likely `e.neimanmarcus.com` or a dedicated click-tracking host). Destination URLs and UTM parameter correctness cannot be confirmed without resolving the redirects. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed bug — but any broken redirect will be invisible in this audit.

**No issues confirmed** in the links that were probed.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Commented-out viewport alternates (dead code)**
Three additional `<meta name="viewport">` tags are present but commented out directly in the `<head>`:
```html
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, user-scalable=no" />-->
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=320, initial-scale=0.29">-->
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=1024">-->
```
These are harmless but indicate the template was patched rather than cleaned; they should be removed to avoid confusion during future edits.

**Global `p { text-align: center }` override**
The stylesheet applies `text-align: center` to all `<p>` elements. Any paragraph-level copy that should be left-aligned will require an inline override on every instance — a maintenance liability.

**`!important` saturation in media queries**
The responsive block applies `!important` to virtually every property. This is common email practice but makes targeted debugging difficult; worth noting for template maintainers.

**Alt text not verifiable** — the HTML is truncated before image tags appear. Confirm all `<img>` elements carry meaningful `alt` attributes (or `alt=""` for decorative images); screen readers and image-blocked rendering depend on this.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Ambiguous active scenario**
The template header documents two scenarios and instructs the sender to "CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW," but only one is commented out:
```
<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */-->
```
It is not possible to confirm from the truncated source which scenario is active or whether `@user` resolves to a hashed email or an `EMAIL_ID` lookup. If the wrong scenario is live, `@user` may be null or resolve to the wrong identifier for personalized link targeting.

**`@uEm` and `@cmdid` from CRM Feed**
Both require a successful `Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", ...)` against the recipient's email address. If a subscriber is absent from `NM_CRM_Feed`, these will return null — downstream tracking or personalization dependent on these values will silently fail for that segment. No fallback is documented in the visible source.

**`@refid = CONCAT(@base_refid, "")`**
This is a no-op concatenation (appends an empty string). If the original intent was to append a segment suffix, the value is likely incorrect for the current send.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header missing (QA WARN)**
The header was not captured by the AgentMail relay. Gmail and Yahoo have required this header for bulk senders (>5k/day) since February 2024. Its absence risks placement in spam folders and blocks Gmail's native unsubscribe UI. Confirm whether SFMC is injecting it at send time and whether the relay is stripping it.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) missing (QA WARN)**
One-click unsubscribe (the `POST` mechanism required by Google/Yahoo bulk sender policy) is not present. This is a separate deliverability risk from the above — even if the `List-Unsubscribe` header is present, the absence of the `Post` variant means one-click unsubscribe will not work in Gmail.

**`Authentication-Results` not found (QA WARN)**
SPF and DKIM pass/fail status is unknown because the relay did not surface the header. If DKIM is broken for `e.neimanmarcus.com`, emails will be treated as unauthenticated by major providers. Verify DKIM signing is active on the sending domain.

**CAN-SPAM physical address and unsubscribe mechanism** — not verifiable from the truncated source. Confirm both are present in the footer.
