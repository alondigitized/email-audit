# Choose one of two FREE sample bags filled with fan faves 💫 Min. spend required.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-21**
## Executive summary

- This is the ninth or tenth Sephora email I've had in about a week, and the subject made me want to open it anyway. A free sample bag is something I'll actually pay attention to. The top delivers. "Pick Your FREE* Sample Bag" sits right under the Savings Event banner with its 30% off badge. The asterisk and the "Min. spend required" in the subject are honest, though the fine print isn't readable at inbox size.
- Then it turns into a gift-set catalog. "For the Beauty Obsessed" runs seven gift sets (Sephora Favorites, Laneige, Kérastase, Fenty, L'Occitane, Ouai, m.ph) in a long scroll. After that come a gift-ideas tool, Prada and Makeup by Dior-style lineup tiles, "You Deserve Nice Things" and the Insider footer. The sample bag is the one offer I'd act on, and it gets buried under everything else.
- I'd click once, on the sample bag, and only if I was already planning a purchase. I wouldn't forward it. The 4X points code at the very bottom is the better tell, and I almost missed it.

## What's working

- The "Pick Your FREE* Sample Bag" headline at the top is plain, and the sample bag image sits next to it. I understood the offer in two seconds.
- The bag lets me choose one of two. Choice feels like respect, not a shout.
- Every gift set shows a price badge with its value, like "$98 / $164 value". I can do the math without clicking.
- The Beauty Insider free-shipping banner is visible. Loyalty members get acknowledged.
- The footer carries a scannable barcode and the code 4XHYDRATE for in-store use. That's useful for someone who buys at the counter.

## What's weak

- Too many jobs in one email: sample bag, Savings Event, free shipping, seven gift sets, a tool, two lineup tiles and a basket push. Neiman Marcus's "25% off one item" email made one point and I got it.
- The sample-bag fine print is tiny. I can't see the minimum spend where I'd decide, only in the subject.
- Every gift set is labeled "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA • LIMITED EDITION". When everything is limited, nothing is.
- The 4X points offer is buried in the barcode footer. Points are the one thing I buy for, and they got the least space. In the 10/18 send it was the first thing I read.
- The email is very long. Macy's at least kept its "confirm now" email to one action.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the sample bag, the minimum spend in plain type beside the button, and the 4X points line. That is the whole story.
- 2. Put the points deadline (ends 10/21) and the code next to the hero, not in the barcode.
- 3. Show three gift sets, not seven, and pick them by price band.
- 4. Drop the "limited edition" tag from items that aren't scarce.
- 5. Make the spend threshold a visible number, like "free with $75", so I know before I scroll.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 4X points on moisturizers with code 4XHYDRATE, ends 10/21.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Gucci, Caudalie and more. Choose yours at checkout.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "Choose one of two FREE sample bags filled with fan faves 💫 Min. spend required."

Only a truncated slice of the HTML was available. That slice has the head and CSS but no body content, so I could only check what the source and QA data show.

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's head and CSS are standard for a table-based ESP template (Epsilon/Harmony). The confirmed problems are a missing plain-text part, three images without alt text, and a viewport meta tag that blocks zoom. The missing unsubscribe and authentication headers are probably artifacts of the AgentMail relay, so they are unconfirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links were reported. QA probed 12 material links and skipped 43 (footer, utility and social links are deprioritized), so the other 43 are unverified.
- At least one tracked image is served from the click-tracking domain: `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...`. This looks like an open-tracking pixel or a redirect-wrapped image, and it has no alt text (see section 3).
- The truncated source doesn't show the body's `<a href>` values, so I can't assess redirect depth or tracking-wrapper behavior.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed by QA, 3 images):**
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...`
  - `https://images.harmony.epsilon.com/.../images/2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg`
  - `https://images.harmony.epsilon.com/.../images/US_4XHYDRATE_Barcode.jpg`
  - `US_4XHYDRATE_Barcode.jpg` looks like a functional image (a barcode or promo code). Without alt text, images-off and screen-reader users lose the code.
  - `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg` is the navigation header. Without alt text, images-off users get no navigation.
- **Zoom restriction (source-confirmed):** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, which is an accessibility failure (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resizing blocked:** the CSS sets `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none; -ms-text-size-adjust: none; text-size-adjust: none` on `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td`. That prevents user and OS text scaling in supporting clients.
- **Invalid robots value (source-confirmed):** `<meta name="robots" content="no index" />`. The valid token is `noindex`, so the space makes the directive invalid. The impact is low, because email clients ignore it, but it matters if a web-view copy is hosted.
- `<title>Sephora</title>` is generic. It is low impact but not descriptive for a hosted web version.
- The XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype with VML/Office namespaces is consistent with Outlook support. No issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%`, `{{`, `[[`, `${`) appear in the visible source or the subject line. The body was truncated, so tokens further down can't be ruled out.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text part missing (QA FAIL):** the text version is 0 chars. A multipart/alternative with an empty or absent text/plain part is a deliverability and accessibility risk, since spam filters weigh HTML-only mail negatively. This is the one QA failure.
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found (QA WARN):** QA itself says the relay may strip these headers. Verify against the raw message at the original receiving mailbox before treating this as a real gap. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, and a sender of Sephora's scale almost certainly sets it.
- **Authentication-Results missing (QA WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because of the relay. Not assessable from this data.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address were not visible in the truncated source. QA says an unsubscribe link was included in probing, but I have no result for it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The truncated source and QA output contain no destination URLs, UTM parameters or landing-page data. The link-probe results don't include final URLs or status codes. The promo ("Min. spend required") can't be matched to a landing page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a plain-text part.** This is the only confirmed QA failure.
2. **Add meaningful alt text** to `US_4XHYDRATE_Barcode.jpg` (the code or offer text), `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg` (nav labels), and the `app.sephora.com/O/...` image. Use `alt=""` if the last one is a pure tracking pixel.
3. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta, and drop `text-size-adjust: none` where it isn't needed to fix Outlook or iOS rendering quirks.
4. **Fix `content="no index"` to `content="noindex"`.**
5. **Re-check headers on the raw message** (List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, Authentication-Results) outside the relay. If they are present, the QA check should treat their absence under AgentMail as inconclusive rather than a warning.
6. **Capture the full HTML and final link URLs** so UTM and landing-page continuity can be audited. The 43 skipped links and the truncated body are blind spots.
