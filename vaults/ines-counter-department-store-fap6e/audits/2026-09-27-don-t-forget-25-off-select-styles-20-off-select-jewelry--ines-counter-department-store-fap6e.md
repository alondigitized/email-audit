# Don’t forget: 25% off select styles + 20% off select jewelry
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-27**
## Executive summary

- Fine, this is the third time this week Neiman's has run the exact same forest-green Friends & Family creative — I logged the reminder on the 25th ("Have you shopped 25% off?") and again the day before that, and now here's "Don't forget" wearing the identical hero banner, same font, same "Shop the Event" button. At this point I could draw this email from memory. The fine print reads "Beauty and fragrance" don't qualify — which for me, a counter regular, is the one thing I'd actually go in for, so the "Friends & Family" halo doesn't even apply to my category.
- That said, I'll give credit where it's due: the merchandising grid this time is genuinely well-curated — Dolce&Gabbana, Theia, Cult Gaia, Gianvito Rossi, Brunello Cucinelli — that's a real cross-section of what I browse, and the InCircle Double Points banner is a smart nudge for someone like me who won't touch a card without points back. But I've seen this exact layout enough times now that it reads as filler, not a fresh push, and there's no beauty section at all despite this being "select styles."
- Would I click? Only out of habit to browse the dresses, not because anything here feels urgent or new. I would not forward this — it's the same email my sister already got twice this week.

## What's working

- The InCircle Double Points callout speaks directly to me — I don't touch a card without points back, and this is the one line that actually rewards loyalty.
- The product grid is genuinely on-brand for me — D&G, Theia, Gianvito Rossi, Cult Gaia are pieces I'd actually stop and look at, unlike Kohl's handbag prop that just sits there as decoration.
- Clean hierarchy — green hero, 25% off, "Shop the Event" button is impossible to miss, no scrolling needed to find the offer.
- No render bugs — every product image loaded, nothing overlapping, nothing broken.

## What's weak

- This is the same Friends & Family creative I've now seen three times in five days — the 24th's gift-card reminder, the 25th's "Have you shopped" nudge, and now this. Sephora at least rotates their hero art between sends; this one is on repeat.
- "Beauty and fragrance" is excluded per the fine print — as a beauty-counter regular that's a real letdown buried at the bottom where most people won't scroll to see it.
- "Exclusions Apply" sitting right under the CTA with no detail feels like the same bait-and-switch texture I flagged on Macy's "confirm now" email — vague caveat, no specifics up front.
- Nothing here acknowledges I'm an InCircle member by name or tier — it's a generic "members earn double points" line, not "Ines, your points are waiting."

## Recommendations

- 1. Rotate the creative — stop reusing the identical dark green banner for the third send this week. Even a different product photo in the hero would break the fatigue.
- 2. Move the beauty/fragrance exclusion out of the fine print and into the subject or hero if it applies — don't let a beauty shopper open this expecting counter savings that aren't there.
- 3. Personalize the points line with actual InCircle tier or point balance — "You're earning 2X points, Ines" beats generic "members earn double points."
- 4. Tighten "Exclusions Apply" into something specific like "Excludes beauty & fragrance" right under the CTA, not three screens down.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last few days — 25% off select styles + double InCircle points`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New arrivals from D&G, Theia, and Gianvito Rossi — shop before it ends 9/30`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your InCircle points double now through 9/30 CT`
