# Just in: 1,000+ designer arrivals for pre-fall

## Executive summary

- Well, this is handsome — the black "BRAND NEW STYLES JUST LANDED" banner has that clean editorial Neiman's look I actually enjoy, and I recognize half the names in that grid: Brunello Cucinelli, Saint Laurent, Zimmermann, Prada. But there's not a single number anywhere in this email. No percent off, no InCircle points, no mention of the Triple Points push Neiman's just ran me on Brunello Cucinelli yesterday (8/23) — the same brand sitting right there in this grid as a plain leather skirt with zero connection back to that offer. It's a catalog, not an invitation.

## What's working

- The hero banner is genuinely elegant — clean typography, no clutter, on-brand for Neiman's quiet-luxury register.
- Product grid mixes recognizable names I'd actually shop — Saint Laurent, Brunello Cucinelli, Zimmermann — that's the right roster for me.
- "Shop All Women's New Arrivals" and "Shop All Men's New Arrivals" buttons are clear, unambiguous CTAs with visible borders.
- No render bugs — images loaded clean, nothing overlapping, nothing broken.

## What's weak

- Zero pricing, zero percent-off, zero mention of InCircle points anywhere — after yesterday's Brunello Cucinelli Triple Points email, this feels like a step backward in relevance.
- No loyalty tier callout at all — Sephora's been dinging me with "0 points" for weeks, which is annoying, but at least it acknowledges I'm a member. This email doesn't even try.
- Men's section takes up half the real estate — sneakers, Prada blazer, Gucci glasses — none of that's my closet, so I'm scrolling past a lot of dead weight.
- No sense of occasion or event tie-in — Macy's "Last Chance" and Neiman's own "Last Call: Extra 25% off" (8/22) at least gave me a reason to act now; this is just "stuff exists."

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the loyalty hook — if InCircle members get early access or bonus points on new arrivals, say so in the hero. That's the single biggest lift given how well Triple Points landed yesterday.
- 2. Split into a women's-only send for me specifically — I don't need Ferragamo sneakers and Emporio Armani tees taking up half my scroll.
- 3. Add at least one price or "starting at $X" cue near the hero — even quiet luxury brands anchor with a number somewhere.
- 4. Tie this to an in-store event or trunk show if one's coming — that's how I actually buy from Neiman's.
