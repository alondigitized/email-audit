# Limited time: Get up to $250 off
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-26**
## Executive summary

- Okay, a straight-up spend-and-save tier promo — no gift card gimmick, no countdown clock, just "spend $400, save $100 / spend $1,000, save $250." I actually like the clarity of that math up top, but the second I hit the merchandise grid I checked out a little — Mac Duggal gown, Badgley Mischka, Jimmy Choo, Gianvito Rossi, Amiri — this is red-carpet-adjacent inventory, not "Tuesday errands" inventory, and it's sitting right after a Brunello Cucinelli send two days ago that felt like it actually knew me.
- The fine print at the bottom is a small paragraph of exclusions — no beauty, no fragrance, no gift registry, no Bergdorf Goodman crossover, no combining with the free-shipping code above it unless you read closely. That's the part that makes me trust the offer less, not more. I'm not clicking through to shop the grid, though I might tap "Shop Handbags" out of curiosity for the Jacquemus basket bag. Not forwarding this one — it's a fine reminder email, not a "look at this" email.

## What's working

- The spend/save table (spend $400/save $100, spend $1,000/save $250) is genuinely the clearest math I've seen from Neiman's all week — no mental gymnastics required.
- Clean editorial layout, consistent black-and-cream palette, nothing overlapping or broken.
- Free shipping banner at $150 is a real secondary value-add, not filler.
- Category shortcut buttons (Shop Women's Clothing, Shop Handbags, etc.) mean I don't have to scroll back up if one product catches my eye.

## What's weak

- No InCircle/loyalty tie-in anywhere near the hero — after the Triple Points send on the 24th, going back to a generic promo code feels like a step down in personalization.
- The product grid skews evening/red-carpet (Mac Duggal gown, Badgley Mischka) more than "everyday closet builder" — doesn't match how I actually shop Neiman's.
- Exclusions paragraph is long and buried at the bottom — beauty, fragrance, gift cards, Bergdorf Goodman all carved out, which makes "up to $250 off" feel smaller once you actually read it.
- Two competing codes in one email (SHOPFALL and SHIPNM) with different minimums — a little confusing for a Tuesday-morning skim.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap at least two of the nine grid products for daytime pieces (a blazer, a tote) — right now it skews gala when the offer is meant to be broad.
- 2. Move a one-line loyalty nod ("InCircle Members also earn double points on this purchase") into the hero block — Neiman's proved on the 24th they know how to make me feel like a name, not a code.
- 3. Trim the exclusions to one clean line under the CTA button instead of a wall of text at the footer.
- 4. Pick one code, not two — either fold free shipping into the same SHOPFALL code or drop the threshold confusion.
