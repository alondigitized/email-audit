# STARTS TODAY: Get 30% off ALL Sephora Collection 😊
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-30**
## Executive summary

- This is the eighteenth Sephora email I've had in about a week, and this one finally has a real headline. The big black "Savings Event" banner and the giant "30% OFF" on Sephora Collection are hard to miss. It's one clear offer with dates (10/30–11/9), and I can read it at a glance. That's more than I can say for the 10/28 "🌟 THIS JUST IN 🌟" send, which shouted and told me nothing.
- Then it keeps going. The page has a points challenge, a credit card pitch, a "Beauty on Demand" strip, product grids, gift sets, a store locator for Kohl's, a holiday gift guide, and a long block of legal text. It's a Macy's-style pile-up under a good hero. Sephora Collection is also the house brand, not the Chanel or Tatcha I shop for. I'd click once to check whether any Collection basics are worth the 30%. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read.

## What's working

- The hero is clear: "30% OFF" in big gold type with the dates under it. I get the offer in two seconds.
- The two codes are laid out plainly: ALLONSALE online and SCSAVE at Kohl's. I like that they tell me the in-store route, because I buy at the counter.
- The "Earn up to 500 Points" Insider challenge is a real member perk, and a points person like me notices it.
- The product grid shows prices ($15, $13, $99) right on the tiles, so I don't have to click to find out.
- "Free shipping and returns on all orders, no minimum" is a trust cue. Returns are a big deal for me.

## What's weak

- The subject says "ALL Sephora Collection," but the body leads with "Insider, Save on ALL." The tone is shouty, with the caps, "STARTS TODAY," and the smiley.
- It's a very long scroll. The credit card pitch, the points challenge, and the gift guide all compete with the one 30% offer.
- The Sephora Collection products don't read as premium to me. A calendar for $99 and a $15 eyeliner don't speak to a designer-counter shopper.
- Kohl's store info takes up a big block, including a map and hours. It's useful if I live near one, but it pushes the real offer down the page.
- The legal text at the bottom is tiny and dense, and I can't read it without zooming. Honest fine print should be readable.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut everything below the product grid except one secondary perk. Offer, grid, and one loyalty line is enough.
- 2. Put one product ahead of the pack. A hero eyeliner or lip product with a review count would give me a reason to click.
- 3. Make the Insider tier benefit specific. Tell me what 30% plus my tier gets me.
- 4. Move the Kohl's store locator into its own send, or personalize it to my ZIP.
- 5. Drop the caps and the emoji. Nordstrom would just say "30% off Sephora Collection."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has two confirmed defects: an empty greeting slot ("Hi ,") and no plain-text part (0 chars). The compliance and authentication warnings (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) may be relay capture gaps rather than sender faults. The HTML is truncated, so I could not check the footer or most link targets.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- QA probed 12 material links and reported no failures. The other 37 were skipped, so link health is only partly verified.
- Two tracking pixels are present:
  - `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=4970269939971072&mi_ecmp=20261030_SC_30_off_Starts_Today_BI` (1x1)
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...` (hidden with `display:none; mso-hide:all`)
- The `mi_u` value is a per-recipient identifier in a URL. This is normal for ESP tracking, but it is not a defect.
- No broken or malformed links are visible in the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **QA false positive:** QA flags `o.gif` as missing alt text, but the source has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`. That is the correct markup for a decorative pixel.
- **Real alt issue:** the `app.sephora.com/O/...` pixel has no `alt` attribute. It is hidden, so the impact is minimal, but it is non-compliant markup.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem.
- **No language attribute:** `<html>` has no `lang`, and only the `xmlns` attributes are set.
- **Likely broken mobile CSS:** inside the `@media (max-width:480px)` block, the `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {...}<![endif]-->` conditional is embedded in the CSS. Non-MSO parsers will read `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size` and `<![endif]--> .mobileHeaderTierRouge` as invalid selectors and probably drop those two rules. In that case `.mobileHeaderTierRouge` would not resize on mobile. I have not confirmed this in a client.
- **Fixed-width layout:** the wrapper uses `width:700px; min-width:700px`, and the media query only adjusts font sizes and logo/icon dimensions. This likely forces horizontal scrolling on narrow viewports. I inferred this from the visible CSS and have not tested it.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. It has no effect in email.
- **Empty structured data:** the JSON-LD contains `"discountCode": ""` and an empty `PromotionCard`. Clients may ignore it, but it carries no usable data.
- **Missing text fallback (QA FAIL):** the plain-text part is 0 chars. This hurts deliverability and text-only clients. It may be a relay capture artifact, so check the raw MIME.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **QA FAIL, confirmed:** the greeting renders with an empty name field ("Hi ,"). There is no fallback value for a missing first name, so the greeting breaks for recipients without a name on file.
- I saw no other unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FNAME]`) in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unverified. If the headers are really absent, one-click unsubscribe is missing, and Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address:** the footer is cut off, so I cannot confirm either. QA says an unsubscribe link was included in the probe.
- The From domain `beauty.sephora.com` is a sending subdomain and looks consistent. This is not a defect.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- I could not verify UTM parameters on CTA links, because they sit in the truncated HTML and the probe skipped 37 links.
- The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=20261030_SC_30_off_Starts_Today_BI` on the pixel. It matches the subject and send date, with no mismatch.
- The preheader says "Save big now through 11/9". I could not check whether the landing page shows the same end date.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a fallback for the greeting (for example "Hi there," when the first name is empty), or suppress the greeting. This is the only confirmed recipient-facing defect.
2. Add a multipart `text/plain` alternative. First check the raw MIME to see whether it exists and the relay dropped it.
3. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` plus SPF, DKIM and DMARC by inspecting the raw message headers, not the relay copy.
4. Move the `<!--[if mso]>` block out of the media query so the mobile tier rules aren't dropped.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1`, add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and give the Epsilon pixel `alt=""`.
6. Fix the QA check so it treats `alt=""` as present, to stop false warnings on decorative images.
7. Re-run the audit on the full HTML, raising the probe limit past 12 links, to cover UTMs, the footer and compliance content.
