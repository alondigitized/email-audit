# Welcome to The North Kohl’s 🎄 Your holiday shopping destination 🎁
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the first Kohl's email in two weeks that isn't the maroon "Lowest Prices of the Season" template. I expected the thirteenth near-copy since 10/19, and it's a new "North Kohl's" holiday world instead. The elves, the snowy village and the cable car are charming, and I'll give them that. But I scrolled a very long way through lifestyle scenes before I found a price. The header does show my status, "KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER," and "5% REWARD." That's the only place it treated me like a known customer.
- The beauty counter is where I spend my money, and there's none of it here. The one Sephora mention is buried in the footer. I did find real deals: a $10 off jewelry coupon, 60% off plus 20% off, a Ninja SLUSH at $299.99, and "Take an extra 20% off" with a code. They sit in the bottom third, though. Macy's puts the offer and the deadline up top, in the subject line.
- I'd skim it and probably click once, on the jewelry coupon. I wouldn't forward it. It's a pretty brand-story email, but I'm here for the points and the price, and they're hiding.

## What's working

- I saw my "KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER" status and "5% REWARD" at the very top, which is rare. Sephora Insider makes me hunt for mine.
- The "Ends Nov. 1" tag next to Kohl's Cash at the top gives me a believable deadline.
- Coupon codes (SAVE20, DOSHOPF10, JEWELRY10) and barcodes at the bottom are ready to use. I don't have to guess how to redeem them.
- "Picked with you in mind" shows actual prices: $299.99 against the $349.99 original, a $2.99–$9.99 towel, and $52 jeans. That feels honest.
- The "Free returns through January 31, 2027" line is exactly what a person who returns at the event cares about.

## What's weak

- Three full scenes of families and cozy boots go by before any offer, and that's too much storytelling. Neiman Marcus' "Wedding-guest elegance" has the same lookbook problem: lovely pictures, no number.
- The hero is a decorative "Welcome to the North Kohl's" banner with no savings in it. The coupon is below it, and the real discount is further down still.
- Nothing here is for the beauty counter. The Sephora at Kohl's mention is only footer text. Sephora Insider would have put a beauty hook at the top.
- The offers contradict each other. "Ends Nov. 1" for Kohl's Cash, a "20% off" code, "Coupons do not apply to certain brands," and a pile of fine print make me work to see what's real. That's the Macy's-style overload I dislike.
- The email is very long. The jewelry offer has to be read twice: $10 off $50, and 60% off plus 20% off with a coupon.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one clear offer, with the code and the date, in the first screen. The elf banner can sit under it.
- 2. Swap one lifestyle scene for a beauty or Sephora at Kohl's block aimed at someone like me.
- 3. Merge the coupon blocks into one "Your offers" strip tied to my rewards status. Show my balance and what I earn.
- 4. Cut the fine print down to one line and link to the rest.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 20% off + $10 jewelry coupon, ends Nov. 1`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Rewards: Kohl's Cash + free returns thru Jan. 31`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code SAVE20 inside, plus your 5% Rewards on holiday gifts.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Kohl's holiday send (`261026_DG_Email_Monday`) with ESP-managed click tracking and several tracking pixels. The HTML has a few markup defects and missing alt text on content images. The compliance and authentication warnings can't be confirmed from the data provided, because the HTML is truncated and the relay doesn't expose headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **66 tracking links were not probed.** All of them use redirect domains such as `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…`. The QA tool could not check destinations, status codes or redirect chains. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Several beacons load on open.** These are `click.chp.kohls.com/o/5f56d143-…`, `mi.kohls.com/p/up/…/o.gif`, `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…` and `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`. Four open-tracking calls to three vendors is heavy. It also means open-rate data will be inflated or fragmented, and Apple MPP and image blocking will skew it.
- **Stray closing tag.** `<img src="…open.aspx?qs=…" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>` has an unmatched `</custom>`. It is invalid markup, though browsers and mail clients usually ignore it.
- **Empty parameter.** The `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/916d6fa08d185233.png` URL contains `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` with no value. This may be an unresolved dynamic field. The URL is truncated, so I can't confirm it.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images.** QA flagged `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png` and `59199073_Product_hdgt30_HolidayGifting_Hero_GIF`. Some of these are `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/` dynamic render images. If they carry the content, images-off and screen-reader users get nothing.
- **Tracking pixels.** `click.chp.kohls.com/o/…` and `kohls.demdex.net/event` have no `alt` attribute.
- **False positive in QA.** `o.gif` is flagged, but the source shows `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, which is correct for a pixel.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** Screen readers and some clients have nothing to announce.
- **Viewport meta blocks zoom.** `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` stops pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Invalid `<head>` content.** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">…</div>` sits inside `<head>`. `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href, which can trigger a stray request to the page URL.
- **Fixed-width tables.** The tables use `width="640"` with a `.container` class and an `@media (max-width:500px)` override. This is a standard pattern and fine.
- **Web fonts.** Roboto and Montserrat are loaded through both `<link>` and `@import` inside a `!mso` block. The fallback stacks (`Arial, Helvetica`) are present. The double load is redundant but harmless.
