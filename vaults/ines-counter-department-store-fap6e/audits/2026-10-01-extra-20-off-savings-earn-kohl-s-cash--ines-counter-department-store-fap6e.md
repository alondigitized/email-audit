# Extra 20% off savings + earn Kohl's Cash ✨
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-01**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again. This is the fourth "EXTRA" send in a week, and I opened it out of habit. The hero is one word, "EXTRA," in red, and nothing under it says what you get. The subject promised 20% off, but the top of the email never says so. The first real offer is "Take $10 off your home purchase of $50 or more with coupon," and that's a different, smaller deal than the one in the subject.
- The "Cozy Finds" home section looks nice. The pillows and throws are calm and nicely styled. Then it turns into a long scroll of category tiles, "your 'just one thing' plan had a good run," a Rewards block, a "Picked with you in mind" grid, and four coupon barcodes at the bottom. The actual 20% code (YOULSAVE20, ends Oct. 4) is buried near the bottom. I'd call this a coupon sheet dressed up as a campaign. It respects my time less than Neiman's did. Their Friends & Family hero said "25% off" and had one "Shop the Event" button. I'd skim this and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The status bar at the top reads "Kohl's Rewards' Member" and "Earn: 5% Rewards." It knows who I am, and that matters to someone who won't use a card without points back.
- The "Cozy Finds" home imagery is warm and well styled. The burgundy and cream palette feels seasonal for early October.
- The coupon section at the bottom has real codes and PINs with end dates (Oct. 4), so I can see the terms.
- The "Kohl's Rewards Member Exclusive" block with the "Maximum reward. Minimum effort." line is the one place the loyalty angle comes through.
- The "Earn $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 spent" line is concrete and easy to understand.

## What's weak

- The "EXTRA" header has nothing under it. This is the same hollow hero I got on 9/28, 9/29 and twice on 9/30. I still have to scroll to find "extra what?"
- The subject promises "20% off," but the first offer I see is "$10 off $50 home." The headline deal is at the very bottom, so the email doesn't deliver the promise made in the subject.
- Too many things compete: a Women/Men/Kids/Home nav, about 12 category tiles, a toys and kids coupon, a product grid and four barcodes. Macy's has the same overload problem, but at least their "confirm now" send had one job.
- Both "Take $10 off" coupons and the "Earn $10 Kohl's Cash" line use almost identical wording. I can't tell which stacks with which without reading the fine print. The "Coupons do not apply to select brands, details" disclaimer also sits right under the product grid.
- Sephora and beauty are listed as a tile, but the Kohl's Cash fine print excludes Sephora at Kohl's. For a beauty-counter shopper that feels like a bait-and-switch.
- Nothing here is aimed at a 47-year-old. The "Women" tile and a few product shots are the only nods. It's the same email everyone gets.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in the hero. Replace the lone "EXTRA" with "Extra 20% off, code YOULSAVE20, ends Oct. 4" and one "Shop now" button.
- 2. Cut the category tile wall and the "just one thing" block. Keep one themed section (Cozy Finds) and one Rewards block.
- 3. Move the barcode coupons into a single "Your coupons" wallet link. Four stacked barcodes read as clutter.
- 4. Say plainly what Kohl's Cash can't be used on, near the offer and not in the footer. A Sephora exclusion should be visible before I click.
- 5. Space out the sends. Five near-identical "EXTRA" emails in a week trains me to ignore them.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 20% off through Oct. 4 — code inside`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Rewards: 20% off + $10 Kohl's Cash`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code YOULSAVE20 on home, bedding and more. Ends Oct. 4.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Earn $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 you spend, plus 5% Rewards.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, 640px layout with an MSO fallback block, and its markup has several validity defects. Most of the compliance and authentication warnings come from the relay or from truncated source, so they are not confirmed defects. The HTML was truncated, so the footer, the body links and the destination URLs could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Destinations unverified.** 73 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they use click-redirect domains. Example: `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABB7InYiOjEsImQiOjUwMTZ9…`. The `qs` blob is opaque, so link health and final destinations are unknown.
- **Four tracking pixels before any content.**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/21566801-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` (no `alt`, no `aria-hidden`)
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1022927_2026101`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?AZQRI2OMZ5IETDNSCE5CNJBHMY.60262&d=60262&bmt=0`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager, no `alt`, `height="0"`, `width="0"`)
  - Loading several open-tracking beacons in series adds redundant requests. It can also inflate or double-count opens.
- **Stray closing tag.** The open.aspx pixel ends with `<img … alt=""></custom>`. `</custom>` has no matching opening tag, which is malformed HTML.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` prevents pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Invalid `<head>` content.** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. A `<div>` there makes parsers close the head early and open an implicit body. The `<meta itemprop>` tags inside it become body content, and Gmail strips microdata anyway.
- **Empty head elements.** `<title></title>` is empty, and `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty `href`, which can trigger a request to the document URL.
- **Missing alt on content images.** The following `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` images and one Scene7 image are flagged by QA:
  - `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`
  - `f441d45d2128ae53.png`
  - `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`
  - `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`
  - `69494b48a79b4240.png`
  - `78308d1d9adb0751.png`
  - `916d6fa08d185233.png`
  - `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/58819319_root_2x`
  
  If these carry offer copy, that content is invisible with images off and to screen readers. I could not confirm this from the truncated source.
- **QA false positive.** `o.gif` is flagged as missing alt, but the source shows `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`. The open.aspx pixel also has `alt=""`. The real alt gaps among the pixels are the `click.chp.kohls.com` image and the `demdex` image.
- **Broken legacy selector.** `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` is missing the leading dot, so it matches nothing. It is low impact.
- **Not an issue.** The Google Fonts `@import` and `<link>` are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` and have `Montserrat, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` fallbacks. Layout tables use `role="presentation"`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source. Recipient identifiers (`mi_u=604230016`, `d_sid=13245196`) are populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unconfirmed.** QA reports no `List-Unsubscribe` header, no `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058), no physical address, and no `Authentication-Results` header.
- **Why they're unconfirmed.**
  - The QA notes say the headers may not be captured by the AgentMail relay.
  - The HTML source is cut off before the footer, so the address and unsubscribe link could not be checked.
  - From a large retailer sending from `s.kohls.com`, a missing physical address is more likely a truncation artifact than a real violation.
- **Needed to settle it.** Raw `.eml` headers and the full footer HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs not verifiable.** Links are wrapped in opaque `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=` redirects, so UTM parameters and landing pages cannot be assessed.
- **Campaign identifiers in image URLs.** `email_name=261001_DG_Email_Thursda…` (truncated) and `mi_ecmp=1022927_2026101` are consistent with each other. The `261001` date encoding matches today, Thursday 2026-10-01.
- **Tracking domains.** All tracking domains are Kohl's-owned subdomains except `demdex.net` (Adobe).

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove `</custom>` and move the `itemscope` `<div>` (or drop it) out of `<head>`.
2. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Add descriptive `alt` to the content images listed in section 3. Add `alt=""` to the `click.chp.kohls.com` and `demdex` pixels.
4. Set a non-empty `<title>` and remove or populate `<link rel="shortcut icon" href="">`.
5. Consolidate the four open-tracking pixels to reduce load and double-counting.
6. Fix the `ReadMsgBody` selector to `.ReadMsgBody`.
7. Re-run the audit against the raw message and the full, untruncated HTML to confirm the unsubscribe headers, footer address, SPF/DKIM/DMARC results, link destinations and UTMs.
8. Fix the QA check that flags `alt=""` images as missing alt.
