# Up to 50% off deals + extra 20% off + Daily Deals = early Black Friday savings 🙌
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-07**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again. I've had one nearly every day this week, and the Nov 6 "Up to 50% off deals + 20% off + TODAY ONLY $15 Kohl's Cash 🥳" send was the seventh by my count. This one is the same story with the Kohl's Cash dropped. Up to 50% off, an extra 20% off and Daily Deals are all stacked in one subject line, and I need a calculator to work out what I'm actually getting.
- The screenshot is a very long scroll. The hero is clear enough: "Early Arrival Black Friday Deals", a big 20%, and a free-shipping bar. Then comes a category nav and tile after tile of pajamas, bedding, boots, vacuums, toys and sweaters. Beauty is a single small tile with a Sephora at Kohl's banner. At the Nordstrom or Saks counter I'm treated like a client. Here I'm one of a thousand people being shouted at.
- I'd mark it as read. I might click once, only because Sephora at Kohl's is the one thing that's mine. I would not forward it.

## What's working

- The hero is easy to read: "Early Arrival", a big 20%, and a "Shop Daily Deals" button I can't miss.
- The free-shipping line sits right under the hero, so I don't have to hunt for it.
- The category nav at the top (Women, Home, Beauty and others) lets me skip to my section.
- The Sephora at Kohl's tile is at least there, and the Daily Deals section gives me hard prices like $23.99 and $11.99.
- Nothing looks broken. Images load and nothing overlaps.

## What's weak

- It's an endless scroll of tiles, each with its own "up to 50%" or "+20% w/coupon". After the fifth tile I stopped reading.
- Beauty is almost buried. It's one small tile deep in the scroll, with no tier or points story and no hint of a beauty event. Neiman Marcus' Triple Points email with the train case at least told me what I'd get. Sephora opens cleaner than this, even when its subject line makes no sense.
- "Up to," "w/coupon" and "select styles" are everywhere. Macy's "LAST CHANCE: confirm now to get your extra 25% off!" is loud too, but it at least gives me one deal to act on.
- Nothing says "you" to me. There's no Kohl's Cash, no Rewards status and no mention of my points. I don't use a card that doesn't give points back, and Kohl's didn't try to earn mine.
- The tiles show mostly pajamas, kids' clothes, vacuums and toys. That isn't a 47-year-old beauty-counter shopper.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer and one deadline, and cut the tile count to a third. Stacking three discounts in the subject and then showing 20 tiles feels like a flea market.
- 2. Add a real beauty module near the top, with Sephora at Kohl's, a clear points or Kohl's Cash earn, and one hero product.
- 3. Show a member perk. If I'm in Kohl's Rewards, tell me my balance or my bonus.
- 4. Put the coupon code and the exclusions up front instead of in tiny print at the bottom of each tile.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's code has several hygiene problems: invalid markup, no `<title>`, missing alt text on content images, and a viewport that blocks zoom. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the AgentMail relay and the truncated source, so they can't be confirmed as real defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Links can't be verified.** 92 tracking links on `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…` were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destinations, redirect chains and 404s are unchecked.
- **Four tracking pixels fire before the first content table:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/2ae7d0d6-…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager, `width="0" height="0"`)

  That is three open-tracking vendors plus an ID-sync call. It adds request overhead, and any blocked pixel produces inconsistent open data.
- **Stray closing tag.** The `open.aspx` pixel is followed by `</custom>`, with no matching opening tag.
- **Plain-text part.** 16,252 of 22,043 characters (74%) are URLs, over the 70% threshold. This is a spam-filter risk, driven by the long `qs=` tokens.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** The QA scan lists 12 images with no `alt`:
  - `59592198_2_2x` and `59592238_3_2x` on `images.am.kohls.com`
  - `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png` and `916d6fa08d185233.png` on `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/`
  - the 1×1 pixels `click.chp.kohls.com/o/2ae7d0d6-…`, `mi.kohls.com/…/o.gif` and `kohls.demdex.net/event`

  The `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` and `images.am` files look like content images, so their absence matters. With images blocked they render as blank space and screen readers read the filename or URL. The `o.gif` pixel does have `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, so the scanner's flag on it is a false positive. The first pixel (`click.chp.kohls.com/o/…`) has no alt and no `aria-hidden`.
- **Empty title.** `<title></title>` is empty, so there is no accessible document title in web view or some clients.
- **Zoom blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` prevents pinch-zoom, a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Invalid head markup.** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. That is invalid, and parsers will close `<head>` early at that point.
- **Duplicate and empty tags.** `<meta name="format-detection">` appears twice, and `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href.
- **Doctype.** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype combines with `xmlns:o` and `xmlns:v` attributes. This is tolerated by clients but inconsistent.
- **Web fonts.** Montserrat and Roboto load via `@import` and `<link>` inside the `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` block. Gmail and Outlook ignore these, but the `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` fallback is declared, so this is low risk.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source. The remainder of the HTML was truncated and not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
All four items below are unconfirmed rather than confirmed defects.
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)** weren't found. The QA tool notes the relay may strip them. Verify against the raw message at the sender.
- **No physical address was detected.** The source is truncated before the footer, so this can't be confirmed. Check the full HTML for a postal address.
- **Authentication-Results** is absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. The From domain is `s.kohls.com` and the tracking domains are `click.s.kohls.com` and `click.chp.kohls.com`, so alignment can't be checked.
- **Visible unsubscribe link.** None was seen in the supplied portion.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters can't be verified.** Links are wrapped in opaque `qs=` tokens, and no `utm_*` values are visible in the supplied source.
- **Campaign IDs are visible only on the image URLs.**
  - Parameters seen: `mi_u=604230016`, `mi_ecmp=1025438_2026117` and `email_name=261107_DG_Email_Saturda…` (truncated).
  - `mi_u` is a per-recipient identifier embedded in image requests.
  - Whether the same campaign ID reaches the landing pages can't be confirmed without resolving the redirects.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` and `images.am.kohls.com/…` content images. Add `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"` to the first pixel.
2. Fill in `<title>`, and remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
3. Move the `itemscope` div out of `<head>`, delete the stray `</custom>`, and remove the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon">` and the duplicate `format-detection` meta.
4. Rerun the audit against the full, untruncated HTML and the raw headers to confirm the physical address, unsubscribe link, `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
5. Resolve a sample of the 92 `click.s.kohls.com` links with a redirect-following probe. Check the final URLs for 200 status, UTM or `mi_*` parameters, and landing-page match.
6. Consider shortening the plain-text part, or trimming link counts, to bring the URL share under 70%.
