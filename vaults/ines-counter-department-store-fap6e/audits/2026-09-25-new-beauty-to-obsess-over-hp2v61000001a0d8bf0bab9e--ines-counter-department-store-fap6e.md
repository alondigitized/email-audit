# New beauty to obsess over 😍
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-25**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the classic Sephora "wall of new products" send — top-to-bottom tiles for Obart, Dior, Innisfree, M·A·C, Oribe, Lys Beauty, Sol de Janeiro, Unite Hair, Eadem — and it's a lot. There's a real hook up top (up to 50% off hair, code KIT), but it gets buried under nine new-launch tiles before I even get a second offer. I like that they flagged three "Clean at Sephora" products since I do care about that, but by tile six I've stopped reading.
- This is basically an amplified version of the "Clean at Sephora" email from 9/21 I gave a 6 — same clean-badge signal, but this time it's stapled to a merchandising catalog instead of a story. Compare that to the 9/20 fall routine email, which led with one real model in her 40s doing one ritual — that one felt like it was talking to me. This one feels like they dumped their whole new-arrivals spreadsheet into a template.
- Would I click? Maybe on the hair sale banner up top since that's a real, timely offer — but I wouldn't scroll through nine product tiles on my phone during a commute, and I definitely wouldn't forward this to a friend. I'd skim the top banner and archive.

## What's working

- The top banner's offer is dead simple: "Up to 50% off Hair Faves," ends today, code KIT — that's the kind of clear urgency Sephora nailed better here than in their fragrance-points loop I've been stuck in since 9/19.
- Three "Clean at Sephora" badges (Obart, Innisfree, Lys Beauty) are visible without me hunting — I do filter for that.
- The hero model shot for Libre Vanille/Santal Couture at least has warmth and a face, unlike the flat product-on-color-block tiles that follow.
- Colorful block backgrounds make each brand distinct enough that I can tell where one product ends and the next begins.

## What's weak

- Nine-plus product tiles in a row with no editorial thread — it reads like a catalog page, not a curated pick, and nothing after the hero targets my age or skin concerns specifically.
- No loyalty/Beauty Insider point callout until the very bottom footer — as a points-back shopper, that's the one thing I actually scan for, and it's an afterthought here.
- Price is only shown for a couple of items (Libre Vanille $155, Oribe $120 set) — most tiles just say "NEW" with no price, so I can't gut-check the ask before I'd click.
- Repetitive "NEW · ONLY AT SEPHORA" stacking tile after tile starts to feel like noise, the email version of Kohl's "EPIC DEALS" tile wall I keep logging.
- The Unite Hair "75¢ ENDS" text is confusing/cut off looking — reads like a rendering issue or bad line break.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to 3-4 curated picks max with one line of "why this, why now" instead of nine flat tiles — Sephora's own 9/20 fall-routine send proved they can do editorial when they want to.
- 2. Move a Beauty Insider points callout ("earn 2X points on new arrivals" or similar) right under the hero — that's my actual purchase trigger, not "new."
- 3. Add price to every tile, not just two — I won't click something I can't price-check in two seconds.
- 4. Fix or clarify the Unite Hair copy — whatever "75¢ENDS" is meant to say, it doesn't land visually.
- 5. Lead with the hair sale banner's energy throughout — that's the one section with real urgency; stretch that tone instead of dropping into a static product dump.
