# Take 20% off | Find fall home must-haves for less 🏠
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-07**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again — that's at least my sixth of the week from them, and this time they've pivoted from "ULTIMATE Clearance" grab-bag to a fall-home theme, which at least gives it a spine. The 20%-off-with-coupon hero up top is impossible to miss, and I'll give them credit for a genuinely pretty bedding shot — sage-green waffle knit, styled like a real room, not a stock photo dump. But two scrolls later the exact same red "ULTIMATE Clearance Event — Save up to 70%" banner I already flagged in Tuesday's email shows back up, and then it dissolves into a "picked with you in mind" grid that's a Ninja Slushi machine, a bath towel, a mattress topper, and jeans — which is not a curated list, that's four different categories thrown at a wall.
- None of this is really speaking to me. I'm the woman who cross-shops Nordstrom, Macy's, and Saks for beauty and buys at the counter event — Kohl's home-goods clearance just isn't my lane, and nothing here tries to change that. Compare this to Sephora's "Discover the art of body care" send I read recently — that one at least stayed in one category start to finish and felt like a catalog with a point of view. This one starts as a home refresh and ends as a warehouse liquidation.
- Would I click? Maybe on the bedding if I were actually redoing a guest room, purely because that one image is genuinely nice. Would I forward it? No — nothing here is a "you have to see this" moment. This gets a skim and a mark-as-read, same as most of what Kohl's sends me this week.

## What's working

- The 20% off hero is unmissable — big red number, coupon code, hard deadline (Aug 16), no hunting required.
- The bedding shot is actually styled well — sage waffle knit on a real-looking bed, not a flat lay. Best visual in the email.
- Rewards status is right at the top ("Kohl's Rewards Member, Earn 5% Rewards") — as someone who won't touch a card without points back, at least that's front and center, unlike Macy's confirm-now email which buried its loyalty angle entirely.
- Four clean category buttons (Home/Women/Men/Kids) up top mean I could jump straight past this if I wanted to — good navigation discipline.

## What's weak

- The Ultimate Clearance 70% banner is a repeat of the exact block from Wednesday's send — I've now seen this red diagonal-stripe treatment three times this week and it's starting to blur together.
- "Picked with you in mind" is not picked with me in mind — a frozen-drink maker, a bath towel, a mattress topper, and jeans have nothing in common except that they're all on kohls.com. Sephora's body-care email stayed in its lane; this doesn't.
- Two competing offers in one email (20% off coupon vs. 70% clearance) makes me unsure which one is actually the real deal — that's the same "which sale is this again" confusion I called out in Tuesday's mashup send.
- Nothing here acknowledges I'm a beauty-counter shopper first — no Sephora-at-Kohl's tie-in even though I know they carry it, which feels like a miss given who's actually in this segment.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one offer and lead with it. Either this is the fall-home 20%-off send or the Ultimate Clearance 70%-off send — stacking both dilutes each and repeats a structure I've already flagged twice this week.
- 2. Cut the "picked with you in mind" grid down to one category instead of four random departments — a slushi maker next to jeans reads like an algorithm, not curation.
- 3. If Sephora-at-Kohl's product exists, this is the email to cross-sell it — give beauty-counter regulars like me a reason this isn't just a home-goods clearance blast.
- 4. Move the coupon code and deadline into the subject or preview line so I don't have to open to find "ends Aug 16."
- **Subject Alt A:** `20% off fall bedding + Kohl's Cash — ends Aug 16`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Fall refresh: new bedding, kitchen sets, 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New sage bedding, kitchen sets, and $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 — coupon inside`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your coupon (GET20) is loaded — shop fall home before Aug 16`
