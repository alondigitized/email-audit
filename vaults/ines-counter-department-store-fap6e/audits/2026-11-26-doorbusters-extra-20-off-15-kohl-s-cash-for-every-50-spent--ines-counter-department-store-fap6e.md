# Doorbusters 🛍️ Extra 20% off 🛍️ $15 Kohl's Cash for every $50 spent 💸
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again, and it's Thanksgiving morning. I can't read most of this. The render is a very long strip, and all I can really see is gold-and-black blocks, Doorbuster tags and "Shop Now" buttons stacked on top of each other. The "Happy Thanksgiving!" line at the top is a polite touch. It's followed straight away by about twenty tiles that all want my attention. Compare that with Sephora's Cyber Week email, which put one hook (the free sample bag) first and let me breathe.
- I did spot a Sephora-at-Kohl's tile (the eyeshadow palette at $14, regular $20), and that's the only one that touches my world. It's buried mid-scroll between luggage and puffer jackets. This is a Kohl's circular dressed up as an email. I wouldn't click, and I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read. The 11/22 through 11/25 sends already gave me this same stack of 20% off plus up to 50% off, so today's version adds nothing new.

## What's working

- The "Happy Thanksgiving!" greeting at the top acknowledges the day, which Macy's urgency emails rarely bother to do.
- The Kohl's Cash tile ("Win up to $100") and the free-shipping banner give me clear value signals near the top.
- Gold-and-black Doorbuster tags make the deal items easy to spot as you scroll.
- The Sephora at Kohl's palette at $14 is the one product that speaks to a beauty buyer.
- A category nav row (Women, Men, Home, Beauty and so on) lets me skip to what I care about.

## What's weak

- Too many tiles with no single hero. It's the same wall of offers I've had since the weekend, and nothing here feels like Thanksgiving-specific news.
- The stacked math is exhausting: "50% off," "+20% w/coupon," "$15 Kohl's Cash per $50," "Kohl's Cash do not apply." That's the Macy's-style overload I avoid. Neiman's at least bundled its three offers into one panel.
- The offer doesn't feel honest. The fine print about exclusions and coupons is crammed under each tile.
- Beauty is one small tile in a sea of luggage, toys and boots. There's no beauty-event signal and no clear tier or points story for me as a Rewards member.
- Tap targets and text are tiny. I can't read the product copy without zooming.
- This is the fifth near-identical Kohl's send this week, so the cadence feels like shouting.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to one hero offer. Pick Doorbusters or the Kohl's Cash and lead with it, with four to six tiles below, not twenty.
- 2. Segment by category. Someone who has been opening beauty should get Sephora at Kohl's at the top.
- 3. State the member benefit in one clear line, for example "Rewards members: earn $15 for every $50".
- 4. Put the exclusions in one place, in plain language.
- 5. Suppress sends after the first two in a stretch, or at least change the creative between them.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Thanksgiving doorbusters, 3 days only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `$15 Kohl's Cash for every $50 you spend`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Rewards members earn extra today. Shipping is free on orders over $75.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sephora at Kohl's palettes from $14, plus 20% off with your coupon.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Kohl's Black Friday send (ESP click domain `click.s.kohls.com`) with solid structure (640px container, `role="presentation"` tables, mobile media queries). It has malformed markup, several unlabeled images, a viewport that blocks zoom, and heavy tracking. Compliance and authentication could not be verified from the captured data. The QA run passed 38%, with 0 errors and 5 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **84 tracking links were not probed.** The `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…` redirects are opaque, so destination and status are unverified.
- **Four separate tracking pixels fire before any content.**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/b86d7766-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1026774_20261126`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`
  
  The demdex (Adobe Audience Manager) beacon is a third-party cross-domain call.
- **Malformed markup:** the `open.aspx` pixel's `<img …>` is followed by a stray `</custom>` closing tag with no matching opener (`…alt=""></custom>`). Some clients may handle this badly.
- **Possible empty dynamic value:** one image URL (`916d6fa08d185233.png`) contains `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` with an empty value. That looks like an unresolved Kohl's Cash redemption-end-date field. The URL is truncated in the QA output, so confirm it in the full source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` disables pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Content images with no alt:** `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png` and `916d6fa08d185233.png` (all `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/`). These are likely the offer images, so with images blocked or in a screen reader the offers are lost. The QA list may be truncated.
- **Pixel images:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/…` and `kohls.demdex.net/event` have no alt. Neither is hidden from assistive tech, though the demdex pixel is sized 0×0.
  - The QA warning for `o.gif` looks like a false positive. The source has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, which is valid for a decorative image.
- **Invalid head structure:** a `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`, and `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href. Both are harmless in most clients but invalid.
- **Doctype mismatch:** the HTML 4.01 doctype is combined with XML-style `/>` self-closing tags.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **No UTM parameters are visible.** Tracking uses the ESP's opaque `qs=` token plus `mi_*` params (`mi_ecmp=1026774_20261126`, `mi_cid`, `mi_mid`, `email_name=261126_DG_Email_Thu_Mi…`). Attribution depends on Kohl's own `mi_` system rather than standard UTMs.

4. Check whether `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` is empty in the actual send, and fix the template fallback if so.
5. Check the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment and the footer address, using the original headers rather than the relay copy.
