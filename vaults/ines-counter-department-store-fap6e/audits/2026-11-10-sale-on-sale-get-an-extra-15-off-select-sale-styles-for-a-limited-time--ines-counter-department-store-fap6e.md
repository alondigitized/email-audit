# Sale on Sale: Get an extra 15% off select sale styles for a limited time
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-10**
## Executive summary

- This is the twelfth Neiman's email since Thursday, and it's the first in a while that has an actual offer in it. It still isn't the one I wanted. Yesterday morning's "InCircle Members: Earn 3x Points" was the only send that spoke to how I shop. Today there are no points and no InCircle mention. I don't see anything that says Neiman's knows who I am.
- The hero is clean. The big black S, the gold field and "EXTRA 15% OFF" are easy to read, and "Exclusions Apply" is at least printed. But "limited time" is doing a lot of work. The real deadline, 11/11 at 10 PM CT, is in the fine print at the bottom. If it ends tomorrow, tell me in the hero. Sephora's "ENDS TODAY" email did that, and I opened it because of it.
- Would I click? Maybe, if I were already hunting for a sale boot or a MOTHER jean. Would I forward it? No. I'd read it and move on, probably with the same shrug I gave yesterday's "1,000+ new signature styles" send.

## What's working

- The hero is easy to read. "EXTRA 15% OFF" is the biggest text, and the black "SHOP SALE" button sits right under it.
- The nine-product grid is labeled by designer: L'AGENCE, Cinq à Sept, MOTHER, A.L.C., Gianvito Rossi, STAUD, Stuart Weitzman, FRAME, Theory. That's the designer access I expect from Neiman's, and it looks like a department store, not a clearance bin.
- The six category buttons at the bottom (women's clothing, shoes, handbags, men's, men's shoes, accessories) are big and easy to tap with a thumb.
- It's quieter than Macy's. It has one offer, no coupon-code scramble and no countdown clocks.
- Nothing is broken. The images load and the text doesn't overlap.

## What's weak

- There's no InCircle mention and no points, though my own history says that's what moves me. I won't put a card down without points back, and this email never acknowledges that.
- The only deadline is the fine print, "Ends 11/11/26 at 10:00 PM CT". The hero just says "limited time". It's the third Neiman's "limited time" email I've gotten this week.
- Nobody wears anything. The grid is flat product shots with no prices and no "was/now", so I can't tell what 15% off an already-reduced $1,200 jacket actually costs me.
- The subject runs to 72 characters, and my phone will cut it off around "select sale styles". "Sale on Sale" is the only part that's a hook.
- There's nothing for beauty, which is the reason I'm at the counter in the first place. Kohl's at least stacked a coupon on top of the percent-off.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the real deadline in the hero: "Ends tomorrow, 10 PM CT". This one change would do the most for the email.
- 2. Add an InCircle line, even a small one: "InCircle members: your points still count on sale styles." If that isn't true, don't say it.
- 3. Show prices. Put a "now $X" under at least three of the nine tiles so 15% off becomes a real number.
- 4. Cut the cadence. Twelve sends in five days trains me to ignore the next one. Merge "new arrivals" and "sale on sale" into one send.
- 5. Rename the "SHOP SALE" button something that tells me what's behind it: "Shop extra 15% off".
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tomorrow: extra 15% off sale styles`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Extra 15% off sale: L'AGENCE, FRAME, Stuart Weitzman`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Online and in stores through 11/11, 10 PM CT. Exclusions apply.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Boots, denim and blazers, taken down once more at checkout.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML source is a table-based XHTML Transitional template with responsive CSS. It has a leaked template-authoring comment and a zoom-blocking viewport tag. The source is truncated and the automated checks were largely inconclusive, so compliance, link and UTM findings can't be confirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** The QA run skipped HTTP probing on 29 tracking links ("Tracking/click-redirect domains — skipped HTTP probe"). Destination status, redirect chains and broken links are therefore unverified.
- **Plain-text part:** The QA run warns that 5517 of 6534 plain-text characters (about 84%) are URLs. This suggests the plain-text alternative is mostly raw tracking URLs. It may add spam-filter risk and reads poorly.
- No broken links are confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 resize-text concern.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang`, so screen readers can't pick a pronunciation profile.
- **Exact-match class selectors:** The mobile rules use `*[class=contenttable]`, `*[class=hide]` and similar. These match only when `class` is exactly that string. Any element with a second class silently skips the responsive rule.
- **Checkbox-hack hamburger nav:** The `.navbar-checkbox:checked~.menu` rules depend on `:checked` and sibling selectors. These are unsupported in Gmail and several other clients, so the nav may not work there. I can't confirm the fallback because the markup is truncated.
- **Dead code:** Three commented-out `<meta name="viewport">` variants and legacy `.ReadMsgBody` / `.ExternalClass` rules remain in the source.
- Image alt text and `role="presentation"` on layout tables can't be assessed because the body was truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Leaked template comment:** The source starts with an unstripped authoring comment: `<!--REQUIRED variables: @email ... @base_refid ... Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", "EMAIL_ID", ...) ... Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "uEm", ...) ... CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW -->`. It also contains `<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->`. These ship to every recipient and expose internal data extension names (`NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`) and field names (`EMAIL_ID`, `uEm`, `cmdid`). It also shows that the template contains an unresolved "choose one scenario" instruction.
- No unresolved `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` tokens are visible in the retrieved portion. Tokens in the truncated remainder can't be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post:** Neither header was found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is a possible gap, not a confirmed one. Check the raw headers at the sending ESP, because Gmail and Yahoo require RFC 8058 one-click for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not present, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. The QA tool attributes this to the relay.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** These can't be verified because the source was truncated.
- The From domain `e.neimanmarcus.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain. That is not an issue in itself.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- UTM parameters, `refid` values and landing page alignment can't be assessed. Links were not probed and the body was truncated.
- The comment references `@refid = CONCAT(@base_refid,"")`, so campaign tracking appears to use a `refid` parameter. Confirm it is appended to links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Strip the AMPscript and authoring comments from the output. Use server-side comment syntax such as `%%[ /* */ ]%%` or a pre-send minify step.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
4. Change the `*[class=x]` selectors to `*[class~=x]` or `.x`. Also confirm that the hamburger nav has a non-`:checked` fallback for Gmail.
5. Capture the raw sent message, not the relay copy, and confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment.
6. Rebuild the plain-text part with readable copy and fewer raw tracking URLs.
7. Re-run the audit on the full HTML with the click-tracker probes enabled. This should cover alt text, link destinations, `refid`/UTM parameters and the footer.
