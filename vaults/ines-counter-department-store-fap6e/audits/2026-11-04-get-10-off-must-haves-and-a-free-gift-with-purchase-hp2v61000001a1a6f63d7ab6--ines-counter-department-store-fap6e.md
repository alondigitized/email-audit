# Get 10% off must-haves AND a FREE gift with purchase ✨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-04**
## Executive summary

- This is the 24th or so Sephora email in about a week, and the subject and the body don't match again. The subject promises 10% off plus a free gift with purchase. What I actually see is a "Mistletoe Must-Haves" hero for lip balms, with the savings buried in small print above it. Same-day delivery with code SPEEDY is the only offer I can read clearly. I'd have to hunt for the gift with purchase.
- The scroll itself is gorgeous. The flavored lip treatments from Summer Fridays, Laneige, Eadem, Ole Henriksen, Kayali, Gisou and Experiment sit on rich berry and chocolate backdrops, and the Clean at Sephora badges are a nice touch. But it's a very long lip-balm catalog when I came for a sale. I'd skim it and maybe tap the Rewards Bazaar-style points banner near the bottom. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The "Mistletoe Must-Haves" hero is seasonal and has a clear Shop Now button. The eye lands there first.
- Each product has the brand, the flavor or shade names and the price right under it. I can tell what a $24 balm is before I click.
- "New," "Only at Sephora" and "Limited Edition" tags give me a reason to care without shouting.
- The new 250-point reward option is the kind of thing I actually shop for. A card or program that doesn't give points back isn't getting my money.
- The barcodes at the bottom let me redeem in store. That's my channel, since I buy and return at the counter.

## What's weak

- The subject line leads with 10% off and a free gift, and neither is the hero. This is the same bait-and-switch I flagged in the 11/3 "Want a FREE gift with purchase, Insider?" email.
- The savings and stacking language is tiny and sits above the hero. Neiman Marcus' "Buy More, Earn More" email puts one gold headline on its offer, and this doesn't.
- It's roughly seven near-identical lip-balm blocks in a row. Nordstrom would curate three. By the fourth block I'm scrolling.
- Nothing speaks to a 47-year-old. There's no skincare, no fragrance until the very bottom, and no tier or Insider status callout.
- The fine print at the bottom is dense and hard to read, and that's where the terms live. Macy's does the same thing, but at least it puts the code up front.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the actual offer in the hero. Show "10% off + free gift with purchase" as one banner above the fold, with the date and the Insider tier it applies to.
- 2. Cut the lip block to three products and move the gift with purchase up where the lip balms are.
- 3. Show my points balance or tier at the top. Saks and Nordstrom do this, and it makes me feel known.
- 4. Add one skincare or fragrance hero for the shopper who doesn't want a $24 lip balm.
- 5. Give the sale emails a break. A single weekly sale email would work better than daily ones.
- **Subject Alt A:** `10% off + a free gift, Insider. Ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your gift with purchase is waiting, Insider`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus free same-day delivery with code SPEEDY. See the gift choices.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Lip balms, fragrance sets and 250-point rewards, all before Sunday.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text fallback:** The text part is 0 chars [FAIL]. Without it, spam filters may score the message worse and text-only clients get nothing. If the relay stripped it, this is a capture artifact. As captured, it is a failure.
- **Missing alt text:** Both flagged images are the hidden tracking pixels above. The practical impact is low, but they should carry `alt=""` explicitly so screen readers skip them.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…, maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.

- **Invalid meta value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`.
