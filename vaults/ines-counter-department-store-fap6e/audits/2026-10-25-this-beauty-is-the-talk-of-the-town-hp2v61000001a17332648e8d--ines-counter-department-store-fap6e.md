# This beauty is the talk of the town 📞
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the thirteenth Sephora email I've had in about a week, and my first reaction was "oh good, another catalog." The top is busy. There's a black "Savings Event, Step 1" banner, then a free trial size offer, and only then the "Buzzy Beauty" hero. The Phlur Heavy Cream hero is pretty, and the "Clean at Sephora" badge is a nice touch. But I had to scroll through two offers before I knew what this email was actually about.
- The free trial size with code GLAMPICKS is the only thing here that felt like it respected me. After that it turns into a product wall. The Marc Jacobs Perfect shows up twice, once in "Your Personalized Edit" and again in "Trending for a Reason." The "personalized" label felt hollow. I don't see a single thing that says Sephora knows me, and my points status isn't visible beyond "You have 0 points." Zero points is a strange thing to print at someone who's been getting a dozen of these emails.
- I'd skim it, maybe tap the trial size if I wanted a Bobbi Brown sample, and then move on. I wouldn't forward it. Nothing here would make me text a friend.

## What's working

- The free trial size (Urban Decay or Bobbi Brown) is a concrete, no-nonsense perk, and it's the best thing in the email.
- The "Buzzy Beauty" hero is clean and elegant, and the Phlur products are photographed beautifully.
- The "Only at Sephora" and "Clean at Sephora" tags help me sort quickly, since I cross-shop Nordstrom and Macy's.
- Prices are printed right under each product ($99, $38, $168, $25). I don't have to click to find out whether something is out of my range.
- The Beauty Insider tier bar (Insider, VIB, Rouge) near the bottom is clear about how tiers work.

## What's weak

- The black "Savings Event, Step 1" banner is vague. What savings? Step 1 of what? It reads like a teaser with no payoff.
- The "Your Personalized Edit" label is false advertising when the same Marc Jacobs perfume appears again two sections later.
- I counted at least six or seven separate offers and sections, so the eye has no single place to land. Nordstrom's beauty emails give me one hero and one reason.
- "You have 0 points" in the header is a downer. It feels like a nag, and it does nothing for a card-with-points person like me.
- The fine print at the bottom is a dense wall of tiny text, and the free trial size has a $30 minimum purchase.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one hero. Either the free trial size or Buzzy Beauty, not both competing in the first two screens.
- 2. Make "personalized" real. If it's my edit, show me something I've browsed or bought. Don't repeat items.
- 3. Tell me what the savings event is. Give a percentage and a date, or drop the banner. Macy's at least tells me "extra 25% off" up front.
- 4. Show points in terms of what I can redeem, not "0 points."

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your free trial size is waiting (code inside)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Pick Urban Decay or Bobbi Brown free with $30. Plus Phlur's new Heavy Cream.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New from Phlur, Rare Beauty, and Summer Fridays. Prices inside.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML structure and CSS are standard for a table-based ESP build (Epsilon/Harmony). Automated QA found one hard failure: the plain-text part is 0 characters. It also found missing alt text on two images and two compliance headers the relay didn't capture. The HTML source was truncated, so findings below are limited to what is visible in it and in the QA data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- QA probed 12 of 60 links (48 skipped, ranked by click likelihood). It reported no failed or redirecting probes, so no broken links are confirmed.
- The first image without alt text is served from `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...`. This path pattern looks like an open-tracking pixel. If so, it is expected, but an empty `alt=""` is still better than a missing attribute.
- The 48 skipped links, including footer and social links, were not verified. Link health there is unknown.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA WARN, 2 images):**
  - The tracking-style image at `app.sephora.com/O/...`.
  - `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg` at `images.harmony.epsilon.com/ContentHandler/images/de0a3226-.../2021_Navs_Header_06`. This is a navigation header image, so missing alt text leaves it unreadable to screen readers and with images blocked.
- **Zoom restriction:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` disables pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resizing disabled:** the CSS sets `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` and `-ms-text-size-adjust: none` on `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td`. This blocks user text-size scaling in iOS Mail and Outlook.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index" />` is invalid. The directive is `noindex`, with no space. It has no effect in email clients, but it is a code defect in the shared "US shell snippet".
- **Generic title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` is the same for every send. Some clients and the web-view version use it as the page title.
- **Plain-text part (QA FAIL):** the text version is 0 chars. Clients that show text-only, plus accessibility tools and spam filters, get no usable fallback.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%`, `{{ }}` or `[FIRSTNAME]` appear in the visible source. The truncated portion was not inspected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe (QA WARN):** not found. QA itself says AgentMail may not capture this header, so this is unconfirmed, not a confirmed defect.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (QA WARN, RFC 8058):** not found, with the same relay caveat. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so this should be checked in the raw headers.
- **Authentication-Results (QA WARN):** missing, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status could not be assessed. QA expected this from the relay.
- QA reports that an unsubscribe link was included in the probe set. Physical address and footer content were not visible in the truncated source and are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable from the data provided. The truncated source and QA output show no CTA hrefs, UTM parameters or landing-page results. No issues are confirmed.

2. Add `alt` text to `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg`, and use `alt=""` on the tracking pixel.

4. Fix `content="no index"` to `noindex`, or remove it, in the shared shell snippet.
