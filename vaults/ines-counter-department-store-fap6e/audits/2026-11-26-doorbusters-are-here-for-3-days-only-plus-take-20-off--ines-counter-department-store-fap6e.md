# Doorbusters are here for 3 days only 👀 Plus, take 20% off 🛍️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again, and it's Thanksgiving morning. The "Happy Thanksgiving!" strip at the top is a nice gesture, but then I get the same wall of gold-and-black "50% off + 20% w/coupon" tiles I've had since the weekend. The screenshot is downscaled to a sliver, so I can't read the fine print. What I can see is a very long scroll of about twenty tiles. It starts with a hero for "Doorbusters, 3 days only" and then goes straight back to the usual stack.
- The doorbuster section is the one new idea. It has a $10 clothing tile, a $14.99 item and "60% off + 20% w/coupon" on bedding and sweaters. But it's buried in the same overload as the 11/24 and 11/25 sends. Nordstrom or Saks would give me one hero and one offer. Kohl's gives me thirty and makes me hunt.
- I wouldn't click. I'd skim the top, see "Coupons do not apply" on half the tiles, and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it either.

## What's working

- The "Happy Thanksgiving!" line with the Friday start time reads like a person wrote it.
- "3 Days Only" on the doorbuster hero gives me a deadline I can believe, since Black Friday is tomorrow.
- The free-shipping-on-$25 banner is easy to spot.
- The Kohl's Cash and Kohl's Rewards callouts, including the extra 10% for members, tell me my loyalty counts.

## What's weak

- It's far too long, and every tile repeats the same giant "50%" badge, so nothing stands out. It's the Macy's-style overload I avoid.
- "Coupons do not apply" and "select styles" show up on tile after tile. That's the fine print I resent, and it undercuts the "+20% w/coupon" headline.
- Almost nothing speaks to me. I see men's and women's clothing, bedding, electronics, boots and kids' wear, but no beauty. Sephora's Cyber Week email led with brand-specific beauty deals. Neiman's put "includes beauty & fragrance" right in the subject.
- This is the fifth or sixth Kohl's send since 11/22. The 11/24 "Enjoy up to 50% off deals" email and yesterday's "Save 20% + extra 10% off home" one already gave me this same pitch.
- Many tiles have tiny text and tiny "Shop Now" links. On a phone those are hard to tap.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to one hero, the doorbusters, with 4–6 products and a real price on each. Move everything else to a single "see all deals" button.
- 2. Say once, near the top, which items are excluded from the coupon. Don't repeat it on every tile.
- 3. Give Rewards members a clear early-access line, the way Macy's and Nordstrom do with their tiers.
- 4. Space out the sends. Once a day on Black Friday week is plenty.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Bedding, boots and sweaters from $10. Rewards members get extra.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Thanksgiving hours, free shipping over $25, and what's excluded.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has several markup defects (invalid `<head>` content, a stray `</custom>` tag, an empty `<title>`, a viewport that blocks zoom), missing alt text on content images, and a plain-text part that is mostly URLs. The compliance and authentication checks came back inconclusive because the headers weren't captured by the relay, so they are not confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **90 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped the HTTP probe on click-redirect domains, e.g. `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7Im...`. Destinations and redirect health are unverified.
- **Four tracking pixels in the first lines of `<body>`:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/0da07e4f-...` (1x1, no `alt`, not wrapped in a hidden container)
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=...`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (third-party Adobe Audience Manager)
- **Stray closing tag:** the `open.aspx` pixel is followed by `<img ... alt=""></custom>`. `</custom>` has no matching opener.
- **Empty dynamic parameter:** one image URL contains `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_p...`. A Movable Ink field is being passed with no value, which suggests a dynamic data point didn't populate.
- **Personal identifier in URLs:** `mi_u=604230016` appears in image query strings. This is likely a user ID, but the data doesn't confirm it.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` disables pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization"><meta itemprop=...></div>` sits inside `<head>`. A `<div>` there makes parsers close the head early, so the `<meta>` tags and anything after them are parsed into the body.
- **Empty `<title></title>`** and **`<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`** (empty href).
- **Malformed selector:** the CSS rule `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` is missing the leading `.`, so it targets nothing.
- **Missing alt text, per QA:**
  - Content images: `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`, and `60479404_1_2x` (`images.am.kohls.com`). Whether these carry content is not confirmed, but they are served as images from `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/`, which suggests they are content images.
  - Tracking pixels: `0da07e4f-...` and `kohls.demdex.net/event` have no `alt`. For `o.gif` the QA flag looks like a false positive, since the source shows `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, which is valid.
- **Layout tables without `role="presentation"`:** the 1x1 pixel table and the `<table width="100%" ... <!-- AAM -->` table.
- **Web fonts:** Google Fonts `<link>` and `@import` tags are inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` fallbacks are declared, so this is acceptable.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) are visible in the truncated source. The one possible problem is the empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` parameter described above. The rest of the HTML was truncated and could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the relay (AgentMail) may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` missing,** so SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `s.kohls.com` can't be verified from this data.
- **Footer:** the unsubscribe link and physical address are in the truncated portion and can't be checked.
- **Plain-text part:** URLs are 15,990 of 21,794 characters (about 73%), over the 70% threshold. This can hurt spam scoring.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. Links use opaque Salesforce Marketing Cloud `qs=` tokens, so UTM parameters and landing pages can't be read from the source. The only campaign tag visible is `mi_ecmp=1026777_20261126`, and it appears on image URLs only.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the `schema.org` `<div>` out of `<head>` (use JSON-LD or put it in the body), and delete the stray `</custom>`.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and `user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
3. Add a meaningful `<title>`, and remove or populate the empty `rel="shortcut icon"` link.
4. Add descriptive `alt` text to the content images listed in section 3. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels that lack it.
5. Fix `ReadMsgBody` to `.ReadMsgBody` and add `role="presentation"` to the layout tables.
6. Check that the Movable Ink `mi_kc_redm_end_dte` field populates, since it is empty in this send.
7. Shorten the plain-text version, for example by trimming tracked URLs, to bring URLs under 70%.
8. Re-test headers from a direct (non-relay) send to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass.
