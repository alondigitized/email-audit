# 20% off + Kohl's Cash ... thanks for being a Kohl's Rewards member 🙌
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-16**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again — and my inbox is starting to feel like Groundhog Day. This is the same three-tier discount ladder I saw in Sunday's Customer Appreciation Event and Wednesday's cozy fall refresh: 30% cardholder, 20% Rewards member, 15% nonmember, stacked in little red boxes right at the top. Then it's home goods, fan gear, a slushie machine, and jeans, all crammed into one endless scroll with zero point of view.
- There's a "Cozy Fall Goals" banner that at least has a seasonal mood to it, and I'll give them credit for the bedding shot — that waffle-knit duvet and stacked throws actually look nice. But between the NFL jerseys, the Serta mattress topper, and "the MVP of the bleachers," I have no idea who this email thinks I am. Neiman Marcus knew exactly who I was with that stylist story; this reads like Kohl's fired a shotgun at every demographic they have.
- Verdict: I'm not clicking, and I'm definitely not forwarding this to a friend. I'll skim the bedding section out of habit, notice the coupon code, and archive it — same as I did three times already this week.

## What's working

- The tiered discount boxes (30/20/15) are at least legible and color-coded so I can find my tier fast as a Rewards member.
- "Cozy Fall Goals" gives the bedding section actual seasonal styling instead of generic banner text.
- The bedding photography is genuinely appealing — soft, layered, gives me a reason to look twice.
- The coupon code and barcode are visible near the bottom for in-store shoppers like me who actually go to the counter.

## What's weak

- This is the same skeleton as Sunday's Customer Appreciation Event and Wednesday's fall refresh — I flagged the identical 30/20/15 box layout both times, and here it is a fourth time.
- Zero focus: bedding, kitchen appliances, NFL jerseys, and jeans all fight for attention with no hierarchy telling me what's actually "the" offer.
- The Kohl's Cash banner is just a flat green bar with text — no urgency, no visual pop, easy to scroll right past.
- "Picked with you in mind" sits directly above a slushie machine and a bath towel — that's not personalization, that's a random assortment pretending to be curated.
- No beauty or personal-care content at all, which is odd for a store that carries Sephora — Sephora's own emails at least center points and rewards, even when tone-deaf about my zero balance.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick ONE hero category per send instead of bedding + kitchen + sports + jeans in a single email — Macy's "LAST CHANCE" email at least had one clear mechanic even if the urgency felt manufactured.
- 2. Stop repeating the identical 30/20/15 tier box design send after send — I've now seen it four times in a week and it's lost all impact.
- 3. Move the Kohl's Cash earn-rate into a real graphic, not a flat green banner — right now it reads like an afterthought.
- 4. If there's no fresh offer, skip the send. Cadence fatigue is real — I audited this same brand three times already this week and each one blurs into the last.
- 5. Add a Sephora-at-Kohl's beauty tile since I'm a beauty-counter shopper — right now this email assumes I only care about home goods and jerseys.

## Technical Audit

Standard ESP-generated (CoherentPath/Kohl's Rewards platform) promotional email with heavy tracking infrastructure; header-level compliance signals (List-Unsubscribe, authentication) could not be verified from available data, and image accessibility is broadly absent.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 63 tracking/click-redirect links present (`click.chp.kohls.com`, `click.s.kohls.com`), consistent with standard ESP click-tracking wrapping; not independently probed per QA notes.
- Multiple pixel trackers present: `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...` (open pixel), `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...` (secondary open pixel), `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=...` (Adobe Audience Manager beacon).
- No issues found with tracking domain structure itself — all resolve to expected Kohl's/CoherentPath infrastructure.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 10 images flagged missing `alt` text by automated QA, including content-bearing images (e.g., `57448461_Product_fahm26_Home_Hero_GIF.gif`) and tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `event`, multiple `.png` beacons under `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/`).
- Tracking pixels lacking `alt` is low-impact (some already carry `alt=""` correctly, e.g. the `o.gif` and `event` pixels per source), but the hero product GIF missing `alt` is a genuine accessibility gap for screen readers.
- MSO/Outlook conditional comments and responsive `@media (max-width:500px)` block are present — no structural rendering issues found in the truncated source.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) found in the visible/truncated source.
- `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` query params on the primary tracking pixel appear to carry resolved subscriber/campaign IDs, not raw template tokens — no issues found.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- List-Unsubscribe header not detected — cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe support at the header level (QA warns this may be a relay/capture artifact of AgentMail rather than the sender's actual header).
- List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) header not detected — same caveat.
- No physical mailing address detected in the truncated HTML — CAN-SPAM requires this; cannot confirm absence vs. truncation without the footer content.
- Authentication-Results header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not detected — sender authentication status unverified via this relay.
- These four findings are flagged WARN, not confirmed failures — the truncated source and relay-based capture limit certainty.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Tracking links use `mi_ecmp=1021870_2026916` and `email_name=260916_DG_Email_Wednesd...` style params rather than standard UTM (`utm_source`/`utm_campaign`) — this is CoherentPath's native attribution scheme, not a defect, but means landing-page UTM alignment can't be assessed from this data.
- Destination/landing URLs are wrapped in `click.s.kohls.com` redirects; final landing page not resolved in this data, so continuity to the actual product/offer page cannot be confirmed.

7. Recommendations
- Add `alt` text to the hero product GIF (`57448461_Product_fahm26_Home_Hero_GIF.gif`) at minimum; decorative tracking pixels can retain `alt=""`.
- Verify List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post headers exist at the actual SMTP layer (not just via this relay capture) to confirm RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe compliance.
- Confirm physical mailing address is present in the full (non-truncated) footer.
- Verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC authentication results directly at the receiving MTA rather than relying on relay-forwarded headers.
