# A glamorous trip to the countryside

## Executive summary

- Now this is Neiman's actually behaving like Neiman's. No crimson block, no "LAST CALL," no percentage sign screaming at me — just a gorgeous editorial shot, tweed and plaid and a Brunello Cucinelli skirt that I'd actually wear this fall. It's the third different tone from this brand in three days — Thursday's giant calligraphic "S" for 40% off, yesterday's clean black-and-white new-arrivals send, and now this. At least they're not repeating themselves the way Kohl's does eight sends a week.
- The "Shop the Look" carousel is the smart move here — Chloé tote, Jimmy Choo boots, Tom Ford sunglasses, Cult Gaia earrings, all shoppable individually with the designer names right under each piece. That's how you sell a lifestyle to someone like me instead of just a discount.
- But there's no offer anywhere. No InCircle points mention, no event RSVP, nothing that tells me as a loyalty member I'm getting anything extra. I'd look, maybe click "Shop the Look" out of curiosity about that skirt, but I'm not forwarding this to a friend — there's nothing urgent or newsy to share, it's just a pretty lookbook page.

## What's working

- The hero image is genuinely aspirational — moody countryside styling, not a generic model-on-white-background shot.
- "Shop the Look" breaking out each piece by designer (Chloé, Jimmy Choo, Tom Ford, Brunello Cucinelli) respects that I want to know exactly what I'm buying before I click.
- Clean nav, no clutter — a relief after Sephora's cluttered "0 points" banner mess or Kohl's promo-stacking.
- Two clear CTAs ("Shop Countryside," "Shop New Arrivals") sit right under the hero without me scrolling far.

## What's weak

- Zero mention of InCircle points, tier status, or any beauty/style-event tie-in — as a member I expect at least a nod to my status, the way Macy's at least tries with its "confirm now" loyalty framing.
- No offer of any kind — fine for a lookbook, but it does nothing to move me toward a purchase today.
- "Shop the Look" is a plain text link, easy to miss under all that product imagery — should be a button like the ones above.
- Subject line "A glamorous trip to the countryside" is pure mood copy, no product or urgency hook to make me open it over anything else in my inbox.

- 1. Add an InCircle callout — even a single line like "Earn 2x points on Countryside styles through Sunday" would turn a browse into a reason to buy now.
- 2. Turn "Shop the Look" into a bordered button matching the two above it — text links get skipped.
- 3. Give the subject a concrete hook tied to the product, not just mood.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Tweed, plaid & Brunello Cucinelli: your fall countryside edit`
