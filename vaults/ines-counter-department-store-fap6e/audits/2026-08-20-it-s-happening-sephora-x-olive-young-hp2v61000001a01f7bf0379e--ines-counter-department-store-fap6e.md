# It’s happening: Sephora x OLIVE YOUNG ⚡
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-20**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this one's actually kind of fun, but it is not for me — and Sephora knows it, because the "0 points" bar is right back at the top again, same as the 8/19 and 8/16 sends. This is a K-beauty collab with Olive Young, and it's a lot of unfamiliar brand names — Reljaran, Thomd, Arencia, Beplain — stacked in a grid like a "Hall of Fame" I've never heard of. If I were 27 and into the K-beauty aisle at Ulta, I'd probably be into this. As a 47-year-old who shops the counter for La Mer and Estée Lauder, this reads like it landed in the wrong inbox.
- What I'll give it: it's visually clean, the free tote gift-with-purchase is a real hook, and "Spotted IRL" with festival photos gives it some social proof energy that most of these blast emails skip. But between the unfamiliar product grid and my Insider balance parked at zero for the third email running, I'm scrolling past this one, not clicking. Not forwarding — unless it's to my niece, who actually knows what a "Cream Moisturizer" from a brand called Bioveal Boh is.

## What's working

- The hero banner is legible and the "SHOP THE BEST" CTA is impossible to miss — bold black button, high contrast.
- The free insulated tote gift-with-purchase is a concrete, specific offer with a clear threshold ($35+), not vague "treat yourself" language.
- "Spotted IRL" with real festival crowd photos is a trust cue I don't see from Neiman Marcus or Kohl's — it makes the collab feel like an actual event, not just an inbox blast.
- Product grid has real names, sizes, and prices visible (not lorem ipsum), so at least the mechanics are clean.

## What's weak

- My Insider balance sits at "0 points" again — third time in five days (8/16, 8/19, now this). It's stopped feeling like an oversight and started feeling like Sephora doesn't know who I am.
- The entire "Hall of Fame" product grid is K-beauty brands I don't recognize — no anchor to anything I've bought before, no "because you liked X" logic.
- Nothing here says "beauty counter," "insider tier," or anything that nods to how I actually shop — it's youth-market K-beauty top to bottom.
- Compare this to Macy's "LAST CHANCE" email — clunky, sure, but at least it was built around an offer I could act on immediately. This is built around a brand story I have zero context for.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment this send — pull Insider members with no K-beauty purchase history out of this list entirely, or at minimum swap the "Hall of Fame" grid for products tied to what I've actually bought.
- 2. Fix the zero-balance bug or suppress the points bar for accounts at 0 — three sends in a row is now a pattern, not a glitch, and it's actively souring me on every email that follows.
- 3. Add one line of context for someone unfamiliar with the brands — "New to K-beauty? Start here" — instead of assuming I already know what Arencia or Biopla are.
- 4. Move "Spotted IRL" higher — it's the most emotionally engaging section and it's buried two-thirds down.
