# 😍 Oh. My. Minis. 😍
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the eleventh Sephora email I've had in about a week. My gut said "another holiday catalog," and it mostly is. It's a nice one, though. The "Mini Sets for the Holidays" hero is clean, and the Laneige lip set at $26 ($36 value) is a real hook. Minis are how I try things before I commit to a full size.
- The email has no loyalty hook for me. I have 0 points, and the points line sits in a tiny strip at the very top. The "Choose a Trial Size" banner above the hero is a different offer, and the code KBEAUTY is buried in the fine print. The hero then points at holiday minis. Sephora is running two stories at once. The Halloween services and Free Samples block lower down make it three. Compare Macy's "LAST CHANCE: confirm now to get your extra 25% off!" It was pushy, but it gave me one number and one deadline.
- Would I click? Probably, on the Laneige or Kérastase minis, because I like a gift set. Would I forward it? No. It's a pleasant scroll with no reason to act today. I'd mark it read and maybe come back if I'm already shopping for a gift.

## What's working

- The "Mini Sets for the Holidays" hero is clean and seasonal, with one button, "SHOP NOW."
- Every product tile shows the price next to the "$ value." The Laneige lip set at $26 ($36 value) and the Kérastase set at $73 ($106 value) let me see the savings without clicking.
- The "NEW • LIMITED EDITION" and "ONLY AT SEPHORA" tags give me a reason to buy here instead of at Nordstrom or Saks.
- The Kérastase, Moroccanoil and Charlotte Tilbury names are brands I actually shop. That feels more like my counter than Neiman Marcus's "Fantasy" mood boards.
- "Gifts Under $50" is a useful, honest price anchor for the season.

## What's weak

- Too many messages compete at the top: the Korean skincare trial size, the free-shipping strip, then the holiday minis hero. I don't know which offer matters.
- The "Insider | You have 0 points" line is tiny. A customer who wants points back on every purchase gets nothing here. There's no points multiplier, no tier perk and no VIB or Rouge benefit tied to these minis.
- The Laneige section takes up a lot of space, and three of the minis tiles are for the same brand. It reads like a brand co-op placement, not a curated gift edit.
- The lower half is a pile of extras: Halloween Beauty Services, Free Samples with a $25+ spend, Gifts Under $50, the app download and the Beauty Insider block. It's a long scroll, and the fine print at the bottom is dense and hard to read on a phone.
- It repeats the formula of the 10/22 "Open for new items" and 10/20 "Beauty that's found only here" sends. Same catalog structure, no new idea.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story and one offer. Lead with minis, and put a single points or gift-with-purchase mechanic in a banner right under the hero. For example, "Build a mini set, earn 2X points." Move the Korean beauty trial offer to its own send.
- 2. Show my tier and my points balance, with a clear next-reward message, instead of "0 points" in small type. Treat me like a known customer.
- 3. Cut the Laneige block to one tile. Add a "Mini gift sets under $30" row with a deadline.
- 4. Drop the Halloween services and samples tiles. Halloween is over in eight days, and they dilute the holiday message.
- 5. Simplify the footer terms into a short, readable summary.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Holiday mini sets from $20, only at Sephora`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Mini gift sets under $30 (Laneige, Kérastase)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Limited-edition sets worth up to $106. Shop before they sell out.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Little gifts, just-right prices. Earn points on every set.`

## Technical Audit

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (3 images, per QA):**
  - `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg` (`images.harmony.epsilon.com/...`) is a content image, probably the nav header. It needs descriptive alt text.
  - `o.gif` and the `app.sephora.com/O/...` image are tracking pixels. Give them `alt=""`, which marks them decorative for screen readers.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index" />`. The valid token is `noindex`. This is low impact, since email clients ignore it, but it matters if a web-view version is hosted.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` is not specific to this email. This is minor.
- **Deprecated CSS:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` is applied broadly and disables text resizing in WebKit clients. Prefer `100%`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible source. The truncated portion was not checked.

## 5. Compliance

- **[WARN] No `List-Unsubscribe` header, and no `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058).** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so verify against the raw original message.
