# Save 20% … now’s the time to shop!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-09**
## Executive summary

- Forty-seven. That's my rough count of Kohl's in my inbox since Memorial Day, and today they walk in with 20% off after spending the last two days trying to convince me that 30% was the deal of the season. Yesterday it was "HOURS LEFT for 30% off." The day before, "ENDS TODAY: 30% off." Now it's 20%? I know how to count backwards, and so do most women my age who watch a sale. That offer regression is the single loudest thing this email says, and it says it without meaning to.
- The email itself is fine in a generic mass-merchant way. Summer dresses up top, four category rows stacked below, a coupon barcode at the bottom, Kohl's Cash called out in the banner. It's tidy, it loads clean, and it does what Kohl's always does: give me everything and hope something sticks. The problem is I'm not a "something" shopper. I cross-shop Nordstrom, Macy's, and Saks. When Neiman Marcus sent me a Bottega Veneta beauty feature last week and Macy's sent me a triggered confirmation email with real account-specific urgency, this reads like a circular flyer with a barcode stapled to it.
- I will mark it read. I will not click. I would not forward — there's nothing here my friends who shop the Nordstrom Anniversary Sale would find worth sharing.

## What's working

- **Kohl's Cash banner is immediately visible** — for the subset of Kohl's loyalists who are counting their cash balance, that header placement is earned and correct.
- **Summer dress hero is seasonally right** — it's July, dresses make sense, and the stripe styling is clean enough.
- **No render bugs** — the email displays cleanly, images loaded, layout held.
- **Barcode coupon is a real redemption mechanism** — showing the actual barcode in the email removes a step between intent and action. Macy's does this well too, and Kohl's matches it here.
- **Price transparency on the dress callout** — $19.99 vs. Reg. $39.99–$43.99 gives me a real number to hold.

## What's weak

- **The offer stepped down from 30% to 20%** — I literally got "HOURS LEFT for 30% off" yesterday. Today's 20% feels like a penalty for opening. That is the opposite of how you reward a subscriber.
- **Beauty is buried in the fourth content row** — it sits between Home and Shoes in a tiny thumbnail strip. I'm a beauty counter regular. This is not how you speak to me.
- **"Now's the time to shop" is not urgency, it's filler** — compare to yesterday's "HOURS LEFT" which at least pretended to have a clock. This one says nothing.
- **Models skew young** — the women's apparel imagery reads junior/contemporary, not the 45–55 woman who actually has a Kohl's card and shops brand events.
- **No loyalty tier or Kohl's Cash balance visibility** — the banner says "earn Kohl's Cash" generically. Macy's confirmed-email last week at least knew I was a Star card member. Kohl's doesn't seem to know I exist.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Never send a lower discount the day after a higher one.** If 30% was a limited event, the follow-up should be something new — a different category, a new arrival story, a loyalty perk — not a 20% off everything email that reads like a markdown from the markdown.
- 2. **Lead the beauty section.** If I'm in your loyalty database as someone who buys beauty at the counter, the hero should rotate. A dedicated Beauty row at position two — not buried at position four — would actually make me look twice.
- 3. **Add real urgency copy to the barcode.** The coupon section says "The savings already started" but the barcode fine print has actual dates. Surface them. "Valid through Sunday 7/13" in the body copy would stop the scroll.
- 4. **Swap "Shop Now" for category-specific CTAs.** "Shop Summer Dresses" and "Shop Beauty" would target far better than three identical "Shop Now" buttons stacked vertically.
- 5. **Age up the models in the women's section.** A single image of a woman who looks 45-50 in a summer dress would signal this email was meant for me, not my daughter.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Beauty picks + 20% off, just for you`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Valid through Sunday — tap to see your offer`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Earn Kohl's Cash on every brand, every dollar`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "Save 20%" Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email renders with one confirmed broken experience (unfilled merge token) and has multiple compliance gaps — missing physical address, absent one-click unsubscribe headers, and unverifiable authentication status via the AgentMail relay. Eleven images lack alt text, a malformed HTML tag is present in the pixel block, and Google Fonts are loaded redundantly.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Three open-tracking pixels detected — one via a malformed tag:**

| Pixel | Domain | Issue |
|---|---|---|
| `c0357954-...` | `click.chp.kohls.com` | Cheetah Digital open tracker; no `alt` attribute |
| `o.gif` | `mi.kohls.com` | Marigold open tracker; no `alt` |
| `open.aspx?7GTRL...` | `click.s.kohls.com` | Third open tracker; wrapped in a `<div>` that closes with `</custom>` — malformed tag |
| `event?d_sid=13245196` | `kohls.demdex.net` | Adobe Audience Manager sync pixel; no `alt` |

The `</custom>` closing tag at the third pixel is invalid HTML and may cause parse errors in strict renderers:

```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?7GTRL...">
</custom>   ← invalid — should be </div>
```

**22 click-tracking links** routed through `click.chp.kohls.com` and `click.s.kohls.com` were not probed — destination URLs and UTM continuity cannot be confirmed from HTML source alone.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — prevents pinch-to-zoom in mobile email clients that honor this attribute. WCAG 1.4.4 failure for low-vision users:
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="..., maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />
```

**Empty `<title>` tag** — screen readers and some email clients use the title for tab/window labeling:
```html
<title></title>
```

**11 images missing `alt` text** (per QA) — includes both tracking pixels and content images (`2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, and others). Content images require descriptive alt text; tracking pixels should use `alt=""` explicitly.

**Google Fonts loaded twice** — both `<link>` tags and `@import` rules load Roboto and Montserrat, adding redundant network requests on clients that render `<head>` CSS:
```html
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Roboto...">  ← link
@import url("https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Roboto..."); ← also @import
```

**Empty favicon `href`** — triggers a wasted network request:
```html
<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">
```

**Schema.org `<div>` placed inside `<head>`** — the `Organization` itemscope block is in `<head>`, which is invalid; browsers will relocate it unpredictably:
```html
<head>
  ...
  <div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">...</div>
</head>
```

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**[FAIL] One unfilled merge token confirmed in rendered source:**

```
%%offer_evnt_nm%%
```

This token was not substituted before send, meaning recipients received the raw variable string. Impact depends on where it appears in the template (likely a promotional event name), but it constitutes a broken customer experience for all recipients.

No other visible merge tokens detected in the truncated HTML.

The physical address absence is the most critical compliance item. CAN-SPAM violations carry per-email fines; if Kohl's footer was truncated from the HTML sample, this should be verified against the full source before escalating.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**UTM parameters not confirmed.** All 22 click links are double-redirect URLs through `click.chp.kohls.com` and `click.s.kohls.com`. The QA checker skipped HTTP probes on these domains, so whether Kohl's appends UTM params (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) at the redirect layer is unverified.

**Campaign identifiers visible in pixel URLs:**
```
mi_ecmp=1017141_202679
email_name=260709_DG_Email_Thursday
```

These are Marigold/Cheetah Digital internal campaign IDs — they will appear in ESP reporting but will not populate standard UTM dimensions in Google Analytics or other web analytics tools unless the redirect layer also appends `utm_*` params.

**`no-referrer` meta** (noted in §5) means even referrer-based fallback attribution is suppressed on the email side. If UTMs are missing from destination URLs, this campaign will appear as direct traffic in site analytics.
