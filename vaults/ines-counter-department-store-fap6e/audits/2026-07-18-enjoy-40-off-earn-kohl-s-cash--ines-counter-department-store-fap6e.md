# Enjoy 40% off + earn Kohl's Cash 🤩
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-18**
## Executive summary

- Email sixty. I know it's sixty because I've been counting, and today I already got one this morning before this landed — same Kohl's, same general pitch, except the number jumped from 20% to 40% and that actually made me pause. Not pause in a good way, exactly — more like the pause when you realize your favorite store has been crying wolf for two months and now they're finally crying louder. The 40% is real lift, and Kohl's Cash on top of that is genuinely the combination I wait for. But the email's hero is a man in a green overshirt. A man. I am a 47-year-old woman who shops the beauty counter, the junior career section, the shoe wall. Kohl's has my purchase history. And they led with a man.
- The Back to School block below the hero is fine for parents, but it's the default layout I've seen since early July — every brand does this pivot in the third week, including Macy's and Neiman Marcus. The coupon barcode at the bottom is useful; I'd screenshot it and walk in-store with it. But I shouldn't have to scroll past a male model and a back-to-school kids spread to find the offer that was in the subject line.
- I'd click through to the women's section, reluctantly. I wouldn't forward this. I'd screenshot the coupon.

## What's working

- **40% is a genuine break from the pattern.** Two months of 20%-off drumbeats, then this — it's the first time I've actually stopped scrolling. That differentiation earns attention.
- **Kohl's Cash visible** — the "Earn $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 spent" line at the bottom speaks directly to me; I will not spend money at Kohl's without that math in my head.
- **The coupon bar code is prominent and practical.** I can screenshot it. It's not buried. That's respecting my time.
- **"Shop Women's" is a real CTA.** It's right under the hero, it's a button, it uses a verb and a noun. Sephora's "Today only ‼️" send nailed this same mechanic — a clear, labeled destination.
- **Season is right.** Summer essentials + Back to School for late July is the calendar Nordstrom and Neiman Marcus are also hitting. Kohl's isn't out of step here.

## What's weak

- **Male hero model for a female-skewing list.** If my purchase history says I buy women's shoes and beauty, why is the above-the-fold model a guy in a neutral overshirt? Neiman Marcus's "Summer Aisle Style" at least showed me women's fashion.
- **40% off is not visible until I scroll.** The subject promises it; the email opens on product, not the offer. The discount needs to be hero-level, not coupon-footer-level.
- **Back to School occupies the middle third with no relevance hook for me.** No kids' ages in my file, presumably. Macy's at least gates BTS content to a secondary section and leads with the adult offer.
- **No urgency, no deadline, anywhere.** "Enjoy" is not a time-bounded word. Even the Sephora "Today only" cadence creates more pull than this.
- **There's a truncated "DEA—" element visible at the top right corner.** Could be a banner for a deal or section label that's clipping — either way it looks unfinished.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Swap the hero to a women's lifestyle image with the 40% offer overlaid.** If I'm in the target audience, the first thing I see should be me — not a man. Sephora does this well; their hero always matches the recipient segment.
- 2. **Surface the 40% in the hero block, not just the coupon at scroll-bottom.** Drop a bold "40% OFF TODAY" badge on the hero image, above the fold.
- 3. **Add a deadline.** Even "ends Sunday" creates the pull this email completely lacks. The Macy's "LAST CHANCE" email — for all its faults — at least had a timestamp I could act on.
- 4. **Replace Back to School as the mid-email block with a women's picks carousel.** Keep BTS in the footer or a secondary send. I did not come to Kohl's for a backpack.
- 5. **Fix the top-right render artifact** — whatever "DEA—" is supposed to say, it should be legible or removed.
- **Subject Alt A:** `40% off ends soon — plus your Kohl's Cash`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your best Kohl's Cash earn yet: 40% off today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Women's, shoes, and more — tap the 40% while it's here.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stack your savings: 40% off + $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50.`
