# Hair Daily Deals starts today 🎉 20–50% off select hair care.
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay, up to 50% off hair, free same-day delivery with code DELIVERED — this one actually opens strong, unlike the fragrance-points merry-go-round Sephora's had me on since the NEWSCENTS code first showed up on 9/19. There's a real model with textured curls up top, actual Briogeo product shots with real markdowns ($42 to $22.40 is a legible number, not a vague "select styles" tease), and a whole calendar grid of "Daily Markdowns" through October that at least signals this isn't a one-day fling.
- That said — this is a hair email, and I'm 47 with color-treated, thinning-at-the-crown hair, and the hero products are scalp scrub and bond repair, which is fine, but nothing here says "for mature hair" or "gray coverage" the way I'd want it to. It also runs long — by the time I hit the calendar grid, the perfume banner, and the value-sets banner, I've forgotten what the original offer even was.
- Would I click? Yes, actually — the Briogeo scalp trio and the Olaplex/K18 20%-off block are real, specific, and in my category. Would I forward it? No. Mark as read after skimming the top third, most likely.

## What's working

- The hero offer is immediate and specific: "Get up to 50% OFF Hair Faves" plus a same-day delivery code, both visible without scrolling far.
- Real prices with real math — $58 → $46.40 on Olaplex No.4, $59 → $47.20 on K18 — that's honest, not "starting at."
- The model in the hero has natural texture, not a 20-something with poker-straight hair — better than last week's generic Dieux shot, closer to what Sephora got right on 9/20 with that "urban decay" (their words, not mine) mature-skin moment.
- "TODAY ONLY" badge plus a calendar grid gives this real urgency without shouting — Neiman's, by comparison, has been recycling the exact same "FRIENDS & FAMILY" green banner for a month; at least Sephora rotates its hook.

## What's weak

- Nothing here speaks to me specifically — no "for color-treated hair," no gray/silver shampoo callout, no mention of thinning. It's a generic hair drop, not a segmented one.
- The email is exhausting by the end — hero, deals, calendar, 20% off block, holiday banner, perfume banner, value sets, loyalty tiers, footer. Kohl's does this same kitchen-sink pileup and I've flagged it every time; Sephora's usually tighter than this.
- The calendar grid is genuinely hard to read at this size — tiny type, low contrast maroon-on-maroon, and I'd need to zoom on my phone to know what's happening on 9/27 versus 9/28.
- The perfume banner ("Perfume's Latest Lineup") and holiday-text-alerts banner are completely off-topic for a "Hair Daily Deals" send — feels bolted on, the same overload problem I called out on Macy's confirmation email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the 20%-off value-size block. Everything past that (holiday alerts, perfume, value sets) dilutes a subject line that promised hair deals — Sephora's 9/20 send was tighter and scored better for it.
- 2. Add one line of segmentation copy near the hero — "for color, curl, or scalp care" — so a 47-year-old with different hair concerns than a 20-something sees herself in it.
- 3. Redo the daily markdown calendar as a simple list for the next 2-3 days instead of a 12-day grid nobody can read on mobile.
- 4. Lead the Briogeo block with the scalp-health angle for mature hair specifically — that's a real point of difference from Olaplex/K18/OUAI, who all pitch to a younger customer.
