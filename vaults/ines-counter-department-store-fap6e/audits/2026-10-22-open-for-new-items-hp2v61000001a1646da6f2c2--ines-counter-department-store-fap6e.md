# 🎉 Open for new items
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction was "new items? Open for what?" The subject reads like a store-hours notice, not an invitation. Inside, it's a perfectly nice Sephora new-arrivals catalog, but it's the tenth Sephora email I've had this week. After the 10/21 "FREE sample bags" send and the 10/20 "Beauty that's found only here" send, a plain new-arrivals grid feels like filler. There's no points multiplier, no code and no dollar-off. The banner at the top says "You have 0 points," which is a strange thing to show someone who buys at the brand event.
- I'd skim it because I like looking at the Saie and Kayali tiles. I probably wouldn't click, because nothing tells me why today. I wouldn't forward it either. If I wanted to tip off a friend, I'd send the 4X points email.

## What's working

- The big brown "NEW ARRIVALS" hero with one Shop Now button is clear. The cushion compact photo is pretty and I knew where to look.
- Prices are right there on every product tile ($26, $33, $49, $99). Sephora doesn't make me click to learn what a thing costs, which Neiman Marcus' mauve "25% off one item" hero also gets right.
- "$155 ($225 value)" on the Saie set and "$44 ($76 value)" on the Benefit set give me a savings story I can judge myself.
- The "First Access: Holiday Drops, 24 hours only" block is the only real urgency in the email. It also speaks to the holiday-gifting shopper in me.
- The Insider/VIB/Rouge tier bar near the bottom reminds me I'm in a program. That matters to me, because I won't use a card that doesn't give points back.

## What's weak

- There's no offer at all. The only exclusive tease, "First Access," is buried below eight product tiles. Macy's at least gave me "extra 25% off" with a deadline in the subject.
- The "You have 0 points" banner right under the hero is unflattering and gives no reason to shop.
- The tier bar at the bottom looks glitchy. "INSIDER" sits in a white field with a red marker, and VIB and ROUGE are grey and red bars with no explanation of what each tier gets me.
- It's the same repeated template as the other Sephora sends this week. Kohl's is doing the same thing with its maroon "Lowest Prices" layout, and both are wearing me out.
- No models are shown anywhere, only product shots, so nothing here says it's for a 47-year-old beauty-counter regular. The emoji subject also feels like it's aimed at someone younger.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a reason to buy today in the hero, such as a points multiplier or a Rouge/VIB early-access window, instead of just "Already obsessed." Move the "24 hours only" Holiday Drops block to the top.
- 2. Replace the "0 points" banner with something useful, like "You're X points from your next reward."
- 3. Make the tier bar a clean, labeled progress graphic that shows my tier and what the next one unlocks.
- 4. Give the hero button a verb and a noun, like "Shop new arrivals," instead of a bare "Shop Now."
- 5. Cut the grid to four hero products. Eight tiles of "new" without a point of view is a catalog, not an email.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage is partial.** 12 links were probed and 49 skipped. The QA data gives no pass/fail result for the probed links, so no broken-link claim can be made.
- **Open-tracking pixels have no alt attribute.**

  - Both look like tracking beacons. Use `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` so screen readers and "image missing alt" lint rules ignore them.
- **Tracking pixel carries a numeric user ID.** `mi_u=4970269939971072` is in the pixel URL. This is normal for ESP tracking, but it is a per-recipient identifier in a URL.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Header image lacks alt text.** `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg` (`images.harmony.epsilon.com/ContentHandler/images/de0a3226-.../2021_Navs_Header_06`) has no alt. If it carries navigation labels, that content is invisible with images off or in a screen reader.
- **Invalid robots meta value.** `<meta name="robots" content="no index" />` should be `noindex`. The space makes the directive invalid for any parser that honors it.
- **Pinch-zoom is blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="..., maximum-scale=1" />` prevents zooming on mobile (WCAG 1.4.4 concern).
- **Text-size controls are disabled.** The CSS sets `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`, `-ms-text-size-adjust: none` and `text-size-adjust: none` on `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td`. This stops user and client text resizing.
- **No `lang` attribute on `<html>`.** The tag is `<html xmlns=... xmlns:v=... xmlns:o=...>`. Screen readers can't pick the correct language.
- **Generic `<title>`.** `<title>Sephora</title>` doesn't describe the message.
- **Plain-text part is empty.** QA reports a FAIL with "Text version is 0 chars", so there is no `text/plain` alternative. This may be a relay capture artifact (see section 5), but treat it as a real issue until confirmed in the raw MIME.
