# Save 20% | Find fall styles you'll love at prices you love 🍁
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again, and today is already the second send. The first was "Step into the Season's Best Shoe Sale." The coupon header is the same one I've seen since 10/8: "20% off with coupon, Ends Oct. 18" next to "$20 off your purchase of $100 or more, Ends Oct. 12." At least the top says what I'm saving. The 10/12 shoe-sale send buried it in a maroon block of fragments. I also like seeing my status, "Kohl's Rewards Member, Earn 5% Rewards," at the very top. It's the only moment this feels like it knows me.
- Then the middle falls apart. The "Fall's most-wanted styles at most-wanted prices" headline is chopped on both sides. I can read "anted styles" and "at most-wa." Under that is a grid of Home, Women, Men, Kids, Shoes, then a Halloween-to-Christmas decorating block, then a Ninja frozen drink maker for $299.99. I'm 47 and shop beauty counters and designer floors. A slush machine is not my fall wardrobe.
- Would I click? Maybe once, for the 20% coupon, if I needed a jacket or towels. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and wait for the next one tomorrow.

## What's working

- The two offers at the top, 20% and $20 off $100, are big and readable with the end dates right under them. Neiman's makes me hunt for its "$50–$200" ladder. Here I don't hunt.
- My member status and 5% earn rate sit in the header. A points-back person notices that.
- The "Plus, earn Kohl's Cash!" strip and the "$10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 spent" line near the bottom (through Oct. 18, redeemable Oct. 19–25) explain the loyalty mechanic clearly.
- "Free returns through January 31, 2027" is a real trust cue. It matters to someone who returns at the brand event.
- The coupon codes and barcodes are all listed at the bottom. I could take them to the register without searching.
- The copy is friendly: "Three reasons to decorate. (Four, if this coupon gets a vote.)" is the one line that made me smile.

## What's weak

- The "Fall's most-wanted styles at most-wanted prices" headline is visibly cut off on both sides. It looks broken, and Sephora's The Chat reads far cleaner.
- It's too much stuff. There's a nav bar, a hero, four category tiles, a decorating block, four unrelated products, then three coupons. This is the Macy's-style overload I avoid.
- The products are random: a $299.99 drink maker, towels, a mattress topper, and Gloria Vanderbilt jeans. Nothing says fall style, and nothing is for a woman my age.
- The offers are crowded with conditions. The $20 off ends today, 10/12, while the 20% runs to 10/18. A "Coupons do not apply to certain brands" note and dense legalese sit at the bottom. The headline is loud and the fine print is quiet, which doesn't feel respectful.
- It's the sixth or seventh near-identical send since 10/8, and the second today. The offer is the same every time and there's no new reason to open it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the clipped headline and cut the middle to one hero, one offer strip and three or four fall products. The page is about four screens too long.
- 2. Put the best-priced item at the top as a concrete product, such as a $29.99 jean or a cardigan. Then add a real "Shop the coupon" button instead of generic "Shop Now."
- 3. Tell me up front which offer expires first. Today's $20 deadline is the only real urgency, and it's hidden.
- 4. Cut the cadence. Seven near-identical sends in a week trains me to ignore you.
- 5. Segment. Show me women's apparel, and Sephora at Kohl's, since you carry it. Without it you're a department store with no counter.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 5% Rewards + extra 20% off fall styles`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code EXTRA20 before midnight. Free returns through January 31.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Earn $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 you spend through Oct. 18.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML has several markup-validity and accessibility defects: an empty `<title>`, a stray `</custom>` tag, a `<div>` inside `<head>`, and a viewport that disables zoom. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the AgentMail relay and the truncated source, so they aren't confirmed defects. Overall QA is 43% pass, with 0 errors and 4 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverifiable tracking links:** 66 links were skipped as tracking/redirect domains. The visible ones (`https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7Im...`) use opaque `qs=` blobs, so destinations and UTMs can't be checked from the source.
- **Four stacked tracking pixels:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/5c3d3a53-...`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager)
- **Duplicate open tracking:** the multiple open pixels add weight and failure points. The first one (`click.chp.kohls.com`) has no `alt`, no hiding wrapper and is not marked decorative.
- **Identifier in URLs:** `mi_u=604230016` appears in the `mi.kohls.com` image URLs. If it's a persistent subscriber ID, it will be exposed to anything that proxies or logs those requests.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom. This fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>`.
- **Malformed markup:**
  - `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." alt=""></custom>` closes a tag that was never opened.
  - `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">…</div>` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will implicitly close `<head>` there, which pushes the following content into the body.
  - `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty `href`.
  - `format-detection` is declared twice.
- **Breakpoint mismatch:** the layout uses a fixed `width="640"` with `class="container"`, but the responsive rules only apply at `max-width: 500px`. Viewports of about 501–640px may overflow horizontally.
- **Missing alt text (QA, 9 images):**
  - The content images are `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png` and `916d6fa08d185233.png`.
  - The product image `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/58379268_2_2x` also lacks alt text.
  - If any of these carry offer or CTA text, it is invisible when images are blocked.
- **QA false positives:** `o.gif` and `open.aspx` already have `alt=""`, which is valid for decorative images. Only the `click.chp` and `demdex` pixels lack the attribute, and both are 1×1 or 0×0.
- **External fonts:** `@import` from `fonts.googleapis.com` is wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, and the font stacks fall back to Arial/Helvetica. No issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FirstName]`) appear in the visible source, and the tracking parameters (`mi_u`, `mi_cid`, `d_sid`) are populated. The truncated body and the subject have not been checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unconfirmed:** the missing `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` headers, and the missing physical address, are not confirmed defects.
  - The headers are stripped or not captured by the AgentMail relay, as the QA notes say.
  - The footer is truncated, so the address and unsubscribe link couldn't be checked.
- **Sender:** `Kohls@s.kohls.com` is an aligned subdomain of the brand domain. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- **Action:** re-check against the raw `.eml` and the full footer before recording any compliance finding.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Cannot assess:** links go through `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…`, and no UTM parameters are visible in the truncated source.
- **Campaign tag:** the images carry `email_name=261012_DG_Email_Mon_PM`, but only some of the `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/` URLs include it. It's missing on `69494b48…`, `78308d1d…` and `916d6fa0…`. This is inconsistent tagging of the Movable Ink images.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
2. Fix the markup: add a descriptive `<title>`, remove `</custom>`, move the schema.org `<div>` out of `<head>` (or use JSON-LD in the body), and drop the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon">`.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 8 content images, and `alt=""` to the remaining pixels.
4. Align the media-query breakpoint with the 640px container (for example, `max-width: 640px`).
5. Consolidate the redundant open-tracking pixels if the stack allows.
6. Re-run the compliance and authentication checks on the raw message source, with the full untruncated HTML, to verify the headers, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, the unsubscribe link and the physical address.
7. Resolve a sample of the `click.s.kohls.com` links to verify the landing pages and UTM parameters.
