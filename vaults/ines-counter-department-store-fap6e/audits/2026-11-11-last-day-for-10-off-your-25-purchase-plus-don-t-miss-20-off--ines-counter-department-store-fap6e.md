# LAST DAY for $10 off your $25+ purchase ⏰ Plus, don't miss 20% off.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again, and this is the same email I got this morning with the furniture rearranged. The subject says "LAST DAY" for $10 off $25, and the top of the email does say "ENDS TODAY." After that it keeps going: a 20% banner, Kohl's Cash, a military 30% tile, then a long holiday-decor tour. It's five offers in the first screen. Counter staff would call that a clearance table, not a counter.
- I'm a points-back person, and I did notice the Rewards line ("Kohl's Rewards members take $10 off your home purchase of $50 or more"). It's buried mid-email as a green strip over a Santa scene. Nothing here speaks to someone who buys skincare and fragrance at brand events. It's stockings, snow globes and a Ninja blender. I'd skim, maybe use the coupon if I were already in the store, and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it.
- Compared with the 11/10 "Stack 20% off + $10 off" send and the 11/11 morning one, this version is a little more honest about urgency. The deadline is real and it's today. But I've now seen the same barcodes three times this week, and urgency is hard to believe on the fourth send.

## What's working

- "ENDS TODAY" with "$10 off your purchase of $25 or more" and "Ends Nov. 11" is a credible deadline. It matches the subject.
- The "Thank you for your service" 30% tile is a respectful, specific message for the people it's meant for.
- The coupon codes and barcodes at the bottom (SAVEMORE, SHOP10, and the others) mean I can redeem in-store or online without hunting.
- The payment and delivery strip (Klarna, DoorDash, Instacart) is practical, and I can see it at a glance.
- The price-forward product tiles ($2.99 towels, $169 Ninja) show the price in a big number, like a store ad.

## What's weak

- Five offers stack at the top (20%, $10 off $25, Kohl's Cash, military 30%, Rewards $10 off $50), so my eye has no landing spot. Sephora Insider's "points = rewards" email opened on one calm green hero. This one shouts.
- The Rewards benefit is hidden under a lifestyle photo instead of leading. As a points-driven shopper, that's the line I actually wanted.
- Nothing in the email is beauty, and nothing treats me as a known customer. Macy's "confirm now for extra 25% off" at least had a single mechanic. Neiman Marcus sends me a mood board with no offer, and Kohl's sends the opposite: an offer pile with no mood.
- The middle third is pure holiday-decor storytelling ("Holiday magic is closer than you think") with no price or deadline. The email is long, and I had to scroll past all of it to get to the useful coupons.
- The fine print on stacking ($25 minimum, "with coupon," category exclusions) is tiny at the bottom. After the subject's "plus, don't miss 20% off," I'm left unsure whether the two stack.

## Recommendations

- 1. Open with one hero: "$10 off $25, ends tonight," with a single button. Move the 20%, Kohl's Cash and military tiles below it.
- 2. Say plainly whether the offers stack, as one line above the fold.
- 3. Lift the Rewards benefit out of the photo and put it in a visible member strip.
- 4. Cut the decor storytelling in half and replace it with fewer, price-led product tiles.
- 5. Use a button label with a verb and a noun, like "Use my $10 coupon," instead of "Shop Now."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Tonight only: $10 off $25 (and 20% off stacks)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your $10 coupon expires at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use it online or in store. Rewards members get an extra $10 off home.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `One coupon, one deadline: tonight. Barcodes inside.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible markup is mostly well-formed: it has `lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` on the main layout tables and an MSO conditional block. Several head-level HTML defects and four tracking pixels without proper alt text are confirmed. The compliance warnings (List-Unsubscribe, authentication results, physical address) can't be confirmed from the data provided. The source is truncated and the headers came through an AgentMail relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **77 tracking links were not probed** (QA INFO), so destination status, redirect chains and 404s are unverified. All `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=...` links are opaque encoded redirects.
- **Four separate 1×1 tracking pixels** load before the content:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/c5d6363d-...`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=...`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager)
- Each pixel adds a third-party request and an extra open or tracking signal. The first pixel (`click.chp.kohls.com`) sits directly in `<body>`, outside any table, with no `alt`, style or `aria-hidden`.
- **Stray closing tag:** `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>` has no matching `<custom>` opening tag.
- **Empty icon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty `href`. Some clients resolve this to the document or base URL and make a spurious request.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):** 7 of the images listed are `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` (`2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`). The QA output was cut off, so there may be more. They appear to be content images (`/p/rp/`) rather than pixels. If they carry offer text, that text is lost with images off and for screen readers. The pixels `o.gif`, `event` and `c5d6363d-...` also lack `alt`.
- **`<title></title>` is empty.**
- **Zoom is disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="..., maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Invalid head content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` with child `<meta>` tags sits inside `<head>`. A `<div>` in `<head>` makes parsers close the head implicitly. The schema.org markup is also of no use in email clients.
- **CSS typo:** `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` is missing the leading `.`, so it targets a non-existent element instead of the class.
- **Missing `role="presentation"`:** the preheader table (`<table width='640' ... class="container">`) and the 1×1 pixel table (`<table width="1" height="1" ...>`) lack it, although the main tables have it.
- **Doctype and syntax mismatch:** the HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is combined with XHTML-style self-closing tags (`<img ... />`). This is low risk.
- **Web fonts:** `@import` and `<link>` for Google Fonts are inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, and `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` fallbacks are declared. No issue found.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source.
- **Empty parameter:** one image URL contains `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_p...`. The redemption end date is empty, which suggests an unpopulated dynamic variable. Whether it affects the rendered image can't be confirmed, because the URL is truncated.
- A subscriber-level identifier (`mi_u=604230016`) appears in pixel and image URLs. This is expected for tracking, but it is a persistent ID in third-party-loaded requests.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** QA reports both headers not found, noting the AgentMail relay may not capture them. This is **unconfirmed**. Check the raw message headers directly. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment is unknown. Verify against the original `.eml`. `From: Kohls@s.kohls.com` is a subdomain, so check alignment there.
- **Physical address:** QA did not detect one. The HTML is truncated before the footer, so this can't be confirmed or refuted from the source provided.
- **Plain-text part:** 13,612 of 19,394 characters (about 70%) are URLs. Long encoded tracking URLs in the plain-text part can be a spam-filter signal.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- All links use opaque `?qs=` redirects, so UTM parameters and landing page alignment **can't be verified**.
- Visible campaign identifiers are `mi_ecmp=1025681_20261111` and `email_name=261111_DG_Email_Wednes...`. The date stamp matches the send date.
- The subject says "LAST DAY". Confirming that the landing pages show the same offer and expiry requires resolving the redirects, which QA skipped.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 7+ `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` content images. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
2. Remove the `<div itemscope>` from `<head>` and the stray `</custom>` tag. Remove `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` and fill in `<title>`.
3. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Fix `ReadMsgBody` to `.ReadMsgBody`, and add `role="presentation"` to the preheader and pixel tables.
5. Check the raw message for List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post and Authentication-Results, and confirm the footer address in the full HTML.
6. Check why `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` is empty in the image URL.
7. Resolve a sample of the `click.s.kohls.com` redirects to check UTM parameters and the landing offer.
8. Consider consolidating the four tracking pixels, and shorten or trim the plain-text URLs.
