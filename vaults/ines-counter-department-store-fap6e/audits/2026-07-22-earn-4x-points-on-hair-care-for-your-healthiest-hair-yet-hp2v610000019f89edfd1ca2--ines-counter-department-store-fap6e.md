# Earn 4X points on hair care for your healthiest hair yet ✨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-22**
## Executive summary

- Three days ago I opened a Sephora hair care email — same 4X points hook, same category — and I scored it an 8 because the points framing got me to actually think about my Olaplex and Dyson spend. Now here's another one. Sephora's marketing team has clearly decided that 4X points on hair care is the July 22 story, but I've already heard it. The creative is genuinely better this time — "Healthy Hair Isn't Luck, It's Science" is an editorial headline that treats me like an adult, and the breakdown by hair type (damaged, dry, frizzy, thinning) makes me feel like someone thought about who's actually opening this. The barcode at the bottom signals they want me in-store, which is exactly how I shop. Still.
- The problem isn't the email. The problem is the cadence. This is the fifth email in four days, and one of them already made this exact points argument. I'm starting to do the Neiman Marcus math — they hit me three times in July with a near-identical 25% off code, and by the third time it stopped feeling like an offer and started feeling like they were desperate. Sephora is one more hair care send away from the same energy.
- I'd mark this read, maybe come back to it if I'm in the mood to actually order Olaplex. I would not forward it. The July 20 bag edit with Charlotte Tilbury — that one I sent to two people.

## What's working

- "Healthy Hair Isn't Luck, It's Science" is a real editorial headline — it makes me feel like this is a curated recommendation, not a blast
- Hair-type segmentation (damaged, dry, frizzy, thinning) is exactly how I shop this category; I don't want a wall of products, I want a drawer I can open
- The in-store barcode at the bottom respects the way I actually buy at Sephora — at the counter, not always online
- The 4X points badge is a real hook for someone like me who tracks points the way others track airline miles
- Model imagery is on-brand and demographically plausible; she looks like someone who takes hair seriously, not a 22-year-old influencer

## What's weak

- The "Up to 40% off Select Lip Picks" section sitting inside a hair care email is a category collision — I nearly scrolled past the 4X points badge because my eye caught "lip picks" and my brain shifted to a different errand
- This is essentially the same promotion as July 19; when a brand sends me the same offer twice in three days, the second one reads as a reminder to ignore the first one
- No time bound visible — 4X points until when? The July 19 send had the same problem; if this expires this weekend I'd click faster, but I have no reason to act today vs. Thursday
- Five emails in four days is starting to feel like the inbox equivalent of a salesperson who follows up every morning; even when the email is good, the volume taxes my goodwill

## Recommendations

- 1. **Anchor the deadline in the subject and hero.** "Earn 4X points on hair care — ends Sunday" is a different click calculus than an open-ended offer. I need a reason to act now, not eventually.
- 2. **Pull the lip picks out entirely or give it its own dedicated send.** The cross-category placement isn't a bonus; it's a dilution. Hair science email stays hair science email.
- 3. **Add one hero product with a name visible at hero size.** The bag edit on July 20 worked because Charlotte Tilbury's name was front-and-center. Olaplex or a K18 or a Redken Acidic Bonding shot at the top of this would anchor the whole editorial.
- 4. **Acknowledge this is the second send.** "In case you missed the 4X points on hair care" is honest, customer-respecting, and would feel less like déjà vu. It's also a light personalization hook that costs nothing.
- 5. **Cadence check.** If the team sends a fifth-in-five-days email, the subject should earn that slot or it should sit in queue until tomorrow.
- **Subject Alt A:** `4X points on hair care ends Sunday — shop by type`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your hair type has an Olaplex moment waiting`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Damaged, dry, frizzy, or thinning — we sorted it for you.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Points expire soon. Here's exactly what to grab.`
