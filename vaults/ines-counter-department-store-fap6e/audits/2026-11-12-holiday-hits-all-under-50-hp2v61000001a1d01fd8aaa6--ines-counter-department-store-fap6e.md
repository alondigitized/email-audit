# Holiday hits, all under $50 🎁
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth Sephora email in a week, and my first thought was "which one is this?" Yesterday's "Exclusive beauty you'll have on rotation" had the free Gisou trial size. The 11/10 "Did you know that points = rewards?" at least spoke to me as a member. This one just shows up with a free-fragrance-sampler banner on top and a gift guide underneath.
- The gift grid is the strongest part. Laneige, Tarte, Phlur, Dolce&Gabbana and Salt & Stone each have a name, a price and a "$X value" next to it. I like seeing what I'm paying and what I'm saving without clicking. But two offers fight at the top. The "Spend More, Get More" fragrance banner and the "Gifts Under $50" hero both want to be the headline. Neiman Marcus's "Exclusive Access, 40% Off" lands one offer and gets out of the way.
- Would I click? Probably one product tile, if I'm already shopping for gifts. Forward it to a friend? No. Mark as read and move on.

## What's working

- Every product tile shows price and value, like "$26 ($36 value)" and "$50 ($77 value)". That tells me the discount without a code.
- The "Spend More, Get More" banner is a real offer. One, two or three free fragrance trial sizes at $50, $80 or $110 gives me a reason to add to my cart.
- The "Clean at Sephora" badges on Salt & Stone, Phlur and Lys Beauty tell me what's in the product without me hunting for it.
- "Shop Must-Have Gifts Under $50" is a clear button label, and it matches the Under $50 theme.
- The Klarna, Afterpay and PayPal block and the "Spot it. Shop it." module give the email a clear ending. They don't clutter the gift grid.

## What's weak

- Two competing headlines at the top, the fragrance spend-tiers and "Gifts Under $50". I had to work out which one the email was really about.
- The tier details and promo code are in small print at the top and bottom. At phone width I'd have to zoom to read the spend thresholds and the "online only" limit.
- I can't see the Insider tier or my points anywhere. The 11/09 "Last Chance to Save" send led with Insider savings, and I'm a Rewards Bazaar member. This treats me like a stranger.
- This is a very long scroll. The Shark Beauty and rhode modules and the credit card, free-shipping and app strips at the bottom feel like Macy's-style overload. Every one is a different ask.
- There are no faces or models in the products. Gift sets in packaging don't speak to a 47-year-old who wants to know how a product wears.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one headline. Lead with Gifts Under $50 and drop the fragrance tiers into a slim secondary strip, or send them separately.
- 2. Put my Insider status or points near the top, even as one line.
- 3. Cut the bottom modules (Shark Beauty, rhode, credit card, app). Keep the gift grid and one pay-later strip.
- 4. Make the fine print readable at phone width, and say plainly that the trial-size offer is online only.
- 5. Say when the gift-set prices end, so there's a real deadline.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering failure (an empty `Hi ,` greeting) and one deliverability failure (a 0-character plain-text part). Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be evaluated because of AgentMail relay capture limits. The 38% pass rate is dominated by missing headers, not by confirmed template defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All links are wrapped in ESP redirects (`https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a1d01fd8aaa6c336f4bbc780a0/...?__dU__=...`). Final destinations can't be seen from the HTML source.
- QA probed 12 material links and skipped 38. It reported no probe failures for the 12, but the 38 skipped links are unverified.
- There are two tracking pixels:
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...` is a hidden open pixel, inline-styled `display:none; mso-hide:all`.
  - `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=4970269939971072&mi_ecmp=20261112_Gifts_Under_50_Stitched` is a second beacon.
  - `mi_u` is a per-recipient identifier in a URL.
  - Neither pixel is an error, but both lack `alt`, as QA flagged.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Probable CSS parse bug:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {...}<![endif]-->` is placed inside the `@media only screen and (max-width:480px)` block in `<style>`. Non-Outlook parsers treat this as part of the selector. That likely invalidates `img.mso-size` and probably the next rule, `.mobileHeaderTierRouge`. The conditional comment belongs outside the `@media` block.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Fixed width:** The wrapper table uses `style="min-width:700px; width:700px"`. The visible CSS has no override for the `.tbl` class on mobile, which risks horizontal scrolling. The source is truncated, so this is unconfirmed.
- **No language attribute:** `<html xmlns=...>` has no `lang`. The `<title>` is the generic "Sephora" instead of the subject.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. This is harmless in email.
- **JSON-LD problems:** The block has `"discountCode": ""` (empty) and `"@type": "PromotionCard"`, which I believe isn't a schema.org type. Gmail will likely ignore it.
- **Missing alt text:** QA flagged both tracking images (`o.gif` and the `app.sephora.com/O/...` pixel). Use `alt=""` since they are decorative.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** The greeting renders as `Hi ,`. The merge field resolved to empty with no fallback.
- The test recipient probably has no first name on file, so this is a missing default rather than a broken field. The template still needs a fallback (for example `Hi there,` or dropping the greeting) so it degrades cleanly.
- The empty `"discountCode": ""` in the JSON-LD is a second unpopulated slot.

## 5. Compliance
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is 0 chars.** There is no `text/plain` alternative. This hurts deliverability and is an accessibility fallback gap. The relay may have dropped it, so verify against the raw MIME.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes AgentMail may strip these headers. These are unconfirmed, not proven absent. Check against the raw message at the original receiver.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown. Sender is `shop@beauty.sephora.com`.
- The physical postal address and in-body unsubscribe link are not visible in the truncated source. QA says an unsubscribe link was included in probing, but I can't confirm it from the excerpt.

- Destination URLs are hidden behind `app.sephora.com/T/...` redirects, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be confirmed from the source or QA data.
