# Hurry for 25% off select styles
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-23**
## Executive summary

- Same forest green banner, same "LIMITED TIME" stacked over that oversized "FRIENDS & FAMILY" serif, same cream "SHOP THE EVENT" button — this is the third or fourth version of this exact hero I've logged from Neiman's this week, and I flagged the identical creative on the 22nd. What's different this time is the "Hurry" urgency in the subject, but the email itself doesn't actually show me a countdown or ticking clock anywhere — just the same static banner with "Ends 9/27/26 10:00PM CT" buried in the fine print at the bottom. The product grid underneath is genuinely well curated though — Mac Duggal gown, Balmain bag, Stuart Weitzman boots, Polo Ralph Lauren shacket — that's a real cross-section of my closet, not random filler.
- The InCircle Double Points banner is a smart touch, and I do care about that since I refuse to shop without points coming back to me. But I've now seen this exact banner-plus-grid template enough times that "Hurry" in the subject feels like a marketing team panicking that the F&F event isn't converting, not a genuine reason for me to act today. I'd skim it, note the event is still on, and archive — not click, not forward.

## What's working

- The InCircle "Double Points" banner directly under the hero speaks to me specifically as a points-driven loyalty shopper — that's the one line that makes me feel like a known customer, not a stranger.
- Product grid is genuinely on-brand for me: Mac Duggal, Balmain, Stuart Weitzman, Polo Ralph Lauren — this is exactly my closet, better curated than the totes-only roundup Neiman's sent on the 21st.
- "25% off select styles + 20% off jewelry" is a specific, legible offer stated once, clearly, right in the hero — no burying it.
- Clean single-column layout, no overlapping text or broken images — tap targets on the six category buttons are big enough for my thumb.

## What's weak

- I've now seen this identical green "FRIENDS & FAMILY" hero and cream button at least three times this week (22nd's "friendly reminder," the F&F announcement, now this) — the subject says "Hurry" but the creative hasn't moved an inch, so the urgency reads as manufactured.
- No visible countdown or deadline anywhere above the fold — I have to scroll all the way to the legal fine print to find "Ends 9/27/26 10:00PM CT." If you want me to hurry, show me the clock.
- "Exclusions Apply" sitting right under the CTA with no detail is the kind of vague hedge that makes me trust the offer less — Saks does this same tone-deaf move and it never lands well with me.
- Nine products with zero pricing shown in the grid — I have to click through blind to find out if the Balmain bag discount actually makes a dent, unlike Sephora's approach of stating the exact point multiplier up front.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an actual countdown or bold "Ends Sunday 10PM CT" directly under the 25% OFF line in the hero — that's the single highest-impact fix since the whole subject line promise ("Hurry") is currently unsupported by the creative.
- 2. Rotate the hero image — reusing the identical green banner for the third time this week trains me to skip it on sight, the same fatigue I called out on Kohl's "Epic Deals" grid.
- 3. Add price or "% off" tags under at least the hero products (Balmain bag, Stuart Weitzman boots) so I don't have to click through blind.
- 4. Move "Plus, InCircle members earn Double Points" up into the hero itself rather than a secondary black bar — that's my strongest personal hook and it's currently underplayed.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your InCircle Double Points + 25% off — ends 9/27`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Mac Duggal, Balmain, Stuart Weitzman — 25% off, plus double InCircle points`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Event ends Sunday at 10PM CT — shop before it's gone`
