# Reminder: Earn a $50 gift card for every $200 spent (up to $500)
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the first Neiman's email in a week that tells me what I'd get. "$50 gift card for every $200 spent" is a number, a threshold and a cap, all in the hero. After Monday's "BRAND NEW STYLES JUST LANDED" catalog cover and the wedding-guest lookbook, I'll take it. It's also a "Reminder," so I've seen this offer before, and the subject admits it.
- I have two problems. Gift cards aren't a discount. The card arrives "within 8 weeks," so I'm paying full price now for a coupon I can use in December. And the hero says "Excludes beauty." I'm a beauty-counter regular, and the one category where I'd happily stack points is carved out. I'd open it, I'd probably click, and I wouldn't forward it. I'd keep it as a reminder that the code expires 10/29.

## What's working

- The hero is clean. The red "$50 GIFT CARD" on blush reads first, then "$200 SPENT," then a black SHOP NOW button. It's honest, unshouty and easy to scan.
- The cap ("$500 maximum reward") and "Excludes beauty" sit right under the button. Saks would have buried both.
- The 3x3 designer grid (Oscar de la Renta, Burberry, Chloé, Zimmermann, Tom Ford) reads as designer access. It's the right tone for me.
- The code FALLNM is printed in the hero, so I don't have to hunt for it.
- The category buttons at the bottom (handbags, shoes, jewelry, home) let me skip straight to my section.

## What's weak

- Beauty is excluded, and that's my category. A gift card instead of points or a discount doesn't fit how I shop. I won't use a card that earns me nothing.
- The deadline is only in the fine print: "before 10/29/26 at 10:00 PM CT." The hero has no urgency. A gift card with a real deadline should say so up top.
- The fine print is a wall of text: 8-week delivery, a 90-day expiry, no sale items, returns subtract from the award. Those are the terms that matter, and they're in tiny type at the bottom.
- The grid is nine tiles with no prices, no sizes and no sign of what qualifies. Every tile links to "select regular-price items," which could be anything. The Zegna jacket is probably $2,000+, so I'd hit the $500 cap fast.
- There's no loyalty or InCircle tier mention anywhere. I'm a known customer, and this treats me like a stranger.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero: "Ends Thursday, 10/29." The "Reminder" subject already promises urgency, but the body doesn't deliver it.
- 2. Say when the card arrives and what it's good for, right under the CTA. "Gift card issued within 8 weeks" shouldn't be a surprise at the bottom.
- 3. Add an InCircle line, like "Members: points still count on qualifying items." That would answer my main question.
- 4. Tag the tiles with price or "qualifies" badges so I know the offer applies.
- 5. If beauty is excluded, say so in the subject or preheader so I don't open it hoping.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Spend $200, earn $50 back, up to $500`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code FALLNM, online and in stores. Beauty excluded.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Designer fall favorites that qualify. Offer ends 10/29 at 10 PM CT.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with an exposed ESP variable comment, a pinch-zoom-blocking viewport tag, and no `lang` attribute. The source was truncated and 29 tracking links were not probed, so link, footer and unsubscribe checks are unverified. QA reported 0 failures and 3 warnings, all header or probe limitations.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified:** QA skipped the HTTP probe on 29 links because they are tracking or click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unknown.
- The truncated source shows no `<a href>` values, so I can't confirm broken links, missing `target`, or unencoded characters.
- No other issues are confirmable.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom on mobile clients that honor it. Remove `maximum-scale=1`.
- **Missing language:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` or `xml:lang`, so screen readers can't pick the correct pronunciation.
- **Weak title:** `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` is generic and has trailing whitespace.
- **Checkbox-hack mobile nav:** the CSS (`.navbar-checkbox:checked~*[class].menu`, `.hamburger`, `.close`) builds a hamburger menu from a hidden checkbox. This is unsupported in Gmail and several Android clients, and it is typically not keyboard-accessible. Confirm there is a fallback nav, because `*[class].menu` is `display:none` with `visibility:hidden` until toggled.
- **Brittle selectors:** `*[class=contenttable]`, `*[class=mobile]` and `*[class=hide]` are exact-match attribute selectors. They fail if any element carries more than one class.
- **Redundant declaration:** `*[class].show` declares `width: auto !important` and later `width: 278px`. The second is overridden, so it is dead code.
- **Font fallback:** `Gotham` in `.headerNav` has no `@font-face` in the visible CSS and will fall through to `Helvetica Neue`, `Helvetica` or `Arial` in most clients.
- Alt text, image dimensions and the preheader are in the truncated portion and can't be assessed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Exposed variable comment:** the opening HTML comment ships in the delivered source. It lists `@email`, `@base_refid`, `@refid`, `@user`, `@emailid`, `@uEm` and `@cmdid`. It also names the internal data extensions `NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated` and `NM_CRM_Feed`, and includes the unresolved instruction "CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW". This leaks internal schema and template-authoring notes and suggests a pre-send strip step is missing. The comment also has unbalanced parentheses, e.g. `Lookup(..., @email))`.
- No unrendered `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `@var` tokens appear in the visible markup outside that comment.
- `@user` is meant to be `HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256`, but the scenario selector is commented out. I can't confirm from the source which identifier appears in link parameters.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** not found. QA notes the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed rather than a defect.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found, with the same capture caveat. If it is truly absent, it conflicts with Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements for one-click unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results:** not present, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `e.neimanmarcus.com`.
- The body footer (physical address, unsubscribe link) is outside the truncated source, so CAN-SPAM body requirements couldn't be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. No hrefs are visible in the truncated source and all 29 tracking links were skipped. The `@refid` and `@base_refid` variables imply campaign tagging, but I can't see whether or how it is applied to URLs.

4. Verify the hamburger nav has a non-checkbox fallback in Gmail and Android.
