# New brands were just added to our Private Designer Sale
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-12**
## Executive summary

- This is NM email number six or seven in my inbox this week — I have genuinely stopped counting — and it arrives the day after I already opened a Designer Sale email offering 50% off. This one rolls in with "UP TO 40% OFF" in red. I notice immediately: that's a step backward. Yesterday was 50%. Today is 40%. That is not how a Private Event is supposed to feel.
- What really gets me is the subject line actually did its job. "New brands were just added" is the kind of line that earns a tap from me — it implies something changed, something worth seeing. I open it and there is not a single brand name anywhere in the email. Not one. The June 9 send named Arkis and Cinq à Sept and it felt like NM knew me. This is a beige rectangle with a sale button. The subject overpromised and the body delivered nothing.
- I'd click out of muscle memory — NM has earned baseline trust with me — but I wouldn't forward it to a friend, and I'd feel a low-grade irritation that I can't quite place until I realize it's because I was baited.

## What's working

- The "PRIVATE EVENT" headline uses the right register for this brand — I do show up to events, and that framing earns at least a second look
- "40% OFF" in red is the visual anchor; no hunting required, hierarchy is clean
- "SHOP SALE" is a real CTA — dark button, verb + noun, not the vague "Discover" that Macy's loves to use
- No render issues: loaded clean, no broken images, no overlapping text, no lorem ipsum
- The minimal NM aesthetic is intact — nothing shouts, nothing crowds

## What's weak

- Subject says "new brands added" — the email names zero brands; that broken promise is what I clock first
- 40% off after yesterday's 50% off Designer Sale feels like a demotion, not an exclusive addition — it undermines the "Private Event" framing entirely
- No InCircle mention anywhere; I don't know if this is members-only or open to everyone, and that distinction matters every time I decide whether to use my card
- No beauty — I'm a counter regular and the navigation bar doesn't even surface a Beauty tab, let alone a hero product
- The stripped-down layout that usually reads as premium now just reads as empty — there isn't a single product image to make the discount feel real

## Recommendations

- 1. **Name the new brands in the body** — the subject promises them, deliver. "Saint Laurent, Toteme, and six others just joined the Private Sale" gives me an actual reason to click. Sephora drops specific product names in their sale emails; NM should extend the same courtesy to their designer sends.
- 2. **Add InCircle-exclusive language** — "Private Event for InCircle members" carries real weight; "Shop Sale" carries none.
- 3. **Show one product image** — a single bag or shoe at the sale price anchors the offer and makes me want to look for more.
- 4. **Add a deadline** — "ends Sunday" changes my calculus entirely; right now nothing in this email creates any urgency beyond the vague word "private."
- 5. **Explain the 40% vs. yesterday's 50%** — if this is an early-access window or a different category cut, say so. Otherwise it reads like a typo or a worse deal.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Neiman Marcus "Private Designer Sale" Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is built on Salesforce Marketing Cloud AMPscript and follows standard table-based HTML email conventions. Four QA warnings require attention: missing `List-Unsubscribe` headers (now mandatory for bulk senders at Gmail/Yahoo), unverifiable authentication results, and a plain-text part that is predominantly URLs — all of which are active deliverability and compliance risks.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**30 tracking links unverifiable**
All click-through links pass through a redirect domain (NM's ESP click-tracker). QA skipped HTTP probing on these. No broken-link confirmation is possible without a full crawl against a live send.

**`@refid` is a no-op concatenation**
```ampscript
@refid = CONCAT(@base_refid,"")
```
Concatenating with an empty string produces an identical value to `@base_refid`. If `@refid` is intended to carry a campaign suffix, the suffix is missing. This will silently produce incomplete tracking IDs if the template was designed to append a segment or variant identifier.

**Plain-text body is >75% URLs**
QA finding: 2,645 of 3,519 plain-text characters are URLs. This is a known spam-filter signal (Spamhaus, Barracuda). Plain-text parts should be human-readable summaries, not raw link dumps.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Dead viewport meta tags left in source**
Three commented-out `<meta name="viewport">` variants remain in `<head>`:
```html
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, user-scalable=no" />-->
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=320, initial-scale=0.29">-->
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=1024">-->
```
Not rendering issues now, but they indicate the template was patched iteratively. If a future editor uncomments the wrong line it breaks mobile scaling.

**Dual-breakpoint media query structure**
Two separate `<style>` blocks define breakpoints at `max-width: 600px` and `max-device-width: 480px` / `max-width: 480px`. Splitting responsive logic across two blocks increases maintenance risk and specificity conflicts. No active rendering failure confirmed from source alone.

**No `alt` attribute evidence visible in truncated source**
Images are present (`.bgimage`, `.ImgSize`, `.ImgSize2x2` classes) but the HTML is truncated before `<img>` tags appear. Cannot confirm `alt` text coverage. This is flagged for manual inspection — missing `alt` text breaks screen-reader accessibility and renders blank in image-blocked clients (common in B2B inboxes).

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Commented-out SHA256 assignment creates ambiguity**
```ampscript
/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */
```
This line is disabled. The active path sets `@user` via a DE lookup:
```ampscript
@user = Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", "EMAIL_ID", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email)
```
The comment says "CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW," implying two audience paths exist. If any downstream link or personalization block expects `@user` to be a SHA256 hash (e.g., for a hashed-email audience match pixel), that block will silently receive a raw `EMAIL_ID` integer instead. This warrants a cross-check against any pixel or identity-resolution calls in the body.

**`@uEm` and `@cmdid` lookups against `NM_CRM_Feed`**
Both are defined in the header comment block. If a subscriber record is absent from `NM_CRM_Feed` (e.g., new opt-ins not yet in CRM), both values return empty string. Any link or conditional that depends on these will degrade silently — no error, just missing params.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header absent** *(QA: WARN)*
Gmail and Yahoo mandate `List-Unsubscribe` headers for bulk senders (enforced since February 2024). Absence risks inbox demotion or bulk-folder placement. This header must be injected at send time by the ESP or MTA — it cannot live in HTML body.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) absent** *(QA: WARN)*
One-click unsubscribe (`List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`) is required alongside `List-Unsubscribe` under the same Gmail/Yahoo policy. Both headers appear missing from what was captured by the AgentMail relay. Confirm whether SFMC is configured to inject these at send time or whether the relay is stripping them.

**`Authentication-Results` header not found** *(QA: WARN)*
SPF and DKIM pass/fail status is unknown. Sending domain is `e.neimanmarcus.com`. If DKIM signing is not confirmed, major ISPs will assign lower sender reputation. This needs verification at the MTA level, not the HTML level.

**CAN-SPAM physical address and unsubscribe mechanism**
Not verifiable from the truncated source. Must confirm footer contains a physical mailing address and a functioning unsubscribe link (required under CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) and §5(a)(3)).
