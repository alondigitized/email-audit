# Don’t miss up to 50% off select beauty! Plus earn 500 bonus points with $75 spend ⏳
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-07**
## Executive summary

- Well, here we go again — Sephora Stacking Weekend, the sequel that just won't end. I've now opened this exact "50% off select beauty, 500 points at $75" combo four times in four days — 9/4, 9/5 twice, and now this one — and today's twist is they've buried the actual sale under an entire Eadem product catalog first. Cue Bar/Chocaïsu Exfoliating Toner, Water Prep Strengthening Face Mist, Cashmere Peel Milky Exfoliant... I don't even know what half of this brand is, and I've been asked to "elevate" with it before I've even seen the number I opened the email for.
- The 50% starburst is back in the top corner, tiny and cramped next to "500" — same real estate fight I saw yesterday. Then it's Eadem, Eadem, Eadem for what feels like a full scroll — eight separate product call-outs, each "ONLY AT SEPHORA," before the deal resurfaces lower down. That's a lot of new-brand education to shove in front of a woman who opened this for the discount, not a skincare lecture.
- Verdict: mark as read, not click. I already redeemed my attention on this same offer twice this week — Neiman Marcus is at least consistent about running the same "S" banner without dressing it up as something new, and Kohl's just throws everything at once and calls it a day. This one pretends to be a fresh "spotlight" email but it's the identical stacking promo wearing an Eadem trench coat.

## What's working

- The 50%/500-points combo banner up top is at least legible and matches what I've come to expect this week — no confusion about the mechanics.
- Product photography is clean and consistent — soft, neutral backgrounds, no clutter, which reads more "prestige" than Kohl's jam-everything layout.
- "Beauty INSIDER" sign-in module near the bottom at least nods to loyalty status, even if it's an afterthought.
- The second 50% banner lower down (pink/blue gradient) gives people who scrolled past the first one a second chance to find the actual deal.

## What's weak

- I have now seen this exact 50%-off-plus-500-points offer four times since Thursday. Sephora, I got it the first time.
- The Eadem section is enormous — eight product blocks — and it's front-loaded before the sale reappears, which buries the thing I actually opened for.
- Nothing here signals I'm a known Beauty Insider tier holder; no "you have X points," no personalized pick, just generic brand merchandising.
- "Real Luxe" and "Hair's Latest Lineup" tiles near the bottom feel tacked on, like they ran out of ideas for what else to cram in.
- The "Virtual Beauty Mixer" scent quiz CTA at the very bottom is buried past what I'd realistically scroll to on my phone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the sale, not the brand launch — if this is meant to be a stacking-weekend reminder, put the 50%/points banner at the very top uncontested, then let Eadem be one section, not the whole email.
- 2. Cut the Eadem block from eight products to two or three "editor's picks" — nobody needs the full assortment in an inbox blast.
- 3. Add a personalization line — "You have 340 points" or "Your Beauty Insider tier: VIB" — Macy's confirm-email at least fakes urgency with specificity; this doesn't even try loyalty-specific copy.
- 4. Vary the creative from the last three sends — reusing the identical red starburst all week reads as autopilot, not a "spotlight."
