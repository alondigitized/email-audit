# 20% off won't stick around forever (hint, hint) ⏰
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's again, and it's the fifth day in a row I've had one of these. My first reaction was that the subject promised urgency and the body didn't deliver it. "20% off won't stick around forever" is cute, but the first thing I see is a sleigh header with "Shop Now" and no number. The 20% only shows up in a small "20% + 20% off w/coupon" block on the decor section, and then at the very bottom as "Take an extra 20% off" with code SAVEBIG. That's the same buried-coupon move as the 11/03 "great day to take an extra 20% off" send.
- It's a mall directory again. Home decor, a hosting guide, a holiday party edit, Clearance up to 70%, DoorDash and Instacart, then four Ninja and Serta products. I do appreciate the Rewards status line at the top, "KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER, Earn 9% REWARDS". That is the one place they treat me like a known customer. The party edit has a family in matching velvet, and the heels and clutch are closer to me, but nothing here is aimed at a 47-year-old who shops beauty. I'd skim it and probably not click. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The Rewards member status and "Earn 9% Rewards" sit at the very top. That's the loyalty cue I look for.
- The "Shop now. Decide later. Free returns through January 31, 2027" block is clear and respectful. Returns matter a lot to me.
- The bottom coupon block has a visible code, a PIN, a barcode and an end date, "Ends Nov. 5". That deadline is credible.
- The clearance banner, "Save up to 70%", is bold and easy to read.
- The Sephora at Kohl's mention is in the footer area, which is a decent nod to beauty shoppers.

## What's weak

- The offer is buried. The sleigh hero has no percentage, and the real 20% code is at the bottom. Sephora's "10% off plus a free gift" subject at least pointed me to the offer.
- There's too much going on. Four category nav rows, decor, a guide, a party edit, clearance, delivery options and products. Macy's "LAST CHANCE: confirm now" email had one job. This one has about ten.
- The "20% + 20% off w/coupon" block reads confusingly. I can't tell if the discounts stack.
- The party-edit models don't speak to me, and there's no beauty at all, even though Kohl's sells Sephora.
- The product picks (a slushie machine, towels, a mattress topper, a slow cooker) feel random. The "Picked with you in mind" label rings hollow, because nothing here looks like my shopping history.
- The dense fine print at the bottom is tiny, and I'd need to zoom to read it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in the hero: "Extra 20% off, code SAVEBIG, ends tonight" with one button. Move the coupon block to the top.
- 2. Cut the page to three sections. Neiman Marcus's quiet "Bejeweled" send shows that one idea is enough.
- 3. Use Rewards data to pick a beauty-leaning module, such as Sephora at Kohl's, for a beauty customer.
- 4. Spell out whether the discounts stack, and show the dollar savings.
- 5. Drop the duplicate nav bars and enlarge the legal text.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day: 20% off + Kohl's Rewards`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code SAVEBIG online or in store. Free returns through Jan 31.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible markup has several malformed-HTML and accessibility defects (invalid `<head>` content, a stray `</custom>` tag, missing alt attributes, zoom disabled). The compliance and authentication warnings come from the AgentMail relay and the truncated source, so they are unconfirmed. Sender: `Kohls@s.kohls.com`.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 67 tracking links were skipped (click-redirect domains), so destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unknown. Visible examples are opaque `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7Im...` tokens.
- **Stacked tracking pixels:** There are four before the first content table. All are 1×1 images, and each adds a third-party request on open.
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/f53e0453-...`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=...`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager)
- **Malformed tag:** The `open.aspx` pixel is followed by a stray `</custom>` (`<img ... alt=""></custom>`). No `<custom>` element was opened, so this is invalid markup. Some parsers or sanitizers may mishandle it.
- **Empty icon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty `href`. An empty href resolves to the document URL, so a client may request the email itself.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom disabled:** The viewport meta is `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0`. This blocks pinch-zoom and is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Empty `<title>`:** `<title></title>` has no content.
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` with child `<meta>` tags sits inside `<head>`. A `<div>` is not allowed there, so parsers implicitly close the head and open the body early. This can misplace the `<style>` and `<link>` content that follows, and Gmail and Outlook strip schema microdata anyway.
- **Missing alt text, content images:** QA flags the following as likely content, not decoration. Alt text can't be confirmed for the `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` images because the source is truncated.
  - `59551394_1_2x`
  - `59551394_Product_hdcu16_Apparel_Ftw_Hero_GIF`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/f441d45d2128ae53.png`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`

  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/78308d1d9adb0751.png`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/916d6fa08d185233.png`
- **Missing alt attribute, tracking pixels:** The `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` and `kohls.demdex.net/event` pixels have no `alt` attribute at all. The `mi.kohls.com/.../o.gif` pixel is flagged by QA, but the source shows `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, so that flag is a false positive.
- **Hero GIF:** Animated GIF hero (`..._Hero_GIF`) has no confirmed alt, and Outlook desktop renders only the first frame.
- **Web fonts:** `@import` of Roboto and Montserrat is guarded by `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, and the stack falls back to `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`. No issue.

- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Not found. QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so absence from the real message is unconfirmed. Verify against the raw message headers. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

- The only campaign identifier in the source is `email_name=261105_DG_Email_Thursda…` on the image URLs. It is truncated and not tied to any destination URL.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Check the raw headers of the actual message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and `Authentication-Results`, and confirm the footer address. This resolves four of the QA warnings.
2. Move the `itemscope` div and its meta tags out of `<head>`, or drop them. Remove the stray `</custom>`. Give `<title>` and `<link rel="shortcut icon">` real values, or remove the icon link.
3. Remove `user-scalable=0, maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Add descriptive `alt` text to the content images and the hero GIF. Add `alt=""` to the `click.chp.kohls.com` and `demdex.net` pixels.
5. Consolidate the four tracking pixels if possible.
6. Resolve a sample of the `click.s.kohls.com` links manually (or rerun with a full, untruncated source) to confirm the landing URLs, UTMs and offer alignment.
