# Save 20% | Give your home a cozy fall refresh 🍂
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-08**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's number seven this week, and honestly? This is the best-organized one they've sent me. It opens with "Status: KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER · Earn: 5% REWARDS" right at the top, which is the first time all week they've actually acknowledged I'm a member before pitching me anything — that's the tier-clarity beat Sephora and Saks nail every time and Kohl's usually skips. The home-refresh theme actually holds together too: cozy gallery wall, ceramic vase, wood console, then decor coupon, then bedding/bath/kitchen — it reads like someone picked a lane instead of dumping every department into one email like Tuesday's "Save an extra 20%" send did.
- Where it falls apart is the back half — activewear at 40% off bolted onto a "fall home refresh" email, followed by a random grid of a slushie maker, bath towels, a mattress topper, and jeans. That's the Macy's-style overload creeping back in after a genuinely clean start. I'd open this one over most of their recent sends because the hero actually matches the subject, but I'm scrolling past the activewear block and the product grab-bag at the bottom without a second look. Not forwarding it — this is "read and archive," not "look what I found" territory.

## What's working

- The Rewards-member status line up top is the kind of "you're a known customer" signal Kohl's has been skipping all week — Saks does this reflexively in every VIB email, Kohl's finally caught up here.
- The hero image — gallery wall, glass vases, ceramic jug — actually looks like a home I'd want, and it's the first Kohl's send this week where the lifestyle shot and the coupon match the subject line instead of feeling recycled.
- "Proof that a little refresh can be a big difference" with clickable Bedding/Bath/Kitchen & Dining pills is a clean, scannable way to shop the theme without dumping the whole catalog on me.
- The 20% coupon code (GET20, PIN 3573) is printed as an actual barcode near the bottom — that's a real "bring your phone to the register" convenience touch I don't see from Nordstrom or Neiman Marcus.

## What's weak

- The FLX activewear block — "UP TO 40% OFF" in giant red — has nothing to do with cozy fall home and just derails the theme right when I was bought in.
- The bottom product grid (slushie maker, towels, mattress topper, jeans) feels like leftover inventory someone needed to move, not "picked with you in mind" like the headline claims — that copy oversells a random assortment.
- "Coupons do not apply to select brands" and "Coupons do not apply to select styles" show up twice with a vague "details" link instead of just telling me what's excluded — Nordstrom's Anniversary Sale emails at least name the excluded brands outright.
- Six-plus emails from Kohl's this week and this is the first one with real thematic discipline — that inconsistency itself is the problem; I can't tell if next Tuesday's email will look like this or like Wednesday's everything-dump.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the activewear and random product grid entirely, or spin them into a separate email — a themed "cozy fall refresh" send should stay in its lane the way today's Kohl's finally attempted; diluting it with FLX and a slushie maker undoes the one good structural decision here.
- 2. Name the excluded brands/styles inline instead of hiding behind "details" — Nordstrom does this and it reads as honest, not evasive.
- 3. Move the Rewards status line into a proper mini-module (points balance, Kohl's Cash progress) instead of one small line at the very top — I want to see what I'm earning, not just that I'm a member.
- 4. Add a deadline countdown near the coupon code itself (Aug. 16 is mentioned twice up top but not near the barcode where I'd actually use it).

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Kohl's Cash is waiting: 20% off fall home`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Gallery walls, cozy textures, real savings — shop the look`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Members: your 20% off coupon expires Aug. 16`

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "Save 20% Cozy Fall Refresh" Email

### 1. Technical Summary
This is a standard MSO/Litmus-pattern HTML4.01 transitional email built for the Kohl's ESP (Coherent Path / "chp.kohls.com" + "mi.kohls.com" stack), with heavy multi-vendor tracking instrumentation and no confirmable unsubscribe/authentication headers in the automated scan.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Redundant open-tracking calls**: four separate open-tracking mechanisms fire on load — `click.chp.kohls.com/o/0924ae01-...` (1x1 img), `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`, `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?NLIRVJR63UTUFNLMXGUSB3PYIM.60263`, and `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager). This is redundant but not a defect per se — flagging for awareness.
- **Malformed markup around the open-tracking pixel**: `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>` — a stray `</custom>` closing tag with no matching opening tag. Invalid HTML that some strict parsers/clients may mishandle.
- **64 tracking/redirect links skipped by automated probe** (QA INFO) — destinations behind `click.chp.kohls.com` could not be verified as live; the encoded `cp_tp=v3.eJy...` payload obscures the true target URL, so link health is unconfirmed rather than confirmed-broken.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom disabled**: viewport meta sets `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0`, which blocks pinch-to-zoom — a WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) concern for accessibility.
- **Missing alt text on 9 images** (QA WARN), including all tracking pixels and at least one content image (`916d6fa08d185233.png`). Tracking-pixel alt text is low priority, but any content/product images with empty `alt=""` will read as blank to screen readers and show nothing if images are blocked (Outlook default).
- Google Fonts (`Roboto`, `Montserrat`) are correctly scoped inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-- -->` conditional comments, so they're excluded from Outlook — no rendering-engine conflict there.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- One tracking pixel contains an **empty merge parameter**: `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_pen...` — the redemption-end-date token resolved blank. This suggests a personalization/merge field that failed to populate for this send. No other unresolved `{{token}}`-style placeholders are visible in the truncated source.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** (QA WARN) — could not confirm one-click unsubscribe support at the header level.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found** (RFC 8058, QA WARN) — one-click unsubscribe (POST) not confirmed.
- **No physical mailing address detected** (QA WARN) — CAN-SPAM requires this; note the HTML source was truncated before any footer, so this cannot be confirmed absent from the actual email body, only absent from the scanned/available content.
- **Authentication-Results header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC status unknown; per QA note this may be a relay artifact (AgentMail) rather than a true absence, so treat as unconfirmed rather than a definitive failure.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No standard `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` parameters are present on the visible links; the platform instead uses proprietary params (`mi_ecmp=1019314_202688`, `email_name=260808_DG_Email_Saturday`, `mi_u=604230016`). This is consistent with the Coherent Path/Kohl's stack but means standard web-analytics UTM attribution cannot be verified from this source.
- Because all outbound links route through `click.chp.kohls.com` redirects with an opaque encoded `cp_tp` payload, and all 64 were skipped by the HTTP probe, **landing-page alignment cannot be confirmed** — destination URLs and HTTP status are unknown from available data.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the stray `</custom>` tag near the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` pixel — invalid markup.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to content images (tracking pixels can remain empty/`alt=""`, which is correct practice for those).
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta to restore pinch-zoom for accessibility.
4. Investigate why `mi_kc_redm_end_dte` resolved empty in the tracking pixel — confirm whether this merge field is expected to be populated for this campaign.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and a physical address are present in the actual production send (headers may be dropped by the relay used for this scan — re-verify against raw SMTP headers from a direct Kohl's send, not the relayed copy).
6. If UTM-based attribution is needed for cross-channel/web analytics, consider appending standard `utm_*` params alongside the proprietary `mi_*`/`cp_*` params on click-through links.
