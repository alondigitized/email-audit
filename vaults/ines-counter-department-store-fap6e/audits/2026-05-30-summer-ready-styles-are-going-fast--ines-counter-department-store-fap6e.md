# Summer-ready styles are going fast!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-30**
## Executive summary

- Here we are again. Twenty-something emails into what has become an endurance test, Neiman Marcus drops another Designer Sale push — and the email itself is clean. Red hero, 50% off headline, a product grid featuring Zimmermann and Valentino Garavani. It looks exactly as you'd expect from NM, which is to say it looks exactly like yesterday's "favorites are selling fast" email with a seasonal adjective swapped in.
- That's the problem. I already got this message. I already decided whether to shop this sale. Today's version reframes the urgency as "Summer-ready styles" instead of "favorites," but the creative, the offer structure, the product grid format — identical. After nineteen-plus emails in two weeks, I don't feel marketed to. I feel managed.
- Mark as read. No forward. If my friend also subscribes to Neiman Marcus, she's already seen it.

## What's working

- **Hero hierarchy does its job**: The red banner with "THE DESIGNER SALE / UP TO 50% OFF" is the first thing the eye lands on — no ambiguity, no hunting for the offer.
- **Category CTA buttons are genuinely useful**: SHOP WOMEN'S CLOTHING, SHOP WOMEN'S SHOES, SHOP DRESSES — these let me skip straight to my category instead of dumping me on a sale landing page.
- **Brand selection speaks my language**: Zimmermann, Akris punto, Valentino Garavani, Burberry. These are names I recognize and some I actually buy. Not fast fashion, not discount-adjacent brands.
- **Exclusions noted inline**: "Exclusions apply" sits directly under the CTA, not buried three screens down in the footer. That's honest.
- **Clean render throughout**: Nothing broken, no image gaps, layout holds. Kohl's linen send this morning was structurally clean too but opened with a parade of emoji — NM at least trusts the product to carry the visual.

## What's weak

- **No prices on any product**: Six-plus images across the grid, zero was/now pairs. This is a sale email — showing me Zimmermann and Akris punto without a price point is window shopping, not commerce.
- **InCircle is completely absent**: I carry a Neiman Marcus card specifically because points on purchases like these accumulate fast. Not one word about member benefits, InCircle tiers, or points earned on sale items. Sephora at least makes my Rouge status feel like it matters on every promotional send.
- **Day two of the same urgency beat**: Yesterday was "favorites are selling fast." Today is "Summer-ready styles are going fast." The copy verbs changed; the structure, hierarchy, and call to action did not. As a subscriber who opened yesterday, this reads as a resend, not a new message.
- **"UP TO 50% OFF" with exclusions is the oldest hedge in retail**: After twenty-plus NM emails, I know "up to" means the headline number applies to one deeply discounted item from a prior season. Macy's runs this exact same play and it landed flat there too.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put one was/now price on the hero or the lead product**: "$695 → $347" on the Zimmermann dress stops my thumb cold. Browse grids without prices are browse grids without a reason to click. This is the single highest-lift change.
- 2. **Add one InCircle line**: "Earn InCircle points on every Designer Sale purchase." Literally eight words. It changes the calculus for every loyal cardholder and makes the NM card feel like an asset over the Saks Fifth Avenue card sitting next to it in my wallet.
- 3. **Segment the re-send, don't blast the full list again**: Two urgency-identical sends in 24 hours erodes belief in the urgency. Non-openers from yesterday get the re-send; yester-openers get a segmented, fresher angle. What NM is doing now mimics the Sephora double-send pattern — "LAST CHANCE" then "ENDS TODAY" — which I flagged as exhausting when Sephora did it.
- 4. **Make the subject line earn its open**: The current line is filler — it tells me nothing that NM in the sender field doesn't already imply.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Designer Sale: Zimmermann, Valentino — up to 50% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your InCircle picks: up to 50% off this weekend only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns added — Akris, Burberry, and more before they're gone`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Final days: shop your saved styles before the sale ends`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Neiman Marcus "Summer-ready styles" Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is a standard Salesforce Marketing Cloud (AMPscript) table-based send with a client-side hamburger-nav CSS hack. Four infrastructure warnings surfaced in QA: missing `List-Unsubscribe` headers (a bulk-sender compliance gap), unknown DKIM/SPF status, an URL-saturated plain-text part, and 26 unverifiable tracking links.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**26 tracking links — all unverifiable**
QA skipped HTTP probes on all 26 click-redirect links (likely `e.neimanmarcus.com` redirects). No destination URL, UTM coverage, or redirect-chain depth could be confirmed.

**Plain-text body is 87% URLs**
QA reports 5,095 of 5,865 plain-text characters are raw URLs. A human-readable plain-text alternative is absent. Spam filters (SpamAssassin, Gmail) penalize plain-text parts that are effectively URL lists with no prose.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Deprecated `max-device-width` media query**
```css
@media only screen and (max-device-width: 480px), screen and (max-width: 480px)
```
`max-device-width` was removed from the CSS spec; modern browsers alias it to `max-width`, but it is dead weight and signals unmaintained CSS.

**Dead iOS 9 animation hack**
```css
body { -webkit-animation: bugfix infinite 1s; }
@-webkit-keyframes bugfix { from { padding: 0; } to { padding: 0; } }
```
iOS 9 reached end-of-life in 2016. This keyframe fires on every render cycle for zero effect and adds noise to the stylesheet.

**Multiple commented-out `<meta name="viewport">` tags**
Three viewport variants are left in comments below the active one. No rendering impact, but it indicates unresolved template debt and inflates source size.

**Hamburger nav uses CSS `:checked` hack**
The mobile nav relies on `input[type=checkbox]:checked ~ .menu` toggling. This works in Apple Mail and most webmail but is broken in Outlook desktop (all versions) and some Samsung Email builds. No conditional comment fallback is present.

**`ExternalClass` / `ReadMsgBody` styles**
These target legacy Hotmail/Outlook.com rendering behavior from pre-2013. Both are no-ops on current clients but harmless.

**Alt text unverifiable**
Full source is truncated; `alt` attribute coverage on product images cannot be confirmed. Given the heavy image layout, missing or generic `alt=""` values would fail WCAG 2.1 AA at the content level.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**`@user` variable is inside a block comment**
```html
<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */-->
```
The variable assignment for `@user` appears to be wrapped in both an HTML comment (`<!-- -->`) and an AMPscript block comment (`/* */`). If this is the only place `@user` is set, it will evaluate as empty at send time. The surrounding note says "CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW," implying a template with two paths where the wrong path may have been left active.

**`@uEm` and `@cmdid` lack null guards**
Both are looked up from `NM_CRM_Feed`:
```
Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "uEm", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email)
Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "cmdid", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email)
```
No `IIF(EMPTY(@uEm), ...)` fallback is visible in the source fragment. If a subscriber is absent from `NM_CRM_Feed`, both tokens render blank, which typically corrupts tracking query strings that embed them.

**`@refid` is a no-op concat**
```
@refid = CONCAT(@base_refid, "")
```
Concatenating an empty string adds nothing. This is either a placeholder from a template where a suffix was removed or dead code.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header absent** *(QA: WARN)*
As of February 2024, Google and Yahoo require bulk senders (≥5,000/day) to include `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers. Their absence risks Gmail/Yahoo routing this to spam or suppressing delivery entirely for non-opted-in segments. The QA note attributes this to AgentMail relay capture, but the header should be present regardless of relay behavior.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) absent** *(QA: WARN)*
One-click machine unsubscribe is not supported. Gmail's "Unsubscribe" button in the UI requires this header; without it, Gmail falls back to a two-step flow or shows no button at all.

**SPF/DKIM status unknown** *(QA: WARN)*
`Authentication-Results` header not captured. Sending domain `e.neimanmarcus.com` is a dedicated ESP subdomain that should have DKIM alignment with `neimanmarcus.com`. Cannot confirm without raw header access.

**CAN-SPAM physical address**
Cannot verify presence of physical mailing address from the truncated source. Required by CAN-SPAM § 7(a)(5) for every commercial message.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All 26 outbound links route through click-redirect URLs on `e.neimanmarcus.com`. Because HTTP probes were skipped, the following cannot be confirmed:

- UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content`) are appended post-redirect
- Destination landing pages exist and return HTTP 200
- Product deep-links match the featured items (no dead SKUs or 404s)

`@cmdid` and `@uEm` tokens (if blank due to the null-guard issue above) would produce malformed tracking query strings on any link that embeds them.

---
