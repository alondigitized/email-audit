# 25% off select regular-price styles ends soon
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-06**
## Executive summary

- There's that big black "S" again — third time in a row, and now it's just wallpaper. I get that Neiman's wants a signature moment, but at this point I could draw it from memory, and it's crowding out an actual product shot in the one spot that should be doing the selling. That said, the mechanics underneath are clean: 25% off, real product grid, real designer names I recognize — Theia, Chiara Boni, Cinq à Sept — no filler.
- The fine print did its job for once, and not in a good way: "Does not apply to beauty or fragrance" is right there, in bold-adjacent black-and-white, no hiding it. As someone who's at the beauty counter more than the dress rack, that line moved me straight past the hero and down to "well, at least the jackets and jeans are nice." Compare this to Sephora's 9/4 stacking-weekend email — say what you want about the repetitive starburst, but at least the beauty offer is the entire point of the email, not an exclusion buried in legal copy.
- Would I click? Mildly — the Cinq à Sept jacket and the L'AGENCE jeans caught my eye enough to scroll. Would I forward it? No. This reads like the same promo skeleton Neiman's has run for three sends now, just with the SKUs swapped.

## What's working

- The product grid is genuinely well-curated — Rickie Freeman, Theia, ALÉMAIS, real event-worthy pieces I'd actually consider for a wedding-guest look.
- 25% off is stated plainly, no hunting for the number, and the deadline is spelled out to the hour (9/7/26, 10pm CT) — that's more honest than most of what lands in my inbox.
- Two clean secondary CTAs (Shop Women's / Shop Men's Clothing) at the bottom give me an easy out if the hero grid didn't land.
- No render bugs — everything's crisp, images loaded, nothing overlapping.

## What's weak

- The big black "S" hero is now a rerun of a rerun — this is the third straight Neiman's send leaning on it, and it's crowding out an actual styled photo, which is what actually gets me to stop scrolling.
- "Does not apply to beauty or fragrance" sitting right in the T&Cs is a real letdown for someone who treats the counter as the reason to open a Neiman's email at all.
- No loyalty/InCircle callout anywhere — if I'm a member, this email doesn't know it, which feels like a missed layer Saks usually remembers to include.
- Nothing here signals "event" — no stylist appointment nudge, no beauty-counter tie-in — just a generic sitewide markdown dressed in serif type.

## Recommendations

- 1. Retire the black "S" for at least one send — swap in an actual lifestyle or model shot in the hero, since three identical banners in a row is starting to read as "we didn't have a new photo ready."
- 2. Move the beauty/fragrance exclusion or at least soften its prominence — or better, run a parallel beauty-specific offer so I'm not immediately deprioritized.
- 3. Add an InCircle member-tier line ("members get X") — even a single line would make this feel like it knows who I am.
- 4. Lead the grid with the Cinq à Sept jacket or the Theia gown instead of alphabetizing by vibe — those are the pieces with the most "stop scrolling" pull.
