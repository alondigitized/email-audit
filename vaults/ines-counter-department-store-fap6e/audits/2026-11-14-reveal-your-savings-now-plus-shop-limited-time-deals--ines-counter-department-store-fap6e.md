# Reveal your savings now 👀 Plus, shop LIMITED-TIME deals.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again, and the sender line, "Super Saturday", is the only thing that tells me this one is different from the other Kohl's emails this week. I got "Save 20% | Earn Kohl's Cash" yesterday, the "extra 20%" one before that, and the $10-off-$25 countdowns on 11/11 and 11/12. A reveal-your-savings tease followed by a long burgundy scroll of category tiles is the same promo in a new costume.
- The image is a very long column, and at the size I can see it, I can't read the fine print or confirm exact percentages. What I can see is a "Super Saturday Deals" header, a loyalty strip at the very top, a stack of "Up to 50% off" and "Up to 30% off" tiles, and a gift-guide section. Further down there's a "Preview the Deals Now" block, a Kohl's Cash barcode, and a wall of legal text. That's the Macy's-style overload I avoid.
- I'd skim it and not click. If I wanted a gift, I'd already be at Nordstrom or Sephora. Forward to a friend? No.

## What's working

- The header has a Super Saturday banner and a loyalty status line at the top, so I know right away I'm being treated as a Rewards member.
- Each tile has a big offer number and its own "Shop Now" button, so every module's ask is obvious.
- The burgundy-and-white blocks keep the scroll orderly, and I don't see broken images or overlapping text.
- The gift-guide split into For Her, For Him and For Kids is useful this close to the holidays.
- The "Preview the Deals Now" block ties into Black Friday timing, so the season feels right.

## What's weak

- It's 15 or more modules long. Sephora Insider's "Meet the best of the best" gave me one idea and a clear lane, and this gives me a flyer.
- The "Reveal your savings" tease doesn't pay off. I see no obvious reveal mechanic, just the same tile stack.
- I can't see any beauty content. The nearest things are apparel, bras and pajamas, so nothing speaks to a beauty-counter shopper.
- The "coupon doesn't apply to select brands" and "select styles" notes sit under almost every tile, and the legal block at the bottom is long. That's the buried-exclusion feeling I don't trust.
- The cadence is exhausting. This is at least the sixth Kohl's send since 11/10. Neiman Marcus's quiet Versace holiday email stood out against all the shouting, and this one adds to it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one hero offer and one button, and cut the 15-tile scroll to three or four modules. Show the member benefit, like Kohl's Cash or the extra-percent-off code, in the first screen.
- 2. Make the reveal real: one clear, tappable "Reveal my offer" button with the code visible right after.
- 3. Cap the cadence. One strong weekend send beats three near-identical ones.
- 4. Move the exclusions into one plain line near the offer instead of repeating them under every tile.
- 5. Add a beauty or fragrance gift module if you want shoppers like me.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Kohl's Super Saturday (2026-11-14)

**1. Technical Summary**
Kohl's ESP-templated email with heavy tracking (SFMC, Movable Ink, Adobe Audience Manager, chp click domain). Confirmed defects are missing image alt text, a zoom-blocking viewport meta, and malformed markup. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because of the relay and HTML truncation.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 81 links go through redirect domains (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…`), so the HTTP probe skipped them. Destinations and link health are unverified, not confirmed broken.
- There are four separate tracking or open pixels:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/b7cb853b-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…&bmt=0`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`
- The first pixel sits directly in `<body>` with no `alt`, no `display:none`, and no hiding wrapper. It can render as a 1px inline element.
- `open.aspx` is followed by a stray `</custom>` closing tag with no opener. This is invalid markup that some parsers may handle inconsistently.
- `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href, which can trigger a request to the document base URL.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- `<title></title>` is empty.
- `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0>` disables pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- QA flags 10 images as missing alt text. Six of them are content images under `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` (`2a5adc5afb647aad`, `f441d45d2128ae53`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44`, `415c8a18fc2950ea`, `69494b48a79b4240`, `78308d1d9adb0751`), and a seventh (`916d6fa08d185233.png`) is also cut off in the report. Offers carried only in images are invisible to screen readers and to image-blocked clients.
- The QA flag on `o.gif` is a false positive. The source shows `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`.
- The `demdex.net/event` pixel has no `alt` attribute at all. The `click.chp` pixel has none either. Both are tracking pixels, so this is low impact.
- The preheader `<table width='640' class="container">` has no `role="presentation"`, unlike the other layout tables.
- The Google Fonts `@import` and `<link>` are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, which is correct. Fallback stacks are present.
- The plain-text part is 14,321 of 20,084 characters URLs (71%), which tripped the >70% warning. This is a consequence of the long tracking URLs.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source.
- The Movable Ink image URLs carry an empty parameter: `…&mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_p…`. The redemption end date is empty, so the dynamic image may render a blank or default date. Verify by rendering.
- `mi_u=604230016` is a subscriber identifier embedded in image URLs. That is expected for Movable Ink, but it is a per-recipient ID in a third-party-visible URL.

**5. Compliance**
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly stripping headers, so it is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so check the raw message.
- `Authentication-Results` was not captured, so SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown. Sender is `Kohls@s.kohls.com`, a subdomain, so alignment should be checked on the original.
- The HTML is truncated, so the footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be verified.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity**
- Destination URLs and UTM parameters are not visible. They sit inside opaque `qs=` tokens on `click.s.kohls.com`, and no probe was run.
- The only campaign identifiers visible are in Movable Ink image URLs: `mi_ecmp=1025887_20261114` and `email_name=261114_DG_Email_Myster…`. These do not confirm landing-page alignment.
- No issues are confirmable from the available data.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add meaningful `alt` text to all `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` content images. Use `alt=""` on decorative or tracking pixels.
2. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Fix the stray `</custom>` tag, fill in `<title>`, and remove or populate the empty `rel="shortcut icon"` href.
4. Hide the `click.chp` pixel (`display:none` or the existing 1x1 wrapper) and consolidate redundant pixels where possible.
5. Check the Movable Ink feed for the empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte` value and add a fallback.
6. Re-run the compliance checks on the raw `.eml` to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass. Resolve a sample of `click.s.kohls.com` links to verify destinations and UTMs.
7. Shorten the plain-text URL ratio by using shorter tracked links or adding more descriptive text. This is low priority.
