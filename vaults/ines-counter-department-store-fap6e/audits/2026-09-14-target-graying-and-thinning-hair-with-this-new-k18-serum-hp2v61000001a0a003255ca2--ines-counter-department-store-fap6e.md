# Target graying and thinning hair with this NEW K18 serum
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-14**
## Executive summary

- Well, look at that "before and after" scalp shot right at the top of the scroll — that got me. I'm 47, I've watched my part get a little wider every year, and a brand finally showing an actual head of hair with "less grey, more hair" callouts instead of a 25-year-old model is the first time in a week of these emails that felt like it was made for me instead of at me. The K18 hair serum section with the "up to 19,000 more hairs and 70% fewer greys" clinical claim is genuinely compelling — that's Ulta-caliber ingredient storytelling I don't usually get from Sephora's blast emails.
- But then it does the thing Sephora always does to me: it can't leave well enough alone. Before I even get to the K18 story I have to wade past a generic "50% off Select Beauty, TODAY ONLY" banner that's basically a rerun of Wednesday's and Thursday's 50%-off sends, then after K18 it just... reverts to an unrelated eight-item grid (Rhode, Charlotte Tilbury, Guerlain, Valentino) with zero connection to hair or greying, then 4X points, then a holiday-drops teaser. It's the "Sephora shows me eleven different things" problem I flagged on the 12th, just with a genuinely good hook buried in the middle of it.
- Would I click? Yes, specifically on the K18 module — that's the one piece of real content here. Would I forward it? Maybe to my sister who's been complaining about her thinning part, cropped to just that section. Would I engage with the rest of the email? No, and Neiman Marcus's "25% off one item" send I reviewed was at least honest about being one offer, even if the fine print buried it — this email doesn't even try to be one offer.

## What's working

- The before/after scalp photo with "LESS GREY" and "MORE HAIR" callout labels is specific, visual proof — not a stock model shot.
- "Retain up to 19,000 more hairs and 70% fewer greys" is a concrete clinical stat, not vague "healthy hair" fluff.
- "Only at Sephora" framing on the K18 launch gives me a reason this has to be Sephora and not Ulta or the K18 site direct.
- Beauty Insider tier bar (Insider / VIB / Rouge) at the bottom at least acknowledges I'm a member, even if it's way too far down to matter.

## What's weak

- The K18 story — the actual reason I'd open this email — is sandwiched between a generic 50% off banner and a completely unrelated fragrance/skincare grid, so it reads like an afterthought instead of the hero.
- "TRENDING NOW / Thank us later" with a plain "Shop Now" button tells me nothing — trending in what category, for whom?
- Eight unrelated product tiles (Rhode, Guerlain, Valentino, Cyklar) dumped after the K18 section with no thread connecting them to hair or greying — classic Sephora "throw the whole store at her" habit I called out on the 10th and 12th.
- 4X points module, "New for Fall" fragrance block, and "Holiday Drops" teaser all compete for attention in the same email — that's four separate campaigns stacked, same overload issue Kohl's and Sephora's own Stacking Weekend send have been running all week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the entire email with the K18 hero — subject, preview, and top banner should all be about the hair story, not a generic 50%-off banner that could belong to any Tuesday send.
- 2. Cut the unrelated 8-item grid entirely, or replace it with 2-3 complementary hair/scalp products so the whole email stays on-theme.
- 3. Move the 50% off banner and 4X points/holiday teaser into a separate send — don't compete with your one genuinely good story.
- 4. Add a Beauty Insider callout near the K18 module specifically ("Rouge members: earn X points on this") since that's the one place tier relevance would actually land.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New K18 serum: fewer greys, more hair in 12 weeks`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your part is about to look different (K18, only at Sephora)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Clinically shown to reduce grey and boost density — exclusive to Sephora`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The before/after we couldn't stop looking at`
