# Make Dad’s day
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-02**
## Executive summary

- Neiman Marcus just changed the channel, and I noticed. After three weeks of Designer Sale banners — the same red hero, same 50% off headline I called out on May 31 when they sent me the Saint Laurent Mombasa editorial — this "THANKS, DAD" flat-lay is a gear shift I genuinely appreciate. The collage is styled well: a nice watch, what looks like a structured grooming kit, sunglasses, a tie, something that reads like a suede wallet. This is the right Neiman's register. Nothing here screams mass-market.
- But here's my problem: I'm a 47-year-old woman who shops for herself first and gifts second. There is no offer in this email — no points multiplier, no complimentary gift wrap, no "order by 6/12 for delivery." The CTAs are soft text links, not confident buttons, and not a single product is named or priced. Neiman's is asking me to browse a gift guide on pure goodwill, with zero hook.
- I'll mark this as read. Maybe I'll pull up the gift guide on my own the week of Father's Day, but this email didn't make that happen today. Nothing to forward — there's no "you need to see this" in it.

## What's working

- **Clean visual hierarchy.** "THANKS, DAD" in dark green is the clear anchor — eye lands there first, no contest.
- **Assortment reads appropriately elevated.** The watch, sunglasses, cologne, and grooming set all look like they belong at Neiman's, not a Kohl's clearance stack.
- **Timely pivot.** After the Designer Sale grind, a single-occasion Father's Day note actually feels fresh — they did not run the red banner again, and I noticed.
- **No clutter.** One message, one occasion, no competing promo blocks. This is the opposite of the Macy's "confirm now for your extra 25% off" triggered-email chaos I reviewed recently.

## What's weak

- **Zero offer, zero incentive.** No points bonus, no free monogramming, no gift-wrap callout. Saks would have threaded in a member perk here; Neiman's gave me nothing to act on today versus next Thursday.
- **Text links, not buttons.** "Shop Father's Day Gifts" and "Shop Dad's Essentials" are small, low-contrast text links sitting quietly under the collage. After a week of Designer Sale red CTAs, this is a whisper.
- **No product names, no prices.** I can see there's a watch and a cologne bottle, but I have no idea what they are or what they cost. Neiman's showed me the Saint Laurent Mombasa *by name* on May 31 — that's the standard they set for themselves.
- **Subject line is table-stakes.** Every retailer in my inbox this week — from Kohl's to Sephora — will run some variant of "Dad" in the subject line. This doesn't cut through at all.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a concrete hook** — "Complimentary gift wrap + 3X points on men's through 6/15" would make me act today instead of maybe later. One loyalty peg is all it takes for someone like me.
- 2. **Replace the text links with a single confident button** — full-width on mobile, high contrast. "Shop the Father's Day Guide" as one anchor CTA, not two competing text whispers.
- 3. **Name and price at least one hero product** — pull the watch forward, give me the brand and a price. Neiman's does this beautifully in editorial mode (the Mombasa send on May 31 is the proof of concept); apply the same discipline here.
- 4. **Sharpen the subject** — "Make Dad's day" is interchangeable with every other brand in my inbox this week.

- **Subject Alt B:** `The Neiman Marcus Father's Day edit`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Watches, grooming, and accessories he'll reach for every day.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Curated gifts, complimentary wrap, and points on every purchase.`

## Technical Audit — Neiman Marcus "Make Dad's day"

**Sender:** NeimanMarcus@e.neimanmarcus.com | **Date:** 2026-06-02

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is a standard Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) deployment using AMPscript for personalization. QA captured four warnings — two compliance header gaps and two deliverability signals — with no hard failures. The primary concerns are unresolved merge-token ambiguity, missing one-click unsubscribe headers, and an unverifiable link graph due to full click-wrapping.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**32 tracking links — destinations unverified**
All outbound links are wrapped in click-redirect URLs (SFMC tracking domain). The HTTP probe skipped all 32. Destination landing pages, UTM parameters, and redirect chain integrity cannot be confirmed without manually resolving each redirect.

**`@refid` CONCAT no-op**
```
@refid = CONCAT(@base_refid,"")
```
CONCAT with an empty string literal is a pass-through — `@refid` equals `@base_refid` exactly. If this variable feeds link query strings or tracking IDs, the extra CONCAT is dead weight and signals a copy-paste artifact. Confirm whether `@refid` was intended to append a suffix (e.g., a variant or split indicator).

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Redundant and conflicting viewport meta tags**
Three commented-out `<meta name="viewport">` tags remain in `<head>`:
```html
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, user-scalable=no" />-->
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=320, initial-scale=0.29">-->
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=1024">-->
```
These are inert as comments but indicate iterative debugging left in production HTML. Low risk, high clutter.

**`<title>` tag has trailing whitespace**
```html
<title>Neiman Marcus </title>
```
Minor, but some preview-pane implementations surface the `<title>` value. Strip the trailing space.

**Duplicate responsive breakpoints across two `<style>` blocks**
The document defines responsive rules at `600px` in block 1 and then again at `480px`/`520px` in a second `<style>` block. No conflict detected in the truncated source, but split style blocks increase parse risk in older Outlook WebApp and Notes clients.

**Alt text — unverifiable**
The HTML is truncated before `<img>` tags appear. Alt attributes cannot be confirmed. This is flagged for manual verification: all product images require descriptive `alt` text; decorative spacers require `alt=""`.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Commented-out SHA-256 scenario is dead code**
```html
<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->
```
This block is doubly-escaped (HTML comment wrapping a JS-style comment). It will never execute. The active code path sets `@user` via an `EMAIL_ID` lookup against `NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, not a hashed email. If this path was meant to support hashed-email identity matching (e.g., for onsite personalization or ad pixels), it is silently broken. Confirm intended behavior and remove dead code.

**Four lookups per send against two DEs**
```
Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", "EMAIL_ID", ...)  ×2
Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "uEm", ...)
Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "cmdid", ...)
```
`@emailid` falls back to a second lookup if not present in the sendable DE. At scale this adds render-time latency. Not a bug, but worth noting if send volume is high.

**No fallback values visible**
None of the AMPscript lookups include `IF EMPTY()` guards in the visible source. If a subscriber row is absent from `NM_CRM_Feed`, `@uEm` and `@cmdid` resolve to empty strings. Confirm these variables are not used in conditionals that could blank out content blocks.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header absent** *(QA WARN)*
The AgentMail relay did not capture a `List-Unsubscribe` header. Gmail and Yahoo (as of February 2024 bulk sender requirements) require this header. Its absence puts delivery to Gmail at risk for senders over 5,000/day. Evidence: QA header scan, no value recorded.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` absent — one-click unsubscribe unsupported** *(QA WARN, RFC 8058)*
One-click unsubscribe (the `POST` mechanism required by Gmail/Yahoo) is not present. These two headers are typically injected by SFMC at send time via the `%%unsub_center_url%%` token; confirm the sending configuration includes them and that the AgentMail relay is not stripping them.

**CAN-SPAM physical address and opt-out link**
Not verifiable from the truncated source — footer is cut off. Manually confirm a physical postal address and functioning unsubscribe link appear in the rendered footer.

**SPF/DKIM authentication status unknown** *(QA WARN)*
`Authentication-Results` header not captured by the relay. `e.neimanmarcus.com` is an SFMC envelope domain; SPF/DKIM should pass if the SAP (Sender Authentication Package) is correctly configured. Cannot confirm from available data.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**UTM parameters — unverifiable**
All 32 links are click-wrapped; no raw destination URLs are visible in the source. UTM attribution (source, medium, campaign, content) cannot be audited without resolving the redirects. Recommend extracting a decoded link map from SFMC before deployment.

**`@base_refid` / `@refid` as tracking anchor**
The `refid` variable appears to be the primary campaign-level tracking token fed into destination URLs. Since `CONCAT(@base_refid,"")` is a no-op (see §2), the value passed downstream equals `@base_refid` verbatim. Verify this token is correctly appended to destination URLs and is consistent with the Campaign Calendar entry.
