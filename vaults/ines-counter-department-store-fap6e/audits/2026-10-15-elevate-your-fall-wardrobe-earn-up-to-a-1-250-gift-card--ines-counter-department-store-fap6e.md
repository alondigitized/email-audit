# Elevate your fall wardrobe & earn up to a $1,250 gift card
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-15**
## Executive summary

- Neiman's again, and this is the third gift-card send in three days. The 10/13 "Treat yourself & earn a $100–$1,250 gift card" email had the same ladder. Today it's a plum block instead of tomato red, with a "Fall wardrobe" subject on top. I got the offer in two seconds, and I do like a spend table I can read at a glance. But I have seen this ladder before, so it didn't excite me.
- The designer grid is the part that feels like Neiman's. Moncler, Louboutin, Jimmy Choo, Valentino and Burberry are what I'd expect from a counter-and-closet shop. Then the nine photos sit on top of six identical plum "SHOP…" bars, and none of them links to anything I can tell is specific. It's a catalog page with a coupon code stapled to it.
- Would I click? Maybe, if I were already planning a coat or boots. It's a "mark as read and remember the code" email, not one I'd forward. The 10/14 "Spotlight on Shearling" email was the one I liked. This one is a sale email dressed up as a wardrobe edit.

## What's working

- The plum hero with the spend-and-earn table is clear. $500 buys $100 back and $5,000 buys $1,250, and I can read it without squinting.
- "Includes Beauty & Fragrance" is the line that caught my eye. I'm a beauty-counter regular, so that line matters to me.
- Code OURGIFT and the "Limited Time" tag sit right under the headline. The deadline is spelled out in the fine print: 10/20/26 at 10 PM CT.
- The designer names under each product tell me exactly what I'm looking at. That's respectful, and it beats Macy's-style sticker clutter.
- The colors are calm and the layout is clean. It's much less shouty than Kohl's "Save 20%" header, which I've now seen five or six times since 10/8.

## What's weak

- This is the third near-identical gift-card ladder in three days, after 10/13's "Treat yourself" and the "$200 off" run before that. The fatigue is real.
- The beauty mention is one small line. Nothing in the grid is beauty, fragrance or a counter brand. Sephora's makeup-set send at least showed me products.
- The fine print is a wall of text, and the important rules are buried in it. Sale items don't count, the gift card arrives within 8 weeks, and returns can forfeit the award. A single "Exclusions Apply" in tiny type up top doesn't make this feel like an honest offer.
- Six identical plum buttons make a boring block with no hierarchy. "Shop Handbags" and "Shop Home" read the same as everything else.
- There's no tier or member recognition. I'm a loyal customer who wants points back, and this treats me as a stranger. It also doesn't say whether the card is "earned" through InCircle.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the ladder to a single, strong headline and show a worked example, such as "Spend $750, get a $175 card." Then bring the exclusions up near the hero in readable type instead of in a wall at the bottom.
- 2. Put at least one beauty or fragrance tile in the grid. "Includes Beauty" is a real hook for me, so show it.
- 3. Link each product tile to its own page and make the tiles feel like a curated edit, not a catalog. Seasonal copy like "boots and shearling" would help.
- 4. Break up the six identical buttons, or cut them to three that match the grid.
- 5. Space out the sends. If the offer has run since 10/13, give me a reason to open that isn't just the same table.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Spend $750, get a $175 gift card — through 10/20`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Boots, shearling & beauty: earn up to $1,250 back`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Beauty & fragrance count. Code OURGIFT ends 10/20 at 10 PM CT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Moncler, Louboutin, Jimmy Choo and more — see how much you'd earn.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Pinch-zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks user zoom in clients that honor it. This conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **No document language:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` or `xml:lang` attribute. Screen readers can't pick the right pronunciation.
- **Checkbox-hack nav:** The mobile menu relies on `.navbar-checkbox:checked~.menu` and `.hamburger`/`.close` toggles. Gmail and many Outlook builds don't support this, and the truncated source doesn't show a fallback. The hamburger-nav CSS, including the `top: 77px` hard-coded menu offset, is more fragile than a plain stacked nav.
- **Non-standard selectors:** `*[class=contenttable]` and similar are exact-match attribute selectors. They fail if an element ever carries more than one class. The DTD URL is also `https://www.w3.org/TR/xhtml1/...` rather than the canonical `http://` form. Both are minor.
- **Alt text and image sizing:** Not assessable from the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Template comment left in the live HTML:** The top of the document contains an unstripped SFMC/AMPscript instruction comment: `<!--REQUIRED variables: @email ... Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", "EMAIL_ID", ...) ... Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "uEm", ...) ... CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW -->`.
  - It exposes internal data extension names and field names (`NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`, `uEm`, `cmdid`) to every recipient.
  - It also shows an unresolved authoring prompt ("CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW") and a leftover `<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->`.
  - It sits before the DOCTYPE, which can push some clients into quirks mode.
- No unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `@var` tokens appear in the visible rendered markup.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe not found (WARN):** QA notes the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. This is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post not found (WARN):** One-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) can't be verified, for the same reason. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results not found (WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `NeimanMarcus@e.neimanmarcus.com`, a subdomain, so alignment needs checking on the raw message.
- **Not auditable:** The visible footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated section.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

2. Add `lang="en"` and `xml:lang="en"` to `<html>`.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
