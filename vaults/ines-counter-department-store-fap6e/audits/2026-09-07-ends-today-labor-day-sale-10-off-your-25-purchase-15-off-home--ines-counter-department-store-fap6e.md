# ENDS TODAY: Labor Day Sale + $10 off your $25 purchase + 15% off home ⏳
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-07**
## Executive summary

- Well, it's Labor Day email number six for me this week, and this one's the "everything must go before midnight" version — Kohl's threw the entire store at the wall: women's, kids, home, luggage, rugs, athletic shoes, a Michigan t-shirt, a Ninja Slush machine, and a Sephora banner for good measure. My thumb went numb scrolling. Compare that to Neiman Marcus's "The Ultimate Coverup" send I opened this week — one gorgeous sequin jacket, one clean headline, done. This is the opposite instinct: throw ten offers at me and hope one sticks.
- The one thing that actually stopped me was the little "Picked with you in mind (your great taste makes it easy)" section near the bottom — a Ninja Slush machine, bath towels, a memory foam mattress topper, and Vanderbilt jeans. That's an attempt at personalization, but it lands oddly generic for a 47-year-old who buys at counter events, not a mattress topper aisle.
- I'm not clicking. I'll skim for the Kohl's Cash barcode since I do use that mechanic when I'm already planning a trip, then archive. Not forwarding — nothing here is gift-worthy or "look at this" material, it's just Tuesday-grind retail email.

## What's working

- The "ENDS TODAY" urgency banner up top is at least credible — today really is the last day, unlike some of the vaguer "hurry" language I've seen from Macy's.
- $10 off $25 plus $5 Kohl's Cash per $25 is a real, stackable mechanic and it's legible at a glance.
- The barcode/coupon codes near the bottom (SHOP10, HOME15) are genuinely useful if I'm actually going to redeem in-store — that's very on-brand for how I shop.
- Category nav buttons (Women/Kids/Home) up top give me a fast lane if I don't want to scroll the whole store.

## What's weak

- This is an absolute wall of offers — 50% off Carter's, 40% off floor care, 50% off bedding, 20% off home decor, 20% off clothing, 75% off Sephora at Kohl's, 50% off luggage, 30% off rugs, 25% off shoes, 20% off team gear, plus clearance up to 85%. Nothing is the hero; everything is the hero, which means nothing is.
- Zero beauty-counter tone, zero designer-access language, zero tier/points clarity beyond a small "Earn 5% Rewards" line at the very top that I almost missed — for someone like me who won't touch a card without points back, that should be far more prominent.
- The 75% off Sephora-at-Kohl's block is buried two-thirds down the email under luggage and rugs — if Kohl's wants beauty shoppers like me to bite, that block needs to be near the top, not sandwiched between bedding and suitcases.
- "Picked with you in mind" personalization doesn't feel personalized at all — a drink maker, bath towels, and a mattress topper isn't reading my history, it's reading "generic household."
- This is the sixth Kohl's Labor Day email I've gotten this week (9/2 through today), and the format hasn't meaningfully changed since the 9/5 sends — same coupon codes, same $10/$5 stack, just repackaged with more categories crammed in.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to one hero offer above the fold — either the $10-off-$25 mechanic or the Sephora beauty callout, not eleven competing discount tiles. Neiman Marcus proved this week that one strong visual beats a wall of percentages.
- 2. Move the Sephora-at-Kohl's 75% off block to the very top if beauty shoppers are a target segment — right now it's my only real reason to open, and it's buried.
- 3. Make the Kohl's Rewards/Cash-back language bigger and repeat it near the CTA — I want to see "earn 5% back" right next to "Shop Now," not in tiny gray type up top.
- 4. Actually personalize the "picked for you" block using purchase history — if I've bought beauty or apparel, don't show me a drink maker.
- 5. Since this is send #6 this week with the same coupon codes, start varying the creative format day-to-day (rotate which category leads) so loyal openers don't feel like they're getting the same email on repeat.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Beauty at Kohl's: up to 75% off Sephora, ends today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: $10 off $25 + your Rewards Cash, today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 5% back in Rewards on everything you shop.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sephora, home, and clearance — all stacking today.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 8 images flagged with missing `alt` text, including tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `event`, several `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` render-tracking pixels) and the primary click-tracking image `e8b61235-...`. Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` (empty, not absent) with `aria-hidden="true"` for spec compliance — one pixel (`mi.kohls.com/p/up/...`) already does this correctly, but others (the `chp.kohls.com/o/...` beacon, `demdex.net/event`, and the `/p/rp/*.png` render pixels) do not.

- Web font `@import`/`<link>` calls to `fonts.googleapis.com` (Roboto, Montserrat) are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` — correct pattern to prevent Outlook fallback issues.
