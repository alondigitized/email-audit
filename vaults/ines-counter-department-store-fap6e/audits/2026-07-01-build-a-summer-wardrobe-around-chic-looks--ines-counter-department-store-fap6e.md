# Build a summer wardrobe around chic looks
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-01**
## Executive summary

- This is Neiman Marcus doing what it does when it wants you to forget it just blasted you with an extra-40%-off stunner yesterday — it pivots to editorial. Clean model, sophisticated plaid moment, Brunello Cucinelli name-drop. I appreciate the palate cleanser. But I'm 47, I cross-shop this store regularly, and I know the difference between a brand talking to me because it has something to offer and a brand talking to me to fill airspace. This is the latter.
- "Winning Combination" is a fine headline. The look is genuinely good — black leather jacket, plaid skirt, knee-highs, the kind of put-together thing I'd want to actually wear. The product flatlay below breaks it down into shoppable pieces, which earns points. But there's no offer, no event, no member pricing, no urgency of any kind. And I say that as someone who's still feeling the glow from that crisp typographic sale email that landed this morning. After that? This feels like a step backward in value.
- I'd mark this as read. I might tap "Shop Brunello Cucinelli" if I had a occasion coming up and some time to browse. I would not forward this to a friend.

## What's working

- **The editorial hero earns its space.** The model, the outdoor setting, the sophisticated layered look — this is the NM I trust for style, not the screaming-markdown NM I sometimes dread.
- **"Shop Brunello Cucinelli" is a specific, confident CTA.** Calling out the brand by name signals curation, not chaos. That's the Saks Fifth Avenue tone I want from NM.
- **The product flatlay is genuinely useful.** Breaking out the jacket, sunglasses, loafers, skirt, and bag individually gives me a path to buy one piece without committing to a whole look.
- **The layout is clean, no render bugs.** Text is legible, images are crisp, nothing overlaps. After watching Macy's occasionally turn their "last chance" urgency emails into layout messes, this holds up fine.
- **The demographic signals are right for me.** This isn't a 22-year-old influencer look. The styling is age-appropriate, the brands are my tier, and the vibe says "she has a life and a closet," not "she has a TikTok."

## What's weak

- **No offer. None.** I "buy at the brand event." There is no event here. This morning's extra-40%-off email existed; why am I getting purely editorial content this afternoon? Even a member exclusive or a complimentary alteration offer would have given me a reason to click today.
- **No loyalty signal whatsoever.** I have an NM InCircle account. This email has no acknowledgment that I exist as a known customer. Compare that to the 6/29 Designer Sale email, which at least gave me a specific sale frame to hang my intention on.
- **"Shop Designer Collections" is the soft CTA I don't need.** That's the equivalent of "explore our homepage." "Shop Brunello Cucinelli" works; "Shop Designer Collections" is filler.
- **The personal stylist callout is buried below the fold.** That's actually the most differentiated offer NM has versus Nordstrom and Saks, and it's an afterthought tucked below the product flatlay.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a member-priced or event hook to the hero.** Even "InCircle members: complimentary style consult with purchase" transforms this from browseable to actionable. Kohl's manages to attach a tangible reward (Kohl's Cash) to every send, even the editorial-leaning ones — NM has the tools, it's not using them.
- 2. **Lead with the stylist offer.** "Shop the Look. Or let us style you." That's actually Neiman Marcus's differentiated weapon against Nordstrom's salesforce and Saks's in-store events. Move it above the fold.
- 3. **Kill "Shop Designer Collections."** Give me a second specific brand or category — "Shop Toteme" or "Shop Summer Suiting" — anything with a noun I can visualize.
- 4. **Bring back the urgency signal from this morning's email.** That 8/10 send worked partly because it had a "starts now" hook. Even a "new arrivals, weekly" badge would give this email a reason-to-exist today instead of three months from now.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shoppable looks from the season's most elevated brands — plus complimentary styling.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Mix a jacket, a plaid skirt, and your summer plans. We'll show you how.`

## Technical Audit — Neiman Marcus "Build a summer wardrobe" Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is an AMPscript-driven Salesforce Marketing Cloud template. The most significant issues are a commented-out SHA256 user token that may be leaking raw email or sending a null value into tracking URLs, and absent `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers that put the send at risk under Google/Yahoo bulk sender requirements.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**36 tracking links skipped (QA)**
All click-redirect domains were excluded from HTTP probing, so destination URLs, redirect chains, and UTM parameter carriage are unverified for the entire send.

**Plain-text version is 86% URLs**
3,138 of 3,647 plain-text characters are URLs. Spam filters (SpamAssassin `MIME_HTML_MOSTLY`, Barracuda scoring) flag ratios above 70%. The plain-text alternative needs human-readable content, not a URL dump.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Conflicting viewport meta tags**
The active tag is correct:
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">
```
Three alternatives are commented out directly below it (fixed-width 320, 1024, and a `user-scalable=no` variant). These are dead weight; commented HTML still ships in the payload and signals housekeeping debt, but they are not functional bugs.

**Two separate `<style>` blocks with overlapping breakpoints**
Block 1 triggers at `max-width: 600px`. Block 2 triggers at `max-device-width: 480px` and `max-width: 520px`. The 480–520px range is covered by both blocks with different class targets (`.mobile` vs `.ImgSize`/`.TxtSize`). No confirmed rendering conflict, but the split increases maintenance risk and parse overhead in older clients.

**`<title>` has a trailing space**
```html
<title>Neiman Marcus </title>
```
Minor; some preview clients surface the title as tooltip text.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**SHA256 hashing is commented out — `@user` value is undefined or raw**

The template header declares the following variable block:
```
@user Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", "EMAIL_ID", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email)
```
followed by this commented-out override:
```html
<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->
```
The comment marker `<!--/* ... */ -->` disables the AMPscript `SET` call entirely. If `@user` is embedded in any tracking or pixel URL (e.g., for ad-platform identity matching), it is currently resolving to the raw `EMAIL_ID` lookup value — not a hashed email — or to an empty string if the lookup fails. This is a potential PII exposure in click URLs and should be confirmed against the actual link parameters in the unscrubbed source.

**`@uEm` and `@cmdid` carry CRM identity in links**
Both variables are looked up from `NM_CRM_Feed` and injected into URLs. No validation or fallback is visible in the truncated source. If a subscriber record is absent from the CRM feed, empty tokens will silently corrupt URL parameters.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header absent (QA)**
Google and Yahoo have required `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click) on all bulk sends ≥5,000/day since February 2024. Their absence causes Gmail to suppress the native unsubscribe UI and increases spam complaint rate, which feeds back into deliverability scoring. This must be set at the ESP/MTA layer in SFMC's send configuration, not in the HTML.

**`Authentication-Results` header not captured**
SPF and DKIM pass/fail status is unknown because the AgentMail relay did not surface the header. The sending domain `e.neimanmarcus.com` should have both SPF and DKIM aligned to `neimanmarcus.com` for DMARC compliance. This cannot be confirmed from the captured source.

**CAN-SPAM physical address and unsubscribe link**
The HTML is truncated before the footer; assuming the physical address and opt-out link are present in the omitted portion. If not, that is a CAN-SPAM §7704(a)(5) and (a)(3) violation.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**UTM parameters unverifiable**
Because all 36 click-redirect URLs were skipped in QA probing, it is not possible to confirm whether UTM campaign, source, and medium parameters are correctly appended to final destination URLs after redirect resolution. Manual spot-check of 3–5 representative links against `neimanmarcus.com` GA4 / Adobe Analytics inbound parameters is required.

**`@refid` constructed as `CONCAT(@base_refid, "")`**
This appends an empty string, producing a refid identical to `@base_refid`. If the intent was to append a suffix (e.g., audience segment), the second argument is blank and the merge is a no-op.
