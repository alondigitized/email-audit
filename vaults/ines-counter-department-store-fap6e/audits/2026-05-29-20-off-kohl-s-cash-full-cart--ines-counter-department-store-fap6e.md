# 20% off 😊 Kohl's Cash 🤑 Full cart 🛒
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-29**
## Executive summary

- Thirteen. That's where I land now — thirteen Kohl's emails in roughly two weeks, and this one walks in wearing the same closing act I saw on May 24: "Kohl's Cash 🤑 Full cart 🛒" is the same creative beat, just reshuffled. Yesterday they finally pivoted to vacation styles. Today they're back to the formula. I noticed the retreat.
- The home goods angle is at least a coherent story — "no place like home" lands better than a fifth iteration of Memorial Day countdown. But I'm the woman at the Chanel counter at Nordstrom, not loading a cart with throw pillows. The 30% off kitchen-and-dining block made me pause for half a second because the blue-and-white table setting photography actually looks elevated — more Macy's than Kohl's — but then "Shop Now" appeared for the sixth time and I remembered where I was. There's no beauty, no skincare, nothing that signals Kohl's knows I'm a loyalty member who shops their cosmetics aisle.
- Marking this read. Not clicking. Not forwarding.

## What's working

- The 20% off offer is visible before a single scroll — on mobile that's the first thing the eye catches, no hunting required
- Kohl's Cash appears in both the header strip and the footer earn block; for a points tracker like me, that double-reinforcement matters
- The Father's Day section gives a concrete deadline — "June 21" is a rare, credible date in a brand that otherwise lives in perpetual open-ended-sale time
- The kitchen-and-dining 30% off imagery reads cleaner and more aspirational than the rest of the email; Macy's would be proud of that table setting shot
- No render issues anywhere — loads clean, no broken blocks, no overlapping text

## What's weak

- The 🤑 emoji in the subject is an immediate brand downgrade — I cross-shop Saks; that face signals a flash-sale pop-up, not a loyalty communication
- "Full cart 🛒" is the fourth or fifth time I've seen that construction in a Kohl's subject line this month; it's no longer clever, it's wallpaper
- Not a single beauty product in the entire email — if Kohl's carries Neutrogena, Maybelline, or NYX, you'd never know from this send
- "Shop Now" repeated across every section is category-agnostic noise — Sephora always tells me *exactly* what I'm clicking toward ("Shop 50% Off Beauty"), Kohl's doesn't
- The Father's Day men's gifts block — clothing, shoes, sports fan gear — is a hard pivot that reads like a different audience entirely

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add one beauty or skincare row.** Even a single "20% off beauty + earn Kohl's Cash" block would make me feel like a known customer. Macy's emails me their Star Rewards beauty events separately; Kohl's should learn that segmentation exists.
- 2. **Retire "full cart 🛒" as a subject hook.** It's appeared in at least two subjects this month and it's spent now.
- 3. **Kill the 🤑 emoji.** Replace it with nothing, or swap the whole subject emoji set for one useful one if you must. The money-mouth face feels like clickbait in a loyalty inbox.
- 4. **Put a deadline on the 20% coupon.** "Shop by June 1" would make me move. An open-ended coupon feels like a bluff.
- 5. **Pick one primary CTA per email.** If this is a home goods send, say "Shop 20% Off Home." Let the secondary sections be secondary.
- **Subject Alt A:** `20% off + Kohl's Cash — home, kitchen & more`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your coupon is here: 20% off home & Father's Day gifts`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus earn $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 spent — shop through the weekend`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Kitchen, patio, fragrance, and gifts on sale now`

### 1. Technical Summary

The email uses standard HTML4 Transitional with table-based layout, MSO conditionals, and Google Fonts — a conventional ESP send. No hard errors found; all flags are warnings surfaced by header capture limitations in the AgentMail relay and missing alt attributes on tracking pixels.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 tracking/click-redirect links skipped** by HTTP probe — all routed through `click.s.kohls.com` and `click.chp.kohls.com`. Link integrity cannot be confirmed programmatically; manual spot-check of CTA destinations is recommended.

Three distinct tracking pixel systems are active simultaneously:
- `click.chp.kohls.com` — Coherent Path behavioral pixel
- `mi.kohls.com` — MessageGears ESP pixel (`mi_u=604230016`, `mi_ecmp=1013486_2026529`)
- `kohls.demdex.net` — Adobe Audience Manager (AAM) segment pixel (`d_sid=13245196`)

This is expected for a retailer of this scale but means any pixel fire failure is silent. No broken `src` URLs detected in the truncated source.

**No issues with link structure itself.** The `<custom>` tag on line `</custom>` after the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` pixel is malformed HTML — not a standard tag. Low impact (browsers ignore unknown tags) but indicates a template stitching artifact.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**11 images missing `alt` text** — all confirmed by QA scan:

| Image | Type | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| `271378b2...` (chp.kohls.com) | Tracking pixel | Low — hidden pixel, but screen readers will announce it |
| `o.gif` (mi.kohls.com) | ESP open-pixel | Low — `aria-hidden="true"` is present ✓ |
| `event` (demdex.net) | AAM pixel | Medium — no `aria-hidden` |
| `2a5adc5a...` through `916d6fa0...` (mi.kohls.com) | Content images | **High** — these appear to be visible product/promo images with no alt text; images-off renders are fully inaccessible |

The `o.gif` pixel correctly uses `aria-hidden="true"` — the other tracking pixels do not. All visible content images (`.png` filenames with `email_name=260529_DG_Email_Friday` in the query string) lack alt text entirely.

`user-scalable=0` in the viewport meta tag blocks zoom on mobile — an accessibility regression for users with low vision. The correct value is `user-scalable=yes` or omitting the attribute.

`<title></title>` is empty. Some email clients surface the title tag; a blank title reduces preheader and accessibility metadata.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unrendered merge tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`, `%FIRST_NAME%`) detected in the truncated source.

`mi_u=604230016` appears hardcoded rather than personalized in some pixel URLs — if this is a shared/test subscriber ID, pixel attribution will be incorrect in production. Confirm this resolves to per-recipient values at send time.

`mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` in the `916d6fa0` pixel URL has an **empty value** — a Kohl's Cash expiration date parameter that was not populated. This is a data merge failure; if this pixel conditionally controls display logic, the blank value may suppress or misrender Kohl's Cash expiry messaging.
