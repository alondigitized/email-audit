# Your 40% off savings are here today, gone tomorrow ...
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-19**
## Executive summary

- Email sixty-two since Memorial Day — at least that's where I stopped counting. I opened this one because "today, gone tomorrow" is the first subject line from Kohl's this week that didn't feel like an outright reprint of yesterday's. But once I'm inside, the déjà vu hits fast: same 40% off, same hero-to-home-goods structure, different linen outfit. Yesterday they sent me two of these. This morning they're already back.
- The email itself is competently assembled. The linen headline copy — "Light and breezy linen (at a tight and breezy cost)" — is the kind of wordplay that feels like a copywriter actually showed up that day. Prices are on screen with coupon applied, which I appreciate. And the Kohl's Cash callout at the bottom? That registered. I will not put anything on a card that doesn't give me something back.
- But there's no beauty in this email. Not a single module. I cross-shop Nordstrom beauty, Macy's fragrance counter, Saks — and Kohl's just sent me linen pants and an "Outdoor Toys" tile like it forgot who I am. I won't click. I won't forward it. It goes in the read pile.

## What's working

- "Light and breezy linen (at a tight and breezy cost)" — clever headline that earns a second read without screaming.
- Specific post-coupon pricing ($34.99, $29.99) visible in the hero — I know what I'd actually pay before clicking anywhere.
- Kohl's Cash section at the bottom is the right note to close on for a loyalty-conscious shopper like me.
- "Deal Days" banner gives the 40% off a named event, which at least makes this feel like a sale rather than a distress signal.
- Clean render — no broken images, no layout drift, the linen hero loads crisp.

## What's weak

- I already got this 40% offer yesterday — twice. "Today, gone tomorrow" loses credibility when tomorrow's send will almost certainly say the same thing.
- Zero beauty content. Not a single module for fragrance, skincare, or cosmetics. Macy's manages to layer in beauty even on an apparel-led send.
- The home goods grid reads like a liquidation map: Bedding, Bath, Kitchen & Dining, Patio, Decor, Outdoor Toys — six categories, zero commitment to any of them.
- "Shop Now" buttons stacked four times with identical copy — Sephora at least tells me I'm shopping "hair care" or "foundations," not a void.
- The men's section holds prominent real estate in what I assume is a women-targeted send. Either segment harder or bury the men's module.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Rest the 40% off offer for one send.** Lead with Kohl's Cash earning — "Earn $30 Kohl's Cash on a $50 linen haul" reframes the value without repeating the word I've been reading for three days straight. Neiman Marcus at least escalates (70% off after 60% off); Kohl's is just running in place.
- 2. **Add one beauty module.** Even a single Lancôme or Clinique branded tile would change what this email means to me. Macy's does this routinely.
- 3. **Collapse the home goods section** into one focused category — lead with bedding styled as a summer refresh destination, and cut Outdoor Toys entirely from this particular list.
- 4. **Differentiate the CTAs** — "Shop linen," "Shop men's," "Shop shoes" over three generic "Shop Now" buttons gives the reader direction instead of friction.
- 5. **Move the men's section down** or convert it to a thin strip — let the first two scrollable modules be fully women-facing.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Deal Days ends tonight: linen under $35 + Kohl's Cash`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your summer linen haul — 40% off, Kohl's Cash, today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Breezy linen + earn $30 Kohl's Cash on a $50 shop — ends midnight`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop linen, earn points, be done before the humidity wins`

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 tracking links skipped** — all destination URLs route through `click.chp.kohls.com` and `click.s.kohls.com` click-redirect domains; final landing page health is unverified by automated checks.

**Malformed closing tag** — pixel at `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?777LVKQNPNBEVNB4SWCHY2K4JM.60268` closes with `</custom>` instead of a valid HTML tag. Benign in most clients but invalid markup.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**13 content images missing `alt` text** — all `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` render images lack alt attributes. Images-off rendering (common in Outlook, Gmail app on mobile data-saver) leaves these slots blank with no fallback text.

- `611d2b1debdc5ae0.png` / `b0aad5bed92b660a.png` — contain `left_promo_code=LPJ9Y27GJLXBX` (likely promo code tile)
- `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` suffix on `916d6fa08d185233.png` — appears to be a dynamic expiry image with empty date parameter (see also §4)

**Three tracking pixels missing `alt=""`** — `42fdc230`, `o.gif`, `kohls.demdex.net/event` have no alt attribute at all. Should be `alt=""` + `aria-hidden="true"` for screen reader hygiene (two of three lack `aria-hidden`).

**`<title>` is empty** — `<title></title>` prevents screen readers from announcing the email window title.

**`user-scalable=0`** in viewport meta — disables pinch-to-zoom, an accessibility regression for low-vision users; WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
