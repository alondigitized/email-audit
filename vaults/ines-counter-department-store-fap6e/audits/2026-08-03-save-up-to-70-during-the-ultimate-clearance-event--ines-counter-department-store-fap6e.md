# 🚨 Save up to 70% during the ULTIMATE Clearance Event 🚨
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-03**
## Executive summary

- My eye actually paused on the red-on-cream color-block this time — that's a different look from the navy circle I've now seen four times this week — but then I clocked "Kohl's" and "Martha Stewart Collection" and immediately knew I wasn't the customer for this. This is a griddle-and-slow-cooker email dressed up as "up to 70% off," and there is not one beauty item, one accessory, or one thing that speaks to how I actually shop a department store.
- The "Ultimate Clearance Event" banner is bold and legible, I'll give them that, and calling out my Rewards status and 5% earn rate up top is a nice touch of not treating me like a total stranger. But then it dumps me into bedding, bath, kitchen appliances, home decor, and finally an adidas activewear closeout — a full department-store fire sale with zero editing. Sephora's "Beauty Insider Celebration" send (9/10 in my inbox this week) opened with one clear hero offer and stayed in my lane the whole way down. This one just keeps scrolling.
- Would I click? No. Would I forward it? Only to my sister who's actually redoing her kitchen — this is a "pass along," not a "keep for me" email. Marking as read and moving on.

## What's working

- The Rewards member status line and 5% earn callout up top acknowledge I'm a known customer, not a cold lead.
- "Save up to 70%" in giant type is unmissable — no hunting for the number.
- The Martha Stewart Collection intro has a real trust cue (recognizable name, clean product photography of the griddle in use).
- Category nav buttons (Home/Women/Men/Kids) right under the hero let me skip straight past what I don't want, if I bother to look.

## What's weak

- Nothing here says "beauty" or "designer access" — for someone who buys at the counter and cross-shops Nordstrom and Saks, this reads like it's for a completely different shopper.
- It's a kitchen-and-home catalog wearing a "clearance event" headline — bedding, bath, kitchen, decor, then activewear tacked on at the end with no narrative thread.
- No specific dollar amounts or percentages anywhere below the hero — just "refresh every room" with generic lifestyle shots and no pricing, so I can't tell if 70% off actually applies to anything I'd want.
- This is at least my fifth Kohl's email this week and my third "up to X% off" framing — the fatigue is real, and swapping the navy circle for a red diagonal doesn't disguise that.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment me out of home-goods blasts entirely, or if Kohl's insists on sending it, lead with the ONE category I've actually shown interest in — right now this is a mass blast, not a merchandising decision.
- 2. Cut the email in half — five product categories plus an unrelated activewear sale in one send is Macy's-style overload without Macy's tier-program payoff.
- 3. Put an actual price or percentage on at least one hero product (the griddle, the slow cooker) instead of vague "up to 70%" that never resolves into a real number I can act on.
- 4. If Rewards status is going to be featured at the top, tie it to something concrete — "you're at 5% earn, spend $50 more for Tier 2" — the way Sephora's Insider Celebration used my Rouge status as the actual hook, not just a label.

# Technical Audit: Kohl's "ULTIMATE Clearance Event" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard responsive MSO/non-MSO conditional template with heavy third-party tracking instrumentation, but ships with several unclosed compliance/header gaps and missing accessibility attributes; a malformed closing tag was also found in the tracking markup.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Multiple tracking/beacon layers present: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/a7017ae9-...` (1x1 impression pixel with base64/encoded `cp_tp` payload), `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...` and `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/...` open-tracking pixels, `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...` open pixel, and `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager beacon).
- **Malformed markup**: `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>` — a stray `</custom>` closing tag with no matching opening tag. This is invalid HTML and should be removed.
- `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer" />` is set globally — this will strip the `Referer` header on any outbound click, which can break referrer-based attribution on landing pages/analytics that don't rely solely on UTM params.
- QA flagged **55 tracking links skipped** from HTTP probing (redirect/click domains) — link destination validity and final landing pages could not be confirmed programmatically.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- QA confirmed **7+ images missing `alt` text**, including content images (`2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`) and tracking pixels. Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` (some already do, e.g., `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...` and the demdex pixel — both correctly have empty/no alt for decorative/non-content images), but the content images above have no alt attribute at all, which is a genuine accessibility gap for screen readers.
- `<title></title>` is empty — no descriptive title set for the message.
- MSO/non-MSO conditional comment structure for Google Fonts (`<!--[if !mso]><!-->...<!--<![endif]-->`) is correctly implemented, so no Outlook rendering risk from font loading.
- Mobile breakpoint media query (`max-width: 500px`) and viewport meta are present and correctly configured.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No user-facing merge tags (e.g., `{{first_name}}`) are visible in the truncated source.
- One tracking parameter appears to be an **unpopulated merge/dynamic field**: `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_pen` in the `916d6fa08d185233.png` pixel URL — the `mi_kc_redm_end_dte` (redemption end date) value is empty, suggesting a token that failed to resolve at send time.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN]** `List-Unsubscribe` header not found — could not be confirmed via headers (may be an AgentMail relay capture limitation rather than a true absence).
- **[WARN]** `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058, one-click unsubscribe) not found — same caveat applies.
- **[WARN]** No physical mailing address detected in the scanned HTML — required under CAN-SPAM. Note the HTML provided is truncated, so the footer (where addresses typically live) may not have been captured; this should be re-verified against the full message body before treating as a confirmed violation.
- **[WARN]** `Authentication-Results` header not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC status could not be verified from this relay.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Tracking pixels consistently carry `mi_ecmp=1018970_202683` and `email_name=260803_DG_Email_Monday`, indicating consistent campaign/email tagging across instrumentation.
- No standard `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` parameters are visible on the sampled URLs (Kohl's uses proprietary `mi_*`/`cp_*` params instead) — cannot assess GA-style attribution continuity from this data.
- Actual CTA click-through destinations were not present in the truncated HTML and were excluded from HTTP probing by QA (55 tracking links skipped), so **landing page alignment could not be verified**.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the stray `</custom>` closing tag in the `click.s.kohls.com` pixel block — invalid markup.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 7 content images flagged by QA; keep `alt=""` on pure tracking pixels.
3. Populate the empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte` parameter or remove it if the redemption-end-date field is not applicable to this send.
4. Confirm (outside this truncated excerpt) that a physical mailing address and unsubscribe link exist in the footer; if genuinely absent, this is a CAN-SPAM violation requiring immediate fix.
5. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and SPF/DKIM/DMARC (`Authentication-Results`) are actually being sent at the MTA level — re-test outside the AgentMail relay to rule out a capture artifact vs. a real gap.
6. Add descriptive `<title>` content for accessibility/preview purposes.
7. Re-run link validation directly (bypassing redirect-skip logic) to confirm the 55 tracking/CTA links resolve to correct, live landing pages.
