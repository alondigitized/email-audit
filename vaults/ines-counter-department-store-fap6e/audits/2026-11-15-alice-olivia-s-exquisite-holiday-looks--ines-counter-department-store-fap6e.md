# Alice + Olivia’s exquisite holiday looks
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- This is the Neiman's email I'd actually want to receive. It's quiet, and the sequins photo is gorgeous. After the black-and-red "40% OFF" shouting from earlier today ("Your early access ends today: up to 40% off") and the three $650-off pushes in two days, this reads like a brand that remembers I have taste. It follows the Balmain and Versace editorial sends, so the formula holds: one big photo, one serif headline, no code.
- It's also a catalog page with no reason to act now. "Graceful Glamour" tells me nothing. There's no price on any of the three Alice + Olivia pieces, no date, no event, and no points angle. I'm someone who buys at the brand event and won't put a card down without points back. A pretty email that never mentions InCircle, or any reason to buy this week, gets admired and then ignored. I'd scroll to the bottom, enjoy the tweed look, and not click. I wouldn't forward it either, because there's nothing to tell a friend.
- Sephora's holiday sends open with the gift and the points multiplier, and Macy's at least tells me the deadline. This one tells me neither.

## What's working

- The hero photo is lovely. Sequins next to lace and a blazer reads like real occasion dressing, not a clearance rack.
- Calm layout. One image, one headline, two outlined buttons. It doesn't shout the way Kohl's "Stacks of Kohl's Cash" does.
- The three product cutouts on white are clean and easy to take in at a glance.
- The tweed copy, "glistening with metallic threads," sounds like a stylist wrote it, not a promo desk.
- The models don't look 22-only. The styling feels like a grown woman's holiday party.

## What's weak

- No prices anywhere. The velvet mini, the lace halter and the beaded jacket are all unpriced. I can't tell whether this is $300 or $900.
- No loyalty or points cue. After four weeks of $650-off codes, a send with zero member benefit feels like it forgot who I am.
- "Graceful Glamour" gives me no season, event or deadline. Holiday party? Which week? Nothing says.
- The two buttons, "Shop Alice + Olivia" and "Shop Contemporary," are thin outlines in light gray text. The second one is vague.
- The product thumbnails aren't labeled with names or prices, so they look like they link to nothing specific. The tweed look has no link or product detail at all.
- The footer is cluttered, with app, advisor, store, account and address-book links crowded together.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one concrete reason to act on top. For example, name an InCircle points multiplier or a gift-with-purchase on Alice + Olivia, and give it a date.
- 2. Add prices under each product and link each one to its own page. Brand name alone isn't enough.
- 3. Replace "Graceful Glamour" with an occasion and a deadline, like "Holiday party dressing, ships in time for 12/20."
- 4. Make the CTA a solid button with a verb and a noun: "Shop party dresses."
- 5. Link the tweed look, name the piece, and add a "Book a stylist" button. That's the one service I'd actually use.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML source has two confirmed issues: internal ESP configuration is exposed in an HTML comment, and the viewport meta tag blocks zoom. Compliance and authentication can't be confirmed from the data provided. The source is truncated and 17 tracking links were not probed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **17 tracking/click-redirect links were not probed.** The QA run skipped the HTTP probe on these. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **The plain-text part is 3199 of 3691 characters URLs (about 87%).** This points to long tracking URLs in the text alternative. It risks poor plain-text readability and a spam-filter signal.
- No broken or malformed links are confirmed in the visible source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile clients that honor it. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **No `lang` attribute.** The tag is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` with no `lang`, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Weak `<title>`.** It is `Neiman Marcus ` (trailing space) and does not reflect the email content.
- **Fragile exact-match class selectors.** The media-query rules use `*[class=hide]`, `*[class=mobile]` and `*[class=contenttable]`. These only match an element whose class attribute is exactly that value. Elements with multiple classes would silently miss the responsive rules. I can't confirm this from the truncated body.
- **CSS-only hamburger nav.** It relies on `:checked` checkbox sibling selectors, which Gmail and several Outlook builds do not support. I can't confirm the fallback behavior from the truncated source.
- **Alt text and image dimensions were not assessable.** The body was truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens are visible in the rendered portion.
- **Internal ESP logic leaks in the source.** The leading comment is delivered to recipients. It reads `REQUIRED variables: @email ... @user Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", "EMAIL_ID", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email)) ... @uEm = Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", ...) ... CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW`. This exposes internal data extension names (`NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`) and field names (`EMAIL_ID`, `uEm`, `cmdid`). It also shows the template's setup instructions were never stripped. The expressions are also malformed, with unbalanced parentheses and missing `=` signs. The scenario comment `SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256` is left in as well.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found.** QA notes it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed rather than a proven absence.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** One-click unsubscribe support is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The `From` domain `e.neimanmarcus.com` is a subdomain, so alignment should be checked on a direct-delivery copy.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and physical address were not assessable because the source is truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters could not be assessed.** All 17 tracking links route through redirect domains and were not resolved, so UTM presence, landing destinations and campaign naming can't be verified.
- The comment references `@refid` and `@base_refid`. These suggest campaign IDs are appended through the redirect layer rather than as visible UTMs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Strip the ESP and AMPscript-style comment block** from the production template. It leaks internal data extension names and field names.
2. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag.
3. **Add `lang="en"`** to `<html>` and give `<title>` meaningful content.
4. **Confirm headers on a direct-delivery seed.** Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, since the relay may be stripping them.
5. **Resolve the 17 tracking links** with a redirect-following probe to confirm final URLs, status codes and UTM/campaign parameters.
6. **Shorten or trim URLs in the plain-text part**, or reduce the tracking-parameter length, to improve the text-to-URL ratio.
7. **Switch to `*[class~=...]` or plain class selectors** if elements can carry multiple classes, so the responsive rules apply reliably.
8. **Re-run the audit on the full HTML**, since the truncation hid the body, footer, images and unsubscribe markup.
