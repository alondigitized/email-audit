# These hot sets + you = ⚡
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the eighth Sephora email I've had in about a week, and my gut said "another catalog." The top is busy. A Savings Event banner with a "30% off" badge sits above a Scent Mini freebie, and then a big pink "Healthy Hair & Scalp Kit" hero takes over. The sets grid underneath is where the email earns its keep. I like a gift set with a price and a "value" price next to it. Armani Beauty at $55 ($80 value) is the kind of thing I actually shop for.
- But I had to dig for the deal. The subject promised sets, and the first thing I met was a scent sample and a hair kit. The Savings Event is the offer I care about, because it ties to my Insider tier and my points. Its banner is tiny, and the "COMING SOON / LEARN MORE" line made it feel like a tease. On 10/18 the 4X points banner led and I read it first. Here the points message is buried.
- Would I click? Probably yes, on the Armani duo or the Fenty set. Would I forward it? No. It's a long scroll and the fine print is a wall. Macy's tells me exactly what I get for confirming. Neiman Marcus's "First Call" send is repetitive, but it states "up to 40% off" in two seconds. Sephora makes me work for it.

## What's working

- The sets grid shows a sale price next to a value price, like "$55 ($80 value)". I can judge the deal in a glance.
- The "NEW · LIMITED EDITION · ONLY AT SEPHORA" tags give me a reason to buy now instead of waiting for Nordstrom.
- Clean at Sephora badges sit on several products. They match what I clicked on in the 10/18 send.
- The brand mix is solid: Armani, Fenty, Rihanna, Glossier, Nécessaire. It feels like a beauty counter, not a drugstore.
- The Tides Foundation donation note on the hair kit is a nice touch. It treats me like an adult with values.

## What's weak

- The 30% Savings Event badge is small and sits above a "COMING SOON" teaser. The strongest offer gets the least space.
- The subject said "hot sets" but the first screen is a scent sample and a hair kit. Neither is a set.
- Insider points and tier benefits aren't visible anywhere in the body. I won't use a card, or buy at a store, without points back.
- The fine print at the bottom is tiny and dense. The Savings Event terms and the APPLELOVE code rules are hard to read on a phone.
- The email is very long. The Korean haircare, perfume and lip liner sections at the bottom feel like leftovers, not a plan.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one clear offer. Put the Savings Event discount in a full-width banner that says my tier and my dates. Then show the sets under it.
- 2. Put the sets grid right after that banner. Move the hair kit and Scent Mini below it.
- 3. Add Insider points per set. "Earn 4X points on these" would be the push I need.
- 4. Cut the three bottom features and shorten the fine print. Link to the terms instead of printing them.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Gift sets up to $80 value, only at Sephora`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New limited-edition sets, now with Insider savings`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Armani, Fenty, Rihanna and more. See what's in each set and your Insider price.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your Savings Event dates, plus the sets worth grabbing first.`
