# 🚨2X points on your purchase and 5X on ALL Sephora Collection ends tomorrow 🚨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- This is Sephora's sixth or seventh send since 10/2, and my gut reaction was "I know, I know, SAYPOINTS." The 10/3 "Hot drops coming through" send carried the same code and the same "Ends 10/7" deadline. The siren emojis in the subject make it louder than the 10/5 "APP EARLY ACCESS" email did, and the body doesn't live up to the shout. The top block is fine. "Earn Points Faster on Top Brands" has the 2X and 5X badges, the code, and the date. Then it turns into a long scroll of holiday advent calendars and gift sets.
- I'd click, but only because the points deadline is real and I won't pay full price without points back. Sephora does this more clearly than Macy's, which made me "confirm now" for its extra 25%. Neiman Marcus gave me a clean spend ladder ($200 off, up to $1,000+) with one lavender hero. Sephora gives me three or four offers stacked on top of each other. I wouldn't forward this. I'd read it, skim the gift sets, and mark it read.

## What's working

- The points banner at the very top, with the 2X and 5X badges, the "Ends 10/7" date, and the SAYPOINTS code, tells me the deal and the deadline right away.
- Every gift set shows a price next to a "value" figure. For example, the Sephora Collection advent calendar is $99 against a $277 value, and the Crown Affair set is $272 against $347. That's how I compare against what Nordstrom or Saks would charge.
- The "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA • LIMITED EDITION" tags on each set give me a reason to buy at Sephora rather than elsewhere.
- The Tom Ford and Crown Affair sets add a bit of prestige. The Tom Ford set at $100 is the one that feels like designer access.
- Klarna, Afterpay, PayPal and Venmo are listed near the top. That's useful if I'm spreading out holiday spend.
- The barcode at the bottom, plus the Kohl's in-store coupon, is practical for someone who shops in person.

## What's weak

- Three or four different offers compete for the eye: the points event, Holiday Vaults, "Use Your $100 Purchase" with SAVETEN, and a Kohl's 10% off. A tier-program shopper like me wants one clear offer, not four.
- The points multipliers appear twice, once at the top and again mid-email with 2X, 3X, 4X, 5X. The second set is harder to read and I can't tell which tier gets which number.
- The email is extremely long, and most of the length is gift sets that sit between me and anything beauty-related. The gold-confetti background is also busy and made the page feel noisy.
- The 5X applies only to Sephora Collection, which is the house brand. The subject says "ALL Sephora Collection," but the best-looking products in the email are other brands.
- I can't see a clear "Beauty Insider" tier callout. If I'm VIB or Rouge, nothing here acknowledges that. It reads like a message to everyone.
- The "Get Gift Ideas Instantly... TRY IT" block feels like a feature promotion, not a reason to buy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer, the points event, and move the Holiday Vaults below it. Put a single button right under the points banner so the first screen has one job.
- 2. Show a small table of tier multipliers (Insider, VIB, Rouge) so I can see my own number, instead of the repeated 2X-5X graphic.
- 3. Cut the gift-set list to three or four and add a "See all sets" button, instead of six full-height blocks.
- 4. Move the Kohl's coupon and the barcode into a separate in-store send or down into the footer. Right now it competes with the online points offer.
- 5. Replace the two siren emojis with one clear deadline. "Ends tomorrow" is enough, and the sirens read as louder than the content.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Points end tomorrow: 2X on everything, 5X Sephora Collection`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day for bonus points, plus holiday sets from $50`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code SAYPOINTS by 10/7. Advent calendars from $99 count too.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your points window closes 10/7. Here's what's worth buying before it does.`
