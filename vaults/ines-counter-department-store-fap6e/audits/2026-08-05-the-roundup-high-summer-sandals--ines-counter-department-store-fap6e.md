# The Roundup: High-Summer Sandals
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- Well, this is a departure — after two straight days of "spend X, get a gift card" math from Neiman's, they've swung all the way back to pure mood-and-merchandise, no numbers anywhere. It's a clean, editorial sandal roundup: one hero image of a YSL quilted sandal styled on an antique gilt chair, then six designer picks — Fendi, Jimmy Choo, Givenchy, Miu Miu, Gucci, Toteme — laid out like a magazine spread. It's genuinely pretty, and as someone who cross-shops Saks and Nordstrom for exactly this kind of curated "here's what's good right now" editorial, the format itself works for me.
- But there's zero reason for me to act today. No price on a single shoe, no percentage off, no "shop the edit before it sells out" urgency — just "Shop Sandals" and "Shop Must-Have Shoes" sitting there in matching outline boxes. I'd click through out of curiosity on the Toteme kitten-heel thong (that's a shape I actually wear), but I wouldn't call this a purchase trigger, and there's nothing here that reads as "we know you" versus "we know sandals exist in August."
- I wouldn't forward this to a friend — it's a nice-looking catalog page, not a deal worth sharing. I'd open it, scroll once, maybe click Toteme, and move on. Compare that to yesterday's "$50–$500 gift card" send, which I actually engaged with because the math was on the table.

## What's working

- The hero shot — sandals styled on that ornate chair — has real editorial polish, more Nordstrom-catalog than Kohl's-clearance-blast.
- Six-brand curation (Fendi, Jimmy Choo, Givenchy, Miu Miu, Gucci, Toteme) is genuinely relevant to a designer-shoe cross-shopper like me — this is the "designer access tone" I want from Neiman's.
- Clean grid layout, generous white space, legible product labels under each shoe — nothing overlapping, nothing broken.
- Two clear CTA buttons ("Shop Sandals" / "Shop Must-Have Shoes") sit right under the headline, so I don't have to hunt for where to click.

## What's weak

- Not one price, discount, or "new markdown" flag anywhere — after Sephora's crisp "4X points on foundations" send and even Neiman's own gift-card emails this week, this feels like a step backward into no-math territory.
- No tier or loyalty signal at all — I'm an InCircle member context aside, there's nothing here acknowledging me as a repeat shopper, just generic product tiles.
- "THE ROUNDUP" as an eyebrow label is a little precious and tells me nothing about why I should look now versus next month.
- Six sandals, zero sizing/availability cues — as someone who returns as much as she buys, I want to know these are in stock in my size before I click through.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a number somewhere — even "shop styles from $395" or "new markdown: up to 30% off select sandals" would give this send a reason to exist beyond "shoes are pretty." This is the single biggest lift available.
- 2. Add InCircle point-earning language near the CTA — "Earn 2X points on sandals through Sunday" would instantly make this feel like it's for me specifically, not a general subscriber blast.
- 3. Swap the vague "THE ROUNDUP" eyebrow for something with a time or occasion hook — "Poolside-ready by Friday" gives a reason to act this week.
- 4. Add a one-line availability nudge under the grid ("Sizes going fast in Gucci, Toteme") — small, but it's the kind of honest urgency that doesn't feel like bait.
