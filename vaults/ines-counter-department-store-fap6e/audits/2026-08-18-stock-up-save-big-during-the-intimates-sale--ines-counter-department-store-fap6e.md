# Stock up & save BIG during the Intimates Sale 👉
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-18**
## Executive summary

- This is the intimates Semi-Annual sale again — same one Kohl's has been running me through all week — but the layout at least earns its keep this time: it's actual product shots, actual prices, four bras stacked with a specific dollar figure under each ($19.99, $26.99, $19.99, $14.99), not another red starburst with a coupon code hiding the real offer. That's a step up from Monday's "20% off Kohl's Cash" send and from the "ENDS TONIGHT... revealed 👀" one that made me hunt for the actual deal.
- But it's also the fourth or fifth intimates-flavored email this week, and past the bra wall it turns into a Kohl's catalog dump — kids' clothes, a slushie maker, bath towels, a mattress topper. I don't shop Kohl's for a Ninja frozen drink machine, and stapling it onto a bra sale tells me this is a "clear the whole store" email wearing an intimates headline.
- Would I click? Maybe, on Maidenform at $19.99 since I actually wear that brand. Would I forward it? No — this is routine mid-tier department-store noise, not something I'd send a friend. Skimmed and archived.

## What's working

- Four bras, four clear prices, four "Shop Now" buttons — no digging for the deal like the "revealed 👀" email made me do.
- "Semi-Annual" framing gives this some credibility as an actual seasonal event rather than another manufactured urgency stunt.
- Rewards status bar up top ("KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER... Earn 5% Rewards") at least acknowledges I'm a known customer, which Kohl's does consistently.
- Brand names I recognize — Bali, Maidenform, Hanes — read like real inventory, not vague "select styles" filler.
- Model diversity across the four hero shots feels more true-to-size than the polished single-model shot Neiman's used in their pre-fall arrivals email.

## What's weak

- The scroll after the bras is exhausting — Women/Men/Kids/Home/Beauty/Shoes tiles, then a slushie maker, bath towels, jeans, and a mattress topper, none of which belong in an "intimates sale" email.
- No end date visible until deep in "Select styles on sale now through August 23" — that's easy to miss, and there's zero countdown urgency compared to yesterday's "ends tonight" framing.
- This is essentially the same red/maroon-on-cream palette and Kohl's Cash cadence I called out in the 2026-08-15 and 2026-08-16 sends — I'm fatigued on this brand's visual language this week.
- No sizing or fit detail (cup range, band sizes) visible on any bra tile — for intimates specifically, that's the one thing I actually need before I click through.
- Compare to Sephora's App Fest email: that one led with one clear offer stack (10% off + free gift) and stayed tight. This one sprawls into six unrelated categories by the midpoint.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email at the bra section. End with a single "Shop All Intimates" CTA instead of pivoting into home goods and kids' apparel — a focused email would score better on relevance for someone like me who came for the bra sale.
- 2. Add a visible countdown or hard end-date banner near the top hero, not buried under "Select styles on sale now through August 23."
- 3. Put a size/fit note under at least the Bali and Maidenform tiles (e.g., "Cups A-DDD") since that's the actual purchase-decision blocker for intimates shoppers.
- 4. Vary the creative — three near-identical maroon/cream sends in four days is starting to read as spam-adjacent even though technically compliant.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Standard multi-vendor marketing email (Coherent Path/click.chp + Kohl's mi.kohls.com/AdobeAAM/s.kohls.com tracking stack) with functional tracking pixels but several unconfirmed compliance headers and widespread missing alt attributes.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 57 tracking/click-redirect links present but skipped by automated HTTP probe (not validated for 200 status/destination) — no confirmation these resolve correctly.
- Multiple tracking pixels fire on load: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...`, `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...`, `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx...` (open tracking), `kohls.demdex.net/event...` (Adobe Audience Manager), plus several `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` pixels — consistent with normal ESP/analytics stacking, no anomalies found.
- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` present — expected for email HTML, not a defect.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 9 images/pixels missing `alt` attributes, flagged by QA: the `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` beacon, `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`, `kohls.demdex.net/event` pixel, and six `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` pixels. Most are 1x1 tracking pixels where alt text is not functionally significant, but their absence is what the automated check flags.
- Malformed markup: `<img ... aria-hidden="true" /></custom>` — the `s.kohls.com` open-tracking pixel `<div>` contains a stray closing `</custom>` tag with no matching opening tag (visible in source right after `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx`). This is invalid HTML and should be removed.
- MSO/Outlook conditional comments and responsive `@media` block for ≤500px present — standard for cross-client rendering, no issues found.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tokens or personalization placeholders (e.g. `{{first_name}}`) observed in the truncated source. No issues found.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- No `List-Unsubscribe` header detected — one-click unsubscribe via mailbox provider UI may not function; may be a relay-capture artifact (AgentMail) rather than absence in the original send.
- No `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058) detected — one-click POST unsubscribe not confirmed supported.
- No physical mailing address detected in the truncated HTML — CAN-SPAM requires one; not confirmed absent from the full email (footer not included in truncated source), but flagged since QA scanned the full doc.
- No `Authentication-Results` header found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be confirmed from available data.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Campaign tracking uses Coherent Path params (`mi_ecmp=1020057_2026818`, `email_name=260818_DG_Email_Tuesday`, `cp_cid=8ba359f132e3c109`) rather than standard `utm_*` query params — no UTM parameters observed in the visible source, so GA/analytics continuity to the landing page can't be verified from this evidence.
- Destination URLs for the 57 click-redirect links were not probed (skipped as tracking domains), so landing-page alignment cannot be confirmed either way.

7. Recommendations
- Remove the stray `</custom>` closing tag near the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` pixel to fix invalid markup.
- Add `alt=""` (empty, decorative) to the 9 flagged tracking pixels/beacons to satisfy accessibility linting even though they're non-visual.
- Confirm with ESP/deliverability team whether `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and `Authentication-Results` are present at the raw SMTP level — current findings may reflect the AgentMail relay not preserving these headers rather than a true sender-side gap.
- Confirm the physical mailing address is present in the full (non-truncated) footer; verify against CAN-SPAM requirements.
- If cross-platform analytics attribution is needed, consider appending standard `utm_*` parameters alongside the existing Coherent Path params on click-through links.
