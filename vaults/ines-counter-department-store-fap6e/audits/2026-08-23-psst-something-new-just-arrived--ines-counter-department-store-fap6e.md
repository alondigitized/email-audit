## Executive summary

- Okay, this is a lot of scrolling for "new arrivals," and my first reaction is that Sephora buried the one thing I'd actually stop for. There's a before/after skin photo up top that's trying to sell me on Tower 28 foundation, and fine, that catches the eye — but then it's just... product, product, product, straight down the page. Drunk Elephant, Chris McMillan, One/Size, Aestura, Dr. Groot, Dolce & Gabbana fragrance — nine different brand blocks stacked like a catalog, no story connecting them.
- This is the fifth Sephora email I've opened in eight days (App Fest 8/17, "0 points" bar three times running 8/19-8/21, Olive Young 8/20), and my Beauty Insider tier still isn't visible anywhere until I'm nearly at the bottom, where there's finally an "Earn points faster" nudge with a 4X/5X card push. - I'd scroll past this, not click. It's a "mark as read while doing something else" email, not a forward-to-a-friend one — nothing here is exciting enough to text a girlfriend about, unlike a real gift-with-purchase event would be.

## What's working

- The before/after skin photo for Tower 28 foundation is a real trust cue — I can see the product doing something, not just sitting on a shelf.
- "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA" repeated on every block does signal exclusivity, which matters to me since I cross-shop Nordstrom and Saks too.
- The Clean at Sephora seal on Tower 28 and Drunk Elephant is a real trust signal for someone who reads ingredient callouts.
- The rewards teaser near the bottom (4X/5X points, free gift with purchase) is the one section that actually speaks my loyalty-driven language.

## What's weak

- Nine brand blocks in a row with zero hierarchy — nothing tells me which one is the actual "hero" of this send, unlike App Fest's email which at least led with one bold banner.
- My points balance and tier status are nowhere near the top — buried below eight product blocks, same problem as the 8/19 and 8/16 sends where "0 points" sat right up front and just felt like nagging instead.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere except a stray "TODAY ONLY" on the AI Fest banner up top that has nothing to do with the rest of the email.
- This is pure product-drop filler compared to Neiman Marcus's gift-card-plus-Triple-Points send, which at least gave me one clear number to chase.
- The imagery skews very young and Instagram-clean — nothing here reads like it was styled with a 47-year-old in mind.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick ONE hero product (the Tower 28 foundation is the strongest visual) and build the whole top of the email around it — before/after, price, one clear CTA — then let the rest of the arrivals live below as a simple grid, not nine full-width blocks.
- 2. Move my Beauty Insider points/tier status to the top, right under the header, the way Neiman Marcus leads with the gift-card number.
- 3. Add real urgency — a "this week only" window on the new arrivals, not just the unrelated AI Fest "today only" banner.
- 4. Cut the block count in half. Nine sequential brand launches reads like a warehouse dump, not a curated drop.
