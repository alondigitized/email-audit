# JUST IN: Extra 20% off for our friends & family 🥰
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-30**
## Executive summary

- Oh good, Kohl's is back — this time I'm apparently "friends & family," which is a nice way of saying "everyone on the list." That blue "FRIENDS & FAMILY" banner with the big navy 20%-off circle is at least a different color from the maroon "ENDS TODAY" banner I've seen four times this week, so points for a fresh coat of paint. But scroll past it and it's the exact same Kohl's playbook — a wall of "Up to X% off + save with coupon" tiles for home, bedding, kitchen, then an activewear blowout, then the barcode at the bottom. It's long. It's a lot.
- What actually caught my eye was the home and kitchen section in the middle — the lemon-print dishware and the blue-and-white bedding look genuinely nice, seasonal, not clip-art. That's more my speed than the Adidas activewear banner near the bottom, which reads like it wandered in from a completely different email aimed at someone twenty years younger than me.
- Would I click? Maybe on the kitchen tile, since I do like a good dish set. Would I forward it to a friend? No — this isn't event-worthy, it's just Tuesday at Kohl's. Compare that to the Sephora "Up to 50% off Select Lip Faves" email that scored a 9 with me — real products, real strikethrough pricing, done in one clean scroll. This one makes me work for it.

## What's working

- The blue "Friends & Family" banner is a nice break from the same tired maroon "ENDS TODAY" creative Kohl's has run four times in the last week.
- The Kohl's Cash green bar and Rewards status line up top acknowledge I'm a member, which Neiman Marcus and Saks do more gracefully but at least it's there.
- The lemon-print dishware and blue floral bedding photography are genuinely appealing and seasonal — not generic stock.
- Clear category nav buttons (Women, Men, Kids, Home) right under the hero make it easy to jump to what I want.

## What's weak

- This is a marathon of an email — five separate "Up to X% off" sections before you even hit the barcode. Sephora gets its offer across in one clean screen; this one needs a full scroll.
- The Adidas activewear banner with the model in workout gear feels bolted on and demographically off — nothing about "Friends & Family 20% off" prepped me for a streetwear-style activewear blowout.
- "Take an extra 20% off" is now competing with "Up to 50% off," "Up to 25% off," "Up to 30% off," and "Up to 40% off" all in the same email — I genuinely can't tell what my actual discount is on anything.
- The barcode-and-code block at the bottom (MYFAMILY20, PIN 4273) is functional but joyless — Nordstrom or Saks would never make me scan a grocery-store barcode to feel like a "friend."

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one offer and lead with it. Five different percentage-off callouts in one email kills clarity — Sephora's single "Up to 50% off" hero is why that email scored a 9 and this one won't.
- 2. Cut the email in half. Drop either the activewear section or one of the three home/kitchen tiles — this doesn't need to be a full department-store circular crammed into one send.
- 3. Move the barcode/coupon code higher, right under the hero, since that's the actual mechanic of "friends & family" — don't make me scroll through five unrelated categories to find it.
- 4. If Adidas/activewear stays, give it its own send — it doesn't belong wedged between bedding and a barcode.

# Technical Email Audit: Kohl's Friends & Family (260730_DG_Email_Thursda)

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard HTML4.01 transitional email built on Cheetah Digital/CoherentPath infrastructure (`chp.kohls.com`, `mi.kohls.com`) with Outlook MSO conditionals and responsive breakpoints; automated QA reports a 43% pass rate driven primarily by missing alt text, missing compliance headers, and an empty `<title>`.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Open-tracking beacons present via three separate mechanisms: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/c9e45127-...` (1x1 img), `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif` (1x1 img), and `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?LVRBTPAZEU4UBO3AJZDWDREDRY.60267&d=60267&bmt=0` — redundant triple open-tracking is a possible artifact of migrating between ESPs (CoherentPath + legacy Kohl's platform).
- Malformed markup: the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` tracking `<img>` is immediately followed by a stray `</custom>` closing tag with no matching opening tag — invalid HTML that some strict renderers may mis-parse.
- Adobe Audience Manager pixel present: `https://kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`.
- QA flagged 57 tracking/click-redirect links skipped from HTTP probing (expected behavior for redirect-wrapped links; destination integrity not verifiable from this data).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- `<title></title>` is empty — no preview/tab text for webmail clients that surface it.
- Viewport meta sets `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0`, disabling pinch-to-zoom — WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) violation and an accessibility regression for low-vision recipients.
- 8 images flagged by QA with missing `alt` attributes — all are tracking pixels/beacons (`c9e45127...`, `o.gif`, `event`, and five `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` pixels). Functionally low-impact since they're invisible 1x1 pixels, but missing `alt=""` (rather than omitting the attribute) is the correct fix so screen readers don't announce raw URLs in clients that don't suppress it.
- Google Fonts (Roboto, Montserrat) loaded via both `<link>` and `@import`, correctly gated behind `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` with a system-font fallback stack (`Montserrat, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`) on `<body>` — no issue.
- `lang="en"` is correctly set on `<html>`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source — no unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) are present in the truncated HTML. Note: the body content is truncated in the source provided, so full-body personalization could not be fully audited.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not found (QA WARN) — may reflect the AgentMail relay not preserving it rather than the sender omitting it; cannot confirm from HTML body alone since one-click unsubscribe is a header-level mechanism.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found (QA WARN) — RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe unconfirmed.
- No physical mailing address detected (QA WARN) — CAN-SPAM requires this; address is typically in the footer, which falls outside the truncated HTML provided, so this should be re-verified against the full source before treating as confirmed.
- `Authentication-Results` header not found (QA WARN) — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown from available data.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Links do not use standard UTM parameters (`utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign`); instead they rely on proprietary tracking params — `mi_mid`, `mi_cid`, `mi_u`, `mi_ecmp=1018713_2026730`, `cp_tp`, `cp_cid` (CoherentPath) and `email_name=260730_DG_Email_Thursda` (truncated value, likely `260730_DG_Email_Thursday`). If the destination site's analytics expects standard UTM query params, campaign attribution will not stitch together without a redirect-layer mapping.
- Landing page alignment cannot be verified: all 57 outbound click links route through `click.chp.kohls.com` redirect wrappers, which QA explicitly skipped during HTTP probing, so final destination URLs are unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Populate `<title>` with preview text for webmail/tab display.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta to restore pinch-zoom accessibility.
3. Add `alt=""` to all tracking-pixel `<img>` tags to mark them decorative for assistive tech.
4. Remove the orphaned `</custom>` closing tag near the `click.s.kohls.com` beacon.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM) are present at the raw SMTP level — current absence may be an AgentMail relay artifact rather than a sender-side gap.
6. Verify the physical mailing address is present in the untruncated footer to confirm CAN-SPAM compliance.
7. If cross-domain analytics continuity matters, confirm the `click.chp.kohls.com` redirect chain appends standard UTM params (or an equivalent mapping) before landing on `kohls.com`.
