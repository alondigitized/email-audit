# Your exclusive offer ends today: 25% off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-03**
## Executive summary

- This is the same black box and the same 25% I got on Sunday, and I've now seen it three days running. The design is still the quiet Neiman's I like. Black hero, one number, one white button, and the code sitting right below it. Nobody is shouting at me.
- The subject promised "ends today" and the body doesn't deliver on it. The only deadline is in the fine print at the very bottom: 11/3 at 10:00 PM CT. Up top it just says "for a limited time." The email I'd actually act on would put the deadline where I can see it. And the exclusions list is long enough to read like a fashion index. Beauty and fragrance are out, and so are Chanel, Dior, Hermès, Gucci, Louis Vuitton and Bottega. That's most of what makes me shop at Neiman's.
- Would I click? Maybe, if I already had a Chloé bag or a Toteme dress in mind. Would I forward it? No. Mark as read and move on.

## What's working

- The black hero with "25% OFF" is clean, and the eye lands on it right away. Sephora's "Insider, everything is on sale!" banner with two stacked badges is much busier.
- The code is in a separate, easy-to-copy band right under the button, with no hunting for it.
- The 3x3 grid is designer names only, with no prices and no starbursts. Kohl's "Top 100 Gifts" mall directory is the opposite.
- The model-free product shots on a pale grey ground feel like a catalog page. Chloé, Toteme and Zegna are the right level for me.
- The six category buttons at the bottom are easy to tap with a thumb.

## What's weak

- The "ends today" urgency is missing from the body. The 10:00 PM CT deadline is only in the small print.
- The exclusions are brutal. Beauty and fragrance are out, along with half the designers I'd want. The hero says "one regular-price item," and the legal text then takes most of that back.
- Nothing here says I'm a known customer. There's no tier, no points and no event, and nothing from my own history. Macy's at least asks me to confirm for an extra 25%. This is a one-size code.
- Several of the products in the grid, like Chloé, Miu Miu and Ferragamo, may be on the exclusion list or close to it. That's a bait-and-switch feeling I really don't like.
- The footer text is tiny, and the fine print is a wall I'd need to zoom into on my phone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero: "Ends tonight at 10 PM CT." The subject already says it, so the body should too.
- 2. Show only products that are eligible for the code, and flag them as such, so the grid matches the offer.
- 3. Cut the exclusion wall into a short "what's excluded" line on top, with a link to the full list.
- 4. Add one line that acknowledges me, like my loyalty status or a category I shop, so it doesn't read like a stranger's coupon.
- 5. Stop sending the same 25% creative three days in a row. Vary the hero or hold the send.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Chloé, Toteme, Zegna and more. Eligible styles only.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a standard table-based XHTML Transitional email with responsive media queries. The notable confirmed issue is that internal Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) template documentation was shipped in the delivered source. The QA warnings (missing `List-Unsubscribe` and `Authentication-Results` headers) are relay-capture limitations, not confirmed defects. The HTML is truncated, so the body links and footer weren't available to check.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 30 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe because they use click-redirect domains. Link validity, redirect chains and destinations are **unverified**.
- No broken links were confirmed. The truncated source exposes no `href` values to inspect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Comment before the DOCTYPE:** the SFMC comment block sits before `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" ...>`. In some older clients (IE-based renderers) anything before the doctype can trigger quirks mode. The risk is low in modern clients.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom in clients that honor it, which is an accessibility concern.
- **Fragile selectors:** the media queries use exact-match attribute selectors such as `*[class=contenttable]`, `*[class=mobile]` and `*[class=hide]`. They stop matching if an element has more than one class, so a mobile override can silently fail.
- **Checkbox hamburger nav:** the CSS (`.navbar-checkbox:checked~.menu`, `.mobileCont:checked`) depends on `:checked` support. Gmail and most Outlook versions don't support it, and the intended fallback is not visible in the truncated source.
- **Stale hacks:** `.ReadMsgBody`, `.ExternalClass` and `-webkit-animation: bugfix` are legacy workarounds. They're harmless but add weight.
- **Alt text and preheader:** the truncated source doesn't show them, so they can't be assessed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The comment block at the top of the delivered HTML exposes internal SFMC and AMPscript details: `NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`, and the field names `EMAIL_ID`, `uEm` and `cmdid`. It also exposes the template instruction text "CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW" and `SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256`. These are template-authoring notes that should have been stripped before send.
- No unrendered merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) are visible in the available portion of the source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. QA itself says the AgentMail relay may not capture them, so this is **unconfirmed**. If they are truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements for one-click unsubscribe would not be met.
- `Authentication-Results` is absent from the captured data, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is **unknown**. The sender domain `e.neimanmarcus.com` can be checked directly in DNS.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and physical address are in the truncated footer, so they can't be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No `href` values are visible in the truncated source, and the tracking links weren't probed. UTM parameters, `refid` handling and landing page alignment cannot be evaluated.
- The comment references `@refid`/`@base_refid`, which suggests campaign attribution goes through a refid parameter. Whether it is populated in the sent links is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Strip the SFMC authoring comment block** before send. It exposes internal data extension and field names, and removing it also restores a clean DOCTYPE at the top.
2. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag.
3. **Make the mobile selectors robust:** use `[class~=contenttable]` or class selectors instead of `*[class=...]`.
4. **Confirm the nav fallback:** check that the checkbox hamburger degrades to visible links in Gmail and Outlook.
5. **Verify the compliance headers on the raw message** rather than the relay copy: `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `e.neimanmarcus.com`.
6. **Re-run the audit on the full HTML** so the following can be checked:
   - redirect chains for the 30 tracking links
   - UTM and refid parameters
   - alt text
   - the footer unsubscribe link and physical address
