# Up to 40% off .... back to school means back to saving 💯
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-19**
## Executive summary

- Same kid-at-lockers hero as yesterday's back-to-school send, so I'm scrolling right past the top fold — that's not for me. But then I hit "Beauty Up to 50% off, Sephora at Kohl's Beauty" with a lipstick glide shot, and that's the one line item in this entire email that made me pause. Buried under bed & bath, shoes, tees, tops, and dresses, but it's there.
- The problem is I have to scroll through five other categories to find it, and once I do, there's no product name, no shade, no brand callout beyond "Sephora" — just a generic tube of gloss. Compare that to Neiman's Brunello Cucinelli triple-points email, which named the exact designer and told me exactly what my points were worth. Kohl's just says "beauty" and moves on. I'm not clicking, and I'm not forwarding this — it's a mass storewide blast dressed up as back-to-school, and my one relevant category is an afterthought.

## What's working

- The "Beauty Up to 50% off" tile is genuinely relevant to me — Sephora at Kohl's is a real reason I'd open a Kohl's email at all.
- Kohl's Cash and "Earn $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 spent" is loyalty mechanics I actually understand and can act on.
- Prices are concrete and specific throughout (Bed & Bath $3.99, Tops $12.99) — no vague "shop now and see."
- Clean category nav bar up top (Women/Men/Girls/Shoes/Home) makes it easy to skip straight past what I don't need.

## What's weak

- The hero is 100% back-to-school kids' imagery — I don't have kids in school, so the first screen is dead space for me, same complaint I had about yesterday's 8/19 back-to-school send.
- Beauty is the 6th category down, after bed & bath, shoes, tees, and tops — my one relevant offer is buried below stuff I'll never buy.
- "Coupons and Kohl's Cash do not apply" sits right under the beauty tile — so my one interesting offer comes with a fine-print asterisk, which feels like the Sephora tone-deaf move where the loyalty mechanic gets waved in front of you then yanked away.
- No product name, shade, or brand shown for the beauty item — just a stock gloss tube. Neiman's told me exactly what Brunello Cucinelli triple points meant in dollars; this tells me nothing.
- This is a wall of near-identical pink-banner-plus-product-photo tiles — Sephora's App Fest at least varies its layout; this is starting to feel like scrolling a circular.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move Beauty/Sephora up near the top for beauty-engaged customers — segment this by purchase history so people like me see it first, not sixth.
- 2. Name an actual product or brand in the beauty tile (a specific Sephora Collection or NYX item), not a generic gloss stock shot.
- 3. Either let Kohl's Cash apply to beauty or don't lead with the loyalty banner right above the exclusion — right now it reads bait-and-switch.
- 4. Swap the back-to-school kid hero for a segmented hero when the recipient has no kids-category purchase history.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 50% off Sephora at Kohl's — plus storewide savings`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your beauty pick: up to 50% off Sephora at Kohl's Beauty`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New Sephora arrivals, up to 50% off — shop before Kohl's Cash ends 8/23.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Beauty, home, and fall favorites — all in one storewide sale.`
