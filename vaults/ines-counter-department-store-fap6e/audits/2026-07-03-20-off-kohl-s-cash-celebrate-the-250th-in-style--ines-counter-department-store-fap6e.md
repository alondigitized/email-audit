# 20% off + Kohl's Cash? Celebrate the 250th in style 💥
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-03**
## Executive summary

- Forty-one. That's roughly my Kohl's count since Memorial Day, and this one arrives wearing red, white, and blue for the 250th. Credit where it's due: the patriotic hook is the first genuinely new creative angle they've given me in a week. After four sends in four days with the same 20% off + Kohl's Cash structure — including two on July 1st alone with nearly identical mechanics — the 250th anniversary framing at least gives me a reason beyond "TODAY ONLY" to care about the timing. The hero reads clearly, the Kohl's Cash callout is right where I'd look for it, and the clearance 70% section is the single most interesting number in the entire email. I got there faster than I expected.
- That said, this is still the same engine in a new paint job. I'm a woman who spends real money at the Lancôme counter and cross-shops Nordstrom's Anniversary Sale — and nothing here signals Kohl's knows that about me. It's a broad family email with patriotic bunting, not a message targeting me specifically. Compared to Neiman Marcus dropping "Up to 60% off. Need we say more?" with editorial confidence, or Sephora calling out my Insider tier, this reads like a Sunday circular that got a holiday costume.
- I'd mark it read. If I'm near a Kohl's this weekend I might pull up that barcode, but I'm not clicking through. No forwarding — there's nothing here a friend of mine would find that she couldn't find in her own inbox.

## What's working

- The 250th anniversary hook ties the offer to a real-world moment — July 4th as a credible deadline is more persuasive than another "HOURS LEFT" countdown
- Kohl's Cash is right in the hero, immediately visible — for a points-or-nothing shopper like me that's a legitimate reason to pay attention
- Fast & Free Store Pickup callout is smart placement for a holiday-weekend email when in-store trips are actually plausible
- The 70% clearance section stopped my scroll — it's the biggest number in the email and it earns its placement
- Barcode/wallet integration at the bottom is a practical touch that actually bridges digital-to-physical

## What's weak

- Fourth Kohl's email in four days with the same 20% off + Kohl's Cash core — the patriotic costume doesn't make the offer feel new
- No beauty or personal-care angle anywhere — Kohl's carries Lancôme and Clinique; not a single product row for that customer
- "Shop Now" is the laziest CTA button they could write — Neiman Marcus brings more editorial nerve with two words than Kohl's does with a full hero
- The stacked 40% + 20% off Kohl's Cash language appears mid-email with no clean explanation — Macy's at least puts the math in plain terms on their confirmation emails
- Mobile product grid is cramped — five category rows stacked tight makes it feel like a printed flyer insert, not a curated email

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add one beauty or fragrance row** — "Beauty brands included" in the hero or a single dedicated section would stop beauty-counter regulars like me from treating this as an apparel-only send
- 2. **Make Kohl's Cash concrete** — "Earn $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 spent" beats the vague earn mechanic that makes me do the math myself
- 3. **Sharpen the CTA** — "Shop the 250th Sale" or "Shop Holiday Clearance" ties the button to the creative hook and the 70% clearance section respectively
- 4. **Loyalty personalization** — Sephora shows me my Beauty Insider tier in every email; Kohl's should surface my current Kohl's Cash balance or the next earn threshold so the mechanic feels personal
- 5. **Trim the category rows** — two or three featured sections maximum; every additional block dilutes the offer hierarchy
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 20% off is waiting — plus Kohl's Cash starts tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The 250th sale: 20% off, Kohl's Cash, and 70% off clearance`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Clearance hits 70% off. Beauty included. Grab your barcode.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Store pickup ready for the weekend — your savings are already loaded.`

### 1. Technical Summary

Functional HTML structure with no broken links detected, but compliance signals are degraded by the AgentMail relay (headers not captured) and the email carries 11 images missing `alt` attributes — the majority of visible content images. Authentication status is unverifiable from captured headers.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 tracking/click-redirect links skipped** by HTTP probe — all routed through `click.chp.kohls.com` and `click.s.kohls.com`. No broken destination URLs confirmed, but link health is unverifiable from this capture.

Three distinct pixel/tracking systems are firing on open:
- `click.chp.kohls.com` (CoherentPath CDP)
- `mi.kohls.com` (Marigold/Sailthru)
- `click.s.kohls.com` (Salesforce Marketing Cloud — `open.aspx`)
- `kohls.demdex.net` (Adobe Audience Manager)

No issues with the links themselves, but the `<custom>` tag after the SFMC open pixel (`</custom>`) is an orphaned/invalid HTML element — likely a template artifact from the ESP that should be `</div>` or removed.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**11 content images missing `alt` text** — including what appear to be product/offer images (e.g., `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`) served via `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/`. Tracking pixels correctly use `alt=""` or `aria-hidden="true"`, but the content images do not. In image-blocking clients (Outlook default, Gmail Promotions tab), recipients see blank space with no fallback text.

Specific offenders from QA:
- `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png` — no `alt`
- `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/f441d45d2128ae53.png` — no `alt`
- `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/c2f81e9ac8daee44.png` — no `alt`
- `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/415c8a18fc2950ea.png` — no `alt`
- (7 additional images)

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — disables pinch-to-zoom on mobile, an accessibility barrier (WCAG 1.4.4).

**`<title>` tag is empty** — screen readers and some clients announce "Untitled" or nothing.

**Google Fonts double-loaded**: Roboto and Montserrat are each requested via both `<link>` and `@import url()` inside the same `<style>` block, doubling the font fetch requests with no benefit.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unrendered merge tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `*|FNAME|*`) visible in the truncated source. Marigold subscriber ID `mi_u=604230016` is populated in pixel URLs, indicating personalization tokens resolved at send time.

Kohl's Cash pixel includes a potentially empty token: `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` (value absent in `916d6fa08d185233.png` src URL) — if this is an expiration date field, it may be a failed merge that should have populated a date string.
