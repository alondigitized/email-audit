# Low prices AND up to 85% off clearance? Win, win 🎉
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-24**
## Executive summary

- This is the tenth Kohl's email I've had in about two weeks, and it's the same maroon "Lowest Prices of the Season" template as 10/19 through 10/23. This one swaps in a "Clearance, up to 85% off" box at the top right, so the subject line has something new to point to. I still scrolled a very long way before I found anything I wanted. The 10/23 "Looking for the lowest prices?" send had the same long stack of tiles.
- I'm a beauty-counter person. I shop Nordstrom, Macy's and Saks for skincare and makeup, and I won't put a card down without points back. There's no beauty in this email at all. It's fleece, flannels, jeans, boots, towels and a Ninja blender. The "Earn Kohl's Cash" strip and the "Plus, earn Kohl's Cash" banner are the only things that look like loyalty. Neither tells me anything about my own status.
- Would I click? Probably not. Would I forward it? Maybe to my sister for the $7.99 long-sleeve tees, but that's a stretch. I'd mark it read. At least the screaming "ENDS TONIGHT" from 10/18 is gone.

## What's working

- The "Up to 85% off" clearance box in the top right is a clear number. It gives the email one new hook compared with the last five.
- The "Select styles on sale now through Oct. 25" line gives a real deadline without shouting.
- Each tile has a starting price, a "Reg." price and a "Shop Now" button. I can see the discount without doing the math.
- "Free returns through January 31, 2027" is a real trust cue. Macy's and Nordstrom don't make that promise this prominently.
- The "Kohl's Cash for every $50 spent" line near the bottom is clear. It's just buried.

## What's weak

- The email is 10+ near-identical maroon tiles, each with a "Lowest Prices of the Season" label. By the fourth one I was numb. Sephora's "Oh. My. Minis." email was a catalog too, but its hero let me find the point fast.
- The "Shop Now" buttons are all the same, in small white text on maroon. They tell me nothing about what's behind them.
- Almost nothing is aimed at a 47-year-old who buys beauty. The models are family and casual-wear types. There's no skincare, fragrance or Sephora-at-Kohl's tile in the main stack.
- The fine print is dense and tiny. It includes "Coupons do not apply to select brands" and "Excludes Sephora at Kohl's purchases." Nothing in the email says the clearance and Kohl's Cash fine print applies to the same items.
- Nothing recognizes me as a member. The header says "Kohl's Rewards Member" in a tiny line, but the body treats me like a stranger. Neiman Marcus at least leads with a designer story when it has no offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the stack to 3–4 tiles and lead with the one offer that's new, which is clearance up to 85% off. Put a single bold hero on it.
- 2. Show my Kohl's Cash balance or expiry date near the top. Reward me for being a member the way Macy's does with its "confirm now" email, even though that one was pushy.
- 3. Give each button its own label, such as "Shop fleece from $9.99" or "Shop boots from $17.99".
- 4. Add one beauty tile. That's where I spend my money, and Sephora at Kohl's is right there.
- 5. Move the fine print into a short readable block and say plainly what's excluded.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Clearance up to 85% off, plus your Kohl's Cash`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Fleece from $9.99 and clearance to 85% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sale prices end Oct. 25. Earn $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Boots from $17.99, jeans from $13.99, free returns through January.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is mostly functional but has several validity and accessibility defects: a stray `</custom>` tag, an invalid `<div>` in `<head>`, an empty `<title>`, a viewport that blocks zoom, and missing `alt` on content images. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the AgentMail relay and the truncated HTML, so they can't be confirmed or refuted from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Not verifiable:** 75 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe (click-redirect domains). The destination, redirect health and UTM handling of `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…` and `click.chp.kohls.com/…` are unknown.
- **Beacon stack:** four tracking or analytics beacons fire before any content. They are `click.chp.kohls.com/o/7052cbee-…`, `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`, `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`, and `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager). Overlapping open-tracking is redundant, and it adds third-party requests.
- **Subscriber identifier in URLs:** `mi_u=604230016` is passed in the query string of the `mi.kohls.com` pixel and image URLs. It is likely a per-recipient key. That is normal for this kind of tracking, but the images are not anonymous.
- **Malformed markup on the SFMC open pixel:** `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?…" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`. The closing `</custom>` has no matching open tag. It's invalid HTML, and some parsers may mishandle it.
- **No `style` on the first pixel:** the first `<img>` (`click.chp.kohls.com/o/…`) has only `width="1" height="1"`. It sits directly in `<body>` with no `display:none` or wrapper, unlike the other pixels.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` disables pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>`.
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. Browsers and clients will implicitly end `<head>` at that point and may move the surrounding content. The schema.org microdata in this position is also not parsed by mail clients.
- **Empty icon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty `href`. It does nothing, and some parsers resolve it to the current document.
- **Missing `alt` on content images:** QA flagged 17 images, and the report was cut off after 16. These are the real defects:
  - Product images: `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/58966978_{1sa,2sa,11sa,14sa}_2x`.
  - Banner or text images: `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/{2a5adc5afb647aad, f441d45d2128ae53, c2f81e9ac8daee44, 415c8a18fc2950ea, 69494b48a79b4240, 78308d1d9adb0751, 916d6fa08d185233}.png`. The `mi_u` and `email_name` parameters suggest dynamically rendered images. If they carry offer text, that text is lost with images off or in a screen reader.
  - Beacons: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/7052cbee-…` and `kohls.demdex.net/event` also lack `alt`. These are tracking pixels, so use `alt=""`.
- **Likely QA false positive:** `mi.kohls.com/p/up/…/o.gif` is flagged, but the source has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`. This one is not a defect.
- **Layout tables:** the 1×1 pixel table and the `<!-- AAM -->` table have no `role="presentation"`. The main layout tables do have it.
- **Doctype mismatch:** the HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is used with XHTML-style self-closing syntax. This is low risk.
- **Web fonts:** Roboto and Montserrat load via `<link>` and `@import` inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. The `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` fallbacks are present, so there is no confirmed problem.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[%…%]`) appear in the visible source. The footer and the rest of the body were truncated, so token coverage there is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
All four flags below are unconfirmed.
- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post:** both are reported missing, but the QA note says the headers may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. Check the raw `.eml` before treating this as a sender defect.
- **Physical address:** the QA check found none, but the HTML provided is truncated before the footer. This is unconfirmed.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, which is expected through the relay. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `s.kohls.com`.
- **Unsubscribe link in body:** not visible in the truncated source, so it is unverified.
- **Other signals:** the Sender and Reply-To headers are not provided. `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` are fine.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. Click URLs are opaque (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…`), the probe skipped all 75, and the truncated source shows no landing-page URLs or `utm_*` parameters. The only campaign identifiers visible are in image URLs: `mi_ecmp=1024445_20261024` and `email_name=261024_DG_Email_Saturd…`. Resolving the redirects would be needed to check UTM presence and landing alignment.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the stray `</custom>` and move the `itemscope` div out of `<head>`. Either put it in `<body>` or drop it.
2. Add `alt` text to the product images (`58966978_*`) and to any banner PNGs that carry offer copy. Use `alt=""` on the pure tracking pixels. Fill in `<title>`.
3. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Delete the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon" href="">`.
5. Audit whether four open and analytics beacons are all needed.
6. Re-run the compliance checks against the raw MIME message (headers plus the full, untruncated HTML) to confirm the List-Unsubscribe headers, the footer address and unsubscribe link, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
7. Follow the redirect chains for the 75 skipped links to check final URLs, UTM or campaign parameters, and landing-page alignment.
8. Fix the QA tooling. The `alt=""` pixels are being flagged, which inflates the warning count, and the alt-text list is truncated.
