# Starts today! You have 10% off 🎉
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-26**
## Executive summary

- "You're new here." Three words buried in the "Wondering Why You Got This?" block, and they stopped me cold. I have been a Beauty Insider long enough to have lived through the VIB Rouge era. I have a Sephora credit card with points on it. I am not new here. And somehow Sephora's system looked at my account and decided I was the right target for a first-purchase acquisition offer tied to Sephora at Kohl's — a channel I don't primarily shop — sent under full Sephora branding with zero indication that the offer is scoped to Kohl's until you hit the fine print.
- And then: Rosie. Again. The May 22 send dropped the name entirely and I gave them real credit for that — it was the cleanest thing they'd sent in weeks. But here we are on May 26 with "Rosie" back in the greeting. Once is a bug. Twice in the same month, with a clean send sandwiched in between, means nobody fixed anything. They just got lucky on one campaign.
- I would not click this. I can't redeem a first-purchase offer — I've already purchased. I would not forward it. My friend who has never been to a Sephora inside a Kohl's would find it equally confusing. I'd mark it read and go back to looking at what Neiman Marcus just dropped in the designer sale.

## What's working

- **10% OFF in massive type** lands immediately — the offer is legible without any effort, which is table stakes Sephora actually cleared here
- **The barcode at the bottom** is one thing I genuinely appreciate; walking into a Kohl's with a scannable code is friction-free, and for in-store shoppers like me it signals the brand thought through the redemption path
- **"STARTS TODAY" + hero alignment** — subject line and hero headline say the same thing without contradicting each other, which the May 26 last-chance send managed to bungle with its fourth-consecutive-same-offer fatigue
- **Drunk Elephant and St. Honoré Beauty in the recommendation rail** — actual Sephora brand names, not a generic homepage dump; someone at least ran a product algorithm here

## What's weak

- **"Rosie" in the greeting** — this is the May 20 failure on repeat; the May 22 clean send was evidence they could suppress the broken token, and they chose not to for this one
- **"Your First Purchase" / "you're new here" for a multi-year Beauty Insider** — this is not a copy problem, it's a segmentation catastrophe; sending an acquisition offer to a loyalty member isn't just irrelevant, it's insulting
- **Kohl's-scoped offer inside full Sephora branding with no upfront disclosure** — I had to reach the exclusion footnote to understand this is for Sephora at Kohl's specifically; Neiman Marcus would brand a sub-channel cleanly rather than hide the scope in fine print
- **"Wondering Why You Got This?" is a defensive dead end** — if you need a block explaining why you sent an email, the segmentation was already wrong; delete the block, fix the segment

## Recommendations

- 1. **Suppress first-purchase offers from Beauty Insider accounts at any tier** — no copy fix rescues the wrong offer for the wrong person; this is a CRM/suppression list problem and it needs to be solved before the next Kohl's send
- 2. **Either fix the merge token or suppress the name field** — the May 22 clean no-name send proves the suppression path exists; use it until the token is audited
- 3. **Brand Kohl's-channel emails as "Sephora at Kohl's" from the subject line forward** — a loyal Sephora proper customer should understand within three seconds that this offer lives in a different door
- 4. **Remove the "Wondering Why You Got This?" explanation block** — it signals Sephora knows the targeting is off; cut it entirely and let the offer stand
- **Subject Alt A:** `New to Sephora at Kohl's? Your 10% off starts today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `10% off at Sephora inside Kohl's — valid through June 1`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code SE3ARPH in store or at Kohls.com/Sephora — offer starts now`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your first Sephora at Kohl's purchase just got cheaper — today only`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[HIGH] `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-to-zoom on mobile**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
WCAG 1.4.4 failure. Prevents users from scaling text. Should be removed or set to `maximum-scale=5`.

**[HIGH] `text-size-adjust: none` applied to body-level selectors**
```css
div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td {
  -webkit-text-size-adjust: none;
  -ms-text-size-adjust: none;
  -moz-text-size-adjust: none;
  text-size-adjust: none;
}
```
Overrides OS-level font scaling. This broad selector list includes `body` and `div`, which suppresses accessibility zoom across all content. Standard practice is to limit this to specific layout containers, not body-level elements.
