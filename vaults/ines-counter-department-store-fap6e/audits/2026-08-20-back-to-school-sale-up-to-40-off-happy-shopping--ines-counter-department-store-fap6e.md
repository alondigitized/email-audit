# Back-to-School Sale 🎒 Up to 40% off 😍 Happy shopping 🛍️
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-20**
## Executive summary

- Third day in a row Kohl's has put a kid at lockers front and center, and for the third day in a row it's just not for me. I don't have kids in school anymore, so "Back to School" is dead air the second it renders — same beat as yesterday's "✏️ Save up to 40%" send and the day before's. What actually stops my scroll is further down: the Bras section — $19.99, "Reg. $42-54," three styles laid out clean with a real price comparison. That's the kind of honest markdown I respond to. The Jeans at up to 30% off with the Kohl's Cash banner stacked right above it is fine too.
- But this email is exhausting. It's a wall of category tiles — Tops, Jeans, Bras, Backpacks, Bedding, Bath, Hanes Basics, a sweepstakes, Clearance, then a random "Picked with you in mind" grid with a bath towel, a slushie maker, jeans, and a mattress topper thrown together with no logic. This is the Macy's-style overload problem, except Macy's at least dresses it up with urgency copy. Kohl's just keeps scrolling and scrolling. I'm not forwarding this to anyone — I'm skimming to the bras section, maybe clicking, and archiving.

## What's working

- The Bras block ($19.99 vs. $42-54 "Reg.") is the one spot that reads like a real deal, not manufactured urgency — that's what pulled my eye.
- "KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER / Earn 5% REWARDS" sits right at the top — at least they acknowledge I'm a member, unlike Sephora's app fest emails that just keep parking my points balance at zero and shouting about it anyway.
- The Kohl's Cash banner ("Earn $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 spent") is a real, concrete loyalty mechanic — I refuse to shop without points back, so this is the one thing in here actually built for someone like me.
- Category nav buttons (Women, Men, Girls, Boys, Shoes, Home) up top are at least a fast way to skip past the kid hero if you know to look for them.

## What's weak

- The hero is a third consecutive back-to-school kid-at-lockers shot — I flagged this exact fatigue on the 8/18 and 8/19 sends, and Kohl's just ran it again.
- The email is a scroll-forever category dump — Tops, Jeans, Bras, Backpacks, Bedding, Bath, Hanes, sweepstakes, Clearance, "Picked for you" — no editorial hand guiding me to anything relevant to a 47-year-old with no school-age kids.
- "Picked with you in mind" personalization block is a towel, a slushie machine, jeans, and a mattress topper — nothing here reads as actually picked for me, it reads like a random product-feed fallback.
- Backpacks and school Bedding/Basics sections are pure noise for my life stage — Neiman Marcus at least keeps its emails to one clean idea even when it misses the offer; this is the opposite problem, too many ideas and none of them land.
- Fine print buried at the very bottom in tiny gray text about "Coupons do not apply to select brands" undercuts the bold 30%/60%/70% numbers up top — feels like the honest-offer trust gap I called out in the Macy's "LAST CHANCE" email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the Bras or Jeans block, not the kid-at-lockers hero — if I'm getting three back-to-school emails in three days, at least rotate which category anchors the top so it's not the same dead-on-arrival image every time.
- 2. Cut this email in half. Pick 3-4 categories max and let "Shop All Back to School" catch the rest — right now it's Tops, Jeans, Bras, Backpacks, Bedding, Bath, Hanes, sweepstakes, and Clearance in one scroll, which is Macy's-level overload without Macy's urgency copy to justify it.
- 3. Fix "Picked with you in mind" — a towel, a slushie maker, jeans, and a mattress topper isn't personalization, it's a shrug. Either make it actually behavioral or cut the section.
- 4. Surface the Kohl's Cash $10-per-$50 mechanic higher and bigger — that's the actual loyalty hook for someone like me, and it's buried as a thin green bar under the hero.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Kohl's Cash is stacking — shop the sale before Aug 23`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus Kohl's Cash on everything through Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Real markdowns on bras, jeans, and home — no kids required`
