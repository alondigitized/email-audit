# 25% off select styles + 20% off jewelry starts now

## Executive summary

- Okay, Friends & Family is back, and this is the one I actually wait for — this isn't the "unique code just for you" theater or the mauve wall-of-fine-print I got from this same sender two days ago on that 25%-off-one-item send. This is the real event: green hero, "25% OFF select styles + 20% off jewelry," InCircle Double Points called out right under the fold. That's the Neiman's I subscribe for.
- But then I scroll and it's the assortment wall again — nine products, no prices on a single one of them, straight into Theia, FRAME, Cinq à Sept, Dolce&Gabbana, Roger Vivier. I like that they're not overselling me on plunging necklines or twentysomething energy — the product mix (structured bags, tailored blazer, over-the-knee boot) reads like it knows I'm not 25. But zero price transparency on an event email is exactly where Neiman's loses me versus Nordstrom, whose Anniversary Sale emails at least show a strike-through price on the hero product so I know what "25% off" actually means in dollars.
- I'd click "Shop the Event" out of habit — I'm InCircle, I want my double points — but I wouldn't forward this to a friend. It's a solid seasonal push, not a standout.

## What's working

- The green "Friends & Family" hero is unmistakable and on-brand — I know instantly this is the marquee event, not a random markdown.
- InCircle Double Points banner sits right where I'll see it before I even scroll — that's the loyalty signal I'm always looking for and Kohl's buries in three separate colored boxes instead.
- Exclusions are flagged up top ("Exclusions Apply") rather than hidden in a footer wall — more honest than some of Neiman's own recent sends.
- Product curation skews mature and put-together — tailored Brunello Cucinelli jacket, Vince loafers, structured Dolce&Gabbana bag — actual demographic fit for me, not a 20-something's closet.

## What's weak

- Not one price or percentage shown on any of the nine products — I have no idea what 25% off a $3,000 handbag actually saves me until I click through.
- "CLAIM YOUR 15% OFF" text-list banner competing with the 25%/20% event math right above it is confusing — which offer applies to what?
- Six category buttons (Women's, Men's, Shoes, Handbags, Home...) after the grid dilutes the single "Shop the Event" CTA I already had.
- No end-date visible until the fine print at the very bottom (9/27/26) — should be on the hero if they want urgency to register with me.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the sale end date ("Ends 9/27") directly under "SHOP THE EVENT" button, not buried in 8pt legal text — Saks does this better, they'll put a countdown right in the hero.
- 2. Add strike-through or "from $X after discount" under at least the hero product so the 25% means something concrete.
- 3. Cut the six-button category grid down to two (Women's / Shoes) or drop it — one CTA per email is enough when the hero already has one.
- 4. Move or delete the 15% text-signup banner — it's a different, weaker offer competing with my actual event math.
