**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again. The 11/18 "Get 20% off" and "Save 20% with coupon" sends are barely cold, and the 11/15 "HOURS LEFT" email is still in my memory. This one tries to feel like an event with "Early Access" and Black Friday, but it's the same 20% coupon in a longer scroll.
- One caveat on my read: the render I'm looking at is a very long, very narrow strip. I can make out the layout, the product tiles and the big numbers. I can't make out the small print. I'm judging structure and hierarchy, not fine text.
- The top is fine. There's a 20% banner with a coupon stamp, then Home/Women/Men/Kids buttons. After that it's a tall stack of "50%" tiles for floor care, kitchen, heaters, Ring doorbells, a Tonies box and a Shark face mask. It's a hardware aisle, not a beauty counter. ## What's working

- The 20% banner and the department buttons are at the very top, so I know the deal in two seconds.
- The oversized "50%" and "$99.99" numbers pop. Nobody has to read to see the discount.
- Sephora at Kohl's gets its own "Coming soon: Sephora Cyber Week" band. That's the only block aimed at someone like me.
- The Kohl's Cash and coupon-barcode block at the bottom is useful, and it tells me this is a loyalty-driven brand.
- The "Shop simple / delivery made faster" section (store pickup, same-day delivery) is practical. It reads as service, not shouting.

## What's weak

- It's a wall of appliances and electronics. Shark, Ring, heaters and Tonies have nothing to do with how I shop.
- There's no single hero. Every tile is the same weight, so my eye has nowhere to land.
- Sephora is buried near the bottom and only teases "coming soon." Neiman Marcus's Dolce&Gabbana send gave me one gorgeous hero and stopped. This one makes me hunt.
- The subject is long, at about 66 characters. It uses "Black Friday," "up to 50%," "Plus, save 20%" and an emoji together, which is crowded.
- The cadence is exhausting. Macy's at least gave me one clear mechanic with its "confirm now" email. Kohl's gives me a new reason every day.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one hero and one deadline, not twelve equal tiles. Move the Sephora at Kohl's block up for beauty-leaning subscribers. Sephora's own sends pile on, but at least they stay on topic.
- 2. Segment. I've opened beauty and apparel and never a vacuum, so show me those first.
- 3. Put the Kohl's Rewards and Kohl's Cash status at the top. I want the points back before anything else.
- 4. Cut the cadence, or make each send clearly different.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML has several markup defects (a `<div>` inside `<head>`, a stray `</custom>` tag, an empty `<title>`) and many images with missing or non-descriptive alt text. Most compliance and authentication checks couldn't be confirmed, because the headers weren't captured and the HTML is truncated. Of 86 tracking links, none were probed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **86 click-tracking links were not HTTP-probed.** They go through `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=...` redirects, so destination validity is unverified.
- **Four stacked tracking pixels at the top of the body:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/23fabe92-...` (1x1, no `alt`)
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif` (1x1)
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=...` (1x1)
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (0x0, no `alt`)

  Redundant opens add weight and third-party calls. The `demdex` pixel is an Adobe Audience Manager sync call.
- **Malformed markup on the `open.aspx` pixel:** `<img ... alt=""></custom>` has an orphaned closing tag with no matching opener.
- **Subscriber identifiers in asset URLs:** `mi_u=604230016` and `mi_ecmp=1026212_20261119` appear on image URLs (`mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png`). This is a design note, not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **`<div itemscope ...>` inside `<head>`:** this is invalid. Parsers will implicitly close `<head>` and start `<body>` at that point, so the following `</head>` is orphaned.
- **Empty `<title></title>`** and **`<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`** with an empty href.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Typo in the CSS selector:** `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` is missing its leading `.`, so the rule matches nothing.
- **Layout tables without `role="presentation"`:**
  - the 1x1 tracking table
  - the `<!-- AAM -->` table
  - the preheader `<table width='640' ...>`

  The main wrapper tables do have `role="presentation"`.
- **Missing alt text, per QA:**
  - **Likely content images:** `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/{2a5adc5afb647aad, f441d45d2128ae53, c2f81e9ac8daee44, 415c8a18fc2950ea, 69494b48a79b4240, 78308d1d9adb0751, 916d6fa08d185233}.png` and `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/60699446_{3,4}_2x`. Screen readers will announce filenames or nothing. The QA list is truncated, so the total is unknown.
  - **Tracking pixels (low impact):** `o.gif` already has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`. QA flags it as a false positive.
- **Font loading:** Google Fonts is loaded twice, via `<link>` and `@import`. Both are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, so Outlook is not affected.
- **Positives:** `lang="en"` is set, and the mobile breakpoint at 500px collapses `.container` and `.col`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The HTML is truncated, so this covers only the part provided.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header found.** QA notes that AgentMail may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so verify against the raw message.
- **No physical address detected.** It is probably in the footer, which the truncated source doesn't include, so this is unconfirmed.
- **`Authentication-Results` not captured.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `s.kohls.com` are unknown.
- **Plain-text part is more than 70% URLs** (15,282 of 21,056 chars). This is a confirmed deliverability risk, caused by long `?qs=` tokens in the tracked URLs.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters can't be verified.** Links are opaque `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=...` redirects and none were resolved. Campaign IDs appear only on image URLs (`mi_ecmp=1026212_20261119`, `email_name=261119_DG_Email_Thursd...`).
- **Landing-page alignment is unchecked.** I can't tell whether the "up to 50% off" and "save 20%" offers match the destination pages.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the `schema.org` `<div itemscope>` into `<body>` or drop it. Remove `</custom>`, fill in `<title>`, and delete the empty favicon `<link>`.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the content images, and `alt="" aria-hidden="true"` to the decorative or tracking images. The 0x0 and 1x1 pixels should also carry `alt=""`.
3. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Fix `.ReadMsgBody`, and add `role="presentation"` to the remaining layout tables.
5. Consolidate the four tracking pixels to the one or two that are needed.
6. Get the raw `.eml` to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results` and the footer address. Re-run the audit on the untruncated HTML.
7. Shorten the plain-text URLs or use a leaner text-part template. Resolve a sample of the tracked links to check UTM and landing-page continuity.
