# Ready, set, SAVE 🎉 Black Friday is here with 50% OFF ✨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- Seventeenth Sephora email in about two weeks, and my first reaction was "I've already seen this one." That said, it is better built than the 11/26 "Stop scrolling: Today's deal is here 😲". A giant "50% OFF" on a black sparkle background, one SHOP NOW button, and a tidy product shelf underneath. The offer is the headline, and I don't have to hunt for it.
- Then the dagger symbols start. "50% OFF^Δ", "30% OFF†", "Available on in-stock merchandise only," "Excludes fragrance." I can't see what the 50% actually applies to. Is it everything, or a handful of items? Nordstrom would tell me up front. The thin purple strip at the very top, "GET FRAGRANCE, GET 4X POINTS††," is the first thing that spoke to me, because I don't use a card or spend a dollar without points coming back.
- Would I click? Probably, yes, because it's Black Friday and I shop the brand event. Would I forward it? No. It's a sale blast, not something I'd send a friend.

## What's working

- The 50% OFF hero is huge and has one clear SHOP NOW button. The eye lands on the offer first.
- The 4X points banner at the very top speaks to me as a Beauty Insider. Points are what I actually shop for.
- The brand-specific 30% panels (Paula's Choice, Fenty Beauty, Farmacy) give me something concrete to shop. That's better than the generic hero alone.
- The product grid with prices crossed out and sale prices shown (Dr. Jart, Summer Fridays, LANEIGE) lets me see the savings without clicking. Lancôme and Fenty give it the designer-adjacent feel I want.
- The store locator and hours for Sephora at Kohl's, with the barcode at the bottom, make it easy to act in person. I buy and return at the brand event, so that's useful.

## What's weak

- The fine print is buried and the exclusions are everywhere. The stacked †, Δ and †† marks read like fine print designed to hide the catch. Macy's does this too, and it's the exact thing I notice.
- Too many deals compete with each other: 50%, 30%, 30%, 30% again, the Cyber Week deals and the Holiday Gift Guide. It feels like Macy's-style overload by the bottom third.
- It repeats what I got on 11/27 ("Black Friday just got real 🌟 50% OFF starts now 🎊"). Same sparkle, same 50%, a few hours apart. That's cadence fatigue.
- The 4X points offer is tied to a code and to fragrance only, and it's tucked into a thin banner. I can't tell what tier I'm in or what I get for it.
- Much of the copy is small. The fine print and some product text need zooming on my phone.

## Recommendations

- 1. State what the 50% actually covers right under the headline, in plain words, with the dates. Cut the symbol clutter.
- 2. Lead with my points status and tier, and make the 4X offer a real button, not a thin strip.
- 3. Cut it to two or three deal blocks and pull back on sending near-identical emails the same day.
- 4. Make the exclusions readable at normal size.
- **Subject Alt A:** `50% off starts now: what's included, through 11/29`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Black Friday: 50% off + 4X points on fragrance`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `In store and online through 11/29. Here's exactly what's 50% off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Beauty Insider: earn 4X points on fragrance with code CYBERSCENT.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML has a confirmed broken personalization slot (`Hi ,`) and no plain-text part (0 chars). The automated QA run could not verify SPF/DKIM or the unsubscribe headers, so those are unconfirmed. Overall QA pass rate is 38%.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probing coverage:** 12 material links were probed and 37 skipped. The QA data reports no broken probed links, but the skipped 37 are unverified.
- **Redirect wrappers:** Links go through the ESP redirector, e.g. `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a21d4f623fa7d5fef4bbc780a0/...?__dU__=...`. Final destinations can't be seen from the source, so landing-page validity isn't confirmed beyond the probes.
- **Two tracking pixels:**
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...` is an open pixel with `display:none` inline. It has no alt attribute.
  - `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=4970269939971072&mi_ecmp=20261127_Cyber_Week_Black_Friday_Fan_Fa...` is a second beacon with no alt attribute. Its `mi_u` parameter looks like a per-recipient identifier.
- **Alt text on pixels:** Missing alt on these hidden pixels has low accessibility impact. Both are flagged by QA, and adding `alt=""` clears the warning.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing plain-text part (FAIL):** The text version is 0 chars. This hurts deliverability and affects text-only clients and screen-reader fallbacks.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Fixed-width layout:** The wrapper table has `style="min-width:700px; width:700px"`. Unless the truncated remainder overrides it, this overflows below 700px viewports, and the `@media (max-width:480px)` rules only target class names.
- **Malformed conditional comment:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {...}<![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` block in `<style>` without a closing `-->`. Some parsers may drop the rules that follow it, including `.mobileDisc` and `.footerBanner`.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index" />` should be `noindex`. This has no practical effect in email clients.
- **Layout and language:**
  - `<html>` has no `lang` attribute.
  - Layout tables have no `role="presentation"` (e.g. `<table ... class="full-wrap tbl_cntr">`).
- **JSON-LD block:**
  - It has an empty `"discountCode": ""`.
  - It uses `PromotionCard`, which isn't a valid schema.org type.
  - Most clients strip `<script>`, so this is harmless but unclean.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Empty greeting (FAIL):** The QA check found `Hi ,`, meaning the first-name field rendered empty. There is no fallback value. This is confirmed. It may be because the test inbox has no profile name, but the template should still handle empty values (e.g. `Hi there,`).
- No unresolved raw tokens (e.g. `%%first_name%%`) were reported.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** QA flags both as not found, but the notes say the AgentMail relay may strip them. This is **unverified, not confirmed non-compliant**. Check the raw message at the source ESP for RFC 8058 one-click support, which Gmail and Yahoo require for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not captured through the relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown.
- **Unsubscribe link and postal address:** QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed and reports no failure. The footer is outside the truncated source, so I can't confirm the physical address.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No UTM parameters are visible in the truncated source. Links use ESP-wrapped redirects with an opaque `__dU__` token, so campaign attribution depends on server-side parameters.
- The campaign ID in the beacon (`mi_ecmp=20261127_Cyber_Week_Black_Friday_Fan_Fa...`) says "Cyber_Week", while the subject says "Black Friday". Whether this reflects a real mismatch can't be determined from the data.
- Landing-page alignment is not evaluable from the data provided.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a fallback to the greeting merge field, or suppress the greeting when the name is empty.
2. Add a multipart `text/plain` alternative.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Fix the `<!--[if mso]>` block so it sits outside the `@media` rule, or close the comment properly.
5. Make the 700px container fluid, e.g. `width:100%; max-width:700px`, and add `lang` and `role="presentation"`.
6. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
7. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the authentication headers against the raw message, not the relay copy.
8. Change `no index` to `noindex`, or remove the tag. Clean up the JSON-LD or drop it.
9. Raise the probe budget so more of the 37 skipped links are checked, and confirm UTM or tracking parameters on the final landing URLs.
