# 20% off AND Kohl's Cash ... immediately yes 👏
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-01**
## Executive summary

- I want to be upfront: I already reviewed a Kohl's email this morning with the same GOSHOP20 code and the same 20% off mechanic. Different subject line, same offer. That's not a fresh send — that's a refresh button, and I noticed immediately. By email thirty-seven or thirty-eight from this brand in June and July combined, my filter is set to "unless the number starts with $50 or more off, prove it."
- That said, once I'm inside, the email does what it needs to do. The 20% off is above the fold, the Kohl's Cash language is right there below it, and I can read the coupon code without opening my app or squinting at a QR code. As someone who genuinely refuses to leave points on the table, that double-earn mechanic earns my attention. What loses it is everything below the hero: TEK GEAR activewear for what looks like a twelve-year-old girl, a "family in constant go mode" section, and four "Shop Now" buttons before I ever reach something that resembles my department. I'm not the family in the matching athletic sets. I never was.
- I'd click to clearance — only clearance — and I would not forward this. Neiman Marcus sent me something on June 30 that felt like it was styled for an actual adult woman with taste. Kohl's feels like it's broadcasting to a zip code.

## What's working

- The 20% off + Kohl's Cash double-stack is the exact proposition that makes me pause — one offer reduces the price, the other earns it back, and I'm trained to look for both.
- The coupon code (GOSHOP20) is printed in the email body with a scannable barcode, which means zero friction at checkout — Macy's "confirm for extra 25% off" triggered send I reviewed recently required multiple steps to unlock; this just hands me the code.
- The offer is above the fold with no editorial preamble — Sephora's stacking weekend email made me scroll past two headers before I understood what was on offer; Kohl's skips straight to the discount.
- The 2X Rewards banner at the top is quiet acknowledgment that I'm a known member, not a cold stranger — small signal, but I notice it.
- The clearance section (up to 70%) at least gives me a reason to scroll even when the hero leaves me cold.

## What's weak

- This is the second Kohl's email I've opened today with the same offer. Same code. Same structure. Different subject line dressing. That's not a campaign — that's a repeat.
- The hero story is kids' TEK GEAR activewear. I am forty-seven years old. I shop beauty. The primary visual is a young girl in a pink athletic skirt. Nothing in that image speaks to me.
- "Go-to styles for the family in constant 'go mode'" — I'm not in go mode. I'm in Sunday-at-the-counter mode. The copy assumes a lifestyle that isn't mine.
- No beauty whatsoever. Kohl's carries fragrance, skincare, makeup. A version of this email exists somewhere that would actually reach me. It wasn't deployed today.
- Four separate "Shop Now" buttons before I hit clearance creates button fatigue before a single product earns the click.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Segment on purchase history.** If I've ever bought beauty at Kohl's — or anything outside of kids' categories — the hero of this email should not be a twelve-year-old in athletic gear. Run a beauty or women's fashion hero with the same 20% + Kohl's Cash engine for shoppers like me.
- 2. **Quantify the Kohl's Cash earn.** "Plus, earn Kohl's Cash" is the promise. "$10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 spent, July 1–14" is the close. One makes me nod; the other makes me calculate what I need to add to my cart.
- 3. **Cut to two CTAs.** One in the hero, one in clearance. Let the content breathe.
- 4. **Don't send the same offer twice in one morning.** If you must run a second send, differentiate by segment — not by subject line emoji alone.
- 5. **Subject Alt A:** `Your 20% off is live — Kohl's Cash earns now`
- 6. **Subject Alt B:** `Stack it today: 20% off + Kohl's Cash, July 1 only`
- 7. **Preheader Alt A:** `Code GOSHOP20 · Kohl's Cash earn window opens today · Shop what you actually need.`
- 8. **Preheader Alt B:** `Every $50 earns Kohl's Cash — use your 20% off first, then let it stack.`

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "20% off AND Kohl's Cash" (2026-07-01)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Well-structured HTML4 email with standard ESP infrastructure (Coherent Path + MessageGears). Primary concerns are missing compliance signals at the header layer (likely artifacts of AgentMail relay capture) and pervasive missing alt text on tracking and content images.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 click-tracking links skipped** — all route through `click.chp.kohls.com` and `click.s.kohls.com`. Destination URLs could not be probed, so broken-link status is unverified.

Three distinct pixel/tracking systems are firing simultaneously:
- `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` — Coherent Path open/click tracker
- `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...` — MessageGears open pixel
- `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...` — secondary open pixel (ESP identity unclear)
- `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` — Adobe Audience Manager segment sync

**Invalid HTML tag:** `</custom>` closing tag at line ~`<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..."><custom>` — orphaned tag, not a valid HTML element. Low impact in email clients but technically malformed.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**11 images missing `alt` text** — confirmed by QA scan. Affected assets:

| Image | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `ee50ff3d...` (click.chp.kohls.com) | Coherent Path open pixel |
| `o.gif` (mi.kohls.com) | MessageGears open pixel |
| `event` (kohls.demdex.net) | AAM pixel |
| `2a5adc5a...`, `f441d45d...`, `c2f81e9a...`, `415c8a18...` (mi.kohls.com/p/rp/) | Rendered content images |
| `69494b48...`, `78308d1d...`, `916d6fa0...` (mi.kohls.com/p/rp/) | Rendered content images |

Tracking pixels intentionally have no alt text — that's standard. However, the `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/` images appear to be **rendered content images** (product/promo art), not pixels. Missing alt text on those is a real accessibility gap; screen readers will announce the filename hash instead of describing the content.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` prevents pinch-to-zoom. Accessibility issue for low-vision users; also flagged by WCAG 1.4.4.

**Empty `<title>` tag** — `<title></title>` provides no document label for screen readers or preview panes.

**Google Fonts loaded via both `<link>` and `@import`** — Roboto and Montserrat are each requested twice (once via `<link>`, once via `@import url()`). Redundant round-trips, no functional breakage.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens (`{{`, `%%`, `*|`) detected in the visible HTML fragment. The `mi_u=604230016` subscriber ID is present and resolved in all pixel URLs, confirming merge fired correctly for this recipient.

`mi_ecmp=1016329_202671` campaign ID is consistent across all `mi.kohls.com` pixel URLs — no mismatch.

No issues found with personalization token rendering.

### 5. Compliance

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header absent** — not detected by QA relay. If absent at send time (not just a capture artifact), this violates Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender requirements (February 2024 mandate). Evidence: QA flag, header not found.

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent** — RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe not confirmed. Same caveat: may be relay artifact, but must be verified.

**[WARN] No physical mailing address detected** in HTML fragment — CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid physical postal address in the email body. Not visible in the provided HTML. May be present in the truncated portion; must confirm.

**[WARN] `Authentication-Results` header absent** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status unknown. `Kohls@s.kohls.com` sending domain should have DKIM-signed mail; verify DMARC alignment for `s.kohls.com` → `kohls.com`.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All 22 destination URLs are behind click-redirect wrappers and could not be probed. UTM parameter presence on landing pages is **unverified**.

The `mi_ecmp` and `mi_mid` values in pixel URLs follow MessageGears conventions but are not UTM parameters — if Kohl's analytics stack relies on `utm_source/utm_campaign` on landing pages, that tracking must be injected by the click-redirect handler, not the email HTML itself. No evidence either way from the available source.

`email_name=260701_DG_Email_Wed_PM` is present in several `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/` image URLs — consistent naming convention, no mismatch detected.
