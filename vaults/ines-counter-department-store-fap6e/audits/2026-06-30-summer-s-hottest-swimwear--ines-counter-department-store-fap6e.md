# Summer’s hottest swimwear
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-30**
## Executive summary

- Neiman Marcus, bless them, has clearly decided to wash the taste of that gift-card drumbeat out of my mouth — the one that ran for what felt like the entire last week of June. And to be fair, this email looks like a spa day compared to "LAST CHANCE for your $50–$500 gift card." The beach hero is serene, the layout breathes, and "Shore Up Your Beach Style" is exactly the kind of quietly clever pun I don't hate. I get it. They're going editorial.
- But here's where I land: there is not a single offer in this email. No percentage off, no member-exclusive, no InCircle points call-out — nothing. I am a woman who will not swipe a card without points accruing. I shop at Neiman Marcus *specifically* because I have the InCircle card. This email just... forgot I exist as a loyalty member. And then — halfway down — it shows me men's swim trunks. I don't buy men's swim trunks. My husband does, but he's not the one who opened this email.
- I'd mark it as read. I wouldn't click. I definitely wouldn't forward it — if a friend wanted a swimsuit roundup, I'd point her to the June 29th Designer Sale send, where there was actually something to buy *toward*.

## What's working

- **Visual hierarchy is clean** — logo, nav, hero, headline, product grid, utility footer. My eye knows exactly where to go.
- **The pun lands without trying too hard** — "Shore Up Your Beach Style" is the kind of copy Nordstrom would approve and Macy's would never find.
- **No render bugs** — every image loads, text doesn't overlap, the layout is crisp. After the Kohl's 🚨🚨 experience this week, this feels like a breath of salt air.
- **Seasonal timing is right** — July 1, swimwear. No argument there.

## What's weak

- **No offer whatsoever** — After the 60% Designer Sale email on June 29th landed at a 9/10 for me, pivoting to full-price editorial with zero discount or benefit signal is a jarring gear shift.
- **Men's swim trunks section** — I am a 47-year-old woman who buys at the beauty counter. I do not need two-thirds of a scroll to land on men's board shorts. Split the list, please.
- **InCircle nowhere to be found** — I earn on everything at Neiman Marcus. Not even a whisper of "InCircle members earn double points on summer styles" or similar. That single line would have moved me.
- **Hero model is very young** — Classic luxury casting, I know, but the woman standing on that beach is twenty-five at most. The June 29th Prada roundup had the same issue. Saks has been better about this lately — their editorial features more aspirational-age styling.
- **Style Advisor module at the bottom feels tacked on** — It's a nice service, but sandwiched below men's trunks after I've already mentally checked out, it reads as footer filler.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add an InCircle/member hook** — Even "Earn double points on swim through July 4th" in a banner below the hero would have triggered a click from me. This is the single highest-lift change.
- 2. **Cut the men's swim trunks entirely** — Or send me a segmented version. If Neiman Marcus knows I buy women's fashion and beauty, they should know not to serve me board shorts.
- 3. **Give the hero product a real entry point** — The hero lifestyle shot is lovely, but clicking anywhere should land me on a curated swim edit, not just the general swimwear category.
- 4. **Elevate the Style Advisor offer** — Move it higher and make it specific: "Not sure what swimwear suits your shape? Chat with your NM advisor." That's actually a reason to click that Macy's and Nordstrom can't match.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your summer swim edit — with points`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Neiman's swim picks, just in time for the 4th`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `InCircle members earn double through July 4th — shop the edit.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New arrivals in swim — shop women's styles curated by your advisor.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- Multiple commented-out `<meta name="viewport">` tags remain in `<head>` (3 alternates). Dead weight; poses no rendering risk but indicates template hygiene debt.
- `<title>` is `"Neiman Marcus "` (trailing space) — minor but will surface in some preview panes and screen readers.
- No `alt` attributes visible in the truncated source to evaluate; cannot confirm compliance.
- Hamburger nav relies on CSS `:checked` pseudo-selector — known to fail in Gmail (web/Android), Outlook iOS, and Samsung Mail. No fallback visible in the fragment.
- Media query breakpoints split across two `<style>` blocks (`max-width: 600px` and `max-device-width: 480px/520px`) — consistent with NM's historical pattern but adds parse complexity in older Outlook WebView clients.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Critical — unresolved variable comment block in deployed HTML:**

```html
<!--REQUIRED variables: @email ... @user ... @emailid ... @uEm ... @cmdid ...
CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW -->
<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->
```

This comment block — including the `HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256` sentinel and the instruction `CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW` — is visible in the delivered source. AMPscript comments (`%%[/* */]%%`) are stripped server-side; HTML comments (`<!-- -->`) are not. The developer left implementation guidance in HTML comment syntax instead of AMPscript comment syntax. No PII leaks (values are not rendered), but:

- The SHA256 scenario selector is unresolved — it is unclear which `@user` path was actually active for this send.
- `@uEm` and `@cmdid` (CRM identifiers) are referenced but their substitution outcome is unverifiable from the output.

### 7. Recommendations

**P0**
- Confirm `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header is set at the MTA/ESP level for all bulk sends. If NM's SFMC instance does not inject it automatically, add it via Send Classification or a custom header in the account send settings.

**P1**

- Resolve the `CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW` ambiguity — confirm which `@user` population path (hashed email vs. lookup) was active and enforce it statically in the production template.
- Verify SPF/DKIM alignment for `e.neimanmarcus.com` sending domain via a relay that preserves `Authentication-Results`.

- Fix `<title>` trailing space.
