# Pre-order new designer arrivals now!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-25**
## Executive summary

- Well, this is a palate cleanser after three straight days of "Earn up to a $1,500 gift card." No points pitch, no urgency countdown — just "BE FIRST," a moody editorial shot, and a grid of pre-fall pieces. I appreciate that Neiman's occasionally remembers it's a luxury retailer and not a coupon mill, but "pre-order" with zero pricing, zero InCircle mention, and zero personal-style-advisor follow-through from that earlier Brunello Cucinelli email (8/24, 8.00/10) leaves me a little cold on the actual next step.
- The designer names are the hook — Oscar de la Renta, Carolina Herrera, Christian Louboutin, Jimmy Choo — that's a real quiet-luxury lineup and it's dressed nicely. But there's no offer, no member benefit, no reason to act *today* versus next week. Compare that to yesterday's "Ends tonight" gift-card email (7.00/10): that one had urgency but felt repetitive; this one has fresh creative but no urgency or incentive at all.
- I'd scroll through it on my phone with a little "ooh, that Carolina Herrera blouse" reaction, maybe tap the hero image out of curiosity — but I wouldn't forward it to a friend, and I wouldn't pre-order anything without knowing if it's returnable at the counter like I usually do. Read and move on.

## What's working

- The "BE FIRST" hero shot is genuinely elevated — moody, editorial, not stock-photo generic, and the model reads age-appropriate for aspirational-not-alienating.
- Six product tiles with real, recognizable names (Oscar de la Renta, Carolina Herrera, Christian Louboutin) — no filler, no lorem ipsum, images all rendered clean.
- The "Match with a personal style advisor" CTA is a nice nod to how I actually shop — I like a human touch, unlike Sephora's endless "0 points" app nagging.
- Clean nav bar (Women/Men/Shoes/Sale) at top — easy to scan, no clutter.

## What's weak

- Zero pricing or offer anywhere — not even "up to X% off" or a InCircle points mention, which after three gift-card emails this week feels like tonal whiplash rather than a deliberate reset.
- "Pre-Order Now" is vague — pre-order for delivery when? No date, no shipping window, nothing that tells me when I'd actually get the Louboutin bag.
- No InCircle tier or points callout at all, even though Neiman's has led with that hard all week (Triple Points, $1,500 gift card) — feels disconnected from the rest of this week's cadence.
- Product tiles have no price tags visible — I have to click through blind, which I don't love doing on my phone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a visible price or "starting at $X" under at least the hero product — I'm not clicking into a pre-order with zero price anchor.
- 2. Tie this to the InCircle program somehow — even a small "InCircle members get early access" line would connect it to the rest of the week's messaging instead of feeling like a separate campaign.
- 3. Give "Pre-Order Now" a real date — "Arriving October" or similar — so it doesn't read as vague marketing filler.
- 4. Bring back "Match with a personal style advisor" as a clickable CTA with a name/photo — right now it's just decorative icon text.
