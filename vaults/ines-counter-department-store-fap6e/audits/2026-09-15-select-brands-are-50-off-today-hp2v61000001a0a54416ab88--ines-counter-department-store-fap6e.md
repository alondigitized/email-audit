# Select brands are 50% off today 🤑
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- So the 50% off banner is right up top, no scrolling required — good, at least this one respects my time before it makes me hunt. But then it immediately pivots into "Beauty's Latest," a whole new-launch story about the InnBeauty Project cleanser and moisturizer, and I have no idea if either of those is actually part of the 50% off deal or a completely separate pitch. That's the same trick Sephora pulled on September 12th with "50% off select beauty⏰" — bury the "what's actually discounted" question under a wall of new-and-shiny.
- Then it's the now-familiar Sephora grid: nine products, zero prices visible until I tap "Add to Cart" and find out at checkout. Tower 28, Kenra, Kiehl's, Rare Beauty by Selena Gomez, Drunk Elephant — fine brands, but this is basically the September 12th "Today only: 50% off select faves 🌶️" email wearing a different hat, right down to the "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA" badge spam. Compare that to the September 12th send that actually showed strikethrough pricing (Clarins $96→$48, Gucci Flora $94) — that one I could evaluate at a glance. This one makes me work for it.
- Would I click? Maybe, out of habit, because I'm a Beauty Insider and I trust the brand enough to poke around. Would I forward it? No — nothing here is personalized or urgent enough to share. I'd mark it read and maybe circle back if I remember later, which for a "TODAY ONLY" deal defeats the purpose.

## What's working

- The 50% off hero is above the fold with a clear "SHOP NOW" button — no hunting required, unlike Neiman Marcus's September 15th "exclusive offer" email which buried its CTA under redundant grid copy.
- "TODAY ONLY" stamp creates real urgency, and it's a clean sans-serif badge, not screaming caps-lock spam.
- The Clean at Sephora seal on multiple products is a trust cue I actually look for — I care what's going on my 47-year-old skin.
- Rewards tiers (Insider/VIB/Rouge) are visible in the footer, so at least the loyalty structure is acknowledged, even if buried at the bottom.

## What's weak

- No prices anywhere on the nine-product grid — every single item just says "Add to Cart," so I can't tell what's 50% off, what's full price, or what the new-launch items cost. The September 12th email at least showed strikethrough pricing.
- The "50% off Select Beauty" hero and the "New and Iconic InnBeauty Project" section blur together — I genuinely can't tell if the new cleanser/moisturizer duo qualifies for the discount or if I'm being upsold full-price product under a discount banner.
- Nine near-identical white product tiles in a row is visual fatigue — Nordstrom's beauty emails at least break up grids with lifestyle photography between product rows.
- "Exclusions/terms apply" tucked in tiny gray text under the hero — that's the fine-print move that makes me trust the offer less, the same move Macy's pulled with its September 15th "confirm now" email.
- Three more promo blocks after the grid (Body Moisturizers, Cult Gaia fragrance, Fragrance Minis) — by that point I've mentally checked out; it's overload, Macy's-style, in a brand I expect better from.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put actual prices or percentage-off tags on every product tile — the September 12th send proved Sephora can do this; this one regressed.
- 2. Clarify upfront whether the InnBeauty Project launch is included in the 50% off or not — a one-line "New arrivals excluded" or "New arrivals included" kills the ambiguity instantly.
- 3. Cut the grid to 6 items max, or break it up with a lifestyle image every 3 products so it doesn't read as a spreadsheet.
- 4. Move "Exclusions/terms apply" up in font size or at minimum keep it near the hero, not shrunk into invisibility.
- 5. Trim the post-grid promo stack to one additional module instead of three — pick the strongest (Fragrance Minis, given the seasonal timing) and cut the rest.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 50% off day: no guessing on prices`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `See exactly what's discounted before you tap Shop Now.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Today's 50% off list — Tower 28, Rare Beauty, Kiehl's, and more.`
