# New Daily Deals ✅ Free Shipping ✅ Kohl's Cash ✅
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-07**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again. This is roughly the tenth Deal Days send since 10/4, and the subject only swaps the emoji for a row of checkmarks. I've gone numb. The top is the same empty maroon block I griped about on 10/5. It looks like a hero that never loaded, and I have to scroll past it to reach anything.
- Once I get past that, the body is a long, dense stack of offers. It has 30% off boots, $14.99 polos, 50% off toys, 50% off pajamas, an 80% off clearance banner, and a Yankee Candle at $14.99. It's Macy's-style overload with none of the Macy's polish. Nothing here speaks to a woman who buys her skincare and fragrance at the counter. The few things I'd touch are the Kohl's Rewards callouts, and even those are home goods.
- Would I click? Probably not. Forward it? No. I'd leave it unread, and a few cards would make me glance at the Kohl's Cash line before I did. Respect for the customer is what I notice in an email, and this one shouts.

## What's working

- The "Kohl's Rewards' members take $10 off your home purchase of $50 or more" green bars tell me my membership means something. That's the closest this email gets to talking to me.
- The "Shop now. Decide later. Free returns through January 31, 2027" line is real reassurance, and as someone who returns at the brand event, I notice it.
- Each tile has one offer, one product and a "Shop Now" link, so the tiles are easy to scan.
- The barcoded coupon codes at the bottom (extra 25% off, $10 off home) are practical. I could use them at the register without hunting.
- The status line at the top, "KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER · Earn 5% REWARDS", shows they know who I am.

## What's weak

- The top hero is a big empty maroon block with nothing legible. It's a dead zone right where the offer should be.
- Too many offers are stacked at once. Boots, polos, jumping beans, candles, toys, sweaters, coats, pajamas and clearance all compete for attention, so no single one lands.
- "+25% off w/coupon" and "Coupons do not apply" appear on neighboring tiles. I have to read fine print to work out what I'd actually pay. That feels like a trap, not honest pricing.
- Almost nothing is for me: men's polos, kids' clothes, toys and pajamas. No beauty at all, and the "Sephora at Kohl's" mention is buried in the footer fine print.
- The email is very long, and the legal text at the bottom is tiny and unreadable on a phone without zooming.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix or remove the hero. Lead with one clear offer, like "Kohl's Cash: earn $10 for every $50 spent," instead of an empty block.
- 2. Cut the tiles from nine to about four, and segment them by what I buy. For me, that would be Sephora at Kohl's and beauty.
- 3. Mark clearly which offers take the coupon and which don't, with a plain "Coupon eligible" tag, not asterisks.
- 4. Move the Kohl's Rewards and Kohl's Cash earn/redeem dates to the top. Sephora and Macy's both put loyalty status and points offers up front, and Sephora's giant "2X INSIDER" at least tells me what the offer is.
- 5. Space out the sends. Neiman Marcus's quiet Burberry editorial earned my attention precisely because it didn't shout, and Kohl's could learn from that.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has several markup defects: a stray `</custom>` tag, a `<div>` inside `<head>`, and an empty `<title>`. It also has widespread missing `alt` attributes and four stacked tracking pixels. Authentication and unsubscribe headers could not be verified because the relay didn't capture them, and the source is truncated, so most links and the footer are unchecked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **73 tracking links unprobed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so none were checked for redirect chains or 4xx/5xx. Links use opaque tokens (`https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABB7InYi…`).
- **Four tracking beacons load in the first lines of `<body>`:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/d0b0ca17-…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`
  
  This is redundant open tracking across three vendors, one of them third-party (Adobe `demdex`). It adds four extra requests and four points of failure.
- **Malformed pixel tag.** `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?…" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>` closes an element that was never opened.
- **Per-recipient IDs in image URLs.** `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1023337_2026107&email_name=261007_DG_Email_Wed_PM…` carries a subscriber ID in every image request. It's functional, but any proxy or log sees it.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, confirmed by QA on at least 10 images.**
  - Content images: `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`, and `59107814_2_2x`.
  - Tracking pixels: `o.gif` and `event`.
  - Screen readers will announce filenames, or nothing. If these carry the offer content, images-off clients lose it.
  - The first beacon (`click.chp.kohls.com/o/…`) has no `alt` at all, while the others use `alt=""`.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Invalid `<head>` content.** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`, which is invalid. Parsers will implicitly close `<head>` there, and the schema markup is useless in email clients anyway.
- **Empty icon link.** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty `href`, which can trigger a request for the document URL.
- **Broken CSS selector.** `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` is missing its leading `.`, so it matches nothing.
- **Layout tables without `role="presentation"`.** The 1×1 tracking table, the "AAM" table and the preheader table (`<table width='640' …>`) lack it. The main wrappers have it.
- **Web font imports.** `@import url(https://fonts.googleapis.com/…)` is wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, so Outlook is protected. Clients that strip `<link>` and `@import` fall back to Arial, which is acceptable.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FirstName]`) appear in the visible source or subject. Footer and body content past the truncation point was not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unverified, not confirmed defects.** The QA tool flagged `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results` as missing. Its notes attribute this to the AgentMail relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. Re-check against raw headers from a direct-delivery inbox.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and physical address.** Neither could be confirmed because the HTML is truncated before the footer.
- **From address.** `Kohls@s.kohls.com` is a subdomain sender and is consistent with the click domains (`*.kohls.com`).

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not verifiable.** All links are opaque `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=` tokens and weren't resolved, so UTM parameters and landing pages can't be assessed.
- **Campaign identifiers visible in image URLs.** `email_name=261007_DG_Email_Wed_PM` and `mi_ecmp=1023337_2026107` are present, but there is no evidence they match any link's final destination.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove `</custom>` and move the schema `<div>` out of `<head>`. Add a real `<title>` and drop or fill the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon">`.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, with the offer text for any image-only deals. Give pixels `alt=""` and `style="display:block"`.
3. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Consolidate the four tracking pixels to one or two vendors.
5. Fix the `.ReadMsgBody` selector.
6. Re-run QA on raw headers (outside the relay) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, one-click POST, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
7. Re-run link resolution on a sample of the 73 `click.s.kohls.com` links to verify final URLs, UTMs and HTTP status. Rerun the footer checks on untruncated HTML.

**Note on the QA tally:** "57% pass rate, 0 issues, 3 warnings" doesn't match the many alt-text warnings listed. The score likely counts header checks the relay couldn't evaluate as failures.
