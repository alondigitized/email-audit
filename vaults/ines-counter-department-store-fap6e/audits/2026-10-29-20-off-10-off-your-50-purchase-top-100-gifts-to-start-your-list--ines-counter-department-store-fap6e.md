# 20% off ✅ $10 off your $50+ purchase ✅ Top 100 gifts to start your list ✅
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-29**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's again, and it's the fourth "20% off + $10 off $50+" send I've had since 10/26. The subject is three green checkmarks in a row, and it reads like a grocery list. I scrolled the whole thing, and it's a very long email that spends most of its length being a gift catalog.
- The top is fine. Status "Kohl's Rewards Member" and "Earn 5% Rewards" sit above a plus-sign graphic: 20% off plus Kohl's Cash. Ends Nov 1. After that comes a wall of tiles: For Her, For Him, For Teens, For Kids, toys, "Top 100 Gifts," home, then a Sephora at Kohl's block, then product cards, then a stack of barcodes. Nordstrom would have sent me one idea. Kohl's sends me the whole mall.
- Would I click? Probably only the Sephora block, and only because I'm a beauty regular. Would I forward it? No. I've seen this exact offer on 10/26, 10/27 and twice on 10/28, so I'd mark it read.

## What's working

- The member status strip ("Kohl's Rewards Member, Earn 5% Rewards") up top treats me like a known customer. I notice that.
- The offer is stated with real numbers and a real end date: Ends Nov 1.
- The coupon block at the bottom has actual codes and barcodes, with 20% off, $10 off $50 and $10 off jewelry. I can use those in store, which suits how I shop.
- "Free returns through January 31, 2027" is a genuine trust cue for holiday gifting. Macy's rarely says that this plainly.
- The Sephora at Kohl's block is clean: "Get 25% off Rare Beauty Makeup & Fragrance," dated Oct 29, with a single "Shop Now."

## What's weak

- Subject line: three ✅ emoji make it read like a checklist or a spam filter test, not a person talking to me. The Sephora Insider "THIS JUST IN" send had the same shouting problem.
- The email scrolls forever, and nothing is the hero. There are about a dozen "Shop Now" buttons, so my eye has nowhere to land after the first screen.
- The beauty offer is buried in the middle, below toys and home. Neiman Marcus would never put a brand-name beauty event under a toy box.
- It's the same offer for the fourth time in three days. Doing it again with a gift guide on top isn't news.
- "Coupons do not apply to select brands" and the small-print block at the bottom make the offer feel less honest. I'd want to know which brands before I click.
- Nothing here speaks to a 47-year-old beauty buyer. The pajama model and kids' tiles are fine, but the product cards are a slushie machine and a mattress topper.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story per send. If it's the stack, lead with the stack and cut the category tiles. If it's the Sephora event, move it to the top and let it be the hero.
- 2. Drop the emoji repetition. Write one plain sentence with the offer and the date.
- 3. Show me which brands are excluded right next to the offer, not in the fine print.
- 4. Cut the cadence. If I got this on 10/26, 10/27 and 10/28, the 10/29 send should be a different offer or it shouldn't go out.
- 5. Segment by what I've bought. For me, that means beauty first. Sephora at Kohl's is the one block I'd actually open.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 20% off + $10 off $50 ends Nov 1`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 5% back in Rewards on every order. Codes inside.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Beauty Insiders: your 25% off Rare Beauty ends tonight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Most of the 5 QA warnings are probably artifacts of the AgentMail relay (missing headers) and the truncated HTML (footer not visible). The source does have real defects: invalid markup (`</custom>`, a `<div>` in `<head>`), an empty `<title>` and favicon href, viewport zoom lock, and several images with no `alt`.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probing skipped:** 77 links on `click.s.kohls.com` and similar redirect domains were not probed, so destination validity and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Stacked tracking pixels:** there are four in the first lines of `<body>`:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/468b9741-…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`
- **Malformed markup after the open pixel:** `<img src="…open.aspx…" alt=""></custom>` ends with a stray `</custom>` tag that has no opener.
- **Empty dynamic parameter:** the image URL for `916d6fa08d185233.png` has `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` with no value. This looks like an unresolved Movable Ink variable. The `mi_kc_event_p…` parameter after it is cut off, so I can't check it.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt`:** the QA scan flags 12 images. Real content images include:
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/f441d45d2128ae53.png`

  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/916d6fa08d185233.png`
  - `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/59205009_2_2x`
  - `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/59205009_3_2x`

  With images blocked or read by a screen reader, any offer or product information in them is lost.
- **False positive:** `o.gif` is flagged, but the source shows `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, which is valid for a decorative image. The `click.chp.kohls.com` and `kohls.demdex.net` pixels do lack `alt`. They are tracking pixels, so this is low impact.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Empty favicon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` can trigger a request to the document's own URL in some clients.
- **Zoom disabled:** the viewport meta has `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0`, which blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will close the head early and may drop the following `<style>` or `<link>` elements. Schema.org microdata is also not supported by Gmail's email markup.
- **Missing `role="presentation"`:** the 1×1 pixel table, the AAM table (`width="100%" align="center"`) and the preheader table (`width='640'`) have no role. The main layout tables do have it.
- **Mixed syntax:** an HTML 4.01 doctype is used with XHTML-style `/>` tags and mixed quote styles. This is low risk.

## 5. Compliance

- **Physical address:** not detected. The HTML is truncated before the footer, so I can't confirm a violation. The check is inconclusive.
- **SPF/DKIM/DMARC:** `Authentication-Results` is absent, so status is unknown. The sender is `Kohls@s.kohls.com`, a dedicated subdomain.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- Click links use opaque `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…` redirects, so UTM parameters and final landing pages can't be verified from the source or QA data.
- Image URLs carry Movable Ink parameters (`mi_u`, `mi_ecmp=1024744_20261029`, `email_name=261029_DG_Email_Thursd…`), which are consistent across images.
- The subject promises "20% off" and "$10 off your $50+ purchase". Those offers can't be matched to landing pages because the links weren't resolved.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` and `kohlsprod/59205009_*` images. Add `alt=""` to the remaining tracking pixels.
2. Remove the stray `</custom>` and move the schema.org `<div>` out of `<head>`. Use `<script type="application/ld+json">` instead if markup is wanted.
3. Fill in `<title>` and drop or fill the empty `rel="shortcut icon"` link.
4. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Fix the empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` parameter in the Movable Ink image URL.
6. Re-run QA on the full, untruncated raw message. That would let you confirm the `List-Unsubscribe` headers, the `Authentication-Results` values and the footer address.
7. Resolve a sample of the `click.s.kohls.com` links (follow the redirects) to check final URLs, UTM parameters and landing-page offers.
