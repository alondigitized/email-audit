# Ends today 👀 Doorbusters + 20% off are on the way out ...
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-28**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's again, and I'm tired. I've lost count of the sends since Wednesday. The 11/27 "Doorbusters + 20% off + $15 Kohl's Cash = JACKPOT 🏆" and the "DOORBUSTERS + up to 50% off deals + 20% off are here 👀" were basically this same email. The "ENDS TODAY" banner at the top is the only thing new. I'm looking at a roughly 12,000-pixel strip, so I'm reading shapes and big numbers. I can see "50%", "20%", "$15 Kohl's Cash" and a wall of "Doorbusters" tiles. I can't read the fine print.
- I'm 47 and I shop the beauty counter. I only found the Sephora-brand fragrance and beauty block near the very bottom, after more than ten screens of flannel, bedding and sweaters. Sephora at Kohl's is the only reason I'd care, and they buried it. Nordstrom and Saks don't make me scroll through a garage sale to find the one thing I came for.
- Would I click? Probably not. Would I forward it? No. I'd mark it read. If the fragrance block were up top, I might have looked.

## What's working

- The "ENDS TODAY" banner at the very top is clear, and it's the one honest urgency cue on a Black Friday weekend.
- The big "50%" and "20%" and the "$15 Kohl's Cash" are readable even at a shrunken size, so the offer is easy to find.
- The "Free shipping with a $25 purchase" strip is a real, concrete benefit.
- The category nav (Women, Men, Kids, Home, Beauty and so on) lets me skip ahead, and Beauty is listed.
- The red and black Kohl's branding is consistent, so I know who sent it.

## What's weak

- Beauty is the one category I care about. It sits at the very bottom, and it's only one block among dozens.
- This is a repeat of the last four or five sends, with the same hero and the same doorbusters. It feels like noise, not a reason to come back today.
- Tiny disclaimers like "Select styles. Coupons do not apply" appear on tile after tile. That feels like fine print working against me, and it puts me off the honesty of the offer.
- Nothing here recognizes me as a Kohl's Card or Rewards member. There's no points or tier message that tells me what I earn, and I won't use a card without points back.
- The strip is enormous, with too many tiles and no single hero product. Even Macy's, with its "LAST CHANCE: confirm now to get your extra 25% off!" email, gave me one thing to do.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one clear ending-tonight offer and a single hero CTA, then cut the email to about six tiles instead of 30.
- 2. Move the Sephora at Kohl's beauty block to the top for people who shop beauty, and segment by category history.
- 3. Show Kohl's Cash dates and Rewards earning up front, with the exclusions in plain language next to the offer.
- 4. Stop sending near-identical emails on consecutive days. Fold them into a single final-day send, like Neiman's "3 ways to save" structure but without repeating the same black box three times.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last day: 20% off + $15 Kohl's Cash ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tonight only: Sephora at Kohl's, up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your Kohl's Cash expires at midnight. Doorbusters while they last.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Beauty, fragrance and 20% off with coupon. Free shipping over $25.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard table-based layout with four open-tracking pixels and 104 click-redirect links. The HTML is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link, physical address and link destinations could not be inspected. The QA run passed 38% of checks, with 0 errors and 5 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverifiable links:** The probe skipped all 104 links because they are tracking redirects (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…`, `click.chp.kohls.com`). Destination status and final URLs are unconfirmed.
- **Four open-tracking pixels in one message:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/286ddf54-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1026908_20261128`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager, third-party)

  Several redundant pixels add request overhead and failure points. Where `referrer: no-referrer` and image blocking apply, they may under-count opens.
- **Plain-text bloat:** 18,468 of 24,318 characters (76%) of the plain-text part are URLs, above the 70% threshold. This is a spam-filter risk and a poor plain-text reading experience.
- **Malformed markup:** The open.aspx pixel is followed by a stray `</custom>` closing tag with no opening tag. The `<head>` also contains `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` with an empty href.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images:** `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` (2a5adc5afb647aad, f441d45d2128ae53, c2f81e9ac8daee44, 415c8a18fc2950ea, 69494b48a79b4240, 78308d1d9adb0751, 916d6fa08d185233) and the product images `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/60479404_1_2x`, `60699446_4_2x` and `60699446_3_2x`. If the hero or offer copy is baked into these images, it is invisible with images blocked or in a screen reader.
- **Tracking pixels without alt:** Most of the "missing alt" warnings are tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `event`, `286ddf54-…`). Add `alt=""` to them and `aria-hidden="true"` where it is missing. The first pixel sits directly in `<body>` and is not hidden.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Invalid head content:** The `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` inside `<head>` is invalid. Parsers will relocate it, and email clients ignore microdata anyway.
- **Missing table roles:** The tracking tables, the AAM table and the preheader table (`width='640'`) lack `role="presentation"`, unlike the main layout tables.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible HTML. The footer and later body sections were truncated and not checked.

- **Physical address:** The check found none. The footer is outside the truncated HTML, so this is **unconfirmed**. Verify against the full source.
- **Authentication-Results:** Absent because of the relay. SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `s.kohls.com` is unknown and not assessable from this data.
