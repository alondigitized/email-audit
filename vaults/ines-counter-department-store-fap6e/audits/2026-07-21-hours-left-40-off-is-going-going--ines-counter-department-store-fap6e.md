# HOURS LEFT ⏳ 40% off is going, going ...
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-21**
## Executive summary

- Email sixty-three. I'm not even sure it's sixty-three — somewhere after the fourth "40% off is ending TODAY" this week I stopped trusting the number. When I saw "HOURS LEFT ⏳" in the subject, my thumb was halfway to archive before the email finished loading. And when it did load? Clearance at 85%, 40% off summer styles, Nike at 50% off, and — I genuinely had to blink — an **Amazon Device Deals** block. I'm supposed to believe my hours are running out to buy a Kindle?
- To Kohl's credit, the summer model in the green dress looked like she could be one of my friends, and the Kohl's Rewards barcode at the bottom is the one honest gesture in an otherwise noisy inbox bomb. But I shop Nordstrom for this category of casualwear, and Nordstrom's clearance event didn't need to mention BBQ budgets to get me to click. This email feels like it was written for someone who still clips Sunday circulars, not someone who expects to be treated as a known customer.
- I'd mark it read. I would not click, and I'd never forward this to anyone I want to keep as a friend.

## What's working

- The green-dress model in the summer lifestyle shot reads mid-40s — one of the few signals in this email that actually sees me.
- 85% clearance is a real number that stops the scroll, same as it did in yesterday's send.
- Kohl's Rewards Exclusive barcode at the bottom acknowledges I'm a member — that's more than Neiman Marcus bothered to do in their garden-party dress email this month.
- The layout is clean enough — no broken images, no overlapping text, no lorem ipsum bleeding through.
- "Summer styles that won't eat into the BBQ budget" is at least seasonal and specific, even if the voice skews younger and more suburban than my taste.

## What's weak

- **Amazon Device Deals** in a fashion/apparel email is disqualifying for me. Sephora's "JUST DROPPED & ONLY HERE" wall of beauty brands, however crowded, at least stayed in its lane.
- The "HOURS LEFT" urgency is a lie I've been told four times this week. I opened the July 19th "today, gone tomorrow" send and 40% was back the next morning. The credibility is gone.
- Five competing sections — summer apparel, clearance, activewear, Nike, Amazon — means zero hierarchy. I don't know what Kohl's actually wants me to buy.
- No beauty content anywhere. I'm a beauty-counter regular. Not a single SKU, serum, or fragrance.
- "BBQ budget" language is friendly but it reads like they're talking to my sister-in-law who shops the Kohl's in Naperville, not to someone who considers Macy's a middle-of-the-road option.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill the Amazon block entirely.** Every off-brand module erodes the fashion credibility Kohl's desperately needs to be taken seriously against Macy's.
- 2. **Pick ONE offer and hero it.** Either 40% off summer or 85% clearance — not both plus Nike plus Amazon competing for my eye in the same scroll.
- 3. **Add beauty.** Even a single Kohl's beauty block — fragrance, skincare, or Sephora-at-Kohl's if that's still live — would have earned a click from me. Macy's last "LAST CHANCE" email at least had a cosmetics module.
- 4. **Retire "HOURS LEFT" until the offer actually expires.** If 40% is back tomorrow, the urgency language is costing you credibility faster than it's driving conversions.
- 5. **Subject Alt A:** `Your Rewards balance + 40% off — today only, Ines`
- 6. **Subject Alt B:** `Clearance hits 85% — summer styles under $10`
- 7. **Preheader Alt A:** `Grab summer pieces before the season closes out — Rewards cash stacks.`
- 8. **Preheader Alt B:** `Your top picks: dresses, sandals, and Nike — all marked down this morning.`

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "HOURS LEFT ⏳ 40% off" (2026-07-21)

### 1. Technical Summary

Email uses a standard ESP-rendered HTML 4.01 Transitional template with three separate pixel-tracking systems firing on open. Automated QA flagged compliance gaps (missing physical address, unsubscribe headers not captured) and pervasive missing alt text across 13 images.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Three overlapping open-tracking systems detected:**
- `https://click.chp.kohls.com/o/59f9e3e7-...` — CoherentPath pixel
- `https://mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016` — MessageGears open pixel
- `https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?PFUYD2JBHH5...` — Salesforce Marketing Cloud open pixel

Firing all three on a single open inflates open-rate metrics across platforms and creates attribution conflicts. The CoherentPath pixel also carries `mi_mid` and `cp_tp` query params that duplicate MessageGears identity data.

**22 click-redirect links skipped by HTTP probe** — destination URLs and HTTP status codes unverified by QA.

**Adobe Audience Manager sync pixel present:**
- `https://kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` — third-party data sync firing on open, no alt text, no `aria-hidden`.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**13 images missing `alt` text** (confirmed by QA). Affected sources include content images, not just tracking pixels:

| Image | Source |
|---|---|
| Product image | `kohlsprod/55869996_root2_2x` |
| Product image | `kohlsprod/55405645_root_2x` |
| Promo banners (×4) | `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d...`, `c2f81e9a...`, `415c8a18...` |
| Tracking pixels (×3) | CoherentPath, MessageGears, Demdex — should carry `alt=""` + `aria-hidden="true"` |

Tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `event`, `59f9e3e7...`) are missing both `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"`, which causes screen readers to announce raw filenames.

**`<title>` element is empty** — `<title></title>`. Some screen readers and email clients announce the document title; an empty title degrades accessibility and may reduce deliverability scoring.

**Malformed closing tag in open-tracking block:**
```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..."> <custom>
```
`</custom>` is not a valid HTML element. Strict parsers may reject or misrender the surrounding block.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — blocks pinch-to-zoom on mobile, an accessibility violation (WCAG 1.4.4).

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens visible in the truncated source. `mi_u=604230016` and `mi_ecmp=1018061_2026721` are populated, indicating subscriber ID and campaign ID rendered correctly.

`mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` (trailing empty value) on two pixel URLs (`916d6fa08d185233.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`) — appears to be an unpopulated dynamic field passed as an empty query param. Benign to rendering but indicates a data-feed gap for that attribute.

### 5. Compliance

**CAN-SPAM:**
- No physical mailing address detected in the truncated source. Required by 15 U.S.C. § 7704(a)(5). May be present below the truncation point — verify in full source.

**List-Unsubscribe headers:**
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not captured by AgentMail relay.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click) header not captured.
- Gmail and Apple Mail require `List-Unsubscribe-Post` for native one-click unsubscribe UI; absence degrades inbox placement signals.

**Authentication:**
- `Authentication-Results` header not captured. SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status unknown through the relay. Sending domain `s.kohls.com` should be verified independently.

**`<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer" />`** — suppresses referrer on all outbound clicks. This is intentional (common practice) but means GA4/site analytics will not receive email referrer attribution on any click-throughs that bypass the click-redirect tracker.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

`mi_ecmp=1018061_2026721` is present on MessageGears pixel URLs but **UTM parameters are not visible** on any destination URLs in the truncated source. All 22 click links route through `click.chp.kohls.com` or `click.s.kohls.com` redirectors — whether those redirectors append UTM params to the final kohls.com URL is unverifiable without resolving the redirect chain.

`<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer" />` (noted above) will strip HTTP referrer on any direct link that bypasses click tracking, further breaking site-side attribution.
