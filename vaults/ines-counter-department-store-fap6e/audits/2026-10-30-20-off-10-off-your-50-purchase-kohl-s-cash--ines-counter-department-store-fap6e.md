# 20% off 👏 $10 off your $50+ purchase 👏 Kohl's Cash 👏
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-30**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again. This is the fifth "20% off + $10 off $50+" send since 10/26, and the clapping-hands emoji is the third punctuation style they've tried on the same offer. After the checkmarks on 10/29 and the train on 10/28, the subject has become wallpaper. The header does say "Kohl's Rewards Member, Earn 5% Rewards," so they know who I am. Then the body treats me like a stranger who needs the same coupon pitch again.
- The one thing that got me to slow down is the Sephora at Kohl's block: "30% off Sephora Collection, 20% off Rouge, first access, ends 11/9." That is the only part of the email aimed at someone like me. It sits about two-thirds of the way down, below a home section, a kitchen-appliance block and bedding. At the counter at Nordstrom or Saks, the beauty comes first. Here it's buried under mattress toppers and a Ninja slushie machine.
- Would I click? Probably the Sephora banner and nothing else. Would I forward it? No. I'd mark it read and wait for the Sephora Insider email.

## What's working

- The Rewards status and "Earn 5% Rewards" line is at the very top, so I can see my tier without hunting for it. For someone who won't use a card without points, that matters.
- The Sephora Savings Event block is clear: two circles, 30% and 20%, "Ends 11/9". It also says first access for Beauty Insiders, which is a real perk.
- The coupon codes with barcodes at the bottom (SAVE20, JEWELRY10) are practical. I can screenshot them and use them in store.
- The shipping and returns reassurance ("Free returns through January 31, 2027") is a trust cue I like.

## What's weak

- The hero offer is "20% off" plus a "$10 off $50+" coupon plus Kohl's Cash. That's three offers stacked in a row, and the eye has no single place to land. Neiman Marcus's "$50 gift card for every $200" is one clean idea.
- The email is a very long scroll, with home, kitchen, bedding, North Kohl's, toy box, Top 100 gifts, and "Picked with you in mind". It's the Macy's overload problem.
- "Picked with you in mind" shows a slushie machine, a towel and jeans. Nothing in it reflects me or anything I've bought.
- Fine print everywhere: "Coupons do not apply to select brands," plus exclusions on the Sephora codes. The 20% comes with "w/coupon" caveats in the hero. It doesn't feel fully honest.
- The North Kohl's elf world is cutesy and aimed at a younger, family audience. It's a different brand voice from the polished Sephora section.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the Sephora at Kohl's event for the beauty segment. Pull the Sephora block to the top for anyone who has bought beauty from them.
- 2. Pick one offer for the hero. The $10 off $50 can live lower down.
- 3. Cut the length by half, and make "Picked with you in mind" actually use my purchase history.
- 4. Make the exclusions visible near the offer rather than in the footer.
- 5. Slow the cadence to one real send every couple of days, as Sephora Insider's constant flow does too, but theirs at least changes the creative.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a Movable Ink + Salesforce Marketing Cloud stack with 77 wrapped tracking links. The visible markup has several malformed or non-semantic elements and many images without alt text. The compliance and authentication checks couldn't be confirmed, because the HTML is truncated and the relay dropped the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No link probing.** All 77 tracking links were skipped as redirect domains. Destination status, redirect chains and landing-page validity are unverified.
- **Link wrapping.** Links use opaque SFMC wrappers (`https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7Im…`), so destinations can't be read from the source.
- **Four tracking pixels before any content:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/4c4f121b-…` is a bare `<img>` directly under `<body>`, with no alt and no hiding style.
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager)
- **Malformed markup.** The `open.aspx` image is followed by a stray closing tag, `…&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`. There is no matching opening `<custom>`, and some parsers handle that badly.
- **Empty favicon link.** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href, which can trigger a request to the page's own URL.
- **Plain-text part.** It is 13,612 of 19,371 characters URLs (70%). That is spam-filter risk and a poor text-only reading experience.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** The QA flagged 14 images:
  - **Likely content images** (4 `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` with `email_name=261030_DG_Email_Friday`, plus `59205033_1_2x` and `59392300_roots_2x`): these are the real accessibility defect, since images-off and screen-reader users get no content. The flagged `rp/*.png` images with no `email_name` param (`69494b48…`, `78308d1d…`, `916d6fa0…`) also look like content.
  - **False positive:** `o.gif` already has `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"`, so the QA flag is incorrect.
  - **Tracking pixels:** `click.chp.kohls.com/o/…` and `demdex.net/event` have no alt. Add `alt=""`.
- **Empty `<title>`.** `<title></title>` has no content.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Layout tables without roles.** The preheader table (`<table width='640' … class="container">`), the 1×1 pixel table and the AAM table have no `role="presentation"`. The main wrapper tables do have it, so this is inconsistent.
- **Invalid `<head>` content.** A `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`, which is invalid. Browsers and clients may close the head early and shift the content.
- **Web fonts.** Roboto and Montserrat load via `@import` and `<link>` inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. Fallbacks (`Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`) are present, so this is low risk.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear. `mi_u=604230016` is a resolved per-subscriber ID in the pixel and image URLs. The truncation prevents a check of the rest of the body.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
All findings below are unconfirmed rather than proven defects:
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** both not found. The QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay not capturing headers, so it can't be treated as a sender failure. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so verify against the raw original message.
- **Physical address:** the QA found none. The HTML is truncated before the footer, so this is unverifiable here.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** missing, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. The From domain (`s.kohls.com`) and the tracking domains (`click.s.kohls.com`, `click.chp.kohls.com`) differ, so alignment is worth checking.
- **Unsubscribe link in body:** not visible in the captured portion.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters:** can't be assessed. Destinations are hidden behind `?qs=` wrappers and no probes ran.
- **Campaign naming:** `mi_ecmp=1024827_20261030` and `email_name=261030_DG_Email_Friday` are consistent with each other and with the send date, 2026-10-30.
- **Dynamic content:** `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` is empty in the `916d6fa0…png` URL. This is likely a Kohl's Cash redemption end date that failed to populate. It's a possible personalization failure, though the URL is truncated.
- **Subject vs. offer:** the subject mentions 20% off, $10 off $50+ and Kohl's Cash. The landing-page match can't be verified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run the audit with the full raw message (headers plus untruncated HTML) to settle the unsubscribe headers, authentication results, footer address and UTM questions.
2. Add meaningful `alt` to the content images (`mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png`, `59205033_1_2x`, `59392300_roots_2x`). Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
3. Remove the stray `</custom>` after the `open.aspx` pixel. Move the `itemscope` div out of `<head>`, or use JSON-LD or a body-level placement.
4. Fill in `<title>`. Remove or populate `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`.
5. Drop `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
6. Add `role="presentation"` to the preheader and pixel tables.
7. Check why `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` is empty in the `916d6fa0…png` URL, and use a fallback if the date is missing.
8. Improve the plain-text part so less of it is raw tracking URLs.
9. Fix the QA check so it doesn't flag images that already have `alt=""` (such as `o.gif`).
