# Saint Laurent’s Fall 2026 Collection
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-10**
## Executive summary

- This is the Neiman's I like. After a week of the $200-off ladder (10/6 "Grab it now: $200 off your purchase," 10/7 "You could save $50–$200," and this morning's "Don't miss out on up to $200 off..."), a Saint Laurent editorial with no code, no countdown and no points math feels like being treated as a customer and not a wallet. A black suit, a slouchy leather hobo, and a white page. I stayed longer than I do on the shouty ones.
- It's also thin. There's no price on the Mombasa, no InCircle mention, and no reason to act today. I'd click "Shop Saint Laurent Bags" out of curiosity. I wouldn't buy off it, because I don't put money on a card with no points back. I'd skim it on my commute and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it, and I'd save the bag for a friend who actually carries Saint Laurent.

## What's working

- The white space and the single-column layout are calm. Sephora's "Hey, Insider" catch-all packs four things into one screen, and this one doesn't.
- "Suited for Any Occasion" is a clear headline. The tailored black suit is aimed at a woman my age, not a teenager.
- The Mombasa copy is specific and a little wry. "An It bag from the early aughts" speaks to someone who remembers the first round.
- Both CTAs are in the category I'm looking at, and the "Shop Saint Laurent" button is outlined and easy to tap.
- No fake urgency. Macy's "LAST CHANCE: confirm now" shouting is exactly what this isn't.

## What's weak

- No price on the bag, no "from $X" and no availability note. At this tier I want to know whether I'm looking at $2,000 or $4,000 before I click.
- There's no InCircle line anywhere. After three days of "3x points" emails, going silent on points feels odd. Sephora puts the Insider points multiplier in the hero.
- "Shop Saint Laurent Bags" is a small, pale underlined link. It's the real product CTA, and it's the weakest thing on the page.
- The subject says "Fall 2026 Collection," but the body shows only one suit and one bag. It reads like a campaign teaser, not a collection.
- It's the fourth Neiman's send in about 24 hours, and the footer icons for the app and a style advisor are generic filler.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a price or "from $X" and the InCircle points on the Mombasa. A shopper like me needs both to justify a click.
- 2. Make the bag link a real button, with copy like "Shop the Mombasa" instead of a category dump.
- 3. Add a third and fourth Saint Laurent piece, so the subject's "collection" claim is true.
- 4. Throttle the cadence. If the offer emails run at this volume, hold editorial sends like this one for a different day.
- 5. Turn the style-advisor icon into a real invitation. For a designer purchase, "Book a Saint Laurent appointment" is more useful than a generic icon.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with responsive media queries. It has no errors in the QA run (0 issues, 4 warnings, 50% pass rate). The source is truncated and 15 tracking links were not probed, so link, footer and compliance checks are incomplete. The confirmed problems are internal ESP template notes left in the shipped HTML and a few accessibility and CSS defects in the `<head>`.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Links not validated.** The QA run skipped HTTP probes on 15 tracking/click-redirect links, so redirect targets, status codes and final landing URLs are unverified.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 2825 of 3310 characters (about 85%) are URLs, over the 70% threshold. Raw tracking URLs dominate the plain-text alternative, which can hurt spam scoring and readability. This is likely driven by the 15 tracking links.
- The visible HTML excerpt contains no `<a>` tags, so I cannot assess individual `href` values.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **No `lang` attribute.** The tag is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">`, with no `lang`. Screen readers cannot pick a pronunciation profile.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` sets `maximum-scale=1`. This blocks pinch-zoom on clients that honor it (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Invalid font fallback.** `.disclaimerText` uses `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, 'sans-serif'`. The quoted `'sans-serif'` is read as a font name, not the generic family, so the fallback chain has no generic family.
- **Weak `<title>`.** `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` has a trailing space and doesn't reflect the subject ("Saint Laurent's Fall 2026 Collection").
- **Hamburger nav not confirmed.** The checkbox hack (`.navbar-checkbox:checked~.menu`) is only supported in some clients (Apple Mail, iOS Mail). Gmail and Outlook strip it. The fallback markup is cut off, so I can't confirm the nav degrades gracefully.
- **Leftover dead code.** Three commented-out viewport meta tags remain, including `width=1024` and `width=320, initial-scale=0.29`. They are harmless but add clutter.
- I could not check image `alt` text, because the body is truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Internal template notes in production HTML.** The file opens with a comment block that includes:
  - `REQUIRED variables: @email … @base_refid … @refid = CONCAT(@base_refid,"") … @uEm … @cmdid`
  - `Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", "EMAIL_ID", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email)`
  - `Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "uEm", …)`
  - `CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW` and `<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->`

  This exposes internal Data Extension names, field names and authoring instructions to every recipient. It also shows the template wasn't stripped of its scenario-selection note before send. The comment sits before the `DOCTYPE`, which can trigger quirks-mode rendering in some clients.
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `@var`) appear in the visible rendered portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found.** The QA tool notes this may be an AgentMail relay artifact, so it isn't confirmed as a sender-side omission.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The same caveat applies. One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders, so check the original message headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `e.neimanmarcus.com`.
- The unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated footer, so I can't confirm them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

4. **Fix the font stack** in `.disclaimerText` to use unquoted `sans-serif`.
