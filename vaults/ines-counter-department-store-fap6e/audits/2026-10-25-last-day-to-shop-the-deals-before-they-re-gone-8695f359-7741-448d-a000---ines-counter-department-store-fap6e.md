# LAST DAY to shop the deals before they're gone ⏳
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-25**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again. This is the twelfth send in about two weeks, and it's the same maroon "Lowest Prices of the Season" template I've seen since 10/19. The "ENDS TODAY" tag is the only new thing, and after eleven emails of "lowest prices," a deadline doesn't read as credible to me. I know this one already. It's a long scroll of tiles for tees, boots, towels, LEGO, kitchen appliances, bras and underwear. Nothing in it says it was meant for me.
- The clearance box is the strongest thing at the top: "Clearance, up to 85% off after an extra 50% off." The "Plus, earn Kohl's Cash" strip sits right under it. As a 47-year-old, I do notice the Status and Earn 9% REWARDS line at the top, because I won't use a card that doesn't pay me back. But it's tiny, and the brand doesn't do anything with it.
- I'd mark it read. I wouldn't forward it. If I clicked at all, it would be on the clearance banner or the boots, and I'd do it out of curiosity, not because I felt a pull.

## What's working

- The "ENDS TODAY" tag and "Select styles on sale now through Oct. 25" give a real, dated deadline.
- The clearance box, "up to 85% off after an extra 50% off," is a big, clear number.
- My rewards status and 9% earn rate sit at the top, so Kohl's does know who I am.
- Each tile has one price, one "Shop Now" button, and "Reg." and "Starting at" copy. That's easy to scan, and the boots at $17.99 caught me.
- "Shop now. Decide later. Free returns through January 27, 2027" is a real trust cue. It speaks to someone like me who returns things.

## What's weak

- It's the same template for the twelfth time. Sephora at least changes the savings-event step banner. This looks like a copy-paste.
- The email is far too long. Ten stacked tiles run well below the fold, and nothing is prioritized. It's the Macy's-style overload I avoid.
- The sports-fan block ("A matchup this good deserves a look to match") for Tampa Bay Rays gear feels random and unrelated to me.
- The beauty and designer cues I shop for are missing. There's no brand name I'd go to a counter for. Neiman Marcus leads with one clear number and a curated feel. This has a bra, panties and a drink maker side by side.
- The disclaimer footer is long and tiny. The "Kohl's Cash" strip and the "Coupons do not apply" lines undercut the offers. I can't tell which tiles stack with coupons and which don't.
- The Rewards line is too small to read without zooming, and it isn't tied to any offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to three or four tiles led by the 85% clearance. A "last day" email should be short and urgent, not a catalog.
- 2. Put the Kohl's Cash amount and my rewards balance next to the hero, so I know what I get back on today's purchase.
- 3. Vary the creative day to day. Eleven identical sends train me to ignore the twelfth.
- 4. State the coupon exclusions on the tile, not at the bottom.
- 5. Segment the sports block out of my send, and give me a women's apparel and beauty-adjacent lead instead.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a fixed-width (640px) table layout with an HTML 4.01 doctype and Google Fonts imports. It carries four open-tracking pixels. The source has several markup defects (a stray `</custom>` tag, a `<div>` in `<head>`, missing alt attributes, no `role="presentation"` on some layout tables). The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive, because the HTML is truncated and the relay strips headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverifiable links:** 73 tracking links were skipped from HTTP probing (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…`, `click.chp.kohls.com`, `mi.kohls.com`). Destination status, redirect chains and landing-page validity are unconfirmed.
- **Four tracking pixels:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/9e594237-…` (no `alt`, no `style`)
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager, third-party domain)
- **Pixel URLs carry an identifier:** `mi_u=604230016` appears in the `mi.kohls.com` pixel and in the image URLs. It looks like a per-recipient ID in URLs that can be logged by image proxies.
- **Malformed markup after the open pixel:** `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?…" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>` has an orphaned `</custom>` closing tag.
- **Empty parameter:** one image URL contains `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_p…`. The date value is empty. This may be intentional, but it is consistent with an unpopulated field.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:**
  - `58967014_1Ls_2x`, `_13Ls_2x`, `_11Ls_2x` and `_12Ls_2x` (`images.am.kohls.com`) are content images.
  - `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png` and `916d6fa08d185233.png` (`mi.kohls.com/p/rp/`) are also flagged. If the images are images-off-sensitive, the email degrades to blank space.
  - The QA list is truncated, so there may be more.
- **Likely false positive:** `o.gif` is flagged, but the source shows `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, which is valid for a decorative pixel. The `event` (demdex) pixel and the `click.chp.kohls.com/o/…` pixel genuinely have no `alt` attribute.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>`.
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will close `<head>` and open `<body>` at that point, so anything after it is parsed unpredictably. The schema.org markup is also not supported by most clients.
- **Empty icon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty `href`.
- **Broken CSS selector:** `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` is missing the leading `.`, so the rule matches nothing.
- **Layout tables without semantics:** the 1×1 table, the AAM table (`width="100%" align="center"`) and the preheader table (`width='640'`) lack `role="presentation"`. Later tables have it.
- **Redundant font loading:** Roboto is loaded twice (`<link>` and `@import`), and both depend on external requests. Fallbacks (`Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`) are present.
- **Mixed doctype:** the HTML 4.01 doctype is combined with XHTML-style `/>` self-closing tags. This is harmless but inconsistent.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the truncated source. The only possible personalization gap is the empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` parameter noted above. The rest of the body and the footer were not available to check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post:** not found. The QA tool says AgentMail may not capture these headers, so this is unconfirmed rather than a defect. RFC 8058 one-click support cannot be verified.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `kohls@s.kohls.com` and the click domain is `click.s.kohls.com`, so the domains look aligned, but this is not proof of authentication.
- **Physical address:** the QA check did not detect one. The footer is truncated out of the supplied source, so I can't confirm whether it is absent.
- **Visible unsubscribe link:** not evaluable from the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No `utm_*` parameters are visible on any observable URL. Click URLs are wrapped in opaque `qs=` tokens, so final destinations and any UTM appending at redirect time can't be checked.
- Campaign identifiers are present only in image URLs: `mi_ecmp=1024491_20261025` and `email_name=261025_DG_Email_Sunday`. The date code matches the send date (2026-10-25).
- Landing-page alignment with the "LAST DAY" subject can't be assessed, because the redirects were not probed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the product and promo images. Add `alt=""` to the `click.chp.kohls.com` and `demdex` pixels.
2. Remove the stray `</custom>` tag. Move or remove the schema.org `<div>` from `<head>`.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta. Fill in `<title>` and remove or fill the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon" href="">`.
4. Fix `ReadMsgBody` to `.ReadMsgBody`. Add `role="presentation"` to all layout tables.
5. Audit the empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` parameter at the source.
6. Consolidate the four open pixels. Consider whether the third-party `demdex.net` pixel and the user ID in image URLs are necessary.
7. Re-run the audit against the raw message to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results` and the footer address. Follow a sample of the `click.s.kohls.com` links to confirm the final URLs, UTM handling and landing pages.
