# PSA 📣 Cyber Week starts early on the app!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- My first reaction was a tired "again?" This is the twelfth Sephora email in about ten days. The 11/21 send, "Cyber Week daily deals just dropped exclusively on the app 👀", already told me Cyber Week was on the app. This one says the same thing with a megaphone emoji.
- The hero is a loud purple sequin banner with a giant "30% OFF" on Color Wow. I can read that number from across the room. The offer is real, but it's one brand's hair products, and nothing in it says it's for me. I'm a counter girl. I buy skincare and fragrance at the brand event, so Color Wow hair mist doesn't make me reach for my phone.
- Below the hero, the email turns into a calendar of brand logos. I'd click once for the Sol de Janeiro 25% off, and only if I were already shopping. I wouldn't forward it to a friend, because it's a catalog with a sparkle filter. I'd mark it read.

## What's working

- The "30% OFF" hero reads instantly. I don't have to hunt for the offer, which Neiman's Black Friday sends bury under qualifiers.
- The 11/23–11/30 daily-deals grid tells me what's coming and when, so I can plan a purchase around a brand I care about. Laura Mercier, Lancôme and Clinique-adjacent names are the ones I'd watch.
- The free-sample-bag banner at the top is a nice nod to a regular. I like a gift with purchase.
- The footer's Sephora Credit Card, Texts and Loves List rows are tidy and easy to scan.

## What's weak

- The sample bag is for "app only with $95 min spend," and the code, BAGINFIRST, is in tiny print at the top right. The headline says FREE and the fine print takes it back. That's the buried-terms feel I can't stand.
- "PSA 📣 Cyber Week starts early" and "starts now" in the hero don't agree with the 11/21 email, which said the daily deals had already dropped. Which is it?
- It pushes the app, but I'm reading this in my inbox. Macy's at least gave me a "confirm now" button for my extra 25%.
- Nothing here mentions my Beauty Insider tier or points. The "Hi, Rosie INSIDER" corner is faint, and I don't see a points multiplier anywhere. I won't use a card without points back, and Sephora knows that.
- The page is very long, with several competing sections: hero, grid, Spot it, Minis, Gift Guide and the footer rows. Kohl's overloads me the same way.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer and put the Insider angle front and center, for example "Insiders: points on the daily deals." Move the sample-bag terms next to the headline, not at the edge of the page.
- 2. Cut the layout by a third. The grid and the sample bag can carry the email. Drop Minis and the Gift Guide from this send.
- 3. Pick a cadence. If the daily deals are coming, send one email on the day, not a preview and a "just dropped" in consecutive days.
- 4. Make it relevant to me. Put skincare or fragrance in the hero for someone with my history.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Cyber Week, app first: 30% off Color Wow today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Insider early access: daily deals from 11/23`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free sample bag with $95 on the app, plus the full 8-day deal calendar.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Olehenriksen, Laura Mercier, Fenty and more, one day each. See yours.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with a 700px fixed-width layout and Epsilon/Sephora redirect tracking. The confirmed defects are an empty greeting slot, a 0-character plain-text part, and a few markup and accessibility problems. The compliance and authentication findings are unverified because AgentMail didn't capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque tracked links:** CTAs go through `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a203b40a7db1dc5ff4bbe5cfc0/…?__dU__=…`. The final destinations aren't visible in the captured source.
- **Incomplete link coverage:** QA probed 12 of 39 links and skipped 27 as footer, utility or social. It reported no probe failures, but the skipped links are unverified.
- **Tracking pixel:** `<img src='https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…' style="display:none; … mso-hide: all"/>` is a hidden open-tracking pixel. It is functional, but it lacks `alt` (see section 3).
- **Empty structured data:** The JSON-LD block (`<script type='application/ld+json'>`) has `"@type": "DiscountOffer", "discountCode": ""` and a bare `"@type": "PromotionCard"`. Both are empty, while the real code `BAGITFIRST` appears only in the preheader. Most clients strip `<script>`, so the practical impact is low.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text part missing:** QA reports "Text version is 0 chars" [FAIL]. If this isn't a relay artifact, it's a deliverability and accessibility gap.
- **Malformed CSS:** In the `<style>` block, `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…}<![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media only screen and (max-width:480px)` block. Non-MSO parsers may treat that text as part of the next selector. That could drop `.mobileHeaderTierRouge` or the rules after it. This is an inference from the source, not a render test.
- **Fixed width:** The wrapper is `<table width="700" style="min-width:700px; width:700px">`. The visible CSS has no mobile override for that table, so narrow viewports may scroll horizontally. The CSS is truncated, so this is unconfirmed.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Missing `lang`:** `<html xmlns=… >` has no `lang` attribute. The `<title>` is the generic "Sephora" rather than the subject.
- **Missing `alt`:** The tracking pixel has no `alt`. It is hidden, so the screen-reader impact is minimal, but `alt=""` is the clean fix.
- **Invalid meta value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. This is cosmetic.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Empty greeting [FAIL]:** QA found an empty name slot (`"Hi ,"`). The first-name field rendered blank with no fallback. This is a confirmed broken-experience defect.
- No raw `{{…}}` or `%%…%%` tokens appear in the captured source.

## 5. Compliance
- **Unverified:** QA could not find `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers. It notes AgentMail may not relay them, so this doesn't confirm a sender-side gap. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both, so check the raw message.
- **Authentication unknown:** `Authentication-Results` is absent from the relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status can't be assessed. The From domain is `beauty.sephora.com`.
- **Footer not captured:** The source is truncated before the footer, so the physical address and the in-body unsubscribe link can't be confirmed. QA says an unsubscribe link was included in probing.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters:** The visible links use redirect-wrapped URLs with `__dU__` rather than plain `utm_*` parameters. UTM presence and landing alignment can't be verified from the captured data.
- **App-first CTA:** The subject, "Cyber Week starts early on the app", points at an app-first promotion. Whether the links resolve to an app deep link or a web fallback wasn't captured.
- **Promo code:** `BAGITFIRST` appears only in the hidden preheader. Whether the landing page carries it forward wasn't tested.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Greeting:** Add a fallback to the first-name merge field (for example `Hi there,`) or suppress the greeting when the name is empty.
2. **Plain text:** Confirm the multipart message has a `text/plain` part. If it's missing, add one with the code `BAGITFIRST` and the unsubscribe URL.
3. **Headers:** Check the raw message source for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` before treating them as defects.
4. **CSS:** Move the `<!--[if mso]>` block out of the `@media` query, or into a separate `<style>` inside a conditional comment.
5. **Accessibility:** Remove `maximum-scale=1`, add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel, and use the subject as the `<title>`.
6. **Mobile layout:** Verify the 700px table collapses below 480px.
7. **Link coverage:** Probe the redirect destinations for UTM and landing alignment, and extend coverage beyond 12 of 39 links.
