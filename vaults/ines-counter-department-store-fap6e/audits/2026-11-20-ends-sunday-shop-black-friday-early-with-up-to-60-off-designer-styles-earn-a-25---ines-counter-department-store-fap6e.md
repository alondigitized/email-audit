# Ends Sunday: Shop Black Friday early with up to *60% off* designer styles + Earn a $25–$1,250 gift card (includes beauty & sale styles)
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- This is the third time today I've opened the Neiman's Black Friday email with the same black box and red border. Earlier I got the "Shop Black Friday early: The Designer Sale is now up to 60% off + Earn a $25–$1,250 gift card" version, and this one is that email with "Ends Sunday" added to the subject. The shouting is the same. Three offers sit in three stacked red-bordered boxes: 60% off the Designer Sale, a $25–$1,250 gift card, and up to 30% off regular-price styles. Nothing tells me which one matters.
- The product grid below is nice. Parfums de Marly, Tom Ford, Jimmy Choo, Chloe and Mac Duggal are the designer access I shop Neiman's for. But the grid is only labels, with no prices and no "was/now." Nothing connects the 60% to any actual item. The fine print runs about as long as the email, and the gift card tiers need a calculator.
- I'd click through once for the beauty line, then close it. I wouldn't forward it. It's the same email I've already seen, and "Ends Sunday" is the only new information.

## What's working

- The grid of designer names (Parfums de Marly, Tom Ford, Jimmy Choo, Chloe) tells me this is the real Neiman's and not a clearance blast.
- The gift card box says "includes beauty & sale styles." That's the line that got my attention, because I buy beauty and I want something back on it.
- The category buttons (Women's Clothing, Handbags, Beauty) are black, wide and easy to tap on a phone.
- The "Limited time only. Exclusions apply." line sits right under the offer. At least it admits there are conditions.
- The Parfums de Marly bottle in the top left is the first product I see, and it's the one that matches how I shop.

## What's weak

- Three offers in three red boxes is too much. Compare Macy's "confirm now to get your extra 25% off," which at least picked one number.
- "SHOP ALL" is generic. It doesn't tell me whether I'm shopping the sale, the gift card event or the 30% off.
- The grid has no prices and no sale markers. Sephora's Dyson email at least showed what was discounted.
- The gift card math is buried in a legal block at the bottom, and the 30% off footnote has a different end date (12/2) than the gift card (11/22). I had to read the fine print to find that out.
- Cadence is wearing on me. I've now had the 11/19 "Private Event: 40% off" email three times, and Kohl's has sent six emails since the weekend. Neiman's is heading the same way.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer. Make the gift card the hero, since it's the member-style reward I respond to, and move the percent-off lines below it as support.
- 2. Put prices on the product grid, with "was/now" and a beauty item in the first row.
- 3. Put the gift card tiers in a small visible table (for example, spend $100 and get $25) instead of the legal block.
- 4. Rename "SHOP ALL" to something like "Shop the Designer Sale."
- 5. Add a real "Ends Sunday 11/22, 10 PM CT" line near the top and stop re-sending the same creative.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends Sunday: up to $1,250 back on designer + beauty`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Designer Sale gift card ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $100, get $25. Beauty counts. Ends 11/22 at 10 PM CT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Parfums de Marly, Tom Ford and Chloe, up to 60% off.`

# Technical Audit: Neiman's Black Friday

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML source is a table-based, XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with media-query responsive CSS. It has one confirmed source-level issue: internal ESP variable documentation is left in the delivered markup. The three automated QA warnings are about header capture through the relay, so they don't confirm any defect in the sender's setup. The source was truncated, so footer, links and body content were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 29 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe (QA INFO). Their redirect targets, status codes and expiry were not verified.
- No broken links are confirmed. The visible source doesn't include the anchors, so link-level issues can't be assessed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` prevents pinch-zoom in clients that honor it, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Title:** `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` has a trailing space and is generic. It is minor.
- **CSS-only hamburger menu:** It relies on `.navbar-checkbox:checked~…` selectors, with `display:none` on the checkbox. Support is limited to clients that allow `:checked` and `<input>`. There are no visible fallbacks in the source. Keyboard and screen-reader access is doubtful, but I can't confirm this from the truncated source.
- **Attribute selectors:** The `*[class=...]` selectors are exact-match only. Any element with multiple classes won't match them (e.g. `class="mobile hide"`). I can't confirm whether this occurs in the truncated body.
- No other rendering defects are confirmed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Internal comment leaked in the sent HTML:** The first node is an HTML comment that wasn't stripped before send. It reads `<!--REQUIRED variables: @email … Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", "EMAIL_ID", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email)) … Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "uEm", …) … CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW --> <!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->`.
  - It exposes data-extension names (`NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`) and field names (`uEm`, `cmdid`, `EMAIL_ID`).
  - It also shows that a hashed-email (SHA-256) identifier is used for tracking.
  - It is dead template scaffolding ("CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW") that was never resolved.
- Unrendered merge tokens in the visible portion: none found.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe not found (WARN):** The QA note says the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. This is unconfirmed, not a verified absence.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found (WARN):** The same caveat applies. One-click support can't be verified.
- **Authentication-Results not found (WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain is `e.neimanmarcus.com`. A check of the original message headers or a seed-list test would settle it.
- The physical address and visible unsubscribe link are in the truncated footer and were not verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- UTM and refid parameters could not be checked because the anchors were truncated and the 29 tracking links were skipped. The template references `@refid`/`@base_refid` from a Campaign Calendar, which suggests campaign IDs are appended. This is inference, not evidence.
- Landing page alignment: not assessable.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Strip the `REQUIRED variables … CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO` comment block in the ESP's pre-send step so it isn't delivered to subscribers.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Re-run the QA against raw `.eml` headers (not the relay copy) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
4. Resolve one sample redirect from the 29 tracking links with a headless GET, and confirm the final URL, status and UTM/refid retention.
5. Re-audit with the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer (address and unsubscribe), alt text, and class-based selector coverage.
