# You get exclusive access to 40% off styles from Manolo Blahnik, Etro & more
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth Designer Sale send I've seen at Neiman's since 11/19, and the first that makes me feel like I was picked. "Exclusive access" and a promo code are a decent nudge. The black box with red "40% OFF" is the same creative I've now seen three times, and my eye lands there and stays there.
- The nine-tile grid underneath is the strongest part. Manolo Blahnik, Khaite, Thom Browne and Ferragamo are names I'd shop. Each tile is labeled by designer, so I know what I'm getting. It's a cleaner "designer access" read than Macy's, which would have buried this in a coupon pile. But the fine print is where it gets me. "Exclusive" is a stretch when it's online only, code-gated and "exclusions apply". I'd click, mostly for the shoes, and I'd skip the forward. After Kohl's "Ends today" on repeat, I'm tired of deadlines, though this one is at least a real date.

## What's working

- The nine-designer grid with labels under each tile. I can see Manolo and Ferragamo without hunting.
- "40% OFF" in red on black is the first thing I read. One number, no stacking, unlike the 11/20 "60% off + gift card + 30%" send.
- The code NMSALE is printed right in the hero, so I don't have to dig for it.
- The category buttons (Women's Shoes, Handbags) let me go straight to what I buy.
- The "personal style advisor" and app links feel like a real department-store counter.

## What's weak

- "Exclusive access" is thin. Nothing here says tier, member or beauty. As a points-back person, I see no loyalty angle at all.
- The deadline, "Ends 11/22/26 at 10:00 PM CT", is only in the footer. The hero just says "Limited time."
- The fine print is small and gray, and the "excludes / interim markdowns" language reads defensive.
- No prices or "was/now" on any tile, so I can't tell what 40% off actually means on a Ferragamo bag.
- There's no beauty at all, even though the 11/20 send mentioned beauty. I'd wanted that after Sephora's gold-glitter parade, at least as a contrast.
- "Shop Early" is a vague CTA that doesn't say what I'm shopping.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the end date and time in the hero ("Ends Sunday 10 PM CT") and drop "Limited time."
- 2. Show a sale price or "from $X" under each designer so the 40% is concrete.
- 3. Add a tier or member line, like "Early for Neiman Marcus InCircle." If it's truly exclusive, say who it's for.
- 4. Make the CTA "Shop the Designer Sale" and link each tile to that designer's sale page.
- 5. Move the code and exclusions into one readable line near the button.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your early 40% off: Manolo, Etro, Khaite`
- **Subject Alt B:** `40% off designer, ends Sunday night`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code NMSALE, online only. Shoes, dresses and bags from Ferragamo, Thom Browne and more.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop before the public sale. Ends 11/22 at 10 PM CT.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The truncated HTML shows a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with a hamburger-nav CSS hack. It has two confirmed code-level issues: an SFMC variable comment left in the delivered markup, and a viewport that blocks zoom. The four QA warnings are mostly relay or capture limitations, not confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** The 29 tracking and click-redirect links were skipped by the QA probe, so their destinations are unverified.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** The QA check found 5488 of 6672 characters (about 82%) are URLs, which is above the 70% threshold. This points to long tracked URLs in the text/plain alternative. It's a minor spam-filter risk, not a functional break.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on supporting clients (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **No document language.** The opening tag is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` with no `lang` attribute, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Exact-match class selectors.** The mobile rules use `*[class=contenttable]`, `*[class=mobile]` and similar. These only match when `class` is exactly that value, so any element with multiple classes (e.g. `class="mobile hide"`) will miss the rule. The truncated source doesn't show whether such elements exist.
- **Hamburger nav depends on `:checked`.** The `.navbar-checkbox:checked~.menu` pattern only works in clients that support the checkbox hack, mainly Apple Mail and iOS. Gmail and Outlook strip it, so confirm the nav has a non-interactive fallback.
- **Minor:** `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` has a trailing space, and `.ReadMsgBody` is a legacy Outlook.com rule.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved tokens in the visible HTML.** I found no `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` placeholders.
- **SFMC internals are exposed.** The delivered HTML starts with a comment containing the AMPscript variable specs: `@email`, `@base_refid`, `@refid`, `@emailid`, `@uEm`, `@cmdid`, and lookups against `NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated` and `NM_CRM_Feed`. These are not rendered, but they leak data-extension names, field names and the CRM join key to anyone who views the source. They also show the template was shipped with its authoring notes, including the "CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW" instruction and the commented-out `SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256`.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool itself says these may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. This is unconfirmed. Verify against the raw message at the sender's MTA before treating it as a defect.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same relay reason. No conclusion can be drawn.
- **Footer not assessed.** The physical address and in-body unsubscribe link are not in the truncated source, so they couldn't be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot assess. The truncated HTML shows no `href` values, and the 29 tracking links were not resolved. The template does reference a `@refid` / `@base_refid` campaign identifier, but I couldn't confirm that it reaches the landing-page URLs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Strip the SFMC variable-spec comment from production sends.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1`, and add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
3. Switch the mobile selectors to `*[class~="…"]` or `[class*="…"]`, and confirm the nav degrades without checkbox support.
4. Check the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` outside the relay.
5. Resolve a sample of the 29 tracked links to verify destinations and UTM/refid carry-through.
6. Shorten the plain-text URLs, or reduce how many links the text part carries.
