## What's weak

- This is the same red tile grid I flagged on 9/21, 9/22, 9/23, and 9/24 — different products, identical layout, and at this point I can't tell one Kohl's email from another in my inbox.
- The subject promises "in your cart" but the email shows generic category deals (jeans, towels, luggage, small kitchen appliances) with zero cart-specific merchandising — that's a bait-and-switch on the premise.

- 2. **Cut the tile count in half.** Pick two categories max and go deep, the way Sephora does with its single-category Daily Deals sends.
- 3. **Lead with Kohl's Cash urgency**, since that's the one genuinely sticky mechanic — move it above the fold instead of burying it as a green strip under the hero.
- 4. **Segment out beauty-forward customers** like me into a Sephora-at-Kohl's-led version instead of tacking it on as one tile among twenty.
