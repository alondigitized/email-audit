# Because you're family: Enjoy 25% off select styles & 20% off select jewelry
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-22**
## Executive summary

- Well, here's Friends & Family again — same forest green hero, same "LIMITED TIME" stacked type over "FRIENDS & FAMILY" in that oversized serif, same cream "SHOP THE EVENT" button I saw in the "friendly reminder" send from the 20th. At this point I could draw this hero from memory. The one genuinely useful addition is the black bar underneath flagging Double Points for InCircle members — that's the kind of loyalty signal that actually gets my attention, and I wish it were higher up instead of buried under the fold.
- The product grid is nicely curated for me — MOTHER denim, a Mac Duggal gown, that Alice + Olivia leather jacket, a Balmain bag, Stuart Weitzman boots — this is genuinely my closet. But fine jewelry, which is literally in the subject line ("20% off select jewelry"), doesn't get a single product tile. That's a miss.
- Would I click? Maybe, on the Balmain bag tile specifically, since it's linked and desirable. Would I forward it? No — this is the third near-identical Friends & Family variant this cycle, and I already flagged the "friendly reminder" version on the 20th. Nothing new enough to share.

## What's working

- The nine-product grid is genuinely on-brand for me — denim, a statement gown, a leather moto jacket, a structured Balmain bag, tall boots — no juniors' stuff, no demographic whiplash.
- InCircle Double Points callout in the black bar is a real loyalty-member perk, and I do care about that.
- Clean single-column layout, generous white space, legible serif — this still reads like Neiman's, not a discount blast.
- "Exclusions Apply" is stated plainly right under the CTA instead of hidden in six-point gray fine print — I respect that.

## What's weak

- Subject promises "20% off select jewelry" but the visual grid has zero jewelry — no rings, no necklaces, no earrings. If it's the offer, show me the product.
- This is the same green hero / same stacked "LIMITED TIME" treatment as the September 20th "friendly reminder" send — I'm getting visual déjà vu, not a fresh campaign beat.
- Six stacked "SHOP [category]" buttons at the bottom (Women's, Women's Clothing, Women's Shoes, Handbags, Men's, Home) is Macy's-style overload dressed up in a nicer font — Neiman's is usually better than a wall of buttons.
- Double Points detail is stranded in a black bar with no visual weight — a member perk like that should be near the hero, not competing with "Exclusions Apply."

## Recommendations

- 1. Put at least one jewelry item in the grid, or drop "20% off jewelry" from the subject if you're not going to visually back it up — right now it's bait-and-switch on the one detail I clicked in for.
- 2. Vary the hero creative from the last Friends & Family send — new color, new model shot, something that doesn't feel like the same email recolored.
- 3. Cut the six-button category wall at the bottom to two or three — I don't need a "Shop Home" button in a Friends & Family fashion email.
- 4. Move the InCircle Double Points line up near the hero CTA, in green-on-cream or a badge, so members like me see it before we've scrolled past three product rows.
