# Yes, please! Grab up to 50% off.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-18**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so a "SURPRISE! 50% OFF" banner in hot pink and purple gradient right at the top — that's a lot of visual noise before I even get to a product. It says "Select Beauty" and "An extra day, just for you," which is a nice touch, but I've now seen Sephora run some version of this "50% off select" mechanic three sends in a row (the 15th, the 16th, and now this one) and honestly it's starting to blur together. Then it pivots into "In the Mix" with a new Chris McMillan Detox Shampoo launch, followed by an absolute wall of eight different "new" product tiles — mascara, hair gel, perfume, lip balm, holiday sets, setting spray, toner pads, concealor. That's a lot to scroll through on my phone before I hit anything that feels like it's actually for me.
- What lands for me: the "Clean at Sephora" badge on the McMillan shampoo, the Beauty Insider tier bar down at the bottom, and the "4X Points" fragrance callout — that's the kind of loyalty-program clarity I actually care about as a Rouge-tier type of shopper. But it's buried after a scroll-and-a-half of new-launch clutter that reads more like a catalog than a curated pick for me.
- Would I click? Maybe the 50% off banner, since that's concrete. Would I forward it to a friend? No — this is Sephora's every-week send, not something special enough to share. I'd open it, skim past the wall of new arrivals, and probably tap the top banner or just archive it.

## What's working

- The 50% off hero banner up top is unmissable — good use of color contrast, no scrolling required to see the offer.
- The "Clean at Sephora" seal on the McMillan shampoo is a trust cue I actually look for — Sephora's better than Ulta at surfacing clean-beauty certification inline like that.
- The Beauty Insider tier strip (Insider/VIB/Rouge) at the footer respects that I'm a member, even if it's small.
- "4X Points on Fragrance" ties a real loyalty mechanic to a real category — that's sharper than Kohl's vague "20% off + open a card" push I logged this week.

## What's weak

- Eight-plus "new" product tiles in a grid is Macy's-style overload — I don't need a full product catalog, I need Sephora to tell me what's relevant to me.
- The 50% off banner never says which categories or brands are actually included — "Select Beauty" is vague, and I have to hunt for exclusions in tiny fine print at the bottom.
- No personalization anywhere — no "Ines, here's what's back in stock" or points balance shown, just a generic broadcast.
- This is basically the same 50%-off-plus-new-launches format as Sephora's own September 15th and 16th sends — the cadence is getting repetitive, not escalating in a way that feels special.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a specific example of what's actually 50% off (a named brand or category) instead of "Select Beauty" — Neiman Marcus's "$50 gift card for every $200 spent" email spelled out the exact tiers, and that specificity is what made it clickable.
- 2. Cut the new-arrivals grid from eight tiles to three, or split it into a separate "new for you" send — the volume kills the hierarchy.
- 3. Surface my actual points balance or tier progress near the top, the way the loyalty section is treated at the bottom — don't make me scroll past a shampoo ad to find out I'm a Rouge member.
- 4. Make the fine print on exclusions visible near the offer, not buried at the footer in gray-on-white.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your extra day: 50% off before it's gone`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus: new Chris McMillan shampoo, and 4X points on fragrance`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Rouge members, your points are waiting — see what qualifies`
