# This autumn, we're falling head over heels for *you*
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-06**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the same Sephora Stacking Weekend I already opened twice this week — 9/4 and 9/5 — but now they've bolted a whole "Autumn Edit" gift-guide onto it, so it's genuinely long. That black-and-white striped shopping bag hero with the red bow is pretty, I'll give them that, it actually looks like a department-store window instead of a coupon flyer. But then it just keeps unspooling: free birthday gift, K-beauty drop, 500 bonus points, credit card pitch, ANOTHER 50% off banner near the bottom that looks bolted on from a completely different email.
- The birthday gift-set module is the one thing that made me pause, because Beauty Insider actually knows I have a birthday on file and that's a real perk, not a generic blast. Everything else is stuff I've already seen from Sephora this week, just resequenced. Compare this to Macy's "confirm now for extra 25%" I graded last time — at least that one was short and knew what it wanted from me. This one wants me to shop four different things at once and doesn't tell me which matters most.
- Verdict: I'd scroll past the top two sections, maybe screenshot the birthday gift module for myself, and mark it read. Not forwarding it — there's nothing here a friend needs urgently, and honestly the redundant 50% banner near the footer makes it look like Sephora's own automation glitched and stapled two campaigns together.

## What's working

- The striped-bag "Autumn Edit" hero photo is genuinely pretty and on-brand — feels like a Nordstrom or Saks seasonal display, not a clearance flyer.
- The birthday free gift set is real personalization — Beauty Insider knows my birthday, that's a program benefit I actually value, unlike Kohl's blasting the same $5-off-$25 to everyone.
- 500 Bonus Points with a clear "Ends 9/10" and a visible use code button — I know exactly what to type and when.
- Product photography in the K-Beauty and gift-set modules is clean and easy to scan.

## What's weak

- This is the third near-identical "50% off Select Beauty / Stacking Weekend" red starburst hero I've gotten from Sephora since 9/4 — I'm fatigued on it already.
- The email doesn't stop — hero, birthday gift, K-beauty, bonus points, credit card, ANOTHER 50%-off banner near the footer. Five different asks with no clear "start here."
- That second 50% off banner near the bottom, with the perfume bottle and "Today Only," looks like a totally separate campaign got pasted in — no visual continuity with the top of the email at all.
- Credit card pitch ("$20 back for every $250 spent") buried mid-scroll where I'd never see it if I bailed after the first fold, and it's a weirdly high spend threshold to lead with.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this down to one offer per send. Lead with the birthday gift (it's the only thing that's actually about me) and push the stacking weekend/bonus points/credit card pitch into separate, shorter emails on different days — I'm getting three of these a week already and it's starting to blur together like Kohl's did.
- 2. Delete or completely re-skin that bottom 50% banner so it matches the top of the email — right now it reads like a layout bug, not a deliberate second CTA.
- 3. Move the "500 Bonus Points, ends 9/10" urgency up near the top — that's a real deadline and it's currently buried under three other modules.
- 4. Give the credit card module its own send with a lower, more attainable entry point than $250.
