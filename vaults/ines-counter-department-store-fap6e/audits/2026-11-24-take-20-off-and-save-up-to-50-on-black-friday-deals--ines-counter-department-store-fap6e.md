# Take 20% off AND save up to 50% on Black Friday Deals 🎉
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again. I've lost count, but the 11/22, 11/23 and two 11/24 sends already hit me with the same "20% off plus up to 50% off" line, and this is another one. The screenshot is shrunk to a sliver, so I can't read the fine print. What I can see is a very long scroll of about twenty tiles: throws, slippers, sweaters, boots, Sephora at Kohl's, LEGO, KitchenAid, Garmin. It has a red "$15 Kohl's Cash" banner up top, a row of category pills, and a "Shop Now" button under almost every tile.
- I would not click. It reads as a flyer, not as a note to me. I buy beauty at the counter, and the one beauty tile, Sephora at Kohl's, is buried mid-scroll. I wouldn't forward it. I'd skim it and mark it read.

## What's working

- The "$15 Kohl's Cash" banner and "Free shipping with a $75 purchase" sit near the top, so the loyalty hook is visible early.
- The category pills (Women, Men, Kids, Home, Toys, Beauty) let me jump straight to Beauty without scrolling.
- The Sephora at Kohl's tile with fragrance gets its own slot. That's the one tile I'd stop on.
- Each tile pairs a price with a "Reg." strike-through, which makes the discount easy to see.
- The closing "Shop Now" band repeats the 50% off message, so it's clear where to go.

## What's weak

- It's about twenty tiles with a different percentage on each, and "Coupons do not apply" is on several. That's the Macy's-style overload I avoid.
- The headline is "Up to 50%" plus "+20% w/coupon," and the exclusions are in tiny print. It doesn't feel like an honest offer.
- It's the same creative as the last four days. Neiman Marcus at least sent a quiet CHANEL Cruise email after its black-box week. Kohl's never lets up.
- Nothing speaks to a 47-year-old beauty shopper. The tiles are slippers, kids' sweaters and toys, and Beauty is one pill out of many.
- The fine print and the "Reg./Sale" lines are too small to read on a phone without zooming.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut it to one hero and about six tiles, with the Kohl's Cash offer and the coupon code stated once, clearly.
- 2. Segment by category. Someone who has clicked on beauty should get a Sephora-at-Kohl's lead, not throws and slippers.
- 3. Slow the cadence. Sephora's Cyber Week run was loud too, but at least each send felt like a new beat.
- 4. Put the exclusions in plain words near the offer, not in the footer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 20% off code + $15 Kohl's Cash, ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Sephora at Kohl's: Black Friday beauty picks`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack your coupon with Kohl's Cash. Here's what's excluded.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fragrance, skincare and gifts, all in one place.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Kohl's sends a table-based, Salesforce Marketing Cloud (`click.s.kohls.com`) email with Movable Ink image personalization. It has several markup defects, 9+ content images without alt text, and four stacked tracking pixels. The compliance warnings come from relay-capture limits and a truncated source, so they aren't confirmed defects. The QA run scored 38% with 0 hard failures and 5 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **91 tracked links were not probed.** Every destination is an opaque redirect, e.g. `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7ImQiOjUwNzAs…`. I couldn't verify final destinations, redirect chains or dead links.
- **Four separate open/tracking pixels fire before any content:**
  - `https://click.chp.kohls.com/o/9cb37773-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
  - `https://mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1026609_20261124`
  - `https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=60275&bmt=0`
  - `https://kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager)
- Three tracking domains and a third-party DMP are involved. The redundancy inflates open counts and adds request overhead.
- **Malformed markup on the SFMC open pixel:** `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?…" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`. The stray `</custom>` closing tag has no matching opener.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images, flagged by QA:**
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`
  - `…/f441d45d2128ae53.png`
  - `…/c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`
  - `…/415c8a18fc2950ea.png`
  - `…/69494b48a79b4240.png`
  - `…/78308d1d9adb0751.png`
  - `…/916d6fa08d185233.png`
  - Product images `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/60479404_1_2x`, `60699446_4_2x` and `60699446_3_2x`
  
  The Movable Ink `rp` images look like content such as banners and offers, so screen readers and image-blocked clients get nothing. The 1×1 pixels also lack alt, except `o.gif`, which has `alt=""` and `aria-hidden`. The QA flag on `o.gif` is therefore a false positive.
- **Viewport meta blocks zoom:** `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` prevents pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility failure (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Empty favicon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`.
- **`<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`.** That is invalid, and parsers will implicitly close the head and move it into the body.
- **CSS defects:**
  - `ReadMsgBody { width: 100% }` is missing its leading `.`, so it matches nothing.
  - `max-height: auto !important` is not a valid value.
- **Web fonts load via `@import` and `<link>` to Google Fonts.** They are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, which is acceptable, but Gmail and some other clients ignore them. The fallback stack is `Montserrat, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. A subscriber identifier (`mi_u=604230016`) is rendered into image URLs. The source is truncated, so I can't check the rest of the body.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) are not found.** QA itself notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is not a confirmed sender defect.
- **Authentication-Results is not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, again because of the relay. The sending domain is `s.kohls.com`, and alignment couldn't be tested.
- **No physical address was detected.** The HTML provided is truncated before the footer, where it would normally appear, so this is unverified.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs:** 16,167 of 21,945 characters (about 74%) are tracking URLs. That exceeds the 70% threshold and can raise spam-filter scores.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters are visible.** Tracking uses opaque SFMC `qs=` tokens, so campaign attribution can't be confirmed from the source.
- Movable Ink image URLs carry `mi_ecmp=1026609_20261124` and `email_name=261124_DG_Email_Tue_PM`. The date matches the send date of 2026-11-24, so the campaign metadata is consistent.
- I couldn't verify landing-page alignment because all 91 links were skipped.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to the seven `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` images and the three `kohlsprod/*` product images, including any offer text rendered as an image. Use `alt=""` for decorative images and pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
3. Fix the markup: delete the stray `</custom>`, move the `itemscope` div out of `<head>`, populate or remove `<title>` and the empty favicon `<link>`, and correct `.ReadMsgBody` and `max-height`.
4. Consolidate the four open pixels. At minimum, review whether `click.chp.kohls.com` and `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` are both needed.
5. Shorten the plain-text part, for example with shorter redirect URLs or text-only links, to get under the 70% URL ratio.
6. Re-run QA against the raw `.eml` to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) and the footer's physical address. These are currently unverified rather than failed.
7. Run a link-resolution probe that follows the `click.s.kohls.com` redirects, then check final URLs for UTM or other attribution parameters and landing-page status codes.
