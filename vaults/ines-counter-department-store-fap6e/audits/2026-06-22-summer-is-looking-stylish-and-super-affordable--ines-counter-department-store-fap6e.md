# Summer is looking stylish (and super affordable) 😎
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-22**
## Executive summary

- June 22, and this is my 25th Kohl's email this month — I counted. Yesterday they sent two in one day, one of them a 40%-off-ENDS-TONIGHT panic, and today they show up with a pleasant "summer is looking stylish" lifestyle send at 20% off. The offer is moving in the wrong direction and they know it, which is probably why the copy leans into vibes rather than the discount.
- That said, the hero line — "When your outfit is almost more fun than your plans" — is genuinely good. It's the kind of casual, confident copy that makes me actually pause. It doesn't shout; it observes. And for a brief moment this felt like a Macy's-caliber editorial send, not a Kohl's blast. Then the Ninja kitchen appliance block appeared and the spell broke completely.
- I would mark this as read. I would note the Deal Days teaser at the bottom (starts tomorrow — that I actually want to know about) and I'd move on. No click, no forward.

## What's working

- "When your outfit is almost more fun than your plans" is charming, low-key, and earns a half-second of genuine attention
- 20% off is visible immediately — no hunting, no scroll required
- The $19.99 starting-at price with a strikethrough is clean and communicates real value at a glance
- "Kohl's Deal Days – Starts Tomorrow" at the bottom is the most useful information in the email; teasing an upcoming sale event is smart editorial
- Pride Month section looks sincere and visually integrated, not bolted on as an afterthought

## What's weak

- "Super affordable" in the subject is mass-market positioning that lands flat when you also cross-shop Saks — I buy at Kohl's, but I don't need the word spelled out
- Ninja kitchen appliances mid-scroll is a complete editorial break; this should be a separate, triggered send for home/kitchen browsers, not inside a summer style send
- "Your family should look the part" frames this as a family shop — I'm not necessarily here for Men and Kids sections
- 20% the day after a 40%-off urgency blitz feels like a demotion; the cadence makes the offer hierarchy make no sense
- Four "Shop Now" buttons that say identical things give me no decision signal at all

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with Deal Days** — if a major sale event starts tomorrow, that is the email. Make the 20% off the warm-up act for what's coming, not the headline of a standalone send.
- 2. **Cut the Ninja block** — or make it a separate triggered send. It breaks the edit entirely for anyone who opened because of "stylish."
- 3. **Differentiate the CTAs** — "Shop Women's Summer" and "Shop New Arrivals" tell me where I'm going; four "Shop Now" buttons tell me nothing.
- 4. **Re-anchor the 20% offer** — "new summer arrivals" or "just dropped" frames a lower percentage in a way that doesn't feel like a step backward from yesterday's 40%.
- 5. **Lose "super affordable" from the subject** — "stylish" does the work; "super affordable" undercuts the tone the hero copy earns back.

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "Summer is looking stylish" (2026-06-22)

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is structurally sound for a bulk commercial send but carries several confirmed accessibility violations, a malformed HTML tag, and three compliance gaps that the QA relay could not verify due to header capture limitations. No unrendered merge tokens detected.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Malformed closing tag on open-pixel div**
```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>
```
The `</custom>` tag is invalid HTML. It will be silently ignored by most parsers but can cause downstream AST parse failures in any email rendering or analytics pipeline that re-parses the source.

**Three separate open-tracking pixels fired on load:**
- `click.chp.kohls.com/o/6b165fdb-...` (CoherentPath)
- `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbb...o.gif` (Messagegears)
- `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...` (Kohl's ESP)
- `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager / demdex)

Four pixels for one open event is redundant and increases load time; demdex may be blocked by aggressive privacy filters (Brave, iOS Mail Privacy Protection) differently than the other three.

**22 click links unverifiable:** All destination URLs pass through redirect domains (`click.chp.kohls.com`, `click.s.kohls.com`) — expected, but means broken-link detection cannot confirm landing page availability.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — disables pinch-to-zoom. Violates WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text, Level AA). Many enterprise email clients ignore this, but it is still a compliance signal in accessibility-regulated industries.
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />
```

**Empty `<title>` tag** — `<title></title>` renders as no subject in some browser-based webmail preview tabs and is a minor deliverability signal for spam filters that parse document metadata.

**Empty favicon `href`** — `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` generates a 404 request to the current host in some webmail clients that honor this tag.

**Duplicate Google Fonts loading** — Fonts are loaded via both `<link>` elements and `@import` inside a `<style>` block for both Roboto and Montserrat. This fires two separate HTTP requests per font family, adding ~200–400ms render latency before fallback fonts display.

### 5. Compliance

**List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post headers not captured** (QA WARN) — the AgentMail relay did not surface these headers. This may be a relay artifact rather than a true absence, but cannot be confirmed. If absent from the actual SMTP delivery, this breaks one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) required by Gmail and Yahoo's 2024 bulk sender rules for senders exceeding 5,000/day.

**Physical mailing address not detected in HTML** (QA WARN) — CAN-SPAM §7 requires a valid physical postal address. The HTML source is truncated; the address may appear in the footer below the cut. If absent from the full render, this is a legal violation.

**Authentication-Results header not captured** (QA WARN) — SPF and DKIM pass/fail status unknown via relay. Cannot confirm DMARC alignment. Kohl's sends from `s.kohls.com` (subdomain); DMARC alignment depends on whether `s.kohls.com` is covered by `kohls.com`'s DMARC policy.
