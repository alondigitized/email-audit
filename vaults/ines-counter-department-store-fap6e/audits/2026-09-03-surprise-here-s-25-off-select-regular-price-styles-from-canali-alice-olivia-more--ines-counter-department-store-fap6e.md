# Surprise: Here's 25% off select regular-price styles from Canali, ALICE + OLIVIA & more
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-03**
## Executive summary

- Well, look at that — a big black "S" doing double duty as the hero graphic and I actually like it. It's editorial, it's got a fashion-magazine feel, and it's not screaming at me the way that Neiman's mango banner has been for two solid weeks now (8/26 tiered saga, the 9/1 rerun, 9/2's "ends soon" — I've seen this show four times). Then I scroll down and there it is anyway: the same pale-yellow "up to $250 off" box, same SHOPFALL code, same fine print. So this is really two emails stapled together — one genuinely nice one, and one they couldn't resist bolting on.
- The product grid is where it gets interesting for someone like me — Canali, ALICE + OLIVIA-adjacent labels, L'AGENCE denim, a Theia gown. That's a real mix of my husband's closet and mine, which Neiman's does better than most because they're not afraid to show menswear and womenswear side by side without treating it as an afterthought. Sephora's grids feel like a wall of product; this feels curated.
- Would I click? Probably, yes — the 25% off regular-price styles is a real, houseable discount and the grid gave me two dresses I'd actually look twice at. Forward to a friend? Maybe the "S is for Sale" banner alone, not the whole email — the $250 rerun at the bottom is the part I'd edit out before sending it on.

## What's working

- The oversized "S" hero is genuinely elegant — it doesn't look like every other retailer's banner-of-the-week, and it reads well without me squinting.
- 25% off is stated plainly with a real "SHOP NOW" button — no hunting for the offer.
- The nine-item grid crosses men's and women's cleanly (Canali next to a Theia gown), which matches how I actually shop — for myself and picking up things for my husband.
- "Match with a personal style advisor" is a nice, quiet nod to the in-store relationship — that's the kind of touch Saks leans into and Macy's never bothers with.

## What's weak

- The $250-off SHOPFALL block at the bottom is the exact same offer/color treatment I've now seen four times this week from this brand — it undercuts the fresh feeling the "S" hero just built.
- Two offers, two codes, two sets of exclusions in one email is a lot of math for a Tuesday — I have to decide which deal applies to which item, and nothing here tells me.
- No loyalty/InCircle point signal anywhere — I'm a rewards-card shopper and there's zero mention of points or tier status on either offer.
- Product names but no prices in the grid — I have to click through nine times just to know if any of this is actually in my budget.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the duplicate $250 block entirely, or clearly separate it as "a second, unrelated markdown event" — right now it reads like they forgot they already sent this offer three times this week.
- 2. Add prices (or "from $X") under each grid item so I'm not click-guessing on nine products.
- 3. Surface InCircle points or tier status somewhere — even a one-line "earn double points" would beat Macy's and Kohl's on loyalty relevance instantly.
- 4. Lead the subject with the hero visual's confidence rather than "surprise" — it undersells a genuinely nice creative.
