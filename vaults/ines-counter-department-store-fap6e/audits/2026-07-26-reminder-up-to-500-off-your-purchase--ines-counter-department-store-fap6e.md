# Reminder: Up to $500 off your purchase
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-26**
## Executive summary

- Oh, we've done this dance before — this is the exact same tiered spend-and-save mechanic Neiman Marcus ran on 7/25 ("Up to $500 off your purchase starts now," which I gave an 8), just re-flagged as a "Reminder" three days later with an actual countdown to 7/27 at 10pm CT baked into the fine print. The tiered table is still the cleanest thing they do — spend $200, save $50, all the way up to $2,000+ for $500 off — and I still respect that it's laid out like a real chart instead of buried in paragraph text. But "reminder" emails only work if I remember getting the first one, and buried in a sea of Last Call and 70%-off blasts, I genuinely don't.
- The product grid is fine — Mac Duggal, Alice + Olivia, Jimmy Choo, Rag & Bone, that STAUD plaid dress is actually cute for fall transition — but nothing here says "your size," "your history," or "you're a Circle member." It's the same generic nine-square merchandising wall NM always runs, just with different names rotated in.
- Would I click? Maybe, mildly, out of habit, since $500 off is real money and I already have the code memorized from three days ago. Would I forward it to a friend? No — there's nothing here worth sharing, it's just a reminder to use a code I either used already or ignored the first time.

## What's working

- The spend/save table is legible at a glance — no math required, which beats how Macy's buries tiered logic in confirmation-email urgency language.
- A real deadline with a real time — "Ends 7/27/26 at 10:00 PM CT" — beats Kohl's vague "LAST DAY" framing I flagged last week for not committing to a clock time.
- STAUD plaid dress and the Rag & Bone denim are on-trend for someone who actually shops my closet, not just "luxury handbag" stereotypes.
- Clean nav, no clutter, no emoji parade — feels like NM, not like Sephora's exclamation-mark sunscreen carnival.

## What's weak

- Zero personalization — no "Ines," no Circle status, no "items you viewed," nothing that says they know who I am.
- "Reminder" implies I've seen this before, but there's no callback to the original send or acknowledgment I might have already redeemed it — just a cold repeat of the same hero.
- The nine-product grid is undifferentiated — no "your size in stock," no price shown, no indication these are actually on sale versus regular price (the offer is code-based, not markdown, so I have to do math at checkout).
- "Exclusions Apply" in tiny gray text under the offer is the kind of soft fine-print dodge that makes me trust the number less — Sephora's save-the-date email at least didn't pretend an offer was cleaner than it is.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make it an actual reminder — reference the original send or add "your code expires in X hours" urgency copy instead of just repeating the hero unchanged.
- 2. Personalize the grid — swap in "recommended for you" or at minimum show my Circle tier and points balance since I'm a loyalty-driven shopper who won't engage without seeing status.
- 3. Show prices or at least "as low as" pricing on the nine products so I can eyeball whether $500 off is even reachable before I click through.
- 4. Move "Exclusions Apply" into a slightly larger, honest line near the code, not a whisper-gray afterthought.
