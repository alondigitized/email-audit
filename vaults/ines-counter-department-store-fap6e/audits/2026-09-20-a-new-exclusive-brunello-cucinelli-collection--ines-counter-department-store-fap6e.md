## What's weak

- Zero product detail — no price, no "new arrivals" count, not even a hint at silhouette (coat? blazer? dress?) beyond what I can guess from the photo.
- No loyalty/InCircle callout anywhere, and I know I'm a member — this would've been the spot to remind me I get points on a Cucinelli purchase, especially at this price point.

- Nothing timely or seasonal-specific in the copy itself — "Urban Harmony" could run in March or September, no fall signal beyond his jacket.

- 2. Work in an InCircle line — "Earn points on every Cucinelli piece" would turn a mood board into a reason to click for someone like me who won't touch a card without rewards.
- 3. Add one more product-forward image below the hero — a close-up of a bag or shoe detail, the way the CHANEL send used two tight shots instead of relying on one wide scene.
