# Up to 40% off: Shop new markdowns on fall styles
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-16**
## Executive summary

- Fourth Neiman's in three days, and this is the same tomato-red "First Call" block I got on 10/14, just reposted. I got the offer in two seconds: up to 40% off, new markdowns, fall. It's clean. But the first "First Call" email at least felt like news. This one is the same news with a new grid under it.
- I do like the product grid. Alice + Olivia, A.L.C., AG, Theory and Veronica Beard is the designer closet I actually shop. But no prices, no "was/now," and "Exclusions Apply" under the button tells me the 40% is the best case. If I'm going to drive to the counter, I want to know what I'm saving.
- Would I click? Maybe, on the Theory cardigan, but only after I'd checked the fine print. Forward it to a friend? No. Mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- The red-and-black "First Call" block is one offer, one button, and easy to read. Nothing competes with it.
- The designer names under each image (Theory, Veronica Beard, Ksubi) are what I scan for. It reads as a curated edit, not a clearance bin.
- Nine products in a 3x3 grid is tidy. The images are crisp and the type is readable without zooming.
- The category buttons at the bottom (women's shoes, handbags, accessories) get me where I'm going quickly. They're wide enough to tap with a thumb.
- The "Match with a personal style advisor" line feels like Neiman's, not a discount chain.

## What's weak

- No prices anywhere. Every tile is a name and a picture, so I can't tell what's actually marked down.
- "Exclusions Apply" is tiny, low-contrast text under the button. The footnote then says "25-40%" and "select categories." That is a gap between the headline and the fine print.
- Nothing about InCircle, points or member pricing. I won't put a card down without points back, and this doesn't mention them at all.
- The hero takes up almost the whole first screen on a phone, so the product grid sits well below the fold.
- The footer is crammed: the unsubscribe and "View in a Web Browser" links are small and bunched together.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put prices on the grid tiles ("$198 → $139") and tell me which items are new markdowns. That would do more than anything else here.
- 2. Add one InCircle line under the hero, like "Members earn points on markdowns," to give me a reason to buy now.
- 3. Move "Exclusions Apply" into a readable size and say what's excluded in a line.
- 4. Vary the hero between sends. Sephora's 10/12 magazine-style send and its makeup sets send at least looked different from each other. Kohl's has run the same 20% header since 10/8, and this is starting to feel like that.
- 5. Space these out. Three gift-card sends and then two "First Call" sends in three days is too much.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible source has no confirmed hard failures (0 QA issues). Three things stand out: internal template and variable comments shipped in the delivered HTML, a viewport tag that blocks zoom, and a plain-text part that is mostly tracking URLs. The compliance and authentication warnings are most likely relay artifacts, and the truncated HTML and skipped link probes leave several categories unverifiable.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified destinations:** QA skipped the HTTP probe on 30 links because they are tracking/click-redirect domains. Destination status, redirect chains and 404s are untested.
- **Plain-text bloat:** 5,675 of 6,647 plain-text characters (about 85%) are URLs. Raw tracking URLs are being printed in the text part, which hurts text-only readability and can look spammy to filters.
- No broken or malformed links are confirmed in the visible source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom, which conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Non-descriptive title:** `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` has a trailing space and doesn't match the subject line. This is minor.
- **Checkbox-hack mobile nav:** the CSS uses `.navbar-checkbox:checked~.menu` and `.hamburger`/`.close` toggles. This pattern is not supported in Gmail and several other clients. Whether a non-interactive fallback exists can't be confirmed because the HTML is truncated.
- **Fragile selectors:** exact-match attribute selectors such as `*[class=contenttable]` and `*[class=hide]` stop matching if an element gets a second class. Responsive behavior depends on single-class markup.
- Image alt text, link text and table roles can't be assessed because the body is truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Authoring comment shipped:** the delivered HTML begins with `<!--REQUIRED variables: @email ... @base_refid ... @refid = CONCAT(@base_refid,"") @user Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", ...) @uEm = Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "uEm", ...) @cmdid ... CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW -->` and `<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->`.
  - It exposes internal data extension names (`NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`), the lookup keys, and the fact that `@user` is a hashed email.
  - It is also unbalanced (`... @email))`). The comment is harmless to rendering but is an information leak and a template-hygiene failure.
- No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%...%%` or `{{...}}` are visible in the excerpt. Whether the `@user`, `@uEm` and `@cmdid` values resolved inside link URLs can't be confirmed.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** not found. The QA tool itself notes the relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed rather than a defect. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so verify it on the original message.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The tool attributes this to the relay. The From domain (`e.neimanmarcus.com`) is a dedicated subdomain, which is good, but alignment isn't verified.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** the footer is outside the truncated excerpt, so CAN-SPAM body requirements can't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM and landing alignment unverified:** no UTM parameters are visible in the excerpt, and all 30 probed links go through tracking redirects. The `@refid`/`@base_refid` variables suggest campaign attribution is carried in a `refid`-style parameter rather than UTMs, but the source doesn't confirm it.
- Landing-page alignment with the "Up to 40% off … fall styles" subject can't be assessed without resolving the redirects.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Strip authoring comments:** remove the `<!--REQUIRED variables ...-->` and `<!--/* SET @user ... */-->` blocks from the send-time template. They leak DE names and personalization internals.
2. **Allow zoom:** remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. **Check headers on the original message:** confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, SPF, DKIM and DMARC on the original message, since the relay copy can't show them. Treat the four warnings as unresolved until then.
4. **Trim the plain-text part:** shorten it by dropping raw tracking URLs from non-essential links, or use a cleaner text template, to bring URLs well under 70%.
5. **Follow a sample of redirects:** resolve 3–5 of the tracked links (hero CTA, nav, footer) to check final status codes, landing pages and attribution parameters.
6. **Test the mobile nav in Gmail:** confirm the checkbox-hack menu degrades gracefully outside Apple Mail and iOS.
7. **Re-run QA on the full HTML:** the truncated source blocked checks on the footer, alt text, link text and merge-token resolution.
