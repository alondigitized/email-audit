# New must-have styles added to our Private Event: 40% off
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- Same email, third time. This is the Private Event 40% off creative I got on 11/18, and the only change is the word "New" in the subject. I looked for the new must-have styles and found none. The screen has no product, no model and no coat. It's a headline and a black button. It's clean, but it doesn't match the subject line.
- I'm also tired. This is about the seventh Neiman's send in five days. I just got "Surprise Sale: 20% off select boots & booties" this morning, and the Dolce&Gabbana holiday email before it was the only one that felt like a counter associate. If you're adding "new" styles, show me one. I'd mark this read and move on. I won't forward it.
- Sephora Insider at least showed me the Dyson, even if it buried it in fragrance sets. Macy's makes me confirm something for 25% off, but that's at least a reason to click. Neiman's gives me nothing new today.

## What's working

- The red "40% OFF" is huge and I can't miss it. I know the offer in half a second.
- One black SHOP NOW button. There's no Macy's-style pile of competing buttons.
- The code NMACCESS sits right under the offer, so I don't have to hunt for it.
- The serif type looks expensive and feels like Neiman's, not a clearance rack.
- The end date, 11/26 at 10 PM CT, is in the fine print, so the deadline is real.

## What's weak

- The subject promises "new must-have styles" and the body shows zero product. That's a bait-and-switch feeling.
- This is a repeat of the 11/18 creative. Sending the same hero twice in two days isn't news.
- "Select designer styles" and "Exclusions apply" tell me nothing. Which designers? I won't click to find out.
- There's no loyalty or InCircle mention. I won't use a card that doesn't give me points, and I don't see any points here.
- The fine print says "online only" and "no adjustments for prior purchases." That should be near the offer, not at the bottom in small type.
- Below the hero, the app download and style advisor blocks feel like filler.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show product. Put three or four of the newly added pieces under the headline, with the designer name and the sale price.
- 2. Say what's new in the hero, such as "Just added: 200+ styles" with a few brand names.
- 3. Add a points line for members. Even "Earn points on every Private Event order" would help.
- 4. Spell out the key exclusions near the button, not only at the bottom.
- 5. Hold the cadence. Stop sending the same event three days running.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML source has a few hygiene problems: leftover SFMC developer comments and a zoom-blocking viewport. Every QA warning is about missing headers or skipped probes, so none is confirmed as a defect. The source was truncated, so footer, link and header checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **14 tracking links not probed.** The QA check skipped them because they are click-redirect domains. Redirect resolution, status codes and final destinations are unverified.
- **Plain-text part is 2638/3579 characters (about 74%) URLs.** This is above the 70% threshold. It is likely tracked URLs inflating the text alternative, and it can hurt spam scoring.
- No broken or malformed links were seen in the visible portion of the HTML.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Invalid font-family value.** `.disclaimerText { font-family: Arial, Helvetica, 'sans-serif'; }` quotes the generic keyword. It is then read as a literal family name, so the generic fallback never applies.
- **Fragile mobile selectors.** The media queries use exact-match attribute selectors such as `*[class=contenttable]` and `*[class=hide]`. They fail if an element ever carries more than one class.
- **CSS-only hamburger nav.** It relies on `.navbar-checkbox:checked~` with `display:none` defaults. It is unsupported in Gmail and other clients that strip `<input>` or `:checked`, so the nav will probably be inaccessible there. I could not confirm this from the truncated source.
- **Minor issues.** `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` has a trailing space and is non-descriptive. The XHTML DTD URL uses `https://www.w3.org/...`, which is harmless but nonstandard.
- Image alt text could not be checked because the source is truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Internal SFMC scaffolding ships in the sent HTML.** The leading comment block exposes the following:
  - Data extension names: `NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated` and `NM_CRM_Feed`.
  - Field names: `EMAIL_ID`, `EMAIL_ADDRESS`, `uEm` and `cmdid`.
  - A template instruction: `CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW`.
  - A commented-out line: `SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256`.

  This leaks internal data-model details to every recipient. The comment should be stripped at send time.
- **Dead code left in the head.** Three commented-out `<meta name="viewport">` variants are still present.
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) were seen in the visible portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool itself notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so I can't confirm a real defect. If the headers are absent at origin, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules (2024 onward) require one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is expected through the relay and is not a confirmed failure. The From domain `e.neimanmarcus.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is good practice.
- The footer, unsubscribe link and physical address are not in the truncated source, so I can't verify them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not verifiable.** The 14 tracking links were not probed and the source is truncated, so UTM parameters and the landing pages for the "Private Event: 40% off" claim are unconfirmed.
- The comment block references `@refid`/`@base_refid` from the Campaign Calendar. That suggests campaign tracking goes through a refid parameter rather than UTM, but this is unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Strip the SFMC variable and instruction comments and the commented-out viewport tags before sending. Most ESPs can do this in a post-processing step.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag so pinch-zoom works.
3. Change `'sans-serif'` to the unquoted `sans-serif` in `.disclaimerText`.
4. Switch the mobile media-query selectors to `[class~=...]` or plain class selectors.
5. Re-run the QA against raw SMTP headers, not the relay copy, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
6. Resolve the 14 tracking links with a redirect-following probe and check their final URLs for UTM or refid params and for 200 responses.
7. Check the plain-text alternative. Shorten or drop tracking URLs in the text part so the URL ratio falls below 70%.
8. Give the hamburger nav a non-checkbox fallback for Gmail and other clients that strip `:checked`, such as always-visible links.
