# Our top picks from the Fall Style Sale are going fast: up to 40% off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-25**
## Executive summary

- The subject promised up to 40% off, and the hero says up to 30%. I opened it expecting the bigger number, and the red "30% OFF" on blush shows I got the smaller one. Neiman's did the same thing on 10/23, when the "30%" email was the first one in a week with a real number. This is the same sale, now with a different headline. I read it as either sloppy or bait, and I notice that sort of thing.
- Below the hero it settles into a clean grid of nine pieces: a Mac Duggal tiered gown, an Alice + Olivia jacket, a Stuart Weitzman suede boot, a Polo Ralph Lauren Fair Isle cardigan. The layout is easy to read. Compared with Macy's, which would have stacked four banners and a coupon code on top of this, it's calm. Compared with Sephora's twelve-emails-in-a-week catalog, it at least has one offer. But there are no prices anywhere, so "up to 30%" tells me nothing about the Stuart Weitzman boot.
- Would I click? Maybe, for the boots. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and wait to see whether the discount is 30 or 40.

## What's working

- The hero is clean. "FALL STYLE SALE / UP TO 30% OFF" in big red serif on blush is unmistakable, and the black SHOP NOW button is easy to hit with a thumb.
- The brand mix matches how I shop. Stuart Weitzman, Polo Ralph Lauren and Alice + Olivia are labels I actually buy, and each is named under its tile.
- The six category buttons (Women's Clothing, Handbags, Jewelry, Shoes) let me skip the grid and go straight to what I want.
- The black Saint Laurent triple-points band stands out from the blush above it. As a points-motivated shopper, I like that loyalty is called out at all.
- Nothing is broken. No overlapping text, no empty image boxes, nothing that looks shouty.

## What's weak

- The subject says 40% and the body says 30%, and the fine print also says "up to 30% off." That mismatch costs trust.
- The product grid shows no prices and no sale markdowns. I can't tell whether anything pictured is actually in the sale.
- Triple points only applies to a Saint Laurent regular-price purchase of $500 or more. The fine print says it ends 10/25 at 10 PM CT, which is tonight, but the band only says "Limited Time." Real urgency is buried.
- The exclusions are heavy. Beauty and fragrance are out, along with sale items and previous purchases. A beauty-counter regular like me gets nothing here, and "Exclusions Apply" under the hero tells me so.
- This is the fourth Neiman's send in about three days, after the 30% email, David Yurman and the gift-card event. The cadence feels like pressure.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject match the body. Pick one number and use it everywhere. If 40% really exists on some items, show those items in the grid with a "40% off" tag.
- 2. Put sale prices on the tiles, with the original price struck through. That alone would raise my click odds.
- 3. State the actual end dates in the hero: "Ends 10/29." Put "Ends tonight" on the points band if it really does end tonight.
- 4. Name the InCircle benefit plainly instead of making me read the legal paragraph.
- 5. Space out the sends. Four in three days is why I'm skimming.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stuart Weitzman boots, Polo knits and Mac Duggal, marked down through Thursday.`

- **Not assessable:** the truncated source ends inside the second `<style>` block, so I can't see any `href` values, link wrappers or UTM parameters.
- No broken links are confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` sets `maximum-scale=1`. Some mobile clients treat this as disabling pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Hamburger nav uses the checkbox hack:** it relies on `.navbar-checkbox:checked~.menu`, `.hamburger` and `.close` inside the `max-width: 600px` media query. Gmail and some other clients strip or ignore this pattern. Whether the nav has a fallback isn't visible in the truncated source.
- **Comment before the doctype:** a multi-line HTML comment sits before `<!DOCTYPE>`. Most clients tolerate this, but content before the doctype can trigger quirks mode in some renderers.
- **Weak `<title>`:** it is `Neiman Marcus ` with a trailing space and no descriptive text.
- **Not assessable:** alt text, `role="presentation"` on layout tables and heading structure are all beyond the truncation point.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Template documentation left in the output:** the opening comment is still in the rendered email. It reads `REQUIRED variables: @email … @base_refid … Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", "EMAIL_ID", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email) … Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "uEm", …) … CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW --> <!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->`.
