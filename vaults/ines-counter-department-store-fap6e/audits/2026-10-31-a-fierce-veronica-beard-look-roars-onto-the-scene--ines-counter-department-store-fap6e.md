**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- This is the quiet Neiman's I like. The zebra-print jacket and skirt on that rock is a handsome hero, and "Wild at Heart" is the only headline. After Wednesday's gift-card and Fall Style Sale shouting, I appreciate an email that doesn't raise its voice. It matches yesterday's "Things We Love" and the Moncler Re/Icons send: editorial, calm, no price anywhere.
- That calm is also the problem. There's no price, no offer, no event, and no hint that I'm a known customer. Nothing here tells me to act today. I'd look at the zebra set, maybe click through to see what the jacket costs, and then leave. I wouldn't forward it. I'd probably mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- The zebra-print suiting hero is striking, and the model's pose feels editorial rather than shouty.
- "Wild at Heart" is one calm line of copy. It has none of the exclamation points Macy's piles on.
- Six Veronica Beard pieces in a clean two-row grid let me see the denim, the blazer and the knits without hunting.
- Two clear outlined buttons, "Shop Veronica Beard" and "Shop Contemporary", tell me where to go.
- The "Match with a personal style advisor" block at the bottom feels like real designer-floor service.

## What's weak

- There are no prices anywhere, and no sale or loyalty cue. Kohl's puts its Rewards bar at the very top, and Neiman's has nothing like that for InCircle.
- The grid items are captioned only "Veronica Beard", six times. I get no product names and no prices, so I can't tell the denim from the blazer by text alone.
- The hero set, the zebra jacket and skirt, isn't one of the six grid items. I'd have to hunt for it.
- The buttons are thin gray outlines with light text, and they feel faint on a phone.
- The footer is the most visible part of the email, and it has no member benefits or events to offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a member hook. "InCircle members: complimentary alterations on Veronica Beard" would give me a reason to act. Sephora's 30% banner is blunt, but at least it's something.
- 2. Put the zebra hero look in the grid, with its name and price, and link the hero itself.
- 3. Replace the six identical captions with product names and prices, so the email works for a skim.
- 4. Make the buttons heavier and fill the primary one.
- 5. Move the style advisor block up under the grid, and make it a booking link.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML source is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with a CSS-only hamburger nav. The source is truncated, so the body, links, alt text and footer could not be checked. The four QA warnings are all inconclusive. They look like relay-capture limits rather than confirmed defects, except for the URL-heavy plain-text part.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified tracking links:** 17 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so their destinations and redirect health are unverified.
- **Plain-text URL load:** the plain-text part is 3216 of 3708 characters URLs (about 87%). This is consistent with long tracked URLs, and a ratio like that can hurt spam scoring.
- **Not assessable:** the link targets are in the truncated portion, so I can't confirm broken or malformed `href`s.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` caps pinch-zoom at 1x, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Brittle media-query selectors:** the rules use exact-match attribute selectors such as `*[class=contenttable]`, `*[class=hide]` and `*[class=showmobile]`. They stop matching if an element carries more than one class.
- **Hamburger nav:** it depends on `.navbar-checkbox:checked~...` sibling selectors. These need `<style>` and `:checked` support, which Gmail and some Outlook clients lack, and the result is also not keyboard-accessible.
- **Legacy and unprefixed rules:**
  - `.ReadMsgBody` and `-ms-text-size-adjust` are legacy and mostly dead.
  - `body { -webkit-animation: bugfix }` is an iOS 9 hack that is still shipping.
- **Title:** `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` has a trailing space and is generic.
- **Not assessable:** alt text, `role="presentation"` and image dimensions are in the truncated portion.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Template comment in the live HTML:** the opening comment is SFMC authoring documentation left in the sent HTML. It lists variables and data-extension lookups (`NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`, `@uEm`, `@cmdid`, `@emailid`) and ends with "CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW". That exposes internal schema and indicates the template wasn't stripped.
- **Unbalanced parentheses:** the comment's `Lookup(...))` examples have an extra closing paren. It's inert inside a comment but suggests the template was copied from unmaintained docs.
- **Unresolved tokens:** none are visible in the supplied source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe missing:** the QA check flagged it, but it may just be the AgentMail relay not capturing the header. This is unconfirmed.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post missing:** the same caveat applies. Without it, RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe isn't verified, which matters for Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules.
- **Authentication-Results missing:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment could not be determined. The From domain `e.neimanmarcus.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain, which is normal.
- **Physical address and unsubscribe link:** they're in the footer, which was truncated, so they were not verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** they can't be assessed. The 17 links are tracking redirects that weren't resolved, and the visible source shows no UTM parameters or `refid` usage.
- **Landing-page match:** it can't be confirmed, because the final destinations weren't followed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run the audit against the raw `.eml` or full headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Treat the current warnings as inconclusive, not failures.
2. Resolve a sample of the 17 tracking links with redirect-following to check final destinations and UTM/`refid` params.
