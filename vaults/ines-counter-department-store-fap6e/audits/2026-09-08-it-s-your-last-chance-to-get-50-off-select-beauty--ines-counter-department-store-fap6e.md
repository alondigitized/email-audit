# ⚠️ It’s your LAST CHANCE to get 50% off select beauty ⚠️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-08**
## Executive summary

- Fifth time this week, Sephora. Fifth. I've now seen "Stacking Weekend" on 9/4, twice on 9/5, on 9/6 with the autumn dressing, and now again on 9/8 wearing an "⚠️ LAST CHANCE ⚠️" costume. Same red starburst, same "50% off select beauty," same 500-points-at-$75 code, same eyelash curler and Huda Beauty foundation I've now been shown three separate times. At this point it's not urgency, it's just Tuesday.
- That said — mechanically, this is a clean, well-organized email. The 50% starburst is impossible to miss, the "Get $10 Beauty Insider Cash" and "Redeem for Rewards" tiles are genuinely useful for someone like me who won't touch a card without points back, and the "Present this email in store" barcode at the bottom is a nice nod to people who still shop counters in person. But I've built up a callus against the "ends today" framing because I've now watched this "last chance" not actually end for five straight days — Kohl's ran the same fake-urgency Labor Day loop until it finally let up, and Sephora's playing the same trick, just longer.
- Would I click? Only if I hadn't already used the code from Sunday. Would I forward it? No — I'd forward the 9/6 Autumn Edit one if anything, since it at least gave me something new to look at.

## What's working

- The 50% starburst is the first thing my eye hits — clear visual hierarchy, no fighting for attention.
- "Get $10 Beauty Insider Cash when you redeem 500 points" speaks directly to my no-points-no-purchase rule.
- In-store barcode at the bottom is a smart touch for a counter regular who still buys and returns in person.
- "Save On Must Haves" grid gives me real, specific products (Huda Beauty, Shiseido, Glow Recipe) instead of vague lifestyle shots.

## What's weak

- This is the same core creative — red starburst, 500 points, $75 spend — I've now opened five times since Thursday; the "⚠️ LAST CHANCE ⚠️" is see-through when yesterday said the same thing.
- The exclamation-point warning emoji in the subject reads spammy, which is unlike Sephora's usual tone — even Neiman Marcus's "$1,250 gift card" push stayed classier than this.
- Huge wall of redundant CTAs (Shop Now, Shop Now, Shop the App, Shop Now, Build Your Basket) — by the fourth one I've stopped registering any of them as urgent.
- "Holiday Exclusives Launch Soon" crammed into a Labor Day clearance email is jarring — pick a lane.

## Recommendations

- 1. Retire this creative for at least two weeks — I've seen this exact starburst and 500-point offer enough times that it's actively training me to ignore Sephora subject lines. Macy's "LAST CHANCE" trick at least had a real trigger behind it; this one's just fatigue.
- 2. Drop the warning-emoji framing — it cheapens a brand that usually reads more polished than Kohl's or Ulta's blast-everything approach.
- 3. Cut the "Holiday Exclusives Launch Soon" block out of a same-day-urgency email; save it for its own send.
- 4. Lead with one hero product rotation instead of recycling the same Huda Beauty foundation and eyelash curler from last week's send.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your Beauty Insider points expire soon — here's what to grab`
- **Subject Alt B:** `50% off select beauty, one more day`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free samples, $10 Beauty Cash, and the products actually worth stacking`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Same-day delivery ends 9/10 at 1pm PT — here's your list`
