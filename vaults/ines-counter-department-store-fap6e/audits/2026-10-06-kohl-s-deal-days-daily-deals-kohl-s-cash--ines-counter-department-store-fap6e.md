# Kohl's Deal Days + Daily Deals + Kohl's Cash = 🎉 🛍️ 💸
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again. This is the Deal Days send from 10/6 with a different emoji string on the subject, and I'm numb to it. Since 10/4 I've had the four "mystery savings" emails, the two on 10/5, and the siren one earlier today. Nothing in the subject says what's new.
- The body is better than the hollow maroon hero I complained about on 10/5 and earlier today. The header now has the Kohl's logo, a "Kohl's Rewards: Member" status line and "Earn 5% Rewards." That is the first time in a week I've felt recognized as a customer. After that it turns into a very long scroll of price tiles. It's about 8,000 pixels tall, with 50% off shoes, 60% off Carter's, 25% off activewear, 40% off handbags, 60% off furniture and 40% off LEGO. There's also a code block, a Kohl's Cash block and a second coupon block at the bottom.
- I'd skim it, enjoy the handbag tile for a second, and move on. I wouldn't forward it. I'm a beauty-counter person, and this is a department store that sends me throws and bath towels. Sephora Insider, even at six sends a week, at least talks to my category. Kohl's gave me a 14.99 bra and a Ninja slush machine.

## What's working

- The header now shows my Rewards status and "Earn 5% Rewards" up top. Compare the empty maroon block from 10/5.
- Each tile has one headline number: "Up to 50%," "60%," "40%." I can scan them fast.
- "Leaves are falling, and so are our prices. Ends October 8" is a real deadline and a seasonal hook. It's the one line that feels like a person wrote it.
- The Rewards-member blocks ("Kohl's Rewards' members take $10 off your home purchase of $50 or more with coupon") appear several times, so the member benefit is visible.
- The product photography is clean. The handbag tile with the brown leather bags is the best one.

## What's weak

- It's far too long. By the third tile, "Up to 40% + 25% off w/coupon," I'd stopped reading. Macy's does a similar thing, but Macy's usually lets one offer lead.
- Too many stacked offers and codes. There are percent-off tiles, "+25% off w/coupon," Kohl's Cash, a "Take an extra 25% off" code, a $10 Rewards code and a free-shipping line. I can't tell which one applies to me or how they combine. That's exactly the fine print I distrust.
- The "Select styles. Coupons do not apply" and "Coupons do not apply to select brands" lines are everywhere, so the 25% coupon feels like a bait-and-switch.
- Nothing is relevant to beauty or my Gen X taste: Carter's, bath towels, LEGO, a memory-foam topper. Neiman Marcus sent me a quiet email on new Prada shoes today with no codes at all, and it felt more respectful.
- The Kohl's Cash block and the barcodes at the bottom are tiny on a phone. I'd have to zoom to read the dates.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one hero offer and cut the body to three or four tiles. I want to know what today's deal is, and the answer shouldn't be fifteen things.
- 2. Say how the coupons stack in one plain sentence near the top, and say what's excluded.
- 3. Segment me. I've been a Rewards member at the beauty counter, so show me beauty and handbags first, and skip the Carter's and LEGO tiles.
- 4. Give me a Rewards points balance or a Kohl's Cash expiry date in the header, so I feel like a known customer.
- 5. Cap the cadence at one Deal Days send per day.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Handbags up to 40% off + your 5% Rewards`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Deal Days end Oct 8: what's worth it`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Rewards members: extra $10 off $50 plus Kohl's Cash through Oct 18.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fall prices, one deadline. Shoes, handbags and bedding, in store and online.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based ESP/Movable Ink build with 73 tracked links. The HTML has several markup defects, including a stray `</custom>` tag, an empty `<title>` and a viewport that blocks zoom. Many content images have no alt text. The compliance and authentication warnings come from header capture limits and HTML truncation, so they are unconfirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click tracking:** 73 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they use tracking redirects (for example `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABB7InYi...`). Their destinations are unverified, so link health is unknown.
- **Opaque `qs` payloads:** the `qs` values are encoded, so I can't check destination, UTM or link-to-content mapping from the source.
- **Stray closing tag:** `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." ...></custom>` has no matching `<custom>` opener. This is invalid markup and some parsers may mishandle it.
- **Stacked tracking pixels:** the email loads four tracker endpoints before any content:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/2810490d-...`
  - `mi.kohls.com/.../o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager)
  
  Each one adds a request, and the 1x1 and 0x0 sizing is inconsistent.
- **Unstyled first pixel:** the `click.chp.kohls.com` pixel has no `alt` and no hiding style (`display:block` or `font-size:0`). Some clients may render it with line-height spacing.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` disables pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Empty `<title></title>`:** the document has no title.
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` with `<meta itemprop>` children sits inside `<head>`. A `<div>` is not valid there, so parsers will close the head early and move it into the body. Gmail and Outlook will strip the microdata.
- **Empty icon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href, which can trigger a request back to the document URL.
- **Doctype mismatch:** the HTML 4.01 doctype is combined with `xmlns:o` and `xmlns:v`, which is harmless but inconsistent.
- **Web fonts:** Roboto and Montserrat are loaded both through `<link>` and `@import`, and are duplicated. Gmail and some Outlook builds drop both, so the fallback stack `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` is doing the work there.
- **Missing alt text:**
  - **Content images (real):** `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png` and `916d6fa08d185233.png`, plus the product image `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/59107814_2_2x`. With images off, or in a screen reader, the offers have no text equivalent.
  - **Tracking pixels (benign):** `o.gif` already has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, so the QA flag looks like a false positive. The `click.chp.kohls.com` and `demdex.net/event` pixels have no `alt` at all, so adding `alt=""` is a cheap fix.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unrendered merge tokens such as `%%...%%`, `{{...}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear in the visible source, but the source is truncated.
- The Movable Ink URL `...mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_pe...` has an empty Kohl's Cash redemption end-date value. This could be intentional if the recipient has no Kohl's Cash, but it may be a data gap. The subject line promotes Kohl's Cash, so I'd check that the dynamic images fall back correctly.
- The subscriber ID `mi_u=604230016` is embedded in image URLs. This isn't an error, but it is a per-user identifier exposed in image URLs.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** QA reports both as not found, noting the AgentMail relay may not preserve them. This is unconfirmed, and I can't verify it from the source.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `s.kohls.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, but alignment is unverified.
- **Physical address:** QA flagged it as missing. The HTML provided is truncated before the footer, so it can't be confirmed either way. Check the full source or the rendered footer before treating this as a violation.
- **Header consistency:** `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` are fine for email.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters can't be verified because every link goes through the opaque `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=` redirect. The destinations were not probed, so landing page alignment is unconfirmed.
- Campaign identification for the Movable Ink images is present in `email_name=261006_DG_Email_Tue_PM` and `mi_ecmp=1023259_2026106`.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add alt text** to all `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` and `59107814_2_2x` content images. Add `alt=""` to the `click.chp.kohls.com` and `demdex.net/event` pixels.
2. **Fix viewport:** remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0`.
3. **Clean up markup:**
   - Remove the stray `</custom>`.
   - Move or remove the `itemscope` div from `<head>`.
   - Populate `<title>`.
   - Drop the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon" href="">`.
   - Deduplicate the font `<link>` and `@import` loads.
4. **Verify the compliance warnings** against the raw MIME source, which should contain `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, `Authentication-Results` and the footer address. The current QA run can't confirm them.
5. **Check the empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=`** to confirm the Kohl's Cash fallback is intentional.
6. **Resolve a sample of the tracked links** (follow redirects) to confirm the destinations and UTM carry-through.
