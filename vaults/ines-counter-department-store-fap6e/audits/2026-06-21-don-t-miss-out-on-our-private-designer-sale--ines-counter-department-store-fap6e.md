# Don’t miss out on our Private Designer Sale
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-21**
## Executive summary

- Seven Neiman Marcus emails since June 15 — I counted before I even tapped this one. And after that "up to 60% off Designer Sale upgrade" on June 19 that actually made me browse, and the clean "$200 off" subject the following day, this arrives like a shrug. "PRIVATE EVENT" in all caps wants to feel exclusive. But I've already been invited to this party. Twice.
- The layout is pure Neiman Marcus — cream background, restrained serif type, hierarchy that never trips over itself. I'll give them that. But there's nothing here to want. No product. No model. No brand callout like that Louboutin roundup they sent two days before, which gave me a specific thing to covet. Just "up to 40% off" in red on a blank page. And here is the part that actually stings: they already told me 60% off on June 19. Walking that offer back with no explanation is the kind of move Macy's pulls, not Neiman Marcus.
- I'd open this — the sender alone earns the tap — but I would not click through. There's nothing to click toward. Mark as read.

## What's working

- **The visual hierarchy is immaculate.** Logo → nav → PRIVATE EVENT → 40% OFF → button. My eye never got lost, which is more than I can say for that seven-module Sephora send earlier this month.
- **"PRIVATE EVENT" framing is appropriate for the brand.** Neiman Marcus can say this without it sounding silly. Macy's cannot.
- **"SHOP SALE" is a clean CTA.** Verb + noun, no ambiguity, contrasts well against the cream background.
- **Zero render issues.** Crisp text, responsive-looking layout, nothing broken.

## What's weak

- **40% off is a step backward from 60% off on June 19.** I already got the upgrade. Going backwards on the percentage — even with "up to" doing the hedging — feels like a bait-and-switch, and it quietly erodes trust.
- **No product imagery.** The Louboutin roundup worked because it showed me a specific thing to desire before I had to commit to a click. This is just a number floating in white space.
- **No deadline.** "PRIVATE EVENT" implies scarcity. When does it end? Without a date, the urgency is theater.
- **The discount got buried in the body, not the subject.** The two strongest Neiman Marcus subjects this month led with the number — "up to $200 off" and "up to 60% off." This one leads with "Don't miss out," which is FOMO wallpaper.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put the number in the subject.** Every high-performing NM send this month has done exactly this. "Don't miss out" tells me nothing; "up to 40% off" tells me something.
- 2. **Add a deadline.** "Ends Sunday" or "this weekend only" changes the math entirely. Without it, I'll shop tomorrow, and then I won't.
- 3. **Show one product.** One editorial shoe shot, one bag. Give me a reason to click before I arrive cold on the sale landing page.
- 4. **Reconcile the 60% vs. 40% story.** If both are simultaneously true ("up to" covers all of it), lead with 60. If they're different events, say so. The silence is the problem.
- 5. **Configure a preheader.** Right now the inbox preview is likely bleeding into fine print or the "View in a Web Browser" link. That's a free line of copy being wasted.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Private Designer Sale: up to 40% off select styles`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your private access: up to 40% off designers`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `No code needed — savings reflected at checkout through Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop before it ends — select designers at their lowest prices`

## Technical Audit — Neiman Marcus "Private Designer Sale"

**1. Technical Summary**

Standard SFMC/AMPscript table-based send with a Salesforce Marketing Cloud ESP relay. QA yields 4 warnings, 0 hard failures; the most significant issues are missing RFC 8058 unsubscribe headers and a plain-text body that is almost entirely bare URLs.

---

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**

| Finding | Evidence |
|---|---|
| 30 tracking links unverified | QA: `30 tracking link(s) skipped` — HTTP probe could not confirm redirect resolution or destination URL integrity |
| Plain-text body is 74.8% URLs | QA: `2600/3477 chars are URLs` — auto-generated plain-text contains raw redirect URLs rather than readable copy; high URL density is a spam-filter signal |

No broken links confirmed, but the 30 unprobed redirects remain unvalidated.

---

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**

| Finding | Evidence |
|---|---|
| `maximum-scale=1` blocks user zoom | `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` — prevents pinch-to-zoom on mobile; accessibility regression |
| Three commented-out `<viewport>` tags | Lines with `width=device-width/user-scalable=no`, `width=320, initial-scale=0.29`, `width=1024` — vestigial cruft from template iteration |
| iOS 9 hamburger animation hack still present | `body { -webkit-animation: bugfix infinite 1s; }` — iOS 9 market share is negligible; this runs an infinite CSS animation on every render needlessly |
| Two separate `<style>` blocks with inconsistent breakpoints | First block breaks at `600px`; second breaks at `480px` and `520px` — not a hard failure but increases rule specificity complexity |
| `<title>` has trailing space | `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` — minor but indicates template hygiene issues |

---

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**

| Finding | Evidence |
|---|---|
| `@user` variable scenario unresolved | Comment reads `CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW` and `SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256` is commented out — `@user` receives the raw EMAIL_ID lookup, not the SHA256 hash. If downstream code expects the hash (e.g., for identity stitching), this is a silent data error |
| Double closing parentheses in `@user` lookup | `Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", "EMAIL_ID", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email))` — double `))`; if AMPscript is lenient this renders silently, but it is a syntax defect |
| `@refid = CONCAT(@base_refid,"")` is a no-op | Concatenating with an empty string does nothing — placeholder logic left in production template |
| `@uEm` and `@cmdid` looked up but unverifiable | Source truncated before use; cannot confirm these are actually rendered into tracking URLs |

**5. Compliance**

| Finding | Severity | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `List-Unsubscribe` header absent | High | QA: `List-Unsubscribe header not found` — required by Gmail/Yahoo for bulk senders (>5k/day) since Feb 2024 |
| `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) absent | High | QA: `List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found` — one-click unsubscribe from inbox UI is non-functional; violates Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender policy |
| SPF/DKIM status unknown | Medium | QA: `Authentication-Results header not found` — AgentMail relay appears to strip or not forward authentication results; cannot confirm `e.neimanmarcus.com` passes SPF/DKIM |
| CAN-SPAM physical address and footer unsubscribe | Unverifiable | HTML source is truncated before footer — cannot confirm presence |

---

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity**

| Finding | Evidence |
|---|---|
| UTM parameters on tracking links unverifiable | All 30 tracked links were skipped during probing; cannot confirm `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` are appended to neimanmarcus.com landing URLs |
| `@base_refid` is the sole campaign identifier | `@refid = CONCAT(@base_refid,"")` — if `@base_refid` is empty or misconfigured in the Campaign Calendar DE, all click attribution is lost silently |

---

**7. Recommendations**

1. **Critical — add `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers** at the ESP/MTA layer for `e.neimanmarcus.com` sends. Absence puts bulk deliverability to Gmail and Yahoo at risk.
2. **Rewrite the plain-text alternative** with human-readable copy rather than auto-generated redirect URL dumps. 74.8% URL density in plain-text triggers spam classifiers.
3. **Resolve the `@user` scenario** — either remove the commented-out `SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256` line or activate it. The current state leaves the variable's value ambiguous and inconsistent.
4. **Fix the double `))`** in the `@user` AMPscript Lookup call.
5. **Verify authentication header passthrough** via AgentMail relay — confirm SPF and DKIM are passing for `e.neimanmarcus.com` by checking raw headers in a test seed address (e.g., via Mail-Tester or GlockApps).
6. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta to restore mobile accessibility compliance.
7. **Clean up dead template artifacts**: three commented-out viewport tags, iOS 9 animation hack, and the no-op `CONCAT` on `@refid`.
