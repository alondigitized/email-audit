# Save 20%  + get Kohl's Cash to top it off  🤑
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-15**
## Executive summary

- Email fifty-five from Kohl's — I've been counting — and it arrives with the same 20% off plus Kohl's Cash formula they have been running at me daily since at least Memorial Day. The 🤑 emoji in the subject line is the tell: this is a brand talking at a crowd, not to a customer. I've got the Kohl's card, I've earned the Kohl's Cash, and I actually visit the Sephora inside my local store — and yet Kohl's sent me a graphic tee for seven dollars and a section about outdoor toys. Macy's at least knows I'm in Star Rewards and writes the subject line around it. Sephora opened nine emails in thirteen days and every one felt like it knew who I was. This one does not.
- I'll scan it because the clearance number is real and my Kohl's Cash balance is real. But I'm not clicking through and I'm certainly not forwarding this to anyone in my circle.

## What's working

- The 20% off hero lands at the very top — no hunting, no scroll, the offer is immediate
- Kohl's Cash earn rate ($10 per $50 spent) is visible at the footer without burying it in asterisks
- The clearance call-out — up to 70% off with a "NEW MARKDOWNS TAKEN" banner — is the strongest copy in the email; that number earns attention
- The email renders cleanly: no broken images, no overlapping text, no lorem ipsum
- Summer/beach creative is on-calendar and the product imagery is color-coordinated

## What's weak

- Kohl's has a Sephora inside it and there is not a single beauty tile, beauty category link, or beauty product in this entire email — that is a genuine miss for a customer like me
- The 🤑 emoji is the opposite of "respects the customer" — Gen X women who cross-shop Nordstrom do not enjoy being emoji-shouted at
- The email fragments into too many sections: clearance, beach fashion, home goods, outdoor toys — there's no narrative thread and no moment where it feels like it was assembled for me
- The home goods block (Bedding, Bath, Kitchen & Dining, Patio, Decor) shows up just when the fashion section starts to feel relevant; the context collapse kills any building momentum
- This is the fifth consecutive day with the same 20% + Kohl's Cash subject beat — Neiman Marcus sent me seventeen emails in thirteen days and I'm already doing the math on unsubscribing there; Kohl's is on the same trajectory

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put Sephora at Kohl's in the email** — one beauty tile, one category CTA. If the data shows I've bought beauty at this store, use it. This is the single change that would most separate this email from email forty-five through fifty-four.
- 2. **Drop the 🤑 emoji** — it reads as coupon-clipper energy, not loyalty-member energy
- 3. **Tighten the product grid to two sections max** — fashion and beauty, or fashion and clearance; not fashion plus home plus outdoor toys
- 4. **Show my Kohl's Cash balance or a progress bar** — "You've earned $X, here's how to spend it before it expires" drives clicks in a way a generic Shop Now never will; Macy's Star Rewards does this and it's the mechanic that actually makes me open the next one
- 5. **Give the clearance a hard deadline** — "Clearance ends Sunday" beats a rotating 70%-off banner that I've learned to tune out
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your Kohl's Cash is waiting — save 20% before it resets`
- **Subject Alt B:** `20% off + Sephora inside — your rewards count today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Clearance up to 70% off. Earn $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 you spend.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fashion, beauty, home — your 20% off applies to all of it.`

### 1. Technical Summary

Structurally standard retail ESP email (HTML4 Transitional, MSO conditionals, table-based layout) with no broken links detected. Primary concerns are relay-layer header gaps masking compliance signals, and pervasive missing alt text on tracking and content images.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **22 click-redirect links skipped** — all route through `click.s.kohls.com` and `click.chp.kohls.com`. HTTP probe was not attempted; broken destination URLs cannot be confirmed or ruled out. Destination integrity must be verified directly against Kohl's ESP (Coherent Path / Salesforce Marketing Cloud) click reports.
- **Three distinct pixel/tracking domains in use:** `click.chp.kohls.com` (Coherent Path open pixel), `mi.kohls.com` (MI3 platform), `kohls.demdex.net` (Adobe Audience Manager). No technical issue, but the fingerprint surface is high — aggressive privacy filters (iOS Mail Privacy Protection, uBlock) will suppress all three.
- **Malformed closing tag:** `</custom>` appears in the open-pixel `<div>` block (`<img ...><custom>`). This is an unclosed/invalid element that could cause parse tree issues in strict renderers.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **11 images missing `alt` text** — confirmed by QA. Includes:
  - Open pixel: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/904e2e5d...` — alt="" acceptable for tracking pixels but absent entirely
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png` through `916d6fa08d185233.png` — these appear to be content/product images based on the `email_name=260715_DG_Email_Wednesd` parameter; missing alt text on content images fails WCAG 2.1 AA (SC 1.1.1) and renders as blank in image-blocked clients
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` — tracking pixel, alt="" or `aria-hidden="true"` should be explicit (the `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif` pixel does correctly use `aria-hidden="true"` — others do not)
- **`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — disables pinch-zoom on mobile; this is a WCAG 2.1 AA failure (SC 1.4.4). Common in retail email but worth flagging.
- **`<title>` is empty** — `<title></title>` means screen readers announce no document title. Should be set to the subject line or a descriptive string.
- **Font loading:** Google Fonts loaded via both `<link>` and `@import` for the same two families (Roboto, Montserrat). Duplicate requests add latency; the `<link>` tags are inside `<!--[if !mso]><!--> ... <![endif]-->` guards, so the `@import` in the unguarded `<style>` block is redundant.

No unrendered merge tokens (`{{`, `%%`, `*|`) detected in the truncated source. The `mi_u=604230016` parameter in MI3 pixel URLs is populated (not a placeholder), indicating subscriber-level personalization fired correctly.

### 5. Compliance

- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** — flagged by QA as potentially missing from the AgentMail relay capture. If absent at delivery, this violates Google/Yahoo's 2024 bulk sender requirements (mandatory for >5K/day senders) and RFC 2369. Must be confirmed against raw received headers, not relay copy.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found** — one-click unsubscribe (required by Google/Yahoo for bulk senders) cannot be confirmed. Same caveat: may be a relay capture gap, but must be verified.
