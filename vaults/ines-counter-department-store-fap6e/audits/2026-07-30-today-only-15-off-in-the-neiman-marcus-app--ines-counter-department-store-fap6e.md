# Today only: 15% off in the Neiman Marcus app
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-30**
## Executive summary

- My first reaction scrolling through this: pretty, on-brand, and then I hit the fine print and felt my interest drain out of the bottom of the email. That mint-green hero with the giant "15% OFF" typography is gorgeous — very Neiman Marcus, very restrained compared to the siren-red "70% OFF LAST CALL" banners they've been hammering me with all week. But then I realize the catch: it's app-exclusive, the code isn't even in the email (you have to scan a QR code or download the app to find out what it is), and the fine print excludes beauty, fragrances, and intimates entirely. I am a beauty-counter regular. That's my whole category, gone before I even open the app.
- The product grid underneath is genuinely nice curation — Chloe, Manolo Blahnik, Burberry, Carolina Herrera, Zegna, that's exactly the mix that keeps me subscribed to Neiman Marcus over, say, Kohl's — but there's no pricing, no strikethrough, nothing that tells me what 15% actually saves me on any of these pieces. Compare that to Sephora's "Up to 50% off Select Lip Picks" send last Wednesday, which had real product grids with actual MAC $25→$15 strikethrough pricing — that one made me want to tap immediately. This one makes me want to close the app store prompt and move on.
- Would I click? Only the "SHOP WOMEN'S CLOTHING" buttons, and only out of habit — not because this offer earned it. I would not download the app for 15% off things I'd have to pay full price on the beauty side anyway. Not forwarding this one; there's nothing here to brag about to a friend.

## What's working

- The hero typography is clean and confident — "15% OFF" in that huge serif-adjacent sans reads instantly, no squinting required, unlike some of the busier Neiman Marcus sends I've seen.
- The product carousel is well-curated for someone like me — Chloe, Burberry, Manolo Blahnik, Carolina Herrera are names I actually shop, not filler.
- "Today only" plus the fine-print deadline ("Ends 7/30/26 at 10:00 PM CT") gives the urgency real teeth instead of vague "limited time" language.
- Sage-green palette and generous white space feel like Neiman Marcus, not a knockoff of the Macy's grid-chart emails I keep getting.

## What's weak

- The offer excludes beauty, fragrances, and intimates by name in the fine print — that's my entire reason for shopping this brand, cut out of the one thing being promoted.
- The code isn't in the email at all — "will be advertised in the app" means I have to download an app or scan a QR code just to find out the discount mechanism. That's real friction for a 15% offer.
- No InCircle or loyalty-tier language anywhere. I check my points before I check anything else, and this email acts like I'm not a member of anything.
- No pricing or strikethrough shown on any of the featured pieces — I'm looking at a Chloe bag with zero indication of what 15% actually saves me.
- The exclusion list in the fine print is enormous — Chanel, Louis Vuitton, Gucci, Dior, Prada, Saint Laurent all carved out — which makes the headline offer feel thinner than it looks at first glance.

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop the app-exclusivity gate, or at minimum put the promo code directly in the email — making me hunt for it in an app I have to download first kills momentum that the clean hero design just built.
- 2. Either include beauty in the offer or don't lead with a beauty subscriber list for an offer that excludes beauty — this is a targeting mismatch that Sephora and even Kohl's don't make.
- 3. Add visible pricing or strikethrough on at least the hero products so the 15% feels tangible, the way Sephora's lip email did with real before/after numbers.
- 4. Surface InCircle status or points-earning language — I don't move on offers that ignore my loyalty tier.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Today only: 15% off, no app required`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your app-only 15% off — code inside`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Chloe, Manolo Blahnik, Burberry and more — see what qualifies before it's gone`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Beauty and fragrance are excluded from today's app deal`
