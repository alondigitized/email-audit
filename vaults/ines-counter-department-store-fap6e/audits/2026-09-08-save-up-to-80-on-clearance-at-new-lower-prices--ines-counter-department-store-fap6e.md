# Save up to 80% on clearance at new lower prices 🙌
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-08**
## Executive summary

- Well, this is a nice change of pace — after a solid week of "ENDS TODAY" Labor Day panic emails, Kohl's finally sent me something that isn't screaming. Clearance up to 80% off, clean hero, fall home decor styled like an actual room instead of a coupon flyer — I'll give them that. But "picked with you in mind" next to a slushie machine, bath towels, men's jeans, and a men's polo? Nobody picked anything with me in mind, that's just four random clearance SKUs with a nice font.
- The fall/Halloween decor block is genuinely the best thing Kohl's has sent me all week — moody styling, the little turkey candle holder, 20% off signage that doesn't feel like a hostage negotiation. That's the section I'd actually scroll to. Compare that to Neiman's New Arrivals send, which trusted a clean grid of Givenchy and Bottega with zero gimmicks — Kohl's still needs the "SAVE NOW" starburst spinning around a percentage, but at least it's calmer than the Kohl's Cash carpet-bombing from earlier this week.
- Would I click? Maybe on the Halloween/fall decor block, since that's a category I actually browse. Forward to a friend? No. Mark as read and move on — this is a 15-minutes-of-my-attention email, not a step-into-the-store email.

## What's working

- The clearance hero is legible and the 80% off number is the first thing my eye hits — no argument there.
- Fall/Halloween decor styling (moody chair, turkey candleholder, dried leaves) feels seasonal and intentional, not slapped together.
- Rewards status bar up top ("Kohl's Rewards Member, Earn 5% Rewards") acknowledges I'm a member, which Sephora's stacking-weekend spam never bothers to do.
- No "ENDS TODAY" urgency screaming — a relief after this week's run.

## What's weak

- "Picked with you in mind" over a slushie maker, a bath towel, men's jeans, and a men's polo is flatly untrue — I'm a 47-year-old woman, not whoever this algorithm thinks I am.
- Two men's items in a four-item "just for you" grid is a real miss — feels like a stranger's inbox, not mine.
- Layout is a wall of category buttons (Home/Women/Men/Kids) plus a home-decor block plus a "for you" grid plus fine print — it's a lot of Kohl's for one email, same overload problem I keep flagging.
- No mention of Kohl's Cash this time, which is inconsistent with every other Kohl's send this week — cadence whiplash.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the "picked for you" personalization — if I've never bought men's clothing, don't put a polo and jeans there. Show me clearance in categories I've actually shopped.
- 2. Lead the whole email with the fall/Halloween decor block — it's the strongest visual and most on-brand-for-me content; the clearance banner can follow.
- 3. Cut the four flat nav buttons (Home/Women/Men/Kids) — redundant with the actual product content below.
- 4. Add one loyalty-specific line — a Rewards-member-only clearance perk would justify that status bar up top.
