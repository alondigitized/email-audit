# ‼️ APP EARLY ACCESS: Glossier, LANEIGE, and more ‼️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-05**
## Executive summary

- My first reaction was that this is Sephora's fifth or sixth send since 10/2, and it opens with two sales at once. The top says "Earn Points Faster" with a 2X and a 5X badge. Directly under it comes "App-Only Deals Start Now!" I'm a points person, so the SAYPOINTS code caught me. But the "early access" in the subject isn't the first thing I see. Points is. The Glossier mini set at $64 ($80 value) is a nice hero, but I had to scroll past the points block to reach it.
- After that it's a long scroll of NEW, ONLY AT SEPHORA gift sets at $25 to $98, then a 10% off $100+ block for Oct 5–6, an Oct 7 "Spend More, Get More" block, a hair care and fragrance row, and Hispanic Heritage Month. It's a department-store circular, and I know that genre. Macy's did the same thing in its "LAST CHANCE: confirm now" send. Neiman Marcus's "Things We Love" was calmer and I trusted it more.
- Would I click? Probably yes, on the LANEIGE Lip Sleeping Mask at $25 or the Westman Atelier duo, since I'd want to check those against Nordstrom. Would I forward it? No. The app-only "early access" framing doesn't feel exclusive when the same email also pushes three other promos.

## What's working

- The Glossier hero is clear. It's one product set with a $64 price and an $80 "value" next to it, so I get the math without reading fine print.
- Every product tile carries a "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA" or "LIMITED EDITION" tag and a price. I can scan the prices without clicking, and that's how I shop.
- The "Clean at Sephora" badge on one tile is a real trust cue for someone my age who reads ingredient lists.
- The brand lineup (LANEIGE, Marc Jacobs Beauty, Westman Atelier, Glossier) fits my counter habits better than the 10/2 brow and hair emails did.
- Loyalty is front and center with Beauty Insider points, the 2X/5X badges and a Rewards Bazaar link. I don't use a card that doesn't pay me back, so that matters.

## What's weak

- Too many offers compete. Points, app-only deals, 10% off $100+, a spend-more-get-more block, the P.S. about Same-Day Delivery and Heritage Month all sit in one scroll. I count at least four promo codes and deadlines (10/7, 10/9, Oct 5–6, Oct 7). I can't tell which one matters.
- The subject promises "APP EARLY ACCESS: Glossier, LANEIGE" but the top of the email leads with points. That's a subject/hero mismatch.
- The "early access" claim feels thin. The same email says app deals will "drop daily through 10/9," so I don't feel I'm getting in early. It reads as the app being pushed.
- I'm glad the footer carries a points barcode, but it plus the long fine-print blocks make the bottom very long and hard to read on a phone. Some of that text is tiny.
- There are no people in the email. Everything is flat-lay product shots, so nothing tells me this is for a woman in her late 40s. Sephora's email from 10/3 ("Hot drops coming through") at least had the points code at the very top, and this one buries the app message.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story per send. If the story is app early access, lead with the Glossier set and move points and the 10% code below it, or save them for the next send. Sephora already ran a clean one-idea send on 10/2 (the brow email, "50% OFF select brow products"), and that worked.
- 2. Put the single deadline and CTA right under the hero, for example "Shop app early access, ends 10/9," instead of the generic "SHOP HOLIDAY."
- 3. Drop the double ‼️ emoji from the subject. It reads as shouting, and I notice when an email shouts instead of respecting me.
- 4. Cut the product grid from 8 tiles to 4 and add one rating or review count per tile.
- 5. Say what makes it "early access," such as "before it hits sephora.com Thursday."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template from Epsilon (`images.harmony.epsilon.com`). The main confirmed defect is an empty plain-text part (0 chars). Compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because AgentMail doesn't expose the headers. The HTML source is truncated, so findings are limited to what is visible and what the QA data reports.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links are confirmed. The QA run probed 12 of 64 material links and skipped 52, and no failures were reported for the probed set. The other 52 are unverified.
- The image with no alt text at `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2gAndjYxMDAwMDAx...` has a long opaque path on the click/tracking host. It is probably an open-tracking pixel. If it is, it should be `width="1" height="1"` with `alt=""` and no visible footprint. The truncated source doesn't let me confirm its dimensions.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (2 images, QA-confirmed):**
  - The `app.sephora.com/O/...` image above.
  - `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg` on `images.harmony.epsilon.com`. This is a navigation header image, so it needs descriptive alt text. Without alt, nav links built from it are unlabeled for screen readers and for image-blocked clients.
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />`. `maximum-scale=1` restricts pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resizing disabled:** the CSS sets `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`, `-ms-text-size-adjust: none` and `text-size-adjust: none` on `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td`. This prevents user text scaling in WebKit clients.
- **No `lang` attribute:** the opening tag is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" xmlns:v=... xmlns:o=...>` with no `lang`, so screen readers can't select the right voice.
- **Invalid robots meta:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index" />`. The valid token is `noindex`. This has no practical effect in email, but it is malformed.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` doesn't distinguish this message. This is minor.
- **Plain-text part missing (QA FAIL):** "Text version is 0 chars". Text-only clients and some spam filters see a multipart message with an empty text/plain part.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The truncated source can't rule them out further down.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found (WARN)** and **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found (WARN).** QA says the relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed rather than a defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers. Verify them against raw headers from the ESP or a direct-to-Gmail seed.
- **Authentication-Results not found (WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment can't be assessed from this capture. From `shop@beauty.sephora.com`, alignment would need checking.
- The QA notes say an unsubscribe link is always included in the probe set. The footer unsubscribe and physical address fall in the truncated portion, so I can't confirm them from the source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues found, but this is not verified. The visible source has no CTA hrefs, and the QA output has no landing-page probe results or UTM data. Links appear to route through the `app.sephora.com` tracking host, which is typically a redirect that carries the campaign parameters.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a text/plain alternative.** It is currently 0 chars (the only QA FAIL). Generate it from the HTML and include link URLs.
2. **Add alt text** to `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg`. Use descriptive text, or `alt=""` for purely decorative images. Set `alt=""` and 1×1 dimensions on the tracking pixel.
3. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta and reconsider `text-size-adjust: none` to restore zoom and text scaling.
4. **Add `lang="en"`** to `<html>` and correct `content="no index"` to `noindex` (or drop it).
5. **Verify List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (`List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`) and SPF/DKIM/DMARC** from raw headers outside the AgentMail relay before treating the WARNs as defects.
6. **Widen link probing.** Only 12 of 64 links were checked. Rerun with the footer, nav and secondary CTAs included, and record the UTM parameters on final landing URLs.
