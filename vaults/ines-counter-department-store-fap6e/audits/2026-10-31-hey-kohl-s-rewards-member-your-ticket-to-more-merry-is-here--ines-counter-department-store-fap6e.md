# Hey Kohl's Rewards member, your ticket to more merry is here 🎟️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's again, and the member bar at the top is the only part that feels like it knows me. "Status: Kohl's Rewards Member | Earn: 5% Rewards" is a good start. But the hero is "5% rewards," in a font that looks like it's melting, over a gift pile and a snowy village. It's loud and bubbly, and it isn't the kind of loyalty message I trust. I earn points on every card I carry. I want to see what I've actually earned.
- The body is a long scroll that sells four things at once: Kohl's Cash, brand gifts, the 40% Kohl's Card offer, and DoorDash and Instacart delivery. Then three barcoded coupons come last, after I've already scrolled about three screens. Those coupons are the real offer, and they're the same 20% off and $10 off $50 I've had five times since 10/26. The 10/30 clapping-hands send and the 10/29 checkmarks send both led with them. Burying them is an odd choice when I've already seen them this week.
- I wouldn't click the hero. I might scroll to the coupons, screenshot a code, and close it. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The "Status: Kohl's Rewards Member | Earn: 5% Rewards" strip is the one moment I felt recognized.
- "Earn $5 Kohl's Cash for every $100 spent" is a clear, simple earn rate. I can do that math.
- "Free returns through January 31, 2027" is a real trust cue for someone who returns at the brand event. The "Excludes Sephora at Kohl's" note is honest.
- The coupons have codes and PINs, an end date (Ends Nov. 1) and a scannable barcode. That's practical and easy to use in store.
- The Kohl's Cash redemption window (Nov. 2–8) is stated plainly.

## What's weak

- The subject promises a "ticket to more merry." Nothing in the body is a ticket, and the coupons are the same ones I got 10/30.
- The best offers are at the bottom. The 20% off and $10 off $50 coupons are below the fold on a phone.
- The 40% Kohl's Card block pushes new credit on a member who already has points. Saks and Nordstrom would never lead with "Save 40% when you open a card" in a member email. It feels like a pitch to a stranger.
- The hero is crowded: bubble font, snow on the button, a gift stack and an "Earn $5" banner. It doesn't read as a calm, premium hero.
- The "NINE WEST" brand tile is the only designer-ish signal. It sits beside a men's coat and a figurine, so I can't tell what the brand section is for. There's no beauty here at all, even though Sephora at Kohl's is in the footer fine print.
- The fine print is a dense block at the bottom, in small gray type I can't read without zooming.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the coupons or the Kohl's Cash earn rate in one clean hero. Put "20% off + $10 off $50" right under the member bar. Drop the 40% card block from member sends.
- 2. Cut the delivery and pickup block, or move it down. It's a different job from "use your rewards."
- 3. Show my actual Kohl's Cash balance or points, or say what I've earned. That would make the member bar mean something.
- 4. Add a Sephora at Kohl's beauty module. Sephora Insider sends me twenty emails a week, but at least it shows what I can buy.
- 5. Neiman Marcus's quiet single-hero send last week was easier on the eye. One image and one headline would help here.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML has structural validity problems: a stray `</custom>` tag, a `<div>` inside `<head>`, and an empty `<title>`. It also has widespread missing `alt` attributes and a zoom-blocking viewport. The compliance and authentication warnings are mostly artifacts of the AgentMail relay and the truncated source, so they can't be confirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 51 tracking links (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=...`, `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...`) were skipped by the HTTP probe, so link validity and redirect destinations are unknown.
- **Four stacked tracking pixels** load on open:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/99781a7d-...`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=...`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager)

  This is redundant open tracking, and it adds third-party requests.
- **Per-user ID in URLs:** `mi_u=604230016` appears in image and link query strings. Images loaded from `mi.kohls.com` expose it to any proxy or cache.
- **Malformed markup:** `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>` has an orphan `</custom>` closing tag. Parsers ignore it, but it is invalid.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content images:** 7 `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` images have no `alt` (`2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`). If these carry the offer and CTA content, as the "rp" naming suggests, the email is unreadable with images blocked and for screen readers.
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixels:** the two 1x1 pixels (`click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` and `kohls.demdex.net/event`) have no `alt`. Add `alt=""`, as the `mi.kohls.com/.../o.gif` pixel in the source already does with `aria-hidden="true"`.
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" content="..., maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` prevents pinch-zoom and fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` leaves no document title for assistive tech or some clients.
- **Div in head:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` with `<meta itemprop>` children sits inside `<head>`. A `<div>` is invalid there, and parsers will implicitly end the head and start the body at that point. Any markup after it, including the `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` and the style blocks, may be handled inconsistently.
- **Empty favicon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty `href`, which can trigger a request for the page URL.
- **Web fonts:** the `@import` of Google Fonts (Roboto, Montserrat) is wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. This is correct, but Gmail strips it, so fallback fonts (`Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`) are present and adequate.
- **Doctype and language:** the HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype with `lang="en"` is acceptable. Layout tables carry `role="presentation"` on the main containers, but the preheader table does not.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source or the subject. The subject's "Hey Kohl's Rewards member" is a static generic greeting, not a failed token. The truncated source can't rule out tokens further down.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** both are flagged as not found. The QA note attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly not capturing headers, so these are unconfirmed rather than confirmed absences. Without them, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements (RFC 8058 one-click) would not be met.
- **Physical address:** "No physical address detected." The HTML provided is truncated before the footer, so this is probably a false positive and needs a check against the full source.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status for `s.kohls.com` is unknown. This is an expected limitation of the relay, not a sender defect.
- **Footer:** the unsubscribe link wasn't visible in the truncated source, so it can't be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Unverifiable:** all links go through the ESP redirector (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=...`), and the probe was skipped, so destination URLs and UTM parameters can't be seen.
- **Campaign identifiers:** the image URLs carry `mi_ecmp=1024911_20261031` and `email_name=261031_DG_Email_LTY_In...`. These are consistent with the send date (2026-10-31) and give the campaign a clear identifier.
- **Naming mismatch:** no standard `utm_*` parameters are visible in the available source. The campaign appears to use the `mi_*` parameter scheme (Movable Ink) instead, which is a tracking-taxonomy difference rather than a defect.
- **Dynamic images:** the `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` parameter on `916d6fa08d185233.png` is empty. That could mean a dynamic field (reward redemption end date) resolved to blank. Confirm whether the rendered image shows a missing date.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all seven `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` images, and `alt=""` to the 1x1 tracking pixels.
2. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Move the `schema.org` `<div itemscope>` out of `<head>`, or drop it. Fill in `<title>`, remove the empty favicon `<link>`, and delete the stray `</custom>` tag.
4. Consolidate the four open-tracking pixels to the ones that are actually needed.
5. Re-run the QA against the full (untruncated) HTML and raw headers to confirm the unsubscribe link, the physical address, `List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
6. Verify the empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte` parameter against the rendered image.
7. Resolve a sample of the redirect links manually to confirm destinations and campaign parameters.
