# Ends soon ⏳ Get your mystery savings before they're gone 🛍️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-04**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again, and this is the fourth "mystery savings" send today. The subject promises a mystery, and I opened it half expecting one. There isn't one. What I got was a home-décor and baby catalog with coupon codes stacked at the bottom. This time the top isn't the hollow red "EXTRA" I've complained about since 9/29. The header is clean, and the Rewards status line ("Status: Kohl's Rewards Member | Earn: 5% Rewards") is a small sign that they know who I am. I'll give them that.
- The problem is that nothing is clear. A $10-off-$50 coupon sits in the pumpkin hero. 30% off bedding and bath sits below it. A baby and toddler sale and a "Picked with you in mind" product grid come after. Then there are three more codes at the bottom, one of them an "extra 20% off." I have to scroll a very long way to find out what the actual deal is. At the Clinique or Estée Lauder counter, someone hands me one thing. Here I get a flea market.
- Would I click? Maybe, if I were already redecorating. I'm a beauty-counter shopper, and there's no beauty in this email at all. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and wait for the weekend, because this brand clearly sends the same promo several times a day.

## What's working

- The "Status: Kohl's Rewards Member" line and the Kohl's Cash banner up top tell me they recognize me as a loyalty customer.
- The earn line ("Earn $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 spent") is a real, concrete loyalty hook. I won't use a card without points back, so that one speaks to me.
- The coupons are shown as scannable codes with end dates ("Ends Oct. 4"). I know exactly what I'm redeeming and when it expires.
- The product grid has real prices and "Orig." strikethroughs, like $229.99 against $349.99 on the Ninja. That kind of honesty I can read quickly.
- The "Pumpkin spice your space" hero is seasonal and well photographed, so it doesn't feel stale.

## What's weak

- The subject said "mystery savings," and the body has no mystery. It's a bait-and-switch in tone, even if the coupons are real.
- There are too many competing offers: $10 off $50, 30% off, starting at 9.99, a baby sale, an extra 20%, and Kohl's Cash. Nothing leads, so the eye wanders.
- Nothing here is for me. There's home décor, baby, toddler, toys and LEGO. The only apparel is one pair of women's jeans, and there's no beauty.
- "Only twice a year" for the baby sale and "Ends soon" for everything else is urgency overload. It's the fourth "mystery" email today and I don't believe it anymore.
- The legal copy at the bottom is dense and tiny. I can't read it without zooming, and the "Coupons do not apply to select brands" caveat is buried in small type.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer. Make the extra 20% code the hero and put it above the fold. Move the décor and baby blocks below it as supporting content.
- 2. Cut the sends to one per day and stop reusing "mystery." If I get four in a day, I stop trusting any of them.
- 3. Segment the content. Show me beauty or apparel, not baby gear, if that's what I've browsed. Sephora, Macy's and Neiman's all do this better for me.
- 4. Collapse the three barcode coupons into one "Your coupons" block with a single clear button.
- 5. Put the Rewards status and the Kohl's Cash earn line in the hero, since those are what actually move me.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your extra 20% off ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `$10 off $50 + Kohl's Cash, ends today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code AJFSEKNBNLB6P on home, bath and bedding. Rewards members earn $10 Cash per $50.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your coupons are in your Wallet. Take 30% off bedding and bath before midnight.`
- Competitors do this better. Sephora's "LAST CALL" hair send put one clearly framed offer with "TODAY ONLY" in the corner right up top. Macy's "confirm now for extra 25% off" was a single mechanic with a single button. Neiman Marcus's Veronica Beard send had no percentages at all and was calmer for it. Kohl's has more offers than all three combined and gives me less direction.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The markup is mostly table-based and responsive, but it has several malformed-HTML defects in `<head>` and the tracking block, and the viewport meta blocks zoom. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the AgentMail relay and a truncated HTML capture, so they can't be confirmed as sender-side failures.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link probing:** 70 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, so none were validated. The one visible CTA is `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABB7InYi…`, an opaque signed redirect with no readable destination.
- **Pixel count:** four separate tracking or analytics pixels fire in the first lines of `<body>`:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/5221ecab-…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`

  This is redundant open tracking, and each pixel is another third-party request.
- **Malformed pixel markup:** the `open.aspx` pixel is followed by a stray `</custom>` closing tag (`<img … alt=""></custom>`). There is no matching opener, so the markup is invalid.
- **Empty icon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty `href`. Some clients will request the current document URL.
- **Fonts:**
  - Roboto and Montserrat are each loaded twice, once via `<link>` and once via `@import`.
  - Only Montserrat appears in the visible `font-family` declarations.
  - Gmail and Outlook ignore web fonts, so the duplicate loads add requests with no benefit there.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` disables pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. HTML parsers will close `<head>` early at that point, so the meta tags and anything after it may be handled unpredictably. Email clients also strip microdata, so it does nothing.
- **Missing alt text:**
  - QA flagged 5 or more images. The content images (`mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, and others) have no `alt`, and I could not confirm this in the truncated HTML. If they are the promo and creative images, the message is unreadable with images off or with a screen reader.
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/…` and `kohls.demdex.net/event` are confirmed to have no `alt` attribute. They should carry `alt=""`.
  - The QA flag on `o.gif` is a false positive. The source has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, which is correct for a decorative pixel.
- **Layout:** the layout tables use `role="presentation"` and a `.container` class that goes to 100% width at ≤500px. No issues found beyond the above.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[[…]]`) appear in the visible source. The per-recipient `mi_u=604230016` in the pixel URLs resolved to a value. The captured HTML is truncated, so tokens further down can't be ruled out.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** QA did not find either header. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture headers. Kohl's sends from an established ESP domain (`s.kohls.com`) and very likely includes them, so I can't confirm a real defect.
- **Physical address:** QA did not detect one. The footer is outside the truncated HTML, so this is unverified.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** missing, and the QA note attributes it to the relay. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is **unknown**, and no failure is evidenced.
- **Sender consistency:** the From domain is `s.kohls.com`, and the tracking domains are `click.s.kohls.com`, `click.chp.kohls.com` and `mi.kohls.com`. All are first-party, so alignment looks consistent.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not assessable:** click URLs are signed redirects (`?qs=…`), and the 70 links weren't followed, so UTM parameters and landing pages can't be verified.
- **Campaign tags:** Kohl's uses its own tagging on image URLs instead of UTM: `email_name=261004_DG_Email_Mystery`, `mi_ecmp=1023147_2026104`. Those appear only on image URLs, not on any click destinations I can see.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
2. Add `alt` text to every content image (the `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` set). Add `alt=""` to the `click.chp.kohls.com` and `demdex.net` pixels.
3. Fix the head and body markup:
   - Move or remove the `itemscope` div from `<head>`.
   - Delete the stray `</custom>`.
   - Remove the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon" href="">`.
   - Populate `<title>`.
4. Consolidate to one font-loading method and drop the unused Roboto.
5. Reduce the four open pixels to the one the team needs.
6. Re-run QA against the raw `.eml` to settle the three unconfirmed items. Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results` and the footer address in the original message, not the relayed copy.
7. Follow a few redirect chains manually to check UTM and landing-page alignment, since the automated check skipped all 70.
