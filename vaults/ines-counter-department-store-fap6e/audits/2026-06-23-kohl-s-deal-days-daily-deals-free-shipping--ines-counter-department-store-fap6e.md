# Kohl’s Deal Days = Daily Deals + free shipping 🙌
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-23**
## Executive summary

- Twenty-seven. That's where I am in June, and I know because I counted the last one — and that was already number twenty-six, also on the twenty-third. Two Kohl's Deal Days emails in one day, subject lines so close they read like a typo of each other: "JUST IN: Kohl's Deal Days + Free Shipping + Daily Deals" and now this one, same structure, just with an equals sign instead of a plus sign and a different hand emoji. I had to double-check my sent folder to make sure I hadn't hallucinated the first one. I didn't.
- The email itself is the Kohl's formula executed faithfully: a waterfall of discount tiles, each one with its own "Shop Now" button, no single thing you'd call a hero. Seventy percent off bedding. Sixty percent off sunglasses. Forty percent off what appears to be a romper. Buried near the bottom — after I've already exhausted my scrolling patience — there's a 75% off clearance section with what looks like beauty and fragrance products, which is actually the most relevant thing in here for me. But by the time I get there, I've mentally moved on. Macy's, for all its inbox noise, at least tends to lead with something that pulls me in. This pulls me in seven directions simultaneously and lands me nowhere.
- I'd mark this read without clicking. I would not forward it. I am actively reconsidering my Kohl's subscription settings.

## What's working

- The **Kohl's Cash reminder** near the bottom is the one personalized loyalty signal I respond to — that's real money I earned and I don't want to waste it.
- **Free shipping** is called out clearly; no minimum threshold drama that I can see, which I appreciate.
- The **75% off clearance section** with what appears to be beauty and cosmetics products is genuinely relevant to me — I just had to scroll past five other sections to find it.
- **No broken images or layout bugs** — the email renders cleanly; Kohl's production quality is consistent.
- The **6 Days of Deals countdown framing** at least implies a finite window, which gives me a reason to act today rather than "someday."

## What's weak

- This is the **second email today with the same campaign concept** — I audited the "JUST IN" version earlier this morning with a 🤗 emoji, now here's the 🙌 version. That is not segmentation; that is spray and pray.
- **No visual hierarchy** — the eye doesn't know where to go. Seven discount modules stacked in a column is a clearance rack, not an email.
- The **beauty content I actually care about is buried below four other category tiles** — bedding and kids' shoes shouldn't be above the fold for me. Sephora's "Last Chance" email this week put the relevant offer in the first 100 pixels; Kohl's made me scroll past a romper and a duvet.
- **"Shop Now" on every single button** — it's the same CTA copy repeated at least six times. Neiman Marcus managed to make a bag feel coveted with real editorial copy; this is the opposite of that.
- The **demographic signals are all over the map** — kids, home, fashion, beauty, clearance. I'm 47, I shop Macy's beauty counter and Saks accessories. There's nothing here that says Kohl's knows who I am.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pick one offer and own it.** The 75% off clearance / beauty angle is actually interesting — lead with that instead of burying it. Everything else can live below the fold.
- 2. **Cut the email in half.** Six discount modules competing for attention means none of them win. Two or three with room to breathe would outperform this scroll marathon.
- 3. **Kill the duplicate send.** If the "JUST IN" version went out this morning, there is no universe where the 🙌 version needs to go out the same afternoon. Pick one, A/B the subject, don't double-tap the same inbox.
- 4. **Use the Kohl's Cash hook as the open driver, not a footnote.** I'm going to click to redeem earned rewards; I'm not going to click because the seventh "Shop Now" button reminded me I like discounts.
- 5. **Give me a CTA with a noun.** "Shop Beauty Clearance" beats "Shop Now" every single time.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your Kohl's Cash is waiting — plus up to 75% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `75% off clearance + free shipping ends in 6 days`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Beauty, home, and fashion clearance — Kohl's Cash redeemable now`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `6 days left to stack your savings with free shipping`

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "Deal Days" Email

**Date:** 2026-06-26 | **From:** Kohls@s.kohls.com | **QA Pass Rate:** 33%

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is built on a sound legacy HTML4 table layout with appropriate MSO/VML conditionals, but has meaningful compliance gaps (missing physical address, unverified unsubscribe headers) and widespread missing alt text on tracking pixels and content images. SPF/DKIM authentication status cannot be confirmed through the relay.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **22 tracking links skipped** by HTTP probe — all routed through `click.chp.kohls.com` and `click.s.kohls.com` redirect domains. Individual link destinations and HTTP status codes could not be validated.
- Three distinct tracking/pixel systems active simultaneously:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com` (CoherentPath click tracker)
  - `mi.kohls.com` (Marigold/Sailthru open pixel + rendered-path images)
  - `kohls.demdex.net` (Adobe Audience Manager segment sync pixel, `d_sid=13245196`)
- **Malformed HTML tag** at line containing the open-tracker: `</custom>` is an invalid closing tag immediately after the `<img>` for `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx`. This is a stray/orphaned tag that will produce a parse error in strict renderers.

```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>
```

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Alt text missing on 11 images**, including non-decorative content images:

| Image | Domain | Issue |
|---|---|---|
| `1e48fb9f...` (CoherentPath pixel) | `click.chp.kohls.com` | Missing alt |
| `o.gif` (open pixel) | `mi.kohls.com` | Missing alt |
| `event` (AAM pixel) | `kohls.demdex.net` | Missing alt; `width="0" height="0"` but no `aria-hidden` |
| `2a5adc5a...png` through `916d6fa0...png` (6 content images) | `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/` | Missing alt on what appear to be product/promo images |

The open-pixel `o.gif` has `aria-hidden="true"` — correct. The AAM pixel (`kohls.demdex.net/event`) and CoherentPath pixel lack both `alt=""` and `aria-hidden`, which is a WCAG 2.1 failure for decorative images used as tracking beacons.

**`<meta name="viewport" content="..., user-scalable=0">`** — disabling user scaling is an accessibility violation (WCAG 1.4.4 Resize Text) and flagged by modern accessibility auditors.

**`<title></title>`** — empty document title. Screen readers announce this; it should be set to match the subject line or a meaningful label.

**Google Fonts loaded three times** (two `<link>` tags + two `@import` in `<style>`) for the same families (Roboto, Montserrat). This is redundant and adds two unnecessary round-trips in webmail clients that honor external CSS.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens visible in the truncated HTML. The `mi_u=604230016` user ID token in Marigold pixel URLs appears correctly substituted. The rendered-path image URLs include `email_name=260623_DG_Email_Tue_PM` — consistent with a June 23 send, not the June 26 date on this audit; confirm this is the correct email artifact.

### 5. Compliance

| Check | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `List-Unsubscribe` header | **WARN** | Not captured by AgentMail relay — cannot confirm presence |
| `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) | **WARN** | Not detected — one-click unsubscribe (Gmail/Apple Mail requirement since Feb 2024) likely non-functional |
| Physical mailing address | **FAIL** | Not detected in truncated HTML — CAN-SPAM §7(a)(5) requires a valid physical postal address |
| `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` | Pass | Present |
| `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` | Pass | Present |

The missing `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header is the highest-priority compliance issue: Gmail and Apple Mail suppress the one-click unsubscribe UI without it, increasing spam complaints. This is enforced for bulk senders (>5k/day) since February 2024.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

UTM parameters cannot be confirmed on destination URLs because all 22 links route through `click.chp.kohls.com` redirect chains that were not probed. The email name token in Marigold image URLs (`email_name=260623_DG_Email_Tue_PM`) uses an internal campaign label, not a standardized UTM value — if this feeds analytics it will appear as a raw internal string rather than a mapped campaign name.

---

### 7. Recommendations

**P0 — Fix before next send:**
1. **Add physical mailing address** to footer HTML. Required by CAN-SPAM; its absence is the most direct legal exposure.
2. **Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers** are set at the ESP/MTA layer (`kohls.com` sending infra). If the AgentMail relay strips them, test raw headers via a seed address outside the relay.
3. **Remove `</custom>` stray tag** after the `click.s.kohls.com` open-tracker `<img>`.

**P1 — Fix in next revision cycle:**
4. **Add `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"`** to all tracking pixels (CoherentPath, AAM). For content images (`mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png`), add descriptive alt text.
5. **Remove `user-scalable=0`** from the viewport meta tag.
6. **Set a non-empty `<title>`** (e.g., `Kohl's Deal Days — Daily Deals + Free Shipping`).
7. **Deduplicate Google Fonts loading** — keep one `<link>` per family, remove the redundant `@import` block.

**P2 — Verify/monitor:**
8. Confirm the `email_name=260623_DG_Email_Tue_PM` date token matches the actual send date (discrepancy vs. 2026-06-26 audit date).
