# Take 20% off + earn Kohl's Cash starting today 🤑
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-24**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so "Friends & Family" banner, "KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER" status right up top — at least they know who I am this time. But then it's straight into denim, backpacks, a slow cooker, bath towels, and a memory foam mattress topper before I hit the coupon barcode at the bottom. That's not a "Friends & Family" event, that's the whole store dumped into one email.
- This is the seventh straight day this brand has hit my inbox — 8/19 through 8/23 was five back-to-school sends with the same locker-kid hero, yesterday (8/23) was the Kohl's Cash home-goods refresh, and today they've swapped in Levi's denim and a "Denim Deal Days" banner. Credit where due: the creative finally moved. But "20% off + Kohl's Cash" is the exact same math as yesterday's "20% off + Kohl's Cash" send — they just changed the wallpaper again.
- Would I click? Maybe on the Levi's block, since that's an actual recognizable brand at a real discount (up to 30% off). Would I forward it? No — there's nothing here that says "this was made for you," it's a catalog dump. Straight to read-and-archive.

## What's working

- The "Friends & Family — Take an extra 20% off with coupon" banner is bold, blue, and unmissable at the top — I don't have to hunt for the offer.
- Kohl's Rewards member status is called out immediately ("Status: KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER") — small thing, but it's a step above Sephora's "you have 0 points" gut-punch I keep getting hit with.
- The Levi's block is the strongest single moment — recognizable brand, "Up to 30% off," clean two-image layout, easy Shop Now button.
- The coupon code and expiration ("Code: SHOPPERS PIN 9355, Ends Aug. 30") is spelled out plainly near the bottom, not buried in six-point fine print like Macy's "confirm now" email did.

## What's weak

- This is category overload on a Macy's-level scale — denim, back-to-school, a sweepstakes, a Ninja Slushi machine, bath towels, jeans (again), and a mattress topper, all in one scroll. Neiman's "1,000+ designer arrivals" email at least stayed in its lane; this one has no lane.
- The "Denim Deal Days" hero pricing is confusing — "Starting at $17.59" stacked against crossed-out $29.99/$49.99 with tiny "with 20% off coupon" text is a lot of math to do before my coffee.
- The back-to-school block (Girls/Juniors/Shoes/Boys/Young Men/Backpacks) is the same beat they've run all week — I don't have kids in school, so that's another dead zone I have to scroll past.
- By the time I reach "Picked with you in mind," it's not picked with me in mind at all — a frozen drink maker, bath towels, and a mattress topper is just merchandising filler with a personalization label slapped on it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick ONE hero — either Denim Deal Days or the Friends & Family 20% off — and build the whole top fold around it instead of stacking two competing headlines. Levi's earns its spot; the rest should move below a fold-break labeled "more ways to save."
- 2. Cut the back-to-school category grid entirely from a Friends & Family send — that's a different campaign, and running it for the seventh day straight (8/19–8/24) is fatigue, not strategy.
- 3. Move the coupon code and expiration date up near the hero, not buried after a dozen product blocks — I shouldn't have to scroll past a slushi machine to find out my code expires Aug. 30.
- 4. Drop "Picked with you in mind" as a label unless it's actually personalized — a mattress topper and bath towels next to size-8 jeans isn't a taste profile, it's a random assortment.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
This is an ESP-templated (Salesforce Marketing Cloud–style) HTML email from Kohl's using the `s.kohls.com`/`chp.kohls.com` tracking domains; core markup is boilerplate MSO/Outlook-safe code, but automated QA flags gaps in unsubscribe headers, sender authentication visibility, and alt-text coverage on tracking pixels.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 71 tracking/click-redirect links (`click.chp.kohls.com`, `click.s.kohls.com`) were skipped by the automated HTTP probe — destination validity for the actual offer CTAs is unconfirmed.
- Multiple tracking/beacon endpoints fire on load: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/1b08a894-...` (1×1 px), `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif` (open pixel), `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` (secondary open tracker), and `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager/Demdex beacon). Triple-redundant open tracking (3 separate open pixels/beacons) is present but not itself a defect.
- Malformed markup: the Demdex-adjacent block closes with a stray `</custom>` tag that has no matching opening tag (`<div style="font-size:0; line-height:0;"> <img ... width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom> </div>`), which is invalid HTML and may cause unpredictable parsing in strict renderers.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 8 tracking/beacon images flagged with missing `alt` attributes: the `click.chp.kohls.com/o/1b08a894-...` pixel, `mi.kohls.com/.../o.gif`, `kohls.demdex.net/event`, and five `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` retargeting pixels. These are non-content 1×1 pixels, so the accessibility impact on screen readers is minimal, but best practice is `alt=""` explicitly (some sibling pixels in this same email already do this correctly, e.g. `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` img has `alt=""`).
