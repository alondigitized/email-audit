# ✨ Sephora App Fest ✨
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-24**
## Executive summary

- Well, "0 points" is right up top again — "INSIDER · You have 0 points" — same as the 8/16, 8/19, and 8/20 sends. That's now four emails in nine days telling me I've got nothing to show for whatever tier I'm in, and it's stopped being an oversight and started being a pattern I notice on sight.
- That said, App Fest itself is a legitimately well-built calendar — Tarte and Dyson on Tuesday, Anastasia Beverly Hills and Make Up For Ever Wednesday, Afterpay stacking Thursday, Charlotte Tilbury Friday. It's the most organized "deals drop daily" layout Sephora's run all month, and the day-by-day black circles are genuinely easy to scan on my phone without zooming. But it's an app-exclusive event dressed up as an email — three separate "24-HOUR APP EARLY ACCESS" callouts and a "Beauty Insider Challenge... on the app" CTA. I read email, I shop counter and in-store; I'm not opening the app for this.
- I'd skim it, maybe screenshot the Charlotte Tilbury day for myself, and move on. Not forwarding — nothing here is a friend-worthy find, it's a house-brand calendar. Neiman's "Triple Points on Brunello Cucinelli" (8/24, 8/10) still beats this cold because it named a brand I'd actually swipe my card for and made the point value legible in one line.

## What's working

- The Tuesday–Friday deal calendar is clean and genuinely scannable — brand names in the circle graphics do the work without me reading paragraphs.
- Charlotte Tilbury's Airbrush Flawless Loose Powder gets a clear, single-product hero on Friday — that's the one card I'd actually pause on.
- "Stacks with other offers" is called out twice, which is the kind of honest mechanic language Macy's "confirm now" games (7/10, 8/22) never bother with.
- The "Beauty Under $25" module at the bottom is a nice low-friction add for someone not ready to commit to a $75+ trial-size splurge.

## What's weak

- "0 points" banner, fourth time in two weeks — at this point it reads as a standing insult to my balance, not a feature.
- Nearly everything of value is gated to the app — "24-HOUR APP EARLY ACCESS" three separate times, plus the Beauty Insider bonus points challenge is "only on the app." I'm an email/in-store shopper; this whole email is a pitch for a channel I don't use.
- The trial-size hero up top (Medik8 retinal serum, AESTURA moisturizer, Ultra Violette sunscreen) needs a $30 purchase to unlock and is also "online only" — another exclusion stacked on an exclusion.
- Visual hierarchy is scattered — trial size hero, then APP FEST banner, then daily deals, then points challenge, then two more shopping modules. By the time I hit "Spot it. Shop it." I've forgotten what the actual headline offer was.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop gating the marquee offer (500 bonus points) behind app-only — or at minimum lead with an email-native offer first and treat the app challenge as a secondary CTA, not the whole email's spine.
- 2. Fix the loyalty math — either show my actual points balance or drop the insider bar entirely on send four; "0 points" repeated this often reads as broken personalization, not a nudge.
- 3. Pull the Tuesday–Friday calendar higher, above the trial-size module — it's the strongest, clearest content in the email and it's currently buried past two banners.
- 4. Add one specific % or $ off number to the hero instead of "Choose a Trial Size" — I don't know what I'm saving until I click.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Beauty Insider bonus is waiting (App Fest week)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New deals drop daily through Friday — no app required for most`
