# Join InCircle & unlock benefits as you shop​
**Score:** 10/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Email six from Neiman Marcus in under 48 hours. I want to say that clearly before I say anything else, because it's the frame through which I'm reading everything. Yesterday's Designer Sale — 50% off, The Row, Louboutin — earned its spot in my inbox. That email was sharp. This one arrives into the tail end of a fatigue spiral, and NM doesn't seem to know or care.
- Which is a genuine shame, because InCircle recruitment is actually the *right* email to send me. I don't buy anything without points back — it's a non-negotiable. The benefits ladder is legible, the model is a composed woman who looks like she could be my age and not an Instagram filter, and "Apply Now" is a clear CTA. On a different day — say, the day after I clicked through the Designer Sale — this could have moved me. Today, it reads like two teams at NM running separate playbooks without talking to each other.
- Would I click? Maybe "Sign In" if I already hold a card and want to check my balance. Not "Apply Now" — that's a credit application, and I'm not handing over my Social Security number on email six of a 48-hour batch. Mark as read. Don't forward.

## What's working

- **Model age and energy**: A poised woman in deep red — not a 24-year-old influencer, not a logo-fest. Sephora's last expert email could have been aimed at literally anyone. This one signals it knows its customer.
- **Concrete earn rate**: "$100 Point Card for every 10,000 points earned" is a specific number tied to a specific reward. Macy's "extra 25% off confirmation" email last week never told me what triggered it or what I'd actually get. This does.
- **Circle tier legibility**: Benefits organized by Circle 1 / 3 / 5 with short descriptors — I can read the ladder in 15 seconds and understand what my spend level earns me.
- **Dual-audience respect**: The "Already an InCircle Member? Sign In" block acknowledges that some of us hold the card already. It's imperfect segmentation but better than pretending everyone on the list is a stranger.
- **Clean, bug-free render**: No overlapping text, no broken images, no lorem ipsum. That's table stakes, but it passes.

## What's weak

- **Six emails in 48 hours**: The sixth send does not exist independently. Every email in that queue diminishes the one before it, and NM has now burned through the goodwill from its own Designer Sale send.
- **"Apply Now" is a high-friction ask at peak fatigue**: Credit card application means a hard inquiry, personal data, a new account. That's not a Tuesday afternoon tap-through — and NM is asking for it when I'm most likely to scroll past.
- **"Discover More" as secondary CTA**: A verb and a vague noun. What am I discovering? Kohl's does this too — "shop now" into a generic landing page. "See All Circle Tiers" or "What Circle 5 Unlocks" would give me a reason to click *that* button specifically.
- **No urgency, no hook**: The Designer Sale had a reason to act *today*. This doesn't. "Apply Now" can always wait until next week, next month, or never.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Trigger on behavior, not a calendar blast**: This email should fire when I've clicked a high-intent send — the Designer Sale, the services email — and haven't signed in as a member. Batch-sending it as email six is leaving conversion on the table.
- 2. **Pair the ask with an earn incentive**: Nordstrom doesn't ask me to apply for the Nordstrom card cold — they attach a "earn double points on your first purchase by May 25" carrot. Give me a time-bounded reason to act right now.
- 3. **Replace "Discover More" with tier-specific language**: "See What Circle 5 Unlocks" makes me feel like I'm close to something. "Discover More" tells me nothing and goes nowhere useful.
- 4. **Segment the CTA**: If I'm already in the loyalty database at any circle level, I should receive the "Check Your Status" version of this email — not the "Apply Now" version. Sending both CTAs to one list is awkward and dilutes the goal.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Points. Free shipping. Concierge. Here's what InCircle gives you.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `$100 back for every 10,000 points. See the full circle tiers.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Apply by May 25 — earn double points on your first purchase.`

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is structurally sound but carries three compliance warnings from the relay layer (missing `List-Unsubscribe` headers, unknown DKIM/SPF status) and two AMPscript variable defects that could silently break identity tracking and campaign attribution.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**36 click-redirect links skipped by QA probe** — destination URLs and UTM parameters could not be verified. This is expected for `e.neimanmarcus.com` click-tracking redirects, but means no confirmation that:
- Links resolve to live pages
- UTM parameters are correctly formed on all 36 links

**`@refid` is a no-op CONCAT:**
```
@refid = CONCAT(@base_refid,"")
```
Concatenating `@base_refid` with an empty string produces the same value as `@base_refid` alone. If this variable is injected into link query strings as a campaign suffix, the suffix is blank. Likely a template placeholder where a string literal (e.g., `"-incircle"`) was never inserted.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Nested `@media` block (invalid CSS):**
```css
@media only screen and (max-device-width: 480px), screen and (max-width: 480px) {
  ...
  @media only screen and (max-device-width: 520px), screen and (max-width: 520px) {
    .ImgSize2x2 { ... }
    .TxtSize2x2 { ... }
  }
}
```
Nested media queries are not valid CSS and are not supported by any email client renderer. The `.ImgSize2x2` / `.TxtSize2x2` responsive rules will never fire. Any 2×2 product grid will render at desktop dimensions on narrow viewports.

**Three commented-out `<meta name="viewport">` tags** — template drift artifact, no functional impact but indicates the template was assembled from multiple sources without cleanup.

**`<title>` has a trailing space:** `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` — minor, but visible in browser tab previews for webmail clients.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**`@user` / SHA256 hash is disabled:**
```amscript
<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */-->
```
The comment wrapper `<!--/* ... */-->` means this line is never executed. If `@user` is used downstream in any link parameter (e.g., for onsite identity resolution or retargeting pixel matching), it will render as an empty string or throw an AMPscript runtime error depending on usage context.

**`@emailid` lookup is defined but depends on `@user`** — if `@user` is blank, any lookup keyed off it will silently return null.
