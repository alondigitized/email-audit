# 10% off ends TOMORROW ⚠️Don’t miss out, Rosie!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- Another Sephora email, and I've lost count. The subject says "10% off ends TOMORROW," but the top of the email is a countdown clock with a free-shipping code (SPEEDY) and a 30% banner next to the 10% Insider banner. Four different offers are competing before I see a single product. Then the hero turns out to be "Holiday Value Sets," which has nothing to do with the subject.
- The product grid is decent. I like seeing Charlotte Tilbury, Summer Fridays and Laneige. But it's a long scroll, and the real fine print and barcodes sit at the bottom. I'd skim it, maybe tap the value sets, and not forward it. I'd mark it read.
- Compared with Nordstrom or Saks, this is the shouting kind of email. Neiman Marcus sent eight in a few days and at least kept one clean promo-code ladder. Sephora gives me four codes in one screen.

## What's working

- The countdown timer and "FINAL HOURS!" fit the subject's "ends tomorrow," so the urgency feels credible.
- The product grid has names and prices I can read at a glance, like the $32 Charlotte Tilbury set.
- Charlotte Tilbury and Laneige are brands I actually shop. The "Only at Sephora" tags are a real reason to buy here and not at Macy's.
- The Insider tier is visible (10% for Insiders, 30% for Rouge), so I can see where I stand.
- The barcodes at the bottom let me use the offer in store, which suits someone who buys at the brand event.

## What's weak

- Four competing offers, ALLONSALE, SPEEDY, UNREADY and the 10%, make me do the math myself. Macy's keeps it to one code.
- The hero is "Holiday Value Sets," not the 10% the subject promised. It's the same subject-and-body mismatch I flagged on 11/4 and 11/7.
- It's the 25th-ish Sephora email this week. Yesterday's was also an "ends tomorrow" 10% send, so the urgency feels manufactured.
- I'm called "Rosie," which isn't my name. That's a personalization miss.
- The fine print is a wall of tiny text, and the exclusions run long. It doesn't feel honest.
- Nothing in it is aimed at a 47-year-old. There's no skincare or anti-aging angle, and the gift guide is generic.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer, the 10% Insider discount with its end date, and move shipping and gift-with-purchase codes below the fold.
- 2. Make the hero match the subject, for example a "Last day for 10% off" banner over the best-selling sets.
- 3. Fix the name merge or drop the name.
- 4. Cut the cadence. One email every two days with a clear reason beats daily near-repeats.
- 5. Add a skincare and prestige-brand row so the grid speaks to me.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Coverage gap:** 12 material links were probed and 36 were skipped. The QA data reports no failures among the probed links, but 36 links are unverified.
- **Redirect wrapping:** Links go through opaque tracked redirects, for example `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a1bba84998beadfff4bbcfb648/.../?__dU__=...`. Final destinations can't be checked from the truncated source.
- **Tracking pixels:** There are two.
  - A hidden pixel at `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...`, styled `display:none; max-height:0; mso-hide:all`.

- **Missing alt on both pixels:** Both fail the QA alt-text check. Neither is user-visible, but a screen reader or an images-off client may announce the raw filename or URL.
- **JSON-LD block:** It declares `"@type": "DiscountOffer"` with `"discountCode": ""` and a bare `"@type": "PromotionCard"`. The empty code and the missing fields mean the markup carries no usable data. It is also loaded over `http://schema.org/`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Fixed-width wrapper:** The outer table is `width="700"` with `style="min-width:700px; width:700px"`. The visible `@media (max-width:480px)` block does not override it. Without an override elsewhere (the source is truncated), this forces horizontal scroll on mobile.
- **Malformed CSS in the media query:** The block contains `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {...} <![endif]-->` inside the `@media` rule. HTML comment and conditional syntax is not valid CSS there. Some parsers may drop the rules that follow it: `.mobileHeaderTierRouge`, `.mobileHeaderTierVIB`, `.mobileHeaderTierBI`, `.mobileIcon`, `.mobileBelong`, `.footerBanner` and `.mobileDisc`.
- **Plain-text fallback:** [FAIL] The text version is 0 chars. Text-only clients, some spam filters and accessibility tools get nothing. Missing plain text is also a deliverability signal.
- **Alt text:** The two pixels above are the only images flagged. Add `alt=""` to both.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` uses an invalid value (it should be `noindex`). It has no effect in email clients.
