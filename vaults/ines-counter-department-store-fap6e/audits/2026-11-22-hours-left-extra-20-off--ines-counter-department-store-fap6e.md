# HOURS LEFT: Extra 20% off ⏰
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again, and I've stopped counting. This is the ninth or tenth send since the weekend, after "ENDS TONIGHT⌛️" and "Ends today: 20% off 👀" on 11/21, and "Take an extra 20% off 🙌" on 11/20. The subject says "HOURS LEFT," but the banner at the top of the email reads "STARTS TOMORROW." So which is it? When a brand contradicts its own urgency inside one email, I stop believing any of it.
- One caveat: the render is a very long, narrow image, and I can read headlines, big percentages and prices but not the fine print. What I can read is a lot of tiles, a lot of "up to 60%," and a "Kohl's Cash" banner. It's a wall of merchandise.
- Would I click? Probably not today. I'd mark it read. I wouldn't forward it, because there's nothing here I'd tell a friend about. A Kohl's Cash or Rewards email I'd keep only if I were already planning a Kohl's run.

## What's working

- The Super Saturday hero with "Extra 20%" is large and easy to read, so the headline offer lands.
- The Kohl's Cash banner near the top gives a spend-and-get-back mechanic. That's the kind of points-back signal I respond to.
- The Kohl's Rewards callouts ("$10 off your home purchase of $50 or more") are repeated down the page, so member perks are visible.
- Some tiles show real prices, like the $6.99 and $19.99 ones, and I can tell what's being sold.
- I see no broken images or overlapping text in the render.

## What's weak

- "STARTS TOMORROW" up top contradicts "HOURS LEFT" in the subject. That's a credibility problem.
- It's a long grid of about 15 category tiles, with no single hero and no clear path. Macy's does this too, but Sephora's Cyber Week email at least opens on one number, "30% OFF Benefit Cosmetics."
- There's no beauty in the visible tiles. I see towels, vacuums, boots, pajamas, headphones and a slow cooker. I'm a counter regular, so nothing here is mine.
- The "Coupons do not apply" and "select styles" notes sit under almost every tile. That reads as exclusion language and undercuts the "extra 20%" headline.
- The cadence is exhausting. "Ends today" has now ended several times this week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Send fewer emails, and make the deadline true. If the sale starts tomorrow, don't say "HOURS LEFT."
- 2. Lead with one hero and one CTA. Neiman Marcus's "Etro for the holidays" email worked because it asked me to look at one thing. Cut the 15 tiles to three or four.
- 3. Put the Kohl's Cash and Rewards math in one clear line near the top: spend X, get Y, redeem by this date.
- 4. Add a beauty or Sephora-at-Kohl's tile, since that's my category. Right now nothing here is aimed at me.
- 5. Move the exclusion language up front, so the "extra 20%" doesn't feel like bait.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud and Movable Ink send. The HTML source has several markup and accessibility defects: an empty `<title>`, zoom-blocking viewport settings, a stray `</custom>` tag, and missing alt text on content images. The compliance checks (unsubscribe headers, physical address, SPF/DKIM) are inconclusive, because the source is truncated and the relay may be stripping headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **79 tracking links were not probed.** They sit on click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and expiry are unverified. Examples: `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…`, `click.chp.kohls.com/o/…`.
- **Four separate open or tracking pixels** load before the content:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/34a2d398-…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager, third party)
- **Malformed markup on the SFMC open pixel:** `<img … width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>` has an unmatched closing tag, `</custom>`.
- **Possible empty dynamic parameter:** one Movable Ink image URL has `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` with no value (`…916d6fa08d185233.png?…&mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_p…`). In an "HOURS LEFT" send, a blank end-date parameter could mean the countdown or offer-end image renders without its date. Confirm this by rendering the image, because the URL is truncated.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **The `<title>` is empty** (`<title></title>`).
- **The viewport meta disables zoom:** `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` in `<meta name="viewport">`. This fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) in clients that honor it.
- **Content images are missing alt text.** The QA run flagged 34 images, and the 14 listed include:
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/{2a5adc5afb647aad,f441d45d2128ae53,c2f81e9ac8daee44,415c8a18fc2950ea,69494b48a79b4240,78308d1d9adb0751,916d6fa08d185233}.png`
  - `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/60699446_4_2x`
  - `…60699446_3_2x`
  
  A missing `alt` attribute (as opposed to `alt=""`) makes screen readers announce the filename or URL. If these images carry the offer or product text, that text is lost when images are blocked.
- **Tracking pixels are inconsistent.** `click.chp.kohls.com/o/…` has no `alt` or `aria-hidden` and sits outside any hidden container. `kohls.demdex.net/event` has `width="0" height="0"` and no `alt`. Only the `mi.kohls.com` `o.gif` has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`.
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` is inside `<head>`, and `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href. Both are invalid and ignored by most clients, but they trigger validators.
- **Missing `role="presentation"`:** the preheader table (`<table width='640' … class="container">`) and the 1×1 `<table width="1" height="1">` don't have it, while the main layout tables do.
- **Web fonts:** Google Fonts are loaded through `<link>` and `@import`. The `font-family` stacks fall back to Arial/Helvetica, so this is low risk. Gmail and Outlook will ignore the web fonts.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source, but the HTML is truncated, so the body and footer are unchecked. Recipient-level identifiers are present in image URLs (`mi_u=604230016`), which look like Movable Ink user keys rather than broken tokens.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. Check the raw message headers at the origin.
- **Physical address not detected.** The visible HTML is truncated before the footer, so this can't be confirmed or ruled out. Verify the footer.
- **Authentication-Results is absent.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sending domain `s.kohls.com` matches the tracking domain `click.s.kohls.com`, which suggests aligned infrastructure, but that is not verified.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs:** 14,043 of 19,784 characters (~71%). This is a spam-filter risk and a poor experience for text-only readers. It comes from long `?qs=` tokens across 79 tracked links.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **No standard UTM parameters are visible** in the captured URLs. Attribution uses SFMC `qs=` tokens and Movable Ink `mi_*` parameters (`mi_ecmp=1026404_20261121`, `email_name=261121_DG_Email_Sat_PM`), which is a valid approach but opaque to site analytics.
- **`email_name` appears on some `mi.kohls.com` image URLs and not others** (for example, `69494b48a79b4240.png` and `78308d1d9adb0751.png` have only `mi_u` and `mi_ecmp`). This is inconsistent, and the parameter may not be needed on every image.
- **Landing-page alignment can't be verified.** The click-redirect probes were skipped, so the offer, "Extra 20% off" and the promo code can't be matched to the landing pages.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, using `alt=""` for decorative ones. Put `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"` on every tracking pixel.
2. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Populate `<title>`, remove the stray `</custom>`, and move or delete the `<div itemscope>` and the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon">` in `<head>`.
4. Add `role="presentation"` to the preheader table and the 1×1 table.
5. Render the image with the blank `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` and confirm the countdown or end date displays.
6. Check the raw origin headers for List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, DKIM, SPF and DMARC, then check the full footer for a physical address.
7. Run a full render and landing-page check on the 79 click-redirect links, covering destination, redirect hops and offer parity.
8. Consider consolidating the four open pixels and shortening or reducing the tracked links to lower the plain-text URL ratio.
