# 40% off + Kohl's Cash is worth a look 👀
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-17**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again. This is the same "extra 40% off, ends Oct. 18" send I've been getting all week, and it's the third one with a stacked "2 DAYS ONLY" banner. The top of the email is that red banner repeated five times, with a big 40% beside it. It reads like someone leaning on the horn. I know the deal after the first line, so I don't need it five times.
- Below the hero it turns into a catalog. There's a Pumpkin spice home section, then bedding, Halloween décor, kitchen appliances and a Season's Best Shoe Sale block. They're all "40% off + save with coupon." Nothing in it is for me. I buy beauty at the counter, and I cross-shop Nordstrom, Macy's and Saks. A slushie machine and a mattress topper don't get me to the register. I'd skim it and mark it read. I wouldn't click and I wouldn't forward it.
- I'll give them credit for one thing. The bottom half is honest. The coupon code and barcode are there, the Kohl's Cash terms are spelled out, and returns are free through January 31, 2027. That's more respect than the banner at the top shows.

## What's working

- The loyalty strip at the top shows my status and says "Earn: 5% Rewards." That's the first thing that made me feel known, and I'm a points person.
- The coupon code and barcode are visible with an "Ends Oct. 18" date. I don't have to hunt for how to redeem.
- "Take $10 off your home purchase of $50 or more with coupon" is concrete and easy to do the math on.
- "Shop now. Decide later. Free returns through January 31, 2027." is a real trust cue. I return at the brand event, so this matters to me.
- Product tiles have list price, sale price and a product photo. That's clear and shoppable.

## What's weak

- The "2 DAYS ONLY" banner is stacked five times. Macy's confirmation email at least had one clear deadline. This one shouts.
- Too many offers compete: 40%, 50%, $10 off, Kohl's Cash, a shoe sale and coupons. Sephora's Valentino email put one hero first and let the rest follow. Here nothing leads.
- Beauty is missing. I'm a beauty-counter regular, and Kohl's carries Sephora. The only mention is a fine-print line saying Kohl's Cash can't be used on Sephora. I read that as a snub.
- The "Picked with you in mind" block has a slushie maker, a towel and a mattress topper. It doesn't feel picked for me.
- Seven or more sends in ten days, and every one has the same header. Neiman Marcus has the same fatigue problem, but at least their latest send was quiet. This one isn't.
- The fine print at the bottom is tiny and dense. I can't read it on my phone without zooming.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show one banner, not five, and put one hero offer under it. One line of "40% off, ends Oct. 18" is enough.
- 2. Add a Beauty or Sephora tile. Give it real placement, not fine print, and be upfront about whether Kohl's Cash can be used on it.
- 3. Show only two or three categories and match them to what I've browsed. I'd rather see fewer blocks.
- 4. Throttle the cadence. If I've gotten this header for ten days, send the next one with a new idea.
- 5. Make the legal text readable, or link it. "Details" links are fine.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your extra 40% off ends tomorrow, Oct. 18`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day: 40% off + $10 Kohl's Cash per $50`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your code and barcode are inside. Free returns through Jan. 31.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Earn Kohl's Cash on every $50 you spend, through Oct. 25.`

## Technical Audit

1. **Technical Summary**
The Kohl's email has a standard ESP-built structure: tracking pixels, a 640px container and a mobile media query. The QA run flagged missing compliance headers, one missing physical address, and about 10 images without alt text. The HTML source was truncated, so I could not confirm the footer, the unsubscribe link, or the 65 click-tracked URLs. Several QA warnings come from the AgentMail relay and are not confirmed defects.

2. **Link & Tracking Issues**
- QA skipped 65 tracking links (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…`, `click.chp.kohls.com`) because they are click-redirect domains. I could not verify their destinations or whether they resolve.
- Four tracking or measurement beacons sit in the body:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/3d9e251a-…` (1x1, no alt)
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif` (alt="" and aria-hidden, correct)
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?FBED6YDN…` (1x1, alt="")
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager, 0x0, no alt)
- The open-pixel markup is malformed: `<img src="…open.aspx?…" alt=""></custom>` has a stray `</custom>` close tag with no matching opener. Browsers ignore it, but it is invalid HTML.
- Both Salesforce Marketing Cloud (`click.s.kohls.com`) and a second platform (`click.chp.kohls.com`, `mi.kohls.com`) track the same email. That is redundant, and the extra beacons add request overhead.

3. **Rendering & Accessibility**
- The QA run flagged 10 images with missing alt text. They include the content images `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png` and `916d6fa08d185233.png`. It also flagged `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/58379268_2_2x`. If these images carry the offer text (40% off, Kohl's Cash), the message disappears when images are blocked, and screen readers get nothing.
- `<title></title>` is empty.
- `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href, which can trigger a request to the document URL in some clients.
- `<div itemscope …><meta …></div>` sits inside `<head>`. That is invalid, and some parsers will close the head early and push the rest into the body.
- The Google Fonts `@import` and `<link>` calls are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. This is fine for Outlook, but Gmail strips them. The font stack falls back to Arial and Helvetica.
- `user-scalable=0, maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 issue.
- The `<img>` tags lack `display:block` and explicit `alt` attributes. Several rely on a global `img { vertical-align: middle }` rule.
- Mobile responsiveness is handled through a `max-width: 500px` media query. The top-level layout tables carry `role="presentation"`, but the 1x1 tracking table and the preheader table do not.

4. **Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the supplied portion. The tracking URLs carry user and campaign identifiers (`mi_u=604230016`, `d_sid=13245196`, `FBED6YDN244EFANPFPNEXDUZGI`). These are by design, but they are persistent per-user IDs embedded in image URLs.

5. **Compliance**
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) headers**: QA could not find either. Per the QA note, AgentMail may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe from bulk senders, so check the raw message headers.
- **Authentication-Results**: not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `s.kohls.com` (a subdomain) and the display name contains a curly apostrophe (`Kohl’s`), which is fine in UTF-8.
- **Physical address**: QA found none. The HTML is truncated, so the footer may hold one. Verify it against the full source, because CAN-SPAM requires it.
- I could not confirm the visible unsubscribe link either, because the footer is outside the supplied excerpt.
- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` are harmless in email.

6. **Email-to-Site Continuity**
- All visible links go through the redirectors, and QA did not probe them. I cannot verify the final landing URLs, UTM parameters or landing-page alignment.
- Tracking is carried through `mi_u`, `mi_ecmp=1024040_20261017` and `email_name=261017_DG_Email_Myster…` on the image URLs. These show a campaign ID and date but not the click-through UTM scheme.
- The `email_name` parameter appears to be truncated at `Myster…`, probably by the QA output.

7. **Recommendations**
1. Check the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Do not treat the QA warnings as defects until you have.
2. Re-run the audit on the full HTML. Confirm the footer address and unsubscribe link, and resolve a sample of the 65 redirect links to check landing URLs and UTMs.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images. Use `alt=""` on the decorative or tracking pixels that lack it.
4. Fix the stray `</custom>` tag, the empty `<title>`, the empty `rel="shortcut icon"` href, and the `<div itemscope>` inside `<head>`.
5. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
6. Review the duplicate tracking stacks (SFMC, `chp.kohls.com`, `mi.kohls.com`, demdex).
