- The product grid is genuinely well-curated — Staud, Alémais, Loewe, McQueen — this is real editorial taste, not filler.
- Clean two-column layout with designer names under each item makes it fast to scan on my phone without zooming.
- "Shop All Women's New Arrivals" and "Shop All Men's New Arrivals" buttons give me an obvious next step per section.

## What's weak

- No InCircle points, no loyalty tier language anywhere — after Wednesday's Triple Points send, this feels like a step backward for a member like me.
- No offer, no urgency, nothing time-bound — it's pure inventory, so there's no reason to act today versus next week.
- Subject line "1,000+ new arrivals" oversells volume, not desirability — Neiman's usually sells me dream over quantity, and this one flipped it.
- Compare this to Wednesday's Brunello Cucinelli send, which spoke my language instantly — this one's generically "browse everything," which is the least Neiman's-feeling of anything they've sent me lately.

- 1. Lead with a curated hero — pick 3 pieces (the Burberry bag, the Gucci heels, the Alémais dress) and build the top of the email around them instead of "1,000+ new arrivals." Quantity isn't the hook, desire is.
- 2. Add an InCircle callout — even just "Earn points on every new arrival" would tie this back to the loyalty thread Neiman's has been running all week.
- 3. Give it a reason to open now — a "just landed this morning" framing or a 48-hour early-access note for InCircle members would create real urgency without needing a discount.
- 4. Trim the assortment shown — 12 products is a lot of scrolling for zero payoff; cut to 6-8 hero pieces and let "Shop All" do the rest.
