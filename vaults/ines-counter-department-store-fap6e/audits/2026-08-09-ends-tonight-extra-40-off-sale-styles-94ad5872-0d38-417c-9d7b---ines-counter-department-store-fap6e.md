- "EXEXTRA 40% OFF" in giant white type on crimson — my eye lands there in under a second, zero hunting.
- "Ends 8/9/26 at 10:00 PM CT" in the fine print is a real, specific deadline — not vague "hurry" language, an actual clock.

## What's weak

- This is the second day in a row I've gotten this nearly identical crimson "EXTRA 40% OFF / LAST CALL" hero — the repetition is starting to register, even if the copy this time is stronger.
- "Exclusions Apply" sits right under the button in tiny type — I appreciate it's not buried in a paragraph, but it's the one honesty-adjacent flag that makes me want to check the fine print before I get excited about a specific item.
- Zero mention of Circle loyalty points or InCircle status anywhere in this email — for a brand event this urgent, I'd expect at least a nod to my member tier.
- No sizing or availability signal on any of the nine products — I have to click through blind to know if my size is even left this close to a sale deadline.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop reusing yesterday's exact hero creative — swap the crimson block treatment or at least rotate the product photography so back-to-back opens don't feel like the same email twice.
- 2. Add InCircle point value or tier callout somewhere near the CTA — even one line like "Plus, earn double points tonight" would make this feel like it's for me specifically, not a mass blast.
- 3. Put a "while supplies last" or low-stock cue near the product grid — with a hard 10PM cutoff, urgency should extend to inventory, not just time.
- 4. Tighten "Exclusions Apply" into something more specific like "Excludes Beauty & Fine Jewelry" so I'm not guessing what's carved out.
