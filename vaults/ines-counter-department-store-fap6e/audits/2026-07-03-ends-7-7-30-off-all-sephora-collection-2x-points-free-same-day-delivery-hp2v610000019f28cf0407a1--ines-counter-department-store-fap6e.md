# Ends 7/7: 30% off ALL Sephora Collection + 2X points + FREE Same-Day Delivery 📣
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-03**
## Executive summary

- This arrived two days after Sephora told me "Stacking Weekend is HERE." I already know — I have it on my radar since Sunday. But I'll be honest: the deadline nudge actually works on me when it's credible and close, and July 7 lands right. Three days is the right moment to remind a Beauty Insider member who hasn't clicked yet.
- What I'm less charmed by is the kitchen-sink energy. The subject stacks three separate value props — 30% off Sephora Collection, 2X points, free same-day delivery — before a megaphone emoji arrives to make sure I noticed. That's not speaking to me, that's shouting past me. Inside the email, the "Fan Favorites" hero is pleasant enough, the product grid is organized and colorful, but then the email keeps going: hair section, app-early-access panel, Beauty Insider banner, another product lineup. By the third scroll I've lost the thread of what this email is actually for.
- I'd click it eventually — the 2X points is the hook for someone like me, and Sephora knows its base. But I wouldn't forward this to a friend. The email works in spite of its structure, not because of it.

## What's working

- **2X points is the right loyalty lever.** I don't spend without points back — that's non-negotiable. Putting it in the subject was smart, even if it got buried between two other offers.
- **"Fan Favorites" framing is warmer than "SALE."** It signals curated bestsellers, not clearance-rack desperation. I respond to that.
- **Product tile grid is clean and well-rendered.** No broken images, the Sephora Collection products read as colorful and organized — I can actually scan what I'd be shopping.
- **Beauty Insider visibility.** The member loyalty section is present and visible — it reminds me this is a subscriber moment, not a public blast. That matters to me.
- **July 7 deadline is specific and believable.** It's in the banner, not buried in fine print.

## What's weak

- **Three stacked offers in the subject is one too many.** By the time I reach "FREE Same-Day Delivery 📣" I've already decided whether to open. The megaphone reads as a brand that doesn't trust its own offer.
- **The hair section is off-mission.** I came in for Sephora Collection — face, color, skincare. Pivoting to "HAIR'S LATEST LINEUP" mid-scroll breaks the narrative and dilutes the 30% off story.
- **The app-early-access panel is noise for existing members.** I already have the app. This real estate should be more product.
- **Product tile text is too small for mobile.** The grid is pretty but on a phone I can't read the product names without zooming — Neiman Marcus's recent "new sale styles" sends gave each item a readable callout at the tile level. This doesn't.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Collapse the subject to one hero offer.** Thirty percent off Sephora Collection is the story. Points and delivery are sweeteners — they belong in the preheader or as secondary callouts inside the body, not competing in the subject line.
- 2. **Give 2X points its own moment inside the email.** A Beauty Insider earning double points on a 30%-off day is leaving real money on the table if she skips this send. Make that the emotional argument, not a footnote.
- 3. **Cut the hair section or make it a separate send.** Stacking Weekend is a Sephora Collection event. Hair can have its own email.
- 4. **Increase product name legibility on mobile tiles.** Bigger type or a named callout under each image — I shouldn't need to pinch-zoom to know what I'm looking at.
- 5. **Retire the app recruitment panel from promo sends.** It's a retention tactic wearing a promotional costume, and it confuses the send's purpose.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Stacking Weekend reminder: 30% off + double points ends Monday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free same-day delivery too — this is the Beauty Insider weekend.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stack your savings before July 7: 30% off, double points, free delivery.`

Value is `no index` (space) instead of `noindex`. Inconsequential for email delivery but indicates a copy-paste defect in the shell template.

**Issue: `maximum-scale=1` blocks user zoom**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
Prevents users from pinching to zoom — WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) failure. Common in email templates but worth flagging for accessibility compliance posture.

**Issue: `text-size-adjust: none` applied broadly**
```css
div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td {
  -webkit-text-size-adjust: none;
  -ms-text-size-adjust: none;
  -moz-text-size-adjust: none;
  text-size-adjust: none;
}
```
Applied to every major element, not just the outer wrapper. Suppresses OS-level text scaling for users who rely on it. This is overkill; the fix should be scoped to the outermost table only.
