# Fashion Finds: Refined Knits
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth Neiman's email I've had in about 24 hours. The Beauty Event email with Triple Points and the train case was the one I wanted, and I haven't seen anything like it since. This one is a pretty, quiet grid of nine sweaters with no offer in it. It respects my time because it doesn't shout. It also gives me no reason to act today.
- It looks like a magazine page, and I do like that. I just don't shop knits at Neiman's at full price when I can wait for the sale. The "Your top styles could now be up to 40% off" send earlier today already has me watching for markdowns. Sephora would have put points or a gift in the first screen, and Macy's would have buried me in coupons. Neiman's gives me neither a reason nor a deadline.
- I'd open it, scroll once, and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it. If I did, it would only be to a friend who already shops Akris.

## What's working

- The "FASHION FINDS" masthead is calm and editorial. It reads like a brand that respects me, unlike Kohl's, which was shouting at me with a party emoji this week.
- The designer line, Bottega Veneta, Givenchy, Akris, Elie Tahari and L'Agence, tells me this is real designer access and not filler.
- The grid is clean. Nine evenly sized tiles on a grey ground make the knits easy to scan.
- The Sweaters & Knits button sits right under the headline, so the first action is obvious.
- The cream cardigan, the burgundy knit and the cape-collar sweater suit a woman my age. They look polished rather than trendy.

## What's weak

- There is no offer at all. No points, no gift, no free shipping and no markdown. After the Triple Points email this morning, that feels empty.
- None of the nine products show a price, a name or a link cue. I can't tell what the cardigan costs, so I wouldn't tap it.
- The footer has six more buttons (Contemporary again, Clothing, Shoes, Handbags, Men's, Kids'). That is a category dump, and "Shop Contemporary" appears twice.
- There is no loyalty cue anywhere. For someone who won't use a card without points back, InCircle is missing entirely.
- This is the fifth send in a day. A pretty email that's easy to ignore stacks up fast.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a loyalty hook in the header band, such as "Earn double points on knits through Sunday." That one line would move me from a pass to a click.
- 2. Add brand and price captions under each tile, for example "L'Agence cardigan, $395". Product detail is what gets me to tap.
- 3. Cut the footer to two or three buttons and drop the duplicate "Shop Contemporary."
- 4. Space out the cadence. Merge this into the sale send, or hold it for a day.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **22 tracking/click-redirect links were not probed.** The QA skipped them as tracking domains, so I can't confirm destinations or that none redirect to errors.
- **Plain-text part is 4156/4685 chars (about 89%) URLs.** This points to long, unshortened tracking URLs in the text alternative. It is a spam-filter and readability risk (QA `[WARN]`).
- No broken links are confirmed in the visible source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on clients that honor it (WCAG 1.4.4). Remove `maximum-scale=1`.
- **Template comment sits before the DOCTYPE.** Content before `<!DOCTYPE>` can push older Outlook and IE-based renderers into quirks mode. Move or strip the comment.
- **Exact-match class selectors are brittle.** The media queries use `*[class=contenttable]`, `*[class=mobile]` and `*[class=hide]`. These stop matching if an element gets a second class, and any mobile-layout breakage on multi-class elements would trace back to them.
- **Nav depends on the checkbox hack.** The hamburger menu (`.navbar-checkbox:checked~.menu`) is unsupported in Gmail and several Outlook clients. The truncated source doesn't show whether the nav links are also reachable without it, so I can't confirm a fallback.
- **Dead CSS in the head.** Several commented-out viewport variants remain (`width=1024`, `width=320, initial-scale=0.29`). This is harmless but adds weight.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens are visible in the truncated source.
- **Internal template documentation shipped in the HTML.** The leading comment lists AMPscript variables (`@email`, `@base_refid`, `@refid`, `@uEm`, `@cmdid`) and Data Extension names (`NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`). It is invisible to readers but exposes internal data-model names to anyone viewing source, and it contains an unselected "CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO" block. Strip comments at send time.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
