# Save 20%  | Bring home a few new things 🏡
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-12**
## Executive summary

- Fifty-two. That is my count of Kohl's emails since Memorial Day, and this is the second one to land in my inbox today — the same Saturday. I know Kohl's. I have the app, the card, and a running tab at the Sephora inside the store. None of that is in this email. What I got instead: a rug, some throw pillows, a back-to-school section for children I do not have, and a 20%-off mechanic I have seen every single day this week. The subject line could have been swapped with the one from July 10th and I would not have noticed.
- I did not click. The Kohl's Cash deadline buried in the footer — through July 14th — was the only thing that gave me pause, and even then I had to go looking for it. Compare that to Sephora's "Obsessed? Same." from a few days ago: terrible subject line, but once I was inside, the brand gave me a feeling. This gives me a clearance rack.
- Mark as read. Not forwarding.

## What's working

- The 20%-off coupon is surfaced in the very first visible row — I do not have to hunt for the offer.
- Kohl's Cash earning window (through July 14) is explicitly called out at the bottom — that is real currency I track.
- The Kohl's Rewards and Kohl's Card badges at the footer at least acknowledge I'm a member, not a cold stranger.
- No render bugs — the email displays cleanly, all images loaded, no text overlap. After the broken-layout disasters I've seen from smaller brands, clean is worth noting.
- "Shop Home" and "Shop Back to School" CTAs are legible and tappable, no squinting required.

## What's weak

- Zero beauty content — not a single Sephora at Kohl's mention in any hero or section, even though that is exactly why I'm on this list.
- Two Kohl's emails in one day is two. I got the "Find must-have looks for a stylish summer" email this morning; this one feels like a pocket-dial.
- The hero copy "Bring home a few new things" is aggressively generic — it tells me nothing new, nothing urgent, nothing personal.
- The back-to-school section (boys, girls, juniors, backpacks) is visually dominant in the lower half and completely irrelevant to a 47-year-old with no school-age kids. Kohl's has my purchase history. This is lazy segmentation.
- Four distinct sections competing for attention — home, kids' clothes, bedding/bath, back-to-school — means I land on none of them. Neiman Marcus sent me one email last week, led with one Saint Laurent bag, and I knew exactly why I was there.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Give me a Sephora at Kohl's section.** One hero image — a new launch, a weekend exclusive, a beauty deal that earns Kohl's Cash — and this email becomes directly relevant to me. Kohl's literally has a Sephora inside the store. Use it in the email.
- 2. **Pick one audience and one category.** Home shopper *or* back-to-school parent, not both. Macy's runs fat emails but at least the top third of the send knows what it is. This email doesn't know what it is.
- 3. **Move the July 14 Kohl's Cash deadline to the subject line.** That is a real, time-bounded reason to act today instead of tomorrow. It is doing nothing in the footer. Sephora would have made that the whole subject.
- 4. **Rest the 20%-off subject line.** It has appeared in every Kohl's subject since Monday. It is wallpaper now. Try a product angle or a benefits angle instead.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "Save 20% | Bring home a few new things" (2026-07-12)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is structurally standard for a large ESP send (MessageGears + CoherentPath) but carries three compliance gaps (unsubscribe headers, physical address, SPF/DKIM unknown), a malformed HTML tag, 11 images missing alt text, and a redundant font-loading pattern. No unfired merge tokens detected in the available source.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Malformed closing tag — `</custom>`**
The open-pixel div block terminates with an invalid `</custom>` tag:
```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?CTK3JN5KKZ7UZNAEDKARVUFHUY...">
</custom>
```
`<custom>` is not a valid HTML element. Most clients will silently ignore it, but it signals a template injection bug and could break in stricter parsers.

**Triple open-tracking pixels**
Three separate open-measurement pixels fire on render:
- `click.chp.kohls.com/o/110da464...` — CoherentPath pixel (missing alt)
- `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbb.../o.gif` — MessageGears pixel (has `aria-hidden="true"`, correct)
- `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?CTK3JN5KKZ7...` — secondary ESP pixel (missing alt)

Plus an Adobe Audience Manager sync pixel: `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`.

Four pixels for one email is higher than typical; each adds latency and a potential block by privacy-filtering clients.

**22 click-redirect links unverified**
All outbound links route through `click.s.kohls.com`. QA probing was skipped for all 22. Landing page UTM alignment is unconfirmed (see §6).

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**11 images missing `alt` text** (QA confirmed):
Includes three tracking pixels and eight content images served from `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/`. Decorative pixels should have `alt=""` explicitly; content images require descriptive alt text for screen readers and image-blocked clients.

Notable: the `o.gif` pixel correctly has `aria-hidden="true"` — the others do not.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />
```
Disabling user zoom is an WCAG 1.4.4 failure and prohibited by iOS accessibility guidelines.

**Empty `<title>` tag**
```html
<title></title>
```
Some clients (Outlook desktop, certain webmail) surface this as window/tab title or as supplementary preview text. Should be set to a meaningful value or a non-rendering whitespace string.

**`<div itemscope>` placed inside `<head>`**
The Schema.org Organization block is injected inside `<head>`, which is invalid HTML. It belongs in `<body>`.
```html
<head>
  ...
  <div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">...</div>
</head>
```

**Redundant Google Fonts loading**
Roboto and Montserrat are each loaded twice — once via `<link>` and once via `@import` — causing two HTTP round-trips per font per client that supports external CSS:
```html
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Roboto...">
<style>@import url("https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Roboto...");</style>
```
Remove the `@import` block; the `<link>` tags are sufficient.

### 5. Compliance

**[HIGH] `List-Unsubscribe` header absent**
QA confirms the header was not captured. Gmail and Yahoo have enforced `List-Unsubscribe` + one-click `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) since February 2024 for bulk senders. Missing headers risk placement in spam or the Promotions tab with no unsubscribe affordance shown.
- Expected: `List-Unsubscribe: <mailto:...>, <https://...>`
- Expected: `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`

Note: these may be present at the SMTP layer but not relayed through the AgentMail capture path — verify directly against raw headers from a delivered copy.

**[HIGH] No physical mailing address detected**
CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid physical postal address in the email body. The HTML is truncated and the footer may contain it, but QA did not detect it. Confirm it is present in the full render.

**[MEDIUM] SPF/DKIM/DMARC status unknown**
`Authentication-Results` header not captured by relay. Sending domain is `s.kohls.com`; authentication posture for this subdomain is unverified in this audit pass.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All 22 click links route through `click.s.kohls.com` (ESP click-tracker), which should append or preserve UTM parameters before redirecting to `kohls.com`. Because QA probing was skipped for all redirect links, final landing page UTM values (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content`) could not be verified.

The `email_name=260712_DG_Email_Sun_PM` convention in image-src params suggests a campaign naming standard exists; confirm it propagates to click-through UTMs for analytics continuity.

**Action required:** Manually follow one CTA link through the redirect chain and inspect the final URL for UTM params.
