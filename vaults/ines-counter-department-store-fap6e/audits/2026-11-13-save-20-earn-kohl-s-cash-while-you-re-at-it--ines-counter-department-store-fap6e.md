# Save 20% | Earn Kohl's Cash while you're at it 💸
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-13**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again. This is the fifth or sixth send since 11/10, and the one from earlier today ("Save an extra 20% ... now's the time to shop 🛒") is still sitting in my inbox. This one is a gift-guide magazine with a coupon taped to the bottom. The top has a "Kohl's Cash" banner and a "Shop Now" button. Under that I get Santa and "The North Kohl's," gift lists for her, him and kids, and a Sephora at Kohl's tile. Then come a Door Busters preview, Klarna, DoorDash and Instacart blocks, and a product grid. The coupon barcodes are at the very bottom, and the 20% off is buried there.
- The offer I actually care about is tiny and has to be hunted for. Neiman Marcus put "40% off" in big red type and I saw it in two seconds. Here I get a long scroll before I find "Take an extra 20% off" with a barcode. The "Earn $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50" is also tucked at the bottom. It's a decent offer, but the email treats me like a stranger who wants Santa.
- Would I click? Probably only the Sephora tile, because I'm curious what Kohl's does with beauty. Would I forward it? No. Mark as read.

## What's working

- The "Earn Kohl's Cash" idea is a real points-back mechanic. That's the one thing that respects how I shop.
- The "Sephora | Beauty" tile is a recognizable beauty draw, and a name I trust.
- The "Wallet" language and barcodes mean I can use the coupons in store without much trouble.
- The Door Busters "Preview Deals" block gives me a reason to come back on a specific date (Nov. 22–28).
- The status bar at the top ("Kohl's Rewards Member, Earn 5% Rewards") at least admits I'm a member.

## What's weak

- The hero is Christmas gifting. The 20% off from the subject line is not the hero.
- The scroll is very long, with Klarna, DoorDash and Instacart blocks in the middle. It feels like Macy's-style overload.
- The product grid ("Picked with you in mind") shows towels, a drink maker and a slow cooker. Nothing in it is personal to me, and the claim is a stretch.
- Fine print is everywhere. "Coupons do not apply to select brands. details" shows up right after the deals, and the Kohl's Cash dates and exclusions are small.
- The cadence is exhausting. 11/10, 11/11 (three emails), 11/12 (two) and now today.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer first: a big "20% off + earn Kohl's Cash" banner with one clear button above the Santa art.
- 2. Cut the delivery and payment blocks, or move them below the coupons.
- 3. Replace the generic "Picked with you in mind" grid with beauty or categories I've actually browsed.
- 4. Send one consolidated promo per week instead of near-duplicates.
- **Subject Alt A:** `20% off + $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your extra 20% off ends Nov. 25`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus $5 Kohl's Cash on orders $40+ with same-day pickup.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your coupons are in your wallet. Gift lists inside.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Kohl's "Save 20% | Earn Kohl's Cash while you're at it 💸"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is mostly standard, but it has several parsing and accessibility defects: an empty `<title>`, a stray `</custom>` tag, and a `<div>` inside `<head>`. QA flagged 10 images with missing alt text, and the compliance and authentication checks couldn't be confirmed. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer and most links were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **75 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination validity and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Duplicate open tracking.** Two open pixels fire independently:
  - `https://click.chp.kohls.com/o/ea27cf18-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
  - `https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=60272&bmt=0`
- **Two further third-party beacons.** `https://mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&…` and `https://kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` fire on image load.
- **Recipient identifier in URLs.** `mi_u=604230016` appears in every `mi.kohls.com` image URL.
- **Link structure.** Visible links use opaque `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…` redirects. Their destinations can't be seen in the source.
- **Zero-size `demdex` pixel.** It uses `width="0" height="0"`, and some clients drop zero-dimension images.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, 10 images flagged by QA.**
  - The seven `https://mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` images (`2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`) are likely content images. If so, image-blocking clients and screen readers will get no content from them.
  - The three tracking pixels (`click.chp.kohls.com/o/…`, `o.gif`, `kohls.demdex.net/event`) are also flagged. The alt flag on `o.gif` looks like a false positive, since the source shows `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`. The `click.chp` and `demdex` pixels genuinely lack `alt`. Neither matters for accessibility, but `alt=""` is cleaner.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0>` prevents pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Malformed markup.**
  - `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx…" alt="">` is followed by a stray `</custom>` closing tag.
  - `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will close `<head>` early at that point, and the `<meta itemprop>` tags then land in `<body>`.
- **Empty icon link.** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href.
- **Web fonts.** Montserrat and Roboto load via `@import` and `<link>` from `fonts.googleapis.com`, which Gmail and Outlook generally strip. Fallbacks to Arial/Helvetica are declared, so this is low risk.
- **Doctype.** HTML 4.01 Transitional is still rendered fine by email clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved tokens** (`{{ }}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) were visible in the truncated source.
- **Possible empty personalization field.** One image URL ends `…mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_p…`. The `mi_kc_redm_end_dte` (Kohl's Cash redemption end date) value is empty, while the subject promotes "Earn Kohl's Cash". This could be legitimate for a recipient with no Kohl's Cash, but a Movable Ink dynamic image may render fallback or blank content. It needs a render check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found.** QA notes this may be an AgentMail relay artifact, so it is unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found (RFC 8058).** Same caveat. One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders.
- **No physical address detected** by QA. The HTML was truncated before the footer, so this can't be confirmed or ruled out from the source provided.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `s.kohls.com`.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters visible.** The visible links are opaque `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…` redirects. The only campaign identifiers in the source are `mi_ecmp=1025803_20261113` and `email_name=261113_DG_Email_Friday`, both on image URLs.
- **Destination alignment unverified.** The 75 tracking links were not followed, so landing pages and the 20% / Kohl's Cash offer cannot be checked against them.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the seven `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` content images, and `alt=""` to the `click.chp` and `demdex` pixels.
2. Add a non-empty `<title>`. Remove the stray `</custom>` and the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon" href="">`. Move the `itemscope` div out of `<head>`.
3. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Check whether `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` renders correctly when empty. Confirm the dynamic image has a sensible fallback.
5. Consolidate duplicate open pixels (`click.chp` and `open.aspx`) if both aren't required.
6. Re-run the audit with raw message headers and the full, untruncated HTML. That would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`, and the footer address.
7. Resolve a sample of the redirect links to confirm landing pages and check for UTMs on the final URLs.
