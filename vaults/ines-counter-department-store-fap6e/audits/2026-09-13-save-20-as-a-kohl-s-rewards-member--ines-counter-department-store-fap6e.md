# Save 20% as a Kohl's Rewards member 🎉
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-13**
## Executive summary

- Here's Kohl's again, my fifth this week if I count Wednesday's cozy fall refresh, Sunday's Customer Appreciation Event, and the two others whose subject lines have started to blur together. Same three-tier discount wall right at the top — 30/20/15 — with my Rewards tier called out in the middle box, which I'll give them credit for, again, because they keep doing it. But then it just sprawls: kitchen, Ninja appliances, floor care, bedding, bath, a Sephora+Kohl's 50% off beauty banner dropped in the middle of a home-goods email, then a random "picked with you in mind" grid with a towel, a slushie maker, jeans, and a mattress topper. That's not curation, that's a clearance aisle.
- The one bright spot for someone like me is the Sephora+Kohl's beauty block — 50% off select beauty, new deals daily — but it's buried two-thirds down after Ninja espresso machines and a vacuum, and it doesn't even show me a product, just a gradient banner. Compare that to Sephora's own Daily Deals send, which at least leads with actual named products even if it's overwhelming. This would get an open from habit, a skim, and a mark-as-read. Not a forward, not really a click — I'd rather go straight to Sephora's site if I want the beauty discount.

## What's working

- My Rewards tier is called out by name and highlighted in its own box, same move they made in the "Customer Appreciation" send.
- The Sephora+Kohl's 50% beauty banner is genuinely relevant to me, even buried where it is.
- Kohl's Cash callouts ($10, $40) on specific items give me a real reason to add to cart if I were already shopping.
- Clean pink/white palette, readable fonts, nothing overlapping or broken.

## What's weak

- This is the fifth near-identical discount-ladder email from Kohl's this week — the fatigue is real and I'm starting to tune it out entirely.
- The beauty offer, the one thing built for someone like me, is a plain gradient banner with zero product shown — no lipstick, no fragrance, nothing to click toward.
- The "picked with you in mind" grid is a random grab bag (towel, drink maker, jeans, mattress topper) with no logic tying it to my actual purchase history.
- Way too much scroll — home, kitchen, vacuums, bedding, bath, beauty, then random picks — I lost the thread of what this email is even about.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the beauty block if you're going to include it — show me an actual Sephora product, not a banner, and put it above the vacuum cleaner.
- 2. Cut this to one category per send. Neiman Marcus's Etro email worked because it was six products and one clear story — this is eight sections fighting each other.
- 3. Make "picked with you in mind" actually personalized or drop the label — a mattress topper next to jeans next to a towel undercuts the promise in the headline.
- 4. Stop repeating the exact 30/20/15 ladder every single send this week — rotate the creative even if the math stays the same.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Fall home refresh, on us (well, 20% off)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sephora beauty deals + your Rewards discount, one email`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Kitchen, floor care, and 50% off beauty — pick your lane`

## Technical Audit

Technical Email Audit: Kohl's "Save 20% as a Kohl's Rewards member"

**1. Technical Summary**
Standard Coherent Path/MessageGears-templated retail email with heavy tracking instrumentation; missing several CAN-SPAM/authentication signals expected at the header level and lacking alt text across all tracking pixels and content images.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 64 tracking/click-redirect links detected (`click.chp.kohls.com`, `click.s.kohls.com`) — automated HTTP probing was skipped for these by design (expected for redirect-wrapped links), so destination validity is unconfirmed and should be manually verified.
- Multiple tracking beacons present: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` (1x1 open pixel), `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...` and `/p/rp/...` (multiple response/impression pixels), `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` (open tracking), and `kohls.demdex.net/event` (Adobe Audience Manager beacon). No functional issues found in the beacons themselves — all resolve to first/third-party tracking infrastructure consistent with Coherent Path/Adobe stack.
- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` present — expected for transactional/marketing email, not a defect.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- 9 images flagged with missing `alt` attributes, all tracking pixels/beacons (`61f2da3d...`, `o.gif`, `event`, and six `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` response pixels). These are 0/1px tracking images, so missing alt is low-impact but should still use `alt=""` with `aria-hidden="true"` for consistency (one pixel, `event`, already does this correctly).
- HTML uses legacy table-based layout with MSO conditional comments and a responsive `@media (max-width:500px)` block — standard for cross-client (Outlook/Gmail) compatibility, no issues found.
- `<title></title>` is empty — minor, affects browser tab/preview text in webmail clients.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No merge tokens (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) visible in the truncated source. No issues found in the visible HTML.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not found. Could be a relay-capture limitation (AgentMail) rather than a true absence — cannot confirm sender-side compliance from this data alone.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)**: not found — one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed.
- **Physical mailing address**: not detected in the (truncated) body. CAN-SPAM requires this; flag for follow-up on the full source, since the visible HTML is truncated before the footer where this typically lives.
- **Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM/DMARC)**: not found — likely an AgentMail relay artifact rather than a true auth failure, per QA note, but cannot be confirmed as pass/fail from available data.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Tracking params present on beacons: `mi_ecmp=1021707_2026913`, `email_name=260913_DG_Email_Sunday`, `mi_cid`, `mi_mid` — consistent internal campaign tagging, no issues found.
- Cannot confirm UTM parameter presence/consistency on the primary CTA/offer links since all 64 click links are wrapped in `click.s.kohls.com`/`click.chp.kohls.com` redirects and were not resolved to final landing URLs by the QA probe.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add `alt=""` to the remaining tracking pixels missing it, for consistency with the one pixel that already has it correctly set.
2. Manually resolve a sample of the 64 wrapped tracking links to confirm final landing-page URLs carry expected UTM parameters and match the current offer/promo.
3. Obtain the full (untruncated) email source to verify physical address presence in the footer before treating the CAN-SPAM finding as confirmed.
4. Verify List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post / Authentication-Results directly against raw SMTP headers from the original sending source (not the AgentMail relay) to rule out a relay-capture artifact versus a true compliance gap.
