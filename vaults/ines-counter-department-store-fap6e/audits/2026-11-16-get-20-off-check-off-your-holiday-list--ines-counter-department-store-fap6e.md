# Get 20% off | Check off your holiday list 🎄
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again, and this is the send where they finally stopped shouting. After the "HOURS LEFT for 40% off ⏰" and "LAST DAY to stack" emails on 11/15, I expected more red-alert panic. This one is a gift guide with a modest "Ends Nov. 21" hero. It's calmer, but it's also a long scroll with nothing to anchor it. Sephora's holiday sends at least open on one clean hero. This opens on a pile of folded sweaters and a Kohl's Cash badge, and I have to work out which offer is the real one.
- The tiles are "Gifts $25 & Under," "$50 & Under," and "Toys $50 & Under." They're budget gift-guide tiles, which is practical but not my lane. I'm a counter shopper. I'm looking for gift-with-purchase, points back, and designer access. Nordstrom would pair a $50 gift edit with a beauty event. Macy's "confirm now" email last week at least gave me a clear deadline and a member perk. Kohl's gives me a Shark vacuum, a frozen drink maker and a mattress topper.
- Would I click? Probably not. I'd skim, see "Take an extra 20% off" buried near the barcodes at the bottom, and wonder why that's not in the subject line or the hero. I wouldn't forward it. I'd leave it unread with the other six this week.

## What's working

- The hero says "Ends Nov. 21" plus the Kohl's Cash badge. The deadline is plain and I can see it.
- The "$25 & Under" and "$50 & Under" tiles are easy to read. They sort gifts by budget and each one has its own label.
- The Rewards bar at the top shows my status and points. That's the one thing here that treats me like a known customer.
- The coupon codes and barcodes at the bottom are clear and scannable, and the "Take $10 off" member offer is concrete.
- The calmer visual tone is a relief after the ALL CAPS sends on 11/15.

## What's weak

- Nothing here targets a 47-year-old beauty shopper. There's no beauty, fragrance or skincare, and nothing from a brand I'd care about. Sephora Insider's "luxe beauty gifts" send, even at nine emails a week, at least speaks to my category.
- The best offer, "extra 20% off," sits at the bottom next to the barcodes, while the hero doesn't say what the discount is.
- Too many ideas compete: gift tiles, Shark, Online Only Daily Deals, a Doordash/Uber Eats/Instacart row, Klarna, and "Picked with you in mind." It reads like Macy's-style overload.
- The fine print under every product is dense and tiny. "Coupons do not apply" on the Shark vacuum makes the offer feel less honest.
- This is the same weekend as the 11/15 sends. I've now had around seven Kohl's emails since 11/10, so the cadence is wearing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put "Extra 20% off" in the hero as the single offer, with one button, "Shop gifts under $50." Move the codes and barcodes up to meet it.
- 2. Cut the Klarna and delivery-app strip, or move it to the footer. It's service clutter.
- 3. Add a beauty or fragrance gift tile. Neiman Marcus's quiet Alice + Olivia send showed that one strong image can carry the email.
- 4. Cap the cadence. Consolidate the same-weekend sends into one.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a wrapped-link Kohl's send (71 tracked links, 4 tracking pixels) with several markup defects: an empty `<title>`, a stray `</custom>` tag, and a viewport meta that blocks zoom. The header and compliance checks (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results, physical address) are unverified because of the relay and the truncated HTML, not confirmed failures.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Links not probed:** All 71 tracking links were skipped, e.g. `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7ImQi…`. Destination validity and redirect health are unverified.
- **Four tracking pixels fire on open:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/df7be6cf-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1025992_20261116`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`
  
  This is redundant, and the Adobe Audience Manager pixel (demdex) is a third-party call.
- **Malformed markup:** `<img src="…open.aspx?…" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>` has an unmatched `</custom>` closing tag. Some parsers may mishandle it.
- **Pixels with no alt text:** The first pixel (`click.chp.kohls.com/o/…`) has no `alt` attribute.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` disables pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>`.
- **Empty icon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href. It is harmless but should be removed.
- **Invalid head content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`, which is invalid. Parsers will move it into the body or drop it.
- **Missing alt text, content images:** QA flagged `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` (2a5adc5afb647aad, f441d45d2128ae53, c2f81e9ac8daee44, 415c8a18fc2950ea, 69494b48a79b4240, 78308d1d9adb0751, 916d6fa08d185233) and `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/60699446_4_2x` and `60699446_3_2x`. If these carry offers or product content, the email is unreadable with images off or in a screen reader.
- **QA false positive:** The `o.gif` pixel has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"` in the source, which is correct for a decorative image. The QA check is treating empty alt as missing.
- **Mixed fonts:** The email loads Google Fonts through both `<link>` and `@import`, guarded by `<!--[if !mso]>`. Fallbacks are declared (`Montserrat, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`), so this is low risk.
