# Ends today: Earn a $50 gift card for every $200 spent (up to $500)
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-29**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Neiman's email I've opened in two days that's built around the gift-card promotion. The "ends today" subject is the same offer as Tuesday's "Earn a $50 gift card" reminder, now with the clock showing. I do respect that the hero is clean. The big red "$50 GIFT CARD" over "$200 SPENT" is readable in a second, and the code FALLNM sits right under it. Neiman's doesn't shout the way Macy's does.
- My problem is that the offer says "Excludes beauty." That's my whole reason for walking into a department store. I'm the customer who buys at the counter and wants points back, and this event gives me nothing on that. The grid is Oscar de la Renta, Burberry, Zimmermann, Chloé and Tom Ford, so it's a ready-to-wear and handbag pitch. I'd still click for a coat or a bag, but I wouldn't click for what I buy most often. I'd probably skim it, note the 10 PM CT deadline, and not forward it.

## What's working

- The hero states the whole deal in one glance: $50 for every $200, a $500 cap, and the code. I never had to hunt for it.
- The "SHOP NOW" button is black on blush, and it's obvious.
- The nine-designer grid is well chosen. The Burberry trench and the Chloé bag look like real aspirational buys, and the product images are clean with no rendering problems.
- The category buttons (women's clothing, shoes, handbags, jewelry, home) let me jump to the section I actually shop.
- The tone is calm and respectful. Nothing is in all caps and there are no exclamation points.

## What's weak

- "Excludes beauty" is tucked into small grey type under the button. For a beauty-counter regular, that's the most important fact in the email, and it's buried.
- The fine print is a huge wall of text. It covers reward tiers, a gift-card timeline of up to 8 weeks, forfeiture on returns, and "select designers excluded." I return things at the brand event, so the return clawback matters to me, and I'd like to see it up top.
- The email never acknowledges me. There's no tier status, no points mention and no "your" anything. A loyalty program member gets treated like a stranger.
- It's the third touch on the same promo in two days, after the Fall Style Sale "ends today" send and Tuesday's gift-card reminder. The urgency stops feeling credible.
- The reward arrives as a gift card in up to 8 weeks. It isn't instant money off, so I'd have to spend more first to get it back later.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move "Excludes beauty; other exclusions apply" next to the hero copy, or flip the angle for beauty customers by pairing this with a points-back offer. Right now it reads like a surprise.
- 2. Add a line for members, such as "Your InCircle points still apply," or show my tier. Sephora Insider at least tells me where I stand, and even Macy's shows member pricing.
- 3. Add a countdown or a clear time in the hero: "Ends tonight, 10 PM CT." The deadline is only visible in the fine print.
- 4. Link each designer tile to a specific product, and show prices or "eligible" badges so I know the item qualifies before I click.
- 5. Trim the legal copy to a short summary with a "Full details" link.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last hours: your $50 gift card is waiting`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code FALLNM, up to $500 back. Beauty excluded, designers included.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Burberry, Chloé, Tom Ford and more count toward your reward.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML (head and CSS only, the body is truncated) is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud template with two confirmed code-level issues: an internal-variable comment in the delivered markup and a zoom-restricting viewport tag. The three QA warnings are header-capture gaps that can't be confirmed as real defects from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 29 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe because they use click-redirect domains. Their destinations, redirect health and UTM handling are **unverified**, not failing.
- The truncated source doesn't show any `href`, so no broken or malformed link can be confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom restriction:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom where honored, which conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4. Most email clients ignore this tag, so the impact is limited.
- **Invalid font fallback:** `.disclaimerText { font-family: Arial, Helvetica, 'sans-serif'; }`. Quoting `'sans-serif'` makes it a literal family name rather than the generic keyword, so the generic fallback is lost.
- **Fragile mobile selectors:** `*[class=contenttable]`, `*[class=mobile]`, `*[class=hide]` and others are exact-match attribute selectors. They stop applying if an element carries more than one class.
- **Hamburger menu:** the nav depends on the `:checked` sibling combinator (`.navbar-checkbox:checked~.menu`). Gmail and Outlook don't support this, so a fallback is needed. I can't tell whether one exists in the truncated body.
- **Content before the doctype:** a large comment sits ahead of `<!DOCTYPE>`. Some clients and parsers can handle this poorly.
- **Not verifiable:** image alt text, `role="presentation"` on layout tables and preheader text are in the truncated body.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Internal data leak:** the delivered HTML opens with a comment exposing template variables and Data Extension names: `NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`, the fields `EMAIL_ID`, `EMAIL_ADDRESS`, `uEm`, `cmdid`, and the `@base_refid`/`@refid` scheme. It also contains the unresolved text "CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW" and `SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256`. The comment doesn't render, but anyone viewing source can see it. It should be stripped before send.
- No unresolved visible merge tokens are shown in the available source.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe** and **List-Unsubscribe-Post** (RFC 8058) were not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers, so this is **inconclusive**. It matters because Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results** was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sending domain `e.neimanmarcus.com` is a subdomain, which is the correct pattern, but alignment can't be confirmed.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and physical address (CAN-SPAM) are in the truncated portion and can't be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Can't be assessed. No destination URLs or UTM parameters are visible, and tracking links were not resolved. The `@refid` variable suggests campaign IDs are appended in the links, but this is unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Strip the AMPscript/variable comment block from production sends. It exposes DE names and field schema, and it sits before the doctype.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Change `'sans-serif'` to unquoted `sans-serif` in `.disclaimerText`.
4. Switch the mobile selectors to `*[class~="..."]` or `.class` so they survive multi-class elements.
5. Confirm a non-CSS fallback exists for the hamburger nav in Gmail and Outlook.
6. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` against the raw message from a direct inbox, not the relay. Check SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment the same way.
7. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and resolve a sample of the 29 tracking links, so link destinations, UTMs and alt text can be checked.
