- Okay, this one's actually kind of pretty — "Iconic Beauty" as a hero with that lavender wash and the tagline "Tens across the board" has some polish to it, more than the usual grid-of-tiles Sephora send. But then I scroll and scroll and scroll — Prada, Prada again, Living Proof, Sol de Janeiro, Miu Miu, Charlotte Tilbury, Crown Affair, Glow Recipe, Korean skincare, fragrance minis, KJ Apa mascara — this is nine-plus product launches stacked in one email with zero narrative connecting them beyond "new stuff exists." That's not an edit, that's the whole warehouse.
- The one thing that did catch my eye is the top banner: "50% off Select Beauty — Today Only, exclusions/terms apply." That's the actual offer, and it's buried above a hero that has nothing to do with it. Everything below is full-price new-launch merchandising, not a sale.

## What's working

- The "Iconic Beauty" hero has real visual restraint compared to Sephora's usual clutter — clean lavender background, one clear CTA button.
- The "50% off Select Beauty" banner up top is a real, specific offer with a deadline ("Today Only").
- Clean/Planet Award badges on Living Proof and Crown Affair are useful trust signals for someone like me who actually reads ingredient callouts.
- Prices are visible on most products (Prada $42–$189, Miu Miu $38–$145), so I'm not guessing.

## What's weak

- This is a launch catalog dressed as a curated "edit" — nine+ brands in one scroll is Macy's-level overload, not Sephora's usual lane.
- The 50% off banner at the top has zero connection to anything below it — none of these new launches are discounted, so the offer feels like a bait-and-switch open.
- No Insider points balance anywhere in this one, which after five straight "0 points" reminders (8/16 through 8/29) is almost a relief, but it also means there's nothing here acknowledging I'm a member at all.
- Nothing here is remotely close to my price-point or need state — Miu Miu perfume, KJ Apa's mascara collab — this reads twentysomething, not 47.

- 1. Split this into two emails: one for the 50% off event (with actual discounted items shown), one for new-launch merchandising. Mashing them together kills both.
- 2. Cut the launch list from nine brands to three max, and lead with something in my actual replenishment zone (dry shampoo, skincare) rather than a fragrance parade.
- 3. Bring back Insider tier/points status if you're going to keep hammering me about the program — silence isn't better than nagging, it's just absence.
- 4. Tie the badge/trust cues (Clean, Planet Award) higher up — that's the one thing genuinely working, don't bury it mid-scroll.
