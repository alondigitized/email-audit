# Save an extra 20% + TODAY ONLY earn $15 Kohl’s Cash 💸
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's again. This is about the sixth one this week, and the subject is trying hard. The "TODAY ONLY" is carrying a lot of weight because the Black Friday emails since Monday have all claimed some version of urgency. The hero is a "$15 Kohl's Cash" badge next to "extra 20% with coupon" and "Free Shipping with a $49 purchase." I can see the offer, but it's stacked three deep. The tiny line under it makes me do math.
- I shop beauty at Nordstrom, Macy's and Saks, and I want points back on every card swipe. So the Kohl's Cash earn is the one thing that speaks to me. The rest is a very long mall directory with sweaters, luggage, slippers, bedding, bras and toys. The Sephora at Kohl's tile with the makeup palette is the only part that's remotely my aisle. I'd skim it and not click. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The hero puts the earn, the 20% and free shipping up top. I can see the offer before I scroll.
- The Kohl's Cash earn is the closest thing to a points-back signal, and that's what I respond to.
- The category chip row (Women, Beauty, Home and so on) under the hero lets me jump to my section.
- The Sephora at Kohl's tile with the palette image is clean, and it's the only beauty moment I noticed.
- Nothing looked broken. The images loaded and the tiles are aligned.

## What's weak

- Same cadence as the "Early Access Black Friday Deals start tomorrow" send earlier today and the "20% off won't stick around forever" one yesterday. "TODAY ONLY" from a brand that emails daily reads as noise.
- Every tile says "Coupons do not apply" or "Select styles," so the extra 20% in the hero doesn't cover much of what follows. That feels like bait-and-switch.
- It's a long scroll of 50%, 60%, 65% and 70% tiles. It's the same Macy's-style overload I tune out. Neiman Marcus's Dior Cruise send had one story and a point of view, and this has neither.
- The hero's $15 Kohl's Cash fine print is tiny on a phone. I'd have to zoom, and I have no idea when I can spend it.
- Nothing is aimed at a 47-year-old beauty shopper. The Sephora tile is buried mid-email and the palette is only one product.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero the one reason to open. Show a single line, "Earn $15 Kohl's Cash today," with the spend threshold and the redemption dates in plain type.
- 2. State up front that the 20% coupon excludes the deal tiles, then stop repeating "Coupons do not apply" on every one.
- 3. Cut the tiles to eight or ten and segment them by category. Sephora does this better with its Insider tier banner, a hero offer and a short beauty grid. Lead with the Sephora at Kohl's section for beauty buyers.
- 4. Cap the cadence. One "last call" is credible and six in a week isn't.
- 5. Link specific products, not just "Shop Now" under each tile.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your $15 Kohl's Cash ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus an extra 20% off with coupon and free shipping at $49. Exclusions apply.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sephora at Kohl's, Daily Deals and early Black Friday prices, all in one place.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based layout with a responsive `@media (max-width:500px)` block and `role="presentation"` on the layout tables. It has several markup defects (empty `<title>`, a stray `</custom>` tag, invalid elements in `<head>`) and heavy third-party tracking. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive. The QA warnings are mostly relay or truncation artifacts, not confirmed failures.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 91 tracking links were skipped from HTTP probing (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…`, `click.chp.kohls.com/o/…`). Destination validity and redirect chains are unknown.
- **Stacked tracking pixels:** There are four tracking beacons before the first content block:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/8761761a-…` (1x1)
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager, sized `0x0`)
  
  This is redundant open tracking across three vendors. Some privacy-focused clients will block the `demdex` call.
- **Malformed markup:** The `open.aspx` image is closed with a stray `</custom>` tag (`…&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`). This is invalid HTML and may be handled inconsistently by parsers.
- **Plain-text part:** 16,076 of 21,861 characters (about 74%) are URLs, which exceeds the 70% threshold. The long encoded `qs=` redirect URLs inflate the text version and can hurt spam scoring.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on content images (confirmed by QA):** The `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` images have no `alt` (`2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`, and others). With images blocked, the email may render empty. Screen readers will read out the filenames.
- **Tracking-pixel alt warnings:** The `click.chp.kohls.com/o/…` and `kohls.demdex.net/event` pixels lack `alt`. The `o.gif` pixel already has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, so that QA warning is a false positive. The remaining pixels should get `alt=""` as well.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will implicitly close the head there, so anything after it (including `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` with its empty `href`) is parsed in the body.
- **Doctype:** HTML 4.01 Transitional with `xmlns:o`/`xmlns:v` is acceptable for Outlook compatibility.
- **Fonts:** Google Fonts are loaded via both `<link>` and `@import`, inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. Fallbacks (`Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`) are present, so this is fine.
- **Dark mode:** The visible HTML has no `color-scheme` meta or `prefers-color-scheme` rules. This is not confirmed as a defect because the source is truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source or the subject line. The source is truncated, so the footer and body below the preheader were not inspected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both are reported missing. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**. Kohl's is a bulk sender, so Gmail and Yahoo's bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe. Verify against the raw message headers.
- **Physical address:** QA found none. The HTML provided is truncated before the footer, so this is **unconfirmed**. Check the footer of the full source.
- **Authentication-Results:** The header is absent, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The relay is the likely cause. The From domain (`s.kohls.com`) matches the click-tracking host (`click.s.kohls.com`), which suggests aligned sending, but this isn't verified.
- **Unsubscribe link in body:** Not visible in the truncated source, so it can't be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters in visible links:** Links are opaque `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…` redirects, so UTM and campaign parameters can't be inspected. Attribution is likely handled by the redirect service.
- **Campaign tag on images:** Images carry `email_name=261106_DG_Email_Friday`, `mi_u`, and `mi_ecmp=1025357_2026116`. The campaign naming is consistent, but I couldn't check whether those values carry through to the landing URLs.
- **Landing pages:** They weren't probed (all 91 links skipped), so landing-page alignment and promo-code carry-over are unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` text to all `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` content images, and `alt=""` to the remaining tracking pixels.
2. Remove the stray `</custom>` tag and move the `itemscope` div out of `<head>`. Remove or fill the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon" href="">`.
3. Fill in `<title>`, for example with the subject line.
4. Drop `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Consolidate redundant open pixels (`click.chp`, `mi.kohls.com/o.gif`, `open.aspx`, `demdex`).
6. Shorten the plain-text part by trimming redirect URLs, or by generating a plain-text version that doesn't repeat every tracked link.
7. Re-check compliance against the raw message: `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`, and the footer address and unsubscribe link. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML.
8. Run a landing-page probe on a sample of redirects, with tracking-domain exclusions relaxed, to verify destinations and parameter pass-through.
