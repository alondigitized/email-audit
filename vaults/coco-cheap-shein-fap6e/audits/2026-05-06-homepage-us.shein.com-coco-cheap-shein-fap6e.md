# Homepage snapshot · us.shein.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- Ok so SHEIN already has my whole search history so it's literally showing me bikini in the bar before I even type anything — kind of iconic. The homepage is pushing a Summer Prep campaign with what looks like up to 90% off, which is very on brand for them. It feels like the same SHEIN I've been opening on my phone since sophomore year of high school — not trying to be anything other than cheap fashion, and it works for that.

## What's working

- **"UP TO 90" off banner** — even if it's marketing math, that number stops my thumb mid-scroll every time
- **"bikini" pre-filled in the search bar** — it remembered what I was looking for or surfaced a trending search; either way it's doing its job
- **"Hot Sellers" + "New Arrivals" quick-access tiles** — I can tap straight into what's trending without digging through nav, which is the whole point
- **Free Shipping bar at the top** — no threshold shown means I'm not doing the "$1.99 more" math in my head; that friction is just gone
- **Category grid (Women, Curve, Kids, Shoes, Home & Living)** — it's organized exactly how I actually think about shopping on SHEIN, not how a buyer at Nordstrom would

## What's weak

- **Banner text is cut off**: "SUMMER P..." and "PREP &..." are both clipped at the right edge — I literally cannot read what the CTA says, which is a hard fail for a flagship promo
- **No urgency beyond the season**: I don't see a countdown timer, "ends Sunday," or any scarcity copy — the 90% off claim just sits there with no reason to act now
- **Zero social proof above the fold**: no review counts, no star ratings, no "4.2M items sold" — for a first-time visitor, SHEIN can feel sketchy without those signals
- **Loyalty points/rewards completely invisible**: I have points from my last three orders and SHEIN just doesn't remind me, so I never use them before checkout
- **The dark left-column tiles feel very 2019**: hot-pink-on-black labels like "Hot Sellers" stacked on top of each other in a narrow column looks dated compared to how TikTok Shop surfaces product

## Recommendations

- **Fix the banner crop**: the summer promo CTA button text is literally unreadable — whoever sized this for mobile broke the hero; ship a fixed viewport width this week
- **Add a "Trending on TikTok" or "Going Viral" horizontal scroll rail** above the category grid — for Gen Z this is the single highest-engagement module and it's nowhere visible
- **Surface the rewards balance in the header**: a small "💰 230 pts" next to the account icon would nudge me to open the loyalty tab every single visit
- **Add one urgency signal to the promo banner**: a "Sale ends Sunday" or a live countdown under the 90% copy — even fake urgency moves the needle and SHEIN knows how to do this
