# Homepage snapshot · us.shein.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay so this is literally my app — SHEIN is the brand I live in. The Memorial Day Sale hero is screaming 90% off which, yes, I know SHEIN's original prices are fake, but it still makes me want to tap everything. This feels like a brand that knows exactly who I am and is just dumping deals at my face the second I open it.

## What's working

- **"90% OFF" Memorial Day hero** — the number is massive and impossible to miss; my thumb is already moving toward it
- **Swimwear hero imagery** — it's May, I need a bikini, this is exactly the vibe
- **"Free Shipping | Free Returns | No Hidden Fees" header bar** — I clock this immediately because I'm always doing the math on whether an order is worth it
- **Category icon grid** — Women, Curve, Dresses, Tops, Shoes, Beachwear all one tap away; I can navigate to anything in two seconds
- **Super Deals rail** — flash sale prices ($3.96, $13.08) are catnip for me specifically; this is haul math in real time
- **Top Trends hashtag naming** (`#DressbyNums`, `#GroceriedKuins`) — chaotic but it scans like TikTok content which honestly makes me feel at home
- **Email signup offering 30% off + Free Shipping + Free Returns** — solid first-order hook that's visible before the footer

## What's weak

- **No countdown timer on the Memorial Day Sale** — if this ends in 3 hours I'm sprinting; if it ends "soon" I'll come back tomorrow and forget
- **Super Deals product cards are tiny** — I can barely see what I'm clicking on; the images are too small to make me want to tap one specifically
- **No social proof on product tiles** — no star ratings, no "10k sold" on the Super Deals cards; I rely on that to filter trash from gems
- **Brand Z!one section** — there's a Chanel bag in there at $63, which either screams dupe or confuses the page's identity; feels off-brand and slightly sketchy
- **No loyalty program visible** — SHEIN has a points system but I see none of it; for a returning user, that's a miss
- **Cookie banner eating screen real estate** at the bottom — minor but it's there

## Recommendations

- **Add a live countdown timer to the Memorial Day Sale hero** — "Ends in 4h 22m" would make me drop everything and start adding to cart right now
- **Put star ratings + sold counts on the Super Deals product cards** — even tiny 4.2★ chips would help me trust the $3.96 item isn't a rag
- **Surface the points/rewards balance in the nav for logged-in users** — I should see my SHEIN points balance before I even scroll; that's a purchase trigger
- **Swap the Brand Z!one section for a "TikTok Made Me Buy It" or trending-hashtag rail** — lean into what actually drives Gen Z purchase decisions instead of a dupe-adjacent luxury corner that makes me uncomfortable
