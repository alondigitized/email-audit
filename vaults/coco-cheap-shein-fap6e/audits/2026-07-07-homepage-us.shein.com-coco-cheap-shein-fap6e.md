# Homepage snapshot · us.shein.com · 2026-07-07
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-07**
## Executive summary

- okay SHEIN knows me — I literally have this app on my home screen and the first thing I see is "UP TO 90%" and a bikini. It's pushing a summer splash sale hard, which makes sense for July. This is peak SHEIN energy: loud deals, swimwear front and center, and a search bar that's already got "bikini" in it like they read my mind.

## What's working

- **"UP TO 90%" splash banner** — that number stops my scroll cold every time, even knowing the asterisk game
- **"Swimwear Edit" shortcut button** — one tap to a curated swim selection is perfect for the July haul mindset
- **"Hot Sellers" + "New Arrivals" buttons** — both visible immediately, which is what I actually use the app for
- **Beachwear category icon** — the bottom category grid surfaces exactly what's seasonal right now
- **Search bar with "bikini" pre-filled** — whether it's a suggestion or autocomplete, it's already inside my head
- **Free Shipping** teased at the very top — that banner is the first thing my eye catches on any SHEIN visit

## What's weak

- **Split hero layout** — left panel (three buttons) and right panel (sale banner) compete for attention; there's no clear visual anchor
- **"DIVE" CTA copy** — cute but vague; does that take me to the sale? the swim shop? unclear
- **"Deals this good..."** copy is cut off — whatever the full hook is, I can't read it, so the urgency fizzles
- **No prices or specific products visible** — just categories and a % number; I want to see actual $6 dresses to trigger the add-to-cart itch
- **No reviews or ratings** anywhere in this viewport — SHEIN's strength is the review volume and I can't see any
- **Curve category** is right next to Women but gets equal visual weight — the main targeting feels slightly scattered

## Recommendations

- **Collapse the split-hero into one full-width panel** — pick either the sale banner or the swimwear editorial, not both; the divided layout kills momentum
- **Change "DIVE" to something like "Shop the Sale"** — one extra word removes all the ambiguity about what tapping does
- **Show a flash deal product card** (one item, real price, star rating) in the hero — even a single $4.99 bikini top with 4.7★ would convert me faster than any % banner
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold prominently** ("Free shipping over $29" or whatever the actual number is) — right now "Free Shi..." is clipped and that detail matters for my cart math
