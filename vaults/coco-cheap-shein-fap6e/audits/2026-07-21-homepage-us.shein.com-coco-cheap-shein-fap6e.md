# Homepage snapshot · us.shein.com · 2026-07-21
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-21**
## Executive summary

- Ok so SHEIN literally said "we see you, college girl" — the Back-to-School banner is front and center and it is giving up to 90% off energy which, yes. This is a fast-fashion homepage that knows exactly who it's talking to: me. It's pushing BTS right now and also teasing beachwear/summer stuff which, makes sense since it's July.

## What's working

- **Back-to-School banner** — "UP TO 90%" is the first thing my eyes go to and BTS is peak-relevant for July if you're a college student stocking up before fall semester
- **"Hot Sellers" tile** — the quick-tap shortcut to what's trending is perfect for how I actually browse SHEIN (I want the algorithm's picks, not the full catalog)
- **"New Arrivals" tile** — I literally refresh SHEIN for new drops, having this above the fold means I don't even have to type anything
- **Category grid (Women, Shoes, Beachwear)** — exactly my three categories, all reachable in one tap
- **"New In" in the nav bar** — I would tap this before anything else, and it's right there
- **Search bar with "bikini" prefilled** — confirms the app remembers my recent search, which is a small but real "this knows me" signal

## What's weak

- **"SCORE A..." CTA button is cut off** — I can see the button but I can't read what I'm scoring. Mobile viewport clipping kills the urgency copy
- **Free shipping copy is truncated** — "Free Shi..." tells me nothing about the threshold; I don't know if I qualify without tapping away
- **No trust signals visible** — no review counts, no "free returns," no secure-checkout badge — none of the stuff that makes me feel OK putting my card in
- **No loyalty/rewards hook** — SHEIN has a points program but there's zero mention of it above the fold; a "check your points" badge would've gotten me
- **Left-tile lifestyle shots are tiny** — "Sunny Escapes" and "New Arrivals" tiles have model images but they're so small I can barely tell what the outfits actually are

## Recommendations

- **Show the full BTS CTA copy** — "SCORE A..." needs to finish its sentence; either shorten the text or reflow the banner so the button reads completely on a standard mobile viewport
- **Add a free-shipping threshold line** — pin "$49+ ships free" or whatever the number is in the top bar so I can plan my cart without tapping away
- **Drop a points/rewards badge in the nav** — even a tiny "🪙 230 pts" next to my avatar tells returning shoppers they have money on the table
- **Swap one Hot Sellers tile for a product close-up** — the lifestyle shots in those left-column tiles are too small to ID the actual pieces; showing the top item from Hot Sellers as a product card would drive faster taps
