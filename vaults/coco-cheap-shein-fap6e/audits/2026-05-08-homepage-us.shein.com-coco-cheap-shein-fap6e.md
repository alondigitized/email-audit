# Homepage snapshot · us.shein.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- Ok so SHEIN is doing what SHEIN does — hitting fast-fashion girlies with summer deals front and center, super low price points, and a "register for 30% off" carrot at the bottom. It reads exactly like the app I use every week to stack discounts before a TikTok haul. This homepage is screaming budget summer wardrobe and I am literally the target customer.

## What's working

- **"Free Shipping / Free Returns / No Hidden Fees" top bar** — SHEIN's reputation is sketchy to some people, so leading with this above everything else is smart. I actually trust it more immediately.
- **"Grow Your Summer Style — UP to 35% OFF" hero** — speaks directly to me. Summer, style, a real percent-off number. Not vague, not luxury-coded. It's my language.
- **Super Deals Flash Sale rail** — $8.84, $21.15 at 54% off — this is pure dopamine for someone doing an $80 haul. I'm already mentally adding to cart.
- **Category grid (Women, Dresses, Tops, Shoes, Beachwear)** — every category I shop is one tap away, icon-style, no buried nav.
- **"SHOP THE EDIT" CTA button** — visible, clearly labeled, above the fold.
- **30% off registration offer at the bottom** — perfect first-visit conversion hook. Free shipping AND free returns on top of 30% off? That's a stacked coupon situation and I live for it.

## What's weak

- **No returning-shopper hook at all** — if I'm a frequent buyer (which I am), there's nothing up top acknowledging me — no rewards points balance, no "welcome back," no saved items count. Feels like it ignores its most loyal customers.
- **"Top Trends" section feels visually flat** — the product images in that rail are small and hard to read at a glance. Doesn't compete with the energy of the Super Deals section.
- **Brand Zone (Donny Josse / Good)** — I have no idea who these brands are and there's no reason to care given. It just looks like ad inventory shoved in.
- **No review stars or ratings on Super Deals products** — I can see prices and discounts but no social proof. For Coco who checks every review before buying, this is a trust gap.
- **Cookie consent banner** — it sits at the bottom and covers some content. Minor, but annoying on mobile.

## Recommendations

- **Add a loyalty/rewards widget above the fold** — even a small "You have 240 points" chip would make returning users feel seen and increase session depth.
- **Put star ratings under each Super Deals product tile** — even showing "4.2★ (1.2k)" would do massive work for conversion on the flash sale section.
- **Replace the Brand Zone with a trending hashtag rail** — lean into TikTok culture with a "#SHEINHaul" or "#SummerOOTD" curated product strip. That's the actual reason I'm here.
- **Swap the "Top Trends" flat grid for a short video loop** — a 3-second autoplay clip of someone doing a try-on would immediately make this feel more app-native and less like a desktop site shrunken down.
