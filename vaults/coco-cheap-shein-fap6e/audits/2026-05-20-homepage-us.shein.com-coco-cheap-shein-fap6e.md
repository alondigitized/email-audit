# Homepage snapshot · us.shein.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- Okay so SHEIN is in full sale-mode right now — the whole hero is one giant blue "MEMORIAL DAY SALE 90% OFF" banner screaming at me. This is 100% a fast-fashion deal site and it's not hiding it. The vibe is very "open the app, get immediately hit with a number that makes your brain go brr."

## What's working

- **The "Free Shipping | Free Returns | No Hidden Fees" top strip** — this is the first thing I read and it removes my three biggest hesitations before I've even seen a product. Smart.
- **The Memorial Day Sale hero banner** — big, loud, blue. "$12." and "$8." callout prices visible in the hero make it feel like a real deal, not just marketing copy.
- **The Super Deals rail** — $5.95, $6.09 (65% off), flash sale prices. That's the scroll bait that keeps me on the app. "#DiverselyYours #NoirGirls" tag on one item shows they're at least trying to include everyone.
- **Category grid immediately below the hero** — Women, Dresses, Tops, Shoes, Beachwear all accessible in two rows. I can get to what I want in literally one tap.
- **"Style Refresh Faves" and "New Arrivals" sidebar buttons** — feels like the app version, which is how I actually shop SHEIN.

## What's weak

- **No returning-shopper recognition** — I've ordered from SHEIN probably 15 times and this homepage has zero memory of me. No "your saved items" or rewards points visible. ASOS shows me my wishlist immediately; SHEIN does not.
- **The 30% email registration offer is buried below the fold** — I almost missed it entirely. If you want me to sign up, put that where I land, not where I have to scroll.
- **"90% OFF" feels like a trap** — it's SHEIN's classic "marked-up then slashed" math. No baseline price shown, no "was $X now $Y." As someone who has posted try-on hauls, I know the original price was never real. This makes me eye-roll a little.
- **Brand Zone with CHANEL visible** — wait, is this a collab or a SHEIN knock-off branded section? This is confusing and potentially off-putting. It reads misleading.
- **No review counts or star ratings on Super Deals products** — just prices and discount percentages. For a first-tap decision, I want to see at least a star rating before I'm interested.

## Recommendations

- **Move the "30% off + Free Shipping" register offer above the fold** — make it a sticky header or inline with the hero. That's a stronger hook than the sale banner alone for anyone who hasn't signed up yet.
- **Add star ratings (even just the number) to each product tile in Super Deals and Top Trends** — "4.7★ (2.3k)" would massively increase my confidence to tap.
- **Clarify the Brand Zone** — if it's a collab, say "SHEIN x [Brand]." If it's a SHEIN house brand, don't use a font that looks like luxury brand logos. That confusion hurts trust.
- **Swap the generic blue sale graphic for a lifestyle shot** — a college-aged girl doing a try-on or pulling clothes out of a SHEIN bag with the sale overlay on top. Give me someone I can see myself as.
