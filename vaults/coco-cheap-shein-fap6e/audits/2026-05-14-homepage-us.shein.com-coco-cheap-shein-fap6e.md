# Homepage snapshot · us.shein.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- OK so SHEIN is doing a whole EARLY SUMMER thing and slapping 90% OFF everywhere — this is literally the exact energy I come here for. The page hits budget-first: prices in the $4–$6 range in the Super Deals rail, a massive discount banner above the fold, and a sign-up bribe for 30% off. This is unmistakably SHEIN — chaotic, discount-heavy, and fully aimed at people who stack coupons and post hauls.

## What's working

- **The 90% OFF hero** — that giant yellow number is the first thing I see and it's exactly why I open SHEIN. Speaks my language immediately.
- **Top trust strip** — "Free Shipping | Free Returns | No Hidden Fees" pinned at the very top builds confidence before I even look at product. Free returns is huge for my try-on content.
- **Category icon grid** — Women, Beachwear, Tops, Shoes, Jewelry all clickable in one scroll. I can get anywhere in two taps, which is essential for app-style browsing.
- **Super Deals rail** — $4.84 Flash Sale and $5.58 items are *exactly* what I screenshot for my TikTok haul price reveals. The price-first layout with big numbers works.
- **"Register and Get" module** — 30% off + free shipping + free returns in one sign-up is a genuinely strong hook, especially for new users.
- **Early Summer timing** — SHEIN is not running stale holiday copy in May. That's not a given.

## What's weak

- **No countdown timer anywhere** — "EARLY SUMMER" sounds like a vibe, not a deadline. I don't know when the 90% off ends so there's zero urgency to tap now vs. tomorrow.
- **Hero has no CTA button** — the banner is loud but there's no "Shop the Sale" button in the hero itself. I'm supposed to just... figure out where to click?
- **90% off feels fake** — I've been on SHEIN enough to know this is a markdown from an inflated original. It's not a lie but it's not really honest either. Doesn't make me trust the actual price.
- **Cookie popup** — "Do Not Sell My Personal Information / Accept" popup is sitting right there at the bottom, cutting off content. Minor but annoying.
- **No product ratings in the Super Deals rail** — I can see prices but no stars or review counts. For a $4.84 tee I need at least a star count before I cart it.
- **"Brand Z One"** label — what is that? It means nothing to me and doesn't make me want to tap.

## Recommendations

- **Add a countdown timer to the hero** — even "Ends in 2 days" in the yellow banner would make me tap immediately instead of saving the tab for later.
- **Drop a visible "Shop Sale" button inside the hero** — right now the hero is a billboard with no entrance. One bright CTA button changes that.
- **Surface star ratings on the Super Deals cards** — I need social proof before I impulse-buy a $4 item. Even just "4.3 ★ (1.2k)" on the thumbnail would close me faster.
- **Rename "Brand Z One" to something that actually means something** — like "Viral Picks" or "TikTok Finds" — or just drop it and run a trending hashtag editorial (#PinkaDellBetties is already right there in the feed).
