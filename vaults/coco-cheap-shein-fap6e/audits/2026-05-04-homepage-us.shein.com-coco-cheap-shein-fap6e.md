## What's working

- **"Free Shipping" top strip** — instant green flag for a $80-haul shopper who's calculating total cost before she even browses. No threshold shown, which makes it feel unconditional.
- **Beachware category circle with the black bikini** — literally what I came here for in May. One tap and I'm in the right section. Clean image, clear label.
- **"Curve" category circle** — SHEIN including extended sizing right on the homepage grid is inclusive in a way fast fashion usually buries in filters. Genuinely appreciated.
- **"New Arrivals" tile** — that's always my first stop. Seeing it front and center without having to dig through nav is a time-saver.
- **Category grid layout** — Women, Curve, Kids, Shoes, Beachware, Home & Living all visible in two rows. Fast visual scan, easy to tap into what I want.

## What's weak

- **Right-side sale banner is cut off** — "SUMMER P...", "PREP & SA..." is unreadable. If this is the main hero offer, failing to render it fully is a conversion killer. I can't act on copy I can't read.
- **No loyalty or rewards hook** — SHEIN has a points system but I see zero mention of it here. For a return shopper like me who stacks coupons, this is a missed moment.
- **No countdown timer or urgency mechanism** — "UP TO 90" off sounds great but there's zero scarcity signal. Is this sale ending tonight or is it basically permanent? Feels stale.
- **Search bar showing "bikini"** — either auto-populated from my history (fine) or it's a default suggestion, but it makes the page feel slightly messy rather than intentional.
- **No product rails visible** — I see category circles but no actual products with prices. My brain doesn't get that dopamine hit of seeing a $9 dress before I even scroll.

## Recommendations

- **Fix the right-side banner crop** — whatever is getting cut off ("SUMMER PICKS"? "PREP & SAVE"?) needs to fully render. If the summer hero can't be read, swap it for a full-width banner at the top instead of this awkward two-column split.
- **Add a product price-anchor rail above the fold** — show me three products right now, with prices like "$7.99 / $12.49 / $6.00." That's what keeps me on SHEIN instead of going to Amazon.
- **Surface the coupon or rewards balance for logged-in users** — even a "You have 240 points — redeem now" chip near the top would make me feel like this page is built for me specifically, not just anyone who landed here.
- **Add "Ends in X hours" or a visible countdown to the 90% off promo** — urgency converts budget shoppers. Even if the deal is rolling, a countdown makes the tap feel necessary.
