# Homepage snapshot · us.shein.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay so SHEIN is literally that site where I spend $80 and get 47 pieces delivered — this homepage is doing the most in the right direction. It's summer, it's Memorial Day, and the front page is screaming bikinis and discounts, which is exactly my vibe rn. It looks like a fast-fashion haul destination that knows its audience better than most.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day "UP TO 90%" banner** — as a coupon-stacker this is the first thing I see and I'm already calculating cart totals in my head
- **Free Shipping top bar** — no threshold shown which reads as unconditional; removes my biggest checkout hesitation
- **Beachwear + Curve + Women circles** — I can get to exactly what I want in literally one tap, and Curve being surfaced early is inclusive without making it weird
- **"Hot Sellers" and "New Arrivals" stacked tiles** — this is how TikTok Shop presents content and it maps to how I actually browse; I trust Hot Sellers the way I trust a FYP algorithm
- **Search pre-filled with "bikini"** — whether that's my last search or a seasonal suggestion, it tells me the site already knows what I'm here for

## What's weak

- **"SHOP THE S..."** — that CTA is cut off on the right edge of the Memorial Day banner and I can't read where it goes; on mobile that's an instant confusion moment
- **No urgency on the Memorial Day deal** — "UP TO 90%" with no end date or countdown doesn't make me feel like I need to move fast; I'll just come back tomorrow (and forget)
- **No trust signals** — no review stars, no "4.2M reviews," no return policy badge anywhere above the fold; for a first-time visitor this would feel sketchy
- **"Style Refresh Festival"** — this label means nothing to me; sounds like something a brand intern named in a Notion doc, not how I talk about shopping
- **Category circles feel small on mobile** — "Home & Living" next to "Beachwear" with the same visual weight is confusing; Home & Living is not why I'm on SHEIN right now

## Recommendations

- **Put a countdown timer on the Memorial Day banner** — "Ends Monday 11:59PM" turns "UP TO 90% off" from a permanent-feeling discount into something I have to act on today
- **Fix the cropped CTA** — "SHOP THE S..." should either be legible or replaced with a full-width button below the banner; cut-off text on the hero is a credibility miss
- **Add a micro-trust strip** — one row with "Free Returns · 4.5★ from 10M+ reviews · Secure Checkout" between the hero and category grid would close the hesitation gap for new visitors
- **Swap "Style Refresh Festival" for a concrete hook** — something like "New drops every day this week" or "100+ new pieces added today" works harder for me than a festival name
