# Homepage snapshot · us.shein.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Okay yeah this is literally my homepage. SHEIN is serving a full Memorial Day "up to 90% off" banner front and center, the search bar's already got "bikini" in it, and the category tiles are swimwear and shoes — which is exactly what I'm here for. It feels like SHEIN knows it's late May and it's leaning all the way into that, which I respect.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day "UP TO 90%OFF" banner** — big, bright, patriotic color scheme, impossible to miss. That's the hook that keeps me from swiping away.
- **"Hot Sellers" button with lifestyle imagery** — two models in summery fits, shows me what's actually trending right now, not just a generic promo.
- **"New Arrivals" section clearly labeled** — Gen Z literally lives in New Arrivals. The fact that it's a top-3 module above the fold is correct behavior.
- **"Beachwear" category circle** — given that "bikini" is in the search bar, having a Beachwear shortcut in the grid is smart; one tap from idea to product.
- **Free Shipping banner at very top** — I'm already doing the math on whether I need to pad my cart; seeing it unconditional right away removes that friction.
- **Red clog-style shoes in the Shoes tile** — those are clearly a Crocs-lookalike moment, very on trend, the kind of thing I'd screenshot and send to my group chat.

## What's weak

- **"UP TO 90%" feels manipulative** — SHEIN does this every sale. The actual items I want are 20-30% off at best, and the 90% deals are on random kitchen gadgets. No clarification visible.
- **No rewards/points balance** — I have a SHEIN account and I'm logged in (presumably), but my points, level, or "10 points from a reward" type hook is nowhere visible. That's a missed retention signal.
- **"Style Refresh Festival" label is vague** — what is that? It sounds like a corporate-invented event name. Compared to "Hot Sellers" and "New Arrivals" which are instantly scannable, this loses me.
- **No countdown timer on the Memorial Day sale** — "Memorial Day" implies a deadline but I can't see when it ends. Urgency without a clock is half-urgency.
- **Nav bar is getting cut off** — "Women Clothing" and then what looks like a "B..." item are cut off at the right edge. On my phone that could be annoying to swipe through.

## Recommendations

- **Add a countdown timer to the Memorial Day banner** — "Ends Monday 11:59 PM" in small text under the 90% copy would actually create urgency instead of just implying it.
- **Drop the "Style Refresh Festival" label, call it something scannable** — "Summer Drops" or "New This Week" communicates immediately; festival marketing language reads as brand-speak.
- **Surface the loyalty points balance** — even a tiny chip that says "You have 430 points" near the search bar would make me feel invested and less likely to bounce to Amazon.
- **Show a real discount number on one product** — replace one of the category circles with a product card showing, say, "Linen Set $8.99 (was $22)" — specific price converts better than percentage theater.
