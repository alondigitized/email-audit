# Homepage snapshot · us.shein.com · 2026-06-09
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-09**
## Executive summary

- Ok so SHEIN is literally doing its thing — beach energy all over the above-the-fold, "UP TO 90% OFF" screaming at me from the right side like it always does, and the three stacked CTAs on the left feel like the SHEIN app I already have on my phone. This is peak summer haul season content and honestly the homepage is very on brand for them. They're pushing beachwear hard right now which makes sense because it's June.

## What's working

- **"Beach Picks" CTA** — right on time for summer, this is exactly what I'm clicking when I'm planning a vacation haul
- **"New Arrivals" button** — my dopamine response is immediate, I always want to see what just dropped
- **HOT DEALS banner** — even if the percentage is probably misleading, "up to 90%" makes me feel like I'd be dumb not to look
- **Category grid** — Women, Curve, Kids, Shoes, Beachwear, Home & Living all visible as icons, I can find my section in one tap
- **"Free Shi..." top bar** — free shipping is a non-negotiable for me, even just seeing it there keeps me from bouncing

## What's weak

- **The search bar has "bikini" pre-filled** — feels weird and a little invasive, like why is that already in there? Makes the page feel less clean
- **The right-side banner is cut off** — "SNAP..." text is clipped, the CTA on that deals banner is not readable; I can't tap what I can't see
- **Three competing left CTAs with no hierarchy** — Hot Sellers vs. New Arrivals vs. Beach Picks are all the same size and color; none feels more important than the others so I just kind of freeze
- **Zero social proof above the fold** — no "5M+ reviews" badge, no star rating, nothing that makes me trust a specific product. For someone new to SHEIN this would feel sketchy
- **No free-shipping threshold spelled out** — the top bar says "Free Shi—" and then cuts off; I need to see the number to know if I'm close

## Recommendations

- **Make one CTA the hero, not three** — pick Beach Picks as the single above-the-fold hero for June and give it a real lifestyle photo behind it, not just a tiny thumbnail. Let the other two live below.
- **Show the full free-shipping threshold** — "Free Shipping on orders $9.99+" is a huge conversion driver for me; don't clip it in the banner
- **Clear the search bar or make it personalized** — either show my actual last search or leave it blank; "bikini" sitting there without context feels off
- **Add a social proof strip** — even a "10M+ 5-star reviews" ticker above the fold would help reassure anyone who's on the fence
