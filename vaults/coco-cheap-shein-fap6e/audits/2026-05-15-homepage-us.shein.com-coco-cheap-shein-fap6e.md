# Homepage snapshot · us.shein.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- Ok so SHEIN is going full "Early Summer" mode right now — the banner is screaming **90% off** and they're really leaning into the deal-first energy I'm here for. This looks exactly like the SHEIN I know from TikTok haul videos: chaotic in the best way, every inch of the page is some kind of offer or category grid. It's clearly a fast fashion dump site and I mean that affectionately.

## What's working

- **The 90% off badge** — that yellow circle with "90%" is literally the first thing my eye goes to. As someone who haul-shops, this is the hook. Done.
- **"Early Summer" seasonal relevance** — it's May, this is exactly what I'm thinking about. Timing is right.
- **Category grid (Women, Curve, Kids, Men, Baby, Jewelry, Dresses...)** — I can get to Women's in one tap, no buried nav needed.
- **"Super Deals" and "Top Trends" rails** — "$13.07 Flash Sale", "$6.33 #LaceElegance" — these are real prices I recognize from SHEIN hauls. Instantly credible.
- **"Brand Zone" with Jordan + Chanel thumbnails** — random but makes the page feel bigger than just fast fashion.
- **30% off + Free Shipping + Free Returns registration panel** — that's a stacked offer for a new email sign-up. I would consider it.
- **"New Arrivals" and "Style Refresh Faves" left-rail tabs** — I always check new arrivals first, good that it's surfaced immediately.

## What's weak

- **"90% off — starts at $8.49" fine print** — the asterisk energy is real. The hero says 90% off, then the small text qualifies it down. Classic SHEIN bait. I've been burned before.
- **The hero image is a teeny tiny bikini model** — she's pretty but I can barely see what's actually being sold. No product name, no item I can tap directly.
- **No social proof above the fold** — no review counts, no "10M+ sold" badge, no TikTok-style "as seen on" signal. For a Gen Z audience this is a miss.
- **The cookie consent bar** covers the bottom of the screen — it's a legit dark pattern and makes the page feel cluttered on mobile.
- **The registration form mid-page feels pushy** — it's got two checkbox legal blurbs and an email input before I've even seen the product. Kills the vibe.
- **"Brand Zone" shows Chanel + Jordan** — these are not SHEIN products and this placement is confusing. Am I on a marketplace now?

## Recommendations

- **Replace the bikini stock-image hero with a lifestyle TikTok-style flat lay or try-on shot** — show a real item I can recognize and want to tap, not a runway pose.
- **Move the "30% off + free shipping" email panel lower** — I want to browse first, then get hit with the sign-up offer. Don't block my scroll path with legal checkboxes before I've seen one product.
- **Add a "trending on TikTok" or "#SHEINfinds" horizontal rail above the fold** — that's literally why I'm here, speak my language.
- **Surface social proof next to any featured deal** — even just "4.2★ (18k reviews)" on a Flash Sale item would make me trust the 90% off claim way more.
