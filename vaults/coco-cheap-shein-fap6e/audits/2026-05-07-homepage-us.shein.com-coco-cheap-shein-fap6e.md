# Homepage snapshot · us.shein.com · 2026-05-07
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- OK so SHEIN is basically my religion, so I'm not gonna pretend I'm surprised by what I'm seeing — it's the usual deal: big sale banner, category grid, free shipping at the top. Right now they're pushing a summer prep situation with an "UP TO 90" something-off promo front and center, which, love to see it. It's not a *wow* homepage but it's familiar enough that I know exactly how to navigate it without thinking.

## What's working

- **"Free Shipping" strip at the very top** — as someone who abandons carts over shipping fees, this is the first thing I need to see and it's the first thing I see
- **"New Arrivals" button** — this is literally why I open SHEIN; landing it as a hero-level tile is smart
- **"SUMMER P... UP TO 90" banner** — the yellow-on-blue colorway pops and reads as a real event, not just a filler promo
- **Category icon grid (Women, Curve, Shoes, Beauty & Health)** — clean grid, one-tap access, I can get to my stuff fast
- **"bikini" pre-filled in the search bar** — either I searched that before or SHEIN knows what May means for a college girl; either way it feels weirdly relevant

## What's weak

- **Left-side button stack (Hot Sellers / New Arrivals / Home & D...)** competing with the right-side promo banner — there's no clear "look here first" moment; my eye bounces
- **No price or threshold shown under "Free Shipping"** — is it free always? Over $29? Over $49? I need the number or I don't trust it
- **No loyalty/rewards hook visible** — SHEIN has a whole points system and there's zero mention of it above the fold; I'd convert faster if I saw "you have 340 points"
- **"Home & D..." button label is cut off** — sloppy truncation, makes the left-column layout feel unfinished
- **No model imagery that feels like me** — the thumbnail models in the left tiles are tiny and I can't actually see a vibe from them

## Recommendations

- **Replace the split left-column/right-banner layout with a single full-width hero** — one summer lookbook shot with a Gen Z model, the discount overlaid, and one CTA button. Stop making me pick a side.
- **Show the free-shipping threshold in the top bar** — change "Free Shipping" to "Free Shipping on $29+" or whatever the number is so I don't have to discover it at checkout and feel tricked.
- **Surface my SHEIN points balance (or a "join rewards" nudge) in the top nav or just below the hero** — I spend more when I know I have points to burn.
- **Swap "Home & D..." label for a clean "Home" label** — takes 10 seconds and removes the jank.
