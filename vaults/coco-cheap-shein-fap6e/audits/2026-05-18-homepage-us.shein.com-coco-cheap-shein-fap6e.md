# Homepage snapshot · us.shein.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- OK so SHEIN's homepage is doing exactly what I expect — chaos in the best way. It's throwing everything at me at once: Memorial Day sale up to 90% off, Hot Sellers, New Arrivals, a dozen category tiles, and what looks like a Super Deals section loading in below. It reads like TikTok Shop's older sister — very mobile-native, very sale-forward. Right now it's pushing Memorial Day hard and beachware, which makes sense since it's May.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day "UP TO 90% OFF" banner** — that number stops my thumb mid-scroll every time, even if I know it's a SHEIN "up to" situation
- **"Hot Sellers" and "New Arrivals" left-side buttons** — these are my two first taps on any SHEIN visit; surfacing them immediately is correct
- **Beachware category tile with bikini model** — it's May, I'm literally trying to find a bikini for Memorial Day weekend, this placement is perfect
- **Search bar pre-loaded with "bikini"** — whether that's a recent search or a personalized placeholder, it's reading my mind right now
- **Mobile-native layout** — the tile grid (Women, Curve, Kids, Shoes, Home & Living, Beachware) scans instantly on a phone, no pinching required

## What's weak

- **"Style Refresh Festival" banner** — what is this? It sounds like a placeholder event name, not a real campaign. No urgency, no offer attached, just vibes.
- **Competing focal points** — three left-side stacked banners vs. one giant right-side Memorial Day block means my eye bounces and I'm not sure where the actual CTA lives
- **"SHOP THE —" is cut off** on the Memorial Day banner — whatever that button says is clipped at the right edge of the viewport, so I can't read it
- **No free-shipping callout** — SHEIN has free shipping thresholds and I always coupon-stack to hit them, but nothing tells me what the number is from this screen
- **Zero trust signals** — no star ratings, no "10M+ orders shipped," no return policy mention, nothing. I already trust SHEIN but a new-to-SHEIN friend would bounce.

## Recommendations

- **Consolidate the left-side banners into one scrollable rail or remove one** — pick between "Hot Sellers" and "New Arrivals" as the hero left-item so the Memorial Day promo gets full-width attention and the visual hierarchy is obvious
- **Add a free-shipping threshold strip just below the nav** — a single line like "Free shipping on orders $29+" is the single highest-ROI trust-plus-conversion element SHEIN could add here, costs nothing
- **Give "Style Refresh Festival" a real offer** — attach a specific % off or a countdown timer so it acts like a campaign, not a filler label
- **Fix the cropped CTA button** — the "SHOP THE" text being cut off at the viewport edge is sloppy; constrain the banner to the safe area or push the button left
