# Homepage snapshot · us.shein.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- ok so this is textbook SHEIN — loud, chaotic in the best way, and throwing 90% off in my face before I've even scrolled. It's pushing an Early Summer swim/festival campaign which is literally perfect timing for me rn. This homepage knows exactly who it's talking to: girls who want to haul 15 items for $80 and still have enough left for a coffee.

## What's working

- **The 90% off hero number** — it's huge, it's orange, it stops the scroll. Even knowing it's a SHEIN discount I still feel the pull.
- **"Free Shipping | Free Returns | No Hidden Fees" top bar** — three objections killed before I even think them. Clean.
- **"Hot Sellers / New Arrivals / Style Refresh Found" left buttons** — these are actually useful shortcuts for a power user like me, especially New Arrivals when I'm hunting fresh TikTok content.
- **Top Trends rail with hashtags** — #EveningGlam and #StrongMom are showing me how to shop by vibe, not just category. That's how I actually think.
- **Super Deals section with $3.79 and $11.24 prices** — those numbers are literally the point of SHEIN for me. Seeing them right on the homepage confirms this is still the haul spot.
- **Early Summer timing** — bikini season is exactly where my head is at right now, this doesn't feel like a recycled campaign.

## What's weak

- **The "BONUS" tech panel on the right side of the hero** — there are what look like Apple/tablet products competing with the swimwear hero. It's visually fighting itself. I didn't come here for electronics.
- **No visible review counts or ratings** on the Super Deals products — SHEIN's review system is actually one of my main decision tools and it's not showing on this page at all.
- **"Start the fest with hot deals"** — "fest" is giving dad-trying-to-be-cool energy. The copy doesn't actually sound like me or anyone I follow.
- **The 30% off registration block** — it's below fold and aimed at new users. As a returning shopper I feel ignored by my own homepage.
- **Brand Zone section with Apple products ($89.99)** — random electronics in a fashion homepage breaks my mental model of what this site is. Feels like a marketplace grab.
- **No size-inclusivity signal above fold** — I know SHEIN has Curve but the hero models are all one body type, which is a miss.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the tech products from the hero zone** — pull the Bonus/Apple tablet panel out of the above-fold hero area entirely. The swimwear campaign is strong; don't split attention with a random electronics shelf.
- **Swap the generic model shots for UGC-style imagery** — show a real-looking try-on grid or stitch-style photos. That's what makes me trust the clothes will actually fit and that's the TikTok format I'm used to.
- **Surface returning-user loyalty data above fold** — show my points balance or "you're X points from free shipping" in the header for logged-in users. Right now that 30% offer feels like it's talking to someone else.
- **Add review stars to the Super Deals product tiles** — even just a star rating and count makes me feel safer clicking. One extra data point = way more taps.
