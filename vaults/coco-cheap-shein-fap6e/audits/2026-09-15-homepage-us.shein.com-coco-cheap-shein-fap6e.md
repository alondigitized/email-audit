# Homepage snapshot · us.shein.com · 2026-09-15
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- Okay this is giving classic SHEIN chaos — a "FLASH FALL Sale UP TO 90%" banner, Halloween Style tiles, and a trending search for "squishy" all crammed together, but a giant cookie consent wall is sitting right on top of the good stuff before I can even see it. It reads like a brand that knows exactly who I am (broke, online, ready to haul) but hasn't figured out how to get out of my way yet.

## What's working

- **"FLASH FALL Sale UP TO 90%" banner** — big bold sale energy, exactly the kind of thing I'd screenshot for a haul caption
- **The trending search chip "squishy"** with the flame icon — that's a smart touch, feels like it's tracking what's actually trending instead of some generic placeholder
- **Halloween Style + New Arrivals + Hot Sellers tile stack** on the left — gives me three tap-in points immediately without digging
- **Category circle rail** (Tops, Shoes, Underwear & Sleepwear, Cell Phones & Accessories) — SHEIN knows their girlies buy random stuff beyond clothes, that's very "$80 haul that somehow includes a phone case" energy
- **"Enjoy 30% OFF on your first order!" sticky banner** — that's the coupon-stack bait that actually gets me to sign in

## What's weak

- **The cookie consent modal** is taking up the entire center of my screen and blocking the actual sale content — I can't even see the full Flash Fall Sale graphic or the "ROMWE / EMERY ROSE / MOTF" sister-brand banners clearly
- **No clear single CTA button** — there's no "Shop Now" anywhere, just competing buttons (Accept All / Reject All / Sign in) fighting for my thumb
- **This is the mobile web version, not the app** — I literally only ever shop SHEIN in-app for the spin-the-wheel points and app-exclusive coupons, so landing on the website already feels like the wrong door
- **"Free Shipping" has no threshold shown** — is it free on everything or do I need to hit $29? That ambiguity makes me trust it less
- **No loyalty/points teaser** (no "SHEIN Club" or points balance) even though that's usually a huge draw for repeat shoppers like me

## Recommendations

- Kill or shrink the cookie modal footprint — make it a bottom sheet instead of a center-screen wall so the Flash Fall Sale hero isn't hidden
- Add one loud, singular "Shop Flash Sale" button over the hero banner instead of leaving it as a static image
- Put an actual number on "Free Shipping" (e.g. "Free Shipping over $29") so I know if my cart already qualifies
- Swap the generic "Sign in" sticky banner for something coupon-coded like "Claim your 30% off code" — makes it feel like a deal, not a login wall
