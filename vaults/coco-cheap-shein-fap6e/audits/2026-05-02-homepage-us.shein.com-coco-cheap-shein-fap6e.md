- **The nav cuts off** — "Women Clothing, B..." — B what?? Men? Beauty? The horizontal nav is clipped and I can't scroll it visually so I don't know what else is there

## Recommendations

- **Expand the right-side flash sale panel or give it its own full-width banner strip** — the promo text is cropped and unreadable, move it to a scrollable banner above the category tiles where the whole headline fits
- **Add a "Under $X" or price-range filter bubble** in the category row (e.g., "Under $15", "Under $30") — that's the #1 reason I'm here, lean into it visually
- **Rename "Pro Accs" to "Accessories"** — one extra word, zero confusion
