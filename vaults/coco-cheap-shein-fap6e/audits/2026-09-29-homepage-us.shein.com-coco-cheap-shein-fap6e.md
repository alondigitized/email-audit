# Homepage snapshot · us.shein.com · 2026-09-29
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- SHEIN is doing SHEIN: a dense, coupon-forward fast-fashion app-in-a-browser that puts a lot on screen at once. Right now it's pushing fall knits with an "UP TO 50% OFF" hero, a "Halloween Style" tile, and a "30% OFF on your first order" bar. Honestly, though, the cookie wall is parked on top of my first screen and blocks half the page.

## What's working

- **"UP TO 50% OFF" orange starburst:** it's the one thing my eye lands on in the hero, and I'm always hunting for the discount first.
- **Free Shipping / Free Returns strip at the very top:** it's low-key but reassuring, since I'm gambling on sizing with an 8-day delivery.
- **Left-side tiles (Hot Sellers, New Arrivals, Halloween Style):** these are my TikTok-haul shortcuts, and they're seasonal and fast to tap.
- **Category circles (Tops, Shoes, Home & Living):** they're clear and easy to tap, and I can jump straight to Tops.
- **Search bar with trending "squishy 🔥":** it tells me what's popping and it's the first thing I'd use.
- **Sub-brand tiles (ROMWE, EMERY ROSE, MOTF):** these are the aesthetics I actually shop, in one glance.

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal:** it's a huge wall of legalese that covers the hero's bottom half and the Women/Curve/etc. circles. The "if you do not take any action, we will continue to set cookies" line feels sneaky.
- **Sticky "30% OFF on your first order!" bar:** it hides the SuperDeals, Trends and Brand Zone product rails, which is exactly where I'd shop.
- **Hero has no CTA button and no product prices:** I can't tell what the mannequin cutouts cost or where they lead.
- **First-order-only coupon:** I'm a coupon stacker on the app, so a web-only new-user code does nothing for me. There's no app-exclusive nudge.
- **Nav bar:** "Beauty" is cut off at the edge and needs the arrow.
- **No sizing, ratings or review counts anywhere I can see:** for a $80 haul I'd want to see the numbers.
- **No countdown timer or flash-sale clock:** I can't tell if the 50% off ends tonight.

## Recommendations

- Make the cookie banner a slim bottom sheet or a compact top strip with the same Accept / Reject buttons. Then the hero and the category row are visible on load.
- Put a real button on the hero ("Shop Fall Knits – Up to 50% Off") plus one or two prices on the mannequin cutouts, for example "$9.99".
- Add an "App-only: extra coupon" or "Download the app for +15% off" chip in the sticky bar for returning shoppers, and show a coupon or points balance next to the cart icon.
- Add a countdown ("Ends tonight") and the free-shipping threshold ("Free shipping over $XX") to the top strip. I need to know if my $80 haul qualifies.
