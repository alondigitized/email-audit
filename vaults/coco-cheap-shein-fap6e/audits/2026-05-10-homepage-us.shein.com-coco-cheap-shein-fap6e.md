# Homepage snapshot · us.shein.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- ok so this is giving exactly what I expected from SHEIN — fast fashion, loud promo, category grid, no nonsense. It's pushing a summer prep sale (looks like up to 90% off which... sure, SHEIN math) and the vibe is "get in, get cheap, get out." Nothing groundbreaking but it's not embarrassing either — it feels like an app I already have downloaded.

## What's working

- **Free Shipping banner** — sticky at top, no threshold shown, which for a $80 haul shopper means I'm not doing math before I shop. Big green flag.
- **"SUMMER P..." promo block** — the teal/cyan color pops against the dark layout and the "UP TO 90" number is big enough to read at a glance. Catches my eye immediately.
- **Category grid (Women, Curve, Kids, Shoes, Home & Living, Beauty & Health)** — clean tiles with lifestyle photos, one tap to get where I want. The Curve tile with the model in the black set is actually really cute and shows body diversity.
- **Search bar pre-loaded with "bikini"** — whether that's my recent search or a suggestion, it's on-brand for May and feels like the app knows the assignment.
- **"New Arrivals" quick-access button** — I'm an app-only, new-drop chaser. Having it surfaced this high means I'm one tap from fresh inventory without digging through nav.

## What's weak

- **Three stacked left-side buttons (Hot Sellers, New Arrivals, Home & D...)** — this layout is chaotic. Hot Sellers and New Arrivals compete for the same shopper behavior in the same visual slot. Pick one hero action or make them tabs.
- **Summer promo banner text is cut off** — "SUMMER P..." doesn't fully render and the button copy says "PREP & ..." which I literally cannot read. If you want me to tap it, finish the sentence.
- **Zero social proof above the fold** — no review count, no "10M+ sold," no TikTok badge, nothing. SHEIN lives and dies on peer trust and none of it is here.
- **No loyalty/rewards hook** — I coupon-stack. SHEIN has an entire points system and it's invisible on this screen. That's a conversion miss for people like me who buy based on bonus credit.
- **"Home & D..." tile on the quick-access strip** — I'm a college student on a clothes haul. Home decor getting equal billing with Hot Sellers and New Arrivals on this entry strip feels like it's not for me.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the left button stack with a single scrollable category chip bar** — "Women · New In · Sale · Shoes · Curve" as horizontal chips takes less space and feels like TikTok/Instagram shop UI, not a 2018 app.
- **Finish the summer banner copy** — "Summer Prep Sale — Up to 90% Off" should be readable in full; crop or resize the promo block so the CTA button text isn't "PREP & ..." with an ellipsis.
- **Add a points/rewards balance chip under the logo** — "You have 240 points" or "Earn 2x points today" converts returning users immediately without scrolling.
- **Drop one UGC/review signal above the fold** — a "4.6 ★ from 2M reviews" badge or a mini TikTok-style thumbnail strip would instant-boost trust for first-timers and keep skeptics from bouncing.
