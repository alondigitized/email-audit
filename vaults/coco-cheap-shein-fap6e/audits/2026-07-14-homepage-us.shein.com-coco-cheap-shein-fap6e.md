# Homepage snapshot · us.shein.com · 2026-07-14
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-14**
## Executive summary

- Okay so literally the first thing that happens when I open SHEIN is a coupon popup begging me to claim 65% off — which, honestly, as someone who coupon-stacks before every order, I'm not mad at it, but it also blocks the whole homepage so I can't even see what I'm shopping. Past the modal, it's peak SHEIN energy: massive sale banner screaming "90% off," a grid of categories I know by heart, and Super Deals below the fold. This brand is pushing a mega sale event right now and it feels like the sale is the product.

## What's working

- **65% OFF modal coupon** — as someone who literally stacks coupons before every cart checkout, having one handed to me the second I land is actually perfect for my shopping style
- **"Free Shipping" + "Free Returns" top strip** — two of the biggest objections handled in one line before I even look at anything else
- **90% off hero banner** — the number is enormous and impossible to miss; sale hunting is my whole thing so this speaks directly to me
- **Category icon grid** — I can see Women's, Accessories, Beauty, Bags, all the buckets I shop in a $80 haul; one tap to get where I need
- **"Bonus System" badge** — I know I have points on SHEIN and seeing it surfaced immediately reminds me to factor that into my haul math

## What's weak

- **The modal blocks the entire homepage** — I literally cannot see what the sale is actually about because a popup is sitting on top of everything; frustrating even as someone who wants the coupon
- **"Super Deals" pricing is tiny and dark** — the "$12.xx" cards below the fold look compressed and I can barely make out the actual numbers or what the items are
- **No urgency signal I can verify** — I see "23" in the hero area which might be a countdown but it's so buried behind the modal I can't confirm if there's a real timer
- **Hero imagery is mostly sale graphics, not lifestyle** — it's a yellow banner screaming numbers, not a TikTok-aesthetic try-on vibe that would actually make me want to screenshot it for inspo
- **No review counts visible** — for a haul brand where I'm buying 12 items at once, seeing star ratings on the featured products would help me know what's actually worth putting in cart

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the coupon modal after 3 seconds or let it slide in as a bottom sheet** — it currently destroys any chance of reading the hero on load; a bottom sheet keeps the deal visible without nuking the page
- **Swap the yellow sale graphic for a lifestyle/try-on image** — a Gen Z girl in a summer OOTD with the 90% off badge overlaid would perform way better for my demographic than a pure text/number banner
- **Surface a countdown timer clearly** — if this sale ends tonight, show me "Ends in 4:32:11" in the hero; that's the one thing that makes me stop scrolling and start buying
- **Add star ratings + review counts to the Super Deals rail** — even just "★ 4.7 (2.3k)" on each card would remove my "is this actually cute or just cheap?" hesitation
