## Executive summary

- Reading the screenshot now.
- Okay this is literally my app. SHEIN's homepage is practically designed in a lab for girls like me — Memorial Day sale at up to 90% off is front and center, "Hot Sellers" and "New Arrivals" are both right there without any digging, and the search bar has "bikini" already in it which is exactly what I'd be looking for in May. This brand is pushing summer haul season hard right now and it knows exactly who's showing up.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day sale banner** — "UP TO 90" off with a patriotic red/white/blue treatment is immediately visible. I'm already doing mental math on how cheap that $12 dress gets after coupon stacking.
- **"Hot Sellers" and "New Arrivals" banners** — These are the two things I tap every time I open SHEIN. Both are above the fold, both have lifestyle imagery of actual outfits. Perfect placement.
- **"Bikini" pre-filled in search** — Whether that's my search history or SHEIN nudging me, it's exactly on-trend for a May haul. No friction getting to what I want.
- **Category circles (Women, Curve, Kids, Shoes, Beachwear)** — Clean one-tap access, no confusion about where to go.
- **Free Shipping strip at the very top** — First thing I see, before the logo. That's how you open with me.

## What's weak

- **"SHOP THE S..."** — The CTA on the Memorial Day banner is literally cut off. I can see a button but cannot read what it says. That's a viewport problem that should not exist on the hero of a major sale.
- **"Style Refresh Festival"** — This copy is giving 2019 brand-team-meeting energy. "Hot Sellers" and "New Arrivals" are clean; "Style Refresh Festival" sounds like it was named in a Zoom call by someone who has never posted a try-on.
- **No loyalty / rewards callout** — SHEIN has a whole points system. I probably have thousands of points sitting there. Show me my balance and I will spend it. This is free conversion and it's not here.
- **No urgency on the sale** — Memorial Day is an implied deadline but there's no countdown, no "ends May 25," nothing to make me open my wallet right now instead of later.
- **Zero trust signals below Free Shipping** — No return policy mention, no reviews, no badges. For someone new to SHEIN this would feel immediately sketchy.

## Recommendations

- **Add a countdown timer to the Memorial Day banner** — "Sale ends in 2d 14h 33m" would convert me on the spot. SHEIN does this in the app sometimes and it works every time.
- **Surface the rewards balance above the fold** — If I'm logged in, show "You have 3,240 points — use them today." That's pure free-money energy and it prints conversions.
- **Fix the cropped CTA** — "SHOP THE SALE" needs to be fully readable at standard mobile viewport. Either widen the button or adjust the banner layout.
- **Swap "Style Refresh Festival" for FYP-native language** — "What's Blowing Up on TikTok," "FYP Finds," or literally anything that sounds like a person under 25 wrote it.
