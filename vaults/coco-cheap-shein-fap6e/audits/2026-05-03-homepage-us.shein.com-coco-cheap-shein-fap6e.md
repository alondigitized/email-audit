## What's working

- **"Free Shipping | Free Returns | No Hidden Fees" top bar** — this is literally the anxiety I have when shopping SHEIN. Seeing it upfront before I even look at product makes me trust the session immediately.
- **"FLAUNT FRESH FITS UP TO 50% OFF" hero** — copy is in my register, the model is wearing loud fun colors, and 50% off is a real number not a vague "deals await."
- **Category icon grid** — Woman, Curve, Beachwear, Tops, Dresses, Shoes, Jewelry all visible in one swipe-row. I can get to my vibe in literally one tap.
- **Super Deals rail** — $8.82 and $3.21 flash-sale prices are the exact dollar range I stack into an $80 haul. Seeing the strike-through % right on the card (70% off) makes me want to tap immediately.
- **Brand Zone with Supreme + New Balance** — unexpected, adds some legitimacy and tells me there's more here than just unbranded basics.

## What's weak

- **No countdown timer on the Flash Sale** — "Flash Sale" label appears but I see no timer. That urgency cue is missing and it makes the deal feel less real.
- **"Pro Aca" button in the hero sidebar** — I genuinely have no idea what this means. It looks like a cut-off label and it breaks the flow.
- **The ROMWE callout in the hero feels like two competing brands fighting for space** — $14.99 ROMWE alongside the SHEIN hero is confusing. Is this a SHEIN page or a ROMWE collab? At a glance it looks like a render artifact.
- **No personalization hook** — I've ordered from SHEIN before. No "picked for you," no "your size is in stock," nothing. It treats me like a first-timer every session.
- **Registration 30% off email capture is below the fold** — this is a huge offer and I almost missed it entirely. Should be woven into the hero or a sticky bar.

## Recommendations

- **Move the 30% off + Free Shipping registration offer into a sticky top banner or weave it into the hero CTA** — burying a 30% offer below the scroll is leaving money on the table. Make it a pill badge next to "New Arrivals."
- **Add a countdown timer to the Flash Sale cards** — even a fake-ish "Ends in 4h 22m" would triple my urgency to tap that $8.82 item before it's gone.
- **Fix or rename "Pro Aca"** — either spell it out ("Student Discount" or "Back to Campus") or remove it. As a college student I'd actually care, but I can't decode the truncated label.
- **Add a "trending on TikTok" or "#SHEINhaul" editorial rail** — SHEIN lives on TikTok and there's no social proof / trending content module above the fold. A simple "🔥 TikTok Made Me Buy It" strip with 4-5 products would speak directly to how I actually discover things.
