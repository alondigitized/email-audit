## What's working

- **"Hot Sellers" and "New Arrivals" quick buttons**: These are literally my first two taps every time I open SHEIN. Having them labeled, thumbnailed, and right at the top of the screen is muscle memory in button form.
- **The model in the hero**: She's in a fitted pink/red mini dress that I would absolutely throw in a TikTok try-on. The styling is current and wearable at $20.
- **"New In" and "Sale" in the nav bar**: Both tabs I live in, both visible without scrolling. No digging required.
- **Category grid**: Women, Curve, Kids, Shoes, Jewelry — tappable and clean. I can get to my section in one move.
- **Free Shipping announcement strip**: Even without a threshold number, it immediately signals budget-friendly territory and puts me in a buying mindset.

## What's weak

- **Hero promotional text is illegible**: I can see "FI..." "FRE..." "UP T..." but the text is cropped at the banner boundary. I literally cannot read what the deal is. That's a wasted hero.
- **"Free Shipping" with no threshold**: The number changes my haul math. Without it, the banner is basically decorative. Classic SHEIN move where the real terms are buried.
- **No rewards/points balance shown**: I've got points. If I could see "420 pts" in the header, I'd be calculating my discount before I even scroll. That number would drive me to add more to cart.
- **"Pro Accs" is a mystery**: Is that "Pro Access" (a membership tier)? "Accessories" abbreviated? The thumbnail doesn't clarify. On mobile, abbreviations just create hesitation.
- **Split layout creates visual noise**: The three left-column buttons and the right-side hero feel like two competing UIs fighting for my attention instead of one cohesive flow.

## Recommendations

- **Show my rewards balance in the header or top strip**: Even a small chip like "🏆 420 pts" next to the logo would immediately drive cart-padding behavior.
- **Make the hero promo number the biggest thing on screen**: If it's "30% OFF," make that "30%" fill the banner. The partial cropped text is actively worse than no text.
- **Add the free-shipping threshold to the announcement bar**: "$49 ships free" changes my haul calculation. It's a conversion lever sitting right there unused.
- **Spell out "Pro Accs"**: Rename it "Accessories" or "Members" — whatever it actually is. Five extra characters beats a confused tap.
