# Homepage snapshot · www.etsy.com · 2026-06-05
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-05**
## Executive summary

- I opened Etsy expecting to browse handmade goods and gift ideas, and instead I got a wall. The page is a bot-detection block — orange Etsy logo at the top, then "Access is temporarily restricted" in plain text, and a diagnostic block at the bottom explaining I've been flagged for unusual activity. There is no homepage, no product imagery, no navigation — just an error state.

## What's working

- The Etsy wordmark in orange renders cleanly and is immediately recognizable — at least I know I landed on the right domain.
- The "Submit feedback" link gives me a path out if this is a mistake.

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is absent.** I see "Access is temporarily restricted" where the shopping experience should be.
- **The diagnostic copy is cold and technical** — "Automated (bot) activity on your network (IP 75.140.9.0)" is jargon that reads like a server log, not a brand message.
- **No recovery CTA for legitimate users** — there's no "Try again," no CAPTCHA, no way to prove I'm human and get back to browsing.
- **A real shopper who taps quickly on mobile** (which I absolutely do) is punished — one of the stated reasons for the block is "Rapid taps or clicks," which is normal touch behavior.
- **White void between the headline and the diagnostic block** — the middle third of the screen is completely empty, making the page feel broken rather than intentional.

## Recommendations

- **Add a CAPTCHA or "I'm not a robot" verification flow** instead of a dead-end block page — let me prove I'm human and immediately route me back to the homepage I came for.
- **Replace the IP/technical language with plain-English copy** ("We want to make sure you're not a bot — tap below to continue") so it feels like a brand moment, not a server error.
- **Increase the tap-threshold before flagging** — mobile users browse fast; penalizing quick taps alienates exactly the engaged shoppers Etsy wants.
- **Surface a minimal trust anchor** (the Etsy star rating, "Shop from 7M+ sellers," anything) even on the block page so the brand impression isn't purely negative while the user waits.
