# Homepage snapshot · www.etsy.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 0/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- Okay so I didn't even get to see the homepage — Etsy hit me with a bot verification wall the moment I landed. What I'm looking at is a "Verification Required" CAPTCHA challenge with a slider puzzle and a clinical explanation about "unusual activity." As a real person who just wanted to browse for a birthday gift, this is a pretty rough first impression for a platform that prides itself on handmade warmth.
- **0/10**

## What's working

- **The Etsy wordmark** — even on a wall of friction, the familiar orange serif logo still reads warm and recognizable. At least I know where I am.
- **Audio CAPTCHA option** — the image/speaker tab toggle is there, which shows some accessibility awareness.

## What's weak

- **Full homepage blocked before any content** — I haven't seen a single product, category, or value prop. For a gifter who just opened the app on her phone, this is dead on arrival.
- **The error copy is cold and technical** — "Automated (bot) activity on your network (IP 47.41.15.19)" reads like a sysadmin log. Nobody shopping for a custom name sign wants to see their IP address.
- **"Slide right to secure your access"** — the slider CTA is generic and a little intimidating. "Secure your access" sounds like a bank, not a small-seller marketplace.
- **No reassurance or brand warmth** — there's zero copy that says "We'll get you back to browsing in a second!" Just a wall of bullet points listing reasons I might be a bot.
- **"Rapid taps or clicks" is accusatory** — I'm on my phone. I tap fast. Listing that as suspicious behavior feels hostile.
- **No path to the homepage anyway** — if I can't or don't complete the slider, I'm just stuck. No guest browse link, no "skip for now."

## Recommendations

- **Rewrite the explanation copy** — replace the bullet list of bot-crime accusations with something like "Just making sure it's you — tap below to keep shopping." One sentence, warm, done.
- **Remove the IP address from user-facing copy** — showing `47.41.15.19` to a shopper adds zero value and reads as surveillance-y.
- **Add a micro-moment of brand personality** — even a small illustration of a handmade product or a line like "Good finds are worth the extra second" would soften the gate and remind me why I came.
- **Offer a fallback browse path** — let me see trending categories or a featured collection while the CAPTCHA resolves, so the visit isn't a dead stop.
