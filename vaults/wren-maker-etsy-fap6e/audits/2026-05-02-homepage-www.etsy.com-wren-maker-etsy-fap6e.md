# Homepage snapshot · www.etsy.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Full review
Okay, so I opened Etsy and immediately hit a wall — literally. The orange logo loads, then nothing but "Access is temporarily restricted." No products, no hero, no categories, no sale banners. Just a cold block screen telling me my device looks suspicious. It lists reasons: rapid taps, bot activity, developer tools. I wasn't doing anything weird — I just opened the app.

This is the worst possible first impression for a return customer. I *live* on Etsy. I have favorites lists, I leave photo reviews, I filter by Star Seller as a default — and the homepage greets me with an accusation and a session ID number like I'm being logged for something. There's not even a "tap here to continue" or a CAPTCHA. Just… nothing. A dead end with a feedback link.

What really stings is that Etsy built its whole brand on warm, human, handmade — and this screen is the exact opposite of that. It's cold, corporate, and completely automated. Not a single product, no "here's what's trending," no "gifts for your people." The orange logo sitting above "Access is temporarily restricted" almost feels like a joke. The brand is there and then immediately absent.

I'd close the tab and come back later, maybe on desktop. Or honestly just go straight to a shop I already follow. This doesn't make me want to browse — it makes me feel like I did something wrong when I didn't.

**1/10**
