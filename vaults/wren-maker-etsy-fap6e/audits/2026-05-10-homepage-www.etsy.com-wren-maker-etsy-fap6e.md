# Homepage snapshot · www.etsy.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- I opened Etsy expecting to browse for Mother's Day gift ideas — handmade candle holders, custom name signs, the usual — and instead got a wall. This isn't a homepage experience at all; it's a bot-detection block page. The only thing visible is the orange Etsy wordmark, an "Access is temporarily restricted" message, and a list of reasons I might be flagged. As a real human shopper, this is jarring and embarrassing — it feels like I got accused of something.

## What's working

- **The Etsy wordmark** renders correctly in the brand's orange — at least I know I'm in the right place.
- **The "Submit feedback" link** is visible and tappable if I want to flag the issue — a minimal recovery path exists.

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is missing** — replaced by a block page that shows absolutely nothing shoppable.
- **"Access is temporarily restricted"** is cold, clinical copy that feels accusatory toward a real customer; zero warmth, zero brand voice.
- **The reason list** ("Automated bot activity on your network") names my IP address (47.41.15.19) publicly on screen — that feels weirdly invasive.
- **No navigation, no search bar, no categories** — I can't route around the block to anything useful.
- **Blank white body area** between the logo and the error panel looks like a broken layout with a giant white void.

## Recommendations

- **Show a friendlier fallback**: Swap the clinical block message for something on-brand — "Hold on, we're checking things! Tap here to continue as a human" — with a CAPTCHA or "I'm not a robot" tap, not just a feedback link.
- **Preserve the search bar**: Even if full page load is blocked, render the search input so a real shopper can still type "custom name sign" and get through.
- **Remove the IP address from the visible message** — showing a shopper their raw IP is jarring and unnecessary for the end-user message.
- **Add a one-tap "Try again" CTA** with a reload button styled in Etsy orange so the path forward is obvious.
