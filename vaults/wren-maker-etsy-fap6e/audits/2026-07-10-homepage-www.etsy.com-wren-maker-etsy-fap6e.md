# Homepage snapshot · www.etsy.com · 2026-07-10
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-10**
## Executive summary

- I opened Etsy expecting to see handmade candle holders and custom name signs and instead got a wall of white with an orange logo and a block message. This isn't a homepage — it's an access restriction page, and from where I'm standing it reads like I just got accused of being a bot. Not exactly the warm, crafty welcome I was hoping for.

## What's working

- The Etsy logo renders correctly in the signature orange — so I at least know I'm on the right site.
- "Submit feedback" link exists, which gives me a tiny path forward if I want to fight through this.

## What's weak

- **The entire above-the-fold experience is a rejection screen** — "Access is temporarily restricted" is the headline. That's catastrophic for engagement.
- **The error message lists "rapid taps or clicks" as a reason**, which feels like getting scolded for how I browse on my phone — that's not my fault.
- **IP address exposed publicly** (`75.140.9.0`) in the body copy — weirdly technical and unfriendly for a marketplace that positions itself as human and handmade.
- **Zero path back to shopping** — no "try again," no search bar, no redirect to a CAPTCHA challenge. Just a dead end.
- **White void between the logo and the error block** — a huge empty gray area with nothing in it, making the page feel broken even beyond the access issue.

## Recommendations

- **Add a CAPTCHA or "I'm not a robot" challenge** instead of a hard block — let me prove I'm human and get back to browsing in seconds rather than giving me a feedback form.
- **Include a "Go to homepage" button** so shoppers who hit this page by accident aren't completely stranded.
- **Soften the language** — "We just need to confirm you're human" lands completely differently than "We detected unusual activity from your device or network."
- **Remove the raw IP address** from the user-facing copy — that's a developer/ops detail, not shopper-friendly messaging.
