# Homepage snapshot · www.etsy.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- I opened Etsy on my phone and hit a wall — literally the first thing I see is "Access is temporarily restricted." There's no homepage, no hero, no products, nothing. Etsy's bot-detection flagged the visit and I'm staring at a white screen with an error card and an incident ID. As far as first impressions go, this is about as bad as it gets.

## What's working

- The Etsy wordmark in orange renders correctly and is immediately recognizable — I at least know what site I'm on.
- The "Submit feedback" link is present, so there's a nominal escape hatch.

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is missing.** All I see is "Access is temporarily restricted" — no categories, no hero, no products, nothing I came here for.
- **The error copy is cold and technical.** "Automated (bot) activity," "developer or inspection tools," "IP 75.140.9.0" — that reads like a server error log, not a message to a customer. It's accusatory and off-putting.
- **No retry CTA.** There's no "Try again" button, no refresh prompt, no path back into the shopping experience. I'm just stranded.
- **Massive blank white space** fills most of the viewport between the headline and the explanatory text block — the layout is broken and visually dead.
- **"Rapid taps or clicks" as a listed reason** is particularly bad UX. I tap things — I'm a shopper on a phone. Telling me my normal behavior got me flagged is frustrating.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Reload Page" button** directly in the restriction card so users have a one-tap path back — right now there's no recovery affordance at all.
- **Rewrite the error copy in human language.** Replace the technical bullet list with one warm sentence: "We hit a snag — tap below to try loading the page again." Drop the IP address and bot language entirely.
- **Tune the bot-detection threshold for mobile.** A real shopper tapping quickly on a phone should not trigger this gate — false positives here burn customer trust on the most common device type.
- **Add a guest search bar** as a fallback so blocked users can at least attempt a search and re-enter the funnel, rather than landing on a fully dead end.
