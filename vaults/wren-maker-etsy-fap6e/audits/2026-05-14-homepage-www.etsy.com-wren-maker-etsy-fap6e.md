# Homepage snapshot · www.etsy.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- I opened Etsy and got hit with a wall — literally a white screen with the Etsy logo and "Access is temporarily restricted." No hero, no products, no categories, nothing. This isn't a homepage experience; it's a bot-detection block page that Etsy served instead of the actual storefront, and as a real shopper on my phone, I'd have no idea why I'm being locked out.

## What's working

- The Etsy logo renders correctly and is recognizable — at least I know what site I'm on
- The "Submit feedback" link is present and functional-looking, giving me one escape hatch

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage:** I never saw it. A bot-detection gate served in place of the actual storefront is a catastrophic funnel failure for any real shopper
- **"Access is temporarily restricted" message:** clinical, cold, zero brand warmth — Etsy is supposed to feel like a cozy marketplace, not a firewall
- **Error copy tone:** bullet-pointing "Automated (bot) activity" and "developer or inspection tools" makes a legitimate shopper feel accused, not welcomed
- **No recovery path:** there's a "Submit feedback" link but no "Try again" button, no redirect to the homepage, no alternative entry point — I'm just stuck
- **Blank white void:** the large empty space between the error headline and the explanation block looks broken and unintentional

## Recommendations

- **Add a graceful fallback CTA:** a prominent "Try again" or "Go to homepage" button below the error message so a real user isn't stranded
- **Rewrite the block copy in Etsy's voice:** something like "Hmm, we hit a snag — tap below to try again" lands softer than listing reasons I might be a bot
- **Serve a degraded but real homepage for borderline sessions:** show cached product rails and categories even under light bot-suspicion, so a false positive doesn't fully kill the session
- **Surface brand warmth in the error state:** even a single lifestyle image or warm brand color in the background would signal "you're in the right place, just one moment"
