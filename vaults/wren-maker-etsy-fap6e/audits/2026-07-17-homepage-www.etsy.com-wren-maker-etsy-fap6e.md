# Homepage snapshot · www.etsy.com · 2026-07-17
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-17**
## Executive summary

- I opened Etsy on my phone and instead of the homepage I got a bot-wall. The Etsy logo is there in its familiar orange serif, but the entire screen below it is just an access-restriction error page — no products, no categories, no hero, nothing. This is a complete failure to deliver any brand experience whatsoever.

## What's working

- The Etsy wordmark in orange renders correctly and is immediately recognizable — at least I know I'm on the right site before I hit the wall.
- The "Submit feedback" link is functional-looking, so there's technically an escape hatch.

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is blocked** — "Access is temporarily restricted" with a white void where every product, hero, and CTA should be. I can't browse, shop, or do anything.
- **The error message is cold and technical** — bullet points listing "Automated (bot) activity," "JavaScript disabled," and "Use of developer or inspection tools" reads like a server admin log, not a message to a real shopper. No warmth, no Etsy voice at all.
- **IP address exposure in the error** — showing `47.229.200.33` publicly on-screen is an odd choice; it doesn't help me as a user and looks sloppy.
- **No recovery path for a real user** — there's no "try again" button, no link to the homepage, no CAPTCHA to prove I'm human. Just a dead end.
- **The "Submit feedback" link is the only action** — not "go back," not "refresh," not "sign in to verify." For a marketplace, abandonment here is 100%.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the generic bot-wall copy with an on-brand human-friendly message** — something like "Oops, something went sideways. Let's get you back to browsing handmade goods." with a clear "Go to Homepage" button.
- **Add a CAPTCHA or verification flow** so real shoppers can prove humanity and immediately land on the actual homepage rather than hitting a dead end.
- **Remove the raw IP address from the user-visible error page** — it adds zero value for shoppers and looks unpolished.
- **Surface a minimal fallback experience** — even just the search bar and top-level nav categories — so shoppers who trigger the block don't lose their session entirely.
