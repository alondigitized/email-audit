# Homepage snapshot · www.etsy.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I literally got blocked. The page that loaded is not Etsy's homepage — it's a bot-detection wall that says "Access is temporarily restricted" with my IP address listed out in plain text. As a real shopper who browses Etsy constantly for gifts and vintage finds, this is a jarring and embarrassing first impression. There's nothing to shop, nothing to discover, no handmade anything — just a cold error screen.

## What's working

- **Etsy logo** renders correctly in its signature orange — at least I know I'm on the right domain and didn't typo something.
- **"Submit feedback" link** gives me one escape route, which is better than a dead end with no action at all.

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is missing.** What loaded is a bot-detection interstitial — no products, no categories, no hero, nothing shoppable.
- **IP address exposed publicly** ("IP 47.41.15.19") in the body copy — feels clinical and vaguely alarming for a shopper who just wanted to browse candle holders.
- **Zero context or empathy in the copy.** "Unusual activity" and "bot activity" read accusatory to a normal person on their phone. A real customer would feel blamed.
- **No retry or home button.** The only CTA is "Submit feedback" — there's no "Go back to Etsy" or "Try again" link. I'm just stuck.
- **Massive empty white space** takes up most of the screen between the logo and the error explanation — visually dead.

## Recommendations

- **Add a clear "Try again / Go to Etsy homepage" button** directly on this page so shoppers aren't stranded — one tap back into the funnel is table stakes.
- **Rewrite the error copy in a warmer, Etsy-appropriate tone:** something like "Oops, we hit a snag — try refreshing or come back in a moment" rather than listing bot-detection technical reasons that make a real customer feel accused.
- **Remove the raw IP address from the user-facing message** — it adds no value to a shopper and reads as surveillance-y.
- **Shrink the dead whitespace** or fill it with a soft Etsy brand illustration so the page at least feels on-brand while I wait to retry.
