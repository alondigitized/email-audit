# Homepage snapshot · www.etsy.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- I opened Etsy on my phone and got a wall — literally a blank white screen with "Access is temporarily restricted" and a bot-detection message. No products, no hero, no categories, nothing to browse. From where I'm standing this looks less like a marketplace and more like a velvet rope that just turned me away at the door.

## What's working

- **Clean error rendering:** The restriction page itself has no broken layout, the orange Etsy wordmark is crisp and on-brand, and the body copy is easy to read in plain English.
- **Feedback link:** "Submit feedback." is there if a real user wants to escalate — at least there's an escape hatch, even if it's a thin one.

## What's weak

- **Zero commerce:** No hero, no categories, no products, no navigation — the entire shopping experience is missing for this visit.
- **Bot-block language is alarming for a real user:** "Automated (bot) activity on your network" and showing a raw IP address (75.140.9.0) feels accusatory and technical; a real shopper hitting this would feel like they did something wrong.
- **No recovery path to actual shopping:** There's no "try again" button, no redirect to homepage, no suggested search — just a dead end and a feedback form.
- **Loyalty and personalization are completely absent:** As a high-value returning buyer who leaves photo reviews and spends on gifts, Etsy has nothing to recognize me with here.
- **Seasonal/campaign relevance:** Zilch. I have no idea if there's a Mother's Day gifting push, a handmade home sale, anything.

## Recommendations

- **Add a user-friendly retry CTA:** Replace the raw IP error block with a "Refresh and try again" button and a warmer message — something like "Oops, we thought you were a bot. You're not, right? Let's try that again." That's very Etsy-voice and keeps real shoppers from bouncing permanently.
- **Implement soft-block fallback with cached homepage shell:** Show at minimum the nav, search bar, and a static hero image even while the JS challenge resolves — users shouldn't see white space while verification runs.
- **Replace technical copy with human copy:** Swap "Automated (bot) activity on your network (IP 75.140.9.0)" for "Something looked unusual — try refreshing or switching to your home Wi-Fi." No raw IPs, no jargon.
- **Surface the feedback link more prominently:** Move "Submit feedback" into a styled button with contrast rather than a plain text link buried below a technical explanation.
