# Homepage snapshot · www.etsy.com · 2026-05-29
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-29**
## Executive summary

- I opened Etsy expecting to browse for a custom name sign or a candle holder for a friend's birthday, and instead I got a wall of white and a bot-block notice. This isn't a homepage — it's an access restriction page, which means I'm getting zero brand experience, zero product discovery, and a pretty rude first impression. From where I'm standing, Etsy looks like it doesn't want me here.

## What's working

- The Etsy wordmark in orange renders correctly and is recognizable — at least I know whose wall I hit.
- The "Submit feedback" link is functional — at minimum there's one escape valve.

## What's weak

- **The entire above-the-fold experience is missing** — a returning logged-in shopper (me, with my Star Seller filter saved) sees nothing but a white void.
- **"Access is temporarily restricted" copy is cold and robotic** — no empathy, no reassurance, nothing that sounds remotely like a handmade-goods marketplace that's supposed to feel warm and human.
- **Bot-block triggers listed in plain text** ("Automated (bot) activity," "developer or inspection tools") — reading that as a real shopper makes me feel accused and unwelcome, not helped.
- **Zero navigation** — I can't tap into Handmade Gifts, Custom Signs, or anything I actually came for. Dead end.
- **Blank middle section** — the large white gap between the logo and the error text reads like broken CSS or a failed JavaScript render, which erodes trust fast.

## Recommendations

- **Humanize the block page** — swap the clinical list of reasons for one warm sentence: "Looks like something went sideways — tap below and we'll sort it out." Match Etsy's brand voice.
- **Add a branded illustration or product photo** to the block page so the visual identity stays intact even in error states; the all-white canvas currently reads as "site is down."
- **Surface a soft CTA** like "Try searching instead" with a search bar — even on an error screen, give me a path forward rather than a dead stop.
- **Investigate bot-detection false-positive rate on mobile** — if real shoppers on phone browsers are hitting this, the signal thresholds need tuning; losing a handmade-gifter mid-browse session is a real conversion loss.
