# We're crushing on new-in Topshop
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-27**
## Executive summary

- This is ASOS pushing a single-brand editorial for Topshop — no discount, no urgency, pure new-in styling. Same playbook as the July 20 "Your edit is here" and July 22 "New arrivals" sends: a hero fashion shot, a product grid, two identical "SHOP" CTAs, done. The image itself is nicely art-directed (the wildflower-field styling gives it a heritage/summer editorial feel) and the copy line ("floaty chiffon, sheer jerseys, lace trims") is more descriptive than the last few sends, but there's still zero commercial hook — no price, no offer, no reason to act today.
- Coming right off the July 26 "up to 40% extra off" stacked-sale send (7/10), this reads as a jarring cadence swing — full-price editorial the day after a discount blitz. Subscribers who just saw a hard sale push aren't primed for a brand-story email 24 hours later. It's competent creative with no commercial teeth, and it's the fourth no-offer editorial send in about a week.

## What's working

- Clean, on-brand hero image — strong model shot with a distinct seasonal/pastoral aesthetic that differentiates Topshop from ASOS's usual studio shots.
- Product grid (5 images) shows real range: dresses, tailored denim with lace-up detail, and outerwear — good variety in one scroll.
- Two clear CTAs ("SHOP TOPSHOP" and "SHOP NOW") both legible, high-contrast, unambiguous.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text is crisp, no overlap or broken layout.

## What's weak

- No offer of any kind — no %, no code, no free shipping, nothing to click for beyond browsing.
- Subject "We're crushing on new-in Topshop" is vague personalization theater, same complaint as July 20's "Your edit is here" — brand voice, not a reason to open.
- No urgency or time-boundedness anywhere in the body.
- Body copy is a single sentence — no sizing, fit, or product-specific detail to support a click decision.
- Third editorial-only send in eight days (July 20, July 22 x2, now July 27) — cadence fatigue risk for anyone not shopping full-price.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a concrete commercial hook — even a soft one ("Free returns on all Topshop" or "New in, from £15") would lift both open and click likelihood; pure editorial sends are the lowest-performing format in this brand's own history (consistently 4/10).
- 2. Tighten the subject to name the actual trend/product instead of "crushing on" — specificity beat vague phrasing every time in this brand's history (July 18 "Extra 30% off" scored 8/10 vs. July 20/22 editorial sends at 4/10).
- 3. Add a preheader with real content — currently leaking junk, wasting prime inbox real estate.
- 4. Space editorial-only sends further from sale sends — going from a 40%-off blitz (July 26) straight into full-price browsing (July 27) whiplashes the subscriber; hold editorial for a day with more gap from the last discount push.
- 5. Add 1-2 lines of product-specific copy (fabric, fit, or "new season staples") near the grid to give browsers a reason to click a specific item, not just "shop now."
