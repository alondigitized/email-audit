# Homepage snapshot · www.asos.com · 2026-05-05
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-05**
## Executive summary

- OK so ASOS is going full editorial minimalism right now — split-screen couple hero, zero campaign copy, just the logo and two big white buttons. It looks expensive and clean which I actually respect, but there's basically nothing telling me *why* I should tap in today. As a Premier member who already shops here weekly, there's nothing here for me specifically — no member perks, no new arrivals, no "hey, here's what just dropped."
- **6/10**
- + Hero imagery speaks to persona — young, editorial, fashion-forward. The woman in red with the beret is very much my aesthetic.
- − No returning-shopper hook visible — no Premier badge, no recently viewed, no rewards balance.
- − No concrete offer above the fold — "first-timer discount" is partially cut off below, and that's not for me anyway as an existing member.
- + Primary CTA clearly visible — "SHOP WOMENS" is unmissable above the fold.
- + Visual hierarchy is clear — split hero, two buttons, eyes know exactly where to land.
- + No render bugs — layout is clean, no overlapping text, no broken images.
- + Demographic signals match — young model, high-fashion editorial, Gen Z energy.
- − No current campaign or season called out — nothing says "Spring," "Festival," "New In," or any campaign.
- − Loyalty/membership benefits not visible without scrolling.
- − No offer to assess for honesty — score 6.

## What's working

- **Split-screen hero with "SHOP WOMENS" / "SHOP MENS"** — instant gender routing, zero friction, exactly what you want on mobile.
- **Editorial black-and-white + colour contrast** — the woman's red jacket pops hard against the muted male side. Arresting, very current, feels premium.
- **Clean mobile nav bar** — search, account, wishlist, and bag are all one tap from wherever I am. That's the ASOS muscle memory I rely on.
- **"New here? Get your first-timer discount"** tile is partially visible below — smart acquisition hook for new visitors landing here from an ad.

## What's weak

- **Zero Premier/loyalty acknowledgement above the fold** — I spend serious money on this site and there's nothing that says "hey Mia, welcome back." No member pricing badge, no "Premier exclusive" banner, nothing.
- **No seasonal or trend hook** — mid-May and not a single word about festival season, summer, or "new in." That's a massive miss for my use case (festival outfit planning).
- **"First-timer discount" tile is useless for me** — it's real estate wasted on someone who already has Premier. A returning member should see something like "new in denim this week."
- **No trust signals at all** — no free returns reminder, no "Klarna available," no review count. Those close hesitant taps and I'd expect to see at least one above the fold.
- **Both CTAs are equally sized and equally weighted** — "SHOP WOMENS" and "SHOP MENS" sit in identical buttons. For a site where womens is likely 70%+ of traffic, the womens CTA should be primary.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "New In" or "Festival Edit" strip** directly below the hero — even a single scrollable row of product thumbnails with "Just Dropped" copy would dramatically increase my scroll depth.
- **Swap the first-timer tile for a Premier member tile** — returning logged-in members should see "Premier member? Your next-day delivery is ready" or a loyalty points balance. Personalise this by login state.
- **Give the "SHOP WOMENS" button primary visual weight** — make it solid black fill, demote "SHOP MENS" to an outline/ghost button. Reflect the actual traffic split.
- **Pin one trend-led copy line to the hero** — even a single line like "Festival season is here" overlaid on the image anchors this to now and gives me a reason to tap beyond habit.
