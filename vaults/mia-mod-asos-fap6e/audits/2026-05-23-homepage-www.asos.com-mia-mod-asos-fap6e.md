# Homepage snapshot · www.asos.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Ok so ASOS is doing the classic split-hero thing — woman on the left serving big sunnies energy, guy on the right in this rust polo, "STYLE EACH MOMENT" in that clean white logo font. It reads premium-ish, very editorial, but weirdly non-committal for a brand I literally pay a Premier subscription for. There's zero personalisation above the fold and the only two options they're giving me are "SHOP WOMENS" or "SHOP MENS" — that's it. In 2026 that feels lazy.

## What's working

- **The split hero visual** — woman in the statement sunnies immediately catches my eye, feels like a festival-edit or a heat-of-summer campaign, which is exactly my mode right now
- **"SHOP WOMENS" button** — high contrast, white pill, dead centre, no ambiguity, taps cleanly on mobile
- **Clean mobile layout** — hamburger menu, search, account, wishlist, bag all in the top bar in the right order; nothing is cluttered or fighting for space
- **The editorial tone** — dark nav, light hero, bold logo overlay feels considered and brand-consistent, not like a generic e-commerce template

## What's weak

- **"STYLE EACH MOMENT" tagline does absolutely nothing** — that could be literally any fashion brand in 2016; it says nothing about what I'm here to shop or why today is the moment to buy
- **"New here? Get your first-timer discount"** — I'm a Premier member with purchase history and this is what you're leading with below the fold? That module should be conditional; for me it's noise, and kind of annoying
- **No Premier acknowledgement anywhere** — I'm paying a subscription fee and there's zero personalisation, no "Your Premier benefits", no free-delivery reminder, no exclusive-drops callout; makes the subscription feel pointless from the home screen
- **No offer, no urgency, no festival hook** — it's late May, festival season is literally happening, ASOS could be screaming "festival fits" at me but instead I get a vague split editorial with no directional campaign energy
- **App download nudge competing for space** — "Download our app for exclusive discounts and the latest drops" is showing me this *on mobile*, which means I'm either already on the app (and this is redundant) or I'm in mobile web and you're trying to bounce me somewhere else; either way it's clunky

## Recommendations

- **Swap the generic tagline for a live campaign hook** — it's May 2026 and festival season is on; "Your festival fits, next-day delivery" with a Coachella/Glastonbury nod would make me tap immediately
- **Add a Premier member strip or personalised banner** — even a thin sticky bar saying "Premier | Free unlimited next-day delivery" reminds me why I'm here and not on ZARA
- **Replace the "New here" module with a new-in rail** — show me 4-6 thumbnail product cards of new womenswear drops; that's the hook that makes me scroll, not an acquisition promo aimed at someone who isn't me
- **Make the hero CTA category-specific** — instead of generic "SHOP WOMENS," test "Shop Festival Fits" or "New In: Denim" during active campaigns; one tap to my actual shopping intent, not to a 50,000-product category page
