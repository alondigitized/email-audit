# Homepage snapshot · www.asos.com · 2026-07-03
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-03**
## Executive summary

- Okay so ASOS knows the assignment aesthetically — two young, hot models, summery outfits, clean mobile layout. It feels like the brand I know, but it's giving very generic fashion homepage energy rather than anything that feels personal to me as a Premier member who's been shopping here for years. Right now it's pushing warm-weather fashion with zero specificity about what's actually new, on offer, or worth tapping.

## What's working

- **Dual hero CTAs ("SHOP WOMENS" / "SHOP MENS")** — instantly splits the audience, no friction finding my lane
- **Model casting** — the female model is wearing a bold black-and-white patterned halter piece that genuinely reads as festival/occasion, which is exactly my shopping trigger
- **Light blue background palette** — airy and summery, feels right for early July, doesn't look like holdover content
- **Mobile nav bar** — search, account, wishlist, bag are all top-row, thumb-reachable; the wishlist heart is prominent which matters to how I actually browse
- **"New here? Get your first-timer discount" strip** — smart acquisition play below the fold, clearly separated from the hero so it doesn't clutter the main message

## What's weak

- **Zero Premier/loyalty acknowledgement** — I pay for ASOS Premier and there is literally no signal that it matters here. No "free next-day" reminder, no member badge, nothing. Makes it feel like ASOS doesn't know me at all
- **"STYLE EACH MOMENT" copy is inert** — it's not wrong but it says nothing. Doesn't tell me what's new, what's on, or why I should tap today versus any other day
- **No offer specificity anywhere in the viewport** — the "first-timer discount" below fold is acquisition-only, so as a returning shopper I have no hook
- **No category depth visible** — denim, festival, going-out — my actual shopping categories — are completely absent. I'd have to drill into nav to find them
- **"Download our app" strip is noise for me** — I'm already on mobile, almost certainly already in the app. This real estate is wasted on a returning mobile user

## Recommendations

- **Add a Premier member strip above the hero** — even one line: "Premier: free next-day on everything. Every time." That's the thing that makes me stay loyal, and it's invisible right now
- **Make the hero copy scannable and specific** — swap "STYLE EACH MOMENT" for something campaign-tied, e.g. "Festival Dresses. New In Daily." — gives me a reason to tap *today*
- **Add one active offer to the hero** — a percentage-off banner or a "New in: 200+ styles this week" counter would do it; right now there's no urgency signal at all
- **Surface a quick-category bar below the CTAs** — a horizontal scroll of chips ("Denim", "Festival", "Going Out", "New In") would let me route in one tap instead of burrowing into the hamburger menu
