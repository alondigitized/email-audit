# Final hours of Sale: up to 40% extra off selected styles
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-26**
## Executive summary

- This is ASOS running the same "extra % off on top of Sale" mechanic it ran on July 18 (extra 30% off, scored 8/10) and July 20 (up to 70% off Sale, scored 4/10), except this time it's stacking both: "up to 40% extra off... on top of up to 70% off." The hero is doing real work — big pink block, clear code (LASTCHANCE), a real deadline (Monday 8am BST), and two unambiguous CTAs. It's a stronger send than the July 20 generic-sale email because there's an actual code and a real cutoff, closer in spirit to July 18's playbook.
- The crack is in the urgency copy itself: the hero says "The Sale ends tonight — this is your last chance," but two lines down it says "Ends Monday 8am BST." Today reads Sunday-night urgency, but the actual deadline is nearly 36 hours later — that's a credibility hit for a subscriber who's seen ASOS cry wolf on urgency copy before (this brand has now sent three "final"/deadline-driven sales inside ten days, including July 18's Monday deadline).
- Net: a competently built discount email with a real, favorable offer, undercut by a self-contradicting urgency line and sale fatigue from the near-weekly cadence.

## What's working

- Hero leads with a specific, meaningful stacked discount (40% extra + 70% base) — one of the stronger discount depths in this brand's recent sends.
- A real promo code (LASTCHANCE) and a real deadline are both visible in the hero, not buried in footer fine print.
- Two clean, high-contrast CTA buttons (Shop Women's / Shop Men's) sit right under the offer with no scrolling needed.
- The product grid below the hero shows six distinct, well-shot items (dress, knitwear, denim, gold heels, jumpsuit, skirt) rather than a generic lifestyle shot, giving the eye something concrete to latch onto.
- A second CTA row (Bestsellers / New Lines / Selling Fast / Holiday) gives multiple entry points for different shopper intents.

## What's weak

- The hero copy is internally inconsistent: "Sale ends tonight" directly contradicts "Ends Monday 8am BST" a few lines later — this is a visible, readable contradiction, not a nitpick.
- No women's or men's model imagery differentiates the two "Shop Women's / Shop Men's" CTAs — every product shot in the grid is on a female model, so the Men's CTA has zero visual support.
- This is the third stacked/urgency sale variant from ASOS in under two weeks (July 18 extra-30%, July 20 up-to-70%, now extra-40%-on-70%) — the "final hours" framing reads less credible each time it repeats.
- The scrolling ticker banner ("SALE: FINAL HOURS...") is cut off mid-word at the frame edge, giving the top of the email an unfinished, glitchy first impression.
- Footer fine print caps the deal hard (max £500 pre-discount spend, marked-down styles only, no stacking with other codes/vouchers/Premier) — the "up to 40% extra" headline oversells what a typical basket will actually realize.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the urgency contradiction first — pick one deadline and say it once. If it truly ends Monday 8am BST, drop "ends tonight" entirely; if there's a tonight cutoff for something (e.g., best stock), separate that claim from the code's actual expiry.
- 2. Add at least one menswear product shot to justify the "Shop Men's" CTA — right now the entire visual grid signals "this is a women's email" and men's is an afterthought button.
- 3. Surface the £500 spend cap or "marked-down styles only" caveat as a small line near the hero code, not just in dense footer legal text — it protects trust and reduces post-click frustration/cart abandonment.
- 4. Vary the mechanic next send — three "stack a discount on top of Sale" emails in ten days is measurable fatigue; consider a non-discount hook (like the July 15 Rina Lipa influencer send that hit 9/10) to reset the pattern.
- 5. Trim the subject line under 50 characters for mobile truncation safety.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Final hours: extra 40% off Sale ends Monday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call — 40% extra off Sale, code inside`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack LASTCHANCE on top of up to 70% off — gone Monday 8am`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your favorites, even cheaper. One code, one deadline.`
