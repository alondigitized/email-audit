# Need some inspo?
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-25**
## Executive summary

- ASOS is back to editorial mode after yesterday's "Up to 30% off" — and this one feels like a step backward. "Need some inspo?" is one of the laziest subject lines a fashion brand can send: zero offer, zero specificity, no hook beyond a vague invitation to browse. The render itself is fine — clean photography, organized grid, the now-familiar "shop new in" rhythm — but there is nothing in this email that wasn't in the May 18 "What's hot this summer" or May 22 leopard edit, both of which had stronger creative angles.
- The body does some quiet work: a "Most Hyped" / Back in Stock module, a top-brands row (New Balance, Levi's, Tommy etc.), and a Download the App nudge. But the hero headline is the subject line repeated verbatim with a sub-line ("Shop the new-in pieces we think you'll love") that adds nothing.
- Net: a filler broadcast. After yesterday's promo, a content-only follow-up makes sense in cadence, but the creative needs a point of view to justify the send. This one doesn't have one.

## What's working

- Photography is strong and on-brand — confident model shots, clean white backgrounds.
- "Most Hyped / Back in Stock" module is a legitimately useful merch hook — scarcity + social proof.
- Top-brands row (New Balance, Levi's, Tommy, Lacoste) signals breadth and pulls brand-loyal shoppers.
- Layout is clean, mobile-friendly, no render bugs or overlapping text.
- "Download the App" CTA gets prominent neon-yellow placement — clear secondary goal.

## What's weak

- Subject and hero headline are identical and generic — "Need some inspo?" commits to nothing.
- No offer, no urgency, no time-bound hook anywhere in the email.
- Hero product imagery is mixed-gender / mixed-category — eye doesn't know where to land.
- Multiple "Shop New In" CTAs that all point to the same place — no editorial filtering.
- Comes 24 hours after a real promotional send, making this feel like padding cadence rather than a real campaign.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Give the email a point of view.** Pick ONE story — "Most Hyped" is right there, demote everything else and lead with it. Hero a single back-in-stock piece with the headline.
- 2. **Kill the duplicate "Need some inspo?" headline.** If you keep the subject, make the hero say something different — a category, a trend, a name.
- 3. **Add a soft offer or member hook.** Even "Free delivery on app orders" would give a reason to click beyond browsing.
- 4. **Tighten the grid.** Six product tiles before the first sub-CTA is too many — three is plenty.
- 5. Subject/preheader rewrites:
- **Subject Alt A:** `Back in stock — and going fast`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The most-hyped pieces of the week`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your wishlist just got restocked — shop before it sells out again.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New Balance, Levi's, Tommy & more — the brands you're saving right now.`
