# Homepage snapshot · www.asos.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- OK so this is very ASOS — editorial, clean, instantly recognisable. The split dual-model hero has that fashion-week energy I love and it doesn't look dated. But as a Premier member who's been shopping here for years, there is literally nothing on screen that acknowledges I exist — no loyalty hook, no discount, just a split-screen "hello, are you a man or a woman?" and that's kind of it.

## What's working

- **The split hero image** — woman with oversized sunglasses + man in rust polo is genuinely editorial. It reads high-fashion not high-street, which is exactly what makes me open ASOS over Boohoo.
- **"SHOP WOMENS" button** — high contrast, large tap target, no mystery about where I'm going. On mobile this matters.
- **"STYLE EACH MOMENT"** — short, punchy, doesn't over-explain. Feels Gen Z without trying too hard.
- **Clean mobile nav** — top bar with search, wishlist, and bag all accessible in one thumb sweep. No clutter.

## What's weak

- **Zero Premier recognition** — I pay for this subscription and there's no "Welcome back, Premier member" or even a free-delivery reassurance. That's a miss.
- **"New here? Get your first-timer discount"** — this is the only offer partially visible and it literally doesn't apply to me. Makes me feel like the front page isn't for me.
- **Generic gendered CTAs** — "SHOP WOMENS / SHOP MENS" tells me nothing. No seasonal, no festival edit, no denim. I could be anyone.
- **No new-arrivals signal** — there's nothing saying what just dropped. For someone who opens ASOS to see what's new, the hero gives me zero reason to explore right now.

## Recommendations

- **Surface Premier member status in the nav** — even a small "Premier" badge next to the account icon or a top-strip "Free next-day delivery is on us" would make existing subscribers feel seen instead of talked past.
- **Swap one generic CTA for a category anchor** — replace "SHOP WOMENS" with "New In: Women's" or "Festival Edits Are Live" — something that gives me a reason to tap *now* rather than eventually.
- **Add a micro-offer strip above the hero** — a single line "Free next-day delivery on all orders with ASOS Premier" reinforces my loyalty and doubles as conversion for non-members.
- **Rotate the hero image to reference the season** — rust tones are fine but if there's a specific campaign (summer, festival, Back to Uni) a visible tag or headline in the image would ground it in time.
