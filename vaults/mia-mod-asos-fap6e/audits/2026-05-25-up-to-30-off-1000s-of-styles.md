# Up to 30% off 1000s of styles
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-25**
## Executive summary

- Finally, ASOS shows up with an actual offer. After a three-email run of editorial-only sends (the leopard edit on May 22, embellished pieces on May 20, summer trends on May 18), this one leads with a real promotional headline — "Up to 30% off 1000s of styles" — plus a code (BANKHOL5) and a Daily Tuesday Bar. That alone is a meaningful break from the cadence.
- The execution is mid, though. The hero is a still-life of perfume, candles, a book, a green box, and a single dress on a model — it reads more like a flatlay collage than a clear category-led hero. The split "SHOP WOMEN'S / SHOP MEN'S" CTAs at the top are smart for a unisex retailer, but the "up to 30%" framing plus a code-gated component buries the actual deal. Visually clean, brand-consistent, but it doesn't feel like an event — it feels like a regular Tuesday with a small bonus.
- Net: a solid 5/10 promo email. The offer is there, the hierarchy is intact, but it's not loud enough to feel like a bank-holiday push.

## What's working

- Clear, big-type promo headline at the top — finally a number after weeks of editorial.
- Two-button gender split (Women's / Men's) up top handles unisex audience efficiently.
- Promo code BANKHOL5 is visible and tied to a holiday hook (UK bank holiday).
- "Daily Tuesday Bars" — a reason-to-return mechanic — gets a callout.
- Footer is tidy: ASOS World, ASOS Premier, Gift Vouchers icons surface loyalty/premium tiers.

## What's weak

- Hero collage is visually busy and category-confused — perfume, candle, book, dress, green box all at once.
- "Up to 30%" is soft; the ceiling-not-floor framing weakens the offer.
- Only one human model in the entire creative — feels more like a marketplace flatlay than a fashion retailer.
- Body text under the headline is small and hard to scan; the BANKHOL5 code doesn't get its own visual treatment.
- Single "SHOP NOW" mid-page is generic — doesn't tie to any specific product shown above it.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with a single hero**, not a collage. Pick one product (the dress on the model) at full bleed with "30% OFF — CODE BANKHOL5" overlaid. The collage dilutes the offer.
- 2. **Make BANKHOL5 a hero element** — boxed, large, copy-to-clipboard styled. Right now it's buried in a sentence.
- 3. **Replace "up to 30%"** with a floor: "20–30% off everything" or call out the specific 30% categories. "Up to" trains shoppers to expect 5% on what they want.
- 4. **Add a countdown** — "Ends Monday, 11:59pm." Bank holiday windows are short; use the urgency.
- 5. **Subject Alt A:** `Bank holiday: 30% off with code BANKHOL5`
- 6. **Subject Alt B:** `Your bank-holiday discount is live — up to 30% off`
- 7. **Preheader Alt A:** `Code BANKHOL5 — plus a new deal every Tuesday`
- 8. **Preheader Alt B:** `Up to 30% off womenswear, menswear & beauty. Ends Monday.`
