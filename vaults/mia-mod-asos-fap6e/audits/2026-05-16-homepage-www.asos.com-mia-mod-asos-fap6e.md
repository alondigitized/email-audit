# Homepage snapshot · www.asos.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Okay so this is giving very "big fashion brand energy" — clean, editorial, clearly ASOS. But honestly it's serving me vibes more than actual reasons to shop. "STYLE EACH MOMENT" sounds like a perfume ad, not the ASOS I know, and I'm seeing zero mention of my Premier perks which is a little rude considering I'm literally paying a subscription.

## What's working

- **Dual model hero** — the split male/female hero with the two CTA buttons ("SHOP WOMENS" / "SHOP MENS") is clean and decisive. No confusion, no wrong turn.
- **Model styling** — the woman's oversized sunglasses and editorial red outfit immediately reads as ASOS fashion-forward. That's the brand I signed up for.
- **Mobile-first layout** — the viewport looks perfectly optimised for mobile. Big buttons, thumb-reach CTAs, nothing feels squished.
- **"New here? Get your first-timer discount"** — spotted at the bottom crop, good acquisition hook for new visitors even if it means nothing to me.

## What's weak

- **"STYLE EACH MOMENT" is doing nothing** — it's so generic it could be on a Zara, H&M, or Shein homepage. No ASOS personality, no edge, no Gen Z register.
- **Zero Premier acknowledgement** — I pay for this subscription and there's no "Premier" badge, no "Free next-day delivery included" reminder, no account hook. That's a miss for retention.
- **No festival, no denim, no trend hooks** — literally my two main reasons to shop ASOS (festival season planning and denim hunting) are nowhere. Just "SHOP WOMENS" — that's it.
- **The app download banner** — if I'm already on ASOS via mobile, I probably have the app. Serving this to an existing mobile user feels like wasted real estate.
- **No urgency, no offer, no newness signal** — nothing is screaming "shop now" or even "look what just dropped." It's passive.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "STYLE EACH MOMENT" for something campaign-specific** — "Festival Season Starts Here" or "New Denim Drops Weekly" would actually pull someone like me in and signal ASOS knows its audience.
- **Add a Premier banner strip above or below the hero** — even a single line like "You're Premier — free next-day on everything" reminds me why I'm here and not on Zara.
- **Replace the app download tile with a trending-now or new-arrivals tile** — show me what just landed in women's, or tease a specific category (festival fits, Y2K denim, going-out tops). Give me a reason to scroll.
- **Surface one concrete offer with a deadline** — even a "Ends Sunday: 20% off sale" strip would convert browsers like me who are mid-scroll at midnight.
