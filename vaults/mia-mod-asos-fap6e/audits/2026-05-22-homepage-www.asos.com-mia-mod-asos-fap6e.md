# Homepage snapshot · www.asos.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- Okay so ASOS is serving full editorial energy right now — split hero, young couple, very campaign-shoot vibes. It reads like a fashion brand that knows it's a fashion brand, which I respect. But there's basically nothing here for me specifically as a Premier member who already has the app and wants to find festival fits or new denim — it's very much a "welcome, stranger" homepage, not a "hey, glad you're back" one.

## What's working

- **The hero woman** — oversized sunglasses, warm rust tones, editorial close-up. That's exactly the energy I come to ASOS for. Instant brand recognition.
- **Two-CTA split** — "SHOP WOMENS" and "SHOP MENS" stacked cleanly. Zero ambiguity about where to go. One tap and I'm in my section.
- **Visual hierarchy** — the ASOS wordmark overlaid on the hero with the tagline below it, then CTAs. Clean, no clutter. Mobile-native layout that actually works on a phone screen.
- **No modal hell** — I landed on the homepage and wasn't immediately assaulted by a pop-up asking for my email. That's a green flag honestly.

## What's weak

- **"STYLE EACH MOMENT"** — this tagline says nothing. It's not seasonal, not specific, not exciting. As a Premier member who visits multiple times a week, there's zero new information here.
- **"New here? Get your first-timer discount"** — I'm literally a Premier subscriber with next-day delivery and I'm seeing a first-timer acquisition module. This is not for me. Wasted real estate.
- **No Premier / loyalty callout above fold** — my membership is invisible. No "Welcome back, Mia" energy, no Premier badge, no reminder that I get free next-day. The thing that makes me choose ASOS over Zara isn't surfaced at all.
- **No denim, no festival, no trend context** — the hero is gorgeous but generic. There's no hook into what's new this week, what's trending, what festival season looks like on ASOS right now.
- **The app download strip** — I already have the app. I'm on it. This feels like they didn't segment their audience at all.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the generic tagline for a live campaign line**: "Festival fits are in. Shop the drop" or "Summer denim just landed" — something that tells me what's happening *right now* and makes me want to scroll.
- **Surface Premier perks in the header or hero**: A small "Premier: Free next-day delivery active" badge near the basket icon would remind me why I'm loyal and why I shouldn't bother going to a competitor.
- **Replace the first-timer module with a returning-user module**: "Back again? Your saved items are waiting" or a recently-viewed carousel would actually drive me toward conversion instead of advertising to me like I'm a stranger.
- **Add a trend or category rail just below the fold**: Even a horizontal scroll of "What's hot this week" tiles — Festival, Y2K Denim, Going Out, etc. — would give me a reason to keep scrolling instead of heading straight to search.
