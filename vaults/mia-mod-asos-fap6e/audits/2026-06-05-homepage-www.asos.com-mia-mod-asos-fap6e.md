# Homepage snapshot · www.asos.com · 2026-06-05
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a classic ASOS mobile homepage — clean, confident, super brand-forward. Right now it's pushing a lifestyle-coded "STYLE EACH MOMENT" hero with a dual-gender shot and two dead-simple CTAs. It reads young, fashion-forward, and aspirational, but it's not doing a lot of the heavy lifting I'd expect from a site that knows I'm already a Premier member logging back in.

## What's working

- **The hero shot** — the couple is young, stylish, and summer-coded. The woman's strappy monochrome dress is exactly the kind of piece I'd tap through on. It doesn't feel stock.
- **Dual CTA buttons** — "SHOP WOMENS" / "SHOP MENS" are clear, full-width, impossible to miss on mobile. No confusion about where to go.
- **Visual hierarchy** — ASOS logo → tagline → CTAs. It's a clean read top to bottom even on a small screen. No clutter.
- **Seasonal relevance** — the blue-sky backdrop and light summer styling feel current and not stale.

## What's weak

- **Zero Premier recognition** — I pay for Premier. There's no "Welcome back," no member badge, no loyalty balance, nothing. The homepage treats me exactly like a stranger.
- **"New here? Get your first-timer discount"** — this is literally the opposite of what I want to see. I'm not new. This actively reminds me that the discount isn't for me.
- **"STYLE EACH MOMENT" is vague** — as taglines go, this one says nothing. What moment? What style? I come to ASOS for denim, festival fits, and next-day delivery. None of that is communicated.
- **No new-arrivals signal** — I shop ASOS partly for the constant drop cadence. Nothing above the fold hints at what just landed.
- **App download banner competing with the hero** — the bottom strip about downloading the app is low-priority info that's eating real estate. I'm already on the website; this feels tone-deaf.

## Recommendations

- **Surface a Premier/member welcome module above the fold** — even a slim "Hey Mia, free next-day on everything" strip would make me feel seen and reduce bounce instantly.
- **Swap the app-download strip for a "New In" teaser** — show me three thumb-sized product tiles of what just dropped in womenswear. That's engagement bait I will actually tap.
- **Make the hero copy do some work** — "STYLE EACH MOMENT" could be "Drop-Ready. Free Next-Day." — something that hits the ASOS value prop, not just vibes.
- **Add a festival/occasion tag under SHOP WOMENS** — a tiny sub-label like "Festival fits, denim, & new drops" gives me a reason to tap over a generic competitor's homepage.
