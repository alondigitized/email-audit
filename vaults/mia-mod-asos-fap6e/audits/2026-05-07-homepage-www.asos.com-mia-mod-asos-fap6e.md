# Homepage snapshot · www.asos.com · 2026-05-07
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- OK so ASOS is leading with this very editorial dual-model hero — oversized sunnies, rust tones, "Style Each Moment" — which honestly is peak ASOS-core and I'm here for the vibe. But as a Premier member who literally shops here weekly, the homepage is doing nothing to acknowledge that I exist — no perks callout, no denim drop, no festival edit. It's welcoming new customers while completely ignoring the ones already locked into the subscription.

## What's working

- **Editorial hero image**: The split woman-with-sunnies / rust-polo-guy composition is genuinely stunning and instantly communicates ASOS's fashion-forward identity — I'd stop scrolling on Instagram for this.
- **"SHOP WOMENS" / "SHOP MENS" split CTA**: Clean, obvious, zero friction to get me to the right gender landing page in one tap.
- **Brand logo in hero**: The white ASOS wordmark overlaid on the hero is bold and confident — reinforces I'm in the right place.
- **Clean mobile nav**: Search, account, wishlist, bag all above the fold in the top bar — muscle memory for a regular.

## What's weak

- **Zero Premier acknowledgment**: I pay for Premier and there's not a single mention of my free next-day benefit, my member status, or anything that rewards loyalty. The first banner I see is "New here? Get your first-timer discount" — which is literally the opposite of talking to me.
- **"Style Each Moment" is generic**: There's no hook to denim, festival season, or any actual trend. It could be the homepage from two years ago.
- **No offer above fold**: As someone who is deal-aware (buys 4, returns 2), having zero price signal above the fold gives me nothing to act on immediately.
- **App download banner below fold**: Pushing the app download to someone who almost certainly already has the app is wasted real estate.

## Recommendations

- **Personalise the hero banner for logged-in Premier members**: Swap "New here? Get your first-timer discount" to "Hey, Premier member — free next-day on everything" with a trust badge. This is one line of code that transforms retention.
- **Add a "Festival Fits" or "Denim Edit" category chip row** directly below the hero CTAs: a horizontally scrollable chip strip ("Festival," "Denim," "New In," "Under £30") keeps me on the page instead of bouncing to search.
- **Inject a seasonal campaign message into the hero copy**: Replace "Style Each Moment" with something tied to the actual moment — if it's festival season, say "Festival Fits, Delivered Tomorrow."
- **Surface a concrete offer in the hero**: Even a single line — "Free next-day on orders over £X" or "25% off new arrivals this week" — gives me a reason to convert right now rather than saving to wishlist and ghosting.
