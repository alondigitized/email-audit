# Homepage snapshot · www.asos.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- Okay, first impression — very clean, very editorial. ASOS is clearly going for that "we're a fashion brand, not just a cheap fast-fashion site" vibe right now with the split hero and the muted, studio-lit aesthetic. The "Style Each Moment" line is giving versatile wardrobe, which I get, but the homepage feels like it's talking to someone brand-new to ASOS — not someone like me who's been on Premier for ages and buys 4 things a week.

## What's working

- **The split hero visual** — woman on the left with the oversized frames, man on the right in rust — is genuinely editorial and looks like something I'd save to a mood board. The colour palette (warm tones, studio whites) feels current.
- **"SHOP WOMENS" CTA** — large, white, impossible to miss. One tap and I'm in. That's the job done.
- **Clean, minimal nav** — search, wishlist, bag all in the top bar. Nothing cluttered. On mobile this is exactly what I want.
- **ASOS logo centre-hero** — restates the brand mid-scroll without feeling try-hard. Confident.

## What's weak

- **Zero Premier recognition** — I pay for ASOS Premier and there is absolutely nothing on this screen acknowledging that. No "Hi, welcome back," no Premier benefits banner, no exclusive-drop teaser. Feels like I'm a stranger.
- **"New here? Get your first-timer discount"** — I am not new. This panel is actively irrelevant to me as a returning Premier member and signals the homepage isn't personalised at all.
- **No category depth above fold** — Nothing about denim, nothing about festival, no trend-led call-to-action. "Shop Womens" is the whole pitch. It's one tap but it tells me nothing about what's actually in there.
- **"Download our app"** — I'm already on mobile, almost certainly already have the app. This panel isn't for me.
- **No urgency or offer** — I came ready to scroll and there's no hook. No "ends tonight," no member price, no new-arrivals callout. Nothing that says *now*.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the generic hero CTA for a trend-led one** — "Shop Festival Edits" or "New Denim Drops This Week" instead of just "Shop Womens." Same one-tap but it tells me something useful.
- **Add a Premier member banner above the hero** — even a thin strip: "ASOS Premier: Free next-day on every order" reminds me I'm getting value and keeps me engaged.
- **Replace "New here? Get your first-timer discount" with a returning-member panel** — "Back for more? Your wishlist is waiting" or a recently-viewed rail would be far more relevant.
- **Surface a seasonal or campaign hook in the hero copy** — even "New In: This Week's Drop" under "Style Each Moment" gives me a reason to tap *now* rather than *later.*
