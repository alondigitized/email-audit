# Homepage snapshot · www.asos.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- OK so ASOS is doing its classic "we are the brand" energy — big logo, split hero, two gender buttons, very clean. It reads as confident but honestly a bit of a snooze for me right now because there's zero hint of what's actually on right now — no drop, no collab, no festival vibes. It feels like a homepage that could have been live in 2023.

## What's working

- **The split hero layout** — woman on left, man on right, ASOS logo centre-stage in white. It's visually confident and the oversized-sunglasses woman has real editorial energy that speaks my language
- **"SHOP WOMENS" button** — white pill button on a dark hero, impossible to miss, exactly one tap to my section
- **Clean top nav** — search, wishlist heart, and bag all where I expect them, zero friction
- **No intrusive modal** — no cookie popup or email-capture overlay crowding the hero, which is a genuine relief on mobile

## What's weak

- **"STYLE EACH MOMENT" tagline** — this is the blandest possible copy. It says nothing about what's new, what's now, or why I should care today vs. any other day
- **"New here? Get your first-timer discount" tile** — I'm a Premier member, this actively alienates me. It's not a returning-user hook, it's a dismissal
- **"Download our app" tile** — I'm already on my phone browsing the site; pushing me to the app from here is friction, not value
- **No Premier mention anywhere** — my free next-day shipping, my status, nothing. As a Premier member I want to feel recognised above the fold
- **No trend or product signal** — no denim, no festival, no new-in rail, no "just dropped" hook. Nothing gives me a reason to tap through beyond reflex

## Recommendations

- **Swap "STYLE EACH MOMENT" for a live campaign line** — something like "Festival Ready — Shop the Drop" or "New In: Denim Edition" would give me a reason to tap immediately rather than shrugging
- **Add a Premier member welcome strip** — even a thin banner above the hero: "Free next-day delivery, as always" or "Your Premier perks are active" costs nothing and makes me feel seen
- **Replace the "New here?" tile with a returning-member hook** — "Back for more? See what's dropped this week" or a "Continue shopping" recently-viewed carousel would convert me way harder
- **Surface one specific product or trend above the fold** — even a single hero featuring a specific denim jacket or festival-ready dress with a product name gives me something to react to
