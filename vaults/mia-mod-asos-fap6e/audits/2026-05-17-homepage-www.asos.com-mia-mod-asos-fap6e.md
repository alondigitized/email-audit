# Homepage snapshot · www.asos.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Okay so ASOS is serving full editorial energy right now — split hero, oversized sunnies, muted earthy tones on the guy. It reads very "cool London couple doing their thing" which is honestly the vibe. But as someone who's been Premier for two years and genuinely lives on this app, there's nothing here that speaks to *me* specifically — no Premier perks, no new drops teaser, no sale hook. It's pretty but it's not pulling me in any harder than the app icon already did.

## What's working

- **Split-panel hero with editorial casting** — the woman in the black square sunnies is exactly the kind of image that stops the scroll. Very Pinterest-coded, very me.
- **Dual-gender CTAs stacked cleanly** — "SHOP WOMENS" / "SHOP MENS" is the clearest homepage bifurcation I've seen; zero ambiguity, both visible without touching the menu.
- **Clean mobile layout** — no jarring layout shift, no text crunched into a corner. The logo centered over the split hero with the tagline underneath is genuinely elegant on a phone screen.
- **App download prompt below the fold** — "exclusive discounts and the latest drops" is a legit hook for anyone not already on the app.

## What's weak

- **"STYLE EACH MOMENT" tells me nothing** — it's a tagline that could belong to literally any fashion brand. There's no seasonal hook, no trend reference, nothing that makes me think "ooh what *is* the moment right now?"
- **Zero Premier acknowledgment** — I'm a paying subscriber and this homepage greets me exactly the same as a first-timer. The "New here? Get your first-timer discount" card below the fold actually makes it worse — it's optimised for acquisition, not retention.
- **No offer above the fold** — not a flash sale, not a "up to 40% off" strip, nothing. For a site that usually has *something* on, this feels stripped back to the point of being unhelpful.
- **No product content visible** — I can't see a single item or category rail. I tap in from the app to shop, and the first screen is basically a brand ad, not a shop.
- **No trust signals** — no "free returns," no "next-day with Premier," no review stars on any product. These are ASOS's actual strengths and none of them are here.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "What's New This Week" rail** directly below the hero CTAs — even three product tiles with new-arrival tags would give me a reason to stay instead of going straight to the search bar.
- **Surface the Premier benefit somewhere above the fold** — a one-line strip ("Free next-day delivery for Premier members") is enough. Remind me I'm already getting something.
- **Swap "STYLE EACH MOMENT" for a seasonal/campaign line** — it's mid-May, summer is loading. "Festival season starts here" or "Summer fits dropping daily" gives the hero actual momentum.
- **Replace the "New here?" card with a returning-member card** — show my Premier renewal date, my wishlist count, or a "pick up where you left off" nudge. Two-panel real estate, use both sides for different audiences.
