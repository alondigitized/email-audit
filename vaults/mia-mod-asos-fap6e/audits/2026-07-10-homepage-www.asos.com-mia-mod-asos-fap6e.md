# Homepage snapshot · www.asos.com · 2026-07-10
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-10**
## Executive summary

- ASOS is showing me the most generic version of itself right now — two good-looking twenty-somethings, a vague tagline, two big buttons, and a couple of below-fold teaser tiles. It reads clean and on-brand but it's playing it safe, nothing about this says "hey Premier member, welcome back." Feels like a new-visitor page, not a returning-shopper page.

## What's working

- **"SHOP WOMENS" button** — full-width, high-contrast white button with bold black text, impossible to miss on mobile, one tap to my category
- **Dual-gender hero** — the couple shot earns its space: she's in a black-and-white wrap halter with gold accessories, which is genuinely my aesthetic
- **Clean mobile nav** — hamburger + search + wishlist + bag, nothing cluttered, exactly how I expect ASOS to behave on my phone
- **Seasonal palette** — that pale blue backdrop and the warm linen-shirt styling read as a proper summer campaign, not recycled stock

## What's weak

- **"STYLE EACH MOMENT" tagline** — this is maximum vague. It says nothing about newness, exclusivity, or why I should care today
- **No Premier hook** — I'm a paying Premier member and the homepage treats me like a stranger; there's no badge, no "free next-day" reminder, no loyalty perk visible
- **"New here? Get your first-timer discount"** — partially visible below the fold; acquisition-first messaging that actually signals I'm not the priority here
- **No urgency or editorial pull** — there's nothing to make me feel like I'm missing a drop, a trend moment, or a limited run; it's two buttons and done
- **App download tile** — the "Download our app for exclusive discounts and the latest drops" tile is visible at the bottom and directly contradicts the experience if I'm already using the mobile site rather than the app; slightly confusing

## Recommendations

- **Surface the Premier membership benefit above the fold** — add a thin banner directly below the nav: "Premier member: free next-day delivery on everything." One line, one second, huge retention signal.
- **Replace "STYLE EACH MOMENT" with a trend hook** — something like "New in: summer's biggest denim drops" or "Festival edits just landed" to give me a reason to tap *now*, not eventually.
- **Add a wishlist or recently-viewed nudge** — I'm clearly signed in (wishlist heart icon is in the nav); a small "Pick up where you left off" module under the hero would convert me faster than another generic tagline.
- **Swap the app-download tile for a category teaser** — "Denim," "Occasionwear," "Festival" tiles would actually get me scrolling; the app CTA is wasted space on a mobile browser session.
