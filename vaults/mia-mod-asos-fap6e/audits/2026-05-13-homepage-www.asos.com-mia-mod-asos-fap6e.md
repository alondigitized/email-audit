# Homepage snapshot · www.asos.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay so ASOS mobile is giving me very clean, very editorial — split hero with a woman dripping in oversized sunnies on the left and a guy in a rust polo on the right, tagline says "STYLE EACH MOMENT." It reads like a fashion magazine cover rather than a shop front, which is very ASOS. But honestly it's not pushing anything specific right now — no sale, no "new in denim," no festival drop — it's just vibes and two big white buttons.

## What's working

- **The split editorial hero** — two young, aspirational models in trend-relevant styling. The woman with the huge frames and the layered reds feels very right now, very ASOS. I clocked her immediately.
- **"SHOP WOMENS" button placement** — white on dark image, above fold, unmissable. One tap and I'm in.
- **Clean mobile nav bar** — hamburger, search, account, heart, bag. Five icons, nothing cluttered. I know where everything is.
- **No modal or cookie wall interrupting the fold** — I can actually see the homepage on arrival, which is not a given with some sites.

## What's weak

- **"STYLE EACH MOMENT" says nothing** — it's aspirational filler. It doesn't tell me there's a denim drop, a festival edit, a Premier perk, or a sale. It's the copy equivalent of a shrug.
- **Zero Premier/loyalty signal** — I pay for ASOS Premier for free next-day delivery. There is no acknowledgement that I exist as a member anywhere on screen. That's a miss.
- **"New here? Get your first-timer discount"** is the only promo partially visible below fold — and it's for new users. As a returning Premier customer, that's actively irrelevant to me and slightly annoying to see first.
- **No category entry points beyond gendered shop-all** — no "New In," no "Denim," no "Festival," no "Trending Now." I have to tap SHOP WOMENS and navigate from there, which adds friction.
- **No current season or campaign context** — the imagery is editorial but dateless. If this was a festival or SS25 drop I'd be way more engaged.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the evergreen tagline with an active campaign hook** — "New in: Festival Dressing" or "Your SS25 denim drop is here" gives me a reason to tap rather than just drift in.
- **Add a Premier member strip under the nav** — one line: "You're Premier. Free next-day on everything." Costs nothing to render and reminds me why I chose ASOS over ZARA today.
- **Add 3–4 category shortcut tiles below the hero CTAs** — "Denim," "Going Out," "Festival," "New In." Let me reach my category in one tap from the homepage, not two.
- **Swap the "New here" promo tile for a "New arrivals this week" editorial rail** — I've already signed up, I don't need acquisition messaging. Show me what just dropped.
