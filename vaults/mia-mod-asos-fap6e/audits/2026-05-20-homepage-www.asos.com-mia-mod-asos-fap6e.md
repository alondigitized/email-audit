# Homepage snapshot · www.asos.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- OK so ASOS is doing a whole editorial brand moment here — "STYLE EACH MOMENT" with two young models, red/rust tones, very fashion-forward. It reads clean and current, not trying too hard. But as an ASOS Premier member who's been shopping here for years, there's literally nothing on this screen telling me there's anything in it for me specifically — no deal, no Premier perk, no "new in" hook. It's a vibe, not a conversion.

## What's working

- **The hero imagery**: split editorial shot — red/rust palette, oversized sunglasses on a young woman — feels exactly on-brand for the ASOS customer I am. Not trying too hard.
- **"SHOP WOMENS" CTA**: white button on a dark hero makes it impossible to miss. One tap and I'm in. No friction.
- **"STYLE EACH MOMENT" copy**: short, punchy, lifestyle-coded. Doesn't talk down to me or try to be overly clever. Very ASOS.
- **App download prompt below fold**: the "exclusive discounts and the latest drops" hook is genuinely compelling for a mobile shopper like me who might not have the app.
- **Clean mobile layout**: hamburger, search, profile, wishlist, bag — everything I need is in the nav bar without clutter.

## What's weak

- **Zero Premier/loyalty acknowledgement**: I pay for Premier. Show me *something* — a free-delivery reminder, a Premier-only drop, anything. Right now this homepage treats me the same as a brand-new visitor.
- **"New here? Get your first-timer discount"**: I can see this teasing below the fold and it actively excludes me. That's the first personalised message I see and it's not for me.
- **No product or category specificity**: "SHOP WOMENS" covers literally everything. Where's denim? Where's festival? No editorial rail, no "New In This Week," nothing to give me a reason to tap beyond generic category browsing.
- **No offer or urgency whatsoever**: it's May 2026 — there should be a sale, a season drop, *something* price-related. The hero is gorgeous but it doesn't give me a reason to act *now*.
- **Sizing confidence signal absent**: as someone who buys 4 and returns 2, I need to feel like size tools are close. Nothing here signals that.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Premier member strip at the top**: one thin banner — "Premier member: free next-day on every order" — it takes up 20px and immediately tells me the site knows who I am.
- **Swap "New here?" with a returning-shopper hook**: something like "Back for more? New in this week →" or surface my recently-viewed items. The new-customer discount module in the first scroll is a dead zone for loyal shoppers.
- **Inject a category teaser into the hero**: a small overlay or second slide showing "New In: Denim" or "Festival Fits Just Dropped" — keep the editorial feel but give the eye something to chase beyond the CTAs.
- **Add a promo strip between hero and below-fold modules**: even "Up to 30% off selected styles" in a thin black banner would give me a reason to tap rather than just browse.
