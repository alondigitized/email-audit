# Homepage snapshot · www.asos.com · 2026-05-04
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-04**
## Executive summary

- Okay so this is very classic ASOS — split hero, editorial models, bold CTAs, nothing fussy. It feels like a brand that knows what it is: a global fashion destination that trusts its name to do the heavy lifting. Right now it's pushing "shop everything" rather than a specific drop or campaign, which honestly feels a bit generic for where I am in my Premier membership journey.
- **7/10**
- + Hero imagery speaks to my persona: young woman, editorial red jacket + beret = fashion-forward Gen Z energy
- + One unmistakable CTA above the fold: "SHOP WOMENS" — clear, tappable
- + Visual hierarchy is clean — split hero, two buttons, eye goes straight to the models then the CTAs
- + No render bugs: layout is clean, images load, no placeholder text
- + Demographic signals match: model reads early-to-mid 20s, styling is on-trend
- + Offer feels honest: nothing manipulative or buried visible above the fold
- − No returning-shopper / Premier hook visible — I'm a member and there's zero acknowledgement of that
- − No concrete offer above fold (no % off, no free delivery threshold)
- − No seasonal campaign copy — could be any week of the year
- − No loyalty/membership benefit visible without scrolling
- Score: 1 base + 6 signals = **7/10**

## What's working

- **Split-screen hero** — woman in red (colour-pop editorial) next to a cool, muted menswear shot is visually arresting; the contrast works on mobile especially
- **"SHOP WOMENS" and "SHOP MENS" CTAs** — two fat white buttons, impossible to miss, no ambiguity about what to tap
- **Model casting** — the woman looks my age, the styling (beret, red coat, gold earrings) fits the kind of statement-look energy I come here for
- **App download strip** — "exclusive discounts and the latest drops" at the bottom is the right language to pull a high-tech shopper like me toward the app

## What's weak

- **"New here? Get your first-timer discount"** — I am absolutely not new here, I have Premier, and this is the first thing I read below the hero. It's screaming at the wrong person
- **Zero Premier acknowledgment** — no "Hi, Premier member" banner, no free next-day delivery callout, nothing that makes me feel like I belong to a tier above a first-timer
- **No festival, denim, or trending category call-out** — the hero is purely brand-level; I came here to shop a specific vibe and there's nothing pulling me toward it
- **Hero copy is literally just the ASOS logo** — no campaign headline, no seasonal hook, no reason to care beyond the images

## Recommendations

- **Add a Premier member strip above the hero** — even just "Free next-day delivery, always. You're Premier." would make me feel seen and reduce bounce
- **Swap the generic "Shop Womens" CTA for something campaign-specific** — "Shop the Festival Edit" or "New Denim Drops" would give me a reason to tap vs. just browsing
- **Surface one concrete offer in the hero** — even "New in: 500+ styles added today" beats a blank brand logo
- **Replace "New here?" panel with a returning-shopper hook** — "Back for more? Your Saved Items are waiting" or a recently-viewed nudge would convert me faster than a first-timer offer
