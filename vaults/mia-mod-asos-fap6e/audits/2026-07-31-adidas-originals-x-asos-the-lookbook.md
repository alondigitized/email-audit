# adidas Originals x ASOS: the lookbook
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-31**
## Executive summary

- This is ASOS running the second beat of the same adidas Originals x ASOS story it opened on July 30 ("Collection 04 is here," 8/10) — except the offer mechanic has flipped from "new collection drop" to pure styling content. "The lookbook" is four influencer outfit posts (@lozvassallo, @lissyroddy twice, @rebeccajackson) each paired with matching product cutouts, styled as a UGC lookbook rather than a straight product push. It's a smart sequencing move: day one sells the collection, day two sells the styling fantasy, which is a more sophisticated cadence than the single-brand editorial sends (Topshop, "New arrivals," "Fringe is in") that have been landing 4-5/10.
- The execution is clean — four distinct real-person shots, each grounded in a real Instagram handle, each paired with tight product cutouts so the reader can shop exactly what's on the body. No render bugs, no lorem ipsum, brand carousel and app-download block intact at the footer. The weakness is the one this brand keeps running into: there's no discount, no urgency, and the single CTA ("SHOP THE COLLECTION") sits at the very top, disconnected from four sections of content below it — so a reader who scrolls past the hero has no CTA to act on until they've scrolled the whole email.
- This is a genuinely good creative idea landing at a mediocre commercial email because ASOS didn't give it a reason to act now. It's the July 30 send's little sibling — same collab, same audience, but content marketing instead of a sales push.

## What's working

- Real influencer handles (@lozvassallo, @lissyroddy, @rebeccajackson) tagged directly under each look, lending authenticity a stock product shot can't.
- Each of the four outfit sections pairs a full-body lifestyle photo with matching product cutouts (jacket, shorts/joggers), so what's worn is immediately shoppable.
- @lissyroddy is featured twice (red set, then striped satin look), reinforcing a specific creator as a face of the collection rather than diluting across four one-off names.
- Consistent grid layout across all four sections makes the email easy to scan even though it's long.
- Clean footer with brand carousel (Mango, Bershka, Topshop, adidas) and app download — no clutter.

## What's weak

- Zero discount, no urgency, no loyalty pricing — matches the pattern from "New arrivals" (4/10) and "Fringe is in" (4/10), the brand's weakest-performing sends.
- Single "SHOP THE COLLECTION" CTA lives only at the top of the email; none of the four outfit sections below have their own "Shop this look" button, so engaged scrollers have no immediate action.
- Subject "the lookbook" is vague and low-specificity compared to the July 30 "Collection 04 is here" — it doesn't signal what's inside (styling inspo vs. new product).
- Two back-to-back sends on the same collab in 48 hours (July 30 → July 31) risks fatigue if a subscriber already engaged or ignored the first one.
- No product names, prices, or sizing visible anywhere — pure lifestyle imagery with no purchase-decision detail.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a "Shop this look" text link or small CTA under each of the four outfit captions — the biggest single lift, since right now the only CTA is orphaned above four scrolls of unlinked content.
- 2. Tie a small, credible incentive to this send specifically (e.g., early access, free shipping on the collection) to differentiate it from a pure content play and give it a reason-to-click beyond aesthetics.
- 3. Sharpen the subject to signal what's inside — styling inspiration, not just "lookbook," which reads as filler after "Collection 04 is here."
- 4. Add product names/prices under at least one item per section — right now nothing indicates cost or fit, which is a real friction point for click-through.
- 5. Space this further from the July 30 send in future sequencing, or explicitly frame it as "part 2" so repeat recipients understand why they're seeing the same collab again.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Real fits from @lozvassallo, @lissyroddy and more`
