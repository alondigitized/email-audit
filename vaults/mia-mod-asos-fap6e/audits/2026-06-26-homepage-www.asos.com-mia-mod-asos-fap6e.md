# Homepage snapshot · www.asos.com · 2026-06-26
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-26**
## Executive summary

- ASOS is giving me clean, editorial summer energy right now — two young models, a breezy light-blue backdrop, a striking black-and-white striped halter dress on the female model that honestly has major festival headliner vibes. It looks like a brand that knows its audience aesthetically but is playing it very safe with the actual homepage content: barely any offer, zero Premier love, and the two below-the-fold teasers I can just about see are aimed at *new* users, not me.

## What's working

- **The female model's outfit** — that black and white striped halter dress is *exactly* the kind of festival-ready piece I come to ASOS for. It stops the scroll.
- **"SHOP WOMENS" CTA** — white on the hero, bold, no ambiguity. One tap and I'm in.
- **Clean mobile layout** — no crowded nav, no banner stack, just the hero and two clear buttons. On a phone screen that's a win.
- **Light blue/editorial backdrop** — feels current, not dusty. Matches summer 2026 without screaming "SUMMER SALE" in a way that already feels worn out.
- **The rainbow-tinged wishlist heart icon in the nav** — feels like a low-key Pride Month nod, which lands naturally for my demographic without being heavy-handed.

## What's weak

- **Zero Premier/loyalty acknowledgement** — I pay for Premier. Free next-day is the whole value prop. Not seeing it anywhere above the fold makes this feel like I'm being treated like a brand-new visitor.
- **"New here? Get your first-timer discount"** — I can see this teaser at the very bottom of the viewport and it's for people who've never shopped here. I've spent hundreds here. This is mildly annoying.
- **No active offer or sale signal** — ASOS almost always has *something* on. If there's a sale or new drop happening, I have zero indication of it from this screen.
- **"STYLE EACH MOMENT" is vague** — it's fine copy but it doesn't tell me anything about what's new, what's on sale, or why I should tap now vs. tomorrow.
- **The male model takes up nearly half the frame** — as a woman shopping womenswear, I'm slightly distracted. A womenswear-first layout would pull me in faster.

## Recommendations

- **Add a slim Premier banner strip above the hero** — even just "Free next-day delivery on this order · Premier member" in white on black. One line. Takes up no real estate and immediately reminds me why I'm here.
- **Swap the generic "STYLE EACH MOMENT" tagline for a seasonal hook** — something like "Festival season is here · New arrivals daily" anchors me in time and gives me a reason to tap.
- **Surface a live offer or new-drop signal in the hero** — even "New in: 200+ styles dropped today" as a small pill badge on the hero image would create urgency without a full sale banner.
- **Reframe the bottom "New here" panel to a returning-shopper reward** — show me something like "Back again? Your wishlist has new price drops" instead of a new-customer discount I can't use.
