# Homepage snapshot · www.asos.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- OK so this is literally my store — I'm a Premier member, I live here. But opening this homepage feels like ASOS forgot I exist. It's defaulting to acquisition mode (first-timer discounts, download the app) instead of speaking to someone who's already mid-scroll through the new-in rail before breakfast. The editorial split-hero is clean and very ASOS — but it's more "fashion brand trying to look expensive" than "here's what you actually want right now."
- **5/10**

## What's working

- **Split editorial hero** — the woman in the red structured jacket and beret is genuinely striking, very that London-fashion-week-but-make-it-ASOS energy. I'd pause on that.
- **Black nav bar with icon-only shortcuts** — search, wishlist, bag all instantly tappable. Mobile-native layout, no fat-finger issues.
- **"SHOP WOMENS" / "SHOP MENS" CTA pairing** — I hate gender-locked browsing but the buttons are large, high contrast, and zero friction. Quick to move past.
- **Clean, fast-feeling layout** — nothing is fighting for attention above the fold. Fast load feel even on mobile.

## What's weak

- **Zero headline copy** — the only text in the hero is the ASOS logo. No trend callout, no "New in this week," no seasonal hook. It's like opening a magazine and the cover just says the masthead.
- **"New here? Get your first-timer discount"** — completely irrelevant to me. I've spent hundreds here. This card is taking up prime real-estate to talk to someone who isn't me.
- **"Download our app"** — I already use the app. I'm probably ON the app. This is noise.
- **No Premier acknowledgement** — I pay for Premier. There's zero nod to that relationship. No "Welcome back," no member-exclusive drop, nothing. Feels like ASOS doesn't know I'm a loyal customer.
- **No trend or category signaling** — festival season, denim edits, going-out looks — nothing. If I didn't already know ASOS, I'd have no idea what to even browse for.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the binary hero with a trend-led editorial card** — e.g. "Festival fits are in" with a lifestyle shot of layered denim and co-ords, not a studio fashion editorial. Shop the look CTA underneath.
- **Swap "New here" card for a Premier loyalty acknowledgement** — something like "You've got free next-day. New drops just landed." makes me feel seen instead of mistaken for a stranger.
- **Add at least one trend strip above the fold** — even a scrollable category pill row ("Festival | Going Out | Denim | Vacay") gives me somewhere to go instantly.
- **Put the "Download the app" card behind a logged-out state** — it's irrelevant to existing app users and clutters the experience for anyone already shopping on mobile.
