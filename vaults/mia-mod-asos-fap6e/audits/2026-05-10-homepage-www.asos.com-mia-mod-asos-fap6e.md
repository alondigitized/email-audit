# Homepage snapshot · www.asos.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- OK so ASOS is going full editorial, clean split between womenswear and menswear with that "STYLE EACH MOMENT" energy — feels considered, not cluttered. But honestly? As someone who already has Premier and has been on this app a hundred times, there is zero acknowledgement that I exist. No Premier badge, no "welcome back," nothing. The brand looks current and the sunglasses aesthetic is very much my vibe, but the homepage is doing almost nothing to convert me specifically.

## What's working

- **The hero model:** That close-up oversized-sunglasses shot is genuinely aspirational — it's the exact aesthetic I save to Pinterest for festival inspo. Stops the scroll.
- **Dual-CTA layout:** "SHOP WOMENS" and "SHOP MENS" stacked clean below the logo — I can get to my section in literally one tap without hunting through a nav.
- **Tagline brevity:** "STYLE EACH MOMENT" is short, punchy, unpretentious. No cringe "find your fit" or "be your best self" nonsense.
- **Zero clutter:** For mobile, this is refreshingly focused. No pop-up cookie banner obscuring the hero (at least not visible here), no 12 competing promo strips.

## What's weak

- **Zero Premier recognition:** I pay for Premier. The homepage has no idea who I am — no "Hey, free delivery ready" badge, no member-pricing callout, no loyalty tier signal. That's a real miss.
- **No concrete offer for me:** The only promo visible is "New here? Get your first-timer discount" — which actively excludes returning customers. There's nothing here that says "here's why you should buy *today*."
- **No denim or festival category entry point:** My two biggest shopping lanes at ASOS and neither is visible. "SHOP WOMENS" is too broad — it doesn't pull me toward what I actually came for.
- **App download tile feels redundant:** I almost certainly already have the app if I'm a Premier member. That real estate could be a "back in stock" rail or a festival-edit shortcut.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Premier member strip:** One thin banner just below the nav — "Premier member: free next-day on everything" — takes 10 seconds to add and immediately validates the loyalty investment for returning shoppers.
- **Swap the first-timer tile for a category shortcut:** Replace "New here? Get your first-timer discount" with a Festival Edit or New Denim Drops tile for returning users — segment by login state if possible.
- **Make the hero CTA category-specific:** Test "SHOP NEW IN WOMEN'S" or even "SHOP FESTIVAL EDIT" — "SHOP WOMENS" is fine but a more specific label would increase tap-through from fashion-intent visitors.
- **Surface one time-sensitive hook:** Even "Delivered tomorrow if you order before midnight" or a "New in: just dropped" label would create enough urgency to push past browse-mode into cart-mode.
