# Homepage snapshot · www.asos.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so ASOS is going full "we're a vibe not a store" energy right now — the split hero with the woman in oversized sunnies and the guy in the rust polo screams editorial shoot more than it screams "come shop." The tagline is as generic as it gets for a brand that literally knows everything about me, and as a Premier member who buys here constantly, I'm getting literally zero recognition on arrival. It's clean, it loads fast, but it's treating me like a stranger.

## What's working

- **Split-gender hero with editorial styling** — the woman on the left with the oversized square frames and bold red is immediately eye-catching and feels like the ASOS I know and love
- **"SHOP WOMENS" / "SHOP MENS" dual CTAs** — big, white, impossible to miss; I know exactly where I'm going in one tap
- **Clean mobile layout** — no clutter, no popups eating my screen, the nav icons (search, account, heart, bag) are all where I expect them in the top bar
- **Rust/earth tone palette** — actually looks current and on-season for right now

## What's weak

- **"STYLE EACH MOMENT" is a nothing tagline** — as someone who's been on ASOS for years, this copy tells me nothing about what's new, what's on sale, or why I should care today
- **Zero Premier acknowledgment** — I pay for Premier and I'm greeted like a first-timer; the "New here? Get your first-timer discount" module below the fold is a slap in the face for a loyalty member
- **No offer, no urgency** — nothing above the fold tells me there's a reason to shop now vs. tomorrow
- **No denim or festival content visible** — as someone who's literally here to hunt denim and plan festival looks, there's no hook into my actual shopping mission
- **App download module below fold** — if I'm already on the site I probably already have the app, this feels misallocated real estate

## Recommendations

- **Swap the generic "STYLE EACH MOMENT" headline for something campaign-specific** — even just "Festival drops are here" or "New denim just landed" would give me a reason to tap
- **Add a Premier member banner or personalised greeting** — even a slim strip saying "Hey Premier member — free next-day on everything you see" would immediately differentiate me from a first-time visitor
- **Replace the "New here?" module with a new-arrivals category strip** — denim, going-out tops, festival edits — something that shows me what dropped this week
- **Surface a concrete offer in a sticky promo strip above the hero** — even "20% off selected styles" with a deadline turns this from a browse into a mission
