# Homepage snapshot · www.asos.com · 2026-05-29
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-29**
## Executive summary

- Okay so I open the app and I'm hit with a really clean, editorial-feeling hero — young couple, bold print dress on her, linen shirt on him, very "ASOS summer campaign" energy. It looks current and aspirational. But as a Premier member who's on here all the time, there's zero "welcome back" energy — no deals, no drops, nothing that makes me feel like the site knows me.

## What's working

- **The hero photo** is genuinely strong — her dress (bold B&W print asymmetric halter) is exactly the kind of statement piece I'd tap on. The styling looks current, not dated.
- **"SHOP WOMENS" CTA** is high-contrast, above-fold, no ambiguity. One tap and I'm in.
- **Visual cleanliness** — no clutter, no competing banners, no pop-up modal forcing me to enter my email before I even see the page. Rare and appreciated.
- **Mobile layout** is tight: nav icons are thumb-sized, the hero fills the viewport properly, nothing feels squished or misaligned.

## What's weak

- **Zero Premier acknowledgment**: I'm paying for Premier and I see the exact same homepage as someone who just Googled ASOS for the first time. No "Hey Mia, new drops just landed" or even a loyalty badge. Missed opportunity.
- **"STYLE EACH MOMENT" is a nothing tagline**: Sounds like a placeholder. Doesn't tell me what's live, what's new, or why I should be here today vs. yesterday.
- **"New here? Get your first-timer discount"** — partially visible at the bottom and irrelevant to me as a repeat customer. That real estate should serve returning users.
- **No denim or festival-specific signal**: I'm a denim hunter and festival-outfit planner — the hero has zero denim and no category teaser aimed at me. The hero is broadly fashionable but doesn't hook my specific use case.
- **No price or offer signal of any kind**: Not even "from £12" or a sale banner. For someone who compares across ASOS, Zara, and Topshop, there's no purchase trigger here.

## Recommendations

- **Surface a Premier member module above the fold**: even a small "Premier | Next-day delivery included" chip on the header would make me feel seen. Returning users convert at much higher rates — don't serve them a first-timer homepage.
- **Swap the generic tagline for a campaign-specific hook**: "Festival Dressing Has Landed" or "New This Week: 600+ Drops" gives me a reason to be here *today*. "STYLE EACH MOMENT" could have been written in 2014.
- **Add a category strip just below the hero**: four thumb-sized tiles — Dresses, Denim, Festival, Shoes — so I can route to my actual intent in one tap without hunting through a hamburger menu.
- **Show the free-shipping / free-returns Premier perk inline**: a sticky or in-hero note like "Free next-day. Free returns. Always." would remind me why I pay for Premier and push me toward adding more to cart.
