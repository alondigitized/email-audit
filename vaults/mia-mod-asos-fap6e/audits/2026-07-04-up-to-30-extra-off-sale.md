# Up to 30% extra off Sale
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-04**
## Executive summary

- This is ASOS's third distinct sale escalation in under two weeks — after 60% off (June 22), extra 10% (June 24), extra 25% (June 28), 70% off (June 29), and a full-price editorial reset on July 1, they're now layering on a "30% extra off" stack on July 4. The offer is real and the mechanism is clear: a discount code ("MORE") with an expiry ("Ends Monday 9am"). But the creative is in autopilot — same white-background editorial shots, same two-CTA split (Shop Women's / Shop Men's), same red-banner hero format that has appeared in every sale email this cycle.
- The brand history problem is acute here. A subscriber who has seen five escalating sale emails in two weeks has learned to wait — ASOS will just sweeten the deal again. This email does nothing to break that pattern or create urgency beyond the Monday deadline. The "YOUR STYLE, FOR LESS" mid-body banner is soft and brand-generic; the top brands rail (Bershka, New Look, Levi's, New Balance) is a visual throwaway at this scroll depth.
- **Verdict:** Competent execution of a tired formula. The code + deadline is the only thing doing real work here. For a July 4 send it's indistinguishable from any of the four emails that preceded it.

## What's working

- **Code + deadline is explicit.** "Use code MORE / Ends Monday 9am" is visible in the hero — low friction for conversion.
- **Dual CTA split is clean.** "Shop Women's" and "Shop Men's" buttons are unambiguous and immediately below the hero copy.
- **No render bugs.** All six lifestyle images load, text is legible, layout is intact.
- **Stack offer is credible.** "Up to 70% off styles" in the sub-copy anchors the total possible discount, making "30% extra" feel additive, not standalone.

## What's weak

- **Offer fatigue is at maximum.** This is the sixth sale-escalation email in 13 days; there's no creative pivot to reset attention the way the June 25 "new brands" email tried.
- **"YOUR STYLE, FOR LESS" banner is dead weight.** It's a generic brand line with no specific offer or product — takes up real estate mid-scroll and contributes nothing.
- **Models and editorial feel recycled.** The white-linen, neutral-palette lookbook aesthetic is identical to prior sends; nothing in the imagery signals this is a new or distinct moment.
- **Code "MORE" is weak.** A code that literally means "more discount" on the fifth escalation email is barely memorable and undersells the mechanic.
- **No urgency cue beyond text.** The Monday 9am deadline is stated but not visually emphasized — same weight as surrounding body copy.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Break the visual loop.** Use a single high-impact product shot with a price callout (e.g., "Was £89, now £32") rather than another lifestyle grid. One concrete product anchors the discount better than six editorial shots.
- 2. **Make the deadline the hero, not the percentage.** "Expires Monday 9am" in a countdown-style treatment (even static: bold red clock icon + time) would outperform yet another "UP TO X% OFF" banner.
- 3. **Retire the generic mid-body banner.** Replace "YOUR STYLE, FOR LESS" with a category-specific hook ("Summer dresses, now stacked") or cut it entirely.
- 4. **Sharpen the code.** "EXTRA30" beats "MORE" — it's self-describing and easier to recall.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your cart just got cheaper. 30% extra, code inside`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack it on top of up to 70% off. Expires Monday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Use MORE at checkout — extra 30% off Sale ends Monday 9am`
