# Meet the new ASOS-exclusive Lioness drop
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-03**
## Executive summary

- This is ASOS running its "genuine collab drop" playbook again — the same beat that landed 8/10 on July 30's adidas Originals x ASOS "Collection 04" send — except this time the exclusive is with Lioness, an Aussie fashion brand, not a recognizable sportswear giant. The hero is strong: a single full-bleed street-style shot of a model in camo cargo pants and heeled mules, with a clean "ASOS EXCLUSIVE: LIONESS" wordmark stamped over the image. It reads as a real launch, not filler editorial.
- Where it loses points versus the adidas send is stakes and specificity. There's no discount, no urgency, and — critically — no explanation of who Lioness is or why a subscriber should care beyond the visual. The email also sprawls immediately into cross-sell (I.AM.GIA, Tiger Mist, Kulani Kinis, Princess Polly, Cotton:On, then a "top brands" logo row) which dilutes the Lioness story before it's even finished being told.
- Net: a well-produced, on-brand collection-drop email that will perform fine for engaged fashion browsers but won't convert casual openers — there's no price lever and no reason to act today.

## What's working

- The hero shot is a genuine full-bleed lifestyle image with clear text overlay ("ASOS EXCLUSIVE: LIONESS") — no lorem ipsum, no broken assets, no overlapping text.
- "Unleash your wild side" plus the camo/utility styling gives the drop a coherent visual theme that's carried through the four-image product grid right below the hero.
- The CTA hierarchy is unambiguous: one dark "SHOP LIONESS" button directly under the hero copy, easy to spot and act on.
- The Lioness/I.AM.GIA/Tiger Mist section groups the exclusive with adjacent Aussie brands using consistent typography and clean logo-style labels, which keeps the page from feeling like a random grab-bag.

## What's weak

- Zero offer mechanic — no %/$ off, no free shipping, no loyalty pricing anywhere in the render, so there's no urgency to click today versus browsing later.
- The email dilutes its own headline: by the second scroll it's already pivoting to "Fresh drops from Aussie brands" and then a completely separate "Shop our top brands" logo wall (Mango, Bershka, adidas, Topshop) that has nothing to do with Lioness.
- No social proof, sizing, or availability info on any product — just images, so anyone curious about fit has to leave the email to find out.
- "Party on the way? Bring the heat" is vague filler copy that doesn't say what Lioness actually is (brand history? first ASOS-exclusive drop? limited run?).

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a reason to act now — even a soft one ("selling fast," "limited pieces," or restock timing) would convert this from a browse email into a click email; right now nothing differentiates urgency from a static lookbook page.
- 2. Cut the page in half. Move the "top brands" logo wall and "finishing touches" accessories grid to a separate send — they compete with Lioness for attention and turn a focused drop email into a generic catalog scroll.
- 3. Give Lioness one sentence of story ("Aussie label known for its wild-child prints, now ASOS-exclusive") so first-time-seeing-the-name subscribers understand what they're clicking into.
- 4. Add product-level detail under at least the hero piece — price, or "shop the look" links per item in the 4-image grid — since right now the grid images aren't obviously clickable to specific products.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Camo cargos, sheer layers, and the Aussie label you'll want first.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
All 12 probed material links (100%) failed with connection timeouts, and the email is missing standard compliance headers (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) alongside 25+ images without alt text — indicating both a broken link-tracking layer and unverified deliverability/compliance posture.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **12/12 probed material links failed** with `The read operation timed out`, all routed through the `official-link.asos.com/f/a/...` and `/q/...` click-tracking redirector (SFMC/ExactTarget-style wrapped URLs). Affected CTAs: "Shop Lioness," "Shop the edit," "ASOS," "Download the App," "ASOS.World," "ASOS Premier," "Gift Vouchers," and 4 image links.
- 30 additional (footer/social/utility) links were deprioritized and not probed — full link inventory is unverified.
- **CDN domain inconsistency**: image sources mix `mcusercontent.com/febe49dc5e6ff6dff3dd05d80/...` (Mailchimp asset CDN) with `images.asos-media.com` and `official-link.asos.com` in the same template. A single campaign referencing a third-party ESP's asset CDN alongside ASOS's own domains is an infrastructure anomaly worth confirming against the actual sending platform.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **25 images missing `alt` text**, including all 4 dynamic product carousel images (`api.asos.com/marketing/dynamicimage/...`), 5 `mcusercontent.com` content images, 5 brand logo images (Mango, Bershka, Stradivarius, Adidas, Topshop), and 4 footer icons (ASOS logo, ASOS World, Premier, Gift Cards). Screen readers and blocked-image fallback states will render these as blank.
- Outlook conditional comments (`<!--[if gt mso 15]-->`) and VML/MSO `PixelsPerInch` handling are correctly present, with `mso-font-alt: Arial` fallback on both `@font-face` declarations — no issue here.
- `@supports (animation:...)` is used to gate the ticker animation with a `.fallback`/`.ticker` toggle — degrades gracefully on non-supporting clients.
- `lang="en"` is set on `<html>`, and `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` is present — both correct.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tags (e.g. `*|FNAME|*`, `{{ }}`) or personalization placeholders are visible in the supplied HTML. Note: the source was truncated, so this cannot be confirmed as exhaustive — flag as inconclusive rather than "no issues found."

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL]** `List-Unsubscribe` header not found (RFC 2369) — may be a relay-capture gap rather than a true absence, but unconfirmed.
- **[FAIL]** `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found (RFC 8058) — one-click unsubscribe cannot be confirmed as supported.
- **[FAIL]** No physical mailing address detected in the source — CAN-SPAM requires a valid postal address in every commercial email.
- **[WARN]** `Authentication-Results` header not found via the AgentMail relay — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown from this capture and needs verification at the MTA level.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
All destination URLs are obscured behind `official-link.asos.com` click-tracking redirects, so the actual landing pages and any UTM parameters are not visible in the static HTML. Combined with the 100% timeout rate on probed links (Section 2), landing-page alignment and UTM continuity could not be verified at all in this pass.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Investigate the `official-link.asos.com` redirector — a 100% timeout rate across all material CTAs, if reproducible in production, means every primary link in this send is broken.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 25 identified images, particularly the product carousel and brand-logo images.
3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent (vs. a relay capture gap) to meet RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe requirements.
4. Verify a physical mailing address exists in the full (non-truncated) template footer; add one if it's genuinely absent, per CAN-SPAM.
5. Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC are passing at send time — `Authentication-Results` was not observed in this capture.
6. Once the redirector is resolving again, re-run link probing to confirm UTM parameters and landing pages match the Lioness drop campaign.
7. Clarify why `mcusercontent.com` (Mailchimp) assets appear alongside ASOS-owned CDN domains in the same template — confirm this is expected infrastructure and not a template/spoofing issue.
