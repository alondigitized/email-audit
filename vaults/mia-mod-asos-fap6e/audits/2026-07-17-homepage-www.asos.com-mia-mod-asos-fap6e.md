# Homepage snapshot · www.asos.com · 2026-07-17
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-17**
## Executive summary

- Classic ASOS energy — clean, aspirational, very "we know you already know us." The hero is doing the heavy lifting with two young, styled models and a lifestyle vibe that screams summer/festival, but the messaging is surface-level generic. Right now it's pushing brand identity over product or promo, which honestly feels a bit lazy for a homepage that should be working harder for me as a returning Premier member.

## What's working

- **Dual hero CTA ("SHOP WOMENS" / "SHOP MENS"):** Clean binary split, zero confusion about where to go. I tap SHOP WOMENS and I'm in.
- **Hero model styling:** The woman's look — striped cutout halter, layered gold bangles — is genuinely aspirational for a festival-outfit hunter like me. I clock her silhouette and think "I need that."
- **Mobile nav bar:** Four icons (search, account, heart, bag) are exactly where I expect them. No relearning, no friction.
- **Blue backdrop on hero:** Light and airy — feels current, not like a clearance dump. Sets the right mood immediately.

## What's weak

- **"STYLE EACH MOMENT" copy:** Totally generic. Could be any brand, any year. Doesn't say denim, festival, new drop, summer — nothing that makes me feel like this moment is *my* moment.
- **No Premier acknowledgement:** I pay for Premier. Show me my free next-day. Even a small ribbon — "Hey, you've got Premier" — would make me feel seen and more likely to buy.
- **"New here? Get your first-timer discount":** I am very much not new here. This card is actively signalling that the homepage is optimised for acquisition, not for retaining me. Mildly annoying.
- **No category hooks below the hero:** The only thing partially visible below the fold is two black info cards. No denim banner, no new arrivals teaser, no "shop the look" pull — nothing that tells me what to browse.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "STYLE EACH MOMENT" for a campaign line with a product anchor** — something like "New this week: festival-ready fits" or "Denim drops just landed." Give me a reason to be here *today.*
- **Add a Premier status bar** — a thin banner at the top or beneath the nav: "You've got free next-day delivery" with my name if logged in. It's a trust signal and a conversion nudge in one.
- **Surface a trending category or editorial tile** — replace the "new here" card with a "what's dropping" or "festival edit" carousel panel so I have somewhere to go beyond the generic gender split.
- **Let the hero model's look be shoppable** — a small "Shop her look →" text link below the hero CTA costs nothing and converts curiosity into a tap.
