# Homepage snapshot · www.asos.com · 2026-07-24
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-24**
## Executive summary

- OK so classic ASOS move — bold editorial hero, two models looking genuinely fit, and they're straight-up telling me to "Style Each Moment" which, yes, that's literally what I'm here for. This feels like the ASOS I know: youth-coded, fashion-forward, no faff. Right now they're pushing a clean brand story rather than a sale, which is... fine, but as a Premier member I was low-key hoping to see something more.

## What's working

- **The woman model and her outfit**: that black-and-white cut-out halter is exactly the kind of thing I'd screenshot and hunt on ASOS. Instant click-magnet for me.
- **"SHOP WOMENS" button**: large, white-filled, impossible to miss — one tap and I'm in. Mobile UX done right.
- **Dual-gender hero layout**: the man is there but the woman pulls focus, which is correct for my session. Hierarchy feels intentional.
- **Light blue editorial backdrop**: airy, summery, on-season — makes the whole page feel current without screaming "SALE."
- **Top nav icons**: search, account, wishlist, and bag all visible at a glance — I can jump straight to my saved items without digging.

## What's weak

- **No Premier love above the fold**: I'm an ASOS Premier member. There's no "Welcome back, Mia" energy, no loyalty balance, no free-next-day reminder. This homepage treats me exactly like a stranger.
- **"New here? Get your first-timer discount"** (partially visible below fold): irrelevant to me, and it's the only promo signal in the viewport. A returning-shopper offer would convert better.
- **"STYLE EACH MOMENT" is vague**: punchy but empty — no category hook, no trend signal, nothing that tells me *what* I'm going to style or *why* now is the moment.
- **Zero product specificity**: there's no "new denim drops," no "festival edit," no trend rail. It's a brand poster, not a shop. I'd have to do all the work myself once I tap through.
- **"Download our app" tile**: I'm already on mobile, I almost certainly have the app. This real estate below the hero is wasted on me.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the generic tagline for a trend hook**: replace "STYLE EACH MOMENT" with something like "Festival Season Is Calling" or "New Denim Drops Weekly" — give me a reason to tap *now*, not just someday.
- **Add a Premier member strip above the hero**: a thin banner ("Free next-day delivery — always. Welcome back.") costs one row of pixels and turns the homepage from cold to warm for every returning member.
- **Replace the "New here?" below-fold tile with a trending-now product rail**: show me three or four items in my browse history category. Dynamic personalisation here would be a huge upgrade.
- **Add a lifestyle/festival sub-hero or category shortcut**: a second row with quick-links ("Dresses," "Denim," "Festival Fits," "New In") below the main CTA would save me a whole navigation tap.
