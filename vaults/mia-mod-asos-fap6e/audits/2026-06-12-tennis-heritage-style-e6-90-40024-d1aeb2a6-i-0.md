- ASOS drops a clean tennis-heritage editorial on June 12 — well-timed for Wimbledon season and a clear tonal contrast to the "20% off 1000s of styles" discount push from June 9. The format is the familiar ASOS brand-spotlight structure: hero model → brand-partner block → accessories premium edit → trending grid. - The weakness is the same one that dragged the Topshop "summer edit" (June 11) to a 7: this is a beautiful editorial with no purchase hook. - **Aesthetic coherence** — the white-on-white palette runs from hero to accessories to the "Trending now" grid; nothing fights the tennis mood.
- **Timely category hook** — "Tennis heritage style" lands squarely in Wimbledon week, making the subject feel editorial rather than manufactured.
- **"VIP Courtside looks" framing** — elevates what could be a basic category CTA into a curated edit, adding perceived exclusivity.
- **Multi-brand structure** — two brand partner sections (hero block + kalia section) give the email three distinct stopping points without feeling cluttered.
- **Clean CTAs** — "SHOP TENNIS WOMEN'S," "SHOP COURTSIDE LOOKS," and "SHOP NOW" are each verb + noun, no ambiguity.

## What's weak

- **No offer or urgency** — pure editorial with zero price signal; following a 20%-off email two days prior, this feels like a step backward for conversion.
- **Hero brand logos are too small** — the collaborative brand logos beneath the hero model are nearly unreadable at mobile scale; the partnership value is invisible.
- **"Trending now" dilutes the story** — the bottom grid abandons the tennis thesis entirely; dresses, casual tees, and activewear feel like an afterthought clearinghouse.
- **No personalization or sizing signal** — a category this niche (tennis heritage) is the perfect moment to show a fit detail or size range; the email misses it.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add one time-bound offer hook** — even "Free next-day delivery on the tennis edit, this weekend only" transforms this from browse-only to act-now without compromising the editorial tone.
- 2. **Make the brand partnership legible** — give the collab brands a full-width logo lockup or a callout line ("Shop the adidas × ASOS Courtside Edit") so the partnership reads as a reason to open, not ambient decoration.
- 3. **Cut or refocus "Trending now"** — replace the generic grid with a tighter "Complete the look" module (shoes, bag, racket cover) that keeps the eye in the tennis universe.
- 4. **Add one product-level detail** — a single line like "Sizes 4–20 · In stock now" under the hero CTA converts browsers who've been burned by stockouts before.

**`font-size: 0em` on all table elements (line in `<style>`):**

**Mobile breakpoint at `max-width: 460px`:**

**`alt` attributes:** Not visible in truncated source — cannot confirm. Required on all `<img>` tags, especially CTAs, for accessibility and for clients that block images by default (Outlook desktop, corporate proxies).

**Double charset declaration:**
