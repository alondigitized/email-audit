# Homepage snapshot · www.asos.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- OK so ASOS is doing that classic "we're a cool editorial brand" thing with a split-image hero and a very safe, very vibes-based tagline. It looks sleek, no question — but as a Premier member who's been shopping here for years, this homepage is giving me absolutely nothing I couldn't get on any ASOS visit ever. It's pushing brand energy over actual content.

## What's working

- **The split hero imagery** — woman with big oversized sunnies on the left, guy in rust polo on the right, it's editorial and instantly feels like ASOS. That sunglasses look has major festival energy, I see it.
- **"SHOP WOMENS" / "SHOP MENS" dual CTA** — dead simple, zero ambiguity, one tap to my side of the store. The white buttons pop hard against the dark hero.
- **Clean nav bar** — hamburger, search, account, wishlist, bag all exactly where I expect them. No friction getting oriented.
- **Model casting** — the woman looks young and styled in a way that matches my world. Not a luxury mood, not a fast-fashion cheapness — right in the ASOS sweet spot.

## What's weak

- **Zero Premier recognition** — I pay for Premier, I'm a loyal returning customer, and this page has no idea I exist. No "Welcome back," no loyalty nudge, nothing. The below-fold panel literally says "New here? Get your first-timer discount" — actively irrelevant to me and slightly annoying.
- **"STYLE EACH MOMENT"** — this tagline is doing nothing. It's placeholder energy. It tells me nothing about what ASOS is selling right now.
- **No offer anywhere above the fold** — not a flash sale, not a percentage, not a "free next-day with Premier" reminder (which is literally my favourite thing about ASOS). Completely missed.
- **No product content** — just two people posing. I can't see denim, a festival dress, a trend capsule — nothing to browse-scroll into. The homepage ends at the hero for everything that matters to me.
- **Seasonal mismatch feel** — the warm rust and burgundy palette reads very autumn/winter. In May I'd expect lighter tones, summer occasion, festival capsule, something current.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the generic tagline for a live campaign hook** — "Festival season starts now" or "Denim edits just dropped" actually tells me why to be here today. Ship it next week.
- **Add a Premier ribbon or persistent banner** — a one-line strip above the hero: "Free next-day delivery, unlimited returns — you're a Premier member." Costs nothing, instantly makes me feel seen.
- **Replace the "New here?" module with a "Back for more?" returning-member panel** — even a simple "Your faves category" or a trending-now carousel. The new-shopper acquisition panel is doing the wrong job for the wrong audience.
- **Add a category chip row below the CTAs** — denim, festival, going out, new in — four tappable chips so I can shortcut past "SHOP WOMENS" and get to my actual category without a full category page load.
