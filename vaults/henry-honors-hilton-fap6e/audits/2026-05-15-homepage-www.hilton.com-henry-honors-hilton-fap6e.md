# Homepage snapshot · www.hilton.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- I'm Diamond on Hilton Honors, I hold the Aspire card, and I just got served an error page when I tried to book a Conrad for next weekend. This isn't a homepage review — it's a brand failure. Hilton is currently presenting itself as a company that can't keep its own website running, which is a rough look when your whole pitch is premium hospitality.

## What's working

- **Self-deprecating error copy** — "Maybe it's us, maybe it's you. (It's probably us)" is actually decent copy. It's honest and disarming. Gen X appreciates a brand that doesn't gaslight you when something breaks.
- **Clear visual hierarchy on the error card** — the teal headline, the body copy, and the "GO BACK" button read in the right order. At least the error state itself is cleanly designed.
- **Reference number is present** — `18.1a0ed217.1778873802.1bee20b2` means I could call support and actually get somewhere. Small comfort, but real.

## What's weak

- **The homepage didn't load** — full stop. This is the single worst outcome for any web property. I'm a high-value loyalty member trying to spend points and the page is dead.
- **No fallback content** — there's no cached version, no "search anyway" form, no deep-link to the app. The only escape hatch is "GO BACK" and a truncated "or try:" line that's cut off below the fold.
- **Zero loyalty signal** — as a Diamond Aspire cardholder, I should be recognized the moment I hit the domain. Instead I get a generic error with no acknowledgment that I exist as a customer.
- **No navigation bar** — the Hilton logo is there, but it's inert. No search, no "Find a Hotel," no way to navigate anywhere from this state.
- **"or try:" trails off the bottom** — whatever recovery option follows that text is invisible. If there's a workaround, I can't see it.

## Recommendations

- **Add a search form directly to the error page** — a destination input + date picker on the error screen means I can still book even when the main page fails. One field, one button, one chance to save the session.
- **Pull my Honors status and greet me by name on retry** — if the CDN can serve the error page, it can serve a cookie-aware "Welcome back, Henry" with my points balance. That would change the emotional temperature of this moment entirely.
- **Add a direct app deep-link CTA** — "Try the Hilton Honors app instead" with a smart banner is a legitimate conversion save that this error page is completely missing.
- **Surface the "or try:" content above the fold** — whatever is below that cutoff (probably hilton.com/find-hotels or the app store link), it needs to be visible without scrolling on a standard phone viewport.
