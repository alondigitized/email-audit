# Homepage snapshot · www.hilton.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- I opened hilton.com expecting to check my Honors balance, scope out a Conrad for my next trip, or at minimum see what bonus-points promos are running — and instead I got a full-screen error page. The brand looks like it can't keep its own front door open, which is a bad look for a company asking me to trust them with $800/night stays. The only thing this homepage is pushing right now is an apology.

## What's working

- **Self-deprecating error copy** — "Maybe it's us, maybe it's you. (It's probably us)." is actually a decent tone for a 42-year-old Gen X guy who has seen enough enterprise outage pages to appreciate some humility. Better than "Oops! 😅".
- **Reference number is visible** — `18.860ed217.1779449766.2f1ff307` is right there. If I need to call Diamond support, I can actually give them something useful. That's a small but real operational win.
- **"GO BACK" button is prominent** — large, teal, centered. Recovery path is not buried.

## What's weak

- **Zero loyalty surface area** — I'm a Diamond member with an Aspire card. Hilton has my login cookie, my stay history, my points balance. None of it surfaces here. Even a soft "Sign in to check your account status" would be better than nothing.
- **No alternative navigation** — The page cuts off with "or try:" and whatever links are below the fold aren't visible. I have no shortcut to the app, the Honors portal, or a search box.
- **The Hilton logo is the only brand element** — No property imagery, no color beyond the error container outline. The page looks like it was assembled in 15 minutes, which — given this is the front door for a premium loyalty program — is embarrassing.
- **No ETA or status context** — Is this a 30-second hiccup or is the site down for maintenance? No timestamp, no status page link, nothing. I'm left guessing whether to reload or give up.

## Recommendations

- **Add a link to the Hilton Honors app** immediately below the error — Diamond members like me will pivot to the app rather than reload a broken browser page; make that pivot frictionless.
- **Surface a status page link** ("Check hilton.com/status for updates") so high-value customers aren't left hitting refresh in the dark.
- **Inject a soft member-recognition message** — even a cookie-based "Welcome back, Henry — we'll be right back" would cost almost nothing to build and signal that the brand values its returning guests.
- **Add the Hilton Honors phone number** for Diamond/Gold members in the error state — when the site is down is exactly when elite customers need a fallback, and burying it forces me to Google it myself.
