# Homepage snapshot · www.hilton.com · 2026-09-29
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- This is Hilton pushing a brand-discovery campaign, not a booking or points play. "New ways to stay with Hilton" sits over a jungle villa in Tulum, with the teaser "Is this Hilton?" It looks premium and the search bar is exactly where I'd expect it. But nothing here speaks to a Diamond member who stacks bonuses, and I don't see a single number: no points promo, no rate, no percentage.

## What's working

- **The search bar:** Destination, dates, guests, and "Special rates" are all one row, and "Find a hotel" is a high-contrast navy button. I can start a booking in two taps.
- **Top nav:** "Hilton Honors", "Offers", and "Credit Cards" are all in the first row. I go straight to Offers and my Aspire-related pages without hunting.
- **"Special rates" with the gift icon:** It sits inside the search bar, so I'd expect to apply member or promo rates before searching. That is where I want them.
- **Hero photography:** The A-frame villa in the jungle is striking and clearly hospitality. It signals "unusual property", which is the intended message.
- **Pause control (bottom right):** A visible pause button on the auto-playing hero is a nice accessibility touch.

## What's weak

- **"Unlock exclusive benefits with Hilton Honors" popup:** It says nothing specific and covers the nav. I'm already a Diamond, so this is noise.
- **Hero copy:** "Is this Hilton?" is a cute riddle. I don't shop on curiosity, I shop on points math. There's no reason to click "Explore new ways to stay".
- **No points or promo message:** I read every points-bonus promo, and nothing on this screen tells me whether there's a bonus or a member-rate advantage.
- **Clipped nav and caption:** "Hilton Grand Vac…" is truncated under the popup, and "La Valise Tulum, an S" runs off the edge. It looks unfinished for a brand at this level.
- **Nothing for the Conrad/Waldorf tier:** The imagery is a boutique jungle villa, not the luxury flagship brands I book.
- **Wording that hints at dynamic pricing:** "Special rates" doesn't say whether I'm seeing a member rate or a public rate. I'm skeptical of that ambiguity.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the popup for signed-in or known visitors:** Show a rewards strip instead, for example "Diamond • X points • next stay bonus". Never show "Join or sign in" over the nav.
- **Add one concrete offer under the headline:** A pill like "Members: up to 25% off + 2,000 bonus points on stays through [date]" makes "Explore" worth tapping.
- **Fix the layout clipping:** Keep the property caption inside the safe area (or let it wrap), and don't let the popup overlap nav items.
- **Rotate a luxury slide into the hero:** Give a Conrad or Waldorf Astoria property a slot, with a "Book with Honors" CTA that opens the member-rate search.
