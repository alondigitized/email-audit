# Homepage snapshot · www.hilton.com · 2026-09-15
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- This reads like Hilton doing what Hilton does — clean, corporate, competent, and light on specifics. "Enjoy savings on fall stays" is a seasonal placeholder headline, and the three cards below the fold (Honors Discount, Breakfast Included, 2X Points Package) are the only real hooks for someone like me. It's pushing a generic fall booking push, not anything that acknowledges I'm Diamond or that I've got an Aspire card sitting in my wallet.

## What's working

- **"Book your stay" CTA** — unambiguous, single tap, right where my eye lands after the search bar.
- **The three offer cards** ("Honors Discount Advance Purchase," "Breakfast Included," "2X Points Package") — the 2X Points card in particular is exactly the kind of thing I scan for as a points stacker.
- **Price Match Guarantee copy** — "backed by our Price Match Guarantee" is a smart trust line for someone skeptical of dynamic pricing like me.
- **Search bar defaults** — "1 room, 1 guest" and a visible "Special rates" dropdown means I can get to member rates fast.

## What's weak

- **No recognition that I'm signed in or Diamond** — the popup says "Unlock exclusive benefits with Hilton Honors, Join or sign in," which is aimed at a prospect, not a 15-year Diamond member. It should know who I am.
- **Hero imagery skews younger** — the lounge scene shows what looks like a group of 20-somethings socializing, not the business/leisure Gen X traveler this brand actually retains.
- **No hard numbers above the fold** — "Enjoy savings" with no visible %, no points multiplier called out until I scroll, no deadline.
- **Offer cards require an extra tap** — the chevron dropdowns hide the actual discount amount, which for someone comparing the points math is friction.

## Recommendations

- Swap the generic "Enjoy savings on fall stays" headline for something that surfaces a number — "Members save up to 20% + earn 2X points on fall stays" — right in the hero.
- If I'm logged in (or recognized via a returning cookie), replace the "Join or sign in" popup with "Welcome back, Diamond member — here's your rate."
- Put the 2X Points Package card first in the row, not third — that's the strongest hook for a points-stacking persona and it's currently getting the least visual weight.
- Add a visible countdown or "book by" date on the Honors Discount card — right now nothing here is time-bounded, so there's no reason to act today instead of next week.
