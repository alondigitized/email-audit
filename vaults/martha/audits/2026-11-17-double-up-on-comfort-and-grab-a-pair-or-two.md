# Double up on comfort and grab a pair (or two)
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "LAST CHANCE" in the red banner got me, because it's the last day of the BOGO thing I've been getting all week. Skechers clearly knows me: "Hi, Martha," my 1000 points and the $5 reward strip are right at the top, same as the 11/16 "Made for the great outdoors" send. Then the hero hits and the flip-board "50% OFF" is sliced in half again, so it reads "00% OFF". That's the same bug as the 11/12 countdown email, and it's the one number I need to see.
- The body is the usual grid: Women, Men, then Kids with a sparkly high-top that's finally something for my 5-year-old. But there's no boy's sneaker for my 9-year-old, no velcro or light-up callouts, and no promo code. I'd probably click Kids' because it's the last day and the deal is real. I wouldn't forward it. This is the sixth Skechers email this week, so it's mostly "mark as read" with a tap on Shop Kids first.

## What's working

- "Hi, Martha" with my 1000 points and the $5 redeem strip tells me they know me and gives me a reason to shop.
- "LAST CHANCE" and "Valid online only thru 11/17" make the deadline feel real on a day I could actually act.
- The "BUY ONE, GET ONE" and "OR 20% OFF 1 PAIR" lines give me two clear ways to save.
- The sparkly kids' high-top has the glitter and pink my daughter loves.
- The "Under $50 / $75 / $100 / $125" tiles let me shop by budget, which is how I shop.

## What's weak

- The flip-board digits are cut in half, so "50% OFF" reads like "00% OFF". It's the key number, and it's broken again.
- Kids gets one shoe, a girls' sparkle high-top. There's nothing for my 9-year-old son: no lace-up, no blue or black.
- No velcro or light-up callout on the kids' shoe, and no family bundle angle even though it's a BOGO.
- Women and Men each get a big block, so Kids feels like the third wheel, even though I'm the mom shopping for two kids.
- The best sellers row is tiny, with no prices, names or labels, so I can't tell what I'd be buying.
- The BOGO terms are in tiny fine print at the bottom: "select regular-priced shoes", with exclusions. I'd want that visible up top.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the flip-board render, or swap it for plain live text like "BOGO 50% OFF". The offer has to be readable.
- 2. Make Kids the lead after the hero, with two tiles: girls' (light-up, velcro) and boys' (lace-up, blue/black). Tell me "buy one for each kid and get 50% off the second pair."
- 3. Add a one-line "what's included" under the CTA, like "Select styles, kids included," so I don't have to scroll to the fine print.
- 4. Show prices and names on the best sellers and link each to its product page.
- 5. Add a members line: "Members: your 1000 points = $5 off, stack it today."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed broken-experience defect, an empty greeting ("Hi ,"), and several source-level problems in the tracking pixels, head markup and accessibility settings. Tracking links, authentication, unsubscribe headers and UTMs could not be verified from the data provided (details below).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Scheme-less pixel URL (confirmed):** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…">` has no `http(s)://`. It resolves as a relative path and will 404 or never fire, so Return Path/Validity seed and deliverability monitoring is lost for this send. It also has no `alt` attribute.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=…&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_BUNDLE_FULL_EN_11172026` is plain HTTP. Mixed-content blocking and proxies may suppress it, which undercounts the data it collects.
- **QA false positive on `o.gif`:** QA flags missing alt, but the source shows `alt=""`. That is valid for a tracking pixel, so no action is needed.
- **Heavy third-party beacon stack:** three `beacon.krxd.net` pixels (`1x1_usermatch.gif` ×2, `1x1_ad_impression.gif`) plus ink1000 and Return Path sit in a hidden block alongside the SFMC open pixel (`click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx`). The krxd beacons carry no `alt`, and some clients flag stacked third-party pixels.
- **Possible hashed-email disclosure:** `partner_uid=adf27fee…189a` (64 hex chars, parameter named `…_sha25`) looks like a SHA-256 of the recipient identity. It is sent to a third party on open. This is inferred from the format, not confirmed.
- **Unverified links:** QA skipped 41 tracking links (`click.emails.skechers.com` redirects) without an HTTP probe, so destination health is unknown.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed `<meta>` tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` both lack `http-equiv`. The charset declaration and IE compat directive are therefore inert, and the client's charset default applies.
- **Zoom restrictions:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on supporting clients. `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }` disables iOS text scaling. Both work against WCAG 1.4.4.
- **No `lang` attribute:** the root element is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` only, so screen readers have no language hint.
- **Missing alt text:** QA flags `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`, served from `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115…/m/1/`. The source is truncated, so I can't tell whether it is content or decorative. If it's content, it needs `alt`.
- **Insecure image host:** the same PNG loads over HTTP, so it may be blocked or trigger mixed-content warnings. The host serves HTTPS elsewhere in the sender's infrastructure, so switching the URL to HTTPS is likely a one-line fix.
- **Plain-text part:** QA reports 4,766 of 6,740 characters (about 71%) are URLs. That is a spam-score risk and a poor plain-text reading experience.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Empty greeting (QA FAIL):** the greeting renders as `Hi ,`, so the first-name field is empty or unresolved with no fallback. SFMC AMPscript should use a fallback such as `%%=IIF(Empty(@firstname),"there",@firstname)=%%` or drop the greeting when the name is empty. The greeting is past the truncation point in the source I was given, so this rests on the QA finding alone.
- No other unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) were visible in the provided source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **Authentication:** QA found no `Authentication-Results` header because the message came via the AgentMail relay. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is **unknown**, which is a pipeline limitation and not evidence of a failure. The `emails.skechers.com` sender, click and image hosts are consistent with each other.
- **Unsubscribe link, `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and physical address:** these were not in the truncated HTML or the QA output, so I can't confirm them either way.
- **Sender address:** `no-reply@emails.skechers.com` is a minor concern, since replies are not accepted.
- **Privacy:** the hashed-identifier beacons in section 2 may need to be covered by the privacy disclosures.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Not assessable:** no landing-page hrefs appear in the truncated source, and all 41 click links were skipped as redirects, so UTM parameters and landing alignment can't be evaluated.
- **Campaign ID:** `PRO_US_NA_U_BUNDLE_FULL_EN_11172026` appears consistently in the ink1000 and krxd beacons, so the ID is at least consistent across those pixels.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the greeting fallback** in AMPscript, or suppress the greeting when the name is empty (the confirmed FAIL).
2. **Fix the Return Path pixel URL** by adding `https://`, and add `alt=""`.
3. **Move the ink1000 pixel and `image.emails.skechers.com` assets to HTTPS.** Add `alt` to the PNG if it is content.
4. **Correct the head metas** to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
5. **Remove `maximum-scale=1` and `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`.** Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
6. **Review the krxd and ink1000 pixels** for privacy and consent, and consider trimming the stack.
7. **Shorten the plain-text version** so URLs are a smaller share of it.
8. **Improve the QA pipeline:** capture the original `Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, resolve tracking redirects (at least a sample) to check UTMs and landing pages, and stop flagging `alt=""` on tracking pixels as missing alt.
