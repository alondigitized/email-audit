# Celebrate America's 250th in Style
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-26**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so I'm scrolling through my inbox during naptime — my daughter finally went down — and this Skechers email slides in. Patriotic, red-white-blue, very July 4th energy. Visually it looks clean and the photos are nice, but my thumb paused for half a second and then kept moving. "Celebrate America's 250th in Style" tells me nothing about what I'm actually getting — no discount in the subject, no kids mention, nothing that says *this is for you specifically, Martha*.
- The email itself is fine once you're inside. There's a women's section, a men's section, sandals, casuals, apparel, and a 25% off apparel offer. But here's the thing: I don't shop for apparel at Skechers. I shop for shoes, and specifically I'm almost always looking for my 5-year-old or my 9-year-old first. Kids don't appear anywhere in the main body — just buried in the footer navigation. Compare that to the June 19 "The Whole Crew Needs Fresh Kicks" email, which stopped my thumb cold because it spoke directly to my reality. This one doesn't do that.
- The thing that actually bothered me before I even opened it: the preview text in my inbox was a wall of `[{ "@context": "http://schema.org/"...` — raw JSON code. That's not a preheader, that's a backend accident. Combined with a subject that offers zero hook, I almost didn't open this at all. I'd mark it read and move on. No click, no forward.

## What's working

- **Timing is right** — July 4th is three weeks out and the red/white/blue palette landed immediately; it feels seasonal, not stale
- **Women's Weekend Essentials section** — female model, clean bike-ride lifestyle shot, this is the demographic I'm in, and it's prominently placed second in the scroll
- **25% off apparel is clearly called out** — the offer block is readable and specific; I know exactly what the discount is
- **Layout is clean on mobile** — no broken images, no overlapping text, sections stack neatly, and the SHOP NOW buttons are tap-friendly
- **Men's section included** — if I were also shopping for my husband, that would be a bonus; it's there without overwhelming the email

## What's weak

- **No kids section in the body** — for a subscriber who consistently clicks kids' content, hiding kids in the footer navigation only is a miss; "The Whole Crew" email got this right and this one doesn't
- **Preview text is schema markup** — `[{ "@context": "http://schema.org/"...` — seeing raw JSON in my inbox before I even open is jarring and eroded my trust for a second
- **25% off apparel, not shoes** — the hero offer is apparel discount; I'm here for shoes, so this didn't move me toward a click
- **No urgency or deadline visible** — "America's 250th" implies July 4th but there's no "ends July 4th" or "this weekend only" anywhere I can see; nothing pulls me to act now vs. later

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the preheader immediately** — the JSON-LD bleed in the preview is the single highest-priority fix; it undercuts every other investment in this email before anyone opens it
- 2. **Add a kids section to the email body** — even a small "Kids' Red, White & Blue Picks" block with one hero image would serve my segment; a mom who opened "The Whole Crew" email doesn't expect to be invisible here
- 3. **Move the offer to shoes or make it broader** — 25% off apparel is too narrow; "25% off sitewide" or "shoes + apparel" would give me a reason to click vs. skip
- 4. **Add a deadline to the offer** — "through July 4th" or "ends Sunday" creates the urgency that's completely missing right now
- 5. **Swap "SHOP NOW" for something specific** — "Shop Patriotic Styles" or "Shop Kids' Picks" tells me exactly where I'm going; generic "SHOP NOW" is friction

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop Men's, Women's & Kids' patriotic picks — offer ends July 4th.`

## Technical Audit — Skechers "Celebrate America's 250th in Style"

**Sent:** 2026-06-26 | **From:** no-reply@emails.skechers.com

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is structurally sound with no broken links or missing compliance elements detected, but has a pervasive HTTP-over-HTTPS image delivery problem affecting 13+ assets and two third-party pixel beacons with mixed security implications.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**26 tracking links skipped** — all pass through `click.emails.skechers.com` (Salesforce Marketing Cloud click-redirect). No broken destinations confirmed, but click-redirect chain cannot be end-to-end validated by automated probe.

**Third-party sync pixels (Salesforce DMP / Krux):**
```
https://beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif?partner=LprH63a7_sfmc_100007688_sha25&partner_uid=adf27fee...
https://beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif?partner=LprH63a7_sfmc_100007688_subid&partner_uid=122277199
```
- `partner_uid` in the second pixel appears to be a raw subscriber ID (`122277199`) sent in plaintext to a third-party domain. Confirm whether this is intentional and covered by privacy policy.
- SHA-256 hash in first pixel is correct practice; the raw ID in the second is a separate data-sharing event.

**Returnpath pixel uses HTTP:**
```html
<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1dbf0dcef79cbad82c12fe25342701e4499" ...>
```
Missing `https://` protocol prefix entirely — will resolve as a relative URL in strict parsers or be blocked as mixed content.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**HTTP image sources** — 13 assets served over `http://` instead of `https://`:

| Asset | Impact |
|---|---|
| `o.gif` (ink1000.com tracking pixel) | Blocked in Gmail, Apple Mail |
| Skechers logo (`dde00662...png`) | Logo may not render |
| `49468f73...png` (unknown, no alt) | Silent failure |
| App Store badge | CTA image broken |
| Google Play badge | CTA image broken |
| Curbside Pickup icon | Feature icon broken |
| AfterPay icon | Feature icon broken |
| Find A Store icon | Feature icon broken |
| Instagram/Facebook/YouTube icons | All social icons broken |

All assets on `image.emails.skechers.com` should be served over HTTPS. This is a CDN configuration issue — the domain supports TLS but the template is generating `http://` hrefs.

**Missing alt text on 2 images:**
- `o.gif` (ink1000 tracking pixel — acceptable for pixels, but flagged)
- `49468f73-4651-4af3-bea2-61d1ae5db486.png` — unknown content image with no alt; if this is a content image (hero/product), it will be invisible to screen readers and when images are blocked.

**CSS media query duplication:** Breakpoint rules for `max-width: 375px` and `max-width: 640px` are defined twice (once in each `<style>` block) with identical declarations. No functional breakage, but increases parse weight unnecessarily.

**`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`** — applied globally via `* {}`. This disables user-initiated text scaling on iOS, which is an accessibility regression for low-vision users.
