# Made for the great outdoors
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Made for the great outdoors" is a shrug for me. I'm not hiking anywhere on a Saturday with a 5-year-old and a 9-year-old. Skechers does know who I am, though. "Hi, Martha," my 1000 points and the $5 reward strip are all at the top again. That's the same strip I got on 11/15, 11/13 and 11/12, so it's the sixth Skechers email in about a week and my eyes slide right past it.
- The BOGO 50% banner is the only thing that grabbed me, and it's tiny and sits above the actual hero. Then it's all adult hiking boots and a woman lacing up on a rock. There's nothing for kids at all. No velcro, no light-up soles, no blue or black lace-ups for my 9-year-old. Kids is just a gray bar way down at the bottom. I'd skim it, maybe tap BOGO, and mark it read. I would not forward this to anyone.

## What's working

- "Hi, Martha" and my 1000 points are right at the top, so I feel recognized.
- The BOGO 50% off or 20% off a single pair banner is clear, and the "auto-applied at cart" line takes the promo-code hassle away.
- The green and earth-tone layout is clean and easy to scan on my phone, and the buttons are big enough to tap.
- The Black Friday countdown fits the season, and the cold-weather boots feel timely.

## What's weak

- There's no kids content anywhere in the hero or body. I'm shopping for a 5-year-old girl and a 9-year-old boy.
- The best offer is squeezed into a thin banner above the hero, so the big "Fresh Air Favorites" image takes over.
- The "GET OUTDOORS" and "SHOP HIKING" buttons are vague. They don't tell me what I'm saving.
- The size grids only cover women's and men's. There's no kids' sizing.
- This is the sixth near-identical promo this week with the same points strip. It feels like noise.
- The fine print is tiny, and the deal is only valid 11/12 to 11/17. I'd have to squint to confirm it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a kids row with a boys' lace-up hiking boot and a girls' pair, plus "BOGO works on kids' pairs." That alone would get me clicking.
- 2. Make the BOGO offer the hero, with a "Ends 11/17" deadline up top instead of burying it.
- 3. Show a real price or savings amount on the product tiles.
- 4. Replace the adult-only size grids with a "Shop kids' sizes" block.
- 5. Use my 1000 points. Something like "You're $5 away" could pull me to the cart.
- 6. Space out the sends. Six in a week trains me to ignore you.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed personalization failure (an empty greeting slot, "Hi ,") and several source-level defects: a protocol-less tracking pixel URL, two `http://` images without alt text, and missing `lang`/`http-equiv` attributes. Authentication, unsubscribe and UTM compliance could not be verified from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken pixel URL (confirmed in source):** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…">` has no scheme. Clients resolve it as a relative path, so the Return Path/Validity seed-tracking pixel will 404 or not fire. It also has no `alt`.
- **Insecure tracking image:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108404282&mi_ecmp=MKG_US_NA_U_MULTI_PROD_EN_11162026` loads over plain HTTP. It may be blocked or rewritten by clients and proxies, which would cause under-reporting.
- **Unverified links:** 42 tracking links on click-redirect domains (`click.emails.skechers.com`) were skipped, so destination status and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Hidden pixels:** Four `beacon.krxd.net` pixels and the open pixel (`click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx`) sit inside `display:none !important` containers. Some clients skip hidden images, so open counts may be unreliable.
- **Identifier in a third-party URL:** The `1x1_usermatch.gif` pixels pass `partner_uid=adf27fee…189a` (a 64-hex value labelled `sha25` in the `partner` parameter, so likely a SHA-256 hash) and a raw `subid` (`122277199`) to a third-party domain. This is a data-sharing point that needs a privacy-policy and consent check.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `o.gif` and `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` (`http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/…`) have no `alt`. The 1x1 pixels are harmless, but the `.png` looks like a content image, so screen readers and image-blocked views get nothing.
- **Mixed content:** The `.png` is served over `http://` from the sender's own image domain. It can be blocked in HTTPS contexts. Serve it over HTTPS.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 resize issue.
- **Missing `lang`:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` attribute.
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` lack `http-equiv`, so they are ignored.
- **Layout tables:** In the visible portion, `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0" border="0">` and `<table class="widthFull" width="640">` have no `role="presentation"`. The HTML is truncated, so I can't confirm this for the rest of the email.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Skechers®</title>` is generic and does not reflect the subject line.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** The QA check found a greeting that renders as "Hi ,". The first-name field resolved to empty and has no fallback. The greeting markup falls outside the truncated source, so I can't point to the exact token. This is a visible defect for every recipient with a blank name, and it is likely systemic for this test inbox.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication unverified:** No `Authentication-Results` header was found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it is not evidence of a sender misconfiguration.
- **Sender address:** `no-reply@emails.skechers.com` is a no-reply address. This is not a CAN-SPAM violation, but any reply mechanism should be provided elsewhere.
- **Not verifiable:** The unsubscribe link, the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, and the physical postal address are not in the truncated source or the QA output. No issue is claimed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs not confirmed:** Click URLs are wrapped in the SFMC redirect (`click.emails.skechers.com`), and the final destination URLs and UTM parameters are not in the visible source. The campaign identifier `MKG_US_NA_U_MULTI_PROD_EN_11162026` appears only in the pixel URLs. I can't confirm that UTMs are present or that landing pages match the "buy one, get one 50% off" preheader offer.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the greeting by adding a fallback (for example, "Hi there,") in the merge expression, or suppress the name segment when the field is empty.
2. Add `https://` to `pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif…`, or remove the pixel if it is no longer needed.
3. Move `ink1000.com/…/o.gif` and `image.emails.skechers.com/…/dde00662….png` to HTTPS.
4. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels and descriptive `alt` text to the `.png`.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag, add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and correct the meta tags to `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`.
6. Add `role="presentation"` to layout tables.
7. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML and raw headers. That would check the unsubscribe link, `List-Unsubscribe`, the physical address, `Authentication-Results` (via a direct-delivery seed), UTM parameters on the resolved destination URLs, and the 42 skipped tracked links.
