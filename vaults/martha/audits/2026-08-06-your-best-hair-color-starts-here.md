# Your Best Hair Color Starts Here ✨
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- Sally again — for the ninth-ish time in under two weeks. My gut reaction before I even open it now is just tired. But this one's actually a decent send: buy-two-get-one-free hair color with a whole grid of products, and my eye landed right on "Buy two, get one free select hair color" without much effort.
- That said, none of this is for me the way this brand thinks it is. I dye my own hair maybe twice a year when I remember, and I'm not scrolling through six different color lines comparing "Purple AF" to "Neon Pink" on a Saturday with my kids climbing on me. It's a fine BOGO email for someone into hair color as a hobby — it's just not urgent enough to make me stop what I'm doing.
- I'd skim it, maybe squint at the buy-two-get-one deal for two seconds, and move on. Not clicking, not forwarding — this isn't a "hey look at this" moment for anyone I know.

## What's working

- The confetti/dot pattern up top actually looks festive and on-brand for a "COLORfest" campaign name — it's colorful without being chaotic.
- "Buy two, get one free select hair color" is a real, specific, easy-to-understand offer, not vague marketing fluff.
- The product grid shows actual boxes/tubes with brand names (L'Oréal, Wella, Arctic Fox) so I know exactly what I'd be buying before I click anything.
- The rewards banner near the bottom ("Earn 2x points... Shop Twice in August, Earn a $5 Reward in September") is a nice loyalty touch if I were actually a program member.

## What's weak

- I dye my own hair maybe twice a year — this whole email assumes a level of hair-color enthusiasm I just don't have, so it reads as "not for me" the second I see it.
- The hero model is styled for a much more done-up, salon-ready look than anything I'm doing between school drop-off and soccer practice.
- It's yet another Sally email in a relentless cadence — at this point I've stopped registering the subject lines individually, they all blur together.
- There are two separate BOGO deals (regular hair color, then Wella toner) stacked on top of each other, which muddies which offer I'm supposed to care about most.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut send frequency — I'm getting near-daily emails from this brand and it's actively training me to ignore all of them, including the good ones.
- 2. Pick ONE hero offer and lead with it big — the buy-two-get-one hair color deal is the strongest thing here; the Wella toner BOGO buried lower should be a secondary mention, not a second full campaign block.
- 3. Segment sends based on actual purchase history — if I haven't bought hair color in six months, don't send me the eighth color-focused email this month; send me something else or throttle back.
- 4. Move the rewards banner ("Earn 2x points") higher — that's actually the part that would get a loyalty-minded shopper like me to open next time, and it's currently buried near the footer.
