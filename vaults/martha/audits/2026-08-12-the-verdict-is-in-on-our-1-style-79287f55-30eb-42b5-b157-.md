- Text-to-join and app download options are visible for anyone wanting more deals later.

## What's weak

- Not a single kids' shoe, kids' section, or family bundle anywhere in the visible email — "KIDS" only shows up as one line of plain text way down in the "Shop Additional Styles" list.
- Whole hero section is adult lifestyle photography — a woman in a maxi skirt by a shipping container, a guy in joggers on a bench — none of that is my Saturday-afternoon shopping mission.

- No back-to-school framing even though the fine print at the bottom literally says "BACK-TO-SCHOOL SALE" — that messaging never makes it into the actual visual hierarchy.
