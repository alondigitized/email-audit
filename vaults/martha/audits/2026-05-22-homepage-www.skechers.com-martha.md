# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a patriotic-moment push — Skechers is going all-in on a Red, White & Blue Collection for Memorial Day weekend, and the BOGO 50% banner is genuinely one of the strongest opening offers I've seen on a footwear homepage. But I opened this app to find something for my 5-year-old and my 9-year-old, and nothing above the fold acknowledges that kids exist. If I wasn't already a loyal Skechers shopper, I'd be poking around the nav wondering where the kids section even is.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF banner** — This is the first thing I see and it's the most compelling offer Skechers could put in front of me. Buying two pairs of kids shoes in one shot at half off the second? That's exactly my weekend errand mindset.
- **Members-only loyalty strip** — "Earn a $5 Reward on $75+" is concrete and motivating. The Login link is right there. I actually want to log in.
- **"Until 5/25" deadline** — That's three days from today. It creates genuine urgency without feeling fake. I'd feel like I'm leaving money on the table if I close the tab.
- **Category nav bar** — Slip-ins, Sandals, Arch Fit, BOBs — these are scannable in under two seconds on mobile, which is all I have while my kids are being occupied in the other room.
- **Seasonal relevance** — Red, White & Blue for Memorial Day weekend is exactly the right energy. The brand feels current.

## What's weak

- **Zero kids presence above the fold** — My primary reason for being here is completely invisible. No "Kids" category in the visible nav, no kids lifestyle shot, no kids promo. The nav cuts off at "G" which might be "Girls" but I can't tell, and I shouldn't have to guess.
- **No shop button in the hero** — The patriotic hero is visually striking but there's no tappable CTA attached to it. "The Red, White & Blue Collection" is a label, not an invitation to shop. I don't know where to tap to see the actual products.
- **Adult couple framing for a mom-shopping context** — Both models are young adults in fashion-forward styling. Nothing telegraphs "practical footwear for the whole family," which is Skechers' actual strength with parents.
- **Loyalty strip requires login** — The $5 reward is members-only. If I'm not logged in (common on mobile), I see a benefit I can't immediately capture. That's a small friction but it's there.
- **Free shipping threshold nowhere visible** — I'd add more to my cart to hit free shipping, but I don't know what that number is without digging.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Kids" category to the visible nav bar** — Right now "Slip-ins, Sandals, Arch Fit, BOBs" are all adult categories. Swap or add "Kids" so I don't have to scroll or hunt. This is a one-week ship.
- **Put a "Shop Kids" CTA tile directly below the hero** — Even a simple banner: "Back-to-summer shoes for kids. BOGO applies." A lifestyle shot of a kid running in Skechers would stop my scroll immediately.
- **Add a visible shop button to the patriotic hero** — "Shop the Collection →" in a contrasting button color. The imagery is good; give me somewhere to tap.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold in the BOGO banner or loyalty strip** — Something like "BOGO 50% + Free shipping over $75" turns the banner from a discount into a full-value proposition and nudges cart size up.
