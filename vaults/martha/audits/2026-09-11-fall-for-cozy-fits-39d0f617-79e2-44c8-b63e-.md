# Fall for cozy fits
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-11**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Hi, Martha," 1000 points, 20% off code EXTRA20 — I've now seen this exact skeleton for a full week straight, right down to the same font treatment on the offer banner. At least this time there's an actual "Kids'" section with a real kid shoe photo instead of just Women's/Men's, so someone over there remembered I have two kids who need shoes more than I need shoes. The "Cozy Fit" fall theme with the leaves is kind of cute, I'll give them that.
- But the kids' shoe shown is one generic sneaker — no color options, no callout for my daughter's velcro/light-up needs or my son's lace-up durability needs, just "Kids'" in yellow letters over a single pink-and-white shoe that could be either kid's or neither. I'm not clicking through for myself, and I'm sure not forwarding this — it's the fourth or fifth near-identical 20% off email this week, my thumb is basically on autopilot to archive at this point.

## What's working

- Kids' section is now visually present with its own shoe photo, which is more than most of this week's sends bothered with.
- The 20% off + code EXTRA20 offer is stated clearly at the top with a real expiration date (9/14).
- My rewards points balance and $5 reward nudge are right up front, which I do actually track.
- Fall leaves + "Fall For Cozy Fit" theme feels seasonally appropriate for September.
- Four clear category CTA buttons (Women's/Men's/Kids'/Wide) make it obvious where to tap.

## What's weak

- The kids' shoe is one single pair with no distinction between a girl style and a boy style — no velcro/sparkle option for my 5-year-old, no rugged lace-up for my 9-year-old.
- This is the same "Hi Martha / points / 20% off" template I've opened all week — zero novelty, so my brain has already tuned it out.
- No family bundle or multi-kid discount mentioned, which is the one thing that would actually get me to buy two pairs at once.
- "SHOP KIDS' COZY FIT" button links to one generic style page, not separated by boy/girl.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split the Kids' section into two shoes side by side — a girl's velcro/light-up style and a boy's lace-up style — so I immediately see something for each kid instead of one ambiguous sneaker.
- 2. Add a "buy 2 pairs, extra 10% off" or family bundle callout — I'm shopping for two kids at once, incentivize that.
- 3. Vary the subject/template noticeably from the last five days — even just moving the points banner or changing the offer format would break the sameness.
- 4. Lead the kids' section higher in the email since that's my actual reason for opening Skechers emails, not buried under Women's and Men's first.
