# Last Call for Holiday Savings
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-05**
## Executive summary

- OK so. Sally. Again. I've got "Celebrate with 25% off" from July 3rd still sitting in my read pile, and now two days later I'm looking at "Last Call for Holiday Savings" — same AMERICA25 code, same 25% sitewide, just a different subject line. At some point "last call" stops meaning anything when you've been doing it for five consecutive sends. That said, I'll be honest: the red hero caught my eye, the code is right there in the first two seconds, and "2-hour delivery" hits because it's a Saturday afternoon and I'm on the couch with the kids.
- The products they're featuring, though — Wella ash blonde toner, ion developer, creme lightener — that's less "mom browsing on her phone" and more "salon professional restocking." I'm not bleaching anything today. The sitewide offer is the real hook; the featured product choices just don't sell it for me.
- I'd scroll through, maybe eyeball the trending section, and close the tab. I'm not forwarding this to anyone — who wants to hear from Sally Beauty a 10th time this month?

## What's working

- AMERICA25 is front and center in the hero — no hunting, no tiny print, just the code staring at me
- "2-hour delivery" shows up twice and that's smart for a lazy Saturday browse
- The red hero with the rose gold airstyler stopped my scroll — feels premium, visually clean
- "Last Day to Save" on July 5th is timing that actually makes sense — the holiday just ended yesterday
- Preview text leads with the real offer and code, not "view this in browser" noise

## What's weak

- This is at minimum the third send with the AMERICA25 / 25%-off formula — after July 2nd's "flash deals" and July 3rd's "celebrate," today's "last call" has zero credibility
- The trending products (creme lightener, volume developer, liquid toner) feel salon-supply-closet, not casual weekend impulse buy
- "Shop now" on a 25%-off-sitewide email tells me literally nothing about where I land — a category or product type would do so much more work
- No Beauty Club angle at all — I'm a member; why aren't my points in this email?

## Recommendations

- 1. **Swap the trending products for everyday replenishable items** — dry shampoo, heat protectant, a curl cream. Things I'd actually add to cart at 25% off right now, not salon developer.
- 2. **Add a loyalty hook in the hero** — one line: "Beauty Club members: stack your points on top of this." Makes me feel like a customer, not a stranger.
- 3. **Hold the deadline** — if AMERICA25 is "ending tonight" today and it was "ending tonight" on July 3rd, the urgency is gone. Pick a date and mean it.
- 4. **Sharpen the CTA to a category** — "Shop 25% Off Hair + Nails" is infinitely better than "Shop now."
- 5. **Subject Alt A:** `Your code: AMERICA25 — 25% off ends tonight`
- 6. **Subject Alt B:** `25% off everything at Sally. Today only.`
- 7. **Preheader Alt A:** `Free 2-hour delivery included. Use AMERICA25 at checkout.`
- 8. **Preheader Alt B:** `Any brand, any category — stock up before midnight.`
