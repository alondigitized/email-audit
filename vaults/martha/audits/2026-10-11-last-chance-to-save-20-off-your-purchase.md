# Last chance to save 20% off your purchase
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-11**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the fifth "Fall Favorites" send I've gotten in about a week, so my first thought was "again?" The 20% off banner is big and impossible to miss, and "LAST CHANCE" with a "Valid thru 10/11" line is a real deadline, since it's today. That makes it the first email this week where the urgency feels honest. The "Hi, Martha" and 1000-points strip at the top tells me they know who I am.
- The rest is a generic Women / Men / Kids grid. I'm shopping for a 5-year-old girl and a 9-year-old boy, and the Kids row is three tiny shoes: a black slip-on, a blue and orange runner, and a lavender slip-on. None of them says velcro, light-up, or durable. I'd tap in because it's the last day and 20% is real money, but I'd land on a generic kids page and have to hunt. I wouldn't forward it. I'd probably open it, check the kids shoes, and decide whether it's worth a cart on my phone tonight.

## What's working

- The 20% OFF is huge and readable on a phone, and the "SHOP NOW" button is clear.
- "LAST CHANCE" plus "Valid thru 10/11" is real urgency, unlike the vague "ends soon" in earlier sends.
- "Hi, Martha," the Member tag, and the 1000 points ("enough for a $5 reward") make me feel like a known customer.
- The Kids section is there, and the blue/orange sneaker is close to what my son wears.
- Klarna/Afterpay and the app download help with budgeting and fast mobile checkout.

## What's weak

- This is the same plaid "Fall Favorites" creative as the 10/8 and 10/9 sends. I'm tired of it, and it makes "last chance" feel less believable.
- Kids is the third section, after Women and Men, and each section is just three shoes with no names or prices. I can't tell what's velcro, light-up, or durable.
- Nothing is shown for a 5-year-old girl beyond one lavender slip-on, and no bundles or promo codes are mentioned.
- The fine print is tiny, and it excludes sale styles and "prior purchases." My points don't appear to stack with the 20%, and I can't tell without squinting.
- "Sign Up for Texts & Get 20% Off" is confusing when I'm already getting 20% off.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the shoe sections with Kids for parents, and label the shoes with details like "Hands-free velcro," "Light-ups," or "Reinforced toe," plus a price.
- 2. Say plainly whether 20% stacks with member points or the extra 10% from earlier sends, and make the exclusions larger and clearer.
- 3. Add a countdown or "Ends tonight at midnight PT" in the hero so the deadline reads in the first screen.
- 4. Retire the plaid creative after this much repetition.
- 5. Replace the generic text-signup tile with something like "Use your 1000 points on top."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed personalization failure (empty greeting, "Hi ,") and several markup and tracking-pixel defects. These include a protocol-less pixel URL, mixed-content `http://` images, and malformed `<meta>` tags. Link, header and compliance checks could not be completed from the truncated source.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Protocol-less pixel URL:** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…">` has no scheme. Clients resolve it as a relative path, so the Return Path (Validity) seed or inbox-placement pixel will not fire.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108404282&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NONPUR_U_SITE_NONPURCH_EN_10112026_A` is plain HTTP, so clients may block it and tracking data is lost. It also has no `alt` (QA WARN).
- **Hidden open pixel:** the primary SFMC open pixel (`click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx?…`) and four other pixels sit inside a `display:none !important` div. Some clients don't load images inside hidden containers, which can undercount opens.
- **Unverified links:** 41 tracking links went through click-redirect domains and were skipped by the HTTP probe (QA WARN). Their destinations are unverified.
- **Third-party beacons:** three `beacon.krxd.net` pixels are included: two `1x1_usermatch.gif` and one `1x1_ad_impression.gif`. They add third-party requests and failure points.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so neither declaration takes effect. The charset is then left to the HTTP/MIME headers.
- **Mixed content:** `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` is non-HTTPS and has no `alt` (QA WARN ×2). If the image is content, it may be blocked and screen readers get nothing.
- **Missing alt on pixels:** the `krxd` and `returnpath` `<img>` tags have no `alt` attribute. The impact is low because they are hidden.
- **No document language:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` or `xml:lang`.
- **Layout tables:** the layout tables in the visible source have no `role="presentation"`.
- **Plain-text part:** 4197 of 5991 characters are URLs (70%+), which is a spam-filter risk (QA WARN).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** the greeting renders as "Hi ," because the name field is empty. This is a broken experience.
- The merge token is probably resolving to a blank value with no fallback. The truncated source doesn't show the token, so I can't confirm the exact AMPscript or `%%FirstName%%` syntax.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Not verifiable:** the unsubscribe link, physical address, `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results are absent from the provided data. The footer is past the truncation point.
- **Data sharing:** `krxd` `partner_uid=adf27fee67323d52536b18110294bf793c265b830082caba4b1a32664832189a` is a 64-hex value, consistent with a SHA-256 of the recipient's email. It is sent to a third party alongside a subscriber ID (`122277199`). Confirm it is disclosed in the privacy policy.
- No confirmed CAN-SPAM violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

- **Click links:** because the 41 click links were skipped and the body is truncated, I can't confirm UTM parameters or landing-page alignment with the "20% off" offer.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Greeting:** add a fallback to the name token (for example `IIF(empty(@FirstName),"there",@FirstName)`) or drop the "Hi ," line when the name is blank.
2. **Pixel URL:** fix `pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif` to use `https://`.
3. **HTTP images:** move `ink1000.com/…/o.gif` and `image.emails.skechers.com/…png` to HTTPS, or remove the ink1000 pixel if it is redundant.
4. **Alt text:** add `alt=""` to the decorative and tracking pixels. Give the PNG meaningful alt text if it carries content.
5. **Meta tags:** change them to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=Edge" />`.
6. **Accessibility:** add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and `role="presentation"` to the layout tables.
7. **Plain text:** shorten or reduce the URLs in the plain-text part. Use shorter or fewer tracked links to bring the ratio below 70%.
8. **Follow-up audit:** run it with the full HTML and raw headers, which would cover:
   - the unsubscribe link and footer address;
   - `List-Unsubscribe` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results;
   - the UTM parameters on destination URLs.
