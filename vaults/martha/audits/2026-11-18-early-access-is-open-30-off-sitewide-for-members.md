# Early access is open! 30% off sitewide for members
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "30% off sitewide for members" is the first Skechers email this week that made me sit up. It's a real discount, and I'm already a member. "Hi, Martha," my 1000 points and the $5 reward strip are right at the top again, so they know who I am. The hero is huge and impossible to miss: "30% OFF SITEWIDE," with a black SHOP NOW button.
- Then it goes generic. Four tiles for Women, Men, Kids and Apparel. The Kids tile shows a boy in blue sneakers, which is close to what I'd buy for my 9-year-old, but nothing for my 5-year-old daughter. No velcro, no light-up soles, no sparkle. The "not stackable with other offers" line also worries me, because I've been counting on my $5 reward and the BOGO.
- I'd click, and I'd go straight to Kids. I wouldn't forward it, but I'd screenshot it for my sister-in-law because 30% off before Black Friday is worth knowing about. After the BOGO and the "last chance" email yesterday, I was waiting for a real deal like this.

## What's working

- The "30% OFF SITEWIDE" hero is big and clear. I understood the offer in one second on my phone.
- It's addressed to me: "Hi, Martha," Member, and 1000 points are right at the top. It feels like they know me.
- "Discount auto-applied at cart" means no promo code to hunt for.
- The Doorbuster section ("starting at $49.99") gives me a second reason to scroll.
- The Kids tile has a boy in navy sneakers, which is close to my son's style. Klarna and Afterpay are nice for a big gift haul.

## What's weak

- The Kids tile is one image and one button. Nothing is split into girls and boys, so I can't get to my daughter's sparkle and light-up shoes in one tap.
- The fine print is buried: "Valid online only thru 11/22. Not stackable with other offers. Exclusions apply." I want to know if my $5 reward and the BOGO still work.
- Women's and Men's use stock-photo models in jeans and bomber jackets. No shoes are visible, so it feels like a clothing ad.
- Doorbuster deals "starting at $49.99" is not cheap for kids' shoes, and no product is shown. Apparel also gets a whole tile when I'm shopping for shoes.
- This is the sixth Skechers email in a week. The countdown, BOGO, colors and "last chance" emails have all started to blur together.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split Kids into "Girls' (velcro, light-up, sparkle)" and "Boys' (lace-up, durable)." Show real shoes in both tiles. This is the biggest lift for me.
- 2. Put a plain-language line under the hero saying whether member rewards and BOGO stack. Don't hide it in the fine print.
- 3. Show one or two hero products with their member price, such as "Light-up sneakers now $34."
- 4. Add a clear countdown to 11/22 so the urgency feels real.
- 5. Replace the Apparel tile with a Family Bundle or Back-to-school-sized shoe pick.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "Early access is open! 30% off sitewide for members"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed broken-personalization defect (empty greeting) and several markup and tracking-pixel defects. Most compliance and continuity checks can't be completed because the HTML is truncated and the relay stripped the auth headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Scheme-less pixel URL (not in QA output):** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…" width="1" height="1">` has no `http(s)://`. It resolves as a relative path, so the request never reaches Return Path. Inbox-placement and read telemetry from this pixel is lost. It also has no `alt` attribute.
- **Insecure third-party pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108404282&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_SITESELECT_FULL_EN_11182026` uses plain HTTP. Clients such as Gmail proxy or block mixed content, so this pixel may not fire.
- **Hashed identifier in beacons:** The three `beacon.krxd.net` URLs (`partner=LprH63a7_sfmc_100007688_sha25`, `…_subid`, and `1x1_ad_impression.gif`) send `partner_uid=adf27fee67323d52536b18110294bf79…`. The `_sha25` partner name suggests this is a hashed email. Hidden `display:none` and `0x0` imgs in an email body are often stripped by clients. They also pass a hashed identifier to a third party, which the privacy policy and consent basis should cover.
- **Unprobed links:** 33 tracking links (the `click.emails.skechers.com` SFMC redirects) were skipped by QA. Destination validity is unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Ineffective charset and compat meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv`. Neither declaration takes effect, so charset falls back to the transport header.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom on mobile. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resize disabled:** `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }` stops iOS from scaling text.
- **Missing alt on `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`:** This image also loads over HTTP (`http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115…`). If it's content, it needs descriptive alt text and an HTTPS source.
- **QA false positive:** The QA flag for `o.gif` missing alt is wrong. The source shows `alt=""`, which is valid for a decorative tracking pixel. The HTTP-source warning for that image is still valid.
- **Layout tables:** The visible layout `<table>` elements have no `role="presentation"`, and `<html>` has no `lang` attribute. Screen readers may announce the tables as data tables and won't know the language.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** The greeting renders as `Hi ,`. The name merge field resolved to empty, and there's no fallback such as "Hi there,". This is a confirmed broken-experience defect.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication:** `Authentication-Results` was not found. QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a limit of the test setup, not a confirmed sender defect.
- **Unsubscribe link, physical address and `List-Unsubscribe` headers:** Not present in the truncated HTML or the provided headers. They are unverified, not confirmed missing.
- **From domain:** `no-reply@emails.skechers.com` is a no-reply sender. This is a minor concern for reply handling.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** Not verifiable. All 33 links are SFMC redirects (`click.emails.skechers.com/?qs=…`), and the final URLs weren't resolved.
- **Campaign identifier:** `PRO_US_NA_U_SITESELECT_FULL_EN_11182026` appears in the pixel URLs. Whether it's carried into the landing-page parameters is unknown.
- **Landing page alignment:** Unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a fallback to the first-name merge field (for example, AMPscript `IIF(Empty(@firstname),"there",@firstname)`), or drop the greeting when the field is empty.
2. Fix the Return Path pixel to `https://pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=…`.
3. Move `ink1000.com/o.gif` and `image.emails.skechers.com/…dde00662….png` to HTTPS. Add meaningful alt text to the PNG if it carries content.
4. Change the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`. Add `lang="en"` and `role="presentation"` on layout tables.
6. Confirm that sharing the hashed identifier with `beacon.krxd.net` is covered by consent and the privacy policy.
7. Re-run QA with the full HTML and original headers (direct SMTP capture, or an inbox that preserves `Authentication-Results`). That would verify the unsubscribe mechanism, `List-Unsubscribe`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, link destinations and UTM carry-through.
