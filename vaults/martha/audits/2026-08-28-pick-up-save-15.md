# ✨ Pick Up & Save 15%
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-28**
## Executive summary

- Same confetti-dot Sally Beauty banner, same everything — I could pick this layout out of a police lineup at this point. This is at least the seventh one this month, and my thumb is already moving toward "swipe" before my eyes finish loading the image. The one thing that actually stopped my scroll for half a second was "Buy Online, Pick Up in Store" — that's an offer I could theoretically use since there's a Sally near my kids' pediatrician — but 15% off hair color and nail products I don't buy isn't enough to pull me back in.
- The "Shop Twice in August, Earn a $5 Reward" banner is the closest thing to something that'd make me open the app, except I'm not even sure I have a rewards account with them, and I'm not going to dig for it mid-Saturday with a 5-year-old climbing on me.
- Not clicking, not forwarding. This one gets the same treatment as the last six: read the subject, glance at the pink circle, archive.

## What's working

- The BOPIS angle ("No minimum purchase required") is at least a new offer shape compared to the usual flat-percent-off blitz.
- Clear deadline ("Ends 08/31") gives me a real reason to act if I cared, instead of vague urgency.
- Klarna's "4 interest-free payments" line is a genuinely useful data point for a budget-conscious mom, even if it's buried below the fold.

## What's weak

- I don't do my own hair color, nails, or styling — this entire category grid (Hair Color, Hair Care, Nails, Styling Tools, Cosmetics) is a hard pass for me every single time.
- Same confetti-dot visual signature as literally every Sally send I've reviewed this month — zero creative refresh, so my brain has fully tuned it out.
- No kids' anything, obviously not their lane, but that also means this brand has nothing in its toolkit to earn a click from someone like me.
- The rewards banner ("Earn a $5 Reward") assumes I already have an account and know my status — no personalization telling me if I'm enrolled or how close I am.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending me the identical confetti banner every few days — even if I were a hair-color shopper, creative fatigue would be killing conversion by now.
- 2. Lead the subject line with the BOPIS/pickup angle since that's the actual news here, not another generic "% off" tease.
- 3. Personalize the rewards block — tell me my actual point balance or how many purchases I'm away from the $5 reward instead of a blanket banner.
- 4. If this brand wants overlap with moms like me, they'd need actual kids'-adjacent content (gift sets, family bundles) — right now there's zero bridge into my world.
