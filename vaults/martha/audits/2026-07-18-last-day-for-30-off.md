# Last Day for 30% Off 💖
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-18**
## Executive summary

- Sally again. That's literally my first thought — I still have yesterday's ion Luxe email sitting unread, and now I've got another one. But I'll be honest: "Last Day for 30% Off" actually stopped my thumb. Eight days ago they sent "Last Day for 20% Off" and I remember thinking the offer wasn't quite enough to bother — now it's jumped to 30%, and that escalation registered. The hero is clean and punchy: pink background, big bold type, "Hair color that cares." I got the offer in about two seconds.
- The catch is this email is entirely built around Overtone — color-depositing masks in Scarlet Red and Royal Plum. Those are products for someone maintaining a vivid fashion shade. That's not me right now. The "Trending Now" grid does throw in some mainstream hair color (ion, L'Oreal), but it also somehow includes nail glue, which feels like an algorithm throwing darts. The whole hero dead-ends for anyone who isn't already an Overtone buyer.
- I'd mark this as read and scroll on. Strong offer, wrong product hero for my life. Not a click, not a forward.

## What's working

- **30% off is real money** — the jump from last week's 20% off actually reads as a genuine escalation; my price-sensitive brain noticed and appreciated it
- **Hero is clean and mobile-friendly** — "LAST DAY" in big type, offer visible above the fold, clear "Shop now" button, no hunting required on a small screen
- **Deadline urgency feels credible** — "Ends today" paired with "LAST DAY" is redundant but convincing; I believe it
- **No render bugs** — email loaded cleanly, images are sharp, layout held together on my phone
- **"\*Exclusions apply" is disclosed upfront** — I appreciate seeing it at the bottom of the hero, not buried three scrolls down

## What's weak

- **Overtone is too niche for me** — I'm not maintaining a vivid hair color, so Scarlet Red and Royal Plum color depositing masks just aren't in my world right now; the entire hero is wasted on me
- **"Trending Now" grid feels random** — ion permanent hair color, L'Oreal hair color… then suddenly nail glue? It reads like an algorithm ran out of on-brand products and grabbed whatever was left
- **Zero segmentation signal** — they have no idea who I am; a practical mom with brown hair and no history of buying Overtone shouldn't be getting this exact send
- **Cadence fatigue is real** — by my count this is at least the seventh email from Sally in nine days; the 30% offer is strong enough to partially cut through, but I'm still a little numb

## Recommendations

- 1. **Segment Overtone sends to vivid-hair buyers** — anyone who hasn't purchased or browsed Overtone should get a different hero entirely; the product is beautiful but self-selecting
- 2. **Add a fallback offer for everyone else** — something like "30% off across color, care, and tools" so subscribers who don't have fashion-colored hair aren't looking at an email that has nothing for them
- 3. **Fix the "Trending Now" grid** — nail glue in a hair color email is a distraction; keep the theme tight or at minimum show me things the algorithm thinks I'd actually buy
- 4. **Make the CTA earn its spot** — "Shop now" is the minimum viable button; "Shop 30% Off Overtone" or even "Get 30% Off Today" keeps the offer present at the click point
- 5. **Batch or space the cadence** — this offer and yesterday's ion Luxe send could have been one email; two in two days for different Sally brands is fatigue territory
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% Off Overtone — Ends Tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 30% Off Ends Today — Shop Overtone`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Color Depositing Masks are on sale — this one's worth it`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Overtone 30% off, ends tonight. Trending picks too.`
