# Ends tomorrow: Save up to $20 ⏳
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-16**
## Executive summary

- Sally Beauty, again. That's basically every one of my kids-shoe-browsing Sunday sessions lately — I open my inbox looking for something for the kids and there's Sally Beauty's confetti graphic staring at me for the eighth time this cycle. This one at least has a real code (COLOR20), a real deadline ("ends tomorrow"), and a tidy $10/$50 or $20/$75 offer, so it's not the worst version of this email I've gotten. But hair color and nail polish just aren't on my radar as a mom shopping for a 5-year-old's light-up shoes and a 9-year-old's sneakers.
- The layout is clean and I can read it fine on my phone without zooming, but nothing here is for me right now. I'm not clicking, and I'm definitely not forwarding it — I'd forward a shoe deal to my sister in a heartbeat, not a hair dye sale.
- Gut check: mark as read, keep scrolling for something actually relevant.

## What's working

- The "ONE DAY LEFT" urgency plus "Ends tomorrow" in the code callout is clear and creates real pressure, not fake scarcity.
- The offer math is dead simple — $10 off $50 or $20 off $75 with one code, no mental gymnastics.
- Product grid ("Shop Your Favorites") is organized and scannable, real photos not stock art.
- The rewards banner (shop twice, earn $5) is a nice loyalty touch if I were actually a Sally regular.
- No visual bugs — everything renders cleanly, buttons are tappable-sized.

## What's weak

- I'm not their target customer for this category at all — nothing here speaks to a mom buying kids' shoes.
- This is yet another Sally send in what's felt like a nonstop stream over the past two weeks — same confetti dots, same energy as five other emails already in my inbox.
- The fine print block at the bottom is huge — feels like it's compensating for how aggressive the "ends tomorrow" urgency is up top.
- Nothing personalized to me beyond my email showing up in the terms — no "hey Martha" or anything that reads as recognizing me as a specific shopper.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut send frequency — I'm getting hit with near-identical COLORfest/rewards emails multiple times a week and I'm tuning all of them out now.
- 2. If this list includes lapsed or low-engagement subscribers like me, test a "Free Shipping" or app-download hook instead of leading with hair color specifically — broaden the entry point.
- 3. Move the rewards program teaser ("$5 Reward in September") higher — that's actually a stronger loyalty hook than another percent-off code.
- 4. Trim the legal/terms block visually — it's dominating the bottom third of the email and reads like clutter on mobile.
