# 30% off early access is almost up ⏱️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the fifth 30% off member email since Tuesday, and at least this time the sale is the hero. "LAST CHANCE!" and a huge "30% OFF SITEWIDE" fill the top of the screen, so I get it in two seconds. I'm a member, and "Hi, Martha" with my 1000 points and the $5 reward strip sit right at the top again. That part works.
- The rest is all grown-ups. A woman in Slip-ins, a man in a hoodie, a pile of tan women's sneakers, and a black performance shoe. Nothing for a 5-year-old who wants sparkles and light-ups, and nothing for my 9-year-old who destroys the toes of every sneaker. "KIDS" is just a word in a list at the bottom. The sizes are women's and men's only.
- Would I click? Yes, probably, because 30% off plus a deadline is real and I'd go straight to Kids. Would I forward it? No. It's the same deal I've seen all week, and the only thing new is that today is the last day.

## What's working

- "LAST CHANCE!" and "30% OFF SITEWIDE" are huge and I can't miss them. Black Friday timing makes the urgency believable.
- "Hi, Martha," my 1000 points and the $5 redeem strip tell me they know I'm a member.
- "Discount auto-applied at cart" means no promo code to hunt for.
- The $49.99 doorbusters block gives me a concrete price to react to.
- The "Valid online only thru 11/22" line makes the deadline clear.

## What's weak

- Nothing for kids anywhere. No girls' velcro or light-ups, no boys' lace-up sneakers, and "KIDS" is a plain text link near the bottom.
- The size grids are women's and men's only. I'm shopping for a 5-year-old and a 9-year-old.
- Four category tiles, all adult. "Court & Classics" is a pile of women's shoes I'd never buy first.
- I can't tell which items the "Exclusions apply" fine print covers until I scroll to the end, and then it's tiny.
- This is the fifth near-identical 30% off send in six days, after "Hands-free & holiday ready" and "Stay in. Step out. Stay cozy." The urgency is starting to feel manufactured.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a kids tile above the fold, for example "Kids' Light-Ups & Sneakers: 30% off today." For members with kid purchase history, make that the hero.
- 2. Add kids' sizes to the size grid (Little Kids, Big Kids), or swap the grid by segment.
- 3. Put "Shop Kids" in the top nav strip, not just at the bottom.
- 4. Show a points or stackable-reward angle, such as "Use your $5 reward on top." I'm loyal to rewards, so that would get me to click.
- 5. State the biggest exclusions next to the CTA, not only in the footer.
- 6. Cut the cadence. One early-access send plus one last-chance send is plenty.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "30% off early access is almost up ⏱️"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed broken-experience defect, an empty greeting that renders "Hi ,". It also has several tracking-pixel problems: a scheme-less pixel URL, HTTP-only images and missing alt text. The HTML supplied was truncated, so the body links, footer and headers could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Scheme-less pixel URL.** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1dbf...">` has no `http(s)://`. Clients will treat it as a relative path, so it likely fails to load and the open or inbox-placement measurement is lost. It also has no `alt`.
- **Insecure tracking pixel.** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108404282&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_SITESELECT_FULL_EN_11222026` uses plain HTTP. Gmail's image proxy and Apple Mail Privacy Protection may block or rewrite it, and an `http://` request can leak the `mi_u` identifier unencrypted.
- **Third-party beacons.** Three `beacon.krxd.net` pixels are present. Two are `1x1_usermatch.gif` with `partner_uid=adf27fee...189a` (a 64-hex value, apparently a SHA-256 hash, labelled `..._sha25`) and `partner_uid=122277199` (labelled `..._subid`). The third is `1x1_ad_impression.gif`. These share a recipient identifier with an ad-data platform, which is a privacy and disclosure concern.
- **Hidden-pixel markup.** Several of these pixels use `width="0" height="0"`, and the Krux pixels have no `alt`.
- **Unverified links.** 44 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe (click-redirect domains), so click-through validity is unconfirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text** (QA-confirmed):
  - `o.gif` (ink1000.com)
  - `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`, which is a content image and not a pixel
- **HTTP image source.** `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-...png` is non-HTTPS and may be blocked or show as a broken image. The same host likely serves other images over HTTPS, so this is inconsistent.
- **Charset and compatibility meta tags are ineffective.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` lack `http-equiv`, so neither is a valid declaration. Without a header-level charset, non-ASCII characters such as `®` and `’` risk mis-rendering.
- **Zoom blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="..., maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Focus outline removed.** The `a { outline: none; }` rule removes keyboard focus indicators.
- **Layout tables.** The tables visible in the snippet (`cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0" border="0"`) have no `role="presentation"`, so screen readers may announce them as data tables.
- **Hidden preheader.** `div.preheader` uses `font-size:1px; display:none !important`. Some clients ignore `display:none`, which could expose the text.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot.** The greeting renders as "Hi ," because the first-name field resolved to empty. The merge field needs a fallback value, or the greeting should be suppressed when the name is blank. The greeting is outside the truncated HTML, so this rests on the QA check.
- The `_sfmc_` and `mi_u` identifiers resolved to values, with no unresolved `%%…%%` tokens visible in the snippet.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] Authentication-Results header not found.** This is expected via the AgentMail relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. It is a test-harness limitation and not evidence of a sender defect.
- **Unverified items.** The footer, physical postal address, unsubscribe link and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are not in the truncated source or QA data, so they could not be assessed.
- The From domain `emails.skechers.com` is a consistent sending subdomain. Click and image hosts share it (`click.`, `image.`), which is favorable for alignment.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The campaign identifier `PRO_US_NA_U_SITESELECT_FULL_EN_11222026` appears in the Krux and ink1000 pixels, so tracking is campaign-tagged.
- The CTA destination URLs and UTM parameters sit in the truncated body or behind the 44 skipped redirects. Landing page alignment and UTM presence could not be verified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a fallback for the first-name merge field (for example `Hi there,`) or suppress the greeting when the name is empty. This is the only confirmed user-facing break.
2. Fix `pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif` to use `https://`, or remove it if it is redundant.
3. Move `ink1000.com/.../o.gif` and `image.emails.skechers.com/.../dde00662-...png` to HTTPS.
4. Add descriptive `alt` text to the content PNG. Use `alt=""` on every tracking pixel.
5. Correct the meta tags: `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`. Drop `maximum-scale=1`.
6. Remove `outline: none` from links, or provide a visible `:focus` style. Add `role="presentation"` to layout tables.
7. Review the Krux user-match pixels against the privacy policy and consent basis, because they transmit a hashed identifier.
8. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and raw headers. That would let you check the footer and unsubscribe link, `List-Unsubscribe`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment, and the destination URLs and UTM parameters on the redirected links.
