# Find your perfect wide fit
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I'll say it: I got the Saturday-scrolling equivalent of a shrug. The top of this email is good. It greets me by name, shows my 1000 points with the "$5 reward, REDEEM" line, and has a big "15% off for guests / 20% for members" banner. That's the loyalty stacking I like, and it echoes yesterday's Fall Haul email. Then the hero shows one woman and one man in adult wide-fit shoes, and I lost interest. Wide fit isn't my problem. It isn't my kids' problem either, and they're who I'm shopping for.
- Below the hero it's a tidy grid of boots and slippers, everyday styles and a "Top Rated" Glide-Step Pro. There's nothing sparkly, nothing with velcro, nothing for a 5-year-old girl and nothing sturdy for a 9-year-old boy. KIDS is a plain text link in the gray "Shop Additional Styles" list, sitting between MEN and WIDE. That's the same buried-kids problem as the "Colors we're falling for" send on 9/17. And it's the second wide-fit email in about two weeks, after "Extra room. Same iconic look" on 9/16.
- Would I click? Maybe, but only to tap the 20% banner and go looking for kids' shoes myself. I wouldn't forward it. I'd probably leave it unread until tonight, when I remember the member discount is ending.

## What's working

- I'm greeted by name, and the 1000 points with the "$5 reward, REDEEM" line show they know I'm a member.
- The 15% guest / 20% member split is big and easy to read. It rewards me for being in Plus, and the "Thru 10/1" deadline feels real.
- The "SHOP NOW" text sits right next to the discount, so the deal and the button are together.
- The Glide-Step Pro five-star quote ("The shoes fit great and feel comfortable") gives me social proof, and comfort is what I care about for long days on my feet.
- The two full-width navy "SHOP NEW WIDE FIT" and "SHOP WIDE FIT BEST SELLERS" buttons are easy to tap on my phone.
- Afterpay and the app download are there for a fast mobile checkout.

## What's weak

- The hero is an adult man and woman with no kids anywhere. Nothing tells me this was made for a mom shopping for a 5-year-old girl and a 9-year-old boy.
- KIDS is a plain text link, buried in the gray list below the product blocks.
- Wide fit is a niche topic, and this is my second wide-fit email in two weeks. The subject doesn't give me a reason to care about it, and I don't see how it relates to my kids.
- The hero copy "Room to move, made for you" is in small, light text on a busy photo. On my phone I had to squint to read it.
- The 20% off is fine print in disguise. The offer only applies to regular-priced items, and the sale exclusions are in tiny legal text at the bottom.
- The boots and slippers, and the taupe and brown palette, feel like a grown-up (maybe older) shopper's email. There's no sparkle and no bold colors.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment the send. If someone has shopped kids' styles, lead with a kids' hero. That could be a pair of light-up sneakers for a girl next to durable black lace-ups for a boy, with a "Wide fit for growing feet" angle if they want to keep the theme. That's the single change that would lift this email most for me.
- 2. Move a KIDS button up next to the two wide-fit buttons, or make it a tile in the grid. Don't leave it as a text link at the bottom.
- 3. Put the member offer in the hero as its own button, like "Take 20% off with Plus". That way I don't have to hunt for it, and it would sit right next to the wide-fit call to action.
- 4. Make the hero headline bigger and darker so it reads on a phone without zooming.
- 5. Add a promo-code line or a family bundle, like "Buy 2 pairs for the kids and save". That would get my attention right away.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed personalization failure (empty greeting, "Hi ,") and several code-level defects: a scheme-less tracking pixel, two `http://` images without alt text, and malformed `<meta>` tags. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and header checks could not be done.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed pixel URL:** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…">` has no `https://` scheme. Clients resolve it as a relative path, so it won't load. The deliverability or seed-tracking pixel is lost.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108404282&mi_ecmp=MKG_US_NA_U_CATEGORY_WIDE_EN_09302026` is served over plain HTTP. Clients may block it or rewrite it.
- **Third-party beacons:** three `beacon.krxd.net` pixels (`1x1_usermatch.gif` ×2, `1x1_ad_impression.gif`) sit in a `display:none` div. One `usermatch` call passes `partner_uid=adf27fee…189a`, a 64-hex value consistent with a SHA-256 hash of the recipient's email. That is a third-party data-sync call to Krux/Salesforce DMP from inside the email.
- **Unprobed links:** 32 tracking links were skipped by QA (click-redirect domains, e.g. `click.emails.skechers.com`). Their destinations and status are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):** `o.gif` (ink1000) has no `alt`. `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` has no `alt`.
- **Missing alt (source):** the two `krxd` `usermatch` pixels, the `ad_impression` pixel and the `returnpath` pixel have no `alt`. The SFMC open pixel does have `alt=""`.
- **HTTP image:** `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-…png` is non-HTTPS (QA-confirmed). Since the same host serves other assets, it likely supports HTTPS.
- **Malformed meta tags:**
  - `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv`, so the charset declaration is ineffective.
  - `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` also has no `http-equiv`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4). `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none }` also blocks iOS text scaling.
- **Focus outline removed:** `a { outline: none; text-decoration: none; }` removes the keyboard focus indicator.
- **Missing document semantics:** `<html xmlns=…>` has no `lang` attribute. The layout tables visible in the source (`<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0" border="0">`, `<table class="widthFull" width="640">`) have no `role="presentation"`.
- **Outdated markup:** the XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype is standard for email and not an issue in itself.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** the greeting renders as `"Hi ,"` because the first-name field was empty. No fallback value is set in the template or data. Rendered merge tokens are not visible in the truncated source, so the exact AMPscript or field name is unconfirmed.
- No unresolved `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` tokens were seen in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Not verifiable:** the truncated source cuts off before the footer, so the unsubscribe link, physical postal address and preference-center link could not be checked.
- **Headers not provided:** SPF, DKIM, DMARC and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` values were not supplied.
- **From address:** `no-reply@emails.skechers.com` is the observed sender. This is not a violation, but confirm that a working reply or unsubscribe path exists elsewhere.
- **Hashed email in beacons:** the `usermatch` calls to a third party (see section 2) may need to be covered by the privacy policy and consent language. This is a disclosure question, not a confirmed defect.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not verifiable:** all visible links are ESP click-redirects and QA skipped them, so final destinations and UTM parameters are unknown.
- **Campaign ID:** the campaign identifier `MKG_US_NA_U_CATEGORY_WIDE_EN_09302026` appears only in the `krxd` and `ink1000` pixel URLs. Whether it maps to `utm_campaign` on the landing pages could not be confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the greeting fallback** with a default such as "Hi there," or drop the name when it is empty (highest priority, QA-confirmed).
2. **Add `https://`** to the `pixel.app.returnpath.net` pixel, or remove it if the service is unused.
3. **Move `www.ink1000.com` and `image.emails.skechers.com` assets to HTTPS.**
4. **Add alt text:**
   - Descriptive `alt` on `dde00662-…png`, if it is a content image.
   - `alt=""` on all tracking pixels (`o.gif` and the `krxd` and `returnpath` pixels).
5. **Fix the meta tags:** use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge" />`.
6. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta and replace `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` with `100%`.
7. **Restore focus styling** on links, i.e. drop the `outline: none` reset or add a `:focus` style.
8. **Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and `role="presentation"` to layout tables.**
9. **Re-run the audit on the full HTML and headers** to check the unsubscribe link, postal address, `List-Unsubscribe`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC and UTM parameters on the resolved click-redirect destinations.
10. **Review the `krxd` hashed-email beacons** with privacy and legal.
