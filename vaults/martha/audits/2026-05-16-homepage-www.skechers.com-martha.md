## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF banner at the absolute top** — as someone buying shoes for two kids and possibly myself, that deal is genuinely exciting and it's the first thing I read
- **Spring Apparel Sale strip** — a second offer visible without scrolling makes the page feel like it rewards staying
- **"Kids'" tab in the navigation rail** — one tap and I'm where I actually need to be; that saves real time
- **"Recently Viewed" tab** — I've been here before and that shortcut means I don't have to remember where I was
- **Full-width "SHOP NOW" button** — big, thumb-friendly, no guessing what to tap on mobile

## What's weak

- **Hero imagery doesn't speak to a family shopper** — a woman in a floral sundress by a pool and a guy on steps reads "vacation" to me, not "durable everyday shoes for a 9-year-old boy"
- **No loyalty callout** — I know Skechers has a rewards program and BOGO is great, but I want to know my points balance or see that members get something extra
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA is destination-ambiguous** — for a mom with a focused mission, clicking a generic button that could drop me anywhere feels like friction before I've even started
- **Zero trust signals** — no "Free returns," no review count, no shipping threshold mentioned anywhere in the first screen

## Recommendations

- **Split the CTA** — put "Shop Kids'" and "Shop Women's" side by side below the hero so I can skip directly to my actual goal instead of hunting after tapping SHOP NOW
- **Add loyalty copy to the promo banner** — "Skechers+ members earn double points on BOGO" would activate me immediately as a returning shopper who tracks rewards
- **Swap one hero panel to a family or kids lifestyle shot** — a kid sprinting on a playground or a mom and child would make me feel like this page was designed for someone like me, not just people going on summer dates
- **Add free shipping threshold to the offer strip** — even "Free shipping over $45" would lower my anxiety before I've added anything to cart
