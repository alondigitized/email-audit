## Executive summary

- Extra 20% off with a code, "hi Martha," my 1000 points sitting right at the top — fine, I've seen that beat every day this week and it's still the same handshake. But then it's a guy in a sweater vest on a stool, followed by Women's Markdowns, then Men's Markdowns. Nowhere — not a single sneaker, not a single row — is there a Kids section. I have a 5-year-old and a 9-year-old and this is the literal reason I open Skechers emails on a Sunday.
- The women's shoes are actually kind of cute (that pink mesh Bobs pair caught my eye for half a second), and $44.99/$48.99 with visible strikethroughs is a real, honest offer. But I'm not buying myself shoes this week, I'm buying my son's next size up in lace-ups before school pictures. This is a scroll-and-close, not a forward.

- "Hi, Martha" + my 1000 points + "you have enough for a $5 reward" up top feels like they know I'm a member, even if it's the same open every time.
- EXTRA 20% OFF is huge, bold, and impossible to miss — no hunting for the offer.
- Actual strikethrough prices ($60→$44.99, $85→$63.99) on real product photos, not vague "up to" language.
- Clean sectioning — Women's Markdowns then Men's Markdowns, each with its own CTA button — so I know exactly where to tap.
- "Valid thru 9/14" gives me a real deadline, not fake urgency.

## What's weak

- Zero kids' shoes. Not one velcro sneaker, not one light-up sole, nothing pink/purple/sparkly for my daughter or a durable lace-up for my son — this is the exact gap I keep hitting with Skechers emails.
- Hero model is a grown man in a sweater vest and slacks — nothing about that says "mom shopping for her kids" or even "mom shopping for herself."
- "Shop Additional Styles" list at the bottom does have a "KIDS" link, but it's buried as plain gray text way after two full product-and-CTA blocks I have to scroll past first.
- This is basically the same "20% off for Plus members" structure as 9/5's "All-day comfort, now on sale" and 9/2's "New season. New arrivals" — I'm losing count of how many times this exact template has landed this week.

- 2. Swap or add a hero shot with kids' shoes in frame, or at minimum a mom-and-kid lifestyle image instead of a solo menswear model.
- 3. Move the "KIDS" link out of the gray footer nav and give it its own visible button like Women's/Men's got.
- 4. Vary the subject line — three days running of "20% off / member savings" language is starting to blur together for me.
