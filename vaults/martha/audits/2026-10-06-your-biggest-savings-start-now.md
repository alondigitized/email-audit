# Your biggest savings start now
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- Sally Beauty again. Another "big savings" email on top of the Appiversary run, the "Spooky season savings" send and Sunday's "Your beauty favorites, 20% off 👏". My inbox is turning into a Sally billboard. This one is clean and easy to scan, but it isn't for me. It's Arctic Fox dye, dip powders and flat irons, and nothing in it speaks to a mom shopping for a 5-year-old and a 9-year-old.
- The "Big Deals" banner and the black "Shop now" button are hard to miss. The offers are real too: BOGO, BOGO 50%, 35% off, 40% off. But there's no single hero deal, so the email feels like four small ads stacked together. I'd skim it on my phone in about five seconds.
- Would I click? Probably not. Maybe the Ion hair dryer at 40% off if I were treating myself, and I shop for myself last. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- The big "Big Deals" banner with a black "Shop now" button is easy to read on my phone, with no zooming.
- The offers are concrete and bold: Buy one get one free, Buy one get one 50% off, 35% off, 40% off. I know the discount right away.
- The pink Sally Rewards strip at the top ($5 off $25 when you sign up) is a loyalty hook I like.
- Each deal has its own headline, product photos and "Shop now" link, so the layout is tidy and nothing looks broken.
- The Klein/Klarna row and the icon row (free shipping on $40+, 2-hour delivery, pick up in store) are useful for planning a quick order.

## What's weak

- Nothing here is for my family. No kids, no school or back-to-fall angle, and no sign it knows who I am.
- There's no hero deal. Four offers compete, and the "Big Deals" banner doesn't say which one is the big one.
- "Time's running out" has no date. The real expiration (10/31/2026) is buried in the fine print at the bottom, so the urgency isn't credible.
- Four stacked "Shop now" links with the same wording make me wonder which one matters.
- This is yet another Sally send in a very short window. After the 9/30 "Last chance" email and the 10/1 "Spooky season savings," "Your biggest savings start now" doesn't feel like news.
- The legal fine print at the bottom is long and dense, which makes me wonder about exclusions.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero offer, such as the 40% off Ion dryer or BOGO color, and put a date on it. "Ends Sunday" gives me a reason to act now.
- 2. Segment by purchase history. A Sally customer who has bought kids' hair accessories or detangler should see those products, not dip powder.
- 3. Make the CTA copy specific, like "Shop BOGO color," instead of four identical "Shop now" buttons.
- 4. Show the rewards benefit next to each offer, such as "Rewards members save extra," instead of only in the top strip.
- 5. Space out the sends. One strong email a week beats a daily "last chance."

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Rewards members get an extra $5 off $25. Deals end 10/31.`

# Technical Audit: Sally Beauty, "Your biggest savings start now"

## 1. Technical Summary
The provided source and QA data show no hard failures. Three third-party tracking pixels have problems: one has an unsubstituted `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro, one loads over plain HTTP, and one has no alt attribute. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe, and most link checks could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **58 tracking links were never probed.** QA skipped the HTTP probe for click-redirect domains, so none of them are verified as resolving or landing correctly.
- **Unsubstituted macro in an impression pixel.** `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&ao=883…` was sent with a literal `[CACHEBUSTER]` in the `r=` parameter. Most ad servers use that value to defeat caching and dedupe impressions. Sent unresolved, it can cause cached or undercounted impressions.
- **HTTP pixel.** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990…` is not HTTPS. Gmail's image proxy typically rewrites it, but some clients block it or show mixed-content warnings. The Dotomi sync pixel may therefore fail to fire.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Four images have no alt text.** All four are tracking pixels (an empty-name image, `pxl.mon-trk.com/…/003aZ00000s0I5yQAE`, `myvisualiq` and `dotomi`). Confirm they carry `alt=""` and are hidden, for example `width="1" height="1"` or `aria-hidden`. Otherwise screen readers may announce the URLs.
- **Malformed `<meta>` tags.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=edge" />` both lack `http-equiv`. The charset declaration and the IE compatibility directive are therefore inert. The `Content-Type` header probably covers charset, but the tags are invalid as written.
- **Unloaded font.** The `a{}` rule specifies `'Founders Grotesk'`, but no `@font-face` for it appears in the visible CSS. It will always fall back to Helvetica. The Open Sans `@font-face` rules look fine.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Unresolved placeholder.** `[CACHEBUSTER]` in the myvisualiq URL is a literal token that was not replaced (see Section 2).
- **Recipient identifier in a pixel URL.** `003aZ00000s0I5yQAE` looks like a Salesforce Contact ID and is likely the intended per-recipient token in the `pxl.mon-trk.com` pixel. It resolved correctly.
- **Visible text.** No other visible tokens were found, but the source was truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
**Not verifiable.** The truncated HTML shows no footer, unsubscribe link, or physical address. No headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF, DKIM, DMARC) were supplied. The From domain `em.sallybeauty.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, but its authentication cannot be confirmed from this data. No issues are asserted.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
**Not verifiable.** Landing pages sit behind the 58 skipped tracking redirects, and the visible source contains no resolved destination URLs or UTM parameters.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the macro.** Make the ESP or ad platform substitute `[CACHEBUSTER]` at send or open time. Otherwise remove the `r=` dependency.
2. **Upgrade the Dotomi pixel.** Serve it over `https://`, and check that `login.dotomi.com` supports TLS.
3. **Add `alt=""` and zero dimensions** to all tracking pixels.
4. **Correct the meta tags:** `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge" />`.
5. **Re-run QA on the full HTML.** Resolve the tracking redirects with a GET and follow them to the final URL. Then verify the UTMs, the unsubscribe and `List-Unsubscribe` headers, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
6. **Treat the "100% pass rate, 0 warnings" summary as unreliable.** It conflicts with the five `[WARN]` entries and the 58 skipped links.
