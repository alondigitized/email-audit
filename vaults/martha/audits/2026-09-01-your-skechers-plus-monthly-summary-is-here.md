# Your Skechers Plus Monthly Summary is Here
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-01**
## Executive summary

- Okay, my monthly points summary — 1000 points, $5 reward earned, that's exactly the loyalty-program stuff that actually keeps me engaged, so props for leading with it. But then it slides straight into "Member Exclusive: 20% off select regular priced styles" with a generic six-shoe grid that's clearly a mixed bag of men's/women's/running styles, not a single kid sneaker in sight, and "We picked these just for you" when nothing about this looks picked for a mom buying for a 5- and 9-year-old.
- It's not a bad email exactly — it's honest, it's not selling me anything shady, and the points/reward math actually feels transactional and real for once instead of another generic sitewide pitch. But it's also not for me in the moment I'd open it, which is hunting for kids' shoes on a Saturday. I'd glance at the $5 reward, maybe tap "View My Account" out of curiosity about the balance, then bounce before I ever hit "Shop Now."
- Wouldn't forward it to a friend — there's nothing shareable here, it's just my account statement dressed up with a sale banner bolted on.

## What's working

- The points balance (1000) and $5 reward front and center feels like real account data, not marketing fluff — I trust it more than the last few "Hi, Martha" sends.
- 20% off member-exclusive with no code required and a real end date (9/7) is low-friction — I don't have to remember UPGRADE again.
- "View My Account" CTA matches the actual content of a monthly summary email — makes sense as a first action.
- Payment/perk footer (Afterpay, Klarna, curbside pickup, free shipping) is useful context even if I skim past it.

## What's weak

- Zero kids' product representation anywhere — "we picked these just for you" is six adult sneakers, no velcro, no light-up soles, nothing for my daughter or son.
- "Kids" is buried as one text link in a six-item list at the bottom, same visual weight as "Clothing" or "New Arrivals" — no effort to route me there.
- The hero banner is mostly a giant "1000" and stats I already half-know, real estate that could've shown me something relevant while it had my attention.
- This is the fifth or sixth Skechers email this week and it's yet another 20% off deal layered on top of yesterday's "LAST CHANCE" 20% off — the cadence is genuinely fatiguing at this point.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap the "we picked these just for you" grid for actual personalization — if I've browsed or bought kids' shoes before, show me kids' shoes, not a random adult assortment.
- 2. Elevate "Kids" out of the plain text list into its own visual tile/button near the top, since that's the category I'm actually here for.
- 3. Add real family-shopping content: family bundle callout or a kids-specific promo code, not just the generic member 20%.
- 4. Tighten the subject/preheader — the current preview is literally leaking raw JSON schema markup, which looks broken in the inbox.

- **Subject Alt B:** `1000 points + 20% off, just for you`
