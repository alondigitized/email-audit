## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email is XHTML 1.0 Transitional with media queries at max-width 499px. QA reports 86% pass, 0 errors and 1 warning. The confirmed issues are in the third-party tracking pixels and the `<head>` markup. The HTML is truncated, so the body, footer and header-level compliance data can't be verified.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 52 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, so none of their destinations were probed. Redirect chains, 404s and expired links are unverified.
- An unresolved macro appears in a pixel URL: `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&ao=883&aca=-11&si=-11&ci=-11&pi=-11&ad=-11&advt=-…`. The literal `[CACHEBUSTER]` was never substituted. The `-11` values look like unset placeholders, so this impression pixel is probably cacheable or recording bad data.
- Mixed content: `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&…` is loaded over HTTP. Clients may block it, which means the Dotomi sync pixel won't fire.
- Two image slots have an empty `src` (QA: `Image missing alt text: : src: `). They are either dead tags or broken output from the template, and they should be removed.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv`, so it declares nothing. `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has the same problem. Encoding then depends on the `Content-Type` header alone, which matters because the subject and likely the body contain emoji.
- `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` attribute, so screen readers can't pick the right language.
- Four tracking pixels have no `alt` attribute: `pxl.mon-trk.com/987bfe61-…/003aZ00000s0I5yQAE`, `impression_pixel`, `UCMController`, and the first empty-src image. These should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`. I can't confirm from the truncated source whether they do.
- No issues found with the viewport meta tag or the `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` tag.
- The `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` override is present and correct.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- Confirmed issue: the unreplaced `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro described above.
- The Monetate tracking pixel path ends in `003aZ00000s0I5yQAE`, which is a Salesforce contact ID (`003` prefix). Its alt text is that same ID. This leaks an internal ID into the markup, and it's a minor accessibility and privacy concern.
- I found no other unresolved tokens in the visible source.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `Authentication-Results` was not found. QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `em.sallybeauty.com` are **unverified, not failed**. Check the raw headers at the original receiving MTA.
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers weren't provided. The footer unsubscribe link and the physical address are in the truncated section. Both are unconfirmed.
- Several third-party trackers (Dotomi, VisualIQ, Monetate) fire from the email. Whether the privacy policy discloses them can't be determined from the source.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
Not verifiable. The link probes were skipped and the body is truncated, so UTM parameters and landing page destinations can't be assessed.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Fix the `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro and the `-11` placeholder values in the `myvisualiq` pixel so they are resolved at send time. Otherwise remove the pixel.
2. Switch the Dotomi pixel to HTTPS, or drop it if the vendor can't serve it over HTTPS.
3. Change the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge" />`.
4. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
5. Give all tracking pixels `alt=""` and explicit 1x1 dimensions. Remove the two empty-`src` images.
6. Stop using the Salesforce contact ID as the pixel's alt text.
7. Re-run the audit against the full HTML and raw headers. That is needed to check `List-Unsubscribe`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, the footer address, UTM parameters, and the 52 redirect links by following them.
