## What's working

- **The Mother's Day hero image** — a brown-skinned mom kissing her daughter's cheek while they both rock pink Skechers. I'm a millennial mom of a 5-year-old girl. I felt this in my chest. Perfect casting.
- **BOGO 50% auto-applied at cart** — the blue banner makes it unmissable and removes friction. I don't have to remember a code. That's huge for a busy mom doing a quick browse session.
- **Layered promos** — BOGO *and* 25% off Spring Apparel in the secondary banner. Two reasons to stay instead of one.
- **"Mother's Day Shop" positioning** — it gives me guilt-free permission to browse for myself, which I almost never do. That's smart framing.
- **Clean, fast-loading layout** — no jank, no spinning placeholders. On mobile that matters.

## What's weak

- **No Kids' shoes shortcut above the fold** — my primary mission is shoes for my 5-year-old and 9-year-old, and I have to go through the hamburger menu to find them. There's zero "Shop Kids" surface visible.
- **No free-shipping threshold mentioned** — I always calculate whether I'm hitting free shipping before I commit to a cart. Not seeing it makes me hesitant.
- **No loyalty/rewards callout** — I'm a rewards-program loyalist. If I have points to spend, I want to know right now, not after I've already decided whether to shop.
- **Hero CTA is generic** — "SHOP NOW" doesn't tell me *what* I'm shopping. Is this women's? Kids'? Everything? A mom on a time-limited scroll session wants to tap with confidence.
- **No social proof** — no star ratings, no "best seller" badges, no review counts. I research before buying and this page gives me nothing to anchor trust.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop Kids" quick-link strip** directly below the hero — two tiles: "Girls (sizes 4–3)" and "Boys (sizes 1–7)." That's the second thing I'd look for after the hero, and it's missing entirely.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold in the promo banner** — swap one of the two banner lines to "Free shipping on orders $75+" so I know my hurdle upfront.
- **Swap "SHOP NOW" for "Shop the Mother's Day Collection"** — more specific, still one tap, tells me exactly where I'm landing.
- **Add a loyalty nudge under the hero** — even one line: "Skechers Plus member? You're earning double points through 5/11." That hooks price-sensitive loyalists without taking up real estate.
