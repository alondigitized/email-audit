# Ends Tonight! 30% off select boots, slippers, & outdoor
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-10**
## Executive summary

- "Ends Tonight" got me to open it, but then I had to work out which deal was the real one. The email has a "LAST CHANCE" 20% off for members and then a separate 30% off boots banner. They overlap, and I couldn't tell if they stack. "Hi, Martha" and my 1000 points are there again, so I'm a known customer. Everything after that is a grid of adult boots and slip-ons, and I'm shopping for a 5-year-old and a 9-year-old.
- I've seen the holiday checklist creative on 11/05, and the same 20% member deal on 10/22 through 10/24. At this point it's wallpaper. The only thing new is the 30% banner. The urgency is real, but it's about boots and slippers I'm not buying today.
- Would I click? Maybe, if I were killing time on the couch. But nothing here takes me to kids' shoes, so I'd probably mark it read and search the site myself. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- "Hi, Martha" with my 1000 points and the $5 reward strip is the only part that feels written for me, and it's right at the top.
- "LAST CHANCE" and the big cream "20% OFF" on dark green are easy to read on my phone, and the eye goes straight there.
- The discount is auto-applied at cart, which I like. There's no code to hunt for.
- The women's and men's size chips (5-5.5, 6-6.5 and so on) are big tap targets and quick to use.
- Klarna and Afterpay show up near the bottom, which is handy when I'm buying two kids' pairs at once.

## What's weak

- Two discounts, 20% and 30%, sit in two big blocks. The fine print says the 30% is for select styles and the 20% is for Plus members, but I can't tell which wins on a given shoe.
- The product grid is all adult boots and slip-ons in tan, blue, cream and brown. Nothing for a 5-year-old girl or a 9-year-old boy.
- The size chips are only Women's and Men's. There's no kids' sizing, so the email assumes I'm shopping for myself.
- KIDS is just one line in the text menu at the bottom, and I'd have to scroll past everything to reach it.
- It's the same "holiday checklist" art I saw on 11/05, so it feels like a repeat.
- Nothing mentions a family bundle, a promo code, or the Plus rewards I could earn.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer. If 30% on boots is the "ends tonight" deal, make it the hero and say plainly whether it stacks with the 20% member discount.
- 2. Add a kids' row to the grid, for example light-up or velcro shoes for girls and lace-up sneakers for boys. Add kids' size chips too.
- 3. Show my points balance as something I can use, like "Your $5 reward is ready. Use it tonight."
- 4. Move KIDS up to the top of the shop menu.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The source has one confirmed personalization failure (empty greeting, `Hi ,`), a malformed tracking-pixel URL (`pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif`, no scheme), and two `http://` images with no alt text. Authentication, unsubscribe and UTM checks can't be confirmed from the truncated HTML and the QA data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Scheme-less pixel URL:** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…">` has no `http(s)://`. Clients resolve it as a relative path, so the request fails. The Return Path/Validity seed-list or inbox-placement pixel never fires. It also has no `alt`.
- **Plain-HTTP tracking pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108404282&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NONPUR_U_SITESELECT_NONPURCH_EN_11102026`. Clients that block mixed or non-HTTPS content will drop it, which undercounts opens or attribution.
- **Not verified:** QA skipped 46 tracking links (`click.emails.skechers.com` redirects) without an HTTP probe, so click-through destinations and redirect health are unchecked.
- **Third-party beacons:** `beacon.krxd.net` (usermatch ×2 and ad_impression) is embedded alongside the SFMC open pixel. The usermatch pixels carry a SHA-256 hash (`partner_uid=adf27fee…`) and a subscriber ID (`partner_uid=122277199`). They are valid HTTPS, but they are cross-site identity-sync calls, and clients that proxy images (Gmail) will fetch them.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):**
  - `o.gif` (ink1000.com), which is also `http://`.
  - `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` (`http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/…`), which is also `http://`. This is a content image on Skechers' own image host, so mixed-content blocking could hide it.
  - Also missing `alt` in the source: the three `beacon.krxd.net` images and the `returnpath` pixel. These are hidden pixels, so the impact is low.
- **Meta tags don't work:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` lack the `http-equiv` attribute. The charset declaration is not valid, so clients that sniff encoding may misrender `®` or other special characters.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1" />` disables pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Skechers®</title>` doesn't describe the message. Minor.
- **Table roles:** the layout tables visible in the truncated source (`<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0" border="0">`, `<table width="1" height="1" …>`) have no `role="presentation"`. Screen readers may announce them as data tables. The rest of the source is truncated, so I can't confirm this for the whole email.

## 7. Recommendations
