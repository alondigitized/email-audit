# 2 for $100 Ends Tonight!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-03**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Back to School Bundle" actually got my attention right away — that's exactly the season I'm shopping for right now with a 5-year-old and a 9-year-old. The "2 for $100" concept is smart because that's literally how I buy shoes for my kids — one for her, one for him, in the same order. I like that they built the whole email around bundling instead of just another sitewide percentage-off blast.
- But then it wanders. After the "one for you, one for them" pairing (a women's pink pair + a men's pair — fine, sure), it pivots to "Head-to-Toe Style" with two adult items, then "Accessorize Your Look" with a kids' sneaker plus a backpack. None of this is actually kids' shoes for my daughter or son specifically — it's really an adult bundle concept dressed up in back-to-school language. I scrolled through looking for an actual boy's lace-up sneaker or a girl's light-up/velcro pair and didn't clearly see either.
- I'd click through to browse kids' styles since the subject line pulled me in, but I'm not clicking any specific product here because nothing shown is actually "my kid's shoe." I wouldn't forward this to a friend — it's fine, not exciting.

## What's working

- "Back to School Bundle" + "2 for $100" is genuinely relevant to how I shop for two kids at once.
- The "ends tonight" urgency at the top is clear and hard to miss.
- Multiple nav buttons (Shop Women's/Men's/Kids/All) make it easy to jump straight to kids' shoes.
- Skechers Plus member code (SAVE15) stacking on top is a nice loyalty touch since I'm in the rewards program.
- "Pick up where you left off" retargeting section is a good personalization signal — feels like they know what I looked at.

## What's weak

- Zero actual kids' product shown clearly — I see adult sneakers, a backpack, and a lifestyle shot of a guy in a hoodie, but nothing that screams "your son's sneaker" or "your daughter's velcro pair."
- The "2 for $100" bundles pictured are adult-adult or adult-kid mixes, not a clean kids' bundle, which is confusing for a school-shoes email.
- No pricing shown per item, no sizing/fit info, no mention of durability or velcro/light-up features that actually matter for my kids.
- Visual hierarchy gets busy fast — bold colors and bundle math (+/=) on every section makes we scroll a lot before finding anything I'd tap.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the hero bundle example with an actual kids' pairing — girl's velcro/light-up shoe + boy's lace-up sneaker — since that's the bundle a mom like me is actually building.
- 2. Add a dedicated "Kids' Back to School" row with both boys' and girls' shoes, sizes, and price per pair, not just adult styles.
- 3. Move the Skechers Plus 15% code higher — it's buried mid-email and it's actually a strong incentive for me as a member.
- 4. Cut the "Head-to-Toe" and "Accessorize" adult sections or move them lower — they dilute the back-to-school focus.
