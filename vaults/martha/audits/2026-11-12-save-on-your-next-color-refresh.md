# Save on your next color refresh
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- Sally Beauty again. By my count it's about the twenty-fourth send since mid-September, and the "Black Friday came early 👀" on Nov 8 already promised me deals, so "Early Black Friday Deals" today feels like a repeat. The black hero with "Holiday hair color, for less." is clean and I get the point in two seconds. But it's hair color, and I'm a mom shopping for a 5-year-old girl and a 9-year-old boy. Nothing here is for them.
- The BOGO 50% off Wella and buy-two-get-one-free AGEbeautiful offers are real, concrete deals, and I'm price-sensitive, so I notice them. The offers are buried under big sparkly-gold product tiles, though. The products are small boxes on glitter, so I can't tell shades apart without clicking. I'd skim it and move on. I wouldn't forward it to a friend, and I'd mark it read.

## What's working

- The hero ("Holiday hair color, for less.") is clear and the black background grabs the eye fast.
- Both offers are specific: "Buy one, get one 50% off" and "Buy two, get one free." I don't have to hunt for what the deal is.
- The "Check off your list" gift edit by price carries over from the Nov 9 and Nov 11 emails, and it's the one block that could apply to me as a mom with a gift list.
- The Klarna strip and the free shipping ($40+) and 2-hour delivery icons help a budget-conscious shopper like me.
- The red strip at the top with the 15% off and the 2-hour delivery offers is easy to spot.

## What's weak

- There's nothing for kids: no velcro, no light-up soles, no pink or sparkle for my daughter, no sneakers for my son. It feels like a stranger's email.
- The "Shop now" links are tiny and underlined, so they're hard to tap on a phone. Only the hero and gift edit have real buttons.
- The product tiles are small boxes on gold glitter, with no shade swatches, prices, or ratings. I can't tell what I'd save.
- Too many offers compete: two top banners, the hero, two BOGO blocks, the gift edit, and Klarna. There's no single clear next step.
- The footer fine print is long and says the offer is "valid online only... buy online and pick up in-store." That narrows the deal, and it isn't mentioned up top.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer and one real button, such as "Shop BOGO 50% off Wella," with price and shade swatches on the products.
- 2. Replace the tiny "Shop now" text links with full-width buttons that are easy to tap on mobile.
- 3. Say "pick up in-store" up top instead of burying it in the footer.
- 4. Use what you know about me (a rewards member) to show my points balance or member pricing, and cut back the cadence.
- **Subject Alt A:** `BOGO 50% off Wella color, today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your color + 2 free boxes: stock up`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Buy 2, get 1 free AGEbeautiful. Pick up in-store in 2 hours.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Rewards members: extra savings on holiday hair color.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The template is a standard table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional build with a mobile media query. The QA run (86% pass, 0 errors, 1 warning) found a malformed `<meta>` pair and an unresolved ad-server macro in a tracking pixel. It also found a raw CRM ID used as image alt text and an `http://` pixel. The source was truncated, so several categories can't be fully verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved macro:** the pixel `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&...` still contains the literal `[CACHEBUSTER]`. Nothing substituted it, so every open sends the same cache-buster value. Clients and proxies can then cache the request, which undercounts impressions.
- **Mixed content:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&...` is loaded over plain HTTP. Clients that block non-HTTPS images will drop it, and it exposes the request parameters in transit.
- **Unverified links:** 54 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destinations, redirect chains and status codes are **unverified**. This isn't a confirmed defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute. The charset and compatibility declarations are therefore inert. This is low risk if the MIME header sets the charset, but the markup is invalid.
- **Alt text:** QA flagged 5 images with missing or non-descriptive alt text. Two have empty names and empty `src` values (likely template placeholders that should be removed). Three are tracking pixels (`pxl.mon-trk.com`, `myvisualiq`, `dotomi`). Pixels should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`, so that screen readers skip them.
- **Raw ID as alt text:** the pixel at `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/987bfe61-.../2333878/003aZ00000s0I5yQAE` uses `003aZ00000s0I5yQAE` as its alt text. That string looks like a Salesforce Contact ID (`003` prefix). Screen readers will read it aloud, and it shows as text when images are blocked.
- **Empty `src` attributes:** two images have a blank `src`. Some clients issue a request to the base URL for these.

No other rendering issues were confirmed in the visible source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- Unresolved `[CACHEBUSTER]` in the `myvisualiq` pixel URL (see §2).
- The Salesforce Contact ID is injected correctly into the pixel path. However, it also leaks into the alt attribute, which is an unintended merge-field exposure.
- No unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `${…}` tokens were visible in the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication:** `Authentication-Results` was not found. The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is **unknown**, not failing. The From domain `em.sallybeauty.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is a good sign. Check the raw headers from a direct delivery to confirm.
- **Unsubscribe and postal address:** the footer and the `List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are outside the truncated source. The `.legal a` and `.EID a` styles suggest a legal footer exists, but I can't confirm it. **Unverified.**

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Because all 54 links went through click-redirect domains and weren't probed, I can't see final landing URLs or UTM parameters. **No issues confirmed.** This category needs a follow-up redirect-resolution pass.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Make the ad server substitute `[CACHEBUSTER]` with a per-send or per-open value, or strip the parameter. Do this before the send is released.
2. Switch the `login.dotomi.com` pixel to HTTPS, or ask the vendor for an HTTPS endpoint.
3. Set `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` on all tracking pixels. Remove the `003aZ00000s0I5yQAE` alt text, and delete or fix the two images with empty `src` values.
4. Correct the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge" />`.
5. Extend the QA tooling:
   - Follow redirects on the 54 tracking links, to capture final URLs and UTM parameters.
   - Parse the full body, so the footer and unsubscribe link are visible.
   - Capture raw headers, including `List-Unsubscribe`, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC can be checked.
