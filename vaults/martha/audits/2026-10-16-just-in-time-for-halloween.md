# Just in time for Halloween 🎃
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-16**
## Executive summary

- Sally Beauty again. This is the fifteenth or so send since mid-September, and I'm tired of them. The hero is a matte black nail with a spiderweb design, and it's a good photo. But the Halloween angle does nothing for me. I'm shopping for a 5-year-old and a 9-year-old, and nothing here is for kids. Nobody is going to press-on a spiderweb mani onto my daughter.
- The "Buy one, get one 50% off" blocks are the real offer, and they show up three times: dip powder, lashes, and vivid hair color. That's clear, and I'd love a BOGO on something I need. But it's dip powder and false lashes, and I don't do either. I like the "pick up in-store" and "2 hour delivery" strips, since that's useful when I'm racing around with kids. But the red banners stacked at the top are a lot to take in before I reach any product.
- Would I click? Probably not. Would I forward it? No. It gets marked read and I move on. It's a decent, clear email that isn't for me, and I've seen it too many times this month.

## What's working

- The hero photo is striking. The spiderweb nails on black make the Halloween theme obvious right away.
- "Buy one, get one 50% off" is a clear, specific offer, and it's repeated at every section.
- Pickup and 2-hour delivery are in the top banners. That helps a busy mom.
- The $5 off $25 rewards signup banner is visible near the top. I like rewards programs.
- Klarna "4 interest-free payments" is a nice touch for bigger orders.

## What's weak

- There are three stacked banners above the hero (rewards, pickup, 2 hour delivery). They compete with the hero, and I don't know which one matters.
- Nothing here is for kids or families. It's all nails, lashes and bright hair dye.
- Only two products per category, and the product names are small and hard to read. The "Shop now" links are tiny text, not buttons, which is a poor tap target on my phone.
- The "Last-minute look" hero is for a Halloween that's two weeks away. There's no deadline on the BOGO, so there's no reason to act today.
- The footer fine print is a wall of text. I can't tell whether the $5 reward and the BOGO stack.
- It follows the 10/15 "Check off your holiday list early" send, so the cadence feels relentless.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one offer first. Lead with the BOGO 50% off, drop two of the three top banners, and put a real end date on it.
- 2. Make the CTAs real buttons, bigger and easier to tap. Right now they're small text links.
- 3. Segment me. If I've bought kids' or family items, show me costume makeup, face paint or sparkly nail kits for kids. I'd click that.
- 4. Pare it to one product block with a clear hero product, instead of three categories I may never buy.
- 5. Slow down the cadence. Fifteen sends in a month teaches me to ignore them.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible markup is a standard XHTML 1.0 Transitional table template with a responsive `<style>` block. The QA run found 0 errors and 1 warning (86% pass), and the issues it did find are concentrated in third-party tracking pixels. The HTML was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and body-link checks could not be done.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved macro in a pixel URL:** `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&...` still contains the literal `[CACHEBUSTER]` placeholder. The ad-server macro was never substituted, so the cache-buster is a static string. Clients that cache the image can undercount impressions.
- **Non-HTTPS pixel:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&...` loads over HTTP. Clients may block it or flag it as mixed content, which would drop the Dotomi (Epsilon) sync.
- **Empty image source:** the QA output lists one `<img>` with an empty `src` (`src: `) and no alt. This is a likely broken or stray tag, but I can't see its position in the truncated source.
- **Links not probed:** 57 tracking or click-redirect links were skipped by the QA tool, so their destinations, redirect chains and status codes are unverified.
- **Salesforce ID in a pixel URL:** `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/987bfe61-.../2324947/003aZ00000s0I5yQAE` embeds what looks like a Salesforce Contact ID (`003` prefix). This is expected for an ESP open pixel, but the ID is recipient-identifying.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels:** `003aZ00000s0I5yQAE`, `impression_pixel` and `UCMController` have no alt attribute. Images with no alt attribute get announced by some screen readers (often by filename or URL), so they should use `alt=""`. Fix this together with the empty-`src` image above.
- **Missing `lang`:** the root element is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` with no `lang` (or `xml:lang`) attribute, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=edge" />` both lack `http-equiv`, so neither does anything. Encoding falls back on the MIME `Content-Type` header, so this is low risk.
- **Font fallback:** `a{font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif}` references a font with no `@font-face` in the visible CSS. It will fall back to Helvetica or sans-serif, which is acceptable but unintended.
- **Viewport and mobile CSS:** the viewport meta and the `max-width:499px` breakpoint are present and well-formed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible HTML. The only unresolved placeholder is the ad-server `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro, covered under Link & Tracking.

## 5. Compliance
- **Authentication:** `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay stripping the header, so it's an inconclusive result and not a sender defect. The From domain `em.sallybeauty.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain.
- **Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe and postal address:** these can't be assessed because the source is truncated and no headers were provided. No violation is claimed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be assessed. The click-redirect links were not probed, and the visible source shows no destination URLs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Make the ad-server integration substitute `[CACHEBUSTER]` at send time. If it can't, remove the VisualIQ pixel.
2. Change the Dotomi pixel to `https://`, or drop it if the vendor doesn't support HTTPS.
3. Remove or fix the `<img>` with the empty `src`.
4. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
5. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and give the two meta tags their `http-equiv` attributes (`Content-Type`, `X-UA-Compatible`).
6. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and raw headers. That would let you verify the unsubscribe link, `List-Unsubscribe`, footer address, SPF/DKIM/DMARC and UTM tagging.
7. Probe a sample of the 57 skipped redirect links manually for destination status and UTM propagation.
