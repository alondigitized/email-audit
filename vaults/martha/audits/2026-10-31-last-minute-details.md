# Last minute details
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- Sally Beauty again, and this one landed on Halloween itself. It's roughly the twentieth send since mid-September. "Last minute details" tells me nothing, and the preview just says "Shop Halloween at Sally." The hero is a gorgeous woman with red curls and orange eye makeup, and it's pretty. But I'm a mom of two who's been done with costumes for weeks. I'm not buying false lashes at 2 PM on October 31st.
- The "2-hour delivery" line is the one thing that made me pause. If I'd forgotten face paint, that would be a lifesaver. But the products are lashes, nail stickers and bright red and purple hair dye, which is nothing for a 5-year-old girl or a 9-year-old boy. I'd read it as a reminder that Halloween is today, then swipe it away. I wouldn't click and I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The hero is striking. The red curls and orange eyeshadow make the Halloween theme obvious at a glance.
- "Get it today with 2-hour delivery" is a real urgency hook for a day-of email.
- "Buy one, get one 50% off Lashes" is a clear, concrete deal, and the pink rewards bar at the top is easy to spot.
- The layout is clean, with products in tidy two-up blocks and a "Shop now" under each.
- The Klarna banner and the icon row (free shipping $40+, in-store pickup) answer my "how do I get it" questions fast.

## What's weak

- "Last minute details" is vague and could be about anything. It doesn't say Halloween or 2-hour delivery.
- It's too late. Halloween is today, and the "Shop now" buttons mostly lead to products I can't use for kid costumes.
- Nothing is for my kids or for me as a mom. It's lashes, press-on nails and semi-permanent hair dye, with no kid-safe makeup or face paint.
- There are four offers competing: $5 off $25 for signing up, 15% off your next buy online, BOGO lashes, and Klarna. The top banners don't say which one matters.
- The $5-off-$25 rewards pitch is the same one I got on Oct 11, 17 and 19, so it feels stale.
- The fine print is a huge block at the bottom, and the 15% off bar has no visible terms near it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Send this on Oct 28 or 29, not Halloween day. At that point 2-hour delivery is a real selling point and I could still act.
- 2. Lead with one offer. Pick BOGO 50% off or 15% off and drop the repeated $5-off banner for existing subscribers.
- 3. Add a kid-safe or family angle, such as face paint, temporary tattoos or glitter for kids, so a mom has a reason to click.
- 4. Make the subject say what's inside, and put the 2-hour delivery in the preheader.
- 5. Cap the cadence. After 19+ sends, I'd rather get fewer, better emails.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Costume emergency? 2-hour delivery today 🎃`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tonight's look, at your door in 2 hours`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Order by 5 PM for same-day delivery. BOGO 50% off lashes ends tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Face paint, glitter and nail stickers, delivered today. Plus 15% off online.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is truncated, so the review covers the head and CSS plus the QA output. Confirmed defects: an unresolved `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro in a third-party pixel, an insecure `http://` pixel, several images with no `alt`, and malformed `<meta>` tags. Authentication, unsubscribe and UTM compliance can't be confirmed from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved macro:** `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&ao=883&aca=-11&si=-11&ci=-11&pi=-11&ad=-11&advt=-…` ships with a literal `[CACHEBUSTER]` in `r=`.
  - The impression request may be served from cache or fail to register.
  - The `-11` values for `aca`, `si`, `ci`, `pi` and `ad` look like unpopulated defaults. That suggests impression attribution is not being populated.
- **Insecure pixel:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&…` uses plain HTTP. Gmail and Apple Mail proxy or block insecure images, and the pixel may not fire. The cookie-sync call is also exposed in transit.
- **Unverified links:** 53 tracking and click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so their destinations and status codes are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Pixel stack:** At least three third-party pixels are visible: `pxl.mon-trk.com`, `myvisualiq.net` and `dotomi.com`. Each adds a request and a blocking or privacy risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on four images (QA-confirmed):**
  - An `<img>` with an empty `src` and no `alt`. An empty `src` can trigger a broken-image icon or a request to the page URL in some clients.
  - `003aZ00000s0I5yQAE` (`pxl.mon-trk.com`).
  - `impression_pixel` (`myvisualiq.net`).
  - `UCMController` (`dotomi.com`).
  - The three tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"` so screen readers skip them. Without `alt`, some readers announce the filename or URL.
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so both are ineffective. The charset declaration is lost, and `<meta name="viewport">` is the only working one.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang`, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Font stack:** `a{font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif}` references a font with no `@font-face`. Only Open Sans is declared, so links fall back to Helvetica. This is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- The `-11` placeholder IDs in the same URL suggest unpopulated dynamic fields.

2. Switch the Dotomi pixel to `https://`, or remove it.
