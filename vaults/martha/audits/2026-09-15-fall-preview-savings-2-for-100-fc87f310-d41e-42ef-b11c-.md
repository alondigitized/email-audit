- The 20% off PREVIEW banner for Plus members is bold and easy to spot even scrolling fast.
- Photography quality is clean and the buttons (orange "Stock Up Now," "Shop Women's/Men's/Kids'") are big, tappable, and unambiguous.

## What's weak

- Only ONE kids' product shown, and it's a girls' sneaker — nothing for my son at all, no boy's lace-up sneaker, no light-up or velcro styles for my daughter either.
- No "family bundle" or multi-kid promo code messaging even though "2 for $100" is literally begging for a "outfit both your kids" angle.
- The kids' section is the smallest, least prioritized block — women's and men's each get a full lifestyle photo, kids get a single product shot with no context.

- Yet another 20% off code this week (PREVIEW instead of EXTRA20) — the offer fatigue is real even if the code name changed.

- 1. Lead with kids in the hero if you want moms like me to open and buy — swap the loafer hero for a "2 for $100: outfit both kids for fall" shot with a boy and girl pair side by side, price broken out per kid.
- 2. Add a boy's sneaker to the kids' row — right now it's 100% girls', which reads as an oversight for anyone shopping for a son.
- 3. Tie the kids' section directly to the 2-for-$100 offer with a badge or price callout so I don't have to click through to check eligibility.
- 4. Give the kids' block equal visual weight to women's/men's — it's currently an afterthought at the bottom of the product stack.
