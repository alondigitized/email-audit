# Our biggest sale of the year is now live!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay, it's finally Black Friday, and this email goes big. The black hero says "30%–50% OFF SITEWIDE" and I get it in about a second. The "Hi, Martha" strip with my 1000 points and the $5 reward is at the very top again. That's a nice touch, but I've seen it six times since Tuesday. After all the "hours left" and "last chance" emails, "now live" feels a bit late. It also feels like the same sale with a new label.
- The sale itself is what I care about. The red "Plus members stack an extra 15% off everything, code BF2026" banner is the first thing that actually made me want to open my cart. But then I scroll and see nothing for my kids. There's no girls' velcro or light-up shoes and no boys' lace-ups. There are only adult models, and the doorbusters are all adult sneakers. "KIDS" is buried in a text list near the bottom.
- Would I click? Probably yes, because of the code, but I'd go straight to Kids through the bottom list. I'd forward it to my sister only for the code. I wouldn't say it's a great email, but it works.

## What's working

- The "30%–50% OFF SITEWIDE" hero is huge and clear, with a "Shop the Sale" button and "Discount auto-applied at cart."
- My name, 1000 points and the $5 reward strip at the top tell me they know I'm a member.
- The "Extra 15% off everything, code BF2026" banner gives me a real reason to stack savings.
- The doorbusters block, "Starting at $49.99," gives me a concrete price to anchor on.
- Klarna and Afterpay are shown for holiday budgeting, which helps a price-sensitive mom.

## What's weak

- Nothing is aimed at my kids: no girls' sparkle or light-up shoes, no boys' durable lace-ups, and all the models are adults.
- "KIDS" is just a text row at the bottom, under Women and Men.
- The doorbuster shoes are tiny, the captions are unreadable, and I see no prices on them.
- The fine print is dense, with exclusions, "select" styles and a login requirement for the code. Because the 15% is for Plus members, it's not clear whether I qualify as a regular member.
- I've now had about seven near-identical sale emails this week, and the "biggest sale of the year" line is just more of the same.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a "Shop Kids" tile in the main grid. Put a girls' sparkle velcro shoe and a boys' black lace-up sneaker side by side, with prices.
- 2. Make the doorbuster products larger, with a price under each and a "Shop" link to each product.
- 3. Say plainly who gets the 15% code (Plus members only) and how to join, in a line near the banner rather than in the fine print.
- 4. Add a family bundle offer, such as "2+ pairs = extra savings," and a "your rewards balance" line tied to the sale.
- 5. Vary the creative across the week so it doesn't feel like the same email on repeat.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack code BF2026 for an extra 15% off. Ends 11/29.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed broken-personalization defect (empty greeting) and a malformed tracking-pixel URL. It also has non-HTTPS image sources and several markup problems in `<head>`. The HTML was truncated and 43 click-tracking links were not probed, so footer, unsubscribe and UTM checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed pixel URL:** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…" width="1" height="1" />` has no scheme. Clients resolve it as a relative path, so the request fails or hits a non-existent path on the sender's domain. Return Path inbox-placement and read tracking is silently lost. The tag also has no `alt` attribute.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108404282&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_BLACKFRI_FULL_EN_11232026` loads over plain HTTP. It may be blocked or rewritten by clients that enforce HTTPS, and it has no `alt`. It sits in a `<table width="1" height="1">` outside the hidden `display:none` wrapper.
- **Unverified click links:** 43 links were skipped as tracking/click-redirect domains (for example `click.emails.skechers.com`). Their destinations and status codes are unverified.
- **Third-party pixels:** Four `beacon.krxd.net` Krux/Salesforce user-match and impression pixels are present. One passes a hashed identifier (`partner_uid=adf27fee…189a`). These add load-time dependencies. They may also need privacy disclosure.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Non-HTTPS image:** `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` is served over HTTP and has no `alt`. It may be blocked as mixed content, and screen readers get no text.
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute. The charset declaration is therefore not valid, and the X-UA-Compatible hint does nothing.
- **Viewport zoom disabled:** `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text-size lock:** `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none }` stops iOS users from resizing text.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang`.
- **Layout tables:** The visible layout tables (for example `<table class="widthFull" width="640">`) have no `role="presentation"`, so screen readers may announce them as data tables. The top-level tracking `<table>` also has no `width`.
- **Preheader:** `div.preheader` is hidden correctly. The tracking images are wrapped in `display:none`, so there is no rendering issue there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** The greeting renders as `Hi ,`. The first-name merge field resolved to empty, and no fallback value is defined in the template. No unresolved `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` tokens were seen in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication:** The `Authentication-Results` header was not found. QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay stripping it, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status cannot be confirmed. This is a limitation of the test pipeline, not a confirmed sender defect.
- **From and sender:** `no-reply@emails.skechers.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain, so alignment is plausible but unverified.
- **Unsubscribe and postal address:** Not verifiable, because the footer and `List-Unsubscribe` headers fall outside the truncated source and the supplied QA data. No violation is asserted.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Unverifiable:** Click URLs are wrapped in `click.emails.skechers.com` redirects, and the HTTP probe skipped them. UTM parameters and landing-page destinations cannot be confirmed from the source provided.
- **Campaign ID:** `PRO_US_NA_U_BLACKFRI_FULL_EN_11232026` appears in both the Krux and ink1000 pixels. It is consistent, which is a positive sign.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the greeting: add a merge-field fallback (for example a `Hi there,` default), or suppress the greeting when the first name is empty.
2. Correct the Return Path pixel to `https://pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=…` and add `alt=""`.
3. Switch `ink1000.com` and `image.emails.skechers.com` assets to `https://`, and add `alt` text, or `alt=""` for decorative or tracking images.
4. Change the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and `role="presentation"` to layout tables.
6. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and the raw message headers. Resolve the redirect chains to confirm UTM parameters and landing pages. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, the footer unsubscribe link, the postal address, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
