# Labor Day savings start now
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-01**
## Executive summary

- Up to 40% off is a real hook, and "Hi, Martha" with my 1000 points sitting right there is nice — at least someone's tracking that I'm a member. But then it's Women's shoe, Men's shoe, another 20% for Plus members... and nowhere, not once, do I see a kid on this page. I'm scrolling through my whole phone screen for my Saturday-morning shoe hunt for a 5-year-old and a 9-year-old, and Skechers gives me two adult sneakers and a stack of category links buried at the bottom.
- It's basically the same story as "Your favorites, now in wide fit" and "Find your comfort" from the last two days — decent adult offer, zero kids' content up front. I'll admit the 40% headline is bigger than anything they've thrown at me this week, so I might actually tap "Shop the Sale" out of curiosity, but I'm not forwarding this to anyone and I'm definitely not buying for my kids off this email.

## What's working

- The "Up to 40% OFF" hero is big, bold, and impossible to miss — best headline number they've sent me all week.
- "Hi, Martha · Member" plus my 1000 points and $5 reward reminder up top feels like they know I'm a repeat customer.
- Labor Day timing is on point — it's genuinely the holiday weekend, not some random stale promo.
- Plus member 20% off is called out clearly with its own red block and a real deadline (9/7).

## What's weak

- Zero kids' shoes anywhere in this email — no velcro, no light-up soles, no pink/purple sparkle for my daughter, no lace-up durable boy styles. "KIDS" is just a plain text link buried in a list near the bottom.
- Two separate CTAs (Women's Sale, Men's Sale) before I even see a kids' option — tells me exactly who this email was built for, and it's not moms shopping for their kids.
- "SEE BELOW FOR DETAILS" under the hero and again under the 20% block feels like there's a catch — turned out to be normal fine print, but it reads like bait right at the moment I'm deciding to tap.
- The background hero image has a "40% OFF" ghosted behind the bold text — minor but a little messy/cluttered looking on a small screen.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a "Kids' Labor Day Sale" section right under the hero, above Women's and Men's — that's the single biggest lift here, since I open these emails specifically hunting for kid shoes.
- 2. Swap one of the two hero shoes for an actual kids' style so the visual matches what I'm shopping for.
- 3. Pull the "SHOP ADDITIONAL STYLES" kids link out of that plain gray list and give it its own button/banner like Women's and Men's got.
- 4. Add a specific kids' price point or bundle callout (e.g., "Buy 2 kids' pairs, save more") since I'm outfitting two kids at once.
