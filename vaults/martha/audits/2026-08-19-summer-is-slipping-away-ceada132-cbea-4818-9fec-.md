# Summer is slipping away ⏳
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so this is the exact same "Summer Lovin' Sale, Up to 40% Off" banner I already saw on 8/14, now dressed up with an emoji countdown subject line about summer "slipping away." I get it, they're trying to manufacture urgency for the same static sale, but I've literally seen this hero image — the couple in tan resort wear by the pool — twice now in five days. Nothing new here.
- The good news, and it's a real improvement over the last several sends I've flagged: there's an actual "SHOP KIDS'" button with a kids' sneaker photo before the footer link maze. That's progress. But the kids' shoe shown is one plain white sneaker with a little floral/pink detail — no velcro strap for my 5-year-old, no light-up soles, nothing screaming boy sneaker for my 9-year-old, and definitely no "family bundle" messaging front and center. I'd glance at it, maybe tap "SHOP KIDS'" out of habit, but I'm not forwarding this to anyone — it's the same sale wearing a different subject line.

## What's working

- The "SHOP KIDS'" button and kids' shoe image are actually present and above the footer, unlike some past sends — small win.
- "Up to 40% off" is a clear, specific number I can register in two seconds.
- Women's, Men's, and Kids' each get their own product shot and button — I can at least jump straight to kids' instead of scrolling forever.
- The deadline "thru 8/19" is concrete, and since that's today, there's real (if last-minute) urgency.

## What's weak

- This is a recycled creative — same "Summer Lovin'" banner, same pool-and-resort couple I saw on 8/14. I'm not fooled by a new subject line on an old sale.
- The kids' shoe shown isn't clearly gendered for either of my kids — no velcro/sparkle for my daughter, no rugged lace-up for my son, so I can't tell at a glance if there's anything for them specifically.
- The preview text in my inbox is straight-up broken — it's showing raw schema.org JSON code instead of real preview copy. That looks glitchy and unprofessional before I even open it.
- The hero models are adult, childless-looking, lounging poolside — not remotely speaking to a mom shopping for school shoes.
- No mention of my rewards points or member pricing anywhere, even though I've been on this list for weeks now.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the broken preview text immediately** — showing raw JSON-LD code in the inbox preview is a real deliverability/trust red flag, not a copy nitpick. This should be the top priority fix.
- 2. Split the kids' section into girls' vs. boys' with two product shots — velcro/light-up for younger kids, lace-up durable styles for older kids — instead of one ambiguous sneaker.
- 3. Swap the hero image for a family scene (or at least stop reusing the exact 8/14 pool photo) so repeat readers like me don't immediately clock it as a rerun.
- 4. Surface loyalty/rewards points messaging near the top — I'm price-sensitive and loyalty-driven, and this email currently treats me like a cold lead.
- 5. Since the sale ends today, lead with that urgency in the hero banner itself, not just fine print under the button.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last day: up to 40% off kids' shoes`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Ends tonight — 40% off + 25% off apparel`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Kids' sneakers, sandals & more — sale ends 8/19`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your rewards points stretch further today only`
