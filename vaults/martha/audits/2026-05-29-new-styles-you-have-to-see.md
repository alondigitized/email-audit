# New Styles You Have to See
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-29**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so I'm sitting here while the kids are eating lunch, and I opened this one mostly out of habit — I'm subscribed to Skechers and the BOGO banner at the top at least confirms there's a real offer inside. But I'm immediately a little annoyed, because the very first hero image is a man in a blue hoodie. Not a woman. Not a kid. A man. I shop Skechers almost exclusively for my 5-year-old and my 9-year-old, and when I do buy for myself it's comfort-first women's styles. So the lead visual is already working against me.
- The Women's section does show up — a nice coral/pink sneaker — and I'll be honest, the sandals tile caught my eye because I do need a pair of casual summer sandals for myself. But then I hit the Skechers Plus "Enter to Win the Ultimate Soccer Fan Prize Package" banner and I just stopped. I don't follow soccer. My kids aren't in soccer. This is a total dead zone for me. It takes up real estate in the scroll and I get nothing from it.
- The BOGO 50% off is the same offer I saw on the Memorial Day send from May 25th. It was fresh then — now it feels like background noise. I'd probably scroll to the Women's section, maybe tap on the sandals, and close. Wouldn't forward this. Wouldn't screenshot it. It's a "mark as read and keep it in mind for later" send, nothing more.

## What's working

- **BOGO banner is front and center.** "Buy One, Get One 50% Off or 20% Off a Single Pair" — immediate, clean, honest. Even if I've seen this offer before, it still makes me stop and think before closing.
- **Women's section has an appealing shoe.** That coral/pink sneaker is actually cute and felt relevant to me — I would have clicked that section specifically.
- **Sandals tile grabbed me.** Summer is here, I need sandals, and that tile was right there. Short scroll, fast tap. Good placement.
- **Layout is clean and loads fast.** No broken images, no overlapping text. I could skim the whole email in about 8 seconds without hitting any friction.
- **Skechers Plus is visible.** I can see it's a loyalty program — even if the sweepstakes isn't for me, the brand is at least surfacing the program.

## What's weak

- **Zero kids content above the fold — or really anywhere in the visual hierarchy.** The only mention of Kids is a plain text link buried in the "Shop Additional Styles" grid at the bottom. That's my primary shopping category and it's invisible.
- **The hero is a man.** I'm a 34-year-old woman. The first face I see in this email is a dude in a hoodie. Not a great first impression.
- **Soccer sweepstakes is completely irrelevant.** Took up a full-width banner slot that could have shown me kids' summer sneakers or light-ups. That's a real opportunity cost.
- **The same JSON schema garbage is still showing as preview text.** This is at minimum the fourth email in a row — I noticed it on the May 27th and May 28th sends, and it was there on the 21st too. At this point it's not a fluke, it's a systemic issue that makes every email look broken before I even open it.
- **BOGO offer fatigue.** This was the Memorial Day offer. It was exciting then. Seeing it again with no new reason to act makes it feel like a permanent discount, which honestly diminishes the urgency.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the preheader bug — this is the most impactful change by far.** The JSON schema leak has been running for at least 8 days across multiple sends. Every email is DOA in the inbox because of it. Whatever is injecting that schema markup into the preview slot needs to be turned off or filtered. This is table stakes.
- 2. **Lead the hero with a woman or a family, not a man.** Your list has tons of moms on it. A mom with a kid, or even just a woman, in the hero would have made me feel seen. The man-first creative is a demographic miss for a significant portion of your list.
- 3. **Surface kids' shoes in the body, not just a text link.** A "For the Kids" tile — even just one image of a light-up sneaker or a kids' sandal — between the Women's and Men's sections would have gotten a tap from me. That's a conversion you're leaving on the table every single send.
- 4. **Retire or refresh the BOGO if it's going to run indefinitely.** If BOGO 50% is basically always available, either own it as a standing program ("always-on member benefit") or create real scarcity with an end date. Right now it reads as a permanent discount dressed up as urgency.
- 5. **Cut the soccer sweepstakes or segment it.** If I'm not in the soccer-engaged segment, that banner is just scroll noise. Replace it for non-soccer audiences with a kids' new arrivals feature or a "ready for summer" category push.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Kids' Summer Kicks Are Here — BOGO 50% Off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New sandals, sneakers & kids' styles — shop BOGO 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Grab a pair for the kids while the BOGO deal is live.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New women's sandals + kids' summer styles — one get one 50% off.`

## Technical Audit — SKECHERS "New Styles You Have to See" (05/29/2026)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

One confirmed broken-experience bug (empty merge token in greeting) and a systemic HTTP image-serving issue affecting 13+ assets. No structural rendering failures detected.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**27 tracked links** route through `click.emails.skechers.com` (Salesforce Marketing Cloud click-redirect). Not probed directly, but volume is consistent with a standard SFMC deployment — no anomaly.

**Open pixel** at `http://www.ink1000.com/…/o.gif` uses plaintext HTTP. Modern clients (Gmail, Apple Mail Privacy Protection) will either block the request or proxy it, degrading open-rate signal accuracy.

**Krux/Salesforce DMP beacons** (`beacon.krxd.net`) are firing two user-match calls plus one ad-impression pixel. These transmit a hashed subscriber UID (`partner_uid=adf27fee…`) to a third-party data platform. Verify this is disclosed in the privacy policy and consistent with current consent posture.

**Return Path seed pixel** (`pixel.app.returnpath.net`) is served over HTTP — same blocking risk as the ink1000 pixel above.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**HTTP image sources (13 affected assets):** All images served from `http://image.emails.skechers.com` are non-HTTPS. Gmail, Outlook.com, and Apple Mail enforce HTTPS; these images will be blocked or proxied, breaking layout and all ALT-text fallbacks simultaneously.

Affected assets include functional UI elements — not just decorative content:
- Logo (`dde00662-…`)
- App Store badge (`0ec56c9f-…`)
- Google Play badge (`9bb508d0-…`)
- Social icons: Instagram, Facebook, YouTube
- Utility icons: Curbside Pickup, AfterPay, Find A Store

**Missing alt text on 2 images:**
- `o.gif` (ink1000 tracking pixel) — low severity, tracking pixel
- `49468f73-…png` (unidentified content image) — requires alt text for screen readers

**`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`** is applied globally via `* {}`. This prevents iOS from scaling small text, which can harm legibility on older devices without compensating viewport-relative font sizing.

**Duplicate media query blocks:** The responsive CSS for `max-width: 375px` and `max-width: 640px` are identical in rules. The 375px block is redundant; it adds parse weight with no differentiated behavior.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**[FAIL — Broken Experience]** The greeting renders as `"Hi ,"` — the subscriber first-name merge token is empty. Evidence from QA: `Greeting has empty name field`. The token likely resolves to null/empty for this subscriber segment, with no fallback value defined (e.g., `%%[IF @firstName != "" THEN]%%` guard or a default like `"Hi there,"`).

This is a confirmed deliverability-class bug — it degrades trust and engagement for any subscriber without a stored first name.

---

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)

**Preheader/subject mismatch:** Subject is "New Styles You Have to See"; preheader text reads "Stock Up On These Must Haves with BOGO 50% Off." These describe different offers — the preheader references a promotional mechanic (BOGO 50% Off) absent from the subject line. This is not a compliance issue but creates a disconnected inbox preview.

**Unsubscribe/physical address:** Truncated HTML prevents full verification, but SFMC deployments standardly include these. Flag for manual confirmation against the footer.

**Authentication headers:** Not derivable from HTML source alone. SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `emails.skechers.com` should be verified at the MTA level — not auditable here.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM Params, Landing Page Alignment)

All 27 destination links pass through `click.emails.skechers.com` redirects. UTM parameter presence on the final destination URLs cannot be confirmed without resolving the redirects. Campaign ID `MKG_US_NA_U_MULTI_ENGAGEDOPEN_EN_05292026_A` is present in the DMP impression pixel, confirming campaign tagging exists at the tracking layer — but UTM passthrough to skechers.com landing pages requires separate verification.
