# 🍂 Your fall beauty routine starts here 🍂

## Executive summary

- Sally Beauty again — I don't even have to check the sender name at this point, I recognize the confetti-dot banner instantly, which tells you exactly how many of these I've scrolled past lately. This one's actually decently put together — clean fall product shots, a real $5-off-$20 offer, a buy-two-get-one on nail stuff — but it's hair color and nail polish, and I'm not the target for either. I'm the mom thumbing through my phone on a Sunday hoping for kids' shoe deals, not a bonding shampoo and gel polish routine.
- The layout is clear, nothing's broken, and if I were actually into nail art or had color-treated hair I'd probably click "Shop Now" on the press-ons. But I'm not, so this is a swipe-past-and-mark-read, not a forward. My friends who'd want this aren't the ones I text shoe deals to.

## What's working

- The fall-themed hero photo and copy ("Your routine, ready for fall") feels current and seasonally appropriate, unlike some of their stale-feeling recent sends.
- The $5 off $20 hair care offer is a clean, specific, legible deal — no fine-print maze to dig through.
- Buy 2 Get 1 Free on nail products is an easy-to-understand value prop with a visible category tag.
- Product photography is crisp and organized into clear sections (hair, nails) so I can scan fast even if I'm not buying.
- Loyalty callout ("Shop Twice in August, Earn a $5 Reward") is visible near the bottom for anyone in the rewards program.

## What's weak

- Zero relevance to my actual life — no kids' products, no family angle, nothing that overlaps with why I open my inbox on weekends.
- This is at least the ninth Sally Beauty email in two weeks per my own history — the cadence is genuinely exhausting and makes we want to unsubscribe rather than engage.
- The hero model and "your routine" framing skews toward someone actively into hair color/nail maintenance, which isn't me — I do my own basic wash-and-go and skip the mani appointments.
- Two hero CTAs at the very top (Buy Online Pick Up In-Store / Free 2-Hour Delivery) before I even get to the actual content feels like clutter, not urgency.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment me out of this list entirely, or at minimum stop sending near-daily near-identical sends — I've now logged this brand nine times in two weeks and it's actively training me to ignore the sender, which kills future opens too.
- 2. If Sally Beauty wants engagement from browsers like me, they need a completely different value prop — I'm not their customer for hair color or gel nails, no subject line tweak fixes that.
- 3. Lead with the loyalty reward math up top, not buried near the footer — "Earn a $5 Reward" after two purchases is a decent hook for people who are already shopping there.
- 4. Drop one of the two redundant top-bar CTAs (BOPIS and 2-hour delivery) — pick the one that actually converts and give the hero image room to breathe.
