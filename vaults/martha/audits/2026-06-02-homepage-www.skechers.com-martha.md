# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-06-02
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-02**
## Executive summary

- I landed on Skechers and the first thing I see is a big bold "GAME ON!" with sporty retro sneakers in green, blue, and red sitting on grass — which reads very much like a golf or athletic shoes campaign aimed at adult men. The brand clearly has a solid loyalty program push going on (Member Month, 2X Points through 6/13) and a BOGO 50% banner right at the top, which honestly made my eyes light up for half a second since I'm buying for two kids. But I'm squinting trying to figure out where the kids' section is.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF banner** — this is literally the best possible message for me. I'm buying for a 5-year-old and a 9-year-old; buy one get one is exactly the math that makes me stay.
- **Member Month loyalty banner** — "Earn 2X Points now thru 6/13" with a hard date tells me there's a real reward on the table. I'm a loyalty program person. The Login link right next to it makes acting on it easy.
- **Top-of-page promotional real estate** — both offers are stacked before I've scrolled at all. The hierarchy of blue BOGO strip → white loyalty strip → nav → hero is clean and readable on mobile.
- **Quick-tap nav categories** — "Slip-ins, Sandals, Arch Fit, BOBs" are right below the loyalty banner. Slip-ins especially could mean quick-for-kids, and Sandals is seasonally correct for June.

## What's weak

- **"GAME ON!" hero is completely off-target for me** — three colorway athletic sneakers on grass screams sports fan or golf shopper. I'm here for a 5-year-old girl and a 9-year-old boy. This hero does nothing for my mission.
- **No "Kids" category visible in the nav** — the visible nav reads "Slip-ins | Sandals | Arch Fit | BOBs | G[cut off]." Kids is the most common search for a parent on Skechers, and it's not visible without horizontal scrolling or tapping the menu. That is a miss.
- **No lifestyle or people imagery** — no models, no kids, no families, no one who looks like my life. Just shoes on turf. That's fine for a sneakerhead, but I'm a mom with a Saturday morning and two opinions to manage.
- **No primary CTA button in the hero** — there's no "Shop Girls'" or "Shop Boys'" button anywhere I can tap directly. Category nav links are there but they're small text tabs, not confident CTAs.
- **No free shipping threshold visible** — as someone price-sensitive buying multiple pairs, I want to know if I'm close to free shipping. It's not mentioned anywhere above the fold.

## Recommendations

- **Add "Kids" as a first-position nav tab** — parents are a massive segment. Put "Kids" ahead of "Arch Fit" or at minimum make the truncated nav's first visible items parent-shopping-friendly; right now "BOBs" takes a slot where "Kids" belongs.
- **Swap hero or add a second hero slide for families** — a Mom + kid image with "Back to summer, sorted" or "Built for all-day play" would let me self-select without hunting. Even a second carousel slide would catch me before I bounce.
- **Add a free-shipping callout to the top strip or below the BOGO banner** — something like "Free shipping on $50+" gives me the budget context I need when I'm planning a two-kid haul.
- **Make the BOGO offer explicitly kids-friendly** — the banner says "BOGO 50% OFF Applied at Cart" but doesn't say what it applies to. A line like "Includes kids' styles" or a "Shop Kids' BOGO" CTA button would convert my curiosity into a click immediately.
