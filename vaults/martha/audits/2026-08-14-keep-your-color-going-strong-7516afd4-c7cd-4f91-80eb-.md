# Keep your color going strong 💓
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-14**
## Executive summary

- Sally Beauty. Again. This is at least the sixth or seventh one of these I've gotten in the last two weeks, and my inbox thumb is basically on autopilot to swipe past it at this point. I signed up for something hair-color related ages ago and I genuinely don't remember why — I have two kids, zero salon time, and I buy my drugstore box color once every few months when the roots get bad, not on a "COLORfest" cadence.
- To be fair, this one's actually laid out pretty well — clear $5-off-$20 deal, a legit BOGO on Wella hair color, and 40% off styling tools, all with real product images instead of the mismatched bait-and-switch mess from a couple weeks back. But none of it is for me right now. It's not marked, addressed, or personalized in any way — no "Hi Martha," no loyalty points balance, nothing that says they know who I am. I'm not clicking, not forwarding, just marking it read and moving on like I do every time this brand lands in my inbox.

## What's working

- The $5-off-$20 offer up top is clear and easy to understand at a glance.
- BOGO free on Wella hair color is a real, specific deal with actual product photos — not last time's Zotos/AGEbeautiful mismatch.
- 40% off styling tools section is clean, with real product shots (brush, flat iron) and prices implied by "Shop now" links.
- The rewards banner ("Shop Twice in August, Earn a $5 Reward in September") is at least a concrete, trackable loyalty mechanic.
- No obvious render bugs — images loaded, text isn't overlapping, layout is tidy.

## What's weak

- Zero personalization — no name, no "member since," no points balance, nothing that treats me like a known shopper.
- Totally irrelevant to my actual shopping habits — I'm not browsing hair color, I'm hunting for kids' Skechers on weekends.
- The redhead model and salon-quality styling content read as aspirational/pro, not "mom squeezing in a 5-minute scroll while the kids are occupied."
- This is the same brand cadence complaint every time — I've flagged this repeatedly and nothing's changed about frequency.
- "Welcome to your best color era" is vague filler copy before you even get to a real offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut send frequency drastically or let people pick a cadence — I'm getting fatigued and it's actively hurting open rates for even their better sends like this one.
- 2. Add basic personalization — first name, loyalty points balance, or "last purchased" callout — so it doesn't read as a cold blast.
- 3. Lead with the strongest, most concrete offer (BOGO Wella) instead of vague "best color era" copy.
- 4. If they have my purchase history, segment me out entirely if I've never bought hair color — stop sending broad blasts to lapsed/irrelevant subscribers.
