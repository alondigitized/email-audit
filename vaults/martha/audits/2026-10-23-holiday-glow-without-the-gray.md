# Holiday glow, without the gray
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-23**
## Executive summary

- Sally Beauty again. This is roughly the nineteenth send since mid-September, and I'm tired of it. After "A gift for the guys" on Oct 20, I got a gray-coverage hair color email. It's a nicely shot photo of a woman my age applying color at her roots, and honestly the model looks like me. But I'm shopping for a 5-year-old girl and a 9-year-old boy, and there isn't one kid thing in here.
- The offers are stacked and messy. The pink bar says $5 off $25 when I sign up, which is the same pitch as Oct 11, 17 and 19. The red banner has a $10 off $50 spooky code. Then the BOGO 50% off sits way down below the hero. I can't tell which deal is the real one.
- I wouldn't click. I'd skim it and mark it read. I might forward the BOGO to a friend who colors her own hair, but that's about it.

## What's working

- The hero photo is a real woman around my age with visible gray roots. It feels honest and not overly airbrushed.
- "100% gray coverage for holiday-ready hair" is a clear, specific promise.
- The BOGO 50% off has a clear end date (Oct 24) and shows the actual products.
- Klarna's "4 interest-free payments" helps with budgeting, and I like seeing free shipping at $40+.
- The big black "Shop now" button is easy to tap with a thumb.

## What's weak

- There are three different offers at the top (rewards $5, spooky code $10/$50, BOGO). I don't know which to chase.
- The spooky code is still running on Oct 23, and "SPOOKY" is stale next to a holiday hero.
- The best deal, the BOGO, is buried below a huge photo, so I'd have to scroll to find it.
- Nothing here is for kids or the family, and there's no sign they know I buy for my children.
- The "Shop now" button has no specific destination. It could go to a category page or the homepage.
- The fine print is a long block of tiny text. I'd have to zoom in to read it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one lead offer and make it the hero. The BOGO 50% is the strongest deal, so I'd put it above the fold with the end date, and drop the spooky banner.
- 2. Link the hero to a specific product or collection, such as "Shop gray coverage."
- 3. Cut back the frequency and stop repeating the $5 rewards pitch. If I'm still not a member after four sends, I'm not joining.
- 4. Segment me. If my purchases are all family or kid stuff, send me something relevant.
- 5. Put the price or the percent off in the button copy, e.g. "Get BOGO 50% off color."

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Sally Beauty, "Holiday glow, without the gray"

## 1. Technical Summary
The template is a table-based XHTML Transitional layout with a 499px mobile breakpoint and heavy third-party tracking. The QA data confirms a few real issues: malformed meta tags, an unresolved `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro in an ad pixel, a non-HTTPS pixel, and missing alt attributes. The HTML was truncated and 48 links were not probed, so compliance and continuity can't be fully verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved macro:** the VisualIQ pixel is `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&ao=883&aca=-11&si=-11&ci=-11&pi=-11&ad=-11…`.
  - `[CACHEBUSTER]` was not replaced at send time, so the pixel URL is literal. It is cacheable by clients and proxies, which can undercount impressions.
  - The repeated `-11` values (`aca`, `si`, `ci`, `pi`, `ad`) look like unpopulated placeholder IDs. This is an inference from the URL. Confirm it with the ad vendor.
- **Mixed content:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&…` loads over plain HTTP. Gmail's image proxy and Apple Mail Privacy Protection may block or rewrite it, so the Dotomi/Conversant sync is likely to fail silently.
- **Unverified links:** 48 tracking links on click-redirect domains were skipped, so destinations, redirect chains and 404s are unconfirmed.
- **Pixel stack:** there are at least three third-party pixels: `pxl.mon-trk.com` (Monetate), `myvisualiq.net` and `dotomi.com`. This adds external dependencies and privacy surface. Any of them failing doesn't break rendering, but each adds a request.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`. `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`. Both are inert. The charset declaration is ineffective, so rendering depends on the MIME header's charset.
- **Missing alt attributes (QA-confirmed):**
  - One image with an empty alt and empty `src`.
  - `003aZ00000s0I5yQAE` (`pxl.mon-trk.com/987bfe61-…/2327825/003aZ00000s0I5yQAE`).
  - `impression_pixel`.
  - `UCMController`.
  - Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"` so screen readers skip them. The empty-`src` image is a broken element.
- **Language:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` or `xml:lang` attribute, so screen readers can't select the correct language.
- **Font stack:** the `a{}` rule sets `font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif`, but `@font-face` defines only Open Sans. Founders Grotesk never loads, so links always use Helvetica.
- **Not assessable:** the source is truncated before the body, so alt text on content images, table `role="presentation"` and dark-mode handling can't be checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. The only unresolved token is the `[CACHEBUSTER]` ad macro in section 2. The body was not visible, so merge fields there are unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication:** `Authentication-Results` was not found. The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. This is a test-harness limitation, not a confirmed sender defect.
- **Unsubscribe and address:** the footer, the physical address, the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, and the unsubscribe link were not in the truncated source or QA data. They are unverified.
- **Sender domain:** `em.sallybeauty.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is good practice.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 48 click-tracking links were skipped, and the visible HTML shows no resolved destinations or UTM parameters. The email-to-site tracking chain can't be confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro substitution in the VisualIQ pixel and populate the `-11` placeholder IDs.
2. Switch the Dotomi pixel to `https://login.dotomi.com/...`. If the endpoint doesn't support HTTPS, drop it.
3. Add `alt=""` and explicit `width="1" height="1"` to all tracking pixels, and remove or fix the image with the empty `src`.
4. Correct the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge" />`.
5. Add `lang="en"` and `xml:lang="en"` to `<html>`.
6. Either load Founders Grotesk or remove it from the `a{}` font stack.
7. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and raw headers. That would let you verify `Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`, the footer, UTM parameters and the redirect destinations of the tracking links.
