# Last chance: These deals end today ⏰
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- Sally Beauty again. I've lost count of the Appiversary sends this month, so a "last chance" email lands like the boy who cried wolf. The 9/28 one was already telling me the deals were ending soon. The red-and-cream template is one I could draw from memory by now.
- Once I opened it, the layout was easy to follow: hero, three deal tiles, then a 25% off sitewide code. None of it is for me, though. It's hair care, clippers, jewelry and a nail box. I don't see a single kid's shoe, and I'm not getting the "Sally" treatment either, because nothing here treats me as a known customer.
- I wouldn't click. I'd skim it, note that FALL25 exists in case I ever need hair stuff, and archive it. I wouldn't forward it to anyone.

## What's working

- The deadline is clear. "Ends September 30" appears on every tile, and the "Ending soon" banner at the top backs it up.
- The offers are concrete: BOGO 50% off hair care, 30% off clippers, and 25% off sitewide with code FALL25. There's no guessing what I'd save.
- The dark-maroon tiles alternate their image side, so it's easy to scan on my phone. Each tile has one link.
- The Klarna line and the "Free shipping orders $40+" icon row are useful for a price-sensitive mom, since they make a bigger basket feel manageable.
- The fine print under the code is short and honest: not combinable, and it excludes Happy Beauty Co. and clearance.

## What's weak

- Nothing here is for me. There are no kids' items, no shoes and no family bundles, and my shopping is for a 5-year-old and a 9-year-old.
- I have no idea which "everyday must-haves" it means. The hero is vague and the "Shop now" button doesn't say what I'd be shopping for.
- Every CTA says "Shop now", including the small underlined text links. Those are tiny to tap on a phone.
- There's no mention of my rewards points or member pricing, even though the footer talks about Sally Beauty Rewards. I'm loyal to rewards programs, so that's a miss.
- The hero model takes up a huge amount of space and pushes the actual deals below the fold. The jewelry and box tiles have no product detail at all.
- Three "ending" cues (the banner, "Ends September 30" on each tile, and "Limited time only") feel like pressure, and it's the same pressure as the last few emails.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 25% off code and its deadline up into the hero, and shrink the model photo so the offer shows up in the first screen on mobile.
- 2. Personalize the tiles from what I've browsed or bought, and show my rewards balance up top.
- 3. Name a product in each tile's CTA, like "Shop BOGO hair care" or "Shop clippers", instead of repeating "Shop now".
- 4. Cut the campaign cadence. After weeks of Appiversary sends, a final-day email needs to say what's actually new, or it reads as noise.
- 5. Make the tap targets bigger with full-width buttons instead of small underlined links.
- **Subject Alt A:** `25% off ends tonight: code FALL25`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your last day for BOGO hair care 💇`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sitewide savings plus BOGO 50% off, gone at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Use FALL25 and your Rewards points before the sale closes.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible source is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with responsive media queries. It has some malformed head markup and three third-party tracking pixels with problems (an unresolved macro, an HTTP source, and a Salesforce-style ID in a URL). The HTML is truncated, so link, footer and header checks below are limited to what QA and the visible source show.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **51 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Unresolved macro in the ad pixel.** `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&ao=883&aca=-11&si=-11&ci=-11&pi=-11&ad=-11...` still contains the literal `[CACHEBUSTER]`. The `-11` values for `si`, `ci`, `pi`, `ad` and `aca` look like unpopulated defaults. Impression attribution from this pixel is likely broken or unreliable.
- **Non-HTTPS pixel.** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&...` loads over HTTP. It will be blocked or proxied by clients that upgrade or block mixed content, so it will not fire consistently.
- **Two images have empty `src` values.** QA flagged them as "missing alt text: : src:" with nothing after `src:`. If the `src` is really empty, these are broken image references, though the truncated output can't confirm that.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** Six images were flagged: the two empty-`src` images, the `pxl.mon-trk.com/.../003aZ00000s0I5yQAE` pixel, `impression_pixel` and `UCMController`. The three tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` and be hidden from assistive tech. The two empty-`src` images need a fix or removal.
- **Malformed meta tags.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=edge" />` have no `http-equiv`, so neither declares anything. The charset declaration is ineffective, and clients that rely on it may misrender non-ASCII characters.
- **No `lang` attribute.** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` or `xml:lang`, which affects screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Font stack.** `a{font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif}` references a font that is never loaded, so links always fall back to Helvetica. This is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Unresolved `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro** in the `myvisualiq.net` pixel (see section 2).
- **Salesforce-style ID in a pixel path.** `003aZ00000s0I5yQAE` matches the Salesforce Contact ID format (`003` prefix). Sending it in a third-party pixel URL exposes a CRM identifier to an ad-tech vendor. This is common practice but worth confirming with Sally Beauty's privacy policy.
- No visible first-name or other merge-token failures in the source provided.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Not verifiable.** The unsubscribe link, physical address footer, `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results are not in the truncated source or the QA data.
- **Sender alignment.** `em.sallybeauty.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain, which is the expected pattern, but authentication was not checked.
- **Third-party pixel disclosure.** Dotomi's `UCMController` is a cookie-sync and identity pixel. Its presence should be covered by the sender's privacy disclosures. This is not a confirmed violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not verifiable.** The link inventory is truncated and all 51 tracked links were skipped, so UTM parameters, landing page alignment and deal-page availability at send time could not be checked.
- The subject "These deals end today" implies a time-limited landing page. Confirm that the destinations still resolve after the deadline and don't show a generic or expired experience.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the `myvisualiq.net` pixel so `[CACHEBUSTER]` is substituted at send time, and populate or remove the `-11` placeholder params.
2. Serve the Dotomi pixel over `https://`, or drop it if it can't be.
3. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels. Fix or remove the two empty-`src` images.
4. Change the head metas to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge" />`. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
5. Review whether the Salesforce Contact ID should be passed to `pxl.mon-trk.com`. If it should, consider a hashed or opaque token instead.
6. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and headers to verify the unsubscribe link and headers, physical address, UTM tagging, and redirect resolution for the 51 tracked links.
