# More hair care, more savings
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-20**
## Executive summary

- Sally. Again. I'm averaging about one of these every two or three days at this point, so my first reflex was to swipe left — but the preview text actually caught me before I did. "Buy two select hair care products and get the third free" is a real offer with real dollar value, and BOGO language stops the thumb in a way "more savings" (the subject) absolutely does not.
- The email itself is clean, loads without drama, and the hero is instantly readable: BOGO deal, deadline, button. That's the whole job. The ion and Wella product tiles below give me something to actually browse. I appreciate the Klarna callout too — splitting a $60 hair order into four payments is the kind of thing my budget brain notices.
- That said, this email treats me like a stranger. No loyalty mention, no "hi Martha," no attempt to tell me which of these products fits my hair type. I'd probably click through to check what qualifies for the BOGO, but I wouldn't forward it — it reads like a mass blast, not a curated pick for me.

## What's working

- **BOGO hero is immediately legible.** "Buy two, get one free select hair care" plus "Ends July 25" plus a button — I got the offer in under two seconds.
- **Deadline creates real urgency.** July 25 is a specific, near date. It's not "limited time" vagueness; it's a Tuesday I can plan around.
- **Trending products section earns a scroll.** Specific products with names and images give me something to react to, not just a category blur.
- **Klarna callout lands right for me.** I'm price-sensitive — knowing I can split the checkout is a genuine nudge.
- **No clutter, no render issues.** The email loads clean, images appear, nothing overlaps. On my phone this is not a given.

## What's weak

- **Subject line phones it in.** "More hair care, more savings" could be from anyone selling anything. I've already seen "Last Day for 30% Off" and "Hair care for pool days" from this same sender — this subject has no personality and no specificity.
- **The model feels brand-shoot, not me.** She's very styled, very editorial. I shop in yoga pants during nap time. The vibe is aspirational in a way that creates distance, not connection.
- **No loyalty or rewards hook.** Sally has a rewards program. I'm a member. There's zero mention of points or member pricing in what I can see — wasted real estate on a price-sensitive subscriber.
- **"Select" qualifier is doing quiet damage.** The BOGO is on "select" items. I know from experience that word can mean "basically nothing I actually want." One line clarifying the scope — "hundreds of items qualify" — would neutralize the skepticism.
- **Cadence fatigue is real.** Per my inbox, this is roughly the eighth or ninth email from them in about three weeks. The offers are rotating, sure, but the volume is pushing into "I'll just unsubscribe" territory.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put the BOGO in the subject line.** The offer is good enough to lead with — bury it in a vague subject and you're fighting the inbox cold open. "Buy 2, Get 1 Free on Hair Care — Ends July 25" is the email; that should be the subject.
- 2. **Add a loyalty / member hook.** Even one line — "Beauty Club members: double points on qualifying items" — makes me feel seen and gives me a second reason to click.
- 3. **Swap the model.** A shot that reads more real-woman, less salon-photoshoot would land better for a 34-year-old who shops for herself after she's handled everything else.
- 4. **Reassure me on "select."** Tell me how many products qualify, or surface one anchor product ("including all ion shampoos") so I'm not dreading a bait-and-switch when I click through.
- 5. **Give the trending products context.** "Best for color-treated hair" or "great on fine/curly/thick" under each tile gives me a reason to care about a specific product instead of treating them as interchangeable noise.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Buy 2, Get 1 Free on Hair Care — Ends July 25`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your BOGO is here: free hair care when you grab two`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop ion, Wella & more — hundreds of products qualify, offer ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stock up before the deal's gone — July 25 is your last day`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**FAIL — `Founders Grotesk` referenced but never defined**

```css

```

No `@font-face` block for `Founders Grotesk` exists in the `<head>`. All anchor text silently falls back to Helvetica.

**WARN — HTTP pixel (mixed content, likely blocked)**

```

Plaintext HTTP. Gmail, Outlook.com, and Apple Mail proxy or block non-HTTPS image loads. This pixel will not fire for a significant portion of recipients.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

```

`[CACHEBUSTER]` is an unreplaced template variable. The impression pixel fires with a literal bracket string, defeating cache-busting and likely corrupting the impression count in VisualIQ reporting.
