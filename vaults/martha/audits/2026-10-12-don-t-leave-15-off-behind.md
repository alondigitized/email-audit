# Don’t leave 15% off behind
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- Sally Beauty again. This is the thirteenth or so send since mid-September, and I'm tired. The subject says "don't leave 15% off behind," but the hero says "Last chance… Ends today!" and I can't tell what I'd be leaving behind. The 15% only applies if I buy online and pick up in store. I'm a mom of two buying for a 5-year-old and a 9-year-old, so hair developer and magenta hair color aren't for me.
- The "Ends today" urgency is credible, and the offer is easy to read: 15%, no minimum. But the products are salon-pro items like a 20 volume developer and a color sealer packette. Nothing here looks like my weekend browsing.
- Would I click? Probably not. I'd skim it, notice the $5-off-$25 rewards banner at the top, and swipe it away. I wouldn't forward it. My friends don't mix their own hair color.

## What's working

- "Last chance" and "Ends today!" give me a real deadline, and I'd believe it.
- "No minimum purchase required" is a clear, honest line. I hate hidden thresholds.
- The hero is huge, with a big black "Shop now" button. It's easy to tap with a thumb.
- The $5 off $25 rewards bar at the top is the best offer in the whole email. I like rewards programs.
- The Klarna "4 interest-free payments" strip and the free shipping at $40+ icon row help a price-sensitive shopper like me.

## What's weak

- The hero is a wall of red and pink with no product, no person and nothing that says "this is for you."
- Buy-online-pick-up-in-store is a limited offer. The small "*Exclusions apply" and the long fine print make me wonder what's actually excluded.
- All four products are pro hair color supplies. Nothing for kids, and nothing in my everyday routine.
- The four "Shop now" links under the products are tiny underlined text. They're hard to tap on a phone.
- Three competing offers sit at the top: the rewards signup, the 2-hour delivery banner and the BOPIS banner. The 15% gets muddled, and the 2-hour delivery ($35 minimum) seems to contradict "no minimum."
- This is the same cadence as 10/11 ("Get $5 off $25 when you join") and 10/9 ("Your Halloween look starts here"). I feel nagged.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the offer plain in the hero: "15% off, pick up in store today." Put the pickup condition in the headline so I know what I'm getting before I tap.
- 2. Show real products that sell. Use best-sellers or items from my past browsing instead of random salon-pro SKUs, and add prices and star ratings.
- 3. Pick one banner at the top, not three. Cut the rewards, delivery and BOPIS bars down to one message.
- 4. Make the product links full-width buttons.
- 5. Space out the sends. I'd rather get two good emails a week than six repeats.
- **Subject Alt A:** `15% off pickup ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 15% off, no minimum`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Order online, grab it in store today. No minimum, ends at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Skip the shipping wait. Pick up your order in store and save 15%.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible source and QA data show no hard failures, but they do show an unresolved macro in a third-party pixel (`r=[CACHEBUSTER]`), a user identifier in image URLs, one non-HTTPS pixel, and several tracking images without `alt`. The HTML is truncated and no headers were supplied, so link, compliance and UTM checks are largely unverifiable.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved macro:** `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&ao=883…`. The literal `[CACHEBUSTER]` was never substituted. Without a per-send value, clients and proxies can cache the pixel, so impressions are undercounted or deduplicated.
- **Mixed content:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990…` is loaded over HTTP. Some clients block or rewrite it, and the Dotomi cookie-sync/UCM call won't fire reliably.
- **Unverified redirects:** 50 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so redirect chains, destination status codes and link expiry are unconfirmed.
- **Heavy third-party tracking stack:** Evergage/Salesforce Interaction Studio, `pxl.mon-trk.com`, VisualIQ and Dotomi are all present. Each adds a remote request and a point of failure.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt`:** QA flagged seven images.
  - The four Evergage dynamic-content images, `…/campaign/tOkMy/Bax31/{1..4}/summary.png`. These are not clearly 1×1 tracking pixels. If they carry product or recommendation content, that content is invisible to screen readers and to image-blocked clients.
  - The three tracking pixels: `003aZ00000s0I5yQAE`, `impression_pixel` and `UCMController`. Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` so they are treated as decorative.
- **Malformed head metas (visible in source):**
  - `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`.
  - `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`.
  - Neither tag does anything as written, so the charset declaration is not in effect. The `Don’t` curly apostrophe in the subject may then depend on header-level charset only.
- **Image-dependent content:** the Evergage images are dynamic and loaded remotely, so with images blocked the section can render empty. A fallback or alt text is needed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Identifier in URL:** the Evergage image URLs include `?userId=martha.st…` (the QA snippet is truncated). This looks like an identifier derived from the recipient's email or name, sent as a plain query parameter to a third-party host. It is a privacy exposure through logs, proxies and referrers. Confirm what the full value is.
- **Unresolved token:** `[CACHEBUSTER]` is the only unresolved macro confirmed (see section 2). No other `{{ }}`, `%%…%%` or `[…]` tokens were visible in the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- No issues found in the supplied data, but this is unverified rather than confirmed. The footer, unsubscribe link, physical address and `List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers were not in the truncated source. SPF, DKIM and DMARC results were not provided. The sender `sallybeauty@em.sallybeauty.com` is a subdomain of the brand's own domain, which is consistent with aligned authentication.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No issues found in the supplied data. The 50 tracking links were not resolved, so UTM parameters, destination URLs and offer alignment with the "15% off" subject could not be checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Have the ESP/ad-server integration substitute `[CACHEBUSTER]` at send time (or drop the VisualIQ pixel if it isn't needed).
2. Change `http://login.dotomi.com/…` to `https://`, or remove it if Dotomi no longer needs it.
3. Add `alt=""` and explicit 1×1 dimensions to the three tracking pixels.
4. Give the four Evergage `summary.png` images descriptive `alt` text if they carry content.
5. Replace the readable `userId=` value with an opaque hashed or token ID, and confirm the full value isn't an email local part.
6. Fix the head tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge" />`.
7. Re-run the audit with the full HTML, raw headers (`Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`) and resolved tracking-link destinations so sections 5 and 6 can be checked.
8. Review the QA tooling. It reports a "100% pass, 0 issues, 0 warnings" summary while listing eight `[WARN]` items, so the summary count is inconsistent with the findings.
