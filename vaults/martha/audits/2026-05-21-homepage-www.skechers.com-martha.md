# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so I'm opening Skechers and immediately it's all flag vibes — big red SKECHERS logo, stars everywhere, a couple of very put-together adults who do not have ketchup on their shoes. It's clearly a Memorial Day push and the BOGO deal at the top is the first thing I actually noticed. But if I'm being real, I came here to find something for my daughter and maybe grab a pair of Slip-ins for myself, and this hero is giving me zero direction toward any of that.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF sticky banner** — This is the first thing I see and it's genuinely appealing. If I'm buying shoes for two kids, BOGO is made for me even if the page doesn't know that.
- **Members-only reward strip** — "Now until 5/25! Members Only – Earn a $5 Reward on $75+" has a deadline, a dollar amount, and a login prompt. That's a real hook and it told me there's a loyalty program in seconds.
- **Category shortcut bar** — Slip-ins, Sandals, Arch Fit, BOBs are one tap away without opening the full menu. I can see myself tapping Sandals immediately.
- **Patriotic timing** — The Red, White & Blue Collection is on-season. It doesn't feel like a stale leftover promotion.

## What's weak

- **No shop button above the fold** — The hero is just a big branded image. I can't tap anything on it. Where do I go? It doesn't invite action.
- **Zero kids' content visible** — Kids isn't in the visible category bar (it's cut off), the hero has no kids or family, nothing above the fold says "we also have shoes for your 5-year-old." That's my primary mission and I'm already feeling like I need to dig.
- **Hero models don't represent me** — A guy in a denim jacket and a woman in a navy sundress are cute, but they're not me. They're not doing anything I relate to on a Sunday afternoon with two kids.
- **Loyalty benefit is vague without clicking** — "Earn a $5 Reward on $75+" tells me the threshold but I have to click "details" to know what the reward actually applies to. On mobile, that's one extra tap I may not take.

## Recommendations

- **Add a tappable CTA button to the hero** — Even a simple "Shop the Collection →" or "Shop Now" overlay on the Red White Blue hero would give me somewhere to go. Right now the hero is a dead zone.
- **Surface a Kids shortcut in the category bar** — Kids is almost certainly cut off at the right side of the nav bar. Move it left, or add a "Shop for Kids" secondary link below the category strip. That's where I'm headed anyway.
- **Swap one hero slot for a family lifestyle image** — Run an A/B: a mom with kids in coordinating patriotic Skechers against the current adult couple. You'd convert people like me who need permission to think "this brand is for my family."
- **Make the BOGO banner more specific** — "BOGO 50% OFF — Kids' shoes included" or "Stock up for summer" would make that deal feel personal rather than generic.
