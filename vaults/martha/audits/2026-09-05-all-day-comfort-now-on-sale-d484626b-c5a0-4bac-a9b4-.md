- There is genuinely nothing here for my daughter's light-up sneakers or my son's lace-ups — I have to scroll all the way to the bottom, past the app download and Afterpay/Klarna blocks, to even find a tiny "Kids Clothing" text link buried in a list of category links. ## What's working

- "20% off regular priced styles" for Plus members is a concrete, real number sitting right at the top, no hunting required.
- Arch Fit and Cozy Fit sections are legible with clear product shots and their own "Shop" CTAs — easy to scan even half-distracted.
- The Klarna/Afterpay and curbside pickup icons at the bottom are genuinely useful info for a budget-conscious mom, even if buried.
- "Hi, Martha" plus my 1000 points again — annoying that it's repetitive across emails, but I'll admit it keeps registering that I'm a known member.

## What's weak

- Zero kids' product presence — no girls' velcro/light-up shoes, no boys' lace-up sneakers, nothing pink/sparkle or blue/durable, just "Kids Clothing" as a plain text link at the very bottom.
- "All Day Comfort" hero is 100% adult-lifestyle imagery — a guy on a stool, a woman doing a leg-kick pose — nothing that signals this is relevant to shopping for a family.
- The "Complete Your Look" apparel section (a sweatshirt on an AI-looking model) is pure scroll-filler that pushes me further from any offer I'd actually act on.
- It's a long, long scroll — Arch Fit, Cozy Fit, Slip-ins, apparel, markdowns — before I hit anything resembling a decision point, and I'm doing this one-handed while my kids are occupied for maybe five minutes.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a kids' shoe rail (girls' light-up/velcro, boys' lace-up) directly under the hero or right after the 20% banner — I'm the one buying for two growing kids constantly, and right now that's an afterthought link at the footer.
- 2. Cut the apparel "Complete Your Look" block entirely for this send — it's dead weight between the shoe offer and the real markdowns.
- 3. Lead with the specific markdown prices (I do see $79.99, $62.00, etc. near the bottom) higher up — those are the most concrete numbers in the whole email and they're buried.
- 4. Vary the "Hi, Martha / 1000 points / 20% off" template — I've seen this same opener structure three sends running this week and it's losing its impact.
