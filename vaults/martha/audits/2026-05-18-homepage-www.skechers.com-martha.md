# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Skechers is clearly pushing their Hands Free Slip-ins line hard right now, and the homepage definitely has that "big promo energy" — BOGO is front and center, the members strip is right there. It looks like a well-put-together mobile page. But I opened this to find shoes for my kids, and the entire hero is two adults posing near a pool — I had to scroll to the very bottom of the screen to even see the Kids' tab.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF banner** — this is the first thing my eye goes to. As a mom buying two pairs at a time for growing kids, buy-one-get-one is actually useful, not just noise.
- **Members Only reward strip** — "Earn a $5 Reward on $75+" with a login link is right there, no scrolling required. Price-sensitive shoppers like me notice this immediately.
- **"Recently Viewed" tab** — that's genuinely useful for a weekend browse session. I look something up Monday, come back Saturday, that tab saves me time.
- **"Kids'" tab visible in category nav** — it's at the bottom of the screen, but it IS there, and it's one tap. That's the minimum for me to not immediately bounce.
- **Clean mobile layout** — no pop-ups, no intrusive modals visible in the screenshot, text is large enough to read without pinching. That matters on a phone.

## What's weak

- **The entire hero is adult Slip-ins with zero kids context** — I came here for my daughter's shoes and my son's sneakers. The hero image is a man sitting on a ledge and a woman in a floral dress near a pool. No kids anywhere. It feels like I walked into the wrong store.
- **"SHOP NOW" is the most generic CTA possible** — it goes to adult Slip-ins. There's no secondary CTA for Kids' or even a split "Shop for the Family" angle that would speak to me.
- **No free shipping signal above the fold** — I always check. If Skechers has a free shipping threshold, it's not visible here. That's a missed conversion nudge.
- **The members reward requires me to do math** — "$5 Reward on $75+" is fine, but I don't know my current balance, I'd have to log in, and there's no "you're X away from your next reward" hook that would actually motivate me.
- **No trust signals** — no star ratings, no review counts, no "free returns" badge. For footwear where fit is everything, I need that before I'll tap anything.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop for the Family" or split-hero** — even a two-column mini-banner below the main hero ("Kids' New Arrivals | Shop Now") would immediately tell moms like me this page has what they came for.
- **Make the BOGO feel like a family deal** — change the framing from "BOGO Slip-ins" to "BOGO across all styles — stock up for the whole family." That's the same discount, targeted directly at me.
- **Show free shipping threshold in the promo bar** — replace or supplement "BOGO 50% OFF Applied at Cart details" with something like "BOGO 50% OFF + Free Shipping on $X+." One bar, two conversion signals.
- **Drop a trust strip below the hero** — a single row: ⭐ 4.8M+ Reviews | Free Returns | Secure Checkout. Costs zero screen real estate and directly addresses my hesitation before tapping to a product page.
