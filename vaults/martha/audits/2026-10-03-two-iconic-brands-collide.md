# Two iconic brands collide
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-03**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction was "John Deere? Skechers?" The subject made me curious, but that's about as far as it got. Then I scrolled and saw a men's work-boot collab, hiking boots and a guy with a backpack on a rock. The only thing in this email for me is the banner at the very top, and that banner is the best part.
- I do like seeing my 1000 points and the "$5 reward, REDEEM" line again, and BOGO 50% off is the deal I wanted when I saw it yesterday ("BOGO is back...for now," 10/2). But the email never connects that deal to the shoes I'm shopping for. Nothing here is for a 5-year-old who wants light-up velcro, or a 9-year-old who destroys the soles of his sneakers. "Kids" is one gray text link near the bottom.
- Would I click? Probably only the "Shop now" in the banner, and only because BOGO works for two kids. I wouldn't forward it. A friend who wants a durable boot for her husband might care, but I'd be sending the offer, not the email. Mostly I'd mark it read.

## What's working

- The BOGO 50% / 20% banner is the first thing I see, and the "Shop now" button is clear.
- My name, "Member," 1000 points and the $5 reward are right at the top. It feels like they know me.
- The hero photo is polished, and the John Deere yellow and green really pops.
- The layout is clean on my phone, with big tap targets on the colored "Shop..." buttons.
- "Weatherproof, wear-tested" and "slip-in in seconds" are practical angles that fit a busy mom, even if the shoes aren't for my kids.

## What's weak

- Nothing for kids. No velcro, no light-up, no pink or sparkle, no blue or black sneakers. The models and shoes all lean adult and outdoorsy.
- The subject and preview don't mention BOGO or my points, so the best offer is buried below the inbox line.
- I can't tell which shoes the BOGO covers. The fine print says "select regular-priced shoes" and "Thru 10/7," and it's tiny and hard to read on my phone.
- The John Deere collab takes up most of the email, and the product grid links to a collection, not to specific shoes.
- This is the fifth or sixth promo in a week (BOGO 10/2, monthly summary 10/1, Fall Haul 9/29). It's the same banner and the same points every time.
- Klarna/Afterpay and "Sign Up for Texts & Get 20% Off" are small and look like an afterthought. I'm already a member, so the 20% text offer is confusing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a kids' module right under the banner, with two tiles: "Girls' light-up velcro" and "Boys' lace-up sneakers," both marked BOGO-eligible. Two kids is exactly how BOGO makes sense for me.
- 2. Lead the subject and preheader with the offer and my member status, not the brand collab.
- 3. Make the deadline bigger ("Ends 10/7") and say which shoes are included, instead of leaving it in the footer.
- 4. Link to specific hero products (price, reviews, sizes) instead of to collections.
- 5. Drop the "Sign up for texts, get 20% off" block for existing members.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed broken-experience defect: an empty greeting slot that renders "Hi ,". The source also has a malformed tracking-pixel URL, non-HTTPS image sources, and missing alt text. The HTML provided is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and click-through URLs could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed pixel URL (confirmed in source):** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…">` has no scheme. It resolves as a relative path and will 404 or never fire, so the Return Path/Validity deliverability pixel is lost.
- **Mixed content:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?...` is a tracking pixel over plain HTTP and may be blocked by clients.
- **Unprobed links:** 30 tracking and click-redirect links were skipped by the QA probe (e.g. `click.emails.skechers.com/...`). Destination status is unverified.
- **Stacked third-party pixels:** Four `beacon.krxd.net` pixels sit alongside the SFMC open pixel (`click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx?...`) and the ink1000 and Return Path pixels. This is a lot of third-party requests and increases the chance of blocking or slow loads. The pixels are hidden with `display:none`, and Gmail may strip or proxy them.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** QA flags two images:
  - `o.gif` (the ink1000 pixel has `alt=""`, which is acceptable for a pixel).
  - `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` (this is a content image, so the missing alt matters if it carries content).
- **Non-HTTPS content image:** `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-…png` may be blocked or show a mixed-content warning. Move it to HTTPS.
- **Ineffective meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` lack `http-equiv`, so neither declares anything. The charset falls back to the HTTP header or client default, which risks encoding glitches on `®` and similar characters.
- **Zoom restrictions:** `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta and `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` block pinch-zoom and text scaling, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Missing semantics:** No `lang` attribute is visible on `<html>`, and no `role="presentation"` is visible on the layout tables (`<table class="widthFull" width="640" …>`).
- **Hidden-content CSS:** The `.hidden`/`.hideme`/`.mobile-hidden` classes are defined repeatedly across media queries, which is redundant rather than broken.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** The greeting renders as "Hi ," because the first-name field is blank. This is a merge-field failure with no fallback value.
- **Hashed identifiers in URLs:** The krxd URLs carry `partner_uid=adf27fee67323d52536b18110294bf793c265b830082caba4b1a32664832189a` (a SHA-256 hash, likely of the email address) and `partner_uid=122277199` (a subscriber ID). The tokens resolved correctly, so this is not a merge failure. It is user-level data going to a third party.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unverifiable:** The truncated HTML cuts off before the footer, so the physical postal address, unsubscribe link and any `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers cannot be confirmed. No raw headers were supplied for SPF, DKIM or DMARC.
- **Sender:** `no-reply@emails.skechers.com` is a subdomain sender, and its alignment is unverified. A no-reply From address is not itself a CAN-SPAM violation.
- **Third-party tracking disclosure:** Hashed-email and subscriber-ID sharing with `beacon.krxd.net` and `ink1000.com` may need coverage in the privacy policy. This depends on jurisdiction and cannot be determined from the source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not verifiable:** All 30 click links go through the `click.emails.skechers.com` redirect and were skipped by the probe. Their final landing URLs and UTM parameters are not visible in the supplied source.
- **Campaign ID:** The pixels carry `campaignid=MKG_US_NA_M_CATEGORY_GENDER_EN_10032026` and `mi_ecmp=MKG_US_NA_M_CATEGORY_GENDER_EN_10032026`, which are consistent with each other. Whether this matches `utm_campaign` on the landing pages is unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a fallback to the first-name merge field (e.g. `Hi there,`) or suppress the greeting when it is empty. This is the only QA FAIL.
2. Fix the Return Path pixel to `https://pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=…`.
3. Switch `http://www.ink1000.com/...` and `http://image.emails.skechers.com/...` to HTTPS. Confirm the image host supports it.
4. Add meaningful alt text to `dde00662-…png`. Keep `alt=""` on tracking pixels.
5. Correct the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
6. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` where possible. Add `lang="en"` and `role="presentation"` to the layout tables.
7. Re-run the audit on the full HTML and raw headers to check the unsubscribe link, postal address, `List-Unsubscribe` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Manually verify the redirect destinations and UTM parameters.
8. The QA summary reads "1 issue(s), 0 warning(s)" while listing 4 WARN items. The summary counter looks inconsistent and is worth checking in the QA script.
