# It's your month — member deals inside
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-18**
## Executive summary

- "Your month" caught me mid-scroll — I'm a Skechers Plus member and when a brand tells me it's *my* month and leads with a BOGO, that's enough to get me in. The 50% off buy-one-get-one is the main event and it does land, visually, right where I need it to. The $250 gift card sweepstakes is a soft bonus — I'll enter that in about 10 seconds with zero expectations, but I appreciate the attempt to make this feel like more than a sale blast.
- That said: I'm looking at broken JSON in the preview text. *Again.* This is the third or fourth time this month with Skechers. At this point I've basically trained myself to ignore it, which is a terrible thing for a brand to normalize. At some point I'm going to assume the whole email is broken and not bother.
- The bigger miss for me is the layout priority. I'm primarily shopping for a 5-year-old and a 9-year-old, and the Kids section lives below Women's and Men's. I'll click it, but I shouldn't have to scroll to feel seen. Would I forward it? Only to a friend who needs shoes for herself — the family angle just isn't front and center enough to share.

## What's working

- The BOGO 50% + 20% off hierarchy is clean and legible — I didn't have to squint or re-read the offer
- "Skechers Plus Members" front and center makes this feel like a member send, not a mass blast
- The $250 gift card sweepstakes adds perceived value without diluting the discount message
- SHOP KIDS' button exists and is visible — appreciated, even if I had to scroll for it
- Clean render — no broken images, no layout collapse, text is readable at phone-thumb size

## What's weak

- Broken JSON preview text, *again* — this is the fourth consecutive Skechers email I've opened this month with schema garbage leaking into the preheader
- "SHOP TODAY" as the primary CTA is filler — no category, no product noun, doesn't tell me what I'm shopping *for*
- The asterisk on "1 PAIR\*" makes me nervous; I always have to scroll to the fine print to figure out what's excluded before I commit
- No deadline visible anywhere — "Member Appreciation Month" tells me when, not *until when*, and urgency without an end date is just vibes
- The women's hero model is styled fashion-forward and aspirational; she's not exactly chasing a kid through Target, which is where my head is when I'm shoe shopping

## Recommendations

- 1. **Move SHOP KIDS' up alongside SHOP WOMEN'S** — or add a "Shop the whole family" CTA directly under the BOGO headline; that's where a dual-household shopper like me would click
- 2. **Add an explicit deadline to the BOGO** — "Ends June 30" or "Through July 4th" turns a passive browse into a timed decision
- 3. **Fix the preview text** — this has leaked JSON in every Skechers email I've opened this month; it's a brand hygiene issue that compounds with cadence (the 6th email in 18 days does not need to also have broken metadata)
- 4. **Replace "SHOP TODAY" with a noun** — "Shop Kids' Deals" or "Shop the BOGO" is still short and has a reason to exist
- **Subject Alt A:** `BOGO 50% off this month — plus enter for $250`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Plus perks: buy one, get one half off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop women's, men's & kids through [date] — BOGO 50% off for Plus members.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `BOGO deal live now + $250 gift card giveaway. Ends soon.`

## Technical Audit — Skechers PLUS "It's your month" (06/18/2026)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email has two structural HTML defects (malformed `<meta>` tags) and a systemic HTTP-vs-HTTPS problem across all hosted image assets, both of which will cause rendering failures in security-strict clients. No merge token blowouts or unsubscribe failures are detectable in the provided excerpt, but the source is truncated.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Return Path pixel has no protocol prefix — broken URL**
```html
<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1dbf0dcef79cbad82c12fe25342701e4499" />
```
Missing `https://` makes this a relative path. The pixel will 404 in all clients, silently breaking inbox monitoring/reputation scoring.

**Third-party DMP sync beacons (Kraxel/Salesforce)**
Three `beacon.krxd.net` pixels fire on open, including a user-match pixel with a hashed subscriber ID:
```
partner_uid=adf27fee67323d52536b18110294bf793c265b830082caba4b1a32664832189a
```
This is data-broker syncing. Confirm legal/privacy sign-off is current, particularly for any EU/CA recipients given GDPR/CCPA scope.

**26 click-tracked links** — all route through `click.emails.skechers.com`. QA prober correctly skipped HTTP resolution. No dead-link issues confirmed, but UTM carrythrough cannot be verified without resolving destinations (see §6).

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Systemic HTTP image hosting — will break in Gmail, Apple Mail (MPP), and enterprise clients**

All 10+ production images are served over `http://` from `image.emails.skechers.com`:
```
http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-...png  ← Skechers logo
http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/11/49468f73-...png
http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/11/0ec56c9f-...png  ← App Store badge
... (7 more)
```
Gmail blocks mixed-content images. Apple Mail's Privacy Protection pre-fetches via HTTPS proxy — HTTP assets time out and render broken. This is the highest-severity rendering defect in the email.

**Third-party tracker also HTTP**
```
http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif
```
Same breakage vector, plus the pixel lacks `alt=""`.

**Malformed `<meta>` tags — missing `http-equiv` attribute**
```html
<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />       <!-- missing http-equiv="Content-Type" -->
<meta content="IE=Edge" />                          <!-- missing http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" -->
```
Without `http-equiv`, both tags are no-ops. Charset declaration failure can cause mojibake in Outlook on Windows; IE compatibility hint is cosmetic but should still be well-formed.

**`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` applied globally**
```css
* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; ... }
```
Disables user font-size control on iOS/Android. Fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text). Use `100%` instead of `none`.

**Missing alt text**
- `o.gif` (ink1000 tracker): decorative, `alt=""` acceptable but currently absent
- `49468f73-4651-4af3-bea2-61d1ae5db486.png`: content image with no alt — screen readers get nothing

### 5. Compliance

**Authentication** — Sending domain `emails.skechers.com` is an ESP subdomain of `skechers.com`. SPF/DKIM alignment depends on ESP configuration (Salesforce Marketing Cloud in this case). The Return Path pixel (`pixel.app.returnpath.net`) implies Validity inbox monitoring is intended — the broken protocol (§2) means reputation data is not being collected for this send.

If the sweepstakes has material terms (which it almost certainly does), verify that "No purchase necessary" / odds language appears in the email body per FTC guidelines.
