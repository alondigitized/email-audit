- Yep, right on cue — another adult-focused lifestyle email with a man in a graffiti alley and a woman in leggings, this time dressed up as "Long Weekend Essentials." My honest first reaction scrolling through: comfy walking shoes for me, sale codes I could actually use, but literally zero acknowledgment that I have a 5-year-old and a 9-year-old who also need shoes before summer's out. Same pattern I flagged on 8/13 and 8/15 — new banner, same all-adult cast.
- That said, I'll admit the 20% off sitewide banner up top with a real code (SKPLUS) caught my eye before I even hit the hero, and the "All-Day Comfort" pink slip-on actually looks like something I'd wear on a school-pickup day. So this isn't a total miss for me personally — it's just a miss for what I actually came to Skechers.com to shop for on a weekend.
- I'd skim it, maybe tap through to check my Plus discount, but I'm not forwarding this to anyone and I'm definitely not scrolling to "Kids" — there's no kids' product shown, just a text link buried in the footer maze.

## What's working

- The 20% off SKPLUS code banner at the very top is clear, has a real code, and a deadline (valid thru 8/25) — I know exactly what to do with that.
- "All-Day Comfort" pink slip-on shoe is genuinely my style and price point — comfort-first, not another chunky trail sneaker.
- Multiple distinct offers (20% sitewide, 25% off bags & apparel) with visible CTA buttons — no hunting required.
- Product benefit callouts like "Hands Free Slip-ins" speak to a mom-on-the-go life, even if the model doesn't.

## What's weak

- Both hero models are adult, gym/lifestyle styled — no sign of my actual shopping mission (kids' shoes) anywhere above the fold.
- "Kids" is just one plain text link buried in a gray link list near the footer, same treatment as "Sale" or "New Arrivals" — no product photo, no color/feature callout for my son or daughter.
- No family bundle or multi-pair promo code despite the "family sale" language in the fine print at the bottom — that's a missed connector to how I actually shop (buying 2+ pairs at once).
- This is basically the same seasonal-transition beat as "Fall's Stepping Up" (8/13) — a new subject line on an unchanged all-adult formula.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap one of the two adult lifestyle hero shots for a kids' shoe pairing (girl's light-up/velcro sneaker + boy's lace-up) — even a small third panel would signal "we thought about your kids too."
- 2. Turn the plain-text "Kids" footer link into an actual product tile with an image, like Cool Casuals and Airport Must-Haves get.
- 3. Call out a family/multi-pair bundle explicitly — "Buy 2 kids' pairs, save an extra X%" — ties the 25% off apparel/bags banner to something I'd actually stack a cart for.
- 4. Lead the subject line with the offer, not the vague "essentials" theme, since the 20% sitewide code is the strongest hook in the email.
