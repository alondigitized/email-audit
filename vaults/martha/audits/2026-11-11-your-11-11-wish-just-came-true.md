# Your 11:11 wish just came true
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- Okay, 30% off sitewide for members is the best deal I've seen from Skechers in weeks. It's a real number, it's huge on the black hero, and "Hi, Martha" with my 1000 points is right at the top. The 11:11 wish thing is cute and not annoying. That's more than I can say for the last three emails, which all had me comparing overlapping deals. This time there's one offer and one button.
- Then I scroll and it turns into a shoe catalog. All four tiles are adult sneakers, and I'm shopping for a 5-year-old girl and a 9-year-old boy. The kids' size buttons are way down at the bottom, and there's nothing in pink, sparkle, light-up or velcro. I'd probably click through and search kids myself, because 30% off the whole site is worth the tap. I wouldn't forward it, but I'd save it for the weekend sitting-down-with-my-phone session.
- The fine print is where I get cautious. It says "select regular-priced items" and "excludes website premieres, sale styles", which sounds like it doesn't match "sitewide". The hero says "auto-applied at cart", so I'd check my cart before I trusted it.

## What's working

- The giant "30% OFF" with "Sitewide for Members" is impossible to miss, and it's the one clear offer.
- "Hi, Martha," plus 1000 points and a "REDEEM" link makes me feel like a known member.
- "Discount auto-applied at cart. Valid online 11/11/26 only." means no code to hunt for, and the one-day deadline feels real.
- The kids' sizes are split into Little Kids (4-8) and Big Kids (8-10), which lines up with my two kids.
- Klarna/Afterpay and the app download are helpful for a mom checking out one-handed.

## What's weak

- The four hero tiles are all adult sneakers, so nothing in the email speaks to a 5-year-old girl or a 9-year-old boy.
- Kids' sizes are about two-thirds of the way down, after the women's and men's rows. I'd have to scroll a lot before I got to my part.
- The "Make your wish count" tiles ("Slip-ins", "Cozy Fit") are generic and don't show prices or what 30% off actually costs.
- "Sitewide" in the hero clashes with the fine print excluding sale styles and premieres. That makes it feel a little bait-and-switch.
- It's the third 30% off in a week, after the 11/05 Holiday Checklist and the 11/10 boots deal. The urgency doesn't land as hard anymore.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a kids' row right under the hero with two tiles, one for girls (velcro, light-up) and one for boys (lace-up, durable), and move the kids' sizes up.
- 2. Name the exclusions in one line right under the button, so "sitewide" doesn't feel like a trick.
- 3. Put a sample price on the product tiles, like "now $55", so I can see the savings.
- 4. Mention a family bundle or an extra reward for kids' orders.
- 5. Make the Shop Now button link to a kids' landing page for members who have bought kids' shoes before.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Email shows one confirmed personalization failure (empty greeting slot, "Hi ,") and several tracking-pixel defects. These include a scheme-less Return Path pixel URL and two non-HTTPS, alt-less images. The HTML source is truncated and the Authentication-Results header is absent, so compliance and link-level checks are only partly verifiable.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed pixel URL (confirmed in source):** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…">` has no `https://` scheme. It resolves as a relative path, so the Return Path (Validity) seed or tracking pixel will not load. Any deliverability or inbox-placement measurement tied to it is lost.
- **Insecure tracking image:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=…&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_SITE_FULL_EN_11112026`. It is plain HTTP, so it may be blocked or trigger mixed-content handling, and it silently loses opens.
- **Stacked third-party beacons:** Five 1×1 images sit in a `display:none` wrapper: the SFMC open pixel (`click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx`), three `beacon.krxd.net` calls, and the Return Path pixel. Many clients strip `display:none` content, so open counts will be unreliable.
- **Hashed identifier in a third-party URL:** `beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif?partner=…_sha25&partner_uid=adf27fee…189a` passes a SHA-256 value to a third party. The `partner=…_sha25` label looks truncated or mistyped, and the next beacon's `_subid` matches the 122277199 subscriber ID. If the partner key is wrong, the user-match call may fail.
- **Click links:** 54 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe (`click.emails.skechers.com` redirect domain), so destination status is unverified. Not a confirmed defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA + source):**
  - `o.gif` (ink1000) has no `alt` attribute.
  - `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` (`http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/…`) also has no `alt`. It is hosted on `http://`, so it is likely a content image, and it will render as a blank or broken box if blocked.
- **Non-HTTPS content image:** the same `.png` loads over `http://`, risking blocking in Gmail proxy, Apple Mail Privacy Protection and strict clients.
- **Ineffective meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` lack `http-equiv`. The charset declaration is not actually applied, which risks mojibake in some clients.
- **Zoom disabled:** the viewport meta has `maximum-scale=1`, which blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Layout tables:** the visible tables (e.g. the `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0" border="0">` wrapper) lack `role="presentation"`. `<html>` has no `lang` attribute.
- **Style block in body:** `<body …><style>div.preheader{display:none!important}</style>` is placed after `<body>`. Some clients strip it, which could expose the preheader text. The inline `display:none !important` on the preheader mitigates this.
- **Text sizing:** `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none }` disables text-size scaling on iOS.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** the greeting renders as "Hi ," with a blank first-name field. The fallback or default value is missing from the SFMC AMPscript or personalization string. Confirmed by QA; the truncated source doesn't show the exact markup.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **Authentication-Results header not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because the message came through the AgentMail relay. This is an observation gap, not a confirmed failure. Verify against the raw headers on `emails.skechers.com`.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- Campaign ID `PRO_US_NA_U_SITE_FULL_EN_11112026` appears consistently in the Krux and ink1000 pixels.
- UTM parameters and landing-page alignment cannot be confirmed. Links are wrapped in `click.emails.skechers.com` redirects and were not followed. The source is truncated before the body links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the greeting.** Add a default fallback to the first-name token (e.g. "Hi there,") or suppress the greeting when the name is empty.
2. **Fix the Return Path pixel.** Change the `src` to `https://pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=…`.
3. **Move to HTTPS.** Change `http://www.ink1000.com/…` and `http://image.emails.skechers.com/…/dde00662-….png` to `https://`.
4. **Add alt text.** Use a meaningful `alt` on the content PNG and `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
5. **Correct the meta tags.** Use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
6. **Allow zoom.** Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Add `lang` to `<html>` and `role="presentation"` to the layout tables.
7. **Move the preheader style.** Put it in `<head>` rather than after `<body>`.
8. **Check the Krux partner parameter.** Verify the `_sha25` partner string and its consent coverage.
9. **Re-run the audit** with full headers and untruncated HTML to verify authentication, unsubscribe and address, `List-Unsubscribe`, and UTM parameters on the resolved click-through URLs.
