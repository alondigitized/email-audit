# Don't ghost these deals 👻
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-08**
## Executive summary

- Sally Beauty again. I laughed at the ghost pun in the subject line, but then I opened it and the email doesn't follow through. The hero says "No tricks here—just savings" over a big spooky nail photo, and then I get four unrelated product tiles. It's pretty and clear enough, but I'm a mom shopping for a 5-year-old and a 9-year-old. Nothing here is for them. It's textured hair care, a $50-off Airstyler, gel polish and a hair color bundle.
- This is also the 7th or so Sally email I've gotten since mid-September. After the Appiversary run, "Spooky season savings" on 10/1, and the 10/5 and 10/6 savings sends, I skim these now. I'd read the offers, maybe save the $5-off rewards banner, and go back to my kid's shoe tabs. I wouldn't click and I wouldn't forward it. I'd leave it unread, or swipe it away.

## What's working

- The "Get $5 off $25 when you sign up" rewards banner at the top is clear. I like anything tied to a rewards program.
- Each tile has one offer and one "Shop now" link, so it's easy to scan on my phone.
- The Klarna "4 interest-free payments" strip is useful for bigger buys like that $50-off Airstyler.
- The free shipping at $40+ and 2-hour delivery icons are easy to find. Fast delivery matters when I'm shopping on a Saturday.
- The Halloween nails in the hero fit the season.

## What's weak

- The hero offer is vague. "No tricks here—just savings" doesn't give me a number, so I have to scroll to find the actual deals.
- Nothing is for kids or family. No bundles, no promo code and no "stock up" angle.
- The "Don't ghost these deals" urgency isn't backed by a date. The only deadline is buried in the fine print (10/31).
- The email is just four products with no theme. BOGO 50% off is limited to "select textured hair care", which doesn't apply to me.
- The fine print is a wall of tiny text, and the exclusions make the offers feel less honest.
- I get too many of these. Seeing the same brand every other day makes the "fast" urgency feel fake.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the single best offer, with a number and an end date, in the hero. Right now the hero is a nail photo and a vague line.
- 2. Pick products from what I've actually browsed or bought. Right now I get a generic mix.
- 3. Cut the send frequency, or space promos out so each one feels special.
- 4. Make the Halloween theme carry through the whole email. The ghost joke fades after the subject line.
- 5. Show a rewards-member price on each tile, so there's a reason to sign in.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a standard XHTML 1.0 Transitional table-based template with several third-party tracking pixels. The source is truncated, so headers, footer, and body links could not be checked. Confirmed issues are malformed `<meta>` tags, a missing `lang` attribute, an unresolved `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro, an `http://` pixel, and alt-text problems on tracking images.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved macro:** `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&ao=883&aca=-11&si=-11&ci=-11&pi=-11&ad=-11…` is sent with the literal `[CACHEBUSTER]` string. The `r` parameter is therefore a constant, so clients and proxies can cache it and undercount impressions. The `-11` values (`aca`, `si`, `ci`, `pi`, `ad`) look like unpopulated placeholders. That is an inference, not confirmed.
- **Insecure pixel:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&dtm_cmagic=f6b5f8&dtm_fid=103&dtm_format=6&cli_promo_id…` is plain HTTP. It can be blocked or flagged as mixed content and may fail to fire in some clients. Dotomi is a Conversant/Epsilon ad-tech domain.
- **Unverified links:** 52 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Their destinations, status codes, and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Many pixels:** There are at least three third-party pixel/tracker images (`pxl.mon-trk.com`, `myvisualiq.net`, `dotomi.com`). Each adds a third-party request on open and blocked-image rendering risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv`, and `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv`. Neither is a valid declaration, so both do nothing. The charset then depends solely on the HTTP/MIME header.
- **Missing `lang`:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` attribute, which screen readers use to pick the pronunciation language.
- **Alt text:**
  - One image has an empty `src` and no alt text.
  - `pxl.mon-trk.com/987bfe61-…/2322666/003aZ00000s0I5yQAE` has the ID `003aZ00000s0I5yQAE` as its alt text. A screen reader will read that string aloud.
  - The `impression_pixel` and `UCMController` images have no alt text. Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` and be hidden from assistive tech.
- **Font fallback:** `a{font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif}` references a font with no `@font-face` in the visible portion. It will fall back to Helvetica.
- **Everything else:** The viewport meta, Apple data-detector overrides, and the `max-width:499px` mobile breakpoint look sound in the visible portion.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The only unresolved token found is `[CACHEBUSTER]` (see section 2).
- `003aZ00000s0I5yQAE` appears to be a Salesforce Contact ID (the `003` prefix). It is embedded in a third-party pixel path and in alt text. That is a recipient-level identifier exposed to the pixel vendor and in visible or readable markup.
- No other merge tokens were visible in the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Cannot verify:** The truncated source ends before the footer. The unsubscribe link, physical postal address, `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results were not provided.
- **Sender:** `sallybeauty@em.sallybeauty.com` uses a subdomain of the brand domain, which is consistent with aligned sending. That is not confirmed without headers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Unverified:** The click links were skipped by the probe, so UTM parameters and landing page behavior could not be checked.
- **No evidence:** There is no evidence in the provided data of a mismatch or of a match.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Make the ESP resolve `[CACHEBUSTER]` at send or render time, or remove the MediaMath/VisualIQ pixel. Also check the `-11` placeholder values in the same URL.
2. Change the Dotomi pixel to `https://`, or drop it if the vendor doesn't support HTTPS.
3. Set `alt=""` on every tracking pixel. Remove the Contact ID `003aZ00000s0I5yQAE` from the alt text, and review whether it belongs in the pixel URL.
4. Fix the meta tags: `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge" />`.
5. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`. Use a more descriptive `<title>`, since "Sally Beauty" is generic and also shows up as the browser-view tab title.
6. Remove the empty-`src` image, or populate it.
7. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML plus raw headers. That would cover the footer, the unsubscribe and `List-Unsubscribe` checks, the authentication results, the UTM parameters on the 52 tracked links, and the redirect destinations.
8. The QA summary reads "100% pass rate, 0 issue(s), 0 warning(s)" but lists six `[WARN]` entries. The pass/fail logic is not counting warnings and should be fixed.
