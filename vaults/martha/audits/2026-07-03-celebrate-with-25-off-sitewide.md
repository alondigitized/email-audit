# Celebrate with 25% off sitewide
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-03**
## Executive summary

- OK so... Sally. Again. I'll be honest — I almost swiped this away on instinct because it's at least the tenth email from them since June 1st, and that number keeps climbing. But the red hero stopped me. "America's 250th" on July 3rd? That's actually good timing, and 25% off sitewide with a visible promo code is exactly the kind of offer that makes me think "OK, let me see if there's anything I actually need."
- Here's my problem though: I open it and the "Stock Up on Beauty Favorites" grid is all professional hair color and developer products. Color Brilliance Permanent Crème, Sensitive Scalp 20 Volume Developer, Permanent Liquid Toner. That's a cosmetology student's shopping list, not mine. I'm a mom trying to maybe touch up my roots before the holiday barbecue, and nothing in this email speaks to that. The 2-hour delivery callout in the hero subtext did catch my eye — that's genuinely useful for a last-minute before-the-holiday purchase. But I'd mark this as read and maybe dig up the code if I actually remember to go to the Sally website later. I wouldn't forward it to a friend.
- The holiday nail art is cute, the layout is clean, the offer is legit. But personalization? Zero. This email treated me like a stranger who happens to be on a list.

## What's working

- **25% off sitewide with AMERICA25 is crystal clear** — visible in the hero, code right there, no hunting required.
- **The America's 250th tie-in lands perfectly.** Sent July 3rd, red background, patriotic nail art — it feels current and intentional, not recycled.
- **The patriotic nail art is charming.** A hand with red/white/blue nail art for July 4th is a fun, feminine visual that actually made me pause.
- **2-hour delivery callout in the hero sub-copy** is a real hook for me — when I shop online, speed matters more than I'd like to admit.
- **Mobile layout is clean and functional.** Nothing broken, nothing overlapping, hero is immediately obvious.

## What's weak

- **The product grid reads like a professional supply run.** Hair color developer, permanent liquid toner, anti-aging liqui-crème — these feel aimed at a stylist, not someone who just wants to do a quick at-home color before a cookout.
- **No expiration date anywhere visible.** AMERICA25 obviously expires around July 4th, but why make me guess? "Ends tonight at midnight" would make me act now instead of closing the tab.
- **"Shop Now" tells me nothing.** Takes me where, exactly? If the button said "Shop Hair Color" or "Shop Nails Sale," I'd know what I'm clicking into.
- **No loyalty or rewards acknowledgment.** I'm in their rewards program — seeing my points balance or a member-exclusive offer would make this feel personal instead of mass-blast.
- **Sally's send frequency is burning me out.** This is email ten or eleven since June 1st. I'm not counting for fun — at some point all their "limited time" language stops meaning anything.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Stamp an expiration date on the offer.** "Code AMERICA25 expires July 4th" in the hero would push me from "maybe later" to "I should do this now."
- 2. **Personalize the product grid based on my purchase history.** If I've bought a specific hair color shade, show me that. Don't assume I'm stocking a salon.
- 3. **Replace generic "Shop Now" with category CTAs.** "Shop Hair Color," "Shop Nail Deals" — anything that tells me where I'm landing.
- 4. **Surface my rewards balance or member status.** Even a line like "You have 450 points — here's how to stretch them further" would make this feel like it was written for me.
- 5. **Slow down the send cadence.** One more near-identical promo email this week and I'm clicking unsubscribe.
- **Subject Alt A:** `25% off everything — code AMERICA25, ends July 4`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 4th of July deal: 25% off sitewide`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Hair, nails & styling tools — 2-hour delivery available now`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping + 25% off. Stock up before the holiday weekend.`

### 1. Technical Summary

The email template is structurally sound (XHTML 1.0 Transitional, valid responsive breakpoints), but contains one unfilled macro in an impression pixel, six webfont resource failures, an insecure HTTP tracking pixel, and four product recommendation images without alt text.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Webfont 404s (6 URLs flagged)**
All six `fonts.gstatic.com/s/opensans/v17/` woff2 URLs returned 404. Two possible causes:
- The QA URL extractor included the trailing `)` from CSS `url(...)` syntax in each URL (e.g., `...mem6YaGs126MiZpBA-UFUK0Zdc1GAK6b.woff2)`), which would itself cause a 404 as an artifact. Verify by probing the URL without the trailing `)`.
- Regardless of the extraction artifact, Open Sans v17 paths on Google Fonts CDN are old (current builds are v40+). The paths may be genuinely stale.

**Impact:** Fallback chain (`'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif`) activates silently — no broken rendering, but intended brand typography is lost.

**27 tracking/click-redirect links** were skipped by the probe. This is expected behavior for ESP click-wrapping. No issue, but UTM pass-through cannot be confirmed automatically (see §6).

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Missing alt text — product recommendation images (4 warnings)**
```
https://7247925.recs.igodigital.com/rr/v2/67377c622e8cdd003fe83bab/i/1/martha.stroll@agentmail.to
https://7247925.recs.igodigital.com/rr/v2/.../i/2/...
https://7247925.recs.igodigital.com/rr/v2/.../i/3/...
https://7247925.recs.igodigital.com/rr/v2/.../i/4/...
```
These are dynamically injected product recommendation images (iGoDigital/Salesforce Einstein). Missing `alt` attributes mean screen readers skip them entirely and images-off rendering shows blank boxes with no product identification. Recommended: inject descriptive alt text at render time via the personalization engine (e.g., `alt="[product_name]"`).

**Insecure HTTP pixel**
```
src: http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990...
```
Mixed-content HTTP image. Gmail, Outlook.com, and Apple Mail will proxy or block HTTP resources. Dotomi retargeting impression will silently fail for a significant share of recipients. Needs upgrade to `https://`.

**Tracking pixel alt attributes** — `pxl.mon-trk.com` and `t.myvisualiq.net` pixels also lack `alt=""`. For 1×1 tracking pixels, the fix is `alt=""` (empty string, not absent), which suppresses screen-reader announcements without accessibility violation.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Unfilled macro — MyVisualIQ impression pixel**

`[CACHEBUSTER]` is a literal string in the deployed email — the macro was never substituted. This breaks cache-busting for the MyVisualIQ impression tracker, causing all sends to share a single cacheable URL. Impression counts from this vendor will be inaccurate (undercounted or deduplicated incorrectly by CDN/proxy caches). This requires an ESP template fix before next send.
