# Beauty's Better at 20% Off✨
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-21**
## Executive summary

- Sally Beauty again — same confetti-dot COLORfest banner I've now scrolled past what feels like a dozen times this month. Honestly at this point my thumb moves before my brain does. That said, if I actually squint at this one, it's not the worst of the bunch: 20% off sitewide with a real code (AUGUST20) and a real end date (August 23) is at least a legible offer, not vague mush.
- But none of it matters for me. I'm not doing my own hair color at home, I've got a 5-year-old and a 9-year-old whose feet are what I'm actually shopping for on my Sunday phone scroll, and this is toner, developer, and color-sealer packettes. Not even close to my radar.
- Not clicking, not forwarding — straight to "seen it, skip it." If anything I'm getting closer to unsubscribing given how relentless this cadence has been.

## What's working

- The 20% off sitewide + AUGUST20 code + August 23 deadline is genuinely clear and easy to read at a glance.
- Rewards banner ("Shop Twice in August, Earn a $5 Reward in September") is a real loyalty mechanic, not vague fluff — I'd notice it if I were actually a haircare shopper.
- Klarna's "4 interest-free payments over $35" is a reasonable value-add for anyone price-sensitive like me, even if I have no use for it here.
- No visible render bugs — everything's clean, aligned, images loaded fine.

## What's weak

- I have two little kids whose shoe sizes I'm tracking weekly, and this email is 100% adult hair-color/toner/developer products — zero relevance to me.
- The product grid (booster, sealer packette, toner, developer) reads like a professional colorist's shopping list, not anything a mom browsing on a Sunday would recognize or need.
- This is basically the same COLORfest confetti creative I saw on 8/15 and 8/16 — the visual fatigue is real, I clock the sender before I even read the subject now.
- Nothing here treats me like a known customer with actual purchase history — it's a blanket sitewide blast, not personalized to what I've ever bought (which, per my inbox, is nothing from Sally).

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment me out of hair-color sends entirely if I've never purchased color/toner products — stop burning a daily slot in my inbox on category-irrelevant offers.
- 2. If Sally must keep emailing broad segments, lead with something universally relevant (nail care, gift cards, general beauty tools) rather than professional colorist SKUs.
- 3. Rotate the creative — the confetti COLORfest banner has run essentially unchanged for over a week; even loyal shoppers will start blind-scrolling past it.
- 4. Cut send frequency — six-plus near-identical promos in two weeks is pushing me toward unsubscribing regardless of offer quality.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Summary
The email uses a heavily instrumented ESP template (Salesforce Marketing Cloud–style, based on `.EID`/dotomi/evergage tracking patterns) with 50 click-tracked links and multiple third-party tracking pixels; several pixels are missing alt text and one loads over plain HTTP.

## Link & Tracking Issues
- 50 tracking/click-redirect links were present but skipped automated HTTP probing (expected behavior for redirect-domain links, not itself a defect — cannot independently confirm final destinations from this data).
- Tracking pixel `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&dtm_cmagic=f6b5f8&dtm_fid=103&dtm_format=6&cli_promo_id` is served over **HTTP, not HTTPS**. Mixed-content pixels are commonly blocked by mail clients (Gmail, Outlook) and by browsers when the email is viewed via webmail over HTTPS, silently dropping this tracking event.
- Other tracking pixels (Evergage/Salesforce Interaction Studio, mon-trk.com, myvisualiq.net) all load over HTTPS — no issue there.

## Rendering & Accessibility
- 4 identical Evergage/Interaction Studio pixels (`sallyholdings.us-4.evergage.com/.../summary.png?userId=martha.st`) are missing `alt` text. As 1x1 tracking pixels this has negligible visual/screen-reader impact but is worth flagging for template hygiene.
- Additional missing `alt` attributes on the mon-trk.com pixel, myvisualiq.net impression pixel, and dotomi UCMController pixel — same category (tracking pixels, not content images), low practical impact.
- No missing `alt` text was flagged on any content/hero images in the truncated source.
- `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` meta tag is present but malformed — it's missing the `content="true"` (or similar) attribute; as written (`<meta name="x-apple-disable-message-reformatting" />`) this is technically non-standard, though most Apple Mail clients tolerate the bare tag.

## Personalization & Merge Tokens
- Evergage pixel URLs embed `userId=martha.st` directly in the image `src` (visible in page source / network requests). This is a live subscriber identifier exposed in a URL rather than a merge tag resolved server-side into hidden tracking infrastructure — flag for review by the ESP/CDP team as a potential PII-in-URL exposure pattern, not a rendering bug.
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `%%FirstName%%`, `{{token}}`) were found in the visible truncated source.

## Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- No unsubscribe link, physical address, or authentication headers (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) were present in the supplied HTML/QA data to evaluate — the source was truncated before the footer/legal block (`.legal` CSS class is defined in `<style>` but its markup wasn't included). **Cannot confirm compliance status from the data provided** — recommend re-running the audit against the full HTML including the footer.

## Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Cannot verify UTM parameter presence/consistency or landing page alignment — all 50 outbound links were tracking/redirect-domain links that were skipped by the automated prober, and the truncated source doesn't expose final destination URLs.

## Recommendations
1. Change the dotomi pixel to HTTPS (`https://login.dotomi.com/...`) to prevent mixed-content blocking of that tracking event.
2. Add empty `alt=""` (not missing `alt`) to all tracking pixels to satisfy accessibility linters cleanly, since they're decorative/non-content.
3. Fix the `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` meta tag to include an explicit `content` attribute.
4. Re-run the audit with the untruncated HTML (footer/legal block) to verify CAN-SPAM unsubscribe/physical-address compliance and confirm UTM/landing-page continuity — these could not be assessed from the data provided.
5. Confirm with the ESP whether embedding `userId` in plaintext pixel URLs is intended practice or should be moved to a POST-based/hashed identifier.
