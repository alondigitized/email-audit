- Nothing here is for kids. There's no velcro, no light-up soles, no pink or purple, and no blue/black lace-ups. I shop for my two kids first and me last, so this feels like a stranger's email.

- The email is long, and there's no urgency. The 11/29 end date is buried in fine print instead of shown as a countdown.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed personalization failure (an empty greeting name slot, "Hi ,") and several markup defects in the tracking-pixel and head sections. Compliance, authentication and UTM checks can't be completed, because the HTML is truncated and the `Authentication-Results` header is missing.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed pixel URL (confirmed in source):** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…">` has no scheme. Clients resolve it as a relative path, so the Return Path/Validity seed-tracking pixel won't load. It also has no `alt`.
- **40 tracking links were not probed.** The QA check skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destinations and redirect health are unverified. The open pixel is `click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx?qs=…`.
- **Plain-HTTP pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108404282&mi_ecmp=MKG_US_NA_U_PERFORM_PROD_EN_11252026` is non-HTTPS and may be blocked or flagged as mixed content.
- **Third-party Krux/Salesforce beacons:** four `beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif` and `1x1_ad_impression.gif` calls. Two of them pass `partner_uid` values in the URL: a 64-hex SHA-256 string (`adf27fee…189a`, likely a hashed email) and the subscriber ID `122277199`. These are extra third-party requests and personal-data exposure.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` attributes (QA-confirmed):** `o.gif` and `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`. The tracking pixels `krxd.net` ×4 and `returnpath` also have no `alt`.
- **Non-HTTPS content image:** `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-….png` is a content image served over HTTP and may be blocked.
- **Head meta tags are malformed:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv`, so neither declares anything. Charset is then left to the HTTP `Content-Type` header.
- **Zoom is restricted:** `viewport … maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom. `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none }` also disables iOS text scaling.
- **Layout tables have no `role="presentation"`.** The tracking tables in the visible portion lack it, and the rest is truncated.
- **Pixels are hidden by `display:none !important`** on the wrapper div. Some clients and privacy proxies don't load images in hidden containers, which reduces open-tracking reliability.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** the greeting renders as "Hi ," with no first name and no fallback. The merge field is empty with no default (for example "Hi there,") in the AMPscript/SFMC logic.
- No unresolved raw tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) are visible in the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication unverified:** no `Authentication-Results` header was found. The QA notes this is expected via the AgentMail relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. It is a gap in the test setup, not a confirmed sender failure. The From domain `emails.skechers.com` is a subdomain, and DMARC alignment is unconfirmed.
- **Unsubscribe and postal address not verifiable:** the source is truncated before the footer, and no `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers were provided, so they can't be assessed.
- **Privacy flag:** the hashed-email and subscriber-ID values sent to `krxd.net` (section 2) should be covered by the privacy policy and consent handling.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not assessable:** the 40 links are click-redirect wrapped and were not resolved, so the final landing URLs and any UTM parameters are unknown.
- The only campaign identifier visible is `MKG_US_NA_U_PERFORM_PROD_EN_11252026`, used in the `ink1000` and `krxd` pixels. Whether it matches the `utm_campaign` on the CTAs is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a default value for the first-name merge field, or fall back to a name-less greeting when it is empty.
2. Fix the Return Path pixel to `https://pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=…`.
3. Switch `ink1000.com` and `image.emails.skechers.com` assets to HTTPS, and add `alt` text to all images (`alt=""` for pixels).
4. Change the head tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=Edge">`.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1`, and add `role="presentation"` to layout tables.
6. Resolve the click-tracking redirects in a follow-up run to confirm final URLs, UTM parameters and status codes.
7. Re-run with the full HTML and raw headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, the footer unsubscribe link, the physical address, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
8. Review the data passed to the `krxd.net` beacons (a hashed email in the URL) against privacy and consent requirements.
