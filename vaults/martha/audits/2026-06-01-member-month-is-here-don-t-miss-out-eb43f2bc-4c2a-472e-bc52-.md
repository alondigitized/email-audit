# Member Month is Here! Don't Miss Out
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-01**
## Executive summary

- Opening this one, the body actually made me stop. "Hi Martha" right at the top, a Plus member badge, a dedicated Member Appreciation Month banner promising 2X points — and then the BOGO 50% off sitting right there before I even have to scroll. As a mom who routinely buys two pairs of kids' shoes at a time, BOGO is practically designed for my life. I scrolled down, found the SHOP KIDS' section with what looked like pink sneakers for my daughter and some darker gray ones for my son, and I genuinely started thinking about whether we need shoes right now.
- The problem is I almost didn't get that far. The preview text in my inbox is JSON code. Again. That's five straight Skechers emails — I flagged it mentally on May 27th, again on May 28th, again this week — and it is still shipping. At this point I've trained myself to just react to the word "Skechers" in the sender field and ignore whatever broken garbage is sitting next to the subject. That is not a subscriber behavior a brand should be optimizing for.
- Inside though, this is probably the most targeted email they've sent me in this whole stretch. The BOGO framing, the loyalty points call-out, the kids' CTA, the AfterPay and Klarna options — all of it hits where I actually live. I'd click through to the kids' section. I would not forward this to a friend because the broken preview text makes the whole thing look janky from the inbox list view.

## What's working

- **"Hi Martha" + Plus member badge** — I feel recognized as an actual customer, not a cold address on a list
- **BOGO 50% off is front and center** — visible immediately on open, no scrolling required, and it's the exact offer structure that benefits a mom buying multiple pairs at once
- **SHOP KIDS' CTA with product images** — pink/light shoes for my daughter, darker sneakers for my son, both visible in the same section
- **2X Points banner** — I have a rewards balance and I'm actively trying to use it; this reminds me there's extra value on the table right now
- **AfterPay and Klarna listed** — I appreciate seeing this even when I don't use it; it signals they're thinking about budget-conscious shoppers

## What's weak

- **The JSON preview text is running for the fifth consecutive email** — this is a systemic unfixed bug at this point and it's killing open rates for anyone who doesn't already have a Skechers reflex
- **No end date on the main BOGO offer** — "SHOP TODAY" without a date means I don't have a real reason not to close and come back tomorrow, which usually means I don't come back
- **Hero model is aspirational runway, not practical-mom** — the tall woman in the flowing maxi dress is lovely but she doesn't look like she's navigating a school drop-off line
- **Kids' section is below Women's and Men's** — for a purchase-history member who buys mostly kids' shoes, that ordering feels backwards

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill the JSON preview text** — this is the single highest-leverage fix and it costs nothing; a blank preheader beats JSON schema every time
- 2. **Add a hard expiration date to the BOGO** — "Ends June 8" in the hero forces a decision this session instead of a vague "maybe later"
- 3. **Reorder sections: SHOP KIDS' first** for member segments with kids' purchase history — or a combined "For Your Family" tile that leads with kids then rolls into Women's
- 4. **Swap the hero model aesthetic** — show someone in actual everyday comfort shoes, not a fashion editorial pose; I need to see myself in the product
- 5. **Put the BOGO + 2X Points together in one visual** — right now they're in separate banners; a single "Stack your member perks: BOGO + 2X Points" message would feel more compelling
- **Subject Alt A:** `"2X Points all month — shop kids & women's BOGO"`
- **Subject Alt B:** `"Your member BOGO is live — 50% off second pair"`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `"Buy One, Get One 50% off + earn double points through June"`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `"Member perk inside: 2X points and BOGO 50% off all month"`
