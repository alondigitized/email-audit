# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- OK so I open this and it's literally a picture of me and my daughter — a mom kissing her little girl on the cheek, both in pink Skechers, flowers everywhere. It's Mother's Day week and they know it. This is a brand leaning hard into the gift/family angle, with a very clear deal upfront. I'm not bouncing.

## What's working

- **The hero image** is doing real work — that mom-and-daughter lifestyle shot with matching pink Skechers is my exact life. I felt something. That's rare on a homepage.
- **BOGO 50% in the top banner** is the single most compelling thing for me personally. I'm buying shoes for two kids — a buy-one-get-one is not a generic promo, it's a reason to fill a cart.
- **Timing is perfect** — Mother's Day hero landing on May 6th means they're catching me exactly during the "do I buy myself a gift or just get the kids something" decision window.
- **Secondary promo strip** (25% OFF Apparel) doesn't cannibalize the hero — it layered an apparel deal alongside the footwear BOGO without cluttering.
- **"Recently Viewed" tab** peeking at the bottom suggests the app/site remembers me, which I appreciate as a repeat shopper.

## What's weak

- **"SHOP NOW" is too generic** — the button sits right below a Mother's Day hero, so context helps, but the copy could say "Shop Mother's Day Gifts" and double the intent. Right now it could be for anything.
- **No kid's shoe shortcut visible** — there's no "Girls' / Boys'" category quick-tap anywhere in this viewport. I'd have to tap MENU and dig, which is friction when I'm on my phone while my kids are occupied for approximately four minutes.
- **No trust signals in this viewport** — no reviews, no "free returns," no "ships in 2 days before Sunday." For a Mother's Day gift purchase, timing guarantees and return flexibility matter a lot and nothing addresses that here.
- **No loyalty rewards callout** — I'm apparently a Skechers customer (Recently Viewed is present), so why isn't my points balance or rewards tier shown? That's free motivation to buy.

## Recommendations

- **Change "SHOP NOW" to "Shop Mother's Day Gifts"** — tiny copy swap, immediately clarifies intent and removes one micro-decision for me.
- **Add a delivery-deadline strip** — "Order by May 10 for Mother's Day delivery" below the hero button. That's a conversion trigger that costs nothing to add and directly addresses my anxiety about whether it'll arrive in time.
- **Surface a Kids' quick-tap row** — even one row of four: Girls' Shoes / Boys' Shoes / Women's / All Gifts. Moms buy for their kids first. Make that path one tap, not buried in MENU.
- **Pull my rewards balance or a "You're X points from your next reward" nudge** into the banner or hero — I'm clearly a returning shopper (Recently Viewed exists), so use that data to give me a reason to hit a spend threshold.
