- Finally, an email that talks to me about my actual kids. The glittery high-top, the pastel light-up sneaker and the black and red light-up boys' shoe are exactly what my 5-year-old and 9-year-old would point at. The "Hi, Martha" and my 1000 points with the $5 reward are still at the top. That's the third send in a row that treats me like a member, after 10/17 and 10/18.

The 10/21 deadline is a few days away, and it's small print in the banner. The girls' and boys' sections are split cleanly, which I like on my phone. ## What's working

- "Hi, Martha" with 1000 points and a "Redeem" link makes me feel like a known member, and the $5 reward is a real nudge.
- The girls' section shows light-up, sparkly, pastel shoes, which is just what my 5-year-old wants.
- The boys' section has black, red and green light-up shoes with a strap, which fits my 9-year-old and the durable-looking soles.
- Separate "Shop All Girls" and "Shop All Boys" buttons are big and bright, easy to tap with a thumb.
- "2 for $100" and "Members take 20% off" give me two ways to save, and I like that the member discount is called out.

## What's weak

- I can't see a single price, sale price or product name on any shoe, so I don't know what "2 for $100" saves me.
- The deadline is only small "Valid thru 10/21" text in the banners. The hero has no urgency.
- The 2 for $100 offer is the same promo from 10/14 and 10/16. It's stale after a week of near-daily emails.
- The boys' shoe is a lace-look strap, not a real lace-up sneaker. There's also nothing about sizes, velcro, or fit.
- The "Shop Light Up Styles" button sits between the headline and the girls' shoe, so it's not obvious where it goes.
- Women, Men, Kids and Wide are plain text links at the bottom, and the fine print at the very end is tiny and dense.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a price on the hero shoes, like "Light-ups from $XX" or "2 pairs for $100 = $50 each". Right now the offer and the shoes feel disconnected.
- 2. Add a clear deadline in the hero, like "Ends 10/21: Halloween light-ups". Then the urgency is credible.
- 3. Link each shoe to its own product page, and add a star rating or "Most loved by parents" label.
- 4. Add a family bundle line for my kids, like "Buy one for each kid and save with 2 for $100".
- 5. Lead with the member perk, such as "Use your $5 reward on light-ups", since I already have the points.

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email has one confirmed broken-personalization defect (empty greeting slot, "Hi ,"). It also has insecure `http://` image sources and a malformed tracking pixel URL. Authentication status could not be verified.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 30 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains (for example `click.emails.skechers.com`). Destination validity is unverified, not confirmed broken.
- Malformed pixel URL: `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…">` has no scheme. Mail clients will resolve it as a relative path and the request will fail, so the Return Path/Validity inbox-placement pixel never fires.
- Third-party tracking beacons sit in the hidden preheader block, all with `width="0"` or `"1"`:
  - Four `beacon.krxd.net` pixels (usermatch ×2, ad_impression ×1).
  - One `ink1000.com` pixel.
  - `partner_uid=adf27fee…189a` appears to be a SHA-256 hash of the recipient's email. It is passed to a third-party data-matching domain, which is a privacy and compliance consideration.
- The `ink1000.com` pixel uses `http://` (see section 3).

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- Mixed content and HTTP images (QA warnings):
  - `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?...`
  - `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`
  - Non-HTTPS sources may be blocked or rewritten. If the `.png` is visible content, it could fail to render. The `image.emails.skechers.com` host should be served over HTTPS.
- Missing alt text (QA warnings) on `o.gif` and `dde00662-…c2290c.png`:
  - The `.png` is on a Skechers-hosted path and is likely content, not a tracker.
  - A missing `alt` attribute means screen readers may announce the filename or URL.
  - The `.gif` is a tracker, so it should carry `alt=""`.
- Several pixels (`beacon.krxd.net` ×3 and the `returnpath` pixel) have no `alt` attribute at all. The QA check did not flag them, but they are likewise missing `alt=""`.
- `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` lack `http-equiv`, so they do nothing. The charset is not declared effectively, which is a minor risk for special characters such as "®".
- The viewport meta sets `maximum-scale=1`, which blocks pinch-zoom and is an accessibility problem (WCAG 1.4.4).
- `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }` disables text resizing in iOS Mail.
- Responsive CSS depends on `display:none` toggles (`.mobile-content`/`.desktop-content`). These are unreliable in some clients, notably Gmail with non-Google accounts and older Outlook.
- The source was truncated, so full-body checks (heading structure, link text, CTA alt text) could not be done.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** the greeting renders as "Hi ," with an empty name field. The name merge value was blank for this recipient, and no fallback default exists (for example "Hi there,").
- Resolved subscriber values are present, such as `subid=122277199` and `mi_u=8108404282`. No unresolved `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` tokens were visible in the truncated source.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **[WARN] Authentication-Results header not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is expected via the AgentMail relay, so it is a monitoring limitation, not a confirmed failure. The From domain `emails.skechers.com` should be checked independently.
- The unsubscribe link, physical postal address and `List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are not visible in the truncated source or QA data. They are unverified, not confirmed missing.
- No issues found beyond the above.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Campaign identifier: `MKG_US_NA_K_CATEGORY_GENDER_EN_10192026` is consistent across the `krxd` and `ink1000` pixels, so campaign naming is consistent.
- Destination URLs and UTM parameters could not be audited. All 30 links are wrapped in `click.emails.skechers.com` redirects and were skipped. The truncated source also hides the final destinations.
- No confirmed issues.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add a fallback default to the first-name merge, for example `%%=IIF(Empty(@firstName),"there",@firstName)=%%` in SFMC AMPscript. Alternatively, suppress the greeting when the name is empty. This is the highest priority because it is a customer-visible defect.
2. Fix the schemeless URL `pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif` by adding `https://`.
3. Serve all images over HTTPS, including `image.emails.skechers.com/.../dde00662-…png` and `ink1000.com/.../o.gif`. Confirm both hosts support TLS.
4. Add `alt` text to `dde00662-…png`. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels (`o.gif`, the `krxd` and `returnpath` pixels).
5. Change the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=Edge" />`.
6. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta and avoid `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`. Use `100%` instead.
7. Verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `emails.skechers.com` using a direct-send test or DMARC reports. Check the `List-Unsubscribe` headers at the same time.
8. Review the third-party data-matching pixels (`krxd`) and the hashed-email `partner_uid` against the privacy policy and consent requirements.
