# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-07
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- Okay, the second this page loaded I saw a mom hugging her little girl in pink sneakers surrounded by flowers and thought — yeah, they know it's Mother's Day week. Skechers is running a full Mother's Day push with BOGO 50% stacked on top of a spring apparel sale, and the mobile layout is genuinely clean. The vibe is warm and seasonal, but the page is more about getting me into the shop than actually helping me find shoes for my kids fast.

## What's working

- **The BOGO 50% banner** — that blue top bar is the first thing I read. "Applied at Cart" means no coupon code hunting. That's exactly how you get a mom with two kids to stay on the page.
- **The hero image** — mom kissing daughter's cheek while the little girl wears pink Skechers. My daughter is five. I felt this immediately. It's specific in a way that "lifestyle photography" usually isn't.
- **The "Mother's Day Shop" headline** — the script font and pink florals match the emotional register of the holiday without being cloying. It reads seasonal, not generic.
- **25% OFF Apparel sub-banner** — stacking a second offer above the fold tells me there's real value here, not just one token deal.
- **Clean mobile layout** — the page isn't fighting itself. No cramped text, no layout jank. On a phone between snack time and homework, that matters more than people realize.

## What's weak

- **"SHOP NOW" tells me nothing about where I'm going** — I shop for my daughter first, my son second, myself last. If that button went to "Shop Girls' Shoes" I'd tap it instantly. "SHOP NOW" for a Mother's Day Shop could mean women's gifts, women's shoes, kids' shoes — I don't know.
- **No kids' category shortcut visible** — no "Boys" / "Girls" quick-link below the hero. I have to open the hamburger menu and dig, which is friction when I'm on my phone with limited attention time.
- **Zero loyalty mention** — I'm in the Skechers rewards program (or would be if they made it obvious). No points balance, no member-only price, no "Elite members get extra 10%." That's a missed retention moment for someone like me who is price-sensitive.
- **No free shipping threshold** — I need to know if my order size will hit free shipping. If BOGO 50% gets me two pairs of kids' shoes, is that enough? No idea from this screen.
- **No deadline for BOGO** — "Applied at Cart" is nice but does this end Sunday? Monday? A sense of urgency would push me to act now rather than bookmarking for later (and then forgetting).

## Recommendations

- **Add a kids' quick-link row below the hero** — two or three pill buttons: "Girls' Shoes," "Boys' Shoes," "Women's Shoes." Lets me route myself to my category in one tap instead of the hamburger menu.
- **Show a BOGO expiration** — add "Ends May 12" or a simple countdown below the top banner. Mother's Day is Sunday. That deadline is real and would push me off the fence.
- **Surface a free-shipping threshold** — add "Free shipping on orders $45+" to the blue BOGO banner or the sub-banner. Most shoe purchases for a kid clear $45 easily; showing this removes a hesitation.
- **Add a loyalty pill to the sub-banner** — "Skechers Elite members earn 2x points this week" or similar. One line, massive retention signal for returning customers.
