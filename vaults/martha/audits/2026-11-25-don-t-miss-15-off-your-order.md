# Don't miss 15% off your order
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- Sally Beauty again, and this is about the twenty-eighth send since mid-September. I'm tired of it. The 15% off is clear and the black hero is hard to miss. But "ends today" on a deal that started as "Black Friday came early 👀" on Nov 8 and was "Early Black Friday Deals" on Nov 12 doesn't feel credible. I've watched this same deadline move for two weeks.
- I'm a mom shopping for a 5-year-old and a 9-year-old on a weekend with my phone in hand. Nothing here is for them. It's hair color boosters, a tint brush and nail glue. The two women with the tote bags are cute, but they're not me or my kids.
- Would I click? Maybe, only if I needed hair color for myself. Would I forward it? No. I'd skim it, see the "buy online, pick up in store" catch, and mark it read.

## What's working

- The 15% off headline is huge and sits on a black background, so I know the offer in about one second.
- "Shop now" is a big white button right under the offer, easy to tap with a thumb.
- The pickup rule is spelled out in the hero: "buy online, pick up in-store."
- The Klarna strip and the 2-hour delivery banner help a price-sensitive shopper like me.
- The Sally Rewards block ($5 bonus reward on a $50 gift card) speaks to my rewards-program loyalty.

## What's weak

- Pickup-only means a trip to the store. I'm not driving there today with two kids just to save 15%.
- The product tiles are all hair-color and salon-pro items. Nothing is for a family or a gift.
- The rewards offer is in-store only and needs a $50 gift card purchase. That's a lot of fine print for $5.
- The legal text at the bottom is a wall. It says no coupon codes, no stacking and exclusions apply, which makes "15% off" feel less honest.
- The store-closure banner at the top competes with the hero. The red strips make the top feel busy before I reach the offer.
- It's the fourth-ish Black Friday-flavored send in two weeks, and the "ends today" deadline has lost its punch.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show a real deadline and a simple reason to act now. Skip the manufactured urgency.
- 2. Let me get the 15% with shipping, or at least with free shipping over $40. Pickup-only is the biggest friction.
- 3. Put a gift or family module near the top, like stocking stuffers or a holiday gift set under $25. That would speak to me as a mom.
- 4. Move the store-closure notice below the hero so the offer lands first.
- 5. Cut the fine print down and link to the full terms.
- **Subject Alt A:** `15% off ends tonight, pick up in store`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 15% off before Thanksgiving`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Order online, grab it in store today. Rewards members get a $5 bonus too.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stores close Thursday, so pick up your gifts and hair favorites today.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has an unresolved ad-server macro (`[CACHEBUSTER]`), a recipient-identifying parameter in image URLs, and an HTTP image, and it has no alt text on its dynamic content images. Authentication, unsubscribe and UTM compliance can't be verified, because the HTML was truncated and the 53 tracked links were never probed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved macro:** the pixel `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&ao=883&aca=-11&si=-11&ci=-11&pi=-11&ad=-11…` still contains the literal `[CACHEBUSTER]`. Several parameters are `-11`, which is typically a "not populated" sentinel. The impression will likely be cached, deduplicated or misattributed.
- **Insecure pixel:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990…` is non-HTTPS and may be blocked or rewritten by clients.
- **Unverified links:** 53 click-redirect links were skipped by the QA probe. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on content images:** four Evergage images (`…/campaign/Vwn6U/je1h1/{1,2,3,4}/summary.png?userId=…`) have no alt text. These are the dynamic recommendation blocks, so their content is invisible to screen readers and to clients with images off.
- **Empty-src images:** two `<img>` tags report an empty `src` and no alt. If they are placeholders for dynamic content that failed to render, they will show broken-image icons.
- **Tracking pixels:** `pxl.mon-trk.com/…/003aZ00000s0I5yQAE` and the `myvisualiq` and `dotomi` pixels have no `alt`. They should carry `alt=""` (and ideally a `width`/`height` of 1) so they are treated as decorative.
- **Ineffective meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute. The charset declaration is therefore not valid, and some clients may mis-decode characters.
- **Language and title:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` attribute, and `<title>` is the generic "Sally Beauty".
