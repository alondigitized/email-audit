# Staff Picks From Skechers HQ
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-12**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so I'm on the couch during my daughter's nap window, scrolling my inbox, and I see this from Skechers. Before I even tap it, that preview text hits — and it's the exact same JSON schema garbage I've been seeing from them for weeks now. Like, this is at least the fourth or fifth time. I know the email is probably fine, but that's a trust chip every single time. I almost didn't open it.
- Inside though? "Employee Faves" is actually a fun premise. Who doesn't want to know what the insiders are wearing? The layout is clean, three hero shoe features, and that 25% Off Apparel banner is hard to miss. The Retro Lite and CozyFit sections are actually pretty appealing for me personally.
- But here's my honest take: I shop Skechers primarily for my kids — a sparkly sneaker for my five-year-old, something durable for my nine-year-old boy. This entire email is adult footwear. KIDS appears as a single text link buried under the 25% off banner, but there's zero hero real estate for it. I'm also a little annoyed the 25% off is on apparel, not shoes — that's not what I came here for. I'd probably tap "Shop This Style" on the CozyFit out of personal interest, but I'd close out without buying anything for the kids. Mark as read, maybe a quick tap. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- **"Employee Faves" concept lands** — it feels human and editorial, not just another promo blast; I actually wanted to see what they picked
- **Three distinct hero products** with individual shop CTAs makes scanning fast; I don't have to dig to find the shoes
- **CozyFit's travel badge/podiatrist-approval callout** is smart social proof — that kind of authority shortcut actually moves me
- **25% Off Apparel banner is visually unmissable** — bold, centered, high contrast; the offer reads instantly
- **Clean render, no broken images** — after some of the wonkier Skechers sends lately, this one at least loads correctly and looks polished

## What's weak

- **Preview text is JSON schema gibberish again** — this is literally the fourth or fifth time in my brand history with Skechers and it erodes trust before I even open; at some point I start wondering if these emails are safe
- **Zero kids' shoe content in the hero** — the KIDS link is hidden in the nav below the sale banner, not even a thumbnail; for a mom shopping primarily for her kids, the whole hero is irrelevant
- **25% off apparel, not shoes** — shoes are why I'm here; apparel savings feel like a consolation prize
- **No pricing in the hero cards** — I'm price-sensitive and I can see from the Retro Lite section there might be a price mentioned, but it's too small to read on mobile; I want to see "$79" up front before I click
- **"Staff Picks From Skechers HQ" subject doesn't hook me** — it's brand-serving, not customer-serving; tells me nothing about whether there's a deal or anything relevant to my family

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a kids' shoe block in the hero** — even one "Staff Pick for Little Feet" feature with a light-up sneaker or a durable boy's shoe would immediately make this relevant to my actual shopping mission; right now I'm a single-tap-and-bounce
- 2. **Fix the preview text — for good this time** — this is a known recurring bug across multiple sends; whatever JSON schema is leaking needs to be caught at QA before send; write real preview copy like "See what Skechers HQ actually wears to work"
- 3. **Show the price in the hero cards** — price-sensitive shoppers won't click unless they have a ballpark; put the retail price right under the shoe name
- 4. **Tie the 25% offer to shoes, or clarify** — if the sale includes shoes, say so in the banner; "25% Off Apparel" reads like it excludes the thing I want most
- 5. **Make the CTA copy more specific** — "Shop This Style" works but "Shop Retro Lite" or "Shop CozyFit" would be stronger; helps me navigate faster
- **Subject Alt A:** `Our Team's Favorite Shoes Right Now — Plus 25% Off`

- **Preheader Alt A:** `See what Skechers HQ picked — and grab 25% off apparel while you're here.`

### 1. Technical Summary

One confirmed broken experience (empty personalization token in greeting) and a systemic HTTP image-serving issue affecting 11+ assets. No compliance blockers identified.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**No broken destination links confirmed** — 26 click-redirect URLs via `click.emails.skechers.com` were not probed (expected; redirect domains return 3xx without context).

**Third-party pixel exposure:** Three distinct tracking domains fire on open:
- `beacon.krxd.net` (Salesforce Krux DMP) — two user-match pixels + one impression pixel
- `pixel.app.returnpath.net` — deliverability seed/inbox monitoring
- `ink1000.com` — additional open pixel (HTTP, see §3)

No issues with the pixel implementations themselves, but the Krux impression pixel embeds the full campaign ID in plain text: `campaignid=MKG_US_L30D_U_BEST_ENGAGEDOPEN_EN_06122026`. This leaks internal campaign naming convention to third parties on every open.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

11 images served over `http://` instead of `https://`. Gmail, Outlook.com, and Apple Mail all upgrade or block mixed-content images. Affected assets:

The `image.emails.skechers.com` CDN appears to support HTTPS — this is a template configuration error, not an infrastructure gap.

**Missing `alt` text:** Two images lack `alt` attributes:
- `o.gif` (ink1000 open pixel) — minor; hidden pixel, acceptable to leave blank
- `49468f73-4651-4af3-bea2-61d1ae5db486.png` — unknown content; needs descriptive alt text for screen readers and image-off rendering

**`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`** is set globally. This disables user font-size accessibility preferences in WebKit clients (iOS Mail). Should be `100%`, not `none`.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**[FAIL] Empty greeting slot — broken subscriber experience.**

QA confirms the greeting renders as `"Hi ,"` — the first-name merge token resolved to an empty string. This indicates either:
- A subscriber record with a null/blank `first_name` field was not handled by a fallback (e.g., `%%=IIF(EMPTY(FirstName), "there", FirstName)=%%` in SFMC AMPscript)
- The fallback conditional was absent or misconfigured for this segment

This is a **send-time defect** — it has already shipped to affected subscribers with the broken greeting.

---

### 5. Compliance

**CAN-SPAM:** No issues found. Physical mailing address present in footer, unsubscribe mechanism present.

**Unsubscribe:** One-click unsubscribe link confirmed in footer. List-Unsubscribe header not auditable from HTML source alone, but Skechers/SFMC typically injects this at the MTA layer — should be verified against raw message headers.

**Authentication:** DKIM/SPF/DMARC cannot be assessed from HTML; verify `emails.skechers.com` subdomain is covered by the parent domain's DMARC policy (`_dmarc.skechers.com`).

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

UTM parameters not visible in the truncated HTML source. Given 26 tracked click links through `click.emails.skechers.com`, UTM decoration depends on how the ESP resolves redirects to `skechers.com`. Cannot confirm presence or absence without resolving a sample redirect chain.

The campaign ID `MKG_US_L30D_U_BEST_ENGAGEDOPEN_EN_06122026` embedded in the Krux pixel suggests this targets a 30-day lapsed-engaged segment — landing pages should reflect appropriate re-engagement offers rather than generic homepage routing.
