# Start checking off that gift list 🎁
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- Sally Beauty again. By my count it's about the twenty-third send since mid-September, and I'm tired of it. This one is a red hero with a bow and "Check off your list early," then a neat gift guide by price. The tiers are easy to scan, and I like that. But it's all hand cream, manicure kits and perfume. I'm shopping for a 5-year-old girl and a 9-year-old boy, and nothing here is for either of them.
- The "under $5 / $10 / $15" grid is the best part. I'm price-sensitive, so I'd skim it. But it has no real discount and no promo code. There's also no rewards message, and I'm a rewards person. After the Nov 9 "Finish your gift list today" and the Nov 8 "Black Friday came early" send, this feels like the same gift-list pitch for the third time in four days.
- Would I click? Probably not. I might tap one hand cream for a teacher gift, but I wouldn't forward it. Mark as read.

## What's working

- The "Gifts under $5 / $10 / $15" sections let me find my budget in a second.
- The red hero with the bow looks festive and has a clear headline.
- The Klarna strip and the "2 Hour Delivery / Free Shipping Orders $40+" icons help with last-minute planning.
- Each product has a brand name and a plain label, so I know what I'm looking at.

## What's weak

- Nothing is for kids. It's hand cream and perfume, and there's no velcro, light-up or sparkle for my daughter and nothing for my son.
- The hero has no price or offer. "Budget-friendly" is vague, and the "Shop now" link is small and plain, not a real button.
- The products have no prices on them, even though the sections are named by price. I have to guess which price each one is.
- There's no rewards or member message, and no promo code. I'd want to see my points or a bonus.
- It's the third gift-list email in a few days, so it feels repetitive.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one real offer in the hero, like "Extra $5 off $25 for rewards members," with an actual button.
- 2. Add prices and a "Shop all under $5" button to each tier.
- 3. Segment by household. A mom with kids could get stocking stuffers, bath sets and kid-friendly gifts.
- 4. Cut the gift-list sends to one a week and make each one different.
- 5. Put the rewards status near the top for members.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible markup is a standard XHTML table-based template with a mobile media query. The checks found an unresolved `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro in a third-party pixel, a non-HTTPS pixel, malformed `<meta>` tags, and no `lang` attribute. The HTML was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and link-level checks could not be confirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved macro:** `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&...` still contains the literal `[CACHEBUSTER]`. The ad-platform macro was never substituted, so the pixel can be cached or deduplicated and impressions may be undercounted.
- **Non-HTTPS pixel:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&...` is loaded over plain HTTP. Gmail's image proxy may upgrade it, but other clients may block it as mixed content, and the request leaks in transit.
- **Link destinations unverified:** 56 tracking links on click-redirect domains were skipped by the HTTP probe. Redirect chains, status codes and final destinations are unconfirmed.
- **Several third-party pixels:** `pxl.mon-trk.com`, `myvisualiq.net` and `dotomi.com` are all present. They add external dependencies and extra request failure points. They are not defects on their own.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed meta tags (source):**
  - `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`.
  - `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`.
  - Neither tag is functional, so no charset is effectively declared in the markup. Rendering usually falls back to the MIME header charset.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang`/`xml:lang`, so screen readers cannot pick a pronunciation language.
- **Image alt text (QA):**
  - Two images have a blank name and blank `src` in the QA output. Confirm whether these are `alt=""` decorative images or have no `alt` attribute at all.
  - The tracking pixels `003aZ00000s0I5yQAE`, `impression_pixel` and `UCMController` have no alt. For 1x1 pixels, `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` and `style="display:none"` is the safe pattern.
- **Font fallback:** `a{font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif}` has no `@font-face` for Founders Grotesk in the visible CSS. It falls back to Helvetica, which is acceptable, but the link font will differ from the Open Sans body.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The only unresolved token is `[CACHEBUSTER]` (see §2).
- The `pxl.mon-trk.com/.../003aZ00000s0I5yQAE` pixel path carries a Salesforce Contact ID (`003` prefix). It resolved to a real value, so it is not a merge failure. It is a per-recipient identifier in a third-party URL, which is a privacy note rather than a defect.
- No other broken tokens (`{{`, `%%`, `*|`) are visible in the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results:** the header was not found, and the QA check attributes this to the AgentMail relay. SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `em.sallybeauty.com` is **unverified, not failed**. It needs a check against raw headers.
- **Unsubscribe link, physical address and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers:** not verifiable. The footer is past the truncation point and no header data was provided.
- The From domain `em.sallybeauty.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is good practice.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. All 56 tracking links were skipped by the probe, and the visible source does not expose destination URLs or UTM parameters.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro.** Have the ESP or ad-server integration substitute it at send time, or drop the pixel.
2. **Serve the Dotomi pixel over HTTPS** (`https://login.dotomi.com/...`), or remove it if the vendor doesn't support HTTPS.
3. **Correct the meta tags:**
   - `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />`
   - `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge" />`
4. **Add `lang="en"` and `xml:lang="en"`** to `<html>`.
5. **Add `alt=""` to the decorative and tracking images**, and meaningful alt text to any content images flagged blank.
6. **Re-run the audit with the full HTML and raw headers** to verify `Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`, the footer address and unsubscribe link, and UTM parameters on the 56 skipped links. Consider a redirect-follow probe with a browser User-Agent for the tracking domains.
