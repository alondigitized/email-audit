# Your Skechers Plus Monthly Summary is Here
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-01**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is basically a loyalty statement, and my first reaction is "I already knew all this." It's the same 1000 points and the same $5 reward I've seen in every Skechers email for two weeks. The "Points Earned: 0 / Points Spent: 0" line is a bit embarrassing for a "summary," because it tells me I did nothing this month.
- The one thing that matters is the "Last chance, 20% off for members, ending tonight" block. It's the third send of the same Fall Haul offer, after 9/29 and this morning's "Last chance" email. Because I got that one hours ago, this banner feels like a rerun. The "We picked these just for you" row is six adult sneakers in beige, navy, white and red. Nothing is for a 5-year-old girl or a 9-year-old boy. Kids is just a plain text link in a list.
- Would I click? Maybe, only if I hadn't already shopped the Fall Haul, and only to check my balance. I wouldn't forward it. I'd skim it, remember I have $5 sitting there, and move on.

## What's working

- My 1000 points and the "$5 reward" progress bar are the first thing I see, and that's the kind of loyalty cue I respond to.
- "20% off for members, auto-applied at checkout" is clear and easy to understand. I don't have to hunt for a code.
- "Ending tonight" is real urgency, and it's backed by the dates in the fine print.
- The "Shop now" and "View my account" buttons are big enough to tap on my phone.
- The Skechers header is recognizable and the layout is clean.

## What's weak

- "Points Earned: 0 / Points Spent: 0" makes the summary feel empty. It gives me no reason to feel rewarded.
- "Picked just for you" is six adult shoes. There's no kids' velcro, light-up or sparkle, and nothing in blue or black for my son.
- The product row has no names, prices or sale tags, and each shoe doesn't clearly link anywhere specific. The images also float with a lot of blank space.
- Kids is buried in a text list, the same as it was in the 9/17 and 9/27 emails.
- This is the third send of the 20% promo in three days, so the urgency feels manufactured.
- The 20% offer excludes sale styles, and I only find that out in the tiny fine print.

## Recommendations

- 1. Personalize the "picked for you" row using household data. Show kids' shoes, ideally one girl's and one boy's, with sale prices and member prices.
- 2. Make the points section useful. Replace the zeros with "You're $X away from your next reward" or "Spend your $5 today, it stacks with 20% off."
- 3. Don't send this the same day as another "last chance" email. Suppress people who got the morning send, or combine the two.
- 4. Move the Kids link to the top of the nav and add a "Back to school / everyday play" tile.
- 5. Move the "excludes sale" note up next to the 20% copy.

## Technical Audit

1. **Technical Summary**
The email has a clean QA pass (0 errors), but the source has several real issues. One tracking pixel has a malformed URL, a dev-environment image host leaks into production, and many images load over plain `http://`. HTML source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe, and the 37 skipped tracking links could not be checked.

2. **Link & Tracking Issues**
- **Malformed pixel URL (confirmed):** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…">` has no scheme. It resolves as a relative path and will 404 or hit the wrong host, so this deliverability and seed-list pixel never fires.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108404282&mi_ecmp=TRG_US_EN_MONTHLYSUMMARY_1_10012026_A` is plain HTTP, has no alt text, and sits in its own 1x1 table. Clients that block mixed content will drop it, and some will show an insecure-content warning.
- **Third-party data-sharing pixels:** Three `beacon.krxd.net` pixels (Salesforce DMP/Krux) pass a SHA-256 hashed identifier (`partner_uid=adf27fee…`) and a subscriber ID (`partner_uid=122277199`). They use `width="0" height="0"`, which some clients ignore, so they may render as a visible 1px element. This is a privacy and compliance concern, not only a rendering one.
- **Click links:** 37 tracking links were skipped by QA (HTTP probe skipped on click-redirect domains). Their destinations are unverified.

3. **Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Dev host in production:** `https://image.devemails.skechers.com/lib/fe33157075640674761d74/m/1/summary-tier-blue-angle.png` is served from the dev image domain. It is the only `devemails` host (the rest use `image.emails.skechers.com`). It may be unavailable to recipients or exposed to environment-level auth. It also has no alt text, and this appears to be the tier badge, which carries the message content.
- **Missing alt text:**
  - `summary-tier-blue-angle.png`
  - `49468f73-4651-4af3-bea2-61d1ae5db486.png`
  - `o.gif` (a tracking pixel, so `alt=""` is acceptable but absent)
- **HTTP image sources:** 12 images load via `http://image.emails.skechers.com/...`. These include the logo (`dde00662-…png`), the app-store badges, the AfterPay and Curbside icons, and the social icons. Gmail and Apple Mail proxy them, but other clients may block them or warn.
- **Preheader hidden with `display:none !important` and `font-size:1px`:** This is acceptable, but there is no zero-width padding after the preheader, so body text may leak into the inbox preview.
- **Meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv`, so it declares no charset. `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` has no `http-equiv` either, so both are inert. `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport tag blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility issue.
- **Layout tables lack `role="presentation"`:** The visible tables (`<table class="widthFull" width="640" …>`) have none.
- **`* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }`:** This disables iOS text scaling.

4. **Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. No unresolved `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` tokens appear in the visible source. The subscriber-specific values (`partner_uid=122277199`, `mi_u=8108404282`) are populated. The points total sits in the truncated portion, so it was not verified.

5. **Compliance**
- **Not verifiable:** The unsubscribe link, physical address, and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are not in the truncated source or the QA data. SPF, DKIM, and DMARC results were also not provided.
- **Sender:** `no-reply@emails.skechers.com` is a no-reply From address, a minor deliverability and CAN-SPAM-adjacent concern (recipients can't reply to opt out).
- **Third-party pixels:** The Krux and ink1000 pixels send identifiers to third parties. Confirm the privacy policy discloses this.

6. **Email-to-Site Continuity**
- **Campaign ID:** `TRG_US_EN_MONTHLYSUMMARY_1_10012026_A` appears in the `krxd` and `ink1000` pixels, which confirms it is a triggered monthly send.
- **UTM parameters:** None could be checked, because the 37 click links were skipped and the source is truncated. Whether links carry `utm_*` params or SFMC `_ri_`/`_ei_` identifiers is unconfirmed.
- **Landing page alignment:** Not verifiable for the same reason.

7. **Recommendations**
1. Fix the scheme on the Return Path pixel: `https://pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?...`.
2. Replace `image.devemails.skechers.com` with the production `image.emails.skechers.com` path and confirm the asset is publicly reachable.
3. Switch all 12 `http://image.emails.skechers.com` and `http://www.ink1000.com` references to `https://`.
4. Add meaningful `alt` text to `summary-tier-blue-angle.png` and `49468f73-…png`. Add `alt=""` to `o.gif`.
5. Correct the meta tags to `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`. Remove `maximum-scale=1`. Add `role="presentation"` to layout tables.
6. Re-run QA on the full HTML to check the unsubscribe link, the physical address, the headers, UTM parameters, and the 37 click-tracked links (resolve redirects and confirm landing pages).
7. Use `display:none;max-height:0;overflow:hidden` on the third-party pixels instead of `width="0" height="0"`.
