# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay, first impression: this is a patriotic seasonal push — the "Red, White & Blue Collection" is front and center, and it's clearly timed for Memorial Day weekend. I appreciate the BOGO deal right at the top and the loyalty banner below it, but as a mom who came here to find shoes for a five-year-old and a nine-year-old, this hero is talking entirely to grown-up me — and honestly, I'm the last person on my list today.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF banner** — it's the first thing I read, in bold white on blue. That's exactly the kind of deal that makes me think "okay I can grab something for both kids in one order."
- **Members reward strip** — "Earn a $5 Reward on $75+" with a deadline of 5/25 creates a reason to login right now rather than browse as a guest.
- **Seasonal hero** — The Red, White & Blue Collection is timely. It's Memorial Day weekend; this doesn't feel stale.
- **Horizontal nav chips** — Slip-ins, Sandals, Arch Fit, BOBs are tappable and visible at a glance; easy to scan with one thumb.

## What's weak

- **No kids path above the fold** — I'm here for a 5-year-old and a 9-year-old. Neither the hero, the nav strip (what's visible), nor any secondary module calls out kids. I have to go hunting.
- **No CTA button** — The hero is all imagery and text; there's no "Shop the Collection" or "Shop Kids" button visible. I have to figure out what to tap.
- **Nav appears cut off** — I can see "Slip-ins, Sandals, Arch Fit, BOBs, G…" and then it's truncated. "Kids" or "Girls/Boys" isn't visible in the horizontal scroll, which is where a mom instinctively looks first.
- **Hero imagery is 100% adult** — a man in jeans and a woman in a navy dress. Not a child, not a family, not a backyard barbecue scene. I don't see myself or my kids anywhere.
- **No trust signals** — no review count, free-returns badge, or star ratings above the fold.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Kids" chip to the visible nav row** — right now I can see Slip-ins, Sandals, Arch Fit, BOBs, and a cut-off letter. Pinning "Kids" as the first or second chip immediately tells moms they're in the right place.
- **Drop a family/lifestyle panel below the patriotic hero** — a Memorial Day barbecue shot with kids in colorful sneakers would let me self-identify without changing the campaign theme.
- **Add a Shop CTA button to the hero** — even "Shop the Collection" in white on blue would give me somewhere to tap instead of staring at imagery.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold** — if it exists, it should be visible before the fold. A price-sensitive mom is doing AOV math before she taps anything.
