# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay, first impression: this is a clean, modern mobile site that knows it's running a deal — BOGO 50% is the first thing I see, which honestly makes me stop scrolling. The brand looks like it's leaning hard into the Slip-ins line right now with a summer lifestyle vibe. It reads as a solid mid-tier athletic brand, not discount-y, not luxury — but it's clearly pitching adults at a pool, not a mom trying to figure out which sneakers fit her second-grader's wide feet.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% banner at the very top** — this is the first thing I see and it lands. As a price-conscious mom I immediately do the mental math: two pairs of kids' shoes could be a real deal here.
- **"Now until 5/25! Members Only - Earn a $5 Reward on $75+"** — I love a reward I can see coming. The deadline gives me a reason to act this weekend, not next month.
- **"Kids'" tab visible in the bottom nav** — I don't have to dig through a hamburger menu to find what I actually came for. One tap to my category.
- **"Recently Viewed" tab** — that's a smart retention feature for someone like me who shops in scattered 10-minute windows. I can pick up where I left off between interruptions.
- **"SHOP NOW" button** — big, white, full-width. Even half-distracted (which I always am), I can't miss it.

## What's weak

- **Zero kids/family content above the fold** — the entire hero is an adult man and woman at a pool. For a mom whose primary shopping goal is her kids, I'm immediately aware this page wasn't built for me.
- **"SHOP NOW" is a throwaway CTA** — shop what? Slip-ins? Kids' shoes? Everything? Generic CTAs make me pause instead of tap.
- **No product imagery or categories for Kids' above the fold** — I have to know to look for the "Kids'" tab. Nothing in the hero is pulling me toward the category I care most about.
- **No trust signals visible** — no star ratings, no "free returns," no review counts. First-time or semi-regular visitors (like me on a new device) get no reassurance before they start browsing.
- **No free shipping callout** — with BOGO running, I want to know: do I get free shipping too? That threshold info would push me to add more to cart. I don't see it.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop Kids'" secondary CTA under the hero** — even a simple pill button underneath "SHOP NOW" that says "Shop Kids' Shoes" would immediately capture parents who landed here for their kids, not themselves.
- **Swap or split the hero for a family-use-case module** — a lifestyle shot of a kid running in Slip-ins or sneakers alongside the adult content signals to me: yes, this is your store too.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold in the top banner** — rotate the banner or add a second line: "BOGO 50% OFF + Free shipping on $X+." That combo is a conversion driver for multi-pair purchases.
- **Add a star-rating or "X reviews" callout near the hero product** — even "47,000 5-star reviews on Slip-ins" in small text below the hero copy would dramatically increase my trust before I tap.
