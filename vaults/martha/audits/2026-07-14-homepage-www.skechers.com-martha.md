# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-07-14
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-14**
## Executive summary

- Okay, first impression: this is a sneaker brand doing a big sporty moment right now. The "GAME ON!" energy with those bold retro kicks reads very athletic, very male-coded — like they're going for a footwear drop rather than helping a mom of two find shoes for her kids' back-to-summer. There are deals, which I appreciate, but I'd have to dig to find anything for my 5-year-old or my 9-year-old.

## What's working

- **Dual promo strip**: The top bar promo code (SUMMER, 20% off) stacked above the "Up To 40% OFF" secondary banner gives me two reasons to stay before I even look at the hero — that's smart for a price-sensitive shopper.
- **Quick nav tabs**: Slip-ins, Sandals, Arch Fit, BOBs sit right below the secondary banner — these are one-tap shortcuts and Sandals is directly useful for summer kids' shopping.
- **Bold summer campaign signal**: The SUMMER code and "Summer Favorites" copy tell me they're running something real right now, which makes me trust the deal more than a vague "sale" banner.
- **Clean mobile layout**: Nav, banners, tabs, hero — nothing is crammed or breaking. On my phone that matters.

## What's weak

- **"GAME ON!" hero is for someone else**: Retro sneakers on turf, checkered race flags, "EST. 1992" branding — this is nostalgia-fueled sneaker culture content aimed at adult men. I'm here for my kids' summer shoes.
- **No kids' category visible at all**: The quick nav shows Slip-ins, Sandals, Arch Fit, BOBs — none of those say "Kids." I'd have to hunt through the hamburger menu, which I might not bother doing.
- **No loyalty hook above the fold**: Skechers has a rewards program. I know because I'm a member. But there's zero acknowledgment of me as a returning customer — no "your balance," no "members-only price." That's a miss.
- **"Shop | Details" links are tiny**: These are not real CTAs for mobile. Two small underlined text links next to a big colorful hero is not a conversion path — it's an afterthought.
- **No people in the imagery**: Just shoes floating on turf. I want to see a kid actually wearing the shoe, or a family, something I can project onto.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero for a family/kids lifestyle shot**: A kid running in Skechers this summer beats three floating adult sneakers. Even a split hero — one adult pair, one kid pair — would signal "we've got your whole family."
- **Add a "Kids" quick nav tab**: Replace "BOBs" or add a fifth tab — this is the most direct path to my shopping goal and it isn't there.
- **Surface the Skechers Rewards hook in the banner strip**: One line like "Members earn 2× points this summer — log in" above the SUMMER code turns a generic promo into a loyalty moment.
- **Make the CTA a real button**: A solid "Shop Summer Deals" button below the hero copy would take 10 minutes to ship and would meaningfully increase taps.
