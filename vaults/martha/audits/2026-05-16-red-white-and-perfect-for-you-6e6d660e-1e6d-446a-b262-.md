# Red, White, and Perfect for You
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so I opened this and the first thing I noticed is — wait, is that JSON code in my preview? Again. I literally reviewed an email from Skechers on May 14 with the exact same issue, and they *still* haven't fixed it. That's not a great first impression when I'm scrolling through my inbox on a Saturday morning with my kids yelling in the background.
- Once I'm inside, though? The email actually looks decent. The patriotic Stars, Stripes & Style theme makes sense for Memorial Day weekend, the BOGO 50% is front and center, and the red/white/blue color blocking is clean and intentional. I can see the deal without scrolling, I can see the women's shoe, I can see a SHOP NOW button. Structurally it's fine.
- But — and this is a real "but" — I primarily open Skechers emails to shop for my kids. Lily needs shoes for summer camp. Jake's sneakers are basically destroyed. There is no featured kids section anywhere in this email. Kids gets one tiny text link buried in the category row at the bottom. I almost scrolled past it completely. I'd mark this read, maybe click through to women's if the shoe catches me, but I wouldn't forward it or feel like Skechers actually knows me.

## What's working

- **BOGO offer is impossible to miss** — "Buy One, Get One 50% Off / 25% off a single pair" is above the fold before the hero image even loads. That's good placement.
- **Memorial Day timing is bang-on** — "Stars, Stripes & Style / Celebrate the long weekend in comfort" feels current and relevant for mid-May. Not stale.
- **Color story is executed well** — The red/white/blue shoe swatch row is a nice visual shortcut; I know immediately what this collection is about.
- **No broken images in the body** — After that May 14 rendering disaster in the inbox, the email body itself is clean.
- **Women's section is featured** — I see a women's shoe (the red/white canvas) with a dedicated CTA. It's not invisible.

## What's weak

- **Preview text is raw JSON code. Again.** The May 14 send ("Your Smoothest Stride Yet") had the same exact bug — Skechers clearly knows about it and still hasn't fixed it. It looks broken and makes me wonder if the email is even safe to open.
- **Zero kids content in the hero or dedicated sections** — Men's and Women's get featured sections with full product photos. Kids gets a single text link in the footer nav. For a mom shopping for two kids, this is a real miss.
- **"SHOP NOW" is the laziest CTA possible** — Every section should tell me *what* I'm shopping. "SHOP KIDS' SUMMER PICKS" would actually get my thumb moving.
- **No loyalty or rewards callout** — I'm a Skechers member and there's no "member exclusive" or "earn points on BOGO" anywhere. I've gotten more personalized treatment from emails that scored lower.
- **The model reads "young fashion brand," not "mom comfort brand"** — The hero woman in the navy mini dress is lovely, but she's not signaling anything I personally relate to.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the preview text leak — this is a P0 issue.** This is the second consecutive week this JSON schema is showing up in inboxes. Someone in dev needs to move the JSON-LD block out of the `<head>` preview zone, or wrap it so inbox clients don't pull it. Highest priority.
- 2. **Add a Kids' section with the same treatment as Women's and Men's.** A photo of patriotic Skechers for kids (sparkly red sneakers for girls, navy lace-ups for boys) with a SHOP KIDS button would dramatically increase relevance for the parent segment.
- 3. **Make CTA copy specific to each section.** "SHOP WOMEN'S PICKS" / "SHOP KIDS' SUMMER SHOES" / "SHOP THE COLLECTION" — generic "SHOP NOW" wastes a click opportunity.
- 4. **Add an explicit deadline to the offer.** "Offer ends May 26" or "Memorial Day weekend only" makes the BOGO feel urgent rather than just ambient.
- 5. **Surface the Skechers rewards angle.** Even a one-liner like "Members: earn double points on BOGO orders" would make me feel seen.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Stars, Stripes & 50% Off — Shop the Memorial Day Sale`
- **Subject Alt B:** `BOGO 50% Off This Weekend — Patriotic Picks for the Whole Family`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Red, white & blue styles for women, men, and kids — buy one, get one 50% off through May 26.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Celebrate the long weekend in new Skechers. BOGO 50% off — shop women's, men's & kids' now.`

## Technical Audit — Skechers "Red, White, and Perfect for You"

### 1. Technical Summary

One confirmed broken experience (empty merge token in greeting) and a widespread HTTP image-serving pattern that risks silent blocking in security-conscious clients. All 27 tracked links route through a click-redirect domain and could not be probed directly.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**No broken destination links confirmed** — all 27 links pass through `click.emails.skechers.com` redirect and were not probed.

**Multiple tracking pixels present:**
- Open pixel: `click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx?45AODH3UQB4EPFLRCK6CAVQAR4...`
- Krux/Salesforce DMP user-match beacons: `beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif` (2 calls — one SHA-256 hashed UID, one raw `subid=122277199`)
- Krux ad impression pixel: `beacon.krxd.net/1x1_ad_impression.gif?confid=so3yexmpm&campaignid=MKG_US_NA_U_MULTI_ENGAGEDOPEN_EN_05162026`
- ink1000 pixel: `http://www.ink1000.com/...` (HTTP, not HTTPS — see §3)
- Return Path seed pixel: `pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif` — **missing protocol prefix** (`http://` or `https://`), making this a relative URL that will resolve incorrectly or fail silently.

**Evidence:** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1dbf0dcef79cbad82c12fe25342701e4499" ...>` — no scheme.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**HTTP images (14 confirmed):** All image assets served from `image.emails.skechers.com` use `http://`, not `https://`. Gmail, Outlook.com, and Apple Mail proxy or block mixed-content images. Affected assets include the Skechers logo, all footer icons (App Store, Google Play, Curbside Pickup, AfterPay, Find A Store, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube), and a hero image.

- Example: `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`
- ink1000 open-tracking pixel also HTTP: `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif`

**Missing alt text (2 images):**
- `o.gif` (ink1000 tracking pixel) — minor, tracking pixels typically use `alt=""`
- `49468f73-4651-4af3-bea2-61d1ae5db486.png` — unknown content; if this is a visible image, missing alt text is an accessibility failure for screen readers

**CSS conflicts — `.show` class defined twice** across two `<style>` blocks with identical declarations (`max-width: 480px` and `max-width: 680px`). The 480px block will silently win due to cascade order, suppressing the 680px rule. This may cause layout issues on tablets.

**`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`** set globally — this disables text scaling on iOS, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure for users who rely on browser zoom.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**FAIL — Empty greeting:** QA confirms the greeting renders as `"Hi ,"` — the first-name merge token resolved to an empty string. This indicates either a missing fallback value in the ESP template or a segment that includes contacts without a `FirstName` field populated.

**No other unrendered token syntax** (e.g., `%%`, `{{`, `[[`) visible in the truncated HTML source.

---

### 5. Compliance

**Preheader/subject mismatch (minor):** Subject line is "Red, White, and Perfect for You"; preheader text is "BOGO 50% Off These Memorial Day Must Haves." These are not contradictory but the preheader is more informative — this is a missed opportunity, not a compliance issue.

**CAN-SPAM:** Physical mailing address and unsubscribe link presence cannot be confirmed from the truncated source, but these are standard in Skechers sends. Flagged for manual verification if the footer was cut off.

**Data leakage in tracking pixels:** The Krux pixel passes `partner_uid=122277199` (raw subscriber ID, unhashed) alongside a SHA-256 hashed variant. Sending an unhashed internal subscriber ID to a third-party DMP (`krxd.net`) should be reviewed against the current privacy policy and any applicable state privacy laws (CCPA, etc.).

- Evidence: `beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif?partner=LprH63a7_sfmc_100007688_subid&partner_uid=122277199`

**Authentication headers:** Not assessable from HTML source alone — DKIM/SPF/DMARC must be verified from raw email headers.

All outbound links pass through `click.emails.skechers.com` redirect — UTM parameters cannot be confirmed without resolving the redirects. The campaign ID `MKG_US_NA_U_MULTI_ENGAGEDOPEN_EN_05162026` appears in pixel calls, confirming campaign tagging exists at the tracking layer.
