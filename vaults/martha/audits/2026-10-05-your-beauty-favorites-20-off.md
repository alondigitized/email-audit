# Your beauty favorites, 20% off 👏
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-05**
## Executive summary

- Sally Beauty again. This is the fifth or sixth send I've gotten from them since mid-September, and I'm still holding a pile of "Appiversary" emails in my head. The 20% off sitewide code is at least clear. The giant yellow circle with BIGDEALS20 can't be missed. But I'm a mom shopping for a 5-year-old and a 9-year-old, and nothing here is for them. Hair color boxes aren't what I'm looking for on a Saturday with my phone.
- The subject says "your beauty favorites," which feels like a stretch. It's not personalized, and it's the same promo they've been blasting. The deal is also less generous than the fine print makes it sound: it excludes clearance, can't be combined, and the code expires 10/7. There's one more problem: the "Holiday Edition" badge in early October is a bit much.
- Would I click? Probably not. I'd skim it, see professional hair dye, and archive it. I wouldn't forward it to a friend either, unless she dyes her own hair at home.

## What's working

- The 20% off sitewide offer and the BIGDEALS20 code are huge and easy to read, so I get the deal in two seconds.
- The black "Shop now" button stands out against the pale background.
- The rewards strip says $5 off $25 for signing up, and the 3x points on app purchases appeals to my loyalty-program side.
- The Klarna "4 interest-free payments" note is helpful when I'm price-sensitive.
- The icon row (free shipping $40+, 2-hour delivery, pickup) is quick and scannable.

## What's weak

- Every product shown is professional hair color (ion, L'Oréal HiColor). There's nothing for kids, nothing for a mom's everyday routine, and nothing that feels like "my favorites."
- The "Holiday Edition" ring text is cut off at the edges and feels premature, so it looks a little sloppy.
- The fine print is a wall of text. "Not combinable, excludes clearance" sits in tiny type under the button, and the 10/7 expiry is buried at the bottom.
- The "Shop now" links under each product are small tap targets on a phone.
- Three competing offers sit at the top (rewards signup, in-store pickup, 2-hour delivery) before I even reach the hero, which dilutes the deal.
- This is another near-identical promo after the 10/2 and 10/1 emails, so I'm fatigued.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the real deadline ("Ends 10/7") in the hero, right next to the code. That's the one thing that would make me act, and right now it's hidden at the bottom.
- 2. Segment the products. Show something other than permanent hair color to people who don't buy hair color, such as hair care, tools, or bestsellers based on past purchases.
- 3. Cut the stacked top banners down to one message so the 20% is the first thing I see.
- 4. Make the product links full-width buttons or tap-friendly cards instead of small underlined text.
- 5. Space out the sends. After "Last chance" on 9/30 and "Spooky season savings" on 10/1, another 20% off promo makes the urgency feel fake.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on four content images:** the Evergage `summary.png` images (`.../campaign/xNP1l/mzy21/1..4/summary.png`) have no alt text. They look like dynamic product recommendations, so screen-reader and images-off users get nothing.

- **Head meta tags are malformed:**
