# Get $5 off $25 when you join
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-11**
## Executive summary

- Sally Beauty again. I've gotten about a dozen of these since mid-September, and this is the first one that asks me to do something useful. A $5 off $25 rewards signup is clear. I do like rewards programs, so my thumb hovered for a second. The pink hero with the big "Join Sally.Rewards & save" headline is easy to read on my phone.
- Then I scrolled and it fell apart for me. The product grid is all hair color: ion and L'Oréal permanent creme dye. I'm a mom shopping for a 5-year-old and a 9-year-old. Nothing here is for kids, and I'm not shopping for myself right now either. The "Use Your Reward on These Favorites" section feels like the email forgot its own point.
- Would I click? Probably not today. If I were already buying hair color, the $5 off would be nice, but I'm not. I'd mark it read. I wouldn't forward it, unless a friend were doing her roots at home. Then I'd text her the $5 deal.

## What's working

- The headline "Join Sally.Rewards & save on your wish list" tells me the deal in one glance, and the "Get $5 off $25+" line is easy to find.
- The black "Join Sally.Rewards" button stands out against the pink, so it's easy to tap with one thumb.
- The two-tier Preferred and Elite comparison makes the program easy to understand. Free to join, 1 point per $1 spent, and free shipping at Elite is a perk I'd actually notice.
- The "15% off" strip for buy online, pick up in-store is a second saving that doesn't compete with the main offer.
- The Klarna and "2 hour delivery" icons near the bottom are reassuring for a busy parent who wants things fast.

## What's weak

- Nothing is for kids. The four products are all permanent hair color, with no gifts and no family angle.
- The fine print is a wall of tiny text. "Must open a new Sally Beauty Rewards loyalty account" and "no coupon required" are buried at the bottom. I had to hunt for the catch, which makes me trust the offer a little less.
- The hero image is a hand holding a wrapped gift next to press-on nails. It's pretty, but it doesn't say "$5 off" or "rewards" at a glance.
- There are two "Join" buttons and a "Join Rewards" button, plus four "Shop now" links and a 15% off link. That's a lot of paths for one message.
- The offer is "October only." It expires 10/31, but nothing in the email makes it feel urgent today.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in the hero image or the very first headline: "$5 off $25 — just for joining." Right now the reward is a small line under a big brand headline.
- 2. Pull the key terms out of the footer: "New members only, one-time use, expires 10/31." One honest line above the fold would build trust.
- 3. Make the product section match the recipient. Show a mix of categories, or use what I've bought or browsed. For me that means a gift-y, family-friendly pick, not permanent hair dye.
- 4. Pick one button and cut the repeats. One "Join" button and one shopping path is enough.
- 5. Add a countdown or a date, like "Ends Oct 31," next to the button.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Sally Beauty, "Get $5 off $25 when you join"

Only the head and CSS of the HTML were available, and no message headers were supplied. Findings below come from that source and the QA output. Anything outside them is marked "not verifiable."

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with responsive CSS. The main defects are an unresolved ad-pixel macro (`r=[CACHEBUSTER]`), an empty-`src` image, an HTTP third-party pixel, and widespread missing `alt` attributes. The QA summary shows "100% pass rate, 0 warnings," yet nine `[WARN]` items are listed, so the scoring doesn't reflect them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved macro:** `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&ao=883&aca=-11&si=-11&ci=-11&pi=-11&ad=-11...`
  - The literal `[CACHEBUSTER]` was never substituted. The pixel can be cached by proxies, such as Gmail's image proxy, which undercounts impressions.
  - The `-11` values (`aca`, `si`, `ci`, `pi`, `ad`) look like default or unpopulated placeholders, so campaign attribution on this pixel is probably incomplete.
- **Empty image source:** one `<img>` has `src=""` and no alt (QA: `Image missing alt text: : src:`). Empty `src` can trigger a broken-image icon or a re-request of the page URL, depending on the client.
- **Insecure pixel:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990...` uses plain HTTP (QA: "Non-HTTPS source may be blocked"). It will likely be blocked or rewritten by some clients, so that tracking call is lost.
- **Unverified links:** 52 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains, and 404s were not tested.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** the QA flagged these images.
  - `summary.png` ×4: `https://sallyholdings.us-4.evergage.com/api/dataset/sallybeauty_prod/campaign/oBZKn/pI601/{1..4}/summary.png`. These are dynamic content blocks, so a screen reader or image-blocked client gets nothing.
  - Tracking pixels: `pxl.mon-trk.com/.../003aZ00000s0I5yQAE`, `myvisualiq` and `dotomi`. These should carry `alt=""` explicitly, plus `width="1" height="1"` and `style="display:none"` if not already set. This can't be confirmed from the truncated source.
  - The empty-`src` image from section 2.
- **Malformed meta tags in `<head>`:**
  - `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` is missing `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, so it declares nothing.
  - `<meta content="IE=edge" />` is missing `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`, so it is inert.
  - Impact is low because the MIME header normally carries the charset, but the tags are invalid.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` or `xml:lang`, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Font stack:** `a{font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif}` has no corresponding `@font-face`. It silently falls back to Helvetica on most clients, which is harmless but unintended.
- **Dynamic images:** the Evergage `summary.png` blocks are server-rendered at open time. If the Evergage endpoint is slow or fails, the blocks render empty with no alt fallback.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Unresolved token:** `[CACHEBUSTER]` is a literal unreplaced macro (see section 2).
- **Possible PII in image URLs:** the Evergage URLs carry `?userId=martha.st...` (truncated in the QA output).
  - If this is an email address or name fragment, it is exposed in a GET query string. That means it lands in proxy logs and in the third-party endpoint's logs.
  - The full value is not visible, so confirm what is actually in the parameter.
- **Contact identifier in path:** the Salesforce-style ID `003aZ00000s0I5yQAE` (a Contact record prefix) is embedded in the `pxl.mon-trk.com` pixel path. This is expected for Marketing Cloud, but the third-party domain receives it.
- No other visible tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) in the available source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Not verifiable:** the headers were not provided, so SPF, DKIM, DMARC alignment, `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` can't be assessed. The footer was truncated, so the unsubscribe link and physical postal address can't be confirmed.
- **From domain:** `em.sallybeauty.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is good practice. Alignment was not checked.
- **Third-party pixels:** the dotomi (Epsilon/Conversant) and VisualIQ pixels are cross-site tracking beacons. These carry disclosure obligations in privacy policies and can matter for GDPR/CCPA consent. This is a policy flag, not a code defect.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not verifiable:** destinations are behind 52 click-redirect URLs that were not resolved, so UTM presence and landing page alignment can't be checked.
- No UTM parameters are visible on the direct (non-redirect) URLs in the available excerpt.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the CACHEBUSTER macro:** have the ESP substitute a unique value at send time, or remove the pixel if it isn't supported. Also check that the `-11` placeholders in the VisualIQ pixel are real campaign IDs.
2. **Remove the empty-`src` `<img>`** or populate it.
3. **Serve the dotomi pixel over HTTPS** (`https://login.dotomi.com/...`) or drop it.
4. **Add `alt` attributes:** descriptive alt on the four `summary.png` blocks, and `alt=""` on all tracking pixels.
5. **Correct the meta tags:** `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
6. **Add `lang="en"`** (and `xml:lang`) to `<html>`.
7. **Review the `userId` parameter** on the Evergage URLs. Use an opaque ID rather than anything derived from the email or name.
8. **Re-run the audit with full headers and full HTML** to check authentication, `List-Unsubscribe`, the footer address, and UTM/landing continuity. Also resolve a sample of the redirect links.
9. **Fix the QA scoring:** "0 warnings" alongside nine `[WARN]` entries means the summary counter isn't counting them.
