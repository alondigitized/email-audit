# Last chance for 20% off your purchase
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-13**
## Executive summary

- Same offer, same "Hi, Martha," same 1000 points sitting up top, same "20% off" banner in the same navy blue I've now seen every single day this week — I said it on the 9th, I said it on the 10th, and here we are on the 12th, 13th... whatever day this is, still EXTRA20. What's actually different this time is the whole body of the email is just men's and women's shoe categories — Casuals, Athletics, Wide Fit, Work & Safety — with zero mention of kids anywhere. Not one velcro sneaker, not one light-up sole, not a single "Kids'" category tile like they at least bothered with a couple sends ago.
- That's a real miss for me specifically. I'm not shopping for a wide-fit work boot for myself on a Saturday afternoon while my kids are occupied — I'm hunting for something in a 5T with sparkles or a durable lace-up in a boy's size 1. This reads like a generic "everyone" email, not one built for a mom with two kids to outfit. I'll skim it for two seconds, see the 20% off is real, and probably just go straight to the app and tap "Kids" myself rather than clicking anything in this email.
- Would I forward it? No. Would I click? Maybe, only to redeem the code — not because anything in the body pulled me in.

## What's working

- The "LAST CHANCE" + "20% OFF" banner is impossible to miss, right up top with a clear "SHOP NOW" button.
- My points balance and reward eligibility ($5 reward available) are front and center — that's the loyalty hook that actually works on me.
- Clean, no render bugs — images all loaded, text is legible, nothing overlapping.
- Category tiles (Casuals, Athletics, Wide Fit, Work & Safety) are visually distinct and easy to tap on mobile.

## What's weak

- Zero kids' content — no girls' or boys' shoes, no "Kids" hero tile, nothing velcro/light-up/pink-purple or lace-up/blue-black. For a mom who shops almost exclusively for her kids, this is the single biggest gap.
- The hero models are a young professional couple in city-street outfits — stylish, but doesn't speak to "mom buying school shoes."
- It's a genuinely long scroll (five separate category sections) before I even hit "Shop Additional Styles," which buries the kids link way at the bottom as a plain text row, easy to miss.
- Same exact urgency language ("Last Chance") as last week's actual last-chance email — makes the deadline feel fake, like it's always "last chance."

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a Kids' hero tile above the fold — right after the offer banner, before Casuals/Athletics — since that's my actual shopping mission, not an afterthought at the bottom.
- 2. Swap one of the two hero lifestyle photos for a parent-and-kid or just kids' shoe shot so the demographic signal actually lands for someone like me.
- 3. Vary the "Last Chance" framing — I've now seen this exact urgency beat multiple times this week and it's lost all credibility.
- 4. Trim the body — five stacked category sections is a lot of scrolling on mobile before getting to anything actionable for me.
