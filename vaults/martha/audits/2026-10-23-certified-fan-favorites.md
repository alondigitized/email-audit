# Certified fan favorites
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-23**
## Executive summary

- Skechers is back in my inbox for the second day in a row with the same 20% member discount. I see "Hi, Martha," my 1000 points and the $5 reward at the top, so it knows me. After that it's a big yellow "BEST of the BEST" banner and three rows of adult shoes. My two kids don't show up anywhere. Yesterday's "Your weekend just got better!" put the 20% off front and center. Today the offer is a small navy strip above a giant headline.
- I'd probably open it for the discount, skim it, and close it. It's pretty, but it's a catalog and not something built for me. I wouldn't forward it. I'd click only if I was already planning to shop this weekend, and I'd go straight to the Kids link near the bottom.

## What's working

- "Hi, Martha," my 1000 points and the $5 reward with a REDEEM button at the top make me feel like a known member. That's the fourth send in a row that does this, and I like it.
- "Members take 20% OFF and earn a $5 reward on $75+" is a real offer. It says "auto-applied at checkout," so I don't have to hunt for a code.
- "Valid thru 10/26" gives me a real deadline, so I can plan to shop this weekend.
- The Most Comfortable / Most Fashionable / Most Technical sections are easy to skim, and each has its own button.
- Klarna/Afterpay, the app download and the "Shop additional styles" list are handy for someone who shops on her phone.

## What's weak

- Nothing is for a 5-year-old girl or a 9-year-old boy. There's no velcro, no light-up soles, no sparkle, no durable lace-ups. Kids only appear as a "Gifts for Kids" button near the bottom.
- The 20% offer is a small strip, and "BEST of the BEST" is what grabs my eye. The deadline and the "see below for details" are tiny gray text. The exclusions are in dense fine print at the very bottom.
- It feels repetitive. The 10/22 email already led with the same 20% sitewide. Before that came a week of "2 for $100" banners, and now there's another "best of" send.
- The man in the shirt takes up a whole screen on my phone, and he isn't who I'm shopping for. Two of the three models also carry a small "AI-generated model" label, which makes the email feel less personal.
- The preheader is a long tracking link in the inbox preview, so I get nothing useful there.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by household. For a customer like me, replace one of the adult blocks with "Kids' best sellers": a light-up velcro sneaker for girls and a lace-up sneaker for boys, each with a price and a member price.
- 2. Make the 20% off the hero. Put "20% OFF for members" in large type, put the end date on the same line, and put "Shop the sale" right under it.
- 3. Fix the preheader so it shows real copy and not a URL.
- 4. Cut the three long adult sections to one compact "Most Comfortable / Fashionable / Technical" row, so a phone user reaches the offer and the kids' link faster.
- 5. Change the subject when the offer repeats day to day. Don't send the same discount under a "best of" label.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Martha, 20% off kids' sneakers thru Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your member 20% + $5 reward ends 10/26`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Light-up, velcro & lace-up picks for school-week shoes, auto-applied at checkout.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `You've got 1000 points and a $5 reward waiting. Members save 20% through Sunday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's QA run failed on an empty greeting slot (`Hi ,`) and has several unsafe or malformed tracking-pixel `<img>` tags. Authentication, unsubscribe and UTM checks can't be confirmed because the HTML is truncated and no headers were captured.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **39 tracking links were not probed.** They are on click-redirect domains such as `click.emails.skechers.com`, so destination health and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Schemeless pixel URL:** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…">` has no `http(s)://`. Clients will resolve it as a relative path against the message or host, so the Return Path (Validity) seed/inbox-monitoring pixel will 404 or never fire. The tag also has no `alt`. The automated QA did not catch this.
- **Insecure pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108404282&mi_ecmp=MKG_US_NA_U_BEST_ENGAGEDL14D_EN_10232026` uses plain HTTP, so it may be blocked or rewritten by proxies and clients.
- **Third-party Krux/Salesforce DMP beacons:** three `beacon.krxd.net/1x1_*.gif` pixels carry `partner_uid=adf27fee…189a`, which looks like a SHA-256 of the recipient's identifier, and `partner_uid=122277199` (subscriber ID). Both are passed in the URL query string to a third-party domain. The pixels are hidden with `width/height="0"` and `display:none`, and have no `alt`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Non-HTTPS content image:** `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` has no alt text, per the QA data. Mixed-content blocking can hide it in some clients, and it gives screen readers nothing.
- **Alt text on `o.gif`:** QA flags it as missing alt, but the source shows `alt=""`. This is a QA false positive, and an empty alt is valid for a decorative pixel.
- **Viewport meta:** `maximum-scale=1` in `<meta name="viewport" …>` blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Text scaling:** the global `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }` rule disables user text scaling on iOS.
- **`<html>` tag:** it has no `lang` attribute.
- **Layout tables:** the visible ones (`<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0" border="0">`, the 1×1 pixel table) have no `role="presentation"`. The rest of the source is truncated, so this can't be checked beyond those tables.
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv`, so neither takes effect. The charset then depends on the HTTP `Content-Type` header.
- **Doctype and CSS:** the XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype is standard for email. The `.mobile-content`/`.desktop-content` toggle uses `display:none` plus `!important` overrides at `min-width: 768px`. That is a standard pattern, and I found no defect in the visible portion.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** the greeting renders as `Hi ,` because the name field is empty. There is no fallback value, which is a broken personalization render. The cause (a missing profile attribute versus a template default) can't be determined from the truncated source.
- I found no unrendered tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] No `Authentication-Results` header found:** the message was received via the AgentMail relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `emails.skechers.com` are unverified. This is a data gap, not a confirmed failure.
- **Unsubscribe link, physical address and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers:** none of these appear in the truncated HTML or in the data provided, so CAN-SPAM and RFC 8058 compliance can't be assessed.
- **Sender:** the From address is `no-reply@emails.skechers.com`. A no-reply sender is a deliverability and reply-path weakness, though not a CAN-SPAM violation by itself.
- **Hashed identifier to third party:** the `partner_uid` SHA-256 passed to `beacon.krxd.net` is a privacy-disclosure consideration. It is a risk flag, not a confirmed violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The destination URLs are hidden behind click-tracking redirects, and the link probes were skipped, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be verified.
- The only campaign identifier visible is `MKG_US_NA_U_BEST_ENGAGEDL14D_EN_10232026`, in the `krxd` `campaignid` and the ink1000 `mi_ecmp` parameters. It suggests an engaged-last-14-days segment. Nothing connects it to on-site analytics.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the greeting by adding a default fallback in the template so a missing name renders as `Hi there,` rather than `Hi ,`. Also check why the name attribute is empty for this subscriber.
2. Add `https://` to the Return Path pixel (`pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=…`) and add `alt=""`.
3. Switch `ink1000.com` and `image.emails.skechers.com/.../dde00662-….png` to HTTPS, and add descriptive `alt` text to the PNG.
4. Add `alt=""` to the three `krxd.net` beacons. Review whether hashed-email and subscriber-ID sharing is covered by the privacy policy.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta, and drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` or replace it with `100%`.
6. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and `role="presentation"` to layout tables. Change the meta tags to `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`.
7. Re-run the audit with full HTML and raw headers to verify `Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`, the footer address and unsubscribe link, and the UTM parameters on resolved click-through URLs. Resolve one or two tracking redirects manually as a spot check.
8. Fix the QA check that reports missing alt on `o.gif`, since `alt=""` is present.
