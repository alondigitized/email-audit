# Save 15% with the Sally app
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-07**
## Executive summary

- Sally again — and this time it's not even a hair color deal, it's an ad to download their app. My first reaction scrolling through was "oh, so this is the whole email?" — no specific product, no hair color grid, just a big circle photo of a woman holding a phone and a button that says "Download now." I get that app installs = data for them, but as a mom juggling two kids' worth of everything on my phone, I am not adding another retail app just to save 15% on a purchase I wasn't planning to make anyway.
- The offer itself — 15% off $35+ — is fine on paper, but it's gated behind an install, which is one extra step further from an actual purchase than I want to deal with on my phone while my five-year-old is mid-meltdown over a snack. Compare this to the "Repair and replenish" send from two weeks ago that actually showed me products — this one feels like a filler send between the real promos.
- Not clicking, not downloading the app, not forwarding. This one gets a swipe and maybe a mental note that I really do need to unsubscribe from this list one of these days.

## What's working

- The 15% off $35+ headline is big, bold, and impossible to miss — clear hero placement.
- The "Download now" button is high-contrast black-on-cream, easy to tap.
- Sally Rewards section (shop twice in August, earn $5 in September) is a nice loyalty touch if I were already a member.
- Icon row at the bottom (In-Store, Online, Free Shipping, 2-Hour Delivery, BOPIS) is a clean, scannable summary of ways to shop.
- Brand look is consistent with prior Sally sends — same confetti/dot border, same red-and-cream palette, so at least it's recognizable in the inbox.

## What's weak

- There's no actual product in this email — no hair color, no tools, nothing I could look at and want. It's just an app ad wrapped in a promo.
- The 15% off isn't redeemable straight from the email — I'd have to go download an app first, which is friction I'm not going to bother with on a Saturday afternoon.
- The preview text in my inbox was a wall of repeated garbled characters (`&#847;` over and over) — that's a broken/junk render that makes the email look spammy before I even open it.
- Nothing here relates at all to what I'm actually shopping for on weekends — this is a total category mismatch, so it's easy background noise.
- Cadence-wise, this is yet another Sally email in what feels like a nonstop stream — I'm numb to their sender name at this point.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with an actual product or category promo, not an app-install ask — app growth campaigns should be a secondary CTA under a real offer, not the entire email.
- 2. Fix the preview text — it's leaking broken HTML entities and that alone could tank open rates for anyone who notices it before opening.
- 3. Let people redeem the 15% off without installing anything — a code they can use in-browser removes the friction that's killing my click here.
- 4. Add urgency to the subject/preview itself (the "expires in 30 days" disclaimer is buried at the bottom of the image) — a real deadline up top would do more than a generic app pitch.
- 5. Slow down the send frequency — I'm getting Sally emails multiple times a week and this one, with no product to look at, is the easiest one yet to ignore.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your $35 purchase just got 15% cheaper`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Download the app in August, save 15% on your next order`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: shop twice, earn a $5 reward in September`
