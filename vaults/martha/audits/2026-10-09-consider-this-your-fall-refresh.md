# Consider this your fall refresh
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the third "fall" email in about a week. It's the same plaid, the same 40% and the same members-only stack as the 10/8 "20% off the shoes you've been eyeing" send. The "UP TO 40% OFF" banner is the first thing I see, and it's a real number. But then it's a mom-of-two's nightmare: it's all women's and men's lifestyle shoes. A woman on a café patio in a cardigan, suede loafers, corduroy, and a guy in a green sweater. There isn't a single kid's shoe in the main body.
- KIDS is buried as one text link in the "Shop additional styles" list near the bottom, below Women and Men. I do like the extra 10% for members stacking on top. I'm a member with 1000 points and a $5 reward ready, and I see that again for what feels like the tenth time. It doesn't say what the 10% applies to or how to get it.
- Would I click? Probably only to the sale page, and then I'd hunt for kids. I wouldn't forward it. I'd scroll it on my phone, notice the 40%, and move on unless the sale page led straight to kids' sneakers.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 40% OFF" banner at the top is huge and I can't miss it. That's the price anchor I look for.
- "Hi, Martha" with my Member status and 1000 points makes it feel like they know me.
- "Members stack an extra 10% off sitewide" rewards me for being in the program, and I'm loyal to rewards.
- The Klarna and Afterpay row helps when I'm buying two pairs at once.
- The texture tiles (suede, corduroy, leather) are clean and easy to tap, and the waterproof callout on the brown sneaker is a useful detail.

## What's weak

- No kids' shoes anywhere in the hero or tiles. Nothing velcro, light-up, or sparkly for my 5-year-old, and no durable lace-ups for my 9-year-old. KIDS is a plain text link at the very bottom.
- Every model is an adult, and two are labeled AI-generated. The women's looks are for someone who isn't me on a weekend with two kids.
- "Up to 40% off select styles" is vague. I can't tell which shoes are actually 40%, and the fine print is tiny and dense.
- The 10% member stack doesn't say whether it stacks on the already-sale items. I'd have to read the microscopic disclaimer.
- The same 1000 points and $5 reward I've seen in the last two weeks are here again. It feels repetitive.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a kids' row. Put a "Back-to-routine kids' sneakers" block with a velcro/light-up girl's shoe and a durable lace-up boy's shoe right under the hero. For a customer whose browsing history is kids, this is the biggest lift.
- 2. Make the offer specific. Say "40% off" on named styles, and say the 10% member stack applies to those styles.
- 3. Cut the three texture tiles down to one or two, and use the room for a "Shop kids" CTA with real product.
- 4. Make KIDS the first item in "Shop additional styles", or bigger than Women and Men for me.
- 5. Add a sale deadline near the top, like "Ends 10/11". It's only in the fine print.
- 6. Give me a new reason to care about my points, like "Your $5 reward + 10% = X on a pair of kids' sneakers".

## Technical Audit

# Technical Email Audit: "Consider this your fall refresh"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed broken personalization slot (`Hi ,`). The visible source also has a malformed tracking-pixel URL, two non-HTTPS images, and head and accessibility gaps. The HTML was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe, link and header checks could not be done.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Protocol-less pixel URL:** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…">` has no scheme. Clients resolve it as a relative path, so it requests a path on the sender's own domain (or nothing) instead of the Return Path/Validity host. The seed or deliverability tracking it feeds will not fire.
- **Non-HTTPS tracker:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108404282&mi_ecmp=MKG_US_NA_U_COMF_PROD_EN_10092026` is mixed content. Gmail's image proxy can rewrite it, but some clients will block it.
- **Non-HTTPS content image:** `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`. The same host is probably available over HTTPS, which would remove the blocking risk.
- **Unverified links:** 32 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe (`click.emails.skechers.com` redirects). Their destinations and status are unverified.
- **Many third-party pixels:** There are 5 hidden pixels in the preheader area, plus the ink1000 pixel. Four of them hit `beacon.krxd.net` or `pixel.app.returnpath.net`. This adds latency and multiplies the points where blocked images or privacy proxies can fail.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `o.gif` and `dde00662-…png` have no `alt` attribute. For the content PNG, screen readers may announce the filename.
- **Tracking pixels without alt:** The two `krxd` `usermatch` pixels and the `returnpath` pixel have no `alt`. They are hidden, so the impact is minor.
- **Ineffective head meta tags:**
  - `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`.
  - `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`.
  - Neither declaration takes effect.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang`.
- **Layout tables lack semantics:** The visible layout tables (e.g. `<table class="widthFull" width="640" …>`) have no `role="presentation"`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `viewport` has `maximum-scale=1`, which blocks pinch-zoom.
- **Hidden-div pixel block:** `display:none !important` is applied on both the `td` and the `div`. Some clients (e.g. Outlook desktop) ignore `display:none` on images, which could expose the 1×1 and 0×0 images as layout shifts.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** The QA check found `Hi ,`, so the first-name field rendered empty. There is no fallback value in the merge logic. It is a customer-visible defect that affects every recipient without a populated name.
- No unrendered `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` tokens were visible in the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Not verifiable:** The footer, physical address, unsubscribe link and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are not in the supplied source. SPF, DKIM and DMARC results were also not provided. The sender is `no-reply@emails.skechers.com`, which is a no-reply address.
- **Third-party data sharing:** The `krxd` pixels pass identifiers to an external data platform:
  - `partner_uid=adf27fee…189a` is a 64-hex value, likely a SHA-256 hash of the subscriber's email.
  - `partner_uid=122277199` is the subscriber ID.
  - The ink1000 pixel passes `mi_u=8108404282`.
  - Whether this is covered by the privacy policy and consent should be confirmed, especially for CCPA/GDPR audiences.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not verifiable:** UTM parameters and landing pages cannot be assessed. All 32 links are `click.emails.skechers.com` redirects that were skipped, and the destinations are not in the truncated source.
- **Campaign ID:** The tracking calls carry `MKG_US_NA_U_COMF_PROD_EN_10092026`. Confirm it matches the `utm_campaign` on the landing pages.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a fallback to the first-name merge, such as `Hi there,`, or drop the greeting when the value is empty. This is the highest priority.
2. Fix `pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif…` to use `https://`.
3. Serve `image.emails.skechers.com` and `ink1000.com` assets over HTTPS.
4. Add `alt=""` to decorative and tracking images and meaningful `alt` text to the content PNG.
5. Correct the head tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
6. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and `role="presentation"` to the layout tables.
7. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag.
8. Re-run the audit on the full HTML and headers so unsubscribe, `List-Unsubscribe` headers, UTMs, and the final destinations of the 32 links can be checked.
