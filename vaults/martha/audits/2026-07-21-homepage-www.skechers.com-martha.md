# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-07-21
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-21**
## Executive summary

- Opening Skechers.com right now, I'm getting a serious performance-sports vibe — an NBA player on stadium bleachers, running shoe front and center, "Comfort That Performs" in all caps. This reads like a brand chasing runners and athletes, not a mom trying to find something cute for her kindergartner or practical for her 9-year-old. The one thing that caught my eye before I could form an opinion was the Care Bears collab banner — my daughter would lose her mind over that — but it's a single small strip at the top and the rest of the page has nothing for me.

## What's working

- **"Skechers x Care Bears: SHOP NOW" banner** — as a mom with a five-year-old, Care Bears jumped out at me immediately. That's the one above-the-fold signal that actually pulls me in.
- **Clean mobile layout** — the nav doesn't feel cluttered, the logo is clear, the cart icon is right there. On my phone this is easy to tap around.
- **"Slip-ins" tab in the category bar** — I know about Slip-ins because I've considered them for my son who's constantly late tying his shoes. Having it in the quick-access row means I can tap straight to it.
- **Aero Burst SI product shot at the bottom** — the shoe itself is photographed well and the navy/orange colorway is sharp. Not for me, but quality image.

## What's weak

- **The hero is entirely off-persona for me**: An NBA player doing a performance running push has zero relevance to a mom shopping for a first-grader's back-to-school shoes or a kid-friendly Care Bears sneaker.
- **No kids' category visible above the fold**: There's no "Kids" tab, no "Shop for them" prompt, nothing. I'd have to dig into the MENU and hunt. That's friction I may not bother with.
- **No offer or savings hook anywhere in the hero**: No "20% off," no "members save extra," no free-shipping threshold. For a price-sensitive shopper, this is a miss.
- **No loyalty/rewards callout**: I'm the type who would join a rewards program if it was surfaced to me — but there's no mention of one above the fold.
- **"A Force to Be Reckoned With" copy** — this tagline is aimed at a very specific sports enthusiast. It doesn't land for everyday practical shoppers.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero (or add a second slide) targeting parents**: "Back-to-school? Your kids' feet are covered." with a shot of a kid in Slip-ins would have converted me instantly on a July visit.
- **Make the Care Bears collab the hero, not a banner strip**: If you have a licensed Care Bears shoe, that's a visual worth a full hero moment for the parent audience — put it front and center with imagery of a kid wearing them.
- **Add a "Kids" tab to the horizontal category bar**: Replace or supplement "BOBs" (which I don't immediately recognize) with a "Kids" tab so I can get there in one tap without opening the full menu.
- **Surface a savings hook above the fold**: Even a simple "Members save 10% sitewide — Join free" in the announcement bar would give me a reason to sign up and stay.
