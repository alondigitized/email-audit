## What's weak

- There's no discount anywhere. After a week of 30% off, a full-price email feels like a letdown.

- This is the sixth or seventh Skechers email this week, so it adds to the pile.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's code has one confirmed personalization failure (empty greeting, `Hi ,`), one malformed tracking pixel (no URL scheme), and two images served over plain `http://` without alt text. Authentication, unsubscribe and click-link integrity could not be verified from the supplied data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed pixel URL:** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…" width="1" height="1" />` has no scheme. Clients resolve it as a relative path, so the request fails and the Validity/Return Path seed-tracking pixel never fires. It also has no `alt` attribute.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108404282&mi_ecmp=MKG_US_NA_U_PERFORM_PERF_EN_11212026` is HTTP. It may be blocked or trigger mixed-content handling, which would lose open or attribution data.
- **Unverified click links:** 29 tracking links on click-redirect domains (e.g. `click.emails.skechers.com`) were skipped by the HTTP probe. Their destinations and status codes are unverified.
- **Many third-party pixels:** Five 1×1 images sit in the hidden preheader table (SFMC open pixel, three `beacon.krxd.net` calls, the Return Path pixel), plus the ink1000 pixel. The ink1000 pixel is in its own table, outside the hidden wrapper.
- **Hidden wrapper:** The `display:none !important` wrapper around the pixels is unreliable in some clients. Pixels placed there may not load.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:**
  - `o.gif` (the ink1000 pixel) has no `alt`.
  - `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` has no `alt`. It appears to be a content image, so screen readers and images-off views get nothing.
  - The three `beacon.krxd.net` images also have no `alt`, though they are hidden.
- **Insecure content image:** `dde00662-…png` loads over `http://`, so it may be blocked or flagged as insecure by some clients.
- **Ineffective meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv`. The charset declaration does nothing, and the `®` in `<title>` depends on the HTTP `Content-Type` header.
- **No zoom on mobile:** `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Unlabeled layout tables:** The visible source shows no `role="presentation"` on layout tables (`<table class="widthFull" width="640" …>`), and `<html>` has no `lang` attribute.
- **Plain-text part:** 2799 of 3962 characters (about 71%) are URLs, which exceeds the 70% threshold.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** The greeting renders with an empty name field (`Hi ,`). The first-name value was blank at send time, and there is no fallback string. No unresolved `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` tokens were found in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication:** `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it is a verification gap rather than a confirmed failure.
- **Unsubscribe and address:** The truncated source doesn't include the footer, so I can't confirm the unsubscribe link or physical address. `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers weren't supplied.
- **Hashed identifiers in URLs:** The krxd pixels carry `partner_uid=adf27fee…189a` (a SHA-256 value, presumably of the email address) and `partner_uid=122277199` (a subscriber ID). These are sent to a third-party DMP on open. This is worth a privacy-disclosure check.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The campaign ID `MKG_US_NA_U_PERFORM_PERF_EN_11212026` appears in the krxd and ink1000 pixel parameters, so it is consistent across pixels.
- Click URLs and landing-page UTMs could not be evaluated. The links are SFMC redirects that were skipped by the probe, and the source is truncated. No issues are confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the Return Path pixel to `https://pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=…`. Without the scheme it never fires.
2. Serve `o.gif` and `dde00662-….png` over HTTPS. Confirm that `https://image.emails.skechers.com/...` resolves.
3. Add `alt` text to `dde00662-….png`, and `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
4. Add a fallback for the first-name merge field (e.g. `%%=IIF(Empty(@firstName),"there",@firstName)=%%` in AMPscript, or drop the name from the greeting). Check why the subscriber record had a blank name.
5. Change the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge" />`. Remove `maximum-scale=1`. Add `lang` to `<html>` and `role="presentation"` to layout tables.
6. Shorten URLs in the plain-text part, or add more descriptive text, to bring the URL share below 70%.
7. Re-run QA with the full headers and full HTML. This should confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC, `List-Unsubscribe`, the footer address, click-link destinations and UTM parameters.
