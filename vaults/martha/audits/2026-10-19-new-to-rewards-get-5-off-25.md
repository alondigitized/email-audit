# New to rewards? Get $5 off $25
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- Sally Beauty again, and I've already seen this offer. It's the Oct 11 "Get $5 off $25 when you join" and the Oct 17 "Join today, get $5 off $25" send again with a new subject line. That's about the seventeenth Sally email since mid-September. The offer is clear and I like rewards programs, but I can't tell whether this is a new promo or a repeat.
- The layout is clean. A big pink hero says "Join Sally.Rewards & save on your wish list," with a black "Join Sally.Rewards" button. Two women with Sally tote bags sit below it. Then there's a Preferred vs. Elite tier comparison, and a row of hair color products. None of it is for me. I'm shopping for a 5-year-old and a 9-year-old, and there are no kids, no shoes, and no family angle. The hair color products are for a different person.
- I wouldn't click today. I might screenshot the $5 off $25 for later if I ever need hair color, but I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- The hero is clear. "$5 off $25+ in October" is a specific offer, and the black button stands out against the pink.
- The Preferred vs. Elite comparison is easy to scan. Free to join, with points on every dollar, is what I look for in a rewards program.
- Elite lists free shipping on every order, which matters to a price-sensitive mom.
- The Klarna strip and the "2 Hour Delivery" bar at the top tell me how I can pay and get it fast.
- The text is big enough to read on my phone, and the buttons are easy to tap.

## What's weak

- I've seen this offer three times in nine days (Oct 11, Oct 17, now). It feels like spam, not a "new to rewards" message.
- Nothing speaks to a mom of two. The models are young women with tote bags, and there's no kid or family content.
- The product row is all hair color and color remover. It's niche and doesn't suit everyday shopping, and the prices are missing.
- The fine print is long, and the offer only works if I'm a new member, who has to sign up and spend within 30 days. A "new to rewards" subject line should say that up front. I'm already getting these emails, so I can't tell whether I'm eligible.
- It's a long scroll for one small offer, and the second "Join Rewards" button comes after a lot of tier detail.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop re-sending the same $5 off $25 join offer under new subject lines. Suppress it for people who already got it twice, or give them something new, like a product or a deadline.
- 2. Say who's eligible, near the top. If it's only for new members, I'd rather be left out than sent this.
- 3. Add prices and a "points you'd earn" line to the product row, and pick products that suit a broader range of shoppers than hair color.
- 4. Cut the page to one offer, one button, and one tier comparison.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free to join. Earn points on every order, plus free 2-hour pickup.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is an XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with Open Sans web fonts and a mobile breakpoint at 499px. Its main confirmed defects are an unresolved `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro in a third-party impression pixel, a non-HTTPS tracking pixel, and missing alt text on several images. The source is truncated, and SPF/DKIM and the tracking-link destinations could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved macro:** the pixel `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&ao=883&aca=-11&si=-11&ci=-11&pi=-11&ad=-11&advt=-…` was delivered with the literal `r=[CACHEBUSTER]`. The ad server's cache-busting substitution never ran. Clients that cache the image will not count repeat opens or impressions, and the literal brackets are not valid URL characters. The `-11` values for `si`, `ci`, `pi` and `ad` look like default or unmapped placeholders.
- **Insecure pixel:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990…` is the only `http://` asset. Clients that block mixed or insecure content will drop it, so that tracking call is lost.
- **Unverified links:** 50 tracking links were skipped by the probe because they are click-redirect domains. Final destinations, redirect chains and dead links are unverified.
- **Third-party pixels:** the email loads at least three third-party trackers: `pxl.mon-trk.com`, `t.myvisualiq.net` and `login.dotomi.com`. These add latency and image-blocking exposure.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** the QA run flagged 7 images with no alt attribute.
  - Four are Evergage dynamic-content images: `https://sallyholdings.us-4.evergage.com/api/dataset/sallybeauty_prod/campaign/pLpNA/HxBq1/{1..4}/summary.png`. These are likely the product-recommendation block, so images-off clients and screen readers get no product information.
  - The other three are tracking pixels (`pxl.mon-trk.com/…/003aZ00000s0I5yQAE`, `myvisualiq` and `dotomi`). They need `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"` so they are explicitly treated as decorative.
- **No document language:** the source shows `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` with no `lang` attribute.
- **Meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so they do nothing. This may be an artifact of capture or sanitization, so confirm against the raw send.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Sally Beauty</title>` is the same on every send.
- **Font stack:** `a{font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif}` references a font with no `@font-face` in the visible source. It falls back to Helvetica, which is low severity.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Unresolved token:** `[CACHEBUSTER]` is the only unresolved token confirmed (see section 2).
- **Identifiers in image URLs:** the Evergage URLs carry `?userId=martha.st…` (truncated in the QA output). This looks like a name- or email-derived user ID in a plain query string. It would appear in proxy logs and any forwarded copy. Confirm the full value is not an email address or other PII.
- **Contact ID in a pixel path:** `003aZ00000s0I5yQAE` follows the Salesforce Contact ID format (`003` prefix). This is a per-recipient identifier and shows the send is personalized.
- **Rest of the body:** no other unresolved `{{ }}`, `%%`, `[ ]` or `%` tokens appear in the visible source, but the HTML was truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication:** `Authentication-Results` was not found. The QA report attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown rather than failing. The From domain `em.sallybeauty.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is normal practice.
- **Unsubscribe and address:** the unsubscribe link, physical postal address and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are not in the supplied data. Compliance cannot be confirmed or denied.
- **Other findings:** no other confirmed compliance defects.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 50 tracking links were skipped, and the visible source contains no resolved destination URLs or UTM parameters. The subject promises "$5 off $25" for new rewards members, and whether the landing page matches that offer was not tested.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the ad-server template so `[CACHEBUSTER]` is replaced at send time or removed. Verify the `-11` placeholder values in the same pixel.
2. Change the dotomi pixel to `https://login.dotomi.com/…`, or drop it if it isn't needed.
3. Add `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"` to all tracking pixels. Add descriptive alt text to the Evergage `summary.png` images, or have the Evergage template emit it.
4. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and use a campaign-specific `<title>`.
5. Move the Evergage `userId` to an opaque token rather than a name-derived string, and confirm the full value is not PII.
6. Re-run the audit against the raw message with full headers (`Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`, `Received-SPF`, DKIM signature) and the untruncated HTML. Resolve the 50 redirect links with a redirect-following probe to check destinations, UTMs and the unsubscribe and address footer.
