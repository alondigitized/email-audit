- 4. Make the "Shop now" links full-width buttons, and drop at least one of the three stacked top banners.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with a 499px mobile breakpoint and a clean template. The QA run found no hard failures (86% pass, 0 issues), but the third-party ad pixels have defects. SPF/DKIM/DMARC, the unsubscribe mechanism and UTM tagging could not be verified from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved macro in the ad pixel:** `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&ao=883&aca=-11&si=-11&ci=-11&pi=-11&ad=-11&advt=-…` still contains the literal `[CACHEBUSTER]`. The `-11` values are the vendor's default for unset IDs. The pixel will fire, but it can be cached and dedupe-collapsed, so impression counts are unreliable.
- **Non-HTTPS pixel:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&…` is plain HTTP. Clients that block mixed or insecure content will drop it, so the sync/conversion tracking is lost for those recipients.
- **Empty `src`:** QA flagged an `<img>` with an empty `src` and empty identifier. This can trigger a request to the page or base URL in some clients and renders as a broken image.
- **Link probing:** 52 tracking/redirect links were skipped, so redirect chains, dead links and final destinations are unverified.
- **Pixel stack:** There are three third-party pixels (`pxl.mon-trk.com`, `t.myvisualiq.net`, `login.dotomi.com`) on top of the ESP's own tracking. This adds latency and more points of failure.
- **Subscriber ID in a URL:** `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/987bfe61-…/2324653/003aZ00000s0I5yQAE` embeds what looks like a Salesforce Contact ID (`003` prefix, 18 characters) in a third-party URL path. This is a data-exposure concern to confirm with the vendor contract.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt attributes:** The QA data lists four images without `alt` (the empty-src image, `003aZ00000s0I5yQAE`, `impression_pixel`, `UCMController`). The three pixels should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` so screen readers skip them and layout doesn't shift. The empty-src image should be removed.
- **`<html>` has no `lang` attribute:** The source shows `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">`. Screen readers can't pick the correct pronunciation.
- **Head meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=edge" />` have no `http-equiv`, so as written they do nothing. The source may have been altered during extraction, so verify against the raw send.
- **Font stack:** `a{font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif}` references a font with no `@font-face`. It falls back to Helvetica, which is harmless but never loads the intended face. The Open Sans `@font-face` rules are fine.
- **Truncated source:** I can't confirm `role="presentation"` on layout tables, a preheader, or the contrast of the `.legal a` colour `#464646`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Unresolved placeholder:** `[CACHEBUSTER]` in the VisualIQ pixel (see above).
- **Other tokens:** No other unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[[…]]` tokens appear in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **Authentication:** The QA warning says `Authentication-Results` is absent because the message came through the AgentMail relay. SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `em.sallybeauty.com` is unknown, not failed.
- **Unsubscribe and address:** The truncated HTML doesn't show the unsubscribe link, physical postal address, or `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers. No conclusion is possible either way.
- **Sender and subject:** The From domain `em.sallybeauty.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is good practice. The subject doesn't appear deceptive.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. Click links are routed through tracking redirects that weren't probed, and the visible source contains no UTM parameters or landing-page URLs. The wrapped links may carry UTMs in their encoded destinations, which should be checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run the QA with the full HTML and raw headers. Confirm the unsubscribe link, postal address, `List-Unsubscribe` headers, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC from the original message.
2. Fix or remove the VisualIQ pixel. Have the ESP substitute `[CACHEBUSTER]` at send time, or drop the pixel.
3. Switch the Dotomi pixel to `https://` or remove it.
4. Remove the empty-`src` image. Add `alt=""` and 1×1 dimensions to all tracking pixels.
5. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and restore `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"` on the meta tags if they are really missing.
6. Review whether the Salesforce Contact ID in the `mon-trk.com` pixel path is acceptable under the vendor's data terms.
7. Follow the redirect chains for the 52 skipped links with a redirect-aware probe, and record their final URLs and UTM parameters.
