# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-06-09
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-09**
## Executive summary

- Skechers is pushing a summer lifestyle campaign with a clean, colorful energy — two models, a BOGO deal front and center, and a loyalty push for Member Month. For me, this reads like a solid casual-footwear brand with a decent deal, but it doesn't feel like it's talking to me as a mom. There's no kids section anywhere in my viewport, and the hero is split between a woman and a man with no family, no kids, no "get everyone ready for summer" angle that would actually stop me from scrolling past.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF banner** — top of screen, blue, impossible to miss. As a mom buying shoes for two kids, BOGO is exactly the kind of deal that makes me stop and think "okay, I could use this."
- **Member Month strip** — "Earn 2X Points now thru 6/13" with a login link shows me there's a reward for being loyal, and the deadline (6/13) creates mild urgency without being aggressive.
- **Horizontal nav categories** — "Slip-ins," "Sandals," "Arch Fit," "BOBs" gives me a quick scan of what's available without needing to hunt through a menu.
- **Clean mobile render** — the layout is not broken, images load, text is readable. That's table stakes but it's worth noting because a lot of sites fail on mobile.

## What's weak

- **No kids category in the nav strip** — "Slip-ins," "Sandals," "Arch Fit," "BOBs" and something cut off at the edge. Kids is my primary reason for being here and I'd have to tap "MENU" to even find it. That's friction I don't have patience for when my kids need something.
- **Split hero with no clear message** — a woman and a man side by side with no headline I can fully read. "Summer" is cut off at the bottom. I don't know what they're selling me or why I should care.
- **No free-shipping callout** — BOGO is great but I also want to know if I'm getting free shipping. A lot of moms (including me) calculate total cost fast. If I'm not seeing that threshold, I'm already doing math in my head and that slows me down.
- **No social proof above the fold** — no star ratings, no "bestseller" badge, nothing that says other parents have bought and loved these. Reviews are a big part of how I decide.
- **Hero imagery doesn't reflect my life** — both models are solo adults. I'm a mom of two. Show me a mom chasing kids, a family at a park, something that makes me feel seen.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Kids" shortcut to the nav strip** — bump it into the first row of categories, even if it pushes "BOBs" off-screen. If I can't see "Kids" in one tap, I'm going to a site that makes it easier.
- **Swap the split hero for a family lifestyle shot** — a mom with kids in Skechers in a summer setting would stop me dead. Pair it with a clear headline like "Get the whole family ready for summer — BOGO 50% off" and a shop-now button.
- **Surface free-shipping threshold** in the top banner or below the hero. Even "$39 ships free" would keep me calculating rather than bouncing.
- **Add a member-exclusive deal badge** to the hero or just below it — something like "Members save an extra 10%" makes the loyalty strip feel more actionable than "Earn 2X Points."
