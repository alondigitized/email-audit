# Hours left for 30% off sitewide
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the sixth 30% off member email since Tuesday, and at least the sale is the hero again. "LAST CHANCE!" and a giant "30% OFF SITEWIDE" fill the top of the screen. "Hi, Martha" with my 1000 points and the $5 reward strip are right above it. I get the deal in two seconds. But I'm tired of it. Yesterday's "30% off early access is almost up" said nearly the same thing, and this one just adds "hours left."
- Then I scroll for the kids and find nothing. The product tiles are Slip-ins, Cozy Fit, Court & Classics and Performance. The models are all adults, and the shoes are adult styles. There's no velcro, no light-up soles and nothing for a 5-year-old or a 9-year-old. "Kids" is a plain text link buried in the "Shop Additional Styles" list. The size pickers only offer Women's and Men's.
- Would I click? Probably yes, because it's the last day and 30% off is real money. I'd go straight to Kids and skip the tiles. I wouldn't forward it. It's a deadline reminder, not something I'd text a friend about.

## What's working

- "LAST CHANCE!" and the huge "30% OFF SITEWIDE" are impossible to miss, and the deadline is credible.
- "Hi, Martha," my 1000 points and the $5 redeem strip make me feel like a known member, and I love a rewards nudge.
- "Discount auto-applied at cart" means no code hunting, which is how I like to shop on my phone.
- The Doorbuster Deals block at $49.99 gives me a second reason to click, and it's clearly labeled as member early access.
- The black "Shop Now" button is clear against the red.

## What's weak

- There's nothing for kids in the hero or the tiles. A mom of a 5-year-old girl and a 9-year-old boy sees only adult shoes and adult models.
- Kids is buried as a text link in the footer-style list. There's no kids size picker either, only Women's and Men's.
- The "Shop Now" button is small for a thumb tap, and the fine print under it is tiny and hard to read without zooming.
- I've now had six near-identical 30% off emails in a week. The urgency feels manufactured, and I've stopped trusting "last chance."
- "Exclusions apply" and "not stackable" are small print, so I don't know whether the deal covers the kids' shoes I'd actually buy.
- The $49.99 doorbusters don't say what the shoes are or what they cost normally, so the price means nothing to me.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a kids row to the hero area, with "Shop Kids" tiles for light-up and velcro girls' styles and lace-up boys' sneakers. That would do more than any other change here.
- 2. Add kids sizes (Little Kid, Big Kid) to the size picker.
- 3. Make the Shop Now button full-width on mobile, and put the end time (for example "ends 11:59 PM PT tonight") above it instead of the vague "thru 11/22."
- 4. Cut the cadence. One reminder is enough, not six.
- 5. Show an actual doorbuster shoe with its was/now price.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email fails one automated check: an empty greeting slot, rendered as "Hi ,". The HTML source also has a malformed tracking pixel URL, `<meta>` tags that don't work as written, a viewport setting that blocks zoom, and two `http://` images with no alt text. The HTML was truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and CTA URLs could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Scheme-less pixel URL:** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…">` has no `https://` or `//`. Clients resolve it as a relative path against the message or host, so it will 404 or never fire. Deliverability and inbox-placement measurement through this pixel is silently lost.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108404282&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_SITESELECT_FULL_EN_11222026_PM` is plain HTTP, so it may be blocked or flagged as mixed content. It also puts a subscriber-linked ID (`mi_u`) in the clear.
- **No HTTP probe:** 45 click-redirect links were skipped by the QA probe, so destination status codes for the CTAs are unverified.
- **Pixels hidden with `display:none`:** Five pixels sit inside `<div style="display:none !important">`, including three `beacon.krxd.net` pixels and `click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx`. Some clients, such as certain webmail and Outlook modes, skip hidden content, which can undercount opens.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, confirmed by QA:**
  - `o.gif` (ink1000.com)
  - `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`
  - The three `beacon.krxd.net` pixels and `returnpath` pixel in source also have no `alt` attribute. This matters little for hidden pixels.
- **Insecure image:** `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-…png` is served over HTTP and may be blocked. Because it is the same host as the brand's image CDN, other images on that host may be loaded over HTTP too.
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv`, so neither takes effect. Some clients may fall back to charset sniffing, which can garble "®" and similar characters.
- **Zoom blocked:**
  - `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
  - `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }` prevents text resizing on iOS.
- **Layout tables:** The visible layout tables (`<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0" border="0">`) have no `role="presentation"`, so screen readers may announce them as data tables.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Skechers®</title>` doesn't describe the message.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Empty greeting (QA FAIL):** The greeting renders as "Hi ," because the first-name field resolved to empty. The token isn't visible in the truncated source.
- **Likely cause:** The subscriber record exists (the `subid` pixel carries `122277199`), so the FirstName attribute is probably null with no default. This is an inference.
- **Impact:** A visible rendering defect on every recipient with a missing name.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results header not found (QA WARN):** This was expected through the AgentMail relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. Treat it as a test-harness limitation, not a confirmed sender failure.
- **Unsubscribe and address:** The unsubscribe link, physical postal address and List-Unsubscribe headers are not in the truncated source or QA data. They are unverified, not confirmed missing.
- **Hashed identifier shared with a third party:** The `beacon.krxd.net` pixels carry `partner_uid=adf27fee…189a`, which looks like a SHA-256 hash, plus the subscriber ID, in a third-party URL. It is a privacy-disclosure consideration, not a CAN-SPAM violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not verifiable:** CTA hrefs are truncated and the 45 tracked links were not probed.
- **Campaign ID:** The campaign identifier is visible as `PRO_US_NA_U_SITESELECT_FULL_EN_11222026_PM` in the pixel parameters. Whether it propagates as UTMs to skechers.com cannot be confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a default value for the first-name token (for example a fallback of "there"). Suppress the comma when the name is empty.
2. Fix the returnpath pixel to `https://pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=…`.
3. Move `ink1000.com/…/o.gif` and `image.emails.skechers.com/…/dde00662-…png` to HTTPS. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels and meaningful alt to the content image.
4. Correct the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge" />`. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and avoid `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`.
5. Add `role="presentation"` to layout tables.
6. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and original headers to check unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe, postal address, SPF/DKIM/DMARC and CTA UTM parameters. Follow the redirects to the final URLs.
