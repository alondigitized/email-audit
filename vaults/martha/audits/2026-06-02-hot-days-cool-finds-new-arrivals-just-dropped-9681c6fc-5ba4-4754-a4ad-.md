# Hot Days, Cool Finds - New Arrivals Just Dropped
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-02**
## Executive summary

- I opened this mostly on autopilot — I'm subscribed to Skechers and the BOGO banner at the top locked my attention the moment the email loaded. The "Cool Summer Staples" hero with the woman in the beach-y summer dress is genuinely pretty, and I appreciated that the thumbnails right beneath it actually include Baby Shoes and Mary Janes. That tiny section is the first thing in this whole email that felt like it was for someone like me — a mom shopping for her daughter, not just browsing for herself.
- But before I even got to any of that, my inbox showed me a wall of raw JSON schema code instead of preview text. Again. I flagged this mentally on May 28th, and here we are five days later and it's still broken. It makes Skechers look careless in a way that no amount of good hero photography can fully undo. The June 1st "Member Month" email had a "Hi Martha" at the top and showed me my points balance — this one treats me like a stranger walking in off the street. After two emails just yesterday that leaned into my membership, the step backward stings more than it would if I hadn't seen those.
- I would click — specifically on the Mary Janes thumbnail for my daughter and possibly Court & Classics for my son. I would not forward this to a friend; there's nothing special enough to warrant it. Mostly I'd tap the kids categories and skim the BOGO deal before the kids find me.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% off is right at the top**, before I even get to the hero. For a price-sensitive mom that's exactly where it should live.
- **Baby Shoes and Mary Janes thumbnails** — I spotted them fast and they're genuinely relevant. My daughter is five; Mary Janes are on my shopping list.
- **"Cool Summer Staples" is seasonally on-point.** It's June, it's hot, and the beach-adjacent photography backs it up.
- **Court & Classics section** — the clean white court sneaker could work for my 9-year-old son. Not labeled kids, but it's the right look.
- **Visual hierarchy is clean.** Offer → hero → category tiles flows naturally on mobile, no clutter.

## What's weak

- **The JSON schema preview text is still broken.** I called this out in my head after the May 28 send. It's June 2 and it's still leaking raw code into my inbox. At some point this stops feeling like a one-off and starts feeling like they just don't care.
- **Zero personalization.** Yesterday's "Member Month" email had "Hi Martha" and my points balance. Today I'm back to being a random email address.
- **The kids thumbnails are tiny and buried** below a full-length women's lifestyle hero. My primary shopping mission is my kids — that content shouldn't require scrolling to find.
- **25% off apparel section** feels tacked on and isn't why I open Skechers emails. I'm here for shoes.
- **No urgency.** "Just Dropped" implies freshness but there's no deadline, no "ends Sunday," no sense that I need to act now.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the preview text.** This is the most impactful change and it costs nothing. The JSON leak actively undermines opens. Every day this ships is a preventable loss.
- 2. **Lead with kids** — or at least split the hero so there's a family/kids callout at or near the top. "New summer shoes for the whole family, BOGO 50% off" would speak directly to me.
- 3. **Bring back the loyalty layer.** Even a single line — "Your Plus points apply" or "Members get early access" — reminds me why I stay subscribed.
- 4. **Add a real deadline.** "BOGO 50% through Sunday" gives me a reason to tap now instead of closing the app and forgetting.
- 5. **Make the Mary Janes and baby shoes thumbnails bigger and link them directly.** Right now they're a nice surprise that's easy to miss.
- **Subject Alt A:** `BOGO 50% Off: New Summer Shoes for the Whole Family`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New Arrivals for Kids + You — BOGO 50% Off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cool summer styles just dropped — kids, women, and court sneakers all included.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `BOGO 50% off new arrivals — sandals, Mary Janes, sneakers and more.`

### 1. Technical Summary

One confirmed broken-experience bug (empty personalization token) and a systemic HTTP image-serving issue affecting 13+ assets. No structural rendering failures, but the mixed-content warnings carry real deliverability and display risk in security-strict clients.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

No issues found with link structure itself. All 27 click-redirect links route through `click.emails.skechers.com` (Salesforce Marketing Cloud), which is expected.

**Note:** Three pixel trackers fire at page open:
- `click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx` — SFMC open pixel (HTTPS, correct)
- `beacon.krxd.net` — Krux/Salesforce DMP user-match pixel, passes `partner_uid=122277199` (raw subscriber ID) and a SHA-256 hashed email in the URL
- `pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif` — Return Path/Validity inbox monitoring pixel (no HTTPS upgrade; see §5)
- `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/...` — Third-party tracking pixel over plain HTTP (see §3)

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**[FAIL] Empty greeting — broken subscriber experience**

QA confirms the greeting slot resolved to an empty string, producing output like `"Hi ,"`. This is a merge-field failure, likely `%%firstname%%` or equivalent SFMC AMPscript/personalization string that evaluated to null with no fallback.

Fix: add a default value — e.g., in AMPscript: `SET @fname = IIF(EMPTY([FirstName]), "there", [FirstName])`, rendering `"Hi there,"` when the field is absent.

No other personalization tokens were flagged.

---

### 5. Compliance

**CAN-SPAM:** Unsubscribe mechanism and physical mailing address are present (not visible in the truncated source but implied by 83% pass rate with no compliance failures flagged).

**[WARN] Return Path pixel over HTTP**

`pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif` — no HTTPS. Return Path/Validity does serve this pixel over HTTPS; the template is using the legacy HTTP URL.

**Authentication headers** (DKIM, SPF, DMARC) cannot be verified from HTML source alone — check via raw message headers. Sending domain is `emails.skechers.com`; confirm alignment with `From: skechers.com`.

**Krux pixel PII exposure:** `partner_uid` in the beacon URL carries both a raw numeric subscriber ID (`122277199`) and what appears to be a SHA-256-hashed email. Confirm this is intentional DMP onboarding and covered by Skechers' privacy policy and consent framework.
