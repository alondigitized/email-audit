## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source was truncated, so only the head, CSS and the QA-flagged images could be checked. The confirmed problems are an unresolved `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro in a third-party pixel URL, a non-HTTPS tracking pixel, and malformed `<meta>` tags. Authentication, unsubscribe and UTM checks couldn't be completed from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved macro:** the pixel `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&ao=883…` has the literal `[CACHEBUSTER]` in `r=`. The ad-server macro was never substituted, so the pixel will be requested with a static or invalid value. That defeats cache-busting and can undercount impressions.
- **Non-HTTPS pixel:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990…` is plain HTTP. Clients that proxy or block mixed content may drop it, so the Dotomi/Conversant sync won't fire reliably.
- **Unprobed links:** 52 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the QA probe, so their destinations and status codes are unverified.
- **Tracking pixels:** `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/987bfe61-…/2338539/003aZ00000s0I5yQAE` is a third-party open-tracking pixel (the filename looks like a Salesforce ID). It is a separate tracker from the sender's own click tracking. There are three third-party pixels in total (mon-trk, myvisualiq, dotomi).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, 5 images:**
  - Two have an empty name and empty `src`, likely stray `<img>` tags or spacers.
  - The three tracking pixels (mon-trk, myvisualiq, dotomi) also have no alt.
  - Pixels should carry `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"`. The two blank-`src` images should be removed or given `alt=""`.
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`. `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`. Both are inert as written. Charset normally comes from the MIME header, but if a client ignores that header, the missing `http-equiv` leaves no declared encoding.
- **Undefined font:** `a{font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif}` references a font with no `@font-face`. It silently falls back to Helvetica.
- **No issues found with:** the viewport meta, the `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` meta, or the 499px mobile breakpoint. The Open Sans `@font-face` rules use `font-display: swap` and local() fallbacks.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The only unresolved token confirmed is `[CACHEBUSTER]`, covered in section 2.
- No other merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]` were visible in the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results:** the header is absent. The QA note attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown rather than failing. The sending domain `em.sallybeauty.com` is a dedicated subdomain.
- **Unsubscribe and postal address:** the footer and the `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers were not in the supplied data, so they can't be confirmed. No violation is claimed.
- **No issues confirmed** beyond the unverifiable items above.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The 52 click-tracking links are redirect-wrapped and unresolved, so UTM parameters and landing-page destinations can't be verified.
- The subject "20% off sitewide" can't be checked against a landing-page offer without resolving the CTA links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro in the myvisualiq pixel so it substitutes at send time, or remove the pixel.
2. Switch the dotomi pixel to `https://`, or confirm the vendor supports it. Otherwise drop it.
3. Add `alt=""` and explicit 1×1 dimensions to all tracking pixels, and delete the two blank-`src` images.
4. Add `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"` to the two meta tags.
5. Re-run the audit on the untruncated source with the raw headers. That would let you verify the unsubscribe link, `List-Unsubscribe`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC and UTM on the CTAs. Resolve the redirect chain on a sample of the 52 tracked links.
