- "Hi, Martha" with my 1000 points and the $5 reward strip at the top makes me feel like a known member, not a stranger.
- The BOGO 50% off or 20% off a single pair banner is a real number, and the "discount auto-applied at cart" line means I don't have to hunt for a code.
- The holiday colors fit the season, and the burgundy, green and white shoes are pretty and easy to read.
- The "Shop Now" buttons are big, full-width and easy to tap with a thumb.
- The kids' link is at least in the "Shop Additional Styles" list, so I can find it.

## What's weak

- Every shoe and both models are adult. There is no sparkle, no light-up, no velcro and no boys' lace-ups, so none of it fits what I'm shopping for.
- The offer is a thin banner above a big lifestyle photo. The pretty picture wins the eye and the savings get buried.
- The "Shop Now" buttons all look the same and don't say which shoe I'm getting, and there are no prices. I'm price-sensitive, so I can't tell what the BOGO would save me on these.
- The size grid is only women's and men's sizes. Kids' sizes are missing, and I'd have to dig for them.
- The Black Friday fine print at the bottom is tiny and has no clear end date, so the urgency doesn't feel real. I'd also have to squint to learn that sale styles are excluded.
- The preview text is junk. It's a raw tracking link instead of a message.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by who I am. I'm a parent who has browsed and bought kids' shoes, so show me a kids' hero with a family BOGO angle, like "BOGO 50% off, grab a pair for both kids". Pink and purple sparkle for her and blue and black lace-ups for him would match the holiday colors already in this email.
- 2. Lead with the deal. Make "BOGO 50% off" the hero headline, with a visible end date and a countdown if it's honest, and let the color story sit below it.
- 3. Make the CTAs specific. Use buttons like "Shop Rich Red Sneakers" and add a price or "from $X" under each shoe.
- 4. Add a kids' size row (toddler, little kid, big kid) next to women's and men's.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "The colors of the season are here"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed broken-experience defect, an empty greeting that renders as `Hi ,`. It also has several markup and accessibility problems: a schemeless pixel URL, two `http://` images without alt text, ineffective `<meta>` tags and a zoom-blocking viewport. The source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and link-level checks could not be completed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Schemeless pixel URL:** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…">` has no `http(s)://`. Clients resolve it as a relative path, so it 404s and the Return Path/Validity seed tracking never fires. The `<img>` also has no `alt`.
- **Unprobed links:** 42 tracking links on click-redirect domains (e.g. `click.emails.skechers.com`) were skipped, so none were checked for dead redirects or correct destinations.
- **Insecure pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108404282&mi_ecmp=MKG_US_NA_U_COLOR_ENGAGEDL14D_EN_11152026` loads over plain HTTP. It may be blocked or trigger mixed-content warnings.
- **Hidden third-party beacons:** four `beacon.krxd.net` images (`1x1_usermatch.gif` ×2, `1x1_ad_impression.gif`) are hidden and have no `alt`. This is a deliverability and privacy-filter risk, and it adds third-party requests on open.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt:** `o.gif` (ink1000.com) and `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` (`http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/…`) have no alt text (QA WARN). The krxd and returnpath pixels also lack `alt`.
- **HTTP image:** `dde00662-…png` is served over `http://` (QA WARN) and may be blocked or replaced by clients that require HTTPS.
- **Ineffective meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv`, so neither declaration is valid.
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `maximum-scale=1` in `<meta name="viewport">` prevents pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4 concern).
- **Missing semantics:** the `<html>` tag has no `lang` attribute, and the visible layout tables (`<table class="widthFull" width="640">`) have no `role="presentation"`.
- **Inconsistent breakpoints:** `.mobile-content`/`.desktop-content` switch at 768px. The responsive utility classes (`.responsive-td`, `.photo`, etc.) switch at 640px, 680px, 480px and 375px. Between 641px and 767px, the mobile-only content may show while the desktop layout rules are still active. This cannot be confirmed without the truncated body.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** the greeting renders `Hi ,` with an empty name field, so the first-name merge field resolved to null with no fallback value. This is a confirmed rendering defect.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication unverified:** no `Authentication-Results` header was found (QA WARN). SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is attributed to the AgentMail relay and is not a confirmed sender fault.
- **Footer not verifiable:** the unsubscribe link, physical postal address and `List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are not in the truncated source or supplied headers.
- **Privacy:** `beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif?partner=…_sha25…&partner_uid=adf27fee…` and `…_subid&partner_uid=122277199` pass a hashed identifier and a subscriber ID to a third party in the image URL. This is visible in the source. Whether it is disclosed or consented to cannot be verified from the email.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No issues confirmed. The campaign ID `MKG_US_NA_U_COLOR_ENGAGEDL14D_EN_11152026` appears consistently in the ink1000 and krxd pixels.
- The destination URLs and any UTM parameters sit behind the click-tracking redirects, which were skipped. UTM presence and landing alignment could not be verified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a fallback to the first-name merge field (e.g. `Hi there,`) or suppress the greeting when the value is empty. This is the only FAIL.
2. Fix `pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif` to a full `https://` URL, or remove it if the seed tracking is no longer used.
3. Serve `dde00662-…png` and `o.gif` over HTTPS, and add `alt` to every `<img>`. Use `alt=""` for decorative images and tracking pixels.
4. Change the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport, add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and add `role="presentation"` to layout tables.
6. Align the 768px content-toggle breakpoint with the 640px layout breakpoint, and test at 641–767px.
7. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and raw headers to verify the unsubscribe link, postal address, `List-Unsubscribe`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC and UTM parameters on destination URLs.
8. Resolve a sample of the 42 tracked links end-to-end to confirm final landing URLs, status codes and UTM parameters.
