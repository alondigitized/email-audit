# Finish your gift list today
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-09**
## Executive summary

- Sally Beauty again. By my count it's about the twenty-second send since mid-September, and I'm tired of it. I opened it because I'm a mom with a gift list, not because I wanted more Sally. The subject says "finish your gift list," but the hero says "Finishing touches for the season," and that's soft. I never find out what the gift is or what I'd save.
- The red hero and the woman touching her face look nice. Then I scroll and it's ion hair color booster, a tint brush and nail glue. That's pro-stylist stuff, not gifts. The only real offer is the "Early Black Friday Deals / Big Bottle Sale, buy one get one 50% off," and it's way down the page below the fold. The Nov 8 email already pushed Black Friday at 40% off, so this feels like a cousin of that one.
- Would I click? Probably not. I'm shopping for a 5-year-old girl and a 9-year-old boy, and nothing here is for kids. I'd mark it read. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The "Sally" logo and the red and gold hero feel festive and are easy to recognize.
- "Free 2-hour delivery" and the "Buy online, pick up in-store" strip suit a busy mom who needs things fast.
- The "Big Bottle Sale" block is bold. BOGO 50% off is a concrete deal I can read in two seconds.
- The Klarna "4 interest-free payments" line is a nice touch for holiday budgeting.
- The icon row (free shipping $40+, 2-hour delivery) is quick to scan on my phone.

## What's weak

- The subject promises a gift list, but the body is mostly hair care and nail glue, not gift ideas.
- The best offer, the BOGO 50% off, is buried below four product tiles when it should be the hero.
- The products have no prices, no stars and no reviews. I research before I buy, and I can't tell if anything is a deal.
- There's nothing about Sally Beauty Rewards or member pricing, even though I'm loyal to rewards programs.
- Nothing for kids or families. I'm a mom of two, and this reads like it's for a salon pro.
- The cadence is tiring, and the black Black Friday banner repeats what I got yesterday.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the BOGO 50% Big Bottle Sale in the hero with a deadline, and make the subject say so.
- 2. Make the gifts real. Use giftable sets with prices and "under $25" tiers instead of random hair tools.
- 3. Show Rewards. Add "Members get extra points" or a member price on each tile.
- 4. Add star ratings and prices to the product tiles.
- 5. Cut back the frequency, or make sends different from each other.
- **Subject Alt A:** `BOGO 50% off gifts, ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Gifts under $25 + free 2-hour delivery`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Buy one, get one 50% off big bottles. Pick up in store in 2 hours.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Rewards members earn extra points on skin care and fragrance sets.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "Finish your gift list today" (Sally Beauty)

## 1. Technical Summary
The source is a standard XHTML table-based template with a mobile media query. The QA run found no hard failures (86% pass, 0 issues, 1 warning). The evidence shows an unresolved ad-pixel macro, an insecure `http://` pixel, several images with missing alt text, and a user identifier in a third-party image URL. The HTML was truncated and 51 links were not probed, so link, compliance and UTM checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved macro:** the pixel `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&...` still contains the literal `[CACHEBUSTER]`. It is not cache-busting and may log bad data or be rejected.
- **Insecure pixel:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990...` is plain HTTP. It may be blocked or rewritten by clients, and it can trigger mixed-content warnings in webmail.
- **Unverified links:** 51 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so their destinations and status are unknown.
- **Pixel stack:** there are at least three third-party pixels (`pxl.mon-trk.com`, `t.myvisualiq.net`, `login.dotomi.com`). Each adds a load dependency and a failure point.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** 9 images are flagged.
  - Four dynamic product images are `summary.png` from `sallyholdings.us-4.evergage.com/api/dataset/sallybeauty_prod/campaign/88L7Q/YBZc1/{1..4}/summary.png`.
  - Two images have an empty `src` and no alt.
  - Three are tracking pixels (`003aZ00000s0I5yQAE`, `impression_pixel`, `UCMController`) that should carry `alt=""`.
  - The product images have no alternative text, so they are unreadable with images off or in a screen reader.
- **Empty `src`:** two `<img>` elements have an empty `src`, which can produce broken-image icons or a request to the page URL.
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=edge" />` have no `http-equiv`, so neither declares anything. The charset is then left to the `Content-Type` header.
- **Font fallback:** `a{font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif}` references a font with no `@font-face` or `src`, so it always falls back to Helvetica. This is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Unresolved token:** `[CACHEBUSTER]` is a literal placeholder in the `myvisualiq` URL (see section 2).
- **Identifier in image URL:** the Evergage URLs carry `?userId=martha.st...` (truncated in the QA output). This looks like a name or email-derived identifier in a query string. It is sent to a third party and can end up in proxy and server logs. The pixel path `pxl.mon-trk.com/.../003aZ00000s0I5yQAE` also carries a CRM-style contact ID.
- **Dynamic image dependency:** the Evergage `summary.png` images are generated per request. If the service fails, the blocks render empty, and with no alt text there is no fallback.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication:** the QA tool reported "Authentication-Results header not found". The cause is the AgentMail relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unverified. It is not confirmed as a failure.
- **Unsubscribe and address:** the unsubscribe link, physical address and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are not visible in the truncated source or QA data. I cannot confirm them either way.
- **Sender:** `sallybeauty@em.sallybeauty.com` uses a subdomain of the brand domain, which is consistent with a properly aligned sending setup.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Insufficient data. The visible source has no CTA hrefs, and all 51 tracking links were unprobed. UTM parameters, redirect chains and landing-page status cannot be assessed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the `myvisualiq` pixel so `[CACHEBUSTER]` is resolved at send time, or remove it.
2. Change the `login.dotomi.com` pixel to `https://`, or drop it.
3. Add meaningful `alt` text to the four Evergage product images. Set `alt=""` on the tracking pixels. Remove or populate the two empty-`src` images.
4. Replace the broken meta tags with `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge" />`.
5. Review whether `userId=martha.st...` and the contact ID in pixel paths expose PII to third parties. Use opaque IDs if they do.
6. Re-run the audit with the full, untruncated HTML. Capture raw headers (`Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`), and follow redirects on the sampled tracking links to check UTM and landing pages.
