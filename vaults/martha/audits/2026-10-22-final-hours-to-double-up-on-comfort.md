# Final hours to double up on comfort
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- My first thought was that it's the last day of "2 for $100" and I've now seen that banner every day since 10/16. The "Final hours" subject is a bit of a boy-who-cried-wolf. The top of the email does treat me like a member: "Hi, Martha," 1000 points, and a $5 reward I can redeem. That's the fourth send in a row doing it, after 10/17, 10/18 and 10/20 ("Find your comfort zone"). I'd like it more if the rest of the email followed through.
- The body is three shoes, one each for women, men and kids. The kids' shoe is a dusty-pink quilted lace-up, with no velcro, no light-up and nothing for my 9-year-old. Compare that to the 10/19 Halloween email, where the glittery high-top and light-up sneakers were exactly what my kids would point at. This one is a generic "everybody" email.
- Would I click? Maybe, because it's the last day and 20% off for members is real money. I'd probably tap "Shop Kids'" and see whether the 2-for-$100 pairs work for both kids. I wouldn't forward it. It's a deadline reminder, not a find.

## What's working

- "Hi, Martha," the Member tag and the 1000 points with a $5 redeem link up top make me feel known, not like a stranger.
- "2 FOR $100" is huge and unmissable, and the "Valid thru 10/21" makes the deadline believable.
- The "Free returns within 45 days (90 for members)" banner takes the risk out of buying sizes for growing kids.
- "Members take 20% OFF" speaks to the rewards-program loyalty I actually have.
- The Klarna/Afterpay row and the 20%-off text signup are handy for a price-sensitive mom.

## What's weak

- Nothing in the kids' section matches my kids: no velcro or light-up for my 5-year-old, and no blue/black durable lace-ups for my 9-year-old.
- The kids' shoe is a quilted pink adult-looking sneaker, and it's the third of three equal blocks.
- The buttons are all category links ("Shop Women's/Men's/Kids'"), not a specific product or pair, so I have to hunt.
- The two stacked offers (2 for $100, and 20% off "almost everything else") leave me unsure whether they combine. The exclusions are buried in tiny fine print at the bottom.
- It's another 2-for-$100 send in a week of near-identical ones, and the "final hours" urgency has been used before.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put kids first and make them specific. Show a light-up or velcro girls' pair and a blue/black lace-up boys' pair, then a "2 pairs for the kids = $100" line.
- 2. Say in one line whether 2-for-$100 and member 20% off stack, so the fine print isn't doing the explaining.
- 3. Use real urgency, such as "Ends tonight at midnight", instead of generic "final hours".
- 4. Link each shoe to its own product page, not a category.
- 5. Show some social proof, like star ratings or "best seller", under the shoes.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Martha, your 20% member discount ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Two pairs for $100 plus 20% off for members. Last day.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has two confirmed defects: an empty personalization slot ("Hi ,") and no plain-text part. The visible markup also has a malformed tracking-pixel URL, non-HTTPS images, malformed `<meta>` tags, and missing accessibility attributes. The HTML is truncated and the 32 tracking links were not probed, so the footer, unsubscribe link, and link destinations could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed pixel URL:** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…" width="1" height="1">` has no scheme. Clients will treat it as a relative path, so the request fails and the Return Path/Validity seed-monitoring pixel never fires.
- **Non-HTTPS images:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108404282&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_SITEBUNDLE_ENGAGEDL7D_EN_10212026` and `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`. Gmail's proxy upgrades most of these, but some clients block mixed content. The `.png` is first-party, so `https://` on that host would fix it.
- **Third-party beacons:** Three `beacon.krxd.net` pixels and one `ink1000.com` pixel sit in a `display:none` wrapper. Two of the `krxd` pixels pass `partner_uid` values (a 64-hex string and `122277199`).
- **Skipped links:** 32 links on `click.emails.skechers.com` redirects were not probed, so their destinations are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv`, so neither declaration takes effect. Encoding then depends on the MIME `Content-Type` header.
- **Missing alt text:** `o.gif` and `dde00662-…png` have no `alt` (QA WARN). The Return Path pixel and the `krxd` pixels also have no `alt`.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">` stops pinch-zoom on mobile.
- **Layout tables:** The visible tables (`<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0" border="0">`, `<table class="widthFull" width="640">`) have no `role="presentation"`. Screen readers may announce them as data tables.
- **Document language:** `<html xmlns=…>` has no `lang` attribute.
- **Pixel containers:** The hidden pixel `<div>` wrappers use `display:none !important`. Some clients don't load images inside hidden containers, which can undercount opens.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Empty greeting:** QA FAIL, "Greeting has empty name field (e.g. 'Hi ,')". The first-name merge field resolved to empty, and there is no fallback string such as "Hi there,". The test recipient may lack a first-name value, but the template should handle that case.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No plain-text part:** QA FAIL, "Text version is 0 chars". A missing text alternative hurts deliverability and accessibility.
- **Authentication:** `Authentication-Results` was not found (QA WARN). The report attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown rather than failing. The sender domain `emails.skechers.com` matches the click and image domains, which is good for alignment.
- **Footer and unsubscribe:** The visible HTML doesn't reach the footer, so I can't confirm the unsubscribe link, the physical address, or `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers. I'm not flagging them as missing.
- **Third-party data sharing:** The `krxd` user-match pixels pass identifiers (`partner_uid=adf27fee…` and `subid=122277199`). The 64-hex value looks like a SHA-256 of the email address, but the source doesn't confirm that. If it is, this is third-party data sharing that the privacy disclosure should cover.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** They can't be verified. The 32 links go through `click.emails.skechers.com` redirects that were skipped, so the landing URLs and any `utm_*` values are unknown.
- **Campaign ID:** The only visible identifier is `PRO_US_NA_U_SITEBUNDLE_ENGAGEDL7D_EN_10212026_B` in the `krxd` and `ink1000` pixels. It is not a UTM parameter.
- **Landing alignment:** The offer in the preheader ("2 for $100 ends today") could not be matched to a landing page because no destination was resolved.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a fallback for the first-name merge field (for example `%%=IIF(Empty(@firstname),"there",@firstname)=%%`) so the greeting never renders "Hi ,".
2. Add a multipart plain-text alternative that carries the offer and the unsubscribe link.
3. Fix the Return Path pixel to `https://pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=…`.
4. Move `image.emails.skechers.com` and `ink1000.com` assets to `https://`, and add `alt=""` to the decorative pixels and meaningful `alt` to `dde00662-…png`.
5. Change the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
6. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, add `role="presentation"` to layout tables, and remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
7. Re-run the audit against the full HTML and the raw headers (not just the AgentMail relay copy). That would let you check `List-Unsubscribe`, `Authentication-Results`, the footer address, and the UTM parameters after redirect resolution.
