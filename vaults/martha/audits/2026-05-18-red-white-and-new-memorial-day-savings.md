## What's working

- **BOGO 50% off is the first thing my eye lands on** — before I register anything else, the deal is already in my brain. That's good email design.
- **Memorial Day timing is dead-on** — end of school year, summer camp season, hot weather starting. This hit my inbox at exactly the right moment.
- **Skechers Plus $3 reward is visible in the hero** — small dollar amount but it signals they know I'm a member and rewards a bigger cart, which is how I actually shop.
- **SHOP KIDS is labeled and present** — not the lead, but I found it quickly without hunting; it exists as its own clear section.
- **Patriotic color story is cohesive throughout** — the email looks purposeful, and that small thing makes me trust the brand a bit more in the moment.

## What's weak

- **Preview text is schema.org JSON. For the fifth consecutive day.** At this point I'm not surprised, I'm just tired. This erodes trust before the email even opens.
- **Kids is CTA number three, behind Women's and Men's** — for a family-segment subscriber, that ordering costs me two extra seconds of scroll and attention I don't have on a Sunday morning.
- **No specific kids product in the hero or the kids section** — a single light-up sneaker or a velcro slip-on would stop my scroll completely. The small generic kid-shoe thumbnail doesn't do that work.
- **No end date anywhere I can see** — "Memorial Day" implies urgency but nowhere does the email say "ends Monday, May 26." I won't manufacture the deadline myself; I'll just bookmark and forget.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the preview text bug today, not after the holiday.** Five sends, five broken previews — that's a loyalty leak in real time, and it's the single highest-ROI fix on the list.
- 2. **Put a kids hero shot in the email body** — one light-up sneaker for girls, one durable athletic shoe for boys, both under the BOGO callout. A mom shopping for two kids with one BOGO is highly motivated; the creative should meet her there.
- 3. **Add an explicit offer expiration** — "BOGO ends Monday, May 26" in the hero. Credible, specific, no extra pressure tactics needed.
- 4. **Segment the CTA order for family shoppers** — any subscriber who has clicked KIDS in the last 90 days should see Kids → Women's → Men's, not the reverse.
- 5. **Give the 25% apparel section its own send** — buried as a secondary block inside a BOGO shoe email, it competes for attention and loses. I tuned it out.

## Technical Audit — Skechers "Red, White, and NEW" Memorial Day Savings

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email has one critical broken-experience bug (empty personalization token in the greeting) and a systemic HTTP image-hosting issue affecting 12+ assets. No structural rendering failures detected, but the mixed-protocol image loading creates client-dependent blocking risk.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**No broken destination links confirmed.** 26 click-redirect URLs via `click.emails.skechers.com` were skipped in HTTP probing (expected for tracked links).

**Third-party pixel exposure:** Three beacon pixels are present in the hidden preheader table:
- `beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif` — two instances with `partner_uid` values (one SHA-256 hash, one raw subscriber ID `122277199`)
- `beacon.krxd.net/1x1_ad_impression.gif` — campaign ID `PRO_US_NONPURCLICK_U_SITEBUNDLE_NONPURCH_EN_05182026`
- `pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif` — inbox monitoring pixel

All load over HTTPS. No issue with these specifically, but the raw subscriber ID in the Krux pixel is worth noting for data hygiene review.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**HTTP image sources (systemic — 12 assets affected):** All images hosted on `image.emails.skechers.com` use `http://` rather than `https://`. Modern email clients (Gmail, Outlook.com, Apple Mail on iOS 15+) proxy or block mixed-content image loads. Affected assets include the Skechers logo, app store badges, social icons, and feature images:

```

... (8 additional assets)
```

One additional HTTP-only tracking pixel via `ink1000.com`:
```
http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif
```

**Missing alt text — 2 images:**
- `o.gif` (ink1000 tracking pixel) — no `alt` attribute
- `49468f73-4651-4af3-bea2-61d1ae5db486.png` (content image, purpose unclear from filename) — no `alt` attribute

The tracking pixel missing alt is low severity; the content image missing alt fails WCAG 1.1.1 for screen readers.

**`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`** is set globally in `* {}`. This prevents iOS from adjusting small text, which can reduce readability on older devices. The safer value is `100%`.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**CRITICAL — Empty greeting slot:**

QA detected a rendered greeting of `"Hi ,"` — the subscriber first-name merge token resolved to an empty string. This is a confirmed broken experience. The template is likely missing a fallback value on the name field (e.g., `%%=v(@firstName,'there')=%%` in SFMC AMPscript, or equivalent).

No other unresolved merge tokens were detected in the truncated source.
