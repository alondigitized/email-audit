# Comfort In Every Stride
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-08**
## Executive summary

- I grabbed my phone while the kids were fighting over the TV remote and opened this one mostly out of habit — I'm subscribed to Skechers and BOGO always makes me at least look. And honestly, the email itself is pretty clean once you're in. The hero "Make Your Move" banner + BOGO 50% off lands above the fold, the walking and running sections are neatly organized, and the 25% off apparel callout is a bonus I almost missed.
- But here's the thing — this is the third email in a row from Skechers where I opened it and thought, *wait, what was the preview text again?* Because in my inbox it was a wall of JSON schema code. Again. Same broken preview bug that was there on June 6th and June 7th. At some point I'm going to start ignoring Skechers previews entirely because I've trained myself that they're always garbage. That's a real loyalty erosion problem.
- As for the content itself: it's a solid adult athletic email with a good offer. But I primarily opened Skechers to shop for my 5-year-old who needs sneakers and my 9-year-old who just blew out the toe on his last pair. Kids is one text link buried at the bottom. I would click the BOGO and navigate to kids myself, but the email did zero work helping me get there. Mark as read, maybe shop, but I wouldn't forward this to anyone.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% off (or 25% single pair) is immediately visible at the top** — that offer structure works for me because I'm usually buying multiple pairs anyway (one for each kid plus maybe something for me).
- **Walking section with women's CTA** — even though I'm shopping for kids first, I do think about my own feet, and seeing walking shoes in a dedicated section feels like something I'd circle back to.
- **25% off apparel** — I didn't expect this and it's a pleasant extra. The female model in that section actually looks like someone my age.
- **"KIDS" is at least listed** in the additional styles row — it's not featured, but at least it's there as a navigation option.
- **Clean, mobile-friendly layout** — sections are clearly delineated, tap targets look reasonable, nothing is overlapping or broken.

## What's weak

- **Preview text is broken JSON-LD schema code — again.** This is the third consecutive send with this bug (June 6, June 7, now June 25). At this point it's a persistent deliverability-adjacent credibility issue that Skechers needs to fix at the template level.
- **Zero kids content above the fold** — or anywhere in the visual sections. My primary reason for opening Skechers is my kids' shoes, and the email treats kids as a footnote text link. There are no product images, no featured styles for boys or girls.
- **Hero model is male and athletic-performance focused** — I'm a 34-year-old mom shopping on her phone. The lone male runner in the hero isn't *wrong* exactly, but it's not speaking to me.
- **No loyalty/rewards mention** — I'm in Skechers Plus. If this BOGO earns points or stacks with member pricing, tell me. That's literally a deciding factor.
- **"Hi Nadine,"** — the personalization in the header has the wrong name. Not Martha, not "Hi [member]," — it says Nadine. That's a data error that undermines any sense that Skechers actually knows me.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the preview text bug — this is the most urgent fix.** Three sends in a row with schema.org JSON as the preheader is an unacceptable pattern. The template is clearly leaking a structured data block into the preview slot. Fix it before the next send, full stop.
- 2. **Add a Kids section with product imagery.** Even a single two-column row showing boys' and girls' styles would capture the reason I actually open Skechers emails.
- 3. **Reference loyalty membership in the BOGO offer copy** — something like "Skechers Plus members: your BOGO stacks with points."
- 4. **Swap or supplement the hero model** — a parent-and-kid lifestyle shot, or at minimum a female athlete, would feel more like me.
- 5. **Fix the personalization token** — "Hi Nadine" is a visible data error in the header.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `BOGO 50% off or 25% off a single pair — shop walking, running, kids and more.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stock up on summer styles for the whole family — BOGO 50% off now.`
