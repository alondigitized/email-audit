## What's weak

- **No kids' category signal anywhere above the fold** — I'm shopping for a 5-year-old and a 9-year-old and the homepage gives me zero indication that kids' shoes exist here. Navigation is behind a hamburger menu.

- **Slip-ins copy is just starting to come in at the bottom** — the product callout is getting cut off. I don't know yet if this is leading me somewhere useful or not.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Kids" quick-link in the top nav or as a pinned category chip below the nav bar** — even a single visible tap to kids' shoes would tell me "yes, this homepage is for me too." Right now I have to go hunting.
- **Swap one of the hero panels for a family/kid lifestyle shot** — a mom helping a kid slip into Slip-ins would speak directly to why I'm here. Keep the celebrity for brand awareness but make room for the actual family use case.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold in the top banner or below the hero** — "Free shipping on orders $50+" or "BOGO + free ship over $X" is conversion gold for a mom buying for two kids at once.
- **Make the BOGO banner link to a landing page that includes kids' categories** — right now "details" is a generic link; if it surfaces a curated "BOGO for the whole family" page, I'd click it immediately.
