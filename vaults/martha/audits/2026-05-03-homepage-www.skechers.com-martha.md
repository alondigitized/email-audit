# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-03
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-03**
## Executive summary

- Okay so this is very "celebrity endorsement meets comfortable sneaker brand" — I can see Brooke Burke and Tony Hawk sitting in lifestyle settings wearing their Skechers, and there's a bold BOGO 50% off banner up top. It's pushing Hands Free Slip-ins pretty hard. As a brand it reads as accessible, mainstream, and deal-forward — which honestly checks some boxes for me — but I opened this hoping to find shoes for my kids and nothing above the fold even hints that kids' shoes exist here.
- **6/10**
- + Concrete offer visible above fold: "BOGO 50% OFF Applied at Cart" — very specific, high impact for a mom buying multiple pairs
- + Second promo also visible: "25% OFF Apparel" with a Shop link
- + Visual hierarchy is reasonably clear: banner → nav → promo strip → hero → Slip-ins CTA flows logically
- + No visible render bugs: layout looks clean and tight on mobile
- + Demographic match partial: Brooke Burke in a pink sweater and jeans reads "practical casual 30s-something woman" close enough to me
- + Seasonal relevance: Spring Apparel Sale in May lands correctly
- − No loyalty/rewards hook visible — I'm a rewards-program shopper and there's nothing acknowledging that
- − Hero does not speak to my actual shopping priority (kids shoes for a 5- and 9-year-old)
- − No unmistakable button CTA — "Shop | details" is a text link, and the Slip-ins text at the bottom is a headline, not a button
- − No returning-shopper hook (no "welcome back," no recently viewed, no points balance)

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF banner** — pinned at the very top in blue, impossible to miss. As someone who might be buying shoes for two kids AND myself, BOGO math is immediately relevant.
- **Second promo strip** — "25% OFF Apparel" with a Shop link stacked right below the nav. Two distinct offers above the fold feels generous.
- **Brooke Burke lifestyle shot** — she's sitting casually in a home setting in a pink sweater and jeans, and it doesn't feel aspirational/unattainable. I can see myself in that energy on a weekend.
- **"Hands Free Slip-ins" positioning** — even though the headline isn't clickable, my mom brain immediately went "easy on/off shoes are great for kids" — so the product concept resonates even if it's aimed at adults in this frame.
- **Mobile layout** — clean, no overlapping text, the two-panel celebrity photo fits the screen well.

## What's weak

- **No kids section in sight** — my primary reason for being here is my 5-year-old daughter and 9-year-old son, and there's zero kids content above the fold. No "Shop Kids" button, no kids imagery, nothing.
- **Celebrity-forward framing** — Brooke Burke and Tony Hawk are fine but this reads like a TV commercial, not a shopping experience tailored to me. I came here to buy shoes, not admire celebrities.
- **No actual button CTA** — "Shop | details" is a small text link and the Slip-ins text is decorative. I shouldn't have to hunt for where to tap.
- **No loyalty/rewards acknowledgment** — I'm a repeat shopper who cares about points and member benefits, and there's nothing up here acknowledging that relationship.
- **No free-shipping info** — for a price-sensitive mom buying a few pairs, knowing the threshold upfront matters before I even start browsing.
- **Apparel sale feels off-mission** — I came for shoes and the secondary banner is pushing apparel. It dilutes the focus.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop Kids" CTA directly in or below the hero** — even a simple "Shop Girls | Shop Boys" row of tiles would immediately serve my actual use case without a full redesign.
- **Swap or supplement the celebrity hero with a family/lifestyle shot** — show a mom grabbing slip-ins for herself and her kid. The Slip-ins concept literally sells itself to parents ("they can put them on themselves"), just aim the imagery.
- **Replace the text "Shop | details" link with a real button** — a filled pill button labeled "Shop the Sale" or "Shop Slip-ins" right below the promo banner strip would double tap-rate with almost zero dev cost.
- **Surface the rewards hook in the top banner or nav** — even "Skechers Plus members earn 2X points this weekend" pinned next to the BOGO would make me feel seen as a returning customer.
