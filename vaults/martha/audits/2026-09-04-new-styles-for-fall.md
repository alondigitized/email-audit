# New styles for fall
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-04**
## Executive summary

- So this is "New styles for fall" and... it's an entire boot and bootie catalog for women, with a fall-fashion model in a fur collar strutting down a city street. Cute, but I opened an email hoping for something for my kids and instead I get suede booties, a "sparkle" charms-for-your-laces upsell, and a Klarna reminder. There's a KIDS link buried way down in the text-link stack at the bottom, tied for billing with CLOTHING and SALE — that's it, that's the whole nod to my actual browsing habits.
- The "Hi, Martha, Member, 1000 points" header and the 20% Plus discount up top are the same beats every Skechers email hits — consistent with Labor Day savings (9/1) and the Plus 20% sends this week, so at least it's not new friction, just the same non-event. The 40% off banner mid-email is the strongest offer visually, but it's generic "select styles," no category called out.
- I'm not clicking through the boots — not my size, not my season yet, not my kid. I'd scroll straight to KIDS at the bottom, and honestly I might just archive this one without doing that because I've learned by now Skechers buries the kids' stuff every time.

## What's working

- The 20% Plus member banner at top is instantly scannable — bold percentage, clear CTA button.
- Product photography is clean, well-lit, no render bugs.
- The 40% off "Shop the Sale" banner has decent visual weight and breaks up the fashion-catalog monotony.
- Member points ("1000 points," "$5 reward") still sitting right at the top is a nice loyalty touch, same as every recent send.

## What's weak

- Zero kids' content — no velcro sneakers, no light-up soles, nothing pink/purple/sparkle for my daughter, nothing durable/lace-up for my son. Just a plain "KIDS" text link at the very bottom.
- The hero model and "Fall for Skechers" boot lineup is 100% adult women's fashion — doesn't match how I actually shop this brand.
- "Add a Little Sparkle" charms/laces upsell feels like a stretch add-on, not something I came here for.
- The 40% off sale banner doesn't say what's included — no category, no product shown, just "select styles."
- This is basically the same 20%-off-Plus-members template I've seen three times this week already (Labor Day 9/1, Best Sellers 9/3) — nothing about it feels new except the boots.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a kids' back-to-school block — light-up sneakers for my daughter, durable lace-ups for my son — right under the discount banner, not buried as a text link at the bottom. That's the single biggest miss given who's actually reading this.
- 2. Make the 40% off banner specific: name the category or show a hero product so it's not just a floating number.
- 3. Segment this send — if I've never clicked women's boots, don't lead a whole email with them; lead with what I've browsed.
- 4. Add a family bundle callout with a promo code — that's the thing that'd actually get me to open my wallet on a Skechers email.
