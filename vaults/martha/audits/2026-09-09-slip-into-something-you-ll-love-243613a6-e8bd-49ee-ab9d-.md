## What's working

- "Hi, Martha" greeting and my 1000 points balance still shows up front, so at least they know I'm a member.
- The 20% off code (EXTRA20) is clearly boxed with a visible expiration date reference.
- Product shots are clean and the slip-in shoes themselves look genuinely comfortable, which fits my "on my feet all day" reality.
- Loyalty perks (Afterpay/Klarna, curbside pickup, app download) are all listed at the bottom, which I do actually use.

## What's weak

- Zero kids' product presence — no velcro, no light-up soles, no lace-up sneakers for my son, nothing pink/purple/sparkle for my daughter. This is 100% an adult slip-in catalog.
- The hero model photo is flagged "AI-GENERATED MODEL," which is a strange and slightly off-putting thing to see in a shoe ad — makes it feel less trustworthy.
- Slip-ins aren't a category I shop for myself — I need shoes I can actually run after a toddler in, not "hands free" convenience shoes.
- "Kids Clothing" is buried in a tiny text link near the bottom, way after two full hero sections and two shop buttons for adult slip-ins.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a kids' back-to-school shoe module above the fold — velcro/light-up for my daughter, lace-up durable sneakers for my son — since that's what actually gets me to click, not adult slip-ins.
- 2. Move the 20% off code higher, right under the points banner, instead of making me scroll through two full hero sections first.
- 3. Drop or clarify the "AI-GENERATED MODEL" tag — if it has to be there for disclosure reasons, make it small and unobtrusive, not something that makes me question the whole photo.
- 4. Segment this send — people who've bought kids' shoes from me before shouldn't get an all-adult slip-in email; show me what's relevant to my purchase history.

## Technical Audit

1. **Technical Summary**: The email renders via standard SFMC (Salesforce Marketing Cloud) tracking infrastructure with hybrid mobile/desktop CSS blocks; automated QA flags a broken personalization token and a missing plain-text MIME part as the two hard failures, alongside widespread non-HTTPS image sourcing.

2. **Link & Tracking Issues**
- 33 tracking/click-redirect links (`click.emails.skechers.com`) were skipped by the HTTP prober — expected for redirect-wrapped SFMC links, not itself a defect, but unverified end-to-end.
- Open-tracking pixel present: `https://click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx?SHZ5KMPNIY2UXPYDZJMNQ2ZNE4.100224...` — HTTPS, fine.
- Third-party pixel served over **HTTP**: `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108404282&mi_ecmp=MKG_US_NA_U_SLIPIN_ENGAGEDL30_EN_09092026_A` — flagged by QA, may be blocked by mail clients enforcing HTTPS-only image loads, and could trigger mixed-content warnings.
- Return Path pixel malformed: `pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=...` is missing the `http(s)://` scheme entirely in the `src` attribute — this will fail to load in any client since it's not a valid absolute or relative URL.
- Kantar/Krux (`beacon.krxd.net`) user-match and ad-impression pixels present, served over HTTPS — no issue.

3. **Rendering & Accessibility**
- Multiple `<img>` tags missing `alt` text (QA-confirmed): `o.gif` and `49468f73-4651-4af3-bea2-61d1ae5db486.png`. All other content images appear to carry descriptive alt attributes (e.g., "Skechers", "AfterPay", "Find A Store").
- Nine images served over `http://` instead of `https://` (QA-confirmed list: logo, product image, app-store badges, curbside/AfterPay/store-locator icons, social icons). On HTTPS-loaded email clients (Gmail, Apple Mail, Outlook.com) these will likely be blocked or proxy-rewritten, causing broken images.
- Media queries and MSO/Outlook conditional-friendly CSS (`.ReadMsgBody`, `.ExternalClass`, `x-apple-data-detectors`) are present and structured correctly for cross-client compatibility — no issues found in the visible markup.

4. **Personalization & Merge Tokens**

This is a data-population or fallback-default issue in the merge field, not a template defect per se, but will visibly render as broken to any recipient whose profile field is empty and lacks a fallback value.

5. **Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**

Recommend verifying the untruncated footer contains a functioning unsubscribe link and physical address per CAN-SPAM.
- No authentication headers (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) were included in the provided data — cannot assess from HTML source alone; this requires raw email headers, not the rendered HTML.

6. **Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Campaign identifier `MKG_US_NA_U_SLIPIN_ENGAGEDL30_EN_09092026_A` is consistently applied across the Krux ad-impression pixel and the ink1000 tracking pixel, indicating consistent campaign tagging at the pixel level.
- Actual click-through UTM parameters cannot be verified because all 33 outbound links route through `click.emails.skechers.com` redirect wrappers, which were skipped by the HTTP prober — landing page alignment and UTM propagation past the redirect are unconfirmed.
