# Technical Email Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard SFMC-style responsive HTML email with multiple tracking/beacon pixels; source is truncated before any visible CTA links, so link/compliance verification is limited to what's present. Primary issues found: pervasive non-HTTPS image sources and two images missing alt text.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 5 tracking pixels fire on open: `click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx` (SFMC open tracking), two Krux/Salesforce DMP beacons (`beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif`), one Krux ad-impression beacon, and an Ink1000 engagement pixel (`ink1000.com/p/up/.../o.gif`).
- The Ink1000 pixel is loaded over **plain HTTP** (`http://www.ink1000.com/...`), not HTTPS — flagged by QA as likely to be blocked by mail clients enforcing TLS-only image loading, which would silently break this tracking vendor's attribution.
- QA reports 38 additional tracking/click-redirect links were skipped from HTTP probing (expected behavior for redirect-wrapped links; cannot independently verify destination health from static analysis alone).
- No visible CTA `<a href>` links are present in the truncated source, so click-through URL structure could not be reviewed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- Two images are missing `alt` text: `o.gif` (Ink1000 pixel) and `49468f73-4651-4af3-bea2-61d1ae5db486.png`. The pixel omission is low-impact (1x1 tracking image), but the second is a content/product image and should have descriptive alt text for screen readers and for when images are blocked.
- **9 content/UI images load over HTTP instead of HTTPS**: the Skechers header logo, a product image, App Store/Google Play badges, Curbside Pickup, AfterPay, Find A Store, and the Instagram/Facebook/YouTube social icons (all served from `image.emails.skechers.com`). Mixed-content blocking in modern mail clients (Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail) may cause these to fail to render, leaving broken-image placeholders across the header, footer nav, and social bar.
- Responsive markup is otherwise solid: proper viewport meta tag, MSO/Outlook conditional-friendly reset styles, `-ms-interpolation-mode`, mobile breakpoints at 375px/640px/680px, and a `.mobile-content`/`.desktop-content` toggle pair for adaptive layout.
- Hidden preheader div is correctly implemented (`display:none !important` + zero font-size) with a legitimate preview line ("Plus, members get a $5 reward...").
