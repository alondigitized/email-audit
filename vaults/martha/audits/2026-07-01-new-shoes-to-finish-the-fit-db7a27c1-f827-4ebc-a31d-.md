# New Shoes to Finish the Fit
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-01**
## Executive summary

- Okay, real talk — the first thing that hit me when this showed up in my inbox wasn't the subject line, it was the preview text. It was literally a string of code. `{ "@context": "http://schema.org/"...` just staring back at me in gray text next to the subject. I don't know what that means and honestly it gave me a half-second of "is this a phishing thing?" before I recognized the Skechers name. Not a great first impression on a Tuesday morning when I'm already skimming 40 emails.
- Inside, the email itself is actually pretty solid. The BOGO 50% off banner is the first thing my eyes went to — love that — and "JUST IN" is clean and easy to read. But here's my honest reaction: I'm opening Skechers hoping to find something for my daughter or my son, and kids aren't featured anywhere prominent. There are text links for "KIDS" buried near the footer, but the entire body is adult women's and men's new arrivals. That's not the reason I open these.
- I'd scroll through for the BOGO since that's a real, usable offer. But I wouldn't click through with any excitement, and I definitely wouldn't forward it. This is also the seventh Skechers email I've gotten in about ten days — yesterday's BOBS send, the July 4th one last week, Prime Time Savings before that — I'm starting to go a little numb on this brand.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% off at the very top.** That banner stops my scroll immediately — I see savings before anything else, which is exactly the right order for someone like me.
- **"JUST IN" with the new arrivals concept is clean.** Simple, unfussy — I understand what this is about at a glance.
- **The women's product shot is appealing.** That pink and white chunky sneaker is actually cute, and I can see the style clearly without zooming on my phone.
- **Two discount signals in one email.** BOGO at top, Up to 40% off further down — that's two reasons to keep scrolling, which is more than most sends give me.
- **Loyalty mention at the bottom.** It's buried, but I'm a mySkechers member and I noticed it. Nice to be reminded I can earn on this.

## What's weak

- **The preview text is broken code.** JSON-LD schema is leaking into my inbox. Before I even opened this, it looked like a technical error — or worse, something suspicious. This is the single biggest problem with the send.
- **No kids shoes featured.** I have a 5-year-old who needs new sneakers for fall and a 9-year-old who destroys shoes faster than I can replace them. Kids isn't just a text link in a footer — it should be its own section with a product image.
- **"Finish the Fit" is a little too fashion-forward for me.** I'm not curating outfits, I'm replacing shoes. Practical copy would land better.
- **Cadence fatigue is real.** This is at least the seventh email in ten days. By the time this one dropped, I'm in auto-archive mode for Skechers. The BOGO has to work extra hard to overcome my "another promo" reflex.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the preview text — this is non-negotiable.** Replace the JSON leak with actual human copy. Something that picks up where the subject line left off and mentions the offer. This single fix would lift open rates more than any subject line test.
- 2. **Add a kids section with a real product image.** Even a two-up grid — one girl's shoe, one boy's shoe, each with a "SHOP KIDS'" button — would make this feel like it was built for a subscriber like me.
- 3. **Unify the hero and the offer.** Right now "JUST IN" and the BOGO live in separate visual zones. Make them work together — something like "JUST IN — BOGO 50% OFF" so the value prop is inseparable from the new arrivals hook.
- 4. **Pull back on cadence.** A 3-4 day gap before this send would have made the BOGO feel like an event, not another Tuesday promo.

## Technical Audit — Skechers "New Shoes to Finish the Fit" (2026-07-01)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is a standard SFMC-delivered HTML build with no critical rendering failures, but it has a systemic HTTP image-serving problem affecting 12+ assets and two third-party tracking pixels that expose subscriber identity data over unencrypted connections.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Third-party identity sync pixels (high concern)**

Two Krux/Salesforce DMP pixels fire subscriber PII over HTTP:

```
http://beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif?partner=LprH63a7_sfmc_100007688_sha25
  &partner_uid=adf27fee67323d52536b18110294bf793c265b830082caba4b1a32664832189a

http://beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif?partner=LprH63a7_sfmc_100007688_subid
  &partner_uid=122277199
```

`partner_uid` on the first pixel is a SHA-256 hash (likely of the subscriber email); the second carries a raw numeric subscriber ID. Both are transmitted unencrypted. `beacon.krxd.net` does support HTTPS — these should be upgraded.

**Ink1000 tracking pixel (medium)**

```
http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108404282
  &mi_ecmp=MKG_US_L60D_U_NEW_ENGAGEDOPEN_EN_07012026
```

HTTP-only open-tracking pixel. `mi_u=8108404282` appears to be a subscriber identifier transmitted in plaintext.

**26 click-redirect links** — all routed through `click.emails.skechers.com` (HTTPS), not probed directly but domain is correct.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**HTTP image sources — systemic**

12 production images are served over HTTP from `image.emails.skechers.com`, including the Skechers logo, app store badges, social icons, and UI icons. Modern email clients (Gmail, Outlook 2016+, Apple Mail with "load remote images" off) will either block or warn on HTTP assets:

```
http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-...png  (logo)
http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/11/49468f73-...png
http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/11/0ec56c9f-...png  (App Store)
http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/11/9bb508d0-...png  (Google Play)
... (8 additional)
```

The image CDN hostname already exists — this is a scheme-only fix (`http://` → `https://`).

**Missing alt text**

Two images have empty or absent `alt` attributes:
- `o.gif` (Ink1000 tracking pixel) — acceptable for tracking pixels but should use `alt=""`
- `49468f73-4651-4af3-bea2-61d1ae5db486.png` — unidentified content image; alt text required for screen readers and image-blocked render

**`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`**

Set globally via `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }`. This prevents iOS Mail from scaling text for readability on small viewports. Should be `100%` not `none`.

**Duplicate media query blocks**

`@media only screen and (max-width: 375px)` and `@media only screen and (max-width: 640px)` contain identical rule sets. This is redundant dead weight (~1.5 KB uncompressed) and creates specificity ambiguity. Merge into a single breakpoint block.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unrendered merge tokens (e.g., `%%FIRST_NAME%%`, `{{subscriber.first_name}}`) visible in the truncated source. The subscriber ID in the Ink1000 pixel (`mi_u=8108404282`) appears to be a resolved value, not an unfired token. No issues found in the visible source.

---

### 5. Compliance

**CAN-SPAM / unsubscribe** — physical address and unsubscribe mechanism not visible in the truncated source but are standard in Skechers SFMC templates; cannot confirm or deny from provided HTML.

**Authentication headers** — not provided in the source excerpt; DKIM/SPF/DMARC pass status for `emails.skechers.com` cannot be assessed here. Should be verified at delivery.

**Data exposure concern (not strictly CAN-SPAM but relevant)**

The HTTP Krux pixels transmit a hashed subscriber email and a raw subscriber ID over an unencrypted channel. Depending on jurisdiction, this may implicate CCPA/GDPR data-in-transit obligations. Should be flagged to the privacy/legal team.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

Campaign ID `MKG_US_L60D_U_NEW_ENGAGEDOPEN_EN_07012026` appears in the Ink1000 and Krux ad-impression pixels, confirming the campaign tag is firing. However, UTM parameters on destination URLs cannot be confirmed without resolving the 26 click-redirect links — this should be validated in the ESP's link checker against `skechers.com` landing pages to ensure `utm_campaign`, `utm_source`, and `utm_medium` are appended post-redirect.
