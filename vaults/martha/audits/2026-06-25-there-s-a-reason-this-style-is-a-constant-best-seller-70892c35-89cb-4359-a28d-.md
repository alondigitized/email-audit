# There's a Reason This Style Is a Constant Best Seller
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-07**
## Executive summary

- I'll be honest — I almost didn't open this one. I saw that preview text in my inbox and it was a wall of bracket gibberish, some kind of code or schema dump that clearly wasn't meant to be there. That's an immediate trust wobble. Even as someone who's used to Skechers promos, my gut said "something went wrong with this email." The subject is one of those soft-sell teaser lines that doesn't actually tell me what's in it — no deal, no style name, nothing concrete.
- Once I opened it though? It cleans up. The BOGO banner is right there at the top, the Arcade Collection looks fresh, and the layout is genuinely pretty clean. The models feel close to my demographic — everyday woman, casual fits, nothing aspirational-to-the-point-of-alienating. The Backless Styles and Slip-ins sections are a nice breakdown for someone like me who actually needs to think about foot ease at the end of a long day.
- The thing that bugs me is this is ALL about the Arcade line for women. I'm usually on Skechers.com shopping for my daughter's light-ups or my son's sneakers first. This email doesn't even gesture at kids until there's a tiny "KIDS" text link buried in the secondary nav at the very bottom. That's a miss. I'd mark this as read and circle back — probably wouldn't click through right now.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% off banner is front and center** — that's the hook and it landed right at the top, exactly where it should be.
- **Three distinct Arcade sub-categories** (47 styles, Backless, Hands-Free Slip-ins) feel curated rather than overwhelming — I can actually parse what's different between them.
- **Model styling feels age-appropriate** — casual tops, relaxed bottoms, not influencer-y. I can actually picture myself in this.
- **25% Off Apparel section** is a low-key bonus find — I wasn't expecting to see clothing deals, which is occasionally relevant for me.
- **"Curbside Pickup" and "Shop Now, Pay Later"** at the bottom — practical options that tell me Skechers knows how I actually shop.

## What's weak

- **The preview text is completely broken** — it's raw JSON-LD schema markup leaking into the inbox. That's the first thing I saw and it made the send look like a technical failure, not a brand I trust.
- **Subject line is too vague** — "There's a Reason This Style Is a Constant Best Seller" doesn't tell me what the style is, what the deal is, or why I should open right now. Compare this to the June 4th "Summer Starts With the Right Pair" send — that one had seasonal specificity in the line itself.
- **Zero kids content in the main body** — I'm a mom who shops for two kids first. The "KIDS" link buried in the secondary nav is an afterthought, not a reason to click.
- **No urgency or deadline** — the BOGO has no end date I can see. Last time they paired BOGO with "ends tonight" and I actually rushed to checkout.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the preview text immediately** — this is the highest-impact change. A broken preheader tanks open rate before anyone even sees the email body. Ship a resend with real copy.
- 2. **Add a kids module** — even one row with "Shop Kids Styles in the Arcade Line" would double the relevance for a parent segment.
- 3. **Give the BOGO a deadline** — "BOGO 50% Off — Ends Sunday" would push me to act instead of saving for later.
- 4. **Make the subject name the thing** — "Arcade Styles: BOGO 50% Off Right Now" beats a teaser every time for deal-motivated shoppers like me.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Arcade styles: BOGO 50% off sitewide`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your new go-to sneaker (BOGO 50% off today)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Slip-ins, backless styles & 47 new looks — fresh comfort for summer.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The Arcade Collection is here — and BOGO 50% off won't last.`

## Technical Audit — Skechers "Constant Best Seller" Email

**Sent:** 2026-06-25 | **From:** no-reply@emails.skechers.com

---

### 1. Technical Summary

One broken personalization token creates a visible rendering defect for all recipients. Fourteen image assets are served over plain HTTP, risking silent block by security-conscious mail clients. All other issues are warnings.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Return Path pixel — malformed URL (no protocol)**

```html
<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1dbf0dcef79cbad82c12fe25342701e4499" />
```

Missing `https://` prefix. Most email clients will not resolve this as a URL; the pixel fires for no one. All other send-time deliverability signals from Return Path are blind for this campaign.

**27 click-tracking links** route through `click.emails.skechers.com` — correct for SFMC. Link probing was skipped (redirect chains); UTM continuity cannot be confirmed without manual spot-check (see §6).

**Three Krux/DMP sync pixels** fire on open:
- `beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif` (×2, SHA-256 hash + raw subscriber ID)
- `beacon.krxd.net/1x1_ad_impression.gif`

Raw subscriber ID `122277199` is passed in plaintext to a third-party domain. Confirm this is consistent with Skechers' data-sharing disclosures and privacy policy.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**HTTP image sources — 14 assets** (blocked by Gmail, Outlook 365, Apple Mail with "Protect Mail Activity" on)

All `http://image.emails.skechers.com/...` and `http://www.ink1000.com/...` sources are non-HTTPS. Affected assets include the logo, app store badges, social icons, and the ink1000 tracking pixel. Switch CDN base URL to `https://`.

```
http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-...  (logo)
http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/11/49468f73-... (unnamed hero)
http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=...                  (tracking pixel)
+ 11 footer icon assets (App Store, Google Play, social, store)
```

**Missing alt text — 2 images**

- `o.gif` (ink1000 pixel) — decorative/tracking, `alt=""` is acceptable but currently absent
- `49468f73-4651-4af3-bea2-61d1ae5db486.png` — appears to be a content image; missing alt is an accessibility gap

**`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`**

```css
* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }
```

`none` disables user font scaling entirely, including for users who rely on it for readability. The WCAG-safe value is `100%`.

**Duplicate media query blocks**

Rules for `.hideme`, `.widthFull`, `.width50percent`, `.borderRight`, `.break`, `.legal`, `.responsive-td`, `.bottom-nav`, `.photo`, `.mobileFont16` are declared identically in both the `max-width: 375px` and `max-width: 640px` blocks. Additionally a third `<style>` block repeats `.show` and `.mobile-hidden` for `max-width: 480px`. No rendering breakage, but dead CSS adds parse weight.

**`<meta>` charset declaration missing `http-equiv`**

```html
<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />
```

Should be:
```html
<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />
```

Without `http-equiv`, this declaration is non-conforming; charset may not be applied in older Outlook/WebKit renderers.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**[FAIL] Empty first-name merge token**

QA confirmed greeting renders as `Hi ,` — the subscriber name field is empty or the fallback value is blank. This is a broken experience for the affected segment. The token itself resolved (no raw `%%FirstName%%` or `{{name}}` visible in source), but the data value is null/empty with no fallback.

**Fix:** Add a fallback in the template — e.g., SFMC AMPscript:
```ampscript
%%[ IF EMPTY(FirstName) THEN SET @fname = "there" ELSE SET @fname = FirstName ENDIF ]%%
Hi %%=v(@fname)=%%,
```

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

Campaign identifier found in pixels: `MKG_US_NONPURCLICK_W_CATEGORY_NONPURCH_EN_06072026`

All 27 CTAs route through `click.emails.skechers.com` redirect chains. Because the QA prober skipped these (correct behavior for tracking URLs), UTM parameter presence on final destination URLs was not verified. Manual spot-check of 2–3 representative CTAs is recommended to confirm:

- `utm_source=email`
- `utm_medium=sfmc` (or equivalent)
- `utm_campaign=MKG_US_NONPURCLICK_W_CATEGORY_NONPURCH_EN_06072026`
- `utm_content` differentiates hero vs. product vs. footer CTAs

Without this, attribution in GA4/Adobe will fall back to direct traffic.
