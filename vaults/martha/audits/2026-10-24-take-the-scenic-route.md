# Take the scenic route
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-24**
## Executive summary

- It's the third day in a row with the same 20% member deal, and this time it's wrapped around a moody mountain shoe. I get the "Hi, Martha," my 1000 points and the $5 reward line again, so Skechers knows who I am. That's nice, but it's also the same top block I saw on 10/22 ("Your weekend just got better!") and 10/23 ("Certified fan favorites"). Nothing here is new.
- The hero is an adult hiking sneaker with two buttons, Women's and Men's Trail & Outdoor. It's pretty, but it isn't for me this weekend. I'm shopping for a 5-year-old girl and a 9-year-old boy, and there's no kid shoe above the fold. "Gifts for Kids" is buried in a maroon box halfway down. Would I click? Maybe to the sale link, because 20% off is 20% off. I'd skip the hero. I wouldn't forward it, since it's the same promo I've already seen.

## What's working

- The "Hi, Martha," 1000 points and "enough for a $5 reward, REDEEM" strip makes me feel like a known member.
- "20% OFF" in big white type on navy is the first thing I see, and "earn a $5 reward on $75+" adds value.
- "Discount auto-applied at checkout" means I don't have to hunt for a code.
- The vertical nav (Women, Men, Kids, Clothing, Sale) has big, easy tap targets on my phone.
- Klatna/Afterpay and the app download are helpful for a mom who shops on mobile and spreads out costs.

## What's weak

- The hero is an adult trail shoe and the buttons are only Women's and Men's. There's nothing for my kids, who are the whole reason I'm here.
- The deal ends 10/26 and has been the same all week. The urgency feels flat.
- "Something for everyone on your list" with Gifts for Kids is a holiday message on 10/24. It feels early, and it fights with the autumn hero.
- The fine print is tiny and gray, and it has exclusions, like no sale items. That makes me doubt "20% off" a little.
- The "Autumn Adventures" title and the hiking story are lovely, but they don't help me find light-up shoes or school sneakers.

- 1. Put a kids' product row right under the 20% banner, such as a sparkly or light-up velcro shoe for a girl and a durable lace-up for a boy. Pull it from what I've browsed before. Segment moms into a kids-first hero.
- 2. Make the "Kids" button the first hero CTA, or add "Kids' Trail & Outdoor" and "Back-to-school durable" buttons.
- 3. Change the creative between sends, since the 20% header has run three days straight. Put the deal's end date in the subject line.
- 4. Add a family bundle or a "2 kids' pairs" offer, plus a quick link to redeem my $5 reward.
- 5. Put the key exclusions, like "excludes sale items," next to the offer instead of only at the bottom.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed functional defect (an empty greeting merge token, `Hi ,`) and several tracking-pixel and markup problems: a scheme-less pixel URL, two `http://` images and missing `alt` attributes. SPF/DKIM/DMARC status could not be checked, and the truncated HTML hides the footer and unsubscribe markup.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken pixel URL (confirmed in source):** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…" width="1" height="1" />` has no scheme. Clients resolve it as a relative path, so the request goes to the wrong host or fails, and the Return Path/Validity seed-tracking pixel never fires. The automated QA did not flag this.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108404282&mi_ecmp=MKG_US_NA_U_MULTI_PROD_EN_10242026` loads over HTTP, so mail clients may block it or show a mixed-content warning.
- **Insecure content image:** `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` also uses `http://`. The same host likely serves HTTPS, so switching the scheme should fix it.
- **Click links not probed:** QA skipped 36 links on tracking and click-redirect domains (`click.emails.skechers.com`), so link health and final destinations are unverified.
- **Third-party beacons:** Four `beacon.krxd.net` beacons (`1x1_usermatch.gif` ×2, `1x1_ad_impression.gif`) and one `ink1000.com` pixel load alongside the ESP open pixel. Each adds a third-party request that can fail or be blocked.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` text:** `o.gif` and `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` have no `alt` attribute (QA-confirmed). The two `krxd` `usermatch` beacons, the `ad_impression` beacon and the `returnpath` pixel have none either. Decorative images should use `alt=""`.
- **Ineffective meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` lack `http-equiv`, so neither declares anything. Charset handling falls back to the headers.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1" />` stops pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Layout tables unmarked:** The layout tables (e.g. `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0" border="0">`) have no `role="presentation"`, so screen readers may announce them as data tables.
- **No language attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang`/`xml:lang`.
- **Hidden tracking markup:** Tracking images sit inside `display:none !important` containers. That is the usual pattern, but the `width="1" height="1"` pixels outside the hidden `div` are still rendered.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** The greeting renders as `Hi ,` because the first-name field is empty. This is a visible broken personalization with no fallback value. I can't quote the markup because the visible HTML is truncated before it.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication unverified:** QA found no `Authentication-Results` header (expected via the AgentMail relay), so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `emails.skechers.com` are unknown. This is a tooling limitation, not proof of a failure.
- **Unsubscribe and physical address not verifiable:** The truncated HTML ends before the footer. I can't confirm the unsubscribe link, postal address or `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers.
- **Hashed email to a third party:** `beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif?…partner_uid=adf27fee…189a` carries a 64-hex value that looks like a SHA-256 hash of the recipient's email, plus a subscriber ID (`partner_uid=122277199`). This is a privacy and consent item to confirm against Skechers' policy and the recipient's opt-in.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Campaign ID present in pixels:** `MKG_US_NA_U_MULTI_PROD_EN_10242026` appears in the `krxd` and `ink1000` pixels, so it is consistent across tracking vendors.
- **UTMs and landing pages unverified:** The 36 click-tracked links were skipped and no destination URLs are visible in the truncated source. UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the "scenic route / outdoor styles" theme cannot be assessed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a fallback to the first-name merge (e.g. a generic greeting) or suppress the `Hi ,` line when the field is empty. This is the highest priority.
2. Fix the Return Path pixel to `https://pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=…`.
3. Switch `ink1000.com` and `image.emails.skechers.com` assets to `https://`.
4. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels and descriptive `alt` text to `dde00662-….png` if it is content.
5. Change the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=Edge">`. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag.
6. Add `role="presentation"` to layout tables and `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
7. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and raw headers (`Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`) to cover compliance and UTM checks. Resolve the click-tracker redirects to validate final URLs.
