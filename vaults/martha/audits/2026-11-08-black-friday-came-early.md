- Sally Beauty again. I think this is about the twenty-first send since mid-September, and I'm tired of it. The black hero with "40% off" in huge cream type is hard to miss, and I get the offer in about two seconds. But it's hair dryers and a flat iron. I'm shopping for a 5-year-old girl and a 9-year-old boy, and a $40-off-ish styling tool isn't on my list this weekend.

I'd probably glance at it, appreciate the 40%, and archive it. I wouldn't forward it. I might screenshot it if I were in the market for a dryer.

## What's working

- The 40% off hero is huge and high contrast, so the offer is clear right away.
- "Early Black Friday Deals" fits the calendar, and a deal-watcher like me notices it.
- The product grid is tidy: four items, with brand names and a "Shop now" link under each.
- The Klarna strip (4 interest-free payments over $35) and the free shipping over $40 icon help when I'm budgeting.
- The gold glitter backgrounds make it look festive without hurting readability.

## What's weak

- Nothing here is for kids or for a mom of two, and there are no family bundles or promo codes.
- "Select styling tools" is vague. I can't tell which products are 40% off, and none of the dryers show a price or a sale price.
- The red banners at the top ("Beauty in a dash", "Free 2-hour delivery") compete with the hero and push the real offer lower.
- The "Shop now" links under the products are small underlined text, which is hard to tap on my phone.
- Nothing mentions rewards points, even though the footer says my Sally Beauty Rewards balance is tied to my account.
- The fine print is a wall of legal text, and the 4pm online-order cutoff is buried in it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show a sale price and a strike-through price on each product, and say which products are in the 40%.
- 2. Put rewards in the hero, for example "Members get extra points on top."
- 3. Move the two red banners below the hero so the offer is the first thing I see.
- 4. Make the product links full-width buttons.
- 5. Put the 4pm cutoff next to the CTA, not in the footer. That gives real urgency.
- 6. Send me something different from my last few emails. The Oct 17 and Oct 19 $5-off-$25 emails were basically the same offer, and this one is another tool or gift pitch after "A gift for the guys."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The truncated source shows a template-built XHTML email with several third-party ad-tech pixels. One of them has an unresolved macro (`[CACHEBUSTER]`) and another loads over `http://`. Link, unsubscribe and authentication checks couldn't be verified from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Not verifiable:** The QA run skipped 51 links as "Tracking/click-redirect domains — skipped HTTP probe". I can't confirm any destination, redirect chain or status code.
- **Unresolved macro:** `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&ao=883&aca=-11&si=-11&ci=-11&pi=-11&ad=-11&advt=-…`
  - `r=[CACHEBUSTER]` was never replaced with a value. Servers and caches can treat the URL as identical on every open, so impressions may be undercounted or deduplicated.
  - Several parameters are `-11`, which looks like a "no value" sentinel. The campaign, placement and ad IDs appear unpopulated.
- **Insecure pixel:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&dtm_cmagic=f6b5f8&dtm_fid=103&dtm_format=6&cli_promo_id…` loads over plain HTTP. Gmail's image proxy fetches it anyway, but it can be blocked or flagged in strict clients. It also leaks the request in cleartext.
- **Identifier in pixel URL:** `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/987bfe61-f108-4c0f-99ff-4b5d2e0c19ef/2332501/003aZ00000s0I5yQAE` embeds what looks like a Salesforce Contact ID (`003…` prefix) in the path. It's a per-recipient identifier exposed in a third-party request.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** Five images have no alt attribute:
  - Two with empty `src` and empty name. These may be parsing artifacts or malformed `<img>` tags, so verify them in the full source.
  - `003aZ00000s0I5yQAE`
  - `impression_pixel`
  - `UCMController`
- **Pixels need explicit empty alt:** The three tracking pixels should have `alt=""`, ideally with `width="1" height="1"` and `role="presentation"`, so screen readers don't announce URLs.
- **Charset meta tag:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, so it doesn't declare a charset.
- **IE compatibility meta tag:** `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`, so it does nothing. Both look like attributes stripped by the template or ESP.
- **Missing language attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang`, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Font fallback:** `a{font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif}` references a font with no `@font-face`. Only Open Sans is defined, so it always falls back to Helvetica.
- **Not assessable:** The HTML is truncated, so I can't evaluate table structure, Outlook conditionals or dark-mode handling.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro is the only unresolved token confirmed (see section 2).
- Nothing else was visible in the truncated source. The subject line has no tokens.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication:** `Authentication-Results` was not found. The QA note attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown rather than failing. Verify against the raw headers or a seed-inbox test.
- **Unsubscribe and physical address:** The unsubscribe link, `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and postal address aren't in the truncated source or QA output. I can't confirm or deny them.
- The `From` address `sallybeauty@em.sallybeauty.com` is on a dedicated sending subdomain, which is good practice.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 51 links are click-redirect URLs that weren't resolved, so UTM parameters and landing pages couldn't be checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the ad-server template so `[CACHEBUSTER]` is replaced at send or render time. Check why the `-11` placeholders weren't populated.
2. Switch the Dotomi pixel to `https://` or remove it.
3. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels. Fix or remove the two `<img>` tags with empty `src`.
4. Restore `http-equiv` on both meta tags and add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
5. Review whether the Salesforce Contact ID should be in a third-party pixel path. A hashed or opaque token would be safer.
6. Re-run QA with redirect resolution, using a seed inbox or a redirect-following probe. That would check link destinations, UTMs, `List-Unsubscribe` headers and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
