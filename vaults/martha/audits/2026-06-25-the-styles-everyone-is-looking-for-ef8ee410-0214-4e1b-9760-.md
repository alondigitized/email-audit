# The Styles Everyone Is Looking For
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-05**
## Executive summary

- Okay so I'm scrolling my inbox on a Wednesday afternoon, kids are doing their thing, and I see this from Skechers. My first reaction is honestly pretty flat. The subject line — "The Styles Everyone Is Looking For" — says nothing. It sounds exactly like those filler lines they use when they don't have a real offer to lead with. And then I glance at the preview text and my phone is showing me what looks like actual code — some weird bracket-and-symbol gibberish. Not a good start. I almost didn't open.
- Once I'm inside, the BOGO 50% off at the very top saves it a little. That banner is clear, fast, and it's the one thing that makes me stay. But then I start scrolling and it's all adult collections — BOBS, Arch Fit, Cozy Fit — and there's nothing for my kids. My daughter needs new sneakers before camp and my son has worn through his last pair. I'm literally in the market *right now* and this email is showing me women's lifestyle shoes. The bottom nav has a KIDS link but there are zero kids products anywhere in the email body. That's a big miss for me personally.
- I'd mark this as read, maybe click over to the women's section for myself if I'm feeling impulsive about the BOGO, but I wouldn't forward this to anyone. It's a perfectly functional email that just doesn't know who I am.

## What's working

- **BOGO banner is the first thing I see** — "Buy One, Get One 50% Off" lives at the very top, right in my face. No scrolling required.
- **"Top Searched. Most Loved." framing** is a smart social proof hook — it implies popularity without making me feel like I'm late to something.
- **Category structure is clean** — BOBS, Arch Fit, Cozy Fit each get their own section with separate Women's/Men's CTAs. Easy to navigate.
- **Arch Fit and Cozy Fit labels** tell me something useful about how the shoe actually feels, which matters when I'm buying for standing all day.
- **The email body renders cleanly** — no broken images, no overlapping text, layout holds together well on mobile.

## What's weak

- **The preview text is broken** — my inbox is showing JSON schema code, not actual copy. Whatever is supposed to appear there got swallowed by a technical error. It reads like gibberish and it actively hurt my open rate. This is the kind of thing that makes me question whether the brand has its act together.
- **Zero kids content** — the bottom nav has "KIDS" but I had to look for it. Nothing in the body is for my five-year-old or nine-year-old, and that's my whole reason for being in the Skechers ecosystem most days.
- **No personalization** — compared to the Member Month email from June 1st (which had "Hi Martha," my points balance, and a 2X reward front and center), this one treats me like a complete stranger.
- **No expiration on the BOGO** — I can't tell if this is today-only or just... always available? That kills any urgency I might have felt.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the preview text — immediately.** This is a must-fix before anything else. An email that shows JSON-LD code in the inbox preview looks broken. Write one actual sentence of copy that picks up where the subject leaves off.
- 2. **Add a kids module.** Even one row — "New for Little Feet" with a single image and a SHOP KIDS button — would make this email relevant to how I actually use Skechers.
- 3. **Put a deadline on the BOGO.** "Ends Sunday" or "This Weekend Only" doubles the urgency with zero creative cost.
- 4. **Lead subject line with the offer**, not the vibe. Something like "BOGO 50% Off — Top Styles This Season" tells me immediately why I should care.
- 5. **Pull in Plus member messaging** for subscribers like me — even a one-liner about earning points on the BOGO would make me feel seen.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Top Searched, BOGO 50% Off — Ends Soon`

## Technical Audit — Skechers "The Styles Everyone Is Looking For"

### 1. Technical Summary
One broken personalization token (empty greeting name) will render visibly in production. All image assets are served over HTTP, creating a systemic mixed-content risk across all email clients that enforce HTTPS.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **26 click-redirect links** routed through `click.emails.skechers.com` — not probed directly (redirect domain), but volume is consistent with Salesforce Marketing Cloud click tracking. No structural anomalies detected.
- **Open pixel** at `https://click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx?7GS43STWI5BE3MFKRWUCIFRYMQ.100218` — HTTPS, no issue.
- **Third-party sync pixels** present: two `beacon.krxd.net` usermatch GIFs (Salesforce DMP) and one ad-impression GIF. These are standard for retargeting but expose a hashed subscriber UID (`partner_uid=adf27fee...`) and a plaintext subscriber ID (`partner_uid=122277199`) to Krux/Salesforce. Confirm this aligns with the subscriber's consent tier.
- **ink1000.com open pixel** served over **HTTP** (`http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif`) — see Section 3.
- **Return Path pixel** (`pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif`) missing protocol — parsed as a relative URL, which will 404 in all clients.

  ```html
  <img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1dbf0...">
  ```
  Should be `https://pixel.app.returnpath.net/...`.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**HTTP images (systemic — 13 assets):** Every image hosted on `image.emails.skechers.com` uses `http://`. Gmail, Outlook.com, and Apple Mail all proxy or block non-HTTPS image loads. Affected assets include the Skechers logo, app store badges, social icons, and product imagery. This is a send-level infrastructure issue, not a per-image fix.

```
http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-...  (logo)
http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/11/49468f73-... (missing alt)
http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/11/0ec56c9f-... (App Store)
... (10 more)
```

**Missing alt text (2 assets):**
- `o.gif` (ink1000.com open pixel) — acceptable for tracking pixels.
- `49468f73-4651-4af3-bea2-61d1ae5db486.png` — product or decorative image with no alt; breaks screen readers and renders as broken icon in image-blocked clients.

**`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`** applied globally via `* {}`. This disables user font-scaling on iOS, which is an accessibility regression and violates WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text). Should be `100%`, not `none`.

**Duplicate breakpoint CSS blocks:** `@media (max-width: 375px)` and `@media (max-width: 640px)` define identical rule sets. Dead weight; no rendering bug, but increases parse time and payload.

**`maximum-scale=1` in viewport meta** prevents pinch-zoom on mobile — accessibility concern (WCAG 1.4.4).

```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**[FAIL — Broken Experience]** The greeting block contains an unresolved merge token: the name field is empty, producing output such as `"Hi ,"` with a trailing comma and no name. This is a production defect visible to all recipients whose first name field is blank or null in the subscriber list.

**Preheader text** is hardcoded (`BOGO 50% Off or Take 20% Off...`) — not personalized or dynamically populated. No token failure, but no segmentation either.

**Subscriber ID exposure:** `partner_uid=122277199` in the Krux pixel is a plaintext internal subscriber ID appended to a third-party beacon URL. Confirm this is intentional and covered by privacy policy.

---

### 5. Compliance

**CAN-SPAM:** Physical mailing address and unsubscribe mechanism should be present — not visible in the truncated source, but QA pass rate of 83% with 0 compliance warnings suggests these are present further down. Flag for manual confirmation.

**Authentication headers:** Not inspectable from HTML source alone. DMARC/DKIM/SPF status for `emails.skechers.com` should be verified at the DNS/header level on a received copy.

**Krux/DMP data sharing:** Hashed email PII (`sha25` partner UID) is transmitted to `beacon.krxd.net` on open. Ensure this is disclosed in the privacy policy and consistent with applicable consent requirements (CAN-SPAM does not require consent, but CCPA/state laws may apply depending on subscriber geography).

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

The campaign ID `MKG_US_NA_U_MULTI_ENGAGEDOPEN_EN_06052026` is embedded in both the ink1000 pixel and the Krux impression beacon, indicating Salesforce campaign tracking is active. However, with 26 click links routed through `click.emails.skechers.com` and not directly inspectable, UTM parameter presence on destination URLs cannot be confirmed from this source. Verify that `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, and `utm_campaign` are appended to all `skechers.com` landing page URLs after redirect resolution.
