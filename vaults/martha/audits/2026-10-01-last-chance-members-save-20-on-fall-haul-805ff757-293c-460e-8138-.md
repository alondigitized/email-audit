# Last chance: Members save 20% on Fall Haul
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-01**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the same Fall Haul email I got on 9/29, with "LAST CHANCE" slapped on the banner. The 20% members offer is real, and my 1000 points and "$5 reward, REDEEM" line are right at the top. I like that Skechers knows who I am. But I've now seen this promo three times this week, counting the wide-fit send on 9/30, so it reads as pressure, not a favor.
- The hero is huge, the offer is clear, and the deadline (10/1) is real. But I scroll the whole thing on my phone looking for my kids' stuff, and all I get is two adult boots, a tan women's slip-on, and a brown men's sneaker. The only kids content is a small "SHOP KIDS'" button, with the stray apostrophe, and a plain "KIDS" bar near the bottom. No velcro, no light-up soles, no pink or purple sparkle, no black or blue lace-ups for my 9-year-old.
- Would I click? Probably, because it's the last day and 20% plus a $5 reward is real money. I'd go straight to "SHOP KIDS'" and hope for the best. Forward it to a friend? No. Mark as read and move on? Honestly, yes, if I'd already seen the 9/29 one.

## What's working

- My name, "Member" status, and the 1000 points with a "$5 reward, REDEEM" line at the top make me feel like a known customer.
- "LAST CHANCE" and "Valid through 10/1" give me a believable reason to act today.
- 20% off sitewide for members is big and impossible to miss. The 15% guest tier also makes my membership feel worth it.
- "Use your $5 in rewards to make this deal even sweeter" tells me I can stack the discount with my points, which is how I like to shop.
- The Klarna/Afterpay row and the "Sign up for texts, get 20% off" block are handy for a budget-minded mom.

## What's weak

- Kids are nearly invisible. There's one small button and one text bar, with no kids' product shown anywhere.
- The imagery and best sellers are all adult fall tones, tan and brown. Nothing is made for a 5-year-old girl or a 9-year-old boy.
- It's a near-repeat of the 9/29 "Fall Haul 20% off for members starts now" email, so my inbox feels spammed.
- The 15% guest line crowds the member offer. It's two big percentages stacked, and I have to work out which one is mine. The tiny asterisks and fine print also make me wonder what's excluded.
- The "SHOP KIDS'" button is a small tap target, and the apostrophe looks like a typo.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a kids' row above the fold or right after the hero, with a girl's velcro/light-up shoe and a boy's black or blue lace-up. For me that's the single biggest lift.
- 2. Drop the guest 15% from the hero for members, and show only my 20% and my points.
- 3. Make the "SHOP KIDS" button bigger, fix the apostrophe, and put it first in the button order.
- 4. Add a real deadline, like "ends tonight at midnight", instead of just "Valid through 10/1".
- 5. Add a back-to-school or "family bundle" angle, or a promo code line, for parents.

## Technical Audit

1. **Technical Summary**
The email has two QA failures, an empty greeting ("Hi ,") and a 0-character plain-text part. It also loads a third-party pixel from a protocol-less host and two assets over plain HTTP. Of the 36 tracking links, none were probed.

2. **Link & Tracking Issues**
- **Malformed pixel URL:** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…">` has no scheme. Clients resolve it as a relative path, so it will 404 or hit the wrong origin and the Return Path/Validity seed tracking never fires.
- **Non-HTTPS tracker:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108404282&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_SITE_LOYL_EN_10012026_A` is an HTTP 1x1 pixel. It is blocked or rewritten by mixed-content-sensitive clients, so open or attribution data is lost.
- **Third-party user-match pixels:** Three `beacon.krxd.net` beacons (`1x1_usermatch.gif` ×2, `1x1_ad_impression.gif`) pass a SHA-256 hash (`partner_uid=adf27fee…`) and a subscriber ID (`partner_uid=122277199`) in the query string. The hash and ID are exposed in URLs that leave the sender's domain.
- **Unverified links:** 36 tracking links to click-redirect domains were skipped (`[WARN] 36 tracking link(s) skipped`). Their destinations are unverified, so dead or expired redirects cannot be ruled out.
- **Hidden pixels:** Several pixels use `width="0" height="0"` with `display:none`. Many clients and image proxies suppress them, so these beacons often won't fire.

3. **Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Missing alt on non-decorative images:**
  - `o.gif` (ink1000 pixel) has no `alt` attribute.
  - `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` (`http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/…`) has no `alt`. If it is content, it is invisible to screen readers and to image-blocked views. The HTML shown doesn't establish whether it is decorative.
- **Mixed content:** `dde00662-…png` is served over `http://` from `image.emails.skechers.com`. It may be blocked or show a mixed-content warning.
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv`, so they are ineffective. The charset declaration is not honored through this tag.
- **Layout:** The outer table uses a fixed `width="640" style="width:640px"` with `.widthFull` responsive overrides. The viewport meta has `maximum-scale=1`, which blocks pinch-zoom. This is an accessibility concern (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Truncated source:** The HTML is truncated, so the rest of the body could not be audited.

4. **Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** The greeting renders as "Hi ," because the name field is empty. There is no fallback value, so the merge field resolves to blank for this recipient.
- No unresolved raw tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source.

5. **Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **[FAIL] Plain-text fallback missing:** The text part is 0 characters, so there is no `text/plain` alternative. This is a deliverability risk and an accessibility gap, and some spam filters penalize HTML-only mail.
- **Unverified in the data given:** The truncated HTML does not show the footer unsubscribe link or the physical postal address. `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF, DKIM and DMARC results were not provided. These can't be assessed and are not flagged as failures.
- **From domain:** `no-reply@emails.skechers.com` is a no-reply sender. Replies are discarded.

6. **Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- **Campaign ID:** `PRO_US_NA_U_SITE_LOYL_EN_10012026_A` appears in the `krxd` and `ink1000` pixels, so campaign naming is consistent across trackers.
- **Click targets not validated:** The 36 skipped links go through `click.emails.skechers.com`, so UTM params and landing-page alignment could not be confirmed.
- **Preheader mismatch:** The preheader reads "Guests save 15% before the offer ends", while the subject says "Members save 20%". The two differ in audience and discount. This is a content-source mismatch rather than a copy review. It may signal a stale or wrong preheader variable.

7. **Recommendations**
1. Fix the greeting by adding a merge fallback, or drop the name when the field is empty. This is the highest-impact failure.
2. Generate and attach a `text/plain` part that mirrors the HTML content.
3. Correct the Return Path pixel to `https://pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=…`.
4. Move `ink1000.com` and `image.emails.skechers.com` assets to HTTPS.
5. Add `alt` to `dde00662-…png` (descriptive text, or `alt=""` if decorative) and `alt=""` to tracking pixels.
6. Replace the `<meta content=…>` tags with `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
7. Check the preheader variable against the subject's offer.
8. Run a redirect probe on a sample of the 36 click-tracked links and check `List-Unsubscribe`, SPF, DKIM and DMARC headers on the raw message.
