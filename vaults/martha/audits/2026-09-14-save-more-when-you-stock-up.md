# Save More When You Stock Up 🎉
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-14**
## Executive summary

- It's Sally Beauty again — the "Appiversary" thing that's now been going for two full weeks in my inbox, still going strong apparently. Zero kids' shoes, zero relevance to me — this is developer/professional-grade hair color kit, scalp developer, and a "bondbar" thing I don't even know what it is. I'm not a hairstylist and I don't color my own hair, so the whole email is just noise.
- The layout itself is actually clean and the tiered offer ($10/$20/$30 off) is at least specific, but it's buried behind an app-only wall — "Shop in app" — which is extra friction for someone standing in a Target parking lot on her phone for five minutes between kid pickups.
- This is a mark-as-read, not a click, not a forward. My friends aren't colorists either.

## What's working

- The tiered savings banner ($10 off $40 / $20 off $80 / $30 off $100) is genuinely clear and specific, better than vague "% off" language.
- Clean layout, no broken images or overlapping text — renders fine on mobile.
- The end date (September 21) gives real urgency instead of vague "limited time."
- Klarna financing callout is a nice trust/utility touch even if it's irrelevant to me personally.

## What's weak

- Every single product shown — scalp developer, color sealer, bondbar kit — is professional hair-color gear, not anything a mom buying kids' sneakers would ever touch.
- The primary offer requires the app to redeem ("Shop in app," "Download the app") — that's friction on top of irrelevance.
- I've now gotten a version of this "Appiversary" email basically every few days since September 1 — same red/pink template, same gimmick, just reshuffled products. It's fatigue at this point, not excitement.
- Nothing here signals they know anything about me as a subscriber — no personalization, no "here's what's trending for families," just a blanket hair-color push.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment me out of hair-color/developer sends entirely — if Sally Beauty even carries kids' or family products, lead with those; if not, this brand just isn't a fit for my inbox and they should stop wasting sends on me.
- 2. Drop the app-only redemption requirement for the tiered discount, or at least offer an in-browser code too — app-gating loses people in a hurry.
- 3. Vary the creative — six-plus near-identical "Appiversary" sends in two weeks reads as spam behavior even when the content changes.
- 4. Lead with an actual expiring markdown price, not a spend-threshold tier, if trying to re-engage a lapsed/irrelevant subscriber.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your biggest Appiversary savings yet — ends 9/21`
- **Subject Alt B:** `$10-$30 off, no app required`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Tiered savings on your Sally Beauty favorites, in-store or online.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stock up before September 21 and save up to $30.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

Email renders via a legacy XHTML Transitional template (Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style, sent from `em.sallybeauty.com`) with standard responsive/mobile CSS; automated QA reports a 100% pass rate but surfaces several WARN-level issues around tracking pixels, missing alt text, and one non-HTTPS image request.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- 60 tracking/click-redirect links were present but skipped by the automated HTTP probe (expected behavior for redirect-domain links, not independently verified as functional).
- Multiple third-party tracking pixels are embedded: Evergage/SAP (`sallyholdings.us-4.evergage.com/.../summary.png?userId=martha.st` — 4 instances), a `mon-trk.com` pixel, an IQ/myvisualiq.net impression pixel, and a Dotomi UCM pixel (`login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController...`).
- **The Dotomi pixel uses `http://` instead of `https://`** — `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&dtm_cmagic=f6b5f8&dtm_fid=103&dtm_format=6&cli_promo_id`. Non-HTTPS resources are commonly blocked by mail clients (mixed-content) and may fail to load, reducing tracking accuracy.
- Evergage tracking pixel URLs contain `userId=martha.st` in cleartext query string — a PII exposure risk if these URLs are logged by intermediate proxies or shared.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- Legacy `XHTML 1.0 Transitional` doctype with `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` and standard mobile breakpoint (`max-width: 499px`) — consistent with typical ESP boilerplate, no structural issues found.
- **8 images are missing `alt` text**, including the 4 Evergage summary pixels, the `mon-trk.com` pixel, the `myvisualiq.net` impression pixel, the Dotomi UCM pixel, and one image with an entirely empty `src` attribute (`src=""`) and no alt — the empty-src image will trigger a broken/placeholder request in some clients.
- Tracking pixels lacking alt text is low-impact (they're 1x1 invisible pixels), but the empty-`src` image should be removed or fixed, as blank `src` attributes can cause some browsers to re-request the current page.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `%%FirstName%%`, `{{token}}`) found in the visible HTML.
- The `userId=martha.st` value embedded in the Evergage pixel URLs appears to be a resolved personalization token — confirms personalization is functioning, but see PII note in Section 2.

- No SPF/DKIM/DMARC header data was included in the provided source, so authentication cannot be assessed from this material.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

## 7. Recommendations

1. Change the Dotomi pixel to `https://` to prevent mixed-content blocking in mail clients.
2. Remove the image with empty `src=""`, or supply a valid source.
3. Add `alt=""` (empty, decorative) to all tracking pixels to satisfy accessibility linters without adding visible text.
4. Confirm the CAN-SPAM footer (unsubscribe link + physical address) is present in the full template — not visible in this truncated excerpt.
5. Manually spot-check a sample of the 60 skipped tracking links to confirm they resolve to the correct UTM-tagged landing pages.
6. Review whether `userId` needs to be passed in cleartext on Evergage pixel URLs, or if it should be hashed/tokenized.
