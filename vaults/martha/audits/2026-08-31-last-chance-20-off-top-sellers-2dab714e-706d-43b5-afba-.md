# Last chance: 20% off top sellers
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-31**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this one actually caught my eye a little more than "Your favorites, now in wide fit" did — 20% off with the same UPGRADE code, but this time it's "LAST CHANCE" and there's an actual deadline (8/31, which is today). And this time I see a "Kids' Cozy Fit" tile right there in the grid, so my kids aren't totally invisible in this send. Still, it's the same code I've now seen at least three times this week (8/26, 8/27, 8/30), so "last chance" feels a little like crying wolf.
- The layout itself is clean and scannable — big hero, clear 20% off banner, a shop now button, then organized by product line with actual tap-able tiles for Women/Men/Kids/Wide. That's more useful than the vague "Shop By Category" mess from a couple days ago. But it's still adult-shoe-first with kids buried as one tile of four under each style, not its own section, and there's zero mention of my daughter's light-up sparkly shoes or my son's durable lace-ups — just "Kids' Cozy Fit" and "Kids' Glide Step," which are grown-up comfort shoes shrunk down, not what I'm actually shopping for on a Saturday.
- I'd probably scroll through this one instead of instantly closing it, because the urgency plus the loyalty $5 reward reminder is doing its job. But I'm not clicking today — I'd just note the code and come back later if I actually need shoes for me, which, let's be honest, is rare.

## What's working

- "LAST CHANCE" banner up top with a real deadline (8/31) gives me an actual reason to act instead of shelving it.
- 20% off + CODE: UPGRADE is front and center, no digging for the offer.
- Kids' Cozy Fit and Kids' Glide Step tiles exist, so I'm not completely ignored this time.
- Skechers Plus $5 reward on $75+ is called out clearly in its own banner — I know I'm a member and I like seeing that.
- Shop Now, Pay Later (Afterpay/Klarna) footer is a nice touch for a two-kid budget.

## What's weak

- The hero models are men's/adult sneakers — no kids product shown until I scroll way down.
- "Kids' Cozy Fit" and "Kids' Glide Step" are just adult comfort styles in small sizes — nothing about velcro, light-up soles, or the pink/sparkle stuff my daughter actually wants, or durable lace-ups for my son.
- Same UPGRADE code I've seen repeatedly this week — the urgency of "last chance" feels undercut when I know it'll probably reappear.
- "Exclusions apply... see below for details" right under the CTA is a little bit of a buzzkill before I've even clicked.
- No family bundle or multi-kid promo mentioned anywhere, which is exactly the kind of thing that would make me actually buy for both kids in one trip.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a kids-specific hero image and callout — even a small "Kids get 20% off too" banner near the top would pull me in immediately instead of me scrolling past adult shoes first.
- 2. Split "Kids" into its own section with actual kid-relevant descriptors (light-up, velcro, sparkle for girls; durable lace-up for boys) instead of folding it into the adult product grid.
- 3. Add a genuine family bundle offer — "buy 2 pairs for the kids, save extra" — since that's literally how I shop.
- 4. Vary the urgency language between sends; three "last chance"-style pushes in a week trains me to ignore the deadline.
- 5. Move "Exclusions apply" fine print below the fold, not directly under the CTA button.
