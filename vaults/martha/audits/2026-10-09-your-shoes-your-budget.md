## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has a confirmed broken personalization slot (`Hi ,`) and several tracking-pixel defects. One pixel has no protocol, two images load over `http://`, and the images lack alt text. The HTML supplied is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe block and most links could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken pixel URL:** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…">` has no `https://` or `//` prefix. Clients resolve it as a relative path against the message or host, so the request fails. This pixel never fires, which likely breaks Return Path/Validity inbox-placement measurement for this send.
- **Non-HTTPS pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108404282&mi_ecmp=MKG_US_NA_U_PRICE_SPEND_EN_10092026` is plain HTTP. QA flagged it, and Gmail's image proxy or Apple Mail Privacy Protection may block or rewrite it.
- **Non-HTTPS content image:** `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`. The domain is the sender's own, so it probably supports HTTPS. Mixed content risks the image being blocked.
- **Unverified links:** QA skipped 31 tracking links (click-redirect domains), so none of their destinations were checked. Link health is unverified, not confirmed broken.
- **Third-party beacons:** Four `beacon.krxd.net` pixels sit in a `display:none` div. Two are `usermatch` calls passing `partner_uid=adf27fee…189a` (a SHA-256 value) and `partner_uid=122277199` (a subscriber ID). They are consistent with the `MKG_US_NA_U_PRICE_SPEND_EN_10092026` campaign ID, so this is not an error. The point is that subscriber identifiers go to a third-party DMP on open.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `dde00662-…png` and `o.gif` have no `alt` (QA-confirmed). The krxd beacons and the returnpath pixel also lack `alt`. The decorative ones should use `alt=""`.
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv`, so both are inert.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Layout tables:** The visible tables have no `role="presentation"`. The `<html>` tag has no `lang` attribute.
- **Hidden pixels:** `display:none !important` is applied on a wrapper div, which some clients strip. The `<td>` and `<div>` wrappers are also hidden. This is low risk but can render stray whitespace.
- The 1×1 pixel table (`<table width="1" height="1">`) is not hidden, so it may leave a visible gap in some clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** The greeting renders as `Hi ,` with no name. The merge field resolved to empty and no fallback is defined. This is visible to the recipient.
- No other raw tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) were seen in the supplied portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unverifiable:** The unsubscribe link, physical postal address and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are not in the truncated source or the QA data.
- **Authentication:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC results were not supplied.
- **Sender address:** The From address is `no-reply@emails.skechers.com`. It is a legitimate subdomain, but a no-reply sender blocks replies.
- **Privacy:** The hashed identifier and subscriber ID sent to krxd.net should be covered by the sender's privacy policy.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Cannot assess:** None of the 31 tracking links were resolved or inspected, so UTM parameters and landing alignment cannot be confirmed.
- The campaign ID `MKG_US_NA_U_PRICE_SPEND_EN_10092026` is consistent across krxd and ink1000. The preheader is "The comfort you crave at a price you love".

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the greeting.** Add a merge-field fallback (e.g. `Hi there,`) or suppress the greeting when the name is empty.
2. **Repair the Return Path pixel.** Change it to `https://pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=…`.
3. **Move images to HTTPS.** Switch `ink1000.com/…/o.gif` and `image.emails.skechers.com/…/dde00662-….png` to `https://`.
4. **Add alt text.** Use descriptive `alt` on `dde00662-….png` and `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
5. **Clean up the head.** Use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" …>` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`. Remove `maximum-scale=1`, add `lang` to `<html>`, and add `role="presentation"` to layout tables.
6. **Re-run QA on the full HTML.** Include the footer and resolved redirect chains, and check the unsubscribe link, `List-Unsubscribe` header, SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment and UTM parameters.
