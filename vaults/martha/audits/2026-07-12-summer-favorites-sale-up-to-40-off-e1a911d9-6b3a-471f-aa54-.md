## Technical Audit — Skechers "Summer Favorites Sale Up to 40% Off"

### 1. Technical Summary

The email has a critical mixed-content problem: the majority of image assets are served over HTTP and will be silently blocked by Gmail, Outlook.com, and Apple Mail in HTTPS-enforced environments. A secondary bug renders the Return Path seed pixel non-functional due to a missing URL protocol.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Return Path pixel — missing protocol (broken)**
```
src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1dbf0dcef79cbad82c12fe25342701e4499"
```
No `https://` prefix. Browsers/email clients interpret this as a relative path, resolving to a URL on the email's own domain. The deliverability seed pixel will never fire.

**Ink1000 engagement beacon — HTTP**
```
src="http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108404282&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_SITESELECT_FULL_EN_07122026_A"
```
Plain HTTP. Mixed-content blocking will suppress this in any HTTPS email client context; also flagged as missing `alt=""`.

**26 click-redirect links skipped by QA** — UTM parameter presence on destination URLs could not be verified (see §6).

**Krux DMP / user-match pixels** — All three fire over HTTPS; no issue.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`<meta>` tags missing `http-equiv` attribute**
```html
<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />          <!-- should be http-equiv="Content-Type" -->
<meta content="IE=Edge" />                            <!-- should be http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" -->
```
Without `http-equiv`, both tags are inert. Charset declaration will be ignored; IE compatibility hint will not apply.

**`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` on the `*` selector**
This prevents iOS Mail from respecting the user's text-size accessibility setting. Standard practice is `-webkit-text-size-adjust: 100%`, not `none`.

**Missing alt text on content image**
```
49468f73-4651-4af3-bae2-61d1ae5db486.png (http://image.emails.skechers.com/...)
```
This appears to be a product or hero image (not a tracking pixel) with no `alt` attribute. Breaks screen readers and degrades image-off rendering.

**Redundant/conflicting CSS blocks**
`.show` class is defined across three separate `<style>` blocks with identical rules at `@max-width: 480px`, `640px`, and `680px`. The duplicate at 480px in the second `<style>` block is entirely redundant with the 640px rule in the first block. Not a hard break, but adds parse weight.

**All `http://image.emails.skechers.com` assets** (10+ images including the Skechers wordmark, App Store/Google Play badges, Curbside Pickup, AfterPay, Find A Store, and all social icons) are served over HTTP. Modern email clients that enforce HTTPS mixed-content rules will block them. The email will render with broken images in the header and footer for a significant share of recipients.
