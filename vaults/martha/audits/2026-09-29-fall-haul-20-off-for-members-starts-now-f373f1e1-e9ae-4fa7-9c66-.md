# Fall Haul 20% off for members starts now
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- Okay, my first thought is that at least it's a real deal. 20% off sitewide for members, and I've got 1000 points sitting right at the top with a "$5 reward, REDEEM" line. That's the loyalty stacking I like. It beats last week's survey email, and it isn't the same recycled EXTRA20 code from 9/14. But then I scrolled, and it's the usual problem. The hero is two boots, one for a woman and one for a man. The Best Sellers shoe is a suede slip-on and the New Arrivals shoe is a brown men's-looking sneaker. There's no light-up sole, no velcro, no sparkle, and no blue or black lace-ups for my 9-year-old.
- There is a "SHOP KIDS'" button in the hero grid, so I'm not totally shut out. I'd tap it on my phone because of the 20%. But I'd have to do the work of finding kids' stuff, which is the same complaint I had on 9/17 and 9/27. I'd click, and I might screenshot the deal for my sister. I wouldn't forward it as-is, because nothing in it says "for your kids."

## What's working

- The 20% for members is huge and impossible to miss. It's also honest about the 15% guest tier, so I know signing in matters.
- "Hi, Martha" with "1000 points" and "enough for a $5 reward! REDEEM" makes me feel like a known member and gives me something to do right away.
- "Valid through 10/1" gives me a real deadline. I'll buy this weekend.
- Four buttons sit right under the offer: Women's, Men's, Kids', Wide. Big, high-contrast, easy to tap with a thumb.
- The fall look is cohesive. Leaves, warm colors and clean type make it feel seasonal, not spammy.

## What's weak

- Zero kids' imagery. No girls' velcro or light-up shoes, no boys' lace-ups. Every shoe shown is adult.
- "SHOP KIDS'" has a stray apostrophe. It's small, but it looks sloppy.
- The offer is confusing. The 20% is huge but the guest 15% is nearly as big, so it takes a second to work out which one is mine. Then the fine print says it excludes sale styles, select accessories and more. That makes "sitewide" feel a little less honest.
- Best Sellers and New Arrivals each get one shoe and a button. Neither shows a price or sizes, so I can't tell whether either is worth clicking.
- The footer text is tiny, and I'd have to zoom to read the exclusions.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a kids' row right under the hero: a girl's pink velcro light-up and a boy's black lace-up, each with "20% off for members" on it. That would lift this email the most.
- 2. Swap "SHOP KIDS'" for "Shop Kids' Shoes" and put it first, or at least make it stand out from the other buttons.
- 3. Make the member offer the hero and shrink the 15% guest line to a small note. Have the guest line say "Not a member? Join free and get 20%."
- 4. Add a family bundle or a "stack your $5 reward" line so the 1000 points feel useful.
- 5. Add prices and star ratings to Best Sellers, plus a kids' best seller.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Members: 20% off through 10/1 (kids' shoes too)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Martha, your 20% member savings ends 10/1`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus use your 1000 points for a $5 reward on back-to-school and fall shoes.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sitewide for members, ends Wednesday. Sneakers for the whole family.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "Fall Haul 20% off for members starts now"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed broken-experience defect, an empty greeting ("Hi ,"). It also has a malformed tracking pixel URL, 11 `http://` image sources, and missing document-level accessibility and encoding declarations. The HTML source was truncated, so the unsubscribe footer, CTA hrefs, and UTM parameters could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Protocol-less pixel URL (broken):** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…">` has no scheme. Mail clients resolve it as a relative path, so the request never reaches the Return Path/Validity domain. That pixel is not firing and seed and deliverability tracking is lost.
- **Unprobed links:** 37 tracking links were skipped by QA as click-redirect domains. Destination validity is unverified.
- **Third-party beacons:** Four beacons (`beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif` ×2, `1x1_ad_impression.gif`, and `ink1000.com/…/o.gif`) are hidden by `display:none` and 0×0 sizing. Two `usermatch` calls send a SHA-256 hashed email (`partner_uid=adf27fee…`) and the subscriber ID (`partner_uid=122277199`) to a third party. Several clients strip or block these, and privacy tools will flag them.
- **Beacon `alt` attributes:** The `krxd.net` beacons have no `alt` attribute.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):** `o.gif` (ink1000.com) and `49468f73-4651-4af3-bea2-61d1ae5db486.png`.
- **Non-HTTPS images (QA-confirmed, 11 total):**
  - `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif`
  - 10 assets on `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/…`, including the header logo, App Store, Google Play, Curbside Pickup, AfterPay, Find A Store, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube.
  - Clients that block mixed content may drop these, including the logo and the social and app badges.
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` both lack `http-equiv`. The charset is therefore not declared, and `®` in `<title>` could garble in some clients.
- **Zoom restrictions:** `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta and `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }` block user text scaling.
- **Missing `lang`:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` attribute.
- **Layout tables:** The layout tables in the visible source (e.g. `<table class="widthFull" width="640" …>`) have no `role="presentation"`.
- **Hidden-content CSS:** `.preheader` has `display:none !important` set twice (inline and in a `<style>` in `<body>`). This is redundant but harmless.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** The greeting renders as "Hi ," because the first-name field is blank. The fallback or default value is missing or not applied.
- **Likely segment mismatch:** The preheader reads "Guests save 15% off their purchase", while the subject says "20% off for members". This suggests the recipient fell into a guest or no-data branch. The empty name field points the same way, but that link is an inference. Confirm the dynamic-content rules for this send.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Cannot assess:** The provided source is truncated before the footer, and no raw headers were supplied. The following could not be verified:
  - unsubscribe link
  - `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`
  - physical postal address
  - SPF, DKIM and DMARC results
- **Sender address:** The From address is `no-reply@emails.skechers.com`. Confirm a working reply or opt-out mechanism exists, since CAN-SPAM requires one.
- **Third-party data sharing:** The hashed-email `krxd.net` calls should be covered by the privacy policy and consent language.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Cannot assess:** The CTA hrefs are not in the truncated source. All 37 links were routed through tracking redirects and skipped, so landing URLs and UTM values are unconfirmed.
- **Campaign ID:** The pixel URLs carry `campaignid=PRO_US_NA_U_SITE_FULL_EN_09292026` and `mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_SITE_FULL_EN_09292026`. Confirm the click-through UTMs use the same campaign ID.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the greeting:** Add a fallback in the personalization string (e.g. `%%=IIF(Empty(@firstName),"there",@firstName)=%%` in SFMC) or drop the name when it is empty.
2. **Fix the Return Path pixel:** Change the `src` to `https://pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=…`.
3. **Move images to HTTPS:** Change all 11 image sources, and confirm `image.emails.skechers.com` serves valid TLS.
4. **Add alt text:** Add `alt=""` to decorative and tracking images. Add descriptive alt text to `49468f73…png` if it is meaningful.
5. **Fix the head:**
   - Use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
   - Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
   - Add `role="presentation"` to layout tables.
   - Remove `maximum-scale=1`, and reconsider `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`.
6. **Check the segment logic:** Resolve the members-versus-guests preheader and subject conflict.
7. **Re-run QA on the full HTML:** Include the footer and the raw headers to verify the unsubscribe link, `List-Unsubscribe`, the postal address, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, and the UTM parameters.
