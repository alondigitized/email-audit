# Selling Out Fast! Pre-order OG's Player Edition Shoe Now
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-01**
## Executive summary

- Ugh. I grabbed my phone during the five minutes the kids were actually playing together quietly, and the first thing I see in my inbox preview is the same busted JSON code leak that hit me earlier TODAY from the same brand. Like, I just reviewed that "New Shoes to Finish the Fit" email this morning and it had the exact same `{ "@context": "http://schema.org/"...` garbage. Now here we go again. Two emails in the same day, same broken preview. That alone tanks my patience before I've even tapped to open.
- Inside the email? It's a very sharp-looking blue-and-orange athlete collaboration shoe. I can see it's some kind of limited "NYC Blue" Player Edition tied to a basketball player named "DG Amunity." I don't know who that is. I'm not trying to be dismissive — I just genuinely do not follow basketball and the name doesn't ring a bell for me. ## What's working

- **PRE-ORDER NOW button is crystal clear** — orange, high contrast against the email background, no ambiguity about what the tap does.
- **"SELLING OUT FAST" creates real urgency** — I believe it for a limited collab drop, and it reads at a glance.
- **The product photography is clean and high-quality** — the three-angle grid below the hero gives me a full picture of the shoe, no broken images.
- **The secondary nav (WOMEN / MEN / KIDS / SALE)** is a small but meaningful lifeline — at least KIDS is clickable if I decide to keep scrolling.
- **Layout holds up on mobile** — no overlapping text, nothing that looked janky or forced me to zoom.

## What's weak

- **That preview text is a repeated offense** — broken JSON schema code, same bug as the earlier send today. Two in one day. I'm going to start reporting these as spam if this keeps up.
- **Zero relevance to my actual shopping list** — no kids' shoes, no women's comfort styles, no price shown anywhere. I can't even compare it to my budget.
- **No price anywhere in the email** — "Pre-order" with no dollar amount attached feels like a commitment I'm being asked to make blind. Hard pass.
- **DG Amunity means nothing to me** — an athlete drop only works if I know the athlete. The body copy leans hard on his legend and I'm sitting here going "...who?"
- **This is the second Skechers email today** — cadence is getting aggressive; two different campaigns in one calendar day makes me feel like I'm being blasted, not marketed to.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the preview text bug immediately** — this is broken twice in one day. Whatever is injecting that JSON schema into the preheader field needs to be patched before the next send. It is visibly destroying open rates.
- 2. **Show the price** — pre-orders without a price are just friction. Put the MSRP in the hero so I can decide in two seconds if it's in my range.
- 3. **Add a segmentation branch** — if I've only ever clicked Kids or Women links, don't make a men's collab drop my primary hero. Relegate it to a secondary module and lead with something relevant to my history.
- 4. **Introduce the athlete for the uninitiated** — one line like "The shoe DG Amunity wore when he hit the game-winning tip-in at the NYC Classic" gives me just enough context to care even if I don't know him.
- 5. **Pull the subject line under 50 chars and lose the exclamation** — "Pre-Order OG's NYC Blue Player Edition" is cleaner and doesn't trip spam instincts.

## Technical Audit — Skechers "OG Player Edition Pre-order" (2026-07-01)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Email is delivered via Salesforce Marketing Cloud on `emails.skechers.com` infrastructure with Krakendata cross-device matching. The primary technical risks are pervasive HTTP (non-HTTPS) image sources across all hosted assets and a plain-text alternative that is URL-dense enough to trigger spam filters.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**HTTP open-tracking pixel (non-HTTPS)**
`http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108404282&mi_ecmp=MKG_US_NA_U_PREORDER_FULL_EN_07012026`
Mixed-content; modern clients (Gmail, Apple Mail image proxy) will block or strip this, invalidating open-rate data for a segment of the list.

**27 click links unverifiable**
All destination URLs flow through `click.emails.skechers.com` redirect. QA probing was skipped for this domain, so UTM parameter coverage on landing pages cannot be confirmed here (see §6).

**Krakendata user-match beacons**
Two `beacon.krxd.net` pixels fire on open — one SHA-256 hashed subscriber ID, one plaintext numeric ID (`partner_uid=122277199`). This is expected for DMP sync but should be reviewed against current privacy policy disclosures, particularly for California (CCPA) residents.

**Return Path / Validity inbox monitoring pixel**
`pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1dbf0dcef79cbad82c12fe25342701e4499` — standard deliverability monitoring, no issue, noted for completeness.

**Plain-text URL density**
QA finding: 2,584 of 3,555 characters in the plain-text part are URLs (73%). Exceeds the 70% threshold that Barracuda and SpamAssassin heuristics flag. Risk: elevated spam score for recipients on aggressive filters.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**HTTP image sources — all hosted assets**
Every image served from `http://image.emails.skechers.com/` is non-HTTPS (11 flagged by QA). Gmail and Outlook 2019+ proxy or block mixed-content images. Broken images confirmed for: Skechers logo, App Store badge, Google Play badge, Curbside Pickup icon, AfterPay icon, Find A Store icon, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube social icons, and at least one editorial image (`49468f73...png`).

**Missing alt text**
- `49468f73-4651-4af3-bea2-61d1ae5db486.png` — no `alt` attribute; content unknown from truncated source, but if editorial it is invisible to screen readers and broken-image fallback shows nothing.
- `o.gif` (ink1000.com pixel) — missing `alt=""`. Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` explicitly.

**`<meta>` attribute errors**
```html
<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />        <!-- missing http-equiv="Content-Type" -->
<meta content="IE=Edge" />                          <!-- missing http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" -->
```
Both tags are missing their `http-equiv` attribute. Charset may fall back to client default in strict parsers.

**`maximum-scale=1` in viewport meta**
Prevents user zoom on mobile. Fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text, AA). Should be removed.

**`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` on `*`**
Global selector suppresses iOS auto-size adjustment on all elements, not just the layout wrapper. This can make small text unreadable on narrow viewports without a user-zoom escape.

**Duplicate breakpoint blocks**
`@media only screen and (max-width: 375px)` and `@media only screen and (max-width: 640px)` contain identical rule sets. The 375px block is entirely redundant (640px subsumes it). Dead CSS increases parse overhead and template maintenance risk.

---

- Subscriber identifier is embedded in the ink1000.com and Krakendata pixels as expected for this ESP setup.

Full merge token audit requires the complete HTML.

### 5. Compliance

**CAN-SPAM / unsubscribe**
Footer is not present in the truncated source. Cannot confirm physical mailing address or one-click unsubscribe link from the provided HTML. These must be present; flag for manual verification against the full render.

**Preheader suppression**
Implemented correctly: `<div class="preheader" style="font-size:1px;display:none !important">` with backup `<style> div.preheader { display: none !important; } </style>`. No issue.

**Authentication headers**
Not derivable from HTML source. DKIM, SPF, and DMARC status must be checked against received headers from a live send. Given SFMC delivery on `emails.skechers.com`, standard SFMC SAP authentication should be in place — verify alignment with the `From:` domain (`emails.skechers.com` vs `skechers.com`).

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

Campaign ID `MKG_US_NA_U_PREORDER_FULL_EN_07012026` is correctly set in Krakendata and ink1000.com tracking pixels, indicating consistent campaign tagging at the ESP layer.

However, because all 27 click links are wrapped in `click.emails.skechers.com` redirects and QA could not probe destination URLs, UTM parameter presence on `skechers.com` landing pages is **unverified**. A manual spot-check of 2–3 CTA links (decode the redirect) is required to confirm `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, and `utm_content` are appended to landing page URLs.
