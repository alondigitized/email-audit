# 🎉 Appiversary deals end soon!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- Sally Beauty, again — I'm losing count of how many "Appiversary" emails have hit my inbox this month, and here's another one telling me it's finally ending. Honestly the 25% off sitewide code with a hard date (September 30) is the clearest offer they've sent me all campaign, so credit where it's due. But it's professional hair color developers, scalp treatments, and color sealer packettes — literally zero relevance to a mom trying to find light-up sneakers for a five-year-old and lace-ups for a nine-year-old.
- I'm not a hairstylist, I don't buy "Absolute Perfection Booster Step 1," and seeing four salon-supply products in a row makes it obvious this brand has no idea who's actually on their list. I'll skim past this one without clicking — not because the deal is bad, but because there's nothing here for me to buy.
- Would I forward it? No. Mark as read and move on — same as basically every Sally send this month.

## What's working

- The 25% off sitewide banner with code FALL25 is big, bold, and impossible to miss — clear offer, clear deadline.
- A specific end date (September 30) creates real urgency, not vague "limited time" fluff.
- Klarna's "4 interest-free payments" section is clean and easy to understand if I were actually buying something.
- Multiple fulfillment options (free shipping $40+, 2-hour delivery, buy online pick up in-store) show up clearly near the bottom.

## What's weak

- Every single featured product is professional hair color/developer gear — nothing a regular mom shopper would recognize or want.
- Zero relevance to my life at all — no kids' anything (not that I'd expect Sally to sell shoes, but there's also no everyday hair-care product I'd actually buy).
- The "Shop Your Beauty Favorites" section assumes I have purchase history in salon supplies I don't have.
- This is at least the sixth near-identical Appiversary send this month — the fatigue is real and this doesn't feel like a "last chance," it feels like the fifth "last chance."

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment product recommendations by actual purchase history — if I've never bought developer or color sealer, don't lead with it. Show me shampoo, styling tools, or nail stuff instead.
- 2. Stop the Appiversary campaign or give it a real close — six-plus emails on the same promo trains me to ignore the "ending soon" language entirely.
- 3. Add a category nav row up top so browsers like me can jump straight to something relevant instead of scrolling past salon gear.
- 4. Lead the hero with the most broadly-appealing use of the 25% code (e.g., "use it on anything, even your Sunday shampoo run") rather than assuming professional-grade interest.
