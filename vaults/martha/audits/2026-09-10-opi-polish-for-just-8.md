# OPI polish for just $8! 💅
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-10**
## Executive summary

- Sally Beauty again — same red-and-cream template I've now scrolled through half a dozen times this month, but I'll give this one credit: an $8 OPI price point with a hard end date (September 13) is at least a real, specific offer instead of another vague "favorites" sale. I don't do gel manis or salon color, but "15-minute mani, no salon required" is a genuinely good hook for a mom who doesn't have time to sit at a nail bar — that's the one line in here that actually speaks to my life.
- That said, I'm scrolling past a full grid of nail polish shades I'm never going to buy, plus a skincare upsell and an app-download pitch stacked underneath. Nothing here is for my kids, nothing here is a bundle, nothing here treats me like anything other than a generic beauty-list subscriber. I'd swipe past this one — not delete-on-sight annoyed like the Appiversary emails, but not opening my wallet either.

## What's working

- The $8 OPI price point is a real, specific number I can register right away.
- "Ends September 13" gives me an actual deadline instead of vague urgency language.
- "15-minute mani, no salon required" speaks directly to a busy-mom time constraint, even though I'm not a nail person.
- The polish grid is clean and easy to scan on mobile — colors are distinct, shop-now links are all in the same spot.
- Free 2-hour delivery and BOPIS banners up top are genuinely useful info if I were shopping for anything here.

## What's weak

- I'm not the audience for a full page of nail lacquer shades — nothing nudges this toward kids, family, or anything I'd actually buy this weekend.
- The skincare cross-sell and app-download block dilute the one clear offer with two more asks before I even get to the footer.
- It's the same brand structure I've seen a dozen times this month — different product, same red banner, same layout, same fatigue.
- No loyalty/rewards callout tied to this specific purchase (Klarna and rewards details are buried way down in the footer legal text, not near the offer).

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a bundle or multi-buy angle ("stock up for $8 each, mix and match 3 shades") since price-sensitive shoppers like me respond more to stacking value than a single-item price.
- 2. Cut the skincare block from this send entirely — one offer, one email, one CTA does better for people already skimming past too many Sally emails this month.
- 3. Pull the Rewards points detail (2x points cited in the footer legal copy) up into the hero — that's the kind of loyalty signal that would actually make me stop scrolling.
- 4. Drop or shrink the app-download pitch — it's the third time this month competing for space against the actual product offer.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Mix and match your favorite OPI shades before the deadline.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `2x Rewards points on every $8 polish, in-app only.`
