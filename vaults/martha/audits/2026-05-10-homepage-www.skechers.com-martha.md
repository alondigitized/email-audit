# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- As a mom of a 5-year-old and a 9-year-old, seeing a homepage with a mom kissing her little girl on the cheek — both in matching pink sneakers — literally stopped my scroll. This feels like Skechers knows it's Mother's Day weekend and went all in. It's a soft, warm, accessible brand vibe: not luxury, not performance-bro, just comfortable shoes for real families who actually want a deal.

## What's working

- **The hero image**: A mom and her little girl in matching pink shoes, cheek to cheek — that's my life. I feel seen immediately.
- **BOGO 50% OFF top banner**: This is the exact deal that makes me buy two pairs instead of one. Best placement on the page — first thing I see.
- **"The Mother's Day Shop" framing**: Smart. I'm coming to Skechers this weekend thinking about my family; this tells me exactly where to go.
- **25% OFF Apparel secondary banner**: Two active deals stacked = feels like a real sale event, not a token discount.
- **Full-width SHOP NOW button**: One thumb tap, no hunting — exactly what I need when I have ten minutes while the kids are occupied.
- **Seasonal timing**: Running this on Mother's Day weekend is exactly right. It would feel stale any other week; right now it feels like they read my calendar.

## What's weak

- **No kids section above the fold**: I primarily come to Skechers for my 5-year-old and 9-year-old. "Mother's Day Shop" is about me — great — but nothing points me to kids' shoes without scrolling or digging into the menu.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible**: As a price-sensitive mom I need to know upfront if my order ships free before I start loading a cart.
- **No loyalty/rewards callout**: If Skechers has a rewards program — and I think they do — this is the moment to remind me. Show me my points balance or pitch the sign-up.
- **No urgency deadline copy**: Mother's Day is literally tomorrow and there's no "Order by Sunday!" or countdown. That's a missed push.
- **Hamburger menu is the only navigation**: Shopping on my phone with limited time, I don't want to dig through menus — I want category shortcuts on screen.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Girls / Boys quick-link strip below the hero**: Two tiles right below "SHOP NOW" would let me jump to kids' shoes in one tap without touching the menu.
- **Surface a free-shipping threshold in the secondary banner**: Append it to the apparel promo strip — price-sensitive moms calculate this before adding a single item.
- **Add a deadline urgency line to the hero**: Something like "Ship by [date] for Mother's Day delivery" or "Offer ends Sunday" — the seasonality is right but the clock isn't ticking on screen.
- **Bring the loyalty hook just below the CTA button**: A single line — "Earn points on every purchase — join Skechers Plus" — costs nothing to render and resonates with repeat buyers like me.
