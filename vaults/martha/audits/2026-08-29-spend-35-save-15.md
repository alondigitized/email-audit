- The top red banner leads with a real, specific offer (15% off, pickup by 8/31) instead of burying it.
- "Get 15% off any $35+ purchase" is a big, clear, legible headline I can read at a glance.
- Klarna's "4 interest-free payments" strip is a nice practical touch for anyone actually filling a cart.

## What's weak

- The offer is gated behind an app download, which is friction I don't have patience for on a Sunday scroll — I want to click and buy, not install something.
- Nothing here speaks to me as a parent — no kids' hair accessories, no family bundle, nothing that says "we know you have a 5- and 9-year-old."
