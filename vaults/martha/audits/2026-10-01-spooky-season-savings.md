# Spooky season savings
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-01**
## Executive summary

- Sally Beauty again. My first thought was "didn't I just delete one of these?" Last night's "Last chance: These deals end today ⏰" (9/30) was supposed to be the finale of the Appiversary run. Now the next campaign starts the very next morning. The "October's treats, no tricks" hero is pretty. The woman with the red curls is fun and on-theme for Halloween. But I'm a mom shopping for a 5-year-old's velcro light-ups and a 9-year-old's lace-up sneakers. Men's beard balm, press-on lashes and a flat iron aren't for me or my kids.
- The hero is a big photo with a vague "Discover can't-miss beauty deals" line. I had to scroll past it to find any actual number. The real offers (20% off men's grooming, BOGO 50% off lashes, 35% off BaBylissPRO irons) sit down in the grid. The tiny "$5 off $25 when you sign up" rewards banner at the top is the only thing that speaks to my loyalty-program habit.
- Would I click? No. Forward it? No. Mark it read and move on. I'd only come back if I needed a hair tool, and I'd do that search myself.

## What's working

- The BOGO 50% off lashes and the 35% off flat irons are real, easy-to-read numbers. I could see the deal in two seconds.
- The alternating tile layout (text left, product right, then flipped) is clean and easy to thumb through on my phone.
- The Halloween framing ("treats, no tricks") feels current for October 1.
- The Klarna "4 interest-free payments" strip is a nice touch for a price-sensitive shopper who splits bigger purchases.
- Free shipping at $40+ and free 2-hour delivery on $35 are right there in the header and icon row.

## What's weak

- Nothing in here is for kids, and nothing is anywhere near a shoe. The whole email is men's grooming, lashes and hot tools.
- The hero takes up about a third of the email on a phone. The CTA is a generic "Shop now" under a vague headline, and no price or percent shows up until I scroll.
- The subject says "spooky season savings," but the email is just "deals all month long." There's no deadline and no code, so there's no reason to act today.
- This is the umpteenth Sally send in a few weeks, after the Appiversary run (9/10, 9/11, 9/14, 9/17, 9/28, 9/30). Every "Shop now" starts to look the same, and there's zero urgency.
- The fine print at the bottom is a wall of tiny text. Nothing in it tells me the deals stack with the rewards.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one specific offer in the hero (e.g. "BOGO 50% off lashes" or "35% off BaBylissPRO") and cut the vague "Discover can't-miss deals" copy. The first screen should show the deal, not just a face.
- 2. Segment by purchase history. If I've never bought men's grooming or lashes, don't lead with them. Show me what I've actually looked at or bought, or at least a "gifts under $25" block.
- 3. Give me a real deadline and a rewards hook, like "Members: earn 2x points through 10/7." My loyalty-minded brain would notice that. A "deals all month long" line gives me nothing to act on.
- 4. Pace the sends. After a month of Appiversary emails, one clear weekly send with a single hero beats five near-identical ones.
- 5. Make the CTA specific ("Shop BOGO lashes" rather than "Shop now") and use a bigger tap target.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses an XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with a 499px mobile breakpoint and 51 click-tracked links. The QA data shows several third-party tracking pixels with problems: an unresolved `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro, a non-HTTPS pixel, and missing alt attributes. The HTML was truncated, so the body, footer and headers could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 51 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe (click-redirect domains). Their destinations and status are unverified.
- **Unresolved macro:** the pixel `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&ao=883&...` still contains a literal `[CACHEBUSTER]`. The ESP or ad platform did not substitute it. The pixel will either fire with a static value, so it can be cached and undercount impressions, or fail.
- **Non-HTTPS pixel:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&...` loads over plain HTTP. Gmail and Apple Mail proxies may block it, and it can trigger mixed-content handling. The Dotomi/Epsilon conversion/UCM sync will be lost for those recipients.
- **Many third-party pixels:** `pxl.mon-trk.com/987bfe61-.../003aZ00000s0I5yQAE`, myvisualiq and dotomi. Each adds a request, and the opens they record are unreliable under Apple Mail Privacy Protection.
- **Empty image entry:** the QA flagged an image with an empty name and empty `src`. If this is a real `<img src="">`, it generates a request to the page or base URL in some clients.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** four images are flagged (the empty-`src` image, `003aZ00000s0I5yQAE`, `impression_pixel` and `UCMController`). The three that are tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` explicitly, plus `width="1" height="1"` and `style="display:none"` or equivalent. With no alt attribute, some screen readers announce the filename or URL.
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so they do nothing. The charset declaration is not applied by the meta tag. This is low risk only if the Content-Type header supplies `charset=utf-8`.
- **Font stack:** `a{font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif}` references a font with no `@font-face` in the visible CSS. Only Open Sans is declared, so Founders Grotesk will always fall back to Helvetica.
- **Fonts:** the Open Sans `@font-face` declarations point to `fonts.gstatic.com` v17 URLs. Most email clients ignore webfonts anyway, but Gmail and Outlook will fall back.
- **Not flagged:** the viewport meta, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` and `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` handling are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Unresolved token:** `[CACHEBUSTER]` in the myvisualiq URL is the only unrendered placeholder visible in the evidence.
- **Not assessed:** no other merge-token problems are visible, but the truncated HTML means greetings and body content were not inspected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Not verifiable:** the footer was cut off, and no raw headers (SPF/DKIM/DMARC, `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`) were provided. A physical address and unsubscribe link cannot be confirmed from the data.
- **Sender domain:** `em.sallybeauty.com` is a dedicated subdomain, which is good practice. Authentication alignment is not confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not verifiable:** all 51 tracking links were skipped, so final destinations, UTM parameters and landing page alignment with the "Spooky season savings" subject could not be checked. The QA run reports 0 landing-page failures only because it did not probe them.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Make the ad platform or ESP substitute `[CACHEBUSTER]` at send or render time, or remove the myvisualiq pixel.
2. Change the Dotomi pixel to `https://login.dotomi.com/...`. Confirm the host supports HTTPS.
3. Add `alt=""` and 1x1 dimensions to all tracking pixels. Remove the image with the empty `src`.
4. Fix the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge" />`.
5. Add Founders Grotesk to the `@font-face` rules, or remove it from the font stack.
6. Resolve the 51 skipped links by following the redirect chain with a GET request. Confirm the final URLs carry the UTM parameters and return 200.
7. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and raw headers so the footer, unsubscribe mechanism, `List-Unsubscribe` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC can be checked.
8. Fix the QA summary. It reports "100% pass, 0 issues, 0 warnings" while listing 5 warnings, so the pass-rate calculation is not counting them.
