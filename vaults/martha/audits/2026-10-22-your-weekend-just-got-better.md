- The four buttons (Women's, Men's, Boys', Girls') put kids' shopping one tap away. That's the most useful thing in the email for me.
- "Earn a $5 reward on purchases of $75+" gives me a reason to put a few things in the cart. I'm already close to that with two kids' shoes.
- "Valid online only thru 10/26" is a believable deadline, and it covers the whole weekend.

## What's weak

- There are no kids' products anywhere. Both hero shoes are adult styles, and the red women's one and the navy men's one aren't what I'm shopping for.
- "Shop Additional Styles" has a Kids link, but it's buried near the bottom after two adult product blocks.

- "New Arrivals" and "Best Sellers" don't have prices, sizes, or reviews. I can't tell if the shoes are worth it, and with no kids' sizes shown I'm not tempted.
- The deal comes right after a week of "Final hours" language, so the sale feels less special. I've seen four 2-for-$100 banners.

- 2. Show a sale price on the product blocks (e.g. "$79.99 → $63.99") so I can see what 20% actually saves.
- 3. Move the fine print into a collapsible or "see details" link, with one readable line about exclusions near the buttons.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "Your weekend just got better!"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email fails the personalization check: the greeting renders as `Hi ,`. The HTML also has a broken tracking pixel (scheme-less URL), several non-HTTPS and alt-less images, and meta tags that don't declare charset. Authentication and unsubscribe compliance could not be verified from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken pixel (confirmed in source):** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…">` has no `http(s)://` scheme. Clients resolve it as a relative path, so the request fails and the Return Path/Validity seed tracking never fires. The tag also has no `alt`.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108404282&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_SITEREWARD_FULL_EN_10222026` is plain HTTP and may be blocked or rewritten by image proxies. It is a mixed-content risk.
- **Pixel volume:** there are 6 tracking beacons: `click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx`, three `beacon.krxd.net` pixels, Return Path and ink1000. Four of these are third-party.
- **Unprobed links:** 32 click-redirect links were skipped by the QA probe. Destination status (200/404/redirect chains) is **unverified**.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):**
  - `o.gif` (the ink1000 pixel)
  - `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`
  - `cd4aa7e9-775d-439d-9828-5e9ed23fdb5a.jpg`

  The last two are content images, not pixels, so screen readers will announce filenames or nothing. The same QA warning flags `o.gif` for missing alt, but the ink1000 pixel is a tracking image, so that one is not an accessibility problem.
- **HTTP image source:** `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-….png` may be blocked, and its sibling assets are HTTPS. This one looks like an inconsistency.
- **Charset not declared:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` both lack `http-equiv`, so they do nothing. The `®` in `<title>` can mis-render in clients that don't default to UTF-8.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4). `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }` also prevents iOS text resizing.
- **Layout tables:** the visible tables (`<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0" border="0">`) have no `role="presentation"`. The HTML was truncated, so I can't assess the rest.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** the greeting renders as `Hi ,` with an empty first-name value. It has no fallback, such as a default like "Hi there," or a conditional that drops the name. The source was truncated before the greeting, so I can't cite the exact SFMC/AMPscript expression. The likely cause is a missing FirstName attribute on this seed recipient, but the production impact is unconfirmed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] Authentication-Results header absent:** the message came through the AgentMail relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `emails.skechers.com` is **unverifiable** here. This is a limitation of the test setup, not a confirmed defect.
- **Unsubscribe and physical address:** the truncated HTML doesn't include the footer, so I can't confirm the unsubscribe link, the postal address, or `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers. Nothing is flagged.
- **Third-party data sharing:** the `beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif` pixels pass `partner_uid=adf27fee…189a` (64 hex chars, consistent with a SHA-256 hash) and `partner_uid=122277199` (subscriber ID). This is a privacy-disclosure item for the sender, not a CAN-SPAM violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Campaign ID observed:** `PRO_US_NA_U_SITEREWARD_FULL_EN_10222026` appears in the krxd and ink1000 pixels. It is consistent with the 10/22/2026 send date.
- **UTMs unverifiable:** all 32 content links go through `click.emails.skechers.com` redirects, so UTM parameters and final landing URLs were not resolved or checked.
- **Preheader offer:** "20% off sitewide & earn a $5 reward on $75+" can't be checked against landing pages because the links weren't followed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a fallback to the greeting merge field, and test with a recipient that has an empty FirstName.
2. Fix the Return Path pixel to `https://pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=…`.
3. Move `ink1000.com/…/o.gif` and `image.emails.skechers.com/…/dde00662-….png` to HTTPS. Confirm ink1000 supports HTTPS before switching.
4. Add descriptive `alt` to `dde00662-….png` and `cd4aa7e9-….jpg`, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
5. Change the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
6. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta, and drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` or set it to `100%`.
7. Add `role="presentation"` to layout tables.
8. For the next QA run: capture the raw headers (Authentication-Results, List-Unsubscribe), supply the full untruncated HTML, and resolve the redirect chains to verify UTMs and landing pages.
