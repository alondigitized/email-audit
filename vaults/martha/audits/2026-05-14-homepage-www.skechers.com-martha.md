# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Now I have a clear picture of what's on screen. Writing the review as Martha.
- Okay, first glance — this looks like a solid, put-together brand that knows what it's selling right now: the Slip-ins Glide-Step. The BOGO banner hits me right away, which honestly makes me stop and pay attention because price is always on my mind. That said, everything above the fold is basically speaking to young adults having a cute summer moment by a pool — not to me, a mom whose whole reason for opening this app is to find something for my 9-year-old before he destroys another pair.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF banner** — It's the first thing I see, in a bold blue strip. That stops my thumb. For a price-sensitive shopper, leading with the deal is exactly right.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA button** — Full-width, high contrast, no ambiguity. I know exactly what to tap.
- **"Kids'" category tab** — It's right there in the tab row at the bottom of the viewport. One tap and I'm in my primary shopping lane. That's the only reason I'm not bouncing immediately.
- **"Recently Viewed" tab** — Hidden gem for someone like me who abandons carts all the time. I can pick up exactly where I left off when I got interrupted to make a snack.
- **Spring Apparel Sale banner** — Secondary promo below the nav feels additive, not cluttered. 25% off apparel is a legitimate offer.

## What's weak

- **Hero imagery is not me** — Two models who look like they're in their mid-20s, no kids, no family context. I can appreciate the product, but I feel zero "that's for me" pull.
- **"SHOP NOW" goes where exactly?** — I'm assuming it's for Slip-ins, which is fine for my own shoes, but my immediate mission is kids' shoes and this CTA doesn't tell me it'll get me there.
- **No loyalty hook visible** — I'm a rewards-program loyalist. If Skechers has a program (they do), the homepage should be whispering that at me constantly. Nothing here.
- **No trust signals in viewport** — No star ratings, no "free returns," no shipping threshold. Before I go deeper, I'd like to know what I'm agreeing to.
- **Apparel sale feels secondary** — I'm here for shoes. Pushing a Spring Apparel Sale is fine but it feels slightly off-mission for a footwear brand homepage.

## Recommendations

- **Swap or split the hero for a family/kids lane**: even a small "Shop Kids'" subcategory card alongside the Slip-ins hero would immediately signal "you're in the right place" to a parent — doesn't need to replace the whole hero, just carve out real estate.
- **Add a rewards/loyalty strip**: a single line below the nav — "You have 240 points. Redeem now →" — would make loyal members feel seen and give fence-sitters a reason to sign up.
- **Make the SHOP NOW button destination explicit**: "Shop Slip-ins" or at minimum "Shop Women's Slip-ins" so I know where it's taking me before I tap. "SHOP NOW" is vague when I have a specific mission.
- **Surface a free-shipping threshold in the promo strip**: something like "Free shipping on $75+ | BOGO 50% OFF" in the top banner does double duty — offer + friction-remover in one line.
