# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- Skechers is leading with their Hands Free Slip-ins right now, and honestly the "hands free" angle resonates with me in theory — I'm a mom of two and anything I can slip on without bending over sounds amazing. But I came here to find shoes for my 5-year-old and my 9-year-old first, and this homepage opens on two childless adults lounging by a pool. The BOGO deal stopped my scroll immediately, though, so I'm not gone yet.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF banner** — I saw it in 0.5 seconds and it immediately changed my mental math. Buying for two kids means BOGO is always relevant to me.
- **"Kids'" tab in the category strip** — It's the third tab, reachable with my thumb, no hunting required.
- **"Recently Viewed" tab** — This is exactly how I shop. I start a session, one of my kids needs something, I come back an hour later. That tab saves me.
- **Spring Apparel Sale secondary banner** — Gives me a secondary reason to browse beyond shoes; 25% off anything is worth a look.
- **Clean mobile rendering** — No layout shifts, no broken images, no weird text overlap. The page just works, which isn't a given.

## What's weak

- **Zero kids in the hero** — Man and woman at a pool. I came here for a 5-year-old and a 9-year-old. I have to mentally exit the hero before I feel like I'm in the right store.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA is doing nothing** — Shop what? For whom? This button could say "Shop Slip-ins," "Shop Kids'," anything with a destination. "SHOP NOW" lands me somewhere generic.
- **No loyalty program signal above fold** — I'm the exact shopper a rewards program is built for, and Skechers isn't reminding me I have points or that I should join to earn them.
- **No free-shipping mention** — When I'm buying two pairs of kids' shoes, the shipping threshold matters a lot. I shouldn't have to scroll to find it.
- **"Glide-Step" is jargon** — That sub-headline means nothing to me until I click through. It's not a word that earns its space.

## Recommendations

- **Add a family/kids secondary hero or split CTA** — Run an "A/B"-style hero on weekends: adult lifestyle left, kids' lifestyle right, with two tappable tiles. Parents represent a huge Skechers segment; earn that tap.
- **Replace "SHOP NOW" with "Shop Slip-ins" + "Shop Kids'"** — Two buttons, two destinations, no ambiguity. Mobile users don't want to guess where they'll land.
- **Move the free-shipping threshold into the blue promo bar** — "BOGO 50% + Free shipping over $X" fits in one line and closes the deal for price-sensitive parents buying multiples.
- **Add a one-line loyalty/rewards hook near the CTA** — Even "Earn 3x Skechers Elite points this week" below the SHOP NOW button would remind loyal customers (and recruit new ones) before they scroll.
