# Say hello to your perfect fit
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- Okay, it's the same Black Friday sale again, just in a Wide Fit costume. The red "30-50% OFF SITEWIDE" banner and my "Hi, Martha" strip with 1000 points sit at the top, so I got the deal in about a second. Then the whole email turns into "Made to Fit" and Wide Fit shoes for adults. That's not what I'm shopping for.
- I have a 5-year-old girl and a 9-year-old boy to buy for, and I scrolled the entire email without seeing one kid's shoe. No velcro, no light-ups, no sparkle, no black or blue sneakers. Kids is just one line in the menu near the bottom. I'd probably tap the red banner out of habit, since the sale is real. After a week of near-identical sends, though, I'm not clicking for a Wide Fit story.

## What's working

- The red "30-50% OFF SITEWIDE" banner is impossible to miss, and the discount is specific.
- "Hi, Martha" with my 1000 points and the $5 reward to redeem shows they know I'm a member.
- The "Plus members: extra 15% off everything, code BF2026" block is clear, and I can copy the code.
- The size grids for women and men make it quick to jump to my size on a phone.
- Klarna and Afterpay are good to see when I'm stacking holiday purchases.

## What's weak

- There's no kids' content at all, which is the reason I shop here. I got a Wide Fit theme with adult models and nothing for my 5- or 9-year-old.
- The subject, "Say hello to your perfect fit," doesn't mention a sale, but the email is half sale and half fit story. I wasn't sure which one it was.
- The "SHOP NOW" buttons are muted and hard to pick out. There are four or five similar CTAs, and they don't say which products they go to.
- The fine print is tiny, and the two overlapping offers (30-50% off plus an extra 15% for Plus members) are confusing. The 15% disclaimer says it doesn't apply to some brands. I read that as "Plus members: *not all* the time."
- It's the same Black Friday email I got on 11/23 ("Our biggest sale of the year is now live!") and 11/25 ("For every athlete on your list"). It feels like the 7th this week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by household. For me, lead with kids' school and play shoes: velcro and light-up styles for my daughter, and durable lace-ups for my son. Wide Fit can stay for the people who want it.
- 2. Add a family bundle or a clear stacked-savings line, like "30-50% off + extra 15% with BF2026 on kids' shoes."
- 3. Make CTAs specific, like "Shop kids' light-ups" or "Shop wide-fit women's," and give them stronger contrast.
- 4. Cut the cadence, or rotate the creative, so it isn't the same sale banner again.
- 5. Make the Plus code more visible at the top rather than near the bottom.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed broken-experience defect: an empty personalization slot that renders "Hi ,". The source also has a malformed tracking-pixel URL, insecure `http://` image sources, and several accessibility and markup gaps. The HTML is truncated, so body links, footer and headers could not be fully verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Scheme-less pixel URL (confirmed from source):** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…">` has no `http(s)://`. Clients resolve it as a relative path, so the request fails and the Return Path/Validity seed-tracking pixel never fires.
- **Mixed content:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108404282&mi_ecmp=MKG_US_NA_U_MULTI_WIDE_EN_11262026` is plain HTTP. QA flagged it as likely to be blocked or warned on.
- **Unverified links:** 45 tracking links on `click.emails.skechers.com` were skipped by the HTTP probe, so redirect health and destinations are unverified.
- **Third-party pixels:** Four `beacon.krxd.net` pixels (user-match with a SHA-256 hash and subscriber ID, plus an ad impression) sit alongside the first-party open pixel. Both user-match beacons carry identifiers in the query string. The hash `partner_uid=adf27fee…` is a hashed email.
- **Scope of the pixel problems:** Only the o.gif and Return Path pixels have a confirmed defect. The rest load as intended.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):**
  - `o.gif` (the ink1000 pixel, tracking only).
  - `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`. This looks like a content image, so screen readers will announce the filename or nothing. This is the one that matters. The pixel also lacks `alt=""` on the krxd and Return Path images.
- **Insecure content image:** The same `dde00662….png` is served over `http://`. This is the more serious `http://` case because it is a visible image that may not display.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resizing disabled:** `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }` prevents iOS text scaling.
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv`, so both are inert. The charset declaration has no effect.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang`, so screen readers can't pick a pronunciation.
- **Layout tables:** The visible layout tables (`<table class="widthFull" width="640">` and the pixel tables) have no `role="presentation"`.
- **Duplicate-content risk:** `.mobile-content` / `.desktop-content` toggles use `display:none` via media query. If both blocks are in the DOM, some clients without media-query support will show both. Not verifiable from the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **FAIL (QA-confirmed):** The greeting renders with an empty name field ("Hi ,"). The subscriber has no first name on file, or the fallback value is missing. There is no default such as "Hi there,". The greeting itself is past the truncation point, so I couldn't confirm the exact markup.
- No unresolved raw tokens (`%%first_name%%`, `{{…}}`) were visible in the portion of the source I had.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication:** `Authentication-Results` was not found. QA notes this is expected via the AgentMail relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. This is a limitation of the test setup, not evidence of a sender failure. `From: no-reply@emails.skechers.com` also uses a non-replyable address, which is a minor point.
- **Unsubscribe, physical address, `List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Unsubscribe-Post`:** Not verifiable. The HTML is truncated before the footer, and headers were not provided.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Campaign ID:** `MKG_US_NA_U_MULTI_WIDE_EN_11262026` appears in the ink1000 and krxd URLs, so it's consistent across vendors.
- **UTMs and destinations:** Not verifiable. Every CTA is wrapped in a `click.emails.skechers.com` redirect that was skipped, and the visible source doesn't expose final URLs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a fallback to the greeting merge field** (e.g. "Hi there,"), or suppress the greeting when the name is empty. This is the only FAIL.
2. **Fix the Return Path pixel** by prefixing `https://` to `pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?...`.
3. **Move `ink1000.com/o.gif` and `image.emails.skechers.com/.../dde00662….png` to `https://`.** Check that the hosts support TLS.
4. **Add alt text:** a descriptive `alt` on `dde00662….png`, and `alt=""` on all tracking pixels.
5. **Fix the `<head>`:** use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and `role="presentation"` to layout tables.
6. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta, and reconsider `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`.
7. **Re-run QA with the full HTML and raw headers** to verify unsubscribe, address, `List-Unsubscribe`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, and resolve the 45 redirect links to check UTMs and landing pages.
