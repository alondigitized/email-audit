# Natural momentum and pillow-like comfort with every step
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-07**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I actually paused on this one — because the email itself? Pretty solid. BOGO 50% off is front and center, the shoe looks genuinely cushiony and comfortable, and "all-day comfort" is exactly what I need chasing two kids around on a Saturday. The Glide-Step hero image is clean, the buttons are clear, and for once there's a specific product highlighted down below (the Slip-ins Glide-Step Pro) so I'm not just dumped on a generic women's page.
- But — and you knew there was a but — I barely made it to any of that, because the preview text in my inbox was, *again*, raw JSON schema code. `{ "@context": "http://schema.org/"...` This is the eighth consecutive Skechers email I've gotten with that exact garbage. At this point I've started mentally filing it under "oh, that's just what Skechers does." That is a terrible mental filing system to be in. I should be excited to open a BOGO email, not rolling my eyes at the inbox row before I even tap in.
- And the other thing? My kids don't exist in this email. KIDS shows up in the footer nav as a tiny link, but nothing in the body. I'm a mom who's primarily in this inbox *for* my kids' shoes. Show me something for my 5-year-old or my 9-year-old and I'm clicking in thirty seconds. Show me only adult Glide-Steps and I'll save it for later — which usually means never. I'd mark this read, not forward it.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF is unmissable** — it's the very first thing in the hero, big and clear. That's the right call.
- **"All-day comfort" hero copy lands for me** — as someone on her feet all day, this phrasing feels genuine, not just marketing fluff.
- **The specific product callout (Slip-ins Glide-Step Pro) is a nice touch** — a dedicated "SHOP THIS STYLE" block is more useful than a generic landing page.
- **Clean visual hierarchy** — hero → New Arrivals → Best Sellers → specific product. My eye moves through it naturally, even on a small screen.
- **No visual render bugs in the email body itself** — unlike some prior sends, the layout held together here.

## What's weak

- **JSON garbage in the preview — again, eighth time** — this has become a brand-level trust leak. I expect it now, and lowered expectations before opening is a real open-rate cost.
- **Zero kids content in the email body** — I'm a mom who shops for her kids first. KIDS lives only in the footer nav, invisible to most people scrolling on mobile.
- **Subject line is too long and too vague** — 56 characters of soft lifestyle copy with no mention of the BOGO. I'm not opening this for "natural momentum."
- **No deadline on the BOGO** — "buy one, get one 50% off" with no end date means I can do it later. I never do it later.
- **No loyalty/Skechers Plus callout** — I'm a member. If there's a member price or member-exclusive offer, that should be in the body, not buried or absent entirely.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the preheader. Now. Permanently.** Eight emails in a row with JSON as your preview text is a deliverability crisis hiding as a tech bug. Whatever is generating that schema markup needs to be removed from the email pipeline entirely before the next send.
- 2. **Move the BOGO into the subject line** — it's your strongest hook and it's completely absent from the inbox row.
- 3. **Add a kids section mid-email** — even one row with "Shop Kids' Styles" and two product images would capture me before I bounce.
- 4. **Put a deadline on the offer** — "ends Sunday" or "this week only" turns a passive save-for-later into an act-now.
- 5. **Add a Skechers Plus member callout** — even a one-liner like "Members save an extra 10%" in the hero band would make me feel seen.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Skechers "Natural Momentum" Email (2026-07-07)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is structurally functional but has a pervasive HTTP image-loading problem affecting 10+ assets, a mixed third-party beacon suite with one protocol-relative URL, and a tracking pixel served over plain HTTP. No broken links were detected, but all 26 click links were skipped in probing (redirect domains), so destination health is unverified.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Open/beacon pixels — mixed HTTP:**
- `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif` — served over plain HTTP. Modern clients (Gmail, Outlook 365, Apple Mail) proxy or block non-HTTPS images, causing this open event to be silently dropped.
- `pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif` — missing the protocol prefix entirely (`http://` or `https://`). This is a malformed URL; the browser will attempt a relative resolve and the pixel will 404.

**Krux/Salesforce DMP beacons:**
Three `beacon.krxd.net` pixels are present (user-match × 2, ad impression × 1). These are HTTPS and structurally fine, but the ad-impression pixel encodes the campaign ID `MKG_US_L30D_U_GLIDE_ENGAGEDOPEN_EN_07072026` — verify this matches the actual send date and segment before use.

**Click links (26 total):** All route through `click.emails.skechers.com` — standard SFMC redirect. Destination health unverified by the QA probe; manual spot-check of final URLs recommended.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**HTTP image sources — 10 assets blocked by client proxies:**

| Asset | Issue |
|---|---|
| `dde00662...png` (Skechers logo) | HTTP only |
| `49468f73...png` (unlabeled) | HTTP + missing alt |
| `0ec56c9f...png` (App Store badge) | HTTP only |
| `9bb508d0...png` (Google Play badge) | HTTP only |
| `00100b23...png` (Curbside Pickup icon) | HTTP only |
| `07cb60b5...png` (AfterPay logo) | HTTP only |
| `fc08601a...png` (Find A Store icon) | HTTP only |
| `b782e0aa...png` (Instagram icon) | HTTP only |
| `a5c4cae0...png` (Facebook icon) | HTTP only |
| `8c5738fc...png` (YouTube icon) | HTTP only |

All sourced from `http://image.emails.skechers.com`. Gmail has proxied external images since 2013; clients that don't proxy will either block or trigger mixed-content warnings. The logo and social icons are the highest-visibility casualties.

**Missing alt text:** Two images have no `alt` attribute — the `o.gif` tracking pixel (acceptable) and `49468f73...png` (not acceptable; unknown content without alt).

**CSS duplication:** Breakpoint rules for `max-width: 375px` and `max-width: 640px` are declared twice with identical rule sets. This is dead weight; no rendering impact but adds ~1 KB to the payload and maintenance risk.

**`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`** is set globally. This suppresses iOS auto-resize, which can make small text unreadable on older iPhones. The safer value is `100%`.

### 5. Compliance

**Preheader:** Present and hidden correctly via `display:none !important`. Content is visible in inbox preview.

**Authentication headers:** Not assessable from HTML alone — requires DKIM/SPF/DMARC header inspection on a received message. `emails.skechers.com` is a known SFMC sending domain; assume standard SFMC authentication unless bounce reports indicate otherwise.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

The QA probe skipped all 26 click links (redirect domains), so UTM parameter presence on final destination URLs is unverified. The campaign string `MKG_US_L30D_U_GLIDE_ENGAGEDOPEN_EN_07072026` is embedded in the Krux beacon — confirm this same campaign identifier (or its UTM equivalent) is appended to Skechers.com click-through URLs so attribution is consistent across DMP and analytics.
