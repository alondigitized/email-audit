# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Opening the Skechers app on a Saturday afternoon, the first thing that jumps out is that BOGO deal at the top — my brain immediately starts calculating what that means for buying shoes for both my daughter *and* my son at the same time. The brand reads as accessible, a little sporty, definitely not trying too hard. Right now it's clearly all-in on the Slip-ins Glide-Step launch, which is a product I've heard about but don't have strong feelings about yet.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF banner** — this is the first thing I see and it immediately makes me think "I can get shoes for Emma *and* Jake in one trip." That framing is perfect for my situation even if they didn't design it with me in mind.
- **"Recently Viewed" tab** — genuinely useful. I do the thing where I browse, get distracted by a kid needing a snack, and come back later. Bookmarking my session without me doing anything is a real win.
- **Kids' tab in the nav** — it's right there alongside Women and Men, one tap. I don't have to go digging through a buried mega-menu to find what I actually came for.
- **Spring Apparel 25% OFF strip** — layering a second promotion tells me this is an active sale period, not a permanent "sale" that means nothing.
- **Clean visual hierarchy** — the page doesn't throw fifteen things at me. Banner, hero, button, nav. Even scrolling tired on a weekend evening, I can parse this.

## What's weak

- **Hero completely ignores kids** — I am here for my 5-year-old and my 9-year-old first, myself maybe third. The Glide-Step hero is two adult lifestyle shots. Not one kid in frame. I have to mentally translate "maybe the kids' version exists somewhere."
- **No loyalty/rewards hook above the fold** — Skechers has a rewards program and I'm the exact kind of repeat buyer who would use it, but there's nothing about points or member perks where I can see it. I'd have to scroll to find out if I'm being rewarded for loyalty.
- **"SHOP NOW" is generic** — the CTA doesn't tell me *what* I'd be shopping. Slip-ins? The Spring Sale? Kids? It could mean anything. A button that said "Shop Kids' Slip-ins" would have me tapping faster.
- **No free-shipping info visible** — for a price-sensitive mom, shipping cost is a conversion killer. I can't see if there's a threshold or if it's free. That uncertainty makes me hesitate.
- **No social proof above the fold** — not a single review count, star rating, or "bestseller" badge on anything. I always want to see what other parents think before I buy shoes for growing feet.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "For the Whole Family" or "Shop Kids'" nudge directly in or below the hero** — even a small secondary CTA or carousel tile showing kids' Slip-ins would stop moms like me from mentally checking out before we scroll.
- **Swap "SHOP NOW" for something scoped** — during a product launch, try "Shop Slip-ins" (the hero's product) or "Shop Kids' New Arrivals" for a secondary CTA. One generic button loses the campaign momentum.
- **Surface the rewards program in the banner stack** — the blue BOGO banner is prime real estate. A second line or rotating badge that says "Earn rewards on every purchase — 3X points this week" would make a repeat buyer like me feel seen.
- **Pin the free-shipping threshold somewhere near the CTA** — even a one-line "Free shipping on orders $75+" under the SHOP NOW button removes a silent objection before I ever get to checkout.
