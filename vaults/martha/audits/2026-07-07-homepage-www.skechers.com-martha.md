# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-07-07
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-07**
## Executive summary

- Opening Skechers on my phone, the first thing I get is two promo banners stacked on top of each other — one screaming BOGO 50% OFF, another for 25% off apparel — and then a full-screen hero of two young, streetwear-dressed adults posing outside what looks like the Skechers HQ. It feels like a brand talking to Instagram-obsessed 22-year-olds, not a mom of two who showed up specifically to grab something for her kindergartner. I see deals, but I have no idea where to find kids' shoes from this screen.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF banner** — leads with real value and it's applied automatically at cart, which is exactly the no-friction deal I respond to.
- **25% OFF Apparel secondary banner** — stacking two offers on the same screen tells me there are deals to be had; keeps me from bouncing immediately.
- **Quick-category nav strip** (Slip-ins, Sandals, Arch Fit, BOBs) — at least I can orient to product types in one tap, even if kids isn't listed there.
- **Clean mobile layout** — no overlapping elements, no broken images, text is legible; the basics are solid.

## What's weak

- **Hero imagery is the wrong demographic entirely** — a 20-something guy in a beanie and baggy streetwear pants is not who I am or who I'm shopping for. This doesn't make me feel seen.
- **No kids/family entry point above the fold** — the nav strip shows Slip-ins, Sandals, Arch Fit, BOBs. Zero mention of Kids. My whole reason for being here is invisible.
- **No primary CTA button** — there's nothing to tap in the hero. The banners have "Shop" and "Details" links, but the hero is just a passive image. On mobile, I want a button.
- **No loyalty program callout** — I'm price-sensitive and I care about rewards. If Skechers has a points program, I can't see it from here.
- **"Limited Time Only" with no actual deadline** — the apparel promo says limited time but gives no end date. That's not urgency, that's just copy.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop Kids" tile or CTA directly in the hero or nav strip** — a mom landing here should be able to tap Kids in one move, not hunt through a hamburger menu.
- **Swap or A/B test the hero image** — swap the Gen Z streetwear shot for a lifestyle image that includes a parent and child, or at minimum show a product shot with a family-oriented caption.
- **Surface the loyalty program above the fold** — even a small "Earn rewards on every purchase" badge next to the logo would catch my eye and remind me to stay loyal.
- **Give the apparel promo a real deadline** — change "Limited Time Only" to something like "Ends Sunday" so it creates genuine urgency I'll act on.
