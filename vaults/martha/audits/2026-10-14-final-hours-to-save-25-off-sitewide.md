- Okay, this is the same Two Day Flash Sale from yesterday's "This deal will be gone in a flash" send. The only change is the "FINAL HOURS" banner on top. The "Hi, Martha" and "Member" bar with 1000 points, enough for a $5 reward, makes me feel known. And "This deal ends today!" is real urgency, since the fine print says 10/13.
- The problem is that nothing here is for my kids. Both hero shoes are adult styles: a burgundy quilted sneaker and a navy slip-on. - The 25% OFF is huge, orange on dark green, and it's the first thing my eye lands on.
- "This deal ends today!" plus "Valid 10/12 - 10/13 only" gives me a deadline I believe.
- "Hi, Martha," Member, and the 1000-points REDEEM line make it feel personal and tied to the rewards program I care about.
- The four Shop buttons (Women's, Men's, Girls', Boys') right under the hero let me jump straight to my kids' sections.
- Klarna and Afterpay badges help when I'm buying two pairs of school shoes at once.

## What's weak

- Both hero shoes are adult styles. Nothing shows kids' shoes, velcro, light-up, or sparkle.
- It's the same sale as yesterday's send, so "Final hours" feels like manufactured urgency on top of a week of near-identical emails.
- The fine print is tiny, and I can't tell which exclusions apply until I scroll to the bottom. "Select regular priced items" makes "sitewide" feel like a stretch.
- Girls' and Boys' are the bottom row of the shop buttons, below Women's and Men's. Kids are my main reason for shopping, so that order feels backwards.
- The points bar only tells me I can get $5, with no mention of stacking it with the 25%.

- 2. Make the buttons Girls' and Boys' first for known parents, or add a "Kids' deals" block.
- 3. Say in plain words whether the $5 points reward stacks with the 25% off.
- 4. Stop re-sending the same creative. Send a single, clearly last-call email, and give it a real change such as a time-of-day deadline, e.g. "Ends tonight at 11:59 PM PT."
- 5. Move the key exclusions up, near the button, in a single readable line.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering and personalization failure: an empty greeting slot (`Hi ,`). It also loads two images over plain `http://` and has an unresolvable relative-URL tracking pixel. The `<meta>` tags are malformed, and the viewport tag blocks pinch-zoom. QA pass rate was 86%.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 32 tracking links on `click.emails.skechers.com` were skipped by the HTTP probe, so their destinations and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Scheme-less pixel URL (broken):** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…" width="1" height="1">` has no scheme. Clients resolve it as a relative path against the message or host, so it won't load. The inbox-placement or Return Path measurement it feeds will under-report. The automated QA did not catch this one.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108404282&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_SITE_ENGAGEDL7D_EN_10132026` is plain HTTP, so it may be blocked or rewritten by proxies and clients. It also has no `alt` attribute.
- **Third-party beacons:** Three `beacon.krxd.net` pixels are hidden inside a `display:none` block. One carries `partner_uid=adf27fee67323d52536b18110294bf793c265b830082caba4b1a32664832189a`, which looks like a SHA-256 hash of an identifier (the partner name ends in `_sha25`). Hashed identifiers in third-party URLs are a privacy and disclosure item to check against the privacy policy.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Ineffective meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv`, so neither declaration takes effect. The charset then depends on the HTTP `Content-Type` header, and special characters such as `®` could garble in some clients.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, a WCAG 1.4.4 concern. `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` on `*` also stops iOS text resizing.
- **Missing alt text and HTTP images:**
  - `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` has no `alt` and loads over `http://`. It may be blocked or show a mixed-content warning.
  - The `o.gif` pixel has the same two problems (see section 2).
- **Document structure:** The visible source has no `lang` attribute on `<html>`, and the layout tables have no `role="presentation"`. The `<title>` is only "Skechers®". I only saw a truncated portion of the source, so later tables may differ.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** The greeting renders as `Hi ,`. The first-name field resolved to empty and has no fallback value. This is a confirmed broken experience. The truncated source doesn't show the exact token, so confirm the AMPscript or personalization-string variable in the full template.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Not verifiable:** The provided data has no raw headers and the footer is cut off. The following could not be checked:
  - `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`
  - SPF, DKIM and DMARC results
  - physical postal address
  - visible unsubscribe link
- **Sender:** The From address is `no-reply@emails.skechers.com`, so recipients can't reply. That is not a CAN-SPAM violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not verified:** All 32 links go through `click.emails.skechers.com` redirects that weren't probed, so landing URLs and UTM parameters are unconfirmed.
- **Campaign ID:** The campaign identifier `PRO_US_NA_U_SITE_ENGAGEDL7D_EN_10132026` appears in both the Krux and ink1000 pixels, so the tracking taxonomy is consistent. Whether it matches the UTMs on the final URLs is unknown.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a fallback to the first-name token (for example `Hi there,` or a greeting with no name) so an empty value never renders `Hi ,`.
2. Fix the Return Path pixel to `https://pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=…`.
3. Switch `ink1000.com` and `image.emails.skechers.com` assets to `https://`. Add `alt=""` to the decorative `o.gif` and descriptive `alt` text to the `.png`.
4. Correct the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and `role="presentation"` to the layout tables.
6. Re-run the QA with the full HTML and raw headers so the unsubscribe, authentication and postal address checks can be done. Resolve a sample of the redirect links to confirm landing pages and UTMs.
7. Confirm that putting a hashed identifier in the Krux `partner_uid` is covered by the privacy disclosures.
