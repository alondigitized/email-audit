# BLACK FRIDAY ALERT: 30-50% off going on now!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- Okay, it's Black Friday itself, and this is the same sale I've gotten all week. The black banner says "30%-50% OFF SITEWIDE" and I get it in about a second. The "Hi, Martha" strip with my 1000 points and the $5 reward is right at the top again. That part I like, because it tells me they know me. Compared to the 11/26 "Say hello to your perfect fit" email, this one at least has a new hook: a promo code and Doorbusters.
- Then the email loses me. The tiles are a woman in a sweater with white sneakers, a guy in boots, a woman in a jacket, and a guy in joggers. There's nothing for my 5-year-old daughter or my 9-year-old son. Kids is one small button in the nav grid. The "Extra 15% off everything, code BF2026" for Plus members is the best part. But on 11/24 they sold me that same stacking as "this never happens!", so I'm numb to it.
- Would I click? Probably yes, but only because it's the actual Black Friday day and I'd go hunting for kids' shoes myself. I wouldn't forward it. It's wallpaper.

## What's working

- The "Hi, Martha" strip with 1000 points and "REDEEM" shows they know I'm a member, and the $5 reward is usable right now.
- The black and red "30%-50% OFF SITEWIDE" hero is readable on my phone without zooming. The "valid online only thru 11/29" line gives me a real deadline.
- "Extra 15% off everything, code BF2026" is clear and easy to copy. Stacking it on the sale is real value for me.
- The Doorbusters block, "Starting at $49.99", is a bold, separate offer. Klarna and Afterpay are handy for a big haul.
- The four nav buttons (Women, Men, Kids, Wide) are big and easy to tap with a thumb.

## What's weak

- No kids' content in the hero or tiles. No velcro, light-up soles, pink or purple, or lace-up school sneakers. Kids is only a button, so I'd have to dig for what I want.
- All four tiles are adult models, and none of them is a product I'd buy. "Apparel" and "Sale styles" are vague. It's a department store flyer, not shoes for my kids.
- The $49.99 doorbuster price is meaningless without seeing a product, and "Price as marked" is unhelpful. I don't know if it's for shoes or a jacket.
- The fine print is tiny and dense, and the Plus code has exclusions. That makes me wonder if my kids' shoes even qualify.
- It's the fourth or fifth near-identical sale email this week (see 11/23, 11/25, 11/26). The urgency has stopped feeling real.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by what I've browsed. Put a kids' row near the top (velcro and light-up for girls, durable lace-ups for boys) with "Shop kids' 30-50% off" as a real product tile, not a nav button.
- 2. Show actual products in the Doorbuster block, with the price on a named shoe. Replace "Shop Doorbusters" with "Shop $49.99 shoes".
- 3. Put my points and the code together: "Martha, your $5 reward plus code BF2026 = extra savings."
- 4. Cut the number of sale emails this week, or give each one a new angle. Right now they all feel the same.
- 5. Make the exclusions shorter and put them next to the code, not at the very bottom.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Martha, kids' shoes up to 50% off + extra 15%`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day: code BF2026 stacks on 30-50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Velcro, light-up & school sneakers from $49.99. Ends 11/29.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `You have 1000 points ready for a $5 reward. Use code BF2026 too.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is delivered through Salesforce Marketing Cloud click and open tracking. QA confirmed a blank greeting merge field (`Hi ,`), and the visible source shows a scheme-less tracking pixel URL, HTTP image sources and malformed `<meta>` tags. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and CTA markup could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken pixel URL:** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…">` has no scheme or leading `//`. Clients resolve it as a relative path, so the request fails or goes to a wrong host. The Return Path/Validity deliverability pixel never fires. QA did not flag this.
- **Unprobed links:** 41 tracking links on click-redirect domains were skipped by QA. Destinations, redirect health and final URLs are unverified.
- **Pixel stack:** There are five or more beacons: `click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx`, three `beacon.krxd.net` pixels (usermatch ×2, ad_impression) and `ink1000.com/p/up/…/o.gif`. Each is another third-party request that can fail or be blocked.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Non-HTTPS images:** QA confirmed two `http://` sources that may be blocked or trigger mixed-content warnings:
  - `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?…`
  - `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`
- **Missing alt text:** `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` is flagged for missing alt. The `o.gif` flag looks like a false positive, because the source shows `alt=""` on that tag, which is valid for a decorative pixel. The two krxd usermatch pixels and the returnpath pixel have no `alt` attribute at all, but they are hidden or 1×1.
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` lack `http-equiv`. The charset declaration is therefore ineffective, and the email relies on the HTTP `Content-Type` header.
- **Missing language attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` attribute, so screen readers cannot pick a language.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom in clients that honor it.
- **Table semantics:** The visible layout tables have no `role="presentation"`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** the greeting renders as `Hi ,`, which means the first-name field resolved to empty. The send has no fallback or default value for that field.
- The `krxd` pixels carry values that look hashed (`partner_uid=adf27fee…` and `partner_uid=122277199`). No unresolved `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` tokens are visible in the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication headers (WARN):** `Authentication-Results` was not found. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, which is expected through the AgentMail relay. The From address is `no-reply@emails.skechers.com`, an aligned subdomain, but alignment cannot be verified here.
- **Unsubscribe and address:** The truncated source does not reach the footer. The visible-unsubscribe link, physical postal address and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers could not be assessed.
- **Third-party data sharing:** The `krxd.net` (Salesforce DMP) pixels pass what appear to be a hashed identifier and a subscriber ID (`partner_uid=122277199`) to a third party. This should be covered in the privacy disclosure.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Campaign ID:** The visible pixels carry `PRO_US_NA_U_BLACKFRI_FULL_EN_11272026_AM` in `mi_ecmp` and `campaignid`, so the campaign is identifiable.
- **UTMs not verifiable:** The CTA links are wrapped in click redirects that QA skipped. UTM parameters, landing pages and the "Members stack an extra 15% off" claim cannot be checked against the destination.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a fallback for the first-name field, for example `Hi there,`, or suppress the greeting when the field is empty. This is the only QA FAIL.
2. Fix the Return Path pixel: change `pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?…` to `https://pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?…`.
3. Switch `http://image.emails.skechers.com/…` and `http://www.ink1000.com/…` to HTTPS, after confirming both hosts serve valid certificates.
4. Add a descriptive `alt` to `dde00662-….png`, or `alt=""` if it is decorative. Add `alt=""` to the hidden pixels.
5. Correct the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and `role="presentation"` to layout tables.
6. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
7. Re-run QA with the full HTML, and with raw headers where possible, so unsubscribe, footer address, `List-Unsubscribe`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC and the resolved UTM parameters on the 41 tracked links can be checked.
8. Reduce the number of third-party beacons, or document them in the privacy policy.
