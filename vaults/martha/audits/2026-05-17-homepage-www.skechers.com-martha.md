# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Skechers is leading with their Slip-ins GLIDE-STEP right now — clean spring vibe, two deals stacked at the top, and a big "SHOP NOW" button that's hard to miss. As a brand it reads accessible and practical, which honestly fits my budget mindset. But the whole above-fold experience is very adult-focused, and I came here with my kids' shoe sizes already in my head.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF banner at the very top** — auto-applied at cart, no code needed. As someone mentally calculating whether I can grab shoes for both kids in one order, this is exactly the math I need to see immediately.
- **"25% OFF Apparel" secondary strip** — two live offers stacked is strong. Even if apparel isn't my priority today, it makes the site feel like it's actively running a sale.
- **"SHOP NOW" button** — full-width, high contrast, zero ambiguity. On mobile this is the right call.
- **"Kids'" tab in category nav** — directly beneath the hero. One tap and I'm in the right section. That's good information architecture for a phone-only shopper.
- **"Recently Viewed" tab** — as a returning shopper who sometimes browses and comes back later, this saves me from digging through history.

## What's weak

- **Hero imagery skips my actual shopping mission** — two young adults, no kids, no family lifestyle. I have to mentally translate "this is also for my 5-year-old" from a hero that's saying "pool party in your 20s."
- **SHOP NOW goes where?** — the CTA is below a Slip-ins adult hero, which implies it goes to adult Slip-ins. As a mom primarily shopping for kids, this CTA is already working against me.
- **No free shipping mention anywhere above fold** — I'm buying 2–3 pairs at a time (one per kid, maybe one for me). I want to know the threshold before I start browsing so I can plan my cart.
- **No trust signals above fold** — no "free returns," no review counts, no "ships in 2 days." For a mobile shopper who's skeptical of shipping times before a birthday, this gap matters.
- **Woman in the hero is a 25-year-old at a pool** — she's not me. I don't relate to this lifestyle shot at all. The Slip-ins might be perfect for school drop-off but the imagery isn't selling that.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero rotation to include a family/kids lifestyle shot** — a mom at a playground or walking her kids to school sells Skechers to me instantly. Keep the Slip-ins messaging but put a real user in a real situation I recognize.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold in the top banner** — replace or alternate the BOGO banner with "Free shipping on $X+ orders" so I know before I start adding to cart.
- **Make the SHOP NOW CTA category-aware** — below the Women/Men/Kids tabs, the CTA should change to "Shop Kids'" when Kids' is selected. Right now it's a static generic button.
- **Add a loyalty nudge** — even a one-liner like "Earn points on every purchase — join Skechers Elite" would reassure me that this is a brand worth returning to, not just a one-time transaction.
