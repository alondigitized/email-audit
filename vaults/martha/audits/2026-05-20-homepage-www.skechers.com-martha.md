# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- Okay, first impression: it's a patriotic sale push — very red, white, and blue, very Memorial Day energy. Skechers is clearly going after adults who want to buy themselves something cute for the long weekend, which... isn't me right now. I'm here for sneakers for a five-year-old who destroys shoes in three weeks and a nine-year-old who already has opinions about brands. The BOGO at the top grabbed me immediately, but I had to mentally do all the work to figure out whether it even applies to kids.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF banner** — auto-applied at cart, no code to remember, that's huge for me. I'm buying at least two pairs (one per kid), so this deal is actually useful.
- **Members-only reward strip** — "Earn a $5 Reward on $75+" with a hard deadline of 5/25 is a real nudge. Five days left, I need to act.
- **Category nav tabs** — "Slip-ins," "Sandals," "Arch Fit," "BOBs," and what looks like a "G" (Girls?) are immediately tappable. I don't have to dig into a hamburger menu to start.
- **Clean mobile render** — no janky layout, no overlapping text, loads visually coherent. My phone-browsing experience didn't start with frustration.
- **Patriotic campaign timing** — Memorial Day sale framing feels current and credible, not stale.

## What's weak

- **Zero kids presence above the fold** — my primary reason for being on this site (shoes for a 5-year-old and a 9-year-old) isn't acknowledged anywhere in this viewport. The hero is entirely adult-focused.
- **No CTA button in the hero** — "THE RED, WHITE & BLUE COLLECTION" is a campaign label, not a call to action. Where do I tap to actually shop it?
- **"G" category is cut off** — if that's "Girls," I really need to see it in full. A truncated nav tab on mobile is just bad UX.
- **No free shipping signal** — I'm spending $75+ anyway for the reward, but I have no idea if shipping is free. That's a conversion killer for someone like me who mentally calculates total cost.
- **Hero models are both adults** — a mom in sneakers chasing a toddler, or a kid in Slip-ins, would've been so much more relevant to my state of mind right now.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop Kids" banner or tile below the hero** — even a simple "Shop Boys | Shop Girls" two-tile row directly under the patriotic hero would catch me before I bounce to the hamburger menu.
- **Put a visible CTA button on the hero** — "Shop the Collection" or "Shop Now" in a contrasting color. The hero is a dead zone right now.
- **Surface the free shipping threshold** — if it exists, put it in the promo bar next to the BOGO. Something like "BOGO 50% OFF + Free shipping on orders $75+" in one line would be a one-two punch.
- **Show the full "Girls" tab** — fix the truncated nav on mobile so "G" resolves to "Girls" or "Kids." One tap to my category is the promise; half a word doesn't deliver that.
