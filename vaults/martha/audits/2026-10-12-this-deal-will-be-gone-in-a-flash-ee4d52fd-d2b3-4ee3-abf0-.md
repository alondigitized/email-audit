# This deal will be gone in a flash
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- Ugh, another one. I've gotten Skechers emails almost every day this week. The 10/11 "Last chance to save 20%" send came yesterday, and now there's a "Two Day Flash Sale" with a bigger number. The 25% OFF in giant orange letters is impossible to miss, and I like seeing "Hi, Martha," Member, and 1000 points up top. But I've seen the fall leaves and the green box all week. "Flash" and "gone" feel hollow when a new "last chance" shows up every 24 hours.
- The hero shoe is a tan embroidered women's slip-on. That's not for my 5-year-old or my 9-year-old. The Girls' and Boys' buttons are right under it, which I appreciate, but they're small tiles with no kid products and no velcro, light-up, or sturdy sneaker anywhere. The "Sitewide" claim also made me squint, because the fine print excludes sale styles, premieres, and more.
- Would I click? Probably, but only because 25% is better than the 20% from earlier this week and it ends tomorrow. I'd tap Shop Boys', check whether it's actually sitewide, and leave. I wouldn't forward it. I'd just mark it read and maybe come back tonight.

## What's working

- The 25% OFF is huge and sits right in the hero. I knew the deal in two seconds.
- "Hi, Martha" plus the Member badge and 1000 points ("enough for a $5 reward, REDEEM") makes me feel like a known customer.
- "Valid 10/12 - 10/13 only" is a real two-day window, so the urgency feels more credible than a vague "ends soon."
- Shop Women's, Men's, Girls', and Boys' sit right under the hero, so I can jump to the kids' section fast with my thumb.
- The Klarna/Afterpay row and the "Sign Up for Texts & Get 20% Off" line are handy to see, and nothing in the layout looks broken.

## What's weak

- The hero shoe is a women's slip-on. Nothing in the email shows girls' or boys' shoes, so it didn't feel like it was for my kids.
- It's the umpteenth fall-themed send this week (10/8, 10/9, 10/10, 10/11 and now 10/12). The "flash" urgency has been used up.
- "Sitewide" in the headline contradicts the fine print, which excludes sale styles, premieres, and select accessories. It feels a little bait-and-switch.
- The fine print is tiny and dense at the bottom. I'd have to zoom on my phone to read it.
- New Arrivals and Best Sellers show one shoe each, in maroon and navy adult styles. There's no kids' product, no price, and no reviews, so there's nothing to help me research.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a kids' hero or a second row.** Put a girls' sparkle or light-up velcro shoe and a boys' lace-up sneaker (blue/black, durable sole) beside the 25% offer. Segment by my past kids' browsing so I see them first.
- 2. **Make the offer honest up top.** Change "Sitewide" to "Select styles," or put the main exclusions in one line under the button, not in 6pt text at the bottom.
- 3. **Use the member perk.** Show something like "You have 1000 pts + 25% off = stack it" and a family bundle or promo code, since I'm a rewards person.
- 4. **Fix the cadence.** Combine this with the weekend sends or give me one clear "last call" instead of daily flash sales.
- 5. **Fix the preheader.** It's currently leaking link junk, so use real copy.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Martha, 25% off kids' shoes ends tomorrow`
- **Subject Alt B:** `25% off for members, 2 days only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus your 1000 points are ready to redeem. Shop girls' and boys' styles.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Members save 25% through 10/13. Velcro, light-up and school-ready sneakers inside.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed broken-experience defect: the greeting renders as "Hi ," because the name merge field is empty. There are also tracking-pixel problems: a scheme-less Return Path pixel URL, plain-HTTP image requests that carry identifiers, and several `<img>` tags with no alt attribute. The source is truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and body links could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Return Path pixel has no scheme:** `src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…"` is a relative URL. Clients will resolve it against the message or base context, so the request will most likely fail. That breaks Return Path's inbox-placement and read measurement. This tag also has no `alt` attribute.
- **Plain-HTTP tracking pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108404282&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_SITE_FULL_EN_10122026`. Proxies or clients that block mixed content may drop it. The `mi_u` value, which looks like a per-recipient ID, also goes over cleartext.
- **Third-party Krux beacons:**
  - Two `beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif` calls pass `partner_uid=adf27fee…189a` (a 64-hex, SHA-256-shaped value) and `partner_uid=122277199` (subscriber ID).
  - A third call, `1x1_ad_impression.gif`, carries the campaign ID.
  - These are HTTPS, but they are cross-domain identifier syncs to an ad-data platform. Confirm they are covered by the sender's privacy disclosures.
  - None of the three has an `alt` attribute.
- **Hidden pixel container:** the pixels sit in a `display:none` div inside a table with `font-size:0;line-height:0`. This is fine functionally, but some clients load hidden images anyway, so the beacons are not guaranteed to be silent.
- **QA alt-text flag on `o.gif` is a false positive:** the source shows `alt=""`, which is valid for a decorative or tracking image.
- **Skipped tracking links:** 31 links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, so none of them were probed. Their destinations and redirect health are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Meta tags are ineffective:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv`, so neither declaration works. Charset is then left to the HTTP/MIME headers.
- **Zoom restricted:** the viewport tag has `maximum-scale=1`, and the CSS has `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }`. Together they block pinch-zoom and text scaling on iOS, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Missing `lang`:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` attribute.
- **Layout tables lack `role="presentation"`:** the visible tables (`<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0" border="0">` and the 640px container) have no role. Screen readers may announce them as data tables.
- **Missing alt on content image:** `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`. QA reports no alt text. The tag is past the truncation point, so I could not verify the QA finding in the source.
- **Same image over HTTP:** it is served over `http://`, so it may be blocked or flagged as mixed content, which would leave a broken image area.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Skechers®</title>` doesn't describe this message. This is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** the greeting renders as "Hi ," with the name field blank. The subscriber ID (`122277199`) and the hashed identifier did populate in the pixel URLs. So the merge itself ran, and the first-name value is empty for this recipient.
- **No fallback:** the template has no default such as "Hi there," or a conditional that drops the greeting when the name is empty.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

  - The unsubscribe link, physical postal address, and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are not visible. The HTML is truncated and no raw headers were supplied.
  - SPF, DKIM and DMARC results are not provided.
- **Sender address:** the From address is `no-reply@emails.skechers.com`. No Reply-To is visible. This is not a violation, but confirm a monitored reply path exists.
- No issues can be confirmed from the available evidence.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not verifiable:** the 31 click-tracking links were skipped and the body is truncated, so no landing URLs, UTM parameters or promo-code carry-through could be checked.
- **Campaign ID is consistent:** `PRO_US_NA_U_SITE_FULL_EN_10122026` appears in both the Krux and ink1000 pixels.
- **Preheader check:** the preheader says "Members take 25% off sitewide for 2 days only". That can't be matched to a landing page without resolving the links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the empty greeting:** add a merge fallback (for example, `%%=IIF(Empty(@firstname),"Hi there","Hi "&@firstname)=%%` in SFMC syntax, or equivalent). If the name is blank, omit the greeting.
2. **Fix the Return Path pixel:** change it to `https://pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=…` and add `alt=""`.
3. **Move to HTTPS:**
   - Serve `ink1000.com/…/o.gif` and `image.emails.skechers.com/…/dde00662-….png` over HTTPS.
   - If the ink1000 endpoint doesn't support HTTPS, drop that pixel.
4. **Add `alt` to every `<img>`:** use `alt=""` on the Krux and Return Path pixels, and a descriptive alt on `dde00662-….png`.
5. **Correct the `<head>`:** use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge" />`. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
6. **Restore zoom and text scaling:** remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag, and change `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` to `100%`.
7. **Add `role="presentation"` to layout tables.**
8. **Re-run the audit on the full HTML and raw headers** to cover:
   - the unsubscribe link and postal address,
   - `List-Unsubscribe` and authentication results,
   - resolved destination URLs and UTM parameters for the 31 skipped tracking links.
