# Ends Today! BOGO 50% Off for Members
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-11**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Ends Today!" in the subject got me — I opened fast because I genuinely needed to see if the BOGO was still live. And honestly, the email delivered on that promise. The Back to School hero is front and center, the BOGO 50% off is unmissable, and there's a Kids' section right in the flow. For a mom who's been staring down a school-supply list since June, this is the right email at the right moment.
- That said, I need to be real: before I even got to that hero, my inbox preview had already let me down — again. Same JSON schema dump I've been seeing from this sender since at least July 2. I've stopped even reading the preview anymore. I open Skechers emails *despite* the preview, not because of it. Nine straight sends with broken preview text is a quality control problem that someone inside that marketing org should be embarrassed about.
- Would I click? Yes — Kids' is right there, the offer is real, and "Ends Today" means I can't procrastinate. Would I forward to a friend? Probably not; I'd just screenshot the Kids' section and text it. Mark as read after clicking? Absolutely not — this one earns a tap.

## What's working

- **Back to School urgency is perfectly timed.** July 11 is exactly when I'm in back-to-school brain mode, and "LAST CHANCE!" in the hero reinforces that without feeling fake.
- **BOGO 50% off is crystal clear in the hero.** No guessing, no fine print blocking the view — offer + fallback option (20% off single pairs) is right there.
- **Kids' section shows actual kids' shoes**, including what looks like pink/colorful styles — that's exactly what my daughter would drag me toward.
- **Skechers Plus branding in the hero** reminds me this offer is for members like me, which makes the deal feel earned rather than mass-blast.
- **Clean visual layout** — Women's, Men's, Kids' stacked in a logical order with one CTA each. No clutter, no guessing where to tap.

## What's weak

- **The preview text is JSON schema garbage. Again.** This is the ninth time I'm looking at that "[{ "@context": "http://schema.org/"..." dump in my inbox. It actively erodes trust and makes me wonder what else is broken on the backend.
- **Kids' section is one generic block** — no split between girls and boys, no callout for age ranges. My 9-year-old son needs something totally different from my 5-year-old daughter, and the single "SHOP KIDS'" CTA sends me back to work I'd rather the email do for me.
- **The Apparel section feels off-brand for this moment.** 25% off jerseys and athletic wear when I'm in back-to-school shoe mode just breaks the flow. Could've been a second email.
- **"SHOP NOW" on the hero is vague.** Shop what, exactly? The hero CTA should say "Shop Kids' BOGO" or "Shop the Sale" — something that matches my headspace.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the preview text.** This is #1 by a mile. Until someone patches that JSON bleed, every subject line is doing heavy lifting it shouldn't have to. A clean preheader like "Today only — buy one pair, get the second 50% off for members" would dramatically lift open rate.
- 2. **Split Kids' into Boys / Girls in the body.** One block for girls (pink, light-up, velcro) and one for boys (athletic, lace-up, durable). That's how I actually shop.
- 3. **Change the hero CTA from "SHOP NOW" to "Shop Kids' BOGO."** Or "Shop Back to School." Something I can act on without having to decode.
- 4. **Either cut the Apparel section or move it below the fold.** I'm a shoes-first shopper and this email has one job today.
- 5. **Surface a featured kids' shoe in the Kids' block.** Even one hero product with a name and price pulls me in faster than a category splash.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends Today: BOGO 50% Off — Shop Back to School`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Members Only: Last Chance for BOGO 50% Off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Today only — buy one pair, get the second 50% off. Kids', Women's & Men's included.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your Skechers Plus BOGO ends at midnight — grab shoes for the whole family.`

## Technical Audit — Skechers "Ends Today! BOGO 50% Off for Members"

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is built on Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) using standard table-based layout. There are two categories of confirmed infrastructure issues: all hosted image assets are served over HTTP (not HTTPS), and the Validity inbox-monitoring pixel has a malformed (protocol-relative) URL that will silently fail.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Broken Validity pixel — missing protocol**

```html
<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1dbf0dcef79cbad82c12fe25342701e4499" width="1" height="1" />
```

The `src` value has no `http://` or `https://` prefix. Email clients will treat this as a relative path, making the request to `[current-domain]/pixel.app.returnpath.net/...` — the pixel never fires. Inbox-placement monitoring data for this send is likely zeroed out.

**Third-party DMP user-match pixels (Krux/Salesforce)**

Two Krux beacons fire on open:

```
https://beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif?partner=LprH63a7_sfmc_100007688_sha25&partner_uid=adf27fee67323d52536b18110294bf793c265b830082caba4b1a32664832189a
https://beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif?partner=LprH63a7_sfmc_100007688_subid&partner_uid=122277199
```

The first passes a SHA-256-hashed email address to a third-party DMP for cross-site audience matching. This constitutes sharing hashed PII with a third party on pixel load — relevant to CCPA/GDPR disclosure requirements (see §5).

**26 click links skipped by QA probe** — all routed through `click.emails.skechers.com` (SFMC redirects). UTM parameter presence on landing URLs cannot be verified from the source alone (see §6).

Gmail and Outlook.com proxy all HTTP images through their own CDNs; Apple Mail on iOS 15+ may block them entirely. The Skechers logo (`dde00662-...png`) loading over HTTP means it is affected in every major client.

**`ink1000.com` tracking pixel also over HTTP**

```
http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108404282&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_SITEBUNDLE_FULL_EN_07112026
```

Same blocking risk, with the additional issue that this pixel has no `alt` attribute at all (empty string is acceptable; absent is not).

**`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` applied globally**

```css
* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; }
```

Setting `text-size-adjust: none` on `*` prevents iOS from scaling text up when a user has accessibility font-size preferences set. WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) applies. Should be scoped to the layout container or removed.

**Duplicate responsive CSS blocks**

The ruleset inside `@media only screen and (max-width: 375px)` is byte-for-byte identical to the `@media only screen and (max-width: 640px)` block. The 375px block is dead code — any device matching 375px also matches 640px and the rules are identical, so the 375px block never contributes. This inflates CSS payload with no effect.

**Missing `http-equiv` on charset meta**

```html
<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />
```

Should be:

```html
<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />
```

Without `http-equiv`, Outlook desktop (MIME-based rendering) does not recognize this as a charset declaration.

**Missing alt text on 2 images**

- `o.gif` (ink1000.com pixel) — `alt` attribute entirely absent
- `49468f73-4651-4af3-bae2-61d1ae5db486.png` — `alt` attribute absent

### 5. Compliance

**Krux DMP pixel — hashed PII disclosure**

The `beacon.krxd.net` user-match pixel transmits a SHA-256 hash of the recipient's email to Salesforce's DMP infrastructure on every open. Under CCPA, this constitutes sharing personal information with a third party for cross-context behavioral advertising. Depending on jurisdiction and consent capture at account creation, this may require disclosure in the privacy policy and a "Do Not Sell or Share" opt-out mechanism. This is not auditable from the HTML alone — verify that account sign-up consent covers DMP audience matching.

**CAN-SPAM footer** — HTML is truncated; cannot confirm physical address or unsubscribe link presence. QA summary reports 0 issues, suggesting these are present in the untruncated source.

**Preheader/subject mismatch**

```html
<div class="preheader">Check back to school shopping off your list early</div>
```

Subject: `Ends Today! BOGO 50% Off for Members`

The preheader copy ("back to school") does not correspond to the offer in the subject line ("BOGO 50% Off"). This is likely a content error (wrong preheader reused from a different send), not a compliance issue, but it affects inbox preview text accuracy.

UTM parameter presence on the 26 click-through links cannot be confirmed — all links route through `click.emails.skechers.com` redirects that were excluded from HTTP probing. Recommend spot-checking decoded destination URLs for `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` alignment with the campaign slug above.
