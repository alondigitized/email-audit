- Skechers Plus member framing in the subhead — feels like they know I'm a member, not a cold blast.

## What's weak

- The "kids" product shot is just another grown-up-style blue sneaker — no velcro, no light-up soles, no pink/purple/sparkle for my daughter, and nothing distinctly boy's-durable-sneaker for my son either.

- 2. Add a "buy 2 kids' pairs, extra 10% off" or bundle nudge — I'm always buying for both kids at once, give me a reason to check out bigger.
- 3. Move "Shop Kids'" up as its own hero banner instead of burying it as the third of three sneaker blocks.
