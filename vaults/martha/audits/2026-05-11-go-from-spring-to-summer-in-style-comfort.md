# Go From Spring to Summer in Style & Comfort
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-11**
## Executive summary

- So I'm on the couch Sunday morning, kids finally distracted, scrolling through my inbox. I see "Skechers" and my thumb hovers — I'm definitely subscribed and I do need new shoes for Lily before summer camp. But that preview text? It's a wall of computer code. Like, literally `@context http://schema.org` garbage. I almost swiped past it assuming it was a glitched spam email.
- Once inside, I'm glad I opened it. "Hi Martha, Hi Summer!" is cute — I notice when a brand actually knows my name. The BOGO 50% off is front and center and that's the number one thing that would make me actually buy something today rather than tab it for later. The visual is clean, the layout doesn't make me zoom in to read anything, and I can see a "SHOP KIDS'" section which matters to me more than the women's section does honestly.
- That said, the kids' section is an afterthought. One purple-ish sneaker, buried after adult styles. My son needs dark lace-up sneakers for school, my daughter wants anything pink and sparkly — I get neither of those signals here. I'd tap SHOP KIDS' anyway because of the BOGO deal, but this email didn't do its homework on why I actually open Skechers emails. I'd mark it read, not forward it.

## What's working

- **The BOGO offer is impossible to miss** — "50% OFF" in huge type in the hero, with the alternative 20% off single-pair option right below it. Two paths to savings, both clear. That's exactly the kind of deal that moves me from browser to cart.
- **Name personalization in the body** — "Hi Martha, Hi Summer!" actually made me smile. Small thing, big difference from feeling like a stranger.
- **SHOP KIDS' button exists** — at least it's there. The lavender colorway does read as girl-friendly (Lily would love it), so it's not totally off for my household.
- **Afterpay and Klarna visible** — I'm price-sensitive and I absolutely use buy-now-pay-later for bigger shoe hauls. Seeing these at a glance reduces commitment anxiety.
- **Seasonal timing is on point** — it's May, we're transitioning to summer, the offer fits the moment. Not stale.

## What's weak

- **The preview text is broken beyond repair** — JSON-LD schema markup is showing verbatim in my inbox. Before I even opened this, my brain read "spam or technical error." This single bug undercuts the whole open rate.
- **Kids section is buried and thin** — Women's first, Men's second, then one shoe for kids. I'm primarily here for the kids. One lavender sneaker doesn't cover a 9-year-old boy who needs durable dark sneakers.
- **No urgency anywhere** — BOGO is great but when does it end? I'll tell myself I'll come back later and I won't. A deadline makes me act now.
- **The apparel block is invisible to me** — 25% off athletic wear is for someone else. As a mom shopping for the kids first, this feels like the email is talking to someone slightly different from me.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the preheader immediately** — the JSON-LD leak is the single biggest priority. Every email going out right now is losing open rate to this. Replace with something like: *"BOGO 50% off — kids, women's & men's styles. This weekend only."*
- 2. **Move SHOP KIDS' up or add a dedicated kids hero row** — lead with the family angle, show a boy sneaker AND a girl sneaker side by side. Martha opens these emails for her kids. Reward that.
- 3. **Add a hard deadline to the BOGO offer** — even "Ends Sunday" or "This weekend only" would push me to buy today instead of leaving the tab open.
- 4. **Lean into the kids-first angle in the subject** — right now the subject reads like it's talking to a solo shopper.
- **Subject Alt A:** `BOGO 50% Off — Kids, Women's & Men's Styles`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Kids Need Summer Shoes. We've Got 50% Off.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Buy one, get one half off — kids' sneakers included. Ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `BOGO 50% off or 20% off single pairs — shop kids' styles before they sell out.`

### 1. Technical Summary

One confirmed broken-experience bug (empty personalization token in greeting) and a pervasive HTTP-over-HTTPS image issue affecting 13+ assets. No structural rendering failures beyond these two patterns.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Broken pixel — missing protocol**
```html
<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1dbf0dcef79cbad82c12fe25342701e4499" />
```
The Return Path/Validity inbox-monitoring pixel has no `http://` or `https://` scheme. Browsers and email clients will resolve this as a relative path — the pixel will never fire, invalidating deliverability reporting for this send.

**HTTP tracking pixel (ink1000.com)**
```
src="http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108404282&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_SITEBUNDLE_FULL_EN_05112026"
```
Plain HTTP. Gmail, Outlook 365, and Apple Mail proxy or block non-HTTPS content; this open-tracking pixel will fail silently in those clients.

**Third-party audience-sync pixels (Krux/Salesforce DMP)**
Three `beacon.krxd.net` pixels are present — one user-match (hashed PII via `partner_uid`), one subscriber ID (`partner_uid=122277199`), and one ad-impression beacon. These pass a hashed email and a plaintext subscriber ID to a third-party DMP on open. Confirm this is disclosed in Skechers' privacy policy and is consistent with current data-sharing consent posture.

**25 click-redirect links** skipped by the HTTP prober — no broken-link verdict available for destination URLs.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**HTTP image sources — 13 assets**
All `image.emails.skechers.com` assets are served over HTTP:
```
http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-...png  (Skechers logo)

```
Gmail proxies HTTP images through `googleusercontent.com`; this may work but introduces latency and watermarked URLs. Apple Mail on iOS 18+ blocks mixed-content images entirely — subscribers on that client will see broken images throughout the email.

**Missing alt text — 2 images**
- `o.gif` (ink1000 tracking pixel) — low severity for a tracking pixel, but technically non-compliant
- `49468f73-4651-4af3-bea2-61d1ae5db486.png` — unknown content image with no alt; renders as broken box if images are off

**`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`**
Applied globally via `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }`. This suppresses iOS's automatic font scaling for users who rely on it — an accessibility regression. The value should be `100%` not `none`.

**Duplicate responsive CSS blocks**
Identical media-query rules are declared twice (once for `max-width: 375px` and once for `max-width: 640px`) and again in a second `<style>` block for `max-width: 480px`. No functional breakage, but file size increases and specificity conflicts are possible.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**[FAIL] Empty greeting — confirmed broken experience**

The QA check confirms the greeting resolves to `"Hi ,"` — the first-name merge token evaluated to an empty string. This indicates either:
- The contact record has a null/blank `FirstName` field and no fallback value was set, or
- The ESP template is missing a default: e.g., `%%[IF EMPTY(FirstName)]%%Friend%%[ELSE]%%%%FirstName%%%%[ENDIF]%%`

This is a user-facing defect for every subscriber with a missing first name.
