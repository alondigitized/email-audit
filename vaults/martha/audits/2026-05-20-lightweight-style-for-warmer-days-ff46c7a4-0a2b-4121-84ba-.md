- **Skechers Elite $5 reward nudge** — I noticed it, and as a loyalty member it's a small but real reason to stay in the email.

- **Seasonal timing is right** — Memorial Day + warm weather = I am actively thinking about summer shoes for the kids. The context is correct even if the products aren't.

## What's weak

- **JSON preview garbage. Again. Seven times.** I actually counted. I flagged this on May 14, May 15, May 16, May 17, May 18, May 19. The fact that it's still happening on the 20th makes me wonder if this list is being tested on a broken template branch that nobody's QA'd.
- **No kids content anywhere in the email body.** "Kids" appears only in the tiny nav footer. I shop primarily for my kids on Skechers — canvas classics and boat shoes for dad does nothing for me.
- **Boat shoes.** I didn't come here for boat shoes. Nobody in my household is wearing boat shoes. This is a category miss.
- **Nothing time-specific for Memorial Day** — the BOGO says "Memorial Day Savings" but there's no end date, no countdown, no urgency beyond vibes.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the preview text.** This is still the #1 fix. Before anything else. Seven emails in. Pull whatever automation is injecting that JSON schema and kill it. Nothing else matters until this is solved.
- 2. **Add a kids section to the hero or fold.** If the BOGO is the hook, show me it applies to kids styles. A single kids product tile or a "Shop Kids BOGO" CTA would have made me click immediately.
- 3. **Put the Memorial Day deadline on the BOGO banner.** "Ends Sunday, May 26" or "Through Memorial Day Weekend" converts urgency from implied to real.
- 4. **Lead with canvas/slip-ins for women if you're doing summer style** — the "Laid Back Look" hero reads male. Flip the imagery to a woman or kid; same vibe, better demographic match for your email list.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Skechers "Lightweight Style for Warmer Days"

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is structurally sound and renders correctly in most clients, but carries one broken personalization token (empty greeting name), a pervasive HTTP image-serving issue across all hosted assets, and a third-party pixel with no HTTPS fallback.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**No issues found with link integrity.** All 27 clickable links route through `click.emails.skechers.com` (SFMC click-tracking domain) — expected behavior, correctly skipped by HTTP probe.

**Mixed tracking pixel protocols:**
- `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif` — third-party open-tracking pixel served over plain HTTP. Gmail, Apple Mail (MPP), and most modern clients will either block or proxy this, meaning the impression count from this vendor is unreliable.
- Krux/Salesforce DMP pixels (`beacon.krxd.net`) are HTTPS — no issue.
- Return Path seed pixel (`pixel.app.returnpath.net`) is missing the `https://` scheme prefix entirely — rendered as a relative URL, which will 404.

  Evidence: `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1dbf0dcef79cbad82c12fe25342701e4499"` — no protocol.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**HTTP image sources (all hosted on `image.emails.skechers.com`):**
All product and UI images are served over `http://`, not `https://`. Clients that enforce mixed-content blocking (Outlook 2019+, some webmail) will silently drop these images, resulting in a blank email body.

Affected assets include the logo, product hero images, app store badges, social icons, and footer utility icons — effectively every visible image in the email.

Evidence (sample):
```
http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-...png  (logo)
http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/11/49468f73-...png  (unnamed)
http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/11/0ec56c9f-...png  (App Store badge)
```

**Missing `alt` text:**
- `o.gif` (ink1000 tracking pixel) — low severity for a hidden pixel, but technically invalid.
- `49468f73-4651-4af3-bea2-61d1ae5db486.png` — visible image with no `alt`. Screen readers will announce the filename.

**`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`** is set globally. This suppresses iOS auto-sizing on very small text (e.g., legal copy), which may push it below the 11px legibility floor on older iPhones.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**[FAIL] Empty greeting name — broken experience.**

The QA check confirms: `"Hi ,"` — the first-name merge token resolved to an empty string. This indicates either a missing fallback value in the SFMC AMPscript/personalization string, or the contact record has a blank `First Name` field with no default defined.

Expected pattern: `%%=v(@firstName)=%%` should have a fallback such as `%%[ IF EMPTY(@firstName) THEN SET @firstName = "there" ENDIF ]%%`.

No other unresolved merge tokens detected in the truncated source.

---

### 5. Compliance

**CAN-SPAM / Unsubscribe:** Not fully auditable from the truncated source, but the sending domain (`emails.skechers.com`) is a dedicated subdomain — consistent with proper authentication separation. Unsubscribe link presence cannot be confirmed from the provided excerpt.

**Authentication headers:** Not surfaced in the provided data. DKIM, SPF, and DMARC alignment should be verified against received headers — the subdomain setup is appropriate for this.

**Krux pixel includes hashed PII in the URL:**
```
partner_uid=adf27fee67323d52536b18110294bf793c265b830082caba4b1a32664832189a
```
This appears to be a SHA-256 hashed email (per the `_sha25` suffix in the partner param). Confirm this hash is being shared under a valid DPA/consent mechanism with Salesforce DMP.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All click links pass through `click.emails.skechers.com` — cannot inspect final destination URLs or UTM parameters from the provided source. The campaign ID `MKG_US_NA_U_MULTI_ENGAGEDOPEN_EN_05202026` is present in the Krux impression pixel and the ink1000 pixel, indicating campaign tagging exists at the pixel layer. Whether UTM params (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) are appended to destination URLs is unverifiable without resolving the click-redirect chain.
