- Nothing here is for my kids. Not one image. "KIDS" exists as a single plain-text link buried in a list of six category links near the footer, sandwiched between "CLOTHING" and "NEW ARRIVALS." If I'm hunting for light-up velcro sneakers for my daughter or durable lace-ups for my son, this email gives me zero reason to tap in.

## What's working

- The 40% off banner at the top is bold, legible, and impossible to miss without scrolling.
- "SHOP THE SALE" is a clear, tappable button right up top — no hunting required.
- The product carousel and category sections (Slip-ins, Cozy Fit, BOBS) are clean, no broken images, no overlapping text.
- Payment/perks footer (Afterpay, Klarna, curbside pickup, app download) is at least informative if I were ready to buy for myself.

## What's weak

- Zero kids' product imagery anywhere in the body — "KIDS" is one plain text link at the very bottom, not a visual section like women's/men's got three times over.
- Subject line promised something personalized ("your search") but delivered a generic "most searched" adult sneaker carousel — that's a bait-and-switch on curiosity.
- This is the same "Summer Lovin' Sale" I already saw Thursday, just re-skinned with a search bar graphic — feels recycled, not new.
- No loyalty/rewards points or member pricing shown anywhere, and I'm a rewards member — treat me like one.
- Preview text is straight-up leaking raw JSON-LD schema code into my inbox, not real copy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with an actual kids' section — a boy's lace-up sneaker and a girl's light-up velcro shoe, each with their own "SHOP KIDS'" button, the same treatment BOBS and Cozy Fit got. This is the single biggest miss.
- 2. Fix the preview text — right now it's raw schema markup, not a sentence. That's a QA bug, not a style choice.
- 3. Make the subject line honest to the content, or make the content honest to the subject — right now "your search" implies personalization that isn't there.
- 4. Add a visible loyalty callout ("Members save an extra X%") since I already shop the rewards program.

Standard SFMC-generated marketing email with open/click tracking, Kruxdigital audience beacons, and a 640px fixed-width table layout; QA flagged widespread HTTP (non-HTTPS) image sources and two missing `alt` attributes, with no broken links or compliance blockers detected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Open-tracking pixel present: `https://click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx?6FOONW4JCIRE7MNWMI4HMHSR5Q.100226&d=100226&bmt=0` (HTTPS, correctly hidden).
- Two Kroxdigital (Salesforce Krux) user-match beacons and one ad-impression beacon load over HTTPS from `beacon.krxd.net` — no issues.
- Third-party tracking pixel `pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?...` is missing an explicit `http://`/`https://` scheme prefix in the source (`src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/..."`), which will resolve relative to the document/base URL in most clients rather than as an absolute request — likely a broken tracking call.
- Secondary tracking pixel `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?...` loads over plain HTTP (see §3).
- 38 click-through links were skipped by the automated HTTP probe because they route through the SFMC click-redirect domain (`click.emails.skechers.com`) — these are redirect-tracked links whose final destinations were not independently verified by this QA pass.

- 9 images use `http://` sources instead of `https://`, which mixed-content/HTTPS-only mail clients (Gmail, Outlook.com, Apple Mail) may block or flag, causing broken images: Skechers logo (`dde00662-...png`), product image (`49468f73-...png`), App Store / Google Play badges, Curbside Pickup, AfterPay, Find A Store, Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube icons — all served from `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/...`.
