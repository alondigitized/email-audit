# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-09-15
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this looks like a legit big brand doing a fall refresh — clean, moody outdoorsy hero, sitewide discount banner right at the top. But it's clearly pushing "adult fall wardrobe" mode right now, not kids' shoes, which is the actual reason I'm on this site on a Saturday afternoon while my two are occupied. I'd need to hunt for what I actually came for.

## What's working

- **The 20% OFF Sitewide banner** at the very top — big, bold, code visible (PREVIEW), that's exactly the kind of "worth clicking in" offer that gets me to stop scrolling past.
- **"2 Pairs for $100 - Applied at Cart"** — this one's actually great for me specifically, since I'm always buying at least two pairs (one for each kid), so a multi-pair deal like this is relevant even if the imagery isn't.
- **The "Recently Viewed" tab** right under the hero — nice touch, means if I was here last weekend eyeing something, I don't have to dig for it again.

## What's weak

- **No Kids category anywhere above the fold.** The nav row under the top banner is all comfort-tech (Slip-ins, Wide Fit, Arch Fit, Max Cushioning, Aero Burst), and the tabs under the hero are Women's / Men's / Fall Looks / Recently Viewed — nothing for kids. That's a real miss for someone like me who is 90% here for my 5- and 9-year-old.
- **Hero imagery is two adults on porch steps, no kids in sight.** Pretty, but it's telling me this page was built for a couple's fall outfit refresh, not a mom stocking up on school shoes.
- **No rewards/loyalty callout visible.** I'm the type who cares about points and member pricing, and I don't see anything about that without scrolling.
- **"SHOP THE FALL GUIDE" is vague.** It's not telling me where my kids' shoes actually live.

## Recommendations

- Add a "Kids" tab into that under-hero row (Women's / Men's / Kids / Fall Looks / Recently Viewed) — one tap and I'm home.
- Swap or duplicate the hero rotation with a family/lifestyle shot — a mom and kid, or just kids in motion — so people like me feel seen in the first three seconds.
- Surface the loyalty/rewards badge or points balance near the top banner, next to the 20% off code — I'm the exact shopper who cares about that.
- Make the CTA button read "Shop Kids" or "Shop Fall for the Family" on at least one rotating hero slide instead of the generic "Shop the Fall Guide."
