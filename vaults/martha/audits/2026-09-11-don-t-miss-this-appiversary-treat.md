## What's weak

- This is nail/hair care top to bottom — zero relevance to a mom shopping for kids' shoes, so I'm scrolling past all of it.
- The hero section is just a tote bag and app-download nagging — that's prime real estate wasted before I even see the offer.
- "Appiversary" is a made-up gimmick word I've now seen plastered across like six emails this month; it means nothing to me.
