# September deals are here!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-03**
## Executive summary

- Sally Beauty, again. At this point I could sleepwalk through this brand's emails — this one at least swaps out the confetti-dot COLORfest banner for a "Refresh your beauty routine for fall" hero, so credit for a new coat of paint. But the actual content is the same wall of BOGO hair care, clippers, jewelry, and a hair color bundle — none of which is remotely on my radar as a mom buying velcro sneakers for a 5-year-old and lace-ups for a 9-year-old.
- Honestly the woman in the hero shot is styled and lit nicely, and the fall framing is at least seasonally appropriate instead of another "COLORfest" rerun. But there's zero personalization, zero acknowledgment that I'm "martha.stroll@..." and not a hairstylist or beauty enthusiast. This is the same brand I've now seen a dozen-plus times this summer with a slightly different skin on it.
- I'm not clicking, not forwarding — this gets archived in about two seconds. It's not offensive, just completely irrelevant to me and part of a cadence that's worn out its welcome in my inbox.

## What's working

- The "Refresh your beauty routine for fall, for less" hero copy is at least seasonally current, not a stale summer promo.
- Clear, specific offers throughout — BOGO 50% off hair care, 30% off BaByliss clippers, BOGO 50% off jewelry, $15.99 hair color bundle — all with real end dates.
- Free shipping/2-hour delivery banners up top are concrete, not vague.
- Klarna financing and the in-store/online/pickup icon row add some trust and convenience signals.

## What's weak

- Nothing here is remotely for my kids or me as a shoe-shopping mom — this is a hair/beauty retailer, so I'm just not the audience regardless of how the email is executed.
- It's the same brand hitting my inbox again with a reskinned version of the same block-of-BOGOs layout I've scrolled past all month.
- No sense of "known customer" treatment — it's mass-blast generic, not tailored to my (nonexistent) purchase history with them.
- Long scroll of near-identical maroon offer blocks makes it feel like more of the same wallpaper rather than a curated "top pick for you."

## Recommendations

- 1. This brand shouldn't even be in my inbox this often if I've never purchased hair color, gel nails, or clippers — better list segmentation would fix more than any copy tweak.
- 2. Lead with ONE hero offer instead of stacking four BOGO/percent-off blocks back to back — pick the strongest (BOGO 50% hair care) and feature it big.
- 3. If they insist on emailing broad audiences, add a "shop by category" quiz or quick-pick at the top so non-hair-color shoppers (like me) can self-select something relevant.
- 4. Cut send frequency — I've flagged this same brand fatigue for weeks now.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your fall beauty refresh starts here`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Save on hair care, clippers, and jewelry this month`
