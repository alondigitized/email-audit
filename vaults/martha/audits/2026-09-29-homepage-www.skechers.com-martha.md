# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-09-29
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- This looks like a performance-running brand right now. The whole first screen is a dark, neon-green "SKECHERS AERO SERIES – OUTRUN THE NIGHT" hero with two glowing running shoes. It's slick, but it's not talking to a mom shopping for a 5-year-old and a 9-year-old. The two promo strips at the top are the only parts that feel useful to me.

## What's working

- **The blue "Members Save 20% OFF" strip:** it's the first thing I see. I'm a rewards-program person, so I know right away there's a member price.
- **The "SHOP AERO RUNNING" button:** it's big, white on dark, and says exactly what it does. There's no guessing.
- **Nav labels like "Slip-ins", "Wide Fit", "Arch Fit" and "Max Cushioning":** these are plain-language benefit terms. I can find comfort and fit without knowing Skechers product names.
- **The "Recently Viewed" tab:** it tells me the site remembers me. On a weekend, when I'm picking up where I left off, that saves me time.
- **The clean layout:** nothing pops up over the page and nothing jumps around.

## What's weak

- **The hero:** it's a glowing neon adult running shoe. Nothing says kids, school, or all-day comfort, so it feels like it's for someone else.
- **No Kids or Girls/Boys link in the top nav:** "Slip-ins" is helpful, but I'd have to open MENU to get to my actual shopping category.
- **"Limited Time Only!" on the apparel and bags offer:** there's no end date, so I can't tell whether it's real urgency or just wording.
- **"Applied at Cart" on both offers:** I don't see a price drop until checkout. As a researcher, I like to see the number before I commit.
- **The tab area under the hero:** there's a big blank gap before the product images, and they're cut off at the bottom. I can't see names, prices, or stars.
- **No people in the imagery:** the hero has no model and nothing that looks like my life.
- **No trust signals:** I don't see free shipping, free returns, or ratings anywhere on this screen.

## Recommendations

- **Add "Kids" and "Back to School / Fall" to the nav row** next to Slip-ins and Wide Fit. Make it one tap from the homepage.
- **Rotate a family or kids hero into the carousel.** A kid stepping into Slip-ins with a parent in the frame would work, and the CTA could read "Shop Kids' Slip-ins".
- **Show the price and star rating on the product rail** and tighten the blank space under the tabs, so the first shoes show above the fold. Put "Members $XX" next to the regular price instead of "applied at cart".
- **Put a real end date on the offers** ("Ends Sunday 10/5") and add a small "Free shipping over $X · Free returns" line under the member strip.
