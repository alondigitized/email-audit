# Black Friday Savings Came Early!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-13**
## Executive summary

- Sally Beauty again. I count about the twenty-fifth send since mid-September, and I'm tired of it. I got "Black Friday came early 👀" on Nov 8 and now "Black Friday Savings Came Early!" on Nov 13. That's the same line with a different hat on it. The black hero with gold "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY DEALS" is easy to read, but it has no number on it. I have to scroll to find out what the deal is.
- Once I scroll, the offers are fine: BOGO 50% off, a $18.99 color bundle, and 40% off styling tools. None of it is for my kids, though. There's no kid shoe, no school-year anything. I'm a mom who shops for my 5-year-old and 9-year-old first and myself last. A hair dryer at 40% off barely registers. I'd skim it, maybe save it, and not click. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The black and gold hero is bold and reads clearly on my phone, and I know right away it's a sale.
- "Buy one, get one 50% off" is a real offer I understand in two seconds.
- The $18.99 hair color bundle has a concrete price and lists what's in it (color, developer, bond repair).
- The Klarna "4 interest-free payments" strip is useful for a price-sensitive mom stretching a budget before the holidays.
- The 2-hour delivery and "15% off your next buy online, pick up in store" bars at the top are real perks.

## What's weak

- The hero has no discount number, so the headline says "deals" and leaves me guessing.
- It repeats the Nov 8 "came early" idea and the Nov 11 and Nov 9 gift-list angle. Nothing feels new, and I can't tell what's a real deal versus the usual rotation.
- Nothing mentions rewards. I'm loyal to loyalty programs, and the only hint is a tiny footer line about points.
- The empty gap next to the hair color bundle is just a model photo with no product or link. It looks like a missing image.
- The fine print is a wall of tiny text, and the offer exclusions make me doubt the 40% off.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the biggest offer in the hero, like "BOGO 50% off" or "Up to 40% off," so the number carries the email.
- 2. Show my rewards status and points balance up top. Members like me respond to that.
- 3. Add one gift or family-friendly row, such as stocking stuffers under $10 or kids' hair care, since I'm shopping for my family this time of year.
- 4. Replace the lone model photo with a product shot and a link.
- 5. Cut the send frequency, or make each email clearly different.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Black Friday early access: 40% off tools`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus a $18.99 color bundle and 2-hour delivery on $35+`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible source is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with responsive CSS and Open Sans web fonts. QA confirms several third-party tracking pixels with defects: an unresolved `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro, an `http://` image source and missing alt attributes. The HTML was truncated, so the footer, links and headers could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved macro:** the MediaMath/VisualIQ pixel is `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&...`. The literal `[CACHEBUSTER]` was not substituted, so the cache-buster is a constant string. Clients and proxies that cache the image (Gmail's image proxy, for example) can serve it from cache and undercount impressions.
- **Non-HTTPS pixel:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&...` is plain HTTP. It may be blocked or rewritten as mixed content, so Dotomi (Conversant) sync and tracking will fail for those recipients.
- **Unverified links:** 55 tracking and click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so their destinations and redirect health are unverified. This is a QA coverage limit, not a confirmed defect.
- **Stacked third-party pixels:** the email carries at least three beacons (`pxl.mon-trk.com`, `t.myvisualiq.net`, `login.dotomi.com`). Each adds a blocking external request, and any one failing can leave broken-image artifacts.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (6 images):** the alt attribute is absent or empty on two images with an empty `src`, `003aZ00000s0I5yQAE` (`https://pxl.mon-trk.com/987bfe61-.../2334197/003aZ00000s0I5yQAE`), `impression_pixel` and `UCMController`. For the tracking pixels, `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"` is correct and harmless.
- **Empty `src` images:** the two images QA reports with `src:` blank render as broken images in some clients and cost a wasted request. Verify against the full source.
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`. `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`. Both are inert as written, so charset declaration relies on the HTTP header alone.
- **Undeclared font:** `a{font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif}` references a font with no `@font-face`, so it always falls back to Helvetica. The only declared face is Open Sans.
- **Open Sans subset:** the `@font-face` blocks use v17 gstatic URLs with a Latin-only `unicode-range`. Most email clients ignore web fonts anyway.
- **Compatibility:** the `u ~ div .pattern` hack and `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` reset are present and fine. The `.filter-item:hover` rule is harmless.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. Only `[CACHEBUSTER]` (section 2) appears as an unresolved token. The rest of the body was truncated, so the full document was not checked for stray `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` tokens.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication:** `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. The QA note attributes this to the AgentMail relay stripping the header, so it is a measurement gap, not evidence of failure. The From domain `em.sallybeauty.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is good practice.
- **Unsubscribe and postal address:** not visible in the truncated source, so they were not verified. The `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers were not provided either. The `.legal a` style rule suggests a footer exists.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The 55 click-tracking links were not probed, and the truncated source shows no landing URLs or UTM parameters. No continuity issues are confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Replace `[CACHEBUSTER]` in the `t.myvisualiq.net` pixel with a per-send or per-recipient value, or confirm the ESP substitutes it at send time.
2. Move the `login.dotomi.com` pixel to `https://`, or drop it if the vendor doesn't support TLS.
3. Add `alt=""`, `width="1"`, `height="1"` and `style="display:block"` to all beacon pixels. Add descriptive alt text to any visible images among the six flagged. Remove or fix the empty-`src` images.
4. Correct the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge" />`.
5. Declare `Founders Grotesk` or remove it from the `a` rule, and use a defined fallback stack.
6. Re-run the audit on the full HTML and raw headers (Authentication-Results, List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post) and probe a sample of the 55 tracking links. That covers sections 4–6.
