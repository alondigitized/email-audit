# Fall color, with a glossy finish 💫
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-17**
## Executive summary

- Sally Beauty again — I feel like I'm on a first-name basis with this brand's Appiversary campaign at this point, and it's still limping along into a third week. This one's at least dressed up differently — hair gloss and hair color instead of nails — but it's the same story: nothing here is for me. I'm scrolling my phone during five minutes of peace while my kids are occupied, and I get a gorgeous model with glossy curls telling me to "get glossy this season." Cute, not relevant.
- There's no kids' anything, no family angle, nothing that treats me like a mom of two who's actually going to open her wallet for velcro sneakers or light-up soles this weekend. It's professional-grade hair color (Veracolor), an ion 24K treatment, and an app download push wrapped around Rewards points — all stuff aimed at someone with way more time and salon-adjacent interest than I have right now.
- Would I click? No. Would I forward it? Definitely not — this isn't even the right category for a friend swap. I'll mark it read and keep scrolling for something with a shoe box in it.

## What's working

- The hero image is genuinely well-shot — warm, polished, and the model's hair actually sells "glossy."
- The offer is at least concrete: "one-step hair gloss," a specific product (Veracolor), and a Klarna 4-payment option clearly called out.
- Shipping/delivery perks (free 2-hour delivery over $35, buy online pick-up in-store) are laid out cleanly near the bottom in icon form.
- No render bugs — everything loaded, text is legible, layout holds together on mobile.

## What's weak

- Zero relevance to my life — no kids' category, no family bundle, nothing pointing at anything I'd actually shop for on a Saturday.
- The Appiversary nag ("Don't have the Sally Beauty app? ... 2x Rewards all September long") is the same beat I've seen repeatedly this month — it's starting to feel like background noise, not a reason to open the app.
- This is a hair-color/hair-care email through and through — if I'm not coloring my own hair at home, there's nothing to hook me.
- The fine print block at the bottom is dense and generic — not a dealbreaker since I never scroll that far, but it signals "mass blast," not "we know you."

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment me out of hair-color sends entirely if I've never clicked one — stop burning impressions on a category I don't buy in.
- 2. If Sally Beauty wants any shot with a mom like me, lead with a kids'/family angle or a loyalty-points nudge tied to something I actually buy, not hair gloss.
- 3. Ease off the Appiversary app-download ask — three-plus weeks of the same CTA reads as desperate, not exciting.
- 4. Tighten the subject/preheader so they're not redundant with each other.
