# Your Halloween look starts here 🎃
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- Sally Beauty again, and this is the seventh or eighth send since the Appiversary run. The Halloween-plans hero with the red curly hair model is eye-catching, but I'm a mom shopping for a 5-year-old and a 9-year-old. A 15% off pickup deal on hair color developer isn't for me. The subject promised a "Halloween look," and then the products are ion color sealers and a 20-volume creme developer. That's a bait-and-switch on the theme. Where's the costume makeup, the face paint, the spooky nails?
- The offer is at least clear: 15% off if I buy online and pick up in store. But that's a hurdle for a busy weekend phone shopper. After yesterday's "Don't ghost these deals" and Tuesday's "Your biggest savings start now," I'm tired of the cadence.
- Would I click? No. Forward it to a friend? Only a friend who dyes her own hair. I'd mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- The hero headline "Halloween plans? Start here." is big, readable on my phone, and has a clear "Shop now" button right under it.
- The 15% off pickup offer is stated plainly in the hero, so I don't have to hunt for it.
- The red-hair model photo is bold and fits the Halloween mood.
- The Sally Rewards banner at the top ($5 off $25) is a nice nudge for someone who likes loyalty programs.
- The icon row (free shipping over $40, 2 hour delivery, buy online pick up in store) gives me quick trust cues.

## What's weak

- The hero promises a Halloween look, but all four products are professional hair color finishing items (booster, developer, color sealers). It doesn't match.
- Nothing is relevant to my kids: no costume makeup, face paint, glitter, or kid-friendly sparkle for a 5-year-old girl.
- The products have no prices, no sale prices and no reviews. I research before I buy, so "Shop now" with nothing else gives me no reason to tap.
- The offer needs me to pick up in store, and the fine print says it's not valid with other promos. That's a lot of friction.
- The footer fine print is a wall of text, and I can't tell whether the Klarna "orders above $35" and the free-shipping "$40+" thresholds work together.
- The hair color developer for a pro stylist feels like a stranger's email. It doesn't treat me like a known customer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the products match the hero. Show costume makeup, face paint, glitter and nail stickers for kids and families, with prices and star ratings.
- 2. Put the offer in the subject line, and lead with "15% off," since that's the one concrete thing.
- 3. Add a "Halloween under $10" or "kid-safe face paint" block for parents.
- 4. Show the pickup deal as one clear line with an in-store pickup button, not buried below the fold.
- 5. Cut the sends this week. Combine these into one or two stronger emails.
- **Subject Alt A:** `15% off Halloween makeup, pick up today 🎃`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Face paint, glitter & more: 15% off pickup`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Order online, pick up in store, and save 15% on costume makeup for the whole family.`

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 50 tracking links were skipped (`Tracking/click-redirect domains — skipped HTTP probe`). Their status, redirect chain and destinations are unverified.
- **Unresolved macro:** the pixel `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&...` still contains the literal `[CACHEBUSTER]`. The ad-server macro was never substituted, so the pixel is likely cached or deduplicated, and impression counts will be unreliable.

- **Many third-party pixels:** the email loads at least three third-party trackers (`pxl.mon-trk.com`, `myvisualiq.net`, `dotomi.com`) in addition to the Evergage image calls. Each adds a failure point and a privacy surface.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** these images have no alt attribute.
  - Four Evergage images: `.../campaign/rfFwW/172J1/{1,2,3,4}/summary.png`. These are likely content blocks, so they need real alt text.
  - An image with an **empty `src`** (`Image missing alt text: : src:`). This is a broken or empty `<img>` that can render a broken-image icon in some clients.
  - Three tracking pixels: `003aZ00000s0I5yQAE`, `impression_pixel` and `UCMController`. These should have `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`, plus `style="display:none"` where appropriate.
- **Meta tags:** the visible `<head>` has `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=edge" />` with no `http-equiv` attribute. Neither tag does anything as written. The charset is not declared in the document, which matters for the 🎃 and other non-ASCII characters if the MIME header is lost.
