# 20% off the shoes you've been eyeing
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay, my first thought was "didn't I just get this one?" It's the Fall Haul 20% members offer from 9/29 and 10/1, now in a "Fall Favorites" plaid banner. The 20% is huge and I can't miss it. Seeing "Hi, Martha," Member, and my 1000 points with the $5 reward at the top feels good, because they know me. But the points line is the same one I've seen in every email for two weeks.
- Then I scrolled and it turned into a generic Women / Men / Kids grid. Kids is the only section I care about, and it's the third one down. It has three shoes, with a black slip-in, a blue and orange runner, and a lavender pair. The lavender one could work for my 5-year-old, and the blue one for my 9-year-old. There's no velcro, no light-up soles, and no mention of a bundle. The fine print says "select regular priced items" and "excludes ... prior purchases, sale styles," and I can't tell whether the shoes shown qualify.
- Would I click? Probably, because 20% off two pairs of kids' shoes is real money. I'd click SHOP KIDS', not the hero button. I wouldn't forward it. I'd also wait to see if BOGO comes back, since last week's BOGO 50% was better for two kids.

## What's working

- The 20% OFF is huge and readable on my phone, and "Valid thru 10/11" gives me a deadline.
- "Hi, Martha," Member, and "1000 points / $5 reward, REDEEM" tell me they know who I am.
- Women/Men/Kids sections with their own orange buttons make it easy to jump to Kids.
- The kids' row includes a lavender pair and a blue/orange pair, which loosely fit my daughter and son.
- Klarna/Afterpay and the app download are handy for a mobile shopper.

## What's weak

- The offer is a repeat of the Fall Haul 20% from 9/29 and 10/1, so there's no urgency or news.
- Kids is third, after Women and Men, even though I'm a kids' shopper.
- The shoes are tiny, with no names, prices, or sale tags. I can't tell what's included.
- There's no velcro, light-up, or durable-sole messaging, and no family bundle or code.
- The exclusions are in tiny fine print at the bottom. "Select regular priced items" makes me doubt the offer.
- "SHOP NOW" is generic, and the hero doesn't link to anything specific.
- The footer is a wall of text.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a Kids block (or personalize the order by past browsing), and name what's in it: "Kids' sneakers, 20% off."
- 2. Put prices, "was/now," and product names under each shoe, and tag items that are excluded.
- 3. Add kid-specific callouts like "Easy velcro" and "Light-up," plus "Stack with your $5 reward."
- 4. Make the CTAs specific: "Shop kids' sneakers" instead of "Shop Now."
- 5. Move the key exclusions up near the hero and mention member stacking.
- 6. Cut the repeated creative, or make it feel different from 10/1.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "20% off the shoes you've been eyeing"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has two confirmed QA failures: an empty greeting token (`Hi ,`) and no plain-text part (0 chars). The HTML also has insecure `http://` images, a scheme-less tracking pixel, and an ineffective charset declaration. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe, and link destinations could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Scheme-less pixel:** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…">` has no `https://`. Clients will treat it as a relative path, so the request will fail and the Return Path/Validity pixel won't fire.
- **`http://` tracking pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108404282&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NONPUR_U_SITE_NONPURCH_EN_10082026_A`. It is insecure and may be blocked or rewritten by proxies (Gmail, Apple MPP).
- **Third-party beacons:** Three `beacon.krxd.net` pixels load alongside the SFMC open pixel (`click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx?…`). One carries `partner_uid=adf27fee67323d52536b18110294bf793c265b830082caba4b1a32664832189a`, which appears to be a SHA-256 hash of the recipient identifier. Together they add several third-party requests per open and send a recipient-linked identifier to an ad-data platform.
- **Unverified links:** 40 tracking links were skipped by QA (click-redirect domains). Their destinations and status codes are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Ineffective meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so neither declares anything. Non-UTF-8 defaults may garble `®` and other special characters in some clients.
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):**
  - `o.gif` (ink1000)
  - `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`
  - Source also shows the `krxd.net` and `returnpath` pixels with no `alt` attribute. They are hidden, so impact is low.
- **`http://` content image:** `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-…png`. This is on the brand's own image host, which already supports HTTPS, so it can be switched. Mixed content may be blocked or show as a broken image.
- **Viewport:** `maximum-scale=1` disables pinch-zoom on mobile, an accessibility issue.
- **Focus outline removed:** `a { outline: none; }` removes the keyboard focus indicator on every link.
- **Missing `role="presentation"`:** The visible layout tables (`<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0" border="0">`) have no `role="presentation"`, so screen readers may announce them as data tables. This is based on the truncated source only.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** The greeting renders as `Hi ,`. The first-name field resolved to empty, and there is no fallback value such as "Hi there,". This is a visible broken-experience defect.
- **Segment mismatch:** The subject and campaign ID (`PRO_US_NONPUR_U_SITE_NONPURCH`) target non-purchasing site visitors, so the send is behaviorally personalized. A blank name undermines that.
- **Token location:** The token's exact location is outside the truncated source, so it could not be inspected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] Missing plain-text part:** The text version is 0 chars. It is a deliverability and accessibility problem, and spam filters can penalize HTML-only mail.
- **Unconfirmed:** Physical address, unsubscribe link, `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results were not in the supplied data. I can't confirm them either way.
- **Sender:** `no-reply@emails.skechers.com` is a no-reply address. It is not a CAN-SPAM violation, but it gives recipients no direct reply path.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The 40 click-tracked links were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with "20% off" could not be verified.
- The only campaign identifier visible is `PRO_US_NONPUR_U_SITE_NONPURCH_EN_10082026_A`, in the `ink1000` and `krxd` pixels. Confirm it is also carried through to the click-through URLs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a fallback to the first-name token** (e.g. `Hi there,`), or suppress the greeting when the name is blank. Check why this recipient's field was empty.
2. **Add a plain-text alternative** to the multipart message. It should include the offer, key links, and the unsubscribe link.
3. **Fix pixel URLs:** add `https://` to `pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif`, and move `www.ink1000.com/…/o.gif` to HTTPS or remove it.
4. **Switch `image.emails.skechers.com/…/dde00662-….png` to `https://`** and add descriptive `alt` text (or `alt=""` if decorative).
5. **Correct the meta tags:** use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
6. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport tag and restore a visible `:focus` style for links.
7. **Add `role="presentation"`** to the layout tables.
8. **Review the Krux hashed-identifier beacons** for privacy-policy and consent alignment, and consider reducing the number of third-party pixels.
9. **Re-run the audit with the full HTML and raw headers** to verify the unsubscribe link, physical address, `List-Unsubscribe`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, and UTM parameters on the 40 skipped links.
