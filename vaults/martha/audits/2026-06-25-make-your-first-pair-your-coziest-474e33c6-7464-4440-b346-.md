# Make Your First Pair Your Coziest
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-06**
## Executive summary

- I grabbed my phone during those precious 10 minutes when both kids were actually occupied, opened my inbox, and immediately felt something was off before I even tapped. The preview text was total gibberish — bracket characters, schema nonsense — and my first instinct was phishing. It's not, but that gut check costs Skechers a second of credibility before I even get inside.
- Once I'm in, it cleans up. The BOGO 50% off banner at the top is always a reason to keep scrolling, and the Cozy Fit hero is visually appealing. But within one scroll I realize this entire email is women's styles for me — Slip-Ins, Backless, Mary Janes, Cozy Clothing. The kids section is a tiny nav link in the footer. My actual shopping mission? Completely unaddressed.
- What really nags me is the "first pair" framing in the subject. Three weeks ago I got a Skechers email that opened with "Hi Martha" and showed me my exact points balance. Now I'm a first-time shopper who needs to be introduced to the concept of cozy shoes? It feels like I landed on the wrong list. I'd tap the BOGO to see if kids are included, then bounce when they're not. Mark as read.

## What's working

- BOGO 50% off banner anchors the very top — it's the first thing my eye catches and it's always a reason to keep scrolling before I even look at the product categories
- The shoe photography in the Slip-Ins and Backless sections is clean and uncluttered — I can actually tell what I'd be buying without having to squint
- "Shop [Category] For Her" CTA labels are specific enough that I know exactly where I'd land — no mystery "Discover" buttons
- The 25% off apparel module lower in the email is a nice secondary hook; I'd consider a hoodie or joggers if I'm already buying shoes
- Mobile layout is genuinely comfortable — text is readable without zooming, tap targets feel reasonable on my phone

## What's weak

- The preheader leaked raw JSON-LD schema markup into my inbox preview — before I opened, it looked like a spam or phishing attempt, and that first impression is hard to undo
- "Make Your First Pair Your Coziest" is a strange subject for a returning subscriber; Skechers had my name and loyalty data three weeks ago, so this "first pair" framing feels like a list segmentation error
- Not a single kids module in the email body — I'm primarily shopping for a 5-year-old and a 9-year-old, and all I get is a tiny "KIDS" nav link buried at the bottom
- "Cozy" reads fall/winter to me; sending a cozy-themed email in June when it's summer outside creates a mild cognitive disconnect that makes the whole thing feel slightly out of time
- No loyalty mention anywhere — after the June 1st points summary and Member Month emails, omitting my balance or a member-exclusive hook makes me feel like a stranger, not a Plus member

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the preheader — this is urgent.** The JSON-LD leak is the single most damaging issue; it tanks open rate before anyone reads a word. A blank preheader would be better than what's currently in the inbox.
- 2. **Add a kids module in the body.** Even one callout — "New Styles for Little Ones" with a SHOP KIDS button — transforms this from a browse I skim into an email I actually act on.
- 3. **Pull my loyalty state.** A single line — "Your Skechers Plus points are waiting" — is the difference between feeling known and feeling like a stranger on a cold list.
- 4. **Fix the "first pair" segmentation.** Don't send new-to-brand subject copy to people who've been on the list for months. This is a list logic fix, not just a copywriting tweak.
- 5. **Update the seasonal frame.** Swap "Cozy" for something that reads summer — all-day comfort, walk-everywhere, etc.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Next Favorite Pair Ships Free — Skechers Slip-Ins`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Hands-free Slip-Ins, backless styles & 25% off apparel — one scroll, one click`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The shoes that go on without hands are BOGO 50% off right now`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Skechers "Make Your First Pair Your Coziest"

---

### 1. Technical Summary

One broken personalization token will render a degraded greeting in production. All product images and several UI assets are served over HTTP, creating broad mixed-content risk across Gmail, Outlook 365, and modern browsers.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

No broken destination links confirmed (27 click-redirect links skipped per QA — tracking domains not probed).

One third-party pixel uses a plain HTTP URL, not a click-redirect:

- `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif` — open-tracking pixel served over HTTP; will be blocked silently in most modern email clients.

Krux/Salesforce DMP beacons (`beacon.krxd.net`) and the Return Path seed pixel (`pixel.app.returnpath.net`) are present. All are scoped to the hidden preheader table, which is correct placement.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**HTTP image sources (mixed content):** 11 of the visible image assets are served from `http://image.emails.skechers.com`. Gmail and Outlook 365 proxy all images over HTTPS; clients that do not proxy (some corporate Outlook, Apple Mail in certain configs) may silently block them. Affected assets:

- Logo: `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`
- Unnamed product image: `49468f73-4651-4af3-bea2-61d1ae5db486.png`
- App Store, Google Play badges
- Curbside Pickup, AfterPay, Find A Store icons
- Instagram, Facebook, YouTube social icons

**Missing alt text:** Two images lack `alt` attributes:

- `o.gif` (ink1000 open pixel) — low risk for a tracking pixel, but fails strict accessibility checks
- `49468f73-4651-4af3-bea2-61d1ae5db486.png` — product image with no alt text; renders as a blank gap with images-off

**CSS viewport lock:** `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta tag prevents user zoom on mobile, which is an accessibility anti-pattern (WCAG 1.4.4).

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**[FAIL — Broken Experience]** The greeting contains an unresolved merge token, producing output such as `"Hi ,"` with an empty name field. This indicates the first-name variable was not populated before send, or the fallback value is an empty string rather than a default like `"there"`. This is the single highest-priority fix.

No other unresolved token patterns detected in the truncated source.

---

### 5. Compliance

No missing unsubscribe link detected in the truncated source (footer region not shown, but standard for Skechers SFMC sends).

CAN-SPAM physical address presence not verifiable from the truncated HTML — should be confirmed in the footer.

No authentication header data provided; DKIM/SPF/DMARC alignment for `emails.skechers.com` cannot be assessed from HTML alone.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

Campaign ID `MKG_US_NONPURCLICK_U_COZY_NONPURCH_EN_06062026` appears consistently across the Krux impression pixel and the ink1000 pixel — internal tracking is coherent.

UTM parameters on destination links cannot be verified (all 27 links are click-redirect URLs through `click.emails.skechers.com`; landing page UTM decoration is downstream of the redirect and was not probed).

---

### 7. Recommendations

| Priority | Issue | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| P0 | Empty `%%first_name%%` merge token | Add a non-empty fallback value (e.g., `%%=IIF(EMPTY(FirstName),"there",FirstName)=%%`) before resend or reuse |
| P1 | HTTP image CDN (`image.emails.skechers.com`) | Update all `src` attributes to `https://image.emails.skechers.com/…` — the domain almost certainly supports HTTPS |
| P1 | HTTP open-tracking pixel (`ink1000.com`) | Switch to `https://` or replace with the SFMC native open-tracking pixel which is already present |
| P2 | Missing alt text on product image `49468f73…` | Add descriptive `alt` text for images-off rendering and screen reader support |
| P3 | `maximum-scale=1` viewport lock | Remove `maximum-scale=1` to comply with WCAG 1.4.4 (user zoom) |
| P3 | Return Path pixel protocol | Confirm `pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif` is reachable over HTTPS |
