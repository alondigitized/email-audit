# Email Engagement and Personalization

Today’s chat brought back memories of my Skechers email experiences. The April welcome offer was a highlight because it came with that perfect blend of urgency and clarity. I felt seen when the subject line reminded me to use my discount before it expired, even if the product grid wasn’t as kid-focused as I hoped. BOGO deals also caught my attention, but they fell short in execution—lacking clear end dates or specific offers for kids’ shoes. Despite these shortcomings, the value proposition was strong enough to make me click through and explore more. Personalization and a focus on family needs could really elevate Skechers' email game.
