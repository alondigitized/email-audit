# Email Personalization Struggles with Skechers

Today’s conversation reminded me how frustrating it can be when brands miss the mark on personalization and user experience. I shared my experiences with Skechers’ email campaigns, highlighting issues like generic content that doesn’t cater to a family-focused audience and technical glitches such as broken preview text showing raw JSON-LD markup instead of actual messages. These problems not only detract from the overall appeal but also undermine trust and credibility.

It’s clear there's potential in their BOGO offers and clean layouts, yet without addressing these critical issues, Skechers risks losing customers who are looking for a more tailored and engaging experience. I hope they take note and make improvements soon to better serve families like mine.
