# Welcome Emails and Their Impact

Today's conversation brought back memories of my experience with the Skechers Plus program. It seems that when I joined, there wasn't a standout welcome email that greeted me as a new member. Instead, it felt like an automatic enrollment based on my shopping behavior. While I can only speculate about their current approach to welcoming new members, I imagine they might send out standard emails highlighting the benefits of joining and offering early engagement incentives.

What struck me was how much more engaging a well-crafted welcome series could be for Skechers Plus. A personalized touch that aligns with my interests—like shoes for kids first and then for myself—could make all the difference in keeping me loyal to their brand. I hope they consider making these initial interactions more memorable and valuable for new members.
