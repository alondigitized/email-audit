# New for fall
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand seasonal catalog send. A person in a red shell and sleeping bag opens the email, and "New Fall Colors" sits in rust on a cream field. A flat-lay of a navy hooded jacket, a quilted jacket, olive pants and a flannel follows, then a 2x2 grid of four products. It's easy to scan and the photography is good.
- It is also generic. The subject, "New for fall", and the copy ("fall is a feeling") promise nothing specific. There's no price, no hero product and no reason to click today. The only commercial hook is the free-shipping line in the top bar, which is a standing perk. After the "Peak Layering Season" send on 10/12 and the near-daily sends since 10/4, this reads as another seasonal mood board.
- Verdict: competent but forgettable, in line with the other sends in the 6/10 range. The design is fine. The weakness is the lack of a reason to act.

## What's working

- The hero photo and the rust "New Fall Colors" headline set the seasonal mood immediately, and the earth tones fit the brand.
- Hierarchy is clear: hero, headline, one pill CTA, flat-lay, product grid, editorial.
- The 2x2 grid shows four distinct products with name labels and individual "Shop" buttons, so the clicks go to specific items.
- The free-shipping bar is visible at the very top, and the fine print is disclosed at the bottom.
- There are no render bugs. Images load, and text and buttons are aligned.

## What's weak

- No prices, discounts or loyalty hooks anywhere. The only offer is free shipping over $99.
- The copy is mood-driven ("fall is a feeling") and the flat-lay has no labels, so the viewer can't tell which garments are being promoted.
- The CTAs are weak and repeated: "Men's New Arrivals", "Explore", "Shop" x4 and "Shop New Arrivals". "Explore" is vague, and there are too many competing buttons.
- The "It's a Magical World" editorial block and the "Earth is now our only shareholder" block pull attention away from commerce, and the story block uses a second rust field.
- The grid is men's only, but the subject and hero don't say so. A women's or mixed-gender subscriber gets no segmentation signal.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one hero product and a reason to buy it. For example, feature the Isthmus Lined Jacket or the quilted jacket with its name, price and one fit or material detail.
- 2. Label the flat-lay, or replace it with one shoppable hero. Right now it's decoration.
- 3. Cut the CTAs to one primary per section, with specific copy such as "Shop men's fall jackets". Drop "Explore".
- 4. Add a price or a reason to buy now, such as "Free shipping over $99 through Sunday", and show it in the hero area rather than only the top bar.
- 5. Add social proof, such as review stars or "Best seller" tags, in the product grid.
- 6. Move the editorial and shareholder content below a stronger commerce block, or send them separately.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Men's fall jackets, cords & flannels are here`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The Isthmus Jacket and 3 more fall favorites`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Corduroy jeans, a lined jacket and a quilted pullover. Free shipping over $99.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New textured knits, classic flannels and double-weave fabric for colder nights.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is structurally sound (`lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` layout tables, MSO conditional CSS, a preheader with a `&zwnj;&nbsp;` spacer). The QA run could not confirm compliance headers or authentication, and the source is truncated, so link, UTM and footer checks are only partly possible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage is thin.** QA probed 1 of 12 material links and skipped 11, so 11 destination URLs have no status-code evidence. That is a coverage limit, not a confirmed broken link.
- **Open pixel is on a separate tracking domain.** `https://trk.na.patagonia.com/wf/open?upn=u001...` is a link-tracking subdomain (the `upn` token looks like a SendGrid-style click/open tracker). I can't tell from the visible source whether CTA links are wrapped in the same redirector. If they are, a failure at `trk.na.patagonia.com` would break every CTA.
- No malformed hrefs or broken link syntax are visible in the retrieved portion.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Open pixel has no alt attribute.** QA flagged `<img src="https://trk.na.patagonia.com/wf/open?upn=...">`. This is low severity because it is a tracking pixel. Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`, plus `style="display:block"` if it isn't already set.
- **`<title></title>` is empty.** Some clients and web-view renderings use it as the page title, and it hurts accessibility on the "view in browser" version. It should hold the subject line or a meaningful title.
- **Web fonts have fallbacks only in the MSO block.** `CopernicusEmail` and `RidgewayEmail` load via `@font-face` from `images.patagonia.com/eMedia/fonts/...`. The `.Serif` and `.NoSerif` classes carry Palatino, Georgia and Helvetica fallbacks only inside the `<!--[if gt mso 9]>` block. The inline `font-family` values in the truncated body would need to be checked for generic fallbacks.
- **Preheader hiding is robust.** It uses `display:none; max-height:0; overflow:hidden`, which is fine.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FNAME]`, and similar) appear in the visible source. I could not check the truncated remainder.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
All three QA warnings are header-capture gaps, not confirmed defects:
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this is unverified.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The same caveat applies. One-click unsubscribe support is unconfirmed, which matters for Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `na.patagonia.com`, and the tracking and image domains are `trk.na.patagonia.com` and `images.patagonia.com`.

The footer (physical address, visible unsubscribe link) is not in the truncated source. QA states an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result was reported.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. The CTA hrefs, UTM parameters and landing pages fall in the truncated section, and the 11 skipped links have no redirect-chain data. Nothing was confirmed as an issue.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run the audit against the raw MIME so `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` are captured. Until then, treat compliance and authentication as unverified rather than failing.
2. Add `alt=""` and explicit 1×1 dimensions to the open pixel at `trk.na.patagonia.com/wf/open`.
3. Fill in `<title>`.
4. Raise the probe budget, or run a second pass, to cover the remaining 11 material links. Record the final resolved URL and UTM set for each CTA.
5. Pass the full, untruncated HTML to the audit so the footer, unsubscribe link and UTM checks can run.
