# Nano or Micro? Pick your puff
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the strongest Patagonia send in the recent run. It has one job, which is helping you choose between two jackets. The split hero shows Nano Puff and Micro Puff side by side with a "Shop" label on each. Two matching burgundy panels follow, each with five short spec bullets that contrast the jackets directly. It reads like a buyer's guide rather than a catalog dump, which makes it more useful than "Fall flannels that stand apart" (10/18) or "New for fall" (10/13).
- The weakness is the same one Patagonia has had all month: there is no offer. The only commercial hook is "Free Shipping on Orders over $99*" in the top strip. Nothing in the body adds urgency or a price or loyalty reason to buy now. The email earns attention and understanding, but it asks for no action beyond browsing. The Hard-Shell vs. Soft-Shell block and the "Earth is now our only shareholder" footer pull attention away from the puffer decision.
- Judgement: a useful, well-built education send. It will drive consideration, but it won't create urgency.

## What's working

- The split hero puts both products above the fold, with a clear "Pick Your Puff" headline that matches the subject.
- The Nano and Micro panels use parallel five-bullet spec lists, so the comparison is easy to scan.
- Each panel has separate Men's and Women's Shop links, so the click goes to the right category.
- The burgundy-and-cream palette is consistent and on brand, and the product photography is clean.
- The Men's Nano Puff Vest and Women's Hi-Loft Nano Puff Hoody add specific products after the comparison.

## What's weak

- There is no promo or price anywhere in the body. The only incentive is the free-shipping strip, which carries an asterisk and long fine print.
- The spec bullets are small, light text on dark burgundy, and are hard to read on mobile.
- The Hard-Shell vs. Soft-Shell block and the shareholder letter at the bottom dilute the puffer message.
- The "Shop" buttons on the hero images are small. Several CTAs go to the same generic "Explore Tech Insulation" destination, and none carries a price or a verb-plus-noun phrase.
- No reviews or social proof appear next to the products.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add one concrete reason to buy now: a price anchor ("From $149"), a Worn Wear used option, or a seasonal deadline. This single change would lift the email the most.
- 2. Add a "Which one is for you?" line under each panel, such as "Choose Nano Puff for daily wear" and "Choose Micro Puff for packing light." Then make the CTAs say "Shop Nano Puff Men's" and so on.
- 3. Increase the bullet font size and contrast in the dark panels.
- 4. Cut the hard-shell promo or move it to its own send, so the email ends on the puffer decision.
- 5. Add a star rating or a review count to each hero product.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible markup is generally sound: `lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` layout tables, an Outlook conditional block, and a hidden preheader with a `&zwnj;&nbsp;` spacer. The QA run found no hard failures, only 3 warnings, and the three header warnings are probably relay-capture artifacts rather than sender defects. The HTML is truncated, so I could only audit the `<head>` and the first few body blocks.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage is partial.** 12 links were probed and 15 skipped, and the QA report lists no failed or redirecting links among the 12. The 15 skipped (footer, utility and social) are unverified.
- **Open pixel:** `https://trk.na.patagonia.com/wf/open?upn=u001.…` is a standard ESP open-tracking pixel. It is on a first-party-branded subdomain (`trk.na.patagonia.com`), which is good for deliverability.
- **Link-wrapping:** The truncated HTML shows no CTA hrefs, so I can't say whether links are wrapped or redirected.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in `<head>`. This is minor, but it is a real defect for web-view and accessibility contexts.
- **Missing alt text** on the tracking pixel (`trk.na.patagonia.com/wf/open…`), flagged by QA. It is a 1×1 beacon, so the impact is low. Adding `alt=""` is the correct fix, and it is also the signal for a decorative image.
- **Positive:**
  - `lang="en"` is set on both `<html>` and the inner `<div>`.
  - Layout tables use `role="presentation"`.
  - Web fonts use `font-display: swap`.
- **Minor markup hygiene:**
  - `<body style>` repeats `margin`, `width` and `-webkit-text-size-adjust` several times.
  - `border-collapse:collapse` is repeated three times in the preheader table.
  - Neither causes a rendering fault.
- **Breakpoints:** Two media queries use `600px` and `540px` with duplicated `.fluid` rules. It works, but it is inconsistent.
- **Web fonts:** The `@font-face` rules for `CopernicusEmail` and `RidgewayEmail` load from `images.patagonia.com`. Clients that ignore `@font-face` (Gmail, Outlook) depend on fallback stacks, and none are visible in the truncated source.
- **Alt text on content images:** I can't verify it because the source is cut off.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. I could not check the truncated remainder.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA itself attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly stripping headers, so the sender's actual behavior is unconfirmed. For a sender of Patagonia's size, I would not treat it as a confirmed defect.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a monitoring gap, not a sender fault.
- **Sender:** `funhogs@na.patagonia.com` is aligned with the branded tracking domain `trk.na.patagonia.com`, which is consistent.
- **Footer and unsubscribe link:** QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but it reports no failure. The physical address and footer fall in the truncated region, so I could not confirm them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The truncated source exposes no CTA URLs, and QA reports no UTM or landing-page data. No issues are confirmed.
