# Find your favorite Nano-Air
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand product-education send for the Nano-Air line. The hero is a mountaineering shot of climbers on a snowy ridge, with a huge "Nano-Air®" wordmark over the sky. Below it a sage panel says "Responsive Warmth" and offers one black "Explore" button. The three weights then stack in order: Light (40g), Ultralight (20g) and the standard 60g Nano-Air. The 20/40/60 numbers in the hero corner are a clever visual key to the grams.
- It does a good job of helping someone choose between weights. It is not a sales email. The only commercial hook is "Free Shipping on Orders over $99" in the top bar. There is no discount, no deadline, and no reason to buy this week, even though it is mid-November. Compared with the Nov 11 down-insulation send and the Nov 15 Torrentshell send, it follows the same catalog format. A subscriber who got all three would see a brand that explains well and rarely pushes.
- Verdict: a solid, honest educational send, but it is long and has no urgency. It lands in the usual 5-6 range.

## What's working

- The hero's 20 / 40 / 60 numbers and the oversized wordmark make the product family clear right away.
- Each jacket gets its own block with a short spec line (40-gram, 20-gram, 60-gram FullRange®), so the weight differences are easy to compare.
- The "NEW" labels on Light and Nano-Air give a reason to look now.
- Product images are clean and on a white background, and the sage, black and white palette is consistent with the brand.
- The three "Shop Nano-Air…" buttons use the same style, so the click path is obvious.

## What's weak

- The primary "Explore" button is vague, and the top of the email has no offer except free shipping over $99.
- The email is very long at about 5,900px, with the Caroline Gleich "Watch" block and the "Earth is now our only shareholder" footer after the products.
- Product blocks show no price, colors or reviews, so there is little to help someone decide.
- The lifestyle image in the middle has the caption "Innovative, breathable insulation tuned for daily wear" over a busy photo. It is hard to read and breaks the flow between the Ultralight and Nano-Air blocks.
- The Gleich video feature has no tie to Nano-Air, so it pulls attention away from the products.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add one concrete reason to act now, such as a price, a member benefit, or "ships before the first storm". Right now there is nothing time-bound.
- 2. Change "Explore" to "Compare the 3 Nano-Airs", and add a price ("from $X") under each product name.
- 3. Cut the Gleich block or move it to a separate send, and keep the email to the three products with the key specs.
- 4. Add a single review quote or star rating to each product block.
- 5. Put the caption text on a solid panel so it can be read over the photo.
