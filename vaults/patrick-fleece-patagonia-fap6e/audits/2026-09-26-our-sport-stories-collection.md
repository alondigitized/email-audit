# Our sport stories collection
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-26**
## Executive summary

- This is Patagonia's eleventh straight non-sale editorial send, and it's the most content-marketing-y yet — it's not even a product email. It's a straight-up storytelling hub: six magazine-style tiles linking to essays/films/podcasts about climbing, surfing, mountain communities, trail advocacy, and beaver-assisted river restoration. There's no product, no price, no CTA that leads anywhere near a cart. The single button at the bottom is "Update Your Preferences," which is a strange thing to make the email's only actionable link.
- Visually it's clean and on-brand — moody outdoor photography, the muted sage-green tile treatment, good typographic hierarchy. But as a "sport stories collection" this reads more like a blog digest than an email that drives revenue, and after ten days of genuine content pivots, this is the one where the brand fully drops the product pretense.
- Judgement: well-crafted as content, weak as marketing email — no offer, no purchase path, and the only CTA is a preference-center link that does nothing for engagement or sales.

## What's working

- Hero image (angler in waders, netted fish, misty mountain backdrop) is striking and immediately signals "outdoor life," on-brand for Patagonia.
- Six-tile grid layout is scannable — each has a bold category label, a one-line hook, and a matching photo.
- Photo variety (climbing, surfing, trail running, beavers) gives a sense of breadth across "sport communities" without feeling repetitive.
- Clean typography and generous whitespace keep it from feeling cluttered despite six content blocks.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text is legible, no overlap.

## What's weak

- Zero product, zero offer, zero price — nothing here converts to a sale.
- Primary (only) button is "Update Your Preferences" — an odd, low-value CTA to spotlight as the sole action.
- Each story tile isn't clickable-obvious as a CTA — no "Read More" or button per tile, just a hope that the headline text is a hyperlink.
- "Honoring the Mountains" tile has a distracting lens-flare/bokeh overlay that looks like a rendering artifact at first glance.
- Six almost-equal-weight tiles with no ranking dilute attention — nothing tells the reader which story to read first.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real CTA per tile ("Read the Story," "Watch the Film") instead of relying on implicit link text — right now nothing signals these are clickable.
- 2. Promote the single strongest story (Moana Whyte's surf piece, given the vivid wave photo) to a full-width hero treatment above the grid, then let the rest be a secondary "more stories" list — right now all six compete equally.
- 3. Swap the "Update Your Preferences" button for something like "Explore All Stories" linking to a content hub — the preferences CTA wastes the one clickable slot in the email on a non-engagement action.
- 4. Fix or remove the bokeh/lens-flare overlay on the "Honoring the Mountains" image — it reads as a glitch, not a stylistic choice.
- 5. Given this is the eleventh content-only send in a row, mix in at least one merchandising module (even a "gear these stories" strip) to give the email a revenue path.
