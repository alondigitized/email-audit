- The three-tier layout (down, midlayer, baselayer) matches the subject and teaches the layering concept at a glance.
- Each product block has a distinct color field, large wordmark-style product name and one benefit line, so it scans well on mobile.
- Button copy on the three category CTAs ("Shop All Down", "Shop All Midlayers", "Shop All Baselayers") is specific and matches the category above it.
- Product benefit lines are concrete: "Abrasion-resistant and lightweight", "Exceptionally breathable", "Quick-drying, sweat-wicking".
- The render is clean, with no broken images, overlaps or layout bugs. The alpine climbing imagery fits the season.

## What's weak

- No visible price, discount, member benefit or deadline, so there's no urgency. The free-shipping strip is small and its qualifiers sit in the footer.
- The hero headline "Warmth All the Way Down" and the "Get Down" button are vague next to the subject's "best layers" promise. "Get Down" reads oddly and doesn't say what you'll get.
- The Colin Haley essay block ("6,000 words about dressing for alpine climbing") is a long-form editorial tile that competes with the shopping CTAs.
- The footer stacks a photo credit, an "Earth is now our only shareholder" tile, free-shipping fine print and the legal block, which makes the bottom third feel heavy and cluttered.
- The pale buttons ("Shop All Midlayers" in tan, "Shop All Baselayers" in maroon) have modest contrast against their fields.
- There are no reviews, ratings or fit and size cues anywhere.

- 2. Make the hero headline match the subject, for example "The Right Layers Under Your Down", and rename the first CTA to "Shop Layering Essentials".
