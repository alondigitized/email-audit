# Black Hole duffels, built to travel tough
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, product-first Patagonia send — hero shot of a climber hauling a duffel up a cliff face, "Built to Travel Tough," then a tidy size-by-size lineup (70L/55L/40L/Cube 3L) with clear use-case labels (weeklong excursions, weekend trips, overnighter). It's the most on-subject-line email in this brand's recent run: unlike the Aug 17 ski-gear-under-a-"summer sale"-subject mismatch or the Aug 15/12 clearance repeats, the subject promises duffels and the top two-thirds delivers exactly that.
- Where it falls apart is the back half: a "Missing Something? Now Up to 50% Off" sale block gets bolted on below the duffel lineup, pivoting away from the stated subject entirely, followed by a Field Report content module and a "Earth is now our only shareholder" footer message. It's scope creep within one email — three different asks competing for the same click.
- Solid top-of-funnel execution, diluted by an unfocused back half. A subscriber who came for duffels gets duffels, but the email doesn't know when to stop.

## What's working

- Subject and hero image match perfectly — climber hauling gear, "Built to Travel Tough" headline, zero seasonal mismatch (a recurring problem in this brand's recent sends).
- Product grid is well organized: each duffel size has a name, use-case line ("Overnighter," "Weekend trips"), a "Shop" link, and a clean product photo.
- "Browse Black Hole Bags" gives a clear category-level CTA after the size breakdown.
- No render bugs — images loaded, text is legible, layout is intact top to bottom.

## What's weak

- The "Missing Something? Now Up to 50% Off" block completely changes subject mid-email — it has nothing to do with duffels and dilutes the primary CTA.
- Three competing CTAs by the end (Browse Black Hole Bags / Shop the Sale / Watch the Field Report) — no single clear next action.
- No price shown anywhere on the actual duffel products — a bag-shopper has to click through blind on cost.
- Free shipping banner at top ("Orders over $99*") is generic boilerplate, not tied to this product story.

- 2. Add price points under each duffel size (70L/55L/40L) — sizing + price together is what actually drives a click for a considered-purchase item like luggage.
- 3. Move "Browse Black Hole Bags" to be the only CTA before any secondary content — right now it's sandwiched between the grid and the sale block, weakening its primacy.
- 4. Drop or move the Field Report module to a separate send — it's a content/brand-story email, not a product email, and doesn't belong after a sale pivot.
