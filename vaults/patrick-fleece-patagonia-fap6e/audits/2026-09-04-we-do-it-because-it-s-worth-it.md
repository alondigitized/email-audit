# We do it because it’s worth it
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-04**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuine departure from every Patagonia send audited this month — no navy/cream sale typography, no "50% off," just a full brand-story piece about surf gear R&D. It's the "Black Hole duffels" playbook applied to surf: a huge wave hero shot, "Worth it." typeset over it, then a "Relentlessly Refined" section walking through the PSI Vest 2.0 and Regulator Wetsuits with real engineering copy (thermal research, flush analysis). It closes with an "Out the Back" surf tour promo and the standing "Earth is now our only shareholder" footer link to Yvon's letter.
- This is Patagonia doing what it does best — mission-driven storytelling wrapped around two specific products — but it is not a shoppable, high-conversion email. There's no discount, no price, no urgency, and the primary CTA is "WATCH" (a video), not "SHOP." For a subscriber who wants deals, this reads as a beautiful ad, not an offer. For someone who cares about the brand's craft narrative, it's one of the stronger sends in the recent history.

## What's working

- The hero wave photo is genuinely striking and the "Worth it." wordmark sits cleanly on the wave's shoulder with no overlap or legibility issues.
- Two-product spotlight (PSI Vest 2.0, Regulator Wetsuits) each get a specific engineering detail rather than generic marketing fluff — "thermal research, flush analysis and movement studies."
- Each product tile has its own labeled CTA pill ("PSI VEST 2.0," "WETSUITS") instead of one vague catch-all button.
- "Out the Back" surf tour section is a clear, distinct secondary CTA with concrete event types (storytelling, wetsuit repairs, ambassador panels).
- Photo caption line ("Oahu, Hawai'i. Daniel Russo... shot 15 miles offshore") adds authenticity and credits the athlete/photographer, reinforcing brand trust.

## What's weak

- No offer anywhere in the body — the only discount signal is the small "Free Shipping on Orders over $99*" utility bar at the very top, easy to miss.
- Primary CTA is "WATCH," which routes attention to a video rather than a product page — high friction for anyone who opened hoping to shop.
- No price, sizing, or availability shown for either featured product, so a click leads to more research, not a near-purchase.
- Subject "We do it because it's worth it" is abstract brand copy, not concrete — gives no hint this email is about surf gear specifically until you open it.
- Product photography (vest, wetsuit) is on flat white studio backgrounds, a jarring cut from the dramatic ocean photography surrounding it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap "WATCH" for a shoppable primary CTA like "SHOP SURF GEAR" — if the video matters, make it a secondary link under the hero, not the only button.
- 2. Add a concrete subject line referencing surf gear so this doesn't blur into generic brand-mission emails in the inbox.
- 3. Add price or "new for Fall" framing to the two product tiles — even without a discount, a starting price reduces click friction.
- 4. Tighten the preview text so it teases the surf-gear story instead of being invisible/junk.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New surf gear, built for 100 duck dives a day`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The wetsuit and vest we rebuilt from scratch`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `PSI Vest 2.0 and new Regulator Wetsuits, tested in the lineup`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Years of R&D, one goal: gear that survives the ocean`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
The email fails automated link probing across nearly every clickable element (11 of 12 probed links returning 404/403), and is missing standard compliance and authentication headers.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 10 of 12 probed links return HTTP 404, and 1 returns HTTP 403, across all major CTAs: "Patagonia" logo, "Worth it.", "PSI Vest 2.0" (both instances), "Men's Regulator 4/3 Hooded Fullsuit", the surfer hero image link, "The Patagonia Surf Tour - Out the Back" (403), "Earth is now our only shareholder. Read Yvon's Letter", "Free Shipping on Orders over $99*", "WATCH", and "WETSUITS".
- All broken links route through the same ESP click-tracking redirector (`trk.na.patagonia.com/ls/click?upn=...`), indicating the tracking/redirect layer itself is failing to resolve to destination URLs rather than a per-link authoring error.
- The open-tracking pixel (`https://trk.na.patagonia.com/wf/open?upn=...`) is present and correctly placed but is missing `alt` text (flagged separately under Rendering & Accessibility).
- Given the breadth of failures (nearly every CTA in the email), this points to an ESP-side tracking domain/redirect misconfiguration or an expired/invalid `upn` signing key rather than isolated bad links.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- `<img>` at `src="https://trk.na.patagonia.com/wf/open?upn=..."` (the open-tracking pixel) is missing `alt` text. Low visual impact since it's a 1x1 tracking pixel, but flagged by automated QA.
- Preheader hack uses zero-width non-joiner + `&nbsp;` padding — implemented correctly, no issue.
- MSO conditional styles and webfont `@font-face` declarations with fallback stacks (Palatino/Georgia, Helvetica/sans-serif) are present — no issues found.
- No other missing alt text or malformed markup identified in the provided source.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found — no unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) or personalization placeholders detected in the provided HTML.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not found. Note: QA flags this may be a relay-capture limitation (AgentMail) rather than a confirmed absence — cannot fully confirm from headers alone.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058, one-click unsubscribe) not found — same caveat applies.
- `Authentication-Results` header not found — SPF/DKIM pass/fail status cannot be verified via the AgentMail relay.
- An unsubscribe link is confirmed present in the footer per QA notes (deprioritized from probing, not confirmed reachable).

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Cannot evaluate UTM parameter presence or landing-page alignment — all destination URLs are wrapped in opaque ESP redirect tokens (`trk.na.patagonia.com/ls/click?upn=...`), and the underlying destination URLs are not resolvable from the provided source. Additionally, the redirects themselves are returning 404/403 (see Section 2), so no landing page can be evaluated at all.

7. Recommendations
- Priority 1: Investigate the click-tracking redirector — the near-total failure rate (10/12 links, spanning header, hero, product, and footer CTAs) strongly suggests a broken/expired signing token or a redirect-domain outage on the ESP side, not individual link authoring errors. Confirm with the ESP (appears to be an Oracle Responsys-style `trk.na.patagonia.com/ls/click` pattern) whether the campaign's tracking domain or `upn` token is valid.
- Priority 2: Add `alt=""` (explicitly empty, since it's a non-decorative tracking pixel with no informational content) to the `wf/open` tracking pixel `<img>` tag.
- Priority 3: Verify `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and SPF/DKIM `Authentication-Results` are actually being sent by the origin MTA — re-test through a non-relay capture path to rule out AgentMail relay stripping before concluding they're absent.
- Priority 4: Once links resolve, re-verify UTM parameters and landing-page-to-email message alignment, which could not be assessed in this pass.
