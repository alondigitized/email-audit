# New winter layers have landed
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent, on-brand winter catalog send. The hero is strong: a person lacing boots in front of a weathered wall, with "Batten Down the Hatches" in large type over a sage panel. The copy is witty ("Booger-freezing cold") and sets a seasonal tone. After that it turns into a catalog of four down and insulated products, with a paired Men's/Women's button after each pair.
- The email has no price, no promotion, and no single hero product. Every section has two or more buttons, so nothing stands out as the one thing to click. It is the fourth catalog-style send in the last two weeks, after the Nov 5 Nano-Air Light launch, the Nov 4 Gift Guide, and the Nov 1 rainwear send. The "Generations of Layers" essay at the bottom is good brand storytelling, but it sits below roughly 4,000px of product grid. Verdict: pleasant and trustworthy, but it won't move anyone who isn't already planning a winter jacket purchase.

## What's working

- The hero image and headline are seasonal and memorable, and the voice is distinctly Patagonia.
- The sage background and white outlined buttons keep the layout consistent and easy to scan.
- Each product pair has a caption naming the item, such as Men's Jackson Glacier Parka and Women's Fitz Roy Down Parka.
- The product photos are clean, and the colors (charcoal, dusty rose, seafoam, burgundy) look good together.
- The "Generations of Layers" story block fits the brand's heritage positioning and gives non-buyers something to read.

## What's weak

- No price or promotion is visible. The only incentive is the "Free Shipping on Orders over $99" banner at the very top.
- There are about eight outlined buttons across the email, which dilutes the primary CTA. "Explore New Arrivals" duplicates the "Men's/Women's New Arrivals" buttons just under the hero.
- The product images are not clearly tied to the buttons below them. The captions name a specific item, but the buttons point to category pages.
- The hero text overlaps the bottom of the photo, with the headline sitting right at the image edge. It reads, but it looks a little tight.
- There is no review or social proof, and no sizing, fit or warmth detail on any jacket.
- The infant and kids' items make the targeting broad.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the button count and make the hero offer a single product story. Pick one jacket, such as the Fitz Roy Down Parka, and use one "Shop the Parka" button.
- 2. Add a reason to act now, such as a price, a member benefit, or a stock or shipping cutoff.
- 3. Add one-line details under each product (fill power, price, "Fair Trade Certified", or a star rating).
- 4. Link each product image and caption directly to its product page.
- 5. Move the "Generations of Layers" story higher, or shorten the product grid to two items.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Down for the deep freeze: new parkas`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Parkas, down sweaters and kids' layers, plus free shipping over $99.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Bone-chilling wind? Here's what we'd wear.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title>`:** `<title></title>` is in the `<head>`. It affects web-view/archive rendering and some assistive tech, and it is a real issue.
- **Tracking pixel without alt:** the only image flagged is the open-tracking pixel, `https://trk.na.patagonia.com/wf/open?upn=u001...`. It carries no content, so the impact is low. It should still have `alt=""` so screen readers skip it. This is the QA check's only image finding.
- **Redundant CSS:** the `<body>` style repeats `margin`, `width` and `-webkit-text-size-adjust`, and the preheader table repeats `border-collapse:collapse` three times. This is harmless but unclean.
- **Preheader:** it is correctly hidden (`display:none; max-height:0; overflow:hidden`), with a `&zwnj;&nbsp;` padding hack to stop body text leaking into the preview. No issues found.
- **Fonts:** web fonts are hosted on `images.patagonia.com` with `font-display: swap`. `.Serif` and `.NoSerif` classes are defined for Outlook (MSO) fallback. No issues found.
