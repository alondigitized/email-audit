# Our summer sale is half off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-24**
## Executive summary

- This is a strong, coherent send — the fifth "sale" email in three weeks, but the first one where the subject, hero copy, and creative all actually line up: "Our summer sale is half off" → "Feeling Half Off?" → "up to 50% off." No seasonal mismatch, no bait-and-switch pivot mid-scroll like the Aug 12 send.
- The escalation from 40% (Aug 5–15 sends) to 50% here is a real, visible step-up, and the product lineup — a black quilted hooded jacket flanked by two colorful fleece/shell options — gives the eye something concrete to land on rather than a vague lifestyle shot. The "Where the Sale Comes Alive" retail-store block is a nice, differentiated CTA (Find a Store) rather than just a second "Shop the Sale" repeat, though it does compete for attention with the actual sale push.
- Judgment: this is Patagonia's best sale creative of the run, but it's still the fifth near-identical "up to X% off past-season styles" email in under three weeks, and fatigue is the real risk here, not execution.

## What's working

- Subject/hero alignment: "half off" subject, "Feeling Half Off?" headline, and "up to 50% off**" callout are all consistent — no mismatch.
- Clear visual hierarchy: hero photo → offer headline → CTA button → product shot → second CTA, in a clean top-to-bottom flow.
- Free shipping banner ($99+) is visible above the fold as a secondary incentive.
- Product imagery is sale-relevant (jacket + two patterned outerwear pieces) rather than generic lifestyle-only shots.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text doesn't overlap, buttons are legible.

## What's weak

- Both CTA buttons say "Shop the Sale" — no differentiation, and neither is linked to a specific product shown above it.
- The asterisk on the 50% off claim ("up to 50% off**") isn't resolved anywhere visible in the main body — the real terms are buried in small gray legal text at the very bottom.
- This is the fifth similar clearance email since Aug 5 (40% → 40% → 40% → 50%) with the same "past-season styles" framing each time — cadence risk is real.
- No urgency element (no deadline, no "ends Sunday") despite this being framed as a bigger discount than prior sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a credible deadline to the hero block ("Ends Monday") — this is the biggest lever missing given it's the largest discount in the campaign run.
- 2. Differentiate the two CTAs — e.g., "Shop Outerwear" under the jacket block vs. "Shop All Sale" at the top — so each has a clear, distinct job.
- 3. Move the fine print on "up to 50%" closer to the offer, or drop the double-asterisk and just state the range plainly in-line.
- 4. Since this is essentially the same past-season clearance as four prior sends, vary the lead category (Aug 15 was cycling, this is outerwear) more deliberately in the subject so subscribers don't pattern-match it as "another 40(ish)% off email."

- **Subject Alt B:** `Our biggest sale markdown yet: 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Jackets, fleece, and shells — up to 50% off past-season styles.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Bigger discount, same great gear. Shop before it's gone.`
