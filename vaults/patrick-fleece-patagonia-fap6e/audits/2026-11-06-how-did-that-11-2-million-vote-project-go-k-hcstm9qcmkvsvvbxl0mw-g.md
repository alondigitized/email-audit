# How did that 11.2 Million Vote Project go?
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- This is a thank-you and impact-report send, not a sales email. It follows the Oct 27 "Ditch clothes for the polls on Election Day" civic send. That email asked subscribers to share vote plans, and this one reports the results. Closing the loop like that builds brand trust, and it's the most distinctive creative Patagonia has sent in the recent run. The orange story card and the three illustrated quote cards hold up well.
- It only works if you actually took part in the vote-plan program. The subject, "How did that 11.2 Million Vote Project go?", is a curiosity hook. I can't tell from the render whether I'm one of the people who shared plans. If I'm not, the "you sent 500K+ vote plans" framing is confusing, and the "11.2M in Patagonia credits" line reads as a mystery. The only CTA is a small, low-contrast outlined button reading "Join Patagonia Action Works". It sits at the very bottom, after about 4,000px of scrolling, and it's a weak ask next to the strong storytelling.
- Verdict: strong brand voice and shareable content, but very little commercial value. A mid-tier engagement send that earns trust and not much else.

## What's working

- The headline "We had a wild idea this election. You ran with it." is warm and puts the reader at the center.
- The orange stat card is scannable. The white bold figures (11.2 million, 480K, $11.2M, 500K+, 30%, 25%, all 50 states) pop against the orange.
- The user quotes (grandpa, fishing buddy, skiing) give real social proof, and the illustrations match each one.
- The illustrations are consistent and distinctly Patagonia, and the circular planet image makes a memorable closer.
- Closing the loop on the Oct 27 send rewards participation and makes this feel like a conversation, not a blast.

## What's weak

- The one CTA, "Join Patagonia Action Works", is a small outlined button at the bottom with weak visual weight. It's easy to miss and ends a very long scroll.
- Nothing here is commercial: no product, no offer, no member benefit. The "$11.2M in Patagonia credits" line is the only value hook, and there's no way to use it from this email.
- The email is very tall (4,295px) and has no navigation. The stat paragraph is one dense block of text.
- Recipients who didn't take part in the vote-plan program get no entry point.
- The quote cards reuse the same orange box three times. They look good, but the repetition adds length without adding information.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the CTA bigger and higher. Use a solid, filled button and repeat it after the stat card, with specific copy such as "See how your vote plans added up" or "Redeem your $ credits".
- 2. If the $11.2M in credits is real and redeemable, link it to a credit-balance or shop page so the email has some commercial purpose.
- 3. Break the stat paragraph into 3 or 4 big-number callouts (480K, 50 states, 35 plans) so the scan takes seconds.
- 4. Cut to two quote cards, or add a "submit your own story" link to the third.
- 5. Add a secondary path for non-participants, such as "Missed it? Here's how to get involved next".

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your vote plans hit all 50 states`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `One of you sent 35 vote plans. See the full results.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `$11.2M in credits earned. Here's how the community did it.`
