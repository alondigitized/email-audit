# A few of our fall favorites
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-01**
## Executive summary

- This is Patagonia's thirteenth straight non-sale send, and it reads like a seasonal catalog page. The "Fall Favorites" hero is a flat-lay of rugby shirts and flannels on cream. Below it sits a fisherman in a dusty tan jacket, then a dark slate copy block with a single "Shop Favorites" pill. It's tidy and on-brand, and it fits October 1. But it asks the reader to browse, not to act.
- Below the hero it's a long scroll of modules: Flannels, Sweatshirts & Hoodies, a 2x2 "More Ways to Layer" grid, then a Spicy Mussels recipe and an "Earth is now our only shareholder" footer. The product modules are well built, with gendered links and short fabric copy. The only offer is a thin "Free Shipping on Orders over $99*" banner at the very top. Next to the 9/29 "Meet the Light Gust collection" launch, this one has less news and less of a hook.
- Verdict: competent, trustworthy and forgettable. It will do fine with loyal fans and won't pull in anyone else. It lands around mid-pack for this brand's recent run.

## What's working

- The "Fall Favorites" hero is a bold, uncluttered flat-lay that sets the season right away.
- Colour-blocked modules (slate, cream, mustard border) give clear section breaks in a very long email.
- Each product has separate Women's and Men's links, plus short fabric copy such as "100% Cotton in Conversion fabric" and "Regenerative Organic Certified."
- The 2x2 layering grid has a "Shop" link under every tile, so there's a direct path to each item.
- The brand voice stays consistent through the recipe and the shareholder sign-off.

## What's weak

- The free-shipping offer is a tiny banner on top and never comes back in the body, so there's no reason to buy today.
- The subject, "A few of our fall favorites," is generic, with no product, price or urgency.
- The single hero CTA, "Shop Favorites," is vague and sits below the fold on mobile, under the hero image and the fisherman photo.
- The 2x2 layering grid has no descriptions or prices, so it's just labelled thumbnails.
- The Spicy Mussels recipe module at the bottom pulls attention away from the apparel, and the dense fine print under it is hard to read.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one clear hook in the hero. That could be a named hero item (the Daily Hoodie or the Lightweight Fjord Flannel) with a price, or a real member benefit. Right now the email has none.
- 2. Put the free-shipping threshold in the hero block, next to the CTA, instead of in the top strip.
- 3. Replace "Shop Favorites" with a specific CTA such as "Shop fall flannels."
- 4. Cut the email to three modules. Drop the layering grid or merge it into the flannel and sweatshirt sections.
- 5. Move the recipe to a separate editorial send. It dilutes a product email.
- 6. Add one proof point, such as a review count or star rating, under the hero products.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based with `role="presentation"`, `lang="en"`, a hidden preheader, and no hard failures in the QA run (0 issues, 3 warnings, 57% pass rate). Header-level compliance and authentication checks could not be confirmed, and the HTML was truncated before the body and footer, so link, UTM and footer evidence is limited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links were reported among the 12 probed. The other 24 were skipped by the click-likelihood ranking, so they are unverified.
- The open pixel at `https://trk.na.patagonia.com/wf/open?upn=u001.GExYxZtjp9JruRLId0swYmR84DOXKrZ6GCNQBGkNmcka4wwV3o9qFe34O1ZtHVp5ehJJkdwIqJ…` is a standard ESP tracking beacon. It is not a defect, but it is the image flagged for missing alt text (see section 3).
- No other link or tracking issues are confirmed from the available source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in `<head>`. This is confirmed in the source. Some clients and assistive tech use the title as the document name.
- **Tracking pixel has no `alt`.** The QA check flagged it. A 1×1 beacon should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip it.
- **Duplicate inline declarations.** The `<body>` style repeats `margin`, `width` and `-webkit-text-size-adjust`, and the preheader table repeats `border-collapse:collapse` three times. This is harmless, only redundant weight.
- **Custom web fonts.** `CopernicusEmail` and `RidgewayEmail` load from `images.patagonia.com` via `@font-face`. Clients that ignore `@font-face` fall back to whatever font stack the inline styles declare, and that stack is not visible in the truncated source.
- The preheader and `&zwnj;&nbsp;` padding hack are implemented correctly. The `lang`, `role="presentation"` and Outlook (`mso`) conditional styles are all present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source. The body past the preheader was truncated, so tokens there could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** (QA WARN). The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture this header, so it may not be a sender defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found** (RFC 8058, QA WARN). The same caveat applies. If it is truly absent, one-click unsubscribe is unsupported, and Gmail and Yahoo expect it from bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found** (QA WARN). SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `funhogs@na.patagonia.com`, and the tracking domain `trk.na.patagonia.com` is on the same `na.patagonia.com` subdomain, which is consistent with domain alignment. Alignment is not verifiable here.
- The footer physical address and unsubscribe link fall in the truncated portion of the source, so they were not verified. The QA run did include an unsubscribe link in its probes.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Cannot be assessed. The visible source contains no CTA `href` values, and links go through the `trk.na.patagonia.com` redirect wrapper. UTM parameters and landing-page alignment would only show up after following the redirects.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-capture the raw message with full headers, bypassing the relay, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Treat the three warnings as unresolved until then.
2. Add a descriptive `<title>`, or at least a subject-matching one, to `<head>`.
3. Add `alt=""` (plus `width="1" height="1"`) to the open-tracking pixel.
4. Rerun the audit with an untruncated HTML source so the CTA hrefs, UTM parameters, footer address and unsubscribe markup can be checked.
5. Optional: remove the duplicated inline style declarations.
