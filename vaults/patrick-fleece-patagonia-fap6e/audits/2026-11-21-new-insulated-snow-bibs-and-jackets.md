- The hero shows two new jackets large and clearly, with the product in focus.
- The product grid is tidy: jackets and bibs side by side, men's and women's, each with a name and a "Shop" link.
- The full-bleed snowboarder photo gives the send a lifestyle moment and breaks up the grid.

- There is no price, no offer and no deadline. Free shipping over $99 is tucked into a tiny top strip.
