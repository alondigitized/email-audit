# Homepage snapshot · www.patagonia.com · 2026-06-26
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-26**
## Executive summary

- This is unmistakably Patagonia — clean, earnest, zero discount energy — and right now it's pushing Capilene Cool performance tees at a trail runner in the Alps. I respect the craft here. The photography alone is worth a pause. But I came in looking for a Snap-T or something from Worn Wear, and this page is talking to someone half my age who runs ultras, not to me.

## What's working

- **The announcement bar "Why Is Patagonia Making Food?"** — that's the one thing that actually made me stop. I care about Patagonia's activism arc and their regenerative food work is exactly the kind of brand story I read top-to-bottom. Smart use of that black bar.
- **The alpine hero photography** — the Matterhorn backdrop is genuinely arresting. Even if I'm not the target for the product, this is the kind of image that says "we actually go places," which I believe.
- **"Manage Your Microclimate" headline** — direct, functional, no marketing fluff. Capilene® is called out by name with the registered mark, which signals this is real gear talk, not a lifestyle vibe-sell.
- **Page cleanliness** — no pop-up, no modal, no GDPR wall on load. Nothing is fighting for attention except the content itself.

## What's weak

- **Zero above-the-fold CTA** — I can't see a single tappable button in this viewport. The hero copy runs off the bottom edge and there's no "Shop Now," no "Shop Capilene," nothing. A hamburger menu and a bag icon are not a call to action.
- **Wrong persona in the image** — a young woman sprinting on a scree field wearing a running vest and hat is not me. I'm a fleece-and-flannels guy who hikes, doesn't race. The demographic miss is significant.
- **No path to Worn Wear or repair** — for a loyalist who repairs first and buys used, there's no signal anywhere in this viewport that the brand still cares about that. No banner, no sub-nav, nothing.
- **Category navigation is fully buried** — one hamburger menu tap to reach anything. For my category (fleece, base layer casual, Worn Wear), I'm one mystery tap away from wherever I want to go.
- **No social proof** — no review counts, no bestseller tags, nothing that says this Capilene lineup is beloved by the community.

## Recommendations

- **Add a secondary strip below the hero** with two or three tiles — one for Worn Wear/repair, one for New Arrivals fleece, one for the activism story — so returning loyalists can self-sort fast without hunting in the hamburger menu.
- **Swap or supplement the hero with an older, male lifestyle shot** — a 40-something scrambling a technical trail in a Snap-T or R1 would land for me without abandoning the performance story for younger runners.
- **Surface the Worn Wear link in the announcement bar rotation** — cycle between "Why Is Patagonia Making Food?" and "Repair Before You Replace → Worn Wear" to serve both the curious newcomer and the loyalist who came to shop used.
- **Add one visible CTA button in the hero** — even just "Shop Capilene Cool" anchored at the bottom of the hero module. Right now the page trails off mid-sentence.
