# New graphic tees on organic cotton
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, product-led catalog send. A climber photo and the headline "Bolder with Graphics" sit over a sage-green field, followed by a vertical stack of six tees. The layout is easy to scan and the product photography is strong. It's also another non-sale Patagonia email, and it comes the day after a civic-engagement send and two days after a vintage-tee send. A subscriber has seen this "here are some shirts" beat all week, and nothing here is new to them.
- The email gives me no reason to click now. There is no price, no member benefit and no deadline. The only offer is the "Free Shipping on Orders over $50*" banner at the very top. The body is six near-identical tiles, each with a small "Shop" link, and the two big category buttons repeat above and below. The Behind the Design / Arctic fish print block and the "Earth is now our only shareholder" close add brand mood but split attention from the tees.
- Judgment: competent and on-brand, but forgettable. It's a 6 like the rest of the recent run.

## What's working

- The hero is simple. A big white headline over a moody climbing photo, with two clear category buttons (Men's / Women's Graphic Tees) right under the copy.
- The product tiles are consistent and uncluttered. Each shirt gets a clean photo, a name and a "Shop" link.
- "Organic cotton" and "layered with a sweatshirt on cool shoulder-season nights" give the product a reason and a season.
- Both men's and women's styles are shown, including the ringer tee and the long-sleeve options.
- No visible render bugs. Images load and spacing is even down the full length.

## What's weak

- There is no price, no promotion and no urgency. Free shipping is the only incentive, and it's in a tiny asterisked top banner.
- Each "Shop" link is a small, low-contrast button under the shirt. That's weak CTA copy, and the images aren't clearly the main tap target.
- The email is long, with six tiles in one column. That's a lot of scrolling on mobile for the same message, and the best tee isn't singled out.
- The Behind the Design block (Watch / Shop the Print) and the shareholder close add competing CTAs and take focus off the tees.
- The thumbnails show no price, colorway or fit detail, and there are no reviews or social proof.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one hero tee, not a generic lifestyle shot, and give it a specific reason to click. For example, name the Fitz Roy design and its story. Then cut the grid to four tiles.
- 2. Make the CTAs specific and put prices on the tiles, for example "Shop the Fitz Roy Tee – $45". Make the images clickable and the buttons higher-contrast.
- 3. Move the Behind the Design story into its own send, or make it the single secondary block, so the email has one job.
- 4. Add a proof point such as "Fair Trade Certified™ sewn" or a review snippet near the hero.
- 5. Vary the cadence. After a week of non-sale catalog sends, pair the graphic-tee message with a member perk or a limited drop.

- **Subject Alt B:** `6 new graphic tees for shoulder season`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Mountain, ocean and trail art on Fair Trade Certified™ organic cotton. Free shipping over $50.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fresh prints for cool-weather layering, from the Fitz Roy skyline to a trail-snail.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible markup is mostly well-built: it uses `lang="en"`, presentation tables, a hidden preheader with a padding hack, and responsive `@media` rules. The QA run flagged no hard failures (0 issues, 3 warnings), but the source is truncated, so the footer, links and CTAs couldn't be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage:** 12 of 28 links were probed and 16 were skipped (footer, utility and social links are deprioritized). The QA output reports no broken links among the 12 probed, but it also doesn't list their status codes. The 16 skipped links are unverified.
- **Click and open tracking:** The open pixel is `https://trk.na.patagonia.com/wf/open?upn=u001...`, which is an ESP tracking redirect on a branded subdomain. This is normal. Because of the redirect wrapping, I can't see final destination URLs in the source I was given.
- **Tracking pixel markup:** The pixel is flagged for missing alt text (see section 3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` in `<head>` is empty (confirmed in source). That's a minor accessibility and client-display issue.
- **Missing alt on the pixel:** The QA check flags the `trk.na.patagonia.com/wf/open` image for missing alt. This is a tracking pixel, so the fix is `alt=""`, not descriptive text. It's low severity.
- **Redundant CSS:** `body` repeats `margin`, `width` and `-webkit-text-size-adjust`, and the preheader table repeats `border-collapse:collapse` three times. This is harmless.
- **Fonts and Outlook:**
  - Web fonts (`CopernicusEmail`, `RidgewayEmail`) load from `images.patagonia.com` as WOFF/WOFF2 with `font-display: swap`. Most clients ignore web fonts, so fallbacks matter, but the font stacks are cut off in the truncated source.
  - An MSO conditional block sets fallbacks for Outlook (`.Serif`, `.NoSerif`).
- **Preheader:** It is present as hidden text ("Plus, meet one of our print designers.") and followed by a `&zwnj;&nbsp;` padding hack. No issues found.
- **Layout:** Tables use `role="presentation"`. No issues found.
- **Not verified:** Alt text on content images, button contrast and tap-target sizes all fall in the truncated portion.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The footer and body are truncated, so token coverage there is unverified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both headers were not found. The QA tool itself says AgentMail may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed, not a confirmed defect**. Check the raw message headers at the original recipient (Gmail "Show original") before concluding anything.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA note attributes this to the relay. I can't confirm authentication either way.
- **Sender:** The From address `funhogs@na.patagonia.com` is on the branded subdomain `na.patagonia.com`. That is consistent with the tracking domain `trk.na.patagonia.com`.
- **Footer:** The unsubscribe link and physical address were not in the visible source. The QA run says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but it doesn't report the result.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The visible source contains no outbound product or CTA hrefs, and links are likely wrapped in the `trk.na.patagonia.com` redirector. I found no evidence of UTM parameters, and no evidence of their absence. The QA data has no landing-page results to compare against the subject ("New graphic tees on organic cotton").

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add content to `<title>` (for example, the subject line).
2. Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` to the open-tracking pixel.
3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` in the original message headers. The relay may be dropping them, so don't treat the missing headers as a Patagonia defect yet.
4. Capture `Authentication-Results` from a direct delivery path, or run a DMARC check on `na.patagonia.com`.
5. Re-run the audit with the full HTML so the footer, CTA hrefs, UTMs and merge tokens can be checked.
6. Raise the link probe limit, or log status codes for the 16 skipped links, so the pass rate (57%) reflects real results.
