# Up to 40% off starts now
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-07**
## Executive summary

- This is Patagonia's first straightforward outlet/clearance sale email in the run I've reviewed — a hard swerve from the Capilene Cool Sun trilogy (July 17, 28, Aug 5) and the Aug 2 fall-launch send. "Up to 40% Off Past-Season Styles" is the clearest, most transactional offer this brand has sent all summer, and the layout backs it up: bold "Need Clothes?" headline, a mountain-shaped "Sale" graphic, four category shortcut buttons, and a white pill "Shop" CTA that's impossible to miss.
- The execution has a real flaw, though — the hero image is a shirtless guy biking next to a lake, which has nothing to do with "past-season styles" being marked down. It's a striking Patagonia lifestyle shot, but it does zero work selling the actual sale. The second half of the email ("Where the Sale Comes Alive," in-store shopping) is a nice touch for a retailer with physical stores but adds length without adding offer clarity, and the real terms (what's excluded, that it's full-priced/outlet exclusions) are buried in fine print at the very bottom.
- Net: this is a competent, clean sale email that will outperform the brand's recent product-story sends on clicks, but it under-delivers on trust because the "up to 40%" ceiling framing and buried exclusions make the offer feel softer than the bold typography suggests.

## What's working

- Single, unambiguous offer stated twice ("Up to 40% Off Past-Season Styles" + giant "Sale" mountain graphic) with no competing messages.
- Four category buttons (Women's, Men's, Kids' & Baby, Packs & Gear) let a shopper skip straight to their department — real segmentation, not just one generic link.
- White pill "Shop" CTA has strong contrast against the navy background and sits right under the offer copy.
- Secondary "Find a Store" CTA is a smart omnichannel nudge that doesn't compete with the primary online offer.
- Clean brand execution — no overlapping text, broken images, or layout breaks anywhere in the render.

## What's weak

- Hero image (shirtless cyclist by a lake) has no visual connection to "past-season styles on sale" — it's a brand lifestyle shot standing in for a product shot.
- "Up to 40%" is a ceiling-only claim with no floor ("20–40%"), and the actual exclusions ("Ineligible items include... full-priced items, gift cards...") are pushed to dense fine print at the bottom.
- No end date or urgency deadline anywhere — "starts now" implies a start but never signals when it ends.
- The "Where the Sale Comes Alive" in-store module repeats the sale message without adding new information, padding the email rather than reinforcing the CTA.
- No loyalty/member-specific pricing or perk shown, despite Patagonia having a base of repeat customers.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the hero lifestyle shot with an actual product flatlay or model wearing past-season pieces — right now the image and the offer are disconnected, which is the single biggest miss here.
- 2. Add a floor to the discount ("20–40% off") and surface the key exclusion (e.g., "excludes Worn Wear, Pro, and gift cards") right under the CTA instead of only in the footer fine print — builds trust without needing a click.
- 3. Give the sale a visible end date if one exists ("Ends 8/14") — "starts now" alone creates zero urgency to act today versus next week.
- 4. Cut or shorten the "Where the Sale Comes Alive" store module — it's off-message for a discount-driven send; save that story for a dedicated retail/community email.
- 5. Change "Shop" to a more specific CTA like "Shop the Sale" — single-word CTAs underperform verb+noun CTAs.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Sale's live: up to 40% off past-season gear`
- **Subject Alt B:** `40% off ends soon — shop past-season styles`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Women's, Men's, Kids' & Packs — all marked down`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Past-season favorites, new low prices`

- **11 of 12 probed links return 404**, all routed through the same Klaviyo redirect host `trk.na.patagonia.com/ls/click?upn=u001...` — including primary CTAs ("Shop", "Sale"), navigation links ("Women's", "Men's", "Kids' & Baby", "Packs & Gear"), body CTAs ("Need a Break?", "Need Clothes?", "Where the Sale Comes Alive"), and image links ("A person rides their bike into a lake.", "Mountain ridge graphic").

- **Net effect: essentially every actionable link in this email is dead**, making the send a non-functional broadcast from a click-through standpoint.
