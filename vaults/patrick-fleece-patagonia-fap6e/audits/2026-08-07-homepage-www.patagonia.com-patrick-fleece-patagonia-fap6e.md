# Homepage snapshot · www.patagonia.com · 2026-08-07
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-07**
## Executive summary

- First thing I get, before I've even seen the actual page, is a full-screen "Sale — Up to 40% Off" popup shoving me toward buying more stuff. That's not the Patagonia I signed up for — the company that ran "Don't Buy This Jacket" doesn't open with a hard sell. Whatever's actually on the homepage is buried behind this thing.

## What's working

- **The single close X, top right** — at least there's one obvious way out, which matters on a small phone screen when I just want the page.
- **"Shop All" as the top button** — if I did want to browse, it's the least pushy option of the bunch, not funneled into a specific sale category.
- **Category buttons (Women's, Men's, Kids' & Baby, Packs & Gear)** — plain, readable, no jargon. That much I can navigate without squinting.

## What's weak

- **The whole popup itself** — a 40%-off blast is the opposite of what I come to this brand for. I want Worn Wear, repair guides, activism updates — not a markdown wall.
- **Two X icons (top and bottom of screen)** — one closes the modal, I assume, but I can't tell what the second one does without tapping it. On a site I trust to be thoughtful, that's sloppy.
- **No mention of Worn Wear, repair, or trade-in anywhere visible** — for a company built on "buy less, use it longer," leading with a sale discount undercuts the whole pitch.
- **No actual homepage content visible** — everything behind the modal is just a sliver of background image. I have no idea what today's story or campaign is.

## Recommendations

- Kill the forced full-screen sale modal, or at minimum make it a small dismissible banner so the actual homepage — presumably with Worn Wear or activism content — is visible immediately.
- If a sale modal must run, put a Worn Wear / repair link in it too, not just "Shop All, Women's, Men's, Kids', Packs & Gear."
- Fix the duplicate close icon — one clear X, and let me see what's underneath.
- Lead the real homepage (once I get past this) with a repair, trade-in, or activism story, not further discount messaging.
