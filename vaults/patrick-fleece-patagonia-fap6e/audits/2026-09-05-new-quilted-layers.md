# New quilted layers
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a real product email, and it's Patagonia's fifth genuine pivot in a row away from the "50% off" navy/cream typography sale template. "New Quilted Layers" hits all the marks of a solid category launch: hero flat-lay of four colorways, clear product name and price context, a tidy 2x2 grid of the full collection, and consistent "Explore/Shop" CTAs throughout. It's clean, on-brand, and easy to scan.
- The problem is targeting, not execution. There's no visible price, no discount, no urgency — just "New Arrivals" and seasonal copy about leaves turning. For a persona motivated by concrete offers, this reads as a nice lookbook rather than something to act on. It also buries a second CTA ("It's a Magical World" editorial content) that dilutes the primary shopping journey. Solid craft, weak pull.

## What's working

- Hero flat-lay of four quilted layers (navy, tan, blush, rust) is visually rich and establishes the collection instantly.
- Clear product hierarchy: hero product shot → name → single "Shop" CTA, repeated cleanly for the 2x2 grid below.
- Copy specifies material details (100% Regenerative Organic Certified cotton, recycled polyester insulation) — concrete, not vague.
- No render bugs — every image loads, text is legible, buttons are consistent black/white pill style throughout.
- Free shipping banner at top gives at least one concrete offer signal ("Free Shipping on Orders over $99").

## What's weak

- No price shown anywhere on any of the five products — a shopper can't gauge whether this fits their budget without clicking through.
- No discount, promo code, or urgency of any kind — just "New Arrivals," which won't create a reason to act now versus later.
- The "It's a Magical World" editorial block mid-email (mountain photo + "Read the Story") pulls focus away from product and toward a brand-content detour before the footer's "Earth is now our only shareholder" note.
- Subject "New Quilted Layers" is generic — it doesn't signal price, season urgency, or anything beyond "new stuff exists."

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a price or "from $X" under at least the hero product — the single biggest lever for click-through, since right now there's zero purchase-decision info visible.
- 2. Cut or move the "It's a Magical World" story block below the footer, or link it separately — it competes with the shopping CTA path.
- 3. Tighten the subject line to reference the season/use-case more specifically than "New Quilted Layers."
- 4. Consider adding one line of urgency or context (e.g., "just landed for fall") to give a reason to open now.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Quilted layers just landed for fall`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your new go-to layer for cooling nights`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Regenerative Organic cotton, recycled insulation — four new colorways`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Warm, easy-wearing, built for shifting weather`
