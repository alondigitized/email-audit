# Down parkas for any kind of cold
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand parka catalog send, and it's the second pass at a campaign Patagonia already ran. The hero repeats "We've Got This Down" from the 2026-11-11 "Warm-by-nature insulation for any kind of cold" email. The subject line even reuses the "any kind of cold" phrasing. The hero is a close crop of a cream puffy parka with a plum "Explore" button. The color-blocked panels below (orange, teal, olive) give each product a clear stage, and the layout is easy to scan.
- There is no reason to act now. The only offer is "Free Shipping on Orders over $99*" in the top bar. There are no prices, no member benefits, no deadline and no reviews. It reads as editorial, and a subscriber who got the 11-11 down email and the 11-16 gift-guide email this week has already seen most of this. It follows the usual pattern of 6/10 sends: polished and brand-consistent, but low on conversion pressure.
- Verdict: a competent 5-6, with brand equity doing most of the work. A reader who already wants a parka will click. Anyone else has no reason to.

## What's working

- The color-blocked product panels (orange Durable Down Parka, teal Tres 3-in-1) give each hero product its own space and a clear visual rhythm.
- Each featured panel has a short, concrete description. For the Tres it says it works as "a waterproof/breathable shell, an insulated jacket, or an insulated parka," which is a real product benefit.
- The four-up "Explore More Styles" grid gives named products (Glacier Parka, Grade VII, Stormshadow, Fitz Roy) for a second chance at a click.
- The "Down Fill Power Explained" tile is useful education that fits a considered purchase like a parka.
- The footer line "Earth is now our only shareholder." is distinctive brand voice, and the free-shipping bar is visible at the very top.

## What's weak

- The hero headline "We've Got This Down" is white type laid over a pale cream parka. "This Down" has weak contrast and is hard to read.
- There is no price, promotion, deadline or member perk anywhere. Nothing creates urgency.
- The "Explore" buttons are vague. The "Shop Women's / Shop Men's" and "Shop" links are small and underlined, and the grid products show no prices.
- The tent and climber photo has no product to point at. Its "Explore" button leads to nothing specific.
- The hero repeats the 11-11 down send, and the cadence is heavy. This is roughly the sixth Patagonia email in nine days (11-09, 11-10, 11-11, 11-15, 11-16 twice).

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a concrete hook in the hero, such as a price point ("Parkas from $249"), a delivery date for gifts, or a stock/size-availability note. This single change would lift the email most.
- 2. Fix hero contrast. Move the headline off the pale jacket or add a dark scrim, and make the headline say something new rather than reusing "We've Got This Down."
- 3. Replace "Explore" with specific CTAs like "Shop Down Parkas" and "Compare Parkas by Warmth," and make the Shop links real buttons.
- 4. Add prices, star ratings or a warmth rating to each grid product so the grid can carry a purchase decision.
- 5. Cut the tent photo or tie it to a product, and send fewer near-duplicate down emails within the same week.
