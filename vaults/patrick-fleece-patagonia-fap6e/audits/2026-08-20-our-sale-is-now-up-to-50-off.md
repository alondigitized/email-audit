# Our sale is now up to 50% off
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-20**
## Executive summary

- This is Patagonia's cleanest sale email of the run — subject and creative finally line up without a seasonal mismatch. "Missing Something?" over a grinning backpacker in the Grand Canyon with a whiskey flask, followed by a bold mountain-silhouette "Sale" block and category buttons (Men's/Women's/Kids'/Packs & Gear), is a straightforward, well-organized clearance email. It's a small step up from the Aug 15 and Aug 12 near-identical clearance sends — the "Missing Something?" hook and the escalation to 50% off (from 40%) at least give it a fresh angle.
- The problem is the same one this brand keeps having: it's the fifth "up to X% off" clearance email since Aug 5, and the second half of the email — a full "How to Raise a Fly Child" story block — has nothing to do with the sale and pushes the CTA further down. The offer itself is also vague: "up to 50% off" with double-asterisked fine print, no specific product, no urgency/deadline anywhere visible.
- Solid execution, tired strategy — a subscriber who's opened four of these in three weeks will skim past this one.

## What's working

- Subject and hero image cohere: "Missing Something?" pairs with a person in the wild who clearly has everything, self-aware humor that lands.
- The "50% Off" callout is the highest discount in this whole sale run — genuine escalation signal if this reader has been holding out.
- Category buttons (Men's, Women's, Kids' & Baby, Packs & Gear) give an immediate, low-friction path to shop without forcing a homepage click.
- No render bugs — text is legible, images loaded, layout is clean top to bottom.
- Free shipping banner on orders over $99 is visible immediately at the top.

## What's weak

- This is the 5th clearance/sale email since Aug 5 (Aug 5, 7, 12, 15, now 20) — cadence fatigue is real and this one doesn't differentiate the offer beyond a bigger percentage.
- "Now Up to 50% Off" has no deadline or urgency — no "ends Sunday," no countdown, nothing time-bound to justify opening now vs. later.
- No specific product or hero item is shown for the sale itself — just a generic mountain/"Sale" graphic, so there's nothing concrete to click toward.
- Nearly half the email (the fly-fishing story) is unrelated editorial content that dilutes the sale CTA and delays the reader from the point.
- Double-asterisk fine print ("biggest markdowns of the year**") signals exclusions without showing them up front — feels like the fine print is doing work the email should do plainly.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a hard deadline to the hero ("Ends Sunday" or similar) — right now nothing creates urgency to act today instead of next week's near-identical email.
- 2. Cut or move the fly-fishing story out of this specific send — a pure clearance email should stay focused; save editorial content for a non-promo send to break up the clearance cadence instead of bolting it onto the same email.
- 3. Replace the generic "Sale" mountain graphic with 2-3 actual marked-down products (with strikethrough pricing) so there's something concrete to click.
- 4. Vary the creative concept from the Aug 12/15 sends — same clearance narrative five times in three weeks needs either a different offer structure (e.g., extra 20% off already-reduced) or a different framing beyond "up to X% off."

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
The email's tracking-wrapped links are returning 404s across all major CTAs, and standard compliance/authentication headers were not detected in automated checks.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
All probed links use Klaviyo click-tracking redirects (`https://trk.na.patagonia.com/ls/click?upn=...`). 11 of 11 probed material links failed with 404, including:
- "Shop the Sale", "Now Up to 50% Off", "Sale" (primary CTAs)
- "Patagonia" (header logo link)
- "Men's", "Women's", "Kids' & Baby", "Packs & Gear" (nav links)
- "Missing Something?", hero image, and hero alt-text link
This is a 100% failure rate on probed links — every tested destination is broken. Note the tracking wrapper itself may not be the fault (Klaviyo redirect infra could be down, or the underlying destination URLs it redirects to are broken); either way, the send is currently non-functional for click-through. 7 additional links (footer/utility/social) were deprioritized and not probed — status unknown.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- Open-tracking pixel (`/wf/open?upn=...`) is missing `alt` text — flagged by QA. Low-impact since it's a 1x1 tracking pixel, but worth fixing for consistency.
- MSO/Outlook conditional comments, `@import` custom font (Klaviyo-hosted), and standard `mj-` (MJML/Klaviyo-generated) responsive CSS are present and structurally normal — no malformed conditional blocks observed in the truncated source.
- No other missing-alt or broken responsive rules detected in the visible HTML.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{ }}`, `*|MERGE|*`) observed in the visible source or preheader text. No issues found.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header: not detected by automated check (may be an AgentMail relay capture limitation rather than a true absence — cannot confirm from HTML source alone).
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe): not detected — same caveat.
- `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC): not detected via relay — authentication status unverified.
- An in-body unsubscribe link is confirmed present per QA notes ("an unsubscribe link is always included"), but its target wasn't included in the probed-link failures list, so its live status is unconfirmed.
- These are flagged as warnings, not confirmed failures, given the relay-capture caveat — recommend verifying directly against raw SMTP/MIME headers rather than the relay-parsed view.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot evaluate — all CTA/nav links resolve through the Klaviyo tracking redirect (`trk.na.patagonia.com`) and none successfully resolved to a final destination URL in this check, so no UTM parameters or landing-page targets could be inspected. This is a downstream consequence of the Section 2 findings, not a separate defect.

7. Recommendations
- Priority 1: Investigate the Klaviyo click-tracking domain (`trk.na.patagonia.com`) — a 100% failure rate across 11 distinct links strongly suggests a systemic issue (expired/misconfigured tracked-link campaign, DNS/redirect service outage, or campaign sent in a broken/test state) rather than 11 independent broken destinations. Verify by curling one tracking URL directly and checking the HTTP response.
- Priority 2: Verify `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` directly against the raw email headers (not the relay-parsed view) before treating these as confirmed gaps.
- Priority 3: Add `alt=""` (empty, since it's decorative/tracking) to the open-tracking pixel `<img>` tag.
- Priority 4: Once links are restored, re-run the QA probe to confirm UTM parameters and landing-page alignment before this campaign is considered send-ready.
