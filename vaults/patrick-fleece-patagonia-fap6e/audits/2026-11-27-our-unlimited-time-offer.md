# Our unlimited time offer
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a brand-voice play, not a promo. Patagonia uses Black Friday week to say "our deal is the same every day": Worn Wear used gear, repairs, and 1% for the planet. It is witty and on-brand, and the hero is strong. An orange fleece pullover sits on teal under a huge "Unlimited time offer*" headline. The tone is confident and anti-hype, in contrast to the "Gift Guide" catalog sends from 11/17 to 11/26.
- The execution undercuts it. The offer is never stated. "What's Our Deal?" and "Explore Value" ask you to click to find out what it is. The body then runs to a large block of teal paragraph copy that mostly repeats the 1% giving line already in the orange section. The only concrete commercial hook, free shipping over $99, is a tiny strip at the very top.
- Judgement: good brand storytelling and a mediocre sales email. It will earn goodwill from loyalists but won't drive many clicks from someone shopping on 11/27.

## What's working

- The hero is bold, with a single product and a big headline. The eye lands there first.
- The contrarian stance is distinctive on Black Friday and fits Patagonia's voice.
- The dark "More bang for your buck." section breaks up the scroll. Its four-tile grid (Buy Quality, Give Back, Care & Repair, Shop Used) is clean and scannable.
- The color blocking (teal, charcoal, coral) gives clear section rhythm. I see no broken images or layout bugs.
- The Worn Wear angle ties to the fleece and outerwear the brand has been pushing in recent weeks.

## What's weak

- No actual offer appears in the hero or CTA. The asterisk on "offer*" resolves only in a wall of text near the bottom.
- The CTAs are vague. "What's Our Deal?" and "Explore Value" don't say what the click leads to.
- The long teal paragraph is a dense block of small text that nobody will read. It repeats the giving copy and says "that's not all" twice.
- The "Daily giveaways." section is misleading. The coral block shows a river-delta photo, not a giveaway, and the copy is about 1% donations, not prizes.
- The free-shipping line is too small. It is the only price-relevant hook and is easy to miss.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the offer concrete in the hero, for example "Worn Wear fleece from $49," and point the CTA at a specific used-gear collection. This lifts the email most.
- 2. Rename the CTAs to "Shop Worn Wear Fleece" and "See Repair Guides."
- 3. Cut the teal paragraph to about three sentences and move the asterisk explanation into the footer.
- 4. Rename "Daily giveaways." to "Daily giving." and use a real product image there.
- 5. Promote free shipping over $99 to a visible banner under the hero.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Black Friday? Our deal's the same every day`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Used Patagonia fleece, lower than new`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Worn Wear gear, repairs and 1% for the planet. No countdown.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Same deal today as any other day. Here's what it is.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible portion of the HTML is clean: it has `lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` layout tables, a hidden preheader and responsive media queries. The automated QA found 0 hard issues and 3 warnings, which are header-capture gaps. The HTML source was truncated, so the body, footer and most links could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Open pixel missing alt:** `https://trk.na.patagonia.com/wf/open?upn=u001...` has no `alt` attribute. Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip them and clients don't show placeholder text when images are blocked. This is low severity.
- **Probe coverage:** 12 material links were probed and 8 were skipped. The QA notes say this is by design, ranked by click likelihood. It is a coverage limit, not a defect, so link health for the 8 skipped links is unverified.
- **Link wrapping:** The pixel is served from a branded tracking subdomain (`trk.na.patagonia.com`), which is good practice. The wrapped link hosts could not be seen in the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty. This is minor, but it affects web-view and accessibility contexts.
- **Pixel alt text:** The pixel has no alt text, as noted above.
- **Positive signs:**
  - `lang="en"` on both `<html>` and the wrapper `<div>`.
  - `role="presentation"` on layout tables.
  - `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`.
  - `@font-face` rules with `font-display: swap`.
  - MSO conditional styles for Outlook.
- **Preheader:** It uses `display:none; max-height:0; overflow:hidden` plus a `&zwnj;&nbsp;` padding hack. This is standard and not a defect.
- **Not verifiable:** Font fallback stacks and image alt text for content images were beyond the truncation point.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** Both are absent from the captured headers. The QA tool itself says this may be an AgentMail relay capture limit, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so verify against the raw original message.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** Not found. This is expected via the relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The `From` domain is `na.patagonia.com`, while the tracking host is `trk.na.patagonia.com`, which suggests aligned subdomains, but this is not proven.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** The QA notes say an unsubscribe link is always included in probing. The footer was truncated, so the physical postal address cannot be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No data is available. The truncated source and QA output include no destination URLs, UTM parameters or landing-page probe results, so this cannot be assessed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt=""` to the open-tracking pixel, and set a non-empty `<title>`.
2. Check the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`. If they are present, fix the relay's header capture. If they are absent, they should be added.
3. Capture `Authentication-Results` (or run a separate SPF, DKIM and DMARC check) so authentication is verifiable.
