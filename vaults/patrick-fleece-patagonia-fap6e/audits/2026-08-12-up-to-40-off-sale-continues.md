# Up to 40% off sale continues
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-12**
## Executive summary

- Same clearance run, third time out — this is a near-repeat of the Aug 7 "Up to 40% off starts now" send, now hedging the offer with a New Arrivals pivot mid-email. The top of the funnel is strong: warm hero photo of friends camping, a big blue "More Fit with Up to 40% Off" hero block, one clear "Shop the Sale" button. But the email immediately splits its own attention — right under the sale hero it pivots to "Discover New Arrivals," a full-price Fall '26 collection, with its own "Explore" CTA. Two competing offers in one scroll, no discount visible anywhere in the product grid, and copy that has to explicitly clarify "you know our past-season gear is up to 40% off, but did you know..." — a tell that the creative itself isn't sure what it's promoting.
- It's competent production — clean layout, brand-consistent navy/cream palette, no render bugs — but strategically confused, and it's the third "40% off" beat inside a week for a subscriber who's already seen this exact hero treatment on Aug 7.

## What's working

- Single unambiguous primary CTA ("Shop the Sale") sits directly under the hero offer, no scrolling needed.
- Hero photo (friends camping/laughing on a rocky shore) is warm, lifestyle-driven, on-brand — distinct from the Aug 11 action-leap and Aug 5 studio shots.
- Free shipping banner ($99+) is visible above the fold as a secondary hook.
- Clean visual hierarchy in the top third: photo → offer → button, no clutter.
- Consistent brand palette (navy, cream, white) and typography throughout.

## What's weak

- Two competing offers in one email: a 40%-off sale hero immediately followed by a full-price "Discover New Arrivals" grid — no discount % or price shown on any of the six featured products.
- Body copy has to over-explain itself ("You know our past-season gear is up to 40% off, but did you know...") — a sign the message is muddled, not tight.
- This is the second "40% off" subject/hero combo in five days (Aug 7: "Up to 40% off starts now"), with near-identical hero copy — high risk of subscriber fatigue.
- No urgency signal (no end date, no "ends tonight" language) despite this being framed as a sale that "continues."
- No product-level detail (price, sizing, availability) on the six New Arrivals items — just image + name-less grid.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Split the offer.** Send the clearance sale and the New Arrivals launch as two separate emails — combining them dilutes both and forces awkward transitional copy.
- 2. If keeping them combined, drop actual sale pricing under 1-2 hero clearance products instead of a vague "up to 40%" banner — concreteness converts better than a range.
- 3. Add a real deadline ("Sale ends Sunday") to justify the "continues" framing in the subject — right now there's no urgency anywhere in the body.
- 4. Since this is the second near-identical "40% off" hero/CTA pairing in a week, rotate the creative — new photo is good, but vary the hero copy and button treatment so repeat opens don't feel like déjà vu.
- 5. Label the New Arrivals grid items (product names, at minimum) so the CTA has something concrete to click toward.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 40% off picks are almost gone`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Fall '26 just landed — plus 40% off past-season`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New arrivals are here. Past-season markdowns won't last.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping on $99+, savings up to 40% off select gear.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

This is a Klaviyo-generated MJML/HTML email from Patagonia with functional inline CSS and MSO conditionals, but 8 of its tracked links return 404s and required compliance headers are absent from the transmitted message.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **7 of 8 tested links return broken redirects.** All CTA and image links route through Klaviyo's click-tracking redirector (`https://trk.na.patagonia.com/ls/click?upn=u001...`) and fail with 404 on resolution, including:
  - "Shop the Sale" (hero CTA)
  - "Discover New Arrivals"
  - "Explore"
  - "Patagonia." (footer/logo link)
  - "Three people wearing rain and running gear sit on a log..." (image link)
  - "Earth is now our only shareholder. Read Yvon's Letter"
  - "Free Shipping on Orders over $99*"
- **1 link fails differently**: "Patagonia.com" returns a redirect response with no `Location` header, so the client cannot follow it at all.
- Given the volume and consistency of failures across distinct `upn` payloads, this points to a broken or expired Klaviyo tracked-link campaign/domain mapping rather than isolated content errors — every primary CTA in the email is non-functional.
- Open-tracking pixel is present and correctly formed: `https://trk.na.patagonia.com/wf/open?upn=u001...` (loads as a 1×1, but see Rendering & Accessibility for its missing `alt`).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- Preheader text block is correctly hidden (`display:none;font-size:1px;color:#ffffff;...max-height:0px;max-width:0px;opacity:0;overflow:hidden`) and padded with zero-width joiners/soft hyphens for Gmail clipping — implemented correctly.
- MSO conditional comments (`<!--[if mso]>`, `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`) and Outlook-specific fixes (`mso-table-lspace`, `.mj-outlook-group-fix`) are present, indicating standard Outlook-compatibility handling.
- Responsive breakpoints via `@media (max-width: 480px)` are defined for column stacking, font sizing, and mobile-only/desktop-only toggles — structurally sound.
- **Accessibility issue**: the open-tracking pixel (`wf/open?upn=...`) is missing `alt` text. Low severity since it's a 1×1 tracking pixel, not user-facing content, but flagged per automated QA.
- Custom web font loaded via `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/YgU9me/custom_fonts.css)` — `@import` in `<style>` is not supported by Outlook/Windows Mail and several webmail clients that strip `<style>` blocks entirely; no fallback web-safe stack is layered ahead of it for those clients (h1/h4 do specify `Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif` as fallback in the font stack itself, which mitigates this).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tags (e.g., `{{ }}`, `{% %}`) or fallback-default artifacts found in the visible/truncated HTML source. No issues found.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** — required for one-click unsubscribe support in Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender requirements; may be a capture gap in the AgentMail relay rather than absence at origin, but cannot be confirmed from available data.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found** (RFC 8058) — one-click unsubscribe (POST-based) cannot be confirmed as supported.
- **Authentication-Results header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unverifiable via the relay; cannot confirm sender authentication for `na.patagonia.com`.
- No visible unsubscribe link/footer was present in the truncated HTML sample provided, so in-body CAN-SPAM footer compliance (physical address, unsubscribe link) could not be evaluated — flag as unverified rather than failing.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

All outbound links are wrapped in Klaviyo click-tracking redirects (`trk.na.patagonia.com/ls/click?upn=...`), which obscure the final destination URL and any UTM parameters until resolved server-side. Since 7 of 8 of these redirects 404 (see Section 2), landing-page/UTM alignment cannot be verified for those links — the continuity chain is broken at the redirect layer before UTM parameters would even become relevant.

## 7. Recommendations

1. **Priority 1**: Investigate the Klaviyo tracked-link/redirect configuration immediately — 7 of 8 links are 404ing and 1 is missing its `Location` header, meaning essentially every CTA in this campaign is non-functional for recipients.
2. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are present at the originating ESP (Klaviyo) and are not being dropped by the AgentMail relay — verify with a raw header capture at the relay boundary.
3. Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC results independently (e.g., via a direct inbox provider check) since `Authentication-Results` wasn't observable through this relay.
4. Add `alt=""` to the open-tracking pixel for markup hygiene (cosmetic, non-blocking).
5. Verify whether `@import`-loaded custom fonts have adequate fallback rendering in Outlook/Windows Mail clients that ignore `<style>` blocks.
