Three tall color-blocked tiles follow: Lightweight (Warm), Midweight (Warmer) and Thermal Weight (Warmest). The "Where the Toad Still Sings" story closes it out. ## What's working

- The "Lightweight / Warmer / Thermal Weight" tiles with Warm, Warmer and Warmest tags make the choice quick to scan.
- The black hero panel with the large white headline gives the eye a clear landing point before the copy.
- Short body copy ("wick moisture and dry fast… prevent sweaty-to-frozen") gives a real functional reason to buy.
- The CTA is repeated: "Explore Capilene Baselayers" appears above and below the tiles. The Midweight tile photo shows the camo pullover clearly.
- It fits the season. Baselayer messaging lands well in late October.
- I see no render bugs. Images load, text is legible and nothing overlaps.

## What's weak

- The subject "Ready for anything" says nothing about baselayers, cold or skiing, so it gives the reader no reason to open.
- There's no price, member benefit or deadline. The free-shipping line is tiny and carries an asterisk.
- The CTA buttons are small, dark-on-dark pills. They are low-contrast and easy to miss against the black field.
- The tiles show no price, color options or reviews. Each arrow just promises more without telling you anything.
- The Toad story at the bottom competes with the commerce goal. It dilutes the email and pushes the footer further down.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject carry the product and the benefit. The body already says it, and the subject should too.
- 2. Make the CTA a high-contrast filled button (white or rust on black), and make it larger.
- 3. Add a "From $XX" price or one or two review stars on each weight tile.
- 4. Move the Toad story to a separate editorial send, or put it far below a compact footer.
- 5. Lead the free-shipping bar with a real threshold and a deadline if there is one.
