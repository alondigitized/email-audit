# Meet the Heyes Peak collection: made for mixed weather
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, product-led catalog send for the Heyes Peak insulated parka and jacket line. The "Peak Layering Season" headline sits on a cream field under a cabin hero photo. Below it, four product blocks alternate between mustard, sage and slate backgrounds. The seasonal timing is right, and the subject's "mixed weather" angle is more specific than most of Patagonia's recent subject lines.
- The email gives no reason to act today. It has no offer, deadline or member benefit, and it is Patagonia's sixteenth straight non-sale send. It follows the 10/05 layering guide and the 10/08 graphic tees, with the same minimalist layout and soft "Shop" links. The hero is a rustic cabin photo rather than a jacket, so the product doesn't appear until the first scroll. The product blocks themselves are polished, but the CTAs are small outlined buttons or plain text links.
- Overall it is competent and on-brand, and it won't drive urgency. It is a 6, in line with the rest of the recent run.

## What's working

- The "Peak Layering Season" headline ties directly to the subject and to the seasonal moment.
- The alternating background colors make the four product blocks easy to scan.
- Each product has a clear name (Women's Heyes Peak Parka, Men's 3-in-1 Parka and so on) with a short, useful benefit line, such as "wear it as an insulated waterproof parka in cold, wet weather."
- Both women's and men's lines are covered, with separate CTAs.
- The "It's a Magical World" editorial footer fits Patagonia's brand voice.

## What's weak

- There is no offer, price, member perk or deadline, so nothing makes the click urgent.
- The hero is a cabin photo with no product in it, so the first screen is mood rather than merchandise.
- The CTAs are weak: lowercase "Shop" text links and small outlined buttons that look secondary against the large imagery.
- The Men's 3-in-1 block has a "Shop" link but no benefit copy, which is inconsistent with the other blocks.
- The footer fine print is dense and tiny, and the free-shipping threshold is easy to miss.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a hero product (the 3-in-1 Parka) above the fold with one solid, high-contrast "Shop the Parka" button.
- 2. Add a reason to act now: a stock or season note ("Sizes selling through"), or a free-shipping or Worn Wear angle.
- 3. Give every product block the same structure: name, one-line benefit, price and a filled button.
- 4. Add review stars or a warmth or waterproof rating as social proof and a product detail.
- 5. Replace the generic "Shop" links with specific copy such as "Shop the Parka".
