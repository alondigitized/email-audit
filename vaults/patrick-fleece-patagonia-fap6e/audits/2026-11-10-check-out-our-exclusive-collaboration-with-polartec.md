# Check out our exclusive collaboration with Polartec
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, confident product-story email for the Re-Tool Air fleece. The hero is a tight crop of an orange textured pullover with a huge "Re-Tool Air" wordmark and the Patagonia × Polartec co-brand lockup, so the eye lands on the product first. The photo grid and the "Wears Like Air, Warms Like Fleece" line add some personality.
- It's a brand-story send, not a sales send. There is no price, no discount and no member benefit. The only commercial hook is a free-shipping bar at the top, and that needs a $99 minimum. Compare the Nov 5 "Nano-Air Light" launch, which was also a single-product push, and the Nov 9 winter layers send. This one has a similar shape and a similar ceiling. In my read it's a 5.
- The links are spread thin: two "EXPLORE" links, "SHOP MEN'S" and "SHOP WOMEN'S", and "Read Yvon's Letter". The vague "EXPLORE" CTAs and the long, editorial body block weaken the click path. A buyer can't tell what the fleece costs or why they should act today.

## What's working

- The hero is strong. The orange pullover, the co-brand lockup and the large "Re-Tool Air" wordmark make the product obvious at a glance.
- The four-image grid shows the fabric in use: climbing, a backlit translucent layer, a fabric close-up and a red-lit night shot. It fits the "Wears Like Air" claim.
- "Wears Like Air, Warms Like Fleece" is a short, memorable benefit line.
- The "SHOP MEN'S / SHOP WOMEN'S" split is easy to use and matches the brand's usual pattern.
- The "Earth is now our only shareholder." footer ties the send back to Patagonia's brand voice.

## What's weak

- There is no price, no offer and no urgency. The only incentive is the free-shipping bar, and it carries an asterisk and a $99 threshold.
- Both "EXPLORE" CTAs are plain underlined text, and "EXPLORE" says nothing about the destination. They're easy to miss and carry little pull.
- The body block in the sage panel is a dense, centered paragraph of low-contrast white text on grey-green. It's tiring to read, and it's the weakest part of the render.
- The hero image is cropped at the chin and never shows the whole garment. The grid images are moody but don't show the product clearly, apart from the translucent shot.
- There are no reviews, no sizing or fit detail, and no product names or prices anywhere. Nothing links to a specific product.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the vague "EXPLORE" links with real buttons labeled "Shop the Re-Tool Air Pullover", linked to the product page rather than a collection. This is the biggest lift.
- 2. Add one product block: the garment's full name, its price and a color/size note, plus a "Free shipping over $99" reminder at the point of decision.
- 3. Cut the story paragraph to two short lines. Link the longer version out with "Read the story".
- 4. Show the whole garment on a clean background in the hero or just below it, so the buyer sees fit and shape.
- 5. Add one proof point: a review star count or "four years in the making".

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Image missing `alt`:** the open pixel above is the only image the QA flagged. Tracking pixels are usually 1×1, so this is low severity. An `alt=""` would be the correct form, and `width="1" height="1"` would help if they're absent.
- **Empty `<title></title>`:** this is in the `<head>` source. Some clients and accessibility tools use the title, and an empty one is a minor accessibility gap.
- **Redundant inline CSS:** `<body style>` repeats `margin`, `width` and `-webkit-text-size-adjust` several times. A table has `border-collapse:collapse` three times. This is harmless but adds weight.
- **Web fonts:** `CopernicusEmail` and `RidgewayEmail` load via `@font-face` from `images.patagonia.com` with `font-display: swap`. Clients that ignore `@font-face` (Gmail, Outlook desktop) fall back to the font stacks. I couldn't confirm those stacks because the body is truncated.
- **Preheader:** hidden with `display:none; max-height:0; overflow:hidden`, with a `&zwnj;&nbsp;` padding hack. This follows standard practice.
- **MSO conditional CSS:** `<!--[if gt mso 9]>` targets Outlook correctly.
- **Responsive:** breakpoints at 600px and 540px use `.fluid` and padding classes. No issues found in the visible code.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible source or in the QA data. The truncated remainder was not inspected.

## 5. Compliance
