# Up to 50% off web specials
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-25**
## Executive summary

- This is a repeat salvo of the Web Specials campaign that launched on May 15 — same offer, same percentage, near-identical subject line. The email arrives on May 25, the last day of the Outlet Sale module included below the fold, meaning most recipients who don't open same-day are looking at an expired event. The structure is clean but crowded: two commercial offers plus an editorial Stories module compete for attention, and none of the three gets proper urgency treatment.
- Compared to the May 15 launch send ("Up to 50% Off Web Specials starts now," 8/10), this reminder is weaker in every dimension — no new hook, no countdown, no differentiated angle. It's the same campaign tire with less air in it. The execution is fine; the strategy is the problem.
- The Stories module at the bottom is editorial padding that has no place in a sale-closing email. Cut it.

## What's working

- Patagonia logo + clean cream layout signals trust immediately — brand equity is doing real work here
- "Up to 50% off past-season clothing and gear—including select Spring '25 styles" is specific enough to earn a skim
- Free Shipping banner at the very top adds a value layer before the hero even loads
- "Take an Extra 50% Off" in the Outlet section is bold and scannable — stack-discount framing is a strong hook when the timing is right

## What's weak

- Outlet Sale dates (May 15–25, 2026) are expired or expiring at send time — any non-same-day open sees a dead deal
- Both primary CTAs read "Explore" — the most generic verb in email marketing; "Shop Web Specials" would do the same job with actual intent
- Subject line is nearly identical to the May 15 launch send — zero differentiation or urgency escalation after 10 days
- No end date on the Web Specials offer — "Don't let this deal slip away **" is urgency theater without a deadline
- Three content modules (Web Specials, Outlet Sale, Stories) fragment attention and dilute the sale close

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add an explicit end date to the Web Specials hero** — "Ends Sunday" or "Final 48 hours" above or inside the CTA button is the single highest-leverage change. Without a deadline the urgency copy is decoration.
- 2. **Replace "Explore" with action copy** — "Shop Web Specials" and "Find a Patagonia Outlet" cost nothing and tell the reader exactly where they're going.
- 3. **Kill the Outlet Sale module if the sale is over** — sending expired offers is a trust tax. Swap it for a product callout (e.g., two hero SKUs from Web Specials) to fill the space.
- 4. **Drop the Stories module entirely** — editorial belongs in its own send. In a sale-closing email it bleeds clicks and signals you don't believe the offer is strong enough to stand alone.
- 5. **Differentiate the subject from May 15** — the launch said "starts now"; the close should say something different.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Web Specials ending soon — up to 50% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last days: 50% off past-season gear`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Past-season clothing + gear online only. Don't wait.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Spring '25 styles included. Sale closing soon.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`<title>` is empty**
```html
<title></title>
```
Some clients (notably VoiceOver on iOS Mail) announce the document title. Missing title is an accessibility gap.

**No `<meta name="viewport">` tag**
The `<head>` includes `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` but omits:
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1">
```
Required for correct initial scale on Android Gmail and some Samsung Mail builds.

**`.woff` listed before `.woff2` in RidgewayEmail `@font-face`**
```css
src: url(RidgewaySans-Medium.woff) format("woff"),
     url(RidgewaySans-Medium.woff2) format("woff2")
```
Browsers/clients attempt formats in declaration order. `.woff2` should be first (smaller, better-supported). CopernicusEmail correctly lists only `.woff2`; RidgewayEmail does not.

**Redundant `border-collapse` on layout table**
```html
style="...border-collapse:collapse;border-collapse:collapse;border-collapse:collapse;..."
```
Triple declaration. No rendering impact, but indicates template authoring error worth cleaning up.

**Conflicting `margin` declarations on `<body>`**
```html
style="margin-right: auto; margin-left: auto; margin: 0px; ... margin: 0;"
```
`margin: 0` (last declaration) overrides `margin: auto` alignment above it. Intended behavior is `margin: 0`, so the `auto` declarations are dead code — but the ordering is fragile if someone edits the template.

**`font-display: swap` in `@font-face`**
Not harmful, but `font-display` is not respected by any current email client. Non-issue functionally; just unnecessary noise.
