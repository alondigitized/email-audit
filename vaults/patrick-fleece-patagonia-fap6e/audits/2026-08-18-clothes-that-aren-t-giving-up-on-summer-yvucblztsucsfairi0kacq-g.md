# Clothes that aren't giving up on summer
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-18**
## Executive summary

- This is Patagonia finally aligning subject and creative — "Clothes that aren't giving up on summer" matches a hero of a parent and toddler wading in a river, and the "Still Summer" headline reinforces it directly. Unlike the Aug 17 "Our summer sale continues" send that jarringly paired summer copy with a skier in full snow gear, this one is coherent top to bottom: warm-weather product (Hawaiian-print shirt, swim trunks, one-piece swimsuit, tank, hats) all genuinely reads as late-summer gear.
- The layout is clean and well-organized — three clear category blocks (Comfortably Cool, Functional Simplicity, Cap Off the Heat) each with its own CTA button, plus a surfing action photo breaking up the product grids. It's a full-price, lifestyle-led email until the very bottom, where a "Sale — Up to 40% Off Past-Season Styles**" banner appears almost as an afterthought below the fold.
- This lands as a solid, on-message product email rather than a discount blast, which is a nice change of pace from the four clearance sends in a row (Aug 5, 7, 12, 15) — but the sale banner buried at the bottom undercuts urgency and could confuse subscribers used to leading with the discount.

## What's working

- Subject and hero image are fully in sync — "Still Summer" messaging matches actual summer product and a beach/river scene, unlike the Aug 17 skier mismatch.
- Clear, scannable structure: three named sections, each ending in a specific CTA button ("Shop Warm Weather," "Shop Men's Warm Weather," "Shop Women's Warm Weather," "Shop Hats & Accessories").
- Good product variety across gender and category (shirt, swimsuit, shorts, tank, hats) without feeling cluttered.
- Strong lifestyle photography (river wading shot, wave/surfer shot, kitesurfer action shot) reinforces the "still summer" narrative.
- No render bugs — images, text, and buttons all display cleanly.

## What's weak

- The 40% off sale is buried at the very bottom with no mention earlier in the email — most readers on mobile won't scroll far enough to see it.
- No specific price call-outs on any product (no "$X" or "% off" next to the shirt, swimsuit, shorts, etc.) — everything is full-price presentation until the final banner.
- "Earth is now our only shareholder" / "Read Yvon's Letter" footer block feels like a non-sequitur tacked onto a product email, not connected to the "still summer" theme.
- Five near-identical black CTA buttons ("Shop Warm Weather," "Shop Men's Warm Weather," "Shop Women's Warm Weather," "Shop Hats & Accessories," plus "Explore") give no visual hierarchy — nothing signals which is the single most important click.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move a hint of the 40% off sale up near the top — even a small "Plus, up to 40% off past-season styles" line under the hero would recover clicks from users who don't scroll to the bottom.
- 2. Add price or "member pricing" callouts under at least the hero product (Tidal Threads Shirt) to give a concrete reason to click beyond browsing.
- 3. Cut or relocate the "Earth is now our only shareholder" footer block — it dilutes the summer-gear focus; save brand-mission messaging for a dedicated send.
- 4. Differentiate the CTA hierarchy — make one button (e.g., "Shop Warm Weather" at top) visually primary and the rest secondary/outlined, so the eye has one clear first action.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Gear that isn't ready for summer to end`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Still warm out. Still time to wear this.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Airflow, comfort, and 40% off past-season styles — all in one place.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `River days, beach nights: the shirt, the suit, the hat.`
