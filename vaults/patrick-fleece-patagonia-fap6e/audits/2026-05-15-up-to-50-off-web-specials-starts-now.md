# Up to 50% Off Web Specials starts now
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- This is Patagonia in full commercial mode — a clean, well-structured sale email with a simple promise: up to 50% off Web Specials, online only, starting today. The hierarchy works. The hero communicates the offer in one read, the segmented CTAs let subscribers self-route by category, and a secondary beat ("New Spring Styles Added") reframes the sale as a live event rather than clearance. An outlet block with a hard date range (May 15–25) adds the only real deadline in the email.
- The execution is brand-consistent and technically clean, but the email stops short of closing. No product is ever surfaced — the whole email is an invitation to browse, not a prompt to buy. That's Patagonia's style, but it costs click intent at every turn.
- Context matters here: this is the third email in five days (welcome May 11, preference center May 13, sale today May 15). For a subscriber who only just onboarded, this cadence moves fast — the sale lands before any real trust has been established, and the preference center email two days ago arguably promised a lighter touch.

## What's working

- Hero delivers the offer without friction: "Up to 50% off / Web Specials / Online Only" reads in under two seconds.
- Segmented CTAs (Men's, Women's, Kids & Baby, Packs & Gear) eliminate guesswork and let the reader route themselves immediately.
- "New Spring Styles Added" sub-section is a smart reason-why — it elevates a clearance event into a restocked sale and gives the reader something new to check.
- Spring Outlet block includes a hard date range (May 15–25, 2026) and a specific location anchor, giving that section genuine urgency the rest of the email lacks.
- Render is clean: consistent typography, no broken images, earth-palette branding held throughout.

## What's weak

- No specific product hero anywhere — the email asks you to go browse, but never hands you something to want before you arrive.
- Stacked asterisk tiers (*, **, ***) for three different offer qualifiers signals "the real deal has fine print" and undercuts the headline discount.
- The "Stories" content block (trails, postcards, people and places) lands mid-scroll in a commercial email and splits attention at exactly the wrong moment.
- Main Web Specials sale carries no end date; only the outlet block has a deadline — that asymmetry makes the hero feel open-ended and low-stakes.
- Three emails in five days from a brand that two days ago asked "what emails do you want?" is a credibility gap — this send doesn't match the preference-center promise.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a sale end date to the hero.** "Through May 25" or "This week only" turns a browse invitation into a decision. The outlet block already has a date; the main event should too.
- 2. **Surface one specific product.** A single jacket or pack — real name, original price crossed out, sale price shown — in the Men's or Women's section converts skimmers into clickers. This is the highest-leverage single change.
- 3. **Cut the Stories block.** Reserve it for a non-commercial send. In this email it's a momentum killer. The editorial content is great Patagonia — put it in the right email.
- 4. **Consolidate the asterisk footnotes.** Three tiers of fine print in one scroll looks like "up to" bait-and-switch. Either simplify the offer structure or merge them into one footnote line.
- 5. **Rewrite the outlet CTA.** "Get the Outlets" is vague. "Find My Nearest Outlet" or "Shop Outlet In-Store" is specific and action-oriented.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Web Specials: 50% off starts now — ends May 25`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Patagonia sale is live — up to 50% off, online only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Past-season gear. Deeper cuts. New spring styles just added.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sale runs through May 25. Free shipping on orders $99+.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`<title>` is empty.**
```html
<title></title>
```
Some clients and screen readers surface the title. Should contain a descriptive string matching the subject line or campaign name.

**Conflicting `-webkit-text-size-adjust` values on `<body>`.**
```
style="... -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; ... -webkit-text-size-adjust: 100%; ..."
```
`none` is declared first, then overridden by `100%` later on the same element. Last value wins so iOS rendering is unaffected in practice, but `none` is an accessibility risk (blocks user text-scaling) and signals a copy-paste authoring error.

**WOFF source order is reversed for all four RidgewayEmail `@font-face` declarations.**
```css
src: url(RidgewaySans-Medium.woff) format("woff"),
     url(RidgewaySans-Medium.woff2) format("woff2")
```
Clients load the first supported format. `.woff` is listed before `.woff2`, so clients that support both download the heavier `.woff` file. Correct order: `.woff2` first, `.woff` as fallback. CopernicusEmail correctly uses `.woff2` only.

**Preheader `<td>` carries `class="hide"`.**
```html
<td class="hide" style="...">
  <div style="display:none; max-height: 0px; overflow: hidden; ...">
    Plus, get extra discounts at our outlets.
  </div>
```
At ≤540px the `.hide` media query fires (`display:none !important`) and suppresses the entire preheader `<td>`. Preview-pane text is parsed from raw source so this doesn't break inbox snippet rendering in practice, but the structure is fragile and the `hide` class on a preheader container is incorrect.

**Redundant `border-collapse:collapse` — three times on the same element.**
```
border-collapse:collapse;border-collapse:collapse;border-collapse:collapse;
```
Harmless but confirms template authoring errors are present.

**Redundant body style properties** — `width: 100%` declared twice; `margin: 0px` and `margin: 0` both present before a third composite margin declaration.

**Developer comments are visible in rendered source.**
```html
<!--PATAGONIA EMAIL WEBFONTS - revised 020525-->
<!--Created with true tender-loving-care for patagonia email channel by HSR-->
```
Not a blocker, but exposes internal naming conventions and revision dates to anyone viewing source.
