# Homepage snapshot · www.patagonia.com · 2026-05-29
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-29**
## Executive summary

- This is exactly what Patagonia should look like — stripped down, mission-forward, no discount confetti cluttering the feed. The "Read Our Work in Progress Report" banner at the top is the first thing I see and honestly that's the only hook I needed. The "Performance Foundations" hero is clean, the mountain biking shot is kinetic, but it's pushing new gear acquisition and I'd have liked at least a whisper of the repair-first ethos somewhere above the fold.

## What's working

- **"Read Our Work in Progress Report" top banner** — I click that before I do anything else. Patagonia doing accountability journalism on itself is the whole reason I trust this brand. Leading with it is the right call.
- **Three-CTA layout ("Explore" / "Men's" / "Women's")** — "Men's" gets me where I'm going in one tap. No treasure hunt.
- **Motion-blur mountain biking hero** — viscerally active, not a mannequin catalog shot. Feels earned, not styled.
- **"Performance Foundations" headline** — terse, confident. Doesn't scream at me. Very Patagonia.
- **Clean mobile layout** — the wordmark, search, cart, and hamburger are all exactly where I expect them on mobile. Nothing fighting for space.

## What's weak

- **Zero Worn Wear / repair signal above the fold** — I'm Patrick Fleece. I'm on this site to check Worn Wear or buy something secondhand. The whole brand philosophy is "don't buy this jacket" and the homepage is quietly trying to sell me new gear. That tension isn't resolved, it's just ignored.
- **"Explore" CTA is soft** — it sits right next to two functional destination buttons and contributes nothing. Where does it go? What am I exploring? Vague CTAs are wasted real estate.
- **No activism content besides the top banner** — one thin strip about a report is the entire mission layer. The partial lifestyle image starting to peek in below the fold looks like a person at a scenic overlook, which is promising, but I can't tell what it's about yet.
- **Sub-copy "For the pursuit of your wildest days"** — generic aspirational language. Patagonia can do better than this. They've written things that made me cry. This reads like it was written by a brand without a soul.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "Explore" for a "Worn Wear" button** — give repair-first customers a direct lane, right there alongside Men's and Women's. It's a $0 ship and it'd make me feel seen.
- **Replace "For the pursuit of your wildest days" with a line that earns it** — pull something from actual activism copy or customer stories. Patagonia has a voice. Use it.
- **Add a thin secondary strip below the hero CTAs**: something like "Your gear worth repairing → Worn Wear" or a link to the current environmental campaign. One line. Doesn't need to be a module.
- **Give the activism banner a destination that isn't a dropdown** — tap the banner, go directly to the Work in Progress Report. Don't make me hunt for it.
