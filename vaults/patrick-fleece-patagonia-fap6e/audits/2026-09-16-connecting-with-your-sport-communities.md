# Connecting with your sport communities
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-16**

- This isn't a product email at all — it's a preference-center pitch disguised as a newsletter, and it breaks Patagonia's recent streak of genuine content sends. After nine straight days of real product stories (Nano Puff, Black Hole bags, Tidal Threads, polar-inspired fall styles), this send has zero products, zero offers, and zero prices. It's purely "pick your sport interests" plumbing with a single editorial story (Queering Climb Mentorship) tacked on at the bottom.
- The hero image is genuinely great — a trail runner mid-stride against a mountain backdrop is a strong, on-brand visual — and the "Explore by Sport" grid is a smart, well-executed segmentation mechanism. - Hero image (trail runner, mountain backdrop) is emotionally strong and on-brand.
- "Explore by Sport" grid is a clean, legible segmentation UI — six clear categories with tappable images.
- Clear single CTA for the segmentation mechanic ("Update Your Preferences") avoids competing asks.
- The bottom editorial story (Queering Climb Mentorship) has genuine, specific storytelling and diverse representation in the photo.

## What's weak

- No product, no price, no offer anywhere in the email — nothing to click for someone just browsing gear.
- Subject "Connecting with your sport communities" is vague and could easily read as a survey/notification, not a compelling reason to open.
- The email buries the one piece of real content (climbing mentorship story) below a long preference-center block — most readers will never scroll that far.

- Preview text isn't visible/distinct in the render — no indication it adds anything beyond the subject.
