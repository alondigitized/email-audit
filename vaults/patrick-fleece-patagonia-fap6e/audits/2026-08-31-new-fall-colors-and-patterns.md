# New fall colors and patterns

## Executive summary

- This is a genuine pivot from the sale-template rut Patagonia's been running since early August — no navy/cream typography sale banner, no "50% off," just a real product story built around "Arctic Dreaming" Fall '26 patterns inspired by polar wildlife. It's the second product-first send in this run (after the 8/22 Black Hole duffels and 8/30 sun-shirt emails), and it's the most fully-realized of the three: hero flat-lay of jackets, a clean 3x3 grid of new-arrival SKUs, category pills, a "Behind the Design" storytelling block with two founder/designer-style photos, a store-locator CTA, and the standard Yvon Chouinard "Earth is now our shareholder" close.

## What's working

- Clean, uncluttered hero flat-lay of the actual new jackets/fleece — no stock photography filler.
- The 3x3 product grid gives real product variety (jackets, shorts, polos, fleece) across a clean white background.
- Category navigation pills (Men's / Women's / Kids' & Baby / Packs & Gear) make it easy to jump to a relevant section.
- "Behind the Design" section with two "Watch" video CTAs adds a differentiated storytelling layer competitors don't typically do.
- No render bugs — fonts, spacing, and images all look clean and correctly loaded.

## What's weak

- No offer of any kind — no price, no % off, nothing that creates urgency to click today versus browsing later.
- No single hero product or "Shop This" CTA tied to the top image — the first CTA ("New Colors & Patterns") is generic.
- Preview text isn't visible/distinct from the top banner bar ("Free Shipping on Orders over $99") — that's functioning as the only preheader-like element and it's stale boilerplate, not campaign-specific.
- "Shop New Arrivals" button is buried after the design-story block, well past the fold — most people won't scroll that far.

- 1. Add a clear single hero CTA tied to the top flat-lay image (e.g., "Shop the Arctic Collection") instead of making people scroll to the vague "New Colors & Patterns" pill button — right now the top of the email has no click target for the hero.
- 2. Surface "Shop New Arrivals" earlier — duplicate it directly under the hero, not just after the storytelling section.
- 3. Give the preview text real, specific copy instead of leaving the shipping banner as the de facto preheader.
- 4. Consider a light urgency layer even without a discount — "new arrivals sell out" framing or "just dropped" language would help since there's genuinely no price incentive here.

## Full review
## 1. Overview

This is a genuine pivot from the sale-template rut Patagonia's been running since early August — no navy/cream typography sale banner, no "50% off," just a real product story built around "Arctic Dreaming" Fall '26 patterns inspired by polar wildlife. It's the second product-first send in this run (after the 8/22 Black Hole duffels and 8/30 sun-shirt emails), and it's the most fully-realized of the three: hero flat-lay of jackets, a clean 3x3 grid of new-arrival SKUs, category pills, a "Behind the Design" storytelling block with two founder/designer-style photos, a store-locator CTA, and the standard Yvon Chouinard "Earth is now our shareholder" close.
