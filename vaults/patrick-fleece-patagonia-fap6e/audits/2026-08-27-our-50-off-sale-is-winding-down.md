# Our 50% off sale is winding down
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a minimal, no-product-photography send — just typography, a mountain silhouette, and a big "Up to 50% Off." It's on-brand for Patagonia (navy/cream, mountain motif, understated) and the "sun is setting" copy ties cleanly to the subject's "winding down" urgency — that's better subject/creative alignment than the brand's run of mismatched sends in July/August. But there's zero product shown, so anyone unsure what's actually on sale has to click blind, and the "Read Yvon's Letter" / Earth-is-our-shareholder block eats prime scroll real estate that could've shown gear.
- The urgency framing here is genuinely the sharpest lever the brand has pulled in this whole run — "winding down" is more credible and less repetitive than yet another "sale is on / sale continues" subject. It's a serviceable send, but the total absence of product imagery caps how good it can be.

## What's working

- Subject ("winding down") and hero copy ("The sun is setting on our summer sale") are thematically consistent — first time in a while urgency language actually matches the creative concept.
- Clean single CTA ("Shop") with clear visual hierarchy — eye goes Sale → discount copy → button → "Up to 50% Off".
- No render bugs — mountain graphic, typography, and button all display correctly.
- Free shipping banner at top adds a second concrete offer signal.

## What's weak

- No product photography at all — after five-plus "sale" emails this month, an all-type layout with no gear shown is a missed chance to actually show what's discounted.
- "Up to 50% Off" is a vague ceiling offer with no floor or category breakdown (what's actually 50% vs. 10%?).
- Brand-values block ("Earth is now our only shareholder" / Yvon's Letter) pushes into the sale email but adds no shopping value and dilutes the CTA.
- This is at least the sixth sale-themed send in under a month — fatigue risk is real regardless of how good this particular execution is.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add one hero product shot or a 3-up grid of top discounted categories (packs, outerwear, fleece) under the "Shop" button — closing the sale with zero product visible is the single biggest gap.
- 2. Replace "Up to 50% Off" with a category-anchored callout, e.g. "Fleece & outerwear, up to 50% off" to reduce the bait-and-switch feel of an unqualified ceiling discount.
- 3. Move the "Earth is now our only shareholder" block out of this transactional email or below a second product CTA — right now it competes with the sale message instead of following it.
- 4. Add a real deadline ("Ends Sunday") since "winding down" implies urgency but gives no concrete cutoff.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: up to 50% off summer styles`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Sale ends soon — up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fleece, outerwear, and packs — while sizes last.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Online and in stores through Sunday.`

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 7 tracked links return 404** via the `trk.na.patagonia.com` click-tracking redirector (Litmus/Sailthru-style `ls/click?upn=...` wrapper):
  - "Patagonia" (header logo)
  - "Sale"
  - "Mountain ridge graphic"
  - "Shop"
  - "Up to 50% Off"
  - "Free Shipping on Orders over $99*"
  - "Patagonia.com" (footer)
- All failures share the same `trk.na.patagonia.com/ls/click?upn=u001...` pattern, suggesting either an expired/misconfigured tracking domain, a broken campaign ID in the `upn` token, or the redirector service itself being down — not isolated per-link errors. Every navigable CTA in the email is affected, which is a full click-through failure, not a partial one.
- Open-tracking pixel (`https://trk.na.patagonia.com/wf/open?upn=...`) was not flagged as broken, so the tracking domain itself may be partially reachable — worth confirming whether `/ls/click` specifically is misrouted vs. the whole subdomain.
