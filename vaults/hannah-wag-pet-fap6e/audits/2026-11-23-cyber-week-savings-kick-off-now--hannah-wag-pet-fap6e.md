# Cyber Week savings kick off now! 🥾 💥
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Cyber Week" in giant magenta letters is impossible to miss, and a dachshund hauling a plush reindeer is a good hero. But after "Up to 40% off is in your stocking" on 11/18 and "The snuggle is real" on 11/16, this is the fourth Chewy sale email in about two weeks. The headline went from 40% to 50%, and then I scrolled down and found the asterisk. The "up to 50%" is really a code ladder (CYBER10 through CYBER50) that you have to enter at checkout. That's a coupon scavenger hunt, not a sale.
- Nothing here speaks to my actual life. I have an anxious senior and a chaotic puppy, and auto-ship is non-negotiable for me. This email never mentions Autoship, not even "extra 35% off your first Autoship order" or a stack with the Cyber codes. The Chewy+ free shipping banner and "free returns for 365 days" in the footer are nice trust cues, and returns are my loyalty test. They're buried at the bottom, though.
- Would I click? Probably, because it's Chewy and I'm going to restock anyway. But I'd be clicking out of habit, not because this email earned it. I wouldn't forward it. I'd archive it and search for the real discount at checkout.

## What's working

- The "CYBER WEEK" hero is huge and unmissable, and the "Shop deals" button sits right under it.
- The dachshund with the plush reindeer is cute and on-brand, with the same festive energy as the 11/7 gingerbread toy email.
- The Dog / Cat / All deals tiles are big and tappable, so I can go straight to my category.
- "Buy 2, get 3rd FREE" is a concrete offer I can understand fast, and it suits a multi-pet household stocking up on toys and treats.
- The footer trust row (24/7 service, free returns for 365 days, Chewy+ shipping) reminds me why I stay loyal.

## What's weak

- "Up to 50% off" is a code ladder with a pile of fine print: limit 5 items per order, 3 orders per customer. I can't tell which code gets me which discount, so the offer doesn't feel honest.
- There's no Autoship callout anywhere. For me that's the biggest miss, because Autoship is the only way I buy.
- The "Flash deals / Surprise savings end soon / Reveal your deals" block is vague. I don't know what I'd be revealing, and "end soon" has no deadline.
- The "Top picks for you" strip has tiny thumbnails and no prices. It doesn't feel personalized, since nothing says it's for a senior dog or a puppy. "Shop now" is repeated four times and the targets are small on a phone.
- The Rx and Pharmacy links are tucked into a footer nav, so there's no prompt for refills or vet-Rx.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add an Autoship line under the hero, something like "Extra savings on Autoship, stacked with Cyber Week." That one change would speak straight to me.
- 2. Replace the code ladder with an automatic discount, or show a single clear code. Name the deadline (12/2) next to the offer, not in the fine print.
- 3. Make "Top picks for you" truly personalized, with real products, prices and a puppy or senior tag. Also add a "Reorder your favorites" tile.
- 4. Replace the vague "Reveal your deals" block with a real offer or a countdown.
- 5. Move free returns and Chewy+ up near the hero. Chewy does this more clearly than PetSmart, whose sale emails usually bury the grooming and services angle.
- For comparison, Petco and PetSmart also run "up to 50% off" holiday sales, but PetSmart usually pairs them with a Treats-member or grooming hook. Chewy's version is cleaner but more generic.
