# Holiday shopping: The snuggle is real
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- Okay, a French bulldog in a green-and-red bow with "UP TO 40% OFF" over it. I'll admit that's cute. But it's basically the same hero as the 11/7 send, "Save up to 40% on gifts for good pets." Two Chewy emails in nine days with the same 40% headline makes this feel like a reminder and not news. The subject line is "The snuggle is real," and nothing in the body mentions snuggling. It's a mismatch.
- The offer is clear and the layout is clean. What's missing is anything that says Chewy knows me. I have an anxious senior and a chaotic puppy, and I'm on Autoship. There's no Autoship mention, no "reorder Biscuit's food" module, and no Rx tie-in. Even "Top picks for you" shows a sushi plush, a treat bag, a cat wand and a ball. That reads as generic, not personal.
- Would I click? Probably a quick "Shop deals" tap, because Chewy is my trust brand and the return policy badge is right there. I wouldn't forward it. It's a decent promo that doesn't give me anything new.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 40% OFF" hero is huge and I can't miss it. The bulldog photo is warm and festive.
- "Shop deals" sits right under the hero in a dark navy button with strong contrast.
- The trust strip at the bottom has 24/7 support, free shipping with Chewy+, free returns for 365 days and happy pets guaranteed. Returns are my loyalty test, and Chewy puts that front and center, which PetSmart rarely does.
- The Dog / Cat / all deals tiles make it easy to jump to my category in one tap.
- The Free $10 eGift card with code APP10 is a concrete extra incentive, and the phone mockup shows what I'd get.

## What's weak

- The "snuggle" subject promises something the body never delivers. There's no cozy or holiday-snuggle theme anywhere in the email.
- There's no Autoship callout. For someone who reads subjects for "Auto-Ship," not seeing a stacked "Save on Autoship + 40% off" is a miss.
- The footnote is a wall of tiny text with a code list (EARLY10 through EARLY40). It's confusing to figure out which code gets me which discount. "Up to 40%" with a limit of 5 items per order and 3 orders is also less generous than the hero suggests.
- "Top picks for you" doesn't feel personal. The thumbnails are tiny, and there's no price, no discount and no dog or cat indicated. Cat wand and dog ball sit side by side with no segmentation.
- Two promos stack on top of each other, 40% off and the app eGift card, with different codes and deadlines. It's a bit much to parse.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put Autoship in the hero or right below it, for example "Extra 35% off your first Autoship order on top of holiday deals." Autoship is my non-negotiable and this would hit it directly.
- 2. Make "Top picks for you" truly personal: show real product names, prices and my pet's name or last-ordered item with a reorder button.
- 3. Simplify the code logic. Show one code, auto-applied if possible, and have the CTA reveal tiers instead of making me decode EARLY10 through EARLY40 in the fine print.
- 4. Match the subject to the creative. Either use a snuggle visual or change the subject to name the offer.
- 5. Add a short Rx or vet-pharmacy nudge. I only see "RX" in the nav, and a line like "Refill Biscuit's meds in 2 taps" would be useful.
