# Our best deals of the year are here 🤩
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Up to 50% OFF" is enormous, and a Boston terrier in plaid PJs on a candy-cane pillow is cute. I get it. But this is my fifth or sixth Chewy "up to 50%" email since Cyber Week started. The 11/23 "Why wait until Friday for up to 50% off?" email and the 11/24 "Cyber Week savings haul" email both promised this same number. Now that it's actually Black Friday, nothing has changed except the red "BLACK FRIDAY" tag. "Our best deals of the year" feels like a bigger claim than the creative backs up.
- The one thing that perked me up is the "ONE DAY ONLY" Free $35 eGift card on a $100 Purina order. That's a real, concrete offer, and I'd spend $100 on food anyway. But it's buried under the hero, and there's no Auto-Ship angle anywhere. I live on Auto-Ship, so a Black Friday email that ignores it feels like it's talking to a stranger.
- Would I click? Probably the Purina eGift card, but only if my dogs eat that food. Would I forward it? No. I'd skim it and archive it.

## What's working

- The giant navy "50% OFF" is unmissable, and the hierarchy is clean. My eye goes straight to the offer.
- The "ONE DAY ONLY Free $35 eGift card with your $100 order" block gives me a specific reason to act today.
- The footer trust row (24/7 customer service, free shipping with Chewy+, free returns for 365 days, happy pets guaranteed) is why I stay loyal. Returns are my loyalty test, and Chewy says it right there.
- The "Chat live now" and phone buttons make support feel human.
- The dog and cat shortcut tiles are easy to tap on my phone.

## What's weak

- There's nothing about Auto-Ship, so I don't know whether the deals stack with my 35% off first order or my recurring discount. The Auto-Ship savings math I care about most is missing.
- The "Top picks for you" row has tiny thumbnails and generic "Shop now" links. It doesn't feel personalized to a senior dog and a puppy. I see a Kong and a lamb toy, but no senior-care or puppy items.
- The fine print is a dense wall of text with a rotating list of promo codes (CYBER10 through CYBER50). That undercuts the "honest offer" feeling, and I can't tell which code gets me 50%.
- The "Shop deals" button is small compared to the huge headline, so the biggest element isn't the most tappable.
- "Up to 50%" on "toys, treats, beds, and more" says nothing about food. Food is my real spend, and the only food mention is the Purina eGift block.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add an Auto-Ship line right under the hero, like "Stack an extra 5% off on Auto-Ship." That's the biggest lift for a customer like me.
- 2. Make the hero button bigger and say what it does, e.g. "Shop 50% off toys & treats."
- 3. Personalize "Top picks" by pet profile (senior or puppy, based on past orders), with larger images and prices.
- 4. Move the Purina eGift card block up and add a countdown to midnight. The "one day only" deadline needs to feel real.
- 5. Collapse the promo codes so the email shows one clear code, or auto-apply the discount.
