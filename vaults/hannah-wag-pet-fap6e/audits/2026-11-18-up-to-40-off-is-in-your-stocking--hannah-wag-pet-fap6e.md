# Up to 40% off is in your stocking
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- Third Chewy "up to 40%" email in about two weeks, after 11/7's "Save up to 40% on gifts for good pets" and 11/16's "The snuggle is real." My first reaction is "okay, I get it." The subject has the stocking pun, but the offer is the same one I've already seen twice. Chewy is still one of the few senders I open on sight.
- The hero is mostly a big empty blue block. There's a lone purple gift box floating at the bottom of it, and it looks like an image didn't finish loading. The "Shop deals" button sits near the top, so the offer isn't hurt, but it feels unfinished. The pug and cat in holiday sweaters are the best part, and they're buried below all that blue.
- I'd probably click once, to see whether anything is actually good for my senior dog and the puppy. Forward it? No. Same promo as last week. There's no auto-ship mention anywhere, and for me that's what decides whether a Chewy deal is a deal.

## What's working

- "Up to 40% OFF" is huge and readable at a glance, with a clear "Shop deals" button.
- The pug and cat in festive sweaters are cute and match my two-pet household.
- Shop dog / Shop cat / Shop all deals tiles are easy tap targets on a phone.
- The footer trust row (24/7 service, free returns for 365 days, free shipping with Chewy+) hits my returns-as-loyalty-test priority directly.
- "Chat live now" and the phone number are reassuring if I have a question about an order.

## What's weak

- Roughly a third of the hero is empty blue with a lonely gift box. It looks broken and pushes the pug below the fold.
- The promo code fine print (EARLY10 through EARLY40) is tiny. "Up to 40%" turns out to be a tiered code system, a limit of 5 items, and a limit of 3 orders. That feels less honest than the headline suggests.
- No Auto-Ship callout or extra discount stacking. For me that's the whole reason I'm on Chewy.
- "Top picks for you" shows sushi plush, a wand toy, balls, and a puzzle box. Nothing for a senior dog, nothing for a puppy, no treats, no Rx. It doesn't feel personal.
- It's the same 40% message for the third time in two weeks, so it reads as fatigue, not urgency.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the dead space in the hero. Put the pug-and-cat photo right under the headline so the first screen has the offer and the cute factor together.
- 2. Add an Auto-Ship line, for example "Stack it with 35% off your first Auto-Ship order." That's the biggest lift for someone like me.
- 3. Show the promo code tiers plainly in the body ("Spend $X, use EARLY30") instead of hiding them in the footer.
- 4. Personalize "Top picks": puppy chews, senior joint supplements, and a reorder module for things I already buy.
- 5. Make the deadline obvious up top. The 11/23 end date is only in the fine print.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your pup's stocking, up to 40% off through 11/23`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Puppy toys + senior treats: 40% off ends Monday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack with Auto-Ship and free Chewy+ shipping. Codes inside.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Pick your tier: EARLY10 to EARLY40. Limit 5 items.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built as a standard responsive table layout with `lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag, MSO conditionals and a hidden preheader. Its main confirmed defects are an empty `<title>`, missing alt text on six images (two of them linked), and click-tracking URLs that return 429 to the QA prober, so none of them could be validated. The HTML was truncated, so footer and compliance markup could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probing was incomplete.**
  - 12 links were probed and 16 skipped by design.
  - Of the 12, 8 returned **429** (rate limiting), so their destinations are unverified, not confirmed broken. These links:
    - the hero CTA, "Up to 40% off treats, toys & everything for their wishlist.* Shop deals"
    - "Shop dog", "Shop cat", "Shop all"
    - the "Chewy" logo link
    - "Find merry gifts for every pet | Shop now"
    - two image links
  - 4 more links were unprobed after the 70s time budget (`.../zp039HBjXQyuEXYhoTlD5Q~~/...`, `.../A5KdKFfB4nxLioH8j7n6-g~~/...`, `.../EOClw1tr0qolcUNHUC490A~~/...`, `.../NwdM463S3hF986r6LKhKGA~~/...`).
  - No link was confirmed broken, and none was confirmed valid by a 2xx/3xx response.
- **Opaque redirect wrapping.**
  - Every link goes through `clicks-paws.chewy.com/f/a/<token>~~/AAQRxRA~/...`, so final destinations can't be read from the source.
  - Two links, "Shop dog" and "Shop cat", share the identical path segment `PAf46dF1KPRaF3deJ9JygZjF3ru2w8ru8gtPVg-2zw3zSOYZuTVfFdihqTUvpuYe...` before their unique suffixes. This is normal for the tracker's encoding and not an error.
- **Tracking pixels.**
  - `meow.chewy.com/p/up/.../o.gif?mi_u=979488259&mi_ecmp=2026-11-18-W-Pre-Cyber-NotSettled` is an open-tracking pixel carrying a subscriber ID (`mi_u`). `clicks-paws.chewy.com/q/...` is a second tracking image.
  - Both are expected, and both were flagged for missing alt (see section 3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty title.** The source has `<title></title>`. Some clients and assistive tech use this, so it should be filled.
- **Linked images with no alt text.** `7ffd0ba076493042.png` and `924fb9302ff1e2e0.png` (`meow.chewy.com/p/rp/...`) are wrapped in links. Their link text resolves to `[image: …png]`, so screen readers and the plain-text part have no usable label for those links.
- **Footer icons without alt.** `footer-icons_returns.png` and `footer-icons_guaranteed.png` (`chirp.media.chewy.cloud/email/images/footer/live-text-icons/`) have no alt text. Their name suggests they carry information, so they are likely not purely decorative.
- **Tracking images without alt.** `o.gif` and the `clicks-paws.chewy.com/q/...` image have no alt. `alt=""` is acceptable for these, but the attribute is absent or empty per QA.
- **Web font dependency.** `chirp.media.chewy.cloud/fonts/fonts-chewy-v2.css` is loaded via `<link>` inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. Gmail and others strip it. Fallback stacks fall in the truncated section and could not be checked.
- **Plain-text part.** 10192 of 11904 characters (about 86%) are URLs because of the long tracked links. Readability is poor, and spam filters may weigh this.
- **No issues found** on the viewport meta, `lang`, or the responsive `@media (max-width:620px)` rules visible in the source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`) appear in the visible source. The only personalization seen is the `mi_u` ID in the pixel URL. The truncated body means this check is incomplete.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No `List-Unsubscribe` header and no `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058).** QA noted the AgentMail relay may strip these, so this is **unconfirmed**. If the headers are truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- **No `Authentication-Results` header.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, again because of the relay. The From domain is `paws.chewy.com` and the tracking domain is `clicks-paws.chewy.com`, so they are aligned, which is a good sign but not proof.
- **Footer not verifiable.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address fall in the truncated section. QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but none appears in the results.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM data available.** Tracked redirects were not resolved because of the 429s, so UTM parameters and landing pages could not be checked.
- The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=2026-11-18-W-Pre-Cyber-NotSettled`, in the pixel URLs.
- The hero CTA text carries an asterisk ("wishlist.*"). Whether the terms are linked or present couldn't be verified in the truncated HTML.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fill the title.** Set `<title>` to the subject or preheader text.
2. **Add alt text.**
   - Add meaningful alt to the two linked images (`7ffd0ba076493042.png`, `924fb9302ff1e2e0.png`).
   - Add alt to the footer icons (`footer-icons_returns.png`, `footer-icons_guaranteed.png`).
   - Add `alt=""` explicitly on the tracking pixels.
3. **Re-run link probing.**
   - Re-probe with throttling or retry/backoff on 429 and a longer time budget.
   - Resolve the redirects to confirm landing pages and UTMs for the 8 rate-limited and 4 unprobed links.
4. **Verify the headers on the original message.** Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` on the raw message or with a direct-to-inbox seed, not through the relay.
5. **Capture the full HTML.** Re-run the audit on the untruncated source to check the footer, unsubscribe link, postal address, font fallbacks and any merge tokens.
6. **Shorten the plain-text part.** Use shorter tracked URLs in the text version (>70% URLs is a deliverability signal).
