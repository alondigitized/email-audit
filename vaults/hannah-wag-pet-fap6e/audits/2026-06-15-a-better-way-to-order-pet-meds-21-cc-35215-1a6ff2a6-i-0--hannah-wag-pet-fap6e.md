# A better way to order pet meds
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-15**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this one actually made me stop scrolling — not because it's flashy, but because it's *useful*. My senior rescue Biscuit has been on anxiety medication for two years and I still find the vet-to-pharmacy handoff exhausting. "We contact your vet & ship fast to your door" is the exact sentence I want to see from Chewy. The hero lands, the category grid is smart, and "Anxiety Relief" gets its own dedicated tile with a cute nervous-looking dog photo that honestly felt like it was made for Biscuit.
- That said, this is a cold pitch for a service I've never been steered toward in years of auto-ship orders. There's zero personalization — no "Hannah, you already order with us, here's where your meds can live too" hook. After that embarrassing "Your Chewy.com account" subject line from last month (which felt like a phishing alert and scored a 3/10 on my last read), this pharmacy email is a genuine recovery. It shows a marketing team that actually has a product to sell.
- I'd click the Anxiety Relief tile, probably not the Flea & Tick one because I've got that locked into auto-ship already. I wouldn't forward this — it's not shareable, it's functional. Mark it as "useful, come back to it" energy.

## What's working

- **"We contact your vet & ship fast to your door"** — that's the actual pain point. The vet callback loop is the worst part of ordering prescription meds and they named it directly in the hero.
- **Anxiety Relief tile** — having a four-category grid that includes Anxiety Relief as one of the four shows real product breadth, and for a two-rescue household with a senior dog on anxiety meds this is immediate relevance.
- **24/7 phone number in a dedicated blue banner** — I actually trust Chewy's customer service and seeing "Call Us 24/7 at 1-800-672-4399" prominently displayed reinforces that this isn't a shady third-party pharmacy.
- **Clean layout, no broken images** — everything rendered beautifully, circular image tiles are friendly and consistent, no garbled copy or blank gaps. After the hollow account-notification email last month, this at least *looks* professional.
- **Free delivery on orders over $49 in the header bar** — visible immediately, removes the friction of wondering "will meds ship free?"

## What's weak

- **Zero auto-ship mention** — I am a Chewy auto-ship customer. The obvious hook here is "add your pet's meds to your existing auto-ship" and it's nowhere. This is the single biggest miss for someone like me.
- **No personalization at all** — Chewy knows my dogs. They know I've ordered from them for years. This email talks to me like I've never heard of Chewy Pharmacy, which is probably true, but the framing is "new customer acquisition" when I'm an existing one. PetSmart sends me grooming reminders tied to past appointment dates — Chewy could do way better here.
- **"Great prices" without any actual prices** — the category grid says "Great prices on meds your pet needs" but there's no specific offer, no percentage off, no first-order deal. Just generic "Shop now." At least a "Save 15% on your first Rx order" hook would've made me act now instead of bookmarking and forgetting.
- **All four CTAs say "Shop now"** — the button copy is identical for every category. "Start Biscuit's Rx" or even "Shop Anxiety Meds" would feel like it was designed for me instead of copy-pasted from a template.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add an auto-ship pharmacy hook in the hero** — Something like "Your meds, on your auto-ship schedule." That one line converts existing customers. Right now this email is doing new-customer acquisition work on a loyal buyer.
- 2. **Lead with a first-Rx offer** — "15% off your first prescription order" in the hero would give me a reason to act today vs. vague "great prices" that make me shrug and close.
- 3. **Differentiate the four CTA buttons** — Change "Shop now" to category-specific copy: "Shop Anxiety Meds," "Shop Flea & Tick," etc. Each tile should feel like it goes somewhere specific.
- 4. **Personalization in the header** — A line like "Hannah, you already trust us for food and treats — now let us handle the meds" would hit hard and cost nothing.
- 5. **Surface the ACHC accreditation in the hero** — It's buried in the trust icons at the bottom. That accreditation badge is meaningful for a mom deciding whether to trust an online pharmacy with her dog's controlled anxiety meds. Lead with it.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your anxious pup's meds, handled by Chewy Pharmacy`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Skip the vet callback — Chewy ships your pet's Rx`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `We contact your vet and ship to your door. Add to auto-ship.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Licensed pharmacists. Free delivery. 24/7 support. Sound familiar?`
