# Save up to 40% on gifts for good pets
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-07**
## Executive summary

- I'll say it: the gingerbread dog toy is adorable, and I almost clicked on that alone. The hero is bold and festive, and "40% OFF" is the first thing I see. Chewy is one of the few brands where I open the email before I've read the whole subject line.
- Then I scroll and the email falls apart. There's one big discount number, and the rules for it are buried in a grey footnote at the bottom. The footnote lists four codes (EARLY10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40) and a "limit 3 orders" rule. Nothing in the email says which code gets which discount. That's not honest "up to 40%." It's a puzzle. And I don't see Auto-Ship anywhere. Not once.
- Would I click? Probably yes, because my puppy destroys toys weekly and the holiday snacks look good. Would I forward it? No. It's a generic blast that treats me like a stranger, not like someone with two rescues and a standing Auto-Ship order.

## What's working

- The hero is clear. "40% OFF" in huge pink type on navy is the first thing my eye lands on, and the gingerbread toy and holiday snacks look fun.
- The "Shop dog / Shop cat / Shop all deals" tiles are big and easy to tap with my thumb.
- The trust row at the bottom (free shipping with Chewy+, free returns for 365 days, 24/7 service) hits what I care about most. Returns are my loyalty test, and Chewy passes it.
- The "Rx" link in the nav is a small nod to my senior's meds, which I do appreciate.

## What's weak

- The discount rules are hidden in tiny grey fine print. The codes EARLY10 through EARLY40 are never explained. I'd have to guess which one gets me 40%.
- "Shop deals" is a small button sitting inside the curved headline. It's easy to miss, and "Shop deals" is vague.
- No Auto-Ship messaging at all. Does the code stack with my 35% off first Auto-Ship order? Does it apply to my recurring orders? I can't tell, and for me that's the whole question.
- "Top picks for you" shows sushi-shaped plush toys, a treat bag, a cat wand and a ball. That's one cat item and nothing that looks tied to my dogs, so "for you" feels false. The four thumbnails are also tiny, and "Shop now" under each one is hard to tap.
- The footer is bulky. There's a big stack of legal lines, a message reference ID and a faded logo. The last time Chewy sent me something plain, the Privacy Policy email on 2026-09-18, it was all legal text and I scored it a 3. This one isn't that bad, but the bottom half is still mostly boilerplate.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the code in the hero. Something like "Use code EARLY40 for 40% off" next to the button. Then show the tier table (spend this much, get this much) right below the hero, not in the fine print.
- 2. Add one line about Auto-Ship stacking, for example "Stacks with Auto-Ship savings." That would get me to click right away.
- 3. Personalize "Top picks for you" using what I've bought. I'm a dog household, so show dog chews and toys, not a cat wand.
- 4. Replace "Shop deals" with a specific CTA like "Shop holiday toys." Make the button bigger and put it below the headline, not inside the arc.
- 5. Cut the footer down. Keep the unsubscribe and legal lines, but drop the reference ID.
- Compared with other brands, PetSmart usually puts the promo code in the hero image, so you never have to hunt for it. Chewy's design is nicer, but their offer clarity is worse. Target-style "Circle" emails also usually put the member price right in the product tile, and Chewy's picks have no prices at all.
