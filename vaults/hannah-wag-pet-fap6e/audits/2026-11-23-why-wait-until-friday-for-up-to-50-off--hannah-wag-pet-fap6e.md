# Why wait until Friday for up to 50% off?
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Up to 50% OFF" in giant white letters on periwinkle is the first thing I see. I'll give Chewy credit for the headline: "Why wait until Friday" is a smart nudge. But this is the second Cyber Week email today, and it comes right after "Cyber Week savings kick off now!" Same brand, same day, same hero idea. I opened it because it's Chewy, and I mostly felt the fatigue.
- The gingerbread toy, gift box and holiday snack bag are cute. Still, none of it is about my house. I have a senior with a sensitive stomach and a puppy who destroys plush toys in about four minutes. I don't see Auto-Ship anywhere, no mention of stacking the Auto-Ship discount with these codes, and no pet name. "Top picks for you" shows a dental chew, a sushi toy, treats and a wand toy, and that's it.
- I'd click once to see what the flash deals are, but I wouldn't forward it. Mark as read, and I'd come back only if I needed to restock.

## What's working

- The "Up to 50% OFF" headline is huge and impossible to miss. The "Cyber Week" pill above it tells me the occasion right away.
- The "Shop dog / Shop cat / Shop all deals" tiles are big and easy to tap with a thumb. That's how I actually shop.
- The footer trust row says "Free returns for 365 days" and "Happy pets guaranteed." Returns are my loyalty test for a brand, so that line lands.
- The "Fast, free shipping with Chewy+" banner and the Rx tab in the nav tell me Chewy knows what I use it for.
- The gift photography is polished and festive, and it matches the season.

## What's weak

- Auto-Ship is missing entirely. Chewy knows I'm an Auto-Ship customer, and there's no "extra savings on Auto-Ship" and no mention of whether the codes stack. That's the benefit I care about most.
- The "up to 50%" claim depends on codes (CYBER10 through CYBER50). The fine print is tiny, and the cap of 5 items and 3 orders is buried at the bottom. It feels less honest than the hero suggests.
- "Surprise savings end soon†" is vague, with no deadline and no number. A mystery box doesn't make me act. The "Reveal your deals" button is a bit gimmicky.
- "Top picks for you" doesn't feel personal. It has no senior-dog or puppy items and nothing I've bought before. The four tiny thumbnails with "Shop now" links are small tap targets.
- It repeats what I got earlier today and on 11/18 ("Up to 40% off is in your stocking"). The 40% to 50% bump is the only real change.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add an Auto-Ship line near the hero, like "Stack 35% off your first Auto-Ship order with Cyber codes." That would lift this more than anything else.
- 2. Make "Top picks for you" actually personal. Use my reorder history, such as the senior joint chews and the puppy food I buy, with prices.
- 3. Put the code and the deadline near the hero instead of in the footer. Say plainly that it ends 12/2 and which codes apply.
- 4. Drop the mystery flash-deal block, or show a real item with a real price.
- 5. Cap the sends. Merge the two same-day Cyber Week emails into one.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Cyber Week: up to 50% off + your Auto-Ship stacks`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Auto-Ship items are on Cyber Week sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Codes CYBER10–50 inside. Free shipping with Chewy+.`
