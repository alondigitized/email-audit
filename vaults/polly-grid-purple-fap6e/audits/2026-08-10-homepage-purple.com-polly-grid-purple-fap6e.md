# Homepage snapshot · purple.com · 2026-08-10
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- This reads like a brand that knows exactly what it's selling and isn't afraid to back it up with numbers — "90% improvement in reported sleep quality," a footnoted claim, a "Sleep Score Validated" badge. Right now they're pushing a Back to School push: free pillows (up to $458 value) with a mattress purchase. It's not shouting at me with a countdown clock, which I appreciate, but it's also not talking to me specifically about the grid tech that got me here in the first place.

## What's working

- **The footnoted stats in the hero** ("90% improvement in reported sleep quality¹," "63% reduction in sleep interfering pain²") — this is exactly the science-explainer register I read twice before I trust a claim.
- **The "Sleep Score Validated" badge** on the hero image — a clinical-looking credential is more persuasive to me than a lifestyle adjective.
- **The trust-icon row** (100 night trial, 10 year warranty, Free shipping, FSA/HSA eligible) sits right under the fold — no digging required.
- **The third product tile** actually shows the purple grid texture on the mattress edge, which is the one visual cue that tells me this isn't just another foam brand in disguise.
- **The $458-value pillow bundle offer** — I'm a bundle-pricing shopper, and this is a real bundle incentive, not a vague "save now."

## What's weak

- **The hero headline "Better nights for brighter school days"** is a seasonal hook, not a product hook — it doesn't mention grid, pressure relief, or temperature, which is the whole reason I'm loyal to this brand over Casper.
- **No loyalty/member pricing visible** — I've bought from them before (bundle shopper), and there's nothing here that acknowledges repeat customers beyond a generic refer-a-friend banner.
- **"Shop + compare mattresses" as the only real CTA** — fine, but it dumps me into a comparison flow instead of straight to the grid technology story ("Learn what makes us different" is secondary and vague).
- **Ratings shown but no review excerpts** — I'd want to see what people say about the grid vs. their old foam bed, not just a star average.

## Recommendations

- Swap the hero headline copy to reference the grid tech directly ("GelFlex Grid: still the coolest, still no motion transfer") instead of a seasonal tie-in that could be about any mattress brand.
- Add a small "Welcome back" or order-history nod near the account icon for repeat shoppers — even a "Your last order: [mattress]" line would do it.
- Pull one customer quote that mentions switching from memory foam into the hero or the trust-icon strip — social proof against foam specifically is my exact hesitation point.
- Put a light deadline on the $458 pillow bundle ("through August 31") — right now it reads open-ended, which makes it feel less urgent to act today.
