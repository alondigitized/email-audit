# Homepage snapshot · purple.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- Purple is leading with a Memorial Day sale right now — big dollar-off number, bundle framing, two clean CTAs — and it's doing exactly what I'd want a mattress brand to do above the fold. This reads like a premium sleep-science brand that knows how to turn a holiday into a real purchase moment. The GelFlex story isn't front-and-center in the viewport, but the bundle-pricing mechanic they're using is my exact trigger.

## What's working

- **"Up to $900 off a mattress + base"** — bundle pricing is my exact decision heuristic; this headline does 80% of the selling for me before I read another word
- **"Shop mattresses" CTA** — dark filled pill button, above the fold, zero ambiguity about where it goes
- **"MEMORIAL DAY SALE" label in orange** — creates temporal urgency without being obnoxious; I know I'm in a window
- **"63% reduction in sleep interfering pain"** with a superscript citation — the science-backed copy with a footnote is exactly what I double-read; it respects my research mode
- **"Refer & get $100"** top banner — I already recommended Purple to my sister-in-law; this is free money I didn't know was sitting there
- **"Learn what makes us different"** secondary CTA — gives the hesitant shopper (or the partner I need to convince) an education path without leaving the page
- **Sleep Score widget** in the lifestyle image corner — trust signal that feels platform-native, not a third-party slapped on

## What's weak

- **No GelFlex grid callout above the fold** — the headline is all about price, not the technology I'm here for; if I were a first-time visitor, I'd have no idea why this mattress costs what it costs
- **"Up to $900 off" is a ceiling, not a floor** — "up to" framing can feel bait-and-switch if the actual savings on the model I want are $200; the footnote link helps but doesn't fully defuse it
- **No free-shipping mention** — for a $1,000+ mattress, I want to see that confirmed before I even open a product page
- **No loyalty or returning-customer signal** — I've bought from Purple before; I see zero acknowledgment that they know me or that I have any status
- **Hamburger-only mobile nav** — there are no visible category pills or shortcuts; getting anywhere besides "Shop mattresses" requires an extra tap into the menu

## Recommendations

- **Add a one-liner beneath the CTA**: "Free delivery + free returns on every mattress" — it costs nothing to say this above the fold and it eliminates a friction point before the cart
- **Inject a GelFlex grid reference** into the hero subtext: "Purple Grid® absorbs 4x more pressure than memory foam — now $900 off" — anchors the sale to the technology, not just the discount
- **Surface the referral banner more clearly**: "You have $100 waiting — refer a friend" vs. the generic current copy would convert existing customers faster
- **Add an urgency line with a hard date**: "Sale ends May 26" next to the Memorial Day label — implied deadlines are weaker than stated ones
