# Homepage snapshot · purple.com · 2026-06-08
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Purple is doing their homework here — the summer hook is clean, the price anchor is front and center, and that "63% reduction in sleep interfering pain" stat stopped me cold (as designed). This feels like a brand that knows its customer is pain-aware and research-hungry, not an impulse buyer. Right now they're pushing mattresses on summer deal framing, which is exactly the kind of moment I'd check the site for.

## What's working

- **"63% reduction in sleep interfering pain.²"** — that footnote superscript is doing heavy lifting. I read science-explainer emails twice; this is the kind of quantified claim that keeps me on the page instead of bouncing.
- **"Summer Savings / Queen mattresses from $999"** — the pink promo banner with a hard price number is exactly the deal signal I'm waiting for to justify pulling the trigger on a full bundle.
- **"Your best summer starts here" + the problem-aware body copy** — smart sequencing: name the pain first, then offer the solution. That's the Purple playbook and it works on me every time.
- **"Refer & get $100" top bar** — subtle but I noticed it. I have one friend who's been asking about mattresses and this makes me feel like I have leverage.
- **Dual CTA structure**: "Shop mattresses" (ready-to-buy) + "Learn what makes us different" (still-researching) — they're not pushing me out the door if I'm not ready yet.

## What's weak

- **No bundle pricing hook visible** — I am literally on this site waiting for the mattress + pillow + sheet set deal and there's zero above-fold signal that bundles exist. That's my actual trigger and it's nowhere.
- **No social proof above fold** — I see zero star ratings, review counts, or "X customers chose this." After the Casper disappointment I need third-party validation before I trust a mattress brand's own science claim.
- **No cool-sleep technology callout** — the whole reason I'm here is the GelFlex grid's thermal regulation. "Stays cool" is in the body copy but the grid itself — the actual differentiator — gets no visual treatment above the fold.
- **Free shipping / trial period missing** — I have no idea from this screen what the return policy looks like. That's a barrier. The Casper return experience trained me to check this before I even think about the cart.

## Recommendations

- **Surface bundle pricing in the promo banner** — swap "Queen mattresses from $999" for "Mattress + Pillow bundle from $X — save $Y" or add a second banner tab. That's the offer that converts my demographic, not the base mattress SKU alone.
- **Drop one trust signal beneath the hero CTA** — add a single line like "4.8★ from 52,000+ reviews · 100-night trial · Free shipping" right below "Shop mattresses." I can scroll to verify it later but I need the reassurance now.
- **Add a GelFlex grid visual thumbnail or animated loop** — even a small egg-drop GIF or grid cross-section graphic in the hero would give the technology a face. The copy says cool, the visual should prove it.
- **Test a "Build your bundle" secondary CTA** — replace or supplement "Learn what makes us different" with "Bundle & save," targeting the shopper who knows the brand but is waiting on the right price package.
