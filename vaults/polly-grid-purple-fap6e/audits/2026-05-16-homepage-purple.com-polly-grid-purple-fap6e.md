# Homepage snapshot · purple.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Purple is leading with a Memorial Day Sale that hits exactly the right nerve for me — a mattress bundle deal with a real dollar figure attached. The page reads like a confident, science-forward brand that knows it has something to prove against the foam incumbents, and the headline is doing the heavy lifting. That said, above the fold it's all mattress + base, and I'm the person waiting for the full trio: mattress, pillow, sheet set.

## What's working

- **"Up to $900 off a mattress + base"** — this is the language of someone who has done their research and is waiting for the right moment. Bundle pricing is my trigger, and this is exactly it.
- **"MEMORIAL DAY SALE" label in orange** — the seasonal signal is credible; it gives the deal a real reason to exist and a built-in deadline without needing a countdown timer.
- **"63% reduction in sleep interfering pain.² Details"** — I read the science emails twice. Seeing a cited stat with a footnote in the hero body copy tells me this brand speaks my language. The superscript alone builds more trust than any star rating.
- **Dual CTA structure** — "Shop mattresses" (primary, filled) and "Learn what makes us different ►" (secondary, outline) is smart. I might want to buy now OR I might need the science refresher. Both doors are open.
- **"Get a king size mattress for queen price"** — practical size translation. I immediately know what I'd be saving on my king.

## What's weak

- **No GelFlex Grid mention above the fold** — I came here as a grid devotee. The technology that differentiates Purple from every foam brand I've returned is nowhere in the hero. Someone landing cold has no idea what makes Purple physically different.
- **Bundle stops at mattress + base** — I wait for the mattress + pillow + sheet set bundle. The base is nice, but the full sleep-system bundle is what makes me open my wallet without hesitation. It's not here.
- **Zero trust signals above the fold** — no review count, no "100-night trial," no free-returns badge. For a $900+ purchase, I need to see at least one safety net before I tap "Shop."
- **The lifestyle image is partially cut off** — the couple scene is cropped at the bottom of the viewport, which makes it feel like an afterthought rather than an aspirational anchor.
- **"Sleep Score" badge is clipped** — whatever that widget is in the bottom right, it's barely readable. Either commit to it or move it.

## Recommendations

- **Add "GelFlex Grid" to the hero headline or subhead** — something like "Up to $900 off GelFlex Grid mattresses + base" immediately tells returning shoppers they're in the right place and differentiates from foam competitors without adding copy length.
- **Introduce a third CTA or banner strip: "See mattress + pillow + sheet bundles →"** — one tap to the full bundle page captures buyers like me who are explicitly waiting for the complete set, and it doesn't disrupt the current hero flow.
- **Surface one trust signal inline** — insert "Free 100-night trial" or "⭐ 4.8 / 50,000+ reviews" as a single line between the body copy and the CTAs. One line, zero extra scroll.
- **Unclip the lifestyle image** — let the couple scene breathe as a full-bleed module below the CTA block instead of cutting it off at the fold boundary.
