# Homepage snapshot · purple.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- Purple is doing exactly what I'd expect right now — leaning hard into Memorial Day with a bold dollar-off number and a clean mobile layout that doesn't make me squint. As someone who already knows the grid story, though, I notice immediately that the hero doesn't mention GelFlex anywhere above the fold, and the sale is "mattress + base," not the full bundle I've been patiently waiting for. Strong sale energy, but it's selling at me, not to me.

## What's working

- **The dollar number is doing heavy lifting.** "Up to $900 off a mattress + base" is the first thing my eye goes to after the sale label. That's a real number, not "up to 30% off" vagueness.
- **"Less pain. Better sleep." tagline** — under the logo, six words, zero fluff. That's actually good brand compression right there.
- **"Learn what makes us different"** secondary CTA with the play arrow is smart for me specifically — I will absolutely tap that and read the science page. That's exactly the kind of rabbit hole I fall into.
- **The 63% pain-reduction claim with the footnote.** I notice it, I appreciate that they linked the "Details," and it immediately makes me want to scroll further. Data > lifestyle photography for me.
- **Clean mobile layout.** No cramped text, no competing banner stacking. On a phone, this is easy to read at arm's length.

## What's weak

- **No grid mention anywhere above the fold.** I came here because I love the GelFlex grid. The hero could be for a Tempur-Pedic sale for all it tells me. That's a brand differentiation miss at the most-watched spot on the page.
- **"Mattress + base" is not the bundle I want.** I'm holding out for a mattress + pillow + sheet set deal. The offer visible here doesn't match my shopping trigger at all.
- **Refer a friend banner feels misplaced as the top message.** For a returning customer who wants to upgrade, a loyalty/order-history hook ("Welcome back — here's your member deal") would convert better than a referral pitch leading the page.
- **The "Sleep Score" badge at the bottom-right corner is half-cropped and confusing.** I can see it but can't tell what it does. Is it a chat widget? A score? It's just visual noise in the current state.
- **No free-shipping threshold, no returns mention, no review stars above the fold.** These are the trust signals that push me over the line from "interested" to "in cart," and they're all deferred to scrolling.

## Recommendations

- **Add a grid callout to the hero line.** Even "Up to $900 off a GelFlex mattress + base" does the job. One word, zero design changes, and it immediately signals to grid loyalists that this sale is for us.
- **Swap "mattress + base" for a bundle promotion in a sticky banner** — "Add our pillow + sheet set for $X" or "Bundle & save more." I'm already primed to buy the bundle; just give me the path.
- **Replace the "Refer a friend" top ticker with a returning-customer hook** like "Member pricing is live — see your deal" or a free-shipping threshold ("Free shipping on all mattresses"). The referral banner belongs in the account dashboard, not the homepage hero.
- **Make the Sleep Score widget either fully visible or fully gone.** Right now it's half-cropped in the corner and adds confusion. If it's a live chat, label it. If it's a gimmick, cut it.
