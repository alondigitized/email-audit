# Homepage snapshot · purple.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Purple is pushing hard for Memorial Day — the hero is leading with a mattress + base bundle discount of up to $900, which is exactly the kind of stacked-value offer I wait for. This reads like a science-forward sleep brand that knows its buyer: couple, quality-conscious, willing to spend if the deal is anchored right. The grid tech isn't front-and-center yet, but the funnel entry is clean.

## What's working

- **The dollar-figure headline** — "Up to $900 off a mattress + base" is the kind of stacked-bundle framing that makes me stop scrolling. That's the offer I've been waiting for.
- **Dual CTA structure** — "Shop mattresses" (primary, dark pill) and "Learn what makes us different" (secondary, outlined pill with arrow) give me both the buy-now path and the research path. I'm a read-the-science-email-twice person. That secondary CTA is genuinely for me.
- **The sub-copy stat** — "63% reduction in sleep interfering pain" with a citation superscript and "Details" link. That's exactly the kind of claim I'll click. It signals they have science behind it, which builds trust fast.
- **Refer & Get $100 banner** — top of fold, carousel format. I already own Purple pillows — this might get me to loop in my sister before I buy.
- **Memorial Day Sale label in orange** — clear campaign context, tells me the clock is ticking without being obnoxious about it.

## What's weak

- **No GelFlex Grid callout above the fold** — the tech that differentiates Purple from Casper (the one I returned) isn't mentioned yet. As someone who bought in specifically because of the grid, this feels like a missed hook.
- **No bundle breakdown visible** — "mattress + base" for up to $900 off is intriguing, but I can't see what's in scope. Does that include pillows? Sheet sets? I need that answered fast or I'm off to figure it out manually.
- **Lifestyle image is cropped and partial** — the couple photo below the CTAs is cut off. It reads as teaser, not content. Slightly janky on mobile.
- **No price anchor visible** — $900 off sounds great, but off what? No "was $X, now $Y" framing. Without the anchor price, I'm doing mental math I don't want to do.
- **Sleep Score chat bubble** — partially overlaps the lifestyle image in the lower right. Feels like a floating widget competing with the hero for attention.

## Recommendations

- **Add the GelFlex Grid name to the hero sub-copy** — even one line: "Powered by Purple's GelFlex Grid — the tech Casper doesn't have." That's a direct shot at the competitive consideration set I'm in.
- **Show the bundle components explicitly** — replace or augment the sub-copy with "Mattress + adjustable base + pillow set, starting at $X." Bundle buyers need to see the full stack to feel the value.
- **Anchor the savings with a "was/now" price** — "Was $4,499 — Memorial Day Sale: $3,599" next to or under the $900 off headline. Right now the discount feels abstract.
- **Move the Sleep Score widget or reduce its size** — it's overlapping content on mobile. Either pin it to a corner with more padding or collapse it to a small floating icon until tapped.
