# Homepage snapshot · purple.com · 2026-09-28
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- This reads like Purple knows exactly who I am before I even scroll — GelFlex Grid front and center, "proven better" headline, SleepScore Labs data right there instead of buried in a footnote. It's pushing the $999 queen sale and the "shop + compare" angle, which is exactly the kind of side-by-side proof I want before I click "add to cart." No Casper-style vague vibes copy here — this is the science-explainer energy I actually read twice.

## What's working

- The GelFlex Grid badge overlapping the hero image — it's literally the reason I'm loyal to this brand, and they lead with it instead of hiding it three scrolls down.
- "But don't take our word for it — try 125k+ 5-star reviews, or the 90% improvement in reported sleep quality found by SleepScore Labs" — this is the exact register I want. A named lab, a number, a footnote marker. Not "you'll love it!" fluff.
- The trust-icon row (100 night trial, 10 year warranty, free shipping, FSA/HSA eligible) sitting right under the fold — no digging required.
- "Queen mattresses from $999" banner with a "Details" link instead of hiding the catch.

## What's weak

- No bundle pricing visible anywhere above the fold — I specifically wait for mattress + pillow + sheet set deals, and all I see is a single-mattress price.
- The hero model reads more late-20s lifestyle-shoot than someone my age — fine for brand aspiration, but doesn't especially say "this is for you, Gen X."
- "Shop mattresses + compare" is a decent CTA but it's a category link, not a specific product — I'd want at least a "Compare mattresses" tool one tap closer.
- The referral banner ("Refer & get $100") competes visually with the sale banner right below it — two navy/dark bars stacked at the top before I even get to the hero.

## Recommendations

- Swap or add a second hero module promoting a Mattress + Pillow + Sheet Set bundle price, not just the solo mattress from $999.
- Merge the referral bar and the sale bar into one strip, or fold "Refer & get $100" into the account/loyalty menu so the top of the page isn't two competing dark banners.
- Turn "Shop mattresses + compare" into a real comparison-table link — a matrix would sell me harder than a category page.
- Cast a model closer to my age bracket in at least one homepage lifestyle shot — even a secondary image lower on the page would help.
