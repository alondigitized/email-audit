# Homepage snapshot · purple.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- Purple is coming in hot with a Memorial Day sale and they know it — "$900 off a mattress + base" above the fold is not shy. This reads like a brand that's confident in its tech and knows how to make a seasonal push land. The science-backed pain copy is exactly the language I've come to expect from them, and I appreciate that they lead with a dollar figure rather than a percentage trick.

## What's working

- **"Up to $900 off a mattress + base"** — I waited for a deal like this. The dollar figure is concrete and the "base" inclusion signals a real bundle push, not just a mattress markdown
- **"Our best price of the year"** claim paired with a summer-readiness angle — that's a reason to act *now*, not just "someday"
- **"63% reduction in sleep interfering pain"** with a footnote — this is exactly how Purple earns credibility with me; they don't just say "sleep better," they back it with a number
- **"Learn what makes us different"** secondary CTA — smart for someone like me who already knows the grid but wants to send the link to a skeptical partner
- **The lifestyle image** — couple in bed, looks like actual rest, not a staged editorial shoot; feels believable
- **Referral banner at top** — "Refer & get $100" is a nice ambient signal that this is a brand with a real community

## What's weak

- **No bundle pricing visible** — I specifically wait for the mattress + pillow + sheet set bundle, and this deal is mattress + base only; the page gives me no signal that my preferred purchase structure is on offer
- **No urgency deadline copy** — "Memorial Day Sale" implies a window, but there's no "ends Monday" or countdown timer forcing my hand; I might bookmark and forget
- **No review counts or star ratings above fold** — for a high-AOV purchase like this, I want social proof in my face before I tap anything
- **Free-shipping threshold not visible** — at this price point it's probably included, but I shouldn't have to wonder
- **No GelFlex branding in the hero** — the thing that makes Purple *Purple* is the grid, and it's not mentioned or shown above fold; for a returning browser like me, that's a missed reinforcement beat

## Recommendations

- **Add a bundle CTA alongside the mattress one** — "Shop mattress + pillow bundle" below "Shop mattresses" would stop me cold; one tap to my preferred purchase format
- **Pin a countdown timer or "Sale ends Monday" line** under the offer headline — Memorial Day has a hard end date, use it; urgency that's real doesn't feel manipulative
- **Inject a trust strip** between the CTAs and the lifestyle image: star rating aggregate ("4.6 stars from 73,000 reviews") and a free-shipping badge would close the loop before I even scroll
- **Surface the GelFlex grid** in a secondary visual — even a small inset showing the grid structure or the egg-drop visual cue would remind me why I'm not on a Casper
