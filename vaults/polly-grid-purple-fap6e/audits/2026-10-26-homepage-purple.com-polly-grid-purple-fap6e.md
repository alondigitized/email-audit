# Homepage snapshot · purple.com · 2026-10-26
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- Purple looks like a confident, polished DTC sleep brand, and right now it's pushing its "10 Years of Better" anniversary and a "10th Anniversary Sale" with queen mattresses from $999. The hero is full of GelFlex Grid close-ups, which is the thing I care about. It's also very mattress-first, and I wait for bundle pricing, so I'd want the bundle deal to show up sooner.

## What's working

- **GelFlex Grid imagery.** The purple grid close-ups on the mattress, the pillow and the hand pressing into the grid tell me this is the egg-drop product I trust. I recognized it in about a second.
- **"Learn what makes us different" secondary button.** It's the "show me the science" path, and I read explainer content twice, so I'd tap it.
- **Trust strip under the hero.** "100 night trial · 10 year warranty · Free shipping · FSA/HSA eligible" answers my objections before I raise them. After the Casper return, the 100 nights matters.
- **Nav.** "Bundles," "Pillows," "Bedding" and "Mattress Quiz" are all one tap away in the top bar. "Sale" is in red, so it's easy to find.
- **Anniversary banner.** The dark navy strip with a white "Shop sale" pill is high contrast and easy to read.

## What's weak

- **No bundle message.** The banner and hero push a single queen mattress price. I buy the mattress, pillow and sheet set together, and the "Bundles" link is only in the nav.
- **"From $999" with no end date.** I can't tell how long the sale lasts, and the "Details" link is small.
- **"The Greatest Sleep Ever Invented®" is superlative hype.** The subhead is better, but I'd prefer a specific claim.
- **Both CTAs are generic.** "Shop mattress collections" doesn't take me to a specific mattress or to a bundle.
- **No reviews or ratings above the fold.** There's no star count and no review volume to back up "proven."
- **Dead space below the trust strip.** There's a large empty beige gap and then a cropped image. The floating yellow chat bubble and the "Feedback" tab on the left edge add clutter on top of that.
- **Top bar offer.** "Refer & get $100" is for people who already own one, so it does nothing for me as a first-time buyer.

## Recommendations

- Change the banner to a bundle: "10th Anniversary Bundle: mattress + pillow + sheets, save $X," with a "Shop bundles" button. This goes straight at the way I shop.
- Add a visible end date or countdown to the anniversary banner, such as "Ends Nov 2," and show the dollar or percent savings next to "from $999."
- Put a star rating and review count (for example "4.x ★ from N reviews") in the trust strip or under the hero CTA.
- Remove the empty band under the trust strip. Put a "Best sellers" or "Shop by feel" rail there with prices and ratings, and make the chat bubble smaller.
