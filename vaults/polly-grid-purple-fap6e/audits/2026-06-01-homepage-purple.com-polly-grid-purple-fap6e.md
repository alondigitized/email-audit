# Homepage snapshot · purple.com · 2026-06-01
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-01**
## Executive summary

- Purple opened with its biggest gun: a countdown, a dollar figure in the $900 range, and "our best price of the year" — all above the fold on mobile. For me, a true believer who's already run the mattress comparison gauntlet and landed firmly on the Grid, this reads like a legit buying moment. My only gripe right out of the gate — this is a mattress + *base* bundle, not the mattress + pillow + sheet set I always wait for.

## What's working

- **The countdown timer** — "SALE ENDS IN 01D : 22H : 35M : 25S" in that orange-red strip creates real urgency without feeling manufactured. A day and change feels credible, not the infinite-reloading fake timer I've seen elsewhere.
- **"63% reduction in sleep interfering pain"** — I literally read those science-explainer emails twice. That specific stat, with a citation superscript, signals Purple knows their customer. That's the GelFlex language. It caught me.
- **"Our best price of the year"** — bold claim, but Purple doesn't run sales every week, so I believe it. Combined with the dollar figure, it makes a case.
- **"Learn what makes us different" secondary CTA** — smart. For a first-time visitor this is the education path. For me it's a reminder that Purple actually has the receipts.
- **Tagline placement** — "Less pain. Better sleep." right under the logo, immediately on load. Clean and on-brief.

## What's weak

- **No bundle pricing visible** — I always wait for mattress + pillow + sheet set deals. What's here is mattress + base (a frame/foundation), which doesn't scratch my itch. If the pillow bundle exists right now, I can't see it.
- **No reviews or social proof above the fold** — not a single star rating, review count, or "as seen in" badge. For an $800+ purchase I want early reassurance I'm not the only one.
- **Refer-a-friend $100 as the announcement bar** — I like the idea but it's a transaction that requires me to go recruit someone. Today I want to spend, not recruit. The top slot could be doing heavier lifting.
- **No free-shipping callout** — I know Purple ships free but it's not stated anywhere I can see. At this price point, confirming free shipping would remove a conversion friction point.
- **No returns / trial period mention** — Purple offers a sleep trial. That's a trust-builder for anyone on the fence, and it's invisible here.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the announcement bar referral for a free-shipping + trial confirmation strip** ("Free shipping. 100-night trial. Free returns.") — that removes three objections in one line and doesn't waste the prime real estate.
- **Add a bundle toggle below the hero CTAs** — something like "Add Purple pillow + sheets from $X more" as a visible cross-sell. I'm primed to buy right now; capture the upsell before I click away.
- **Drop a trust strip between the hero and the lifestyle image** — star rating count, trial length, and a returns badge. Three icons, one row. Takes ten minutes to ship and it would bump my conversion confidence considerably.
- **Make the footnote ² content visible** — either expand "Details" inline or add one parenthetical in the body copy. "Up to $900 off select sizes" takes ten words and removes the bait-and-switch anxiety.
