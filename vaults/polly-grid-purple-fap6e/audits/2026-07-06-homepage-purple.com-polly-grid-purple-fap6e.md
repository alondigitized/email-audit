# Homepage snapshot · purple.com · 2026-07-06
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-06**
## Executive summary

- This is Purple doing what Purple does best — leading with science and discounting hard when they want to move mattresses. The countdown timer and the $800-off headline land immediately, and the copy ("63% reduction in sleep interfering pain") is exactly the kind of stat I'll read twice. What it's NOT doing is selling me the bundle — I'm here for mattress + pillow + sheet set pricing, and all I see is mattress + base.

## What's working

- **The countdown timer** — "SALE ENDS IN 01D : 20H : 38M : 27S" in that coral/red strip is impossible to ignore and creates real urgency without being obnoxious about it
- **The $800 headline** — dollar amounts are more compelling than percentages for big-ticket items; this is the right call
- **The science subtext** — "63% reduction in sleep interfering pain" is exactly the kind of claim I stop to read; the ² footnote makes it feel cited, not made up
- **The lifestyle image** — a woman around 39 sitting relaxed on the mattress, not a model in a studio; I can see myself there
- **The Sleep Score badge** — small but visible; signals third-party credibility
- **Two-CTA approach** — the "Learn what makes us different" secondary CTA is smart for someone like me who is still in research mode

## What's weak

- **No bundle pricing** — I came here ready to spend on the full mattress + pillow + sheet set package; the offer is "mattress + base" only, and that's not how I buy
- **No loyalty or member hook** — I've been in their email list for years; there's nothing here acknowledging me as a returning customer
- **"Shop + compare mattresses" CTA is broad** — I want to go straight to GelFlex grid models, not a comparison page with entry-level foam options alongside
- **No free-shipping callout above the fold** — for a $2,000+ purchase this should be front and center
- **The referral banner at the top** — I'm already here; telling me to refer a friend before I've even seen the hero feels like a missed opportunity for a contextual welcome-back message

## Recommendations

- **Add a bundle toggle or "Build your sleep bundle" CTA** alongside the mattress offer — Polly Grid is not alone in waiting for the three-piece package; surface it above the fold, even as a secondary option
- **Swap the generic referral banner for a returning-customer hook** — something like "Welcome back. Your saved mattress is still in your cart." or a loyalty points balance would immediately signal Purple knows who I am
- **Anchor the "Shop + compare" CTA to GelFlex grid** — "Shop GelFlex Grid mattresses" is one extra word and turns a category page tap into a product-line tap
- **Add "Free shipping + free returns" in a line under the CTA buttons** — this is table stakes for a mattress brand and it's buried off-screen right now
