# Homepage snapshot · purple.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Purple is coming in hard for Memorial Day with a clean, science-forward offer that honestly reads like it was written for me — dollar-specific savings, a footnoted clinical claim, and a comparison CTA that respects the way I actually shop. The brand looks premium and confident. What it's pushing right now is the mattress + base bundle, and it knows how to make that feel worth clicking.

## What's working

- **"Up to $900 off a mattress + base"** — Specific dollar amounts always beat percentages for me. I can do math. This lands immediately.
- **"MEMORIAL DAY SALE" banner in orange** — Named sale on a known calendar date makes the deadline implicit and credible without screaming.
- **Dual CTAs: "Shop mattresses" + "Compare mattresses"** — The second button is doing real work. I always want to compare before I commit, and Purple is letting me do that without forcing me into a one-way funnel.
- **"63% reduction in sleep interfering pain.² Details"** — That footnote superscript is a green flag. This brand knows I'm going to fact-check them, and they're not hiding the source.
- **Science-forward copy register** — "Sleep interfering pain" is clinical and precise. Casper never talked like this. Purple clearly knows who they're selling to.

## What's weak

- **No pillow or sheet set in the bundle** — The offer is mattress + base. I specifically wait for mattress + pillow + sheet set bundle pricing. This sale is adjacent to my trigger, not quite it.
- **No loyalty hook above the fold** — If I'm already a GelFlex devotee, nothing on this page acknowledges that relationship. No rewards balance, no member price, no "welcome back" signal.
- **Zero social proof visible** — This is a $900+ considered purchase. Not one review count or star rating above the fold. That's a gap for first-timers and returners alike.
- **No free-shipping confirmation** — I probably assume free delivery on a mattress, but I shouldn't have to assume. One line would do it.

## Recommendations

- **Add the pillow + sheet bundle to the hero offer** — Change "mattress + base" to "mattress + base + GelFlex pillow + sheets" or add a secondary line below the headline. That's my actual checkout trigger.
- **Drop a "★4.8 · 40,000+ reviews" trust chip** directly below the hero subtext — one line, no scrolling required. It's a proven lever for five-figure sleep purchases.
- **Replace the refer-a-friend announcement bar with a member-pricing or loyalty callout** — If Purple has a rewards tier or Purple Pass, that's more relevant to a returning grid devotee than an acquisition referral CTA.
- **Add "Sale ends May 26" or a countdown below the CTA buttons** — Memorial Day is implied but not confirmed. One date line adds urgency without tricks.
