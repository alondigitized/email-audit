# Homepage snapshot · purple.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Purple is leading with the deal — $900 off a mattress and base for Memorial Day — and the execution is clean, confident, and well-timed. But I'm a GelFlex Grid devotee and the word "grid" is nowhere in this hero. This reads like a price-off sheet for first-time mattress shoppers, not a message to someone who already returned a Casper specifically because of the grid's pressure-relief science.

## What's working

- **The headline scale** — "Up to $900 off" is a genuine attention-stopper on mobile. I'm not scrolling past a four-digit markdown.
- **Dual CTA layout** — "Shop mattresses" paired with "Compare mattresses" is exactly right for someone like me. The Compare button is the one I'm tapping first.
- **Memorial Day flag** — The orange "MEMORIAL DAY SALE" label tells me this is live and time-sensitive without being frantic or fake.
- **Footnoted science claim** — "63% reduction in sleep interfering pain.² Details" — I actually like the footnote. It means there's a study. I will find that study and read it twice.
- **Clean mobile render** — No competing visual noise, no layout jank, fast-looking page. Nothing making me want to close the tab.

## What's weak

- **Zero GelFlex Grid mention** — The grid tech is why I chose Purple over Casper. This hero could be any mattress sale from any brand. New shoppers are left with pricing, not the product story that justifies the price.
- **Bundle pricing absent** — I wait specifically for mattress + pillow + sheet set bundles. What's on offer is mattress + base, which is fine, but it's not the purchase my wallet opens for.
- **Lifestyle image cut off** — The couple in bed barely clears the viewport edge on mobile. It reads as a cropped thumbnail, not persuasion.
- **No review counts or social proof** — There's nothing above the fold that tells me 150,000 other people chose this. For a skeptic who returned a competitor's product, that number matters.
- **No free-shipping callout** — I shouldn't have to hunt for shipping terms before I decide to tap into a product page on a $1,000+ purchase.

## Recommendations

- **Add a GelFlex Grid reference to the hero** — Even one line of body copy: "Powered by the GelFlex® Grid — not memory foam." Existing fans feel seen; first-timers get curious and Google it.
- **Introduce a bundle CTA** — A third button or a line under the hero: "See the Mattress + Pillow + Sheet Bundle." That's the SKU I'm waiting to pull the trigger on.
- **Surface a review count badge** — "⭐ 4.8 · 150,000+ reviews" as a one-liner under the headline. Zero design overhead, significant credibility payoff.
- **Pin a free-shipping strip** — One line: "Free shipping + free returns on all mattresses." Put it between the logo and the sale headline. This should not be below the fold.
