# Homepage snapshot · purple.com · 2026-07-20
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-20**
## Executive summary

- Purple is running a Back to School push with a warm family lifestyle hero and a queen mattress price anchor at $999 — clean, timely, seasonally on point. As someone who already bought into the GelFlex grid and has the egg-drop ad burned into my brain, this homepage feels pitched at people who have never heard of Purple, not at returning devotees waiting for bundle pricing to drop. Competent execution, wrong audience depth.

## What's working

- **$999 queen anchor in the promo banner** — clean price floor, I know immediately where the deal starts without digging through a nav
- **Family lifestyle hero** — warm, multicultural couple laughing in bed with their kid; that is genuinely my Saturday morning and it lands
- **"Back to School" framing timed correctly** — late July is the right window for this campaign; doesn't feel stale or premature
- **SleepScore badge on the hero image** — third-party validation embedded naturally inside the photo rather than shoved into the footer where I'd never see it
- **"Less pain. Better sleep." tagline** — short, medically adjacent, direct; fits the register I expect from a brand I trust to explain the science

## What's weak

- **Zero GelFlex grid science above the fold** — the whole reason I picked Purple over Casper is the technology, and it's invisible here; a new visitor has no idea why this mattress costs more than foam
- **No bundle pricing hook** — I specifically wait for the mattress + pillow + sheet set combo, and the one CTA drops me into a generic sale page, not a bundle builder
- **Chat widget overlaps the hero headline** — the word "advantage" in "unfair advantage" is literally hidden behind the purple chat bubble; sloppy on mobile
- **No urgency deadline** — "Back to School" implies a window but commits to nothing; without an "ends August 4" I'll bookmark this and come back whenever
- **Hamburger-only nav** — no visible category strip for Mattresses / Pillows / Bundles, so reaching the bundle page costs me two taps before I've even started browsing

## Recommendations

- **Add a bundle-deal tile alongside the single-item CTA** — put "Shop the Bundle (Mattress + Pillow + Sheets)" next to "Shop sale" with a bundled starting price; that's the conversion I'm actually waiting to make
- **Surface one GelFlex grid callout above the fold** — even one line like "The grid that floats pressure points — not foam" with a tap through to the science explainer; give new visitors the egg-drop moment
- **Add a hard expiry date to the Back to School banner** — "Ends Aug 4" closes the mental escape hatch and stops me from saving it for later
- **Fix the mobile chat widget z-index** — it should never overlap the primary hero headline; drop it lower or apply tighter clearance rules for the text block above
