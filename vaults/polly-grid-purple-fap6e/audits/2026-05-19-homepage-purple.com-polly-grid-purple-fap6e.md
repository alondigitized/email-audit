# Homepage snapshot · purple.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- This is my brand doing exactly what my brand does — leading with science and backing it up with a number big enough to actually move me. "Up to $900 off a mattress + base" plus the "63% reduction in sleep interfering pain" footnote is the one-two punch Purple does better than anyone in this category. That said, the hero image is generic bedroom lifestyle — not the GelFlex grid I actually came here for — and the bundle I've been patiently waiting on (mattress + pillow + sheet set) is nowhere in the above-the-fold experience.

## What's working

- **The "MEMORIAL DAY SALE" label in orange** — instantly signals urgency and a real campaign moment, not generic evergreen copy
- **The dollar-amount headline** — at Purple's price point, "$900 off" converts better than any percentage; I can do that math in my head in half a second
- **The "63% reduction in sleep interfering pain²" subtext** — footnoted, with a Details link. That's the science-explainer voice I read twice. This is why I'm not at Casper's site right now
- **The dual-CTA structure** — "Shop mattresses" for the deal-ready, "Learn what makes us different" for the science-curious. I'm both, and Purple knows it
- **No forced modal on landing** — I hit the page, saw the offer, nobody tried to wrestle my email out of me before I could breathe

## What's weak

- **The hero image is generic bedroom lifestyle** — a couple in bed. Purple's entire brand equity is the GelFlex grid. Where's the egg-drop? Where's the pressure-map cutaway? This could be any mattress brand's stock art
- **"Mattress + base" isn't my bundle** — I wait for mattress + pillow + sheet set packages. The base I already have. The offer I actually want isn't anywhere in this viewport
- **No urgency anchor** — "Memorial Day Sale" implies a deadline but doesn't commit to one. No countdown, no "ends May 26." I'm interested but not clicking today because nothing is chasing me
- **"Refer & get $100" is doing nothing for a returning shopper** — I've already been converted. Give me a loyalty credit or a returning-buyer exclusive, not a cold referral banner

## Recommendations

- **Swap the bedroom lifestyle shot for a GelFlex grid close-up or egg-drop visual** — Purple's differentiation lives in that image; the hero has to earn it, and right now it isn't
- **Add "ends May 26" or a visible countdown below the offer headline** — I've been telling myself I'd buy on the right sale; give me one concrete deadline to stop waiting
- **Surface the mattress + pillow + sheet set bundle** — even a single line like "Bundle and save more — add pillows + sheets at checkout" would hold me on the page and drive AOV
- **Replace the refer-a-friend banner with a returning-buyer hook** — "Back for more? Your Memorial Day exclusive is inside" does more for retention than a generic referral banner aimed at strangers
