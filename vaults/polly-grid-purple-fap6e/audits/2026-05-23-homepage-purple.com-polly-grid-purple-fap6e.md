# Homepage snapshot · purple.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Purple is in full Memorial Day mode, and they're not being subtle about it — $900 off a mattress and base is the whole story above the fold. As someone who came here already sold on the grid technology, I'm a little underwhelmed that the science angle is buried in supporting copy rather than leading. This feels like any mattress brand running a May sale, not the egg-drop-ad people I know and trust.

## What's working

- **The dollar amount is big and credible.** "Up to $900 off" in bold stops the scroll. That's real money on a considered purchase and it earns a second look.
- **"Our best price of the year"** is the kind of claim I actually believe from a brand I already follow. It sets stakes for the sale without feeling scammy.
- **The "63% reduction in sleep interfering pain² Details" line** — I know Purple does the sleep-science thing and I would absolutely tap "Details." That footnote with a citation style signals they can back it up. Very on-brand for a company that put a raw egg on a grid.
- **Secondary CTA "Learn what makes us different"** is exactly what I'd want for a fence-sitter or someone new. Good funnel thinking.
- **Clean mobile layout** — no horizontal scroll, no text crammed into corners. The cream background feels premium, not cheap.

## What's weak

- **Zero GelFlex/grid identity above the fold.** I came here because of the grid. There's nothing in the hero that says *Purple specifically* — swap the logo and you could be on any mattress sale page. The product differentiation that drives my loyalty is invisible.
- **"Mattress + base" bundle is not my bundle.** I'm waiting for mattress + pillow + sheet set pricing. This deal doesn't match my buying pattern and I'd have to go hunting to find out if the full bundle is on sale too.
- **No trust signals like review counts or ratings** anywhere visible. For a ~$1,000+ purchase I want to see "40,000 five-star reviews" or similar social proof before I even tap the CTA.
- **The referral banner ("Refer & get $100")** is aimed at me going out and finding friends, not rewarding me for being here already. Feels like the wrong message for someone who's a returning fan.
- **Lifestyle image at bottom is barely visible** — I can see a couple in bed but not enough to feel aspirational or connected to the product.

## Recommendations

- **Add a grid/GelFlex callout to the hero** — even a one-liner like "Powered by the GelFlex Grid" under the headline would anchor why Purple's sale is different from a random mattress retailer's.
- **Surface the full bundle deal** — if a mattress + pillow + sheet set bundle exists in the Memorial Day sale, put it in the hero or immediately below it. "Bundle and save more" with a tap-to-explore would serve a shopper like me directly.
- **Drop social proof just below the CTA** — a star rating line ("Rated 4.8 stars by 60,000+ sleepers") fits neatly between the CTA and the lifestyle image without adding scroll burden.
- **Flip the referral strip to a loyalty/VIP signal** — "Member? Log in to see your exclusive pricing" would convert a returning devotee faster than asking me to recruit strangers.
