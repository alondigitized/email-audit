# Homepage snapshot · www.booking.com · 2026-09-27
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-27**
## Executive summary

- Booking.com knows exactly one thing about me before I've even scrolled: dangle 10% off and get me to log in. Before I can see a single deal, date, or destination, a "Genius — Sign in, save money" modal slams down over the whole search form. It reads like a growth-team A/B test win, not a homepage built for someone who's already Genius Level 3 and just wants to search.

## What's working

- **The Genius branding itself** — I already filter by Genius perks, so seeing that gold-and-blue logo front and center is at least the right shorthand for my brand.
- **"Save 10% or more"** — a real number, not vague "great deals" fluff. That's the kind of concrete offer I actually read past.
- **Stays tab pre-selected** — behind the modal I can see the Stays/Flights/Flight+Hotel toggle is already on Stays, so whoever built this knows hotel search is the default entry point.

## What's weak

- **The modal blocks my actual task** — check-in date, occupancy, and the Search button are all sitting right there, greyed out under an overlay I have to dismiss first. That's friction between me and the filter chips I came for.
- **No recognition that I'm already Genius Level 3** — this is a cold-acquisition "sign in and save" pitch, not a "welcome back, here's your Level 3 pricing" message. For a repeat Genius user that's a missed personalization layer.
- **Hero copy is completely unreadable** — I can only make out fragments ("F...", "se...", "Fr...", "ho...") bleeding around the modal edges. Whatever the actual value prop is, it's invisible on open.
- **Zero imagery** — no destination photo, no traveler shot, nothing that signals "couples getaway" or "solo trip." Just chrome and a modal.
- **No urgency or deadline** — "10% or more" has no clock on it, so nothing pushes me to act now vs. later.

## Recommendations

- Swap the generic "Sign in, save money" modal for a returning-Genius variant — detect existing members and show "Your Genius Level 3 discount is active" instead of a cold acquisition pitch.
- Don't block the search form on load — let me see check-in dates and the Search button before asking me to log in; gate the discount at checkout instead.
- Put one destination or lifestyle photo in the hero so there's something for the eye to land on besides form fields.
- Add a small "free cancellation" or "breakfast included" chip near the search box — those are literally the two filters I hunt for first.
