# Homepage snapshot · www.booking.com · 2026-05-31
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-31**
## Executive summary

- Booking.com is all business — global accommodation platform with a functional search-first layout — but what I'm actually seeing first is a Genius sign-in modal wall that's physically covering the homepage hero. There's a search form underneath it, some stay/flight tabs at the top, and somewhere behind the popup there's copy I literally cannot read. The whole above-the-fold experience right now is: "please log in before we show you anything."

## What's working

- **Genius modal CTA — "Sign in or register"**: full-width blue button, massive tap target on mobile. For someone who knows the Genius program, "save 10% or more" is exactly the hook that gets a click.
- **Stays tab as default active state**: I didn't have to think — the app opened to my primary use case. Flights and Flight + Hotel are right there if I want to add a bundle.
- **Search form fields already surfaced**: Check-in/Check-out + guest count + the gold Search button are visible below the modal. The action path is clear the second I dismiss the popup.
- **Modal has a clear X dismiss button**: I can get out. It's not a forced gate. That earns some goodwill.

## What's weak

- **Modal as first viewport experience**: I'm a Genius Level 3 user — the app should recognize my session and skip this entirely. Treating me like a new visitor is a brand misstep. I'm not "saving money with a free membership," I'm already deep in the loyalty stack.
- **Hero is 100% invisible**: There's apparently copy behind that modal ("F..." and "S..." peeking from the left edge) but I cannot read a single word of it. If Booking is running a summer campaign, a last-minute deal push, or anything seasonal — I have no idea.
- **No free cancellation signal anywhere**: This is the first filter chip I tap on every search. Nowhere in the above-the-fold area does "free cancellation" appear. That's a miss for my persona specifically.
- **No personalization or recent-search recall**: Zero "pick up where you left off" energy. No "Barcelona, 3 nights" from my last visit. No "your saved properties" nudge. Just a cold search form.
- **"2 adults · 0 children · 1 room" default**: I mostly travel solo or as a couple — 2 adults is technically fine, but there's no sense the app knows me at all.

## Recommendations

- **Auto-dismiss the Genius modal if the user has an active session / cookie**: I shouldn't see a sign-in wall when I'm already Genius L3. Instead, replace it with a "Welcome back, Beck — your Genius discount is active" inline badge on the search form.
- **Surface "Free cancellation" as a persistent chip below the Stays tab**: One tap to toggle it as a default filter. This is table stakes for my persona and costs nothing in UI real estate.
- **Expose a last-minute deals strip or a price-drop rail above the search form**: Even one "Prices dropped this week" chip with a destination image would give me a reason to browse instead of just execute a planned search.
- **Show the hero behind the modal — or scrap the modal for signed-in users entirely**: The hero copy is invisible. Whatever campaign is running is being completely wasted on the first paint.
