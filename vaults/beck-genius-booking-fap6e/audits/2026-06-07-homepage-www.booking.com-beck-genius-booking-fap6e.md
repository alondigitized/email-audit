# Homepage snapshot · www.booking.com · 2026-06-07
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-07**
## Executive summary

- Booking.com lands immediately on brand — dark navy, clean Genius logo, everything I'd expect from a platform I've used enough to hit Level 3. But the first thing I actually see is a modal blocking the entire homepage, asking me to sign in to access the exact savings I already have. That's a friction-first welcome for a returning user, and it immediately tells me this site doesn't know me yet in this session.

## What's working

- **The Genius sign-in modal** — headline "Sign in, save money" is exactly the language that hooks deal-hunters like me. The 10% figure is concrete and I trust it.
- **The Genius branding** — the yellow dot on the "i" is iconic at this point. I know what it means without reading.
- **The search form** — visible behind/below the modal: destination field, check-in/out dates, guests/rooms, and the gold "Search" button. The whole flow is laid out without scrolling.
- **Tab navigation** — Stays (pre-selected), Flights, Flight + Hotel. I can see my primary mode immediately without hunting.

## What's weak

- **The modal is a dark pattern for returning users** — if I have Genius Level 3, I'm clearly a loyal customer. Throwing a sign-in interruption at me before I can even see the homepage hero is the opposite of a VIP welcome.
- **Hero content completely blocked** — I have no idea what campaign Booking is running right now. Summer sale? Flight deals? City spotlight? Can't tell. The modal ate it all.
- **No urgency or time-bounded offer** — "save 10% or more" is the standard Genius evergreen. There's no "last 3 rooms," no countdown, nothing that would make me move faster.
- **No free cancellation signal above the fold** — that's literally the first filter I toggle. It's not mentioned anywhere visible.
- **No breakfast / bundled perks callout** — same deal, the "breakfast included" hook I hunt for is nowhere in the first screen.

## Recommendations

- **Detect returning Genius users and skip the sign-in modal** — or at minimum show a "Welcome back, Beck" state that surfaces my Genius level and current savings balance instead of cold-treating me like a new visitor.
- **Swap the modal for a persistent banner** — put the "You're saving 10% as a Genius member — sign in to unlock" as a slim strip at the top, not a full-screen takeover. Let the hero breathe.
- **Surface a free-cancellation filter chip or badge prominently in the hero area** — even just "Filter by: Free cancellation · Breakfast included" as pre-set quick filters would immediately speak to how I actually shop.
- **Add a time-based hook** — "Early-bird rates for July: book by June 15" or "Last-minute weekend deals" in the hero would give me a reason to act now vs. come back later.
