# Homepage snapshot · www.booking.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- Booking.com hits me with a Genius sign-in modal before I even see the homepage — and honestly, as a Genius Level 3 member who lives for that member pricing, I'm conflicted. The offer is real and relevant, but covering the entire screen before I've had a single second to orient myself feels aggressive. Underneath the modal I can just barely make out a resort-looking hero and the search form, which tells me the page is doing travel booking correctly in skeleton form — but I'm reviewing a popup, not a homepage.

## What's working

- **Genius modal headline ("Sign in, save money"):** Direct, benefit-led, zero fluff. I read "save 10% or more" and my filter-chip brain immediately registers this as a Genius member deal. That's the pull.
- **"Sign in or register" CTA button:** Blue, full-width, impossible to miss. No ambiguity about what happens next.
- **Tab navigation above the modal:** "Stays / Flights / Flight + Hotel" is visible and tappable even with the modal up — I can dismiss and jump to my category without hunting.
- **Genius branding with the crown icon:** The logo up top already shows my Genius level badge. Booking knows who I am before I even tap in, which creates continuity with the modal's message.
- **Search form partially visible below the modal:** "Where are you going?" and the date/guest fields are right there. One dismiss and I'm already in booking mode.

## What's weak

- **Full-screen modal on first load:** As a Genius Level 3 member I'm already signed in on my app — seeing a "sign in" nag on mobile web feels like Booking.com forgot I exist. This modal would make me tap X immediately.
- **Zero travel context visible:** No destination imagery, no "Genius deals in [city]," no "your saved properties" — nothing to inspire a search. The entire above-fold is a sign-in gate.
- **No free cancellation mention anywhere:** That's my first filter. Not even a badge or a "fully refundable" callout in the modal subtext.
- **No urgency or deal specificity:** "10% or more" is the offer but there's no "5 properties left at this price" or countdown anywhere. For a last-minute deal hunter, this is a missed heartbeat.
- **No breakfast-included or Genius-exclusive property signal:** My second filter is breakfast included — nothing here suggests Booking.com knows that about me or has properties matching it.

## Recommendations

- **Surface the member's last search or saved trip behind the modal:** "Welcome back, Beck — you were looking at Lisbon" beats a generic sign-in nag for a returning Genius member. One line of personalization turns this from friction to a hook.
- **Add "free cancellation" to the modal subtext or a persistent badge:** "Save 10%+ on fully refundable stays" speaks directly to my filter habits and makes the Genius offer feel safer, not just cheaper.
- **Replace the generic "Sign in or register" copy with a benefit-specific variant:** "Sign in to unlock Genius deals" or "Access your Genius 3 rates" would convert me faster than a generic CTA that reads like a paywall.
- **Show at least one deal card or destination thumbnail behind (or beside) the modal:** Even a blurred "Genius rates from $89/night in Barcelona" peeking through would give me a reason to sign in rather than just a threat that I'm missing out.
