# Homepage snapshot · www.booking.com · 2026-07-19
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-19**
## Executive summary

- First screen I get is a Genius sign-in modal eating the entire viewport — so Booking is immediately leading with its loyalty pitch, which fine, I respect the hustle. As a Genius Level 3 guy who hunts the 10%-off badge before anything else, the message is directionally right. But I'm already a member, and calling me to "sign in or register" like I might be a tourist stumbling in for the first time is a swing and a miss on personalization.

## What's working

- **Genius branding in the modal** — the blue Genius logo is the first thing I see, and as Level 3 that's my identity on this platform. Speaks my language instantly.
- **"Save 10% or more" copy** — this is the kind of concrete number I scan for in subject lines. Seeing it above the fold before I even touch the search bar primes me correctly.
- **Search form visible below the modal** — even with the interstitial up, the destination/dates/occupancy fields are partially visible. That's smart — I know the tool is right there once I dismiss.
- **Tab bar (Stays / Flights / Flight + Hotel)** — one tap to my primary category. Clean, no friction finding Stays.

## What's weak

- **Modal treats me like a stranger** — "Sign in or register" conflates a Genius Level 3 power user with a brand-new visitor. If Booking knows who I am (cookies, app, prior session), this should say "Welcome back, Beck — sign in to unlock your Genius rate." The "or register" half is noise.
- **Entire homepage content is hidden** — I can't see a single deal, destination, or campaign image. A deal hunter's first move is to scan for price signals; this modal removes all of them.
- **No free-cancellation callout in the modal** — that's my #1 filter chip and it's nowhere in the Genius pitch. "Save 10%" is great, but "save 10% on free-cancellation stays" would be a direct hit.
- **No urgency in the offer** — "save 10% or more" is evergreen, not a reason to act now. No "limited Genius inventory" or "prices rising this weekend" signal.

## Recommendations

- **Personalize the modal for returning members** — detect the logged-out-but-returning state via cookies and swap "Sign in or register" for "Sign back in — your Genius Level 3 rates are waiting." Kill the "or register" branch for clearly returning users.
- **Add a free-cancellation callout to the Genius pitch** — literally append one line: "Includes free-cancellation options." That's the filter I set before I scroll anywhere.
- **Add a soft dismiss shortcut** — a "Continue browsing" text link below the CTA button so I can peek at the homepage deals without committing to sign-in. Right now X is my only escape, which reads as pushy.
- **Show one deal teaser behind or below the modal** — even a blurred "Amsterdam from $89/night — Genius exclusive" peek strip would give me a reason to sign in rather than just asking me to.
