# Homepage snapshot · www.booking.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- First thing I see opening Booking.com is a full-screen Genius sign-in modal — before I can even read the hero. As a Genius Level 3 member, I appreciate that they know who their high-value customers are, and "save 10% or more" is exactly the hook I respond to. But the entire homepage is being held hostage by this interstitial, and that's a friction call I have complicated feelings about.

## What's working

- **Genius modal with "save 10% or more"** — hits me exactly where I live. I read every price-drop email subject; a guarantee of 10%+ is the kind of number that stops my scroll.
- **"Stays" tab already selected and highlighted** — I didn't have to hunt for where to go. One tap and I'm in my category.
- **"2 adults · 0 children · 1 room" default in the search form** — that's me and my partner, pre-filled. Tiny detail, meaningful shortcut.
- **"Sign in or register" modal has a visible X/dismiss** — they're pushy but not trapping me. Points for the escape hatch.
- **Account icon has a red notification dot** — if I'm already logged in, that teases something new waiting for me (a deal, a reward update), which I'd tap.

## What's weak

- **The modal blocks the entire hero** — I cannot read what the homepage is actually promoting. As someone who came here to browse deals, I'm immediately detoured into an auth flow instead of seeing what's on offer.
- **No free cancellation mention anywhere above the fold** — that's filter chip #1 for me. I always search with it. Why isn't it in the pitch?
- **No lifestyle imagery whatsoever** — the whole screen is a navy nav, a white modal, and a search form. No destination photography, no "picture yourself here" pull. Travel is a visual purchase.
- **Search form destination field is behind the modal** — the actual destination input is obscured. I can see the date fields and guest count but not the primary search entry point.
- **No Genius Level 3 acknowledgment** — the modal says "free Booking.com membership" like I'm new here. I'm L3. Speak to me like it.

## Recommendations

- **Personalize the modal for known Genius members** — if I'm logged in or returning, swap "Sign in, save money" for "Welcome back, Beck — your L3 discount is unlocked." Show me my saved properties or recent searches instead of treating me like a cold visitor.
- **Add a free-cancellation badge to the search form** — a small "Free cancellation available on most stays" line under the Search button costs nothing and captures my #1 filter preference before I even run a query.
- **Swap the full-screen modal for a sticky Genius bar** — a slim banner at the top ("Sign in to save 10%+ with Genius — it's free") preserves the homepage experience while still driving the sign-in conversion, without blocking the hero.
- **Show one destination card or deal tile below the search form** — even one "Trending: Lisbon from $89/night" teaser would give me something to react to and pull me deeper into the funnel.
