# Homepage snapshot · www.booking.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- First thing I see when I land? A Genius sign-in modal eating the entire homepage. Behind it there's a search form half-visible and what might be an interesting hero — no idea, the popup killed it. Booking knows their loyalty game is the product, and they're leading with it hard. This feels like a platform built for deal-hunters like me, but the execution on this cold load is friction-first.

## What's working

- **Genius modal headline "Sign in, save money"** — dead-simple value prop for someone like me who thinks in deal deltas. No fluff.
- **"10% or more" specificity** — I respond to numbers. "Save more" is meaningless; a percentage hooks me.
- **"Sign in or register" CTA** — blue button, full width, no ambiguity. Mobile-optimized tap target.
- **Stays / Flights / Flight + Hotel nav tabs** — the "Stays" tab is already active and selected, exactly where I'd start. One tap to switch if I need flights.
- **Search form visible below modal** — I can see the date-picker and occupancy field peeking through. The Search button is there. The core job-to-be-done is accessible.
- **Profile icon with red notification dot** — signals I may already have an account; the system seems to know me even before I sign in.

## What's weak

- **Modal blocks the hero entirely** — the homepage is saying "F... S..." and I can only guess what the actual value prop is. If Booking spent money on a May campaign, I'll never see it.
- **No free cancellation callout above the fold** — that's my first filter, always. It's not here. The modal pushed it out.
- **No breakfast included or deal-filter shortcut** — as Genius Level 3 I'm here to filter fast, and nothing on screen lets me do that.
- **Modal shown to what looks like a returning user** — there's a red notification badge on the profile icon suggesting I already have an account. Showing me a "sign in" modal anyway is tone-deaf.
- **No current campaign or date-range urgency** — zero signal about whether there's a Memorial Day deal, a last-minute window, anything. The page is dateless.
- **No recently viewed or personalized suggestions** — I've been on Booking hundreds of times; there's no "pick up where you left off" hook at all.

## Recommendations

- **Auto-dismiss the Genius modal for recognized or logged-in sessions** — if the profile icon is lit with a notification badge, I'm likely a returning user. Fire the modal only for truly unrecognized cold sessions; show me my Genius dashboard instead.
- **Surface a "Free cancellation · Breakfast included" quick-filter strip below the search bar** — these are my two pre-scroll filters; baking them as one-tap chips saves me 30 seconds every visit.
- **Add a seasonal urgency strip behind (or above) the modal** — a slim banner like "Memorial Day weekend: prices dropping fast → search now" would give me a reason to act immediately rather than just sign in and wander.
- **Inject a "Your recent searches" or "Prices changed for your saved trips" hook** — Genius Level 3 means I have data. Show me a price-drop alert for a hotel I've looked at and I'm converting in under a minute.
