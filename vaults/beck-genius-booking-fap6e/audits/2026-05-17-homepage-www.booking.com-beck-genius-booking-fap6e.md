# Homepage snapshot · www.booking.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- First thing I see is a Genius sign-in modal eating almost the entire viewport — "Sign in, save money." For a Genius Level 3 member like me who literally built my travel habits around this program, it's the right message in the right place. Booking.com is leading with their loyalty hook hard, and that's not a bad call. But the modal buries all the destination imagery, deals, and product rails behind it, so the homepage is essentially a login wall right now.

## What's working

- **Genius modal headline "Sign in, save money"** — shortest possible path to the value prop for a returning member; I immediately know why I should sign in
- **"Save 10% or more" specificity** — a real percentage rather than "exclusive member rates" feels credible
- **Full-width blue CTA** — high contrast, clear label, no guessing if it's tappable
- **Navigation tabs (Stays / Flights / Flight + Hotel)** — visible above the modal; one tap to my category without any friction
- **Search form below the modal** — already pre-populated with "2 adults · 0 children · 1 room," respects whatever context I set last session

## What's weak

- **Modal blocks the entire hero** — the destination image behind it (looks like a resort with a blue/teal waterfront) is completely obscured; I can't dream-browse before committing to sign in
- **"Or more" on the discount** — I know the Genius tiers go higher, but a Genius 3 member would want to see their actual tier benefit ("you get 15–20% off") not the generic floor offer
- **No deal hooks visible** — as someone who reads every price-drop subject line, I want to see a "Flash deal: Barcelona this weekend from $89" above the fold, not just an auth prompt
- **No free cancellation badge** — my #1 filter; zero mention of it in this viewport
- **No urgency or time-bound signal** — the Genius offer has no deadline, no "book by Sunday," nothing to drive tap-now behavior
- **Modal close button (X) is the only escape** — no "continue as guest" path visible, which feels slightly coercive

## Recommendations

- **Surface a live deal behind the modal** — even a blurred-but-readable "Today's deal: Rome from $79" in the hero background gives me a reason to sign in *right now* rather than just someday
- **Tier-personalized Genius copy** — if you know I'm Genius Level 3, say "You unlock up to 20% off" not "save 10% or more"; the generic floor price undersells the relationship
- **Add a free cancellation badge to the search form** — a small "Free cancellation available" chip next to the date fields costs nothing and hits my #1 filter criterion before I even start searching
- **Inject one urgency cue** — "Limited deals expiring tonight" or a count of deals in my last-searched destination would turn this auth prompt into a genuine FOMO trigger
