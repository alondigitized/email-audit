# Homepage snapshot · www.booking.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Booking.com opens with a full-screen Genius interstitial before I've even seen a destination. For a Genius Level 3 member like me, the hook is relevant — "save 10% or more" is my language — but it's still an interruption gate that buries the actual homepage behind a modal. The brand reads utility-first, no-frills, deal-focused, which tracks with how I actually use the product. Right now it's pushing Genius sign-in hard, which tells me they're in an acquisition/retention push.

## What's working

- **The Genius modal targeting**: I'm literally Genius Level 3 and this modal opens with the Genius logo in gold-and-blue. "Sign in, save money" is exactly the two-word pitch that makes me stop scrolling on email subjects. This is the right message for the right person.
- **Concrete savings number**: "save 10% or more" beats vague language like "exclusive member benefits." I know what 10% means on a $200/night hotel. That math happens instantly for someone like me.
- **Search widget defaults**: "2 adults · 0 children · 1 room" — correct default for how I actually search (solo or couple trips). No friction to change.
- **"Stays" pre-selected in nav**: I don't have to tap anything to get to my primary use case. One fewer barrier.
- **Profile notification dot**: The red dot on the account icon tells me something is waiting — could be a price drop alert. That's a pull mechanic that works on me every time.

## What's weak

- **Modal blocks the hero entirely**: The actual homepage — any campaign imagery, seasonal messaging, destination inspiration — is invisible. I came to browse, not to authenticate. The X button is there but this is still an interruption tax on every new session.
- **No destination or property imagery above fold**: Travel is visual. I should see a coastal villa or a Tokyo skyline or *something* that makes me want to search. Instead I get a white card with a logo.
- **Zero "free cancellation" or "breakfast included" signal**: These are my first two filters on every search. They're not mentioned anywhere in this viewport. This page doesn't know I care about them.
- **No early-bird or last-minute deal framing**: No "prices from $X" rail, no "deals this weekend," no urgency hook beyond the Genius pitch. Price-drop hunting is my whole thing and I don't feel it here.
- **No trust signals beyond Genius brand**: No review counts, no "X million properties," nothing that tells me why Booking.com has what I'm looking for versus a competitor.

## Recommendations

- **Show one personalized deal strip behind the modal or immediately after closing it** — "Deals for Genius members this weekend" with two or three real properties and prices. I closed the modal, don't make me start from zero.
- **Surface a "free cancellation" badge or filter chip in the search widget** — even a pre-checked toggle labeled "Free cancellation only" would signal that Booking.com knows what I care about before I even search.
- **Swap the blocking modal for a sticky banner** — put "Genius: sign in to save 10%+" as a dismissable top strip instead of a full-screen gate. The offer lands just as well without killing the browse experience.
- **Add a "Last-minute deals" or "Price drops this week" shortcut below the search bar** — one destination card with a crossed-out price and a new price. That's the deal pocket I'm hunting and it would make me scroll instantly.
