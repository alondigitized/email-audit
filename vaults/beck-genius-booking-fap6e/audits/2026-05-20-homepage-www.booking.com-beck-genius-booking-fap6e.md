# Homepage snapshot · www.booking.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- Booking.com opens with a full-screen Genius sign-in modal before I can see a single deal, property card, or price drop. The Genius branding lands — that's literally my program — but I'm Level 3, not some first-timer clicking a Facebook retarget ad. Treating me like an unknown visitor and gating the homepage behind a sign-in wall feels off. The actual page behind the modal is a warm-destination resort shot that I can barely make out. Brand reads premium and global; right now it's pushing membership acquisition hard.

## What's working

- **Genius branding front and center**: The blue Genius logo with the gold star badge is immediately recognizable and signals "this place knows me" — even if it doesn't actually recognize my Level 3 status.
- **"Sign in, save money" headline**: Clean, direct, zero fluff. Millennial-appropriate register, not trying too hard.
- **"Save 10% or more" specificity**: A percentage beats "save more" every time. The concreteness makes me want to tap.
- **Search form still accessible below the modal**: "Where are you going?" + check-in/out + guest config is all visible below the fold of the modal — the intent path isn't totally buried.
- **Clear X to dismiss**: Top-right close button is easy to find, no dark-pattern tricks to trap me in the modal.

## What's weak

- **No Level 3 recognition**: Every Genius member gets the same sign-in prompt. I've stayed 30+ times; showing me the same acquisition pitch as a new user is a miss. A "Welcome back, Level 3" hook would land completely differently.
- **Entire homepage deal layer is blocked**: The property cards, featured destinations, and whatever promotional strip sits below the hero — all invisible. I can't browse before being forced to decide whether to sign in.
- **No free-cancellation signal**: "Free cancellation" is literally the first filter I toggle. It's not mentioned anywhere in what's visible.
- **No urgency or deal context**: No "X properties just dropped in price" or "last-minute deals for this weekend" copy. The deal-hunter signal that should pull me in is completely absent above fold.
- **Resort image behind modal is generic luxury**: What little I can see of the background hero image reads expensive resort — fine, but there's no deal framing layered on it, no "from $89/night" or early-bird hook.

## Recommendations

- **Detect Genius Level 3 and swap the modal copy**: Instead of "Sign in, save money," show "Welcome back, Genius Level 3 — your 12% discount is waiting." Personalization this simple would completely change the emotional tone.
- **Surface a deal strip below the sign-in prompt**: Keep the modal but show 2-3 property cards or a "Deals expiring today" rail underneath, so I can preview what I'm signing in for before committing the tap.
- **Add "Free cancellation · Breakfast included" as visible badge copy**: Even just one line under the Genius offer — "All your filters, ready to go" — signals that my preferences are saved and waiting.
- **Introduce a time-bound urgency signal**: "12 properties near you dropped in price today" pinned to the modal or the search bar below would activate the deal-hunter reflex immediately.
