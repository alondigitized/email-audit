# Homepage snapshot · www.booking.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- Booking.com is showing me a cruise push the moment I open the app, which is a wild choice — I'm here to hunt hotels with free cancellation and breakfast, not plan a balcony cabin. The Genius Level 3 loyalty identity I've earned is completely invisible above the fold. This feels like a platform clearing inventory on cruises, not a homepage built for me.

## What's working

- **Concrete deadline** — "Limited-time cruise offer: Book by 5/20" is exactly the kind of time-bounded copy that makes me pay attention. I read every subject line that says "price drop" and this scratches that itch.
- **Clear dollar value** — "Up to $1,200 to spend at sea" is a real number, not vague "savings." I can evaluate that instantly.
- **Unambiguous CTA button** — "Explore balcony deals" is direct; I know exactly where that tap goes.
- **Search bar below the fold cut** — "Where are you going?" is immediately accessible if I want to skip the promo entirely. Low friction path to my actual use case.
- **Tab bar (Stays / Flights / Flight + Hotel)** — I can see the Stays tab is active, which tells me the default context is roughly right even if the hero isn't.

## What's weak

- **Cruise hero on a hotel app** — I'm Genius Level 3 on stays. Booking.com knows my booking history and there's zero evidence it's using that data to personalize this screen for me.
- **Zero Genius loyalty surfacing** — My Genius Level 3 status, member discounts, or any loyalty callout is completely absent above the fold. That's my whole identity on this platform.
- **No "free cancellation" or "breakfast included" signals** — My two non-negotiable filters aren't hinted at anywhere. If I didn't already know Booking has those filters, this page wouldn't tell me.
- **No recently-viewed or saved properties** — I'm a returning power user; the app knows my history and ignores it entirely here.
- **Cruise imagery doesn't match me** — A resort balcony over what looks like Atlantis/Nassau is beautiful but it reads as a couples/family cruise product, not the solo/couples hotel deal I'm after.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the cruise hero for a personalized Genius deals rail** — I'm Level 3; show me 3 properties I've saved or recently viewed with the Genius discount badge and a free-cancellation icon. That's a tap, not a browse.
- **Add a "Your Genius discounts are live" sticky banner** — One line at the top of the hero: "Genius Level 3 · Up to 20% off your next stay." Costs nothing to ship, converts returning users immediately.
- **Inject filter-chip shortcut buttons into the search bar area** — Small chips below "Where are you going?" reading "Free cancellation · Breakfast included · Tonight" would get me to my preferred filtered view in one tap instead of three.
- **Move the cruise promo to a secondary card below the search bar** — It's a valid offer; just don't let it eat 100% of above-the-fold real estate when the user base is dominated by hotel bookers.
