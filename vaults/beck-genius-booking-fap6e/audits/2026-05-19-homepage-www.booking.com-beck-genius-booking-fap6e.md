# Homepage snapshot · www.booking.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Booking.com is doing exactly what I'd expect from them — utility-first, dark-blue everything, search widget front and center. But the first thing I see isn't a destination photo or a deal — it's a Genius sign-in modal blocking whatever hero copy is behind it. For a Genius Level 3 loyalist like me, this feels like being carded at a club I'm already a member of. The bones are right, but the execution on mobile open is a friction disaster.

## What's working

- **The Genius modal's "save 10% or more" copy** — as someone who hunts price-drop emails, seeing a concrete percentage immediately registers. It doesn't say "exclusive savings," it says 10%. That's the right register.
- **The search widget layout** — destination field, check-in/out dates, guest count, and Search button are all visible and structured in a single scrollable stack. One-action path to results.
- **"Stays" tab is pre-selected** — I'm a hotel guy, not a flights guy, and the default state gets me there without any friction.
- **Genius branding in the modal** — the yellow dot on "Genius" is instantly recognizable to a Booking loyalist. Feels premium, not generic.

## What's weak

- **Full-page modal on load** — covering the hero, the offer context, and any lifestyle imagery on first open is the single biggest engagement killer. I'm here to browse a deal or check a property — this modal is a bouncer, not a welcome.
- **Hero copy completely hidden** — whatever the headline was supposed to say (I can see partial letters: "F," "S," "Fr," "h" peeking around the modal edges) is totally unreadable. The whole brand moment is wasted.
- **Zero lifestyle imagery visible** — the background behind the modal is flat dark navy. No beach, no boutique hotel lobby, no couples-in-a-café. For a travel platform, this is a missed emotional hook.
- **No free cancellation mention above fold** — that filter chip is the first thing I reach for on every search, and it's not even teased here. Same with "breakfast included." These are my two primary trust signals and neither is surfaced.
- **Redundant account icon with red dot** — if I'm signed in, why am I seeing a Genius sign-in modal? If I'm not signed in, why does my icon have a red notification? The logged-in state logic feels inconsistent.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the modal on first load for recognized accounts** — if there's any session data, cookie, or app-state suggesting I've opened Booking before, suppress this modal. Surface the Genius discount as a persistent header strip instead ("Genius Level 3: 10–20% off your next stay").
- **Swap the flat navy hero for a seasonal lifestyle shot** — put a couple checking into a boutique hotel in May light, or a solo traveler with a coffee at a terrace hotel. Booking has thousands of property photos; use them to set the mood before I search.
- **Add a "Free cancellation deals" or "Last-minute deals" shortcut chip** below the search bar — I filter for this every single time. Give me a one-tap path into the deal pocket I actually live in.
- **Expose the Genius tier status** in the header (e.g., "Level 3" badge next to my account icon) so I feel rewarded immediately rather than prompted to "register" like a new user.
