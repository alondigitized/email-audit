# Homepage snapshot · www.kay.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- I opened Kay.com and the first thing I got was a full-screen email capture modal blocking everything behind it. I can see they're running a Memorial Day sale — up to 50% off — which honestly caught my eye because I literally have a Kay alert set in my holiday deal calendar. But the aggressive popup before I even got to browse feels like a bait-and-switch, and all the stuff I actually care about — engagement ring styles, diamond clarity, financing options — is buried under this lightbox.

## What's working

- **The 50% off Memorial Day headline**: That's a legitimate number and it landed for me immediately — I'll come back to check this sale.
- **Current campaign timing**: Showing a Memorial Day promo on Memorial Day weekend is basic but correct. It signals the site is live and maintained.
- **"Offer valid through 05/25/26 at 11:59 PM ET" fine-print deadline**: A real expiration date makes the offer feel credible vs. vague "limited time" language I see at Zales.
- **"ENGAGEMENT" category tab visible at bottom**: Even below the modal I can see the engagement section is one tap away — that's the breadcrumb I need to keep me from bouncing entirely.

## What's weak

- **Forced email-capture modal on first load**: This is the worst possible first impression for a comparison shopper. I'm actively cross-shopping Jared right now and Kay is making me fill out a form before I can even see a ring.
- **Primary CTA is "SIGN UP TO BE THE FIRST TO KNOW ABOUT OFFERS LIKE THIS"**: That's 12 words for an email subscribe button. I'm here to shop an engagement ring, not join a mailing list. The CTA is misaligned with my intent.
- **No financing visibility**: I'm financing-aware and this is likely a $2,000–$5,000 purchase. There's zero mention of 0% APR, Kay Credit, or monthly payment estimates in what I can see — Jared puts that above the fold.
- **Diamond/clarity content nowhere visible**: I read every diamond-clarity email; Kay has nothing in the visible viewport that speaks to that interest. This page feels like a mass-market sale event, not a jewelry destination.
- **Fine print volume**: Four separate exclusions crammed under the offer in tiny type — "while supplies last," "cannot be combined," "varies by store and online," "see Jewelry Expert for details." That's a lot of asterisks before I've even seen a product.

## Recommendations

- **Kill or delay the email modal**: Fire it on exit-intent or after 30 seconds of browsing, not on page load. Every second I spend dismissing a popup is a second I'm not looking at rings.
- **Add a financing teaser to the hero banner**: A single line like "0% APR for 12 months on qualifying purchases" would stop me from bouncing to Jared's site immediately — that's the comparison-point I'm always checking.
- **Swap the modal CTA to "Shop the Sale + Get Early Access"**: If you're going to force the signup form, at least make the button copy sound like I get something tangible right now, not just future emails.
- **Surface an engagement-ring specific sub-hero**: Even a sticky strip below the modal saying "Engagement Rings: Up to 50% Off — Shop Now" with a direct link would route high-intent shoppers like me past the noise.
