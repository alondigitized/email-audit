# Homepage snapshot · www.kay.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Okay so I literally can't even see Kay's homepage because they hit me with a full-screen email capture modal the second I landed. What I *can* see is that there's a Memorial Day sale going on — "Up To 50% OFF† Memorial Day Specials" — which, as someone who tracks the holiday jewelry calendar obsessively, I actually want to know about. But the forced popup is giving me anxiety. I'm an engagement ring shopper; I don't want to hand over my email before I've even seen a single diamond.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day timing:** As someone who tracks the holiday jewelry sale calendar, a 50% off Memorial Day promo is exactly what I'd be hunting for in mid-May — the timing is right.
- **Specific expiration date:** The fine print actually tells me "Offer valid through 05/25/26 at 11:59 PM ET" — that's rare and I appreciate knowing there's a real deadline, not manufactured urgency.
- **"ENGAGEMENT" tab visible at the bottom:** Even behind the modal, I can see the nav has an Engagement category one tap away — that's reassuring.
- **Clean modal rendering:** No broken images, no glitchy text — the modal itself looks polished on mobile.

## What's weak

- **Forced email modal as the first experience:** I came to shop diamonds, not join a list. This is the single biggest bounce trigger for someone like me — I'm already comparison-shopping Zales and Jared, and if Kay makes me work before I see any product, I'm going back to Chrome with the other tabs open.
- **35% vs. 50% discrepancy:** The top banner says "Up to 35% Off Everything" and the modal says "Up To 50% OFF†" — that instantly feels like bait-and-switch. Which is it? As a financing-aware shopper who reads the fine print, this erodes trust immediately.
- **Zero engagement-ring messaging in the modal:** If you know I'm a bridal shopper (maybe from past sessions), lead with "Save on Engagement Rings." Instead this is a generic sale pitch.
- **No financing mention:** For a major jewelry purchase, "financing available" or "0% APR" would be a huge hook. Nothing here.
- **"Exclusions apply" without specifics:** I know engagement rings are often excluded from blanket sales. Not telling me upfront means I'm skeptical the 50% even applies to what I want.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the forced modal on first visit; trigger it at exit intent or after 30 seconds of scroll.** Let me see the engagement collection first — earn my email, don't demand it as a toll.
- **Resolve the 35%/50% copy conflict:** Pick one number or be transparent ("Up to 50% on select styles, up to 35% sitewide") — the mismatch reads as deceptive.
- **Add a financing hook inside any email-capture modal:** Even one line — "Plus 12-month 0% APR available" — dramatically increases sign-up intent from shoppers like me who are managing a big purchase.
- **Make the email CTA bridal-specific:** Swap "SIGN UP TO BE THE FIRST TO KNOW ABOUT OFFERS LIKE THIS" for "Get Bridal Sale Alerts" with a ring image — same email, same list, far higher conversion from engagement shoppers.
