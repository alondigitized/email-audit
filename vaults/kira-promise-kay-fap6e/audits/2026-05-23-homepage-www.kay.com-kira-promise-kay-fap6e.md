# Homepage snapshot · www.kay.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Kay is literally throwing a pop-up in my face before I can see a single ring, but honestly? "Up to 50% off Memorial Day Specials" is the exact headline that makes me stop scrolling TikTok and open a new tab. It reads like a classic jewelry-chain holiday push — not the most elegant experience, but the offer is exactly what I've been waiting for. The problem is I can't actually see the homepage yet because this modal is sitting on top of everything.

## What's working

- **The offer itself** — "Up to 50% Off Memorial Day Specials" is exactly the kind of sale event I have saved in my calendar. I would read this email.
- **Hard deadline in the fine print** — "Offer valid through 05/25/26 at 11:59 PM ET." gives me two days and a real timestamp; that's genuine urgency, not fake.
- **Email capture framing** — "Be the first to know about offers like this" is smarter than "subscribe to our newsletter." It frames the value for me.
- **Background imagery** — the soft-focus couple behind the modal signals romance and jewelry without screaming at me, which fits the emotional register of engagement shopping.

## What's weak

- **The modal blocks everything** — I landed on Kay.com to *see rings*, and before I see a single stone I'm being asked for my email. This is backwards for a shopper who already has intent.
- **"Exclusions apply. See details below."** appears directly under the big offer — that caveat is doing real damage to the excitement. It makes me wonder if the ring I want is excluded.
- **The fine print paragraph is dense and tiny** — four sentences of legalese crammed below the CTA. For a financing-aware buyer who reads every word, this raises flags, not confidence.
- **No ring category shortcut** — even on the modal, a small "Shop Engagement Rings →" link would let me skip the email step and go straight to what I came for. Instead I have to dismiss and find it myself.
- **CTA copy is too long** — "SIGN UP TO BE THE FIRST TO KNOW ABOUT OFFERS LIKE THIS" is running six words past where it should stop.

## Recommendations

- **Add a secondary CTA on the modal**: a small "Shop the Sale →" text link alongside "No, Thanks" so intent-driven shoppers can skip the email gate without feeling punished.
- **Swap the vague exclusions caveat for a positive reframe**: instead of "Exclusions apply. See details below," try "Includes engagement rings, wedding bands, and more." Tell me what IS included.
- **Tighten the CTA copy** to "Get Early Access to Offers" — shorter, still valuable, same idea.
- **Surface a ring-category tile or product thumbnail inside the modal** so the visual context is jewelry, not just a black button and a text box.
