# Homepage snapshot · www.kay.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Kay Jewelers is running a Memorial Day push, and the moment I land on the page I get zero seconds to breathe — an email-capture modal covers basically the entire screen. The brand signals "sale event" loudly, but the actual homepage content (products, categories, engagement imagery) is almost totally obscured. What little I can see underneath tells me they know their audience — "ENGAGEMENT" is right there in the bottom nav — but the modal wall means I'm being asked for my email before I've seen a single ring.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day discount headline** — "Up To 50% OFF† Memorial Day Specials" is a strong hook for someone like me who tracks the holiday sale calendar religiously. I know Memorial Day is *the* jewelry sale weekend and 50% is a number that makes me want to stay.
- **Deadline in the fine print** — "Offer through 05/25/26 at 11:59 PM ET" is a real, specific deadline. I actually appreciate that it's spelled out rather than vague "limited time."
- **ENGAGEMENT tab in the bottom nav** — even with the modal covering everything, I can see that category is one tap away once I dismiss.
- **Modal design is clean** — no garish colors, no countdown timer gimmick, just a simple email field and a button. Easy to read on mobile.

## What's weak

- **Modal-as-first-impression is a conversion killer for me** — I came here to look at engagement rings, not hand over my email before I've seen a single product. This is the number-one reason I'd tap "No, Thanks" and immediately feel slightly annoyed.
- **35% vs. 50% discrepancy** — the top banner says "Up to 35% Off Everything" and the modal says "50% OFF." Which is it? That inconsistency in the same viewport makes me suspicious before I've even entered the site.
- **No loyalty hook** — I have a Kay account and I cross-shop Zales constantly. If my rewards balance or member pricing were visible here, that would be a real reason to stay. There's nothing.
- **Zero product context** — the hero image behind the modal is barely visible. I can't see a ring, a diamond, a price point — nothing that would actually motivate me to browse.
- **Fine print is dense and defensive** — "Excludes previous purchases. Cannot be combined with any other offer. Promotions, prices, styles and availability may vary by store and online." That's a lot of "actually, maybe not" stacked right underneath the big offer.

## Recommendations

- **Delay the email modal by 8–10 seconds or trigger it on exit-intent** — let me land on the actual homepage first, see an engagement ring, maybe browse one category, *then* hit me with the signup ask. I'm far more likely to give my email after I've seen value.
- **Reconcile the offer messaging in one viewport** — pick one number (50% or 35%) and make it consistent from the top banner through the modal. Right now it looks like a mistake or a trick.
- **Add a "shop engagement rings" shortcut CTA inside the modal** — if you're going to block me anyway, at least give me a button that says "Shop Engagement Rings" alongside the email field. I might click that even if I skip the signup.
- **Surface a loyalty hook in the modal or banner** — something like "Kay Rewards members get an extra 10%" would make me feel seen as a returning shopper and give me a reason to log in rather than bounce to Zales.
