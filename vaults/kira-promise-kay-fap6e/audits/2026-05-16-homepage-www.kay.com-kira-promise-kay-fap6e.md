# Homepage snapshot · www.kay.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Kay is running a Memorial Day push and they're leading hard with an email capture modal that covers literally everything on the page — so my first impression is a popover, not a brand. Behind the modal I can barely make out what looks like a romantic couple moment and the word "ENGAGEMENT" in the nav, which, okay, that's me. But the gate-keeping my experience behind an email field before I even get to see the homepage? That's giving 2015 energy.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day 50% OFF headline** — as someone tracking the holiday sale calendar, this is exactly the kind of event I wait for. The offer is immediately legible at full-screen size.
- **Hard deadline in the fine print** — "Offer valid through 05/25/26 at 11:59 PM ET." gives me a concrete date to act on. That's actually credible.
- **"ENGAGEMENT" category visible in the nav stub** — even through the modal, I can see that category is one tap away once I dismiss this thing.
- **Top banner** — "Memorial Day Sale: Up to 35% Off Everything" is present above the modal fold, so I have two reinforcing sale signals at once.

## What's weak

- **Full-screen email capture modal on first load** — this is the single biggest conversion killer for me. I came to browse engagement rings, not hand over my email address before I've seen a single product. My instinct is "No, Thanks" and bounce.
- **50% OFF with an asterisk** — the fine print lists "Excludes previous purchases. Cannot be combined with any other offer. Promotions, prices, styles and availability may vary by store and online." That's a lot of carve-outs for something presented as a sweeping sale.
- **No financing callout** — I specifically cross-shop Kay vs. Zales vs. Jared on payment plans. There's zero mention of 0% APR, Kay Credit, or any pay-later option in what's visible here.
- **Background hero totally obscured** — I can barely see the lifestyle image behind the modal. If there's a beautiful engagement ring shot back there, it's being wasted.
- **No trust signals** — no star ratings, no "free shipping," no returns mention anywhere above the fold.

## Recommendations

- **Delay the email modal by 30–45 seconds or trigger on exit intent** — let me see at least one product before you ask for my email. I'd be far more willing to sign up after seeing a ring I like.
- **Add a financing teaser to the modal itself** — something like "Plus, 0% APR for 12 months on your engagement ring" would make the email sign-up feel like it's worth something to me specifically.
- **Surface the engagement ring category more aggressively** — even if the modal stays, add a "Shop Engagement Rings in the Sale →" link inside it so I have a path to product without dismissing.
- **Swap the generic dagger-footnote for a cleaner exclusion disclosure** — move the fine print to a tooltip or expandable rather than a wall of small text that makes the 50% OFF feel sketchy.
