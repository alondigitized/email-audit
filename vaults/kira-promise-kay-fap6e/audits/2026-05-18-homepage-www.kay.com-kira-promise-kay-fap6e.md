# Homepage snapshot · www.kay.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- Oh, Kay. I literally had your Memorial Day sale circled on my calendar, and the first thing you serve me is a full-screen email-grab modal. Classic. This feels like a mid-tier mall jeweler trying to play the "exclusive deal" card — the 50% off headline is genuinely exciting for someone ring shopping, but the forced popup and the fine-print math immediately put me on guard. The ENGAGEMENT tab peeking out at the bottom is doing more for my attention than anything in the modal itself.

## What's working

- **"Up To 50% OFF† Memorial Day Specials" headline** — as someone who tracks the Kay sale calendar, that number stops me cold. Right hook for the right moment.
- **ENGAGEMENT tab at the bottom** — even buried under the modal, seeing that single word makes me want to dismiss the popup and get there immediately.
- **Specific offer deadline in fine print** — "Offer valid through 05/25/26 at 11:59 PM ET" is credible and exact. I read the fine print on every deal, and a real date anchors the urgency.
- **"No, Thanks" is clearly visible** — the exit isn't hidden or grayed out. That's the minimum bar for dark-pattern decency, and they cleared it.

## What's weak

- **Forced full-screen modal before I've seen anything** — this is the digital equivalent of a salesperson blocking the store entrance. I came to browse rings, not surrender my email first.
- **35% vs. 50% offer discrepancy** — the sticky banner says "up to 35% Off Everything" and the modal says "Up To 50% OFF." Which number is real? That kind of gap reads as discount inflation to someone who reads diamond-clarity marketing emails for sport.
- **Email CTA as the only above-fold action** — "SIGN UP TO BE THE FIRST TO KNOW ABOUT OFFERS LIKE THIS" is the wrong move. I already know about the offer. Let me shop it.
- **Zero financing signal** — I'm financing-aware, and engagement rings are a four-figure purchase. "As low as $X/mo" or "0% APR for 12 months" should be somewhere on this screen. Total miss.
- **No loyalty/rewards hook** — I cross-shop Kay and Jared on every quote. If Kay has a rewards program, I can't tell from here, and that's a missed retention lever.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop Engagement Rings" secondary CTA** alongside "No, Thanks" — give me a path straight to the ring category without having to dismiss and navigate two steps. The email ask is fine; locking all other action is not.
- **Resolve the 35%/50% messaging conflict** — the banner and modal need to agree. Clarify the distinction ("up to 50% on select styles, up to 35% sitewide") or fix the banner copy. Right now it reads as a classic bait-and-switch setup.
- **Add one line of financing copy to the modal** — "0% APR available on qualifying purchases" is enough. For a bridal shopper this is conversion-critical.
- **Surface a loyalty hook** — even "Kay Rewards members earn extra points this weekend" would make me pause before opening Jared's app.
