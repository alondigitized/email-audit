# Homepage snapshot · www.kay.com · 2026-07-12
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-12**
## Executive summary

- Kay is clearly in summer-sale mode, but the very first thing I see landing on the homepage is a full-screen email-capture modal that blocks everything else. For a brand trying to compete with Zales and Jared on engagement rings, leading with "give us your email before you see anything" is not the move — especially when I can already read the fine print exclusions, and the thing I actually came here to shop (solitaire engagement rings) is literally listed as excluded from the sale.

## What's working

- **"Up to 35% off" headline** — as someone tracking the holiday sale calendar, a storewide summer event is the kind of thing I'd want to hear about, and the percentage is right there, no digging required
- **Clean modal design** — white card, KAY logo up top, logical reading order; it doesn't feel janky or low-rent the way some jewelry pop-ups do
- **"No, Thanks" dismiss option** — at least it's clearly visible and not hidden in a size-2 font somewhere off-screen
- **Couple in background** — even mostly obscured, I can tell the imagery is romantic/lifestyle, which is the right emotional register for where I am in life right now

## What's weak

- **Forced email gate before I've seen anything** — I haven't browsed one product, one category, or one price point, and the brand is already demanding my inbox. This is the fastest way to make me hit "No, Thanks" and bounce to Zales
- **"Now + Forever Solitaire Rings" excluded from the sale** — it's right there in the asterisk fine print, which means the headline offer is a near-complete mismatch for an engagement ring shopper. The 35% off feels like bait-and-switch the second I read the exclusions list
- **No engagement-ring framing anywhere** — the word "engagement" doesn't appear in the modal at all. If I'm deciding between Kay, Zales, and Jared, nothing here gives me a reason to stay
- **The fine-print exclusion list is enormous** — Le Vian® Vault, KAY Signature™, clearance, custom design, specialty-priced items, gift cards, repairs — the list is so long it undermines the whole offer. What *is* actually 35% off?
- **Zero financing mention** — I shop on financing. A 35% off event with no mention of "or apply for Kay financing" is a missed layup

## Recommendations

- **Swap the email popup for a contextual interstitial** — show me one engagement-ring lifestyle image and the offer first; let me fall in love with something before asking for my email. Even a 5-second delay changes the dynamic entirely
- **Add an engagement-ring carve-out to the headline** — "Engagement rings excluded" needs to either be surfaced immediately OR engagement rings need their own parallel offer; hiding the exclusion in fine print feels manipulative to someone who actually reads the asterisk
- **Surface a financing hook in the modal** — "Shop now, pay over time with Kay financing" as a single line below the CTA costs nothing and directly addresses the #1 barrier for my purchase
- **Personalize the background image** — make the couple in the background clearly engageable/proposal-adjacent; a ring on a hand, a down-on-one-knee moment, anything that signals "this is your store for this moment"
