# Homepage snapshot · www.kay.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Kay is a brand I already have on my radar — I've gotten their emails before and cross-shopped them against Zales and Jared. But the very first thing I see when I open the app is a full-screen modal demanding my email before I can see a single ring, and that honestly makes me want to hit "No, Thanks" and keep scrolling Instagram instead. The one thing they got right: there's a Father's Day banner peeking out at the top, so at least they know what month it is.

## What's working

- **Father's Day banner** — I can see "Father's Day Gift Guide | Explore Now" at the top of the screen. That's timely, and I'd absolutely be thinking about gifts for my partner's dad right now alongside shopping for a ring.
- **Brand recognition** — KAY JEWELERS logo is front and center in the modal header. I know this brand. That's a baseline trust signal even if the execution is weak.
- **Romantic background imagery** — The couple visible behind the modal looks young and in love. That's the exact energy I'm in right now. If this were the actual hero instead of wallpaper behind a popup, it would land hard.

## What's weak

- **Full-screen blocking modal with no offer** — This is the biggest miss. You're interrupting my shopping journey before I've even seen one ring, and you're not even giving me a reason to sign up. No "get 10% off your first order," no "unlock member pricing" — just "be the first to know." Know what? I already follow Kay on Instagram. I'm not giving you my email for nothing.
- **Zero engagement ring context above the fold** — I came here to shop for an engagement ring. The modal doesn't mention rings, diamonds, bridal, or anything in my category.
- **No financing teaser** — I always check financing options before I commit to a jeweler. Nothing about monthly payments or "as low as $X/mo" is visible, and that's a real miss for someone like me who is actively comparing financing across Zales, Jared, and Kay.
- **The background couple is wasted** — That romantic lifestyle image is exactly what should be the hero, but it's been demoted to blurry backdrop behind an email form. Prioritization fail.

## Recommendations

- **Add a tangible offer to the email modal** — "Sign up and get 15% off your first order" or "unlock exclusive member pricing" would flip this from a toll gate to a value exchange. I've seen Zales do this and I actually signed up.
- **Swap the modal trigger for an inline or slide-in email capture** — Let me see the homepage first. If the content is good, I'll opt in naturally. Blocking the full viewport before I've seen a single product is the fastest way to lose me.
- **Surface financing in the hero or a sticky banner** — "0% APR for 12 months" above the fold would give me an immediate reason to choose Kay over Jared. That's a real conversion driver for my purchase stage.
- **Bring the romantic couple photo to the foreground as an actual hero** — It's already a great image. Make it work by putting "Find the Ring She'll Never Take Off" over it and a "Shop Engagement Rings" CTA underneath.
