# Homepage snapshot · www.kay.com · 2026-07-19
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-19**
## Executive summary

- Kay is coming in hot with "The Storewide Event" — a 35% off sale framed around fine jewelry with a ticking countdown. As someone who is actively cross-shopping engagement rings against Zales and Jared, the hero image (a woman's hands stacked with rings, a diamond pendant necklace, bracelets, and a gold watch) speaks directly to me. The brand reads upscale-accessible, like it wants my business — but above the fold it's short on the details I actually need before I trust a purchase this size.

## What's working

- **The hero image** — rings on a woman's hands, layered diamond pendant, gold bracelet and watch. This is exactly the aesthetic I'm building for my partner's ring search. I immediately see myself in this image.
- **The countdown timer ("17 HRS : 39 MINS")** — I calendar Kay's holiday events the way some people track flights. A real deadline makes me stop scrolling Instagram and open the app.
- **"The Storewide Event" copy** — clean, no-fluff, gets to the point. "35% Off* Select Styles" lands because it's a specific number, not "Big Savings Await."
- **The "ENGAGEMENT" category shortcut at the bottom** — one tap and I'm in the right place. Beats digging through a hamburger menu.
- **Search bar prominently placed** — I'm a comparison shopper; I'll search "cushion cut engagement ring" before I browse, and the bar is right there.

## What's weak

- **No loyalty hook above the fold** — Kay has a rewards program. I have a Kay card. I see zero acknowledgment of that. Zales shows my points balance. That matters when I'm deciding where to buy a $3,000 ring.
- **"Shop Now" in the promo strip is tiny and generic** — it blends into the banner. There's no big, bold hero CTA button to drive me into the sale.
- **No free-shipping callout** — for a ring at this price point, I need to know shipping is free and fast. It's not here.
- **No financing callout above the fold** — Kay offers 0% APR promotional financing, which is a major reason I'd choose Kay over a local jeweler. I can't see it. Jared often leads with this for big-ticket items.
- **Asterisk on "35% Off*"** — I know it's on select styles, but without a quick sense of whether engagement rings are included, I'm immediately wondering if this sale even applies to what I'm shopping for.

## Recommendations

- **Add a financing teaser to the hero subtext** — swap "Up to 35% Off* Select Styles" for "Up to 35% Off + 0% APR Financing Available" or run a second line below. That single addition would separate Kay from Zales for a buyer like me.
- **Add a loyalty/rewards callout in the promo strip** — even "Rewards members earn 10% back" next to the countdown would make me feel like this brand knows I'm a returning customer, not a first-time visitor.
- **Make the hero CTA a real button** — replace the "Shop Now" text link with a white pill button labeled "Shop Engagement Rings" placed over the hero image. Removes ambiguity about where the sale applies.
- **Surface a free-shipping threshold** — pin "Free Shipping on Orders $50+" or whatever the threshold is to the promo strip, or place it below the hero. It's table stakes for jewelry e-commerce and builds trust before checkout.
