# Homepage snapshot · www.kay.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so the first thing I see when I land on Kay is not the homepage — it's an email-capture modal slamming a "Up to 50% OFF Memorial Day Specials" pitch in my face before I can see a single ring. I get it, they want my email, but the whole screen is blocked. Kay reads like a mid-tier mall jeweler that knows how to run a holiday sale calendar but doesn't feel particularly *me* — it's broad, not curated.

## What's working

- **The Memorial Day deal language** — "Up to 50% OFF" in big bold type is the right message for someone like me who tracks holiday jewelry sales specifically. I'd clock this deal against what Zales or Jared is running right now.
- **"Be the first to know" framing** — this is smart. I read every promotional email from jewelers. Positioning the signup as early access, not spam, is the right angle.
- **The offer expiration date** — "05/25/26 at 11:59 PM ET" is in the fine print but it IS there, which gives the deal a real deadline rather than a vague "limited time."
- **ENGAGEMENT tab visible at the bottom** — even through the modal, I can see that engagement is a top-nav category, which tells me I'm in the right place.

## What's weak

- **Conflicting discount claims** — the sticky banner reads "Up to 35% Off Everything" while the modal headline screams "Up to 50% OFF." That's a 15-point gap and it makes me immediately suspicious before I've seen a single ring. Which is it?
- **The modal blocks everything** — I can't evaluate the homepage because there isn't one visible. No hero, no product imagery, no category grid, nothing.
- **No ring imagery** — the couple behind the modal is so faint I can barely see it. I came here shopping for an engagement ring. Show me a ring.
- **Heavy exclusions copy** — "Excludes previous purchases. Cannot be combined with any other offer. Promotions, prices, styles and availability may vary by store and online." That's four carve-outs before I've scrolled an inch. Not a confidence builder.
- **No loyalty hook** — I cross-shop Zales and Jared. If Kay has a rewards program, this is the moment to flash it. Nothing.
- **Blue focus ring on the modal** — minor visual issue but it gives the modal a browser-default, unpolished look on mobile.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the percentage conflict immediately** — pick one number (50% or 35%) and use it everywhere above the fold. The discrepancy reads as bait-and-switch even if it isn't.
- **Swap the email modal for an offer banner + inline hero** — put "Sign up for early Memorial Day access" as a slim dismissable banner, not a full-screen takeover. Show me the actual homepage with ring imagery behind it.
- **Surface a specific engagement ring in the hero** — show a halo cut or three-stone setting with a price and a "shop Memorial Day sale" CTA. I need to see a product to believe the deal applies to what I want.
- **Add a rewards/financing callout** — even one line like "Kay Credit Card holders save an extra 10%" would be conversion gold for a financing-aware shopper like me who is budgeting for a ring.
