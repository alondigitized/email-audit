# Earn up to 4,000 AAdvantage® bonus miles on holiday shopping
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-02**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-offer loyalty send. Earn up to 4,000 bonus AAdvantage® miles on eShopping purchases through Nov. 20. The Shop now button is clear, and the layout is uncluttered. The headline, "Turn gift-giving into your next getaway," is the best creative idea in the email.
- The offer is weaker than it looks. The headline and the holiday photo carry the top of the email, and the actual number, "up to 4,000," sits in a paragraph below the image in plain body type. The tier math (500 miles at $200, 1,500 at $600, 4,000 at $1,600) is only in the fine print. A member who sees "4,000 miles" will assume it's easy to earn, and it takes $1,600 of spend. That is the honesty problem here.
- The hero also doesn't show the rest of the brand's recent pattern. Over the last two weeks the brand has sent this same member a Black Friday-adjacent "2X miles" email on 10/19, an AT&T partner offer on 10/17, and an account summary on 10/23. This one is the clearest and the most concrete of them. It still reads as another "earn miles by spending" ask.

## What's working

- The headline "Turn gift-giving into your next getaway" ties holiday shopping to travel, which is the brand's actual value.
- There is one offer and one button, with no competing modules, so the path to action is obvious.
- The deadline is explicit (Nov. 20, 11:59 p.m. ET), and it fits the season.
- The member ID ("AAdvantage® Member 6V8****") at the top signals that this is a loyalty send.
- The "1,200 stores" line gives a credible reason to click.

## What's weak

- The "up to 4,000 miles" number is buried in body copy, not shown in the hero or on the button.
- The spend tiers that decide how many miles you actually get are only in the fine print at the bottom.
- "Shop now" is generic and gives no hint of the miles reward.
- The lifestyle image shows a woman with gift bags but no brand names, so nothing shows what you could buy.
- A small blue "?" icon sits at the bottom right of the footer, which looks like a broken image.
- The "You are why we fly" banner takes up a lot of space and does nothing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the offer into the hero. Put "Earn up to 4,000 bonus miles" in large type over or under the image, with the Nov. 20 date next to it.
- 2. Show the tiers in a three-step visual in the body: spend $200 for 500 miles, $600 for 1,500, $1,600 for 4,000. This makes the offer honest and shows members a realistic target.
- 3. Change the button to "Earn bonus miles" or "Start earning miles".
- 4. Replace the generic "You are why we fly" banner with three or four named retailers from the eShopping mall.
- 5. Fix or remove the broken icon in the footer.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $200, $600 or $1,600 at 1,200+ stores. Bonus ends Nov. 20.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Bonus miles on top of the miles you already earn. Shop through eShopping.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML has several code-level defects: malformed MSO conditional comments, an unclosed `@media` block, and viewport settings that block zoom. Five of the 12 probed tracked links failed automated QA, but four of those are 403s that look like bot-blocking by the click tracker, not confirmed breakage. The source was truncated and the headers were not captured, so the footer and compliance checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **403 on four tracked links** (`l.loyalty.ms.aa.com/rts/go2.aspx`). They are "Shop now" (`h=6672976`), "American Airlines" (`h=6672970`), "Turn gift-giving into your next getaway" (`h=6672975`) and "You are why we fly" (`h=6672977`).
  - All four return 403 to the probe. This pattern usually means the redirector blocks non-browser user agents.
  - I can't confirm real recipients are affected. A manual browser check is needed before treating these as broken.
- **Relative redirect target** on the "6V8****" link (`h=6672971`). The probe error was `unknown url type: '/aadvantage-program/profile/account-summary?c=EML|SOLO|20261102|AADV|MKT|Partner||eShopping'`.
  - The redirector appears to return a scheme-less `Location`. RFC 7231 permits relative references, so many clients will resolve it, but the probe could not.
  - The unencoded `|` characters in that query string are also not valid per RFC 3986. The tracked URL encodes them as `%7c`, so the redirector decodes them on the way out.
- **Inconsistent tracking coverage.** The `x=` and `hp2=` parameters appear on the logo link (`h=6672970`), the "You are why we fly" link (`h=6672977`) and the account link (`h=6672971`). They are absent from "Shop now" (`h=6672976`) and "Turn gift-giving…" (`h=6672975`), which carry only `tp` and `pi`.
- **Probe coverage:** only 5 of 12 material links were probed, and 7 were skipped. The unsubscribe link is stated to always be included, but its result isn't listed, so I can't confirm it.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed MSO conditionals.** `<!--[if mso]>` has no matching `<![endif]-->`, and `<!--[if gte mso 9]>` is opened inside it. There are three openers and two `<![endif]-->` closers. The `gte mso 9` font block is also duplicated verbatim.
- **Unclosed `@media (prefers-color-scheme:dark)`.** The block ends after the `[data-ogsc] .dark-bs{…}` rule with no closing brace before `</style>`. Browsers auto-close it, but strict parsers may drop the final rules.
- **Blanket dark-mode override.** `span, a, td { color: #ffffff !important; }` forces every text colour to white in dark mode, including elements on light or image backgrounds. `.white-text` is remapped to `#181E25`, which looks inverted.
- **Zoom blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4). `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }` overrides the `100%` set on `body` and disables iOS text scaling.
- **Global `line-height:100%`** on `*` can clip wrapped text in some clients.
- **XHTML 1.0 doctype with unclosed void tags:** `<meta name="x-apple-disable-message-reformatting">` and the `<link rel="preconnect">` tags are not self-closed.
- **External font dependency.** The `@import` and `<link>` for `fonts.googleapis.com` are stripped by Gmail and others. The MSO font stack does fall back to Arial/Helvetica.
- **Non-HTTPS image:** `http://i.loyalty.ms.aa.com/wpm/1021/ContentUploads/american_airlines/CTAs/oneworld_logo_special_online_80x80.gif` ("oneworld logo"). It may be blocked or flagged as mixed content.
- **Duplicated preheader.** There are two identical hidden `<span>` preheaders in the source, so the preview text may repeat.
- **Ambiguous link text.** The link text "6V8****" gives screen readers no destination context.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The link text "6V8****" is a rendered, masked member number, not a raw token. No unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[[…]]` tokens appear in the visible source. The truncated portion was not reviewed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Verify against the raw original message. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unverifiable from this capture. The From domain `loyalty.ms.aa.com` and the tracking domain `l.loyalty.ms.aa.com` are aligned subdomains.
- The footer, physical address and unsubscribe markup were beyond the truncation point, so I can't assess them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Proprietary tracking, no standard `utm_*` parameters.** Campaign data is carried in `x=c|EML|…` and `c=EML|…`.
- **Inconsistent field order and casing:**
  - `c|EML|SOLO|20261102|AADV|MKT|Partner||eShopping` on the logo and account links.
  - `c|EML|eshopping|20261102|AADV|MKT|SOLO||eShopping` on "You are why we fly". Here `SOLO` and `eshopping` are swapped, and `eshopping` is lowercase.
  - This will fragment analytics grouping.
- **Destinations can't be resolved.** Four of the five tracked links returned 403 to the probe, and the fifth returned a relative redirect. Landing-page alignment with the "up to 4,000 bonus miles" offer is therefore unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the four 403 links in a real browser or with a browser UA. If they pass, allow-list the QA probe so false positives stop skewing the pass rate (currently 27%).
2. Make the redirector emit an absolute `Location` with percent-encoded `|` (`%7C`) in the account-summary redirect.
3. Standardize the `x=`/`c=` campaign string (field order and case), and apply `x=`/`hp2=` consistently to all CTAs. Consider adding standard `utm_*` parameters.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` on the raw message, and check SPF/DKIM/DMARC from the original headers.
5. Fix the MSO conditional nesting, remove the duplicate `gte mso 9` block, and close the dark-mode `@media` block.
6. Scope the dark-mode colour override to specific classes, not `span, a, td`.
7. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and the `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust:none }` rule.
8. Serve the oneworld logo over HTTPS and give the account link descriptive text or an `aria-label`.
9. Remove the duplicate preheader span.
