# A complimentary inflight treat is waiting for you
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-13**
## Executive summary

- This is the second "free treat" email in about two weeks for the same new member. The Sept 28 "Welcome aboard. Here's a treat to celebrate" email already offered a free inflight drink or snack. This one is nearly identical, with a centennial skin and a new deadline of October 23. Curt is a newcomer with zero miles, so a small, concrete perk is the right thing to send. The email is clean, honest and has one CTA.
- The execution undersells the offer. The headline is "Cheers to 100 years," so the brand's anniversary gets top billing and the freebie comes second. The actual offer sits in a paragraph below a lifestyle photo, and the blue "Register now" button follows that. The offer gets no headline, no badge and no visual weight. The subject line is stronger than the body, because it names the benefit that the body buries.
- Verdict: a solid, trustworthy, low-effort send that is a near-repeat of September 28. It will convert the people who open it, but it won't stand out.

## What's working

- One offer and one CTA: a free inflight drink or snack, and a single "Register now" button.
- The deadline is bolded in the body copy: "Register by October 23, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. CT."
- It is personalized with "Hi Curt" and a visible member ID in the header.
- The blue button is high-contrast and centered, so it is easy to find.
- The centennial branding is on-season and consistent with the brand's current campaign.
- There are no render bugs: images load and the layout is clean.

## What's weak

- The headline "Cheers to 100 years" is brand-centric. The free drink isn't named until the body paragraph.
- Key terms are in the footer: redeem by Dec 31, 2026, on an eligible flight. The asterisk and "Terms apply" make a small perk feel lawyered.
- Two sentences about accepting terms by registering, including "1-click registration," read as legal friction right before the CTA.
- The Sept 28 email offered the same "treat" with no clear differentiation, so the cadence feels repetitive.
- The lower half is a generic loyalty pitch ("Loyalty Point Rewards," "Learn more") plus a stock 100-year plane banner. It dilutes the single offer and adds a second link competing with the CTA.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the offer the headline: "Your free inflight drink or snack is ready," with a "Register by Oct 23" badge in the hero. Give the offer the visual weight the anniversary currently has.
- 2. Say what this is, and how it differs from the Sept 28 welcome treat. If it is the same perk, a "reminder" framing would be more honest and better for cadence.
- 3. Cut the two-sentence terms block above the button to one line, and put the redemption window (valid through Dec 31) next to the CTA.
- 4. Make the CTA more specific: "Claim my free drink" instead of "Register now."
- 5. Drop the lower loyalty paragraph or turn it into a single line. Let the banner be a footer, not a second pitch.

- **Subject Alt B:** `A free inflight drink or snack, on us`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Register by Oct 23 to claim your complimentary drink or snack. Redeem by Dec 31.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `A centennial thank-you for new AAdvantage members. It takes one tap to register.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has a confirmed empty-name greeting ("Hi ,"), a tracked logo link that returned 403, and a tracked account link that the prober couldn't resolve. The source also has several rendering and accessibility defects. Compliance and authentication headers couldn't be checked, because the relay didn't capture them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **403 on the logo link.** The "American Airlines" link failed with 403:
  `https://l.loyalty.ms.aa.com/rts/go2.aspx?h=6636866&tp=i-1NHD-…&x=c|EML|SOLO|20261013|ADV|MKT|LYLTY|ERINC|US_LYLTY_NEW_MEMBER_PROMO&hp2=58ce2698…`
  A 403 on a tracked redirector is often bot-blocking of the probe rather than a failure for real recipients. This is not confirmed as broken for users, but it is unverified.
- **Unresolved redirect target.** The "6V8****" link (`h=6636867`) redirects to `/aadvantage-program/profile/account-summary?c=EML|SOLO|20261013|ADV|MKT|LYLTY|ERINC|US_LYLTY_NEW_MEMBER_PROMO`. The QA tool failed with "unknown url type" because the target is a relative path. Relative `Location` headers are valid HTTP, so this is probably a probe limitation. The destination itself was never verified.
- **Low coverage.** Only 3 of the 12 material links were probed (9 skipped). The main CTA's status is unknown unless it is one of the 3.
- **Insecure image.** The "oneworld logo" image loads over HTTP: `http://i.loyalty.ms.aa.com/wpm/1021/ContentUploads/american_airlines/CTAs/oneworld_logo_special_online_80x80.gif`. It may be blocked or flagged as mixed content. The tracked links and the other hosts are HTTPS.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Text-size adjust disabled.** `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; -ms-text-size-adjust: none; line-height:100% }` overrides the `100%` set on `body`. This blocks user text scaling on iOS and forces tight line-height everywhere.
- **Broken mobile background.** `#swapbg` in the `max-width:480px` media query uses `url("20230508_HTML-32134_M_bg.png")`, a relative path. It won't resolve in an email client, so the mobile background fails to load. The filename also carries a 2023 date, so the template looks stale.
- **Dark-mode contrast risk.** `span, a, td { color:#ffffff !important; }` forces all text white in dark mode. Only `.lightblue-bg` and `.cta-bg` get background overrides. Any cell with a light background that isn't covered would become white-on-light. The source is truncated, so I couldn't confirm which cells are affected.
- **Unclosed media query.** The `@media (prefers-color-scheme:dark) {` block is never closed before `</style>`. Parsers usually recover, but strict ones (some webmail sanitizers) may drop the rules.
- **Redundant font loading.** Roboto is loaded by both `<link rel="stylesheet">` and `@import`. The two identical `<!--[if gte mso 9]>` blocks are also duplicates. Both are harmless but wasteful.
- **Preheader is fine.** The hidden span has `display:none`, `mso-hide:all` and padding entities, and the preheader text is correct. `lang="en"` is set and the oneworld logo has alt text.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Empty greeting (confirmed).** The QA check found "Hi ," with an empty name field. The template has no fallback, such as "Hi there," or an omitted salutation.
- **Inconsistent data.** The masked member number ("6V8****") rendered correctly while the first name didn't. The name field is probably empty in the profile or the merge is misconfigured.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058).** Both headers were not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so I can't confirm they are missing from the original send. Bulk-sender requirements from Gmail and Yahoo expect both headers.
- **Authentication-Results.** The header wasn't found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown. The From domain (`loyalty.ms.aa.com`) matches the tracking and image subdomains, which is a good sign for alignment, but it isn't proof.
- **Footer.** The in-body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are not visible in the truncated source. They are neither confirmed nor flagged. The QA summary says an unsubscribe link was probed, but no result is shown.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Tracking scheme.** The links use a proprietary `x=` / `c=` code (`EML|SOLO|20261013|ADV|MKT|LYLTY|ERINC|US_LYLTY_NEW_MEMBER_PROMO`) and no `utm_*` parameters. The code does carry through to the destination as `?c=…`, so campaign attribution is intact. It will only show up in analytics that are configured to read `c=`.
- **Destination mismatch.** The preheader promises "Register by Oct. 23 to enjoy a snack or drink on us". One probed link lands on the authenticated `/aadvantage-program/profile/account-summary` page, not a registration page. The registration CTA wasn't among the probed links, so I can't confirm it lands on a matching page.
- **Date code.** The `20261013` in the campaign code matches the send date.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a merge fallback for the first name, for example `{{FirstName|there}}`. Suppress the comma when the value is empty.
2. Re-probe the logo link and the registration CTA with a browser-like User-Agent, or verify them manually, to separate a 403 from bot-blocking. Raise the probe cap so the primary CTA is covered.
3. Serve the oneworld logo over HTTPS (`https://i.loyalty.ms.aa.com/…`).
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag. Drop the `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust:none }` rule and the blanket `line-height:100%`.
5. Replace the relative `#swapbg` background URL with an absolute HTTPS URL, or remove it if the template is no longer used.
6. Close the dark-mode `@media` block. Scope the `color:#ffffff !important` rule to elements with dark-mode backgrounds, then test in Outlook and Apple Mail dark mode.
7. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` on the raw original message, since the relay's copy is not authoritative. Do the same for the SPF, DKIM and DMARC results.
8. Consider adding `utm_*` parameters alongside the `c=` code for cross-tool analytics, and confirm that the registration CTA lands on a registration flow.
