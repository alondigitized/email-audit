# Homepage snapshot · www.aa.com · 2026-10-26
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the standard AA homepage. The booking widget does the real work, and the hero is a Citi AAdvantage card ad: "Earn 80,000 bonus miles" over a Santa Monica pier sunset. I already hold the Citi Executive card, so the biggest thing on the page isn't aimed at me. A "Have an AAdvantage® account? Log in here." popover is also sitting over the top-right corner.

## What's working

- **Booking widget directly under the hero.** From / To / Depart / Return / Search is above the fold with "Round trip" preselected. BUR is prefilled, so I'm one airport away from searching.
- **"Redeem miles" checkbox right in the form.** I only book mileage tickets when business shows web saver, so I'm hunting award space. This is the toggle I use most, and it's one tap away.
- **"Advanced / Multi-city search »" link.** I build multi-leg itineraries constantly, and this is where I'd go.
- **Log in / Join in the header.** Both are visible at the top, and Log in is a solid blue button.
- **oneworld mark top left.** It signals the alliance network, which matters to me for partner awards.

## What's weak

- **The hero ad isn't for me.** "Earn 80,000 bonus miles" is a Citi card acquisition pitch. I'm Executive Platinum and already carry the Executive card. Nothing here tells me about award availability, upgrades or status.
- **The pier photo says nothing about flying.** It's a pretty beach scene with no aircraft, cabin or lounge, so it doesn't register as an airline.
- **The blue popover over the top-right.** "Have an AAdvantage® account? Log in here." tells me to log in next to a Log in button that's already visible. It's a nag, and it sits on top of the hero.
- **"OFFER ENDING SOON" with no date.** That's vague urgency. Ending when?
- **Thin blue headline on an orange sky.** The contrast is soft, and the subline gets lost in the pier detail.
- **The card art shows "LINDA WALKER".** It's a placeholder name, so it reads as a mockup, and it sits right beside the carousel arrow.
- **"Terms apply" in small type over the dark pier.** It's the disclosure for an 80,000-mile offer and it's nearly invisible.
- **Weak category access.** Plan travel, Travel information and AAdvantage® are collapsed menus. There are no quick tiles for Award travel, Upgrades or Status.

## Recommendations

- **Personalize the hero for logged-in or cookied members.** Swap the card ad for status-aware copy such as "Executive Platinum: search award space" with a button. Keep the card offer for non-members.
- **Kill the login popover, or make it conditional.** The Log in button is already blue and in the header. If you keep any nudge, tie it to a recognized returning visitor and don't stack it on the hero.
- **Put a date on the urgency and move the disclosure up.** Change "OFFER ENDING SOON" to "Ends Nov 30" and put the key terms (annual fee, spend requirement) as visible text under the headline.
- **Add a quick-link row under the form.** Use "Award travel," "Upgrades," "Status" and "Saved trips" so I can reach my tasks in one tap on mobile.
