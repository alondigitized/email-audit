# Homepage snapshot · www.aa.com · 2026-08-10
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- Same AA.com I've had bookmarked for fifteen years — booking widget front and center, no wasted motion. Right now they're pushing a Citi/AAdvantage card acquisition offer (80,000 bonus miles) instead of anything about actual flying, which tells me marketing owns the homepage this week, not ops. Functional, a little cluttered with overlapping pop-ups, exactly what I expect from an airline site that still runs on duct tape and tribal knowledge.

## What's working

- **The booking widget is right where it should be** — Flight tab pre-selected, "Redeem miles" checkbox sitting right there for guys like me who book on points.
- **"Have an AAdvantage® account? Log in here"** — direct nod to existing elite members instead of funneling everyone through a generic sign-up path.
- **"Advanced / Multi-city search"** link visible without digging — I still book multi-city international runs the old-fashioned way.
- **Bag fee and fee-change links** sitting right under the search box — small thing, but it's the kind of practical info a frequent flyer actually needs.

## What's weak

- **The hero is a credit-card acquisition pitch, not a flying pitch.** I already carry the Citi AAdvantage Executive card for lounge access — "find the card that's right for you" is a new-customer message, not a retention one.
- **Two overlapping pop-ups** — the "Log in here" bubble sits half on top of the hero image, and a cookie-consent modal blankets "Today's offers" and the travel-alerts bar at the bottom. Sloppy for a site this mature.
- **"Today's offers" imagery is a shopping couple and a carful of twenty-somethings** — doesn't remotely reflect who's actually clicking a Search button on an airline homepage at 6am from a gate.
- **No fare, no route, no status callout** — for a road warrior, I want to see something like upgrade inventory or a saver-fare nudge, not a generic mileage bonus.

## Recommendations

- Swap the hero for something that speaks to existing elites — e.g., "Check your upgrade list" or a saver-fare business-class callout — and push the credit card offer to a secondary banner.
- Kill the double pop-up stack; let the cookie banner render below the fold or as a slim footer bar instead of blocking "Today's offers."
- Replace the lifestyle stock photos in "Today's offers" with something route- or cabin-specific (a business-class cabin shot, a lounge shot) that matches who's actually booking here.
- Put a real date on "LIMITED-TIME OFFER" — "Ends 8/31" beats a vague badge for anyone who's been burned by AA promo fine print before.
