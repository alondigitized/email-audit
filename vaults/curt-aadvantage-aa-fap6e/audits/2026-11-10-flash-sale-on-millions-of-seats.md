# Flash sale on millions of seats
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a miles-redemption flash sale, and the "Flash sale on millions of seats" subject oversells it. The hero is a red countdown banner over a New York skyline, with "More seats for less" above it. That is a good urgency device, and it sits on a clear single idea: 72 hours, award seats from 8,000 miles. The six destination tiles each have their own button, and the layout is easy to scan.
- The persona problem is the account card. It shows 0 award miles and 0 Loyalty Points. For this recipient, every price in the email is unreachable. Nothing explains how to earn miles quickly, so the email promotes an offer the recipient can't use. Compare the 2026-10-23 account summary, which at least turned that same 0 balance into a path to 40,000 miles. Here the balance is stated and then ignored.
- The execution has smaller problems too. The timer reads 02:04:34:29, which is about two days, but the copy promises 72 hours. Nearly every offer carries an asterisk, and the fine print runs very long. The tiles are generic travel imagery. Judgment: polished and credible as a brand send, but it targets people with miles, and this recipient has none.

## What's working

- The countdown banner in red is the first thing the eye lands on and gives the send real urgency.
- Each tile names a region plus a concrete mileage floor, such as "U.S. from 8,000* miles" and "Europe from 22,500* miles."
- Short, consistent blue buttons on each tile keep the layout scannable.
- The dark theme and photography make the hero and tiles stand out.
- The footer includes an app download prompt and clear account links.

## What's weak

- The account card shows 0 award miles and 0 Loyalty Points, so the redemption offer doesn't apply to this recipient.
- The countdown (about 2 days) doesn't clearly match the "next 72 hours" copy, which weakens the urgency.
- The fine print is dense and heavily asterisked, and many of the claims are conditional.
- Button copy is inconsistent ("Book now," "Book today," "Find flights," "Search flights," "Find fares"), and none of it says "with miles."
- The images are generic scenic shots, with nothing tied to the recipient.
- The subject line says "seats," which suggests cash fares, but the body is about miles.

## Recommendations

- 1. Branch by miles balance. For zero-balance members, lead with "Earn miles fast" (card, shopping, or buy-miles offers) and show a cash fare sale or an earn path instead.
- 2. Say "with miles" in the subject and hero so the audience qualifies themselves.
- 3. Make the timer and the copy agree (72 hours versus the visible countdown).
- 4. Use one button label across tiles, such as "See award seats," and put the lowest-mileage deal first.
- 5. Move the key terms up into the body and shorten the legal block.
- **Subject Alt A:** `72 hours: flights from 8,000 miles`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your miles go further for 3 days`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `U.S. awards from 8,000 miles, plus Europe and Asia. Ends Nov. 11.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Short on miles? See how to earn enough for your next trip.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is structurally sound: it has `lang`, a `<title>`, a hidden preheader, and tracked links on a first-party domain (`l.loyalty.ms.aa.com`). Two probed links failed with a control-character error, there is a dead relative background URL in the mobile CSS, and one image is served over `http://`. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the relay and can't be attributed to the sender.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Two probe failures** (QA `[FAIL]`, "URL can't contain control characters"):
  - "Download our mobile app": `https://l.loyalty.ms.aa.com/rts/go2.aspx?h=6685226&tp=i-1NHD-GT-1QJI-gD7SOD-1w-8eyqQ-1c-1LSF1-…`
  - "American Airlines" (logo/header): `https://l.loyalty.ms.aa.com/rts/go2.aspx?h=6685230&tp=…`
- **Unconfirmed cause:** the extracted URLs contain no visible control characters. The error text is the Python `http.client` path-validation message, and `%7c` (pipe) is already percent-encoded. Two causes are possible:
  - Whitespace or a line break inside the raw `href` in the source, which the truncated HTML doesn't show.
  - A defect in the QA fetcher.
- **Severity:** only 2 of 12 probed links failed, and both use the same `go2.aspx` pattern as the links that passed. Treat this as unverified until the raw `href` bytes are checked.
- **Failed probes mean no landing check:** the destination and final HTTP status for the mobile-app and logo links are unknown.
- **Dead background URL:** the `@media (max-width:480px)` rule `#swapbg { background-image: url("../20230508_HTML-32134_M_bg.png") }` uses a relative path. Email has no base path, so it will 404 or never load on mobile. It looks like a leftover from the template build.
- **Skipped links:** 22 links were not probed (footer, social and similar). Their status is unknown.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom in clients that honor it. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Dark-mode override:** `@media (prefers-color-scheme:dark) { span, a, td { color:#ffffff !important; } }` is a blanket rule. Any cell whose background isn't remapped (only `.lightblue-bg` and `.cta-bg` are) will render white text on a light background, so contrast can fail.
- **Mixed content:** `<img alt="oneworld logo" src="http://i.loyalty.ms.aa.com/wpm/1021/ContentUploads/american_airlines/CTAs/oneworld_logo_special_online_80x80.gif">` is non-HTTPS. It may be blocked or proxied with a warning. The same host likely serves HTTPS.
- **Redundant font loading:** Roboto is requested three times (`<link rel="preconnect">`, `<link href=…css2>` and `@import` in `<style>`). `@import` is unsupported in Gmail and several other clients. The `[if gte mso 9]` block is also duplicated verbatim.
- **Global `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust:none; line-height:100% }`:** this disables user text-size adjustment on iOS. It also resets line-height everywhere, which the `.f24` override has to counter.
- **Good practice:** `lang="en"` and `xml:lang`, a `<title>`, and the preheader hidden with `display:none` plus `mso-hide:all`.
- **Not verifiable:** the HTML is truncated, so alt text coverage and table `role="presentation"` could not be checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The footer and body are truncated, so this is limited to what was visible.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found:** the QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so absence isn't evidence the sender omitted them. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both. Verify against the raw message from a non-relay inbox.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because of the relay. Nothing here shows a failure.
- **Body unsubscribe link and postal address:** both sit in the truncated footer and could not be verified. The QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but its result isn't listed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters.** Attribution relies on the proprietary `x=` parameter, which carries a pipe-delimited campaign string: `EML|SOLO|20261109|MKT|USPFMOC|AA Brand-Airline|ERINC|US_ROUTE_TRAN_MAXMILES`. This is consistent, but site analytics need to parse it.
- **Inconsistent tagging:** the logo link has `x=c%7cEML%7c…` while the app link has `x=EML%7c…`. The extra `c|` prefix could split reporting by link.
- **Landing page alignment is unverified:** the two failed probes mean the final URL, status and content match for the flash-sale CTAs couldn't be confirmed.
- **Redirect hop:** all links go through `l.loyalty.ms.aa.com/rts/go2.aspx`, so there is one extra redirect. The domain is aligned with the sender (`loyalty.ms.aa.com`).

## 7. Recommendations
1. Inspect the raw `href` bytes for `h=6685226` and `h=6685230` for whitespace, newlines or tabs. If they are clean, re-probe with a client that percent-encodes the path.
2. Remove or fix the relative `#swapbg` background URL by using an absolute HTTPS asset URL.
3. Change `http://i.loyalty.ms.aa.com/…oneworld_logo…gif` to `https://`.
4. Drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
5. Scope the dark-mode `span, a, td { color:#fff !important }` rule to elements with a remapped background.
6. Remove the `@import` and the duplicate MSO style block, keeping a single font load method.
7. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results against the original message, not the relay copy.
8. Normalize the `x=` tagging so every link uses the same prefix.
