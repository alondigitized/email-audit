# Curt, view your October 2026 account summary
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-23**
## Executive summary

- This is a transactional-style account summary with a promotional email attached. The summary card is personalized to Curt and honest about where they stand. They have 0 award miles, 0 Loyalty Points, and need 40,000 for Gold. The "Last chance to claim your gift" banner is the only real hook. It offers a complimentary inflight drink or snack coupon, and the deadline is Oct. 23 at 11:59 p.m. CT.
- The offer sits below a card of zeros, so it competes with a discouraging first impression. After it come six offer tiles (AT&T, Admirals Club, Make-A-Wish, referral, gift cards, hotels), a "View more offers" button, and three destination tiles (Paris, Sydney, St. Kitts). Nothing is clearly the lead. The "Register now" link inside the blue banner is small, so the one deadline-driven action is the least prominent control. The bottom third is dense fine print that adds friction and no value.
- A real subscriber would also recognize the repeats. The free-treat coupon is the same one from the Sept 28 "Welcome aboard" and Oct 13 "A complimentary inflight treat" sends. The AT&T 20,000-mile offer reruns the Oct 17 "Earn miles with AAdvantage® partners and offers" email. The Sydney tile echoes the Oct 20 "Discover Australia from $999*" send. For a member with a zero balance, this looks like a recycled catalog with a deadline. It is competent but undifferentiated.

## What's working

- The summary card is personalized with the name "Curt", the as-of date (October 21, 2026), and the status ladder showing 40,000 points to Gold. This gives a real reason to open.
- The gift banner has a hard, credible deadline (register by Oct. 23, 11:59 p.m. CT) and a concrete freebie.
- The "100 years" logo and the "As a new AAdvantage member" line tie the email to the current campaign and to the recipient's newness.
- The render is clean. I see no broken images, overlaps, or misaligned tiles. The two-column offer grid is tidy.
- Offers carry specifics: 20,000 bonus miles per line with AT&T (offer ends Nov. 18), and up to 5,000 miles for referrals with a spend threshold (by Oct. 30).

## What's weak

- The first screen leads with "0 / 0" balances, which is a downer. The only compelling offer is pushed below it.
- "Register now" is a small inline link instead of a button. The "View more offers" and "Explore more destinations" buttons are generic and sit lower in the email.
- Six offer tiles plus three destination tiles dilute focus. Every tile has a different CTA ("Learn more", "Find gifts", "Book a stay"), and none is the clear lead.
- The free-treat coupon and the AT&T offer are repeats of the Oct 13 and Oct 17 sends, and Sydney echoes Oct 20. The cadence feels like recycled content.
- The legal and fine-print block at the bottom is very long, and the referral terms are buried there. It makes the email feel heavy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the gift. Put the "Last chance to claim your gift" banner directly under the greeting, and move the balance card below it or shrink it. Make "Register now" a full-width button.
- 2. Cut the body to one primary offer and two secondary offers. Drop Make-A-Wish, gift cards, and the three destination tiles from this send.
- 3. Make the zero balance useful. Replace "You need 40,000" with a nearest-step message such as "Earn your first miles with one flight or one AT&T switch".
- 4. Use one CTA style and verb-plus-noun copy throughout, for example "Claim my free drink" and "Get 20,000 miles".
- 5. Collapse the legal copy behind a "Terms" link, or keep only the terms for the featured offer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Curt, claim your free inflight drink by Oct. 23`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your AAdvantage® summary: 0 miles, one free gift`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Register by 11:59 p.m. CT tomorrow to keep your complimentary drink or snack coupon.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `See your status, then start earning with 20,000 bonus miles from AT&T.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: AAdvantage October 2026 Account Summary

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with MSO fallbacks and media queries. The one confirmed rendering defect is an empty greeting name slot, even though the subject line has "Curt". The probed tracking links returned 403s and a TLS error, which look like probe or bot-filter artifacts rather than confirmed breakage. The header-based compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because of the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **403 on three tracked CTAs and the preference link** (all `https://l.loyalty.ms.aa.com/rts/go2.aspx?...`):
  - "Register now »" (`h=6658581`)
  - "Learn more »" (`h=6658593`)
  - "Update Email Preferences" (`h=6658570`)
  - The 403s come from an ESP redirector (`go2.aspx` is the Selligent/Marigold pattern). That is typical of bot or user-agent filtering of automated probes. The QA data does not show that real clients fail, so this is **unconfirmed breakage**. It needs a re-test with a browser-like client.
- **TLS error on "Book a hotel stay »"** (`h=6658597`): `TLSV1_ALERT_PROTOCOL_VERSION`. The probing client's TLS negotiation was rejected, which suggests `l.loyalty.ms.aa.com` requires TLS 1.2 or later and the probe offered less. This is likely a probe limitation. It does show the tracking host rejects legacy TLS, which is acceptable.
- **Empty link text in the QA output:** each 403 entry has a blank link label before the parenthetical. This may be image-wrapped links without alt text (see Section 3).
- **Coverage gap:** only 12 of 65 links were probed and 53 were skipped. Footer, social and legal links were not verified.
- **Tracking structure:** the links use opaque `h`, `tp` and `pi` parameters. The preference link also carries `x=c|EML|ESUM|20261021|ADV|MKT|eSummary||eSummary` and `hp2=<64-hex>`. The `pi` value is identical across links, so it is likely a per-recipient identifier.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Mixed content:** five `http://` image sources, all decorative or logo images:
  - `http://www.aa.com/content/images/email/AAirmail/template/spacer_clear.gif` (4 instances; alt text is "American Airlines" or "spacer")
  - `http://i.loyalty.ms.aa.com/wpm/1021/ContentUploads/american_airlines/CTAs/oneworld_logo_special_online_80x80.gif`
  - Some clients may block or rewrite these. Switch them to `https://`.
- **Spacer alt text:** spacer GIFs have `alt="American Airlines"` on some instances. Screen readers will announce the brand name repeatedly for non-content images. Spacers should have `alt=""`.
- **Fonts:** `@font-face` loads woff files from `https://www.aa.com/content/fonts/american-v2/...`. The fallback chain includes `local('OpenSans')`, and the MSO block forces Arial. This is fine.
- **Attribute-selector media queries** such as `*[class=contenttable]` are unsupported in some clients, and Gmail ignores them. Those clients get the desktop layout.
- **CSS-only interactivity:** the template uses checkbox/radio-based carousels and tabs (`input { display:none }`, `#car1:checked ~ .car1`). These work in only a few clients (mainly Apple Mail), and others will fall back to a default state. The `nocontentInteractive` classes suggest a fallback exists, but the truncated source prevents confirming it.
- **Not assessable:** the HTML is truncated, so I can't confirm `lang`, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, or preheader markup.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Confirmed defect:** the QA check found an empty greeting slot ("Hi ,"). The subject line renders "Curt", so the data exists upstream. The body token appears to resolve empty, possibly from a different field than the subject. There is no visible fallback such as "Hi there,".

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found:** the QA tool flags that the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. This is **inconclusive**. Check the raw message at the original receiving mailbox.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain `loyalty.ms.aa.com` was not validated.
- **In-body unsubscribe:** the "Update Email Preferences" link exists but returned 403 to the probe. The QA note says an unsubscribe link is always probed, and none appears as a separate failure. A dedicated unsubscribe link may exist in the skipped or truncated footer, but I can't confirm it from the data.
- **Physical address:** not visible in the truncated source, so it could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Tracking parameters:** the links use ESP redirect tracking and no visible UTM parameters. The campaign taxonomy sits in the `x=` parameter on the preference link (`EML|ESUM|20261021|ADV|MKT|eSummary`). The final destinations were not resolved (403 and TLS error), so landing page alignment and parameter pass-through to aa.com could not be verified.
- **Redirect resolution:** none of the probed tracked links resolved to a final URL, so I can't state whether the CTA copy ("Register now", "Book a hotel stay") matches its landing pages.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the greeting:** add a fallback to the first-name token in the body (for example, "Hi there,") and check why the body token resolves empty while the subject token works.
2. **Re-test the 403 links** with a real browser user agent and TLS 1.2+ before treating them as broken. If they still fail, escalate to the ESP, especially for the preference link.
3. **Verify headers at the original mailbox:** `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results`.
4. **Convert the five `http://` image sources to `https://`.**
5. **Set `alt=""` on spacer GIFs.**
6. **Confirm a visible unsubscribe link and a physical postal address** in the footer.
7. **Resolve redirect chains** for the tracked CTAs and verify the final destinations and any UTM or campaign parameters.
