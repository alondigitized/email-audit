# Update to select AAdvantage® benefits
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a partnership-termination notice dressed in dark-mode-friendly styling, and it's a fundamentally different beast from every prior American email in the brand history — this one delivers bad news instead of selling something. American and Hyatt are unwinding their "enhanced relationship," and the email exists to get members to link their accounts before a Nov. 15, 2026 deadline so they don't lose earned status/points. There's no promo, no CTA to spend money, no sweepstakes — just a compliance-flavored, legally-worded operational update.
- The writing is dense and reads like it was drafted by the same team that writes the terms-and-conditions block at the bottom. There's a real deadline and a real action (link your accounts), but it's buried in the third paragraph after two paragraphs of throat-clearing about the "mutual decision." A member who skims will miss the Nov. 15 date entirely.
- Judgment: functionally necessary, poorly prioritized. This needed a "Action required by Nov. 15" headline treatment, not a headline about mutual corporate handshakes.

## What's working

- The single "Link your accounts →" text CTA is present and unambiguous in intent.
- The three-bullet timeline (link by Nov. 15, earn until Feb. 28, status through Feb. 29, 2028) is genuinely useful and specific once you reach it.
- Clean, uncluttered layout — no competing offers or visual noise fighting for attention.
- No render bugs — text, logo, and hero image all display correctly.

## What's weak

- Headline "Update on Hyatt and American: Your next steps" buries the actual news (partnership ending) and the actual urgency (a hard deadline) behind vague corporate framing.
- The single most important fact — link accounts by Nov. 15, 2026 or lose rewards — is three paragraphs deep, not in the subject, preview, or hero.
- No visual emphasis (bold, color, box) on the Nov. 15 deadline; it reads with the same weight as the "we're excited to continue building" filler line.
- The CTA is a plain text link with an arrow, not a button — easy to miss on mobile, especially against the dark background.
- Generic AA100 tail photo at the bottom adds nothing; that space could reinforce the deadline instead.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rewrite the hero headline to lead with the action and deadline, not the corporate narrative: something like "Link your accounts by Nov. 15 to keep your Hyatt benefits." This is the single highest-impact fix — it turns a skimmable paragraph into an actionable headline.
- 2. Turn "Link your accounts →" into a real button with contrasting color, placed higher on the page (above or immediately after the bullet list, not after a closing pleasantry paragraph).
- 3. Visually isolate the Nov. 15 deadline (bold, colored text, or a small callout box) so it survives a skim.
- 4. Cut the first two paragraphs to one sentence ("American and Hyatt are ending their enhanced partnership") and move straight into "here's what you need to do."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Action needed: link your AAdvantage & Hyatt accounts by Nov. 15`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Hyatt-AAdvantage benefits are changing — here's what to do`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Link your accounts by Nov. 15, 2026 to keep earning World of Hyatt rewards`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Deadline inside: what the Hyatt partnership change means for your miles`
