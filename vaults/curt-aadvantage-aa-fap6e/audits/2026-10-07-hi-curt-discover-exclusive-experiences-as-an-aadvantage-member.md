# Hi Curt, discover exclusive experiences as an AAdvantage® member
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-07**
## Executive summary

- This is an events-and-experiences catalog. It never commits to an offer. The hero says "Events and experiences just for you," followed by eight tiles: concerts, football, baseball, hockey, Cowboys tickets, *A Few Good Men*, and Broadway. Almost every tile ends in the same blue button ("Get tickets" or "Use miles today"). Nothing is priced or dated, and no mile cost appears anywhere. The email also says "just for you," but Curt has no miles and the content isn't tailored to him.
- The email is clean and well built, with no broken images or layout bugs. But it is a brand-awareness send for a miles-redemption product. Curt's earlier account summary showed zero miles, so "use your miles" gives him nothing to act on. It follows the Sept 17 partner-offers catalog and the Oct 5 winter-getaway tile stack. That is a third send in a row that shows many things and recommends none.
- Verdict: competent and inoffensive, but low-intent and forgettable. It is a 4 or 5 in practice.

## What's working

- The "Hello, Curt" greeting and the "AAdvantage® Member" label in the header acknowledge the recipient.
- The hero photo and the tile imagery are crisp and relevant, and the layout is a clean two-column grid.
- The buttons are consistent, visible and high-contrast.
- The AA brand is trusted, the centennial "100" logo is current, and the footer is clear.
- The two sections, "AAdvantage Events tickets" and "Exclusive AAdvantage experiences," give a simple structure.

## What's weak

- There is no concrete offer: no mile prices, no discount, no dates and no deadline. "Use miles today" implies urgency but gives no reason for it.
- Eight near-identical CTAs and two "explore more" buttons compete with each other, and no single hero event leads the email.
- The copy is generic ("Nothing matches the energy of a live show"). It names no artist, team, game, date or city, apart from the Cowboys and a couple of shows.
- The relevance claim doesn't hold. The email says "just for you," but Curt has no miles to spend, and nothing ties the events to his location or interests.
- The "Hi Curt" subject and the personalized greeting are the only personalization. The rest is the same blast every member would get.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one hero event, such as a specific game or show, with a date and a mile price ("Cowboys vs. ___, Nov 16, from 18,000 miles"). Cut the other tiles to two or three at most.
- 2. Segment by miles balance. Members with zero miles should get an "earn miles" angle, such as a credit card or shopping bonus, instead of a redemption pitch.
- 3. Replace the generic buttons with specific ones ("See Cowboys tickets," "Find Broadway dates").
- 4. Add a real deadline or an on-sale window so the urgency is credible.
- 5. Localize the events to the recipient's nearest hub or city.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Concerts, games & Broadway for AAdvantage members`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Tickets from 15,000 miles. Pick your game or show.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Earn miles, then spend them on live events near you.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has a 14% QA pass rate. All six event CTAs ("Get tickets", "Explore more AAdvantage Events", "Don't miss a performance") fail with more than 10 redirects, and both header and logo links return 403 to the probe. The greeting slot is also empty in the body, and the unsubscribe and auth headers could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Excessive redirects (>10), 6 links.** All go through the `l.loyalty.ms.aa.com/rts/go2.aspx` tracker:
  - `h=6627106`, `6627108`, `6627110` and `6627112` ("Get tickets")
  - `h=6627113` ("Explore more AAdvantage Events SM")
  - `h=6627105` ("Don't miss a performance")
  
  Most clients and in-app browsers abort or warn well before 10 hops, so these are likely to dead-end for real users. The redirect chain is probably looping, for example a cookie or consent bounce or a tracker-to-SSO loop. The probe could not confirm a final destination.
- **403 on 2 links.**
  - `go2.aspx?h=6627082…` ("American Airlines" header link)
  - `go2.aspx?h=6627087…` ("Events and experiences just for you")
  
  Both return 403 at the tracker. This may be bot-blocking of the probe rather than a user-facing failure, so treat it as unconfirmed. The same host works for other links in the batch, though, so it is worth manual verification.
- **Tracking parameters are non-standard.** Only some links carry `x=…EML|SOLO|20261007|AADV|MKT|AAdvantage||EventsExperiences` (pipe-delimited). The `h=6627105`–`6627113` CTAs show no `x` or `hp2` parameter in the QA output. No `utm_*` parameters appear on any probed URL. Campaign attribution depends entirely on the redirector.
- **Coverage gap.** Only 12 of 38 links were probed. The other 26 were skipped as footer, utility or social links, so their status is unknown.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Insecure image.** The "oneworld logo" loads from `http://i.loyalty.ms.aa.com/wpm/1021/ContentUploads/american_airlines/CTAs/oneworld_logo_special_online_80x80.gif`. Clients that block mixed content may drop it. The same host likely serves HTTPS.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Blanket dark-mode override.** `@media (prefers-color-scheme:dark) { span, a, td { color:#ffffff !important; } }` forces white text on every `span`, `a` and `td`. Any element that keeps a light background in dark mode will have white text on white. Only `.lightblue-bg` and `.cta-bg` get background overrides.
- **Unclosed `@media` block.** The dark-mode `<style>` ends `… { color: #ffffff !important; } </style>` with one closing brace short. Browsers auto-close it, but strict parsers and some clients may drop the rules.
- **Redundant web-font loading.** Roboto is loaded three ways: `<link rel="stylesheet">`, `@import`, and `preconnect`. Gmail and Outlook strip all of them, and the MSO fallback is `Helvetica Neue, Arial`.
- **Duplicated MSO block.** The `<!--[if gte mso 9]>` style block appears twice, identical.
- **Ambiguous link text.** "Get tickets" appears four times with no distinguishing text in the link. Screen-reader users get four identical links unless `aria-label` or `title` attributes are present, and the truncated source doesn't show them.
- **Global paragraph style.** `p { margin:0 auto; text-align:center }` makes all paragraph text center-aligned by default.
- The preheader is hidden correctly (`display:none`, `mso-hide:all`) and padded with `&#847;&zwnj;&nbsp;` entities. `lang="en"` is set.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Empty greeting slot (QA FAIL).** The body greeting renders as "Hi ," with no first name.
- **Inconsistent with the subject.** The subject renders "Hi Curt", so the first-name value existed at send time. That points to a missing or misnamed merge field in the body template, or a fallback that resolves to blank, and not to missing profile data.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found.** The QA tool notes the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found (RFC 8058).** This is subject to the same capture caveat. One-click unsubscribe can't be confirmed, and Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this sample. The sending domain `loyalty.ms.aa.com` aligns with the tracking and image host `*.loyalty.ms.aa.com`, which is positive.
- The unsubscribe link was reportedly always probed, but the QA output doesn't list it. The footer link and physical address are not visible in the truncated source, so they can't be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Landing alignment can't be assessed. All six CTA chains exceed 10 redirects without resolving, and the two 403 links never reached a landing page.
- The campaign code `EventsExperiences` and the date `20261007` are embedded in the `x=` parameter. Whether it survives to the destination, and whether the destination matches the "AAdvantage Events" promise, is unverified.
- No `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` is present, so downstream web analytics will likely rely on referrer or the redirector's own cookie.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Debug the redirect loop** on `go2.aspx?h=6627105/06/08/10/12/13`. Run `curl -IL --max-redirs 20` and check for cookie-gated or consent-gated bounces. This is the highest-impact fix, because it breaks every primary CTA.
2. **Manually verify the two 403 links** (`h=6627082`, `h=6627087`) with a normal browser user agent, to rule out probe blocking.
3. **Fix the body greeting merge field** so it matches whatever the subject line uses, and add a non-blank fallback such as "Hi there,".
4. **Serve the oneworld logo over HTTPS**: change the `src` to `https://i.loyalty.ms.aa.com/…/oneworld_logo_special_online_80x80.gif`.
5. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta.
6. **Narrow the dark-mode rule** to elements with known dark backgrounds, and close the `@media` block's brace.
7. **Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`** on the raw message at the origin ESP, since the relay may be stripping them. Also confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment from the raw headers.
8. **Differentiate the repeated "Get tickets" links** with an event name in the link text or `aria-label`.
9. Remove the duplicate MSO style block and the redundant font-loading methods.
