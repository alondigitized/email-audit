# See what’s coming for AAdvantage® members
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-07**
## Executive summary

- This is American Airlines pivoting again — after the Aug 4 "Experiences" email tried event redemptions and the Aug 4 destinations email reverted to generic deals, this one is a straight loyalty-program roadmap: "here's what's coming for AAdvantage members" over the next few months. It's the softest-sell email in this recent stretch — no flight deal, no miles-buying pitch, just a preview of future perks (food/beverage coupons, an NYT subscription option, cash+miles booking, FOX One streaming) wrapped around one live offer (5X miles on vacation packages) buried two-thirds down.
- The execution is clean — no render bugs, consistent AA branding, clear section hierarchy — but the content is almost entirely future-tense ("coming soon," "new options to look forward to," "more routes to come"). There's nothing to actually redeem today except the vacation-package multiplier, and the primary CTA ("Learn about the updates") doesn't promise anything concrete. It reads more like a loyalty-program newsletter than a promotional email.
- Compared to the July buy-miles and destination-deal sends, this earns real goodwill points for being informational rather than another ask — but it will underperform on clicks because there's no urgent, specific reason to act right now.

## What's working

- Clean, bug-free render — hero image, headline, and body copy all display correctly with no overlapping text or broken assets.
- Clear visual hierarchy — "Discover what's ahead" headline and cabin photo anchor the top of the email before the eye moves into the bulleted benefit list.
- Heavy loyalty-benefit framing throughout (food/bev coupons, Million Miler gifts, NYT subscription) reinforces membership value without asking for money.
- Social proof included near the bottom — Newsweek's #1 Best Airline Rewards Program and Freddie Awards recognition add credibility.
- Genuine format break from the recent cadence of destination deals and buy-miles pitches (per brand history), which reduces fatigue.

## What's weak

- No concrete, immediate offer above the fold — the hero content is entirely "coming soon" language with no dates, no specific numbers, nothing to click toward today.
- Primary CTA "Learn about the updates" is vague marketing-speak — it doesn't tell the reader what they'll get for clicking.
- The one real, actionable offer (5X miles on AA Vacations packages) is positioned as an afterthought below the fold, disconnected visually from the roadmap content above it.
- Subject line and preheader carry zero specificity or urgency — "See what's coming" could describe almost any AA email and doesn't hint at the food/beverage coupons, NYT deal, or FOX One streaming actually inside.
- No personalization signal in the subject/preview despite the email being addressed to a masked account number inside ("AAdvantage® Member 6V8****").

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the subject and hero with the single most concrete, available-now benefit (food and beverage coupons, or FOX One streaming) instead of a vague "what's coming" teaser — specificity beats curiosity here.
- 2. Rewrite the primary CTA from "Learn about the updates" to something that names the payoff, e.g. "See your new rewards" or "Unlock food & beverage coupons."
- 3. Move the 5X miles vacation offer up near the hero or give it its own visually distinct band — right now it's the only real conversion driver and it's buried.
- 4. Add a date or window to at least one "coming soon" item (NYT subscription, cash+miles booking) so the roadmap doesn't read as indefinitely vague.
- 5. Write a preheader that surfaces a specific perk instead of leaving it blank/generic — free real estate that's currently wasted.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New AAdvantage perk: food & drink coupons in the air`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your AAdvantage benefits just got bigger`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 5X miles on your next vacation package`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Coupons, streaming, and more member perks inside`

# Technical Audit: AAdvantage "Mid-Year Updates" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard MSO/Outlook-hardened HTML template with dark-mode support, but automated QA shows a 22% pass rate driven by three broken/malformed tracked links and a missing CAN-SPAM unsubscribe mechanism.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken link (403)** — CTA "Book a vacation package >" → `https://l.loyalty.ms.aa.com/rts/go2.aspx?h=6532389&tp=i-1NGB-...` returns 403.
- **Broken link (403)** — Logo/header link "American Airlines" → `https://l.loyalty.ms.aa.com/rts/go2.aspx?h=6532385&tp=i-1NHD-...` returns 403.
- **Malformed redirect target** — Masked account-number link ("6V8\*\*\*\*") → `https://l.loyalty.ms.aa.com/rts/go2.aspx?h=6532386&tp=...` resolves to an invalid relative URL: `unknown url type: '/aadvantage-program/profile/account-summary?c=EML|SOLO|...'`. The `go2.aspx` redirector appears to be forwarding a path-only destination without a scheme/host, which will fail in any client that doesn't silently coerce it against a base URL.
- All three broken links share the same redirect host (`l.loyalty.ms.aa.com`) and campaign tag `x=c%7cEML%7cSOLO%7c20260806%7cMKT%7cUSMCOC%7cLYLTY%7cEREDU%7cUS_LYLTY_AWRN_MIDYEARUPDATES`, suggesting a redirector/ESP-side config issue for this send rather than isolated bad URLs.
- Of 12 material links probed, only these 3 failed; 11 lower-priority (footer/utility/social) links were deprioritized and not probed — full link inventory not verified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- Font loading is fragile: `<link rel="preconnect">`/`<link href="...fonts.googleapis.com...">` in `<head>` plus a redundant `@import url(...)` inside `<style>` — most email clients (Outlook, Gmail app) strip both `<link>` tags and `@import`, so Roboto will not render in the majority of clients; MSO conditional-comment fallback to Helvetica/Arial is correctly present as a backstop.
- Dark-mode support implemented via `@media (prefers-color-scheme:dark)` and `[data-ogsc]` selectors (Outlook.com dark mode) — both light and dark paths are defined, no contradictory rules observed.
- Mobile responsive breakpoint present at `max-width: 480px` with explicit width/height overrides (`.w12`–`.w342`, `.h116`) — standard hybrid/fluid pattern.
- `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` and `.ExternalClass`/`.ReadMsgBody` resets present for Apple Mail/Outlook normalization.
- Preheader text uses zero-width joiner/shy-hyphen padding (`&#847;&zwnj;&nbsp;&#8199;&shy;` repeated) to suppress body-text leakage into the preview snippet — functions as intended, not a defect.
- Truncated source does not include `<img>` tags with `alt` attributes in view; image alt-text coverage cannot be verified from available evidence.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- One merge token confirmed: masked AAdvantage number rendered as "6V8\*\*\*\*" (partial account number personalization). The token renders correctly as text, but its underlying link is the malformed relative-URL redirect described in Section 2 — personalization content is fine, the destination binding is broken.
- No other merge-token placeholders (e.g., unresolved `{{...}}` or `%%...%%` syntax) observed in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **[FAIL] No unsubscribe link found in body** — CAN-SPAM requires a visible, functioning opt-out mechanism; none detected.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header not found** — may be a relay-capture limitation (AgentMail) rather than absence at origin, but combined with the body-level failure this is a compliance risk.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found** (RFC 8058) — one-click unsubscribe cannot be confirmed as supported.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status unknown for this message via the AgentMail relay; cannot confirm sender authentication.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- Campaign tracking string is consistent across all three probed links: `c=EML|SOLO|20260806|MKT|USMCOC|LYLTY|EREDU|US_LYLTY_AWRN_MIDYEARUPDATES` — UTM/campaign tagging schema is uniform, no mismatched campaign codes between links.
- The account-summary CTA's redirect target (`/aadvantage-program/profile/account-summary?c=...`) is a relative path with no scheme/host, which breaks the email→site handoff for that link regardless of client-side resolution behavior — this is the same root cause flagged in Section 2, not a separate issue.
- **[WARN] Non-HTTPS image source**: oneworld logo loads over `http://` — `http://i.loyalty.ms.aa.com/wpm/1021/ContentUploads/american_airlines/CTAs/oneworld_logo_special_online_80x80.gif`. Mixed-content blocking may suppress this image in HTTPS-enforcing clients/webmail.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the two 403-ing redirector links (`h=6532389`, `h=6532385`) at the `l.loyalty.ms.aa.com` redirect layer — likely an expired or misconfigured tracking-link entry for this send.
2. Fix the malformed redirect for the account-summary link (`h=6532386`) so `go2.aspx` resolves to a fully-qualified URL rather than a bare path.
3. Add a visible, functioning unsubscribe link in the email body to satisfy CAN-SPAM.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are present at the originating MTA (not just check the relay-captured copy) to support one-click unsubscribe per RFC 8058.
5. Verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC configuration and confirm `Authentication-Results` is present on the raw (pre-relay) message.
6. Change the oneworld logo `src` from `http://` to `https://` to avoid mixed-content image blocking.
