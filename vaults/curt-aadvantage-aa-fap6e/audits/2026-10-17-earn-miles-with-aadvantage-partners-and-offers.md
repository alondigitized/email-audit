# Earn miles with AAdvantage® partners and offers
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-17**
## Executive summary

- The subject says "partners and offers," and the hero says "Exclusive offer from AT&T." The email is really a single AT&T promotion: 20,000 AAdvantage bonus miles per line for switching carriers, with a blue "Switch and earn today" button. That is a concrete offer and the strongest part of the email. Below it sits a four-tile catalog (Marriott Bonvoy, AAdvantage Exchange shopping, a refer-a-friend bonus, and vacation packages) that dilutes the hero. The recipient is a brand-new member with no miles, so most of the earn-and-burn pitch doesn't apply to them yet.
- The footer fine print runs about as long as the email body. It includes tiered referral bonus tables, promo codes (SKI2026, SEA2026) and blackout dates. The headline AT&T offer reads clean at the top, but the "Save from slopes to shore" tile ("save up to $200") and the 5,000-mile referral tile both depend on that legal text.
- Judgement: a competent, honest catalog. It is better than the Oct 5 winter-getaway send because it leads with one number, but it still slides into tile-stacking. It also lands four days after the Oct 13 "complimentary inflight treat" email, so this brand is sending this subscriber something every few days.

## What's working

- The hero commits to one offer: 20,000 bonus miles per line from AT&T, with a deadline (Oct. 31, 9 p.m. PT).
- The "Switch and earn today" button is full-width blue and clearly visible, and its verb is specific.
- The "AAdvantage Member" badge and AA branding at the top make the sender easy to recognize.
- The tile CTAs ("Find hotels," "Shop now," "Learn more," "Book now") are consistent and scannable.
- The closing "You are why we fly" band is a clean brand sign-off.

## What's weak

- The subject ("Earn miles with AAdvantage® partners and offers") is generic and doesn't mention AT&T, the strongest hook.
- The hero offer is about a phone plan, which is off-category for an airline loyalty email. It is also a high-friction ask, since it means switching carriers.
- The four tiles are equal in weight, so nothing guides the eye after the hero. "Learn more" is a weak CTA.
- The tile copy is dense and carries footnote superscripts (¹ ² ³), which signals fine print before the click.
- The legal disclaimers fill roughly half the render, and the referral rules and promo code tables are hard to digest.
- A member with zero miles gets no personalized framing, such as "earn your first miles."

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the subject with the single concrete offer, "20,000 miles for switching to AT&T," or send a different offer to a member with a zero balance.
- 2. Cut the tile grid from four to two, and pick tiles that fit a zero-balance member (for example, first-miles earning through the shopping portal).
- 3. Move the fine print into a "Terms" link or collapse it, keeping only the offer end date and a short summary.
- 4. Replace "Learn more" with a specific CTA such as "Refer a friend, earn 5,000 miles."
- 5. Add a line addressing the member's current status, for example "You're 20,000 miles from your first award."

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "Earn miles with AAdvantage® partners and offers"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a well-formed XHTML table layout with dark-mode and Outlook (MSO) handling, and every link goes through an HTTPS click-tracker (`l.loyalty.ms.aa.com/rts/go2.aspx`). Most of the automated QA "FAIL" results look like probe artifacts rather than confirmed defects. Real issues are a stale or malformed campaign code, one `http://` image, a possibly broken relative redirect on the member-number link, and accessibility-hostile CSS and viewport settings. The HTML was truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and alt text could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **"Doubled-domain" FAIL on "Switch and earn today" (`h=6647784`): likely false positive.**
  - The `x=` parameter decodes to `www.aa.com/web/i18n/aadvantage-program/earn-miles/att-offer.html?c=EML||20261016|MKT|USMCOC|PTR|ERINC|PNL||US_LYLTY_TRAN_ATT_20K`.
  - That is one destination host behind a tracker host, with no repeated domain in the destination.
  - The check seems to be matching `aa.com` appearing in both the tracker and destination hosts. It is reported twice (two FAIL lines for the same URL).
- **403 on 5 links: not confirmed as broken.**
  - The affected links are `h=6647787` (Marriott), `6647785` (aadvantageeshopping), `6647786` (aavacations), `6647790` and `6647783`.
  - These are third-party and brand hosts that commonly block automated probes, and the tracker returned 403 to the probe. Treat them as "unverified", not "broken". The 15% pass rate is inflated by this.
- **Possible real defect, the `6V8****` link (`h=6647791`).**
  - The probe error was `unknown url type: '/aadvantage-program/profile/account-summary?c=...'`. The redirect chain produced a scheme-less, host-less destination.
  - The `x=` value for this link, and for `h=6647790` and `h=6647783`, is only a campaign string (`c%7cEML%7cPNL%7c20261016...`) with no destination host. The destination appears to be resolved server-side from `h=`/`pi=`.
  - If the redirector emits a relative `Location`, the link fails for clients that don't resolve it against `loyalty.ms.aa.com`. Re-test with a real browser session. The page is also login-gated.
- **Stale or malformed campaign code on "Learn more" (`h=6647785`).**
  - The destination is `www.aadvantageeshopping.com/refer-a-friend____.htm?source=cl_AA__cl__em__PartNsltr_RAF_20261009&...&seg=&strm=&...`.
  - The path ends in `____` (looks like an unresolved placeholder).
  - The source code is dated `20261009`, while the send is `20261016`.
  - `seg=` and `strm=` are empty, so downstream attribution may be incomplete.
- **Inconsistent campaign code structure.** `h=6647783` uses `c|EML||20261016|MKT|USMCOC|PNL||...` with empty fields (no `PTR` segment). The others carry `PTR|ERINC`. The AA Vacations link is dated `20261014`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Non-HTTPS image (confirmed in source).**
  - `http://i.loyalty.ms.aa.com/wpm/1021/ContentUploads/american_airlines/CTAs/oneworld_logo_special_online_80x80.gif` (alt "Oneworld") may be blocked or rewritten by clients.
  - Switch to `https://`.
- **Pinch-zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1">` blocks user zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text-resize disabled.** `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; line-height: 100% }` overrides user text scaling, and the universal selector forces 100% line-height everywhere. That can clip wrapped or enlarged text.
- **Dark-mode override risk.**
  - `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark) { span, a, td { color: #ffffff !important; } }` forces white text on every `span`, `a` and `td`.
  - Any cell without a `.lightblue-bg` or `.cta-bg` dark background will render white-on-light.
  - Also, the `@media` block in the second `<style>` is missing its closing `}` for the outer rule in the source shown, so the `[data-ogsc]` rules may be swallowed by the parser. This is unverified because the output is truncated.
- **Relative background asset.** `#swapbg { background-image: url("20230508_HTML-32134_M_bg.png") }` is a relative path inside the mobile media query. It won't resolve in an email client, and the filename is a 2023 leftover. Whether an element with `id="swapbg"` is used could not be confirmed from the truncated HTML.
- **Minor redundancy.**
  - The identical MSO `<style>` conditional block is duplicated.
  - Roboto is loaded via both `<link>` and `@import`. Gmail strips these, so rely on the fallback stack.
- **Headings, alt coverage and landmarks:** not verifiable from the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%…%%`) are visible in the truncated source.
- The link text `6V8****` is a rendered, masked AAdvantage number, so the token is working. The only concern is the broken destination described in section 2.
- The "doubled-domain merge error" label is a false positive. See section 2.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found, and `Authentication-Results` was not found.
- The QA tool itself attributes this to AgentMail relay stripping, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC and one-click unsubscribe status are **unknown, not failed**. Check the raw `.eml` or the original headers before flagging.
- The body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are in the truncated portion and could not be verified. The QA summary says an unsubscribe link was included in probing but gives no result.
- The From domain `loyalty.ms.aa.com` matches the tracker domain `l.loyalty.ms.aa.com`, which supports alignment. This is not conclusive without the headers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **aa.com links** carry the proprietary `c=EML||20261016|…` code and no `utm_*` parameters. This is consistent with AA's own taxonomy and is not an error.
- **Partner links** are mixed:
  - Marriott (`marriott.com/offers/resort-vacations-1355738`) has no campaign parameters.
  - aadvantageeshopping uses `utm_source=cl&utm_medium=em&utm_campaign=PartNsltr&utm_content=RAF` plus the dated `source=` code.
  - aavacations uses a `c=EML…` code.
- The stale `20261009` date and the `____` path on the shopping link are the only continuity defects confirmed by source.
- Landing page alignment could not be verified, since the probes were blocked or errored.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the 403 and "doubled-domain" links with a browser-like user agent, and suppress the doubled-domain check when the repeated host is only the tracker host and the `x=` destination.
2. Fix the `aadvantageeshopping` link:
   - remove the `____` placeholder from the path
   - update `source=` to the send date (`20261016`)
   - populate or drop the empty `seg=` and `strm=` params
3. Test the `6V8****` link in a real browser. If the redirect `Location` lacks a scheme or host, correct the redirector configuration.
4. Change the oneworld logo `src` to `https://`.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1`, and replace `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` with `100%`.
6. Scope the dark-mode `color: #ffffff !important` to elements that have dark backgrounds, and verify the CSS braces close correctly.
7. Replace the relative `20230508_HTML-32134_M_bg.png` reference with an absolute HTTPS URL, or delete it if unused.
8. Re-run the audit against the full raw message so the headers and footer (unsubscribe link, address) can be checked.
