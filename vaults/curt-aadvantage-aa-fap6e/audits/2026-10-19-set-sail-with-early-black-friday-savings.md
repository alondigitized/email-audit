# Set sail with early Black Friday savings
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**

- The hero promises "Unwrap 2X AAdvantage® miles," but the subject says "Set sail with early Black Friday savings." The two lead with different offers. The body copy then resolves the "2X" claim into 2 miles per $1 spent, with 1 base mile and 1 bonus mile. That is a modest, honest earn rate, but it sits in a plain paragraph under the hero and isn't treated as the offer. The real deal is spread across four cruise-line tiles, each with its own bullets: Royal Caribbean, Norwegian, Holland America and Seabourn. The tile offers are up to $750 instant savings, 50% off, up to 100,000 bonus miles and others, and they compete with each other.
- The email is a cruise catalog, like the Oct 5 "Find your perfect winter getaway" send, which also stacked near-identical tiles under a generic hero. The inbox promise and the body don't quite match. The subject says "Black Friday savings," but the body is a miles promotion with cruise-line deals attached. The strongest piece is the Seabourn tile's "Up to 100,000 AAdvantage® bonus miles," and it is buried in the bottom-right tile.
- This is a decent, clean layout with a real mileage earn offer, but it has too many offers and a mismatch between subject and hero. The terms are long, with a booking window of 10/18–10/31 for sailings through 12/31/28. For a member who has been getting a different promotional theme every few days, this is a middling send.

## What's working

- The hero headline is large, bold and easy to read. It names the loyalty currency, and the Alaska-style cruise image sets the theme.
- The offer math is spelled out in the first paragraph: 2 miles per $1 spent, plus 1X Loyalty Points toward status.
- The single blue "Cruise into joy" button sits directly under the copy and is clearly the primary action.
- The four tiles are visually consistent, with a cruise-line name, three bullets and a "Book now" button, so they scan quickly.
- The member ID is visible in the header, which signals personalization.

## What's weak

- The subject says "early Black Friday savings," but the hero leads with 2X miles. Black Friday appears only in the body copy as "Black Friday Preview."
- Four cruise lines with different offers (specialty dinner for 2, 50% off, $200 on board, 100,000 bonus miles) leave no single hero deal.
- The "Cruise into joy" CTA is vague. It doesn't say what you get or where the click goes.
- The four "Book now" buttons are identical and non-specific, so they don't say which deal or ship you are booking.
- "Up to" language ("Up to $750," "Up to 50% off," "Up to 100,000 miles") reads as a ceiling rather than a real offer. The fine print adds a processing fee, a booking window and sample-pricing caveats.
- The deadline is only in the legal footer (midnight EST on 10/20/26). Nothing in the body creates urgency.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero offer and make the subject and hero match it. For example, lead with "2X miles on every cruise" and demote the cruise-line tiles to supporting content.
- 2. Put the deadline in the hero or just below the headline instead of the legal copy.
- 3. Make the CTA specific, such as "Earn 2X on a cruise" or "Browse cruises," and give each tile button a distinct label like "See Royal Caribbean deals."
- 4. Cut the grid to two tiles, or lead with the strongest deal. Seabourn's 100,000 bonus miles is the standout.
- 5. Add a short "how to earn" line near the top: "Book by 10/31, earn 2 miles per $1."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with dark-mode CSS and a preheader. The main confirmed problems are an HTTP 403 on a tracked link, an `http://` image source, an unbalanced MSO conditional comment, and a viewport that blocks zoom. The source is truncated and the QA run's pass rate was 43%. Anything below the truncation point (footer, unsubscribe block, most images) could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] 403 on the "American Airlines" link:** `https://l.loyalty.ms.aa.com/rts/go2.aspx?h=6655106&tp=i-1NHD-GT-1Ptn-...&x=c|EML|SOLO|20261019|AADV|MKT|AAdvantage||AADV-Cruises&hp2=52f6...d6d7`. This is a Responsys-style redirector. A 403 from a probe usually means bot filtering of the tracking host, not a dead link, so a real recipient may be fine. It is unverified either way, and the final destination was never resolved.
- **Insecure image:** `http://i.loyalty.ms.aa.com/wpm/1021/ContentUploads/american_airlines/CTAs/oneworld_logo_special_online_80x80.gif` ("oneworld logo"). Some clients block or warn on non-HTTPS images. The same host very likely serves HTTPS, so the fix is probably only a scheme change.
- **Probe coverage:** only 12 of 27 links were probed (15 skipped). The remaining links, including the unsubscribe URL result, are unreported.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Unbalanced MSO conditionals:** the head opens three (`<!--[if mso]>`, then `<!--[if gte mso 9]>` twice) but has only two `<![endif]-->`. The first `[if mso]` block is never closed, which can cause Outlook to mis-parse or drop the rest of the head.
- **Duplicate block:** the identical `[if gte mso 9]` style block appears twice.
- **Viewport:** `maximum-scale=1` in `<meta name="viewport">` disables pinch-zoom on mobile (WCAG 1.4.4 concern).
- **Text-size adjust:** `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; line-height: 100% }` overrides the `body` rule of `100%`. It stops iOS text scaling and forces tight line height on everything.
- **Dark mode:** the `@media (prefers-color-scheme:dark)` block sets `span, a, td { color:#ffffff !important }`. Any text cell without a dark background class would render white on white. The visible source doesn't show an instance, so this is a risk to test rather than a confirmed bug.
- **Fonts:** Roboto is loaded both by `<link>` and by `@import` in the same head, which is redundant. Gmail ignores both, so the fallback stack matters.
- **Alt text:** only the oneworld logo was evidenced to have alt text. The other images are in the truncated section.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens were visible in the HTML provided. The `hp2=` value in the link is a per-recipient hash, which is expected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] No `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header.** The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may strip these, so this is unconfirmed. Check the raw headers at the source.
- **[WARN] No `Authentication-Results` header.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown because of the relay. The tracking host (`l.loyalty.ms.aa.com`) is a subdomain of the From domain (`loyalty.ms.aa.com`), which is consistent with domain alignment.
- **Footer:** the visible unsubscribe link and postal address are below the truncation point, so they were not verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The tracked link carries no `utm_*` parameters. Attribution runs through the opaque Responsys token and the `x=` field (`...|20261019|AADV|MKT|AAdvantage||AADV-Cruises`). That is internally consistent with the cruise-themed subject, but it is not standard UTM tagging.
- The 403 blocks landing-page checks, so I can't confirm where the link goes or whether the offer matches the "early Black Friday / Double AAdvantage miles" message.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the 403 link with a browser-like user agent or a real browser. If it still fails, escalate to the sender.
2. Change the oneworld logo `src` to `https://`.
3. Add the missing `<![endif]-->` so the `[if mso]` block is closed, and remove the duplicate `[if gte mso 9]` block.
4. Drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
5. Remove `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` from the `*` selector, and give `line-height` a real value instead of `100%`.
6. Scope the dark-mode `span, a, td { color:#fff !important }` rule to classed elements only, then test it in Apple Mail and Outlook dark mode.
7. Check the raw message source (not the AgentMail relay copy) for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
8. Raise the probe cap, or probe the unsubscribe and footer links explicitly, so the remaining 15 links get coverage.
