# Get closer to the action with AAdvantage Perks℠
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-14**
## Executive summary

- This is a five-team sweepstakes catalog dressed up as a "fandom" email — enter to win a trip, a flight, or 100K miles for the Cowboys, Eagles, Rams, Chargers, or Panthers. It's the same beat American keeps hitting: the 2026-09-08 "100,000 AAdvantage bonus miles" send was also a sweepstakes with the grand prize being a shared pool, not a per-entrant guarantee, and this one repeats that structure five times over with no odds disclosed anywhere visible. There's no purchase-based offer, no fare, no deadline — just five "Enter to win" buttons stacked vertically.
- Visually it's clean and on-brand (stadium hero, consistent team-tile layout, the "100" anniversary branding throughout), but it's also a wall of near-identical modules that force a lot of scrolling before landing on the one real revenue CTA ("Search flights") buried below the fold. The account summary at the bottom is another repeat of the "0 Award miles / 0 Loyalty Points" awkwardness seen in the 2026-08-28 account-summary email — a lapsed-looking account being shown a sweepstakes instead of a reactivation offer is a miss.
- Judgment: this is a low-value send for most subscribers — a sweepstakes with unclear odds, five competing CTAs, and no concrete fare or deadline. It'll get opens on team-name curiosity but won't convert.

## What's working

- Clean, uncluttered layout — each team tile follows the same visual pattern (logo, headline, one-line pitch, single blue button), so it's scannable even though there are five of them.
- Strong hero image (packed stadium, American branding integrated into the roof) sets the "big game" mood immediately.
- "Search flights" CTA gives a fallback for people who don't want to gamble on a sweepstakes, tying the promo back to an actual bookable product.
- Personalization present ("Hi Curt") and the account summary module shows real membership context.

## What's weak

- Five separate sweepstakes stacked in one email creates decision paralysis — no single hero offer to focus on, unlike the 2026-09-09 Europe fare email that scored well specifically because it had one hero fare.
- No odds, entry deadline, or prize-value disclosure visible anywhere — "100K reasons to enter" and similar copy oversell a prize pool without stating who's actually eligible or when this closes, same fine-print problem flagged in the 2026-09-08 miles-giveaway send.
- Account summary shows 0 Award miles / 0 Loyalty Points — the same discouraging balance seen in the August account-summary send — surfacing a sweepstakes to a seemingly dormant account instead of a re-engagement offer feels tone-deaf.
- No real urgency — nothing tells the reader why they should act today versus next week.
- The "Search flights" CTA has no visible fare, city, or date attached — it's a generic link, not a concrete offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to one or two featured teams (e.g., the reader's home market team) instead of five, and put the entry deadline and odds directly under the headline — the current format is a scroll-fatigue generator with no differentiation from the last sweepstakes send.
- 2. Add a real closing date ("Enter by Sept 30") to create credible urgency; right now nothing suggests it's time-bound.
- 3. Replace or supplement the sweepstakes with a fare-based CTA up top for the segment of subscribers unlikely to feel lucky — pair "Search flights" with an actual price point instead of a bare button.
- 4. Segment by home city/team where possible so the reader only sees one relevant tile instead of five, tightening the email and reducing the wall-of-tiles feel.
- 5. Don't pair a 0/0 loyalty snapshot with an entry-to-win pitch — for accounts with no activity, swap in an earn-miles or reactivation message instead.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your team, your away game — enter by Sept 30`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `First-class flights, tickets, and 100K-mile prizes — five teams, one entry`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cowboys, Eagles, Rams, Chargers, Panthers: pick your team, enter to win`
