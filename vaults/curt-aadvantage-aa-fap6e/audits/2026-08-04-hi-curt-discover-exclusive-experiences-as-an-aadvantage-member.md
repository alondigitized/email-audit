## What's weak

- No pricing or mileage cost is shown anywhere — not even a "starting at X,XXX miles" — so there's no way to gauge value before clicking.
- No urgency or deadline on any offer; nothing signals why Curt should act today versus next month.
- Six roughly-equal CTAs dilute focus — there's no clear "hero" offer for the eye to land on first, just a grid of similar-weight cards.

- Preview text is missing/not distinct — nothing to complement the subject in the inbox.

- **Inconsistent campaign tagging**: the two 403'd content links carry an `x=` campaign param (e.g. `x=c|EML|SOLO|20260804|AADV|MKT|AAdvantage||EventsExperiences`), while all 5 excessive-redirect CTAs (Find tickets, Explore more, Witness tennis, Feel the rumble, Be there for game day) lack the `x=` param entirely — attribution would be incomplete even if the links resolved.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- Roboto webfont is loaded three redundant ways (`<link rel="preconnect">` ×2, `<link rel="stylesheet">`, and a duplicate `@import` inside `<style>`). Most webmail clients (Gmail, Outlook.com) strip external font `<link>`/`@import` rules, so this will silently fall back — no non-MSO fallback font stack is defined outside the `<!--[if gte mso 9]>` block, meaning non-Outlook clients that also block webfonts get an unstyled default font.
- The `<!--[if gte mso 9]>` conditional style block is duplicated verbatim (two identical blocks in `<head>`) — dead weight, no functional impact.
- Dark-mode CSS applies a broad, unscoped rule: `[data-ogsc] span, a, td { color: #ffffff !important; }`. Without per-element exceptions, this risks white-on-white text if any span/td has a light background not covered by `.lightblue-bg`/`.cta-bg`.
- Preheader hidden span (`display:none !important`) uses an extremely long chain of zero-width/invisible character entities for spacing padding — functional but adds unnecessary bytes to head-of-email weight.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

This indicates the subject-line and body-content merge fields are pulling from different data bindings, or the body template's name field isn't populated for this send.
