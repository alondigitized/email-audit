# Curt, view your August 2026 account summary
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-28**
## Executive summary

- This is American Airlines' monthly account-summary email, and it lands with a genuinely awkward number front and center: Curt's balance shows "0 AWARD MILES" and "0 LOYALTY POINTS," which reads more like a lapsed or brand-new account than a valued member being courted with six offer tiles and three destination tiles. That's a real problem for a "your account summary" send — the entire premise is personalized data, and the personalized data on display is a zero.
- Past that stumble, the body reverts to the same offer-grab-bag pattern seen in the Aug 17 "Earn miles with AAdvantage partners" email — Stand Up To Cancer, Miles for Heroes, AT&T, AAdvantage Dining, MaximizeMiles vacations, and a concert-ticket tile, none of which is actually about Curt's account status. The destinations block (Auckland, Aruba, Rome) is generic wanderlust filler, not tied to Curt's history or home airport.
- Net: functional and clean-rendering, but it undersells its own premise. An "account summary" email should lead with something to be proud of or act on; this one leads with a zero and buries the actual CTA (manage account) as a small top-right button.

## What's working

- Clean, no render bugs — no overlapping text, broken images, or placeholder copy anywhere in the scroll.
- The top module clearly states purpose ("AAdvantage account summary," "Summary as of August 26, 2026") with a visible "Manage your account" button.
- Consistent AA brand voice and visual system throughout — navy/red palette, consistent button styling, real photography.
- Progress-to-status framing ("You need 40,000 Loyalty Points to reach AAdvantage Gold") is a legitimate engagement hook when the numbers aren't zero.

## What's weak

- Balance display shows 0 Award Miles and 0 Loyalty Points — for an account-summary email this reads as broken or embarrassing, not motivating.
- Six unrelated offer tiles (charity donation, military miles, AT&T, dining, vacation packages, concert tickets) crowd out the one thing this email claims to be about: Curt's account.
- The destinations block (New Zealand, Aruba, Rome) has zero connection to any account data — no "based on your searches" or "from your home airport" personalization, just generic bucket-list imagery.
- Primary CTA is diffuse — there are 9+ buttons total, all with equal visual weight, so nothing signals "click this first."
- Same cadence problem flagged in the Aug 17 audit: this is another loosely-themed link dump wearing the copy of a different premise (this time "account summary" instead of "partner offers").

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix or suppress the zero-balance display — if Curt genuinely has 0/0, don't lead the email with it; if it's a data/rendering issue, that's the single highest-impact fix since it undermines the whole "summary" premise.
- 2. Cut the offer-tile grid from six to two or three and tie them to something in the actual account (e.g., "you're 40,000 points from Gold — here's the fastest way to close the gap") rather than an undifferentiated charity/telecom/dining/concert mix.
- 3. Replace the generic destinations block with searches/routes tied to Curt's actual flight or search history, or drop it entirely in favor of one strong status-progress CTA.
- 4. Consolidate to a single primary CTA above the fold ("See your status progress") and demote everything else to a secondary "more ways to earn" section.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Curt, here's your AAdvantage progress toward Gold`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your August AAdvantage summary is ready, Curt`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `See your miles, loyalty points, and what's next for status.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `You're 40,000 points from Gold — here's the fastest path.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a templated AA Advantage account-summary email (legacy MSO/VML/table markup with carousel toggle hacks) built on a redirect-tracking domain (`l.loyalty.ms.aa.com`) that is failing the majority of automated QA checks — most notably an empty personalization token and multiple broken/blocked tracked links.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken links (HTTP 403), 3 confirmed:**
  - "Donate today »" → `l.loyalty.ms.aa.com/rts/go2.aspx?h=6558989...`
  - "Book now »" → `l.loyalty.ms.aa.com/rts/go2.aspx?h=6558991...`
  - "Update Email Preferences" → `l.loyalty.ms.aa.com/rts/go2.aspx?h=6558968...`
- **Excessive redirect chain:** "Find tickets »" (`h=6558993`) exceeds 10 hops before resolving — likely to time out or fail in strict mail clients/link scanners.
- All tracked links route through the `l.loyalty.ms.aa.com/rts/go2.aspx` redirector with opaque `h=`/`tp=`/`pi=` params — no way to verify final destination from source alone; the 403s and redirect-depth failures indicate the redirect service itself, not the destination, is unhealthy for these specific `h` IDs.
- Coverage caveat: only 12 of 63 total links were probed (51 skipped per click-likelihood ranking); unprobed footer/social links were not verified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- Heavy reliance on legacy Outlook conditional comments (`<!--[if gte mso 9]>`), VML namespaces, and checkbox-driven CSS carousels (`#car1:checked ~ .car1`, `#tab1:checked ~ .tab1`) — these interactive patterns silently degrade to static/broken states in clients without full CSS selector support (most mobile and webmail clients).
- `@font-face` sources (`americansans-*.woff`) are proprietary AA assets; no fallback issue noted beyond the existing `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` stack, which is correctly declared.
- No alt-text data available in the truncated source to confirm image accessibility compliance.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** name field renders as "Hi ," — merge token failed to populate despite subject line correctly resolving to "Curt, view your August 2026 account summary." This indicates the greeting-block token and subject-line token pull from different (or inconsistently populated) data fields.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] List-Unsubscribe header not found** — may simply reflect relay capture limits (AgentMail) rather than absence on the origin message; cannot confirm from source alone.
- **[WARN] List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found (RFC 8058)** — one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed.
- **[WARN] Authentication-Results header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status cannot be verified via this relay.
- In-body unsubscribe mechanism ("Update Email Preferences") is present but currently **returns 403** (see §2) — functionally this is a CAN-SPAM compliance risk if the only in-body opt-out link is broken, independent of header-level unsubscribe support.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Tracked links use AA's internal redirect param schema (`h`, `tp`, `pi`, `hp2`) rather than standard `utm_*` query params — e.g. `x=c|EML|ESUM|20260826|ADV|MKT|eSummary||eSummary` on the preferences link encodes campaign/date/program metadata in a pipe-delimited custom format. Continuity to landing pages cannot be verified since the redirector itself is returning 403s for 3 of the 4 evidenced links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix or reissue redirect IDs `h=6558989`, `h=6558991`, `h=6558968` on `l.loyalty.ms.aa.com` — currently 403.
2. Investigate the >10-hop redirect chain on `h=6558993` ("Find tickets »") — collapse to reduce timeout/blocklist risk.
3. Fix the greeting merge-token binding so the name field populates consistently with the subject-line token (both should resolve to "Curt").
4. Prioritize fixing "Update Email Preferences" specifically — a broken unsubscribe/preferences link is a compliance-relevant defect regardless of header-level unsubscribe status.
5. Migrate the 5 flagged `http://` image sources (`spacer_clear.gif`, `oneworld_logo_special_online_80x80.gif`) to `https://` to avoid mixed-content blocking in modern mail clients.
6. Confirm List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post headers and Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM) are present at the true send source — current absence may be a relay-capture artifact of AgentMail rather than a sender-side gap; verify against raw headers at the MTA.
