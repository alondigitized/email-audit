# Homepage snapshot · www.allbirds.com · 2026-07-22
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-22**
## Executive summary

- Classic Allbirds — clean, confident, product-first. They're clearly pushing the Dasher H2 as a big new drop, and the minimal aesthetic still does exactly what it always has. What it's NOT doing is making me feel like a returning customer who's bought three pairs, which at this point I kind of expect.

## What's working

- **"ALL NEW DASHER H2 COLLECTION" badge + hero image**: New-drop framing is exactly right for me — I pre-order, and this signals "something just dropped, pay attention"
- **"Super Natural." tagline**: The double meaning earns it — I'm buying Allbirds specifically because of the biodegradable/natural materials story, and this copy activates that value without being preachy
- **Free shipping at $100**: I hit this threshold on basically every Allbirds order; it lands as a real benefit, not padding
- **Minimal nav (hamburger + wordmark + search + bag)**: Feels like a native app, not a bloated retail site — appreciated as a tech worker who hates UI clutter
- **"SHOP MEN" / "SHOP WOMEN" split CTAs**: Clean gendered split gets me to the right product surface in one tap

## What's weak

- **Laced shoe as the hero**: I refuse laces — that's my whole deal — and the entire above-fold image is a laced runner. A no-lace loyalist scrolling past this is already being pointed toward a product they wouldn't buy
- **No material callout in the hero**: "Super Natural." makes a promise; the page doesn't back it up. What's it made of? Merino? Tree fiber? Eucalyptus? This is exactly the information I came for
- **Zero social proof above fold**: No review count, no star rating, no "bestseller" badge on a $130+ shoe — that's a miss for first-timers and a missed reinforcement for returning buyers
- **No returning-customer recognition**: After three pairs, nothing here acknowledges I've been here before. No account nudge, no loyalty points callout, nothing
- **Two equal-weight CTAs**: "SHOP MEN" and "SHOP WOMEN" sitting side-by-side as identical buttons forces a micro-decision; on mobile, one should dominate

## Recommendations

- **Carousel the hero** to show both a laced and slip-on Dasher H2 SKU — or lead with the slip-on variant for the no-lace segment; don't bet the whole above-fold experience on one silhouette
- **Add a material line under the headline**: something like "Natural Merino wool upper · FSC-certified sole" turns "Super Natural." from marketing into proof, which is what converts people like me
- **Surface a returning-buyer signal in the nav**: a small account/rewards badge next to the bag icon would change how a repeat customer experiences this page without redesigning anything major
- **Make one CTA collection-specific**: swap "SHOP WOMEN" for "Shop Dasher H2" when you're running a launch campaign — let the hero banner drive to the specific drop
