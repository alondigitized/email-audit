# Homepage snapshot · www.allbirds.com · 2026-06-17
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-17**
## Executive summary

- This is Allbirds doing what Allbirds does — clean, minimal, sustainability-forward, no unnecessary noise. The Dasher H2 drop is front and center, and the "Super Natural" line is basically my brand promise distilled into two words. But they're showing me a laced runner as the hero, and I literally refuse laces — so right out of the gate, this is me saying "yeah, cool shoe, where are the loungers?"

## What's working

- **"Super Natural." tagline** — two words that hit the core reason I pick Allbirds over any other casual shoe. It's not trying too hard.
- **"SHOP MEN" pill button** — directly in the hero, no hunting through a hamburger menu to get to my gender. I can tap it in one move.
- **Dasher H2 positioning as a new drop** — "ALL NEW" language activates pre-order instincts immediately. I want to know specs.
- **Free shipping threshold ($100) in the sticky banner** — I always check this. It's there without me having to scroll to the footer.
- **Mobile layout is tight** — no crowding, no overlapping, the image fills the frame the way it should. Zero friction in the render.

## What's weak

- **The hero shoe has laces.** I don't do laces. The most prominent product on the page is not my product. That's a miss for someone in my purchase pattern.
- **No person in the frame** — just feet on concrete. I can't see if this is my lifestyle or my dad's lifestyle. A shot of a guy in joggers walking to a coffee shop would do more.
- **Zero sustainability proof in the hero** — "Super Natural" is the claim, but no "70% natural materials" or carbon-offset badge anywhere above the fold. I care about the actual spec, not just the vibe.
- **No loyalty hook for returning customers** — three pairs bought and the site greets me like a stranger. Not even a "Members get early access to the H2" teaser.
- **Two-CTA split ("SHOP MEN" / "SHOP WOMEN")** dilutes focus slightly on a mobile screen — both buttons compete for the same visual weight.

## Recommendations

- **Add one sustainability stat to the hero** — overlay "Made with X% natural materials" or the carbon number directly on the product image; it converts my "I like the vibe" into "I trust the claim."
- **Surface a laceless/slip-on product rail below the hero** — even one scrollable row of Tree Loungers or Mizzle Slip-ons makes the page relevant to non-runner shoppers like me.
- **Introduce a returning-customer badge in the top nav** — something like a loyalty indicator or "New in your size" chip that fires when I'm logged in; converts my brand familiarity into actual urgency.
- **Swap one of the hero CTAs to product-specific** — "See Dasher H2 Details" alongside "SHOP MEN" so launch-day browsers can go straight to the shoe without a category detour.
