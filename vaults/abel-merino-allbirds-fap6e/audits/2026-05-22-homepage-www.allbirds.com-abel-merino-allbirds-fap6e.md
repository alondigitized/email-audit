# Homepage snapshot · www.allbirds.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- Allbirds is leading hard with a Memorial Day sale — 30% off when you drop $150 or more — and the whole above-the-fold is basically one big coupon announcement on a muted blue card. It reads like a sustainable shoe brand that's comfortable playing the discount game this weekend. No frills, no fluff, just the offer front and center.

## What's working

- **The $150 threshold is explicit.** I own three pairs, I know an Allbirds cart hits $150 without trying. This offer is immediately useful to me — I was already eyeing the Lounger refresh.
- **"Discount Automatically Applied at Checkout"** removes the coupon-code anxiety. That one line of subhead copy is doing heavy conversion lifting.
- **MEN'S tab in the nav** is the first item — one tap from anywhere on this page to my actual category.
- **Memorial Day timing** lands right. This is exactly the kind of seasonal window I wait for to scoop a second pair.

## What's weak

- **No CTA button in the hero card.** I'm staring at a big discount announcement with no button to act on it. The offer is clear but the exit ramp is invisible.
- **The lifestyle image is anonymous legs.** Just dark pants and off-white slip-ons from mid-thigh down — could be anyone, any brand, any decade. Doesn't sell the product, doesn't sell a feeling.
- **No returning-customer acknowledgment.** I've bought here three times. There's zero signal that this site knows me — no "Your rewards," no "Pick up where you left off," nothing.
- **Nav truncation on "BESTSELLE"** — looks like "BESTSELLERS" is getting clipped on mobile. Minor but sloppy on a brand that's otherwise clean.
- **No urgency signal.** "Memorial Day Sale" implies a deadline but there's no "Ends Monday" or countdown. I might close the tab and forget about it.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop Men's Sale" button to the hero card** — even a ghost/outline button on the blue background. Right now the offer floats with no exit ramp.
- **Swap the anonymous-legs lifestyle shot for a shoe-forward hero** — show the Runners or Tree Loungers in context (commute, standing desk, weekend errand). That's who actually buys here.
- **Add a Memorial Day countdown** — even just "Ends Monday" in the promo strip above the nav. One line, drives urgency without being sleazy.
- **Surface a returning-shopper hook** — a thin "Welcome back" or rewards-balance line in the header for logged-in users would immediately make me feel seen and less likely to bounce.
