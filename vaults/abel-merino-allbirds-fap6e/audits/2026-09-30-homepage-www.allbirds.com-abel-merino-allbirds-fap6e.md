# Homepage snapshot · www.allbirds.com · 2026-09-30
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- Allbirds still looks like the calm, natural-materials sneaker brand I know, but the first thing I hit is a full-screen "Unlock 10% Off Your Order" popup. It covers most of the hero. Behind it I can make out a fall street-style campaign for the "NEW DASHER NZ COLLECTION" and the line "...ble. Super Natural."

## What's working

- **Free shipping strip ("Free ground shipping on orders over $100"):** it's clear and easy to spot. A single pair of Allbirds shoes will usually clear that threshold.
- **Clean nav (NEW ARRIVALS / SHOP ALL / MEN / WOMEN):** I can get to Men's in one tap, and the search and bag icons are where I expect them.
- **Seasonal street-style hero:** the quilted jacket, city sidewalk and mesh sneaker close-up feel current. The "Dasher NZ" label makes me curious, since NZ hints at the merino wool I care about.
- **Popup product shot:** the suede-and-cream retro runner on a moss-texture background looks good and sells the natural-material feel.

## What's weak

- **Popup on first load:** it blocks the hero headline and the "SHOP MEN" button before I've seen anything. I'd close it on reflex, and some people would bounce.
- **10% needs email and texts:** the copy says "email and texts", so I expect a phone-number ask after "CONTINUE". That's more friction than I want for 10%.
- **Hero imagery:** the lead model is a woman. The only male-coded shot is a close-up of feet and a mesh sneaker with laces, which is exactly what I avoid.
- **Wool and sustainability claims:** nothing I can see mentions merino, biodegradable materials or carbon footprint. Those are my two decision drivers.
- **Blank tiles under the hero:** the four rounded tiles at the bottom are empty dark blocks with no image or label, so they look unloaded.
- **No trust signals:** no ratings, review counts or returns policy are visible.

## Recommendations

- **Delay the popup:** trigger it on exit intent or after about 30% scroll, and show the hero headline and "SHOP MEN" button first.
- **Add a material line to the hero:** something like "Merino wool. Naturally soft. Biodegradable." near the Dasher NZ label, so a wool-and-eco shopper like me sees the claim in the first three seconds.
- **Add a returns line to the top strip:** "Free ground shipping over $100 · 30-day returns". It's cheap to add and speaks to my return habits.
- **Make the tile rail show images and labels immediately:** use real product or category images with names like "Runners", "Loungers" and "Weather-mate" (or the current equivalents).
