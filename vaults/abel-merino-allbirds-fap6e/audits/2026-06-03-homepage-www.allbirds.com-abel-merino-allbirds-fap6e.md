# Homepage snapshot · www.allbirds.com · 2026-06-03
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-03**
## Executive summary

- I opened this and immediately clocked "ALL NEW DASHER NZ COLLECTION" — NZ means New Zealand merino to me and that's my catnip. "Wildly Comfortable. Super Natural." is two-thirds of my entire buying checklist in four words. This homepage is doing exactly what Allbirds is supposed to do: tell me the new drop is here, why the materials are legit, and get out of my way.

## What's working

- **"ALL NEW DASHER NZ COLLECTION" eyebrow** — three words and I'm already calculating whether this fits my lineup. "New" is my pre-order trigger; "NZ" signals the wool story I care about.
- **"Wildly Comfortable. Super Natural." headline** — this is my vocabulary, not marketing copy. No buzzword soup, no feature list.
- **"Easy Returns." in the announcement bar** — I return within the 30-day window regularly and with zero guilt. Seeing this at the very top removes any hesitation before I even hit the hero.
- **Free Shipping over $75 threshold** — stated upfront, easy math: one pair clears it.
- **Dual pill CTAs (SHOP MEN / SHOP WOMEN)** — no gendering ambiguity in the hero, I don't have to hunt.
- **MEN'S and NEW ARRIVALS** both visible in the sub-nav — the two tabs I'd naturally hit are right there.

## What's weak

- **Zero loyalty recognition** — three pairs in my closet and this page treats me like a stranger. No rewards balance, no "early access for members," no Flock (or whatever their loyalty program is) callout. That's a real miss for a returning customer.
- **No discount or active price reduction** — free shipping is fine but I'm conditioned to wait for the end-of-season sale. Nothing here breaks that patience or tells me waiting would cost me.
- **Carousel with hidden content** — there's a pause button and a dot indicator, which tells me slides 2 and 3 exist. I have no idea what's in them. If there's a sale or a second new release buried in slide 3, I'm probably not swiping to find it.
- **"BESTSELLE..." nav item clipped** — it's truncated on the right edge of the mobile viewport. Minor, but it reads sloppy on a clean minimal brand.
- **No material badge on the hero shoe** — I'm already asking "is this merino? Is it recycled?" The answer is probably yes but it's not visible on screen. A small "NZ Merino · Natural" tag adjacent to the product image would seal it.

## Recommendations

- **Surface a logged-in loyalty hook in the announcement bar or hero overlay** — "Flock members get early access to Dasher NZ" or a rewards balance chip turns a returning customer into an immediate tapper.
- **Add a material micro-badge to the hero image** — anchor something like "NZ Merino · Naturally dyed" directly onto the shoe in the hero. I'm asking the question; answer it before I scroll.
- **Make the carousel navigable at a glance** — show thumbnail previews or a label on the dot indicators so I know if slide 2 is a sale or just a colorway variant.
- **Fix the "BESTSELLE..." nav truncation** — either shorten the label to "BEST" or adjust the sub-nav to scroll horizontally without clipping.
