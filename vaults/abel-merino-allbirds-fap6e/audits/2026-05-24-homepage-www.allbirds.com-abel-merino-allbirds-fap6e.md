# Homepage snapshot · www.allbirds.com · 2026-05-24
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-24**
## Executive summary

- Allbirds is running a Memorial Day sale right now — 30% off $150+ — and they're leading with that everywhere, from the sticky banner to the full hero card. As someone who times purchases around exactly these windows, the offer lands. But the homepage is purely promotional scaffolding at this point: I get the deal, I see the nav, and then it just kind of trails off into a cropped lifestyle shot. There's no product, no sustainability copy, no laces-free callout — just "spend $150 and save."

## What's working

- **The $150 threshold is calibrated right for me.** One pair of Runners or Loungers at full price gets me close or over. This isn't a bait-and-switch "spend $400 to save."
- **The double-exposure on the offer** — sticky banner at top AND full hero card — means I couldn't miss it even if I was half-paying attention while commuting.
- **"Discount Automatically Applied at Checkout"** removes friction anxiety. I've been burned by forgotten promo codes before, so that line is genuinely reassuring.
- **MEN'S is first in the nav bar** — one tap to my category. No hunting required.
- **The lifestyle shot below the hero** shows laceless low-profile shoes, which is squarely my aesthetic. Even cropped, the vibe is right.

## What's weak

- **No CTA button in the hero.** The whole card is a sales pitch but there's nowhere to tap. I'm left staring at an offer with no action step — that's a gap.
- **Zero sustainability/material copy above the fold.** Wool feel and biodegradable claims are a big part of why I chose Allbirds in the first place. There's nothing here to reinforce that. It could be any generic shoe sale.
- **"BESTSELLE" is cut off in the nav.** On a mobile viewport this narrow, the tab bar truncates and looks sloppy.
- **No loyalty hook.** I'm a repeat buyer with three pairs. If there's a rewards program or member pricing, I have no idea from this screen.
- **The lifestyle image is a tight crop of legs.** I can't tell what shoe that is, whether it's new, whether it's in my size. It's vibe-only, no product utility.

## Recommendations

- **Add a button to the hero card** — "Shop Men's Sale" or "Shop the Sale" with a direct link into the men's filtered-by-sale view. The offer is strong; the drop-off is that there's no action to take.
- **Swap one line of hero subtext to a sustainability hook** — even "Made with natural materials. 30% off this weekend." costs nothing and speaks to why loyalists like me are here.
- **Fix the nav truncation** — "BESTSELLERS" deserves to actually show. Either reduce font size or abbreviate to "BEST" so it doesn't look like a render error.
- **Surface a returning-member line** — if Allbirds has a rewards or member program, a small badge or "Welcome back — your 30% applies" line would convert repeat buyers faster than a cold acquisition message.
