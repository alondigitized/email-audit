# Hydrated skin
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a solid, on-brand product-education send — a genuine break from the "SECRET SALE" countdown-timer machine Drunk Elephant has been running since mid-August. It leads with a "welcome to the club" 15%-off banner (neon yellow border, discount code visible), then pivots into an actual ingredient story for B-Hydra Intensive Hydration Serum: three callout circles for Pro-Vitamin B5, Sodium Hyaluronate, and Pineapple Extract, backed by a consumer-perception stat block (97% smoother, 93% more hydrated, 93% more radiant). It closes with a cross-sell ("plays well with others") and a $100 spend threshold for deluxe samples.
- The subject line "Hydrated skin" is the weak link — it's generic to the point of being forgettable, especially compared to this brand's usual punchier hooks ("Who's feeling merry?!", "Well damn, 20% off"). - Verdict: good creative execution, weak subject/preview, offer stacking that splits attention — a 6/10 email that could be a 7-8 with a sharper subject and one consolidated offer.

- Clear visual hierarchy: neon-yellow-bordered offer banner immediately draws the eye on open.
- Ingredient education section uses concrete, specific callouts (Pro-Vitamin B5, Sodium Hyaluronate, Pineapple Extract) rather than vague "clean beauty" language.
- Real, cited social proof (97%/93%/93% stats with sample size and study duration disclosed in fine print) builds credibility.
- Product bundling section ("B-Hydra + C-Firma + Protini") gives a concrete cross-sell reason to buy more than one item.
- No render bugs — images, text, and buttons are all clean and legible.

## What's weak

- Subject line "Hydrated skin" gives zero indication of the 15%-off welcome offer that's the actual hero of the email — massive missed opportunity.
- Two offers compete: the 15% club code up top and the $100-spend deluxe-sample GWP at the bottom have no connective tissue or single CTA hierarchy.
- "USE CODE: CRMD_EW-P2Z3-Z3L5-S2" is a clunky, hard-to-read alphanumeric string sitting right next to "SHOP NOW" — friction for anyone trying to type or screenshot it.
- The hero model/skin-focus shot appears only once, fairly deep in the email (after two full sections of copy) — the emotional "this could be your skin" payoff is delayed.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rewrite the subject to reference the offer and the product, since that's what's actually driving the open — e.g. reference the 15% welcome discount directly rather than the generic "Hydrated skin."
- 2. Pick one hero CTA. Either lead with the 15%-off welcome offer OR the ingredient story — right now the banner and the "PLEASE HYDRATE RESPONSIBLY" section both compete for top billing seconds apart.
- 3. Move the 97%/93%/93% stat block above the ingredient-circle diagram — social proof numbers are a stronger scroll-stopper than the ingredient science for a first-time reader.
- 4. Simplify the discount code display — larger type, fewer characters visually crammed next to the button, maybe "tap to copy" language.
- 5. Consider cutting the $100-spend GWP section entirely from this send or moving it to a separate email — it's a different audience trigger (existing high-spend customers) than the welcome-offer audience this email seems to target.
