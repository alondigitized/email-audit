# Who’s feeling merry?!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-06**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth "SECRET SALE! 20% off" send in as many weeks — the exact same green banner language as 2026-09-05's "Keep it between us" send — but it's bolted onto genuinely new creative underneath: a French-patisserie-styled holiday collection launch ("The Drunk Elephant Atelier") with a fashion-editorial conceit (Gala Glow, Couture Shows, Maison Moisture, Finishing Touches). That's the first real seasonal/collection news this brand history has shown, and the styling — houndstooth backgrounds, ribbon-wrapped boxes, serif italic headline — is a clear step up in production value from the flat product-grid sends.
- The problem is the email doesn't know what it's selling. Is this a 20% off sitewide sale, or a holiday collection launch? The hero banner and the holiday hub fight for the same pixel space, and the four kits below get generic descriptive copy ("a bespoke blend of moisture, brightness, and luminosity") with no prices, no bundle savings, no indication these kits are even part of the 20%-off promo. A shopper can't tell if buying "The Gala Glow" gets them the sale price.
- Net: strong new visual concept, muddled offer architecture. Worth a 6 — better than the fatigued "secret sale" sends, held back by unclear pricing and no product photography differentiation between the four kits (three of the four thumbnails are near-identical beige/pink box shots on the same teal background).

## What's working

- New seasonal creative (holiday atelier concept) breaks the five-week streak of repeating "secret sale" hero treatments.
- Clear visual hierarchy: green offer bar → yellow holiday hero → four-kit breakdown → repeated CTA — the eye has an obvious path.
- No render bugs: all text is legible, boxes/ribbons render cleanly, CTAs are all present and tappable.
- Legal/terms line is visible directly under the code, not buried in a footer wall (partial fine-print honesty).
- Consistent "SHOP NOW"/"SHOP HOLIDAY" CTA styling throughout reinforces brand voice.

## What's weak

- No prices or bundle savings shown anywhere for the four holiday kits — a $ or "value $120" would make the CTA meaningful.
- Unclear whether the 20% code applies to the holiday kits at all; footer fine print says it excludes "bundles," which these box sets visually look like — that's a bait-and-switch risk if kits are excluded.
- Three of four kit thumbnails use the same teal houndstooth background and similar box shapes, making them visually redundant on a quick scroll.
- Two competing offers (sitewide 20% vs. holiday collection) with no single dominant CTA — a reader has to pick a lane themselves.
- "SECRET SALE" framing is the same conspiratorial hook already used and critiqued in the 2026-09-05 send — no new angle five sends in.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one lead offer. If the holiday collection is genuinely new, lead with it and demote the 20% code to a secondary banner — don't run two competing heroes back to back.
- 2. Add a price or "starting at $X" under each kit name so the CTA has something concrete to act on.
- 3. Clarify in-line (not just footer fine print) whether the 20% code applies to these kits, since "bundles" are excluded per the terms.
- 4. Vary the background/styling per kit thumbnail so the four don't blur together in a scroll.
- 5. Retire "SECRET SALE" copy — it's the sixth use and was already flagged as fatigued; the holiday launch is a better hook.
