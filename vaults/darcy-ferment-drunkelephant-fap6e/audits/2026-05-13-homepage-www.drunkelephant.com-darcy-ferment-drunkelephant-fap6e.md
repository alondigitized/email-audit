- Drunk Elephant opens with the T.L.C. Sukari Babyfacial front and center — the one product I replace on autopilot every single month — and immediately signals a reformulation with "enhanced formula." That's the kind of thing that stops me mid-scroll and makes me read every word. ## What's working

- **T.L.C. Sukari Babyfacial as the hero product** — leading with the cult repurchase SKU is the right call for a returning shopper like me; I see it and immediately lean in
- **"Enhanced formula" subhead** — I read every reformulation note; this is exactly the hook that makes me want to tap through and see what changed (AHA percentages? pH? texture?)
- **Free shipping threshold in the top banner** — "$40+" is laughably easy to clear on a Babyfacial order; removes a friction point before I've even thought about it
- **Lifestyle image of woman applying product** — grounds the product in use without being overly aspirational or too young for me
- **"OG at-home 'facial'"** — the language "OG" lands for Gen X; it respects the product's history while signaling improvement

## What's weak

- **Headline is cropped mid-word** — "Please exfoliate resp" is a carousel slide that didn't fully load or render inside the viewport; I can't read the full call to action and that's sloppy
- **No CTA button above the fold** — I can see product and copy but there's no "Shop Now," "Learn More," or "See What Changed" button in view; the conversion path is invisible
- **No reformulation detail above fold** — "enhanced formula" teases but delivers nothing; as someone who compares old vs. new ingredient lists, I want at least one specificity to click into
- **Navigation is hamburger-gated** — the icon bar up top has a hamburger, search, and bag but no exposed category links; getting to "Mini Sets" or the full routine requires extra taps
- **No trust signals or reviews** — for a reformulated cult product, a "4.8 stars from 28K reviews" badge would do a lot of work here and it's nowhere to be seen

## Recommendations

- **Complete the headline in-viewport** — ensure the carousel slides are fully rendered before the screenshot fires (and before a real user sees it); "Please exfoliate resp" is an immediate credibility dent for a science-forward brand
- **Add a persistent "What changed" CTA button** — something like `See the new formula →` directly on the hero so reformulation-curious shoppers like me can go straight to the ingredient comparison without hunting
- **Surface one ingredient callout in the hero subhead** — even "now with 25% AHA blend, rebalanced pH" would stop me cold and earn the scroll; the current copy is warm but vague
- **Pin a "Mini Sets" or "Build Your Routine" shortcut in the nav bar** — expose it as a text link under the hero rather than burying it in the hamburger; my entire purchase pattern is built around the mini subscription set
