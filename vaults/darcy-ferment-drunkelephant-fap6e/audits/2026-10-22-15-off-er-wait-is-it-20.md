# 15% off! Er, wait. Is it 20%?
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth Subscribe & Save send in four days. It follows 10/19 "15% off, you say?", 10/20 "Happy National Health Education Week!" (also 15% off) and 10/21 "Do I hear 15% off?". The subject is a cute self-correction joke, and the body does resolve it: 15% off the first delivery, 20% off the second, 10% off after that. But the hero headline, "AND YOU'LL NEVER RUN OUT AGAIN," reads as the end of a sentence nobody wrote. The offer only appears in the paragraph below it.
- The product stack of Protini, C-Firma, B-Hydra and T.L.C. is clean and on-brand, and the 15/20/10 ladder is easy to scan. The weaknesses are a generic "SHOP NOW" button, a gift-guide module that competes with the single offer, and the deadline (Oct 27) and "new subscriptions only" terms sitting in small grey fine print. After three near-identical sends, a subscriber has no new reason to act.
- Verdict: a tidy but repetitive reminder. The subject earns the open on curiosity, and the body doesn't give the click a fresh reason.

## What's working

- The subject's "15%... wait, is it 20%?" gag fits the brand's playful voice and is distinctive in an inbox.
- The body copy spells out the full 15/20/10 ladder in bold, so the value is clear in one read.
- The pastel product stack against the grey backdrop is on-brand and shows the range at a glance.
- A bordered "SHOP NOW" button sits directly under the offer copy, so there is one obvious action.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The headline "AND YOU'LL NEVER RUN OUT AGAIN" has no setup line, and the offer is demoted to body text.
- It is the fourth send on the same Subscribe & Save event this week, which feels like fatigue, not urgency.
- "SHOP NOW" is generic, and the products are not individually linked or named.
- The end date (Oct 27, 2:59 am EST) and the new-subscriptions-only restriction are buried in small grey text.
- The "Gift of Gorgeous Skin" module splits attention, and its "GIFT GUIDE ME" text link is weak.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in the hero headline, for example "15% off your first delivery. 20% off your second." Then add a deadline line above the button: "Ends Oct 27."
- 2. Change the CTA to something specific, like "Start my subscription" or "Build my routine." Give each product in the stack its own link and a price.
- 3. Move the end date and the new-subscribers-only condition out of the fine print and next to the CTA.
- 4. Cut the gift-guide module from reminder sends, or move it to a separate campaign. Space out the reminders so the next one adds something new, such as a bestseller bundle or a reorder-savings calculator.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Marigold/Selligent-style send (`cheers.drunkelephant.com`) built on an XHTML 1.0 Transitional, table-based layout. QA found no hard failures, only 3 warnings: missing alt text on two tracking images, three `http://` social icons, and absent compliance and authentication headers (probably a relay artifact). The HTML source was truncated, so footer, body links and UTM parameters could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **11 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so I can't confirm they resolve or that destinations match the offer.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** `https://mi.drunkelephant.com/p/up/80acaf1c74f9e002/o.gif?mi_u=98161986&mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_subweekresend_p`. Its campaign ID (`subweekresend`) indicates this is a resend of an earlier send.
- **Second tracking image:** `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/?e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app&c=us_de_broad…`. It is a second third-party tracker alongside the `mi.` pixel.
- **Recipient email in a URL:** the `e=` parameter puts the recipient's plaintext email address in the query string. It will show up in proxy, CDN and server logs.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `o.gif` and the `everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/` image have no `alt` attribute. Both look like tracking pixels, so the impact is low, but they should use `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Insecure image sources:** the Facebook, Instagram and TikTok icons load over `http://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/lib/fe3211717064057c751c78/m/1/…png`:
  - `cc02d07d-c9bb-4057-b5d7-62f3bec2c521.png` (Facebook)
  - `9b452915-64bf-4b29-b08b-c4a42802457a.png` (Instagram)
  - `29f975e1-6f94-429c-ae2b-8ee6f90786cb.png` (TikTok)

  These can be blocked or flagged as mixed content, and have no HTTPS fallback.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` stops pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns=…>` has no `lang`, so screen readers can't pick a language. `<title>Drunk Elephant</title>` is generic.
- **Fragile web fonts:**
  - Fonts load through render-blocking `<link>` tags and `@import url(https://db.onlinewebfonts.com/…)` inside `<style>`. `@import` is unsupported or stripped in most email clients.
  - `BrownRegular` is pulled from `drunkelephant.com/on/demandware.static/…/v1719565389232/…`, a versioned asset path that can break when the site redeploys.
  - A third-party font host adds an extra request and a privacy leak, and the fallback behavior depends on the font stacks in the truncated part of the source.
- **Questionable CSS:**
  - `<!-- … -->` HTML comments sit inside the `@media` block (`<!-- *[class=contenttable] … -->`), which can break parsing in some clients.
  - `.vouchertext4` and `.fsname` set `max-width:12–15px` and `max-height` with `!important`, which risks clipping text on mobile.
