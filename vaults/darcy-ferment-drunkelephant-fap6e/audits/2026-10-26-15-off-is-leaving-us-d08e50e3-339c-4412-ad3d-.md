# 15% off is leaving us...
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the seventh Subscribe & Save send since 10/19, and the last-call version of it. The countdown banner reads 00 days, 10 hours, 46 minutes, which is credible urgency. The subject, "15% off is leaving us...", is a playful break-up line, and it fits the brand's run of puns ("15% off, you say?", "Do I hear 15% off?"). The offer is clear: 15% off the first delivery, 20% off the second and 10% off after that. The fine print confirms the end time of Oct 27 at 2:59 am EST.
- The hero copy is weaker than the subject. "AND YOU'LL NEVER RUN OUT AGAIN" is a sentence fragment that only makes sense if you remember the earlier sends. The product shot is attractive, but it is a pastel lineup with no prices, names or reviews. The single "SHOP NOW" button is small and sits above the images, so the whole product block is decoration. The gift-guide module is a second, unrelated offer. It splits attention at the point where the email should be pushing a single deadline.
- Overall this is a clean, competent last-call email, and about as good as the series gets. It also carries the series' fatigue. A subscriber who has seen six of these has little new reason to open it. The urgency is real, but the creative is nearly identical to 10/25's "Up to 20% off".

## What's working

- The countdown banner is high-contrast and shows hours, minutes and seconds, so the deadline is obvious.
- The 15/20/10% ladder is spelled out in bold within the hero copy, so the offer is easy to scan.
- The subject's "leaving us" framing is a fresh angle on a long campaign. It is honest about being a last call.
- The clean white-and-grey layout keeps the products and the dark banner as the focal points.
- The fine print is visible and plain. It states free shipping, the end time, new-subscription-only and no stacking.

## What's weak

- The hero headline "AND YOU'LL NEVER RUN OUT AGAIN" is a fragment with no setup. It sells a benefit, not the deadline or the discount.
- "SHOP NOW" is a small, outlined, low-contrast button. It is the weakest element on the page, and it is the only CTA for the offer.
- The product image is not clickable-looking or labelled. There are no names, prices, ratings or "best seller" cues.
- The gift-guide module dilutes the urgency and sends the reader to a non-discounted destination.
- The new-subscription-only restriction appears only in the fine print below the fold. A returning customer finds out late.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rewrite the hero headline to lead with the deadline and the number: "Last call: 15% off, then 20%, then 10% for good." Put the 15/20/10 ladder in a visual strip, not a paragraph.
- 2. Make the CTA a solid, filled, larger button with a specific verb and noun, such as "Start my subscription". Repeat it under the product image.
- 3. Label the hero products with names, prices and star ratings, and link each one to its own page.
- 4. Remove the gift-guide module from a last-call send, or move it below the fine print.
- 5. Put "New subscriptions only" in a short line directly under the CTA.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 15% off ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your shelves, restocked for 15% less`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Subscribe by 2:59 am EST for 15% off, then 20% off your second box.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping, cancel anytime, and the offer ends in hours.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a table-based XHTML template with several code-level defects: invalid `@import` placement, non-HTTPS social icons, no `lang` attribute, and a recipient email address embedded in a tracking-pixel URL. The QA run scored a 57% pass rate (0 errors, 3 warnings). The HTML source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and CTA markup could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Recipient email in a URL:** the tracking pixel `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/?e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app&c=us_de_broad…` puts the recipient's address in the query string in plaintext. It can end up in proxy, CDN and third-party logs.
- **Unverified click links:** 12 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped, so none of the CTA destinations were probed. Whether the redirects resolve, or whether the landing pages return 200, is unconfirmed.
- **Second pixel with an identifier:** `https://mi.drunkelephant.com/p/up/80acaf1c74f9e002/o.gif?mi_u=98161986&mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_subweekends_pro…` carries a user ID (`mi_u`). It is a normal open pixel, but it means two separate open-tracking vendors are in use.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid `@import`:** the `<style>` block has `body {…}` first and the three `@import url(https://db.onlinewebfonts.com/…)` rules after it. `@import` must come before all other rules, so these are ignored by compliant parsers. The `<link rel="stylesheet">` tags in `<head>` are stripped by Gmail and other clients, and the `@font-face` fallback only works in clients that allow remote fonts.
- **Non-HTTPS images:** Facebook, Instagram and TikTok icons use `http://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/lib/fe3211717064057c751c78/m/1/{cc02d07d-…, 9b452915-…, 29f975e1-…}.png`. They may be blocked or flagged as mixed content.
- **Missing alt text:** `o.gif` and the `everestengagement.com` pixel have no `alt`. They are 1×1 tracking pixels, so they should carry `alt=""` explicitly, which marks them decorative for screen readers. The impact is low.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom on mobile, which is an accessibility problem (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **No document language:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" …>` has no `lang`/`xml:lang` attribute.
- **Possibly active "commented" CSS:** inside the `@media (max-width: 600px)` block, `<!-- *[class=contenttable] { width:414px !important;} -->` and the `mobile` rule use HTML comment tokens, not CSS comments. Parsers treat `<!--` and `-->` as ignorable tokens, so the enclosed rules may still apply and force 414px widths. This is worth testing because the source was truncated.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Drunk Elephant</title>` does not match the subject. This is a minor issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The recipient value in the tracking URL is populated, and no unresolved `{{ }}`, `%%`, or `[FIRST_NAME]` tokens appear in the visible source. Body copy was truncated, so coverage is partial.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** both are absent from the captured headers. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. If the headers are really missing, it breaks Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender one-click unsubscribe requirements.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `info@cheers.drunkelephant.com` are unknown. This is a limitation of the relay, not a confirmed failure.
- **Footer:** the unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be verified because the source was truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Campaign identifiers:** the `mi_ecmp` and `c=` values (`us_de_broadcast_promotion_subweekends_pro…`) show campaign tracking through the vendors' own parameters.
- **Not verifiable:** the 12 click links were not probed, so I can't confirm UTM propagation, offer alignment ("15% off") or landing-page status.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Stop putting the recipient email in the `everestengagement.com` pixel URL. Use an opaque ID instead.
2. Serve the three social icons over `https://`.
3. Move the `@import` rules to the top of the `<style>` block, or remove them, and rely on the `@font-face` rules with web-safe fallbacks.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
5. Add `lang="en"` and `xml:lang="en"` to `<html>`, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
6. Replace the `<!-- … -->` wrappers around the `contenttable` and `mobile` rules with `/* … */`, or delete them, then re-test mobile layout.
7. Confirm that `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` are set at the ESP. Capture the raw headers (not via the relay) to verify SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
8. Re-run the audit against the full HTML. Then probe the redirect chains for the 12 skipped links and check UTM and landing-page parity.
