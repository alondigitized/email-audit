# Kamo plus one
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a solid product-education email with a buried offer. The hero is strong: "MIX IT. BUILD IT. MAKE IT YOUR OWN." sits over a clean stack of Kamo bottles, with a green "SHOP KAMO" button above the fold. The three use-case blocks (full coverage, tinted moisturizer, undereye concealer) are easy to scan and use diverse models. Together they answer the question "what do I do with this?"
- The real news is the gift-with-purchase, and the email hides it. "Kamo plus one" hints at an offer, but the pick-your-own-pouch deal sits at the bottom behind a small "SHOP NOW" text link. The terms are a grey paragraph of fine print that runs from 10/10 to 10/17. The hero never mentions it. A reader who skims the top half sees a how-to-use email and has no reason to act this week.
- This follows the 10/1 "You might be a foundation person" send, so it is another Kamo education beat. It is better than that one on usability. It is weaker than the 10/4 "A flood of hydration" send, which put a price anchor in the hero. Verdict: a good lesson with the incentive in the wrong place.

## What's working

- The hero headline is short and active, and the product shot is polished.
- Both "SHOP KAMO" buttons are dark green and high-contrast, so the primary action is obvious.
- The three alternating image/text blocks make Kamo's versatility easy to take in.
- The model photography is diverse, and the texture swatch shows the product well.
- Gift-with-purchase dates (10/10 to 10/17) give the offer a real deadline.

## What's weak

- The offer is buried. "PICK YOUR OWN POUCH DAY!" is in the bottom third, and the terms are tiny grey text.
- The offer's requirement is complicated: Kamo plus a qualifying moisturizer, with four named options.
- The bottom CTA, "SHOP NOW", is a small underlined text link. It is weaker than the buttons above it.
- A black bar sits under the hero image and looks like a rendering artifact.
- Nothing in the body shows a shade range, a price, or any social proof.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the gift offer into the hero as a banner, for example "Free pouch + B-Hydra sample with Kamo + a moisturizer." Lead with the incentive and keep the education below it.
- 2. Replace the grey fine print with a short visible line such as "Through 10/17" next to the offer. Keep the legal copy in the footer.
- 3. Turn "SHOP NOW" into a full green button, "Shop the Kamo set", and link it to a page with the bundle preselected.
- 4. Remove the black strip under the hero image.
- 5. Add one review or shade-count proof point (for example "40 shades") near the first CTA.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout sent through a Marketing Cloud-style ESP (`image.cheers.drunkelephant.com`) with Movable Ink and Everest tracking. The confirmed problems are the recipient email address in a tracking URL, three non-HTTPS images, and missing `lang` and alt attributes. The QA run had a 57% pass rate with 0 errors and 3 warnings. The truncated HTML and the skipped link probes limit what could be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Email address in a URL:** the Everest tracking URL carries the recipient address in plain text: `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/?e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app&c=us_de_broad…`. The address will appear in third-party server logs, referrers and proxy caches.
- **Unverified links:** 12 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Their destinations and status codes are not confirmed.
- **Mixed content:** three footer icons load over `http://`:
  - `http://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/lib/fe3211717064057c751c78/m/1/cc02d07d-c9bb-4057-b5d7-62f3bec2c521.png` (Facebook)
  - `…/9b452915-64bf-4b29-b08b-c4a42802457a.png` (Instagram)
  - `…/29f975e1-6f94-429c-ae2b-8ee6f90786cb.png` (TikTok)
  
  Clients that don't proxy images may block them or show a security warning. The same host is likely to serve HTTPS, so this is probably a template-level fix.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on tracking images:**
  - `https://mi.drunkelephant.com/p/up/80acaf1c74f9e002/o.gif?mi_u=98161986&mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_kamoprotinirese`
  - the Everest image at `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/`
  
  These look like tracking pixels. If so, `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` would keep screen readers from announcing the URL.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" …>` has no `lang`, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **External font dependencies:** fonts load from `db.onlinewebfonts.com` through `<link>` tags and `@import`. Gmail and Outlook strip or ignore both, and the font stack depends on a third-party host. A web-safe fallback after `BrownRegular`, `BrownBold` and the other custom families is needed. The visible excerpt doesn't show whether one exists.
- **Malformed CSS:** HTML comment markers (`<!-- *[class=contenttable] {…} -->`) sit inside the `@media (max-width: 600px)` block. CSS parsers drop those rules. That is harmless here because they were commented out on purpose, but it is brittle.

No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source. The recipient address is populated in the Everest URL, so the issue there is exposure rather than a failed merge.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** neither header was found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so verify against the raw original message.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. This is expected through the relay and isn't a confirmed defect.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address:** the HTML is truncated, so neither could be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No landing-page verification:** link probes were skipped and the visible excerpt shows no destination URLs, so landing-page alignment can't be confirmed.
- **Campaign IDs are in place:** `mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_kamoprotinirese` and `c=us_de_broad…` are tied to the campaign. Neither shows `utm_*` parameters in the captured excerpt. Check whether the ESP appends them at redirect.

2. Change the three social icon URLs from `http://` to `https://`.
3. Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` to both tracking images.

6. Check the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results to settle the compliance warnings.
