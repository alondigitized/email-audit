# 😍 Starts now! Drunk 30% off Black Friday
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the Black Friday payoff send, and the hero is clear: "It's on! BLACK FRIDAY 30% OFF SITEWIDE" over the pink gift box and ornaments. The offer is the same 30% the brand has run since the 11/25 "Biggest sale of the year: 30% off." The email has more going on than the offer needs. It carries a free-shipping banner, a free full-size B-Goldi gift at $110+, a Trunk 7.1 bundle at 20% off with code DRUNK20, an "UP TO 40% OFF" strip, and a four-kit grid. That is five offers, and each is a separate mechanic.
- The creative is strong, with bold color blocks, clean product photography, and social proof on the kit grid ("674 PEOPLE ARE CHECKING THIS OUT"). Underneath, though, it is the Cyber Week email again with a "Black Friday" label. A subscriber who got "Up to 40% off, you say?" on 11/26 will see the same pink ornaments and the same "last chance" strip. The brand has now sent this offer in many near-identical emails, so the cadence is tired.
- Judgment: well executed, but crowded and repetitive. The hero is good, but the stacked offers and the heavy fine print weaken it.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer plainly: "BLACK FRIDAY 30% OFF SITEWIDE" in large type, with "It's on!" adding energy.
- The neon green free-shipping banner at the top is visible immediately and removes a purchase barrier.
- The B-Goldi "free full-size, $39 value" gift with a $110+ threshold is a concrete incentive that also nudges order size.
- The kit grid shows strikethrough prices, star ratings, and "people are checking this out" counts, which adds proof and urgency.
- The color blocking (pink, aqua, green) matches the brand and separates the sections clearly.

## What's weak

- Five offers compete: 30% sitewide, 20% with DRUNK20, up to 40%, free shipping, and a free gift. The reader has to work out which discount applies to what.
- The Trunk 7.1 block uses a different code and a different percentage, and its "$340 / $733 VALUE" badge sits over a large photo with no button. The only action in that section is the text line above it.
- The CTAs are small and weak. "SHOP NOW" appears in a thin outlined box, and the text links ("SHOP NOW" under the Goldi and 40% blocks) are tiny.
- The four-line fine print at the bottom has asterisk tiers (*, **, ***, ****) that the body copy never points to. Combined with "cannot be combined," this undercuts how honest the offer feels.
- The "UP TO 40% OFF, Last Chance!" strip repeats the claim that earlier subjects like "Up to 40% off." oversold.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut to one hero offer plus one incentive. Keep 30% sitewide and the free B-Goldi gift. Move Trunk 7.1 and the 40% strip to a single secondary row, or drop them.
- 2. Replace the thin "SHOP NOW" button with a solid, filled button, such as "Shop 30% off sitewide," and repeat it once below the hero.
- 3. Make Trunk 7.1 a clickable block with a real button, and say clearly that DRUNK20 is a separate offer.
- 4. Add a real end time near the top, such as "Ends Sunday 11:59 p.m. ET," in place of the generic "Last Chance!"
- 5. Shorten the fine print and attach the asterisks to the claims they cover.
- 6. Change the creative between sends so the 11/26 and 11/27 emails don't look identical.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Black Friday is on: 30% off + a free full-size gift`
- **Subject Alt B:** `30% off everything. Your free B-Goldi is inside.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free full-size B-Goldi on orders $110+. Ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free ground shipping, kits from $58, and a gift with $110+.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt:** the two tracking images above have no `alt` attribute. They should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip them.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` stops pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid `@import`:** the `@import url(https://db.onlinewebfonts.com/...)` rules come after the `body{...}` rule in the same `<style>` block. `@import` must come first, so browsers ignore them. The `<link>` tags in `<head>` do the same job and are the only font loading that works.
- **Font support:** the fonts come from third-party hosts (`db.onlinewebfonts.com`, `www.drunkelephant.com/on/demandware.static/...`) via `@font-face`. Gmail and Outlook drop these, so the rendering depends on a fallback stack. I can't tell whether one exists in the truncated source.
- **CSS comments:** HTML comments (`<!-- *[class=contenttable] ... -->`) sit inside the `@media` block. They are invalid there and are dropped by the CSS parser. This is harmless here but is fragile.
- **Fixed mobile sizes:** `.vouchertext4` and `.fsname` set `max-width:15px` and `max-height:12px` under 418px and 600px. This risks clipping text, but I can't confirm that from the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- **Merge tokens:** no unresolved tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRSTNAME]` appear in the visible source.
