# Homepage snapshot · www.drunkelephant.com · 2026-07-05
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-05**
## Executive summary

- Drunk Elephant's homepage is doing what I'd expect — clean, clinical product photography, a science-adjacent headline, and a sale ticker doing the heavy lifting on urgency. The B-Hydra hero is on-brand for a formula nerd like me, but the mobile layout is clipping the headline and CTA at the fold, which means the brand is literally cutting off its own pitch. Right now this feels like a brand speaking my language but tripping over its own shoelaces.

## What's working

- **The B-Hydra hero product selection** — this is not a random launch push; it's a core replenishment SKU that people like me actually buy monthly. Featuring it in a sale context is smart.
- **"35% off select kits" ticker** — I subscribe to bigger mini sets precisely because they're economical. A kit discount above the fold speaks directly to my purchase behavior.
- **"Free ground shipping on all orders"** — no threshold to calculate. That is genuinely frictionless and I can see it before I tap anything.
- **Product photography is clean and clinical** — the stacked mini-cube format of the smaller products reads as premium and science-lab, not spa-fluffy. That visual language fits the brand I know.
- **"You dew you: Your daily dose of vitamin B" sub-copy** — the ingredient-forward descriptor ("vitamin B," "hydrate and illuminate") gives me functional information at a glance, which is how I shop.

## What's weak

- **Hero headline is cut off at the fold** — "drate responsibly" is not a headline; it is a layout failure. On the viewport I'm looking at, the top of "Hydrate" is clipped. This is the brand's primary message and it is literally unreadable.
- **CTA is clipped to "Pl"** — I cannot tell if this says "Shop," "Learn," or anything else. A truncated CTA is not a CTA.
- **No ingredient or formula context in the hero** — for a brand built on the Suspicious Six and clean formulation, the hero copy does not mention what makes B-Hydra worth 35% off. "Hydrate responsibly" is catchy but not scientific.
- **Navigation is hamburger-only** — as a high-frequency shopper who knows exactly what I want (T.L.C. Sukari Babyfacial, B-Hydra, Protini), I want to tap directly to a category or product. One tap to hamburger, then hunt through a menu, is unnecessary friction.
- **No reviews or social proof above the fold** — star ratings on the featured serum or a "10,000+ reviews" badge would reinforce that this isn't a gimmick launch.
- **Urgency copy is playful, not precise** — "Tick, tock....sale-ing away. So stop reading and get on it!" is cute but gives me no deadline. Ends tonight? This weekend? I need the date to decide if this is a "buy now" or a "check back tomorrow."

## Recommendations

- **Fix the hero text clipping on mobile** — "Hydrate responsibly" should be fully visible above the fold. This is a one-line CSS fix (reduce hero image height or adjust text container) that should ship today, not next sprint.
- **Expose the CTA button text fully** — if the button says "Shop B-Hydra" or "Shop the Kit," that copy does conversion work. A clipped "Pl" does nothing. Resize or reposition the button within the hero container.
- **Add a hard deadline to the sale ticker** — swap "sale-ing away" for "Ends July 7 at midnight" or equivalent. Specific deadlines convert better than implied urgency, and I will buy faster if I know the clock.
- **Surface a "My Routine" or loyalty hook** — even a single line like "Replenishing? Members save an extra 10%" would signal that Drunk Elephant knows I'm a repeat buyer, not a first-time browser.
