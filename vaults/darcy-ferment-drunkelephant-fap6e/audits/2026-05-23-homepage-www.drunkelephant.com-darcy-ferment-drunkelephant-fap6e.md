# Homepage snapshot · www.drunkelephant.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I respect it — Drunk Elephant is not wasting my time. The Friends & Family event is front and center, 25% off sitewide, and they're stacking GWPs on top of it. This is the kind of sale I have a literal calendar alert for. That said, this homepage is doing the minimum for someone who is already a devotee; there's zero ingredient-level copy, no Suspicious 6 callout, and the navigation is basically invisible.

## What's working

- **"25% OFF SITEWIDE" headline** — massive, impossible to miss, and the specific % matters to someone who is calculating whether to stack Babyfacial + B-Hydra in one order
- **Product hero imagery** — the rainbow DE product pile is immediately brand-recognizable; I know exactly what I'm looking at and which SKUs I want
- **GWP stacking copy** ("gifting a Hydra and the Bright kit with orders $100+ plus a free full-size F-Balm mask with orders $175+") — this is smart reward laddering and I'm already doing the AOV math in my head
- **Free shipping banner** at the very top is clean and direct — "$40+ at checkout" is an easy threshold to hit

## What's weak

- **No CTA button visible above the fold** — there's a gorgeous offer and then... products. I shouldn't have to figure out how to shop the sale
- **"The whole shebang!" copy register** — that's cute-casual, and I'm a formulation nerd. I want to know which acids and peptides are in this sale, not feel like I'm at a yard sale
- **No ingredient or science hook** — Drunk Elephant built its brand on the Suspicious 6. Above the fold there is zero signal for *why* these products are worth buying beyond the discount
- **Navigation is just a hamburger menu** — no quick-tap to Acids, Moisturizers, or SPF; I have to hunt
- **No urgency signal** — "Friends & Family" implies limited time but there's no end date, no countdown, nothing that tells me this expires before my next session

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop the Sale" CTA button** directly in the hero — make it tappable, bold, and specific ("Shop Acids," "Shop Routine Bundles," or at minimum "Shop All 25% Off")
- **Surface an end date** on the promotional banner or below the hero — even "ends Sunday" would close my "I'll come back later" loop immediately
- **Drop in one ingredient-forward sub-headline** below "25% OFF SITEWIDE" — something like "Your Babyfacial, B-Hydra, and full routine. All of it." earns trust from science loyalists without alienating newcomers
- **Add a sticky nav strip** with 3-4 category tiles (Acids, SPF, Eye, Sets) immediately below the hero so I can route to my category in one tap instead of opening the hamburger
