# Homepage snapshot · www.drunkelephant.com · 2026-05-31
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-31**
## Executive summary

- Drunk Elephant is leading with its B-Hydra™ Intensive Hydration Serum and some vitamin B positioning — very on-brand for their signature formulation story. The mobile layout is frustrating though: the hero headline is mid-slide-transition and I'm reading "drate responsibly" and "Pl" like a broken autocomplete. It looks like a brand that knows exactly what it's selling but hasn't QA'd its carousel on a real phone.

## What's working

- **Free shipping banner** — "$40+ at checkout" is immediately legible and I'll hit that threshold with one B-Hydra refill. No asterisks, no "select items" qualifier visible.
- **B-Hydra™ product photography** — the stacked blue gift-set packaging is clean and distinctive, immediately identifiable to anyone who subscribes to their Mini sets.
- **"You dew you" body copy** — vitamin B callout, "hydrate and illuminate thirsty skin" — this is the ingredient science framing I actually read. Not "glow up," not "unleash radiance," it names the mechanism.
- **Model placement in secondary section** — the close-up of a woman applying product near the eye area reads mature-skincare, not freshman dorm routine.

## What's weak

- **Carousel mid-transition on mobile** — "drate responsibly" and "Pl" are cut-off remnants of an auto-playing slide. On a phone this is a first impression wreck. I can't read the headline, I can't act on it.
- **Zero CTA button above the fold** — I see product imagery, I see copy fragments, I see no tappable "Shop B-Hydra" or "Shop the Set" button. Drunk Elephant is counting on me to figure it out.
- **Smiley-face icon in nav is cryptic** — I assume that's the rewards/loyalty entry but it's completely unlabeled. As a subscriber-level buyer I want to know my points balance the second I land.
- **No ingredient callouts in hero** — "hydrate responsibly" is the copy but it doesn't say what's in it. I'm the person who reads every reformulation note. Give me a B5 badge, a peptide callout, anything.

## Recommendations

- **Pause the carousel or ship a static hero for mobile** — the mid-transition crop is a conversion killer. Either kill the carousel on mobile or add a minimum dwell time that prevents the screenshot state I'm seeing.
- **Add a visible "Shop B-Hydra" button on the hero** — the product photography is doing all the heavy lifting and then asking me to navigate blind. One button with product copy converts better than a beautiful dead image.
- **Label the loyalty icon** — "Your Points: —" or "DE Rewards" next to the smiley. I'd tap immediately if I knew I was close to a reward tier.
- **Drop one ingredient badge on the hero image** — a small "Vitamin B5 + Panthenol" chip overlay would speak directly to buyers like me who are in this ecosystem because of the formulation philosophy, not the packaging color.
