# Singles Day, right this way
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-09**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Singles Day send in a row with the same offer. It follows 11/5 "Singles Day steals" and 11/7 "All the singles!", and all three offer a four-piece sample set at $100 and a puffer tote at $150. The creative is clean and the offer is easy to read. The "HAPPY SINGLES DAY!" hero, the "spend $100" line and the product pedestal shot make the deal clear in a few seconds. It's also well timed, since 11.11 is two days out.
- The problem is that it adds nothing new. A subscriber who ignored the first two will ignore this one, and "Singles Day, right this way" gives no reason to open. The Atelier block at the bottom is the only new element, and it's buried below a large tote image. The fine print also shows the promo window ending 11/12 at 2:59 a.m. ET. That deadline is a real urgency hook, and the email never uses it in the body.
- Verdict: competent and on brand, but redundant. It's a 6, held back by cadence fatigue and the missing deadline.

## What's working

- The hero headline and "Just spend **$100** and it's yours!" state the offer plainly, with the dollar threshold in bold.
- The pedestal product shot shows the gift items (Amino Rain and D-Bronzi packettes, C-Firma, Protini), so the reward is concrete.
- The two-tier structure ($100 sample set, $150 puffer tote) gives a reason to spend more.
- The lavender puffer tote image is bold and visually distinct from the product shots.
- The fine print is complete and readable, with a dated window and exclusions, so the offer feels honest.

## What's weak

- It repeats the 11/5 and 11/7 sends almost exactly, which is the 3rd near-identical promo in 5 days.
- The deadline (ends 11/12, 2:59 a.m. ET) appears only in the fine print, so the email has no visible urgency.
- The tote image takes up about a third of the email, and its "SHOP NOW" button floats awkwardly above the image.
- The Atelier module is the only new content. It sits below the fold, its "SHOP NOW" is a small text link, and the copy is vague ("most glowing, radiant skin you've ever had").
- Every CTA says "SHOP NOW", with no verb plus noun and nothing specific to a product or category.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero: "Ends 11/11 — free gift with $100." The offer is the same, but it now reads as a final call rather than a repeat.
- 2. Move the Atelier block up and make it the new-news hero. It is the only fresh element, and it gives repeat openers something to click.
- 3. Shrink the tote image and fold it into a single "Spend $150, get the tote" strip, with the CTA below it.
- 4. Replace the generic buttons with specific copy such as "Claim my free gift" and "Shop the Atelier".
- 5. Skip a third near-identical send. Hold it until the last day and make it a short "final hours" reminder.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free sample set ends 11.11`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: your Singles Day gift`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $100, get 4 minis. Spend $150, get the puffer tote. Ends 11/12.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: peek inside our first-ever holiday Atelier.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML source and QA data show a few confirmed code-level issues: recipient email exposed in a tracking URL, non-HTTPS images, missing alt text, and invalid font `@import` placement. Header-level compliance and authentication could not be verified, and the HTML was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Recipient email in a URL query string:** `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/?e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app&c=us_de_broad…`. The plaintext address can leak via logs, referrers and forwarded mail.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://mi.drunkelephant.com/p/up/80acaf1c74f9e002/o.gif?mi_u=98161986&mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_singlesdayresen…` carries a per-user ID (`mi_u`). This is expected for an open pixel, but it has no alt text (see section 3).
- **Unprobed links:** 12 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the QA probe, so their destinations and redirect health are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):**
  - `o.gif`: the tracking pixel should use `alt=""`.
  - The image inside the `everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/` link has no alt. Because it is linked, screen readers may announce the raw URL.
- **Non-HTTPS images:** the Facebook, Instagram and TikTok icons load from `http://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/lib/fe3211717064057c751c78/m/1/…png` (`cc02d07d-…`, `9b452915-…`, `29f975e1-…`). Clients may block them or show mixed-content warnings. The host already serves other assets over HTTPS, so switching these should be straightforward.
- **Invalid `@import` placement:** the three `@import url(https://db.onlinewebfonts.com/…)` rules come after the `body{…}` rule inside `<style>`. Per CSS, `@import` must precede all other rules, so they are ignored. The same fonts are also loaded through `<link>` tags, which most email clients strip.
- **Third-party font dependency:** `Brown Bold`, `Light` and `Thin` are loaded from `db.onlinewebfonts.com`, a third-party host with no SLA. `BrownRegular` is loaded from `www.drunkelephant.com/on/demandware.static/…/v1719565389232/…`, a versioned path that could break on a site deploy. Fallback stacks should be verified.
- **HTML comments inside a media query:** `<!-- *[class=contenttable] … -->` sits inside the `@media (max-width: 600px)` block. Some parsers will treat it as invalid CSS.
- **No `lang` attribute:** the `<html>` tag has only `xmlns` attributes, so there is no `lang`.
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `maximum-scale=1` prevents pinch-zoom on supporting clients, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source. The recipient email is correctly substituted in the `e=` parameter, though it should not be exposed there (see section 2).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both are not found. The QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed** rather than a verified sender defect. If the headers are truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. The From domain `cheers.drunkelephant.com` is a subdomain of `drunkelephant.com`, which could be checked directly via DNS.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** not verifiable, because the HTML is truncated before the footer.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Campaign identifiers are present: `mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_singlesdayresen…` and `c=us_de_broad…`. Both are truncated in the data provided.
- No standard `utm_*` parameters are visible in the available source, and the destination URLs were not probed (12 tracking links skipped). Landing page alignment cannot be confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the plaintext email from the `everestengagement.com` URL, for example by using an opaque token.
2. Change the three social icon URLs from `http://` to `https://`.
3. Add `alt=""` to `o.gif`, and meaningful alt text to the linked image at `…/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/`.
4. Move the `@import` rules to the top of the `<style>` block or remove them. Self-host the fonts and define web-safe fallbacks.
5. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, remove `maximum-scale=1`, and remove the HTML comments from the CSS.
6. Check List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results against the raw message from the original sender, since the relay may have dropped them.
7. Resolve the 12 skipped tracking links to confirm their final destinations and UTM parameters.
