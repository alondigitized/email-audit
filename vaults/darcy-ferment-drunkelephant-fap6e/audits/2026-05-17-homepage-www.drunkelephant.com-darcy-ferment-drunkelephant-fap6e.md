# Homepage snapshot · www.drunkelephant.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- This is Drunk Elephant in full-on reformulation-announcement mode, and as someone who replaces T.L.C. Sukari Babyfacial every single month, I felt my pulse tick up the second that pink bottle loaded. The brand is clearly leading with the Babyfacial reformulation launch — "An icon reimagined" — which is exactly the kind of copy that makes me simultaneously excited and nervous. Clean, premium, minimal. Very DE.

## What's working

- **The T.L.C. Babyfacial hero placement**: Leading with my exact monthly repurchase item is not an accident — this is targeted. The pink bottle photography is clean and instantly recognizable.
- **"An icon reimagined: The OG at-home 'facial,' now with an enhanced formula."**: This copy is doing real work. It honors the product history while signaling change — that framing respects both loyalists and skeptics, which is exactly who DE's core customer is.
- **"Exfoliate responsibly" headline**: Hits the science-nerd register perfectly. Not cutesy, not clinical. Exactly the tone that made me trust this brand.
- **Free shipping banner**: $40 threshold is visible, unambiguous, and reachable in one click for any existing customer.
- **Lifestyle shot**: The model applying what appears to be a peel/mask to her cheek is showing product use — I appreciate seeing the action, not just the bottle.

## What's weak

- **No CTA button visible in this viewport**: I can see carousel arrows but no "Shop Now," "Shop T.L.C.," or "Learn More" button. That's a miss — a reformulation announcement demands an immediate pathway to ingredient details or the product page.
- **"Enhanced formula" with no ingredient signal**: This is the thing that will make science nerds like me anxious, not enthusiastic. What changed? AHA/BHA ratios? New acids? Skin-identical add-ins? I need one line of that in the hero copy or this is just marketing noise.
- **No loyalty hook visible**: DE has a rewards program and I have points. Nothing here acknowledges I'm a repeat customer.
- **No reviews or ratings**: On a reformulated product especially, social proof is critical. Zero stars/review count visible.
- **"xfoliate responsibly" appears truncated**: The "E" seems to be cut off or in a different visual treatment — on a phone viewport this looks slightly sloppy for a premium brand.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "What's new in the formula" micro-callout in the hero**: Even one line — "Now with X% glycolic acid + [new ingredient]" — would stop reformulation-skeptics from bouncing to Reddit to find out what changed before they buy.
- **Surface a CTA button in the hero viewport**: "Shop the New Babyfacial" or "See What Changed" — either works. Carousel arrows do not drive purchase intent.
- **Add a returning-customer module or loyalty signal**: "Your points: [X]" or even a generic "Rewards members earn on every order" strip just below the banner would recapture repeat buyers.
- **Add review count to the hero or just below it**: "4.6 stars · 28,000+ reviews" next to the product — that number exists and it does heavy lifting for a reformulated icon.
