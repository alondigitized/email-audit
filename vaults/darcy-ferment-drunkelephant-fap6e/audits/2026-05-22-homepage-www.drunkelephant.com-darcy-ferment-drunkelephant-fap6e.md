# Homepage snapshot · www.drunkelephant.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- This is Drunk Elephant in full Friends & Family mode — 25% off sitewide, tiered gifts, that unmistakably colorful product cluster front and center. As someone who restocks T.L.C. Sukari Babyfacial on a near-monthly basis, I felt my wallet flinch the moment this loaded. The offer is real, the products are recognizable, but the page is leaning hard on the deal itself without giving me a single CTA button to act on it.

## What's working

- **25% OFF SITEWIDE in the hero** — I don't need to decode a promo code or calculate a discount. It's there. It's sitewide. Done.
- **Product cluster photography** — I can spot Sukari, C-Firma, Protini. These aren't anonymous bottles. Seeing my actual products is a dopamine hit.
- **Free shipping threshold in the top banner** — "$40+" is low enough that I'd hit it on a single-item restock, so the bar isn't punishing.
- **Tiered GWP copy below the fold** — "Hydra and the Bright kit with $100+, free full-size F-Balm with $175+" is exactly the kind of layering math I respect. I will absolutely engineer my cart around $175.
- **"The whole shebang!" headline** — Slightly cutesy, but it communicates sitewide inclusivity immediately, which matters when I'm wondering if my acid is excluded.

## What's weak

- **No CTA button in the hero** — There's a giant promotional graphic and no visible "Shop the Sale" button. I have to figure out how to actually start shopping. That's friction at the worst possible moment.
- **Navigation is fully collapsed** — Hamburger menu only. I can't see whether "Acids," "Serums," or "Moisturizers" are one tap away. For a F&F event when I'm ready to spend, I want category shortcuts visible.
- **No urgency signals** — No end date, no countdown timer, no "sale ends Sunday" copy. Friends & Family events have hard cutoffs. Showing it builds real urgency and I'd use it.
- **No person, no skin** — The entire above-the-fold is product-on-black. There's no lifestyle context, no skin close-up, nothing that shows me what these products are *doing*. For a science nerd that's fine but it's a missed warmth signal.
- **No trust layer** — No review count, no "free returns," no "secure checkout." For a first-time visitor this page would feel thin on credibility.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop the Sale" button to the hero** — Bright yellow, center-bottom of the hero block. Right now the offer just sits there. One button ships this week.
- **Surface a sale-end countdown or date** — Even "Sale ends Sunday, May 25" in the hero sub-copy converts browsers into buyers. DE knows when F&F ends. Tell me.
- **Expand the nav or add a shortcut strip below the banner** — Three pill-buttons ("Acids," "Moisturizers," "Serums") would let me jump to my restock list without hunting in a hamburger.
- **Add the F&F threshold callout to the hero itself** — The $100/$175 gift tiers are compelling but they're below the fold. Move at least the $175 F-Balm tier into the hero sub-copy so I'm calculating my cart immediately.
