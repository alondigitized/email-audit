# Homepage snapshot · www.drunkelephant.com · 2026-07-12
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-12**
## Executive summary

- This is Drunk Elephant in full hydration-push mode — the B-Hydra™ Intensive Hydration Serum is the lead actor, stacked front and center in their signature clean-clinical aesthetic. The "Hydrate responsibly" headline is the kind of tongue-in-cheek DE wordplay I'd normally appreciate, but the carousel is caught mid-transition and the copy is literally cut in half on my screen. As a brand loyalist who tracks every formula change, I expect better from them than a broken viewport.

## What's working

- **Free shipping banner** — "$40+ at checkout" is visible the second the page loads. I clear that threshold with one Babyfacial, so it registers immediately.
- **B-Hydra product stacking shot** — clean, clinical, ingredient-forward. The product photography speaks my language: no flowers, no spa candles, just the serum.
- **"You dew you" copy block** — "Your daily dose of vitamin B to hydrate and illuminate thirsty skin" is short, functional, science-adjacent without being a whitepaper. That's the right register for me.
- **Seasonal alignment** — pushing a vitamin B hydration serum in July makes biological sense. I expect my skin's barrier to be more stressed in summer; this lands.

## What's weak

- **Carousel mid-transition bug** — "drate responsibly" with the "Hy" sliced off is not a vibe. I read reformulation notes for fun; I notice broken text immediately.
- **No above-the-fold CTA button** — I can see carousel nav arrows (|| ← →) and the word "Pl" (probably "Play" for a video), but there is no "Shop Now" or "Shop B-Hydra" button visible. I should not have to hunt.
- **Hero is product-only, no routine context** — As a layering nerd, I want to see where B-Hydra sits in the stack. Is this AM? PM? What does it layer over/under? The image gives me no sequence cue.
- **No trust layer in viewport** — Zero star ratings, no review count, no "certified clean" badge. DE earned goodwill with me a long time ago, but a first-time buyer sees nothing here.
- **Loyalty program invisible** — The smiley-face nav icon is the only hint Drunk Points exists. No "earn X points on this" nudge on the hero.

## Recommendations

- **Fix the carousel clipping bug before anything else** — "drate responsibly" reads as a typo to a loyal customer and as amateurism to a new one. Either slow the transition timing or add proper overflow: hidden to the text container.
- **Add a hard "Shop B-Hydra" button directly on the hero** — Overlay it bottom-left of the product image. Button copy should be specific, not "Shop Now."
- **Introduce a one-line layering callout beneath the product name** — Something like "Step 2 in your AM routine. Apply after C-Firma, before SPF." That's the kind of micro-copy that converts a skin nerd on the spot.
- **Pull Drunk Points into the banner or hero** — "Earn 340 points on your next B-Hydra." I know my balance; remind me to use it.
