# Kamo in the headlines!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- This is a social-proof launch email for Kamo Triple-Peptide Cover Drops, and it's the most product-focused Drunk Elephant send in this run. After a week of Subscribe & Save countdowns (10/19–10/26) and two gifting pivots (10/28 "All the gifts.", 10/30 "The powerhouses"), this one goes back to a single hero product. The sculptural green-and-white hero with foundation dripping from the pump is distinctive, and "KAMO REVIEWS ARE IN!" is a clear headline.
- The press quotes from PopSugar, the New York Post and Byrdie carry the email. "25 shades" and "natural satin finish" are the concrete details a foundation shopper wants. The weakness is the offer. The only incentive is a $75+ gift-with-purchase (6-shade sample card plus a D-Bronzi sample), and it sits in the third block, below two CTAs that don't mention it. The gift-guide block at the bottom then pulls attention away from Kamo.
- Overall it's a solid, polished send with a weak commercial hook. It reads as brand-building more than conversion. It's about a 6, held back by a buried offer and no price or deadline in the main flow.

## What's working

- The hero image is memorable and on-brand: the pump with foundation flowing onto stacked blocks, in green and cream.
- Three short, attributed press quotes (PopSugar, NY Post, Byrdie) give credible third-party proof.
- The quotes address the foundation questions that matter: shade range (25 shades), finish, and a light feel that doesn't look greasy.
- The swatch graphics at the edges of the quote section show the shade range visually and add movement to the layout.
- There are two clear "Shop Kamo" buttons, one above the fold and one after the quotes.

## What's weak

- The gift-with-purchase ($75+, sample card plus D-Bronzi) is in the third block, and the deadline appears only in the fine print (through 11/5/2026). The hero buttons say nothing about it.
- There is no price, no shade-finder link and no "find your shade" CTA, which is the main barrier to buying foundation online.
- The "Present Tense?" gift-guide block competes with the Kamo story and has a vague CTA ("Shop now") with no offer.
- The CTA buttons are thin outlined boxes with small text. They're easy to miss next to the large hero.
- Shopper reviews aren't shown. Press quotes are good, but there are no star ratings or customer count.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the gift-with-purchase into the hero: "Spend $75+, get a free 6-shade card + D-Bronzi sample. Ends 11/5." This is the biggest lift because it turns a PR email into an offer email.
- 2. Make the primary CTA specific, such as "Find your Kamo shade," and link it to a shade finder or quiz. Foundation buyers need shade confidence before they click.
- 3. Add a customer star rating and review count next to the press quotes.
- 4. Cut the gift-guide block, or move it below the footer fine print. Keep one story per send.
- 5. Make the buttons solid-filled and larger so they stand out from the hero.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible markup is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with several code-level defects: invalid `@import` placement, `http://` image sources, and the recipient's address exposed in a tracking-pixel URL. The compliance and authentication checks came back inconclusive because the relay didn't capture the headers. The HTML was truncated, so footer and body links were not reviewable.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **13 tracking links not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination validity and redirect chains are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Recipient email exposed in a URL.** The second pixel is `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/?e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app&c=us_de_broad…`. The plaintext address in the query string can leak through proxy logs, CDN logs and referrers.
- **Two third-party tracking pixels.** One is `mi.drunkelephant.com/p/up/80acaf1c74f9e002/o.gif?mi_u=98161986&mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_kamopress_produ…`. The other is the Everest pixel above. The `mi_u=98161986` parameter is a persistent user ID.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid `@import` placement.** The three `@import url(https://db.onlinewebfonts.com/…)` rules come after the `body{…}` rule in the `<style>` block. Per the CSS spec, `@import` must precede all other rules, so browsers ignore them. The same fonts are also loaded through `<link>` tags in `<head>`, which most email clients strip.
- **Malformed CSS comments.** `<!-- *[class=contenttable] {…} -->` and `<!-- *[class=mobile] {…} -->` sit inside the `@media` block. HTML comment markers are not valid CSS comments, and they can break parsing of the rule that follows in some clients.
- **Insecure image sources.** Three social icons use `http://`: Facebook, Instagram and TikTok at `http://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/lib/fe3211717064057c751c78/m/1/…png`. Clients that enforce HTTPS or proxy images may block them, and they trigger mixed-content warnings in webmail.
- **Missing alt on pixels.** `o.gif` and the Everest pixel have no alt text. Both are tracking images, so the impact is low. An empty `alt=""` would be correct for them.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom, which conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Missing language attribute.** `<html xmlns=…>` has no `lang` attribute, and `<title>` is the generic "Drunk Elephant".
- **Font stack dependency.** The `@font-face` rules load from `db.onlinewebfonts.com` and `drunkelephant.com/on/demandware.static/…/v1719565389232/`. Fallback fonts are not visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm them.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source.
- The recipient email is rendered into the Everest pixel URL (see §2), which is a data-exposure issue.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` missing.** QA reports it as not found, with the note "may not be captured by AgentMail relay". This is unconfirmed because it may be a capture artifact.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) missing.** The same caveat applies. One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules.
- **`Authentication-Results` missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status could not be assessed. The From domain is `cheers.drunkelephant.com`, and image hosting on the same subdomain suggests an ESP-managed sending domain.
- **Footer not reviewable.** The physical address and the in-body unsubscribe link fall outside the truncated HTML, so I can't verify them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Campaign tags present but untested.** The pixel carries `mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_kamopress_produ…`, and the Everest URL carries `c=us_de_broad…`. Both are truncated.
- **UTM parameters unverified.** All 13 content links were wrapped in tracking redirects and not resolved, so I can't confirm UTM propagation or landing-page alignment.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the `@import` rules to the top of the `<style>` block, or drop them and rely on `@font-face` with web-safe fallbacks.
2. Remove the `<!-- -->` wrappers inside the `@media` block, or switch to `/* */`.
3. Serve the social icons over `https://`.
4. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels and `lang="en"` to `<html>`. Drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Stop putting the recipient email in the Everest pixel query string. Use an opaque token instead.
6. Re-run the QA against raw message headers, not the relay copy, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
7. Follow the redirects on the 13 tracking links to check landing URLs and UTM carry-through. Fetch the untruncated HTML to audit the footer.
