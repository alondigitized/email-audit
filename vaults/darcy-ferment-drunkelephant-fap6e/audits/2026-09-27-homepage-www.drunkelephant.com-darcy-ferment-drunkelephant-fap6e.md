# Homepage snapshot · www.drunkelephant.com · 2026-09-27
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-27**
## Executive summary

- This reads like a brand launching a new product and hoping I don't ask hard questions about it. The hero is all packaging glamour shots for "Kamo Triple-Peptide Cover Drops" — a color-cover product, not a treatment — and the promo banner is pushing a "moisturizer + Kamo bundle" discount. Nothing here talks to me as a Suspicious 6 person; it's a foundation-adjacent launch dressed up with a "clinically proven to firm skin" claim I'd want backed by actual data before I believe it.

## What's working

- **The offer banner up top** — "20% off a full-size moisturizer + Kamo bundle" — at least gives me a number and a reason to keep reading, even if it's not my category.
- **Clean product photography** — the green-and-white Kamo boxes with the peel-back color swatch window are shot well, no compression artifacts, no overlap.
- **The shade-range arm shot** — showing 25 shades across a real range of skin tones is a legitimate trust signal, even if it's for a coverage product I won't buy.

## What's weak

- **"NEW LAUNCH AL"** — this heading is visibly cut off (should almost certainly read "NEW LAUNCH ALERT") and there's a broken-looking glyph "⌐!" sitting next to it. That's a render bug I can see with my own eyes, and on a skincare-science brand it makes me trust the whole site a little less.
- **No ingredient or actives callout anywhere above the fold** — I read every reformulation note, and this hero gives me zero information about what's actually in the product beyond "triple-peptide" and "clinically proven to firm skin," which is a claim with no citation in sight.
- **No visible primary CTA button** — the carousel controls (pause, back, forward arrow) are navigation for the module, not a "Shop Now" or "Shop Kamo" button. I have to guess where to tap.
- **Wrong category for me entirely** — I'm a T.L.C. Sukari Babyfacial subscriber, not a coverage-drops shopper. There's no serum, no Suspicious-6-free callout, nothing for my actual routine on this screen.

## Recommendations

- Add a real "Shop Kamo" or "Shop Now" button under the product shot instead of relying on carousel arrows as the only tap targets.
- Fix the "NEW LAUNCH AL" truncation and remove or replace the broken glyph icon — it's a visible credibility hit on a site whose whole pitch is scientific precision.
- Put an ingredient/actives chip near the hero copy ("with 3 peptides, hyaluronic acid, no [Suspicious 6 ingredient]") so ingredient-literate shoppers like me get a reason to stay instead of scrolling past a launch that isn't for us.
- Surface a serum or Babyfacial restock/subscription nudge somewhere above the fold — even a small secondary banner — so repeat actives shoppers aren't stranded on a coverage-product hero.
