- Distinct product-set naming ("The Gala Glow," "The Couture Shows," "Maison Moisture," "The Finishing Touches") gives each tile a reason to exist rather than being interchangeable.

- Two CTA touchpoints (top "SHOP HOLIDAY" and bottom repeat) bookend the email.

## What's weak

- No price or discount shown anywhere, unusual for this brand's recent cadence and a real friction point for gift-set purchases.
- Subject line "Who's feeling merry?!" has nothing to do with the actual "Atelier"/couture concept in the email — a missed chance to carry through the strongest creative idea in the send.

- Product photography is beautiful but small and busy; it's hard to tell at a glance what's actually inside each set (jars vs. tubes vs. count).
