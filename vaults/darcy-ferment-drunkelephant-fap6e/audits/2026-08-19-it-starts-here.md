# It starts here
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, well-organized product-roundup email — five cleansers laid out in a simple alternating grid, each with one line of copy and a "Shop Now" link. It's the most catalog-like send from Drunk Elephant in this stretch: no clinical claims, no ingredient theater, just "here's the lineup, pick one." The hero banner leads with a real acquisition offer (free deluxe sample on new $50+ subscriptions) rather than a discount, which is a different lever than the SheerID 20%-off sends from 2026-08-05/08-15.
- The subject line "It starts here" is the weak link — it tells you nothing about cleansers, summer, or the free-sample offer sitting right in the header band. Compare that to "Introducing Kamo Drops!" (2026-08-11, 7/10), which won on subject specificity alone. Here, the visual execution carries the email; the subject undersells it.
- Solid, on-brand, low-risk send. It won't drive urgency, but it's a legitimate "know your cleanser lineup" reference email that a subscriber would scroll through without friction.

## What's working

- Clear visual hierarchy: hero headline → hero shot → "Shop Now" button, all above the fold.
- Every one of the five products gets its own row with a repeated "Shop Now" link — no dead ends.
- The header promo band (free deluxe sample, $50+ subscription) is honest and legible, not buried.
- Seasonal framing ("wash away summer grime") fits the August send date.
- Clean, consistent Drunk Elephant visual language — sketch-style elephant logo, minimalist product photography, no render bugs.

## What's weak

- Subject "It starts here" is generic and doesn't hint at the offer, the product category, or urgency — a missed chance given the strong hero copy underneath it.
- No preview/preheader text was supplied for this send — that's either missing or stripped, and it's a wasted second line of real estate in the inbox.
- Every CTA says "Shop Now" — undifferentiated, no product-specific action copy anywhere in five repeats.
- No price discount on the individual products; the only incentive is a conditional free-sample-with-subscription offer buried in fine print at the bottom.
- No social proof, reviews, or ingredient/skin-type detail for any of the five SKUs — just one generic line each.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the subject line first — "It starts here" tests nothing and reveals nothing. Anchor it to the actual hook (cleanser lineup + free sample).
- 2. Add a preheader that names the offer instead of leaving it blank — free real estate currently unused.
- 3. Vary CTA copy per product ("Get the Jelly Cleanser," "Try the Micellar Water") instead of five identical "Shop Now" links.
- 4. Pull the free-deluxe-sample offer terms up near the header band in plain language, not just in the small-print block at the very bottom.
- 5. Add one line of skin-type or ingredient detail per product (e.g., "for oily/combo skin") — the current one-liners are too vague to differentiate five products.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your cleanser lineup, sorted`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Free sample when you subscribe $50+`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `5 cleansers, 1 free deluxe sample with new subscriptions.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Jelly, cream, or balm — pick your summer cleanser.`
