# 30% off. Black Friday style.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-28**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth Black Friday-day send from Drunk Elephant, and it is the same creative again. The hero reads "It's on! BLACK FRIDAY 30% OFF SITEWIDE" over the pink gift box, with a neon free-shipping bar above it. The subject, "30% off. Black Friday style.", matches the hero. It is clean and on brand.
- The body then stacks four more offers on top of that one: a free full-size B-Goldi at $110+, "UP TO 40% OFF" last chance, "Trunk 7.1 IS HERE AND 20% OFF" with code DRUNK20, and 30% off kits. That is five promos in one email, and the eye has nowhere to settle after the hero. The fine print is also long and full of exclusions. Compared with the earlier 11/27 sends ("Black Friday means 30% off", "Well, hello, Black Friday!"), nothing new is happening here, so it reads as another reminder. Judgement: competent but redundant. The send adds volume, not value.

## What's working

- The hero is clear: "BLACK FRIDAY 30% OFF SITEWIDE" in large type over a strong pink product image.
- The neon-yellow free-shipping bar is easy to spot and adds a second reason to buy.
- The kit grid shows a struck-through price, the sale price, a star rating and "738 PEOPLE ARE CHECKING THIS OUT". That is real social proof and urgency.
- The B-Goldi free full-size gift ("a value of $39") is a concrete, tangible perk.
- Brand voice is consistent and playful ("Black Friday fever is here!", "KIT 'EM WHILE THEY'RE HOT!"). I see no broken images or layout bugs.

## What's weak

- Five offers (30% off, free shipping, free B-Goldi, up to 40% off, Trunk 20% off with a code) compete for attention. "30% off" and "up to 40% off" sit in the same email and muddy the message.
- The "UP TO 40% OFF, Last Chance! While supplies last" banner is vague. It doesn't say which products it covers.
- The Trunk 7.1 block needs a code (DRUNK20) but has no visible button or link. It is a dead end next to a $340 "$733 VALUE" claim.
- The fine print is long and has stacked exclusions (gift cards, no combining, kits excluded from 40%). That undercuts the honest, simple "30% sitewide" promise.
- Every CTA is a generic "SHOP NOW", and the small text links under the Goldi and 40% blocks are easy to miss.
- This is the fifth near-identical send in about three days (11/25 "Up to 40% off." through today), so fatigue is likely.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut to one hero offer plus one bonus: 30% sitewide and the free B-Goldi at $110+. Drop the "up to 40%" banner and fold the Trunk into a separate send.
- 2. Give the Trunk block a real button ("Shop the Trunk — code DRUNK20") or remove it.
- 3. Replace generic "SHOP NOW" with specific copy: "Get my 30% off", "Claim free B-Goldi", "Shop kits".
- 4. Add a clear end time near the top ("Ends Sunday 11:59 pm ET") instead of burying the 11/29 deadline in the fine print.
- 5. Vary the creative on later sends in the sequence (new product, a different hero) so the fifth email isn't the same as the first.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free full-size B-Goldi + 30% off, ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last chance: 30% off sitewide`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $110+ and get a free $39 B-Goldi. Free ground shipping on everything.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Kits, serums and moisturizers, all 30% off through 11/29.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source shows no broken-link or merge-token failures. Confirmed issues are three non-HTTPS social icons, a tracking pixel and a link image with no `alt`, a subscriber email address exposed in a URL query string, and an invalid CSS `@import` placement. The compliance and authentication warnings come from header capture through the AgentMail relay, so they are unverified rather than confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed tracking links:** 19 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe because they use click-redirect domains, such as `drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com`. Destination validity and redirect chains are therefore **unverified**. This is a coverage gap, not a defect.
- **Recipient email in a URL:** the Everest link `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/?e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app&c=us_de_broad…` puts the recipient's address in a query parameter. The address will appear in redirect logs, referrers and proxy logs. It is also unclear whether it is hashed or encoded.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** `https://mi.drunkelephant.com/p/up/80acaf1c74f9e002/o.gif?mi_u=98161986&mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_bfpmreminder2_p…` carries a numeric user ID (`mi_u`). That is expected for a pixel, but the pixel is a separate `mi.drunkelephant.com` host from the sending and image domains (`cheers.drunkelephant.com`, `image.cheers.drunkelephant.com`).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on two images:**
  - The `o.gif` tracking pixel. Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`.
  - The `everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/` image, which the QA check flagged as an image with no `alt`.
- **Non-HTTPS images:** all three social icons load over `http://`, so mail clients or proxies may block them or show mixed-content warnings:
  - `http://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/lib/fe3211717064057c751c78/m/1/cc02d07d-c9bb-4057-b5d7-62f3bec2c521.png` (Facebook)
  - `…/9b452915-64bf-4b29-b08b-c4a42802457a.png` (Instagram)
  - `…/29f975e1-6f94-429c-ae2b-8ee6f90786cb.png` (TikTok)
- **Invalid `@import` placement:** in the `<style>` block, the three `@import url(https://db.onlinewebfonts.com/…)` rules come after `body{…}`. CSS requires `@import` to precede all other rules, so browsers ignore them. The `<link rel="stylesheet">` tags in `<head>` do load the fonts, so the `@import` lines are redundant as well as invalid.
- **HTML comments inside `@media`:** `<!-- *[class=contenttable] {…} -->` and `<!-- *[class=mobile] {…} -->` sit inside the `@media (max-width:600px)` block. Some parsers may treat them as stray tokens, and the rules inside are dead code either way.
- **Third-party font dependency:** the Brown Bold, Light and Thin fonts are served from `db.onlinewebfonts.com`. Most email clients strip `<link>` tags and webfonts, so this adds external requests with little benefit. Fallback stacks are not visible in the truncated source.
- **Truncated source:** the HTML was truncated, so the template body, any `<img>` dimension attributes, `lang` and `role="presentation"` usage could not be checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[First Name]` appear in the visible source. The recipient email is substituted into the tracking URL (see Section 2).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unverified, likely a capture artifact:** the QA check reports `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results` headers as not found. Its own note says the relay may not preserve them, so there is no evidence that the sender omitted them. Check the raw `.eml` or the original headers before treating this as a defect.
- **Not verified in the truncated source:** the in-body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address. Neither appeared in the visible portion, so I can't confirm or rule them out.
- **From domain:** the From address is `info@cheers.drunkelephant.com`, a subdomain. SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for it could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Campaign identifiers:** the campaign is identified only through Everest and Marketing Cloud parameters (`c=us_de_broad…`, `mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_bfpmreminder2_p…`). No `utm_*` parameters appear in the visible source.
- **Landing URLs:** these were not visible, and the 19 tracking links were not probed, so I can't confirm the landing pages, attribution parameters, or whether the offer matches the subject line, "30% off. Black Friday style."
- **Naming:** the campaign string says `bfpmreminder2`, which suggests a reminder send. This is informational only.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Change the three social icon URLs from `http://` to `https://` on `image.cheers.drunkelephant.com`, after confirming the host serves valid TLS.
2. Add `alt=""` to the `o.gif` pixel (with `width="1" height="1"`). Add descriptive `alt` text to the Everest `…/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/` image.
3. Stop passing the plaintext email in `?e=` on the Everest URL. Use a hashed or opaque subscriber ID.
4. Move the `@import` rules above all other CSS, or delete them and rely on the `<link>` tags. Remove the `<!-- -->` wrappers inside `@media`, and add web-safe fallbacks for the Brown fonts.
5. Re-run the audit against the raw message source to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) before treating them as failures.
6. Extend the QA probe to follow the redirects on the tracking domains. That would let it check final landing URLs, UTM or campaign parameters, and the unsubscribe and postal address in the full, untruncated HTML.
