# We’ve got your routine right this way.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuine departure from the "keep it between us" secret-sale machine Drunk Elephant has been running non-stop since mid-August — no countdown timer, no discount code, no SheerID gate. Instead it's a quiz-tool push ("Build Your Routine") paired with a spend-$100-get-deluxe-samples GWP offer. That's a healthier long-term play than another 20%-off blast, but it also means there's no hard discount for a deal-driven subscriber to act on today.
- The bigger problem is execution: the fourth product tile in the "Beste No.9 / B-Hydra / Protini / Finish" row is a broken, unrendered outline — no image loaded. In a clean, minimal, white-space-heavy layout like this, one dead product image reads as sloppy and undermines the "trust us with your skincare routine" pitch the whole email is making.
- Net: interesting strategic pivot (tool-led personalization instead of fatigue-inducing discounts), let down by a visible render bug and a soft, quiz-gated CTA with no visible product payoff.

## What's working

- Clean hierarchy: eyebrow banner → bold headline → explainer copy → single green CTA button, easy to scan top to bottom.
- Genuine offer variety after a long discount-only streak — this reads as a real content/tool push, not a repackaged sale.
- Secondary "Fall Skin, Sorted" module gives a concrete, specific GWP offer (deluxe samples on $100+ spend) with clear qualifying terms in the fine print.
- Brand identity (logo, elephant mark, muted sage/pink-adjacent palette) is consistent and recognizable at a glance.

## What's weak

- The fourth product image ("Finish") is a broken/empty outline box — a visible render failure in the hero module.
- Primary CTA "Build Your Routine" is vague and adds friction — it routes to a quiz, not a product or offer, so there's nothing to click straight into.
- No dollar/percent offer visible anywhere in the hero; the only real incentive (GWP) is buried below the fold in smaller type.
- Top banner ("New Kamo Triple-Peptide Cover Drops is making headlines") is disconnected from the email's actual content — the email never mentions Cover Drops again.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the broken "Finish" product image before this ships again — a dead asset in a 4-product hero row is the single biggest visible defect here.
- 2. Give the hero module a concrete hook — either name the GWP offer ("Spend $100, get 2 deluxe minis free") in the hero itself, or swap the vague "Build Your Routine" CTA for something like "Get My Routine" tied to a visible incentive.
- 3. Resolve the top-banner mismatch — either make the banner about the quiz tool, or drop the Cover Drops teaser since it's never paid off in-email.
- 4. Move the "Fall Skin, Sorted" GWP details (the $100+ threshold and included samples) up into visible, bolded copy rather than small gray print under the module.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your skin, sorted in 3 questions`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Stop guessing. Get your routine.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Take the quiz, get matched, plus deluxe samples on $100+`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `3 questions to your best fall skin`
