# Homepage snapshot · www.drunkelephant.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- Drunk Elephant opens with its crown jewel front and center — the T.L.C. Sukari Babyfacial — and the copy is leading with a reformulation announcement. For me that's crack. As a brand, this reads: science-first, confident, not discounting. What they're pushing right now is clearly the re-launch of the Babyfacial with an "enhanced formula," which means I need to know every single ingredient that changed before I approve this into my routine.

## What's working

- **The Babyfacial hero choice** — leading with your most cult, most repurchased SKU is exactly right for someone like me. I came here for this product and it's the first thing I see.
- **The reformulation copy** — "An icon reimagined: The OG at-home 'facial,' now with an enhanced formula" triggers my reformulation-radar in the best way. I will scroll to read the full ingredient changelog.
- **The free-shipping banner** — $40 threshold in plain language, no hoops. My monthly Babyfacial order clears that easily.
- **Clean mobile layout** — no overlapping elements, product photography is high-res and color-accurate, live chat tab is there if I need to interrogate an ingredient.

## What's weak

- **Cropped headline** — "xfoliate responsibly" is not a headline, it's a fragment. The "E" is gone off the left edge. Even on a deliberate scroll-reveal treatment, this reads as a render issue on first load and a missed first impression.
- **Zero CTA button above the fold** — the only tappable affordance I can see are the carousel navigation arrows. I should not have to hunt for a way into the product.
- **No ingredient delta / reformulation detail** — if you're going to lead with "enhanced formula," I need a "What changed?" link or at minimum a badge pointing to a comparison. Otherwise I'm skeptical, not excited.
- **No loyalty hook** — I'm a high-frequency buyer and there is no account badge, no rewards balance, no "member pricing" signal. This page doesn't know I exist.
- **Carousel auto-play** — the pause/play controls suggest this is auto-advancing, which on mobile is a dark pattern that pulls focus away from your hero story before I've finished reading it.

## Recommendations

- **Expose the full headline** — "Exfoliate responsibly" should be fully visible on load; either adjust the scroll-reveal breakpoint or lock the hero height so the heading isn't cropped on a standard viewport.
- **Add a single above-the-fold CTA** — "See what's new in the formula" or "Shop Babyfacial" as a button directly under the sub-headline. One tap, no searching.
- **Surface a "Formula Update" badge or inline link** — given the reformulation angle, a small callout ("New: AHA/BHA blend updated — see the full INCI") would convert skeptical science nerds who are mid-repurchase decision.
- **Add a loyalty/account recognition strip** — even a "Welcome back, Darcy — you have 420 points" in the nav banner turns a returning buyer into a fast-track converter.
