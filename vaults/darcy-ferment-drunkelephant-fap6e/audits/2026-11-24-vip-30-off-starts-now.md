# VIP 30% off starts now!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- This is the real Cyber Week send, arriving two days after the 11/22 "PSA: 30% off starts soon!" teaser. The VIP early-access framing works. A countdown banner ("Early access ends in… 00 days 14 hours 52 minutes 30 seconds") sits above a hero that says "CYBER WEEK 30% OFF SITEWIDE." Under that, the code DRUNKVIP is stated plainly. The offer is clear, the deadline is credible, and the product grid carries sale prices with strikethroughs.
- The weakness is sprawl. Three discount messages compete: 30% sitewide, "Up to 40% off" last-chance kits, and a free gift over $100. The VIP is asked to remember a promo code, but the code sits in body copy rather than on the button. The fine print also carries real exclusions: no gift cards, no stacking, and kits excluded from the 30%. The "Everything is 30% off!" line overstates it. Compared with the 11/21 gift guide (8/10), this one is more transactional and less useful, but it delivers the news the teaser promised.
- Verdict: a solid, honest-enough sale launch. It would be stronger with one offer, a one-tap code, and a hero that shows a single product.

## What's working

- The countdown banner (14 hours left) gives credible urgency and fits the VIP early-access story.
- The hero states the offer in large type: "CYBER WEEK 30% OFF SITEWIDE."
- The product grid shows sale prices against struck-through originals, for example Protini at $69.30 vs $99.00, with star ratings.
- Social proof is visible: "881 people are checking this out."
- The $158 Bespoke Brightening Kit at $69.30 echoes the 11/17 "$158 of skincare for $69?!" hook, so the deal feels familiar.

## What's weak

- The promo code DRUNKVIP is plain text in the teal block. Nobody can tap to copy it, and the SHOP NOW button doesn't mention it.
- Three offers stack: 30% sitewide, up to 40% off, and a free gift with a $100 minimum. They dilute each other.
- "Everything is 30% off!" and "sitewide" conflict with the fine print, which excludes gift cards, last-chance kits and stacked discounts. A buried exclusion like this hurts the sense of honesty.
- The three mid-email CTAs ("SHOP NOW" as button, link and link) all say the same thing and go nowhere specific. The free-gift SHOP NOW link is small and low-contrast.
- The hero shows a crowded product cluster with no single hero product. The "Beste is yet to come" pun and the "lucky duck" copy bury the actual mechanic.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the code on the button: "Shop 30% off with DRUNKVIP," or auto-apply it through the link. This is the biggest lift.
- 2. Cut to one offer in the hero. Move the free gift and the up-to-40% banner below the grid, or lead with the gift threshold only.
- 3. Replace generic "SHOP NOW" with specific CTAs such as "Shop moisturizers" and "Claim your free kit."
- 4. Lead the grid with the $158 kit at $69.30, since it is the strongest deal and has proven appeal.
- 5. Change "Everything is 30% off!" to "30% off almost everything" and surface the main exclusions near the offer.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Early access ends tonight. Plus a free kit on orders over $100.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

- **Two tracking pixels:** `mi.drunkelephant.com/p/up/80acaf1c74f9e002/o.gif?mi_u=98161986&mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_bfcmallvipstart` and the Everest URL above. Both are open or engagement pixels. They give no signal when images are blocked, and Apple Mail Privacy Protection inflates them.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `o.gif` (mi.drunkelephant.com) and the Everest pixel have no `alt` attribute. Tracking pixels should have `alt=""`, plus `width="1" height="1"` and `style="display:none"` where appropriate.
- **Mixed content:** Three social icons load over `http://`: Facebook `cc02d07d-…png`, Instagram `9b452915-…png` and TikTok `29f975e1-…png`. All are on `image.cheers.drunkelephant.com`. Some clients and proxies will block or rewrite them. These images do have alt text.
- **Invalid `@import`:** `@import url(https://db.onlinewebfonts.com/…)` comes after `body{…}` in the `<style>` block. `@import` must precede all other rules, so browsers ignore these three imports. The `<link>` tags in `<head>` still load the fonts.
- **Malformed CSS inside `@media (max-width:600px)`:** `<!-- *[class=contenttable] {…} -->` and `<!-- *[class=mobile] {…} -->` are HTML comments, not CSS comments. Inside an at-rule block, the `<!--` and `-->` tokens become part of the selector prelude. Those rules are dropped, and the following `*[class=vouchertext4]` rule is likely dropped too, because the `-->` prefixes it.
- **Pinch-zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` disables zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **No `lang` attribute:** The `<html>` tag has none, only `xmlns`.
- **Font dependency:** The custom fonts depend on `db.onlinewebfonts.com` and the drunkelephant.com Demandware static path, which is a versioned path (`v1719565389232`). No fallback stack was visible in the truncated source.
