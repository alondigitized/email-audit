# Another round of Protini!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand holiday kit send with a sloppy second half. The hero is strong. Three Protini kits sit under "ALL THE FILLS" on a yellow banner, and the copy explains the offer in one line: one Protini plus one refill. A "SHOP NOW" button sits above the fold, and the product shot with the "$89 VALUE: $135" badge makes the deal easy to read.
- The email then adds an unrelated Kamo pouch promo with different creative and a different tone. The terms for it sit in grey fine print at the bottom. That makes it two offers in one email. The pouch deadline (10/17 at 2:59 AM ET) is the only real urgency anywhere, and it's buried. The kit itself has no deadline.
- It's also close to repeat content. It follows the Oct 7 and Oct 9 Made-to-Measure kit sends ("Great, glow, and gorgeous", "Before the fall…") and the Oct 15 Kamo email ("Making Kamo even better"). A subscriber would see this as the same kit with a new hero. Judgement: solid execution, low novelty, and a hero offer that's softer than it should be.

## What's working

- The hero is clear: a bold headline, three kits and a yellow block with a visible "SHOP NOW" button, all above the fold.
- The "$89 VALUE: $135" badge on the kit photo puts a concrete number on the offer.
- The product copy is skimmable: "Lightweight gel-cream", "Packed with peptides", "Visibly firms and strengthens skin".
- The brand voice is consistent and playful ("cuckoo bananas crazytown"), and the yellow, teal and lilac palette matches the earlier holiday sends.
- Nothing is broken. The layout is clean, the images load, and the spacing is consistent.

## What's weak

- "VALUE: $135" is a bundle value, not a discount, so the savings are unclear. The email never says what you save.
- The Kamo pouch promo competes with the kit, and its mismatched creative makes it look like a leftover.
- The pouch terms in the grey fine print are long and hard to read. That's where the only real deadline sits.
- The two "SHOP NOW" buttons are thin outlined boxes that look weak against the bold hero. The generic copy doesn't say what's being shopped.
- No reviews, ratings, or "why Protini" proof appear anywhere, and there's no loyalty or member angle.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one offer. Cut the pouch promo, or move it to its own send. The kit should get the whole email.
- 2. State the savings plainly, for example "Save $46: Protini + refill, $89" next to the badge. Add a real deadline if one exists.
- 3. Make the CTAs specific and solid: "Get the Protini + refill kit". A filled button would stand out more than an outline.
- 4. Add one proof point, such as a star rating or a short review quote, near the product shot.
- 5. If the pouch promo stays, put its deadline in the headline instead of the fine print.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `One jar, one refill, $46 in savings. Limited-edition holiday kit.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on two images.** Both are tracking images, so `alt=""` is the correct fix.
  - `o.gif` (`mi.drunkelephant.com`)
  - the `everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/` beacon
- **Three social icons load over plain `http://`.** The sources are `http://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/lib/fe3211717064057c751c78/m/1/…png`, for Facebook (`cc02d07d-…`), Instagram (`9b452915-…`) and TikTok (`29f975e1-…`). Clients and proxies may block or warn on mixed content. Other images on the same host may use `https`, so the mixed usage looks inconsistent.
- **`<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>`** disables pinch-zoom on mobile, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Web fonts depend on third parties.** The `<link>` and `@import` entries load from `db.onlinewebfonts.com`, and `@font-face` loads from `www.drunkelephant.com/on/demandware.static/…`. Most email clients ignore these, so fallback stacks matter, but I can't see them in the truncated source.
- **`@import url(...)` rules come after the `body {…}` rule.** Per CSS, `@import` must precede other rules, so these are invalid and will be ignored. The `<link>` tags do the same job in the few clients that support them.
- **HTML comments (`<!-- *[class=contenttable] {…} -->`) sit inside the `@media` block.** Some parsers will mishandle the rule after them.
- **`<html>` has no `lang` attribute** in the visible portion. This hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **The `<title>` is only "Drunk Elephant".** It is generic and does not match the subject.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved merge tokens** (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[[…]]`) in the visible source.
- **The recipient's email is in plaintext in a URL.** The beacon contains `?e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app`, so the address appears in the query string of a third-party request. That exposes PII in logs, referrers and proxies. A hashed or opaque ID would avoid this.
- **A numeric subscriber ID is also exposed.** `mi_u=98161986` is in the open-pixel URL.
