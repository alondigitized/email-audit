# Now you see it: 25% off
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-24**
## Executive summary

- "Now you see it: 25% off" is email #6 in a Drunk Elephant Friends & Family blitz that started May 21 — and the creative fatigue is finally showing. The hero is the same 25% OFF SITEWIDE wordmark we've now seen four straight emails in a row, with the same "30% off kits, 15% off RAD will still ship" header band, the same threshold spend callouts ($100, "FULL SIZE GIFT"), and the same product grid at the bottom labeled "TIME TO STOCK UP."
- The subject line "Now you see it: 25% off" is playful and brand-voicey — it leans on Drunk Elephant's signature wink — but the body underneath delivers nothing new. There's no fresh angle, no new bundle, no new threshold, no new product hero. By day four of identical creative, even a fan starts deleting on sight.
- Judgement: still a competent send with clean hierarchy and a strong offer, but it's the weakest of the F&F sequence so far because it's purely a frequency tap. The Skechers-walker persona isn't the target buyer for this skincare brand to begin with, so business impact stays low.

## What's working

- Subject line keeps the brand's playful voice ("Now you see it") and resolves with the concrete offer.
- 25% OFF SITEWIDE wordmark is huge and unmissable — eye lands on it instantly.
- Threshold gifts ("Spend $100+" + "FULL SIZE GIFT" sticker) add a stack-the-cart incentive on top of the % off.
- Product grid at the bottom shows specific SKUs with prices, not just a vague "shop now."
- Three clearly themed modules (Future's Looking Bright / Mask Me Anything / Time to Stock Up) break up an otherwise repetitive offer.

## What's weak

- This is the 4th near-identical "25% off sitewide" creative in 4 days — fatigue is real.
- Hero copy under the 25% lockup ("don't miss this sale… stock up so don't wait!") reads slightly off / ungrammatical.
- No new angle versus yesterday's "Sup with the 25% off?" or "Drunk classics are 25% off!" — same modules, same products.
- Subject doesn't tell you anything you didn't already learn three emails ago; the "now you see it" tease doesn't pay off with a hidden discount or surprise reveal.
- Fine-print block at the bottom is dense and visually heavy for a promo email.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pick a new angle for day 4.** Stop repeating "25% off sitewide" in the headline — pivot to a routine, a category (sunscreen for Memorial Day), a bestseller bundle, or a last-call countdown. The offer can stay; the wrapper has to change.
- 2. **Pay off the "now you see it" tease.** If the subject promises a reveal, the hero should be a hidden gift, mystery bundle, or surprise threshold — not the same wordmark.
- 3. **Add a deadline.** Day 6 of the sequence with no visible end date is suspicious. "Ends Monday 11:59 PT" would force the click.
- 4. **Tighten hero microcopy.** "With 25% off sitewide, it's a perfect opportunity to stock up so don't wait!" — rewrite to one crisp sentence.
- 5. **Lead with one hero product, not a wordmark.** A single SKU + price + savings ("Protini was $68, now $51") would out-convert the abstract banner at this point in the sequence.
