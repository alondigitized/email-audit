# Homepage snapshot · www.drunkelephant.com · 2026-06-07
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-07**
## Executive summary

- This is unmistakably Drunk Elephant doing what Drunk Elephant does — centering a hero around ingredient science (vitamin B hydration) with their signature pastel product stacking. The brand is pushing B-Hydra right now, probably a summer hydration campaign, and the tone is their usual cheeky-meets-clinical. As a returning customer who knows this product intimately, I felt immediately at home — and immediately frustrated by how little I could actually *do* from this first screen.

## What's working

- **The B-Hydra product stack image** — those layered blue bottles are immediately recognizable to anyone in the DE ecosystem and signal ingredient authority, not just aesthetics.
- **"You dew you: Your daily dose of vitamin B to hydrate and illuminate thirsty skin"** — this sub-copy actually *tells* me the ingredient and the function. For a skin-care nerd, that's the difference between stopping and scrolling past.
- **Free shipping banner** — "$40+ at checkout" is pinned at the very top and the threshold is genuinely reachable on one DE serum. That's a real offer, not a $200 minimum hiding in the footer.
- **Carousel controls** — the pause/prev/next arrows tell me there's more content to explore without forcing autoplay, which I appreciate on mobile.

## What's weak

- **Truncated hero headline** — "ase hydrate responsibly" is a broken reading experience. The first syllable or two of the headline is visually cut off by the image. I cannot tell if it says "Please hydrate responsibly" or something else. On mobile, this is a layout failure.
- **No CTA button above the fold** — I can see a product, I can read ingredient copy, and I have zero tap target to actually go buy the thing. The entire conversion layer is below scroll.
- **Hamburger-only navigation** — all of DE's category depth (serums, SPF, value sets, the Mini collections I subscribe to) is hidden behind a hamburger. One tap gets me to the menu; two more taps to reach what I want. That's friction.
- **No social proof, reviews, or trust signals** — I'm a returning customer who reads reformulation notes obsessively. A "4.8 stars from 12,000 reviews" badge would still reassure me I haven't been reading about a quiet formula change.
- **No loyalty callout** — DE has a rewards program. I'm here regularly. Nothing on this screen acknowledges I might be a repeat buyer.

## Recommendations

- **Fix the hero headline crop** — whether it's a padding issue or an image-text overlap bug, the truncated "ase hydrate responsibly" needs a code fix this week. The headline is the brand's first sentence; losing the first syllable is not acceptable.
- **Add a visible "Shop B-Hydra" or "Shop Now" button** overlaid on or immediately below the hero image — this screen is doing product education with zero tap target to convert.
- **Surface one returning-customer hook in the top nav or banner** — even a subtle "rewards" icon next to the cart would signal to regular shoppers that DE sees them. The banner real estate is currently a one-liner; a loyalty nudge fits there.
- **Swap the hamburger for a persistent category strip** (Serums / SPF / Sets / Minis) below the logo — four categories, one tap to any of them, no buried nav. DE's product lines are tight enough to support this on mobile.
