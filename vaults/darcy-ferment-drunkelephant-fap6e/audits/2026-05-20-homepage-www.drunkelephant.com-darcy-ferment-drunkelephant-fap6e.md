# Homepage snapshot · www.drunkelephant.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- They put the T.L.C. Sukari Babyfacial front and center, which is smart — I literally buy a new one every month. The hero is announcing a reformulation, which is either going to be the most compelling thing I've ever seen on a brand homepage or the thing that makes me spiral into ingredient comparison threads for the next two hours. Right now it looks like Drunk Elephant knows exactly what their loyalists care about, but the above-the-fold execution leaves me hunting for the "tell me more" button that doesn't seem to be there.

## What's working

- **T.L.C. Sukari Babyfacial as the hero product** — they picked the right horse. If you know the brand, you know this is the anchor SKU and putting the reformulated version front-and-center is a bold, confident move.
- **"An icon reimagined: The OG at-home 'facial,' now with an enhanced formula"** — this copy is doing real work for me specifically. "Reimagined" + "enhanced formula" triggers the part of my brain that immediately wants to see the before/after ingredient list. That's engagement by reformulation anxiety.
- **Free shipping banner** — $40 threshold is right where my average Drunk Elephant reorder sits. One bottle of Babyfacial clears it easily. Not aspirational, actually reachable.
- **Product photography** — the two-bottle layout (one upright, one angled) shows the new packaging format cleanly. Clean white/pink palette is very on-brand and legible on mobile.

## What's weak

- **No CTA button visible above the fold** — the carousel arrows ("← ‖ →") are not tappable shopping CTAs. I have no idea where to tap to actually get to the product page or learn more about the reformulation.
- **Carousel caught mid-transition** — "te responsibly" and "Pleas" sitting at the bottom of the screen looks like a layout break at a glance. Even if it's just a timing artifact, it undermines the premium feel.
- **No loyalty hook above the fold** — I've been buying Babyfacial monthly for years. Zero acknowledgment that I exist as a returning customer. No rewards callout, no "member exclusive" badge, nothing.
- **No trust signals** — no star ratings, no review count, no "as seen in" badge — nothing that would reassure a new visitor or validate my habitual repurchase with social proof.
- **Lifestyle shot below the fold cut** — the partial face-applying-product image is cut off, which makes it feel incomplete rather than aspirational.

## Recommendations

- **Add a visible "Shop the Reformulation" CTA button directly on the hero** — not just carousel arrows. The reformulation is the story; give me somewhere to go with it, with a button overlaid on or directly beneath the product shot.
- **Swap the lifestyle crop for the full face-application image** — or replace it with a before/after ingredient callout ("same acids, enhanced formula") that speaks to the reformulation-curious buyer who reads every changelog.
- **Add a loyalty/rewards strip beneath the free-shipping banner** — one line: "DE Perks member? You're earning X points on this order." I should feel recognized when I land.
- **Fix or pause the carousel during page load so the mid-slide text bleed doesn't appear** — "te responsibly" as orphaned text on a premium skincare homepage is a QA catch they should ship this week.
