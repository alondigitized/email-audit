# Technical Audit: Drunk Elephant "VIP time's almost up!"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with ESP click tracking (Marketing Cloud-style `cheers.drunkelephant.com` and `everestengagement.com`). The QA run found 0 hard failures and 3 warning groups (57% pass rate). The main confirmed issues are 3 insecure `http://` images, 2 images with no alt text, and CSS that is invalid or hurts accessibility. The compliance and authentication checks could not be verified from this capture.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **17 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them because they are click-redirect domains. Destination status, redirect chains and landing-page validity are unverified.
- **Recipient email is exposed in a URL.** The Everest URL is `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/?e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app&c=us_de_broad...`. A plaintext address in a query string ends up in redirect logs, proxies and Referer headers.
- **Two tracking pixels have no `alt`.** They are `https://mi.drunkelephant.com/p/up/80acaf1c74f9e002/o.gif?mi_u=98161986&mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_bfcmvipends_pro...` and the Everest `/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/` image. If these are 1x1 pixels, they should carry `alt=""`, `width="1"`, `height="1"` and `style="display:none"`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Insecure images.** The Facebook, Instagram and TikTok icons load over `http://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/lib/fe3211717064057c751c78/m/1/...png`. Clients that proxy images or enforce HTTPS may block them. The domain presumably serves HTTPS, so switch the scheme.
- **Invalid `@import` placement.** The `<style>` block starts with the `body{...}` rule, and the three `@import url(https://db.onlinewebfonts.com/...)` statements follow it. Per the CSS spec, `@import` must come first, so these are ignored. Fonts then depend only on the duplicate `<link>` tags and `@font-face` blocks, which many clients strip.
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **HTML comments inside `@media`.** The `<!-- *[class=contenttable] {...} -->` markers sit inside the `@media (max-width: 600px)` block. Some parsers may mishandle the rules around them.
- **Mobile rules look suspect.** `.vouchertext4` and `.fsname` set `max-width:15px` and `max-height:12px`/`15px` with `!important`. This could clip text on small screens, but I can't confirm that without a render.
- **Third-party font host.** Fonts are fetched from `db.onlinewebfonts.com` and from a versioned demandware path (`v1719565389232`). A stale version path would 404, and the fallback stack is not visible in the truncated source.
- **Truncated source.** I could not check semantic structure, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, or alt text on the remaining images.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%first_name%%` or `{{...}}` were visible in the truncated source or the QA output.
- The only personalization finding is the plaintext address in the tracking URL (see section 2).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unverified, not confirmed missing**. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so check the raw message.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown for the same relay reason. The From domain `cheers.drunkelephant.com` is a subdomain of the brand, and alignment should be checked on the original message.
- **The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not visible** in the truncated HTML, so I can't confirm either one.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- Click URLs are wrapped in redirects, and the `mi_ecmp` campaign ID `us_de_broadcast_promotion_bfcmvipends_pro...` is cut off in the QA output.
- No `utm_*` parameters could be verified, and the final landing URLs were not resolved, so I can't assess campaign alignment between email and site.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Change the three social icon `src` values from `http://` to `https://`.
2. Add `alt=""` and 1x1 dimensions to the two tracking pixels.
3. Move the `@import` rules to the top of the `<style>` block, or remove them in favor of the `<link>` tags.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
5. Remove the `<!-- -->` comments inside `@media`, and review the `max-width:15px` rules on `.vouchertext4` and `.fsname`.
6. Replace the plaintext `e=<email>` in the Everest URL with a hashed or opaque subscriber ID.
7. Check the raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. Rerun the QA with full headers instead of relying on the relay-stripped copy.
8. Resolve the redirect chains for the 17 skipped links to check final URLs, UTM parameters and HTTP status.
