# Well, hi, 20% off.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- "Well, hi, 20% off" reads like a straightforward percentage-off email until you actually parse it: this isn't a sitewide sale, it's a SheerID occupation-verification discount for teachers, students, military, first responders, and healthcare workers. That's a meaningfully different email than the subject line and hero headline suggest, and it means the "20% off" hook doesn't actually apply to most subscribers opening this.
- Production quality is clean — the hero shot is on-brand, the copy has personality ("sharpened pencils, warm sweaters"), and the funnel (get verified → get code → recurring monthly code) is explained simply with five clearly labeled occupation buttons. But this is functionally a list-segmentation/enrollment email wearing a sale email's clothes, similar in spirit to "SMS SOS" from 7/25 — the real estate goes to a gate, not a purchase.
- Compared to "10% off looks good on you" (8/1, 8.00/10) — a Subscribe & Save push — this is the second discount-mechanism email in five days that isn't a real, universal offer. That's a cadence problem worth flagging: subscribers who don't fit one of the five verified categories get two "discount" emails in a row that don't actually apply to them.

## What's working

- Hero visual is strong — clean product-adjacent skincare application shot, on-brand minimalist styling.
- Seasonal hook ("sharpened pencils, warm sweaters") is timely and well-written for early August back-to-school transition.
- The 3-step explainer (verify → get code → recurring monthly code) removes ambiguity about how the mechanism works.
- Five occupation CTAs are large, evenly spaced, and easy to tap — no crowding or overlap.
- No render bugs: clean image, no broken assets, no lorem ipsum, footer intact with social icons and legal copy.

## What's weak

- The subject and hero lead with "20% off" as if it's a universal offer, but it's gated behind third-party occupation verification — most subscribers can't act on it.
- Fine print at the bottom excludes Drunk Merch, bundles, Kamo Triple-Peptide Cover Drops, and gift cards, and can't be combined with other discounts — buried exclusions that undercut the "we appreciate you" framing.
- No specific product is featured anywhere in the email — it's entirely about the discount mechanism, so there's nothing for a non-qualifying subscriber to click into.
- No loyalty or general-subscriber benefit shown, so anyone outside the five categories gets zero value from this send.
- Back-to-back discount-mechanism emails (this + 8/1's Subscribe & Save push) risk fatigue for anyone not shopping around either mechanism.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split the send: subscribers who match a Reese/SheerID-eligible segment get this occupation-discount version; everyone else gets a real product-led seasonal email instead of a discount they can't use.
- 2. Feature at least one hero product (e.g., the seasonal "skincare refresh" language should link to an actual refresh routine/kit) so non-qualifying subscribers still have something to click.
- 3. Move the exclusions out of tiny footer fine print and into a single honest line near the offer ("Excludes Kamo Cover Drops, bundles & gift cards") so it doesn't read as buried.
- 4. Add a real deadline to the "limited time only" line — a date drives more urgency than vague phrasing.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Verified? That's 20% off, every month.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Get verified with SheerID, unlock 20% off — plus a new code monthly.`
