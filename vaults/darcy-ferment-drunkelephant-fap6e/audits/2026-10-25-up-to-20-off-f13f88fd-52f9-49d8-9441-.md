# Up to 20% off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth send in the Subscribe & Save push since 10/19. It is the cleanest version of the offer so far, because the countdown, the 15/20/10% ladder and the product lineup are all on one screen. The dark "HURRY, OFFER ENDS IN" bar (00 days, 20:27:39) is the strongest element, since it gives a real deadline. The deadline is real: the fine print says the offer ends Oct 27 at 2:59 am EST.
- The weaknesses are the subject line and repetition. "Up to 20% off" undersells and slightly misstates the offer. The discount is 15% on the first order, 20% on the second and 10% after that, and only on new subscriptions. A subscriber who has seen "15% off, you say?" and "20% off?" in the same week gets nothing new here. The hero headline, "AND YOU'LL NEVER RUN OUT AGAIN," starts mid-thought with no setup. It reads like the second half of a conversation I wasn't part of.
- Judgment: a competent last-day reminder that is fine as a final nudge but adds nothing to a week-long stream. The gift guide block dilutes the single ask.

## What's working

- The countdown banner puts the deadline at the top in high-contrast white on charcoal.
- The offer ladder is stated in plain bold type: 15% off the first delivery, 20% off the second, 10% after that.
- The product lineup (Protini, C-Firma, B-Hydra, T.L.C.) is crisp and on-brand, and the pastel colors against grey make a clear focal point.
- The hero is one idea with one outlined SHOP NOW button, so there is no competing CTA above the fold.
- The layout has no broken images, overlaps or gaps.

## What's weak

- The headline begins with "AND" and has no preceding line, so the opening reads as an orphaned sentence.
- "SHOP NOW" is a generic, thin outlined button. It is easy to miss and says nothing specific.
- The qualifiers are buried in small grey fine print at the bottom: new subscription orders only, not valid on past purchases, and not combinable with other offers.
- The gift guide block ("GIFT GUIDE ME") pulls attention away on the last day of the offer, and its text link is tiny.
- The product images are not individually clickable-looking and carry no names or prices, so I can't tell what I'd actually get.
- This is the sixth near-identical send since 10/19 ("15% off, you say?", "Do I hear 15% off?", "20% off?"), and nothing here escalates or changes.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rewrite the hero as a complete, self-contained pitch. For example, "Subscribe & Save: 15% off your first order, 20% off your second" with the countdown directly above it. Drop the dangling "And."
- 2. Use a solid filled button with specific copy, such as "Start my subscription" instead of "SHOP NOW".
- 3. Say "last chance" and "tonight" explicitly. On the final send, the urgency should be the headline rather than a small banner.
- 4. Add product names and a "from $" price under the hero lineup, with each one linked to its own subscribe page.
- 5. Move the key qualifier ("new subscriptions only") up beside the offer instead of leaving it in the footer.
- 6. Cut or move the gift guide block, or send it as a separate campaign.
- 7. Reduce cadence. Four sends over the week would have done the same job.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 12 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destinations, redirect chains, and status codes are unconfirmed.
- **Unlabeled tracking images:** two tracking images have no `alt` attribute:
  - `https://mi.drunkelephant.com/p/up/80acaf1c74f9e002/o.gif?mi_u=98161986&mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_subweekendssoon`
  - `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/?e=…&c=us_de_broad…`

- **Email address in a URL:** the everestengagement pixel carries the recipient address in plaintext as `e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app`. If these URLs are logged or forwarded, the address leaks through query strings.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Mixed content:** three images use `http://` and may be blocked or rewritten by clients:

  - `.../29f975e1-6f94-429c-ae2b-8ee6f90786cb.png` (TikTok)
