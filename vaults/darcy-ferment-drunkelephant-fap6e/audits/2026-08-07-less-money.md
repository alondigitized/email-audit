# Less money!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-07**
## Executive summary

- "Bundle Up, People!" is a clean, single-purpose bundle push — 25% off two curated AM/PM routines, plus a free-gift-with-purchase kicker up top. It's the most product-explanatory send from this brand in a while: instead of just listing SKUs, it walks through *how* to use them ("1 PUMP C-Luma + 1 PUMP Protini + 1 DROP B-Goldi"), which is a genuinely useful hook for a brand whose whole thing is routine-building.
- The subject line "Less money!" undersells it badly, though — it's vague, low-energy, and doesn't hint at skincare, bundles, or the free-gift offer that's actually the strongest hook in the email. Compare to 8/1's "10% off looks good on you," which at least got a number and a voice into the subject.
- The body execution is solid: clear value stamps ($179/$156), a legible "AM vs PM" structure, and a value-conscious CTA ("SHOP BUNDLES") repeated top and bottom. It's a tighter, more product-forward send than 7/27's "Our top shelf" catalog dump, but it's held back by weak top-of-funnel copy and a completely product-less hero image (just cream texture, no bundle photography).

## What's working

- Value-stamped bundle framing ($179 Value / $156 Value) makes the 25%-off math feel concrete rather than abstract.
- AM/PM structure with pump/drop counts gives a genuine usage instruction, not just a product list — useful and differentiated.
- Free deluxe-sample-plus-free-shipping banner at the very top adds a second, larger incentive layer above the fold.
- Two CTAs (top of bundle content and after both bundles) reduce scroll friction to convert.
- Clean, on-brand visual language — consistent product photography, consistent gray card backgrounds, legible typography throughout.

## What's weak

- Subject line "Less money!" is vague and low-signal — it doesn't say skincare, bundle, sale, or amount, and reads more like spam bait than a Drunk Elephant send.
- Hero image is just abstract cream swatch texture — no bundle products are shown until scrolling past the fold, so the "eye lands on the offer" test fails at first glance.
- No visible expiration/urgency date on the 25% bundle offer itself (only the free-gift terms carry a date, buried in fine print at the bottom).
- No social proof (ratings, review counts) on either bundle, despite this being a considered, multi-product purchase decision.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the subject line first — "Less money!" is the single biggest drag on this send; replace with something that names the mechanic (bundle, %, or gift).
- 2. Put a bundle product shot in the hero, not just cream texture — let people see what they're buying before they scroll.
- 3. Add a visible countdown/expiration to the 25%-off bundle offer near the top, not just in the fine print.
- 4. Add a one-line "why these two together" or a star-rating snippet under each bundle to build purchase confidence.
- **Subject Alt A:** `25% off, zero guesswork: shop the bundles`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your AM + PM routine, bundled and 25% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus a free gift on $85+ and free shipping on everything`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Two bundles, one easy routine — mix, apply, done`
