# Last day for 20% off
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-31**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth occupation-verification 20%-off send in six weeks, and it's the weakest execution of the mechanic yet. The countdown timer ("00 DAYS 14 HOURS 50 MINUTES 36 SECONDS") creates real urgency, but everything below it works against the offer: a Kamo cross-sell banner interrupts before the hero even loads, the actual CTA is a plain gray "GET VERIFIED" button that undersells a time-boxed discount, and then the same five occupation categories get repeated as a second block of buttons further down — redundant real estate that should have gone to product proof or the gift-with-purchase mechanic.
- Compare this to "Best time for 20% off…" (2026-08-26, 5/10) and "20% off is better than ever!" (2026-08-15, 6/10) — this SheerID play has now run three times with almost identical structure (hero copy, verify CTA, category buttons), and fatigue is setting in. The one genuinely new element is the Kamo Triple-Peptide free-gift tie-in, but it's buried as a secondary banner and mid-page module instead of leading.
- Judgement: functional but tired creative. The timer is doing most of the urgency work; the layout and copy haven't evolved since the last two sends in this series.

## What's working

- Countdown timer up top is a strong, credible urgency signal — specific hours/minutes remaining, not vague "ends soon" language.
- Model shot with product application is clean and on-brand, reinforcing the skincare-refresh narrative.
- Product grid at the bottom includes star ratings and "X people are checking this out" social proof, which is new/good relative to prior sends.
- Clear step-by-step "how you get 20%" instructions reduce friction for first-time SheerID verifiers.

## What's weak

- Primary CTA ("GET VERIFIED") is a flat gray button with no visual pop — it doesn't match the neon-green accent color used elsewhere, so it doesn't draw the eye first.
- The five occupation-category buttons are shown twice (once implied in copy, once as a full button block against a plain gray background) — visually repetitive and eats scroll real estate.
- Kamo banner at the very top pushes the seasonal 20%-off hero (the subject line's actual promise) below the fold on mobile.
- This is the third near-identical SheerID verification layout in six weeks — same five buttons, same copy beats ("teacher, student, first responder, military, healthcare") — creative fatigue is visible.
- Fine print at the bottom is dense and stacked (gift terms + discount terms + exclusions) — technically visible but easy to skip, bordering on buried.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make "GET VERIFIED" the visually dominant CTA — use the same neon-green from the header/banners so the eye lands on it first, not on the gray occupation-button block.
- 2. Cut the duplicate occupation-category button block — keep one instance, ideally the interactive one, and use the freed space to move the Kamo free-gift offer higher since it's the freshest hook in the send.
- 3. Move or shrink the top Kamo banner so the 20%-off hero (what the subject promises) is the first thing visible on mobile, not the second.
- 4. Given this is the third SheerID send in six weeks, vary the visual template — e.g., swap the five-button grid for a single dropdown/select to signal this isn't just a repeat of "Best time for 20% off…" and "20% off is better than ever!"

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 20% off code expires in 14 hours`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Verify in 60 seconds, save all month long`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: free Kamo tinting drops with select purchases`
