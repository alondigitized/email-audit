# Kamo the Killer
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-17**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Kamo Drops send in a week, and it's the strongest execution yet — "The secret weapon" (2026-08-09, 5/10) buried the product in ingredient theater, "Firmer skin?" (2026-08-13, 6/10) pivoted to a clinical claim, and now "Kamo the Killer" lands the clearest hero yet: a 25-shade clock-face product shot, two clean before/after model blocks with hard percentages, and an actual bare-face/after comparison further down. This is the send that finally shows the product *doing its job* rather than just asserting it.
- Where it stalls is the same place this brand keeps stalling: two "SHOP KAMO" buttons and one "SHOP NOW" all point at the same single SKU, there's no price or discount anywhere in the body, and the header banner promotes a *different* offer (free deluxe sample on $50+ subscriptions) that's disconnected from the Kamo push entirely. Strong hero, no reason to act today.

## What's working

- The 25-shade "clock" visual is a genuinely clever, scroll-stopping hero image that communicates range at a glance.
- Two model blocks pair a real face with a hard stat (100% radiance/pore improvement, 100% firmness, 91% texture) — concrete and legible.
- The bare-face/after comparison photo is honest, unretouched-looking proof, not a glamour shot.
- Shade-matching CTA ("FIND YOUR SHADE") plus a named shade (Medium 15W) makes this feel personalized and useful.
- Clean brand execution — no render bugs, no overlapping text, consistent green/cream palette throughout.

## What's weak

- No price, no percentage off, no discount anywhere in the body — this is a pure product-education email masquerading as urgency ("Killer").
- The top banner (free deluxe sample, subscription $50+) is a completely different offer than the rest of the email and creates split focus before the hero even loads.
- Three CTAs ("SHOP KAMO" x2, "SHOP NOW") all go to the same single product — no bundle, no cross-sell, no reason to explore further.
- Asterisked fine print for the free-gift offer is stacked at the very bottom in tiny type, separate from where it's promised at the top — a subscriber has to hunt for the actual terms.
- Subject "Kamo the Killer" promises drama the body doesn't really deliver — it's clinical stats and a product shot, not shock value.

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop the header banner or replace it with a Kamo-specific hook — right now it competes with the hero instead of reinforcing it. If free-sample-on-subscription is the real push this cycle, make that the subject; if Kamo is the push, cut the banner.
- 2. Add a visible price or intro offer near the top CTA — three identical "SHOP KAMO" buttons with zero pricing signal is the biggest drag on click-through here.
- 3. Move the free-gift terms directly under the banner claim, not buried in bottom-of-email legal copy.
- 4. Swap "Kamo the Killer" for language that matches what's actually inside — the email undersells itself by promising something punchier than the calm, clinical creative delivers.
- **Subject Alt A:** `25 shades. 100% saw results.`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Find your Kamo shade in 10 seconds`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Clinically proven coverage, 6-shade sample card included`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: free deluxe sample on $50+ subscriptions`
