# Homepage snapshot · www.drunkelephant.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Drunk Elephant is leading with what appears to be a reformulated T.L.C. Sukari Babyfacial launch — "An icon reimagined: The OG at-home 'facial,' now with an enhanced formula" — and as someone who buys this product monthly and reads every single reformulation note, that copy stopped me cold. The brand looks premium and editorial, clean lines, very on-brand aesthetic. But this feels like a teaser page: it surfaces the most important news in my skincare life and then leaves me hunting for a button to actually learn more.

## What's working

- **The Babyfacial hero**: This is the one product in my routine I would never switch away from. Seeing it front and center — and being told the formula changed — has my full attention before I even consciously decide to engage.
- **"An icon reimagined… now with an enhanced formula"**: That sub-copy is precision-targeted at obsessive repurchase customers. I need to know what changed immediately.
- **Free shipping banner**: $40+ is realistic for any single DE item. I'm not hunting for it; it's the first thing I see at the very top.
- **"Please exfoliate responsibly" wit**: Classic DE voice. It rewards the nerd who gets the joke. This is exactly the copy register I respond to.
- **Live Chat tab**: Visible on the right edge. For someone with ingredient questions, knowing I can ask someone right now is genuinely reassuring.

## What's weak

- **No CTA button visible**: I can see product imagery and sub-copy but I cannot find a tappable button that says "Shop" or "See What's New." I should not have to guess where to tap.
- **Zero formula detail above the fold**: "Enhanced formula" is the most consequential phrase on this page for me and there is no supporting context. What changed? AHAs? The pH? The percentages? I've been using this monthly for years — I need more than a teaser.
- **No price or product-page shortcut**: The hero shows the product but doesn't surface the price or confirm one tap gets me to the PDP. For a returning customer who just wants to confirm and reorder, that frictionizes what should be a 10-second decision.
- **No returning-customer acknowledgment**: DE knows I'm a repeat buyer (or should). No "back in stock," no "members save," no loyalty balance. This page treats me like a stranger.
- **Editorial image on the right**: The model applying product looks early-to-mid 20s. DE's core repeat-purchase customer for the Babyfacial skews 35–50. Small casting miss.

## Recommendations

- **Add a visible CTA button to the hero**: "Shop the New Babyfacial" or "See the Formula Update" — anything tappable with explicit copy, placed beneath the sub-headline.
- **Surface one formula-change detail above the fold**: Even one line — "Now with X% glycolic" or "Reformulated with [ingredient]" — would turn my curiosity into a tap instead of anxiety. Reformulation news without context makes loyal customers nervous.
- **Add a free-shipping confirmation to the hero or sub-banner**: "Ships free on this order" next to the product shot closes the loop for returning customers about to hit buy.
- **Introduce a returning-customer callout**: Even a "Your favorite, reimagined" banner or a loyalty-balance nudge would signal DE sees me as a known shopper, not a tourist.
