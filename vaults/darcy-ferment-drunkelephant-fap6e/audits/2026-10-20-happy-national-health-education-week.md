# Happy National Health Education Week!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a verification-gated discount dressed up as a holiday greeting. The hero is clean: a lilac Amino Rain mirror, big type, and "plus 15% off" in magenta. But the subject line sells none of it. The hook, a free mirror on $100+ orders plus 15% off for teachers, students, healthcare workers, first responders and military, is invisible until the open.
- The body is where the email loses momentum. The offer is restated in "We appreciate you!" copy, then five identical white outline buttons sit on a grey marble photo. The page then ends on fine print: "valid only until 10/23/2026 at 4:00 p.m. EST while supplies last, after reverts to 15% off." That reads as garbled, and the real deadline only appears at the very bottom.
- This is the seventh send in about 11 days (10/9 through 10/20). Against the Subscribe to Save send on 10/19 (7/10) and the Protini kits on 10/16 (7/10), this one is narrower and more friction-heavy. Anyone outside the five eligible groups gets nothing from it. Judgement: decent design, weak targeting, and a subject line that gives the reader no reason to open.

## What's working

- The hero is on-brand and legible. The lilac mirror and a magenta "FOR ALL!" and "plus 15% off," pull the eye to the offer first.
- The offer is concrete: a free Amino Rain mirror at $100+, plus 15% off.
- The "GET VERIFIED" button sits above the fold, and the three-step explainer (verify with SheerID, get a code, save monthly) is easy to follow.
- The five eligibility buttons are large and centered, and each names its group, so a qualifying reader knows where to tap.
- It's a clean render with no broken images or overlapping text, and the brand voice is consistent with prior sends.

## What's weak

- The subject is a generic greeting with no offer, product or urgency, so it gives no reason to open.
- The offer is gated to five groups, and the hero doesn't make clear that everyone else gets nothing. "FOR ALL!" contradicts that.
- Five identical outline buttons on a grey marble photo have no hierarchy and low contrast.
- The deadline (10/23, 4:00 p.m. EST) and the "reverts to 15% off" wording are buried in grey footer text, and the sentence is confusing.
- Verification through a third party (SheerID) adds friction before any product is shown, and no product images or reviews appear below the hero.
- Cadence is heavy: the 10/19 "Subscribe to Save" email went out only a day earlier.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the offer and the deadline in the subject, and make clear who it's for, so non-eligible readers can skip it. A segmented send would be better still.
- 2. Move the deadline (Oct 23, 4 p.m. EST) into the hero next to the offer, and fix the "reverts to 15%" sentence so it reads plainly.
- 3. Collapse the five buttons into one primary "GET VERIFIED" button, with the groups as a short text list or a smaller secondary row.
- 4. Add two or three product tiles (Protini, C-Firma, B-Hydra) with prices to show what the 15% applies to. Right now nothing in the body is shoppable.
- 5. Reduce frequency, or suppress this send for recipients who bought within the last few days.

# Technical Audit: Drunk Elephant, "Happy National Health Education Week!"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's link tracking, merge fields and rendering mostly work. The automated QA found 0 hard failures and 3 warnings. The real defects are three `http://` social icons, a recipient email address in a tracking URL, and a few accessibility and CSS problems in the head. Compliance and authentication can't be confirmed from the data given. The HTML source was truncated, so anything below the head is unverified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **12 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and 404s are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **The recipient's email address is in a tracking URL query string.** The pixel `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/?e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app&c=us_de_broad…` carries the address in cleartext. It will appear in proxy and server logs and in referrer data. A hashed or opaque ID would be safer.
- **Tracking uses two separate third-party and subdomain systems.** One is `mi.drunkelephant.com/p/up/80acaf1c74f9e002/o.gif?mi_u=98161986&mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_nathealthweeksh…`. The other is `drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com`. The second domain is not aligned with the `cheers.drunkelephant.com` sender domain.
- **Two tracking pixels have no `alt` attribute.** These are `o.gif` and the everestengagement image. They should use `alt=""` so screen readers skip them.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Three images use `http://`.** These are the Facebook, Instagram and TikTok icons, served from `http://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/lib/fe3211717064057c751c78/m/1/…png`. Gmail's image proxy typically rewrites these. Other clients may block them or show a mixed-content warning. The same host should serve HTTPS.
- **The viewport meta blocks zoom.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom in clients that honor it, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **The `<html>` tag has no `lang` attribute.** The opening tag is `<html xmlns=… xmlns:v=… xmlns:o=… xmlns:w=…>`.
- **The web-font `@import` rules are invalid and will be ignored.** The three `@import url(https://db.onlinewebfonts.com/…)` rules come after the `body{…}` rule in the same `<style>` block. CSS requires `@import` to come first.
- **Fonts are loaded from several hosts.** They come from `db.onlinewebfonts.com` and from versioned `drunkelephant.com` demandware static paths. Many clients strip `<link>` and `@font-face`, so fallbacks matter. The font stacks aren't visible in the truncated source.
- **Stray HTML comments sit inside the `@media (max-width:600px)` block.** Examples are `<!-- *[class=contenttable] {…} -->` and `<!-- *[class=mobile] {…} -->`. In some parsers these can invalidate the adjacent selector, such as `*[class=mobile1]`. This is likely but not confirmed. Remove the comments.
- **The `<title>` is generic.** It reads "Drunk Elephant" and doesn't match the subject line. This is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The recipient address is resolved in the tracking URL, and no unresolved tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRSTNAME]` appear in the visible source. The truncated body was not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them. Whether the sender sets them can't be confirmed from this data. If they are absent at the origin, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not captured.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, and the relay is the likely cause.
- **The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address can't be verified.** Both would sit in the footer, which is cut off in the truncated HTML.
- **The From domain is well-formed.** It is `info@cheers.drunkelephant.com`, a branded subdomain.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters can't be verified.** The 12 click-tracking links were skipped and the final landing URLs are not in the data.
- **The campaign is identified through vendor-specific parameters.** The visible example is `mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_nathealthweeksh…` on `mi.drunkelephant.com`. This is not standard `utm_*`. Whether `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` survive the redirect to drunkelephant.com can't be confirmed.
- **Landing page alignment is unverified** for the same reason.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the `http://` image sources.** Serve the three social icons from `https://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/…`.
2. **Stop putting the plaintext email in URLs.** Replace `e=<address>` in the everestengagement pixel with a hashed or opaque subscriber ID.
3. **Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.**
4. **Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta**, and add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
5. **Move the `@import` rules to the top of the `<style>` block**, or drop them in favor of the `<link>` tags. Delete the `<!-- … -->` comments inside the media query.
6. **Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` at the origin ESP.** Check the raw message before the relay, and check SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `cheers.drunkelephant.com`.
