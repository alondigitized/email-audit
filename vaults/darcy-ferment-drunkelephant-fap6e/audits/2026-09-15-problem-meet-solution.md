# Problem, meet solution.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuine break from the sale-code drumbeat — no "SECRET SALE," no countdown timer, no "20% off" banner for the first time since before 2026-09-05's "Lookitchoo, 20% off!" Instead it's a product-education send built around a "problem, meet solution" concierge concept: dull/dry/uneven/puffy skin each get a named product with a scannable two-bullet benefit. It's polaroid-and-washi-tape art direction that's clean and on-brand, and it's a smart way to reset after six straight discount emails without losing the reader entirely.
- The mechanic works better as strategy than as this specific execution. The problem-solution matching is a good hook, but there's no price anchor, no bundle logic, and no urgency anywhere until a small "Fall Skin, Sorted" GWP module near the bottom — which is the only real offer in the whole email and it's buried below four full problem/solution pairs. For a reader who isn't already loyal to the brand vocabulary (B-Goldi, Protini, C-Firma, Ceramighty), this reads as a lot of scrolling before any reason to act now.
- Net: solid creative variety after a repetitive stretch, but it under-monetizes the send by hiding the one concrete offer (spend $100+, get deluxe samples) at the bottom instead of leading with it.

## What's working

- Clear visual hierarchy per section — "problem" as a bold question, product name in brand-teal link color, two benefit bullets underneath.
- Polaroid/washi-tape photography style is distinctive and consistent across all four product shots.
- Two SHOP NOW CTAs bracket the main content (top and after the four pairings), so scanners have an easy out.
- The "you're gonna like these guys" module at the bottom adds real social proof — star ratings and "X people are checking this out" counts on all four tiles.
- Legal/fine print at the bottom clearly states sample thresholds and the exact promo window (9/12–9/21), which is honest and specific.

## What's weak

- The only actual offer (spend $100+ for deluxe samples) is a small, low-contrast module wedged between the shop grid and the "gonna like these guys" section — it should be the hero.
- No urgency signal anywhere above the fold; the 9/21 deadline only appears in 8pt fine print at the very bottom.
- Four sequential problem/solution blocks with near-identical layout make the email very long without pacing variety — by "Puffy?" the pattern has worn thin.
- No pricing or member/loyalty pricing shown anywhere, so there's no clear reason to click now vs. bookmark for later.
- Subject "Problem, meet solution." is vague and generic for a skincare brand — could apply to nearly any wellness or self-help email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Promote the "Spend $100+, get deluxe samples" offer into the hero position with the actual deadline (ends 9/21) visible — that's the one lever this email has and it's currently buried.
- 2. Compress the four problem/solution blocks into a single scrollable module (e.g., a 2x2 grid) so the email is skimmable in one screen instead of four full-screen scrolls.
- 3. Add a specific line under each product bullet indicating who it's for (skin type/age range) rather than just symptom — deepens relevance signal.
- 4. Pull the "1,235 people are checking this out" social-proof mechanic up near the top hero — it's a strong trust signal currently wasted at the bottom.
