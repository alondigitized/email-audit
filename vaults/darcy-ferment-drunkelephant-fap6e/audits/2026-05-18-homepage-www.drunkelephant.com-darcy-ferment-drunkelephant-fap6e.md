# Homepage snapshot · www.drunkelephant.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- Okay, they put the T.L.C. Sukari Babyfacial front and center — the one product I buy on autopilot every single month — and they're leading with a reformulation story. That is genuinely smart brand work. This reads like a science-forward, ingredient-led brand that knows its loyalists and is betting the homepage on a product relaunch rather than a seasonal promo sweep.

## What's working

- **T.L.C. Sukari Babyfacial hero shot**: Two-unit product composition (standing pump + tilted bottle) is clean and ingredient-art adjacent. For someone who reads every reformulation note, this is a hook, not just decor.
- **"Exfoliate responsibly" headline**: The word "responsibly" is doing heavy lifting — it flags AHA/BHA risk awareness without going clinical. That's the Suspicious 6 language center of my brain lighting up.
- **"An icon reimagined: The OG at-home 'facial,' now with an enhanced formula"**: This is direct communication to loyalists. It acknowledges heritage, signals a real change, and doesn't spin the reformulation as purely a marketing refresh. I respect that.
- **Free shipping at $40 banner**: $40 threshold is a gimme when the Babyfacial retails well above that. No hoop-jumping required.
- **Inset lifestyle image**: The small shot of a woman applying product grounds the science in real skin use. It's not a makeup ad or a 22-year-old influencer shot — it reads functional.

## What's weak

- **No visible CTA button**: The entire above-fold experience is product + copy + no tap target. I have to scroll or guess to move forward. That's a miss for conversion intent.
- **Nav is icon-only**: Hamburger + search + location + smiley + bag. Nothing labeled. On mobile especially, I shouldn't have to decipher whether the smiley is loyalty, chat support, or aesthetic.
- **"Exfoliate responsibly" is partially clipped**: The "E" is cut off on the left edge — looks like a scroll-reveal animation that hasn't triggered yet or a viewport crop issue. First impression reads as a layout glitch.
- **No ingredient or formula callout**: If you're telling me the formula changed, show me one data point. "Enhanced formula" without "now with X% glycolic" or "PHAs added" is marketing copy, not science copy. I want proof.
- **No reformulation reassurance**: Loyalists like me are inherently suspicious of reformulations. The page tells me it changed but doesn't tell me it's better or equivalent. That anxiety is not addressed above the fold.

## Recommendations

- **Add a ghost CTA below the hero headline**: Something like "Shop the New Formula →" or "See What Changed →" — one tap, above the fold, anchored directly under "Exfoliate responsibly."
- **Replace "enhanced formula" with one specific signal**: Pull a single ingredient or percentage change into the hero subhead. "Now with PHAs added" or "15% AHA/BHA — same, reformulated base" converts curious loyalists. Right now I'm intrigued but not yet reassured.
- **Label the loyalty icon**: Put "Rewards" under the smiley or replace it with a rewards-balance chip for logged-in users. The unlabeled icon is invisible value.
- **Fix or delay the "Exfoliate responsibly" clip**: Whether it's a scroll-reveal trigger or a viewport edge issue, the cropped capital E reads as broken on first load. Either extend the animation start point or pad the container so the headline lands whole.
