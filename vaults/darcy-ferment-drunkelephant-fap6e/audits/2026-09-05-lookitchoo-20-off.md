# Lookitchoo, 20% off!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth "20% off" send in five weeks, and it's leading with the weakest hook yet — "Keep it between us" / "our secret sale is for you" is the exact conspiratorial framing Drunk Elephant already used on 2026-09-04's "Well damn, 20% off," just one day earlier. There's no SheerID gate this time, which is a relief after four occupation-verification sends, but the code (DRUNKVIP20) with no visible expiration on the hero itself makes it feel less special than the brand's own copy claims.
- Where this send actually earns points is the second module: a free 5-piece set ($36 value) with orders $110+, tied to "summer ending" seasonal messaging that's genuinely current for early September. That's a real second offer, clearly priced, and it's the most concrete thing in the email — better than the vague "20% off your Drunk go-tos" framing up top.
- Cadence is the real problem here. Five near-identical discount emails in five weeks is fatigue territory, and this one doesn't bring a new mechanic (no quiz, no countdown, no gate) to justify another send — it's coasting on tone alone.

## What's working

- Free 5-piece gift-with-purchase ($36 value, $110+ threshold) is a specific, well-priced second offer with a clear visual price callout.
- "Summer Ending? Not on our watch." ties the free-gift module to a real, current seasonal moment.
- Product grid at the bottom includes star ratings and "X people are checking this out" social-proof signals, which the last few sends often skipped.
- Clean layout, no render bugs — hero, product shot, and grid all display correctly with no overlapping text or broken images.
- Single clear CTA button ("SHOP NOW") directly under the hero offer, visible without much scrolling.

## What's weak

- The "secret sale" / "keep it between us" conceit is a direct rerun of 2026-09-04's tone just one day later — no new creative idea.
- No visible expiration or countdown on the hero offer itself — all the real terms (dates, exclusions) are buried in dense fine print at the very bottom.
- This is the fifth 20%-off email in five weeks with no new mechanic (no quiz, no gate, no urgency timer) — pure repetition risk.
- The free-gift threshold ($110+) and the fine print exclusions (no Kamo Cover Drops, no bundles, no gift cards) aren't surfaced anywhere near the offer itself.

## Recommendations

- 1. Kill the "secret sale" framing — it was just used yesterday. If the code is genuinely evergreen, don't dress it up as exclusive; if it's not, add a real countdown to the hero.
- 2. Surface the free-gift threshold and exclusions above the fold instead of leaving them in bottom fine print — "spend $110, get a $36 gift" is a strong enough second CTA to deserve its own visible terms.
- 3. Space out sends — five 20%-off emails in five weeks trains subscribers to wait for the next one instead of acting now.
- 4. Add a real urgency element (countdown timer or explicit "ends [date]" near the code) since the current copy implies urgency ("summer ending") without giving a deadline.
