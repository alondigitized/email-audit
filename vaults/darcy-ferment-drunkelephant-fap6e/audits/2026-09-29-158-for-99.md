# $158 for $99?
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- The subject line promises a price hook, "$158 for $99?", and the body never delivers it. I can't find "$158", "$99", "save", "value" or a percentage anywhere in the render. It's a generic "2026 Holiday Collection / Drunk Elephant Atelier" launch. A subscriber who opens on the price curiosity gets a lookbook with no prices.
- The creative itself is polished. The pastel hero, the stacked kit boxes, the yellow band and the alternating image/text modules are clean and on-brand. But it's the fourth or fifth kit-and-bundle push in about two weeks, after the 2026-09-27 "How about a great skin day?" holiday kit send and the 2026-09-25 bundle email. Nothing here says why this is different or why to act now.
- Verdict: good visual execution wrapped around a broken promise. The subject is stronger than the body, and the body doesn't support it.

## What's working

- The hero is instantly legible: a stacked kit shot, a "2026 HOLIDAY COLLECTION" banner, and one white "SHOP HOLIDAY" button in a yellow block.
- The four modules alternate image left and right, so the scroll has rhythm and each kit gets its own clear name (Gala Glow, Couture Shows, Maison Moisture, Finishing Touches).
- The copy has personality, e.g. "Our limited-edition holiday collection is here. And it is spectacular." and "Prêt-à-porter skincare."
- There are no broken images, overlaps or layout glitches. The footer is clean.
- "Limited-edition" gives a mild scarcity cue.

## What's weak

- The subject's "$158 for $99?" is not paid off anywhere in the render. There's no price, no strikethrough and no savings callout, so the email undercuts its own hook.
- Every module CTA is a small underlined "SHOP NOW". That's generic, low-contrast and easy to miss, and it doesn't name the kit or the price.
- Nothing is time-bounded and there's no member or loyalty benefit. There's no reason to click today rather than next week.
- The tagline ("A personalized stylist for dewy, glowy, radiant skin") is vague filler between the hero and the modules. There's no product detail, skin-type guidance or reviews to help a shopper choose between four kits.
- The footer is tiny and low-contrast, and the two "SHOP HOLIDAY" buttons are identical, so the closing CTA adds nothing.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Make the body match the subject.** Put the actual deal in the hero: "Haute Hydration kit: $99 (a $158 value)". Show the $158 struck through with $99 beside it on each module. This one change lifts open-to-click more than anything else.
- 2. **Replace the vague "Shop now" links with specific CTAs.** Use "Get the Gala Glow kit — $99" and put a price on every module.
- 3. **Add a credible deadline or stock cue** (e.g. "Limited-edition, while sets last") and, if applicable, a members-only line.
- 4. **Add social proof.** A star rating or "best seller" tag on at least one kit would help.
- 5. **Cut the tagline and put a one-line "who it's for" under each kit instead.**
- 6. **Use one button style for all module CTAs**, sized for thumbs on mobile.
- **Subject Alt A:** `The $99 holiday kit worth $158`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Save $59 on Haute Hydration`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Limited-edition holiday sets, priced below the sum of their parts.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** Both pixels above (`o.gif` and the `everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/` image) have no alt attribute. Add `alt=""` to mark them as decorative.
- **Insecure images:** Three footer social icons load over `http://`. Some clients may block or rewrite them, and they can trigger mixed-content warnings.
  - `http://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/lib/fe3211717064057c751c78/m/1/cc02d07d-c9bb-4057-b5d7-62f3bec2c521.png` (Facebook)
  - `…/9b452915-64bf-4b29-b08b-c4a42802457a.png` (Instagram)
  - `…/29f975e1-6f94-429c-ae2b-8ee6f90786cb.png` (TikTok)
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1" />` disables pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **No document language:** `<html xmlns=… >` has no `lang` attribute, and `<title>` is only "Drunk Elephant".
- **Invalid `@import` placement:** The three `@import url(https://db.onlinewebfonts.com/…)` rules come after the `body {…}` rule in the `<style>` block. `@import` must come first, so browsers ignore them. The same fonts are also loaded through `<link>` tags and `@font-face`, so the imports are redundant.
- **Third-party font hosting:** The Brown Bold, Light and Thin fonts load from `db.onlinewebfonts.com`. Most email clients ignore web fonts, and the third-party host adds a render dependency.
- **Versioned font URLs:** The BrownRegular font uses versioned Demandware static paths (`v1719565389232`), which may break when the site deploys.
