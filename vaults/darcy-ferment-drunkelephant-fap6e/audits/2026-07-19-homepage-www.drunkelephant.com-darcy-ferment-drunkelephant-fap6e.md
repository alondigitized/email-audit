# Homepage snapshot · www.drunkelephant.com · 2026-07-19
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-19**
## Executive summary

- This is unmistakably DE — that pale blue, the product-as-sculpture photography, the cheeky-but-science-y copy voice. They're pushing B-Hydra right now, which is a solid workhorse pick for summer, and the "hydrate responsibly" angle is exactly the kind of winking skincare nerd bait that works on me. But above the fold, this is more mood board than storefront — I can see the product and I can feel the brand, but I cannot find a button to buy anything.

## What's working

- **The $40 free-shipping banner** — sits right at the top, impossible to miss, and for anyone buying a full-size B-Hydra ($52) that threshold is crossed before I even add anything else
- **The B-Hydra product photography** — the stacked serum-on-serum visual is science-lab-meets-art-director; it tells me this is a hero product right now, and I know exactly what it is without reading the label
- **"You dew you: Your daily dose of vitamin B to hydrate and illuminate thirsty skin"** — this copy hits the right register: playful wordplay + functional ingredient callout in one sentence. DE knows their customer speaks both fluently
- **The "hydrate responsibly" carousel headline** — classic DE. Science + snark = I'm listening

## What's weak

- **No CTA button above the fold** — I can see the product, I can see the copy, but where do I tap? There is no "Shop B-Hydra" or "Build Your Routine" button anywhere in this viewport. A returning customer who wants to restock has to go hunt in the hamburger menu
- **Hamburger-only nav** — zero quick-access category strips or tabs visible. If I want to jump to Acids or Mini Sets, I'm digging through a menu. On mobile that's friction I don't have patience for when I know exactly what I want
- **No loyalty/rewards callout** — DE has a loyalty program. I'm a monthly repurchaser. Show me my points somewhere up here
- **Carousel state mid-scroll** — the "ase hydrate responsibly" headline being partially clipped is technically a carousel, but in a screenshot it reads sloppy. The arrow controls are tiny and the copy feels amputated
- **No social proof in viewport** — not a single star rating, review count, or "bestseller" badge. The product photography is gorgeous but nothing tells a first-time buyer that 40,000 people swear by this serum

## Recommendations

- **Add a persistent "Shop Now" or "Restock B-Hydra" CTA button** directly on the hero carousel card — tapping the product image is not a call to action, it's a hope
- **Surface the loyalty tier or points balance** in the top nav (a small "You have 340 points" chip) so returning customers feel seen immediately
- **Add a horizontal category scroll strip** below the hero (Moisturizers · Acids · Sets · Minis) — one tap to where I want to go, no hamburger required
- **Pin a "Bestseller" or "#1 Hydrating Serum" badge** on the B-Hydra product image — the photography earns the brand trust but social proof converts the click
