# Homepage snapshot · www.drunkelephant.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- This is Drunk Elephant's homepage doing exactly one thing: announcing a reformulation of T.L.C. Sukari Babyfacial, which happens to be the product I replace every single month. The brand looks premium, minimal, very pink — on-point for the DE aesthetic — but the execution above the fold is half a slide-deck: a gorgeous product shot, a science-lite headline, and then… nothing to tap. For someone who wants the full reformulation breakdown *immediately*, this hero teases without delivering.

## What's working

- **T.L.C. Sukari Babyfacial as the hero product** — This is the right product to lead with for a lapsed or active DE customer. I see those pink pumps and I'm already reading.
- **"An icon reimagined… now with an enhanced formula"** — This is the reformulation signal I hunt for. DE is surfacing it on the homepage, not burying it in a product-page footnote.
- **"Exfoliate responsibly" headline** — Fits DE's voice perfectly. "Responsibly" is a nod to not over-exfoliating, which is Suspicious-6-adjacent thinking. It speaks my language without being preachy.
- **Free shipping $40+ banner** — Visible, prominently pinned at the top, threshold is easily clearable in a single Babyfacial restock.
- **Carousel indicators (pause/back/forward)** — Signals there's more content to explore, not a dead-end single slide.

## What's weak

- **No above-the-fold CTA button** — There is literally no tappable button in this viewport. The hero has copy and an image but I'm expected to figure out what to tap. For a reformulation announcement I'm primed to read, this is a missed layup.
- **Hero headline is cropped** — "xfoliate responsibly" has its first letter cut off on the left edge. On a mobile viewport this reads sloppy, especially for a brand with this aesthetic precision.
- **No formula details in hero sub-copy** — "Enhanced formula" tells me nothing. What changed? More AHAs? New BHAs ratio? I want the science shorthand right here. "Now with 25% AHA + PHAs and a reduced fragrance profile" would actually land for someone like me.
- **No loyalty or rewards callout** — I'm a subscriber-tier customer and there's zero acknowledgment of that relationship above the fold.
- **Inset model image is too small** — The right-side lifestyle shot (someone applying product to their face) is thumbnail-sized. It adds visual noise without adding information.

## Recommendations

- **Add a CTA button directly in the hero** — "See What Changed" or "Shop the New Formula" would close the loop on the reformulation tease and give my thumb somewhere to go.
- **Fix the cropped headline** — Full text should be "Exfoliate responsibly" — the leftmost "E" is clipped. This is a one-line padding fix and it's distracting.
- **Add one science data point to the sub-copy** — Replace "enhanced formula" with the specific ingredient or % change. Even a badge like "Now pH-optimized" would satisfy the science-nerd contingent without cluttering the hero.
- **Surface a loyalty/subscriber hook in the announcement bar or hero** — A second pill in the announcement banner that says "Subscribe & save 15%" or "Refill Club pricing" would activate repeat-purchase intent immediately for customers like me.
