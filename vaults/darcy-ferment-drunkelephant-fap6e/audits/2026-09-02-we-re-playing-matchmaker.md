# We’re playing matchmaker
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-02**
## Executive summary

- This is Drunk Elephant's sixth Kamo Drops send in under a month, and it finally cracks the tool the brand should have led with: a shade-finder quiz with a full diverse-arm swatch chart and a real bare-face/after comparison. Compared to "Firming, smoothing skincare" (2026-08-21, 6/10) and the launch-hype send (2026-08-11, 7/10), this is the most conversion-oriented execution — it solves the actual objection ("will this match my skin?") instead of restating clinical claims.
- The problem is structural: the shade finder is the whole email, but the CTA that gets you there ("Find Your Shade") sits above a wall of swatch arms with no button beneath it, and the real purchase driver — the $36-value free gift with $110+ orders — is buried at the very bottom in a cramped teal-on-white block that looks like an afterthought, not the close.
- This is a solid mid-pack send: strong core idea, weak hierarchy and pacing. Fix the CTA placement and promote the gift-with-purchase and this jumps a full point.

## What's working

- The shade-finder concept with actual arm swatches across five skin tones is genuinely useful, differentiated content — not another generic promo banner.
- The bare-face/after comparison photo is a clear, non-gimmicky before/after that shows real product performance.
- "MEET YOUR MATCH" is a strong, on-brand headline that ties subject line to hero copy cleanly.
- Product packaging shot with texture swatches gives a tactile, premium feel consistent with the brand.

## What's weak

- No second CTA button after the swatch section or the bare-face/after photos — a reader scrolling past "Find Your Shade" has nothing to click until the very bottom.
- The $36-value gift-with-purchase offer is visually the weakest element in the email despite being the strongest concrete incentive (free 5-piece set at $110+) — small text, no button styling, no bold visual separation.
- The top gray banner ("NEW KAMO TRIPLE-PEPTIDE COVER DROPS IS MAKING HEADLINES") is generic press-bait copy that doesn't match the "matchmaker" personalization angle of the subject.
- Long single-column stack (swatch grid → product shot → before/after → gift block) makes this a heavy scroll with only one real CTA near the top.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a second CTA button ("Shop Kamo Drops" or "Find Your Shade") directly under the before/after photos — that's the moment of highest purchase intent and there's currently nothing to click.
- 2. Redesign the gift-with-purchase block as a proper hero band with a visible button — right now a $36 free-gift offer is the strongest lever in the email and it reads like fine print.
- 3. Swap the generic press-bait top banner for something that reinforces the shade-match/personalization hook, e.g. "Not sure which shade is you? Let's find out."
- 4. Tighten the shade swatch section — five full arm images is a lot of scroll before the payoff; consider a horizontal scroll or condensed grid on mobile.
