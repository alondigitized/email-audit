# The ultimate hack?
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-24**
## Executive summary

- "Reboot Camp" is a solid editorial concept — a three-product overnight reset routine that naturally packages T.L.C. Framboos, B-Hydra, and Lala Retro into a logical exfoliate → hydrate → moisturize stack. The 35% off trios offer buried two-thirds down is the strongest commercial hook in the email, and the GWP bag (spend $30+) at the close adds a second conversion incentive. ## What's working

- **Reboot Camp narrative is tight.** "Mix these guys together, apply, and wake up with refreshed, rejuvenated skin" makes the overnight-reset use case instantly legible.
- **Ingredient copy is genuinely differentiated.** Each product gets a distinct functional sentence (12% AHA + 1% SA for Framboos, HA serum for B-Hydra, ceramide blend for Lala Retro) — that's product detail the rubric rewards.
- **35% off trios is a real offer.** Not a percentage-off-everything blur; it's specifically positioned as "less is a good thing" with a playful tone consistent with DE's voice.
- **The bag GWP is eye-catching.** The lavender "Making It Rain" bag visual stands out from the white/pink palette and gives a second reason to click even for someone who doesn't want the trios.
- **No spam signals, clean typographic hierarchy.** The email reads well; Drunk Elephant's signature bold-pink-on-white aesthetic is intact.

## What's weak

- **Subject line-to-body disconnect is severe.** "The ultimate hack?" appears nowhere in the email creative. The body says "Reboot Camp" and "overnight reset" — neither of which maps to "hack." Curiosity bait that doesn't resolve inside the email destroys trust.
- **The strongest offer (35% off) is buried below three product-description modules.** Subscribers who skim the hero and nothing else see no price signal — the subject gave them none either.
- **"SHOP AW" button is a broken render** in the Making It Rain section. Whatever the intended copy, it's visually broken.
- **B-Hydra fatigue is real.** This is the fourth email featuring B-Hydra in 12 days. Even as part of a trio, it visually anchors this email to the same product cadence that's been running since June 13.
- **No deadline or urgency tied to the 35% offer.** The offer stands alone without a "ends Sunday" or "while supplies last" signal, which deflates click momentum.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[HIGH] HTML comment syntax inside `<style>` block**
```css
<!-- *[class=contenttable] { width:414px !important;} -->
<!-- *[class=mobile] { width:414px !important;height: auto !important;} -->
```
HTML comment delimiters (`<!-- -->`) inside a `<style>` block are invalid. Some email clients (particularly older Outlook and Yahoo) may expose or misparse these as literal text or drop the surrounding rules entirely.

**[HIGH] `maximum-scale=1` in viewport meta**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
Blocks user-initiated zoom on iOS. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) violation and also penalized by Gmail's accessibility scanner.

**[MEDIUM] `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` on wildcard selector**
```css
*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; ...}
```
Value should be `100%`, not `none`. Setting `none` disables iOS automatic font scaling entirely, causing text to render at unreadable sizes on small screens. Correct value: `-webkit-text-size-adjust:100%`.

**[MEDIUM] Triple font loading — redundant requests**
The same three Brown font variants (Bold, Light, Thin) are loaded three ways simultaneously:
1. `<link>` tag from `db.onlinewebfonts.com`
2. `@import` inside `<style>` from `db.onlinewebfonts.com`
3. `@font-face` block from `db.onlinewebfonts.com`

This fires 6 redundant external HTTP requests before any body content renders. Remove the `<link>` tags and `@import` rules; keep only the `@font-face` declarations.

**[MEDIUM] Brand fonts served from third-party CDN for 3 of 4 variants**
`BrownBold`, `BrownLight`, and `BrownThin` load from `db.onlinewebfonts.com` — a font-redistribution CDN with no SLA. `BrownRegular` correctly self-hosts from `drunkelephant.com/on/demandware.static/...`. The three CDN-hosted variants should be migrated to the same self-hosted path.

**[LOW] Missing `lang` attribute on `<html>` element**
```html
<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" ...>
```
No `lang="en"` present. Required for screen reader language detection and flagged by accessibility validators (Litmus, Email on Acid).

**[LOW] `<title>` is non-descriptive**
```html
<title>Drunk Elephant</title>
```
Screen readers and some clients (Apple Mail) expose the `<title>` as the document label. Should reflect the email subject or campaign purpose.
