# Last day: FREE $60 gift
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth Singles Day send in a row with the same offer, and the only new thing is the countdown banner. The timer reads 00 days, 15 hours, 53 minutes, so the urgency is real, and it is the strongest element here. But the body is the same four-piece sample set at $100 and puffer tote at $150 that went out on 11/5, 11/7 and 11/9. A subscriber who has seen all three has nothing new to learn.
- The subject "Last day: FREE $60 gift" is a real improvement over the earlier vague ones. It is concrete and time-bound. The problem is that the email never says "$60" or "free" anywhere, and the gift only comes with a $100 spend. "FREE" is a stretch, and the $60 value never shows up in the body to back it up. A subscriber who opens on that promise has to dig through the fine print to find the condition.
- The 20% off Kamo + moisturizer bundle sits in the dark banner and is the only true price cut. It's buried in small caps and competes with the gift offer, so the email carries two offers with no clear lead. Overall this is a competent last-chance reminder, but it is stale and cluttered. I'd put it around 6.

## What's working

- The live countdown banner (00 : 15 : 53 : 29) makes the deadline credible and visible at the very top.
- The "Last day" subject framing matches the email's own urgency.
- The hero is clean. The sample products on the pedestal show what you get, and the gray-on-white styling is on brand.
- The lavender and silver puffer tote is a strong visual and a fun, giftable reward.
- The gift thresholds ($100 sample set, $150 tote) are stated in plain language in the body copy.

## What's weak

- The offer is a repeat of the 11/5, 11/7 and 11/9 sends. The creative is essentially unchanged, so this is fatigue on top of fatigue.
- The subject promises a "FREE $60 gift", but the email never mentions $60 or "free". The gift requires a $100 purchase, so the subject overpromises.
- Two offers compete. The 20% Kamo bundle is in small banner text and the gift is in the hero, so neither wins.
- The tote section has a lot of white space and a floating "SHOP NOW" button over the image. The tote takes up about 450px of scroll for a $150 threshold most people won't reach.
- The Atelier block at the bottom is off-message for a last-day push, and its small "SHOP NOW" text link is weak.
- The key terms are in tiny gray fine print: gift minimums, the 11/12 2:59 a.m. ET cutoff and the exclusions.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one offer per send. For a last-day email, lead with the gift at $100 and make the $60 value visible in the hero ("$60 value, yours free with $100"). Keep the 20% Kamo bundle as a single secondary line.
- 2. Make the CTA copy specific. Replace the three generic "SHOP NOW" buttons with "Claim my free gift" in the hero and "Add the tote at $150" in the tote section. A single bold button is better than three small outlined ones.
- 3. Tighten the tote block. Cut the height by half and put the CTA below the image instead of on top of it.
- 4. Cut or move the Atelier block. It belongs in a post-holiday send, not a last-day push.
- 5. Put the real deadline in clear text ("Ends 2:59 a.m. ET tomorrow"). Don't leave it in the fine print.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links not verified.** 14 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, so landing destinations and redirect health are unconfirmed.
- **Recipient email in a pixel URL.** The image `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/?e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app&c=us_de_broad…` puts the address in a plaintext query string. It will appear in proxy, CDN and server logs.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text** on two images: `o.gif` and the `everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/` pixel. Both look like tracking pixels. They should carry `alt=""`, and they should be `width="1" height="1"` if they aren't already.
- **Non-HTTPS images.** The Facebook, Instagram and TikTok icons load over `http://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/lib/fe3211717064057c751c78/m/1/…png` (`cc02d07d-…`, `9b452915-…`, `29f975e1-…`). Clients may block them or show mixed-content warnings.
- **Invalid `@import` placement.** The three `@import url(https://db.onlinewebfonts.com/…)` rules come after the `body {…}` rule inside `<style>`. CSS requires `@import` to come first, so they are ignored. The `<link>` tags in `<head>` and the `@font-face` rules carry the font loading instead.
- **Third-party font dependency.** The `Brown Bold`, `Light` and `Thin` fonts load from `db.onlinewebfonts.com`. Most clients strip `<link>` and `@font-face`, and the font host adds an external dependency and a tracking surface.
- **Malformed CSS.** The HTML comments `<!-- *[class=contenttable] {…} -->` sit inside the `@media` block. They are harmless in most clients but can break parsing in some.
- **Mobile sizing.** `.vouchertext4` and `.fsname` set `max-width:15px` and `max-width:12px` under the media queries. That looks very restrictive and may clip text on mobile. I can't confirm this without a render, and the HTML is truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[[…]]`) are visible in the supplied source. The only personalization seen is the resolved address in the pixel URL (see section 2).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `cheers.drunkelephant.com` should be checked against the raw headers.
- **Body footer not verifiable.** The HTML was truncated before the footer, so the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters unverified.** The 14 click links were not probed, and no `utm_*` parameters are visible in the pixel URLs.
- **Campaign naming.** The visible tags use `mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_singlesdayends_`. The campaign name refers to Singles' Day ends, while the subject says "Last day: FREE $60 gift". I can't confirm a mismatch without seeing the landing pages.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Switch the social icon URLs from `http://` to `https://`. The same host `image.cheers.drunkelephant.com` likely supports TLS.
2. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels, with explicit 1×1 dimensions.
3. Replace the plaintext `e=<email>` in the pixel URL with an opaque or hashed ID.
4. Move the `@import` rules to the top of `<style>`, or drop them in favor of the `@font-face` rules. Add web-safe font fallbacks.
5. Remove the HTML comments from inside the `@media` block.
6. Re-run QA against the raw `.eml` to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
7. Expand the QA probe to cover the 14 skipped tracking links. Follow the redirects to the final URL and check UTM parameters and landing-page status.
