# Up to 40% off. Pass the savings.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the Cyber Week sale, now in its fourth day, and it carries two offers at once. The hero says "CYBER WEEK 30% OFF SITEWIDE." A small banner above it says "UP TO 40% OFF, Last Chance! While supplies last." The subject leads with the 40%, but the body leads with 30%, and the 40% only applies to a Last Chance subset. A subscriber who clicks on "40%" lands on a 30% hero.
- The layout is strong. The pink ornaments, the three-jar hero and the teal copy block make it clean and recognizable. The free Hydra and Bright kit at $100+ is the best hook in the email. It sits below the fold in a small right-aligned block with a gray "SHOP NOW" link. The product grid has real prices, strikethroughs and "people are checking this out" social proof, which is useful.
- Overall it is a competent send with a muddled message. It repeats the 11/25 "Up to 40% off." send, and it is the fifth or sixth Cyber Week email in five days. The offer hierarchy is the problem. The creative is fine.

## What's working

- The "CYBER WEEK 30% OFF SITEWIDE" hero is large and unmistakable, and the eye lands on it first.
- The product grid shows concrete sale prices with strikethroughs, for example Protini at $69.30, down from $99.
- Star ratings and "1,512 people are checking this out" add social proof and mild urgency.
- The free gift callout ("FREE GIFT VALUE $32") with the Hydra and Bright kit gives a reason to spend $100+.
- The sender name "Drunk Elephant Cyber Week" is recognizable, and the pink and ornament creative matches the earlier sends.

## What's weak

- The 40% and 30% offers compete. The subject and banner say up to 40%, but the hero says 30%, and the 40% terms are only in the fine print.
- The yellow-green subscription banner (30% off a first subscription order and free shipping) adds a third offer before the hero.
- Every "SHOP NOW" is a small text link, and the only boxed button is in the teal block. The "Last Chance" banner CTA is easy to miss.
- The free gift is a small block below the fold, and its $100 threshold is only explained in the footnote.
- The footer fine print is dense. It lists exclusions such as gift cards, Trunk 7.1 and Dopp bundles, and a one-per-order limit.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one headline offer. Keep "30% off sitewide" as the hero and make the 40% Last Chance items a separate, clearly labeled section. Drop the subscription banner from this send.
- 2. Move the free gift up and make it a button: "Spend $100, get a free $32 kit." Put the threshold in the visible copy.
- 3. Turn the "SHOP NOW" links into buttons, and make the CTAs specific, for example "Shop moisturizers."
- 4. Add a deadline, such as "Ends tonight 11:59 PM ET" in the hero. It is only in the footnote now.
- 5. Vary the creative day to day. This is the same ornament and jar imagery as the 11/24 and 11/25 sends.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is mostly standard ESP (Marketo/Cheetah-style) output with a few real defects: non-HTTPS images, missing alt text on two tracking pixels, a viewport that blocks zoom, and a recipient email address in a tracking URL. The header-based compliance checks (List-Unsubscribe, authentication) can't be confirmed, because the relay appears to strip those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **18 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped them because they are click-redirect domains, so none of their destinations or HTTP statuses are verified.
- **PII in the pixel URL.** The pixel at `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/?e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app&c=us_de_broad…` carries the recipient's plain-text email address in the query string. The `mi.drunkelephant.com/p/up/80acaf1c74f9e002/o.gif?mi_u=98161986&mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_bfcmreminderam_…` pixel carries a user ID (`mi_u`). Both URLs can end up in proxy and CDN logs.
- **Two tracking vendors.** The email loads two separate pixel and tracking stacks (`mi.drunkelephant.com` and `everestengagement.com`).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** `o.gif` and the `everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/` image have no `alt` attribute. Both are tracking pixels, so the impact is low. Adding `alt=""` and explicit `width="1" height="1"` is the correct fix.
- **Non-HTTPS images.** Three social icons load over `http://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/lib/fe3211717064057c751c78/m/1/`:
  - Facebook: `cc02d07d-c9bb-4057-b5d7-62f3bec2c521.png`
  - Instagram: `9b452915-64bf-4b29-b08b-c4a42802457a.png`
  - TikTok: `29f975e1-6f94-429c-ae2b-8ee6f90786cb.png`

  Clients that block mixed content or proxy images may drop them.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Web fonts.** Fonts load through `<link>` and `@import` from `db.onlinewebfonts.com` (Brown Bold, Light, Thin), and `@font-face` points at `drunkelephant.com/on/demandware.static/…`.
  - Most clients ignore these, and Gmail strips them entirely.
  - The email depends on third-party domains for fonts. The `@import` lines also sit after the `body{}` rule, where CSS requires them to come first, so they're invalid and ignored.
- **Malformed CSS.** Inside the `@media (max-width: 600px)` block there are HTML comments, e.g. `<!-- *[class=contenttable] { width:414px !important;} -->`. These are not valid CSS comments there. They can corrupt the selector of the following rule, so `*[class=mobile]`/`*[class=hide]` mobile overrides may fail in strict parsers.
- **Doctype.** XHTML 1.0 Transitional is fine for email. No issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`) are visible in the truncated source. The only personalization is the email address and ID in the tracking pixels (see section 2). The remainder of the HTML was truncated, so nothing beyond this can be confirmed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058).** Neither header was found. QA notes AgentMail may not capture them, so this is **unconfirmed**, not a verified violation. Check the raw message at the source ESP. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe from bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results.** The header is absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. That is expected through the relay. The From domain is `cheers.drunkelephant.com`, with images on `image.cheers.drunkelephant.com`, so the sending and image domains are aligned.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address.** Both fall outside the truncated HTML, so neither can be verified.

- **Landing pages.** They can't be checked against the email because the click-redirect destinations weren't resolved.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Switch the three social icons to `https://` (`image.cheers.drunkelephant.com`).
2. Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` to both tracking pixels.
3. Remove the plain-text email from the `everestengagement.com` pixel URL and use an opaque token instead.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.

6. Check the raw message at the source for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and for SPF, DKIM and DMARC. Fix the AgentMail capture so these are logged.
