# 20% off is going, goi—
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-08**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth "Keep it between us" secret-sale send in five weeks and the second one with a live countdown timer bolted on, following 2026-08-31's "Last day for 20% off." At least the countdown here reads as genuinely credible (16 hours, not a suspiciously round expiration), and the product shot underneath the hero code is one of the stronger ones this brand has run — a well-composed cluster of the actual SKUs (Virgin Marula Oil, Protini, C-Firma, D-Bronzi) rather than a generic bottle-on-white.
- But the fundamentals are stale. Same pink "BETWEEN US" wordmark, same gray hero background, same DRUNKVIP20 code mechanic seen repeatedly. The free 5-piece gift with $110+ orders is a genuinely new lever that could have been the lead story, but it's buried below the fold as a secondary module instead of the hero.
- Subject line is a liability on its own: "20% off is going, goi—" is a deliberate cutoff/glitch effect that reads as broken or spammy in an inbox rather than clever, and it doesn't preview well against the countdown urgency actually inside the email.

## What's working

- The countdown timer (16:48:43) is specific and creates real, credible urgency without a suspiciously round number.
- The product cluster hero shot is well-composed and shows five real, recognizable SKUs rather than a single generic bottle.
- The gift-with-purchase module ("$36 value" free 5-piece set) is a genuinely new offer lever not seen in recent sends.
- Social proof counters ("1,260 people are checking this out") add a helpful trust signal on the product grid.
- Star ratings are visible on every grid product, reinforcing quality without requiring a click.

## What's weak

- Subject line "20% off is going, goi—" is a truncated/glitch gimmick that will render as broken or cut-off in most inbox previews, not urgent.
- "Keep it between us" / secret-sale framing is a direct repeat of 2026-09-07, 2026-09-05, and 2026-09-04 — this is the fourth occurrence of nearly identical hero copy.
- The gift-with-purchase offer, the freshest idea in the email, is pushed below the primary CTA instead of leading.
- Two separate offers (20% off code vs. free gift at $110+) compete for attention with no visual hierarchy distinguishing which one the reader should act on first.

## Recommendations

- 1. Retire "Keep it between us" — it's now run four times in five weeks and has lost any conspiratorial effect. Lead with the countdown + the free gift instead, since that's the actually new asset this send has going for it.
- 2. Fix the subject line — the cutoff gimmick reads as a rendering error, not urgency. Use the countdown number itself, which is concrete and true.
- 3. Give the two offers (code vs. gift) a clear primary/secondary visual hierarchy — right now they're two same-weight blocks fighting for the same click.
- 4. Vary the hero palette/wordmark treatment from the last three sends so subscribers don't tune it out as "the same email again."
