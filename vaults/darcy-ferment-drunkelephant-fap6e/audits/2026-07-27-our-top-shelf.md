# Our top shelf
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-27**
## Executive summary

- "Our top shelf" is a full-catalog reintroduction disguised as a bestsellers roundup, and it's the most product-dense send from this brand yet — eight hero SKUs across two rows, followed by five category tiles (Moisturizers, Serums, Cleansers, Masks & Treatments, Kits & Bundles). Compared to the tighter, single-premise sends like "Golden hour every hour" (9/10, two products) or "Hot takes" (10/10, five products with a coherent sun-recovery narrative), this one sacrifices focus for breadth. There's no unifying visual idea here beyond "here is our whole catalog" — the gray-circle backdrop behind the product grid is the only real design flourish.
- The gift-with-purchase mechanic in the top banner is the actual offer, but it's buried in dense gray-bar copy at 3 lines of small caps text with a nested asterisk-qualified subscription add-on — a stark contrast to this brand's cleaner recent hooks. "YOUR BEST SKIN EVER? EASY, PEASY." is serviceable but generic, and it undersells what's actually a decent GWP threshold offer.
- This lands as a mid-pack, catalog-clearing send: well-produced product photography, zero render bugs, but no editorial spine and a buried offer. Call it a 6 — competent execution, forgettable premise.

## What's working

- Clean, bug-free render — all eight product shots, all five lifestyle banners, and both CTA buttons display correctly with no overlap or broken images.
- Product grid is well-organized with consistent "[Verb] with... PRODUCT NAME" labeling under each SKU, making it scannable despite the density.
- Category tiles at the bottom (Moisturizers, Serums, Cleansers, Masks & Treatments, Kits & Bundles) give a clear secondary path for anyone not sold on the specific bestsellers shown.
- The gray circular backdrop behind the 8-product grid adds a touch of visual structure to what could otherwise be a flat catalog dump.

## What's weak

- The actual offer — free 5-piece set on $110+ orders — is stuck in dense, small, all-gray banner text with three qualifying conditions crammed into one paragraph; nothing in the hero itself communicates "free gift."
- "YOUR BEST SKIN EVER? EASY, PEASY." is generic aspirational copy with none of the personality this brand has shown recently ("Hot takes," "Golden hour every hour").
- Eight products in the hero grid is overwhelming with no single-product focus — compare to "Golden hour every hour," which committed to two SKUs and scored 9/10.
- Five additional category banners after the bestsellers grid make this an unusually long scroll with no new information, just repackaged shop-now paths.
- No urgency or offer messaging appears anywhere near the primary "SHOP NOW" CTA — a shopper has to read the gray legal-style banner at the very top to know a gift exists at all.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pull the GWP offer out of the gray banner and into the hero as the actual headline — "Free 5-Piece Gift With Any $110 Order" beats "Your best skin ever? Easy, peasy." as a subject-to-hero throughline; this is the single biggest lift available since the offer is genuinely good but currently invisible.
- 2. Cut the hero grid from 8 products to 3-4 actual bestsellers to restore the focus that made "Hot takes" and "Golden hour every hour" score 9-10.
- 3. Move or condense the fine print (dates, exclusions, subscription terms) into a single small line at the bottom rather than the dense 3-line top banner.
- 4. Trim the five category tiles to 2-3, or combine into a single "Shop by Category" module with a smaller footprint — the current length risks fatigue after the "SMS SOS" and repeated promo cadence noted in the last two weeks.
- 5. Add a countdown or explicit end-date near the top CTA (the fine print shows the offer runs 7/27–8/3, but that credible short window never surfaces above the fold).
