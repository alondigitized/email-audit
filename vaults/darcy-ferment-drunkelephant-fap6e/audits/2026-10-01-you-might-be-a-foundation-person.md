# You might be a foundation person
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-01**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Kamo product-education send in two weeks, and it's the most complete one. It has a clinical-claims hero, a shade-spectrum visual and a before/after, with the 20% bundle offer in a banner above the headline. The shade "clock" image is the best creative here. It shows the 25-shade range at a glance and looks like Drunk Elephant.
- The problem is that the email leads with "RESULTS LIKE: DAMN," not the offer. The only price hook is a small white-on-gray strip, "TWO IS BETTER THAN... NOT TWO!", which reads as a riddle. The "foundation person" subject is a decent curiosity hook, but the body never answers it. The 9/25 send ("The world is your moisture") had the same problem: the same 20% Kamo + moisturizer bundle, buried. Four of the last seven sends push Kamo, so this one reads as more of the same unless the offer is sharper.
- Verdict: a polished, trustworthy send with a clear Kamo CTA. It's undersold by a muddy offer line, a subject the body doesn't pay off, and a cadence that's getting repetitive.

## What's working

- The shade-spectrum "clock" hero is distinctive and shows the range of skin tones without any copy.
- "SHOP KAMO" is a clear green button, repeated after the clinical stats, and it matches the brand palette.
- The clinical proof blocks (100% immediate radiance, 91% texture) are scannable and are paired with diverse models.
- The bare-face vs. after photo of an older model broadens appeal beyond the usual young-skin imagery.
- The 20% bundle offer is concrete, and the fine print states the terms.

## What's weak

- The offer sits in a small banner, with a riddle-like line ("TWO IS BETTER THAN... NOT TWO!") that obscures the discount.
- The subject says "foundation person," but the body never speaks to that identity. It goes straight to product claims and says Kamo is foundation/concealer/skin tint "that's also great for your skin."
- The fine print is tucked below the fold, and the missing space in "checkout.Offer" is visible. Terms are restrictive: it needs one Kamo and one eligible moisturizer in a single transaction, it can't be combined with other codes, and it's "while supplies last."
- The "25 SHADES?!" section shows only one swatch (Medium 15W). "FIND YOUR SHADE" is a small underlined link, and it should be the main conversion tool for a foundation launch.
- The email is long, with a lot of empty gray space in the footer, and there's no urgency beyond "while supplies last."

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the offer the hero. Put "20% off Kamo + a full-size moisturizer" in the headline and keep "RESULTS LIKE: DAMN" as the kicker. Right now the eye lands on the joke first.
- 2. Turn "FIND YOUR SHADE" into a real button and move it directly under the clock graphic. Shade matching is the main barrier to buying foundation online.
- 3. Replace the "TWO IS BETTER THAN... NOT TWO!" line with plain copy: "Buy Kamo + any full-size moisturizer, get 20% off."
- 4. Add a real deadline, such as "Ends Sunday," to replace "while supplies last."
- 5. Move the stat blocks down and fix the missing space in the fine print.
- 6. Space out Kamo sends or vary the angle. After 9/19, 9/23 and 9/25, a fourth Kamo email in under two weeks needs a new hook, such as reviews, a tutorial or a shade-matching story.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has no hard failures: the automated QA found 0 issues and 3 warnings, with a 57% pass rate. The confirmed problems are three `http://` social icons, two images with no alt text, the recipient's email address in a tracking URL, and CSS that some clients will handle badly. Header-based checks (List-Unsubscribe, authentication) couldn't be verified. The HTML source was truncated, so I can't rule out issues further down.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracked links not probed:** 14 links were skipped because they go through click-redirect domains (`mi.drunkelephant.com`, `drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com`). Their final destinations and status codes are unverified.
- **Recipient email in a URL:** the pixel URL `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/?e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app&c=us_de_broad…` carries the address in plaintext. That is PII in a query string, so it can show up in proxy, CDN and referrer logs.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://mi.drunkelephant.com/p/up/80acaf1c74f9e002/o.gif?mi_u=98161986&mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_kamoclinicals_p…` is a standard open pixel. It also carries a numeric user ID (`mi_u=98161986`).
- **Mixed content:** the Facebook, Instagram and TikTok icons load over `http://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/lib/fe3211717064057c751c78/m/1/…png`. Clients that proxy images over HTTPS, or block mixed content, may drop them.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `o.gif` and the `everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/` image have no `alt` attribute. Both look like tracking pixels. They should have `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"`, so screen readers skip them and some clients don't show placeholder text.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid `@import`:** the `<style>` block declares `body {…}` first and then three `@import url(https://db.onlinewebfonts.com/…)` rules. `@import` is only valid before other rules, so these are ignored. The same fonts are also loaded through `<link>` tags in `<head>`, which is redundant.
- **HTML comments inside CSS:** the `@media (max-width: 600px)` block contains `<!-- *[class=contenttable] {…} -->` and `<!-- *[class=mobile] {…} -->`. In CSS these are legacy CDO/CDC tokens. Some parsers will drop the adjacent rule, which could affect `*[class=mobile1]` in that block.
- **Third-party font hosts:** `BrownBold`, `BrownLight` and `BrownThin` come from `db.onlinewebfonts.com`, and `BrownRegular` comes from `www.drunkelephant.com/on/demandware.static/…/v1719565389232/…`. Most email clients ignore web fonts, so the font stack needs a safe fallback. I couldn't see whether one is declared, because the source is truncated.
- **Unusual mobile sizing:** `.vouchertext4` and `.fsname` set `max-width:12–15px` and `width:17px !important` at ≤418px and ≤600px. This is likely to clip text on small screens.
