# 15% off, you say? Our Subscribe to Save Event starts now!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-idea send. The Subscribe & Save event gives 15% off the first delivery, 20% off the second, and 10% off every one after. The grey hero, the large headline and the lined-up Protini, C-Firma, B-Hydra and T.L.C. bottles make a calm, premium layout, and a single "SHOP NOW" button sits above the fold. It follows the brand's usual look from "Another round of Protini!" (Oct 16) and "Kamo plus one" (Oct 11).
- The offer is the weak point. The headline "AND YOU'LL NEVER RUN OUT AGAIN" is a punchline with no setup, because it only works if you've read the subject. The real hook, "15% off", is buried in body copy. The deadline (ends Oct 27) and the free-shipping perk appear only in the grey fine print at the bottom. Four different percentages in one paragraph make the offer harder to read than it needs to be.
- Overall it's a competent, on-brand, low-risk send that under-sells its own mechanic. I'd rate it about a 6–7. It beats the recent kit emails on clarity but has no urgency and no specific product to click.

## What's working

- One clear mechanic, with a tiered discount that rewards staying subscribed.
- Clean, uncluttered layout with no render bugs: no broken images, overlaps or gaps.
- The product shot is attractive, and the bright cap colours (teal, orange, blue, pink) give a clear focal point.
- The "SHOP NOW" button is visible in the top third of the email.
- The tone is playful and on-brand ("forevermore", "you say?"). The subject and hero copy echo each other.

## What's weak

- The hero headline carries no offer. "15% off" appears only in the paragraph below it.
- The deadline (Oct 27, 2:59 am EST) and the free shipping are only in small grey fine print. The email creates no urgency.
- "SHOP NOW" is a generic label on a thin outline button with low contrast. It links to no specific product or subscription flow.
- Four percentages in a single run-on paragraph (15, 20, 10) make the savings feel complicated, and the product image is not tied to the offer.
- The email never says what "Subscribe & Save" is. It doesn't explain how to subscribe or mention that you can cancel anytime, outside the fine print.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in the hero. For example: "15% OFF YOUR FIRST DELIVERY" in large type, with "then 20% off your second, 10% after" as a smaller line underneath.
- 2. Move the deadline and free shipping up near the button. For example: "Free shipping · Cancel anytime · Ends Oct 27."
- 3. Change the CTA to "Start Subscribing" or "Subscribe & Save 15%", with a filled, high-contrast button.
- 4. Label the products with names and subscription prices, and link each to its own subscribe page. The Protini cream is the obvious hero.
- 5. Show a small three-step visual of 15% → 20% → 10% instead of describing it in a paragraph.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Only the head and CSS of the HTML were available (the source is truncated), so body links, the unsubscribe footer and CTA URLs could not be inspected. The confirmed issues are plain-HTTP images, alt-less tracking pixels, a recipient email address exposed in a tracking URL, and invalid `@import` placement. The three header warnings are unverified because the relay probably didn't capture those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 10 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Their destinations, status codes and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Email address in URL:** The `everestengagement.com` pixel URL embeds the recipient address: `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/?e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app&c=us_de_broad…`. This puts PII in a query string, where it can end up in proxy and server logs and in referrers. The address is unencoded, with a literal `@`.
- **Tracking pixels:** There are two. One is Movable Ink: `https://mi.drunkelephant.com/p/up/80acaf1c74f9e002/o.gif?mi_u=98161986&mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_subweekstarts_p…`. The other is the Everest `/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/` URL above. Both are third-party and will be blocked by Apple Mail Privacy Protection and by image-blocking clients, so open metrics will be unreliable.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** Both tracking pixels have no `alt` attribute (`o.gif` and the Everest `/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/` image). Add `alt=""`, plus `width="1" height="1"` and `style="display:none"` if they aren't already set. Screen readers may otherwise announce the filename or URL.
- **Insecure images:** Three social icons load over plain HTTP from `image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/lib/fe3211717064057c751c78/m/1/`: Facebook (`cc02d07d-…png`), Instagram (`9b452915-…png`) and TikTok (`29f975e1-…png`). Clients and proxies may block them as mixed content, leaving broken icons. Their alt text is present, so the broken-image fallback is readable.
- **Invalid `@import` placement:** In the `<style>` block, `@import url(https://db.onlinewebfonts.com/…)` appears after the `body {…}` rule. Per CSS spec, `@import` must come first, so these three imports are ignored. The `<link>` tags in `<head>` still load the same fonts, so the practical impact is small.
- **Third-party font dependency:** The Brown Bold, Light and Thin fonts are loaded from `db.onlinewebfonts.com`, and BrownRegular from `drunkelephant.com/on/demandware.static/…`. Outlook and Gmail ignore web fonts, and the CSS has no fallback stack that can be verified from the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FirstName]`) are visible in the available source. The only personalization is the resolved email in the Everest URL (see §2).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unverified headers:** `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results` were not found. The QA tool itself says this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay, so don't treat it as a confirmed sender failure. It matters for Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules, so check the raw headers of the original message.
- **Body footer unconfirmed:** The unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked because the source is truncated.
- **Sender domain:** The From address is `info@cheers.drunkelephant.com`, on a dedicated subdomain. Authentication status is unknown.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Campaign IDs:** `mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_subweekstarts_p…` and `c=us_de_broad…` show a consistent campaign naming scheme across the pixels.
- **CTA destinations unverified:** The CTA and landing URLs were not visible, and the tracking links were not probed. UTM parameters, redirect chains and whether the landing page matches the "15% off Subscribe to Save" offer are all unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Switch the three social icons to HTTPS (`https://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/…`).
2. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
3. Stop passing the plaintext email in the Everest pixel URL. Use an opaque subscriber ID instead.
4. Move the `@import` rules to the top of the `<style>` block, or drop them in favor of the existing `<link>` tags. Add web-safe font fallbacks.
5. Re-run the QA on the full, untruncated HTML. Also pull the raw message headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF, DKIM and DMARC. Then follow the 10 skipped tracking links manually to check UTM parameters and landing page alignment.
