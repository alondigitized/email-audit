# Homepage snapshot · www.drunkelephant.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Drunk Elephant is leading with a Friends & Family sitewide sale — 25% off, plus gift-with-purchase tiering at $100 and $175. For a brand loyalist who is literally on a monthly replenishment cycle for the Babyfacial, this is the right message at the right moment. It reads like a clean, product-first prestige skincare brand that knows its formula is the hero, even if the homepage execution leaves some conversion mechanics on the table.

## What's working

- **25% OFF SITEWIDE in the hero** — I immediately know this applies to my Babyfacial restock, my Protini refill, my C-Firma. Sitewide with no exclusion list is rare and I respect it.
- **Product stack as hero image** — Showing the actual recognizable bottles (Protini green, the dropper serum, the signature forms) is smarter than lifestyle photography for a returning shopper. I don't need to see a model — I need to see the product I'm about to buy.
- **Shipping threshold in the top banner** — "$40+ for free shipping" is immediately visible. My order will clear that by a factor of five, but a threshold callout grounds the value math fast.
- **"The whole shebang!" copy below the fold** — This is classic DE brand voice. Playful, confident, doesn't over-explain. The GWP tiers ($100+ and $175+) reward exactly the kind of multi-product cart I build every month.
- **Friends & Family banner positioning** — Leading with "FRIENDS & FAMILY" above the percentage anchors the offer in a familiar seasonal context. I know what this event is; the framing confirms we're in the right window.

## What's weak

- **No CTA button in the hero** — The entire hero is a static offer display. Where do I tap? On mobile, I'm sitting on a 25% off announcement with no obvious next action above the fold. This is a conversion hole.
- **No end date or urgency signal** — Friends & Family sounds time-limited but I have zero information about when it ends. No "ends Sunday," no countdown. For a sale this significant, the lack of a deadline reduces the impulse to act now rather than come back later.
- **No loyalty hook** — DE has no rewards program, and it is painfully obvious here. For someone who buys the Babyfacial every single month, there is no mechanism to recognize me as a top-tier repeat customer. That's a brand-level gap but it shows up acutely on a sale homepage.
- **GWP asterisk** — "plus a free full-size F-Balm mask with orders $175+.*" The asterisk at the end of an already compound conditional offer makes me want to immediately find the fine print. The goodwill of a generous GWP is partially offset by the feeling that something might be excluded.
- **No product callout for hero-level SKUs** — If DE knows I'm coming back for the Babyfacial monthly, why isn't the Babyfacial named in the hero? "Your TLC is 25% off" would convert me faster than a generic "sitewide."

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop the Sale" button to the hero** — Right below "SITEWIDE," one full-width button. Button copy could be "Shop 25% Off" or even "Shop All — 25% Off." The offer without a tap target is a missed conversion on mobile.
- **Add an expiration signal** — "Ends Sunday" or a simple date stamp below the offer text. Friends & Family events have natural end dates; surface it. Even "while it lasts" is better than silence.
- **Name the hero products** — The product stack in the image is gorgeous and I recognize everything, but a first-time visitor or lapsed customer won't. Two or three product names in the image overlay (Protini, C-Firma, Babyfacial) would serve both acquisition and retention simultaneously.
- **Surface the GWP tiers more cleanly** — "Free full-size F-Balm with $175+" should be a proper badge or banner strip, not buried in body copy below the fold with an asterisk. That's a meaningful incentive; treat it like one.
