# $158 of skincare for $69?!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand holiday kit send, and the $69.30 price against a $158 value is the strongest hook. The subject line's "$158 for $69?!" puts a concrete deal in front of the reader. The hero backs it up with a yellow-and-sky-blue frame, the kit photographed in a plaid box, and a round price badge. The eye lands on the kit and the price first, which is the right order.
- The email then splits its message. A dark banner at the top pushes a separate promo: free sample duos at $65, $95 and $125, up to 6. The body shifts to three single-product blurbs (Protini, C-Luma, Beste No. 9) that never mention the kit price. The three "SHOP NOW" links are small and underlined, and the copy doesn't say whether each product is in the kit or sold on its own. The footnote ends the promo on 11/19, but nothing visible in the email itself shows that deadline.
- Compared with the 11/13 and 11/15 Maison Moisture refill sends, which told one product story, this one is more commercial and more price-led. It is a solid 6. It would be better with one offer, a visible deadline and a kit-specific CTA.

## What's working

- The price badge ("$69.30 / VALUE: $158") sits on the hero image, so the deal is visible without reading any copy.
- The hero has strong visual hierarchy: the yellow band, the kit shot, the headline and the single outlined "SHOP NOW" button.
- The product blocks alternate left and right, which keeps the scroll rhythm easy and makes each one scannable.
- The packaging and plaid backgrounds match the holiday kit, so the whole email looks cohesive.
- Each blurb is short and benefit-led: firmness for Protini, luminosity for C-Luma, makeup removal for Beste No. 9.

## What's weak

- The top banner promotes a second offer (free sample duos with tiered spend and an asterisk), which competes with the $69.30 kit.
- The headline "MERRY AND BRIGHT, FOR STARTERS" is vague and says nothing about the offer, the kit or the savings.
- The three product "SHOP NOW" links are small, underlined and identical. They don't say whether they go to the kit or to individual products.
- The deadline (ends 11/19, 2:59 a.m. ET) appears only in tiny footnote text, so the urgency is invisible.
- The email never states which products are in the $158 kit or what the saving is, and the body copy doesn't mention the kit price at all.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the kit the only offer. Move the sample-duo promo into a thin secondary strip or a separate send, and add a visible deadline line under the hero, such as "Ends Nov 19".
- 2. Replace the headline with the deal, for example "The Bespoke Brightening kit: $158 of skincare, $69.30." Make the hero button read "Get the kit".
- 3. Tell the reader what's in the kit. Label each product block with its size or "in the kit", and show the kit contents in one row.
- 4. Make the product CTAs bigger and specific ("Shop the kit"), and add a bold final CTA with the price.
- 5. Add social proof, such as a review star rating or a bestseller tag on Protini.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Insecure images:** three social icons load over `http://` and may be blocked or flagged as mixed content:
  - `http://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/lib/fe3211717064057c751c78/m/1/cc02d07d-c9bb-4057-b5d7-62f3bec2c521.png` (Facebook)

- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid `@import`:** the `@import url(https://db.onlinewebfonts.com/...)` rules come after the `body{…}` rule in the same `<style>` block. `@import` must come first, so these are ignored and the fonts are loaded only by the `<link>` tags. - **Global CSS resets:** `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center;}` and `*{line-height:100%}` apply to every element. This is fragile in clients that honor them.
- **Stray HTML comments:** `<!-- *[class=contenttable]… -->` sits inside the `@media` block. Some parsers may mishandle it.
- **Other concerns:** `max-width:15px` and `max-height:12px` on `.vouchertext4` and `.fsname` under the mobile media queries could clip text, but I can't confirm that without the markup.
