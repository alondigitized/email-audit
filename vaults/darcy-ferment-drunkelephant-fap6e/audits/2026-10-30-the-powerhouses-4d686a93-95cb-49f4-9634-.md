# The powerhouses
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-30**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand holiday gifting email with one clear idea: small gifts under $40. The yellow and peach houndstooth hero is bold and recognizable. "Who you calling cute?" is a playful headline that fits Drunk Elephant's voice. The hero pairs it with the D-Bronzi bottle and "All under $40!", so the offer is easy to understand.
- It follows the 10/28 "All the gifts." send as the next step in the gifting push. That's a relief after a week of near-identical Subscribe & Save emails. But the subject "The powerhouses" doesn't match the hero's "cute" and "little gifts" framing, and the preview text isn't visible. The body is a tidy stack of three products, each with a price bubble and a SHOP NOW button. That's easy to scan, but it's repetitive and has no urgency or social proof.
- Overall it's a solid, polished send with a clear price anchor. It's undercut by a vague subject, five identical-looking CTAs, and a free-gift offer ($75+ for the Kamo + D-Bronzi sample card) buried at the bottom with dense fine print.

## What's working

- The "All under $40!" line in the hero gives a concrete, scannable offer.
- Price bubbles ($37 with "$50 value", $24, $39) put the numbers right on the products.
- The yellow, peach and white palette is distinctive and consistent with the brand.
- Each product block has a short, witty description ("Adios, flaky skin", "butteriest, nourishing-est").
- The bundle's "$37 value: $50" shows real savings.

## What's weak

- The subject "The powerhouses" doesn't say "gifts" or "under $40", so it ignores the email's strongest hook.
- Every CTA is a generic "SHOP NOW" in a thin outlined button, with no product-specific verb or noun.
- The hero product is D-Bronzi, which appears again as the third product block. That's redundant.
- The $75+ free-gift offer (Kamo + D-Bronzi sample card) is at the bottom, and its fine print is tiny and dense (one per order, 10/28 to 11/5, excludes gift cards).
- There are no reviews or social proof, and no shipping or holiday-delivery cutoff to make "last-minute" feel real.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put "under $40 gifts" in the subject and make the preview carry the proof. The subject is the biggest drag on this send.
- 2. Move the $75+ free-gift offer higher, either into the hero or right after the first product. Reduce the fine print to one clear line with an "ends 11/5" deadline.
- 3. Replace the three identical "SHOP NOW" buttons with specific CTAs like "Get the lip duo" and "Get the hand cream".
- 4. Don't use D-Bronzi as both the hero and a body product. Feature the lip duo or the hand cream in the hero instead.
- 5. Add a star rating or review line under each product, and a holiday shipping deadline.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML is mostly sound, but it has a few concrete defects: an invalid `@import` placement, `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta, three `http://` images, and the recipient's email address exposed in a tracking-pixel URL. The header checks (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) came back empty, probably because the AgentMail relay strips them. They are unverified, not confirmed failures. The HTML source was truncated, so the body links and footer weren't inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **14 tracking links were not probed.** They are click-redirect domains, so the QA check skipped the HTTP probe. Final destinations, redirect chain length and status codes are unverified.
- **Pixels without `alt`:**
  - `https://mi.drunkelephant.com/p/up/80acaf1c74f9e002/o.gif?mi_u=98161986&mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_holstockingstuf…`
  - `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/?e=…&c=us_de_broad…`
  
  Both are open or tracking beacons. They should carry `alt=""` so screen readers and image-off clients don't announce a URL.
- **Two separate tracking stacks** (`mi.drunkelephant.com` and `everestengagement.com`) fire on the same send. That adds third-party request overhead and more points of failure.
- **Recipient email in a URL.** The Everest pixel includes `e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app` in plain text. That leaks the address to any proxy or log that sees the URL.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid `@import` placement.** The `@import url(https://db.onlinewebfonts.com/...)` rules come after `body {…}` in the `<style>` block. Per the CSS spec, `@import` must come before all other rules, so clients that honor the spec will ignore all three.
- **Redundant font loading.** The same fonts are also loaded through `<link>` tags to `db.onlinewebfonts.com`, a third-party host. `BrownRegular` is loaded from a versioned `drunkelephant.com/on/demandware.static/.../v1719565389232/fonts/` path, which will break if that build is replaced. Most clients (Gmail, Outlook) ignore web fonts anyway, so the fallback stack matters.
- **HTML comments inside `@media`.** `<!-- *[class=contenttable] {…} -->` sits inside the `@media (max-width:600px)` block. This is invalid CSS, and some parsers may drop the rules around it.
- **Pinch-zoom blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1" />` prevents zooming, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **No `lang` attribute.** The `<html xmlns=…>` tag has none. `<title>` is the generic "Drunk Elephant" rather than the subject.
- **Insecure images.** Three social icons load over `http://`:
  - `http://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/lib/fe3211717064057c751c78/m/1/cc02d07d-…png` (Facebook)
  - `…/9b452915-…png` (Instagram)
  - `…/29f975e1-…png` (TikTok)
  
  They may be blocked or flagged as mixed content, and the same host is probably available over HTTPS.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) are visible in the available HTML. The only per-recipient data seen is the email in the pixel URL, covered in section 2.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** (QA WARN). The relay may have dropped it, so this is unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found** (RFC 8058, QA WARN). One-click unsubscribe support can't be verified. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found** (QA WARN). SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `cheers.drunkelephant.com` could not be assessed.
- **Footer not checked.** The visible HTML doesn't show the in-body unsubscribe link or postal address, so those are unverified, not missing.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
The visible tracking parameters (`mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_holstockingstuf…`, `c=us_de_broad…`) identify the campaign, but they are not UTM parameters. Final landing URLs are hidden behind unprobed redirects, so UTM presence and landing-page alignment can't be confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the `@import` rules to the top of the `<style>` block, or remove them in favor of the existing `<link>` tags. Make sure the fallback font stack is sound.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Switch the three social icons to `https://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/...`.
4. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels, add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and set `<title>` to the subject.
5. Stop putting the raw recipient email in the pixel URL. Use an opaque ID instead.
6. Remove the HTML comments inside the `@media` block.
7. Check the raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, since the relay may be stripping them. If they are really absent, add them.
8. Resolve the 14 redirect links in a follow-up probe to confirm final URLs, status codes and UTM handling.
