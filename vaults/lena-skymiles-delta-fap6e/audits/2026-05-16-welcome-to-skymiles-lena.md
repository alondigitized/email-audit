# Welcome To SkyMiles®, Lena
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a transactional welcome email confirming Lena's new SkyMiles membership — not a promotional send. It does exactly what it needs to: surface the member number immediately and bundle onboarding next steps. The sky/clouds hero is on-brand and the layout is clean at a glance. The problem is that "welcome" emails are the highest-intent moment in the loyalty lifecycle, and Delta squanders it by burying the starter-pack value prop beneath a wall of small copy and dense legal text. The above-the-fold card reveal is smart; everything below it is a missed opportunity to create genuine excitement about the program.
- The email is functional and correct, but it reads like it was designed for compliance first and for a new member's emotional engagement second. A new SkyMiles member just opted in — this should feel like a reward, not a registration receipt.

## What's working

- **Personalization in the hero and subject** — "Lena" appears in the subject line, creating an immediate sense of a tailored moment rather than a bulk blast.
- **Member number surfaced front-and-center** — the SkyMiles card with the account number is the first thing you see after the hero; this is the one thing a new member actually needs.
- **Clean brand execution** — Delta's blue, white, and red color palette with the sky/clouds motif is polished and consistent throughout.
- **Starter Pack section exists** — the "SkyMiles Starter Pack" module is a smart structural choice for onboarding; it signals there's more value to unlock.
- **Social links in footer** — Delta includes all major platforms, which matters for a loyalty relationship that extends beyond email.

## What's weak

- **No concrete offer above the fold** — "Starter Pack" implies value but the email never delivers a specific number (miles, bonus, discount) that makes the membership feel immediately worthwhile.
- **Dense legal text dominates the scroll** — the lower 40%+ of the visible email is fine print, which signals to a new member "this is complicated" rather than "this is exciting."
- **CTAs are unreadable at render size** — button copy is too small to parse confidently; if a CTA can't be read in a screenshot, it won't get tapped on mobile.
- **No urgency or activation hook** — there's nothing to do *right now* — no bonus miles offer for a first flight, no expiry on a welcome reward, no app download prompt with a benefit attached.
- **Evergreen framing feels cold** — "Welcome" as the sole emotional beat doesn't tell Lena *why* she should care about SkyMiles beyond having the card number.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a concrete welcome reward above the fold** — "You've got 1,000 bonus miles waiting — earn them on your first flight" turns passive registration into an active goal. This single change would lift click rate meaningfully.
- 2. **Collapse the legal text** — move dense T&Cs behind a "Terms apply" link; replace that scroll real estate with a simple 3-step "here's how to earn" explainer.
- 3. **Make CTA copy legible and specific** — swap any generic buttons for something like "Book Your First Flight" or "Download the App — Earn Miles Faster"; tie each to a tangible benefit.
- 4. **Add a time-bounded activation hook** — "Earn 2x miles on your first purchase within 30 days" creates urgency without feeling manipulative.
- 5. **Rewrite the subject to signal value, not just confirmation:**
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your SkyMiles number is ready, Lena — here's your starter reward`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Welcome, Lena. 1,000 bonus miles to get you started`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your membership number + a bonus to use on your first flight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Everything you need to earn miles from day one — in one email.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[WARN] Malformed HTML document structure**
The source emits visible `<!-- ... -->` comment blocks and a `<div>` wrapper *before* the `<!DOCTYPE html>` declaration. Per spec, anything before DOCTYPE forces quirks mode in standards-compliant parsers. Additionally, there are **two `<html>` opening tags** — one with `xmlns`/VML namespace attributes and one plain — with no intervening close tag. While most email clients are forgiving, this is a structural defect that can cause unpredictable rendering in edge clients.

```html
<!-- pre-DOCTYPE content and divs here -->
<!DOCTYPE html>
<html xmlns="..." lang="en"> <!-- first html tag -->
<html>                        <!-- second html tag — invalid -->
```

**[WARN] `meta` viewport uses semicolons instead of commas**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width; initial-scale=1.0; user-scalable=yes">
```
The CSS `content` attribute for viewport requires comma-separated values. Most clients tolerate this, but it is non-conformant.

**[WARN] Image missing alt text**
`3f473969-95d8-43bc-8d81-7bdb8c2d4dd8.png` has no `alt` attribute. When images are blocked (common in B2B inboxes), the image renders as a blank space with no context. This also fails WCAG 2.1 §1.1.1.

**[WARN] HTTP image source (mixed content)**
Same image: `http://image.o.delta.com/lib/fe5815707c6201787313/m/10/3f473969-95d8-43bc-8d81-7bdb8c2d4dd8.png`

Non-HTTPS sources are actively blocked by several clients (Apple Mail with "Protect Mail Activity" enabled, Outlook with strict security policies). The rest of the asset domain appears to use HTTPS; this is a one-off omission.

### 5. Compliance

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header absent**
The AgentMail relay did not capture or forward a `List-Unsubscribe` header. CAN-SPAM does not mandate this header, but Gmail and Yahoo Mail use it to surface the unsubscribe button in the UI — its absence increases spam folder placement risk.

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent (RFC 8058)**
One-click machine unsubscribe (required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender rules for >5,000 msg/day senders) depends on this header. If Delta is sending at that volume — near-certain for a welcome flow — this is a deliverability compliance gap. The header may be present at send time but stripped by the relay; Delta's ESP configuration should be verified.

**[WARN] `Authentication-Results` header absent**
SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status cannot be confirmed through the relay. Sender domain is `t.delta.com`; alignment with the `delta.com` DMARC policy should be confirmed via direct header inspection or ESP reporting.
