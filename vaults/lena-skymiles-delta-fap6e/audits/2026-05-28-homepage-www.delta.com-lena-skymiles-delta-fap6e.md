# Homepage snapshot · www.delta.com · 2026-05-28
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-28**
## Executive summary

- Delta.com is presenting a clean, functional booking experience on mobile — navy brand feel, search widget front and center, BUR pre-filled which tells me they're using location context. But the above-the-fold hero is a credit card acquisition push, and that's where it loses me: I'm a Diamond Medallion, I almost certainly have the AMEX card already, and "Welcome Offer As High As 100,000 Miles\*" with asterisk fine print doesn't feel like a message aimed at me. It feels like they're renting my homepage real estate to their co-brand partner.

## What's working

- **Flight search widget with pre-filled origin (BUR):** This is smart. I didn't have to type Burbank — that's one fewer tap and it signals the app knows roughly where I am. This is the single most useful thing on the screen.
- **Clean tab navigation (Book / Check-in / My Trips / Flight Status):** The four tabs cover 95% of what I actually do on delta.com. They're labeled plainly and I can tap My Trips without hunting.
- **Mode switcher (Flights / Stays / Cars / Vacations):** Prominent and scannable. I book Stays on Delta sometimes for the miles; good that it's one tap away.
- **Red "Log in" button in the header:** Stands out immediately. Delta is doing the right thing making login obvious — if I were logged in, this whole page would look different and better.
- **"Delta AMEX Cards" and "Gift Cards" quick-links at the bottom:** Useful as secondary actions without competing with the primary booking flow.

## What's weak

- **Credit card acquisition hero for a Diamond Medallion:** I almost certainly already have the Delta AMEX Platinum or Reserve. Showing me a "Welcome Offer" for a card I own (or have evaluated and declined) wastes the most valuable pixel real estate on my visit.
- **No logged-in/loyalty surface:** My SkyMiles balance, Medallion status, upgrade waitlist, or available Certificates should be one of the first things I see. Right now delta.com treats me the same as someone who has never flown.
- **"Learn More" CTA copy is vague:** For a 100K mile offer, I'd expect "See My Offer" or "Check Your Offer" — something that implies personalization. "Learn More" is the lowest-effort CTA copy imaginable.
- **No operational reliability signal anywhere:** As a Diamond, I scan every Delta communication for on-time stats, delay alerts, or reliability messaging. None visible.
- **Destination field is empty:** BUR is pre-filled but "Destination" just sits there blank. No suggested routes, no trending destinations, no "where did you last fly?" prompt. Missed chance to accelerate my booking.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the credit card hero for a personalized loyalty surface on logged-in sessions:** Show my SkyMiles balance, Medallion tier, and one actionable CTA like "You have 2 Upgrade Certificates — use them before they expire." Treat me like a Diamond, not a credit card prospect.
- **Add a "Where to next?" destination suggestion strip:** Inject 3–4 routes from BUR with fare starting points ("BUR → JFK from $189") directly below the search widget. This converts browse intent into booking intent in one scroll.
- **Change "Learn More" to "See Your Offer":** Implies the 100K miles number was calculated for me specifically. Zero engineering lift, measurable CTR improvement.
- **Add a Medallion status badge or login prompt with benefit teaser:** Something like "Log in to see your Upgrade Waitlist and Choice Benefits" just below the tab bar would pull Diamonds like me into a personalized state immediately.
