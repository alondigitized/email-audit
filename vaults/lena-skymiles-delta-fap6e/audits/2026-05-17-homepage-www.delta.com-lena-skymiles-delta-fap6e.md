# Homepage snapshot · www.delta.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Opening delta.com on my phone and the first thing I see is a pitch for a credit card. I've been Diamond for three years — the AMEX Gold card is already in my wallet, and I'm here to book a flight, not pick up another piece of plastic. The bones of the app are fine — clean tab nav, flights search already populated with BUR — but Delta clearly doesn't know I'm standing here, and that's a miss for the most loyal tier of flyer they have.

## What's working

- **Flights search box pre-filled with BUR (Burbank, CA):** That's my home airport. Whether it's IP-based or a cookie, it saves me a step and signals the app is paying attention — even if just barely.
- **Tab nav (Book / Check-in / My Trips / Flight Status):** Exactly the four things I open delta.com to do, in roughly the right priority order. One tap to where I need to be.
- **Clean mobile layout:** No cramped text, no broken images, no layout jank. It renders fast and feels premium — appropriate for the brand.
- **Notification bell with badge (3):** I notice that immediately. If one of those is an upgrade notification, I'm tapping it before anything else.
- **"80,000 Miles" number in bold:** Even if the card push is wrong for me, the number itself is scannable and clear. Good headline typography.

## What's weak

- **Hero is pure prospect acquisition — zero Diamond relevance:** I have the card. This entire above-the-fold moment is wasted on me. A returning Diamond should see something that speaks to her actual relationship with the airline.
- **Not logged in, so completely anonymous experience:** My status, my upgrade waitlist, my SkyMiles balance — invisible. Delta knows who I am when I open the app. The web experience should too, but instead I see "Sign Up / Log in" as if I'm a stranger.
- **"Learn More" CTA is the weakest possible copy:** It tells me nothing. Learn more about what? For a $200+ annual-fee card push, this should be "See Your Offer" or "Check My Miles Offer" — something that makes the action feel personal and low-risk.
- **No operational reliability signal anywhere:** That's the stat I scan every email for. Nothing — no on-time percentage, no "98% of flights operate as scheduled," nothing that reinforces why I pay more to fly Delta.
- **Asterisk fine print undermines the 80K headline:** The gray "Minimum purchase required. Terms apply." at the bottom of the hero is classic bait-and-switch energy. I notice it, and it makes me trust the offer less.

## Recommendations

- **Detect logged-in Diamond members and swap the hero entirely:** Replace the card acquisition hero with a status-aware module — my SkyMiles balance, next upgrade waitlist position, or a "Your Choice Benefits are available" CTA. This is table stakes for a loyalty program this sophisticated.
- **Replace "Learn More" with personalized CTA copy:** If the card push stays for logged-out visitors, change the button to "See My Offer" or "Check Your 80K Offer" — something that sounds like it's meant for one person, not a billboard.
- **Surface one operational reliability signal above the fold:** Even a single line like "99.1% of Delta flights arrived on schedule this month" reinforces the core reason I choose Delta over a cheaper fare. It's already in my emails — bring it to the homepage.
- **Add a Diamond/Medallion welcome rail for authenticated users:** A slim horizontal strip showing status level, miles to next tier, and upgrade availability could live just below the search box. Takes one tap of real estate, returns enormous loyalty signal.
