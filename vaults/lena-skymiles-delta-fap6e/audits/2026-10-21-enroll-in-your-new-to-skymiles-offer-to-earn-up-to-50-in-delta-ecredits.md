# Enroll In Your New To SkyMiles® Offer To Earn Up To $50 In Delta eCredits
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-purpose welcome-style send. The hero reads "Your Next Adventure Is Brewing", with the Delta x Starbucks co-brand and "Earn up to $50 in eCredits" under it. The eye lands on the hero, then the two-step explainer: Step 1 is $25 for enrolling, Step 2 is another $25 for linking accounts and making qualifying Starbucks purchases by 11/21/2026. The structure is easy to scan.
- The hero copy undersells the offer. "Adventure is brewing" is a pun, and the money only appears in the small line underneath. The only button above the explainer is a generic "Learn More", and the Starbucks purchase requirement and "new to SkyMiles" eligibility only become clear once you read the steps. The long fine-print block at the bottom is dense but standard for Delta.
- This is a refreshing change from the Amex SkyMiles Reserve pitch Delta sent Lena six times (Aug 28 through Oct 16, most recently "Earn Two Flight Certificates. 50,000 Bonus Miles…"). It's a different offer and a different audience. Judgment: a solid, mid-pack send. A sharper hero and a specific CTA would make it good.

## What's working

- The two-step $25 + $25 structure makes the $50 total concrete and easy to follow.
- The Delta x Starbucks co-brand is instantly recognizable and lifestyle-friendly.
- The 11/21/2026 deadline gives credible time-bounding.
- Visual hierarchy is clean, with generous white space. I saw no broken images or layout bugs.
- It breaks the repetitive Amex card cadence with a fresh offer.

## What's weak

- The hero headline is a pun with no value in it. The dollar amount is secondary.
- The top CTA, "Learn More", is vague and doesn't say "Enroll".
- "New to SkyMiles" eligibility and the Starbucks account requirement add friction and aren't clear up front.
- The enroll, link and purchase steps make the real payoff feel like work for $25.
- The footer fine print and the star-rating survey are long and low-value, and the partner-logo block repeats the pitch.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the money in the hero: "Earn up to $50 in Delta eCredits with Starbucks". Keep the pun as a subhead.
- 2. Change "Learn More" to "Enroll Now" and repeat it after Step 2.
- 3. State the eligibility ("New to SkyMiles members") and the deadline in the first line of the hero.
- 4. Show a single example of a qualifying Starbucks purchase to make Step 2 feel easy.
- 5. Collapse the legal copy behind a link and drop the survey from the promo.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New to SkyMiles? Get $50 in Delta eCredits`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your $50 Delta eCredit offer ends 11/21`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Enroll, link Starbucks Rewards, sip your way to $25 + $25.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Two quick steps. Credits land in your Delta account.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The truncated source shows a Delta global template with some markup validity problems and 3 image defects: 2 insecure `http://` images and missing `alt` on 8 `<img>` tags in total. The compliance and authentication WARNs look like AgentMail relay capture gaps, not confirmed sender failures. Link, UTM and unsubscribe checks could not be completed from the available data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified:** 41 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destinations, redirect health and UTM mapping are unconfirmed.
- **Insecure image hosts:** two images load over plain HTTP from the same sending domain that serves other assets over HTTPS:
  - `http://image.o.delta.com/lib/fe5815707c6201787313/m/10/3f473969-95d8-43bc-8d81-7bdb8c2d4dd8.png`
  - `http://image.o.delta.com/lib/fe5815707c6201787313/m/9/5c194397-2d5c-4b1c-8911-b3ad7dbe0298.png`
  
  Gmail's image proxy typically rewrites these. Some clients block or warn on mixed content.
- No broken or malformed `href` values are evidenced in the visible source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` (8 images):**
  - The two `http://` images above.
  - Five instances of `https://image.o.delta.com/lib/fe3711727164047e771173/m/1/spacer.png`.
  - The QA check does not distinguish an absent `alt` from `alt=""`. For the spacers, `alt=""` is correct and the fix is to confirm it is present. For the two content-bearing PNGs, a descriptive `alt` is needed. If either carries the offer text or a CTA, that content is unavailable when images are blocked.
- **Invalid document structure:**
  - Content precedes the doctype: the lookup-script `<div style="font-size:0px; line-height:1px;">` and comments appear before `<!DOCTYPE html>`. That can trigger quirks mode in some clients.
  - Nested `<html>` tags: `<html xmlns=… lang="en" dir="ltr"> <html>`.
  - A `<div>` inside `<head>`: `<div style="font-size:0px; line-height:1px;"> <style …>` ("BEGIN CSS BLOCK"). Parsers may eject it into the body or drop the styles it wraps.
- **CSS selector typo:** `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` is missing the leading `.`, so it targets a nonexistent `<ReadMsgBody>` element. The rule is dead.
- **Viewport meta:** `content="width=device-width; initial-scale=1.0; user-scalable=yes"` uses semicolons instead of commas, so it is non-standard.
- **Positive:** `lang="en"`, `dir="ltr"`, a `<title>`, `color-scheme` meta tags and MSO `DontUseAdvancedTypographyReadingMail` are present, and the template changelog notes aria tags were added.
- The source is truncated, so the body markup (roles, table semantics, heading order) is not auditable.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found. QA notes the relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**. Verify against the raw message at the original receiving inbox. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, and the cause is again the relay. The From domain `o.delta.com` is a subdomain, so alignment should be checked against the raw headers.
- **Body footer:** the unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated portion, so they cannot be confirmed.
- **Template residue:** internal authoring comments ship in the production HTML, such as `<!-- DRAG IN "78329_TMPL_CSS_Custom_Fonts_shared" TO ADD WHITNEY & LATO FONTS -->` and the version changelog. This exposes template IDs and is a minor hygiene issue, not a compliance failure.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 41 tracked links were skipped, and the visible source contains no resolvable destination URLs or UTM parameters. Nothing is flagged.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve `3f473969-…png` and `5c194397-…png` over HTTPS and add descriptive `alt` text. Set `alt=""` explicitly on the five `spacer.png` instances.
2. Fix the structure: move the doctype to the first line, remove the nested `<html>`, and move the lookup `<div>` out of `<head>`, either to `<body>` or by using comments or ESP-side processing.
3. Correct `ReadMsgBody` to `.ReadMsgBody`, and use commas in the viewport meta.
4. Strip template-authoring comments from the sent HTML.
5. Re-run the compliance and authentication checks against the raw `.eml` at the original receiving inbox, not the AgentMail relay. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `o.delta.com`.
6. Resolve the click-redirect chains with a follow-redirects probe that allows the tracking domains. Then verify the landing URLs and UTM parameters, and that the enrollment CTA lands on the matching SkyMiles offer page.
