# Homepage snapshot · www.delta.com · 2026-07-23
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-23**
## Executive summary

- Opening delta.com on my phone and this is... not what I expected. The page is dominated by a nature shot of a dead tree against a blue sky, with a cookie consent banner eating half the screen. Above that, a small "Discover Travel Products" text block is the only branded content visible. For a Diamond Medallion who logs in expecting her SkyMiles balance and a booking widget, this feels like the site didn't recognize me at all.

## What's working

- **SkyMiles mention in the subhead** — "get exclusive SkyMiles® Member benefits" at least signals this is the right place for a member, even if it doesn't do more than that
- **Cookie banner is dismissible** — "I understand" button is visible and tappable; it doesn't force me through a multi-step flow

## What's weak

- **Hero imagery is completely off-brand** — a dead, leafless tree trunk against a bright blue sky with snow-dusted pines in the background does not say "fly Delta One to JFK"; it reads like a stock photo placeholder or an error state
- **No booking widget above the fold** — as someone who comes to delta.com to book flights, the entire reason I opened the app is absent
- **No login/loyalty prompt** — nothing is inviting me to sign in to see my Diamond Medallion status, SkyMiles balance, or Choice Benefits countdown; I feel anonymous
- **"Details" as the only CTA** — this is the weakest possible button copy; it tells me nothing about what I'll find or why I should tap
- **Cookie banner obscures content** — takes up roughly a quarter of the screen and sits on top of whatever else might be below the tree photo
- **Aerial mystery image at the top** — the crop is so tight I can't tell if I'm looking at a beach, a jungle, or a parking lot; zero aspirational lift

## Recommendations

- **Surface a personalized booking widget immediately** — for a returning Diamond, the first screen should be an origin/destination search with my recent routes pre-populated; every second that isn't a booking path is a wasted moment
- **Swap the dead-tree hero for a Delta One cabin shot or an airport lifestyle image** — something that makes a comfort-first traveler feel the premium promise; July is peak summer travel and this image reads like January in upstate New York
- **Add a sticky "Sign In to See Your Status" bar** — Diamond Medallions need to know their Choice Benefits deadline; a one-tap login hook at the top converts loyal members instantly
- **Replace "Details" with a verb that earns the tap** — "Explore Vacation Packages" or "Add a Hotel" tells me exactly what I'm getting; "Details" tells me nothing
