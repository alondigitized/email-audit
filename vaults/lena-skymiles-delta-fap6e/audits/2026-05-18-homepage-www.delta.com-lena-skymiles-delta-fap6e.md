# Homepage snapshot · www.delta.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- Delta's homepage is doing airport-kiosk energy right now — functional, clean, and completely indifferent to the fact that I fly 150,000 miles a year on this airline. The above-the-fold real estate is dominated by a credit card acquisition push that assumes I don't already have the AMEX Platinum Delta card sitting in my wallet. It feels like I landed on a bank's homepage, not my airline.

## What's working

- **Booking widget loads first** — BUR origin is pre-populated (location aware or cached), which cuts one tap for a returning flyer. The Flights/Stays/Cars/Vacations row gives me everything I actually book in one glance.
- **Navigation is tight** — Book, Check-in, My Trips, Flight Status is exactly the four tasks I come here for, in exactly the order I prioritize them.
- **Aerial hero image** — the tropical coastline shot is gorgeous and does the heavy lifting of "Delta goes somewhere worth going." As someone who thinks in destinations, not terminals, this lands emotionally.
- **100,000 Miles headline** — the number is bold, legible, and scannable at arm's length. Even if the offer doesn't apply to me, I clock the scale and respect the brand confidence.

## What's weak

- **Not logged in = not recognized** — I'm a Diamond Medallion and the page greets me like a cold prospect. No SkyMiles balance, no "Welcome back," no status tier visible anywhere. That's a missed warmth moment and a missed conversion moment.
- **Credit card push to an existing cardholder** — If Delta knows I'm a Diamond, they know I have the card. This module is pure card-acquisition cold targeting shoved into premium real estate.
- **"Learn More" CTA is a dead end** — It says nothing about what I'm learning *toward*. For a high-intent return visitor, "Apply Now" or "See Your Offer" would be more honest about the destination.
- **No operational reliability signal** — I literally read the on-time stats in every Delta email. There's nothing here about operational performance, upgrade availability, or Choice Benefits — the three things I actually want to know.
- **"To — Destination" is empty** — I have an origin (BUR) but no suggested destinations, trending routes, or recent searches. The widget is half-finished and wastes the space.

## Recommendations

- **Surface my SkyMiles balance and Medallion status in the header when detected as returning** — even pre-login, a "Sign in to see your balance" micro-hook would convert me immediately and make the page feel like mine.
- **Swap the AMEX acquisition hero for a "Your Summer Benefits" module** — show Diamond Choice Benefit selection reminders, upgrade clearance rates on popular routes, or complimentary Delta One upgrade availability. That's what actually moves a Medallion to book sooner.
- **Pre-fill "To" with recently searched destinations or trending routes from BUR** — I opened delta.com to book something. Help me remember where I was going, or suggest LAX → JFK, LAX → LHR, the routes a West Coast business traveler actually flies.
- **Add an on-time performance badge or route reliability stat** — even a small "93% on-time, last 30 days" chip under the booking widget would differentiate Delta from United in the 5 seconds I'm deciding whether to check prices here or on Google Flights.
