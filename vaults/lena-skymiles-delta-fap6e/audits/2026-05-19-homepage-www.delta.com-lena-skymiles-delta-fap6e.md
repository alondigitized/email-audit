# Homepage snapshot · www.delta.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Delta.com is greeting me — a Diamond Medallion member who has probably logged thousands of segments — like a total stranger trying to sell me a credit card. The hero is a card acquisition push for 80K miles, which is a welcome offer for people who don't already have the Amex card I've had for six years. The booking widget is clean and functional, but the entire above-the-fold experience is built for acquisition, not retention.

## What's working

- **Booking widget is front and center** with BUR/Burbank pre-filled — smart cookie/local-storage use that saves me a tap. That's the most useful thing on the screen for me.
- **Top navigation is crisp**: Book, Check-in, My Trips, Flight Status — exactly the four actions I actually do on this app. No fluff.
- **The notification bell has a "3" badge** — that's a pull. Even logged-out, it signals something is waiting for me, which will get me to tap Log In.
- **Card imagery and bold headline** make the offer scannable in under two seconds — good execution on a campaign I personally don't need.

## What's weak

- **The hero is dead weight for me.** I have the Delta Amex Reserve. An 80K welcome offer means nothing to an existing cardholder. This is prospecting copy served to someone who's already converted.
- **I'm not logged in.** A Diamond Medallion member opening delta.com on her phone expects to see her mileage balance, her upcoming trip, and her tier. None of that is here.
- **No flight deals or fare transparency.** I actively look for upgrade availability and operational reliability data. The homepage offers neither.
- **"Learn More" is a weak CTA.** For an offer this size (80K miles), "See Your Offer" or "Check My Eligibility" would feel personal. "Learn More" is generic.
- **Mountain backdrop is beautiful but disconnected.** There's no plane, no airport, no human in the scene. For a travel brand, this is an oddly stationary visual.

## Recommendations

- **Log me in automatically or surface a persistent "Welcome back, Lena" strip** with my mileage balance and Diamond status — if I've used the app before, the homepage should greet me, not prospect me.
- **Swap the hero for a member-relevant module**: upcoming trip summary, a targeted upgrade offer on my next BUR segment, or my Choice Benefits selection deadline if one is approaching.
- **Replace "Learn More" with targeted copy**: "Already a cardholder? Explore your benefits" or route non-cardholders to "See My Offer" — one small copy branch that stops insulting loyal members.
- **Add a fare deal strip below the booking widget** — even a "Today's top deals from BUR" rail would give me a reason to engage beyond just booking what I already planned.
