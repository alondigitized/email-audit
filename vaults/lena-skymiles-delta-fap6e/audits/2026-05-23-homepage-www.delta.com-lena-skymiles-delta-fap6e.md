# Homepage snapshot · www.delta.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Delta.com is serving me a credit card acquisition pitch as the hero — 80,000-mile welcome offer front and center, mountain backdrop, gold Delta AMEX gleaming. As a Diamond Medallion who almost certainly already has this card, that immediately feels like I walked into the wrong aisle. The booking widget is functional and pre-populated with BUR (nice geolocation touch), but the dominant message is "get the card," not "where are you flying next, Lena?"

## What's working

- **Geolocation-populated origin field (BUR / Burbank, CA):** I didn't type anything and my home airport is already in the search box. That's frictionless and earns goodwill fast.
- **Top-of-page booking widget with multi-product tabs:** Flights, Stays, Cars, Vacations, Cruises are all one tap away. I can route directly to what I need.
- **"Book / Check-in / My Trips / Flight Status" nav rail:** These are exactly the four jobs I come to do. Zero cognitive load.
- **80,000 Miles callout is high-contrast and readable:** Even if I'm not the target, the number pops and the card visual is polished.
- **Mountain scenery backdrop:** Seasonal, aspirational, communicates travel rather than finance.

## What's weak

- **Hero is credit card acquisition, not flight inspiration:** For a Diamond Medallion, this reads as Delta treating me like a prospect, not a VIP. My Choice Benefits cycle, upgrade availability, or a relevant route deal would land better.
- **"May be as high as" copy:** That hedge immediately undercuts the 80K headline. I notice it. It signals the actual offer is lower for most people.
- **No Medallion or loyalty status surface:** Nowhere above the fold confirms Delta knows who I am. No balance, no tier badge, no "you have X miles" hook. That's a miss for a logged-out Diamond.
- **"Learn More" CTA is generic:** On a card that's already pushing a specific miles number, "Learn More" is weak. "Check My Offer" or "See Your 80K Offer" would be far stronger.
- **No flight deals or fare promo:** Delta is a premium airline with a loyalty-first customer base. Serving me only a credit card ad with no flight offer means I'm looking past the hero the moment I load the page.

## Recommendations

- **Personalize the hero for logged-in Diamond members:** Show my SkyMiles balance, Medallion tier, and a complimentary upgrade notice — or at minimum a "Welcome back, Lena" module. The AMEX card is an acquisition product; suppress it for verified elite members.
- **Swap "Learn More" for "Check My Offer":** The hero already names a specific value. The CTA should reflect that specificity — "Check My Offer" implies a personalized result, which increases click intent.
- **Add a flight-deal strip between the booking widget and the AMEX card:** Even one "Fares from BUR starting at $X" row would anchor me in flight-booking mode before the card pitch arrives.
- **Surface the operational reliability stat somewhere above the fold:** I read it in every email. A "98.9% completion rate last 30 days" badge or similar would be a meaningful trust signal for a reliability-obsessed Diamond.
