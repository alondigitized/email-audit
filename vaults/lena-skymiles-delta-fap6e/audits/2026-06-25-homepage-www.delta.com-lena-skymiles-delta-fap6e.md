# Homepage snapshot · www.delta.com · 2026-06-25
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-25**
## Executive summary

- I opened delta.com expecting the booking bar, my SkyMiles balance, and maybe a Delta One or upgrade-bid callout — and instead I'm looking at a dead tree trunk against a blue sky and an aerial action shot that looks like a skateboard park. I genuinely cannot tell what Delta is selling right now. The only legible marketing copy I see is "Discover Travel Products," which is so generic it could be anyone. For a Diamond member on a phone, this landing is a complete miss.

## What's working

- **"Discover Travel Products" subtext** at least mentions SkyMiles® Member benefits explicitly — the one moment the page acknowledges I exist as a loyalty member
- **Large full-bleed photography** loads cleanly without obvious image breakage — the page doesn't appear to have a rendering crash, just very odd content choices
- **Cookie consent banner** is visible and functional, which is table stakes compliance done correctly

## What's weak

- **The hero image** — an aerial shot of what looks like a skateboarder or activity from above — has zero connection to air travel, Delta branding, or premium travel; I had to look twice to confirm I was on delta.com
- **The dead tree image** taking up roughly half the visible viewport is baffling; it communicates nothing about flights, upgrades, or destinations
- **No flight search bar anywhere** above the fold — as a Diamond member who books direct on delta.com specifically, the absence of a "From / To / Date" module is a deal-breaker
- **No SkyMiles callout** — I track my balance and Choice Benefits cadence obsessively; landing here with zero loyalty signal feels like Delta doesn't know who I am
- **"Details" as the only CTA** is almost comedically understated for a brand I spend thousands of dollars with annually
- **Cookie banner overlapping content** at the bottom adds friction before I've even oriented myself on the page

## Recommendations

- **Replace the current hero imagery immediately** with something that signals premium air travel — a Delta One cabin shot, a recognizable destination city, or a reliability/on-time stat that speaks to operational confidence (which is literally what I read for in every email)
- **Surface the flight search bar above the fold** — this is the number-one job of delta.com for a returning member; burying it or removing it from the viewport kills direct bookings
- **Add a SkyMiles recognition module** in the top section — even a "Sign in to see your status and Choice Benefits" banner would signal that Diamond and Medallion members get a differentiated experience
- **Swap "Details" for a specific action CTA** — something like "Explore Member Benefits" or "Book a Trip" with Delta red button styling; "Details" is what you put on a content card, not a hero
