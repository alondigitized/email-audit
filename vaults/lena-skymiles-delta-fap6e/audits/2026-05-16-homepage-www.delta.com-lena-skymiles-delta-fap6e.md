# Homepage snapshot · www.delta.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Delta's mobile homepage is clean, on-brand dark navy with the flight search widget front and center — exactly where I'd expect it. What's dominating the viewport right now is an Amex credit card co-branded acquisition push for 80,000 miles, not a booking experience built for someone who already has Diamond status. It reads like Delta is using me, an already-loyal frequent flyer, as a vehicle to sell me a credit card I probably already have.

## What's working

- **Flight search widget above the fold** — "Flights / Stays / Cars / Vacations / Cruises" tabs are right there, my origin "BUR – Burbank, CA" is already populated. That's a real convenience signal; Delta knows where I am.
- **Clean navigation rail** — "Book / Check-in / My Trips / Flight Status" covers my core jobs-to-be-done in four taps. No clutter.
- **Dark navy brand palette** — Feels premium and instantly recognizable as Delta. I trust it.
- **No modal/interstitial friction** — The page just loads. No cookie banners or pop-up overlays eating my screen.

## What's weak

- **The Amex 80,000-mile hero is wasted real estate for me** — I'm Diamond. I almost certainly have an Amex Delta card. Showing me a new-cardholder acquisition banner as the hero is tone-deaf. It's not a flight deal, it's not a SkyMiles balance hook, it's not a Choice Benefit reminder.
- **No personalization whatsoever above the fold** — I'm not logged in, and Delta is treating me like a cold prospect. A returning Diamond should see at least a "Log in to see your miles" nudge that feels warm, not a generic "Sign Up" button in plain text.
- **"Learn More" is a weak CTA for this context** — If this is the hero offer, I need "Apply Now" or something that matches the action. "Learn More" signals uncertainty about what I'm even doing.
- **No operational reliability signal anywhere on screen** — I specifically read for on-time stats, cancellation rates, and operational performance data. Zero of that is present.
- **No Choice Benefits or status-relevant callout** — It's mid-May. Choice Benefits window matters. There's no "Select your 2026 Medallion Choice Benefit" prompt, which is a missed personalization layup.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the Amex hero with a logged-in status module** — Even without auth, show a "Log in to see your miles + upgrade eligibility" tile that makes me feel like a member coming home, not a lead to convert.
- **Add an operational reliability strip** — A single line below the search widget: "Delta ranked #1 in on-time arrivals among major U.S. carriers — Q1 2026." Costs nothing, earns everything with someone like me.
- **Swap "Learn More" for contextually correct CTA copy** — If the goal is card acquisition, say "Check Your Offer" or "Apply Now." "Learn More" is a landing page hedge, not a conversion CTA.
- **Surface the Choice Benefits reminder banner for known Medallion members** — Post-login state should immediately show a "Your 2026 Choice Benefit selection closes [date]" strip. I'd tap that immediately.
