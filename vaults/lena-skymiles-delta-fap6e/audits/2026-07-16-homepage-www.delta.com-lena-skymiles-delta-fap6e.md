# Homepage snapshot · www.delta.com · 2026-07-16
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-16**
## Executive summary

- Opening delta.com on my phone, I'm not getting the polished loyalty experience I expect from an airline I spend thousands of dollars a year flying. The viewport shows a "Discover Travel Products" sub-module, a massive blue-sky-and-dead-tree image that reads more like a nature photography site than a premium carrier, and a cookie consent banner blocking the bottom of the screen. Whatever the actual hero is, I'm not seeing a flight-search widget, my SkyMiles balance, or any Diamond-tier acknowledgment. This looks like a mid-scroll moment, not a curated first impression.

## What's working

- **"Discover Travel Products" copy-block** — the sub-headline explicitly mentions "SkyMiles® Member benefits," which is the one signal that acknowledges I exist as a loyalty member. It's buried and small, but it's there.
- **Clean text contrast** — the small-print section over the white background is legible at a glance; no readability issues.
- **Cookie consent is conspicuous** — the red "I understand" button is easy to dismiss fast, which is the one tap I'd actually want to take quickly.

## What's weak

- **The dead tree / blue sky image** — takes up roughly 40% of the viewport with zero copy, zero CTA, zero relevance to flying Delta. I have no idea what this is communicating. If this is a sustainability campaign visual, there's no label to tell me that.
- **No flight search widget visible** — the single most important module for an airline homepage is completely absent from what I can see. I open delta.com to book or check a flight, not to discover "travel products."
- **No loyalty surface** — Diamond Medallion status gets me zero recognition on this screen. No tier badge, no balance, no "your Choice Benefits are ready" prompt. This is a miss for retention.
- **Aerial rocky-landscape top image** — the top crop appears to show a dusty overhead view of climbers or canyon terrain. This signals adventure/outdoor gear, not a carrier I trust for operational reliability.
- **"Details" as sole CTA** — a lowercase, unformatted link labeled "Details" is the least compelling call-to-action possible. No button affordance, no urgency, no specificity about what I'd find.

## Recommendations

- **Lead with the flight search widget on mobile load** — pin the origin/destination/date input to the top of the viewport so I can search in one tap. Every millisecond I spend scrolling past lifestyle imagery is a millisecond closer to going to Google Flights.
- **Surface my SkyMiles balance and Medallion tier in the nav or a sticky banner** — a logged-in Diamond member should see "Diamond | 87,432 miles" immediately. This is the easiest personalization win and it costs nothing.
- **Replace the dead-tree blue-sky image with a campaign visual that has copy and a CTA** — if this is an environmental/sustainability story, give it a headline and a "Learn more" button. An image with no context is just noise.
- **Swap "Details" for a high-contrast button with specific copy** — something like "Explore Vacation Packages" or "Book Hotel + Flight" gives me a reason to tap. "Details" tells me nothing.
