# Homepage snapshot · www.delta.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Delta.com hits me as clean and functional the second it loads — the booking widget is right there, which is the right instinct. But the moment I scroll past the search form, the whole above-the-fold real estate gets handed to a Delta AMEX card acquisition pitch. As a Diamond Medallion who almost certainly already carries the card, I feel completely unrecognized. This homepage is talking to a prospect, not to me.

## What's working

- **Booking widget front and center** — "Flights" tab is pre-selected, BUR is already populated (presumably from a prior session or location), which is a solid personalization signal for a direct-booker like me
- **Persistent top nav** — "Book," "Check-in," "My Trips," "Flight Status" are all one tap away; that's exactly the four things I do on this app
- **Log-in button visible** — red and prominent, easy to get to my account fast
- **Notification badge (1)** — that little "1" on the bell actually gets my attention; I want to know what it is

## What's weak

- **The hero is a credit card ad** — I'm here to book a flight or check upgrade status. A 100,000-mile card acquisition offer is completely irrelevant to a Diamond Medallion who almost certainly has the Amex Reserve already
- **"Learn More" CTA is vague** — for a $500+ annual-fee card with a complex offer structure, "Learn More" undersells. And for me it's noise, not a call to action at all
- **No logged-in state or personalization above the fold** — no "Welcome back, Lena," no SkyMiles balance, no upcoming trip summary. Delta has all my data; none of it shows
- **Zero reliability or operational stats visible** — I literally read the on-time performance stat in every Delta email. The homepage doesn't surface any of that
- **No Delta One or premium cabin hook** — I book Delta One when it's a business trip. There's no upgrade availability, no Delta One promo, nothing that speaks to premium cabin loyalty

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero for a logged-in, personalized module** — show my SkyMiles balance, my next upcoming trip, and my current Choice Benefits deadlines. I should never see a card acquisition pitch if I'm already in the Medallion tier
- **Surface a flight deal or route-specific promo** — something like "Nonstop BUR → JFK from $X — book before [date]" uses the BUR origin that's already in the search field and gives me an actual reason to convert
- **Add an operational reliability badge above the fold** — a simple "Delta: #1 on-time performance in April" chip near the booking widget costs nothing and speaks directly to why I pick Delta over cheaper carriers
- **Replace "Learn More" with "See My Offer"** — if the card ad must stay, at least make the CTA feel personalized and direct rather than generic acquisition-funnel copy
