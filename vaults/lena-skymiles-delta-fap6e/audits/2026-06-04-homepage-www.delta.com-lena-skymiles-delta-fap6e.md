# Homepage snapshot · www.delta.com · 2026-06-04
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-04**
## Executive summary

- Delta's homepage is showing me a SkyMiles *acquisition* push — "Join for Free" — when I'm their best customer. The search widget is smart (pre-filled BUR for Burbank, which is where I am), and the booking interface is clean and functional. But the hero is spending its real estate recruiting strangers into SkyMiles when it could be talking to the Diamond Medallion staring back at it.

## What's working

- **Pre-filled BUR origin in the search widget** — Delta pulled my location and saved me a step. For someone who books constantly, this is the right instinct.
- **Tabbed booking rail (Flights / Stays / Cars / Vacations / Cruises)** — everything I might need is one tap away; clear, efficient, no cognitive load.
- **"My Trips" in the top nav** — I can get to my itinerary in one tap without logging in first, which matters when I'm at the gate and in a hurry.
- **Notification badge (3) on the bell icon** — signals that there's personalized activity waiting for me, which is a reason to log in.
- **Delta AMEX Cards strip at bottom** — I'm a cardholder; seeing that integration acknowledged is a soft trust signal.

## What's weak

- **"Join for Free" hero CTA is dead air for me** — I've been a Diamond for three years. This is the biggest misfire. The hero slot is the most valuable real estate on the page and it's spending it recruiting beginners.
- **No Medallion benefit callout** — Same-day confirmed changes, Sky Priority boarding, complimentary upgrades — none of it is visible. Delta's differentiator vs. United/AA for someone like me is operational reliability; that word doesn't appear once.
- **"Join SkyMiles" in the top-left** — I'm not logged in, so Delta doesn't know who I am, fair. But even a "Welcome Back / Log in to see your status" message would feel warmer than generic acquisition.
- **No fare deals or mileage offers** — Nothing pulls me toward an action beyond booking. No "Earn 2x miles this weekend," no SkyMiles flash sale. I came to get value, not just to search.
- **Hero image reads ambiguous** — Dark cabin scene could be Delta One, but it's too murky to feel aspirational. I can barely tell what the product is.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the "Join for Free" CTA for a Log In prompt with a value hook** — "Log in to see your upgrade waitlist and Choice Benefits" converts returning Diamond members immediately and costs nothing to ship.
- **Replace or augment the hero with an operational reliability stat** — Something like "99.5% on-time completion, June 2026" or "Delta One: now serving 22 transcon routes." That is the reason I pick Delta over a cheaper fare, and it's invisible here.
- **Add a member-pricing or miles-bonus banner above the search widget** — Even a thin strip saying "SkyMiles Medallion members: earn 9x miles on all Delta.com bookings" reminds me why I'm here and not on Google Flights.
- **Make the notification badge actionable without logging in** — Tapping the bell with 3 notifications should prompt a quick log-in modal, not a dead tap. Friction at that moment is a missed conversion.
