# Homepage snapshot · www.delta.com · 2026-07-02
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-02**
## Executive summary

- I opened delta.com expecting my SkyMiles dashboard, a flight search widget, and maybe a Diamond-tier recognition moment — and instead got an aerial overhead shot of what looks like a skateboarder, followed by a dead tree trunk pointing at a blue sky. There is a cookie consent modal plastered across the bottom. This feels like a brand campaign page misfired as a homepage, completely disconnected from what a loyal, high-frequency traveler needs the second she opens the app.

## What's working

- **SkyMiles brand mention** in the "Discover Travel Products" body copy at least signals this is a loyalty-aware product, not a generic travel aggregator.
- **Cookie consent is standard and non-hostile** — the "I understand" button is clear, no dark pattern there.

## What's weak

- **The hero imagery is baffling.** An aerial overhead shot of what looks like someone skateboarding on sand, followed by a dead tree trunk against blue sky — neither communicates "fly Delta" to me. If this is meant to be evocative travel photography, it reads as a miscropped editorial asset.
- **No flight search widget visible.** As a Diamond Medallion who opens delta.com to book, search, or check status, the absence of any search or booking UI above the fold is a total failure.
- **"Details" as the only CTA** is almost invisible — a lowercase hyperlink under a paragraph of small body text is not a CTA.
- **No Medallion recognition.** I'm logged in or about to log in — where's "Welcome back, Lena" or my SkyMiles balance?
- **The cookie modal obscures the bottom of the page** without revealing anything useful underneath.
- **"Discover Travel Products" is a weak headline** for a flagship airline homepage. It reads like a sidebar widget, not a hero.

## Recommendations

- **Replace these two imagery blocks with a single flight-search hero** — the canonical delta.com pattern of a destination photo behind the booking widget is what Diamond members expect and need; ship that layout instead.
- **Surface a logged-in or "sign in to see your miles" rail immediately** — even an unauthenticated user should see a SkyMiles balance prompt as the first interactive element.
- **Upgrade the CTA to a full button** — "Search Flights" or "Book Now" in Delta red, full-width on mobile, directly below any value prop copy.
- **Audit the hero asset pipeline** — the dead tree trunk image looks like a CMS error or a broken asset swap; QA before the next deploy.
