# Homepage snapshot · www.delta.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- Delta.com greets me with a credit card acquisition pitch, not a travel experience. As a Diamond Medallion who's already deep in the SkyMiles ecosystem and probably already holding an AMEX Reserve, the hero is optimized for someone who is *not* me. The bones are solid — clean layout, functional search widget with my home airport pre-filled — but the brand is spending its most valuable screen real estate recruiting card members rather than rewarding loyalty.

## What's working

- **BUR pre-fill in search widget** — the origin field already knows I fly out of Burbank. That's the kind of frictionless detail that keeps me booking direct instead of going to Google Flights.
- **Flights tab selected by default** — correct assumption. I'm here to book a flight, not a rental car.
- **Clean mobile layout** — no overlapping elements, no layout shift, the navigation is tight and legible. Delta's app-like mobile execution is genuinely good.
- **"My Trips" in the top nav** — quick access to my itineraries is exactly what I need when I'm en route and checking in.
- **"Log in" is prominent** — easy to get to my account and see my actual status once I tap it.

## What's weak

- **Card acquisition as the hero** — I'm a Diamond Medallion. There's an extremely high probability I already carry this card. Showing me a 100,000-mile welcome offer I can't redeem is noise at best, mildly insulting at best.
- **Not logged in = anonymous experience** — Delta knows me. I've been Diamond for years. The fact that the page shows generic "Sign Up / Log in" instead of my name, SkyMiles balance, or upcoming trip is a real miss. This is where the loyalty experience should shine.
- **"Learn your offer" copy is weaselly** — "which could be as high as" followed by the same number twice is the kind of hedged language I hate. Just say what the offer is or don't show it to me.
- **No flight fare deals above the fold** — not a single fare, not a single route promo, no seasonal fare sale. For someone who just wants to book, there's no incentive surfaced here.
- **Zero operational signals** — I read the on-time stats in every email. Nothing here about reliability, on-time performance, or operational status. That's a missed brand signal for flyers like me.

## Recommendations

- **Personalize the hero for logged-in returning members** — if a cookie or session exists, swap the card acquisition hero for a "Welcome back, Lena — you're 12,000 miles from Platinum 360" or a next-trip confirmation widget. Card offers should be a secondary tile, not the hero, for existing cardholders.
- **Surface a fare deal or route promo above the fold** — even a "Flash Sale: BUR → JFK from $189" banner would give me a reason to engage rather than just search and leave.
- **Add a logged-in state shortcut** — a single "Log in to see your benefits" micro-CTA directly in the search area would dramatically improve relevance for loyalty members who arrive on mobile.
- **Replace "Learn More" with "See Your Offer"** — if the offer is personalized, the CTA should say so. "Learn More" is generic; "See Your Offer" signals something tailored.
