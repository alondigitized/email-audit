# Reminder: Your $50 in Delta eCredits Is Waiting To Be Earned
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-purpose reminder for the Delta x Starbucks "New to SkyMiles" offer. It follows the 2026-10-21 send, "Enroll In Your New To SkyMiles® Offer To Earn Up To $50 In Delta eCredits". The hero reads "Your Next $25 eCredit Is a Sip Away" over a Delta/Starbucks co-brand lockup and a lifestyle photo. One red "LEARN MORE" button sits right under the personalized body copy. The eye lands on the offer first.
- The mechanics make the offer harder to act on than the hero implies. Two steps (enroll, then link a Starbucks Rewards account) are needed to earn $25 each, and the body copy is dense. There is also a long legal block, and a "Don't have a Starbucks Rewards account? JOIN NOW" box. This is a welcome-flow reminder for a person who already enrolled or got the earlier email. The urgency is a deadline of 11/21/2026, and it only appears in the body copy.
- It's a solid, honest reminder, but a generic CTA and a heavy two-step structure hold it back. The Skymiles Dining, Uber, Uber Eats and Airbnb partner strip near the bottom dilutes focus slightly. It is still a better fit than the repeated Amex card pitches Lena has been getting.

## What's working

- The hero is clear: "$25 eCredit is a sip away" with the Delta x Starbucks lockup and a lifestyle image.
- A single red "LEARN MORE" button follows right after the hero, so the CTA is easy to find.
- The "How to earn up to $50" section splits the offer into Step 1 and Step 2, each with a $25 callout.
- A member-ID badge ("SkyMiles® Member") at the top signals that the email is personalized.
- It is a different offer from the Amex card pitches Lena has been getting, which makes a fresh change of pace.

## What's weak

- "LEARN MORE" is a generic CTA, so the button doesn't say what you get or what to do.
- The two-step earn path (enroll, then link accounts) adds friction, and the second step depends on a Starbucks Rewards account.
- The fine print is a very long wall of tiny text, which feels heavy and buries the conditions.
- The deadline is not prominent in the hero. The 11/21/2026 date sits in the body copy, so the "reminder" has little visible urgency.
- The partner strip (Dining, Uber Eats, Airbnb) and the "Join Now" box compete with the main CTA.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace "LEARN MORE" with a specific CTA such as "Link Starbucks & Earn $25". Move the deadline into the hero as a badge, e.g. "Ends 11/21".
- 2. Show the user's progress, such as "Step 1 done, $25 earned" or "$25 still waiting". A real reminder should say where the person stands.
- 3. Cut the partner strip, or move it below the legal block, so there is one offer and one CTA.
- 4. Collapse the legal copy into a short summary with a "Full terms" link.
- 5. Make the subject specific by naming the Starbucks element and the deadline.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Link your Starbucks Rewards by 11/21 and earn your first $25 eCredit.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on Delta's global template and renders with a few structural HTML defects, one non-HTTPS image, and missing alt attributes. Authentication and unsubscribe headers could not be verified because the relay did not capture them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **36 tracking links were not probed.** The QA check skipped them as click-redirect domains, so their destinations, redirect chains and expiry are unverified.
- **Non-HTTPS image:** `http://image.o.delta.com/lib/fe5815707c6201787313/m/10/3f473969-95d8-43bc-8d81-7bdb8c2d4dd8.png`. Clients that block mixed content may drop it, and it is the only `http://` asset flagged. The other assets use `https://image.o.delta.com/...`, so this one looks like an oversight.
- The HTML source was truncated, so I can't confirm that every `href` is well-formed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content image:** `3f473969-95d8-43bc-8d81-7bdb8c2d4dd8.png`. Image-blocking clients and screen readers get no text equivalent.
- **Missing alt text, spacers:** `spacer.png` (`https://image.o.delta.com/lib/fe3711727164047e771173/m/1/spacer.png`) appears 5 times. These should carry an explicit `alt=""` so screen readers skip them. The QA check can't tell a missing attribute from an empty one.
- **Invalid document structure in the source:**
  - Content (comments and a `<div>`) comes before `<!DOCTYPE html>`, which can trigger quirks mode in some clients.
  - There is a nested `<html>` opening tag right after `<html xmlns=...>`.
  - There is a `<div style="font-size:0px; line-height:1px;">` inside `<head>`, which is invalid. Parsers will move it into `<body>` and may drop the `<style>` blocks it wraps.
- **Dead selector:** `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` has no leading dot, so it matches a nonexistent element instead of `.ReadMsgBody`.
- **Viewport meta:** `width=device-width; initial-scale=1.0; user-scalable=yes` uses semicolons instead of commas, which is non-standard.
- **Accessibility tooling is partly in place:** the 06/02/2025 template note says aria tags were added, and `<w:DontUseAdvancedTypographyReadingMail/>` is present. I can't confirm aria coverage in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unresolved merge tokens. The lookup, variable and Omniture blocks are HTML comments, so they aren't rendering as raw text. The body past the truncation point wasn't visible.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found.** The QA tool notes this may be a relay capture gap, so it is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The same caveat applies. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so check this against the raw headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unverified. The sender is `DeltaAirLines@o.delta.com`, a subdomain of `delta.com`. Check alignment in the raw message.
- The footer's physical address and unsubscribe link sit beyond the truncated source, so they are unconfirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. Tracking links were skipped and the source was truncated, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be confirmed. Nothing here is flagged as broken.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` text to `3f473969-95d8-43bc-8d81-7bdb8c2d4dd8.png` and set `alt=""` on all `spacer.png` instances.
2. Switch the `http://image.o.delta.com/...3f473969...png` URL to `https://`.
3. Fix the document structure:
   - Put `<!DOCTYPE>` first.
   - Remove the duplicate `<html>` tag.
   - Move the `<div>` wrappers out of `<head>`.
   - Change `ReadMsgBody` to `.ReadMsgBody`.
   - Use commas in the viewport meta.
4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` against the raw message headers rather than the relay copy.
5. Resolve a sample of the 36 tracking links in a separate pass to confirm destinations and UTM consistency.
