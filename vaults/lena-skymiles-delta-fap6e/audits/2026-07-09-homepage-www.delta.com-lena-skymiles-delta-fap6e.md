# Homepage snapshot · www.delta.com · 2026-07-09
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-09**
## Executive summary

- I opened delta.com expecting a clean flight-search experience or at least a SkyMiles dashboard nudge — what I got instead was an overhead drone shot of something hard to identify and a cookie banner eating the bottom of the screen. The one legible piece of copy pushes "Discover Travel Products" (hotels, car rentals, cruises) which reads like Delta is trying to be Expedia rather than the reliable premium carrier I book direct. This page is working against itself for a Diamond Medallion who already knows exactly why she flies Delta.

## What's working

- **SkyMiles mention in the subhead** — "get exclusive SkyMiles® Member benefits" at least acknowledges the loyalty program exists. For someone tracking Choice Benefits cadence, any loyalty callout is table stakes.
- **Clean mobile layout structure** — no overlapping text or broken images in the readable section; the copy block under "Discover Travel Products" is legible even if the content is off-target.

## What's weak

- **Hero image #1 (overhead shot)** — indistinct on mobile. I genuinely cannot tell if this is a skateboarding clip or a rock-climbing shot. Zero connection to flying Delta or premium travel.
- **Hero image #2 (bare tree, blue sky)** — visually striking but context-free. No destination label, no route, no sense of "this is where Delta takes you."
- **"Details" as the primary CTA** — this is the weakest possible call to action. It reads like a disclaimer link, not an invitation to transact.
- **No flight search above the fold** — I'm a Diamond who books direct on delta.com. The homepage should lead with the search widget; instead it buries me in ancillary travel products.
- **Cookie banner** — occupies meaningful real estate at the bottom of an already sparse screen, making the page feel interrupted before I've even started.
- **"Discover Travel Products" framing** — I didn't open delta.com to discover hotels and car rentals. I'm here to book or check my SkyMiles. This cross-sell feels like it belongs on page three, not the hero.

## Recommendations

- **Lead with the flight-search widget or a personalized returning-member banner** — a logged-in Diamond should see their miles balance, an upgrade waitlist nudge, or a "your next trip" module before anything else.
- **Swap the abstract overhead hero for a Delta One cabin or Lounge lifestyle shot** — something that visually confirms "premium, reliable, worth the fare premium" to a Medallion who has alternatives.
- **Replace "Details" with a real CTA button** — "Search Flights," "Check My SkyMiles," or "View My Benefits" — any of these would reduce bounce for someone like me.
- **Add a visible summer deal or miles-bonus strip** — even "Earn 3X miles on international Delta One bookings through July 31" would anchor me. Right now there's zero transactional reason to stay.
