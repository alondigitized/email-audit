# Homepage snapshot · www.delta.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- This is Delta doing what Delta does best structurally — clean nav, search front-and-center — but the above-the-fold pitch is a credit card acquisition play aimed at someone who doesn't already have the Delta AMEX. As a Diamond Medallion who very likely already carries that card, I'm being pitched a "welcome offer" that doesn't apply to me. The biggest miss: I'm not logged in, so there's zero personalization — no miles balance, no Diamond status acknowledgment, no upgrade availability. For a brand that earns my premium fare loyalty every single week, this opening screen is anonymous.

## What's working

- **BUR pre-filled as origin.** Delta detected my location (Burbank, CA) and pre-populated the departure airport. That's exactly the friction-reduction I expect from a high-tech brand. One less tap.
- **Flights tab is already selected.** I don't have to navigate to tell it I want to fly. The default is right for my primary use case.
- **Search widget placement.** It's above the fold, functional, and visually clear. Book → Check-in → My Trips → Flight Status nav is exactly how I think about my interactions with Delta.
- **Clean render.** No jank, no layout shift, no broken images. The dark navy brand palette is consistent and sharp on mobile.
- **Notification bell with badge.** There's a badge on the bell icon — if that's my upgrade or check-in notification, that's useful operational info one tap away.

## What's weak

- **Not logged in.** This is the single biggest failure. I'm a Diamond Medallion — I should land on a homepage that knows who I am. My miles balance, my upgrade availability on upcoming flights, my Choice Benefits status. Instead I get the same cold screen as a first-time visitor.
- **AMEX card hero for an existing cardholder.** The dominant above-fold module is a credit card welcome offer. I have the Delta AMEX Platinum Reserve. This is the wrong message. It wastes the most valuable real estate on the page.
- **No operational reliability signal.** I read the on-time performance stat in every email Delta sends me. There's nothing here that reinforces Delta's operational story — no punctuality badge, no upgrade queue stat, nothing.
- **No upgrade or travel horizon teaser.** I have upcoming trips. Delta knows this. A "your next flight is in 3 days" or "upgrade seat available on ATL→LAX" hook would make me feel like I landed in the right place.
- **"Gift Cards" as a feature link.** Below the hero, the two quick-access links are "Delta AMEX Cards" and "Gift Cards." Neither is relevant to a Diamond Medallion opening the app mid-week to manage travel.

## Recommendations

- **Log me in by default or show a persistent "Welcome back, Lena" rail.** If Delta knows my location well enough to pre-fill BUR, it knows enough to surface a login prompt with my initials or a "continue as Lena" shortcut. The first thing I should see is my miles balance and status.
- **Replace the AMEX hero with a flight-moment hero.** Swap the card acquisition module for a "Your next trip is in 3 days — check in now" or "Upgrade available: LAX→JFK Delta One" card. That's a conversion, not a distraction.
- **Add an operational reliability badge above the fold.** A single line — "86% on-time departures this month" or the current D:0 stat — would immediately speak to why Diamond Medallions pay the premium fare. Ship it as a persistent strip under the search widget.
- **Swap "Gift Cards" quick link for "My Upgrades" or "Choice Benefits."** Diamond Medallions are in active Choice Benefits selection season. Surface the thing I actually need to do.
