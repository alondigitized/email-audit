# Homepage snapshot · www.delta.com · 2026-08-06
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- Whatever loaded on my screen doesn't feel like the Delta I book direct with every time. Instead of a flight search bar or the on-time reliability stat I usually skim past out of habit, I'm looking at a vague "Discover Travel Products" blurb sitting on top of a photo that's mostly blank blue sky with a dead tree trunk in it. If I didn't know better, I'd think the page hadn't finished loading.

## What's working

- The "Discover Travel Products" card at least name-checks "SkyMiles® Member benefits" — as a Diamond Medallion, that phrase is the one thing on this screen that's actually speaking to me.
- The cookie consent banner is plain and not trying to trick me — clear "I understand" button, no dark-pattern maze to dismiss it.

## What's weak

- That huge blue-sky image below the product card is basically empty — just sky and a bare tree trunk. No copy, no CTA, no product in it. It reads like a broken or badly cropped image render, not a deliberate design choice.
- There's no flight search box, no route/date picker, nothing that lets me actually start booking — which is the entire reason I'm on delta.com.
- No operational reliability stat anywhere in this view. That's one of the first things I look for and it's just absent here.
- The "Discover Travel Products" copy is generic upsell (hotels, cars, cruises) rather than anything tailored to a Delta One / SkyMiles-tier flyer like me.
- The cookie banner is pinned at the very bottom eating into what little content is visible, on top of an already sparse screen.

## Recommendations

- Cut or drastically crop that blank blue-sky image — it's wasting the entire lower half of the screen with nothing actionable in it.
- Put a flight search widget (from/to, dates, "Book" button) at the top of this view, not a generic travel-products upsell card.
- Surface the on-time performance stat or a SkyMiles balance/status callout near the top — that's what pulls me in every time.
- Turn "Details" into an actual button with specific copy like "Explore Hotels & Cars" so it doesn't read as an afterthought link.
