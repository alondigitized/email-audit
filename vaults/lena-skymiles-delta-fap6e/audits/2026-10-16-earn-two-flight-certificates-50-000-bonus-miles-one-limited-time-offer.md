# Earn Two Flight Certificates. 50,000 Bonus Miles. One Limited Time Offer.​
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-16**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth send of the same Amex SkyMiles Reserve pitch to Lena: two Comfort flight certificates, 50K bonus miles, and a $10K spend in 6 months. It follows Aug 28, Sep 4, Sep 18, Sep 25 and Oct 8. The creative is clean. A dark hero with the card art sits above a "FIRST TIME EVER" kicker, and a blue "VIEW DETAILS" button follows. Four destination tiles (San Juan, St. Thomas, Los Angeles, New York) come after that, then a second CTA.
- The execution is competent, but "first time ever" no longer works after six sends in about seven weeks. The only new element is the "Offer ends November 4, 2026" line, which adds real urgency, but it is small body text. It is not in the hero or the subject. The email also ends in a very long wall of legal copy, which makes the offer feel less honest.
- Verdict: solid craft, heavy fatigue, and a primary CTA ("VIEW DETAILS") that is weaker than the second one ("VIEW OFFER").

## What's working

- The hero is clear. "EARN TWO DELTA COMFORT FLIGHTS" and "+50K BONUS MILES" make the eye land on the offer first.
- The Delta branding is recognizable, and the card art ties the offer to the product.
- The destination tiles (San Juan, St. Thomas, LA, NY) add visual interest and give the certificates concrete uses.
- The November 4 deadline gives the send a credible end date for the first time in this run.
- The Lena-personalized intro paragraph and the "Skymiles Member" header tag make it feel one-to-one.

## What's weak

- It is the sixth near-identical send since Aug 28, and the offer is unchanged. "First time ever" now reads as stale.
- The hero CTA, "VIEW DETAILS," is passive. The "VIEW OFFER" button below the tiles is better but sits further down.
- The deadline and the $550 annual fee are in small text under the hero, so the urgency and the cost are easy to miss.
- The $10K-in-6-months spend requirement is only in the body copy, not in the hero.
- The footer legal block is very long and dense. It dwarfs the creative and weakens the sense that the offer is honest.
- The destination tiles don't link the certificates to specific fares or routes.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero ("Ends Nov 4") and replace the "first time ever" kicker with a countdown.
- 2. Change the hero CTA to "Apply Now" or "Claim 2 Certificates," and use the same copy on the second button.
- 3. State the $10K spend and the $550 fee near the hero so the offer reads as honest.
- 4. Give the tiles real value, such as "Fly to San Juan from $X with a certificate."
- 5. Cut the cadence. Suppress or vary the creative for people who have already seen it several times.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Lena, your 2 flight certificates expire Nov 4`
- **Subject Alt B:** `2 Comfort certificates + 50K miles: ends Nov 4`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $10K in 6 months and unlock two round-trip certificates. Offer ends Nov 4.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `San Juan, LA, New York: where would your certificates take you?`

# Technical Audit: Delta Air Lines, "Earn Two Flight Certificates. 50,000 Bonus Miles."

## 1. Technical Summary
The template is a mature Salesforce Marketing Cloud build with dark-mode, MSO and accessibility handling. The QA run found one confirmed asset defect: an image with no alt text served over `http://`. Several HTML structure problems are also visible in the source. The footer and body were truncated, and the compliance headers were not captured by the relay, so those areas can't be fully verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **28 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination, redirect chain and UTM integrity are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Insecure image URL:** `http://image.o.delta.com/lib/fe5815707c6201787313/m/10/3f473969-95d8-43bc-8d81-7bdb8c2d4dd8.png` uses plain HTTP. Some clients block or rewrite non-HTTPS images, which can leave a blank slot. The same host likely serves the other images, so check whether they use `https://`.
- No broken links were reported (0 issues).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `3f473969-95d8-43bc-8d81-7bdb8c2d4dd8.png` has no `alt` attribute. Screen readers will announce the filename or skip the image, and nothing displays if images are blocked. The missing attribute is worse than an empty `alt=""` if the image is decorative.
- **Content before the doctype:** Comments and a `<div style="font-size:0px; line-height:1px;">` precede `<!DOCTYPE html>`. This can push some clients into quirks mode.
- **Duplicate root element:** `<html xmlns=...>` is immediately followed by a second bare `<html>`, which is invalid.
- **`<div>` inside `<head>`:** The `<!-- BEGIN CSS BLOCK -->` `<div>` wraps the `<style>` tags. Parsers will close `<head>` early at this point, so the styles may be treated as body content. Gmail and Outlook generally tolerate this, but it is invalid markup.
- **Non-standard viewport syntax:** `content="width=device-width; initial-scale=1.0; user-scalable=yes"` uses semicolons, where the spec uses commas. Most clients tolerate it.
- **Ineffective CSS selector:** `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` has no leading dot, so it targets a nonexistent element instead of the class `.ReadMsgBody`. This is harmless and dead code.
- **Positives:** `lang="en" dir="ltr"` is set, `<title>` is present, and `color-scheme` meta tags and Outlook `DontUseAdvancedTypographyReadingMail` are included.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unrendered tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `${…}`) appear in the truncated HTML. The "SET VARIABLES / LOOKUP SCRIPTS" comments are inert. The truncated body was not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** QA did not find either header. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**. Verify against the raw message before treating it as a defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results:** This header is missing, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The note attributes it to the relay.
- **Footer:** The visible HTML is truncated before the footer, so the physical address and unsubscribe link can't be confirmed.
- **Sender:** `DeltaAirLines@o.delta.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, consistent with proper alignment. This is not confirmed by headers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 28 tracking links were skipped, so the final landing URLs and campaign parameters were never resolved. No mismatch is confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a descriptive `alt` (or `alt=""` if decorative) to `3f473969-95d8-43bc-8d81-7bdb8c2d4dd8.png`.
2. Switch `image.o.delta.com` image sources to `https://`, for this asset and all others.
3. Fix the template structure: put `<!DOCTYPE html>` first, remove the duplicate `<html>`, and move the CSS wrapper `<div>` out of `<head>`.
4. Change the viewport meta to comma-separated values and fix the `.ReadMsgBody` selector.
5. Re-run QA against the raw message, not the relay copy, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
6. Resolve a sample of the 28 tracking links through their redirects, checking for HTTP 200, HTTPS final URLs and consistent UTM parameters.
