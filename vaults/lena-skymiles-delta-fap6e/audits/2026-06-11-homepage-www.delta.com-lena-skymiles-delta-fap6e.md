# Homepage snapshot · www.delta.com · 2026-06-11
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-11**
## Executive summary

- This reads like a Delta homepage that knows who it's building for — a SkyMiles loyalist who tracks mileage the way other people track a bank balance. The brand feels reliable and transactional in exactly the right way: no lifestyle fluff, just the booking widget and a real offer with a real deadline. What it's pushing right now is a SkyMiles AMEX card acquisition, which lands very differently depending on whether I already carry that card.

## What's working

- **"Earn Up to 90,000 Miles" headline**: That number earns my attention immediately. I track accrual velocity the way other people track interest rates.
- **Booking widget pre-populated with BUR**: My home airport shows as the origin — the friction to starting a search is basically zero.
- **"My Trips" in the primary nav**: Half the time I come to delta.com to check on something, not book something. Having that front-and-center respects how I actually use the site.
- **Notification bell with 3 alerts**: Pulls me toward logging in right away to see what's pending — smart low-pressure engagement.
- **July 15, 2026 deadline**: A specific end date I can act on or not. No artificial "ends tonight!" pressure — just a real calendar date.

## What's weak

- **Free bag pitch lands wrong for a Diamond Medallion**: I already get free checked bags as a status perk. Leading with that as an AMEX card benefit either means Delta isn't segmenting this offer by status tier, or they are and I'm not the target — but I'm here anyway, and it reads as noise.
- **No logged-in personalization**: I'm not authenticated, so I'm looking at a generic card acquisition module instead of my SkyMiles balance, my next booked trip, or my Choice Benefits expiration. For a Medallion-level user, that's a missed moment of recognition.
- **Credit card acquisition dominates the viewport**: Below the booking widget, the entire above-fold visual story is "get this card" — I came to book a flight.
- **No destination inspiration**: Not one "Fly to Lisbon from $X" or "Summer deals from BUR" callout. The page is all utility, no wanderlust.

## Recommendations

- **Build a logged-in hero module for Diamond Medallion members**: Replace the card acquisition banner with a personalized status snapshot — miles balance, next tier progress, Choice Benefits reminder. That's the homepage I actually want.
- **Suppress the AMEX bag benefit pitch for existing cardholders / Diamond members**: Show the 90k offer only to non-cardholders. For me, lead with an accelerator miles offer or a companion certificate callout instead.
- **Add a destination rail below the booking widget**: Three cards — top routes from my home airport, with pricing — turns this from a utility page into a reason to book something I hadn't planned.
- **Move "free bag" fine-print messaging to mobile app where it's contextual**: On the web homepage, every square pixel competing with the booking flow should earn its place. This one doesn't.
