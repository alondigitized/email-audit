# Homepage snapshot · www.delta.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- Delta's homepage is showing me a credit card acquisition pitch as the hero — which is fine for a first-time visitor but feels completely off-pitch for someone like me who's been Diamond for three years and already has the Reserve card in my wallet. The booking widget is front and center, which I appreciate, but everything below it is screaming "become a SkyMiles customer" rather than "welcome back, here's what matters to your trip."

## What's working

- **Flight search widget is immediately usable** — origin pre-filled to BUR/Burbank, swap arrows, destination field ready. One tap to start booking, which is exactly what I want.
- **Top navigation is clean and functional** — Book, Check-in, My Trips, Flight Status are all there without clutter. "My Trips" is one tap away.
- **80,000 miles is a real number** — even if the offer doesn't apply to me, the specificity signals that Delta isn't hiding the headline value behind vague "earn more miles" language.
- **No forced interstitials or pop-up modals** — I landed on the page and it just worked. That matters on mobile more than most brands realize.

## What's weak

- **I am not logged in, and Delta doesn't care** — The header shows "Sign Up / Log in" but makes no attempt to recognize me or prompt a personalized view. I'm Diamond. My miles balance, my upcoming flights, my upgrade waitlist position — none of that is on screen. This is the #1 miss.
- **The hero is a credit card ad, not a travel homepage** — "Welcome Offer As High As 80,000 Miles*" is squarely aimed at new customers. As a returning Diamond, this feels like being served a first-timer ad on my own airline's app.
- **"Learn More" is the weakest possible CTA copy** — For a card offer I likely already have, this button is dead air. Even for the target audience, "Learn More" signals friction, not conversion.
- **No operational reliability signal anywhere** — This is the stat I look for in every email and it's absent from the viewport. On-time performance, upgrade availability, even a brief "your SkyMiles never expire" reassurance — nothing.
- **Asterisk fine print and hedging copy** — "which may be as high as 80K Miles" plus "*Minimum purchase required. Terms apply." in the same card feels like Delta is already pre-apologizing for the actual offer.
- **No Diamond or elite tier acknowledgment** — Even a generic "Log in to see your status" prompt would tell me Delta knows I exist.

## Recommendations

- **Personalize the hero for recognized/logged-in users** — Even a soft "Welcome back, sign in to see your miles and upgrades" swap on the hero would dramatically shift the experience for returning high-value customers. Delta has the tech; ship it.
- **Replace "Learn More" with action-oriented copy** — "Check My Offer" or "See If You Qualify" for the card, or better yet, A/B test showing the booking-focused hero to users who already have SkyMiles cookies.
- **Add a thin loyalty status bar below the nav** — Something like "You have 124,000 miles · 3 upgrades pending" for logged-in users. No scrolling required. This is table stakes for an airline app and it's absent here.
- **Swap the adventure-hiker imagery for a Delta One cabin or airport lounge shot** — A clean, premium travel aesthetic speaks directly to business travelers like me. Patagonia vibes belong on an REI homepage.
