# PRICE DROP ON BODYSUITS 📣
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-02**
## Executive summary

- This is the tenth Carter's email in ten consecutive days, and the creative team is clearly stretching. "PRICE DROP ON BODYSUITS" is a smart pivot — product-specific, category-focused, a departure from the generic clearance banners that have dominated since June 23. The problem is that the email doesn't deliver on the promise: the hero shows six "Easy Snap Bodysuits" with zero visible prices, then drops to the same $2.99 clearance band that has run in every send since the June 23 launch. The subject-to-body contract is broken. A subscriber who opens expecting to see "bodysuits went from $X to $Y" will find a category grid with no pricing and a familiar clearance module they've now ignored nine times.
- The bodysuit grid itself is the best creative choice in this send — clean product photography, six styles shown, a scrollable visual entry that beats the lifestyle-heavy sends from earlier in the run. The layout is clean, no bugs, Carter's brand identity is tight. But there's no new reason to buy today. No deadline, no prices on the hero products, and a store-locator module that pads the email without adding value.
- The cadence is the underlying problem. Carter's Rewards is visible at the bottom, the offer is real, the brand is trusted — but at 10 straight daily emails, the list is fatigued. Yesterday's "CLEARANCE FROM $2.99 🚨🚨" did the same job with more emoji and less product focus. This send is marginally better editorially, but it's fighting subscriber exhaustion more than any creative execution can solve.

## What's working

- Six clean bodysuit product images create the strongest hero this clearance run has shown — product-forward beats the generic "CLEARANCE EVENT" banners.
- $2.99 price anchor is visible and prominent in the clearance band below the hero.
- Shop Baby / Shop Toddler / Shop Kid navigation links enable fast self-selection for the right shopper.
- Carter's visual identity is immediately recognizable; the email renders cleanly with no bugs, broken images, or layout breakage.
- Carter's Rewards block appears in the footer — the loyalty connection is at least present.

## What's weak

- The subject promises a "price drop on bodysuits" but the hero bodysuits carry no visible prices — the promise is undelivered in the body.
- The $2.99 clearance block is the same creative unit from every send since June 23; there is no new reason to click it today.
- The store locator module ("Carter's near you!") is a dead-weight filler in a clearance/price-drop email — it interrupts the purchase funnel.
- No deadline anywhere in the email, which means "urgency" is purely implied and easily ignored for the tenth day running.
- "Shop Now" CTA copy is the most generic option available; this is the 10th consecutive use of it.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Show the actual price drop on the hero bodysuits.** If Easy Snap Bodysuits moved from $18 to $10, put `~~$18~~ $10` under each product image. The subject promises it — the body must deliver it. This single change fixes the broken subject-to-body contract.
- 2. **Add a hard deadline.** "Clearance ends July 4th" or "Price valid through Sunday" converts the perpetual event into an actionable window. Nothing else in this email creates real urgency.
- 3. **Kill the store locator module.** Replace it with a sizing/fit callout ("Snaps from neck to hem — easy on, easy off") or a social proof line ("Our #1 rated newborn essential"). The locator is for a different intent altogether.
- 4. **Make the CTA copy specific.** "Shop $2.99 Bodysuits" beats "Shop Now" in every test. Make the offer the button label.
- 5. **Consider a 48-hour cadence pause.** Ten straight daily emails is eroding the list faster than this sale is converting it. A gap before the next send will lift open rates on the re-engagement.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Bodysuits just dropped — see new prices`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Easy snap bodysuits: now $X — while sizes last`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop the price drop before your size sells out.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `$2.99 clearance + new lower prices on Easy Snap Bodysuits.`
