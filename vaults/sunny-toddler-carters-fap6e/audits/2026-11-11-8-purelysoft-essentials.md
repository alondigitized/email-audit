## What's working

- The hero is clear: "$8 PurelySoft™" in large type, a "today only!" badge and a visible "Shop Now" button.
- The six-product grid shows real $8.00 prices against struck-through originals ($18–$20), which makes the savings concrete.
- The "We price match" line under the hero is a useful trust and risk-reducer.
- The "coming 11.12 / 11.13 / 11.14" teaser ($12 shoes, $10 fleece pjs, $7 sleep & play pjs) gives subscribers a reason to keep opening.
- The Rewards banner states a specific benefit: "$5 reward when you spend $50+, 11/10–11/16."

## What's weak

- The "50-60% off site & store" banner sits above the hero and competes with the Day 8 deal for first attention. It's the same generic offer that Nov 8 and Nov 9 sends led with.
- The email has too many modules. Between the hero and the footer there are the product grid, the teaser strip, Little Planet, clearance, Rewards and a store locator.
- The Little Planet "20% off" section is off-theme for a PurelySoft $8 email. Its CTA is a faint outlined button that is easy to miss.
- The clearance block offers "up to 70% off" with four category buttons (Baby Girl, Toddler Girl, Girl, Baby Boy and so on). It pulls attention to a different offer.
- The product tiles show no names, sizes or ratings, and there are no reviews or social proof anywhere.

## Recommendations

- 1. Remove the "50-60% off" banner from the top on days with a "today only" hero, so the $8 deal is the first thing the eye lands on.
- 2. Cut the lower modules down to one secondary offer, such as the 11.12 teaser, and drop Little Planet and clearance from this send.
- 3. Label the grid products and add a rating or "best seller" cue, then make the tiles linkable to individual products.
- 4. Make the "Shop Today's Deal" and Little Planet CTAs solid, high-contrast buttons.
- 5. Put a deadline in the hero, such as "ends midnight ET".
