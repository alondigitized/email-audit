# Homepage snapshot · www.carters.com · 2026-07-18
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-18**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I actually love this — it's OshKosh back-to-school season and Carter's is leading with it hard. The clearance banner up top caught my eye immediately because $3.99 is basically what I'm paying per item when I stack rewards anyway, so this feels like my language. It looks like a brand that knows I'm prepping my kid for fall and trying to stretch every dollar.

## What's working

- **"Limited time clearance flash! Shop from $3.99" banner** — this is the first thing I see and it triggers the coupon-stacker in me immediately. I'm opening this tab.
- **OshKosh back-to-school framing** — "the next class of OSHKOSH ICONS" plus "back-to-school fit check" is millennial-parent coded. "Fit check" is my generation's language, not a grandma-targeted brand voice.
- **"Shop Styles from $5" CTA** — specific price anchor gives me a mental spend floor. I know what I'm getting into.
- **Hero image: denim jacket with OshKosh cuff detail** — that's exactly the kind of layer piece I'm looking for for September. It shows the product, not just a lifestyle vibe.
- **Search bar front and center** — I know what I need (2T pajamas, 3T denim) and I can get there in one tap.

## What's weak

- **No pajama or sleepwear surface** — my literal default cart item is pajamas and I see zero indication they exist above the fold. This is a miss for my shopping mode.
- **CTA is cut off** — "Shop Styles from $5" button text is visible but the button itself is barely peeking out. On mobile this feels like I might swipe right past it.
- **No loyalty/rewards hook** — I have a Rewarding Moments account and I want to see my points or a "members get extra X% off" nudge. That's what makes me stack and buy more.
- **"From" pricing creates skepticism** — "from $3.99" and "from $5" are exciting but if I tap and the $3.99 items are three onesies in size 6M I'm going to feel baited.
- **No urgency depth** — "limited time" with no date or countdown means I might save it for later and forget.

## Recommendations

- **Add a countdown timer to the clearance banner** — "Ends Sunday at midnight" or "48 hours left" would make me tap immediately instead of adding to my mental to-do list.
- **Drop in a Rewarding Moments member callout** — even a small "Sign in to see your member price" line below the CTA turns this from a browsing visit into a loyalty-stacking session.
- **Show a pajama category tile below the hero** — or put "Sleepwear from $X" as a secondary CTA. It's the highest-frequency purchase in my cart and a single visible tile would hook me every visit.
- **Anchor the "from $X" claim** — change to "300+ clearance styles from $3.99" or badge the hero image with a product count so the price promise feels real, not bait-and-switch.
