# $7 ONLY: Fleece PJs & Sleep & Play 🌙
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- This is Day 11 of the 12 Days of Deals, and the hero does its job. A smiling baby in blue plaid fleece PJs sits on a lime-green panel with a "today only!" badge and a "$7 one-piece fleece & sleep & play PJs" headline. A "Shop Now" button and a price-match strip sit under it. The $7 price point is the clearest offer Carter's has run in this series.
- The email then loses focus. A "Up to 60% off holiday styles" banner sits above the hero and competes with it. Below the hero there is a 70% off clearance block, a Day 12 teaser ($4 graphic tees & bodysuits, coming 11.15), a "Shop it all" row and a Little Planet 20% off block. That is five offers in one send. It reads as a catch-all rather than a single-day send.
- Judgement: a strong hero on a cluttered body. It's a good fit for a parent of an infant or toddler, but the subject is cluttered with the "$7 ONLY:" prefix. The cadence is also heavy, since this is the second daily send in the series and sits right after the Nov 13 "Up to 60% off holiday styles" recap.

## What's working

- The hero offer is concrete: "$7 one-piece fleece & sleep & play PJs" with a "today only!" badge.
- The baby model and plaid fleece PJs match the infant and toddler demographic and the season.
- The price-match strip ("If a price drops within 14 days… we'll match it") adds trust.
- The "coming 11.15" teaser for $4 graphic tees and bodysuits gives subscribers a reason to come back tomorrow.
- The Little Planet block ("organic holiday pjs season") reinforces the PJ theme with a warm image.
- There are no visible render bugs: no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The "Up to 60% off holiday styles" banner sits above the hero and splits attention before the $7 offer lands.
- The 70% off clearance block is large and red, and it pulls the eye away from the daily deal.
- Five offers in one email dilutes the "$7 ONLY" urgency.
- The "Shop Now" button on the hero is a low-contrast white button over the photo, and the "today only!" badge sits close to it.
- The bottom legal text is long and dense. The "*" qualifiers on 60% off, 70% off and 20% off are only explained there.
- The Nov 13 send also led with "Up to 60% off holiday styles", so the top banner is a repeat of the previous day's creative.

## Recommendations

- 1. Remove the top "Up to 60% off" banner and put the $7 PJs hero first. That one change would lift the email most.
- 2. Shrink the clearance block into a single line link, and keep the Day 12 teaser as the only secondary offer.
- 3. Make the hero CTA specific and high contrast, for example a solid button reading "Shop $7 PJs".
- 4. Add a visible deadline such as "ends midnight ET" next to the "today only!" badge.
- 5. Show two or three more $7 PJ styles under the hero so there is more than one product to click.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard Attentive/Movable Ink template. The HTML is structurally sound, with `lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag, `role="presentation"` layout tables and a hidden preheader. The automated QA run reported a 16% pass rate, but most of those failures look like probe artifacts (bot-blocked redirects and relay-stripped headers), not confirmed defects. The only issues I'd stand behind from the source and QA data are the missing alt text and the empty `<title>`.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **12 of 12 probed links returned 403.** Every probed link is an Attentive redirect (`https://carters.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...`). That covers the hero CTA ("Up to 60% Off | Holiday styles | Shop Now"), "Tops from $5 >", "Sets from $12 >", the brand tiles (carter's, osh kosh bigosh, little planet, Otter Avenue, Skiphop), and both "Display images to show real-time content" links.
- **Likely cause:** a uniform 403 across every link on one redirect domain points to bot or WAF filtering of the QA probe, not to dead destinations. That is an inference, since the final landing pages were never reached. I can't confirm any link is broken for real recipients.
- **Coverage gap:** 42 of 54 links were skipped, so footer links and unsubscribe handling are unverified.
- **Two tracking layers:** Attentive click redirects plus Movable Ink pixels (`mi.carters.com/p/up/.../o.gif`, `mi.carters.com/p/rp/264477f43986d8bb.png`). This is not a defect, but it adds two third-party dependencies for render and click tracking.
- **Fallback links:** the "Display images to show real-time content" links are Movable Ink fallback anchors. They need to resolve for recipients with images off, and the probe couldn't confirm that.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is in the `<head>`. Some clients and the web-view version use it for the tab or page name.
- **Missing alt text (per QA):**
  - The Movable Ink image `264477f43986d8bb.png`.
  - The four service banners on `d3ptznaucccg76.cloudfront.net/MzczMjo6U0VSVklDRVNfQkFOTkVSXzAx…` through `…BTk5FUl8wNF92My5wbmc…`.
  - These are the only real accessibility findings. If they carry offers or service messages, that content is lost to screen readers and to image-blocked inboxes.
- **False positives:** `ho.gif` is flagged as missing alt, but the source shows `alt=""` on it. That is correct for a hidden 1×1 pixel. `o.gif` is almost certainly a tracking pixel too, though its markup was truncated.
- **Plain text:** QA reports a 0-character text part. This may be an AgentMail capture artifact, so confirm against the raw MIME before treating it as a sending defect. If it is real, a missing text/plain part is a deliverability and accessibility issue.
- **Fine:** viewport meta, a `max-width:660px` responsive breakpoint, an MSO conditional block, and `x-apple-data-detectors` handling.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or subject.
- The Movable Ink URLs carry a subscriber identifier (`mi_u=1150794179`) and a campaign tag (`mi_ecmp=11_14_2026_CAR_SAT_AM_ENGAGERS`). Both resolved to real values.
- The truncated source limits this check to the portion shown.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** QA itself notes the AgentMail relay may strip them. Check the raw headers at the source, because Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results missing:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `marketing.carters.com`, a dedicated marketing subdomain, which is good practice, but alignment can't be verified from this data.
- **Footer not verified:** the physical address and in-body unsubscribe link were outside the truncated source and the probe's skipped set.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters can't be assessed.** All links are opaque Attentive redirect tokens, so the final URLs and any `utm_*` values are not visible. No continuity problems were confirmed.
- No landing-page checks were possible, because the probe was blocked before reaching the destinations.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run the link probe with a browser-like User-Agent, or against the resolved destinations, to separate WAF blocking from real failures. Include the footer and unsubscribe links.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four services banners and the `264477f43986d8bb.png` image.
3. Populate `<title>` with the subject or campaign name.
4. Check the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, a text/plain part and `Authentication-Results`. Treat all four as unverified, not failed, until then.
5. Verify that the final redirect destinations carry consistent UTM parameters.
6. Fix the QA tooling. Skip alt checks when `alt=""` is present on 1×1 pixels, and flag when every probe returns the same status, since that points to a blocking problem.
