# CLEARANCE FROM $3.99 🚨🚨
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-18**
## Executive summary

- This is Carter's shifting gears from the week-long EXTRA20 code blitz — the June 17 "50% OFF ENTIRE SITE & STORE" send broke that cycle, and now this is a natural follow-up: a clearance-depth push with a price anchor as low as $3.99. The structural logic is sound. You've escalated from code-based savings to a site-wide percentage, and now you're clearing inventory with hard dollar floors. That's a coherent markdown arc.
- Execution-wise, the email is clean but thin. The hero banner delivers the $3.99 anchor clearly, the category pills (Baby Girl, Toddler Girl, Girl, Baby Boy, Toddler Boy, Boy) give parents a fast triage path, and the product grid below it shows real prices with visible strikethroughs. The "more markdowns added!" line adds a credible freshness signal. What's missing: any product specificity, a deadline, or a reason to act today vs. tomorrow. Clearance promo fatigue is real — Carter's has now sent eight or nine times in nine days, and this one does nothing to interrupt the blur.
- The two 🚨🚨 sirens in the subject are a minor misstep after what was a relatively clean June 17 send. It reads louder than the offer warrants, and it pairs badly with a body that's actually calm and organized. The email is functional but forgettable.

## What's working

- **$3.99 price anchor is the right hook** — the "FROM $3.99 CLEARANCE" hero is prominent, immediately legible, and the most compelling number Carter's has shown in this entire sequence.
- **Category pills are efficient** — six quick-tap buttons (Baby Girl, Toddler Girl, Girl, Baby Boy, Toddler Boy, Boy) let parents filter without scrolling.
- **Strikethrough pricing is visible in the grid** — original prices are crossed out and sale prices are bolded in red, so the discount depth is readable at a glance.
- **"More markdowns added!" freshness signal** — small but useful; tells repeat openers there's new inventory without requiring a separate send.
- **"SHOP IT ALL" section with age-band splits** is a clean secondary navigation tier.

## What's weak

- **No deadline** — clearance typically moves on urgency; "while supplies last" or even a vague "this weekend" would materially lift click rates, and there's none here.
- **No single hero product or featured item** — the grid is six small thumbnails at roughly equal visual weight; nothing stops the eye or creates desire.
- **Double 🚨🚨 in subject** — the alarm-siren emoji stack signals desperation and spam, undercutting the credibility Carter's earned with the cleaner June 17 send.
- **"SHOP IT ALL" CTA copy is weak** — the sub-category CTAs ("Shop Baby ›", "Shop Toddler ›", "Shop Kid ›") are fine, but "SHOP IT ALL" is the vaguest possible label for the primary grid section header.
- **Cadence fatigue** — this is the ninth or tenth touch in nine days; no creative device acknowledges or rewards that loyalty, making this feel like more noise.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a hard deadline.** "Clearance ends Sunday" or "Prices reset Monday" — even if soft — would give parents a reason to click today. This is the single highest-lift change available.
- 2. **Drop one emoji siren, or replace both.** The 🚨🚨 double hit is the most recognizable spam-trigger emoji in promotional email. One is fine if the offer genuinely warrants urgency; two reads as shouting. Consider 🏷️ or no emoji at all given the credible price anchor in the subject.
- 3. **Feature one hero product.** Pick the deepest-discounted, most-clicked item from past clearance sends and put it above the grid with its own image, original price, sale price, and a direct CTA. Even one anchor item reduces "where do I start" paralysis.
- 4. **Strengthen the "SHOP IT ALL" section header.** Replace with "Clearance from $3.99" — repeat the lead value prop as the section label so even skim-readers can't miss it.
- 5. **Acknowledge the subscriber.** After nine sends in nine days, a single line like "You've been with us all sale — here's the deepest clearance yet" costs nothing and converts fatigue into appreciation.
