# Up to 60% off holiday styles ☃️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-13**
## Executive summary

- This is the Day 11 send of the 12 Days of Deals, and it reads like a recap of the week's promos. The email carries a "Up to 60% off holiday styles" hero, a "70% off clearance" block, a teaser for the next two days' deals, a puffer jacket feature and a "Shop it all" row. None of these gets priority. The hero is clean and the 60% is big, but the red 70% clearance block right below it competes for the same attention. The eye has no single place to land.
- The email is a near repeat of the Nov 12 "HOLIDAY STYLES - Up to 60% off 🎁" send. It uses the same hero and the same 70% clearance block, with a new snowman emoji. A subscriber who got that one yesterday will see this as the same email again. It also gives away the next two days' deals ("coming 11.14: $7 fleece & sleep & play pjs", "coming 11.15: $4 graphic tees & bodysuits"). Those are the sharpest offers in the email, and they're locked behind dates. Today's "Shop Today's Deal" button sits under those teasers and no today's deal is visible. The Day 11 deal is missing from the render.
- Verdict: competent and on-brand, but cluttered and repetitive. It's a 5, with the lift coming from brand recognition and clear offers.

## What's working

- The hero is easy to read: a bold "60% OFF*" on a dark green sparkle texture with a "Shop Now" button.
- The 70% clearance block has big type on red, and the "New Markdowns Added" line gives a reason to look again.
- The category buttons (Baby Girl, Toddler Girl, Girl, Baby Boy, Toddler Boy, Boy) make it easy to jump to the right size group.
- The "Shop it all" row shows prices ("Tops from $5", "Bottoms from $7", "Sets from $12"), which is concrete and useful.
- The footer has the store locator, the Rewards+ perks and the price-match terms, so the member benefits are visible.

## What's weak

- The hero and the red clearance block both lead with a big percentage. Two competing headline offers dilute each other.
- The only "Shop Today's Deal" button points at the Day 11 deal, but the email shows only the upcoming $7 and $4 deals. Today's deal itself is absent.
- This repeats the Nov 12 "HOLIDAY STYLES - Up to 60% off" creative almost exactly. It's the same hero and the same clearance block, so it feels stale.
- The puffer jacket section ("The packable puffer", "Packs down small enough to fit in a backpack (or diaper bag)") is a big, low-urgency block with no price and no reviews. It pushes the useful "Shop it all" prices well down the email.
- The asterisks on "60% OFF*" and "70% OFF*" lead to a long wall of fine print at the bottom, with exclusions such as Little Planet and Skip Hop. That undercuts how honest the offer feels.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero. Lead with the actual Day 11 deal, or the 60% offer, and drop the clearance block lower. Today's deal should be visible without needing a "Shop Today's Deal" button.
- 2. Drop or shrink the teaser row for 11.14 and 11.15. Move it to a one-line footer strip so it doesn't steal attention from today's offer.
- 3. Replace the puffer block with a price-led product grid, with real prices and star ratings, so the click has something specific to land on.
- 4. Put the key exclusions in one line right under the hero ("Excludes Little Planet, Skip Hop") instead of burying them in the footer.
- 5. Vary the creative from the Nov 12 send. The same hero two days running trains subscribers to skip it.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Day 11: today's deal + 60% off holiday styles`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Holiday styles up to 60% off, ends soon`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus a peek at tomorrow's $7 fleece pjs and $4 tees.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Clearance now up to 70% off, in store and online.`
