# LIMITED TIME: 50% off PJs & more ✨
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-08**
## Executive summary

- The hero is the best Carter's has run this week. A magenta starburst block reads "long weekend stock-up / 50% OFF* / PJs, sets & more" with a Shop Now button. The creative has changed after the four-day LAYERUP run (10-05 to 10-07), with no gradient code banner and no code to remember. It also gives a reason to buy, since the long weekend is real: the rewards strip says 2X points run through October 12. Compared with the 10-04 "Today online only - $7+ PJs" send, the 50% headline is easier to read at a glance.
- The body doesn't deliver on the subject line. The "We think you'll love these…" grid is nine unlabeled tiles with no prices or names. Most are jeans, joggers and white bodysuits, and only one tile, bottom right, is a PJ-style footed sleeper. Someone who opened for PJs has to hunt for them. The email then stacks four more offers: a Halloween block, Tees from $6, Licensed from $10, Sets from $11, and $2.99+ clearance. The 50% message gets diluted by the time the reader scrolls. "LIMITED TIME" in the subject has no visible end date outside the small rewards strip.
- Verdict: a solid, seasonal, loyalty-aware send with a strong top third and a weak middle. Fixing the product grid so it matches the headline would lift it more than any subject-line tweak.

## What's working

- The hero is clear: "50% OFF*" in large white type on magenta, with "PJs, sets & more" directly under it. The eye lands on the offer first.
- "Long weekend stock-up" gives the sale a seasonal reason, and the 2X points strip backs it with a date (through October 12, 2026).
- The "For their first Halloween" block (pumpkin booties, skeleton set) is timely and well photographed. It's a good gift-and-occasion hook for baby shoppers.
- The "Shop it all" row has real price anchors: Tees from $6, Licensed from $10, Sets from $11.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images, overlaps or odd gaps. The footer has loyalty and store-pickup reassurance (curbside, cardholder perks, in-app points).

## What's weak

- The subject promises PJs, but the nine-tile grid shows jeans, bodysuits and joggers, with a single PJ-like tile. The headline and the merchandising don't match.
- The grid tiles have no names, prices or sale badges. "We think you'll love these…" gives no reason to click any of them.
- Urgency is soft. "LIMITED TIME" has no deadline in the hero, and the only date is the 2X points strip at the top, which refers to points rather than the 50% sale.
- The asterisk on "50% OFF*" leads to fine print at the very bottom ("Select Styles. Other exclusions may apply."), and the hero doesn't say what is excluded.
- The page runs long, with five or six competing offers. The "Now open near you! Commerce, California" store block is irrelevant to most recipients. The sister-brand logo row and the rewards strip push the hero down.
- This is another near-daily send, after the LAYERUP run and the PJ send on 10-04. Even with fresh creative, cadence fatigue is a risk.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the grid match the subject. Show actual PJs and sleepwear with name and sale price under each tile, such as "Snug-fit 2-pack PJs, $9.99 (was $20)". Even a simple "PJs from $X" row would do it.
- 2. Put the deadline in the hero: "Ends Monday 10/12" under the 50% line. Move the 2X points to a pill next to it instead of a separate strip.
- 3. Replace "Shop Now" with "Shop PJs" or "Shop 50% off PJs". Make it a solid button, because the outlined ghost button is low contrast on the magenta.
- 4. Cut the page down: drop the store-locator block (or geo-target it) and merge Halloween and Shop It All into one section.
- 5. Spell out the main exclusions in one line under the hero, so the asterisk doesn't feel like a trap.
- **Subject Alt A:** `PJs, sets & more are 50% off through Monday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Long weekend stock-up: 50% off PJs 🎃`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 2X Rewards points through 10/12 and clearance from $2.99.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sleepwear, bodysuits & first-Halloween finds, all stocked up for the long weekend.`

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**

This includes the hero CTA ("long weekend stock-up | 50% Off* | Pjs, Sets & More | Shop Now"), the logo link ("Carter's"), the brand links (OshKosh, Little Planet, Otter Avenue, Skiphop), the Rewards link, and the jeans and bodysuit product tiles.
- A uniform 403 across every tracked link points to the redirector rejecting the QA probe's user agent or IP. It does not confirm that real subscribers are blocked. Treat it as unverified, not as a confirmed break.
- Only 12 links were probed and 42 were skipped. Coverage of the final destinations is therefore incomplete.
- The tracking links are fully wrapped, so the destination URLs and UTM parameters cannot be inspected from the source.
- Three tracking pixels are present:
  - `https://carters.attentivemail.com/ss/o/.../ho.gif` (1x1, in a hidden `pixel-wrapper` div)
  - `https://mi.carters.com/p/up/745a17bbb73445ad/o.gif?mi_u=1150794179&mi_ecmp=10_08_2026_CAR_THU_STO_ENGAGERS`
