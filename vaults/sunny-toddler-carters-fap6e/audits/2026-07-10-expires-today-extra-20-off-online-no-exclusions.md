# Expires today❗️EXTRA 20% off online (no exclusions)
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-10**
## Executive summary

- Carter's pivots off eighteen days of consecutive clearance emails with a genuine value proposition: "no exclusions" on an EXTRA 20% off sitewide. That's a meaningful differentiator — subscribers who've been burned by fine-print-heavy promos will notice, and "Expires today" is believable precisely because this brand rotates offers daily. The OshKosh back-to-school hero is seasonally accurate for mid-July, and the lifestyle imagery reads clean and aspirational.
- The structural problem is that the email tries to be four emails at once. The hero sells a premium "ICONIC FIRST DAY LOOK" brand moment. Then a "WE THINK YOU'LL LOVE THESE" algorithmic grid appears. Then an OshKosh Avenue lifestyle section. Then a "70% OFF CLEARANCE" blowout closes it out. Each section could carry a send on its own. Together they fight for attention and none of them wins.
- Bottom line: the subject line is the best thing about this email. The "no exclusions" angle is strategically correct; the body doesn't earn it.

## What's working

- **"No exclusions" is the right differentiator.** After weeks of asterisk-heavy clearance deals, saying it plainly in the subject treats subscribers like adults who've been burned before.
- **"Expires today" urgency is credible.** Eighteen days of daily sends means subscribers have internalized the cadence — they know today's offer genuinely won't be there tomorrow.
- **OshKosh first-day hero is seasonally on-calendar.** "ICONIC FIRST DAY LOOK" in July hits the back-to-school consideration window before the August rush.
- **Dual offer structure covers two buyer modes.** Sitewide 20% off captures full-price intent; 70% clearance captures bargain hunters — two audiences, one send.

## What's weak

- **Four competing sections, no editorial hierarchy.** Hero → personalization grid → lifestyle brand moment → clearance blowout creates cognitive overload; the eye never settles on the primary offer.
- **Clearance section undermines the "no exclusions" premium message.** You can't simultaneously position as "sitewide, no fine print" and "70% OFF* CLEARANCE" without looking like two different emails stapled together — because you are.
- **"WE THINK YOU'LL LOVE THESE" grid looks like filler.** Neutral-palette baby basics feel tonally disconnected from the bold OshKosh brand story directly above.
- **OshKosh Avenue copy is illegible at mobile render size.** The tagline under the yellow lifestyle section is too small to read — the section's strongest asset (the photography) is undercut by unreadable copy.
- **Day 18 of consecutive sends.** Even a genuinely different offer gets mentally filed as "another Carter's promo" by this point in the cadence.

## Recommendations

- 1. **One email, one story.** Run either the sitewide 20% "no exclusions" email or the clearance email — not both. The no-exclusions angle is the stronger hook today; drop the clearance section entirely or move it to its own mid-week send.
- 2. **Let the OshKosh hero breathe.** Remove the "WE THINK YOU'LL LOVE THESE" block or push it below the fold. Two full sections of brand storytelling before the first SKU grid would make this feel premium instead of reactive.
- 3. **Fix the OshKosh Avenue copy.** The lifestyle photo is the best asset in the email. The copy is unreadable. Minimum 16px, and add a clear "Shop First Day Looks" CTA button directly beneath it.
- 4. **Add a deadline time, not just a date.** "Expires today" is good; "Expires tonight at 11:59 PM ET" is better — it creates a specific action window rather than a vague today-ish urgency.
