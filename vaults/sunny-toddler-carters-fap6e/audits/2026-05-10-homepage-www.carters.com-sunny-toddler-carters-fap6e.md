# Homepage snapshot · www.carters.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Carter's knows its audience — I opened this and immediately felt seen: a real mom smooshed between a laughing toddler and a baby in a bucket hat, with a Mother's Day message that doesn't feel corporate. It's clearly mid-May and they leaned in. But the homepage is all emotion, no action — I can see a promo code, I can feel the vibes, and then... nothing tells me where to tap to actually shop.

## What's working

- **The lifestyle photo** — mom in sunglasses kissing a toddler while a baby in a striped bucket hat grins is genuinely relatable. I paused on it. That's Carter's at its best.
- **The SUMMER20 banner** — it's the first thing I read. A stacker like me clocks a promo code in milliseconds, and "extra 20% off" is a clear, actionable number.
- **"Happy Mother's Day" subhead** — small line but it confirms the campaign timing and makes it feel personal. Someone thought about me, not just the algorithm.
- **Search bar placement** — prominent, full-width, right below the nav. I can get to pajamas in two seconds without hunting.
- **The tag/coupon icon in the nav** — signals that deals are surfaced in-app; that's a trust signal for a coupon-focused shopper like me.

## What's weak

- **No CTA button above the fold** — the hero copy lands emotionally but then just... stops. Where do I tap? "Shop Mother's Day Gifts"? "Shop All Kids"? Nothing. That's a real miss.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — I always check this before I mentally commit. Not seeing it here means I have to go dig, which adds friction.
- **No product category tiles above the fold** — Carter's is a multi-category brand (baby, toddler, pajamas, OshKosh). I have to scroll or open the hamburger to know where I am.
- **No urgency signal on the SUMMER20 code** — "extra 20% off" with no end date feels less compelling than "ends Sunday." Coupon stackers need a deadline.
- **Loyalty is invisible** — I shop Carter's frequently enough to have a Rewards balance. I should see "You have $X to spend" or something that rewards my loyalty right on load.

## Recommendations

- **Add a CTA button to the hero** — "Shop Mother's Day Gifts →" in white on the pink background, directly below the "Happy Mother's Day" subline. One button. One tap. Done.
- **Swap the offer banner copy** to include a deadline: "Code SUMMER20 = extra 20% off — this weekend only." Coupon-stacking moms need the urgency hook to act fast vs. save-for-later.
- **Surface 3–4 category chips below the hero** — Baby, Toddler, Pajamas, OshKosh — so users who aren't browsing the sale can route themselves instantly without the hamburger.
- **Inject a personalized loyalty nudge** in the banner or below the search bar: "Rewards member? You have $15 waiting" pulls return shoppers straight to cart behavior.
