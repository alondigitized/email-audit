# Up to 60% off holiday PJs 🎄
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a busy Black Friday catch-all dressed up with a holiday-PJ subject line. The top of the email delivers on the subject: a red "black friday 50-60% OFF* SITE & STORE" banner over a strong flat-lay of festive pajamas. After that it turns into a mini-catalog. There is a "$2.99 clearance flash" panel, a six-tile gift guide, an OshKosh "buy 1, get 2 free" jeans block, price match, a gift bag offer, and rewards icons.
- The subject says "up to 60% off," but the hero says "50-60% off." The PJ promise is also the only PJ content in the email. A subscriber who opened for pajamas gets one image and a button, then is pushed toward clearance, gifts and denim.
- Compared with the 11-20 and 11-21 sends, which each had one red panel and one Shop Now button, this one is more diluted. The offer is still clear and the design is clean. It is a solid send, not a sharp one.

## What's working

- The hero is unmistakable. The red "black friday 50-60% OFF*" banner sits above a bright pajama flat-lay that matches the subject.
- The "$2.99 CLEARANCE FLASH" panel gives a concrete, low-price anchor, and the category buttons (Baby Girl, Toddler Boy, etc.) make shopping by size easy.
- The "FIND YOUR HOLIDAY PJ VIBE" headline and the "Shop up to 60% off*" button tie the subject to a specific action.
- The footer keeps the trust and loyalty elements: price match, the Rewards icons, and the in-store gift bag offer.
- Nothing renders badly. There are no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- Too many competing offers: 50-60% off, $2.99 clearance, BOGO-2-free OshKosh jeans, a $75+ gift bag, and price match. The eye has no single destination below the hero.
- The subject's "up to 60%" doesn't match the hero's "50-60%." It is also a different framing from the prior sends, and the PJ promise is thin after the first screen.
- The $2.99 clearance panel has no product imagery, just white category buttons on red. It feels like filler.
- The fine print under the hero and the long disclaimers at the bottom make the offer feel conditional, and the "*" is everywhere.
- The email is very long, about 4,700px, and the cadence is heavy. This is another near-duplicate in a run of Black Friday sends (11-16, 11-17, 11-20, 11-21).

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the email actually about pajamas. Replace the gift guide and OshKosh block with a 4-6 product PJ grid showing price and sizes, since that is what the subject promised.
- 2. Align the subject and the hero on the same number and wording.
- 3. Cut the secondary offers to one. Keep the clearance panel and move the rest to a one-line footer strip.
- 4. Add a real deadline to the hero, such as a countdown or "ends tonight," as the 11-16 send did.
- 5. Put product photos in the clearance panel and add one review or "best seller" tag.
