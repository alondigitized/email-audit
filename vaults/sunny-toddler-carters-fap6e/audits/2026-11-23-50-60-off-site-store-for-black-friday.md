# 50-60% off* site & store for Black Friday 🎉
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the Black Friday workhorse again, and it's the cleanest version I've seen. The red block reads "black friday 50-60% OFF* SITE & STORE" with one white Shop Now button, and the eye lands on it right away. The two kid-photo bands above and below it give the offer warmth without competing with it.
- The problem is repetition. This is at least the fifth send in a week with the same 50-60% hero: 11/17, 11/20, 11/21 and two on 11/22. The only new thing here is the subject's "for Black Friday 🎉", and the 11/20 "Extra 30%... FRIDAY30" code is gone. Below the hero the email adds a price-match promise, Baby's First Christmas, shop-by-age, and a $2.99 clearance block. All of that is solid but low-urgency. It's a competent send that a subscriber will skim past by now.

## What's working

- The hero is unmistakable: 50-60% OFF in huge white type on solid red, with a bold, centered Shop Now button.
- "WE PRICE MATCH. If a price drops within 14 days..." is a real trust builder and sits right under the first fold.
- "Shop Baby / Toddler / Kid" gives a quick way into the catalog by age.
- The "FROM $2.99 CLEARANCE FLASH" block has a strong price anchor, and the gender and age tiles make it easy to navigate.
- There are no render bugs. Images load, text is legible, and the layout is clean.

## What's weak

- Nothing is new since 11/22. The same hero, offer and banner have run for days, so fatigue is the real risk.
- There is no deadline. "Black Friday" implies urgency, but no date, countdown or "ends Monday" appears anywhere.
- The Baby's First Christmas section has a vague CTA, a generic "Shop Now", and nothing says which product or price it leads to.
- The loyalty and perks icons at the bottom are tiny and low-contrast. Rewards is a real lever and it's buried.
- The disclaimer block is long, and "Exclusions apply" next to a 50-60% claim feels hedgy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a dated deadline to the hero, such as "Ends Cyber Monday" or "Today only", and bring back an extra-savings code. The 11/20 send had one, and it gave the sends something to differentiate.
- 2. Rotate the hero creative and lead with a different category each day (PJs, outerwear, baby) rather than reusing the identical red block.
- 3. Replace the Baby's First Christmas "Shop Now" with "Shop baby Christmas outfits" and show a starting price.
- 4. Move Rewards and the price-match message up into a single trust strip near the top.
- 5. Condense the legal copy, or link to it.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Baby's first Christmas, from $2.99 🎄`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Price match guarantee + free gift bag with $75 in-store`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Clearance from $2.99, plus Rewards points in the app`

# Technical Audit: carter's, "50-60% off* site & store for Black Friday 🎉"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a standard table-based, Attentive-wrapped template with a viewport meta tag, `lang="en"` and a hidden preheader. The automated checks flag a 0-character plain-text part, missing alt text on content images and an empty `<title>`. The 403s on every probed link, and the missing unsubscribe and authentication headers, are probably artifacts of how the checks were run, so they can't be confirmed from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed material links returned 403.** That covers "Shop Baby >", "Shop Toddler >", "Shop Kid >", "Shop Now", the "carter's" logo, "osh kosh bigosh", "little planet", "Otter Avenue", "Skiphop" and the "A major milestone…" link.
  - Every URL is an Attentive click-tracking redirect, `https://carters.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/<token>`.
  - A uniform 403 across a single redirect domain looks like bot filtering of the QA probe, not dead links. I can't confirm it's a false positive: the data doesn't show the redirect destination or a human-browser result.
  - Verify with a real-browser click-through before treating these as broken.
- **Redirect-wrapped links are very long.** Tokens run roughly 500–700 characters. This is normal for Attentive but makes the source heavy. If the HTML is near Gmail's ~102 KB clipping threshold, the footer and unsubscribe could be clipped. The truncated source doesn't show the total size, so this is unconfirmed.
- **39 links were not probed.** The footer, utility and social links are deprioritized, so their status is unknown.
- **Tracking pixels are present and structured correctly.**
  - `carters.attentivemail.com/ss/o/.../ho.gif` is a 1x1 pixel inside a `display:none` wrapper.
  - `mi.carters.com/p/up/.../o.gif` is a second open pixel from another system.
  - The second pixel means two tracking vendors, which is not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is in `<head>`. Some clients and web views use it for the tab or title, and it hurts accessibility.
- **Missing alt text on content images (real issue).** Four cloudfront banners have no alt attribute:
  - `d3ptznaucccg76.cloudfront.net/MzczMjo6U0VSVklDRVNfQkFOTkVSXzAx…png` (SERVICES_BANNER_01)
  - `…_02`
  - `…_03`
  - `…_04_v3`
  - Images-off rendering and screen readers get nothing for these. If they carry the offer or service information, that content is lost.
- **Likely false positives on the pixels.**
  - `ho.gif` already has `alt=""`, which is correct for a decorative pixel.
  - `o.gif` and `mi.carters.com/p/rp/264477f43986d8bb.png` also look like tracking or render pixels. I'd only confirm the `.png` isn't a visible image.
- **No issues found with the following:**
  - The viewport meta tag, `lang="en"` and the MSO conditional blocks (VML/Office namespaces, `PixelsPerInch`) are all present.
  - The preheader is hidden with `display:none; max-height:0; opacity:0`, with padding characters.
  - The mobile breakpoint is `@media (max-width:660px)`, and `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` is handled.
- **Web font:** League Spartan loads via `<link>` inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. It is correctly excluded from Outlook, but the visible source doesn't show a fallback stack.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible source. The source is truncated, so the rest of the body is unchecked.
- Tracking URLs carry `mi_u=1150794179` (a per-recipient ID) and `mi_ecmp=11_23_2026_CAR_MON_STO_ENGAGERS`. The campaign name implies an "engagers" segment. The IDs resolved, so personalization is functioning.

## 5. Compliance
- **Plain-text fallback is missing:** `[FAIL] Text version is 0 chars`. This is the most defensible deliverability defect. HTML-only messages can score worse with spam filters and are inaccessible in text-only clients. It could still be a capture limitation of the relay, since the harness couldn't see the MIME parts.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA note says the relay may strip them, so I can't confirm they're absent from the original send. If they really are missing, that conflicts with Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements (one-click unsubscribe).
- **`Authentication-Results` is absent,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment is unknown. The sending domain is `marketing.carters.com`, with Attentive as the likely ESP, and the relay doesn't expose this. Check the raw headers from a direct-to-inbox test.
- **Footer unsubscribe and physical address:** not visible in the truncated source and not probed, so they can't be assessed. The QA note says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but none appears in the failure list.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- Every destination sits behind an `attentivemail.com` redirect, so I can't see the final landing URLs or confirm UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`).
- There is no data on landing-page alignment. The 403s block resolution, so the CTA-to-page match was not verifiable.
- The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=11_23_2026_CAR_MON_STO_ENGAGERS` on the `mi.carters.com` pixels. It's a separate tracking system, not a UTM.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-probe the links with a browser user-agent or a real click-through.** This resolves whether the Attentive 403s are probe blocking. If they are genuinely broken, fix them first, since they're primary CTAs.
2. **Add a plain-text MIME part,** at least a text version with the key offer and links.
3. **Verify the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` headers** on the original message, not the relayed copy. Also check SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `marketing.carters.com` in raw headers.
4. **Add descriptive alt text to the four cloudfront banner images,** and populate `<title>` with the subject or brand.
5. **Check total HTML size** against the ~102 KB Gmail clipping limit, given the long redirect tokens.
6. **Capture the post-redirect landing URLs** to confirm UTM tagging and that each CTA lands on the matching category page.
