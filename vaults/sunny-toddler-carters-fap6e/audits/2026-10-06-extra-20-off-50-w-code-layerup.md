# Extra 20% off* $50+ w/ code: LAYERUP 🚨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- This is a repeat of yesterday's send. On 10-05 the subject was "Extra 20% off* online on $50+ ‼️", and today's is "Extra 20% off* $50+ w/ code: LAYERUP 🚨". It has the same gradient LAYERUP banner and the same dark-green "UP TO 50% OFF*, Go all in on fall" hero. The only real change is a new PJ block and a clearance block. A subscriber who got both will see no reason to open this one.
- The render itself is clean and easy to scan. The code banner sits at the top, the 50% off hero follows, then a product grid, the PJ story, a red 70% clearance block and the store-locator and rewards strip. Nothing is broken. The problem is that three different offers compete in one scroll: extra 20%, up to 50% and up to 70%. The eye has no single place to land, and the product grid carries no prices.
- Verdict: a competent but redundant send. It is a middling reminder with no new news, and I'd expect it to do worse than yesterday's because of fatigue.

## What's working

- The LAYERUP code is the first thing under the logo, so the stackable-savings mechanic is clear early.
- The green hero is bold and readable. "50% OFF*" is large, with a clear "Shop Now" outlined button.
- The "COZY PJS FOR ALL KIDS" block has a lifestyle image and a specific "Shop from $9" CTA. It is the most concrete action in the email.
- The red clearance block has quick-tap segment buttons (Baby Girl, Toddler Boy and so on). That is a useful way for a parent to jump to their own kid.
- The footer carries the rewards, curbside and in-app points benefits, plus a store locator.

## What's weak

- The three stacked offers (extra 20%, up to 50%, up to 70%) blur together, and the real discount is hard to work out.
- The product grid is nine unlabeled tiles with no names, prices or reviews, so there's nothing to act on.
- The email has no deadline, no end date and no countdown. The 🚨 in the subject promises urgency that the body never delivers. The 10-04 "Today only" PJ send had a live countdown and was much stronger.
- Asterisks and a long exclusions block make the offer feel conditional. The "$50+" threshold and code requirement add friction.
- The hero and grid are generic fall imagery with no children or families in them, so nothing signals who it's for.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer. Make the LAYERUP banner the hero, with a real end date or countdown, and cut or demote the 50% and 70% blocks to one line each.
- 2. Put prices and product names on the grid tiles, such as "Fleece joggers $12", so there is something to click.
- 3. Give each block a specific CTA, such as "Shop PJs from $9" and "Shop clearance", instead of "Shop Now".
- 4. Vary the creative day to day. Yesterday's hero should not run again today.
- 5. Drop the siren emoji unless there is a real deadline to back it up.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Layer up for less: PJs from $9 + extra 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code LAYERUP on $50+ — stacks on fall markdowns up to 50% off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cozy PJs from $9, plus new clearance markdowns up to 70% off.`
