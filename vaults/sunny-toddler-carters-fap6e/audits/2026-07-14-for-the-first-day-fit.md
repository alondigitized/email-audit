# For the first day fit ✅
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-14**
## Executive summary

- Carter's follows yesterday's "Back to school prep starts now!" pivot with something more substantive: an OshKosh B'Gosh new shop launch built around a "next class of icons" brand moment. The hero image — two confident kids in coordinated OshKosh outfits — is the best creative this brand has sent in this audit run, and the subject line "For the first day fit ✅" lands the BTS narrative in 23 characters without a discount in sight. That's notable restraint from a brand that spent early July screaming "FINAL HOURS" every other day.
- The structural problem is that this is two emails in a trench coat. Above the fold: a brand-moment OshKosh new shop launch. Below: a 70% off clearance strip with category filter buttons. These don't cohere. A subscriber who opens for the "first day fit" story lands on an OshKosh icons launch and then hits the same clearance mechanic that dominated July 1–9. The email can't decide whether it's a launch event or a clearance push, and that ambiguity costs both stories.
- Net: the best subject line and hero of the July run, undermined by a two-story structure that neither story deserves. Genuine potential here if Carter's commits to the OshKosh launch as its own send and stops bolting clearance onto everything.

## What's working

- The OshKosh hero image is strong — real kids, dynamic pose, genuine "icons" energy that earns the headline
- "NEW SHOP: THE NEXT CLASS OF ICONS" is the sharpest headline copy in this audit history; it's aspirational without being empty
- Subject line is short (23 chars), BTS-seasonally precise, and the ✅ emoji earns its place as a "check it off the list" visual
- "WE THINK YOU'LL LOVE THESE!" product grid bridges the new shop launch to specific shoppable items
- 70% off clearance is a real, substantial offer — not the exclusion-riddled 20%-off blasts of July 10

## What's weak

- The new shop launch and the clearance section fight each other — the email has no single story a subscriber could describe in one sentence
- "Shop Now" CTA copy is generic; loses the chance to close the subject-to-button loop ("Shop the First Day Fit" or "Shop OshKosh")
- No price anchor in the hero — the OshKosh new shop presents zero starting-price context, so the value proposition doesn't land until the clearance banner forces it
- Second consecutive BTS-themed send (after July 13's "Back to school prep starts now!") risks feeling like the same beat on replay, especially for subscribers already tuned to Carter's daily cadence
- Clearance section at the bottom reads as a holdover reflex — it dilutes the genuine newness of the OshKosh launch story

## Recommendations

- 1. **Split into two sends.** The OshKosh launch deserves its own clean email: hero + grid + price anchor. Move clearance to the next day's send — it will convert better without a competing story above it.
- 2. **Price anchor in the hero.** One line — "Starting at $X" — directly under "THE NEXT CLASS OF ICONS" closes the "is this affordable?" question that the clearance section is currently answering from two screens down.
- 3. **Specific CTA copy.** Replace "Shop Now" with "Shop OshKosh Icons" or "Find the First Day Fit" — ties the button back to what drew the open.
- 4. **Lead with the launch story in the subject**, not just the occasion:
- **Subject Alt A:** `OshKosh is here. Dress them for Day 1.`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New: The First Day Icons (from $X)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Carter's OshKosh new shop just launched — school-ready styles starting at $X`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `From $X. Because the first day of school only happens once.`
