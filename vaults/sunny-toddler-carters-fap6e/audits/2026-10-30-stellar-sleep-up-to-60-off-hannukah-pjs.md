# Stellar Sleep │ Up to 60% off Hannukah PJs 🕯️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-30**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent seasonal send with a muddled message. The teal "Stellar Sleep Week" banner and the Hanukkah-themed PJ hero are warm and on-brand. The "Celebrate in Hanukkah PJs" block with a "Shop up to 60% off*" button gives one clear path. The offer is cleanly time-boxed: "Only from Oct 29–Nov 3."
- The email then splits its attention. The PJ grid shows Christmas-style gingerbread and holiday prints, not Hanukkah items. A "Top-Rated Gifts" block of a xylophone, wands and gloves has nothing to do with sleep. A red "Up to 70% off clearance" block competes with the 60% PJ offer. The subject and hero promise a focused Hanukkah PJ story, and the body turns into a general Carter's catalog.
- Judgement: a decent, safe send that is weaker than the 10/29 "Up to 60% off glow-in-the-dark PJs" launch. That one kept the sleep theme tight. This one dilutes it by day two, and the "Hannukah" typo in the subject undercuts it. It is also the same Stellar Sleep Week event, now on its third send in two days after the 10/29 launch and the PJ sweepstakes.

## What's working

- The "Stellar Sleep Week" banner with "Only from Oct 29–Nov 3" gives a credible, visible deadline.
- The hero photo of Hanukkah-print PJs and a wrapped gift is warm, seasonal and clearly about the product.
- The "Shop up to 60% off*" button is clear and sits right under the hero.
- The footer perks strip (curbside, cardholder perks, extra points in the app, Rewards) puts loyalty benefits in view.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The PJ grid shows pink and green gingerbread and holiday prints. That contradicts the Hanukkah hero and the subject line.
- The "Top-Rated Gifts" block (xylophone, wands, gloves) goes off-theme with a generic "Shop Now" button.
- The red "Up to 70% off clearance" block competes with the 60% PJ offer, so the email carries two headline discounts.
- The first fold has no number. "Dreamy deals on ALL PJs" is vague, and the 60% only appears at the button below the hero.
- The subject misspells "Hanukkah" as "Hannukah." The brand's own email spells it correctly.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the body match the hero. Fill the PJ grid with Hanukkah-print sets, or drop the Hanukkah framing from the subject and hero.
- 2. Put "UP TO 60% OFF PJs" in the teal banner or on the hero image so the offer is visible on the first mobile fold.
- 3. Cut the gifts block, or move it below the clearance block. Keep one primary offer.
- 4. Fix the typo: "Hanukkah."
- 5. Give each PJ tile a price or "from $X" tag and a size/age range. Add a review count to back up "Top-Rated."
- 6. Merge the clearance block into a small secondary strip so it doesn't compete with the 60% offer.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally sound (`lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` layout tables, MSO fallbacks, responsive CSS). However, all 12 probed links returned 403, the plain-text part is empty, and many images lack alt text. Several of these findings may come from the capture setup, as noted below.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **12 of 12 probed links returned 403.** All are Attentive click-tracking redirects on `carters.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...`. This includes the primary CTA "Shop up to 60% off*", the logo ("carter's"), brand links ("osh kosh bigosh", "little planet", "Otter Avenue", "Skiphop") and the hero image link.
  - A uniform 403 across every redirect is most likely Attentive's bot or scanner blocking the QA prober, not broken links. Real recipients probably don't see it.
  - The final destinations were never resolved, so landing-page correctness is unverified.
- **Link text "Display images to show real-time content"** appears on 4 links (probed). This is fallback alt or text for dynamic or live-content image blocks, and it becomes the link text when images are blocked. Confirm these are intentional links and not leftover placeholders.
- **The hero link text is a long alt string** ("INTRODUCING Stellar Sleep Week | When the clocks fall back... | Maximize their s…"). It is likely truncated in the audit, but it is a verbose accessible name.
- **Two tracking pixels:** `carters.attentivemail.com/ss/o/.../ho.gif` and `mi.carters.com/p/up/745a17bbb73445ad/o.gif`. Both are expected. The second embeds `mi_u=1150794179` (a subscriber ID) in a URL, which is normal for ESP tracking.
- 49 links were skipped, so footer, social and utility links were not verified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** Some clients and screen readers use the title, so it should be populated.
- **Images flagged for missing alt (9):**
  - the two hero and gifting images on `d3ptznaucccg76.cloudfront.net` (`MzczMjo6Q0FSXzEwMjkyNl9IRVJPXzcuanBnOjoy...`, `...R2lmdGluZ19TRUNPTkRBUllfNC5qcGc...`)
  - the four `SERVICES_BANNER_01–04` PNGs
  - the `mi.carters.com/.../264477f43986d8bb.png` image
  - These are content images, and if they carry text or offers, that content is invisible with images off.
  - **Likely false positives:** `ho.gif` already has `alt=""` in the source. That is valid for a decorative pixel, so the check is misreporting. `o.gif` is also a tracking pixel and doesn't need alt text.
- **Positives:** `role="presentation"` on layout tables, a viewport meta tag, a hidden preheader (with zero-width and soft-hyphen padding), `x-apple-data-detectors` handling, and Outlook `mso` conditionals.
- **Web font:** the Google Fonts `<link>` (League Spartan) is wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. Gmail ignores it, so fallback fonts are needed. This can't be checked because the source is truncated.
- The source is truncated, so I can't verify the fonts, the stack, or the buttons' VML fallbacks.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FNAME]`) are visible in the available source. The `mi_u` and `mi_ecmp` parameters on the Monetate-style pixels are resolved values.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA check notes that the AgentMail relay may strip these. That makes this an unverified gap, not a confirmed failure. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers for senders of this size.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, again because of the relay. The From domain is `marketing.carters.com`, a subdomain, so check alignment directly.
- **Plain-text part is missing: 0 chars.** This is a real deliverability and accessibility risk if it holds on the sent message. It could also be a capture artifact.
- The footer unsubscribe link and the physical address were not in the truncated source or the probed set, so they are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- This could not be assessed. Every link is wrapped in an Attentive redirect and returned 403, so destination URLs and UTM parameters are not visible.
- The campaign is identifiable from the pixel parameter `mi_ecmp=10_30_2026_CAR_FRI_NOON_ENGAGERS`. It names a send-segment ("ENGAGERS") and a date.
- Recommended follow-up: resolve the redirects with a browser-like user agent or a real-client click-through, then check UTMs and whether the landing page matches the "Up to 60% off" claim.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the Attentive redirects with a real browser fingerprint or a manual click-through. That should confirm the 403s are bot-blocking and let you validate destinations and UTMs.
2. Add a plain-text alternative. First confirm whether it is truly absent or just missing from the AgentMail capture.
3. Capture raw headers from a direct-to-inbox seed. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `marketing.carters.com`.
4. Add meaningful `alt` text to the hero, gifting and service-banner images. Treat `ho.gif` and `o.gif` as false positives for the alt check.
5. Populate `<title>` with the subject or campaign name.
6. Confirm the four "Display images to show real-time content" links are intentional.
7. For our QA tooling: treat 403 from known ESP click domains (`*.attentivemail.com`) as "unverified", not FAIL. Also stop flagging `alt=""` pixels as missing alt.
