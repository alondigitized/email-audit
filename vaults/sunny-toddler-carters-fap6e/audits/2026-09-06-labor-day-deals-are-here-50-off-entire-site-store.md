# 🇺🇸 Labor Day deals are here - 50% off* entire site & store
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-06**
## Executive summary

- This is Carter's cleanest recent send precisely because it stops lying to itself: the skeleton-costume/pumpkin hero that got dressed up as "Labor Day" on 9/4 and 9/5 is back again, but this time it's honestly labeled "PREP FOR SPOOKY SEASON!" as a distinct module rather than being crammed under the Labor Day banner. That's a real fix from the last two sends, even if the underlying asset is the same one subscribers have now seen three times in a week.
- The top of the email does the job — pink-orange gradient hero, bold 50% OFF, extra 20% callout with a real code (LABORDAY), and a genuinely nice flat-lay of jogger pants in three colors with a clear Shop Now button. Where it falls down is bloat: this is a much longer email than Carter's usual send, stacking a hero, a 9-tile "Just For You" grid, a "Must-Have Deals" row, the Halloween module, and a store locator, without much visual rest between them.
- Net: solid top, cluttered middle, honest (if recycled) bottom. Call it a 6.5, rounding to a 6 or 7 depending on tolerance for length — better structural honesty than 9/4-9/5 but not a standout like the 9/2 or 9/6-earlier PJ sends.

## What's working

- The hero gradient, bold "50% OFF SITE & STORE," and yellow "EXTRA 20% OFF" code block are legible and hierarchy is clear at first glance.
- The jogger flat-lay (pink, olive, navy) is a real, well-lit product photo, not a recycled laundry pile.
- The Halloween content is now correctly bucketed under its own "PREP FOR SPOOKY SEASON!" header instead of being mislabeled as Labor Day inventory, which fixes the exact complaint from the 9/4 and 9/5 sends.
- Store locator module with address and "Get Directions" CTA is a nice local-relevance touch not seen in prior audited sends.

## What's weak

- The skeleton-costume-with-pumpkin-props photo is the third appearance this week (9/4, 9/5, now 9/6) — subscribers who opened those emails will recognize it instantly.
- The email is very long for a promo send — hero, 9-item grid, 3-item deals row, Halloween block, and store locator all in one scroll, which dilutes urgency rather than reinforcing it.
- The "Just For You" 3x3 grid is generic jeans/onesies/bodysuits with no pricing or labels, so it reads like filler rather than a personalized selection.
- No countdown or explicit end date for the Labor Day sale, despite this being pitched as an event with urgency.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email by a third — drop the "Just For You" grid or merge it into "Must-Have Deals"; six modules deep is too much scroll for a single-offer promo.
- 2. Retire the skeleton/pumpkin hero for at least one more cycle — it's now run three sends in a row and dilutes both the Halloween tease and the Labor Day urgency.
- 3. Add a hard deadline ("Ends tonight" / "Through 9/7") near the hero to justify the urgency framing in the subject.
- 4. Give the "Just For You" grid actual prices or category labels so it doesn't read as stock filler.
