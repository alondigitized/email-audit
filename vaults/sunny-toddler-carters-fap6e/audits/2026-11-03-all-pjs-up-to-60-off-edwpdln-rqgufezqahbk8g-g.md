# ALL PJs up to 60% off 🩵
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-03**
## Executive summary

- This is the last-day Sleep Week send, and it arrives with a dead countdown. The timer reads 00:00:00:00 and the banner says "ONLY FROM OCT 29-NOV 3". The email says the sale is ending or over, and the subject still pitches "ALL PJs up to 60% off" as live. Under the subject's "ALL", the hero is a gingerbread-print family in green PJs, which is warm and seasonal. It is the same creative as the Nov 1 "Up to 60% off DreamPlush PJs 🧸" send.
- The body then drifts. A 3x3 PJ grid follows the hero and is decent. After it come a "Shop by Category" row (Tops from $6, Pants from $8, Sets from $11), a red "UP TO 70% OFF* CLEARANCE" block, and a store-event bar. That is four competing offers in one email, and the 70% clearance block is visually louder than the PJ offer the subject promised. By the sixth Carter's send in a week, a subscriber has seen the "$6 tops" message on Oct 30 and Nov 2 and the PJ message several times. The cadence is fatiguing.
- Verdict: a competent template with a stale hero, a zeroed countdown, and a diluted message. Middling.

## What's working

- The gingerbread family photo is warm, seasonal and on-theme.
- The "Stellar Sleep Week" banner at the top ties the send to the campaign.
- The 3x3 PJ grid shows the product, with prints that match the hero.
- The category tiles give specific price anchors: Tops from $6, Pants from $8, Sets from $11.
- Loyalty and perks icons (Rewards, cardholder perks, curbside) sit near the footer.

## What's weak

- The countdown shows 00:00:00:00 and the banner says the sale ran Oct 29-Nov 3. It reads as expired or about to expire, and it undercuts the credibility of the "up to 60% off" offer.
- The primary CTA, "Shop up to 60% off*", is a thin outlined button that is easy to miss under a very large photo.
- The red 70% clearance block outshouts the PJ offer, and it splits the message after the subject promised PJs.
- The PJ grid has no prices, names or reviews, and the tiles don't show where they link.
- The asterisked offers lean on a long disclaimer block, and "Select Styles" tempers the "ALL PJs" claim.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the countdown so it counts down to a real end time, or remove it once the sale has closed. A zeroed timer is the biggest credibility hit in this email.
- 2. Make the hero CTA a solid, filled button with a verb and a noun, such as "Shop PJs up to 60% off".
- 3. Cut the email to one offer. Drop the clearance block and the category row, or move them well below the PJ grid.
- 4. Add prices (for example "from $14") and a rating to the PJ grid tiles.
- 5. Vary the hero across the week so it doesn't repeat the Nov 1 creative.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sleep Week ends tonight. Matching family sets from $14.`
