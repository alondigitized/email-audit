# Homepage snapshot · www.carters.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Carter's came to play. This is literally my homepage. Memorial Day sale, 50% off the entire site — I'm already opening a second tab to check what sizes my daughter aged into. This is the kind of deal I screenshot and text to my mom group immediately. The brand is pushing a big seasonal sale event hard, and for someone who literally sizes up monthly, this is the exact right message at the exact right time.

## What's working

- **The top announcement bar** ("Shop season-low prices! 50% off* entire site & store") sets the tone before I even see the logo — I already know this visit is worth my time
- **The Memorial Day hero** does the heavy lifting: cursive "memorial day sale" in red, bold "50% OFF* / ENTIRE SITE & STORE" in huge black type, "Lowest prices of the season!" subtext — I don't need to hunt for the reason to shop
- **The flat-lay imagery** of actual toddler summer pieces (floral peplum, denim shorts, pink sundress) shows me exactly what I'm here for — no ambiguity, no lifestyle fluff
- **The search bar is front and center** — as a mom with a mental list of SKUs, I can go straight to "pajamas 4T" without digging through nav
- **The profile icon has a badge** — if that's a loyalty reward notification, it's a hook that rewards me for already having an account

## What's weak

- **"Shop Now" is generic** — on a 50%-off Memorial Day event, the CTA should be "Shop the Sale" or "Shop Girls Tees" not a catch-all; I have no idea where it takes me
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — for a mom who's calculating whether to add one more bodysuit to hit free shipping, this is a missed nudge
- **No loyalty/rewards text** — Carter's has a rewards program but I see zero mention of it above the fold; this is prime real estate for "Rewards members earn double points this weekend"
- **No urgency signal** — "Memorial Day sale" implies a deadline but there's no "ends Monday" or countdown anywhere I can see; I might close the tab and come back... and forget
- **Flat-lay only** — I'd love to see a quick lifestyle image of an actual toddler in the clothes; as a first-time mom I'm always checking fit and proportion on real kids

## Recommendations

- **Add a deadline line under the hero CTA** — even "Ends Monday, May 27" would create urgency without feeling gimmicky; this is Memorial Day, the end date is already known
- **Swap "Shop Now" for "Shop the Sale"** or add a second CTA below it like "Shop Baby | Shop Toddler | Shop Girls" to route mobile shoppers faster
- **Add a loyalty callout in the hero or sticky bar** — something like "Rewards members: earn 2x points on sale" would give me a reason to log in, not just browse as a guest
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold** in the announcement strip or below the hero — "Free shipping on $35+" would push my cart from $28 to $38 instantly
