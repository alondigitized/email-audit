# Back to school, better than ever 🔔✨
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-31**
## Executive summary

- Carter's opens strong visually and then buries the lede under too many competing asks. The hero — a moody flat-lay of maroon/olive fall separates with "first-day fit? handled" — is a confident, on-brand back-to-school hook, similar to the 7/31 "Straight A's" send that scored 8.00. But where that email stayed focused, this one stacks three distinct offers into a single scroll: "up to 50% off" back-to-school, a Spider-Man x Marvel collab with no stated discount, and a BOGO 50% clearance flash sale lifted straight from the 7/30 send. Three "Shop Now"-style CTAs across three different value props means no single offer wins the eye.
- The product grid is also a mismatch for the "back to school" framing — onesies, footed pajamas, and baby rompers dominate the "we think you'll love these" section, which reads more infant/toddler than school-age. Given this brand has now run back-to-school messaging four times in the last week (7/28, 7/29, 7/31 "Straight A's", and this one) plus a recycled clearance banner from 7/30, subscriber fatigue is a real risk here even though the individual creative pieces are competent.
- Verdict: a well-produced but overstuffed send — good hero, diluted middle, recycled bottom third.

## What's working

- The hero photo and "first-day fit? handled" headline are a genuinely strong, on-theme opener with clean, unbroken rendering.
- "Shop up to 50% off*" is a clear, well-placed primary CTA button directly under the value prop copy.
- The Spider-Man x Marvel section is a fun, differentiated content beat that breaks up an otherwise standard promo email.
- Store locator, curbside pickup, Rewards+, and app icons at the bottom give the email a complete, trustworthy brand footer.
- No visual bugs — images load cleanly, text doesn't overlap, and the notebook-paper clearance banner renders correctly.

## What's weak

- Three separate offers (50% off back-to-school, Spider-Man collab, BOGO 50% clearance) compete for attention with no clear single priority.
- The "we think you'll love these" product grid skews toward infant/baby items (onesies, footed sleepers) that undercut the back-to-school narrative.
- The clearance flash sale creative is a near-repeat of the 7/30 "LIMITED TIME – clearance flash sale" send, reinforcing the recycled-creative pattern already flagged in that history.
- "Better than ever" in the subject is filler superlative with no concrete hook — no % off, no product, no urgency.
- This is the brand's fourth back-to-school-themed send in a week (7/28, 7/29, 7/31, plus this one), and cadence fatigue is starting to show.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to one offer per send — lead with "up to 50% off" back-to-school and move Spider-Man and clearance to separate, spaced-out emails so each CTA is unambiguous.
- 2. Swap the product grid to school-age apparel (the pleated skirts/crews/track sets referenced in the subhead) rather than infant onesies — right now the copy and the products don't match.
- 3. Retire the clearance banner creative for at least a cycle; reusing the 7/30 asset verbatim reads as low-effort to a subscriber who's seen four Carter's emails in a week.
- 4. Add a real preview/preheader — none is currently populating, which is a wasted inbox-real-estate opportunity.
- 5. Give the subject a concrete anchor instead of "better than ever."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 50% off first-day outfits 🍎`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Pleated skirts, track sets, 50% off — today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus: Spider-Man styles just landed for the new school year.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `BOGO 50% off clearance ends soon — shop before it's gone.`
