# 40% off their big-screen besties 🩵
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth Carter's send I've seen in a week, and it's a fine holiday-PJ push. It's weakened by a muddled subject and a split offer. The "Stellar Sleep Week" banner and the flat-lay of bow-wrapped character PJs (Snoopy, Grinch, Mickey) are warm and seasonal. "Choose your character" ties the creative together. It's a solid continuation of the 10/29 and 10/30 Sleep Week sends.
- The subject "40% off their big-screen besties" doesn't say pajamas anywhere. A parent has to open the email to find out what's on sale. Inside, the single clear offer, 40% off licensed characters, is a small ghost button under the hero. Below it, a 3x3 grid of gingerbread PJs has no prices or links I can tell, and a red "Up to 70% off clearance" block competes with the headline offer. The 40% offer is then diluted by two asterisks and a long block of fine print.
- Judgement: pleasant and on-season, but it needs a clearer subject and a single dominant CTA. Stronger than a generic clearance blast, weaker than the 10/29 glow-in-the-dark launch.

## What's working

- The "Stellar Sleep Week" teal banner with "ONLY FROM OCT 29-NOV 3" gives a credible, visible deadline and keeps the campaign consistent with earlier sends.
- The hero flat-lay of character PJs with red and green velvet bows is giftable and seasonal, and it's the first thing the eye lands on.
- "Choose your character / Dr. Seuss' The Grinch" copy gives a clear reason to click.
- The "Shop by Category" row has real price anchors: tops from $6, pants from $8, sets from $11.
- The rewards and perks strip (curbside, cardholder perks, in-app points) is visible for members.

## What's weak

- The subject never says PJs or sleepwear, and "big-screen besties" is cryptic. The hero never says "besties" either.
- The primary CTA "Shop 40% off*" is a thin outlined ghost button with a small label. It's the weakest-looking element on the page.
- The red "UP TO 70% OFF* CLEARANCE" block is loud and competes with the 40% offer. Two headline discounts with two asterisks reads as muddled.
- The 3x3 PJ grid has no prices, names or visible CTA. It's pretty but not very actionable.
- The fine print is long and dense, and "select styles" limits the 40% offer. Both undercut trust in the headline.
- The hero is a flat-lay with no children shown, so there's little emotional or demographic pull for a parent shopper.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rewrite the subject so it says what is on sale and when it ends, for example "40% off Grinch & Snoopy PJs through Nov 3."
- 2. Make the primary CTA a solid, filled button in brand blue, with a noun and size cue: "Shop 40% off character PJs."
- 3. Add prices or "from $X" labels, and links, to the 3x3 grid. Add a short badge such as "Grinch" or "Snoopy" so each tile earns its click.
- 4. Demote the 70% clearance block to a smaller strip below the category row, or drop it from this send. Keep one hero offer.
- 5. Replace one tile or the hero with kids wearing the PJs, which would add warmth and demographic fit.
- 6. Add one line of social proof, such as a star rating or "Top seller."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Grinch & Snoopy PJs: 40% off thru Nov 3`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Holiday PJs they'll actually love, 40% off 🩵`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stellar Sleep Week ends Nov 3. Character sleepwear from $6, plus rewards points.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Pick their favorite character. Matching sets, gowns and 2-packs inside.`
