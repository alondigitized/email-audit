# ‼️ Back-to-school stock up szn is here ‼️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-08**
## Executive summary

- This is the same lavender-denim-stool hero from yesterday's send (8/7, "📓 Back-to-school stock up from $5 📓") with the identical "BUY 1 GET 2 FREE denim" badge, repackaged under a new, spam-flagged subject line stacked with double exclamation marks. The creative itself is clean — genuinely nice product photography, a clear grid of "we think you'll love these," lifestyle shots for baby/toddler/kid category entry, and a bold red 70% off clearance block with gender/age-segmented buttons. But this is now the fourth time this week Carter's has reused a hero image within 24-48 hours (denim stool on 8/7 and 8/8; hopscotch chalk on 8/5, 8/6, 8/7), and the subject line degradation — from a concrete "$5" price point yesterday to a vague, punctuation-heavy "szn is here" today — is a step backward for open likelihood even though the body content is nearly as strong as before.
- The email converts well once opened: unambiguous "Shop Now" CTA under the hero, a well-organized product grid, and a high-contrast clearance banner with six specific category buttons. The weak links are the subject line's spam-adjacent formatting and the fact that a subscriber who opened yesterday's near-identical email has almost no reason to re-engage today.
- Judgement: solid execution, undercut by lazy asset reuse and a subject line that's a downgrade from yesterday's.

## What's working

- The hero photography (stacked folded denim on a wood stool against periwinkle) is clean, seasonally appropriate, and on-brand.
- "BUY 1 GET 2 FREE denim" and "PACK THEIR BACKPACK: FROM $5" together give two distinct, concrete offers above the fold.
- The "Shop It All" section with baby/toddler/kid lifestyle photos and named CTAs ("Shop Baby >", "Shop Toddler >", "Shop Kid >") gives clear category entry points.
- The red 70% off clearance block is high-contrast and segments by age + gender (Baby Girl, Toddler Girl, Girl, Baby Boy, Toddler Boy, Boy) — easy to scan and click straight to a relevant assortment.
- No render bugs — images loaded, text is legible, no overlap or broken layout.

## What's weak

- This is a same-day-adjacent rerun of yesterday's (8/7) hero and BUY 1 GET 2 FREE denim badge — zero new creative for anyone who opened that email.
- Subject line "‼️ Back-to-school stock up szn is here ‼️" is vaguer and more spam-coded (double bang emoji, ALL-CAPS-adjacent "szn") than yesterday's cleaner, price-anchored "📓 Back-to-school stock up from $5 📓."
- No urgency or deadline anywhere in the body — "clearance" and "new markdowns added" imply timeliness but there's no stated end date, weakening the case for opening today versus tomorrow.
- Store locator block ("Carter's near you") pushes a curbside/rewards module before any social proof or reviews — feels like real estate that could reinforce the offer instead.
- Fine print at the bottom (BUY 1 GET 2 FREE denim exclusions, 70% off "based on MSRP or original ticketed price") means the headline numbers overstate real savings on many items.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap the hero: reusing the exact 8/7 denim-stool image two sends in a row is the single biggest drag here — put a fresh scene in front of a subscriber who already saw this yesterday, or at minimum change the styling/angle.
- 2. Fix the subject line — trade the vague "szn is here" + double exclamation for the same concrete-offer approach that scored well yesterday.
- 3. Add a real deadline to the clearance banner ("70% off ends Sunday") — right now nothing in the body creates urgency to act today.
- 4. Move the store-locator/curbside module below the clearance CTA block, or drop it from this send — it's not driving the primary offer.
