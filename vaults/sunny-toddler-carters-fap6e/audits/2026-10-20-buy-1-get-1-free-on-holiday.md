# Buy 1, Get 1 free on holiday 🌟
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand BOGO send with a strong top fold. A red "RSVPs are calling / BUY 1, GET 1 FREE*" banner sits over a warm photo of a toddler with a bow in front of a tree, with "watch them glow" across it. The offer and the mood land in about two seconds. On a day when Carter's has sent a stream of "up to 60% off Black Friday" emails (Oct 17 "PREVIEW", Oct 19 "FINAL COUNTDOWN" and "ENDS SOON"), a real BOGO is a more concrete hook.
- The body is weaker. The "We think you'll love these" grid is nine plain product shots of jeans, joggers and white bodysuits. There are no prices, names, holiday styling or links to anything specific. It doesn't back up the holiday promise in the hero, and it reads like a generic bestseller grid. The 70% off clearance block then competes with the BOGO as a second headline offer, and the BOGO fine print ("first item at MSRP… excludes select styles and clearance") is buried in the footer.
- Verdict: a solid, safe send with a good hero and a lazy middle. Its one real edge is the BOGO, and it doesn't use that edge in the products or the deadline.

## What's working

- The BOGO banner and "watch them glow" photo give a clear, seasonal hero that shows above the fold.
- The CTA button is full-width, high-contrast white on the photo, and repeats the offer.
- "Shop by Age" (Baby / Toddler / Kid) with lifestyle photos is a useful, parent-friendly way to navigate.
- The footer rewards and perks row (curbside, cardholder perks, in-app points) adds member value without cluttering the body.
- Layout renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlaps.

## What's weak

- The product grid is nine near-identical basics (jeans, joggers, white bodysuits) with no prices, names or holiday items. It contradicts the "holiday styles" promise.
- There is no deadline or end date anywhere, which is odd for a promo that repeats the "ends soon" language of the week's earlier sends.
- The 70% off clearance block is a second big red offer that splits attention from the BOGO.
- The BOGO terms are only in the footer, and the asterisked terms ("first item at MSRP", "excludes clearance") undercut the "free" claim.
- The hero CTA, "Buy 1, Get 1 Free* Holiday", is a restated offer and not a verb-led action. It reads awkwardly.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the product grid with holiday-specific items: pajamas, plaid, velvet, holiday sets. Add names and "BOGO" or sale prices under each. This is the biggest lift because it makes the click concrete.
- 2. Add a credible end date ("Ends Sunday") or a countdown. Cadence is heavy, and urgency is the only thing missing from the BOGO.
- 3. Move the clearance block below the age navigation, or shrink it so BOGO stays the single hero offer.
- 4. Change the CTA to "Shop BOGO Holiday Styles" and put a one-line qualifier ("First item at full price, second free") beside it.
- 5. Link each product tile to its product page, not the generic category.
