- The hero image matches the subject line. The PJs are the first thing you see.
- "Shop from $9" is a specific verb plus price, and the outlined button is easy to find.
- The product photography is soft and seasonal, and it fits the "movie marathons & family nights" copy.
- The red clearance block is the strongest visual contrast in the email. Its Baby/Toddler/Girl/Boy buttons are easy to tap.
- The Rewards, curbside and in-app perks row is a tidy loyalty reminder at the bottom.

## What's weak

- The "We think you'll love these" grid has no prices, names or links I can see. It also repeats near-identical white bodysuits and black joggers. It looks like a filler recommendation block, not curation.
- The OshKosh × Sergio Hudson section repeats the 10/22 send, and it is unrelated to PJs.
- The email carries three competing offers: PJs from $9, the Sergio Hudson collection, and up to 70% off clearance*. The "from $9" price is never repeated or tied to a specific product.
- The CTA buttons are low-contrast ghost outlines. The hero button is easy to skim past.
- There is no urgency, no end date and no social proof. The footer fine print for the 70% claim is long and sits far from the offer.

- 2. Put the offer in the hero image or headline, for example "Soft knit PJs from $9". At the moment the price only appears on the button.
- 3. Make the primary CTA a filled button in the brand blue or red, and keep the copy specific, for example "Shop PJs from $9".
- 4. Add a credible time limit, such as "Sleepwear deal ends Sunday". Put the Rewards benefit near the CTA, for example "Earn 2X points on PJs".

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a well-formed Attentive/Movable Ink build with a viewport meta, `lang="en"` and a hidden preheader. But it has no plain-text part, an empty `<title>`, and missing alt text on several content images. The automated link probe returned 403 on all 12 wrapped Attentive tracking links, so link health and destinations are unverified. This doesn't necessarily mean the links are broken for recipients.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links returned 403.** These include "Shop from $9", "carter's", "osh kosh bigosh", "little planet", "Otter Avenue", "Skiphop", the hero image link and four product-image links. Every one is an Attentive click-redirect of the form `https://carters.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/<opaque token>`.
- **Probable cause is bot blocking, not a broken campaign.** A uniform 403 across every link on the same redirect domain points to the redirector rejecting the QA client's user-agent or IP. A genuinely dead campaign would more likely produce mixed codes. I can't confirm either way from this data, so the links need a manual or real-browser check.
- **Coverage is thin.** 12 links were probed and 49 skipped, so about 80% of links were never checked, including the footer links.
- **Tracking pixels.**
  - `carters.attentivemail.com/ss/o/.../ho.gif` is an open pixel inside the hidden `.pixel-wrapper`.
  - `mi.carters.com/p/up/745a17bbb73445ad/o.gif?mi_u=1150794179&mi_ecmp=10_23_2026_CAR_FRI_STO_ENGAGERS` is a Movable Ink pixel.
  - Both are expected. The `mi_u` parameter carries a per-recipient ID in a URL, which is normal for Movable Ink but worth knowing about.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`[FAIL]` Plain-text part is 0 chars.** There is no `text/plain` alternative. This hurts deliverability and accessibility and is a spam-filter signal. It is a real finding unless the relay stripped the part, which I can't tell from this data.
- **`[WARN]` `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are not found.** The QA notes the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check the raw headers on the original message.
- **`[WARN]` `Authentication-Results` is absent,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- **Visible footer unsubscribe and physical address** weren't in the truncated source, so I can't confirm them.
- **Sender domain:** `marketing.carters.com` is a subdomain of the brand's own domain, which is good for alignment. I haven't verified DKIM alignment.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters can't be verified.** Every CTA is wrapped in an opaque Attentive redirect, and the 403s prevented resolving the final URLs. Whether destinations carry `utm_*` parameters, and whether "Shop from $9" lands on a PJ or sleepwear page matching the "Soft knit PJs from $9" subject, are both unconfirmed.
- **Campaign ID:** the only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=10_23_2026_CAR_FRI_STO_ENGAGERS`, which suggests an "engagers" segment. It is Movable Ink's parameter, not a UTM.
- **Unrelated link targets:** the product-image links point to jeans, bodysuits and joggers, not pajamas. Whether this is a content mismatch is outside this technical scope, but the destination URLs are worth checking.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the 12 Attentive links with a real browser user-agent, or a headless browser that follows redirects. This tells you whether the 403s are bot blocking or real. Then resolve the final URLs and check for UTMs.
2. Add a `text/plain` alternative. Attentive can generate one.
3. Capture the raw message headers, bypassing the relay. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
4. Add descriptive alt text to the hero GIF, the four service banners and the Movable Ink image. Set `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
5. Populate `<title>`, for example with the subject line.
6. Raise the link probe cap, or specifically include the footer, unsubscribe and preference-center links. Probing only 12 of 61 limits confidence.
