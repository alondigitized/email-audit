## What's working

- **The stacked-discount hero** — "40% OFF + EXTRA 20%" with an explicit code is literally how I shop Carter's. I already know how to coupon-stack here; this confirms the method immediately.
- **The banner + hero redundancy** — the promo banner at the top reinforces the code ("Code SUMMER20 = extra 20% off*") and the hero repeats it. As a mobile shopper I might have missed one; two placements means it lands.
- **The lifestyle image** — a young mom physically playing with a laughing toddler is me on a Tuesday. It's not a flat product shot; it's a mood shot that says "your summer with your kid."
- **Mobile-first layout** — the search bar is prominent, the hamburger and cart are thumb-reachable. Nothing fights for space.

## What's weak

- **"Shop Now" is too generic** — Carter's has baby, toddler, and kids all in one place. "Shop Now" takes me somewhere, but where? If I'm sizing up fast (which I am, monthly), I want "Shop Toddler" or "Shop Girls 2T–4T" so I land in my aisle.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — I always check this before I hit add-to-cart. If I can't see "$35 ships free" above the fold, I'm tabbing away to do math before I even browse.
- **Asterisk without disclosure** — "40% OFF*" has a star but no fine print in sight. As someone who reads sale exclusions carefully, that asterisk immediately makes me skeptical about whether my pajamas stack.
- **No loyalty/rewards callout** — Carter's has a rewards program. If I could see my points balance or a "Members get an extra X% off" nudge, I'd start mentally calculating faster.
- **No urgency signal** — no countdown, no "ends Sunday," nothing. The offer floats in time. I'll bookmark it for later and forget.

## Recommendations

- **Add a deadline or countdown banner**: "Sale ends May 11 — 40% off + code SUMMER20" would push me to add to cart tonight instead of next week.
- **Swap "Shop Now" for category-specific CTAs**: "Shop Toddler Sale" and "Shop Baby Sale" side by side, so I land directly in size-appropriate inventory in one tap.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold in the promo banner**: the top strip has room — append "| Free shipping on $35+" right next to the code callout.
- **Add a rewards/loyalty nudge**: a small pill below the CTA — "Earn rewards on this purchase. Sign in →" — keeps high-LTV repeat shoppers like me engaged without a full loyalty takeover.
