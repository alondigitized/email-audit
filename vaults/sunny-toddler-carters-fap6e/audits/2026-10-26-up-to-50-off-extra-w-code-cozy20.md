# Up to 50% off + extra w/ code: COZY20 🩵
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand flash-sale send. The hero is the strongest part. "cozy flash, up to 50% OFF*, + EXTRA 20% OFF, code COZY20, Ends Wednesday" has a clear hierarchy, a real deadline and a visible code. Anyone who opened the 10/24 "COZY FLASH 🚨" email has seen this exact creative, so it reads as a re-send.
- The body is where it loses focus. Below the hero is an unlabeled "WE THINK YOU'LL LOVE THESE" grid of 9 plain boys' jeans, joggers and white bodysuits. They have no prices, no names and no link to the cozy theme. The PJ module and the 70%-off clearance block pull in two more directions. The "Stellar Sleep Week" banner at the top promotes a different event that starts Oct 29, which competes with the sale that is live now.
- Judgement: a competent reminder, but it is cluttered and repetitive. It would lift if it showed one hero and a few real products with prices.

## What's working

- The hero is dominant, with one number (50%), a stacked bonus (extra 20%) and the code COZY20 in a highlighted band.
- "Ends Wednesday" gives a credible deadline right at the top.
- The festive PJ photo (gingerbread, plaid, candy-cane prints) fits the season and is the best-looking image in the email.
- The red 70% clearance block has clear category buttons (Baby Girl, Toddler Boy and so on), so shoppers can go straight to their size.
- The Rewards and perks strip is in the footer, and nothing is broken or overlapping in the render.

## What's weak

- The "Stellar Sleep Week / Coming Soon" banner above the hero promotes Oct 29–Nov 3 and splits attention from the live sale.
- The 3x3 "you'll love these" grid shows near-identical jeans, joggers and bodysuits. It has no prices, no sale badges and no cozy tie-in, and it takes a lot of vertical space.
- Three competing offers stack up: 50% + 20%, 70% clearance and Sleep Week. That is a lot for one send.
- The hero's "Shop Now" is a thin outline button that lacks visual weight. The footer fine print also runs long (asterisks, exclusions, "not valid with any other offer").
- It has been about 36 hours since the near-identical "COZY FLASH 🚨" (10/24) and the "$9+ Cozy PJs 🧸" send (10/25), so this is the third cozy/PJ push in three days.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the grid, or replace it with 4–6 cozy PJs showing sale price and a "with COZY20" price, so the hero offer pays off on real products.
- 2. Move the Sleep Week banner below the hero, or drop it until the flash sale ends.
- 3. Make the hero button a solid fill with specific copy, such as "Shop cozy PJs" instead of "Shop Now".
- 4. Add a countdown or "Last day Wednesday" nudge on the final send, and space the cozy sends further apart.
- 5. Put the clearance block and Rewards teaser below the product grid.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: extra 20% off cozy PJs`
- **Subject Alt B:** `COZY20 ends Wednesday 🩵`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack code COZY20 on up to 50% off PJs, bodysuits & more.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Extra 20% off select styles online, ends Wednesday.`

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **Coverage is thin.** 12 links were probed and 46 skipped, so the footer and social links are untested. An unsubscribe link is meant to be probed, but none appears in the findings I have.
- **Two tracking pixels sit in the HTML.**
  - `carters.attentivemail.com/ss/o/…/ho.gif`, a 1×1 pixel with `alt=""`.
  - `mi.carters.com/p/up/745a17bbb73445ad/o.gif?mi_u=1150794179&mi_ecmp=10_26_2026_CAR_MON_AM_ENGAGERS`, a second open pixel.
