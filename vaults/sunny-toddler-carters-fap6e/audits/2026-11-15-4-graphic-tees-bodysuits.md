- This is the Day 12 finale of the 12 Days of Deals, and the hero does its job. A "DAY 12 / $12 & UNDER" banner sits over a blue panel with a big "$4 graphic tees & bodysuits" headline. A pile of holiday-themed kids' tops (Santa on a motorbike, "Dad's Mini") sits below it, with a "today only!" badge and a Shop Now button. The $4 price point is the strongest and most concrete offer in the whole run.
- Below the hero, the email falls back into the usual Carter's habits. A "Up to 60% off holiday styles" strip sits above the hero. ## What's working

- The $4 price is huge and legible, and it sits in the hero next to the product.
- The "today only!" badge adds credible urgency.
- The product pile is colorful and seasonal, and it shows the actual items on sale.
- The price-match banner right under the hero adds trust.
- The $5 rewards banner gives members an extra reason to buy.

## What's weak

- The "Up to 60% off holiday styles" strip above the hero pushes the $4 offer down and splits the focus.
- The 70% off clearance block competes with the hero as a second big offer.
- The packable puffer and the "Staying in" PJ modules have no price or offer, so they look like filler.
- The email gives no sense of "Day 12 / last day." The finale moment is wasted.
- The hero's Shop Now button sits on top of the product pile, covering the products.

- 3. Put the Shop Now button below the product pile so it doesn't cover the items. Add size-range or "newborn–5T" copy.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally sound (lang, viewport, `role="presentation"` tables, preheader). However, all 12 probed tracked links returned 403, the text part is empty (0 chars), and several linked images lack usable alt text. The 403s are probably bot-blocking of the QA prober rather than real breakage, so I could not verify any destination.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **12 of 12 probed links returned 403.** They are all `https://carters.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...` redirect URLs. This covers the "Shop Now", "Shop PJs", "Skiphop", "Otter Avenue", "little planet", "osh kosh bigosh", "carter's" logo, "MEMBERS GET A $5 REWARD*" and "Up to 60% Off | Holiday styles" links.
- **Likely a probe artifact.** A uniform 403 across every Attentive redirect, including the logo, looks like ESP bot protection. It does not look like 12 separate broken campaigns. This is not confirmed, and a real-browser or mobile-UA check is needed.
- **Real-time-content link.** The "Display images to show real-time content" link (also 403) is a dynamic-content/web-view link. If it fails for real users, that fallback is lost.
- **Unprobed links.** 44 links were skipped by design, so coverage is partial.
- **Tracking pixels.** There are two. `carters.attentivemail.com/ss/o/.../ho.gif` is hidden inside `.pixel-wrapper`. `mi.carters.com/p/up/.../o.gif?mi_u=1150794179&mi_ecmp=11_15_2026_CAR_SUN_AM_ENGAGERS` is a Movable Ink request. It carries a subscriber ID (`mi_u`) and a segment label (`ENGAGERS`) in the query string.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty title.** `<title></title>` is empty in the `<head>`.
- **Linked images without usable alt text.** The QA tool shows two linked images with `[image: MzczMjo6...]` as their link text, so no meaningful alt exists. The "Shop Now" hero and PJ secondary images are in this group. Any screen reader or images-off client will announce a filename-style string or nothing for those links. Four `SERVICES_BANNER_0x.png` images and `264477f43986d8bb.png` (Movable Ink) are also flagged.
- **False positives.** The `ho.gif` pixel already has `alt=""` in the source, which is correct for a decorative image. The `o.gif` pixel is likely the same. Neither needs a fix.
- **Plain-text fallback.** The text part is 0 chars ([FAIL]). Clients that render text-only, and spam filters that weigh multipart/alternative, get nothing.
- **Preheader padding.** The preheader is padded with `͏` and soft-hyphen characters, which is standard. The style is hidden with `display:none`. No issue.
- **No issues found** for viewport, charset, MSO conditionals, or the Google Fonts `<link>` (it is wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible truncated source. The source is truncated, so the footer and body below the first rows are not checked.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post.** Both headers were not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. If they are really absent, there is no RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe, which Gmail and Yahoo require for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results.** The header was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `marketing.carters.com`.
- **Footer.** Unsubscribe and physical-address presence could not be confirmed from the truncated source. The QA tool says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but it does not list the result.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Destinations unverifiable.** Every link is wrapped in an Attentive redirect, and the probes returned 403, so I could not resolve final URLs. I cannot confirm UTM parameters or landing-page alignment with the "Up to 60% Off", "$4 graphic tees" or "$5 reward" claims.
- **Campaign ID.** The only campaign signal visible is `mi_ecmp=11_15_2026_CAR_SUN_AM_ENGAGERS` on the Movable Ink pixel.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the Attentive links with a browser-like UA or a real browser to separate prober blocking from real failures. Until then, treat the 403s as unconfirmed.
2. Add a `text/plain` part. A 0-char text version is the clearest confirmed defect.
3. Set a meaningful `<title>` (for example, the subject line).
4. Add descriptive alt text to linked hero and PJ images and the services banners, so the links have readable names.
5. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC, on the original message headers, not the relay copy.
6. Follow the redirects to the final URLs and check UTM parameters and landing-page match.
7. Suppress the `ho.gif` and `o.gif` missing-alt warnings in the QA rules, since both are decorative pixels.
