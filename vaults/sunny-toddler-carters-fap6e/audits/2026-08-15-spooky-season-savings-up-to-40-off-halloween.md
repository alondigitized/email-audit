- The "fall photo ready" lifestyle image is a genuinely new asset this week — cozy, on-brand, well-styled.

## What's weak

- Hero creative is the identical "baby's first Halloween" sweatshirt / "the boo is back" image used on 8/13 and 8/14 — third consecutive day, subscriber fatigue risk.
- No pricing specifics anywhere (no $ amounts, no "starting at") — everything is "up to X% off," which reads as vague after a week of the same framing.
