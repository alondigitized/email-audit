# Making its debut: OshKosh X Sergio Hudson 🎭
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a brand-story send, and a good-looking one. The hero is a red-framed photo of a girl in striped red overalls with big "OSHKOSH × SERGIO HUDSON" lettering. It gives the eye one place to land. The mid-email photo of Sergio Hudson with two kids adds a human face to the collab. It feels more editorial than the promo emails Carter's has sent lately, such as the 10/21 "HOLIDAY SALE - Buy 1, Get 1 free ✨" and the run of "Up to 60% off Early Black Friday" sends.
- The weakness is that there's no reason to act today. There's no price, no offer, no deadline and no named product, only a "Shop Now" button under "BOLD, ONE-OF-A-KIND, EXCLUSIVELY OSHKOSH." Compared with the BOGO sends from the last two days, this is a lower-pressure email. That's a welcome change in cadence, but it asks for a click on brand curiosity alone.
- The footer also carries coupon and rewards fine print ("Coupon valid for one-time use only...") that matches nothing in the email. It reads like a template leftover.
- Overall it's a strong visual launch and a soft commercial one. It should do well with people who already like OshKosh, and it won't convert many price-driven shoppers.

## What's working

- The hero is the clear first read. The big lettering, red frame and single model make the collab obvious in seconds.
- The OshKosh/Carter's/Little Planet/Otter Avenue/Skip Hop header shows the brand family without crowding the hero.
- The Sergio Hudson photo with two kids gives the collab a face and a story, and the "Fashion Icon" copy adds credibility.
- The four-photo grid shows denim, stripes and red looks, so the range feels real, not just one hero item.
- The red, black and cream palette is consistent from top to bottom, and the layout renders cleanly with no broken images.

## What's weak

- There is no offer, price point or deadline anywhere, so nothing pushes a click today.
- "Shop Now" is generic and appears twice, with the same label for the hero and the grid. "Learn More" in the middle splits attention.
- None of the grid images is labeled with a product name, price or size range, so a shopper can't tell what they'd be buying.
- The coupon and rewards fine print in the footer doesn't match any offer in the email and may confuse readers.
- The Harlem's Fashion Row banner near the bottom is a second story that adds length without helping the sale.

- 1. Add one concrete commercial hook to the hero, such as an early-access window, a launch-week perk or a free-shipping threshold. Make the CTA say what's inside, for example "Shop the collection."

- 3. Label the grid with product names and prices, and link each tile to its own product page.
- 4. Cut the "Learn More" button. Fold the Hudson story into the hero copy, or make it a text link.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is structurally sound: it has a `lang` attribute, `role="presentation"` layout tables, MSO conditionals, and a preheader. Its main technical weaknesses are a 0-character plain-text part, an empty `<title>`, and alt-text gaps. All 8 probed material links returned 403, but that points to bot-blocking of the click-tracker. The QA run reports a 19% pass rate.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 8 probed `carters.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...` links returned 403.** They cover "Shop Now", the OshKosh logo, "osh kosh bigosh", "little planet", "Otter Avenue", "Skiphop", and the two Sergio Hudson / Harlem's Fashion Row editorial links.
  - Every link is wrapped in an Attentive click-tracker, and all 403s are on that host.
  - A uniform 403 across one redirector most likely means the tracker is rejecting the automated probe (bot or User-Agent filtering). It is not confirmed that real recipients are blocked.
  - The real destination for each link is unverified. Re-test with a browser-like client before calling the links broken.
- **Coverage is partial.** 12 links were probed and 15 were skipped, mostly footer and social links, so the audit can't speak to those.
- **The tracking pixel is present and correct.** `ho.gif` on `carters.attentivemail.com/ss/o/...` is a 1x1 open pixel inside a hidden div.
- **A subscriber ID appears in an image URL.** `https://mi.carters.com/p/rp/264477f43986d8bb.png?mi_u=1150794179&mi_ecmp=10_22_2026_OKB_THU_NOON_ENGAGERS&mi_campaign=10...`
  - `mi_u=1150794179` looks like a per-recipient identifier exposed in a plain GET query string.
  - The campaign tag shows the send went to the "ENGAGERS" segment.
  - This is an observation, not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` in `<head>`. Some clients and web-view versions use it as the tab or page title.
- **Alt text on linked images:**
  - The header logo image (`MzczMjo6T3NoS29zaF9Mb2dvX0VtYWlsX0hlYWRlci5wbmc6Oj...`, hosted on `d3ptznaucccg76.cloudfront.net`) has no usable alt text. The link text is extracted as `[image: MzczMjo6T3NoS29zaF9Mb2dvX0VtYWlsX0hlYWRl]`.
  - `mi.carters.com/p/rp/264477f43986d8bb.png` was flagged for missing alt.
  - The `ho.gif` flag is a false positive. The source shows `alt=""` on a hidden 1x1 tracking pixel, which is correct for a decorative image.
- **Long alt strings:** Some editorial links have a concatenated heading and body as their accessible name. Examples are "OSHKOSH X SERGIO HUDSON | BOLD, ONE-OF-A-KIND..." and "HARLEM'S FASHION ROW | Connects designers of color...". Screen readers will read these in full.
- **Fonts:** The `<link>` to Google Fonts (League Spartan) sits inside a `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` block. Many clients strip it and fall back to the system font.
- **Layout:** Responsive rules exist (`@media (max-width:660px)`, `.stack .column`, `.mobile_hide` and `.desktop_hide`). No defects were found in the visible source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) were found in the visible HTML. The source is truncated, so the rest of the body is unchecked. The only personalization found is the `mi_u` ID in the image URL described above.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers, so this is unconfirmed, not a proven omission. Check them in the raw message headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same reason.
- **Plain-text part is missing or empty (0 chars).** This is a FAIL. Multipart emails without a text alternative can score worse with spam filters, and text-only clients see nothing. It could also be a capture artifact, but the QA check treats it as a defect.
- **Unsubscribe link and physical address:** The unsubscribe link is always included in probing, and it is not in the failed list. The footer is truncated out of the supplied source, so the unsubscribe link and postal address can't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters can't be verified.** Every CTA is an opaque Attentive redirect (`/e/c/<token>`), so UTMs can't be read from the HTML.
- **Landing pages weren't resolved.** The 403 responses blocked redirect resolution, so alignment between the "OshKosh X Sergio Hudson" messaging and the destination pages is untested.
- The "Skiphop" and "Otter Avenue" links likely lead to sub-brand destinations, but this is unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the 8 failing tracker links with a browser-like User-Agent, or follow the redirect chain manually, to separate bot-blocking from real breakage. Resolve the final URLs and check their UTMs.
2. Add a plain-text MIME part. Confirm in raw headers whether it is truly absent or just not captured.
3. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` from the original message, not the relay copy.
4. Fill in `<title>` with the subject or campaign name.
5. Add descriptive alt text to the header logo (for example, "OshKosh B'gosh") and to the `mi.carters.com` image. Shorten the concatenated link and alt strings on the editorial blocks.
6. Suppress the `ho.gif` alt-text warning in the QA tool, since `alt=""` is correct there.
7. Raise the probe cap so the 15 skipped links, including the footer and unsubscribe, can be covered.
