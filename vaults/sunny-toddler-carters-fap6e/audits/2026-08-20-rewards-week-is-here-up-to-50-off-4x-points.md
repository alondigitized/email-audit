# Rewards week is here ✨ Up to 50% off* + 4X points
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a loyalty-program push dressed as a sale email, and it's a decent one. The blue gradient hero with the sparkle motif is clean, the "REWARDS WEEK" branding is legible, and "UP TO 50% OFF + 4X POINTS" is a genuinely strong stacked offer that reads clearly at a glance. It's also a clear break from the fabric-texture "UP TO 50% OFF PLUS EXTRA 20% OFF" hero that's been recycled since 8/14 (8/14, 8/16, 8/19) — this send at least earns a distinct visual identity tied to its subject.
- Where it falls down is specificity and product relevance: the "PERKS OF BEING A REWARDS MEMBER" panel is generic loyalty boilerplate that could run in any month, and the 9-product grid below has zero labels, prices, or category context — just a wall of denim, bodysuits, and rompers with no indication of what's on sale or why these items were picked. For a "member benefits" email this needed either a clearer breakdown of the earn structure or tighter merchandising; instead it splits the difference and does neither fully.
- Judgment: a solid mid-tier send — better than the repeated stacking-discount emails, but the generic perks block and unlabeled product grid cap it well short of the after-school clearance sends' polish.

## What's working

- The blue gradient hero with sparkle accents is visually distinct from every other recent send in the brand history — no repeated fabric-texture creative.
- "UP TO 50% OFF + 4X POINTS" is a clear, stacked, concrete offer with the earn-rate spelled out ("$5 reward for every $25 spent").
- Two "Shop Now" CTAs (hero + after perks panel) give the eye multiple clean exit points without clutter.
- No render bugs — all nine product thumbnails load cleanly, brand sub-logos (OshKosh, Little Planet, Otter Avenue, Skip Hop) render sharp.

## What's weak

- The perks panel ($5/100 points, exclusive offers, birthday gift, app exclusives) is generic loyalty copy with no tie to Rewards Week specifically — it could run any week of the year.
- The 9-item product grid has no prices, no product names, and no sale badges — a shopper has to click blind to find out what's actually discounted.
- No urgency or end date anywhere in the email — "Rewards Week" implies a window but no dates are shown, so there's no reason to act today vs. next week.
- The perks icons (reward tag, megaphone, birthday cake, phone) are flat and clip-arty relative to the polished hero above them — a visual step down mid-email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a start/end date or countdown to "Rewards Week" in the hero — right now the name implies urgency the copy never delivers.
- 2. Label the product grid with at least category name and price/discount per item — nine unlabeled thumbnails give shoppers no reason to click a specific one.
- 3. Tie the perks panel copy to this specific event (e.g., "This week only: earn 4X points on every purchase") instead of evergreen loyalty-program boilerplate.
- 4. Add a member name or points-balance personalization line near the top ("You have X points") to make it feel like a real account email, not a mass send.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 4X points window is open — up to 50% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Rewards Week: earn 4X points before it ends`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack member points with storewide markdowns — this week only`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `$5 back for every $25 you spend, plus up to 50% off`
