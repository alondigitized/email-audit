# $6+ Limited edition PJs for the fam 🌙
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- This is a strong Black Friday send, and the subject and hero match. "$6+ Limited edition PJs for the fam" is exactly what the pink hero shows: a mom and two kids in matching candy-cane pajamas, a "black friday exclusive" badge, and "from $6 limited edition family PJs, plus $3 cozy socks to match." The price anchor is specific and the eye lands on it first.
- After the hero, the email reverts to the usual catch-all. There's a four-tile grid of "FROM $2 / $9 / $8 / $4" deals, then a red "UP TO 70% OFF* CLEARANCE" block with six category buttons, a price match banner, and a free gift bag promo. The hero is focused, but everything below it dilutes the PJ story. The 11/22 "holiday PJs" and 11/24 "cozy holiday looks" sends already pushed this theme, so it's the third pajama-adjacent beat in four days.
- Overall it's a clear upgrade on the generic "50-60% off*" sends in the history, because it leads with a real price point instead of a percentage range. The grid below it ignores the PJ promise, so the click path is weaker than the open.

## What's working

- The subject and hero agree: "$6 limited edition family PJs" appears in both.
- The "from $6" and "plus $3 socks" stack is a concrete, low-price anchor that beats the earlier "50-60% off*" copy.
- The hero photo is warm and on-brand, and one white Shop Now button is clearly visible.
- "FREE SHIPPING ON ALL ORDERS*" sits at the very top as a clean, low-friction hook.
- The product grid carries price-led captions ("FROM $2 PER PIECE", "FROM $4") with verb-led links like "Shop Multipack Bodysuits >".

## What's weak

- The grid below the hero shows bodysuits, outfit sets, character styles and tops. None of them is the family PJs the subject promised.
- Every product link is a "Shop X >" text link that's small next to the hero. The only real buttons are Shop Now and the clearance category buttons.
- The clearance block is a second big red hero, which splits attention and brings back the "UP TO 70% OFF*" percentage language.
- The asterisks on the free shipping, clearance and gift bag offers push the exclusions to a long legal block at the bottom.
- The free gift bag banner ("$75+ in-store purchase") is a store-only offer that's irrelevant to most readers on mobile.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the PJ story run through the whole email. Replace at least two of the four grid tiles with family PJs, matching sets and the $3 socks, and link each to a PJ landing page, not the general sale.
- 2. Link Shop Now to a family PJs collection with the matching sizes grouped, and rename it "Shop Family PJs".
- 3. Cut the clearance block or move it below the fold in a smaller form. One hero offer is cleaner.
- 4. Add social proof, such as a star rating or "bestseller" tag, on the PJ tiles.
- 5. Add a real deadline, for example "Cyber Monday ends 11/30", so the "limited edition" claim has a clock.
