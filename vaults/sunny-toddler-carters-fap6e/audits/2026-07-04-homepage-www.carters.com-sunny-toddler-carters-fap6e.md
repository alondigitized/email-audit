# Homepage snapshot · www.carters.com · 2026-07-04
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-04**
## Executive summary

- This is SO my homepage — Carter's is speaking directly to me. Mom in sunglasses with a laughing toddler and a baby in a blue bucket hat? That's my Saturday morning right there. They're pushing a summer staples campaign with up to 50% off and the whole vibe says "someone on this team actually knows a first-time mom is opening this on her phone while a toddler is climbing on her."

## What's working

- **"summer staples" + "Stock up on the MVPs of your summer packing list."** — the MVP language is pure millennial mom. I felt seen.
- **The hero photo feels real:** tattooed mom in sunglasses, laughing toddler in yellow, baby in a blue striped bucket hat. Not a catalog model. I want to buy everything that baby is wearing.
- **Top promo banner** ("Up to 50% off* for a limited time!") is the first thing I see — gets my thumb moving before I've even processed the hero.
- **"Shop the Collection"** CTA is clean and unambiguous. I know exactly what happens when I tap it.
- **Carter's wordmark front-and-center** in the nav — zero brand confusion on mobile, which matters when I'm half-asleep at 6am.

## What's weak

- **"for a limited time!" urgency is soft** — no end date, no countdown. I don't know if I have two hours or two weeks, so the urgency doesn't stick.
- **No quick category links above the fold** — as someone who knows she needs pajamas in 18M, 2T, and 3T right now, a hamburger menu means extra taps I don't have patience for.
- **No free-shipping callout** — this is a HUGE missed opportunity. A "Free shipping on $35+" line is the thing that makes me pad the cart.
- **No loyalty/rewards hook** — I coupon-stack religiously. If I have points or cash to apply, I want to see that balance the second I open the app.
- **The asterisk on 50% off*** is tiny and mysterious — I immediately start wondering if the things I actually want are excluded.

## Recommendations

- **Add a free-shipping threshold bar** directly under the main banner — "Free shipping on orders $35+" is one line and directly changes my cart behavior.
- **Swap the hamburger nav for 3–4 quick-tap category pills** under the search bar: "Baby," "Toddler," "Pajamas," "Sale" — that's literally my entire purchase universe.
- **Add a hard end-date to the "limited time" copy** — even "ends July 7" would make me stop scrolling and start shopping.
- **Surface rewards balance in the nav bar** for logged-in users — the notification icon is already there; put a dollar amount next to it.
