# HOLIDAY SALE - Buy 1, Get 1 free ✨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand BOGO send that is nearly a repeat of yesterday's "Buy 1, Get 1 free on holiday 🌟". The top fold is strong. A red "RSVPs are calling / BUY 1, GET 1 FREE*" banner sits over a warm photo of a toddler with a bow in front of a tree, with "watch them glow" across it. The offer and the button are visible immediately.
- Below the hero the email loses focus. The "We think you'll love these" grid is nine unlabeled product tiles with no names, prices or links I can identify. Two more themed blocks (Grinch, Christmas Card) and a 70% off clearance banner then compete with the headline BOGO. A reader who came for BOGO ends up with three or four different offers.
- My judgement is competent but stale. It is the second BOGO creative in two days, after a week of Black Friday preview sends. It works as a reminder but won't win attention.

## What's working

- The hero offer is clear in the first screen: red banner, "BUY 1, GET 1 FREE*" and a visible white CTA button.
- The festive toddler photo is warm and seasonal, and it carries over from yesterday's mood-driven creative.
- The Grinch block ("Shop from $10") gives a concrete entry price and a licensed, giftable hook.
- The sibling-matching Christmas card block is a strong, specific use case for parents.
- The loyalty and service icons at the bottom (curbside, credit card perks, in-app points, Rewards) are useful, and the fine print is present.

## What's weak

- The "We think you'll love these" grid has no product names, prices or labels, so it reads as filler. It is mostly denim, plain bodysuits and joggers, which are not festive and not obviously BOGO-related.
- The email stacks BOGO, "Shop from $10" and "Up to 70% off clearance". That dilutes the headline and muddies the offer.
- No deadline or urgency appears anywhere, even though the subject says "HOLIDAY SALE". The BOGO terms are only in the footer, and they exclude select styles and clearance.
- The hero CTA, "Buy 1, Get 1 Free* Holiday", is clumsy and just repeats the banner.
- The email is very long, with roughly five sections to scroll through.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the three competing sections to one. Lead with BOGO, and drop the clearance banner or move it to a separate send.
- 2. Add a real deadline, for example "Ends Sunday" or a countdown timer, as in the 10/19 Black Friday sends.
- 3. Label the product grid with names and the BOGO price, such as "Pick any 2, pay for 1", or replace it with specific festive hero items.
- 4. Change the CTA to a verb plus noun, such as "Shop BOGO holiday pajamas". Surface the main fine print ("first item at MSRP, equal or lesser value") near the hero.
- 5. Space out sends. Yesterday's BOGO email and this one are too similar, so vary the angle (gifting, family matching, a last-order date).

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Mix and match styles. Second one's free, through Sunday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard Attentive-sent, table-based layout with a viewport meta tag, MSO conditionals, and a mobile media query. The QA run found no confirmed code defects in the HTML. The automated findings are the 12 probed links returning 403, a 0-char plain-text part, and missing alt text on content images. The 403s and the missing headers are probably artifacts of the probe and the AgentMail relay, so I have marked those as unconfirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links returned 403.** Every one is an Attentive click-redirect on `carters.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...`. That covers the hero ("RSVPs are calling BUY 1, Get 1 FREE*…"), "Shop from $10", the brand links (carter's, osh kosh bigosh, little planet, Otter Avenue, Skiphop) and the product images.
- **Likely cause is bot-blocking.** A uniform 403 across every link on one redirect domain looks like blocking of the non-browser prober. It does not look like 12 independently dead links. The final destination URLs were never reached, so I can't confirm that any link is actually broken for subscribers.
- **48 of 60 links were not probed.** This includes footer and social links, so their status is unknown.
- **Tracking pixels are present.** There is one Attentive open pixel (`carters.attentivemail.com/ss/o/.../ho.gif`) and one `mi.carters.com/p/up/.../o.gif?mi_u=…&mi_ecmp=10_21_2026_CAR_WED_STO_ENGAGERS`. Two pixels from two vendors is not a defect.
- **The `mi_u=1150794179` parameter** is a subscriber-level identifier in a URL. This is an observation, not an error.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **`<title></title>` is empty.** This is confirmed in the source.
- **Seven content images have no alt text.** The affected images are all on `d3ptznaucccg76.cloudfront.net`: the `MzczMjo6Q0FSXzEwMjAyNl9TRUNPTkRBUllf…` secondary banners (two) and the `SERVICES_BANNER_01` to `_04_v3` images (four). The seventh is `mi.carters.com/p/rp/264477f43986d8bb.png`. Because the HTML is truncated, I can't check the `<img>` tags themselves, so this rests on the QA data.
- **The two tracking-pixel flags are false positives.** The `ho.gif` tag has `alt=""` in the source, which is correct for a decorative 1×1 image. The `o.gif` pixel is likely the same.
- **Product images do have alt text.** Their link text reads "Image of Boys Dark Wash Relaxed-Fit Classic Jeans" and similar.
- **The hero link text is very long.** It is the whole banner alt string ("RSVPs are calling BUY 1, Get 1 FREE* All holiday | watch them glow | Buy 1, Get …"). That makes for a verbose accessible name.
- **Plain-text part is empty.** QA reports "Text version is 0 chars", so clients that render plain text will show a blank body. This is the only FAIL outside the link checks.
- **No other issues found.** `lang="en"` is set, tables use `role="presentation"`, and the preheader is hidden correctly. The Google Fonts `<link>` is wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered merge tokens such as `{{ }}`, `%%`, or `[FIRSTNAME]`. The remainder of the HTML is truncated, so this covers only the portion shown.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA notes that the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so absence in the captured message does not prove the sender omitted them. Verify against raw headers from a direct-to-Gmail or Yahoo seed.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not captured.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, and QA attributes this to the relay.
- **The unsubscribe link wasn't verifiable.** QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but none appears in the findings. The footer is outside the truncated HTML, so the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are unconfirmed.
- **The From address is on a dedicated marketing subdomain.** It is `marketing.carters.com`, with the Attentive tracking domain `e.attentivemail.com` handling clicks.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. Every link goes through an opaque Attentive redirect, and the 403s blocked resolution to the carters.com destination. So I can't confirm UTM parameters or that the landing page matches the "Buy 1, Get 1 free" offer. The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=10_21_2026_CAR_WED_STO_ENGAGERS` on the `mi.carters.com` pixel.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Resolve the click URLs with a browser-like request.** Use a realistic User-Agent and follow redirects with a real client, then re-test. This separates real breakage from probe blocking, and it lets you check UTM parameters and destination alignment.
2. **Add a plain-text MIME part.** The current 0-char text part is a deliverability and accessibility gap.
3. **Add meaningful alt text to the seven content images.** Use `alt=""` only for ones that are purely decorative.
4. **Fill in `<title>`** with the subject or campaign name.
5. **Confirm headers outside the relay.** Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC from raw headers. Don't treat the current WARNs as sender defects until then.
6. **Shorten the hero link's alt text** to a concise label.
7. **Improve the audit tooling.** Probe the footer unsubscribe link and the address block, and raise the probe cap (12 of 60 links).
