# Homepage snapshot · www.carters.com · 2026-06-06
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-06**
## Executive summary

- OK, Carter's — my ride-or-die. This homepage is screaming summer sale energy and I am here for it. The clearance banner hit me right away and the teal hero with that big "UP TO 50% OFF" is exactly the dopamine hit that keeps me opening every Carter's email. Heritage, cute baby imagery, solid offer — this is a brand that knows who's paying the bills.

## What's working

- **"Clearance from $3.99" top banner** — I see this and my thumb is already tapping. That's a price point that makes me feel like I *have* to look. Pajama sets at $3.99? I'm in.
- **"UP TO 50% OFF*" hero on teal** — High contrast, big type, above the fold on mobile. This is exactly what I need to justify opening the app to my husband.
- **Baby lifestyle photo** — That sweet close-up of a baby below the hero is emotionally on target. I feel seen. That's my kid.
- **"Limited time online!" top strip** — Creates enough urgency to stop the scroll, even if it's vague on the deadline.
- **Search bar is prominent** — As a mom who knows what she wants (size 2T footed pajamas, navy, on sale), having search front and center is huge.

## What's weak

- **"Shop Now" is doing too much work** — That CTA is generic. I don't know if I'm shopping summer tops, clearance, baby, or toddler. A first-time mom with three tabs open is going to hesitate.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — I literally structure my cart around free shipping. Not showing that above the fold is leaving money on the table with me every single time.
- **"over 160 years of know how"** — This heritage copy is barely visible at the bottom and it's not what moves me. I care about fit and price, not history.
- **No pajama/sleepwear callout** — Pajamas are in my cart at all times. Carter's is *the* place for pajamas and there's zero mention of them anywhere in this viewport.
- **Asterisk on "50% OFF*"** — No fine print visible, just the asterisk dangling there. Feels like there's a catch I'd have to hunt for.

## Recommendations

- **Replace "Shop Now" with category-specific CTAs** — Even two buttons like "Shop Baby" and "Shop Toddler" would dramatically reduce friction. A mom of a 14-month-old does not want to wade through big kid sizes.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold in the hero or directly under the CTA** — "Free shipping on $35+" next to "Shop Now" would close carts faster than any other single change.
- **Add a pajamas/sleepwear callout module** — It's your signature category. Put a "Pajama faves from $8" strip under the hero. That's a Sunny click every time.
- **Kill the asterisk or resolve it inline** — Show the offer condition right there: "Up to 50% off — select styles." Don't make me hunt for the catch.
