# Homepage snapshot · www.carters.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Carter's opened with the exact homepage I fantasize about every time I get one of their emails — "50% OFF* ENTIRE SITE & STORE" for Memorial Day, front and center, no hunting required. This brand looks confident and deal-forward, which is the whole reason I have their push notifications on. Right now it's pushing a sitewide sale hard, and as a person who literally plans her clothing haul around Carter's semi-annual events, this is the signal I came to see.

## What's working

- **The double-reinforced offer** — "50% off* entire site & store" hits in the announcement bar AND the hero. Two exposures before I even process the CTA. That's the confirmation I need to trust I'm in the right place.
- **"Lowest prices of the season!"** — That specific line lands hard for someone who times purchases around sales peaks. It adds stakes to acting now vs. waiting.
- **Toddler flat-lay imagery in the hero** — The floral ruffle top, denim shorts, and pink romper are visually cute, clearly baby/toddler sizing, and look fresh — not leftover clearance.
- **"Shop Now" CTA placement** — Clean white button, big enough to tap with a thumb on mobile, no friction in finding the entry point.
- **Current campaign timing** — Memorial Day is May 25 and I'm seeing this on May 19. That's the right window to build a cart.

## What's weak

- **No loyalty hook surfaced** — I'm a Carter's Rewards member. There's a tag icon in the nav that might be my rewards or deals page, but it's unlabeled. I should see "You have 450 points — worth $X this sale" or at minimum "Rewards members earn extra today."
- **Free-shipping threshold is missing** — I always scope this before I build a cart. I need to know if I'm at $35, $50, or $75 to plan my haul. Not seeing it above the fold might cause me to underbuild my cart or bail mid-checkout.
- **"Shop Now" is too generic** — Sitewide sale means I could land on anything. A toddler mom wants "Shop Toddler Girl," "Shop Baby Boy," "Shop PJs" — not a blank portal.
- **No lifestyle imagery** — Floating clothes on a pale blue background are fine, but a real toddler giggling in the floral set would make me immediately project my kid onto the product.
- **Zero social proof above fold** — No star ratings, no "bestseller" badges, no "1,847 moms bought this." I trust reviews more than sale banners when it comes to actually adding to cart.

## Recommendations

- **Split the hero CTA into two quick-tap tiles** — "Shop Baby" and "Shop Toddler" side by side below "Shop Now," or replace the single CTA with a 2-up category entry. One extra tap is the difference between me landing somewhere useful and rage-scrolling.
- **Move the free-shipping threshold into the announcement bar** — Swap "Shop season-low prices!" for "50% off + free shipping over $35 this weekend." That combo is what gets me to checkout with a full cart.
- **Add a loyalty member callout to the hero** — Even a single line: "Rewards members, earn 2x points this sale." I have the app, I'm logged in — show me the benefit of being a repeat buyer.
- **Drop a "Fan Favorites in Pajamas" best-seller rail immediately below the hero** — A quick horizontal scroll of top-rated sleepwear sets with review counts. I will always stop scrolling for pajamas, and it would pull me deeper into the page instantly.
