# Extra credit: 50% off* back-to-school styles 🍎
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-07**
## Executive summary

- This is the third send in three days built on the exact same hopscotch-chalk-sidewalk hero and the exact same "50% OFF entire site & store" headline that ran on 8/5 and 8/6. The subject line tries to buy some freshness with "Extra credit" wordplay and an apple emoji, but the moment the email opens, a subscriber who read yesterday's or the day-before's send will recognize the creative instantly — that's fatigue, not novelty.
- What earns this email credit is everything below the hero: a genuinely new "BUY 1 GET 2 FREE denim" banner with a nice lavender studio shot of stacked jeans, a tight nine-item "we think you'll love these" grid, and a bold red 70%-off clearance block with category shortcut buttons (Baby Girl/Boy, Toddler Girl/Boy, Girl/Boy). The store locator and rewards-perks strip at the bottom is a solid, uncommon touch for a promo email. Render quality is clean throughout — no broken images, no overlapping text.
- Net: this is a well-built, on-brand email let down by the header/offer being a near carbon copy of the last two sends. A subscriber checking their inbox three days running sees the same 50% off banner and the same chalk sidewalk — that's the single biggest drag on this send's performance, not execution quality.

## What's working

- Hero message hierarchy is immediate and legible: "50% OFF ENTIRE SITE & STORE" is the first thing the eye lands on, with a clear "Shop Now" button beneath it.
- The BUY 1 GET 2 FREE denim banner is genuinely fresh creative (soft lavender background, styled jean stack) and stands out from the rest of the layout.
- Product grid ("WE THINK YOU'LL LOVE THESE...") is clean, well-cropped, and gives a real sense of assortment (rompers, bodysuits, denim, tees) without feeling cluttered.
- The red 70% clearance block with six category buttons (Baby/Toddler/Girl/Boy) is an efficient, low-friction way to route different shoppers straight to their segment.
- Store locator with map + "Get Directions" and the curbside/rewards icon row add real utility rarely seen in a straight promo blast.

## What's weak

- The hero art and 50% off headline are an exact repeat of the 8/5 and 8/6 sends — this is now a 3-peat, and the brand history already flagged 8/6 as "essentially a rerun" of 8/5.
- No models or kids shown anywhere in the email — every image is a flat lay or product stack, so there's no lifestyle/demographic signal to connect with a parent shopper emotionally.
- No urgency device at all (no "ends today," no countdown, no date) despite this brand using "⏰ ENDS TODAY" as recently as 8/3 — this send reads flatter than its siblings.
- Three separate offers competing for attention (50% off site-wide, BOGO denim, 70% clearance) dilutes the "one clear CTA" principle — a subscriber has to choose which deal is "the" deal.
- "Shop Now" is used twice and is generic; neither instance tells you what you're shopping for.

## Recommendations

- 1. Retire the hopscotch-chalk hero for at least one cycle — three consecutive days on the same background image is the clearest lever to pull. Swap in a lifestyle shot of a kid in back-to-school gear to add the demographic warmth this send is missing.
- 2. Pick one hero offer and subordinate the others. Given BUY 1 GET 2 FREE denim is the freshest asset in the email, consider leading with it instead of the now-stale 50% off banner, and push clearance/50% into secondary blocks.
- 3. Add a real urgency element (a specific end date or "final days") to the hero — this brand has used it effectively before (8/3) and this send has none.
- 4. Replace generic "Shop Now" CTAs with specific copy tied to the block ("Shop Denim," "Shop Clearance") to sharpen intent signaling.
- **Subject Alt A:** `BOGO alert: buy 1, get 2 FREE denim 🎒`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 50% off site-wide ends soon`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus up to 70% off clearance — new markdowns just added`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Tees from $5, polos from $6, and free denim when you buy 1`

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **12 of 12 probed links return HTTP 403**, all routed through the Attentive click-tracking redirector `https://carters.attentivemail.com/ss/c/u001.../h{N}/h001...`. Affected links include the primary hero CTA ("Carter's & OshKosh back-to-school shop | 50% Off*..."), the "Buy 1, Get 2 Free*" CTA, brand nav links ("Carter's", "osh kosh bigosh", "little planet", "Otter Avenue", "Skiphop"), and 4 product image links.

- Open/tracking pixels are present and load from two separate vendors: `carters.attentivemail.com/ss/o/...ho.gif` (Attentive open tracking) and `mi.carters.com/p/up/...o.gif` / `mi.carters.com/p/rp/...264477f43986d8bb.png` (secondary MI tracking, likely Movable Ink), both firing on render.
