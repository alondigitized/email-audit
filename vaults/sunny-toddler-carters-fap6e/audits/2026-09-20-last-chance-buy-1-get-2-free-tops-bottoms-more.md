# ⏰ Last chance — Buy 1, get 2 free* tops, bottoms & more
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-20**
## Executive summary

- This is Carter's running the same "Buy 1, Get 2 Free" creative from 2026-09-18 ("⚡️ Happening now, Shop buy 1, get 2 free* tops, bottoms & more"), now re-wrapped two days later with an "urgent" 00:00:00:00 countdown timer and "Last chance" subject framing. The hero delivers a clean, legible offer stack — the orange BUY 1, GET 2 FREE banner is unmissable, followed by a full size-grid, a genuine product shot for the Sleep & Play PJs, a 70% clearance banner, price-match callout, and a store-opening notice. Visually it's one of the more organized sends in this run.
- The problem is credibility, not execution: a countdown timer frozen at all zeros next to "Last chance" is either a broken render or a stale/reused asset, and either way it undercuts the one thing urgency copy is supposed to do — feel real. Combined with the fact that this is the third time this exact BOGO offer has been served in five days (09-16 five-star baby event, 09-18 BOGO, now 09-20 "last chance" BOGO), a subscriber will read this as recycled inventory dressed up with urgency language, not a genuine deadline.
- Score this a mid-pack send: strong visual hierarchy and a real offer, dragged down by the countdown-timer bug and repetitive cadence.

## What's working

- The orange hero banner makes the BUY 1, GET 2 FREE offer the unmistakable focal point — big, bold, centered.
- Full size grid (NB through 12/14) is shown up front, useful for parents who filter by size before anything else.
- The Sleep & Play PJ product shot is a real, warm image (two-way zip garments laid out) rather than stock/lifestyle filler, reinforcing "designed with baby in mind."
- Secondary offers (70% off clearance, price match, new store opening) are cleanly segmented into distinct color blocks rather than crammed together.
- Loyalty/app perks (Carter's Rewards, curbside, cardholder perks) are visible at the bottom for existing members.

## What's weak

- The countdown timer reads 00:00:00:00 — for a "Last chance" email this reads as either broken or an expired/reused urgency asset, which is worse than no timer at all.
- This is the same BOGO offer Carter's already sent on 2026-09-18; re-serving it as "last chance" two days later without a visible new deadline feels like manufactured urgency, not a real one.
- No specific expiration date/time is shown anywhere in the visible creative to back up "last chance."
- Three promotional pushes (hero BOGO, clearance, price match) plus a store opening in one email dilutes focus — a subscriber has to choose which CTA matters.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix or remove the countdown timer — a frozen 00:00:00:00 timer is actively harmful to trust; if it can't render a live countdown reliably, replace it with a static "Ends tonight at 11:59pm ET" line.
- 2. Differentiate this send from the 09-18 BOGO email — add a visible reason it's the last chance (e.g., "Ends today only") so repeat subscribers don't feel spammed with the same offer.
- 3. Trim to one primary CTA above the fold — lead with BOGO + Shop Now, then move clearance/price-match/store-opening lower or split into a follow-up send.
- 4. Add a real deadline date/time near the hero copy, not just in the subject line.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: Buy 1, get 2 free tops & bottoms`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day for BOGO — 2 free tops/bottoms`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus up to 70% off clearance, new markdowns added`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sizes NB–12/14 in stock — shop before it's gone`

1. Technical Summary
The email fails on link infrastructure and deliverability basics: every probed CTA/nav link returns HTTP 403, the plain-text MIME part is empty, and no unsubscribe or auth headers were detected in the relay capture.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All 12 material links probed return **403 Forbidden**, including the primary CTA ("Buy 1, Get 2 Free | Tops, Bottoms, & More | Shop Now"), the logo link ("Carter's"), and sub-brand nav links ("osh kosh bigosh", "little planet", "Otter Avenue", "Skiphop"). All routed through `carters.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...` (Attentive's click-tracking redirector).
- Uniform 403s across a shared redirect domain point to a redirector/link-wrapping fault (expired signature, bot/UA blocking, or link-generation error at send time) rather than isolated dead links — every tested destination is unreachable.
- Impression-tracking pixels resolve to distinct hosts (`carters.attentivemail.com/ss/o/...`, `mi.carters.com/p/up/...`, `mi.carters.com/p/rp/...`) and were also flagged as broken (403) in the QA pass, suggesting the fault may extend beyond the click-tracking redirector to the tracking/attribution layer generally.
- 63 additional (mostly footer/social/utility) links were not probed; unsubscribe-link reachability specifically was not verified since it's excluded from probing by design.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
