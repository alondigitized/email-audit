# Get 50% off Christmas PJs online for limited time only
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-27**
## Executive summary

- This is now the third Christmas-PJ email in three days from Carter's — following the 7/25 "EARLY ACCESS – 50% off Christmas faves" launch and the 7/26 "Christmas PJs now 50% off" follow-up — and the fatigue is starting to show. The creative itself is competent: a cozy red/green PJ hero, a clean "'TIS ALWAYS THE SEASON" headline, and a tidy 3x3 product grid that gives shoppers real style choices instead of one hero SKU.
- The problem is the countdown timer at the very top reads 00:00:00:00 — the exact same broken-clock bug flagged in the 7/26 send. A "limited time only" subject line sitting above a timer stuck at zero is a credibility hole, and three consecutive Christmas-in-July sends with the same visual gimmick makes the urgency feel manufactured rather than real.
- Below the fold the email sprawls into two unrelated pushes — a Spider-Man back-to-school collection and an "UP TO 70% OFF* CLEARANCE" block — which dilutes the Christmas PJ narrative the subject line promised. This reads as a decent single-offer email that got bloated into a three-offer email, with an unresolved bug undercutting the one thing it's supposed to sell urgency on.

## What's working

- Hero styling is strong — the red/green PJ flat-lay with twinkle lights and "'tis always the season" is genuinely festive and on-brand.
- The 3x3 product grid gives shoppers real pattern/style choice (stripes, prints, matching family sets) rather than one static hero image.
- "ENDS MONDAY" badge on the hero gives a concrete, credible deadline that the countdown timer fails to reinforce.
- Store locator with mapped address and "Get Directions" CTA is a nice localized touch for an omnichannel shopper.
- Footer disclosures (exclusions, T&Cs, unsubscribe) are present and legible, not buried in tiny gray-on-gray text.

## What's weak

- The countdown timer reads 00:00:00:00 — dead on arrival, same bug called out in the 7/26 send and still unfixed.
- Subject promises "Christmas PJs" but the body pivots to Spider-Man back-to-school and a separate 70%-off clearance block — three offers competing for one send.
- "Shop Now" is used as the CTA copy on all three primary buttons (PJs, Spider-Man, and implicitly clearance's category buttons) — no differentiation, no urgency language carried into the button itself.
- This is the third near-identical Christmas-PJ email in the cycle (7/25, 7/26, 7/27) — cadence is aggressive enough to read as spam-adjacent to an engaged subscriber.
- No sizing, fit, or availability information visible on any of the nine PJ products in the grid — a parent buying kids' sleepwear typically needs size range up front.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix or remove the countdown timer.** A stuck 00:00:00:00 clock is worse than no clock — it actively signals the email is templated/stale. This is the single highest-impact fix since it undermines the "limited time only" subject line directly.
- 2. **Cut the email down to one offer.** Split Spider-Man back-to-school and the 70%-off clearance block into separate sends — a subscriber who opens for "Christmas PJs" and gets three unrelated campaigns is more likely to disengage than click.
- 3. **Replace generic "Shop Now" with offer-specific CTA copy** — e.g., "Shop 50% Off PJs" — so the button reinforces the deal instead of forcing the reader back up to re-read the headline.
- 4. **Throttle cadence.** Three Christmas-PJ sends in three days is going to trigger fatigue; consolidate into one strong send with a real (working) deadline instead of re-running the same creative.
- **Subject Alt A:** `50% off Christmas PJs — ends Monday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: Christmas PJs, half off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Matching family sets, striped onesies & more — before the deadline.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Soft, festive, half price. Sizes going fast.`
