# REWARDS WEEK! 🌟 Up to 50% off* & 4X points all week long!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the third recycled version of the same blue-gradient "REWARDS WEEK" hero — it ran 8/20 as "Rewards week is here ✨," repeated with zero visual changes on 8/21 ("For our very best 💙"), and now shows up again today with the sparkle motif, gradient, and "UP TO 50% OFF + 4X POINTS" stack completely unchanged. At this point it's not a hero, it's wallpaper — a subscriber who opened any of the last three sends has already seen this exact image.
- Below the hero, the email actually does more work than its predecessors: a real 9-item "We think you'll love these" product grid, a genuinely well-shot Skip Hop play-gym feature with a "NEW!" badge, and a bold red 70% off clearance block with category buttons (Baby Girl/Boy, Toddler Girl/Boy, Girl, Boy) that gives shoppers a real next step. That mid-body structure is solid — it's just buried under a hero that no longer earns attention.
- Net: functional but fatigued. The offer math (50% off + 4X points) is decent, the layout is clean and bug-free, but the creative repetition three days running will suppress opens from anyone who's seen this campaign already.

## What's working

- The 9-product grid ("We think you'll love these...") gives concrete, browsable merchandise instead of just banner copy.
- The Skip Hop "Painted Dreams 4-in-1 Play Gym" section has a strong lifestyle photo, a clear "NEW!" badge, and a specific, benefit-led product description.
- The 70% off clearance block uses bold red-on-white contrast and category-specific buttons (Baby Girl, Toddler Boy, etc.), making the CTA genuinely actionable rather than generic.
- No render bugs — text is legible, images load cleanly, layout holds together top to bottom.
- Store locator block with address and "Get Directions" adds local relevance for anyone near that Commerce, CA location.

## What's weak

- The hero is an exact repeat of the 8/20 and 8/21 sends — same blue gradient, same sparkle icon, same "Shop Now" outline button, same copy structure. Zero creative refresh in three days.
- "Up to 50% off" and "up to 70% off clearance" both lean on the unqualified "up to" — no anchor product or price shown to make the offer feel real.
- Two competing CTAs of similar visual weight (hero "Shop Now" vs. clearance's six category buttons) dilute the single clear next action.
- The subhead "$5 reward for every $25 spent" is small and easy to miss — it's actually the most concrete number in the hero and it's underweighted.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Replace the hero art.** Three consecutive sends with the identical blue-gradient/sparkle creative is the single biggest drag here — swap in seasonal or product photography (kids in new fall styles) behind the Rewards Week messaging so it doesn't read as a re-send.
- 2. **Lead with the $5-per-$25 mechanic**, not just "4X points" — it's the only tangible number in the hero and currently the smallest text on the banner.
- 3. **Cut hero CTAs to one.** Keep "Shop Now" in the hero pointed at the sale; let the clearance block further down own its own category CTAs so the email has a clear primary vs. secondary action.
- 4. **Add a real deadline** ("Rewards Week ends Sunday") somewhere near the hero — nothing in the render signals urgency beyond the name of the promotion.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Rewards Week: $5 back for every $25 you spend`
- **Subject Alt B:** `4X points + up to 50% off — ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack points on new fall arrivals, clearance, and Skip Hop favorites.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New markdowns just added to up to 70% off clearance.`
