# Homepage snapshot · www.carters.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay, first thing I see is a promo code banner right at the top and a big bold discount hero — Carter's is leading with savings energy, which honestly is *exactly* how you get me. The lifestyle shot peeking in below the fold with a young mom and her toddler feels like they're speaking directly to me. This is a summer-sale push and they're not being shy about it.

## What's working

- **Double-stacked discount math** — "40% OFF + EXTRA 20% with SUMMER20" is the kind of deal that makes a coupon-stacker like me do the mental arithmetic immediately. I felt it.
- **Promo code strip at the very top** — showing SUMMER20 in the announcement bar means I don't have to dig for it at checkout. That alone reduces my abandonment anxiety.
- **Mom+toddler lifestyle photo** — the partial image I can see at the bottom of the viewport is warm, real-feeling, and looks like *me* with my kid, not a stock-photo stranger.
- **Search bar is prominent** — right below the nav, full-width, easy to tap. As someone who types "footed pajamas 18M" directly, I appreciate that it's not hidden.
- **Clean mobile layout** — no overlapping elements, text is legible at thumb-scroll speed. Carter's app/mobile experience historically gets this right and it shows here.

## What's weak

- **"Shop Now" CTA is too generic** — I have no idea if tapping it takes me to toddler sale, baby sale, or the whole sitewide mix. For someone who is laser-focused on sizing up her 18-month-old, I want "Shop Toddler Sale" or at minimum a hint.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — I always calculate whether my cart hits the free-ship minimum. That number should be above the fold. It's not.
- **No loyalty / Rewarding Moments callout** — I earn points on every Carter's purchase and I would 100% engage with a "You have X points to redeem" or "Members get extra access" hook if it were here. It's absent.
- **No urgency signal** — the offer is generous but there's no "ends Sunday" or countdown. For a deal-stacker like me, urgency is what turns browse into buy *right now* vs. "I'll come back."
- **The asterisk on 40% OFF*** — I notice it, and I don't know what it excludes without scrolling. A tiny "excl. clearance" inline would make the offer feel less bait-y.

## Recommendations

- **Rename the CTA to "Shop Toddler & Baby Sale"** — or at minimum surface two CTAs split by age segment. One tap to the right section matters enormously on mobile when I'm doing this one-handed during nap time.
- **Add free-shipping threshold to the promo banner** — change the strip to "Code SUMMER20 = extra 20% off* select styles + free shipping over $35" so the math clicks immediately.
- **Surface a Rewarding Moments balance or member-exclusive badge** — even a generic "Members earn double points this weekend" callout in the hero area would give loyal shoppers a reason to log in *before* they shop.
- **Add a soft urgency line under the CTA** — something like "Sale ends Sunday" right below the "Shop Now" button costs nothing to add and drives the "add to cart now" decision I'm always on the edge of.
