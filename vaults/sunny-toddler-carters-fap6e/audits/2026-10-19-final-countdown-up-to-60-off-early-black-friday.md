# FINAL COUNTDOWN: Up to 60% off Early Black Friday 🚨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth Black Friday preview send from Carter's in about a week, and it's the first with a real countdown timer. The "FINAL COUNTDOWN" subject promises a deadline, and the timer at the top (00 days, 13 hours, 33 minutes, 49 seconds) backs it up. That's a credible urgency device. The rest is a long, stacked layout: the black "preview / UP TO 60% OFF*" hero over a lime flat-lay, a "Just For You" grid, a second black banner for "$10 OFF* OUTERWEAR" with puffer jackets, a red "UP TO 70% OFF* CLEARANCE" block, and a store-locator strip.
- The email is clear and readable, and the outerwear section is the strongest piece. "$10 off" on a named product (The Packable Puffer) is much more concrete than another "up to 60%." The weakness is that the creative is almost the same as the Oct 16 and Oct 17 sends, so a subscriber has seen the top banner several times. The "Just For You" grid is also weak. It shows unlabeled pants and bodysuits with no prices, names or visible links.
- Verdict: a competent, urgent send that reads like part of a stale series. The countdown helps it more than anything else, and the offer is still vague at the top.

## What's working

- The countdown timer sits right under the logo, gives a visible deadline, and matches the subject's urgency.
- The black "UP TO 60% OFF*" hero has strong contrast and is readable in one second.
- The "$10 OFF* OUTERWEAR" banner names a product, The Packable Puffer, and shows four jackets in good colors. It is a concrete, seasonally right offer.
- The red 70% clearance block with segment buttons (Baby Girl, Toddler Girl, Girl, Baby Boy, Toddler Boy, Boy) makes navigation easy.
- The footer perks row (curbside, cardholder perks, in-app, Rewards+) shows loyalty benefits without cluttering the body.

## What's weak

- The top creative is nearly identical to the Oct 16 "Up to 60% off Black Friday preview ✔️" send. Repetition makes the urgency feel manufactured.
- "Up to 60%" with an asterisk is vague, and the disclaimers sit far down the page. The 70% clearance block can read as a conflicting offer.
- The "Just For You" grid has no prices, product names or labels. It is a wall of denim, joggers and white bodysuits with nothing to anchor a click.
- The email is very long, with four large blocks before the footer. The outerwear offer, the most specific one, sits below the fold.
- No reviews or social proof appear anywhere.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the most concrete offer. Put the $10 off Packable Puffer, or the $8 fleece PJs from Oct 17, in the top hero instead of the generic 60% banner.
- 2. Add price tags and product names to the "Just For You" tiles. Cut it to 3–4 items, or drop it.
- 3. Vary the creative between sends. Rotate heroes by category and make the countdown the one constant.
- 4. Tie the countdown to a plainly stated end event ("preview ends tonight at midnight") so the urgency is explicit.
- 5. Move the key disclaimers closer to the offer or shorten them.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: $10 off puffers + up to 60% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `⏰ 13 hours left: early Black Friday prices`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Packable Puffers are $10 off, plus clearance up to 70% off. Preview pricing ends at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Bodysuits, jeans, joggers and outerwear, all marked down before the big day.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is built on a standard table layout with `lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag, and MSO conditionals. Every one of the 12 probed links returned 403, and the plain-text part is empty. The 403s most likely come from the click-tracker blocking the scanner, so they are not confirmed user-facing breakage. The source was truncated and header capture is limited by the relay, so several checks could not be completed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links returned 403.** That covers the logo ("Carter's"), the brand links ("osh kosh bigosh", "little planet", "Otter Avenue", "Skiphop"), the hero ("Black Friday preview | Up To 60% Off*") and 6 product image links ("Image of Boys Dark Wash Relaxed-Fit Classic Jeans", etc.). All sit on `https://carters.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...`.
  - A 100% failure rate across unrelated destinations points to bot or scanner blocking by the Attentive redirector, not 12 separate broken links. The final landing pages were never resolved, so link health is **unverified**, not confirmed broken.
- **Opaque redirect URLs.** Each click URL is a single ~600–800 character token path with no readable destination, so UTMs and landing pages can't be audited from the source.
- **Two tracking stacks in one message.**
  - Attentive: open pixel `carters.attentivemail.com/ss/o/.../ho.gif` plus the `carters.e.attentivemail.com` click domain.
  - A second system: `mi.carters.com/p/up/745a17bbb73445ad/o.gif?mi_u=1150794179&mi_ecmp=10_19_2026_CAR_MON_AM_LARGEAUDIENCE`, plus `mi.carters.com/p/rp/264477f43986d8bb.png?mi_u=...`.
  - This means two open pixels. The `mi_u` parameter is a numeric per-recipient ID in a URL query string.
- **Skipped links.** 42 links were not probed, including footer and social links. Their health is unknown.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **`<title></title>` is empty** in `<head>`. This is minor, but it affects web-view and some client tab titles.
- **Missing alt text on four content-sized images.** These are the `d3ptznaucccg76.cloudfront.net/MzczMjo6U0VSVklDRVNfQkFOTkVSXzAx…` through `…BNDRfdjM…` banners (`SERVICES_BANNER_01`–`04`). If they carry text or links, that content is invisible with images off or in a screen reader.
- **`mi.carters.com/.../264477f43986d8bb.png`** is flagged for missing alt. It appears to be a tracking or reporting asset (`/p/rp/`). If it is a 1×1 image, `alt=""` is the correct fix.
- **False positive on `ho.gif`.** The source shows `alt=""` on it, which is correct for a tracking pixel. The QA warning is wrong here. `o.gif` on `mi.carters.com` may be the same case, but its markup was truncated.
- **Web font fallback.** `League Spartan` loads through `<link>` inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, which many clients ignore. I couldn't confirm a fallback stack in the truncated body.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The source was truncated, so the body and footer are only partly covered.

## 5. Compliance
- **No plain-text alternative.** QA reports "Text version is 0 chars", a **FAIL**. This could be a relay capture artifact, but if the real message is HTML-only, it hurts spam scoring and accessibility.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** These are warnings and may only mean AgentMail doesn't preserve the headers. I can't confirm either way, but Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both.
- **`Authentication-Results` absent**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `marketing.carters.com`. Check the raw headers before concluding anything.
- **Footer not verifiable.** The unsubscribe link and physical address are past the truncation point. None of the 12 failed links is labeled as an unsubscribe link, so the unsubscribe URL's status is unknown.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not verifiable. Because the redirects returned 403, no landing pages or UTM parameters were observed. The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=10_19_2026_CAR_MON_AM_LARGEAUDIENCE`, which is a tracking parameter and not evidence of landing page alignment.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the 12 links with a browser-like User-Agent, following redirects, or resolve them through the ESP's link-preview feature. Until then, treat the 403s as inconclusive.
2. Pull the raw message headers (not the relay copy) and confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass.
3. Verify the multipart text part in the original send. If it really is empty, add a plain-text version.
4. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four `SERVICES_BANNER` images, and set `alt=""` on the `mi.carters.com` pixels.
5. Fill in `<title>`.
6. Fix the QA checker so it doesn't flag `alt=""` as missing, and so it handles bot-blocked 403s on known ESP click domains as "unverified" instead of "FAIL".
