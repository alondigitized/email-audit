# Prepping early? 👀 Up to 40% off* back-to-school looks
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-19**
## Executive summary

- Carter's is doubling down on a creative week that's starting to show wear. The OshKosh B'Gosh "next class of Icons" hero launched with real punch in the July 14 "For the first day fit ✅" send — confident photography, strong brand moment. Here it is again five days later, rewrapped with a new subject line. The BTS angle holds, but the email underneath the hero tells a different story: a product grid of baby rompers and onesies, a "Latest New Baby Essentials" section with its own 25%-off ask, and — inevitably — the dashed-red-border $3.99 clearance flash module that has appeared in every Carter's send since July 15. This single email is running three separate discount messages for three unrelated shopping moments.
- The subject is actually the strongest Carter's has written this week. "Prepping early? 👀" is a genuine question-hook that addresses the reader rather than shouting at them, and pairing it with the 40%-off anchor gives the open a real reward. That goodwill evaporates fast once you're inside: recycled hero, off-brief baby content, and a clearance wallpaper module that has lost all urgency through pure repetition.
- Carter's has a good BTS campaign lurking inside a bloated send. The problem is editorial discipline — or the lack of it.

## What's working

- **The "Prepping early?" subject hook** is the most distinctive opening Carter's has used this week — the question format actually addresses the reader's mindset rather than announcing a sale.
- **OshKosh hero photography** remains visually strong: confident kids, aspirational first-day-of-school framing, clean typography on the "Icons" lockup.
- **40% off is front and center** in the hero copy and isn't buried — the value is immediately legible.
- **Shop Now CTA** is clearly styled as a button and well-placed at the hero level.
- **No render bugs** — the email loads cleanly with no broken images or layout issues.

## What's weak

- **Hero is recycled from July 14** — a subscriber who opened "For the first day fit ✅" five days ago is looking at the same photograph with a different subject line.
- **Three competing discounts** (40% off BTS, 25% off baby essentials, $3.99 clearance) create message chaos and make it impossible to know what Carter's actually wants you to buy.
- **Baby essentials section has zero BTS relevance** — "prepping early for school" doesn't include infant onesies; this belongs in a separate segmented send.
- **The clearance flash module** ($3.99, dashed red border) is now in its fifth consecutive Carter's email in five days — it has zero urgency and reads as permanent decoration.
- **No deadline anywhere** — three separate offers and not one has a "through Sunday" or "ends Monday" anchor to create real purchase urgency.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Drop the clearance flash module entirely from BTS-framed emails.** The $3.99 module undercuts the 40%-off BTS premium positioning. Clearance gets its own standalone sends — it doesn't ride shotgun on aspirational back-to-school moments.
- 2. **Retire the July 14 OshKosh hero.** Strong creative needs rest. Swap in a different BTS angle — backpacks, classroom-ready layering, name-label personalization — to signal a new editorial chapter.
- 3. **Kill the baby essentials section.** Send it separately to parent-of-infant segments. Its presence here actively confuses the BTS narrative.
- 4. **Add a credible deadline to the 40% off offer.** "Through Sunday" or "ends July 21" would instantly lift the click-urgency ceiling.
- 5. **Tighten to a single offer with a single CTA.** One send, one shopping moment, one discount. Three offers = no offer.

- **Subject Alt B:** `BTS looks from 40% off — grab yours before they go`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `OshKosh icons, Carter's classics — this week only`

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Open pixel subscriber ID appears hardcoded:**
```
https://mi.carters.com/p/up/745a17bbb73445ad/o.gif?mi_u=1150794179&mi_ecmp=07_19_2026_CAR_SUN_STO_ENGAGERS
```
`mi_u=1150794179` is a raw integer in the source — not a merge token syntax like `%%subscriber_id%%` or `{{mi_u}}`. ### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Empty `<title>` tag:**
```html
<title></title>
```
Some clients (Samsung Mail, older Android webviews) display the title tag in the tab or thread header. An empty title degrades that surface.

**Google Fonts will not load in most clients:**
```html
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=League+Spartan:wght@400;500;600;700&display=swap" ...>
```
Gmail (both web and app), Outlook (all versions), and Yahoo block external CSS. The `<!--[if !mso]>` guard protects Outlook specifically, but Gmail and Yahoo will fall through to the stack fallback (`Arial, Helvetica Neue, Helvetica, sans-serif`). This is expected behavior for email — no rendering break — but confirm the fallback stack visually matches intended layout at all responsive widths.

**Open pixel `alt` attribute malformed:**
```html
<img src="https://mi.carters.com/.../o.gif" ... alt>
```
`alt` with no value is valid HTML5 but `alt=""` is the correct semantic form for decorative/tracking images. Some strict parsers and accessibility scanners will flag this.

**Preheader padding uses soft hyphens (U+00AD):**
```
­ ­ ­ ­ ...
```
The Combining Grapheme Joiner (`U+034F`) characters are standard and safe. The interleaved `­` (soft hyphen) padding is functional but flagged as suspicious by some aggressive spam heuristics (SpamAssassin `SOFT_HYPHENS` rule). Not a blocking issue at major ISPs today.
