# Here for every first 🫶
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-01**

- Carter's opens June with a brand-heritage send built around a tender newborn photo and "OVER 160 YEARS OF KNOW-HOW" headline — a legitimate emotional lane. The problem is the email can't commit to the bit. Two scrolls down, the warm brand-story mood shatters and a bright red "UP TO 70% OFF* CLEARANCE" banner takes over. The two halves actively undercut each other.
- The May 27 "Just dropped! Our latest baby must-haves" send scored 9/10 precisely because it went fully editorial with zero discount language. The May 28 "UP TO 50% OFF SUMMER" worked because it committed to the discount. This send hedges both bets and lands between them. ## What's working

- Newborn hero photo is genuinely warm — correct demographic signal, executed well.
- "OVER 160 YEARS OF KNOW-HOW" is a rare heritage trust anchor for Carter's; credible and distinct from their usual discount blasts.
- Price-category tiles ("Tees from $5," "Shorts from $4," "PJs from $9") are specific and conversion-friendly — best UX section in the email.
- 70% clearance is the strongest offer in the recent send history; the offer itself is real.
- Clean render — no broken images, no layout bugs, no overlapping elements.

## What's weak

- Subject delivers zero offer signal after five straight days of promotional Carter's email — softest hook of the week.
- 70% clearance is buried below the fold behind a brand-story hero that doesn't mention a discount; their best lever is invisible until you scroll.
- Emotional heritage tone and blowout-sale clearance banner are tonally incompatible — the email reads like two different briefs got merged at deadline.
- "Shop Now" hero CTA is completely generic and disconnected from any visible offer — a subscriber who doesn't scroll has no reason to click.
- No urgency signal anywhere — the clearance has no deadline, no countdown, no "ends Sunday" to create pressure.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the 70% clearance in the hero and subject.** The clearance is the most compelling thing in this email; don't hide it three sections down behind a brand story.
- 2. **Split the brand-story and clearance into separate sends.** The May 27 editorial proved Carter's can do zero-offer emotional email with great results — but only when it's the whole send, not a preamble to a blowout banner.
- 3. **Add a deadline to the clearance.** "Ends Sunday" or "This week only" would convert fence-sitters. Right now the clearance reads as permanent markdown, which reduces urgency.
- 4. **Replace "Shop Now" with an offer-specific CTA** — "Shop 70% Off Clearance" or "Shop Baby from $5" ties the button to visible value on the same screen.
- 5. **Rewrite the subject to surface the price:**
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 70% off — baby clearance starts now`

- **Preheader Alt A:** `160 years of know-how, prices you'll love this summer`

## Full review
## 1. Overview

Carter's opens June with a brand-heritage send built around a tender newborn photo and "OVER 160 YEARS OF KNOW-HOW" headline — a legitimate emotional lane. The problem is the email can't commit to the bit. Two scrolls down, the warm brand-story mood shatters and a bright red "UP TO 70% OFF* CLEARANCE" banner takes over. - Emotional heritage tone and blowout-sale clearance banner are tonally incompatible — the email reads like two different briefs got merged at deadline.

5. **Rewrite the subject to surface the price:**
   - **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 70% off — baby clearance starts now`
