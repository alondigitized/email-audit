# $2.99+ clearance IS ON 🔥
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-03**
## Executive summary

- Carter's sends its eleventh email in eleven days and names it like it's the opening salvo: "$2.99+ clearance IS ON 🔥." The creative energy of "IS ON" implies an event just launched — but subscribers have been receiving this same clearance story since June 23. The fire emoji and exclamatory framing cannot manufacture fresh urgency the inbox has spent ten consecutive sends learning to skip.
- Mechanically, this email holds up. The coral hero is clean, the $2.99 anchor is immediately readable, and "ENDS IN 6 DAYS" gives a real deadline. The "PICKS FOR YOU" grid adds browseable depth, and the loyalty block at the footer reinforces member value. Nothing is broken.
- But this is a strategically exhausted send. The creative is structurally indistinguishable from the July 1 "CLEARANCE FROM $2.99 🚨🚨" and the June 29 "100s of styles from $2.99 👀" — same price anchor, same product grid, same CTA hierarchy. On July 3 — the eve of July 4th weekend — there is an obvious seasonal hook sitting completely unused. This email would have scored a 9 as send three; as send eleven, the only people clicking are those who missed the last ten.

## What's working

- The $2.99 hero is legible immediately — price anchor dominates without competition from secondary copy
- "ENDS IN 6 DAYS" deadline is credible and visible in the first viewport without scrolling
- The "PICKS FOR YOU" thumbnail grid gives the body real merchandise depth and browsability
- Carter's loyalty/rewards section in the footer is cleanly visible, reinforcing member value for enrolled shoppers
- Render is clean throughout — no broken images, no layout issues, no overlapping text

## What's weak

- "IS ON" implies launch energy that this event lost around day four — after ten prior sends, the framing reads as desperation
- "PICKS FOR YOU" looks like a generic assortment grid, not individualized recommendations — 12 unrelated baby basics don't signal personalization
- "Shop It" CTA under the Rompers block is vague compared to "Shop Bodysuits" and "Shop Toddler," creating an inconsistent hierarchy
- July 3rd is the single most natural hook of the campaign's entire run (July 4th weekend, summer stocking up, holiday proximity) — this email ignores it entirely
- No new creative element separates this from the July 1 or June 29 sends; a subscriber who opened either of those has no new reason to act today

## Recommendations

- 1. **Introduce a July 4th angle in the hero.** "Stock up before the long weekend" or "Clearance closes July 9 — shop before the BBQ" turns "ENDS IN 6 DAYS" from an abstract countdown into a tangible lifestyle moment. This is the highest-leverage change available and requires zero new creative — just a copy swap on the hero.
- 2. **One specific hero product above the grid.** A single "$2.99 snap bodysuit — our most-stocked clearance item" card with a link to that PDP creates a reason to click that the category grid cannot. Right now the only entry point is "Shop Now" to the whole clearance landing page.
- 3. **Fix CTA copy consistency.** "Shop It" → "Shop Rompers" to mirror the naming pattern of the flanking blocks.
- 4. **Rest or rotate the $2.99 creative.** If the campaign must continue, introduce a new frame — a category spotlight, loyalty-member early access, or "new styles added" hook — rather than re-running the same coral hero for a twelfth time.
- **Subject Alt A:** `July 4th weekend: Carter's clearance from $2.99`
- **Subject Alt B:** `6 days left — bodysuits, rompers, shorts from $2.99`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stock up before the holiday weekend. Clearance ends July 9.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Hundreds of summer styles marked down — snap bodysuits from $2.99, rompers from $4`
