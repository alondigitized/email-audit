
Gmail, Outlook, and Apple Mail on iOS strip external `<link>` stylesheets. `League Spartan` will not render in these clients. Confirm a safe fallback font stack is defined inline (e.g., `font-family: 'League Spartan', Arial, sans-serif`) on all text elements — not just in the `<link>` tag.

