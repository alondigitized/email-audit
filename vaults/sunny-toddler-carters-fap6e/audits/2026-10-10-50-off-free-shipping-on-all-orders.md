# 50% off + FREE shipping on ALL orders 🌟
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-10**
## Executive summary

- This is the third pass at the long-weekend magenta starburst, after 10-08 "LIMITED TIME: 50% off PJs & more ✨" and 10-09 "Ends soon ‼️ 50% off PJs, sets & more". The new news is free shipping on all orders, which is a real sweetener. The subject line leads with it, but the hero shrinks it to a small line under the offer. The hero itself is clean: "50% OFF*" dominates and there is one Shop Now button.
- The body undercuts the hero. The 50% off is for "PJs, sets & more", but the "We think you'll love these" grid is nine jeans, joggers and onesies, with no pajamas. The grid is also nine uniform tiles with no prices or labels. A $2.99+ clearance block and a store-opening module pile on after that. The email works as a sale reminder but gives the shopper no reason to act today.
- Verdict: a competent, recognizable send. It is a repeat in a week that has already had four LAYERUP sends and two PJ sends, and the free-shipping hook is under-used.

## What's working

- The hero hierarchy is clear. "50% OFF*" is the largest element, with the Shop Now button directly below.
- Free shipping is added to the offer as a separate benefit, and it was not in the 10-08 or 10-09 sends.
- "Long weekend stock-up" ties the email to the current moment.
- The Shop It All row gives starting prices (Tees from $6, Licensed from $10, Sets from $11).
- The $2.99+ clearance block has gender and age shortcut buttons (Baby Girl, Toddler Boy and so on), which help navigation.
- No render bugs. Images load and nothing overlaps.

## What's weak

- The product grid doesn't match the offer. The hero promotes PJs and sets, but the grid is jeans, joggers and basics.
- The grid tiles have no prices, names or discount flags, so there is nothing to click for.
- "FREE shipping on ALL orders" in the subject sits next to an asterisked 50% off with fine print saying "Select Styles". Both claims are broader than the email supports.
- There is no deadline. "Long weekend" implies urgency, but no end date or "Ends Monday" appears, even though yesterday's subject said "Ends soon".
- Four stacked modules (hero, grid, clearance, store opening) compete for attention. The Now open near you! block looks like it came from a different campaign.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the grid match the hero. Fill it with the 50% off PJs and sets, and show a crossed-out price and sale price on each tile.
- 2. Turn free shipping into a banner or a second line in the hero, and give it a threshold or an end date so it reads as credible.
- 3. Add a real deadline to the hero, such as "Ends Monday 11:59 PT".
- 4. Change the CTA from "Shop Now" to "Shop 50% off PJs".
- 5. Cut the store-opening module, or send it only to a local segment.
- 6. Vary the creative. This is the third send with the same starburst.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free shipping + 50% off PJs, thru Monday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Long weekend stock-up: 50% off PJs & sets`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus free shipping on every order. Sale ends Monday night.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Matching sets from $11, tees from $6. Stock up before it's over.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML has sound basics: `lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag, a mobile media query, and a hidden preheader. The 0-char plain-text part is a confirmed defect. The 12 "broken link" results are most likely a probe artifact (see section 2), and the header-based compliance checks are inconclusive because of the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links returned 403.** Every one is an Attentive click-tracking redirect on `carters.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...`. These covered the logo ("Carter's"), brand links (Osh Kosh, Little Planet, Otter Avenue, Skiphop), the hero banner, and the product tiles ("Boys Dark Wash Relaxed-Fit Classic Jeans", "Boys French Terry Joggers - Black", and others).
- **A 100% failure rate on one domain points to bot-blocking of the QA fetcher, not 12 separate dead links.** It is not proof that the links work for real recipients. Destinations were not resolved, so link health is unverified.
- **Probe coverage was low.** 12 links were probed and 39 skipped, so about 76% of links are untested. The 16% pass rate is therefore not meaningful as a deliverability signal.
- **The links hide their destinations.** Every link is wrapped in a long opaque redirect, so no final URL can be read from the source.
- **Tracking pixels:** `carters.attentivemail.com/ss/o/.../ho.gif` (1x1, hidden, inside a `display:none` wrapper). `mi.carters.com/p/up/.../o.gif` and `mi.carters.com/p/rp/264477f43986d8bb.png` carry `mi_u` and `mi_ecmp` parameters. This is standard open and engagement tracking, not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **The plain-text part is missing (0 chars).** This is a confirmed defect. It hurts spam scoring and text-only clients.
- **`<title></title>` is empty.** This is a minor issue in webmail "view in browser" contexts.
- **Four banners lack alt text:** `d3ptznaucccg76.cloudfront.net/MzczMjo6U0VSVklDRVNfQkFOTkVSXzAx...png` through `...zA0X3Yz...png` (SERVICES_BANNER_01 to 04). Alt is absent, not just empty, so screen readers will read the file name or skip the image. This is a real issue if the banners carry text or links.
- **The `ho.gif` flag is a false positive.** The source shows `alt=""` on it, which is correct for a decorative pixel. `o.gif` and `264477f43986d8bb.png` are probably the same kind of pixel, but their markup was truncated, so I couldn't check them.
- **Product images do have alt text** (for example "Image of Boys French Terry Joggers - Black"). The "Image of" prefix is redundant but harmless.
- **Positives:** the MSO conditionals and `mso-hide` are present, and the preheader is hidden properly with padding characters.
- **Not verifiable:** the Google Fonts `<link>` (League Spartan) is wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` so Outlook ignores it. The font-family fallback stack was truncated, so I couldn't check it.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) are visible in the supplied source. The Movable Ink parameter `mi_u=1150794179` is a populated numeric user ID. `mi_ecmp=10_10_2026_CAR_SAT_STO_LARGEAUDIENCE` is a populated campaign ID. The truncated source limits how far I can confirm this.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) are not found.** The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is inconclusive. Confirm against the raw headers in a direct-to-Gmail or Outlook test.
- **Authentication-Results is not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown.** The sender is `carters.email@marketing.carters.com` with links on `attentivemail.com`. Alignment is unverified.
- **The footer was truncated.** I couldn't confirm the in-body unsubscribe link or the physical postal address. The QA note says an unsubscribe link is always probed, and none appears among the failures. That suggests one exists, but I couldn't see it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All destinations sit behind Attentive redirects that returned 403 to the probe, so I couldn't read the final URLs, UTM parameters, or landing-page match. Apart from Attentive's `ss/o` pixel, the only campaign tagging visible is Movable Ink's `mi_ecmp` on the tracking pixels, and that isn't a UTM.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a plain-text part.** This is confirmed and high priority.
2. **Add alt text to the four `cloudfront.net` service banners.** Describe their content, or use `alt=""` if they are purely decorative.
3. **Re-test the links with a real browser User-Agent, or follow the redirects manually.** Confirm the 403s are bot-blocking before logging them as broken, and resolve the final URLs to check UTMs and landing pages.
4. **Capture the raw message headers** (List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, Authentication-Results) from a direct send instead of the relay.
5. **Fill in `<title>`.** Low priority.
6. **Treat the "missing alt" flags on tracking pixels as noise.** Suppress them in the QA check by detecting 1x1 or hidden images.
