# STOCK UP: BOG2 🆓
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- Carter's returns to the BUY 1, GET 2 FREE well just four days after the May 16 send that earned a 9/10 for the same mechanic. The structural bones are solid — a lifestyle hero, a category grid, a character-drop moment, and the clearance anchor all stack reasonably well. But this email is carrying three distinct offers (BOGO tops/bottoms, characters from $8, $2.99 clearance) and none of them get enough room to breathe or convert. The SUMMER STOCK UP grid — six cute products — has no prices and no CTAs, which is the single biggest waste of real estate in the email.
- The subject "STOCK UP: BOG2 🆓" is the weakest link. BOG2 is not a recognized acronym outside Carter's internal creative team; BOGO is the standard shorthand, and even that needs a word to land. A subscriber scanning their inbox at 7am is going to read "BOG2" as noise and skip past it, especially after six consecutive days of Carter's hitting their inbox.
- The email is competent and renders cleanly, but it's treading water on an offer that already had its moment. If BOGO is returning, it needs a reason — new styles, extended sizes, a shorter window — to justify the repeat.

## What's working

- **BOGO hero copy is clear** — "BUY 1, GET 2 FREE* TOPS & BOTTOMS" reads instantly; no parsing required once you're in the email.
- **Character drop section is a smart addition** — "HERE WE GO!" tee with the big-screen characters angle adds novelty and a fresh entry point at a $8 price floor.
- **$2.99 clearance row holds its value** — the red banner with segmented category buttons (Baby Girl / Toddler Girl / Girl / Baby Boy / Toddler Boy / Boy) is efficient and action-oriented; the May 17 clearance email proved this anchor converts.
- **Clean render** — no broken images, no overlapping text, no lorem ipsum; the email is visually polished.

## What's weak

- **BOG2 in the subject is opaque** — it is not a standard acronym and will read as a typo or junk code to most subscribers; BOGO is already a stretch for some audiences.
- **SUMMER STOCK UP grid has zero prices and zero CTAs** — six products displayed with no price anchors and no way to tap through; this section builds desire and then leaves the subscriber nowhere to go.
- **No urgency anywhere in the email** — the BOGO offer has no deadline, no countdown, no "ends Sunday" hook; the May 14 email with a live countdown timer shows Carter's knows how to add urgency when they want to.
- **Hero lifestyle image delays the offer** — the "COOL PARTY" kid tee photo dominates above the fold and the BOGO headline sits below it; on mobile, the offer likely requires a scroll before it's visible.
- **BOGO repeat without a differentiator** — the May 16 send already ran this exact offer; there's no "new styles added," "extended sizes," or shortened window to give a subscriber who passed last time a reason to act now.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the subject line first** — replace BOG2 with explicit language. "STOCK UP: BOG2 🆓" reads as a malformed coupon code; rewrite so the mechanic is instantly legible.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Buy 1, get 2 FREE — tops & bottoms 🆓`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Stock up: 3 pieces, pay for 1 👕`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New summer styles added. Mix & match tops and bottoms — offer won't last.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The easy styles that last — grab 3 for the price of 1, this week only.`
- 2. **Add a deadline to the BOGO** — even "Ends Sunday" in the hero or a small countdown banner above the fold would sharply increase urgency; this offer has been live since May 16 with no stated end date.
- 3. **Price the SUMMER STOCK UP grid** — add "$X and up" or individual price badges to each product tile; six products shown with no price is six missed conversion opportunities.
- 4. **Rewrite "Shop Now" to match the offer** — change to "Shop BOGO Tops & Bottoms" so the CTA reinforces the mechanic right at the decision point.
- 5. **Acknowledge the repeat** — a single line like "New styles, same deal: buy 1, get 2 free" tells returning subscribers this isn't the same old send and rewards the ones who came back.
