# FINAL HOURS - Up to 50% off + extra 20% online ✨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-28**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth COZY20 push in five days, after "COZY FLASH 🚨" on 10/24, "$9+ Cozy PJs 🧸" on 10/25, the 10/26 code send and "ONLINE ONLY !!" on 10/27. It's also the second Carter's send today, after "Up to 50% off clearance ends today ‼️". The hero is the same sherpa-texture "cozy flash, up to 50% OFF*, + EXTRA 20% OFF, code COZY20" block, and "FINAL HOURS" is the only new element. The offer and code are clear, but the creative is stale.
- Below the hero the email loses focus. A nine-tile grid of unlabeled basics (white bodysuits, joggers, jeans) has no prices, names or captions. Then a red "UP TO 75% OFF* LAST-CHANCE CLEARANCE" block competes with the hero. The footer fine print says the 20% code excludes clearance, so a skimmer could think the two offers stack when they don't. A gift guide section follows. That's four messages in one scroll.
- Verdict: a competent, on-brand last-call send, but one that drifts from its own deadline. Urgency lives in the subject line, and the body says "Ends Wednesday" with no countdown or "tonight" language. It's a solid 6–7, held back by fatigue and an unfocused body.

## What's working

- The hero hierarchy is strong. "50% OFF*" is the biggest element, the green "+ EXTRA 20% OFF" band sits below it, and the code COZY20 is stated right there.
- The "Shop Now" outlined button is visible and sits directly under the offer.
- The Carter's logo and sub-brand strip (OshKosh, little planet, Otter Avenue, Skip Hop) make the sender instantly recognizable.
- The red 75% clearance block is high-contrast, and the Baby/Toddler/Girl/Boy buttons are a quick way to get to the right size range.
- The footer perks row (curbside pickup, cardholder perks, in-app points, Rewards) shows loyalty benefits.

## What's weak

- The "We think you'll love these" grid has no prices, product names or sale callouts. It's nine near-identical basics on grey tiles, and nothing tells you why to click.
- Two competing offers sit in one scroll: "50% + extra 20%" and "75% off clearance". The code excludes clearance, which only shows up in the fine print.
- "FINAL HOURS" in the subject isn't carried into the body. The hero still says "Ends Wednesday" with no time or countdown, so the urgency feels thin.
- The gift guide block is a third message that dilutes the last-call push, and it has no offer attached.
- The hero is nearly identical to the 10/24–10/27 sends, so it gives no new reason to act.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the body match the subject. Replace "Ends Wednesday" with "Ends tonight at midnight ET". Add a countdown timer or a "Today only" tag above the offer.
- 2. Cut the gift guide and the generic product grid from a last-call send. Replace the grid with 3–4 hero products that show price, a "was/now" and the code-applied price, such as "$12 → $9.60 with COZY20".
- 3. Separate the offers. Move the 75% clearance block into a clearly labeled "no code needed" strip, and add "Code excluded on clearance" next to it, so nobody feels misled at checkout.
- 4. Rotate the creative. After five days of the same sherpa hero, use a lifestyle shot of kids in the pajamas or outerwear, or at least change the background.
- 5. Change the CTA from "Shop Now" to something specific, such as "Shop cozy PJs" or "Shop 50% off + 20%".
- 6. Write a real preheader that adds new information.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: extra 20% off w/ COZY20`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call for cozy PJs + 50% off 🧸`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code COZY20 by midnight ET. Clearance is up to 75% off with no code.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `PJs from $9, sweaters, jeans and more. Your extra 20% disappears tonight.`
