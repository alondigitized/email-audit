# Ends soon ‼️ 50% off PJs, sets & more
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- This is the 10-08 "LIMITED TIME: 50% off PJs & more" send with a new subject line. The hero is the same magenta starburst: "long weekend stock-up / 50% OFF* / PJs, sets & more" with a Shop Now button. Only "Ends soon ‼️" is new, so the email makes an urgency claim the body never backs up. There's no countdown, no end date and no deadline anywhere in the render.
- The body is weaker than the hero. The "We think you'll love these…" grid has nine tiles. It shows jeans, white bodysuits, black and navy joggers, and one footed sleeper. Only the sleeper is a PJ, so the grid contradicts the 50% off PJs headline. There are no prices, product names or per-item links I can see. Below the grid, BOGO "Buy 1, Get 2 FREE*", "$2.99+ Clearance" and a store-opening module compete with the hero. The email ends up as a generic promo roundup.
- Judgment: a competent but lazy resend. It follows a week of near-identical sends (LAYERUP four days in a row, then 50% PJs yesterday). A subscriber would see it as the same offer again with louder punctuation. The gap between "ends soon" and a hero with no deadline is the main weakness.

## What's working

- The hero is clean and the 50% OFF* is the clear focal point, with a visible Shop Now button.
- The "long weekend stock-up" framing is seasonal and gives a reason to buy now.
- The BOGO block in teal is visually distinct and easy to scan.
- The clearance block has category buttons (Baby Girl, Toddler Girl, Girl, Baby Boy, Toddler Boy, Boy), which helps shoppers get to their size.
- The footer has a loyalty strip: curbside, cardholder perks, app points and Rewards.

## What's weak

- "Ends soon" has no support in the body. There's no date, countdown or "ends Monday".
- The product grid doesn't match the PJ offer. Eight of nine tiles are jeans, joggers or bodysuits.
- The grid tiles show no prices, names or discount badges, so there's no sign of the 50% saving.
- There are four competing offers: 50% off, BOGO, $2.99+ clearance and store-opening. The hero loses focus.
- The "Now open near you" Commerce, CA module is a store ad in a promo email, and it adds clutter. The footer disclaimers are long and dense.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real deadline in the hero, such as "Ends Monday 11:59pm PT", or use a countdown timer. This would make the subject line honest.
- 2. Fill the grid with PJs from the 50% offer, and add "Now $X" prices and a "50% off" badge on each tile.
- 3. Drop one of BOGO or clearance, or move them below the fold, so there is one hero offer.
- 4. Change the CTA from "Shop Now" to "Shop 50% off PJs".
- 5. Don't resend the same hero on consecutive days. Rotate the creative or add a new angle, such as matching family sets.

## Technical Audit

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed material links returned 403.** They are all `https://carters.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...` Attentive redirect URLs. These include the hero CTA ("long weekend stock-up | 50% Off*..."), "Buy 1, Get 2 Free*", the logo ("Carter's"), the brand links (osh kosh bigosh, little planet, Otter Avenue, Skiphop), and five product image links.

- 32 links were skipped by the prober (footer and social), so their health is unknown.
- Tracking links are opaque, roughly 600-character base64-style tokens. This is normal for Attentive, but it means the destination can't be audited from the source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-probe the redirect links** with a browser-like user agent or a headless browser that follows redirects. Confirm that the final URLs on carters.com resolve with 200 and carry `utm_*` parameters. Until then, don't treat the 403s as real breakage.
