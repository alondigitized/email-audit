# Homepage snapshot · www.carters.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Carter's went RIGHT for my jugular — a stacked "up to 40% OFF + EXTRA 20% with CODE: SUMMER20" deal is literally the kind of screenshot I would send to my mom group in a heartbeat. The brand feels warm, familiar, and summer-ready, with a gorgeous lifestyle shot of a mom and a toddler below the hero that made me double-take because that could genuinely be me and my kid. Right now they are clearly pushing a summer sale event anchored to that promo code.

## What's working

- **The double-stack deal banner + hero** — "40% OFF* PLUS EXTRA 20% off with CODE: SUMMER20" is coupon-stacker catnip. The code is big, bold, and copy-pasteable. I already mentally added it to my clipboard.
- **The lifestyle photo** — a mom with sunglasses laughing with her toddler in a striped summer outfit. I am *that* person. That image made me pause and feel seen in a way a product flat-lay never would.
- **Deal messaging as the full hero** — no fluff, no brand story. Just: here's money off, here's the code, here's the button. Respect.
- **Top promo strip** — the "Code SUMMER20 = extra 20% off*" banner at the very top reinforces the offer twice so I can't miss it even if I skim.
- **Clean mobile render** — search bar, nav icons, hero, and lifestyle image all load without jank. Feels fast.

## What's weak

- **Zero loyalty hook above the fold** — I have Carter's Rewards. Show me my balance or a "member extra" callout. I'm already logged in (that bell icon has a "1" notification) — use that!
- **"Shop Now" is too generic** — I have pajamas, bodysuits, and shorts on my mental list. "Shop Sale" or "Shop Toddler Sale" would pull me in faster than a catch-all CTA.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — as a mobile shopper I always check: "do I need to add something to get free shipping?" That answer isn't anywhere in this viewport and it matters for cart size.
- **Asterisks on both the hero and the banner** — "*select styles" is doing a lot of quiet work. Fine print isn't shown, which creates a small trust wobble right when I'm about to tap.
- **No urgency signal** — the sale feels open-ended. No "ends Sunday" or countdown timer means I can mentally file it under "I'll come back later" and then forget.

## Recommendations

- **Add a member/rewards callout to the hero or strip** — even just "Rewards members get an extra 5% back" or surface my points balance since I appear to be logged in (notification badge says 1). This is the difference between a sale and *my* sale.
- **Swap "Shop Now" for "Shop Toddler Sale"** — or rotate it to the top category (babies / toddlers / kids) with explicit labels so I can one-tap to my section without digging through the hamburger.
- **Surface free-shipping threshold in the promo strip** — something like "FREE shipping on $35+ | Code SUMMER20 = extra 20% off" gives me two reasons to tap in one line of text.
- **Add a soft deadline to the hero** — "Ends Sunday" or a 3-day countdown badge on the hero image is enough to make me stop saving the tab and start actually shopping.
