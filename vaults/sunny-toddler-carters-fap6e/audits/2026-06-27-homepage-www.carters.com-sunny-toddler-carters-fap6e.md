# Homepage snapshot · www.carters.com · 2026-06-27
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-27**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I literally live in Carter's — this is my brand. The second I opened the page I felt that warm familiar feeling: cute kids, summer fun, and a big sale banner right at the top. Carter's is running a summer push right now and they are leading with lifestyle over product, which is very much their aesthetic. It reads exactly like a brand that knows millennial moms are scrolling while their kid dumps Cheerios on the floor.

## What's working

- **The top promo banner** — "Your summer MVPs: Up to 50% off\* for a limited time!" catches me immediately. I am a sale-stacker and 50% off is the exact number that makes me stop scrolling.
- **The hero lifestyle photo** — a tattooed millennial mom with her toddler and baby, laughing outside in summer. I felt seen. This is not a staged studio family — it looks like a real life moment.
- **"Summer staples" / "MVPs of your summer packing list"** — the copy is casual and relatable. "Packing list" tells me Carter's knows I'm prepping for a vacation or camp, which is exactly what I'm doing right now.
- **"Shop the Collection" button** — it's right there, high contrast, easy to tap on mobile. No hunting.
- **The search bar** — "Find your product" front and center. I often come in knowing I need 2T pajamas or a specific swim set, so I love a prominent search.

## What's weak

- **"For a limited time" is doing zero work** — no end date, no countdown, no "ends Sunday." "Limited time" is brand-speak for "we're not actually telling you when." I need urgency to convert, not a hint of urgency.
- **No free-shipping callout above the fold** — this is a huge miss. I always check the shipping threshold before I start filling my cart. If it's buried below the fold I might bail before I find it.
- **No pajamas, no category shortcuts** — I have pajamas in my cart at all times, but I have to tap the hamburger or scroll to find them. There's no category rail or shortcut strip visible.
- **Asterisk on 50% off** — that little star is the first thing my coupon-brain notices. I know it means "not on what you actually want." Feels like fine print bait.
- **No product images above the fold** — the hero is lifestyle only. I love the photo but I can't tell what's actually on sale or featured. I need to see product to get into shopping mode.

## Recommendations

- **Add a free-shipping threshold to the promo banner** — change it to "Up to 50% off + free shipping on $35+\*" so I know my typical PJ haul qualifies before I even start.
- **Replace "for a limited time" with a real deadline** — "Ends July 4" or a 48-hour countdown chip next to the banner gives me a reason to buy *now* instead of saving the tab.
- **Add a category shortcut strip below the hero** — four chips: "Pajamas," "Swim," "Baby," "Toddler." I can get there in one tap instead of digging into the hamburger menu.
- **Swap the opaque hero CTA for a category-specific one** — instead of "Shop the Collection," try "Shop Summer Staples" or even "Shop Pajamas & Swim" — specific destination copy converts better on mobile.
