# $9+ Cozy PJs 🧸

## Executive summary

- This is a PJ-led send with a strong hero and a messy back half. The top has a cozy-flash banner ("up to 50% off* + extra 20% off", code COZY20, ends Wednesday). Under it is a big flat-lay of pink floral and teal dino pajamas, then "COZY PJS FOR ALL KIDS" and a "Shop from $9" button. - After the hero, it loses focus. The "We think you'll love these" grid shows jeans, white bodysuits and joggers, with no PJs, no prices and no labels. Then come an OshKosh × Sergio Hudson banner, a 70% off clearance block, a Sleep Week teaser and a store locator. That's five competing messages. ## What's working

- The subject and hero match: "$9+ Cozy PJs" lands on the PJ flat-lay and the "Shop from $9" button.
- The flash-sale banner is at the very top, with the percentage, the extra 20%, the code and the deadline all in one strip.
- The PJ photo is large and clean, and the pink floral and teal dino pair covers both girls and boys.
- The "Shop from $9" button is clear and sits right under the hero copy.
- The Sleep Week block ("Only from Oct 29-Nov 3", "Add to Calendar") gives people a next reason to come back.

## What's weak

- The "We think you'll love these" grid is all jeans, bodysuits and joggers, with no PJs, no prices and no captions. It undercuts the PJ hero.
- The 70% off clearance block competes with the 50% + 20% banner. Three different discount numbers appear in one email.
- The OshKosh × Sergio Hudson banner is large and fashion-led, and it pulls attention away from the sale. It was already promoted on 10/22.
- The first-fold banner is small. The "Ends Wednesday" and "COZY20" text is tiny and low-contrast on the beige background.
- The fine print is long, and "*" appears on every headline discount. The offer feels conditional.
- Cadence: this is the 4th sale-style send in 4 days, and it repeats the 10/24 COZY FLASH and the 10/23 PJ hero.

- 2. Put the code in the hero button copy: "Shop PJs, code COZY20". Make the "Ends Wednesday" line larger.
- 3. Cut the clearance block and the Sergio Hudson banner from this send. Move them to their own sends.
- 4. Keep Sleep Week as a small footer teaser only.
