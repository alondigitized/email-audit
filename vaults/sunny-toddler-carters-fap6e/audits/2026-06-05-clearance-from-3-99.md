# CLEARANCE FROM $3.99 🚨❤️
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-05**
## Executive summary

- Carter's goes bottom-of-funnel today, trading the summer teaser framing it's leaned on all week for a hard clearance floor: $3.99. The subject lands the price before you read a word of body copy, and the red hero translates that promise immediately above the fold. After six consecutive summer-vibe sends — culminating in yesterday's vague "Summer loading..." ellipsis — the shift to a concrete clearance hook is a welcome editorial gear change and the sharpest subject line Carter's has written in seven days.
- The email earns its hero but loses focus mid-scroll. The Little Planet organic sub-brand section — tucked between the clearance product grid and the category browse row — belongs in a different send entirely. It carries a premium, earthy aesthetic that clashes with the discount urgency above it and stalls the momentum exactly where the email should be pushing to cart.
- Verdict: strongest subject of the week, clean structure, one tonal misfire that costs clicks.

## What's working

- **$3.99 floor in both subject and hero** — the price is visible before and immediately after opening; no hunting for the offer
- **Category segmentation buttons** (Baby Girl / Toddler Girl / Toddler Boy) let the subscriber self-select rather than landing on an undifferentiated clearance dump
- **Red oversized hero type** — immediate visual hierarchy; eye lands on the price without effort
- **"PEEP WHAT'S NEW IN CLEARANCE" product grid** — six specific items rendered cleanly, casual copy fits Carter's voice
- **Clean render** — no broken images, no overlapping text, layout holds throughout

## What's weak

- **Little Planet mid-email section is a tonal mismatch** — premium organic sub-brand aesthetics dropped into a clearance urgency email kills discount momentum; this is a separate send
- **No deadline or scarcity copy** — "clearance" implies limited stock but the email provides no end date, countdown, or "while supplies last" signal to close the loop on urgency
- **🚨 alarm emoji paired with ❤️ is tonally incoherent** — 🚨 reads panic/alert; ❤️ reads brand warmth; together they undercut each other rather than reinforcing a message
- **No loyalty or rewards hook** — a clearance email is the exact moment "Rewards members save an extra 10%" would convert fence-sitters; it's absent

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the Little Planet section entirely** — or save it for a standalone brand-story send. Every pixel below the clearance product grid should funnel toward checkout, not pivot to a premium sub-brand pitch.
- 2. **Add an explicit deadline** — "Clearance ends Sunday" or "Sizes go fast — shop now" would give the urgency that 🚨 alone can't carry.
- 3. **Replace 🚨❤️ with one emoji** — 🔥 or ⏰ reads coherently as scarcity; ❤️ belongs on a loyalty/heritage send, not a clearance alarm.
- 4. **Surface a loyalty CTA** — "Rewards members: extra 10% off clearance" converts the brand's most engaged subscribers who are already conditioned to the daily email cadence.

- **Subject Alt B:** `$3.99 clearance 🔥 limited stock`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Baby Girl, Toddler & Boys — while sizes last`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New clearance drops added. Shop before they're gone.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`<title>` element is empty:**
```html
<title></title>
```
Screen readers (NVDA, JAWS in browser-rendered email) and some email client tab labels will show a blank title. Should be `<title>carter's Clearance Sale</title>` or equivalent.

**Google Fonts loaded via `<link>` — will fail in majority of clients:**
```html
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=League+Spartan:wght@400;500;600;700&display=swap" rel="stylesheet">
```
Gmail (all variants), Outlook (all versions), and Samsung Mail block external stylesheet `<link>` tags. League Spartan will not load; fallback stack in `font-family` declarations will govern rendering. This is expected behavior for this pattern, but should be intentional with a tested fallback.

**No issues** with MSO/VML conditionals — `PixelsPerInch`, `AllowPNG`, and `DontUseAdvancedTypographyReadingMail` are all present and correctly structured.

**`role="presentation"` on all layout tables** — correct.

**`lang="en"` on `<html>`** — correct.

**Preheader padding technique** uses mixed Unicode: U+034F (combining grapheme joiner) and U+00AD (soft hyphen) mixed with U+FEFF-adjacent characters. This is functional but the mix of character types can behave inconsistently across spam filters that score Unicode anomalies. A consistent run of a single invisible character (e.g., `&zwnj;` or `&#847;`) is cleaner.
