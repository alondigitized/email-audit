# TODAY ONLY - 60% off online 🩵
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- This is the cleanest Carter's send of the week, and it's honest about being a one-day event. The teal starburst hero says "60% off* online, today only, 100s of styles added" with a Shop Now button. A live countdown timer (13 hours, 45 minutes left) sits above it. The urgency is credible because the timer and the subject line agree. After four straight days of PJ and LAYERUP promos, a new color and a new discount depth is a real change.
- The weak spot is what comes after the hero. The email shifts to "New in: for their chill era," a soft lifestyle flat-lay of blue rocket-print sets, a gray bear hoodie and a knit beanie. That's pretty, but it's a mood image rather than a deal. The "Shop it all" row has three category links, then a red "$2.99+ clearance" block. Two price messages compete. The 60% off headline gets undercut by an asterisk and by small-print "Select styles. Other exclusions may apply."
- Verdict: a strong, urgent hero on a good day to be sending. The body is under-built for a one-day sale, with no product pricing, no reviews, and a lot of whitespace around a single image.

## What's working

- The countdown timer above the hero makes "today only" visible, not just claimed.
- The hero is high-contrast: white type on teal, with one offer and one button. The eye lands there first.
- The teal palette is a fresh break from the magenta starburst of 10-08 through 10-10.
- The category row (Baby / Toddler / Kid) lets a shopper self-sort in one tap.
- The loyalty and utility strip (curbside, cardholder perks, app points, Rewards) shows member benefits.

## What's weak

- The hero doesn't say which products are 60% off, just "100s of styles added." The asterisk and "select styles" fine print make the claim feel less honest.
- The "New in" flat-lay has no price, no product name, and no sale tag. Its CTA, "Shop Matching Sets," is a different message from the headline offer.
- The red "$2.99+ clearance" block competes with the 60% off hero and dilutes the single-offer focus. "Markdowns" is also spelled "Morkdowns" in the rendered image.
- The "Now open near you!" store block, at 100 Citadel Dr., Commerce, CA, interrupts the flow and is irrelevant to an online-only sale.
- The disclaimers are long, and the body has no reviews or social proof.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the flat-lay with a 6-product grid showing the sale price and the original price, so the 60% off claim has proof. Keep the hero and move the clearance block below it.
- 2. Make the hero CTA specific, such as "Shop 60% off now," and put "Select styles, online only" right under the headline, not at the bottom.
- 3. Drop the store-opening block from an online-only promo, or move it to a segmented send.
- 4. Fix the "Morkdowns" typo and add a star rating or "best seller" tag to the hero products.
- 5. Add a short "Ends tonight at midnight ET" line next to the timer so the deadline reads without doing math.

## Technical Audit

The email's code is structurally sound, with a viewport meta tag, `lang="en"`, MSO conditional comments and a preheader. The automated QA run found real gaps in the plain-text part, the `<title>` and image alt text. The 12 probed-link 403s, the missing unsubscribe headers and the unknown authentication status can't be confirmed from this data. The 16% pass rate is mostly those 403s, so it overstates the problems.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links returned 403.** Every one is on the `carters.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...` click-tracking redirect. That covers the main CTA ("60% off* online | today only..."), Shop Baby, Toddler and Kid (each twice), Shop Matching Sets, the logo ("Carter's"), "osh kosh bigosh", "little planet" and "Otter Avenue".
- **The 403s are probably not real breakage.** A uniform 403 across every link on one ESP redirect domain points to bot or user-agent filtering of the probe. A single dead link would look different. The final destinations were never reached, so this is unverified, not confirmed broken.
- **34 links were skipped.** This covers footer, social and utility links, so their status is unknown.
- **Tracking pixels (informational):**
  - `carters.attentivemail.com/ss/o/.../ho.gif` is an open pixel in a hidden `.pixel-wrapper` div.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **Missing alt text on content images.** The QA flags are real for these images:

- **Footer not verifiable.** The HTML was truncated before the footer, so I can't confirm a physical postal address or a visible unsubscribe link. The QA did probe an unsubscribe link, but its result isn't listed among the failures.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the Attentive links with a browser-like User-Agent, or follow the redirects manually, to confirm whether the 403s are bot-blocking. Then record the final destination URLs and UTMs.
2. Add `alt` text to the four `SERVICES_BANNER` images, the secondary GIF and the Movable Ink PNG. Set `alt=""` explicitly on the tracking pixels.
3. Populate `<title>`, for example with the subject line.
4. Add a multipart `text/plain` alternative.
5. Inspect the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and for SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment, outside the relay. Without that, treat the compliance results as inconclusive.
