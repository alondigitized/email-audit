# LIMITED EDITION: $6+ family PJs today only 🌠
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- This is the third send in two days for the same limited-edition family PJ creative. The 2026-11-16 send, "$6+ Limited edition family PJs 🌟", used the same pink hero and the same "from $6" offer. The hero is still strong. A countdown timer reading 00 days, 02 hours, 51 minutes and 52 seconds sits above a pink panel, with a big "$6" and a stack of candy-cane PJs. The "today only online!" badge adds urgency.
- The body is a catch-all. After the PJ hero come a Black Friday 50-60% banner, a "Festive Fit Check" block, bomber jackets and a 70% clearance block. Four different messages compete, and the PJ offer gets diluted by the time you scroll. It is also a long email, with a dense legal footer. I'd call it a competent, on-season send that repeats itself. It rates a 7 on execution, but a subscriber who saw yesterday's version would feel the repetition.

## What's working

- The hero is clear. "from $6 LIMITED EDITION FAMILY PJS" with a "plus, add $3 cozy socks" upsell is one concrete offer.
- The countdown timer and the "today only online!" badge give credible, time-bound urgency.
- Three candy-cane print swatches under "Black Friday Exclusive PJs" show the pattern options and the matching-family angle.
- The red "black friday 50-60% off* site & store" banner puts a sitewide offer in front of everyone.
- The "Shop from $7" jacket CTA is specific and price-anchored.

## What's weak

- The send repeats yesterday's "$6+ Limited edition family PJs" creative almost exactly, so cadence fatigue is likely.
- Too many competing blocks: PJs, sitewide, plaid and knits, jackets, clearance and a gift bag. The 70% clearance block has no focal product.
- Most CTAs say only "Shop Now", and the "Festive Fit Check" CTA doesn't say what you're shopping.
- The fine print is heavy, and the asterisks on the 50-60% and 70% claims weaken trust in the offer.
- The sibling-brand logo strip at the top (OshKosh, little planet, Otter Avenue, Skip Hop) pushes the hero down. The PJ hero is not fully visible without scrolling on mobile.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the PJ hero, one sitewide banner and one clearance block. Remove "Festive Fit Check" and the jackets, or move them to a separate send.
- 2. Differentiate from yesterday. Lead with the "ends tonight" countdown and a new angle, such as the matching sizes from baby to adult, instead of re-running the same creative.
- 3. Replace "Shop Now" with "Shop family PJs", "Add $3 socks" and "Shop jackets from $7".
- 4. Move the sibling-brand strip below the hero, or shrink it.
- 5. Show a price on each swatch and link each one to its own PJ style.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: $6 family PJs 🌠`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Matching PJs from $6, gone at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Add $3 socks to any pair. Sizes for the whole crew.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Candy-cane prints, baby to adult. Online only, today.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Built from an Attentive/Stensul-style template with all links wrapped in Attentive redirects. QA found 12 of 12 probed links returning 403, a 0-character plain-text part, and no alt text on 8 linked content images. The 403s are probably bot-blocking rather than real breakage, so they need confirming.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links returned 403.** Every one is a `https://carters.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...` redirect, covering the "Shop Now" buttons, the "carter's", "osh kosh bigosh", "little planet", "Otter Avenue" and "Skiphop" brand links, the hero link and the image links.
  - A uniform 403 across every redirect looks like Attentive's click-tracker rejecting the automated probe (UA/IP filtering), not 12 dead destinations. This is not confirmed.
  - Re-test with a real browser UA, or follow one link manually, before treating it as a production defect.
- **Link text is not a usable label.** Three image links are reported with text `[image: MzczMjo6Q0FSXzExMTYyNl9TRUNPTkRBUllfMS5w…]`, which is an auto-generated base64 filename.
  - A fourth link has the text "Display images to show real-time content". It is a tracked link, which suggests a live-content or "view in browser" link wrapped in the redirect.
- **Two tracking pixels are present.**
  - `carters.attentivemail.com/ss/o/.../ho.gif` is the Attentive open pixel.
  - `mi.carters.com/p/up/745a17bbb73445ad/o.gif?mi_u=1150794179&mi_ecmp=11_16_2026_CAR_MON_PM_RETARGETING` is a second-party pixel.
  - `mi.carters.com/p/rp/264477f43986d8bb.png?mi_u=…&mi_campaign=1` is a third beacon, and it is not hidden.
  - `mi_u=1150794179` is a per-recipient identifier embedded in the URL.
- **Coverage is partial.** 45 links were skipped by the probe, so footer, social and unsubscribe links are unverified except where the QA harness always includes the unsubscribe link.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 8 linked content images.** The src values are CloudFront objects with base64-encoded names, e.g. `d3ptznaucccg76.cloudfront.net/MzczMjo6Q0FSXzExMTYyNl9TRUNPTkRBUllfMS5w…`.
  - Affected: `..._SECONDARY_1.png`, `..._DRESSY_SECOND…jpg`, `..._SECONDARY_8.jpg`, and `SERVICES_BANNER_01` through `SERVICES_BANNER_04_v3`.
  - Where these images are the only content of a link, the link has no accessible name and the HTML falls back to the filename string.
  - With images blocked, these blocks render empty.
- **False positive:** `ho.gif` is flagged but the source shows `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"`. That is correct for a decorative pixel, so no action is needed.
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in `<head>`. This is minor, but it affects the browser-tab title and some clients' view-online title.
- **Positives, no action:** `lang="en"` is set, the viewport meta is present, and the MSO conditional comments and VML namespaces are correct. The preheader is hidden properly with `display:none; max-height:0; opacity:0`.
- The League Spartan web font is loaded inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, so it falls back safely in Outlook.
- The HTML was truncated, so I could not verify the remaining layout tables, `role` attributes or the mobile `.row-*` selectors beyond what is shown.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible source or the subject. The only per-recipient value visible is `mi_u=1150794179` in the pixel URLs, and it is populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No plain-text alternative.** The QA check shows `Text version is 0 chars` [FAIL]. A missing text/plain part is a deliverability and spam-filter risk and an accessibility gap. It could be a capture artifact of the relay, but the QA check is a hard FAIL.
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) were not found** [WARN]. QA notes the headers may simply not be captured by the AgentMail relay.
  - If they are truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules (one-click unsubscribe) are not met.
  - Verify against the raw message headers.
- **Authentication-Results not found**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. QA attributes this to the relay. It cannot be assessed from this data.
- **Footer not visible.** The HTML is truncated, so I cannot confirm the physical postal address or the in-body unsubscribe link from source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters and destination URLs cannot be verified.** Every link is an opaque Attentive redirect (`/e/c/<token>`), and probing stopped at the 403.
  - So I cannot confirm UTM parameters, landing-page alignment with "from $6 LIMITED EDITION FAMILY PJS" or the "$3 cozy socks" offer, or whether the destination is a working page.
- The campaign identifier `11_16_2026_CAR_MON_PM_RETARGETING` is exposed in the `mi_ecmp` pixel parameter. This confirms tracking is configured, but not what the click-through carries.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the Attentive redirects with a browser-like UA and follow the redirect chain. This separates probe-blocking from real breakage, and it also exposes the final URLs and UTMs for section 6.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 8 linked content images, e.g. product or offer text for the secondary tiles and the service names for the banners. This gives the links an accessible name.
3. Add a text/plain MIME part. Confirm whether the empty result is a relay artifact by checking the raw source.
4. Inspect the raw headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment on `marketing.carters.com`.
5. Populate `<title>` with the subject or campaign name.
6. Review the "Display images to show real-time content" link. It is tracked and has non-descriptive text.
7. Fix the QA tooling: skip or accept `alt=""` on 1×1 tracking pixels to avoid the false positive. Also, the 403 FAILs against ESP click-tracker domains should be downgraded or flagged "probe blocked" rather than "broken experience".
