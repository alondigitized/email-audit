# Homepage snapshot · www.carters.com · 2026-05-07
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Carter's is not playing around right now — 40% off *plus* an extra 20% with a code, right at the top, no hunting required. As someone who literally tracks Carter's promo cadence in my head and has SUMMER20-type codes saved in my notes app, this homepage makes me feel seen. It reads like a brand that knows its core shopper is a deal-stacking mom on her phone during nap time.

## What's working

- **The stacked discount structure** — "up to 40% OFF* PLUS, EXTRA 20% OFF" is Carter's coupon-stacker catnip. The math is visible before I even scroll.
- **CODE: SUMMER20 is right there in the hero** — no digging through emails, no copy-paste from a text. It's already on the page I'm looking at.
- **The "Shop Now" button** — white on the warm gradient, high contrast, tappable-looking even on a small screen.
- **The lifestyle image** — mom with sunglasses, toddler grinning in a summer scene. That's my weekend. It pulled me right in.
- **The top promo banner** — "Code SUMMER20 = extra 20% off* select styles!" is a clean reinforcement of the hero before the hero even loads.
- **The search bar** — I almost always type "pajamas" directly on Carter's. Having it front and center is a fast lane to my usual cart.

## What's weak

- **No category shortcuts visible** — I want to see "Baby," "Toddler," "Pajamas" tabs without tapping the hamburger. One tap to my category feels one tap too many right now.
- **No free-shipping threshold mentioned** — I build my cart around the free-ship number. Not seeing it above the fold means I lose that anchoring signal early.
- **No urgency signal on the promo** — "SUMMER20" sounds seasonal but I have no idea if this ends in 2 hours or 2 weeks. A "today only" or "ends Sunday" line would have had me tapping faster.
- **Generic CTA copy** — "Shop Now" is fine but "Shop the Sale" or "Stack & Save" would match the energy of the deal messaging more directly.
- **No loyalty balance hook** — the bell badge with "1" notification is doing something, but it's not telling me my Rewarding Moments balance or that I have rewards to redeem. That missed a big pull for a returning shopper.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Toddler" and "Baby" shortcut row** directly below the search bar — two tappable chips so I can skip the hamburger entirely and land in my category in one gesture.
- **Drop "Ends [date]" or a countdown strip** under the hero copy — "Sale ends Sunday" costs nothing to add and turns a passive browser into an urgent tapper.
- **Swap "Shop Now" for "Shop the Sale"** — keeps the CTA unambiguous while reflecting the actual offer context, not just a generic action.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold** — a single line like "Free shipping on $35+" tucked under the CTA button would anchor my cart-building behavior from the first screen.
