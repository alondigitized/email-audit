# Up to 60% off family matching 💫
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is carter's Black Friday catch-all again, now wearing a "family matching" subject line. The subject promises matching outfits, but the top of the email leads with the generic red "black friday 50-60% OFF* SITE & STORE" banner. The hero photo is a strong one: two toddlers in festive sweaters, captioned "festive & photo worthy". The matching theme only arrives below it, in "SIBLING OUTFITS: SORTED" with a "Shop up to 60% off*" button.
- After that the email scatters. There is a "$2.99 CLEARANCE FLASH" block, six gender and age tiles, two product cards, a Buy 1 Get 2 Free OshKosh jeans block, a price-match banner and a gift-bag banner. There are at least five distinct offers, which dilutes the subject's promise. It's the fourth near-identical Black Friday send in a week, after 2026-11-20 and 2026-11-21 and the "holiday PJs" send earlier today. The creative is competent, but it doesn't earn a high score.

## What's working

- The hero photo is warm, on-season and emotionally on-brand. The siblings in festive sweaters read as holiday family photos.
- The red banner states the core offer, "50-60% OFF* SITE & STORE", with a visible "Shop Now" link.
- The "$2.99 CLEARANCE FLASH" block is a strong price anchor, and the red panel stands out.
- The gender and age tiles (Baby Girl, Toddler Boy and so on) give easy category entry points.
- The loyalty and service row at the bottom (curbside, cardholder perks, Rewards) gives members something to see.

## What's weak

- The subject promises "family matching", but the hero and banner are generic Black Friday. The matching message only shows up in a small text block below the photo.
- The body has too many competing offers: 50-60%, up to 60%, $2.99, from $10, from $8, BOGO jeans, price match and a gift bag. No single offer dominates.
- The hero banner is a text "Shop Now >" link, not a button. The real buttons are thin outlined boxes that are easy to skip.
- The "Outfit sets" and "Super comfy lounge" cards have no price visuals beyond small "Shop from $10" and "Shop from $8" text links.
- The email is very long, with a dense legal block. The asterisked exclusions are buried at the bottom while the "*" offers sit at the top.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero match the subject. Show a real family-matching set with a "family matching, up to 60% off" headline, and move the generic Black Friday banner below it or shrink it.
- 2. Cut the body to one hero offer and two supporting modules. Drop the jeans BOGO or the gift bag for this send.
- 3. Turn "Shop Now >" and the outlined buttons into solid, high-contrast buttons with specific copy, such as "Shop matching sets".
- 4. Add price tags and a reviews or best-seller callout to the product cards.
- 5. Include a deadline, such as "ends Monday", to give the urgency a credible end date.
