# Up to 50% off* ☀️😎✨🫶
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-02**
## Executive summary

- Carter's is back with a 50% off summer send — the same offer, the same percentage, and functionally the same creative as May 28's "UP TO 50% OFF SUMMER" email, sent five days ago. The structure is familiar: teal/mint hero block, a "MADE FOR EVERY MOMENT" product grid, an Otter Avenue toddler section, and a red clearance band at the bottom escalating to 70% off. Nothing is broken; everything renders cleanly. The problem isn't execution — it's repetition.
- The one structural improvement over the May 28 version is the Otter Avenue mid-section: a full-bleed yellow lifestyle image of a toddler in summer gear with the "Toddler-Friendly Summer" hook breaks the product-grid monotony and gives the eye somewhere interesting to land. The clearance 70% band at the bottom is also a real escalation signal for price-conscious shoppers.
- But leading with the same subject vocabulary ("Up to 50% off*") as May 28 after a week of constant discount messaging is a self-defeating cadence. A subscriber who opens this expecting something new will recognize the beat immediately. Carter's burned its goodwill on the June 1 brand-heritage send and then immediately pivoted back to discount mode — the whiplash is real.

## What's working

- **Teal hero is clean and on-brand** — "UP TO 50% OFF*" is legible at a glance with a clear "Shop Now" CTA.
- **Otter Avenue lifestyle section earns its real estate** — the toddler photography and "Toddler-Friendly Summer" copy break the grid and feel editorial.
- **Clearance escalation is smart** — following the 50% hero with a 70% clearance band creates a natural price ladder, giving deal-seekers a second reason to scroll.
- **Product grid variety** — the 9-item MADE FOR EVERY MOMENT grid shows range across categories (rompers, tees, matching sets) without feeling padded.
- **Clean render** — no broken images, no layout bugs, footer social + app links all visible.

## What's weak

- **Subject line is a near-verbatim repeat of May 28** — "Up to 50% off* ☀️😎✨🫶" against "UP TO 50% OFF SUMMER ☀️😎" is the same sentence with extra emojis. A subscriber who sees this in-inbox will feel like they're being re-served a stale promo.
- **Four emojis in the subject** — ☀️😎✨🫶 piled together reads as spam-adjacent and dilutes the clarity the percentage gives you.
- **No urgency or deadline anywhere** — neither the subject, hero, nor clearance section names a date. "Shop Now" is evergreen-speak; it creates no reason to act today versus next Tuesday.
- **CTA copy is generic** — "Shop Now" on a button with no product context. Compare May 31's "Matching sets from $12" energy: a verb + a price is more clickable than a verb + nothing.
- **Hero product category vague** — "Summer ready tees, shorts & more" is the same descriptor Carter's has used all month. No specific hero item, no new-arrival hook.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Surface the clearance escalation in the subject** — the 70% off clearance is the most differentiated offer in this email and it's buried below the fold. Lead with it.
- 2. **Add a real deadline** — "Ends Sunday" or "This week only" in the hero gives the offer urgency it completely lacks right now.
- 3. **Retire the four-emoji stack** — one thematic emoji does more than four decorative ones. Keep ☀️ or 🫶, drop the rest.
- 4. **Punch up the hero CTA** — "Shop Baby Girl Sale" or "Shop Summer Clearance" over "Shop Now."
- 5. **Otter Avenue deserves a dedicated CTA** — the lifestyle image has no explicit button; shoppers who respond to the editorial feel have nowhere to go.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 70% off clearance — summer's going fast`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Summer clearance starts at 50% off ☀️`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Baby, toddler & kids styles — tees, shorts, matching sets on sale now`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Clearance up to 70% off — shop before it sells out`
