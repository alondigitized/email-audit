# ENDS WEDNESDAY: 50-60% off*site & store ⭐
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, high-contrast clearance push, and the headline does its job. "ENDS WEDNESDAY! 50-60% OFF* SITE & STORE" sits on a pastel holographic hero, and the eye lands on it first. The deadline and the discount are both stated once, with a single "Shop Now" button. It is a straightforward, competent promo.
- The rest of the email dilutes that focus. Below the hero it adds a 3×3 grid of plain basics (white bodysuits, black, navy and grey joggers, jeans), a "Baby's First Christmas" editorial, a "coming 11.06 / 11.07 / 11.08" deal teaser, a 70%-off clearance block, and a store-locator banner. That is five or six competing messages. The "12 Days of Deals" sender name sets up a daily-deal rhythm, but this send leaves the daily-deal slot to a teaser for tomorrow's deals.
- It is the fifth Carter's send in five days. The previous day's "TODAY ONLY - $9 bodysuit pants sets" had one hard price. This one trades that for a vague percentage range, and Carter's is now repeating the "ends" urgency it used on Nov 3 ("ALL PJs up to 60% off"). Overall it is a solid but forgettable 6 to 7.

## What's working

- The hero is clear. "ENDS WEDNESDAY!" in a black tag sits directly above a huge "50-60% OFF*", so the deadline and the offer register in one glance.
- The "Picked just for you!" grid is a clean, uncluttered product tile set that looks tidy on a phone.
- The "coming 11.06 / 11.07 / 11.08" tiles tease specific prices ($10 OshKosh jeans, $8 character tees, $10 multipack bodysuits), which gives a reason to come back.
- The red "UP TO 70% OFF* CLEARANCE" block with the four-way Baby/Toddler/Girl/Boy navigation is easy to scan and use.
- The footer perks row (curbside, cardholder perks, in-app points, Rewards) is a useful trust and loyalty cue.

## What's weak

- The "Picked just for you!" label sits over a generic grid of basics. Nothing shows a price, a name or a reason it was picked for this recipient.
- The email carries too many competing modules, which dilutes the hero. The "Baby's First Christmas" editorial and the 70% clearance block pull in different directions.
- The "50-60% OFF*" range is soft, and the asterisk leads to dense fine print at the bottom. "Excludes Little Planet, Skip Hop, Carter's Bedtime…" is a long list, so the discount applies to less than it first appears.
- The "Shop Now" CTAs are generic and are repeated across the email. The hero button is also a thin outlined box with low visual weight for the primary action.
- The "Get Directions" store banner and the "12 days of deals under $12" text feel disconnected from the rest. It is also unclear whether this send is a Day 3 deal or a separate sale.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story. Either lead with the deadline sale and cut the baby-Christmas editorial and the clearance block, or send the Day 3 deal and drop the generic "50-60%" hero. Two sales in one email dilute both.
- 2. Put prices on the product grid. Show "$X" or "% off" on each tile, and replace "Picked just for you!" with something specific such as "Baby basics, now $6-$12".
- 3. Make the primary CTA a solid, filled button with specific copy such as "Shop 50-60% off baby basics". Use it once near the hero and once near the end.
- 4. Shorten the fine print visible near the hero and put the biggest exclusions next to the asterisk.
- 5. Break the repetition. Five promos in five days sit close together, so vary the creative or merge sends.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 50-60% off ends Wednesday ⏰`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Baby basics from $6 – sale ends Wednesday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Bodysuits, joggers & denim up to 60% off in store and online, plus Day 3 deals coming tomorrow.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Take 50-60% off sitewide through Wednesday, and see what's new in clearance.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Click-tracking links are wrapped in `carters.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...` redirects, and all 12 probed links returned 403. The QA run also found no plain-text part and no visible authentication or unsubscribe headers. Because the HTML was truncated and the headers likely weren't captured by the relay, several of these findings are unconfirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **12 of 12 probed links returned 403.** They include the hero CTA ("ENDS WEDNESDAY! | 50-60% OFF*..."), the brand links (carter's, osh kosh bigosh, little planet, Otter Avenue, Skiphop) and the product tiles (Baby 5-Pack Bodysuits, Boys French Terry Joggers, etc.). Every failing URL is an Attentive redirect (`/e/c/<long token>`).
- **Likely cause:** the 403 probably comes from Attentive blocking an automated, non-browser client, not from dead links. Every redirect failed the same way, so a single cause is more likely than 12 separate breakages.
- **What this means:** the check shows the destination pages are unverified. It does not show they are broken. A real-browser or real-client click-through is needed to confirm.
- **Coverage:** only 12 links were probed and 47 were skipped, so the rest are untested.
- **Link length:** the redirect tokens are roughly 500–900 characters. This is normal for Attentive but makes the URLs opaque, and the final landing URLs and any UTM parameters can't be inspected.
- **Tracking pixels:** there are three open-tracking beacons.
  - `carters.attentivemail.com/ss/o/.../ho.gif`
  - `mi.carters.com/p/up/745a17bbb73445ad/o.gif?mi_u=1150794179&mi_ecmp=11_05_2026_CAR_THU_NOON_ENGAGERS`
  - `mi.carters.com/p/rp/264477f43986d8bb.png?mi_u=...&mi_campaign=11`
- **Subscriber ID:** the `mi_` pixels carry a numeric subscriber ID (`mi_u=1150794179`) in the query string. This is normal for tracking, but the ID is exposed in the image URL.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** four banner images on `d3ptznaucccg76.cloudfront.net` (`...SERVICES_BANNER_01.png` through `..._04_v3.png`, base64-style filenames) have no `alt`. With images blocked, these would render as blank space. The HTML is truncated, so I couldn't confirm whether they carry key offer text.
- **Pixel `alt` warnings:** `ho.gif` already has `alt=""`, and the `mi.carters.com` beacons are tracking pixels. Those warnings are false positives for decorative images.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty. This is minor, but some clients and web views use it.
- **Positives:** `lang="en"` is set, layout tables use `role="presentation"`, the preheader is hidden properly with padding characters, and a mobile breakpoint at 660px is present.
- **Web font:** League Spartan loads from Google Fonts inside an `[if !mso]` block. Clients that strip `<link>` will fall back to a default font.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible source. The `mi_u` value is a resolved ID, not a leaked placeholder.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers. Treat this as unverified, not confirmed missing. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check the raw message headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same relay reason. The From domain is `marketing.carters.com`, and alignment with the Attentive sending domain can't be verified from this data.
- **Plain-text part is 0 characters.** This is a confirmed QA FAIL. A missing text alternative can hurt spam scoring and break text-only clients. It may be a relay capture artifact, so verify against the raw MIME.
- **Unsubscribe link and physical address:** neither is visible in the truncated HTML, so I can't confirm them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters and landing pages can't be verified.** All links are Attentive redirects that returned 403, so the final URLs weren't resolved.
- **Campaign ID:** the only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=11_05_2026_CAR_THU_NOON_ENGAGERS`, in the pixel URLs. It tells you this is the 11/05 noon send to an "ENGAGERS" segment, and it is not a UTM tag.
- **Offer wording:** the subject says "50-60% off*site & store", and the hero link text carries the asterisk disclaimer "*Savings based on origin...". I couldn't check landing page alignment.
