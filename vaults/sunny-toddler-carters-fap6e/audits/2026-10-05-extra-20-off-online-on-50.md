The Dr. Becky / Good Inside block also repeats from 10-01. A subscriber would see this as the 10-01 email with a coupon strip on top.
- The subject promises the extra 20% off, but the eye goes to the 50% block. The thing the subject sells is a banner in a pale gradient with small white text. The "Shop Now" button leads with a different offer than the subject does. After a run of "from $5/$6/$7" and "today only" sends, "extra 20% off on $50+" is a fair incentive. ## What's working

- The code LAYERUP and the $50+ threshold are stated in the top banner, so the mechanic is clear.
- The black-outlined "Shop Now" button on the green hero is easy to find.
- The 3x3 product grid (jeans, bodysuits, joggers, sleeper) is clean, evenly spaced and fits the fall theme.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.
- The footer perks row (curbside, cardholder, app points, Rewards) is tidy, and the 70% clearance block has clear category buttons.

## What's weak

- The extra-20% banner is thin, low-contrast white-on-gradient text, and it sits above a much louder 50% hero.
- The grid has no prices, product names, or "$50+" math, so there is no reason to pick one item.
- The 50% hero and the Dr. Becky block are repeats from the 10-01 send, which makes the cadence feel stale.
- There is no deadline or urgency, even though the 10-04 send used a live countdown.
- Four competing offers dilute the message, and the fine print runs to about 15 lines of exclusions.

- 4. Cut the Dr. Becky block, or move it below the clearance block. It is off-offer and was already sent on 10-01.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based, MSO-conditional template with a responsive media query. Automated QA reports a 16% pass rate, but most of the failures are link probes blocked with 403. Two real issues are the empty plain-text part (0 chars) and an empty `<title>`.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **12 of 12 probed links returned 403.** They are all `https://carters.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...` redirect URLs, and the failures cover the hero CTA, the "Extra 20% Off* Your $50+ Purchase" CTA, the brand tiles (Carter's, OshKosh, Little Planet, Otter Avenue, Skip Hop) and product images.
  - A uniform 403 across every Attentive link, including the brand logo, points to the ESP's bot or WAF filtering the QA prober. It is not evidence that the links are dead for subscribers. **I can't confirm a broken experience from this data.**
  - Destinations were never resolved, so click-through was not verified.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://carters.attentivemail.com/ss/o/.../ho.gif` is 1x1 inside a `display:none` wrapper. Opens are tracked on a different subdomain (`carters.attentivemail.com`) from the click-tracking domain (`carters.e.attentivemail.com`), but that is normal for Attentive.
- **Coverage gap:** 42 links were skipped, so footer, utility and social links are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in `<head>`. This is minor, but some clients and web-view and archive pages use it.
- **Missing plain-text part:** QA reports `Text version is 0 chars`. This is a real deliverability and accessibility defect (multipart/alternative with no text body).
- **Missing alt text, per QA:** five CloudFront banner images lack alt (`MzczMjo6U0VSVklDRVNfQkFOTkVSXzAx…` through `…BTk5FUl8wNF92My5wbmc…`, plus `…car-email-drbecky-secondary.jpg`).
  - If these banners carry text such as service or offer messaging, that content is invisible with images off or in a screen reader.
- **QA false positive:** it flags `ho.gif` for missing alt, but the source shows `alt=""`. That is correct for a decorative tracking pixel, so no action is needed.
- **Positives visible in the truncated source:**
  - `lang="en"` and a viewport meta tag are present.
  - `role="presentation"` is on the layout tables.
  - The `x-apple-data-detectors` reset and an Outlook `PixelsPerInch` block are included.
  - The Google Fonts `<link>` is wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, so Outlook skips it.
- The source is truncated, so rendering below the header is unverified.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source contains no unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA itself notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. It matters because Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so verify against the raw message at the source ESP.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, again likely because of the relay. The From domain is `marketing.carters.com`. Check the raw headers received directly, not via the relay.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and physical address:** neither appears in the truncated source or in the QA output, so I can't confirm or deny either. QA states an unsubscribe link is always probed, and none is listed among the failures.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. Every link goes through an Attentive redirect (`/e/c/<opaque token>`), and all probes returned 403 before redirecting. UTM parameters and landing-page destinations can't be assessed from this data. The email includes the preheader code `LAYERUP`, but whether it is auto-applied on the landing page can't be tested.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a plain-text alternative.** This is the only confirmed deliverability defect.
2. **Re-run link QA with a browser-like User-Agent or via redirect-following.** Treat the 403s as probe blocking until a destination is resolved, then check UTMs on the final URLs.
3. **Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results** from the original delivery, not the AgentMail relay.
4. **Add descriptive alt text** to the five CloudFront banner images.
5. **Populate `<title>`** (e.g. with the subject line).
6. **Suppress the `ho.gif` alt warning** in the QA checker when `alt=""` is present on a 1x1 image.
