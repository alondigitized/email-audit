# OshKosh® Reissued: Favorites from the vault
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-18**
## Executive summary

- This is a brand-story send with a promo bolted on underneath. The "From the Vault" hero is the best part. It has a red striped frame, a vintage OshKosh B'gosh collage and a baby in plaid overalls. It looks distinctive and feels like a reissue. But the hero has no price, no deadline and a generic "Shop Now" button. The only offer language sits below the fold of the first screen.
- The body then loses the thread. Under "JUST FOR YOU" is a 3x3 grid of plain jeans, joggers and white bodysuits. None of it looks like the throwback prints and silhouettes the hero promised. After that comes the familiar black "Black Friday preview / UP TO 60% OFF*" strip, then a red "UP TO 70% OFF* CLEARANCE" block. That makes three different messages in one email. This follows the Oct 16–18 run of near-identical Black Friday preview sends, such as "Multipack bodysuits from $2 per piece 🩵" earlier today. It reads as another discount email with one nostalgic block on top.
- Verdict: a good creative idea, weakly executed. Either commit to the vault story with real reissue product, or lead with a hard price like the Oct 17 "$8+ Cozy fleece PJs ✨" send.

## What's working

- The "From the Vault / The Genuine Article / Reissued" hero is distinctive and different from the recent starfield banners.
- The baby in OshKosh plaid overalls shows the product in use and carries the nostalgia angle.
- The "Black Friday preview / UP TO 60% OFF*" strip is clear and seasonal.
- The Carter's Rewards, curbside and in-app points icons make the loyalty benefits visible.
- The layout renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The hero copy ("Nostalgic looks that last") has no price or urgency, and the grey outlined "Shop Now" button is low-contrast.
- The "JUST FOR YOU" grid shows generic jeans, joggers and white bodysuits. They don't match the reissue hero, and none have prices, names or labels.
- There are three competing promos: the reissue, the 60% Black Friday preview and the 70% clearance. The eye has no single place to land.
- The 60% and 70% claims both carry asterisks, and the long fine print makes the offer feel less clean.
- The Black Friday preview messaging is the fifth or sixth repeat this week, so subscribers will skim past it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero deliver the offer. Add a price or deadline line, such as "Reissued OshKosh overalls from $X" or "Vault styles, limited run". Change the button to "Shop OshKosh reissues".
- 2. Replace the "JUST FOR YOU" grid with the actual reissued pieces: the plaid overalls, throwback prints and the styles in the hero. Add prices and names to each tile.
- 3. Cut one of the two discount blocks, either the 60% preview strip or the 70% clearance block. Keep the Black Friday preview only if it supports the vault story.
- 4. Give the hero button a filled, high-contrast style.
- 5. Make the subject say what's special, and add a price point or a limited-run angle.
- **Subject Alt A:** `OshKosh B'gosh is back: vault styles from $X`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The plaid overalls are back 🧸 limited reissue`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Throwback prints, today's fits. Plus up to 60% off early Black Friday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our favorite OshKosh archive pieces, reissued for fall. Shop before they're gone.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally sound, with a viewport meta tag, `lang="en"`, MSO fallbacks, a hidden preheader and a mobile media query at 660px. The automated QA's 16% pass rate is driven almost entirely by 403s on Attentive tracking redirects, which are probably bot-blocking by the prober and not real broken links. The email also has no plain-text part and has several images without alt text.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links returned 403.** Every one is a `https://carters.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...` click-tracking redirect. That includes the hero, "Shop Now", the brand links (osh kosh bigosh, little planet, Otter Avenue, Skiphop), and the product image links. A 403 from every redirect, with no variation, points to Attentive's edge rejecting the QA probe's user-agent or IP. It does not point to dead destinations. The final landing pages were never reached, so link validity is unverified, not confirmed broken.
- **Coverage is limited.** Only 12 of 60 links were probed and 48 were skipped (footer, social, utility), so those can't be assessed.
- **The tracking URLs are fully opaque.** They are base64-style tokens of about 600–1000 characters. Very long tracked URLs are fragile in some clients, though no clipping or truncation is confirmed here.
- **Two tracking pixels are present.** One is `carters.attentivemail.com/ss/o/.../ho.gif`. The other is `mi.carters.com/p/up/745a17bbb73445ad/o.gif?mi_u=1150794179&mi_ecmp=10_18_2026_OKB_SUN_NOON_OKB`. Two separate open-tracking vendors run in one email. This is not an error, but it is extra weight and a privacy surface.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text part is missing.** The QA reports "Text version is 0 chars". If the relay didn't just drop it, this is a real deliverability and accessibility gap.
- **`<title></title>` is empty.** Screen readers and some web-view clients use it.
- **Image links have no usable accessible name.** The QA flags the linked images below with no alt text. Their link text resolves to the filename, for example `[image: MzczMjo6T0tCX1E0XzIwMjZfUkVJU1NVRURfSEVS]`.
  - the hero banner, `d3ptznaucccg76.cloudfront.net/MzczMjo6T0tCX1E0XzIwMjZfUkVJU1NVRURfSEVSTyslMjgxJTI5LmdpZi...`
  - four `SERVICES_BANNER_01` to `04_v3.png` images
  - `mi.carters.com/p/rp/264477f43986d8bb.png`
- **Images blocked by default lose their meaning** without alt text on the hero and service banners.
- **The `ho.gif` warning is a false positive.** The source shows `alt=""` on the `ho.gif` tag, which is correct for a decorative pixel. `o.gif` is probably also a pixel, but its tag was cut off in the truncated source.
- **Positives:** product images carry descriptive alt text, such as "Image of Boys Dark Wash Relaxed-Fit Classic Jeans". Dark-mode behavior can't be assessed from the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source.
- The per-recipient value `mi_u=1150794179` in the `o.gif` URL is a resolved ID, not a failed merge.
- The preheader begins with a stray `&amp; Up to 60% off...`, which suggests the front of a template string was cut off. This is a minor sign of a truncated dynamic field.
- The rest of the body was truncated, so tokens further down can't be ruled out.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA itself notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. I can't confirm a real violation, but one-click unsubscribe is a Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirement, so verify it on the raw message.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not captured**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. The QA attributes this to the relay.
- **The footer was not visible**, so I can't confirm the physical postal address or an in-body unsubscribe link. The QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but none of the 12 reported links is identified as one.
- The sender is `marketing.carters.com`, a subdomain separate from the click domain `carters.e.attentivemail.com`. That is normal for an ESP, but link-domain alignment is unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not assessable.** The 403s kept the probe from following any redirect, so landing URLs and UTM parameters are unknown.
- The only visible campaign identifier is `mi_ecmp=10_18_2026_OKB_SUN_NOON_OKB`, with `mi_campaign` on the `264477f43986d8bb.png` image. It sits on a pixel and image, not on the click links.
- The copy ("OshKosh Reissued", "Early Black Friday", "up to 60% off") can't be matched to destination pages without resolving the redirects.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the Attentive redirects with a browser-like user-agent or the ESP's allowlist. Until then, treat the 403 failures as unconfirmed, not broken.
2. Check the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, plus SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment, outside the relay.
3. Add a multipart `text/plain` alternative (currently 0 chars).
4. Add meaningful `alt` text to the hero and the four `SERVICES_BANNER` image links. Use `alt=""` on the pixels and set a real `<title>`.
5. Follow the redirect chains with a working client and confirm UTM and campaign parameters are on the final URLs.
6. Check why the preheader starts with `&`, and confirm the footer has a postal address and a working unsubscribe link.
