# ENDS SOON: 50-60% off* site & store ⏰
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth near-identical "50-60% off site & store" send in a week (Nov 5, Nov 8 ⏳, Nov 9 🚨, now ⏰). It also lands on Day 8 of the 12 Days of Deals, after the "$8 PurelySoft™ essentials" email earlier today. The hero is a pastel holographic panel reading "ENDS WEDNESDAY! 50-60% OFF* SITE & STORE" with a "Shop Now" button. The offer is clear, but a subscriber has seen this creative three times already.
- The body is a grab bag. It has a "get ready for upcoming deals" teaser strip ($12 shoes, $10 fleece pjs, $7 fleece & sleep & play), a packable puffer, a red "up to 70% off clearance" block, a $5 Rewards banner and a store-locator block. Today's own deal is nowhere in the hero, and the email is branded "12 Days of Deals" in the sender name. The only way to reach it is the small "Shop Today's Deal" button under the teasers. That is a mismatch.
- Verdict: competent but redundant. It is a clearance reminder dressed up as a deal-day email. The cadence will train recipients to ignore the urgency.

## What's working

- The hero offer is large and legible: "50-60% OFF*" in bold black type over a clean panel, with a visible "Shop Now" button.
- The coming 11.12, 11.13 and 11.14 teaser tiles give a reason to come back, with concrete prices ($12, $10, $7).
- The $5 Rewards banner shows the member benefit and its date window (11/10–11/15).
- The puffer is a seasonally right product with a clean hero photo and one-line benefit copy ("packs down small enough to fit in a backpack").
- The footer utility row (curbside, cardholder perks, app points) is tidy, and the store-locator block is useful.

## What's weak

- It is the fourth 50-60% off send this week, so the creative and offer feel stale. Only the emoji changes.
- Today's Day 8 deal is buried behind a small "Shop Today's Deal" button below the teasers, even though the sender name is "12 Days of Deals".
- The "ENDS WEDNESDAY!" urgency is weak. Today is Wednesday, so the deadline is tonight but the email never says that.
- Too many competing blocks: 50-60% off, up to 70% off clearance, teasers, the puffer and Rewards. There is no single dominant ask.
- The asterisks and the long exclusions list make the offer feel hedged. Nearly every price claim carries fine print.
- The clearance category buttons (Baby Girl, Toddler Girl and so on) are plain and generic, with no imagery.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with today's Day 8 deal in the hero, and demote the 50-60% off message to a slim banner. The sender name promises a daily deal, so deliver it.
- 2. Stop sending a third and fourth identical clearance email. Either consolidate into one "last day" send or vary the creative and angle.
- 3. State the deadline plainly ("ends tonight, 11:59 PM PT") instead of "ENDS WEDNESDAY".
- 4. Cut the body to one hero, one product row and one Rewards banner. Drop either the 70% clearance block or the puffer.
- 5. Put product photos on the clearance category tiles so they work as visual entry points.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last day: 50-60% off ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Day 8 deal + 50-60% off, ends tonight ⏰`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus a sneak peek at $12 shoes, $10 fleece pjs and $7 sleep & plays coming 11.12–11.14.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stock up on the packable puffer and clearance up to 70% off before midnight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a conventional table-based Attentive/Stripo-style build with an MSO conditional block, a preheader and a viewport meta tag. The QA pass rate is 17%, but most of the "failures" are 403s on Attentive click-tracking redirects, which are probably a probe artifact. The confirmed defects are an empty `<title>`, missing alt text on linked images, and no plain-text part.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **403s on all 10 probed material links** (hero CTA, "Shop Now", rewards banner, brand tiles, services banners, "Display images…"). All are `https://carters.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/<token>`. A uniform 403 on every tracked redirect, including the `carter's` logo link, points to Attentive blocking the automated probe rather than 10 independently broken links. **Not confirmed as a real break.** The final destinations were never resolved, so link health is unverified.
- **Only 12 of 51 links were probed.** The other 39 were skipped by design.
- **Every link is wrapped in an opaque Attentive redirect.** The destination URL and any UTM parameters can't be read from the source.
- **Two third-party tracking pixels are present:**
  - `carters.attentivemail.com/ss/o/.../ho.gif`
  - `mi.carters.com/p/up/.../o.gif?mi_u=1150794179&mi_ecmp=11_11_2026_CAR_WED_NOON_LARGEAUDIENCE`
  - The second pixel carries what looks like a per-recipient identifier (`mi_u`) in the query string. This is normal for tracking but worth knowing.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in `<head>`. This is confirmed in the source.
- **Linked images have no usable alt text.** For these CTAs the link text resolves to the image filename, for example `[image: MzczMjo6Q0FSXzEwMjAyNl9TRUNPTkRBUllfMy5n]`. They are on `d3ptznaucccg76.cloudfront.net`:
  - the secondary banner (`...Q0FSXzEwMjAyNl9TRUNPTkRBUllfMy5naWY...`)
  - `SERVICES_BANNER_01` through `_04_v3`
  - `264477f43986d8bb.png` (`mi.carters.com`)
  - For screen-reader and images-off users, the destination of those links is unlabeled.
- **The `ho.gif` and `o.gif` alt warnings are a false positive.** `ho.gif` already has `alt=""`, and both are 1×1 tracking pixels, so there is no real issue.
- **"Display images to show real-time content"** is a live-content dependency. With images blocked, that module has no fallback.
- **Positive:** `lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` on layout tables and a viewport meta tag are all present. The mobile breakpoint is 660px. The Google Fonts `<link>` is wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]>`, so Outlook skips it.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible HTML contains no unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`).

## 5. Compliance
- **Plain-text part is 0 characters (FAIL).** There is no `text/plain` alternative. That hurts deliverability and accessibility. The relay may have dropped it, so check the original send.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` were not found (WARN).** The QA notes say the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. Without them, RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` is missing (WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment can't be assessed from this capture. The sender domain is `marketing.carters.com`.
- **Footer unsubscribe and address:** not visible in the truncated HTML, so not verified. The unsubscribe link was reportedly included in probing.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. Every destination sits behind an Attentive redirect that returned 403 to the probe, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment could not be checked. The `mi_ecmp=11_11_2026_CAR_WED_NOON_LARGEAUDIENCE` campaign tag on the pixel shows the campaign is tagged internally.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the Attentive redirects with a browser-like User-Agent, or resolve them manually, to confirm the 10 "403" links. Treat them as unverified, not broken.
2. Add descriptive alt text to every linked image, such as "Shop 50–60% off site & store". Keep `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
3. Fill in the `<title>`, for example with the subject line.
4. Add a `text/plain` multipart alternative.
5. Check the raw source message for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and for SPF/DKIM/DMARC results, since the relay may have hidden them.
6. Capture the final redirect destinations to audit UTM consistency with the landing pages.
