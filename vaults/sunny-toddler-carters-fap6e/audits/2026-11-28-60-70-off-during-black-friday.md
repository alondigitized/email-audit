# 60-70% off during Black Friday 🔥
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-28**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, loud Black Friday send. The pastel-holographic hero puts "60-70% OFF almost everything" and a "lowest prices of the year" badge up top, under a red free-shipping banner. The eye lands on the number first. Below it come priced product tiles, a BOGO jeans block and a clearance panel, and the layout holds together with no visible render bugs.
- It's also the fourth near-identical Black Friday send in four days. On 11/26 the headline was "60-70% off (includes clearance)", and 11/27 repeated the $6+ PJ hero. This email repeats that 60-70% message almost word for word and adds nothing new. The hero has no kids or families in it, which is a step back from the PJ and Thanksgiving heroes. The fine print is also heavy: asterisks on the headline, the shipping banner, the BOGO and the clearance panel, plus a long block of exclusions.
- Verdict: a competent, well-built promo that is stale on arrival for anyone who has seen the last few. Fine as a reminder, but no reason to open it over the 11/26 send.

## What's working

- The hero is clear. "60-70% OFF" is the biggest element, with a single "Shop Now" button beneath it.
- The free-shipping banner sits above the hero, so a second offer is visible immediately.
- The product grid shows real was/now prices ($9.00 vs $24.00, $5.50 vs $18.00). That makes the discount concrete.
- The "Shop it all" row gives entry prices ("Bodysuits from $4", "PJs from $7"). The clearance panel's Baby Girl / Toddler Boy / Girl / Boy buttons make navigation easy for a parent.
- The price-match promise and the Rewards strip near the bottom add trust and a loyalty hook.

## What's weak

- The hero has no people. After the family-in-PJs creative of 11/25 and 11/27, this feels generic and emotionally flat.
- It repeats the 11/26 "60-70% off (includes clearance)" message with no new reason to act. There is no deadline and no "ends tonight" or "last day" cue.
- The headline carries an asterisk and a footnote saying "*includes clearance". A reader sees "almost everything" and then the exclusions block ("Little Planet, Skip Hop, select new arrivals..."), which undercuts the claim.
- Three different "Shop Now" buttons all point at the same sale. The BOGO jeans block is a big image with the same generic CTA.
- The product tiles are small and give no sizes, ratings or names. A page of fine print and legal text closes the email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with something new. Add a real deadline ("Ends tonight" or "Cyber Monday extended") or a doorbuster, so the send isn't a re-run of 11/26.
- 2. Put a kid or a family back in the hero, as in the 11/25 PJ send, and keep the big number.
- 3. Make the CTA specific: "Shop 60-70% off baby" instead of "Shop Now". Add a countdown or a clear end date in the hero.
- 4. Cut the headline asterisks to one and move the exclusions summary under the hero.
- 5. Add social proof, such as star ratings on the product tiles or a "top rated" tag, and name the products.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 60-70% off kids' clothes ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Bodysuits from $4, PJs from $7 – Black Friday ends soon`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus free shipping on every order and 70% off clearance.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Buy 1, get 2 free OshKosh jeans – today's deals won't last.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built from a standard table-based template (Attentive ESP with a Movable Ink live-content layer). The head has a viewport meta tag, `lang="en"`, MSO conditionals and a hidden preheader. The automated QA run is mostly inconclusive: all 12 probed links returned 403 and the header checks failed. Those results most likely come from the relay and the scanner, not from the email.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links returned 403.** They all go through `https://carters.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/<token>`, including "Shop Now", "BUY 1, GET 2 FREE* OSHKOSH® JEANS", the brand links (carter's, osh kosh bigosh, little planet, Otter Avenue, Skiphop) and the hero banner.
  - A 100% failure rate across every link, with the same status on every Attentive redirect, points to bot-blocking of the click tracker, not 12 dead links. I can't confirm the links work for real recipients. Treat the result as unverified, not as confirmed breakage.
  - The probe also never reached the final destination, so the real landing pages were not checked.
- **Four links use the anchor text "Display images to show real-time content".** These are the fallback text for the Movable Ink dynamic blocks, and each is a separate tracked Attentive redirect. If an image fails to load, the recipient sees that text as the link label.
- **52 links were skipped.** Footer, social and utility links were deprioritized, so their status is unknown.
- **The probe's link text for the hero banner is the full alt string**, "lowest prices of the year! | black friday | 60-70% OFF* ALMOST EVERYTHING | *inc…". That is long, but it works as a text fallback.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in `<head>`. This is minor, but some clients use it for the tab or preview title.
- **Missing alt text on content images (confirmed).** Several CloudFront images have no alt attribute. Their file names look like `SERVICES_BANNER_01.png`, `…02.png`, `…03.png`, `…04_v3.png` and `SECONDARY_6.jpg`. With images blocked, the service banners and secondary hero are blank for the recipient and unreadable to screen readers.
- **Two QA alt flags are probably tracking pixels:**
  - `ho.gif` is a 1×1 pixel in a hidden `pixel-wrapper` div and already has `alt=""` in the source, so that flag is a false positive.
  - `o.gif` and `264477f43986d8bb.png` are on `mi.carters.com`, the Movable Ink domain. The first looks like a pixel. The second may be live content, and I can't tell from the truncated source whether it needs alt text.
- **Preheader padding** is hidden correctly (`display:none; max-height:0; opacity:0`) and uses the usual ͏ and soft-hyphen filler. No issue.
- **Responsive CSS is present.** The `@media (max-width:660px)` block stacks columns and resizes buttons. No issue.
- **Google Fonts (League Spartan)** is loaded with `<link>` inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. That is correct. Clients that strip `<link>` (Gmail and others) will fall back, and I couldn't see the `font-family` fallback stack in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the truncated source.
- Movable Ink URLs carry per-recipient parameters: `mi_u=1150794179` and `mi_ecmp=11_27_2026_CAR_FRI_PM_ENGAGERS`. The campaign ID suggests an "engagers" segment send. That is expected behavior and not a defect.
- The rest of the body is truncated, so I can't rule out tokens further down.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are not found.** The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. If the headers are really absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require them for senders of this volume.
- **`Authentication-Results` is not found**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is expected through the relay and is a gap in the audit, not a finding against the sender.
- **The plain-text part is 0 characters (FAIL).** This is a real flag in the data, but the relay may also drop the text part. If the text part is truly missing, it hurts spam scoring and accessibility.
- **Unsubscribe link and physical address:** neither is visible in the truncated HTML, so I can't confirm CAN-SPAM compliance from this sample. The QA tool says it always probes the unsubscribe link, and it is not among the 12 failed links I can identify.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters can't be verified.** Every CTA is wrapped in an Attentive redirect, and the 403s blocked resolution, so I can't see the destination URLs or any `utm_*` values.
- **Landing-page alignment** with the "60-70% off", "Buy 1, Get 2 Free* OshKosh jeans" and brand-section claims can't be checked for the same reason.
- **Tracking domains:** the Attentive host is `carters.e.attentivemail.com`, the open pixel is on `carters.attentivemail.com`, and the Movable Ink host is `mi.carters.com`. Having `e.` and non-`e.` Attentive subdomains is fine, but it is a second host to include in deliverability and link-allowlist checks.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-run link validation** with a browser-like user agent, or follow redirects from a real inbox, to get the final URLs. Then check UTM values and landing pages. Don't treat the 12 × 403 results as real breakage until that's done.
2. **Add alt text** to the CloudFront service banners and secondary hero. Keep `alt=""` on the pure tracking pixels.
3. **Set a `<title>`**, even a short one.
4. **Check the raw message source** at the ESP, not the relay copy, for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, a plain-text part, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment.
5. **Improve the Movable Ink fallback.** Replace "Display images to show real-time content" with descriptive alt text or a static fallback image for each dynamic block.
6. **Capture the full HTML** (this one was truncated) to verify the footer unsubscribe link and physical address, and to scan for unresolved tokens.
