# Ending soon 🚨 50% off* site & store
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, confident Black Friday teaser — the holographic-foil diagonal, black hero panel, and "(super) Early Black Friday! 50% OFF* SITE & STORE" copy land immediately and the flat-lay product shot (OshKosh, Little Planet, Skip Hop pieces) reinforces the multi-brand portfolio without feeling cluttered. It's functionally the same creative Carter's ran on 2026-09-25 ("🎉 Early Black Friday - 50% off* site & store") — same hero treatment, same 50% off site & store claim — just with a new subject line pushing urgency ("Ending soon") instead of a teaser angle.
- The problem is the subject/body mismatch: "Ending soon" implies a closing deadline, but nothing in the visible creative — not the hero, not any banner — shows a countdown, date, or "last chance" marker. That's the same subject-vs-hero disconnect that dragged the 09-26 and 09-27 sends down; here it's milder since the offer itself (50% off) is still front and center, but the urgency claim itself is unsubstantiated.
- Below the fold this is a solid, well-organized catalog send — grid of jeans/basics, a clearly labeled "Early Black Friday Deals" row with real starting prices ($5, $8, $8), a bold 70% off clearance module with tidy category buttons, and a "now open near you" store locator. No render bugs. Overall a competent, on-brand execution let down by a subject line that oversells urgency the body doesn't back up.

## What's working

- Hero is unmissable: black background, holographic foil diagonal, large white "50% OFF* SITE & STORE" type — best-in-class visual hierarchy for this brand's recent run.
- "Early Black Friday Deals" section gives concrete starting prices (Bodysuits from $5, Cozy PJs from $8, Lounge from $8) — genuinely useful, specific info.
- Clearance module (up to 70% off, "New Markdowns Added") is bold and well-labeled with clear category shortcut buttons (Baby Girl/Boy, Toddler, Girl, Boy).
- No layout breakage, broken images, or overlapping text anywhere in the render.
- Store locator block ("Now open near you") with address and Get Directions button adds a relevant local-relevance touch.

## What's weak

- Subject says "Ending soon 🚨" but nothing in the visible email shows a deadline, countdown, or expiration date — the urgency claim is unsupported by the creative.
- This is essentially the same hero/offer as 2026-09-25's "Early Black Friday" send just three days later — repetitive creative for anyone who opened that one.
- No loyalty/Rewards-member pricing or personalized discount banner visible this time (unlike several recent sends with a "you've got 15%" banner) — a missed chance to reward known subscribers.
- Nine product tiles ("We think you'll love these...") are shown with no names, prices, or ratings visible — just bare thumbnails, low information value for a click decision.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the "Ending soon" claim visible in the hero itself — add a date/time-bound line ("Ends Sunday 11:59pm") under the 50% off headline so subject and creative agree; right now the urgency is all in the subject and nowhere in the body.
- 2. Add prices or at least a "from $X" tag under the nine bare product tiles in the "We think you'll love these..." grid — currently zero purchase-decision info there.
- 3. Differentiate this from the 09-25 send — swap the hero product flat-lay or add a distinct headline treatment so repeat openers don't feel like they got the same email twice.
- 4. Bring back the personalized "you've got X% off" banner used in recent sends if this subscriber has an active offer — it consistently reads as the strongest engagement lever in this brand's history.
