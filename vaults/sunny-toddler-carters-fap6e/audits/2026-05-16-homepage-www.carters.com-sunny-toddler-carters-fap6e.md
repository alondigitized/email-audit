# Homepage snapshot · www.carters.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Carter's came ready for Memorial Day weekend — this is a big, bold sale homepage that knows exactly who I am: a mom refreshing my kid's wardrobe every other month because she keeps growing out of everything. The 50% off message is impossible to miss, the toddler clothing is front-and-center, and the whole vibe says "don't overthink it, just shop." It's not particularly warm or storytelling-driven, but honestly? I don't need a story. I need deals on a Tuesday while my daughter naps.

## What's working

- **The 50% off headline is massive and unmistakable** — as a coupon-stacker I clocked it in under a second, and "ENTIRE SITE & STORE" tells me I don't have to hunt for what's included
- **The sticky top banner reinforces the deal** — "Shop season-low prices! 50% off* entire site & store" doubles down so even if I scrolled past the hero I'd remember the offer
- **Toddler summer clothing in the product imagery** — strawberry print, denim shorts, pink romper are spot-on for what I'm currently buying; seeing the actual product category instead of a lifestyle photo with no product is genuinely useful
- **"Lowest prices of the season!" feels like permission to buy right now** — that line does quiet urgency without being pushy
- **Memorial Day framing gives me a reason to act this week**, not "whenever" — I mentally register this as a real sale with a real end

## What's weak

- **"Shop Now" CTA is completely generic** — not "Shop Baby Deals" or "Shop Pajamas" or even "Shop the Sale"; it tells me nothing about where I'll land
- **No free-shipping threshold anywhere visible** — as a mom who adds things to cart over multiple sessions, knowing "free shipping on $35" would keep me in checkout mode; I have to hunt for it
- **No loyalty/Rewarding Moments mention above the fold** — I have a Carter's account and I always check my points before checkout; not seeing this makes me feel like a new visitor
- **Zero urgency mechanism** — "Memorial Day sale" implies an end date but there's no countdown timer, no "sale ends Sunday," nothing that makes me feel like I need to move now versus tomorrow
- **No person in the imagery** — I connect more when I see a kid actually wearing the strawberry top than a flat-lay; a single lifestyle shot would make me click faster

## Recommendations

- **Add a sale countdown timer or end-date line** under "Lowest prices of the season!" — even "Ends Monday, May 26" would be enough to push me from browsing to buying before the weekend
- **Swap "Shop Now" for a category-specific CTA** like "Shop Toddler Girl Deals" or "Shop Baby & Toddler" — I tap faster when I know I'm landing somewhere relevant to me
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold in the top banner** — replace or append "Free shipping on $35+" so the banner does double-duty (deal + delivery) and I stop second-guessing my cart size
- **Add a Rewarding Moments/loyalty badge near the account icon** with current points or a teaser like "You have 240 points — use them today!" — returning moms check this constantly
