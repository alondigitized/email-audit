# Code: LAYERUP 🔓 Extra 20% off $50+
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-07**
## Executive summary

- This is the third LAYERUP send in a row. Since 10-05 the creative has barely moved: the gradient code banner on top, then the dark-green "UP TO 50% OFF*, Go all in on fall" hero. Today's subject, "Code: LAYERUP 🔓 Extra 20% off $50+", is the most direct of the three. It leads with the code and puts the threshold in the subject. But nothing in the render pays off the 🔓 or says why today is different from yesterday.
- The body is a generic catalog. There is a grid of nine unlabeled product tiles (jeans, white bodysuits, black and navy joggers, one pink footed sleeper). None has a price, name, or link cue. The Spider-Man character block is the one specific, appealing item. The red "70% OFF* Clearance" block adds a third offer, so the email carries 20%, 50%, 70% and "from $10" at once. The stacked percentages dilute the code offer.
- Judgement: competent and on-brand, but recycled. A subscriber who saw the 10-05 and 10-06 sends has no new reason to open or click. It lands in the middle of the range.

## What's working

- The gradient banner states the offer plainly: "EXTRA 20% OFF* YOUR $50+ PURCHASE", with the code "LAYERUP" and a chevron link.
- The banner lists the included brands (Little Planet, Otter Avenue, Skip Hop), which clarifies scope.
- The "UP TO 50% OFF*" hero has a large white headline on dark green, and it reads clearly.
- The Character Shop block with the Spider-Man apparel is the strongest visual. It has a concrete "Shop from $10" CTA.
- The clearance block has Baby Girl, Toddler Girl, Girl, Baby Boy, Toddler Boy and Boy buttons, so shoppers can go straight to their size or gender segment.
- The footer is clean: store locator, curbside, Rewards, and app perks.

## What's weak

- The 3x3 product grid has no prices, names, or labels. Nothing tells you what is on sale or by how much.
- The email stacks four numbers: 20%, 50%, 70% and $10. No single offer wins.
- The hero "Shop Now" CTA is generic and ghost-styled, with a thin outline on a dark photo. The banner code is small, light text on a pastel gradient, so the key offer line is easy to miss.
- The creative is almost identical to the 10-05 and 10-06 sends. This is the third day of the same banner and hero.
- The "Now open near you!" Commerce, CA store block is hyper-local and may not apply to most recipients. It adds length, and the legal text below is very long.
- The product images read as flat, near-identical dark denim and joggers. The grid is visually monotonous.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rebuild the hero around one offer. Put "EXTRA 20% OFF $50+" and the code `LAYERUP` in a large copy-to-clipboard-style button, and drop the "up to 50%" swatch. If the 50% message must stay, make it a secondary strip.
- 2. Add price and name to every product tile, for example "Slim jeans $14.40 w/ code", and make each tile link to its product page.
- 3. Cut the grid from nine tiles to six, and group them by category with headers.
- 4. Make the hero CTA specific and filled, such as "Shop 50% off fall styles". Replace generic "Shop Now".
- 5. Add a deadline, such as an end date or "Ends Sunday". The subject implies urgency but the render shows none.
- 6. Rotate the creative. If the code runs for a week, change the hero daily, for example day 1 jeans, day 2 PJs, day 3 character.
- 7. Move the store opening block into a geo-targeted segment.
- **Subject Alt A:** `LAYERUP: extra 20% off $50+ ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your code is live: 20% off fall layers`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Jeans, joggers & Spidey tees. Prices drop at checkout.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stack it on up to 50% off fall styles. Online only.`

## 1. Technical Summary

The QA run's 16% pass rate is mostly 11 probe 403s on Attentive redirect links, which are probably bot-blocking rather than real breakage. The one hard failure is the empty plain-text part.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **11 of 12 probed links returned 403**, all on `https://carters.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...`. The affected links are:
  - the hero CTA ("Extra 20% Off* Your $50+ Purchase")
  - "Up to 50% Off*"
  - the brand tiles (Little Planet, Otter Avenue, Skiphop, OshKosh, Character Shop)
  - the product images
  - the logo
- **Likely false positive:** a uniform 403 across every link on one ESP click-tracking domain, with no 404/5xx mix, points to Attentive rejecting the automated probe. It does not show that subscribers hit a dead link. Treat it as **unconfirmed**, not as a confirmed broken experience.
- **Destinations not verified:** the redirect wrapping hides every landing URL and UTM parameter. Only 12 links were probed and 45 were skipped.
- **Tracking pixels:**
  - `https://mi.carters.com/p/up/745a17bbb73445ad/o.gif?mi_u=1150794179&mi_ecmp=10_07_2026_CAR_WED_AM_ENGAGERS` puts a numeric user ID (`mi_u`) and a segment label (`..._ENGAGERS`) in the URL.
  - A second Attentive open pixel, `.../ho.gif`, is also present, so two separate open-tracking beacons fire.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in `<head>`. Some clients and screen readers use it as the document name.
- **Missing alt text, from the QA data:**
  - The four `SERVICES_BANNER_01–04` images on `d3ptznaucccg76.cloudfront.net` are flagged.
  - So are the `SECONDARY_1.jpg` image and `mi.carters.com/p/rp/264477f43986d8bb.png`.
  - The HTML is truncated, so I could not confirm these in the source.

  - The preheader uses `display:none;max-height:0;opacity:0` with the `͏`/`­` padding characters. That is correct.

## 5. Compliance
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is 0 chars.** A multipart message with no text alternative can hurt spam scoring and breaks text-only and accessibility clients. If the relay is stripping it, it should be confirmed against the raw message.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers. Both are required for Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender compliance. Check the original message headers before treating this as a real gap.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment cannot be assessed from this capture. The From address uses `marketing.carters.com`, a subdomain, which should be checked for alignment.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** the footer was truncated, so neither could be checked. The QA run reports an unsubscribe link was always included in the probe, but no result was shown for it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. All CTAs sit behind Attentive redirects, the probes were blocked, and no landing page or UTM data was captured. The following can be inferred from the visible text only:

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-probe the redirect links** with a browser-like user agent, or resolve the full redirect chain in a real browser. This will confirm whether the 403s are bot-blocking and will reveal the final landing URLs and UTMs.
