# 40% off their favorite characters 💙
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-03**
## Executive summary

- This is the Nov 3 closer of Stellar Sleep Week, and it's the weakest version of the message. The subject promises "favorite characters," and the hero delivers: bow-wrapped Snoopy, Mickey and Grinch-style PJs in a nice flat-lay. But the "CHOOSE YOUR CHARACTER" block only gives a small ghost button reading "Shop 40% off*". Nothing tells me which characters, and the nine-product grid underneath has no prices or names.
- The bigger problem is the countdown timer at the top. It reads 00 days : 00 hours : 00 minutes : 00 seconds, so the one urgency device in the email shows the sale as already over. The banner says "Only from Oct 29–Nov 3," so this is the last day. A zeroed clock makes the send look stale instead of urgent.
- The rest of the email is a catalog. Below the PJs come "Shop by Category" (Tops from $6, Pants from $8, Sets from $11), a red "Up to 70% off clearance" block and a store promo. This is the 7th or so Carter's send in a week. The 11/2 "Tops & bottoms as low as $6" and 11/1 "Up to 60% off DreamPlush PJs" emails covered the same ground, and this one adds nothing new. It's competent but redundant.

## What's working

- The hero flat-lay of character PJs with red and green velvet bows is warm, seasonal and on-subject.
- The "Stellar Sleep Week" banner keeps the campaign consistent with the 10/30 to 11/1 sends.
- The 40% offer is concrete and matches the subject line.
- Category tiles with starting prices ($6, $8, $11) are scannable and give a low entry price.
- The "Up to 70% off clearance" block is bold, high-contrast and easy to find.

## What's weak

- The countdown timer reads all zeros. It signals "expired" on what should be the last-chance day.
- The primary CTA, "Shop 40% off*", is a thin outlined button with small grey text. It is the weakest-looking element on the page.
- The nine-product grid has no names, prices or character labels, so I can't tell which products are the "favorite characters."
- Too many competing offers: 40% off PJs, $6 tops, 70% clearance and 12 days of deals under $12. The hierarchy fades after the hero.
- There's no social proof, no reviews and no member-specific pricing. Rewards appears only as a footer icon strip.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix or remove the countdown. If the clock is live, make it show the real time left. If it can't, replace it with static text like "Last day: ends tonight".
- 2. Make the hero itself the link. Give it a solid, full-width CTA like "Shop Grinch & Snoopy PJs – 40% off", and drop the thin ghost button.
- 3. Label the grid. Put a character name and sale price on each tile, such as "Grinch 2-pc $14.40", and link each tile to its product.
- 4. Cut the second half. Pick PJs plus one secondary offer instead of also running categories, clearance and the store promo.
- 5. Put Rewards benefits near the top, for example "Members: extra points on PJs".
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last day: 40% off Grinch & Snoopy PJs 💙`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Ends tonight – character PJs 40% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sleep Week wraps up at midnight. Grab Grinch, Snoopy & Mickey sets before they're gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Matching holiday PJs for the whole family, plus up to 70% off clearance.`

# Technical Audit: "40% off their favorite characters 💙" (Carter's Sleep Week)

The HTML is a table-based, responsive build with a preheader and a `lang="en"` attribute. The QA run still scored 16% pass: all 12 probed tracked links returned 403, there is no plain-text part, and several images lack alt text. The 403s and the header warnings are probably artifacts of the probe and the AgentMail relay, so they need manual confirmation.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **12 of 12 probed links returned 403.** Every one is an `https://carters.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...` click-tracking redirect. The affected link texts are:
  - "carter's"
  - "osh kosh bigosh"
  - "little planet"
  - "Otter Avenue"
  - "Skiphop"
  - "INTRODUCING Stellar Sleep Week…"
  - the hero image link `[image: MzczMjo6Q0FSXzEwMjkyNl9IRVJPXzUuanBnOjoy]`
  - 4× "Display images to show real-time content"
- **Likely cause:** a uniform 403 across all Attentive redirects points to bot or user-agent blocking of the QA prober, not 12 independently broken links. I can't confirm that from the data, so retest with a browser-like UA or a real click-through before calling them broken.
- **Visible link text on four links is the Movable Ink fallback copy.** "Display images to show real-time content" appears as link text 4 times. Each of those links wraps a live-content image, and the fallback text would show if images are blocked or the dynamic content fails to load.
- **Probe coverage is partial:** 52 links were skipped, so the footer, social and unsubscribe links were not validated here.
- **Tracking pixels:**
  - `carters.attentivemail.com/ss/o/.../ho.gif` is the Attentive open pixel (1×1).

  - These are expected, but the email runs two tracking stacks.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in `<head>`. This is minor, but it affects web-view and screen-reader context.
- **Images missing alt text (QA WARN):**
  - `o.gif` (the `mi.carters.com` Movable Ink pixel)
  - `264477f43986d8bb.png`
  - the hero image
  - four `SERVICES_BANNER_01–04` banners
  - The hero and banners sit inside links, so with no usable alt the link has no accessible name. The `[image: MzczMjo6…]` link text confirms the hero's accessible name is the base64 filename.
- **`ho.gif` is a likely false positive.** The source shows `alt=""` on it, which is correct for a decorative tracking pixel.
- **No plain-text alternative.** QA reports "Text version is 0 chars" (FAIL). This affects accessibility and spam scoring. It may also be a relay-capture artifact, so check the raw MIME.
- **Dark-mode, MSO and responsive code look fine in the visible source.** The source includes MSO conditionals, the `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` reset, and a `@media (max-width:660px)` block. The source is truncated, so the remaining body wasn't evaluated.
- **Web font:** the Google Fonts `<link>` (League Spartan) is correctly wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. Clients that strip `<link>` will fall back to system fonts.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The only subscriber-level value is `mi_u=1150794179` in the Movable Ink URLs, which is a user ID that resolved normally. The `mi_ecmp` value suggests an "ENGAGERS" segment send on 11/03.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found (WARN).** QA notes it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. Verify against the raw message headers.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found (WARN).** One-click unsubscribe is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results not found (WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture. Sender domain is `marketing.carters.com`.
- **Footer content (physical address, unsubscribe link) is not in the truncated source or the probed set**, so I can't assess it. The QA tool says an unsubscribe link is always probed, and none appears among the 12 results.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- Destination URLs and UTM parameters can't be verified. Every probed link is wrapped in an Attentive redirect, and the probe got 403 before reaching the landing page.
- I can't confirm that UTMs survive the redirect or that the landing page matches the Sleep Week offer.
- Several links are brand-level (Osh Kosh, Little Planet, Otter Avenue, Skiphop). Whether they land on the intended pages is unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run link checks with a real browser UA, or follow the redirect chain manually, to separate probe blocking from real breakage. Do this first, since it drives the 16% score.
2. Add a plain-text MIME part, or confirm one exists in the raw message.
3. Check raw headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
4. Add descriptive `alt` text to the hero and the four service banners. Use `alt=""` on `o.gif` and the other decorative pixels.
5. Fill in `<title>`.
6. Make sure the Movable Ink fallback text isn't what recipients see when images are off. Use meaningful link text or alt.
