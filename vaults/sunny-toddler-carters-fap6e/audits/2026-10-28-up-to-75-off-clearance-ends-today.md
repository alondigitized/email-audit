# Up to 75% off clearance ends today ‼️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-28**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent last-day clearance send with a muddled top fold. The red "UP TO 75% OFF* LAST-CHANCE CLEARANCE" block matches the subject and has a clean grid of audience buttons (Baby Girl, Toddler Girl, Girl, Baby Boy, Toddler Boy, Boy). A recognizable brand sends a real offer on the right day.
- The problem is the stack above it. The leftover "cozy flash / up to 50% + extra 20% / code COZY20" banner sits first and competes with the 75% clearance hero. This follows "ONLINE ONLY !! Up to 50% off + extra 20% ☁️" on 10/27 and the earlier COZY20 sends on 10/24, 10/25 and 10/26, so a subscriber sees a fifth overlapping promo in five days. Two offers with different rules, plus a body that says "While supplies last" against a subject that says "ends today", dilute the urgency.
- The back half is generic. The product grid shows plain white onesies, black joggers and jeans with no prices, names or sale badges, so it reads as a catalog rather than clearance. The Grinch block is a pleasant seasonal beat, but "Shop from $10" is unrelated to the clearance. Judgement: a solid 6, with real upside if the email leads with one offer.

## What's working

- The red 75% OFF clearance block is high contrast and instantly readable, and it matches the subject.
- The six audience buttons (Baby Girl through Boy) give fast, clear segmentation paths.
- The Grinch "From the Big Screen" block ties to the season and gives a distinct reason to scroll.
- The loyalty strip at the bottom (Carter's Rewards, cardholder perks, in-app points) keeps member benefits visible.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The "cozy flash" banner sits above the clearance hero, so two offers compete for the eye.
- The product grid has no prices, no "was/now" and no sale badges, so it doesn't show the clearance value.
- The body says "While supplies last" while the subject says "ends today". The only dated deadline in the body, "Ends Wednesday", belongs to the other promo.
- The legal footer is very long and repeats clauses. It includes a "BUY 1, GET 1 FREE HOLIDAY" disclaimer for an offer not shown in the email.
- This is the fifth near-identical promo in five days after the 10/24 to 10/27 COZY20 sends, which makes the urgency feel routine.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the 75% clearance block the hero and move the COZY20 banner below it, or drop it for this send. One offer per email.
- 2. Put "Ends tonight" with a specific time inside the red hero, so the body matches the subject.
- 3. Replace the generic grid with real clearance items showing a price, a strikethrough and a "% off" badge. Link each to its product page.
- 4. Add one primary button ("Shop clearance") above the audience buttons.
- 5. Trim the footer disclaimers to the offers actually shown.
- 6. Space out the cadence. Skip a day after four sends in a row.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has a sound responsive structure (`lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` tables, MSO conditionals, preheader). The QA run shows 12 of 12 probed links returning 403, no plain-text part, and several content images with no alt text. Header-level compliance and authentication could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links returned 403.** They are all Attentive click-tracking redirects on `carters.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...`. They cover the logo ("carter's"), the brand tiles (osh kosh bigosh, little planet, Otter Avenue, Skiphop), the "cozy flash" banner, "Shop from $10", and five product images (bodysuits, joggers, jeans).
- **The 403s are probably bot-blocking, not dead links.** A uniform 403 across every redirect from one ESP domain usually means the tracker rejects the QA fetcher's user agent or IP. A broken campaign would more likely show 404s or 410s for individual links.
- **I can't confirm the links work.** The probe never reached a destination, so landing-page status is unverified. Re-probe with a browser-like UA, or follow one link manually, before treating this as a real failure.
- **Coverage is partial.** 45 of 57 links were skipped, including the unsubscribe link, which is meant to be always included. The result doesn't say whether it was probed.
- **There are two tracking pixels.**
  - Attentive: `carters.attentivemail.com/ss/o/.../ho.gif`.
  - Carter's own: `mi.carters.com/p/up/745a17bbb73445ad/o.gif?mi_u=1150794179&mi_ecmp=10_28_2026_CAR_WED_AM_ENGAGERS`.
  - The second URL carries a per-recipient ID (`mi_u`) and a campaign/segment name (`..._AM_ENGAGERS`) in plain query params. This is a minor privacy and hygiene concern, not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
