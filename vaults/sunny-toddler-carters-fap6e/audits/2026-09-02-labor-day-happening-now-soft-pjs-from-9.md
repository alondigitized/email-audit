# ❤️ Labor Day happening now - Soft PJs from $9 🧸
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-02**
## Executive summary

- This is the strongest hero Carter's has run this week, and by a wide margin — a real, well-styled flat-lay of two actual PJ sets (sage stripe zip-up and blue paper-airplane print) replaces the recycled folded-laundry-pile photo that's carried every send since 8/26, echoing the pivot that made 8/31's "cozy PJs from $9" the week's best performer. It's genuinely on-message for a parent shopping bedtime basics, and the "Just for You" personalized grid plus a distinct "Character Shop" (new PAW Patrol tie-in) give this send more substance than most of the week's single-offer blasts.
- Where it falls down is structure: the PJ hero and headline promise "PJs from $9," but the very next section is jeans, bodysuits, and rompers — not pajamas — so the email abandons its own hook almost immediately. There's also no discount percentage or urgency cue anywhere, just a soft "Sale" badge and a flat $9 price claim that isn't visually reinforced by product pricing shown anywhere in the grid. Solid creative, undisciplined merchandising — ship the hero, fix the follow-through.

## What's working

- Real, well-lit product photography for the hero (two distinct PJ sets) — a clear step up from the generic sunset-gradient/laundry-pile creative that dominated 8/25–8/30.
- Clear single primary CTA ("Shop Now") directly under a concrete price claim ("PJs from $9").
- "Just for You" personalization section signals segmentation, even if generic.
- Distinct sub-brand/character tie-in (PAW Patrol, "just in time for the big-screen premiere") adds a timely, non-generic reason to click.
- Local store CTA with address and "Get Directions" is a nice omnichannel touch not seen in prior audited sends.

## What's weak

- Hero promises pajamas; the first product grid below it is jeans and bodysuits, not PJs — a mismatch between hook and follow-through.
- No visible discount percentage, coupon code, or deadline anywhere in the email — just a static "Sale" graphic, which is weaker than the "50% off" framing used in 8/26–8/30 sends.
- Product tiles throughout ("Just for You," "Shop It All") have no visible prices, sizes, or names — pure image grids with no purchase-decision info.
- "The Character Shop" PAW Patrol section undercuts urgency by shifting attention away from the Labor Day sale message entirely.

## Recommendations

- 1. Reorder or re-theme the first product grid so it's actually pajamas/sleepwear — right now the email breaks its own promise one scroll past the hero. This is the single biggest fix.
- 2. Add a visible discount (e.g., "up to 50% off" or "$5 tees, PJs from $9") directly on or near the hero badge — the current "Sale" tag carries no number.
- 3. Put prices under at least the "Just for You" tiles; a personalized grid with zero pricing information does little to drive clicks.
- 4. Add a real deadline ("Ends tonight" / "Sale ends 9/7") near the hero CTA — nothing in the render signals urgency beyond the word "Sale."
- 5. Consider trimming or moving "The Character Shop" lower — it's good creative but currently dilutes the PJ/Labor Day narrative right when the reader should be moving toward checkout.
