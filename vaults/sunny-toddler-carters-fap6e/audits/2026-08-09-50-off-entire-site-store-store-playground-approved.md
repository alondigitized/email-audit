# 50% off* ENTIRE site & store store 👉 Playground approved ✅
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-09**
## Executive summary

- This send is a mashup of two creatives Carter's has already run this week: the hopscotch-chalk-sidewalk hero (from 8/5, 8/6, 8/7) sits at the top, and the lavender-denim "BUY 1 GET 2 FREE" stool shot from 8/7 and 8/8 reappears halfway down as a second module. Nothing here is new creative — it's a remix of the two most-repeated assets of the week, bolted together with a fresh "UP TO 70% OFF CLEARANCE" red banner and a store-locator block.
- The subject line is the worst offense: "50% off* ENTIRE site & store store 👉 Playground approved ✅" has a visible typo ("store store"), a random emoji pointer, and a checkmark tacked on — it reads like it was assembled from fragments of two different subject tests without a final proofread pass. That typo alone would make me question the sender's quality control before I even open it.
- The body itself is competently laid out — clear hero, grid of product shots, denim callout, clearance banner, store locator — but the cadence problem is now impossible to ignore: this is the fourth email in five days leaning on the same 50%-off-entire-site offer and the same two hero images, just resequenced. Judgment: solid production, weak subject line, and creative fatigue that's starting to show.

## What's working

- Hero is clean and legible: "50% OFF ENTIRE SITE & STORE" with clear asterisk disclosure sits right on the chalk-hopscotch background with a visible "Shop Now" button.
- Product grid (9 items) gives a real assortment preview — pants, bodysuits, rompers, denim, sleepwear — without feeling cluttered.
- Clearance banner ("UP TO 70% OFF" with category buttons: Baby Girl, Toddler Girl, Girl, Baby Boy, Toddler Boy, Boy) gives a second, distinct, easy-to-scan offer with direct category navigation.
- Store locator module with address and "Get Directions" button is a nice local-relevance touch not seen in prior audited sends.
- No render bugs — images load, text doesn't overlap, layout holds together end to end.

## What's weak

- Subject line has a visible typo ("site & store store") and a redundant emoji/checkmark stack — this reads as an unedited draft, not a shipped subject.
- Hero image is the third reuse of the hopscotch-chalk-sidewalk creative this week (8/5, 8/6, 8/7) and the denim stool shot is a rerun from just yesterday (8/8) and 8/7 — zero new hero photography.
- Same 50%-off-entire-site headline offer that's run essentially unchanged since 8/5 — four sends in five days on the identical top-line number.
- "BUY 1, GET 2 FREE" denim module duplicates yesterday's badge and imagery almost pixel-for-pixel, undermining any sense this is a distinct send.
- Two competing "biggest deal" claims in one email (50% off entire site vs. up to 70% off clearance) dilute the primary CTA — the eye doesn't know which offer is the one to act on.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the subject line typo and cut the emoji stacking — this is the single highest-impact, lowest-effort fix and it's currently shipping broken.
- 2. Retire the hopscotch-chalk hero for at least one send; it's now appeared 3 times in 5 days and readers who've opened more than one of these will recognize it instantly.
- 3. Pick one hero offer per email — either lead with 50% off entire site or 70% off clearance, not both — and demote the other to a smaller module.
- 4. Rotate the denim "Buy 1 Get 2 Free" placement or swap it for a different back-to-school category (shoes, backpacks) since it just ran yesterday.
- 5. If the store-locator module is new, keep it — it's genuine incremental value and differentiates this send from the rest of the week's run.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 70% off clearance + 50% off everything else`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns just added across baby, toddler & kids`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Buy 1, get 2 free denim — while sizes last`
