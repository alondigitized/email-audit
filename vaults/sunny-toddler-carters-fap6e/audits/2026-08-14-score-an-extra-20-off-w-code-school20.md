## What's weak

- Hero has no photography, no kids, no seasonal cue — a flat fabric-texture background is the weakest visual asset compared to the sweatshirt/pumpkin heroes from the two prior days.
- The 9-tile product grid has zero labels, names, or prices — just cropped fabric/clothing photos, so there's nothing to click toward specifically.
