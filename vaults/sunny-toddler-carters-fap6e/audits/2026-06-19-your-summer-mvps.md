# YOUR SUMMER MVPs 😎🙌
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-19**
## Executive summary

- Carter's is back the day after the June 18 "CLEARANCE FROM $3.99 🚨🚨" send, and this time they've swapped the clearance-first hierarchy for a warm summer-staples brand play. The hero is genuinely strong — a baby in a floral outfit with a bow headband, outdoors, with "summer staples" overlaid — and the structure flows logically: hero → product category grid (dresses from $8, rompers from $9, sets from $12) → $3.99 clearance → store finder → rewards. Clean render, no bugs.
- The problem is the subject line. "YOUR SUMMER MVPs 😎🙌" is a theme, not an offer. After a week of EXTRA20 codes, a "50% OFF ENTIRE SITE" step-change, and then a hard-clearance anchor at $3.99, regressing to vague MVP language is a step backward. The subscriber who opened June 18's 🚨🚨 clearance email gets served the same $3.99 clearance section here — positioned lower in the funnel, with "More markdowns added!" as the only freshness signal.
- The cadence math is punishing. This is roughly the ninth or tenth Carter's email since June 9. The summer-staples creative is charming, but "YOUR SUMMER MVPs" will land in inboxes already fatigued by the daily drum, and it offers nothing to make opening feel urgent or rewarded.

## What's working

- Baby hero image is emotionally spot-on for the parent audience — strong visual hook.
- Age/gender clearance segmentation (Baby Girl / Toddler Girl / Girl / Baby Boy / Toddler Boy / Boy) makes it fast to navigate to the right shop.
- Price anchors in the summer haul ($8 dresses, $9 rompers, $12 sets) give the mid-body section real utility.
- "More markdowns added!" on the clearance tier gives the recycled $3.99 offer at least a thin freshness signal vs. June 18.
- Clean render — no overlapping text, broken images, or layout breaks.

## What's weak

- "YOUR SUMMER MVPs" subject is all-caps + vague — no price, no category, no offer to open for.
- The $3.99 clearance section is substantively identical to June 18's lead offer; two consecutive clearance sends dilutes both.
- "Shop Now" CTA is the most generic possible button copy — competing directly against every other email in the inbox.
- No time deadline or code anywhere in the visible body, so there's no reason to click today vs. tomorrow.
- The mid-body product grid images are small and low-detail — hard to feel excited about specific items without being able to see them clearly.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead the subject with the price anchor**, not the theme. "MVPs" is a wrapper; $3.99 is the hook. Swap them.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Summer MVPs from $3.99 👀`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New markdowns: dresses $8, rompers $9, sets $12`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `More markdowns just added — stock up before sizes go`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Baby Girl · Toddler Girl · Girl · Baby Boy · sets from $12`
- 2. **Give the clearance a "new additions" hook** — show a specific new item or category that wasn't there June 18 rather than just "More markdowns added!" That copy is invisible to skimmers.
- 3. **Add a deadline.** Even "through the weekend" or "while supplies last" on the clearance section creates a reason to act now instead of tomorrow.
- 4. **Promote the $3.99 higher in the hierarchy** (or make the summer haul price anchors the primary CTA hook) — right now the best offer is buried below the fold.
- 5. **Give "Shop Now" a modifier** — "Shop Summer MVPs," "Shop Clearance," or "Shop Girls" depending on the segment. Cost: zero. Lift: real.

### 1. Technical Summary

Email is built on a standard Attentive template with MSO/VML Outlook conditionals correctly applied. ### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Open pixel hidden in suppressed container — medium risk**

```html
<div class="pixel-wrapper" style="display:none;max-height:0px;max-width:0px;opacity:0;overflow:hidden;">
  <img src="https://carters.attentivemail.com/ss/o/u001.6x9r1fsrCn5_kOCShC5EZg/4rm/9r26YhoFRFyn56XLwSmzYg/ho.gif" .../>
</div>
```

The tracking pixel's parent `div` is fully suppressed (`display:none` + `max-height:0` + `overflow:hidden`). Clients that respect CSS before prefetching images (some WebKit-based mobile clients, strict corporate proxies) may skip this load entirely, undercounting opens. Standard practice is to place the 1×1 pixel directly in the `<body>` stream with size/opacity suppression on the `<img>` itself, not by hiding the container.

**Click tracking:** Cannot fully assess — HTML is truncated. Confirm all CTA `href` values pass through Attentive's click-tracking domain before resolving to `carters.com`.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Google Fonts `<link>` unsupported in major clients — high risk**

```html
<!--[if !mso]><!-->
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=League+Spartan:wght@400;500;600;700&display=swap" rel="stylesheet">
<!--<![endif]-->
```

`League Spartan` loads only in Apple Mail and a small subset of clients. Gmail (all platforms), Outlook, and Yahoo Mail strip `<head>` `<link>` tags — these clients will fall back to whatever the browser/OS default is. No explicit `font-family` fallback stack is declared in the visible CSS. If headline sizing is tuned for League Spartan's metrics, fallback rendering may cause text overflow or truncation, especially in the large-font blocks (`.row-9` headings scaled to 45px on mobile).

**Empty `<title>` tag**

```html
<title></title>
```

Some clients (Samsung Mail, older Outlook.com) surface the `<title>` as tab text or in accessibility trees. An empty value is benign but sloppy; `Carter's — Summer MVPs` or similar is low effort.

**MSO/VML conditionals:** Correctly implemented. `<o:PixelsPerInch>96</o:PixelsPerInch>` and `<w:DontUseAdvancedTypographyReadingMail/>` are both present.

**Preheader padding:** Mixed Unicode techniques — U+034F (`͏`) and soft-hyphens (`­`) are both used. Soft hyphens can render as visible `-` in certain older clients (BlackBerry, some Outlook versions). Consistent use of U+034F or zero-width non-joiners is preferred.

**Tracking pixel `alt` attribute:** Correctly set to `alt=""`. No issue.

**Role attributes:** Outer `<table>` carries `role="presentation"` — correct. Cannot assess inner blocks from truncated source.
