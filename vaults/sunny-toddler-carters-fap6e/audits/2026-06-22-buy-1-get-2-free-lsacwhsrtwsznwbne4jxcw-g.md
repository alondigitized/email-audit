# BUY 1, GET 2 FREE
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-22**
## Executive summary

- Carter's repeats the June 20th "Buy 1, get 2 free tops & shorts" offer almost verbatim — same mechanic, same category, two days later. The creative swaps in a tropical-summer lifestyle hero (palm trees, pink-gradient sky) and pairs it with a second-act clearance section ("FROM $3.99"), so there are actually two distinct offers competing for attention in one email. That dual-offer structure is the email's central tension: the hero is aspirational-summer, the body drop is discount-depth, and neither fully wins.
- The product grid itself is competent — six clear shots of shorts and polos organized visually, with a pink dress tucked at the bottom — and the category nav (Baby Girl / Toddler Girl / Girl / Baby Boy / Toddler Boy / Boy) is smart for a catalog brand. The clearance $3.99 red block is aggressive but it works as a scroll stopper.
- Where this falls flat is cadence and freshness. A parent who opened the June 20th "Buy 1, get 2 free tops & shorts 🔥" send will feel like they're looking at a near-identical email. The all-caps subject with no emoji and no deadline doesn't differentiate this from that send. The overall execution is solid but the strategic decision to re-run the same offer so quickly chips away at the credibility of each individual send.

## What's working

- **Hero offer legibility** — "BUY 1, GET 2 FREE / summer's best tops & shorts" is immediately readable against the gradient background; no ambiguity about what you're getting.
- **Category nav** — the six Baby Girl / Toddler Girl / Girl / Baby Boy / Toddler Boy / Boy tiles are exactly what a parent needs to quickly route to the right aisle.
- **Clearance block contrast** — the red $3.99 section creates a clear visual break and adds a second value layer for bargain shoppers who missed the June 18th clearance send.
- **Carter's Rewards logo** — the loyalty callout in the lower third reinforces member value without cluttering the hero.
- **Product grid** — clean, well-lit product shots that show the actual merchandise in the offer category.

## What's weak

- **Same offer, two days later** — June 20th was "Buy 1, get 2 free tops & shorts." This is June 22nd with the same mechanic and same category. No new angle, no escalation, no "extended by popular demand" framing.
- **Dual-offer dilution** — the BUY 1 GET 2 FREE hero and the $3.99 clearance block fight each other; no clear winner, and neither is given enough space to breathe.
- **No deadline visible** — the offer has no expiration anywhere in the hero or body copy, removing the one lever that would create urgency.
- **Generic lifestyle hero** — the palm-tree/sunset stock imagery has no Carter's product in it; it reads like a summer travel ad rather than a kids' clothing brand.
- **"Shop Now" CTA** — same generic copy that appears in every Carter's send; no specificity to the tops-and-shorts context.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a deadline to the hero** — "Ends Sunday" or "Through June 24 only" would single-handedly lift click urgency without any other change. The June 20th send had the same gap; two misses in a row.
- 2. **Pick one offer and own it** — Either lead BUY 1 GET 2 FREE all the way through, or lead clearance. Stacking both splits the click intent and trains subscribers that there's always a bigger offer below the fold.
- 3. **Change the hero image** — Put an actual Carter's kid in the hero. The tropical scene looks like clip art. The product grid already shows the merchandise; lead with it.
- 4. **Differentiate from June 20th** — Add a new-arrivals angle, a "second chance" frame, or expand the category (add swimwear) so the offer doesn't feel like a copy-paste.
- 5. **Sharpen the CTA** — "Shop Tops & Shorts" over "Shop Now" routes intent and matches the offer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Buy 1, Get 2 Free — ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your last chance: B1G2 on summer must-haves`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Tops, shorts & more — summer's best styles, two free when you buy one.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stock up now: buy one top or short, get two free through the weekend.`
