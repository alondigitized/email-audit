# 50-60% off* site & store ⏳
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- This is Carter's falling back to a generic clearance push in the middle of its own 12 Days of Deals campaign. The hero says "ENDS WEDNESDAY! 50-60% OFF* SITE & STORE" on a pastel holographic panel. The offer is clear and the eye lands on it first. But the sender name still reads "12 Days of Deals," and the hero says nothing about the daily deal. The campaign is relegated to a "get ready for upcoming deals!" strip. The Day 5 send earlier today was a punchy $10 multipack bodysuit offer, and this one feels like a step down from it.
- The body drifts into holiday PJs, a print swatch grid and a 70%-off clearance block. These give three competing messages after the hero, and the PJ and print sections have no price. The "coming 11.09 / 11.10 / 11.11" teaser is the most interesting part. It gives a reason to come back, but it is small and sits below the hero. The 50-60% deal is also almost identical to the 11/05 "ENDS WEDNESDAY" send, so this reads as a repeat, not news.
- Verdict: competent and readable, but repetitive and diluted. It has a strong, credible deadline, but a subscriber who just saw four daily-deal emails gets a recycled message.

## What's working

- The hero is high-contrast, with "50-60% OFF*" in large black type on the pastel panel and a visible Shop Now button.
- "Ends Wednesday" is a credible, specific deadline.
- The teaser row ($12 World's Best Overalls, $12 character crews, $8 PurelySoft™) gives specific prices and a reason to return.
- The footer has a store locator, directions, curbside and rewards callouts that help a parent plan a trip.
- The layout is clean, with consistent black-outlined buttons and no broken images.

## What's weak

- The sender name promises "12 Days of Deals," but the hero doesn't mention the campaign or today's deal. "Shop Today's Deal" is buried mid-email and doesn't say what the deal is.
- The holiday PJs and the print swatch grid have no prices and no clear hero product. The swatch circles are cropped and some text is cut off ("FE…", "NAVI…").
- Three discount tiers compete: 50-60% off, "up to 70% off clearance" and the $12 and $8 teasers.
- The 50-60% hero repeats the 11/05 send almost word for word, so the cadence feels stale.
- The asterisked exclusions are tucked into small type at the bottom, so the offer feels less clear than the hero suggests.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero the Day 6 daily deal, or at least pair it with the 50-60% off, so the 12 Days sender name pays off. Put a specific product and price in the hero.
- 2. Move the "coming 11.09 / 11.10 / 11.11" teaser directly under the hero. It is the strongest return-visit hook.
- 3. Cut the print swatch grid, or give it a price and a headline such as "PJs from $X".
- 4. Fold the 70% clearance block into one line, or drop it, so there is one primary message.
- 5. Add a short exclusions note near the hero ("Excludes Little Planet, Skip Hop…") so the offer reads as more honest.
