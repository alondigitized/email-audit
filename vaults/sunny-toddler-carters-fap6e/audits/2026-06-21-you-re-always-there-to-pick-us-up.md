# You’re always there to pick us up 💙
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-21**
## Executive summary

- This is Carter's Father's Day brand warmth send — dated June 21, Father's Day 2026 — and it does exactly one thing: says thank you. The hero image of a father lifting or embracing a child is warm and visually competent, and the copy ("you're a dad. But you're also a DJ. And sometimes a chair.") is the best prose Carter's has written in this audit series. It earns genuine affection. What it does not do is sell anything. No offer, no CTA button, no product imagery — just a card in email form.
- Placed one day after the June 20 "Buy 1, Get 2 Free" hard-offer push, this makes commercial sense as a palate cleanser. But it's a near-zero-revenue send by design. The only commercial touch is a Carter's Rewards badge buried at the bottom alongside social icons — easy to miss and attached to no action.
- Judged as a brand email, it's solid but not exceptional. The copy is warm; the art direction is clean. Judged as a driver of clicks or purchases, it essentially opts out of that game entirely.

## What's working

- **Copy is genuinely good.** "Sometimes a chair" and "fire-breathing dragon trying to capture — or just hug — the hero" reads as human, not templated.
- **Timely.** Sent on Father's Day itself (June 21); topic and calendar are perfectly aligned.
- **Cadence reset.** After eight EXTRA20 sends and a B1G2 push, this warmth-only break is the right tone shift — a subscriber doesn't feel sold at on Father's Day.
- **No render bugs.** Hero image loads, text is legible, layout is clean.
- **Subject line is emotionally resonant and clean** — 36 chars, no spam signals, fits mobile.

## What's weak

- **Zero CTA.** No button, no product link, no "shop gifts for dad" nudge — this email generates no clicks.
- **No offer whatsoever.** Father's Day is a purchase occasion; Carter's left revenue on the table compared to competitors who pair a warmth message with a one-day offer.
- **Hero image is small on webview.** The emotional centrepiece of the email is compressed; the man-and-child image doesn't get the canvas it deserves.
- **Rewards badge is passive.** Carter's Rewards is shown as a tiny icon badge with no copy hook — a "shop for dad, earn points" line would have connected the warmth to commerce.
- **No product or gift ideas.** The copy mentions gourmet meals, spaghetti nights, and dragon hugs — any one of those is an excuse to link a specific product category.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a single soft CTA.** A text link or low-key button — "Shop gifts for Dad →" or "Treat dad to something new" — converts the emotional moment into at least one click path without undermining the warmth. This is the single highest-lift change.
- 2. **Expand the hero image.** The father-child photo should fill the full-width container; right now it's undersized and loses emotional punch.
- 3. **Weave Rewards into the sign-off.** After "Thank you fathers, grandfathers and father figures," add: "Shopping for dad? Rewards members earn double points today." One sentence turns the badge into a conversion hook.
- 4. **Add a minimal gift-ideas grid below the letter.** Three hero products — a tee, a onesie, a Father's Day set — with prices. The warmth copy can stay; the grid sits beneath it as a quiet shop floor.

### 1. Technical Summary

Two tracking pixels are present (Attentive + Movable Ink). One pixel contains what appears to be a hardcoded subscriber ID instead of a dynamic merge tag, which is the primary technical concern. The HTML is truncated, so compliance footer and CTA link UTMs cannot be fully verified.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Hardcoded subscriber ID in Movable Ink pixel — HIGH**

```
https://mi.carters.com/p/up/745a17bbb73445ad/o.gif?mi_u=1150794179&mi_ecmp=06_21_2026_CAR_SUN_AM_LARGEAUDIENCE
```

`mi_u=1150794179` is a literal integer. In production sends this should be an ESP merge tag (e.g. `%%subscriber_id%%`, `{{contact.id}}`, etc.). If this fired as-is across the full list, all opens would be attributed to a single subscriber record. Verify whether this was replaced at send time by the ESP or if it escaped unrendered.

`mi_ecmp` is a static campaign string — acceptable for a campaign-level identifier.

**Dual open-pixel setup — LOW**

Both Attentive (`carters.attentivemail.com`) and Movable Ink (`mi.carters.com`) pixels fire on open. This is a deliberate dual-ESP/personalization-platform pattern, but confirm both are expected to count opens; duplicate open attribution can inflate reporting.

**Malformed `alt` attribute on Movable Ink pixel:**

```html
<img src="https://mi.carters.com/..." alt>
```

`alt` without `=""` is invalid HTML5. Functionally equivalent in most parsers, but should be `alt=""` for spec compliance.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Google Fonts via external `<link>` — MEDIUM**

```html
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=League+Spartan:wght@400;500;600;700&display=swap" rel="stylesheet">
```

Wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!--> ... <!--<![endif]-->` so it skips Outlook, but Gmail (web and Android) strips `<link>` tags in `<head>` entirely. League Spartan will not render in Gmail; the declared fallback is `Arial, Helvetica Neue, Helvetica, sans-serif`. Confirm the design degrades acceptably — no technical breakage, but mismatched visual expectations.

**Empty `<title>` tag:**

```html
<title></title>
```

Some screen readers and browser-based email clients (e.g. Gmail web in a new tab) surface the `<title>`. Should be set to the subject line or campaign name.

**Link color override:**

```css
#MessageViewBody a { color: inherit; text-decoration: none; }
```

This suppresses all default link styling in Gmail. If any inline link text (non-button) is present, it will be visually indistinguishable from body copy. Verify no inline hyperlinks rely on default underline/color to convey they are clickable.

**Preheader padding technique:** Zero-width non-joiners (`͏`) and soft hyphens (`­`) used for filler — standard practice, no issue.

**MSO/VML conditional comments and `role="presentation"` on tables** — correctly implemented.
