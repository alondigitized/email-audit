# Christmas PJs now 50% off 🎁✨
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-26**
## Executive summary

- Carter's follows through on the jarring Christmas pivot flagged in the 7/25 "EARLY ACCESS" send — this is now the second Christmas-PJ email in two days, and the countdown timer at the top reads 00:00:00:00, which reads as broken or expired the instant it's opened. The hero itself is genuinely strong: rich green background, ornament accents, a tight three-pajama color story (red stripe, green stripe, navy print), and "'tis always the season" is a decent line — though it's an odd thing to say one day after screaming "back to school" all week.
- The bigger problem is scope creep: this single email tries to sell Christmas PJs, OshKosh back-to-school, AND up-to-70%-off general clearance, three unrelated campaigns stacked top to bottom with zero narrative connecting them. A subscriber who clicked for Christmas pajamas scrolls into denim jackets, then into a red clearance category grid. That's three emails wearing a trench coat.
- Net: strong top-of-funnel creative undercut by a stale/broken countdown timer and a body that abandons focus almost immediately. This lands solidly mid-pack — worse than the cohesive 7/25 send, on par with the clearance-heavy 7/24 push.

## What's working

- Hero photography is the strongest visual asset in this batch — three pajama colorways styled together on a seasonal green backdrop with ornament garland reads premium and on-brand for Christmas.
- "ENDS MONDAY" badge gives a concrete, credible deadline directly on the hero image.
- The 3x3 product grid under "THE MAGIC STARTS NOW!" gives clear at-a-glance variety (stripes, prints, a dress) with clean product-on-white styling.
- Clear price anchor — "50% OFF* Christmas PJs" is specific and appears twice (hero badge + headline).

## What's weak

- The countdown timer reads 00:00:00:00 — this looks expired/broken on open and actively undercuts the urgency message, the opposite of its intended effect.
- Three unrelated promotions (Christmas PJs, OshKosh back-to-school, up to 70% off clearance) are crammed into one email with no transition — each section could be its own send.
- This is a second Christmas-themed email in as many days after a full week of back-to-school messaging; the whiplash the 7/25 email introduced hasn't settled, it's compounding.
- The OshKosh section uses fall/utility jacket styling that visually contradicts the Christmas hero just above it — no seasonal continuity within the same scroll.
- Preview/subject promise Christmas PJs specifically, but over half the email is dedicated to other categories — a bait-and-switch on scroll depth.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix or remove the countdown timer — a 00:00:00:00 display is worse than no timer at all; if the offer is still live, the timer logic is broken and needs an immediate check.
- 2. Split this into three separate sends (Christmas PJs, OshKosh back-to-school, clearance) — each has a real hero and offer and deserves its own subject/send rather than being buried mid-scroll.
- 3. If it must stay one email, cut it down to Christmas PJs + one supporting CTA at most, and drop OshKosh/clearance entirely for this send.
- 4. Reconsider the Christmas-vs-back-to-school sequencing — pick one seasonal narrative per week rather than alternating daily; the audience can't build the "always the season" instinct if it flips every 24 hours.
