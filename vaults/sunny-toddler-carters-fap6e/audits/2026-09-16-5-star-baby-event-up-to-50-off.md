# 🌟 5-Star baby event — Up to 50% off* ✨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-16**
## Executive summary

- This is Carter's biggest swing of the week — "Five-Star Baby Event, up to 50% off, lowest prices of the season" — and it's a genuinely strong hero: cute baby, bold orange starburst treatment, an actual price ceiling stated up front, and a clean "Shop Now" button. Compared to the brand-campaign detour of 2026-09-15's "Let every child's light shine," this is back to the formula that works — hero offer, clear number, obvious CTA — and it's executed cleanly with no render bugs.
- Where it falls down is the body: "We Think You'll Love These" is a nine-item grid of jeans, bodysuits, and pajamas with zero pricing on any tile, so the specific 50% claim from the hero has nothing to anchor to below the fold. Then a second offer — "Shop from $2.40 per piece" — appears for multipacks, and a third — "Up to 70% off clearance" — appears further down. Three different offers in one email dilutes the "lowest prices of the season" headline instead of reinforcing it.
- Net: a well-built top half undercut by a body that doesn't carry the number through. Solid, not great.

## What's working

- Hero has a real number and real urgency framing ("Five-Star Baby Event," "up to 50% off," "lowest prices of the season") — matches the subject exactly.
- Genuine, warm hero photo (smiling baby) with on-brand orange/star motif, no stock-photo staleness.
- "Shop Now" CTA button is high-contrast and unmissable directly under the offer.
- Sister-brand nav bar up top (OshKosh, Little Planet, Cat & Jack, Skip Hop) signals the multi-brand event without cluttering the hero.
- Clean layout end to end — no overlapping text, no broken images, category CTA grid (Baby Girl/Toddler Girl/Girl/etc.) is well organized.

## What's weak

- The 9-tile "We Think You'll Love These" grid has no prices, no product names, and no visible connection to the 50%-off headline — it reads as generic filler.
- Three competing offers in one email (50% off event, $2.40/piece multipacks, 70% off clearance) blur which one the reader should act on.
- The hero says "up to 50% off" but nothing on the page shows an actual discounted price anywhere — the whole email is asterisked, unanchored percentages.
- "New Baby Loves" section has decent styling but its CTA ("Shop from $2.40 per piece") is a weaker, less urgent ask sandwiched between two more aggressive offers, so it gets lost.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a price or "50% off" badge on at least the top row of the product grid — right now the number in the hero has nothing to prove it once you scroll.
- 2. Cut to two offers max: lead hero (50% off event) + clearance. Fold the multipack/$2.40 push into the main event instead of running it as a third distinct promo.
- 3. Add product names/labels under grid tiles — right now shoppers can't tell what they're looking at without hovering.
- 4. Consider moving the clearance block right under the hero (it's the strongest secondary offer at 70%) and pushing the ungapped grid further down.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 50% off + new markdowns — Five-Star Baby Event`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Bodysuits, jeans & PJs — all in on the season's biggest sale`
