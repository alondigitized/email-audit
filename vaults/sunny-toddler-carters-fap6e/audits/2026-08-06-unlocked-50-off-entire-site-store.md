# 🔓 Unlocked: 50% off* entire site & store
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- This is essentially a rerun of yesterday's email (8/5, "50% OFF* ENTIRE SITE & STORE 📚✏️") with the same hopscotch-chalk sidewalk hero, the same 50% off entire-site offer, and the same "Shop Now" button — the only real difference is a "🔓 Unlocked" framing device bolted onto the subject line. Below the fold it's a solid, well-organized email: a clean 9-up product grid, a genuinely fun Spider-Man collab section, a bold red 70% off clearance block with category shortcuts, and a store-locator block for Carter's Rewards+ members.
- The execution quality is fine — no render bugs, clear hierarchy, legible CTAs — but the strategy is thin. Running the identical hero creative and identical topline offer two days in a row (8/5 → 8/6) is the kind of cadence fatigue a subscriber notices immediately, even if today's subject line tries to disguise it with a "lock" gimmick.
- Judgment: a competent send riding on reused creative. It'll perform reasonably because the offer and layout are solid fundamentals, but it's not earning its own send — it's a subject-line reskin of yesterday's email.

## What's working

- Hero delivers the offer instantly and with no ambiguity: 50% off, entire site & store, one clear "Shop Now" button.
- The Spider-Man collab section ("Every hero starts somewhere") is genuinely differentiated creative — a licensed IP tie-in most competitors can't replicate.
- Exclusions/fine print are surfaced immediately under the offer (yellow highlight) instead of buried in the footer.
- Rewards+/cardholder perks and a store locator with a real address and "Get Directions" CTA give this send a multi-channel (online + in-store) purpose.
- Clean 9-product grid gives shoppers a fast visual scan of what's actually on offer.

## What's weak

- The hero art and headline offer are a near-exact repeat of yesterday's 8/5 send — same hopscotch chalk background, same "50% OFF ENTIRE SITE & STORE" copy block, same button placement.
- "Shop Now" is used three separate times (hero, Spider-Man block, implied on clearance buttons) with zero differentiation in CTA copy — none of them tell you what you're actually clicking into.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere in the hero or subject — "50% off" with no "ends tonight" gives a reader no reason to act now versus later.
- Clearance block ("UP TO 70% OFF") is arguably the stronger, fresher offer, but it's buried below two other content sections instead of leading.
- No models/lifestyle imagery anywhere — every product shot is a flat lay, which flattens the back-to-school emotional hook the copy is going for.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Stop re-running the same hero two days in a row.** If the offer is genuinely the same as 8/5, fold it into one email with a "still time to save" angle instead of sending a full duplicate creative — or lead with the fresher 70% off clearance instead.
- 2. Give each "Shop Now" button distinct copy tied to its section ("Shop the Sale," "Shop Spidey Styles," "Shop Clearance") so the CTAs aren't visually and semantically identical three times in one email.
- 3. Add a real deadline to the hero ("Ends Sunday") — the "🔓 Unlocked" subject implies something time-limited but nothing in the body backs that up.
- 4. Move the 70% off clearance block up, above the general product grid — it's a bigger number and newer news ("New Markdowns Added") than the repeated 50% off hero.
- 5. Swap at least one flat-lay grid for a lifestyle/model shot to make the back-to-school framing land emotionally, not just visually as product tiles.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 50% off code just unlocked (ends soon)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New markdowns: up to 70% off clearance + 50% off site`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Back-to-school essentials, Spider-Man styles, and clearance up to 70% off — all in one shop.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sitewide savings plus fresh clearance markdowns just added.`
