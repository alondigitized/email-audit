# $6+ Limited edition family PJs 🌟
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-theme Black Friday send. A countdown timer (12 hours left) sits above a pink hero: "from $6 LIMITED EDITION FAMILY PJS, plus, add $3 cozy socks to pair!" A "today only online!" badge and a Shop Now button finish it. The hero is clear and the deadline looks credible. The price is concrete and the product is seasonal. It's a clear step up from Sunday's "$4 Graphic tees & bodysuits" resend, which was a straight repeat.
- Below the hero the email loses focus. It stacks a candy-cane swatch row, a 50-60% off Black Friday banner, a Baby's First Christmas grid, a Shop It All row and a 70% off clearance block. It ends with a free gift bag banner. That is six or seven competing offers. The "from $6" hero price is also easy to miss against the "50-60% off" and "70% off" claims further down, and the "today only online" badge doesn't say how it connects to the countdown.
- Verdict: a strong top third and a long, cluttered bottom. It's a solid 7 on the hero alone, held back by the offer sprawl and the dense fine print.

## What's working

- The countdown timer (00 days, 12 hours, 43 minutes, 31 seconds) creates real urgency above the hero.
- The hero price "from $6" is large and readable, with the $3 socks add-on as a cheap upsell.
- The "today only online!" badge ties the deal to a single day.
- The candy-cane swatch row shows the actual print, so you can judge the product at a glance.
- The "Shop It All" row gives entry points with prices: Bodysuits from $4, PJs from $8, Sets from $10.
- The clearance block splits into Baby Girl, Toddler Girl, Girl, Baby Boy, Toddler Boy and Boy, so shoppers can self-select.

## What's weak

- Too many offers compete after the hero: family PJs, 50-60% off, 70% off clearance and a $75+ gift bag.
- The Black Friday red banner is visually heavier than the hero price, so the eye splits.
- Three Shop Now buttons use the same generic copy. The thin white outline buttons are also low-contrast on the pink hero.
- The fine print is long and dense, and the asterisked 50-60% and 70% claims come with many exclusions.
- The "Baby's First Christmas" block has no price or deadline, so it adds length without urgency.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the middle. Keep the hero, the PJ swatch row and one sitewide banner, and drop Baby's First Christmas or fold it into Shop It All.
- 2. Make the hero button solid and high-contrast, with specific copy such as "Shop family PJs from $6".
- 3. Put the 50-60% off figure and the "today only" window in one line near the top, so the deal reads as one story.
- 4. Give each swatch tile its own link and label, such as "Candy cane," "Green" and "Red."
- 5. Add star ratings or a "matching sizes for the whole crew" proof point near the PJs.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Candy-cane prints in sizes for everyone. Plus 50-60% off sitewide.`
