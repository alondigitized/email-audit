# BABY SALE: Up to 50% off* + extra 20%

## Executive summary

- This is Carter's fifth "5-Star Baby Event" wrapper since 2026-09-16, and the personalized "You've got 15% off" banner from 2026-09-23's send returns almost unchanged, now sitting above a cute baby-star hero rather than the orange starburst hero from prior sends. The mechanics are identical to the whole run: same orange starburst treatment lower in the email, same BABY20 code, same "up to 50%/70% off" stack, same price-match and rewards blocks. The one genuine change is the hero photo — a warm, well-lit baby face — which is a stronger opening image than the flat starburst art used in the four prior sends.
- The email is functionally fine and would scan clearly on mobile, but subscribers who opened any of the last four sends will recognize this instantly as the same offer in new packaging. The "you've got 15% off" personalization is a real asset that's being under-leveraged — it's small gray text above the fold instead of being the headline offer.

## What's working

- Hero baby photo is warm and on-persona, a clear step up from the flat starburst art in the 2026-09-21/22/23 sends.
- Clear single dominant CTA ("Shop Now") sitting directly under the offer stack.
- Offer stack is legible at a glance: 50% off + extra 20% with a visible code (BABY20).
- Product grid is well organized into logical categories (jeans, basics, baby items) with "from $X" pricing on some.
- No render bugs — no broken images, overlapping text, or placeholder content visible.

## What's weak

- This is the fifth near-identical "5-Star Baby Event" creative in under two weeks (2026-09-16, 19, 21, 22, 23) — fatigue risk is real at this point.
- The "You've got 15% off" personalization is buried as small text at the very top and never resurfaces near the CTA or in the offer stack itself.
- Two competing discount asks stack on the page (50%+20% via BABY20, then a separate 70% off clearance further down) which dilutes which offer is "the" offer.
- "Tap to Apply" for the 15% off is vague — unclear if it stacks with BABY20 or replaces it, no visible resolution of that conflict.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the 15% personalized offer the headline, not a footnote — either merge it clearly with the 50%+20% stack ("Your 15% + Extra 20%") or drop the redundant generic stack entirely so there's one unambiguous number.
- 2. Vary the creative structure, not just the hero photo — subscribers on this list have now seen "Five-Star Baby Event / up to 50% + extra 20%" five times; a genuinely different layout or offer framing is needed to reset fatigue.
- 3. Clarify whether the 15% code and BABY20 stack or conflict — resolve this ambiguity directly in copy near the CTA.
- 4. Consider dropping or subordinating the 70% clearance block — two big offer blocks on one email splits attention and looks like a bait-and-switch when the top offer is only 50%+20%.
