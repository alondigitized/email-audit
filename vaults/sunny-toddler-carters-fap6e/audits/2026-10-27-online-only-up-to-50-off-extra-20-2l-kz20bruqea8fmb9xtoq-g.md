# ONLINE ONLY !! Up to 50% off + extra 20% ☁️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth COZY20 send in four days, following "COZY FLASH 🚨" on 10/24, "$9+ Cozy PJs" on 10/25 and "Up to 50% off + extra w/ code: COZY20" on 10/26. The hero is clean. A sherpa texture carries "Ends Wednesday / cozy flash / up to 50% OFF*", a green "+ EXTRA 20% OFF" band and the code COZY20. The hierarchy is clear and the deadline is real.
- The body undercuts it. The "We think you'll love these" grid is nine near-identical items: white bodysuits, denim and black or navy joggers. None has a name, a price or a label, so it reads as a generic bestseller dump and not a cozy-flash edit. It also never shows the cozy product the hero promises.
- The rest of the email splits attention. A red "Up to 75% off last-chance clearance" block competes with the 50%+20% offer. Baby's First Christmas and a "Stellar Sleep Week" teaser add more messages. A subscriber who has seen this hero all week gets no new reason to click. Judgement: competent, but repetitive and unfocused.

## What's working

- The hero hierarchy is strong: deadline, "50% OFF*", highlighted extra 20% band and the code, all visible in the first screen.
- The COZY20 code is stated plainly and is easy to copy.
- The red clearance block has clear category buttons (Baby Girl, Toddler Girl, Girl, Baby Boy, Toddler Boy, Boy) that make navigation easy.
- The Baby's First Christmas lifestyle imagery is warm and seasonally right.
- The "Stellar Sleep Week, Oct 29–Nov 3" teaser with an Add to Calendar link is a smart way to plant a future send.

## What's weak

- The product grid has no prices, names or sale badges. The nine tiles are visually repetitive and say nothing about the cozy-flash offer.
- The 75% clearance block competes with the 50%+20% hero, and the "*" offers are confusing when stacked.
- The Baby's First Christmas, Sleep Week and store locator sections make the back half scattered, with no single story.
- The hero is the same creative as the three previous sends, so cadence fatigue is likely.
- The hero "Shop Now" is a thin outlined button with low contrast against the busy texture. The email also has no social proof or reviews.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the generic grid with cozy-flash products (PJs, fleece, sherpa), each showing the original price, the "after code" price and a "Shop" link. This would lift clicks the most.
- 2. Cut the back half to one extra message. Keep the hero, the product grid and either the clearance block or Sleep Week.
- 3. Make the hero CTA a filled green button reading "Shop cozy flash" or "Get 50% + 20% off".
- 4. Vary the hero creative from the last three sends, or add a live countdown for the Wednesday deadline.
- 5. Give the clearance block a distinct audience or timing so it doesn't compete with the hero.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Online only: cozy picks up to 50% off + 20%`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use COZY20 on bodysuits, jeans & joggers before Wednesday.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
