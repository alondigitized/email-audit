# Buy 1, Get 2 FREE* Denim + Uniforms
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-25**
## Executive summary

- Carter's opens with a "PICKS FOR YOU" personalized six-tile product grid — a reasonable engagement mechanic, but it immediately undercuts the subject line's entire premise. The subject sold the reader on a "Buy 1, Get 2 FREE Denim + Uniforms" event; the rendered email contains no such offer anywhere in the visible body. What does appear is the orange $2.99 clearance block — now on its **third consecutive mailing this week** (debuted June 23, tucked into the June 24 PJs send, and back here on June 25). The gap between subject promise and email delivery is blatant enough to register as a bait-and-switch to any subscriber who opened specifically for the denim deal.
- The "Carter's Near You" store-locator tile with a live map is genuinely differentiated — few promotional emails include omnichannel anchors at this cadence, and it's worth preserving. The Carter's Rewards visibility at the bottom is solid. But neither can rescue an email where the lead offer isn't there.
- Honest judgment: Carter's subject line credibility is taking on water. After the June 22–23 clearance escalation, the June 21 Father's Day double-send, and now a B1G2 subject with no B1G2 content, subscribers are being trained to distrust the subject. Once that happens, open rates decay faster than creative can recover.

## What's working

- **Personalized picks grid** signals curation and can surface relevant product for returning parents if the recommendations are accurate
- **Three-way shop split** (Baby / Toddler / Kid) gives parents a fast routing path without hunting
- **Carter's Near You** store tile with map + directions is a distinctive omnichannel element that peers rarely include mid-promo
- **Carter's Rewards section** keeps loyalty program top of mind for members
- **Clean render** throughout — no broken images, no layout breakage, no overlapping elements

## What's weak

- **Subject-body disconnect**: Subject promises "Buy 1, Get 2 FREE Denim + Uniforms" — that offer does not appear anywhere in the rendered email body
- **$2.99 clearance block is on its third appearance this week** — same orange creative, same floor price; creative fatigue is real and the block is increasingly easy to scroll past
- **No time-bound urgency anywhere visible** — no expiry date on the clearance, no end date on any offer, zero deadline pressure
- **Generic CTA copy** — "Shop Now" and "Shop Baby / Toddler / Kid" don't reference denim, uniforms, or a B1G2 mechanic the subject promised

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put the B1G2 denim offer in the email body** — if the subject sells B1G2 denim + uniforms, the hero must lead with it; the current "picks" grid has nothing to do with that promise and will confuse or anger the subscriber who opened specifically for it
- 2. **Add an expiry date to the clearance block** — "Ends Sunday, 6/29" is the single lowest-effort change that converts browsers into buyers; an open-ended clearance trains subscribers to procrastinate
- 3. **Retire or reskin the $2.99 clearance creative** — three sends of the same orange block in three days has burned it; if it must continue, frame it as "New drops added" or "Final days" to signal freshness
- 4. **Add a denim/uniforms shop tile to the category row** — a fourth tile bridging the subject to a click path is a one-hour build that closes the current disconnect
- 5. **Replace "Shop Now" CTA** with offer-mirrored copy: "Claim My B1G2" or "Shop Denim + Uniforms"

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Tab character in Movable Ink pixel URL — confirmed defect**


The `mi_ecmp` value terminates with `%09` (URL-encoded tab character). This is a template merge artifact — a trailing tab was included when the campaign code was injected. Either way the raw value in logs/reporting will differ from the intended campaign ID `06_25_2026_CAR_THU_STO_LARGEAUDIENCE`.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Google Fonts `<link>` will fail silently in most clients**

```html
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=League+Spartan:wght@400;500;600;700&display=swap"
      rel="stylesheet" type="text/css">
```

Wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!--> ... <!--<![endif]-->` to exclude Outlook desktop, but Gmail (webmail and Android) strips `<link>` tags entirely, and Apple Mail blocks external stylesheets under some privacy/proxy configurations. League Spartan will not render for a large share of recipients. Fallback stack `Arial, Helvetica Neue, Helvetica, sans-serif` appears in inline styles, which mitigates layout breakage, but the font load is wasted for most recipients.

**Empty `<title>` tag**

```html
<title></title>
```

Screen readers that announce document title (NVDA on Windows, some older iOS VoiceOver versions) will either read nothing or fall back to the sender name. Low severity but trivially fixable — set to match subject line.

**VML / MSO conditional comments** — present and correctly structured. No issues.

**Preheader padding** — uses a mix of `&#847;` (word joiner) and `&shy;` (soft hyphen) invisible characters to suppress inbox preview bleed-through. Technique is valid; character count is sufficient.
