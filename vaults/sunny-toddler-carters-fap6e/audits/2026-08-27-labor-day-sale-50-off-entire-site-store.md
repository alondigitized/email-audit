# ❤️ Labor Day sale - 50% off* entire site & store 💙
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, brand-appropriate Labor Day send from Carter's — a warm pink-to-orange gradient hero with a real folded-laundry pile of denim, florals, and knits gives it a lived-in, tactile feel that stands apart from the flat blue-gradient "Rewards Week" hero this brand ran five straight days (8/20–8/25). The "50% OFF ENTIRE SITE & STORE" headline is bold and unambiguous, and the "JUST FOR YOU" personalized grid plus the "$2.99 CLEARANCE" block and "Carter's near you" local module round it out into a genuinely full-funnel email rather than a single-offer blast.
- Where it falls short: the offer is undercut almost immediately by a dense fine-print disclaimer stacked right under the headline ("Savings based on MSRP. Exclusions Apply."), and the exclusion list at the bottom is long enough (Little Planet, OshKosh, Otter Avenue, PurelySoft, Carter's Bedtime, swimwear, new arrivals, select styles, outerwear...) that "entire site & store" reads as aspirational rather than literal. The email also does a lot — hero, 9-item grid, tees section, clearance section, store locator, rewards — which spreads attention across five separate CTAs instead of one clear next action.
- Net: this is one of the stronger creative executions from this brand recently (breaks the blue-gradient rut for the second time in two days, following 8/26's pink-orange "Labor Day starts now"), but the offer's credibility takes a hit from heavy fine print, and the multi-module layout dilutes an otherwise strong hero.

## What's working

- Fresh warm gradient hero (pink-to-orange) with a genuine product photo — a real stack of folded kids' clothing, not a stock lifestyle shot — breaks the recycled blue "Rewards Week" look that dominated 8/20–8/25.
- Headline hierarchy is immediately readable: "50% OFF" in large bold type is the first thing the eye lands on.
- "JUST FOR YOU" personalized product grid (3x3) gives concrete, clickable products instead of just a generic banner.
- Clearance block ("FROM $2.99") uses a distinct red color to visually segment it from the main offer, avoiding confusion between the two deals.
- Store locator module with an address and "Get Directions" button is a nice touch for a brand with physical retail.

## What's weak

- Fine print ("Savings based on MSRP. Exclusions Apply. See details below.") sits directly under the 50% headline, undermining the offer's confidence before the reader even scrolls.
- Five distinct CTAs compete for attention (Shop Now, Shop from $5, six clearance category buttons, Get Directions) — no single dominant next action.
- The bottom disclaimer block is long and lists numerous brand/category exclusions (Little Planet, OshKosh, Otter Avenue, PurelySoft, Carter's Bedtime, swimwear, new arrivals, etc.), which contradicts the "entire site & store" framing.
- No urgency/deadline is visible anywhere in the render (no "ends [date]" or countdown), despite this being framed as a Labor Day sale.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the exclusions/fine print entirely out of the immediate hero area — keep only the offer and a single asterisk, with details pushed to the footer only. This is the single biggest lift: right now the disclaimer competes visually with the 50% headline.
- 2. Add a visible deadline ("Ends Monday 9/1 at midnight ET") to the hero to create real urgency — currently nothing signals this is time-bound.
- 3. Cut the number of competing CTAs — the clearance section's six category buttons plus "Shop Now" plus "Shop from $5" plus "Get Directions" is too much; pick one primary CTA (Shop the Sale) and demote the rest.
- 4. Tighten the "50% OFF ENTIRE SITE & STORE" claim or soften it slightly (e.g., "up to 50% off") given how many categories are excluded — reduces the bait-and-switch feel once a reader clicks through.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus new markdowns from $2.99 — shop before it's gone`
