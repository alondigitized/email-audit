# Up to 60% off glow-in-the-dark PJs 🌟
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-29**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, themed launch send. "Stellar Sleep Week" gets a branded teal banner, and a warm photo of three kids in star-and-moon PJs carries the glow-in-the-dark hook. The "Watch them glow!" inset callout on the PJ photo is the best creative moment. After the hero, the email loses focus. It's a good concept that is diluted by a generic product grid and a clearance block that competes with the Sleep Week message.
- The grid under "SLEEP WEEK HAPPENING NOW!" shows jeans, a pink jacket, hoodies, a vest and bodysuits. Almost none of it is pajamas. It contradicts the headline and the subject. The red "UP TO 70% OFF* CLEARANCE" block then adds a second, bigger-looking offer than the 60% in the subject. Compared with the 10/28 "Up to 75% off clearance ends today" send, this feels like the same clearance module recycled under a new theme. After five COZY20 sends in five days, a fresh angle helps, but the execution doesn't fully back it up.
- Verdict: a good hero and a good subject, undermined by an off-theme middle and a competing offer. Expect solid opens and a leaky click path.

## What's working

- The "Stellar Sleep Week" banner gives the send a fresh, dated theme ("Only from Oct 29-Nov 3") with real urgency.
- The hero photo is warm and on-theme. The "Watch them glow!" circle inset shows the product benefit.
- The "Shop up to 60% off*" CTA matches the subject's offer and sits directly under the hero.
- The three-tile "Sleep Week deals on all PJs" row (family matching 60%, glow-in-the-dark 40%, Little Planet organic 20%) segments the offers clearly.
- The footer has useful extras (curbside, cardholder perks, in-app points, Rewards), and the disclaimers are fully visible.

## What's weak

- The nine-item grid under "SLEEP WEEK HAPPENING NOW!" is mostly jeans, jackets, a vest and bodysuits, not PJs. It undercuts the theme.
- The red "UP TO 70% OFF* CLEARANCE" block is visually the loudest element and outbids the 60% subject offer.
- The grid tiles have no prices, labels or individual links. It's an anonymous wall of product.
- The glow-in-the-dark tile text sits on busy photos, with white text over the images. "Glow-in-the-dark & character PJs" is hard to read, and it's the product the subject promised.
- The hero CTA is a thin outlined, low-contrast button. It's easy to miss against the white background.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the generic grid with glow-in-the-dark PJs only. Show 6 items with price callouts and a "Glows in the dark" badge, so the grid pays off the subject.
- 2. Demote or remove the clearance block, or move it below the footer perks. One offer per send.
- 3. Make the hero CTA a solid, filled button, ideally with a code or "Ends Nov 3" in the copy.
- 4. Put the glow-in-the-dark tile at the top of the three-tile row. Fix the text contrast with a darker gradient behind the labels.
- 5. Give the 60% offer a clear label ("Matching family PJs") so the deepest discount isn't buried under a vague "Up to".
