# (Early) Black Friday: Up to 60% off 🖤
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-13**
## Executive summary

- This is Carter's best creative of the week. The black starfield hero with "BLACK FRIDAY preview" and "UP TO 60% OFF*" reads in about a second. The lime backdrop and flat-lay of baby and toddler clothes make it feel like a seasonal event, not another teal or magenta starburst. A single Shop Now button sits right under the offer.
- The problem is credibility. Yesterday's 10-12 send said "60% off online ENDS TODAY 💫", and today the same 60% returns under a Black Friday banner six weeks early. A subscriber who believed "ends today" has just learned that deadlines here are soft. The email also carries two competing numbers, "up to 60%" in the hero and "up to 70% off clearance" in the red block, and the 70% band pulls attention from the hero.
- Verdict: strong first screen, weak second half. The unlabeled "Just for You" grid does little, and the real merchandising only starts with the two feature tiles. It is a solid send that would hold up better with a sharper subject line and a single offer.

## What's working

- The hero has clear hierarchy: a "preview" script, a large lime "UP TO 60% OFF*", a one-line category scope ("Sets, tops, bottoms & more—from baby to big kid"), and a Shop Now button.
- The black and lime palette breaks from the week's teal and magenta starbursts and signals a new campaign.
- The two feature tiles have specific, price-led copy: "The 5-Star Sleep & Play™ / Shop from $7" and "Bestselling Bodysuits / Shop from $2 Per Piece". They give concrete entry prices and a reason to click.
- The Baby Girl / Toddler Girl / Girl / Baby Boy / Toddler Boy / Boy button grid in the clearance block lets the reader self-sort by age and gender.
- The footer carries curbside, cardholder perks, in-app points and Rewards, and the new-store block has a Get Directions button.

## What's weak

- The 60% hero and the 70% clearance block are two headline numbers fighting for attention.
- The "Just for You" grid is nine bare product shots with no names, prices or visible labels. It looks like generic bestsellers, not personalization, and it gives no reason to tap one over another.
- The email shows no deadline, countdown or end date. "Preview" implies a future event, which undercuts urgency after yesterday's "ends today".
- The Commerce, California store block is a local-store ad for a reader who may be nowhere near it. It adds length without moving the offer.
- The footer asterisk ("UP TO 60% OFF*") leads to a dense legal block, and the hero gives no hint of what is excluded.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one offer. Either make clearance a small secondary strip or remove it, so "up to 60%" is the only headline number.
- 2. Put a real date on it ("Early access through Sunday") so "preview" reads as a deadline, not a vague tease.
- 3. Label the "Just for You" grid with product names and sale prices, or cut it to the two feature tiles plus four to six priced products.
- 4. Move the new-store block to the footer or make it location-conditional.
- 5. Change the Shop Now button to something category-specific, such as "Shop Early Black Friday Deals".

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email's HTML is well formed (responsive media query, VML/MSO conditionals, preheader, UTM-style `mi_*` tracking), but it has no plain-text part and no accessible alt text on several images. Every probed Attentive click-tracking link returned 403, which is probably bot-blocking of the probe rather than dead links.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- All 12 probed material links returned 403. They are all `https://carters.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...` redirect URLs. This includes:
  - the "Shop from $7 >" CTA
  - the hero "Black Friday preview | Up To 60% Off*" link
  - the brand links (Carter's, OshKosh B'gosh, Little Planet, Otter Avenue, Skip Hop)
  - the product image links (Boys Dark Wash Relaxed-Fit Classic Jeans, Kid Medium Blue Wash Straight-Leg Jeans, Boys French Terry Joggers - Black)
- The 403 is uniform across every domain and path, so this looks like a bot or WAF block on the tracking domain, not 12 independent breakages. The QA data can't confirm whether real recipients reach the landing pages. The final destinations were never resolved, so the real links remain unverified.
- 47 links were skipped, so coverage is only about 20% (12 of 59).
- The tracking redirects are long (500+ characters), opaque URLs. This is normal for Attentive, but it means destination parameters can't be audited from the source.
- Link-text labels such as "Carter's | The 5-star Sleep & Play™ | With a 2-way zip for easy changes | Shop f..." look like image alt text reused as link text. They are not a defect on their own.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- Images missing alt text (per QA):
  - `ho.gif` (`carters.attentivemail.com/ss/o/.../ho.gif`) and `o.gif` (`mi.carters.com/p/up/.../o.gif`) are 1x1 tracking pixels. `ho.gif` already has `alt=""` in the source (`width="1" height="1"`), which is correct for a decorative image, so the QA flag is a false positive there.
  - `264477f43986d8bb.png` (`mi.carters.com/p/rp/...`) looks like a tracking or redirect image.
  - Four CloudFront banner images (`MzczMjo6U0VSVklDRVNfQkFOTkVSXzAx...` through `...BNNOVJfMDRfdjM...`, named `SERVICES_BANNER_01` to `04_v3`) are content images with no alt text. These are real accessibility gaps, and they are the only confirmed ones.
- `<title></title>` is empty. Some clients and screen readers use it for the page name.
- The web font `League Spartan` is loaded through `<link>` inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. It is correctly excluded from Outlook, and clients that strip `<link>` will fall back to system fonts.
- The preheader padding uses `͏` and soft-hyphen characters, which is a standard technique. The preheader div has `display:none`, so it is hidden correctly.
- `lang="en"` is set and the viewport meta is present. No other rendering issues are visible in the truncated source.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) are visible in the truncated source or the QA output.
- Tracking URLs contain subscriber-level identifiers: `mi_u=1150794179` and `mi_ecmp=10_13_2026_CAR_TUE_STO_LARGEAUDIENCE`. These are expected for the ESP and are not defects. They are present in the pixel URLs, which are static and not personalized in the visible content.
- The source was truncated, so merge tokens in the body can't be ruled out.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **Plain-text part is missing.** QA reports `Text version is 0 chars`. This is a deliverability and accessibility problem for text-only clients and spam filters.
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. QA notes that the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed gap. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both, so the raw headers need checking.
- `Authentication-Results` is absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment can't be assessed through this relay.
- The sender is `marketing.carters.com`, a subdomain separate from the tracking domain `carters.e.attentivemail.com`. The tracking domain doesn't match the From domain, which is common but worth confirming for alignment.
- The physical address and a visible unsubscribe link are not in the truncated source. QA says an unsubscribe link was included in probing, but its result isn't separately listed.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Destination URLs and UTM parameters can't be verified because every link is wrapped in opaque Attentive redirects that returned 403.
- The `mi_ecmp` value (`10_13_2026_CAR_TUE_STO_LARGEAUDIENCE`) shows the campaign name and date match this send (2026-10-13). That's useful for attribution but doesn't confirm landing-page alignment.
- Landing-page alignment with the subject line ("(Early) Black Friday: Up to 60% off") and the "Shop from $7" CTA is unverified.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Re-probe the Attentive links with a browser-like User-Agent, or follow the redirects with a headless browser, to separate bot blocking from real breakage. Then record the final URLs and their UTM parameters.
2. Add `alt` text to the four CloudFront `SERVICES_BANNER_*` images. Set `alt=""` explicitly on the tracking pixels `o.gif` and `264477f43986d8bb.png`.
3. Add a plain-text MIME part. A 0-character text version is the only confirmed deliverability defect.
4. Check the raw headers from the original message (not the AgentMail relay) for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` to settle the compliance warnings.
5. Fill in the empty `<title>` with the campaign or subject line.
6. Raise the probe limit, or add the footer unsubscribe and address checks, so the 47 skipped links get some coverage.

**Confidence:** the 403s, missing text part and missing alt text are confirmed by QA data. The compliance header findings are unconfirmed because of the relay.
