# Buy 1, get 1 free - holiday 🌟
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the third near-identical BOGO send in three days. It follows "Buy 1, Get 1 free on holiday 🌟" on 10/20 and "HOLIDAY SALE - Buy 1, Get 1 free ✨" on 10/21. The top fold is strong. A red "RSVPs are calling / BUY 1, GET 1 FREE*" banner sits over a warm toddler-with-bow photo, and a full-width "Buy 1, Get 1 Free* Holiday" button sits at the bottom of the hero. The offer is clear in under two seconds.
- Below the hero the email loses focus. It runs a plain 3x3 grid of basics (jeans, bodysuits, joggers), a Grinch block, a red puffer, and a "70% OFF* Clearance" banner. That's four competing messages after a clean BOGO hero. The 70% clearance banner also undercuts the BOGO. A subscriber who saw "Up to 60% off Early Black Friday" a few days ago now sees two different discount stories in one email.
- Verdict: competent and on-brand, but a repeat. A subscriber who got the 10/20 and 10/21 versions has no new reason to open this one, and nothing here creates urgency.

## What's working

- The hero banner leads with the offer. "BUY 1, GET 1 FREE*" is large, white on red, and readable at a glance.
- The hero photo is warm and seasonal, and the CTA button sits right under it.
- The Grinch and puffer blocks each have one image, one line of copy and one button, so they scan quickly.
- The Baby Girl / Toddler Girl / Girl / Baby Boy / Toddler Boy / Boy buttons in the clearance section let a parent jump straight to their kid's size and gender.
- The render is clean. There are no broken images, overlapping text or empty gaps.

## What's weak

- The "*" on BOGO has no visible qualifier near the offer. The terms (first item at MSRP, equal or lesser value, exclusions) are buried in the footer fine print.
- The product grid has no prices, names or CTAs. Nine tiles of jeans, onesies and joggers give nothing to react to, and it isn't clear whether they're BOGO-eligible.
- The 70% off clearance banner competes with the BOGO headline and dilutes the message.
- There is no deadline anywhere. "RSVPs are calling" is vague and gives no end date.
- The repeated hero ("watch them glow") and offer from 10/20 and 10/21 make this feel like a resend.
- Rewards and loyalty are only footer icons, so members get no visible perk.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Make the send do something different.** If BOGO runs for several days, rotate the creative and add a real end date ("Ends Sunday") to the hero. As it stands, this is the third identical send in three days.
- 2. **Put the BOGO terms next to the offer.** Add a one-line qualifier under the headline, such as "Equal or lesser value. Select styles."
- 3. **Cut the 70% clearance banner**, or move it to a separate send, so the email carries one offer.
- 4. **Make the product grid shoppable.** Add prices or "BOGO" badges and link each tile to its product page, so it's clear what's eligible.
- 5. **Show Rewards status near the top** if the recipient is a member, for example "Earn points on every BOGO".

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a well-formed responsive template with a preheader, VML/MSO fallbacks and a Google Fonts link. The automated QA run (16% pass) reports 11 tracked links returning 403, a 0-character plain-text part, and missing alt text on 7 content images. Several of these are probably artifacts of the probe or the AgentMail relay, as noted below.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **11 of the 12 probed links returned 403.** All are `https://carters.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...` redirect-wrapped links. They cover the hero ("RSVPs are calling BUY 1, Get 1 FREE*"), "Shop from $10", the brand links (carter's, osh kosh bigosh, little planet, Otter Avenue, Skiphop) and the product tiles (jeans, bodysuits, joggers).
- **The 403s are probably bot-blocking, not dead links.** Every failure is on the same ESP click-tracking host, which commonly rejects non-browser user agents. Nothing in the QA data shows the destinations are broken. Verify with a real-browser or mobile-UA request before treating them as outages.
- **Destinations can't be checked.** The wrapped links hide the final URL, so landing-page validity is unconfirmed.
- **Tracking pixels are present and working as designed:**
  - `carters.attentivemail.com/ss/o/.../ho.gif` is 1x1, in a hidden `pixel-wrapper` div.
  - `mi.carters.com/p/up/745a17bbb73445ad/o.gif?mi_u=...&mi_ecmp=10_22_2026_CAR_THU_PM_ENGAGERS` is a second-party open pixel.
  - The pixel URL carries a subscriber identifier (`mi_u=1150794179`). That is a privacy note, not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in `<head>`. Real but minor.
- **Missing alt text on content images (real issues).** These are the `d3ptznaucccg76.cloudfront.net` images (`MzczMjo6Q0FSXzEwMjAyNl9TRUNPTkRBUllfMi5qcGc...`, `..._3.gif`, `SERVICES_BANNER_01` to `04_v3.png`) and `mi.carters.com/p/rp/264477f43986d8bb.png`. Their filenames are opaque base64, so screen readers and images-off clients get no usable text. If these are the services banners, they carry content. Check whether any are linked, because alt text on a linked image doubles as the link name.
- **The two tracking-pixel warnings are false positives.** The `ho.gif` source shows `alt=""`, which is correct for decorative images. `o.gif` is likely the same, but the HTML is truncated, so I can't confirm it.
- **Redundant alt prefix.** Product links use "Image of Boys Dark Wash Relaxed-Fit Classic Jeans" and similar. The "Image of" prefix is noise for screen readers.
- **Render paths are fine.** The mobile `@media (max-width:660px)` rules, `role="presentation"` layout tables, and MSO conditionals are present. I found nothing broken in the visible source.
- **Web font fallback.** The League Spartan `<link>` is wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, so Outlook skips it. I could not see the `font-family` stack because the source is truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unresolved merge tokens such as `{{...}}`, `%%...%%` or `[FirstName]`. The only per-recipient data is the `mi_u` and `mi_ecmp` pixel parameters, which resolved. The body is truncated, so tokens further down can't be ruled out.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may be stripping them, so this is unconfirmed. Check the raw headers at the origin. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture. Not a confirmed failure.
- **Plain-text part missing (0 chars). FAIL.** This can hurt deliverability and accessibility. It may also be a relay or capture artifact, so check the original MIME for a `text/plain` part.
- **Footer not visible.** The truncated HTML shows no unsubscribe link or physical postal address. The QA report says an unsubscribe link was always included in probing, but it is not among the listed results. Verify the footer for CAN-SPAM.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM and landing-page alignment can't be assessed.** Click URLs are Attentive-wrapped, and the 403s block resolving them.
- **Campaign ID:** the only visible identifier is `mi_ecmp=10_22_2026_CAR_THU_PM_ENGAGERS` on the pixel, which indicates an "engagers" segment send on 10/22/2026.
- **The "BUY 1, Get 1 FREE*" hero and "Shop from $10" CTA can't be matched to their landing pages.** That needs a browser-based redirect trace.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the attentivemail links with a browser-like UA or a headless browser to confirm whether the 403s are blocking. Capture the final URLs and UTM parameters in the same pass.
2. Inspect the raw message at the origin, not the relay copy, for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
3. Confirm a `text/plain` alternative exists in the MIME. If it doesn't, add one.
4. Add descriptive alt text to the CloudFront and `mi.carters.com/p/rp/` content images, and drop the "Image of" prefix from product alts.
5. Populate `<title>`, for example with the subject line.
6. Confirm the footer has a working unsubscribe link and a physical address.
