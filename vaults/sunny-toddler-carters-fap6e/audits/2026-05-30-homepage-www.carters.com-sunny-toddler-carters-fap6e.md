# Homepage snapshot · www.carters.com · 2026-05-30
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-30**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is my store — I literally have the app bookmarked — and the second I open it I'm seeing "UP TO 50% OFF" in big letters on a teal background with a baby face staring back at me. That's Carter's doing what Carter's does: leading with a deal and a cute kid. It's clearly in summer-prep mode pushing tops and shorts, and there's a fresh baby essentials drop teased in the announcement bar up top.

## What's working

- **"UP TO 50% OFF*" hero banner** — I am a coupon-stacker by nature. This stops my scroll immediately. It's the first thing I read.
- **"Just dropped! Shop the New Baby Essentials Collection."** announcement bar — the phrase "just dropped" speaks to me in a millennial way, and new essentials are always relevant when I'm constantly sizing up.
- **Baby close-up photo** — that soft, warm, drowsy baby face below the hero is *exactly* the emotional trigger Carter's should be pulling. It makes me think of my kid and want to buy them things.
- **Search bar is prominent** — as a mobile shopper who knows what I want (6M pajama sets, 18M shorts), I appreciate having search front and center rather than buried.
- **Coupon/tag icon in the nav bar** — this feels like a shortcut to deals and I'd tap it immediately.

## What's weak

- **"Shop Now" CTA is too generic** — I don't know if I'm going to a summer sale page, a baby essentials page, or the whole site. "Shop Summer Deals" or "Shop the Sale" would tell me where I'm landing.
- **No free shipping threshold visible** — I always calculate if my pajama haul qualifies. Not seeing it above the fold means I have to go hunting, and that's friction.
- **No loyalty/rewards balance visible** — I have a Carter's Rewards account. Show me my points or a "Members get extra 20% off" hook and I'm converting faster.
- **"UP TO 50% OFF*" asterisk** — that little star is doing a lot of work. Is it sitewide? Excludes clearance? I've been burned before and now I always second-guess it.
- **No urgency signal** — no countdown, no "ends Sunday," nothing telling me this deal doesn't last forever. I'll close the app and forget about it.

## Recommendations

- **Replace "Shop Now" with destination-specific copy** — "Shop Summer Sale" or "Shop Baby Essentials" so I know exactly what clicking gets me. One word change, ships today.
- **Add a free-shipping threshold to the announcement bar** — swap or add to "Just dropped!" with something like "Free shipping on $35+ | New Baby Essentials just dropped." Two things I need, one bar.
- **Surface a loyalty/rewards hook in the hero** — even a small "Rewards members: extra 15% off" badge under the CTA button would make me feel seen as a returning customer and push me to convert faster.
- **Add a deadline or urgency line under the hero subtext** — "Offer ends June 2" or a small countdown chip. I'm a BOGO season-sale shopper — deadlines make me act.
