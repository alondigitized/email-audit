# Homepage snapshot · www.carters.com · 2026-07-11
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-11**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I literally shop here weekly so I already feel at home — this is exactly the Carter's/OshKosh I know and trust. Right now they're pushing hard into back-to-school OshKosh with a coupon code front and center, which honestly feels made for me. The vibe is trendy-but-accessible, millennial-mom energy, and the offer in the top banner had me reaching for my screenshot button the second the page loaded.

## What's working

- **SCHOOL20 banner strip** — the code is right at the top, no digging, no "find the offer" hunt. This is how you do mobile promos.
- **"Shop Styles from $5" CTA** — price-anchor CTAs like this are crack for my brain. $5 entry point makes me feel like I'm in control.
- **OshKosh brand placement** — using the OshKosh sub-brand prominently is smart; I think of OshKosh and Carter's as the same ecosystem, so this reads as "you're in the right place."
- **Back-to-school timing** — I'm already mentally shopping fall/school fits in July. This campaign anticipates my planning behavior perfectly.
- **"Remixing our heritage staples"** — millennial-coded copy. I respond to nostalgia language because I literally grew up in OshKosh overalls.

## What's weak

- **No loyalty callout** — I have a Carter's account and I've been stacking rewards for months. There's no "You have $X in rewards" or "Member exclusive" hook anywhere in this fold. Missed opportunity to keep me from going to check Amazon.
- **Account badge is a ghost** — that red "1" on the account icon is cryptic. Is it an order update? A reward? A promo I'm missing? A millennial mom with a full inbox is not going to tap that without a reason.
- **Hero crop is too tight** — it's a child's arm and a denim jacket cuff. I get the artsy angle, but I'm shopping on my phone while my toddler naps and I want to see the full outfit fast. This makes me work too hard to understand what I'm actually buying.
- **Asterisk on the promo** — "Extra 20% off*" — that asterisk is doing a lot of work silently. I want to know right now if that stacks with sale items or if it's full-price only.
- **No search pre-populated or category shortcuts** — as someone who comes here specifically for pajamas, I'd love a quick-tap row like "Pajamas | Bodysuits | Denim | Shoes" rather than having to open the hamburger.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Your Rewards: $X" line** next to or under the promo banner — I check my rewards every visit and a visible balance would keep me out of the app and on the site longer.
- **Expand the asterisk inline** — even just "(excl. clearance)" in tiny text under the banner removes doubt and keeps me from rage-closing when the code doesn't work at checkout.
- **Swap the tight editorial crop for a full-outfit shot** — show the OshKosh denim jacket on a kid standing up with a backpack or at a desk. I need to imagine my toddler in it immediately, not squint at a sleeve.
- **Add a horizontal quick-category strip** below the search bar ("Pajamas • Onesies • Denim • Shoes") — one-tap access to the aisles I actually live in, no hamburger required.
