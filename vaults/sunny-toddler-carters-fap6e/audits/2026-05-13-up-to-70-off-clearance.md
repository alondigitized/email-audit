# Up to 70% off CLEARANCE!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Carter's is running a clean, competent clearance blast. The 70% off hero lands immediately, the category pills give the subscriber a fast self-serve path, and the seasonal tie-in ("summer camp packing list") is timely. No render bugs. This is a technically solid promotional email.
- The problem is downstream of the hero: after the clearance hook, the email pivots to "New & Trending" — full-price items — before returning to "Shop It All." That kills the clearance urgency. There's also no deadline anywhere in the email, which turns "clearance" from a scarcity signal into a casual suggestion. And with zero personalization, Carter's is treating every subscriber on the list identically, whether they have a newborn or a 10-year-old.
- Reliable send. Not a precise one.

## What's working

- 70% off headline is large, readable, and above the fold — the offer is immediately clear
- Category pills (Baby Girl / Toddler Girl / Girl / Baby Boy / Boy) let subscribers route themselves in one tap
- "New markdowns added!" sub-headline adds recency signal without screaming
- Email renders cleanly — no broken images, no layout collapse, consistent Carter's aesthetic
- Rewards program callout at the footer acknowledges loyalty members
- Summer camp/summer staples framing is seasonally appropriate for mid-May

## What's weak

- No deadline — "clearance" sounds urgent but the email gives zero reason to act today vs. Thursday
- "New & Trending" section actively contradicts the clearance push — detours the reader toward full-price items mid-scroll
- No personalization anywhere — same email whether you buy newborn onesies or 7-year-old shorts
- The asterisk on "70% OFF*" is prominent but fine print is buried at the very bottom; "up to" scope is never clarified in the body
- "Carter's Near You" with a map at the bottom of a clearance email is noise — it wastes scroll real estate on a low-urgency CTA

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a deadline.** "Clearance prices reset Sunday" or a 3-day countdown would convert browsing intent into purchase urgency. Single biggest lift available.
- 2. **Cut "New & Trending."** A clearance email should not route the reader toward full-price items. Remove it entirely for a cleaner funnel.
- 3. **Surface a specific floor price in the hero.** "Baby girl styles from $2.99" is more compelling than "up to 70% off" — floor pricing drives clicks even when the headline percentage doesn't.
- 4. **Personalize the category lead.** If Carter's knows this subscriber buys baby girl, lead with baby girl first in both the pills and the hero image.
- 5. **Kill "Carter's Near You."** Relocate to a transactional email or a dedicated store-finder send — it dilutes the clearance message here.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Clearance ends Sunday — up to 70% off kids' styles`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Baby & kids clearance: new markdowns, up to 70% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Baby girl onesies from $2.99 · New prices added today`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New markdowns just dropped — shop by age before they're gone`
