# Comfy tops & bottoms from $6 💙
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-30**
## Executive summary

- This is a catalog-style everyday basics send with a clean hero and a weak offer. The hero is a photo of heart-print, pink and olive joggers with "COMFY TOPS & BOTTOMS" and a "Shop from $6" button. That is a real price anchor, but only a starting price. Nothing says what discount it represents, and nothing gives a deadline or code.
- The body then loses focus. A 3x3 grid of white onesies, black joggers and denim has no prices, labels or individual links I can see. A "Pick your PJs" row follows, then a red "UP TO 70% OFF* CLEARANCE" block with a button grid. The email is trying to sell basics, PJs and clearance at once. The earlier "Stellar Sleep Week" and COZY20 sends each had one clear story, and this one doesn't.
- Judgement: competent and on-brand, but a mid-funnel filler send. Coming right after the 10/29 sweepstakes and glow-in-the-dark PJ emails, and the 10/28 clearance send, it repeats the clearance beat without adding urgency.

## What's working

- The hero photo is bright and uncluttered, and the pastel joggers fit the "comfy" subject.
- "Shop from $6" gives a concrete entry price and a visible bordered button.
- The red 70% OFF clearance block stands out strongly, and its audience buttons (Baby Girl, Toddler Boy and so on) are easy to tap.
- The "Pick your PJs" row with glow-in-the-dark, character and holiday tiles carries the seasonal theme.
- The loyalty and utility strip (curbside, cardholder perks, app points, Rewards) is present and tidy.

## What's weak

- The "WE THINK YOU'LL LOVE THESE…" grid has no prices, names or visible CTAs, so it reads as filler.
- The email has three competing messages: $6 basics, PJs and 70% clearance. The eye has no single destination.
- The "From $6" offer has no deadline, no code and no savings framing. The only urgency is in the clearance block, and it is generic.
- The clearance block says "New Markdowns Added" but has no end date, which is weaker than the 10/28 "ends today" send.
- The sender name "Carter's Sleep Week" doesn't match the subject and hero, which are about everyday basics rather than sleepwear.
- The legal fine print at the bottom is long and dense.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story. Either make basics the hero with a clear deal ("$6+ tops & bottoms, 2 days only"), or lead with clearance. Cut the other.
- 2. Put prices and product names under the grid items. Each tile should link to a specific product and show a "$6" or "$8" price badge.
- 3. Add a real deadline to the hero, for example "Ends Sunday". Right now it is the weakest part of the email.
- 4. Make the hero button specific, such as "Shop tops & bottoms from $6".
- 5. Make the sender name match the content. Use "Carter's" unless the email is actually about sleep.
- 6. Move the clearance block up, or merge it with the offer, instead of leaving it as an afterthought below the PJs.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **12 of 12 probed links returned 403.** All are `https://carters.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...` redirects. This includes the "Shop from $6" CTA, the brand links (carter's, osh kosh bigosh, little planet, Otter Avenue, Skiphop), the hero image link, and 4 product-image links.
- **The 403s are probably bot-blocking, not broken links.** A uniform 403 on every Attentive redirect, from a headless prober, is the usual signature of the ESP's bot filtering. Nothing here confirms a user-facing failure. Retry with a real browser UA, or follow the redirects manually, before treating this as a defect.
- **The destination URLs behind the redirects are unknown.** The 403s blocked resolution, so I can't verify landing pages or product-link validity.
- **Coverage is low.** 51 links were skipped, so the footer, social and unsubscribe links are not validated beyond the "always probe unsubscribe" rule.
- **Two tracking pixels are present.**
  - `carters.attentivemail.com/ss/o/.../ho.gif` is the Attentive open pixel.
  - `mi.carters.com/p/up/745a17bbb73445ad/o.gif?mi_u=1150794179&mi_ecmp=10_30_2026_CAR_FRI_AM_ENGAGERS` is a second-party pixel.
