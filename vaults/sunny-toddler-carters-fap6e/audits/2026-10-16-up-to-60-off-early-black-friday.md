# Up to 60% off (Early) Black Friday 💥
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-16**
## Executive summary

- This is the third near-identical "Black Friday preview" send in four days (Oct 13, Oct 15 and Oct 16 "Up to 60% off Black Friday preview ✔️"). The black starfield banner with "UP TO 60% OFF*" is clear. But it's a thin strip, and the real hero is a big, soft flat-lay of cream, gray and blue long-sleeve bodysuits. The eye goes to the bodysuits first, not the offer.
- The rest of the email is a grab bag: a "Bestselling Bodysuits" block, a 3x3 "Just for You" grid of jeans, joggers and white bodysuits, an OshKosh "From the Vault" tile and a red "Up to 70% off clearance" banner. The two different percentages (60% and 70%) and four competing CTAs dilute the Black Friday message. The "Just for You" grid has no prices, names or links I can see, so it carries little weight.
- Overall it's competent but cluttered and repetitive. It's a 6 for a subscriber who has seen the same banner all week.

## What's working

- The black "Black Friday preview / UP TO 60% OFF*" banner is legible and has a "Shop Now" link above the fold.
- The bodysuit flat-lay is clean, on-brand and clearly baby-focused.
- "Shop from $2 Per Piece" gives a concrete, low-price hook under "Bestselling Bodysuits".
- The "Just for You" grid hints at personalization.
- The clearance block's Baby Girl / Toddler Girl / Girl / Baby Boy / Toddler Boy / Boy buttons make it easy to jump to the right size.

## What's weak

- The offer banner is a short strip above a much larger lifestyle image, so the product outweighs the promo.
- The email carries "Up to 60% off" and "Up to 70% off clearance" at once, which muddies the message.
- The OshKosh "From the Vault" tile is a heritage story with no discount. It pulls attention away from Black Friday and repeats the Oct 14 send.
- The "Just for You" grid shows no prices, names or reviews, so there's no product detail to act on.
- The legal footer is long, and the "*" exclusions are only visible at the bottom.
- The creative repeats the Oct 13, 15 and 16 sends, so it adds little new.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the offer the hero. Enlarge the "Up to 60% off" banner and drop the bodysuit image beneath it as supporting art.
- 2. Pick one discount story. Either cut the clearance block or fold it into one "Black Friday preview" message.
- 3. Add prices, ratings or "from $X" labels to the "Just for You" grid and link each tile to its product.
- 4. Move the OshKosh tile to its own send, or give it a Black Friday discount tie-in.
- 5. Add a real deadline or an explicit "preview ends [date]" so the urgency is credible.
- 6. Vary the hero creative from the last three sends.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Black Friday came early: up to 60% off 💥`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Bodysuits from $2 + up to 60% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sets, tops, bottoms & more, baby to big kid. Preview ends soon.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Bestselling bodysuits from $2, plus extra markdowns on clearance.`

The text part is empty, several non-pixel images lack alt text, and the unsubscribe and authentication headers couldn't be verified. The HTML source is truncated, so findings are limited to what is visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links returned 403.** They are all `https://carters.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...` redirects. This covers the header logo ("Carter's"), brand links (osh kosh bigosh, little planet, Otter Avenue, Skiphop), the hero ("Black Friday preview | Up To 60% Off*"), "Bestelling Bodysuits", and the product tiles (jeans ×3, 5-pack bodysuits, joggers).
  - A uniform 403 on every wrapped link points to ESP/CDN bot filtering of the probe, not to dead links. Real recipient clicks are unconfirmed either way.
  - The 16% pass rate is therefore unreliable for link health.
- **Destinations are not verifiable.** 43 links were skipped, and the 12 probed ones never resolved past the Attentive redirect. Landing pages and their parameters can't be checked.
- **Tracking pixels:** `carters.attentivemail.com/ss/o/.../ho.gif` and `mi.carters.com/p/up/745a17bbb73445ad/o.gif?mi_u=1150794179&mi_ecmp=10_16_2026_CAR_FRI_NOON_HIGHPROP`. Two vendors' pixels are present, and `mi_u` is a per-recipient identifier in the URL. This is not a defect, only a note.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in the `<head>`. This is minor, but it affects web-view and some screen-reader contexts.
- **Missing alt text, likely real:** four CloudFront banners (`d3ptznaucccg76.cloudfront.net/MzczMjo6U0VSVklDRVNfQkFOTkVSXzAx…`, `…02…`, `…03…`, `…04_v3…`) and `mi.carters.com/p/rp/264477f43986d8bb.png`. - **`ho.gif` flag is a false positive.** The source shows `alt=""` on the 1×1 pixel, which is correct for a decorative image. `o.gif` is probably the same, but its markup isn't in the truncated source.
