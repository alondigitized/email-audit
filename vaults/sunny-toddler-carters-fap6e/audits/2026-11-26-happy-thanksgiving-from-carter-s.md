## Executive summary

- This is a warm, well-made Thanksgiving courtesy send that gets the tone right. The hero shows a laughing family at the table in plaid, with "happy thanksgiving!" in script and a clear "we're closed today, but shop online anytime. See you in stores tomorrow." It reads like a brand being human on the holiday. It's also a smart pause between the 11/26 "BLACK FRIDAY: 60-70% off" and tomorrow's Black Friday push.
- The commercial offer is weak, though. The first third of the email is a Beyond Bedtime donation story with a "Donate At Checkout" button, so the top CTA asks the reader to give rather than buy. The real offers, "Limited edition PJs from $6" and "Shop up to 70% off*", sit well below the fold. The "up to 70% off" is only in the last button label, with no banner or price anchor.

# Technical Audit: "Happy Thanksgiving from Carter's 🤎"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a structurally sound Attentive-built template (`lang="en"`, viewport meta, `role="presentation"` tables, preheader). It has no plain-text part, several unlabeled linked images, and an empty `<title>`. All 12 probed links returned 403 from the Attentive redirector, which is probably bot-blocking rather than real breakage (see below).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links returned 403.** Every one is a `carters.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/<token>` redirect. The probed links include "Shop Now", "Shop up to 70% off*", "Donate At Checkout", the brand tiles (carter's, osh kosh bigosh, little planet, Otter Avenue, Skiphop, Beyond Bedtime) and both image links.
  - A uniform 403 on a single ESP click-tracking host points to the probe being blocked, not 12 independent dead links.
  - Destinations were not resolved, so the 403s are **unconfirmed as real breakage**. Re-test with a browser-like UA or follow the redirect chain manually.
- **Empty link text on the first link.** The QA label shows an empty anchor with a link text beginning "happy thanksgiving! | We're closed today…", so the text appears to come from an image or a concatenated block. See the alt-text item below.
- **26 links were not probed.** Footer, utility and social links were skipped by the QA ranking. Coverage is partial.
- **Tracking pixels:**
  - `carters.attentivemail.com/ss/o/.../ho.gif` is a 1x1 pixel in a `display:none` wrapper, with `alt=""` in the source.
  - `mi.carters.com/p/up/745a17bbb73445ad/o.gif?mi_u=1150794179&mi_ecmp=11_26_2026_CAR_THU_AM_ENGAGERS` is a second open pixel. It carries a per-recipient ID (`mi_u`) and a campaign/segment tag in the query string. This is not a defect, but it means there are two separate open-tracking systems.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Linked images with no usable alt text.** The two CloudFront GIFs are `…car_112526_secondary_4.gif` and `…secondary_5.gif`. They are wrapped in links, and the QA output uses `[image: MzczMjo6…]` (the filename) as the link text. Screen readers and image-blocked clients get no meaningful link label.
- **Four more content images are missing alt:** `SERVICES_BANNER_01.png` through `04_v3.png` on `d3ptznaucccg76.cloudfront.net`. If they carry text or links, that content is lost with images off.
- **False positives:** the `ho.gif` and `o.gif` tracking-pixel alt warnings are not defects. `ho.gif` already has `alt=""`, which is correct for decorative images. `o.gif` could not be verified in the truncated source.
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in `<head>`.
- **Plain-text fallback is 0 characters** (`[FAIL]`). This is a deliverability and accessibility gap for text-only clients. It could be a relay capture artifact, but treat it as a real gap until a raw MIME part is confirmed.
- **Fonts:** League Spartan is loaded via a Google Fonts `<link>` inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. This is correctly guarded for Outlook, and Gmail and others will fall back. No issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible HTML. The source was truncated, so this applies to the visible portion only.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool itself notes that the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unverified, not confirmed missing**. Check the raw message at the origin or in a seed inbox. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results` not captured**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sender is `marketing.carters.com`, but the envelope domain used by Attentive was not verified.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** not visible in the truncated source, so they could not be verified. The QA tool says an unsubscribe link is always probed, and none appears in the failure list.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. All CTAs are Attentive-wrapped, so the final URLs and any `utm_*` parameters are hidden behind the 403 redirects. The only visible campaign identifier is `mi_ecmp=11_26_2026_CAR_THU_AM_ENGAGERS` on the open pixel, which shows the send was an "engagers" segment. No landing-page checks were possible.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the 12 Attentive links with a browser-like UA and follow the redirects. Confirm final destinations return 200 and carry the expected UTMs. This will likely clear the 403 failures.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the two linked GIFs and the four `SERVICES_BANNER` PNGs. The linked ones matter most, since the alt text becomes the link's accessible name.
3. Populate `<title>` (for example with the subject line).
4. Ship a real `text/plain` part. The 0-char version is the one confirmed deliverability defect.
5. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC from the raw headers, not the relay copy.
6. Re-run the QA on the full, untruncated HTML, and probe the 26 skipped links, including the footer unsubscribe link.
