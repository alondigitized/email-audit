# 💙 Our best-seller — Bodysuits from $7 🩵
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-14**
## Executive summary

- This is Carter's cleanest bodysuit send in the recent run: a genuine hero image of four folded bodysuits (stripes, prints, solid navy) matched to a subject line and headline that actually agree — "Our Best-Seller — Bodysuits from $7" and "OUR BESTSELLING BODYSUIT." No holiday-costume bait-and-switch, no folded-in seasonal banner pretending to be something else. That consistency alone puts it ahead of the 2026-09-09 "All things holiday" send and the 2026-09-12 PJ send that broke its own promise in the very next section.
- But the email breaks its promise almost immediately after the hero: "WE THINK YOU'LL LOVE THESE..." is a 9-item grid of jeans, sweatpants, rompers, and a cardigan — only 2 of 9 tiles are bodysuits. Then it pivots into an OshKosh western-fall editorial with a model shot, then a red 70%-off clearance block, then a store locator. It's a well-produced Frankenstein of four unrelated marketing beats stitched under one bodysuit-flavored subject line.
- Net: strong top, unfocused middle, generic bottom. Above the fold this earns real credit; as a whole it's a mid-pack send.

## What's working

- Hero image is sharp, well-lit, on-brand, and actually shows the product named in the subject line (bodysuits).
- Subject, header, and hero copy are all aligned around the same "bestseller" bodysuit story — no bait-and-switch.
- Clear, unambiguous "Shop from $7" CTA button directly under the hero, no scrolling needed to find it.
- No render bugs — layout, image loading, and text are all clean throughout.
- Store locator block with "Get Directions" adds a genuine local-relevance touch.

## What's weak

- "We think you'll love these" grid is mostly non-bodysuit product (jeans, joggers, footed sleepers, a cardigan) — undercuts the bodysuit focus set up by the hero.
- The OshKosh "Usher in the New Frontier" section is a full unrelated sub-campaign wedged into a bodysuit email — visual whiplash from baby bodysuits to a western-styled model shot.
- The 70% off clearance banner is generic, undated, and disconnected from both the bodysuit offer and the OshKosh section — three offers competing in one email.
- No price shown on the actual bodysuit hero beyond "$7" in the CTA button — no product callouts, sizes, or pack count for a bestseller claim.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email down to one story: bodysuits hero + a "you'll love these" grid that's actually all bodysuits/multipacks. Move OshKosh and clearance into their own dedicated sends — this is the single biggest lift since right now three campaigns dilute each other.
- 2. If OshKosh and clearance must stay, demote them below a hard visual break and cap the whole email at two offers max, not three.
- 3. Add a supporting detail under "Shop from $7" — pack size (e.g., "5-pack") or fabric callout ("100% cotton") since "bestseller" claims usually lean on a concrete reason.
- 4. Tighten the grid section headline to match content, or fix the content to match the headline — right now they contradict each other on click.
