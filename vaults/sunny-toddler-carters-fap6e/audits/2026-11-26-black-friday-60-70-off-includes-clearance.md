# BLACK FRIDAY: 60-70% off (includes clearance) 🖤
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is carter's Black Friday escalation, and it's the strongest offer in the series. On 11/23 the headline was "50-60% off* site & store." On 11/21 it was the same 50-60% panel. Now the hero says "60-70% OFF almost everything" in huge black type on a pastel holographic background. The "*includes clearance" line backs up the subject, and a "lowest prices of the year" badge adds urgency. The eye lands on the number immediately, with one Shop Now button beneath it.
- The body is a clean stack of price-led modules. First come six $5.50-$10 product tiles, then a limited-edition PJs block from $6, then Shop It All with bodysuits from $4. A red "up to 70% off clearance" panel and price-match and gift-bag footers follow. The free-shipping bar on top is an extra hook. The weakness is that the module list is long and generic, and the hero doesn't say this is the final day or two. The long exclusions block is also a lot of fine print against a 60-70% claim. Still, it's a well-executed send and a clear step up from the 50-60% emails earlier in the week.

## What's working

- The hero number "60-70% OFF" is huge and unmissable, with one white Shop Now button.
- It's a real escalation over the 50-60% sends from 11/21 and 11/23, and the "lowest prices of the year" badge sells it.
- The red "FREE SHIPPING ON ALL ORDERS*" bar at the top adds a second concrete offer.
- The product tiles show real sale prices with strikethrough originals, such as $9.00 against $24.00.
- The "limited edition PJs from $6" block and the "$3 cozy socks" copy carry forward the PJ theme from the 11/25 send.
- The price-match promise and the 70%-off clearance panel with category buttons give shoppers an easy way into the sale.

## What's weak

- The hero never says when the sale ends, so there's no deadline, even though the subject says "BLACK FRIDAY."
- The email stacks many modules (product grid, PJs, Shop It All, clearance, price match, gift bag) with five or more CTAs, which dilutes the main message.
- The product grid shows no names or sizes, and no reviews or social proof appear anywhere.
- The legal and exclusions block at the bottom is long. Several asterisks are on the offer, and the free shipping and gift bag carry conditions that aren't visible up top.
- The PJ and socks images lean on flat-lay shots with no children shown, which is less emotional than the family hero from 11/25.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a clear deadline to the hero, such as "Ends Friday 11:59pm ET" or a countdown, so the urgency is credible.
- 2. Cut the module count. Keep the hero, one product grid, and the clearance panel, and drop the redundant Shop It All row.
- 3. Make the CTA specific, such as "Shop 60-70% off" or "Shop Black Friday deals," instead of a generic "Shop Now."
- 4. Put a short eligibility note beside the free-shipping bar (for example "no minimum") so the offer feels honest.
- 5. Add review stars or a "best seller" tag to a few of the products.
