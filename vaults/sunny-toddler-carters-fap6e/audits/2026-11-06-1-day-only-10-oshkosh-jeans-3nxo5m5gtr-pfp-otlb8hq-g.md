# 1 DAY ONLY - $10 OshKosh® jeans 🚨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- This is Day 3 of the 12 Days of Deals, and the daily-deal mechanic works. The hero is one clear offer: "$10 OshKosh® jeans" on a Day 3 tag, with a "today only!" badge and a Shop Now button over a clean denim photo. The six-product grid underneath is all $10 jeans with crossed-out prices ($28 to $40), so the savings are easy to see. The subject's "1 DAY ONLY" matches the hero.
- The weak spot is the stack above the hero. A "50-60% OFF* SITE & STORE, Ends Wednesday!" banner sits on top, and Wednesday was yesterday (Nov 5). Today is Friday, Nov 6, so the first thing a reader sees is expired, competing copy. The lower half then adds a 70% clearance block, a "shop it all" row, a store-locator banner and a long disclaimer block. Those dilute a send that should be one offer for one day.
- Overall it's solid but not sharp. A parent of small kids who needs jeans would click. Someone who doesn't would be pulled in several directions. It's weaker than the Nov 4 launch, which was cleaner.

## What's working

- The hero states one offer: "$10 OshKosh® jeans," with a "Day 3" tag and "today only!" badge.
- The product grid shows $10.00 against struck-through original prices ($28 to $40), which makes the discount concrete.
- The "coming 11.07 / 11.08 / 11.09" teaser ($8 character tees, $10 multipack bodysuits, $12 overalls) gives subscribers a reason to keep opening.
- The price-match banner under the hero adds trust right where the buyer decides.
- The pastel holographic style carries over from the earlier sends, so the series feels consistent.

## What's weak

- The "Ends Wednesday! 50-60% OFF*" banner at the top is stale on Friday. It competes with the hero and reads as a mistake.
- The email stacks too many offers: 50-60% off, the $10 jeans, 70% off clearance, "from $5" and "from $7", and the upcoming deals. The "today only" focus gets lost.
- The hero's Shop Now button is small and low-contrast white on denim, and it sits next to the "today only!" badge.
- The category tiles (Baby Girl, Toddler Girl, Girl, Baby Boy, Toddler Boy, Boy) are plain white boxes with no imagery. They're easy to skip.
- The footer is long and dense, with several screens of legal text. The email still ends with an asterisk-heavy "*50-60% OFF" disclaimer that doesn't relate to today's $10 jeans.

## Recommendations

- 1. Remove the stale top banner. A time-sensitive daily-deal send should not carry a promo that has already ended. Put the hero first.
- 2. Make the Shop Now button solid and high-contrast, such as blue or red, and place it directly under "$10 OshKosh® jeans."
- 3. Add a countdown or an "ends tonight at midnight" line to make the "1 day only" urgency credible.
- 4. Cut the 70% clearance block or move it below the footer fold. The send should be about the jeans.
- 5. Link each product tile to its own page, and add sizes or ratings (such as "sizes 12M-14") to cut friction.
