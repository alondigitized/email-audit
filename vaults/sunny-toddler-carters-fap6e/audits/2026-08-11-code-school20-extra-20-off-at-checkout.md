# Code: SCHOOL20 = Extra 20% off* at checkout ✨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuinely different creative from the hopscotch-chalk and lavender-denim heroes that have been on repeat all week — the quilted-fabric-texture hero with a clean green square frame is new, and the "UP TO 50% OFF PLUS EXTRA 20% OFF" stack with a visible code (SCHOOL20) is a real, specific offer. It's a step up from the fatigue of 8/8–8/10, but it's still back-to-school markdown mail with a generic 3x3 product grid and no urgency element (no countdown, no expiration date visible).
- The email is well-organized top to bottom: hero offer → product grid → Otter Avenue sub-brand spotlight → 70% off clearance by category → store locator → footer. That's a lot of email, and the clearance section at 70% off arguably competes with and undercuts the top-of-funnel 50%+20% message — a subscriber's eye may drift straight to the bigger number lower down.
- Overall this lands as a mid-pack send: real offer, real code, clean render, but no urgency and a cannibalizing second offer later in the email. Solid, not exceptional.

## What's working

- Clear, specific stacked offer in the hero: "UP TO 50% OFF PLUS EXTRA 20% OFF...with code: SCHOOL20" — a genuinely different visual (quilted fabric texture, green frame) from the repeated hopscotch/denim heroes this week.
- The code is displayed directly in the hero, not buried — reduces friction, no separate "reveal code" step visible.
- Otter Avenue sub-brand section has good, distinct lifestyle photography (model in denim + graphic sweatshirt) with a lighter, more playful support copy line.
- No render bugs — images load, text is legible, layout is clean and mobile-appropriate.
- Store locator block with address and "Get Directions" adds a useful local touch for shoppers near that store.

## What's weak

- The 70% off clearance banner near the bottom outguns the hero's "50% + 20%" offer, which undercuts the primary CTA's urgency and could pull clicks to a completely different section.
- No deadline/urgency anywhere in the email — no "ends tonight," no countdown, unlike several of this week's sends (e.g. 8/10's countdown-timer close).
- The 3x3 product grid has zero labels, prices, or CTAs on the tiles themselves — just images, so it reads as filler between the hero and the Otter Avenue block.
- Subject line's checkout-code framing doesn't hint at the size of the discount ("50% off" is nowhere in the subject), so the email undersells its own best number before the open.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real deadline in the hero ("Code ends tonight" / "Through 8/12") — this week's brand history shows urgency consistently outperforms (9/10 sends on 8/6, 8/7 vs. 6-7/10 on non-urgent sends).
- 2. Either drop the 70% clearance section or move it above the fold and make it the hero — don't let two competing top-line offers sit in the same email.
- 3. Add at least a starting price or category label under each grid tile so the "We think you'll love these" section has a functional CTA, not just decoration.
- 4. Surface the 50% number in the subject line since it's the stronger hook than the code mechanic alone.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your code's ready: 50% off + 20% more ✨`
