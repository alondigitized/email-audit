# 50% OFF* ENTIRE SITE & STORE 📚✏️
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- This is Carter's biggest number of the week — 50% off entire site & store — and the hero nails it: a hopscotch-chalk sidewalk background is genuinely fresh creative compared to the notebook-paper flash-sale hero that's run three-plus times since 7/30, and the offer sits in a clean white-bordered box that reads instantly. But two blocks down, the email undercuts its own headline by stacking a second, redder "UP TO 70% OFF CLEARANCE" banner with six category buttons — bigger, louder, and visually competing with the 50% off hero for the "main offer" slot.
- The merchandising grid is fine but generic — nine product tiles with no labels, prices, or callouts, just "we think you'll love these." The brand is doing double duty pushing back-to-school savings and clearance liquidation in the same breath, and a subscriber who's seen the 70%-off clearance banner in prior sends (it's a near-verbatim reuse of assets from the recurring clearance blasts) will read this as "another Carter's sale email," not the site-wide event the subject promises.
- Net: strong top-of-funnel hook, weak follow-through. This lands as a solid-but-not-great send — better than the repetitive clearance-flash-sale emails, worse than the 8/4 "sunny-day styles" send that stayed disciplined on one offer.

## What's working

- Hero background (chalk hopscotch grid) is new, on-theme for back-to-school, and not a repeat of the tired notebook-paper clearance creative.
- The 50% off callout is legible at a glance — big white type, clear bordered box, single "Shop Now" CTA.
- "Shop It All" section with real kid/toddler/baby lifestyle photography and named category links (Shop Baby/Toddler/Kid) gives clear, low-friction navigation.
- Store locator block with an actual address and "Get Directions" CTA is a nice, uncommon local-relevance touch.
- No render bugs — everything loads, no overlapping text, no broken images.

## What's weak

- Two competing sale offers (50% off site-wide + up to 70% off clearance) fight for primary-CTA attention; the eye doesn't know which one to act on.
- The nine-tile "We think you'll love these" grid has zero product names, prices, or badges — pure guesswork for the recipient.
- The red clearance block reuses the same look/weight this brand has leaned on across five sends this week, so it reads as filler rather than a distinct offer.
- Exclusions are dense and only surface in tiny italic type near the hero and again in a long legal block at the bottom — "Excludes new arrivals, select licensed styles, select outerwear..." is a lot of asterisked fine print for a "50% off ENTIRE SITE" headline.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero offer. If 50% off site-wide is the headline, drop the second full-width 70% clearance banner or shrink it to a single small link ("Clearance also on sale →") so it doesn't compete visually.
- 2. Add product name + price to at least the top row of the "We think you'll love these" grid — even three labeled items beats nine anonymous thumbnails.
- 3. Move the exclusions summary closer to the CTA in slightly larger type (not full legal block) so the offer feels honest up front rather than buried at the footer.
- 4. Swap the generic product grid header for something back-to-school specific ("Fresh first-day fits, 50% off") to reinforce the campaign theme established in the hero.
- **Subject Alt A:** `50% off everything for back-to-school (yes, everything)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Site & store: 50% off starts now 📚`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns just landed on clearance too — up to 70% off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `First-day outfits, backpacks, shoes — all half off through Sunday.`
