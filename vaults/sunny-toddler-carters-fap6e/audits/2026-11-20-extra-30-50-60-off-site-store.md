# Extra 30% + 50-60% off* site & store💥
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- This is carter's Black Friday workhorse, and it reads like one. A red panel carries "black friday 50-60% OFF* SITE & STORE" with "+ EXTRA 30% OFF select styles online with: FRIDAY30" and a Shop Now button. The eye lands on the offer immediately. It is nearly the same creative as the 2026-11-17 launch ("50-60% off* site & store + extra 30% online 🖤"), so this is a reminder send, not news.
- Below the hero, the email adds a price-match promise, a six-product grid with sale prices, a Buy 1, Get 2 Free OshKosh jeans block, category tiles, a 70% clearance panel, and a free gift bag with a $75+ in-store purchase. That is a lot of offers, and the hierarchy thins out after the hero. Four offers beyond the hero compete for the click.
- It works for a parent shopping for babies and toddlers, but it has no urgency. There is no countdown, no "ends tonight" and no "last days". After the daily deadline-driven sends earlier this week (the "today only" PJs, "LAST CHANCE" tees), this one feels flat.

## What's working

- The hero offer is unmistakable. "50-60% OFF" is the largest type in the email, on a saturated red field, with a visible Shop Now button.
- The FRIDAY30 code is stated plainly beneath the percentage, so the stacked discount is easy to grasp.
- The price-match line ("If a price drops within 14 days of your purchase, we'll match it") builds trust and lowers purchase risk.
- The product grid shows real prices with strikethroughs ($9.00 from $24.00, $5.50 from $18.00), which makes the savings concrete.
- The category tiles (Baby Girl, Toddler Girl, Girl, Baby Boy, Toddler Boy, Boy) and the "Shop It All" row let a shopper self-segment quickly.

## What's weak

- There is no deadline anywhere in the render. For Black Friday week this is a missed urgency lever, especially when the 11-16 and 11-17 sends used "today only" and "last chance" framing.
- Too many competing offers: 50-60% off, extra 30%, BOGO-style 2 free jeans, up to 70% clearance, and a gift bag. No single one wins after the hero.
- The product grid has no individual labels or CTAs. Prices appear, but there are no product names and no reviews or ratings.
- The "*" exclusions and "select styles" caveats on the offers are only resolved in the long fine print at the bottom. The footer disclaimers run very long and dense.
- The OshKosh block is a large denim flat-lay with no children or models, which feels off-brand for a kids' retailer and takes up a lot of vertical space.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a deadline. Put a countdown or "Ends Sunday" strip above the hero so the send has a reason to be opened today.
- 2. Cut the body to one secondary offer. Keep the extra 30% code and the price match, and drop or demote clearance and the gift bag.
- 3. Label the product grid with names and add a "Shop Doorbusters" button below it, so the grid links to something specific.
- 4. Replace the OshKosh flat-lay with a photo of a child wearing the jeans, and shorten the block.
- 5. Move the key exclusions into a short line directly under the hero so the "*" feels honest at a glance.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends Sunday: extra 30% off with FRIDAY30`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Code FRIDAY30 + 50-60% off, ends soon ⏰`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus we'll price match for 14 days. Baby tops from $4.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Doorbusters, OshKosh buy 1 get 2 free, and clearance up to 70% off.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a standard Stensul/Attentive-style table build with `lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag, a preheader and a mobile media query. Three things are confirmed. There is no plain-text part (0 chars). Every probed tracked link returned 403. Several images are missing alt text. The 16% QA pass rate comes almost entirely from the 403s, which are probably a probe artifact (see section 2).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **12 of 12 probed links returned 403.** All are `https://carters.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/<token>` click-tracking redirects. They cover the "Shop Now", "BUY 1, GET 2 FREE* OSHKOSH® JEANS", "carter's", "osh kosh bigosh", "little planet", "Otter Avenue" and "Skiphop" links, and four "Display images to show real-time content" links.
- **Likely cause is bot-blocking.** A uniform 403 across every tracked link on one ESP domain looks like bot filtering on the redirector. A link-level defect would not hit all of them. The final landing URLs were never resolved, so this is unconfirmed. A real-client or browser-based click test is needed before calling these broken.
- **Link text on the hero link is a page title.** One link reads `Black Friday | 50-60% Off* Site & Store + Extra 30% Off | Select Styles online w…`. This looks like image alt text or a title string reused as the link label. It is not a defect by itself, but it is a poor accessible name.
- **Four identical "Display images to show real-time content" links.** These point to separate tokens. They are probably alt text on Movable Ink dynamic image blocks (`mi.carters.com/p/rp/…png`), so the live-content images may not render when images are off.
- **Coverage gap.** Only 12 links were probed and 52 were skipped, so footer, social and unsubscribe links are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **`<title></title>` is empty.** This is confirmed in the source.
- **Images missing alt text (per QA):**
  - `https://mi.carters.com/p/up/745a17bbb73445ad/o.gif`
  - `https://mi.carters.com/p/rp/264477f43986d8bb.png`, a content image (a Movable Ink render)
  - Four CloudFront banners: `MzczMjo6U0VSVklDRVNfQkFOTkVSXzAx…` through `…0A_v3…`. The base64 decodes to `SERVICES_BANNER_01–04`.
- **The `ho.gif` flag is a false positive.** The source shows `alt=""` on the 1×1 `ho.gif` Attentive pixel, which is correct for a decorative image. `o.gif` is probably also a tracking pixel, but I can't confirm that from the truncated source.
- **No plain-text alternative.** QA reports "Text version is 0 chars". This may also be a relay-capture artifact, since the same pipeline flags missing headers.
- **Positives visible in the source:**
  - The Outlook (MSO) conditional blocks and the Google Fonts fallback inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` are correct.
  - `x-apple-data-detectors` is neutralised.
  - The preheader is hidden with `display:none;max-height:0;opacity:0`.
- **Preheader padding.** It uses `͏` and soft-hyphen filler to stop body text bleeding into the inbox preview. This is fine, though it adds roughly 1 KB of markup.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or subject. The tracking images carry `mi_u=1150794179` and `mi_ecmp=11_20_2026_CAR_FRI_AM_ENGAGERS`, so a subscriber ID and a campaign/segment tag are resolved. These are Movable Ink query parameters, not defects. The truncated source cannot show any in-body personalization.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found (WARN).** The relay may have stripped it. It cannot be confirmed from this data.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found (WARN).** The same caveat applies. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules expect both headers.
- **Authentication-Results not found (WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `marketing.carters.com` and the click domain is `carters.e.attentivemail.com`. Alignment can't be assessed without the headers.
- **Footer unverified.** The footer (physical address, unsubscribe link) was truncated, so CAN-SPAM body requirements can't be confirmed. The unsubscribe link is always probed, and it isn't among the 403 failures, but the data doesn't show its result.
- **Plain-text part is empty.** See section 3. This also counts as a deliverability and compliance hygiene issue.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Final destinations not verified.** Because all tracked links returned 403, the post-redirect URLs, UTM parameters and landing-page promo state were not captured.
- **Promo code not verifiable.** The preheader advertises `Code: FRIDAY30 (online exclusive)`. Nothing confirms the code is carried in the link or auto-applied.
- **Dynamic image URLs are unchecked.** `mi.carters.com/p/rp/…` renders are generated per open, and their content could not be verified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the Attentive redirects with a browser UA or Playwright and capture the final URL. Check UTMs, the `FRIDAY30` carry-through and the landing-page offer. Until then, treat the 12 × 403 result as unconfirmed.
2. Add a real plain-text part. A 0-char text body is the one deliverability defect that is confirmed regardless of the relay.
3. Fetch the raw `.eml` headers (via Gmail or a direct seed inbox) to confirm List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Then decide whether the two compliance warnings are relay artifacts.
4. Add descriptive alt text to the four `SERVICES_BANNER_0x` images and the `264477f43986d8bb.png` render. Set a non-empty `<title>`.
5. Replace the "Display images to show real-time content" fallback alt text on the dynamic blocks with meaningful alternatives, or add a static fallback.
6. Suppress or `alt=""` the `o.gif` pixel if it is a tracking image, and have the QA rules skip 1×1 pixels so they stop producing noise.
