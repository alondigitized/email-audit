# ⭐ Win a year’s worth of PJs ⭐
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-29**
## Executive summary

- This is a loyalty-driven sweepstakes send, and it works as a brand beat but is weak as a conversion email. The "Stellar Sleep Week Sleepstakes" star hero is the strongest element. It keeps the theme from the 10/29 "Up to 60% off glow-in-the-dark PJs 🌟" send, and the headline "WIN CARTER'S PJS FOR A YEAR!" is clear. The mechanic is narrow: 10 Rewards members each win a $200 gift card, and only by showing up at a store on November 1 between 2 and 3pm.
- That narrowness undercuts the email. After five days of COZY20 discount pushes (10/24 through 10/28), the subscriber gets no price offer here. Below the hero, the 3x3 product grid has no prices, no labels and no visible links to specific items. The grid sits under "SLEEP WEEK HAPPENING NOW!" with no stated deal behind it. The "Pick Your PJs" row (Glow-in-the-Dark, Character, Holiday) is a better, more specific category set. Overall it is a decent brand and traffic play, but a soft revenue email. I'd call it a 5.

## What's working

- The star-shaped "Stellar Sleep Week Sleepstakes" hero is on-theme, carries the teal brand color, and reads at a glance.
- The headline "WIN CARTER'S PJS FOR A YEAR!" states the prize plainly, and the body copy gives the winner count and dollar value ($200 x 10).
- The Rewards tie-in and the footer perks strip (curbside, cardholder perks, extra points in the app) reinforce loyalty.
- The "Pick Your PJs" row gives three clear category paths with arrow-style link labels.
- The "Find A Store" button repeats mid-email and again in the light-blue band, so the CTA is easy to find.

## What's weak

- The sweepstakes is only for Rewards members who show up in a store for one hour on one day. The email doesn't say how to join Rewards, and the real condition sits in plain body copy.
- The 3x3 product grid has no prices, no names and no discount signal. "Sleep Week happening now!" gives no offer.
- The "Find A Store" CTA is a gray outlined button that looks weak. It also sends people to a store locator rather than to shopping.
- The rules link ("*Click here for official rules.") is tiny and gray. The legal block at the bottom is long, and it covers coupon language that doesn't match this sweepstakes.
- The email has no urgency beyond November 1. It also doesn't mention the COZY20 sale that the previous sends were pushing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the Rewards sign-up or "Join free" CTA next to the sweepstakes copy, so non-members have a path in. Make the primary buttons solid teal.
- 2. Add price or offer labels to the grid (for example "PJs from $9" or the current percent off), or cut the grid to 3 hero products with prices.
- 3. Move "Pick Your PJs" up directly under the sweepstakes block. It is the most specific shopping content.
- 4. Put the date and time (Nov 1, 2-3pm) in the hero or a badge so it can't be missed.
- 5. Use an "Enter" or "Shop PJs" CTA alongside "Find A Store" so there is an online path.
- **Subject Alt A:** `10 winners: $200 in PJs, Nov 1 🌟`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Win $200 for Carter's PJs (Rewards members)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stop by a Carter's store Nov 1, 2-3pm for your chance to win.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Rewards members: 10 winners get a $200 gift card. See rules.`
