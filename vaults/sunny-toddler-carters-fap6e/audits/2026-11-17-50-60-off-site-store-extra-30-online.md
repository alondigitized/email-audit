# 50-60% off* site & store + extra 30% online 🖤
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- This is the main Black Friday launch for carter's, and the hero does the job. A red panel carries a huge "50-60% OFF* SITE & STORE", then "+ EXTRA 30% OFF select styles online with: FRIDAY30" and a Shop Now button. The eye lands on the number first. It's a big step up from the single-category $4, $6 and $7 doorbusters of the past week (the 11-16 "$6+ Limited edition family PJs" and the 11-15 "$4 Graphic tees & bodysuits" sends). Those were narrow. This one is the umbrella event.
- The body is where it loses focus. Below the hero there are six products, a bomber jacket block, a "shop it all" row, a 70% clearance block, a gift bag banner and a loyalty footer. That's seven or more competing asks, and none of them points to the hero deal. The "50-60% off*" headline is heavily qualified by the asterisk and the dense fine print. The 30% code applies only to "select styles", and the render never shows which ones. A baby and kids shopper can't tell what the real price will be.
- Judgement: a strong top fold on a high-stakes day, but a typical retail "everything" email below it. It will perform on the brand and the number, not on clarity.

## What's working

- The hero is unmissable: "black friday 50-60% OFF SITE & STORE" in huge white type on red, with a single Shop Now button.
- The code "FRIDAY30" is shown in the hero, so the extra 30% is actionable without a click.
- The price-match banner ("If a price drops within 14 days...") is a real trust builder for a deal-wary parent.
- The product grid shows real prices with strike-throughs ($9.00 from $24.00, $12.00 from $34.00), so the savings are concrete.
- The category chips (Baby Girl, Toddler Boy and so on) make it easy to self-segment.
- The "FREE* gift bag with your $75+ in-store purchase" banner gives a reason to go to a store.

## What's weak

- The fine print is dense and heavy. "50-60% off*" and "select styles" make the offer feel conditional.
- The email stacks too many offers: 50-60% off, an extra 30%, up to 70% off clearance, jackets from $7, and a gift bag. It competes with itself.
- The hero offers two numbers at once (50-60% and an extra 30%). The hero doesn't say how they combine, or which items qualify for the code.
- The tile prices ($9, $12, $10, $18.80, $19.60) don't show whether FRIDAY30 is already applied. The tiles also have no names or links I can identify.
- This is the heaviest-weight send of the week and there is no urgency. There's no end date or countdown, unlike the 11-16 send's "12 hours left" timer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one clear deal math line in the hero, e.g. "Extra 30% off with FRIDAY30 on top of 50-60% off. Ends Monday", and add a deadline.
- 2. Show a "price after code" line on at least the first three products, so shoppers see the final price.
- 3. Cut the lower modules to the hero, the grid, the price match and the clearance block. Drop the jackets block or move it into the grid.
- 4. Add a "Shop FRIDAY30 styles" button that goes to a landing page of eligible items, so "select styles" stops being a mystery.
- 5. Move the key terms (exclusions, one use per customer per day) into a one-line note under the CTA. Keep the full legal text at the bottom.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Black Friday is on: 50-60% off + extra 30% (code inside)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Code FRIDAY30 takes 30% off on top of 50-60% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Baby bodysuits from $4, jackets from $7, and we'll price match for 14 days.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Use FRIDAY30 online today. Plus up to 70% off clearance.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is structurally sound: it has a doctype, `lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag, MSO conditionals and a preheader. The QA run flagged 12 of 12 probed Attentive redirect links as HTTP 403, a missing plain-text part, and missing compliance and authentication headers. Most of these look like probe or relay artifacts rather than confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links returned 403.** Every one is a `https://carters.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/<token>` click-tracker. The links include "Shop from $7", "carter's", "osh kosh bigosh", "little planet", "Otter Avenue", "Skiphop", the hero link, and five "Display images to show real-time content" links.
  - A uniform 403 across a single redirect host points to bot or WAF blocking of the QA prober. It does not point to broken campaign links.
  - It is **unconfirmed** whether real recipients can reach the destinations. Final landing URLs were never resolved.
- **Link text is blank in the QA record for the first link.** "Shop from $7" is reported with no text, so it is likely an image-wrapped link. Check that the `<img>` has meaningful `alt`.
- **Five "Display images to show real-time content" links.** This text is the fallback alt for dynamic-content (live-content) images, and the links wrap them.
  - If the live-content service is unavailable, recipients see this literal string as the link label.
  - It is also a poor accessible name for a link.
- **Only 12 of 63 links were probed.** The other 51 are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty title.** `<title></title>` is present in the `<head>`. This is confirmed in the source.
- **Missing alt text, QA-flagged on 9 images.**
  - **`ho.gif` is a false positive.** The source shows `alt=""` on the Attentive 1×1 pixel, which is correct for a decorative image.
  - **Content images have no meaningful alt.** The CloudFront images (`MzczMjo6Q0FSXzExMTYyNl9TRUNPTkRBUllfOC5qcGc…`, `…U0VSVklDRVNfQkFOTkVSXzAx…` through `…0A…`, and `264477f43986d8bb.png`) are flagged. If they are banners or offer images, their offer text is invisible to screen readers and to image-blocked clients. The truncated source doesn't show whether the flag means a missing attribute or `alt=""`.
  - **`o.gif`** from `mi.carters.com` is a tracking pixel, so it only needs `alt=""`.
- **External web font.** League Spartan loads from `fonts.googleapis.com` inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. It is ignored by many clients, so fallback font stacks should be verified. The truncated HTML doesn't show them.
- **No other rendering issues are evident.** The responsive rules at `max-width:660px` and the `.desktop_hide` / `.mobile_hide` classes are standard. The preheader padding is hidden correctly.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered merge tokens such as `{{…}}` or `%%…%%`. The Movable Ink pixel carries a resolved `mi_u=1150794179`.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.**
  - Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
  - QA notes that AgentMail may strip these headers, so this is **unverified**, not a confirmed gap.
- **`Authentication-Results` is absent**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. The same relay caveat applies.
- **Plain-text part is 0 characters (FAIL).**
  - A multipart/alternative with no text part raises spam-filter risk and degrades text-only clients.
  - It may also be a relay-capture artifact. Verify against the raw message from the ESP.
- **Footer not verified.** The footer (physical address, visible unsubscribe link) is cut off in the truncated source. QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, and none appears in the failure list. Its presence is therefore unconfirmed rather than missing.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters can't be verified.** Destinations are hidden behind Attentive's encoded redirects, and probes were blocked.
- **Campaign identifiers are visible only in the Movable Ink pixel:** `mi_ecmp=11_17_2026_CAR_TUE_STO_ENGAGERS`, plus `mi_campaign=11_…`.
  - The `ENGAGERS` segment name is exposed in a URL query string.
  - It is low-risk, but it does disclose segmentation.
- **Several domains in use.** Links and the pixel use `carters.e.attentivemail.com`, `carters.attentivemail.com`, `mi.carters.com` and `cloudfront.net`, while the From domain is `marketing.carters.com`. Mixed tracking domains are a minor deliverability and branding consideration.
- **Promo code.** The preheader says `FRIDAY30`. Whether the landing page applies it can't be confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-probe the links with a browser-like client.** Use a real browser User-Agent, or follow the redirect once from a clean IP, to separate bot-blocking from real breakage. Resolve the final URLs and check their UTMs.
2. **Check the raw ESP message** for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`, and a `text/plain` part. Treat these as real findings only if they are absent there too.
3. **Fill the empty `<title>`** and give content images meaningful `alt`.
4. **Fix the "Display images to show real-time content" fallback.** Set a descriptive alt or fallback, or aria-label, on the live-content links.
5. **Raise probe coverage beyond 12 of 63 links** and confirm the footer unsubscribe link and physical address in the full HTML.
