## What's weak

- The email buries its own hook: after one hero section, it pivots to an unrelated 9-item grid (jeans, joggers, plain bodysuits) that has nothing to do with the "Sleep & Play PJs" subject.
- Too many competing CTAs stacked vertically — Shop from $8, Shop from $2.40, 70% off clearance (6 sub-buttons), price match, store locator — dilutes the single offer that earned the open.
- The 70% off clearance banner has an asterisk with no visible disclaimer nearby (it's pushed to the bottom legal block), which reads as a soft bait risk even though full terms exist further down.
- "New Baby Loves" section has no visible price or CTA copy tied to the products shown until a separate button below — a second disconnected pitch competing with the first.

- 2. If the grid stays, make it thematically related (other PJ styles/sizes) rather than jeans and joggers — right now it looks like a totally different campaign spliced in.
- 3. Move the 70% off clearance asterisk disclaimer directly under the banner, not buried in the footer legal block.
