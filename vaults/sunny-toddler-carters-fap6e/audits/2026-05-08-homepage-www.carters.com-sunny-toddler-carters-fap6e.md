# Homepage snapshot · www.carters.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Carter's is speaking my language immediately — a stacked discount code front and center, warm summer energy, and a mom-and-kid lifestyle image that literally looks like my Saturday. This is a deal-first brand doing exactly what I come here for: stacking offers, no fuss. It's very much pushing a summer sale moment right now with a concrete promo code and a cheerful, sunlit vibe.

## What's working

- **The stacked discount copy** — "up to 40% OFF* PLUS, EXTRA 20% OFF with CODE: SUMMER20" is exactly the coupon-stacker energy I come to Carter's for. Two discounts, one screen, code right there. Done.
- **The "Shop Now" button** — white on the warm gradient, impossible to miss. No friction getting started.
- **The top banner strip** — "Code SUMMER20 = extra 20% off* select styles!" reinforces the offer at the very top. Double exposure means I won't miss it even if I only glance.
- **The lifestyle image at the bottom of the viewport** — a young mom in sunglasses playing with her kid is *me*, and it's pulling me down the page naturally.
- **The search bar** — prominent, full-width, right below nav. I know what I want (6-month pajamas) and I can get there instantly.
- **The chat widget** — bottom right, blue bubble, non-intrusive. Good to know it's there if sizing questions come up.

## What's weak

- **"Shop Now" CTA is generic** — it doesn't tell me what I'm shopping. "Shop Baby Sale" or "Shop Pajamas & More" would get me clicking faster because I'm not here for a treasure hunt.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — as a mom who's constantly calculating whether I'm hitting the minimum, I want that number on the first screen. A "$35 ships free" note would seal the deal immediately.
- **No urgency signal** — there's no "ends Sunday" or countdown anywhere in the viewport. A deal this good deserves a deadline to push me off the fence.
- **The asterisks everywhere** — "40% OFF*" and "select styles" appear three times above the fold. It's standard retailer language but it seeds doubt in my deal-stacker brain about what's actually included.
- **Loyalty/rewards not surfaced** — I have Carter's rewards and I know that bell badge probably means points waiting, but nothing is calling it out. A "You have $X in rewards — shop now" would send me straight to checkout.

## Recommendations

- **Make the CTA category-specific**: swap "Shop Now" for "Shop Baby & Toddler Sale" — one word change, dramatically higher relevance for moms landing here.
- **Add a free-shipping callout to the hero or top banner**: move "Free shipping on $35+" next to or below the SUMMER20 code strip so deal-stackers can do the math without hunting.
- **Add a deadline to the promo**: even "Offer ends Sunday" in small text under the CTA creates urgency without changing the visual design.
- **Surface the rewards balance in the nav**: if the bell badge means I have points or rewards, say "Rewards: $X" on tap — don't make me hunt through the account menu to remember why I should care.
