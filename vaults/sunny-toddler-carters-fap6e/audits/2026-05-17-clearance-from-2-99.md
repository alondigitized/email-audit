# Clearance from $2.99!🔥
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- This is Carter's seventh email in seven days — a relentless clearance drumbeat capped by a $2.99 floor-price hero. The anchor is genuinely sharp: "$2.99 FROM CLEARANCE / More markdowns added!" lands immediately, with gender/age category pills below so subscribers can self-route in one tap. For a value-seeking parent it's a credible, legible offer.
- The problem is structural confusion. Carter's embeds the BOGO ("Buy 1, get 2 free* play-ready tops & bottoms") as a full mid-email module — identical in substance to yesterday's 5/16 send scored 9/10. Then a third "Lowest Prices of the Season" module competes below that. Three offers, three CTAs, one email. The clearance hero gets diluted the moment the reader scrolls even a centimeter.
- The send is competent but fatigued. A subscriber who opened yesterday's BOGO email arrives here with nothing new to act on except a price floor that, once they realize it's "from $2.99 on the cheapest tee," doesn't feel as generous as the subject promised. Solid execution on a tired idea.

## What's working

- **$2.99 anchor is immediately visible** — "FROM $2.99 CLEARANCE" is the largest text in the email and lands before any scroll.
- **Category pills are well-placed** — Baby Girl / Toddler Girl / Girl / Baby Boy / Toddler Boy / Boy give instant self-routing without forcing the subscriber to navigate a crowded landing page.
- **Clean render** — no broken images, no overlapping text, no layout cracks visible.
- **"DOORBUSTERS" label** sets expectation of steep pricing from the moment the email opens.
- **Short, punchy subject** (~22 chars) reads fully on every mobile device.

## What's weak

- **BOGO module is a straight repeat of yesterday's 5/16 send** — a subscriber who opened that email (scored 9/10) gains nothing new here; the recycle signals creative exhaustion.
- **Three competing offers kill hierarchy** — clearance pricing, then BOGO, then "Lowest Prices of the Season" turns a price-floor email into a catalogue scroll.
- **"Shop Now" CTA is generic** — doesn't reinforce the $2.99 promise or tell the subscriber what they're navigating to.
- **No urgency mechanism** — the subject implies heat (🔥) but there's no deadline, countdown, or "while supplies last" reminder in the body.
- **Seventh email in seven days** — cadence fatigue is real; the subscriber has seen Carter's every single day this week, making any "lowest price" claim feel routine rather than remarkable.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill the BOGO module.** It was yesterday's hero — running it again as a secondary module trains subscribers to tune it out. Replace with a "New to clearance today" product grid that reinforces the freshness claim ("More markdowns added!").
- 2. **Make the CTA specific.** Change "Shop Now" → "Shop $2.99+ Clearance" or "See All Clearance Deals" so the click destination is unambiguous.
- 3. **Add one urgency signal.** A simple "Prices drop as stock sells — shop now" line under the hero, or a stock-scarcity badge on 2-3 products, would justify the 🔥 in the subject.
- 4. **Collapse to one offer.** Either the clearance floor price OR the BOGO, not both. Pick the stronger hook (clearance wins at $2.99) and commit.
- 5. **Thin the cadence.** After seven consecutive days, consider a 1-day gap before the next send — or segment this to clearance-intenders only, not the full list.

- **Subject Alt B:** `$2.99 clearance — new drops added today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Baby, toddler & kids styles just marked down — prices drop as stock sells.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop by age: Baby Girl, Toddler Boy, and more — clearance starts at $2.99.`
