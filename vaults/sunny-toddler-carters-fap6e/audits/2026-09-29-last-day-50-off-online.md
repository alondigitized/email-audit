# LAST DAY! 50% off* online 🖤
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, well-built Black Friday teaser, but the subject line is a lie. It says "LAST DAY!" and the body says "(super) Early Black Friday!" The countdown timer at the top shows 00 days, 13 hours, 35 minutes, 49 seconds, so the urgency is real. The hero itself never says "last day" or "ends tonight". The black panel with the holographic-foil diagonal, the "50% OFF* ONLINE" headline and the flat-lay of fleece PJs, bodysuits and sweaters is strong and lands immediately, like the 09-25 and 09-28 sends.
- The email is a bit thin after the hero. "We think you'll love these…" is nine unlabeled, unpriced product tiles: jeans, white bodysuits, joggers and footwear-less pajamas. There are no prices, no names and no reviews. The "Early Black Friday deals" row (bodysuits from $5, cozy PJs from $8, lounge from $8) does better, because it has real prices. The red "Up to 70% off clearance" block then adds a second offer that competes with the 50% headline.
- Carter's has now sent Black Friday-flavored 50% off for five days running (09-25, 09-26, 09-27, 09-28, and today). The countdown is the only thing that's new. I'd score it decent, and it earns its slot as the closing send of a run. But it's another near-identical send, and the subject doesn't match the hero.

## What's working

- The hero is high-contrast, and "50% OFF* ONLINE" is unmissable in large white type on black.
- The countdown timer at the top gives real, visible urgency, with 13 hours left.
- The "Early Black Friday deals" row has concrete price points: bodysuits from $5, cozy PJs from $8, lounge from $8.
- The Rewards, curbside and app-perks icon row is a tidy footer for loyalty benefits.
- The layout is clean and scannable, with a single "Shop Now" button in the hero.

## What's weak

- The hero never says "last day", so the subject's urgency depends on the timer alone.
- The "We think you'll love these…" grid has nine tiles with no prices, names or labels. It's dead weight, and it's not clear what the tiles link to.
- The red "Up to 70% off clearance" block competes with the 50% hero and muddies the single offer.
- The asterisked 50% has a long block of fine print with many exclusions (Little Planet, Skip Hop, Otter Avenue, outerwear and more), and none of it is visible above it.
- It's the fifth near-identical 50% Black Friday send in five days, so fatigue is likely.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero: "Ends tonight" or "Last day", next to the 50% headline. Then the subject and the creative agree.
- 2. Cut the nine-tile grid, or add prices and a "from $X" label to each tile so it does some work.
- 3. Move the "Early Black Friday deals" row up and make it the main product section, since it's the only part with prices.
- 4. Either drop the clearance block or demote it, so one offer leads.
- 5. Add a short line of exclusions near the hero, for example "excludes Little Planet and Skip Hop".

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is structurally sound (`lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` layout tables, responsive `@media (max-width:660px)` rules, preheader). The QA run flagged 12 of 12 probed links as 403, a missing plain-text part, and missing alt text on several images. Most of the link and header findings are probably artifacts of the probe and the AgentMail relay, and I've marked each one that way below.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **12/12 probed links returned 403.** Every one is an Attentive click-tracking redirect (`https://carters.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...`). The affected links include:
  - the logo ("Carter's")
  - the brand tiles (osh kosh bigosh, little planet, Otter Avenue, Skiphop)
  - the hero ("(super) Early Black Friday!...")
  - six product image links (jeans, bodysuits, joggers)
- **Likely bot-blocking, not a broken campaign.** Uniform 403s across all link types on a single ESP redirect domain point to the tracker rejecting the QA client. If the links were dead, I'd expect 404/410 or a mix of statuses. I can't confirm recipient behavior from this data, so treat this as unverified. Re-probe with a browser-like UA and follow redirects to the final destination.
- **All links are wrapped in ESP tracking redirects.** Each URL is a long opaque token (300+ characters). This is normal for Attentive but hides the final destination from the audit. 47 links were skipped by the prober.
- **Open-tracking pixels are present.**
  - `https://carters.attentivemail.com/ss/o/.../ho.gif` (Attentive)
  - `https://mi.carters.com/p/up/745a17bbb73445ad/o.gif?mi_u=1150794179&mi_ecmp=09_29_2026_CAR_TUE_AM_LARGEAUDIENCE` (Movable Ink)
- **The Movable Ink URLs carry a subscriber-level ID** (`mi_u=1150794179`). This is standard, but confirm it isn't forwarded or logged anywhere it shouldn't be.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in `<head>`. This is confirmed in the source.
- **Four banner images have no alt attribute.** These are the `d3ptznaucccg76.cloudfront.net/MzczMjo6U0VSVklDRVNfQkFOTkVSXzAx...` files (`SERVICES_BANNER_01` to `_04_v3`). If they carry content, such as offers or links, they are invisible to screen readers and to clients that block images.
- **Two Movable Ink images may need alt text.** `o.gif` looks like a tracking pixel and is probably fine decorative. `mi.carters.com/p/rp/264477f43986d8bb.png?...mi_campaign` is a dynamically rendered image. If it carries visible content, it needs alt text.
- **`ho.gif` warning is a false positive.** The source shows `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"` inside a `display:none` wrapper. That is correct for a decorative pixel.
- **Web font is loaded via `<link>` to Google Fonts (League Spartan).** It sits inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, which is correct, but Gmail and many clients ignore it. I couldn't verify a fallback stack because the source is truncated.
- No other rendering issues are visible in the truncated HTML.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%...%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source. Only the first part of the HTML was available, so the rest is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text fallback is 0 characters (FAIL).** If the message really lacks a `text/plain` part, that hurts deliverability and accessibility. The relay may have dropped the part, so check the raw MIME on the original send.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers not found.** The QA tool itself notes these may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. This is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both, so verify against the raw headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown, again because of the relay. The From domain (`marketing.carters.com`) and the tracking domains (`attentivemail.com`, `carters.e.attentivemail.com`) differ, so confirm DKIM alignment on the raw message.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** neither was visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm them. The QA tool says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result is listed for one.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Can't be verified.** All destinations sit behind Attentive redirects, every probe returned 403, and the HTML is truncated. UTM parameters and landing page alignment are unconfirmed.
- The only campaign identifier visible is Movable Ink's `mi_ecmp=09_29_2026_CAR_TUE_AM_LARGEAUDIENCE`. That is a tracking label, not a UTM.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run link probing with a browser-like user agent and redirect following, so the 403s can be classified as bot-blocking or genuinely broken. Then capture final URLs and UTMs.
2. Inspect raw headers and MIME on the original message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results` and the `text/plain` part. Don't score these from the relay copy.
3. Add a non-empty `<title>`.
4. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four CloudFront banners and to the Movable Ink content image. Leave decorative pixels as `alt=""`.
5. Suppress the `ho.gif` alt-text warning in QA when `alt=""` is present, since it's a false positive.
6. Feed the QA checker the full HTML, not a truncated copy, so the footer, unsubscribe and address checks can run.
