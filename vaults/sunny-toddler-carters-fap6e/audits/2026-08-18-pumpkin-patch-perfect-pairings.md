# Pumpkin-patch perfect pairings 🎃
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-18**
## Executive summary

- The hero here is genuinely nice — a warm, well-shot sibling-matching photo with "gather the fam" script overlay, and it actually delivers on the subject line's pumpkin-patch/fall-family framing, unlike some of the recent stock-fabric-texture sends in this brand's history. But it's a bait-and-switch structurally: the subject promises "pumpkin-patch perfect pairings," the hero sells "Just In For Fall" sibling matching sets, and then the email pivots hard into a generic "UP TO 70% OFF CLEARANCE" red block with a 9-item product grid that has zero connection to fall matching or pumpkin patches — it's random jeans, onesies, and rompers.
- This is the brand's biggest offer in the recent history I've seen (70% beats the 8/17 "up to 75%"* clearance and the 40-50% stacks from the school-savings run), so the deal itself is strong. But the email tries to do two campaigns in one send — full-price seasonal lifestyle push, then deep clearance — and neither wins clearly because the CTA hierarchy is split three ways (Shop Family Matching / grid images with no buttons / six category buttons under Clearance).
- Net: strong visual asset, confused message. This reads like a template stitched from two different priorities rather than one coherent campaign.

## What's working

- The hero photo is high quality, on-brand, and directly supports the "pumpkin-patch perfect pairings" subject with real sibling-matching product in shot.
- Single clear primary CTA button ("Shop Family Matching") directly under the hero and subhead.
- The 70% off clearance banner has strong color contrast (red block, white bold text) and is impossible to miss scrolling down.
- Six segmented category buttons (Baby Girl/Boy, Toddler Girl/Boy, Girl/Boy) under clearance let shoppers self-select without extra clicks.
- Store locator block with address, map thumbnail, and "Get Directions" adds useful local relevance.

## What's weak

- The "We think you'll love these..." 9-image grid has no CTA buttons, no prices, and no product names — just photos, so there's nothing to click to act on them.
- Total message split three ways: seasonal matching sets, an unrelated product grid, and deep clearance — no single throughline.
- The clearance section's product grid images bear no relation to "pumpkin patch" styling — it's jeans and basic onesies, undercutting the seasonal narrative the hero set up.
- "*" asterisk on 70% OFF is unresolved until the very bottom fine print — buried disclaimer pattern flagged in past sends too (8/17 "75% off*" had the same issue).
- No urgency/deadline anywhere in the body — "New Markdowns Added" implies freshness but gives no expiration.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one message for this send: either lead with the fall-matching lifestyle story and drop the disconnected clearance product grid, or lead with the 70% off deal and make the hero product shown match that discount — don't run both as equals.
- 2. Add prices or "Shop Now" links directly under each of the 9 grid images — right now they're decorative dead weight.
- 3. Move the exclusions/fine-print asterisk resolution ("Savings based on MSRP...") up near the 70% callout, not buried at the email's bottom.
- 4. Add a real deadline to the clearance banner (e.g., "Ends Sunday") — none of the recent brand history sends have used credible urgency either, and it would differentiate this send.
- 5. Swap at least 2-3 of the grid images for pumpkin-patch/fall-themed pieces to keep the seasonal thread alive through the whole email.
