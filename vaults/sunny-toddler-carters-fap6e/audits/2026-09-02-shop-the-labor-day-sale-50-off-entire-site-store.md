- This is the same pink-orange sunset gradient hero and folded-laundry-pile photo that's run all week — the exact composition that carried the 8/26–8/30 sends — but today it's paired with new, better-organized "Just for you" merchandising blocks below it: a 6-tile grid (denim, onesies, PJs) plus a dedicated Buy 1 Get 2 Free denim section with its own OshKosh product shot. ## What's working

- The 6-tile "Just for you" grid gives concrete, clickable product categories instead of a single generic banner.
- The Buy 1, Get 2 Free Denim section has its own hero image and clear CTA, giving a second distinct reason to click beyond the sitewide %.
- Store locator block with map, address, and "Get Directions" adds a genuine local-relevance touch.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text is legible, buttons render correctly.
- Rewards/loyalty icons (curbside, cardholder perks, extra points) are visible near the footer for existing members.

## What's weak

- Hero image is the identical pink-orange sunset gradient + folded-laundry pile that's run essentially unchanged since 8/26 — six-plus days of the same creative.
- Subject line is nearly a word-for-word repeat of 8/30's "50% off* entire site & store" — no new hook for someone who already saw that one.
- Three separate offers (50% sitewide, BOGO denim, up to 50% off Halloween) stacked in one email dilutes which CTA to prioritize.
- The Halloween "Prep for Spooky Season" block feels off-cadence for a Labor Day-titled email — mixed seasonal messaging in the same send.

- 2. Pick one primary offer for the top fold; move BOGO denim and Halloween down as secondary blocks (they already are, but the subject/hero should reflect a single headline over "everything").
- 3. Vary the subject line so it doesn't read as a rerun of 8/30.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Buy 1, get 2 FREE denim — plus 50% off everything`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Spooky season is here 🎃 + Labor Day 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New drops, BOGO denim, and Halloween PJs — all in one sale`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Skeleton PJs, pumpkin graphics, and 50% off sitewide`
- 4. Add a short line of urgency near the top ("Sale ends tonight") if the deadline is real — nothing in the render signals time-boundedness.
