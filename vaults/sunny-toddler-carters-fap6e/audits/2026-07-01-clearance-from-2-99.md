# CLEARANCE FROM $2.99 🚨🚨
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-01**
## Executive summary

- This is day nine of Carter's same clearance-event run that kicked off June 23, and the creative is showing it. The $2.99 anchor is still prominent and the email renders cleanly, but there is no new reason to click today versus the June 29 send ("100s of styles from $2.99 👀") — no refreshed inventory claim, no deadline, no new category hook. The brand has now trained its list to treat $2.99 clearance as a permanent fixture rather than an urgent event.
- The "PICKS FOR YOU" personalization grid is the single thing that keeps this from feeling like a pure batch-and-blast. But the incongruous "NEW & TRENDING / Shop Summer Staples" block at the bottom is recycled wholesale from the June 27 Summer Staples email and actively undermines the clearance message — it tells the reader this is a template fill, not a curated send.
- The subject line's ALL CAPS + double 🚨🚨 pairing is the clearest signal of creative fatigue. A single siren on day one reads urgency; two sirens on day nine reads desperation. Without an end date anywhere in the email, the urgency is performative — and subscribers have learned it.

## What's working

- The coral hero banner with "FROM $2.99 CLEARANCE EVENT" is above the fold, immediate, and legible — the offer lands before any scroll.
- "PICKS FOR YOU" breaks the broadcast feel; a personalized grid is the right mechanic for a repeat clearance send.
- "SHOP IT ALL" category row — Bodysuits / Shorts / Rompers, each with a price anchor — gives navigation structure without requiring the reader to browse blind.
- Email renders cleanly: no broken images, no layout gaps, no overlapping text.
- Carter's Rewards badge appears in the footer, reinforcing the loyalty ecosystem.

## What's weak

- Subject ALL CAPS + double 🚨🚨 reads as a panic signal on the ninth consecutive send — it's the boy who cried wolf.
- No urgency deadline anywhere in the email body: no "ends Sunday," no "while supplies last + a date," no countdown. Without it the $2.99 offer has no reason-to-act-now.
- The "NEW & TRENDING / Shop Summer Staples" section is copy-pasted from the June 27 Summer Staples email and is directly off-message for a clearance send — it dilutes the one job this email has.
- "Shop Now" is the weakest CTA possible; after nine days of the same event, it needs more pull — a category ("Shop $2.99 Bodysuits") or a deadline ("Shop Before It's Gone — Ends July 4").
- Nine emails in nine days has burned the cadence; this send arrives with negative goodwill already priced in.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a deadline, everywhere.** "Ends July 6" in the hero, in the category row, in the footer. This is the single change that would lift click rate most on a mature clearance send. The entire event has been running without a visible expiry.
- 2. **Cut the Summer Staples block.** The "NEW & TRENDING" section is off-brief and signals creative padding. Replace it with a "bestsellers in clearance" row tied to the best-converting categories — bodysuits historically drive Carter's clearance volume.
- 3. **Fix the subject line.** Drop ALL CAPS. Drop one siren. Add the deadline.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Clearance ends Sunday — styles from $2.99`
- **Subject Alt B:** `$2.99 clearance: last days on 100s of styles`
- 4. **Write a real preheader.** The hero banner copy ("FROM $2.99 CLEARANCE EVENT") is likely bleeding into the preheader and repeating the subject verbatim.
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Bodysuits, rompers, shorts — stock up before it's gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your picks are waiting — clearance from $2.99 ends soon.`
- 5. **Sharpen the primary CTA.** Swap "Shop Now" for "Shop $2.99 Bodysuits" or "Shop Clearance — Ends July 6."
