# 60% off online ENDS TODAY 💫
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- This is the same-day repeat of this morning's "TODAY ONLY - 60% off 🩵" send, with a harsher subject. The creative is nearly identical: the teal starburst hero says "60% off* online, today only, 100s of styles added" under a live countdown at 00:09:55:39. The subject now shouts "ENDS TODAY," and the countdown makes that credible.
- The layout is clean. The hero is followed by a Halloween block ("Prep for spooky season!") and a "Shop it all" row with entry prices (Tees from $6, Licensed from $10, Sets from $11). Below that is a red "$2.99+ Clearance" block with six audience buttons. There are no render bugs.
- The weakness is repetition. A subscriber who opened the morning send gets almost the same email again, with no new reason to click. Two competing offers (60% off and $2.99+ clearance) and a store-opening block for a Commerce, CA location dilute the one-day urgency. It's a solid, competent send, but it's the second one today and offers nothing new.

## What's working

- The live countdown timer sits at the very top and backs up "ends today" with a real deadline.
- The hero gives one number and one deadline ("60% off* online, today only") with a visible Shop Now button.
- The Halloween block is timely, with a skeleton onesie and pumpkin pants flat lay that fits mid-October.
- The "Shop it all" row shows entry prices ($6, $10, $11), which gives concrete anchors.
- The six-button Baby/Toddler/Girl/Boy grid in the clearance block lets a parent jump straight to their kid's category.
- The rewards and curbside icon row in the footer is a reasonable place for loyalty reminders.

## What's weak

- It is a near-duplicate of this morning's "TODAY ONLY - 60% off" send, so it repeats rather than escalates. Week-long cadence (10-08 to 10-11) has been all back-to-back promos.
- The CTA is the generic "Shop Now". The secondary button ("Shop up to 60% off*") is slightly better but still doesn't name a product.
- The "$2.99+ Clearance" block competes with the 60% hero and muddies the one-day message.
- The "Now open near you!" store block is a local pitch that probably doesn't apply to most recipients and breaks the flow.
- The 60% headline carries an asterisk with "Select Styles," while the hero implies broader coverage. The exclusions sit at the bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Don't send a near-identical second email on the same day. If you must, change the content, for example an "Last hours" version that leads with the top three Halloween or sleepwear items and a real price, not the same hero.
- 2. Make the CTA specific: "Shop Halloween PJs" or "Shop 60% off tees" instead of "Shop Now."
- 3. Drop or move the store-opening block and keep the email focused on one offer. If clearance stays, put it below the product grid.
- 4. Add social proof or a bestseller tag to the product row, since the email currently has no reviews.
- 5. Show the exclusions in a short line under the hero so the "select styles" caveat isn't a surprise.
