# 🎉 Early Black Friday - 50% off* site & store
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-25**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, well-executed Black Friday teaser — the black-and-holographic-foil hero pops immediately, "50% OFF SITE & STORE" is unmissable, and the flat-lay product shot (fleece PJs, bodysuits, sweaters) is on-brand and appealing. But it's fundamentally the same email skeleton Carter's has run all September — swap the hero color, keep the "you've got 15% off" banner, keep the tiered "up to 70% off clearance" red block, keep the category grid. A subscriber who's opened even two of the last five sends will recognize this instantly.
- The "(super) Early Black Friday" framing is a smart seasonal pivot — it's the first genuinely new campaign beat since the "5-Star Baby Event" and "Buy 1 Get 2 Free" runs, which is worth crediting. But it's undercut by stacking three separate offers (50% off, 15% off personalization banner, up to 70% clearance) with no clear priority, which dilutes the "early Black Friday" urgency that should be the whole point of this send.
- Solid, professional execution, mediocre differentiation. A 6/10 send that would be a 7-8 if it trimmed the competing offers and leaned harder into "Black Friday" as a distinct, less-frequent event.

## What's working

- Hero is legible at a glance: dark background, bold white "50% OFF SITE & STORE" text, holographic diagonal stripe signals a "special/premium" event tier visually.
- Product flat-lay is well-styled and season-appropriate (fleece, PJs, sweaters) without being cluttered.
- Clear single CTA button ("Shop Now") directly under the hero offer.
- Good structural variety below the fold — bestseller grid, price-anchored deal callouts ("Bodysuits from $5"), then a distinct clearance block — gives multiple entry points.
- "Now open near you" store-locator module is a nice, uncommon touch for a real-life/omnichannel nudge.

## What's weak

- Three competing offers in one email (15% personalized, 50% site & store, up to 70% clearance) with no visual hierarchy telling the reader which one actually applies to them.
- The "You've got 15% off" banner is the same recycled personalization module from the 2026-09-23 send — it reads as boilerplate, not personalization, at this point.
- "Early Black Friday" is a strong hook but there's no date/countdown/deadline anywhere in the visible email — no urgency mechanism to back up "early."
- Red clearance block's 6-button category grid (Baby Girl/Toddler Girl/Girl/Baby Boy/Toddler Boy/Boy) is repetitive layout filler that adds scroll without adding information.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero offer and mute the rest — lead with "Early Black Friday: 50% off," move the 15%-off banner into the hero itself as "plus your 15% unlocks extra savings" rather than a separate top module, and drop or shrink the clearance block to a single link.
- 2. Add a visible countdown or "ends [date]" to justify "Early Black Friday" — right now nothing distinguishes this from any other 50%-off send.
- 3. Replace the 6-button clearance grid with 2-3 fewer, larger tiles (e.g., just "Girls" and "Boys") to cut visual repetition.
- 4. Vary the creative — this hero style (dark background, foil stripe) risks becoming as fatigued as the orange starburst "5-Star Baby Event" wrapper if reused past this one send.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All 12 probed material links resolve through the Attentive click-tracking domain `carters.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...` and every one returned HTTP 403, including the primary CTA ("Tap to Apply"), logo link ("Carter's"), nav links ("osh kosh bigosh," "little planet," "Skiphop," "Otter Avenue"), the preview/subject link, and all product image links (jeans, bodysuits, joggers).
