# ENDS SOON: Up to 60% off (Early) Black Friday ‼️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the seventh Black Friday preview send in about a week. It does one thing better than the earlier ones: the countdown timer (00 days, 09 hours, 53 minutes) now matches the "ENDS SOON" subject. It is the same email as this morning's "FINAL COUNTDOWN", with the timer about four hours further down. The black "Black Friday preview / UP TO 60% OFF*" banner sits over a green flat-lay of kids' outfits with a single "Shop Now" button. The hierarchy is clear and nothing is broken.
- The body is where it loses steam. The "Just for you" grid is nine bare product tiles (jeans, joggers, bodysuits) with no prices, names or labels. The OshKosh "From the Vault" block is lifted from the Oct 18 "Favorites from the vault" send. The "UP TO 70% OFF* CLEARANCE" block adds a second, bigger number that competes with the 60% hero. A subscriber who has seen six near-identical sends will recognize this as the same creative with a clock on it.
- Verdict: competent and clear, but fatigued. The urgency is real, but nothing is new, and the hard-priced hooks that worked earlier ("Fleece PJs from $8", "Bodysuits from $2") are pushed down to small links in the middle.

## What's working

- The countdown timer at the top gives the "ENDS SOON" subject a credible deadline you can see.
- The hero is clean: high-contrast black banner, a bright "UP TO 60% OFF*" line, "baby to big kid" scope, and one obvious "Shop Now" button.
- The two-up row gives concrete prices ("Shop from $7", "Shop from $2 Per Piece"), which beat the vague percentage.
- The footer strip (curbside pickup, Rewards, in-app points) and the store-locator block are tidy and on-brand.
- The layout renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The "Just for you" grid shows nine untitled, unpriced tiles with no headline offer, so it adds scroll without giving a reason to click.
- The "UP TO 70% OFF* CLEARANCE" block undercuts the 60% hero, so there are two competing headline offers.
- The OshKosh Vault block repeats the Oct 18 creative and reads as filler in an urgency email.
- The hero uses a generic "Shop Now" button, and the asterisked terms are buried in small grey text at the very bottom.
- This is the seventh near-identical preview send in a week, so the "ends soon" claim feels like routine rather than a real deadline.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a hard price in the hero ("Fleece PJs from $8" or "Bodysuits $2 each") next to the 60% claim. This is the single biggest lift, and Oct 17's "$8+ Cozy fleece PJs" showed it works.
- 2. Replace the unlabeled grid with 3–4 priced, named products and a "was/now" price, or cut it.
- 3. Remove the OshKosh Vault block from this send, or save it for a non-urgency email.
- 4. Fold the clearance block into one line, or drop it. Keep one offer per send.
- 5. Change the CTA to something specific, like "Shop Black Friday Preview Deals", and add a one-line summary of the exclusions near the button.
- 6. Pause the daily preview cadence for 48 hours so the next send reads as a real last-chance email.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The Attentive-sent HTML is structurally sound: it has a doctype, `lang="en"`, a viewport meta, MSO conditionals and a mobile breakpoint at 660px. The automated QA data is largely inconclusive, though. All 12 probed links returned 403, and the plain-text part, `List-Unsubscribe` and auth headers couldn't be read. The only issues confirmed from the source are missing alt text on four banner images and an empty `<title>`.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links returned 403**, including the hero CTAs ("Shop from $7 >"), brand tiles ("osh kosh bigosh", "little planet", "Otter Avenue", "Skiphop") and product images. Every one is an Attentive click-tracker, `https://carters.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/<token>`.
- A uniform 403 on every redirector URL most likely means the tracker blocks automated probes. It does not show the links are broken for recipients. **Unverified:** the final destinations were never resolved, so link health is unknown.
- 47 links were skipped by the prober, so footer, social and unsubscribe coverage is unconfirmed. The QA note says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but none appears in the list of failures shown.
- **Link text is unusually long.** Several links use concatenated marketing copy as the text (e.g. "Carter's | The 5-star Sleep & Play™ | With a 2-way zip for easy changes | Shop f…"). That text is verbose for screen readers; see section 3.
- **Tracking pixels:**
  - Attentive open pixel: `carters.attentivemail.com/ss/o/.../ho.gif`.
  - Movable Ink: `mi.carters.com/p/up/745a17bbb73445ad/o.gif` and `mi.carters.com/p/rp/264477f43986d8bb.png`.
  - These are standard, but the Movable Ink URLs carry `mi_u=1150794179` (a subscriber identifier) and `mi_ecmp=10_19_2026_CAR_MON_NOON_RETARGETING` in the query string. Both are visible to anyone the email is forwarded to.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed):** the four CloudFront banners (`.../MzczMjo6U0VSVklDRVNfQkFOTkVSXzAx…`, `…_02`, `…_03`, `…_04_v3`) have no alt. The decoded filenames are `SERVICES_BANNER_01.png` to `_04_v3.png`. These are likely content images, so recipients with images blocked or using screen readers get nothing.
- **QA false positives:** `ho.gif` is flagged for missing alt, but the source shows `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"`. That is correct for a decorative pixel. The two `mi.carters.com` tracking images look like pixels too, but their markup is truncated, so I can't confirm whether they carry `alt=""`.
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in `<head>`. This is minor, but some clients and web-view renderings use it.
- **Web font:** League Spartan is loaded via `<link>` inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. Clients that strip `<link>` (Gmail) fall back to the font stack, so this needs a fallback to be declared. I can't verify that from the truncated source.
- **Preheader:** it is hidden with `display:none` and padded with `͏` and soft-hyphen (`­`) characters. This is a standard technique and not an issue.
- **Mobile:** the media query is `max-width:660px`, with `.stack .column{width:100%}` and per-row overrides. I found no issues in the visible CSS.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source or the subject. The source is truncated, so the rest of the body is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text fallback missing:** QA reports `[FAIL] Text version is 0 chars`. A multipart/alternative with no text part hurts deliverability and accessibility. It could be a relay artifact, so confirm in the raw MIME.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes AgentMail may strip these headers. **Unverified:** Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so check the raw message headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` not present** (SPF, DKIM and DMARC unknown). This is expected through the relay and not evidence of a defect.
- **Footer, physical address and visible unsubscribe link:** not verifiable. They fall beyond the truncated HTML.
- **From address:** `carters.email@marketing.carters.com` is on a branded subdomain, which is good practice. Alignment with the Attentive sending domain couldn't be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** the destination URLs are hidden behind Attentive's opaque redirect tokens, so UTMs can't be checked. **Unverified.**
- **Landing page alignment:** the promised "Up to 60% off", "$7+ PJs" and "$2+/piece multipack bodysuits" can't be compared to the landing pages, because no link resolved past the 403.
- **Campaign tag:** `mi_ecmp=10_19_2026_CAR_MON_NOON_RETARGETING` indicates retargeting. It is useful for attribution and is the only campaign identifier visible.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-probe the links** with a browser User-Agent, or follow the redirects manually, to rule out bot-blocking. Don't treat the 403s as real breakage until then.
2. **Add descriptive `alt` text** to the four `d3ptznaucccg76.cloudfront.net` service banners. Where an image is only decorative, set `alt=""`.
3. **Check the raw MIME** for a `text/plain` part, `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and `Authentication-Results`. Fix the QA relay so these are captured.
4. **Fill in `<title>`** with the subject line.
5. **Shorten link and `alt` text:** use short link text, or an `aria-label` on tile links, in place of concatenated copy.
6. **Resolve the final destination URLs** and verify UTMs and price/offer parity on the landing pages.
7. **Fix the QA check:** it should recognize `alt=""` as valid for 1x1 pixels, so it doesn't flag `ho.gif`.
