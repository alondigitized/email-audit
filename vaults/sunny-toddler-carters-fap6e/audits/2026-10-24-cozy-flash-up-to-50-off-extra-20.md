# COZY FLASH 🚨 Up to 50% off + extra 20%
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-24**
## Executive summary

- This is a solid flash-sale send with a strong top fold. A green-framed hero on a cozy sherpa texture reads "Ends Wednesday / cozy flash / up to 50% OFF*", with a highlighted "+ EXTRA 20% OFF" band and the code COZY20. The eye lands on the offer first. The mechanic is clear, and the code gives a reason to act.
- The body dilutes it. The "We think you'll love these" grid is nine unlabeled tiles of jeans, bodysuits and joggers. None has a price, name or link cue. The packable puffer and holiday sweater blocks are pleasant but pull toward different goals. The 70% off clearance block at the bottom competes with the headline offer. After the BOGO holiday run on 10/20 to 10/22 and yesterday's "Soft knit PJs from $9", this is another discount push. The new stacked-code mechanic is the freshest beat.
- Judgement: good hero, loose back half. A 6 to 7 send that would convert better if the grid showed prices and the deadline were repeated.

## What's working

- The hero puts the offer front and center: "up to 50% OFF" in large green type, with a highlighted "+ EXTRA 20% OFF" band.
- The promo code COZY20 is shown right under the offer, so there's no hunting for it.
- "Ends Wednesday" gives a clear deadline without shouting.
- The puffer block is a single hero product with a useful benefit line: "Packs down small enough to fit in a backpack (or diaper bag)."
- The sibling-matching sweater block ties to the season and the Christmas card moment, with a "Shop Holiday" button.
- The brand header, footer perks row (curbside, Rewards, in-app points) and Find a Store block are tidy and on-brand.

## What's weak

- The nine-tile product grid has no prices, names, sale badges or captions, and there's no CTA under it. It looks like filler.
- The offer is hard to parse. "Up to 50% off" plus "extra 20% off" on "select styles" with a code leaves the real discount unclear. The asterisks are heavy, and the fine print only appears at the bottom.
- The "UP TO 70% OFF* CLEARANCE" block is a second headline offer that competes with the 50% + 20% message. Its category buttons are generic.
- The hero's "Shop Now" button is thin and outlined, with low contrast on the pale background, so it is weaker than the offer itself.
- Cadence fatigue: this follows three BOGO sends in three days and a PJs send yesterday, so the "flash" urgency feels less credible.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the product grid. Add prices ("$12 after code"), short names and a "Shop Boys' Jeans" style CTA, or cut it to 3 to 6 hero items with before and after prices.
- 2. Make the offer math plain. For example: "Up to 50% off, plus 20% off extra with COZY20 through Wednesday." Show one worked example price.
- 3. Make the hero CTA a solid, filled button with a verb and noun, such as "Shop cozy flash deals".
- 4. Drop or demote the 70% clearance block, or fold it into the footer, so the email has a single offer story.
- 5. Repeat the Wednesday deadline near the middle CTAs, or add a countdown.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Cozy flash: extra 20% off, ends Wednesday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Use COZY20 for 20% off extra, thru Weds`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Up to 50% off kids' fleece, joggers & puffers, plus an extra 20% with code COZY20.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stack your savings on cozy basics before Wednesday. Code inside.`
