# $2.99+ clearance IS ON NOW 🔥
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-23**
## Executive summary

- Carter's sends a second clearance floor-drop in five days. June 18 opened with "CLEARANCE FROM $3.99" — this June 23 send knocks the floor to $2.99 and calls it a fresh event. The hero is technically sound: orange brand color, clear price anchor, visible "Shop Now" button, age-segmented browse links. As a standalone email it is functional.
- The real problem is what subscribers have already seen. The cadence — Saturday $3.99 clearance → Monday BUY 1 GET 2 FREE → Thursday $2.99 clearance — trains the audience to wait. "Why click today if the floor keeps dropping?" kills conversion faster than a broken CTA. Carter's has a compounding urgency-credibility debt from this run of sends, and this email makes no effort to pay it down.
- Bottom line: competent creative, real price anchor, undermined by a too-fast re-clearance cadence and a subject that promises urgency ("IS ON NOW") the body fails to deliver via any actual deadline.

## What's working

- **Price floor in the hero** — "FROM $2.99" is more persuasive than a percentage-off generic; Carter's does this better than most kids' brands.
- **"MORE MARKDOWNS ADDED!" product grid** — before/after prices on individual items create tangible, item-level proof of the deal.
- **Age-segmented browse (Baby / Toddler / Kid)** — lets a multi-age parent jump directly to their shelf; smart for a clearance send where inventory varies.
- **Carter's REWARDS section** — keeps loyalty framing present during a price-motivated send without disrupting the offer hierarchy.
- **Clean render** — images load, layout holds, no overlapping text or broken elements.

## What's weak

- **Cadence déjà vu** — this is structurally identical to the June 18 "CLEARANCE FROM $3.99" send, five days later; subscribers who opened that one have no signal this is meaningfully new.
- **"IS ON NOW" with no deadline** — the subject shouts urgency that the email body never backs up; not a single expiration date or countdown visible.
- **Generic CTA** — "Shop Now" throws away the specific price anchor established in the subject and hero; the button should echo "$2.99+" or "Shop Clearance."
- **No preheader copy** — the inbox preview line appears blank or pulls footer junk, wasting the most-read real estate after the subject.
- **"100s of styles added!" is unverifiable** — the product grid shows six items; the breadth claim isn't visualized anywhere.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a hard deadline** — "ends Sunday" or a 48-hour window; "IS ON NOW" without a close date is noise. This is the single highest-lift change.
- 2. **Rewrite the CTA** — "Shop Now" → "Shop $2.99 Styles" or "See All $2.99+" to keep the price anchor in the click path.
- 3. **Differentiate from June 18** — call out specifically what's new ("200 styles added since Saturday," a featured category, or a new price tier) so repeat openers feel rewarded rather than re-sold.
- 4. **Write a real preheader** — this should be the first thing authored, not the last; currently it reads as blank or footer text.
- 5. **Anchor urgency visually** — one "ends Sunday" stamp near the hero or product grid would cost nothing and recover the credibility lost by "IS ON NOW."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Clearance just dropped to $2.99 — ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New $2.99 markdowns: baby, toddler & kid`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `100+ new styles hit the floor — grab them before they're gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `$2.99 is the new floor. Baby, toddler, and kid clearance — shop by age.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Google Fonts loaded via external `<link>` tag**
```html
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=League+Spartan:wght@400;500;600;700&display=swap" ...>
```
This will fail silently in Gmail, Outlook, and most corporate mail clients (external CSS is stripped). The declared fallback stack `Arial, Helvetica Neue, Helvetica, sans-serif` is present and adequate — font degradation is expected and handled.

**Empty `<title>` element**
```html
<title></title>
```
Some email clients (notably Samsung Mail, older Android clients) display the `<title>` value in the thread list. An empty title may display as "Untitled" or use the subject line inconsistently. Low severity but easily fixed.

**Bare `alt` attribute on mi.carters.com open pixel**
```html
<img src="https://mi.carters.com/..." alt>
```
`alt` without `=""` is valid HTML5 but `alt=""` is the conventional form for tracking pixels and is more reliably handled across all parsers. The Attentive pixel correctly uses `alt=""`.

**No issues found** with MSO/VML conditional blocks, responsive breakpoints, or preheader padding character usage (both U+034F and U+00AD soft-hyphen filler characters are intentional and standard).
