# LAST CHANCE - $4 Graphic tees & bodysuits 🚨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a straight resend of Day 12's "$4 graphic tees & bodysuits" creative, now with "LAST CHANCE 🚨" on the subject. The hero still works. A big "$4" sits over a pile of holiday tops (Santa on a motorcycle, "Dad's Mini"), with a "DAY 12 / $12 & UNDER" banner and a "today only!" badge. The eye lands on the price first, and the Shop Now button is clear.
- The problem is cadence and credibility. A subscriber got this offer on Nov 15 ("$4 Graphic tees & bodysuits 💫") and now gets it again a day later. The "DAY 12" banner and "today only!" badge are stale, so "last chance" reads as a manufactured deadline. Add the 🚨 after the Nov 9 and Nov 11 sends, and it feels like the same pressure tactic again.
- The body is a crowded stack: a "Up to 60% off" banner above the hero, then 70% clearance, a Shop It All row, and a PJ block. Overall it's solid but undifferentiated from yesterday's send.

## What's working

- The "$4" is the biggest element in the hero, so the offer is understood in about a second.
- The product pile is colorful, seasonal and shows a range of tops, which makes $4 feel like real value.
- "We price match" with a 14-day guarantee is a good trust line sitting right under the hero.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.
- The Shop Now button sits on the product photo, directly where the eye ends up.

## What's weak

- This is the same creative as the Nov 15 finale, and "DAY 12" and "today only!" are still showing. That undercuts the "LAST CHANCE" claim.
- A small "Up to 60% off" banner sits above the Day 12 hero and competes with it.
- The 70% clearance block is a second big red hero, so the email has two competing headline offers.
- Every primary button says "Shop Now" or "Shop PJs". None names the product.
- The asterisked offers (60% off, 70% off, price match) push the exclusions into small print at the bottom.
- The store-locator "Get Directions" module is generic, not personalized, and sits low in the email.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Refresh the creative for the resend.** Replace "DAY 12" and "today only!" with "Ends tonight" or "Last day" so the subject's urgency matches what the email shows. If the deal really ended Nov 15, don't claim "last chance" at all.
- 2. **Cut the top "Up to 60% off" banner and shrink the clearance block.** Let the $4 tees stand alone above the fold.
- 3. **Name the product on the button.** For example, "Shop $4 tees" or "Get $4 bodysuits".
- 4. **Add a specific sizing or stock cue.** For example, "Sizes NB–5T" or "Selling fast in 3T".
- 5. **Skip or segment this send** for people who clicked or opened the Nov 15 version. Send it only to non-openers.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last day: $4 graphic tees (sizes going fast)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Santa tees for $4 — ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 70% off clearance and a 14-day price match.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Holiday tees & bodysuits from NB to 5T, all $12 & under.`
