# HOLIDAY STYLES - Up to 60% off 🎁
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a Day 9 send that tries to do three jobs at once: a "Holiday styles up to 60% off" hero, the Day 9 "$12 shoes" deal, and a "70% off clearance" block. The sender name ("12 Days of Deals") and the subject ("HOLIDAY STYLES - Up to 60% off 🎁") point in different directions. Anyone who followed the daily $12 / $8 / $12 sequence from Nov 9–11 will see the daily deal sitting second, under a generic markdown banner. That is a step back from the cleaner single-deal emails earlier this week.
- The mid-email "coming 11.13 / 11.14 / 11.15" teaser ($10 fleece pjs, $7 sleep & plays, $4 graphic tees) is the best idea here. It builds a reason to come back for the next three days. The Grinch section at the bottom is a charming, seasonal product shot, but it sits too far down to help.
- The email is busy and has too many CTAs: Shop Now, five audience tiles, Shop Today's Deal, Shop from $10 and Get Directions. It is competent, but the hierarchy is muddy and the "up to 60%" and "up to 70%" claims compete with each other. Judgement: average and a little incoherent, after a week of near-identical offers.

## What's working

- The "coming 11.13 / 11.14 / 11.15" teaser row gives a concrete reason to return daily, with specific prices ($10, $7, $4).
- The Day 9 yellow panel is bold and readable. "$12 shoes" and the "today only!" badge are clear, and the shoe photography is crisp.
- The "We price match" line under the hero adds some trust and lowers purchase risk.
- The Grinch crew photo is seasonal and gift-oriented, and "Shop from $10" gives it an entry price.
- The Baby Girl / Toddler Girl / Girl / Baby Boy / Toddler Boy / Boy tiles let a parent jump straight to the right size.

## What's weak

- The hero offer ("Up to 60% off, Holiday styles") is the least specific thing in the email. It is dark, low-energy and moody, and it buries the real Day 9 deal below it.
- There are two competing discount claims, "up to 60%" and "up to 70% clearance". The asterisks and the long exclusions block make the offer feel less honest.
- The email has too many CTAs and too many sections for one send, and the thin outlined "Shop Now" buttons are weak. The hero button is a ghost button on a dark background with low contrast.
- The sender name "Carter's 12 Days of Deals" doesn't match a subject that leads with a generic holiday-styles sale. This is the fifth or so broad "% off" send this week, which makes it feel like more of the same.
- The footer is long, with dense legal copy. It is visible, but this is normal for this brand.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the Day 9 "$12 shoes" panel first and make it the single hero. Move the "up to 60%" and clearance blocks lower, or cut one of them.
- 2. Use one discount message per email. If clearance must stay, make it a slim banner, not a full red block.
- 3. Make the CTA buttons solid, high-contrast fills with specific copy, for example "Shop $12 shoes" instead of "Shop Now".
- 4. Keep the "coming 11.13–11.15" teaser and move it up. It is the best cadence tool in the email.
- 5. Align the subject and the sender name so both promise the daily deal.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Day 9: $12 shoes, today only 👟`
- **Subject Alt B:** `$12 & under shoes + a peek at tomorrow's $10 fleece pjs`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus: price match for 14 days and what's dropping 11.13.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sizes go fast on today's deal. Fleece pjs for $10 arrive tomorrow.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's 17% QA pass rate is mostly a probing artifact. 11 of the 12 probed links returned HTTP 403 from the Attentive click-tracking domain. The plain-text part is empty, and the header-based compliance checks couldn't be completed. The HTML source is truncated, so destination URLs, the footer and the unsubscribe link couldn't be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **11 of 12 probed links returned 403.** All are redirects on `carters.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/<token>`. They cover the hero CTA ("Up to 60% Off | Holiday Styles | Shop Now"), the logo ("carter's"), the brand tiles (osh kosh bigosh, little planet, Otter Avenue, Skiphop), "Shop from $10", and the "coming 11.13 $10 two-piece fleece pjs…" block.
- **The 403 is probably bot-blocking, not a broken link.** Every failing URL is on the same ESP redirect domain with the same status, so it looks like the prober was rejected. It is not confirmed that real recipients get a 403. The redirect targets were never resolved, so the destinations are unverified.
- **"Display images to show real-time content" links appear twice** (two separate tracked URLs). This text is the fallback for dynamic or live-content images. Both links also returned 403 and have no resolved destination.
- **One probed link has link text `[image: MzczMjo6Q0FSXzExMDQyNl9TRUNPTkRBUllfNS5q]`.** That is a CDN filename standing in for alt text, which points to a missing or auto-generated alt on a linked image (see section 3).
- **39 links were skipped** by the prober's ranking, including footer and social links. Their status is unknown.
- **Two open-tracking pixels are present:**
  - `carters.attentivemail.com/ss/o/.../ho.gif`
  - `mi.carters.com/p/up/745a17bbb73445ad/o.gif?mi_u=1150794179&mi_ecmp=11_12_2026_CAR_THU_AM_ENGAGERS`
- **The Movable Ink pixel puts identifiers in the query string:** `mi_u=1150794179`, which looks like a subscriber ID, and a campaign/segment label ending in `_ENGAGERS`. This is a minor exposure when the pixel URL is forwarded or inspected.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **`<title></title>` is empty** in the `<head>` (confirmed in source).
- **The plain-text part is 0 characters.** This is a deliverability and accessibility failure. It could be a capture artifact of the AgentMail relay, but as received there is no text alternative.
- **Alt text is missing on 8 images per QA:**
  - `ho.gif`
  - `o.gif`
  - `MzczMjo6Q0FSXzExMDQyNl9TRUNPTkRBUllfNS5qcGc…`
  - `264477f43986d8bb.png`
  - the four `MzczMjo6U0VSVklDRVNfQkFOTkVS…` PNGs (`SERVICES_BANNER_01` to `04_v3`, served from `d3ptznaucccg76.cloudfront.net`)
- **The first one is a false positive.** In the source, `ho.gif` has `alt=""`, which is correct for a tracking pixel. The `o.gif` pixel is likely the same.
- **The linked content images and the four services banners are real gaps.** Without alt text, the hero secondary image and the banners are inaccessible to screen readers. They are also blank when images are blocked, which matters because the email has "Display images" fallback links.
- **Present and correct:** `lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, and the MSO conditional blocks. The preheader is hidden with `display:none`, and the 1×1 pixels are hidden the same way.
- **The Google Fonts `<link>` (League Spartan) is wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`.** Clients that block external CSS will fall back. The fallback stack wasn't visible in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) are visible in the available source. The only per-recipient data seen is `mi_u` in the Movable Ink pixel (see section 2).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` was not found (WARN).** The QA notes this may be stripped by the AgentMail relay, so it's unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was not found (WARN).** One-click unsubscribe can't be confirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The cause is the relay, not necessarily the sender.
- **The footer isn't in the truncated source.** The unsubscribe link and physical postal address couldn't be verified. The prober always includes an unsubscribe link, and none appears among the listed failures, so it either passed or wasn't reported.
- **The sender domain is consistent:** `marketing.carters.com`, with tracking on `attentivemail.com` and `mi.carters.com`.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Can't be assessed.** All campaign links are wrapped in Attentive redirects and the probes returned 403, so no final landing URL or UTM parameters were observed.
- **No UTM parameters are visible in the available source.** Attribution appears to rely on Attentive's wrapper plus the Movable Ink `mi_ecmp` value.
- **The "coming 11.13 / 11.14" block links to a tracked URL.** Whether it lands on a matching page for those dates is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-probe the tracked links** with a browser-like user agent, or follow the redirect with a real browser session. This separates prober blocking from true breakage, and it would also reveal the destinations and UTMs.
2. **Fix the empty `<title>`** by setting a descriptive title or the subject line.
3. **Add alt text** to the four services banners and the linked content images. Keep `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
4. **Include a multipart text/plain alternative.** If the 0-character result is a relay capture artifact, confirm by checking the raw MIME from the sending side.
5. **Capture the raw headers** (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from a direct inbox copy rather than the AgentMail relay. Then confirm SPF, DKIM, DMARC alignment and RFC 8058 support.
6. **Re-run with the full HTML** so the footer's unsubscribe link, postal address and the remaining 39 links can be audited.
7. **Consider moving `mi_u` and the segment label** out of a query string visible in the pixel URL, or hash them.
