# Buy 1, Get 2! Because one pair is never enough! 👖
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-12**
## Executive summary

- Carter's just sent the exact same subject line — "Buy 1, Get 2! Because one pair is never enough! 👖" — that it sent five days ago on July 7, which scored 9/10 in this audit history and was described as a "genuine creative refresh." Whatever the intent (re-send to non-openers, scheduling error, recycled template), a subscriber who opened July 7's email sees zero reason to open this one. The subject line is the biggest single problem with this send.
- Inside the email, the OshKosh B'gosh BOGO denim hero renders cleanly and the mechanic is still solid. But the body tries to carry three simultaneous offers: the BOGO hero up top, a "Baby Essentials" grid with a "Shop 10% Off" CTA in the middle, and a jarring red "UP TO 70% OFF*" clearance block at the bottom. The clearance block in particular reads like a leftover from the eighteen-day clearance drum that ran through early July and still hasn't been fully put to bed.
- **Verdict:** The creative is capable but the subject line makes this send largely invisible to anyone who was already engaged with Carter's on July 7. Fix the subject, drop the clearance block, add a deadline — and this same BOGO mechanic could still work.

## What's working

- OshKosh B'gosh hero image renders cleanly — kids + stacked denim, no broken images or layout gaps
- "BUY 1, GET 2 FREE*" is immediately legible and the value prop is clear at a glance
- "Three pants, endless outfits. The back-to-school starts here." is the strongest copy line in the email
- Carter's Rewards footer reinforces loyalty positioning for members
- No render bugs anywhere in the layout — clean top to bottom

## What's weak

- **Exact duplicate subject line from July 7** — any subscriber who read that send gets zero new signal to open
- Three competing offers (BOGO denim, 10% off baby essentials, 70% off clearance) fracture the CTA and dilute all three
- "Shop Now" button copy has no noun — doesn't reinforce the BOGO or the product at all
- No urgency visible anywhere — no end date, no countdown, no "ends Sunday" — makes the BOGO feel like a standing offer
- Fine print asterisks on "FREE\*" and "70% OFF\*" with a dense disclaimer block at the bottom undercut the headline offer credibility

## Recommendations

- 1. **Rewrite the subject line** — this is the only change that matters before anything else. The July 7 send already rang this bell; subscribers who engaged won't open the same line again.
- **Subject Alt A:** `OshKosh B'gosh: 3 pairs for the price of 1 — back-to-school done`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Back-to-school denim BOGO is live — 3 pairs, 1 price 👖`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Three pants, zero excuses — BOGO ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stock up before sizes run out — OshKosh B'gosh BOGO denim.`
- 2. **Add a deadline to the hero** — even "Ends July 14" in the hero subhead would generate urgency that's completely absent right now. BOGO without a deadline is a coupon, not a campaign.
- 3. **Cut the 70% off clearance block** — it's the third offer in one email and it's tonal whiplash after a fresh OshKosh BOGO hero. Clearance should be its own send on its own day.
- 4. **Rewrite the primary CTA** — "Shop Now" is dead copy. Swap for "Shop the BOGO" or "Get 3 Pairs" to reflect what the subscriber is actually clicking toward.
- 5. **Anchor a price in the hero** — "Three pants" is good but "Three pants from $14" or "3 for $XX" gives the subscriber a reason to act without needing to click first.
