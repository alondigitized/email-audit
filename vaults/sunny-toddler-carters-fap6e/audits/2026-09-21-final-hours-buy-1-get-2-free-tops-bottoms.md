# 💥 FINAL HOURS - Buy 1, get 2 free* tops & bottoms
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-21**
## Executive summary

- This is the same "Buy 1, Get 2 Free" creative Carter's has now run three times in a week — first on 2026-09-18 as "Happening now," then again on 2026-09-20 as "Last chance," and now a third pass on 2026-09-21 under a "FINAL HOURS" subject. The countdown timer even renders as 00:00:00:00, the same broken/expired-looking clock flagged in the 09-20 send — nothing was fixed.
- Below the hero, the email just repeats the standard stack: size grid, 5-Star Baby Event, 70% clearance, price match, store locator. There's no new creative, no new offer, no urgency signal beyond the subject line's screaming "FINAL HOURS." A subscriber who opened the 09-18 or 09-20 emails will recognize this instantly as the same send re-wrapped again.
- Judgement: functionally fine, creatively lazy, and the fake-urgency countdown is now actively working against the brand's credibility across three sends.

## What's working

- Hero offer (Buy 1, Get 2 Free tops & bottoms) is bold, high-contrast, and impossible to miss.
- Clear "Shop Now" CTA button sits directly under the offer with no scrolling needed.
- Size grid is well organized and useful for parents who shop by size.
- Price match banner and "Now open near you" localized store block add real utility.
- No render bugs — images, buttons, and text all display cleanly.

## What's weak

- Countdown timer displays 00:00:00:00 — reads as expired or broken, not urgent.
- This is the third near-identical "Buy 1, Get 2 Free" send in a week (09-18, 09-20, now 09-21) — pure repetition fatigue.
- No new visual hook or product shot — it's the same orange starburst hero as prior sends, just re-labeled "FINAL HOURS."
- Three competing offers (B1G2, 50% off baby event, 70% clearance) crammed in with no hierarchy telling the reader which one actually applies to them.
- "FINAL HOURS" in the subject isn't backed by any visible expiration date/time in the body — feels like manufactured urgency.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the countdown timer — a static 00:00:00:00 undercuts the entire "final hours" premise; if it's really ending today, show real hours remaining.
- 2. Stop re-running the identical hero art three times in eight days — swap in a new lifestyle/product shot so repeat openers don't immediately disengage.
- 3. Pick one lead offer per send instead of stacking B1G2 + 50% off + 70% off — the layered clearance blocks dilute the hero's urgency.
- 4. Add an explicit end date/time near the hero copy so "final hours" is verifiable, not just a subject-line claim.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your last shot at buy 1, get 2 free (ends 11:59pm)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sizes NB–12/14 still in stock — shop before it's gone`
