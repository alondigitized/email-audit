# EXTRA 20% off w/ code 👀👉
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- Carter's delivers a timely summer promotional email leading with a stacked 40% off + extra 20% discount. The seasonal framing — summer-ready product grids, a vacation swimwear feature — is well-matched to the May send date, and the brand is instantly recognizable. The problem is sprawl: five distinct content sections (hero sale, summer product grid, vacay swimwear, recycling program, clearance) are packed into a single email, which dilutes the core offer and gives the reader too many exits before they commit to any one CTA. The subject line truncates the most useful information ("w/ code" — what code?) and the lack of any personalization or deadline makes this feel like a high-volume blast, not a targeted send.

## What's working

- The stacked discount (40% + extra 20%) is bold and clearly communicated in the hero — it's a genuinely good offer and leads correctly
- Summer and vacation seasonal framing is precisely on-time for early May
- The clearance module with labeled category tabs (Baby Girl, Baby Boy, Toddler Girls, etc.) gives direct navigation and reduces friction for parents who know what they need
- Bright, colorful product photography is on-brand and eye-catching
- The recycling program cross-promo ("Bring in your old clothes") adds a brand-value signal without being preachy — placed appropriately below the fold

## What's weak

- **The promo code is invisible.** Subject says "w/ code" but the hero never shows the actual code — this creates friction at the exact moment intent is highest
- **Too many competing sections.** Five separate value propositions (hero sale → summer grid → vacay swimwear → recycle CTA → clearance) means none gets full attention. This reads like four emails merged into one
- **"Shop Now" is a dead CTA.** It has no category anchoring — "Shop Girls' Summer" or "Shop Baby Sale" would do the same job with higher conversion signal
- **No urgency.** The main offer has no deadline. "Ends Sept. 11" appears only on the recycling module — the sale itself floats with no end date
- **No personalization.** No first name, no category preference, no loyalty tier visible — it reads as a pure mass blast

## Recommendations

- 1. **Show the code in the hero.** Add a visible, copyable promo code block directly under the headline. "Use code SUMMER20 at checkout" eliminates the friction "w/ code" creates.
- 2. **Cut to two sections.** Hero offer + one product module. Move clearance and recycling to a footer strip or a separate email.
- 3. **Deadline the main offer.** Even "through Sunday" or "ends May 11" creates urgency without feeling manipulative.
- 4. **Upgrade CTA copy.** Swap "Shop Now" for "Shop Boys' Summer Styles" and "Shop Girls' Vacation Looks" — category-specific CTAs consistently outperform generic ones.
- 5. **Test a personalized subject.** If the list is segmented by past purchase category, use it: `"Boys' summer starts here — extra 20% inside"` vs. the current generic line.
