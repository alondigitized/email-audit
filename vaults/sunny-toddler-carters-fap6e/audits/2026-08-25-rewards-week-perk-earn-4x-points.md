# REWARDS WEEK PERK: earn 4X points 💙
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the same blue-gradient "REWARDS WEEK" hero that's now run five days straight — 8/20, 8/21 (twice), 8/22, and today — with zero visual changes: same sparkle motif, same "UP TO 50% OFF + 4X POINTS" stack, same outline "Shop Now" button. The subject line ("REWARDS WEEK PERK: earn 4X points 💙") is just a rewording of the identical offer that's already appeared four times this week under different subjects.
- Below the fold it does more work than most of this brand's sends: a real product grid across three categories (OshKosh, Little Planet, Skip Hop), a dedicated Buy 1 Get 2 Free denim callout with a nice close-up lifestyle shot, and a red clearance block with size/category shortcut buttons (Baby Girl, Toddler Girl, Girl, Baby Boy, Toddler Boy, Boy) that's genuinely useful for fast navigation. The structure is sound — the problem is entirely the top: a fifth consecutive identical hero image is fatigue, not creative.
- Verdict: solid layout, real offers, zero creative rotation. This reads like the brand ran out of new hero art and shipped copy tweaks instead.

## What's working

- Product grid is clean, well-lit, and spans multiple sub-brands (OshKosh, Little Planet, Skip Hop) without feeling cluttered.
- Buy 1 Get 2 Free denim section has a distinct, well-composed macro shot that breaks up the page visually.
- Clearance block uses clear category buttons (Baby Girl/Boy, Toddler Girl/Boy, Girl, Boy) that shortcut straight to relevant shopping — genuinely useful for a parent shopping for a specific kid.
- No render bugs — every image loads, no overlapping text, no broken layout.

## What's weak

- Hero creative is now a five-time repeat (8/20, 8/21 ×2, 8/22, today) with no visual variation — same gradient, same sparkles, same button style.
- Three separate offers (50% off/4X points, Buy 1 Get 2 Free, 70% off clearance) are stacked with no hierarchy — the eye has nowhere obvious to land first.
- "4X points" only benefits enrolled Rewards members, but nothing in the hero signals membership status or reminds non-members how to join.
- Subject promises a specific "perk" but the body leads with the same generic sale banner as every other send this week — the perk framing doesn't show up visually.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the hero art — rotate in new photography or at least a different color treatment before sending a sixth straight day of the same blue gradient; repetition is actively training subscribers to skip this email.
- 2. Pick one hero offer and demote the other two to a scroll — three competing CTAs (Rewards, BOGO, Clearance) flatten the whole page's hierarchy.
- 3. Add a small "You have X points" or "Member since..." personalization line to the hero to make the 4X points perk feel earned, not generic.
- 4. Move the Buy 1 Get 2 Free denim block up nearer the top — it's the most distinct visual asset in the email and it's buried below three rows of product tiles.
