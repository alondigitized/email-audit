# ⚡️ Happening now, Shop buy 1, get 2 free* tops, bottoms & more
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-18**
## Executive summary

- This is Carter's biggest offer stack of the run — "Buy 1, Get 2 Free" tops/bottoms up top, a 25% off Skip Hop registry push, and a 70% off clearance banner all crammed into one send. The hero delivers on the subject with a bold orange starburst and a real size-grid for shoppers to self-select, which is a genuinely useful touch not seen in prior Carter's sends this week. But the email tries to be three emails at once — PJs, Skip Hop baby gear, and clearance — and by the third offer the hierarchy collapses into a wall of category buttons with no product hero to anchor it.
- The bedtime-PJ section is the best visual moment: an actual lifestyle shot of monster-print pajamas and slippers that feels current and seasonal, unlike some of the brand's stock-catalog imagery from earlier in the week (2026-09-17 "2-way zip Sleep & Play PJs"). But three stacked full-width offers plus a price-match banner plus a store-locator module before the footer is a lot to scroll through for one send.
- Overall this is a strong top and a muddled middle-to-bottom. Solid, not great — the "Buy 1, Get 2 Free" promise is clear and the size grid is a nice add, but offer-stacking fatigue drags it down to the same 7-range the brand has been living in all week.

## What's working

- The hero clearly renders "BUY 1, GET 2 FREE" with a visible orange Shop Now button, matching the subject line exactly.
- A full interactive-looking size grid (NB through 12/14) sits right under the hero, letting a parent jump straight to their kid's size.
- The bedtime-PJs section uses a genuine, well-art-directed lifestyle photo (monster PJs, matching slippers) that feels seasonal and specific.
- The Skip Hop section has a real callout badge ("up to 25% off") over an actual product-in-use photo, not a generic banner.
- No render bugs — images, text, and buttons all display cleanly with no overlap or broken assets.

## What's weak

- Three separate offers (BOGO, Skip Hop 25%, clearance 70%) compete for attention with no single clear priority — the eye doesn't know where to land after the hero.
- The clearance section is just six plain text-in-box category links (Baby Girl, Toddler Girl, Girl...) with no product image, a flat close to what started as a vibrant email.
- "Buy 1, Get 2 Free" is immediately undercut by the asterisk and dense fine print at the bottom explaining it's really a tiered discount — bait-and-switch risk if a shopper takes the subject literally.
- The store-locator ("Now open near you!") and rewards icon row feel bolted on at the end, adding length without adding conversion value.
- Subject uses "Happening now" urgency language, but nothing in the body reinforces a deadline or end-time for the BOGO offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to one offer per send — lead hard with BOGO 2-free, and move Skip Hop 25% and clearance 70% to separate emails this week; stacking dilutes all three.
- 2. If keeping the multi-offer structure, add a product image to the clearance section instead of plain text buttons — right now it's the flattest part of the email.
- 3. Add a real deadline ("Ends tonight at midnight ET") near the hero CTA to justify the "Happening now" urgency in the subject.
- 4. Clarify the BOGO mechanic in the hero itself (e.g., "3rd & 4th item free" or similar) rather than burying the real terms in the fine print — reduces bait-and-switch feel.
- 5. Trim the footer stack (price match, store locator, rewards icons) into a single condensed row to shorten scroll length.

## Full review
## 1. Overview

This is Carter's biggest offer stack of the run — "Buy 1, Get 2 Free" tops/bottoms up top, a 25% off Skip Hop registry push, and a 70% off clearance banner all crammed into one send. The hero delivers on the subject with a bold orange starburst and a real size-grid for shoppers to self-select, which is a genuinely useful touch not seen in prior Carter's sends this week. But the email tries to be three emails at once — PJs, Skip Hop baby gear, and clearance — and by the third offer the hierarchy collapses into a wall of category buttons with no product hero to anchor it.

The bedtime-PJ section is the best visual moment: an actual lifestyle shot of monster-print pajamas and slippers that feels current and seasonal, unlike some of the brand's stock-catalog imagery from earlier in the week (2026-09-17 "2-way zip Sleep & Play PJs"). But three stacked full-width offers plus a price-match banner plus a store-locator module before the footer is a lot to scroll through for one send.

Overall this is a strong top and a muddled middle-to-bottom. Solid, not great — the "Buy 1, Get 2 Free" promise is clear and the size grid is a nice add, but offer-stacking fatigue drags it down to the same 7-range the brand has been living in all week.

All 12 probed material links resolve through `carters.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...` and returned 403 on probe, including the primary CTA ("Buy 1, Get 2 Free | Tops, Bottoms, & More | Shop Now"), the logo link ("Carter's"), all category links ("osh kosh bigosh", "little planet", "Otter Avenue", "Skiphop"), "Shop Cozy PJs", "GEAR UP FOR BABY & SAVE", "Shop Now", and three tracking-pixel wrapper links labeled "Display images to show real-time content". A 403 on a redirect-service domain typically indicates an expired/invalid click-tracking token, a bot-detection block on the prober's user agent, or a link-generation error at send time — this needs verification against a real client render before treating it as fully broken, but it affects 100% of sampled links.
