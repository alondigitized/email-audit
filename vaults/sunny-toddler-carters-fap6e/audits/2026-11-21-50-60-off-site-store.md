# 50-60% off* site & store 🌟
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is carter's Black Friday evergreen: a big red panel reading "black friday 50-60% OFF* SITE & STORE" with a single Shop Now button, over two photo rows of kids. The offer is unmistakable and the hierarchy is clean. Anyone who has seen the 11/17 and 11/20 sends will recognize it, because it's the same hero minus the "+ EXTRA 30% with FRIDAY30" line.
- That missing line is the real story. The earlier sends had a stackable code and a reason to act. This one drops the code, adds no deadline and no countdown, and leans on a "We Price Match" band that reassures rather than pushes. The lower half does solid merchandising: priced product grid, a $2.99 clearance flash, and a Baby's First Christmas block. It's a long scroll for a repeat message, though, and the offer is weaker than the one the subscriber saw yesterday.
- Verdict: competent and on-season, but the fourth or fifth near-identical Black Friday send. Open rates will come from sender trust, not from anything new here.

## What's working

- The hero is huge and legible: "50-60% OFF* SITE & STORE" in white on red with a clear Shop Now button.
- The price-match band ("If a price drops within 14 days...") builds trust and reduces purchase risk.
- The product grid shows concrete prices with strikethroughs ($9.00 from $24.00, $5.50 from $18.00), so the savings are visible.
- The "$2.99 Clearance Flash" block has sub-category buttons (Baby Girl, Toddler Boy and so on), which helps shoppers self-sort.
- The Baby's First Christmas section adds seasonal, emotional relevance.
- The rewards and free-gift-bag footer band gives loyal shoppers an in-store reason to act.

## What's weak

- There's no urgency: no countdown, no "ends Monday", no "today only". Yesterday's email had the FRIDAY30 code, and this one drops it.
- The fine print under the hero ("Savings based on original ticketed price. Exclusions apply.") is tiny, and the asterisks undercut the honesty of "50-60% off".
- Five full sections plus a long legal block make the email very long, and the actual hero offer is buried in repetition.
- Most links are generic: Shop Now, Find a Store, and "Bodysuits from $4 >". There's no single hero product or clear route into a category.
- The "FROM $2.99" clearance block and the 50-60% hero compete as two headline offers.

## Recommendations

- 1. Restore the stackable code ("+ extra 30% with FRIDAY30") or add a real deadline. This is the single biggest lift, since the previous send's code gave a reason to click now.
- 2. Add a countdown timer or "Ends Monday" badge directly under the hero.
- 3. Cut the lower half to one product grid and one category band. Move the "$2.99 clearance" to the second slot and drop the "Shop It All" repeat.
- 4. Make the Shop Now buttons category-specific ("Shop baby deals", "Shop PJs from $8").
- 5. Move the exclusions closer to the offer in plain language ("Excludes Little Planet, Skip Hop") instead of tiny grey text.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Black Friday ends soon: 50-60% off + price match 🖤`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Baby & kids deals from $2.99, plus 50-60% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Price drops within 14 days? We'll match it. Shop doorbusters now.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Clearance from $2.99 and bodysuits from $4. Your size may not last.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is sound (`lang="en"`, `viewport`, MSO conditionals, `role="presentation"` tables). The QA run flags 12 Attentive-wrapped links returning HTTP 403, a 0-character plain-text part, and missing alt text on several content images. Most of the 403 and header findings are probably probe or relay artifacts rather than confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links returned 403.** Every one is a `carters.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...` click-tracking redirect. That includes the logo ("carter's"), brand tiles ("osh kosh bigosh", "little planet", "Otter Avenue", "Skiphop"), the hero ("black friday | 50-60% OFF*…") and six "Display images to show real-time content" links. A uniform 403 across one ESP's redirect domain points to bot blocking of the QA fetcher, not dead links. Treat it as **unverified, not confirmed broken**. Re-probe with a browser-like User-Agent or from a seeded inbox before escalating.
- **The first link has empty link text.** The logo link is labelled "carter's" by the QA tool, but the report shows a blank text field. Check that the logo `<img>` has alt text.
- **Six "Display images to show real-time content" links.** This text is the fallback alt/anchor for live-content (countdown or dynamic) blocks. Each link wraps its own redirect, so these blocks are click-wrapped separately. If images are blocked, the anchor text is all the recipient sees.
- **Probe coverage is partial.** Only 12 of 58 links were probed and 46 were skipped, so the footer, social and product links are unchecked.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Banner images have no alt text.** Four `d3ptznaucccg76.cloudfront.net/MzczMjo6U0VSVklDRVNfQkFOTkVSXzA1…` files (SERVICES_BANNER_01, 02, 03, 04_v3) are flagged. These look like content images, not decorative ones.

- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA note says this may be because the AgentMail relay strips them. The result is **inconclusive**. Verify against raw headers from a direct-delivery test, since Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both.

- **Plain-text part is 0 characters.** The QA run marks this as a FAIL. It is only a real defect if the original message lacks a `text/plain` part. A relay could also drop it, so confirm in the raw MIME.
- **The visible unsubscribe link and physical address could not be checked.** Neither appears in the truncated source or the probed set, though the QA note says an unsubscribe link is always probed. Confirm the footer.
