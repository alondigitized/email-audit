# ❣️ Made to last: Tops & bottoms from $6
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-13**
## Executive summary

- This send opens strong and then muddies its own message. The hero is a clean, on-brand product shot (folded pants in pink, olive, navy) paired with a direct "Their New Favorites / Shop from $6" CTA — that's exactly what the subject line promised. But by the third scroll the email has pivoted into a full second campaign: a monster-print pajama/plush set under "Meet Their Bedtime Besties," complete with its own "Shop from $9" CTA. Then a third, visually loudest block hits — a red "UP TO 70% OFF* CLEARANCE" banner with six category buttons — which is a bigger, bolder offer than anything the subject line actually advertised.
- This is the same pattern flagged in Carter's last several sends (9/12, 9/11, 9/9): a tidy hero that matches the subject, followed by unrelated bolt-on sections that dilute the promise. Here it's especially pronounced because there are three separate CTAs (Shop from $6, Shop from $9, six clearance category buttons) competing for the click, and the biggest one isn't the one the subject sold.
- Net: a well-photographed, bug-free email that undercuts itself with scope creep. Trim it to one offer and this jumps from a mid-pack send to a genuinely strong one.

- Hero image is a clean, on-brand product shot with no render bugs, overlapping text, or placeholder content.
- "Their New Favorites" headline and "Shop from $6" CTA directly echo the subject line's tops & bottoms/$6 promise.

- Loyalty/membership perks (curbside, cardholder perks, Rewards) are visible in the footer icon row.

## What's weak

- The email folds in two unrelated campaigns (bedtime/monster PJs, then 70%-off clearance) that have nothing to do with the "tops & bottoms from $6" subject.
- The 70% off clearance banner is visually the loudest element in the email — bigger, bolder, and higher-contrast than the $6 offer that got top billing in the subject and hero.
- Three separate CTAs (Shop from $6, Shop from $9, six clearance buttons) create decision paralysis instead of one clear next action.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere — "New Markdowns Added" is the closest thing to time-sensitivity and it's vague.
- No preview/preheader text was visible in this render to complement the subject.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one offer and build the whole email around it — either the $6 tops & bottoms story or the clearance sale, not both plus a pajama tangent. This is the single highest-leverage fix; it's the same scope-creep issue flagged in the last several Carter's audits.
- 2. If clearance must stay, move it above the fold and make it the hero — right now it's buried under two unrelated sections, wasting its own urgency.
- 3. Cut the "Bedtime Besties" monster PJ block entirely from this send, or spin it into its own email — it has no connection to "tops & bottoms" and breaks the narrative thread.
- 4. Reduce to a single primary CTA button style/color so the eye isn't split three ways.
- 5. Add a real deadline ("Ends Sunday," "Through 9/15") to the clearance banner to justify its size and urgency.
