# BUY 1, GET 2 FREE ✨
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Carter's pivots off yesterday's Memorial Day sitewide 50%-off blowout with a category-narrowed BOGO: "BUY 1, GET 2 FREE* TOPS & BOTTOMS." That's a smart structural shift — a BOGO reads as additive value rather than a price cut, and tops & bottoms are exactly what parents replenish heading into summer. The email also debuts a Sesame Street collab, which is fresh creative that hasn't appeared in any of the prior six sends.
- The execution is competent but sprawling. Four distinct offer modules compete below the fold: the BOGO hero, a Summer Stock Up product grid, the Sesame Street sale (50% off with a badge), and a $2.99 clearance strip in red. The hero lands cleanly, but by the time the subscriber reaches clearance the email has lost its spine. The asterisk on "GET 2 FREE*" is the trust-eroding element — coming one day after a "50% off everything" send, a subscriber immediately wonders whether the BOGO is actually better or just differently restricted.
- The subject does its job in under 20 characters. The real risk is cadence: this is email six in approximately six days from Carter's, and even a strong offer reads as noise at that frequency.

## What's working

- **Hero offer reads in one glance** — "BUY 1, GET 2 FREE* TOPS & BOTTOMS" needs no decoding; the BOGO structure is immediately understood.
- **Sesame Street collab is genuinely new creative** — Elmo and Cookie Monster haven't appeared in any prior audited send; the 50% badge on that module gives it a separate, novelty-driven click path.
- **$2.99 from-price anchor** — the clearance strip's red background and from-price signal is a strong value hook for price-sensitive parents without cannibalizing the hero.
- **Summer Stock Up grid is timely** — seasonal positioning is accurate and the product mix (bright colors, warm-weather cuts) matches the moment.
- **Carter's Rewards visible** — the loyalty badge in the footer reinforces membership value without cluttering the body.

## What's weak

- **Four offers fragment attention** — BOGO, Summer Stock Up, Sesame Street, and Clearance all ask for different clicks with no single through-line after the first scroll.
- **Asterisk on FREE*** — fine print at the bottom is dense; coming the day after a sitewide sale, a subscriber will hesitate before clicking.
- **No urgency** — unlike the 5/14 "ENDS TODAY" email with its live countdown timer, this send has zero deadline, making deferral the path of least resistance.
- **Generic CTA copy throughout** — "Shop Now" appears on every section; no button uses a specific verb+noun that matches the offer (e.g., "Shop Tops & Bottoms").
- **Cadence is at ceiling** — six emails in six days; even a well-executed send is fighting subscriber exhaustion at this frequency.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a deadline to the hero** — "BOGO ends Sunday" in the subtext or a small urgency badge would substantially lift click rate; the 5/14 email showed Carter's subscribers respond to countdowns.
- 2. **Cut to two modules** — BOGO hero + Sesame Street is a complete email. Move clearance to a footer strip, cut the generic Summer Stock Up grid entirely.
- 3. **Rewrite CTA buttons** — hero: "Shop Tops & Bottoms"; Sesame section: "Shop Sesame Styles"; clearance: "Shop from $2.99."
- 4. **Surface the exclusion in the hero subtext** — if the asterisk means select styles or online only, say "select styles, online only" in the subtext beneath the headline. Upfront transparency reduces cart abandonment more than it suppresses clicks.
- 5. **Suppress to recent openers** — at this send frequency, limiting to subscribers who opened in the last 14 days protects deliverability and maintains list health.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Buy 1, get 2 free — tops & bottoms, ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your kids will destroy these (that's why they're free)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `BOGO on tops & bottoms + 50% off Sesame Street styles through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stock up before summer starts: buy one, get two free on kids' basics.`
