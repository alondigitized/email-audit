## What's working

- **"Anything for Cats Deals" top banner** — I notice it immediately and it names litter specifically, which is literally on my auto-ship. That's a hook for me.
- **"Pounce on savings" headline** — It's a cat pun but it doesn't talk down to me. It's just a little fun. I can handle that.
- **Subtext specificity** — "food, litter, furniture & more" is a real list of what I actually buy. Not vague, not generic.
- **"Shop meow" CTA** — Visible, tappable, clear where it leads. No ambiguity.
- **Deal deadline (5/12)** — I appreciate a real date. It makes the promo feel credible, not just "SALE!!!" forever.

## What's weak

- **No Treats membership hook** — I'm a Treats member and I don't see my rewards, a member price badge, or a "sign in to see your savings" prompt anywhere. That's a missed personalization opportunity.
- **No specific discount amount** — "Save on" is vague. Save 10%? 30%? I read labels; I read numbers. Give me something concrete.
- **No product imagery above fold** — The entire hero is red background + text. There's a partial cat photo below the fold but nothing I can actually react to yet.
- **No grooming services mention** — I bring my long-hair in monthly for grooming. That's revenue for them and a loyalty driver for me, and it's completely invisible here.
- **Asterisk on the offer** — "thru 5/12*" — that star worries me a little. What's excluded? I've been burned before.

## Recommendations

- **Show a member-personalized line above the fold** — Even just "Treats members save an extra 10%" next to the banner would make me feel seen as a returning customer instead of a stranger.
- **Add a specific discount figure to the hero subtext** — Swap "Find big deals" for "Save up to 35%" or whatever the actual number is. Concrete beats clever every time.
- **Drop in a lifestyle product shot** — A single image of a cat on a piece of furniture or next to a litter box in the hero would give the visual the warmth it's missing right now.
- **Add a "Book Grooming" chip or nav shortcut** — One tap in the nav to salon services. I should not have to hunt for it every month.
