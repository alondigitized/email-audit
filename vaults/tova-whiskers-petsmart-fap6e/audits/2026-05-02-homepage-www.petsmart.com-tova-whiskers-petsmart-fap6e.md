# Homepage snapshot · www.petsmart.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- This looks like a brand that's decided the only thing I care about is saving money — the entire above-the-fold is just a promo code blasted twice. It's pushing a sitewide 20% off deal ending May 3rd, which okay, fine, but I opened PetSmart because I wanted to check grooming appointments or browse cat food, and this feels like I walked into a store and someone immediately shoved a coupon in my face before I even got through the door. Generic deal-of-the-week energy.
- **4/10**

## What's working

- **The promo banner at the top** — I did notice "EXTRA 20% OFF" immediately, and if I was already planning to restock litter or treats this week, that code might get used. The expiry date (5/3) creates a soft nudge.
- **Search bar is prominent** — it's right there after the nav. I know what I came for; I'll just use search. At least they didn't bury it.
- **"Shop now" CTA is clear** — no ambiguity, no cutesy verb. Works fine for me.

## What's weak

- **Zero cat content above the fold** — I have two cats. This page gives me nothing cat-specific. The hero could be for fish food or dog collars; I genuinely can't tell.
- **"Splash into extra savings" headline is meaningless** — "Splash"? Is that a water theme? A summer theme? It reads like copy someone approved without asking what it means.
- **The promo code appears twice in the first screen** — banner AND hero body text. That's redundant and makes the page feel cheap, like a flyer not a brand.
- **No category shortcuts visible** — I'm a repeat customer. I want a tap to "Cat Food," "Grooming," or "Litter." None of that is here; I'm expected to search or scroll.
- **"Enable accessibility" link placed weirdly** — it's sitting right under the promo banner, styled so plainly it looks like a broken element. That's a UX rough edge.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the generic lifestyle headline with a category-split hero** — show cats on one side, dogs on the other, let me tap into my species. PetSmart knows my account history; use it.
- **Add a quick-tap icon row below the search bar** — Cat Food / Litter / Grooming / Treats. Four icons. I'd tap Grooming in two seconds and never have to read that promo copy.
- **Move the promo code to the banner only** — repeating it in the hero body makes the page look desperate. Trust that I read the top.
- **Change "Splash into extra savings" to something specific** — if this is a spring sale, say "Spring sale." If it's a May savings event, say that. Wordplay that means nothing to me is noise.
