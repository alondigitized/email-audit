# Homepage snapshot · www.petsmart.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Full review
The first thing I see is a promo banner stretched across the top — "EXTRA 20% OFF thousands of items online with code: SAVE20 thru 5/3" — and then the whole hero repeats it again in big teal letters: "Splash into extra savings." That's it. That's the whole first screen. A discount. No cats, no dogs, no products, no categories — just "give us money and save money." I get it, it's a sale, but I came here to actually shop for something specific and I'm immediately being asked to do math about a coupon code I'll probably forget by the time I get to checkout.

The "Shop now" button is there but it's completely unanchored. Shop for what? I have two long-haired cats, I care about food ingredients, I book grooming appointments — none of that is signaled anywhere. The color palette is summery and cheerful but that botanical/floral design peeking in at the bottom feels like they're trying to sell me garden furniture. The "Splash" language makes me think fish or outdoor, which is exactly not my life.

The navigation is fine — hamburger menu, search bar, cart — medium-tech-comfortable me can work with that. But I had to scroll past a wall of "SAVINGS SAVINGS SAVINGS" to even get to a search box. And there's an "Enable accessibility" prompt that's just sitting there above the logo, which reads a little unfinished on mobile.

I would have liked to see something cat-specific right out of the gate — grooming, food, litter — anything that says they know I'm a cat person and not just a wallet. As a PetSmart regular who comes in specifically for grooming, this homepage gives me nothing to work with. I'd open the search bar and move on.

**4/10**
