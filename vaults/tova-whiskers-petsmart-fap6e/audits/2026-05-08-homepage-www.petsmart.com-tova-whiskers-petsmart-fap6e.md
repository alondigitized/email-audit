# Homepage snapshot · www.petsmart.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay, PetSmart clearly knows I exist — the second I land, the entire page is yelling "CATS" at me, which, fine, you have my attention. The red hero is bold and the banner up top calls out litter specifically, which as someone who buys litter on auto-ship, I noticed. But this is all very deal-push, very "spend now," and not a lot of substance behind it — no product imagery, no loyalty signal telling me what I've already got going, nothing that makes me feel like a regular.

## What's working

- **Top banner "Anything for Cats Deals: Save on toys, litter & more thru 5/12*"** — litter is my highest-frequency replenishment item; seeing it called out explicitly stops my scroll reflex.
- **"Pounce on savings" hero** — it's a pun but it works; I know immediately this is a cat-focused sale page, not a dog-first brand moment.
- **Time deadline on the deal ("thru 5/12*")** — gives me a reason to act today rather than close the tab and forget.
- **Clean layout, no forced modal** — I opened the page and nothing jumped out demanding my email before I could read anything. Appreciated.
- **"Shop meow" button placement** — high on the screen, contrast color (blue on red), easy tap target on a phone.

## What's weak

- **Zero Treats member acknowledgment** — I am a Treats member. I use the app. There is no indication the brand knows that. No balance, no "member pricing," nothing. That's a miss.
- **No product imagery in the hero** — it's all typography on a red field. A photo of an actual cat (ideally a longhair, since grooming is part of my routine) would connect faster than wordplay.
- **"Shop meow" is vague** — cute, but it doesn't tell me if I'm landing on a curated litter deal or a general sale dump. As someone who reads labels, I want precision.
- **No free-shipping signal** — I don't know what threshold triggers free shipping and that matters to me when I'm stacking a litter order.
- **The asterisk on the offer** — "thru 5/12*" has a footnote I can't read without scrolling or zooming. That fine print instinct kicks in.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the flat red hero for a lifestyle shot of a longhair cat** — even a partial image. The glimpse of fur at the very bottom of the viewport is doing more emotional work than the entire red field above it.
- **Add a Treats member greeting to the top banner** — something like "Treats members: extra 5% off cat litter thru 5/12" turns a generic promo into a returning-customer retention play.
- **Replace "Shop meow" with "Shop Cat Deals"** — or at minimum "Shop the Cat Sale." The pun is fine once; on a CTA button it costs clarity.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold** — one line under the CTA ("Free shipping on orders $49+") would reduce my hesitation before tapping through.
