# Homepage snapshot · www.petsmart.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Opened PetSmart and the first thing I'm looking at is a woman with a parrot on her shoulder — pretty sure I own cats, not a macaw. The blue promo banner at the top is doing the heavy lifting here, and it's doing it well: 20% off with a clear code and a deadline. But the hero itself feels like it's casting a wide "all pets welcome" net rather than speaking to me and my two Persians specifically.

## What's working

- **The promo banner** — blue strip at the very top, "EXTRA 20% OFF… code: SAVE20 thru 5/15" is impossible to overlook and gives me something actionable immediately
- **The hero headline** — "Let the spoiling begin / EXTRA 20% OFF online" echoes the banner in a warmer tone; "spoiling" resonates with how I actually think about my cats
- **Clean page load** — no jank, no cumulative layout shift, no placeholder images; for medium tech comfort like mine, that matters more than people think
- **Urgency without panic** — the 5/15 deadline is visible but not screaming at me with a countdown clock, which I find more trustworthy

## What's weak

- **The bird hero image** — I own cats. A lovebird with a smiling stranger signals this page isn't built for me; I start scanning for whether this is even the right store
- **Zero Treats member recognition** — I have an account, I'm in the app regularly, and the homepage treats me like a first-time visitor; no rewards balance, no "Welcome back," nothing
- **No CTA button visible** — the hero text confirms the offer but I have to guess where to tap; the bottom of the hero is cut off and I can't tell if a button is just below the fold or missing entirely
- **No cat-category entry point** — no "Shop Cats," no "Cat Food," no fast-lane into my actual department; the hamburger menu makes me work for it
- **No free-shipping threshold** — I auto-ship litter, I know roughly what I spend, but I can't see if this order qualifies without digging

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero image for a cat lifestyle shot** — a cat lounging with a treat bag or a long-hair getting a brush would signal immediately that this page is for me; PetSmart can A/B test by species for returning logged-in users
- **Surface my Treats balance in the header** — even a small "2,450 pts" next to the account icon would make me feel seen and give me a reason to spend enough to reach the next reward tier
- **Add a visible CTA button in the hero** — "Shop the Sale" or "Use SAVE20" right under the offer copy; one tap, not a hunt through the hamburger menu
- **Pin a free-shipping threshold** in the promo banner or directly under it — "Free shipping on orders $49+" alongside the 20%-off code would remove the one remaining friction point before I start filling my cart
