# Homepage snapshot · www.petsmart.com · 2026-05-07
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- PetSmart opened with a full cat deal push — "Anything for Cats Deals" banner right at the top, red hero with litter, food, and furniture called out by name, and a playful "Pounce on savings" headline. As someone who auto-ships litter and is always looking at what's on promotion for my two indoor cats, this felt immediately relevant. It's not a sophisticated experience, but it's pointed squarely at people like me, and that counts for a lot.

## What's working

- **"Anything for Cats Deals" top banner** — this is the first thing I see, and it names litter specifically. That's my auto-ship item and I still notice when it's on sale.
- **"Pounce on savings" + sub-copy** — calling out food, litter, furniture, and more in the hero body copy is unusually specific for a homepage. I know exactly what's being promoted.
- **"Shop meow" CTA button** — it's a blue button against a red field, impossible to miss. The punny name doesn't bother me; Gen X can handle a cat pun.
- **Deadline in copy** — "thru 5/12" gives me a reason to not close the tab and forget about it.
- **Clean mobile render** — search bar, nav, hero, CTA all in the right places. No chaos.

## What's weak

- **No specific savings amount** — "Save on toys, litter & more" is fine but I've been burned before by "sale" pages where everything is 5% off. A "save up to 30%" or even a "$5 off litter" line would do a lot more work.
- **No loyalty hook** — I'm a Treats member. There's zero acknowledgment of that above the fold. A "Treats members save extra" badge would hit me right where I live.
- **Navigation is hamburger-only** — the collapsed menu means I can't see whether "Cat" is one tap away. For a returning customer in a focused category, that's friction.
- **No product imagery above the fold** — the hero is type-heavy on red. The partially visible cat at the bottom suggests lifestyle content, but I can't see it yet. A bag of litter or a familiar brand shot would make this feel more like a shopping page.
- **No free-shipping info** — I buy online constantly. Where's the threshold? It should be in the banner or near the CTA.

## Recommendations

- **Add the savings number to the banner**: Change "Save on toys, litter & more" to "Save up to 35% on cat faves" — one number makes it a real offer.
- **Put a Treats member callout near the CTA**: A single line — "Treats members: earn points on every purchase" — right under the "Shop meow" button would catch my eye and reinforce loyalty membership value.
- **Show a product or brand in the hero**: Drop in a recognizable litter brand logo or a product thumbnail next to the headline. It makes the deal feel tangible instead of abstract.
- **Surface free-shipping threshold in the promo banner**: Tack "Free shipping over $49" onto the top strip — it takes up no extra space and closes a mental loop I always have before I start adding to cart.
