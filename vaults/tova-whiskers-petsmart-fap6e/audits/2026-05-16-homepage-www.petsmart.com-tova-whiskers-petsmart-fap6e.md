# Homepage snapshot · www.petsmart.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so PetSmart opens straight into an autoship pitch — which, honestly, is not the worst call for me since I already auto-ship litter. It reads like a brand that knows its recurring-purchase customers are its lifeblood right now and is putting all its chips on that. Nothing about cats specifically above the fold, no grooming hook, no ingredient story — just one message, one button, full stop.

## What's working

- **The autoship lead**: For someone who auto-ships litter every month, this hero feels like PetSmart read my receipt. It's not wasted real estate.
- **"Subscribe & save" button**: Clear, benefit-forward copy — I know exactly what happens if I tap it.
- **Top banner reinforcement**: The blue alert bar repeats the same offer, which means the message hits twice before I even get to the hero. That kind of repetition actually works on me.
- **Clean search bar**: It's prominent and right below the nav. If I want to search for a specific food brand or litter, one tap gets me there.

## What's weak

- **Zero cat-specific signal above the fold**: The word "cat" does not appear anywhere I can see. I have two of them. A photo of a cat, a mention of cat food, grooming for long-hairs — anything would tighten the relevance.
- **No Treats membership mention**: I'm a Treats member. If there's a benefit for existing members who add autoship, I can't see it. That's a missed loyalty-reinforcement moment.
- **"1st order" framing assumes I'm new**: I'm not. The free same-day delivery hook is for first-time autoship orders. As an existing autoship customer, the headline "just got better" hooks me — but then the offer is for someone else. That's a slight bait-and-switch in the copy.
- **No grooming or in-store entry point**: Monthly grooming is a big part of my PetSmart relationship and there's no surface for it above the fold.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "current members get X" line** beneath the hero subhead — even one sentence acknowledging that existing autoship customers get the upgrade too would close the gap between the headline promise and the fine print.
- **Swap in a cat hero image**: The bottom of the page shows a cat starting to peek in, but the main hero is pure text on a gray background. Put a long-hair cat or a can of cat food in the shot — it signals "this place is for you" before I read a word.
- **Surface the Treats balance or a Treats CTA** in the nav or directly below the hero. A line like "Treats members earn 8× on autoship" would make me tap immediately.
- **Add a secondary CTA for in-store services** (Grooming, Salon, Vet) alongside the autoship button — one sentence and a ghost button would do it without crowding the layout.
