# Homepage snapshot · www.petsmart.com · 2026-06-01
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-01**
## Executive summary

- PetSmart is leading with its 40th anniversary Treats loyalty push, and honestly that top banner and the big red hero are hard to miss. The brand reads like a reliable pet-supply chain that's genuinely excited about its birthday — but if you came here to browse cat food brands or book a grooming appointment, you'll need to go find that yourself. It's not unwelcoming, it's just... not talking to me specifically.

## What's working

- **Treats loyalty banner + hero combo:** The blue top bar and the red hero both push the same promotion — as a Treats member I know immediately there's something to claim before June 14.
- **Single clear CTA:** "Activate now" is a white button on red, impossible to miss. No decision fatigue, no competing buttons.
- **Visual hierarchy:** The red background, oversized white headline, and search bar make this feel organized. On a phone screen that matters.
- **Timely, specific offer:** 4,000 points per day (not a vague discount) with a hard deadline is the kind of concrete benefit I actually respond to.

## What's weak

- **Dog-first imagery:** I have two cats. The partially visible fluffy dog at the bottom is the only animal shown above the fold. Nothing here signals that PetSmart sees me as a cat owner.
- **No category access above fold:** I can't see any shortcut to Cat, Grooming, or Litter without tapping the hamburger menu or using search. One tap should get me to my aisle.
- **Anniversary framing is brand-centric, not me-centric:** "4,000 pts for 40 years" is about PetSmart's milestone. I'm here for my cats, not to celebrate the company's birthday.
- **No trust signals:** No review counts, no "free pickup" mention, no returns badge. I shop here regularly but a first-timer would have nothing to hold onto.

## Recommendations

- **Add a cat-specific thumbnail or CTA strip just below the hero** — even a horizontal icon row ("Dogs | Cats | Fish | Birds") would let me tap directly to my category without opening the menu.
- **Swap the banner copy to be member-benefit framing:** "Your Treats points are multiplying — earn 4,000/day thru 6/14" feels more like it's talking to *me*, not recruiting me.
- **Surface one cat product or grooming callout below the fold** — a "Top-rated cat litter" or "Book grooming" module would catch my eye immediately and signal the store carries what I actually need.
- **Expose the asterisk terms inline or drop the asterisk:** "No exclusions on cat food and litter" would close the loop for me and remove the hesitation.
