# Homepage snapshot · www.petsmart.com · 2026-07-13
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I'll give PetSmart credit — they led with something that actually matters to me. The whole above-the-fold experience is screaming "Member Days" at me, which as a Treats member, I actually appreciate. It's not the most polished design I've ever seen — very bold, very red, very loud — but the message is unambiguous: this week is for people like me who have been shopping here long enough to have points. The vibe is "loyalty event," which honestly made me stop scrolling Instagram to pay attention.

## What's working

- **The "Activate & earn 5,000 pts PER DAY" top banner** — I see it before I even see the logo. Hard to miss, and the mechanic is spelled out in one line.
- **"Member Days" badge with the Treats heart logo** — that heart icon is the Treats program mark; as someone enrolled, it registers instantly. I know this event is for me.
- **"Get $10 back in savings" headline** — no ambiguity. It's dollars, not percentages, not "up to" language. Refreshingly direct.
- **Red-to-blue gradient hero** — it's bold and high energy. For a promotional event, this looks like something is actually happening, not just a static brand refresh.
- **$49+ threshold disclosed in the top banner** — I know right away whether my typical cart qualifies. Mine usually does.

## What's weak

- **No CTA button visible in the viewport** — the hero cuts off before any action button appears. On a mobile screen, "Get $10 back in savings" with nowhere to tap is a dead end. I'm reading a poster, not shopping.
- **Zero cat content above the fold** — I have two cats. Nothing here says "cat food," "grooming," "litter." It could be a dog site. Entirely generic loyalty messaging.
- **No navigation shortcuts to my categories** — just a hamburger and a search bar. One extra tap to find cat food or grooming services feels like friction when I opened the app with intent.
- **The asterisk on "7/19*"** — I notice these. I'm 47, not a first-time shopper. I know fine print lives somewhere below and I'm mildly suspicious until I read it.
- **"Enable accessibility"** as a standalone text link at the top is visually jarring and breaks the otherwise polished feel.

## Recommendations

- **Add a tappable CTA button in the hero viewport** — "Shop & Earn" or "Activate My Points" should be visible without scrolling. The current hero is all announcement, no action.
- **Show one cat-specific hook alongside the loyalty messaging** — a small "Shop Cat Treats & Earn Double" tile beneath the hero wouldn't require a redesign but would pull me from "awareness" to "intent" in one scroll.
- **Surface my Treats balance in the logged-in header** — if I'm in the app and enrolled, show me "You have 3,240 pts" next to the account icon. That's the fastest possible loyalty hook.
- **Swap the hamburger-only nav for a category shortcut strip** — two or three tappable pills below the search bar ("Cat," "Dog," "Grooming") would cut the path to my category from two taps to one.
