# Homepage snapshot · www.petsmart.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is interesting — they're leading with autoship, which is literally how I shop here. I auto-ship litter, so this headline isn't some random push; it's talking directly to what I actually do on this app. That said, what I'm seeing is essentially a one-message homepage: autoship got better. Nothing about my Treats membership, nothing about grooming, nothing about treats or food — just that one thing. It feels promotional more than a full storefront.

## What's working

- **"Autoship just got better" headline** — This is genuinely well-targeted. I already auto-ship litter, so this isn't noise; it's news I'd actually care about.
- **Single clear CTA: "Subscribe & save"** — No ambiguity. I know exactly what the button does.
- **Blue announcement banner at the top** — Restates the autoship + free same-day delivery offer, so I can't miss it even if the hero doesn't load properly.
- **Clean, simple layout** — Search bar is immediately visible. Not cluttered. Medium-tech-comfort me doesn't feel overwhelmed.
- **Cat visible at the bottom of the frame** — Even partially, it signals this is a cat-owner page, not a dog-food store.

## What's weak

- **No Treats member recognition** — I'm a paying loyalty member and there's zero acknowledgment of that above the fold. No "Hi Tova, your points balance is X" or even a generic "Treats member? Your order ships FREE." That's a miss.
- **"1st order" qualifier on the free delivery** — I've been a customer for years. This offer is for new autoship subscribers, which I may not be. That creates a moment of confusion: is this for me or not?
- **No grooming or treats/food content visible** — I buy cat food, cat treats, and pay for monthly grooming. None of that is surfaced. The entire viewport is one message.
- **No ingredient or quality signals** — I read labels. I care about what's in the food. Nothing about nutrition, quality brands, or vet-recommended anything. That's a big part of why I choose PetSmart over a random site.
- **No category shortcuts** — One tap to "Cat Food," "Litter," "Grooming" would help me navigate faster. The hamburger menu hides everything.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Treats welcome module just below the hero** — Even a slim strip: "Treats member: you have X pts · Free shipping on orders $49+" would immediately tell me this page knows who I am and why I'm here.
- **Surface cat-specific category shortcuts below the fold** — Three tiles: "Cat Food," "Litter & Auto-ship," "Grooming & Spa." One tap from homepage to where I'm actually going.
- **Qualify the free-delivery offer** — Swap "1st order" language for something that includes existing members: "New to autoship? Get FREE Same-Day Delivery. Already subscribed? [Manage your plan]." Don't make your best customers feel excluded.
- **Add a brief trust/quality signal in the hero subtext** — Something like "Vet-trusted brands, ingredient transparency" under the autoship copy. Costs nothing, speaks to ingredient-label readers like me.
