# Homepage snapshot · www.petsmart.com · 2026-09-28
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- This reads like a big-box retailer leaning hard into subscribe-and-save right now — the whole top third of the page is one message: sign up for Autoship, get 40% off. I don't hate it, I already do auto-ship for litter, but the page is talking to "pet owners" in general, dog included front and center, not specifically to a cat person like me. It's clean and functional, just not tailored.

## What's working

- **The Autoship hero** — "Autoship, save, REPEAT" with the 40% OFF badge is exactly the kind of deal that gets me to actually subscribe, since I already reorder litter this way.
- **Tidy Cats litter tile in the product strip** — that's literally what I auto-ship. Good that it's front-row.
- **Royal Canin kitten food** also in that strip — even though my cats aren't kittens, it tells me this row is genuinely stocked with cat products, not just dog stuff.
- **"11,764,897 lives saved"** — a nice, quiet trust signal near the top without being preachy about it.

## What's weak

- **Dog dominates the hero** — he's bigger, in sharper focus, lying front and center; my cat is up on a box in the background. If I'm a cat-only household, the visual is telling me this is a dog site with a cat as an afterthought.
- **No grooming callout up top** — I book monthly grooming for my long-hair, and there's zero mention of Pet Services or grooming above the fold, just Autoship messaging.
- **"See terms*"** on the tiered discount — 40%/20%/10% across three orders is already a mouthful, and burying the fine print behind an asterisk makes me want to double-check before I trust it.
- **No ingredient or product detail visible** — I read labels before I buy, and the product tiles (Pro Plan, Tidy Cats, etc.) show packaging only, nothing that tells me what's actually in the bag from the homepage.

## Recommendations

- Swap in a hero shot that's cat-forward for cat-owner segments — same box-and-treats scene, just make the cat the one in focus instead of the dog.
- Add a small "Book grooming" or "Find a groomer near you" tile near the top nav strip, not buried under "Pet Services."
- Simplify the offer copy to one clean line ("40% off your first order, plus 20% and 10% off the next two") instead of leaning on the asterisk.
- Add a short "Shop by pet: Cats" quick-link right under the hero, next to the product carousel, so I don't have to scroll or dig into the nav.
