# Homepage snapshot · www.petsmart.com · 2026-06-08
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so PetSmart is going all-in on their 40th anniversary loyalty push — the whole above-the-fold experience is basically a giant "activate your points" billboard. As someone who already uses the Treats program, I get why they're doing this, but the hero image shows what looks like a fluffy dog, not my long-haired cats, and there's zero acknowledgment of anything I actually came here to shop. It reads like a loyalty re-engagement campaign, not a shopping homepage.

## What's working

- **The loyalty hero is hard to miss.** "4,000 pts for 40 years!" in big white type on red — I knew the offer in two seconds, which is impressive on a small phone screen.
- **The top sticky banner** reinforces the same offer with the deadline, so I get the message whether I read the hero or just the banner.
- **"Activate now" button** is blue on red — high contrast, tappable, no guessing what it does.
- **Search bar is prominent** and right under the nav. As someone who knows what I want (grooming shampoo, specific litter brand), I can skip the browsing and go straight there.
- **Deadline is visible** ("thru 6/14") — that creates a nudge without being screamy.

## What's weak

- **Dog in the hero image.** I have cats. Two of them. That dog photo at the bottom of the screen doesn't register as "my store." Small thing, but when you're a cat household you notice.
- **No category navigation above the fold.** There's a hamburger menu but no quick-tap rails for Cats, Food, Grooming, etc. I have to actively hunt.
- **Zero product surface.** The entire above-the-fold area is about activating loyalty — no food, no grooming, no litter. I can't see anything I'd actually put in a cart.
- **No trust or convenience signals.** Free shipping threshold? Auto-ship reminder? Curbside? Nothing. As someone who auto-ships litter, a little "auto-ship active" or "free shipping on $35" nudge would be relevant.
- **The asterisk.** "4,000 PTS PER DAY on $40+ orders & services thru 6/14*" — that star tells me there are exclusions somewhere. I'd want to know before I plan a grooming appointment around this.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the dog for a cat (or at least both).** For a multi-species retailer, defaulting to dog imagery in the hero excludes roughly half your pet household base. On a homepage, show me a cat.
- **Add a quick-category strip below the hero.** Two rows of icon tiles — Cats, Dogs, Grooming, Food, Treats — so after I read the loyalty promo I have somewhere to go with one tap.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold.** A small strip like "Free shipping on $49+" or a cart-progress indicator would be a low-lift conversion unlock, especially for auto-ship customers like me.
- **If I'm already a Treats member, show my balance.** Instead of "Activate now" (which implies I haven't joined), recognize returning shoppers with a personalized hook like "You have X pts — earn 4,000 more today."
