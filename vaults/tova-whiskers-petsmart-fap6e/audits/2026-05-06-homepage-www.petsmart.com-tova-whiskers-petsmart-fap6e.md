# Homepage snapshot · www.petsmart.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I'll give PetSmart credit — they knew what they were doing when they built this screen. The whole thing is cat-forward, which, as someone with two indoor cats and a monthly grooming appointment on the books, is exactly my lane. It reads like a sale push, not a browse experience, but for a quick "is there a deal on litter right now?" check, this homepage delivers a fast answer.

## What's working

- **The top banner** — "Anything for Cats Deals: Save on toys, litter & more thru 5/12" lands in under two seconds. Litter is literally on my auto-ship, so any litter deal catches my eye immediately.
- **"Pounce on savings" headline** — corny but I smiled. The cat pun is on-brand and doesn't feel forced for a pet retailer. It's the right register for me — not too millennial-cutesy, not clinical.
- **"Shop meow" CTA** — same energy. Clear action, cat-themed, one tap. I know exactly where it goes.
- **The partial cat image below the fold** — I can see the start of an "ANYTHING FOR CATS" module with what looks like an actual cat. That pulls my eye down. I want to see what's there.
- **Date-stamped urgency** — "thru 5/12" with today being May 6 means I have less than a week. That's real pressure, not fake scarcity.

## What's weak

- **No specific discount visible** — "Save on toys, litter & more" tells me nothing about the actual deal. Am I saving 10%? 40%? As someone who reads ingredient labels, I also read price tags carefully. Vague savings language makes me suspicious before I've even tapped.
- **No loyalty hook above the fold** — I'm a Treats member. Not seeing my points balance or any "member pricing" callout is a miss. I earn on every purchase; remind me of that.
- **Navigation is hamburger-only** — Medium tech comfort here, and on mobile I find buried hamburger menus annoying. There are no visible category shortcuts above the fold. If I wanted grooming supplies instead of the cat deals, I'd have to dig.
- **No trust signals** — No review counts, no "free shipping over $X," nothing. I'm used to seeing that strip on most pet retail sites. Its absence makes the page feel slightly thin.
- **The hero is text-heavy and image-light above fold** — I can't see a single product or cat clearly. The bottom of the hero only hints at a cat. Show me my cat's world, not just words about it.

## Recommendations

- **Add the specific discount to the banner**: Change "Save on toys, litter & more" to "Save up to 40% on toys, litter & more" — one number does more work than the word "save."
- **Surface my Treats balance or a member CTA** in the top nav bar, next to the account icon. One small "500 pts" badge would immediately tell me I have skin in the game.
- **Swap the text-dominant hero for a lifestyle image** of two cats (indoor, domestic — my cats, not show cats) with the deal copy overlaid. The red background is a lot of color without enough visual payoff.
- **Add a horizontal category shortcut strip** below the search bar — "Cat Food · Litter · Grooming · Toys" — four taps, all my categories, no hamburger required.
