# Homepage snapshot · www.petsmart.com · 2026-10-26
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- This looks like PetSmart in full Halloween mode. It leads with a big "EXTRA 20% OFF" code promo and a poodle in a skeleton costume. The deal is clear and I like the member and Autoship links up top. But the first screen is all dog, and I'm a two-cat household that buys litter on auto-ship.

## What's working

- **The code banner at the very top** ("Save an EXTRA 20% OFF… SAVE20 thru 11/1*") repeats the hero offer, so I can't miss it.
- **The "Treats Rewards" and "Autoship" links** in the top bar. I already use both, so I know where to go.
- **Store info at the top** ("Alhambra • Opens today at 9am"). I do grooming in-store, so I notice that right away.
- **The Cat Food, Cat Treats and Prescriptions tiles** in the purple band. They show real products I recognize, like an indoor-health bag and Temptations.
- **The "Deals" link in red** stands out in the nav.
- **The "11,764,897 Adoptions" counter** gives the brand some heart and credibility.

## What's weak

- **The hero is a poodle in a skeleton suit with no cat in sight.** It's cute, but it isn't for me.
- **The orange "THOUSAND of items" circle is clipped** at the right edge, so it looks broken.
- **The hero text "thousands of items online with code:" crowds the striped divider.** It's a little cramped.
- **"Thousands of items" is vague.** I'm a label reader, and I want to know if my litter and food are included before I trust it. The "*" hides the exclusions.
- **The purple band mixes dog and cat items in one carousel.** The first tile is dog food, and the Disposable Dog pads are on the far right. I have to swipe past dog stuff to find cat things.
- **No litter tile is visible.** Litter is my auto-ship staple, and I only see Autoship as a small top link.
- **No reviews or ratings** appear on any product tile.

## Recommendations

- **Swap or rotate the hero** to show a cat, for example a cat in a Halloween costume or a "Cat Care Week" panel. Cat households are a big audience and are invisible here.
- **Fix the "THOUSAND of items" circle** so the text isn't clipped, and give the headline and the striped divider more breathing room.
- **Add a "Litter & Autoship" tile and "Cat" and "Dog" tabs** to the purple band, so cat shoppers can get to litter and cat food in one tap. Put a visible "Save 5% with Autoship" note near it.
- **Add star ratings and a one-line exclusions note** ("Excludes select brands, see details") next to the "*" so the 20% feels honest.
