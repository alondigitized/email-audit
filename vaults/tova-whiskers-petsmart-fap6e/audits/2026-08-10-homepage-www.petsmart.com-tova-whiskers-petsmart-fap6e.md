# Homepage snapshot · www.petsmart.com · 2026-08-10
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- This reads like a big-box pet store leading with its subscription program, not a seasonal sale — the whole top of the page is built around "Autoship." That actually lines up with how I already shop (litter's on auto-ship, has been for two years), so I don't feel like I'm being sold something foreign. It's a mass-market page trying to funnel everyone into a subscribe habit, and the pet imagery is sweet, if dog-heavy for a cat household like mine.

## What's working

- **"Unbox more with Autoship" hero** — I already auto-ship litter, so this isn't a stretch pitch, it's literally my routine getting a discount attached to it.
- **The tiered discount (40%/20%/10% off first three orders)** — concrete numbers I can do math on, not vague "save more" language.
- **"Get faves right on schedule" strip with Cat Litter and Cat Food & Treats icons** — both land in my actual cart every month, one tap away.
- **"11,764,970 lives saved" counter** — a nice trust nudge near the top without being preachy.

## What's weak

- **That "40%" badge on the top right of the hero is cut off** — I can only read "1st ORDER 40%O..." — looks like the page didn't render fully, which makes me trust the offer a little less.
- **The hero photo is dog-forward** — the dog is in the foreground taking up most of the frame, cat's perched behind on the box. I'm a two-cat household, so the imagery skews past me even though the copy doesn't.
- **No grooming callout above the fold** — I book grooming for my long-hair every month and there's nothing here pointing me toward Pet Services, just the nav tab.
- **"Sign up, earn points, get treats" reads like I'm not a member yet** — I already collect Treats rewards, so this generic acquisition line doesn't acknowledge that.

## Recommendations

- Fix the clipped "40%" badge so the full offer is legible without guessing.
- Swap the hero photo crop so the cat is as prominent as the dog, or run a cat-only variant for logged-in cat-product shoppers.
- Add a small grooming-appointment CTA near the top nav or hero — something like "Book grooming near you" — since that's a recurring in-store trip for me.
- If I'm a recognized Treats member, replace "Sign up, earn points, get treats" with my actual points balance instead of a signup pitch.
