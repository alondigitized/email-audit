# Homepage snapshot · www.petsmart.com · 2026-07-06
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-06**
## Executive summary

- PetSmart's homepage is currently all-in on one message: save 20% with a promo code. I appreciate a straight deal, but this page doesn't know me — there's no cat anywhere in sight, no nod to loyalty perks, just a green lawn and a discount. It feels like a flyer in the store parking lot, not a homepage built for someone who just auto-shipped two bags of litter last week.

## What's working

- **The promo banner + hero redundancy**: The blue top strip and the full-screen hero both carry the SAVE20 message — I can't miss it. For a deal-hunter, this is smart repetition.
- **Promo deadline is front and center**: "thru 7/12" tells me exactly how long I have. That's the nudge I need to pull out my phone and check if my litter is in the sale.
- **Clean mobile layout**: Hamburger menu, account icon, logo, store locator, cart — all visible, nothing clashing. The search bar sits right under the nav where I'd expect it.
- **"Shop now" button is unambiguous blue**: Stands out against the green background. Even on a small screen I know where to tap.

## What's weak

- **Zero cat content above the fold**: I have two cats. I came for cat stuff. The hero is literally a patch of grass with no animals, no products, no "treats" or "grooming" — nothing that says this store is for me.
- **Generic CTA copy**: "Shop now" tells me nothing about where I'd land. Is this all cats? All pets? Will I wade through dog food before I find the Fancy Feast sale?
- **No loyalty hook**: I'm a Treats member — my rewards balance should be right there next to my cart icon. It's not. That's a missed opportunity to keep me from switching to Chewy.
- **The asterisk on 7/12\***: The asterisk is doing a lot of work I can't see. That alone makes me pause before I feel excited about the 20%.
- **No category tiles, no department navigation visible**: Below the hero there's nothing — no quick-tap to "Cats" or "Grooming." I'd have to dig through the hamburger menu to get where I want to go.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the grass-and-nothing hero for a cat lifestyle shot**: Show a fluffy long-hair cat (the grooming demo animal, basically), headline still leads with the 20% off. Now the same promo works twice as hard for the cat-owner segment.
- **Surface the loyalty balance in the nav bar**: Put my Treats points next to the cart icon — even just a star icon with a number. It costs one nav slot and it dramatically reduces my temptation to price-shop elsewhere.
- **Replace "Shop now" with category-specific CTA buttons**: "Shop Cat" and "Shop Dog" below the hero — two taps, no ambiguity. I don't want to scroll through dog toys to reach the feline section.
- **Resolve the asterisk above the fold**: Either drop the exclusions language into a small inline line ("excl. rx, grooming") or kill the asterisk. As it stands it erodes trust right when the deal should be building it.
