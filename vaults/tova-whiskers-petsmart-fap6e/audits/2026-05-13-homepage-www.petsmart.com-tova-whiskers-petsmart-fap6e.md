# Homepage snapshot · www.petsmart.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- I opened PetSmart expecting to see my cats — or at least a cat — and instead I'm looking at a woman with a parrot on her shoulder. The 20% off promo is real and the deadline is in two days, which is the most useful thing on this screen. But as a cat household who is here to grab grooming supplies and maybe stock up on treats, this homepage feels like it's talking past me.

## What's working

- **"EXTRA 20% OFF thousands of pet faves online with code: SAVE20 thru 5/15*"** — that blue sticky banner is impossible to miss and gives me everything I need: the code, the discount amount, the deadline. I noticed it before I even processed the logo.
- **Search bar placement** — right below the nav, easy to tap. At medium tech comfort, I appreciate when I can just type "furminator" and get where I'm going.
- **"Let the spoiling begin"** — warm, pet-parent language that does feel like it's speaking my language even if the image doesn't back it up.
- **Clean render, no friction** — nothing is overlapping, nothing is broken, the page loaded without any modal ambush.

## What's weak

- **Bird hero image** — I have two cats. A lovebird on someone's shoulder tells me nothing about whether this sale includes the stuff I actually buy. PetSmart serves every pet type but the hero should have rotated to something more universally relatable, or at minimum feline.
- **No Treats member hook** — I'm a Treats member. I want to see my rewards balance or a "members save extra" callout above the fold. Nothing.
- **CTA button is cut off** — I can see the promo copy but the actual tap target is below the visible screen. That's a missed moment right when my purchase intent is highest.
- **Hamburger-only navigation** — there are no visible category chips or shortcuts. To get to cat food or grooming I have to open a menu. One extra tap is not a big deal, but it slows me down.
- **No free-shipping mention** — I use auto-ship for litter, so I know it exists, but a first-glance reminder of the threshold would push me toward adding more to cart right now.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero or rotate it to cats/dogs first** — birds are 10% of the pet-owning population. Lead with cats or dogs and use a multi-species carousel if needed. A longhair cat mid-groom would speak directly to people like me.
- **Add a Treats member pill or balance chip to the top nav** — even a small "Treats: 450 pts" next to the account icon would make returning shoppers feel seen immediately.
- **Pull the CTA button up** — crop or compress the hero image so the "Shop now" button lands above the fold on a standard phone screen. Right now the offer exists but the action doesn't.
- **Replace the "Enable accessibility" text link with a free-shipping threshold line** — something like "Free shipping on orders $49+" would do more work in that real estate.
