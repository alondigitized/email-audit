# Homepage snapshot · www.petsmart.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay, they led with autoship — that's me to a T, so I'm not immediately annoyed. The whole above-the-fold screen is basically one big pitch to subscribe and get free same-day delivery, which is practical and I respect it. But I came here as a Treats member who also needs to think about grooming appointments, and none of that is on my screen yet.

## What's working

- **"Autoship just got better" hero headline** — direct, functional, speaks to the habits of someone who reorders litter every month. I didn't have to decode it.
- **"FREE Same-Day Delivery or Store Pickup" subhead** — concrete benefit, no vague "fast shipping" language. I know exactly what I'm getting.
- **"Subscribe & save" CTA button** — clear blue button, no ambiguity, doesn't say "Discover" or "Explore."
- **The top sticky banner** — repeats the offer and gives me the option to act immediately; good belt-and-suspenders placement.
- **Partially visible cat imagery below the fold** — that's enough to signal I'm in the right place before I even scroll.

## What's weak

- **No Treats member hook anywhere** — I have points. I have a history. The page treats me like a stranger. That feels like a miss for what should be a loyalty-driven brand.
- **"Subscribe & save" copy without the save amount** — save HOW much? 10%? 5%? The vagueness makes me skeptical before I even tap.
- **No category navigation visible** — I can't see a direct path to cat food, grooming, or litter. The hamburger menu hides everything; a returning shopper like me shouldn't have to dig.
- **No grooming/services entry point** — I go in-store for my long-hair's monthly groom. That's a key conversion for me and it's invisible here.
- **The page looks generic** — it could be a subscription pitch for dog food, fish food, anything. No specificity for cats, no hook for multi-pet households.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Welcome back, Tova" or Treats balance chip** in the header — even just a points balance number next to the account icon would immediately signal you know me.
- **Show the actual savings percentage on the "Subscribe & save" CTA** — swap "Subscribe & save" for "Subscribe & save 10%" so the value is impossible to miss.
- **Add a two-tap category shortcut rail** below the hero — Cat, Dog, Small Animal, Bird, with icons. One tap to my litter aisle should not require a hamburger menu.
- **Surface a grooming CTA in the sticky banner or just below the fold** — even a small "Book grooming" link next to the autoship pitch would catch me before I leave.
