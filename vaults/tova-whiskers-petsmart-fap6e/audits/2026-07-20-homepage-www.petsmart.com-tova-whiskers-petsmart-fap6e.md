# Homepage snapshot · www.petsmart.com · 2026-07-20
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-20**
## Executive summary

- As a two-cat household who reads every ingredient label and has had Autoship running for over a year, landing on "Purr-worthy savings" feels like PetSmart actually did their homework on me for once. This is clearly a feline-focused push right now — the BOGO deal is the whole story above the fold. My one complaint is I still don't know *what* the BOGO covers, and that asterisk on "see terms*" already has my guard up.

## What's working

- **"Purr-worthy savings" headline** — punny but immediate; I knew within one second this page had something for me
- **Sub-copy clarity** — "Big BOGO deals, plus more for your favorite feline thru 8/2*" gives me an offer type, a species focus, and a deadline in one line
- **Mobile readability** — red background, bold white text, high contrast; legible on a small screen without squinting
- **Single CTA** — one blue "Shop now" button with no competing links fighting for the tap
- **Top Autoship banner** — even though it's aimed at new sign-ups, it reminds me the program exists and that there may be savings to stack

## What's weak

- **"Shop now" is too vague** — BOGO on what? Cat food? Treats? Litter? Grooming tools? I need one more word to know if this is worth my time
- **No Treats membership callout** — I have an account; I want to know if BOGO stacks with my points or if I'm leaving rewards on the table
- **No product imagery in the hero** — the red background is bold but abstract; a bag of treats or litter would make the deal feel concrete
- **Asterisk overload** — two asterisked disclosures above the fold with no inline explanation reads as "there's a catch," which makes me slower to tap
- **Hamburger menu only** — no shortcut tabs to Cat Food, Cat Treats, Grooming; finding my specific category requires an extra tap I shouldn't need

## Recommendations

- **Swap "Shop now" for "Shop Feline BOGO Deals"** — one extra word tells me exactly where I'm landing; reduces friction and hesitation
- **Add a Treats member micro-banner** — even a one-line "Treats members earn 2X points on BOGO" would make me feel recognized and incentivized
- **Drop a product thumbnail into the hero** — a bag of Temptations or Fancy Feast below the headline makes the BOGO feel real and browsable, not abstract
- **Expand the asterisk inline** — replace "see terms*" with a small chevron/expandable "Details" link; same legal coverage, less "gotcha" energy for a customer who reads the fine print
