# Homepage snapshot · www.petsmart.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so PetSmart went all-in on cats right now — the entire above-the-fold experience is one big cat deal push, and honestly, as someone with two indoor cats who auto-ships litter every month, they caught me at the right moment. The branding is clean and punchy, though the "Shop meow" button made me roll my eyes just a little. It feels like a site that knows its audience today but doesn't quite know *me* specifically as a returning Treats member.

## What's working

- **The top banner + hero alignment:** "Anything for Cats Deals" in the banner and "Pounce on savings" in the hero work together — the message is consistent and doesn't contradict itself, which sounds basic but a lot of sites can't manage it.
- **"Food, litter, furniture & more":** That sub-copy is doing real work. Three of the most frequent cat-owner purchase categories in one line. That's my shopping list.
- **Deadline urgency:** "thru 5/12\*" with today being May 10 creates genuine two-day pressure without being fake. I appreciate a real date over "limited time only."
- **Clean mobile render:** No overcrowding, type is readable, button is thumb-sized. This is a phone-first experience done right.

## What's weak

- **"Shop meow" button copy:** I'm 47, not 12. It's trying to be cute, and I get it, but "Shop Cat Deals" would have gotten my tap faster. The pun slows me down for a beat.
- **No Treats member acknowledgment:** I'm logged in (or should be), and there's zero signal that the site knows me. No rewards balance, no personalized greeting, no member-exclusive pricing flag. That's a miss for a loyalty program that's supposed to be a differentiator.
- **No specific savings figure:** "Save on toys, litter & more" — save *how much*? Even "up to 30% off" would sharpen this. Right now it reads like a soft promise.
- **No ingredient or product-quality signals:** I read labels. There's nothing here about premium brands, nutrition, or grooming-specific content. The page is all about price, not quality — and I care about both.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Treats member balance chip or "Member deals inside" badge** near the CTA — it costs almost nothing to render and makes returning shoppers feel seen.
- **Replace "Shop meow" with "Shop Cat Deals"** or at minimum "Shop Savings" — the pun is cute once, but clear beats clever every time on mobile.
- **Put a specific discount figure in the hero sub-copy:** "Save up to 30% on food, litter, furniture & more thru 5/12" converts better than the vague "find big deals."
- **Add a grooming or premium-brand callout below the fold teaser** — a line like "Royal Canin, Hill's, and grooming essentials included" tells label-readers like me that this isn't just clearance junk.
