# Homepage snapshot · www.petsmart.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- PetSmart is running a Memorial Day sale and making sure I know about it — the red promo banner and the hero both say the same thing twice. As a PetSmart regular who's here mainly for cat food, litter auto-ship, and my long-hair's monthly grooming appointment, I appreciate the deal, but the hero dog on the lawn doesn't exactly speak to me. This looks like a mass-retail site doing a holiday push, not a site that knows I'm a cat person.

## What's working

- **Double-decker promo placement** — the red sticky banner and the hero copy both push SAVE20, so I can't miss the deal even if I skim
- **"thru 5/28" deadline** — that's a real calendar anchor, not vague "limited time"; it makes me feel like I should check what's in my cart before the weekend
- **Search bar above the fold** — I know what I want (Purina Pro Plan, Arm & Hammer litter); being able to search immediately without navigating a menu is exactly how I use this app
- **Clean mobile layout** — hamburger, account icon, cart icon, search — nothing is fighting for space, the hierarchy is legible on a phone screen

## What's weak

- **Dog in the hero, every time** — I have two cats, I'm here every month, and the main image is a golden retriever rear end on a lawn. I don't feel seen.
- **No Treats member callout** — I've been a Treats member long enough to have accumulated points, and there's zero acknowledgment of that above the fold. No "your rewards balance" or "member price" anywhere.
- **"Shop now" goes nowhere specific** — that CTA could dump me on a general sale page with 10,000 dog products before I reach cat food. No category shortcut for cats.
- **No free-shipping info** — this is an online-code deal and I have no idea if there's a free-shipping threshold. For auto-ship items like litter, that matters.
- **Accessibility link sits awkwardly** — "Enable accessibility" appears as a plain hyperlink between the promo banner and the nav bar. It's oddly prominent and breaks the visual flow.

## Recommendations

- **Add a cat-owner persistent entry point** — even a small "Cats" shortcut chip below the search bar (next to "Dogs," "Fish," etc.) would let me get to my section in one tap instead of drilling through the hamburger menu
- **Surface Treats member balance or a member-exclusive badge** — replace or supplement the generic SAVE20 banner with "Treats members get an extra 5% on top" — I'd feel rewarded for loyalty, not just lumped in with everyone else
- **Swap the hero image for a lifestyle shot that includes cats** — or at minimum rotate: dog one day, cat the next. Half their shoppers have cats; the hero is doing nothing for us right now
- **Add free-shipping threshold to the promo banner** — something like "Free shipping over $49 · SAVE20 for 20% off thru 5/28" turns the banner into a decision driver, not just a code reminder
