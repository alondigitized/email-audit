# Homepage snapshot · www.razer.com · 2026-07-12
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-12**
## Executive summary

- Okay so I open Razer.com expecting Chroma, black chassis, aggressive green lighting — and instead I get a pastel blue sky with a bunny rabbit headset? This is the Razer x Cinnamoroll collab and the whole vibe is kawaii cute, not RGB gaming. Razer is clearly chasing a collab audience right now, and if that's you, cool — but it's not me.

## What's working

- **"BUY" button** — zero ambiguity. I know exactly what to do if I want this product. That dark button against the lighter background actually pops.
- **Back-to-School banner** — "bonus Razer skin with any eligible Razer Blade purchase" is a real hook for my age group, and Back-to-School in July is timed right.
- **Mobile layout** — hamburger + logo + search/cart is clean and standard. No clutter, thumb-friendly placement.
- **"LEARN MORE" + "BUY" dual CTA** — the split lets me browse before committing, which is how I actually shop (I always research before I pull the trigger).

## What's weak

- **The hero is not for me.** "CLOUDY DAYS, COZY PLAYS." is cute copy for a Sanrio fan, but as someone who has a Synapse profile named "VALORANT_KILL_MODE" this doesn't resonate at all.
- **No Chroma, no RGB, no green.** Razer's whole identity is the triple-snake + aggressive green lighting aesthetic. This page is baby blue clouds. I literally double-checked I was on razer.com.
- **No price or deal signal.** I shop gold-box deals. If there's a promo price on the Cinnamoroll kit, I can't see it — the banner says "perks" without a number.
- **No category nav shortcut.** On mobile the hamburger is my only path to mice, keyboards, headsets. I can't tap into peripherals in one move from this hero.
- **Product image is small and cropped.** I can see what looks like a white headset but it's partially cut off. I can't tell if it's the Barracuda, Kraken, or something else.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero on a rotating basis** — show the Cinnamoroll collab as a secondary carousel slide, put a Chroma peripherals or Blade deal front and center for the main audience, then let the collab be its own landing page.
- **Add a price to the Back-to-School banner** — even "Blades from $1,299" or "save $X on kits" gives gold-box shoppers a reason to tap, versus vague "upsized perks."
- **Surface a quick-nav strip below the hero** — "Mice | Keyboards | Headsets | Laptops" as tappable pills so I can skip straight to peripherals without going into the hamburger.
- **Show the Chroma ecosystem somewhere above the fold** — even a small "Synapse-ready" badge or a Chroma glow on the product would reassure me this is still a gaming brand.
