# Homepage snapshot · www.razer.com · 2026-07-19
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay so Razer is coming in with their Hammerhead V3 X Hyperspeed earbuds — console-focused, split between Xbox and PlayStation colorways. The RGB on that Xbox case is *chef's kiss* but my first thought is "why am I seeing console earbuds?" I'm here for the desktop setup drops, not the Switch-on-the-couch crowd. The page is clean and dark like Razer always is, but the targeting is just... not me right now.

## What's working

- **The RGB glow on the Xbox case** — that Razer green lighting effect under the earbud case is genuinely eye-catching, speaks to anyone who has Chroma-everything on their desk
- **Split Xbox / PlayStation CTA buttons** — two distinct green and blue buttons that actually match each platform's color identity is smart execution, very native to how Razer thinks about their ecosystem
- **Back-to-School banner** — seasonal, relevant, and positioned at the very top as a thin strip that doesn't hijack the hero
- **Dark theme / typography** — the full-bleed black with massive bold white text is 100% on-brand and doesn't feel like a legacy retailer trying to look cool

## What's weak

- **Console earbuds don't hit my setup** — I came for keyboards, mice, headsets, maybe Chroma-compatible gear for my streaming rig; this hero is targeting couch gamers
- **Zero pricing above the fold** — no MSRP, no "starting at," no sale price anywhere; I can't evaluate if this is a gold-box-level deal or not without tapping
- **No PC / streaming peripherals shortcut** — hamburger-only nav means I can't one-tap to mice or keyboards; I have to dig
- **No rewards or Insider callout** — I'm a repeat Razer buyer and there's nothing reminding me I have points or member pricing
- **No social proof** — no star rating, no review count, nothing on the earbuds that tells me if they're actually good

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero to a PC streaming setup shot** — show a lit desk with the Hammerhead plugged into a MacBook or PC dongle, not just the console cases; it broadens appeal without dropping the console buyers
- **Surface a specific price or member price under the CTA** — even "From $99.99 | Razer Insider: $89.99" gets me thinking about value before I tap
- **Add a nav shortcut strip** — a horizontal scrollable chip row (Mice · Keyboards · Headsets · Controllers) just below the header banner so I can one-tap to my category without the hamburger
- **Add a review count to the hero** — "4.7★ (2,400+ reviews)" next to the product name costs nothing and turns the scroll into a tap
