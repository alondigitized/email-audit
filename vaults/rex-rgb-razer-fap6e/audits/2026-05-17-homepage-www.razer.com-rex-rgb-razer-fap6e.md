# Homepage snapshot · www.razer.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- OK so I opened razer.com and it's all dark, all RGB, all business — immediately feels like my brand. But the whole above-the-fold push is a laptop (Razer Blade 18) and I'm here for the peripherals, the keyboard drama, the Chroma accessories. The Members Fest cashback deal is genuinely solid though — $80 back on $99+ is the kind of number that makes me open the nav.

## What's working

- **Razer Members Fest banner** — cashback + 30% off codes in one strip, readable at a glance; $99 threshold is fully reachable on a single peripheral buy
- **Blade 18 RGB wave animation on the screen** — that swirling green/yellow Chroma display is pure eye candy for me, exactly the vibe I want from a Razer device
- **Dark mobile layout** — zero white-space chaos, zero lifestyle fluff; it reads like a Synapse profile came to life
- **"ONLY AT RAZER" badge** — exclusive signal, makes me feel like I'm in the right place and not just price-comparing on Amazon
- **Dual CTAs ("Learn More" + "Buy")** — gives me the option to investigate before committing, which I always do before a big Razer drop

## What's weak

- **No peripheral entry point above the fold** — I'm a keyboard/mouse/headset buyer; the entire first screen is a $3k laptop and zero path to the categories I actually click
- **Hamburger-only nav on mobile** — my category is one extra tap away behind a hidden menu; that's friction for a return visitor who just wants to hit Keyboards or Peripherals directly
- **No human/streamer imagery** — just a laptop on a dark background; I want to see someone at a battle-station, a desk with Chroma glow, a streaming rig — something that mirrors my actual setup goals
- **"AI DEVS" sub-headline** — that's not my identity; it pulls the copy toward a productivity/work audience and slightly dilutes the gaming signal
- **No trust signals** — no review stars, no "free shipping over X," no "easy returns"; mobile first-time visitors have nothing to lean on

## Recommendations

- **Add a persistent peripheral nav strip** below the hero (Mice / Keyboards / Headsets / Streaming) — one row, icon + label, zero extra scroll; this is what I'm here for and right now it's invisible
- **Swap the sub-headline** from "For Gamers & AI Devs" to something like "For Gamers. Built to Dominate." — keep it in my lane, lose the developer detour
- **Include a free-shipping callout** in the Members Fest banner (e.g., "Free shipping on orders $49+") — this is table stakes for mobile conversion and it's not there
- **Add a mini Chroma desk-setup lifestyle shot** as a secondary module directly under the hero — not a model ad, just a lit-up battlestation that mirrors what I'm building
