# Homepage snapshot · www.razer.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay so Razer is leading with the Huntsman V3 Tenkeyless 8KHz and honestly? As someone who has a Synapse profile literally named "Warzone_Comp" I feel personally called out. This is peak Razer energy — black background, RGB glow on the keyboard, big spec callout front and center. It's a hardware brand doing a product launch page, not really a shopping homepage, which is both cool and kinda annoying when I'm trying to browse.

## What's working

- **"RAZER HUNTSMAN V3 TENKEYLESS 8KHZ"** — leading with the spec in the headline is the right call for me. I already know what 8KHz polling means and it makes me stop scrolling instantly.
- **"FOR THE PRO" subhead** — short, punchy, no fluff. That's the copy register I actually respond to.
- **Dual CTA layout ("Learn More" + "Buy")** — giving me the option to research OR buy right now without forcing a decision is smart. I'm a spec-checker before I commit.
- **RGB lit keyboard product shot** — it's glowing. Chroma is the whole identity. The visual does the selling without any lifestyle overhead.
- **Black background / dark theme** — looks like my desktop wallpaper. Fully native to the aesthetic I already live in.

## What's weak

- **No price or offer visible** — I cross-shop this exact keyboard category against Logitech G and Corsair. If I don't see a price or a deal hook, my first instinct is to open a tab and check Amazon gold-box deals. That's a conversion leak Razer is handing competitors.
- **No Synapse / Chroma ecosystem callout** — the whole reason I stay Razer is the ecosystem lock-in. Not mentioning Synapse integration or Chroma RGB profiles above the fold is a missed loyalty trigger.
- **No social proof whatsoever** — no review count, no star rating, no "X units sold." I'm 24, I check reviews before I spend $150+ on a keyboard, full stop.
- **Hamburger-only navigation on mobile** — I can't see what categories exist without opening the menu. If I came here to browse mice or headsets, there's zero breadcrumbing visible.
- **No urgency or scarcity signal** — if this is a launch, say it. "Now available" or a launch date creates FOMO. Right now it reads like it could've been live for a year.

## Recommendations

- **Add a price + deal badge directly under the hero headline** — even just "$149.99 | Free shipping" keeps me from tabbing out to Amazon before I hit "Buy."
- **Drop a Chroma/Synapse badge or callout below the product image** — "Works with Synapse 3 — 16.8M colors, game-linked profiles" is a one-liner that hits my exact buying trigger and differentiates from Corsair iCUE.
- **Put a star-rating chip (e.g., ★ 4.7 — 2,840 reviews) under the product name** — it takes one line of space and it's the single biggest trust signal for my age group.
- **Surface a horizontal category strip below the hero** — Mice | Keyboards | Headsets | Streaming | Controllers — so I can tap my way deeper without opening the hamburger.
