# Homepage snapshot · www.razer.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Razer pulled up with a full Wuthering Waves collab drop and honestly that's immediate brain activation for me — I literally have a Synapse profile called WuWa already. Dark background, that Razer green logo, pink Chroma-coded game art, this screams right at my setup aesthetic. The Members Fest banner is doing real work too, but the hero itself is more of a hype teaser than a shop page, so conversion is going to suffer.

## What's working

- **Members Fest banner** — hits instantly, before I even read the hero. 15% off plus free shipping is a real incentive and "Shop Now >" is a direct path to deals
- **Razer x Wuthering Waves collab headline** — the "RAZER | WUTHERING WAVES" lockup in all-caps white on black is immediately legible; I know exactly what the campaign is in under a second
- **Color palette** — the pink/magenta Wuthering Waves art against that deep black background is Chroma energy through and through; it's the exact aesthetic of my desk right now
- **Green CTA button** — Razer brand green button on black is extremely high contrast, stands out from the rest of the hero, hard to miss even on a small phone screen
- **Razer snake logo + WuWa collab mark** — the dual-logo treatment below the hero is clean; the pink snake variant is a nice Chroma touch

## What's weak

- **"LEARN MORE" CTA** — I don't know if I'm clicking to a product page or just a collab lookbook. "Shop the Collab" or "Get the Gear" would actually push me to convert
- **No product visible anywhere** — no mouse, keyboard, headset, or peripheral in the entire viewport. I can't see what I'm even supposed to want
- **"RESONANCE IN HAND" tagline** — it's evocative but vague. What does it mean for a peripheral? Mouse? Controller? Headset? Give me something to latch onto
- **No price anchoring** — the Members Fest banner promises up to 15% off but I can't see what's on sale or what the starting prices look like
- **Zero trust signals** — no reviews, no rating counts, no "free returns," nothing that closes the gap between hype and buy

## Recommendations

- **Swap "LEARN MORE" for "Shop the Collab"** — one word change, immediately tells me there's product to buy, removes ambiguity about whether this is a marketing splash or a shopping destination
- **Add at least one product shot below the hero art** — even a single mouse or headset in Wuthering Waves colorway would lock in the "I need this for my setup" impulse purchase; right now the hero is all branding, zero product
- **Surface the Members Fest end date** — the banner says "get up to 15% off" but with no deadline I have zero urgency to act now vs. tomorrow; even "through Sunday" would move that needle
- **Pin the Members Fest offer into the hero or as a sticky chip** — the banner is small and easy to miss; a "Members get 15% off this drop" callout tied directly to the Wuthering Waves hero would make the discount feel exclusive and targeted
