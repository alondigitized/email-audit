# Homepage snapshot · www.razer.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Okay so Razer opens with the Blade 18 front and center — pure dark-room gaming energy, Chroma keys glowing, screen animation flexing. This is clearly a premium laptop push, not a peripherals drop, which is a little wild for me since I'm mostly here for a new keyboard or some Chroma strips. The vibe is unmistakably Razer though — black and green, no nonsense.

## What's working

- **The Chroma RGB keyboard glow on the Blade 18** — that wave animation across the screen plus the lit keys is genuinely sick. Stopped my scroll immediately.
- **"GAMERS & AI DEVS" headline** — hits exactly right. Not "professionals" or "creatives." Gamers. That's my word.
- **Dual CTA layout ("Learn More" + "Buy >")** — respects that some people are browsing and some people already know they want it. I appreciate that.
- **Dark room background** — the staging with the moody green-lit workspace behind the laptop? That's literally what my desk looks like. On-brand and on-demo simultaneously.
- **Razer snake logo centered in nav** — clean mobile header, instantly recognized, no bloat.

## What's weak

- **No price anywhere** — I'm a gold-box shopper. If you're not showing me a number or a deal above the fold, I'm already mentally tabbing to Amazon to check.
- **No peripheral content visible** — I came here for keyboards and mice. The entire above-fold is one laptop. Nav is hamburger-gated so I can't see if peripherals are even reachable in one tap.
- **Zero loyalty or Razer Gold callout** — Razer has a rewards program. Why is that not on the homepage? I have points and I have no idea what they're worth.
- **No trust signals** — no review count, no "free shipping on orders over $X," nothing. For a $2,000+ laptop, that's leaving conversion on the table.
- **"Learn More >"** CTA is weak — I already know what a Blade 18 is. That button feels like it's for someone's dad who stumbled onto the site.

## Recommendations

- **Add a price or "Starting at $X" beneath the headline** — even a starting price anchors the purchase intent and stops the bounce to Amazon for comparison shopping.
- **Surface a peripheral rail below the hero** — drop a horizontal scroll of Chroma keyboards, mice, and headsets. A lot of mobile Razer visitors aren't here for a $2K laptop; give them a fast path to the $80–$200 accessories.
- **Add a Razer Gold / rewards CTA in the header** — even a small "💎 Gold: 240 pts" badge or "Sign in for rewards" nudge turns this from a billboard into a relationship.
- **Swap "Learn More >" for a specific feature hook** — something like "See Chroma in action >" or "240Hz, RTX 5090 →" tells me exactly what I'm clicking into and builds hype.
