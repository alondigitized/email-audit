# Homepage snapshot · www.razer.com · 2026-05-31
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-31**
## Executive summary

- Bro, I land on Razer's homepage and the first thing I get is a full-screen modal shoved in my face before I can even breathe. The hero behind it is a BLACKPINK x Razer collab — pink everything, which is literally my whole setup — and I can barely see it. The site is pushing a gaming chair discount and the brand knows exactly who it's talking to, but the delivery is chaos.

## What's working

- **BLACKPINK x Razer hero** — I can see the pink setup and that pink chair in the background behind the modal. Pink Razer is my entire identity. If that hero were visible without a modal in the way, it would stop my scroll cold.
- **The chair deal in the modal** — "Unlock up to $200 Off" is the kind of gold-box-energy deal I actually screenshot and send to the group chat. Streaming setup builders think about chairs.
- **Green CTA button** — Razer green on black, high contrast, zero ambiguity. I know exactly what tap does what.
- **Razer logo + nav icons** — top bar is minimal and clean: hamburger, logo, search, cart. I know how to navigate even with the modal up.

## What's weak

- **The modal is the entire above-the-fold experience** — I came to see what Razer is running right now and I get forced into a chair ad before I can see anything. That's friction, not conversion.
- **No RGB peripherals anywhere visible** — I'm a peripheral buyer first. I cross-shop keyboards. Nothing about Chroma, DeathAdder, BlackWidow, or headsets is visible in this viewport.
- **"Up to $200 off" is vague** — no starting price, no specific SKU. That "up to" is doing a lot of work. Gold-box shoppers want the actual number.
- **BLACKPINK collab almost fully obscured** — the actual hero that would make me stay is hidden. That's a miss.
- **No urgency signal** — no timer, no "48 hours only," no stock count. Chair deals should have urgency.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the interrupt modal, make the BLACKPINK x Razer hero full-bleed** — let the pink collab breathe as the actual above-the-fold experience with its own CTA. That hero is already selling me; the modal is killing the vibe.
- **Add a sticky promo strip above the nav**: "BLACKPINK x Razer drops now — Chair Special: up to $200 off" so the deal stays present without hijacking the page.
- **Show a specific chair starting price** — swap "up to $200 off" for "from $X" or "now $349" so gold-box shoppers can immediately calibrate whether this is worth tapping.
- **Put a Razer Gold / Rewards balance hook in the nav bar** — a logged-in user's point balance next to the cart icon is a one-line change that drives repeat purchase.
