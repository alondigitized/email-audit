# Homepage snapshot · www.razer.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 10/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Okay so Razer opened with the Blade 18 and honestly I'm not mad at it — this is exactly the kind heat that makes me want to start a new Synapse profile. The Members Fest banner up top is a nice touch since I'm already a Razer member, and the laptop on display has that signature green Chroma wave going full send on the screen. It's pushing premium PC hardware right now, which tracks.

## What's working

- **"Razer Members Fest" promo banner** — hits me before the hero even loads. $80 back + 30% off codes? As a member I'm already doing mental math.
- **"ONLY AT RAZER" exclusivity badge** — that gold badge above the headline reads as collector-brain bait. This SKU is mine-only, which matters to me.
- **Razer Blade 18 hero image** — the RGB wave pattern on the display is genuinely gorgeous. That green-to-yellow Chroma gradient is exactly the aesthetic I'd set as a Synapse profile. It sells the vibe without needing a person.
- **Dual CTA layout ("Learn More" + "Buy")** — respects that I might want specs OR I might just want to cart it. Good split for early vs. late-funnel me.
- **Tight copy** — "THE EDGE OF PERFORMANCE FOR GAMERS & AI DEVS" is short, punchy, zero fluff. Very my speed.

## What's weak

- **No nav categories visible** — mobile hamburger means I can't see Mice, Keyboards, Headsets without a tap. If I'm cross-shopping peripherals (which I always am), I'm one extra step away from my actual browse path.
- **Zero social proof** — no review stars, no "X units sold," no community signal anywhere above the fold. For a $3K+ laptop I need some external validation.
- **No urgency on the Members Fest** — no end date, no countdown. If this sale ends Sunday or in 3 hours, I should know that now.
- **"Up to US$80 back" is vague** — what spend tier gets me the full $80? The banner doesn't say. That ambiguity makes the deal feel smaller than it probably is.
- **No Chroma peripheral content** — I came here as a keyboard/peripheral builder too. The Blade 18 is great but there's nothing on screen for my impulse RGB buys.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Members Fest ends [date]" timestamp to the banner** — even "Ends Sunday" would create real urgency and drive faster clicks to the Members landing page.
- **Swap the dual plain-text CTAs for one styled button** — a solid Razer-green "Buy Now" button with a secondary "See Full Specs" text link performs better on mobile than two equal-weight arrows.
- **Surface the spend tier breakdown inline** — "Spend $99 → $20 back / $199 → $50 back / $299 → $80 back" right in the banner removes the vagueness and makes the $80 feel achievable.
- **Add a horizontal peripheral scroll rail below the hero** — even 3 tiles (a keyboard, a mouse, a headset) would let me start a second tab on the Razer BlackWidow without digging into nav.
