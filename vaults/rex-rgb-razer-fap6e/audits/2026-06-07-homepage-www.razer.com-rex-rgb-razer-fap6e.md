# Homepage snapshot · www.razer.com · 2026-06-07
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-07**
## Executive summary

- Yo, this is peak Razer aesthetic — pitch-black background, snake logo, electric green accents, very clean on mobile. But bro, they're hitting me with a **backpack** as the hero? I'm here to drop money on a new keyboard or maybe upgrade my headset stand and the front page is like "check out this bag." The brand vibe is immaculate, the product choice is baffling.

## What's working

- **Dark-mode brand identity** — the all-black background with Razer green on both backpacks is *exactly* my aesthetic. Whoever set this up knows the audience.
- **"BUY" button** — no ambiguity. It's outlined, right there, in-your-face. I don't have to hunt for it.
- **Tagline copy** — "BUILT FOR BATTLE. READY FOR THE GRIND" lands right for me. That's my language.
- **Mobile layout** — tight, nothing overflowing, product image is clean and well-lit. Renders sharp on phone.

## What's weak

- **Wrong hero product for me** — a Rogue Backpack V4 is not what I'm showing up to Razer.com to buy. I want to see the BlackWidow, the DeathAdder, a Chroma controller — something for my desk.
- **Zero pricing information** — no price on the hero product, no discount badge, no "starting at $X." I can't even gauge if this is worth tapping.
- **No navigation shortcuts** — it's just a hamburger menu. I can't get to Keyboards or Mice in one tap. That's buried.
- **No social proof** — no star ratings, no "thousands of reviews," nothing that tells me other people bought this and loved it.
- **No rewards/Insider hook** — as someone who buys Razer gear regularly, I want to see my Insider status or at least a prompt to sign in. Cold page has no memory of me.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero to an RGB peripheral** — put the BlackWidow V4 or Kraken V3 front and center with a Chroma lighting glow shot. That's the product that closes for someone like me. Reserve backpack for a secondary carousel slot.
- **Surface pricing + a deal above the fold** — even "from $79" or a "Gold Box"-style badge would get me to tap. Right now there's nothing to react to financially.
- **Add a nav shortcut strip below the hero** — a horizontal scroll rail of "Keyboards · Mice · Headsets · Accessories" keeps me from bouncing when the hero isn't my category.
- **Drop a Razer Insider sign-in prompt** — even a one-liner like "Sign in to see your Insider rewards" converts returning buyers and reminds me this ecosystem has points I should be spending.
