# Homepage snapshot · www.razer.com · 2026-07-05
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-05**
## Executive summary

- Bro, Razer dropped a Wuthering Waves collab and made it the entire homepage — anime-art gaming chairs front and center with a pre-order CTA. This reads as a limited-drop, hype-first brand play: they're not selling me a keyboard today, they're selling a moment. The Chroma pink bleeding in from the right side of the hero? Chef's kiss for the aesthetic, honestly.

## What's working

- **Razer x Wuthering Waves co-brand** — this collab is exactly the kind of limited drop that makes me open a new tab and start budgeting. The chair art with Rover/Jinhsi imagery is immediately recognizable to anyone who plays.
- **Dual CTA layout** ("LEARN MORE" + "PRE-ORDER") — giving me an option to vibe first or commit right now is smart. Doesn't feel pushy.
- **Top banner loyalty pitch** — "signature exclusives, max rewards, extra year of warranty" hits the right notes for buying direct vs. Amazon. That extra warranty line actually matters to me.
- **Pink-to-black color gradient on the hero** — the Chroma pink bleed on the right edge matches the brand energy perfectly. It's not trying too hard; it just IS Razer.
- **Minimal mobile nav** — hamburger menu kept clean, logo centered, search + cart accessible. No cluttered header junk.

## What's weak

- **No keyboard or peripheral focus** — I'm here partly to cross-shop keyboards and I see zero product category nav above the fold. The entire viewport is one collab. No Huntsman, no BlackWidow, nothing.
- **No pricing visible anywhere** — "PRE-ORDER" with no price anchoring gives me nothing to chew on. How much is the chair? Am I walking into a $400 or $800 situation?
- **No gold-box or sale signal** — I buy at deals. Nothing here tells me there's a sale running, not even a vague "up to X% off" strip.
- **Anime characters ≠ people like me** — the imagery is all product + illustrated anime figures, not a real person in a setup. I can't see myself in it, just the product.
- **No urgency signal** — PRE-ORDER with no deadline, no "ships by," no stock count. What's the timeline here?

## Recommendations

- **Add a price anchor under the PRE-ORDER button** — even "Starting at $X" kills the anxiety of tapping blind. One line of copy, ships next week.
- **Drop a thin "Shop Keyboards" shortcut bar below the hero** — product category chips ("Keyboards · Headsets · Mice · Chairs") let me pivot without hunting through the hamburger menu. Takes maybe two hours to build.
- **Add a collab deadline or shipping date** — "Pre-order by July 31 · Ships September" creates urgency without fake countdown timers. Honest and converts better.
- **Show the Razer Gold reward earn on the PRE-ORDER button** — "PRE-ORDER · Earn 2,400 Gold" right on the CTA turns a purchase into a loyalty investment. Huge for Razer Gold users.
