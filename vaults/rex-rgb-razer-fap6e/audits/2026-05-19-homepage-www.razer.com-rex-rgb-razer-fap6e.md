# Homepage snapshot · www.razer.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay this slaps immediately — black background, green Chroma glow, Razer snake front and center. This is unmistakably Razer and they're pushing the Blade 18 hard with a Members Fest deal running across the top. For a guy like me who literally has a Synapse profile named "Blade Dreams," this hits different than basically any other brand's homepage could.

## What's working

- **"ONLY AT RAZER" exclusivity badge** — that little orange pill above the headline is doing real work. Rex doesn't need a reason to shop Razer, but "only at Razer" confirms I can't price-match this elsewhere. It locks the session.
- **Dual CTA layout** — "LEARN MORE" (outlined) and "BUY" (filled green) side by side respects the two modes I'm in: research mode and wallet-out mode. Both exist depending on the day.
- **Blade 18 with Chroma on the screen** — the laptop's display is showing that green wave animation and Razer logo. That's not a product shot, that's a flex. I'm staring at it.
- **Members Fest banner** — discount + free shipping in one line, right at the top. No scrolling required to know there's a deal active.
- **Dark background, RGB-forward palette** — matches my entire desk aesthetic. Page literally looks like my battlestation.

## What's weak

- **Hamburger nav on mobile** — all my categories (keyboards, mice, headsets — my actual shopping) are hidden behind a tap. I have to go hunt for them. If I landed here for a keyboard deal I'd be clicking through blind.
- **No people in the shot** — I get it, product-first is Razer's thing, but no lifestyle context means I'm not seeing myself in the setup. A quick cut of a streamer at a desk with the Blade would land harder.
- **"Up to 15% off select gear"** — both "up to" and "select" are doing a lot of legal hedging. What's actually 15% off? I'd immediately tap "Shop Now" just to figure out if this deal applies to anything I actually want.
- **No peripheral callout** — I'm here for keyboards and Chroma RGB headsets, not just laptops. The entire above-the-fold is one product (Blade 18). Cross-sell to peripherals is completely absent until I scroll.

## Recommendations

- **Add a peripheral strip directly below the hero** — even a horizontal scroll rail showing "Also in Members Fest: BlackShark V2 HyperSpeed, BlackWidow V4 75%, DeathAdder V3" with deal badges. Don't make me navigate to find the discounts.
- **Show the Members Fest savings in a more concrete format** — swap "up to 15% off select gear" for something like "Members save up to 15% + free shipping — 3 days left." A deadline turns browse into urgency.
- **Drop the hamburger for the most-visited categories** — even two icon-pill tabs (Laptops | Peripherals) pinned below the logo on mobile would let me land in my lane without an extra tap.
- **Replace or augment the hero with a streaming-setup shot** — one secondary carousel slide showing a full desk build (Blade, Chroma peripherals, RGB lighting strips) would speak directly to setup builders like me.
