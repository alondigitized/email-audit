# Homepage snapshot · www.amazon.com · 2026-07-20
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-20**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Amazon opened straight to back-to-school backpacks and honestly that's *me* right now — my group chat has been blowing up about school supply lists all week. This looks like a site that knows the calendar and put the most universally relevant parent thing front and center. The vibe is utilitarian-but-busy, which is very Amazon, but the hero is doing real work for me today.

## What's working

- **"Shop school year faves / Backpacks from our Top 50+ list"** — this hero pulled me in immediately. I have two kids and I've been putting off the backpack search for two weeks. This is the right message at the right time.
- **Brand-name backpacks front and center** — I can see an Adidas, a North Face, and a print backpack. Real brands I actually buy. That's confidence-building without a word of copy.
- **Orange hero blocks** — loud enough to read on a dim phone screen during school pickup, not trying to be pretty, just functional. Very Amazon.
- **"Amazon Haul" in the nav** — I actually use this for cheap household stuff, and seeing it in the top nav gives me a micro-dopamine hit knowing I can hop there.

## What's weak

- **No Prime hook anywhere visible** — I am a Prime member. That's the entire foundation of my relationship with Amazon. Where's my badge, my delivery promise ("Get it tomorrow"), my member price? Missing completely.
- **No price signal on the hero** — "Top 50+ list" is nice but I want a number. "From $19.99" or "Up to 40% off" would make me tap faster.
- **Right-side carousel tile** (the "New releases / World's smart... / Oura R...") is half-cut off and I can't tell what it is — it reads as a layout glitch even if it isn't.
- **No explicit CTA button** — the whole hero is presumably tappable but there's no "Shop now" or "See the list" button text. I'm inferring I should tap it, not being invited.
- **"Health AI" in the nav** — this feels oddly clinical for a general homepage and I genuinely don't know what to do with it on a Tuesday afternoon.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Prime delivery promise to the hero** — even just "Get it by Thursday — free with Prime" under the hero copy would light me up and tie my membership to the urgency of the purchase.
- **Anchor the hero with a price floor** — "Backpacks from $14.99" or "Top picks, 30% off" converts browsers to tappers. A list alone is not an offer.
- **Replace the half-cropped right carousel tile with a full-bleed second CTA** — show me a second school-season category (lunchboxes, supplies) rather than a cut-off wearables promo I can't even read.
- **Add a visible "Shop the list" button overlay** on the hero image so it's unmistakably a CTA, not just a lifestyle image.
