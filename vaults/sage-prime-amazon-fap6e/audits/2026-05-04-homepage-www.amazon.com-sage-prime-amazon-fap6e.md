## What's working

- **"Designer gifts for Mom" module** — the Cartier gold belt and Gucci quilted bag images are genuinely pretty and the headline is dead-on for Mother's Day week. I slowed down.
- **Yellow "Join Prime" button** — color contrast against the blue hero is strong. Even if it's not for me, it's a textbook CTA execution.
- **Location personalization** — "Delivering to Monterey ... 91754" at the top tells me I'm logged in and Amazon knows where I am. Small trust signal that matters.
- **Product tile layout** — two-column grid with clean brand labels (Cartier, Gucci) under each image looks polished and gift-browse-friendly.

## What's weak

- **Hero is wasted on me** — "Join Prime" with a yellow button is 100% new-member acquisition. As a Prime lifer, this entire hero just… doesn't exist for me. No welcome-back messaging, no "Your Prime benefits today" hook, nothing.
- **Hero text is visibly clipped** — "Fast, free shipp..." runs off the right edge of the screen. That's a broken layout I can actually see, and it makes the page feel unfinished on mobile.
- **Navigation is thin and unhelpful** — "Amazon Haul," "Medical Care," "Amazon Basics" — none of those are my day-to-day: no Household, no Subscribe & Save, no Baby/Kids. The nav bar doesn't help me get to my categories faster.
- **No trust or social proof** — not a single review count or star rating visible anywhere in the screenshot. The designer gift tiles are aspirational but I can't see why I should trust a Gucci bag on Amazon without some signal.
- **No urgency** — if "Designer gifts for Mom" is for Mother's Day, where's the deadline? No "Order by May 10" or "Only X left." It's gift season and they left conversion on the table.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the Join Prime hero with a returning-member module** — show my Prime perks dashboard, a Lightning Deal countdown, or a "Your Subscribe & Save is due" nudge. I'm already paying $139/year; remind me why.
- **Fix the hero text overflow on mobile** — "Fast, free shipp..." truncating at the viewport edge is a basic responsive bug. Either reduce font size or rewrite to fit the mobile breakpoint.
- **Add a deadline to the "Designer gifts for Mom" section** — "Free delivery by Mother's Day if ordered by May 10" would push me from browsing to buying in one sentence.
- **Surface a category shortcut row** — a quick-tap strip of my actual categories (Household, Kids, Kitchen) below the hero would cut friction for my phone-during-school-pickup browsing habit.
