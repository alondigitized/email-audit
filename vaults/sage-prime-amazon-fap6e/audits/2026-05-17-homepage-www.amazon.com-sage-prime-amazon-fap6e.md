# Homepage snapshot · www.amazon.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- I come to Amazon the way I turn on the tap — expecting it to just work — and this homepage opens by trying to sell me a subscription I already have. The big blue hero is a Prime recruitment pitch, which means the whole above-the-fold is aimed at someone who isn't me. Below that, "The Italy Edit" feels like a glossy magazine editorial: illy espresso, a designer chair, Ray-Bans, shaving cream. Aspirational? Sure. Useful on a Tuesday night when I need paper towels and a birthday gift? Not really.

## What's working

- **"Join Prime" CTA button** — the yellow pill on blue is a masterclass in contrast; even skimming I clock it instantly
- **The Italy Edit — illy coffee tile** — grocery/gourmet is adjacent to my lane; if I'm low on coffee I might tap it
- **Clean visual hierarchy** — hero → editorial grid is uncluttered; no competing banners fighting for attention
- **Delivery address header** — "Delivering to Alhambra 91801" immediately anchors me in my real world; it's a subtle trust signal that says Amazon knows me

## What's weak

- **The entire hero is for non-Prime users** — I'm Prime. Leading with a Prime recruitment pitch is like showing me a car commercial when I already own the car.
- **No household staples entry point** — no Subscribe & Save callout, no "reorder your essentials," nothing for the mom who's here because she ran out of Tide pods
- **"The Italy Edit" is wrong lifestyle energy** — designer chairs and Ray-Bans are not what I'm browsing for at 9 p.m. after the kids are in bed
- **Navigation is thin** — "Amazon Haul," "Medical Care," "Amazon Basics" — none of those are my fast-path categories (Household, Grocery, Toys, Beauty)
- **Zero social proof above the fold** — no review counts, no ratings, no bestseller badges; this page trusts its brand alone to close

## Recommendations

- **Swap the Prime acquisition hero for a returning-member personalized hero** — "Welcome back, Sage. Your Subscribe & Save ships Thursday" or a seasonal "Stock up for summer" with a household-essentials shortcut grid
- **Add a Lightning Deals or daily-deals strip above the editorial tiles** — even a single "Today's Deal: 30% off Instant Pot ends in 4h" would make me lean in
- **Replace or augment "The Italy Edit" with a "This Week's Picks for Your Home" carousel** — curated but practical; show me things I'd actually put in my cart
- **Surface one category shortcut row ("Shop your usuals")** — a horizontal pill-nav with Grocery, Household, Kids, Beauty takes up one line and immediately tells me this page is for me
