# Homepage snapshot · www.amazon.com · 2026-07-06
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-06**
## Executive summary

- Opening Amazon and being hit with a "Join Prime" acquisition push feels like showing up to a store where you're already a loyalty member and the greeter asks if you've heard of their rewards card. The hero is doing Prime marketing, not shopping. Below the fold there's a soccer merch module that's clearly riding the FIFA World Cup 2026 wave, which — timely, sure — but not why I opened Amazon during school pickup.

## What's working

- **"Join Prime" CTA button** — the gold button on the blue hero is high-contrast and impossible to miss. Even if the pitch is wrong for me, the button itself is well-executed.
- **Visual hierarchy in the hero** — big bold headline, clear CTA below it, nothing cluttered. I know exactly what Amazon wants me to do, even if I don't want to do it.
- **Soccer merch module timing** — USA soccer balls, Argentina jerseys, a FIFA 2026 product thumbnail — this is clearly pegged to the World Cup. For households with kids in rec leagues or sports-fan partners, this is a legitimate hook right now.
- **Delivery location header** — "Delivering to Alhambra 91801" tells me immediately that Prime delivery is active at my address. Low-key trust signal that I actually appreciate.

## What's weak

- **Hero is an acquisition pitch to an existing member** — "Ship it. Stream it. Love it. / Join Prime" is a cold-acquisition message. I've had Prime for years. This isn't speaking to me; it's speaking past me.
- **Zero personalization visible** — No "Good evening, Sage," no recently viewed row, no "reorder your Seventh Generation dish soap" nudge. Amazon *knows* me, but this viewport doesn't show it.
- **No price signal above the fold** — Not one deal, badge, or discount visible before scrolling. Amazon's homepage is usually wallpapered in Lightning Deals and deal-of-the-day strips. This feels unusually bare.
- **Navigation is truncated badly on mobile** — I can see "Health AI," "Amazon Haul," "Medical Care ▾," and then it clips off. My go-to categories (household, grocery, kids) aren't surfaced.
- **Soccer merch is not my reflex purchase** — The first product module is sports merch. That's a narrow slice of why I open the app. Even if I have a kid in soccer, this isn't the household-staple vibe I'm here for.

## Recommendations

- **Surface the returning-member rail above the Prime pitch** — If I'm logged in, lead with "Buy again" or "Your Subscribe & Save is due" before any acquisition messaging. I'm worth more retained than acquired.
- **Swap the hero for a deal-of-the-day callout** — A concrete "Today only: 30% off household essentials" with a countdown beats a static brand tagline every time for someone like me who shops on impulse during pickup line waits.
- **Pin two or three category shortcuts in mobile nav** — "Household," "Kids," and "Grocery" should be right there in the nav bar. "Health AI" and "Amazon Haul" are not my muscle memory destinations.
- **Add a loyalty reinforcement line under the Prime button** — Even one line like "You're a Prime member — free delivery on this order" reframes the whole hero from acquisition to affirmation.
