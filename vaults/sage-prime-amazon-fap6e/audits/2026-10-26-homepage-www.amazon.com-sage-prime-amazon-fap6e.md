# Homepage snapshot · www.amazon.com · 2026-10-26
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- Amazon is going all-in on Halloween. I see four purple and green hero cards (free shipping, trick-or-treating under $20, costumes under $50, party finds under $20), then a row of category tiles below them. It's loud and a little busy, but it's a good fit for what I'm buying this week. I have two kids and a trick-or-treat bag to fill.

## What's working

- **"Trick-or-treating under $20" card:** the Trolli, Ghirardelli and Sour Patch imagery next to a pumpkin bucket is exactly my school-pickup shopping list. The price cap tells me I can add to the cart without thinking.
- **"Costumes under $50 for women" card:** the big black headline on green is the easiest hero to read, and the price ceiling is clear.
- **"Free shipping on Halloween finds" and the Join Prime button:** shipping is the thing I care about, and the button is easy to spot.
- **Top nav (Groceries, Today's Deals, Coupons, Pharmacy, Whole Foods, Pet Supplies):** my household-resupply categories are one tap away.
- **Red "% off / Limited time deal" badges in the Amazon Devices tile:** they read as deals at a glance, which suits my impulse Lightning Deal habit.

## What's weak

- **Sign-in flyout:** it pops open over the nav and the fourth hero card. It hides "Beauty" and other categories, and it makes the page feel cluttered.
- **No "welcome back" feel:** nothing about Subscribe & Save, reorders or my cart. For someone who treats Amazon like a utility, that's the most useful thing they could show me.
- **Hero card 1 is murky:** the purple cloud with a blurry glass sphere and nail polish doesn't show me what "Halloween finds" are. The only CTA on the page sits on the weakest image.
- **Cards 2–4 have no buttons:** I have to guess that the whole card is tappable. The carousel arrow on the right edge is also easy to miss.
- **No ratings or review counts** on any visible product tile, so I have nothing to judge quality by.
- **Tile mix:** "Tory Burch new arrivals," Zappos boots and Amazon Devices don't speak to a mom doing Halloween and household resupply this week.
- **Auto-playing video:** the pause and mute controls on the hero cards add noise.

## Recommendations

- Delay the sign-in flyout until someone hovers or taps Account, or collapse it to a small "Sign in for your deals" chip. That keeps the full nav and hero visible.
- Add a returning-shopper strip right under the hero with "Buy it again," "Subscribe & Save due soon," and a "Your Prime delivery by [day]" line. For the Halloween push, add "Order by Thursday for Halloween delivery."
- Replace card 1's abstract cloud image with a real product collage (candy, costumes, decorations). Put a visible "Shop Halloween" button on every hero card, and state the free-shipping threshold in plain words.
- Show star ratings and review counts on the product tiles, and swap one of the fashion tiles for a "Halloween candy & snacks" or "Household essentials" tile.
