# Homepage snapshot · www.amazon.com · 2026-05-05
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-05**

- Amazon is doing two things at once in this viewport: pitching Prime to people who don't have it yet, and riding the Mother's Day wave with a "Designer gifts for Mom" section featuring Cartier and Gucci. As someone who already has Prime and is here to restock dish soap and maybe grab a Lightning Deal, neither of those things is why I opened this app. The brand looks polished and seasonally aware, but it's treating me like a stranger instead of a regular.
- **6/10**
- + "Designer gifts for Mom" section is visible and seasonally on-point — Mother's Day is days away, I'm a mom, I'll look
- + "Join Prime" button is visually unmistakable — yellow on blue, hard to miss
- + Visual hierarchy is clean — Prime banner anchors the eye, product grid follows naturally
- + Current campaign matches the calendar (Mother's Day, early May 2026)

## What's working

- **"Designer gifts for Mom" section** — the timing is sharp. Mother's Day is this weekend, I'm a mom, and even if I'm not buying Gucci, this reminds me the gift-giving window is open.
- **"Join Prime" CTA button** — yellow pill on a blue field is visually clean and impossible to miss. Good button craftsmanship even if I don't need it.
- **Location indicator ("Delivering to Monterey ... 91754")** — small but meaningful. Signals the site knows roughly where I am, which means delivery estimates will be relevant.
- **"Medical Care" in the nav** — as a mom of two, this category is genuinely useful and I'm glad it's surfaced without digging.

## What's weak

- **Prime acquisition hero for a Prime member** — the entire above-the-fold hero is wasted on me. I already pay for Prime. Showing me "Join Prime" is like a coffee shop greeting a loyalty regular with "First time here?" every visit.
- **Hero text is cropped** — "Fast, free shipp..." and "plus streaming" are cut off at the right edge. Even on a phone, incomplete text looks broken and chips at trust.
- **No personalized hooks** — no recently-viewed items, no "pick up where you left off," no Subscribe & Save reminders. This is Amazon's biggest superpower and it's completely absent here.
- **Luxury brand positioning doesn't match my cart** — Cartier and Gucci are aspirational, not the daily-utility items I'm here to grab. The gift angle is fine but it's not doing anything for conversion on my actual shopping intent.
- **No Lightning Deals, no price drops, nothing urgent** — I came here partly hoping to catch a deal. There's nothing above the fold that gives me a reason to shop *now*.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the "Join Prime" hero for a returning-member banner** — something like "Your Prime benefits are active — free same-day on 5M+ items" keeps the Prime brand visible without insulting the people who already pay for it.
- **Surface a Subscribe & Save "restock due" module above the fold** — if the app knows I'm a repeat buyer, show me "3 items in your S&S due this week." That's one tap to cart and it directly serves my actual use case.
- **Add a Lightning Deals countdown strip** — even a single "Deal ends in 2:14:09" banner would give me a reason to act now instead of closing the app during school pickup.
- **Fix the hero text clipping** — the right-edge cutoff on the Prime headline needs responsive text sizing or a line-break so the copy completes inside the viewport.
