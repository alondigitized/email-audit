# Homepage snapshot · www.amazon.com · 2026-05-07
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- Okay so I open Amazon on my phone during pickup line and the hero is... NBA Playoffs? With two basketball players taking up most of my screen? This is the everything store and the best they've got for me above the fold is playoff game coverage. The jewelry grid below it is more my speed, but by the time I scrolled past the sports imagery I was already mentally checking out. Amazon feels like Amazon — massive, functional, always there — but this particular homepage snapshot is clearly not dressed for me.

## What's working

- **Personalized delivery header** — "Delivering to Monterey ... 91754" tells me instantly I'm logged in and my address is set. That one line saves me from having to think about whether my order will go to the right place.
- **"New season jewelry" grid** — necklaces, rings, a nice watch, gold flower earrings. This is genuinely appealing and browsable. Spring refresh energy, product images are clean and uncluttered.
- **NBA Playoffs timeliness** — I'll give them credit for not running a Christmas promo in May. At least it's seasonally current, even if it's not for me.
- **Tappable product tiles** — the jewelry items look like product cards I can tap directly into, which is exactly how I browse on my phone.

## What's weak

- **NBA Playoffs hero** — Two male basketball players in team jerseys is the single biggest piece of real estate on the page, and it has nothing to do with why I open Amazon. This isn't even a shop-NBA-gear CTA — it feels like a brand partnership splash that just... sits there.
- **Zero Prime/Subscribe & Save signal** — I am the textbook Prime subscriber and there's no membership hook, no "your Prime benefits," no S&S reminder anywhere in this viewport.
- **No visible CTA button** — I can't see a single tappable button with copy above the jewelry grid. The hero has no "Shop now," the nav is text-only. Where do I *go*?
- **20% badge is clipped** — There's a red discount badge partially visible at the far right edge, but I can't tell what it's on. Feels accidental rather than promotional.
- **Navigation bar is thin** — "Amazon Haul," "Medical Care," "Amazon Basics" — none of those are where I shop most. Household essentials, Baby & Kids, Kitchen — not visible.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the NBA Playoffs hero for a personalized "Good evening, [name]" module** — returning Prime users on mobile should see a you-specific carousel (recently viewed, Subscribe & Save due, a trending item in household). The sports splash belongs in a category page, not the homepage for a logged-in mom.
- **Surface the Prime membership bar** — a single sticky strip that says "Prime | Free delivery on this order | Subscribe & Save active" would make me feel like the subscription is working for me right now.
- **Make the jewelry CTA explicit** — add a "Shop New Arrivals" button under the jewelry grid header. Right now I'm guessing those tiles are tappable; a button removes the doubt.
- **Fix the clipped 20% badge** — either give that promo module enough width to breathe, or move it into its own full-width strip with the product name and a "Ends tonight" label.
