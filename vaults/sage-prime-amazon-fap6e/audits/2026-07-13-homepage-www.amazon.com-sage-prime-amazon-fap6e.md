# Homepage snapshot · www.amazon.com · 2026-07-13
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-13**
## Executive summary

- Amazon is running a full back-to-school push right now — the entire hero is dedicated to "Our Top 100+ picks for college" on a loud purple background. As a brand, this is Amazon doing its usual "be everything to everyone" thing, but right now it's loudly talking to an 18-year-old loading a dorm room, not me refilling Cascade pods after school pickup. The bones of a fast, familiar storefront are here, but this homepage isn't speaking to me.

## What's working

- **Seasonal accuracy** — back-to-school in July is on time; even if I'm not the primary target, this signals Amazon is running a real campaign, not recycling banners
- **Clean, uncluttered layout** — the purple hero with product tile grid has no banner chaos or pop-ups fighting for my attention; I can actually see the page
- **Logged-in state recognition** — my delivery address in the header makes the experience feel personalized even when the hero isn't aimed at me
- **"Save on Amazon Devices >"** peeking below the fold — Echo and Fire tablets are things I actually buy, so this pulls me to scroll down
- **"Health AI" in the nav** — genuinely interesting tap for someone who uses Amazon for household health needs; feels like a new thing to explore

## What's weak

- **Hero is completely off-persona** — a gaming controller, dorm sneakers, and college pens are not in my cart; my cart has dish soap, kids' multivitamins, and whatever was on sale in Subscribe & Save
- **No above-fold offer with a price anchor** — "Top 100+ picks" is editorial, not promotional. My deal reflex only fires when I see "20% off" or "starting at $X"
- **No CTA button with action copy** — the hero is a tappable image tile with no labeled button. I shouldn't have to guess that I'm supposed to tap a purple image block
- **Prime/member benefits are invisible** — as a Prime member, the whole reason I'm on Amazon is the membership working for me. Nothing above the fold says "free delivery" or shows my status
- **Nav is off for me** — "Health AI" and "Medical Care" are prominent, but "Grocery," "Household," and "Subscribe & Save" are nowhere in the top bar on this viewport

## Recommendations

- **Replace the monolithic hero with a returning-Prime-member module** — Amazon already knows I'm logged in; show me "Your Subscribe & Save ships Friday — add-ons close tonight" or a curated "based on your last 3 orders" strip; that's the conversion driver a college picks banner isn't
- **Add a Prime benefits strip directly under the nav** — a single thin bar: "Free same-day on $25+ | Prime members" reminds me why I'm here and makes the membership feel active above the fold
- **Anchor the college picks hero with a price badge** — even "from $8.99" or "deals from $15" in the corner converts better than a purely editorial frame
- **Reorder the mobile nav for returning shoppers** — push "Grocery," "Household," or "Subscribe & Save" before "Health AI"; the current order reads like it was built for first-time visitors, not people who buy from Amazon three times a week
