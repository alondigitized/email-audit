# Homepage snapshot · www.amazon.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so I pull up Amazon on my phone during pickup line and the first thing I see is "Join Prime" — as if I haven't been a Prime member since 2013. The hero is a straight-up acquisition pitch aimed at someone who hasn't signed up yet, which means this page has absolutely no idea who I am. Below that there's a "Summer occasionwear" shelf that's at least seasonally on-point, but I'm already irritated before I get there.

## What's working

- **"Summer occasionwear" category module** — the blue dress, gold earrings, and heels are at least seasonally right for May; I'd actually tap into this for a birthday dinner outfit
- **"Join Prime" CTA button design** — that yellow pill button is super visible, good contrast against the blue background; the button itself works even if the targeting is off
- **Amazon Basics in the nav bar** — I genuinely buy Amazon Basics constantly (batteries, ziplock bags, paper towels), so having it one tap away in the nav is a small win
- **Location personalization** — "Delivering to Monterey... 91754" at least signals the delivery infra knows where I am, even if the rest of the page acts like it doesn't

## What's weak

- **The hero is an acquisition play, not a retention play** — I'm already paying $139/year; showing me a "Join Prime" pitch wastes the most valuable real estate on the entire page
- **Hero text is cut off on the right edge** — "Fast, free shipp..." with the rest clipped looks like a render bug; on a phone I shouldn't need to mentally complete sentences
- **Zero personalization above the fold** — no greeting, no recently-viewed items, no "your Subscribe & Save is ready," no nothing; feels like I'm a stranger
- **Nav bar pushes Amazon Haul and Medical Care front-and-center** — these are not why I open the app daily; Household, Grocery, or Baby/Kids would feel more relevant to my actual cart
- **No active deal or discount** — not a single price, badge, or Lightning Deal visible; if I'm checking during pickup line I want to see something worth buying right now

## Recommendations

- **Swap the "Join Prime" hero for a personalized returning-member hero** — even something like "Welcome back — your Prime perks" with a "Shop today's deals" CTA would immediately feel like Amazon knows me
- **Surface a Lightning Deal or time-sensitive offer in the hero slot** — I open the app partly out of deal-reflex; a countdown timer on a household item would make me tap immediately
- **Fix the hero text clipping** — the headline should be rewritten to fit the mobile viewport without truncation; "Fast, free shipping + Prime Video" fits; "Fast, free shipp..." does not
- **Pin a Subscribe & Save callout above the fold** — for someone like me managing household resupply, a "Your next S&S delivery ships in 3 days — update your order" module is more valuable than any acquisition banner
