# Homepage snapshot · www.amazon.com · 2026-09-28
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- Popped open Amazon on my phone right between carpool lines and got hit with a full fashion editorial — brown tones, zebra print pillows, a Tory Burch shoot. It reads more like a Nordstrom mood board than the "grab paper towels and my kid's shin guards" app I actually use. The one thing that did land was "Prime Big Deals drop Oct 6-7" — that's the kind of date I'll actually remember to come back for.

## What's working

- **"Prime Big Deals drop Oct 6-7" tile** — specific date, feels real, I'd actually set a reminder.
- **"Save on Amazon Devices" module** — 47%/56%/40% off badges are the kind of Lightning Deal I impulse-tap during pickup line.
- **Nav bar has Groceries, Amazon Home, Pharmacy** right up top — my actual weekly errands are one tap away even if the hero doesn't show them.

## What's weak

- The entire top carousel (brown tones, "Maximalist Edit," designer watches) is styled for someone browsing a fashion mood board, not someone restocking paper towels and granola bars.
- I'm not signed in and there's zero nod to that — no "welcome back," no reorder shortcut, no rewards balance. For a Subscribe & Save regular that's a missed layup.
- That "Hello, sign in" dropdown is sitting right on top of the Prime Big Deal Days banner text, cutting it off mid-sentence. Looks glitchy.
- Nothing here screams "restock" — no diapers, no household essentials, no "buy again" rail, which is 80% of what I actually use this app for.

## Recommendations

- Swap the top-left hero tile from the flat-lay fashion shot to a "Reorder your essentials" or "Subscribe & Save" module — that's the actual daily-use case for shoppers like me.
- Add a "Buy it again" or recently-purchased rail right under the hero — I want my paper towels and kids' snacks one tap away, not designer watches.
- Fix the overlap where the account dropdown covers the Prime Big Deal Days banner — clean that up before it ships to more people.
- Since I'm not signed in, at least show a generic "Sign in for your Prime deals" nudge tied to something concrete (my order history, my list) instead of just a bare Sign in button.
