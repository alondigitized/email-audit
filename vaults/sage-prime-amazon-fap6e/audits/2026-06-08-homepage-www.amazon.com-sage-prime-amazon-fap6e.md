# Homepage snapshot · www.amazon.com · 2026-06-08
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-08**
## Executive summary

- Amazon is coming in hot with Early Prime Day energy — the hero is basically screaming "don't wait, deals are live now." For me, this reads as exactly the kind of homepage I expect to see when a sale is brewing: big bold numbers, a familiar blue palette, and that Prime logo front and center. It's pushing fashion/lifestyle right now with this "Blue hues" module, which is a little more curated than the usual chaotic Amazon splash.

## What's working

- **"Up to 70% off" + Prime Day branding** — I see this and my finger is already moving. This is the combo that gets me every time. Seventy percent is a number that stops a scroll.
- **"Early Prime Day" nav tab** — It's right there in the top navigation bar. One tap to the deals page. No hunting required.
- **"Limited-time offer" label** — Small but effective. It reinforces urgency without being obnoxious about it.
- **"Blue hues" curated module** — This is actually a smart move. Showing a coordinated aesthetic (blue dress, blue earrings, blue bag, blue bikini) feels more boutique-y than Amazon usually gets credit for. If I'm in a shopping mood, this pulls me in.
- **Delivery location visible in header** — "Delivering to Alhambra 91801" signals the account is active and my address is saved. That small personalization signal makes this feel like MY Amazon, not a generic storefront.

## What's weak

- **Hero text is clipped on the right** — "Shop Amazon de…" is cut off in the screenshot. Whether this is a viewport issue or a real layout bug, it looks sloppy on mobile.
- **No "pick up where you left off" module** — My Amazon homepage usually has a recently-viewed or "Keep shopping for…" rail. I don't see any of that here. A returning power-user like me misses that immediately.
- **"Blue hues" is a bit random for early June** — The category feels summery, which is seasonally fine, but it's not connected to the "up to 70% off" Prime Day message. The two modules feel disconnected, like the deal hero and the editorial content belong to different pages.
- **No household/essentials hook above fold** — I'm a Subscribe & Save person. Staples, baby. There's nothing nodding to replenishment or household deals, which is a real miss for someone like me.
- **Right-side content is cut off** — The "Lu…" section and a partial shoe image are visible but unusable. On a real phone this may be swipeable, but it creates a slightly incomplete impression.

## Recommendations

- **Pin a "Your Subscribe & Save is ready" or "Reorder your staples" strip** just below the hero for Prime members — even a single line with a "Reorder" button would convert me instantly.
- **Fix the hero text wrap on mobile** — "Shop Amazon deals" should fit on one line or gracefully break; the clipped "de…" is a polish issue that undercuts an otherwise strong offer.
- **Connect the curated editorial module to the sale** — "Blue hues — all on sale this Prime Day" with a deal badge on each tile would bridge the disconnect between the hero's price message and the fashion content below.
- **Add a "Recently viewed" or "Back to your cart" nudge** somewhere in the first two screens — for a habitual user like me, that's often the fastest path to a purchase.
