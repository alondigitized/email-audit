# Homepage snapshot · www.amazon.com · 2026-08-10
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- This reads like the everything-store grab bag it is — no single hero, no "welcome back Sage," just four competing banner tiles and a wall of category rails. Right now it's simultaneously pushing Halloween decor, summer swim, back-to-school water bottles, and designer resale bags, which feels less like a curated push and more like an algorithm throwing spaghetti at the wall.

## What's working

- The "Save on Amazon Devices" rail with 45%/63%/20% off tags is exactly the impulse-deal bait that gets my thumb moving.
- "Backyard BBQ essentials" (marinades, Ruffles, serveware) is genuinely useful for me right now in August — that's a real resupply trigger.
- "Shop by school category" and "Get your dorm essentials" acknowledge back-to-school season, which is timely for a mom juggling two kids.
- Clean grid layout, nothing broken or overlapping — easy to scan on my phone during pickup line.

## What's weak

- I'm not signed in and the page doesn't nudge me to — no "Hello, Sage" personalization or Subscribe & Save callout anywhere above the fold.
- The top banner strip is scattered: Halloween cat cauldron next to a beach bikini shot next to a Louis Vuitton resale ad — nothing says "this is for you," it says "this is for everyone and no one."
- "Curated designer picks" (Chanel, Rolex, Van Cleef & Arpels) is aspirational window dressing that has zero relevance to my weekly Target-run-replacement shopping.
- No mention of my Subscribe & Save items or a "reorder" shortcut, which is genuinely how I use Amazon most.

## Recommendations

- Swap the pre-loved luxury bag banner for a Subscribe & Save reminder tile — show me what's due to reorder.
- Lead the hero strip with something household-relevant (diapers, snacks, school supplies) instead of leading with device deals and designer resale.
- Add a "Hi, welcome back" personalization bar above the nav so it feels like it knows me, not a cold storefront.
- Tighten the seasonal mix — either commit to back-to-school as the dominant theme right now or clearly separate "Halloween prep" into its own later section instead of tile #2.
