# Homepage snapshot · www.amazon.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so Amazon opened straight into a big blue Prime recruitment pitch — "Fast, free shipping plus streaming" — which would be great if I weren't already a Prime member. Below that there's a "Summer occasionwear" module with dresses, jewelry, and heels that honestly? Not mad at it for a May browse. But this homepage is clearly working harder to sign up new Prime subscribers than to say hi to the customers who already spend $200 a month here.

## What's working

- **"Fast, free shipping plus streaming" hero** — the two-line value prop is instantly legible on a phone screen and hits the exact reason I pay for Prime. Even as an existing member, it's a quick gut-check that I'm in the right place.
- **"Join Prime" yellow CTA** — high-contrast on blue, right size for a thumb tap. No confusion about what the page wants you to do.
- **"Summer occasionwear" product tiles** — dresses, jewelry, heels, clutch — this is a solid above-fold product card for browsing on phone during school pickup. Pretty imagery, clear category labels.
- **Season timing** — May + summer occasion framing is appropriate. Not stale, not premature.

## What's weak

- **Prime acquisition banner shown to what is almost certainly an existing Prime member** — this is the homepage's biggest miss for me. I'm not joining Prime; I've been a member for years. Show me what's new in my reorder list, not a recruitment pitch.
- **No personalization above fold** — no delivery estimate, no "back in stock" alert, no loyalty status. Amazon knows everything about me and chose to show me a generic acquisition screen.
- **Nav bar is sparse and off-target** — "Amazon Haul," "Medical Care," "Amazon Basics" is visible in the nav strip. None of those are my first taps. Where's Grocery, Household, or even a quick search shortcut?
- **Hero text truncated at right edge** — "Fast, free shipp..." and "plus streaming" are cut off. Even if this is viewport clipping, it looks half-baked on a phone.
- **No social proof above fold** — no review counts, no "bestseller" badges on the product tiles visible.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the Prime acquisition hero for a returning-member banner** — if the user is logged in, show "Your next Subscribe & Save ships in 3 days" or a personalized "Back to your browsing" rail instead of "Join Prime." Amazon has the data; use it.
- **Add a deals strip directly under the hero** — a horizontal scrolling ribbon of "Today's Deals" or "Lightning Deals" would land exactly how I shop (impulse, on phone, price-sensitive to flash discounts).
- **Promote the household/grocery category in the visible nav** — replace "Amazon Haul" (off-brand for me) with "Household" or "Grocery & Essentials" as a quick-tap destination in the nav bar for repeat shoppers.
- **Put star ratings and Prime badges on the Summer occasionwear tiles** — four gold stars would make me tap a dress way faster than a clean product photo alone.
