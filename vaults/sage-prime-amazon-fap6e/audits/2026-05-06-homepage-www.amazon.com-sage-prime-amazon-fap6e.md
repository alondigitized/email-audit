# Homepage snapshot · www.amazon.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- This looks like the standard Amazon mobile homepage doing what Amazon always does — leading with a Prime acquisition push and a rotating hero. Right now it's screaming "fast shipping" and teasing a designer bag deal, which honestly feels weirdly premium for a Tuesday night scroll after bath time. It's Amazon, so I know where everything is, but the page isn't rolling out the red carpet for me as someone who's already a Prime member.

## What's working

- **Yellow "Join Prime" button** — visually impossible to miss, high contrast against the blue hero; even if it's misdirected at me, the tap target is satisfying
- **"15% off select designer brands" module** — a real, specific discount with recognizable brand thumbnails (Chanel, Louis Vuitton) that makes me stop scrolling for a beat
- **Top nav "Amazon Basics" and "Best Sellers" links** — two shortcuts I actually use when I'm resupplying household staples; they're right there without digging
- **Hero copy brevity** — "Fast shipping on millions of items" is five words I process instantly during pickup line; no cognitive load

## What's weak

- **"Join Prime" CTA is wasted on me** — I'm already a Prime member; showing me an acquisition button instead of something like "Your Prime deals today" feels like the page doesn't know who I am
- **Designer bag module is off-category** — Chanel and Louis Vuitton are beautiful but I'm not here for a splurge; this is Subscribe & Save territory, not luxury handbag territory
- **No household or family imagery anywhere** — zero lifestyle signals that this is a store for someone managing a home with two kids
- **Hero text is cropped** — "Fast shipping on millions of it" cuts off on the right edge; looks unfinished even if it's a viewport issue
- **No price anchoring for the free-shipping benefit** — "fast shipping" is vague; I want to see the Prime threshold or "free same-day" language that actually means something

## Recommendations

- **Replace the "Join Prime" hero CTA with a "Shop your Subscribe & Save" or "Prime deals for you" button** — I'm already signed in as a Prime member; the hero should reward retention, not pitch acquisition
- **Swap the designer-bag module for a "household essentials" or "top reorders" rail** — show me the paper towels, the vitamins, the dog food I bought last month; that's what keeps me in the app
- **Add a "free same-day delivery" or "arrives tomorrow" message to the hero** — concrete delivery language tied to my ZIP is the single fastest conversion trigger for someone like me
- **Surface a Subscribe & Save savings callout** — a line like "You're saving $14/month on subscriptions" in the header area would make me feel seen and stickier
