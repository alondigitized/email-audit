# Homepage snapshot · www.amazon.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- This is Amazon doing what Amazon does — leaning hard into the Prime pitch and riding a real promotional moment with Memorial Day. First impression: it's competent but oddly tone-deaf for a returning Prime member like me, because the entire hero is a recruitment banner aimed at people who *don't* have Prime yet. The Memorial Day Sale grid below it is actually what I came for, but I have to scroll past a "Join Prime" button to get there.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day Sale grid** — four category tiles (clothing, lawn & garden, headphones, home textiles) are exactly the kind of spontaneous mid-week browsing that gets me. One tap and I'm in.
- **"Join Prime" button hierarchy** — even though the message is wrong for me, the yellow button on blue is high contrast and the page never makes me guess where to look first.
- **Seasonal relevance** — "Shop Memorial Day Sale" is doing real work. It matches the actual calendar date and puts a sale frame on everyday categories I'd buy anyway.
- **Category diversity in the grid** — the mix (fashion, outdoor, electronics, home) covers the kind of restocking + impulse-buy combo that fills my cart.

## What's weak

- **Prime acquisition hero is dead weight for existing members** — I've had Prime for years. Showing me "Join Prime" feels like Amazon forgot I exist. The whole hero slot is wasted.
- **No prices, percentages, or savings amounts visible** — "Memorial Day Sale" without a single number is just a label, not an offer. Tell me it's 30% off or $15 back and I'll tap.
- **Hero text is visually clipped** — "Fast, free shipp..." and "plus streaming" are both cut off on the right edge. This looks broken on mobile.
- **No personalization hook above the fold** — Amazon knows everything about me. My recently viewed items, my Subscribe & Save status, my wishlist — none of it is here. This looks like the homepage for a first-time visitor.
- **Nav bar prioritizes "Health AI" and "Amazon Haul"** — interesting features, but not my first-tap habits. My usual categories (household, kids, pantry) aren't surfaced at all.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the Prime acquisition hero for a returning-member module** — show me my rewards balance, upcoming Subscribe & Save shipments, or a "You have X items on your wishlist — some are on sale" hook. One sentence of personalization would make me feel seen.
- **Add a savings number to the Memorial Day Sale header** — "Up to 40% off" or "Save an extra 15% with Prime" turns a category browse into a deal hunt. I'll stay longer if there's a number to chase.
- **Fix the mobile hero crop** — the headline text is literally cut off. Either shorten the copy or adjust the layout so the full value prop is legible without horizontal scrolling.
- **Surface one time-bound urgency signal** — a small "Sale ends Monday" badge on the Memorial Day grid would create just enough pressure to make me tap now instead of "saving it for later" and forgetting.
