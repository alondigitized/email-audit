# Homepage snapshot · www.amazon.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Amazon is in full Prime-recruitment mode here — the hero is basically a billboard saying "hey, you should have Prime," which is mildly ironic since I've been a Prime member since 2012. Below that it pivots into a "Summer occasionwear" grid with dresses and jewelry, which is fine but feels like a mall catalogue, not the "I need laundry detergent and a new lunchbox by Thursday" utility store I actually come here for. As a brand it looks clean and confident, but this particular screen feels like it doesn't know who I am at all.

## What's working

- **"Join Prime" yellow button** — high-contrast, impossible to miss, and the surrounding blue makes it pop even on a small phone screen during school pickup
- **"Summer occasionwear" grid** — clean square tiles with actual product photography (a blue maxi dress, yellow flower earrings, gold heels, pink clutch); the category is at least seasonally relevant in mid-May
- **Delivery location detection** — "Delivering to Monterey ... 91754 / Update location" at the top tells me Amazon already has my address, which builds trust fast
- **"Terms apply." disclosure** — it's small but it's there, right under the CTA; I appreciate the transparency even if I won't read it

## What's weak

- **The Prime pitch to a Prime member** — this view appears to show a logged-out or unrecognized session; for someone who shops here daily the "Join Prime" hero is a miss and makes the page feel cold and generic
- **Hero text truncation** — "Fast, free shippi..." is visibly cut off on the right side of the screen; on a phone this looks like a rendering issue and undermines the polished brand
- **Navigation bar choices** — "Amazon Haul," "Medical Care," "Amazon Basics" are the three visible links; none of these is where I'd go first for household staples or a Lightning Deal
- **No urgency, no deals above the fold** — I'm a Lightning Deal browser and there's nothing here saying "this expires in 2 hours" or "today only"; I'd have to scroll to find any sale hooks
- **No social proof** — no review stars, no "bestseller" badges, nothing that would stop my thumb mid-scroll

## Recommendations

- **Detect returning Prime members and swap the hero** — replace "Join Prime" with a personalized hook like "Welcome back — your Subscribe & Save ships Friday" or surface a Deal of the Day relevant to my purchase history
- **Fix the hero text truncation** — the headline should be fully readable on a standard phone viewport; either shorten the copy or adjust padding so "Fast, free shipping plus streaming" doesn't clip
- **Add a Lightning Deals strip above the fold** — even a single "Ends in 3:42" timer with a product tile would dramatically increase engagement for impulse-buy shoppers like me; put it between the hero and the occasionwear grid
- **Swap "Amazon Haul / Medical Care / Amazon Basics" for category shortcuts that match peak intent** — "Household," "Grocery," "Kids" or a personalized carousel based on my order history would make the nav immediately useful
