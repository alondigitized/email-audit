# Homepage snapshot · www.amazon.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Amazon is literally the store I live in, so landing here feels familiar — but this load hit me with a "Join Prime" pitch I've had since before my oldest could read. Below that, a Summer occasionwear grid with a pleated maxi and sparkly earrings actually makes me want to tap, but the hero real estate is completely wasted on me. This homepage looks like it forgot I already pay my $14.99 a month.

## What's working

- **Summer occasionwear grid** — the blue pleated maxi, gold snowflake earrings, strappy heels, and pink structured clutch are genuinely pretty product shots. I was not planning to browse fashion today but those images make me want to.
- **Clean mobile layout** — tiles are well-spaced, no crowding, images load at a reasonable size. Browsing during school pickup this would not frustrate me.
- **45% badge on right rail** — a real number peeking into frame is enough to make me swipe. Amazon knows this trick and it works on me every time.
- **"Join Prime" button contrast** — the yellow on blue is loud and unmissable, good execution on the button itself even if the whole module is misdirected at me.

## What's weak

- **"Join Prime" hero** — I've had Prime since before my kids started school. Showing me acquisition copy for a service I already subscribe to is a genuine miss. This slot should know I'm logged in and give me something useful.
- **Zero personalization** — Amazon has my entire purchase history, my Subscribe & Save schedule, my location. None of it is surfaced. I'm seeing the same screen as someone who has never bought anything here.
- **Nav doesn't surface my categories** — "Amazon Haul," "Medical Care," "Amazon Basics." Where is Household? Grocery? Deals? I tap "All" by reflex because these quick-links don't go where I actually shop.
- **No trust signals on occasionwear** — no star ratings, no "Free returns on fashion," nothing that makes a $45 impulse dress feel safe to add to cart on a phone screen.

## Recommendations

- **Detect logged-in Prime subscribers and replace the hero** — swap "Join Prime" for a personalized hook: active order status, an expiring Lightning Deal, or a Subscribe & Save reorder nudge. I should never see a sign-up CTA for something I already have.
- **Pin a Deals or Household shortcut in the top nav** — drop "Medical Care" (low-frequency for most shoppers) and put a Deals or Household tab where I can reach my regular categories in one tap.
- **Add a review count or "Free returns" badge to the occasionwear tiles** — even "4.5 ★ (2,847)" on the dress image would cut friction for a mobile impulse buy.
- **Drop a Subscribe & Save reminder module between the hero and occasionwear** — "Your next delivery: 4 items, ships in 3 days" would make this page feel like it knows me and keep me from opening a competitor app.
