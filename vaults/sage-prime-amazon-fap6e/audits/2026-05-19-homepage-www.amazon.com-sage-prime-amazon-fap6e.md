# Homepage snapshot · www.amazon.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- This is Amazon doing what Amazon does — serving up a Prime acquisition push as the above-the-fold hero, then pivoting to a random editorial called "The Italy Edit" before I can even see what's on sale this week. It reads like a utility homepage that knows I'm here but isn't quite sure what I need today. There's no household chaos energy, no "your Subscribe & Save ships tomorrow" hook — just a blue banner asking me to join a membership I've had for eight years.

## What's working

- **Location personalization at the top** ("Delivering to Alhambra 91801") — I immediately know Amazon knows who I am and where my packages land. That's a micro-trust signal I register instantly.
- **"Join Prime" yellow button** — the color contrast against the blue hero is strong. Even though the CTA isn't for me, the button design is textbook clarity.
- **"The Italy Edit" visual grid** — the illy coffee tin, the orange ghost chair, the Ray-Bans all look beautiful together. It's aspirational in a way that makes me pause for a second, which is the point.
- **Sale rail on the right** — the "35% off" badge on what looks like a Hisense TV tells me deals are here, which is legitimately relevant to my household-upgrade browsing.

## What's weak

- **The hero is a Prime recruitment pitch** — I have Prime. Seeing "Join Prime" as the first thing above the fold feels like being offered a loyalty card at a store where I already have one. It's a wasted hero slot for a returning shopper.
- **Hero text is cut off** — "Fast, free shipp..." wraps awkwardly off the right edge of the viewport. On mobile, the copy should fit the screen; this looks like a desktop design that wasn't responsively tested.
- **"The Italy Edit" is editorially random** — coffee, a designer chair, sunglasses, and what looks like shaving cream. Aspirational, sure, but I came here to reorder dish soap and check Lightning Deals, not browse a curated lifestyle collection I didn't ask for.
- **No household, no Subscribe & Save, no kids** — there's nothing in this above-the-fold viewport that speaks to my actual Amazon life. Amazon Haul and Amazon Basics are in the nav bar but they're not featured.
- **Right-side sale module is almost entirely cropped** — the 35% deal teaser is barely peeking in. If that's a great sale, let me see it.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the Prime pitch hero with a personalized returning-shopper hero** — even a "Back to stock: your Subscribe & Save items" or "Your Lightning Deals today" module would immediately pull me in vs. the generic acquisition banner.
- **Fix the mobile hero text wrapping** — "Fast, free shipp..." truncating mid-word on a phone screen is a polish issue Amazon shouldn't be shipping. Either shorten the copy or adjust the container.
- **Rename or re-theme the editorial module for the season** — "The Italy Edit" is lovely but feels disconnected from May. A "Memorial Day Refresh for Your Home" or "Summer Stock-Up" edit would connect the lifestyle curation to utility in a way that lands for me.
- **Surface the sale rail** — whatever is 35% off deserves its own above-the-fold strip, not a half-cropped peek on the right edge of the screen.
