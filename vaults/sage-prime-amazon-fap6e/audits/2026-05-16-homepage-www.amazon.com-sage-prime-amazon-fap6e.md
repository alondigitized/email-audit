# Homepage snapshot · www.amazon.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Okay, first thought: Amazon is pitching me Prime like I'm a stranger who just wandered in off the street. I have literally had a Prime membership since 2013. The hero is a giant blue acquisition ad, and the only actual shopping content I can see is something called "The Italy Edit" — which is cute, but I came here to restock dish soap and maybe grab a Lightning Deal, not to plan a Roman holiday. It feels like the algorithm forgot I exist.

## What's working

- **"The Italy Edit" category tiles** — Grocery & gourmet food and Home & furniture are genuinely in my wheelhouse. I'd actually tap into both of those.
- **Location aware header** — "Delivering to Alhambra 91801" confirms my address is recognized even before I log in, which is a small trust signal I appreciate.
- **"Amazon Basics" in the nav bar** — That's my brand. That's where I live. Good to have it front and center.
- **"Join Prime" CTA button** is visually unambiguous — yellow on blue, good contrast, no guessing.

## What's weak

- **The entire hero is an acquisition pitch aimed at non-members** — I am a deeply loyal Prime subscriber and Amazon is greeting me like we've never met. That's a miss at the most fundamental personalization level.
- **Hero text is visually cut off** — "Fast, free shipp..." and "plus streaming..." are chopped on the right edge. On a phone, the hero copy is literally illegible. That's a rendering failure, not a choice.
- **No deals, no Lightning Deals, no "Today's deals"** — Amazon's superpower is the deal engine, and none of it is showing above the fold. This is the first thing I look for when I open the app.
- **No people anywhere** — The Italy Edit tiles show a coffee can, a chair, sunglasses, and grooming product. A suburban mom of two connects to lifestyle imagery. This is a catalog, not a homepage.
- **"Amazon Haul" and "Medical Care" in the top nav** feel mismatched — Medical Care next to Amazon Haul is a confusing pairing for a quick browse session.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the Prime acquisition hero for a personalized returning-member banner** — even something generic like "Welcome back — today's best deals" with a rotating deal tile is miles better than "Join Prime" for someone who's already in.
- **Add a "Today's Deals" strip immediately below the hero** — one horizontal scrollable row of Lightning Deals or limited-time offers. This is what I actually open the app for and it should be the second thing I see.
- **Fix the hero text rendering on mobile** — the copy is clipping off the right edge; either shorten the headline or adjust viewport scaling so the full value prop is readable.
- **Inject a lifestyle image into "The Italy Edit"** — even one tile with a warm kitchen moment or a family around a dinner table would make this feel like it's speaking to me rather than at me.
