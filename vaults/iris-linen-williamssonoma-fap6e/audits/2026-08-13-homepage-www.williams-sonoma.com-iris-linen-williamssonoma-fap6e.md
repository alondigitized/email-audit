# Homepage snapshot · www.williams-sonoma.com · 2026-08-13
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- Before I can even see what's for sale, a giant popup box is asking for my email and phone number to enter a sweepstakes for a "Hill House Nantucket Getaway." I can tell this is Williams Sonoma from the logo and the sliver of a beautifully set table peeking out behind the modal, but the actual homepage — new arrivals, cookware, whatever's on sale — is completely blocked. It reads like a brand more interested in capturing my contact info than showing me what I came here to buy.

## What's working

- The "Free Shipping on Cookware, Electrics, Cutlery & More" banner at the very top is exactly my kind of offer — I'm a cookware person, so that catches my eye immediately.
- The sliver of imagery I can see behind the modal — a moody table set with wine glasses and what looks like a floral runner — has that elevated entertaining vibe that fits my weekend-baker, registry-graduated-to-All-Clad sensibility.
- The nav bar itself is clean and organized, with "Cookware" and "Gifts" both one tap away, which I appreciate since I don't like hunting through menus on my phone.
- The tile collage inside the popup (floral table setting, stacked pillows, a flower-draped shopfront) is genuinely pretty — it's on-brand imagery even if it's in the wrong place right now.

## What's weak

- The sweepstakes modal is the single biggest problem — it completely covers the homepage the moment I land, so I can't see any actual products, promotions, or new arrivals until I dismiss it.
- Asking for my phone number "optional" alongside email, with a paragraph about "automated marketing text messages," feels like a lot to ask for before I've even seen a product.
- I can't evaluate the real hero, category rail, or loyalty program because they're all hidden — this homepage isn't doing its job as a homepage right now.
- The tiny "No, Thanks" link at the bottom is easy to miss against the busier "ENTER TO WIN" button above it — the visual hierarchy nudges me toward signing up rather than browsing.

## Recommendations

- Delay the sweepstakes popup by a few seconds or trigger it on exit-intent/scroll instead of instantly on page load, so I see the actual homepage first.
- Make the "No, Thanks" dismiss option visually equal in weight to "ENTER TO WIN" — right now it reads like an afterthought.
- Drop the phone number field entirely for a sweepstakes; it's an unnecessary ask that makes the whole thing feel like a data grab dressed up as a giveaway.
- Once the modal is gone, lead with the cookware/free-shipping banner content translated into an actual hero image and CTA — that's the offer that would actually get me shopping.
