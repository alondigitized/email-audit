## What's working

- **"Spring Cookware Event" hero** — three words and I know this page is for me. No lifestyle fluff, no ambiguity. The sage green background reinforces a kitchen-adjacent color palette I respond to.
- **Le Creuset limited-time deal module** — showing a specific SKU ("Signature Enameled Cast Iron Deep Round Oven, 6½-Qt.") with a real was/now price ($460 → $289.95) is exactly how you convert someone like me who has been watching that pot for two years.
- **"FREE SHIPPING" strip** — visible without scrolling, zero friction to add to cart mentally.
- **Clean, uncluttered mobile layout** — no aggressive pop-ups, no overlay, no cookie banner obstructing the view. For medium-tech-comfort me, this is a relief.

## What's weak

- **No loyalty hook above the fold** — I've been a Williams-Sonoma customer for a decade. Nothing says "welcome back" or shows me my rewards balance. That's a miss for a brand whose loyalty program (The Key) is a real differentiator.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA is generic** — "Shop Le Creuset" or "Shop the Event" would have felt more specific and enticing for someone who knows exactly what she's looking for.
- **No urgency deadline** — "LIMITED TIME DEAL" with no end date is wasted opportunity. A "Ends Sunday" or countdown would push me to act instead of tab-away and forget.
- **No imagery of the actual cookware in the hero** — the sage green text-only hero is elegant but abstract. A shot of the Dutch oven on a stovetop would stop the scroll harder.
- **Free shipping threshold not stated** — "FREE SHIPPING" is promised but I can't tell if it's sitewide or $150+. That ambiguity slows the mental add-to-cart.

## Recommendations

- **Add a countdown or date to the Le Creuset module** — "Ends May 11" costs nothing to publish and meaningfully accelerates purchase decisions for someone like me who deliberates.
- **Swap the sage text-only hero for a lifestyle cookware shot** — copper pot on a marble counter, morning light, no model needed. Give me something to aspire to, not just a sale sign.
- **Surface a loyalty CTA** — even a one-liner: "The Key members earn double points through Sunday" would make me feel seen as a repeat customer, not a first-time visitor.
- **Replace "SHOP NOW" with "Shop the Event" or "Shop Le Creuset"** — specificity signals confidence and reduces hesitation.
