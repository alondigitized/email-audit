# Homepage snapshot · www.williams-sonoma.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Williams-Sonoma knows exactly who they're talking to this weekend. The moment the page loads I see a bold red Memorial Day sale banner screaming "up to 75% Off" with free shipping on cookware — and then, right below it, a Le Creuset Dutch oven marked down from $460 to $289.95. This is a premium kitchen brand doing exactly what it should during a sale weekend: leading with product I already want, at a price that makes me feel like I've been waiting for the right moment.

## What's working

- **The Memorial Day red banner**: Full-bleed crimson with white serif type is instantly authoritative — this doesn't look like a clearance bin, it looks like an event.
- **"Free Shipping on Cookware, Cutlery & Electrics"**: That's category-specific, not a vague "$X minimum" — I know immediately it applies to the thing I came here to buy.
- **Le Creuset product card below the hero**: $289.95 struck through from $460 on the 6½-Qt. Dutch oven. I have been eyeing a second Dutch oven. This is the hook.
- **Two distinct CTAs**: "SHOP LIMITED TIME DEALS" vs. "SHOP CLEARANCE" — I can self-sort immediately into "I want a deal on good stuff" vs. "I want the real end-of-life discounts." That's a smart fork.
- **Search bar prominently above the fold**: For a site with this breadth, surfacing search immediately is the right call on mobile.

## What's weak

- **"Up to 75% Off" is doing a lot of work**: In my experience, "up to" usually means one spatula at 75% and everything I want at 20%. No clarification of what's genuinely at the deep end of that range.
- **No Key Rewards / loyalty callout**: I've shopped here enough to have points. Telling me I could earn or redeem on this purchase right now would be a significant push — instead there's silence.
- **No urgency deadline**: "Memorial Day Sale" implies Monday, but there's no "Ends May 26" or countdown. I might tell myself I'll come back tomorrow and never return.
- **No social proof on the Le Creuset feature**: That $289.95 product card has no star rating, no "4,200 reviews" — for a $290 item I want confirmation before I tap "Shop Now."
- **Top nav is hamburger-only on mobile**: I can't browse to "Bakeware" or "Linens" without opening a menu. For someone who arrived via an email and knows what she wants, that's fine — but for discovery it adds friction.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Ends Monday, May 26" deadline line** directly under the sale headline — one line of text, zero engineering lift, meaningful urgency for a customer who is comparison shopping.
- **Surface Key Rewards prominently**: A small strip under the hero — "Key Rewards members earn 10% back. Sign in to apply." — would close the loop for any returning shopper who recognizes her account icon.
- **Add star ratings to the Le Creuset product card**: Even just "★★★★★ 4,211 reviews" under the price converts fence-sitters. That product has thousands of reviews — use them.
- **Clarify the tier structure of the sale**: "Up to 75% off — cookware from 20%, electrics from 30%" gives me the map so I know whether to click through or wait for a better week.
