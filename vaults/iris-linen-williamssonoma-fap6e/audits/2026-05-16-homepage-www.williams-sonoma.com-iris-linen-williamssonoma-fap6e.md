# Homepage snapshot · www.williams-sonoma.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- This is Williams-Sonoma doing what Williams-Sonoma does best — making me feel like I should be setting a more beautiful table. The "Alfresco Dining" hero is lovely and seasonally spot-on for May, and the imagery (blue-and-white plates, that gorgeous yellow ceramic pitcher, a wine bottle half in frame) is exactly the kind of thing I'd tear out of a magazine. What it's not doing is giving me a reason to act right now — no offer, no membership hook, nothing pulling me past "that's pretty" into "I need that."

## What's working

- **The hero image itself** — blue and white patterned plates, a lemon-water pitcher, a wine bottle, fresh greenery — this is my dinner party in aspirational form. Stops my thumb cold.
- **"ALFRESCO DINING" headline** — two words that land perfectly for a warm May evening. No overcrowding, no jargon.
- **Seasonal accuracy** — they didn't leave a Valentine's Day or winter holiday hero up. That sounds like a low bar but it matters; it tells me the brand is paying attention.
- **Clean mobile layout** — the search bar is prominent and accessible, the nav icons are appropriately sized for a phone screen. Nothing feels broken.

## What's weak

- **No offer above the fold at all** — not even a free-shipping threshold. For a brand at Williams-Sonoma price points, I need some reason to commit beyond pretty pictures.
- **"SHOP TABLE SETTING INSPIRATION" CTA copy is soft** — "Inspiration" as a destination tells me I'm going to browse a mood board, not find a product. That's one extra tap between me and anything in my cart.
- **No loyalty acknowledgment** — I've spent real money here over the years. Nothing says "welcome back" or hints at member pricing.
- **The top navigation bar** ("Williams Sonoma Home | Business to Business | Pottery") is an odd trio — "Business to Business" feels out of place and slightly cold on what should be a warm, inviting homepage.

## Recommendations

- **Overlay a secondary badge or banner strip** with a concrete offer — "Free shipping on orders $150+" costs nothing to add and takes one of my biggest hesitations off the table before I scroll.
- **Swap "INSPIRATION" for something that gets me one tap closer to product** — "SHOP THE TABLE" or "SHOP ALFRESCO" would do it; keeps the editorial feel while making the destination feel transactional.
- **Add a thin personalization or loyalty strip below the hero** — even a simple "Key Rewards member? See your points balance." signals that they know me.
- **Move "Business to Business" out of the consumer-facing top nav** — replace that slot with a high-value consumer category link (Cookware, Bakeware, or Sale) so my category is one tap away, not buried in the hamburger.
