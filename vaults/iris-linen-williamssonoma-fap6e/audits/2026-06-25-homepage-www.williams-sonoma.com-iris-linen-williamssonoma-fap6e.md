# Homepage snapshot · www.williams-sonoma.com · 2026-06-25
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-25**
## Executive summary

- I opened Williams-Sonoma and got hit with a sweepstakes popup before I could even see the store. The brand is clearly leaning into a Hill House collaboration right now — white linens, Nantucket, aspirational East Coast summer — which honestly does speak to my aesthetic. But covering the entire homepage with a lead-gen modal the second I land is not the welcome I expected from a brand I've shopped with for fifteen years.

## What's working

- **The tablescape image** — green produce on beautiful plates against a linen-draped surface is exactly the kind of aspirational-but-attainable image that makes me stop scrolling. That's my Saturday morning.
- **The Hill House collab framing** — "VIP shopping at the new Hill House store, a professional photo session" reads like an event worth caring about; it's specific and experiential, not generic.
- **The woman in the image** — white sundress, brunette, somewhere in her 40s — looks like me, not like someone half my age. That's rarer than it should be.
- **The sweepstakes copy is clear** — "Enter for a Chance to Win" is plain English, no tricks about what I'm signing up for (at least in the headline).

## What's weak

- **The modal itself** — interrupting me before I see a single product is a dark pattern. I came to browse copper pans or check the registry section; instead I'm filling out a sweepstakes form.
- **No submit button visible** — the form has email and phone fields but the CTA button is cut off below the fold of the modal. I have no idea what I'm tapping to proceed.
- **SMS consent fine print** — four sentences of small-print legal text about wireless carriers and automated marketing underneath the form. It reads like a terms-of-service dump, not a gracious brand invitation.
- **Underlying homepage is completely invisible** — I cannot see a single category, product, banner, or nav link behind the modal. There is no way to evaluate what's actually in the store.
- **No loyalty hook** — I've been a Williams Sonoma customer for years. No "Welcome back" moment, no rewards balance, nothing acknowledging me as a returning shopper.

## Recommendations

- **Delay the modal by 30 seconds or trigger it on exit-intent** — let me see the homepage for a moment before asking for my email. I'm far more likely to enter a sweepstakes after I've been reminded why I love this brand.
- **Swap the phone-number field for a cleaner two-field form with a visible submit button** — right now the CTA is cut off. If I can't see the button, I can't convert.
- **Add a "No thanks, take me to the store" skip link in large, accessible text** — the X close button is small and easy to miss on mobile; a plain-language skip makes the experience feel respectful rather than trapped.
- **Move the SMS fine print to a collapsed accordion or linked disclosure** — "Wireless carriers are not liable for undelivered messages" does not belong in the first thing I read on a premium cookware site.
